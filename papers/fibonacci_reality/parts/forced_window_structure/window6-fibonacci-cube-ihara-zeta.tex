\subsection{Window-six Fibonacci-cube Ihara--Hashimoto polynomial}
\label{subsec:window6-fibonacci-cube-ihara-zeta}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
Let $\{0,1\}^6$ be the set of binary words of length $6$. Define
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_6=\{w\in\{0,1\}^6 : \text{no two adjacent coordinates of }w\text{ are both }1\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite set with $|X_6|=21$. The graph $\Gamma_6$ has vertex set $X_6$, and two distinct vertices $v,w\in X_6$ are joined by an undirected edge exactly when they differ in one coordinate. Equivalently, adjacency is the Hamming-distance-one relation restricted to the no-adjacent-ones words. Direct finite enumeration gives $38$ undirected edges. Hence the directed-edge set
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_6^{\rightarrow}=\{(v,w)\in X_6\times X_6 : v\text{ and }w\text{ are adjacent in }\Gamma_6\}
\end{aligned}
$$
has $76$ elements. For a directed edge $e=(v,w)$ write $o(e)=v$, $t(e)=w$, and $\overline e=(w,v)$. The graph is connected, so its first Betti number is
$$
\begin{aligned}
38-21+1=18.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Non-backtracking kernel.}
Index the rows and columns of a $76\times 76$ matrix $B_6$ by directed edges in $E_6^{\rightarrow}$. Define
$$
\begin{aligned}
B_6[e,f]=1
\quad\text{if and only if}\quad
 t(e)=o(f)\text{ and }f\ne \overline e,
\end{aligned}
$$
and put $B_6[e,f]=0$ otherwise. Thus $B_6$ is the Hashimoto transition matrix for one-step non-backtracking motion on the finite graph $\Gamma_6$. Its characteristic zeta inverse is the integer polynomial $\det(I_{76}-uB_6)$.

\paragraph{Exact determinant factorization.}
The finite determinant is even in $u$ up to the Betti factor and has the following exact form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(I_{76}-uB_6)=(1-u^2)^{18}P(u^2)Q(u^2).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(s)=&\,1+10s+49s^2+162s^3+434s^4+1050s^5+2273s^6\\
&+4128s^7+5376s^8+3456s^9,
\end{aligned}
$$
and
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q(s)=&\,1+8s-2s^2-350s^3-2541s^4-10696s^5-32073s^6\\
&-73376s^7-128427s^8-162816s^9-132480s^{10}-51840s^{11}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All coefficients displayed here are part of the finite integer output of the construction: the input is only the $21$ vertices, the $38$ undirected Hamming edges, and the non-backtracking transition rule above.

\paragraph{Bass determinant check.}
Let $A_6$ be the $21\times 21$ adjacency matrix of $\Gamma_6$, so $A_6[v,w]=1$ precisely when $v$ and $w$ differ in one coordinate and both lie in $X_6$. Let $D_6$ be the diagonal $21\times 21$ degree matrix, with $D_6[v,v]$ equal to the degree of $v$ in $\Gamma_6$. The corresponding Bass-side determinant is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(I_{21}-uA_6+u^2(D_6-I_{21}))=(1-u^2)P(u^2)Q(u^2).
\end{aligned}
$$
Combining this identity with the Betti number $18$ gives the displayed Hashimoto determinant factor $(1-u^2)^{18}$ on the directed-edge side.

\paragraph{Trace witnesses.}
For each positive integer $m$, the trace $\operatorname{tr}(B_6^m)$ counts length-$m$ closed directed non-backtracking walks in $\Gamma_6$, with a choice of starting directed edge. Because the graph is bipartite under parity of Hamming weight, every odd non-backtracking trace vanishes. The first even trace witnesses recorded by direct finite multiplication are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(\operatorname{tr}B_6^4,\operatorname{tr}B_6^6,\operatorname{tr}B_6^8,\operatorname{tr}B_6^{10},\operatorname{tr}B_6^{12})
=(176,984,7088,58800,448664).
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated primitive cyclic non-backtracking counts, obtained by subtracting proper repetitions before quotienting by cyclic rotation of the starting edge, are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(C_4,C_6,C_8,C_{10},C_{12})=(44,164,864,5880,37292).
\end{aligned}
$$
For the displayed lengths this extraction is witnessed by the identities
$$
\begin{aligned}
4C_4&=176,\\
6C_6&=984,\\
8C_8&=7088-176,\\
10C_{10}&=58800,\\
12C_{12}&=448664-984-176.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the determinant factorization is accompanied by independent closed-walk data from the same finite non-backtracking kernel.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The window-six Fibonacci-cube graph $\Gamma_6$ therefore supplies a closed finite spectral package: a $21$-vertex carrier, $38$ undirected Hamming-distance-one edges, a $76$-state directed non-backtracking transition matrix, the exact Ihara--Hashimoto inverse
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(I_{76}-uB_6)=(1-u^2)^{18}P(u^2)Q(u^2),
\end{aligned}
$$
the matching Bass determinant
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(I_{21}-uA_6+u^2(D_6-I_{21}))=(1-u^2)P(u^2)Q(u^2),
\end{aligned}
$$
and the listed even trace and primitive-cycle witnesses. The construction is wholly finite: every matrix entry is determined by the word predicate, Hamming adjacency, and the single non-reversal rule for directed edges.
