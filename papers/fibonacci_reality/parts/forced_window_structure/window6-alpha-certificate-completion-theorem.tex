\subsection{Window-6 alpha certificate-completion theorem}
\label{sec:window6-alpha-certificate-completion-theorem}

\paragraph{Certificate carrier.}
The finite forced-window carrier supplies the symbolic witness
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{FiniteWitness}_{W6}
=
\left(
D_0,\ s_6,\ \rho_6,\ Q_{6,\alpha},\ D_6^{*},\ G_{10}
\right),
\end{aligned}
$$
where
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_0&=47=F_9+F_7=F_{10}-F_6,\\
s_6&=7,\\
\rho_6&=10,\\
Q_{6,\alpha}(u)&=1-\frac{1}{2}u+\frac{8}{9}u^2,\\
D_6^{*}&=47+\phi^{-7}-\frac{1}{2}\phi^{-17}+\frac{8}{9}\phi^{-27},\\
G_{10}&=\mathrm{Z}/10\mathrm{Z}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This witness is finite and symbolic.  It does not choose an absolute clock origin, does not certify the response functor, does not certify the coefficient use, and does not identify a physical value of $\alpha$.

The external alpha-readout carrier is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_{\alpha}=G_{10}\times Q_{\mathrm{space}}\times R_{\mathrm{space}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
An element of $M_{\alpha}$ consists of a clock-origin choice, a response certificate, and a physical representation certificate.  A complete readout package has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{C}_{\alpha}
=&
\ \mathrm{ClockCouplingCert}(g)
+\mathrm{ResponseFunctorCert}\\
&+\mathrm{CoefficientUseCert}
+\mathrm{PhysReadoutCert}_{\alpha}
+\mathrm{MetrologicalGaugeSelectionCert}.
\end{aligned}
$$

\begin{theorem}[Alpha certificate completion]
\label{thm:window6-alpha-certificate-completion}
Assume $\mathrm{FiniteWitness}_{W6}$ and the certificate package $\mathcal{C}_{\alpha}$.  Assume the response certificate selects
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{Z6}
=
47+\phi^{-7}-\frac{1}{2}\phi^{-17}+\frac{8}{9}\phi^{-27},
\end{aligned}
$$
the readout certificate selects
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_6(D)
=
2\pi
\frac{21+34C_6(D)}
{21\phi^{-6}+34C_6(D)\phi^{-7}},
\qquad
C_6(D)
=
\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}\cos^2(\pi/\phi)+\frac{1}{D\phi^5},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the metrological certificate selects the static-impedance branch $a=0$.  Then the completed readout determines the candidate
$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_{Z6}^{-1}
=R_6(D_{Z6})
=137.035999177006279\ldots .
\end{aligned}
$$
The implication is a certificate-completion theorem.  It is not a theorem of the form $\mathrm{Window6}\Rightarrow \alpha$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The finite witness supplies the displayed $D_{Z6}=D_6^{*}$ only as a symbolic window value.  The clock certificate fixes the external origin used to read the exponents, the response functor certificate permits the displayed residual polynomial to be used as a response law, the coefficient certificate permits the entries $1,-1/2,8/9$ to be used as coefficients of that response law, and the physical readout certificate interprets $R_6(D)$ as the inverse fine-structure readout.  The metrological gauge certificate supplies the static-impedance branch selection.  Substitution of the certified $D_{Z6}$ into the certified readout rule gives the displayed scalar.  Without the certificate package, the same finite witness remains only a gauge-indexed symbolic datum.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Monotone inversion.}
Let
$$
\begin{aligned}
A=21,\qquad B=34,\qquad x=\phi^{-6},\qquad y=\phi^{-7}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For $D>0$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_6(D)=2\pi\frac{A+BC_6(D)}{Ax+BC_6(D)y}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $x>y>0$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial R_6}{\partial C}
=
2\pi\frac{AB(x-y)}{(Ax+BCy)^2}
>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Also
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dC_6}{dD}=-\frac{1}{D^2\phi^5}<0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dR_6}{dD}<0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every experimental scalar $R_E$ lying in the image of a positive interval for $D$ therefore has at most one inverse value $D_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(E)}$ in that interval.  The experiment path ledger is consequently a monotone inverse ledger, not an arbitrary coordinate choice.

\paragraph{Exponent-gauge branch separation.}
For $a=0,\ldots,9$ define
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_6^{(a)}
=
47+\phi^{-(7+a)}
-\frac{1}{2}\phi^{-(17+a)}
+\frac{8}{9}\phi^{-(27+a)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently,
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_6^{(a)}=47+\phi^{-a}\phi^{-7}
\left(1-\frac{1}{2}\phi^{-10}+\frac{8}{9}\phi^{-20}\right).
\end{aligned}
$$
The parenthesized factor is positive.  Hence $D_6^{(a)}$ is strictly decreasing as an ordinary integer-indexed list for $a=0,\ldots,9$, and the ten exponent-shift branches are pairwise distinct.  Since $R_6(D)$ is strictly decreasing in $D$, the corresponding displayed inverse-alpha values are strictly increasing along this exponent-shift list.

The metrological gauge certificate may be recorded as the finite table
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_6(D_6^{(0)})&=137.035999177006279\ldots,\\
R_6(D_6^{(1)})&=137.036014983967651\ldots,\\
R_6(D_6^{(2)})&=137.036024757607050\ldots,\\
R_6(D_6^{(3)})&=137.036030799729983\ldots,\\
R_6(D_6^{(4)})&=137.036034534609852\ldots,\\
R_6(D_6^{(9)})&=137.036040033754215\ldots .
\end{aligned}
$$
Together with the static-impedance metrological interval
$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha^{-1}=137.035999177\pm 0.000000021,
\end{aligned}
$$
this certificate admits the zero branch and excludes the displayed nonzero exponent-shift branches.  Already the first nonzero branch has offset
$$
\begin{aligned}
137.036014983967651-137.035999177006279
\approx 1.58\cdot 10^{-5},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is about $752$ quoted standard uncertainties at radius $2.1\cdot 10^{-8}$.  This is a selection by physical readout certificate, not an internal selection by the finite window.

\paragraph{Clock-coupling boundary.}
The finite cyclic carrier has full residue support at every local event of the Window-6 construction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{r\in\mathrm{Z}/10\mathrm{Z}:z(w,r)\in P_{10}\}
=
\mathrm{Z}/10\mathrm{Z}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore the finite window cannot distinguish an absolute clock origin.  The role of the clock-coupling certificate is to supply an external map
$$
\begin{aligned}
\iota:R_6\to \mathrm{Z}/10\mathrm{Z}
\end{aligned}
$$
and to certify that the three response sites $q_0,q_1,q_2$ are read on the same ten-step clock as the residues $7,17,27$.  This is exactly the point at which the clock-gated expression
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_6^{*}=D_0+\phi^{-7}Q_{6,\alpha}(\phi^{-10})
\end{aligned}
$$
becomes a candidate for a physical readout rather than only a finite symbolic display.

\paragraph{Coefficient-use boundary.}
The finite window supplies two coefficient witnesses:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{\mathrm{alt}}&=-\frac{1}{2},\\
p_{\mathrm{esc}}&=\frac{|dU_R|}{6|U_R|}=\frac{32}{36}=\frac{8}{9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first is the alternating Jordan-mode eigenvalue of the displayed operator $A_0$, and the second is the right-boundary escape ratio of the finite edge ledger.  These facts do not by themselves prove that the residual response polynomial must use those values as coefficients.  The coefficient-use certificate is the separate map
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{CoefficientUseCert}:
\left(1,\lambda_{\mathrm{alt}},p_{\mathrm{esc}}\right)
\longmapsto
\left(1,-\frac{1}{2},\frac{8}{9}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
as coefficients of $Q_{6,\alpha}$.  This boundary prevents the boundary-escape witness from being read as a physical coefficient theorem before the coefficient-use certificate is supplied.

\paragraph{Physical readout certificate.}
The observable shell gives the mathematical readout
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_6(D)
=
2\pi
\frac{21+34C_6(D)}
{21\phi^{-6}+34C_6(D)\phi^{-7}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
It also admits the order-parameter form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha
=
\frac{P_6\phi^{-6}+P_7\phi^{-7}}{2\pi}
=
\frac{\phi^{-5}+m\phi^{-8}}{4\pi}
\end{aligned}
$$
after a representation choice.  The physical readout certificate is the assertion that this mathematical scalar is the inverse fine-structure readout for the selected representation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{PhysReadoutCert}_{\alpha}:
\frac{\langle r\rangle}{2\pi}\longmapsto \alpha .
\end{aligned}
$$
Without this certificate the observable shell determines only a scalar and an effective index; it does not determine a physical constant.

\paragraph{Experiment path ledger.}
For a low-energy non-running readout path $E$, the inverse ledger has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_E
=
D_{Z6}
+N_E\phi^{-27}
+\mu_E\phi^{-37}
+\cdots .
\end{aligned}
$$
The path certificate records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{ExperimentReadoutCert}(E)
=
\left(N_E,\mu_E,\gamma_E,\mathrm{ReadoutType}_E\right),
\end{aligned}
$$
with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{ReadoutType}_E\in
\{&
\mathrm{StaticImpedance},
\mathrm{MagneticMoment},
\mathrm{RecoilPhasePath},\\
&\mathrm{RunningAlpha},
\mathrm{GreenSusceptibility}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the static-impedance path the defining certificate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_E=D_{Z6},
\end{aligned}
$$
hence $N_E=0$.  Magnetic-moment, recoil, running, and Green-susceptibility paths are different readout ledgers and need not share the same $N_E$.

\paragraph{Green scale separation.}
The right-boundary Green row supplies
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_R(1)=\frac{26401}{2^{13}\cdot 571}.
\end{aligned}
$$
It belongs to the $\phi^{-37}$ layer:
$$
\begin{aligned}
D(1)-D_{Z6}=\Delta_R(1)\phi^{-37}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The unit $\phi^{-37}$ scale produces an inverse-readout displacement of order $10^{-11}$, while the full coefficient $\Delta_R(1)\phi^{-37}$ produces a still smaller displacement of order $10^{-13}$.  Therefore a recoil-scale $\phi^{-27}$ displacement is not a Green-layer displacement.  A genuine Green-susceptibility claim must carry the $571$ certificate and must be tested at the corresponding scale.

\paragraph{Boundary of the theorem.}
The theorem proves a conditional completion pattern:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{FiniteWitness}_{W6}\\
&+\mathrm{ClockCouplingCert}\\
&+\mathrm{ResponseFunctorCert}\\
&+\mathrm{CoefficientUseCert}\\
&+\mathrm{PhysReadoutCert}_{\alpha}\\
&+\mathrm{MetrologicalGaugeSelectionCert}\\
&\Rightarrow
\alpha_{Z6}^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
$$
It does not prove $\mathrm{Window6}\Rightarrow\alpha$.  It does not claim that $8/9$ is a response coefficient before $\mathrm{CoefficientUseCert}$ is supplied.  It does not interpret a recoil $\phi^{-27}$ offset as a Green $\phi^{-37}$ effect.  The finite window supplies the skeleton; the certificates complete the physical readout.
