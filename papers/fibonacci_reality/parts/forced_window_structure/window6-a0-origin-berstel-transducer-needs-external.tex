\subsection{Fold-gauge operator from a four-state mismatch transducer}
\label{sec:window6-a0-origin-berstel-transducer-needs-external}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite carrier consists of four named states
$\{a,b,c,d\}$ together with a directed multigraph whose edges are marked by a binary predicate called mismatch.  The directed marked edge relation is exactly the following finite table: there is one match edge from $a$ to $a$, one mismatch edge from $a$ to $b$, one match edge from $a$ to $d$, one mismatch edge from $b$ to $c$, one mismatch edge from $c$ to $a$, two match edges from $c$ to $b$, and one match edge from $d$ to $a$.  Thus the only multiplicity greater than one is the pair of match edges $c\to b$.  The parameter $u=e^\theta$ records mismatch weight: a match edge has weight $1$, while a mismatch edge has weight $u$.

\paragraph{Weighted edge operator.}
For states $i,j\in\{a,b,c,d\}$, define the matrix entry of $A_\theta$ by the exact finite sum
$$(
\begin{aligned}
(A_\theta)_{ij}
&=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{e:i\to j} u^{\mathbf{1}_{\mathrm{mismatch}}(e)} .
\end{aligned}
)$$
Equivalently, ordering the states as $a,b,c,d$, the edge table gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_\theta
&=\frac{1}{2}
\left[
\begin{array}{cccc}
1 & u & 0 & 1\\
0 & 0 & u & 0\\
u & 2 & 0 & 0\\
1 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}
\right].
\end{aligned}
$$
The entries are read directly from the finite table: the row $a$ records $a\to a$, $a\to b$, and $a\to d$; the row $b$ records $b\to c$; the row $c$ records $c\to a$ and the two parallel match edges $c\to b$; and the row $d$ records $d\to a$.  No limiting process or numerical fitting is involved in this construction.

\paragraph{The fold-gauge specialization.}
The fold-gauge operator is the specialization at $\theta=0$, equivalently $u=1$.  Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_0=A_{\theta=0}
&=\frac{1}{2}
\left[
\begin{array}{cccc}
1 & 1 & 0 & 1\\
0 & 0 & 1 & 0\\
1 & 2 & 0 & 0\\
1 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}
\right]\\
&=
\left[
\begin{array}{cccc}
1/2 & 1/2 & 0 & 1/2\\
0 & 0 & 1/2 & 0\\
1/2 & 1 & 0 & 0\\
1/2 & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}
\right].
\end{aligned}
$$
This is an exact rational matrix obtained by substituting $u=1$ into the symbolic mismatch-weighted family.  The construction certifies that the displayed $A_0$ is the zero-tilt member of the stated four-state transducer family.

\paragraph{Spectral certificate.}
Exact rational linear algebra gives the characteristic polynomial
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(\lambda I-A_0)
&=\frac{(\lambda-1)(2\lambda-1)(2\lambda+1)^2}{8}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence the spectrum is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{spec}(A_0)&=\{1,1/2,-1/2\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with algebraic multiplicity $2$ at $-1/2$ and algebraic multiplicity $1$ at each of $1$ and $1/2$.  The defect at the repeated eigenvalue is also finite and exact: one checks
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}\left(A_0+\frac{1}{2}I\right)&=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since the ambient space has dimension $4$, the eigenspace for $-1/2$ has dimension $1$.  Together with the algebraic multiplicity $2$, this proves that the $-1/2$ generalized eigenspace consists of one Jordan block of size $2$.

\paragraph{Window6 origin verdict.}
The construction above is a forward-verifiable reconstruction of $A_\theta$ and $A_0$ from the stated four-state marked edge table.  Its origin status is nevertheless external to the Window6 internal data.  The transducer and its marked edge table are taken as a paper-sourced finite structure; they are not uniquely forced by $\{0,1\}^6$, by the four-cell partition with sizes $(27,22,9,6)$, by Foldbin fibers, or by the directed edges of $Q_6$.  The correct obligation status is therefore needs-external and protocol-gated: once the four-state transducer is supplied, the matrix family and the spectral facts are exact, but the transducer itself is not derived from the Window6 data alone.

\paragraph{Separation from the edge-matrix algebra question.}
This origin packet answers a different question from non-membership in a separately specified edge-matrix algebra. The two conclusions are compatible: exact reconstruction from the transducer does not imply Window6-internal forcedness.
