\subsection{Forced Window: Window Six Alpha Completion External Readout Theorem}
\label{sec:window6-alpha-certificate-completion-external-readout-theorem}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Certificate-relative statement.}
The Window6 alpha route is represented as a certificate-completion theorem. The finite recurrence and Binet data supply an algebraic witness, while the clock, response, coefficient-use, physical-calibration, and metrological branch data are external certificates.  The target statement is therefore the forward implication
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{FiniteWitness}_{W6}+\mathrm{ClockCouplingCert}+\mathrm{ResponseFunctorCert}
+\mathrm{CoefficientUseCert}\\
&\quad+\mathrm{PhysReadoutCert}_{\alpha}+\mathrm{MetrologicalGaugeSelectionCert}
+\mathrm{StableAlphaReadoutCompose}_{W6}\\
&\quad+\mathrm{CertRelNoConverse}_{W6,\alpha}
\Longrightarrow
\mathrm{Readout}_{\alpha}(\Gamma_{\alpha})=\{\alpha^{-1}_{Z6}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Gamma_{\alpha}$ denotes the supplied certificate stack, and the readout value is the certificate-relative singleton $\alpha^{-1}_{Z6}=137.035999177006279\ldots$.  The implication is not a rule for dropping $\Gamma_{\alpha}$ and is not a reconstruction theorem for the certificates from the displayed number.

\paragraph{Algebraic carrier.}
The minimal carrier $A_{W6}$ contains only the finite Window6 arithmetic: $D_0=47$, $s_6=7$, $\rho_6=10$, the polynomial $Q_{6,\alpha}(u)=1-\frac{1}{2}u+\frac{8}{9}u^2$, and the displayed Binet element
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_6^{*}=47+\varphi^{-7}-\frac{1}{2}\varphi^{-17}+\frac{8}{9}\varphi^{-27}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same carrier records the exponent-shift family
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_6^{(a)}=47+\varphi^{-(7+a)}-\frac{1}{2}\varphi^{-(17+a)}+\frac{8}{9}\varphi^{-(27+a)},
\qquad a\in \mathrm{Z}/10\mathrm{Z}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residue clock modulo $10$ records that the exponents are in one ten-step grading.  It does not choose an integer lift, a real embedding, a numerical branch, or a physical clock origin.  Those choices enter only through the external certificate layer.

\paragraph{Branch selection and readout margin.}
A branch-selection certificate supplies a real embedding and an admissible exponent-shift representative.  For a readout interval $J$, the stable margin condition is local rather than global: the selected branch value lies in $J$, and every competitor stays at positive distance from $J$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
y_b\in J,\qquad
\delta_J=\min_{a\neq b}\operatorname{dist}(y_a,J)>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This $\mathrm{ReadoutMargin}_{W6}(J)$ condition is enough for the certified window to be single-valued at the readout layer.  It does not say that the finite recurrence internally chooses the branch; it says that the external metrological readout excludes the competing branches within the declared interval.

\paragraph{Coefficient-use compatibility.}
The coefficient witnesses are separated from the coefficient theorem.  The value $q_1=-1/2$ is witnessed by the alternating Jordan mode, and the value $q_2=8/9$ is witnessed by the right-boundary escape count.  A $\mathrm{CoefficientUseCert}$ is still required before these witnesses may be used as coefficients of the response polynomial.  The compatibility condition
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{CoeffUseMarginCompat}_{W6}
\end{aligned}
$$
asserts that coefficient admissibility does not enlarge, shift, or rebranch the singleton already selected by the readout margin.  Without this condition, a branch-unique readout could still become a larger coefficient fiber at the next layer.

\paragraph{Physical calibration and clock phase.}
The physical readout certificate is a calibration-preserving bridge from the selected response scale to the external alpha scale.  It is not implied by coefficient uniqueness: a calibration map unconstrained by $\mathrm{PhysReadoutCert}_{\alpha}$ could send the same selected coefficient to another target value.  The clock-coupling certificate and the metrological branch-selection certificate must also preserve phase.  The clock phase used to name the exponent representative must match the phase used by the external alpha readout, unless a separate phase-insensitivity certificate is supplied.

\paragraph{Stable composition.}
The completed theorem composes the certificates as a singleton-pullback argument.  Each stage carries both a selected singleton and an exclusion margin for the competitors that could otherwise enter the final alpha band. The role of $\mathrm{StableAlphaReadoutCompose}_{W6}$ is to preserve that singleton through branch selection, coefficient interpretation, calibration, and clock-phase transport.
$$
\begin{aligned}
B_{\mathrm{alg}} \longleftarrow B_{\mathrm{branch}}
\longleftarrow B_{\mathrm{coeff}}
\longleftarrow B_{\mathrm{phys}}
\longleftarrow \{\alpha^{-1}_{Z6}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If the final fiber is not a singleton, the excess-fiber diagnostic assigns the failure to the first external compatibility square that does not factor through the preceding equality.  Algebraic equality inside $A_{W6}$ is then treated as algebraic equality or label redundancy, not as a failure of the Window6 recurrence identity.

\paragraph{Experiment path ledger.}
Experiment paths are recorded by a ledger rather than folded into the finite witness.  For a low-energy non-running readout one may write
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_E=D_{Z6}+N_E\varphi^{-27}+\mu_E\varphi^{-37}+\cdots.
\end{aligned}
$$
The static-impedance path has $N_E=0$ by definition of the certificate target. Magnetic-moment and recoil paths are separate readout ledgers, and their $\varphi^{-27}$-scale offsets are not identified with the Green layer.  The Green susceptibility scale is a $\varphi^{-37}$ layer and must carry its own $571$ certificate.

The local inventory for this theorem is \texttt{FiniteWitness\_W6}, \texttt{ClockCouplingCert}, \texttt{ResponseFunctorCert}, \texttt{CoefficientUseCert}, \texttt{PhysReadoutCert\_alpha}, \texttt{MetrologicalGaugeSelectionCert}, \texttt{StableAlphaReadoutCompose\_W6}, \texttt{CertRelNoConverse\_W6\_alpha}.

\paragraph{Boundary of the result.}
The chapter does not claim that the Window6 recurrence identity alone implies $\alpha^{-1}_{Z6}$; it does not claim that the $8/9$ boundary-escape witness is a response coefficient theorem without $\mathrm{CoefficientUseCert}$; it does not identify $\varphi^{-27}$ recoil-scale terms with Green $\varphi^{-37}$ or $571$-certificate effects; and it does not authorize BEDC writeback.
