\subsection{Forced-window certificate completion for the six-step alpha route}
\label{sec:window6-alpha-certificate-completion-external-readout-theorem}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
Fix the six-position binary window
$W=\{0,1\}^6$.  Its elements are words $w=(w_0,w_1,w_2,w_3,w_4,w_5)$ with each $w_i\in\{0,1\}$, hence $|W|=2^6=64$.  The finite construction used in this subsection is not a physical reading of $W$; it is a closed combinatorial package whose data can be inspected without invoking any external measurement convention.  A six-window witness is a finite record
$$
\begin{aligned}
F=(W,E,P,D_0,s_6,\rho_6,Q,D_6^\ast),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $E\subseteq W\times W$ is a finite directed relation, $P$ is a finite partition of $W$, $D_0$ is an integer base level, $s_6$ is an integer shift, $\rho_6$ is an integer response index, $Q$ is a rational polynomial of degree at most two, and $D_6^\ast$ is the scalar assembled from these entries.  The concrete numerical slots carried by the witness are
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_0&=47,\\
s_6&=7,\\
\rho_6&=10,\\
Q(u)&=1-\frac{1}{2}u+\frac{8}{9}u^2,\\
D_6^\ast&=47+\varphi^{-7}-\frac{1}{2}\varphi^{-17}+\frac{8}{9}\varphi^{-27}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\varphi$ is used only as a formal positive scale parameter inside the displayed finite expression.  The powers $\varphi^{-7}$, $\varphi^{-17}$, and $\varphi^{-27}$ record the three displayed scale positions of the witness; no physical scale law is inferred from their appearance.

\paragraph{Predicates and gated conclusion.}
Let $\operatorname{FiniteWitness}_{6}(F)$ mean that the displayed finite record is present with $W=\{0,1\}^6$, with finite $E\subseteq W\times W$, with $P$ a partition of $W$, and with the stated numerical entries.  Let $\operatorname{ClockCouplingCert}(F)$, $\operatorname{ResponseFunctorCert}(F)$, $\operatorname{CoefficientUseCert}(F)$, $\operatorname{PhysReadoutCert}_{\alpha}(F)$, and $\operatorname{MetrologicalGaugeSelectionCert}(F)$ be separate predicates.  They are not hidden consequences of $\operatorname{FiniteWitness}_{6}(F)$; each names an additional finite certificate boundary that must be supplied before the terminal reading is licensed.  The conditional target of this subsection is therefore the implication
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname{FiniteWitness}_{6}(F)\\
&\quad{}\wedge\operatorname{ClockCouplingCert}(F)\\
&\quad{}\wedge\operatorname{ResponseFunctorCert}(F)\\
&\quad{}\wedge\operatorname{CoefficientUseCert}(F)\\
&\quad{}\wedge\operatorname{PhysReadoutCert}_{\alpha}(F)\\
&\quad{}\wedge\operatorname{MetrologicalGaugeSelectionCert}(F)\\
&\Longrightarrow \alpha_{\mathbb Z_6}^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a completion statement for certificates.  It is not a derivation of $\alpha_{\mathbb Z_6}^{-1}$ from the six-window recurrence data alone.  In particular, the symbol $\alpha_{\mathbb Z_6}^{-1}$ is treated as the named endpoint of the certified readout, not as a value obtained merely by evaluating $D_6^\ast$.

\paragraph{Relations, partitions, and kernels.}
The finite relation $E$ is the directed adjacency table of the witness.  Since $W$ has $64$ elements, the ambient relation space $W\times W$ has $4096$ possible ordered pairs, and any admissible $E$ is a subset of this finite table.  The partition $P=\{P_1,\ldots,P_m\}$ is required to satisfy
$$
\begin{aligned}
&P_i\subseteq W,\\
&P_i\cap P_j=\emptyset\quad\text{for }i\ne j,\\
&\bigcup_{i=1}^{m}P_i=W.
\end{aligned}
$$
From $E$ one obtains the $64\times64$ incidence matrix $A_E$ by indexing rows and columns by elements of $W$ and setting
$$
\begin{aligned}
(A_E)_{x,y}=\begin{cases}1,&(x,y)\in E,\\0,&(x,y)\notin E.\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$
The response kernel associated with the displayed polynomial is the finite matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
K_E=I-\frac{1}{2}A_E+\frac{8}{9}A_E^2,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $I$ is the $64\times64$ identity matrix.  This formula is only the internal use of the rational coefficients $1$, $-1/2$, and $8/9$.  The assertion that these coefficients may be used as response coefficients for a physical reading is exactly what $\operatorname{CoefficientUseCert}(F)$ must provide.

\paragraph{Witness extraction.}
The extraction map from a completed witness records only finite information.  It sends $F$ to the tuple
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Extract}(F)=(64,4096,D_0,s_6,\rho_6,Q,D_6^\ast,A_E,K_E,P).
\end{aligned}
$$
Substituting the displayed entries gives the exact scalar identity
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_6^\ast=D_0+\varphi^{-s_6}-\frac{1}{2}\varphi^{-(s_6+\rho_6)}+\frac{8}{9}\varphi^{-(s_6+2\rho_6)},
\end{aligned}
$$
which, because $D_0=47$, $s_6=7$, and $\rho_6=10$, is precisely
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_6^\ast=47+\varphi^{-7}-\frac{1}{2}\varphi^{-17}+\frac{8}{9}\varphi^{-27}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The monotone inversion work item concerns the finite function $R_6(D)$ attached to this same witness: one must show, on its stated domain, that the relevant branch is ordered strongly enough for inversion to be unambiguous.  The exponent-gauge work item concerns separating the $7$, $17$, and $27$ exponent positions so that a formal branch choice cannot be silently exchanged for a metrological convention.

\paragraph{Boundary tasks.}
The clock-coupling boundary asks for an explicit finite certificate connecting the six-window clock index to the response record.  The coefficient-use boundary asks for a certificate that the displayed $8/9$ boundary-escape entry is allowed to function as a response coefficient, rather than merely as a rational number in a polynomial.  The physical-readout boundary asks for the separate certificate that turns the completed finite response record into an $\alpha$-indexed statement.  The metrological-gauge boundary asks for the selection rule that fixes the comparison scale.  The experiment-path ledger records which finite entries are used before any comparison with measurement language is attempted.  The Green-scale separation task records that terms at scale $\varphi^{-27}$ are not, by this witness alone, reclassified as Green-scale $\varphi^{-37}$ terms or as effects belonging to a separate $571$-certificate calculation.

\paragraph{Local non-consequences.}
No physical-constant identification follows from $\operatorname{FiniteWitness}_{6}(F)$ alone.  No global flow law follows from the recurrence identity alone.  No external dependency is produced by the witness unless a named certificate supplies it.  No metrological comparison follows without $\operatorname{MetrologicalGaugeSelectionCert}(F)$.  No interpretation beyond the finite construction follows without the separate predicates listed in the conditional implication.  In particular, the six-window recurrence identity by itself does not imply $\alpha_{\mathbb Z_6}^{-1}$; the $8/9$ boundary-escape entry is not a response-coefficient theorem without $\operatorname{CoefficientUseCert}(F)$; the $\varphi^{-27}$ recoil-scale term is not identified with Green $\varphi^{-37}$ behavior or with a $571$-certificate effect; and no writeback authority is granted by this packet.
