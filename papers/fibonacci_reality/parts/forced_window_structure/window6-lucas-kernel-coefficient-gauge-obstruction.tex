\subsection{A finite Lucas kernel on the golden companion window}
\label{sec:window6-lucas-kernel-coefficient-gauge-obstruction}

\paragraph{Carrier.}
The carrier is the integer companion matrix
$M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ for the polynomial $x^2-x-1$.  All arithmetic in this subsection is carried out inside the integer matrix algebra generated by $M$.  No limiting argument, numerical approximation, or external measurement is used.  The finite window consists of the three exponent positions
$\{7,17,27\}$ together with the three coefficient coordinates attached to those positions.  A coefficient assignment is therefore an integer triple $(a,b,c)$, interpreted as the matrix expression
$$
\begin{aligned}
W(a,b,c)=aM^{27}+bM^{17}+cM^7.
\end{aligned}
$$
The only predicate used here is exact vanishing: a triple belongs to the kernel predicate for this window when $W(a,b,c)$ is the zero $2\times 2$ integer matrix.

\paragraph{Lucas input.}
The scalar $123$ is not fitted from data.  It is obtained from the Lucas recurrence
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_0&=2,\\
L_1&=1,\\
L_{n+2}&=L_{n+1}+L_n,
\end{aligned}
$$
which gives the exact value $L_{10}=123$.  The matrix powers used in the certificate are obtained by direct integer powering of $M$.  In particular,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{10}&=\begin{pmatrix}89&55\\55&34\end{pmatrix},\\
M^{20}&=\begin{pmatrix}10946&6765\\6765&4181\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These displayed matrices give an entirely finite check of the base relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{20}-123M^{10}+I=0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $I$ is the $2\times 2$ identity matrix.

\paragraph{Forced window relation.}
Multiplying the base relation on the left by $M^7$ gives the exact shifted identity
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{27}-123M^{17}+M^7=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the integer triple
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1,-123,1)
\end{aligned}
$$
is a kernel vector for the three selected exponent positions.  Equivalently, if the three coordinates are ordered as $27,17,7$, then
$$
\begin{aligned}
W(1,-123,1)&=M^{27}-123M^{17}+M^7\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
If the coordinates are instead named by the ascending slots $7,17,27$, the same relation is the same one-dimensional direction with the same entries attached to the corresponding three terms.  The content is the exact annihilation of those three matrix powers by the Lucas value $L_{10}=123$.

\paragraph{Partition of coefficient triples.}
The finite arithmetic relation induces an equivalence relation on integer coefficient triples.  For triples $u=(a,b,c)$ and $v=(a',b',c')$ in $\mathbf{Z}^3$, declare
$$
\begin{aligned}
u\sim v
\end{aligned}
$$
when there is an integer $t$ such that
$$
\begin{aligned}
(a,b,c)-(a',b',c')=t(1,-123,1).
\end{aligned}
$$
This partitions $\mathbf{Z}^3$ into cosets of the integer span of $(1,-123,1)$.  The certificate therefore does not single out one preferred representative of a coefficient triple inside such a coset.  It proves that the displayed direction is invisible to the matrix expression on the selected three slots, because adding $t(1,-123,1)$ changes $W(a,b,c)$ by
$$
\begin{aligned}
t(M^{27}-123M^{17}+M^7)=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite construction consequently supplies a gauge direction for the three-slot coefficient window, not a unique coefficient normalization.

\paragraph{Witness extraction.}
The witness consists of four pieces of finite data: the matrix $M$, the Lucas recurrence computation giving $L_{10}=123$, the directly computed matrix powers $M^{10}$ and $M^{20}$, and the multiplication of the base identity by $M^7$.  From these data one extracts the triple $(1,-123,1)$ and verifies its vanishing by integer matrix arithmetic alone.  The verification does not require identifying an eigenvalue, introducing a real-number approximation, or comparing with any measured quantity.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This subsection proves only the finite matrix statement just described.  It does not identify any physical constant, does not recover the coefficients from a numerical value, does not compare the construction with metrological data, and does not assert a global law beyond the three exponent slots $7,17,27$.  It also does not provide a bridge from this integer matrix certificate to any physical representation.  Such a bridge would have to be supplied as a separate certificate before any interpretation outside the finite construction could be claimed.
