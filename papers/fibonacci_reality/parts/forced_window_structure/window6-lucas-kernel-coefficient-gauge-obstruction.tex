\subsection{Lucas kernel obstruction for the forced window}
\label{sec:window-lucas-kernel-coefficient-gauge-obstruction}

\paragraph{Carrier.}
The finite carrier is the integer companion matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$
It represents multiplication by the class of $x$ in the rank-two integer algebra $\mathbb{Z}[x]/(x^2-x-1)$, because the relation $x^2=x+1$ is exactly the recurrence encoded by the two columns of $M$. The Lucas values used in this packet are fixed from first principles by $L_0=2$, $L_1=1$, and $L_{n+2}=L_{n+1}+L_n$. Direct recurrence evaluation gives the exact integer $L_{10}=123$. The finite window under study is not a continuum or a measured signal; it is the three-element exponent set
$$
\begin{aligned}
S=\{7,17,27\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coefficient module attached to this window is $\mathbb{Z}^3$, with a triple $(a,b,c)$ read as the integer matrix combination
$$
\begin{aligned}
(a,b,c)\longmapsto aM^7+bM^{17}+cM^{27}.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Predicate and finite relation.}
Define the annihilation predicate $A(a,b,c)$ on $\mathbb{Z}^3$ by
$$
\begin{aligned}
A(a,b,c)\quad\text{if and only if}\quad aM^7+bM^{17}+cM^{27}=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
All matrix powers in this predicate are ordinary integer powers of the displayed $2\times 2$ matrix. The certificate uses the directly computed identities
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{10}&=\begin{pmatrix}89&55\\55&34\end{pmatrix},\\
M^{20}&=\begin{pmatrix}10946&6765\\6765&4181\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$
From these exact matrices one verifies entry by entry that
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{20}-123M^{10}+I=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Multiplication on the left by $M^7$ gives the finite-window identity
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{27}-123M^{17}+M^7=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the triple
$$
\begin{aligned}
k=(1,-123,1)
\end{aligned}
$$
satisfies $A(1,-123,1)$. This is the Lucas-kernel direction for the forced window $\{7,17,27\}$: the middle coefficient is exactly $-L_{10}$, and the outer coefficients are both $1$.

\paragraph{Partition of coefficient space.}
The kernel direction determines an equivalence relation on coefficient triples. For $(a,b,c),(a',b',c')\in\mathbb{Z}^3$, set
$$
\begin{aligned}
(a,b,c)\sim(a',b',c')
\end{aligned}
$$
when there is an integer $t$ such that
$$
\begin{aligned}
(a-a',b-b',c-c')=t(1,-123,1).
\end{aligned}
$$
This partitions $\mathbb{Z}^3$ into cosets of the rank-one subgroup $\mathbb{Z}(1,-123,1)$. The finite certificate therefore distinguishes a relation in the three exponent positions but does not choose a unique representative of a coefficient class. If one triple is used as a representative, then every triple
$$
\begin{aligned}
(a+t,b-123t,c+t)
\end{aligned}
$$
for $t\in\mathbb{Z}$ lies in the same coefficient class for the purpose of this window. The obstruction is exactly this gauge freedom: the arithmetic relation fixes the direction annihilating the slots, while any attempted coefficient selection must supply additional information before it can prefer one representative over another.

\paragraph{Witness extraction.}
The witness is extracted by finite integer arithmetic only. First compute $L_{10}=123$ from the displayed Lucas recurrence. Next compute $M^{10}$ and $M^{20}$ by repeated multiplication of $M$ over $\mathbb{Z}$. The equality $M^{20}-123M^{10}+I=0$ is then an equality of four integer entries. Finally multiply by $M^7$, again inside integer matrices, to obtain $M^{27}-123M^{17}+M^7=0$. Reading the coefficients in the ordered exponent list $(7,17,27)$ gives $(1,-123,1)$. No limiting argument, approximation, real embedding, or physical measurement is used.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This subsection proves only a finite companion-matrix relation and the induced coefficient-class partition for the three forced exponents. It does not identify any physical constant, does not infer the coefficient $123$ from numerical proximity, does not impose a global dynamical law, and does not compare the construction with metrological data. A separate representation certificate would be needed before this arithmetic packet could be connected to any physical interpretation.
