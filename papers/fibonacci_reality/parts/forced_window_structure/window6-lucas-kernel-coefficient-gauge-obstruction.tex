\subsection{Forced Lucas Kernel for the Slots $7,17,27$}
\label{sec:window6-lucas-kernel-coefficient-gauge-obstruction}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The construction uses only integer arithmetic in the quadratic companion-matrix carrier. Let
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the companion matrix for the polynomial $x^2-x-1$. The finite data of the packet are the three exponent positions $7,17,27$, the Lucas integer $L_{10}$, and the integer powers of $M$ needed to test the corresponding relation. The Lucas sequence is fixed from first principles by
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_0&=2,\\
L_1&=1,\\
L_{n+2}&=L_{n+1}+L_n.
\end{aligned}
$$
Direct recurrence evaluation gives $L_{10}=123$. No limiting argument, approximation, real embedding, or interpretation of the eigenvalues is used in this arithmetic certificate.

\paragraph{Predicate and slot partition.}
For an ordered coefficient triple $(a,b,c)$ attached respectively to the exponent slots $(27,17,7)$, define the finite annihilation predicate
$$
\begin{aligned}
A(a,b,c) \quad\text{to mean}\quad aM^{27}+bM^{17}+cM^7=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The only partition used here is the separation between the active three-slot block $\{7,17,27\}$ and all other exponents, which play no role in the certificate. Inside that active block, the coefficient direction is the integer triple
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1,-123,1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the claimed finite test is exactly $A(1,-123,1)$.

\paragraph{Integer matrix witnesses.}
The powers are obtained by direct integer matrix multiplication. The certified values needed for the base relation include
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{10}&=\begin{pmatrix}89&55\\55&34\end{pmatrix},\\
M^{20}&=\begin{pmatrix}10946&6765\\6765&4181\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$
With the identity matrix $I$, direct subtraction gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{20}-123M^{10}+I&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the same as the polynomial relation $x^{20}-123x^{10}+1=0$ after evaluating $x$ at the matrix $M$ in the quotient determined by $x^2-x-1$.

\paragraph{Forced relation at the selected slots.}
Multiplying the displayed base identity on the right, equivalently within this matrix carrier, by $M^7$ yields the three-slot identity
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{27}-123M^{17}+M^7&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore the predicate $A(1,-123,1)$ holds. The witness is finite and inspectable: the integer $123$ is produced by the Lucas recurrence, the matrices $M^7$, $M^{17}$, and $M^{27}$ are obtained by ordinary integer matrix powering, and the four entries of the resulting matrix sum are all zero.

\paragraph{Coefficient gauge.}
The same calculation also fixes the local ambiguity of coefficient descriptions on the active block. Any coefficient triple assigned to the slots $7,17,27$ is determined only modulo the integer span of the direction $(1,-123,1)$ when the relation is read through this three-slot annihilation test. In other words, adding an integer multiple of $(1,-123,1)$ changes the displayed slot coefficients while preserving the finite matrix equality tested by $A$. This is a coefficient-gauge obstruction, not a measurement claim and not a selection rule for a preferred representative.

\paragraph{Boundary of the construction.}
The certificate proves only the finite companion-matrix relation just displayed. It does not identify $123$, the coefficient vector, or the matrix spectrum with any physical constant. It does not infer coefficients from numerical proximity, metrological data, or an external comparison. It also does not choose a unique coefficient representative in the quotient by the Lucas-kernel direction. No global flow law, no physical representation map, no external dependency, and no interpretation beyond this finite arithmetic construction follows without a separate certificate supplying that bridge.
