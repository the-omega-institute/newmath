\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The carrier is the integer companion matrix
$M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ for the polynomial $x^2-x-1$.  All arithmetic in this packet takes place in the finite integer matrix family generated by powers of $M$, together with the three selected exponent slots $7$, $17$, and $27$.  The scalar $123$ is not introduced by approximation: it is the tenth Lucas value, computed from the recurrence $L_0=2$, $L_1=1$, and $L_{n+2}=L_{n+1}+L_n$, so that $L_{10}=123$.  The finite predicate attached to a triple $(a,b,c)\in \mathbb{Z}^3$ is that it annihilates the ordered slot triple $(M^7,M^{17},M^{27})$, namely
$$\begin{aligned}
aM^7+bM^{17}+cM^{27}=0.
\end{aligned}$$
The associated coefficient direction is the one-dimensional integer span of $(1,-123,1)$ whenever this displayed equality is satisfied by that generator.

\paragraph{Direct matrix evidence.}
The required powers are obtained by ordinary integer matrix multiplication.  The two central computed powers are
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{10}&=\begin{pmatrix}89&55\\55&34\end{pmatrix},\\
M^{20}&=\begin{pmatrix}10946&6765\\6765&4181\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}$$
With $L_{10}=123$, these matrices satisfy the exact identity
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{20}-123M^{10}+I=0.
\end{aligned}$$
This is the finite base relation in the quotient presentation $\mathbb{Z}[x]/(x^2-x-1)$ as realized by the companion matrix $M$.  Multiplying the same identity on the left by $M^7$ gives the slot relation
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{27}-123M^{17}+M^7=0.
\end{aligned}$$
Equivalently, in the ordered slots $(7,17,27)$, the coefficient vector
$$\begin{aligned}
(1,-123,1)
\end{aligned}$$
is an integer kernel direction.  The witness is therefore finite and explicit: compute $L_{10}$ from the stated recurrence, compute $M^7$, $M^{10}$, $M^{17}$, $M^{20}$, and $M^{27}$ by integer powering, verify $M^{20}-123M^{10}+I=0$, and then multiply by $M^7$ to obtain the displayed annihilation of the three selected slots.

\paragraph{Partition and coefficient gauge.}
The construction separates three finite layers.  The carrier layer is the companion-matrix orbit of $M$.  The relation layer is the base identity $M^{20}-123M^{10}+I=0$ and its shifted instance $M^{27}-123M^{17}+M^7=0$.  The coefficient layer records triples assigned to the exponent slots $7$, $17$, and $27$.  On that last layer, the arithmetic does not choose a unique representative: any coefficient triple is determined only modulo the subgroup generated by $(1,-123,1)$.  Thus the packet proves a forced kernel direction, not a canonical normalization of all possible slot coefficients.  The obstruction is exactly that the finite relation makes the three-slot coefficient record insensitive to adding an integer multiple of
$$\begin{aligned}
(1,-123,1).
\end{aligned}$$
