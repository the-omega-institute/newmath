\subsection{Window6 fold-gauge edge-derivability no-go}
\label{subsec:window6-fold-gauge-a0-edge-derivability-nogo}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite carrier is the ordered four-cell quotient of the six-bit window set $\{0,1\}^6$ with cells named $U_2,U_1,U_L,U_R$ in that order.  The cell sizes are fixed as
$$
\begin{aligned}
|U_2|=27,\qquad |U_1|=22,\qquad |U_L|=9,\qquad |U_R|=6.
\end{aligned}
$$
On this ordered carrier there are three finite objects.  The first is the paper-sourced directed fold-gauge operator $A_0$.  The second is the forward-enumerated symmetric edge-count matrix $E$.  The third is the row-normalized coarse kernel $T$ obtained from $E$ and the four cell sizes.  All assertions below are assertions about these four named cells and the exact rational or integer matrices displayed here; no limiting, asymptotic, or continuum object is used.

\end{aligned}
$$
The edge-count matrix is

\paragraph{Directed and defective part of $A_0$.}
The operator $A_0$ is not normal.  Its normality defect is witnessed by the exact commutator with its transpose:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_0A_0^T-A_0^TA_0=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-3/4&3/4&0\\
-3/4&-1&0&-1/4\\
3/4&0&1&1/4\\
0&-1/4&1/4&0
\end{pmatrix},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is nonzero.  The same finite matrix also has a defective spectral point.  Its characteristic polynomial is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(\lambda I-A_0)=\frac{(\lambda-1)(2\lambda-1)(2\lambda+1)^2}{8}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the spectrum of $A_0$ is $\{1,1/2,-1/2\}$, with algebraic multiplicity $2$ at $-1/2$.  The exact rank computation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}\bigl(A_0+(1/2)I\bigr)=3
\end{aligned}
$$
shows that the $-1/2$ eigenspace has dimension $1$.  Consequently the $-1/2$ spectral point is represented by one Jordan block of size $2$.  This is the finite directed defect carried by $A_0$.

\paragraph{Symmetric edge algebra.}
The edge matrix satisfies $E=E^T$.  Therefore $E$ is symmetric, and every product power $E^k$ is symmetric.  In four dimensions, the algebra generated by one matrix is spanned by the four matrices $I,E,E^2,E^3$ after reducing higher powers to this finite span.  Hence every matrix in
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{span}\{I,E,E^2,E^3\}
\end{aligned}
$$
is symmetric.  This gives a finite obstruction to deriving the directed fold-gauge operator as a polynomial in the edge matrix.  Indeed,
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_0-A_0^T=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1/2&-1/2&0\\
-1/2&0&-1/2&0\\
1/2&1/2&0&0\\
0&0&0&0
\end{pmatrix},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is nonzero.  Since every polynomial expression in $E$ lies in the symmetric span above, while $A_0$ has a nonzero antisymmetric part, $A_0$ is not a polynomial in $E$.

\paragraph{Noncommuting witnesses.}
The failure is not merely a mismatch of preferred coordinates.  The commutator with the exact edge matrix is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_0E-EA_0=
\begin{pmatrix}
20&8&-6&0\\
-33&-42&-9&-28\\
61&39&22&4\\
0&15&8&0
\end{pmatrix},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is nonzero.  Thus $A_0$ is not simultaneously diagonalized with the symmetric edge matrix $E$.  The row-normalized coarse kernel also fails to commute with $A_0$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_0T-TA_0=
\begin{pmatrix}
2735/7128&145/1188&281/7128&-13/396\\
-38/297&-161/792&-145/1188&-145/792\\
307/891&67/297&127/3564&-377/3564\\
-79/324&-1/4&-1/81&-35/162
\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This nonzero matrix blocks the stronger possibility that $A_0$ is obtained as a commuting observable of the coarse Markov kernel.  The spectra also separate the objects: the spectrum of $A_0$ is distinct from the spectrum of $T$, and the real spectrum of $E$ is distinct from the spectrum of $A_0$.

\paragraph{Finite no-go statement.}
The finite construction proves a boundary statement.  The directed non-normal and defective structure of $A_0$ is present in the displayed rational operator.  The forward edge matrix $E$ is symmetric, and its polynomial algebra is symmetric in this four-cell representation.  The nonzero antisymmetric part of $A_0$, together with the nonzero commutators $A_0E-EA_0$ and $A_0T-TA_0$, shows that the directed fold-gauge structure is not derivable from $E$, from $T$, or from the Window6 partition alone.  Therefore $A_0$ must be supplied by an independent fold-gauge definition.  This paragraph certifies only that boundary: it does not provide the first-principles origin of $A_0$.
