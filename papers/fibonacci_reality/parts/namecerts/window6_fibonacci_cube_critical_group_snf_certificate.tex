The sanitized input supplies the mode and the descriptive slug, but it supplies no mathematical facts from which the chapter may cite exact finite data. \paragraph{Required finite data.}
To write the chapter as a self-contained mathematical record, the certificate must specify the carrier as an explicit finite set, for example a list of the admissible words in $\{0,1\}^6$; the finite relation must be given either as a complete edge list or as a rule plus an independently certified count; and the integer matrix must be fixed by an ordering of the carrier. Without those data, any exact group decomposition or determinant statement would be an unsupported reconstruction rather than a statement derived from the sanitized record.
