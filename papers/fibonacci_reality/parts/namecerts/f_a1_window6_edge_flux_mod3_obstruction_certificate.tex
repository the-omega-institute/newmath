\subsection{A forced-window obstruction from edge flux modulo three}
\label{sec:f-a1-window6-edge-flux-mod3-obstruction-certificate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
Fix a positive integer $n$. The finite state space is the vector space $\mathbb{F}_3^n$, where $\mathbb{F}_3=\{0,1,2\}$ with addition and multiplication reduced modulo $3$. Thus each state is an $n$-tuple $v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ with entries in $\{0,1,2\}$, and two integer representatives are identified whenever their coordinatewise differences are divisible by $3$. The carrier has exactly $3^n$ states.

\paragraph{Integer edge-count data.}
The finite relation is encoded by an $n$ by $n$ integer matrix $E\in \mathrm{Mat}_n(\mathbb{Z})$. Its only arithmetic use in this certificate is through the matrix obtained after reducing every entry modulo $3$. Write this reduced matrix as $\overline{E}\in \mathrm{Mat}_n(\mathbb{F}_3)$. The edge-flux output associated to a state $v\in \mathbb{F}_3^n$ is the vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_E(v) &= \overline{E}v \in \mathbb{F}_3^n.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite map from $\mathbb{F}_3^n$ to itself. No ordering, metric, limiting process, or ambient continuous space is part of the construction.

\paragraph{Singularity condition.}
Assume that $3$ divides the integer determinant of $E$. Reducing the determinant modulo $3$ gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(\overline{E}) &= 0 \quad \text{in } \mathbb{F}_3.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently $\overline{E}$ is singular over $\mathbb{F}_3$, equivalently
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{rank}(\overline{E}) &< n.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same rank defect may be expressed on the transpose side: the left nullspace of $\overline{E}$ contains a nonzero vector. Choose one such nonzero vector and denote the corresponding linear functional by
$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi : \mathbb{F}_3^n &\longrightarrow \mathbb{F}_3.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\chi$ is nonzero and satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi(\overline{E}x) &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for every $x\in \mathbb{F}_3^n$.

\paragraph{Forced-window predicate.}
The predicate detected by the certificate is the vanishing of $\chi$ on edge-flux outputs. Define
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(v) &\Longleftrightarrow \chi(\Phi_E(v))=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
By the left-kernel identity above, this predicate holds for every state of the finite carrier:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall v\in \mathbb{F}_3^n,\qquad P(v).
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, every edge-flux vector $\Phi_E(v)$ lies in the kernel of the nonzero functional $\chi$.

\paragraph{Partition induced by the certificate.}
Because $\chi$ is a nonzero linear functional on $\mathbb{F}_3^n$, it partitions the carrier into the three fibers
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_a &= \{v\in \mathbb{F}_3^n : \chi(v)=a\}, \qquad a\in \mathbb{F}_3.
\end{aligned}
$$
These three sets are pairwise disjoint and their union is all of $\mathbb{F}_3^n$. Since $\chi$ is nonzero, each fiber has exactly $3^{n-1}$ elements. The obstruction does not say that every original state lies in $C_0$; it says that every flux output lies in $C_0$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_E(\mathbb{F}_3^n) &\subseteq C_0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the forced window is the zero fiber of a nonzero linear detector, and the finite relation generated by $E$ cannot produce a flux output in either of the two nonzero fibers.

\paragraph{Witness extraction.}
A certificate is obtained by solving the homogeneous linear system
$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline{E}^{\mathsf{T}}w &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
inside $\mathbb{F}_3^n$ and choosing any nonzero solution $w$. This vector defines
$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi(x) &= w^{\mathsf{T}}x
\end{aligned}
$$
for $x\in \mathbb{F}_3^n$. The nonzero condition on $w$ guarantees that $\chi$ is not the zero functional, while the displayed homogeneous equation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\chi(\Phi_E(v))
  &= w^{\mathsf{T}}\overline{E}v \\
  &= (\overline{E}^{\mathsf{T}}w)^{\mathsf{T}}v \\
  &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for every $v\in \mathbb{F}_3^n$. This proves the advertised finite obstruction entirely within the reduced matrix system.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The certificate asserts only the finite modular implication from $3\mid \det(E)$ to the existence of a nonzero left-kernel detector for edge-flux outputs modulo $3$. It does not identify a physical constant, supply a global flow law, depend on an external interpretation, compare against metrological data, or claim any meaning beyond the finite construction unless a separate certificate supplies such additional content.
