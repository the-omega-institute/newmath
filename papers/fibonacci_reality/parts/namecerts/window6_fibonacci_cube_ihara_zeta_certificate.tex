\subsection{Window-six Fibonacci cube and finite Ihara determinant certificate}
\label{sec:window6-fibonacci-cube-ihara-zeta-certificate}

\paragraph{Carrier.}
The finite carrier is the set of binary words of length six with no adjacent occupied positions.  Write a word as $w=(w_1,w_2,w_3,w_4,w_5,w_6) \in \{0,1\}^6$.  The admissibility predicate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A(w) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad w_i w_{i+1}=0 \text{ for every } i \in \{1,2,3,4,5\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The vertex set is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
V=\{w \in \{0,1\}^6 : A(w)\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite graph relation is the symmetric Hamming-one relation restricted to $V$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
E=\{\{u,v\}: u,v \in V,\ u \ne v,\ \#\{i \in \{1,2,3,4,5,6\}:u_i \ne v_i\}=1\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This gives a completely finite six-window graph: every vertex is a permitted six-bit configuration, and every edge toggles exactly one bit while remaining inside the admissible carrier.

\paragraph{Finite partition and exact size.}
Partition $V$ by Hamming weight.  Let
$$
\begin{aligned}
V_k=\{w \in V : w_1+w_2+w_3+w_4+w_5+w_6=k\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The possible weights are $0,1,2,3$.  The exact counts are obtained by placing $k$ occupied sites with at least one empty site between consecutive occupied sites.  Equivalently, after removing the forced separating empty sites, one chooses $k$ positions from $7-k$ slots.  Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#V_0&=1,\\
\#V_1&=6,\\
\#V_2&=10,\\
\#V_3&=4.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#V=\#V_0+\#V_1+\#V_2+\#V_3=1+6+10+4=21.
\end{aligned}
$$
The edge count is also exact.  Each edge is uniquely oriented downward by changing one $1$ into $0$.  Therefore the number of edges is the total number of occupied positions among all admissible words:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#E=0\cdot 1+1\cdot 6+2\cdot 10+3\cdot 4=38.
\end{aligned}
$$
The graph has cyclomatic rank
$$
\begin{aligned}
r=\#E-\#V+1=38-21+1=18,
\end{aligned}
$$
because the all-zero word is connected to every vertex by repeatedly changing a $1$ into $0$.

\paragraph{Adjacency and degree kernel.}
Fix any ordering $V=\{v_1,\ldots,v_{21}\}$.  Define the $21 \times 21$ adjacency matrix $M$ by
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_{ij}=\begin{cases}
1, & \text{if } \{v_i,v_j\}\in E,\\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$
Define the diagonal degree matrix $D$ by
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{ij}=\begin{cases}
\#\{v \in V : \{v_i,v\}\in E\}, & \text{if } i=j,\\
0, & \text{if } i\ne j.
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite Ihara determinant kernel is
$$
\begin{aligned}
K(t)=I_{21}-tM+t^2(D-I_{21}),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $I_{21}$ is the $21 \times 21$ identity matrix.  This kernel is intrinsic: changing the ordering of $V$ conjugates $M$, $D$, and $K(t)$ by the same permutation matrix, so the determinant below is independent of the listing of the vertices.

\paragraph{Directed-edge transition.}
Let $\vec E$ be the set of directed edges.  Since $\#E=38$, one has $\#\vec E=76$.  A directed edge is written $(a,b)$ with $\{a,b\}\in E$.  Define the non-return transition matrix $B$ indexed by $\vec E$ as
$$
\begin{aligned}
B_{(a,b),(c,d)}=\begin{cases}
1, & \text{if } b=c \text{ and } d\ne a,\\
0, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$
This $76\times 76$ matrix records one-step continuation of a directed walk while forbidding immediate reversal.  The associated reciprocal finite zeta polynomial is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z(t)^{-1}=\det(I_{76}-tB).
\end{aligned}
$$
The same quantity is obtained from the smaller vertex kernel by the Bass determinant identity in this finite setting:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(I_{76}-tB)=(1-t^2)^{18-1}\det(I_{21}-tM+t^2(D-I_{21})).
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z(t)^{-1}=(1-t^2)^{17}\det(K(t)).
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the certificate identity for the six-window construction.  It is a finite algebraic statement about a specified graph and two explicitly defined matrices.

\paragraph{Witness extraction.}
A verifier can reconstruct the certificate without any outside data.  First enumerate all $64$ binary words in $\{0,1\}^6$.  Second retain exactly those satisfying $w_iw_{i+1}=0$ for all adjacent indices; this yields $21$ vertices.  Third connect two retained words precisely when they differ in one coordinate; this yields $38$ undirected edges and $76$ directed edges.  Fourth build $M$, $D$, and $B$ from the displayed rules.  Finally compute the two determinants and compare them after multiplying the vertex determinant by $(1-t^2)^{17}$.  Every coefficient in the comparison is an integer obtained from the finite row data just described.

No physical-constant identification, global flow law, external dependency, metrological comparison, or interpretation beyond this finite construction is implied by the certificate itself.
