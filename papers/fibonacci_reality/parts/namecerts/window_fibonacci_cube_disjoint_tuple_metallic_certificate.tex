\subsection{Insufficient finite data for a window certificate}\label{sec:window-fibonacci-cube-disjoint-tuple-metallic-certificate-insufficient-data}
\paragraph{Available record.} The supplied mathematical record contains no finite rows, no declared carrier, no predicate table, no relation table, no partition, no matrix or kernel, no exact count, no exact identity, and no witness-extraction rule. Because the requested chapter must state all objects from first principles and must not add material beyond the supplied finite data, the certificate cannot be written as a self-contained mathematical construction from the present record.
\paragraph{Required finite content.} To make the chapter writable, the record must provide the finite carrier, for example a specified subset of $\{0,1\}^n$ or another explicit finite set; the local predicates selecting admissible words; the disjointness or tuple relation as an explicit finite relation; the partition blocks or equivalence classes; the finite matrix, adjacency rule, kernel, or transfer operator whose entries are to be used; and the exact counts and algebraic identities to be proved. If a metallic number or spectral value is part of the intended construction, the record must give the matrix or recurrence and the exact polynomial or eigenvalue identity, while keeping any physical comparison outside the theorem statement.
\paragraph{Boundary.} No physical-constant identification, global flow law, external dependency, metrological comparison, or interpretation beyond an explicitly supplied finite construction follows from the empty record.
