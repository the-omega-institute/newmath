\paragraph{Status of the finite object.}
The supplied data identify the intended object as a name-certificate chapter for a window-six Fibonacci-cube matching enumerator, but they do not supply the finite rows from which the certificate can be reconstructed. A self-contained certificate of this kind must begin with an explicit carrier, for example a specified subset of $\{0,1\}^6$, and must state the admissibility predicate deciding membership in that subset. It must then give the finite relation whose matchings are to be enumerated, or an equivalent adjacency matrix, incidence matrix, transfer kernel, or edge list. Without those rows, there is no determinate finite structure to count.

\paragraph{Missing first-principles ingredients.}
The certificate cannot be completed from the present sanitized input because none of the following data are present: the carrier set, the local exclusion rule defining the Fibonacci-cube window, the finite matching relation, the partition of vertices or edges used by the enumerator, the recurrence or matrix product used to count matchings, the exact count returned by that enumerator, and the witness extraction rule connecting a counted object back to a concrete row of the finite structure. These are not cosmetic details. They determine whether the chapter proves a statement about one graph, another graph, or no graph at all.

\paragraph{What a sufficient finite certificate would contain.}
A ready certificate would state the carrier as an explicit finite set $C\subseteq\{0,1\}^6$, give a predicate $P(w)$ such that $w\in C$ exactly when $P(w)$ holds, and list or define a symmetric irreflexive relation $E\subseteq C\times C$. It would then define a matching as a subset $M\subseteq E$ whose edges have pairwise disjoint endpoints. If an enumerator is used, the text would define its finite states, its transition matrix, and its initial and terminal vectors. The central identity would have the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
N &= u A_1 A_2 \cdots A_k v,
\end{aligned}
$$
with every entry of every finite matrix fixed in the prose or in a displayed table. The proof would then show that the product counts exactly the matchings of $E$ and neither omits nor duplicates any matching.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No physical-constant identification, global flow law, external dependency, metrological comparison, or interpretation beyond the finite construction follows from the supplied data. In particular, any numerical resemblance to a physical parameter would require a separate certificate giving the finite comparison rule, the numerical tolerance, and the reason that the comparison is mathematically meaningful.
