\paragraph{Finite record.}
The record concerns the claim $\mathrm{AgeClockShiftUp}$ in the $\mathrm{age\_signature}$ layer for the frozen GSE142439 methylation-array comparison. The finite carrier is
$$
\begin{aligned}
B_R=(M,W,P,S_A,\Delta_A,C,L,N),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $M$ is the paired methylation beta matrix restricted to covered Horvath353 CpGs, $W$ is the Horvath353 coefficient table with its intercept, $P$ is the set of eight normal/treated donor pairs, $S_A$ is the Horvath DNAm age readback, $\Delta_A$ is the treated-minus-normal paired age difference, $C$ is the collection of cell-stratum and leave-one-donor-out checks, $L=\mathrm{age\_signature}$ is the layer tag, and $N$ is the local non-promotion rule. The contact $\mathrm{k\mbox{-}a.gse142439.horvath353}$ reports sixteen methylation-array samples organized as eight normal/treated pairs. The available platform coverage is $334$ of the $353$ Horvath clock CpGs, so the observed clock support fraction is $334/353$. The pair set is split across two recorded cell types, $\mathrm{SkinFibroblasts}$ and $\mathrm{VeinEndothelialCells}$.

\paragraph{Readback rule.}
The only numerical readback used here is the Horvath353 DNAm-age score obtained by applying the coefficient table $W$ to the covered coordinates of $M$, with the intercept included. For each donor pair, the recorded comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_A(d)=S_A(\mathrm{treated},d)-S_A(\mathrm{normal},d).
\end{aligned}
$$
The robustness condition is directional rather than mechanistic: the two cell-type strata must each have negative mean $\Delta_A$, and the pooled mean must remain negative after deleting any one donor pair. The verified fact $\mathrm{cellstate.age\mbox{-}clock\mbox{-}shift.robustness.gse142439}$ has experiment run $\mathrm{7aa5719138bb}$, contact $\mathrm{k\mbox{-}a.gse142439.horvath353}$, experiment identifier $\mathrm{gse142439\_horvath353\_age\_clock\_shift\_robustness}$, support value $\mathrm{true}$, and verdict $\mathrm{directionally\_robust}$ as of $2026\mbox{-}06\mbox{-}09\,18{:}18{:}24+00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Robustness interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is that this curated matrix-and-coefficient contact supports a negative treated-minus-normal Horvath353 age-signature shift that is not confined to only one of the two recorded cell-type strata and is not destroyed by removal of a single donor pair. In symbols, the supported finite assertion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\overline{\Delta_A}(\mathrm{SkinFibroblasts})<0,\\
&\overline{\Delta_A}(\mathrm{VeinEndothelialCells})<0,\\
&\overline{\Delta_A}(P\setminus\{d\})<0\quad\text{for every recorded donor }d\in P,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $|P|=8$. This is a scoped clock-readback statement. It says that, under the fixed sample pairing, fixed CpG coverage floor, and fixed Horvath353 weight table, the observed treated condition moves the DNAm-age signature downward in a way that passes the two listed robustness filters. It does not assert that every methylation clock would agree, that all unmeasured CpGs behave similarly, or that the result persists outside these sixteen samples.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The contact is explicitly silent about cell identity, realized cellular function, safety, renewal, organismal phenotype, and immortality. A negative Horvath353 delta, even one that is cross-cell-type and leave-one-donor-out robust in this finite table, is not evidence that the treated cells preserved identity markers, gained youthful function, avoided oncogenic or toxic risk, entered a durable maintenance regime, improved organismal state, or acquired indefinite propagation potential. No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows from this record unless a separate assay at the relevant layer supplies that information. The same restriction applies to stronger phrases such as identity-preserving age reset, rejuvenation, partial reprogramming, renewable maintenance, and immortality potential: they remain outside the evidential reach of this methylation-array contact.

\paragraph{Failure condition.}
The packet would lose its supported status if either cell-type stratum had a nonnegative mean paired DNAm-age delta, if any leave-one-donor-out pooled mean became nonnegative, if the frozen normal/treated pairing or Horvath353 coverage ceased to be available, or if a promoted biological interpretation were accepted from this contact alone. Since the recorded finite verdict is $\mathrm{directionally\_robust}$ with $\mathrm{support}=\mathrm{true}$, the present chapter records the age-signature robustness result while keeping all higher-layer biological readings null.
