\subsection{Donor-resolved pluripotency break in GSE165177 day-10 MPTR fibroblasts}
\label{subsec:identity-samesig-break-decomposition-gse165177}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record for \texttt{identity-samesig.break-decomposition.gse165177} is a finite cell-identity packet
$B_B=(E,P,S_I,Q_I,D_I,P_{\max},C,L,N)$.  Here $E$ is the curated Log2 RPM marker-expression table from the GSE165177 transient-reprogramming RNA-seq matrices, and $P$ is the set of three donor-matched exp2 day-10 pairs comparing fibroblast negative controls with transiently reprogrammed fibroblasts.  The identity panel $S_I$ contains ten fibroblast-associated markers,
$\{\mathrm{COL1A1},\mathrm{COL1A2},\mathrm{COL3A1},\mathrm{THY1},\mathrm{DCN},\mathrm{LUM},\mathrm{VIM},\mathrm{PDGFRA},\mathrm{FAP},\mathrm{S100A4}\}$,
used only to compute donor-level identity distance.  The pluripotency panel $Q_I$ contains five markers,
$\{\mathrm{POU5F1},\mathrm{NANOG},\mathrm{LIN28A},\mathrm{LIN28B},\mathrm{SOX2}\}$,
used to detect activation of the pluripotency boundary.  The source table also carries $\mathrm{KLF4}$ and $\mathrm{MYC}$, so the curated contact has seventeen named gene rows across the selected donor samples.  The layer label is $L=\mathrm{cell\_identity}$, and $N$ is the local no-promotion rule: a cell-identity expression comparison cannot by itself certify an age reset, a safety property, renewable maintenance, organismal preservation, or immortal capacity.

\paragraph{Coordinate computation.}
For each of the three donor pairs, the treated-minus-control identity displacement is summarized by the RMS quantity
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_I(d) &= \left(\frac{1}{10}\sum_{g\in S_I}\big(E_{\mathrm{treated}}(d,g)-E_{\mathrm{control}}(d,g)\big)^2\right)^{1/2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the same donor, each pluripotency coordinate is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_Q(d,q) &= E_{\mathrm{treated}}(d,q)-E_{\mathrm{control}}(d,q),\qquad q\in Q_I,\\
P_{\max}(d) &= \max_{q\in Q_I}\Delta_Q(d,q).
\end{aligned}
$$
The predeclared support condition requires at least three donor pairs, full coverage of all identity and pluripotency markers, $D_I(d)\leq 1.5$ Log2 RPM for every donor, and no pluripotency marker increase above $1.0$ Log2 RPM.  The break condition is the complement at the finite-row level: absent pairing, absent marker coverage, an identity RMS excursion above $1.5$, or any $Q_I$ coordinate exceeding the hard $+1.0$ Log2 RPM boundary.  The classification coordinate $C$ keeps the donor and marker responsible for the strict no-positive and hard plus-one tests rather than compressing the outcome into a single pass/fail flag.

\paragraph{Reality contact.}
The contact \texttt{k-i.gse165177-mptr} is RNA-seq expression evidence, not a methylation-clock assay, phenotypic safety assay, longitudinal maintenance experiment, organismal study, or test of immortal potential.  Its observed content is limited and useful: the processed matrices expose donor-labelled fibroblast negative-control samples, transiently reprogrammed fibroblast samples, and iPSC boundary samples, with gene-level Log2 RPM values for the ten identity markers, the five pluripotency markers, and the two additional reprogramming factors $\mathrm{KLF4}$ and $\mathrm{MYC}$.  The contact can test the cell-identity layer, and it explicitly cannot test age signatures, realized function, safety boundaries, renewable maintenance, organismal maintenance, or cross-layer relations.
