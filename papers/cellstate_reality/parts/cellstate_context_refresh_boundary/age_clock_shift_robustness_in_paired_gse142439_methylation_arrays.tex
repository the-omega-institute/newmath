\paragraph{Finite record.}
The record for \texttt{age-clock-shift.gse142439.cross-cell-type-robustness} is a bounded age-signature packet, not a general biological rejuvenation claim. Its carrier is $B_R=(M,W,P,S_A,\Delta_A,C,L,N)$. \paragraph{Reality contact.}
The contact \texttt{k-a.gse142439.horvath353} supplies methylation-array evidence only. It records $16$ methylation-array samples arranged as $8$ normal/treated donor pairs. The array platform covers $334$ of the $353$ Horvath clock CpGs, so the coverage fraction used by the packet is $334/353$. The recorded pair set is split across two cell-type strata, \texttt{SkinFibroblasts} and \texttt{VeinEndothelialCells}. The contact resolution consists of CpG-level beta values for the covered Horvath353 markers, coefficient weights, the Horvath intercept, sample annotations, and person-level normal/treated pairing. These data can test the age-signature layer, but they do not measure cell identity, realized function, safety, longitudinal renewal, organismal maintenance, cross-layer relations, or immortality.

\paragraph{Readback rule.}
The finite readback applies the Horvath353 coefficient table to the frozen beta matrix and compares treated and normal samples only within matched donors. For donor $i$, the relevant scalar is the paired difference
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_A(i) &= S_A(\mathrm{treated}_i)-S_A(\mathrm{normal}_i).
\end{aligned}
$$
The packet is counted as directionally robust only when both recorded cell-type strata have negative mean paired deltas and every leave-one-donor-out overall mean delta is still negative. Thus the sign condition is not merely an aggregate average over all $8$ pairs; it also requires that the two recorded cell-type partitions agree in direction and that no single donor is necessary to keep the overall sign below zero.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}

Under the supplied finite row, none of those failure conditions is recorded for the age-signature robustness probe, while all stronger biological readings remain blocked.
