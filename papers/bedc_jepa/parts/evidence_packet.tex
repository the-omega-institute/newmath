\paragraph{Manuscript discipline.}
The paper follows a separation discipline: numerical records are not copied
into the manuscript as uncontrolled prose.  They are first summarized in a
durable claim table with source, pattern, classifier, stability, metrics,
ledger, not-claimed, record, and fact-owner fields.  The manuscript writes
only the claims admitted by that table and
keeps unsupported candidates as ledger or cannot-claim rows.  In the current
claim table, the admitted latent operational claim is
\texttt{door\_key\_context\_visible}; the source-gap predicates
\texttt{has\_key}, \texttt{door\_open\_or\_unlocked}, and
\texttt{goal\_reachable\_with\_current\_state} remain outside the admitted
latent-meaning claim boundary.

\paragraph{Source specification.}
The source is \texttt{boundary-gated-ou-world}.  Its latent dimension is $2$,
with sample count $768$, $\rho=0.84$, radius $1.0$, and gap width $0.14$.
The nonlinear observation is \texttt{sinusoidal-parabolic-shear}.

\paragraph{Pattern specification.}
The pattern is \texttt{latent-plus-distinction-plus-gap}.  It asks for three
readbacks from the same world process: continuous state recovery, operational
inside-boundary distinction, and boundary gap detection.

\paragraph{Quality-backend projection.}
The quality-backend candidate is a non-canonical projection of the same
BEDC-JEPA evidence records into a backend-style quality interface.  It exposes
the world-state contract, the S0/S1/S2/S3 readback pattern, selected metric
cells, and ledger rows for source, classifier, stability, generalization, and
mechanism debt.  Its forbidden surfaces explicitly exclude model-runner
execution, canonical writer execution, terminal verdict projection, and raw
benchmark regeneration.  Thus the candidate is an audit projection over the
declared evidence scope, not a new experiment, not a canonical BEDC backend,
and not a claim of global model quality.
The quality-lab export table compiles the same evidence-scope audit,
reproducibility summary, index,
and quality-backend records into a downstream export row.  It is a durable
projection of the evidence record rather than an additional empirical result.

\paragraph{Latent Claim Certificate Protocol.}
The latent-claim certificate records instantiate the protocol in
Section~\ref{sec:latent-claim-certificate}.  The current certificate record
applies the fail-closed rule to the native MiniGrid feature carrier for the
declared DoorKey readback predicate.  It records source gaps for operational
names whose required source traces are not present in the current evidence
record: \texttt{has\_key}, \texttt{door\_open\_or\_unlocked}, and
\texttt{goal\_reachable\_with\_current\_state}.  The conformal audit therefore
does not assert that every latent distinction is interpretable.  It asserts
only that each candidate distinction ends in one of two states: a certified
singleton operational claim under the declared risk level, or an explicit
gap ledger entry with coverage, source, or stability debt.
