\section{Triadic Route Taxonomy}
\label{sec:unified-reality-triadic-route-taxonomy}

The preceding certificate systems can be compressed into three
conditional routes.  Each route is a sufficient row for the RH fixed-half
section, and none is an unconditional proof until its source predicate
has been constructed from the completed zeta zero ledger.  The taxonomy
prevents the route from oscillating between equivalent surface names:
ghost exclusion, stable packet capture, and triadic closure are distinct
ways of naming the missing source theorem.

\begin{definition}[Closed-generatable zero]
\label{def:unified-reality-closed-generatable-zero}
A critical-strip zero coordinate $s$ is \emph{closed-generatable} when it
belongs to the closed generability carrier
$$
s\in\mathcal G_{\zeta}
$$
of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-closed-generability-carrier}.  Equivalently,
it has a zero-relevant packet with active capture, legal solenoid or
prime-register hidden fibre, finite routing compatibility, unit-boundary
stability, and no noncompact real positive gauge.
Write this row as
$$
\mathsf{ClosedGeneratable}_{\zeta}(s).
$$
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[No ghost zeta zero row]
\label{def:unified-reality-no-ghost-zeta-zero-row}
The \emph{no ghost zeta zero row} is
$$
\mathsf{NoGhostZetaZero}_{\zeta}
:=
\prod_{s:\ComplexUp}
\left(
\mathsf{ZetaZero}(s)
\times
\mathsf{InCritStrip}(s)
\to
\mathsf{ClosedGeneratable}_{\zeta}(s)
\right).
$$
A ghost zero would be a completed analytic zero row in
$\mathcal A_{\zeta}$ which has no closed triadic generation certificate
in $\mathcal G_{\zeta}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{corollary}[No ghost zeros are conditionally sufficient]
\label{cor:unified-reality-no-ghost-zeros-conditionally-sufficient}
If
$$
\mathsf{NoGhostZetaZero}_{\zeta}
$$
holds, then the RH fixed-half section exists.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Let $z:\mathcal Z_{\zeta}$ have coordinate $s$.  The no-ghost row gives
$\mathsf{ClosedGeneratable}_{\zeta}(s)$, hence $s\in\mathcal G_{\zeta}$.
By
\autoref{def:unified-reality-closed-triadic-no-real-scale-row} and the
compact-scale obstruction of
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-real-positive-scale-not-closed-compact-residue},
closed generability supplies the fixed-half row.  Varying $z$ gives the
RH section.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Stable packet capture row]
\label{def:unified-reality-stable-packet-capture-row}
The \emph{stable packet capture row} is the assertion that every
critical-strip zero coordinate $s$ has a canonical packet $P_s$ with:
$$
\mathsf{RoucheCapture}(P_s,s),
\qquad
\mathsf{LeeYangStable}(P_s),
$$
$$
\mathsf{SolenoidCompatible}(P_s,s),
\qquad
\mathsf{NondegenerateActiveHeight}(P_s).
$$
Write this source predicate as
$$
\mathsf{ZetaStablePacketCapture}_{\zeta}.
$$
It is stronger than merely displaying a finite model: the packet must be
zero-relevant for the completed zeta row.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Stable packet capture is conditionally sufficient]
\label{thm:unified-reality-stable-packet-capture-conditionally-sufficient}
If
$$
\mathsf{ZetaStablePacketCapture}_{\zeta}
$$
holds, then the RH fixed-half section exists.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $z:\mathcal Z_{\zeta}$ have coordinate
$$
s=\frac12+\delta+i\tau.
$$
The stable packet capture row supplies a packet $P_s$ which captures the
completed zero and is Lee--Yang stable.  The local channel has the form
$$
u_a(s)=e^{-(s-1/2)h_a}
=
e^{-\delta h_a}e^{-i\tau h_a}
$$
with some active height $h_a>0$.  If $\delta>0$, then all nearby active
channels lie inside the unit boundary; if $\delta<0$, they lie outside
it.  Lee--Yang stability excludes packet zeros in those regions, while
Rouch\'e capture forces the packet to have the same local zero count as
the completed zeta readout.  Thus $\delta\#0$ is impossible.  Located
fixed-half collapse gives $\delta=0$, and the RH section follows from
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-rh-fixed-half-section-equivalence}.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Zeta triadic closure tower row]
\label{def:unified-reality-zeta-triadic-closure-tower-row}
The \emph{zeta triadic closure tower row} is the assertion that every
critical-strip zero coordinate $s=1/2+\delta+i\tau$ has a packet
$$
x_s\in X_\infty
$$
inside an inverse-limit routing carrier such that:
$$
\mathsf{ActiveCapture}(x_s,s),
\qquad
\Delta(x_s)=\delta,
$$
$$
P x_s\in\overline{\{\mathscr R_\infty^m x_s:m\in\mathbb Z\}},
$$
and the same packet carries solenoid compatibility, finite triadic
closure, and either the parity-closure defect row or the cofinal
energy-bound row of the oriented certificate system.
Write this source predicate as
$$
\mathsf{ZetaTriadicClosureTower}_{\zeta}.
$$
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Triadic closure tower is conditionally sufficient]
\label{thm:unified-reality-zeta-triadic-closure-tower-conditionally-sufficient}
If
$$
\mathsf{ZetaTriadicClosureTower}_{\zeta}
$$
holds, then the RH fixed-half section exists.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $z:\mathcal Z_{\zeta}$ have coordinate $s=1/2+\delta+i\tau$.  The
triadic closure tower row supplies a packet $x_s$ with
$$
\Delta(x_s)=\delta
$$
and parity closure
$$
P x_s\in\overline{\{\mathscr R_\infty^m x_s:m\in\mathbb Z\}}.
$$
By
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-parity-closure-kills-odd-defect},
$$
\Delta(x_s)=0.
$$
Thus $\delta=0$.  The fixed-half section follows through
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-rh-fixed-half-section-equivalence}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The three source rows form one proof target]
\label{cor:unified-reality-three-source-rows-one-proof-target}
The following rows are conditionally sufficient for the same fixed-half
section:
$$
\mathsf{NoGhostZetaZero}_{\zeta},
\qquad
\mathsf{ZetaStablePacketCapture}_{\zeta},
\qquad
\mathsf{ZetaTriadicClosureTower}_{\zeta}.
$$
They differ in presentation, not in the downstream obstruction: each must
construct zero-relevant source data that excludes noncompact real scale.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The first row places every completed zero in the closed generability
carrier.  The second supplies the strongest local version: canonical
Rouch\'e capture plus Lee--Yang stability and solenoid compatibility.
The third supplies the routing version: a parity-closed inverse-limit
packet or an energy-bounded oriented certificate.  The conditional
implications are the preceding two theorems and
\autoref{cor:unified-reality-no-ghost-zeros-conditionally-sufficient}.
In each case, the unproved content is source construction, not the
fixed-half algebra performed after the source data are available.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Stable packet capture is the sharpest local target]
\label{cor:unified-reality-stable-packet-capture-sharpest-local-target}
Among the three rows, $\mathsf{ZetaStablePacketCapture}_{\zeta}$ is the
sharpest local analytic target.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
No-ghost exclusion is a global generability statement.  The triadic
closure tower is a routing and parity statement over an inverse-limit
carrier.  Stable packet capture names the local analytic object whose
construction would feed both: a canonical zero island, Rouch\'e capture,
Lee--Yang stability, solenoid compatibility, and nondegenerate active
height.  Thus it is the local source theorem at which the route should
concentrate before the global closure language is invoked.
\end{proof}
