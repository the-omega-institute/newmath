\section{Solenoid Lee--Yang Source Gap}
\label{sec:unified-reality-solenoid-lee-yang-source-gap}

The combined solenoid Lee--Yang target must be read as a source theorem,
not as another name for the fixed-half surface.  A row of the form
``completed zeta zero implies zero normal displacement'' has the same
logical shape as RH unless it is backed by an independent mechanism that
constructs the zero-relevant resonance carrier.  The route becomes
noncircular only when the zero predicate is connected to a stable
dominant resonance family, a tail-isolated local zero island, a compact
solenoid phase lift, and a far-end energy bound.

\begin{definition}[Fixed-half alias row]
\label{def:unified-reality-fixed-half-alias-row}
For a critical-strip zero coordinate $s$, write
$$
\mathsf{CritZero}(s)
:=
\mathsf{ZetaZero}(s)\times\mathsf{InCritStrip}(s).
$$
A proposed RH-facing row is a \emph{fixed-half alias row} when its only
zero-specific content is one of the fixed-surface readouts
$$
\mathsf{OnCritLine}(s),
\qquad
\delta(s)=0,
\qquad
\delta(s)^2=0,
$$
or a direct equivalent such as zero normal energy or no normal hair,
without a displayed source mechanism producing these rows from
dominance, stability, solenoid compatibility, or tail control.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-half aliases do not discharge the proof load]
\label{thm:unified-reality-fixed-half-aliases-do-not-discharge-proof-load}
A fixed-half alias row does not by itself prove the RH fixed-half
section.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The RH section asks for a total assignment
$$
\prod_{s:\ComplexUp}
\left(
\mathsf{CritZero}(s)\to\mathsf{OnCritLine}(s)
\right).
$$
A fixed-half alias row names the output surface of this assignment, or a
readout equivalent to that surface.  It does not supply a contradiction
to the hidden normal-coordinate counterform of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-hidden-normal-coordinate-counterform}, since
that counterform is precisely a zero-source row with $\delta\#0$ whose
normal coordinate has not yet been linked to the zero ledger.  By
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-hidden-normal-coordinate-circularity-boundary},
rewriting the fixed surface in mirror, prime-window, modulus, or energy
coordinates leaves the counterform unexcluded until a zero-relevance
mechanism is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Dominant solenoid source mechanism]
\label{def:unified-reality-dominant-solenoid-source-mechanism}
A \emph{dominant solenoid source mechanism} for a critical-strip zero
coordinate $s$ is a packet consisting of a resonance family
$$
\rho:\mathsf{StableResFam}_{\zeta}(s),
$$
together with the displayed rows
$$
\mathsf{DominantFamily}(\rho),
\qquad
\mathsf{TailBound}(\rho),
\qquad
\mathsf{RoucheGap}(\rho),
$$
$$
\mathsf{SolenoidCompatible}(\rho,s),
\qquad
\mathsf{FarEndEnergyBound}(\rho).
$$
The dominance row identifies the zero-relevant family.  The tail and
Rouch\'e rows make the local zero island stable under the completed
readout.  The solenoid row places the visible phase and the hidden
prime-adic registers inside a compact $S$-solenoid.  The far-end bound
controls the normal energy along the cofinal prime-adic windows of the
same family.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Source mechanisms are the noncircular content]
\label{thm:unified-reality-source-mechanisms-are-noncircular-content}
The RH-facing solenoid Lee--Yang route is noncircular exactly at the
point where it supplies a dominant solenoid source mechanism from the
completed zero ledger.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The compact solenoid argument is already structural: by
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-solenoid-compatible-resonance-no-normal-scale},
a solenoid-compatible zero-relevant family has no nontrivial positive
scale character, hence no $\delta$-scale.  The finite-to-infinite energy
argument is also structural: by
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-off-half-cofinal-resonance-energy-unbounded},
an off-half coordinate forces unbounded square energy along cofinal
prime-adic support.  Neither argument constructs the zero-relevant
family from the zero predicate.

A dominant solenoid source mechanism supplies the missing source data.
Dominance separates the active family from arbitrary projected chords.
The tail and Rouch\'e rows ensure that the family controls a local zero
island of the completed readout.  Solenoid compatibility puts the hidden
degrees of freedom in the compact prime-adic fibre rather than in a
noncompact real scale coordinate.  The far-end energy bound then becomes
a theorem about the same zero-relevant family, not an asserted synonym
for $\delta=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The combined bound must be read as a source theorem]
\label{cor:unified-reality-combined-bound-source-reading}
The row
$$
\mathsf{ZetaSolenoidLeeYangBound}_{\zeta}
$$
is noncircular only in its source reading: every critical-strip zero
comes with a stable dominant solenoid resonance source.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
If the combined bound is read only as
$$
\mathsf{CritZero}(s)\to\delta(s)=0
$$
or as
$$
\mathsf{CritZero}(s)\to
\sup_{B\preceq\rho}\mathcal N_B(s)<\infty
$$
with no independently constructed $\rho$, it is a fixed-half alias row in
the sense of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-fixed-half-alias-row}.  In contrast,
\autoref{def:unified-reality-zeta-solenoid-lee-yang-bound} quantifies
over a displayed stable family and a solenoid Lee--Yang bound witness.
\autoref{def:unified-reality-dominant-solenoid-resonance-certificate}
then refines the witness into dominance, solenoid compatibility, and
far-end energy data.  Under that reading, the row is a zeta-specific
source theorem rather than a relabeling of the fixed-half output.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The source gap is the remaining theorem]
\label{thm:unified-reality-source-gap-remaining-theorem}
The remaining theorem for the solenoid Lee--Yang route is the source
construction
$$
\mathsf{CritZero}(s)
\to
\sum_{\rho:\mathsf{StableResFam}_{\zeta}(s)}
\mathsf{DominantSolenoidSource}(\rho,s),
$$
where $\mathsf{DominantSolenoidSource}(\rho,s)$ denotes the packet of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-dominant-solenoid-source-mechanism}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Once such a source packet is supplied, its solenoid row gives
$\delta=0$ by
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-solenoid-compatible-resonance-no-normal-scale}.
Its far-end bound also gives $\delta=0$ by the unbounded-energy
contradiction in
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-solenoid-bound-component-same-exclusion}.
Thus the downstream implication to the RH fixed-half section is already
accounted for by
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-zeta-solenoid-lee-yang-bound-conditionally-sufficient}.
What remains is not another surface equivalence, but the construction of
the source packet from the completed zero row: the active family, its
dominance, its local Rouch\'e gap, its compact solenoid phase carrier,
and its far-end bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite templates are source templates]
\label{cor:unified-reality-finite-templates-are-source-templates}
The quartic spectral carrier and prime-splitting tower patterns support
the source theorem only as templates for dominance and carrier growth;
they are not substitutes for the zeta-specific source construction.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The quartic carrier contributes the finite pattern recorded in
\autoref{cor:unified-reality-quartic-carrier-finite-stability-template}:
zero location follows after a dominant family, local interference, and a
Rouch\'e remainder bound have been displayed.  The prime-splitting tower
pattern contributes the carrier-growth pattern recorded in
\autoref{cor:unified-reality-finite-spectral-tower-splitting-templates}:
one prime source can expose many local rows through a tower, so the zeta
family should be allowed to be cofinal rather than a single fixed finite
window.  These templates identify the shape of the source certificate.
They do not prove that every completed zeta zero carries such a
certificate.
\end{proof}
