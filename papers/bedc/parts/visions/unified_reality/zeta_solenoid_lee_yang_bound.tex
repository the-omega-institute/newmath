\section{Zeta Solenoid Lee--Yang Bound}
\label{sec:unified-reality-zeta-solenoid-lee-yang-bound}

The solenoid boundary and the resonance-bound boundary describe two
faces of the same RH-facing proof role.  The solenoid face says that a
zero-relevant phase state realized in a compact $S$-solenoid cannot
carry an additional real positive scale.  The bound face says that a
zero-relevant resonance family must have uniformly bounded normal energy
along its finite prime-adic windows.  The combined target is therefore a
zeta-specific Lee--Yang theorem with both parts displayed: each completed
zero must be captured by a stable dominant resonance tower whose phase
data is solenoid-compatible and whose normal energy is bounded at the
far end.

\begin{definition}[Zero-specific cofinal resonance tower]
\label{def:unified-reality-zero-specific-cofinal-resonance-tower}
For a critical-strip row $s$, a \emph{zero-specific cofinal resonance
tower} is a stable zero-relevant resonance family
$$
\rho:\mathsf{StableResFam}_{\zeta}(s)
$$
whose finite windows
$$
B\preceq\rho,
\qquad
B\subset\PrimeUp\times\NatUp,
$$
form a cofinal prime-adic support in the sense of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-cofinal-resonance-support}.  The tower is
indexed by the zero row $s$; different completed zero rows may have
different displayed windows, depths, and dominant resonance islands.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Solenoid Lee--Yang bound witness]
\label{def:unified-reality-solenoid-lee-yang-bound-witness}
For a critical-strip row $s$, a \emph{solenoid Lee--Yang bound witness}
is a stable zero-relevant resonance family $\rho$ together with the rows
$$
\mathsf{SolenoidCompatible}(\rho,s),
$$
$$
\mathsf{CofinalPAdicSupport}(\rho),
$$
and
$$
\sup_{B\preceq\rho}\mathcal N_B(s)<\infty.
$$
The first row is the compact solenoid phase condition of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-solenoid-compatible-resonance-lift}.  The
second row says that the finite windows of $\rho$ are cofinal in the
prime-adic resonance sense.  The third row is the uniform normal-energy
bound of \autoref{def:unified-reality-zeta-resonance-bound}.
Write $\mathsf{SolenoidLeeYangBoundWitness}(\rho,s)$ for this displayed
packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Zeta solenoid Lee--Yang bound]
\label{def:unified-reality-zeta-solenoid-lee-yang-bound}
The \emph{zeta solenoid Lee--Yang bound} is the row
$$
\mathsf{ZetaSolenoidLeeYangBound}_{\zeta}
:=
\prod_{s:\ComplexUp}
\left(
\mathsf{ZetaZero}(s)
\times
\mathsf{InCritStrip}(s)
\to
\sum_{\rho:\mathsf{StableResFam}_{\zeta}(s)}
\mathsf{SolenoidLeeYangBoundWitness}(\rho,s)
\right).
$$
It asserts that every completed critical-strip zero is carried by some
zero-specific stable dominant resonance tower whose phase data lies in a
compact solenoid and whose normal energy remains bounded along its
cofinal finite windows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The solenoid Lee--Yang bound is conditionally sufficient]
\label{thm:unified-reality-zeta-solenoid-lee-yang-bound-conditionally-sufficient}
If
$$
\mathsf{ZetaSolenoidLeeYangBound}_{\zeta}
$$
holds, then the RH fixed-half section exists.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $z:\mathcal Z_{\zeta}$ have complex coordinate $s$, and write
$$
s=\frac12+\delta+i\tau.
$$
The zeta solenoid Lee--Yang bound supplies a witness $\rho$ for the zero
row of $s$.  In particular it supplies
$$
\mathsf{SolenoidCompatible}(\rho,s).
$$
By
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-solenoid-compatible-resonance-no-normal-scale},
this gives $\delta=0$.  Hence $\Re(s)=1/2$, and
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-rh-fixed-half-section-equivalence} supplies
the fixed-half section.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The bound component gives the same exclusion]
\label{thm:unified-reality-solenoid-bound-component-same-exclusion}
Let a completed critical-strip zero carry a solenoid Lee--Yang bound
witness $\rho$.  Then the normal-energy component alone excludes
$\delta\#0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The witness includes cofinal prime-adic support and the bound
$$
\sup_{B\preceq\rho}\mathcal N_B(s)<\infty.
$$
If $\delta\#0$, then
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-off-half-cofinal-resonance-energy-unbounded}
gives
$$
\sup_{B\preceq\rho}\mathcal N_B(s)=\infty,
$$
contradicting the bound.  Thus $\neg(\delta\#0)$, and the located
fixed-half collapse row gives $\delta=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Two noncircular readings of the same target]
\label{cor:unified-reality-two-noncircular-readings-same-target}
The zeta solenoid Lee--Yang bound can be attacked either as a compact
phase theorem or as a finite-to-infinite energy theorem.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The compact phase reading uses
\autoref{lem:unified-reality-compact-phase-no-positive-scale-character}:
a compact solenoid phase carrier has no nontrivial real positive
character, so a solenoid-compatible zero-relevant state cannot retain the
factor $e^{-\delta h_{p,j}}$.  The energy reading uses
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-finite-resonance-energy-reads-off-half-displacement}
and
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-off-half-cofinal-resonance-energy-unbounded}:
off-half displacement forces unbounded positive normal energy along any
cofinal prime-adic resonance tower.  Both readings are proof roles for
the same zero-relevance claim rather than restatements of the target
surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Dominant solenoid resonance certificate]
\label{def:unified-reality-dominant-solenoid-resonance-certificate}
A \emph{dominant solenoid resonance certificate} for a completed zero
row consists of the following displayed data:
$$
\mathsf{StableResFam}_{\zeta}(s),
\qquad
\mathsf{DominantFamily}(\rho),
$$
$$
\mathsf{SolenoidCompatible}(\rho,s),
\qquad
\mathsf{FarEndEnergyBound}(\rho).
$$
The stable-family row says which resonance tower is zero-relevant.  The
dominance row separates that tower from visible but irrelevant resonance
data.  The solenoid row supplies the compact phase carrier.  The bound
row supplies the finite-to-infinite normal-energy control.
Write $\mathsf{DominantSolenoidResonanceCertificate}(\rho,s)$ for this
certificate packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dominant solenoid certificates supply the combined bound]
\label{thm:unified-reality-dominant-solenoid-certificates-supply-combined-bound}
If every completed critical-strip zero carries a dominant solenoid
resonance certificate with cofinal prime-adic support, then
$$
\mathsf{ZetaSolenoidLeeYangBound}_{\zeta}
$$
holds.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a zero row $s$, the certificate supplies the stable family $\rho$,
its dominance row, its solenoid compatibility row, and its far-end energy
bound.  Cofinal prime-adic support supplies the tower condition in
\autoref{def:unified-reality-zero-specific-cofinal-resonance-tower}.
The far-end energy bound is exactly the inequality
$$
\sup_{B\preceq\rho}\mathcal N_B(s)<\infty.
$$
Thus the displayed data are precisely a solenoid Lee--Yang bound witness
for $s$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite spectral and tower-splitting templates]
\label{cor:unified-reality-finite-spectral-tower-splitting-templates}
The combined target keeps the roles of the finite templates distinct:
quartic spectral geometry models dominance and local zero isolation,
while prime-splitting tower patterns model why a zero-specific
resonance family may expose many local prime rows without being a single
fixed finite window.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The quartic carrier is used only as a finite dominance template, as in
\autoref{cor:unified-reality-quartic-carrier-finite-stability-template}:
zero location follows after the zero-relevant dominant family and its
remainder bound are displayed.  A prime-splitting tower is a different
template: one displayed rational prime can generate many local prime
rows in higher layers, so the resonance family attached to a zero should
be allowed to grow through a tower.  Neither template proves the zeta
claim by itself.  Together they explain why
\autoref{def:unified-reality-zeta-solenoid-lee-yang-bound} quantifies
over a zero-specific cofinal resonance tower rather than over one
universal finite prime window.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The remaining proof load is zeta-specific dominance]
\label{thm:unified-reality-solenoid-bound-remaining-proof-load}
The unclosed content of the combined route is the implication
$$
\mathsf{ZetaZero}(s)
\times
\mathsf{InCritStrip}(s)
\to
\sum_{\rho:\mathsf{StableResFam}_{\zeta}(s)}
\mathsf{DominantSolenoidResonanceCertificate}(\rho,s).
$$
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
All subsequent consequences are already displayed.  A dominant solenoid
resonance certificate gives the combined bound by
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-dominant-solenoid-certificates-supply-combined-bound}.
The combined bound gives the RH fixed-half section by
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-zeta-solenoid-lee-yang-bound-conditionally-sufficient}.
What is not supplied by the zero predicate itself is the construction of
the stable dominant resonance family, its solenoid-compatible phase
carrier, and its far-end energy bound.  That construction is the
zeta-specific Lee--Yang theorem targeted by this route.
\end{proof}
