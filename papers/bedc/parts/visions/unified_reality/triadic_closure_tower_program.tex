\section{Triadic Closure Tower Program}
\label{sec:unified-reality-triadic-closure-tower-program}

The projection dictionary can be organized as a constructive tower.  The
tower does not claim an unconditional proof of the Riemann hypothesis.
Its role is to state the certificate whose construction would discharge
the zero-rigidity problem.  Each layer records four rows:
$$
\Omega_n:=(O_n,G_n,I_n,F_n),
$$
where $O_n$ is the layer object, $G_n$ is the generation rule from the
preceding layer, $I_n$ is the invariant retained by the layer, and $F_n$
is the information lost in the projection fibre.  The discipline is that
no later layer may introduce an unledgered object; every carrier must be
generated from previous rows and the triadic roles of distinction,
symmetry, and time.

\begin{definition}[Triadic construction layer]
\label{def:unified-reality-triadic-construction-layer}
A \emph{triadic construction layer} is a packet
$$
\Omega_n=(O_n,G_n,I_n,F_n).
$$
The generation row
$$
G_n:O_n\leadsto O_{n+1}
$$
is legal only when it is a displayed operation on the current carrier
and the triadic roles.  The invariant $I_n$ is the datum preserved by
the layer, and the fibre $F_n$ records which data are no longer visible
after projection to the next readout.
\end{definition}

\begin{principle}[No unledgered later object]
\label{prin:unified-reality-no-unledgered-later-object}
In a triadic construction tower, an object at layer $n+1$ is licensed
only by the preceding packet and the displayed generation rule.  A
zero-relevant endpoint packet, solenoid carrier, selector, routing
space, or energy bound cannot be introduced as an external primitive.
\end{principle}

\begin{definition}[Zeta triadic closure tower certificate]
\label{def:unified-reality-zeta-triadic-closure-tower-certificate}
For a critical-strip zero coordinate
$$
s=\frac12+\delta+i\tau,
$$
a \emph{zeta triadic closure tower certificate} consists of a point
$x_s$ in a recursive packet space $X_\infty$ together with the following
rows:
$$
\mathsf{ActiveCapture}(x_s,s),
\qquad
\mathsf{DefectExtraction}(\Delta(x_s)=\delta),
$$
$$
\mathsf{PrimeRouting}(X_\infty,\mathscr R),
\qquad
\mathsf{TriadicClosure}(\mathsf{PrimeCharge},\mathsf{Parity},\mathsf{Phase}),
$$
$$
\mathsf{ParityClosure}\left(
Jx_s\in\overline{\{\mathscr R^m x_s:m\in\mathbb Z\}}
\right),
$$
and a bound-versus-growth row
$$
W_n\to\infty,
\qquad
\mathcal E_n(s)\le B_s<\infty.
$$
Active capture means Rouch\'e or local-degree capture of the completed
zero.  Defect extraction identifies the packet defect with
$\Re(s)-1/2$.  Prime routing supplies the inverse-limit successor
structure.  Triadic closure permits only unit phase and prime-register
gauge, not a hidden positive real scale.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Triadic closure tower certificates are conditionally sufficient]
\label{thm:unified-reality-triadic-closure-tower-certificates-conditionally-sufficient}
If every critical-strip zeta zero carries a zeta triadic closure tower
certificate, then the RH fixed-half section exists.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $z:\mathcal Z_{\zeta}$ have complex coordinate
$$
s=\frac12+\delta+i\tau.
$$
The certificate supplies a packet $x_s$ with
$$
\Delta(x_s)=\delta.
$$
By the parity-closure row,
$$
Jx_s\in\overline{\{\mathscr R^m x_s:m\in\mathbb Z\}}.
$$
The abstract parity-routing theorem
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-parity-closure-kills-odd-defect} gives
$$
\Delta(x_s)=0.
$$
Therefore $\delta=0$ and $\Re(s)=1/2$.  Since the zero row was arbitrary,
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-rh-fixed-half-section-equivalence} gives
the RH fixed-half section.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Energy gives the same contradiction]
\label{cor:unified-reality-triadic-energy-gives-same-contradiction}
In a zeta triadic closure tower certificate, the bound-versus-growth row
also excludes $\delta\#0$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
For a finite active packet $A_n$,
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-energy-square-triadic-closure-failure}
gives
$$
\mathcal E_n(s)=\delta^2W_n,
\qquad
W_n:=\sum_{a\in A_n}h_a^2.
$$
If $\delta\#0$ and $W_n\to\infty$, then
$$
\mathcal E_n(s)\to\infty.
$$
This contradicts the zero-compatible bound
$$
\mathcal E_n(s)\le B_s<\infty.
$$
Hence $\neg(\delta\#0)$, and located fixed-half collapse gives
$\delta=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Triadic closure source obligations]
\label{def:unified-reality-triadic-closure-source-obligations}
The triadic closure route leaves three source obligations:
$$
\mathsf{CanonicalEndpointCapture},
\qquad
\mathsf{LeeYangUnitBoundary},
\qquad
\mathsf{PrimeRoutingParityClosure}.
$$
The first constructs, for each completed zero, a dominant endpoint packet
with Rouch\'e or winding capture.  The second proves a unit-boundary or
bounded-energy theorem for that packet.  The third constructs the
recursive routing closure and the parity relation inside one packet
space.
\end{definition}

\begin{corollary}[The source obligations are not fixed-half aliases]
\label{cor:unified-reality-triadic-source-obligations-not-aliases}
The source obligations in
\autoref{def:unified-reality-triadic-closure-source-obligations} are
zeta-specific construction problems, not restatements of
$\Re(s)=1/2$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Canonical endpoint capture asks for a packet built from the completed
zero ledger, as in
\autoref{def:unified-reality-zeta-endpoint-bisection-certificate}.
Lee--Yang unit boundary asks for an independent stability or compactness
row, as in
\autoref{def:unified-reality-zeta-solenoid-lee-yang-row}.  Prime routing
parity closure asks for the mirror packet to lie inside the same
recursive closure, as in
\autoref{def:unified-reality-zeta-parity-closure-tower-certificate}.
None of these rows is the statement $\delta=0$ written in another
coordinate.  Each row supplies a missing source mechanism consumed by
the conditional theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Selector, solenoid, and endpoint rows form one program]
\label{cor:unified-reality-selector-solenoid-endpoint-one-program}
The Zeckendorf selector, solenoid Lee--Yang, endpoint bisection, parity
closure, and energy-bound rows are components of one triadic closure
program.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The Zeckendorf selector supplies a legal prime-charge family, as in
\autoref{def:unified-reality-zeckendorf-godel-prime-selector}.  The
solenoid carrier supplies compact phase and prime-register fibre
discipline.  The endpoint bisection row supplies a candidate
zero-relevant finite packet.  The parity closure row supplies the
topological odd-defect cancellation mechanism.  The energy row supplies
the cofinal contradiction when the normal displacement is nonzero.
Together these rows instantiate the spine of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-zeta-triadic-construction-spine}.
\end{proof}
