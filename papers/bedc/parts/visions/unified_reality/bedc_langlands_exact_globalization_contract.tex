\section{BEDC--Langlands Exact Globalization Contract}
\label{sec:unified-reality-bedc-langlands-exact-globalization-contract}

The Langlands closure chain can be compressed into a certificate DAG
statement. Local packet rows, finite Hecke windows, parameter states,
trace identities, multiplicity formulae, endoscopic transfers,
Tannakian separation, Chebotarev uniqueness, cofinal tail closure, and
no-hidden residual discipline are separate certificates. Exact
globalization says that once they are connected by complete NameCert
and ledger-pullback data, their composite has no unaddressed residual.

\begin{definition}[Langlands certificate DAG]
\label{def:unified-reality-langlands-certificate-dag}
A Langlands certificate DAG is a finite acyclic graph
$$
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{Lan}}
$$
whose nodes are drawn from the certificate interfaces
$$
\mathsf{LocalPacketCert},\quad
\mathsf{ParameterStateCert},\quad
\mathsf{FiniteHeckeProjectorCert},
$$
$$
\mathsf{StabilizedTraceFormulaCert},\quad
\mathsf{StableMultiplicityFormulaCert},\quad
\mathsf{EndoscopicTransferCert},
$$
$$
\mathsf{TannakianSeparationCert},\quad
\mathsf{ChebotarevCert},\quad
\mathsf{CofinalAutomorphicTailClosure},\quad
\mathsf{NoHiddenLanglandsResidual}.
$$
Each node $C$ carries a NameCert row
$$
\operatorname{NameCert}(C)
=
(\operatorname{SourceSpec}_C,
\operatorname{PatternSpec}_C,
\operatorname{ClassifierSpec}_C,
\operatorname{StabilityCert}_C,
\operatorname{LedgerPolicy}_C).
$$
The sink node is the closed global Langlands row
$$
\mathsf{GlobalLanglandsObject}(\Pi_\psi,\psi).
$$
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Ledger pullback edge]
\label{def:unified-reality-langlands-ledger-pullback-edge}
An edge
$$
C\longrightarrow C'
$$
of $\mathcal D_{\mathrm{Lan}}$ consists of a readout map from the
source interface to the target interface and a pullback ledger
$$
\Lambda_{C\to C'}
$$
with the following property. Any residual created by composing the two
interfaces either projects to a residual already visible in
$\operatorname{LedgerPolicy}_C$, projects to a residual already visible
in $\operatorname{LedgerPolicy}_{C'}$, or is recorded as an
edge-compatibility row in $\Lambda_{C\to C'}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Node exactness]
\label{def:unified-reality-langlands-node-exactness}
A node $C$ of the certificate DAG is exact when every residual in the
readout scope of $C$ is finite-visible through a detector named by
$C$, or is recorded by $\operatorname{LedgerPolicy}_C$. A closed exact
node carries no unresolved ledger row.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Finite-window exactness]
\label{def:unified-reality-langlands-finite-window-exactness}
For a finite Hecke window
$$
\mathcal W=(S,R,\mathcal B)
$$
as in \autoref{def:unified-reality-finite-hecke-window}, a packet
$\Pi$ is exact over $\mathcal W$ for $\psi$ when
$$
\lambda_{\mathcal W}(\Pi)
=
a_{\mathcal W}(\psi)
$$
and every residual not measured inside $\mathcal W$ is recorded in the
window-tail ledger attached to the bound $\mathcal B$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Ledger pullback preserves exactness]
\label{thm:unified-reality-ledger-pullback-preserves-exactness}
Let $C\to C'$ be a ledger pullback edge. If $C$ and $C'$ are exact, then
their composite interface is exact.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $\rho$ be a residual of the composite interface. If its projection
to the source interface is nonzero, exactness of $C$ makes it
finite-visible or records it in $\operatorname{LedgerPolicy}_C$. If its
projection to the target interface is nonzero, exactness of $C'$ does
the same using $\operatorname{LedgerPolicy}_{C'}$.

It remains to consider the case in which both projections vanish but
the composite residual is still asserted to be nonzero. Such a residual
is not a source residual and not a target residual; it is precisely a
compatibility failure of the edge. By
\autoref{def:unified-reality-langlands-ledger-pullback-edge}, every
edge-compatibility failure is recorded in $\Lambda_{C\to C'}$. Hence
the composite is exact.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Certificate DAG induction]
\label{thm:unified-reality-langlands-certificate-dag-induction}
Let $\mathcal D_{\mathrm{Lan}}$ be a Langlands certificate DAG. Suppose
every source node is exact and every edge is a ledger pullback edge.
Then every node obtained by composing certificates along
$\mathcal D_{\mathrm{Lan}}$ is exact. If the sink is closed, the sink
has no unresolved residual.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose a topological ordering of the finite acyclic graph. Source nodes
are exact by hypothesis. Assume all predecessors of a node $C'$ are
exact. Composing $C'$ with each predecessor along its incoming ledger
pullback edge preserves exactness by
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-ledger-pullback-preserves-exactness}.
Since the incoming set is finite, repeated application gives exactness
of the composite interface at $C'$. Induction over the topological
ordering proves exactness for every node, including the sink.

At a closed sink, any finite-visible residual must be recorded by the
sink ledger policy, and any ledger residual is incompatible with
closedness if unresolved. Omitting it violates ledger discipline. Thus
the closed sink carries no unresolved residual.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal exactness dichotomy]
\label{thm:unified-reality-cofinal-exactness-dichotomy}
Assume every window of a cofinal Hecke tower has an exact
finite-window realization of $\psi$. If bounded automorphic finiteness
and the complete automorphic tail ledger hold, then either there is a
global packet $\Pi_\psi$ matching every certified finite detector, or a
cofinal realization sequence enters the automorphic tail ledger.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose one exact realizing packet on each finite window. This gives a
cofinal window realization in the sense of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-cofinal-window-realization}. The
window-to-global alternative of
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-window-global-alternative} applies:
bounded complexity along an infinite subsequence gives a global packet
matching all certified unramified finite detectors, while absence of
such a subsequence sends the realization sequence to the automorphic
tail ledger.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Closed cofinal exactness gives a global packet]
\label{cor:unified-reality-closed-cofinal-exactness-global-packet}
Under the hypotheses of
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-cofinal-exactness-dichotomy}, if closed
accepted Langlands rows carry no unresolved automorphic tail ledger,
then there is a global packet $\Pi_\psi$ matching every certified finite
detector.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The dichotomy gives either a global packet or an automorphic tail row.
The tail row is either unrecorded, contradicting ledger discipline, or
recorded but unresolved, contradicting closedness. Hence the global
packet alternative remains.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Exact globalization contract]
\label{def:unified-reality-exact-globalization-contract-lan}
The contract
$$
\mathsf{ExactGlobalizationContract}_{\mathrm{Lan}}
$$
consists of:
\begin{itemize}
\item a Langlands certificate DAG with NameCert data at every node;
\item complete ledger pullback on every edge;
\item exact finite-window realization on a cofinal Hecke tower;
\item bounded automorphic finiteness and a complete automorphic tail
ledger;
\item the no-hidden residual certificate of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-no-hidden-residual-completeness-certificates};
\item closedness against unresolved residual ledgers.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ExactGlobalizationContract for Langlands]
\label{thm:unified-reality-exact-globalization-contract-lan}
Assume
$$
\mathsf{ExactGlobalizationContract}_{\mathrm{Lan}}.
$$
Then there is a global packet $\Pi_\psi$ and
$$
R_{\mathrm{Lan}}(\Pi_\psi,\psi)=0.
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The certificate DAG induction of
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-langlands-certificate-dag-induction}
shows that the composed Langlands interface is exact: every residual is
finite-visible or ledgered. Exact finite-window realization on the
cofinal tower, together with
\autoref{cor:unified-reality-closed-cofinal-exactness-global-packet},
produces a global packet $\Pi_\psi$ matching every certified finite
detector.

Assume that
$$
R_{\mathrm{Lan}}(\Pi_\psi,\psi)\ne0.
$$
By
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-no-hidden-langlands-residual-master-lemma},
the residual is finite-visible or ledgered. A finite-visible residual
is already in the DAG readout scope and must be recorded by the relevant
ledger policy. A ledger residual is recorded by construction. In both
cases an unresolved row contradicts closedness, while omission
contradicts ledger discipline. Therefore no residual remains.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BEDC--Langlands exact closure]
\label{thm:unified-reality-bedc-langlands-exact-closure}
If the Langlands certificate DAG has NameCert data at every node,
complete ledger pullbacks on every edge, exact finite-window
realization on a cofinal tower, no-hidden residual completeness, and
closedness, then Langlands reciprocity holds on the certified
unramified finite detector scope:
$$
\lambda_{v,r}(\Pi_\psi)
=
\operatorname{tr}r(\psi(\operatorname{Frob}_v)).
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The hypotheses are exactly the data of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-exact-globalization-contract-lan}. By
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-exact-globalization-contract-lan}, there
is a global packet and the total Langlands residual vanishes. Projecting
the equality
$$
R_{\mathrm{Lan}}(\Pi_\psi,\psi)=0
$$
to the unramified summand gives
$$
R_{\mathrm{unr}}=0.
$$
For the detector $(v,r)$, this is precisely
$$
\lambda_{v,r}(\Pi_\psi)
=
\operatorname{tr}r(\psi(\operatorname{Frob}_v)).
$$
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Functorial exact closure]
\label{cor:unified-reality-functorial-exact-closure}
Let
$$
\eta:{}^LH\to{}^LG
$$
be an $L$-group homomorphism. If a closed $H$-side packet
$(\Pi_H,\psi_H)$ is given and the target parameter
$$
\psi_G:=\eta\circ\psi_H
$$
satisfies the exact globalization contract on the $G$-side, then there
is a $G$-side packet $\Pi_G$ such that
$$
\lambda_{v,r}(\Pi_G)
=
\operatorname{tr}r(\eta(\psi_H(\operatorname{Frob}_v))).
$$
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-bedc-langlands-exact-closure} to the
target parameter $\psi_G$. The resulting packet satisfies
$$
\lambda_{v,r}(\Pi_G)
=
\operatorname{tr}r(\psi_G(\operatorname{Frob}_v)).
$$
Substitute $\psi_G=\eta\circ\psi_H$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[$GL_n$ exact closure]
\label{cor:unified-reality-gln-exact-closure}
For $G=GL_n$, assume the local $GL_n$ packet certificate, the
Rankin--Selberg detector closure used by the converse theorem, exact
finite twist windows, the twist tail ledger, and closedness. Then the
$GL_n$ Langlands reciprocity readout holds on the certified
unramified scope.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For $GL_n$, local packets are singleton packets, and the component and
endoscopic rows of the general certificate DAG reduce to the local
packet, converse-theorem, and twist-ledger rows. The
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-gln-converse-bridge-closure} supplies the
global automorphic packet whenever all finite Rankin--Selberg detector
conditions hold or sends the failure to the twist tail ledger.
Closedness excludes unresolved twist-tail data. The exact globalization
contract then removes the remaining Hecke--Frobenius residual.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[$GL_1$ two-square shadow]
\label{cor:unified-reality-exact-globalization-gl1-two-square-shadow}
For $G=GL_1$, $F=\mathbb Q$, and $K=\mathbb Q(i)$, the exact closure
readout specializes at odd primes to
$$
p=a^2+b^2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\chi_{-4}(\operatorname{Frob}_p)=1.
$$
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The $GL_1$ certificate DAG is class field theory with singleton
packets and scalar Hecke--Frobenius detectors. The quadratic character
of $\mathbb Q(i)/\mathbb Q$ has value $1$ exactly at split odd primes.
Splitting in $\mathbb Q(i)$ is equivalent to
$$
p\equiv 1\pmod 4,
$$
and Fermat's two-square theorem identifies this condition with
$p=a^2+b^2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Boundary of exact globalization]
\label{thm:unified-reality-exact-globalization-boundary}
The exact globalization contract is a conditional composition theorem.
It does not construct the local packet certificates, stable trace
formula, stable multiplicity formula, endoscopic transfer, Chebotarev
input, bounded automorphic finiteness, or cofinal tail certificate. It
states that, once those nodes and their ledger pullbacks are supplied as
NameCert data, their composite cannot hide a residual outside the
finite-detector or ledger alternatives.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The proof of the contract uses only DAG induction, edge ledger
pullback, the cofinal window-to-global dichotomy, and the no-hidden
residual master lemma. Each analytic or representation-theoretic
ingredient is consumed as a certificate node. Thus the theorem records
the exact BEDC globalization mechanism and leaves construction of the
input certificates to their own modules.
\end{proof}
