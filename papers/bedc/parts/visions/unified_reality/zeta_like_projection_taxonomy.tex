\section{Zeta-Like Projection Taxonomy}
\label{sec:unified-reality-zeta-like-projection-taxonomy}

The finite objects used in the zeta route do not all have the same
projection status.  Some are direct finite projections of the Riemann
zeta prime tower.  Others are finite projections of the same
zeta-like spectral mechanism without being truncations of the Riemann
zeta function itself.  The distinction is essential: it prevents the
finite quartic and elliptic examples from being misread as literal
Riemann-zeta windows while preserving their role as closed laboratories
for the same observation, recursion, spectrum, normalization, symmetry,
zero-ledger, and far-end pattern.

\begin{definition}[Direct Riemann-zeta finite projection]
\label{def:unified-reality-direct-riemann-zeta-finite-projection}
A \emph{direct Riemann-zeta finite projection} is a finite readout
obtained by restricting the Euler prime dimensions, the occupation
depths, or the recursive parity nodes of the Riemann zeta carrier.  The
basic prime-window projection is
$$
\zeta_S(s):=\prod_{p\in S}(1-p^{-s})^{-1}.
$$
The finite occupation-depth projection is
$$
\zeta_{S,M}(s)
:=
\prod_{p\in S}
\left(1+p^{-s}+p^{-2s}+\cdots+p^{-Ms}\right).
$$
The prime-adic parity windows
$$
A\subset\PrimeUp\times\NatUp
$$
of \autoref{def:unified-reality-prime-adic-parity-window} are direct
finite windows of the recursive parity expansion of the same Euler
carrier.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Universal zeta-like carrier]
\label{def:unified-reality-universal-zeta-like-carrier}
The \emph{universal zeta-like carrier} is the schematic packet
$$
\mathcal Z_{\Omega}
:=
(
\mathsf{Obs},
\mathsf{Rec},
\mathsf{Spectrum},
\mathsf{Normalize},
\mathsf{Sym},
\mathsf{ZeroLedger},
\mathsf{FarEnd}
).
$$
It records the mechanism by which finite observations are recursively
folded into spectral data, normalized to a unit or boundary surface,
transported by a symmetry row, constrained by a zero ledger, and kept
separate from an apophatic far-end absorption row.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Zeta-like finite projection]
\label{def:unified-reality-zeta-like-finite-projection}
A \emph{zeta-like finite projection} is a finite carrier projection of
$\mathcal Z_{\Omega}$ that displays the rows
$$
\mathsf{Obs},
\quad
\mathsf{Rec},
\quad
\mathsf{Spectrum},
\quad
\mathsf{Normalize},
\quad
\mathsf{Sym},
\quad
\mathsf{ZeroLedger}
$$
inside a finite laboratory.  It need not be a truncation of the Riemann
zeta Euler product.  Its projection status depends on preserving the
mechanism, not on sharing the same prime-indexed carrier.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Direct windows are zeta-like projections]
\label{thm:unified-reality-direct-windows-zeta-like-projections}
Every direct Riemann-zeta finite projection is a zeta-like finite
projection, but not every zeta-like finite projection is a direct
Riemann-zeta finite projection.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The direct windows inherit their observation rows from finite prime
sets, their recursion from Euler multiplication or occupation-depth
extension, their spectrum from the finite Hamiltonian readouts of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-finite-hamiltonian-prime-layer}, their
normalization from the centered channels, their symmetry from the
fixed-half mirror, and their zero-relevance boundary from the completed
zeta route.  Hence they are zeta-like projections.

The converse fails because a finite carrier may realize the same
mechanism without being a prime-window truncation of the Riemann zeta
function.  The quartic carrier of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-quartic-spectral-common-carrier} is such a
case: its recurrence, generating function, companion spectrum, elliptic
normalization, real projection, dominance row, and endpoint-zero law are
finite spectral rows, not a finite Euler product over primes.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The quartic carrier is a zeta-like finite projection]
\label{thm:unified-reality-quartic-zeta-like-finite-projection}
The fixed quartic spectral carrier is a zeta-like finite projection of
$\mathcal Z_{\Omega}$, not a direct Riemann-zeta finite projection.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The observation row is the rational generating-function source
$Z(t,y)=N(t,y)/D(t,y)$.  The recursion row is the order-four recurrence
for $Z_m(y)$.  The spectrum row is the companion-matrix and spectral
polynomial readout
$$
\det(\lambda I-T(y))=\Pi(\lambda,y).
$$
The normalization row is the elliptic transport to
$$
E:\quad Y^2=4\lambda^3-4\lambda+1.
$$
The symmetry and zero-ledger rows are the real projection, branch
dominance, and endpoint-zero law isolated in
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-quartic-marks-zero-relevance-boundary}.
These rows instantiate the zeta-like mechanism.  None of them is a
finite restriction of the Riemann-zeta prime-indexed Euler product.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Projection status ledger]
\label{def:unified-reality-projection-status-ledger}
The \emph{projection status ledger} records, for a finite carrier $F$,
which of the following rows it carries:
$$
\mathsf{DirectRiemannWindow}(F),
\qquad
\mathsf{ZetaLikeFiniteProjection}(F),
\qquad
\mathsf{ZeroRelevantBoundary}(F).
$$
The first row implies the second when the finite Riemann window is read
with its spectral, normalized, and zero-boundary ledgers.  The second row
does not imply the first.  The third row is additional: it records that
the finite projection has identified the boundary surface that actually
controls its zero ledger.
\end{definition}

\begin{corollary}[Projection status separates analogy from truncation]
\label{cor:unified-reality-projection-status-separates-analogy-truncation}
The finite quartic, elliptic, and finite-field style zeta models should
be compared with Riemann zeta through the zeta-like mechanism ledger, not
through literal Euler-product truncation.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
A literal truncation must preserve the prime-indexed Euler carrier.  The
quartic model preserves recurrence, spectral normalization, symmetry, and
zero-boundary rows instead.  Therefore its correct comparison point is
$\mathcal Z_{\Omega}$, not the direct finite Euler window
$\zeta_S(s)$.  This keeps the finite model useful without overstating its
relation to Riemann zeta.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite projection energy is the completed-readout test]
\label{thm:unified-reality-finite-projection-energy-completed-readout-test}
For the Riemann-zeta direct windows, an off-half critical-strip row is
seen by every prime-adic parity window as nonzero second-order energy.
Thus the RH-facing completed-readout test is whether this finite
projection energy can coexist with the completed global zero ledger.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-noncentrality-finite-window-energy}, if
$s=1/2+\delta+i\tau$ and $\delta(s)\#0$, then every prime-adic parity
window $A$ carries
$$
E_A^{\mathrm{pad}}(s)\#0.
$$
By
\autoref{cor:unified-reality-sphere-boundary-geometric-energy-refusal},
this is the same obstruction as failure to lie on the finite
prime-adic parity unit sphere.  The remaining completed-readout test is
therefore not visibility of the defect, which is already finite, but
zero-relevance of that defect in the completed zeta carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Finite projections identify the no-absorption target]
\label{cor:unified-reality-finite-projections-identify-no-absorption-target}
The finite-projection framework reduces the RH-facing gap to the claim
that nonzero finite-projection normal energy cannot be absorbed by the
infinite completed zeta readout as a zero row.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Direct finite windows detect the normal energy by
\autoref{thm:unified-reality-finite-projection-energy-completed-readout-test}.
Zeta-like finite projections such as the quartic carrier show why
zero-relevance must be separately proved: a visible spectral branch or
defect matters only after dominance, normalization, and zero-ledger rows
identify the relevant boundary.  Thus the remaining target is the
no-absorption row already isolated by
\autoref{def:unified-reality-no-hidden-far-end-correction} and the
defect-zero incompatibility row of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-rh-defect-zero-incompatibility-route}.
\end{proof}
