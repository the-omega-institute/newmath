\section{Prime Charge, Parity, and Phase Dictionary}
\label{sec:unified-reality-prime-charge-parity-phase-dictionary}

The parity closure route benefits from a sharper projection dictionary.
The three primary reads are not parity, energy, and an unspecified
third observable.  The more stable triad is prime charge, parity, and
phase.  Energy is a derived second-order observable: it is the square
readout of a failure of these three rows to close.

\begin{definition}[Prime charge, parity, and phase projections]
\label{def:unified-reality-prime-charge-parity-phase-projections}
The \emph{prime charge--parity--phase dictionary} assigns the three
ontic roles to their zeta-facing projections as follows:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.23\linewidth}p{0.28\linewidth}p{0.38\linewidth}}
\textbf{Ontic role} & \textbf{Primary projection} & \textbf{Zeta read}\\
Distinction or primality & prime charge, label, fibre & $p$, $v_p$, $S$, $(p,j)$, $\prod_{p\in S}\mathbb Z_p$\\
Symmetry or unit boundary & parity, mirror, unit circle & $J(s)=1-\overline{s}$ and $\delta\mapsto-\delta$\\
Time or recursion & phase, action, routing & $e^{-i\tau h_a}$ and $\mathcal R:X\to X$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
For a node $a=(p,j)$, the prime charge determines the positive height
$$
h_a:=2^j\log p.
$$
For $s=1/2+\delta+i\tau$, the local channel is
$$
U_a(s):=e^{-(s-1/2)h_a}
=
e^{-\delta h_a}e^{-i\tau h_a}.
$$
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Prime charge]
\label{def:unified-reality-prime-charge}
The \emph{prime charge} of a displayed resonance node is its discrete
prime-register row.  In a finite prime family $S$, this register is read
through the compact fibre
$$
\prod_{p\in S}\mathbb Z_p
$$
behind the solenoid projection
$$
\Sigma_S\twoheadrightarrow\mathbb T.
$$
It records which prime slice, valuation depth, or parity depth a node
belongs to.  It is therefore a distinction read, not a real positive
scale.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Prime charge determines the height ledger]
\label{thm:unified-reality-prime-charge-determines-height-ledger}
For every displayed node $a=(p,j)$, the prime charge determines the
Hamiltonian height
$$
h_a=2^j\log p,
$$
and hence determines the normal defect and phase rows
$$
\log|U_a(s)|=-\delta h_a,
\qquad
\arg U_a(s)\equiv-\tau h_a\pmod{2\pi}.
$$
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The node label $a=(p,j)$ records both the prime slice $p$ and the parity
depth $j$.  The height row is therefore $h_a=2^j\log p$.  Substituting
this into
$$
U_a(s)=e^{-\delta h_a}e^{-i\tau h_a}
$$
gives the modulus and argument readouts.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Energy as derived closure-defect observable]
\label{def:unified-reality-energy-derived-closure-defect-observable}
For a finite active packet $A$, the first-order normal defect of a node
is
$$
d_a(s):=\log|U_a(s)|=-\delta h_a.
$$
The associated energy is the second-order read
$$
\mathcal E_A(s):=\sum_{a\in A}d_a(s)^2.
$$
It is not a primary ontic projection.  It forgets the parity orientation
of the defect and records only its magnitude.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Energy is the square of triadic closure failure]
\label{thm:unified-reality-energy-square-triadic-closure-failure}
For $s=1/2+\delta+i\tau$ and a finite active packet $A$,
$$
\mathcal E_A(s)
=
\delta^2\sum_{a\in A}h_a^2.
$$
Under the mirror $J(s)=1-\overline{s}$, the first-order defect changes
sign, while the energy is unchanged.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The defect formula gives $d_a(s)=-\delta h_a$, so
$$
\mathcal E_A(s)=\sum_{a\in A}\delta^2h_a^2
=
\delta^2\sum_{a\in A}h_a^2.
$$
The mirror sends $\delta$ to $-\delta$, so $d_a$ changes sign.  Squaring
removes the sign, hence the energy is mirror-even.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Triadic closure defect]
\label{def:unified-reality-triadic-closure-defect}
A \emph{triadic closure defect} for a zero-relevant packet is the
failure of the prime-charge ledger, parity/unit-boundary row, and
phase-routing row to close in one packet.  In the local zeta channel the
defect has first-order read
$$
\Omega_a(s):=\log|U_a(s)|=-\delta h_a.
$$
The unit-boundary row is
$$
\Omega_a(s)=0,
$$
or equivalently
$$
|U_a(s)|=1.
$$
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Unit triadic closure forces the fixed-half row]
\label{thm:unified-reality-unit-triadic-closure-forces-fixed-half}
Let $a$ be a displayed node with $h_a>0$.  If
$$
|U_a(s)|=1
$$
for $s=1/2+\delta+i\tau$, then
$$
\delta=0.
$$
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The modulus read is
$$
|U_a(s)|=e^{-\delta h_a}.
$$
If this equals $1$, then
$$
-\delta h_a=0.
$$
Since $h_a>0$, the located real readout gives $\delta=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Oriented triadic closure tower]
\label{def:unified-reality-oriented-triadic-closure-tower}
An \emph{oriented triadic closure tower} for a critical-strip zero
$s=1/2+\delta+i\tau$ is a zero-relevant recursive packet tower carrying:
$$
\mathsf{PrimeChargeLedger},
\qquad
\mathsf{ParityOddDefect},
\qquad
\mathsf{PhaseRouting},
$$
$$
\mathsf{WindingCapture},
\qquad
\mathsf{SolenoidGaugeDiscipline},
\qquad
\mathsf{EnergyOrUnitBound}.
$$
The winding row records Rouch\'e or local-degree capture of the zero.
The solenoid row permits phase and prime-register fibre information, but
not a hidden real positive scale.  The energy or unit-bound row prevents
the triadic closure defect from remaining nonzero along the tower.
\end{definition}

\begin{corollary}[Oriented triadic closure is a proof-role refinement]
\label{cor:unified-reality-oriented-triadic-closure-proof-role-refinement}
The oriented triadic closure tower refines the parity closure tower by
separating the three primary projections from the derived energy
observable.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The parity closure tower of
\autoref{def:unified-reality-zeta-parity-closure-tower-certificate}
uses a signed defect $\Delta$, routing invariance, and parity oddness.
The present dictionary identifies where these rows come from.  Prime
charge supplies $h_a$, phase routing supplies $e^{-i\tau h_a}$ and the
successor relation, and parity supplies the sign change
$\delta\mapsto-\delta$.  Energy appears only after the first-order
defect is squared.  Thus the oriented triadic tower keeps the primary
projection data visible instead of treating energy as a primitive row.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Solenoid fibres carry charge but not real scale]
\label{cor:unified-reality-solenoid-fibres-carry-charge-not-real-scale}
The solenoid fibre is the natural carrier for prime charge, while the
factor $e^{-\delta h_a}$ is a noncompact scale defect rather than a
legal hidden charge.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
By
\autoref{def:unified-reality-prime-charge}, the hidden fibre behind a
finite solenoid phase projection is $\prod_{p\in S}\mathbb Z_p$.  This
records prime-register information.  By
\autoref{lem:unified-reality-compact-phase-no-positive-scale-character},
a compact solenoid phase carrier has no nontrivial continuous positive
real character.  Therefore the real factor $e^{-\delta h_a}$ cannot be
absorbed into the prime-register fibre.  It is precisely the closure
defect measured by
\autoref{def:unified-reality-triadic-closure-defect}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[RH-facing use of the projection dictionary]
\label{cor:unified-reality-rh-use-projection-dictionary}
In the zeta route, the proof-relevant projections are prime charge,
parity, phase routing, winding capture, and the derived energy bound.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Prime charge determines the heights $h_a$.  Parity records the sign of
$\delta$.  Phase routing records the unitary part
$e^{-i\tau h_a}$ and the recursive successor structure.  Winding capture
is required to make the packet zero-relevant rather than merely visible.
Energy detects whether a nonzero triadic closure defect is being carried
along a cofinal tower.  These are exactly the proof-role components
named in
\autoref{def:unified-reality-oriented-triadic-closure-tower}.
\end{proof}
