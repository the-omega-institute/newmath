\chapter{Pseudotheory Refutation Gate Map}
\label{ch:pseudotheory-refutation-gate-map}
\origin{ai}

This chapter records the present audit surface for pseudotheory refusal.  It is
not a carrier design and not a witness schema.  Its purpose is to say where
each refutable failure mode is checked, which checks block assertions, and why
taste-sensitive stability remains outside automatic demotion.

\section{Stateful and stateless refusal}
\label{sec:pseudotheory-refutation-stateful-stateless}

Only reconstruction needs a persistent witness.  A reconstruction refusal
compares a candidate against the corpus: the evidence is not contained in the
candidate alone, because the refutation is equality of its structural-DNA
payload with a prior payload.  The registry therefore stores the target, prior,
canonical payload, and grounding status.  The loader accepts the row only after
an independent structural-DNA grounding check, so the witness is a cache of a
corpus relation, not an extra theory object.

Padding, axiom-crossing, and unpaid discovery are stateless refusals.  Padding
is recomputed from the claimed classifier shift and its checked disagreement
support.  Axiom-crossing is recomputed from source text and kernel axiom
dependencies.  Unpaid discovery is recomputed from the claim's own
\texttt{PositiveDiscovery} fields: semantic naming certificate, row evidence,
ledger membership, debt, and scope seal.  Persisting these as another witness
family would duplicate the existing gate surface without adding soundness.

\section{Prime asymmetry}
\label{sec:pseudotheory-refutation-prime-asymmetry}

BEDC can automatically refute some claims because a refutation may be a finite
counter-reading: same structural payload as a prior, no classifier shift,
forbidden axiom dependency, or missing certificate data.  The opposite judgment
is different.  Saying that a chapter is worth admitting to the horizon is not
only the absence of these finite failures; it is a taste judgment about
stability, usefulness, and fit with the surrounding theory.

This is the prime asymmetry.  Composite-like failures may be found by an audit
gate.  A positive value judgment is not created by the complement of those
failures.  \texttt{ChapterTasteGate} supplies the carrier display/readback
discipline, but it does not replace human admission of taste-sensitive
stability.

\section{Carrier-repair boundary}
\label{sec:pseudotheory-refutation-carrier-repair-boundary}

The refutation map is apophatic.  It says why a claim may not be asserted as
discovery.  It does not say that the carrier must be replaced by a different
carrier, and it does not turn every refusal into a constructive repair recipe.
That boundary is essential: synonymy is evidence for demotion, not evidence
that the target and prior must be recoded into different domains; padding is
absence of paid classifier shift, not an invitation to alter the carrier until
the score improves; taste-sensitive stability is an admission judgment, not an
automatic rewrite target.

Thus the design rejects cataphatic carrier repair as the foundation of
pseudotheory refusal.  The gates remain refusal surfaces: structural-DNA
collision, classifier-bit failure, axiom dependency, unpaid certificate data,
and human-only taste admission.
