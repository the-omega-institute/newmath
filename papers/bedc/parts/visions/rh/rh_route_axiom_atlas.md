# RH-Route Axiom-Footprint Atlas

Machine-readable companion to the RH-route work. Adopts the
`bedc_mathlib_bridge` harness discipline (measured axiom footprints + first-class
boundary/refutation rows) for the mathlib-free, 0-axiom RH-route core.

**Single source of truth.** Route *prose* lives in the RH vision chapters
(`\autoref{ch:visions-rh-exploratory-route-ledger}` and the per-route chapters);
this file adds only the layer those chapters do not carry — a per-brick,
machine-checkable **axiom footprint** keyed by `lean_target`, with negative
results (`measured_boundary`, `refuted`) sitting beside the positive ones so the
whole line is visible together. Axiom measurement itself remains authoritative in
`bedc_ci.py axiom-purity --strict`; the footprints below are cross-checked
against it (and, on demand, against `#print axioms`) by
`tools/rh_route_atlas_check.py`.

`axiom_footprint: []` means the target is CIC-pure (0-axiom: no `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, or `Quot.sound`). This is the RH-route core invariant; a
non-empty footprint on a `formalized_theorem` row is a check failure, not a
recorded boundary. Boundaries record cost/reason and export **no**
axiom-carrying closed theorem.

## Row status vocabulary

- `formalized_theorem` — closed, 0-axiom theorem landed in `BEDC.Derived.RHRoute`.
- `conditional_theorem` — honest `_of_<obligation>` implication; the obligation is a named, **un-inhabited** capture field (not faked, not inhabited).
- `measured_boundary` — cannot be done 0-axiom here, is RH-equivalent (no leverage), or is blocked by a tooling limit; recorded with reason, **not** bridged.
- `refuted` — attempted and rejected (vacuous / wrong); kept visible as a bad-example with the lesson.
- `paper_principle` — documented in paper prose only; no Lean target claimed.

`rh_leverage`: `none` (a faithful restatement / diagnostic, no reduction of RH difficulty) · `diagnostic` (an honest conditional shape whose hypothesis is open) · `brick` (a genuine finite unconditional constraint).
