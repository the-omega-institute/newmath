\chapter{BEDC--ANT Interpretation Bridge and Tail Closure Equivalence}
\label{ch:visions-rh-bedc-ant-bridge}
\origin{ai}

This chapter records a faithful interpretation bridge from the classical
analytic number theory presentation of the zeta route into the existing BEDC
prime-window obligation package. It is a bridge and a conditional theorem, not
an unconditional RH theorem. The remaining obstruction is exactly the
off-critical infinite Euler-tail closure row: an off-critical principal
residual may not legally close unless its radial exponent is zero.

\section{Finite Euler Layers Do Not Cancel the Principal Part}
\label{sec:rh-bedc-ant-finite-layer-no-cancellation}

\begin{lemma}[Classical finite-layer holomorphy at nontrivial zeros]
\label{lem:rh-ant-finite-euler-layer-holomorphic}
Fix a finite prime bound $P_N$ and set
$$
D_{\leq P_N}(s)
=
\sum_{p\leq P_N}\sum_{k\geq1}\frac{\log p}{p^{ks}}
=
\sum_{p\leq P_N}\frac{\log p}{p^s-1}.
$$
The poles of $D_{\leq P_N}$ lie on $\Re(s)=0$. Hence, at every nontrivial
zero $\rho$ with $0<\Re(\rho)<1$, the finite layer is holomorphic and has
zero principal part:
$$
[D_{\leq P_N}]_{\rho}=0.
$$
Consequently the principal residual of $-\zeta'/\zeta$ at $\rho$ is carried
entirely by the tail ledger $\mathsf{TailLedger}_{>P_N}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
This is a classical analytic statement, not a BEDC finite-kernel theorem. For
each fixed prime $p$,
$$
\sum_{k\geq1}\frac{\log p}{p^{ks}}
=
\log p\cdot\frac{p^{-s}}{1-p^{-s}}
=
\frac{\log p}{p^s-1}.
$$
The denominator vanishes exactly when
$$
p^s=1,
\qquad
s=\frac{2\pi i n}{\log p}
\quad(n\in\mathbb Z),
$$
so every pole of each finite summand lies on the imaginary axis. A finite sum
has no additional poles. Therefore $D_{\leq P_N}$ is holomorphic at every
nontrivial zero in the open critical strip, and its Laurent principal part
there is zero. The pole of $-\zeta'/\zeta$ at $\rho$ cannot be cancelled by
the finite Euler layer; it is a tail-ledger residual.
\end{proof}

The lemma is the exact reason the bridge is honest. The finite layer has no
local analytic mechanism capable of cancelling the zero's principal part, but
this observation does not solve the global tail-rigidity problem.

\section{Tail Closure and RH}
\label{sec:rh-bedc-ant-tail-closure-rh}

\begin{definition}[ANT off-critical tail closure]
\label{def:rh-ant-no-off-critical-tail-closure}
The ANT statement
$$
\mathsf{NoOffCriticalTailClosure}_{\mathrm{ANT}}
$$
says that the infinite Euler tail may legally close a zero-compatible
principal residual only when the residual has no off-critical radial exponent:
for $\rho=1/2+r+i\gamma$, legal closure of
$$
R_{\rho}(u)=-m_{\rho}e^{u/2}e^{ru}e^{i\gamma u}
$$
forces $r=0$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tail closure is exactly RH strength]
\label{thm:rh-ant-no-off-critical-tail-closure-equivalence}
In the ANT reading,
$$
\mathsf{NoOffCriticalTailClosure}_{\mathrm{ANT}}
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathsf{RH}.
$$
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If RH holds, every nontrivial zero has $r=0$, so there is no off-critical
tail residual to close. Conversely, if
$\mathsf{NoOffCriticalTailClosure}_{\mathrm{ANT}}$ holds and an off-critical
zero existed, the finite Euler layer would be holomorphic at that zero by
\autoref{lem:rh-ant-finite-euler-layer-holomorphic}; the nonzero principal
part of $-\zeta'/\zeta$ would therefore have to live in the infinite tail,
contradicting the no-off-critical-tail closure row. Hence no off-critical
zero exists.
\end{proof}

Thus the bridge does not reduce RH to a weaker BEDC kernel fact. It identifies
the remaining unproved analytic obligation with RH-strength content.

\section{BEDC Conditional Readback}
\label{sec:rh-bedc-ant-bedc-readback}

Under $I$, the BEDC name for the O4 row is the following alias.
\leandef{BEDC.Derived.RHRoute.BedcAntBridge.NoOffCriticalTailClosure}

The aliased target is the existing prime-window statement-only obligation
recorded in \autoref{def:rh-prime-window-defect-zero-incompatibility}. It is
the row that says a located nontrivial zero cannot coexist with compatible
finite prime-window defect data. It is not inhabited here.
\leanstmt{BEDC.Derived.RHRoute.PrimeWindowDefect.PrimeWindowDefectZeroIncompatibility}

The checked BEDC theorem is the conditional handoff:
\begin{theorem}[BEDC tail closure package implies located ConstructiveRH]
\label{thm:rh-bedc-ant-conditional-constructive-rh}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.RHRoute.BedcAntBridge.noOffCriticalTailClosure\_imply\_constructiveRH}
\leanvariant{BEDC.Derived.RHRoute.PrimeWindowDefect.obligations\_imply\_constructiveRH}
Given the O4 alias
$\mathsf{NoOffCriticalTailClosure}$, the existing
$\mathsf{FiniteWindowGlobalCollapse}$ row, and the bookkeeping O1/O3 rows,
the BEDC prime-window obligation package implies the located
$\mathsf{ConstructiveRH}$ predicate.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Lean proof does not prove O4 and does not create a canonical obligation
witness. It builds a
$\mathsf{PrimeWindowRHObligations}$ record from the supplied assumptions and
then calls the already checked prime-window theorem
$\mathsf{obligations\_imply\_constructiveRH}$.
\end{proof}

The status is therefore precise:
$$
\mathsf{NoOffCriticalTailClosure}_{\mathrm{ANT}}
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathsf{RH},
\qquad
\bigl(
\mathsf{NoOffCriticalTailClosure}_{\mathrm{BEDC}}
\wedge
\mathsf{FiniteWindowGlobalCollapse}
\wedge
\mathsf{O1}
\wedge
\mathsf{O3}
\bigr)
\Rightarrow
\mathsf{ConstructiveRH}.
$$
The first line is a classical equivalence statement about the tail residual.
The second line is a kernel-checked conditional BEDC handoff. Neither line is
an unconditional RH theorem.
