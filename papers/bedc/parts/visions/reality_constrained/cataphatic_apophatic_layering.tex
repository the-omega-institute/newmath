\section{Cataphatic-apophatic layering}
\label{sec:cataphatic-apophatic-layering}
\origin{ai}

This section records the reality-constrained reading of the rational,
located, and apophatic layers. The named base line is the displayed
rational layer $\mathbb Q$: a value at this layer is a finite rational
name together with the exact equality relation already carried by
$\RatUp$. The cataphatic extension is the located-real layer: a real
presentation is read through witnessed rational approximations,
finite windows, moduli, and apartness evidence, not through an
uninspected quotient representative. The apophatic far end is the socket
at which a request for unsupported pointification, arbitrary supremum, or
global representative selection is refused rather than imported.

\begin{principle}[Non-collapse invariant]
\label{prin:cataphatic-apophatic-noncollapse-invariant}
Let $w$ be a located-real presentation. The formal non-collapse invariant
requires every rational $q$ to be separated from $w$ by displayed
apartness evidence and, more strongly, to keep producing such evidence
beyond any proposed equality modulus at a fixed displayed separation
scale for that $q$. With a separating rational metric kit, this
invariant rules out an exact rational retraction:
there is no $q:\mathbb Q$ such that $w$ is
$\mathsf{LRealEq}$-equivalent to the stationary rational presentation of
$q$.
\end{principle}
\concretizedIn{ch:concrete-instances-noncollapse-layer-namecert}{Finite NameCert carrier for the rational, located, and apophatic non-collapse layer.}

\begin{proof}
The Lean theorem cited above is the checked core. An assumed retraction
supplies an equality modulus. The invariant supplies, beyond that same
modulus, a rational snapshot of $w$ apart from $q$. The separating metric
law says that the displayed apartness and the displayed equality-close
row cannot both hold at that snapshot.
\end{proof}

The slogan for the layer is:
\emph{The Omega does not deny points; it refuses unjustified pointification.}
Thus the refusal is not a refusal of rational points, named located
windows, or certified limits. It is a refusal to collapse a located or
residual surface to a rational name, selected representative, arbitrary
supremum, or terminal object without the certificate that performs that
specific collapse.

The geometric language sometimes attached to this picture is only an
intuition. Calling the rational layer an ``axis'', calling located
apartness a transverse read, or drawing a three-way picture of rational
names, located approximation, and apophatic sockets does not assert an
inner product, a projection operation, a three-way law, a pro-object
construction, or a universal-property theorem about future refinements.
In BEDC terms, ``undiscovered axis'' means stability under later
universe-polymorphic or context-extension refinements, not a quantified
family of future directions.

The constructive boundary is the same one used by located analysis.
Located suprema, located cuts, and finite-window apartness may be
admitted when their moduli and witnesses are displayed. An arbitrary
supremum or unrestricted representative selector is not admitted by this
section. Such a selector would be an external supply socket in the sense
of \autoref{ch:visions-external-supply-without-axioms}, and any use of it
must remain visibly socketed through $\ApophaticFarEndSocketUp$ rather
than silently converted into a point of the cataphatic layer.

\begin{remark}[Formal scope]
\label{rem:cataphatic-apophatic-formal-scope}
The checked Lean content is the invariant-to-no-retraction theorem and
the exact-kit boundary theorem: every point in the exact rational metric
kit retracts to a rational snapshot, so that kit does not carry a
non-collapse witness. This section does not claim a checked $\sqrt2$
construction, a diagonal irrational construction, a located-supremum
theorem, a rational trichotomy theorem, or an apophatic
universal-property theorem. Those are separate arithmetic and interface
targets.
\end{remark}
