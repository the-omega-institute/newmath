\begin{principle}[Static Green-exactness of right-boundary flux]
\label{prin:zeckendorf-static-green-exact-right-boundary-flux}
For any finite weighted graph decomposed into an internal block set $I$
and a deleted boundary block $B$, let $L_B$ be the Dirichlet reduced
Laplacian on $I$ and let $b_B$ be the static cut-source whose $i$th
coordinate is the total edge weight from $i\in I$ into $B$. Then
$$
  L_B\mathbf{1}_I=b_B.
$$
Indeed, the $i$th coordinate of $L_B\mathbf{1}_I$ is the full weighted
degree of $i$ minus the weights from $i$ to the other retained vertices,
which leaves exactly the cut weight from $i$ to the deleted boundary.
Thus every static Dirichlet cut-source is Laplacian-exact and has zero
class in $\operatorname{coker}(L_B)$.

Order the Window-six edge-flux blocks as
$(U_2,U_1,U_L,U_R)$, with microscopic volumes
$$
  (|U_2|,|U_1|,|U_L|,|U_R|)
  =
  (27,22,9,6).
$$
The edge-count skeleton is
$$
  E
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
  28&63&23&20\\
  63&21&21&6\\
  23&21&2&6\\
  20&6&6&2
  \end{pmatrix}.
$$
Deleting the right block gives the reduced Laplacian
$$
  L_R
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
  106&-63&-23\\
  -63&90&-21\\
  -23&-21&50
  \end{pmatrix},
$$
where each diagonal entry is the total edge count from the retained
block to all other blocks. The right-boundary cut vector seen from the
retained blocks is
$$
  b_R
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
  20\\
  6\\
  6
  \end{pmatrix}.
$$
It satisfies the integral exactness identity
$$
  L_R
  \begin{pmatrix}
  1\\
  1\\
  1
  \end{pmatrix}
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
  20\\
  6\\
  6
  \end{pmatrix}
  =
  b_R.
$$
Thus the static right-boundary escape source is a reduced-Laplacian
image by the general Dirichlet cut identity. It is a Green-exact source
before any localization, and therefore also over
$R_\zeta=\mathbb{Z}[1/6]$.
\end{principle}

\begin{theorem}[Static-Green separation for Dirichlet cut-flux]
\label{thm:zeckendorf-static-right-boundary-source-green-zero}
The Window-six Green layer detected by the reduced Laplacian is
nontrivial, but the static right-boundary cut-flux source does not
activate it. More precisely,
$$
  \det L_R
  =
  123336
  =
  2^3\cdot3^3\cdot571,
$$
so after localization at $6$ the non-invertible determinant factor is the
$571$-Green factor. The class of $b_R$ in the corresponding cokernel is
zero. Consequently, for the static low-energy electromagnetic readout
whose Green input is the right-boundary cut source, the Green coefficient
in
$$
  D_{6,\alpha}^{G}
  =
  D_{6,\alpha}^{\star}
  +
  q_3\varphi^{-37}
  +
  O(\varphi^{-47})
$$
is $q_3=0$. This does not remove the $571$-Green layer from dynamic
readouts. For the coarse Markov relaxation kernel determined by the same
edge skeleton,
$$
  T
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
  28/81&7/18&23/162&10/81\\
  21/44&7/22&7/44&1/22\\
  23/54&7/18&2/27&1/9\\
  5/9&1/6&1/6&1/9
  \end{pmatrix},
$$
with stationary weight
$$
  \pi=(27/64,22/64,9/64,6/64),
$$
the centered right-block observable
$$
  f_R=\mathbf{1}_R-\frac{3}{32}\mathbf{1}
$$
has Green response
$$
  Zf_R
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
  -58991/877056\\
  -41093/292352\\
  -69167/877056\\
  821233/877056
  \end{pmatrix},
  \qquad
  Z=(I-T+\mathbf{1}\pi^T)^{-1}.
$$
Since $877056=2^9\cdot3\cdot571$ and
$292352=2^9\cdot571$, this centered relaxation observable does read the
$571$-Green layer. Its Green susceptibility is
$$
  \chi_R
  =
  \langle f_R,Zf_R\rangle_\pi
  =
  \frac{821233}{9355264}
  =
  \frac{821233}{2^{14}\cdot571}.
$$
The corresponding Green--Kubo response is
$$
  G_R
  =
  2\chi_R-\operatorname{Var}_\pi(f_R)
  =
  \frac{423817}{4677632}
  =
  \frac{423817}{2^{13}\cdot571}.
$$
The static variance baseline is
$$
  \operatorname{Var}_\pi(f_R)
  =
  \frac{87}{1024}.
$$
Therefore the fully activated Green-excess coefficient is
$$
  \Delta_R(1)
  =
  G_R-\operatorname{Var}_\pi(f_R)
  =
  \frac{26401}{4677632}
  =
  \frac{26401}{2^{13}\cdot571}.
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By
\autoref{prin:zeckendorf-static-green-exact-right-boundary-flux},
$b_R=L_R(1,1,1)^T$. This is the Window-six instance of the general
Dirichlet cut identity $L_B\mathbf{1}_I=b_B$. Hence $b_R$ is zero in the
cokernel of $L_R$ over $\mathbb{Z}$ and over $R_\zeta$. The determinant
computation displays the $571$-Green layer after the powers of $2$ and
$3$ have become units, but exact sources have zero class in that layer.

Equivalently, modulo $571$ one may use the left cokernel functional
$$
  \ell=(448,409,1).
$$
Then
$$
  \ell(b_R)
  =
  448\cdot20+409\cdot6+6
  =
  11420
  =
  20\cdot571,
$$
so $\ell(b_R)\equiv0\pmod{571}$. This modular vanishing is the residue
shadow of the integral exactness identity above. Therefore the static
right-boundary source contributes no $\varphi^{-37}$ Green correction.

The relaxation statement is a direct computation from the displayed
stochastic matrix. The row sums of $T$ are $1$, and $\pi T=\pi$ because
the row weights are the block volumes divided by $64$. Applying the
fundamental Green operator $Z=(I-T+\mathbf{1}\pi^T)^{-1}$ to the centered
right-block observable gives the displayed vector. Its denominators have
one factor of $571$, so the dynamic centered response is not killed by
the static exactness identity. The displayed values of $\chi_R$ and
$G_R$ are obtained by the definitions
$\chi_R=\sum_i\pi_i f_R(i)(Zf_R)(i)$ and
$G_R=2\chi_R-\sum_i\pi_i f_R(i)^2$; their denominators again contain the
single $571$ factor. Subtracting the static variance baseline isolates
the Green-excess coefficient $\Delta_R(1)$. Thus $q_3=0$ is the static
cut-flux conclusion for the infrared readout, while a running or
relaxation response must use a separate Green-excess coefficient, for
example
$$
  D_{6,\alpha}(z)
  =
  D_{6,\alpha}^{\star}
  +
  \Delta_R(z)\varphi^{-37}
  +
  O(\varphi^{-47}).
$$
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral response interpolation]
\label{thm:zeckendorf-spectral-response-interpolation}
The family of
\autoref{def:zeckendorf-window-six-spectral-response-family} interpolates
between the static cut-flux readout and the first Window-six
Green-excess readout:
$$
  \alpha_{Z6}^{-1}(0)
  =
  137.035999177006279014596667\ldots,
$$
and
$$
  \alpha_{Z6}^{-1}(1)
  =
  137.035999177006153002727216\ldots.
$$
Moreover $\Delta_R(z)$ is a rational spectral response of the four-state
Window-six Markov kernel. The characteristic polynomial is
$$
  \chi_T(t)
  =
  (t-1)
  \frac{48114t^3+7263t^2-506t-55}{48114},
$$
and the denominator
$$
  55z^3+506z^2-7263z-48114
$$
in $\Delta_R(z)$ is the reciprocal form of the non-stationary cubic
factor. Thus the $571$ denominator in the fully activated Green-excess
coefficient is the arithmetic trace of the Window-six Green spectrum, not
a static correction to $\alpha_{Z6}^{-1}(0)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At $z=0$, \autoref{def:zeckendorf-window-six-green-running-seed} gives
$\Delta_R(0)=0$, so $D_{6,\alpha}(0)=D_{6,\alpha}^{\star}$ and the
readout is the static cut-flux phase-reservoir value. At $z=1$, the same
definition gives
$$
  \Delta_R(1)=\frac{26401}{2^{13}\cdot571},
$$
which yields the displayed endpoint after substitution into
\autoref{def:zeckendorf-window-six-spectral-response-family}.

For monotonicity, differentiate the displayed rational formula for
$\Delta_R(z)$:
$$
  \Delta_R'(z)
  =
  \frac{
    3(539165z^4+26109270z^3+421527321z^2
    +2355372756z+1286087220)
  }{
    512(55z^3+506z^2-7263z-48114)^2
  }.
$$
The derivative is positive on $0\leq z\leq1$. Since
\autoref{thm:zeckendorf-denominator-predictivity-requirement} shows that
the readout $R(D)$ decreases with $D$, activating Green-excess decreases
$\alpha_{Z6}^{-1}$ and increases the coupling. The characteristic
polynomial statement follows by computing the characteristic polynomial
of the displayed coarse Markov matrix $T$ in
\autoref{thm:zeckendorf-static-right-boundary-source-green-zero}. The
reciprocal cubic identity is exactly
$$
  55z^3+506z^2-7263z-48114
  =
  -z^3
  \left(
    48114z^{-3}+7263z^{-2}-506z^{-1}-55
  \right).
$$
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Golden--Markov audit field]
\label{thm:zeckendorf-golden-markov-audit-field}
Let $\lambda$ be any non-stationary eigenvalue of the Window-six coarse
Markov kernel $T$, so that
$$
  48114\lambda^3+7263\lambda^2-506\lambda-55=0.
$$
The dynamic spectral response is naturally read over the compositum
$$
  K_6^{\mathrm{audit}}
  =
  \mathbb{Q}(\varphi,\lambda).
$$
The golden phase scale contributes the quadratic field
$\mathbb{Q}(\varphi)=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt5)$, while the non-stationary Markov
spectrum contributes the cubic field $\mathbb{Q}(\lambda)$. When these
two fields are linearly disjoint, $K_6^{\mathrm{audit}}$ has degree $6$
over $\mathbb{Q}$ with basis
$$
  1,\ \varphi,\ \lambda,\ \lambda\varphi,\ \lambda^2,\ \lambda^2\varphi.
$$
Thus the Window-six response separates into a static golden phase layer
and a dynamic cubic Markov spectral layer. The $Z_6\to U(1)$ phase gate,
the shell $\varphi^{-6}$, and the degree-six audit field are three
readings of the same two-by-three structure: quadratic golden scale
times cubic Markov spectrum.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The golden field has degree $2$ because $\varphi$ satisfies
$x^2-x-1=0$. The Markov spectral field is generated by a root of the
non-stationary cubic factor displayed in
\autoref{thm:zeckendorf-spectral-response-interpolation}. Linear
disjointness gives the product degree $2\cdot3=6$ and the displayed
tensor-product basis. The static readout uses only the golden phase shell
and the $Z_6$ phase gate. The dynamic Green-excess denominator uses the
reciprocal cubic spectral factor. Hence the unified spectral response
lives over the compositum rather than over the golden field alone.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Static denominator closed; Green-excess remains separate]
\label{thm:zeckendorf-static-denominator-green-running-separation}
The low-energy static denominator is closed at the static cut-flux layer:
$$
  D_{6,\alpha}^{\star}
  =
  47+\varphi^{-7}-\frac12\varphi^{-17}
  +\frac89\varphi^{-27}.
$$
The first candidate Green-running coefficient is the centered
Green-excess kernel
$$
  q_{3,\mathrm{run}}(z)=\Delta_R(z),
  \qquad
  \Delta_R(1)=\frac{26401}{2^{13}\cdot571}.
$$
Thus the subsequent certificate problem is not whether to add another
static term to $D_{6,\alpha}^{\star}$, but whether a physical
Green-excess activation parameter $z(Q)$ and higher-window data replace
the fixed Window-six seed by a refinement flow
$$
  X_6\rightsquigarrow X_{m(Q)}.
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The static part is the conclusion of
\autoref{thm:zeckendorf-static-right-boundary-source-green-zero}: every
static Dirichlet cut-source is Laplacian-exact, and the right-boundary
source therefore gives $q_3=0$ for the infrared readout. The dynamic
coefficient is the Green--Kubo response after subtracting the static
variance baseline of the centered right-boundary observable. Since
$\Delta_R(0)=0$, the Green-excess seed contributes no correction at the
static point. For $0<z\leq1$ the displayed denominator is negative while
the polynomial $3z(1595z^2+24477z+26730)$ is positive; the leading minus
sign therefore makes $\Delta_R(z)>0$. Because the readout $R(D)$ is
decreasing in $D$, the Green-excess term lowers $\alpha^{-1}$ and raises
the coupling. At nonzero scale the effect is controlled by Green-excess
activation and window-refinement data, not by a change to the static
denominator.
\end{proof}
