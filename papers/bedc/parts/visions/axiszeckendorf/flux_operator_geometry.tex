\begin{principle}[Window-six flux-operator geometry]
\label{prin:zeckendorf-window-six-flux-operator-geometry}
The full edge-flux dynamics should not be read as an ordinary hidden
metric geometry. At the stable-address level, the full edge-flux action
saturates the operator algebra on the \(21\)-dimensional visible
Window-six space, and it preserves no nonzero invariant bilinear form and
no nonzero invariant multiplication. Thus the physical representation
cannot be based on a fixed hidden inner product, symplectic form, bracket,
or commutative product. The natural object is instead a flux-operator
geometry: stable Zeckendorf addresses, transition operators, phase gates,
boundary escape flux, and golden-shell projection. For electromagnetic
targets this supports an impedance or coupling readout rather than a pure
curvature-scalar readout.
\end{principle}
