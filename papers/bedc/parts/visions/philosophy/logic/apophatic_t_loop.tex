\subsection{Apophatic T-loops}
\label{subsec:philosophy-apophatic-t-loops}

The two-loop discipline separates a productive ground closure from
the meta closures refused by Tarski-style obstruction. The gap
discipline then explains how a commitment that exceeds displayed
records remains usable without being promoted to an internal theorem.
The apophatic $T$-loop is the composite of these two readings: a
records-side history may return to itself through a boundary
inscription, but the far-end supply position that makes the return
possible is not absorbed into the history.

\begin{definition}[Formal strange loop]
\label{def:philosophy-formal-strange-loop}
A \emph{formal strange loop} is a self-reference pattern in which the
described layer and the describing layer are consumed as the same
object-layer stratum. In such a loop the system can use its own
description as a total truth, runtime, explanation, or decision
predicate for the layer being described.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Reality-why gap]
\label{def:philosophy-reality-why-gap}
Let $C$ be a closed observational context, let $h\in\Hist$ be a
records-side history visible to $C$, and suppose that
$\operatorname{Meaning}_{C,\Pi}(x,\ell,\kappa)$ holds in the sense of
\autoref{def:reality-constrained-meaning}. A \emph{reality-why gap} at
this site is the failure of the closed records of $C$ to discharge the
question
$$
\mathsf{Why}_{\mathrm{real}}
\bigl(\operatorname{Meaning}_{C,\Pi}(x,\ell,\kappa)\bigr)
$$
while the corresponding label, prediction, action, or proof use
continues to be consumed by later records.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Apophatic T-loop]
\label{def:philosophy-apophatic-t-loop}
An \emph{apophatic $T$-loop} is a ledgered composite
$$
\begin{gathered}
h
\longrightarrow
\kappa
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Meaning}_{C,\Pi}(x,\ell,\kappa)
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{RealityConstraint}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{GAP}_{T}
\bigl(\mathsf{Why}_{\mathrm{real}}\bigr)\\
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{InscriptionPoint}(\mathsf{T},C,t)
\longrightarrow
h',
\end{gathered}
$$
where $h,h'\in\Hist$, $h'$ is a records-side successor or refinement of
$h$, the $\mathsf{GAP}_{T}$ row exposes a non-internalized far-end
supply position, and the inscription point is a local records-side
entry rather than $\mathsf{T}$ itself.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Apophatic T-loop theorem]
\label{thm:philosophy-apophatic-t-loop}
Assume a closed observational context $C$, a visible history
$h\in\Hist$, a displayed record $x$, a classifier $\kappa$ with
$\kappa(x)=\ell$, and a reality-constrained meaning packet
$\operatorname{Meaning}_{C,\Pi}(x,\ell,\kappa)$. If the associated
reality-why question is not closed by the records and closed-generation
rules of $C$, while the commitment is still used for continuation, then
the admissible return to records has the form of an apophatic $T$-loop.
This loop is a stratified return, not a formal strange loop.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By \autoref{def:reality-constrained-meaning}, the meaning packet
contains a source record, a classifier, a reality constraint, a
continuation consequence, and a gap ledger. The equation
$\kappa(x)=\ell$ supplies only the classifier field. The reality-facing
use of the label also requires preservation of the $\Pi$-signature,
downstream continuation, and exposure of any unclosed source or residue.

By \autoref{def:philosophy-reality-why-gap}, the why-question at the
site is not discharged by the closed records of $C$, but the
commitment is still consumed by later continuation. Hence the missing
discharge cannot be recorded as an internal theorem without confusing
unclosed supply with generated content. By the gap-socket discipline of
\autoref{def:philosophy-gap-socket} and the gap--$T$ synthesis of
\autoref{thm:philosophy-gap-t-boundary-synthesis}, the missing
discharge must instead be exposed as a socket whose far end is the
apophatic supply position $\mathsf{T}$.

By \autoref{thm:philosophy-t-cannot-be-internalized}, $\mathsf{T}$ does
not enter the object layer as a state, record, rule, axiom, or carrier.
By \autoref{def:philosophy-inscription-point}, the only admissible
records-side return is a local inscription site at which the
forward-binding supply is actualized for the history. Thus the return
has the displayed composite
$$
\begin{gathered}
h
\longrightarrow
\kappa
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Meaning}_{C,\Pi}(x,\ell,\kappa)
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{RealityConstraint}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{GAP}_{T}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{InscriptionPoint}(\mathsf{T},C,t)
\longrightarrow
h'.
\end{gathered}
$$

It remains to rule out formal layer collapse. If the composite were a
formal strange loop in the sense of
\autoref{def:philosophy-formal-strange-loop}, then the records-side
history could consume the boundary supply position, its description,
or its truth condition as object-layer data of the same stratum. That
would either internalize $\mathsf{T}$, contradicting
\autoref{thm:philosophy-t-cannot-be-internalized}, or close the meta
loop over truth, runtime, explanation, or total decision, contradicting
\autoref{thm:philosophy-meta-must-stay-open} and
\autoref{cor:philosophy-only-ground-closes}. Therefore the composite
is a stratified return through inscription, not a formal strange loop.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[T-loop productivity]
\label{cor:philosophy-t-loop-productivity}
An apophatic $T$-loop is productive exactly when it returns as a
ledgered records-side refinement and keeps $\mathsf{T}$ outside the
object layer.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
If the loop returns by a ledgered refinement $h'$, the observational
history has gained a visible record. If $\mathsf{T}$ remains outside
the object layer, the meta loop remains open. Conversely, identifying
$\mathsf{T}$ with $h$, with an observation record, or with a truth
predicate would erase the boundary and turn the return into the
formal layer collapse excluded by
\autoref{thm:philosophy-apophatic-t-loop}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Conscious reading]
\label{cor:philosophy-conscious-t-loop-reading}
When the source history factors through a focused conscious record,
the apophatic $T$-loop gives a consciousness-like readback without
adding subject-substance.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
A focused conscious record is records-side data: generated history,
local continuation trace, selector ledger, and self-proxy. Applying
\autoref{thm:philosophy-apophatic-t-loop} to such a source changes
only the site of inscription. The far-end position remains
non-internalized, and no extra subject parameter is added to $\Cont$ or
to $\Hist$ identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Meaning-bearing focused readback]
\label{def:philosophy-meaning-bearing-focused-readback}
Let $L_N=(I,H,\Theta,F,S,D,G,R,J,Q,\rho,A)$ be a focused ledger, let
$C_F=(H(F),\Theta(F),S(F),\rho)$ be its focused chain, and suppose that
$x$ is a displayed record in $H(F)$. A \emph{meaning-bearing focused
readback} occurs at $x$ when $S(F)$ selects a classifier $\kappa$ with
$\kappa(x)=\ell$ and the selected assignment is carried by
$\operatorname{Meaning}_{C,\Pi}(x,\ell,\kappa)$. The self-proxy $\rho$
then reads a reality-constrained meaning packet at the focused
endpoint; it does not create the reality constraint and it does not
replace the packet by a private phenomenal object.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Reality--meaning--consciousness relation]
\label{thm:philosophy-reality-meaning-consciousness-relation}
In a focused observational context, reality constrains classifiers into
reality-constrained meaning; meaning that enters the focused ledger is
readable by the self-proxy as a conscious record; and any question or
action that asks for the ultimate reality source of that meaning can
return to records only through an apophatic $T$-loop when the
reality-why question is not internally closed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By \autoref{def:reality-constrained-meaning}, a label is meaningful in
the relevant sense only as a packet containing a source record,
classifier, reality constraint, continuation consequence, and gap
ledger. Thus the transition from classification to meaning is already a
transition through reality constraint rather than through the bare
equation $\kappa(x)=\ell$.

If the displayed record lies in a focused ledger, then
\autoref{def:records-side-conscious-carrier} identifies the records-side
carrier of the conscious reading as
$$
\mathsf{ConsciousRecord}(L_N,F)
:=
(H(F),\Theta(F),S(F),\rho).
$$
Together with
\autoref{def:philosophy-meaning-bearing-focused-readback}, this says
that the self-proxy reads a selected meaning-bearing record at the local
endpoint. The conscious record is therefore a records-side reading of
reality-constrained meaning, not an independent source of the
constraint.

Now suppose the focused record continues by question, prediction,
explanation, action, or proof use, and the continuation asks why the
meaning is reality-supported beyond the displayed records. If that
reality-why question is not closed by the records and rules of the
context, \autoref{thm:philosophy-apophatic-t-loop} applies to the
meaning packet. The admissible composite is
$$
\begin{gathered}
\mathsf{Reality}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{RealityConstraint}
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Meaning}_{C,\Pi}(x,\ell,\kappa)
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{ConsciousRecord}(L_N,F)\\
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{Question}/\mathsf{Action}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{GAP}_{T}
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{InscriptionPoint}(\mathsf{T},C_F,t)
\longrightarrow
\mathsf{ConsciousRecord}(L'_N,F').
\end{gathered}
$$
By \autoref{thm:selfcenter-local-inscription-not-t}, the inscription
side of the conscious position is local inscription rather than
$\mathsf{T}$ itself. Hence the loop relates reality, meaning, and
conscious readback through a stratified boundary return, not by making
consciousness a creator of reality or by making $\mathsf{T}$ an
object-level answer.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Meaning is readable reality constraint]
\label{cor:philosophy-meaning-readable-reality-constraint}
Meaning that reaches conscious readback is a reality-constrained
structure readable by a focused self-proxy.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The reality constraint belongs to the meaning packet by
\autoref{def:reality-constrained-meaning}. The focused self-proxy is a
component of the records-side conscious carrier by
\autoref{def:records-side-conscious-carrier}. Therefore a
meaning-bearing focused readback reads the constrained packet locally; it
does not convert meaning into unconstrained interior labeling.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Consciousness does not create reality]
\label{cor:philosophy-consciousness-does-not-create-reality}
The records-side conscious carrier does not create the reality source of
the meaning it reads.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The carrier
$\mathsf{ConsciousRecord}(L_N,F)=(H(F),\Theta(F),S(F),\rho)$ supplies a
focused history, trace, selector, and self-proxy. None of these fields
is the reality constraint field of
$\operatorname{Meaning}_{C,\Pi}(x,\ell,\kappa)$. The constraint is part
of the reality-constrained meaning packet itself, while $\rho$ reads the
selected packet at the focused endpoint. Thus conscious readback is not
creation of reality.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Question and action expose the socket]
\label{cor:philosophy-question-action-expose-socket}
When a focused conscious record asks why a meaning is reality-supported
beyond its displayed records, the unclosed route is a socket and an
inscription point, not an internal final answer.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The question targets the unclosed reality-why component of the
meaning-bearing continuation. By
\autoref{thm:philosophy-reality-meaning-consciousness-relation}, if the
records and rules do not close that component, the admissible return is
through $\mathsf{GAP}_{T}$ and
$\mathsf{InscriptionPoint}(\mathsf{T},C_F,t)$. By
\autoref{thm:philosophy-t-cannot-be-internalized}, this return cannot be
an internal object-level answer identical with $\mathsf{T}$.
\end{proof}
