It sits over the integer source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-int-namecert}, the rational comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the dyadic arithmetic route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the stream-name and regular-sequence readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Archimedean floor carrier]
\label{def:archimedean-floor-carrier}
An $\ArchimedeanFloorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(I,J,Q,D,S,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\IntUp$ source row, displaying the lower integer candidate and its successor relation.
\item $J$ is the successor-bound row, displaying the upper integer candidate as the successor of the lower row and refusing any detached integer choice.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ comparison row, where integer entries are read as rationals before any real-facing comparison is formed.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ comparison row, carrying the finite dyadic budget used to inspect the two rational inequalities.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, exposing the regular rational name whose finite window is being compared.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, certifying that the compared regular sequence is the displayed real-name endpoint seen by downstream consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the integer, rational, dyadic, stream-name, regular-sequence, real-seal, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the integer bracket, rational comparison, dyadic comparison, regular-sequence readback, real seal, transport, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance row binding the integer bracket to the L10 Real and Completion route.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ArchimedeanFloorUp}$ row over $\ArchimedeanFloorUp$, $\IntUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,J,Q,D,S,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for total trichotomy of real names, quotient real equality, selected limits, ambient real completeness, a global floor operator, or an integer-part handoff that skips the displayed window.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Archimedean floor NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:archimedean-floor-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ArchimedeanFloorUp$ carrier
$$
F=(I,J,Q,D,S,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ArchimedeanFloorUp}$ surface consists exactly of lower-integer source exposure, successor upper-row exposure, rational comparison exposure, dyadic comparison replay, stream-name and regular-sequence readback, real-seal exposure, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, provenance, local naming, and non-escape from total real trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected limits, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, host floor functions, and any integer-part route outside the displayed bracket.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:archimedean-floor-carrier}. The rows $I$ and $J$ are the only integer rows, so the candidate floor and its successor are displayed before any comparison is read.

The comparison rows are $Q$ and $D$. They convert the integer bracket into rational and dyadic inspection data, giving finite evidence for the lower and successor bounds without invoking trichotomy of arbitrary real names.

The readback rows $S$ and $R$ then identify the compared object with the displayed regular sequence and its real seal. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same window, so no host floor operator, selected limit, quotient real equality, or ambient real-completeness claim is exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Archimedean floor window extraction]
\label{thm:archimedean-floor-window-extraction}

$$
\IntUp \longmapsto \RatUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp.

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The rational and dyadic comparison rows $Q$ and $D$ are then read before the real endpoint is sealed. These rows provide the finite lower-bound and successor-upper-bound checks consumed by the window.
