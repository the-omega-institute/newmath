Following Bishop and Bridges and Troelstra--van Dalen, the $\BishopChoiceSequenceUp$ packet records a finite observation surface for choice sequences without importing a selected infinite choice function. It sits over the finite observation interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the tail-equivalence boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-tail-equivalence-namecert}. The packet is designed for Real and Completion consumers that need a finite observed window and a stable handoff, not an ambient equality of streams.

\begin{definition}[Bishop choice-sequence carrier]
\label{def:bishop-choice-sequence-carrier}
A $\BishopChoiceSequenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(S,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the finite schedule row listing the observation indices and tolerance requests that have actually been opened.
\item $W$ is the observed $\StreamNameUp$ window read from that schedule, with no coordinate for an unobserved tail.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger attached to the same finite schedule and observed window.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row produced from the displayed window and tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $S,W,D,R$ have been consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the schedule, observed window, tolerance ledger, readback, seal, and local certificate rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for schedule opening, finite observation, dyadic tolerance checking, regular readback, and Real-name handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopChoiceSequenceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyTailEquivalenceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopChoiceSequenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for countable choice, quotient stream equality, a selected limit, trichotomy, host equality of real names, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop choice-sequence window stability]
\label{thm:bishop-choice-sequence-window-stability}
Let $X=(S,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\BishopChoiceSequenceUp$ carrier. If a later finite schedule extends the inspection while preserving the already displayed schedule row $S$, observed $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$, and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger $D$, then the original regular readback row $R$ and terminal $\RealUp$ seal row $E$ remain the same rows under the displayed $H,C,P,N$ transport.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-choice-sequence-carrier}. The only observed analytic rows before transport are $S,W,D,R,E$, so the classifier has no hidden stream tail from which to obtain a second readback.

The later schedule preserves $S,W,D$. Therefore the finite observation and dyadic tolerance data used to form $R$ are unchanged inside the packet.

The transport row $H$ compares the displayed rows componentwise, and the replay row $C$ replays the same finite route. The provenance and local naming rows $P,N$ name that same packet, so the terminal seal $E$ travels with $R$ rather than being recomputed from unobserved choice data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop choice-sequence Real-name handoff]
\label{thm:bishop-choice-sequence-real-name-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$ name exported by an accepted $\BishopChoiceSequenceUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
S\longmapsto W\longmapsto D\longmapsto R\longmapsto E.
$$
The handoff from finite choice-sequence observation to a Real-facing consumer is therefore mediated by the observed $\StreamNameUp$ window, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row before the terminal seal is available.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier definition. The terminal seal row $E$ occurs after the schedule, observed window, tolerance ledger, and regular readback rows, and the classifier reads no separate Real constructor.

Use the window-stability theorem for later observations. Preserving the displayed finite window and tolerance ledger preserves $R$ and $E$, so a Real-facing consumer cannot replace the finite route by a selected limit or by equality of host streams.

Thus the handoff is the displayed chain through $S,W,D,R,E$. The packet supplies a local name for the finite observation route and leaves ambient completeness, quotient stream equality, countable choice, trichotomy, and selected limits outside the certificate.
\end{proof}
