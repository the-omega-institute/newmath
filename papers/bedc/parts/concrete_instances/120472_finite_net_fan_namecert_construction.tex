\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FiniteNetFanUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-finite-net-fan-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Brouwer and Bishop--Bridges finite-net arguments, the $\FiniteNetFanUp$ packet records a finite ProbeBundle fan handoff for total-boundedness and compact-uniform consumers. It sits between the finite-net source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the compact-metric consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-name window of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular-rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite fan of mesh-controlled probes; it is not open-cover compactness, selected subcover extraction, quotient real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.

\begin{definition}[FiniteNetFan carrier]
\label{def:finite-net-fan-carrier}
A $\FiniteNetFanUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(B,D,J,W,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\ProbeBundleUp$ center bundle, recording the finite family of probe centers, membership rows, and bundle indices used by the net.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ mesh-radius ledger, recording the positive dyadic tolerance and every comparison budget consumed by the fan.
\item $J$ is the finite fan-refinement row, listing the bounded refinement branches, parent-child mesh inclusions, and coverage witnesses read from the finite net.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row through which each probe center and refinement branch exposes only the finite observations requested by $D$.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular rational windows attached to $W$ and to the dyadic mesh ledger.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for the center and branch observations after $B,D,J,W,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the center bundle, mesh radius, fan refinement, window, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through $D$, then through $B,J,W,Q,E,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteNetFanUp$, $\ProbeBundleUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteNetFanUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows and their componentwise transports. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, a selected subcover, quotient real equality, a hidden completion theorem, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FiniteNetFan NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-net-fan-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\FiniteNetFanUp$ carrier $F=(B,D,J,W,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteNetFanUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed rows, center-bundle visibility through $B$, mesh-radius accounting through $D$, finite fan-refinement visibility through $J$, StreamName window exactness through $W$, RegSeqRat readback through $Q$, terminal Real sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:finite-net-fan-carrier}. The packet exposes exactly the center bundle, mesh-radius ledger, finite fan-refinement row, window row, readback row, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

The finite-net obligations are read from $B,D,J$. The bundle $B$ displays the centers, $D$ displays the mesh radius and comparison budget, and $J$ displays the bounded refinement branches and coverage witnesses.

The Real-facing obligations are read from $W,Q,E$. StreamName windows expose the finite observations, RegSeqRat readback turns those windows into regular rational rows, and the Real seal is downstream of that readback.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the route. Since no carrier coordinate names open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness, none of those data can be exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FiniteNetFan ProbeBundle handoff]
\label{thm:finite-net-fan-probebundle-handoff}
Every total-boundedness, compact-metric, Real, or compact-uniform consumer of an accepted $\FiniteNetFanUp$ carrier
$$
F=(B,D,J,W,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
reads a finite fan only through the route
$$
\ProbeBundleUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
For each positive mesh request, the consumer receives the displayed probe centers from $B$, the dyadic mesh radius from $D$, the finite refinement branches from $J$, the finite observation windows from $W$, the regular rational readback from $Q$, and the terminal seal from $E$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct over the finite ProbeBundle fan recorded by $B$ and $J$. The empty branch has no exported center, and the carrier still exposes the mesh ledger $D$, structural rows, and local name before a consumer can read the packet.

For a branch extension, project the next probe center from $B$ and the next refinement row from $J$. The mesh comparison for that branch is read from $D$, so the branch cannot be consumed as a detached point selection.

The branch then passes through $W,Q,E$. The StreamName window supplies only the finite observations requested by the mesh ledger, RegSeqRat readback records the regular rational values, and the Real seal is terminal for that branch.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route for every branch. Thus total-boundedness and compact-uniform consumers receive finite ProbeBundle fan data without importing open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
