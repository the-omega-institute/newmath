It sits after the compact-metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the total-bounded finite-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Finite epsilon-net chain carrier]
\label{def:finite-epsilon-net-chain-carrier}
A $\FiniteEpsilonNetChainUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(X,K,T,D,S,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the compact metric source row, read through $\CompactMetricUp$ before any finite-net chain is inspected.
\item $K$ is the total-bounded finite-net family. For each displayed tolerance cell it exposes the corresponding $\TotallyBoundedUp$ center bundle and coverage ledger.
\item $T$ is the finite tolerance chain, ordered by the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ comparison rows that relate successive epsilon levels.
\item $D$ records the dyadic slack and mesh-budget rows used when the chain refines one finite net into the next.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row consumed by completion-facing readers before any regular rational readback is available.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the displayed stream windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $L$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $X,K,T,D,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact-metric, total-bounded, dyadic, stream, regular-rational, Real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite-chain request through $X,K,T,D,S,R,L,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteEpsilonNetChainUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteEpsilonNetChainUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $X,K,T,D,S,R,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an ambient open cover, a host enumeration of centers, a selected completion point, quotient equality of streams, countable choice, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite epsilon-net chain NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-epsilon-net-chain-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteEpsilonNetChainUp$ carrier $E=(X,K,T,D,S,R,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteEpsilonNetChainUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the compact metric source, the total-bounded finite-net family, the dyadic tolerance chain, mesh-budget rows, finite StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, terminal Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every compactness or completion-facing obligation must read the finite route
$$
\CompactMetricUp,\quad
\TotallyBoundedUp,\quad
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad
\StreamNameUp,\quad
\RegSeqRatUp,\quad
\RealUp,\quad
\BHist,\quad
\hsame,\quad
\Cont,\quad
\Pkg,\quad
\NameCert .
$$
No obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-epsilon-net-chain-carrier}. Its compactness source is $X$, its finite-net data are $K$, and the only refinement data are the dyadic tolerance chain $T$ and mesh-budget rows $D$.

Read the completion-facing boundary next. The finite StreamName windows $S$ and RegSeqRat readback $R$ are displayed before the terminal Real seal $L$ can be consumed, so the Real handoff remains downstream of finite observations.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only the same packet. Since open covers, host center enumerations, selected completion points, quotient stream equality, countable choice, and ambient completeness are absent from the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite epsilon-net chain Real-completion handoff]
\label{thm:finite-epsilon-net-chain-real-completion-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$ or completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\FiniteEpsilonNetChainUp$ carrier must consume the compact-metric row $X$, the total-bounded finite-net family $K$, the dyadic tolerance chain $T$, the mesh-budget row $D$, the finite StreamName windows $S$, and the RegSeqRat readback $R$ before it may inspect the terminal seal $L$. The handoff is the finite route
$$

$$
not an open-cover compactness argument, not a selected Cauchy limit, not quotient stream equality, not countable choice, and not ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Finally apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:finite-epsilon-net-chain-namecert-obligations}. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite route, while the missing classical and ambient principles have no carrier coordinate from which they could be exported.
\end{proof}
