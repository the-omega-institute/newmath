\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\PseudocompactSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-pseudocompact-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Engelking, the $\PseudocompactSpaceUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface for spaces whose displayed real-valued continuous reads carry boundedness ledgers. It sits between the topological source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the compactness comparison boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, the real-valued map route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The chapter names boundedness of displayed continuous real reads; it does not claim open-cover compactness, countable subcover extraction, or a host function-space theorem.

\section{Pseudocompact Carrier}
\label{sec:pseudocompact-space-carrier}

\begin{definition}[Pseudocompact space finite packet]
\label{def:pseudocompact-space-carrier}
A $\PseudocompactSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(T,F,B,R,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row naming the underlying open-set interface.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row for the displayed real-valued continuous read.
\item $B$ is the boundedness ledger for the read carried by $F$.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row naming the lower and upper real bounds used by $B$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topological, continuous-map, boundedness, real-bound, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay from the topology row through the continuous real-valued read to the boundedness ledger.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PseudocompactSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PseudocompactSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. In particular, it has no coordinate for finite subcover choice, countable compactness, uniform continuity, a compact-open function space, quotient equality of maps, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\section{Certificate Obligations}
\label{sec:pseudocompact-space-obligations}

\begin{theorem}[Pseudocompact space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:pseudocompact-space-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:pseudocompact-space-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\PseudocompactSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the rows
$$
T,F,B,R,H,C,Q,N,
$$
topology exposure through $T$, continuous real-valued read exposure through $F$, boundedness-ledger exactness through $B$, real-bound sealing through $R$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $Q$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:pseudocompact-space-carrier}. The packet lists exactly the topology, continuous-map, boundedness, real-bound, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The topology-facing coordinate is $T$, and the real-valued continuous read is exposed only through $F$. Thus a certificate reader cannot replace the displayed read by an arbitrary host function or by a compact-open function-space object.

The boundedness obligation is the row $B$ sealed by the $\RealUp$ row $R$. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve this route rowwise, while $Q$ and $N$ only record provenance and local naming.

Since the carrier contains no finite-subcover, countable-compactness, uniform-continuity, quotient-map, or ambient real-completeness coordinate, those objects are outside the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Pseudocompact bounded continuous handoff]
\label{thm:pseudocompact-space-bounded-continuous-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$ or boundedness-facing consumer of an accepted $\PseudocompactSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
P=(T,F,B,R,H,C,Q,N)
$$
must first expose the $\TopologyUp$ source row $T$, the $\ContinuousMapUp$ real-valued read row $F$, and the boundedness ledger $B$ before it may consume the real-bound seal $R$. The exported handoff is a finite bounded-continuous-read route over the same topology, continuous-map, boundedness, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it does not export open-cover compactness, a finite subcover selector, countable compactness, quotient equality of maps, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a boundedness-facing consumer of the accepted carrier. The only real-bound coordinate available to that consumer is the row $R$, and $R$ is displayed as the seal for the boundedness ledger $B$.

Apply \autoref{thm:pseudocompact-space-namecert-obligations}. It forces the topology source to be read through $T$, the real-valued continuous map through $F$, and the boundedness ledger through $B$ before the real-bound seal can be consumed.

The replay and transport rows $C$ and $H$ keep this route inside the same finite packet, and the rows $Q$ and $N$ only source and name the route. Hence the handoff cannot introduce open-cover compactness, a finite subcover selector, countable compactness, quotient equality of maps, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
