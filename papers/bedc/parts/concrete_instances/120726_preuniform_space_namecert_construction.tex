Following Weil and Bourbaki uniform-space terminology, the $\PreuniformSpaceUp$ packet records a finite entourage surface before the full uniform-space closure rows are available. It sits between the mature uniform interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the entourage-base carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-entourage-base-namecert}, and the preuniform-base surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-preuniform-base-namecert}. The packet names the L10 substrate where neighbourhood and completion-facing consumers may inspect displayed entourage data without receiving symmetry, composition, separatedness, quotient completion, or selected-limit structure.

\begin{definition}[PreuniformSpace carrier]
\label{def:preuniform-space-carrier}
A $\PreuniformSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(X,E,W,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the carrier-point row, listing the finite point histories whose neighbourhood comparisons are being inspected.
\item $E$ is the entourage-base row, read through $\EntourageBaseUp$ and $\PreuniformBaseUp$ as a finite family of displayed binary neighbourhood requests.
\item $W$ is the finite neighbourhood-window row, recording the inspected point pairs, base indices, and local membership evidence available to the consumer.
\item $T$ is the topology or metric handoff row, exposing only the finite neighbourhood data that may later be read by $\UniformSpaceUp$, topological, metric, or completion-facing consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the point row, entourage-base row, finite-window row, handoff row, provenance, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same preuniform route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PreuniformSpaceUp$, $\PreuniformBaseUp$, $\EntourageBaseUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PreuniformSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,E,W,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a uniform-space symmetry law, a composition-refinement law, separatedness, quotient equality of Cauchy names, selected completions, countable choice, or host powersets.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[PreuniformSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:preuniform-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PreuniformSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
U=(X,E,W,T,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PreuniformSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier-point exposure through $X$, entourage-base exposure through $E$, finite neighbourhood-window exposure through $W$, topology or metric handoff exposure through $T$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:preuniform-space-carrier}. The analytic rows are the point histories $X$, the displayed entourage-base row $E$, the finite neighbourhood windows $W$, and the handoff row $T$.

The obligation order is finite. A consumer first reads the point and entourage rows, then the inspected neighbourhood windows, and only then the topology or metric handoff surface. No row in this route asserts symmetry, composition refinement, separatedness, quotient completion, or selected limits.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same displayed data. Since the excluded uniform and completion objects are absent from the carrier, the local naming surface is exhausted by the listed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[PreuniformSpace entourage-base route]
\label{thm:preuniform-space-entourage-base-route}
Every neighbourhood read exported by an accepted $\PreuniformSpaceUp$ packet factors through the displayed route
$$
\EntourageBaseUp,\PreuniformBaseUp \longmapsto W \longmapsto T .
$$
Thus a topology, metric, or completion-facing consumer receives finite preuniform neighbourhood data rather than a full $\UniformSpaceUp$ packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier $U=(X,E,W,T,H,C,P,N)$. The only row that names binary neighbourhood data is $E$, and it is explicitly the entourage-base row attached to the preuniform-base surface.

The finite-window row $W$ restricts each consumer request to displayed point pairs, base indices, and membership evidence. The handoff row $T$ may expose those finite neighbourhood reads to topology, metric, or completion-facing consumers, but it does not create uniform symmetry or composition data.

Finally replay the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. They preserve the same entourage-base route under componentwise transport and displayed continuation, so every exported neighbourhood read remains downstream of $E$ and $W$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[PreuniformSpace uniform-consumer handoff]
\label{thm:preuniform-space-uniform-consumer-handoff}
Any $\UniformSpaceUp$ or completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\PreuniformSpaceUp$ carrier must first expose the point row $X$, the entourage-base row $E$, the finite neighbourhood window $W$, and the handoff row $T$. The handoff may be consumed as preuniform input for later uniform closure, but it does not itself export diagonal refinement, symmetry, composition, separatedness, quotient completion, countable choice, selected limits, or host powersets.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:preuniform-space-namecert-obligations}. It places every consumer-visible read inside the displayed rows $X,E,W,T,H,C,P,N$.

Use the entourage-base route of \autoref{thm:preuniform-space-entourage-base-route}. The $\UniformSpaceUp$ or completion-facing consumer can inspect only the preuniform neighbourhood data carried by $E$ and filtered through $W$ before reaching $T$.

The uniform laws named in the statement require carrier coordinates that the preuniform packet does not store. Therefore the handoff is a finite substrate for later uniform closure, not a proof of full uniform-space closure or a completion principle.
\end{proof}
