\begin{definition}[Hahn series finite-window carrier]
\label{def:hahn-series-carrier}
A $\HahnSeriesUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
H=(K,G,W,C,O,V,T,A,S,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the coefficient-field row, read through the scalar $\FieldUp$ source.
\item $G$ is the ordered-exponent row, recording the finite ordered abelian-group window currently inspected.
\item $W$ is the finite support-window row, listing displayed exponents and their coefficient cells.
\item $C$ is the coefficient row, assigning a $K$-coefficient to each exponent listed in $W$.
\item $O$ is the ordered-support comparison row, recording the finite least-exponent and tie-breaking comparisons visible inside $W$.
\item $V$ is the valuation-head row, naming the least displayed exponent and its coefficient when the inspected window is nonzero.
\item $T$ is the truncation row, recording restriction of the displayed support to lower and upper finite cuts.
\item $A$ is the finite addition, multiplication, and scalar-action row over displayed windows.
\item $S$ is the stability row, recording that the displayed arithmetic output has finite support inside the requested window and preserves the ordered-support comparisons.
\item $R$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for coefficient, exponent, support, comparison, valuation, truncation, arithmetic, stability, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HahnSeriesUp$, $\FieldUp$, $\PolynomialUp$, $\FpsUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HahnSeriesUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,G,W,C,O,V,T,A,S,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a completed support class, infinite coefficient summation, host well-order proof, algebraic closure, selected valuation field, or ambient ordered-field completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hahn series finite-window operation stability]
\label{thm:hahn-series-finite-window-operations}
For every accepted $\HahnSeriesUp$ carrier $H=(K,G,W,C,O,V,T,A,S,R,P,N)$, the finite operation row $A$ exports addition, multiplication, and scalar action only after the coefficient row $C$, ordered-support comparison row $O$, valuation-head row $V$, and truncation row $T$ have been displayed. The stability row $S$ records that every exported operation remains inside the requested finite support window and preserves the ordered-support comparison data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows from \autoref{def:hahn-series-carrier}. The operation row $A$ is downstream from the coefficient field $K$, ordered-exponent row $G$, finite support window $W$, coefficient row $C$, comparison row $O$, valuation row $V$, and truncation row $T$.

Addition is read coefficientwise over the displayed window. Multiplication is read through the finite convolution cells named by $W$ and checked against the displayed ordered-support comparisons in $O$.

The stability row $S$ is the only row that can certify the output window. Since $S$ records finite support containment and preservation of comparison data, a consumer cannot turn the operation row into an infinite summation, host well-order proof, or ambient valuation-field theorem.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:hahn-series-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}
