Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive metric completion, the $\LocatedMetricReflectionUp$ packet records the finite route by which locatedness, metric distance, and separated reflection are displayed before a completion consumer reads a point or limit row. It sits over the located-space surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedspace-namecert}, the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the metric-completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the separated-metric reflection of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedmetricreflection-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the L10 countable-completion interface as displayed NameCert data; it is not quotient completion, selected limits, host equality of points, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Located metric reflection carrier]
\label{def:located-metric-reflection-carrier}
A $\LocatedMetricReflectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(X,M,Q,S,W,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\LocatedSpaceUp$ row, carrying the finite locatedness request, positive-gap budget, and completion-facing refusal boundary.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row whose distance witnesses are exposed before any completion row can be read.
\item $Q$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ route, or the equivalent $\CompletionUp$ face, carrying the displayed Cauchy or filter completion handoff.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ classifier row that turns displayed zero-distance evidence into a separated uniqueness read.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ inspection row through which source observations, distance requests, locatedness budgets, and completion windows are read.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal attached to distance and positive-gap readback before completion or separatedness is consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for locatedness, metric distance, completion route, separated reflection, stream window, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a located metric request through $X,M,W,E$, then through $Q$ or $S$ according to whether the consumer asks for completion or separated reflection.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedMetricReflectionUp$, $\LocatedSpaceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedMetricReflectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,M,Q,S,W,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient points, selected limits, host point equality, countable choice, an unrestricted completion operator, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located metric reflection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-metric-reflection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedMetricReflectionUp$ carrier
$$
L=(X,M,Q,S,W,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedMetricReflectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of locatedness exposure through $X$; metric distance exposure through $M$; completion-route exposure through $Q$; separated-reflection exposure through $S$; finite StreamName inspection through $W$; Real sealing through $E$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$. Every completion or separated-reflection consumer must read locatedness and metric distance before it can consume the completion handoff or separated classifier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier in \autoref{def:located-metric-reflection-carrier}. The displayed rows are exactly $X,M,Q,S,W,E,H,C,P,N$, so a consumer has no route to completion or separatedness before the locatedness and metric rows have been displayed.

The locatedness row $X$ supplies the finite request and positive-gap budget, while the metric row $M$ supplies the distance witnesses read through $W$ and sealed at $E$. These rows are the only source of located metric information in the packet.

The completion and separated-reflection rows are downstream. Row $Q$ may be consumed only after the located metric read has been exposed, and row $S$ may turn zero-distance evidence into a uniqueness read only after the same located metric route has been displayed. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that route without adding quotient points, selected limits, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located metric reflection completion handoff]
\label{thm:located-metric-reflection-completion-handoff}
Every completion-facing read from an accepted $\LocatedMetricReflectionUp$ carrier factors through the ordered route
$$
\LocatedSpaceUp \to \MetricUp \to \StreamNameUp \to \RealUp \to \MetricCompletionUp
$$
before any separated uniqueness consumer may inspect $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$. Thus locatedness and metric reflection are read before completion or separatedness; the handoff exports no quotient point, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host equality of completed points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier rows $X,M,W,E,Q$ first. The locatedness row fixes the finite request and positive-gap boundary, the metric row fixes the displayed distance evidence, the StreamName row fixes the finite observations, and the Real seal records the numerical readback.

Only after that route is visible can a completion-facing consumer read $Q$. The completion handoff is therefore a row of the same finite packet rather than an ambient completion operator or a quotient class.

Finally, if a consumer asks for separated uniqueness, it must continue through $S$ after the completion-facing route has exposed the located metric data. The support rows $H,C,P,N$ replay and name the same sequence, so no selected limit, host equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle is exported.
\end{proof}
