\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UpperSemicontinuityUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-upper-semicontinuity-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki and Kelley on topological threshold tests and Bishop--Bridges constructive real analysis, the $\UpperSemicontinuityUp$ packet records upper semicontinuity as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits beside the real-name carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the Real-and-Completion lower-threshold sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lower-semicontinuity-real-namecert}, and the epigraph boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-epigraph-namecert}. The packet names only displayed upper-threshold rows for real-valued reads; it is not a host theorem about limits, a selection principle for suprema, a countable-choice schedule, or an ambient completeness assertion for $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[UpperSemicontinuity carrier]
\label{def:upper-semicontinuity-carrier}
An $\UpperSemicontinuityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(X,T,F,W,R,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source row whose inspected points are named through finite BEDC histories rather than host points.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ threshold row whose basic neighborhoods are displayed before an upper cut is accepted.
\item $F$ is the value-row handoff for the real-valued map under inspection.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ schedule selecting the source and value windows used by each threshold query.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row that exposes the real-name approximants consumed by the threshold test.
\item $A$ is the upper-threshold ledger, recording finite evidence for requests of the form $f(x)<q$ and the corresponding open upper-cut route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, topology, value, schedule, readback, upper-threshold, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an upper-threshold request through $X,T,F,W,R,A,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UpperSemicontinuityUp$, $\RealUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows, with sibling pointers to the lower-semicontinuity and epigraph packets.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UpperSemicontinuityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for semantic limits, selected suprema, quotient equality of functions or real names, countable choice, host topological completeness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[UpperSemicontinuity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:upper-semicontinuity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UpperSemicontinuityUp$ carrier $U=(X,T,F,W,R,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UpperSemicontinuityUp}$ surface consists exactly of five obligation families:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed source, topology, value, schedule, real-readback, and upper-threshold rows;
\item upper-threshold exposure through $A$, requiring each request $f(x)<q$ to be read only after $X,T,F,W,R$ are visible;
\item real-name sealing through $R$ and the sibling $\RealUp$ boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert};
\item componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay through $H$ and $C$;
\item $\Pkg$ provenance and local naming through $P$ and $N$, including the comparison surface beside \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lower-semicontinuity-real-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-epigraph-namecert}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:upper-semicontinuity-carrier}. The only analytic rows are $X,T,F,W,R,A$, so carrier admission and threshold exposure must be read from those displayed coordinates.

The upper-threshold row is not a hidden limit principle. A request $f(x)<q$ first exposes the source and topology rows $X,T$, then the value handoff $F$, then the stream schedule and regular-rational readback $W,R$, and only then the upper ledger $A$.

The structural rows preserve the same route. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ move the displayed rows componentwise, while $P$ and $N$ source and name the packet without adding a second mathematical coordinate.

Thus the five listed obligation families exhaust the local NameCert surface. Since the excluded limit, supremum-selection, quotient, choice, host-completeness, and ambient-completeness principles are absent from the carrier, no accepted read may recover them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[UpperSemicontinuity real seal scope]
\label{thm:upper-semicontinuity-real-seal-scope}
Every Real-facing consumer of an accepted $\UpperSemicontinuityUp$ packet reads the upper-threshold route through
$$
X,T,F,W,R,A \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
The route may compare its upper-cut ledger with the lower-threshold and epigraph siblings, but it reaches $\RealUp$ only through displayed real-name readback rows and local NameCert provenance. It does not supply semantic limsup data, host limits, selected suprema, countable choice, quotient equality of real names, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the threshold request. The source and topology rows $X,T$ fix the finite neighborhood surface, and the value row $F$ fixes the displayed map read.

Next use the Real-facing readback. The stream schedule $W$ selects the finite windows, while $R$ exposes regular-rational approximants at the real-name boundary. The upper-threshold ledger $A$ is read only against those displayed rows.

Finally apply the structural surface. The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet, with sibling comparison pointers to lower semicontinuity and epigraph data only as provenance. Because no excluded principle appears as a row, the Real-facing seal remains scoped to the displayed $\UpperSemicontinuityUp$ carrier.
\end{proof}
