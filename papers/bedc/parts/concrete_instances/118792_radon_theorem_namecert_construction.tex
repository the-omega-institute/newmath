\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RadonTheoremUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-radon-theorem-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Radon and the standard finite convexity theorem, the $\RadonTheoremUp$ packet records the finite certificate surface for a Radon partition of a point family in an affine space. It consumes the convex membership carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, the affine-dependence and translation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-affinespace-namecert}, the finite face and hull ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polytope-namecert}, and the finite simplex inventory of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-simplicialcomplex-namecert}. The packet exposes only the displayed finite point family, the affine-dependence witness, the two-block partition, the convex-hull overlap row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not export an ambient Euclidean topology, a selected separating hyperplane, Helly closure, or a Caratheodory reduction.

\begin{definition}[Radon theorem finite convex-geometry carrier]
\label{def:radon-theorem-finite-convex-geometry-carrier}
A $\RadonTheoremUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(A,F,D,L,U,V,H,Q,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $A$ is the finite-dimensional $\AffineSpaceUp$ ambient row that scopes point differences, affine combinations, and the displayed dependence witness.
  \item $F$ is the finite point-family row, listing the inspected points as $\BHist$ entries and recording their finite index surface.
  \item $D$ is the affine-dependence witness row, with coefficients read through the scalar and affine rows and with total coefficient sum classified as zero.
  \item $L$ is the two-block partition row, splitting the finite index surface into the positive and negative coefficient blocks used by the Radon construction.
  \item $U$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ row reading the positive-block normalized affine combination as a convex-hull point.
  \item $V$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ row reading the negative-block normalized affine combination as the same convex-hull point after affine-dependence transport.
  \item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $A,F,D,L,U,V,Q,P,N$.
  \item $Q$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the affine-dependence calculation and of the two convex-hull membership reads.
  \item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RadonTheoremUp$, $\AffineSpaceUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$, $\PolytopeUp$, $\SimplicialComplexUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
  \item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RadonTheoremUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host convex hull, a topological compactness assertion, a separating hyperplane, or a full Helly-family theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Radon theorem NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:radon-theorem-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:radon-theorem-finite-convex-geometry-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\RadonTheoremUp}$ surface consists exactly of the finite point family row $F$, the affine-dependence witness row $D$, the two-block partition row $L$, the positive and negative convex-hull overlap rows $U,V$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $Q$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the packet $R=(A,F,D,L,U,V,H,Q,P,N)$. The finite point family $F$ gives the only inspected points, while the affine row $A$ is the only ambient structure available for dependence and affine-combination reads.

Use the dependence witness $D$ to split the finite index surface into the two blocks recorded by $L$. The signs of the displayed coefficients determine the blocks, and the zero-sum affine-dependence row is the calculation that identifies the two normalized affine combinations.

Read the positive block through $U$ and the negative block through $V$. Both rows are $\ConvexSetUp$ reads, while the finite face and simplex bookkeeping is supplied only through the $\PolytopeUp$ and $\SimplicialComplexUp$ provenance rows carried by $P$. Thus the overlap point is exposed as two convex-hull membership rows classified by the displayed affine-dependence calculation, not as a host geometric object.

Finally transport and replay preserve the same surface. The $H$ row transports every displayed component by $\hsame$, the $Q$ row replays the affine-dependence calculation and convex-hull membership reads by $\Cont$, and $P,N$ bind those rows to the local name. No additional coordinate remains from which to read Helly, Caratheodory, topology, or separation data, so the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
