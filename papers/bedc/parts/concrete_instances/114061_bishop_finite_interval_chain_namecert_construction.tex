\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BishopFiniteIntervalChainUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-finite-interval-chain-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopFiniteIntervalChainUp$ packet records the finite interval-chain carrier used when compact interval arguments reduce a cover request to adjacent endpoint checks and refinement over a displayed list. It sits after the dyadic interval arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-arithmetic-namecert}, the real interval enclosure rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-interval-enclosure-namecert}, and the compact-cover Lebesgue ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-cover-lebesgue-ledger-namecert}. The packet is a finite NameCert surface for interval-chain reasoning; it is not open-cover compactness, a selected subcover, countable choice, quotient real equality, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Bishop finite interval chain carrier]
\label{def:bishop-finite-interval-chain-carrier}
A $\BishopFiniteIntervalChainUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(E,A,R,M,I,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the endpoint-interval row. It records the finite list of located endpoint intervals, their dyadic endpoint readbacks, and the real interval-enclosure seals inspected by the chain.
\item $A$ is the adjacency and overlap row. It records, for each consecutive pair, the displayed nonempty overlap or endpoint contact certificate that lets a local cover row pass to the next interval.
\item $R$ is the refinement-map row. It records the finite monotone map from a coarse interval chain to a finer chain and the endpoint containment certificates for every refined cell.
\item $M$ is the cover-membership row. It records the named cover packet, the interval cell named by each cover row, and the finite witness that every inspected point of the chain lies in one displayed member.
\item $I$ is the finite induction row. It records the base cell, the successor transport along adjacency, and the terminal interval claim produced by primitive recursion over the chain.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoint intervals, adjacency and overlap rows, refinement maps, cover membership, finite induction, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same interval-chain route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopFiniteIntervalChainUp$, $\DyadicIntervalArithmeticUp$, $\RealIntervalEnclosureUp$, $\CompactCoverLebesgueLedgerUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopFiniteIntervalChainUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed tuple $E,A,R,M,I,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an external open-cover compactness theorem, a selected subcover, countable choice, quotient real equality, trichotomy on real names, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop finite interval chain NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-finite-interval-chain-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopFiniteIntervalChainUp$ carrier $Q=(E,A,R,M,I,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopFiniteIntervalChainUp}$ obligations are exactly carrier admission, endpoint-interval stability, adjacency and overlap transport, refinement-map exactness, cover-membership locality, finite induction replay, componentwise $\hsame$ stability, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:bishop-finite-interval-chain-carrier}. The accepted packet displays endpoint intervals, adjacency and overlap certificates, refinement maps, cover-membership rows, finite induction data, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Endpoint-interval stability is read from $E$ together with the dyadic and real interval enclosure rows named in $P$. Transport by $H$ preserves the same endpoint readbacks and enclosure seals, so no quotient equality or external real-completeness principle is introduced.

Adjacency and refinement obligations are carried by $A$ and $R$. Each successor step has a displayed overlap or endpoint-contact certificate, and each refined cell has a displayed containment certificate in its coarse ancestor. These rows make the chain handoff finite.

Cover locality is supplied by $M$, while finite induction is supplied by $I$. The base interval opens the first cover row, the successor clause moves across the displayed adjacency certificate, and primitive recursion over the finite chain reaches the terminal interval claim.

Finally $C,P,N$ replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no further coordinate, every local NameCert obligation is one of the listed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop finite interval chain refinement induction]
\label{thm:bishop-finite-interval-chain-refinement-induction}
Every accepted $\BishopFiniteIntervalChainUp$ refinement request factors through the finite route
$$
E,A \longmapsto R,M \longmapsto I,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus any interval-chain cover argument that consumes the packet must inspect endpoint intervals and adjacent overlaps before it can replay refinement, cover membership, and induction over the chain.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted carrier $Q=(E,A,R,M,I,H,C,P,N)$. The source stage is $E,A$: the endpoint row fixes the finite interval list, and the adjacency row records the overlap or endpoint-contact certificates for consecutive cells.

The refinement stage is $R,M$. The refinement map can only split a displayed cell into finitely many contained subcells, and the cover-membership row records which named cover packet accepts each inspected cell.

The induction stage is $I$. Its base clause opens the first interval row, while its successor clause transports the local cover statement across the displayed adjacency or overlap certificate. Primitive recursion over the finite chain therefore supplies the terminal claim without selecting a subcover outside the packet.

The final rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same data. The route cannot produce open-cover compactness, countable choice, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness because none of those objects appears in the carrier.
\end{proof}
