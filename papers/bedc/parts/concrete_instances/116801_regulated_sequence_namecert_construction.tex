\label{def:regulated-sequence-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, listing the inspected sequence indices and the visible window schedule.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, naming the dyadic precision demand attached to that window.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readiness row, recording that the displayed window and tolerance data are acceptable for regular rational readback.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream, tolerance, readiness, and seal rows.

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegulatedSequenceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegulatedSequenceUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable-choice schedules, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
