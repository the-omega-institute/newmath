\begin{definition}[Metric derived-set carrier]
\label{def:metric-derived-set-carrier}
A $\MetricDerivedSetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(M,S,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the ambient $\MetricUp$ row, including the point classifier, distance-observation rows, and finite metric-law ledger used by the neighbourhood tests.
\item $S$ is the subset row whose accumulation boundary is being inspected.
\item $Q$ is the punctured-ball witness ledger: each displayed radius request records a candidate point, a positive puncture condition, a subset witness row, and a metric-distance read showing the witness lies in the requested punctured ball.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window surface used to read candidate names, subset witnesses, and inspected radius windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the metric-distance and radius comparisons carried by $Q$ and $W$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the metric, subset, punctured-ball, window, and readback rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transports for $M,S,Q,W,R,E$ and for the provenance and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from metric and subset reads through punctured-ball witnesses, finite windows, regular rational readback, and the terminal Real seal.

\begin{theorem}[Metric derived-set NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metric-derived-set-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetricDerivedSetUp$ carrier $D=(M,S,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MetricDerivedSetUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric-source admission through $M$, subset admission through $S$, punctured-ball witness coverage through $Q$, finite StreamName window visibility through $W$, RegSeqRat readback through $R$, Real sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The metric row, subset row, punctured-ball witness ledger, finite windows, regular rational readbacks, and terminal Real seal are the only mathematical rows exposed to a consumer.

The punctured-ball clause is finite because $Q$ records the inspected radius requests and their displayed subset witnesses. Each witness is read through $W$ and each distance comparison is read through $R$ before the Real seal $E$ is available.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier has no compactness, selector, quotient, choice, or completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
