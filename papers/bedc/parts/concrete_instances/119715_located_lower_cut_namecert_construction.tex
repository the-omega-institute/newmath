\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedLowerCutUp$}

Following Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis, the $\LocatedLowerCutUp$ packet records the lower-cut face of a bounded real family by finite lower-bound ledgers, located approximation windows, and regular-rational readback, before any ambient completeness principle for $\RealUp$ is available. It is the constructive lower-cut companion to the located-infimum route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinfimum-namecert} and the upper-bound sibling route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedsupremum-namecert}, with the bounded-family source supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedrealfamily-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[LocatedLowerCut carrier]
\label{def:located-lower-cut-carrier}
A $\LocatedLowerCutUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(F,L,A,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the bounded-family source row, read through the finite family window and the common-bound data of $\BoundedRealFamilyUp$.
\item $L$ is the lower-bound ledger. It stores the finite comparisons certifying that inspected family values lie above the proposed lower surface.
\item $A$ is the located approximation ledger. For each displayed precision request, it gives a finite family-side approximation that separates accepted lower reads from rejected stronger lower candidates at that precision.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row that fixes the finite observations of $F,L,A$ admitted by the packet.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row for the windowed rational readback.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $F,L,A,W,R$ have been read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the bounded-family row, lower-bound ledger, located approximation ledger, window, handoff, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for lower-bound reads, located approximation reads, classifier transport, regular-rational handoff, and real-seal readback.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedLowerCutUp$, $\LocatedInfimumUp$, $\LocatedSupremumUp$, $\BoundedRealFamilyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedLowerCutUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for Dedekind completeness, quotient real equality, trichotomy, arbitrary infimum, countable choice, or host completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedLowerCut NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-lower-cut-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedLowerCutUp$ packet $K=(F,L,A,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedLowerCutUp}$ obligation surface is exhausted by the bounded-family source, lower-bound ledger, located approximation ledger, $\StreamNameUp$ window, $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff, $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local naming rows. A consumer asking for Dedekind completeness, quotient real equality, trichotomy, arbitrary infimum, countable choice, host completeness, or a standard bridge asks for data outside the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-lower-cut-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly $F,L,A,W,R,E$: the family source, lower-bound ledger, located approximation ledger, finite observation window, regular-rational handoff, and real seal.

Read the lower-cut obligations through those rows. The lower-bound part is sourced by $L$ over $F$, while the located approximation part is sourced by $A$ inside the finite window $W$. The rows $R$ and $E$ only repack and seal the displayed windowed observations.

The structural rows do not add analytic strength. The transports in $H$, replays in $C$, provenance in $P$, and local name row $N$ preserve the same finite route. Since none of the displayed rows contains Dedekind completeness, quotient equality, trichotomy, arbitrary infimum, countable choice, host completeness, or a standard bridge coordinate, none can be exported by the certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedLowerCut window induction]
\label{thm:located-lower-cut-window-induction}
For every accepted $\LocatedLowerCutUp$ packet $K=(F,L,A,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, induction over the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$ constructs the displayed located approximation ledger $A$ from the bounded-family source $F$, lower-bound ledger $L$, handoff row $R$, real seal $E$, transport row $H$, replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$. The induction does not use Dedekind completeness, quotient real equality, trichotomy, arbitrary infimum, countable choice, or host completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Proceed over the finite window row $W$. The empty window has no precision request, so no located approximation entry is required.

For a successor window, read the next precision request together with the bounded-family source $F$ and the lower-bound ledger $L$. The carrier requires the corresponding located approximation entry in $A$, and the induction hypothesis handles the remaining window entries.

After the window ledger is built, $R$ repacks those entries as regular-rational readback and $E$ seals only that readback. The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite construction. No step asks for a completed Dedekind cut, quotient equality of real names, trichotomy, arbitrary infimum, countable-choice selector, or host-completeness theorem.
\end{proof}
