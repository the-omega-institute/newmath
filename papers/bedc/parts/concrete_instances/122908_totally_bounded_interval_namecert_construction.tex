\begin{definition}[Totally bounded interval carrier]
\label{def:totally-bounded-interval-carrier}
A $\TotallyBoundedIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(A,B,L,D,Q,M,F,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ and $B$ are the lower and upper endpoint histories, each sealed through $\RealUp$ only after its regular rational readback has been displayed.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedIntervalUp$ membership row, exposing interval entry and endpoint comparison only through displayed locatedness data.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ mesh-radius row naming the positive dyadic radius requested by the total-boundedness read.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the endpoint and mesh histories.
\item $M$ is the finite mesh schedule, recording the displayed dyadic centers between $A$ and $B$ at radius $D$.
\item $F$ is the finite $\ProbeBundle$ net row whose probes name exactly those displayed centers.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for endpoint, center, and radius histories after $Q$ and $M$ are exposed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoints, located membership, dyadic radius, regular readback, mesh schedule, finite net, real seal, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from endpoint histories to located membership, from located membership to dyadic mesh extraction, from mesh extraction to regular readback, and from readback to the Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TotallyBoundedIntervalUp$, $\RealUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ProbeBundle$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TotallyBoundedIntervalUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed finite rows.
\end{definition}
