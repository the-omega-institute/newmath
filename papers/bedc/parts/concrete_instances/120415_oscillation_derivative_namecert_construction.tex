\label{def:oscillation-derivative-carrier}

$$

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $X$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row, carrying the visible metric comparisons and named source points used by the derivative request.
\item $W$ is the metric/Real finite-window row: it pairs a finite neighbourhood schedule around the inspected source points with the $\RealUp$ value windows read from $F$.
\item $O$ is the oscillation-bound row, recording the displayed lower and upper value comparisons and the rational bound tested inside $W$.
\item $R$ is the refinement ledger, recording which finite neighbourhood windows survive the derivative step and which are discarded at the displayed bound.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the function surface, metric source, finite windows, oscillation bounds, refinement ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

\begin{theorem}[OscillationDerivative NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:oscillation-derivative-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\OscillationDerivativeUp$ carrier $D=(F,X,W,O,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\OscillationDerivativeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed rows; function-surface admission through $F$; metric-source compatibility through $X$; finite neighbourhood and value-window admission through $W$; oscillation-bound exactness through $O$; refinement-ledger exactness through $R$; componentwise transport stability through $H$; displayed replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:oscillation-derivative-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the rows $F,X,W,O,R,H,C,P,N$, so each local NameCert obligation must be read from one of these rows.

The analytic rows are read in finite order. The function-surface row $F$ supplies the displayed real-valued observations, the metric row $X$ supplies the source comparisons, and the finite-window row $W$ binds neighbourhood inspection to the value windows already visible through $F$.

The derivative step is then the route through $O$ and $R$. The oscillation-bound row records the tested lower and upper value comparisons at a rational bound, while the refinement ledger records only the displayed surviving and discarded windows.

Transport and replay preserve that route. The rows $H$ and $C$ cannot add a completed hierarchy, selected neighbourhood basis, quotient function equality, countable choice, point-set measurability, or ambient completeness principle, while $P$ and $N$ source and name only the listed packet. Hence the displayed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}
