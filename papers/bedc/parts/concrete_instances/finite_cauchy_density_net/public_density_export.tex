\begin{theorem}[Finite Cauchy density-net public density export]
\label{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-public-density-export}
Every $\RealUniformStructureUp$, $\CauchyModulusUp$, $\RegularSequenceSpaceUp$, or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ packet receives the public density package exactly through the finite rows
$$
D,W,R,E,M,T,C,P,L .
$$
The export is the displayed dyadic density ledger $D$, finite StreamName window $W$, RegSeqRat readback $R$, terminal Real seal $E$, finite modulus row $M$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $T$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and local $\NameCert$ row $L$. It does not assert completed-real separability, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, quotient representatives, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, or host real equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First apply finite Cauchy density-net extraction, \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-extraction}. It exposes $D,W,R,E,M,T,C,P,L$ as the finite density witness extracted from the accepted carrier.

Next use the stream route and sibling route, \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-stream-route} and \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-sibling-route}. These results place $\RealUniformStructureUp$ and $\CauchyModulusUp$ consumers downstream of the same StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, dyadic ledger, Real seal, modulus, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Now apply tail extraction, \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-tail-extraction}. It identifies the completion-facing reads used by $\RegularSequenceSpaceUp$, $\RealUniformStructureUp$, and $\CauchyModulusUp$ with the same finite packet rows.

Finally use refinement, \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-refinement}. Refining the dyadic cell surface preserves the displayed density route and adds no coordinate for completed-real separability, ambient completeness, quotient representatives, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, or host real equality. Thus the public density package exported to the listed consumers is exactly $D,W,R,E,M,T,C,P,L$.
\end{proof}
