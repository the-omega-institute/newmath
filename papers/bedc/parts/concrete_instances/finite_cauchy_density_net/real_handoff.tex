\begin{theorem}[Finite Cauchy density-net Real handoff]
\label{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-real-handoff}
Every accepted $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ packet consumed by a $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ completion route must pass through the finite density rows, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, the dyadic net route, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row before any Real-facing density read is available. The handoff exports no selected subsequence, quotient stream, ambient completeness coordinate, or hidden completion object outside the displayed finite net rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the density-net carrier through the child routes already read by this hub. The finite density rows and dyadic net route supply the only admissible net data, while the regular readback route exposes the corresponding $\RegSeqRatUp$ windows.

The Real-facing consumer then reads the completion seal through the displayed $\RealUp$ route. Transport and replay preserve the same finite density rows, and provenance with the local NameCert row records that no outside completion coordinate has been introduced.

Since selected subsequences, quotient streams, and ambient completeness objects are not rows of the packet, the Real handoff cannot export them.
\end{proof}
