Every $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ consumer must read its density witness through the dyadic, regular-readback, and real-seal sibling route. The mesh cells are supplied by the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}; the regular rational inspection is supplied by the $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}; and the terminal endpoint is sealed only through the $\RealUp$ rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. Thus a finite density read reaches completion-facing consumers as displayed dyadic mesh data, regular readback data, and RealUp seal data, not as an ambient density theorem or a selected dense subset.
