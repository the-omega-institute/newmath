\begin{theorem}[Finite Cauchy density-net sibling dependency]
\label{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-sibling-dependency}
Every accepted $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ carrier consumes its density row through the dyadic arithmetic surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-namecert}, the finite dyadic cell surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}, and the regular rational density rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} before a completion-facing consumer may read the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. In carrier notation, the dependency route is the displayed path
$$
D,W,R,E,M,T,C,P,L
$$
of \autoref{def:finite-cauchy-density-net-packet}, where $D$ records the dyadic interval net and tolerance ledger, $W$ records the inspected stream window, $R$ records the regular rational readback, and $E$ records the terminal real seal. The route therefore records a concrete sibling dependency edge through dyadic interval nets and RegSeqRat rows, not a kernel-only density assertion, quotient representative, selected limit, countable-choice net, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-cauchy-density-net-packet}. Its analytic rows are exactly the dyadic ledger $D$, stream window $W$, regular readback $R$, terminal seal $E$, and modulus row $M$, with $T,C,P,L$ preserving that same packet.

Apply \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-l10-sibling-density-route}. It already forces every completion-facing density read through the dyadic, StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real sibling chapters before the read is exported.

Now refine the dyadic step by the finite cell surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}. The dyadic interval net supplies the displayed cells and incidence rows, while the $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ packet supplies the density witness, finite modulus, and Real-facing seal.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-real-seal-nonescape}. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient representatives, selected limits, countable-choice nets, host equality, or ambient completeness, the only available dependency route is the displayed sibling route through $D,W,R,E,M,T,C,P,L$.
\end{proof}
