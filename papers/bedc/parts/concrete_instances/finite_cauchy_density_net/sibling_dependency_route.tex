\begin{theorem}[Finite Cauchy density-net sibling dependency]
\label{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-sibling-dependency}
Every accepted $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ carrier consumes its density row through the concrete L10 sibling chain
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp \longrightarrow \StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \RealUp
$$
before a completion-facing consumer may read the exported density-net data. The dyadic tolerance ledger is supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the inspected finite window by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. In carrier notation, the dependency route is the displayed path
$$
D,W,R,E,M,T,C,P,L
$$
of \autoref{def:finite-cauchy-density-net-packet}, where $D$ records the dyadic cell and tolerance ledger, $W$ records the inspected StreamName window, $R$ records the RegSeqRat readback, and $E$ records the terminal Real seal. The route therefore records a concrete sibling dependency edge through dyadic tolerance, finite stream windows, regular readback, and Real sealing, not a kernel-only density assertion, quotient representative, selected limit, countable-choice net, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-cauchy-density-net-packet}. Its analytic rows are exactly the dyadic ledger $D$, stream window $W$, regular readback $R$, terminal seal $E$, and modulus row $M$, with $T,C,P,L$ preserving that same packet.

Apply \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-l10-sibling-density-route}. It already forces every completion-facing density read through the dyadic, StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real sibling chapters before the read is exported.

Now refine the dyadic step through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert} and the finite cell surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}. The dyadic core supplies the tolerance arithmetic read by $D$, and the interval net supplies the displayed cells and incidence rows.

The finite stream and readback steps are then forced by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. The $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ packet can export density only after $W$ and $R$ are displayed, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} remains downstream of those rows.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-real-seal-nonescape}. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient representatives, selected limits, countable-choice nets, host equality, or ambient completeness, the only available dependency route is the displayed sibling route through $D,W,R,E,M,T,C,P,L$.
\end{proof}
