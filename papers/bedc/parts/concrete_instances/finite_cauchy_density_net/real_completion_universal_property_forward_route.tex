\begin{theorem}[Finite Cauchy density net universal-property forward route]
\label{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-real-completion-universal-property-forward-route}
Every $\RealCompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ consumer that accepts finite density evidence from a $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ packet must first read the displayed rows
$$
D,W,R,E,M,T,C,P,L
$$
of \autoref{def:finite-cauchy-density-net-packet}. The route passes through the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} before it reaches the universal-property packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-completion-universal-property-namecert}. Thus the universal-property consumer receives a displayed finite density route, not quotient streams, selected representatives, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient completion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the finite density-net carrier of \autoref{def:finite-cauchy-density-net-packet}. The dyadic density ledger is $D$, the inspected stream window is $W$, the regular rational readback is $R$, the terminal Real-facing row is $E$, and the finite modulus and structural rows are $M,T,C,P,L$.

Apply the sibling dependency theorem \autoref{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-sibling-dependency}. It forces the density read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ before any completion-facing consumer may use it.

Now read the universal-property sibling route of \autoref{thm:real-completion-universal-property-sibling-route}. A $\RealCompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ consumer can expose its universal-property row only after the dyadic, stream, regular-readback, completion-interface, and Real-seal rows have been displayed. The finite density-net packet supplies the first four visible rows and the Real seal through $D,W,R,E$.

Finally replay $M,T,C,P,L$. These rows keep the finite density route, transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming attached to the universal-property handoff. Since neither carrier contains coordinates for quotient stream equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, host real equality, or ambient completion, the displayed forward route is the only exported dependency.
\end{proof}
