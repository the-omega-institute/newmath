\begin{definition}[Cauchy convergence rate carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-convergence-rate-carrier}
A $\CauchyConvergenceRateUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(E,I,R,D,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the tolerance request row. It displays the dyadic error level to be met before any index is exposed.
\item $I$ is the unary index row assigned to the tolerance in $E$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular Cauchy name at the displayed index.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ bound row comparing the requested tolerance with the finite tail bound.
\item $S$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row consumed only after $E,I,R,D$ have been fixed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for tolerance, index, RegSeqRat readback, dyadic bound, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through the unary index, regular-rational readback, dyadic bound, and Real seal rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyConvergenceRateUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyConvergenceRateUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these nine rows. It has no coordinate for a selected Cauchy limit, quotient equality of names, countable choice, hidden tail witnesses, host equality, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy convergence rate NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-convergence-rate-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyConvergenceRateUp$ carrier $Q=(E,I,R,D,S,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyConvergenceRateUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, tolerance request exposure, total unary-index assignment for displayed tolerances, monotone tolerance refinement, RegSeqRat handoff, $\RealUp$ nonescape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-convergence-rate-carrier}. Carrier admission and tolerance exposure are the projections of $Q$ and $E$.

The rate obligations are carried by $I,D$. Totality means that every displayed tolerance row has a displayed unary index row, and monotone refinement means that a sharper displayed tolerance is routed through the same finite ledger before its index can be consumed.

The readback and seal obligations are carried by $R,S$. The row $R$ is the regular-rational handoff, while $S$ is downstream of $E,I,R,D$ and therefore cannot export a Real-facing use without the rate rows.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are exhausted by $H,C,P,N$. Since the carrier has no coordinate for selected limits, quotient equality, countable choice, hidden tail witnesses, host equality, or ambient completeness, the nonescape clause follows by exhaustion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy convergence rate RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:cauchy-convergence-rate-regseqrat-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$-facing use of an accepted $\CauchyConvergenceRateUp$ packet first reads the $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows that expose the stream window, the displayed dyadic tolerance, and the regular-rational readback. No Real-facing consumer receives the seal row $S$ from $Q=(E,I,R,D,S,H,C,P,N)$ without those finite rows and their transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the accepted carrier $Q=(E,I,R,D,S,H,C,P,N)$. The provenance row $P$ names the stream, dyadic, regular-rational, and Real surfaces that the packet is allowed to consume.

The tolerance row $E$ and index row $I$ expose the rate request. The dyadic row $D$ fixes the finite tolerance comparison, and the regular-rational row $R$ fixes the readback point for the displayed Cauchy name.

The Real seal row $S$ is downstream of those rows by the carrier definition. Componentwise transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same order under every accepted presentation, and $N$ names only this local packet.
\end{proof}
