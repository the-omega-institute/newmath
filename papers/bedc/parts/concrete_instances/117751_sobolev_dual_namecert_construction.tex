Following Sobolev and Adams--Fournier, the $\SobolevDualUp$ packet records the finite dual-test-action surface over the Sobolev carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sobolev-space-namecert}. It reads the weak-derivative rows of $\WeakDerivativeUp$, the norm budgets of $\NormUp$, the scalar seals of $\RealUp$, and the completion handoff of $\MetricCompletionUp$ before any dual-facing consumer can inspect a test action or a budgeted functional value. The packet gives PDE, variational, and weak-form consumers a displayed BEDC route rather than a host distribution-dual quotient, a Hahn--Banach extension, or a selected representative of a function class.

\begin{definition}[SobolevDual carrier]
\label{def:sobolev-dual-carrier}
A $\SobolevDualUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D^\ast=(S,T,A,R,B,M,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source Sobolev carrier row, read from the $\SobolevSpaceUp$ packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sobolev-space-namecert} after its source-function, $\WeakDerivativeUp$, $\NormUp$, integrability, $\MetricCompletionUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows have been displayed.
\item $T$ is the test-action family row. It records the finite test windows, derivative-coordinate tags, support tags, and admissible probe names on which the dual action may be evaluated.
\item $A$ is the action ledger row. It binds each displayed test window in $T$ to a finite action value request over the source Sobolev data in $S$.
\item $R$ is the scalar $\RealUp$ readback row for action values, norm estimates, and dual-budget comparisons.
\item $B$ is the norm and dual-budget row. It records the finite bound, tolerance, and comparison data by which the action ledger is admitted as a bounded dual-facing read.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ handoff row, available only after $S,T,A,R,B$ have been displayed and only for the completion route named by this dual packet.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for Sobolev source, test-action family, action ledger, scalar readback, norm and dual budget, completion handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from every dual-facing request through $S,T,A,R,B,M,H,P,L$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SobolevDualUp$, $\SobolevSpaceUp$, $\WeakDerivativeUp$, $\NormUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SobolevDualUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,T,A,R,B,M,H,C,P,L$. It has no coordinate for host distribution-dual quotients, quotient equality of functions, Hahn--Banach extension, choice, PDE solvability, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SobolevDual NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sobolev-dual-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\SobolevDualUp$ carrier
$$
D^\ast=(S,T,A,R,B,M,H,C,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SobolevDualUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, test-action coverage, dual-budget exactness, SobolevSpace handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No dual obligation is stored outside those finite rows, and no consumer may escape to a host distribution-dual quotient before reading the displayed Sobolev source and test-action route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:sobolev-dual-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the Sobolev source row $S$, test-action family $T$, action ledger $A$, scalar row $R$, dual-budget row $B$, completion handoff $M$, and structural rows $H,C,P,L$.

Test-action coverage is finite. Every action value must first name a test window in $T$ and then read the action ledger $A$ against the displayed $\SobolevSpaceUp$ source row $S$; there is no separate coordinate for a host distribution space or a quotient representative.

Dual-budget exactness is read through $R$ and $B$. The scalar seal $R$ is the only real-valued readback, while $B$ records the bound and tolerance data that make the action available to dual-facing consumers.

The handoff to SobolevSpace is therefore prior to every dual read. The rows $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route, so replay and provenance cannot add Hahn--Banach, choice, PDE-solvability, verification, or bridge data.
\end{proof}
