\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UnconditionalBasisUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-unconditional-basis-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Banach-space basis theory as in Lindenstrauss--Tzafriri and Wojtaszczyk, the $\UnconditionalBasisUp$ packet records the finite BEDC naming surface for basis coordinates whose inspected partial sums remain stable under displayed sign changes. It refines the coordinate route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-schauder-basis-namecert}, sits beside the frame-bound stability of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riesz-basis-namecert}, and uses the finite orthonormal comparison discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-orthonormal-basis-namecert}. The packet names only finite sign-window stability data; it is not a full Banach-space basis theorem, not countable choice, not a selected infinite series, and not quotient equality.

\begin{definition}[Unconditional basis carrier]
\label{def:unconditional-basis-carrier}
An $\UnconditionalBasisUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(B,V,N,W,S,K,Q,\Gamma,H,C,P,Z)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\SchauderBasisUp$ source row, exposing the basis-vector stream, coordinate readback, finite partial sums, tail estimates, and reconstruction route consumed by the packet.
\item $V$ is the $\VecSpaceUp$ row over the same endpoint, recording the vector operations and finite linear-combination syntax used by signed sums.
\item $N$ is the $\NormUp$ row naming norm evaluations, triangle ledgers, and comparison rows for the displayed signed partial sums.
\item $W$ is the finite window row, listing the inspected index set, coefficient names, truncation bound, and every sign pattern $\sigma_i\in\{-1,1\}$ admitted inside the packet.
\item $S$ is the signed partial-sum row, recording the displayed sums $\sum_{i\in W}\sigma_i c_i b_i$ and their unsigned comparison sums.
\item $K$ is the unconditional-stability budget row, displaying the finite bound or modulus by which every signed sum in $S$ remains controlled by the same coordinate window.
\item $Q$ is the coordinate reconstruction ledger, requiring every consumer read to pass through the Schauder source row, the finite sign window, the signed-sum row, and the stability budget before reaching a named vector endpoint.
\item $\Gamma$ is the local classifier, comparing accepted packets only by the shared Schauder source, vector and norm source rows, sign-window rows, signed sums, stability budget, reconstruction ledger, and structural rows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $B,V,N,W,S,K,Q,\Gamma,C,P,Z$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a coordinate request through the same finite sign-window route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UnconditionalBasisUp$, $\SchauderBasisUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\NormUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $Z$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UnconditionalBasisUp}$ row binding the packet.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host unconditional convergence theorem, countable-choice schedule, selected infinite series, quotient vector equality, or ambient Banach-space completion proof.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional basis NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:unconditional-basis-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UnconditionalBasisUp$ carrier $U=(B,V,N,W,S,K,Q,\Gamma,H,C,P,Z)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UnconditionalBasisUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, Schauder source compatibility, vector and norm source compatibility, finite sign-window exposure, signed partial-sum exposure, unconditional-stability budget display, coordinate reconstruction routing, classifier stability, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local naming. A consumer may inspect signed coordinates only along the displayed route
$$
B,V,N,W \longrightarrow S,K \longrightarrow Q,\Gamma \longrightarrow H,C,P,Z .
$$
It receives no host unconditional convergence theorem, no countable-choice selector, no selected infinite synthesis series, no quotient vector equality, and no ambient completion coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:unconditional-basis-carrier}. The rows $B,V,N$ expose the Schauder source, vector operations, and norm comparisons, while $W$ exposes the only finite sign windows on which signed coefficient reads may be requested.

The signed partial-sum row $S$ and the stability budget row $K$ are displayed beside the same window row. Thus a sign-changed read can compare only the inspected signed sums with their named coordinate window, and it cannot appeal to an external unconditional convergence theorem.

The reconstruction row $Q$ is downstream of $B,V,N,W,S,K$. The classifier $\Gamma$ compares packets by the same displayed rows, and the structural rows $H,C,P,Z$ preserve that route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Since the carrier contains no host unconditional convergence coordinate, countable-choice selector, selected infinite synthesis series, quotient vector equality, or ambient completion proof, the listed rows exhaust the local naming surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional basis finite sign-window stability]
\label{thm:unconditional-basis-sign-window-stability}
Every coordinate consumer exported by an accepted $\UnconditionalBasisUp$ carrier reads sign stability through a finite inspected window. For each displayed sign pattern $\sigma_i\in\{-1,1\}$ in $W$, the signed partial sum $\sum_{i\in W}\sigma_i c_i b_i$ is admitted only through the signed-sum row $S$ and the stability budget row $K$, after the $\SchauderBasisUp$ source row $B$, the vector row $V$, and the norm row $N$ have been exposed. The exported read is therefore a finite-window stability certificate, not a Banach-space theorem about all signs, not an infinite series selector, not countable choice, and not quotient equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $U=(B,V,N,W,S,K,Q,\Gamma,H,C,P,Z)$. The carrier definition places every sign pattern in the finite window row $W$ and every signed partial sum in $S$, so a consumer has no source for signs outside the inspected window.

Use \autoref{thm:unconditional-basis-namecert-obligations}. It forces the consumer to read the Schauder source and the vector and norm rows before any signed sum can be compared, then to pass through the budget row $K$ for the displayed stability check.

The reconstruction ledger $Q$ and classifier $\Gamma$ keep the read on the same finite surface. The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve that surface, while the carrier omits host unconditional convergence, countable choice, selected infinite synthesis, quotient equality, and ambient completion. Hence the exported stability statement is exactly the finite sign-window certificate named by the packet.
\end{proof}
