\begin{definition}[Hyperbolic visual metric modulus carrier]
\label{def:hyperbolic-visual-metric-modulus-carrier}
A $\HyperbolicVisualMetricModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\mathsf{BHist}$ packet
$$
V=(G,B,S,D,M,R,O,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the $\HyperbolicVisualMetricUp$ source row supplying the visual metric read over the displayed hyperbolic boundary surface.
\item $B$ is the $\HyperbolicBoundaryShadowUp$ boundary-shadow row that records which shadow comparison is being measured.
\item $S$ is the finite shadow-window row, read through the observer-expansion surface of $\HyperbolicObserverExpansionUp$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for visual-radius and shadow-depth requests.
\item $M$ is the modulus row assigning each displayed dyadic tolerance in $D$ a finite boundary-shadow depth for the rows $G,B,S$.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for the visual-distance readback, available only after $G,B,S,D,M$ are exposed.
\item $O$ is the observer-expansion row tying the visual metric and shadow modulus to the finite observer surface named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-observer-expansion-namecert}.
\item $W$ is the nonescape row refusing completed boundary quotients, selected geodesic representatives, global hyperbolic compactifications, and physical horizon semantics.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $G,B,S,D,M,R,O,W$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a boundary-shadow request through the visual metric, dyadic tolerance, modulus, Real seal, observer-expansion, and nonescape rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HyperbolicVisualMetricModulusUp$, $\HyperbolicVisualMetricUp$, $\HyperbolicBoundaryShadowUp$, $\HyperbolicObserverExpansionUp$, $\DyadicShadowConeUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\mathsf{BHist}$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HyperbolicVisualMetricModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier is modulus data for a displayed visual metric route. It is not a completed Gromov boundary, not a quotient boundary equality, not a selected geodesic, not a physical horizon, and not an ambient compactness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hyperbolic visual metric modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hyperbolic-visual-metric-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HyperbolicVisualMetricModulusUp$ carrier $V=(G,B,S,D,M,R,O,W,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HyperbolicVisualMetricModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item visual metric admission for the row $G$ and its displayed boundary surface;
\item boundary-shadow admission for the row $B$ and its finite shadow comparison;
\item shadow-window admission for $S$ through the observer-expansion route;
\item dyadic tolerance admission for $D$ and modulus admission for $M$;
\item Real-seal discipline, requiring $R$ to be read only after $G,B,S,D,M$ have been displayed;
\item observer-expansion compatibility for $O$;
\item transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$;
\item nonescape through $W$, excluding completed boundary quotients, selected geodesic representatives, global hyperbolic compactifications, physical horizon semantics, and ambient compactness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hyperbolic-visual-metric-modulus-carrier}. The geometric rows are $G,B,S$, the tolerance and modulus rows are $D,M$, and the Real-facing row is $R$.

The modulus is finite because every request in $M$ is indexed by a displayed dyadic tolerance in $D$ and by the already visible shadow rows $G,B,S$. No boundary quotient or selected geodesic coordinate is available to answer a request.

The Real-seal and observer-expansion obligations are then forced by the row order. The seal $R$ is downstream of $G,B,S,D,M$, while $O$ records the observer-expansion route that receives the same finite shadow-depth data.

Finally $W,H,C,P,N$ record refusal, transport, replay, source, and local naming. These rows preserve the same packet and refuse every global or physical endpoint listed in the statement, so the obligations exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
