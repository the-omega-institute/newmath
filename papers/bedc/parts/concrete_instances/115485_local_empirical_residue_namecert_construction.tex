\begin{definition}[Local empirical residue carrier]
\label{def:local-empirical-residue-carrier}
A $\LocalEmpiricalResidueUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(H,K,F,J,D,O,L,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $H$ is the finite observation-history row, naming the trace, probe bundle, observed signature, and model signature under inspection;
\item $K$ is the classifier row used to judge agreement on the displayed trace;
\item $F$ is the local fit or finite-failure row, recording the current empirical comparison under $K$;
\item $J$ is the local closure row, read through $\LocalEmpiricalClosureGateUp$, stating that the current finite inquiry has been settled;
\item $D$ is the failure-surface residue row, listing the displayed continuations or refinements that would defeat the present fit;
\item $O$ is the open-observation row, recording that the packet does not enumerate all future histories;
\item $L$ is the nonfinality ledger, naming the refused final-fit, final-induction-oracle, hidden-continuation, and total-confirmation reads;

The carrier has no coordinate for unrestricted empirical confirmation, selected future histories, total continuation enumeration, host physical-trajectory equality, or a final induction oracle.

\begin{theorem}[Local empirical residue NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:local-empirical-residue-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocalEmpiricalResidueUp$ carrier $R=(H,K,F,J,D,O,L,T,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocalEmpiricalResidueUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite observation-history exposure through $H$, classifier exposure through $K$, local fit or finite-failure reading through $F$, local closure exposure through $J$, failure-surface residue through $D$, open-observation retention through $O$, nonfinality refusal through $L$, componentwise transport through $T$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:local-empirical-residue-carrier}. The empirical rows are precisely $H,K,F,J,D,O,L$: a finite trace, a classifier, a local fit or failure judgment, a local closure row, a residue failure surface, an openness row, and a nonfinality ledger.

The rows $D,O,L$ keep the local judgment from becoming final. The failure-surface residue records displayed ways the present fit could be defeated; the openness row refuses an enumeration of all future histories; and the nonfinality ledger records the refused oracle reads.

Finally $T,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since none of these structural rows adds a selected continuation, a total confirmation judgment, host trajectory equality, or a final induction oracle, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Local empirical residue nonfinality]
\label{thm:local-empirical-residue-nonfinality}
Every consumer of an accepted $\LocalEmpiricalResidueUp$ packet that reads the local closure row $J$ must also read the failure-surface residue $D$, open-observation row $O$, and nonfinality ledger $L$. Thus local empirical settlement may be used as finite evidence, but it cannot be exported as unrestricted future fit, total empirical confirmation, or a final induction oracle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the local closure boundary of \autoref{thm:local-closure-not-global-closure}. A finite inquiry may settle the displayed trace without settling all admissible future histories.

The carrier records this separation in the same packet. The row $J$ is paired with $D,O,L$, so any accepted read of local closure exposes the remaining failure surface, the open-observation boundary, and the refused finality claims.

Transport, replay, provenance, and naming preserve that packet without adding new empirical coordinates. Therefore a downstream consumer can cite local empirical residue only as finite nonfinal evidence, not as a total confirmation or final induction principle.
\end{proof}
