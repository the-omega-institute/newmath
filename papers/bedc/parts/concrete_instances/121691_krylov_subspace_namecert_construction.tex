\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KrylovSubspaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-krylov-subspace-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Krylov and the standard Arnoldi/Lanczos finite-iteration literature, the $\KrylovSubspaceUp$ packet records the finite span generated by repeated application of one displayed linear operator to one displayed seed vector. It sits after the matrix carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, the vector-space carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, the linear-map surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-linearmap-namecert}, and the control-facing observer route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-luenberger-observer-namecert}. The packet names only the finite iterate-and-span certificate; it does not export an infinite cyclic subspace, a spectral decomposition, a chosen orthonormal Arnoldi basis, a Lanczos tridiagonalization, or host equality between matrix and operator presentations.

\begin{definition}[KrylovSubspace carrier]
\label{def:krylov-subspace-carrier}
A $\KrylovSubspaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(V,A,v,n,I,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V$ is the $\VecSpaceUp$ row carrying the finite vector-space operations, zero row, scalar action, and classifier used by the packet.
\item $A$ is the common $\LinearMapUp/\MatrixUp$ row. It records both the operator action and the finite coordinate matrix surface through which iterates are inspected.
\item $v$ is the seed-vector row, displayed as a vector of $V$ before any iterate ledger is consumed.
\item $n$ is the finite depth row naming the last iterate admitted to the packet.
\item $I$ is the finite iterate ledger
$$
v,\quad Av,\quad A^2v,\quad \ldots,\quad A^n v,
$$
with each entry read through the same $V$ and $A$ rows.
\item $L$ is the span and provenance ledger. It records finite linear-combination support over the iterate list $I$ and the refusal to identify the span with a host subspace outside the displayed rows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $V,A,v,n,I,L,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the seed-vector request through the finite iterate and span ledgers.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KrylovSubspaceUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\PolynomialUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KrylovSubspaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an infinite-dimensional closure, a quotient invariant subspace, a selected orthogonal basis, a minimal polynomial theorem, spectral convergence, or equality between the matrix row and the linear-map row beyond the displayed provenance.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[KrylovSubspace NameCert boundary]
\label{thm:krylov-subspace-namecert-boundary}
For every accepted $\KrylovSubspaceUp$ carrier
$$
K=(V,A,v,n,I,L,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\KrylovSubspaceUp}$ surface is exhausted by five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the vector-space row $V$, operator row $A$, seed row $v$, depth row $n$, iterate ledger $I$, span ledger $L$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$;
\item finite iterate closure, requiring each displayed entry of $I$ to be generated by repeated use of the same $A$ on the same seed row $v$ inside $V$;
\item classifier transport, requiring every span membership or nonmembership read to pass through the componentwise $\hsame$ row $H$ and the replay row $C$;
\item matrix-linear-map provenance, requiring the coordinate matrix surface and the linear-map action to be linked only by the displayed $A$ and $P$ rows;
\item ledger exhaustion, requiring Arnoldi, Lanczos, reachability, observability, or polynomial-action consumers to read only $V,A,v,n,I,L,H,C,P,N$ before citing the packet.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation for $\KrylovSubspaceUp$ is stored outside those five rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:krylov-subspace-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes one vector-space row, one matrix-linear-map row, one seed vector, one finite depth, one finite iterate ledger, one finite span ledger, and the four structural rows.

Finite iterate closure is read from $I$. Since each entry is displayed as $A^k v$ for $0\leq k\leq n$, the packet never needs an infinite orbit or a host subspace completion.

Classifier transport and replay are the rows $H$ and $C$. They preserve the vector, operator, iterate, and span ledgers componentwise and do not add a hidden basis, spectral coordinate, or convergence claim.

Finally read provenance through $P$ and local naming through $N$. The matrix and linear-map presentations are connected only by the displayed row $A$, with $\PolynomialUp$ appearing as finite polynomial-action provenance rather than as a minimal-polynomial theorem. Therefore the five listed obligations exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}
