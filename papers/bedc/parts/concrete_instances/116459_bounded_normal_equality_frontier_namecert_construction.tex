\begin{definition}[Bounded normal-equality frontier carrier]
\label{def:bounded-normal-equality-frontier-carrier}
A $\BoundedNormalEqualityFrontierUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(I,F,L,N,E,R,H,C,P,A)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the closed MetaCIC input row, listing the two closed terms, their type context, and the bounded equality query.
\item $F$ is the finite normalizer-fuel row, recording the shared fuel value and the bounded evaluation trace admitted for each side.
\item $L$ is the closedness ledger, recording the closed-term and closed-normal-form checks consumed by the frontier.
\item $N$ is the finished-normal-form row, displaying the two bounded normal forms and the witnesses that the displayed fuel has reached finished endpoints.
\item $E$ is the equality-checker row, recording the structural comparison of the finished normal forms.
\item $R$ is the refusal row, naming unrestricted beta conversion, arbitrary-fuel completeness, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient syntax equality, and kernel consistency as unavailable endpoints.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the input, fuel, closedness, finished-normal, equality-checker, refusal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the audit-map query through $I,F,L,N,E,R,H,P,A$.

\item $A$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedNormalEqualityFrontierUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $I,F,L,N,E,R,H,C,P,A$. It has no coordinate for an unrestricted conversion decider, arbitrary-fuel normalization completeness, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient syntax equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded normal-equality frontier NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bounded-normal-equality-frontier-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BoundedNormalEqualityFrontierUp$ carrier $Q=(I,F,L,N,E,R,H,C,P,A)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedNormalEqualityFrontierUp}$ surface consists exactly of closed input exposure, finite normalizer-fuel exposure, closedness ledger exposure, finished-normal-form exposure, equality-checker exposure, refusal-boundary retention, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bounded-normal-equality-frontier-carrier}. The rows $I,F,L,N,E,R$ are the complete normal-equality audit data, and $H,C,P,A$ are the structural rows that transport, replay, source, and name that data.

The fuel row $F$ bounds the normalizer traces. The closedness row $L$ records that the query and the finished endpoints are closed, while the finished-normal row $N$ exposes the two normal forms before any equality result is read.

The equality-checker row $E$ is therefore consumed only under the retained refusal row $R$. Since no carrier coordinate contains unrestricted beta conversion, arbitrary-fuel completeness, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient syntax equality, or kernel consistency, those endpoints cannot be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded normal-equality frontier finished soundness route]
\label{thm:bounded-normal-equality-frontier-finished-soundness-route}

$$
I \longmapsto F \longmapsto L \longmapsto N \longmapsto E \longmapsto R,H,C,P,A

The route is conditional on displayed fuel, closedness, finished endpoints, and structural equality of the displayed normal forms; it is not unrestricted beta-conversion decidability, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient syntax equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:bounded-normal-equality-frontier-namecert-obligations}. A consumer must expose the input, bounded fuel, closedness ledger, finished normal forms, equality checker, refusal boundary, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before reading the frontier.

The refusal row $R$ remains in the exported route. A consumer that wants arbitrary fuel, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient syntax equality, or kernel consistency must supply a separate certificate outside this packet.
\end{proof}
