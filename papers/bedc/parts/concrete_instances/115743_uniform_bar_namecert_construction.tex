\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniformBarUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-bar-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Brouwer, the $\UniformBarUp$ packet records the finite-depth bar extraction used when a compact-uniform-continuity argument turns pointwise inspection into one shared modulus. It sits between the finite fan rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert}, the compact source rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the metric and continuity surfaces of $\MetricUp$ and $\ContinuousUp$, and the rational precision rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}. The packet is a finite certificate over displayed bar decisions, compact probes, pointwise modulus reads, rational tolerances, $\BHist$ histories, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and a local $\NameCert$ row.

It is not Brouwer bar induction, not open-cover compactness, not a selected infinite branch, and not a host modulus function. A downstream L10 compact-uniform-continuity or real-completion consumer reads only the finite depth, the inspected witness window, and the rational precision ledger named by the packet.

\begin{definition}[UniformBar carrier]
\label{def:uniform-bar-carrier}
A $\UniformBarUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(F,K,M,A,R,D,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\FanTheoremUp$ finite fan row supplying a decidable bar and unary branch-depth ledger;
\item $K$ is the $\CompactUp$ or $\CompactMetricUp$ source row whose finite probes are inspected by the bar;
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source-distance row used to compare neighbouring compact probes;
\item $A$ is the $\ContinuousUp$ or $\ContinuousMapUp$ pointwise-modulus row attached to each displayed probe;
\item $R$ is the positive $\RatUp$ tolerance request whose response is the extracted uniform depth;
\item $D$ is the accepted finite depth row, read as a unary bound common to all displayed probe branches;
\item $W$ is the finite witness window listing exactly the probe prefixes and pointwise modulus rows inspected before depth $D$;
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transports for the fan, compact, metric, continuity, rational, depth, witness-window, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay routes for bar inspection, compact-probe readback, pointwise-modulus readback, rational-tolerance comparison, and uniform-depth export;
\item $P$ is $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformBarUp$, $\FanTheoremUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\ContinuousUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformBarUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an infinite branch, an open cover, a selected subcover, an ambient compactness theorem, a host modulus function, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[UniformBar NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-bar-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformBarUp$ carrier $U=(F,K,M,A,R,D,W,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UniformBarUp}$ surface consists exactly of fan-bar exposure through $F$, compact-source exposure through $K$, metric comparison through $M$, pointwise continuity exposure through $A$, rational tolerance exposure through $R$, finite-depth exposure through $D$, witness-window exposure through $W$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-bar-carrier}. The rows $F,K,M,A,R$ fix the fan, compact, metric, continuity, and rational inputs before any uniform depth can be read.

The row $D$ is the only finite-depth output, and $W$ records the exact probe prefixes and pointwise modulus reads inspected to justify it. Thus the bar extraction is a finite ledger over displayed rows, not an appeal to an infinite branch space or an open-cover compactness principle.

Transport $H$ and replay $C$ preserve those rows componentwise. Provenance $P$ and the local row $N$ then repack the same inventory as the local $\NameCert_{\UniformBarUp}$ surface, so the obligation surface contains no hidden subcover selection, host modulus function, or real-completion coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[UniformBar finite-depth modulus]
\label{thm:uniform-bar-finite-depth-modulus}
Every $\CompactUniformContinuityUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, or L10 real-completion consumer that reads an accepted $\UniformBarUp$ carrier receives a modulus only through the finite route
$$
\FanTheoremUp,\ \CompactUp,\ \CompactMetricUp,\ \MetricUp,\ \ContinuousUp,\ \ContinuousMapUp,\ \RatUp,\ D,\ W,\ H,\ C,\ P,\ N .
$$
The extracted modulus is the displayed depth $D$ together with the witness window $W$ and its rational tolerance ledger. It cannot be replaced by open-cover compactness, Brouwer bar induction, a selected infinite branch, a host supremum of pointwise moduli, quotient real equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:uniform-bar-namecert-obligations}. Any consumer read must expose the fan-bar row, compact source row, metric comparison row, pointwise continuity row, rational tolerance row, depth row, witness-window row, and the structural rows of the packet.

The finite fan row contributes only the decidable bar and unary branch-depth ledger, as in the public finite fan route of \autoref{thm:fantheorem-public-uniform-bar-export}. The compact and metric rows determine which probes are inspected, and the continuity rows attach pointwise modulus data only to those probes.

Finally read the rational request through $R$ and the extracted depth through $D$. The witness window $W$ lists every pointwise row used before that depth, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite route under transport, replay, provenance, and naming. Since the carrier exposes no open-cover, branch-selection, host-modulus, quotient-real, or real-completion coordinate, the consumer receives exactly the finite-depth modulus described above.
\end{proof}
