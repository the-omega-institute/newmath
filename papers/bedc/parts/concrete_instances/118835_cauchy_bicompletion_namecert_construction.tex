\begin{definition}[Cauchy bicompletion carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-bicompletion-carrier}
A $\CauchyBicompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(L,R,W,Q,E,U,M,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ and $R$ are the left and right $\StreamNameUp$ Cauchy-window rows;
\item $W$ is the paired finite-window row, recording the common precision request and the inspected left and right tails;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the paired windows;
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ route reached only after the paired readback row has been displayed;
\item $U$ is the $\UniformCauchyBicompletionUp$ comparison row, used only as the uniform two-sided corridor for the same finite pair;
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$ coherence row, recording unit, bind, and associativity readback for the displayed completion route;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the two sides, paired window, readback, Real route, uniform corridor, coherence row, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite bicompletion route;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyBicompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\UniformCauchyBicompletionUp$, $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyBicompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion, selected completion representatives, ambient host equality, choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy bicompletion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-bicompletion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyBicompletionUp$ carrier $B=(L,R,W,Q,E,U,M,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyBicompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, paired Cauchy-window discipline, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback exactness, $\RealUp$ route nonescape, $\UniformCauchyBicompletionUp$ corridor compatibility, $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$ coherence, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, local naming, and refusal of quotient completions, selected representatives, ambient host equality, choice, and ambient real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-bicompletion-carrier}. The rows $L,R,W,Q,E,U,M,H,C,P,N$ are the whole local certificate surface, so every obligation must be assigned to one of those coordinates.

The paired-window obligation is $W$ over the two source rows $L$ and $R$. It records the finite left and right tails inspected for the same precision request before readback can proceed.

The readback and completion-facing obligations are $Q,E,U,M$. The row $Q$ supplies regular rational readback, $E$ is the downstream real route, $U$ records compatibility with the uniform two-sided corridor, and $M$ records the completion monad-law coherence visible to consumers.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite bicompletion packet. Since the carrier has no quotient-completion, selected-representative, host-equality, choice, or ambient-completeness coordinate, those objects cannot be exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy bicompletion real-completion route]
\label{thm:cauchy-bicompletion-real-completion-route}
Every $\RealUp$ or regular Cauchy completion consumer of an accepted $\CauchyBicompletionUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
L,R,W \longmapsto Q \longmapsto E,U,M \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the consumer receives paired Cauchy-window rows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, the terminal $\RealUp$ route, the $\UniformCauchyBicompletionUp$ corridor, $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$ coherence, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. It receives no quotient completion, selected representative, ambient host equality, choice, or ambient real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted packet $B=(L,R,W,Q,E,U,M,H,C,P,N)$. The left and right source rows are only stream-window data, and the paired row $W$ forces them to answer the same finite precision request.

The regular rational readback row $Q$ is then the only way to expose the paired Cauchy windows as completion data. The terminal real row $E$ remains downstream of $Q$, so a real-completion consumer cannot skip the finite readback.

The uniform corridor $U$ and coherence row $M$ are parallel checks on the same displayed route. They record two-sided uniform compatibility and completion monad-law behavior without adding a quotient completion or selected representative.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the route. Once those rows are exhausted, the packet has no remaining coordinate for ambient host equality, choice, or ambient real completeness.
\end{proof}
