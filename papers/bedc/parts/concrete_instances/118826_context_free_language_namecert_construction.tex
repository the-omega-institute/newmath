\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ContextFreeLanguageUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-context-free-language-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Hopcroft--Ullman and standard formal-language theory, the $\ContextFreeLanguageUp$ packet names a language by finite grammar and derivation data before any recognizer, pumping, or membership consumer reads a host language set. It sits beside the regular-language continuation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-language-continuation-namecert}, the finite-control surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-automaton-namecert}, the stack-recognizer surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pushdown-automaton-namecert}, and the derivation-window certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-context-free-pumping-lemma-namecert}. The packet records the grammar, derivation tree, yield, and classifier rows that those consumers may inspect; it is not an ambient set of words, a host-language equality principle, or a semantic closure theorem.

\begin{definition}[Context-free language carrier]
\label{def:context-free-language-carrier}
A $\ContextFreeLanguageUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(G,V,\Sigma,P,D,Y,M,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the grammar row naming the start symbol and the finite context-free grammar surface.
\item $V$ is the finite nonterminal alphabet row.
\item $\Sigma$ is the finite terminal alphabet row.
\item $P$ is the production ledger, listing every admitted production as a finite row over $V$ and $\Sigma$.
\item $D$ is the derivation-tree row, recording the finite tree whose internal nodes read productions from $P$.
\item $Y$ is the yield stream row, recording the terminal frontier obtained from $D$.
\item $M$ is the membership classifier row, accepting a word only when it is the displayed yield of an admitted derivation tree.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $G,V,\Sigma,P,D,Y,M,C,Q,N$.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from grammar admission through productions, derivation tree, yield stream, and membership classifier.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ContextFreeLanguageUp$, $\ContextFreeGrammarUp$, $\PushdownAutomatonUp$, $\ContextFreePumpingLemmaUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ContextFreeLanguageUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an infinite parse forest, host language equality, unrestricted grammar quotient, choice of a canonical derivation, complement closure, determinization, or ambient semantic set membership.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Context-free language NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:context-free-language-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ContextFreeLanguageUp$ carrier $L=(G,V,\Sigma,P,D,Y,M,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ContextFreeLanguageUp}$ surface consists exactly of grammar admission through $G,V,\Sigma,P$, derivation stability through $D$ and $H$, yield exactness through $Y$, pumping-boundary nonescape through the $\ContextFreePumpingLemmaUp$ route, classifier transport through $M$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $Q$, and local naming through $N$.

No accepted packet exports host language equality, an infinite parse forest, a grammar quotient, a selected canonical derivation, complement closure, determinization, or an ambient semantic language set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:context-free-language-carrier}. The grammar, nonterminal, terminal, and production rows $G,V,\Sigma,P$ are the only rows that admit grammar syntax.

The derivation tree and yield rows are downstream of that syntax. Componentwise transport through $H$ preserves $D$ and $Y$, and replay through $C$ records the route from productions to the derivation tree and then to the terminal yield.

The membership classifier $M$ reads only the displayed yield of an admitted derivation tree. The $\ContextFreePumpingLemmaUp$ sibling may inspect finite derivation-window data, but it receives no infinite parse forest or semantic language quotient.

Finally $Q$ and $N$ preserve provenance and local naming for the same finite packet. Since none of the excluded objects is a carrier coordinate, no local NameCert obligation can export it.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Context-free language pushdown handoff]
\label{thm:context-free-language-pushdown-handoff}
Every $\PushdownAutomatonUp$ consumer that accepts a language-membership row from an accepted $\ContextFreeLanguageUp$ packet must first read the grammar row $G$, the nonterminal row $V$, the terminal row $\Sigma$, the production ledger $P$, the derivation-tree row $D$, the yield row $Y$, and the classifier row $M$. The handoff exports only the displayed grammar-and-derivation surface and cannot replace it by a host language set, a hidden stack oracle, a canonical derivation selector, or grammar quotient equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier projection. The only membership row is $M$, and it is listed after the grammar, production, derivation, and yield rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:context-free-language-namecert-obligations}. Classifier transport is bound to the same finite rows $G,V,\Sigma,P,D,Y,M$ and to the componentwise $\hsame$ transport in $H$.

A pushdown consumer therefore reads the grammar and derivation surface before accepting a language-membership row. The continuation row $C$ records that replay order, while $Q$ and $N$ preserve provenance and naming without adding a second membership source.

The excluded objects are absent from the carrier. Hence the pushdown handoff supplies a finite grammar-and-derivation interface rather than a host language set, stack oracle, derivation selector, or quotient equality principle.
\end{proof}
