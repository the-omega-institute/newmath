\begin{theorem}[Bishop regular completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-regular-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopRegularCompletionUp$ carrier
$$
B=(D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the dyadic tolerance ledger, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, terminal Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item finite-window exposure, requiring every completion-facing request to inspect $S$ before the readback row $R$ is consumed;
\item dyadic tolerance exactness, requiring $D$ to supply the same budget used by the stream-window comparison and the regular rational readback;
\item Real seal nonescape, requiring $E$ to be read only after $D,S,R$ have been displayed and replayed through $H,C,P,N$;
\item exclusion of quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-regular-completion-carrier}. Its analytic rows are exactly $D,S,R,E$, with $H,C,P,N$ supplying transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The source side begins with $D$ and $S$. The dyadic ledger fixes the tolerance budget for the request, while the stream-window row fixes the finite observations allowed at that budget.

The regular readback row $R$ is downstream of those source rows. Thus a completion-facing consumer receives regular rational data only through the displayed dyadic and stream evidence.

Finally, the terminal seal $E$ is reached only after the same route has been replayed and named. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for quotient equality, a selected host limit, countable choice, trichotomy, host equality, or ambient completeness, those principles are outside the local certificate.
\end{proof}
