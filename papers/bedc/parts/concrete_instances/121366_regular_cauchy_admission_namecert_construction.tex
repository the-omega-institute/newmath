Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis treats a regular Cauchy name as usable real data only after the finite rational observations, tolerance ledger, and terminal seal have been kept separate. The $\RegularCauchyAdmissionUp$ packet records that admission surface between the regular rational sequence of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} and the real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. It also keeps the source schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert} and the dyadic arithmetic ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert} visible to completion-facing readers.

The packet is an admission route, not a real-completion theorem. It names the finite data that a later $\RealUp$ or completion chapter may consume when it reads a regular rational name as Real-facing data, while refusing quotient stream equality, selected representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, and ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy admission carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-admission-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyAdmissionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ source schedule, recording the finite observation windows through which downstream readers ask for rational data.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, carrying the regular rational sequence data visible on the windows named by $S$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, carrying the dyadic precision cells, slack conversions, and comparison budgets used to audit the regularity of $R$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the stream schedule, regular readback, and dyadic tolerance ledger have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,R,D,E$ and for the local classifier row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay routes from a Real-facing request back through $E$, $D$, $R$, and $S$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyAdmissionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyAdmissionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only by replaying these rows. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy admission NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-admission-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyAdmissionUp$ carrier $A=(S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyAdmissionUp}$ obligation surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. The obligations require every Real-facing read to expose $S$, $R$, and $D$ before the seal row $E$ is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-admission-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly $S,R,D,E$, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same route.

The regular rational readback row $R$ is not a Real object by itself. A consumer must first read the stream windows in $S$ and the dyadic tolerance ledger in $D$ before the seal row $E$ can be exposed.

Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same finite order. Since neither row adds quotient stream equality, selected representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, or completeness, the local NameCert obligations are exhausted by the displayed packet surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy admission Real-seal route]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-admission-real-seal-route}
Every $\RealUp$-facing read of an accepted $\RegularCauchyAdmissionUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
S \longmapsto R \longmapsto D \longmapsto E .
$$
Thus a completion or Real-facing consumer sees the stream schedule, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, and terminal seal in order. The route exports no quotient stream equality, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a consumer of the seal row $E$. By the carrier definition, $E$ is the terminal row reached from the displayed regular rational data and its dyadic tolerance ledger.

The stream schedule $S$ supplies the finite request windows, and $R$ supplies the regular rational observations visible on those windows. The ledger $D$ then records the precision and comparison data needed before the seal is public.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name this route without adding host-level or quotient coordinates. Hence every Real-facing read factors through $S,R,D,E$ and cannot export the excluded principles.
\end{proof}
