\begin{definition}[Lindeberg-condition carrier]
\label{def:lindeberg-condition-carrier}
A $\LindebergConditionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(P,X,V,T,D,R,E,H,C,Q,N).
$$
Here $P$ is the source $\ProbSpaceUp$ row carrying the event classifier and total-mass normalization. The row $X$ is the triangular-window $\RandomVarUp$ display, including the finite row index, the visible summand range, and the local law token read by each displayed summand. The row $V$ is the variance-budget ledger naming the finite total variance request, the component variance rows, and the positive scale comparison used by the window. The row $T$ is the truncation-threshold row, recording the displayed cutoff, the truncated summand window, and the finite exceptional-event ledger. The row $D$ is the $\DistributionUp$ readback for the truncated and untruncated finite sums. The row $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ comparison row used to compare the displayed variance and tail budgets. The row $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal handoff reached only after $P,X,V,T,D,R$ have all been exposed. The row $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the probability source, triangular window, variance budget, threshold, distribution readback, RegSeqRat comparison, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local name. The row $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a truncation request through $P,X,V,T,D,R,E$ and then through the structural rows. The row $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LindebergConditionUp$, $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\RandomVarUp$, $\DistributionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$. The row $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LindebergConditionUp}$ row. The classifier compares accepted packets only through these eleven rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lindeberg-condition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lindeberg-condition-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LindebergConditionUp$ carrier $L=(P,X,V,T,D,R,E,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LindebergConditionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the probability source, triangular random-variable window, variance budget, truncation threshold, distribution readback, RegSeqRat comparison, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item truncation-window exactness, requiring every tail event and truncated summand read to pass through the displayed threshold row $T$ over the same finite window $X$;
\item variance-budget compatibility, requiring the scale and component variance reads to pass through $V$ before any RegSeqRat comparison row $R$ is exposed;
\item distribution and Real-seal handoff, requiring $D$ and $R$ to precede the terminal seal row $E$;
\item nonescape from the asymptotic central limit theorem, characteristic-function limits, countable choice, quotient distribution equality, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows of \autoref{def:lindeberg-condition-carrier}. The probability source $P$ and triangular window $X$ provide the only random-variable data, while $V$ and $T$ provide the only variance and truncation coordinates.

The comparison route is then forced through $D,R,E$. The distribution row $D$ reads the displayed finite sums, the RegSeqRat row $R$ compares the finite variance and tail budgets, and the Real seal row $E$ is visible only after those rows are already present.

Finally $H,C,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no coordinate names an asymptotic theorem, characteristic-function convergence, quotient-law equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
