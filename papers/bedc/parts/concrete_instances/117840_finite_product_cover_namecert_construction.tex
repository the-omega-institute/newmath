\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FiniteProductCoverUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-finite-product-cover-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges and Kelley on finite products of compact metric covers, the $\FiniteProductCoverUp$ packet records the finite product-cover route used by compact metric and uniform-modulus consumers. It sits between the product metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-productmetric-namecert}, the compact product route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-metric-product-namecert}, the compact metric source surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite refinement route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cover-refinement-namecert}, and the product constructor surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-prod-namecert}. The packet names a finite cover of a displayed product metric object; it does not choose subcovers, assert open-cover compactness, import countable choice, or identify quotient product points.

\begin{definition}[Finite product cover carrier]
\label{def:finite-product-cover-carrier}
A $\FiniteProductCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(L,R,J,M,K,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the left finite cover row, a finite list of $\FiniteCoverRefinementUp$ cells over the left $\CompactMetricUp$ source;
\item $R$ is the right finite cover row, a finite list of $\FiniteCoverRefinementUp$ cells over the right $\CompactMetricUp$ source;
\item $J$ is the product pairing row, recording the $\ProdUp$ cell pairs obtained from the displayed left and right cover cells;
\item $M$ is the $\ProductMetricUp$ ledger that reads distances only through the displayed left and right metric rows;
\item $K$ records the $\CompactMetricProductUp$ and $\CompactMetricUp$ source rows consumed by the product-cover request;
\item $B$ is the finite radius budget row, recording the product-radius allowance and the component radius budgets assigned to $L$ and $R$;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $L,R,J,M,K,B,C,P,N$;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the left cover, right cover, product pairing, product metric ledger, compact metric sources, radius budget, transport, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteProductCoverUp$, $\ProductMetricUp$, $\CompactMetricProductUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\FiniteCoverRefinementUp$, $\ProdUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteProductCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an arbitrary open cover, a selected subcover, countable choice, quotient point equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite product cover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-product-cover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteProductCoverUp$ carrier $F=(L,R,J,M,K,B,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteProductCoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of left finite cover exposure, right finite cover exposure, product pairing exposure, product metric ledger exposure, compact metric source exposure, finite radius budget exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-product-cover-carrier}. The cover data are exactly the finite left and right rows $L,R$, and the product-cover cells are exactly the product pairing row $J$.

The metric and compactness-facing reads are displayed separately. The row $M$ supplies the $\ProductMetricUp$ ledger, $K$ supplies the $\CompactMetricProductUp$ and $\CompactMetricUp$ source rows, and $B$ records the finite radius budget used by the product-cover request.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same finite route. Since the carrier stores no arbitrary open cover, selected subcover, countable-choice schedule, quotient point equality, ambient Real completeness, Lean theorem, or bridge row, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite product cover product-net route]
\label{thm:finite-product-cover-product-net-route}
Every product-net consumer of an accepted $\FiniteProductCoverUp$ packet factors through the displayed route
$$
L,R,B \longmapsto J \longmapsto M,K .
$$
It first reads the left and right finite cover rows and the finite radius budget, then forms only the displayed $\ProdUp$ pair cells, and only then consumes the $\ProductMetricUp$, $\CompactMetricProductUp$, and $\CompactMetricUp$ rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with $F=(L,R,J,M,K,B,H,C,P,N)$. The left and right finite cover rows are the only available component cover data, and $B$ is the only radius budget attached to them.

Read the product pairing row $J$. It pairs cells already present in $L$ and $R$, so it cannot introduce an unseen component cover cell, an arbitrary open cover, or a selected subcover.

Now consume the product metric ledger $M$ and the compact metric source row $K$. These rows read the paired cells as a product metric cover over the displayed compact metric sources, while $H,C,P,N$ keep the route stable under transport, replay, provenance, and naming.

Thus every product-net route is componentwise and finite. No step supplies countable choice, quotient product point equality, ambient Real completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{proof}
