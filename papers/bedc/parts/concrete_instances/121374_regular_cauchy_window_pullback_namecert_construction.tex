Following Bishop--Bridges regular Cauchy completion, the $\RegularCauchyWindowPullbackUp$ packet names the finite pullback of a requested real precision to the stream windows and regular-rational rows that witness it. It refines the diagonal-limit route of \autoref{sec:real-completeness-regular-cauchy} by sitting beside the finite observation discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet records a finite pullback surface for regular-Cauchy windows; it is not a selected limit, quotient stream equality, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy window pullback carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-window-pullback-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyWindowPullbackUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(P,S,R,D,E,H,C,Q,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the precision request row, naming the requested real-window budget and its finite regular-Cauchy tail index.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row opened at exactly the indices demanded by $P$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the displayed stream windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording the finite dyadic comparisons used to transport the requested precision through $S$ and $R$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $P,S,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the precision, window, readback, tolerance, seal, replay, provenance, and local name rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a precision request through $P,S,R,D,E$.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyWindowPullbackUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyWindowPullbackUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for selected limits, quotient stream equality, countable choice, host real equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy window pullback handoff]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-window-pullback-handoff}
Every accepted $\RegularCauchyWindowPullbackUp$ packet factors a Real-completion precision request through the displayed finite route
$$
\StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \DyadicRatCoreUp \longrightarrow \RealUp .
$$
The route exposes the finite stream windows and dyadic tolerance ledger before a terminal $\RealUp$ seal can be read.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-window-pullback-carrier}. The precision row $P$ fixes the finite tail window to inspect, and the only observations opened by the packet are the coordinates listed in $S$.

The readback row $R$ interprets those observations as regular rational data, while the dyadic ledger $D$ records the tolerance comparisons needed by the same finite request. These rows are displayed before the seal row is available.

The terminal row $E$ therefore records only the RealUp boundary attached to the finite pullback. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for selected limits, quotient stream equality, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient completeness, every completion-facing read stays on the displayed route.
\end{proof}
