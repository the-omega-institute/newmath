\begin{theorem}[BishopLocatedDiagonalSequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-diagonal-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-diagonal-sequence-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedDiagonalSequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for $B=(S,Q,D,L,R,H,C,P,N)$;
\item analytic exposure of the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $Q$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row $D$, $\RegularSequenceSpaceUp$ source row $L$, and $\RegularCauchyDiagonalUp$ diagonal row $R$;
\item structural stability through componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$ and displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$;
\item provenance and local naming through $P$ and $N$;
\item nonescape for quotient streams, selected diagonal representatives, countable-choice schedules, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows of \autoref{def:bishop-located-diagonal-sequence-carrier}. The analytic data are exactly $S,Q,D,L,R$, and the structural data are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

The schedule, readback, and tolerance rows expose the finite observation surface. The regular-sequence source row $L$ keeps the family-level read inside $\RegularSequenceSpaceUp$, while the diagonal row $R$ is unavailable until those finite rows have been displayed.

Transport and replay preserve this route componentwise. Since the carrier has no row for quotient equality, choice of representatives, countable-choice schedules, or ambient completeness, the listed obligations exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BishopLocatedDiagonalSequence L10 route]
\label{thm:bishop-located-diagonal-sequence-l10-route}
Every completion-facing read of an accepted $\BishopLocatedDiagonalSequenceUp$ carrier passes through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-sequence-space-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} before the diagonal read is public. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the regular-sequence source row $L$. It binds the diagonal read to the accepted $\RegularSequenceSpaceUp$ carrier before any completion-facing endpoint is visible.

Read the finite observation rows next. The $\StreamNameUp$ schedule $S$ fixes the inspected windows, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $Q$ supplies the regular rational readback, and the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$ records the displayed tolerance budget.

Only then consume the diagonal row $R$. The $\RegularCauchyDiagonalUp$ selector receives the finite window, readback, and tolerance data already exposed by the source route, so it cannot introduce quotient streams or selected representatives.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$ into the $\RealUp$ seal. The terminal real-facing read is therefore public only after the displayed L10 route has passed through the regular-sequence source, StreamName schedule, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance surface, and Real seal.
