\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RealNetClusterUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-real-net-cluster-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Moore--Smith and Kelley on nets and cluster points, the $\RealNetClusterUp$ packet records the finite route by which a displayed real-valued net exposes cluster-data windows before any Real or Completion consumer reads a terminal seal. It sits after the directed index surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-directedset-namecert}, the Moore--Smith net carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-moore-smith-net-namecert}, the finite stream-name windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names cluster evidence as displayed cofinal windows and regular-rational reads; it is not a quotient of subnet choices, a selected limit point, a host compactness theorem, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Real net cluster carrier]
\label{def:real-net-cluster-carrier}
A $\RealNetClusterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(I,N,W,S,Q,R,H,T,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\DirectedSetUp$ index row naming the directed order used by the net.
\item $N$ is the $\MooreSmithNetUp$ row, exposing the net values only through the directed index row $I$.
\item $W$ is the cofinal-window ledger, listing finite tails and refinement requests over the directed order.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row that records the inspected value windows selected by $W$.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the displayed value windows.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the directed, net, window, stream, and rational-readback rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the directed index, net, cofinal-window ledger, stream windows, rational readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a cluster request through $I,N,W,S,Q,R,H,P,L$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealNetClusterUp$, $\DirectedSetUp$, $\MooreSmithNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealNetClusterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,N,W,S,Q,R,H,T,P,L$. It has no coordinate for selected subnets, quotient equality of real names, host cluster points, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real net cluster NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-net-cluster-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealNetClusterUp$ carrier
$$
C=(I,N,W,S,Q,R,H,T,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RealNetClusterUp}$ surface consists exactly of directed-index admission through $I$, Moore--Smith net admission through $N$, cofinal-window exposure through $W$, finite observation through $S$, regular-rational readback through $Q$, terminal Real seal exposure through $R$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $T$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $L$. The certificate requires every cluster-facing read to display a cofinal window before it may inspect the stream-name and regular-rational rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-net-cluster-carrier}. The only analytic rows available to a cluster-facing consumer are the directed index $I$, the Moore--Smith net row $N$, the cofinal-window ledger $W$, the observation row $S$, the readback row $Q$, and the Real seal $R$.

The cofinal-window obligation is local to $W$. A cluster request may refine a tail in the directed order, but it must do so through the displayed ledger before any value window in $S$ is exposed.

Readback and sealing are downstream. The regular-rational row $Q$ is attached to the displayed observations, and the Real seal $R$ is reached only after $I,N,W,S,Q$ have been replayed.

The structural rows $H,T,P,L$ preserve that route. Since no selected subnet, host cluster point, quotient equality, compactness theorem, countable-choice principle, or ambient completeness row occurs in the carrier, the local NameCert surface cannot export one.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real net cluster cofinal-window route]
\label{thm:real-net-cluster-cofinal-window-route}
Every cluster-data read exported by an accepted $\RealNetClusterUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\DirectedSetUp \to \MooreSmithNetUp \to W \to \StreamNameUp \to \RegSeqRatUp \to \RealUp .
$$
In the notation of \autoref{def:real-net-cluster-carrier}, the cofinal-window ledger $W$ refines the directed index row $I$ before the observation row $S$, regular readback row $Q$, or real seal $R$ is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:real-net-cluster-namecert-obligations}. They make the directed index and Moore--Smith net rows prerequisites for every cluster-facing read.

Use the cofinal-window ledger next. The ledger $W$ records the finite tails and refinement requests over $I$, so a consumer cannot inspect a value window before the relevant cofinal window has been displayed.

The observation and readback rows then follow the fixed order $S,Q,R$. The stream-name row exposes only the inspected value windows, the regular-rational row reads those observations, and the Real seal is terminal.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming do not add a second route. They carry the same displayed path through $H,T,P,L$, so the cluster-data read remains finite and non-quotient.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real net cluster nonescape]
\label{thm:real-net-cluster-nonescape}
An accepted $\RealNetClusterUp$ carrier cannot export a selected subnet, a chosen cluster point, quotient equality of real names, host compactness, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness. Every public cluster-facing consumer is confined to the rows $I,N,W,S,Q,R,H,T,P,L$ of \autoref{def:real-net-cluster-carrier}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows. The data coordinates name only a directed index, a Moore--Smith net, a cofinal-window ledger, finite observations, regular-rational readback, a Real seal, and structural certificate rows.

Apply the cofinal-window route, \autoref{thm:real-net-cluster-cofinal-window-route}. It forces every cluster-facing read to pass through the displayed directed and window data before any Real-facing seal can be consumed.

The excluded objects would require additional coordinates: a selected subnet needs a choice row, a chosen cluster point needs a detached point row, quotient equality needs a quotient read, and compactness or ambient completeness needs a global closure principle. None is present in $I,N,W,S,Q,R,H,T,P,L$.

Thus every accepted consumer remains inside the finite NameCert packet and cannot escape to those stronger principles.
\end{proof}
