\begin{theorem}[Abel--Dirichlet test NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:abel-dirichlet-test-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AbelDirichletTestUp$ carrier $T=(S,A,B,D,Q,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\AbelDirichletTestUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed eleven-row packet;
\item source-window exposure through $S$;
\item Abel summation exposure through $A$, preserving the finite summation-by-parts and boundary-term rows;
\item Abel-test face exposure through $B$, preserving the bounded partial-sum, monotone null coefficient, and tail ledger rows;
\item Dirichlet-test face exposure through $D$, preserving the bounded partial-sum, coefficient-window, null-tail, and tail-schedule rows;
\item comparison exactness through $Q$, requiring the two test faces to meet at the displayed $\AbelDirichletComparisonUp$ row before completion-facing readback;
\item $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback and $\RealUp$ endpoint exposure through $R$ and $E$;
\item componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming through exactly $H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:abel-dirichlet-test-carrier}. The source, summation, test-face, comparison, readback, endpoint, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows are exactly the coordinates of $T$.

Read the analytic rows in order. The Abel summation row $A$ supplies the finite summation-by-parts window. The Abel face $B$ and Dirichlet face $D$ then contribute their displayed bounded-partial-sum and tail-control data.

The common convergence-test obligation is the row $Q$. It forces the two faces to pass through the existing Abel--Dirichlet comparison surface before $R$ and $E$ can be read by a completion-facing consumer.

Finally read $H,C,P,N$. These structural rows transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route, so the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}
