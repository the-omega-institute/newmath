\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\TotallyBoundedRealUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\TotallyBoundedRealUp$ packet records a finite-net real-object certificate for Real and Completion consumers. It sits beside the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the finite-net source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the compact-metric consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}. The packet names a totally bounded real row only through displayed finite nets, regular rational windows, dyadic radii, and real seals; it is not open-cover compactness, a quotient completion, a selected limit, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[TotallyBoundedReal carrier]
\label{def:totally-bounded-real-carrier}
A $\TotallyBoundedRealUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(E,M,T,D,W,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, recording the endpoint real-name boundary exposed to downstream Real and Completion consumers.
\item $M$ is the metric row on the displayed real-name surface, with distance requests, endpoint classifiers, and comparison histories read through the same finite packet.
\item $T$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite-net row, including the displayed center bundle, membership rows, coverage witnesses, and mesh ledger.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the metric and finite-net rows for positive radius requests.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row that exposes only the regular observation prefixes used to inspect the real names.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular rational windows attached to $W$ and $D$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the real seal, metric row, finite-net row, dyadic ledger, finite windows, rational readbacks, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through $D$, then through the finite-net row $T$, the metric row $M$, the stream window $W$, the rational readback $Q$, and finally the real seal $E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TotallyBoundedRealUp$, $\RealUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TotallyBoundedRealUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows and their componentwise transports. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient real equality, selected limits, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[TotallyBoundedReal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:totally-bounded-real-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\TotallyBoundedRealUp$ carrier $R=(E,M,T,D,W,Q,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\TotallyBoundedRealUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the real seal, metric row, totally bounded finite-net row, dyadic tolerance ledger, stream window, regular rational readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item finite-net exactness, requiring every positive tolerance request to read the center bundle, membership rows, coverage witnesses, and mesh ledger displayed by $T$;
\item real-window exactness, requiring every Real-facing read to pass through the finite observation row $W$, the regular rational readback row $Q$, and the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ before the seal $E$ is consumed;
\item metric compatibility, requiring the distance comparisons in $M$ to use the same dyadic radii and real-name windows displayed by $D,W,Q,E$;
\item transport and replay stability, requiring $H$ and $C$ to preserve the ordered route from tolerance request to finite net, metric comparison, rational readback, and real seal;
\item provenance non-escape, requiring $P$ and $N$ to name only the displayed rows and to export no open-cover compactness, selected subcover, quotient equality, selected limit, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No local obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:totally-bounded-real-carrier}. The packet exposes exactly the real seal, metric row, finite-net row, dyadic tolerance ledger, stream window, regular rational readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

Finite-net exactness is read from $T$. A positive tolerance request can reach only the displayed center bundle, membership rows, coverage witnesses, and mesh ledger carried by the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ row.

Real-window exactness and metric compatibility are read together. The stream window $W$, regular rational readback $Q$, and dyadic ledger $D$ display the real-name observations before the seal $E$ is exposed, while the metric row $M$ uses those same windows and radii for every comparison.

Finally inspect $H,C,P,N$. Transport and replay preserve the displayed route, and provenance names only the listed rows. Since the carrier has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient equality, selected limits, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness, none of those objects can be exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[TotallyBoundedReal finite-net handoff]
\label{thm:totally-bounded-real-finite-net-handoff}
Every $\CompactMetricUp$, Real, or Completion consumer of an accepted $\TotallyBoundedRealUp$ carrier
$$
R=(E,M,T,D,W,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
reads the totally bounded real object through the finite route
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \TotallyBoundedUp \longmapsto \MetricUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
For each positive tolerance request, the consumer receives a displayed dyadic radius from $D$, a finite center bundle from $T$, metric comparisons from $M$, a regular rational window from $W,Q$, and the terminal real seal $E$. The handoff exports no open-cover compactness, selected subcover, quotient real equality, selected limit, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the tolerance request. The replay row $C$ sends it first to the dyadic ledger $D$, so every radius and comparison budget is displayed before a finite-net row can be read.

Next read the finite-net row $T$ and the metric row $M$. The center bundle, membership rows, coverage witnesses, and mesh ledger provide the total-bounded source, while the metric comparisons use exactly the dyadic radii already displayed by $D$.

The Real-facing portion then passes through the finite window $W$ and the regular rational readback $Q$ before reaching the seal $E$. Thus a downstream compact-metric, Real, or Completion consumer sees a real object only after the finite net and the rational-window evidence have been exposed.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve that route. The packet therefore hands off finite-net real data without adding any open-cover compactness, selected subcover, quotient real equality, selected limit, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness coordinate.
\end{proof}
