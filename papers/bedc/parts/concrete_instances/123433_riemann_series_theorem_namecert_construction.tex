\begin{definition}[Riemann series theorem carrier]
\label{def:riemann-series-theorem-carrier}
A $\RiemannSeriesTheoremUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\RealSeriesUp$ source row, exposing signed terms only through finite coefficient and partial-sum windows.
\item $P_{+}$ is the positive-block ledger, recording the finite list of positive term blocks admitted by the current rearrangement stage.
\item $P_{-}$ is the negative-block ledger, recording the finite list of negative term blocks admitted by the current rearrangement stage.
\item $W$ is the signed partial-sum window ledger. It names the finite windows where alternating positive and negative block reads are compared.
\item $A$ is the rearrangement schedule row. It records the finite order in which the displayed positive and negative blocks are read, without selecting an ambient infinite permutation.
\item $T$ is the tolerance and target-readback row, carrying the dyadic tolerance requests and the regular rational target windows used by the consumer.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ comparison row attached to $T$.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source series, block ledgers, signed windows, schedule, tolerance/readback rows, dyadic comparisons, Real seal, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from every rearranged partial-sum read through $S,P_{+},P_{-},W,A,T,D,E,H,Q,N$.

\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary permutation choice, quotient equality of infinite series, selected infinite sums, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}
