It sits beside the finite source interfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-stream-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the terminal seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the real Bolzano--Weierstrass and sequential compactness routes of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-bolzano-weierstrass-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sequential-compact-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Finite subsequence compactness carrier]
\label{def:finite-subsequence-compactness-carrier}
A $\FiniteSubsequenceCompactnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(F,S,L,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the finite source-window row, listing the bounded block of source observations available to the compactness consumer.
\item $S$ is the subsequence schedule row, recording only the displayed monotone index cells selected inside $F$.
\item $L$ is the compact or located source row, exposing the finite compact support, located interval, or finite-net witness from which the schedule is read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the scheduled observations.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, recording the dyadic mesh, comparison radius, and readback precision used by $R$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $F,S,L,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source window, subsequence schedule, compact source, readback, tolerance, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite compactness request through $F,S,L,R,D,E,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteSubsequenceCompactnessUp$, $\FiniteStreamUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteSubsequenceCompactnessUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $F,S,L,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host-selected infinite subsequence, quotient stream equality, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite subsequence compactness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-subsequence-compactness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteSubsequenceCompactnessUp$ carrier
$$
K=(F,S,L,R,D,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteSubsequenceCompactnessUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite source-window admission through $F$, subsequence schedule visibility through $S$, compact or located source exposure through $L$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, dyadic tolerance accounting through $D$, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-subsequence-compactness-carrier}. The only rows available to a compactness consumer are $F,S,L,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$.

The source and schedule rows are finite. Row $F$ exposes the inspected source block, row $S$ records the monotone schedule inside that block, and row $L$ supplies the displayed compact or located support before any readback is public.

The analytic read proceeds through $R,D,E$. The regular rational row reads only scheduled observations, the dyadic row fixes the tolerance ledger, and the Real seal is downstream from both.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for host subsequence selection, quotient stream equality, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite subsequence Real-seal nonescape]
\label{thm:finite-subsequence-compactness-real-seal-nonescape}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The readback rows $R$ and $D$ then account for the scheduled rational observations and the dyadic tolerance used to compare them. Therefore a Real-facing consumer cannot reach $E$ without first reading the displayed finite source and schedule route.
