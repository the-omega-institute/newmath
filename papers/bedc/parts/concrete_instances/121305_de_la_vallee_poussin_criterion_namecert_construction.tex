\begin{theorem}[de la Vallee Poussin NameCert obligations]\label{thm:de-la-vallee-poussin-namecert-obligations}

\item carrier admission for the $\MeasureUp$ row $M$, the $\IntegralUp$ row $I$, and the family window inspected by the criterion;
\item growth-row exposure through $G$, requiring the convex gauge, finite thresholds, and growth reads to remain displayed before any uniform-integrability handoff;
