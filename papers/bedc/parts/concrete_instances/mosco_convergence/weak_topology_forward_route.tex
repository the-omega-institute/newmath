\begin{theorem}[Mosco weak-topology forward route]
\label{thm:mosco-convergence-weak-topology-forward-route}
Let $M=(F,W,L,R,C,H,T,P,N)$ be an accepted $\MoscoConvergenceUp$ carrier. Any weak-liminf consumer of $M$ must read the weak-topology probes $W$ and the weak-liminf ledger $L$ before a $\Gamma$-convergence or epigraphical-limit consumer reads the variational comparison through $R,C,H,T,P,N$. In particular, the routes of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-weak-star-topology-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-gamma-convergence-namecert} meet on the same visible rows $F,W,L,R,C,H,T,P,N$, not on a host theorem equating weak convergence, Mosco convergence, Gamma convergence, or epigraphical limits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:mosco-convergence-carrier}. The weak side is displayed by the lower-semicontinuity row $F$, the weak-topology row $W$, and the weak-liminf ledger $L$.

The variational comparison side is downstream. A Gamma-facing or epigraphical-limit consumer may read recovery and comparison data only after the weak-liminf side has exposed $F,W,L$, and then only through $R,C,H,T,P,N$.

The structural rows keep the same packet visible. Transport, provenance, and local naming do not add a host equivalence among convergence notions, so the forward route is the finite weak-topology route stated above.
\end{proof}
