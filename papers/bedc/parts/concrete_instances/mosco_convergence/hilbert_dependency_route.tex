\begin{theorem}[Mosco Hilbert dependency route]
\label{thm:mosco-convergence-hilbert-sibling-route}
Let $M=(F,W,L,R,C,H,T,P,N)$ be an accepted $\MoscoConvergenceUp$ packet. If the weak-liminf row $L$ and strong-recovery row $R$ are Hilbert-facing, then any consumer must display the $\HilbertUp$ carrier, inner-product classifier, convex-set row, weak-topology row, and recovery-sequence provenance before the Mosco classifier is read. Thus the Hilbert certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hilbert-namecert} remains a visible sibling dependency of every such Mosco read, and no weak-liminf or strong-recovery route may hide the Hilbert support inside the Mosco packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:mosco-convergence-carrier}. The weak-liminf side is read through $F,W,L$, so a Hilbert-facing lower-bound consumer must expose the weak topology and the Hilbert carrier before the lower-bound classifier can be consumed.

The strong-recovery side is read through $R,C,H$. Its recovery sequence is meaningful only over the same convex support and inner-product classifier, so the Hilbert row and convex-set row remain visible before recovery provenance is accepted.

Finally, the transport, provenance, and local naming rows $T,P,N$ bind both sides to the same finite packet. They add no hidden equality or ambient completion principle; they only keep the Hilbert carrier, inner-product classifier, convex row, weak-topology row, and recovery-sequence provenance displayed along the Mosco route.
\end{proof}
