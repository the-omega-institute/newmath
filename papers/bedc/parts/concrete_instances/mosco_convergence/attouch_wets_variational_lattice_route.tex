\begin{theorem}[Mosco Attouch-Wets variational lattice route]
\label{thm:mosco-convergence-attouch-wets-variational-lattice-route}
Let $M=(F,W,L,R,C,H,T,P,N)$ be an accepted $\MoscoConvergenceUp$ carrier.
Every Attouch-Wets, $\Gamma$-convergence, weak-Hilbert, convex-support, or
epigraphical-limit consumer of $M$ must read the weak-liminf side through the
displayed rows $F,W,L$ and the strong-recovery side through the displayed rows
$R,C,H$ before using the structural rows $T,P,N$. Thus the sibling routes of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-attouch-wets-convergence-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-gamma-convergence-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-weak-convergence-hilbert-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, and
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-epigraphical-limit-namecert} receive only the
finite Mosco row lattice, not a host theorem equating variational convergence
notions or selecting minimizing sequences.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:mosco-convergence-carrier}. The lower
variational side is exactly the finite row sequence $F,W,L$, while the strong
recovery side is exactly $R,C,H$.

Read the Attouch-Wets, $\Gamma$-convergence, weak-Hilbert, convex-support, and
epigraphical-limit siblings through those rows. The weak-Hilbert and convex
support routes can be inspected only before the lower-bound and recovery
ledgers are consumed, and the Attouch-Wets, $\Gamma$-convergence, and
epigraphical consumers receive the same displayed packet afterward.

The transport, provenance, and local naming rows $T,P,N$ preserve the finite
row lattice. Since the carrier has no coordinate for a host variational
equivalence, selected minimizer, hidden epigraphical theorem, or ambient
compactness principle, every listed sibling route remains tied to the
displayed Mosco rows.
\end{proof}
