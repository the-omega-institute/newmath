\begin{theorem}[Mosco weak-strong sibling route]
\label{thm:mosco-convergence-weak-strong-sibling-route}
Let $M=(F,W,L,R,C,H,T,P,N)$ be an accepted $\MoscoConvergenceUp$ carrier. Every weak-liminf read of $M$ must pass through the $\WeakStarTopologyUp$, $\WeakConvergenceHilbertUp$, and $\HilbertUp$ sibling surfaces before the lower-bound ledger $L$ can be consumed, while every strong-recovery read must pass through $\MetricCompletionUp$ and the $\MoscoRecoverySequenceUp$ sibling before any $\Gamma$-convergence or epigraphical-limit consumer may use the packet. Thus the routes of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-gamma-convergence-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-epigraphical-limit-namecert} receive Mosco data only after the weak topology, Hilbert carrier, convex support, metric completion, and recovery-sequence rows have all been displayed through the same $F,W,L,R,C,H,T,P,N$ packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:mosco-convergence-carrier}. The weak side is visible only through $F,W,L$, so a weak-liminf consumer must display the weak topology probes, the weak-convergence Hilbert route, and the Hilbert carrier rows before reading the lower-bound ledger.

The strong side is a different route. The recovery row $R$ and convex-support row $C$ identify the allowed approximating data, while the completion handoff $H$ keeps the metric completion boundary visible before recovery data feed a downstream variational consumer.

Finally apply the existing Gamma and epigraphical dependency routes. They can consume the Mosco packet only through the same structural rows $T,P,N$, and these rows add no host equivalence among weak convergence, strong recovery, Gamma convergence, or epigraphical limits.
\end{proof}
