The $\RegularCauchyWindowCofinalityUp$ route is a sibling dependency surface for finite Real and Completion readback, not an isolated Cauchy primitive.

The cofinal window is consumed as the finite $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback scheduled through $\StreamNameUp$ windows: \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} supplies the regular rational handoff, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert} supplies the inspected finite windows that make the cofinal tail visible to downstream consumers.

The same cofinal tail window also consumes the $\CauchyModulusUp$ schedule row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchymodulus-namecert} before $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ consumers may read the window. This is a substantive dependency on the displayed modulus schedule, not a host choice of a tail index.
