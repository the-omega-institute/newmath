\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-banach-operator-graph-norm-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Banach and Rudin on closed operators between Banach spaces, the $\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp$ packet records the graph norm of a displayed operator as finite NameCert data. It sits over the Banach carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-namecert}, the public bounded-linear-operator surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedlinearoperator-namecert}, the closed-graph route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closed-graph-theorem-namecert}, the metric rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the complete-metric witness rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, and the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the displayed domain graph and its norm-sum route without replacing the graph by a quotient carrier or a host function-space object.

\begin{definition}[Banach operator graph-norm carrier]
\label{def:banach-operator-graph-norm-carrier}
A $\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(X,Y,T,\Gamma,A,M,Q,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source $\BanachUp$ row, including the source norm, metric, complete-metric, and real-seal reads.
\item $Y$ is the target $\BanachUp$ row, including the target norm, metric, complete-metric, and real-seal reads.
\item $T$ is the operator action row, read either through a displayed $\BoundedLinearOperatorUp$ packet or through a graph-closed operator row whose closedness is audited by $\ClosedGraphTheoremUp$.
\item $\Gamma$ is the graph row, recording source endpoints, target endpoints, and graph-membership witnesses for the displayed action.
\item $A$ is the graph-admission row, stating that only source endpoints whose graph membership is displayed in $\Gamma$ are admitted to the graph-norm domain.
\item $M$ is the metric readback row, routing source and target distance requests through $\MetricUp$ and $\CompleteMetricUp$ data.
\item $Q$ is the norm-sum row, recording the exact graph-norm expression $\|x\|_X+\|T x\|_Y$ as a displayed $\RealUp$ read.
\item $L$ is the completion handoff row, recording the Cauchy-to-limit route for graph-norm Cauchy data through $X$, $Y$, $\Gamma$, $M$, and $Q$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, target, operator, graph, admission, metric, norm-sum, completion handoff, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from graph admission through metric readback, norm-sum exactness, graph-closedness stability, and completion handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp$, $\BanachUp$, $\BoundedLinearOperatorUp$, $\ClosedGraphTheoremUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotienting the graph, selecting host representatives, importing an ambient operator space, or replacing the displayed norm-sum by an external equality theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Banach operator graph-norm obligations]
\label{thm:banach-operator-graph-norm-obligations}
For every accepted $\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp$ carrier $G=(X,Y,T,\Gamma,A,M,Q,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp}$ surface consists exactly of graph-norm carrier admission, graph-closedness stability, norm-sum exactness, completion handoff, and refusal of quotient graph export.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.BanachOperatorGraphNormUp.BanachOperatorGraphNormCarrier\_namecert\_obligations}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:banach-operator-graph-norm-carrier}. The accepted graph-norm domain is read through $A$ and $\Gamma$, so a source endpoint is admitted only when its graph-membership row is displayed.

Graph-closedness stability is read through $T,\Gamma,L,H,C$. The operator action is either already bounded-linear through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedlinearoperator-namecert} or is routed through the closed-graph certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closed-graph-theorem-namecert}; in both cases transport by $H$ and replay by $C$ preserve the same graph rows.

Norm-sum exactness is the role of $Q$. The source and target norm reads are measured through the metric and complete-metric rows $M$ and sealed as $\RealUp$ data, so the graph norm is exactly the displayed sum $\|x\|_X+\|T x\|_Y$.

Completion handoff is the role of $L$. A graph-norm Cauchy read supplies compatible Cauchy data in $X$ and $Y$, replays graph membership through $\Gamma$, and returns a displayed graph limit through the complete-metric rows.

Finally, the carrier inventory rules out quotient graph export. No row of $G$ stores a graph quotient, selected representative, ambient operator-space point, or host equality theorem, so every consumer remains inside the displayed NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Banach operator graph-norm completion handoff]
\label{thm:banach-operator-graph-norm-completion-handoff}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp$ packet factors through
$$
X,Y,T,\Gamma,A,M,Q,L,H,C,P,N .
$$
The handoff reads source completeness, target completeness, graph membership, the exact norm-sum row, and the graph-closedness row before exporting graph-norm completion data.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.BanachOperatorGraphNormUp.BanachOperatorGraphNormCompletionHandoff}
\begin{proof}
Start with the Banach source and target rows $X$ and $Y$. Their metric and complete-metric data provide the only Cauchy and limit witnesses available to the graph-norm packet.

Next read the graph rows $T,\Gamma,A$. The action row and graph-membership row determine which source endpoints are in the graph-norm domain, and the admission row prevents a consumer from reading outside the displayed graph.

Use $M$ and $Q$ for the graph norm itself. Metric readback supplies the source and target distances, while the norm-sum row records the exact real-valued graph-norm expression.

Finally apply $L,H,C,P,N$. The completion handoff replays graph-closedness through the same rows, then transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming preserve the packet. No quotient graph or external selector is exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Banach operator graph-norm no quotient graph]
\label{thm:banach-operator-graph-norm-no-quotient-graph}
No accepted $\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp$ consumer can replace the displayed graph row $\Gamma$ by a quotient graph, host function graph, or selected representative while retaining the local $\NameCert_{\BanachOperatorGraphNormUp}$ read.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project an accepted carrier $G=(X,Y,T,\Gamma,A,M,Q,L,H,C,P,N)$. The only graph coordinate is $\Gamma$, and graph-domain admission is mediated by $A$.

The metric and completion rows do not add another graph object. They only measure source and target reads, seal the norm-sum value, and replay graph-closedness through the completion handoff.

Transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming preserve the same displayed coordinates. Since the carrier contains no quotient graph, host function graph, or selected representative row, replacing $\Gamma$ by such an object leaves the accepted NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
