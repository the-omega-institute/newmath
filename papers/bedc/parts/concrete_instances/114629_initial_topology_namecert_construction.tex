\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\InitialTopologyUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-initial-topology-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki and Kelley on initial topologies, the $\InitialTopologyUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface where a source carrier is named by the weakest displayed topology making a finite family of maps continuous. It is adjacent to the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert} and the continuous-map handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}: the chapter names only finite inverse-image subbasis generation, finite intersection and union ledgers, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

\begin{definition}[Initial topology carrier]
\label{def:initial-topology-carrier}
An $\InitialTopologyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(S,I,T,F,B,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source carrier row whose points receive the generated topology;
\item $I$ is the finite index row for the displayed family of target spaces;
\item $T$ assigns to each index in $I$ a $\TopologyUp$ target row with its named open-row classifier;
\item $F$ assigns to each index a displayed map from $S$ to the corresponding target carrier;
\item $B$ is the subbasic inverse-image ledger, listing the rows $F_i^{-1}(V)$ for target opens $V$ named by $T_i$;
\item $U$ is the finite intersection and finite union ledger generated from $B$, with entries named as source opens and no appeal to an arbitrary open-set powerset;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,I,T,F,B,U$ and the local certificate rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay, showing that each $F_i$ reads source opens through $B$ and $U$ before it is consumed as continuous;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\InitialTopologyUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\InitialTopologyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares two packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for an ambient powerset topology on $S$, a choice-selected open basis, an infinite subbasis closure, or a host proof that a larger topology is available.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Initial topology NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:initial-topology-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\InitialTopologyUp$ carrier $Q=(S,I,T,F,B,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\InitialTopologyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the source row, finite index row, target topology rows, displayed map rows, subbasic inverse-image ledger, finite intersection and union ledger, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming. Its classifier stability is generated by the same rows, and its exactness claim is limited to the statement that every displayed source open is generated from the inverse images in $B$ by the finite ledger $U$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:initial-topology-carrier}. The source, index, target, map, subbasic, and finite-generation rows are the only rows from which the local certificate can read.

The subbasic rows are fixed before the generated source-open rows are read. Each entry of $B$ is an inverse image of a target-open row along a displayed map in $F$, and each entry of $U$ is obtained from finitely many such rows by the listed intersection and union constructors.

Transport and replay add no new open rows. The $\hsame$ row moves the displayed data componentwise, the $\Cont$ row replays the same inverse-image route, and the $\Pkg$ and $\NameCert$ rows source and name the finite packet. Thus no ambient powerset topology, selected basis, or infinite closure operation enters the obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Initial topology continuous-map handoff]
\label{thm:initial-topology-continuous-map-handoff}
Let $Q=(S,I,T,F,B,U,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\InitialTopologyUp$ carrier. For each index in $I$, any downstream $\ContinuousMapUp$ consumer of the displayed map row $F_i$ must first read the target topology row $T_i$, the inverse-image subbasis ledger $B$, the finite generation ledger $U$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and the local $\NameCert$ row $N$. The handoff exports continuity of the named maps only through the generated source topology and not through a detached ambient topology on $S$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a request for the continuous-map row associated to $F_i$. Carrier admission exposes the corresponding target topology row $T_i$ and the subbasic inverse-image entries in $B$ before any continuity read is available.

The source topology read then passes through $U$. This ledger is finite and generated from the displayed inverse images, so the consumer receives exactly the source-open rows needed to replay the inverse-image test for $F_i$.

Finally apply the structural rows. The $\hsame$ row keeps the same source, target, map, and generated-open entries attached; the $\Cont$ row records the replay order; and $P,N$ source and name the packet. Hence the $\ContinuousMapUp$ handoff is the finite initial-topology route, not an external topology assertion.
\end{proof}
