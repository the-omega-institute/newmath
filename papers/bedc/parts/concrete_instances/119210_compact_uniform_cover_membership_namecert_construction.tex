\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompactUniformCoverMembershipUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-cover-membership-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following the finite-cover reading of \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity}, the $\CompactUniformCoverMembershipUp$ packet records the cover-membership ledger that must be displayed before a compact-uniform-continuity proof folds local radii into a uniform modulus. It sits between the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the pointwise-modulus surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the finite subcover ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-subcover-ledger-namecert}, and the cover-read consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-cover-read-namecert}. The packet isolates the finite membership test for a source point against a bundled compact-net center; it does not export the final lower-bound fold or the completed $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff.

\begin{definition}[Compact-uniform cover-membership carrier]
\label{def:compact-uniform-cover-membership-carrier}
A $\CompactUniformCoverMembershipUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(K,F,L,B,x,c,\rho,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row exposing the finite compact-net data.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ pointwise-modulus row attached to the displayed map at compact-net centers.
\item $L$ is the $\FiniteSubcoverLedgerUp$ row that records which bundled cover entries are available.
\item $B$ is the $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ index row naming the finite cover entry that is inspected.
\item $x$ is the source-point history being routed through the compact cover.
\item $c$ is the displayed center row attached to the bundled cover entry.
\item $\rho$ is the positive radius row attached to $c$ by the pointwise-modulus data.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ comparison row recording that the inspected source point lies inside the displayed radius window.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact-net, pointwise-modulus, finite-subcover, ProbeBundle, point, center, radius, comparison, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact-net coverage through the bundled center and radius membership test.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactUniformCoverMembershipUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\FiniteSubcoverLedgerUp$, $\RatUp$, $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformCoverMembershipUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for an external point-set cover, a selected subcover, a global modulus, quotient point equality, countable choice, or ambient compactness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact-uniform cover-membership obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-cover-membership-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactUniformCoverMembershipUp$ carrier
$$
M=(K,F,L,B,x,c,\rho,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformCoverMembershipUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact-net admission through $K$, pointwise-modulus admission through $F$, finite-subcover ledger admission through $L$, bundled cover-entry admission through $B$, source point and center exposure through $x,c$, radius exposure through $\rho$, rational membership comparison through $Q$, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-uniform-cover-membership-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the compact-net row, pointwise-modulus row, finite-subcover ledger, bundled cover entry, inspected point, center, radius, rational comparison, and structural rows.

The membership test is local to $B,x,c,\rho,Q$. The bundle row names the finite cover entry, the source-point and center rows name the comparison endpoints, the radius row supplies the local modulus window, and the rational comparison row records that the inspected point is inside that window.

The compact and continuous rows are dependency rows, not hidden selectors. The compact row supplies only the finite-net source for the bundle, and the continuous row supplies only the radius attached to the displayed center.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite membership packet. Since no row stores an external point-set cover, selected subcover, global modulus, quotient point equality, countable choice, or ambient compactness theorem, none can be exported by the local NameCert.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cover membership before uniform-modulus cover read]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-cover-membership-cover-read-handoff}
Every $\CompactUniformCoverReadUp$ or $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer using an accepted $\CompactUniformCoverMembershipUp$ packet must first display the route
$$
K,L,B,x,c,\rho,Q
$$
before it may fold radii or consume a tolerance-only modulus. The handoff supplies only a finite membership row for one bundled compact-net center, not an external subcover selector, a completed lower-bound fold, quotient point equality, countable choice, or an ambient compactness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compact source $K$ and finite-subcover ledger $L$. They determine the finite cover entries that may be inspected by the compact-uniform route.

Choose only a displayed bundle entry $B$. The packet then exposes the inspected source point $x$, the bundled center $c$, and the radius row $\rho$ attached to that center by the pointwise-modulus row $F$.

Read the rational comparison $Q$. This row is the membership evidence needed before the cover-read chapter can fold radii across the finite bundle.

The packet stops at membership. The lower-bound fold and final tolerance-only consumer remain downstream in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-cover-read-namecert}, so this chapter exports no selected subcover, global modulus, quotient point equality, countable choice, or ambient compactness principle.
\end{proof}
