\begin{definition}[Poincare disk isometry carrier]
\label{def:poincare-disk-isometry-carrier}

$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\UnitDiskUp$ source-and-target disk row for the displayed points under inspection.
\item $F$ is the disk-map row naming the finite transport on those displayed points.
\item $G$ is the $\HyperbolicMetricUp$ or $\PoincareDiskMetricUp$ metric-preservation row for every displayed source-target pair.
\item $A$ is the $\PoincareDiskAutomorphismUp$ reversible-map row, including inverse and composition readbacks for $F$.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PoincareDiskIsometryUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,F,G,A,B,Q,H,C,P,N$ and has no coordinate for a non-invertible transport, hidden metric-preservation proof, completed disk model, quotient boundary object, or physical observer trajectory.
\end{definition}
