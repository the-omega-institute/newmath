Following Bishop--Bridges constructive completion practice, the $\LocatedDyadicCompletionUp$ packet records the finite BEDC route from located dyadic tolerance data to regular rational streams and the terminal real-name seal. It sits over the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a displayed completion interface for the current L10 Real and Completion phase; it does not import quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Located dyadic completion carrier]
\label{def:located-dyadic-completion-carrier}
A $\LocatedDyadicCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(D,S,R,L,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording dyadic radii, common refinements, and positive located gaps;
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row inspected by each tolerance request;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row reached after the stream windows have been displayed;
\item $L$ is the located split row, answering each dyadic comparison request before any real seal is exposed;
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, read only after $D,S,R,L$ have been projected;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the displayed dyadic, stream, regular, locatedness, and real rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for tolerance admission, finite-window readback, regular-sequence handoff, located split, and real sealing;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedDyadicCompletionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedDyadicCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,S,R,L,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, a selected completion limit, countable choice, detached order trichotomy, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located dyadic completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-dyadic-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedDyadicCompletionUp$ carrier $K=(D,S,R,L,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedDyadicCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of five finite rows: dyadic tolerance admission through $D$; finite stream-window inspection through $S$; regular rational readback through $R$; located comparison discharge through $L$; and real-seal nonescape through $E,H,C,P,N$. No obligation row exports quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, detached trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The dyadic tolerance ledger is exactly $D$, so every radius, refinement, and positive-gap request begins inside $\DyadicRatCoreUp$.

The finite observation route is $S$ followed by $R$. Thus a completion-facing consumer reads only displayed stream windows and their $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback before it can ask for a real boundary.

The located split row is $L$. It answers the comparison request associated with the displayed dyadic budget, but it is not a detached trichotomy principle and it does not select a global representative.

Finally inspect $E,H,C,P,N$. The terminal $\RealUp$ seal is reached only after the four rows $D,S,R,L$ have been projected, while the structural rows transport, replay, source, and name that same finite packet. Since the carrier has no excluded coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located dyadic completion Cauchy handoff]
\label{thm:located-dyadic-completion-cauchy-handoff}
Every $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\LocatedDyadicCompletionUp$ carrier factors through the route
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp .
$$
In particular, the Cauchy handoff from dyadic tolerance data to a real-name boundary is mediated by displayed finite windows and regular rational readback, not by quotienting Cauchy streams or selecting a limit object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the accepted carrier $K=(D,S,R,L,E,H,C,P,N)$. A regular rational consumer can inspect the readback row only after $D$ supplies the tolerance ledger and $S$ supplies the finite stream windows.

A real-name consumer reaches $E$ only after $R$ and $L$ have been read. The located row $L$ supplies the comparison response attached to the same dyadic budget, so the handoff carries the Cauchy information already displayed by $D,S,R$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve this order under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Hence the only exported route is the displayed dyadic-to-stream-to-regular-to-real chain, with no quotient stream or selected limit coordinate available.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located dyadic completion nonescape]
\label{thm:located-dyadic-completion-nonescape}
No accepted $\LocatedDyadicCompletionUp$ carrier can be used as evidence for quotient stream equality, countable choice, a choice-selected completion limit, detached trichotomy of real names, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$. Any downstream claim using the carrier must consume the displayed rows $D,S,R,L,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The carrier definition lists every exported coordinate. The computational rows are $D,S,R,L,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

Quotient stream equality and selected limits would require a coordinate identifying arbitrary Cauchy names or choosing a representative beyond the displayed stream windows. No such coordinate appears in the carrier.

Countable choice, detached trichotomy, and ambient real completeness would require a global principle independent of the finite dyadic ledger. The carrier instead routes comparison through $L$ and real sealing through $E$ after $D,S,R$ have already been displayed.

Therefore any downstream use must consume the listed rows, and the excluded principles cannot escape from the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedDyadicCompletionUp}$ packet.
\end{proof}
