\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CantorSeriesExpansionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cantor-series-expansion-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Cantor and Hardy--Wright, the $\CantorSeriesExpansionUp$ packet records a variable-base digit expansion as finite denominator-product windows before the expansion can enter the regular rational and Real seal pipeline. It sits beside the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the sibling Engel denominator route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-engel-expansion-namecert}. The packet names only finite base, digit, partial-sum, and tail-bound data; it does not quotient digit streams, select an infinite sum, invoke trichotomy, assume countable choice, or use ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Cantor series expansion carrier]
\label{def:cantor-series-expansion-carrier}
A $\CantorSeriesExpansionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(B,D,S,T,W,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the variable-base row, listing the accepted finite base prefix and the denominator products generated from it.
\item $D$ is the digit-bound row, recording for each displayed base entry the corresponding finite digit and its admissible bound.
\item $S$ is the finite partial-sum row, displaying the rational sums obtained from the visible base and digit windows.
\item $T$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance-ledger row, bounding the unread tail of the denominator-product expansion for each precision request.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, selecting exactly the base, digit, partial-sum, and tail-ledger prefixes inspected by a read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from $B,D,S,T,W$ and no other numeric coordinate.
\item $L$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after the regular rational readback row is displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the base, digit, partial-sum, tolerance-ledger, stream-window, readback, Real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a precision request through
$$
B,D,W \longmapsto S \longmapsto T \longmapsto R \longmapsto L .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CantorSeriesExpansionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\EngelExpansionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CantorSeriesExpansionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for quotient digit-stream equality, selected infinite sums, arbitrary base-choice functions, trichotomy, countable-choice selection, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CantorSeriesExpansion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cantor-series-expansion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CantorSeriesExpansionUp$ carrier $Q=(B,D,S,T,W,R,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CantorSeriesExpansionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the variable-base row, digit-bound row, finite partial-sum row, dyadic tolerance ledger, stream windows, regular readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item base-prefix visibility, requiring each denominator product consumed by a partial sum or tail bound to be generated from the displayed row $B$;
\item digit-bound exactness, requiring every displayed digit in $D$ to be checked against the corresponding displayed base entry before $S$ is read;
\item finite partial-sum exactness, requiring $S$ to expose only $\RatUp$ sums generated from the base and digit windows selected by $W$;
\item dyadic tail control, requiring every unread-tail claim consumed by $R$ or $L$ to pass through the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger $T$;
\item readback order, requiring the finite route $B,D,W \longmapsto S \longmapsto T \longmapsto R \longmapsto L$ before any Real-facing consumer receives the packet;
\item transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming for exactly the rows recorded in $H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No CantorSeriesExpansion NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier $Q=(B,D,S,T,W,R,L,H,C,P,N)$. The projection exposes the base prefix, digit bounds, partial sums, tolerance ledgers, stream windows, readback rows, and structural rows as finite BEDC coordinates.

The base and digit data are read before the analytic handoff. The row $B$ supplies denominator products, while $D$ supplies only digits whose bounds have been displayed against those bases. The row $W$ fixes the finite window in which both rows may be inspected.

The summation and tail steps are separate. The row $S$ records rational partial sums generated from the displayed base and digit windows, and the row $T$ records dyadic bounds for every unread tail before a regular rational or Real-facing consumer can use the expansion.

Finally $R$ and $L$ are downstream of $T$. Componentwise transport in $H$ and replay in $C$ preserve the same displayed route, while $P$ and $N$ only source and name it. Since the excluded host and choice data are absent from the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CantorSeriesExpansion Real handoff]
\label{thm:cantor-series-expansion-real-handoff}
Let $Q=(B,D,S,T,W,R,L,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CantorSeriesExpansionUp$ carrier. Every Real-facing read of the Cantor series expansion factors through
$$
B,D,W \longmapsto S \longmapsto T \longmapsto R \longmapsto L
$$
with structural support $H,C,P,N$. Thus the consumer receives variable-base rows, digit-bound rows, finite rational partial sums, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledgers, $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and a terminal $\RealUp$ seal, all as finite BEDC data.

The handoff supplies no quotient stream, no selected infinite sum, no arbitrary base-choice function, no trichotomy branch, no countable-choice selector, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle. It is therefore a traditional Cantor-series representation route in the L10 Real and Completion neighborhood, parallel to the Engel route but not identified with it as a host real theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a downstream consumer of $R$ or $L$. By \autoref{def:cantor-series-expansion-carrier}, neither row is an independent real object; both are downstream of the displayed base, digit, partial-sum, stream-window, and dyadic tail-ledger rows.

The consumer first reads $B$, $D$, and $W$. These rows expose the accepted denominator-product prefix, the digit bounds, and the finite window in which the expansion is being inspected.

Next the consumer reads $S$ and $T$. The partial-sum row records finite rational sums, while the dyadic tolerance ledger records the unread-tail control needed before regular rational readback is public.

The row $R$ then repacks the displayed sums and bounds as a $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and the terminal row $L$ seals only that regular rational route as a $\RealUp$ presentation. The structural rows transport, replay, package, and name the same route, so none of the excluded principles can be recovered from the packet.
\end{proof}
