\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-finite-open-cover-nerve-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Alexandroff and the standard nerve construction for finite covers, the $\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp$ packet records the finite simplicial incidence data carried by a displayed open cover without importing open-cover compactness, a selected refinement theorem, or ambient choice. It sits beside the compact source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, the compact metric finite-net source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite lower-bound fold of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finitenetminimumfold-namecert}, and the finite cover-fold route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probe-bundle-cover-fold-namecert}. The packet is a named nerve carrier over displayed cover rows, nonempty finite-intersection rows, incidence rows, $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ compatibility, $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ data.

\begin{definition}[Finite open-cover nerve carrier]
\label{def:finite-open-cover-nerve-carrier}
A $\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
N=(K,O,I,J,B,H,C,P,M)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the compact source row, read through $\CompactUp$ or $\CompactMetricUp$, that bounds the cover inventory as finite data.
\item $O=(O_1,\ldots,O_n)$ is the displayed finite open-cover row.
\item $I$ is the finite nonempty-intersection ledger, listing exactly those finite subfamilies of $O$ whose displayed intersection witness is present.
\item $J$ is the incidence row, recording vertex-to-cover membership and simplex-to-intersection membership for the nerve.
\item $B$ is the $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ compatibility row, aligning cover cells, finite probes, and lower-bound folds with the cover-fold surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probe-bundle-cover-fold-namecert}.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,O,I,J,B,C,P,M$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact source rows through cover membership, finite-intersection witnesses, nerve incidence, probe-bundle compatibility, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ProbeBundleCoverFoldUp$, $\FiniteNetMinimumFoldUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $M$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover compactness, a selected subcover theorem, a host simplicial realization, quotient point equality, countable choice, ambient metric completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite open-cover nerve NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-open-cover-nerve-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp$ carrier $N=(K,O,I,J,B,H,C,P,M)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact-source exposure, finite open-cover exposure, nonempty finite-intersection ledger exposure, nerve incidence exposure, $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ compatibility, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation is discharged by host open-cover compactness, a selected external refinement, quotient point equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-open-cover-nerve-carrier}. The rows $K,O,I,J,B$ are the whole mathematical surface: the compact source, the finite cover, the displayed nonempty intersections, the incidence relation, and the probe-bundle compatibility ledger.

The nerve data are finite because $O$ is displayed as a finite row and $I$ lists only the finite subfamilies whose intersection witness is present. The incidence row $J$ reads from those two rows, so it cannot introduce a host realization or an unlisted refinement.

The structural rows $H,C,P,M$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for the excluded compactness, choice, quotient, completeness, Lean, or bridge data, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite open-cover nerve compact-uniform route]
\label{thm:finite-open-cover-nerve-compact-uniform-route}
Every compact-uniform consumer that reads an accepted $\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp$ packet must factor through the displayed route
$$
\CompactUp/\CompactMetricUp \longmapsto O \longmapsto I \longmapsto J \longmapsto \mathsf{ProbeBundle} \longmapsto \FiniteNetMinimumFoldUp .
$$
The nerve row supplies finite cover incidence for the compact-uniform modulus route, including the cover-fold consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probe-bundle-cover-fold-namecert}, but it supplies no open-cover compactness theorem, selected subcover, host geometric realization, quotient point equality, countable-choice selector, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:finite-open-cover-nerve-namecert-obligations}. A consumer first sees the compact source row $K$ and the finite cover row $O$, then the nonempty finite-intersection ledger $I$ and incidence row $J$.

The probe compatibility row $B$ is read after those cover and incidence rows. It aligns the nerve inventory with the same finite probes and lower-bound folds used by the cover-fold route, so a compact-uniform modulus consumer receives finite incidence and fold data rather than a background compactness theorem.

The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve this route. No additional row can provide an external subcover selector, host realization, quotient equality, choice principle, or ambient real-completeness datum.
\end{proof}
