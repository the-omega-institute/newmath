\begin{definition}[Constructive closed interval locator carrier]
\label{def:constructive-closed-interval-locator-carrier}

$$
C=(L,U,A,D,M,S,R,E,H,K,P,N)
$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the lower endpoint admission row, read as a located real endpoint already named in the surrounding interval history.
\item $U$ is the upper endpoint admission row, read as the corresponding located real endpoint in the same history.
\item $A$ is the endpoint-order admission row, recording the finite evidence that the displayed lower endpoint is admitted before the displayed upper endpoint for this closed-interval read.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ enclosure ledger of dyadic cells and radii used to shrink rational endpoint windows.
\item $M$ is the located membership readback row, recording which rational-interval locator query is accepted, rejected outside the displayed enclosure, or deferred to a smaller dyadic cell.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ observation surface for endpoint and membership windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those finite windows and dyadic tolerance cells.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the closed-interval endpoint pair and the accepted membership read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoint admission, dyadic enclosure, membership readback, finite windows, regular rational readback, and terminal seal.

\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares packets only through the displayed endpoint admission, dyadic enclosure, membership readback, regular-rational handoff, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. It has no coordinate for trichotomy on real endpoints, quotient real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host compactness, or an ambient closed-interval object.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive closed interval locator NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:constructive-closed-interval-locator-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ConstructiveClosedIntervalLocatorUp$ carrier
$$
C=(L,U,A,D,M,S,R,E,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveClosedIntervalLocatorUp}$ obligation surface consists of five finite rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item endpoint admission, requiring the lower and upper endpoint rows $L,U$ and the endpoint-order admission row $A$ to be displayed before any locator read is exported;
\item dyadic enclosure, requiring every closed-interval refinement to pass through the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger $D$ and the finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows $S$;
\item located membership readback, requiring every accepted membership read to be one of the rational locator reads in $M$, interpreted through the same $D,S,R$ route;
\item Real-seal handoff, requiring any endpoint-pair or membership consumer to reach the terminal $\RealUp$ row $E$ only after $L,U,A,D,M,S,R$ have been displayed;
\item nonescape from trichotomy, choice, and quotient equality, requiring every consumer to remain inside $L,U,A,D,M,S,R,E,H,K,P,N$ and forbidding real-order trichotomy, quotient completion, host equality of real names, countable choice, selected endpoint representatives, and ambient compactness.
\end{enumerate}
These rows are exactly the NameCert surface for constructive closed-interval location over the L10 Real and Completion route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:constructive-closed-interval-locator-carrier}. Endpoint admission is supplied by the rows $L,U,A$, so the packet exposes a lower endpoint, an upper endpoint, and their displayed finite ordering admission before any locator read can be consumed.

The enclosure and membership rows are read next. The dyadic ledger $D$ records the finite tolerances and cells, while $M$ records the rational locator answers accepted at those cells. The finite windows $S$ and regular-rational row $R$ are the only route from these cells to endpoint and membership observations.

The handoff to real names is terminal. A consumer reaches $E$ through the displayed continuation route $K$ and the package provenance $P$, with componentwise transport supplied by $H$ and local naming by $N$.

Since no coordinate of the carrier stores a trichotomy oracle, quotient completion, selected endpoint representative, countable-choice functional, host equality of real names, or ambient compactness theorem, the five displayed rows exhaust the obligation surface.
\end{proof}
