\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BirkhoffPolytopeUp$}

Following Birkhoff and von Neumann, the $\BirkhoffPolytopeUp$ packet records the finite convex-combinatorial object whose points are doubly stochastic matrices and whose displayed vertices are permutation matrices. It sits after the matrix carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, the polytope carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polytope-namecert}, the convex-set surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, the rational and real scalar readbacks of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the finite permutation rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-permutation-namecert}, the finite list carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-list-namecert}, and the permutation-flow decomposition route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-birkhoff-von-neumann-finite-namecert}. The packet names the finite convex hull and stochastic-row boundary; it is not a host theorem about arbitrary matrix spaces, quotient matrix equality, selected decompositions, linear-programming duality, or compactness by choice.

\begin{definition}[Birkhoff polytope carrier]
\label{def:birkhoff-polytope-carrier}
A $\BirkhoffPolytopeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(M,R,C,V,L,A,D,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MatrixUp$ square-matrix row, including the finite row and column index lists on which every entry is read.
\item $R$ is the row-stochastic ledger, displaying nonnegative entries and unit row sums through $\RatUp$ or $\RealUp$ scalar rows.
\item $C$ is the column-stochastic ledger, displaying the matching unit column sums over the same finite index spine.
\item $V$ is the finite permutation-vertex row, listing the $\PermutationUp$ matrix rows admitted as vertices of the polytope.
\item $L$ is the finite $\ListUp$ convex-combination ledger, carrying displayed scalar weights, total-one readback, and support membership for the listed vertices.
\item $A$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ affine-membership row for the convex hull generated by $V$ and $L$.
\item $D$ is the decomposition handoff row, available only after the stochastic ledgers and the finite vertex list have been displayed; it may cite the route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-birkhoff-von-neumann-finite-namecert}.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for matrix, stochastic, vertex-list, convex-combination, affine-membership, decomposition, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same finite convex-combinatorial rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BirkhoffPolytopeUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\PolytopeUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$, $\RatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\PermutationUp$, $\ListUp$, $\BirkhoffVonNeumannFiniteUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BirkhoffPolytopeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,R,C,V,L,A,D,H,K,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host matrix equality, arbitrary ambient matrix spaces, selected convex decompositions, linear-programming duality, open-cover compactness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Birkhoff polytope NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:birkhoff-polytope-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BirkhoffPolytopeUp$ carrier
$$
B=(M,R,C,V,L,A,D,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BirkhoffPolytopeUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite matrix admission, row-stochastic exposure, column-stochastic exposure, permutation-vertex exposure, finite-list convex-combination exposure, convex-set affine membership, decomposition handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every consumer that treats a doubly stochastic matrix as a point of the Birkhoff polytope must read $M,R,C$ before $V,L,A,D$; every consumer that treats the point as a convex combination of permutation matrices must read the finite list and decomposition rows before transport or local naming is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:birkhoff-polytope-carrier}. The stochastic matrix surface is exactly $M,R,C$: the finite square matrix row, nonnegative entry reads, and unit row and column sum ledgers.

The vertex and convex-combination surface is $V,L,A$. The permutation vertices are displayed as finite $\PermutationUp$ matrix rows, the convex weights are displayed as a $\ListUp$ ledger with scalar readbacks through $\RatUp$ or $\RealUp$, and the affine-membership row reads those two surfaces through $\ConvexSetUp$ and $\PolytopeUp$.

The decomposition handoff $D$ is downstream from both surfaces. When a decomposition-facing consumer needs the Birkhoff--von Neumann finite route, it cites \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-birkhoff-von-neumann-finite-namecert} only after the stochastic rows and vertex-list rows are already visible.

Finally $H,K,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no row for host matrix equality, arbitrary ambient matrix spaces, selected decompositions, linear-programming duality, compactness by choice, Lean verification, or bridge checking, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Birkhoff polytope finite vertex route]
\label{thm:birkhoff-polytope-finite-vertex-route}
Every accepted $\BirkhoffPolytopeUp$ read that exports a convex-combinatorial conclusion factors through the finite route
$$
\MatrixUp \longmapsto \PermutationUp \longmapsto \ListUp \longmapsto \ConvexSetUp \longmapsto \PolytopeUp .
$$
The route exposes the doubly stochastic matrix rows, the finite permutation-matrix vertex list, the convex weight ledger, affine membership, and the polytope naming row before any downstream matrix, optimization, or decomposition consumer may inspect the result.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:birkhoff-polytope-namecert-obligations}. A convex-combinatorial read cannot bypass the matrix row $M$, the stochastic ledgers $R,C$, or the permutation-vertex row $V$.

Next read the finite list row $L$. It supplies the displayed weights and vertex support, so the affine row $A$ receives an actual finite convex-combination ledger rather than an external selector.

The $\PolytopeUp$ source is present through provenance and the affine-membership row: it names the convex hull surface generated by those vertices, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-birkhoff-von-neumann-finite-namecert} may supply the finite decomposition route when a stochastic matrix is consumed as such a convex combination.

The structural rows then preserve exactly that route. No host equality, selected decomposition oracle, linear-programming theorem, or compactness principle is available inside the packet.
\end{proof}
