\begin{definition}[Picard iteration budget carrier]
\label{def:picard-iteration-budget-carrier}
A $\PicardIterationBudgetUp$ carrier is a finite packet
$$
B=(S,K,W,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the seed row, naming the initial point, accepted metric source, and contraction orbit entry used by the budget.
\item $K$ is the contraction-modulus row, storing the displayed contraction comparison and the finite modulus read from the Banach contraction surface.
\item $W$ is the iterate-window row, listing the finite interval of Picard iterates inspected by the budget request.
\item $D$ is the dyadic error-budget row, recording the requested tolerance, the residual comparison, and the finite arithmetic certificate that the window is long enough.
\item $E$ is the endpoint-handoff row, exposing only the completion-facing or Real-facing boundary available after $S,K,W,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the seed, modulus, window, dyadic error budget, endpoint handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the seed row through the contraction modulus, iterate window, dyadic budget, endpoint handoff, transport, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PicardIterationBudgetUp$, $\CauchyContractionOrbitUp$, $\BanachContractionUp$, $\PicardIterationUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PicardIterationBudgetUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,K,W,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a hidden infinite orbit, a selected fixed point, quotient equality of endpoint names, countable choice, host function extensionality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Picard iteration budget NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:picard-iteration-budget-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PicardIterationBudgetUp$ carrier $B=(S,K,W,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\PicardIterationBudgetUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the seed row, contraction-modulus row, iterate-window row, dyadic error-budget row, endpoint-handoff row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.

The classifier agreement obligation requires two accepted packets to agree precisely when these rows agree componentwise through the displayed $\hsame$ route. The stability obligation requires the same row order to be preserved by $\hsame$ transport and $\Cont$ replay before any endpoint handoff is consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:picard-iteration-budget-carrier}. The analytic part is $S,K,W,D,E$; the structural certificate part is $H,C,P,N$.

The seed row and contraction-modulus row determine what orbit may be inspected. The iterate-window row then restricts the read to finitely many Picard rows, and the dyadic error-budget row records the tolerance comparison attached to that window.

Endpoint handoff is terminal for the budget route. Since $E$ is downstream of $S,K,W,D$, no consumer can read endpoint data without first exposing the finite seed, modulus, iterate window, and dyadic budget rows.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. No excluded object is a carrier coordinate, so no hidden infinite orbit, selected fixed point, quotient endpoint equality, choice principle, host extensionality rule, or ambient Real-completeness theorem is a local obligation.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:picard-iteration-budget-exactness}

\begin{proof}
