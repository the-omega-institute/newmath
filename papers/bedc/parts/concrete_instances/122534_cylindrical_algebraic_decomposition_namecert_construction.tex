\item $P$ is the polynomial-family row, listing the admitted coefficient arrays, variable levels, formal derivatives, and specialization requests.

\begin{theorem}[Cylindrical algebraic decomposition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cylindrical-algebraic-decomposition-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:cylindrical-algebraic-decomposition-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\CylindricalAlgebraicDecompositionUp}$ surface consists exactly of five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the finite packet $D=(R,P,G,S,I,C,L,H,A,Q,N)$ over the displayed $\RealClosedFieldUp$ and $\PolynomialUp$ sources;
\item projection coverage through $G$, requiring every discriminant, resultant, leading-coefficient, derivative, and specialization row used by the decomposition to appear in the finite projection ledger;
\item sign-invariance through $C$, requiring every admitted polynomial row from $P$ to have one displayed sign row on each retained section or sector;
\item lifting and sample readback through $S,I,L$, requiring root counts, isolating windows, cell-stack links, and sample windows to be read before any higher cylinder level is accepted;
\item nonescape from host semialgebraic sets, selected roots, classical trichotomy, quotient equality, global algebraic closure, and choice, enforced by the transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows $H,A,Q,N$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cylindrical-algebraic-decomposition-carrier}. The rows $R$ and $P$ give the only ordered-field and polynomial sources, while $G,S,I,C,L$ are the finite algebraic rows that can be inspected before the structural rows are read.

Projection coverage is finite because $G$ lists exactly the resultants, discriminants, leading coefficients, derivatives, specialization rows, and reduction traces used by this packet. A consumer cannot appeal to an unlisted projection polynomial.

Sign-invariance is read from $C$ only after the Sturm and isolation rows have split each base cell into the displayed sections and sectors. Thus the sign row is tied to a retained cell, not to a host semialgebraic object.

The lifting read is the sequence $S,I,L$: first count roots, then retain isolating windows, then attach the lifted section or sector to its parent cell and sample window. The structural rows $H,A,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same finite packet. Since no coordinate stores host semialgebraic sets, selected roots, trichotomy, quotient equality, global algebraic closure, or choice, the five rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
