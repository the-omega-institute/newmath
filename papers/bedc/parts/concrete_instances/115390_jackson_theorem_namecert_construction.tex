\label{thm:jackson-theorem-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:jackson-theorem-carrier}. The analytic rows are $X,F,M,R,D,P,T,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,Q,N$.

The modulus row $M$ is the only source of quantitative continuity data. The rate row $R$ and the degree or frequency row $D$ therefore read a finite budget from the displayed modulus rather than from an external smoothness theorem.

The polynomial row $P$ and the Fejer row $T$ supply the approximation family. The error row $E$ then binds that family to the inspected function and tolerance, while $H,C,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route.

Since no further coordinate is present, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations and exclude selected best approximants, quotient function equality, host compactness, unbounded Fourier convergence, choice, and ambient real completeness.
