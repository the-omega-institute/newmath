Following Alexandrov's finite-topology viewpoint and the standard finite examples built from distinguished points, the $\ExcludedPointTopologyUp$ packet records the excluded-point topology as a finite BEDC NameCert carrier. It is the order-dual neighbour of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-particular-point-topology-namecert}, consumes the indexed open-row surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, exports observable-open reads to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert}, and supplies a $T_0$ comparison route for \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tzero-reflection-namecert}. The packet names only the finite rule saying that every nonempty proper open row avoids the distinguished point; it does not import a host powerset, arbitrary point equality, excluded middle over subsets, or a separation theorem beyond the displayed rows.

\begin{definition}[Excluded-point topology carrier]
\label{def:excluded-point-topology-carrier}
An $\ExcludedPointTopologyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(X,p,T,O,S,Z,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the finite point-name ledger.
\item $p$ is the distinguished excluded point row.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ indexed-open row whose generated opens are $\emptyset$, the whole displayed point row, and the displayed nonempty proper rows that do not contain $p$.
\item $O$ is the open-membership exactness row, recording that a proper open read is admitted only after the row has displayed avoidance of $p$.
\item $S$ is the $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ observable-open handoff, reading every accepted membership request as a displayed truth row.
\item $Z$ is the $\TZeroReflectionUp$ comparison row, exposing the specialization contrast between the excluded point and ordinary displayed points.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the point ledger, distinguished point, topology, open-exactness, observable truth, $T_0$ comparison, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the point ledger and excluded point through $T,O,S,Z$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ExcludedPointTopologyUp$, $\ParticularPointTopologyUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, $\TZeroReflectionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ExcludedPointTopologyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,p,T,O,S,Z,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host powerset topology, selected arbitrary subsets, quotient point equality, or ambient separation data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Excluded-point topology NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:excluded-point-topology-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ExcludedPointTopologyUp$ carrier
$$
E=(X,p,T,O,S,Z,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ExcludedPointTopologyUp}$ surface consists exactly of point-ledger admission through $X$, distinguished-point naming through $p$, excluded-point open generation through $T$, proper-open avoidance exactness through $O$, observable-open handoff through $S$, $T_0$ comparison through $Z$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The point data are only $X$ and $p$, and the topology data are only the indexed open rows admitted by $T$.

The excluded-point rule is carried by $O$. A proper open row can be consumed only when its finite membership ledger displays that $p$ is absent, while $\emptyset$ and the whole displayed point row are recorded as the two boundary opens of $T$.

Observable reads pass through $S$, and the specialization comparison passes through $Z$. These rows expose truth values and $T_0$ contrast only from the displayed open ledger, not from a host powerset.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no other coordinate is present, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Excluded-point topology Sierpinski and T0 handoff]
\label{thm:excluded-point-topology-sierpinski-handoff}
Every $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ or $\TZeroReflectionUp$ consumer of an accepted $\ExcludedPointTopologyUp$ packet must read the finite route
$$
X,p \longrightarrow T \longrightarrow O \longrightarrow S,Z \longrightarrow H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus a membership truth row is observable only through the excluded-point open ledger, and the $T_0$ comparison is read only after the same observable-open route has displayed the distinguished point boundary. The handoff exports no host powerset, arbitrary subset selector, quotient point equality, proof irrelevance for opens, or ambient separation theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with $X$ and $p$. They are the only point-source rows, so an open-membership request has no ambient point set outside the displayed ledger.

Read $T$ and $O$ next. The topology row supplies the indexed opens, while the exactness row forces every nontrivial proper open to avoid the distinguished point before it can be consumed.

The observable-open handoff $S$ turns those indexed membership reads into Sierpinski truth rows. The $T_0$ comparison row $Z$ then reads the same observable-open data to separate the distinguished point from ordinary displayed points when the finite ledger contains the corresponding witness.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve this route as $H,C,P,N$. The refused host objects have no carrier row, so neither consumer can extract them from the packet.
\end{proof}
