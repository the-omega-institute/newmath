\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\IdempotentCompletionSplittingUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-idempotent-completion-splitting-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Mac Lane on idempotent splittings and Eilenberg--Moore completion algebras, the $\IdempotentCompletionSplittingUp$ packet records the finite categorical surface where a completion idempotent is consumed by an explicit splitting row. It sits beside the category surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-category-namecert}, the monad surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-monad-namecert}, the completion category of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-category-namecert}, the Eilenberg--Moore completion algebra of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-eilenberg-moore-completion-algebra-namecert}, and the regular rational and real completion route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a splitting interface for L10 completion consumers; it is not a quotient completion, not a selected limit, and not host equality of completed objects.

\begin{definition}[Idempotent completion splitting carrier]
\label{def:idempotent-completion-splitting-carrier}
An $\IdempotentCompletionSplittingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(O,E,S,R,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the object row, naming the completion object read through $\CauchyCompletionCategoryUp$ and the underlying $\CategoryUp$ surface.
\item $E$ is the idempotent row, naming the displayed endomorphism on $O$ and its finite idempotence witness.
\item $S$ is the splitting row, carrying the displayed retract object together with section and retraction arrows inside the completion category.
\item $R$ is the retract law row, exposing the two composite equalities only as finite category-law and completion-algebra comparisons.
\item $T$ is the terminal completion-readback row through $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ when the splitting is consumed by the L10 route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for object, idempotent, splitting, retract-law, readback, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route from object and idempotent rows to the splitting row and then to the completion readback row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\IdempotentCompletionSplittingUp$, $\CauchyCompletionCategoryUp$, $\EilenbergMooreCompletionAlgebraUp$, $\CategoryUp$, $\MonadUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\IdempotentCompletionSplittingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $O,E,S,R,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, ambient split-idempotent completion, countable choice, host equality, or Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Idempotent completion splitting NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:idempotent-completion-splitting-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\IdempotentCompletionSplittingUp$ carrier $K=(O,E,S,R,T,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\IdempotentCompletionSplittingUp}$ surface consists exactly of object exposure, idempotent exposure, splitting-row exposure, retract-law exposure, completion-readback exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, local naming, and non-escape from quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier in \autoref{def:idempotent-completion-splitting-carrier}. The only displayed rows are $O,E,S,R,T,H,C,P,N$, so each local obligation must be read from one of those rows.

The categorical obligations are exhausted by $O,E,S,R$. The object and idempotent rows name the endomorphism being split, the splitting row names the retract data, and the retract-law row names the two finite composites inside the completion category and Eilenberg--Moore algebra surface.

The analytic completion readback is downstream of the splitting data. Row $T$ may expose the regular rational, dyadic, and Real-facing route, but only after the categorical splitting rows have already been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier contains no quotient, selection, countable-choice, host-equality, or completeness coordinate, the non-escape obligation follows by exhaustion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Idempotent completion splitting nonescape]
\label{thm:idempotent-completion-splitting-nonescape}
Every $\IdempotentCompletionSplittingUp$ consumer must expose $O,E,S,R,T,H,C,P,N$ before reading a completed object through a split idempotent. The packet cannot export a quotient completion, a selected splitting, ambient Karoubi completion, host equality of completed objects, countable choice, trichotomy, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a consumer that reads a completed object through an idempotent. The carrier first exposes $O$ and $E$, so the read is attached to the displayed object and displayed endomorphism.

The consumer then reaches the splitting only through $S$ and the retract laws only through $R$. These rows live inside the finite completion-category and completion-algebra surfaces, so they do not import an ambient category of all split idempotents.

The terminal readback row $T$ is reached after the splitting rows. It may pass through $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$, but it does not add quotient completion equality or a selected representative.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite route. Therefore a consumer receives the displayed splitting packet and no stronger quotient, selection, choice, trichotomy, host-equality, or completeness principle.
\end{proof}
