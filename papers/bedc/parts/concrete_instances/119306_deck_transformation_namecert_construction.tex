\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DeckTransformationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-deck-transformation-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Hatcher and Spanier on covering-space automorphisms, the $\DeckTransformationUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which a covering projection admits a fiber-preserving automorphism route with displayed inverse and composition rows. It sits beside the path-lifting certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-covering-space-path-lifting-namecert}, the homotopy rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homotopy-namecert}, the continuous-map carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, and the topology surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}. The packet names deck transformation data as finite carrier and NameCert rows; it does not import a universal cover, monodromy action, quotient path space, selected global sheet, or host equality of maps.

\begin{definition}[DeckTransformation carrier]
\label{def:deck-transformation-carrier}
A $\DeckTransformationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(\pi,a,u,i,c,s,h,r,p,n)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\pi$ is the covering projection row, read as a $\ContinuousMapUp$ packet from the displayed total-space topology to the base $\TopologyUp$ row.
\item $a$ is the automorphism route. It records a displayed total-space self-map whose projection readback follows $\pi$ on every accepted finite test row.
\item $u$ is the base-preservation ledger. It exposes the row-by-row comparison witnessing that $\pi$ after $a$ reads as the same covering projection route.
\item $i$ is the inverse row. It records the displayed inverse route and the two finite identity readbacks for $a$ followed by $i$ and $i$ followed by $a$.
\item $c$ is the composition row. It records closure of accepted automorphism routes under displayed composition over the same projection ledger.
\item $s$ is the sheet-action classifier. It compares finite sheet reads by the covering projection, path-lifting route, and endpoint seal, not by a host-level global sheet selector.
\item $h$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the projection, automorphism route, preservation ledger, inverse row, composition row, sheet classifier, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $r$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a deck-transformation request through $\pi,a,u,i,c,s,h,p,n$.
\item $p$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DeckTransformationUp$, $\CoveringSpacePathLiftingUp$, $\HomotopyUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $n$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DeckTransformationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a universal covering space, monodromy action, quotient path object, hidden group representation, selected global sheet, or host equality of continuous maps.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[DeckTransformation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:deck-transformation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DeckTransformationUp$ carrier
$$
D=(\pi,a,u,i,c,s,h,r,p,n),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DeckTransformationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, projection preservation through $\pi,a,u$, inverse exactness through $i$, composition closure through $c$, sheet-classifier stability through $s$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $h$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $r$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $p$, and local naming through $n$. Every deck-transformation consumer can inspect these rows, but cannot escape to universal-cover data, quotient-path data, monodromy actions, fundamental-group representations, selected global sheets, or host equality of maps.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:deck-transformation-carrier}. The accepted tuple displays the covering projection, automorphism route, base-preservation ledger, inverse row, composition row, sheet classifier, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Projection preservation is the content of $u$. It forces every accepted read of $a$ to return through the same covering projection route $\pi$, so the automorphism is fiber-preserving at the level visible to the packet.

Inverse exactness is read from $i$. The two identity readbacks are finite rows in the carrier, and no host-level equality of continuous maps is added beyond those displayed rows.

Composition closure is read from $c$. Whenever two accepted automorphism routes share the displayed covering projection ledger, their composite is admitted by the same finite surface and remains attached to $\pi$.

The sheet-action classifier $s$ is stable because it is computed from the projection ledger, the path-lifting route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-covering-space-path-lifting-namecert}, and the endpoint seal already named there. Transport, replay, provenance, and naming through $h,r,p,n$ preserve the same rows, so the certificate has no remaining place to store universal-cover data, quotient-path data, monodromy actions, or fundamental-group representations.
\end{proof}
