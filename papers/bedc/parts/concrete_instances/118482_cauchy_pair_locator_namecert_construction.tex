\item $B$ is the second $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, carrying the regular rational observations read from the second name.
\item $S$ is the shared $\StreamNameUp$ window row, recording the finite windows inspected from both names before a locator request is answered.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, displaying the dyadic radius, split tolerance, comparison budget, and endpoint accounting used by the pair read.

\label{thm:cauchy-pair-locator-namecert-obligations}

\item carrier admission for the two regular readback rows, shared stream windows, dyadic tolerance ledger, pair-modulus row, locator row, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row;
