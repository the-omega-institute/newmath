\begin{theorem}[Proper metric completion boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:proper-metric-completion-boundary-namecert-obligations}

No NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows, and no accepted read can recover a quotient completion, a selected representative, a countable-choice selector, an open-cover compactness principle, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The proper metric route is finite. The source row $M$ supplies a radius-window ledger, $R$ records the corresponding closed-ball membership, and $C$ records the compact-metric witness attached to that same window.

The completion-facing row is downstream of those windows. The boundary request $Q$ can be read only after the compact-ball handoff $K$ and the alignment row $W$ are exposed, and its radius comparisons are sealed by $E$ through the displayed Real readback.

Finally, $H,T,P,N$ preserve this same route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion points, selected representatives, countable choice, open-cover compactness, host equality, or ambient completeness, the local NameCert surface cannot export any of those principles.
\end{proof}
