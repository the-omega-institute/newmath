\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BalancedTruncationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-balanced-truncation-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Moore's balanced realization construction and Antoulas's reduced-order modeling treatment, the $\BalancedTruncationUp$ packet records the finite certificate surface that turns reachability and observability energy rows into a reduced state-space model with an explicit error ledger. It sits after the state-space presentation of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-statespacemodel-namecert}, the Gramian surfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-reachabilitygramian-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-observability-gramian-namecert}, the Hankel window of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hankel-operator-namecert}, and the Riccati/control consumers of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riccati-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lqr-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-modelpredictivecontrol-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[Balanced truncation carrier]
\label{def:balanced-truncation-carrier}
A $\BalancedTruncationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(S,G_r,G_o,H,k,E,T,R,Q,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the finite state-space row read through $\StateSpaceModelUp$, including the state, input, output, and transition matrices used by the reduction request.
\item $G_r$ is the $\ReachabilityGramianUp$ row and $G_o$ is the $\ObservabilityGramianUp$ row, both attached to the same displayed state-space row $S$.
\item $H$ is the $\HankelOperatorUp$ window comparing the reachable and observable energy coordinates visible in $G_r$ and $G_o$.
\item $k$ is the truncation-order row, bounded by the displayed state dimension and recorded as finite BEDC data rather than as an ambient spectral projection.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ error-bound row for the discarded Hankel window.
\item $T$ is the reduced state-space row obtained by reading $S,G_r,G_o,H,k,E$ and exposing only the retained coordinates.
\item $R$ is the $\RiccatiUp$ dependency row available to control consumers that compare reduced Riccati data with the source model.
\item $Q$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the state-space, Gramian, Hankel, order, error, reduced-model, Riccati, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from every reduction request through $S,G_r,G_o,H,k,E,T,R,Q,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BalancedTruncationUp$, $\StateSpaceModelUp$, $\ReachabilityGramianUp$, $\ObservabilityGramianUp$, $\HankelOperatorUp$, $\RiccatiUp$, $\LQRUp$, $\ModelPredictiveControlUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BalancedTruncationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,G_r,G_o,H,k,E,T,R,Q,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an unlogged infinite-dimensional Hankel operator, hidden singular-vector basis, host norm equality, external optimality theorem, selected controller, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Balanced truncation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:balanced-truncation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BalancedTruncationUp$ carrier $B=(S,G_r,G_o,H,k,E,T,R,Q,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BalancedTruncationUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item common state-space admission, requiring $G_r$ and $G_o$ to be read as Gramian rows over the same displayed $\StateSpaceModelUp$ row $S$;
\item Hankel-window exposure, requiring $H$ to display the finite comparison window between reachable and observable energy rows before any truncation order is consumed;
\item order and error binding, requiring $k$ and the $\RealUp$ error-bound row $E$ to be visible before the reduced row $T$ is exported;
\item downstream control handoff, requiring Riccati, LQR, and model-predictive-control consumers to read only the displayed reduced row and its source rows;
\item ledger non-escape, requiring $Q,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed packet, with no hidden singular-vector basis, host norm equality, detached optimality theorem, selected controller, or ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:balanced-truncation-carrier}. The reachability and observability rows are both tied to the same state-space row $S$, so the energy comparison cannot be read from two unrelated models.

The Hankel row $H$ is downstream of $G_r$ and $G_o$. Thus the truncation order $k$ and the error-bound row $E$ are attached to a displayed finite Hankel window rather than to an unlogged operator or hidden basis.

The reduced row $T$ is exported only after $S,G_r,G_o,H,k,E$ are visible. Control consumers then receive a finite handoff through $R$ and the provenance rows, not an external controller or an ambient optimality principle.

Finally, $Q,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same coordinates. Since the refused objects do not occur in the carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Balanced truncation Hankel-Riccati handoff]
\label{thm:balanced-truncation-hankel-riccati-handoff}
Every downstream $\RiccatiUp$, $\LQRUp$, or $\ModelPredictiveControlUp$ consumer of an accepted $\BalancedTruncationUp$ packet must pass through the displayed $\HankelOperatorUp$ window $H$, truncation-order row $k$, $\RealUp$ error-bound row $E$, and reduced state-space row $T$ before reading any Riccati or finite-horizon control data. The handoff exports no hidden singular-vector basis, detached Hankel norm equality, selected controller, infinite-horizon optimality theorem, or ambient state-space completion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the rows $S,G_r,G_o,H,k,E,T,R,Q,C,P,N$ from \autoref{def:balanced-truncation-carrier}. The Riccati dependency row $R$ appears only after the Hankel window, truncation order, error-bound row, and reduced model have been displayed.

Use the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:balanced-truncation-namecert-obligations}. Hankel-window exposure binds $H$ to the two Gramian rows, while order and error binding force $k,E,T$ to precede any exported reduced-control read.

The downstream handoff clause then restricts Riccati, LQR, and model-predictive-control consumers to the displayed reduced row and its provenance. Transport and replay through $Q$ and $C$ keep the handoff inside the same finite packet.

Because no hidden basis, detached norm equality, selected controller, infinite-horizon optimality theorem, or ambient completion coordinate is present in $B$, none can be exported by the handoff.
\end{proof}
