Following Girard proof nets, the $\ProofNetUp$ packet records proof objects as finite link-and-switching data rather than as host proof trees or quotient proof identities. It sits between the syntax and deduction rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-firstorder-namecert}, the proof-term corridor of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lambdacalc-namecert}, the normalization-by-evaluation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-normalization-by-evaluation-namecert}, the logical-relation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-logical-relation-namecert}, and the MetaCIC critical path of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metaciccriticalpath-namecert}. The packet names finite link tables, switching choices, cut interfaces, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it does not assert full normalization, confluence, quotient proof equality, a completed semantic model, or a global cut-elimination theorem.

\begin{definition}[Proof-net carrier]
\label{def:proof-net-carrier}
A $\ProofNetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(L,S,C,B,R,H,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the link table, listing axiom, tensor, par, unit, dereliction, contraction, weakening, and cut links only as finite syntax rows.
\item $S$ is the switching row, recording the finite switching choices and connectedness checks exposed to a consumer.
\item $C$ is the cut-interface row, naming the displayed cut pairs and the local redex boundary without collapsing proof nets by cut conversion.
\item $B$ is the boundary row, tying conclusions, formula occurrences, and sequent-facing endpoints to the $\FirstOrderUp$ and $\LambdaCalcUp$ source surfaces.
\item $R$ is the proof-net route row consumed by $\NormalizationByEvaluationUp$, $\LogicalRelationUp$, and $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ readers before they may cite proof-object data.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for link tables, switching rows, cut interfaces, boundaries, routes, provenance, and local naming.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ProofNetUp$, $\FirstOrderUp$, $\LambdaCalcUp$, $\NormalizationByEvaluationUp$, $\LogicalRelationUp$, $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ProofNetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for completed proof normalization, Church--Rosser confluence, quotient proof equality, a semantic completeness theorem, or a host evaluator.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Proof-net NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:proof-net-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ProofNetUp$ carrier $P=(L,S,C,B,R,H,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ProofNetUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the link table, switching row, cut-interface row, boundary row, proof-net route row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. A consumer may read proof-net data only after the finite links, switchings, cuts, boundaries, and route rows are displayed in the same packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:proof-net-carrier}. The packet exposes only $L,S,C,B,R,H,Q,N$.

The proof-object obligations are exhausted by $L,S,C,B,R$. Link data are finite syntax rows, switching data are finite checks, cut data are local interfaces, and boundary data keep the proof-net endpoints attached to the $\FirstOrderUp$ and $\LambdaCalcUp$ surfaces.

The rows $H,Q,N$ transport, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores global normalization, confluence, quotient proof equality, semantic completeness, or a host evaluator, the listed rows are exactly the local certificate obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Proof-net cut-elimination route]
\label{thm:proof-net-cut-elimination-route}
Every cut-elimination-facing read of an accepted $\ProofNetUp$ packet must first expose
$$
L,S,C,B,R,H,Q,N
$$
and may then hand the displayed local cut-interface route to $\NormalizationByEvaluationUp$, $\LogicalRelationUp$, or $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ consumers. The handoff is a finite proof-net carrier route over displayed links, switchings, and cut interfaces; it is not a proof of full normalization, unrestricted confluence, quotient proof equality, or a completed semantic model.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the link and switching rows $L,S$ first. They display the finite graph and switching data available to a proof-net consumer.

Next read the cut-interface row $C$ and boundary row $B$. These rows identify the local redex boundary and the sequent or proof-term endpoints without quotienting proof nets by cut conversion.

Finally read the route and structural rows $R,H,Q,N$. They allow the normalization-by-evaluation, logical-relation, and MetaCIC critical-path consumers to cite the same finite route, while the carrier inventory excludes full normalization, confluence, quotient proof equality, and completed semantic-model coordinates.
\end{proof}
