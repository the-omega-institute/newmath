\label{def:finite-window-subcover-carrier}

$$

\begin{theorem}[Finite-window subcover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-window-subcover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteWindowSubcoverUp$ carrier
$$
B=(U,F,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteWindowSubcoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of cover-request visibility through $U$, finite source and candidate-cell exposure through $F$, $\StreamNameUp$ window reads through $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, dyadic tolerance accounting through $D$, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The cover-facing data are finite. Row $U$ fixes the request, row $F$ lists the displayed candidates, and row $S$ opens only the inspected StreamName windows.

\begin{theorem}[Heine--Borel finite-window subcover route]
\label{thm:heine-borel-finite-window-subcover-route}
Every subcover read exported by an accepted $\FiniteWindowSubcoverUp$ carrier stays inside the displayed finite windows $U,F,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. The route does not assert general open-cover compactness, arbitrary subcover selection, countable choice, quotient stream equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Next read the numerical rows. The rational thresholds in $R$ and dyadic comparisons in $D$ are attached to the same finite StreamName windows, so no cover membership can be imported from outside $U,F,S$.

Finally consume the Real seal $E$ only after the finite readback route has been displayed. The support rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that route uniformly, and the carrier contains no coordinate for the excluded open-cover, selector, choice, quotient, or ambient-completeness principles.
\end{proof}
