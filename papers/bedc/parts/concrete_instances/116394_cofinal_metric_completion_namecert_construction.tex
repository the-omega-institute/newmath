\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CofinalMetricCompletionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cofinal-metric-completion-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Bourbaki on uniform and metric completions, and Bishop--Bridges on constructive completion by regular approximants, the $\CofinalMetricCompletionUp$ packet names the finite cofinal-window surface by which a metric-completion consumer reads regular rational evidence before reaching a terminal real-name seal. It sits between the metric completion carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the functorial uniform-completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformcompletionfunctor-namecert}, the Cauchy-completion handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionfunctor-namecert}, the finite StreamName route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the RegSeqRat readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the RealUp seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a completion-facing cofinal-window certificate; it is not quotient completion equality, a selected representative, a choice function, or an ambient completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Cofinal metric-completion carrier]
\label{def:cofinal-metric-completion-carrier}
A $\CofinalMetricCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(M,U,F,W,R,D,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ source row exposing the metric-completion request surface.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformCompletionFunctorUp$ route row through which completion-facing functorial reads are admitted.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$ handoff row downstream of $M$ and $U$.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ cofinal-window ledger naming the finite observation windows served to the completion request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger read from $W$ and $R$.
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $M,U,F,W,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, functorial route, Cauchy handoff, cofinal windows, readback, tolerance, seal, and structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CofinalMetricCompletionUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\UniformCompletionFunctorUp$, $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CofinalMetricCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier for accepted carriers compares only $M,U,F,W,R,D,S,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, host completed-real equality, selected limits, countable choice, or an ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal metric-completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cofinal-metric-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CofinalMetricCompletionUp$ carrier $K=(M,U,F,W,R,D,S,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CofinalMetricCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric-completion source exposure through $M$, uniform functorial route exposure through $U$, Cauchy-completion handoff through $F$, cofinal StreamName windows through $W$, RegSeqRat readback through $R$, DyadicRatCore tolerance through $D$, terminal Real seal access through $S$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier of \autoref{def:cofinal-metric-completion-carrier}. The metric source, uniform functorial route, Cauchy handoff, cofinal-window ledger, readback, tolerance, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows are exactly the coordinates of one finite $\BHist$ packet.

The metric-completion request first reads $M$ and then the functorial route $U,F$. This places the request on the same completion-facing path used by the sibling UniformCompletionFunctor and CauchyCompletionFunctor certificates before any Real-facing row is available.

The cofinal-window surface is then fixed by $W,R,D$. Each precision request is served by a displayed StreamName window, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance ledger, so the consumer cannot replace the route by quotient completion equality, a hidden representative, or host-level complete metric data.

Finally $S,H,C,P,N$ seal and name the same route. Since no excluded object appears among the carrier coordinates, the local NameCert obligations are exhausted by the displayed finite packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal metric-completion L10 handoff]
\label{thm:cofinal-metric-completion-l10-handoff}
Every L10 completion-facing read exported by an accepted $\CofinalMetricCompletionUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
M,U,F,W,R,D,S,H,C,P,N.
$$
In particular, a consumer reaches the $\RealUp$ seal only after reading the $\MetricCompletionUp$ source, the $\UniformCompletionFunctorUp$ route, the $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$ handoff, the $\StreamNameUp$ cofinal-window ledger, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, and the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row. The rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route and do not add quotient completions, selected limits, choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:cofinal-metric-completion-namecert-obligations}. They expose the route inventory and require every accepted consumer to read the displayed metric, uniform-functorial, Cauchy-handoff, cofinal-window, readback, tolerance, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The sibling metric-completion and uniform-completion-functor chapters supply the source shape for the first three rows: $M$ is the metric-completion entry point, while $U,F$ keep the request on the functorial completion handoff rather than on a host completion object.

The finite L10 window is the middle segment $W,R,D$. It records the StreamName window, the RegSeqRat readback, and the DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger used by the request before the terminal Real seal is read.

The remaining rows $S,H,C,P,N$ only seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Therefore the handoff exported to downstream completion consumers is exactly the displayed cofinal-window packet.
\end{proof}
