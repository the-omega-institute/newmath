Following Whitney and Hirsch on tubular neighbourhoods, the $\TubularNeighborhoodUp$ packet records the finite naming surface for a submanifold, its normal bundle, a metric-controlled exponential chart, and the resulting neighbourhood image. It reads the source manifold through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-manifold-namecert} and the smooth atlas boundary through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-smoothmanifold-namecert}; it reads the bundle row through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bundle-namecert} and the vector-bundle carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vectorbundle-namecert}; it reads the metric and exponential-chart rows through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannianmetric-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-expmap-namecert}; and it reads radius and image budgets through the Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The certificate names the displayed finite tubular route without asserting a global collar, selected closest-point projection, quotient equality of normal vectors, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Tubular neighborhood carrier]
\label{def:tubular-neighborhood-carrier}
A $\TubularNeighborhoodUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(S,N,G,E,R,U,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the submanifold and source-manifold row, read through $\ManifoldUp$ and $\SmoothManifoldUp$, including the displayed inclusion data and the finite atlas windows on which the construction is inspected.
\item $N$ is the normal-bundle row, read through $\BundleUp$ and $\VectorBundleUp$, carrying the displayed fibre complement and transition compatibility data for the source row.
\item $G$ is the $\RiemannianMetricUp$ row, exposing the metric, orthogonality, and fibre-length comparisons used to choose the visible normal window.
\item $E$ is the $\ExpMapUp$ row, recording the finite exponential-chart request from the normal-bundle window into the ambient manifold chart.
\item $R$ is the radius ledger, a $\RealUp$ tolerance row that bounds the visible normal vectors and records the local injectivity budget used by the displayed chart.
\item $U$ is the neighbourhood image row, naming the visible image of the radius-bounded normal vectors under the exponential-chart row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,N,G,E,R,U,C,P,L$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from submanifold reads through the normal-bundle, metric, exponential-chart, radius, and image rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TubularNeighborhoodUp$, $\ManifoldUp$, $\BundleUp$, $\RiemannianMetricUp$, $\ExpMapUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TubularNeighborhoodUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these ten rows. It has no coordinate for a global retraction, selected nearest point, quotient normal-vector equality, trichotomy of distances, or ambient manifold completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tubular neighborhood NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:tubular-neighborhood-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\TubularNeighborhoodUp$ carrier $T=(S,N,G,E,R,U,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\TubularNeighborhoodUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, submanifold and source-manifold visibility, normal-bundle attachment, Riemannian metric readback, exponential-chart replay, radius-ledger boundedness, neighbourhood-image exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:tubular-neighborhood-carrier}. The source row $S$ supplies the displayed manifold and submanifold windows, while $N$ attaches the normal-bundle data to those same windows.

Read the analytic rows next. The metric row $G$ supplies the orthogonality and fibre-length comparisons, the exponential-chart row $E$ records the visible chart request, and the radius row $R$ bounds the normal vectors before the image row $U$ is exposed.

The structural rows $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no row for a global retraction, selected nearest point, quotient normal-vector equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Tubular neighborhood normal-bundle handoff]
\label{thm:tubular-neighborhood-normal-bundle-handoff}
Every $\TubularNeighborhoodUp$ consumer of the neighbourhood image row must first read the finite handoff
$$
\ManifoldUp,\BundleUp,\RiemannianMetricUp,\ExpMapUp,\RealUp
\longmapsto

$$
In particular, the normal-bundle row and metric row are visible before the exponential-chart image is consumed, and the radius ledger is visible before the image is treated as a neighbourhood.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the carrier rows $S,N,G,E,R,U,H,C,P,L$. The source manifold row and normal-bundle row determine which fibre windows are eligible for the tubular request.

The metric row then fixes the finite orthogonality and length comparisons used by the request. Only after those comparisons are displayed can the exponential-chart row $E$ replay the normal vector into the ambient manifold chart.

\end{proof}
