\begin{theorem}[Constructive ultrametric completion minimality handoff]
\label{thm:constructive-ultrametric-completion-minimality-handoff}
Every $\ConstructiveUltrametricCompletionUp$ completion-facing read that consumes the strong-triangle dyadic radius ledger, the Cauchy route, and the separated-completion handoff also routes through the $\CauchyCompletionMinimalityUp$ service boundary before it exports a completion interface. The exported route therefore displays the ultrametric source, dyadic-radius ledger, separated uniqueness handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows before any $\CompletionUp$ consumer can inspect the interface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:constructive-ultrametric-completion-namecert-obligations}. It forces every accepted read to expose the ultrametric source, dyadic ledger, strong-triangle row, Cauchy route, separated-completion handoff, completion interface, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Now read the minimality boundary of \autoref{thm:cauchy-completion-minimality-namecert-obligations}. Its service surface contains the same source metric row, dense or completion-facing handoff, universal route, separated uniqueness boundary, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows needed before a completed read can be exported.

Thus an ultrametric completion consumer cannot jump from the strong-triangle dyadic ledger directly to a public completion interface. It must pass through the displayed $\CauchyCompletionMinimalityUp$ boundary, and the route exports no quotient completion, selected representative, countable-choice selector, host equality of limits, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness claim.
\end{proof}
