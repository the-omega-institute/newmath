\begin{definition}[DedekindCompletionBoundary carrier]
\label{def:dedekind-completion-boundary-carrier}
A $\DedekindCompletionBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(L,R,D,S,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedCutRealUp$ located-cut exposure row, recording lower--upper cut evidence only through displayed finite observations.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row used when a cut-side observation is compared with a regular-Cauchy presentation.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger that bounds every comparison window used by $L$ and $R$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row carrying the bounded stream observations through which the comparison is replayed.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after the located-cut, regular-readback, dyadic, and stream rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the located-cut, regular-readback, dyadic, stream, Real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion-facing request through $L,R,D,S,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DedekindCompletionBoundaryUp$, $\LocatedCutRealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DedekindCompletionBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion, quotient-real equality, a selected representative, host real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or an ambient construction of $\RealUp$ from Dedekind cuts.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dedekind boundary located-cut route]
\label{thm:dedekind-completion-boundary-located-cut-route}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\DedekindCompletionBoundaryUp$ carrier $B=(L,R,D,S,E,H,C,P,N)$ reads the cut-side data only through the displayed route
$$
\LocatedCutRealUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The route may expose located cut evidence, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerances, finite stream windows, and the terminal Real seal, but it does not export quotient completion or construct $\RealUp$ from Dedekind cuts.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:dedekind-completion-boundary-carrier}. The only cut-side row is $L$, and it is paired in the same packet with the regular rational readback $R$, the dyadic ledger $D$, and the finite stream-window row $S$.

The continuation row $C$ fixes the order in which a completion-facing request can inspect those rows. A request first sees located-cut evidence, then compares it against regular rational readback, then checks the dyadic tolerance ledger and the finite stream observations before the Real seal $E$ is visible.

The structural rows $H,P,N$ transport, source, and name only that displayed route. Since the carrier has no quotient-completion or Dedekind-construction coordinate, the consumer receives a boundary certificate rather than a construction of $\RealUp$ from cuts.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dedekind boundary no quotient scope]
\label{thm:dedekind-completion-boundary-no-quotient-scope}
For every accepted $\DedekindCompletionBoundaryUp$ carrier, the public scope is confined to the rows $L,R,D,S,E,H,C,P,N$ of \autoref{def:dedekind-completion-boundary-carrier}. In particular, the packet refuses quotient completion, quotient-real equality, selected representatives, host real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, and any claim that $\RealUp$ is constructed from Dedekind cuts.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The carrier definition lists every visible row. None of those rows stores a quotient relation, an equivalence class, a selected representative, a host equality proof, a choice schedule, or a trichotomy decision.

Apply the located-cut route of \autoref{thm:dedekind-completion-boundary-located-cut-route}. It shows that the only Real-facing handoff passes through $\LocatedCutRealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal.

It remains only to inspect the structural rows. The $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ entries preserve, replay, source, and name the displayed finite route; they do not add a quotient completion object or a construction principle for $\RealUp$.
\end{proof}
