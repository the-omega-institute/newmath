Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive metric completion, the $\MetricCompletionSequenceUp$ packet records the finite sequence route by which complete-metric Cauchy data is read as a named completion row. It sits beside the stream sequence surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the complete-metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, and the separated uniqueness route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-namecert}. The packet names the completion-sequence handoff as finite BEDC data; it does not assert ambient completeness of $\RealUp$, quotient Cauchy-sequence equality, selected representatives, or countable choice.

\begin{definition}[Metric-completion sequence carrier]
\label{def:metric-completion-sequence-carrier}
A $\MetricCompletionSequenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(X,S,M,W,L,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row equipped with the complete-metric interface consumed from $\CompleteMetricUp$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ sequence row exposing the finite observation schedule of the Cauchy data.
\item $M$ is the Cauchy modulus row, recording the displayed tail budget used by the sequence.
\item $W$ is the completion witness row from the complete-metric carrier.
\item $L$ is the limit row reached only after $X,S,M,W$ have been displayed.
\item $U$ is the separated uniqueness seal routed through $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, sequence, modulus, completion witness, limit, uniqueness, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from sequence inspection to completion witness, limit read, and uniqueness read.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetricCompletionSequenceUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetricCompletionSequenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,S,M,W,L,U,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotienting Cauchy sequences, selecting a host completion representative, invoking countable choice, or declaring $\RealUp$ complete.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Metric-completion sequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metric-completion-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetricCompletionSequenceUp$ carrier $Q=(X,S,M,W,L,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MetricCompletionSequenceUp}$ surface consists of five obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier exposure for the displayed metric source, sequence, modulus, completion witness, limit, uniqueness, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows;
\item classifier stability under componentwise $\hsame$ transport of all displayed rows;
\item Cauchy-window exactness, stating that sequence inspection is read through $S$ and the modulus row $M$ before any completion witness is public;
\item limit-seal routing, stating that $L$ is reached through the complete-metric witness $W$ and its $\RealUp$ readback rows;
\item separated uniqueness routing, stating that any second limit comparison is exposed only through $U$ and the $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ handoff.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metric-completion-sequence-carrier}. It displays exactly the source, sequence, modulus, completion witness, limit, uniqueness, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows before admission.

The Cauchy-window and limit-seal obligations are ordered by the packet coordinates: $S$ and $M$ are inspected before $W$, and $W$ is inspected before $L$. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve this order for every accepted read.

The uniqueness row $U$ is the only route comparing a second displayed limit. Since the carrier has no quotient, selected representative, choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Metric-completion sequence regular Cauchy seal]
\label{thm:metric-completion-sequence-regular-cauchy-seal}
Let a $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular Cauchy sequence be read through the $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$ of an accepted $\MetricCompletionSequenceUp$ packet and sealed as a $\RealUp$ distance row through the completion witness $W$. Then the resulting limit row $L$ is a sealed complete-metric limit row. If another displayed limit row is compared with it, the comparison passes only through the separated uniqueness seal $U$ and $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$; no part of the route asserts completeness of $\RealUp$ or identifies quotient Cauchy names.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the regular Cauchy name through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} and the finite observation schedule through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. The modulus row $M$ records the displayed tail budget before the complete-metric witness is available.

Next consume the complete-metric witness $W$ from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}. The $\RealUp$ readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} seals the distance observations attached to the displayed completion route, producing the limit row $L$ inside the packet.

Finally route any competing limit comparison through $U$ and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-namecert}. This gives separated uniqueness for the displayed complete-metric limit and does not turn the construction into ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, quotient sequence equality, or a selected representative.
\end{proof}
