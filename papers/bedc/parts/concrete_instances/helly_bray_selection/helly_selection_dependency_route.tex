\begin{theorem}[Helly--Bray Helly-selection dependency route]
\label{thm:helly-bray-selection-helly-selection-dependency-route}
Every $\DistributionUp$ or compactness-facing consumer of an accepted $\HellyBraySelectionUp$ packet must first expose the finite $\HellySelectionUp$ selection surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-helly-selection-namecert} together with the bounded real sequence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedrealsequence-namecert}. In carrier notation, the read passes through the bounded-variation source row, Jordan signed-variation row, finite window rows, regular rational readback, terminal Real seals, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows before the distribution-facing route of \autoref{thm:helly-bray-selection-distribution-forward-link} is public. It is therefore not a free weak-convergence theorem, measure-completion theorem, quotient-function equality, choice-selected subsequence, or ambient compactness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:helly-bray-selection-carrier}. Its only sequence-facing data are the bounded-variation row, Jordan signed-variation row, finite windows, regular rational readback, and terminal Real seals.

Read those rows through the finite-window route of \autoref{thm:helly-bray-selection-finite-window-route}. The same bounded source discipline is the one exported by the $\HellySelectionUp$ packet, and the bounded real sequence route supplies the finite window and bound rows before any compactness-facing consumer can inspect them.

Finally attach the distribution forward link. Since \autoref{thm:helly-bray-selection-distribution-forward-link} exposes distribution consumers only after the Helly--Bray carrier rows have been displayed, the downstream route receives the Helly-selection and bounded-sequence surfaces as dependencies rather than as hidden host compactness or choice principles.
\end{proof}
