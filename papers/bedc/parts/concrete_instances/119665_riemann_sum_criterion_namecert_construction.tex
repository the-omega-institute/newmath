\begin{definition}[Riemann sum criterion carrier]
\label{def:riemann-sum-criterion-carrier}
A $\RiemannSumCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(P,M,A,S,R,H,K,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the partition row: a finite tagged-partition ledger whose adjacent interval endpoints are read through the Riemann-sum source.
\item $M$ is the mesh row: a finite mesh-bound and refinement-budget record consumed from $\RiemannSumMeshUp$.
\item $A$ is the sample row: the finite sample-point and evaluation-window data attached to the tagged partition.
\item $S$ is the sum row: the finite Riemann-sum value ledger, with all scalar readbacks routed through $\RegSeqRatUp$ before any terminal $\RealUp$ seal is inspected.
\item $R$ is the refinement row: the directed refinement comparison that records how a later partition may refine the displayed partition and preserve the inspected sum budget.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the partition, mesh, sample, sum, refinement, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an integration-facing request through $P,M,A,S,R,H$.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RiemannSumCriterionUp$, $\RiemannSumUp$, $\RiemannSumMeshUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RiemannSumCriterionUp}$ row naming exactly this finite criterion packet.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a selected limiting partition, quotient function equality, an open-cover compactness theorem, a host supremum, countable choice, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{proof}

Finally $H,K,G,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier contains no row for open-cover compactness, selected gauges, quotient equality of functions, host suprema, countable choice, or ambient completeness, these displayed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
