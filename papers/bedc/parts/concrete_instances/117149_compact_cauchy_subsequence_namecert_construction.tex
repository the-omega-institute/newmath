\begin{theorem}[Compact Cauchy subsequence obligation surface]
\label{thm:compact-cauchy-subsequence-obligation-surface}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The displayed rows expose the compact metric source, the sequential compactness window, the original stream observations, the selected finite subsequence window, the dyadic tolerance ledger, the regular readback, and the terminal real seal.

The compact and sequential rows are finite. The $\CompactMetricUp$ source supplies only compact-metric carrier and finite cover data, while the $\SequentialCompactUp$ row supplies only the subsequence-window surface already named by the sibling chapter.

The Cauchy readback rows are downstream. The stream row $S$ opens the inspected observations, the window row $W$ records the selected finite indices, $D$ records the dyadic comparison budget, and $R$ is the regular rational readback available to later consumers.

Finally the Real seal $E$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route. Since the carrier contains no field for host subsequence selection, quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local obligation surface.
\end{proof}
