\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CofinalReindexingUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cofinal-reindexing-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on cofinal subsequences of regular Cauchy names, the $\CofinalReindexingUp$ packet records the finite reindexing route by which a displayed tail request is moved along a monotone cofinal index row before any regular rational readback or Real seal is consumed. It sits between the general reindexing surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-reindexing-namecert}, the regular Cauchy specialization of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-reindexing-namecert}, the finite tail-map packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cofinal-tail-map-namecert}, the stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite cofinal-tail reindex certificate; it is not quotient stream equality, not a selected subsequence limit, not countable choice, not host real equality, and not ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Cofinal reindexing carrier]
\label{def:cofinal-reindexing-carrier}
A $\CofinalReindexingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(M,T,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the cofinal monotone index row, recording the finite target-to-source request map, its displayed order comparisons, and the finite reachability witnesses for the target requests under inspection.
\item $T$ is the tail-window row, recording the target tail cuts and the source cuts selected by $M$ for the current finite request window.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row read after $T$ has fixed the target and source windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row reached only after the stream windows have been routed through $M$ and $T$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row exposed only after the regular rational readback has been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,T,S,R,E,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a target precision request through the cofinal index row, tail-window row, StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, and Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CofinalReindexingUp$, $\CauchyReindexingUp$, $\RegularCauchyReindexingUp$, $\CofinalTailMapUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CofinalReindexingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected subsequence limits, countable choice, host real equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal reindexing NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cofinal-reindexing-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CofinalReindexingUp$ carrier $G=(M,T,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CofinalReindexingUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the cofinal monotone index row, tail-window row, $\StreamNameUp$ window, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming row. The obligations require each target request to reach a displayed source cut through $M$ and $T$ before any stream window, regular readback, or Real seal is consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cofinal-reindexing-carrier}. The rows $M,T,S,R,E$ are the complete analytic route, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that route.

The index row $M$ supplies only finite monotone comparisons and cofinal reachability witnesses for the requests under inspection. The tail-window row $T$ records the target cuts and the corresponding source cuts selected by that row.

Once $M$ and $T$ are fixed, the $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$ may be read, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ is downstream of the same finite windows. The terminal seal $E$ is therefore a Real-facing read of the displayed reindexing route, not a host-selected subsequence, quotient stream, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal reindexing tail-window stability]
\label{thm:cofinal-reindexing-tail-window-stability}
Let $G=(M,T,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ and $G'=(M',T',S',R',E',H',C',P',N')$ be accepted $\CofinalReindexingUp$ carriers whose cofinal index rows and tail-window rows agree through the displayed classifier. Then every $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ read transported from $G$ to $G'$ uses the same finite target cuts, source cuts, monotone comparisons, and cofinal reachability witnesses before reaching the Real seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the classifier of \autoref{def:cofinal-reindexing-carrier}. Agreement of $M,T$ fixes the target requests, source cuts, order comparisons, and cofinal witnesses visible to both packets.

Apply \autoref{thm:cofinal-reindexing-namecert-obligations}. The stream window $S$ and regular readback $R$ cannot be read until the index and tail-window rows have been displayed, so their transported versions remain attached to the same finite route.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve only componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and naming. They do not add a selected subsequence, quotient equality, countable-choice schedule, host equality, or ambient completeness row, so the transported Real seal remains downstream of the same tail-window data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal reindexing Real-seal nonescape]
\label{thm:cofinal-reindexing-real-seal-nonescape}
Every $\RealUp$-facing read of an accepted $\CofinalReindexingUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
M \longmapsto T \longmapsto S \longmapsto R \longmapsto E .
$$
Thus the terminal seal is reached only after the cofinal monotone index row, tail-window row, $\StreamNameUp$ window, and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback have been displayed. The read exports no quotient stream equality, selected subsequence limit, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a consumer of the seal row $E$. By \autoref{thm:cofinal-reindexing-namecert-obligations}, the seal is downstream of $R$, and $R$ is downstream of the stream window $S$.

The stream window is not free-standing. It is read only after $M$ has selected displayed source cuts for the target requests and $T$ has recorded the corresponding tail windows.

Componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming keep that route visible. Since none of the excluded host or quotient objects is a carrier coordinate, the Real-facing consumer cannot bypass the finite cofinal reindexing packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal reindexing seed scope]
\label{thm:cofinal-reindexing-seed-scope}
The $\CofinalReindexingUp$ seed closes exactly the finite cofinal reindexing route from displayed target requests to source cuts, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, and the terminal Real seal. It supplies a handoff surface for Real and Completion consumers using \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-reindexing-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-reindexing-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cofinal-tail-map-namecert}; it does not close quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, ambient completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The seed route is the carrier route of \autoref{def:cofinal-reindexing-carrier}. It displays the cofinal index row, tail windows, stream window, regular readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Use \autoref{thm:cofinal-reindexing-tail-window-stability} for stability of the finite source and target windows, and use \autoref{thm:cofinal-reindexing-real-seal-nonescape} for the terminal Real-facing boundary.

These results are exactly the finite handoff needed by the surrounding reindexing, tail-map, StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real chapters. They do not add any row for the excluded quotient, selection, choice, completeness, verification, or bridge claims.
\end{proof}
