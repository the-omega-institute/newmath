\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RationalEmbeddingUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-rationalembedding-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive rational constants as real names, the $\RationalEmbeddingUp$ packet records the standard constant rational route from a $\RatUp$ row into a $\RealUp$ seal without turning host rational equality, quotient streams, selected limits, trichotomy, or ambient completeness into primitive coordinates. It sits between the rational source surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the constant finite-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the real seal boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the dyadic constant-name route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicembedding-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[RationalEmbedding carrier surface]
\label{def:rational-embedding-carrier-surface}
A $\RationalEmbeddingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(q,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed coordinates:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $q$ is the $\RatUp$ source row naming the accepted rational point;
\item $S$ is the constant $\StreamNameUp$ schedule whose admitted windows all read the same rational source row $q$;
\item $R$ is the stationary $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback obtained from the constant schedule $S$;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and observation row used to compare the stationary rational reads at the displayed finite precisions;
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $q,S,R,D$ have been exposed;
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transports for the rational source, constant schedule, stationary readback, dyadic tolerance row, real seal, package provenance, and local naming row;
\item $C$ stores the displayed $\Cont$ routes from $q$ to $S$, from $S$ to $R$, from $R$ to $D$, and from $D$ to $E$;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RationalEmbeddingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier is a finite certificate for rational constants as real names. It contains no quotient stream, no host real equality row, no selected limit, no trichotomy principle, no host rational equality oracle, and no ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RationalEmbedding constant sequence route]
\label{thm:rational-embedding-constant-sequence-route}
Every accepted $\RationalEmbeddingUp$ packet routes its $\RatUp$ source row $q$ through the constant $\StreamNameUp$ schedule $S$ and the stationary $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$ before any $\RealUp$ read is available. Thus a consumer of the real seal first sees the rational source, then the constant finite-window schedule, then the stationary regular-rational row, and only then the dyadic observation row and seal.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.RationalEmbeddingUp.RationalEmbeddingCarrier\_constant\_sequence\_route}
\begin{proof}
Project the coordinates of \autoref{def:rational-embedding-carrier-surface}. The continuation row $C$ displays the route from $q$ to $S$ and the route from $S$ to $R$, while $H$ records that these coordinates are transported componentwise with the same package provenance.

The constant schedule $S$ has no independent stream seed: each admitted window is read from $q$. The stationary row $R$ is therefore the regular-rational readback of that constant schedule, not a separate rational sequence.

Only after $R$ is displayed can the packet expose the dyadic observation row $D$ and then the real seal $E$. Hence every real-facing consumer reads $q,S,R$ in that order before reaching any $\RealUp$ coordinate.
\end{proof}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.RationalEmbeddingUp.RationalEmbeddingConstantSequenceRoute}

\begin{theorem}[RationalEmbedding RealUp seal boundary]
\label{thm:rational-embedding-real-seal-boundary}
For every accepted $\RationalEmbeddingUp$ carrier $K=(q,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the $\RealUp$ seal row $E$ factors through the displayed dependency chain
$$
\RatUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \NameCert_{\RationalEmbeddingUp}.
$$
The seal exports only the local rows $q,S,R,D,H,C,P,N$ as its boundary data. It does not export a quotient stream, host real equality, a selected limit, trichotomy, host rational equality as an oracle, or ambient completeness for arbitrary Cauchy names.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a read of the real seal $E$. By the carrier definition, $E$ is reachable only through the route entries in $C$ whose predecessors are $D$, $R$, $S$, and $q$, and the same predecessors are named in the package row $P$ and local certificate row $N$.

Apply \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-constant-sequence-route}. It identifies the route to $R$ as the constant rational schedule read from $q$ through $S$. The dyadic row $D$ then supplies only the finite tolerance and observation data attached to that stationary readback.

The remaining rows $H,C,P,N$ are transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows for the same finite route. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for a quotient stream, host real equality, selected limit, trichotomy, host rational equality oracle, or ambient completeness, the $\RealUp$ seal boundary is exactly the displayed finite dependency chain.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RationalEmbedding stationary readback exactness]
\label{thm:rational-embedding-stationary-readback-exactness}
For every accepted $\RationalEmbeddingUp$ carrier $K=(q,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the stationary $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ is exactly the finite readback of the constant $\StreamNameUp$ schedule $S$ sourced by the $\RatUp$ row $q$. The later $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$ may inspect only this stationary readback before the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is exposed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier surface of \autoref{def:rational-embedding-carrier-surface}. The rows $q,S,R,D,E$ appear in dependency order, and the continuation row $C$ displays the route from the rational source to the constant schedule, then from the schedule to the stationary readback.

Apply \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-constant-sequence-route}. It identifies $S$ as the constant finite-window schedule whose admitted windows all read $q$, and identifies $R$ as the stationary regular-rational readback of that schedule.

Apply the seal-boundary theorem \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-real-seal-boundary}. It confines the real-facing route to $q,S,R,D,H,C,P,N$ and gives $D$ no coordinate other than the dyadic tolerance and observation row attached to the stationary readback.

Therefore the finite readback seen by $D$ is exactly the stationary row $R$ generated from $S$ and $q$. No quotient stream, selected limit, host real equality, or ambient completeness coordinate can alter that readback before the seal is reached.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RationalEmbedding NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:rational-embedding-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RationalEmbeddingUp$ carrier
$$
K=(q,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N),

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:rational-embedding-carrier-surface}. Its displayed rows are precisely $q,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$, so every local obligation must be read from that finite packet.

Use \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-constant-sequence-route} to identify the constant schedule and stationary readback obligations, then use \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-real-seal-boundary} to place the dyadic observation row and real seal behind the same route.

The rows $H,C,P,N$ supply transport, replay, provenance, and naming for no additional mathematical object. Hence the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RationalEmbedding StreamName scope]
\label{thm:rational-embedding-streamname-scope}
Every $\StreamNameUp$ read inside an accepted $\RationalEmbeddingUp$ carrier is the constant finite-window schedule sourced by the displayed $\RatUp$ row $q$. It cannot be consumed as a free stream, quotient stream, selected Cauchy representative, or independently varying regular-rational sequence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the schedule coordinate $S$ in \autoref{def:rational-embedding-carrier-surface}. The carrier describes $S$ as the constant $\StreamNameUp$ schedule whose admitted windows all read $q$.

Apply \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-stationary-readback-exactness}. It forces the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ to be the stationary readback of $S$, with the dyadic row $D$ allowed to inspect only that stationary row before the real seal is exposed.

Since no carrier coordinate stores a free stream, quotient stream, selected representative, or independent regular sequence, every StreamName read remains inside the displayed constant schedule scope.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RationalEmbedding nonescape]
\label{thm:rational-embedding-nonescape}
Every accepted $\RationalEmbeddingUp$ consumer that reaches the $\RealUp$ seal remains on the finite route
$$
q,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N.
$$
It exports no quotient stream equality, selected host limit, trichotomy, host rational equality oracle, arbitrary Cauchy-name completeness, or ambient $\RealUp$ equality principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-real-seal-boundary}. It factors the seal through the rational source, constant schedule, stationary readback, dyadic observation row, transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.

Next use \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-streamname-scope}. This prevents the stream coordinate from becoming a free Cauchy name or detached representative before the seal is read.

The carrier definition contains no row for quotient equality, selected limits, trichotomy, host rational equality as an oracle, completeness for arbitrary names, or ambient real equality. Therefore the real-facing consumer cannot escape the finite rational-constant route.

\begin{theorem}[RationalEmbedding scoped dependency spine]
\label{thm:rational-embedding-scoped-dependency-spine}
Every accepted $\RationalEmbeddingUp$ read that reaches the rational-constant real seal factors through exactly the scoped dependency spine
$$
\RatUp \longrightarrow \StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \DyadicRatCoreUp \longrightarrow \RealUp
$$
together with the structural rows $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and the local $\NameCert_{\RationalEmbeddingUp}$ rows. The read may inspect the carrier coordinates $q,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$ only along this order. It has no alternate path through quotient streams, selected host limits, trichotomy, arbitrary real completeness, host rational equality as an oracle, or ambient $\RealUp$ equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier surface of \autoref{def:rational-embedding-carrier-surface}. The displayed rows already order the rational source, constant schedule, stationary readback, dyadic observation, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming coordinates.

Apply the constant-sequence route of \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-constant-sequence-route}. It forces the $\RatUp$ row $q$ to be read before the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule $S$, and it forces $S$ to be read before the stationary $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$.

Next use stationary readback exactness and the RealUp seal boundary, \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-stationary-readback-exactness} and \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-real-seal-boundary}. These place the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$ after the stationary readback and place the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ after the dyadic observation row.

Finally use the StreamName scope and nonescape theorems, \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-streamname-scope} and \autoref{thm:rational-embedding-nonescape}. They keep the stream coordinate constant and keep every real-facing consumer on $q,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$, so the only remaining rows are the structural $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ coordinates of the same finite packet.
\end{proof}
