\begin{definition}[Arithmetized provability carrier]
\label{def:arithmetized-provability-carrier}
An $\ArithmetizedProvabilityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(N,F,D,S,G,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $N$ is the $\NatUp$ numeral-code row, carrying unary natural-number names for symbols, formula indices, proof-line indices, and finite proof-code lengths.
\item $F$ is the $\FirstOrderUp$ formula-code row, displaying symbol codes, variable rows, term rows, formula constructors, and the well-formed formula classifier used by the proof predicate.
\item $D$ is the $\ComputableUp$ derivation-check row, recording the bounded primitive-recursive inspection of a finite proof-code list against the displayed rule and axiom-code table.
\item $S$ is the substitution row, recording capture-aware replacement of numeral codes into formula-code slots and the finite compatibility ledger for free-variable bookkeeping.
\item $G$ is the diagonal-code row, displaying the self-application code produced from $F$ and $S$ while keeping the resulting formula code inside object syntax.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for numeral codes, formula codes, derivation checks, substitution rows, diagonal rows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a provability request through $N,F,D,S,G$ and then through the structural rows $H,P,L$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ArithmetizedProvabilityUp$, $\NatUp$, $\FirstOrderUp$, $\ComputableUp$, $\GroundLoopBoundaryUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ArithmetizedProvabilityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. No coordinate stores a host truth predicate, semantic satisfaction relation, completeness theorem, consistency proof for the represented theory, or unbounded meta-level proof search.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ArithmetizedProvability NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:arithmetized-provability-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ArithmetizedProvabilityUp$ carrier $A=(N,F,D,S,G,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ArithmetizedProvabilityUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission; numeral-code visibility; formula-code exactness for the displayed first-order constructors; proof-code induction over the finite derivation rows inspected by $D$; substitution stability for the rows in $S$; diagonal-code stability for $G$; derivation-check replay through $C$; componentwise transport through $H$; provenance through $P$; local naming through $L$; and non-escape for host truth, semantic completeness, ambient consistency, and unbounded meta-level proof search.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:arithmetized-provability-carrier}. The finite $\BHist$ packet exposes exactly $N,F,D,S,G,H,C,P,L$, so carrier admission and local naming have no hidden coordinate beyond the displayed rows.

Read the syntactic substrate through $N$ and $F$. The numeral-code row gives the finite natural-number codes used by the packet, and the formula-code row gives the first-order constructors and well-formedness classifier that those numbers index.

Read the proof predicate through $D$. Its accepted rows are finite derivation-check rows, so the proof-code obligation is induction over the displayed proof-line list rather than a semantic appeal to truth or completeness.

Read substitution and diagonalization through $S$ and $G$. The substitution row records the capture-aware replacement ledger, and the diagonal-code row records only the object-level self-application code produced from that ledger.

Finally $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name this same finite route. Since no coordinate stores a host truth predicate, semantic satisfaction relation, completeness theorem, ambient consistency proof, or unbounded proof search, those objects cannot be projected from the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ArithmetizedProvability finite proof-code induction]
\label{thm:arithmetized-provability-finite-proof-code-induction}
Every accepted $\ArithmetizedProvabilityUp$ proof-code read is generated by induction over the displayed finite derivation rows in $D$ and is then transported only through
$$
N,F,D,S,G,H,C,P,L .
$$
The read may certify that a displayed code is accepted by the finite derivation checker for a displayed formula code. It cannot import host truth, semantic completeness, an ambient consistency theorem, or a meta-level assertion that the represented formula is true.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the derivation-check row $D$. A proof-code read is accepted only when the finite list of proof lines passes the displayed rule and axiom-code checks, so the generating argument proceeds by induction over that finite list.

At the base row, the checker reads an initial displayed axiom-code or hypothesis-code entry through $N$ and $F$. The entry remains a syntactic formula-code row.

At each successor row, the checker reads only the earlier displayed lines named by the finite indices in $N$ and the rule-shape data in $F$. The row added at that step is therefore produced by the same finite derivation-check surface, not by an external semantic oracle.

Substitution and diagonal rows are handled after the syntactic check. The row $S$ records the capture-aware substitution budget, while $G$ records the diagonal code that remains inside the formula-code surface.

The structural rows $H,C,P,L$ then transport, replay, source, and name the result. Because no later row adds truth, completeness, consistency, or unbounded proof search, the accepted proof-code read is exhausted by the finite induction route stated above.
\end{proof}
