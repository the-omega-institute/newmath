\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompletionUnitDenseImageUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-completion-unit-dense-image-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, Kelley, and Bourbaki on constructive completion and dense image maps, the $\CompletionUnitDenseImageUp$ packet records the surface by which the completion unit exposes its image as finite dense-source data. It sits after the unit certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionunit-namecert}, the general dense embedding interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dense-embedding-namecert}, the metric completion dense embedding of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-completion-dense-embedding-namecert}, the completion universal-property surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completionuniversalproperty-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the dense image of the unit without quotient representatives, selected completion points, countable choice, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[CompletionUnitDenseImage carrier]
\label{def:completion-unit-dense-image-carrier}
A $\CompletionUnitDenseImageUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(X,C,u,D,W,R,E,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the displayed source metric or regular-Cauchy row entering the completion unit.
\item $C$ is the accepted $\CauchyCompletionUnitUp$ row that admits $X$ into the completion-facing surface.
\item $u$ is the unit-image row, recording the displayed image of $X$ inside the completion boundary.
\item $D$ is the dense-image witness row read through $\DenseEmbeddingUp$ and $\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row used by the dense-image request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same window and tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $C,u,D,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, unit, dense-image, window, readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from source admission through the dense image and completion universal-property route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompletionUnitDenseImageUp$, $\CauchyCompletionUnitUp$, $\DenseEmbeddingUp$, $\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$, $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompletionUnitDenseImageUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, host equality of completed objects, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompletionUnitDenseImage NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:completion-unit-dense-image-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\CompletionUnitDenseImageUp$ carrier $U=(X,C,u,D,W,R,E,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompletionUnitDenseImageUp}$ surface consists exactly of source admission through $X$, unit exposure through $C,u$, dense-image exposure through $D$, finite window exposure through $W$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal real sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $K$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

Every completion-facing consumer must read the unit row $C$, the unit-image row $u$, and the dense-image row $D$ before it can consume $W,R,E,H,K,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:completion-unit-dense-image-carrier}. The tuple lists exactly the source, completion unit, unit image, dense-image witness, window, readback, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The unit exposure is supplied by the $\CauchyCompletionUnitUp$ row. It sends the displayed source into the completion-facing boundary as finite unit data before any density request is inspected.

The dense-image exposure then reads $D$ through $\DenseEmbeddingUp$ and $\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$. The finite window $W$ and readback row $R$ are the only numerical route offered to the real seal $E$ and to universal-property consumers.

Finally $H,K,P,N$ preserve and name the same route. Since the carrier has no quotient, selected representative, choice schedule, host equality, or ambient completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CompletionUnitDenseImage universal route]
\label{thm:completion-unit-dense-image-universal-route}
Every completion universal-property consumer of an accepted $\CompletionUnitDenseImageUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\CauchyCompletionUnitUp \longrightarrow \DenseEmbeddingUp \longrightarrow \MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp \longrightarrow \CompletionUniversalPropertyUp
$$
with the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$ and the $\RealUp$ seal row $E$ already displayed. Thus the universal-property handoff receives a dense image of the unit as finite BEDC data, not as a quotient completion class, selected completion representative, countable-choice selector, host equality proof, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the accepted carrier $U=(X,C,u,D,W,R,E,H,K,P,N)$. The completion unit row $C$ and unit-image row $u$ are the only source-to-completion entry data available to the consumer.

Next apply the dense embedding siblings. The rows $D,W,R$ expose the dense-image witness, finite source window, and regular rational readback before any completion universal-property row can be inspected.

The real seal $E$ is terminal for the numerical route, and $K$ replays the same source-to-dense-image path for the universal-property consumer. Transport and provenance through $H,P,N$ do not add another completion object.

Therefore every universal-property handoff factors through the displayed unit, dense-image, metric-completion dense embedding, readback, and real-seal rows, with none of the excluded quotient, choice, selected-representative, host-equality, or ambient-completeness data exported.
\end{proof}
