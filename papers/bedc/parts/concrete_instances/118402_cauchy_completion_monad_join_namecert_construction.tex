\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion monad join NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-monad-join-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-monad-join-carrier}. The rows $O,U,B,I,S,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$ are the whole public surface, so carrier admission and row exposure are exactly these projections.

The join-specific obligations come from $O,U,B,I$. The outer completion row must be displayed first, the unit row records admission into the completion surface, the bind row records the finite flattening schedule, and the idempotence row forces already completed inner data back to the same displayed boundary.

The readback obligations come from $S,D,R,E$. These rows are downstream of the join route, so a consumer cannot inspect a stream window, dyadic tolerance, regular-rational readback, or Real seal detached from $O,U,B,I$.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are exhausted by $H,C,P,N$. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, hidden stream witnesses, or ambient completeness, the nonescape clause follows by exhaustion.
\end{proof}

\end{proof}
