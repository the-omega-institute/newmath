\begin{definition}[Located limsup carrier]
\label{def:located-limsup-carrier}
A $\LocatedLimsupUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(S,T,W,Q,U,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the bounded real-sequence source row whose upper-tail behaviour is inspected.
\item $T$ is the finite tail-index ledger, recording the displayed tail starts and successor rows available to the packet.
\item $W$ is the finite window row, listing the inspected sequence entries and rational comparison requests inside the displayed tails.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the windowed tail data.
\item $U$ is the located upper-envelope row, answering each displayed rational cut request by an upper-tail bound or a lower displayed occurrence before a limsup read is exported.
\item $R$ is the $\LocatedSupremumUp$ handoff row for the exposed tail-envelope surface.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row naming the upper-limit read after $S,T,W,Q,U,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, tail, window, readback, located-envelope, handoff, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a rational upper-limit request through the same rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedLimsupUp$, $\BoundedRealLimsupUp$, $\LocatedSupremumUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedLimsupUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for classical subsequence compactness, a selected subsequence, quotient stream equality, a host limsup operator, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located limsup NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-limsup-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedLimsupUp$ carrier $L=(S,T,W,Q,U,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedLimsupUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the bounded source, finite tail ledger, finite windows, regular-rational readback, located upper-envelope row, located-supremum handoff, terminal real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming row. Every Real or Completion consumer must read $S,T,W,Q,U,R$ before the seal $E$, and every located upper-limit request remains inside the displayed finite rows named by $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\leanstmt{BEDC.Derived.LocatedLimsupUp}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-limsup-carrier}. The source, tail, window, readback, located-envelope, handoff, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows are the only available coordinates.

The tail discipline starts with $S$ and $T$. A consumer may inspect only the tail starts and successor rows displayed by $T$, and the inspected entries are exactly the entries listed in $W$.

The analytic readback then passes through $Q,U,R,E$. The regular-rational row $Q$ reads the finite windows, the located-envelope row $U$ answers each displayed rational cut request, the $\LocatedSupremumUp$ handoff row $R$ packages the upper-envelope surface, and the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is exposed only after those rows are visible.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no coordinate stores a selected subsequence, quotient stream, host limsup, countable-choice selector, or ambient completeness principle, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
