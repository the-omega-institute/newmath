\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FiniteNormalizationDiamondUp$}

Following Newman and Takahashi-style finite diamond reasoning for terminating rewrite systems, the $\FiniteNormalizationDiamondUp$ packet records the candidate-mediated local-join budget used by the MetaCIC normalization audit route. It sits beside the normalization budget of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicnormalizationbudget-namecert}, the candidate-mediated strong-normalization handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-candidate-mediated-sn-namecert}, and the candidate diamond budget of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-candidate-diamond-budget-namecert}. The packet names only finite candidate evidence, local two-front reductions, join budget, no-infinite-reduction readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it does not assert full confluence, unconditional normalization, or subject-reduction closure.

\begin{definition}[Finite normalization diamond carrier]
\label{def:finite-normalization-diamond-carrier}
A $\FiniteNormalizationDiamondUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(K,E,S,A,P_i,A_p,L,J,Z,T,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the candidate-evidence row, carrying the reducibility-candidate data used by the closed term under inspection.
\item $E$ is the closed-candidate row, recording closedness of the type and term together with the candidate evidence consumed by the normalization route.
\item $S$ is the candidate-set membership row, exposing membership, soundness, and beta-closure evidence when the route is read through a candidate set.
\item $A$ is the Pi-adequacy row for binder and function evidence.
\item $P_i$ is the application-adequacy row for applying a Pi-side candidate to a displayed argument candidate.
\item $A_p$ is the local two-front reduction row, recording the two finite one-step or bounded-step frontiers whose endpoints must be compared.
\item $L$ is the local join-budget row, listing the bounded join witnesses and the residual rows inspected by the local diamond check.
\item $J$ is the accepted join row, present only when the two-front endpoints are joined inside the displayed budget.
\item $Z$ is the no-infinite-reduction readback row exported from the candidate-mediated strong-normalization surface.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the candidate, closed-candidate, candidate-set, adequacy, two-front, join-budget, accepted-join, no-infinite-reduction, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same candidate-mediated diamond route.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteNormalizationDiamondUp$, the MetaCIC candidate and adequacy rows, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteNormalizationDiamondUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for global Church--Rosser confluence, unconditional normalization of every closed typed term, erased candidate evidence, subject-reduction closure, quotient syntax equality, oracle reduction, Lean verification, or a standard bridge.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite normalization diamond NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-normalization-diamond-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteNormalizationDiamondUp$ carrier
$$
F=(K,E,S,A,P_i,A_p,L,J,Z,T,C,G,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteNormalizationDiamondUp}$ surface consists exactly of candidate evidence, closed-candidate rows, candidate-set membership, Pi adequacy, application adequacy, local two-front reductions, join budget, accepted local join, no-infinite-reduction readback, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every normalization-diamond read must keep the candidate rows visible and must expose the join as a bounded local witness rather than as full confluence or unconditional normalization.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows. Candidate evidence $K$, closed-candidate evidence $E$, and candidate-set membership $S$ are the only sources from which strong-normalization and no-infinite-reduction reads may be obtained.

Read the adequacy rows $A$ and $P_i$ before inspecting any application frontier. They ensure that the local route remains candidate-mediated through Pi and application cases instead of becoming componentwise termination of unrelated parts.

The two-front row $A_p$, join budget $L$, and accepted join row $J$ then record the finite diamond check. The no-infinite-reduction row $Z$ is read from the same candidate-mediated surface, and the structural rows $T,C,G,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly that finite packet.

Since the carrier has no row for global confluence, unconditional normalization, erased candidate evidence, subject-reduction closure, quotient syntax equality, oracle reduction, Lean verification, or bridge checking, the listed rows exhaust the local $\NameCert$ obligations.
\end{proof}
