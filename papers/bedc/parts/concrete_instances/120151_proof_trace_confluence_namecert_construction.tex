\begin{definition}[ProofTraceConfluence carrier]
\label{def:proof-trace-confluence-carrier}
A $\ProofTraceConfluenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(T,R,N,D,C,B,F,H,E,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the MetaCIC proof-trace row naming the two reduction traces or local peak being compared.
\item $R$ is the residual-substitution row, retaining the substitution-compatibility input when a beta-residual branch requires it.
\item $N$ is the closed-normal fragment row, recording the closed normal, sort, or impossible closed-variable cases that are already unconditional.
\item $D$ is the conditional-diamond input row, naming either the substitution-compatibility input for a parallel diamond or the closed parallel-diamond input for closed star confluence.
\item $C$ is the confluence-audit consumer row, read against the audit map and confluence audit packet.
\item $B$ is the blocked-premise row, recording which residual-substitution, closed-diamond, subject-reduction, or normal-form consistency input remains visible.
\item $F$ is the frontier route through $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ and $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for trace, residual, closed-normal, diamond, audit, blocked-premise, frontier, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $E$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
T,R,N,D,C,B,F \longmapsto H,P,L .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ProofTraceConfluenceUp$, $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$, $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$, $\MetaCICConfluenceAuditPacketUp$, $\SubjectReductionDischargeUp$, $\NormalFormConsistencySealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ProofTraceConfluenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,R,N,D,C,B,F,H,E,P,L$. It has no coordinate for unconditional arbitrary closed Church--Rosser, unrestricted residual substitution, a hidden subject-reduction theorem, quotient syntax equality, evaluator soundness, or normal-form consistency detached from its seal.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ProofTraceConfluence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:proof-trace-confluence-obligations}
For an accepted $\ProofTraceConfluenceUp$ carrier
$$
Q=(T,R,N,D,C,B,F,H,E,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ProofTraceConfluenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for proof traces, residual-substitution input, closed-normal fragments, conditional diamond input, audit consumer, blocked-premise, frontier, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item residual visibility, requiring every full parallel-diamond read to display the residual-substitution row $R$ or record its absence in $B$;
\item closed-diamond visibility, requiring every closed star-confluence read to display the closed-diamond row $D$ or record its absence in $B$;
\item closed-normal fragment separation, requiring $N$ to remain a shape-limited unconditional fragment rather than a full confluence theorem;
\item frontier and audit handoff, requiring $F$ and $C$ to route every consumer through the critical-path, normalization-frontier, and confluence-audit packets;
\item non-escape, excluding unconditional closed confluence, unrestricted subject reduction, quotient syntax equality, evaluator soundness, and unsealed normal-form consistency.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is stored outside $T,R,N,D,C,B,F,H,E,P,L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:proof-trace-confluence-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes proof traces, residual-substitution data, closed-normal fragments, conditional-diamond inputs, audit rows, blocked premises, frontier rows, and structural rows.

The residual and closed-diamond clauses are premise-retention clauses. A full parallel-diamond route must display $R$, and a closed star-confluence route must display $D$; when either input is absent, the blocked-premise row $B$ remains visible.

The closed-normal row $N$ is shape-limited. It records the available normal, sort, and impossible closed-variable cases without adding a coordinate for arbitrary closed confluence.

Finally $F,C,H,E,P,L$ preserve and name the same route through the critical-path and audit surfaces. Since the excluded global endpoints are not carrier coordinates, the displayed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ProofTraceConfluence conditional handoff]
\label{thm:proof-trace-confluence-conditional-handoff}
Every confluence-facing consumer of an accepted $\ProofTraceConfluenceUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\MetaCICCriticalPathUp \longmapsto \MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp \longmapsto T,R,N,D,C,B \longmapsto H,E,P,L .
$$
If the consumer claims full parallel diamond, it must display the substitution-compatibility input in $R$. If it claims full closed star confluence, it must display the closed-diamond input in $D$. Otherwise the blocked-premise row $B$ remains part of the exported handoff.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the frontier row $F$. It places the proof-trace read after the critical-path and normalization-frontier surfaces, so a confluence-facing consumer receives a displayed route rather than a detached theorem.

Apply \autoref{thm:proof-trace-confluence-obligations}. The residual visibility and closed-diamond visibility obligations force $R$ or $D$ to be present for the corresponding full confluence claim, while $B$ records any retained premise.

The closed-normal fragment row $N$ may discharge only its shape-limited cases. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming then preserve the same finite handoff through $H,E,P,L$, and no absent global endpoint can be cited as if it were a packet field.
\end{proof}
