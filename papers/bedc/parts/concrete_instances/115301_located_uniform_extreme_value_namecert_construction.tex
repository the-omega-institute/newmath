Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\LocatedUniformExtremeValueUp$ packet records a paired lower-and-upper finite-net extreme-value certificate for uniformly continuous maps on compact metric sources. It is the finite-net companion to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-located-maximum-namecert} and sits over the finite-net handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-heine-cantor-finite-net-namecert}, the compact source row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the uniformly continuous map row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the stream schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the attained-extreme comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-extreme-value-theorem-namecert}. The packet exposes located upper and lower value windows without selecting extrema, importing open-cover compactness, identifying quotient real names, invoking countable choice, or asserting ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Located uniform extreme-value carrier]
\label{def:located-uniform-extreme-value-carrier}
A $\LocatedUniformExtremeValueUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(K,F,N,W,R,E,L,U_b,M,H,C,P,Q)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, exposing only the compact metric inspection surface consumed by finite-net reads.
\item $F$ is the uniformly continuous $\ContinuousMapUp$ row, exposing the value request interface over $K$.
\item $N$ is the $\HeineCantorFiniteNetUp$ row, recording the finite center family, radius schedule, and coverage witness through which every extreme-value request enters.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule row for the finite family of value windows attached to the centers of $N$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational lower and upper endpoints exposed by $W$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the displayed value names before any comparison row is consumed.
\item $L$ is the located lower-bound row, comparing each displayed center value with the finite family through rational lower windows.
\item $U_b$ is the located upper-bound row, comparing each displayed center value with the finite family through rational upper windows.
\item $M$ is the paired extreme-value ledger row, recording that the lower and upper rows share the same source, map, finite net, value windows, rational readback, and Real seal.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, map row, finite-net cover, stream schedule, rational readback, Real seal, lower row, upper row, paired ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an extreme-value request through $K,F,N,W,R,E,L,U_b,M,H,P,Q$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedUniformExtremeValueUp$, $\BishopLocatedMaximumUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\HeineCantorFiniteNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $Q$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedUniformExtremeValueUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only by the displayed compact source, uniformly continuous map row, finite-net cover, value-window schedule, regular rational readback, Real seal, lower and upper located comparison rows, shared ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. It has no coordinate for a selected minimum, selected maximum, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniform extreme-value obligations]
\label{thm:located-uniform-extreme-value-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedUniformExtremeValueUp$ carrier $U=(K,F,N,W,R,E,L,U_b,M,H,C,P,Q)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedUniformExtremeValueUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed compact source, uniformly continuous map row, finite-net cover, value windows, rational readback, Real seal, lower row, upper row, paired ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item finite-net exposure, requiring every extreme-value request to pass through the centers and coverage witnesses named by $N$;
\item value readback through $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$, requiring $W,R,E$ to be visible before either bound row is consumed;
\item lower-bound located comparison through $L$ and upper-bound located comparison through $U_b$;
\item shared-ledger exactness through $M$, requiring both bound rows to use the same finite net and value-readback route;
\item refusal of selected extrema, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, countable choice, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-uniform-extreme-value-carrier}. The rows $K,F,N$ expose the compact source, map row, and finite-net coverage surface that every later value read must use.

Read the value rows next. The stream schedule $W$ supplies finite lower and upper windows, $R$ reads those windows as regular rational data, and $E$ seals the displayed readbacks as $\RealUp$ names before a comparison can be used.

The lower row $L$ and upper row $U_b$ can consume only the Real-sealed finite family already displayed by $N,W,R,E$. The shared ledger $M$ then records that both comparisons used the same route, while $H,C,P,Q$ transport, replay, source, and name that finite route. These rows exhaust the listed obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniform extreme-value finite-net exposure]
\label{thm:located-uniform-extreme-value-finite-net-exposure}
Every extreme-value read exported by an accepted $\LocatedUniformExtremeValueUp$ carrier factors through the displayed finite route
$$
K,F,N \longmapsto W,R,E \longmapsto L,U_b,M \longmapsto H,C,P,Q .
$$
Thus a consumer can inspect only the compact source, uniformly continuous map row, finite net, value windows, located lower and upper comparisons, and Real seal before reaching the local naming row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the source and map rows. The compact metric source $K$ and uniformly continuous map row $F$ become visible to an extreme-value read only through the finite-net cover $N$.

The value route is fixed before either bound row appears. The stream schedule $W$ records finite observation windows attached to the net, the regular rational row $R$ reads those windows, and the terminal seal $E$ is reached before any extreme-value comparison is available.

The located lower row $L$ and upper row $U_b$ compare the same Real-sealed finite family. The ledger $M$ prevents one bound from using a different net or value-readback route. Replaying the same rows through $H,C,P,Q$ gives the displayed factorization.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniform extreme-value bounds]
\label{thm:located-uniform-extreme-value-bounds}
An accepted $\LocatedUniformExtremeValueUp$ carrier exposes paired lower and upper value windows only as located finite-net comparisons. It does not expose an argmin, an argmax, a quotient equality class for real names, an open-cover compactness proof, a choice functional, or an ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the excluded coordinates in \autoref{def:located-uniform-extreme-value-carrier}. The carrier contains rows for $L$, $U_b$, and $M$, but no coordinate that names a minimizing point, maximizing point, quotient real equality token, open-cover proof, choice functional, or completeness witness.

Apply \autoref{thm:located-uniform-extreme-value-obligations}. The only comparison obligations are the lower row, the upper row, and the shared finite-net ledger. Therefore any accepted bound read is a located finite-net comparison read, not an extraction of one of the excluded objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniform extreme-value NameCert route]
\label{thm:located-uniform-extreme-value-namecert-route}
The $\LocatedUniformExtremeValueUp$ NameCert route is the paired-bound route obtained by adding an upper located comparison row to the finite-net maximum-location route of $\BishopLocatedMaximumUp$ while keeping the same compact source, uniformly continuous map, finite net, value-window schedule, rational readback, and Real seal interface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the sibling route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-located-maximum-namecert} for the compact source, map row, finite net, value-window schedule, rational readback, and Real seal. The present carrier keeps those rows as $K,F,N,W,R,E$.

Add the lower and upper located comparison rows $L$ and $U_b$ and bind them with the shared ledger $M$. The replay and provenance rows $H,C,P,Q$ then name the same finite route for both bounds, so the NameCert route is precisely the paired-bound route described in the statement.
\end{proof}
