Following finite-dimensional linear algebra and bounded-operator theory, the $\FiniteRankOperatorUp$ packet records the displayed finite-rank route by which a linear operator factors through a finite-dimensional image row before compactness, spectral, Hilbert, or functional-analysis consumers may read it. It sits after the vector-space carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, the linear-map surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-linearmap-namecert}, the finite matrix readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, the bounded-linear-operator boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedlinearoperator-namecert}, and the compact-operator sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactoperator-namecert}. The packet names the finite image ledger and its operator handoff; it does not assert compact approximation, spectral decomposition, a selected basis for every image, quotient function equality, or an ambient functional-analysis theorem.

\begin{definition}[Finite-rank operator carrier]
\label{def:finite-rank-operator-carrier}
A $\FiniteRankOperatorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(X,Y,T,I,B,M,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ and $Y$ are the source and target $\VecSpaceUp$ rows.
\item $T$ is the $\LinearMapUp$ row from $X$ to $Y$; when norm control is requested, the same row is also read through $\BoundedLinearOperatorUp$.
\item $I$ is the finite image-window row, listing displayed target vectors that span the image of $T$ for the requested source window.
\item $B$ is the finite-dimensional basis or spanning ledger for $I$, read only as BEDC rows and not as a host basis selector.
\item $M$ is the optional $\MatrixUp$ presentation row tying the displayed coefficients of $T$ to the finite image ledger.
\item $E$ is the exactness row saying that every displayed operator value in the request lands in the span recorded by $I$ and $B$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the vector-space endpoints, operator row, image ledger, basis ledger, matrix presentation, exactness row, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an operator read through the finite image rows and back to bounded, compact, spectral, Hilbert, or functional-analysis consumers.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteRankOperatorUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\BoundedLinearOperatorUp$, $\CompactOperatorUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteRankOperatorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for an ambient operator algebra, an infinite basis, a quotient representative of the image, a compactness theorem, or a spectral theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-rank operator NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-rank-operator-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteRankOperatorUp$ carrier $R=(X,Y,T,I,B,M,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteRankOperatorUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, source and target vector-space exposure, linear-map action exposure, finite image-window exactness, optional matrix readback compatibility, bounded-operator handoff when norm control is displayed, compact-operator handoff through the finite image rows, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming.

Any $\CompactOperatorUp$, $\SpectralRadiusUp$, $\HilbertUp$, or $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$ consumer that reads finite-rank data from $R$ must first pass through $X,Y,T,I,B,M,E,H,C,P,N$. The read exports no ambient compactness theorem, no selected infinite approximation scheme, no spectral decomposition, no quotient image equality, and no host operator-space object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-rank-operator-carrier}. The vector-space endpoints $X,Y$, the operator row $T$, the finite image rows $I,B$, and the exactness row $E$ are displayed before any consumer-facing row is available.

Matrix compatibility is only a readback route. If $M$ is present, its coefficients are tied to the same $T,I,B$ rows; if it is absent, the carrier still exposes finite image exactness directly through $E$.

Bounded and compact consumers receive only the finite route recorded by $C$ and $P$. The bounded handoff reads the norm-controlled operator row when it is displayed, while the compact handoff reads finite-rankness through the finite image ledger rather than through an ambient compactness theorem.

Transport is componentwise through $H$, and naming is local through $N$. Since no carrier coordinate stores an infinite basis, a selected approximation sequence, quotient image equality, spectral decomposition, or host operator space, the obligations listed above exhaust the $\FiniteRankOperatorUp$ naming surface.
\end{proof}
