\label{thm:sobolev-bridged-finite-derivative-window}
Every Sobolev packet bridge read factors through the finite derivative-window route
$$
D,M,V,L,G,T,H,C,P,N .
$$
The rows $D,M,V,L,G$ expose the $\RealUp$ domain, metric or complete-metric base, norm or Hilbert codomain, finite-energy magnitude ledger, and weak $\DerivativeUp$ window; $T$ records the trace-boundary window; and $H,C,P,N$ provide componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows before any analytic consumer reads a $\SobolevUp$ object. \end{theorem}
