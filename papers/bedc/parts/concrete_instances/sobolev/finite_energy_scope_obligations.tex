\begin{theorem}[Sobolev finite-energy obligation scope]
\label{thm:sobolev-finite-energy-obligation-scope}
Every finite-energy consumer of an accepted $\SobolevUp$ packet must expose the displayed $\RealUp$ domain row $D$, the $\MetricSpaceUp$ or $\CompleteMetricUp$ source row $M$, the $\NormUp$ or $\HilbertUp$ codomain row $V$, the $\IntegralUp$ magnitude ledger $L$, the weak $\DerivativeUp$ test row $G$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and local $\NameCert$ row $N$ before it reads Sobolev energy data. The scope refuses distribution theory, Lebesgue completion, quotient equality of functions, density of smooth functions, compact embedding, and trace theory.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.SobolevUp.SobolevFiniteEnergyObligationScope}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sobolev-bhist-carrier}. The finite-energy read has exactly the analytic rows $D,M,V,L,G$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Use the obligation package and weak-derivative ledger. They place the Real domain, metric or complete-metric source, norm or Hilbert codomain, integral magnitude, and derivative test rows on the same finite route before any analysis-facing consumer can cite the packet.

Finally apply the completion-boundary theorem. The refused analytic objects are not coordinates of the carrier and are not introduced by transport, replay, provenance, or local naming, so the finite-energy scope is exhausted by the displayed rows.
\end{proof}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.SobolevUp.SobolevCarrier\_finite\_energy\_obligation\_scope}

\label{thm:sobolev-trace-boundary-obligation-route}
Any boundary-facing, PDE-facing, or variational read that attempts to use $\SobolevUp$ trace language must first pass through the same finite route

D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N .
$$
