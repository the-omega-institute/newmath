\begin{theorem}[Sobolev energy-trace scope obligation]
\label{thm:sobolev-energy-trace-scope-obligation}
Let $S=(D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\SobolevUp$ packet. If a consumer exposes finite energy rows, weak derivative rows, boundary trace rows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ readbacks, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert_{\SobolevUp}$ row, then the consumer may export only the finite energy-to-trace scope route through $D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N$. It may not export Hahn--Banach separation, unrestricted distribution theory, measure completion, quotient function equality, ambient Hilbert or Banach completeness, or a trace operator detached from the displayed packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the finite-energy scope obligations of \autoref{thm:sobolev-finite-energy-obligation-scope}. They force every energy-facing read to display the Real domain row, metric or complete-metric source row, norm or Hilbert codomain row, integral magnitude ledger, weak derivative test row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before Sobolev energy data is consumed.

Next apply the trace-boundary obligation route of \autoref{thm:sobolev-trace-boundary-obligation-route}. Trace-facing language is therefore read only along the same finite packet, with the boundary read downstream of the energy and weak-derivative rows rather than as an external boundary operator.

Finally combine the two routes. The readbacks to $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ are ledger and seal rows inside the displayed package, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name that package. Since no listed coordinate is Hahn--Banach separation, unrestricted distribution theory, measure completion, quotient function equality, or ambient Hilbert or Banach completeness, the exported scope is exactly the finite energy-to-trace route.
\end{proof}
