\begin{theorem}[Sobolev energy-trace scope obligation]
\label{thm:sobolev-energy-trace-scope-obligation}
Let $S=(D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\SobolevUp$ packet. If a consumer exposes finite energy rows, weak derivative rows, boundary trace rows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ readbacks, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert_{\SobolevUp}$ row, then the consumer may export only the finite energy-to-trace scope route through $D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N$. It may not export Hahn--Banach separation, unrestricted distribution theory, measure completion, quotient function equality, ambient Hilbert or Banach completeness, or a trace operator detached from the displayed packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the finite-energy scope obligations of \autoref{thm:sobolev-finite-energy-obligation-scope}. They force every energy-facing read to display the Real domain row, metric or complete-metric source row, norm or Hilbert codomain row, integral magnitude ledger, weak derivative test row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before Sobolev energy data is consumed.

Next apply the trace-boundary obligation route of \autoref{thm:sobolev-trace-boundary-obligation-route}. Trace-facing language is therefore read only along the same finite packet, with the boundary read downstream of the energy and weak-derivative rows rather than as an external boundary operator.

Finally combine the two routes. The readbacks to $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ are ledger and seal rows inside the displayed package, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name that package. Since no listed coordinate is Hahn--Banach separation, unrestricted distribution theory, measure completion, quotient function equality, or ambient Hilbert or Banach completeness, the exported scope is exactly the finite energy-to-trace route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sobolev energy-trace carrier obligation]
\label{thm:sobolev-energy-trace-obligation-carrier}
For every accepted $\SobolevUp$ packet $S=(D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N)$, an energy-trace consumer receives only the finite carrier rows for derivative evidence $G$, integral magnitude evidence $L$, Hilbert or norm evidence $V$, metric or complete-metric evidence $M$, terminal $\RealUp$ domain evidence $D$, and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback carried through the displayed magnitude and domain rows. The carrier surface has no row for a host weak derivative, measure-theoretic completion, quotient function carrier, selected representative, or ambient Hilbert completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sobolev-bhist-carrier}. Its analytic coordinates are exactly the domain row $D$, metric or complete-metric row $M$, norm or Hilbert row $V$, integral magnitude row $L$, and derivative row $G$.

Use the finite-energy obligation scope, \autoref{thm:sobolev-finite-energy-obligation-scope}. It forces an energy-facing read to expose the derivative, integral, Hilbert or norm, metric, and Real rows before Sobolev data is consumed.

The regular-rational readback is not a separate completion object. It is read only through the displayed magnitude and domain ledgers already attached to $D,M,V,L,G$, so the excluded host analytic objects remain absent from the carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sobolev energy-trace kernel scope]
\label{thm:sobolev-energy-trace-kernel-scope}
Every energy-trace consumer of an accepted $\SobolevUp$ packet factors through $\BHist$, componentwise $\hsame$, displayed $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, local $\NameCert$, and the displayed L10 rows $D,M,V,L,G$ before it may use any boundary trace language. The consumer reads the same kernel packet $S=(D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N)$ and cannot detach a trace operator from the energy, derivative, integral, metric, Real, and RegSeqRat evidence rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:sobolev-energy-trace-obligation-carrier}. It identifies the only analytic rows available to an energy-trace read: $D,M,V,L,G$ together with the regular-rational readback carried by those rows.

Next apply the trace-boundary obligation route, \autoref{thm:sobolev-trace-boundary-obligation-route}. Boundary-facing language is downstream of the same carrier and cannot be read as an external trace operator.

Finally expose the structural rows. The $\BHist$ packet stores the rows, $H$ transports them componentwise by $\hsame$, $C$ replays them through $\Cont$, $P$ records provenance, and $N$ names the local certificate. Thus every trace consumer remains inside the displayed kernel-scoped route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sobolev energy-trace nonescape]
\label{thm:sobolev-energy-trace-nonescape}
No accepted $\SobolevUp$ energy-trace read exports host weak derivatives, measure-theoretic completion, quotient functions, selected representatives, or ambient Hilbert completeness. Its complete export surface is the finite route
$$
D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N
$$
with the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ readbacks attached to the displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the kernel-scope theorem, \autoref{thm:sobolev-energy-trace-kernel-scope}. It places every energy-trace consumer on the finite packet rows $D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N$ before trace language is available.

Apply the completion boundary, \autoref{thm:sobolev-completion-boundary}. Downstream Real, integral, derivative, Hilbert, functional-analysis, PDE, variational, and compact-embedding consumers remain inside the displayed carrier and local NameCert surface.

The refused objects are not coordinates of the carrier. Since host weak derivatives, measure completion, quotient functions, selected representatives, and ambient Hilbert completeness are absent from $D,M,V,L,G,H,C,P,N$, they cannot escape from an accepted energy-trace read.
\end{proof}
