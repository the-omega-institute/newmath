\begin{definition}[Cantor nested interval carrier]
\label{def:cantor-nested-interval-carrier}
A $\CantorNestedIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(L,U,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ and $U$ are finite endpoint histories whose entries are read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ as lower and upper rational endpoints.
\item $D$ is a dyadic diameter budget assigning each displayed index $k$ a dyadic bound for the interval width.
\item $W$ is the finite window in which the packet checks endpoint order and nesting for all displayed adjacent stages.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row that turns the endpoint windows into regular rational midpoint or endpoint reads.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached from those regular reads.
\item $H,C,P,N$ are the componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert_{\CantorNestedIntervalUp}$ rows.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier accepts only histories where each displayed interval is closed, rational, nested in the previous displayed interval, and bounded by the displayed dyadic diameter budget. It contains no coordinate for a selected point, a quotient real equality, trichotomy of endpoints, Heine--Borel compactness, or full real completeness.
\end{definition}

\label{thm:cantor-nested-interval-namecert-obligations}
