It sits after the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the finite stream-window discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Bishop interval order carrier]
\label{def:bishop-interval-order-carrier}
A $\BishopIntervalOrderUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(L,U,A,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the lower $\RealUp$ endpoint row, exposed only as a named real boundary.
\item $U$ is the upper $\RealUp$ endpoint row, paired with $L$ by the same interval carrier.
\item $A$ is the located-apartness witness row, recording positive separation evidence when the order comparison is read constructively.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare finite endpoint observations.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row naming the finite endpoint reads inspected by the tolerance ledger.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the same windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the lower and upper endpoints after $S,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoints, apartness, dyadic tolerances, stream windows, regular readback, real seals, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an interval-order request through $L,U,A,D,S,R,E,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopIntervalOrderUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopIntervalOrderUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $L,U,A,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for real trichotomy, quotient interval equality, selected representatives, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop interval order NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-interval-order-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopIntervalOrderUp$ carrier $I=(L,U,A,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopIntervalOrderUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the lower endpoint, upper endpoint, located-apartness witness, dyadic tolerance ledger, stream-window row, regular rational readback, terminal real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-interval-order-carrier}. Its interval data are exactly the endpoint rows $L,U$, the apartness row $A$, the dyadic tolerance row $D$, the finite window row $S$, the regular readback row $R$, and the terminal seal row $E$.

The endpoint rows are read through the same finite observation route. The stream windows and regular rational readback provide the displayed data that the dyadic tolerance ledger may compare, and the apartness row records only positive separation evidence visible through that route.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for trichotomy, quotient interval equality, host real equality, selected representatives, or ambient completeness, no further local NameCert clause can be read from it.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop interval order apartness route]
\label{thm:bishop-interval-order-apartness-route}
Every constructive order read exported by an accepted $\BishopIntervalOrderUp$ carrier factors through
$$
L,U \longmapsto S,R,D \longmapsto A \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus a consumer may use displayed located-apartness data to separate endpoint intervals, but it cannot replace that route by trichotomy, quotient intervals, host real equality, selected endpoint representatives, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:bishop-interval-order-namecert-obligations}. Any interval-order consumer must first read the lower and upper endpoint rows together with their finite stream windows and regular rational readback.

The dyadic tolerance ledger is the comparison budget for those reads. A positive order separation is therefore available only through the located-apartness row $A$, which is downstream of the displayed endpoint observations.

The terminal real seal and structural rows do not add a classical comparison principle. They only seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same apartness-routed interval packet.

Consequently every exported constructive order read factors through the displayed route, and all non-displayed real-comparison principles remain outside the packet.
\end{proof}
