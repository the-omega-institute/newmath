$$

\item $E$ is the entourage and tolerance row, keeping the requested closeness data explicit before any completion handoff.

\begin{theorem}[Bourbaki Cauchy-filter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bourbaki-cauchy-filter-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BourbakiCauchyFilterUp$ carrier
$$

\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:bourbaki-cauchy-filter-carrier}. The first three rows expose the Cauchy-filter source, the finite base, and the entourage or tolerance request as displayed data rather than as an ambient filter quotient.

The next four rows order the completion read through finite windows, regular rational readback, dyadic accounting, and the terminal Real seal. Thus a consumer cannot reach $S$ before the rows $F_0,B,E,W,R,D$ have been read.
