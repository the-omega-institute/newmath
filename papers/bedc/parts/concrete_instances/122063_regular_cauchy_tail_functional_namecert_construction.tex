\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which tail requests are exposed.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger attached to the inspected window.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the analytic rows and the structural rows.

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy tail-functional NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-tail-functional-namecert-obligations}

$$

the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyTailFunctionalUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the regular rational source, finite stream window, dyadic tolerance ledger, tail-functional row, terminal Real seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-tail-functional-carrier}. Its rows list the regular rational source $S$, stream window $W$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, tail-functional row $T$, seal row $R$, and structural support $H,C,P,N$.

The tail read is ordered by the carrier. A consumer must expose $S,W,D$ before the tail-functional row can be read, and the seal row $R$ is downstream from that finite tail read.

The structural rows do not add analytic content. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming keep the same packet visible, so the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy tail-functional window stability]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-tail-functional-window-stability}
Let $F=(S,W,D,T,R,H,C,P,N)$ and $F'=(S',W',D',T',R',H',C',P',N')$ be accepted $\RegularCauchyTailFunctionalUp$ carriers. If the source, window, and dyadic rows are transported componentwise by the displayed $\hsame$ data in $H$ and $H'$, then the tail-functional rows $T$ and $T'$ remain on the same local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyTailFunctionalUp}$ surface before either packet reaches its $\RealUp$ seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the transported source rows first. Componentwise $\hsame$ identifies the regular rational sources and their finite stream windows before the tail-functional row is consumed.

The dyadic tolerance ledgers are transported along the same displayed route. Hence the tail-functional rows compare finite requests inside the same window-and-tolerance surface rather than through a host equality test.

The seal rows and structural rows are downstream. Since $R,H,C,P,N$ and $R',H',C',P',N'$ only seal, transport, replay, source, and name the transported packet, window stability is exactly stability of the displayed finite rows.
\end{proof}
