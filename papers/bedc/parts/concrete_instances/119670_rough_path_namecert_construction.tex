Following Lyons' rough path theory, the $\RoughPathUp$ packet records an enhanced finite-variation route for paths whose iterated path-step data must be inspected before a controlled differential equation consumer may read a terminal real-name seal. It sits beside the Young integral surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-young-integral-namecert}, the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. The packet names the displayed enhancement and control-budget rows only; it is not a host rough-path quotient, selected lift, analytic existence theorem, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle for $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Rough path carrier]
\label{def:rough-path-carrier}
A $\RoughPathUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(I,V,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the enhanced path-step row, listing first-level path steps, second-level iterated path-step witnesses, Chen-composition checks, and the finite interval ledger on which they are read.
\item $V$ is the $p$-variation and control-budget row, recording the admissible variation exponent, control windows, superadditive budget checks, and the finite margins used by downstream integration requests.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row for the inspected time windows and endpoint requests.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for rational observations of path-step data, iterated path-step data, and control budgets.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $I,V,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for enhanced path-step data, variation budgets, stream observations, readback rows, the Real seal, provenance, and the local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a rough-path request through enhanced path-step data, variation control, stream windows, regular rational readback, and the Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RoughPathUp$, $\YoungIntegralUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RoughPathUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $I,V,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host enhancement quotient, a selected lift, a hidden area process, countable choice, an analytic existence theorem for differential equations, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Rough path NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:rough-path-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RoughPathUp$ carrier $X=(I,V,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RoughPathUp}$ surface is exhausted by enhanced path-step data, $p$-variation and control-budget checks, $\StreamNameUp$ observation, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No accepted consumer may replace those rows by a host rough-path quotient, a selected lift, an unrecorded area process, countable choice, an analytic existence theorem, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:rough-path-carrier}. The row $I$ exposes the first-level and iterated path-step data together with the displayed Chen-composition checks, and $V$ exposes the finite $p$-variation and control budget used to read that path-step data.

The observation and readback route is then fixed. The row $S$ records the inspected endpoint windows, $R$ gives the regular rational readback for the same displayed windows, and $E$ is available only after those rows have been consumed.

Transport and replay preserve the route. The rows $H$ and $C$ carry componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same path-step, budget, observation, readback, and seal rows, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming.

Since the excluded objects have no coordinate in $I,V,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$, a consumer using one of them has left the accepted packet rather than reading the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Rough path p-variation handoff]
\label{thm:rough-path-p-variation-handoff}
Every accepted $\RoughPathUp$ read that is later consumed by a controlled integration or controlled differential equation interface first factors through
$$
\YoungIntegralUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
using the displayed rows $I,V,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. The handoff exposes enhanced path-step data and a $p$-variation control budget before any Real-facing read, and it exports no host lift, quotient rough path, selected area representative, countable-choice schedule, or ambient Real-completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the enhancement and variation rows. The row $I$ supplies the finite path-step and iterated path-step ledger, while $V$ records the control windows and $p$-variation margins needed by a Young-style controlled integral request.

Next read the finite observations. The $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$ fixes the endpoint windows inspected by the consumer, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ attaches rational readback to the same windows before the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is exposed.

Finally use $H,C,P,N$. Componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite route, so any downstream controlled-equation interface receives the enhanced path-step and control-budget data without importing a host lift, quotient rough path, selected area representative, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
