\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\EgorovTheoremUp$}

Following Egorov and Lebesgue, the $\EgorovTheoremUp$ packet records the theorem-level finite route from almost-uniform convergence data to measure-convergence consumers without adding a host measure-completion primitive. It sits after the finite measure and integration ledgers of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-measure-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-integral-namecert}, the lower-semicontinuity route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fatou-lemma-namecert}, the domination route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dominated-convergence-namecert}, the large-set continuous approximation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lusin-approximation-namecert}, and the regular-rational and real-name seals of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite NameCert carrier for the theorem interface: it exports exceptional-set and uniform-window rows that later Fatou, dominated-convergence, and Lusin-style readers can consume, but it does not claim ambient measure completion, almost-everywhere quotient equality, selected representatives, countable choice, or the full host Egorov theorem.

\begin{definition}[Egorov theorem carrier]
\label{def:egorov-theorem-carrier}
An $\EgorovTheoremUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(M,I,F,D,L,R,E,A,W,U,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the finite $\MeasureUp$ row whose measurable-set ledger bounds every exceptional-set read.
\item $I$ is the $\IntegralUp$ row used by downstream integral consumers after the exceptional and uniform rows have been displayed.
\item $F$ is the $\FatouLemmaUp$ source row, exposed only as a lower-bound and liminf-facing consumer of the same finite measure ledger.
\item $D$ is the $\DominatedConvergenceUp$ source row, exposed only as a domination and integrability consumer of $M,I,A,W,U$.
\item $L$ is the $\LusinApproximationUp$ source row, exposing large-set continuous approximation only after the exceptional-set budget is visible.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback route for all displayed real-valued observations and tolerance comparisons.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached by the regular-rational readback route, not by a host real-completeness argument.
\item $A$ is the finite exceptional-set row, recording the measurable approximants, the finite measure budget, and containment checks used by the theorem packet.
\item $W$ is the finite uniform-window row, recording the index threshold, precision requests, and observation sites on which the theorem route is uniform outside $A$.
\item $U$ is the almost-uniform convergence row, asserting that the displayed $R,E,W$ comparisons are uniform on the complement of the displayed exceptional budget.
\item $Q$ is the theorem-handoff row, routing accepted reads from $A,W,U$ into the Fatou, dominated-convergence, Lusin, and integral consumers without changing their carrier rows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,I,F,D,L,R,E,A,W,U,Q$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite route
$$
M,I,F,D,L,R,E,A,W,U,Q \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EgorovTheoremUp$, $\MeasureUp$, $\IntegralUp$, $\FatouLemmaUp$, $\DominatedConvergenceUp$, $\LusinApproximationUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EgorovTheoremUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,I,F,D,L,R,E,A,W,U,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for completed measure algebras, quotient almost-everywhere equality, selected representatives, countable exceptional unions, countable choice, or an ambient theorem object outside the finite carrier.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Egorov theorem NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:egorov-theorem-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EgorovTheoremUp$ carrier
$$
G=(M,I,F,D,L,R,E,A,W,U,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\EgorovTheoremUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the finite measure row, integral row, Fatou row, dominated-convergence row, Lusin row, regular-rational readback row, real-name seal, exceptional-set row, uniform-window row, almost-uniform convergence row, theorem-handoff row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every theorem-level public read must pass through $A,W,U,Q$ before it can reach the Fatou, dominated-convergence, Lusin, or integral consumers.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:egorov-theorem-carrier}. The theorem-facing rows are $A,W,U,Q$: they name the finite exceptional budget, the finite uniform window, the almost-uniform comparison, and the handoff to downstream consumers.

The rows $M,I,F,D,L,R,E$ supply the measure, integral, Fatou, dominated-convergence, Lusin, regular-rational, and real-name dependency surface on which those four rows are interpreted. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly the same finite route.

Since the carrier contains no completed measure algebra, quotient almost-everywhere equality, selected representative, countable exceptional union, countable choice row, or ambient theorem object, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Egorov theorem finite handoff]
\label{thm:egorov-theorem-finite-handoff}
For every accepted $\EgorovTheoremUp$ carrier $G$, any downstream almost-uniform convergence read by a Fatou, dominated-convergence, Lusin, or integral consumer factors through the finite route
$$
A,W,U,Q \longrightarrow F,D,L,I .
$$
The handoff preserves the displayed $\MeasureUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows and does not replace them by selected exceptional sets, almost-everywhere quotient equality, selected representatives, countable choice, or ambient measure completion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the theorem-handoff row $Q$. By carrier admission, $Q$ is displayed only together with the exceptional-set row $A$, uniform-window row $W$, and almost-uniform convergence row $U$.

Read the downstream consumers next. The Fatou row $F$, dominated-convergence row $D$, Lusin row $L$, and integral row $I$ receive the same finite measure ledger and the same regular-rational and real-name seals already named by $M,R,E$.

Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve those rows componentwise. Because no selected exceptional set, quotient almost-everywhere equality, selected representative, countable-choice row, or measure-completion row occurs in $G$, the handoff is exhausted by the displayed finite route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Egorov theorem exceptional-window nonescape]
\label{thm:egorov-theorem-exceptional-window-nonescape}
Every accepted $\EgorovTheoremUp$ packet exposes its theorem-level convergence content only through the paired exceptional-set and uniform-window rows $A,W$ together with the almost-uniform row $U$. A consumer may refine a finite observation schedule through the same $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ rows, but it may not bypass $A,W,U$ by invoking ambient measure completion, almost-everywhere quotient equality, selected representatives, countable choice, or the full host Egorov theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The finite observation schedule is read through $W$, and each precision comparison is evaluated through $R$ and sealed by $E$. The exceptional-set budget is read through $A$, whose finite measure ledger is interpreted by $M$.

The almost-uniform row $U$ binds these two reads together. Thus any refinement of observations that preserves the same $A,W,U,R,E$ data gives the same public theorem-level verdict.

No other carrier coordinate can export the convergence content. In particular $Q,H,C,P,N$ only hand off, transport, replay, source, and name the displayed rows, so the forbidden ambient objects have no projection from the packet.
\end{proof}
