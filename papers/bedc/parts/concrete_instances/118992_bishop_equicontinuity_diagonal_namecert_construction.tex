\begin{theorem}[Bishop equicontinuity diagonal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-equicontinuity-diagonal-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-equicontinuity-diagonal-carrier}. The compact finite-net source is $K$, the family-radius source is $F$, and the bounded-family ledger is $B$; these are precisely the rows whose sibling chapters are \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-equicontinuityfamily-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-function-family-namecert}.
