\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KuratowskiClusterSetUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-cluster-set-namecert}
\origin{human}

From Kuratowski convergence of closed sets and Kelley--Moore--Smith net cluster-set practice, the $\KuratowskiClusterSetUp$ packet records boundary cluster evidence by finite hit--miss windows rather than by a host powerset of possible cluster points. It sits beside $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$, $\MooreSmithNetUp$, $\FilterBaseUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ as the L10 net/filter cluster-set carrier used by completion-facing reads.

The hit--miss rows are read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-convergence-namecert}, the net and filter source rows through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-moore-smith-net-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-filterbase-namecert}, and the compact/metric support rows through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert} before the carrier exposes $K_h,K_m,C,M,S$ to any cluster-set consumer.

\begin{definition}[Kuratowski cluster-set carrier]
\label{def:kuratowski-cluster-set-carrier}
A $\KuratowskiClusterSetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(S,F,K_h,K_m,C,M,B,X,R,E,T,U,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the net or filter source row, naming either a $\MooreSmithNetUp$ index-window read or a $\FilterBaseUp$ base-window read.
\item $F$ records the finite source-frontier windows consumed by the cluster-set request.
\item $K_h$ is the Kuratowski hit ledger: each row names a displayed source window, a metric neighborhood, and a later witness whose image meets that neighborhood.
\item $K_m$ is the Kuratowski miss ledger: each row names a finite exclusion or compact containment window showing that the inspected boundary claim does not escape the displayed metric neighborhood.
\item $C$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ support row used when compact subwindows make the hit and miss ledgers finite.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ distance-observation row used by the hit, miss, compact-support, and Real-facing reads.
\item $B$ is the cluster-hit witness row, storing only displayed later-window hits and never a selected cluster point.
\item $X$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ readback row for source-name and observation-name replay.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational row used to read metric tolerances and shrinking windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for completion-facing consumers.
\item $T,U,P,N$ are respectively componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert_{\KuratowskiClusterSetUp}$ naming row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host powerset of cluster points, a selected cluster point, quotient equality of closed sets, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Kuratowski cluster-set NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kuratowski-cluster-set-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KuratowskiClusterSetUp$ carrier
$$
K=(S,F,K_h,K_m,C,M,B,X,R,E,T,U,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\KuratowskiClusterSetUp}$ surface consists exactly of net-or-filter source admission, finite frontier-window admission, Kuratowski hit-window admission, Kuratowski miss-window admission, compact and metric support, displayed cluster-hit witnessing, $\StreamNameUp$ readback, $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance readback, $\RealUp$ sealing, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:kuratowski-cluster-set-carrier}. The mathematical rows are precisely $S,F,K_h,K_m,C,M,B,X,R,E$; the structural rows are $T,U,P,N$.

The hit and miss obligations are separated. A hit request must read a later-window witness from $K_h$ and $B$, while a miss request must read the finite exclusion or compact containment evidence from $K_m$ together with the metric row $M$ and compact support row $C$.

The readback rows $X,R,E$ expose only names, rational tolerances, and terminal Real-facing seals downstream of those finite windows. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same route, so no additional NameCert clause can be supplied by a powerset, selected point, quotient, choice principle, or ambient completeness proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kuratowski cluster-set net-filter handoff]
\label{thm:kuratowski-cluster-set-net-filter-handoff}
Every net-facing or filter-facing cluster-set read exported by an accepted $\KuratowskiClusterSetUp$ carrier factors through the displayed route
$$
\MooreSmithNetUp/\FilterBaseUp,\quad \KuratowskiConvergenceUp,\quad \CompactMetricUp,\quad \MetricUp,\quad \StreamNameUp,\quad \RegSeqRatUp,\quad \RealUp .
$$
The handoff exposes the source frontier, Kuratowski hit and miss windows, compact or metric support, cluster-hit witness, stream-name replay, regular rational tolerance row, and terminal Real seal before any completion consumer may use the cluster-set boundary. It exports no host powerset, selected cluster point, quotient closed-set equality, countable-choice functional, or ambient Real completeness argument.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.KuratowskiClusterSetUp.KuratowskiClusterSetNetFilterHandoff}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the source row $S$. A net read uses the displayed $\MooreSmithNetUp$ window and a filter read uses the displayed $\FilterBaseUp$ base-window route; both are recorded as finite frontier data in $F$ before a cluster request is available.

Next read the Kuratowski rows. The hit ledger $K_h$ and miss ledger $K_m$ are the only rows where the boundary condition is tested, and their metric and compact support is supplied by $M$ and $C$. The cluster-hit witness row $B$ records later-window hits rather than a selected cluster point.

Finally pass through $X,R,E,T,U,P,N$. These rows provide stream-name replay, regular rational tolerance readback, Real-facing sealing, transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier contains no powerset, quotient, selected point, choice functional, or ambient completeness coordinate, the net/filter handoff is exhausted by the displayed finite route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kuratowski cluster-set sequential compact route]
\label{thm:kuratowski-cluster-set-sequentialcompact-route}
Every sequential-compact cluster consumer of an accepted $\KuratowskiClusterSetUp$ carrier must read the displayed sibling route through $\SequentialCompactUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$, including the chapter routes of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sequentialcompact-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}. \end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.KuratowskiClusterSetUp.KuratowskiClusterSetSequentialCompactRoute}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the compact source. The $\SequentialCompactUp$ chapter routes sequential cluster reads through displayed compact-metric and finite-window data, while the $\CompactMetricUp$ route supplies only the compact support row visible here as $C$.

Next expose the metric and readback rows. The carrier rows $M,X,R,E$ provide metric observations, stream-name replay, regular rational tolerance readback, and the terminal Real seal before the Kuratowski hit and miss ledgers can be consumed.

Finally return to $K_h,K_m,B,T,U,P,N$. The hit and miss ledgers remain finite boundary rows transported and replayed through the same packet; they do not become a host subsequence selector, a countable-choice witness, a selected cluster point, quotient closed-set equality, or ambient Real completeness theorem.
\end{proof}
