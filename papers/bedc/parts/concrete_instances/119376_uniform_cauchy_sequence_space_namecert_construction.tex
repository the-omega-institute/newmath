\begin{theorem}[Uniform Cauchy sequence space obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-cauchy-sequence-space-obligations}

$$

$$
and through no quotient sequence equality, selected limit, host sequence extensionality, countable-choice modulus, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-cauchy-sequence-space-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly $S,R,D,Q,M,E$, and the structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

The finite schedule $S$ exposes the inspected windows, while $R$ records the regular rational readback extracted from those windows. The dyadic ledger $D$ supplies the tolerance cells used by every comparison.

The sequence-space row $Q$ admits the ordinary Cauchy surface. The common modulus row $M$ is the extra uniform coordinate: it binds all admitted names to one displayed tail budget before the terminal seal $E$ can be consumed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only this packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores quotient equality, selected limits, host extensionality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:uniform-cauchy-sequence-space-nonescape}
