\begin{definition}[Dirichlet--Abel summability carrier]
\label{def:dirichlet-abel-summability-carrier}
A $\DirichletAbelSummabilityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(S,A,D,U,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\RealSeriesUp$ source row, exposing the real-valued term and partial-sum observations only through the series packet.
\item $A$ is the $\AbelTestUp$ face row, recording the Abel-style monotone coefficient and tail-control evidence available for the same source.
\item $D$ is the $\DirichletTestUp$ face row, recording bounded partial-sum data, null-tail data, and the finite Dirichlet estimate schedule for the same source.
\item $U$ is the shared $\UniformCauchySeriesUp$ handoff row reached only after the Abel and Dirichlet faces have both displayed their finite evidence.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the common summability windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $S,A,D,U,R$ are visible.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,A,D,U,R,E,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a summability request through the source, the two test faces, the uniform handoff, readback, seal, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DirichletAbelSummabilityUp$, $\RealSeriesUp$, $\AbelTestUp$, $\DirichletTestUp$, $\UniformCauchySeriesUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DirichletAbelSummabilityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for host convergence of the series, selected infinite sums, quotient equality of series names, countable choice, detached Abel or Dirichlet witnesses, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dirichlet--Abel summability NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dirichlet-abel-summability-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DirichletAbelSummabilityUp$ carrier
$$
D=(S,A,D,U,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DirichletAbelSummabilityUp}$ surface consists exactly of RealSeries source admission, Abel-test face exposure, Dirichlet-test face exposure, shared UniformCauchySeries handoff, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal nonescape, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dirichlet-abel-summability-carrier}. The analytic rows are $S,A,D,U,R,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

Read the source and the two test faces first. The RealSeries row $S$ fixes the displayed term and partial-sum data, while $A$ and $D$ record the Abel and Dirichlet evidence for that same source.

Next read the completion-facing handoff. The row $U$ is shared by the two faces, so a consumer cannot reach the uniform Cauchy surface from only one detached test witness. The readback row $R$ and terminal seal row $E$ remain downstream of the shared handoff.

Finally the transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve the same finite route. Since no excluded object is a carrier coordinate, the local certificate cannot export host summability, a selected infinite sum, quotient series equality, countable choice, or ambient real completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dirichlet--Abel L10 summability handoff]
\label{thm:dirichlet-abel-summability-l10-handoff}

$$
\RealSeriesUp \longmapsto \AbelTestUp \longmapsto \DirichletTestUp \longmapsto \UniformCauchySeriesUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The route is a finite L10 handoff from two convergence-test faces to one completion-facing readback; it does not export a host series-summability theorem, selected infinite sum, quotient equality of series names, countable-choice schedule, detached test witness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:dirichlet-abel-summability-namecert-obligations}. They require the rows $S,A,D,U,R,E,H,C,P,N$ to be read as one packet before a Real-and-Completion consumer inspects the endpoint.

The Abel and Dirichlet faces are both attached to the same RealSeries source. Thus the consumer first reads $S$, then the Abel face $A$ and the Dirichlet face $D$ as sibling evidence for the same displayed series.

The shared uniform handoff $U$ is downstream of both faces. Once $U$ is visible, the regular rational readback $R$ and the Real seal $E$ can be consumed as completion-facing data, but neither row supplies an independent summability theorem or selected limit.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Therefore every L10 read remains inside the displayed summability packet.
\end{proof}
