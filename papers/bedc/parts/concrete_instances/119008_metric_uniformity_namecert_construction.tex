Following Bourbaki and Kelley on uniform structures generated by metrics, the $\MetricUniformityUp$ packet records the finite BEDC handoff from displayed metric balls to uniform entourages. It sits between the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the uniform-space surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the complete-metric route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the compact-metric row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the stream-name windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only the entourage basis generated by positive Real radii and its completion-facing readers; it does not add host uniform-space primitives, quotient filters, selected limits, open-cover compactness, or ambient Real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Metric uniformity carrier]
\label{def:metric-uniformity-carrier}
A $\MetricUniformityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(M,E,B,C,F,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ row whose distance observations and named balls are the source of every entourage request.
\item $E$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ entourage family generated by positive $\RealUp$ radii from $M$.
\item $B$ is the finite ball-basis row, listing the inspected centers, positive radius names, and inclusion witnesses used to cover one requested entourage.
\item $C$ is the Cauchy-filter handoff row for completion-facing consumers that read Cauchy data through the generated entourages.
\item $F$ is the completion-facing row that exposes the same generated uniformity to $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, stream-window, and regular-readback consumers.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transports for $M,E,B,C,F$ and the local naming row.
\item $T$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a metric-ball request through positive-radius generation, entourage exposure, Cauchy-filter handoff, and completion-facing readback.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetricUniformityUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetricUniformityUp}$ registration row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. There is no coordinate for primitive uniformity independent of the metric row, quotient Cauchy-filter equality, a selected completion point, open-cover compactness, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MetricUniformity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metric-uniformity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetricUniformityUp$ carrier $U=(M,E,B,C,F,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MetricUniformityUp}$ obligations are exhausted by carrier admission for the displayed metric row, generated entourage family, finite ball-basis row, Cauchy-filter handoff, completion-facing row, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming; by positive-radius generation of each entourage from $M$ through $B$; by completion-facing consumption through $C$ and $F$; and by ledger refusal for host uniform-space primitives, quotient filters, selected completion points, open-cover compactness, countable choice, trichotomy, and ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metric-uniformity-carrier}. The analytic rows are $M,E,B,C,F$, and the structural rows are $H,T,P,N$.

The generated-entourage obligation first reads a positive $\RealUp$ radius and a metric ball from $M$. The finite basis row $B$ records the inspected centers and inclusions needed to present that ball request as an entourage in $E$.

Completion-facing consumers are downstream of the generated row. A Cauchy-filter read uses $C$, and the complete-metric or compact-metric read uses $F$, only after $E$ and the finite basis data have been exposed.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve exactly this route. Since the packet has no independent coordinate for host uniformity, quotient filters, selected completion points, open-cover compactness, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetricUniformity entourage basis]
\label{thm:metric-uniformity-entourage-basis}
Every entourage exposed by an accepted $\MetricUniformityUp$ packet factors through a finite metric-ball basis row. More precisely, a consumer of $E$ must display the metric row $M$, a positive $\RealUp$ radius, and the finite basis row $B$ before the entourage can be read as a $\UniformSpaceUp$ surface. The read cannot replace that finite route by a primitive host entourage family or by quotient equality of filters.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted packet $U=(M,E,B,C,F,H,T,P,N)$. The carrier definition makes $E$ generated data rather than a primitive uniformity row.

To read an entourage, the continuation replay $T$ first exposes the metric observation in $M$ and the positive radius name. The row $B$ then supplies the finite ball-basis evidence for the requested scale and center data.

Only after those rows are displayed may the consumer read $E$ as a $\UniformSpaceUp$ surface. Componentwise transport in $H$ preserves the same $M,B,E$ route, while $P$ and $N$ record no independent host entourage source.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetricUniformity Cauchy-filter handoff]
\label{thm:metric-uniformity-cauchy-filter-handoff}
Every $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, or $\RealUp$ completion-facing read of an accepted $\MetricUniformityUp$ packet must pass through the generated entourage row $E$, the finite basis row $B$, the Cauchy-filter handoff $C$, and the completion-facing row $F$. Thus metric completion consumers see a finite Metric-to-UniformSpace route, not a host completion object, selected Cauchy limit, quotient filter, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a completion-facing consumer. Carrier admission gives access only to $M,E,B,C,F,H,T,P,N$.

The entourage read is forced by \autoref{thm:metric-uniformity-entourage-basis}. Hence $M$ and $B$ are visible before any uniform-space consumer can inspect $E$.

The Cauchy-filter handoff $C$ is the next available completion row, and $F$ is the row through which complete-metric, compact-metric, stream-window, regular-readback, and Real-seal consumers read the generated uniformity.

The structural rows preserve and name the same finite route. Because no carrier row stores a host completion object, selected limit, quotient filter, countable-choice schedule, open-cover compactness, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness, none of those objects can be exported by the handoff.
\end{proof}
