\begin{definition}[Apartness cut carrier]
\label{def:apartness-cut-carrier}
An $\ApartnessCutUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(L,U,K,S,R,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the lower cut-observation row, containing only finite rational lower observations and their downward-use discipline.
\item $U$ is the upper cut-observation row, containing only finite rational upper observations and their upward-use discipline.
\item $K$ is the located-cut row, read through $\LocatedCutUp$ and carrying displayed gap witnesses between lower and upper observations.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ request schedule for the inspected cut windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row exposed after $S$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for radii, endpoint gaps, and dyadic comparison slack.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $K,S,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $Q$ is the apartness-facing query row: it records positive separation requests and their displayed rational witnesses, but it does not decide equality or trichotomy.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the lower, upper, located-cut, stream, regular-rational, dyadic, real-seal, apartness-query, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an apartness query through $L,U,K,S,R,D,E,Q,H,P,N$.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ApartnessCutUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $L,U,K,S,R,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for cut quotients, real trichotomy, a selected apartness oracle, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
