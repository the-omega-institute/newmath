\begin{definition}[Baire dense-open schedule carrier]
\label{def:baire-dense-open-schedule-carrier}
A $\BaireDenseOpenUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(D,W,S,R,T,C,H,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the dense-open request row, recording the finite list of open predicates, dense-witness obligations, and inspected refinement indices.
\item $W$ is the complete-metric window row, recording the displayed ball, radius, refinement, and nonempty-witness data available from $\CompleteMetricUp$ and $\BaireMetricUp$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule row controlling which dense-open requests and complete-metric windows may be inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational radii, shrinkage witnesses, and tolerance comparisons attached to $W$.
\item $T$ is the topology row, recording that each inspected dense-open request is read through the displayed $\TopologyUp$ basis and not through an ambient host set.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from $D,S$ to $W$, from $W$ to $R$, and from $T$ to the local dense-open read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for dense-open rows, complete-metric windows, schedules, rational readback, topology rows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BaireDenseOpenUp$, $\BaireMetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BaireDenseOpenUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,W,S,R,T,C,H,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a choice-selected generic point, a completed residual set, open-cover compactness, quotient equality of names, or ambient completeness beyond the displayed complete-metric windows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Baire dense-open schedule obligation surface]
\label{thm:baire-dense-open-schedule-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\BaireDenseOpenUp$ carrier $G=(D,W,S,R,T,C,H,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BaireDenseOpenUp}$ surface consists exactly of five finite rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item dense-open request admission through $D$, including the displayed open rows, dense-witness obligations, and inspected refinement indices;
\item complete-metric window admission through $W$, with BaireMetric and CompleteMetric radii exposed before any schedule read is consumed;
\item finite observation scheduling through $S$ and rational readback through $R$, so every inspected radius and shrinkage claim is replayed through StreamName and RegSeqRat rows;
\item topology and replay compatibility through $T,C,H$, preserving the same dense-open and window rows under $\Cont$ replay and componentwise $\hsame$ transport;
\item provenance and local naming through $P,N$, attaching the packet to the BaireMetric, CompleteMetric, Topology, StreamName, and RegSeqRat sources named in this chapter.
\end{enumerate}
The surface explicitly refuses a choice-selected generic point, open-cover compactness, quotient equality of names, and ambient completeness not already displayed in the complete-metric window row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:baire-dense-open-schedule-carrier}. The dense-open row $D$ and complete-metric window row $W$ are the only mathematical rows visible before replay, so a consumer must name the inspected open predicates and the corresponding finite metric windows.

Next read the schedule and rational rows. The $\StreamNameUp$ schedule $S$ determines which entries of $D$ and $W$ are inspected, while the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ records the rational radius and shrinkage readback for those entries.

Finally apply the topology, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows $T,C,H,P,N$. These rows preserve the displayed packet and do not add a selector, a host intersection, quotient equality, or an ambient completeness theorem. The five listed rows therefore exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
