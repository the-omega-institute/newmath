\begin{definition}[Proof-relevant normal-join carrier]\label{def:proof-relevant-normal-join-carrier}

$$

\item $S$ is the closed-normal source row, exposing the closed term, normal-source classifier, and atom cases admitted by the checked fragment.
\item $W$ is the proof-relevant join witness row, recording the finite common reduct evidence for the closed-normal or atom branch.
\item $B$ is the beta-star handoff row, carrying the transport from the parallel or atom join witness into the multi-step beta-star route.
\item $L$ is the conditional-boundary ledger, recording that the full substitution-compatibility input and arbitrary closed-source confluence remain outside this packet.
