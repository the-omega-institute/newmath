\begin{definition}[Regulated Cauchy filter carrier]
\label{def:regulated-cauchy-filter-carrier}
A $\RegulatedCauchyFilterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(B,F,Q,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\FilterLimitBasisUp$ row exposing the finite basis used by completion and limit consumers.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ source row whose accepted reads remain tied to the displayed basis row $B$.
\item $Q$ is the $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ completion-facing row available only through the same displayed basis and source filter.
\item $L$ is the $\CauchyFilterLimitUp$ boundary row that records the limit-facing handoff without selecting a limit.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row opened by the regulated read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback row attached to the same windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and modulus ledger controlling the requested precision.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $B,F,Q,L,W,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for basis, source filter, completion row, limit row, windows, readback, tolerance, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,F,Q,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient filters, ultrafilters, selected limits, countable-choice schedules, host filter equality, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regulated Cauchy filter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regulated-cauchy-filter-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegulatedCauchyFilterUp$ carrier $G=(B,F,Q,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegulatedCauchyFilterUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite basis exposure, Cauchy-filter source admission, completion-row handoff, filter-limit boundary admission, finite stream-window inspection, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance regulation, terminal Real-seal ordering, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming for the rows $B,F,Q,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regulated-cauchy-filter-carrier}. The only available rows are $B,F,Q,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$, so carrier admission exposes finite basis, source filter, completion, limit boundary, window, readback, tolerance, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming data.

The regulated completion route begins with $B$ and $F$. The filter-limit basis and Cauchy-filter source force a completion-facing request to consume finite basis data before it can inspect $Q$ or $L$.

Next read the window and regularity rows $W,R,D$. The StreamName window is finite, the RegSeqRat row reads that window as regular rational data, and the DyadicRatCore row records the displayed tolerance budget for the requested precision.

Finally $E,H,C,P,N$ order and preserve the same route. Since the excluded quotient, selection, choice, host-equality, and ambient-completeness objects are absent from the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regulated completion route]
\label{thm:regulated-cauchy-filter-completion-route}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The consumer then opens the finite window $W$, reads it through $R$, and checks the requested precision through $D$. Only after those rows are displayed can the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ be consumed.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are confined to $H,C,P,N$. Hence every accepted consumer remains inside the displayed finite packet rather than entering host filter equality, quotient real equality, countable choice, selected limits, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
