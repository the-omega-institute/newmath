\begin{definition}[Cauchy uniform criterion carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-uniform-criterion-carrier}
A $\CauchyUniformCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ family of inspected tail windows.
\item $D$ is the shared $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by all displayed windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback obtained from those windows and tolerances.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal-facing row exposed only after $S,D,R,M,F$ have been read.
\item $M$ is the common modulus row naming the uniform threshold discipline.
\item $F$ is the finite family-index row recording which displayed windows are covered by the same threshold.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream, dyadic, regular-readback, real-seal, modulus, family, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a quotient Cauchy class, selected limit, global choice schedule, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy uniform criterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-uniform-criterion-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\CauchyUniformCriterionUp$ carrier $U=(S,D,R,E,M,F,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyUniformCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite window-family exposure, dyadic tolerance exactness, regular rational readback, real-seal non-escape, common-modulus visibility, family-index coverage, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming for the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-uniform-criterion-carrier}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The rows $S$ and $F$ expose the inspected family of windows, and the row $M$ supplies the common threshold discipline used by every displayed member of that family.

The dyadic ledger $D$ is then read before the regular readback row $R$. The real-seal-facing row $E$ is downstream of both the tolerance ledger and the regular readback, so it cannot be used as an independent limit selector.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no further coordinate, the NameCert surface is exhausted by the listed rows and cannot export quotient streams, selected limits, choice, host real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy uniform criterion Real-completion handoff]
\label{thm:cauchy-uniform-criterion-real-completion-handoff}
Every Real-completion consumer of an accepted $\CauchyUniformCriterionUp$ carrier must read the finite route
$$
S,F,M,D,R \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the consumer receives a displayed family of StreamName windows, one common modulus, the shared dyadic tolerance ledger, the RegSeqRat readback, and only then the RealUp seal-facing row. The handoff exports no quotient Cauchy class, selected limit, host equality of real names, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier inventory of \autoref{def:cauchy-uniform-criterion-carrier}. The family row $F$ and common modulus row $M$ are visible before the tolerance ledger is consumed, so the route records uniformity as displayed finite data.

Next read $D$ and $R$. These rows are the only tolerance and regular-rational readback surfaces available to the consumer, and the seal-facing row $E$ appears only after them.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$. The same transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows preserve the finite route, preventing a consumer from replacing it by a quotient completion, selected endpoint, host equality theorem, choice schedule, or ambient completeness assertion.
\end{proof}
