Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\RealApartnessLocatorUp$ packet records the finite located-apartness locator surface needed when Real and Completion consumers compare two displayed real names without asking for trichotomy. It sits after the apartness witness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apartnessreal-namecert}, the located-real boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedreal-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a locator interface for positive apartness evidence; it does not assert quotient real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Real apartness locator carrier]
\label{def:real-apartness-locator-carrier}
A $\RealApartnessLocatorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(L,A,D,S,R,O,G,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedRealUp$ request row, exposing the two real-name sides and the located comparison request read by the consumer.
\item $A$ is the $\ApartnessRealUp$ witness row, carrying the positive apartness evidence made available to the locator.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row that fixes the positive dyadic gap scale used by the comparison.
\item $S$ is the paired $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row for the two real names.
\item $R$ is the paired $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row computed from the displayed windows and dyadic tolerance.
\item $O$ is the located-observation row that records which finite read of $L,A,D,S,R$ produces the positive locator answer.
\item $G$ is the positive gap row; it records the witnessed dyadic separation without turning the packet into an exhaustive order decision.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal-facing row reached only after $L,A,D,S,R,O,G$ are displayed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the located request, apartness witness, dyadic tolerance, stream windows, regular readback, locator observation, gap, seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a locator request through $L,A,D,S,R,O,G,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealApartnessLocatorUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\ApartnessRealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealApartnessLocatorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for total order trichotomy, quotient real equality, a selected representative of a real name, countable choice, an independent completion object, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real apartness locator NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-apartness-locator-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealApartnessLocatorUp$ carrier
$$
Q=(L,A,D,S,R,O,G,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RealApartnessLocatorUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, located-real request exposure, apartness-witness exposure, dyadic tolerance exactness, paired stream-window exposure, regular-rational readback, located-observation display, positive-gap display, Real-seal routing, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-apartness-locator-carrier}. The analytic rows are $L,A,D,S,R,O,G,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The located-real row $L$ supplies only the comparison request. The apartness row $A$ supplies positive evidence, while the dyadic row $D$ fixes the tolerance at which that evidence is read.

The stream windows $S$ and regular readback $R$ make the inspected comparison finite. The observation row $O$ and positive gap row $G$ then display the locator answer without adding a global order branch.

Finally $E,H,C,P,N$ route, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no excluded equality, representative choice, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy, independent completion object, or ambient-completeness coordinate appears in $Q$, the local certificate obligations are exhausted by the stated rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real apartness locator window exactness]
\label{thm:real-apartness-locator-window-exactness}
Every accepted $\RealApartnessLocatorUp$ locator answer is read through the displayed finite window route
$$
L,A,D,S,R,O,G .
$$
The $\StreamNameUp$ windows and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readbacks used by $O$ are exactly the rows recorded in the same carrier as the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and $\ApartnessRealUp$ witness. No consumer may replace them by a quotient real-name comparison, hidden representative, or external trichotomy test.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:real-apartness-locator-namecert-obligations}. They require the located request, apartness witness, dyadic tolerance, stream windows, regular readback, observation, and gap rows to be displayed in one carrier.

The observation row $O$ is downstream of $L,A,D,S,R$. Thus the window and readback consumed by the locator are the same $S$ and $R$ stored with the tolerance row $D$ and apartness row $A$.

The positive gap row $G$ records the finite result of that route. Because the carrier contains no separate equality or order oracle, a consumer cannot replace the route by quotient real comparison, a hidden representative, or trichotomy.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real apartness locator Real-seal handoff]
\label{thm:real-apartness-locator-real-seal-handoff}
Any $\RealUp$-facing or Completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\RealApartnessLocatorUp$ packet may read the seal-facing row $E$ only after the located request, apartness witness, dyadic tolerance, stream windows, regular readback, observation row, and positive gap row are visible. The handoff therefore factors through
$$
L,A,D,S,R,O,G,E,H,C,P,N
$$
before the consumer reaches the Real seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use window exactness, \autoref{thm:real-apartness-locator-window-exactness}. It places $L,A,D,S,R,O,G$ before any locator answer is public.

The seal-facing row $E$ is a downstream row of the same carrier. It is reached only after the located-apartness answer has been displayed, so the Real-facing read cannot skip the dyadic tolerance, window, readback, observation, or gap rows.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route. They do not create a selected representative or a completion endpoint, and hence the Real-seal handoff remains a finite NameCert route rather than an assertion of $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real apartness locator nonescape]
\label{thm:real-apartness-locator-nonescape}
No accepted $\RealApartnessLocatorUp$ packet exports trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, an independent completion object, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness. Every exported read remains confined to the displayed rows $L,A,D,S,R,O,G,E,H,C,P,N$ and to their $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ structure.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The only analytic rows are the located request, apartness witness, dyadic tolerance, stream windows, regular readback, observation, positive gap, and seal-facing row.

Apply the Real-seal handoff theorem. It shows that even Real-facing and Completion-facing consumers reach the endpoint only through the displayed locator route.

The remaining rows transport, replay, package, and name the same route. Since none of the refused objects is a carrier coordinate or a structural row, it cannot escape from an accepted packet.
\end{proof}
