\label{thm:riemann-sum-modulus-refinement-stability}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The comparison row $Q$ has no independent source of partition or tolerance data. It can compare Riemann sums only by replaying the same mesh, refinement, tagged-partition, and tolerance rows.

Finally transport and replay through $H,C,P,N$. Componentwise $\hsame$ preserves the controlling rows, $\Cont$ replays the same finite route, and provenance and local naming add no selected refinement family or limiting process. Hence both reads expose the same comparison boundary.
