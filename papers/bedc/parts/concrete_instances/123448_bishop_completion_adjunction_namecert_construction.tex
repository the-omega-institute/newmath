\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BishopCompletionAdjunctionUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive completion and Mac Lane on adjunctions, the $\BishopCompletionAdjunctionUp$ packet records the L10 real-and-completion phase in which the Bishop reflector route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-completion-reflector-namecert}, the Cauchy adjunction route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-adjunction-namecert}, and the universal-arrow route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-universal-arrow-namecert} are read through one finite NameCert carrier. The source and seal rows are the naming spine of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a displayed adjunction surface for Bishop completion; it does not identify quotient completions, select limits, invoke countable choice, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[BishopCompletionAdjunction carrier]
\label{def:bishop-completion-adjunction-carrier}
A $\BishopCompletionAdjunctionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(B,C,U,E,Q,D,S,R,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BishopCompletionReflectorUp$ row, exposing the regular Cauchy input, separated identity, completion seal, reflector arrow, and local reflector ledger before any adjunction consumer reads the packet.
\item $C$ is the $\CauchyCompletionAdjunctionUp$ row, recording the source, dense embedding, completion seal, extension candidate, uniqueness ledger, and universal-mapping handoff.
\item $U$ is the $\CauchyCompletionUniversalArrowUp$ row, naming the unit and extension route that carries source-side reads into completion-side consumers.
\item $E$ is the displayed extension row, read only after $B,C,U$ have been exposed.
\item $Q$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row for source observations and extension tests.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and threshold readback row.
\item $S$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source and regular-rational handoff row.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached after the stream, dyadic, and regular-rational rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $B,C,U,E,Q,D,S,R,K,P,N$.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopCompletionAdjunctionUp$, $\BishopCompletionReflectorUp$, $\CauchyCompletionAdjunctionUp$, $\CauchyCompletionUniversalArrowUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCompletionAdjunctionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no quotient-completion equality row, no selected limit representative, no countable-choice schedule, no host function extensionality witness, and no ambient completeness field for $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BishopCompletionAdjunction NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-completion-adjunction-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-completion-adjunction-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCompletionAdjunctionUp}$ surface consists exactly of five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission, requiring an accepted object to expose only the packet $A=(B,C,U,E,Q,D,S,R,H,K,P,N)$;
\item reflector-adjunction alignment, requiring the Bishop reflector row $B$ and the Cauchy adjunction row $C$ to be read before the universal-arrow row $U$;
\item unit and extension order, requiring the displayed extension row $E$ to be consumed only after the unit/universal route in $U$ has been displayed;
\item naming-spine readback, requiring every source and seal read to pass through $Q,D,S,R$ over $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$;
\item structural stability, requiring all accepted comparisons to use the componentwise $\hsame$ row $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay row $K$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$.
\end{enumerate}
These rows supply no quotient-completion equality, selected limit, countable-choice selector, host function equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:bishop-completion-adjunction-carrier}. The finite packet exposes $B,C,U,E,Q,D,S,R,H,K,P,N$ and no other coordinate.

The reflector-adjunction alignment reads $B$ and $C$ before $U$. The row $B$ supplies the Bishop reflector surface, while $C$ supplies the Cauchy adjunction surface; the universal-arrow row can only compare source-side and completion-side reads after both rows are visible.

The unit and extension order is then read from $U$ and $E$. A unit-side source read is admitted through $U$, and the extension row $E$ is consumed only as the displayed completion-side continuation of that route.

The naming-spine readback uses $Q,D,S,R$. Stream windows, dyadic tolerances, regular rational handoff, and the terminal real seal are the only source and seal rows in the carrier.

Finally, structural stability is confined to $H,K,P,N$. Since those rows only transport, replay, package, and locally name the displayed packet, they cannot create quotient completions, selected limits, choice schedules, host function equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BishopCompletionAdjunction universal property]
\label{thm:bishop-completion-adjunction-universal-property}
Every accepted $\BishopCompletionAdjunctionUp$ packet reads the unit, extension, and uniqueness route from the finite carrier
$$
A=(B,C,U,E,Q,D,S,R,H,K,P,N).
$$
The unit side is the $B,C,U$ route through $\BishopCompletionReflectorUp$, $\CauchyCompletionAdjunctionUp$, and $\CauchyCompletionUniversalArrowUp$; the extension side is the $E,Q,D,S,R$ route through the displayed stream, dyadic, regular-rational, and real-seal rows; and uniqueness is comparison through $H,K,P,N$. This proves only the displayed finite universal-property route. It does not assert quotient completion equality, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the unit side. The carrier exposes $B$ as the Bishop reflector row, $C$ as the Cauchy adjunction row, and $U$ as the universal-arrow row, so a source-side consumer has to read those rows in that order before it can ask for an extension.

Next read the extension side. The extension row $E$ is followed by the finite naming spine $Q,D,S,R$, which records stream windows, dyadic tolerance readback, regular rational handoff, and the terminal real seal.

For uniqueness, compare two accepted extension reads only through the structural rows. The componentwise transport row $H$, continuation replay row $K$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$ preserve the same displayed unit and extension data.

The claimed universal property is therefore the finite route read from the carrier itself. Because no carrier row stores quotient equality, a selected limit, a choice schedule, or ambient Real completeness, none of those principles is asserted by the theorem.
\end{proof}
