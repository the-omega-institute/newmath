Following Hardy--Wright on finite continued fractions and rational convergents, the $\FiniteContinuedFractionUp$ packet records the finite-prefix object that sits between rational tree navigation and L10 rational readback. It is a bounded partial-quotient carrier whose endpoint can be consumed as a displayed $\RatUp$ row before any infinite continued-fraction tail, quotient real equality, or choice-selected limit is present. The packet is positioned beside the rational arithmetic carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the Stern--Brocot tree of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stern-brocot-namecert}, the Farey-neighbor route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-farey-sequence-namecert}, the finite-prefix continued-fraction surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuedfraction-namecert}, the convergent ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continued-fraction-convergents-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[FiniteContinuedFraction carrier]
\label{def:finite-continued-fraction-carrier}
A $\FiniteContinuedFractionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(A,N,D,Q,S,B,R,E,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the finite partial-quotient spine, including its length row and the displayed prefix order.
\item $N$ and $D$ are the $\RatUp$ numerator and denominator recurrence rows for the last displayed convergent.
\item $Q$ is the normalized $\RatUp$ endpoint read from $N$ and $D$.
\item $S$ is the sibling route row tying the prefix to $\SternBrocotUp$, $\FareySequenceUp$, $\ContinuedFractionUp$, and $\ContinuedFractionConvergentsUp$.
\item $B$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budget row used when the endpoint is exposed to a regular rational consumer.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational-readback row built from the finite endpoint and dyadic budget.
\item $E$ is the optional $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $R$ has been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for partial quotients, rational endpoints, sibling routes, dyadic budgets, readback rows, the seal row, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite-prefix request through $A,N,D,Q,S,B,R,E,H,P,L$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance row over $\FiniteContinuedFractionUp$ and all listed sibling carriers, and $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteContinuedFractionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these finite rows. It has no coordinate for an infinite tail, quotient equality of real names, a selected limit, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FiniteContinuedFraction NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-continued-fraction-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteContinuedFractionUp$ carrier $F=(A,N,D,Q,S,B,R,E,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteContinuedFractionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following ten obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed finite partial-quotient spine and its length row;
\item recurrence admission for the numerator and denominator rows $N,D$;
\item rational endpoint normalization through the $\RatUp$ row $Q$;
\item sibling compatibility with $\SternBrocotUp$, $\FareySequenceUp$, $\ContinuedFractionUp$, and $\ContinuedFractionConvergentsUp$ through $S$;
\item dyadic budget exposure through the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $B$;
\item rational-readback exposure through the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$;
\item terminal real-seal discipline for the optional $\RealUp$ row $E$;
\item componentwise transport through $H$;
\item displayed replay and package provenance through $C$ and $P$;
\item local naming through $L$, with no extra obligation outside the displayed packet.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier and recurrence admission are projections from \autoref{def:finite-continued-fraction-carrier}. The prefix row $A$ fixes the finite list being inspected, while $N$ and $D$ record the last convergent by displayed recurrence data.

The rational endpoint is read through $Q$. Since $Q$ is a $\RatUp$ row attached to the displayed numerator and denominator rows, rational readback is available before any infinite continued-fraction object is mentioned.

Sibling compatibility is carried by $S$. The Stern--Brocot and Farey rows supply finite rational navigation, while the continued-fraction and convergent rows supply the prefix and recurrence interpretation of the same endpoint.

The L10 readback route is then forced through $B,R,E$. The dyadic budget row precedes the regular rational row, and the optional real seal is terminal, so the certificate exports no quotient real equality, selected limit, or choice principle.

Finally, $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, package, and name the same finite packet. No additional coordinate remains from which another local obligation could be read.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FiniteContinuedFraction rational readback]
\label{thm:finite-continued-fraction-rational-readback}
Every accepted $\FiniteContinuedFractionUp$ packet exposes its rational endpoint through the finite route
$$
A,N,D \longmapsto Q \longmapsto B \longmapsto R \longmapsto E .
$$
Thus an L10 consumer may read the endpoint as $\RatUp$ data and, when requested, as a $\RegSeqRatUp$ row with a terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The consumer receives no infinite continued fraction, no quotient real equality, no countable-choice selector, and no ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier projection. The only rational endpoint coordinate is $Q$, and it is downstream of the finite prefix and recurrence rows $A,N,D$.

The dyadic and regular rows are ordered after the endpoint. The row $B$ supplies the finite tolerance budget, and $R$ is the regular rational readback that consumes that budget together with $Q$.

The real-facing row $E$ is terminal. Because the carrier has no coordinate for an infinite tail or a completed quotient real, every downstream read remains inside the displayed finite route.
\end{proof}
