It sits between the reflector packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionreflector-namecert}, the universal mapping surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-adjunction-namecert}, the regular-Cauchy reflector row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-completion-reflector-namecert}, and the Real completion adjunction packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-completion-adjunction-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Cauchy completion reflector unit carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-completion-reflector-unit-carrier}
A $\CauchyCompletionReflectorUnitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(X,R,J,D,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source metric and Cauchy-observation row, including the finite source histories consumed before a completion request is public.
\item $R$ is the $\CauchyCompletionReflectorUp$ or $\RegularCauchyCompletionReflectorUp$ row that exposes the target completion-reflector surface over the same source histories.
\item $J$ is the unit embedding row from the source row into the reflector row, read as displayed finite data rather than as a quotient-class injection.
\item $D$ is the dense unit-image row, requiring reflector-facing reads to expose the source image before universal mapping or Real-completion consumers can use the completed surface.
\item $T$ is the triangle replay row, tying the unit read to the adjunction and Real-completion rows named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-adjunction-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-completion-adjunction-namecert}.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$$
X\longmapsto J\longmapsto R\longmapsto D,T\longmapsto H,P,N .

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyCompletionReflectorUnitUp$, $\CauchyCompletionReflectorUp$, $\RegularCauchyCompletionReflectorUp$, $\CauchyCompletionAdjunctionUp$, $\RealCompletionAdjunctionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,R,J,D,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, host equality of completed objects, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyCompletionReflectorUnit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-reflector-unit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompletionReflectorUnitUp$ carrier $U=(X,R,J,D,T,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionReflectorUnitUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the source row, reflector row, unit embedding, dense unit image, triangle replay, componentwise transport, displayed continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.

Every reflector-unit consumer must read $X,J,R,D,T,H,C,P,N$ in the displayed order before it exports a completion-facing handoff. The packet exports no quotient completion, selected representative, countable choice, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-reflector-unit-carrier}. The tuple exposes the source row $X$, reflector row $R$, unit row $J$, dense-image row $D$, triangle replay row $T$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

The unit embedding is read before the reflector target is used. A completion-facing consumer therefore obtains source access through $J$, then reads the reflector row $R$, and only after that exposes the dense unit-image row $D$.

The triangle row is a replay row. It refers back to the displayed adjunction and Real-completion adjunction packets through $P$, while $H$ and $C$ transport and replay only the same finite coordinates. Since no refused host-side principle appears as a row of the carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
