\begin{theorem}[DyadicIntervalCover standard bridge premise]
\label{thm:dyadicintervalcover-standard-bridge-premise}
Every standard bridge read of an accepted $\DyadicIntervalCoverUp$ packet must expose the lower endpoint rows, upper endpoint rows, mesh row, radius ledger, finite membership row, $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal, $\BishopIntervalCompactUp$ handoff, $\UniformModulusUp$ lower-bound route, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row before any interval-cover interpretation is public. The bridge premise exports no open-cover compactness theorem, selected subcover, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.DyadicIntervalCoverUp.DyadicIntervalCoverStandardBridgePremise}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier $I=(L,U,M,R,V,W,Q,A,H,C,P,N)$ from \autoref{def:dyadic-interval-cover-carrier}. The rows $L,U$ are the lower and upper endpoint rows, $M$ is the mesh row, $R$ is the radius ledger, and $V$ is the finite cover-membership row.

Apply \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-cover-bridge-finite-route}. It forces every bridge consumer through the endpoint, mesh, radius, membership, window, readback, seal, interval-compact handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows before a standard interval-cover interpretation can be read.

Next use \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-cover-uniform-modulus-bridge-consumer}. The $\UniformModulusUp$-facing lower-bound route is therefore a consumer of the same displayed finite rows, not a detached open-cover or selected-subcover principle.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-cover-bridge-refinement-boundary}. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay can refine only the listed rows, while $P,N$ preserve provenance and local naming. Since the carrier has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the standard bridge premise cannot export any of those objects.
\end{proof}
