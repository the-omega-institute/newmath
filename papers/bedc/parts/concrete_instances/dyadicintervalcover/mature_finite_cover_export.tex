\begin{theorem}[DyadicIntervalCover mature finite-cover export]
\label{thm:dyadic-interval-cover-mature-finite-cover-export}
Every mature $\DyadicIntervalCoverUp$ consumer that exports finite-cover data must first factor through the displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint rows, $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal, $\BishopIntervalCompactUp$ finite-cover handoff, $\UniformModulusUp$ lower-bound route, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows. The mature export is public only after those rows preserve the finite cover route isolated by the bridge-facing children; it does not export open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient real equality, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, host compactness, or a hidden completion coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.DyadicIntervalCoverUp.DyadicIntervalCoverMatureFiniteCoverExport}
\begin{proof}
Start with the public finite-cover export of \autoref{thm:dyadicintervalcover-public-finite-cover-export}. It exposes the accepted packet only through dyadic endpoints, mesh and radius data, finite cover-membership rows, stream windows, regular-rational readback, a Real seal, the Bishop interval compact handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The regular Cauchy bridge window and bridge export children then bind the Real and Completion handoff. Apply \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-cover-regular-cauchy-window} to force regular-name reads through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BishopIntervalCompactUp$, and $\UniformModulusUp$ rows, and apply \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-cover-bridge-export} to project the same finite route as the exported bridge surface.

Finally compare with the bridge-facing public handoff of \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-cover-bridge-handoff}. It preserves exactly the finite public-cover route and explicitly leaves bridge checking and stronger compactness or completion principles outside the packet. Therefore a mature finite-cover export has to be the displayed route above, with open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient real equality, countable choice, ambient Real completeness, host compactness, and hidden completion data refused.
\end{proof}
