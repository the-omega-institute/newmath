\begin{theorem}[CauchyProductRegSeq product projection crosslink]
\label{thm:cauchy-product-regseq-product-projection-crosslink}
Every downstream projection route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-product-projection-namecert} must first read the shared $\CauchyProductRegSeqUp$ product-regularity packet
$$

Consequently, the projection route adds no quotient streams, selected product limits, host multiplication, countable-choice selector, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle to the product-regularity source.

Thus every projection consumer factors through the shared product-regularity packet before selecting the left or right readback route. Since neither carrier contains quotient streams, selected product limits, host multiplication, countable choice, or an ambient $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate, the crosslink exports none of that data.
\end{proof}
