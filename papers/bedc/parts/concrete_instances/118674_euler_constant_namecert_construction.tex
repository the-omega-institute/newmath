Following Euler and Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis, the $\EulerConstantUp$ packet records the Euler--Mascheroni constant as a finite harmonic-log comparison route rather than as a host real constant. It sits after the harmonic-window divergence surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-oresme-harmonic-divergence-namecert}, the logarithm comparison packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-logarithm-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the finite comparison data for $H_n-\log(n)$ windows; it is not a host limit, quotient real equality, selected convergence oracle, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Euler constant carrier]
\label{def:euler-constant-carrier}
An $\EulerConstantUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(H,L,D,R,E,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $H$ is the harmonic partial-sum window ledger. It records the inspected finite indices, the displayed partial sums $1+\cdots+1/n$, and the Oresme-facing comparison evidence inherited from $\OresmeHarmonicDivergenceUp$.
\item $L$ is the $\RealLogarithmUp$ comparison row for the same displayed indices, read as $\log(n)$ only after the harmonic window has been fixed.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row. It records the positive dyadic budget used to compare the displayed harmonic-log difference windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational windows obtained from $H,L,D$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row naming the Euler-constant candidate after the harmonic, logarithm, dyadic, and regular rational rows have been displayed.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the harmonic windows, logarithm comparisons, dyadic budgets, readback rows, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
H,L,D,R \longmapsto E,T,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EulerConstantUp$, $\OresmeHarmonicDivergenceUp$, $\RealLogarithmUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EulerConstantUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $H,L,D,R,E,T,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an ambient real limit, a quotient equality of real names, countable choice, a selected convergence oracle, or host-side logarithm completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Euler constant NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:euler-constant-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EulerConstantUp$ carrier $G=(H,L,D,R,E,T,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\EulerConstantUp}$ surface consists exactly of harmonic partial-sum windows through $H$, $\RealLogarithmUp$ comparisons through $L$, positive dyadic comparison budgets through $D$, regular rational readback through $R$, the terminal $\RealUp$ seal through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $T$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. Its coordinates are precisely the harmonic window, logarithm comparison, dyadic tolerance, regular readback, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The comparison route starts with finite harmonic data. The row $H$ exposes only inspected partial sums and the Oresme-facing harmonic comparison surface; it does not provide a completed infinite series.

The logarithm and tolerance rows are then read against the same windows. The row $L$ supplies the $\RealLogarithmUp$ comparison for the displayed index, and $D$ gives the dyadic budget before $R$ can read the harmonic-log difference as regular rational data.

Finally $E,T,C,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier stores no ambient limit selector, quotient real equality, countable-choice schedule, or host completeness theorem, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Euler constant regular Cauchy handoff]
\label{thm:euler-constant-regular-cauchy-handoff}
Every Real-and-Completion consumer of an accepted $\EulerConstantUp$ carrier reads the Euler-constant candidate only through the finite route
$$
\OresmeHarmonicDivergenceUp,\ \RealLogarithmUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp .
$$
In particular, the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is available only after the harmonic window $H$, logarithm comparison $L$, dyadic tolerance row $D$, and regular rational readback row $R$ have been displayed through $T,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted carrier $G=(H,L,D,R,E,T,C,P,N)$. A consumer can read the real seal only at the row $E$.

The row $E$ is downstream of the finite harmonic-log comparison route. The consumer must first inspect the harmonic window $H$, compare the same index through $L$, attach a positive dyadic budget through $D$, and read the resulting difference through $R$.

Transport and replay preserve this order. The rows $T$ and $C$ identify and replay the displayed route, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming. Hence the handoff reaches $\RealUp$ through regular Cauchy readback data rather than through an ambient completeness theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Euler constant nonescape]
\label{thm:euler-constant-nonescape}
An accepted $\EulerConstantUp$ packet cannot export a host-level Euler--Mascheroni constant, quotient equality of real names, countable choice, a selected convergence oracle, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness. Any consumer claiming such data must supply it from a different certificate, because the local carrier stores only $H,L,D,R,E,T,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the carrier coordinates. The source data are finite harmonic windows, logarithm comparisons, dyadic budgets, and regular rational readbacks; the target data are a displayed real seal together with transport, replay, provenance, and naming.

None of these rows is a host real constant, quotient representative, choice selector, convergence oracle, or ambient completeness theorem. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay preserve the listed rows rather than adding new host-side objects.

Therefore a consumer can obtain such data only by citing an additional certificate. The local $\EulerConstantUp$ NameCert itself exports exactly the displayed harmonic-log route.
\end{proof}
