\begin{definition}[Hyperbolic sector-expansion carrier]
\label{def:hyperbolic-sector-expansion-carrier}
A $\HyperbolicSectorExpansionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(E,A,B,R,O,G,W,T,F,K,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the $\HyperbolicSectorEnvelopeUp$ source row carrying the finite
sector endpoints, visual-radius row, observer-window row, and refusal row.
\item $A$ is the sector-aperture row, naming the visible opening budget of the
sector in the observer window.
\item $B$ is the boundary-shadow row tying the aperture to a displayed
$\HyperbolicBoundaryShadowUp$ readback.
\item $R$ is the visual-radius comparison row inherited from the envelope and
read again at the expanded sector boundary.
\item $O$ is the observer-window row selecting the finite focus in which the
expansion is measured.
\item $G$ is the expansion-comparison row, recording the before-window and
after-window sector readings and their classifier comparison.
\item $W$ is the visual-boundary handoff row through
$\HyperbolicVisualBoundaryUp$.
\item $T$ is the transport row preserving aperture, boundary shadow, visual
radius, observer window, and expansion comparison under componentwise
classifier transport.
\item $F$ is the refusal row excluding a completed hyperbolic boundary, hidden

\item $K$ is the TasteGate request row requiring $\Nontrivial\
\HyperbolicSectorExpansionUp$ and $\FieldFaithful\

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for
$E,A,B,R,O,G,W,T,F,K,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through sector envelope, aperture,
boundary shadow, visual radius, observer window, expansion comparison,
transport, refusal, TasteGate requests, and naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HyperbolicSectorExpansionUp$,
$\HyperbolicSectorEnvelopeUp$, $\HyperbolicObserverExpansionUp$,
$\HyperbolicVisualBoundaryUp$, $\HyperbolicBoundaryShadowUp$,
$\BoundaryShadowExpansionCocycleUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HyperbolicSectorExpansionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for ambient boundary compactification, selected
geodesic representatives, host hyperbolic metric equality, a completed sector

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hyperbolic sector-expansion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hyperbolic-sector-expansion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HyperbolicSectorExpansionUp$ carrier
$X=(E,A,B,R,O,G,W,T,F,K,H,C,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\HyperbolicSectorExpansionUp}$ surface consists exactly of sector
envelope exposure through $E$, sector aperture through $A$, boundary shadow
through $B$, visual-radius comparison through $R$, observer window through $O$,
expansion comparison through $G$, visual-boundary handoff through $W$,
transport through $T$, refusal through $F$, TasteGate requests through $K$,
componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

No accepted read may expose an expansion comparison without displaying the
aperture row, boundary-shadow row, visual-radius row, observer-window row,
refusal row, and provenance row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:hyperbolic-sector-expansion-carrier}. The envelope row $E$ exposes
the endpoint, radius, observer-window, and refusal data on which any sector
expansion must depend.

The aperture, boundary-shadow, visual-radius, and observer-window rows
$A,B,R,O$ then delimit the measured expansion. The comparison row $G$ can be
read only after these coordinates are visible, and the visual-boundary handoff
$W$ remains downstream of the finite boundary-shadow surface.

Transport, refusal, TasteGate requests, componentwise history equality, replay,
provenance, and naming are then projected from $T,F,K,H,C,P,N$. Since no hidden
geodesic sector, completed boundary, host metric equality, or global observer
map appears among the coordinates, the listed rows exhaust the local
certificate obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hyperbolic sector-expansion observer route]
\label{thm:hyperbolic-sector-expansion-observer-route}

$$

\longmapsto (A,B,R,O)
\longmapsto (G,W,T,F)
\longmapsto (K,H,C,P,N).
$$
The route exposes finite sector aperture, boundary-shadow readback,
visual-radius comparison, observer-window focus, expansion comparison,
visual-boundary handoff, transport, refusal, TasteGate requests, replay,
provenance, and local NameCert rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the sector-envelope row. It provides the finite endpoint and
observer-window context, so the expansion packet does not begin from a completed
boundary sector.

Read the aperture, boundary-shadow, visual-radius, and observer-window rows

displayed.

The expansion comparison, visual-boundary handoff, and transport rows then
record the only admitted observer route. Refusal prevents the route from being
promoted to host metric equality, hidden geodesic selection, or a global
observer dynamics.

Finally the TasteGate request, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and
local naming rows keep the same finite packet available to downstream
observer-expansion chapters.
\end{proof}
