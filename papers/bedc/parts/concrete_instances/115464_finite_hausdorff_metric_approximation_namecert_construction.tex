\begin{definition}[Finite Hausdorff metric approximation carrier]
\label{def:finite-hausdorff-metric-approximation-carrier}
A $\FiniteHausdorffMetricApproximationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(X,K,\varepsilon,E_A,E_B,D,H,R,S,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the shared $\MetricSpaceUp$ row, carrying the point classifier and distance rows used by the approximation.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, including the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite-net field read at the displayed tolerance.
\item $\varepsilon$ is the positive tolerance row, read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and, when a Real-facing bound is requested, through the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ seal route.
\item $E_A$ and $E_B$ are two finite $\ProbeBundle$ net rows for the compact names being compared, including center membership and coverage witnesses exposed by the finite-net handoff.
\item $D$ is the directed Hausdorff comparison ledger: every center of $E_A$ has a displayed distance row to an admitted center of $E_B$, and every center of $E_B$ has a displayed distance row to an admitted center of $E_A$, all bounded by the same tolerance budget.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, compact-metric row, tolerance row, the two finite nets, directed comparison ledger, readback row, seal row, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for any rational or dyadic Hausdorff bound exposed to a completion-facing consumer.
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the finite nets, directed distance ledger, and regular-rational readback have been displayed.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
X,K,\varepsilon,E_A,E_B \longmapsto D \longmapsto R \longmapsto S .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteHausdorffMetricApproximationUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\HausdorffMetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteHausdorffMetricApproximationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an ambient finite subset, selected dense set, open cover, selected subcover, quotient completion, countable-choice schedule, host hyperspace equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite Hausdorff metric approximation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-hausdorff-metric-approximation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteHausdorffMetricApproximationUp$ carrier $F=(X,K,\varepsilon,E_A,E_B,D,H,R,S,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteHausdorffMetricApproximationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed metric source, compact-metric row, tolerance, two finite nets, directed Hausdorff comparison ledger, componentwise transport, regular-rational readback, terminal Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Every approximation read must expose $E_A,E_B,D$ before $R$ or $S$ is consumed, and the ledger exports no open-cover compactness, selected subcover, quotient completion, countable choice, selected limit, host hyperspace equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:finite-hausdorff-metric-approximation-carrier}. The metric and compact rows are $X,K$, the tolerance row is $\varepsilon$, and the finite approximation data are exactly the two net rows $E_A,E_B$ together with the directed comparison ledger $D$.

The finite-net rows are prerequisite data. A center can be used in the directed Hausdorff ledger only after it is visible in the relevant $\ProbeBundle$ and admitted through the compact-metric and total-bounded source rows.

The directed comparison ledger is then forced by $D$. It records both directions of the finite Hausdorff estimate under the same tolerance budget, so a consumer cannot replace the row by an open cover, a selected dense subset, or a host hyperspace construction.

The readback and seal rows are downstream. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ and terminal $\RealUp$ seal $S$ expose only the finite bound already produced by $E_A,E_B,D$, while $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-net Hausdorff handoff]
\label{thm:finite-hausdorff-metric-approximation-finite-net-handoff}
Every compact-metric, Hausdorff-metric, or completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\FiniteHausdorffMetricApproximationUp$ packet reads the finite route
$$

It receives no open-cover compactness theorem, selected subcover, quotient completion, selected limit, countable choice, host hyperspace equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Apply the directed ledger $D$. It supplies the two finite Hausdorff comparison directions before any rational or Real-facing bound can be exported, so the handoff remains a finite-net route rather than an open-cover or host-hyperspace argument.

Finally read $R$ and $S$. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback packages the bound for completion-facing consumers, and the $\RealUp$ seal is reached only after the finite nets and dyadic comparison ledger have been displayed.
