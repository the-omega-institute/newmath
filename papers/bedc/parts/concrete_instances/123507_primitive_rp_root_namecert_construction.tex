\begin{definition}[Primitive RP root carrier]
\label{def:primitive-rp-root-carrier}

$$

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $N$ is the odd-length reflection-centered dilation parameter row.
\item $B$ is the finite signed-block or neutral-window row whose primitive root is inspected.
\item $A$ is the finite address row, storing the visible reflection-centered site addresses and their scale-order comparisons.
\item $S$ is the scale-order row, recording the minimum site or block order read by the primitive-root request.
\item $U$ is the primitive-root row, displaying the inverse-dilated block or window after the scale-order row has been read.
\item $M$ is the RP-matrix retention row, stating that the matrix read is inherited from the primitive root through the finite self-similarity route of \autoref{thm:innovation-input-rp-matrix-depends-on-primitive-root}.
\item $F$ is the pruning classifier row, admitting primitive windows as active tests and sending nonprimitive windows to their displayed root.

\end{enumerate}
The carrier compares accepted packets only through $N,B,A,S,U,M,F,K,H,C,P,L$. It contains no coordinate for an infinite solenoid point, quotient class of RP windows, hidden primitive oracle, ambient spectral theorem, or global classification of all RP constraints.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Primitive RP root NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:primitive-rp-root-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PrimitiveRPRootUp$ carrier
$$
R=(N,B,A,S,U,M,F,K,H,C,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PrimitiveRPRootUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, scale-order exactness, primitive-root display, RP-matrix retention, classifier stability, and ledger non-escape.

Carrier admission displays the finite odd parameter, block or window, address row, scale-order row, root row, matrix-retention row, pruning classifier, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Scale-order exactness forces every root read through $A,S$ before $U$ is available. Classifier stability and ledger non-escape keep all accepted reads inside $K,H,C,P,L$ and exclude hidden addresses, quotient windows, infinite solenoids, external RP oracles, and ambient spectral classification.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:primitive-rp-root-carrier}. The finite request displays the odd parameter $N$, the block or window $B$, the address row $A$, and the scale-order row $S$ before any primitive root is available.

The primitive-root row $U$ is downstream of that displayed scale-order read. Matrix and spectrum retention are not independent coordinates; they are read through $M$ by citing the primitive-window route of \autoref{thm:innovation-input-rp-matrix-depends-on-primitive-root}.

Finally use $F,K,H,C,P,L$. The pruning classifier sends nonprimitive windows to $U$, and the structural rows transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no infinite address, quotient-window, hidden-oracle, or global spectral coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
