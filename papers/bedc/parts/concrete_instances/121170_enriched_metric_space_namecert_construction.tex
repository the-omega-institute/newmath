\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\EnrichedMetricSpaceUp$}

Following Lawvere enriched metric spaces and the Isbell--Yoneda completion tradition, the $\EnrichedMetricSpaceUp$ packet records the distance-as-hom profile that L10 completion routes need before a $\LawvereMetricUp$ or $\YonedaCompletionUp$ consumer reads completion-facing data. It sits over the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the formal-ball surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-formalball-namecert}, the dyadic and regular rational readbacks of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the directed-distance packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lawvere-metric-namecert}, and the Yoneda-profile route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-yoneda-completion-namecert}. The packet names only the finite enriched-distance profile shared by those readers; it does not construct a quotient completion, select colimits, import host enriched categories, or assume ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[EnrichedMetricSpace carrier]
\label{def:enriched-metric-space-carrier}
An $\EnrichedMetricSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(X,D,U,T,B,Y,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
whose rows have the following public roles:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the displayed source-object row, read through the ordinary $\MetricUp$ sibling when a symmetric metric source is present.
\item $D$ is the enriched distance or hom-profile row, exposing directed values before any $\LawvereMetricUp$ or $\LawvereMetricCompletionUp$ consumer reads them.
\item $U$ is the unit row, certifying the displayed self-distance or identity-hom value.
\item $T$ is the triangle or composition row, certifying the finite comparison that composes two displayed hom values into a third.
\item $B$ is the $\FormalBallUp$ probe row, carrying radius-indexed observations of the same profile.
\item $Y$ is the $\YonedaCompletionUp$ profile row, recording the finite presheaf-style read seen by completion consumers.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ seal row, with $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budgets attached before any terminal distance read.
\item $H$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,D,U,T,B,Y,R$.
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay row for the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EnrichedMetricSpaceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\LawvereMetricUp$, $\FormalBallUp$, $\YonedaCompletionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EnrichedMetricSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a quotient completion, selected colimit, host enriched category, ambient real-completeness theorem, choice functional, Lean proof credential, or standard bridge.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[EnrichedMetricSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:enriched-metric-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EnrichedMetricSpaceUp$ carrier $E=(X,D,U,T,B,Y,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\EnrichedMetricSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of seven finite obligation rows: carrier admission for the displayed source and profile rows; unit exactness through $U$; triangle or composition exactness through $T$; $\FormalBallUp$ and $\YonedaCompletionUp$ handoff through $B,Y$; $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ nonescape through $R$ with $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budgets; classifier transport through $H,C$; and provenance exhaustion through $P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:enriched-metric-space-carrier}. The rows $X,D,U,T,B,Y,R,H,C,P,N$ are the entire public surface, so carrier admission is exactly admission of those displayed rows.

The unit and triangle obligations are read from $U$ and $T$. They attach identity and composition evidence to the same enriched distance row $D$, so a reader cannot replace them by a host enriched category or by an untracked order law.

The completion-facing handoff is the pair $B,Y$. The formal-ball probe row displays finite center-radius observations, and the Yoneda row reads the same enriched profile before any $\LawvereMetricCompletionUp$ route can inspect a completion seal.

Finally $R,H,C,P,N$ seal the route. The real-valued read passes through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budgets, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and $\RealUp$ sealing; transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same packet and add no quotient completion, selected colimit, choice principle, Lean credential, or bridge check.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[EnrichedMetricSpace Lawvere-Yoneda route]
\label{thm:enriched-metric-space-lawvere-yoneda-route}
Every $\LawvereMetricUp$ and $\YonedaCompletionUp$ consumer of an accepted $\EnrichedMetricSpaceUp$ carrier reads the same enriched distance profile through
$$
\EnrichedMetricSpaceUp \longrightarrow \FormalBallUp \longrightarrow \DyadicRatCoreUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \RealUp
$$
before any completion-facing seal is available. Thus $\LawvereMetricCompletionUp$ receives finite FormalBall, dyadic-budget, regular-rational, and real-seal data for the displayed profile, not a quotient completion, selected colimit, host enriched category, ambient completeness theorem, or choice-selected representative.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:enriched-metric-space-namecert-obligations}. They force the source row, enriched distance row, unit row, triangle row, formal-ball probe, Yoneda profile, real-seal row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming to remain rows of one packet.

A $\LawvereMetricUp$ consumer reads $D,U,T$ as the directed-distance profile and its unit-composition ledger. A $\YonedaCompletionUp$ consumer reads the same profile only after $B$ has exposed finite formal-ball probes.

The terminal read then passes through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ inside $R$. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion, selected colimits, host enriched categories, ambient completeness, or choice, both consumers receive exactly the displayed finite route.
\end{proof}
