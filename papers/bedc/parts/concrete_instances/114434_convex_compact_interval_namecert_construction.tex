Following Bishop and Bridges' constructive analysis of intervals, and the Bourbaki treatment of convex compact subsets of the real line, the $\ConvexCompactIntervalUp$ packet records the finite NameCert surface by which a closed bounded interval is read as a convex compact interval through located finite nets. It sits beside the closed interval carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedinterval-namecert}, the located finite-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-closed-interval-finite-net-namecert}, the finite $\epsilon$-net packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, the compact metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the stream-window interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. The packet names the constructive compact-interval route only: it does not assert an open-cover Heine--Borel theorem, ambient completeness of $\RealUp$, quotient equality of interval names, selected suprema, or countable choice.

\begin{definition}[Convex compact interval carrier]
\label{def:convex-compact-interval-carrier}
A $\ConvexCompactIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(I,L,F,M,R,E,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\ClosedBoundedIntervalUp$ source interval, including its endpoint ledger and closed containment classifier.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedClosedIntervalFiniteNetUp$ row that records located interval reads as finite observation windows.
\item $F$ is the $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ net row obtained from those located windows and the displayed tolerance ledger.
\item $M$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ consumer row, read as compact-metric finite-net data rather than as open-cover compactness.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row that repacks the finite interval observations as regular rational data.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the closed interval, located net, finite net, compact-metric row, and regular handoff have been displayed.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ request window through which endpoint, net, and tolerance reads are inspected.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $I,L,F,M,R,E,W$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
I,W \longmapsto L,F \longmapsto M,R \longmapsto E .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ConvexCompactIntervalUp$, $\ClosedBoundedIntervalUp$, $\LocatedClosedIntervalFiniteNetUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ConvexCompactIntervalUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. No coordinate stores an open-cover compactness proof, ambient real completeness, quotient equality of interval names, a selected supremum, or a choice-selected point.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Convex compact interval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:convex-compact-interval-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ConvexCompactIntervalUp$ carrier $K=(I,L,F,M,R,E,W,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ConvexCompactIntervalUp}$ surface is exhausted by the following finite rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the closed bounded interval, located finite-net row, finite $\epsilon$-net row, compact-metric row, regular rational handoff, Real seal, stream window, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item convex interval source compatibility, requiring all convexity and endpoint reads to be inherited from the displayed $\ClosedBoundedIntervalUp$ row $I$;
\item located-net visibility, requiring every compactness-facing request to pass through $W,L,F$ before the compact-metric row $M$ is available;
\item Real-seal stability, requiring $R$ and $E$ to be downstream from the same finite interval and net rows;
\item ledger non-escape, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to export no open-cover Heine--Borel principle, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, quotient equality of interval names, selected supremum, or countable-choice schedule.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside $I,L,F,M,R,E,W,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:convex-compact-interval-carrier}. The only analytic rows available are the closed bounded interval source, the located finite-net row, the finite net row, the compact-metric consumer row, the regular rational handoff, the Real seal, and the stream request window.

Convexity and endpoint discipline are inherited from $I$. The carrier has no second ambient real line or host interval equality, so a consumer cannot replace the closed interval source by an untracked convex set.

Located compactness is read through the finite route $W,L,F,M$. The request window exposes only finite endpoint, tolerance, and net observations before the compact-metric row is visible, so the route is finite-net compactness rather than open-cover compactness.

Finally, $R,E,H,C,P,N$ preserve and name that same route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for ambient Real completeness, quotient equality, selected suprema, or countable choice, the displayed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Convex compact interval located-net route]
\label{thm:convex-compact-interval-located-net-route}
Every $\CompactMetricUp$ or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\ConvexCompactIntervalUp$ packet receives the finite dependency route
$$
\ClosedBoundedIntervalUp,\ \LocatedClosedIntervalFiniteNetUp,\ \FiniteEpsilonNetUp,\ \CompactMetricUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \BHist,\ \hsame,\ \Cont,\ \Pkg,\ \NameCert .
$$
The route reads the closed interval source through located finite windows, repacks those windows as finite $\epsilon$-net and regular rational data, and reaches the terminal Real seal only after the compact-metric row is displayed. It exports no open-cover Heine--Borel theorem, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, quotient interval equality, selected supremum, or countable-choice data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier projection. The interval row $I$ supplies the closed bounded source, while $W$ gives the finite request window through which endpoints and tolerances are inspected.

Apply the located-net rows $L$ and $F$. These rows convert the displayed window into located finite-net data and then into the finite $\epsilon$-net surface consumed by $M$.

The regular and Real-facing rows are downstream. The handoff $R$ repacks the same finite observations as regular rational data, and the seal $E$ is available only after the preceding closed-interval, located-net, finite-net, and compact-metric rows have been read.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite route. Since no excluded compactness, completeness, quotient, supremum, or choice coordinate occurs in $K$, the located-net route is exactly the dependency surface stated above.
\end{proof}
