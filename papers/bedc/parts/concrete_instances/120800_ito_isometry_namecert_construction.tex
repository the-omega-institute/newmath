\begin{definition}[ItoIsometry carrier]
\label{def:ito-isometry-carrier}
An $\ItoIsometryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(\Omega,I,X,B,\Delta,V,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\Omega$ is the $\ProbSpaceUp$ source row on which the probability-facing readback is interpreted.
\item $I$ is the consumed $\ItoIntegralUp$ simple adapted-step integral packet.
\item $X$ is the finite simple adapted-process row, indexed by the same displayed partition and filtration windows as $I$.
\item $B$ is the $\BrownianUp$ finite-prefix source row exposing the Brownian cell-difference schedule.
\item $\Delta$ is the Brownian cell-difference row aligned with the simple-process cells.
\item $V$ is the quadratic-variation ledger for the displayed Brownian cell differences and step-process coefficients.
\item $L$ is the $L^2$ norm-comparison row, read through the $\HilbertUp$ and $\NormUp$ sibling surfaces, that compares the finite integral-sum norm ledger with $V$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, integral, simple-process, Brownian, cell-difference, quadratic-variation, and norm-comparison rows.
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay row for the same finite packet.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ItoIsometryUp$, $\ItoIntegralUp$, $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\BrownianUp$, $\MartingaleUp$, $\HilbertUp$, $\NormUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.

The carrier exports no selected refining partition, no almost-sure quotient, no completed $L^2$ space, no ambient stochastic-integral operator, and no measure-completion theorem.

\begin{theorem}[ItoIsometry NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:ito-isometry-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ItoIsometryUp$ carrier $Q=(\Omega,I,X,B,\Delta,V,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ItoIsometryUp}$ surface consists exactly of probability-source exposure through $\Omega$, simple-integral exposure through $I$, simple adapted-process exposure through $X$, Brownian source and cell-difference exposure through $B,\Delta$, quadratic-variation ledger exposure through $V$, finite $L^2$ norm-comparison exposure through $L$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:ito-isometry-carrier}. The source, integral, simple-process, and Brownian rows are displayed before any norm comparison can be read.

The quadratic-variation row $V$ and norm-comparison row $L$ are tied to the same finite partition and cell-difference schedule. Thus the certificate compares the visible simple integral sum with the visible quadratic-variation ledger, not with a selected completed stochastic integral.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that packet rowwise. Since the carrier has no coordinate for almost-sure quotienting, measure completion, selected refinement, or completed $L^2$ operator theory, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ItoIsometry finite L2 stability]
\label{thm:ito-isometry-l2-stability}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The martingale and Hilbert-facing dependencies are provenance rows, not hidden completions. They allow the finite norm comparison to be consumed through the displayed sibling surfaces while keeping the Brownian cell-difference and quadratic-variation ledgers visible.

Therefore a stability read that changes $X$, $\Delta$, $V$, or $L$ leaves the carrier. No completed $L^2$ space, selected limiting integral, or quotient process equality is available from transport, replay, provenance, or local naming.
