\begin{definition}[Dvoretsky finite-section carrier]
\label{def:dvoretsky-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the Banach and norm source row, read through the $\BanachSpaceUp$ and $\NormUp$ certificates.
\item $N$ is the finite-dimensional section row, naming the finite subspace, coordinate window, and section inclusion under the same norm source.
\item $S$ is the Hilbert comparison row, recording the finite Euclidean model and the comparison map to the selected section.
\item $D$ is the $\BanachMazurDistanceUp$ distortion ledger for the comparison between $N$ and $S$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Banach source, section row, Hilbert comparison, distortion ledger, provenance, and local name.

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BanachSpaceUp$, $\NormUp$, $\HilbertUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$, $\BanachMazurDistanceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

\begin{theorem}[Dvoretsky NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dvoretsky-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:dvoretsky-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\DvoretskyUp}$ surface consists exactly of Banach and norm source exposure through $B$, finite-dimensional section exposure through $N$, Hilbert comparison through $S$, Banach--Mazur distortion readback through $D$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $L$.

\begin{proof}
Project the finite packet $Q=(B,N,S,D,H,C,P,L)$. The first four rows display the mathematical content: source normed Banach data, the finite section, the Hilbert comparison row, and the distortion ledger.

Read the structural rows next. The transport row $H$ compares the displayed rows componentwise, while $C$ replays only the route from source to section, from section to Hilbert comparison, and from comparison to distortion ledger.

Finally inspect $P$ and $L$. They source and name the same finite packet, and neither supplies an additional compactness, basis-selection, quotient, choice, or global Euclideanization row. Hence the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.

\begin{theorem}[Dvoretsky finite-dimensional section handoff]
\label{thm:dvoretsky-finite-dimensional-section-handoff}

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:dvoretsky-namecert-obligations}. They place the distortion ledger $D$ after the section and comparison rows, and they keep $H,C,P,L$ as preservation, replay, provenance, and naming data for the same route.
