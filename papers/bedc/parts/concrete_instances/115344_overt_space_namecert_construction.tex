\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\OvertSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-overt-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Johnstone, the $\OvertSpaceUp$ packet records witnessable positive contact for a displayed constructive space as finite NameCert data. It sits between the cover/refinement presentation of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-formal-topology-namecert}, the point-free open rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locale-space-namecert}, the positive real-contact surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-overt-real-namecert}, and the compact positive-cover consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-compact-overt-namecert}. The packet supplies positivity data to located real and formal-topology consumers without importing host open-set primitives, powersets, selected points, excluded middle over opens, or choice.

\begin{definition}[Overt space carrier]
\label{def:overt-space-carrier}
An $\OvertSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(S,B,P,W,R,L,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the underlying space or formal presentation row whose positive observations are being named.
\item $B$ is the basic-neighbourhood row read from $\FormalTopologyUp$ or $\LocaleSpaceUp$, exposing only finite cover and refinement data.
\item $P$ is the positivity predicate row. It records witnessable contact with the displayed basic neighbourhood, not a host proposition over arbitrary opens.
\item $W$ is the finite witness row that certifies the positive contact claimed by $P$.
\item $R$ is the located-real specialization row through which $\OvertRealUp$ consumers read positive interval contact.
\item $L$ is the located compact handoff row through which $\LocatedCompactOvertUp$ consumers combine positive contact with finite cover data.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the space, basis, positivity, witness, specialization, compact handoff, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of a positive-contact request through $S,B,P,W,R,L,H,G,N$.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\OvertSpaceUp$, $\FormalTopologyUp$, $\LocaleSpaceUp$, $\OvertRealUp$, $\LocatedCompactOvertUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\OvertSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exposes no powerset of opens, no host open membership relation, no selected point of contact, no spatial quotient, no trichotomy over basic opens, and no choice principle for witnesses not already displayed in $W$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Overt space positivity stability]
\label{thm:overt-space-positivity-stability}
Every accepted $\OvertSpaceUp$ carrier preserves positive contact through the displayed $\hsame$ and $\Cont$ rows. Concretely, if a consumer reads a positive neighbourhood claim from $O=(S,B,P,W,R,L,H,C,G,N)$, then the claim must be replayed from the same basis row $B$, the same positivity row $P$, and the same finite witness row $W$ after transport. The replay may be consumed by formal-topology, locale, overt-real, or located-compact-overt siblings, but it cannot be replaced by a host open-set predicate, a selected point, a powerset operation, or a choice-selected witness.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Overt space located-real specialization]
\label{thm:overt-space-located-real-specialization}
Every located-real or located-compact consumer of an accepted $\OvertSpaceUp$ packet factors through
$$
\FormalTopologyUp,\ \LocaleSpaceUp,\ \OvertRealUp,\ \LocatedCompactOvertUp .
$$
The formal-topology and locale rows supply finite basic-neighbourhood and cover-refinement data, the $\OvertRealUp$ row specializes positive contact to located real intervals, and the $\LocatedCompactOvertUp$ row consumes the same witness data together with finite compact-cover rows. The route exports positivity evidence, not host opens, quotient spatial equality, trichotomy, selected points, or ambient real completeness.
\end{theorem}
