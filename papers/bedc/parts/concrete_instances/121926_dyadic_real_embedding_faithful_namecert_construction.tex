\begin{theorem}[Dyadic real embedding faithfulness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-real-embedding-faithful-namecert-obligations}

\item dyadic source exposure, requiring every consumer to read the displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger $D$ before it reaches the completion seal;
