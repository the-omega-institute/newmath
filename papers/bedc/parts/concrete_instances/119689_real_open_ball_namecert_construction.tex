\begin{definition}[Real open ball carrier]
\label{def:real-open-ball-carrier}
A $\RealOpenBallUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(c,r,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $c$ is the centre $\RealUp$ seal row, already exposed through finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback.
\item $r$ is the positive $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius row, with its positivity witness and tolerance ledger displayed before any neighbourhood membership read is consumed.
\item $W$ is the finite family of $\StreamNameUp$ windows opened from the centre and tested real names at the requested precision.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the windows in $W$.
\item $D$ is the dyadic comparison ledger for the distance-from-centre estimate, recording the finite rational inequality needed to certify membership inside the radius row $r$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the tested name after the windows, readback, and dyadic comparison have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for centre, radius, windows, readback, comparison, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a real-neighbourhood query through $c,r,W,R,D,E,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealOpenBallUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\OpenBallUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealOpenBallUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $c,r,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host topology, quotient real equality, selected limits, open-cover compactness, real-order trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real open ball NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-open-ball-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealOpenBallUp$ carrier $B=(c,r,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealOpenBallUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, positive dyadic radius admission, finite-window membership, classifier transport, Real-seal handoff, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming for the rows $c,r,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission projects the finite packet of \autoref{def:real-open-ball-carrier}. The only real source rows are the centre seal $c$, the terminal tested seal $E$, and the window-readback route $W,R$ between them.

The radius obligation is the positive dyadic row $r$. Its positivity and tolerance ledger are visible before the comparison row $D$ can certify that the tested name lies inside the displayed neighbourhood.

Finite-window membership is therefore exhausted by $W,R,D$. The StreamName windows expose the requested finite observations, the RegSeqRat row reads those observations back as regular rational data, and the DyadicRatCore comparison row records the strict radius test.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly the same route. Since no further coordinate is present, the carrier cannot export host topology, quotient real equality, selected limits, open-cover compactness, real-order trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real open ball dyadic radius stability]
\label{thm:real-open-ball-dyadic-radius-stability}
Let $B=(c,r,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RealOpenBallUp$ carrier. If $r'$ is a positive $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius row whose displayed tolerance ledger refines the ledger of $r$, and the same comparison row $D$ certifies the tested name inside $r'$, then the packet
$$
B'=(c,r',W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
uses the same finite route and remains a $\RealOpenBallUp$ carrier with the same centre, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier $B$. All non-radius coordinates are already fixed by the finite route from centre and tested Real seals through StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, and the dyadic comparison row.

Replace only the radius row by the displayed positive refinement $r'$. The hypothesis says that the refined tolerance ledger is still a $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row and that the same comparison row $D$ certifies membership inside that radius.

The structural rows need no new data. Componentwise transport $H$, replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ are read over the same coordinates after substituting $r'$ for $r$ in the radius slot.

Thus $B'$ exposes the same finite neighbourhood route. The stability statement does not supply a chosen smaller radius outside the packet, a quotient-real equality, a trichotomy decision, or any ambient completeness theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real open ball StreamName--Real handoff]
\label{thm:real-open-ball-streamname-real-handoff}
Every neighbourhood consumer of an accepted $\RealOpenBallUp$ carrier reaches $\RealUp$ only through the finite route
$$
\StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
recorded by $W,R,D,c,E,H,C,P,N$. In particular, a consumer cannot bypass the StreamName windows, detach the RegSeqRat readback, replace the DyadicRatCore radius test by host real equality, or obtain ambient $\RealUp$ completeness from the neighbourhood row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the finite windows $W$. A neighbourhood request is allowed to inspect only the displayed StreamName windows for the centre and tested real names.

The row $R$ is then the only regular rational readback available to the consumer. It converts those windows into the rational data used by the dyadic comparison ledger.

The radius test is exactly the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$ together with the positive radius row $r$. Only after that finite comparison is displayed can the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ be consumed beside the centre seal $c$.

The remaining rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name this order of access. Therefore every Real-facing read factors through StreamName, RegSeqRat, and DyadicRatCore rows, and no host topology, quotient real equality, selected limit, open-cover compactness, trichotomy, or ambient completeness row is exported.
\end{proof}
