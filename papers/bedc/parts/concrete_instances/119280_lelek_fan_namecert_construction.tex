Following Lelek on endpoint-dense fans, the $\LelekFanUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface of the classical smooth-fan continuum whose endpoints are dense. It sits beside the pseudo-arc reconstruction of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pseudo-arc-namecert}, the compact-metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the path-connected carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-path-connected-space-namecert}, the Peano-continuum route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-peano-continuum-namecert}, and the observable-open classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert}. The packet names finite branch windows, endpoint-density rows, compact metric support, and local fan-root coherence before any compact-continuum neighbour may read it.

\begin{definition}[Lelek fan carrier]
\label{def:lelek-fan-carrier}
A $\LelekFanUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet

$$
L=(K,P,A,B,E,D,H,C,G,N)
$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, exposing finite metric covers, diameter bounds, and compact-metric provenance for the current fan window.
\item $P$ is the $\PathConnectedSpaceUp$ row, recording finite arc-chain data from the root window to each displayed branch point.
\item $A$ is the $\PeanoContinuumUp$ row, supplying the compact, connected, locally connected continuum surface inspected by branch consumers.
\item $B$ is the branch ledger, listing the root row, finite branch-prefix rows, attachment rows, and branch-order compatibility data.
\item $E$ is the endpoint row, marking branch endpoints as finite named boundary records rather than as a completed endpoint set.
\item $D$ is the endpoint-density row, recording for each inspected basic branch neighbourhood a finite endpoint witness whose carrier route remains inside that neighbourhood.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact metric, path-connected, Peano-continuum, branch, endpoint, density, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from finite fan-window requests through $K,P,A,B,E,D,H,G,N$.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LelekFanUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\PathConnectedSpaceUp$, $\PeanoContinuumUp$, $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, $\PseudoArcUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LelekFanUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate is exactly the assertion that these ten rows form one finite endpoint-dense fan packet. It has no coordinate for a host continuum, a completed endpoint set, quotient equality of continua, open-cover compactness, selected branches outside the displayed ledger, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lelek fan NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lelek-fan-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LelekFanUp$ carrier $L=(K,P,A,B,E,D,H,C,G,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LelekFanUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact-metric admission through $K$, path-connected route admission through $P$, Peano-continuum support through $A$, branch-ledger readback through $B$, endpoint exposure through $E$, endpoint-density exposure through $D$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $G$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lelek-fan-carrier}. The compact source $K$, path-connected route $P$, and Peano-continuum support $A$ are read before the branch rows, so every continuum-facing consumer begins from displayed finite metric covers, finite root-to-branch chains, and the local continuum support.

The rows $B,E,D$ supply the fan-specific content. The branch ledger $B$ records root and branch-prefix windows, the endpoint row $E$ names the boundary records, and the density row $D$ gives a finite endpoint witness inside each inspected branch neighbourhood.

Transport and replay keep the same packet visible. The rows $H$ and $C$ preserve the compact, path, continuum, branch, endpoint, and density rows under $\hsame$ and $\Cont$, while $G$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming.

Since the carrier has no host continuum coordinate, completed endpoint set, quotient equality row, open-cover compactness proof, selected branch outside $B$, countable-choice row, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness witness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Lelek fan endpoint-density handoff]
\label{thm:lelek-fan-endpoint-density-handoff}
Every accepted $\LelekFanUp$ carrier hands endpoint density to compact-continuum neighbours through the finite route

$$
K,P,A,B,E,D \longmapsto H,C,G,N.
$$

In particular, a $\CompactMetricUp$, $\PathConnectedSpaceUp$, $\PeanoContinuumUp$, or $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ consumer first reads the compact fan window, the root-to-branch route, the continuum support, the branch ledger, the endpoint row, and the endpoint-density row before it may classify the packet as an endpoint-dense fan surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:lelek-fan-namecert-obligations}. The compact metric and continuum rows $K,P,A$ determine the finite neighbourhood and path data available to a downstream consumer.

The endpoint-density handoff is the row $D$ read together with $B$ and $E$. For each inspected neighbourhood, $B$ fixes the branch window, $E$ names the endpoint candidate, and $D$ records that the candidate lies in the displayed neighbourhood. The $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ classifier may observe the neighbourhood row only after these finite branch and endpoint rows have been exposed.

Finally $H,C,G,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same dependency route. Thus the handoff remains a displayed compact-continuum certificate, not a hidden host theorem about a completed endpoint set.
\end{proof}
