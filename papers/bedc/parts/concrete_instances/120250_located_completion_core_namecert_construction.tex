\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedCompletionCoreUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\LocatedCompletionCoreUp$ packet records the finite located Cauchy-completion core that a Real-facing construction may consume before any quotient completion or selected limit is named. It sits beside the metric completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the complete metric service of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the Cauchy filter surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfilter-namecert}, the finite StreamName interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The carrier is a finite L10 completion spine; it is not a quotient completion object, a chosen limit, a countable-choice schedule, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[LocatedCompletionCore carrier]
\label{def:located-completion-core-carrier}
A $\LocatedCompletionCoreUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(M,S,F,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the completion source row, naming the metric or completion-facing input before any completed point is exposed.
\item $S$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ service row, recording only the displayed service interface used by the completion read.
\item $F$ is the $\LocatedCauchyFilterUp$ row, carrying the finite located Cauchy filter evidence read by the core.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window through which Cauchy requests and located tests are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the displayed window and filter data.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording the precision, radius, and comparison budget visible to the read.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal available only after $M,S,F,W,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, service, located Cauchy filter, window, readback, tolerance ledger, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from any limit-facing request through $M,S,F,W,R,D,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedCompletionCoreUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\LocatedCauchyFilterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedCompletionCoreUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The packet has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected completed points, hidden representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedCompletionCore NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-completion-core-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedCompletionCoreUp$ carrier $L=(M,S,F,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedCompletionCoreUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the completion source, complete service, located Cauchy filter, finite window, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, terminal Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name;
\item located Cauchy exposure, requiring every completion-facing read to display $F$ before any readback or seal is consumed;
\item finite-window exactness, requiring $W$ and $D$ to carry the visible requests, tolerances, and comparison budget used by $R$;
\item Real seal non-escape, requiring $E$ to be consumed only as the terminal seal downstream of $M,S,F,W,R,D$;
\item structural exhaustion, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed finite packet.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside these five rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:located-completion-core-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the completion source, complete service row, located Cauchy filter, finite window, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, terminal Real seal, and structural rows.

Located Cauchy exposure is the row $F$. Since $F$ is upstream of $W,R,D,E$, a completion-facing consumer cannot treat the terminal seal as a selected completed point before the located filter evidence is visible.

Finite-window exactness uses $W,D,R$. The window stores the inspected requests, the dyadic ledger stores the tolerance data, and the readback row records only the regular rational data available through those finite rows.

The remaining rows transport, replay, source, and locally name the same packet. Because the carrier omits quotient completion equality, selected completed points, hidden representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, and ambient Real completeness, no NameCert obligation can export them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedCompletionCore choice-free limit route]
\label{thm:located-completion-core-choice-free-limit-route}
Every limit-facing read of an accepted $\LocatedCompletionCoreUp$ carrier factors through the displayed finite rows
$$
M,S,F,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N .
$$
In particular, a consumer receives the completion source, complete service, located Cauchy filter, finite StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger, Real seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row, and receives no quotient completion, selected limit, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:located-completion-core-carrier}. The completion-facing input is $M$, the only complete-service coordinate is $S$, and the located Cauchy evidence is the displayed filter row $F$.

The finite analytic read is then forced through $W,R,D$. The StreamName window supplies the inspected requests, the RegSeqRat row supplies regular rational readback, and the dyadic ledger supplies the tolerance budget attached to that read.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:located-completion-core-namecert-obligations}. They place the terminal Real seal $E$ downstream of $M,S,F,W,R,D$ and exhaust every structural read through $H,C,P,N$.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completions, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness, a limit-facing consumer cannot cite any of those principles as $\LocatedCompletionCoreUp$ evidence.
\end{proof}
