Following Wijsman and Kelley, the $\WijsmanConvergenceUp$ packet records the convergence read induced by distance-to-set probes on a metric hyperspace. It consumes the Wijsman topology surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-wijsman-topology-namecert}, the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the closed-subset source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperspace-namecert}, and the finite name/readback chain of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names convergence of hyperspace objects by finite distance-evaluation rows; it is not a host sequential topology, a quotient of closed-set equality, a selected nearest-point principle, or an ambient completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Wijsman convergence carrier]
\label{def:wijsman-convergence-carrier}
A $\WijsmanConvergenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
V=(T,M,H,S,R,E,Q,A,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\WijsmanTopologyUp$ source row whose distance-evaluation opens supply the convergence classifier.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row from which all point-to-set distance requests are read.
\item $H$ is the $\HyperspaceUp$ closed-subset source row carrying the finite hyperspace names being compared.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window family for the approximating hyperspace name, the candidate limit name, and the finite probe points.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback family attached to the stream windows and distance requests.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ comparison-seal row for the displayed distance values.
\item $Q$ is the finite convergence-question row, recording which probe windows require the distance-to-set values of the approximating names to agree with the limit name at the displayed rational tolerance.
\item $A$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $T,M,H,S,R,E,Q$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for topology-source admission, metric-source admission, hyperspace-source admission, stream-window inspection, regular rational readback, real comparison, and convergence-question reads.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\WijsmanConvergenceUp$, $\WijsmanTopologyUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\HyperspaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\WijsmanConvergenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,M,H,S,R,E,Q,A,C,P,N$. It exports no host sequential topology, quotient closed-set equality, selected nearest point, pointwise choice over all probes, or ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Wijsman convergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:wijsman-convergence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\WijsmanConvergenceUp$ carrier
$$
V=(T,M,H,S,R,E,Q,A,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\WijsmanConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the Wijsman topology source, metric source, hyperspace source, stream windows, regular rational readbacks, real comparison seal, convergence-question row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item topology-source compatibility, requiring every convergence read to cite the $\WijsmanTopologyUp$ distance-evaluation surface before it can inspect a probe;
\item metric readback exposure, requiring each probe comparison to pass through $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows for the same finite window;
\item hyperspace-name attachment, requiring the approximating name and candidate limit name to remain attached to the displayed $\HyperspaceUp$ row;
\item componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $A$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, and $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$ for the same finite route;
\item local naming through $N$, excluding host sequential topologies, quotient closed-set equality, selected nearest points, pointwise choice over all probes, and ambient completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No Wijsman convergence obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:wijsman-convergence-carrier}. The displayed rows contain the Wijsman topology source, metric source, hyperspace names, stream windows, regular rational readbacks, real comparison seal, convergence-question row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row.

Use the topology source row $T$ to bind the convergence classifier to the distance-evaluation topology of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-wijsman-topology-namecert}. A convergence read therefore cannot be a free host sequential-topology read; it must cite the finite Wijsman distance probes.

Read each probe through the metric and name rows. The rows $M,H,S,R,E,Q$ expose the metric distance request, the hyperspace names under inspection, the finite stream windows, the regular rational readbacks, the real comparison seal, and the rational tolerance question as one displayed route.

Finally, $A,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier has no coordinates for quotient closed-set equality, selected nearest points, pointwise choice over all probes, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
