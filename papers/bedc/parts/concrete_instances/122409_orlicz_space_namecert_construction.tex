\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\OrliczSpaceUp$}

Following Orlicz and the classical presentations of Krasnoselskii--Rutickii and Rao--Ren, the $\OrliczSpaceUp$ packet records the Luxemburg-gauge construction of an Orlicz function space as displayed BEDC NameCert data. It sits after the functional-analysis carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-functionalanalysis-namecert}, the norm ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-norm-namecert}, the measure carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-measure-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the vector-space surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}. The packet names only the finite rows by which a Young-function ledger, measurable-function row, modular readback, Luxemburg gauge, vector operations, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert are exposed; it does not import a host Banach-space object, quotient representatives, selected almost-everywhere classes, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Orlicz space carrier]
\label{def:orlicz-space-carrier}
An $\OrliczSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(A,\Phi,M,F,I,L,V,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the measure-domain row. It records the measurable source and its $\MeasureUp$ NameCert route.
\item $\Phi$ is the Young-function row. It displays the convex growth ledger, null-at-zero read, and finite real-valued comparison data used by the modular.
\item $M$ is the measurable-function admission row. It records the function names inspected by the packet and keeps their measurable status visible before any norm read.
\item $F$ is the finite real-readback row. It routes every scalar comparison through $\RealUp$ and the local functional-analysis surface.
\item $I$ is the modular row. It records the displayed integral-read of $\Phi(|f|)$ as a measure-facing ledger, not as a host integral over quotient classes.
\item $L$ is the Luxemburg-gauge row. It records the finite scale test for the value
$$
\inf\{\lambda>0 : I(f/\lambda)\leq 1\}
$$
only through displayed positive-scale and modular rows.
\item $V$ is the vector-space row. It records addition and scalar multiplication requests as $\VecSpaceUp$ reads that preserve $A,\Phi,M,F,I,L$.
\item $R$ is the norm-readback row. It names the $\NormUp$ consumer route through the Luxemburg gauge and exposes no external Banach-space completion.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for domain, Young-function, measurable-function, real-readback, modular, Luxemburg-gauge, vector-space, norm-readback, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a consumer request through $A,\Phi,M,F,I,L,V,R,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\OrliczSpaceUp$, $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$, $\NormUp$, $\MeasureUp$, $\RealUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\OrliczSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,\Phi,M,F,I,L,V,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient measurable-function classes, selected representatives, countable choice, host almost-everywhere equality, Banach-space completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Orlicz space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:orlicz-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\OrliczSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
O=(A,\Phi,M,F,I,L,V,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\OrliczSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the measure-domain row, Young-function row, measurable-function row, finite real-readback row, modular row, Luxemburg-gauge row, vector-space row, norm-readback row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item modular exposure, requiring each consumer of a Luxemburg value to first read $A,\Phi,M,F,I$;
\item positive-scale visibility, requiring the row $L$ to display the finite positive-scale tests used by the Luxemburg gauge before any $\NormUp$ readback is exported;
\item vector-operation stability, requiring $V$ to keep addition and scalar multiplication inside the same $A,\Phi,M,F,I,L$ route;
\item norm-reader boundary, requiring every $\NormUp$ or functional-analysis consumer to pass through $R$ after the modular and vector rows have been displayed;
\item ledger nonescape, excluding quotient measurable-function classes, selected representatives, countable choice, host almost-everywhere equality, Banach-space completeness, Lean verification, and bridge checking.
\end{enumerate}
No Orlicz-space NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:orlicz-space-carrier}. The tuple lists exactly the domain, Young-function, measurable-function, real-readback, modular, Luxemburg-gauge, vector-space, norm-readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The modular obligation is the row $I$. It can be read only after $A,\Phi,M,F$ are displayed, so the integral expression remains a measure-facing ledger over named functions and real comparisons.

The Luxemburg obligation is the row $L$. Its positive-scale tests are displayed finite reads of the modular row, hence a norm consumer cannot replace them by a host infimum over quotient representatives.

The vector and norm-reader obligations are the rows $V$ and $R$. They keep addition, scalar multiplication, and $\NormUp$ readback attached to the same modular route, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that route.

The remaining exclusions follow from the carrier inventory. Since quotient measurable-function classes, selected representatives, countable choice, host almost-everywhere equality, Banach-space completeness, Lean verification, and bridge checking do not occur among the coordinates, none of those data can be exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Orlicz space Luxemburg route]
\label{thm:orlicz-space-luxemburg-route}
Every $\NormUp$, $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$, or vector-space consumer of an accepted $\OrliczSpaceUp$ packet must read the Luxemburg gauge through the displayed route
$$
A,\Phi,M,F,I \longmapsto L \longmapsto V,R,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the norm-facing value is a local NameCert route through measure data, Young-function data, finite real readback, modular exposure, and positive-scale tests. It is not a host quotient-space norm, selected almost-everywhere representative, countable-choice family of scales, ambient Banach-space completion, or external $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier and expose $A,\Phi,M,F,I$. These rows display the measure source, Young-function ledger, admitted measurable functions, real comparisons, and modular read before a Luxemburg request is available.

Read the Luxemburg row $L$. It records the positive-scale test surface over the modular row, so the gauge remains a finite route through the displayed carrier rather than an external norm formula.

The vector row $V$ and norm-readback row $R$ are downstream of $L$. They let vector-space and norm consumers inspect the same route, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The refused endpoints are absent from the carrier. Therefore a consumer can receive only the displayed Luxemburg route and cannot extract quotient representatives, selected almost-everywhere classes, countable-choice scales, Banach-space completion, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
