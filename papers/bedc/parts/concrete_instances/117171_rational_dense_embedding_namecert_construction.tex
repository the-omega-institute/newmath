Following Bishop and Bridges, the rational dense embedding into the constructive real line is read through rational source data, finite tolerance windows, and regular Cauchy handoff rows rather than through an ambient density theorem. The $\RationalDenseEmbeddingUp$ packet sits between the rational carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the equality discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-equivalence-setoid-namecert}. It supplies the finite connector by which rational approximation data are consumed by $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ without importing host real equality, quotient representatives, selected limits, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Rational dense embedding carrier]
\label{def:rational-dense-embedding-carrier}
A $\RationalDenseEmbeddingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(Q,D,W,S,R,H,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ source row, recording the rational candidate and the rational order data visible to the embedding read.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, recording the dyadic budget used to compare the rational source against a requested real window.
\item $W$ is the dense-witness window row, recording the finite interval request and the displayed rational candidate admitted inside that window.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row through which the finite window is inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row attaching the rational and dyadic observations to a regular rational name surface.
\item $H$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $Q,D,W,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $T$ is the componentwise $\hsame$ transport row for the rational source, dyadic tolerance, witness window, stream observation, regular readback, real seal, and structural rows.
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay route by which a consumer rereads the same dense-embedding packet through the same rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance row over $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyEquivalenceSetoidUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RationalDenseEmbeddingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports no host real equality, no quotient representative, no trichotomy branch, no selected limit, no countable-choice schedule, and no ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Rational dense embedding NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:rational-dense-embedding-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\RationalDenseEmbeddingUp$ carrier $E=(Q,D,W,S,R,H,T,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RationalDenseEmbeddingUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the $\RatUp$ source row, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, dense-witness window, $\StreamNameUp$ observation row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff, $\RealUp$ seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data;
\item dense-window exactness, stating that each accepted rational candidate is read inside the displayed finite request window and not from a hidden density oracle;
\item regular-rational handoff, stating that each accepted read reaches $\RegSeqRatUp$ only through the displayed rational, dyadic, witness-window, and stream-observation rows;
\item real-seal ordering, stating that the $\RealUp$ seal is consumed only after $Q,D,W,S,R$ have been displayed;
\item nonescape, stating that the packet contains no host real equality, quotient representative, trichotomy branch, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness row.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is the tuple displayed in \autoref{def:rational-dense-embedding-carrier}. Its rows name one rational source, one dyadic tolerance, one dense-witness window, one finite stream observation, one regular rational handoff, one Real seal, and the structural rows for transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Dense-window exactness is read from $W$ together with $Q$ and $D$. The rational candidate and dyadic tolerance are displayed before the finite request window is certified, so the packet has no hidden density oracle or host completeness row.

Regular-rational handoff and real-seal ordering are the roles of $S,R,H$. The stream observation exposes the finite window, the RegSeqRat row records the regular rational readback, and only then may the Real seal be consumed.

The nonescape clause follows from row exhaustion. The provenance may cite $\CauchyEquivalenceSetoidUp$ as an equality-discipline boundary, but no row stores host real equality, quotient representatives, trichotomy, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Rational dense embedding RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:rational-dense-embedding-regseqrat-handoff}
Every accepted dense-embedding read into $\RealUp$ factors through the displayed $\RatUp$ source row $Q$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row $D$, dense-witness window $W$, $\StreamNameUp$ observation row $S$, and $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row $R$ before the $\RealUp$ seal $H$ is consumed. The exported route is finite certificate data for $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ consumers; it exports no host real equality, quotient representative, trichotomy branch, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The first available source data are the rational candidate in $Q$ and the dyadic tolerance budget in $D$.

Next read the dense witness through $W$ and $S$. The window row records the finite interval request and accepted rational candidate, while the StreamName observation exposes only the displayed finite window.

Now pass through $R$ before consuming $H$. This records the regular rational name surface used by $\RegSeqRatUp$ and makes the terminal $\RealUp$ seal a consequence of the displayed certificate route rather than of an external density theorem.

The remaining rows transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier has no host equality, quotient, trichotomy, selected-limit, choice, or completeness coordinate, every accepted dense-embedding read remains inside the Rat/DyadicRatCore/StreamName/RegSeqRat/Real corridor.
\end{proof}
