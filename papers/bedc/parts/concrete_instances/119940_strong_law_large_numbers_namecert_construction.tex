\begin{theorem}[Strong law large numbers NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:strong-law-large-numbers-namecert-obligations}

A consumer may cite the strong law only after all rows $P,R,A,C,B,F,E,H,T,Q,N$ are visible in the carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The rows $C,B,F,E$ then route the averages through Chebyshev tail budgets, Borel--Cantelli finite-window schedules, the finite-window strong-law handoff, and Real tolerance seals. This is the only convergence-facing analytic segment in the carrier.

\end{proof}

\label{thm:strong-law-large-numbers-finite-window-handoff}

$$

\begin{proof}
