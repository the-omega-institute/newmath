$$

\item $R$ is the Dini modulus request row. It records the displayed neighbourhood request, the local oscillation bound, and the monotone precision schedule used by the packet.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance budget. It displays the input tolerance, the output tolerance, the local split cells, and the finite descent route binding them to $R$.
\item $T$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row. It records the rational observations returned from the accepted StreamName windows under the dyadic tolerance budget.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. It is reachable only after the StreamName windows, Dini request row, dyadic budget, and rational readback row have been displayed.

\begin{theorem}[Dini modulus continuity NameCert obligations]\label{thm:dini-modulus-continuity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DiniModulusContinuityUp$ carrier
$$
M=(W,R,D,T,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DiniModulusContinuityUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the StreamName window ledger, Dini modulus request, dyadic tolerance budget, RegSeqRat readback row, Real seal, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row;
\item source-window exactness, requiring every observation used by the modulus request to appear in $W$;
\item Dini-request exactness, requiring the neighbourhood request, oscillation bound, and monotone precision schedule to be the displayed cells of $R$;
\item dyadic budget compatibility, requiring $D$ to bind the input and output tolerances to the finite local split cells consumed by $R$ and $T$;
\item rational readback exactness, requiring every exported rational observation to come from $T$ after $W,R,D$ have been displayed;
\item Real-seal non-escape, requiring $E$ to remain downstream of $W,R,D,T$ and the structural rows;
\item componentwise structural preservation, requiring $H$ and $C$ to transport and replay the displayed packet without changing the modulus route;
\item provenance exhaustion, requiring $P$ and $N$ to source and name exactly the displayed packet and no quotient stream, host limit, ambient Real completeness, selected representative, countable-choice row, Lean verification, or bridge checker.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dini-modulus-continuity-carrier}. The projection exposes one source-window ledger, one Dini modulus request row, one dyadic tolerance budget, one regular rational readback row, one terminal Real seal, and the structural rows.

The window and request obligations are read from $W$ and $R$. A continuity read can inspect only the displayed StreamName windows, and the neighbourhood request, oscillation bound, and monotone precision schedule must be the cells named in $R$.

The tolerance and readback obligations are then read from $D$ and $T$. The dyadic budget displays the local split cells used by the Dini request, while $T$ lists the regular rational observations returned from those windows.

Finally, $E,H,C,P,N$ only seal, transport, replay, source, and name the displayed route. Since the carrier has no further analytic coordinate, the local NameCert exports exactly the listed finite obligations and no ambient completion principle.
\end{proof}
