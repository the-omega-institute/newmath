The Window6 forward-math package realizes six Fibonacci-cube proof-object shapes as finite NameCert data: a closed-zero count identity, a seam-gluing law, a sharp saturation-deficit inequality, a live-tail congruence with exact finite-state memory, a signed involution with a fixed block, and a zero-mark Galois closure. Each shape is recorded here as a bounded Window6 surface over Boolean words, local six-letter windows, and the no-adjacent-$1$ Fibonacci-cube constraint. The finite word surface is consumed through the no-adjacent support correspondence of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fibonaccicube-namecert} and the two-endpoint tag discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bool-namecert}; the Window6 rows therefore refine existing finite-word and Boolean NameCert carriers rather than introducing a detached proof-object island.

\label{sec:window6-seam-gluing-parity}

\begin{theorem}[Window6 seam scanner gluing]
\label{thm:window6-seam-gluing-parity}
Let $x$ and $y$ be Boolean words of length at least $5$. The even-two-hit Window6 scanner is compositional under append: the scan of $x++y$ is the scan of $x$, plus the scan of $y$, plus the bounded seam contribution determined only by the suffix of $x$ of length $5$ and the prefix of $y$ of length $5$:
$$
\nu_2(x++y)=\nu_2(x)+\nu_2(y)+\operatorname{straddle}(\operatorname{suf}_5(x),\operatorname{pref}_5(y)).
$$
The parity reading is the corresponding Boolean xor of the three summands.
\end{theorem}

\label{thm:window6-live-tail-congruence}

$$
\operatorname{realSuffixWindows}(p,s)=\operatorname{windows}_6(\operatorname{tail}_5(p)++s).
