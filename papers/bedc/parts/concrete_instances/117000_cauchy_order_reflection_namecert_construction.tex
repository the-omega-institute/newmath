\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CauchyOrderReflectionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-order-reflection-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges' constructive treatment of Cauchy real names, the $\CauchyOrderReflectionUp$ packet records the finite route by which Cauchy-tail comparisons are reflected into Real-facing order reads. It sits after the Cauchy criterion surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycriterion-namecert}, the regular rational sequence row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the order interfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-order-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totalorder-namecert}, the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet exposes only displayed tail windows, dyadic comparison ledgers, order rows, and Real-facing seals; it does not assert trichotomy, Dedekind completeness, quotient real classes, selected representatives, or host $\RealUp$ equality.

\begin{definition}[CauchyOrderReflection carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-order-reflection-carrier}
A $\CauchyOrderReflectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(C,T,O,D,R,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\CauchyCriterionUp$ row recording the accepted tail-window request and its finite Cauchy evidence.
\item $T$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tail row that turns the accepted Cauchy window into regular rational readback data.
\item $O$ is the $\OrderUp$ or $\TotalOrderUp$ comparison row used only after $C$ and $T$ have been displayed.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ comparison ledger for the inspected dyadic tolerances and rational cuts.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row that records the Real-facing order read after the finite tail comparison has passed through $O$ and $D$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for Cauchy criteria, regular-tail rows, order rows, dyadic ledgers, Real seals, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Real-facing order request back through $R,D,O,T,C$ and then forward through the same rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyOrderReflectionUp$, $\CauchyCriterionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\OrderUp$, $\TotalOrderUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyOrderReflectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only by these displayed coordinates. It contains no host quotient of Cauchy names, no chosen representative, no trichotomy branch, no Dedekind-completeness row, and no ambient host $\RealUp$ equality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyOrderReflection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-order-reflection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyOrderReflectionUp$ carrier
$$
Q=(C,T,O,D,R,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyOrderReflectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the Cauchy criterion row, RegSeqRat tail row, order comparison row, dyadic comparison ledger, Real seal row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local naming row; tail-stability of accepted comparisons; and ledger nonescape for quotient real classes, selected representatives, trichotomy, Dedekind completeness, and host Real equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-order-reflection-carrier}. The visible rows are $C,T,O,D,R,H,K,P,N$, and every NameCert obligation is read from one of those rows.

Carrier admission records the Cauchy criterion, regular-tail readback, order comparison, dyadic ledger, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name. No other analytic row is present in the packet.

Tail-stability is the compatibility of $C,T,O,D,R$ under the componentwise transport $H$ and replay $K$. The nonescape clauses follow by inventory: quotient classes, selected representatives, trichotomy, Dedekind completeness, and host Real equality are not coordinates of the carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyOrderReflection tail stability]
\label{thm:cauchy-order-reflection-tail-stability}
Let $Q=(C,T,O,D,R,H,K,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CauchyOrderReflectionUp$ carrier. Any accepted replacement of a Cauchy tail window transported by $H$ and replayed by $K$ preserves the displayed order read only through the same $C,T,O,D,R$ route. The reflected Real-facing comparison is stable as finite tail and dyadic comparison data, not as host equality of completed Real objects.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the Cauchy criterion row $C$. It names the accepted tail window and supplies the finite Cauchy evidence used by the comparison request.

Read the regular-tail row $T$ and the dyadic ledger $D$. These rows translate the tail replacement into finite regular rational and dyadic comparison data before any order row is consumed.

Now inspect $O$ and $R$. The order comparison row reads only the displayed finite comparison, and the Real seal records the reflected comparison only after that route is visible.

Transport by $H$ is componentwise and replay by $K$ follows the same displayed route. Since no coordinate stores host equality of completed Real objects, the stable comparison remains a finite $\CauchyOrderReflectionUp$ read.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyOrderReflection nonescape]
\label{thm:cauchy-order-reflection-nonescape}
Every Real-facing order consumer of an accepted $\CauchyOrderReflectionUp$ packet must pass through the finite route
$$
\CauchyCriterionUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \OrderUp,\ \TotalOrderUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RealUp .
$$
The consumer may inspect Cauchy-tail windows, regular rational readback, order comparison rows, dyadic comparison ledgers, Real seals, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming, but it cannot obtain trichotomy, Dedekind completeness, quotient classes, selected representatives, or host Real equality from this packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:cauchy-order-reflection-namecert-obligations}. It lists the admitted rows of the carrier and the only stability and nonescape obligations attached to the local name.

Use \autoref{thm:cauchy-order-reflection-tail-stability}. A tail replacement reaches a Real-facing comparison only through the displayed $C,T,O,D,R$ route, so an order consumer cannot bypass the Cauchy and dyadic windows by invoking a host quotient or selected representative.

The remaining exclusions are carrier inventory. The packet has no trichotomy branch, Dedekind-completeness coordinate, quotient-class coordinate, representative selector, or host Real-equality row, so no Real-facing order read can export them.
\end{proof}
