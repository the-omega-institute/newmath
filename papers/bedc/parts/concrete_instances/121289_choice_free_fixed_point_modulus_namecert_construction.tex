\begin{definition}[Choice-free fixed-point modulus carrier]
\label{def:choice-free-fixed-point-modulus-carrier}

$$
M=(I,T,S,D,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the interval source row imported from the $\BishopIntervalContractionUp$ route, including the located endpoint and interval-admission data consumed by the fixed-point request.
\item $T$ is the contraction self-map row, recording that the displayed map preserves the interval source and exposes its contraction witness.
\item $S$ is the shrinkage modulus row, converting each requested fixed-point tolerance into a finite contraction-stage budget.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the shrinkage row and by every downstream rational read.
\item $Q$ is the dyadic comparison row that records the retained radius, error, and slack cells for the requested tolerance.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row naming exactly which contraction stages are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows and the same dyadic tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $I,T,S,D,Q,W,R$ have been displayed.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChoiceFreeFixedPointModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,T,S,D,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a chosen fixed point, selected limit, quotient stream equality, countable choice, host completeness, or an ambient fixed-point theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free fixed-point modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:choice-free-fixed-point-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ChoiceFreeFixedPointModulusUp$ carrier
$$
M=(I,T,S,D,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ChoiceFreeFixedPointModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the interval source, contraction self-map, shrinkage modulus, dyadic tolerance, comparison, finite-window, regular readback, terminal real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item interval compatibility, requiring each fixed-point tolerance request to read $I$ and $T$ before any shrinkage budget or window is exposed;
\item modulus exactness, requiring $S,D,Q$ to be the sole source of contraction depth, tolerance, radius, error, and slack data;
\item finite readback, requiring $W$ and $R$ to be downstream of the same shrinkage and dyadic rows before the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ can be consumed;
\item non-escape, requiring $E,H,C,P,N$ to export no selected limit, quotient real equality, countable-choice schedule, host Picard theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{enumerate}
No choice-free fixed-point modulus obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:choice-free-fixed-point-modulus-carrier}. The displayed tuple $I,T,S,D,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$ is the entire public surface of the packet.

The interval and contraction rows are read first. A tolerance request cannot inspect a stage window until the interval source $I$ and self-map row $T$ have been displayed, so the packet inherits the interval-contraction route before any fixed-point modulus data is visible.

The modulus rows then determine the finite budget. The shrinkage row $S$ reads the requested tolerance through the dyadic ledger $D$ and comparison row $Q$, and these rows are the only place where contraction depth, radius, error, or slack data occur.

Finally, the readback and seal remain downstream. The finite windows $W$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$ are consumed before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same route. Since the carrier contains no selected limit, quotient equality, countable-choice row, host Picard theorem, or ambient completeness coordinate, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
