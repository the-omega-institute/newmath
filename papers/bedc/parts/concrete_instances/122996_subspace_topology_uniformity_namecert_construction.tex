\begin{definition}[Subspace topology-uniformity carrier]
\label{def:subspace-topology-uniformity-carrier}
A $\SubspaceTopologyUniformityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(T,A,O,U,E,M,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the ambient $\TopologyUp$ row whose open-set ledger supplies the topological source.
\item $A$ is the displayed admission row for the subcarrier being restricted.
\item $O$ is the $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ relative-open row read through $T$ and $A$.
\item $U$ is the $\LocatedUniformSpaceUp$ source row whose entourages are available only through displayed located requests.
\item $E$ is the restricted-entourage row, recording pairs of admitted points and the inherited entourage witness.
\item $M$ is the optional $\MetricSpaceUp$ comparison row for metric-induced entourages on the same admitted points.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ readback seal for finite radius or distance budgets consumed by $M$ and $U$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $T,A,O,U,E,M,R,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of topology and uniformity reads through the same admitted subcarrier.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SubspaceTopologyUniformityUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\SubspaceTopologyUp$, $\LocatedUniformSpaceUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SubspaceTopologyUniformityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a hidden powerset of the subspace, a selected entourage filter, quotient subset equality, ambient completion, or unrestricted metrization.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Subspace topology-uniformity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:subspace-topology-uniformity-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

If a metric comparison is present, it is read only through $M$ and reaches a finite radius or distance budget through $R$. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this same finite route.

Since the carrier has no row for a hidden powerset, selected entourage filter, quotient subset equality, completion, or unrestricted metrization, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.

\begin{theorem}[Subspace topology-uniformity entourage restriction route]
\label{thm:subspace-topology-uniformity-entourage-restriction-route}
Every public restricted-entourage read of an accepted $\SubspaceTopologyUniformityUp$ packet first exposes the ambient topology row $T$, the admission row $A$, the relative-open row $O$, and the located-uniform row $U$. It then reads the restricted-entourage row $E$ and, when a metric comparison is requested, the $\MetricSpaceUp$ row $M$ and $\RealUp$ seal $R$. The export is a finite compatibility route between subspace topology and restricted uniformity, not an ambient uniform-space construction or completion theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Metric comparison is downstream: $M$ may compare the restricted entourage with a metric ball only after the route has already exposed $T,A,O,U,E$, and $R$ seals only the displayed finite budget. The replay row $C$ preserves this order for consumers.

Thus every public read factors through the rows listed in the statement and then through $H,C,P,N$. No hidden subspace powerset, selected filter, quotient carrier, completion principle, or global metrization row is available.
\end{proof}
