It sits after the Cauchy-filter carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfilter-namecert}, the uniform-completion interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformcompletion-namecert}, the metric-completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite stream-name windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \label{def:cauchy-filter-category-carrier}

$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the object row, listing accepted $\CauchyFilterUp$ packets and their finite basis, refinement, compatibility, and endpoint windows.
\item $M$ is the morphism row, listing refinement maps and limit-compatible handoff maps between displayed Cauchy-filter objects.
\item $I$ is the identity row, assigning to each displayed object its local refinement identity over the same basis and compatibility surface.
\item $A$ is the composition row, composing two compatible morphism rows only when their middle Cauchy-filter object is displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for objects, morphisms, identities, composition, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from object admission through refinement maps, identity, composition, transport, provenance, and the local name.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyFilterCategoryUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $O,M,I,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient filter equality, selected limits, countable-choice schedules, ambient complete objects, quotient real equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy filter category NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-filter-category-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyFilterCategoryUp$ carrier $K=(O,M,I,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyFilterCategoryUp}$ surface consists exactly of Cauchy-filter object exposure through $O$, refinement and limit-compatible morphism exposure through $M$, identity exposure through $I$, composition exposure through $A$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-filter-category-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the rows $O,M,I,A,H,C,P,N$.

The object row keeps the $\CauchyFilterUp$ data visible before any categorical read. The morphism row then records only refinement and limit-compatible maps between displayed objects; it does not create a quotient object or choose a limit.

The identity and composition rows are local BEDC rows over the same displayed objects. Transport, replay, provenance, and naming preserve that packet surface, so the listed rows exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy filter category composition nonescape]
\label{thm:cauchy-filter-category-composition-nonescape}

The composition may be consumed by $\UniformCompletionUp$ and $\MetricCompletionUp$ routes as a finite refinement interface, but it exports no quotient completion, selected limit, countable choice, ambient complete object, quotient real equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:cauchy-filter-category-namecert-obligations}. They force the object, morphism, identity, and composition rows to be exposed before transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The completion-facing siblings may consume this interface as finite refinement data. Since the carrier has no row for quotient filter equality, selected limits, choice schedules, ambient complete objects, quotient real equality, or Real completeness, composition cannot export any of those claims.
\end{proof}
