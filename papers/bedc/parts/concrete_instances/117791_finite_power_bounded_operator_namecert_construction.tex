Following Dunford--Schwartz and Bishop--Bridges normed-operator analysis, the $\FinitePowerBoundedOperatorUp$ packet records finite iterates of a bounded linear operator before spectral-radius or Neumann-series consumers inspect the bound. It sits between the bounded-operator route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedlinearoperator-namecert}, the scalar-bound surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-operator-norm-namecert}, the finite inverse-series route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-neumann-series-namecert}, the spectral-radius boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-spectral-radius-namecert}, and the rational-to-real readback chain of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite power corridor; it does not assert a spectral-radius limit, a Neumann inverse, a quotient equality of operators, or an ambient completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Spectral-radius finite-power carrier]
\label{def:spectral-radius-finite-power-carrier}
A $\FinitePowerBoundedOperatorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(T,n,A,B,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\BoundedLinearOperatorUp$ row naming the source endpoint, target endpoint, endpoint action, linearity ledger, and displayed norm-bound surface.
\item $n$ is the unary power-depth row specifying the finite iterate window that may be inspected.
\item $A$ is the finite iterate row. It records the accepted powers $T^0,T^1,\ldots,T^n$ only as displayed operator rows and product ledgers.
\item $B$ is the displayed norm-bound ledger for the same finite window. Each entry reads its scalar bound through the $\OperatorNormUp$ surface and keeps the product-bound ancestry visible.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ readback route for the rational bound entries and terminal real seals used by downstream comparisons.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $T,n,A,B,R$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a bounded-operator power request through the same finite iterate and norm-bound rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FinitePowerBoundedOperatorUp$, $\BoundedLinearOperatorUp$, $\OperatorNormUp$, $\NeumannSeriesUp$, $\SpectralRadiusUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FinitePowerBoundedOperatorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate admits exactly these rows. Its classifier compares accepted packets only through the bounded-operator source row, unary depth, finite iterate ledger, displayed norm-bound ledger, rational and real readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral-radius finite-power bound]
\label{thm:spectral-radius-finite-power-bound}
For every accepted $\FinitePowerBoundedOperatorUp$ carrier
$$
F=(T,n,A,B,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
all public finite-power and norm-bound information is exposed only through the rows $T,n,A,B,R,H,C,P,N$. A $\SpectralRadiusUp$ consumer may read the finite iterate window and displayed norm bounds, and a $\NeumannSeriesUp$ consumer may read the same finite corridor before checking residual rows, but neither consumer receives a spectral-radius limit, a Neumann-series inverse, quotient operator equality, selected representative data, or an ambient completeness coordinate from this packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:spectral-radius-finite-power-carrier}. The bounded operator is the row $T$, and the only iteration budget is the unary depth row $n$.

The finite powers are then exactly the entries of $A$. Their scalar controls are exactly the entries of $B$, with rational and real readback passing through $R$.

Transport and replay do not add analytic coordinates. The rows $H$ and $C$ move and replay the same finite iterate and norm-bound ledger, while $P$ and $N$ only source and name that surface.

Thus a spectral-radius or Neumann-series consumer can inspect the finite corridor but cannot recover a limiting radius, an inverse, quotient equality of operators, selected representatives, or ambient completeness data from the carrier.
\end{proof}
