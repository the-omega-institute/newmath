Following Bishop and Bridges on compact metric uniform continuity and Brouwer fan-bar arguments, the $\CompactUniformFanModulusUp$ packet records the finite fan-to-uniform-modulus route used by L10 Real and Completion consumers. It sits over the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the pointwise continuous-map rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the finite fan-bar modulus surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-fan-bar-modulus-namecert}, the uniform-modulus handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet turns displayed compact finite-net and fan-bar data into a BEDC-native modulus certificate; it does not assert arbitrary open-cover compactness, choose an infinite branch, select an external Lebesgue number, quotient real names, invoke countable choice, import a host modulus function, or use ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Compact uniform fan modulus carrier]
\label{def:compact-uniform-fan-modulus-carrier}
A $\CompactUniformFanModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(K,F,B,L,T,U,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the compact finite-net row read through $\CompactMetricUp$; it displays the finite center schedule, mesh radius, and cover-membership data consumed by the fan route.
\item $F$ is the pointwise $\ContinuousMapUp$ modulus row, attaching a source-radius witness to each displayed center and requested target tolerance.
\item $B$ is the $\FiniteFanBarModulusUp$ bar row; equivalently it may be read through the fan-theorem surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert} when the fan proof has already exposed a finite bar.
\item $L$ is the rational lower-bound fold, taking the finitely many radii displayed by $K,F,B$ to one positive dyadic tolerance.
\item $T$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger that records the requested target precision, the finite fold value, and the comparison budget used by the output route.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row produced by $L$ and $T$.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row opened by the uniform-modulus request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular rational approximants observed inside the opened windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $K,F,B,L,T,U,W,R$ have been replayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact-net, continuous-map, fan-bar, lower-bound, tolerance, handoff, window, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a compact-uniform-modulus request through $K,F,B,L,T,U,W,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactUniformFanModulusUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\FiniteFanBarModulusUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformFanModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only by transporting these finite rows to a common compact-fan tolerance surface.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform fan modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-fan-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactUniformFanModulusUp$ carrier
$$
M=(K,F,B,L,T,U,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformFanModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, compact finite-net exposure, pointwise continuous-map modulus exposure, finite fan-bar exposure, rational lower-bound fold, dyadic tolerance ledger, $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff, $\StreamNameUp$ window replay, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-uniform-fan-modulus-carrier}. The only analytic source rows are $K,F,B,L,T,U,W,R,E$, and the only structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The compact and continuity data are read before the fan route. The finite-net row $K$ supplies the displayed centers and mesh data, while $F$ supplies the pointwise modulus rows attached to those centers.

The fan contribution is finite because $B$ is a displayed bar-modulus row. The lower-bound fold $L$ and tolerance ledger $T$ consume the finitely many rows from $K,F,B$ before the uniform-modulus output $U$ is visible.

Finally $W,R,E$ route the output into StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real-facing reads, and $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover compactness, selected infinite branches, selected external Lebesgue numbers, quotient map equality, countable choice, host modulus functions, or ambient Real completeness, no local obligation can export those objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform fan modulus Real route]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-fan-modulus-real-route}
Every $\RealUp$-facing compact-uniform-modulus read exported by an accepted $\CompactUniformFanModulusUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\CompactMetricUp\longmapsto\ContinuousMapUp\longmapsto\FiniteFanBarModulusUp\longmapsto\UniformModulusUp\longmapsto\StreamNameUp\longmapsto\RegSeqRatUp\longmapsto\RealUp .
$$
The route displays compact finite-net data, pointwise continuous-map moduli, finite fan-bar data, the rational lower-bound fold, the dyadic tolerance ledger, the uniform-modulus handoff, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before a Real consumer may read the output.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the obligations of \autoref{thm:compact-uniform-fan-modulus-namecert-obligations}. They expose $K,F,B,L,T,U,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$ as the whole local NameCert surface.

The compact-to-fan prefix is $K,F,B$. The compact metric finite-net data in $K$ and the pointwise continuous-map rows in $F$ are consumed by the finite bar-modulus row $B$, so the route never needs an infinite branch or arbitrary open-cover selection.

The modulus output is then forced through $L,T,U$. The fold $L$ and ledger $T$ produce a displayed dyadic tolerance before $U$ is available as a $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff.

The Real-facing suffix is $W,R,E$. StreamName windows are opened first, RegSeqRat readback follows, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal is visible only after those finite rows have been replayed. The structural rows preserve the same route, so the exported Real read is exactly the displayed finite corridor.
\end{proof}
