\begin{theorem}[Sard theorem NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sard-theorem-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SardTheoremUp$ carrier $S=(M,J,R,V,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SardTheoremUp}$ surface consists exactly of chart admission through $M$, finite-jet critical-window support through $J$, regular-value consumption through $R$, displayed value-window exposure through $V$, terminal $\RealUp$ readback through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $S=(M,J,R,V,E,H,C,P,N)$. The chart row $M$ fixes the finite manifold windows available to the theorem-level read, while the finite-jet row $J$ supplies the derivative, rank-defect, and critical-window evidence.

Next read the regular-value row $R$ together with the inspected value window $V$. The regular-value route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sard-regular-value-namecert} consumes the finite critical-window support of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sard-theorem-finite-jet-namecert}, so the theorem-level packet uses exactly the displayed handoff rather than an ambient measure argument.

Finally read $E,H,C,P,N$. The Real row seals the finite value-window comparison, and the structural rows transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the excluded host coordinates are absent from the carrier, they cannot discharge any local NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the packet under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. No omitted host coordinate can supply measure-zero reasoning, transversality, smooth-topology closure, or a selected regular value.
