\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform Cauchy family NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-cauchy-family-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactUniformCauchyFamilyUp$ carrier
$$
K=(X,N,F,\sigma,R,D,U,Q,E,H,C,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformCauchyFamilyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the compact source, finite net, family row, shared schedule, regular readbacks, dyadic tolerance ledger, uniform-family row, compact-uniform row, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; compact-net exactness requiring $N$ to remain attached to $X$; shared-modulus exactness requiring $\sigma,D,U$ to be common to every displayed family member and net cell; Real-seal nonescape requiring $E$ to be downstream of $Q$ and $U$; and ledger exhaustion excluding open-cover choice, selected limits, quotient streams, component-specific moduli, countable choice, host real equality, and ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-uniform-cauchy-family-carrier}. The compact source, finite net, family row, shared schedule, regular readbacks, dyadic ledger, uniform-family row, compact-uniform row, and seal row are all explicit entries of the finite packet.

The compact-net and shared-modulus clauses are forced by the single rows $N,\sigma,D,U$. The net row remains attached to $X$, while the family route uses the same schedule and dyadic ledger for every displayed component and every displayed net cell.

The Real seal is downstream of this data. The row $Q$ binds the compact-net and uniform-family routes, and $E$ appears only after $Q$ and $U$ have been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. Since no additional coordinate appears in the carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface and exclude the refused compactness, choice, quotient, and completeness routes.
\end{proof}
