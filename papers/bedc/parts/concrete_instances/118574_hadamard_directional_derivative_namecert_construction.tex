It sits between the point-direction interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-gateaux-derivative-namecert} and the full linear approximation interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-frechet-derivative-namecert}. \label{def:hadamard-directional-derivative-carrier}

$$

\item $A$ is the base-point and neighbourhood window over which the directional approach is inspected;
\item $V$ is the displayed direction row, including the finite perturbation window through which nearby directions are compared;

\begin{theorem}[Hadamard directional derivative NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hadamard-directional-derivative-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:hadamard-directional-derivative-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\HadamardDirectionalDerivativeUp}$ surface consists of carrier admission, direction-window exposure, finite quotient scheduling, perturbation stability, linear-response handoff, classifier stability, and non-escape for the same finite packet $D=(X,A,V,Q,L,S,R,H,C,P,N)$. Direction-window exposure requires $V$ before any quotient read. Finite quotient scheduling requires every inspected step and perturbed direction to be listed in $Q$. Perturbation stability requires the comparison row $S$ to keep the quotient reads attached to $L$ and $R$. The non-escape clause excludes host function equality, quotient germs, selected limits, unrestricted Frechet differentiability, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed packet. Carrier admission exposes exactly the rows $X,A,V,Q,L,S,R,H,C,P,N$, so there is no hidden coordinate for a host derivative or a selected limiting value.

The direction-window and quotient clauses follow from the order of the carrier. The direction row $V$ is displayed before $Q$, and $Q$ is the only finite schedule from which quotient reads may be taken.

The stability and handoff clauses use $S,L,R$. The row $S$ compares all listed perturbation reads against the same $\LinearMapUp$ response $L$ through the $\RealUp$ tolerance seal $R$.

Finally, $H$ and $C$ transport and replay only those rows. Thus a consumer asking for host function equality, quotient-germ equality, an unrestricted Frechet derivative, a selected limit, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness leaves the accepted packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hadamard directional derivative finite-step stability]
\label{thm:hadamard-directional-derivative-finite-step-stability}
For every accepted $\HadamardDirectionalDerivativeUp$ carrier

A $\GateauxDerivativeUp$-facing consumer may read the displayed directional value through $V,Q,L,S,R$, while a $\FrechetDerivativeUp$-facing consumer must still supply a separate full linear-approximation packet. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Therefore the packet supplies a stable Hadamard directional read for $\GateauxDerivativeUp$-style consumers, but it does not by itself provide the global linear approximation required by $\FrechetDerivativeUp$.
\end{proof}
