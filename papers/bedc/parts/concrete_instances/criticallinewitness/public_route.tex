\label{thm:critical-line-witness-public-route}
Every public $\CriticalLineWitnessUp$ read factors through the supplied-zero row $Z$, the strip-modulus ledger $S,M$, the rational comparison ledger $Q$, the zeta-zero boundary, the $\mathsf{RH}$ nonproof boundary, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert_{\CriticalLineWitnessUp}$ row before it can be consumed as a critical-line witness. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the public consumer factorization of \autoref{thm:critical-line-witness-public-consumer-factorization}. It requires every public witness consumer to stay inside the supplied packet rows before reading the witness conclusion.

Use strip-zero route exactness, supplied-zero readback, and the zeta-zero boundary: \autoref{thm:critical-line-witness-strip-zero-route-exactness}, \autoref{thm:critical-line-witness-supplied-zero-readback}, and \autoref{thm:critical-line-witness-zeta-zero-boundary}. These rows keep the supplied zero and critical-strip side on the finite $Z,S,H,C,P,N$ route.

Next apply the rational and modulus side through the existing strip-modulus ledger surface, then package the downstream boundary with \autoref{thm:critical-line-witness-rh-consumer-boundary-package}. The consumer receives the witness as a finite zero-source, strip-modulus, rational-comparison, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming route.

The cited boundary package is explicitly nonproof-facing. Hence no $\mathsf{RH}$ proof, global zero classification, quotient equality, or hidden analytic-continuation certificate is exported by the public route.
\end{proof}
