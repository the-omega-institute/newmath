The $\CriticalLineWitnessUp$ zeta--RH dependency route makes the horizontal handoff explicit: the local witness consumes the $\ZetaUp$ source and $\ZetaZerosUp$ zero rows before any RH-facing boundary chapter can read it.

\begin{theorem}[CriticalLineWitness zeta--RH dependency route]
\label{thm:critical-line-witness-zeta-rh-dependency-route}
Every accepted $\CriticalLineWitnessUp$ packet that exports a critical-line witness to an RH-boundary consumer must first display the finite $\ZetaUp$ source route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-zeta-basic-namecert}, the zero-side witness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-zeta-zeros-namecert}, and the local witness rows $Z,S,M,R,Q,H,C,P,N$. The export may then be consumed by the $\RHRouteHaltingBoundaryUp$ packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rh-route-halting-boundary-namecert}, but only as a finite critical-line witness row; it does not supply an $\mathsf{RH}$ proof, a global zero classifier, analytic-continuation data, quotient representatives, host equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.CriticalLineWitnessUp.CriticalLineWitnessZetaRhDependencyRoute}
\begin{proof}
Read the zeta source first. The sibling surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-zeta-basic-namecert} supplies the displayed $\ZetaUp$ row before the local critical-line witness packet can expose its own source coordinate.

Next expose the zero-facing route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-zeta-zeros-namecert}. The local rows $Z,S,M,R,Q,H,C,P,N$ then name the finite witness, strip, modulus, rational comparison, transport, replay, provenance, and local certificate data consumed by $\CriticalLineWitnessUp$.

Finally pass the exported row to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rh-route-halting-boundary-namecert}. That consumer receives the witness as one finite boundary input, so the route remains a BEDC dependency edge rather than an RH proof, global zero classifier, analytic-continuation theorem, quotient representative, host equality, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{proof}
