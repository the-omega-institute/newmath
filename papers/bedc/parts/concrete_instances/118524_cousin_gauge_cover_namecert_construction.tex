The packet is not an open-cover compactness principle, a hidden subcover selector, a quotient equality of covers, or an ambient real-completeness assertion.

\begin{definition}[Cousin gauge-cover carrier]
\label{def:cousin-gauge-cover-carrier}
A $\CousinGaugeCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(D,T,F,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the gauge-assignment row over the displayed interval or compact interval source.
\item $T$ is the finite tagged-cell row; each entry records its tag, cell boundary, and local gauge-admission witness.
\item $F$ is the finite-refinement row, recording subdivisions or shrinkages used to make each tagged cell subordinate to $D$.
\item $S$ is the Cousin cover-selection row, read through the finite cover surfaces of $\CousinCoverUp$, $\CousinFiniteCoverUp$, and $\FiniteGaugeCoverUp$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,T,F,S$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a gauge-cover request through $D,T,F,S,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CousinGaugeCoverUp$, $\CousinCoverUp$, $\CousinFiniteCoverUp$, $\FiniteGaugeCoverUp$, $\CousinLemmaUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CousinGaugeCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,T,F,S,H,C,P,N$. It contains no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected subcovers outside the displayed finite cells, quotient cover equality, countable choice, host interval equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cousin gauge-cover finite refinement]
\label{thm:cousin-gauge-cover-finite-refinement}
Let $G=(D,T,F,S,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CousinGaugeCoverUp$ carrier. Every finite cover row exported by $G$ factors through the displayed refinement route
$$
D,T \longmapsto F \longmapsto S,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the cover is assembled from gauge-assigned tagged cells and finite refinement rows before the Cousin selection row is consumed. It exports no open-cover compactness theorem, no selected subcover outside $T,F$, no quotient equality of covers, no countable choice, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows $D,T,F,S,H,C,P,N$. The gauge row and tagged-cell row expose the only local cover data available to a consumer.

The refinement row $F$ records the finite step that makes the tagged cells subordinate to the gauge assignment. Since $F$ is a displayed row of the same packet, the consumer cannot replace it by an external compactness or subcover principle.

Finally the Cousin selection row $S$ is replayed through $H,C,P,N$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming keep the exported cover attached to the same finite tagged cells, so every accepted cover read follows the displayed route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cousin gauge-cover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cousin-gauge-cover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CousinGaugeCoverUp$ carrier
$$
G=(D,T,F,S,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CousinGaugeCoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of gauge-assignment admission through $D$, tagged-cell exposure through $T$, finite-refinement exactness through $F$, Cousin cover-selection exposure through $S$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. No local obligation is discharged by open-cover compactness, hidden subcover choice, quotient cover equality, host interval equality, countable choice, or ambient real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:cousin-gauge-cover-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the gauge assignment, tagged cells, finite refinement, Cousin selection, and structural rows.

The cover-facing obligations are exhausted by $D,T,F,S$. Gauge admission is read from $D$, the finite cell witnesses are read from $T$, refinement is read from $F$, and the Cousin-facing finite cover is read from $S$.

The structural obligations are exhausted by $H,C,P,N$. Componentwise transport preserves the displayed rows, replay follows the finite route, provenance names the source packets, and the local NameCert row admits only the listed data.
\end{proof}
