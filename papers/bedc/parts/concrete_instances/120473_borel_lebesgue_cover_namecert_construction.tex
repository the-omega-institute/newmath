\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BorelLebesgueCoverUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-borel-lebesgue-cover-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis and the Borel--Lebesgue
finite-cover theorem for closed intervals, the $\BorelLebesgueCoverUp$ packet
records the finite subcover boundary as BEDC NameCert data. It sits after the
interval compactness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-heineborelinterval-namecert},
the closed-bounded interval net of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedintervalnet-namecert}, the located
interval-cover row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-interval-cover-namecert},
and the rational readback rows of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the finite
subcover certificate available for a displayed interval cover; it does not
import general open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient real
equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Borel--Lebesgue cover carrier]
\label{def:borel-lebesgue-cover-carrier}
A $\BorelLebesgueCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(I,K,L,D,S,Q,R,F,H,C,P,N)
$$
where:
\begin{itemize}
\item $I$ is the displayed $\HeineBorelIntervalUp$ closed-interval source.
\item $K$ is the $\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp$ finite interval-net row over
the same endpoints.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedIntervalCoverUp$ row whose located cover tests are
available on the displayed interval net.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare interval
endpoints, cover radii, and finite-net refinements.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ inspection schedule for the finite cover
windows.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same schedule and
dyadic ledger.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the interval endpoints and
cover radii read through $D,S,Q$.
\item $F$ is the finite subcover-boundary row: it records that the displayed
finite window covers the interval source through $I,K,L,D,S,Q,R$.
\item $H,C,P,N$ are the componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$
replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert_{\BorelLebesgueCoverUp}$ rows.
\end{itemize}
The carrier exports no arbitrary open-cover compactness principle, no selected
subcover outside $F$, no quotient real equality, and no ambient completeness
field for $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Borel--Lebesgue cover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:borel-lebesgue-cover-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:borel-lebesgue-cover-carrier}, the local
$\NameCert_{\BorelLebesgueCoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier
admission for $B$, interval-source exposure through $I$, closed-bounded net
exposure through $K$, located-cover exposure through $L$, dyadic and regular
readback through $D,S,Q,R$, finite subcover-boundary exposure through $F$, and
structural replay through $H,C,P,N$. No NameCert read may replace those rows by
general open-cover compactness, a choice-selected subcover, quotient real
equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. Its displayed coordinates are precisely
$I,K,L,D,S,Q,R,F,H,C,P,N$, with the interval source, interval-net row,
located-cover row, rational readback rows, finite subcover boundary, and
structural rows all present before the local name is read.

The NameCert obligation for interval exposure reads $I,K,L$ together: the
closed interval, finite net, and located cover are not separate ambient
principles. The rational obligation then routes endpoint and radius comparisons
through $D,S,Q,R$.

Finally $F,H,C,P,N$ record the finite subcover boundary, transport, replay,
provenance, and local naming surface. Since no coordinate stores arbitrary
open-cover compactness, a selected subcover oracle, quotient real equality, or
ambient Real completeness, the local NameCert surface cannot export them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Borel--Lebesgue cover finite-subcover boundary]
\label{thm:borel-lebesgue-cover-finite-subcover-boundary}
Every consumer of an accepted $\BorelLebesgueCoverUp$ packet receives a finite
subcover boundary only after the rows
$$
\HeineBorelIntervalUp,\quad
\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp,\quad
\LocatedIntervalCoverUp,\quad
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad
\StreamNameUp,\quad
\RegSeqRatUp,\quad
\RealUp
$$
have been displayed inside the same carrier and replayed through $F,H,C,P,N$.
The exported finite boundary is therefore a packet-local certificate over the
displayed interval cover, not a claim of general open-cover compactness,
selected subcovers, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier and NameCert obligations. They place the interval
source $I$, interval-net row $K$, located-cover row $L$, dyadic ledger $D$,
inspection schedule $S$, regular readback $Q$, and Real seal $R$ before the
finite boundary row $F$.

The finite boundary row records coverage only for the displayed window over
that source. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the same rows,
while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming for the same packet.

Thus a downstream compact-uniform-continuity or interval-cover consumer can
read the finite subcover boundary only through the displayed packet. The
excluded compactness, selected-subcover, quotient-equality, and completeness
claims have no carrier coordinate.
\end{proof}
