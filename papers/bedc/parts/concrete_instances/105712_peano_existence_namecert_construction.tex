\begin{definition}[Peano existence carrier]
\label{def:peano-existence-carrier}
A $\PeanoExistenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(I,V,W,E,Q,M,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the interval source row, binding the initial time, initial state, local radius, and finite rational time window through the $\OdeUp$ source surface.
\item $V$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ vector-field row, read on the displayed time-state window and classified only through its continuous-map graph and modulus ledgers.
\item $W$ is the closed $\RealUp$ interval and state-window row that bounds the Euler-polygon construction before any solution read is exported.
\item $E$ is the finite Euler polygon approximation ledger, including step windows, endpoint approximants, and local slope reads from $V$.
\item $Q$ is the compact-containment row, recording that every displayed polygon segment remains in the certified window $W$.
\item $M$ is the modulus row used to compare nearby polygon windows and to pass from finite polygon data to a Cauchy-style local solution row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for interval sources, vector-field rows, real windows, polygon ledgers, containment rows, modulus rows, replay rows, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the ODE request through $I,V,W,E,Q,M$.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PeanoExistenceUp$, $\OdeUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PeanoExistenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It contains no coordinate for uniqueness, Lipschitz dependence, a selected maximal solution, ambient host compactness, or a choice principle.
\end{definition}
