\begin{theorem}[LawOfTotalVariance martingale variance consumer route]
\label{thm:law-of-total-variance-martingale-variance-consumer-route}
Every $\MartingaleUp$ or $\MartingaleConvergenceUp$ variance-facing consumer of an accepted $\LawOfTotalVarianceUp$ packet must first read the displayed $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\RandomVarUp$, $\CondExpUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows of \autoref{def:law-of-total-variance-carrier}. Only after those rows are exposed may the consumer route the variance decomposition through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-namecert} or \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-convergence-namecert}.

The route exports no host almost-sure quotient, unrestricted conditional-expectation operator, countable-choice representative, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:law-of-total-variance-carrier}. The probability source $P$, random-variable endpoint $X$, conditioning face $C$, regular-rational readback $R$, and Real seal $S$ are visible before any variance-facing consumer can read $M,V,W,T$ as a decomposition package.

For a $\MartingaleUp$ consumer, replay the displayed probability, random-variable, and conditional-expectation rows through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-namecert}. The variance row is therefore attached to the finite adapted-sequence surface rather than to a host random-variable quotient.

For a $\MartingaleConvergenceUp$ consumer, route the same displayed rows through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-convergence-namecert}. The convergence-facing read receives the bounded-window and rational-readback surface already sealed by $R$ and $S$, not an unrestricted conditional-expectation operator or an ambient completeness theorem.

The carrier has no coordinate for almost-sure quotient equality, countable choice, a host conditional-expectation operator, or external real completeness. Thus the martingale-facing route can export only the displayed finite rows named above.
\end{proof}
