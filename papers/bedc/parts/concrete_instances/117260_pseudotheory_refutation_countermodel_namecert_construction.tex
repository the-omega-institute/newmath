The $\PseudotheoryRefutationCountermodelUp$ packet realizes the refusal
taxonomy of \autoref{ch:pseudotheory-refutation-gate-map} as finite NameCert
data. It sits after the generic refusal packet of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-refutation-gate-namecert}, the
pseudotheory-specific gate of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pseudotheory-refutation-gate-namecert}, the
discovery-certificate discipline of
\autoref{def:automated-discovery-score-certificate}, and the TasteGate boundary
of \autoref{ch:visions-theory-stability-taste-gate}. The packet does not
certify positive discovery; it records the finite countermodel rows that block
an asserted positive payload when its evidence is unpaid.

\begin{definition}[Pseudotheory refutation countermodel carrier]
\label{def:pseudotheory-refutation-countermodel-carrier}
A $\PseudotheoryRefutationCountermodelUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(S,C,A,D,T,B,X,R,V,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the structural-DNA collision row, naming the candidate payload, a
prior payload, and the checked equality of their structural fingerprints.
\item $C$ is the classifier-shift failure row, recording the asserted before
and after classifiers together with the missing or neutral disagreement
support.
\item $A$ is the axiom-scan row, carrying the source-token and kernel-dependency
evidence for forbidden axiom, sorry, or dependency exposure.
\item $D$ is the certificate-debt row, recording unpaid semantic naming,
row-evidence, ledger-membership, cost, debt, or scope-seal obligations.
\item $T$ is the TasteGate admission-boundary row, marking the human-only value
and stability judgment that is not computed by the cheap refusal gate.
\item $B$ is the transport row carrying componentwise $\hsame$ comparisons for
$S,C,A,D,T,X,R,V,K,P,N$.
\item $X$ is the replay row, recording displayed $\Cont$ replay from the
positiveDiscovery payload through the countermodel rows.
\item $R$ is the provenance row tying the packet to the refutation-gate,
pseudotheory-refutation-gate, discovery-certificate,
theory-stability-taste-gate, and NameCert surfaces.
\item $V$ is the verdict row, storing which of $S,C,A,D$ is displayed against
the asserted positive payload.
\item $H$ is the admission-history row preserving the candidate's local
chapter, carrier, classifier, and evidence ledger names.
\item $K$ is the local audit-key row used to replay the refusal without reading
unlisted corpus data.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over
$\PseudotheoryRefutationCountermodelUp$, $\RefutationGateUp$,
$\PseudotheoryRefutationGateUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and
$\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local
$\NameCert_{\PseudotheoryRefutationCountermodelUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no
coordinate for a positive-discovery witness, an automatic TasteGate admission,
an unscanned axiom surface, or a hidden corpus oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Pseudotheory refutation countermodel NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:pseudotheory-refutation-countermodel-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PseudotheoryRefutationCountermodelUp$ carrier
$$
Q=(S,C,A,D,T,B,X,R,V,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PseudotheoryRefutationCountermodelUp}$ surface consists
exactly of structural-DNA collision admission, classifier-shift failure
admission, axiom-scan admission, certificate-debt admission, TasteGate
boundary marking, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay,
provenance, verdict selection, admission-history preservation, audit-key
replay, $\Pkg$ source, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:pseudotheory-refutation-countermodel-carrier}. The refusal rows
are $S,C,A,D$, the taste boundary is $T$, and the structural rows are
$B,X,R,V,H,K,P,N$.

Rows $S,C,A,D$ correspond to the finite countermodel classes: structural-DNA
collision, classifier-shift failure, axiom exposure, and unpaid certificate
debt. The verdict row $V$ records which displayed row is being used against
the asserted positive payload.

The TasteGate boundary $T$ separates cheap refusal from human admission. It can
mark where value and stability judgment is required, but it is not a positive
discovery witness and does not convert the absence of a countermodel row into
admission.

Finally $B,X,R,H,K,P,N$ transport, replay, source, preserve, audit, package,
and name the same finite refusal route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for
the excluded host objects, these rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Pseudotheory refutation countermodel audit route]
\label{thm:pseudotheory-refutation-countermodel-audit-route}
Every audit read of an accepted
$\PseudotheoryRefutationCountermodelUp$ carrier factors through the finite
route
$$
S,C,A,D,T,B,X,R,V,H,K,P,N .
$$
If the verdict row displays a structural-DNA collision row, classifier-shift
failure row, axiom-scan row, or certificate-debt row, then the audit blocks the
asserted positive payload unless the payload supplies the corresponding paid
evidence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with
\autoref{thm:pseudotheory-refutation-countermodel-namecert-obligations}. It
identifies $S,C,A,D$ as the only rows that can carry a cheap finite refusal.

The verdict row $V$ reads one of those rows against the asserted payload. The
replay row $X$ and audit-key row $K$ make the read repeatable without importing
unlisted corpus data or a hidden oracle.

The TasteGate boundary $T$ is read separately from the cheap refusal rows. It
marks human admission requirements and therefore cannot be used as automatic
positive evidence.

Componentwise transport, provenance, admission history, package source, and
local naming preserve the same finite route. Hence every accepted audit read is
the displayed countermodel route, and a positive payload without the matching
paid evidence is blocked by the displayed verdict.
\end{proof}
