\begin{theorem}[Real diagonal non-surjection Real seal dependency]
\label{thm:real-diagonal-non-surjection-real-seal-dependency}
Every $\RealDiagonalNonSurjectionUp$ diagonal read reaches its contradiction
surface only after the $\RealUp$ seal route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} and the displayed
$\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ finite-window rows are visible. The
packet is not a host cardinality theorem, not a quotient-surjection argument,
and not an appeal to ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:real-diagonal-non-surjection-carrier}. The only analytic rows
available before the terminal real-name handoff are the enumeration slice
$E$, the finite diagonal schedule $W$, the dyadic bit row $B$, the bit-flip
row $F$, and the regular rational readback $R$.

Apply \autoref{thm:real-diagonal-non-surjection-diagonal-witness}. The
contradiction surface is reached by comparing an inspected enumeration row
with the flipped bit at the displayed diagonal window. That comparison is
read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and then through $\RegSeqRatUp$ before the
seal row $S$ can expose the witness as $\RealUp$ data.

Now use the $\RealUp$ seal route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The seal does not add a
separate cardinality principle; it only marks that the already displayed
regular-rational finite windows have been admitted as the real-name row used
by the diagonal witness.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for host cardinality, quotient
surjection, selected enumeration inverse, completed powerset, countable
choice, or ambient real completeness, every diagonal contradiction remains
inside the displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ seal
dependency route.
\end{proof}
