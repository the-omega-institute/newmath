Following Sylvester and standard numerical-semigroup treatments, the $\FrobeniusCoinUp$ packet records the two-generator coin problem as finite BEDC data. It consumes unary natural-number rows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nat-namecert}, addition rows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-add-namecert}, and the finite semigroup surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-semigroup-namecert}. The packet names a displayed conductor window, not an ambient theorem about all natural numbers, an external greatest-common-divisor algorithm, quotient semigroup equality, or an unbounded search over representations.

\begin{definition}[Frobenius coin carrier]
\label{def:frobenius-coin-carrier}
A $\FrobeniusCoinUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(A,B,G,C,W,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ and $B$ are positive unary $\NatUp$ coin rows.
\item $G$ is the displayed coprime row, recording a finite witness that the carried coin rows have greatest common divisor $1$ when that witness is supplied.
\item $C$ is the finite conductor row, recording the displayed bound at which the representability window begins.
\item $W$ is the $\AddUp$-generated representability window, listing finite coefficient pairs and their displayed sums for the conductor-facing interval.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the coin rows, coprime row, conductor row, window row, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FrobeniusCoinUp$, $\NatUp$, $\AddUp$, $\SemigroupUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FrobeniusCoinUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,B,G,C,W,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host natural arithmetic, quotient semigroup equality, a hidden Euclidean algorithm, or an unbounded enumeration of all sums.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Frobenius coin NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:frobenius-coin-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FrobeniusCoinUp$ carrier $F=(A,B,G,C,W,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FrobeniusCoinUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the two positive unary coin rows; the displayed coprime row; representability-window exactness for the finite $\AddUp$ sums; the conductor bound row; the finite nonrepresentable ledger below the conductor-facing window; componentwise transport through $H$; displayed replay through $T$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:frobenius-coin-carrier}. The rows $A,B,G,C,W$ are the only arithmetic rows, and each is finite: two positive unary coin rows, one coprime witness row, one conductor row, and one displayed sum window.

The representability data are read through $W$. Each accepted entry of $W$ supplies a finite pair of unary coefficients and an $\AddUp$ replay of the corresponding sum, while every excluded entry below the displayed conductor is accounted for by the finite nonrepresentable ledger attached to the same window.

The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name that finite inventory. Since the packet has no host arithmetic coordinate, no quotient semigroup equality coordinate, and no unbounded-search coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Frobenius coin conductor-window boundary]
\label{thm:frobenius-coin-conductor-window}
Every conductor-facing consumer of an accepted $\FrobeniusCoinUp$ carrier reads only the displayed finite $\NatUp$, $\AddUp$, and $\SemigroupUp$ rows
$$
A,B,G,C,W \longmapsto H,T,P,N .
$$
In particular, the consumer receives the two positive coin rows, the copied coprime witness when present, the finite representability window, the finite below-conductor ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it receives no host natural-number arithmetic, no quotient semigroup equality, and no unbounded search procedure.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a conductor-facing request. Carrier admission forces the request to inspect $A$ and $B$ as positive unary $\NatUp$ rows, not as ambient natural numbers.

The coprime and conductor information is then exposed by $G$ and $C$. If the coprime row is absent, the packet does not claim the conductor theorem; if it is present, the row is still only the displayed finite witness carried by the packet.

Representability is read through $W$, where every accepted sum is generated by finite $\AddUp$ replay over the two coin rows and every conductor-facing refusal is recorded in the finite ledger. The $\SemigroupUp$ dependency is consumed only as this finite addition-generated surface.

Finally $H,T,P,N$ preserve the same route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. No remaining carrier field can provide host arithmetic, quotient equality, or an infinite search, so every consumer is confined to the displayed conductor window.
\end{proof}
