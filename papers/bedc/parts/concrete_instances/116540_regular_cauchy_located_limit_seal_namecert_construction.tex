\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for the regularity budget and located comparison cells.

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy located limit-seal obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-located-limit-seal-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyLocatedLimitSealUp$ carrier $L=(W,R,D,A,S,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyLocatedLimitSealUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed nine-row packet;
\item $\StreamNameUp$ window locality through $W$;
\item $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback through $R$;
\item $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and located-cell exactness through $D$;
\item $\LocatedRealUp$ located-route compatibility through $A$, together with $\RealUp$ seal ordering, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming through $S,H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No additional NameCert obligation is exported for quotient equality, selected limits, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-located-limit-seal-carrier}. Its classifier-visible coordinates are precisely the finite window, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, located row, terminal seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The window-locality and readback obligations are forced by the order of $W$ and $R$. A consumer first sees the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window and then reads it as a regular rational packet through $\RegSeqRatUp$.

The dyadic and located obligations are the next displayed rows. The row $D$ supplies the tolerance and located-cell comparisons, and the row $A$ records the located-real surface read only from the same $W,R,D$ route.

Finally, the seal and structural rows add no second source. The row $S$ is exposed only after $W,R,D,A$ have been replayed through $H,C,P,N$, and the carrier contains no quotient, selected-limit, host-equality, choice, or completeness coordinate. Hence the five listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
