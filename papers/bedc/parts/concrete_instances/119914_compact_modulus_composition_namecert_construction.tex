\begin{definition}[Compact modulus composition carrier]
\label{def:compact-modulus-composition-carrier}
A $\CompactModulusCompositionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(X,F,B,L,Q,R,T,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\CompactUp$ row carrying the displayed finite cover or finite-net packet at the requested tolerance.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousUp$ row whose pointwise modulus evidence is read at the cover centers named by $X$.
\item $B$ is the finite cover bundle row. Its entries name centers, radii, membership certificates, and the cover incidence relation used by the compact row.
\item $L$ is the local modulus ledger assigning to each displayed cover entry a pointwise rational precision row.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ rational fold row that compares the finitely many local precision and radius rows.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ tolerance and seal row consumed by the final uniform ledger.
\item $T$ is the $\MetricUp$ triangle and distance-comparison row that transports a source pair through the chosen cover center.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformContinuityUp$ handoff row produced from the finite cover, local modulus, rational fold, real tolerance, and metric rows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact cover, continuity, local modulus, rational fold, real seal, metric comparison, uniform handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a uniform-modulus request through $X,F,B,L,Q,R,T,U,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactModulusCompositionUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\ContinuousUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\UniformContinuityUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactModulusCompositionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these finite rows. It has no coordinate for a host finite-subcover selection theorem, a selected global modulus, quotient point equality, open-cover compactness, choice over cover entries, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact modulus composition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-modulus-composition-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactModulusCompositionUp$ carrier $K=(X,F,B,L,Q,R,T,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactModulusCompositionUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact finite-cover admission through $X,B$, pointwise continuous-modulus admission through $F,L$, rational finite-fold exactness through $Q$, real tolerance sealing through $R$, metric triangle transport through $T$, uniform-continuity export through $U$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-modulus-composition-carrier}. The compact cover rows $X,B$, continuous row $F$, local modulus row $L$, rational fold $Q$, real seal $R$, metric row $T$, and uniform handoff $U$ are visible coordinates of the same finite packet.

The local modulus rows are read only along the displayed cover entries. The rational fold $Q$ compares those finitely many local rows and radius rows, so the packet produces a single tolerance-facing ledger without selecting a host-level global modulus.

The metric row $T$ transports a source pair through the cover center chosen by the finite cover row, and $U$ records the resulting uniform-continuity handoff. The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this same route.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, quotient point equality, cover-entry choice outside $B$, a selected global modulus, host finite-subcover selection, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact modulus composition uniform route]
\label{thm:compact-modulus-composition-uniform-route}
Every uniform-continuity consumer of an accepted $\CompactModulusCompositionUp$ packet reaches its tolerance-only ledger through the finite route
$$
\CompactUp,\ \ContinuousUp,\ \RatUp,\ \MetricUp,\ \RealUp
\longmapsto
\UniformContinuityUp .
$$
More precisely, it must read the compact finite-cover rows $X,B$, the pointwise-modulus rows $F,L$, the rational finite-fold row $Q$, the real tolerance seal $R$, and the metric triangle row $T$ before the uniform handoff $U$ is exported.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a uniform-modulus request. The replay row $C$ routes the request first to the compact finite-cover data $X,B$, so a displayed cover entry is available before any local modulus row is consumed.

At the selected cover entry, the continuous row $F$ and local ledger $L$ expose the pointwise precision data. The rational fold row $Q$ then supplies a finite lower-bound row across all displayed cover entries, and the real seal $R$ records the tolerance being served.

Finally the metric row $T$ gives the source and target triangle estimate needed by the uniform handoff $U$. Transport, provenance, and local naming preserve the same rows, so the consumer receives a finite compact-cover composition route rather than a host compactness theorem or a selected global modulus.
\end{proof}
