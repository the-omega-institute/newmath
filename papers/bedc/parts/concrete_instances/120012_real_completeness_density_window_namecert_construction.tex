$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $D$ is the $\CauchyCompletionDensitySourceUp$ dense-source row.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row through which approximation requests are observed.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row reached after the window row.

\begin{theorem}[RealCompletenessDensityWindow NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-completeness-density-window-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-completeness-density-window-carrier}. The packet displays the vision route, dense source, finite observation window, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
