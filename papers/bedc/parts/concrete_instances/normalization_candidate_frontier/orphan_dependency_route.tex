\begin{theorem}[NormalizationCandidateFrontier sibling route]
\label{thm:normalization-candidate-frontier-sibling-route}
Every accepted $\NormalizationCandidateFrontierUp$ carrier
$$
F=(T,E,B,S,I,O,H,C,P,N)
$$
has a substantive sibling route through the candidate socket of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-normalization-candidate-socket-namecert}, the candidate-normalization confluence handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-namecert}, and the parallel-diamond frontier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-namecert}. The route consumes $E$ as candidate-normalization evidence, $B$ as the bounded local route, $S,I$ as projected normalization rows, and $O$ as the retained obstruction boundary before any local-diamond or typed-SN consumer may read the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:normalization-candidate-frontier-carrier}. The rows $T,E,B,S,I,O,H,C,P,N$ are the displayed frontier inventory, and the positive normalization projections $S,I$ are downstream of candidate evidence and bounded routing.

Read the candidate socket first. It supplies the sibling source for $E$, so the frontier packet cannot be treated as a kernel-only normalization row detached from candidate-normalization evidence.

Next route through the candidate-normalization confluence handoff and the parallel-diamond frontier. The handoff exposes the candidate-normalization boundary consumed by local confluence reads, while the parallel-diamond frontier records the local-diamond rows that can inspect the frontier only after $E,B,S,I,O$ remain visible.

Finally apply the local confluence scope and typed SN scope, \autoref{thm:normalization-candidate-frontier-local-confluence-scope} and \autoref{thm:normalization-candidate-frontier-typed-sn-scope}. They keep local-diamond and typed-SN consumers inside the displayed rows with transport, replay, provenance, and local naming attached. The sibling route is therefore a concrete dependency in the MetaCIC lattice, not a claim of global strong normalization, unrestricted subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient syntax equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{proof}
