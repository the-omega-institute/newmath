\begin{definition}[Kan fibration carrier]
\label{def:kan-fibration-carrier}
A $\KanFibrationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source $\SimplicialSetUp$ map row, carrying the displayed domain, codomain, face, and degeneracy ledger of the map under inspection.
\item $A$ is the horn-boundary packet, naming the finite horn shape, missing face, boundary inclusion, and compatibility rows read from the simplicial-set sibling.
\item $B$ is the finite filler witness, recording one displayed simplex row and its boundary checks for the horn packet $A$.
\item $F$ is the $\ModelCatUp$ fibration route, reading the same map as a displayed fibration-class row inside the model-category lifting surface.
\item $M$ is the lifting-square route that binds the horn inclusion, the source map, the filler witness, and the fibration row into one finite lifting packet.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the simplicial map, horn-boundary packet, filler witness, model-category fibration route, lifting square, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a horn-lifting request through $S,A,B,F,M,H,P,N$.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KanFibrationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,A,B,F,M,H,C,P,N$. It stores no global filler function, no host simplicial set, no quotient homotopy class, no ambient model-category axiom, and no selected fibrant replacement object.
\end{definition}

\begin{proof}

The horn data are read through the simplicial-set sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-simplicialset-namecert}. \label{thm:kan-fibration-horn-lifting-scope}

\begin{proof}

The filler row $B$ is then inspected only as a finite witness for that displayed horn packet. The model-category fibration row $F$ and lifting-square row $M$ bind the same map and filler to the lifting surface exposed by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-modelcat-namecert}.
