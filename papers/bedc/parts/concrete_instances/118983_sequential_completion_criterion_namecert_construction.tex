\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SequentialCompletionCriterionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sequential-completion-criterion-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges and Kelley, the $\SequentialCompletionCriterionUp$ packet records the finite sequential criterion that an L10 completion route may read before treating a regular Cauchy stream as completion-facing data. It sits after the metric substrate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the complete-metric row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, and the completion-existence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-completion-existence-namecert}. The packet is a displayed criterion and handoff object; it is not a quotient completion, selected representative functional, countable-choice principle, trichotomy oracle, open-cover compactness argument, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Sequential completion criterion carrier]\label{def:sequential-completion-criterion-carrier}
A $\SequentialCompletionCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(M,S,R,D,E,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, exposing the named distance comparisons and the separated metric source inspected by the criterion.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, listing the finite stream observations that the completion route is allowed to read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular Cauchy readback row attached to the displayed stream windows.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger, recording the finite tolerance requests and regularity bounds used by the readback.
\item $E$ is the completion-seal row, stating that the displayed source, stream windows, readback, and tolerance ledger are accepted as completion-facing data for the named route.
\item $U$ is the separated uniqueness row, comparing two displayed readbacks only through metric separation, regular tolerances, and the same completion seal.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, stream windows, readback, tolerance ledger, completion seal, separated uniqueness row, replay, provenance, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a sequential completion request through
$$
M,S,R,D \longmapsto E,U,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SequentialCompletionCriterionUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricCompletionExistenceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialCompletionCriterionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only this finite sequential criterion. It has no coordinate for a quotient completion, selected representative, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or unlogged equality of limits.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential completion criterion NameCert obligations]\label{thm:sequential-completion-criterion-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\SequentialCompletionCriterionUp$ carrier $Q=(M,S,R,D,E,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialCompletionCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the metric source, stream-name windows, regular Cauchy readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, completion-seal row, separated uniqueness row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row;
\item source readability, requiring every distance comparison and separatedness use to pass through the displayed $\MetricUp$ row $M$;
\item sequential observation, requiring the completion-facing stream requests to be named by the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row $S$ before any rational readback is consumed;
\item regular readback, requiring the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ and dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ to witness the finite Cauchy bounds used by the criterion;
\item completion sealing and uniqueness, requiring $E$ and $U$ to bind the displayed readback to the completion route and to compare candidate readings only through separated metric data;
\item ledger refusal for quotient representatives, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, and any equality assertion not displayed in $M,S,R,D,E,U,H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sequential-completion-criterion-carrier}. The rows $M,S,R,D,E,U$ are the analytic part of the criterion, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet.

Source readability is forced by $M$. Any metric comparison used by the completion route is read as displayed metric data, so the criterion cannot appeal to a hidden equality of points or an ambient complete space.

The sequential part is then ordered through $S,R,D$. The stream windows name the observations, the regular rational readback supplies the Cauchy data attached to those observations, and the dyadic ledger records the tolerance requests that are actually inspected.

The completion seal $E$ and separated uniqueness row $U$ close the finite criterion. They certify that the displayed readback is completion-facing and that two displayed readings are compared only by the separated metric rows and the same tolerance ledger.

Finally $H$ and $C$ replay these rows componentwise, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming. Since none of the refused objects appears as a carrier coordinate, quotient representatives, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness cannot be exported by the packet.
\end{proof}
