\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BishopCauchySpaceUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopCauchySpaceUp$ packet presents the completion-facing Cauchy-space substrate as finite NameCert data before quotient completion or countable selection is invoked. It reads the dyadic tolerance arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal Real name of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the finite density handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cauchy-density-net-namecert}, and the general completion interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}. The packet names the displayed Cauchy-space infrastructure; it is not a quotient construction, a selected limit, countable choice, host equality of reals, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[BishopCauchySpace carrier]
\label{def:bishop-cauchy-space-carrier}
A $\BishopCauchySpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(D,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row;
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row attached to that tolerance;
\item $S$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular rational Cauchy data observed through $W$;
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ readback row reached only through $D,W,S$;
\item $E$ is the $\CompletionUp$ exposure row, with the finite dense handoff constrained by $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for tolerance, window, regular readback, Real readback, completion exposure, and local packet rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from dyadic tolerance through stream windows, regular rational reads, Real reads, finite density handoff, and completion exposure;
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCauchySpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these nine displayed rows. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, an unshown representative, countable choice, host real equality, or an ambient completeness oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BishopCauchySpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-cauchy-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopCauchySpaceUp$ carrier
$$
B=(D,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCauchySpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $B$, dyadic tolerance readback through $D$, finite stream-window readback through $W$, regular rational Cauchy readback through $S$, Real readback through $R$, completion exposure through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-cauchy-space-carrier}. The rows $D,W,S,R,E$ are the completion-facing Cauchy-space route, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name that route.

Read the dependencies in order. The dyadic row fixes the tolerance, the stream row fixes the finite observation window, the regular-rational row supplies the Cauchy readback, and the Real row is exposed only after those rows have been displayed.

The completion row $E$ is therefore a finite exposure surface rather than an ambient completion principle. The density handoff is constrained by $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$, and the general $\CompletionUp$ interface is read only through the displayed rows.

No further NameCert obligation can be projected, because the carrier has no quotient, representative-selection, host-equality, countable-choice, or ambient-completeness coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BishopCauchySpace finite-density handoff]
\label{thm:bishop-cauchy-space-finite-density-handoff}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\BishopCauchySpaceUp$ carrier
$$
B=(D,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
must factor its finite density read through the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row $D$, $\StreamNameUp$ window row $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $S$, $\RealUp$ readback row $R$, and $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ handoff recorded by $E$. The handoff may expose the displayed $\CompletionUp$ route, but it may not select quotient representatives, import countable choice, compare host reals, or assert ambient completeness outside the finite packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:bishop-cauchy-space-namecert-obligations}. They identify the completion-facing surface as the rows $D,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$ and no others.

The finite density handoff is carried by $E$ and sourced by the provenance row $P$. Thus a consumer reaches the completion interface only after preserving the dyadic tolerance, stream window, regular rational readback, and Real readback that $E$ exposes.

The structural rows $H,C,N$ keep that route componentwise, replay it as displayed continuation data, and name it locally. Since quotient representatives, countable choice, host real equality, and ambient completeness are absent from the carrier, no downstream completion consumer can recover them from the finite density handoff.
\end{proof}
