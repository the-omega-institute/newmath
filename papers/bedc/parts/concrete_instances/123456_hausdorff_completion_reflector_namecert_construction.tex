Following Bishop and Bridges and the Bourbaki separated-completion reflector discipline, the $\HausdorffCompletionReflectorUp$ packet records the finite BEDC data needed to pass from a completion request to a separated completion handoff. It sits beside the completion-reflection route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-reflection-namecert}, the separated-reflection route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separated-completion-reflection-namecert}, the metric completion surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the stream-name windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular-rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a reflector-side NameCert carrier; it is not a quotient construction, an ambient Hausdorff-space theorem, a choice of representatives, or an unbounded completion oracle.

\begin{definition}[Hausdorff completion reflector carrier]
\label{def:hausdorff-completion-reflector-carrier}
A $\HausdorffCompletionReflectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(Q,S,M,W,A,D,G,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the completion-request row, recording the input metric object, requested tolerance, and completion read sought by the packet.
\item $S$ is the separated-reflector row, recording that indistinguishable Cauchy data must pass through the separated reflection of $\SeparatedCompletionReflectionUp$ before it is exported.
\item $M$ is the metric-completion handoff row, tying the request to the $\MetricCompletionUp$ and $\CauchyCompletionReflectionUp$ carrier surfaces.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, listing the finite stream windows inspected by the reflector before the separated handoff.
\item $A$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, recording the regular rational witnesses used to compare the named windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row for dyadic precision cuts used in the separated comparison.
\item $G$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, saying that Real-facing reads consume only the separated completion handoff after $A$ and $D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for completion request, separated reflector, metric-completion handoff, stream windows, regular-rational readback, dyadic tolerance, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
Q,S,M,W,A,D \longmapsto G,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HausdorffCompletionReflectorUp$, $\CauchyCompletionReflectionUp$, $\SeparatedCompletionReflectionUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HausdorffCompletionReflectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $Q,S,M,W,A,D,G,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a quotient proof, chosen representative, host Hausdorff theorem, external completion functor, countable choice, or ambient Real-completeness argument.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hausdorff completion reflector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hausdorff-completion-reflector-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HausdorffCompletionReflectorUp$ carrier
$$
R=(Q,S,M,W,A,D,G,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\HausdorffCompletionReflectorUp}$ surface consists exactly of completion-request exposure through $Q$, separated-reflector exposure through $S$, metric-completion handoff through $M$, StreamName window exposure through $W$, RegSeqRat readback through $A$, DyadicRatCore tolerance exposure through $D$, Real seal ordering through $G$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hausdorff-completion-reflector-carrier}. Its rows are exactly $Q,S,M,W,A,D,G,H,C,P,N$.

The completion request, separated reflector, and metric-completion handoff are the structural entry rows. They force a completion read to pass through the separated reflector before any Real-facing result can be named.

The StreamName, RegSeqRat, and DyadicRatCore rows provide the finite observable data used by the handoff. They expose windows, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance cuts without adding a quotient or representative-choice coordinate.

Finally $G,H,C,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, source, and locally name the same route. Since the carrier has no host Hausdorff theorem, external completion functor, countable-choice row, or ambient Real-completeness coordinate, the displayed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
