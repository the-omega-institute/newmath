\begin{definition}[BlackBoxFiberWitness carrier]
\label{def:black-box-fiber-witness-carrier}
A $\BlackBoxFiberWitnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(B,S,M,K,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the visible behavior row read from samples, outputs, traces, failure cases, or an observation protocol.
\item $S$ is the support-candidate row naming the classifier candidate that supports the behavior.
\item $M$ is the missing-certificate row, recording which source, pattern, classifier, stability, or ledger-policy fields are absent or incomplete.
\item $K$ is the hidden-fiber row, kept distinct from the visible behavior row and used only as an opacity boundary.
\item $L$ is the ledger row recording present evidence, missing fields, and debt for the target $(B,S)$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the visible behavior, support candidate, missing-certificate, hidden-fiber, ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for opening behavior evidence, checking support, preserving missing-field data, and refusing hidden-fiber identification.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BlackBoxFiberWitnessUp$, classifier-forming learning, hard-certificate layers, interpretability classifier novelty, formal-verification certified interpretability, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BlackBoxFiberWitnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares packets only through $B,S,M,K,L,H,C,P,N$. No coordinate identifies the hidden fiber with visible behavior, supplies a complete explanation, or imports model-internal semantic truth.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BlackBoxFiberWitness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:black-box-fiber-witness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BlackBoxFiberWitnessUp$ carrier $F=(B,S,M,K,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BlackBoxFiberWitnessUp}$ surface consists exactly of visible behavior admission, support-candidate admission, missing-certificate preservation, hidden-fiber separation, ledger accounting, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:black-box-fiber-witness-carrier}. The displayed rows are $B,S,M,K,L,H,C,P,N$, with $B$ and $K$ occupying separate coordinates.

The support and certificate rows are read next. The row $S$ records the classifier candidate supporting $B$, while $M$ records the certificate fields that remain absent or incomplete.

The ledger row $L$ binds present evidence, missing fields, and debt for the same target. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve those distinctions, and $P,N$ only source and name the finite packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BlackBoxFiberWitness visible-hidden route]
\label{thm:black-box-fiber-witness-visible-hidden-route}
Every accepted $\BlackBoxFiberWitnessUp$ read reaches the hidden-fiber boundary only through the finite route
$$
B \longrightarrow S \longrightarrow M \longrightarrow K \longrightarrow L \longrightarrow H,C,P,N .
$$
The visible behavior row $B$ and the hidden-fiber row $K$ remain distinct throughout the route, and the support candidate may be consumed only together with the missing-certificate row and ledger row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with carrier projection. The visible behavior $B$, support candidate $S$, missing-certificate row $M$, hidden-fiber row $K$, and ledger row $L$ are separate fields.

Use \autoref{thm:black-box-fiber-witness-namecert-obligations}. It preserves missing-certificate data and hidden-fiber separation as explicit NameCert obligations, so a consumer cannot turn support evidence into a closed explanation.

Finally replay the route through $H,C,P,N$. These rows transport and name the same finite fields, not a semantic identification between visible behavior and hidden model fiber.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BlackBoxFiberWitness nonescape]
\label{thm:black-box-fiber-witness-nonescape}
No accepted $\BlackBoxFiberWitnessUp$ packet exports a hidden model-internal truth, a complete interpretability certificate, an identification of hidden fiber with visible behavior, or a verification-hardening proof. It exports only the finite rows $B,S,M,K,L,H,C,P,N$ and the visible-hidden route stated above.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The carrier of \autoref{def:black-box-fiber-witness-carrier} lists the entire accepted inventory. Hidden-fiber opacity is represented by the row $K$, while certificate debt is represented by $M$ and $L$.

Apply the visible-hidden route of \autoref{thm:black-box-fiber-witness-visible-hidden-route}. Every consumer reaches $K$ only after $B,S,M$ and before the structural rows, so the packet cannot bypass missing-certificate data.

The hard-certificate and verification siblings may later harden a certificate, and classifier-novelty siblings may compare explanation states. This packet itself supplies neither a complete certificate nor hidden semantic truth, because those objects are absent from $F=(B,S,M,K,L,H,C,P,N)$.
\end{proof}
