\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ConstructiveUniformBoundedImageUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-constructive-uniform-bounded-image-namecert}
\origin{human}

Bishop-style constructive compact metric analysis bounds the image of a uniformly continuous map by reading finite source nets through a displayed modulus and then sealing the resulting radius as a real-name row. The $\ConstructiveUniformBoundedImageUp$ packet records that finite-net bounded-image route as NameCert data. It consumes the compact source surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the compact interval source route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-compact-interval-namecert}, the graph data of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, the dyadic-radius arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a bounded-image certificate; it does not introduce open-cover compactness, a host image subset, selected extrema, quotient real equality, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Constructive uniform bounded-image carrier]
\label{def:constructive-uniform-bounded-image-carrier}
A $\ConstructiveUniformBoundedImageUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(K,F,U,M,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is a compact finite-net source row, read either through $\CompactMetricUp$ or through the interval finite-net source data of $\CompactIntervalUp$.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ graph row from the displayed source carrier to the target metric carrier.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ row that sends a target-radius request to a source-net radius request for the same graph row.
\item $M$ is the target $\MetricUp$ distance row used to compare all displayed image centers with the chosen base image center.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius ledger containing the source radius, target tolerance, finite maximum-radius fold, and comparison scale.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row that packages the dyadic radius ledger as a regular rational bound schedule.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ bound seal reached only after the compact source row, map graph, uniform modulus, target metric distance row, dyadic ledger, and rational readback have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,F,U,M,D,R,E$ and the local structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a bounded-image request through source nets, graph evaluation, uniform-modulus routing, dyadic radius folding, rational readback, and the terminal bound seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ConstructiveUniformBoundedImageUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\CompactIntervalUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveUniformBoundedImageUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for open-cover compactness, host image subsets, selected extrema, quotient equality of real names, choice-selected image centers, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive uniform bounded-image NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:constructive-uniform-bounded-image-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ConstructiveUniformBoundedImageUp$ carrier $B=(K,F,U,M,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveUniformBoundedImageUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, compact finite-net source admission through $K$, continuous-map graph admission through $F$, uniform-modulus compatibility through $U$, target metric-distance admission through $M$, dyadic radius-ledger admission through $D$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal $\RealUp$ bound sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:constructive-uniform-bounded-image-carrier}. The packet displays the compact source row, graph row, modulus row, target metric row, dyadic ledger, rational readback, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming as the only available coordinates.

The analytic rows are read in finite order. First the compact source row $K$ supplies finite net centers, then the graph row $F$ evaluates the map on those centers, and the uniform modulus row $U$ ties the requested target radius to the displayed source coverage radius.

The boundedness rows are then forced through $M,D,R,E$. The target metric row measures finite image-center distances, the dyadic ledger records the finite radius fold, the regular rational row reads the radius schedule, and the $\RealUp$ row seals the resulting bound.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no excluded host-image, selected-extremum, quotient, choice, open-cover, or completeness coordinate occurs in the carrier, those data cannot be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive uniform bounded-image finite-net bound]
\label{thm:constructive-uniform-bounded-image-finite-net-bound}
Every bounded-image consumer of an accepted $\ConstructiveUniformBoundedImageUp$ carrier receives only the finite route
$$
K,F,U,M,D,R,E,H,C,P,N .
$$
The bound is obtained by evaluating the displayed $\ContinuousMapUp$ graph on the compact finite-net centers from $\CompactMetricUp$ or $\CompactIntervalUp$, transporting the tolerance request through $\UniformModulusUp$, folding the resulting target distances in the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger, reading the radius through $\RegSeqRatUp$, and sealing it through $\RealUp$. The route is a finite bounded-image modulus, not an open-cover compactness theorem, a host image subset, a selected maximum, quotient real equality, or an ambient completeness claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compact source row $K$. It exposes finite source centers and coverage radii only; if the source is an interval packet, the interval row is read as the same finite-net source surface.

Read the map and modulus rows $F,U$. The graph row supplies the image centers, while the uniform modulus converts the requested target radius into the source-net radius used by $K$.

Now compute the displayed radius ledger. The target metric row $M$ measures distances from the chosen base image center to the finite list of image centers, and the dyadic ledger $D$ records the finite maximum-radius fold together with the target tolerance scale.

Finally read $R$ and $E$. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row packages the dyadic radius as a regular rational bound schedule, and the $\RealUp$ row seals that schedule as the bounded-image radius. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve this route without adding open-cover compactness, host image subsets, selected extrema, quotient real equality, choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
