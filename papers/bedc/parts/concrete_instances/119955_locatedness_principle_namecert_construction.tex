\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatednessPrincipleUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-locatedness-principle-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis and Troelstra--van Dalen on located predicates, the $\LocatednessPrincipleUp$ packet records the finite comparison surface by which a real or completion consumer may ask for a displayed located comparison without importing an omniscience principle. It sits beside the Ishihara trick boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ishihara-trick-namecert}, the lesser limited omniscience boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lesser-limited-omniscience-namecert}, the Markov principle boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-markov-principle-boundary-namecert}, the located real packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedreal-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a local locatedness service only: it does not claim LLPO, LPO, Markov principle, trichotomy, selected witnesses, quotient reals, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Locatedness principle carrier]
\label{def:locatedness-principle-carrier}
A $\LocatednessPrincipleUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(Q,A_-,A_+,I,O,B,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the rational comparison request row for the bounded real or completion-facing object being inspected.
\item $A_-$ and $A_+$ are the two located approximation rows that bracket the request by negative and positive rational margins.
\item $I$ is the $\IshiharaTrickUp$ boundary row used only to record finite inspection of a branch-shaped comparison.
\item $O$ is the $\LesserLimitedOmniscienceUp$ boundary row marking where two-alternative inspection is refused as a global rule.
\item $B$ is the $\MarkovPrincipleBoundaryUp$ row recording that negative evidence is named as boundary data, not accepted as a principle.
\item $R$ is the $\LocatedRealUp$ comparison row through which endpoint or cut-shaped locatedness is read.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after the request, approximation, and boundary rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the request, approximation, boundary, located-real, real-seal, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from the rational comparison request through the approximation rows, boundary rows, located-real read, and Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatednessPrincipleUp$, $\IshiharaTrickUp$, $\LesserLimitedOmniscienceUp$, $\MarkovPrincipleBoundaryUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatednessPrincipleUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for LLPO, LPO, Markov principle, trichotomy, selected witnesses, quotient reals, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Locatedness principle NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:locatedness-principle-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatednessPrincipleUp$ carrier
$$
L=(Q,A_-,A_+,I,O,B,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatednessPrincipleUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed rational comparison request, two approximation rows, boundary rows, located-real read, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item approximation locatedness, requiring $A_-$ and $A_+$ to be read before any $\LocatedRealUp$ or $\RealUp$ consumer receives a comparison answer;
\item boundary discipline, requiring every branch-shaped read to pass through $I,O,B$ as named inspection boundaries rather than as imported decision principles;
\item real-boundary handoff, requiring the located-real row $R$ and terminal seal $E$ to appear only after the comparison and boundary rows have been displayed;
\item nonescape for LLPO, LPO, Markov principle, trichotomy, selected witnesses, quotient reals, and ambient completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:locatedness-principle-carrier}. The packet displays exactly one request row, two approximation rows, three boundary rows, one located-real read, one Real seal, and four structural rows.

The approximation rows provide the located comparison service. A consumer reads $Q$, then $A_-$ and $A_+$, before any located-real or Real-facing row is available.

The branch-sensitive rows are only boundary data. The Ishihara, lesser-limited-omniscience, and Markov-boundary rows record finite inspection and refusal surfaces, so they cannot be consumed as global LLPO, LPO, Markov principle, or trichotomy.

Finally $R,E,H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the displayed route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for selected witnesses, quotient reals, or ambient completeness, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Locatedness principle real boundary]
\label{thm:locatedness-principle-real-boundary}
Every $\LocatedRealUp$ or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\LocatednessPrincipleUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
Q\longmapsto(A_-,A_+)\longmapsto(I,O,B)\longmapsto R\longmapsto E\longmapsto(H,C,P,N).
$$
Thus the consumer may receive a displayed located comparison, but it receives no LLPO, LPO, Markov principle, trichotomy, selected witness, quotient-real equality, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The request row $Q$ is the only entry point for a comparison query.

Read the approximation rows next. The rows $A_-$ and $A_+$ provide the finite located margins before any endpoint, cut, or Real-seal read can occur.

The boundary rows are downstream of those approximations. They record where Ishihara-style branch inspection, lesser-limited-omniscience refusal, and Markov-sensitive negative evidence are named, without adding a decision coordinate.

The located-real and Real rows are then $R$ and $E$. Transport, replay, provenance, and naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route, so no selected witness, quotient-real equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness row can be exported by the packet.
\end{proof}
