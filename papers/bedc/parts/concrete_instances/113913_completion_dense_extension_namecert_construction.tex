\begin{definition}[Completion dense extension carrier]
\label{def:completion-dense-extension-carrier}

$$
E=(S,D,Q,M,F,U,W,R,A,H,C,P,N)

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source metric row whose dense subcarrier supplies the domain of the initial map.
\item $D$ is the dense embedding row, read through the $\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$ surface before any completion value is inspected.
\item $Q$ is the completion seal row supplied by the Cauchy completion functor and completion universal-property route.
\item $M$ is the uniform-continuity modulus row for the source map on displayed source histories.
\item $F$ is the extension candidate row, carrying only the completion-facing value histories generated from dense source approximants.
\item $U$ is the uniqueness ledger row comparing two extension candidates after they agree on the displayed dense embedding and modulus data.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row for dense approximants and target observations.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row for those windows.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, dense embedding, completion seal, modulus, extension, uniqueness, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompletionDenseExtensionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
Accepted packets are compared only through the displayed source, density, completion, modulus, extension, uniqueness, readback, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\end{definition}

\label{thm:completion-dense-extension-namecert-obligations}

Every completion-extension consumer must read $S,D,Q,M,F,U$ before it can consume $W,R,A,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The finite readback remains downstream of those analytic rows. StreamName windows and RegSeqRat readback in $W,R$ can be read only after the dense source, completion seal, and modulus route have been displayed, and the terminal Real seal $A$ is reached only after the same route is visible.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that packet. Since none of the rows stores quotient completion equality, a selected limit, countable choice, host function equality, or ambient Real completeness, the local NameCert obligations are exhausted by the listed finite rows.
\end{proof}
