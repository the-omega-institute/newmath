\begin{definition}[Recursive orthogonal-axis normal-form carrier]
\label{def:recursive-orthogonal-axis-normal-form-carrier}
A $\RecursiveOrthogonalAxisNormalFormUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(X,L,R,O,G,T,H,C,P,N)

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source object row whose public readback is being normalized.
\item $L$ is the finite axis-layer row, recording the currently available orthogonal axes and their recursive parent relation.
\item $R$ is the readback row, sending finite axis coordinates back to the source classifier resolution.
\item $O$ is the orthogonality row, recording pairwise axis separation inside the layer.
\item $G$ is the residual-gap row, recording what remains outside the current axis layer.
\item $T$ is the normal-form row, recording the finite coordinate expression accepted for public export.
\item $H$ is the componentwise $\hsame$ transport row for source, axis, readback, orthogonality, residual-gap, and normal-form data.
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay row from source inspection through axis readback to normal-form export.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RecursiveOrthogonalAxisNormalFormUp}$ row.

\begin{theorem}[Recursive orthogonal-axis normal-form NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:recursive-orthogonal-axis-normal-form-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:recursive-orthogonal-axis-normal-form-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\RecursiveOrthogonalAxisNormalFormUp}$ surface consists exactly of source admission through $X$, axis-layer exposure through $L$, readback exposure through $R$, orthogonality exposure through $O$, residual-gap visibility through $G$, normal-form export through $T$, transport through $H$, replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. Its rows explicitly display the source, finite axis layer, readback, orthogonality, residual-gap, normal-form, transport, replay, provenance, and local-name data.

The axis rows precede normal-form export. A public conclusion can reach $T$ only after $L,R,O,G$ have been displayed, so the normal form is tied to a finite axis layer and to the residual gap left by that layer.

Rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route. They may transport, replay, source, and name the packet, but they cannot add a hidden basis, erase the residual gap, or publish a normal-form conclusion before the finite axis row has been read.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Recursive orthogonal-axis readback]
\label{thm:recursive-orthogonal-axis-normal-form-axis-readback}

$$

$$
before any normal-form conclusion is exported. In particular, the finite axis row $L$ and readback row $R$ are public prerequisites for the normal-form row $T$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:recursive-orthogonal-axis-normal-form-namecert-obligations}. It lists the finite axis-layer row and the readback row before the normal-form export row.

The orthogonality and residual-gap rows make the factorization non-vacuous. Row $O$ records the separation of the displayed axes, while $G$ records what the current layer does not resolve.

Finally read the structural rows. Componentwise transport and displayed replay preserve the route from $X$ through $L,R,O,G$ to $T$, and provenance and local naming record the same finite packet. Thus every public readback factors through the finite axis row before a normal-form conclusion is available.
\end{proof}
