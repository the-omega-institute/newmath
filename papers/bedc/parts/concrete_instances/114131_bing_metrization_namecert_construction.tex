\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BingMetrizationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-bing-metrization-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bing and the Nagata--Smirnov metrization tradition, the $\BingMetrizationUp$ packet records the finite BEDC route by which a developable regular topological source exposes a metric handoff through displayed star-refinement and cover-separation rows. It sits after the Moore-space development surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-moore-space-namecert}, beside the regular locally finite cover route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nagata-smirnov-metrization-namecert}, over the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, and before the metric-space consumer surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-space-namecert}. The packet names only the displayed Bing-style metrization route; it does not assert host topology equality, selected infinite covers, paracompactness as an external theorem, quotient point equality, or ambient real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Bing metrization carrier]
\label{def:bing-metrization-carrier}
A $\BingMetrizationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(T,M,D,S,R,L,E,Q,G,F,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row, carrying the open-row classifier, point-neighbourhood requests, and finite membership reads.
\item $M$ is the $\MooreSpaceUp$ development row, exposing the countable development windows and local star-neighbourhood requests inherited from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-moore-space-namecert}.
\item $D$ is the displayed development-level schedule. It records the finite levels inspected by the packet and the point rows whose stars are tested.
\item $S$ is the star-refinement row, binding each accepted neighbourhood request to a displayed star around the named point.
\item $R$ is the regularity-separation row, recording finite closed-neighbourhood separation data read from the topology source.
\item $L$ is the locally finite cover ledger shared with the Nagata--Smirnov route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nagata-smirnov-metrization-namecert}.
\item $E$ is the cover-shrink row, tying $S,R,L$ to the finite separation checks used before a metric row can be consumed.
\item $Q$ is the dyadic radius schedule used by the metric handoff.
\item $G$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ handoff row. It is available only after $T,M,D,S,R,L,E,Q$ have been exposed.
\item $F$ is the topology-metric compatibility row, recording that displayed metric balls from $G$ answer exactly the topology windows read through $T,M,D,S,R,L,E$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all displayed rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a topology request through development, star-refinement, regularity, locally finite covers, shrink data, dyadic radii, metric handoff, compatibility, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BingMetrizationUp$, $\MooreSpaceUp$, $\NagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BingMetrizationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for host topology equality, selected infinite covers, paracompactness as an external theorem, quotient point equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bing metrization NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bing-metrization-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BingMetrizationUp$ carrier $B=(T,M,D,S,R,L,E,Q,G,F,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BingMetrizationUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the topology source, Moore development row, level schedule, star-refinement row, regularity-separation row, locally finite cover ledger, cover-shrink row, dyadic radius schedule, metric-space handoff, topology-metric compatibility, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row;
\item development readability, requiring every Bing-style neighbourhood request to pass through $T,M,D,S$ before any cover-shrink or metric row is inspected;
\item regular cover separation, requiring the rows $R,L,E$ to expose the finite closed-neighbourhood and locally finite cover checks that license the metric handoff;
\item metric handoff exactness, requiring $Q,G,F$ to keep the $\MetricSpaceUp$ row downstream of the displayed development and cover-separation route;
\item refusal, requiring $P$ and $N$ to name only this finite packet and to refuse host topology equality, selected infinite covers, external paracompactness, quotient point equality, countable choice, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bing-metrization-carrier}. The projection exposes exactly the topology source, Moore development row, development-level schedule, star-refinement row, regularity-separation row, locally finite cover ledger, shrink row, dyadic radius schedule, metric-space handoff, topology-metric compatibility row, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Development readability is forced by the row order. A neighbourhood request starts at $T$, passes to the Moore development row $M$, chooses only the displayed finite schedule $D$, and then reads the star-refinement witness $S$.

The metric handoff is downstream of the separation route. Rows $R,L,E$ record the closed-neighbourhood and cover-shrink checks, while $Q,G,F$ record the dyadic radius schedule, the metric-space handoff, and its finite compatibility with the original topology windows.

Since the tuple contains no host topology equality, selected infinite cover, external paracompactness theorem, quotient point equality, countable-choice row, or ambient completeness coordinate, the local NameCert cannot export those principles.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bing metrization metric handoff]
\label{thm:bing-metrization-metric-handoff}
Every $\MetricSpaceUp$ consumer of an accepted $\BingMetrizationUp$ packet receives the finite route
$$
\TopologyUp,\ \MooreSpaceUp,\ D,\ S,\ R,\ L,\ E,\ Q,\ G,\ F,\ H,\ C,\ P,\ N .
$$
The metric-space row $G$ is consumed as a downstream handoff tied to the displayed development stars, regular separation rows, locally finite cover ledger, and topology compatibility windows. It is not a host metric, not a selected infinite cover construction, and not a claim that the source topology equals an external topology.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier rows in order. The topology source and Moore development rows are displayed before a star-refinement request can be answered, so the metric-facing route remains attached to the source topology and its development data.

Next read the separation and cover rows. The regularity row $R$, locally finite ledger $L$, and shrink row $E$ provide the finite Bing-style cover-separation data needed before a dyadic radius schedule or metric-space handoff is available.

The rows $Q,G,F$ then supply the downstream metric interface and its topology compatibility check. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve exactly the same route, while $P$ and $N$ record the sibling dependencies and local naming row.

The carrier contains no host topology equality, selected infinite cover, external paracompactness theorem, quotient point equality, countable-choice row, or ambient completeness coordinate. Therefore the $\MetricSpaceUp$ consumer receives only the finite BEDC handoff named above.
\end{proof}
