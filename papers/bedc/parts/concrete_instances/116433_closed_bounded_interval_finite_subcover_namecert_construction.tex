\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ClosedBoundedIntervalFiniteSubcoverUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive compact intervals, the $\ClosedBoundedIntervalFiniteSubcoverUp$ packet records the finite route by which a displayed closed bounded interval cover request is reduced to inspected located subinterval rows and a finite covering list. It sits after the interval-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedintervalnet-namecert}, the finite compact-open exhaustion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-open-exhaustion-namecert}, and the located endpoint discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}. The packet names a constructive finite-subcover handoff for closed bounded intervals; it is not a general open-cover compactness principle, not a selected cover from a host powerset, and not an ambient completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Closed bounded interval finite-subcover carrier]
\label{def:closed-bounded-interval-finite-subcover-carrier}
A $\ClosedBoundedIntervalFiniteSubcoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(I,N,E,L,S,W,H,C,P,M)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the closed bounded interval source row, including its endpoint reads and boundedness witness.
\item $N$ is the $\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp$ row supplying the finite dyadic interval-net inspection surface.
\item $E$ is the $\CompactOpenExhaustionUp$ row recording the finite exhaustion stage used by the cover request.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedIntervalUp$ row that keeps each inspected subinterval attached to displayed endpoint comparisons.
\item $S$ is the finite subcover list, whose entries are selected only from rows already displayed by $N,E,L$.
\item $W$ is the cover-witness row matching every retained net cell to an entry of $S$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval, net, exhaustion, located subinterval, subcover, and witness rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClosedBoundedIntervalFiniteSubcoverUp$, $\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp$, $\CompactOpenExhaustionUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $M$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedBoundedIntervalFiniteSubcoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,N,E,L,S,W,H,C,P,M$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, host compactness, countable choice, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Closed bounded interval finite-subcover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:closed-bounded-interval-finite-subcover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClosedBoundedIntervalFiniteSubcoverUp$ carrier $K=(I,N,E,L,S,W,H,C,P,M)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedBoundedIntervalFiniteSubcoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of interval-source admission, finite interval-net exposure, compact-open exhaustion-stage exposure, located subinterval comparison, finite subcover listing, cover-witness matching, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:closed-bounded-interval-finite-subcover-carrier}. The rows $I,N,E,L,S,W,H,C,P,M$ are the full packet, so no accepted read can appeal to a detached open-cover object or a host compactness theorem.

The constructive cover side is finite. The interval-net row $N$ supplies the inspected dyadic cells, the exhaustion row $E$ fixes the displayed cover stage, and the located row $L$ keeps the endpoint comparisons attached to those cells.

The subcover list $S$ is then checked by the witness row $W$. Each entry is matched against a displayed cell before the structural rows $H,C,P,M$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Closed bounded interval finite-subcover route]
\label{thm:closed-bounded-interval-finite-subcover-route}
Every accepted $\ClosedBoundedIntervalFiniteSubcoverUp$ packet exports a finite-subcover read only through
$$
\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp,\quad \CompactOpenExhaustionUp,\quad \LocatedIntervalUp .
$$
In carrier notation, the consumer-visible route is
$$
I,N,E,L,S,W,H,C,P,M .
$$
The route supplies a finite list and displayed coverage witnesses for the inspected closed bounded interval request; it exports no general open-cover compactness principle, cover selection from a host powerset, quotient $\RealUp$ equality, countable choice schedule, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite interval-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedintervalnet-namecert}. It gives the inspected cells before any cover witness is read.

Next use the compact-open exhaustion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-open-exhaustion-namecert} and the located interval boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}. These rows attach each cover request to a displayed exhaustion stage and endpoint comparison.

Finally apply the local obligations above. The rows $S,W$ list and verify the finite subcover, while $H,C,P,M$ only preserve and name that displayed route. Since no other carrier coordinate exists, the excluded compactness, choice, quotient, and completeness objects cannot be exported.
\end{proof}
