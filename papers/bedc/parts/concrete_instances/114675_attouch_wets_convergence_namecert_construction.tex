Following Attouch and Wets on variational convergence, the $\AttouchWetsConvergenceUp$ packet records local bounded-window convergence of displayed closed metric figures as finite L10 NameCert data. It sits after the bounded observation topology of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-attouch-wets-topology-namecert}, the closed-set hit--miss route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-convergence-namecert}, the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the Hausdorff-distance boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hausdorff-distance-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only displayed bounded windows, local distance ledgers, Kuratowski handoff rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not assert a host closed-set topology, selected nearest points, choice-based compactness, quotient equality of closed figures, or ambient hyperspace completion.

\begin{definition}[AttouchWetsConvergence carrier]
\label{def:attouch-wets-convergence-carrier}
An $\AttouchWetsConvergenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(X,T,W,K,D,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row for the ambient metric classifier and its finite distance observations.
\item $T$ is the $\AttouchWetsTopologyUp$ bounded-window row naming the inspected local balls and their tolerance schedule.
\item $W$ is the finite window ledger recording the displayed closed-set observations inside each bounded ball.
\item $K$ is the $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$ handoff row that reads lower-hit and upper-miss evidence from the same displayed windows.
\item $D$ is the local distance ledger, comparing the bounded-window observations by $\HausdorffDistanceUp$ rows and rational tolerance entries.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for window radii, tolerance thresholds, and distance comparisons.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $T,W,K,D,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, topology, window, Kuratowski handoff, distance, readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a bounded-window convergence request through the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\AttouchWetsConvergenceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\AttouchWetsTopologyUp$, $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$, $\HausdorffDistanceUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AttouchWetsConvergenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host hyperspace topology, arbitrary closed-set quotient, selected projection, choice-based compactness principle, or completed hyperspace carrier.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[AttouchWetsConvergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:attouch-wets-convergence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AttouchWetsConvergenceUp$ carrier $A=(X,T,W,K,D,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\AttouchWetsConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission; metric-source exposure through $X$; bounded-window topology exposure through $T$; finite window coverage through $W$; Kuratowski lower-hit and upper-miss handoff through $K$; local Hausdorff-distance comparison through $D$; regular rational readback through $Q$; terminal Real sealing through $R$; componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:attouch-wets-convergence-carrier}. The rows $X,T,W,K,D,Q,R,H,C,P,N$ are the only displayed coordinates available to the local NameCert.

The source and window obligations are $X,T,W$. They fix the ambient metric classifier, the bounded local balls, and the closed-set observations inspected inside those balls.

The convergence handoff obligations are $K,D,Q,R$. The Kuratowski row reads lower-hit and upper-miss evidence, the distance ledger records local Hausdorff comparisons, the regular row records the rational thresholds, and the Real seal is visible only after those finite rows have been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores a host hyperspace topology, arbitrary closed-set quotient, selected projection, choice-based compactness principle, or completed hyperspace carrier, none of those objects belongs to the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[AttouchWetsConvergence Kuratowski handoff]
\label{thm:attouch-wets-convergence-kuratowski-handoff}
Every Kuratowski-facing consumer of an accepted $\AttouchWetsConvergenceUp$ packet reads the finite route
$$
X,T,W\longmapsto K\longmapsto D,Q,R\longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the consumer receives lower-hit and upper-miss evidence only after the $\AttouchWetsTopologyUp$ bounded-window row and the displayed window ledger have been exposed, and it reaches $\RealUp$ only through regular rational readback and the local distance ledger.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the metric and topology rows. The metric source $X$ supplies the ambient distance classifier, while $T$ names the bounded Attouch--Wets windows in which convergence is inspected.

Read the finite window ledger next. The row $W$ lists the displayed closed-set observations available inside those bounded balls; no Kuratowski consumer can inspect a hit or miss claim before this ledger is present.

Apply the Kuratowski handoff row $K$. It consumes the displayed windows as lower-hit and upper-miss evidence, and the distance and regular rows $D,Q$ provide the bounded Hausdorff and rational tolerance comparisons before the terminal Real seal $R$ is visible.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the route as local BEDC data. Therefore the handoff supplies a finite convergence packet rather than host closed-set topology, quotient equality, selected nearest points, choice-based compactness, or ambient hyperspace completion.
\end{proof}
