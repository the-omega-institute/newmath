\begin{definition}[Real separation via uniformity carrier]
\label{def:real-separation-via-uniformity-carrier}
A $\RealSeparationViaUniformityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the $\RealUniformStructureUp$ row whose Real-induced entourages supply the separation test.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ row whose finite filter requests are read only through the displayed entourage surface.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row inspected after the separated filter request is displayed.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback reached from those windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ endpoint and seal row consumed after the regular rational readback.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $U,F,S,Q,R,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Real uniform entourage request through Cauchy-filter separation, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, and the Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealUniformStructureUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealSeparationViaUniformityUp}$ row.

\begin{theorem}[Real separation via uniformity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-separation-via-uniformity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealSeparationViaUniformityUp$ carrier $E=(U,F,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealSeparationViaUniformityUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed Real-uniform entourage row, Cauchy-filter row, stream-window row, regular rational readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item uniform separation, requiring every separated filter request to read the $\RealUniformStructureUp$ row $U$ before the $\CauchyFilterUp$ row $F$ is available;
\item finite Real readback, requiring $S$ and $Q$ to mediate every Real-facing read before $R$ is consumed;
\item classifier stability, requiring componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$ and displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$ to preserve the route $U,F,S,Q,R$;
\item ledger non-escape, requiring $P$ and $N$ to export no quotient filter, separated quotient completion, selected limit, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:real-separation-via-uniformity-carrier}. The accepted object exposes only $U,F,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N$.

Uniform separation follows from the displayed source order. The filter row $F$ is available only after the Real-induced entourage row $U$ has been read, so a consumer cannot separate Cauchy-filter evidence by an external quotient or host equality.

Finite Real readback is mediated by the stream and regular rational rows. The window row $S$ is inspected first, $Q$ reads the regular rational data, and $R$ seals only the resulting Real-facing name.

Transport and replay preserve this same route. The row $H$ supplies componentwise $\hsame$ stability, and $C$ repeats the route $U,F,S,Q,R$ under displayed continuation.

Finally, provenance is exhausted by $P$ and $N$. Since the excluded quotient, selection, trichotomy, compactness, choice, host-equality, and ambient-completeness data are not rows of the carrier, the local NameCert surface cannot export them.
\end{proof}
