\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DushnikMillerDimensionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-dushnik-miller-dimension-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Dushnik and Miller on the dimension of partially ordered sets, the $\DushnikMillerDimensionUp$ packet records the finite order-theoretic certificate by which a displayed poset is represented as the intersection of a finite family of linear extensions. It sits after the separated order surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poset-namecert}, the inherited preorder route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-preorder-namecert}, the total-order comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totalorder-namecert}, and the membership discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-setlike-namecert}. The packet names only finite extension rows, an intersection ledger, a minimal-cardinality witness, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not import a host powerset of orders, quotient posets, choice-selected extension families, or ambient set-theoretic dimension.

\begin{definition}[Dushnik--Miller dimension carrier]
\label{def:dushnik-miller-dimension-carrier}
A $\DushnikMillerDimensionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(P,L,I,M,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the $\PosetUp$ source row, including the inherited $\PreorderUp$ comparison ledger and antisymmetry data for the displayed carrier.
\item $L$ is a finite family of $\TotalOrderUp$ rows on the same displayed carrier, each row carrying a linear-extension ledger for the order named by $P$.
\item $I$ is the intersection ledger: for any displayed pair of carried elements, the $\PosetUp$ comparison holds exactly when every row of $L$ compares the pair in that direction.
\item $M$ is the minimal-cardinality witness, recording a displayed natural-number bound for the family $L$ and a local refusal ledger for smaller displayed families.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the poset source, total-order family, intersection ledger, minimality witness, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a dimension request through the poset source, the finite linear-extension family, the intersection ledger, and the minimality witness.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DushnikMillerDimensionUp$, $\PosetUp$, $\PreorderUp$, $\TotalOrderUp$, $\SetLikeUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DushnikMillerDimensionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for a host powerset of all linear orders, quotient poset equality, a choice-selected extension family, or an ambient cardinal-invariant theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dushnik--Miller dimension NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dushnik-miller-dimension-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DushnikMillerDimensionUp$ carrier $D=(P,L,I,M,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DushnikMillerDimensionUp}$ surface consists exactly of source admission for $P$, finite total-order family admission for $L$, linear-extension compatibility of each row of $L$ with the $\PosetUp$ comparison, intersection exactness through $I$, minimal-cardinality witnessing through $M$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $Q$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dushnik-miller-dimension-carrier}. The poset source is the displayed row $P$, and the only extension data are the finitely listed total-order rows in $L$.

Read the compatibility and intersection rows next. Each total order in $L$ extends the comparison of $P$, while $I$ records the reverse read: a comparison belongs to $P$ precisely when all listed total orders compare the pair in that direction.

Finally inspect the minimality, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows $M,H,C,Q,N$. Since the carrier has no coordinate for all host linear orders, quotient posets, selected extension families, or ambient cardinal invariants, the NameCert obligations are exactly the displayed finite rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dushnik--Miller linear-extension route]
\label{thm:dushnik-miller-dimension-linear-extension-route}
Every downstream consumer of an accepted $\DushnikMillerDimensionUp$ packet that reads order dimension must factor through
$$
P,L \longmapsto I,M \longmapsto H,C,Q,N .
$$
The consumer sees a $\PosetUp$ source, a finite family of $\TotalOrderUp$ linear-extension rows over the same carrier, the intersection ledger, and the minimal-cardinality witness before any dimension value is public. It receives no host powerset of orders, quotient poset, choice-selected family of extensions, or ambient set-theoretic dimension argument.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the accepted carrier $D=(P,L,I,M,H,C,Q,N)$. A dimension consumer can inspect the source order only through $P$, and it can inspect linear extensions only through the finite family $L$.

The intersection ledger $I$ is then load-bearing: it states both that every row of $L$ extends $P$ and that the common comparison induced by all rows of $L$ recovers exactly the displayed poset comparison. The minimality row $M$ supplies the local cardinal witness and the refusal of smaller displayed families.

The rows $H,C,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Because no additional coordinate is present, the route cannot be replaced by a host powerset search, quotient-poset equality, a choice-selected family of extensions, or an ambient cardinal-invariant proof.
\end{proof}
