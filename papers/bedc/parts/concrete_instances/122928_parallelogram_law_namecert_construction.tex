\begin{definition}[Parallelogram-law carrier]
\label{def:parallelogram-law-carrier}
A $\ParallelogramLawUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(V,N,I,P,H,C,R,L)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V$ is the $\VecSpaceUp$ source row, recording the displayed vector carrier, zero, addition, inverse, scalar action, and finite linear-combination windows used in the law.
\item $N$ is the $\NormUp$ row over $V$, recording the norm endpoints for $x$, $y$, $x+y$, and $x-y$ together with the finite comparison ledger used by the packet.
\item $I$ is the $\InnerProductUp$ candidate row, recording the pairing table and symmetry, additivity, scalar, and positivity witnesses that may induce the displayed norm.
\item $P$ is the parallelogram identity ledger. For every displayed pair $(x,y)$ in the finite window it stores the two norm-square sides
$$
N(x+y)+N(x-y)
\quad\hbox{and}\quad
2N(x)+2N(y)
$$
as $\RealUp$-sealed rows, together with the equality readback for that pair.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the vector, norm, inner-product, parallelogram, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the vector row through the norm and inner-product rows to the parallelogram ledger.
\item $R$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ParallelogramLawUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\NormUp$, $\InnerProductUp$, $\HilbertUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ParallelogramLawUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted packets only by these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for host vector equality, completed Hilbert structure, quotient norm equality, unbounded analytic closure, or an inner-product reconstruction not recorded in $I$ and $P$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Parallelogram-law NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:parallelogram-law-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ParallelogramLawUp$ carrier $Q=(V,N,I,P,H,C,R,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ParallelogramLawUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed rows, vector-source exposure through $V$, norm-readback exposure through $N$, inner-product candidate exposure through $I$, parallelogram identity accountability through $P$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $R$, and local naming through $L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:parallelogram-law-carrier}. The vector source row $V$ is the only place where the vector operations and finite pair window are stored.

The norm row $N$ exposes the four norm endpoints for each displayed pair, while $I$ exposes the inner-product candidate whose induced norm is being tested. Neither row supplies a host equality principle or a completed Hilbert object.

The ledger $P$ records the two parallelogram sides and their $\RealUp$ readback for the same pair window. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve that row order, and provenance and naming through $R$ and $L$ package the same finite data.

Since no other coordinate appears in $Q$, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}
