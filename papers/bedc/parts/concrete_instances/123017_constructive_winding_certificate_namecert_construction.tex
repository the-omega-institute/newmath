\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ConstructiveWindingCertificateUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-constructive-winding-certificate-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop-style constructive topology and the constructive argument-principle tradition, the $\ConstructiveWindingCertificateUp$ packet turns the rational-polygon layer of \autoref{ch:visions-rh-constructive-winding-certificate-layer} into finite NameCert data. It sits over the winding-number carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-winding-number-namecert}, the integer carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-int-namecert}, the rational endpoint carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the complex-number interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-complex-namecert}. The packet records the topological half of an argument-principle route; it does not count zeta zeros, supply a located $\zeta$ evaluator, or prove the analytic bridge from a function trace to the rational polygon.

\begin{definition}[Constructive winding-certificate carrier]
\label{def:constructive-winding-certificate-carrier}
A $\ConstructiveWindingCertificateUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(V,E,I,Q,R,C,S,A,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V$ is the finite cyclic list of rational complex vertices, with each coordinate read through $\RatUp$ and then through the displayed $\RealUp$ and $\ComplexUp$ interfaces.
\item $E$ is the oriented edge list obtained from $V$.
\item $I$ is the $\IntUp$ signed crossing-count row for the positive real ray.
\item $Q$ is the $\WindingNumberUp$ row that packages the crossing sum as a winding-number read.
\item $R$ records rational margin data keeping every certified edge away from the crossing discriminants used by the finite verifier.
\item $C$ records cyclic-rotation and orientation-reversal bookkeeping for the same polygon.
\item $S$ records edge-subdivision and box-split rows for finite polygon refinement.
\item $A$ records the argument-principle handoff boundary: the packet may be consumed by a later analytic trace certificate only after that trace is separately shown to lie in the rational polygon tube.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for vertices, edges, crossing rows, margin rows, refinement rows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $K$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from vertex data to edges, from edges to signed crossings, and from signed crossings to the winding-number row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ConstructiveWindingCertificateUp$, $\WindingNumberUp$, $\IntUp$, $\RatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\ComplexUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveWindingCertificateUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for zeta evaluation, analytic continuation, zero counting, Rouch\'e comparison, root location, or an ambient homotopy principle outside the displayed finite polygon data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive winding-certificate NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:constructive-winding-certificate-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ConstructiveWindingCertificateUp$ carrier $W=(V,E,I,Q,R,C,S,A,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveWindingCertificateUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the rational vertex cycle, oriented edges, signed $\IntUp$ crossing count, $\WindingNumberUp$ read, margin certificates, cyclic and orientation bookkeeping, subdivision and box-split rows, analytic handoff boundary, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier in \autoref{def:constructive-winding-certificate-carrier}. Its rows list the whole rational-polygon certificate, from vertices through crossings and winding-number readback to refinement and handoff bookkeeping.

The integer and rational data are finite. The vertices and edges are read through $\RatUp$, $\RealUp$, and $\ComplexUp$ rows, while the crossing sum is read through $\IntUp$ before it becomes a $\WindingNumberUp$ row.

The stability rows $R,C,S$ account for margin preservation, cyclic rotation, orientation reversal, edge subdivision, and box splitting. The structural rows $H,K,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name only that displayed packet.

Because the analytic trace and zero-counting obligations are not carrier rows, the NameCert surface cannot export them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Rational-polygon winding route]
\label{thm:constructive-winding-rational-polygon-route}
Every public winding read exported by an accepted $\ConstructiveWindingCertificateUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\RatUp \longmapsto \ComplexUp \longmapsto \IntUp \longmapsto \WindingNumberUp .
$$
The read is stable only under the displayed margin, cyclic-rotation, orientation-reversal, subdivision, and box-split rows. It is not a statement about $\zeta$, not a zero-counting theorem, and not an analytic argument-principle bridge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the vertex row $V$ and edge row $E$. They expose a finite rational polygon before any signed crossing is computed.

Read the crossing row $I$ through $\IntUp$ and then the public winding row $Q$ through $\WindingNumberUp$. The packet therefore reaches winding data by finite arithmetic on rational complex edges.

Use the rows $R,C,S$ for the allowed certificate operations. They preserve the same finite winding read under margin-preserving perturbation, cyclic rotation, orientation reversal, edge subdivision, and box splitting.

The analytic handoff row $A$ is only a boundary row. Since no zeta evaluator, zero counter, or argument-principle theorem is a carrier coordinate, the exported route remains the rational-polygon topological certificate only.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive winding-certificate nonescape]
\label{thm:constructive-winding-certificate-nonescape}
No accepted $\ConstructiveWindingCertificateUp$ consumer can extract a zeta-zero count, a located $\zeta$ evaluator, an analytic-continuation theorem, a Rouch\'e comparison, or a global argument-principle bridge from the packet. Any later analytic consumer must first display a separate trace-to-polygon certificate and then consume only the finite rational-polygon winding row named here.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:constructive-winding-certificate-namecert-obligations}. It exposes vertices, edges, signed crossings, winding readback, stability rows, handoff boundary, and structural rows, but no analytic evaluator.

Use the rational-polygon route of \autoref{thm:constructive-winding-rational-polygon-route}. It identifies the public winding read as a finite route through rational complex vertices, integer crossing sums, and the $\WindingNumberUp$ row.

The handoff boundary $A$ records where a later analytic certificate may attach. It does not itself contain the analytic trace, the function-theoretic comparison, or the zero-counting theorem, so those claims cannot escape from the packet.
\end{proof}
