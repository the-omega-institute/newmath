It sits beside the trapping-region certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-bendixson-trap-namecert}, the displayed ODE source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ode-namecert}, the dynamical-state surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dynsystem-namecert}, the differential-form route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-diffform-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the regular-rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Bendixson--Dulac carrier]
\label{def:bendixson-dulac-carrier}
A $\BendixsonDulacUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(O,D,F,G,S,W,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the $\OdeUp$ planar vector-field row, carrying the local state window and the right-hand-side read;
\item $D$ is the $\DynSystemUp$ local-flow row, recording the finite time-window dynamics consumed by the criterion;
\item $F$ is the $\DiffFormUp$ divergence-form row for the displayed Dulac multiplier and vector field;
\item $G$ is the finite sign ledger asserting that $\operatorname{div}(gX)$ has one displayed sign on the inspected region;
\item $S$ is the boundary-separation row recording where the sign ledger is read relative to the planar window boundary;
\item $W$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ finite readback row for the coefficient and divergence estimates used by $G$;
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for those estimates;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the ODE, dynamics, form, sign, boundary, readback, seal, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the ODE and dynamics rows through the divergence sign ledger and Real-facing readback;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BendixsonDulacUp$, $\OdeUp$, $\DynSystemUp$, $\DiffFormUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;

\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a selected periodic orbit, global flow completion, quotient path equality, measure-theoretic area, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bendixson--Dulac NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bendixson-dulac-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BendixsonDulacUp$ carrier $B=(O,D,F,G,S,W,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BendixsonDulacUp}$ surface consists exactly of planar ODE admission through $O$, local dynamics admission through $D$, differential-form divergence readback through $F$, finite sign-ledger visibility through $G$, boundary-separation visibility through $S$, regular-rational readback through $W$, Real-seal readback through $R$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bendixson-dulac-carrier}. The rows $O,D,F,G,S,W,R$ are the analytic certificate rows, and $H,C,P,N$ are the structural rows that preserve, replay, source, and name the same finite packet.

The ODE and dynamics rows display the planar local-flow window. The differential-form row supplies the divergence read, while the sign ledger and boundary-separation row record how the Dulac sign is inspected on the finite region.

The regular-rational and Real-seal rows expose only the displayed coefficient and divergence estimates. Since no further coordinate is present, the local NameCert obligations are exhausted by the listed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bendixson--Dulac divergence handoff]
\label{thm:bendixson-dulac-divergence-handoff}
Every cycle-exclusion consumer of an accepted $\BendixsonDulacUp$ packet must first read the route
$$
\OdeUp,\ \DynSystemUp,\ \DiffFormUp,\ G,\ S,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp,\ H,\ C,\ P,\ N .
$$
The divergence handoff may be used as a finite sign certificate beside the Poincare--Bendixson trap route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-bendixson-trap-namecert}, but it exports no selected periodic orbit, no global flow completion, no quotient path equality, no measure-theoretic area principle, and no ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the NameCert obligations theorem \autoref{thm:bendixson-dulac-namecert-obligations}. It forces every accepted read through the displayed ODE, dynamics, differential-form, sign-ledger, boundary, regular-rational, Real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The sign ledger is the only row that records the divergence condition. Therefore a consumer cannot obtain a cycle-exclusion handoff without preserving the displayed divergence-form read and the finite Real-facing estimates that support its sign.

The comparison with \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-bendixson-trap-namecert} is a consumer route only. That sibling supplies a trapping-region packet, while this packet supplies a divergence sign packet; neither row set creates a selected orbit, a completed global flow, quotient path equality, measure theory, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
