\begin{definition}[Moore metrization carrier]
\label{def:moore-metrization-carrier}
A $\MooreMetrizationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(S,C,T,D,F,G,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\MooreSpaceUp$ source row, carrying the displayed development windows and point-neighbourhood refinement reads.
\item $C$ is the $\CollectionwiseNormalUp$ separation row, exposing the familywise closed-row separation ledger used by the development.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ base row shared by the development, closed-family, open-neighbourhood, and metric-open comparison reads.
\item $D$ is the development compatibility row, recording that the inspected development windows refine the topology source without selecting a host sequence of covers.
\item $F$ is the $\FrinkMetrizationUp$ handoff row, translating finite star-refinement and separation reads into the metric-candidate ledger.
\item $G$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ endpoint row, exposing the resulting distance comparison and metric-open reads through finite BEDC data.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,C,T,D,F,G,K,P,N$ and for the displayed development-to-metric reads.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from Moore-space development through collectionwise-normal separation, topology comparison, Frink handoff, metric endpoint, transport, provenance, and local naming.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MooreMetrizationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,C,T,D,F,G,H,K,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected developments outside $S$, host powersets, quotient topology equality, open-cover compactness, or an untracked metrization theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Moore metrization NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:moore-metrization-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MooreMetrizationUp$ carrier $M=(S,C,T,D,F,G,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MooreMetrizationUp}$ surface consists exactly of Moore-space source admission through $S$, collectionwise-normal separation admission through $C$, topology-source alignment through $T$, development compatibility through $D$, Frink handoff through $F$, metric endpoint exposure through $G$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $K$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:moore-metrization-carrier}. The rows $S,C,T,D,F,G,H,K,P,N$ are the whole local surface.

The source rows are $S,C,T,D$. They bind the Moore development, the collectionwise-normal separation ledger, and the topology source before any metric endpoint can be read.

The metric construction rows are $F$ and $G$. The Frink handoff reads only the displayed development and separation data, and the metric endpoint exposes only the resulting finite distance and metric-open comparisons.

Finally $H,K,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and locally name the same finite route. Since no excluded host object is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
