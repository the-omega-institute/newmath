Following Hardy and Wright's treatment of Farey sequences and mediants, the $\FareyMediantIntervalUp$ packet records the finite interval-refinement surface where two determinant-neighbor rational endpoints generate a displayed mediant and a dyadic subinterval before any Real or Completion consumer reads the result. It sits over the rational carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the terminal real-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the Stern--Brocot branch row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stern-brocot-namecert}, and the Farey adjacency ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-farey-sequence-namecert}. The packet is finite interval data for rational refinement; it is not a quotient-real equality theorem, a selected representative, a trichotomy principle, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Farey mediant interval carrier]
\label{def:farey-mediant-interval-carrier}
A $\FareyMediantIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(L,R,M,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the left rational endpoint row, read through $\RatUp$ and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ numerator, denominator, sign, and scale data.
\item $R$ is the right rational endpoint row, read through the same rational and dyadic arithmetic surface.
\item $M$ is the mediant row. It records the rational
$$
\frac{p_L+p_R}{q_L+q_R}
$$
formed from the displayed endpoint numerators and denominators, together with the determinant-neighbor and Farey adjacency checks for $L$ and $R$.
\item $D$ is the dyadic-refinement row. It records the chosen child interval, the finite dyadic tolerance budget, and the gap comparison used by downstream Real and Completion consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoint, determinant-neighbor, Farey, mediant, dyadic-refinement, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite route
$$
L,R \longmapsto M \longmapsto D \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FareyMediantIntervalUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\SternBrocotUp$, $\FareySequenceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FareyMediantIntervalUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $L,R,M,D,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient real equality, selected endpoint representatives, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Farey mediant interval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:farey-mediant-interval-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FareyMediantIntervalUp$ carrier
$$
I=(L,R,M,D,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FareyMediantIntervalUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the two rational endpoint rows, determinant-neighbor evidence, Farey adjacency row, mediant row, dyadic-refinement row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item endpoint exactness, requiring $L$ and $R$ to be displayed $\RatUp$ rows with dyadic arithmetic support before a mediant is formed;
\item determinant-neighbor exactness, requiring the endpoint rows to expose the finite determinant-one check used by the Farey and Stern--Brocot sibling routes;
\item mediant exactness, requiring $M$ to use only the displayed endpoint numerators and denominators;
\item dyadic-refinement exactness, requiring $D$ to choose a displayed child interval and a finite dyadic tolerance budget downstream of $M$;
\item structural stability, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed rows above.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is stored in a quotient real equality proof, selected representative, host real order, countable-choice functional, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The endpoint data are exactly $L$ and $R$, and the arithmetic information available to the packet is the rational and dyadic data displayed in those rows.

The determinant-neighbor and Farey checks are finite rows attached to the endpoint pair. They identify the pair as an adjacent rational bracket for the sibling Stern--Brocot and Farey routes without adding a host order, trichotomy branch, or selected Real representative.

The mediant row is then forced by the endpoint numerators and denominators. Since $M$ contains no other arithmetic coordinate, the inserted rational is read only from the displayed endpoint rows.

Finally $D$ records the child interval and dyadic budget, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same packet by componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and naming. These rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Farey mediant dyadic refinement]
\label{thm:farey-mediant-interval-dyadic-refinement}
Let
$$
I=(L,R,M,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
be an accepted $\FareyMediantIntervalUp$ carrier. Every dyadic refinement read factors through the finite chain
$$
L,R \longmapsto M \longmapsto D .
$$
Thus the child interval used by a Real or Completion consumer is selected only after the determinant-neighbor endpoint rows, the Farey mediant row, and the dyadic tolerance budget have been displayed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the endpoint rows $L$ and $R$. Their determinant-neighbor evidence gives the finite rational bracket that may be refined.

Read the mediant row $M$. The inserted rational is computed from the displayed endpoint numerators and denominators, so the refinement has no independent representative or host-order coordinate.

The dyadic row $D$ then compares the resulting child interval with the finite tolerance budget. A downstream Real or Completion request can read only this displayed child interval, not a quotient real equality, a selected endpoint representative, or an ambient completeness witness.
\end{proof}
