\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row opened for that dyadic request.

\begin{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy rate-envelope finite-window induction]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-rate-envelope-finite-induction}

$$

$$
be an accepted $\RegularCauchyRateEnvelopeUp$ carrier. $$

$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

For the extension step, append one displayed window entry. The carrier keeps the same request ledger $D$ and the same tail handoff $T$, so the appended read is still part of the finite rate envelope rather than a separate representative or choice schedule.

After the induction, the consumer reaches $S$ only through $D,W,R,T$. Componentwise transport $H$ and replay $C$ preserve that order, while $P$ and $N$ only record provenance and naming. Hence no Real-facing read bypasses the finite envelope route.
\end{proof}
