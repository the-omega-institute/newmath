\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RationalIntervalPartitionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-rational-interval-partition-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\RationalIntervalPartitionUp$ packet records a finite partition of a rational interval as displayed rational endpoints, dyadic refinement witnesses, stream-window access, regular-rational readback, and a terminal Real-facing boundary. It sits after the rational carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the dyadic rational core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite partition certificate only; it is not an open-cover compactness theorem, a selected refinement principle, quotient stream equality, arbitrary real interval arithmetic, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Rational interval partition carrier]
\label{def:rational-interval-partition-carrier}
A $\RationalIntervalPartitionUp$ carrier is a finite packet
$$
P=(I,E,O,L,D,S,Q,R,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the closed rational interval row, recording the left endpoint, right endpoint, and rational endpoint comparison through $\RatUp$;
\item $E$ is the finite endpoint-list row, recording the ordered rational breakpoints from the left endpoint to the right endpoint;
\item $O$ is the adjacent-order row, recording for each neighboring pair the displayed rational comparison that makes a subinterval;
\item $L$ is the local-length row, recording rational length data for each adjacent subinterval without completing it to a real measure;
\item $D$ is the dyadic-refinement row, recording the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ witnesses used when a downstream reader asks for dyadic tolerances on the partition;
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, recording finite observation windows for the ordered endpoint list and its adjacent pairs;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, recording regular rational observations of endpoint windows and dyadic tolerance rows;
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ handoff row, available only after $I,E,O,L,D,S,Q$ have been displayed;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for interval endpoints, endpoint lists, adjacent-order rows, length rows, dyadic witnesses, stream windows, regular readback, real handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through $I,E,O,L,D,S,Q,R,H,G,N$;
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RationalIntervalPartitionUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RationalIntervalPartitionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary cover, a host supremum or infimum, a selected compactness witness, quotient equality of real names, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Rational interval partition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:rational-interval-partition-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RationalIntervalPartitionUp$ carrier
$$
P=(I,E,O,L,D,S,Q,R,H,C,G,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RationalIntervalPartitionUp}$ surface consists exactly of rational interval admission, finite endpoint-list admission, adjacent-order checks, local rational length rows, dyadic refinement witnesses, stream-window exposure, regular rational readback, terminal Real-facing handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:rational-interval-partition-carrier}. The interval data are exactly the rational endpoint row $I$, finite endpoint list $E$, adjacent-order row $O$, and local length row $L$.

The refinement and observation obligations are the rows $D,S,Q,R$. Dyadic tolerance requests read $D$, finite endpoint observations read $S$, regular rational consumers read $Q$, and Real-facing consumers reach $R$ only after the preceding rows are present.

The structural rows $H,C,G,N$ preserve and name that same finite route. Since no carrier coordinate stores an arbitrary open cover, selected refinement, quotient real equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Rational interval partition completion handoff]
\label{thm:rational-interval-partition-completion-handoff}
Every $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\RationalIntervalPartitionUp$ packet reads the partition through the route
$$
\RatUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \RealUp .
$$
In particular, the consumer must expose the rational interval row $I$, finite endpoint-list row $E$, adjacent-order row $O$, local length row $L$, dyadic-refinement row $D$, and stream-window row $S$ before it may read the regular-rational row $Q$ or terminal Real-facing row $R$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted carrier $P=(I,E,O,L,D,S,Q,R,H,C,G,N)$. The only interval source is the rational row $I$, and the only partition source is the finite endpoint list $E$ together with the adjacent-order checks $O$.

The local length row $L$ and dyadic-refinement row $D$ keep each subinterval inside rational and dyadic data. The stream-window row $S$ is therefore the only finite observation surface for downstream reads.

The regular-rational row $Q$ is downstream of those windows, and the terminal Real-facing row $R$ is downstream of $Q$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve this order and do not add an open-cover compactness row or a selected limit.
\end{proof}
