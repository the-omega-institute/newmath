\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\OneOverNZeroLimitUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-one-over-n-zero-limit-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\OneOverNZeroLimitUp$ packet records the elementary convergence of the reciprocal natural-number row to zero as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits beside the unary $\NatUp$ index surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nat-namecert}, the rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the Archimedean tail-bound route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-archimedean-property-namecert}, the stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational sequence surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet exposes the displayed threshold route for the classical sequence $n \mapsto 1/(n+1)$ without importing host limits, quotient reals, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient real completeness.

\begin{definition}[One-over-$n$ zero-limit carrier]
\label{def:one-over-n-zero-limit-carrier}
A $\OneOverNZeroLimitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(N,Q,A,W,R,E,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $N$ is the unary $\NatUp$ index row, read as the finite index window for the reciprocal sequence.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ reciprocal row, displaying the rational value $1/(n+1)$ on the inspected index.
\item $A$ is the $\RealArchimedeanPropertyUp$ tail-bound row, assigning a displayed unary threshold to every positive tolerance read.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row through which finite requests for indices and tolerances are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular rational reciprocal sequence.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after the Archimedean threshold and reciprocal readback rows are displayed as a zero-limit witness.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the index, reciprocal, threshold, window, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through $A,W,R,Q,N$ and into the terminal seal $E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\OneOverNZeroLimitUp$, $\NatUp$, $\RatUp$, $\RealArchimedeanPropertyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\OneOverNZeroLimitUp}$ row naming exactly this reciprocal zero-limit packet.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier admits no coordinate for a host limit operator, quotient real equality, trichotomy over reals, countable choice over tolerances, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[One-over-$n$ zero-limit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:one-over-n-zero-limit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\OneOverNZeroLimitUp$ carrier $Z=(N,Q,A,W,R,E,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\OneOverNZeroLimitUp}$ surface consists of five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed unary index row $N$ and reciprocal rational row $Q$;
\item positive-tolerance routing through the $\RealArchimedeanPropertyUp$ threshold row $A$;
\item finite-window and regular-rational readback exactness through $W$ and $R$;
\item terminal zero-limit sealing through the $\RealUp$ row $E$ only after $A,W,R,Q,N$ are visible;
\item componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming closure through $H,C,P,L$.
\end{enumerate}
Every accepted zero-limit read must display these rows before a consumer may use the reciprocal sequence as a real zero-limit witness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:one-over-n-zero-limit-carrier}. The only index and value data are the unary natural row $N$ and the rational reciprocal row $Q$, so carrier admission is finite.

The tolerance request is then routed through $A$. That row is an accepted $\RealArchimedeanPropertyUp$ read, so it supplies a displayed unary threshold before any reciprocal tail claim can be consumed.

The window and readback rows $W$ and $R$ keep the sequence inspection finite. They expose the same reciprocal row $Q$ on the inspected indices and do not add a selected infinite subsequence or a host limit.

Finally $E$ seals the displayed tail witness as a $\RealUp$ zero-limit read, while $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no other coordinate is present, the five listed rows are the complete local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One-over-$n$ Archimedean tail]
\label{thm:one-over-n-zero-limit-archimedean-tail}
Every positive tolerance read accepted by a $\OneOverNZeroLimitUp$ packet receives a displayed unary threshold through $\RealArchimedeanPropertyUp$ before the reciprocal row is sealed as a $\RealUp$ zero-limit witness. In particular, the consumer reads the route
$$
\RealArchimedeanPropertyUp,\ \NatUp,\ \RatUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
as finite BEDC data, not as a host limit theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a positive tolerance request. The continuation row $C$ sends that request to the Archimedean row $A$, where the tolerance receives a displayed unary threshold.

The threshold is read together with $N$ and $Q$. Past that unary index, the inspected rational reciprocal values are routed through the finite window $W$ and regular readback $R$.

Only after this route is visible does the seal row $E$ present the zero-limit witness to a $\RealUp$ consumer. The carrier lacks host limits, quotient reals, trichotomy, countable choice, and ambient completeness, so the Archimedean tail is the only exported route.
\end{proof}
