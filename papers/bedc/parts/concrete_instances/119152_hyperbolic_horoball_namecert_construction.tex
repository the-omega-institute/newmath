\begin{theorem}[Hyperbolic horoball NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hyperbolic-horoball-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name this finite route. Since the excluded global objects have no carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
