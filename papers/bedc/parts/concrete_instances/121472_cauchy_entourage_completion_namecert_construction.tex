packet names the finite L10 completion route in which Cauchy-entourage filter

\begin{definition}[Cauchy entourage completion carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-entourage-completion-carrier}
A $\CauchyEntourageCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(F,B,A,U,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyEntourageFilterUp$ source row, exposing Cauchy requests
as finite entourage-filter observations before any completion read is public.
\item $B$ is the $\EntourageBaseUp$ row, naming the base entourages and
refinement ledger consumed by the Cauchy request.
\item $A$ is the $\RegularCauchyEntourageBasisUp$ row, aligning regular Cauchy
windows with the displayed entourage-base generators.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformEntourageCompletionUp$ row, recording the uniform
completion handoff read downstream of $F,B,A$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row shared by the source,
regular basis, and uniform completion reads.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those finite windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the entourage,
regularity, and completion comparisons.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $F,B,A,U,S,R,D$
have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Cauchy-entourage
filter, entourage base, regular basis, uniform completion, stream, readback,
dyadic, Real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion request through
the route
$$
F,B,A \longmapsto U,S,R,D \longmapsto E,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyEntourageCompletionUp$,
$\CauchyEntourageFilterUp$, $\EntourageBaseUp$,
$\RegularCauchyEntourageBasisUp$, $\UniformEntourageCompletionUp$,
$\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$,
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyEntourageCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through
$F,B,A,U,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient filters, selected
representatives, selected limits, countable-choice schedules, host equality,
or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy entourage completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-entourage-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyEntourageCompletionUp$ carrier
$K=(F,B,A,U,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\CauchyEntourageCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier
admission for the displayed rows, CauchyEntourageFilter source exposure through
$F$, EntourageBase refinement exposure through $B$,
RegularCauchyEntourageBasis alignment through $A$, UniformEntourageCompletion
handoff through $U$, StreamName window preservation through $S$, RegSeqRat
readback preservation through $R$, DyadicRatCore tolerance preservation through
$D$, Real-seal preservation through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$,
displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through
$N$. No local NameCert clause is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-entourage-completion-carrier}. The
projection exposes exactly the source, base, regular-basis, uniform-completion,
finite-window, readback, dyadic, Real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and
local naming rows.

The source route is read first. A Cauchy-entourage request must display $F$,
then open the base-entourage row $B$, and then align that base with the regular
Cauchy entourage-basis row $A$ before the uniform completion row $U$ is
available.

The L10 readback route is downstream of that source route. StreamName windows,
RegSeqRat readback, and DyadicRatCore tolerances are read as the shared rows
$S,R,D$ before the terminal seal $E$ can be consumed.

Finally attach $H,C,P,N$. These rows transport, replay, source, and name only
the displayed packet. Since the carrier has no quotient, selected
representative, selected limit, choice schedule, host-equality, or ambient
completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy entourage completion Real handoff]
\label{thm:cauchy-entourage-completion-real-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$-facing consumer of an accepted $\CauchyEntourageCompletionUp$
carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\CauchyEntourageFilterUp,\ \EntourageBaseUp,\ \RegularCauchyEntourageBasisUp
\longmapsto \UniformEntourageCompletionUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp
\longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
In carrier notation, every such read passes through
$$
F,B,A \longmapsto U,S,R,D \longmapsto E,H,C,P,N .
$$
The handoff exports no quotient filter, selected representative, selected
limit, countable-choice schedule, host equality of completed points, host Real
equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the NameCert obligation surface of
\autoref{thm:cauchy-entourage-completion-namecert-obligations}. It confines
every accepted Real-facing read to the displayed carrier rows.

Read the source side in row order. The Cauchy-entourage filter row $F$, the
entourage-base row $B$, and the regular Cauchy entourage-basis row $A$ must be
visible before the uniform completion handoff row $U$ can be opened.

Read the finite completion side next. The StreamName row $S$, RegSeqRat row
$R$, and DyadicRatCore row $D$ provide the common observation and tolerance
route that reaches the seal row $E$.

The structural rows preserve this route. Componentwise $\hsame$, displayed
$\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row record the
same finite handoff, so no omitted quotient, selector, choice, host-equality,
or ambient-completeness coordinate can be introduced downstream.
\end{proof}
