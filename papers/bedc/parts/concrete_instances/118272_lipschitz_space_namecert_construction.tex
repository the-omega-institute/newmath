\begin{theorem}[LipschitzSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lipschitz-space-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}

Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient function equality, selected representatives, open-cover compactness, countable choice, measure-theoretic regularity, or ambient Real completeness, none of those principles can be projected from the local NameCert surface.
