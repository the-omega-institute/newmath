\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DedekindCutBoundaryUp$}

Following Dedekind and Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis, the $\DedekindCutBoundaryUp$ packet records the L10 constraint that Dedekind cuts are comparison data, not a construction of $\RealUp$. It sits between the located cut surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedcut-namecert}, the Dedekind real comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dedekind-real-namecert}, the regular Cauchy comparison route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dedekind-cauchy-comparison-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the finite boundary at which a cut-shaped read may be compared with regular Cauchy data without becoming an ambient real construction.

\begin{definition}[Dedekind cut boundary carrier]
\label{def:dedekind-cut-boundary-carrier}
A $\DedekindCutBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(L,D,Q,R,S,Y,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedCutUp$ comparison row, recording only the displayed lower--upper separation witnesses used by finite consumers.
\item $D$ is the $\DedekindRealUp$ comparison row, read as a cut-shaped interface rather than as a constructor for $\RealUp$.
\item $Q$ is the $\DedekindCauchyComparisonUp$ boundary row comparing $L,D$ with regular Cauchy readback data.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the displayed regular rational windows.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row naming the finite inspected entries of the regular rational name.
\item $Y$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row carrying the displayed rational comparison budgets.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, visible only after the comparison route $L,D,Q,R,S,Y$ has been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the located-cut, Dedekind-real, comparison, regular-readback, stream-window, dyadic, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from cut-shaped observations through the comparison row and then through the regular Cauchy readback to the seal $E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DedekindCutBoundaryUp$, $\LocatedCutUp$, $\DedekindRealUp$, $\DedekindCauchyComparisonUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DedekindCutBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a quotient cut, host supremum, selected representative, trichotomy branch, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dedekind cut boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dedekind-cut-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DedekindCutBoundaryUp$ carrier $B=(L,D,Q,R,S,Y,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DedekindCutBoundaryUp}$ obligation surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the $\LocatedCutUp$ comparison row, $\DedekindRealUp$ cut-shaped comparison row, $\DedekindCauchyComparisonUp$ boundary row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\StreamNameUp$ finite windows, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance budgets, terminal $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dedekind-cut-boundary-carrier}. The analytic rows are $L,D,Q,R,S,Y,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The cut-shaped rows are comparison data. The located-cut row $L$ and the Dedekind-real row $D$ are not read directly as a real constructor; they first pass through the $\DedekindCauchyComparisonUp$ row $Q$.

The regular readback rows are finite. The row $R$ is inspected through the stream windows $S$ and dyadic tolerance ledger $Y$ before the terminal seal $E$ can be read.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier lists no quotient cut, selected representative, trichotomy branch, countable-choice selector, host supremum, or ambient completeness row, the displayed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dedekind cut boundary Real non-escape]
\label{thm:dedekind-cut-boundary-real-nonescape}
Every $\RealUp$ read exported by an accepted $\DedekindCutBoundaryUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\LocatedCutUp,\ \DedekindRealUp \longmapsto \DedekindCauchyComparisonUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
Thus the packet may compare cut-shaped observations with regular Cauchy data, but it cannot construct $\RealUp$ from Dedekind cuts or export quotient cut equality, selected representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, host supremum, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $B=(L,D,Q,R,S,Y,E,H,C,P,N)$. By \autoref{thm:dedekind-cut-boundary-namecert-obligations}, the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is downstream of the located-cut, Dedekind-real, comparison, regular-readback, stream-window, and dyadic rows.

The cut-shaped observations therefore cannot reach $E$ directly. They are first compared at $Q$, then read through the regular rational row $R$, the finite stream windows $S$, and the tolerance row $Y$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve this route but add no constructor coordinate. Hence a downstream consumer may use the packet as a boundary between Dedekind-cut comparison and regular Cauchy readback, not as a construction of $\RealUp$ from cuts.
\end{proof}
