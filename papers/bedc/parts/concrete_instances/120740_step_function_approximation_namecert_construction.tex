\begin{definition}[Step-function approximation carrier]
\label{def:step-function-approximation-carrier}
A $\StepFunctionApproximationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(I,V,D,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the finite interval partition row, listing adjacent bounded intervals by displayed endpoint names.
\item $V$ is the rational value row assigning one rational value token to each interval cell of $I$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint ledger, recording every endpoint, tolerance cell, and comparison budget used by the partition.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row that records which finite name windows are inspected before a cell value may be read.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, exposing the regular rational route through the finite windows and dyadic endpoint ledger.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the partition, rational values, endpoint ledger, windows, and regular readback have all been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $I,V,D,W,Q,R,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through partition selection, endpoint inspection, window readback, regular rational comparison, and Real sealing.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\StepFunctionApproximationUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\StepFunctionApproximationUp}$ row naming the displayed approximation packet.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for host-level function equality, arbitrary choice of partitions, quotient equality of reals, measure integration, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Step-function approximation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:step-function-approximation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\StepFunctionApproximationUp$ carrier $A=(I,V,D,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\StepFunctionApproximationUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite partition admission through $I$, rational value admission through $V$, dyadic endpoint and tolerance exposure through $D$, finite StreamName window exposure through $W$, RegSeqRat readback through $Q$, Real sealing through $R$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

No local obligation is stored outside those rows, and no accepted read can add host function equality, partition choice, quotient real equality, measure integration, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:step-function-approximation-carrier}. The rows $I,V,D,W,Q,R$ expose the analytic data: a finite interval partition, one rational value per cell, dyadic endpoints and tolerances, finite StreamName windows, regular rational readback, and the terminal Real seal.

The structural rows then keep that data local. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row preserve the same finite packet without adding a host function or a quotient real.

Since the carrier lists every available coordinate, the named rows exhaust the local obligation surface. Requests for arbitrary partition choice, measure integration, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness are refused before the packet can be exported to a Real-facing consumer.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Step-function approximation RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:step-function-approximation-regseqrat-handoff}

$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp \longrightarrow \StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \RealUp .
$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Next read the StreamName and RegSeqRat rows. The window row $W$ records the finite observation surface, and the readback row $Q$ turns that surface into a regular rational route without invoking a host function or an unbounded choice of partitions.
