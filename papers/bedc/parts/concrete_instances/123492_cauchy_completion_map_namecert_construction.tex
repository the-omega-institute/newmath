$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the regular Cauchy source row, read through $\RegSeqRatUp$ before any completion-map consumer receives a target value.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row recording the source observations inspected by the map.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the source row, the finite windows, and the completion-map replay.
\item $M$ is the displayed completion-map replay row, recording how the regular source and tolerance ledger are sent through the Bishop completion route and the $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ handoff.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,W,D,M,E,C,P,N$.

\begin{theorem}[CauchyCompletionMap NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-map-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompletionMapUp$ carrier
$$
K=(X,W,D,M,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionMapUp}$ surface consists exactly of five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission, requiring every public read to project from the displayed packet $K$;
\item source-window tolerance exposure, requiring $X,W,D$ to be read as the regular source, finite stream window, and dyadic tolerance ledger;
\item completion-map replay exactness, requiring $M$ to route the same source data through the Bishop regular Cauchy completion and completion-universal-property siblings;
\item Real-seal and continuation nonescape, requiring $E$ and $C$ to preserve the displayed completion-map route before any Real-facing consumer reads it;
\item structural naming, requiring componentwise transport through $H$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{enumerate}
These obligations export no quotient completion, selected limit, countable-choice selector, host equality of functions or streams, hidden completion object, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier $K=(X,W,D,M,E,H,C,P,N)$. The tuple itself supplies carrier admission and lists every row available to a public consumer.

Read the source side first. The row $X$ is the regular Cauchy source, $W$ records the finite source windows, and $D$ records the dyadic tolerances used by those windows.

Next read the completion-map replay row $M$. Its route is the displayed handoff from the regular Cauchy source through the Bishop regular Cauchy completion packet and the completion-universal-property packet; it is not a host function or quotient map outside the carrier.

The Real-facing boundary is then $E$, with continuation replay through $C$. These rows preserve the same finite map route before a consumer reaches the terminal $\RealUp$ seal.

Finally, $H,P,N$ transport, source, and name the same displayed rows. Since no coordinate stores quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, host function equality, hidden completion objects, or ambient Real completeness, the five listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
