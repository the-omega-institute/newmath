\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ParacompactLocallyFiniteUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-paracompact-locally-finite-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki and Dugundji on paracompact covers and locally finite refinements, the $\ParacompactLocallyFiniteUp$ packet records the finite BEDC handoff by which a displayed paracompact cover request is read as a locally finite cover ledger. It sits between the paracompact refinement carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-paracompact-namecert}, the locally finite cover surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locally-finite-cover-namecert}, and the indexed-open topology rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}. The packet names only the displayed cover request, refinement assignment, local finite windows, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert rows needed by later partition-of-unity consumers; it does not assert a full partition of unity, a choice-selected refinement theorem, arbitrary subcover extraction, host powerset equality, or open-cover compactness.

\begin{definition}[Paracompact locally finite carrier]
\label{def:paracompact-locally-finite-carrier}
A $\ParacompactLocallyFiniteUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(T,A,Q,R,L,W,I,H,C,S,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row, exposing the indexed open operations on which the cover request and local windows are read.
\item $A$ is the displayed open-cover request row, naming the cover cells before any refinement or local-finiteness read is available.
\item $Q$ is the $\ParacompactUp$ refinement source row, carrying the finite paracompact packet whose locally finite refinement ledger is being handed off.
\item $R$ is the refinement-assignment row, listing the displayed refinement cells and their assignment back into the cover request $A$.
\item $L$ is the $\LocallyFiniteCoverUp$ ledger row, recording the local neighbourhood windows and finite incidence lists used to read the refinement as locally finite.
\item $W$ is the coverage and containment row, recording that the displayed refinement covers the same source region as $A$ and that every refinement cell is contained in its assigned cover cell.
\item $I$ is the downstream interface row consumed by $\PartitionOfUnityUp$ only as support-window data, with no weight or sum-to-one coordinate.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology, cover request, paracompact source, refinement assignment, locally finite ledger, coverage, downstream interface, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a paracompact cover request through $T,A,Q,R,L,W,I,H,S,N$.
\item $S$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ParacompactLocallyFiniteUp$, $\ParacompactUp$, $\LocallyFiniteCoverUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\PartitionOfUnityUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ParacompactLocallyFiniteUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,A,Q,R,L,W,I,H,C,S,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected host-indexed refinement family, arbitrary subcover choice, partition-of-unity weights, countable choice, quotient open equality, or a global paracompactness theorem detached from the displayed packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Paracompact locally finite NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:paracompact-locally-finite-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ParacompactLocallyFiniteUp$ carrier
$$
P=(T,A,Q,R,L,W,I,H,C,S,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ParacompactLocallyFiniteUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the topology source, cover request, paracompact source, refinement assignment, locally finite ledger, coverage and containment row, downstream interface, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item topology and cover exposure, requiring every refinement read to display $T$ and $A$ before any locally finite ledger is consumed;
\item paracompact-source exposure, requiring the refinement assignment $R$ to be read through the displayed $\ParacompactUp$ packet $Q$;
\item local-finiteness exactness, requiring $L$ to expose the neighbourhood windows and finite incidence lists supplied by $\LocallyFiniteCoverUp$ before the refinement can be used as locally finite;
\item coverage and containment exactness, requiring $W$ to record both coverage of the displayed source region and containment inside assigned cover cells;
\item downstream-interface discipline, requiring $I$ to expose only support-window data for $\PartitionOfUnityUp$ and no weights or sum-to-one rows;
\item $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ stability, requiring $H,C,S,N$ to preserve exactly the listed rows.
\end{enumerate}
No local obligation for the paracompact-to-locally-finite handoff is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:paracompact-locally-finite-carrier}. The accepted tuple displays exactly the topology source, open-cover request, paracompact packet, refinement assignment, locally finite ledger, coverage and containment row, downstream support interface, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Topology and cover exposure are forced by row order. A refinement read starts with $T$ and $A$, so the handoff cannot begin from an unlogged host-indexed cover family.

Paracompact-source exposure is the projection to $Q$ and $R$. The refinement assignment is usable only as a row transported from the displayed $\ParacompactUp$ packet, with each refinement cell assigned back into the cover request.

Local-finiteness exactness is the projection to $L$. The row records neighbourhood windows and finite incidence lists in the sense of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locally-finite-cover-namecert}; the existence of a refinement family alone is not read as local finiteness.

Coverage and containment are read from $W$, while the downstream support boundary is read from $I$. These rows make the handoff available to later partition-of-unity work only as cover and support-window data, not as weights, sums, or selected functions.

Finally $H,C,S,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the classifier has no further coordinate, selected refinements, arbitrary subcover choice, partition-of-unity data, countable choice, quotient open equality, and detached global paracompactness cannot be exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Paracompact locally finite handoff route]
\label{thm:paracompact-locally-finite-handoff-route}
Every accepted $\ParacompactLocallyFiniteUp$ packet exports the finite route
$$
\TopologyUp \longrightarrow \ParacompactUp \longrightarrow \LocallyFiniteCoverUp
$$
before any partition-of-unity consumer may read support-window data from it. The exported route is a handoff from displayed paracompact refinement rows to displayed locally finite cover windows; it is not a choice-based refinement theorem, an arbitrary open-cover compactness theorem, a full partition of unity, or an ambient point-set topology construction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier of \autoref{def:paracompact-locally-finite-carrier}. The rows $T,A,Q$ display the topology, cover request, and paracompact source before the refinement assignment is available.

Use the obligation theorem \autoref{thm:paracompact-locally-finite-namecert-obligations}. It forces the refinement assignment $R$, locally finite ledger $L$, and coverage row $W$ to remain visible before a consumer reads the handoff.

The downstream interface row $I$ is only support-window data. It may be consumed by $\PartitionOfUnityUp$ after the locally finite route is displayed, but the carrier contains no weight, sum-to-one, selected-function, or arbitrary subcover coordinate.

The structural rows $H,C,S,N$ preserve this order, so every exported read factors through the displayed topology, paracompact refinement, and locally finite cover surfaces.
\end{proof}
