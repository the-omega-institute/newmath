\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedUniformCauchyFilterUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-located-uniform-cauchy-filter-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki on uniform spaces, Kelley on filters, and Bishop and Bridges on constructive completion, the $\LocatedUniformCauchyFilterUp$ packet records the finite BEDC route in which uniform Cauchy evidence and located filter evidence are read by the same completion-neighbour surface. It joins the uniform Cauchy filter rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-cauchy-filter-namecert} with the located filter rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-cauchy-filter-namecert}, while keeping the stream, dyadic, regular-rational, and real-seal faces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} explicit. The carrier is not a quotient of filters and does not select a limit; it only names the rows a completion-neighbour consumer may inspect.

\begin{definition}[Located uniform Cauchy filter carrier]
\label{def:located-uniform-cauchy-filter-carrier}
A $\LocatedUniformCauchyFilterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(U,F,L,S,R,D,T,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the $\UniformCauchyFilterUp$ row, carrying the displayed uniform-space, filter-base, entourage-Cauchy, stream-window, regular-rational, and completion-handoff surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-cauchy-filter-namecert}.
\item $F$ is the $\LocatedCauchyFilterUp$ row, carrying the displayed located-tail, basis-refinement, sequence-handoff, and real-seal surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-cauchy-filter-namecert}.
\item $L$ is the completion-neighbour location row, recording that the uniform entourage request and the located-tail request name the same finite neighbourhood read before any seal-facing consumer proceeds.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row supplied to the joined filter readback, using only the observation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row attached to those windows and to the same located-uniform request, as in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, carrying the dyadic entourage scale, located-ball slack, comparison cells, and radius conversions used by the joined request; it is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}.
\item $T$ is the located-uniform tail row, requiring the uniform Cauchy row $U$ and located filter row $F$ to meet the same displayed dyadic tolerance before $R$ or $E$ can be exported.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal-facing row, exposed only after $U,F,L,S,R,D,T$ have been displayed through the real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the uniform filter, located filter, neighbourhood location, stream, regular-rational, dyadic, tail, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion-neighbour request through $U,F,L,S,R,D,T,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedUniformCauchyFilterUp$, $\UniformCauchyFilterUp$, $\LocatedCauchyFilterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedUniformCauchyFilterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier compares accepted packets only through the displayed rows $U,F,L,S,R,D,T,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient filter equality, selected limits, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, host real equality, open-cover compactness, ultrafilters, or a second dyadic source.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniform Cauchy filter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-uniform-cauchy-filter-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedUniformCauchyFilterUp$ carrier
$$
X=(U,F,L,S,R,D,T,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedUniformCauchyFilterUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, uniform Cauchy filter exposure, located Cauchy filter exposure, completion-neighbour location, finite StreamName window readback, RegSeqRat regular-rational readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance accounting, located-uniform tail compatibility, terminal RealUp seal ordering, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming for the finite carrier of \autoref{def:located-uniform-cauchy-filter-carrier}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:located-uniform-cauchy-filter-carrier}. The analytic rows visible to the certificate are exactly $U,F,L,S,R,D,T,E$.

The uniform row $U$ is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-cauchy-filter-namecert}, and the located row $F$ is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-cauchy-filter-namecert}. The neighbourhood location row $L$ identifies the same finite completion-neighbour request for both rows before downstream readback begins.

The sequence-facing part of the route is finite. The window row $S$ uses \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the tolerance row $D$ uses \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the regular rational row $R$ uses \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. The tail row $T$ binds these rows to the displayed located-uniform request.

Finally the seal-facing row $E$ is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} only after $U,F,L,S,R,D,T$ have been displayed. The support rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same route, so no quotient filter, selected limit, countable-choice selector, host equality, ambient completeness, open-cover compactness, ultrafilter, or second dyadic source can be exported.
\end{proof}
