\begin{definition}[Theorem genome evolution carrier]
\label{def:theorem-genome-evolution-carrier}

$$
G=(F,R,D,Q,M,S,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the theorem-flow row, recording source theorem objects, proof loci, statement loci, certificate loci, and consumer-facing theorem roles.
\item $R$ is the recognizer row, recording which theorem-flow entries are admitted as genome-bearing theorem records.
\item $D$ is the theorem-DNA row, recording role-complete feature, circuit, classifier, stability, intervention, ledger, and seal surfaces.
\item $Q$ is the classifier-shift row, recording the before and after classifier phenotype when a mutation claims discovery-facing movement.
\item $M$ is the mutation row, recording compression, recombination, certificate insertion, ledger exposure, and classifier-shifting genome moves.
\item $S$ is the selection row, recording the score, taste-gate, novelty, reuse, and downstream-consumer coordinates used to rank genome moves.
\item $L$ is the ledger row, recording which mutation or selection reads are admitted, refused, or held as obligations.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for theorem-flow, recognizer, theorem-DNA, classifier-shift, mutation, selection, ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a theorem-genome audit request through $F,R,D,Q,M,S,L,H,P,N$.

\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for unledgered discovery, score-only discovery, theorem sentence identity as genome identity, proof compression as classifier shift, or a mutation whose before and after classifier rows are not displayed.

\begin{theorem}[Theorem genome evolution NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:theorem-genome-evolution-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\TheoremGenomeEvolutionUp$ carrier $G=(F,R,D,Q,M,S,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\TheoremGenomeEvolutionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, theorem-flow exposure, recognizer admission, theorem-DNA exactness, classifier-shift visibility, mutation classification, selection accounting, ledger non-escape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:theorem-genome-evolution-carrier}. The theorem-facing rows are $F,R,D,Q,M,S,L$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The theorem-flow and recognizer rows identify the genome-bearing theorem records. The theorem-DNA row supplies the role-complete loci before any mutation or selection row can be inspected.

Classifier-shift visibility is supplied by $Q$. A mutation row $M$ may be read as discovery-facing only when its before and after classifier phenotypes are displayed there; compression or recombination without $Q$ remains a genome move but not a classifier-shift claim.

Selection accounting is supplied by $S$ and bounded by the ledger row $L$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite route, so no unledgered discovery or score-only discovery can escape.
\end{proof}
