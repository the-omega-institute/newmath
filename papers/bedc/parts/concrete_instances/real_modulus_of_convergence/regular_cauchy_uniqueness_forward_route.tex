\begin{theorem}[Real modulus RegularCauchyUniqueness forward route]
\label{thm:real-modulus-regular-cauchy-uniqueness-forward-route}
Every $\RegularCauchyUniquenessUp$ consumer that compares two Real representatives through a $\RealModulusOfConvergenceUp$ packet first reads the displayed dyadic tolerance row, the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal rows. The comparison route is
$$
\RealModulusOfConvergenceUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp \longmapsto \RegularCauchyUniquenessUp .
$$
Thus the uniqueness consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-uniqueness-namecert} may compare representatives only after the finite modulus lattice has exposed its tolerance, window, readback, and seal data.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Read the $\RealModulusOfConvergenceUp$ carrier as the finite packet whose tolerance, window, readback, monotone ledger, and Real seal rows are already displayed in the local NameCert construction. These rows determine which tail comparison is available to a downstream consumer before any representative comparison is formed.

The $\RegularCauchyUniquenessUp$ consumer then uses the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, the same $\StreamNameUp$ finite window, and the same $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff route before it reaches the $\RealUp$ seal. Therefore the uniqueness comparison is not a direct quotient assertion on Real representatives; it is a routed read through the modulus packet and its finite sibling rows.
\end{proof}
