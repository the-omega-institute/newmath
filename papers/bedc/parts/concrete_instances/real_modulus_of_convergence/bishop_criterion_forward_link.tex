\begin{theorem}[Real modulus Bishop criterion forward link]
\label{thm:real-modulus-of-convergence-bishop-criterion-forward-link}
Every $\BishopCompletionCriterionUp$ consumer of an accepted $\RealModulusOfConvergenceUp$ packet must first display the finite convergence route
$$
\CauchyModulusUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
This forward link consumes the sibling criterion surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-completion-criterion-namecert} and the sibling modulus surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-convergence-modulus-namecert}.
The criterion may then consume the local convergence-modulus ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and $\NameCert$ rows, but it cannot read a selected limit, quotient stream equality, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, detached completion object, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness datum from the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:real-modulus-of-convergence-carrier}. The carrier exposes the dyadic tolerance row, finite StreamName window, regular-rational readback, monotone convergence-modulus ledger, Real seal, and structural rows before any completion criterion can consume it.

Use the Cauchy-modulus handoff and tail-stability routes of \autoref{thm:real-modulus-convergence-cauchy-modulus-handoff} and \autoref{thm:real-modulus-of-convergence-tail-stability}. They force a criterion request through $\CauchyModulusUp$, the displayed $\StreamNameUp$ window, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback before the Real seal is available.

The dyadic tolerance and Real-seal rows then remain visible by \autoref{thm:real-modulus-of-convergence-window-composition}. The Bishop completion criterion receives exactly that finite route plus the local transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows, with no hidden selected limit or ambient completion principle.
\end{proof}
