\paragraph{Sibling dependency route.}
The $\GrothendieckGroupUp$ difference-pair congruence is read only after its
source monoid, target abelian-group, additive semiring, and free-generator
neighbours have been exposed. Concretely, the route consumes the monoid carrier
of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-monoid-namecert}, the abelian-group target
surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-abgroup-namecert}, the additive
semiring route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-semiring-namecert}, and the
generator discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-freemonoid-namecert}.
These sibling references are dependency edges for the displayed
difference-pair and universal-handoff rows, not additional theorem content.
