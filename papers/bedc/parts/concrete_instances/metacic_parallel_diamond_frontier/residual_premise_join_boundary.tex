\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC residual-premise join boundary]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-premise-join-boundary}
Every candidate-mediated local join read exported by an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet must display the critical-pair envelope, residual-substitution dependency route, retained premise row, finite residual witness row, candidate-normalization scope, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row before it exports a join. The boundary does not claim premise-free confluence, global normalization, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the critical-pair envelope and residual-substitution dependency route. These rows expose the redex overlap, residual substitutions, and finite witness data that make the local join a displayed packet rather than an ambient confluence assertion.

The retained premise row is read before the candidate-normalization scope. Thus the join remains mediated by the candidate and by the visible residual premise; it is not promoted to a premise-free statement.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve exactly that finite route. Since the carrier has no rows for global normalization, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency, none of those claims is exported by the boundary.
\end{proof}
