\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond residual-choice boundary]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-choice-boundary}
Every candidate-mediated parallel-diamond read of an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet consumes residual choice only as a displayed finite ledger row on the route
$$
P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N .
$$
The residual-choice row is read after the critical-pair envelope and before the residual-join handoff: the critical-pair rows $K,J$ and residual-substitution premise $P$ expose the finite envelope, the residual ledger keeps the chosen residual windows visible, and the residual join is consumed only through the same bounded frontier. The boundary does not provide host choice, global confluence, strong normalization, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the critical-pair envelope of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-critical-pair-envelope}. It exposes candidate rows and residual-substitution data as part of one finite frontier route before any parallel-diamond request may be consumed.

Next use the residual-substitution dependency route of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-substitution-dependency-route}. That route keeps the retained premise $P$ visible before the critical-pair, candidate-join, residual-frontier, closed-checker, and obstruction rows.

Apply finite residual witness consumption from \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-finite-residual-witness-consumption}. The local residual witness is consumed through $P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N$, so the residual-choice row is a displayed finite ledger entry rather than an external selector.

Finally attach the retained-premise boundary of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-retained-premise-boundary}. It keeps the premise, candidate-normalization scope, residual-substitution route, finite residual witness, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming visible before the residual join. Since no cited row stores host choice, global confluence, strong normalization, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency, none of those endpoints is exported by the residual-choice boundary.
\end{proof}
