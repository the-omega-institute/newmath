\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond residual-join obligations]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-join-obligations}
Every frontier residual join exposed by an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet must display the critical-pair source row, retained residual-substitution window, candidate-normalization budget, local join witness, obstruction-retention row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, and $\NameCert$ provenance before a consumer may read the join. Equivalently, the obligation surface is the carrier-local spine
$$
P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N ,
$$
where $P$ is still the residual-substitution premise, $K,J,R$ give the critical-pair and candidate frontier rows, $S,C,B,O$ give the bounded checker, closed-normal, budget, and obstruction rows, and $H,T,G,N$ give transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The surface is a bounded candidate-mediated residual-join obligation. It is not a parameter-transport equivalence theorem, global confluence theorem, unbounded normalization theorem, subject-reduction theorem, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel-consistency certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First read residual-join locality,
\autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-join-locality}. It places every accepted residual join inside the finite frontier spine and keeps the critical-pair, retained premise, local join, bounded checker, obstruction, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows visible.

Next apply the residual-scope route of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-scope}. This prevents the retained residual-substitution premise from being discharged after the local join is consumed, and it keeps $P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N$ in the same carrier-local packet.

Now use the residual-normalization window and candidate-mediated strong-normalization handoff, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-normalization-window} and \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-sn-handoff}. These rows expose the candidate-normalization budget and local witness only after the typed residual window, bounded checker rows, and obstruction row have been displayed.

Finally repack the displayed rows as the residual-join obligation surface. Since the packet contains no coordinate for parameter-transport equivalence, global confluence, unbounded normalization, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency, the exposed join remains a local obligation over the stated finite frontier spine.
\end{proof}
