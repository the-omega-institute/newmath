\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond frontier residual-row visibility]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-row-visibility}
Any accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ consumer that exports a parallel-diamond handoff must keep the residual-substitution row $P$, local join row $J$, typed residual budget row $R$, componentwise transport row $H$, displayed replay row $T$, provenance row $G$, and local $\NameCert$ row $N$ visible. The consumer cannot cite bounded normal equality, closed checker data, or confluence audit packets as a replacement for the residual row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the frontier carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes the residual-substitution premise, candidate or local join, residual-frontier budget, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows as displayed coordinates rather than as ambient metadata.

Cite the residual-substitution boundary and local-join ledger sibling routes. The boundary keeps the substitution premise explicit, while the local-join ledger reads the join only after the residual rows and bounded-check rows have been exposed.

The remaining claim is nonescape of the carrier rows. Bounded normal equality, closed checker data, and confluence audit packets can be consumed only through the displayed frontier route; none supplies a second coordinate that discharges or hides the residual-substitution row.
\end{proof}
