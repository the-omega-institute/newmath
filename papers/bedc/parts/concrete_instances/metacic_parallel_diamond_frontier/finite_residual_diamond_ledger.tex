\begin{definition}[MetaCIC finite residual-diamond ledger carrier]
\label{def:metacic-finite-residual-diamond-ledger-carrier}
A finite residual-diamond ledger for $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ is a carrier-local subpacket
$$
\Lambda=(K,P,J,B,O,H,T,G,N)
$$
read from an accepted frontier packet $F=(P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N)$ with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the critical-pair source row naming the bounded local peak under inspection.
\item $P$ is the retained residual-substitution premise row attached to that source.
\item $J$ is the candidate join witness row, read only as a proposed local join for the displayed peak.
\item $B$ is the bounded checker status row for the finite residual diamond request.
\item $O$ is the obstruction-retention row recording any still-visible blocker to an unconditional handoff.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the ledger rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same finite residual request.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance row for the frontier packet and its sibling confluence consumers.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp}$ row naming the ledger as carrier-local data.
\end{enumerate}
The ledger stores no premise-free confluence theorem, no subject-reduction theorem, no evaluator equality, no quotient term equality, and no kernel-consistency certificate.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC finite residual-diamond ledger local join obligations]
\label{thm:metacic-finite-residual-diamond-ledger-local-join-obligations}
Every local residual-diamond read of an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet factors through a finite ledger $\Lambda=(K,P,J,B,O,H,T,G,N)$ of \autoref{def:metacic-finite-residual-diamond-ledger-carrier}. The read may cite the ledger as bounded local join evidence under the retained residual-substitution premise and visible obstruction row, but it cannot be cited as an unconditional parallel diamond, full Church--Rosser theorem, subject-reduction theorem, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel-consistency certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the critical-pair envelope of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-critical-pair-envelope}. It supplies the bounded source row $K$ and keeps the peak inside the finite frontier packet.

Next read the residual-substitution dependency route of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-substitution-dependency-route}. This places the retained premise row $P$ before the candidate join can be consumed, so the ledger cannot erase the premise.

Now use the candidate diamond budget route of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-seed-route} together with residual-join locality, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-join-locality}. These rows expose the candidate join $J$ and bounded checker status $B$ only as a local finite route through the accepted frontier.

Finally apply the retained-premise closure budget of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-retained-premise-closure}. The obstruction row $O$ and structural rows $H,T,G,N$ remain displayed, so the resulting ledger is a bounded residual-diamond obligation surface rather than any of the refused global confluence or kernel endpoints.
\end{proof}
