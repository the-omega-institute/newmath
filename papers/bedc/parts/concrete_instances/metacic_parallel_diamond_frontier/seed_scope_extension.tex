\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond frontier seed scope]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-seed-scope}
Before any candidate-mediated strong-normalization route is exported from an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet, the only seed-scope reads are finite critical-pair windows, residual substitution rows, candidate-normalization rows, and local join ledgers. In carrier notation these are the displayed rows $P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N$ read as retained frontier data, not as subject reduction, unrestricted confluence, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, global normalization, residual-substitution discharge, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier from \autoref{def:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-carrier}. The finite critical-pair windows are carried by $K$ and $R$, the residual-substitution row remains $P$, and the candidate-normalization read is routed through $J,C,B$ with the obstruction row $O$ still visible.

Apply the seed-obligation and seed-readiness routes, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-seed-obligations} and \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-seed-readiness}. They force every public seed read through the displayed frontier rows before any confluence-audit or normalization-facing handoff can be inspected.

The local join ledger is then completed only by transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows $H,T,G,N$. Since no carrier coordinate exports subject reduction, unrestricted confluence, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, global normalization, residual-substitution discharge, or kernel consistency, the stated rows are exactly the seed-scope reads available before the candidate-mediated strong-normalization route is exported.
\end{proof}
