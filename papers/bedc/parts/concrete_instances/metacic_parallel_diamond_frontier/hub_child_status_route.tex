\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond hub child status route]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-hub-child-status-route}
The seed, bridged, and mature status rows for $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ are read only through the child carrier route. A status reader must first expose the carrier rows of \autoref{def:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-carrier}, the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-obligations}, the seed closure status of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-seed-closure-status}, and the closurestatus export and guard routes of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-closurestatus-export} and \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-closurestatus-guard}. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the child carrier. The displayed route of \autoref{def:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-carrier} is the finite packet
$$
P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N
$$
and the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-obligations} names those rows as the visible premise, critical-pair, candidate-join, residual-frontier, checker, bounded-check, obstruction, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data.

Next read the seed status through \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-seed-closure-status}. That theorem keeps the seed-level status attached to the same displayed frontier packet rather than to a detached confluence or consistency assertion.

Finally apply the closurestatus export route and the closurestatus guard. They force bridged and mature status readers through the retained child rows before any status marker is consumed. Since the cited rows contain no coordinate for residual-substitution discharge, subject reduction, full Church--Rosser, evaluator equality, quotient equality, or kernel consistency, the hub status route exports only the guarded child carrier.
\end{proof}
