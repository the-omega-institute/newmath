\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond retained-premise scope]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-retained-premise-scope}
Every retained premise used by a $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ read must be displayed as one of the residual-pair, candidate-normalization, local-join, closed-checker, or obstruction rows already named in the frontier carrier
$$
P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N .
$$
In particular, a retained premise can be consumed only as carrier-local evidence through those rows, and cannot be read as an unconditional closed parallel-diamond theorem, subject-reduction theorem, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel-consistency certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the frontier carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-carrier}. The residual-pair and premise-retention rows are $P,K,R$, the candidate-normalization row is $J$, the local-join and closed-checker rows are $S,C,B$, and the obstruction row is $O$.

Apply the residual boundary route of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-substitution-dependency-route}. It places every residual-pair use behind the retained premise row and keeps the critical-pair and residual-frontier rows visible.

Next use the candidate-normalization and local-join boundaries, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-normalization-scope} and \autoref{thm:metacic-finite-residual-diamond-ledger-local-join-obligations}. These theorems allow candidate and local-join data to be read only through the displayed finite route, with the retained premise still attached.

Finally apply the retained-premise discharge boundary and obstruction ledger, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-retained-premise-discharge-boundary} and \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-obstruction-budget}. The closed-checker and obstruction rows remain visible through the structural rows $H,T,G,N$, so no consumer can reread the retained premise as an unconditional closed parallel diamond or any of the refused kernel-level endpoints.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond local retained-premise factorization]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-local-diamond-retained-premise-factorization}
Any local diamond read from an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet that consumes candidate join and residual-frontier rows factors through the retained premise row, critical-pair row, candidate-normalization row, local-join row, closed-checker row, closed-normal fragment row, bounded-check row, obstruction row, and structural rows
$$
P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N .
$$
The factorization is conditional on those displayed rows. It is not full Church--Rosser, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, global normalization, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with retained-premise scope, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-retained-premise-scope}. It requires every premise used by the local diamond read to remain one of the displayed carrier rows.

Apply the residual-substitution dependency route of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-substitution-dependency-route}. The route places the retained premise row $P$ before the critical-pair, candidate join, residual-frontier, closed-checker, and obstruction rows can be consumed.

Use candidate-normalization scope from \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-normalization-scope}. The candidate row $J$ remains inside the same conditional spine rather than becoming an external normalization theorem.

Apply the finite residual-diamond ledger local-join obligations of \autoref{thm:metacic-finite-residual-diamond-ledger-local-join-obligations}. They read the local join, closed checker, closed-normal fragment, bounded-check, and obstruction rows only after the retained premise and residual-frontier data have been displayed.

Finally use the retained-premise discharge boundary of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-retained-premise-discharge-boundary}. Since the structural rows $H,T,G,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet, the local diamond read cannot be promoted to any refused global metatheorem or kernel endpoint.
\end{proof}
