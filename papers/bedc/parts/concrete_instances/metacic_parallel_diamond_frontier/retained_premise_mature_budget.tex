\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond retained-premise mature budget]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-retained-premise-mature-budget}
Every candidate-mediated SN/confluence consumer of $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ must carry the retained premise budget $P,J,R,B,O$ through the public certificate, finite residual witness consumption, candidate SN residual budget, public frontier budget, and bridged export before reading any parallel-diamond handoff. The carried budget records the residual-substitution premise row, candidate-join row, residual-frontier row, bounded-check row, and obstruction row as finite packet data; it is not residual-substitution discharge, unrestricted Church--Rosser, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, global conversion decidability, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the public certificate of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-public-certificate}. It exposes the residual-substitution premise, critical-pair, candidate-join, residual-frontier, closed-checker, closed-normal, bounded-check, obstruction, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows as one finite public handoff.

Next consume the finite residual witness through \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-finite-residual-witness-consumption}. That route makes a candidate-mediated SN/confluence consumer read $P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N$ before it can inspect the frontier, so the retained rows $P,J,R,B,O$ remain visible inside the larger finite route.

Apply the candidate SN residual budget and public frontier budget, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-sn-residual-budget} and \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-public-frontier-budget}. Together they isolate $P,J,R,B,O$ as the budget that must be carried forward before any public diamond handoff is read.

Finally apply the bridged export of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-bridged-export}. The bridged read may expose the guarded frontier to an external consumer only while preserving the same retained evidence rows and the structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming route. Therefore the mature budget cannot be reread as residual-substitution discharge, unrestricted Church--Rosser, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, global conversion decidability, or kernel consistency.
\end{proof}
