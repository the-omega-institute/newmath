\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond critical-path consumer route]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-critical-path-consumer-route}
Every critical-path consumer of an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet must expose the residual-substitution premise row $P$, critical-pair row $K$, candidate-join row $J$, closed-checker row $S$, closed-normal row $C$, bounded-check row $B$, and obstruction row $O$ before any local confluence handoff is public. The required consumers are the critical-path packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metaciccriticalpath-namecert}, the confluence audit packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-confluence-audit-packet-namecert}, and the normalization-confluence bridge of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-normalization-confluence-bridge-namecert}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-carrier}. Its confluence-facing coordinates include $P,K,J,S,C,B,O$, so none of the three consumer chapters can read the frontier through a smaller public row set.

The $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ consumer receives the residual-substitution premise, critical-pair, and obstruction rows as route pressure rather than as a discharged local-confluence theorem. The $\MetaCICConfluenceAuditPacketUp$ consumer receives the candidate-join, closed-checker, and bounded-check rows as audit data before it may inspect the local handoff. The $\MetacicNormalizationConfluenceBridgeUp$ consumer receives the closed-normal row and the same obstruction boundary as bridge-facing evidence.

Thus the critical-path, confluence-audit, and normalization-confluence consumers share the full retained frontier. A local confluence handoff is public only after those rows have been displayed, and the route still does not assert residual-substitution compatibility, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{proof}
