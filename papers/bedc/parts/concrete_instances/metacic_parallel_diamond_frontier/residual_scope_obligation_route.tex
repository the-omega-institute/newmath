\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond frontier residual scope]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-scope}
Every residual-scope consumer of an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet must preserve the finite rows
$$
P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N .
$$
The consumer may read residual-join locality, the residual grid, locality scope, and the obstruction budget, but it may not discharge residual substitution, subject reduction, global confluence, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with residual-join locality, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-join-locality}. It forces every residual-join read through the displayed frontier rows and keeps the packet local to the accepted carrier.

Refine the consumer route through the residual grid and locality scope, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-grid} and \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-join-locality-scope}. These theorems separate the residual-facing rows $P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O$ from the structural rows $H,T,G,N$ while keeping both groups inside one finite packet.

Finally apply the residual obstruction budget, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-obstruction-budget}. It keeps the retained premise and obstruction rows visible, so a residual-scope consumer has no route to discharge residual substitution or to promote the local frontier to subject reduction, global confluence, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{proof}
