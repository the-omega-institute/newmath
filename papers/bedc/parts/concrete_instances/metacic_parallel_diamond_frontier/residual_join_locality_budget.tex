$$

$$

Those child files identify $P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N$ as the complete local surface and keep the obstruction row visible. Since no child in this route exports a global confluence, normalization, conversion, subject-reduction, evaluator, quotient, substitution-discharge, or consistency coordinate, the handoff remains a carrier-local budget.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond frontier residual budget handoff]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-budget-handoff}

$$

\begin{proof}

\end{proof}
