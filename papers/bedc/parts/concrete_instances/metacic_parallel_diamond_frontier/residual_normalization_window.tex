\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond residual-normalization window]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-normalization-window}
Every accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet whose displayed rows expose
$$
P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N
$$
may hand a candidate-mediated residual peak to a normalization consumer only through the retained residual-substitution premise row $P$, the candidate-normalization row $J$, the residual-frontier row $R$, the closed-checker and closed-normal rows $S,C$, and the obstruction row $O$, with the structural rows $H,T,G,N$ still visible. The window does not prove strong normalization, subject reduction, unrestricted confluence, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-carrier}. Its public rows expose the residual-substitution premise, critical-pair data, candidate-normalization row, residual-frontier row, closed-checker row, closed-normal row, bounded-check row, obstruction row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Use the candidate-normalization scope of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-normalization-scope}. It forces a normalization-facing read of $J$ to keep $P$ and $O$ visible and to remain inside the same finite spine.

Next consume the finite residual witness and candidate-mediated strong-normalization handoff, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-finite-residual-witness-consumption} and \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-sn-handoff}. These rows allow a residual peak to be presented to the consumer only after $R,S,C$ and the bounded obstruction data have been displayed.

Finally apply residual-join locality, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-join-locality}. Since the obstruction row remains retained and no carrier coordinate discharges $P$ or promotes $J$ to a global theorem, the exported object is only the stated residual-normalization window.
\end{proof}
