\end{theorem}

Project the frontier carrier from \autoref{def:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-carrier}. The branch and candidate data are visible only through the critical-pair row $K$, candidate-join row $J$, residual-frontier row $R$, and obstruction row $O$, with the structural rows attached.

Finally read the MetaCIC critical-path packet as a socket boundary. The critical-path consumer receives the residual premise, residual windows, and obstruction rows as readback data, so the seed-readiness route exposes the two branches and common join candidate without discharging normalization, subject reduction, unrestricted confluence, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{proof}
