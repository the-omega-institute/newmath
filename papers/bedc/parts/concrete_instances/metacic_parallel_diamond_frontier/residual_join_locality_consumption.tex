\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-join-locality-consumption}

$$

$$
where $P$ retains the residual-substitution premise, $K,J,R$ expose the critical-pair and candidate rows, $S,C,B,O$ expose the typed window, residual witness, bounded checker, and obstruction rows, and $H,T,G,N$ provide transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. \end{theorem}

Next read the residual-substitution dependency route of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-substitution-dependency-route}. This keeps the retained premise row $P$ inside the same finite packet while the candidate join and residual frontier rows are opened.

Now consume the residual-join locality rows of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-join-locality} and the budget of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-residual-join-locality-budget}. These identify $P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N$ as the carrier-local residual surface and keep the obstruction row visible.

Finally attach the candidate-normalization scope of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-normalization-scope} and finite residual witness consumption from \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-finite-residual-witness-consumption}. The candidate row and bounded witness ledger are therefore read before the frontier $\NameCert$ row, so the exported handoff remains the displayed residual-locality budget rather than a global confluence, subject-reduction, evaluator-equality, quotient-equality, substitution-discharge, or consistency theorem.
\end{proof}
