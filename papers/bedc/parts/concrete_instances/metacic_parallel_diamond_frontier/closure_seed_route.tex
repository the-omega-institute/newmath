\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond frontier closure seed route]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-closure-seed-route}
Every seed-closure read for an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet must expose the residual-substitution premise row, critical-pair row, candidate-join row, residual-frontier row, closed checker seal, closed-normal fragment, bounded-check row, and obstruction row before any conditional parallel-diamond route is consumed. The route is a finite seed surface for the local frontier; it does not prove residual substitution, subject reduction, full Church--Rosser, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-carrier}. The residual-substitution premise, critical-pair data, candidate joins, residual frontier, closed checker, closed-normal fragment, bounded-check row, and obstruction row are displayed carrier coordinates before a confluence consumer can read any diamond-shaped conclusion.

Apply \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-obligations}. The obligation surface requires carrier admission, row visibility, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming for exactly those finite frontier rows.

Use \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-handoff} and \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-critical-pair-envelope}. These keep the conditional handoff downstream of the displayed critical-pair and residual rows, and they prevent the handoff from replacing the obstruction budget by a global confluence theorem.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-critical-pair-obstruction-budget}. The consumed route is therefore finite and seed-local: it records the displayed obstruction frontier and does not discharge residual substitution, subject reduction, full Church--Rosser, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{proof}
