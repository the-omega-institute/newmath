\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-substitution-dependency-route}

Finally apply the premise-retention and discharge-obstruction surfaces already present in the frontier chapter. They keep $P$ and the obstruction row visible for every consumer, so the dependency route remains a premise boundary rather than any of the refused confluence endpoints.
\end{proof}
