\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC parallel-diamond candidate-mediated SN handoff]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-sn-handoff}
Every candidate-mediated strong-normalization budget consumed by an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet must expose, in order, the critical-pair envelope, residual-substitution row, candidate-normalization scope, finite residual witness, and residual-row visibility surface before any confluence or decidability consumer reads the frontier. The consumed route is the finite spine
$$
P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N ,
$$
with $P$ retained as the residual-substitution premise, $K,J,R$ retained as the local critical-pair and residual-frontier rows, $S,C,B,O$ retained as the bounded checker and obstruction rows, and $H,T,G,N$ retained as transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. The handoff is therefore a candidate-mediated frontier budget, not a proof of unrestricted confluence, unbounded strong normalization, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, decidability of all reductions, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First read the critical-pair envelope through \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-critical-pair-envelope}. It exposes $K,J,R,S,C,B,O$ as a bounded local-overlap surface and keeps the frontier carrier rows displayed.

Next read the residual-substitution dependency route of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-substitution-dependency-route}. That route places the retained premise row $P$ before the candidate join, residual frontier, closed-checker, and obstruction rows can be consumed.

The candidate-normalization scope of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-normalization-scope} keeps $J$ inside the same conditional spine. It prevents a normalization consumer from hiding $P$ or retyping the candidate row as premise-free confluence.

Now consume the finite residual witness of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-finite-residual-witness-consumption}. The witness reads typed windows, residual frontier data, bounded local-join checks, candidate evidence when present, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before any closed parallel-diamond route can use it.

Finally apply residual-row visibility, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-row-visibility}. The residual row remains visible through $H,T,G,N$, so confluence and decidability consumers receive only the displayed candidate-mediated budget rather than a discharged substitution theorem, global normalization theorem, evaluator equality, quotient equality, or kernel-consistency certificate.
\end{proof}

$$

The handoff keeps the residual-substitution premise $P$, critical-pair envelope, candidate row, bounded checker rows, obstruction row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming visible before any confluence or decidability consumer reads the frontier.
