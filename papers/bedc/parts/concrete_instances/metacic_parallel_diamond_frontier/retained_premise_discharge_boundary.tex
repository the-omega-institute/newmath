Apply the retained-premise closure and mature budgets. Those budgets require the residual-substitution premise and the obstruction-facing bounded check to remain visible through candidate-mediated diamond reads.

The listed off-table metatheorems are not rows of the packet. Since the carrier exports only the displayed finite evidence and structural transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows, the local read records a discharge boundary rather than a full residual-substitution or confluence theorem.
