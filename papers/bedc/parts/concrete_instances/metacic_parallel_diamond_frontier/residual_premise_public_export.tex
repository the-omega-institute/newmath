\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC residual premise public export]
\label{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-premise-public-export}
Any public confluence handoff for an accepted $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet must expose the residual-substitution premise row $P$ before reading the critical-pair row $K$, candidate-join row $J$, residual-frontier row $R$, closed-checker row $S$, closed-normal fragment row $C$, bounded-check row $B$, obstruction row $O$, transport row $H$, replay row $T$, provenance row $G$, and local naming row $N$. The public export cannot retype the packet as premise-free Church--Rosser, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-obligations}. It displays the finite packet $P,K,J,R,S,C,B,O,H,T,G,N$ and keeps $P$ as a retained residual-substitution premise rather than as a discharged theorem.

Apply the handoff and critical-pair exhaustion theorems, \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-handoff} and \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-critical-pair-exhaustion}. They force the confluence-facing read to pass through $P$ before the critical-pair, candidate-join, residual-frontier, checker, closed-normal, bounded-check, and obstruction rows can be consumed.

Finally use the obstruction triad of \autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-obstruction-triad}. The obstruction row $O$ records exactly the endpoints not supplied by the packet, so the public handoff cannot be reread as Church--Rosser, subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient equality, or kernel consistency without dropping a displayed premise.
\end{proof}
