\label{def:abel-tauberian-boundary-carrier}

$$

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $L$ is the $\AbelLimitTheoremUp$ boundary row, recording the Abel-limit route available at the endpoint.
\item $T$ is the $\TauberianRemainderUp$ control row, recording the finite remainder budget that licenses the endpoint read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, presenting the regular rational windows consumed by the Real-facing boundary.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, Abel-limit, Tauberian-remainder, readback, Real-seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Abel-Tauberian boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:abel-tauberian-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AbelTauberianBoundaryUp$ carrier
$$
A=(S,L,T,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\AbelTauberianBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for $A=(S,L,T,R,E,H,C,P,N)$;
\item source and Abel-limit exposure through $S$ and $L$;
\item Tauberian remainder exposure through $T$;
\item $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback and $\RealUp$ seal nonescape through $R$ and $E$;
\item componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:abel-tauberian-boundary-carrier}. The rows $S,L,T,R,E,H,C,P,N$ are the complete finite packet.

The analytic rows are read in order. The source row supplies the inspected series or stream schedule, the Abel-limit row exposes the endpoint route, and the Tauberian remainder row supplies the finite control that prevents the endpoint read from becoming a selected sum or a quotient-stream assertion.

The readback and seal rows then carry the endpoint to the Real-facing boundary. Since $R$ and $E$ are displayed coordinates, the packet can export only a regular rational readback sealed by $\RealUp$.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. No carrier coordinate names host equality, countable choice, ambient completeness, or an off-packet summation method, so the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
