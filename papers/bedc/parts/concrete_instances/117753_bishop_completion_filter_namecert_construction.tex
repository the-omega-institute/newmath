\begin{definition}[Bishop completion-filter carrier]
\label{def:bishop-completion-filter-carrier}

$$

\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ source row, exposing the filter-level Cauchy data before any completion read is attempted.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedCauchyFilterUp$ row, recording the located basis-refinement and tail-decision data consumed by the Bishop-style completion route.
\item $O$ is the $\RegularCauchyCompletionObjectUp$ row, naming the regular Cauchy completion object reached from the located filter source.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformSpaceCompletionFilterUp$ row, recording the uniform entourage and filter-completion handoff for the same displayed source.

\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row inspected by the filter, object, uniform, and dense-image ledgers.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from those finite windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $F,L,O,U,D,W,R$ are visible.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $F,L,O,U,D,W,R,E,C,P,N$.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop completion-filter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-completion-filter-namecert-obligations}

$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCompletionFilterUp}$ surface consists exactly of Cauchy-filter source admission, located-filter exposure, regular completion-object admission, uniform completion-filter handoff, dense-embedding route exposure, StreamName window admission, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal nonescape, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-completion-filter-carrier}. The analytic rows are $F,L,O,U,D,W,R,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

Read the completion source first. The Cauchy filter row $F$ is refined by the located filter row $L$, and the regular completion-object row $O$ can be consumed only after those source rows are visible.

Next read the completion-facing handoffs. The uniform completion-filter row $U$ and dense embedding row $D$ preserve the same displayed source while the finite window row $W$ and regular rational readback row $R$ provide the observable data used by completion consumers.

Finally the Real seal row $E$ is downstream of $F,L,O,U,D,W,R$. The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve the same finite route, so no quotient filter, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, hidden cofinal subfilter, host equality, or ambient completeness theorem can be exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}
