\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SobolevPoincareFiniteEnergyUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sobolev-poincare-finite-energy-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Sobolev, Poincare, Brezis, and Evans, the $\SobolevPoincareFiniteEnergyUp$ packet records the finite-energy handoff by which a displayed Sobolev packet and a displayed Poincare-control packet jointly expose bounded-domain energy data. It sits over the weak-derivative carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sobolev-namecert}, the norm-control estimate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-inequality-namecert}, the energy ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-constructive-sobolev-energy-namecert}, the rational readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite energy-control certificate; it does not import Hahn--Banach separation, measure completion, selected representatives, quotient equality of functions, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Sobolev--Poincare finite-energy carrier]
\label{def:sobolev-poincare-finite-energy-carrier}
A $\SobolevPoincareFiniteEnergyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(S,Q,D,W,Y,A,R,Z,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\SobolevUp$ weak-derivative source row whose function, domain, codomain, magnitude, and derivative-window data are exposed before any energy read.
\item $Q$ is the $\PoincareInequalityUp$ control row, recording the bounded-domain, boundary, or zero-mean route used to compare the displayed function magnitude with its derivative magnitude.
\item $D$ is the bounded domain or bounded inspection window row through which the Poincare control is read.
\item $W$ is the dyadic tolerance schedule supplied by $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ for the finite energy comparison.
\item $Y$ is the $\ConstructiveSobolevEnergyUp$ row that stores the displayed derivative-energy and magnitude ledgers.
\item $A$ is the dyadic accounting row, recording the finite rational inequalities consumed by the control estimate.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational energy and norm-control observations.
\item $Z$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the Sobolev, Poincare, dyadic, and rational readback rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,Q,D,W,Y,A,R,Z$ and their dependency rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite energy request through Sobolev exposure, Poincare control, dyadic accounting, rational readback, and the Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SobolevPoincareFiniteEnergyUp$, $\SobolevUp$, $\PoincareInequalityUp$, $\ConstructiveSobolevEnergyUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SobolevPoincareFiniteEnergyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for host measure completion, quotient equality of functions, selected representatives, global compactness, Hahn--Banach separation, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sobolev--Poincare finite-energy NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sobolev-poincare-finite-energy-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SobolevPoincareFiniteEnergyUp$ carrier $E=(S,Q,D,W,Y,A,R,Z,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SobolevPoincareFiniteEnergyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, Sobolev weak-derivative exposure, Poincare control exposure, bounded-domain or finite-window admission, dyadic tolerance accounting, constructive energy readback, $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal nonescape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sobolev-poincare-finite-energy-carrier}. The analytic rows are $S,Q,D,W,Y,A,R,Z$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The Sobolev row $S$ supplies only the displayed weak-derivative and magnitude surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sobolev-namecert}. The Poincare row $Q$ supplies only the bounded-domain or zero-mean control route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-inequality-namecert}. Thus the energy-control handoff is read through displayed Sobolev and Poincare rows before any downstream energy consumer receives it.

The dyadic schedule $W$ and accounting row $A$ keep every tolerance comparison inside $\DyadicRatCoreUp$. The constructive energy row $Y$ and rational readback row $R$ then route the observed bounds through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-constructive-sobolev-energy-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}.

Finally the Real seal $Z$ is downstream of those rows, and $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same packet under transport, replay, provenance, and naming. Since the carrier omits measure completion, quotient equality, selected representatives, Hahn--Banach separation, and Real completeness, no accepted local NameCert route exports them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sobolev--Poincare finite-energy handoff]
\label{thm:sobolev-poincare-finite-energy-handoff}
Every PDE, variational, trace, or metric consumer that reads an accepted $\SobolevPoincareFiniteEnergyUp$ packet must traverse
$$
\SobolevUp,\ \PoincareInequalityUp,\ \ConstructiveSobolevEnergyUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp
$$
through the displayed rows $S,Q,D,W,Y,A,R,Z,H,C,P,N$. The handoff exports a finite energy-control route and nothing outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:sobolev-poincare-finite-energy-namecert-obligations}. It makes Sobolev exposure, Poincare control, bounded-window admission, dyadic accounting, constructive energy, rational readback, and the Real seal first-class obligations of one packet.

A downstream consumer can therefore read a finite energy-control datum only after these rows have been exposed in order. Omitting the Sobolev row loses the weak-derivative source; omitting the Poincare row loses the control estimate; omitting the dyadic and rational rows loses the finite numerical accounting; and omitting the seal loses the terminal Real boundary.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ keep that route stable under transport, replay, provenance, and naming. Thus the exported handoff is exactly the displayed finite route, not a completed Sobolev space theorem or an external separation principle.
\end{proof}
