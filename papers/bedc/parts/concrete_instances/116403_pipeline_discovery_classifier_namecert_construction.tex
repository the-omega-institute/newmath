\begin{theorem}[Pipeline discovery classifier NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:pipeline-discovery-classifier-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\PipelineDiscoveryClassifierUp$ carrier $K=(D,B,A,W,G,L,T,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\PipelineDiscoveryClassifierUp}$ surface consists exactly of pipeline admission, before-classifier exposure, after-classifier exposure, public weight-profile exposure, theorem-genome route exposure, classifier-channel ledger exposure, TasteGate boundary exposure, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The pipeline row $D$ supplies the admission calculus, while $B,A,L$ supply the concrete classifier-channel comparison and keep record growth separate from classifier movement.

The weight row $W$ and genome row $G$ supply the public scoring and theorem-genome surfaces needed before a positive discovery claim can be read. The TasteGate row $T$ records that any Lean realization must keep the carrier nontrivial and field-faithful rather than collapsing the rows into a prose classifier.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same packet. Since the carrier contains no hidden weight, unledgered lineage, or prose-only discovery coordinate, the local certificate obligations are exhausted by the displayed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Pipeline discovery classifier positive-claim gate]
\label{thm:pipeline-discovery-classifier-positive-claim-gate}
Any positive discovery claim read through an accepted $\PipelineDiscoveryClassifierUp$ packet must display a concrete disagreement between the before-classifier row $B$ and after-classifier row $A$, together with the public weight profile $W$, theorem-genome route $G$, classifier-channel ledger $L$, TasteGate boundary $T$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$. If $B$ and $A$ agree on the displayed source scope, the packet may record automation or record growth through $D$ and $L$, but it does not classify the claim as positive discovery.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:pipeline-discovery-classifier-carrier}. The only before/after classifier comparison is the pair $B,A$, and the only record-growth separation row is $L$.

Use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:pipeline-discovery-classifier-namecert-obligations}. A positive read must expose the classifier comparison, public weights, theorem-genome route, TasteGate boundary, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming as one packet.

If $B$ and $A$ agree on the displayed source scope, the classifier-channel ledger has no disagreement row to feed the positive claim. The pipeline row $D$ may still record generated material, but record growth without classifier disagreement is routed as non-positive by the displayed carrier boundary.
\end{proof}
