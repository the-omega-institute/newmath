\label{def:bolzano-weierstrass-selector-carrier}

$$

\item $M$ is the monotone selector ledger read through $\MonotoneSubsequenceUp$; each displayed entry records a selector position, its source window, and the comparison row that makes the selected window monotone inside the finite packet.
\item $Q$ is the cofinal evidence row for the inspected selector thresholds, limited to thresholds that occur in the packet.
\item $W$ is the selected $\StreamNameUp$ window family obtained from $B,M,Q$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger read from the selected windows before any regular rational or real-facing handoff is available.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for full Bolzano--Weierstrass, sequential compactness, a choice-selected subsequence, quotient-stream equality, host real equality, a least cluster selector, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
