\begin{definition}[Argument principle carrier]

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the $\mathsf{PLContour}$ row with $\ComplexUp$ endpoints, segment rows, and a displayed non-boundary locus for zeros and poles.
\item $F$ is the $\HolomorphicUp$ row on the displayed contour neighbourhood outside the recorded pole locus.
\item $Z$ is the finite zero ledger, listing local zero witnesses and multiplicity rows that are visible to the packet.
\item $P_0$ is the finite pole ledger, listing local pole witnesses and order rows that are visible to the packet.
\item $W$ is the winding-number row computed from the contour row and the displayed zero-pole boundary data.
\item $I$ is the $\ContourIntegralOperationUp$ row for the logarithmic-derivative contour read.
\item $R$ is the $\ContourResidueHandoffUp$ row that exposes the corresponding residue-facing data.
\item $Q$ is the $\ContourReparameterizationLedgerUp$ row recording that allowed contour refinements preserve the same visible winding and integral rows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $G,F,Z,P_0,W,I,R,Q$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for contour, holomorphic, zero, pole, winding, integral, residue, reparameterization, provenance, and naming reads.
\item $K$ is $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ArgumentPrincipleUp$, $\ComplexUp$, $\HolomorphicUp$, $\ContourIntegralOperationUp$, $\ContourResidueHandoffUp$, $\ContourReparameterizationLedgerUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets with these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for quotient path equality, analytic continuation outside the displayed domain, unbounded zero searches, or a host meromorphic-function object.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Argument principle NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:argument-principle-namecert-obligations}

An argument-principle consumer receives no host path quotient, analytic-continuation oracle, unbounded zero search, or global meromorphic-function principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The contour row $G$, holomorphic row $F$, zero ledger $Z$, pole ledger $P_0$, winding row $W$, integral row $I$, residue handoff $R$, and reparameterization ledger $Q$ are the only analytic coordinates available to the local certificate.

The counting read is therefore forced through the displayed zero and pole ledgers before it may inspect the winding row. The contour-integral operation and residue handoff expose the corresponding logarithmic-derivative route, while the reparameterization ledger keeps permitted contour refinements attached to the same visible rows.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve this finite inventory. \end{proof}
