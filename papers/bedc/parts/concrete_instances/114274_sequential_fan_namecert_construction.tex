\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SequentialFanUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sequential-fan-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Brouwer and Bishop--Bridges on fans and choice sequences, the $\SequentialFanUp$ packet records the finite fan surface needed by Real and Completion readers as BEDC naming data. It sits beside the bar discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert}, the binary prefix source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cantorspace-namecert}, the finite-window interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only finite prefix trees, displayed bar witnesses, finite stream windows, regular readbacks, Real seals, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not assert open-cover compactness, a selected branch, countable choice, host quotient equality, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[SequentialFan carrier]\label{def:sequential-fan-carrier}
A $\SequentialFanUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(T,B,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is a finite prefix-tree row for a fan, read through the binary-prefix and fan surfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cantorspace-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert}.
\item $B$ is a displayed bar witness over $T$, recording the finite prefix boundary at which every accepted branch read stops.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row attached to the prefixes selected by $B$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the same finite windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $T,B,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $T,B,W,R,E,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the prefix-tree row through the bar witness, finite stream windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SequentialFanUp$, $\FanTheoremUp$, $\CantorSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialFanUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,B,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an infinite branch selector, open-cover compactness, quotient stream equality, host real equality, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SequentialFan NameCert obligations]\label{thm:sequential-fan-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SequentialFanUp$ carrier
$$
S=(T,B,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialFanUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the prefix tree, bar witness, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, terminal Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item fan-prefix compatibility, requiring every inspected branch request to enter through the displayed finite tree $T$ and the bar boundary $B$;
\item stream-window exposure, requiring each Real-facing observation to read the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row $W$ before any regular rational readback is consumed;
\item regular-readback exactness, requiring $R$ to be the only row by which the stream windows are interpreted as $\RegSeqRatUp$ data;
\item terminal-seal nonescape, requiring the $\RealUp$ row $E$ to be exposed only after $T,B,W,R,H,C,P,N$ have been displayed and excluding selected branches, quotient streams, host real equality, selected limits, countable choice, ambient completeness, Lean verification, and bridge checking.
\end{enumerate}
No SequentialFan obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The fan data are $T$ and $B$, the finite analytic observations are $W$ and $R$, and the terminal naming row is $E$.

The prefix compatibility obligation is forced because the carrier has no second tree coordinate and no branch selector. A branch-facing request therefore reaches an observation only after the displayed bar row has bounded the prefix length.

The analytic handoff is then finite. The $\StreamNameUp$ row $W$ supplies the windows, and $R$ supplies the regular rational readback for exactly those windows. Transport $H$ and replay $C$ preserve the same rows before provenance and local naming are read.

Since $E$ is the only Real-facing coordinate and the packet has no slot for quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, ambient completeness, Lean verification, or bridge data, the local NameCert surface is exactly the finite list stated above.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[SequentialFan Cauchy-window boundary]\label{thm:sequential-fan-cauchy-window-boundary}
Every Cauchy-facing or Real-facing read accepted by a $\SequentialFanUp$ packet factors through the finite fan-bar window before it reaches the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. Equivalently, the visible route is
$$
\FanTheoremUp,\ \CantorSpaceUp \longmapsto T,B \longmapsto \StreamNameUp(W) \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp(R) \longmapsto \RealUp(E)
$$
with the displayed $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ support rows. A read that bypasses the finite bar window, inserts a selected branch, or distinguishes terminal seals by quotient stream equality is outside the accepted $\SequentialFanUp$ carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier of \autoref{def:sequential-fan-carrier}. The only prefix data are the finite tree row $T$ and the bar witness $B$; hence a Cauchy-facing request cannot inspect an unbounded branch inside the packet.

The finite bar boundary determines which StreamName windows are visible. Those windows are recorded in $W$, and the regular rational readback row $R$ is attached to the same displayed windows.

The terminal Real seal $E$ is read only after $T,B,W,R$ and the support rows $H,C,P,N$ have been displayed. Therefore every accepted Cauchy or Real read follows the stated route through the finite fan-bar window before the RegSeqRat and Real rows are exposed.

The exclusion clause follows from the missing carrier coordinates: there is no selected branch, quotient stream equality, selected limit, countable-choice row, ambient completeness row, or alternate Real seal through which a consumer could bypass the finite window boundary.
\end{proof}
