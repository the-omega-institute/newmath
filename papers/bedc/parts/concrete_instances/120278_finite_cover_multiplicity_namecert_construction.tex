It sits beside the compact cover nerve of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-cover-nerve-namecert}, the finite cover nerve of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cover-nerve-namecert}, the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the rational lower-bound fold of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-lower-bound-fold-namecert}, and the downstream $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}. The packet names finite overlap depth and rational-fold rows; it does not assert open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, host set equality, countable choice, or an ambient Lebesgue-number theorem.

\begin{definition}[FiniteCoverMultiplicity carrier]
\label{def:finite-cover-multiplicity-carrier}
A $\FiniteCoverMultiplicityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(K,V,O,D,W,R,F,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-cover source row, including the displayed $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ of centers and radius histories.
\item $V$ is the cover-member row, recording which displayed cover members contain each inspected source point.
\item $O$ is the finite overlap-incidence row inherited from the cover-nerve route.
\item $D$ is the multiplicity-depth row, recording the displayed finite bound on how many cover members can be simultaneously active at an inspected point or face.
\item $W$ is the rational-fold weight row assigning each active member its $\RatUp$ radius or tolerance contribution.
\item $R$ is the rational lower-bound and rational-fold readback row consumed by compact-uniform modulus arguments.
\item $F$ is the $\FiniteCoverNerveUp/\CompactCoverNerveUp$ sibling handoff row.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer row reached only after the finite depth and rational-fold rows are displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for finite cover source, member, overlap, depth, weight, fold, handoff, and structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through cover membership, overlap incidence, multiplicity depth, rational folding, sibling handoff, and uniform-modulus consumption.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteCoverMultiplicityUp$, $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$, $\CompactCoverNerveUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\RatUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverMultiplicityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,V,O,D,W,R,F,U,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected subcovers, quotient equality of cover members, host point equality, countable choice, or ambient compactness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FiniteCoverMultiplicity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cover-multiplicity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteCoverMultiplicityUp$ carrier
$$
M=(K,V,O,D,W,R,F,U,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverMultiplicityUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission; compact finite-cover source visibility through $K$; cover-member exposure through $V$; overlap-incidence readback through $O$; multiplicity-depth exactness through $D$; rational-fold weight exposure through $W,R$; cover-nerve sibling compatibility through $F$; uniform-modulus handoff through $U$; componentwise transport and replay through $H,C$; and provenance and local naming through $P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:finite-cover-multiplicity-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the compact source, cover-member, overlap, depth, rational weight, fold, sibling handoff, uniform-modulus, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The cover and overlap obligations are read through $K,V,O$. Each membership and incidence row is tied to the same displayed finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$, so the certificate cannot widen to an arbitrary open-cover statement.

The multiplicity obligation is the row $D$. It counts only simultaneously active displayed members or faces already visible through $V,O$, and therefore records overlap depth as finite packet data rather than as host set cardinality.

The rational and uniform obligations are the route $W,R,F,U$. The weights and fold rows expose the rational accounting, the sibling handoff ties that accounting to the finite cover nerves, and the uniform-modulus row is terminal.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite inventory. Since no excluded object is a coordinate of the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FiniteCoverMultiplicity overlap-depth handoff]
\label{thm:finite-cover-multiplicity-overlap-depth-handoff}
Every compact-uniform-continuity consumer of an accepted $\FiniteCoverMultiplicityUp$ packet reads overlap-depth and rational-fold data through the finite route
$$
K,V,O \longmapsto D,W,R \longmapsto F,U \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
The route supplies finite cover membership, finite overlap incidence, displayed multiplicity depth, rational-fold weights, cover-nerve compatibility, and a $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff. It exports no selected subcover, no host nerve complex, no quotient equality of covers, no countable choice, and no ambient compactness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The source-side route is $K,V,O$: compact metric cover rows expose the finite probe spine, cover membership records active members, and overlap incidence records the finite face data.

The accounting route is $D,W,R$. Multiplicity depth is computed only over the displayed active members, while the rational weight and fold rows record the finite radius accounting used by the uniform-modulus construction.

The consumer route is $F,U,H,C,P,N$. The cover-nerve handoff keeps the packet aligned with the sibling finite-nerve chapters, and the uniform-modulus row is consumed only after the depth and fold rows are visible.

Because the carrier has no coordinate for selected subcovers, host complexes, quotient cover equality, choice, or ambient compactness, every compact-uniform consumer remains inside the displayed finite route.
\end{proof}
