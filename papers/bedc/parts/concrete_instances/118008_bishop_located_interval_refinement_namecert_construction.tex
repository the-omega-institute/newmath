Following Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis, the $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementUp$ packet records the finite service that refines located rational interval cells into dyadic readback windows and regular-Cauchy request rows. It sits between the located-cell surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}, the rational and dyadic interval rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rationalinterval-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the regular-Cauchy consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. The packet is a readback service, not a quotient real construction, a selected endpoint operator, a trichotomy principle, or an ambient completeness assumption.

\begin{definition}[Bishop located interval refinement carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-interval-refinement-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(L,Q,D,S,G,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedIntervalUp$ source row, containing the displayed interval cell, locatedness witness, and finite request boundary.
\item $Q$ is the $\RationalIntervalUp$ endpoint ledger read from the same displayed cell. It records rational lower and upper witnesses only as named rows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ refinement ledger. It supplies dyadic subcells, midpoint rows, and positive dyadic radii used by finite readback.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ request schedule row. It lists the finite observation windows through which downstream names ask for interval refinement.
\item $G$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-Cauchy handoff row. It records the finite modulus requests and tail windows accepted by the regular sequence consumer.
\item $A$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row. It exposes only the real-name boundary used to certify that the handoff is a readback of the same located interval packet.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $L,Q,D,S,G,A,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite route
$$
L,Q \longmapsto D \longmapsto S,G \longmapsto A .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\RationalIntervalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier of an accepted packet compares only the displayed rows above. It contains no coordinate for quotient reals, selected endpoints, trichotomy, arbitrary choice of nested cells, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located interval refinement NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-interval-refinement-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementUp$ carrier
$$
R=(L,Q,D,S,G,A,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the located interval source, rational interval ledger, dyadic refinement ledger, stream request schedule, regular-Cauchy handoff, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item endpoint readback, requiring each rational endpoint row in $Q$ to be read through the located interval cell $L$ rather than through a selected host endpoint;
\item dyadic refinement, requiring every finite subcell read to pass through the dyadic ledger $D$ before it reaches a stream or regular-Cauchy consumer;
\item request compatibility, requiring the schedule row $S$ and the regular-Cauchy row $G$ to expose the same finite tolerance windows;
\item nonescape, requiring downstream Real, stream, dyadic, and regular-Cauchy consumers to read only $L,Q,D,S,G,A,H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No BishopLocatedIntervalRefinement obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-interval-refinement-carrier}. The displayed tuple contains the located source cell, rational endpoint ledger, dyadic refinement ledger, stream schedule, regular-Cauchy handoff, real seal, and structural rows.

Endpoint readback uses $L$ and $Q$. The rational endpoint rows are named observations of the located cell, so no host endpoint is selected and no trichotomy branch is imported.

Dyadic refinement is mediated by $D$. Each finite subcell and radius read passes through the dyadic ledger before $S$ or $G$ may inspect it.

Finally compare the request rows $S$ and $G$ through $H$ and replay them through $C$. Provenance and local naming are restricted to $P$ and $N$, so downstream consumers cannot extract quotient reals, selected endpoints, arbitrary nested-cell choice, or ambient completeness from the packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located interval refinement Cauchy route]
\label{thm:bishop-located-interval-refinement-cauchy-route}
Let
$$
R=(L,Q,D,S,G,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
be an accepted $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementUp$ carrier. Every finite readback from a located interval cell to a regular-Cauchy request factors through
$$
L,Q \longmapsto D \longmapsto S,G \longmapsto A,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the route converts located interval evidence into dyadic and regular-Cauchy finite obligations over $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\RationalIntervalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$, without importing quotient real equality, selected endpoints, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the located interval source row $L$ and rational endpoint ledger $Q$. They display the cell to be refined and the rational boundary observations consumed by the finite route.

Apply the dyadic ledger row $D$. It refines the displayed rational cell into dyadic subcells and radius rows, giving the finite windows read by the stream schedule $S$.

Use the regular-Cauchy handoff row $G$ together with the real seal $A$. The handoff exports only finite modulus requests and tail windows, and the seal records that these requests read the same displayed source rather than a quotient real.

The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name exactly this chain. Since no other coordinate is present, the readback cannot select endpoints, invoke trichotomy, identify quotient reals, or assume ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
