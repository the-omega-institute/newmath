\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BishopChainConnectedUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-chain-connected-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive connectedness of intervals, the $\BishopChainConnectedUp$ packet records interval chain-connectedness as finite NameCert data over located interval rows, dyadic interval-net cells, finite chain ledgers, and Real-facing readbacks. It sits next to the metric chain-connected route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-chain-connected-metric-namecert}, consumes the finite interval-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}, and reads its Real-facing source rows through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives downstream interval and intermediate-value consumers a chain witness surface without invoking open-cover compactness, quotient equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Bishop chain-connected carrier]
\label{def:bishop-chain-connected-carrier}
A $\BishopChainConnectedUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(I,A,Z,D,J,W,R,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the located interval row, including the named endpoints, locatedness witnesses for endpoint comparisons, and the interval side on which the chain is read.
\item $A$ is the endpoint-pair row whose members are to be joined inside the located interval surface.
\item $Z$ is the finite chain ledger. It lists the inspected chain nodes, adjacency witnesses, and the finite successor route from the first endpoint row to the second endpoint row.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance and endpoint-arithmetic ledger used by the chain steps.
\item $J$ is the $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ cell row that supplies the finite dyadic interval-net cells through which the chain route is inspected.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row for endpoint names, chain nodes, and cell endpoints.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those same windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $Q$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $I,A,Z,D,J,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval row, endpoint pair, chain ledger, dyadic data, interval-net cells, windows, readbacks, Real seal, and structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite route
$$
I,A,Z,D,J \longmapsto W,R \longmapsto Q,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopChainConnectedUp$, $\ChainConnectedMetricUp$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopChainConnectedUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only this displayed finite packet. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, quotient equality of real names, a selected global path, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop chain-connected NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-chain-connected-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopChainConnectedUp$ carrier $B=(I,A,Z,D,J,W,R,Q,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopChainConnectedUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item interval admission: the located interval row $I$ and endpoint-pair row $A$ are displayed before any chain read;
\item finite chain ledger: the chain nodes, adjacency rows, and successor route are exhausted by the finite ledger $Z$;
\item dyadic cell support: every chain step reads the dyadic arithmetic row $D$ and the $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ cell row $J$ before it reaches a Real-facing boundary;
\item source readback: endpoint and cell observations pass through the common $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$ before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $Q$ is consumed;
\item structural provenance: $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and locally name only the displayed rows of the carrier.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.BishopChainConnectedUp.BishopChainConnectedCarrier\_namecert\_obligations}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:bishop-chain-connected-carrier}. The located interval row, endpoint pair, finite chain ledger, dyadic arithmetic, interval-net cells, window, readback, Real seal, and structural rows are all coordinates of the same finite packet.

The chain obligation is finite because $Z$ supplies the only allowed node and adjacency list. A consumer can replay a chain step only by reading a displayed successor entry of $Z$ together with the dyadic support rows $D$ and $J$.

The Real-facing obligation is downstream. The StreamName window $W$ and RegSeqRat readback $R$ are read before $Q$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that ordering componentwise. Since the carrier has no coordinate for open covers, quotient equality, choice, trichotomy, or completeness, none of those principles can be exported by the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop chain-connected interval handoff]
\label{thm:bishop-chain-connected-interval-handoff}
Every interval-facing consumer of an accepted $\BishopChainConnectedUp$ carrier must read the located interval row $I$, endpoint pair $A$, finite chain ledger $Z$, dyadic tolerance and endpoint-arithmetic row $D$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ cell row $J$, $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal $Q$ in that displayed order. The handoff may then pass the packet to Real interval, compact interval, or intermediate-value consumers as a finite chain-connected source, but it may not replace the source by open-cover compactness, host real equality, quotient stream equality, selected paths, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.BishopChainConnectedUp.BishopChainConnectedCarrier\_interval\_handoff}
\begin{proof}
Start with the obligation theorem \autoref{thm:bishop-chain-connected-namecert-obligations}. It forces every accepted interval read to expose the located interval and endpoint-pair rows before a chain witness is consumed.

Next read the finite chain and dyadic support rows. The ledger $Z$ supplies the chain nodes and adjacency route, while $D$ and the interval-net row $J$ provide the dyadic endpoint arithmetic and finite cell incidence needed by each step.

Finally pass through the source readback rows $W,R,Q$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. This is the same Real-facing order used by the dyadic interval-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert} and the metric chain-connected sibling \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-chain-connected-metric-namecert}. Thus downstream interval consumers receive a displayed finite chain-connected packet and no ambient compactness, equality, choice, or completeness endpoint.
\end{proof}
