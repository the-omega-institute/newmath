Following Menger and Busemann, the $\MetricMidpointConvexityUp$ packet records the finite midpoint-convexity surface for a displayed metric carrier before any ambient geodesic or convexity theorem is read. It sits over the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the real-valued distance comparison row used by $\RealMetricUp$, the geodesic segment route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-geodesic-metric-namecert}, the convex-set membership boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, and the regular-Cauchy midpoint packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-midpoint-namecert}. The packet names midpoint witnesses and distance ledgers as finite BEDC rows; it does not assert host midpoint uniqueness, open-cover compactness, selected geodesics, quotient point equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Metric midpoint convexity carrier]
\label{def:metric-midpoint-convexity-carrier}
A $\MetricMidpointConvexityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(X,R,G,V,W,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ carrier row, including the point classifier, distance request surface, and metric-law ledger used by all midpoint reads.
\item $R$ is the $\RealMetricUp$ distance row, recording the real-valued distance comparison only through finite rational or dyadic observations.
\item $G$ is the $\GeodesicMetricUp$ segment row that displays the two endpoints and the inspected midpoint witness as geodesic data, not as a host path choice.
\item $V$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ membership row for the inspected subset, including endpoint admission and midpoint admission as visible convexity-ledger entries.
\item $W$ is the $\RegularCauchyMidpointUp$ row that supplies the finite regular-Cauchy midpoint window read by Real-facing consumers.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for the two endpoint distances, the midpoint distance comparison, and the convexity admission budget.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for metric, distance, geodesic, convex-membership, midpoint-window, dyadic-ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a midpoint-convexity request through $X,R,G,V,W,D$ and then to $H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetricMidpointConvexityUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\RealMetricUp$, $\GeodesicMetricUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$, $\RegularCauchyMidpointUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetricMidpointConvexityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,R,G,V,W,D,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected global geodesic, a quotient point equality, a host convexity principle, an open-cover compactness theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Metric midpoint convexity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metric-midpoint-convexity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetricMidpointConvexityUp$ carrier $M=(X,R,G,V,W,D,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MetricMidpointConvexityUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed metric row, real-distance row, geodesic midpoint row, convex-set membership row, regular-Cauchy midpoint window, dyadic tolerance ledger, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. It also requires endpoint exposure through $X$, distance exactness through $R,D$, midpoint witness exposure through $G,W$, convex-membership exactness through $V$, and non-escape for selected geodesics, quotient point equality, host convexity, open-cover compactness, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metric-midpoint-convexity-carrier}. The rows $X,R,G,V,W,D,H,C,P,N$ are the complete accepted inventory, so every local naming obligation must be read from those rows.

The midpoint witness is finite. The metric row $X$ exposes the endpoints and point classifier, the geodesic row $G$ exposes the inspected midpoint witness, and the regular-Cauchy midpoint row $W$ gives the Real-facing finite window for the same midpoint read.

The convexity and distance ledgers are then local. The row $V$ records endpoint and midpoint membership, while $R$ and $D$ record the real-valued distance comparisons and dyadic tolerance budget before any consumer can inspect a terminal real-distance value.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the displayed route. Since the packet contains no row for a selected global geodesic, quotient point equality, host convexity theorem, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness, none of those claims can be exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}
