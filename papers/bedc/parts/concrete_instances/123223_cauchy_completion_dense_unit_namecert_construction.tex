Following Bishop--Bridges constructive completion and the Kelley language of dense embeddings into completions, the $\CauchyCompletionDenseUnitUp$ packet isolates the dense unit leg of the Cauchy completion surface. It reads the unit certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionunit-namecert}, the universal mapping packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-adjunction-namecert}, and the dense-reflector packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-dense-reflector-namecert} before any completion-facing consumer can use the dense image. The chapter names the unit-as-dense-image route as finite BEDC data; it does not assert quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[CauchyCompletionDenseUnit carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-completion-dense-unit-carrier}
A $\CauchyCompletionDenseUnitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(X,U,D,A,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source metric, regular-Cauchy, or finite-window row being admitted into the completion surface.
\item $U$ is the $\CauchyCompletionUnitUp$ row that sends $X$ through the displayed stream-window, dyadic-tolerance, regular-readback, and terminal seal route.
\item $D$ is the dense-image row, recording that the unit image is visible to completion consumers as a dense source-side packet.
\item $A$ is the $\CauchyCompletionAdjunctionUp$ row that lets completion-facing consumers compare their reads with source-side finite-window consumers over the dense unit image.
\item $R$ is the $\CauchyCompletionDenseReflectorUp$ row that exposes the same unit image to dense-reflector consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, unit, dense-image, adjunction, reflector, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the source row through the unit row, dense-image row, adjunction row, and reflector row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyCompletionDenseUnitUp$, $\CauchyCompletionUnitUp$, $\CauchyCompletionAdjunctionUp$, $\CauchyCompletionDenseReflectorUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionDenseUnitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion representatives, selected Cauchy limits, host equality of completed points, countable-choice schedules, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyCompletionDenseUnit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-dense-unit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompletionDenseUnitUp$ carrier $J=(X,U,D,A,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionDenseUnitUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, source exposure through $X$, unit admission through $U$, dense-image exposure through $D$, adjunction handoff through $A$, dense-reflector handoff through $R$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed continuation replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, local naming through $N$, and non-escape for quotient completions, selected representatives, host equality, countable choice, and ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-dense-unit-carrier}. The tuple exposes exactly the source row, unit row, dense-image row, adjunction row, reflector row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row.

The source and unit obligations read $X$ and $U$ first. The unit row is the only route by which the source enters the completion-facing surface, so dense-image exposure cannot be supplied by a detached completion object.

The dense-image, adjunction, and reflector obligations then read $D,A,R$ in that order. The adjunction row compares completion-facing consumers with source-side finite-window consumers, while the dense-reflector row makes the same unit image visible to reflector consumers.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier contains no quotient representative, selected limit, host equality, choice schedule, or ambient completeness row, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyCompletionDenseUnit density handoff]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-dense-unit-density-handoff}
Every completion-facing or dense-reflector consumer of an accepted $\CauchyCompletionDenseUnitUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
X\longmapsto U\longmapsto D\longmapsto(A,R)\longmapsto(H,C,P,N).
$$
Thus the dense unit leg is the displayed unit image together with its dense-image replay, not a quotient completion class, selected representative, host equality assertion, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier inventory. The source row $X$ reaches completion consumers only through the unit row $U$ and the dense-image row $D$ displayed in the same packet.

Next use the sibling routes. The adjunction packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-adjunction-namecert} consumes the dense image through $A$, while the dense-reflector packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-dense-reflector-namecert} consumes it through $R$.

It remains to keep both consumers on the same unit leg. The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the route $X,U,D,A,R$ without adding any second completion object. The absent quotient, selected-limit, host-equality, choice, and ambient-completeness coordinates therefore cannot be exported by the handoff.
\end{proof}
