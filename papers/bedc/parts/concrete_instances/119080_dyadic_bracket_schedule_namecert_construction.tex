$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $U$ is the upper $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint row inspected at the same scale.
\item $I$ is the finite inspection schedule row, recording the order in which bracket widths, endpoint comparisons, and tolerance checks are replayed.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row opened by the inspection schedule.

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic bracket schedule NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-bracket-schedule-namecert-obligations}

Every Real or Completion consumer must read those rows before treating $E$ as a real-name seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-bracket-schedule-carrier}. The endpoint rows $L,U$ are the only dyadic bracket endpoints available to the local classifier, and the inspection row $I$ is the only schedule row.

Read the finite window route next. The row $W$ opens the $\StreamNameUp$ window requested by $I$, and $R$ is the regular rational readback from the displayed endpoint and window data.

The Real-facing output is downstream of that readback. The seal row $E$ appears only after $R$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, hidden choice schedules, host real equality, ambient completeness, or bridge certificates, the displayed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic bracket schedule regular-Cauchy handoff]
\label{thm:dyadic-bracket-schedule-regular-cauchy-handoff}

Thus the consumer receives dyadic lower and upper bracket endpoints, the inspection schedule, a finite StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, and the terminal Real seal, but receives no quotient filter, selected limit, countable-choice selector, host equality, ambient Real completeness, or bridge certificate.
\end{theorem}

The regular-Cauchy handoff is the row $R$. It is read only after $L,U,I,W$ have appeared, so it cannot be detached from the displayed bracket schedule or replaced by a selected completed limit.

The terminal seal $E$ is downstream of $R$. Componentwise transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same order, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming without adding any alternate Completion-facing coordinate.
