\begin{theorem}[Bishop L10 completion-terminal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-l10-completion-terminal-namecert-obligations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item dyadic, stream, and regular readback admission through $D,S,Q$;

\item componentwise transport and displayed replay through $H,C$;

\end{enumerate}
These five rows exhaust the completion-terminal obligation surface and add no quotient stream, choice, hidden-tail, detached-seal, or ambient-completeness coordinate.

\begin{proof}
