\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SearchableCompactUp$}

Following Escardo searchable compactness and Bishop constructive compactness, the $\SearchableCompactUp$ packet records a finite-search compactness interface as BEDC NameCert data. It sits beside the finite bar and modulus routes of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fanfunctional-namecert}, the Boolean-prefix source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cantorspace-namecert}, the finite-net compact metric surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the modulus handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, and the finite observation schedules of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. The packet exports searchable finite selectors for displayed predicates and modulus consumers; it does not export open-cover compactness, choice-selected witnesses, quotient stream equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[SearchableCompact carrier]
\label{def:searchable-compact-carrier}
A $\SearchableCompactUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(K,B,Q,F,M,W,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the compact source row, read through either $\CompactMetricUp$ finite nets or $\CantorSpaceUp$ prefix windows;
\item $B$ is the finite branch or net boundary row supplied by $\FanFunctionalUp$ when the source is a fan-like searchable domain;
\item $Q$ is the inspected decidable predicate row, opened only on the finite windows named by the carrier;
\item $F$ is the finite-selector row, recording the retained candidate list and the first accepted candidate when the inspected predicate is witnessed;
\item $M$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer row used when a downstream continuous predicate asks for a bounded search depth;
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation schedule for predicate, prefix, and finite-net reads;
\item $R$ is the replay ledger from compact source and predicate windows to selector admission and modulus handoff;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,B,Q,F,M,W,R$;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite-search route;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SearchableCompactUp$, $\FanFunctionalUp$, $\CantorSpaceUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SearchableCompactUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary open cover, a choice-selected witness outside the retained finite window, a host compactness theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SearchableCompact NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:searchable-compact-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\SearchableCompactUp$ carrier $S=(K,B,Q,F,M,W,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SearchableCompactUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact source admission through $K$, finite boundary admission through $B$, decidable predicate exposure through $Q$, finite selector admission through $F$, modulus handoff through $M$, finite observation scheduling through $W$, replay through $R,C$, componentwise transport through $H$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:searchable-compact-carrier}. Its source-facing rows are $K$ and $B$: they expose compact metric finite nets, Cantor prefix windows, or fan-functional finite bar data before any selector is read.

The predicate and selector rows are read next. The row $Q$ opens only the displayed finite predicate windows, and $F$ records the finite retained candidates and accepted candidate boundary when such a boundary is displayed.

Finally $M,W,R,H,C,P,N$ route the selector to downstream modulus and observation consumers. Because the carrier has no coordinate for an arbitrary open cover, selected global witness, host compactness theorem, or ambient real completeness, those objects cannot be exported by the local NameCert.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[SearchableCompact finite-selection soundness]
\label{thm:searchable-compact-finite-selection-soundness}
Every finite-selection read exported by an accepted $\SearchableCompactUp$ carrier factors through
$$
K,B,Q,F,M,W,R,H,C,P,N .
$$
The consumer first reads the compact source and finite boundary rows, then the displayed decidable predicate windows, then the retained selector row, and only then the modulus and replay rows. Thus the exported search result is a constructive finite selector over the displayed packet, not an open-cover compactness proof, a choice-selected witness outside the packet, or a $\RealUp$ completeness argument.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the compact source rows. The row $K$ supplies the finite compact metric or Cantor prefix source, and $B$ supplies the fan-functional or finite boundary data when the search route requires a bounded branch depth.

The predicate row $Q$ is inspected only through $W$. The selector row $F$ is therefore tied to the finite observation schedule and cannot name a candidate outside the displayed predicate windows.

Apply the replay rows $R$ and $C$, then transport and provenance through $H,P,N$. The modulus consumer $M$ can read only the same finite route. Hence the finite selector is sound for the packet that was displayed, while open-cover compactness, choice-selected witnesses, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness remain outside the carrier.
\end{proof}
