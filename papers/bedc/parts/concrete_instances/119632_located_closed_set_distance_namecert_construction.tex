Following Bishop and Bridges constructive metric analysis, the $\LocatedClosedSetDistanceUp$ packet records the finite data by which a distance-to-a-closed-set read is consumed without a host subset equality, a selected nearest point, countable choice, or ambient completeness. It sits over the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the stream-name and regular rational readback rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the scalar real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the located-real comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedreal-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[Located closed-set distance carrier]
\label{def:located-closed-set-distance-carrier}
A $\LocatedClosedSetDistanceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(M,S,Q,L,D,R,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row naming the ambient metric comparison used by the distance read.
\item $S$ is the closed-set name row, exposed only through finite negative and positive location tests.
\item $Q$ is the query-point row naming the inspected point of the metric source.
\item $L$ is the located-window row recording finite lower and upper distance cuts for $Q$ against $S$.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance row, read through $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\StreamNameUp$ schedules.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ and $\LocatedRealUp$ scalar seal for the resulting distance name.
\item $W$ stores the finite witness ledger tying each accepted cut to a displayed metric comparison.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, set-name, query, located-window, tolerance, scalar, witness, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a distance query through the visible rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedClosedSetDistanceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedClosedSetDistanceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host closed subset, equality of subsets, a chosen nearest point, quotient equality of metric spaces, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located closed-set distance NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-closed-set-distance-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedClosedSetDistanceUp$ carrier $Z=(M,S,Q,L,D,R,W,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedClosedSetDistanceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, metric-source exposure through $M$, closed-set name exposure through $S$, query-point exposure through $Q$, located-window admission through $L$, dyadic tolerance scheduling through $D$, scalar sealing through $R$, witness-ledger exactness through $W$, componentwise transport through $H$, replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-closed-set-distance-carrier}. Its analytic rows are exactly $M,S,Q,L,D,R,W$, followed by structural transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The located-window row $L$ may be read only with the query row $Q$, the closed-set name row $S$, and the metric source $M$ still visible. The tolerance row $D$ supplies finite $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\StreamNameUp$ schedules, while $R$ seals the result at the $\RealUp$ and $\LocatedRealUp$ boundary.

Finally $W,H,C,P,N$ record witness exactness, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming for the same displayed rows. Since no carrier coordinate stores a host subset, selected nearest point, quotient metric equality, choice principle, or completeness theorem, these rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located closed-set distance metric handoff]
\label{thm:located-closed-set-distance-metric-handoff}
Every metric consumer of an accepted $\LocatedClosedSetDistanceUp$ packet must read the finite route
$$
M,S,Q,L,D,R,W \longmapsto H,C,P,N
$$
before using the distance name. The handoff supplies a metric-source row, a closed-set name row, a query-point row, located lower and upper cuts, dyadic tolerance schedules, a real located scalar seal, finite witness exactness, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not supply a host subset equality, a selected nearest point, an ambient completion theorem, or countable choice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:located-closed-set-distance-namecert-obligations}. It forces any accepted distance read to expose $M,S,Q,L,D,R,W$ before the structural rows may transport, replay, source, or name the result.

Now pass to a metric consumer. The consumer receives the distance as a located scalar row sealed by $R$, with its dyadic tolerance schedule $D$ and finite metric witness ledger $W$ still attached to the source metric $M$.

The remaining rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name that route only. Because the carrier contains no coordinate for a host subset equality, selected minimizer, ambient completion, or choice principle, the metric handoff is exactly the displayed finite packet.
\end{proof}
