\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FareyApproximationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-farey-approximation-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Hardy--Wright on rational approximation through Farey neighbors, the $\FareyApproximationUp$ packet records the finite rational-density route that passes from denominator-bounded Farey rows to real-name approximation consumers. It sits between the ordered neighbor surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-farey-sequence-namecert}, the branch-address surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stern-brocot-namecert}, the rational readback window of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-rational-approximation-namecert}, the regular rational sequence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names finite inspection data for a displayed real input and tolerance budget; it does not assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, a global choice of best approximants, or a quotient-real equality theorem.

\begin{definition}[FareyApproximationUp carrier]
\label{def:farey-approximation-carrier}
A $\FareyApproximationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(E,Q,B,F,S,R,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ input name whose finite rational window is being inspected.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row exposing the rational samples available at the requested budget.
\item $B$ is the denominator and tolerance bound row.
\item $F$ is the $\FareySequenceUp$ neighbor row, recording the adjacent rational endpoints selected at the displayed denominator level.
\item $S$ is the $\SternBrocotUp$ branch row that replays the same endpoints as a finite left-right address.
\item $R$ is the rational output row admitted by the Farey bracket.
\item $W$ is the finite error-window row witnessing that $R$ lies inside the displayed tolerance read from $B$ and $Q$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transports for the real name, readback, bound, neighbor, branch, rational output, window, provenance, and local certificate rows.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replays by which consumers read the finite approximation window.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FareyApproximationUp$, $\FareySequenceUp$, $\SternBrocotUp$, $\RealRationalApproximationUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FareyApproximationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
No coordinate supplies a selected best approximation, an infinite Farey process, a real-completeness proof, or a countable-choice selector.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FareyApproximationUp NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:farey-approximation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FareyApproximationUp$ carrier $A$, the local $\NameCert_{\FareyApproximationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for
$$
E,Q,B,F,S,R,W,H,C,P,N,
$$
Farey-neighbor exposure, Stern--Brocot branch replay, rational readback from the displayed real-name samples, finite tolerance-window exactness, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and nonescape from selected-best-approximant or $\RealUp$ completeness claims.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:farey-approximation-carrier}. The rows $E,Q,B,F,S,R,W$ are the whole mathematical surface: a displayed real name, a finite rational readback, a denominator and tolerance budget, the Farey neighbor bracket, the matching Stern--Brocot branch address, a rational output row, and the finite window check.

The route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-farey-sequence-namecert} supplies the ordered neighbor inspection, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stern-brocot-namecert} supplies the branch replay for the same bracket. The rows $H$ and $C$ keep these reads componentwise and displayed.

The provenance row $P$ and local row $N$ record that no other obligation is available. Since no coordinate carries a selector for best approximants or a real-completeness witness, the NameCert surface stops at the finite rational-density window.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FareyApproximationUp real-density handoff]
\label{thm:farey-approximation-real-density-handoff}
Every accepted $\FareyApproximationUp$ carrier exposes a finite rational-density handoff
$$
\RealUp \longleftarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longleftarrow \FareySequenceUp \longleftrightarrow \SternBrocotUp \longrightarrow \RealRationalApproximationUp
$$
for the displayed input row $E$ and budget row $B$. The handoff gives downstream consumers the rational output $R$ together with the finite error window $W$, but it does not select a denominator-optimal approximant and does not close the ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read $Q$ from the displayed real-name input $E$ at the budget $B$. This produces only the finite rational samples admitted by the regular rational sequence route.

Next read the Farey neighbor row $F$ and the Stern--Brocot branch row $S$. These two rows identify the same finite rational bracket: $F$ as an ordered neighbor inspection and $S$ as a branch-address replay. The rational output row $R$ and window row $W$ are consumed through that bracket.

Finally, the continuation row $C$ exposes the resulting finite approximation window to the $\RealRationalApproximationUp$ consumer. Because the carrier contains neither a best-approximant selector nor a completeness construction for all Cauchy data, the handoff remains a finite rational-density packet.
\end{proof}
