\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BarInductionCauchyModulusUp$}

Following Brouwer's bar induction practice, Troelstra--van Dalen's fan arguments, and the Bishop--Bridges use of explicit Cauchy moduli, the $\BarInductionCauchyModulusUp$ packet records a finite route from bar evidence to a modulus row for regular real names. The route sits beside \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchymodulus-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bairespace-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert}; it exposes only finite $\BHist$ rows, transport, replay, provenance, and a local $\NameCert$ seal.

\begin{definition}[Bar induction Cauchy modulus carrier]
\label{def:bar-induction-cauchy-modulus-carrier}
A $\BarInductionCauchyModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
(B,F,W,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
whose rows have the following roles:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is a decidable bar row for finite prefixes of the displayed branch space.
\item $F$ is the fan or finite-cover row certifying that the bar is read through a bounded prefix cover.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row that exposes the finite observations used by the bar test.
\item $S$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same window.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row whose precision request is discharged by the bar.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row produced after the bounded readback.
\item $H,C,P,N$ are respectively the $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert_{\BarInductionCauchyModulusUp}$ naming rows for the same finite packet.
\end{enumerate}
The accepted carrier may be consumed as a $\CauchyModulusUp$ source only through the displayed bar and fan rows; it contains no countable-choice selector, host infinite branch, quotient stream representative, or ambient Real-completeness witness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bar induction Cauchy modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bar-induction-cauchy-modulus-obligations}
An accepted $\BarInductionCauchyModulusUp$ carrier satisfies the following five NameCert obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier habitation by the finite rows $B,F,W,S,D,E,H,C,P,N$ of \autoref{def:bar-induction-cauchy-modulus-carrier};
\item bar admissibility, saying that every tolerance request reaches the decidable bar row through a finite prefix accepted by $B$;
\item fan boundedness, saying that the usable prefix is selected only from the finite cover row $F$;
\item readback exactness, saying that the $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows read the same bounded prefix and tolerance request;
\item transport, replay, provenance, and local naming stability along $H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bar-induction-cauchy-modulus-carrier}. Its visible rows already name the decidable bar, finite fan cover, StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, dyadic tolerance, Real seal, and structural naming data.

The bar and fan obligations are read before the analytic rows. The row $B$ supplies the decidable stopping test for the tolerance request, and $F$ supplies the bounded prefix cover through which the stopping test is admitted.

The readback rows then consume the same prefix. The $\StreamNameUp$ row gives the finite observation window, $\RegSeqRatUp$ reads the rational data on that window, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ records the tolerance, and $\RealUp$ seals only the resulting bounded readback.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ carry the same packet through transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. No obligation contains a selected infinite branch, a countable-choice function, quotient stream equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bar induction Cauchy modulus fan handoff]
\label{thm:bar-induction-cauchy-modulus-fan-handoff}
Every $\CauchyModulusUp$ or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\BarInductionCauchyModulusUp$ packet reads the modulus only through the finite route
$$
\FanTheoremUp,\quad
\BaireSpaceUp,\quad
\StreamNameUp,\quad
\RegSeqRatUp,\quad
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad
\RealUp .
$$
The consumer must first inspect the decidable bar row $B$ and the fan cover row $F$, then the StreamName window $W$, RegSeqRat readback $S$, dyadic tolerance $D$, and terminal Real seal $E$. The route exports no countable choice, host infinite branch selection, quotient streams, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Start with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:bar-induction-cauchy-modulus-obligations}. It places the decidable bar and finite fan cover before the analytic readback rows.

The $\CauchyModulusUp$ read is therefore a bounded-prefix read. A tolerance request reaches $D$ only after $B$ admits a finite prefix and $F$ bounds the usable cover, so the modulus row cannot be read as a host function selected from infinitely many branches.

The $\RealUp$ read is also bounded. The terminal seal $E$ is exposed only after the $\StreamNameUp$ window and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback have been matched to the same dyadic tolerance row, so quotient stream representatives and ambient Real completeness are not available to the consumer.
\end{proof}
