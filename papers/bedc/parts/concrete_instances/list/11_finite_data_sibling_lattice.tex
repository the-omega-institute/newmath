\begin{theorem}[List finite-data sibling lattice]
\label{thm:list-finite-data-sibling-lattice}
Every public $\ListUp$ spine read factors through the finite-data siblings
$\BoolUp$, $\OptionUp$, $\SumUp$, and $\ProdUp$ before it exports constructor or
length classifier data. The sibling lattice is the one displayed by
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bool-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-option-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sum-namecert}, and
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-prod-namecert}: Bool supplies tag rows, Option
supplies absence and presence endpoints, Sum supplies branch alternatives, and
Product supplies component envelopes for payload and tail rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the finite list spine as carrier projection data. Before a public empty,
cons, append, reverse, or length row can leave the $\ListUp$ certificate, the
spine must expose the tag discipline, endpoint shape, branch selection, and
component envelope that make the row finite.

The existing sibling route and anchor,
\autoref{thm:list-finite-data-sibling-route} and
\autoref{thm:list-finite-data-sibling-anchor}, identify those rows with the Bool,
Option, Sum, and Product certificates. Projecting the public spine through those
four sibling surfaces gives exactly the constructor classifier and length
classifier data, and no host list induction principle is used.
\end{proof}
