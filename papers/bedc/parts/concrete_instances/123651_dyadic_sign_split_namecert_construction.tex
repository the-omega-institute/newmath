Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive real-number comparison, the $\DyadicSignSplitUp$ packet records the finite sign-branch certificate used when dyadic comparisons are handed to located real and apartness consumers. It sits over the dyadic rational core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the absolute-value ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicabsolutevalue-namecert}, the located real carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedreal-namecert}, and the sign-facing apartness boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-apartness-sign-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Dyadic sign-split carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-sign-split-carrier}
A $\DyadicSignSplitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(D,A,L,G,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ input row carrying the dyadic numerator, denominator exponent, tolerance, and comparison budget;
\item $A$ is the $\DyadicAbsoluteValueUp$ magnitude row read from the same dyadic datum;
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedRealUp$ observation row whose finite window receives the dyadic comparison;
\item $G$ is the $\RealApartnessSignUp$ sign-facing apartness row;
\item $B$ is the finite branch ledger, with one displayed branch among negative, zero-window, or positive, and with the corresponding dyadic gap or zero-window evidence attached;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transports for the dyadic, absolute-value, located-real, sign, branch, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ routes from $D$ through $A,L,G,B$ before any sign consumer may read the branch;
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\DyadicAbsoluteValueUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\RealApartnessSignUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicSignSplitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no trichotomy principle, quotient rational equality, host total order, selected real representative, unrestricted sign function, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness datum.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic sign-split NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-sign-split-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-sign-split-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicSignSplitUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $S=(D,A,L,G,B,H,C,P,N)$, dyadic input visibility through $D$, absolute-value magnitude readback through $A$, located-real window binding through $L$, sign-facing apartness routing through $G$, branch-ledger exactness through $B$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The rows $D,A,L,G,B,H,C,P,N$ are the complete inventory of an accepted sign-split packet, and the branch ledger $B$ is the only row that records negative, zero-window, or positive output evidence.

Read the dependency route in order. The dyadic datum is first exposed through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, its magnitude is read through $\DyadicAbsoluteValueUp$, and the same finite comparison is then attached to the $\LocatedRealUp$ observation row and the $\RealApartnessSignUp$ sign-facing row.

Finally use the support rows. The componentwise transport, continuation replay, package provenance, and local NameCert rows preserve only the displayed dyadic, magnitude, located-window, apartness-sign, and branch-ledger data. Since no carrier coordinate stores trichotomy, quotient rational equality, host total order, selected real representatives, unrestricted sign functions, or ambient real completeness, those endpoints cannot be exported by an accepted packet.
\end{proof}
