\label{def:compact-tail-selector-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ row whose finite stream windows are being bounded by the selector.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the inspected stream windows and tolerance observations.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ row, already tolerance-only before the selector reads it.

\begin{theorem}[CompactTailSelector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-tail-selector-namecert-obligations}

$$
K,S,R,U,D\longmapsto W\longmapsto E,H,C,P,N
$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the rows of \autoref{def:compact-tail-selector-carrier}. The packet exposes compact metric data, stream windows, regular readbacks, a uniform modulus, a real seal, the selected tail-window row, a dyadic comparison row, and the structural rows.

The selected window $W$ is downstream of the compact metric source and the uniform-modulus row. A consumer therefore cannot inspect a tail threshold without first displaying the compact-net coverage, the stream windows being bounded, the regular rational readback, and the tolerance comparison that ties the modulus to the requested precision.

The rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve this route under classifier transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming. Since no coordinate stores countable choice, arbitrary open covers, sequential compactness, selected subsequences, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, the listed obligations exhaust the local surface.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:compact-tail-selector-finite-ledger}

\end{proof}
