\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\EventuallyUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-eventually-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki, Kelley, and Bishop--Bridges on directed sets, nets, and finite tail evidence, the $\EventuallyUp$ packet records the generic eventual-tail substrate consumed by L10 completion routes. It sits after the directed index surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-directedset-namecert}, the Moore--Smith net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-moore-smith-net-namecert}, the filter-facing eventuality row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-eventually-filter-namecert}, the stream naming surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names finite eventual-tail evidence only; it does not provide selected limits, quotient filters, countable choice, host equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Eventually carrier]
\label{def:eventually-carrier}
An $\EventuallyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(D,W,T,S,R,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DirectedSetUp$ row, naming the directed index relation whose tails are inspected.
\item $W$ is the finite window row, listing the index entries and comparison witnesses visible to the packet.
\item $T$ is the tail-threshold row, recording the displayed threshold after which the inspected predicate is required.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ row, exposing the finite stream reads used to inspect the tail.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row, supplying regular rational readback where the eventuality is consumed by real-name or completion routes.
\item $A$ is the $\RealUp$ terminal-read row available only after the directed, window, threshold, stream, and regular readback rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,W,T,S,R,A,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an eventuality consumer through the directed-tail rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EventuallyUp$, $\DirectedSetUp$, $\MooreSmithNetUp$, $\EventuallyFilterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EventuallyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets whose eventual read uses $D,W,T,S,R,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected limit, quotient filter equality, countable-choice schedule, host equality of streams, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Eventually NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:eventually-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EventuallyUp$ carrier $E=(D,W,T,S,R,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\EventuallyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, directed-index exposure through $D$, finite-window exposure through $W$, tail-threshold exposure through $T$, stream readback through $S$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal $\RealUp$ handoff through $A$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.EventuallyUp.EventuallyCarrier\_namecert\_obligations}
\leandef{BEDC.Derived.EventuallyUp.EventuallyCarrier}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:eventually-carrier}. The tuple lists the directed index row, finite window, tail threshold, stream read, regular readback, Real-facing handoff, and structural rows as the whole accepted surface.

The tail read is row-ordered. A consumer first exposes the directed set $D$ and finite window $W$, then reads the threshold row $T$ before any stream or regular-rational tail evidence is available.

Finally $S,R,A,H,C,P,N$ replay the same finite eventuality packet for completion-facing consumers. Since no selected-limit, quotient-filter, countable-choice, host-equality, or ambient Real-completeness coordinate appears in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Eventually directed-tail handoff]
\label{thm:eventually-directed-tail-handoff}
Every L10 net, filter, stream, regular-rational, or real-completion consumer of an accepted $\EventuallyUp$ packet receives eventual-tail evidence through the finite sibling route
$$
\DirectedSetUp
\longmapsto
\MooreSmithNetUp
\longmapsto
\EventuallyFilterUp
\longmapsto
\StreamNameUp
\longmapsto
\RegSeqRatUp
\longmapsto
\RealUp .
$$
The handoff exports only the displayed directed index, finite window, threshold, stream readback, regular rational readback, terminal real-facing row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.EventuallyUp.EventuallyCarrier\_directed\_tail\_handoff}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier projection. The directed row $D$, finite window $W$, and threshold row $T$ expose the tail boundary before any consumer can read stream or regular-rational data.

Next read the sibling surfaces. $\MooreSmithNetUp$ supplies the net-facing interpretation of the directed row, $\EventuallyFilterUp$ supplies the filter-facing eventuality row, $\StreamNameUp$ supplies finite readback, and $\RegSeqRatUp$ supplies the rational approximation row used by real-name consumers.

The terminal row $A$ then hands the displayed tail evidence to $\RealUp$ without adding a selected limit or completeness theorem. Transport through $H$, replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$ preserve the same finite route and add no quotient filter, countable choice, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
