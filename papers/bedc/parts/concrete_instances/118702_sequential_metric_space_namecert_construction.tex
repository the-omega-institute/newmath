Following Bishop and Bridges and Kelley, the $\SequentialMetricSpaceUp$ packet records a sequence-indexed metric interface as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits between the $\MetricUp$ carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the stream naming discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the $\RealUp$ completion seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the filter-side completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfilter-namecert}. The packet names sequential Cauchy observations without importing host sequences, quotient completions, or an ambient completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[SequentialMetricSpace carrier]
\label{def:sequential-metric-space-carrier}
A $\SequentialMetricSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(X,M,U,O,C,S,R,H,Q,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the named point carrier read from the underlying $\MetricUp$ surface;
\item $M$ is the metric observation row, recording only displayed distance comparisons and no host metric library object;
\item $U$ is the finite $\mathrm{UnaryHistory}$ index row used to address the observed sequence windows;
\item $O$ is the observation table pairing each displayed unary index window with metric reads in $M$;
\item $C$ is the sequential Cauchy ledger, expressed as finite tolerance-to-window data over $U,O$;
\item $S$ is the stream-name handoff row consumed by $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readers;
\item $R$ is the real-readback row consumed by $\RealUp$ and by the $\CauchyFilterUp$ comparison route;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for point, metric, index, observation, Cauchy, stream, real-readback, continuation, provenance, and naming rows;
\item $Q$ records the displayed $\Cont$ routes from metric observations to stream, regular rational, real, and Cauchy-filter consumers;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and the local certificate;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialMetricSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host infinite sequence, quotient completion, selected representative of a Cauchy class, classical choice principle, or ambient theorem asserting that every sequential Cauchy object has a limit.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SequentialMetricSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sequential-metric-space-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\SequentialMetricSpaceUp$ carrier $B=(X,M,U,O,C,S,R,H,Q,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert$ surface exposes exactly the point carrier $X$, metric observation row $M$, unary history index row $U$, observation table $O$, sequential Cauchy ledger $C$, stream-name handoff $S$, real-readback row $R$, componentwise transport $H$, displayed continuation routes $Q$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$. Every $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, or $\CauchyFilterUp$ consumer must read through those displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The rows $X,M,U,O,C$ give the finite metric and sequential observation surface, while $S$ and $R$ are the only rows that export the sequence-indexed data to downstream completion readers.

The transport and continuation rows preserve the same inventory. Componentwise $\hsame$ movement through $H$ can move an accepted packet only row by row, and every $\Cont$ route in $Q$ begins with the displayed metric observations and sequential Cauchy ledger.

Finally inspect $P$ and $N$. Provenance names the metric, stream, regular rational, real, and Cauchy-filter consumers, and local naming records that no host sequence, quotient completion, selected representative, or ambient completeness theorem is part of the certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[SequentialMetricSpace Cauchy readback]
\label{thm:sequential-metric-space-cauchy-readback}
For an accepted $\SequentialMetricSpaceUp$ carrier $B=(X,M,U,O,C,S,R,H,Q,P,N)$, every Cauchy readback consumed by $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, or $\CauchyFilterUp$ factors through the finite ledger
$$
U,O,C,S,R.
$$
The readback may use the displayed metric observations and the sequential Cauchy ledger, but it cannot replace them by a host sequence, quotient class, unindexed filter, or a completed real supplied outside the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the index and observation rows. The row $U$ supplies the displayed unary windows, and $O$ attaches the finite metric observations used by the Cauchy ledger $C$.

The handoff rows then determine the downstream reads. Stream and regular rational consumers read through $S$, while real and Cauchy-filter consumers read through $R$ together with the same ledger data.

The support rows $H,Q,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name that finite route. Since the carrier lists no host sequence, quotient completion, unindexed filter, or external completed real coordinate, every accepted Cauchy readback remains inside $U,O,C,S,R$.
\end{proof}
