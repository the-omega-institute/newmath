\begin{definition}[BishopCompletionOperator carrier]
\label{def:bishop-completion-operator-carrier}
A $\BishopCompletionOperatorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row naming the observed Cauchy-name windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row used for every precision request.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $S,R,D$ have been exposed.
\item $U$ is the Bishop completion extension and universal-boundary row, recording the finite handoff by which a completion consumer reads the operator output.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for stream windows, regular readbacks, dyadic tolerances, Real seals, extension rows, replay rows, provenance rows, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a precision request through $S,R,D$, then through $E$ and $U$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopCompletionOperatorUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCompletionOperatorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient Cauchy equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BishopCompletionOperator NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-completion-operator-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopCompletionOperatorUp$ carrier $O=(S,R,D,E,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCompletionOperatorUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, stream-window exposure, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance exposure, ordered $\RealUp$ seal access, Bishop extension and universal-boundary exposure through $U$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. A public completion-operator read must pass through those rows before it may cite the Real-facing seal or the universal-boundary row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted finite $\BHist$ packet. The visible carrier rows are precisely $S,R,D,E,U,H,C,P,N$, so carrier admission exposes stream windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance data, the ordered Real seal, and the extension boundary as displayed data.

The operator read is ordered. A precision request first reaches the finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows, then the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, then the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, and only afterward the $\RealUp$ seal and the universal-boundary row $U$.

Transport and replay are structural. The row $H$ moves the listed coordinates componentwise by $\hsame$, while $C$ replays the displayed continuation route and $P,N$ source and name the same packet. Since no quotient Cauchy equality, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate appears in the tuple, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

$$

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Because the packet contains no additional coordinate, the factorization cannot produce quotient Cauchy equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host real equality, or completeness of $\RealUp$.
