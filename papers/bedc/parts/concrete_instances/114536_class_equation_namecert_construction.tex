\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ClassEquationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-class-equation-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Artin, the class equation records the conjugacy-orbit decomposition of a finite group as the sum of the center and the noncentral conjugacy classes. The $\ClassEquationUp$ packet consumes the finite group rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}, the orbit-stabilizer handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-orbit-stabilizer-namecert}, the orbit-counting ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-burnside-orbit-count-namecert}, and the Sylow-facing subgroup route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sylow-namecert}. It names only the displayed conjugation action, center row, noncentral orbit ledger, stabilizer-index accounting, and finite counting read; it does not import a host quotient by conjugacy, selected representatives, a full subgroup lattice, or a character table.

\begin{definition}[ClassEquation carrier]
\label{def:class-equation-carrier}
A $\ClassEquationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(G,Z,A,O,S,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the consumed finite $\NameCert_{\GroupUp}$ row, including multiplication, unit, inverse, finite support, and componentwise group transport.
\item $Z$ is the displayed center row, read as the finite list of group endpoints whose conjugation action is fixed by every visible group row.
\item $A$ is the conjugation-action row of $G$ on its displayed finite support.
\item $O$ is the finite noncentral conjugacy-orbit ledger, with each retained orbit read through the action row $A$.
\item $S$ is the stabilizer-index ledger supplied by the orbit-stabilizer route; it records the centralizer-style stabilizer row and the finite orbit-size read for each noncentral orbit.
\item $L$ is the class-equation counting row, displaying the center-orbit split
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf{groupSize}(G) =
\mathsf{centerSize}(Z) + \mathsf{noncentralOrbitSum}(O,S).
\end{aligned}
$$
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $G,Z,A,O,S,L$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from group rows through conjugation, center exposure, orbit-stabilizer accounting, and the final counting row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClassEquationUp$, $\GroupUp$, $\OrbitStabilizerUp$, $\BurnsideOrbitCountUp$, $\SylowUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClassEquationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host conjugacy quotient, selected class representatives, quotient subgroup equality, character-theoretic class functions, or an ambient finite-group structure theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ClassEquation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:class-equation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClassEquationUp$ carrier $E=(G,Z,A,O,S,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ClassEquationUp}$ surface consists of five finite rows: carrier admission for the displayed group, center, conjugation-action, orbit, stabilizer-index, counting, transport, replay, provenance, and naming data; classifier stability under componentwise $\hsame$ for those same rows; center-orbit split exactness for the fixed conjugation rows and the noncentral orbit ledger; orbit-stabilizer ledger replay through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-orbit-stabilizer-namecert}; and nonescape, requiring every class-equation consumer to factor through $G,Z,A,O,S,L,H,C,P,N$ without receiving a host conjugacy quotient, selected representative, subgroup lattice, character table, or ambient finite-group classification.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection of \autoref{def:class-equation-carrier}. The group row $G$, center row $Z$, conjugation action $A$, noncentral orbit ledger $O$, stabilizer-index ledger $S$, and counting row $L$ are all finite $\BHist$ data.

Classifier stability is componentwise. The group coordinate is compared by the finite $\GroupUp$ classifier, the center and action rows are transported by the same $\hsame$ ledger, and the orbit, stabilizer, and counting rows are replayed through $H$ and $C$.

The center-orbit split is read before the final counting row. Fixed conjugation rows enter through $Z$, while noncentral rows enter through $O$ and then through the orbit-stabilizer ledger $S$; the resulting finite sum is exactly the displayed row $L$.

The orbit-stabilizer dependency is the sibling route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-orbit-stabilizer-namecert}, and the finite orbit accounting is compatible with the orbit-counting surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-burnside-orbit-count-namecert}. Thus Sylow-style consumers may cite the class-equation row only through the displayed group and subgroup-facing ledgers.

Nonescape follows from the packet inventory. Since no coordinate contains a host conjugacy quotient, selected representative, subgroup lattice, character table, or ambient classification theorem, no accepted consumer read can extract one from $\ClassEquationUp$.
\end{proof}
