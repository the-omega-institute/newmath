\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\MackeyTopologyUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-mackey-topology-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Mackey and Bourbaki on dual pairs and locally convex topologies, the $\MackeyTopologyUp$ packet records the finite BEDC rows that name a Mackey-style topology through displayed dual-pair probes, bounded-set compatibility, and seminorm windows. It sits after the weak topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-weak-topology-namecert}, the bounded-family surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bornology-namecert}, the barrelled-space boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-barrelled-space-namecert}, the topological-vector-space handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topvecspace-namecert}, and the linear-map rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-linearmap-namecert}. The packet names only finite dual-pair, comparison, bounded-family, seminorm-window, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it does not import host locally convex space completeness, Hahn--Banach separation, arbitrary choice, quotient functional equality, Lean verification, or bridge checking.

\begin{definition}[Mackey topology carrier]
\label{def:mackey-topology-carrier}
A $\MackeyTopologyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(D,W,B,S,T,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the dual-pair source row, naming the vector carrier, displayed functional window, evaluation relation, and finite pairing ledger.
\item $W$ is the $\WeakTopologyUp$ comparison row over the same dual-pair source, recording which weak-neighbourhood reads are consumed before the Mackey row is exposed.
\item $B$ is the bounded-set and $\BornologyUp$ row, listing the displayed bounded families against which uniform convergence windows are tested.
\item $S$ is the seminorm-window ledger, with each row naming a finite bounded-family probe, its seminorm read, and the neighbourhood row generated by that read.
\item $T$ is the $\TopVecSpaceUp$ handoff row, exposing addition-continuity and scalar-continuity only after $D,W,B,S$ have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the $\LinearMapUp$ handoff row for dual-pair evaluations and bounded-family probes that downstream operator consumers may inspect.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,W,B,S,T,L$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a topology request through the same dual-pair, weak-topology, bornology, seminorm, topological-vector-space, and linear-map rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MackeyTopologyUp$, $\WeakTopologyUp$, $\BornologyUp$, $\BarrelledSpaceUp$, $\TopVecSpaceUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MackeyTopologyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for an ambient locally convex topology, a selected full dual, a quotient equality of functionals, a Hahn--Banach separator, a completeness theorem, arbitrary choice, or bridge evidence.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Mackey topology NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:mackey-topology-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MackeyTopologyUp$ carrier $M=(D,W,B,S,T,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MackeyTopologyUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed dual-pair source $D$;
\item weak-topology comparison through $W$ over the same vector and functional windows;
\item bounded-family and bornology exposure through $B$;
\item seminorm-window admission through the finite ledger $S$;
\item topological-vector-space and linear-map handoff through $T$ and $L$;
\item nonescape through $H,C,P,N$, excluding host locally convex space completeness, Hahn--Banach separation, arbitrary choice, quotient functional equality, Lean verification, and bridge checking.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:mackey-topology-carrier}. The tuple $D,W,B,S,T,L,H,C,P,N$ is the whole accepted packet, so each obligation row is a finite coordinate read.

The topology comparison begins with the dual-pair source $D$ and the weak-topology row $W$. These rows expose only displayed evaluation and weak-neighbourhood data, so a consumer cannot replace them by an ambient weak topology or by equality of a full dual space.

Next read the bornology and seminorm data. The row $B$ names the bounded-family surface, and every seminorm request must pass through one of the finite windows in $S$ before any topological-vector-space or linear-map handoff is available.

Finally the rows $T,L,H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since none of those rows stores completeness, Hahn--Banach separation, arbitrary choice, quotient functional equality, Lean verification, or bridge evidence, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Mackey topology dual-pair finite route]
\label{thm:mackey-topology-dual-pair-finite-route}
Every topology read exported by an accepted $\MackeyTopologyUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
D \longmapsto W \longmapsto B \longmapsto S \longmapsto T,L \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus a downstream consumer may read the Mackey topology only as displayed dual-pair data compared with weak-topology rows, bounded-family rows, finite seminorm-window rows, and the named topological-vector-space and linear-map handoffs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct over the displayed row order of the carrier. The base row is the dual-pair source $D$, which supplies the vector, functional-window, evaluation, and pairing data from which all later rows are read.

For the first step, the weak-topology comparison row $W$ consumes only $D$. For the next step, the bornology row $B$ consumes the same source and lists the bounded-family tests without selecting a host family or a quotient functional equality.

For the seminorm step, each entry of $S$ names a finite bounded-family probe and its generated neighbourhood row. The handoff rows $T$ and $L$ are therefore reached only after the weak-topology comparison and bounded-family ledger have already been displayed.

The terminal rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Repacking these projections gives the displayed finite route and prevents any consumer from importing completeness, Hahn--Banach separation, arbitrary choice, or bridge checking.
\end{proof}
