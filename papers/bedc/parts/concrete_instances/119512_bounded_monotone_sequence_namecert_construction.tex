\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-monotone-sequence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp$ packet names the finite data by which a monotone real sequence and its displayed bounds are read by Real-and-Completion consumers. It sits after the finite stream interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the apartness-facing real comparison route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apartnessreal-namecert}. The packet records bounded monotonicity as a NameCert surface, not as a completeness theorem or a selected limit.

\begin{definition}[Bounded monotone sequence carrier]
\label{def:bounded-monotone-sequence-carrier}
A $\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(S,W,R,M,U,L,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ source row naming the finite observation schedule for the sequence.
\item $W$ is the family of finite stream windows inspected by Real and Completion consumers.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ comparison-tail ledger attached to those windows.
\item $M$ is the monotone-step row, recording the displayed adjacent or tail comparison used by the sequence direction.
\item $U$ is the upper-bound row, read through the same regular-rational tail ledger.
\item $L$ is the lower-bound row, retained for apartness and interval-facing consumers even when the monotone direction only needs one bound.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the sequence values and bound endpoints.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream windows, regular tails, monotone rows, bound rows, Real seals, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from stream-window reads through regular-rational comparison tails and then to the Real seal rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\ApartnessRealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a selected supremum, a convergent subsequence, Bolzano--Weierstrass, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded monotone sequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bounded-monotone-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp$ carrier $B=(S,W,R,M,U,L,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite $\StreamNameUp$ window exposure, $\RegSeqRatUp$ comparison-tail readback, monotone-step exposure, upper-bound and lower-bound row exposure, terminal $\RealUp$ seal ordering, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bounded-monotone-sequence-carrier}. The sequence is visible only through the stream row $S$ and the finite windows $W$, so no consumer can inspect an unbounded host sequence directly.

The comparison data are the regular-rational tail row $R$ together with the monotone-step row $M$ and the bound rows $U,L$. These rows expose bounded monotonicity before any Real endpoint is read.

The Real seal row $E$ is downstream from the displayed stream and regular-tail data. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route, so the listed rows exhaust the local certificate obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded monotone sequence comparison-tail route]
\label{thm:bounded-monotone-sequence-comparison-tail-route}
Every Real-and-Completion consumer of an accepted $\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp$ packet reads the route
$$
S,W
\longmapsto
R
\longmapsto
M,U,L
\longmapsto
E
$$
together with $H,C,P,N$. The route may expose bounded monotone comparison data to completion-facing consumers, but it does not export a limit, a least upper bound, a convergent subsequence, or a Bolzano--Weierstrass conclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the finite-window stage. The only sequence observations are the windows $W$ named by the $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$.

Next read the $\RegSeqRatUp$ comparison tails. The monotone row $M$ and the bound rows $U,L$ are meaningful only after those regular-rational tails have been displayed.

Finally the $\RealUp$ seal row $E$ records the sequence values and bound endpoints reached by the same tail route. Since no carrier coordinate names a limit, supremum, subsequence selection, or compactness principle, the consumer obtains only the bounded-monotone packet surface.
\end{proof}

\falsifiablePrediction{An accepted $\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp$ read is refuted if the monotone row $M$ is preserved while the StreamName windows $W$ or RegSeqRat comparison tails $R$ are removed.}

\independenceWitness{streamname, regseqrat, real, apartnessreal: the stream packet supplies finite windows without monotone and bound rows; the regular rational route supplies tail readback without the Real seal; the Real packet supplies endpoint seals without sequence monotonicity; and apartness-facing comparison packets do not jointly carry bounded monotone sequence windows.}
