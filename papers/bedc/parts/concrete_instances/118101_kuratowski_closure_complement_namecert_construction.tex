\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KuratowskiClosureComplementUp$}

\origin{human}

Following Kuratowski's closure-complement theorem, the $\KuratowskiClosureComplementUp$ packet records the finite alternation surface generated by a closure row and a complement row before any host powerset equality is read. It is grounded in the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the closed-set boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedset-namecert}, the closure operator packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-closure-namecert}, and the two-point specialization witness of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert}. The chapter names the closure/complement alternation route as BEDC certificate data only; it does not assert a host set algebra, quotient equality of subsets, Boolean powerset extensionality, or a completed lattice of all opens.

\begin{definition}[Kuratowski closure-complement carrier]
\label{def:kuratowski-closure-complement-carrier}
A $\KuratowskiClosureComplementUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(T,F,L,C,A,B,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the ambient $\TopologyUp$ carrier whose finite open, closed, and specialization rows provide the inspection context.
\item $F$ is the $\ClosedSetUp$ boundary row naming the closed-stable subsets allowed in the certificate.
\item $L$ is the $\KuratowskiClosureUp$ closure row, including extensivity, monotonicity, and idempotence reads already displayed by the closure packet.
\item $C$ is the complement row, recording only the formal exchange between a named subset row and its displayed complement row inside the finite topology packet.
\item $A$ is the alternation ledger for words in the two symbols closure and complement, with every accepted word reduced through the displayed finite route.
\item $B$ is the fourteen-boundary row, recording that no accepted alternation consumer can distinguish more than the finite Kuratowski boundary packets generated by $L$ and $C$.
\item $H$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology, closed-set, closure, complement, alternation, and boundary rows.
\item $R$ is displayed $\Cont$ replay for reading $T,F,L,C,A,B,H$ in that order.
\item $P$ is $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KuratowskiClosureComplementUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\ClosedSetUp$, $\KuratowskiClosureUp$, $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KuratowskiClosureComplementUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only by the finite alternation ledger and its displayed transports. It has no coordinate for host powerset equality, quotient subset equality, Boolean algebra completion, arbitrary choice of representatives, or a full topology of all opens.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Kuratowski closure-complement NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kuratowski-closure-complement-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\KuratowskiClosureComplementUp$ carrier $K=(T,F,L,C,A,B,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\KuratowskiClosureComplementUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, closure-row exposure, complement-row exposure, finite alternation replay, fourteen-boundary retention, componentwise transport, provenance, and non-escape from host powerset principles.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:kuratowski-closure-complement-carrier}. The visible rows are the topology context, closed-set boundary, closure operator row, complement row, alternation ledger, fourteen-boundary row, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

The closure obligation is read from $L$ together with the topology context $T$ and the closed-set boundary $F$. It exposes only the finite closure operator behavior already admitted by the Kuratowski closure packet.

The complement and alternation obligations are read from $C$ and $A$. Each alternation word is replayed through the displayed closure/complement rows, so the certificate tracks a finite route rather than an ambient subset function space.

Finally, $B,H,R,P,N$ record the boundary, transport, replay, source, and naming rows. Since no row contains host powerset equality, quotient subset equality, Boolean algebra completion, representative choice, or an all-opens carrier, none of those principles is exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kuratowski closure-complement fourteen route]
\label{thm:kuratowski-closure-complement-fourteen-route}
Every accepted $\KuratowskiClosureComplementUp$ consumer reading a closure/complement word factors through the finite route
$$
\TopologyUp\longmapsto\ClosedSetUp\longmapsto\KuratowskiClosureUp\longmapsto C\longmapsto A\longmapsto B\longmapsto(H,R,P,N).
$$
The route records the traditional Kuratowski fourteen-set boundary as finite NameCert data, not as host-level equality of all subsets or as an ambient Boolean powerset algebra.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the ambient topology and closed-set rows. They provide the only admissible inspection context for subset, closedness, and specialization reads.

Read the closure row next. The $\KuratowskiClosureUp$ packet supplies the closure behavior used in each alternation step, and the complement row supplies the other generator of the word ledger.

The alternation ledger $A$ records the finite word route, while $B$ stores the displayed fourteen-boundary retention. Transport and replay through $H$ and $R$ keep the same finite rows under componentwise comparison, and $P,N$ source and name the packet.

Thus the consumer receives only finite closure/complement alternation evidence. The read cannot turn into host powerset equality, quotient subset equality, Boolean completion, arbitrary representative choice, or a topology-of-all-opens principle because no such row appears in the carrier.
\end{proof}
