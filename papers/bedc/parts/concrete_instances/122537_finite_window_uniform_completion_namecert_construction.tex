\begin{definition}[Finite-window uniform completion carrier]
\label{def:finite-window-uniform-completion-carrier}
A $\FiniteWindowUniformCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(R,U,S,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the $\UniformCompletionReflectorUp$ row, naming the source uniform object, completion target, dense route, and reflector-facing route visible to the packet.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformCauchyCompletionUp$ row, recording the uniform Cauchy family and the completion-facing request inspected by the finite window.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row; it exposes only the stream windows used by the uniform Cauchy request.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, budgeting every displayed comparison made by the finite windows.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row on those same windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $R,U,S,D,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the reflector, uniform Cauchy, stream-window, dyadic-tolerance, regular-readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a uniform completion request through $R,U,S,D,Q,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteWindowUniformCompletionUp$, $\UniformCompletionReflectorUp$, $\UniformCauchyCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteWindowUniformCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,U,S,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion objects, selected limit representatives, countable choice, host equality of completed points, detached Real completeness, or an ambient completion theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-window uniform completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-window-uniform-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteWindowUniformCompletionUp$ carrier $W=(R,U,S,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteWindowUniformCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item reflector admission through the $\UniformCompletionReflectorUp$ row $R$;
\item uniform Cauchy admission through the $\UniformCauchyCompletionUp$ row $U$;
\item finite observation through the $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$;
\item dyadic tolerance exactness through the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$;
\item regular rational readback through the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $Q$;
\item terminal sealing through the $\RealUp$ row $E$;
\item componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay through $H$ and $C$;
\item $\Pkg$ provenance and local naming through $P$ and $N$.
\end{enumerate}
These rows exhaust the certificate surface and add no quotient completion, selected limit, countable-choice, host-equality, detached-completeness, or ambient-completion coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-window-uniform-completion-carrier}. Its visible coordinates are $R,U,S,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N$, so every local obligation must be read from one of those rows.

The completion request enters through the reflector row $R$ and the uniform Cauchy row $U$. The only observations available to that request are the StreamName windows $S$, the dyadic tolerance row $D$, and the RegSeqRat readback row $Q$.

The terminal row is $E$. Since $E$ is downstream of the finite observation stack, a Real-facing consumer cannot read a completed point, selected representative, or ambient completeness theorem before the displayed rows have been exposed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. No refused host-side principle appears as a carrier coordinate, so the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
