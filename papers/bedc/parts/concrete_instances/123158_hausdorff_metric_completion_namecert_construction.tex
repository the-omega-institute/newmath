\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HausdorffMetricCompletionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-hausdorff-metric-completion-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges and Kelley on separated metric completion, the $\HausdorffMetricCompletionUp$ packet records the completion of a separated metric source as finite NameCert data. It sits over the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the separated classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedmetric-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the Hausdorff boundary for a separated completion route; it does not assert host equality of Cauchy representatives, quotient endpoints, ambient Real completeness, or a completeness theorem for every external metric space.

\begin{definition}[Hausdorff metric completion carrier]
\label{def:hausdorff-metric-completion-carrier}
A $\HausdorffMetricCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
H=(M,S,C,R,E,T,Q,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the source $\MetricUp$ row, carrying the distance read, triangle ledger, and located comparison rows used by the completion route.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparatedMetricUp$ reflection row for the same source, exposing the zero-distance classifier before any endpoint equality is public.
\item $C$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ Cauchy completion row, carrying the accepted Cauchy family, convergence witness, and completion admission surface.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for regular rational distance estimates along the Cauchy tails.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row used only to read the zero-distance and positive-distance comparisons already displayed by $M,R,S$.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, separated classifier, completion row, regular-rational readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $Q$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a requested comparison tolerance through $M,S,C,R,E,T,P,N$ to the separated completion handoff.
\item $K$ is the Hausdorff/separated completion handoff row: zero-distance coincidence of completed points may be read only through $S$ after $R$ and $E$ have sealed the displayed metric comparison.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HausdorffMetricCompletionUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\SeparatedMetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HausdorffMetricCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient representatives, host equality of endpoints, selected limits, countable choice, ambient Real completeness, or an external Hausdorff-space theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hausdorff metric completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hausdorff-metric-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HausdorffMetricCompletionUp$ carrier
$$
H=(M,S,C,R,E,T,Q,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\HausdorffMetricCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of source metric admission, separated-metric reflection, Cauchy completion admission, regular rational distance readback, Real seal discipline, separated handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every equality-like read of completed points must expose $M,S,C,R,E$ before it may consume the handoff row $K$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hausdorff-metric-completion-carrier}. Its visible source rows are the metric row $M$, separated classifier $S$, completion row $C$, regular-rational readback $R$, and Real seal $E$.

The equality-like read is not a quotient read. It must first pass through the regular rational tail estimates in $R$ and the Real seal in $E$, and only then through the separated classifier supplied by $S$.

The structural rows $T,Q,P,N$ preserve the same finite route. Since no quotient representative, selected endpoint, host equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness coordinate occurs in the packet, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hausdorff metric completion separated route]
\label{thm:hausdorff-metric-completion-separated-route}
Every accepted $\HausdorffMetricCompletionUp$ packet reads completed-point coincidence through the separated route
$$
\MetricUp
\longmapsto
\CompleteMetricUp
\longmapsto
\RegSeqRatUp
\longmapsto
\RealUp
\longmapsto
\SeparatedMetricUp .
$$
The route starts with the source distance row $M$, compares Cauchy tails through $C$ and $R$, seals the zero-distance comparison through $E$, and exposes the public Hausdorff handoff only through the separated classifier $S$ and the handoff row $K$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the metric source row $M$ and the completion row $C$. They provide the distance and Cauchy-tail data used to compare two completed-point reads.

Next use $R$ and $E$. The regular rational row records the inspected tail estimates, while the Real seal permits only the displayed zero-distance or positive-distance comparisons to be read.

Finally apply the separated classifier $S$ and the handoff row $K$. The public coincidence read is therefore a separated-metric read over the completed route, not quotient equality, host endpoint equality, selected limits, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hausdorff metric completion real boundary]
\label{thm:hausdorff-metric-completion-real-boundary}
The $\HausdorffMetricCompletionUp$ packet uses $\RealUp$ only as a seal boundary for the displayed regular rational distance comparisons. A consumer may read $E$ after $R$ has displayed the required $\RegSeqRatUp$ tail estimates, but $E$ does not export Real completeness, host equality of real numbers, quotient representatives of Cauchy sequences, or an external completeness principle for the source metric space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the numeric side of \autoref{def:hausdorff-metric-completion-carrier}. The row $R$ is the regular rational readback surface, and $E$ is the Real-facing seal for comparisons already supplied by $M,C,R$.

The separated route of \autoref{thm:hausdorff-metric-completion-separated-route} places $E$ before $S$ and $K$. Thus the Real row is a boundary for the comparison, not a source of completed-point equality by itself.

Since the carrier contains no ambient Real completeness coordinate, quotient representative, host real equality row, or external completion theorem, every Real-facing read remains inside the displayed finite packet.
\end{proof}
