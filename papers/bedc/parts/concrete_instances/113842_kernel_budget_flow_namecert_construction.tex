\begin{definition}[Kernel budget flow carrier]
\label{def:kernel-budget-flow-carrier}
A $\KernelBudgetFlowUp$ carrier is a finite packet
$$
F=(B,H,K,G,L,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BMark$ budget row, naming the finite kernel-facing request and the bounded inspection scope.
\item $H$ is the $\BHist$ flow row, recording the ordered budget events accepted by the packet.
\item $K$ is the kernel-scope row, naming the accepted kernel witness or audit witness that the flow is allowed to inspect.
\item $G$ is the $\Gap$ row, recording refused, absent, or not-yet-certified budget edges without erasing them.
\item $L$ is the ledger row, pairing every visible budget event with the source, acceptance, gap, and consumer rows it may reach.
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay row from budget request through history, kernel scope, gap accounting, ledger readback, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KernelBudgetFlowUp$, $\KernelAuditWitnessUp$, $\KernelAcceptanceWitnessUp$, $\KernelInductiveAcceptanceUp$, $\FiniteMultiHistPacketUp$, $\BMark$, $\BHist$, $\Gap$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KernelBudgetFlowUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,H,K,G,L,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host-kernel soundness, unrestricted proof checking, hidden traces, unbounded fuel, erased gap rows, or theorem-proof evidence.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Kernel budget flow NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kernel-budget-flow-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KernelBudgetFlowUp$ carrier $F=(B,H,K,G,L,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\KernelBudgetFlowUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the BMark budget row, BHist flow row, kernel-scope row, Gap row, ledger row, displayed Cont replay, Pkg provenance, and local naming.

The flow obligation requires each budget event in $H$ to be accounted for by $L$ before a downstream consumer reads it. The gap obligation requires refused or unavailable edges to remain visible in $G$ rather than being reclassified as accepted kernel evidence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:kernel-budget-flow-carrier}. The rows $B,H,K,G,L$ are the budget-flow data, while $C,P,N$ replay, source, and name the same data.

The BMark row bounds the request, and the BHist row orders the events. The kernel-scope row says which accepted witness may be inspected, while the Gap row keeps unavailable or refused edges visible.

The ledger row is the exact accounting surface. Every downstream read of a budget event must pass through $L$, so the packet cannot replace the ledger by a hidden trace or a host-kernel endorsement.

Finally, $C,P,N$ preserve and name the displayed route. Since no excluded object is a carrier coordinate, no local NameCert obligation can export unrestricted proof checking, host soundness, unbounded fuel, or theorem-proof evidence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kernel budget flow finite realization]
\label{thm:kernel-budget-flow-finite-realization}
Every accepted $\KernelBudgetFlowUp$ read factors through displayed
$$
\BMark,\ \BHist,\ \Cont,\ \Pkg,\ \Gap,\ L,\ \NameCert
$$
rows, with $L$ the finite ledger of budget-event accountability. Thus the packet realizes kernel-budget flow as finite BEDC data rather than as an ambient kernel theorem, an untracked execution trace, or a hidden proof object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:kernel-budget-flow-namecert-obligations}. They expose the BMark request, BHist flow, kernel-scope witness, Gap row, ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The budget and history rows fix the finite event surface. The kernel-scope row restricts which accepted witness the flow may inspect, and the gap row preserves refused or missing edges.

The ledger row $L$ is then the only route from events to consumers. The replay and provenance rows do not create additional events; they preserve the finite route already displayed.

Therefore the realization is finite and row-local. It exports no ambient kernel theorem, hidden trace, theorem proof, or unbounded budget source.
\end{proof}
