\begin{definition}[Polish completion carrier]
\label{def:polish-completion-carrier}
A $\PolishCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(X,D,M,S,B,W,R,Q,E,H,C,K,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the separable metric source row whose dense naming surface is inspected before completion.
\item $D$ is the countable dense-window ledger read as finite $\StreamNameUp$ observations, not as a completed choice sequence.
\item $M$ is the $\BishopMetricCompletionUp$ row carrying the constructive metric-completion handoff.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparableCompletionUp$ row binding the dense source to the completion route.
\item $B$ is the $\PolishSpaceUp$ target boundary row, read only after the separable and complete-metric rows have been displayed.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule used by completion-facing readers.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row attached to the same windows.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the completion and readback rows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached after $W,R,Q,M,S,B$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, dense-window, completion, Polish boundary, readback, tolerance, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for quotient Cauchy equality, selected completion representatives, a countable-choice dense sequence, trichotomy, host equality of real names, or ambient completeness outside the named packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Polish completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:polish-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PolishCompletionUp$ carrier $P=(X,D,M,S,B,W,R,Q,E,H,C,K,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\PolishCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the separable metric source, finite dense-window ledger, Bishop metric completion handoff, separable completion row, Polish-space boundary, StreamName window schedule, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger, terminal Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

No NameCert obligation is stored in a quotient of Cauchy names, a selected representative, a host dense sequence, a countable-choice schedule, trichotomy, host real equality, or an ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:polish-completion-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the rows $X,D,M,S,B,W,R,Q,E,H,C,K,N$.

The source and dense-window rows are finite. The row $X$ supplies the separable metric source, while $D$ exposes only inspected dense windows through the same finite schedule discipline used by $\StreamNameUp$.

The completion-facing rows are downstream. The Bishop metric completion row $M$ and separable completion row $S$ are read before the Polish boundary $B$, and the window, readback, dyadic tolerance, and Real seal rows $W,R,Q,E$ are visible before any public completion read is exported.

Finally $H,C,K,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since none of the excluded host or choice principles appears as a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Polish completion complete separable route]
\label{thm:polish-completion-complete-separable-route}
Every Polish-space-facing read of an accepted $\PolishCompletionUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$

$$
The route first displays the separable metric source and dense-window ledger, then reads finite StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, and DyadicRatCore tolerances, then passes through Bishop metric completion and separable completion before the Polish boundary and terminal Real seal are exposed. It exports a public BEDC completion route, not a host theorem asserting complete metrizability, a quotient completion class, a selected limit, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations above. They force every consumer to expose the separable metric source, dense-window ledger, completion handoffs, window schedule, regular readback, tolerance ledger, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

Use the sibling completion route. The $\BishopMetricCompletionUp$ row supplies the constructive complete-metric handoff, while the $\SeparableCompletionUp$ row keeps the dense source visible before the $\PolishSpaceUp$ boundary is read.

Finally follow the Real and regular-rational support rows. The finite window $W$, readback $R$, tolerance ledger $Q$, and seal $E$ keep the public route inside displayed BEDC data. Because the classifier has no coordinate for quotient classes, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, or ambient completeness, the complete separable route is exhausted by the displayed packet.
\end{proof}
