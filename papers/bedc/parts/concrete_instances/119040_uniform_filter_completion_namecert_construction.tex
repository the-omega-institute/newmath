\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniformFilterCompletionUp$}

Following Kelley and Bourbaki on uniform spaces and filters, the $\UniformFilterCompletionUp$ packet records the L10 completion surface where a displayed Cauchy-filter base is read through a uniform-completion handoff before any completion consumer sees a real-facing seal. It sits beside the functorial completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformcompletionfunctor-namecert}, the Cauchy-filter base route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-filter-base-namecert}, the Cauchy-filter completion criterion of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-filter-completion-criterion-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite StreamName windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a filter-based completion surface without quotient completions, selected limits, choice, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Uniform filter completion carrier]
\label{def:uniform-filter-completion-carrier}
A $\UniformFilterCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(F,B,Q,G,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\UniformCompletionFunctorUp$ route row, exposing the functorial completion handoff used by downstream completion consumers.
\item $B$ is the $\CauchyFilterBaseUp$ basis row, listing the finite filter-base requests and directed refinements consumed by the completion route.
\item $Q$ is the $\CauchyFilterCompletionCriterionUp$ criterion row, requiring the filter route to pass through displayed basis, window, readback, and seal data.
\item $G$ is the $\UniformCompletionUp$ source row, binding the same uniform source to the functorial and criterion rows.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule for the filter-base and completion requests.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the displayed windows and tolerance requests.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, readable only after $F,B,Q,G,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the functorial, filter-base, criterion, uniform-source, window, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite completion route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformFilterCompletionUp$, $\UniformCompletionFunctorUp$, $\CauchyFilterBaseUp$, $\CauchyFilterCompletionCriterionUp$, $\UniformCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformFilterCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $F,B,Q,G,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient filter equality, quotient completion equality, selected representatives, selected limits, countable choice, host equality of completed objects, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform filter completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-filter-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformFilterCompletionUp$ carrier $U=(F,B,Q,G,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UniformFilterCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the functorial uniform-completion route, Cauchy-filter base, Cauchy-filter completion criterion, uniform source, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Every completion-facing obligation is read through those rows before a consumer may inspect the terminal seal, and no obligation is stored in quotient filters, selected limits, choice, host equality, or ambient completion data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-filter-completion-carrier}. The tuple displays exactly the rows $F,B,Q,G,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$.

Read the completion source first. The uniform source row $G$ and functorial handoff row $F$ fix the displayed uniform-completion route, while the Cauchy-filter base row $B$ supplies the finite basis data consumed by that route.

Read the criterion and numerical rows next. The criterion row $Q$ requires the completion read to remain attached to the displayed basis route, the StreamName row $S$ schedules the finite observations, the RegSeqRat row $R$ reads back those windows, and the Real seal $E$ is exposed only after that readback.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since quotient completions, selected limits, choice, host equality, and ambient completeness are absent from the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform filter completion handoff]
\label{thm:uniform-filter-completion-handoff}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\UniformFilterCompletionUp$ carrier reads the finite route
$$
\UniformCompletionFunctorUp \longmapsto \CauchyFilterBaseUp \longmapsto \CauchyFilterCompletionCriterionUp \longmapsto \UniformCompletionUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The route exports filter-based completion data only through displayed BEDC rows. It exports no quotient completion, quotient filter, selected representative, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host equality of completed objects, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:uniform-filter-completion-namecert-obligations}. It confines every accepted read to the displayed functorial, filter-base, criterion, uniform-source, window, readback, seal, and structural rows.

The filter route passes through $B$ before the criterion row $Q$ can be consumed. Thus a completion-facing consumer cannot replace the basis data by an unrecorded filter or a quotient-filter equality.

The completion route passes through $F,G,S,R,E$ in the displayed order. The functorial and uniform-source rows precede the finite windows, the regular rational readback precedes the Real seal, and the structural rows preserve that same order.

Therefore the accepted handoff is exactly the finite route in the statement. The excluded quotient, selector, choice, host-equality, and ambient-completeness objects are not coordinates of the carrier.
\end{proof}
