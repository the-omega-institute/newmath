\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\MaschkeProjectorUp$}

Following Maschke's theorem for finite-group representations, the $\MaschkeProjectorUp$ packet isolates the finite averaging-projector construction from the semisimplicity packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-maschke-theorem-namecert}. It reads the finite group source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}, the represented vector source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, the representation-row bookkeeping of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-representationring-namecert}, and the orthogonality-side ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-schurorthogonality-namecert}. The packet names the projector and invariant-complement route only; it does not close the full $\MaschkeTheoremUp$ semisimplicity certificate.

\begin{definition}[Maschke projector carrier]
\label{def:maschke-projector-carrier}
A $\MaschkeProjectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(G,V,R,S,A,I,Q,H,C,K,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the finite $\GroupUp$ row supplying the finite averaging support, unit, inverse, and multiplication checks;
\item $V$ is the $\VecSpaceUp$ row supplying the represented vector carrier, zero, addition, and scalar-action rows;
\item $R$ is the $\RepresentationRingUp$ row naming the representation action consumed by the average;
\item $S$ is the invariant-subspace row whose inclusion into $V$ is the target of the complement construction;
\item $A$ is the averaging row that stores the finite group average of a raw projection along the representation action;
\item $I$ is the invariance row recording that the averaged map commutes with the displayed group action;
\item $Q$ is the complement row exposing the kernel-image decomposition read by downstream invariant-complement consumers;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $G,V,R,S,A,I,Q$;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the averaging, invariance, and complement routes;
\item $K$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\GroupUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\RepresentationRingUp$, $\SchurOrthogonalityUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and local naming;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MaschkeProjectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host representation category, a quotient-subrepresentation carrier, a selected basis, a global semisimplicity theorem, or the full $\MaschkeTheoremUp$ packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Maschke projector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:maschke-projector-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MaschkeProjectorUp$ carrier
$$
P=(G,V,R,S,A,I,Q,H,C,K,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MaschkeProjectorUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite group admission through $G$, vector-source admission through $V$, representation-row admission through $R$, invariant-subspace admission through $S$, averaging-row exactness through $A$, invariance exposure through $I$, complement exposure through $Q$, componentwise $\hsame$ stability through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $K$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:maschke-projector-carrier}. The group, vector, representation, invariant-subspace, averaging, invariance, and complement rows are exactly the analytic coordinates of the packet.

The structural rows $H,C,K,N$ preserve the same finite surface. They transport, replay, source, and name the projector data without adding a host category or quotient representation.

Since the full $\MaschkeTheoremUp$ packet is not a coordinate of the carrier, the local NameCert obligations stop at the averaging projector and its invariant-complement route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Maschke projector invariant-complement route]
\label{thm:maschke-projector-invariant-complement-route}
Every invariant-complement consumer of an accepted $\MaschkeProjectorUp$ packet must read the route
$$
\GroupUp \longmapsto \VecSpaceUp \longmapsto \RepresentationRingUp \longmapsto A \longmapsto I \longmapsto Q .
$$
The averaging row $A$ is the only row that may turn a raw projection into an invariant projector, the invariance row $I$ is the only row that may expose equivariance of that projector, and the complement row $Q$ is the only row that may export the resulting invariant complement.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the finite group and vector rows. They supply the finite averaging support and the represented target on which the average is computed.

Next read the representation row. The action data from $\RepresentationRingUp$ is what lets the averaging row $A$ replay the group average as an internal row rather than as an external operator.

The invariance row $I$ is downstream of $A$, and the complement row $Q$ is downstream of $I$. The Schur-orthogonality sibling may support the finite representation ledger, but it does not add a basis selector or a hidden semisimplicity theorem.

Finally transport the route through $H,C,K,N$. These rows keep the invariant-complement consumer inside the displayed $\MaschkeProjectorUp$ packet and prevent a read of the larger $\MaschkeTheoremUp$ certificate.
\end{proof}
