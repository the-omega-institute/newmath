\begin{theorem}[CauchyFilterCompletion bridged route]
\label{thm:cauchy-filter-completion-bridged-route}
The accepted $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ route exports its completion-facing bridge only through the existing semantic $\NameCert_{\CauchyFilterCompletionUp}$ row, uniform filter readback, completion handoff, and completion-functor factorization rows. Thus any bridge-facing consumer receives exactly the displayed route
$$
C,W,T,R,L,H,K,P,N
$$
from the accepted carrier and receives no quotient filter, selected limit, host equality, countable-choice selector, detached completion object, or ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier and semantic NameCert rows of \autoref{def:cauchy-filter-completion-carrier} and \autoref{thm:cauchy-filter-completion-namecert-obligations}. They fix the only exported coordinates as the Cauchy-filter source, finite windows, tolerance ledger, regular readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Use uniform readback and completion handoff, \autoref{thm:cauchy-filter-completion-uniform-filter-readback} and \autoref{thm:cauchy-filter-completion-completion-handoff}. They keep uniform-facing and completion-facing consumers on that same displayed route.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:cauchy-filter-completion-completion-functor-factorization}. The functorial vocabulary can repackage the route for completion consumers, but it adds no coordinate outside $C,W,T,R,L,H,K,P,N$, so the bridge-facing export is exactly the finite NameCert route.
\end{proof}
