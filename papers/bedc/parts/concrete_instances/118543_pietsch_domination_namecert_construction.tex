It sits over displayed Banach rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-namecert}, bounded operator and norm rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedlinearoperator-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-operator-norm-namecert}, finite measure rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-measure-namecert}, normalized probability rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert}, and terminal scalar rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[PietschDomination carrier]
\label{def:pietsch-domination-carrier}
A $\PietschDominationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
(X,Y,T,N,A,M,P,R,H,C,Q,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ and $Y$ are source and target $\BanachUp$ rows;
\item $T$ is a $\BoundedLinearOperatorUp$ row from $X$ to $Y$;
\item $N$ is the associated $\OperatorNormUp$ bound row for $T$;
\item $A$ is a finite absolutely-summing test ledger, listing only displayed finite families of carried source endpoints and their target norm reads;
\item $M$ is a $\MeasureUp$ row exposing the finite event and value windows used by the domination read;
\item $P$ is a $\ProbSpaceUp$ normalization row over the same measure surface;
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ scalar-bound row through which the domination constant and all endpoint comparisons are read;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Banach, operator, norm, measure, probability, and Real rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite domination request;
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PietschDominationUp$, $\BanachUp$, $\BoundedLinearOperatorUp$, $\OperatorNormUp$, $\MeasureUp$, $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PietschDominationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}

The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed coordinates. It has no coordinate for a selected Hahn--Banach functional, an external probability measure chosen by choice, quotient equality of operators, a host domination theorem, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[PietschDomination NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:pietsch-domination-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PietschDominationUp$ carrier $(X,Y,T,N,A,M,P,R,H,C,Q,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\PietschDominationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, Banach endpoint exposure, bounded-operator exposure, operator-norm bound exposure, finite absolutely-summing test-window coverage, measure-window coverage, probability-normalization coverage, Real scalar-bound readback, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.

Every domination read by a consumer must factor through
$$
\BanachUp,\ \BoundedLinearOperatorUp,\ \OperatorNormUp,\ \MeasureUp,\ \ProbSpaceUp,\ \RealUp,\ \BHist,\ \hsame,\ \Cont,\ \Pkg,\ \NameCert .
$$
The certificate does not export Hahn--Banach separation, countable or dependent choice, a full domination theorem, selected representing measures, quotient operator equality, ambient Banach-space completeness beyond the displayed input rows, or bridge checking data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier coordinates. The source, target, operator, and norm data are the rows $X,Y,T,N$, so any operator-facing read is already routed through the existing Banach and bounded-operator packets.

The finite absolutely-summing tests are the entries of $A$. The measure and probability rows $M,P$ expose only their displayed event, value, and normalization windows, while the scalar endpoint $R$ supplies the Real-facing comparison data.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are exactly $H,C,Q,L$. Since the carrier has no further field, a consumer cannot recover a selected Hahn--Banach witness, a choice-selected probability measure, a quotient operator equality, or a completed domination theorem from the packet.
\end{proof}
