\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FareyIntervalRefinementUp$}

Following Hardy--Wright on Farey sequences and Stern--Brocot intervals, the $\FareyIntervalRefinementUp$ packet records a finite rational interval-refinement route between adjacent Farey boundary rows and the real-facing readback surface. It consumes the Farey-neighbor certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-farey-sequence-namecert}, the Stern--Brocot branch route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stern-brocot-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the rational approximation handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-rational-approximation-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a non-quotient finite refinement witness; it is not real completeness, a selected infinite branch, trichotomy, quotient equality, or a host density theorem.

\begin{definition}[Farey interval-refinement carrier]
\label{def:farey-interval-refinement-carrier}
A $\FareyIntervalRefinementUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(F,S,D,W,R,A,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\FareySequenceUp$ row naming the adjacent rational boundary pair and its denominator budget.
\item $S$ is the $\SternBrocotUp$ row naming the same interval by a finite branch word and mediant path.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for endpoint conversion, interval width, and requested precision.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ family of finite windows opened before a real-facing consumer reads the retained interval.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row assembled from the displayed windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $A$ is the $\RealRationalApproximationUp$ handoff row recording the rational interval as a finite approximation window.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $F,S,D,W,R,A$ have been exposed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Farey row, Stern--Brocot row, dyadic tolerance ledger, stream windows, regular readback, approximation handoff, real seal, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from $F,S,D$ through $W,R,A,E$ and then through the structural rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FareyIntervalRefinementUp$, $\FareySequenceUp$, $\SternBrocotUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealRationalApproximationUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FareyIntervalRefinementUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient real equality, a selected infinite Stern--Brocot branch, trichotomy, countable choice, host order completeness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Farey interval-refinement NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:farey-interval-refinement-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FareyIntervalRefinementUp$ carrier
$$
I=(F,S,D,W,R,A,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FareyIntervalRefinementUp}$ surface consists exactly of Farey adjacency admission, Stern--Brocot branch agreement for the same rational interval, DyadicRatCore tolerance control, StreamName finite-window exposure, RegSeqRat readback, RealRationalApproximation handoff, RealUp seal boundary, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:farey-interval-refinement-carrier}. The packet displays the Farey boundary pair, the Stern--Brocot branch row, dyadic endpoint tolerances, finite windows, readback, approximation handoff, real seal, and structural rows.

The interval agreement obligation is finite. The Farey row $F$ supplies adjacent rational boundaries at the displayed denominator budget, while the Stern--Brocot row $S$ supplies the finite mediant path for the same interval. Their compatibility is read before any downstream approximation row is available.

The real-facing obligations are downstream of that agreement. The dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ controls endpoint conversion and interval width; the windows $W$ expose the inspected prefixes; the readback $R$ and approximation handoff $A$ route the same finite interval to the seal row $E$.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly those rows. Since the carrier contains no quotient representative, selected infinite branch, trichotomy witness, choice principle, host completeness theorem, or ambient Real completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Farey interval-refinement real readback]
\label{thm:farey-interval-refinement-real-readback}
Every $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\FareyIntervalRefinementUp$ packet must read the finite route
$$
\FareySequenceUp,\ \SternBrocotUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealRationalApproximationUp,\ \RealUp
$$
through the displayed rows $F,S,D,W,R,A,E,H,C,P,N$. The packet can therefore serve Real and completion-facing consumers as a non-quotient rational interval-refinement witness, but it exports no proof of $\RealUp$ completeness, no selected limiting real, and no quotient equality of real names.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted packet and project its displayed rows. The Farey row $F$ and Stern--Brocot row $S$ fix the same rational interval before any dyadic or real-facing row is consulted.

Next read the dyadic and stream route. The tolerance ledger $D$ records the finite endpoint and width budget, and $W$ records the finite windows opened under that budget. The regular readback $R$ is assembled only from those displayed windows and tolerances.

The rational approximation handoff $A$ is downstream of $F,S,D,W,R$. Only after that handoff has been displayed may the terminal row $E$ be read as a $\RealUp$ seal.

Rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Thus every Real-facing consumer receives a finite interval-refinement witness and cannot extract a selected limit, quotient representative, trichotomy witness, countable-choice selector, host completeness theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness claim.
\end{proof}
