\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform triangle estimate ledger obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-triangle-estimate-ledger-obligations}
For an accepted $\CompactUniformTriangleEstimateLedgerUp$ packet $T=(K,F,U,M_X,M_Y,c,\rho,\varepsilon,\eta,S,Y,H,C,P,N)$, the local obligation surface is exactly compact net center projection, pointwise modulus readback, source metric triangle transport, target metric triangle transport, epsilon-third splitting, UniformModulus handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the compact metric row $K$ and choose the displayed finite-net center $c$ from its carrier rows. The continuous map row $F$ then supplies the pointwise modulus row $\rho$ at that center, so the source estimate starts with finite compact-net data rather than a selected subsequence.

Read the source metric triangle row $S$ from $M_X$. It combines the distance from $x$ to $c$ with the distance from $x$ to $x'$ inside the local source ball named by $\rho$.

Read the target metric triangle row $Y$ from $M_Y$. The epsilon-third budget $\eta$ splits the requested tolerance $\varepsilon$ into the pointwise modulus contribution and the two triangle contributions, so the target estimate closes by finite rational arithmetic and the displayed metric-law rows.

The row $U$ is the only UniformModulus handoff. The transport row $H$, replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$ preserve the same compact-net, pointwise-modulus, source-triangle, target-triangle, and tolerance-budget rows; hence no sequential compactness, global choice of centers, quotient equality, or ambient continuity principle escapes the ledger.
\end{proof}
