\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RealSegmentUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges on located intervals in constructive real analysis, the $\RealSegmentUp$ packet records a closed real segment by finite endpoint, located-order, apartness, and tolerance rows rather than by a host subset of an ambient real line. It sits after the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}. The packet gives compact-interval and uniform-modulus consumers a local interval object without importing Heine--Borel, open-cover compactness, sequential compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[RealSegment carrier]
\label{def:real-segment-carrier}
A $\RealSegmentUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(L,U,O,A,D,R,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the lower endpoint $\RealUp$ seal.
\item $U$ is the upper endpoint $\RealUp$ seal.
\item $O$ is the located order row witnessing that the endpoint comparison is read as displayed constructive order data.
\item $A$ is the located apartness row that separates exterior probes from the accepted closed segment boundary.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance-window ledger for interval enclosures, endpoint budgets, and refinement thresholds.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for endpoint approximants and accepted subinterval endpoints.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row that exposes only bounded endpoint and tolerance observations.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoint seals, located order, apartness, dyadic windows, regular readback, finite windows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a segment request through $L,U,O,A,D,R,W$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealSegmentUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealSegmentUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for Heine--Borel compactness, open-cover compactness, sequential compactness, a selected midpoint process, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RealSegment NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-segment-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealSegmentUp$ carrier
$$
S=(L,U,O,A,D,R,W,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RealSegmentUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five rows of obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item endpoint admission for the lower and upper $\RealUp$ seals $L,U$;
\item located boundary evidence through the order row $O$ and apartness row $A$;
\item finite tolerance exposure through the dyadic ledger $D$, regular rational readback $R$, and stream window $W$;
\item componentwise transport and displayed replay through $H,C$;
\item source provenance and local naming through $P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
Every accepted interval read is therefore routed through $L,U,O,A,D,R,W,H,C,P,N$, and no certificate obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-segment-carrier}. The endpoint seals $L,U$ are the only terminal real-name rows, while $O,A$ are the only rows that can witness located order and apartness at the boundary.

The finite tolerance rows are $D,R,W$. They expose dyadic budgets, regular rational endpoint approximants, and finite observation windows before any downstream compact-interval or modulus consumer reads the segment.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly the same packet. Since no excluded coordinate occurs in the carrier, the five listed obligation groups exhaust the local NameCert surface and do not export compactness or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RealSegment located subinterval transport]
\label{thm:real-segment-located-subinterval-transport}
Let $S=(L,U,O,A,D,R,W,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RealSegmentUp$ carrier. If a subinterval request refines the endpoint classifier through transported $\hsame$ rows and a smaller dyadic tolerance window, then the accepted subinterval row remains inside the same displayed route
$$
L,U,O,A,D,R,W,H,C,P,N.
$$
Endpoint classifier transport and dyadic tolerance refinement therefore preserve accepted subinterval rows without introducing Heine--Borel compactness, open-cover compactness, sequential compactness, selected witnesses, quotient equality of real names, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the endpoint rows $L,U$ and the located boundary rows $O,A$. Transport along $H$ preserves the componentwise endpoint classifier data, so the subinterval request still reads the same lower and upper real-name seals together with the same located order and apartness evidence.

Next refine the tolerance window. The dyadic ledger $D$ records the smaller budget, while $R$ and $W$ expose the corresponding regular rational endpoints and finite observation windows. Thus the refined subinterval is admitted through the same tolerance rows rather than through a new compactness principle.

Finally replay the transported request through $C$ and source it through $P,N$. The accepted subinterval row is still a row of the original finite packet, so no excluded compactness, quotient, choice, or ambient completeness endpoint is exported.
\end{proof}
