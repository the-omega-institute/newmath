\label{def:bishop-nested-interval-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $U$ is the upper-endpoint stream row, exposing each finite upper endpoint through the same dyadic and stream rows.
\item $D$ is the dyadic width-budget row, recording the displayed shrinking tolerance assigned to each inspected interval window.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ request row selecting the endpoint windows consumed by the packet.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback row for the paired endpoint streams and their shrinking-width budget.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $S$ and $R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoint streams, width budgets, windows, regular readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.

\begin{theorem}[Bishop nested interval monotone endpoint obligation]
\label{thm:bishop-nested-interval-monotone-endpoint-obligation}
For every accepted $\BishopNestedIntervalUp$ carrier
$$
I=(L,U,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the monotone endpoint obligation consists exactly of lower-endpoint stream exposure through $L$, upper-endpoint stream exposure through $U$, dyadic width-budget exposure through $D$, finite window exposure through $S$, regular rational readback through $R$, Real sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-nested-interval-carrier}. The endpoint rows $L$ and $U$ are the only rows that expose interval endpoints, and the budget row $D$ is the only row that records shrinking-width data.

Read the monotonicity obligation through finite windows. The row $S$ selects the inspected endpoint windows, so endpoint order and interval inclusion are checked only on displayed $\StreamNameUp$ requests and dyadic comparisons.

The regular and Real-facing rows are downstream. Row $R$ reads the paired endpoints and their width budget as regular rational data, while $E$ seals only that displayed readback as $\RealUp$ data.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no carrier coordinate supplies host completed points, open-cover compactness, selected intersections, quotient equality, trichotomy, choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the monotone endpoint obligation.
\end{proof}
