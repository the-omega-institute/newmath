\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\GrassmannianUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-grassmannian-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Griffiths and Harris on Grassmannians and Plucker coordinates, the $\GrassmannianUp$ packet records a finite subspace-row certificate before any consumer reads a Grassmannian as an ambient projective variety. It sits after the vector-space carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, the finite matrix ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, the determinant boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-determinant-namecert}, the alternating-word carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-exterior-algebra-namecert}, and the homogeneous-coordinate surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-projectivespace-namecert}. The packet names only displayed spanning rows, rank minors, wedge coordinates, projective incidence, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not select a host quotient vector space, import a scheme, assert Schubert calculus, or expose an ambient moduli object.

\begin{definition}[Grassmannian carrier]
\label{def:grassmannian-carrier}
A $\GrassmannianUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(V,M,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V$ is the consumed $\VecSpaceUp$ source row, displaying the ambient vector carrier, scalar action, and finite list of candidate generators.
\item $M$ is the $\MatrixUp$ coordinate row whose columns display the chosen finite generator table and the row operations visible to downstream consumers.
\item $D$ is the $\DeterminantUp$ minor ledger, recording the rank-window minors used to certify the displayed dimension and nonzero Plucker coordinates.
\item $E$ is the $\ExteriorAlgebraUp$ wedge row, carrying the alternating product of the displayed generator rows and the induced coordinate table.
\item $Q$ is the $\ProjectiveSpaceUp$ homogeneous-coordinate row for the wedge coordinate, read only after $V,M,D,E$ are visible.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $V,M,D,E,Q$ and for the package and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay routes by which consumers read the vector row, matrix table, determinant ledger, exterior coordinate, and projective endpoint in that order.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\VecSpaceUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\DeterminantUp$, $\ExteriorAlgebraUp$, $\ProjectiveSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\GrassmannianUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a selected basis outside the displayed table, quotient subspace equality, a host projective variety, Schubert stratification, moduli functor, or scheme-theoretic Plucker ideal.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Grassmannian NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:grassmannian-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:grassmannian-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\GrassmannianUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $G=(V,M,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N)$, vector-source exposure through $V$, matrix-coordinate exposure through $M$, determinant-rank exposure through $D$, exterior-coordinate exposure through $E$, projective-coordinate exposure through $Q$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Any accepted $\GrassmannianUp$ consumer must read those rows before it may cite a subspace, rank, Plucker coordinate, or homogeneous endpoint.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows. The only admitted packet is $G=(V,M,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N)$, so the local certificate can expose no data beyond those displayed coordinates.

The algebraic source is consumed in order. The vector-space row $V$ supplies the ambient finite vector operations, the matrix row $M$ displays the generator table, and the determinant row $D$ certifies the rank-window minors visible in that table.

The geometric endpoint is then read through $E$ and $Q$. The exterior row $E$ turns the generator table into an alternating coordinate, and the projective row $Q$ names its homogeneous class only after the preceding rows have been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no slot for quotient subspace equality, a host projective variety, Schubert data, a moduli functor, or a scheme-theoretic Plucker ideal, none of those objects is exported by the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Grassmannian Plucker ledger]
\label{thm:grassmannian-plucker-ledger}
Every Plucker-facing read of an accepted $\GrassmannianUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\VecSpaceUp,\ \MatrixUp,\ \DeterminantUp,\ \ExteriorAlgebraUp,\ \ProjectiveSpaceUp,\ H,\ C,\ P,\ N .
$$
The determinant minors and alternating wedge coordinate are ledger rows of the same carrier $G=(V,M,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N)$, and the projective endpoint $Q$ cannot be consumed as an independent projective variety, quotient-subspace classifier, Schubert cell, moduli point, or hidden comparison row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:grassmannian-namecert-obligations}. It forces every accepted read to expose the vector source, matrix table, determinant ledger, exterior coordinate, projective endpoint, and structural rows of one packet.

Read the Plucker coordinate through the determinant and exterior rows. The row $D$ supplies exactly the visible minors of $M$, and the row $E$ packages the same data as an alternating coordinate rather than as a separate ambient construction.

Now consume the projective endpoint. The row $Q$ is available only after $V,M,D,E$ have been projected, while $H,C,P,N$ keep transport, replay, provenance, and local naming tied to the same finite carrier.

Thus the Plucker ledger is a row-exhaustion statement for the displayed packet. No independent projective variety, quotient-subspace equality, Schubert cell, moduli point, or hidden comparison row is present in $G$, so no consumer can read one from the accepted certificate.
\end{proof}
