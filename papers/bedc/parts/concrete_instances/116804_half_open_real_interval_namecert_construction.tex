Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\HalfOpenRealIntervalUp$ packet records a half-open real interval $[a,b)$ as finite endpoint, containment, and boundary data rather than as a host subset of the classical real line. It sits between the dyadic endpoint ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the located endpoint surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-real-interval-namecert}, the closed interval boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedinterval-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the traditional interval shape with an included left boundary and an excluded right boundary, while keeping topology, quotient real equality, selected endpoint representatives, trichotomy, and ambient real completeness outside the carrier.

\begin{definition}[HalfOpenRealInterval carrier]
\label{def:half-open-real-interval-carrier}
A $\HalfOpenRealIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(L,U,O,D,W,R,E,A,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ and $U$ are the lower and upper endpoint $\RealUp$ seal rows.
\item $O$ is the endpoint-order row, recording the displayed route from $L$ to $U$ through finite comparison data.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ boundary ledger, carrying dyadic lower cells, upper-cut cells, positive-radius rows, and one-sided comparison budgets.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row used to inspect endpoint and candidate observations.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the same finite windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal handoff for candidate membership reads.
\item $A$ is the left-admission row, saying that the lower endpoint is included by the displayed boundary route.
\item $B$ is the right-refusal row, saying that the upper endpoint is excluded unless a stricter displayed left-of-upper witness is present.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transports for endpoint seals, order, dyadic boundary data, finite windows, readback, seal handoff, one-sided boundary rows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a membership request through $L,U,O,D,W,R,E,A,B,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HalfOpenRealIntervalUp$, $\LocatedRealIntervalUp$, $\ClosedBoundedIntervalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HalfOpenRealIntervalUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only by these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for a host topology, quotient real equality, selected representatives, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[HalfOpenRealInterval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:half-open-real-interval-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HalfOpenRealIntervalUp$ carrier
$$
I=(L,U,O,D,W,R,E,A,B,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\HalfOpenRealIntervalUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the two endpoint $\RealUp$ seals, endpoint-order row, dyadic boundary ledger, finite windows, regular rational readback, terminal seal handoff, one-sided boundary rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item located endpoint exactness, requiring endpoint and containment reads to pass through the $\LocatedRealIntervalUp$ and $\ClosedBoundedIntervalUp$ sibling surfaces before the half-open boundary is consumed;
\item dyadic boundary exactness, requiring the left-inclusion and right-exclusion reads to use the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger $D$;
\item regular readback exactness, requiring every membership observation to pass through the finite window row $W$ and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$ before reaching the $\RealUp$ seal handoff $E$;
\item left-boundary admission, requiring $A$ to display that $L$ belongs to the interval boundary;
\item right-boundary refusal, requiring $B$ to display that $U$ is not admitted as a member unless a stricter below-$U$ witness is supplied;
\item structural stability, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed rows above.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is stored in a host subset predicate, quotient real equality proof, selected endpoint representative, trichotomy branch, choice functional, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The projection exposes exactly the endpoint seals, endpoint-order row, dyadic boundary ledger, finite windows, regular readback, terminal seal handoff, one-sided boundary rows, and structural rows.

The located and closed-bounded sibling surfaces supply the endpoint discipline. They ensure that endpoint, order, and containment reads begin from displayed interval rows rather than from a host real-line subset.

The dyadic and regular-rational rows then determine the one-sided boundary. The ledger $D$ supplies the lower cells, upper-cut cells, and positive comparison budgets, while $W$ and $R$ expose the finite observations used by the candidate and endpoint seals.

Finally $A$ admits the left endpoint and $B$ refuses the right endpoint as a member of the half-open boundary. The rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same packet, so no host topology, quotient equality, selected representative, trichotomy branch, choice functional, or ambient completeness theorem can be read from the certificate.
\end{proof}
