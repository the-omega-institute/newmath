\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CofinalFunctorUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cofinal-functor-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Mac Lane on cofinal functors and limit computation, the $\CofinalFunctorUp$ packet records the finite BEDC carrier that lets a limit read pass through a displayed functor whose comma witnesses are already visible. It sits after the category carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-category-namecert}, the functor rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-functor-namecert}, and the limit surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-limit-namecert}. The packet contains category rows, a functor row, limit rows, finite comma-witness rows, connectedness witnesses, classifier transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row; it does not import a host functor category, a quotient equality on cones, or an ambient theorem that computes limits outside the displayed rows.

\begin{definition}[Cofinal functor carrier]
\label{def:cofinal-functor-carrier}
A $\CofinalFunctorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(C,D,F,L,U,W,K,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ and $D$ are the source and target $\CategoryUp$ rows;
\item $F$ is the $\FunctorUp$ row from $C$ to $D$;
\item $L$ is the $\LimitUp$ row read in $D$;
\item $U$ is the finite comma-object witness ledger, one row for each target object tested by the limit read;
\item $W$ is the connectedness witness ledger for the displayed comma rows;
\item $K$ is the classifier-transport row sending cone and comparison membership through $F,U,W,L$;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the two category rows, the functor row, the limit row, comma witnesses, connectedness witnesses, classifier transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the target-object read, comma-witness read, connectedness read, cone comparison, and limit handoff;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CategoryUp$, $\FunctorUp$, $\LimitUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CofinalFunctorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host functor category, a selected terminal object in a comma category, quotient equality of cones, an ambient category of diagrams, or an external limit-computation theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal functor NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cofinal-functor-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:cofinal-functor-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\CofinalFunctorUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for $Q=(C,D,F,L,U,W,K,H,R,P,N)$;
\item functor-row compatibility between the displayed $\CategoryUp$ rows $C,D$ and the $\FunctorUp$ row $F$;
\item comma-witness visibility, forcing every tested target-object row to expose its finite witness inside $U$ before the limit read can consume it;
\item connectedness witness routing through $W$, with no hidden host comma category;
\item classifier transport through $K$ for cone membership, comparison arrows, and limit-facing rows;
\item componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$ for all displayed rows;
\item displayed $\Cont$ replay through $R$ for witness inspection, connectedness routing, and limit handoff;
\item dependency provenance through $P$ and the local naming row $N$.
\end{enumerate}
Thus a readable $\CofinalFunctorUp$ packet is exhausted by finite Category, Functor, Limit, comma-witness, connectedness, transport, replay, provenance, and NameCert rows.
\end{theorem}
\leanstmt{BEDC.Derived.CofinalFunctorUp}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the displayed carrier. The first four rows provide only the two category surfaces, the functor row between them, and the target-side limit row. The comma-witness ledger $U$ is read before any limit-facing consumer can treat $F$ as cofinal, and the connectedness ledger $W$ is the only route by which the packet records the finite connectivity evidence.

The classifier and replay obligations are then forced by $K,H,R$. Cone membership and comparison rows move through the displayed functor, comma witnesses, and connectedness witnesses by $K$; componentwise sameness is retained in $H$; and the only continuation route is the finite replay row $R$.

Finally read the provenance and naming rows $P,N$. Since the carrier has no coordinate for a host functor category, quotient cone equality, selected comma terminal object, ambient diagram category, or external limit theorem, the listed finite rows exhaust the local $\NameCert_{\CofinalFunctorUp}$ obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal functor limit route]
\label{thm:cofinal-functor-limit-route}
Every limit read through an accepted $\CofinalFunctorUp$ carrier factors through the displayed $\CategoryUp$ rows $C,D$, the $\FunctorUp$ row $F$, the $\LimitUp$ row $L$, the comma-witness ledger $U$, the connectedness ledger $W$, and the classifier and replay rows $K,H,R$. The exported route is a finite BEDC handoff from the source category through the cofinal functor into the target-side limit row; it exports no host functor category, no quotient equality of cones, no selected comma-category terminal object, and no ambient limit-computation theorem outside $Q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project an accepted carrier $Q=(C,D,F,L,U,W,K,H,R,P,N)$. Any limit-facing read must first display the category rows and functor row, because $F$ is the only coordinate connecting $C$ to $D$ and $L$ is the only coordinate naming the target-side limit surface.

Next read $U$ and $W$. The finite comma-witness rows make the target-object tests visible, and the connectedness rows record the required routing between witnesses. The classifier row $K$ transports cone and comparison membership through these displayed rows, while $H$ and $R$ keep the same finite handoff stable under $\hsame$ and $\Cont$ replay.

The provenance and local naming rows close the packet. With no coordinate for a host functor category, quotient cone equality, or external limit computation, the limit route cannot bypass the displayed Category, Functor, Limit, comma-witness, connectedness, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\end{proof}
