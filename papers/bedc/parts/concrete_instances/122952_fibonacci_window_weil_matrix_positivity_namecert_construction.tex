\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for the Fibonacci-Window Weil Positivity Matrix}

\origin{human}

Following Weil and Bombieri, this packet records a finite positive-semidefiniteness shadow of the Weil explicit-formula criterion. The carrier is the displayed degree-$3$ Fibonacci-hat test window together with a concrete rational approximant of the Weil quadratic form on that window. It is a finite rational matrix certificate, not a global positivity theorem for all test functions.

The matrix is the scaled integer form
$$
W=
\begin{pmatrix}
303318 & -12152 & -193136\\
-12152 & 303318 & -12152\\
-193136 & -12152 & 303318
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
For $x=(x_0,x_1,x_2)$ the represented quadratic form is
$$
x^{\mathsf T}Wx
=303318(x_0^2+x_1^2+x_2^2)
-24304(x_0x_1+x_1x_2)
-386272x_0x_2 .
$$
The corner entry is genuinely negative, so positivity is not termwise positivity.

\begin{definition}[Fibonacci-window Weil matrix packet]
\label{def:fibonacci-window-weil-matrix-packet}
The finite packet consists of the rational matrix $W$, the coordinate row $x_0,x_1,x_2$, the symmetric coordinates
$$
u=x_0+x_2,\qquad v=x_0-x_2,\qquad w=x_1,
$$
and the two positive-semidefiniteness subcertificates
$$
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
110182 & -24304\\
-24304 & 606636
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
d=496454.
$$
The displayed determinant comparison is
$$
(-24304)^2=590684416
\leq 66840367752
=110182\cdot 606636.
$$
The minimum-eigenvalue subcertificate uses the shifted integer matrix $W-100000I$:
$$
B_{\mathrm{sh}}=
\begin{pmatrix}
10182 & -24304\\
-24304 & 406636
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
d_{\mathrm{sh}}=396454,
$$
with
$$
(-24304)^2=590684416
\leq 4140367752
=10182\cdot 406636.
$$
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Fibonacci-window Weil matrix positive definiteness]
\label{thm:fibonacci-window-weil-matrix-psd}
For all rational $x_0,x_1,x_2$,
$$
x^{\mathsf T}Wx\geq 0.
$$
More precisely, the exact rational identity
$$
\begin{aligned}
2(x^{\mathsf T}Wx)
&=(110182)u^2+2(-24304)uw+(606636)w^2\\
&\quad +(496454)v^2
\end{aligned}
$$
holds for $u=x_0+x_2$, $v=x_0-x_2$, and $w=x_1$. The first line is nonnegative by the $2\times2$ positive-semidefiniteness criterion for $B$, and the second line is nonnegative by the $1\times1$ criterion for $d$.

The same coordinates certify the minimum-eigenvalue lower bound for the scaled integer form:
$$
x^{\mathsf T}Wx\geq 100000(x_0^2+x_1^2+x_2^2).
$$
Equivalently, the inspected rational Weil matrix $10^{-9}W$ satisfies
$$
10^{-9}W\succeq 10^{-4}I.
$$
Indeed,
$$
\begin{aligned}
2x^{\mathsf T}(W-100000I)x
&=(10182)u^2+2(-24304)uw+(406636)w^2\\
&\quad +(396454)v^2,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the shifted $2\times2$ block and shifted scalar block are nonnegative by the displayed determinant and scalar comparisons.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The displayed change of variables separates the symmetric and antisymmetric coordinates for the endpoints $x_0$ and $x_2$. Expanding $u^2$, $v^2$, and $uw$ gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
&(110182)(x_0+x_2)^2+2(-24304)(x_0+x_2)x_1+(606636)x_1^2\\
&\quad +(496454)(x_0-x_2)^2\\
&=606636(x_0^2+x_1^2+x_2^2)-48608(x_0x_1+x_1x_2)-772544x_0x_2\\
&=2(x^{\mathsf T}Wx).
\end{aligned}
$$
The diagonal entries $110182$ and $606636$ are nonnegative, and the determinant comparison in \autoref{def:fibonacci-window-weil-matrix-packet} verifies the $2\times2$ block. The scalar coefficient $496454$ is nonnegative. Therefore the right-hand side is a sum of a nonnegative $2\times2$ quadratic block and a nonnegative $1\times1$ block. Since $2>0$, the original quadratic form is nonnegative.

For the lower bound, replace the diagonal coefficient $303318$ by $203318=303318-100000$ and keep the off-diagonal coefficients unchanged. The same expansion gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
&(10182)(x_0+x_2)^2+2(-24304)(x_0+x_2)x_1+(406636)x_1^2\\
&\quad +(396454)(x_0-x_2)^2\\
&=406636(x_0^2+x_1^2+x_2^2)-48608(x_0x_1+x_1x_2)-772544x_0x_2\\
&=2x^{\mathsf T}(W-100000I)x.
\end{aligned}
$$
The shifted diagonal entries $10182$ and $406636$ are nonnegative, the determinant comparison for $B_{\mathrm{sh}}$ verifies the shifted $2\times2$ block, and $396454$ verifies the shifted $1\times1$ block. Hence $x^{\mathsf T}(W-100000I)x\geq0$, which is the displayed lower bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite Weil-positivity shadow boundary]
\label{thm:fibonacci-window-weil-shadow-boundary}
The packet is a finite Weil-positivity shadow: a concrete rational approximant of the degree-$3$ Fibonacci-windowed Weil quadratic form is positive semidefinite despite genuine negative off-diagonal entries, so positivity is a nontrivial cancellation certified by symmetric diagonalization onto the $2\times2$ and $1\times1$ positive-semidefiniteness criteria.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The rational matrix $W/10^9$ is the inspected approximant to the Weil functional $T(\psi_r*\psi_s^\sharp)$ on the three-dimensional Fibonacci-hat test space. Its entries are computed from the von Mangoldt prime contribution for $n\leq F_5^2=25$ together with the archimedean $\Gamma$-factor logarithmic-derivative density. This construction is non-circular: it does not input zeta zero locations.

The Lean theorem is exact only after the rational entries are fixed. It proves the rational positive-semidefiniteness statement, the eigenvalue lower bound $W\succeq 10^{-4}I$ for the normalized matrix, and the displayed change of variables. The eigenvalue lower bound $W\succeq 10^{-4}I$, together with the external entry-certification error of order $10^{-9}\ll 10^{-4}$, shows the exact degree-$3$ Fibonacci-windowed Weil matrix is positive definite, not merely that the rational approximant is positive semidefinite.
\end{proof}
