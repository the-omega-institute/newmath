\begin{definition}[BoundedContinuousTest carrier]
\label{def:bounded-continuous-test-carrier}
A $\BoundedContinuousTestUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(K,M,F,U,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row when compact test support is
available.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row used for metric-domain reads outside a
compact support window.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row whose graph or pointwise-modulus
surface supplies the displayed probe.
\item $U$ is the finite bound ledger, recording the inspected upper and lower
real bounds for the probe values.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ comparison row for probe values, bounds, and
tolerance readback.
\item $E$ is the endpoint or tolerance window naming which finite probe
evaluation is being inspected.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact, metric,
continuous-map, bound, real-comparison, endpoint, replay, provenance, and
naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a probe request through compact
or metric source admission, continuous-map readback, finite bound inspection,
real comparison, and endpoint readback.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BoundedContinuousTestUp$,
$\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$,
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedContinuousTestUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It
has no coordinate for a host space of continuous functions, a supremum norm
completion, a selected dense family, weak compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$
completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BoundedContinuousTest NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bounded-continuous-test-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BoundedContinuousTestUp$ packet
$B=(K,M,F,U,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\BoundedContinuousTestUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact or
metric source admission, $\ContinuousMapUp$ probe admission, finite bound-ledger
inspection, $\RealUp$ comparison, endpoint or tolerance readback,
componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance,
and local naming. Every downstream $\PortmanteauUp$ or compact-metric consumer
must read the displayed $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$ or $\MetricUp$,
and $\RealUp$ rows before it may treat the probe as a bounded-continuous test.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bounded-continuous-test-carrier}. Its rows
name the compact support, metric source, continuous-map probe, finite bound
ledger, real comparisons, endpoint window, transport, replay, provenance, and
local certificate.

Probe admission is routed through $F$. The continuous-map row supplies the
finite graph or modulus observations, while $K$ or $M$ supplies the source
metric surface on which those observations are read.

Boundedness is a finite ledger. The row $U$ records the inspected bounds and
$R,E$ provide the real comparison and tolerance window, so a consumer obtains a
bounded-continuous probe only after these rows have been displayed together.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same finite surface.
Since no carrier coordinate stores a host function space, supremum norm
completion, selected dense family, weak compactness, or ambient completeness,
the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
