\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DyadicMidpointCompletionUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive Cauchy completion by finite approximation data, the $\DyadicMidpointCompletionUp$ packet records the midpoint-refinement route by which dyadic bracket approximants are turned into regular rational reads and then into a Real name. It sits over the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the constructive completion neighbour of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-richman-completion-namecert}. The packet names a finite midpoint-refinement completion surface; it is not Dedekind completeness, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Dyadic midpoint completion carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-midpoint-completion-carrier}
A $\DyadicMidpointCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(D,E,J,W,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ arithmetic row, recording the admitted dyadic endpoints, midpoint operation, interval inclusion comparisons, and tolerance cells used by the refinement.
\item $E$ is the endpoint row. It lists the finite left and right dyadic endpoints inspected at each refinement stage and records their displayed nesting order.
\item $J$ is the midpoint-refinement schedule. It records which midpoint bisections are performed, which half-interval survives each displayed comparison, and the finite dependency of every later bracket on earlier endpoint rows.
\item $W$ is the finite window budget. It records the number of endpoint and midpoint observations needed for each admitted precision request before any regular rational readback is exposed.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ readback row obtained from the finite endpoint and midpoint windows $E,J,W$.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regularity witness. It certifies that the stream windows read through $S$ satisfy the displayed dyadic Cauchy bounds supplied by $D$ and $W$.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, readable only after the dyadic endpoints, midpoint schedule, finite window budget, StreamName row, and RegSeqRat witness have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the dyadic arithmetic, endpoint row, midpoint schedule, window budget, StreamName readback, RegSeqRat witness, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
D,E,J,W \longmapsto S \longmapsto Q \longmapsto R .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicMidpointCompletionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicMidpointCompletionUp}$ row naming exactly this finite midpoint-refinement packet.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $D,E,J,W,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for Dedekind completeness, open-cover compactness, quotient reals, host equality, selected limits, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic midpoint completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-midpoint-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicMidpointCompletionUp$ carrier
$$
M=(D,E,J,W,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicMidpointCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for dyadic endpoints, the midpoint refinement schedule, the finite window budget, StreamName readback, RegSeqRat regularity, Real sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every Real-name consumer of the packet must read the finite route
$$
D,E,J,W \longmapsto S \longmapsto Q \longmapsto R
$$
before it can consume the terminal seal.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Project the accepted packet $M=(D,E,J,W,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$. The carrier exposes dyadic arithmetic, endpoint brackets, midpoint choices, precision windows, stream readback, regular rational bounds, the Real seal, and the four structural rows in the displayed order.

The endpoint and midpoint data are finite. The row $D$ supplies dyadic arithmetic and comparisons, while $E$ lists the endpoint brackets and $J$ lists the midpoint bisections that refine them. The window row $W$ fixes, for each admitted precision request, the finite prefix of those rows that must be inspected.

Readback then proceeds through $S$ and $Q$. The $\StreamNameUp$ row is obtained only from the displayed finite windows, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row records the regular Cauchy bounds witnessed by the dyadic tolerance ledger and midpoint schedule.

Finally $R,H,C,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no carrier row stores Dedekind completeness, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, host equality, a selected limiting representative, or ambient Real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
