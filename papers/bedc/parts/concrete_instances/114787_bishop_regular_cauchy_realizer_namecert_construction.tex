\begin{definition}[BishopRegularCauchyRealizer carrier]
\label{def:bishop-regular-cauchy-realizer-carrier}
A $\BishopRegularCauchyRealizerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}

\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row, carrying the finite windows at which the rational name is inspected.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, recording the rational values attached to those windows.
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyModulusUp$ row that binds a precision request to a displayed window budget before any Real-facing seal is read.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ endpoint seal row receiving the regular Cauchy name only after $D,S,Q,M$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for dyadic tolerance, stream window, rational readback, modulus, endpoint seal, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay along the same route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\CauchyModulusUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCauchyRealizerUp}$ row.

\begin{theorem}[BishopRegularCauchyRealizer NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-regular-cauchy-realizer-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopRegularCauchyRealizerUp$ carrier $R=(D,S,Q,M,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCauchyRealizerUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed dyadic tolerance ledger, stream-window row, regular-rational readback row, Cauchy-modulus row, Real seal, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Every Real-facing read of the packet must expose the route
$$
D,S,Q,M \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
It cannot replace the route by quotient equality of streams, a host-selected limit, countable choice, or an ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The accepted packet displays only the rows $D,S,Q,M,E,H,C,P,N$, and the local naming row has no coordinate outside that list.

The dyadic tolerance row $D$ supplies the precision scale, the stream row $S$ supplies the finite inspection windows, and the regular rational row $Q$ supplies the rational values at those windows. The modulus row $M$ is the only admitted route from a precision request to a window budget.

The endpoint seal $E$ is downstream from those four rows. Componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, and provenance through $P$ preserve the same route before the local $\NameCert$ row $N$ names it.

The excluded objects are absent from the carrier. Therefore the NameCert obligations expose the finite regular Cauchy realizer route and do not create quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
