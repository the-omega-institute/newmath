source metric triangle control, pointwise modulus rows at the selected center,

the rational tolerance rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert},

\begin{definition}[Compact-uniform source-target triangle ledger carrier]
\label{def:compact-uniform-source-target-triangle-ledger-carrier}
A $\CompactUniformSourceTargetTriangleLedgerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$
packet
$$
T=(K,X,Y,C,R,A,S,U,H,G,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the compact finite-net row, exposing the selected
$\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ center and the coverage witness for the source point.
\item $X$ is the source $\MetricSpaceUp$ row, including the distance witness
from the source point to the selected center and the source triangle witness
for the second source point.
\item $Y$ is the target $\MetricSpaceUp$ row, including the triangle witness
that combines the two pointwise target estimates.
\item $C$ is the pointwise-modulus row at the selected compact-net center.
\item $R$ is the positive $\RatUp$ tolerance ledger, carrying the
$\varepsilon/3$ target budgets and the source precision budget.
\item $A$ is the center-alignment row, tying the compact-net center used by
$K$ to the center consumed by $C$, $X$, and $Y$.
\item $S$ is the final source-triangle row, showing that the second source
point lies inside the same pointwise-modulus window.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer handoff row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,X,Y,C,R,A,S,U,G,P,N$.
\item $G$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact-net coverage through
source triangle control, pointwise modulus, target triangle control, and the
uniform-modulus handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over
$\CompactUniformSourceTargetTriangleLedgerUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\RatUp$,
$\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local
$\NameCert_{\CompactUniformSourceTargetTriangleLedgerUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. It
has no coordinate for host compactness, an external center selector, semantic

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact-uniform triangle ledger NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-source-target-triangle-ledger-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactUniformSourceTargetTriangleLedgerUp$ carrier
$$
T=(K,X,Y,C,R,A,S,U,H,G,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformSourceTargetTriangleLedgerUp}$ surface
consists exactly of compact finite-net admission, source metric admission,
target metric admission, pointwise-modulus admission, positive RatUp tolerance
admission, center alignment, final source-triangle admissibility,
UniformModulus handoff, componentwise hsame transport, displayed Cont replay,
Pkg provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of
\autoref{def:compact-uniform-source-target-triangle-ledger-carrier}. The
estimate rows are $K,X,Y,C,R,A,S,U$, and the structural rows are $H,G,P,N$.

The compact finite-net row $K$ supplies the selected center only as displayed
bundle data. The alignment row $A$ keeps that center identical to the one used
by the source metric row $X$ and the pointwise-modulus row $C$.

The source triangle row $S$ is then forced through $X$ and the RatUp ledger
$R$. The target triangle row in $Y$ can be consumed only after the two
pointwise target estimates from $C$ have been displayed.

Finally the handoff row $U$ receives the same finite estimate by the displayed
transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows. Since no excluded host object
is a carrier coordinate, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert
surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact-uniform final source-target estimate]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-source-target-triangle-ledger-final-estimate}
Every $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer of an accepted
$\CompactUniformSourceTargetTriangleLedgerUp$ carrier receives the final
compact-uniform estimate only through the finite route
$$
K,X,Y,C,R,A,S,U,H,G,P,N .
$$
When $K$ selects a center $c$, $X$ displays source distances from $x$ and
$x'$ to $c$, $C$ displays the two pointwise target estimates at $c$, and $Y$
displays the target triangle row, the handoff $U$ certifies the
tolerance-only uniform-modulus ledger without adding a host compactness theorem,
a separate center selector, quotient point equality, semantic continuity, or
ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the compact-net row $K$ and the alignment row $A$. They give one displayed
center for the whole route, so the source triangle and target triangle cannot
choose different compact-net witnesses.

Use the source metric row $X$ and the source-triangle row $S$. The source point
$x$ is covered by the compact net, and the displayed source distance from $x$
to $x'$ keeps $x'$ inside the same pointwise-modulus window named by $R$.

Apply the pointwise-modulus row $C$ at the selected center. It supplies the two
target estimates from the center to the two source points, using only the
positive RatUp tolerance ledger.

The target metric row $Y$ composes those two target estimates by its triangle
row. The continuation row $G$ and provenance row $P$ then deliver exactly this
finite estimate to the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff $U$, while $H$ and $N$
record classifier-stable transport and local naming. No absent host object can
be read from the accepted packet.
\end{proof}
