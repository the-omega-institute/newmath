Following Vietoris hyperspace practice and the domain-theoretic lower powerdomain reading of closed-set completion, the $\LowerVietorisCompletionUp$ packet records completion through finite lower-hit observations. It sits after the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the Vietoris topology surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vietoris-topology-namecert}, the lower topology route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lower-topology-namecert}, the Cauchy ideal route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-ideal-namecert}, the finite probe cover fold of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probe-bundle-cover-fold-namecert}, and the completion reflector of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-reflector-namecert}. The packet names only the finite lower-hit, directed-ideal, and completion-handoff rows needed by downstream hyperspace completion consumers.

\begin{definition}[Lower Vietoris completion carrier]
\label{def:lower-vietoris-completion-carrier}
A $\LowerVietorisCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
V=(T,L,H,I,F,D,R,S,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row, exposing the ambient open observations used by the lower-hit classifier.
\item $L$ is the $\LowerTopologyUp$ row, recording that observations are read by hit data and not by upper miss data.
\item $H$ is the $\VietorisTopologyUp$ hyperspace row, restricted to the lower subbasis generated by displayed opens.
\item $I$ is the $\CauchyIdealUp$ directed-ideal row, storing the finite tail of lower-hit obligations that is sent to completion.
\item $F$ is the finite $\ProbeBundleCoverFoldUp$ row, listing the displayed opens whose nonempty intersection with the approximating closed set is observed.
\item $D$ is the directed lower-hit stability row, asserting that the finite hit list is monotone along the ideal tail.
\item $R$ is the $\CompletionReflectorUp$ handoff row, carrying the lower-hit ideal into the completion corridor.
\item $S$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology, lower topology, Vietoris, ideal, finite probe, stability, reflector, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from ambient opens to lower-hit probes, from probes to directed ideals, and from directed ideals to the completion handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LowerVietorisCompletionUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\LowerTopologyUp$, $\VietorisTopologyUp$, $\CauchyIdealUp$, $\ProbeBundleCoverFoldUp$, $\CompletionReflectorUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LowerVietorisCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for upper Vietoris miss tests, Hausdorff distance, compactness, selected limits, quotient equality of closed sets, powerset extensionality, or ambient metric completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lower Vietoris completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lower-vietoris-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LowerVietorisCompletionUp$ carrier $V=(T,L,H,I,F,D,R,S,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LowerVietorisCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of ambient topology admission through $T$, lower topology admission through $L$, lower Vietoris hyperspace readback through $H$, directed Cauchy ideal visibility through $I$, finite lower-hit probe exposure through $F$, directed lower-hit stability through $D$, completion handoff through $R$, componentwise transport through $S$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lower-vietoris-completion-carrier}. Rows $T,L,H$ identify the ambient-open, lower-topology, and Vietoris hyperspace sources before any completion read is consumed.

The lower-hit data is finite at each read. Row $F$ lists the displayed opens, and $D$ records monotone stability of those hits along the directed ideal row $I$. Thus a consumer cannot replace the lower-hit route by an upper miss test, a metric Hausdorff estimate, or an extensional powerset equality.

The terminal rows preserve the same route. Row $R$ is the only completion-facing handoff, while $S,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the packet without adding compactness, selected limits, quotient equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Lower-hit completion consumer route]
\label{thm:lower-vietoris-completion-consumer-route}
Every hyperspace completion consumer of an accepted $\LowerVietorisCompletionUp$ packet must first read the finite route
$$
T,L,H,I,F,D,R,S,C,P,N .
$$
The exported object is therefore a lower-hit completion handoff from directed finite observations; it is not a Fell topology packet, an upper Vietoris classifier, a Hausdorff metric completion, or a selected completed closed set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:lower-vietoris-completion-namecert-obligations}. It identifies the entire accepted local surface, so every consumer receives the topology, lower topology, lower Vietoris, directed ideal, finite probe, stability, completion, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The classifier is lower-hit only. The finite probe row records observed intersection with displayed opens, and the directed stability row keeps those observations coherent along the Cauchy ideal tail.

Consequently the completion reflector row can export only this finite lower-hit handoff. Since no row stores upper miss tests, Fell convergence, Hausdorff metric estimates, a selected closed-set limit, or quotient equality, none of those structures is available to the consumer.
\end{proof}
