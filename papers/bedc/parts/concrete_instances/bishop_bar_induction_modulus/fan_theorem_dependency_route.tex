\begin{theorem}[Bishop bar-induction modulus fan-theorem dependency]
\label{thm:bishop-bar-induction-modulus-fan-theorem-dependency}
Every $\RealUp$ or Completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\BishopBarInductionModulusUp$ packet must first read the $\BrouwerBarInductionUp$ barred-spread row and the $\FanTheoremUp$ finite-fan row before the $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ seal rows can be consumed. In carrier terms, the barred-spread row $B$ and finite tree-depth ledger $T$ precede the window row $W$, readback row $R$, modulus output $M$, terminal seal $E$, transport rows $H$, continuation rows $C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$. The packet exports a finite modulus handoff, not countable choice, open-cover compactness, quotient stream equality, selected branch data, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-bar-induction-modulus-carrier}. The rows $B$ and $T$ are displayed before the analytic window route; $B$ records the barred-spread ancestry and $T$ records the finite fan boundary.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:bishop-bar-induction-modulus-namecert-obligations}. The barred-spread ancestry clause requires the $\BrouwerBarInductionUp$ row before a modulus read, and the fan-boundary exactness clause confines the finite fan surface to $T$.

Route the remaining rows through the window boundary of \autoref{thm:bishop-bar-induction-modulus-window-boundary}. The $\StreamNameUp$ row $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$, modulus output $M$, and $\RealUp$ seal $E$ are consumed only after the barred-spread and finite-fan rows have been displayed.

Finally use the carrier non-escape clauses built into the same obligations. Since countable choice, open-cover compactness, quotient stream equality, selected branch data, and ambient Real completeness are not coordinates of $K=(B,T,I,W,R,M,E,H,C,P,N)$, they cannot replace the finite dependency route.
\end{proof}
