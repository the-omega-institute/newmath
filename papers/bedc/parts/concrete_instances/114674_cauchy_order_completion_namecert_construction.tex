\begin{definition}[Cauchy order completion carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-order-completion-carrier}
A $\CauchyOrderCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\CauchyUp$ source row, naming the accepted Cauchy packet whose completion-facing order route is being inspected.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row attached to the same Cauchy source.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the displayed windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and comparison ledger used to compare displayed readbacks.
\item $Q$ is the order-comparison row, read through $\RegularCauchyOrderUp$ and carrying only displayed lower, upper, and apartness-facing comparisons of the regular Cauchy names.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the Cauchy source, stream windows, rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, and order-comparison row have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,S,R,D,Q,E,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an order or completion request through $X,S,R,D,Q,E$ and the structural rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyOrderCompletionUp$, $\CauchyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegularCauchyOrderUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyOrderCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, a selected limit, trichotomy, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy order completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-order-completion-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\CauchyOrderCompletionUp$ carrier $O=(X,S,R,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyOrderCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the Cauchy source row, stream-window row, RegSeqRat readback row, DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger, order-comparison row, Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. No local obligation may replace those rows by quotient completion equality, a selected limit, trichotomy, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier definition. The packet exposes exactly $X,S,R,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N$, so carrier admission and local naming are reads of the displayed tuple.

The Cauchy and readback rows are opened first. The source row $X$ fixes the accepted Cauchy packet, $S$ fixes the inspected windows, $R$ fixes the regular rational values, and $D$ fixes the dyadic tolerance ledger.

The order-comparison and seal rows are downstream. The row $Q$ reads only the displayed regular Cauchy order data, and $E$ seals that same route for Real-facing consumers.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the route. Since the excluded host and quotient objects are absent from the carrier, they cannot become NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
