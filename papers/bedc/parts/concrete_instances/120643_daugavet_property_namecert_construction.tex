Following Kadets and Shvidkoy on the Daugavet property in Banach-space geometry, the $\DaugavetPropertyUp$ packet records the finite row surface by which a Banach carrier exposes the rank-one norm equation to later operator and geometry consumers. It sits over the norm carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-norm-namecert}, the Banach rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-namecert}, the scalar-functional surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-linear-functional-namecert}, the linear-map carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-linearmap-namecert}, and the James-space comparison packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-james-space-namecert}. The packet names a displayed rank-one equation ledger; it is not a host class of all rank-one operators, not a selected norming functional, not a quotient operator algebra, and not an ambient compactness or reflexivity theorem.

\begin{definition}[Daugavet property carrier]
\label{def:daugavet-property-carrier}
A $\DaugavetPropertyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(B,N,F,L,R,J,E,H,C,P,A)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BanachUp$ source row whose vector and completeness reads are visible to the packet.
\item $N$ is the $\NormUp$ row used to compare the identity-plus-operator norm with the operator norm.
\item $F$ is the $\BoundedLinearFunctionalUp$ row naming the scalar functional in the displayed rank-one factorization.
\item $L$ is the $\LinearMapUp$ row naming the vector action used by the same rank-one factorization.
\item $R$ is the rank-one operator ledger, recording the finite factorization data read through $F$ and $L$.
\item $J$ is the $\JamesSpaceUp$ comparison row, present only as a Banach-geometry sibling and not as a reflexivity theorem.
\item $E$ is the equation row recording that the displayed rank-one read is judged against the norm equation $\lVert I+T\rVert=1+\lVert T\rVert$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Banach, norm, functional, linear-map, rank-one, James-space, equation, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DaugavetPropertyUp$, $\BanachUp$, $\NormUp$, $\BoundedLinearFunctionalUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\JamesSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $A$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DaugavetPropertyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets whose Daugavet read is exhausted by $B,N,F,L,R,J,E,H,C,P,A$. It has no coordinate for all bounded operators on the host space, a selected norming vector, a quotient operator class, weak compactness, reflexivity, or an ambient Banach-space theorem outside the displayed rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Daugavet property rank-one equation]
\label{thm:daugavet-property-rank-one-equation}
For every accepted $\DaugavetPropertyUp$ carrier
$$
D=(B,N,F,L,R,J,E,H,C,P,A),
$$
any consumer read of the rank-one equation must first expose the Banach source row $B$, the norm row $N$, the bounded-functional row $F$, the linear-map row $L$, and the rank-one ledger $R$. Only after those rows are visible may the equation row $E$ be read as the finite statement that the displayed rank-one operator $T$ satisfies $\lVert I+T\rVert=1+\lVert T\rVert$ for the named Banach row. The $\JamesSpaceUp$ row $J$ remains a comparison surface, and the structural rows $H,C,P,A$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:daugavet-property-carrier}. Rows $B$ and $N$ expose the Banach source and norm comparison data before any operator equation is available.

The rank-one stage is $F,L,R$. The bounded functional and linear-map rows name the displayed factorization, and the ledger $R$ records that the operator read is finite and local to those rows.

Only then can the equation row $E$ be consumed. It records the equality for the displayed rank-one operator and the displayed Banach norm, while $J$ stays a sibling comparison row rather than a source of reflexivity or weak compactness.

Finally $H,C,P,A$ keep the same finite route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier has no coordinate for a host operator space, selected norming data, quotient equality, or ambient Banach theorem, the consumer cannot export any of those endpoints.
\end{proof}
