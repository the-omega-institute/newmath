\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SkorohodSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-skorohod-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Skorokhod and Billingsley, the $\SkorohodSpaceUp$ packet records a real-valued cadlag path space as finite BEDC NameCert data. It consumes the cadlag path carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cadlag-real-function-namecert}, the terminal real-name route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the metric interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. The packet gives later stochastic-process and path-topology chapters a finite path-space surface without quotient path equality, selected path limits, probability laws, host compactness, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[SkorohodSpace carrier]
\label{def:skorohod-space-carrier}
A $\SkorohodSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(K,E,M,W,T,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CadlagRealFunctionUp$ source row, exposing right-continuity and left-limit data only through finite cadlag windows.
\item $E$ is the family of $\RealUp$ endpoint rows read from the displayed path windows and endpoint probes.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ comparison row used to record path-window tolerances and finite comparison radii.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation schedule for time windows, endpoint probes, and path-window requests.
\item $T$ is the time-change window ledger, naming only finite monotone window comparisons and their tolerance budgets.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational observations produced by $W,T,M$ and sealed through $E$.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for cadlag windows, real endpoint rows, metric tolerances, time-change windows, rational readback, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a path-space request through $K,E,M,W,T,R,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SkorohodSpaceUp$, $\CadlagRealFunctionUp$, $\RealUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SkorohodSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,E,M,W,T,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient path equality, selected limits, probability laws, Borel sigma-algebras, host time-change equivalence, compactness, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SkorohodSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:skorohod-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SkorohodSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
S=(K,E,M,W,T,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SkorohodSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of cadlag-window admission, $\RealUp$ endpoint-row readback, $\MetricUp$ tolerance ledger exposure, $\StreamNameUp$ observation scheduling, time-change window ledger exposure, $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational readback, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:skorohod-space-carrier}. The cadlag source row $K$ is the only path row, and the endpoint, metric, stream, time-change, and rational readback rows are exactly $E,M,W,T,R$.

The cadlag-window obligation is inherited through the source chapter \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cadlag-real-function-namecert}. Endpoint requests then pass through the real-name route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, while metric comparisons pass through the tolerance row $M$ rather than through a host path metric.

The scheduling and readback obligations are finite. The rows $W$ and $T$ expose time windows and time-change windows, and $R$ records the regular rational observations available after those windows and tolerances have been displayed.

Finally the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, provenance, and locally name the same route. Since no excluded analytic or probabilistic object is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[SkorohodSpace cadlag-window handoff]
\label{thm:skorohod-space-cadlag-window-handoff}
Every path-space consumer of an accepted $\SkorohodSpaceUp$ packet must first read the $\CadlagRealFunctionUp$ window row $K$, the $\RealUp$ endpoint rows $E$, the $\MetricUp$ tolerance ledger $M$, the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule $W$, the time-change window ledger $T$, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$ before transport, replay, provenance, or local naming can be consumed. The handoff is a finite path-space route, not quotient path equality, a selected path limit, a probability law, a host Skorohod topology theorem, or an ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a path-space request. The only path source is the cadlag row $K$, so a consumer must first expose the finite right-continuity and left-limit windows supplied by the cadlag route.

Endpoint and comparison data are downstream of those windows. The rows $E$ and $M$ supply Real-valued endpoint rows and metric tolerance budgets, while $W$ and $T$ record the finite observation and time-change windows on which comparisons are allowed to run.

The rational readback row $R$ is available only after the window and tolerance ledgers have been displayed. Transport through $H$, replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$ preserve that same route for downstream stochastic-process chapters.

Because quotient path equality, selected limits, probability laws, host topology theorems, and ambient completeness have no carrier coordinate, no path-space consumer can obtain them through this handoff.
\end{proof}
