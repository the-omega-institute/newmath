\begin{definition}[Constructive Cauchy-Dini carrier]
\label{def:constructive-cauchy-dini-carrier}
A $\ConstructiveCauchyDiniUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(S,M,W,K,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the stream-source row, listing the finite observation schedule for the monotone approximation family.
\item $M$ is the monotone comparison row, recording the Dini-side order comparisons visible in the inspected windows.
\item $W$ is the finite compact-window row, read through the compact uniform modulus surface and carrying only displayed finite cover or finite-net data.
\item $K$ is the Cauchy modulus row, recording the finite tail budget that turns the monotone compact-window comparison into a Cauchy read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same scheduled windows and Cauchy budget.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, consumed only after $S,M,W,K,R$ have all been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream source, monotone comparison, compact window, Cauchy budget, readback, seal, and structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite Cauchy--Dini route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ConstructiveCauchyDiniUp$, $\CompactUniformModulusUp$, $\CauchyModulusUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

\begin{theorem}[Constructive Cauchy-Dini NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:constructive-cauchy-dini-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:constructive-cauchy-dini-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveCauchyDiniUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, stream-source visibility, monotone comparison stability, finite compact-window exposure, Cauchy modulus ordering, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal ordering, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from the tuple $D=(S,M,W,K,R,E,H,C,P,N)$. The stream source, monotone comparison, compact window, Cauchy budget, regular readback, and terminal seal are all displayed rows of the same finite packet.

The analytic handoff is ordered. A consumer must first read the inspected stream windows in $S$, then the monotone comparison row $M$, then the compact finite-window row $W$, and only then the Cauchy modulus row $K$.

The readback and seal do not add hidden data. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ reads the same window and budget, while $E$ seals the resulting real-facing row after $R$ is visible. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that route without importing host completeness, quotient equality, open-cover compactness, selected limits, choice, or an unrecorded convergence theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive Cauchy-Dini finite-modulus handoff]
\label{thm:constructive-cauchy-dini-finite-modulus-handoff}
Every accepted $\ConstructiveCauchyDiniUp$ read that reaches a $\RealUp$ consumer factors through the finite route
$$
S,M,W,K,R,E,H,C,P,N .
$$
In particular, the compact finite-modulus rows and monotone Dini rows must be displayed before the Cauchy modulus is read, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback must be displayed before the terminal real-name seal. The handoff exports no host uniform-limit theorem, quotient equality of real names, open-cover compactness proof, selected limiting function, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:constructive-cauchy-dini-namecert-obligations}. They place the stream source, monotone comparison, compact finite window, and Cauchy modulus in one displayed packet before any readback row is available.

Next read the regular-rational handoff. The row $R$ is attached to the same scheduled windows and the same Cauchy budget, so a consumer cannot detach it from $S,M,W,K$.

Finally consume the terminal seal. The row $E$ is downstream of $R$, and $H,C,P,N$ keep the route componentwise transported, replayed, sourced, and named. Since the excluded host objects have no carrier coordinate, the $\RealUp$ consumer receives only the finite Cauchy--Dini modulus handoff.
\end{proof}
