\begin{definition}[d'Alembert ratio test carrier]
\label{def:dalembert-ratio-test-carrier}

$$

\item $S$ is the $\RealSeriesUp$ source row, exposing only the inspected term stream and finite partial-sum windows.
\item $A$ is the adjacent-term row, listing the displayed pair windows $a_n,a_{n+1}$ after the relevant source threshold.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ margin row, recording the rational witness $q<1$ used by the d'Alembert comparison.
\item $M$ is the ratio-bound row, displaying the tail comparison $|a_{n+1}| \leq q|a_n|$ only through the rows $A,Q,W,R,D$.

\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule for the adjacent terms, margin checks, partial sums, and tail requests.
