It sits over the idempotence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-idempotence-namecert}, the functorial route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionfunctor-namecert}, the finite window source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[CauchyCompletionStabilizer carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-completion-stabilizer-carrier}
A $\CauchyCompletionStabilizerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(C,I,F,W,D,R,E,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the source completion row, recording the first completion-facing read before any repeated-read stabilization is available.
\item $I$ is the $\CauchyCompletionIdempotenceUp$ row, exposing the finite idempotence comparison between the first completion read and the repeated completion read.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$ row, carrying the functorial route that transports the same displayed completion request.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row used to inspect the repeated read.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row controlling the finite window and comparison budget.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the same window and dyadic tolerance rows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $C,I,F,W,D,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source completion, idempotence comparison, functorial route, finite window, dyadic tolerance, regular readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
C \longmapsto I,F \longmapsto W,D,R \longmapsto E .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyCompletionStabilizerUp$, $\CauchyCompletionIdempotenceUp$, $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionStabilizerUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $C,I,F,W,D,R,E,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected limits, countable choice, host equality between completed objects, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyCompletionStabilizer NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-stabilizer-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompletionStabilizerUp$ carrier
$$
S=(C,I,F,W,D,R,E,H,T,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionStabilizerUp}$ surface consists exactly of source completion exposure through $C$, repeated-read stabilization through $I$, functorial completion transport through $F$, finite StreamName window exposure through $W$, DyadicRatCore tolerance exposure through $D$, RegSeqRat readback through $R$, Real sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $T$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-stabilizer-carrier}. The displayed rows are the source completion, idempotence, functorial route, finite window, dyadic tolerance, regular readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The completion rows are read first. Row $C$ supplies the source completion read, row $I$ compares it with the repeated read, and row $F$ keeps the comparison inside the functorial completion route.

The analytic rows remain downstream of that route. The finite window $W$, dyadic tolerance $D$, regular readback $R$, and terminal seal $E$ are part of the same packet, so a consumer cannot detach a selected limit or a quotient completion object from the repeated-read comparison.

Finally $H,T,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same carrier. Since quotient completion, selected limits, countable choice, host equality, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness are absent from the packet, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
