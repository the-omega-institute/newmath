\begin{theorem}[FieldExt vector-space sibling dependency]
\label{thm:fieldext-vecspace-sibling-dependency}
Every accepted $\FieldExtUp$ carrier must pass through the base $\FieldUp$ row, the extension $\FieldUp$ row, the embedding row between them, and the $\VecSpaceUp$ scalar-action row before the local $\NameCert_{\FieldExtUp}$ surface is readable. Thus the packet reaches the field endpoint surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-field-namecert} and the vector-space surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert} only through displayed algebraic sibling rows, not through an ambient host extension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted $\FieldExtUp$ carrier to its base-field row, extension-field row, embedding row, and scalar-action row. The two field rows supply the endpoints read by the field sibling, while the embedding row records the accepted comparison between those endpoints.

The $\VecSpaceUp$ scalar-action row is then read over the displayed base field. It is the row that lets a vector-space consumer see scalar multiplication from the extension packet, and it cannot be reconstructed from the local name alone.

Only after these rows are visible does the local $\NameCert_{\FieldExtUp}$ surface become readable. Hence every accepted read factors through the Field and VecSpace siblings named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-field-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}.
\end{proof}
