\begin{theorem}[FiniteCoverNerveUniformModulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cover-nerve-uniform-modulus-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project an accepted packet through \autoref{def:finite-cover-nerve-uniform-modulus-carrier}. The compact, continuous, cover, nerve, Lebesgue-number, rational-comparison, metric, and uniform-modulus rows are all explicit coordinates.

The transport coordinate $H$ preserves those rows componentwise. The replay coordinate $C$ lets compact, continuous, metric, rational, cover, and uniform-modulus consumers read the same finite packet, but it does not create an open cover or a host minimum.

Consequently the local NameCert obligations are exactly the displayed finite rows. Since the excluded objects have no carrier coordinate, they cannot be used to discharge the certificate surface.
