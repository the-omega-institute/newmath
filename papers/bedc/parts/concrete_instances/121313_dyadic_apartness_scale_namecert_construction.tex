\begin{theorem}[Dyadic apartness scale NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-apartness-scale-namecert-obligations}

$$

\item regular-window exposure requiring each separation read to pass through the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ before the Real seal is consumed;

The located-apartness and metric clauses then route the same data through $L$ and $M$. The witness is positive separation at a displayed scale, and the RealMetric row records only the finite estimate needed for that separation.

Finally, $E,H,C,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, package, and name the same finite rows. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for trichotomy, quotient equality, selected limits, choice, ambient completeness, or bridge checking, those objects cannot be exported by the NameCert surface.
