\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HeineBorelFiniteCoverUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-heine-borel-finite-cover-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Borel and Bishop and Bridges, the $\HeineBorelFiniteCoverUp$ packet records the constructive closed-interval finite-cover route by which displayed dyadic meshes and radius ledgers produce a finite cover bundle before any Real or compact-metric consumer reads compactness-facing data. It sits over the closed-bounded interval net of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedintervalnet-namecert}, the compact metric handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the dyadic rational arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the rational comparison row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite closed-interval certificate: it exposes the displayed bundle and its extraction route without asserting open-cover compactness for arbitrary $\RealUp$ subsets.

\begin{definition}[Heine--Borel finite-cover carrier]
\label{def:heine-borel-finite-cover-carrier}
A $\HeineBorelFiniteCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(I,M,U,L,E,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the closed bounded interval source row, read through $\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp$, with named endpoint seals, boundedness data, and the interval membership route.
\item $M$ is the dyadic mesh row, read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and $\RatUp$, recording the mesh scale, rational cell endpoints, and cell-containment comparisons.
\item $U$ is the finite cover bundle, listing the displayed cover entries, their inspected cells, and the finite membership witnesses used by the certificate.
\item $L$ is the Lebesgue-radius ledger, recording the positive radius comparisons that make each mesh cell small enough for one displayed cover entry.
\item $E$ is the compact-net extraction row, packaging the finite net centers and coverage witnesses handed to $\CompactMetricUp$ consumers.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the interval endpoints, inspected cell representatives, and covered points.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval source, dyadic mesh, finite cover bundle, radius ledger, compact-net extraction, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from closed-interval inspection through dyadic mesh selection, finite cover bundle inspection, Lebesgue-radius comparison, compact-net extraction, and Real sealing.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HeineBorelFiniteCoverUp$, $\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HeineBorelFiniteCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these ten rows. It has no coordinate for arbitrary $\RealUp$ subsets, unrestricted open covers, trichotomy, host completeness, quotient equality, selected subcovers outside the displayed finite bundle, or bridge data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Heine--Borel finite-cover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:heine-borel-finite-cover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HeineBorelFiniteCoverUp$ carrier $F=(I,M,U,L,E,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HeineBorelFiniteCoverUp}$ obligation surface consists exactly of closed bounded interval source admission, dyadic mesh admission, finite cover bundle exposure, Lebesgue-radius ledger exactness, compact-net extraction, Real sealing, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:heine-borel-finite-cover-carrier}. The interval source $I$ is read before the dyadic mesh $M$, so the mesh cells are attached to a displayed closed bounded interval rather than to an arbitrary subset of $\RealUp$.

Read the finite cover bundle $U$ and the radius ledger $L$. These rows state which finitely many cover entries are inspected and which positive radius comparisons certify that the displayed cells fit inside those entries.

The extraction row $E$ and Real seal $R$ are downstream of the same displayed rows. Componentwise transport $H$, replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ preserve that route and add no open-cover compactness, trichotomy, host completeness, quotient equality, or hidden subcover selector. Hence the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Heine--Borel finite-net extraction]
\label{thm:heine-borel-finite-cover-net-extraction}
Every $\CompactMetricUp$ or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\HeineBorelFiniteCoverUp$ packet must read the compact-net extraction row through the finite route
$$
\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp,\quad
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad
\RatUp,\quad
U,\quad
L,\quad
\RealUp,\quad
\hsame,\quad
\Cont,\quad
\Pkg,\quad
\NameCert .
$$
The extracted object is the displayed finite cover bundle together with its mesh and Lebesgue-radius ledger; it is not an open-cover compactness theorem for arbitrary $\RealUp$ subsets and not a selected subcover outside that displayed bundle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:heine-borel-finite-cover-namecert-obligations}. It places compact-net extraction after the interval source, dyadic mesh, finite cover bundle, radius ledger, and Real seal have all been displayed.

The rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name exactly this route. A compact-metric or Real-facing consumer can therefore receive $E$ only as the extraction attached to $I,M,U,L,R$.

Since no carrier coordinate stores arbitrary subsets, unrestricted open covers, trichotomy, host completeness, quotient equality, or selected subcovers outside $U$, the extraction exports only the finite closed-interval cover certificate.
\end{proof}
