\label{def:kuratowski-finite-axioms-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the underlying displayed-object row whose subset requests are read through $\SetLikeUp$;
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ row exposing the finite open and closed tests available to the packet;
\item $S$ is the $\SetLikeUp$ membership classifier row for every subset name mentioned by the ledger;

\item $A$ is the extensivity row, recording that each displayed member of a named subset remains visible after closure;
\item $I$ is the idempotence row, recording that a second closure read adds no new displayed subset request;
\item $U$ is the binary-union row, recording that closure of a displayed union is read by the two component closure rows and the union classifier;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,T,S,E,A,I,U,C,P,N$;

\begin{theorem}[Kuratowski finite axiom NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kuratowski-finite-axioms-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:kuratowski-finite-axioms-carrier}. The rows $X,T,S$ supply the displayed object, topology surface, and membership classifier before any closure axiom is inspected.

Read the finite axiom ledger next. The empty row $E$, extensivity row $A$, idempotence row $I$, and binary-union row $U$ are the only closure-axiom rows in the packet, and each is tied to the SetLike classifier rather than to an ambient powerset operation.

Finally apply transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$. These rows keep the four finite axiom checks attached to the Kuratowski closure, topology, SetLike, and finite-set sibling sources, so the certificate exports no arbitrary-union closure, quotient subset equality, selected hull, separation theorem, compactness principle, or host closure operator.
\end{proof}
