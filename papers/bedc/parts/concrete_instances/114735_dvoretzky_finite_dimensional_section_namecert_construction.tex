Following Dvoretzky's finite-dimensional almost-Euclidean section theorem and Milman's finite-dimensional formulation, the $\DvoretzkyFiniteDimensionalSectionUp$ packet records displayed witness data for a fixed finite normed-space window. It sits over the Banach carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-namecert}, the inner-product comparison rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-innerproduct-namecert}, the vector-space carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, the metric rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Dvoretzky finite-dimensional section carrier]
\label{def:dvoretzky-finite-dimensional-section-carrier}
A $\DvoretzkyFiniteDimensionalSectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(V,N,I,M,E,D,H,C,P,Q)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V$ is the finite $\VecSpaceUp$ window: a named finite vector list, its span rules, scalar reads, and the finite coordinate table used by the section.
\item $N$ is the normed-space row inherited from the local $\BanachSpaceUp$ surface, restricted to the displayed window and not to an ambient complete Banach object.
\item $I$ is the $\InnerProductUp$ and Euclidean-candidate row, recording the comparison form, Gram entries, and the finite Euclidean norm candidate on the same section window.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ comparison row, recording the finite metric reads for the norm row and the Euclidean-candidate row.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ scalar seal for all displayed norm, inner-product, distance, and distortion values.
\item $D$ is the distortion ledger, consisting of explicit real-sealed lower and upper comparison entries for the displayed window.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $V,N,I,M,E,D$.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from vector-space coordinates to norm, metric, inner-product, and distortion reads.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DvoretzkyFiniteDimensionalSectionUp$, $\BanachSpaceUp$, $\InnerProductUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $Q$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DvoretzkyFiniteDimensionalSectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier reads only $V,N,I,M,E,D,H,C,P,Q$. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary ambient Banach space, a selected high-dimensional subspace, a concentration argument, quotient equality of norms, or a completed infinite-dimensional object.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dvoretzky finite-dimensional section obligations]
\label{thm:dvoretzky-finite-dimensional-section-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dvoretzky-finite-dimensional-section-carrier}. The finite vector-space source is $V$, the norm row is $N$, the Euclidean-candidate row is $I$, the metric comparison row is $M$, and the Real-sealed distortion ledger is $E,D$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,Q$ transport, replay, source, and name those displayed rows. They do not add a second source of norm comparison or a hidden subspace selector.

Thus every accepted obligation is read from $V,N,I,M,E,D,H,C,P,Q$. Any global existence theorem, concentration estimate, selected subspace, quotient norm equality, or ambient completeness principle would require a coordinate not present in the packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dvoretzky Euclidean window readback]
\label{thm:dvoretzky-finite-dimensional-section-euclidean-window}

\begin{proof}
