\begin{theorem}[Energy-refined private-coordinate NameCert obligations]

\begin{proof}

The formula row $K$ is downstream of both the private-coordinate witness rows and the energy rows. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. \label{thm:energy-refined-private-coordinate-polynomial-handoff}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Next expose the energy and coefficient rows $X,W,Z,Y$. They record incidence patterns, the finite energy sum, trigger size, and polynomial coefficients before the formula row $K$ is visible.

Finally read $K$ as the energy-refined formula row. The provenance row names the polynomial, natural-number, and Boolean siblings only as consumers of the displayed ledger; it does not replace the private-coordinate and energy rows by one of those sibling classifiers. The structural rows therefore preserve the energy-refined carrier surface for later TasteGate nontriviality and FieldFaithful projection checks.
