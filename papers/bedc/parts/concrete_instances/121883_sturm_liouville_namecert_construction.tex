\begin{definition}[Sturm--Liouville carrier]
\label{def:sturm-liouville-carrier}

$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the $\OdeUp$ coefficient-and-equation row for the inspected second-order scalar equation on the displayed interval.
\item $B$ is the regular-endpoint boundary row, naming the two endpoint cells, boundary forms, separated or coupled boundary clauses, and the finite compatibility checks attached to $O$.
\item $W$ is the $\WronskianUp$ row used to compare endpoint solutions and boundary-admissible solution pairs.
\item $H$ is the $\HilbertUp$ row carrying the inner-product and norm surface on which boundary-admissible solution rows may be compared.
\item $T$ is the $\SpectralTheoremUp$ spectral-data row read only after the ODE, boundary, Wronskian, and Hilbert rows have been displayed.
\item $R$ is the regular-endpoint resolvent packet, recording the finite Green-kernel, endpoint, and inhomogeneous-source reads that remain inside the same boundary surface.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for eigenvalue, norm, and endpoint scalar reads.
\item $K$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the ODE, boundary, Wronskian, Hilbert, spectral, resolvent, and Real-seal rows.

\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host unbounded operator, quotient function equality, selected eigenbasis, endpoint distribution, classical compact-resolvent theorem, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sturm--Liouville NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sturm-liouville-namecert-obligations}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sturm-liouville-carrier}. The ODE, boundary, Wronskian, Hilbert, spectral, resolvent, and Real-seal rows are the only mathematical rows available before the structural support is read.

The ODE and boundary rows supply the regular-endpoint source. The Wronskian row compares endpoint solutions inside the same displayed boundary surface, and the Hilbert row supplies only the carried inner-product and norm comparison data.

\begin{theorem}[Sturm--Liouville regular-endpoint handoff]
\label{thm:sturm-liouville-regular-endpoint-handoff}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Apply the carrier definition. The Wronskian row $W$ is attached to the same ODE and boundary rows, and the Hilbert row $H$ is available only as the comparison surface for those displayed solution rows.
