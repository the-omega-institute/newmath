Following Nikodym and Dunford--Schwartz, the $\NikodymBoundednessUp$ packet records the finite NameCert surface by which a pointwise bounded family of countably additive measures exposes one displayed bound on the inspected variation rows. It sits over the measure carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-measure-namecert}, the local regular rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-radon-measure-namecert}, the density handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-radon-nikodym-namecert}, the bounded-family discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformboundedness-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite reconstruction of the traditional boundedness route; it is not a host measure-completion theorem, a selector for subfamilies, or an ambient Banach-dual principle.

\begin{definition}[Nikodym boundedness carrier]
\label{def:nikodym-boundedness-carrier}
A $\NikodymBoundednessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(M,A,F,W,T,B,D,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MeasureUp$ source row, carrying the measurable-event surface and the displayed countable-additivity rows that the family is allowed to inspect.
\item $A$ is the finite event-algebra window. It lists the measurable rows $E_1,\ldots,E_n$ on which the family is tested and records their transport under the $\MeasureUp$ classifier.
\item $F$ is the measure-family ledger, a finite schedule of signed or finite-variation $\MeasureUp$ rows $\nu_i$ over the same displayed measurable surface.
\item $W$ is the pointwise boundedness window. For every event row in $A$, it records a $\RealUp$ bound for the displayed family values $\nu_i(E_j)$.
\item $T$ is the variation-window row, recording the finite disjoint decompositions of the displayed events and the variation sums that may be read from $F$.
\item $B$ is the candidate uniform $\RealUp$ bound for the displayed variation rows.
\item $D$ is the $\RadonNikodymUp$ density handoff row used only when a displayed member of $F$ is read through a dominated local density.
\item $R$ is the $\RadonMeasureUp$ regularity row for the locally finite event windows used by $A,T,D$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,A,F,W,T,B,D,R,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a requested family-bound read through
$$
M,A,F,W,T,D,R \longmapsto B,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NikodymBoundednessUp$, $\MeasureUp$, $\RadonMeasureUp$, $\RadonNikodymUp$, $\UniformBoundednessUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\NikodymBoundednessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a completed measure algebra, a choice-selected subfamily, a quotient of measures, a hidden dual Banach space, or a global countable-additivity principle beyond the displayed rows of $M$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Nikodym boundedness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:nikodym-boundedness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\NikodymBoundednessUp$ carrier
$$
K=(M,A,F,W,T,B,D,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\NikodymBoundednessUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed measure source, event-algebra window, measure-family ledger, pointwise boundedness window, variation window, candidate bound, Radon--Nikodym density handoff, Radon regularity row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item family coherence, requiring every member of $F$ to read the same $\MeasureUp$ event surface $M$ and the same event-window row $A$;
\item pointwise boundedness admission, requiring $W$ to supply only scheduled event reads and their $\RealUp$ bounds;
\item variation-window exactness, requiring $T$ to build every exposed variation sum from the displayed finite decompositions and values in $F$;
\item Radon and Radon--Nikodym compatibility, requiring $R$ and $D$ to be local handoff rows rather than ambient completion or domination principles;
\item uniform-bound exposure through the single $\RealUp$ row $B$ and the replay row $C$;
\item package provenance and local naming through $P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No local obligation exports measure completion, countable additivity beyond the displayed rows of $M$, choice-selected subfamilies, quotient equality of measures, or ambient Banach-space duality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:nikodym-boundedness-carrier}. The projection exposes exactly the measure source, event window, family ledger, pointwise boundedness window, variation window, bound row, Radon--Nikodym density handoff, Radon regularity row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Family coherence is read from $F$ together with $M$ and $A$. Every family member is evaluated only on events admitted by the same displayed $\MeasureUp$ surface, and each event read is transported by the same classifier row. Thus no detached measurable universe or quotient of event algebras is available.

The boundedness data is local. The row $W$ records pointwise $\RealUp$ bounds on the scheduled event reads, while $T$ records the finite disjoint decompositions used to compute the displayed variation sums. The candidate bound $B$ is downstream from those two rows and cannot be read before them.

The Radon rows are also local. The row $R$ supplies regularity only for the displayed windows, and $D$ supplies a Radon--Nikodym density handoff only for a displayed dominated member of the family. Neither row contains measure completion, a selected subfamily, or an ambient dual-space identification.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the classifier sees only the listed coordinates, the excluded host principles cannot become NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Nikodym boundedness measure-family route]
\label{thm:nikodym-boundedness-measure-family-route}
Every accepted $\NikodymBoundednessUp$ family-bound read factors through the displayed route
$$
\MeasureUp,\RadonMeasureUp,\RadonNikodymUp,\UniformBoundednessUp,\RealUp .
$$
Concretely, the read first opens the common measure source $M$ and event window $A$, then inspects the family ledger $F$ and pointwise bound window $W$, then computes only the variation rows listed in $T$, then uses the local Radon and Radon--Nikodym rows $R,D$ when those rows are present, and only then exposes the uniform $\RealUp$ bound $B$ through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.NikodymBoundednessUp.NikodymBoundednessMeasureFamilyRoute}
\begin{proof}
Start with the obligation theorem \autoref{thm:nikodym-boundedness-namecert-obligations}. It says that an accepted read may inspect only the displayed rows of the carrier.

The route begins at $M,A,F,W$. These rows name the measurable surface, the finite event window, the family members, and the pointwise $\RealUp$ bounds. This is the measure-family analogue of the finite bounded-family discipline carried by $\UniformBoundednessUp$.

The variation and regularity rows are downstream. The row $T$ constructs variation reads from finite decompositions of the events in $A$, while $R$ and $D$ may be consulted only as local $\RadonMeasureUp$ and $\RadonNikodymUp$ handoffs attached to those displayed events and measures.

The endpoint is $B$. Because $B$ is replayed through $C$ after $M,A,F,W,T,R,D$, a consumer cannot replace the finite route by measure completion, a countable-choice selection of subfamilies, unrestricted countable additivity, or an ambient Banach-dual theorem.
\end{proof}
