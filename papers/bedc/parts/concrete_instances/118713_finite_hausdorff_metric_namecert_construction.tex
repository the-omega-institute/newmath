\begin{definition}[Finite Hausdorff metric carrier]
\label{def:finite-hausdorff-metric-carrier}
A $\FiniteHausdorffMetricUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(X,E,D,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source $\MetricSpaceUp$ row, carrying the point classifier and distance observations used by the finite family.
\item $E$ is the finite enumeration row naming the admitted points and the finite-cardinal boundary through which the carrier is inspected.
\item $D$ is the full pairwise distance ledger on the enumerated points, with every distance read through the same $\MetricSpaceUp$ row.
\item $S$ is the separatedness ledger: every zero-distance row in $D$ is routed back to point-classifier identity on the enumerated carrier.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used for positive-radius, equality-threshold, and Hausdorff-facing comparisons.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,S,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,E,D,S,Q,R$ and the local certificate row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite route from the metric source and enumeration through the pairwise ledger, separatedness ledger, dyadic tolerance ledger, and Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteHausdorffMetricUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\SeparatedMetricUp$, $\FiniteCardinalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteHausdorffMetricUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient point equality, open-cover compactness, selected dense subsets, host finite subsets, countable choice, completion classes, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite Hausdorff metric NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-hausdorff-metric-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteHausdorffMetricUp$ carrier $F=(X,E,D,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteHausdorffMetricUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed metric source, finite enumeration, pairwise distance ledger, separatedness ledger, dyadic tolerance ledger, Real seal row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every Hausdorff-facing read must expose $E,D,S,Q$ before $R$ can be consumed, and the ledger exports no quotient equality, open-cover compactness, selected dense subset, host finite subset, countable-choice schedule, completion class, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:finite-hausdorff-metric-carrier}. The metric row is $X$, the finite carrier row is $E$, and the metric data are exactly the pairwise ledger $D$ together with the separatedness ledger $S$ and dyadic tolerance ledger $Q$.

The finite enumeration is prerequisite data. A Hausdorff-facing or completion-facing consumer can read a point only after it appears in $E$, and can read a distance only after the corresponding pairwise row in $D$ has been displayed.

Separatedness is internal to the same finite route. The row $S$ routes zero-distance witnesses back through the point classifier of $X$, while $Q$ records the dyadic tolerance comparisons used by positive-radius and Hausdorff bounds. Thus the route cannot be replaced by quotient equality or a host finite subset.

Finally read the Real seal and structural rows. The $\RealUp$ seal $R$ exposes only the already displayed finite metric data, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite separated metric Hausdorff handoff]
\label{thm:finite-hausdorff-metric-separated-handoff}

$$

$$

It receives no quotient completion object, selected dense subset, open-cover compactness theorem, countable-choice schedule, host finite subset, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Open the pairwise ledger next. The row $D$ supplies all distance reads on the enumerated carrier, and $S$ supplies the separatedness return from zero distance to classifier identity on that same finite carrier.

The dyadic and Real rows are downstream. The $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger $Q$ records the comparison budget used by Hausdorff-facing reads, and the $\RealUp$ seal $R$ is reached only after the finite metric and separatedness rows have already been displayed.
\end{proof}
