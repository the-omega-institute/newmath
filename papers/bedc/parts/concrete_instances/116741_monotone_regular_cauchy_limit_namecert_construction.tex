\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the same stream windows.

\begin{theorem}[Monotone regular Cauchy limit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:monotone-regular-cauchy-limit-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The only analytic rows are $S,R,D,O,M,T,L,E$, so every limit read starts with finite stream windows and regular rational readback.

The order and monotonicity rows are downstream of that readback. Row $O$ compares only displayed regular rational windows, while $M$ records the monotone direction for those same windows.

The tail ledger $T$ then routes a dyadic tolerance through a finite threshold, and $E$ checks that the candidate $\RealUp$ row $L$ is exposed only after this route has been replayed. Thus the real-facing output is a finite NameCert route rather than a host supremum.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same row surface. Since the excluded objects are absent from $K$, the listed obligation families exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Monotone regular Cauchy limit induction route]
\label{thm:monotone-regular-cauchy-limit-induction-route}

The route exports no host least-upper-bound theorem, no selected supremum, no quotient equality of real names, no countable-choice selector, and no ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use induction on the displayed window extension in $S$. The base window exposes the initial regular rational readback through $R$ and records the initial dyadic tolerance cell through $D$.

For the successor window, append the next readback cell and compare it with the previous visible cell through $O$. The monotonicity ledger $M$ stores only this displayed comparison, so the induction step has no access to a global order theorem.

The regular Cauchy row $T$ is then replayed at the requested tolerance. It supplies the finite tail threshold needed by the candidate real-name row $L$, and $E$ records that the real seal is reached only through the same finite route.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the induction route under $\hsame$, replay it under $\Cont$, record its provenance, and name the local certificate. Therefore every induction step remains inside the visible BEDC rows.
\end{proof}
