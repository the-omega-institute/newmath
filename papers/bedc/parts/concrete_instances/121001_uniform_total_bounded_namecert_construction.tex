\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniformTotalBoundedUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-total-bounded-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\UniformTotalBoundedUp$ packet records total boundedness as a uniform finite-net certificate whose radii are read from displayed dyadic precision rows rather than from open-cover compactness or choice-selected finite subcovers. It sits after the total-bounded source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the finite-net interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, and the compact-metric consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}. The packet names the finite uniform coverage route consumed by compact, completion, and uniform-continuity chapters while excluding host enumerations of centers, selected subcovers, quotient metric equality, countable choice, and ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Uniform total-bounded carrier]
\label{def:uniform-total-bounded-carrier}
A $\UniformTotalBoundedUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(M,D,F,W,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the metric source row, exposing the point carrier, point classifier, distance witness, zero-distance, symmetry, triangle, and distance-transport ledgers read by the packet.
\item $D$ is the dyadic precision-radius row. It records the positive requested tolerance, its dyadic refinement, and the radius ledger used to ask for a finite net at that scale.
\item $F$ is the finite net family row. For each precision request supplied by $D$, it displays a closed $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ of centers, center-carrier rows over $M$, and membership rows for those centers.
\item $W$ is the coverage witness row. It records, for every inspected source point, a bundled center from $F$ and a displayed metric distance comparison whose bound is the dyadic radius carried by $D$.
\item $T$ is the uniform transport row, recording componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, dyadic radius ledger, finite net family, coverage witnesses, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a precision request through $D$, then through $M,F,W,T,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformTotalBoundedUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\FiniteNetUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformTotalBoundedUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,D,F,W,T,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected finite subcovers, host lists of centers, quotient equality of metric points, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform total-bounded finite-net obligation]
\label{thm:uniform-total-bounded-finite-net-obligation}
For every accepted $\UniformTotalBoundedUp$ carrier
$$
U=(M,D,F,W,T,C,P,N),
$$
the finite-net obligation surface consists exactly of metric source exposure through $M$, dyadic precision-radius exposure through $D$, finite net family exposure through $F$, coverage witness exposure through $W$, uniform $\hsame$ transport through $T$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Any compact-metric, completion-facing, or uniform-continuity consumer that reads total boundedness from the packet must expose these rows before it may use the finite-net claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-total-bounded-carrier}. The only analytic rows are the metric source $M$, dyadic radius ledger $D$, finite net family $F$, and coverage witness row $W$; the remaining rows transport, replay, source, and name that same finite packet.

Read a tolerance request through the dyadic row $D$. The finite net family $F$ is then indexed by the displayed radius rather than by an external cover. Its centers are closed $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ entries with center-carrier rows over $M$.

Coverage is the row $W$. For each inspected source point it supplies a bundled center and a metric comparison bounded by the dyadic radius already displayed by $D$. The transport row $T$ and replay row $C$ preserve this route uniformly across $\hsame$-equivalent presentations.

Finally $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming for the same rows. Since the carrier contains no open-cover, selected-subcover, host-enumeration, quotient-equality, countable-choice, or ambient-completeness coordinate, every exported total-boundedness claim is exactly the listed finite-net obligation surface.
\end{proof}
