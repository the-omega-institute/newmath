Following McShane, Whitney, and Bishop--Bridges constructive metric analysis, the $\FiniteLipschitzExtensionUp$ packet records the finite certificate by which a Lipschitz extension request over a displayed finite subcarrier is read as a bounded ambient extension window. It sits between the reusable interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lipschitz-extension-namecert}, the McShane--Whitney envelope certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-mcshane-extension-namecert}, the finite metric observations of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-metric-space-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite extension surface; it is not a host function-space construction, not a selected global formula, and not an appeal to countable choice or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Finite Lipschitz extension carrier]
\label{def:finite-lipschitz-extension-carrier}
A $\FiniteLipschitzExtensionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(X,A,L,B,G,W,R,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the ambient $\MetricUp$ row naming the metric observations used by the finite extension request.
\item $A$ is the displayed finite subcarrier row, including membership evidence, inclusion into $X$, and the finite enumeration exposed to downstream readers.
\item $L$ is the source $\LipschitzMapUp$ row over $A$, carrying the finite graph window and displayed Lipschitz constant.
\item $B$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ bound row recording the rational tolerance and constant reads used by the extension certificate.
\item $G$ is the candidate finite ambient graph row, exposed only as finitely many source and target observations.
\item $W$ is the refinement-window row comparing requested finite ambient observations with the enumerated source window.
\item $R$ is the restriction-agreement row, requiring every source-admitted candidate read to replay the source $\LipschitzMapUp$ row through $A,L,B$.
\item $S$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for source values, candidate values, and rational tolerance readback.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, finite subcarrier, source map, bound, graph, window, restriction, real seal, provenance, and local name rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite ambient extension request through $X,A,L,B,G,W,R,S,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteLipschitzExtensionUp$, $\LipschitzExtensionUp$, $\McShaneExtensionUp$, $\FiniteMetricSpaceUp$, $\LipschitzMapUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteLipschitzExtensionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for host function equality, quotient function spaces, selected representatives, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite Lipschitz extension NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-lipschitz-extension-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteLipschitzExtensionUp$ carrier $F=(X,A,L,B,G,W,R,S,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteLipschitzExtensionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite subcarrier enumeration, source Lipschitz-map admission, rational bound readback, finite graph admission, refinement-window comparison, restriction-agreement exactness, real seal handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and ledger non-escape. The finite route is
$$
X,A \longmapsto L \longmapsto B \longmapsto G,W \longmapsto R,S \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
No obligation is discharged by a host extension formula, an equality of ambient functions, or an unlisted completion principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:finite-lipschitz-extension-carrier}. The displayed packet exposes exactly the rows $X,A,L,B,G,W,R,S,H,C,P,N$, so every local obligation must be read through that inventory.

The finite subcarrier and source-map clauses read $A$ and $L$. A source value can enter the extension certificate only after the finite membership row has exposed its enumeration and the $\LipschitzMapUp$ row has supplied the displayed graph and bound data.

The rational bound, candidate graph, and window clauses read $B,G,W$. These rows keep the extension request finite: $B$ supplies regular rational tolerances, $G$ stores finite ambient graph observations, and $W$ records the requested finite refinement window.

Restriction exactness is the row $R$, while the real-facing seal is the row $S$. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route componentwise and do not add host function equality, selected representatives, countable choice, or ambient completeness. Hence the listed finite route exhausts the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite Lipschitz extension window factorization]
\label{thm:finite-lipschitz-extension-window-factorization}
Every $\LipschitzExtensionUp$, $\McShaneExtensionUp$, $\FiniteMetricSpaceUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\FiniteLipschitzExtensionUp$ packet must factor its finite extension observation through
$$
A,L,B \longmapsto G,W \longmapsto R,S
$$
before exporting the observation to transport, replay, provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row. The factorization exports the displayed finite graph and its restriction route, not a host function object, not a selected global McShane envelope, and not ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a finite extension observation. The request first reads the finite subcarrier row $A$, because the source side of the observation is meaningful only against the displayed enumeration and inclusion route.

Next read $L$ and $B$. The source $\LipschitzMapUp$ row supplies the finite graph and Lipschitz constant, while the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row supplies the rational tolerance budget used to compare source and ambient values.

The candidate graph and refinement rows $G,W$ then determine the ambient window being exported. The restriction row $R$ checks agreement over source-admitted points, and the real seal $S$ is the only terminal real-facing handoff available to downstream consumers.

Finally the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ replay and source that same factorization. The sibling certificates in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lipschitz-extension-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-mcshane-extension-namecert} may consume the route, but this finite packet contributes only the displayed finite graph, refinement window, restriction, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{proof}
