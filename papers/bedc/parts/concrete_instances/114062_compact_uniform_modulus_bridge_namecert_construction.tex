\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform modulus bridge NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-modulus-bridge-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The source lock is carried by $X,K,F$. The compact row and continuity row are read over the same metric source before any probe classifier is opened, so the bridge cannot mix finite nets and pointwise moduli from different carriers.

Finite coverage and pointwise-modulus binding are carried by $K,F,P$. Each bundled center has a displayed coverage row and a displayed pointwise-modulus row, and the classifier only sees those finite entries.
