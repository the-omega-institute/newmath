\paragraph{Frechet and topological-vector crosslink.}
The $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ carrier depends on the $\TopVecSpaceUp$ source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topvecspace-namecert} for the compatibility between vector addition, scalar multiplication, and the displayed topology before its seminorm and convex-balanced neighbourhood rows can be read as local convexity. The same packet feeds the $\FrechetSpaceUp$ carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-frechet-space-namecert} by adding countable seminorm-family, metrizable replay, and completion-facing constraints over that topological-vector surface. Thus the route from $\TopVecSpaceUp$ through $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ to $\FrechetSpaceUp$ is a finite NameCert dependency chain, not a host-level assertion of an ambient locally convex complete vector space.
