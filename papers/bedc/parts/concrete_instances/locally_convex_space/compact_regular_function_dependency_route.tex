\begin{theorem}[Locally convex compact-regular-function dependency route]
\label{thm:locally-convex-space-compact-regular-function-route}
Every compact-regular real-function or functional-analysis consumer that cites the $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ packet must first read the topological-vector-space source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topvecspace-namecert}, the seminorm ledger, and the convex-balanced neighbourhood ledger from the source certificate. The route lets compact regular real-function packets use $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ as a source for functional-analysis neighbourhood control, while the exported rows remain $V,T,S,B,M,H,C,P,N$ and do not become a selected dual, an open-cover compactness proof, or an ambient host locally convex predicate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the source carrier of \autoref{def:locally-convex-space-carrier}. The topological-vector-space row $T$ is the displayed route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topvecspace-namecert}, while $S$ and $B$ are the only seminorm and convex-balanced neighbourhood rows exposed to downstream consumers.

A compact-regular real-function packet may therefore cite the locally convex source only after those rows are visible. Functional-analysis consumers receive the same route through transport, replay, provenance, and local naming in $H,C,P,N$.

Since no row stores a selected dual, open-cover compactness proof, or host locally convex predicate, the dependency is a finite cross-reference route rather than an additional compactness or duality principle.
\end{proof}
