\begin{theorem}[Locally convex space forward lattice anchor]
\label{thm:locally-convex-space-forward-lattice-anchor}
Every forward dependency read of an accepted $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ packet factors through the displayed lattice
$$
\VecSpaceUp,\ \TopVecSpaceUp,\ \NormUp,\ \FunctionalAnalysisUp,\ S,\ B,\ H,\ C,\ P,\ N .
$$
Here $S$ is the finite seminorm ledger and $B$ is the convex-balanced neighbourhood ledger. The sibling consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-functionalanalysis-namecert} may cite local convexity only after these rows have been exposed, so the forward lattice records a finite NameCert dependency rather than an isolated analytic predicate.
\end{theorem}

The local-convexity evidence is then read from $S$ and $B$. The seminorm ledger supplies the finite probe family, and the neighbourhood ledger supplies the convex-balanced rows attached to the same topological-vector source.

Finally transport, replay, provenance, and naming are supplied by $H,C,P,N$. Repacking these rows gives the forward lattice above and leaves no coordinate for a detached locally convex predicate, selected dual, Hahn--Banach row, or weak-star compactness theorem.
\end{proof}
