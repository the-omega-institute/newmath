\begin{theorem}[Locally convex space sibling route]
\label{thm:locally-convex-space-sibling-route}
Every $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ consumer must read the $\SeminormUp$ evidence carried by the finite seminorm-family route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-seminormed-space-namecert}, the $\TopVecSpaceUp$ topology row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topvecspace-namecert}, and the $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$ consumer boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-functionalanalysis-namecert} before treating the locally convex carrier as available. The local carrier is therefore a horizontal dependency through displayed seminorm, topological-vector, functional-analysis, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows, not a detached host predicate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:locally-convex-space-carrier}. The seminorm ledger, topological-vector source, convex-neighbourhood rows, and structural rows are all visible coordinates of the same finite packet.

The seminorm evidence is read first through the finite family route cited by the seminormed-space chapter, and the topology row is read through $\TopVecSpaceUp$. Only after those sibling rows are displayed may a $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$ consumer use the packet as a locally convex source.

The remaining rows transport, replay, source, and name the same dependency surface. Since no coordinate stores a selected dual, Hahn--Banach principle, weak-star compactness theorem, or ambient locally convex topology predicate, the consumer receives only the displayed sibling route.
\end{proof}
