The same read also consumes the $\TopVecSpaceUp$ operation-continuity surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topvecspace-namecert} and the $\MetricCompletionUp$ handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert} before complete locally convex replay is available.
