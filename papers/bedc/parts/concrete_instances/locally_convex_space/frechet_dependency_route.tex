\paragraph{Frechet dependency route.}
The $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ packet supplies the finite seminorm-family rows and convex balanced neighbourhood-filter rows consumed by the $\FrechetSpaceUp$ carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-frechet-space-namecert}. Conversely, the Frechet route reads those locally convex rows only after its topological-vector source and before its compatible metric and completion-facing rows, so the dependency is horizontal: $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ provides the local convexity evidence, while $\FrechetSpaceUp$ repacks that evidence with metrizability and completeness data without importing an ambient complete locally convex host, a selected metric, quotient completion equality, countable choice, or an infinite seminorm enumeration.
