The $\TightApartnessUp$ packet sits between the constructive apartness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-apartness-namecert}, the positive-distance route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-apartness-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Tight apartness carrier]
\label{def:tight-apartness-carrier}
A $\TightApartnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(A,E,T,S,C,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the apartness evidence row. It records the positive separation branch read from a $\LocatedApartnessUp$ or $\MetricApartnessUp$ source before any equality-facing conclusion is exported.
\item $E$ is the equality/classifier row. It records the classifier boundary whose accepted equality branch is the only branch allowed to match non-apartness.
\item $T$ is the tightness row. It states that a displayed failure to produce apartness at the inspected boundary routes to the classifier row $E$, rather than to trichotomy or host negation.
\item $S$ is the symmetry and cotransitivity-facing stability row. It keeps the positive apartness evidence stable under endpoint swap and under finite cotransitivity witnesses.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an apartness or equality-boundary request through $A,E,T,S,H,R,P,N$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for apartness evidence, classifier rows, tightness boundaries, stability rows, replay, provenance, and the local name.
\item $R$ is the Real-facing readback row. It allows a downstream $\RealUp$ consumer to inspect the same positive gap or non-apartness boundary without receiving a completed real equality theorem.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TightApartnessUp$, $\LocatedApartnessUp$, $\MetricApartnessUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TightApartnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $A,E,T,S,C,H,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for trichotomy, quotient equality, selected points, countable choice, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tight apartness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:tight-apartness-namecert-obligations}

Every obligation is checked along the finite route
$$
A\longmapsto E\longmapsto T\longmapsto S\longmapsto C,H,R,P,N .
$$
No obligation row exports trichotomy, quotient equality, selected points, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:tight-apartness-carrier}. The row $A$ is the only source of positive apartness evidence, and the row $E$ is the only equality/classifier boundary available to an equality-facing consumer.

The tightness row $T$ binds non-apartness to $E$. Since the carrier has no total comparison row, this boundary is a finite classifier handoff rather than a trichotomy decision or host negation principle.

The stability row $S$ keeps the apartness branch attached under endpoint swap and finite cotransitivity-facing transport. The rows $C,H,R,P,N$ then replay, transport, read back, source, and name the same finite route.

Thus the local NameCert surface is exhausted by the listed rows. The excluded trichotomy, quotient, selection, choice, and real-completeness data are not carrier coordinates.
\end{proof}
