\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\NashEmbeddingUp$}

Following Nash on isometric embeddings of Riemannian manifolds, the $\NashEmbeddingUp$ packet records only the finite chartwise certificate surface needed to name an embedding candidate inside BEDC. It sits between the manifold chart carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-manifold-namecert}, the metric rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannianmetric-namecert}, the vector target surface of $\VecSpaceUp$ and $\EuclideanSpaceUp$, and the terminal scalar seals of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is not a host Nash theorem, not a selected global chart, not a quotient manifold equality, and not an ambient completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Nash embedding carrier]
\label{def:nash-embedding-carrier}
A $\NashEmbeddingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
N=(M,G,A,E,D,R,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\ManifoldUp$ finite chart row, listing the inspected chart names, chart domains, and overlap rows used by the embedding read.
\item $G$ is the $\RiemannianMetricUp$ row, exposing only the finite metric-component ledger over the displayed chart windows.
\item $A$ is the chartwise atlas-to-target ledger, recording which chart rows are sent into the finite $\VecSpaceUp$ and $\EuclideanSpaceUp$ target rows.
\item $E$ is the embedding-candidate row, naming the finite coordinate functions and point-separation obligations inspected in the displayed chart windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DerivativeUp$ comparison row, storing the finite derivative or metric-pullback comparisons used to test that $E$ preserves the displayed metric rows of $G$.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ scalar seal row for every metric coefficient, coordinate coefficient, derivative comparison, and pullback comparison consumed by $D$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for chart rows, metric rows, target rows, embedding-candidate rows, derivative comparison rows, scalar seals, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a chartwise embedding request through $M,G,A,E,D,R,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NashEmbeddingUp$, $\ManifoldUp$, $\RiemannianMetricUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\EuclideanSpaceUp$, $\DerivativeUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\NashEmbeddingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host embedding operator, a global Nash existence theorem, quotient equality of manifolds, selected global charts, an unbounded coordinate family, or ambient Euclidean completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Nash embedding NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:nash-embedding-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\NashEmbeddingUp$ carrier $N=(M,G,A,E,D,R,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\NashEmbeddingUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the finite chart row, metric-component admission, atlas-to-target admission, embedding-candidate admission, derivative or metric-pullback comparison admission, Real scalar sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.

No obligation can be discharged by reading a host Nash theorem, a global embedding operator, quotient manifold equality, selected global charts, or ambient Euclidean completeness outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:nash-embedding-carrier}. The available rows are exactly $M,G,A,E,D,R,H,C,P,L$, so every naming obligation must be assigned to one of those coordinates.

The geometric obligations are chartwise. The row $M$ supplies the inspected chart windows, $G$ supplies the finite metric ledger over those windows, $A$ routes the same chart windows into the target rows, and $E$ records the finite embedding-candidate data that downstream consumers may inspect.

The comparison obligations are scalar-sealed. The row $D$ stores only derivative or metric-pullback comparisons over the displayed chart windows, and $R$ seals the scalar coefficients as $\RealUp$ rows before the structural rows $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the route. Since no other coordinate appears, the excluded host and quotient objects are not part of the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Nash embedding finite chart metric route]
\label{thm:nash-embedding-finite-chart-metric-route}
Every metric-facing consumer of an accepted $\NashEmbeddingUp$ packet must read the finite route
$$
\ManifoldUp,\ \RiemannianMetricUp,\ \VecSpaceUp,\ \EuclideanSpaceUp,\ \DerivativeUp,\ \RealUp
$$
through the displayed rows $M,G,A,E,D,R,H,C,P,L$ before treating the packet as an isometric-embedding witness. The route exports finite chartwise metric comparison data, not an ambient existence theorem or a globally selected coordinate system.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the chart and metric rows. The rows $M$ and $G$ determine the only chart windows and metric coefficients that can be inspected by a consumer.

Next read the target and comparison rows. The row $A$ routes the displayed chart windows into the finite vector and Euclidean target rows, $E$ supplies the embedding-candidate ledger, and $D$ records the derivative or metric-pullback comparisons against the metric row $G$.

Finally apply the scalar and structural rows. The row $R$ seals every coefficient and comparison through $\RealUp$, while $H,C,P,L$ keep transport, replay, provenance, and local naming attached to the same finite route. Thus a metric-facing consumer receives only chartwise comparison data and cannot replace it by a host Nash existence theorem, selected global chart, quotient manifold equality, or ambient completeness fact.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Nash embedding Euclidean seal nonescape]
\label{thm:nash-embedding-euclidean-seal-nonescape}
Every $\EuclideanSpaceUp$ or $\VecSpaceUp$ consumer of an accepted $\NashEmbeddingUp$ packet receives the target rows only after the chart ledger, Riemannian metric ledger, embedding-candidate ledger, derivative or pullback comparison ledger, and Real scalar seal have all been exposed. The Euclidean target seal therefore cannot escape into a host embedding operator, quotient manifold equality, selected global charts, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect \autoref{def:nash-embedding-carrier}. The target rows appear only through $A$ and $E$, and both rows are downstream of the chart row $M$ and the metric row $G$.

Use \autoref{thm:nash-embedding-finite-chart-metric-route}. It forces every target-facing read through $D$ and $R$, so the Euclidean coordinate data are consumed only after derivative or pullback comparisons and Real scalar seals are displayed.

Then apply \autoref{thm:nash-embedding-namecert-obligations}. The structural rows $H,C,P,L$ preserve the same finite route and add no host operator, quotient equality, selected global chart, or completeness row. Hence the Euclidean target seal stays inside the local $\NashEmbeddingUp$ packet.
\end{proof}
