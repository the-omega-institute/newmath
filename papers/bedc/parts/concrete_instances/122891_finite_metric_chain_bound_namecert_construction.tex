Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive metric estimates, the $\FiniteMetricChainBoundUp$ packet records the finite triangle-chain ledger by which metric distances, dyadic rational tolerances, and regular Cauchy readbacks are compared before compact finite-net or complete-metric consumers may use the estimate. It sits over the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the rational and dyadic ledgers of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite-net source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totally-bounded-metric-namecert}, the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. The packet names a finite ledger for chained triangle estimates only; it does not choose a path, assert a host geodesic, prove completeness, or import countable choice.

\begin{definition}[Finite metric chain-bound carrier]
\label{def:finite-metric-chain-bound-carrier}
A $\FiniteMetricChainBoundUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(M,R,D,T,C,W,Q,E,H,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, exposing the metric distance observations used by the chain.
\item $R$ is the $\RatUp$ rational comparison row for the displayed intermediate bounds.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording dyadic endpoint and error budgets for the chain.
\item $T$ is the finite $\TotallyBoundedUp$ or totally-bounded-metric net row consumed by compact finite-net readers.
\item $C$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, present only when the chain is read as a compact finite-net estimate.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which endpoints and intermediate observations are inspected.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback obtained from $W$ and $D$.
\item $E$ is the terminal estimate row, making the accumulated bound public only after $M,R,D,T,C,W,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, rational, dyadic, finite-net, compact, window, readback, estimate, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteMetricChainBoundUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteMetricChainBoundUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,R,D,T,C,W,Q,E,H,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected shortest path, a host geodesic, quotient equality of metric names, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite metric chain-bound triangle obligation]
\label{thm:finite-metric-chain-bound-triangle-obligation}
For every accepted $\FiniteMetricChainBoundUp$ carrier
$$
F=(M,R,D,T,C,W,Q,E,H,P,N),
$$
the public triangle-chain estimate is read only after the metric source $M$, rational comparison row $R$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, finite-net row $T$, compact metric row $C$, finite window row $W$, and regular rational readback row $Q$ are visible. The terminal estimate row $E$ may therefore be consumed by compact finite-net and complete-metric Cauchy readers only as the displayed finite ledger
$$
M,R,D,T,C,W,Q,E,H,P,N .
$$
It exports no selected path, geodesic representative, quotient metric equality, countable-choice schedule, host completion, or ambient compactness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-metric-chain-bound-carrier}. The metric row $M$, rational row $R$, and dyadic row $D$ provide the only distance and tolerance data available to the chain.

Read the finite-net and compact rows next. The rows $T$ and $C$ make the estimate available to totally bounded and compact metric consumers without turning it into an open-cover compactness or selected-subsequence theorem.

Finally read the finite observation window $W$ and regular rational readback $Q$ before the estimate row $E$ is public. Transport, provenance, and local naming preserve exactly this row sequence, so no excluded host object can be exported by the triangle-chain obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite metric chain-bound NameCert surface]
\label{thm:finite-metric-chain-bound-namecert-surface}
The local $\NameCert_{\FiniteMetricChainBoundUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $M,R,D,T,C,W,Q,E,H,P,N$; metric-distance admission through $M$; rational comparison through $R$; dyadic tolerance exactness through $D$; compact finite-net exposure through $T$ and $C$; finite-window inspection through $W$; regular rational readback through $Q$; terminal estimate nonescape through $E$; componentwise transport through $H$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the carrier rows. The analytic part of the NameCert surface is exactly the metric source, rational comparison, dyadic tolerance ledger, finite-net row, compact source, window row, regular readback, and terminal estimate.

The structural rows then preserve that analytic surface. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport keeps the same fields visible, package provenance records the sibling sources, and the local $\NameCert$ row names the packet.

Since the carrier contains no row for selected paths, geodesics, quotient metric names, choice schedules, host completions, or ambient compactness, the local certificate cannot export any of those readings.
\end{proof}
