\begin{theorem}[AxiomDependencyTuple subject-reduction socket dependency route]
\label{thm:axiom-dependency-tuple-subject-reduction-socket-dependency-route}
Every accepted $\AxiomDependencyTupleUp$ supply-ledger read that names missing MetaCIC supply must first consume the concrete subject-reduction discharge socket of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subject-reduction-discharge-socket-namecert}. The tuple may then record the selected host conversion, quotient equality, $\mathsf{Classical.choice}$, $\mathsf{propext}$, ambient theorem, or MetaCIC supply name as a row of $S$, but no consumer may inspect that row as kernel supply until the socket route has exposed whether the supply is discharged, restricted, or refused.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $A=(M,W,S,H,C,P,N)$ from \autoref{def:axiom-dependency-tuple-carrier}. The only row that can name external or MetaCIC supply is the required-supply ledger $S$, and the witness row $W$ is consumed only after the mode row $M$ has selected the relevant reading.

Apply \autoref{thm:axiom-dependency-tuple-supply-ledger-readback} and \autoref{thm:axiom-dependency-tuple-kernel-supply-visibility}. These results force the ledger row to be visible before the witness can use it, and they prevent any hidden kernel-side coordinate from supplying the same material.

The concrete sibling route is \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subject-reduction-discharge-socket-namecert}. Reading that socket before $S$ records whether the named subject-reduction supply is discharged, restricted, or refused. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name the same ledger entry without promoting it into an exported BEDC rule.
\end{proof}
