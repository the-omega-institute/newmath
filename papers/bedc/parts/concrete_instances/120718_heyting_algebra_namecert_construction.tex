\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HeytingAlgebraUp$}

\origin{human}

Following Heyting and Brouwer, the $\HeytingAlgebraUp$ packet records the constructive algebraic reading of implication as a right adjoint to finite meet, rather than as Boolean complement or excluded middle. It sits beside the Boolean endpoint of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-booleanalgebra-namecert}, the meet--join surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lattice-namecert}, the order carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poset-namecert}, the point-free reading of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locale-space-namecert}, and the open-set discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}. The packet supplies the intuitionistic algebraic interface needed by Real and Completion chapters while refusing powerset, quotient, ultrafilter, prime-filter, and excluded-middle coordinates.

\begin{definition}[Heyting algebra carrier]
\label{def:heyting-algebra-carrier}
A $\HeytingAlgebraUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
H=(L,\bot,\top,\mathsf{meet},\mathsf{join},\mathsf{imp},H_s,C,P,N),
$$
where:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the bounded-lattice row, read through the displayed $\LatticeUp$ and $\PosetUp$ sibling obligations.
\item $\bot$ and $\top$ are the bottom and top endpoint rows, stable under the classifier inherited from the bounded-lattice surface.
\item $\mathsf{meet}$ and $\mathsf{join}$ are finite meet and join rows, transported only through componentwise $\hsame$ over the displayed lattice packet.
\item $\mathsf{imp}$ is the implication row, recording for displayed $a,b,c$ the finite adjunction read that $a \wedge b \leq c$ exactly when $a \leq b \Rightarrow c$ inside the same classifier-stable order.
\item $H_s$ records bounded-lattice stability, implication-adjunction stability, and refusal of Boolean complement as a required coordinate.
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay row for the bounded-lattice and implication-adjunction data.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HeytingAlgebraUp$, $\BooleanAlgebraUp$, $\LatticeUp$, $\PosetUp$, $\LocaleSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HeytingAlgebraUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $L,\bot,\top,\mathsf{meet},\mathsf{join},\mathsf{imp},H_s,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for Boolean complement, powerset representation, quotient order equality, excluded middle, prime filters, ultrafilters, or Stone duality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Heyting algebra NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:heyting-algebra-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HeytingAlgebraUp$ carrier $H=(L,\bot,\top,\mathsf{meet},\mathsf{join},\mathsf{imp},H_s,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HeytingAlgebraUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, classifier exactness, bounded-lattice endpoint exposure, meet and join exposure, implication-adjunction exposure, transport stability, replay stability, provenance, and local naming.

In particular, a consumer may read the implication row only after the bounded-lattice rows and the $\PosetUp/\LatticeUp$ order comparison are visible. The certificate does not supply Boolean completeness, a complement operation, powerset membership, quotient classes, excluded middle, prime filters, ultrafilters, or Stone representation data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:heyting-algebra-carrier}. The finite packet exposes the bounded-lattice row $L$, endpoints, meet, join, implication, stability row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row as separate coordinates.

The classifier is inherited only through the displayed order and lattice rows. Thus any accepted consumer must preserve the $\PosetUp$ comparison, the $\LatticeUp$ meet--join surface, and the endpoint rows before the implication row can be inspected.

The implication obligation is the adjunction row in $H_s$. It records the finite comparison between $a \wedge b \leq c$ and $a \leq b \Rightarrow c$ inside the same order, so implication is read as a constructive residual of meet.

Finally the structural rows $C,P,N$ replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no complement, powerset, quotient, excluded-middle, prime-filter, ultrafilter, or Stone-duality coordinate occurs in the carrier, none can be exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Heyting algebra implication adjunction]
\label{thm:heyting-algebra-implication-adjunction}
Let $H=(L,\bot,\top,\mathsf{meet},\mathsf{join},\mathsf{imp},H_s,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\HeytingAlgebraUp$ packet whose $\PosetUp$ and $\LatticeUp$ histories are classifier-stable. For displayed rows $a,b,c$ in the order carrier, the BEDC implication row is read by the finite adjunction relation
$$
a \wedge b \leq c \quad \text{iff} \quad a \leq b \Rightarrow c
$$
inside those classifier-stable histories. This read is an implication-adjunction certificate, not a Boolean complement, powerset, quotient, or excluded-middle coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the bounded-lattice rows from \autoref{def:heyting-algebra-carrier}. The order comparison, meet row, join row, and endpoints are already supplied through the $\PosetUp$ and $\LatticeUp$ sibling histories, so the displayed expressions $a \wedge b \leq c$ and $a \leq b \Rightarrow c$ are read in the same classifier-stable order.

Next read the implication row and the stability row $H_s$. These rows record implication only as the right-adjoint comparison to finite meet. They do not add an independent complement operation or a classical truth-value discriminator.

Transport and replay preserve the same relation. Componentwise $\hsame$ and displayed $\Cont$ move the bounded-lattice and implication rows together, while $P$ and $N$ source and name the same finite packet.

Therefore the implication row is exactly the displayed adjunction relation over the accepted Poset and Lattice histories. Since the packet has no Boolean complement, powerset, quotient, or excluded-middle coordinate, those readings are refused rather than silently imported.
\end{proof}
