\begin{definition}[Hadamard determinant inequality carrier]
\label{def:hadamard-determinant-inequality-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $M$ is the $\MatrixUp$ row carrying the displayed square matrix and its
finite row ledger.
\item $D$ is the $\DeterminantUp$ row naming the determinant read for the same
displayed matrix.
\item $I$ is the $\InnerProductUp$ row used to read row inner products and
orthogonality comparisons when they are displayed.
\item $N$ is the $\NormUp$ row listing the finite row-norm reads and their

\item $G$ is the $\GramDeterminantUp$ handoff row, recording the Gram matrix

\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HadamardDeterminantInequalityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows.

\begin{theorem}[Hadamard determinant inequality NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hadamard-determinant-inequality-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HadamardDeterminantInequalityUp$ carrier
$$
H=(V,M,D,I,N,G,R,B,E,T,C,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\HadamardDeterminantInequalityUp}$ surface consists
exactly of carrier admission for the vector-space, matrix, determinant,
inner-product, norm, Gram determinant, row-product, bound, equality-case,
transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; row-norm exposure through
$N$ and $\RealUp$; determinant exposure through $M,D$; Gram handoff through
$I,G$; row-product budget exposure through $R$; determinant-bound exactness
through $B$; equality-case exposure through $E$; and non-escape from host
spectral theory, selected orthonormal bases, quotient matrix equality, hidden
row operations, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:hadamard-determinant-inequality-carrier}. The displayed matrix
row $M$ is read together with the vector-space row $V$, and the determinant
row $D$ is attached to that same square matrix.

The row-norm data are then read through $N$ and sealed through $\RealUp$. The
inner-product row $I$ supplies only displayed scalar products, while the Gram
handoff $G$ packages those reads as a finite Gram determinant surface.

The bound ledger $B$ is downstream of $D,G,R$. It can compare the displayed
determinant read with the Gram determinant route and the finite row-product
budget, but it cannot add spectral data or a selected orthonormal basis.

Finally, $T,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet.
Since no remaining coordinate stores quotient matrix equality, hidden row
operations, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, the listed obligations exhaust
the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hadamard row-norm determinant-bound route]
\label{thm:hadamard-determinant-inequality-row-norm-bound-route}
Every determinant-estimate consumer of an accepted

(\InnerProductUp,\NormUp) \longmapsto \GramDeterminantUp \longmapsto \RealUp

row-norm ledger, Gram determinant handoff, row-product budget, bound ledger,

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

matrix row $M$ and determinant row $D$ before any bound can be read.

Next read the row geometry. The inner-product row $I$ and norm row $N$ expose

The Gram determinant row $G$ is then the only bridge from row geometry to a
determinant comparison. The product budget $R$ and bound ledger $B$ record the
finite row-norm product and the displayed comparison with the determinant row.

The equality-case row $E$ remains internal to displayed zero-row or
orthogonality evidence. It does not select a basis or invoke a spectral
decomposition. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through

\end{proof}
