$$

\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net coverage row over the same interval carrier.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ row exposing the finite cover-window entries inspected by the selector.

\begin{theorem}[Heine--Borel cover-selector finite-net subcover]
\label{thm:heine-borel-cover-selector-finite-net-subcover}

The route exports no arbitrary open-cover compactness theorem, countable choice schedule, host cover selector, quotient real equality, selected completed real, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:heine-borel-cover-selector-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the interval source, compact-metric finite-net source, finite cover windows, rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, selector ledger, coverage ledger, Real seal, and structural rows.

Read the finite-net side first. The $\HeineBorelIntervalUp$ and $\CompactMetricUp$ rows identify the same displayed interval-net surface, while $W,R,D$ restrict inspection to finitely many named cover entries and tolerances.

The selector ledger $F$ and coverage ledger $L$ are downstream of those rows. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite packet, so the excluded choice, quotient, open-cover compactness, selected-real, and ambient-completeness principles cannot be read from the carrier.
