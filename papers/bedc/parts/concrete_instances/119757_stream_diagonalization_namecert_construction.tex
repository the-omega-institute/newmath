\begin{theorem}[Stream diagonalization NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:stream-diagonalization-namecert-obligations}

\item window exposure, requiring the $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$ and diagonal-window row $W$ to display the finite stream observations being read;
\item diagonal selection, requiring $W$ to identify the finite diagonal path before the real seal $E$ is consumed;
\item regular readback stability, requiring the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ to preserve the displayed rational readings under the transport row $H$;

No obligation is stored outside $S,R,T,W,E,H,C,P,N$.

The stability and tolerance obligations are read through $R$ and $T$. Regular rational readback supplies the finite values, while the dyadic ledger accounts for the tolerances used in comparing those values along the diagonal window.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same displayed route. Since the carrier has no additional coordinate, the local NameCert surface is exhausted by the listed rows and cannot export a countable-choice selector, quotient stream equality, host limit, or ambient completeness claim.
