\begin{definition}[Bauer simplex carrier]
\label{def:bauer-simplex-carrier}
A $\BauerSimplexUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(S,K,V,R,E,Z,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\ChoquetSimplexUp$ source row, carrying the compact-convex
probability-boundary interface already named by the Choquet simplex packet.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactUp$ row whose compact source observations bound the
ambient simplex and the extreme-boundary request.
\item $V$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ row exposing affine-combination and convex
subcarrier data available to the packet.
\item $R$ is the $\RadonMeasureUp$ readback row for finite probability-window
and boundary-facing measure data.
\item $E$ is the $\KreinMilmanFiniteExtremeUp$ finite extreme-witness row,
opened only as displayed exposed-face and candidate-extreme evidence.
\item $Z$ is the closed extreme-boundary ledger, recording finite closedness
tests for the boundary route after $S,K,V,R,E$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for Choquet, compact,
convex, Radon, finite extreme, closed-boundary, replay, provenance, and local
naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact-convex probability
requests through the finite extreme-boundary route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BauerSimplexUp$,
$\ChoquetSimplexUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$, $\RadonMeasureUp$,
$\KreinMilmanFiniteExtremeUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$
rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BauerSimplexUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,K,V,R,E,Z,H,C,P,N$.
It has no coordinate for a host Bauer simplex object, selected barycentric
measure, quotient measure equality, ambient locally convex completeness,
choice-selected extreme point, or bridge witness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bauer simplex NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bauer-simplex-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BauerSimplexUp$ carrier $B=(S,K,V,R,E,Z,H,C,P,N)$, the
local $\NameCert_{\BauerSimplexUp}$ surface consists exactly of Choquet-simplex
source admission through $S$, compact-source exposure through $K$,
convex-carrier exposure through $V$, Radon-measure readback through $R$, finite
extreme-witness admission through $E$, closed extreme-boundary ledger visibility
through $Z$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$,
package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

Every compact-convex probability-boundary consumer of the packet must read
$$
S,K,V,R,E,Z,H,C,P,N
$$
before treating the packet as Bauer-simplex evidence. The route exports no
selected representing measure, quotient measure equality, host closed-boundary
theorem, ambient locally convex completeness, or bridgeChecked evidence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bauer-simplex-carrier}. The Choquet source,
compact source, convex source, Radon readback, finite extreme witness, and
closed extreme-boundary ledger are exactly the rows $S,K,V,R,E,Z$.

Read the source rows first. The Choquet simplex row already packages compact
convex probability-boundary data, while $K$ and $V$ keep the compact and affine
convex observations displayed as separate finite rows.

Next attach measure and extreme-boundary data. The Radon row $R$ supplies only
finite regular-measure readback, the Krein--Milman row $E$ supplies only finite
exposed-face and candidate-extreme evidence, and $Z$ records closed
extreme-boundary tests over those displayed rows.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no
remaining coordinate stores a selected representing measure, quotient measure
equality, host closed-boundary theorem, ambient locally convex completeness, or
bridge witness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bauer simplex closed extreme-boundary handoff]
\label{thm:bauer-simplex-closed-extreme-boundary-handoff}
Every closed extreme-boundary read exported by an accepted $\BauerSimplexUp$
packet factors through the displayed route
$$
\ChoquetSimplexUp,\quad \CompactUp,\quad \ConvexSetUp,\quad
\RadonMeasureUp,\quad \KreinMilmanFiniteExtremeUp .
$$
The handoff reaches the boundary ledger $Z$ only after the Choquet source,
compact source, convex affine row, Radon readback row, and finite
Krein--Milman extreme-witness row have been displayed. It exports no selected
extreme point, no global representation theorem, no quotient measure equality,
no host closed-subset equality, and no ambient compact-convex completeness
principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the Choquet source. \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-choquet-simplex-namecert} supplies the compact-convex probability-boundary surface but refuses representing-measure uniqueness and selected extreme points.

Next read the compact and convex rows. The compact source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert} and the convex carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert} remain visible as $K$ and $V$, so the handoff cannot replace them by a host compact convex body.

Now attach measure and finite extreme evidence. \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-radon-measure-namecert} contributes only the displayed regular-measure readback, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-krein-milman-finite-extreme-namecert} contributes only finite exposed-face and candidate-extreme rows.

Therefore the closed extreme-boundary ledger $Z$ is downstream from the listed route. Because the carrier has no selected extreme point, global representation theorem, quotient measure equality, host closed-subset equality, or ambient compact-convex completeness row, the handoff is exactly the finite boundary route stated above.
\end{proof}
