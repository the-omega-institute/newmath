It sits after the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the convex-set carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, and the bounded linear functional surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedlinearoperator-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Tukey depth carrier]
\label{def:tukey-depth-carrier}
A $\TukeyDepthUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(R,X,H,S,M,W,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ scalar seal used for displayed depth values and threshold reads.
\item $X$ is the inspected finite sample row, carrying only the displayed data window and its local membership reads.
\item $H$ is the affine halfspace-window row, represented by a displayed functional threshold and the induced side test.
\item $S$ is the convex support row, tying each inspected halfspace to the $\ConvexSetUp$ boundary read used by the depth comparison.
\item $M$ is the mass or count ledger for the inspected sample points lying in the displayed halfspace window.
\item $W$ is the finite window row listing the halfspace probes actually read by the packet.
\item $T$ records the transported comparison between the displayed point, the halfspace side test, and the scalar depth read.
\item $C$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $R,X,H,S,M,W,T,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over the real, convex-set, linear-functional, sample-window, and local depth rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TukeyDepthUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,X,H,S,M,W,T,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host measure space, an infinite halfspace family, an attained infimum, a selected centerpoint, quotient sample equality, or bridge credentials.
\end{definition}

\label{thm:tukey-depth-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:tukey-depth-carrier}. The packet displays exactly the real scalar seal, sample window, halfspace window, convex support, count ledger, finite probe list, transported comparison, provenance, and local naming rows.

Finally $C,P,N$ transport, source, and name the same finite route. Since no coordinate ranges over all host halfspaces or all subsets of the sample, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
