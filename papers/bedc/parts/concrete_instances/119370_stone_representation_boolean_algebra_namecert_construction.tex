\label{def:stone-representation-boolean-algebra-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $U$ is the ultrafilter-observation row, recording only finite membership tests and finite compatibility checks against the Boolean source row.
\item $T$ is the $\StoneDualityUp$ Stone-space classifier row attached to the Boolean source.
\item $L$ is the clopen-row ledger, reading each displayed Boolean element as a topology-facing clopen row through $\TopologyUp$.
\item $M$ is the regular-open comparison row, allowing \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-open-algebra-namecert} to compare clopen rows with its closure-interior route without replacing the Boolean source.

\begin{theorem}[Stone representation Boolean algebra NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:stone-representation-boolean-algebra-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\StoneRepresentationBooleanAlgebraUp$ carrier $S=(B,U,T,L,M,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\StoneRepresentationBooleanAlgebraUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed Boolean source, ultrafilter-observation row, Stone-space classifier, clopen ledger, regular-open comparison, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; Boolean operation exposure through $B$; finite ultrafilter compatibility through $U$; Stone-space classification through $T$; clopen readback through $L$; regular-open comparison through $M$; and non-escape through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:stone-representation-boolean-algebra-carrier}. Its Boolean source is $B$, its representation-facing rows are $U,T,L,M$, and its structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The Boolean obligations are inherited as displayed rows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-booleanalgebra-namecert}. Zero, one, join, meet, complement, order, and transport data must be visible before any Stone-facing row is read.

The representation rows then remain finite. The ultrafilter row $U$ records only displayed membership and compatibility tests, while $T$ and $L$ read the Stone-space classifier and clopen ledger supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stoneduality-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}.

Finally $M,H,C,P,N$ compare, transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. Since no carrier coordinate stores a host spectrum, quotient Boolean equality, selected ultrafilter family, ambient compactness theorem, or replacement Boolean algebra, the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}
