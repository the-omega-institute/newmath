\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KolmogorovWidthUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-kolmogorov-width-namecert}

Following Kolmogorov and Tikhomirov on $n$-widths, the $\KolmogorovWidthUp$ packet records finite-dimensional approximation width as displayed NameCert data. It sits after the norm and normed-space rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-norm-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-normedspace-namecert}, the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the metric comparison row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the vector-space and linear-map rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-linearmap-namecert}, and the terminal real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite approximation ledger; it does not import a host infimum, choose an optimal subspace, quotient a normed space, invoke compactness by open covers, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Kolmogorov width carrier]
\label{def:kolmogorov-width-carrier}
A $\KolmogorovWidthUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(K,A,E,M,Q,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the source compact family row, read through $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\NormUp$, and the surrounding $\VecSpaceUp$ source.
\item $A$ is the finite-dimensional approximant packet, displaying the chosen dimension bound, basis-window ledger, $\LinearMapUp$ projection or inclusion row, and admissible finite subspace data.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ error-bound row recording the displayed radius by which $A$ approximates every inspected member of $K$.
\item $M$ is the norm or metric comparison row, naming the $\NormUp$ and $\MetricSpaceUp$ faces used to read distances from source elements to the finite-dimensional approximant.
\item $Q$ is the classifier row, comparing accepted width packets only through $K,A,E,M,L,H,C,P,N$.
\item $L$ is the width ledger, recording inspected precision requests, candidate subspace witnesses, failure rows for too-large errors, and the refusal of a host infimum or selected best subspace.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, approximant, error-bound, norm or metric, classifier, ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact-family inspection through finite-dimensional approximation rows, norm or metric comparison, $\RealUp$ error readback, and refusal boundaries.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KolmogorovWidthUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\NormUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KolmogorovWidthUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host-level infimum over all subspaces, an optimal-subspace selector, quotient norm equality, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Kolmogorov width NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kolmogorov-width-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KolmogorovWidthUp$ carrier $W=(K,A,E,M,Q,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local NameCert surface consists exactly of compact-family admission, finite-dimensional approximant admission, $\RealUp$ error-bound readback, norm or metric comparison, classifier exactness, width-ledger exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every approximation-width consumer must read these rows before treating the packet as a finite-dimensional approximation certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:kolmogorov-width-carrier}. The analytic rows are $K,A,E,M,Q,L$, while $H,C,P,N$ provide transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Read the sibling sources. The compact metric, metric, norm, vector-space, linear-map, and real-name chapters supply the rows named by $K,A,E,M$; none supplies a hidden optimal subspace or host infimum.

The classifier and ledger rows then bind the approximation claim to the displayed finite data. Since $Q$ compares only the listed coordinates and $L$ records both candidate witnesses and failure boundaries, every accepted consumer remains inside the stated NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kolmogorov width finite approximation route]
\label{thm:kolmogorov-width-finite-approximation-route}
Every finite approximation, compactness, or completion-facing read exported by an accepted $\KolmogorovWidthUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\CompactMetricUp,\ \MetricSpaceUp,\ \VecSpaceUp,\ \LinearMapUp,\ \NormUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
carried by $K,A,E,M,Q,L,H,C,P,N$. The read may expose a displayed finite-dimensional approximant and a displayed error ledger, but it cannot export a host infimum, selected optimal subspace, quotient norm equality, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:kolmogorov-width-namecert-obligations}. They force a consumer to display the compact source, finite-dimensional approximant, norm or metric comparison, real error bound, classifier, ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

The finite-dimensional row $A$ is the only approximant coordinate. It may name a basis-window ledger and linear-map row, but it is not an optimizer over all subspaces.

The width ledger $L$ records requested precision, candidate witnesses, and failure boundaries. Thus the exported read is a finite approximation route over displayed data, with the excluded host infimum, selected optimum, quotient equality, open-cover compactness, countable choice, and ambient completeness objects absent from the carrier.
\end{proof}
