\begin{definition}[Dyadic uniform cover-transfer carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-uniform-cover-transfer-carrier}
A $\DyadicUniformCoverTransferUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(U,D,W,Q,R,I,K,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the upstream $\DyadicUniformCoverUp$ row whose finite cover cells are being transferred.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint, radius, overlap, and tolerance ledger used by the transfer.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ observation row for cover-cell endpoints and membership reads.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational center and endpoint data exposed by $W$.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,W,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $I$ is the $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ row that packages the cover cells as interval-net data.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ consumer row, available only after $U,D,W,Q,R,I$ have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the finite transfer ledger, recording that the same cover cells and tolerance comparisons feed the stream, regular-rational, Real, interval-net, and compact-metric rows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the cover, dyadic, stream, regular-rational, Real, interval-net, compact-metric, transfer-ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
U,D,W,Q,R,I,K,L \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicUniformCoverTransferUp$, $\DyadicUniformCoverUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicUniformCoverTransferUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $U,D,W,Q,R,I,K,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected subcovers, quotient stream equality, host real equality, countable choice, selected limits, or ambient compactness and completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic uniform cover-transfer NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-uniform-cover-transfer-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicUniformCoverTransferUp$ carrier $T=(U,D,W,Q,R,I,K,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicUniformCoverTransferUp}$ surface consists exactly of upstream dyadic-cover exposure through $U$, dyadic endpoint and tolerance exposure through $D$, finite StreamName window exposure through $W$, regular-rational readback through $Q$, Real sealing through $R$, dyadic interval-net handoff through $I$, compact-metric handoff through $K$, finite transfer-ledger exactness through $L$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Every L10 completion or compactness consumer must read $U,D,W,Q,R,I,L$ before treating $K$ as public, and no obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-uniform-cover-transfer-carrier}. The transfer source is $U$, the arithmetic and readback rows are $D,W,Q,R$, the interval-net and compact-metric consumers are $I,K$, and the finite transfer ledger is $L$.

Apply the upstream dyadic-cover handoff of \autoref{thm:dyadic-uniform-cover-l10-handoff}. It forces any compactness or completion read to expose the dyadic ledger, finite stream window, regular-rational readback, Real seal, and finite cover row before using the cover as a source.

The new transfer ledger $L$ keeps that same finite cover route attached to the interval-net row $I$ and the compact-metric row $K$. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the displayed rows, while $P,N$ source and name the same packet. Since no excluded open-cover, selected-subcover, quotient, choice, limit-selection, or ambient-completeness object appears as a coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
