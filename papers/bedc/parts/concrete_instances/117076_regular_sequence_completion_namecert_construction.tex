\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RegularSequenceCompletionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-regular-sequence-completion-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on regular Cauchy sequences and constructive completion, the $\RegularSequenceCompletionUp$ packet records the finite BEDC route by which a regular rational sequence is admitted to a real-name seal without quotient streams, countable choice, or an ambient completeness theorem. It sits after the stream-window interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the cofinal tail-budget surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cofinal-tail-budget-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives L10 Real and Completion consumers a displayed completion route for regular sequences, not a selected host limit.

\begin{definition}[Regular sequence completion carrier]
\label{def:regular-sequence-completion-carrier}
A $\RegularSequenceCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(S,D,R,T,E,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ row exposing finite windows of the sequence name.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row exposing dyadic tolerances and arithmetic comparison data.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row reading the regular rational approximants through $S$ and $D$.
\item $T$ is the $\CofinalTailBudgetUp$ row recording which tail windows are cofinal for each displayed tolerance request.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal route, allowing a completion-facing consumer to read a real-name boundary only through $S,D,R,T$.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,D,R,T,E,K,P,N$.
\item $K$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay of the stream, dyadic, regular-rational, tail-budget, real-seal, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularSequenceCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\CofinalTailBudgetUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularSequenceCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,D,R,T,E,H,K,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a quotient of streams, a selected representative, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular sequence completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-sequence-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularSequenceCompletionUp$ carrier $C=(S,D,R,T,E,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularSequenceCompletionUp}$ surface consists of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed $\BHist$ packet $C$;
\item window and tolerance exposure through $\StreamNameUp$ and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$;
\item regular rational readback through $\RegSeqRatUp$;
\item cofinal tail-budget exposure through $\CofinalTailBudgetUp$;
\item real-seal replay through $\RealUp$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-sequence-completion-carrier}. The analytic data are exactly the finite stream row, dyadic tolerance row, regular-rational row, tail-budget row, and real-seal row.

The structural rows do not add analytic content. Transport through $H$ preserves the displayed rows componentwise, replay through $K$ reopens the same finite route, and $P,N$ record provenance and local naming.

Since the packet contains no quotient-stream coordinate, selected representative, host real equality, choice functional, or ambient completeness field, the NameCert obligations are exhausted by the five rows above.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular sequence completion Real-seal route]
\label{thm:regular-sequence-completion-real-seal-route}
Every $\RealUp$-facing consumer of an accepted $\RegularSequenceCompletionUp$ packet must factor its read through the finite window row $S$, dyadic tolerance row $D$, regular rational readback row $R$, cofinal tail-budget row $T$, real-seal route $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row. The consumer cannot extract quotient-stream equality, a selected limit, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness from the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:regular-sequence-completion-namecert-obligations}. It requires the stream, dyadic, regular-rational, tail-budget, and real-seal rows before the packet can be consumed as a completion route.

The sibling route is finite: \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} supplies the regular rational readback, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cofinal-tail-budget-namecert} supplies the cofinal tail budget, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} supplies the terminal seal. The present packet binds those rows to one completion-facing carrier.

Therefore a consumer may cite a displayed regular-sequence completion route, but it cannot manufacture any of the refused quotient, selection, equality, choice, or ambient-completeness data.
\end{proof}
