Following Weyl and Kuipers--Niederreiter, the $\FiniteUniformDiscrepancyUp$ packet records finite uniform-discrepancy ledgers over dyadic real windows as BEDC inspection data. It reads low-discrepancy sequence windows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-low-discrepancy-sequence-namecert}, dyadic rational budgets from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, finite cell coverage from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, terminal real-name sealing from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and finite observation schedules from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. The packet names a finite discrepancy ledger for displayed windows; it is not an asymptotic equidistribution theorem, a selected limiting density, a host measure equality, or an ambient completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Finite uniform discrepancy carrier]
\label{def:finite-uniform-discrepancy-carrier}
A $\FiniteUniformDiscrepancyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(L,W,Q,E,R,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the $\LowDiscrepancySequenceUp$ source row supplying the finite sampled sequence segment;
\item $W$ is a finite ledger of dyadic test windows, each with displayed endpoints and a requested scale;
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and arithmetic ledger used to compare observed counts with dyadic volume rows;
\item $E$ is the $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ cell ledger whose boxes refine the displayed windows at the active scale;
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ readback and seal row for the dyadic volumes and discrepancy bounds named by $W,Q,E$;
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation schedule used to inspect the sampled points and endpoint names;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for sequence windows, dyadic tolerances, net cells, count ledgers, Real seal rows, and stream schedules;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the sampled sequence through window counting, dyadic comparison, finite-net coverage, StreamName inspection, and Real sealing;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteUniformDiscrepancyUp$, $\LowDiscrepancySequenceUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteUniformDiscrepancyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only finite sampled windows, dyadic count comparisons, finite-net cells, Real seal readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. It contains no row for an infinite limiting distribution, selected subsequence, host measure space, or asymptotic discrepancy principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite uniform discrepancy NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-uniform-discrepancy-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteUniformDiscrepancyUp$ carrier $D=(L,W,Q,E,R,S,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteUniformDiscrepancyUp}$ obligations are exactly carrier admission, finite-window exposure, dyadic count comparison, finite-net coverage, StreamName observation, Real seal descent, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.

Every discrepancy read factors through $L,W,Q,E,R,S,H,C,P,N$. The packet exports no asymptotic equidistribution theorem, selected limiting density, quotient stream equality, host measure equality, countable-choice sampling schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The sampled sequence segment is visible only through $L$, and the tested boxes are the displayed dyadic windows in $W$.

The discrepancy comparison is finite. Counts are compared with dyadic volumes through $Q$, and the window coverage is refined through the finite cell ledger $E$ before any Real-facing read reaches $R$.

The observation and structural rows $S,H,C,P,N$ keep this route attached to the same finite packet. Since no carrier coordinate names an infinite limiting distribution, host measure equality, selected subsequence, or ambient completeness principle, the local certificate surface is exhausted by the listed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite uniform discrepancy L10 route]
\label{thm:finite-uniform-discrepancy-l10-route}
Every L10 consumer of an accepted $\FiniteUniformDiscrepancyUp$ packet reads the finite route
$$
\LowDiscrepancySequenceUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \FiniteEpsilonNetUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RealUp,\ \BHist,\ \hsame,\ \Cont,\ \Pkg,\ \NameCert
$$
before consuming a discrepancy bound. The route first exposes the sampled low-discrepancy segment, then the dyadic window and tolerance ledger, then the finite epsilon-net cells, then the stream inspection schedule, and only then the terminal Real seal for the displayed discrepancy bound.

Thus the packet is a finite quantitative-analysis object over dyadic real windows, not a statement about asymptotic uniform distribution or a completed measure-theoretic limit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the sampled sequence row supplied by $\LowDiscrepancySequenceUp$. A consumer receives only the finite segment named in the carrier, so every later count is tied to displayed sampled points.

Next consume the dyadic and finite-net rows. $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ supplies the arithmetic budgets for window volume and count comparison, while $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ supplies the finite cells used to refine the inspected boxes.

Finally read the finite observation schedule and Real seal. $\StreamNameUp$ displays the inspected windows, and $\RealUp$ seals only the discrepancy bounds already produced from $L,W,Q,E$. The structural rows $\BHist,\hsame,\Cont,\Pkg,\NameCert$ preserve this route and exclude any bypass through asymptotic sequence principles or ambient measure completeness.
\end{proof}
