\label{def:cauchy-stability-modulus-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the Cauchy source row, carrying the finite comparison data and the source history whose stability is being read.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance row, recording the displayed tolerance request and any smaller tolerance admitted by the same packet.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ finite-window row that turns the Cauchy comparison into regular rational readback at the requested tolerance.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row naming the finite stream windows inspected by $R$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Cauchy source, dyadic tolerance, regular window, stream observation, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the tolerance request through Cauchy comparison, finite stream windows, and regular rational readback.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyStabilityModulusUp$, $\CauchyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyStabilityModulusUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $C,D,R,W,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, hidden completion operators, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy stability modulus finite-window stability]
\label{thm:cauchy-stability-modulus-finite-window-stability}

The accepted read remains inside $C,D',R,W,H,T,P,N$ and exports no quotient stream equality, selected limit, countable choice, host real equality, hidden completion operator, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The rows $R$ and $W$ are read at the transported finite windows. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport in $H$ keeps the regular rational readback and stream observations attached to the same Cauchy source while the tolerance is refined.

The replay row $T$ then repeats the tolerance-to-window route for $D'$. Provenance and local naming through $P,N$ identify this as the same $\CauchyStabilityModulusUp$ certificate surface.

Thus shrinking a displayed dyadic tolerance preserves accepted finite-window stability, and all excluded completion or quotient data remain outside the carrier.
\end{proof}
