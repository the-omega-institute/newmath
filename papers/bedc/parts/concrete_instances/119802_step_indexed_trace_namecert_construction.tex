\begin{definition}[Step-indexed trace carrier]
\label{def:step-indexed-trace-carrier}
A $\StepIndexedTraceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(F,S,Q,E,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the finite fuel row read by the total host.
\item $S$ is the substrate row, either a $\CellularAutomatonUp$ local rule/orbit source or a MetaCIC bounded evaluator source.
\item $Q$ is the finite prefix row containing the trace segment produced at fuel $F$.
\item $E$ is the endpoint row reached at the end of the prefix, including normal-form or timeout readback when such a row is displayed.
\item $R$ is the refusal row for every read that asks for global halting, an infinite orbit, a fuel-erased endpoint, a hidden normalizer, or an unbounded substrate theorem.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $F,S,Q,E,R$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from fuel to substrate source, from source to prefix, from prefix to endpoint, and from absent global claims to the refusal row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\StepIndexedTraceUp$, $\StepIndexedTotalHostUp$, $\MetaCICFuelTraceUp$, $\BoundedUniversalTraceUp$, $\CellularAutomatonUp$, $\MetaCICStrongNormalizationBoundaryUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\StepIndexedTraceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $F,S,Q,E,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for fuel-free halting, an all-time cellular orbit, unrestricted MetaCIC normalization, host evaluator equality, substrate theorem content, or quotient equality of traces.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Step-indexed trace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:step-indexed-trace-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\StepIndexedTraceUp$ carrier
$$
T=(F,S,Q,E,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\StepIndexedTraceUp}$ surface consists exactly of fuel exposure through $F$, substrate-source exposure through $S$, finite prefix exposure through $Q$, endpoint readback through $E$, refusal exactness through $R$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The fuel row $F$ is the first public coordinate, and the substrate source $S$ is only read at that displayed fuel.

The trace body is $Q,E$. The prefix row $Q$ records the finite segment, and the endpoint row $E$ records the finite readback reached by that segment.

The refusal row $R$ is part of the same carrier, not an external convention. It rejects global halting, infinite orbit, fuel-erased endpoint, hidden normalizer, and unbounded theorem reads.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. Since no additional coordinate is present, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Total-host substrate handoff]
\label{thm:step-indexed-trace-total-host-handoff}
Every total-host or substrate consumer of an accepted $\StepIndexedTraceUp$ packet must read the route
$$
\StepIndexedTotalHostUp \longmapsto F \longmapsto S \longmapsto Q \longmapsto E \longmapsto R \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
The route may pass through $\MetaCICFuelTraceUp$, $\BoundedUniversalTraceUp$, $\CellularAutomatonUp$, and $\MetaCICStrongNormalizationBoundaryUp$ as sibling evidence, but no consumer may erase $F$ and treat the prefix or endpoint as a global halting read.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the total-host boundary. A host consumer reaches this packet only through the fuel discipline already exposed by $\StepIndexedTotalHostUp$, so every trace read begins with $F$.

Read the substrate side next. The row $S$ identifies the finite cellular or MetaCIC source, $Q$ records the prefix generated at the displayed fuel, and $E$ records the endpoint visible at that prefix.

The sibling rows provide comparison evidence without replacing the packet. $\MetaCICFuelTraceUp$ accounts for bounded evaluator replay, $\BoundedUniversalTraceUp$ accounts for universal finite prefixes, $\CellularAutomatonUp$ accounts for local cellular rows, and $\MetaCICStrongNormalizationBoundaryUp$ accounts for the normal-form boundary.

The refusal row $R$ then blocks the fuel-erased read. Transport, replay, provenance, and naming preserve the same packet, so no consumer can convert $Q$ or $E$ into global halting or unbounded substrate content.
\end{proof}
