Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\CauchySignFlipBudgetUp$ packet records the finite sign-flip budget consumed when a regular Cauchy rational name is negated and then sealed as a Real-facing name. It sits after the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the displayed negation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchynegation-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives later Real algebra certificates a finite budget object for sign reversal, rather than treating negation as an ambient completed-real operation.

\begin{definition}[Cauchy sign-flip budget carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-sign-flip-budget-carrier}
A $\CauchySignFlipBudgetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(S,D,R,F,G,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row naming the finite source windows opened by a requested negation read.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budget row recording endpoint scale, requested precision, and the sign-flipped dyadic tolerance rows attached to $S$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the source regular rational name over the same windows.
\item $F$ is the finite sign-flip ledger, pairing each displayed dyadic endpoint in $D$ with its negated endpoint and the same tolerance request.
\item $G$ is the $\RegularCauchyNegationUp$ consumer row that reads $S,D,R,F$ before any downstream algebraic consumer may use the negated name.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the regular readback, dyadic sign-flip ledger, and negation consumer row have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the window, dyadic budget, readback, sign-flip ledger, negation consumer, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite sign-flip request through $S,D,R,F,G,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchySignFlipBudgetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RegularCauchyNegationUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchySignFlipBudgetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,D,R,F,G,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, host real negation, trichotomy, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or bridge credentials.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy sign-flip budget window stability]
\label{thm:cauchy-sign-flip-budget-window-stability}
Let $B=(S,D,R,F,G,E,H,C,P,N)$ and $B'=(S',D',R',F',G',E',H',C',P',N')$ be accepted $\CauchySignFlipBudgetUp$ carriers. If the $\StreamNameUp$ window rows, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budget rows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows, and structural rows agree under the displayed $\hsame$ and $\Cont$ transport, then the sign-flip ledger and terminal Real-seal route cannot diverge. Every public negation read is forced through the same finite route
$$
S\longmapsto D\longmapsto R\longmapsto F\longmapsto G\longmapsto E
$$
and its transported counterpart for $B'$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project both carriers through \autoref{def:cauchy-sign-flip-budget-carrier}. The public analytic coordinates are exactly the window row, dyadic budget row, regular readback row, sign-flip ledger, negation consumer row, and Real seal row.

The assumed $\hsame$ and $\Cont$ agreement identifies the source windows, the dyadic tolerance budget, and the regular readback. Since $F$ has no independent coordinate beyond the displayed dyadic endpoint sign reversal over those rows, the sign-flip ledger is transported with the same finite windows.

The $\RegularCauchyNegationUp$ row $G$ is downstream of $S,D,R,F$, and the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is downstream of $G$. Thus a Real-facing consumer cannot select a second negation route or a second seal route. The transported packet $B'$ follows the same argument with primed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy sign-flip budget Real-seal boundary]
\label{thm:cauchy-sign-flip-budget-real-seal-boundary}
Every $\RealUp$ algebra, apartness, sum, difference, or additive-inverse consumer of an accepted $\CauchySignFlipBudgetUp$ carrier must expose the $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ sign-flip budget, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RegularCauchyNegationUp$ consumer row, terminal $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming rows. The packet exports no quotient stream equality, selected host limit, completed-real inverse law, trichotomy, ambient completeness, or bridge credential.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the carrier inventory of \autoref{def:cauchy-sign-flip-budget-carrier}. The sign-flip budget is displayed before the negation consumer row, and the negation consumer row is displayed before the terminal Real seal.

Apply \autoref{thm:cauchy-sign-flip-budget-window-stability}. Any later algebraic or apartness read preserves the same finite window and dyadic-budget route under the stored transport and replay rows.

The excluded objects have no carrier coordinate and no provenance row. Hence they cannot be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
