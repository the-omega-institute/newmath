\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\MinkowskiDimensionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-minkowski-dimension-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Minkowski and Bouligand's covering-number practice, the $\MinkowskiDimensionUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface at which compact metric scale data, dyadic radii, and lower and upper covering-number ledgers become a named dimension read. It sits beside the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the compact finite-net boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the dyadic scale language of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the gauge-cover sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hausdorff-dimension-namecert}, and the finite scale-cover sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-box-dimension-namecert}. The chapter names the covering-number packet used for Minkowski lower and upper reads; it does not add Hausdorff measure, selected optimal covers, ambient Euclidean coordinates, host logarithm completeness, or choice over scales.

\begin{definition}[Minkowski dimension carrier]
\label{def:minkowski-dimension-carrier}
A $\MinkowskiDimensionUp$ carrier over a compact metric source $X$ is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(M,K,S,N,C,R,L,T,P,A)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the inherited $\MetricUp$ row on $X$, including point histories, distance-witness rows, and ball-membership comparisons.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row. It exposes the finite-net and total-boundedness data before any covering number is read.
\item $S$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ scale row, listing positive dyadic radii ordered by refinement toward smaller scales.
\item $N$ is the finite covering-number ledger. For each displayed scale in $S$, it records a finite cover family, the visible count of that family, and the lower and upper comparison windows used by the Minkowski read.
\item $C$ is the classifier row. It compares accepted packets only through metric source data, compact finite-net data, dyadic scales, cover membership, covering counts, and lower and upper ledger entries.
\item $R$ is the scale-refinement row. It records how a smaller dyadic radius reuses or refines the previous finite cover ledger without selecting a host-minimal cover.
\item $L$ is the limsup and liminf ledger. It records the finite window comparisons that distinguish lower Minkowski, upper Minkowski, and exact balanced reads.
\item $T$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay for metric points, dyadic scales, cover families, covering counts, refinement rows, and lower and upper ledgers.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MinkowskiDimensionUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BoxDimensionUp$, $\HausdorffDimensionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $A$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MinkowskiDimensionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an arbitrary outer measure, countable-cover choice, a selected optimal cover, ambient Euclidean embedding, host logarithm analysis, quotient compactum, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Minkowski dimension NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:minkowski-dimension-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MinkowskiDimensionUp$ carrier
$$
Q=(M,K,S,N,C,R,L,T,P,A),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MinkowskiDimensionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the metric and compact rows, positive dyadic scale exposure, finite cover membership, covering-number comparison, classifier stability, scale-refinement transport, lower and upper limsup and liminf ledger reads, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation is stored outside $M,K,S,N,C,R,L,T,P,A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:minkowski-dimension-carrier}. The rows $M$ and $K$ supply the metric source and compact finite-net boundary, while $S$ supplies the positive dyadic scales at which covering numbers are inspected.

For each displayed scale, $N$ records a finite cover family and its visible count. The classifier row $C$ can therefore compare accepted packets through finite membership, radius, and count data only.

Scale refinement is controlled by $R$. It transports the covering-number ledger to smaller dyadic radii through displayed refinement rows rather than by selecting a host-minimal cover.

The lower and upper dimension reads are the ledger row $L$. It records the finite liminf and limsup comparison windows used by Minkowski lower, upper, and balanced reads, so no ambient logarithm-completion theorem or outer-measure construction is invoked.

The transport row $T$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $A$ preserve the same packet. Since no row stores countable-cover choice, selected optimal covers, Hausdorff measure, Euclidean embedding data, quotient compacta, Lean verification, or bridge checking, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
