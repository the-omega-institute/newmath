The $\TheoremDnaComplexityScoringUp$ packet realizes the theorem-DNA complexity-scoring vision of \autoref{ch:theorem-dna-complexity-scoring} as finite NameCert data. It packages role-locus completeness, mass-vector extraction, weighted-score rows, refusal boundaries, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming so downstream discovery classifiers can inspect score reads without treating theorem text or proof length as a complete theorem object.

The score packet is consumed beside \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-theorem-genome-evolution-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-discovery-pipeline-calculus-namecert}: theorem-genome supplies the lineage rows, while discovery-pipeline supplies the classifier-shift consumer route.

\begin{definition}[TheoremDnaComplexityScoring carrier]
\label{def:theorem-dna-complexity-scoring-carrier}
A $\TheoremDnaComplexityScoringUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(T,R,D,M,W,Q,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the theorem-candidate event-flow row.
\item $R$ is the recognizer row that assigns theorem roles to subflows.
\item $D$ is the eight-locus theorem-DNA row, displaying statement, dependencies, proof, certificates, ledger, status, canonical-site, and closing-seal loci.
\item $M$ is the mass-vector row, recording the nine coordinates for the eight role masses and recognizer overhead.
\item $W$ is the public weight row, listing the nine nonnegative weights used to turn $M$ into a scalar score.
\item $Q$ is the weighted-score comparison row, computing the accepted scalar score only from $M$ and $W$.
\item $B$ is the refusal-boundary row, rejecting score reads that omit any role locus, mass coordinate, weight coordinate, recognizer row, transport row, replay row, provenance row, or local name row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for theorem flow, recognizer, theorem-DNA loci, mass vector, weight profile, score comparison, refusal boundary, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a scoring request through $T,R,D,M,W,Q,B,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TheoremDnaComplexityScoringUp$, \autoref{ch:theorem-dna-complexity-scoring}, theorem-flow recognition, theorem-genome evolution, classifier-level information, theorem-DNA taste gates, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TheoremDnaComplexityScoringUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted scoring packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for statement-only theorem identity, proof-length-only scoring, hidden weights, unanchored recognizer overhead, prose novelty, or a score read outside the displayed finite packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[TheoremDnaComplexityScoring obligation surface]
\label{thm:theorem-dna-complexity-scoring-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\TheoremDnaComplexityScoringUp$ carrier $S=(T,R,D,M,W,Q,B,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\TheoremDnaComplexityScoringUp}$ surface consists exactly of theorem-flow exposure, role-locus completeness, mass-vector extraction, public weight exposure, weighted-score computation, refusal-boundary exactness, componentwise $\hsame$ stability, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. A score read is accepted only after all eight role loci and all nine mass and weight coordinates are visible.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:theorem-dna-complexity-scoring-carrier}. The theorem object is not read from a sentence alone; it is read through the candidate row $T$, recognizer row $R$, and eight-locus DNA row $D$.

The vector part is supplied by $M$, and the scalarization is supplied by $W$ and $Q$. Since $Q$ is defined only from the displayed vector and weight rows, no hidden score coordinate is available.

The refusal boundary $B$ records exactly the missing-row cases. Transport through $H$ preserves the role, vector, weight, and score coordinates together, while $C,P,N$ replay, source, and name the same finite packet.

Thus the NameCert obligation surface is exhausted by the listed finite rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[TheoremDnaComplexityScoring refusal boundary]
\label{thm:theorem-dna-complexity-scoring-refusal-boundary}
Any attempted $\TheoremDnaComplexityScoringUp$ score read that omits one of the eight theorem-DNA role loci, one of the nine mass coordinates, one of the nine public weight coordinates, the recognizer row, the refusal-boundary row, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, or local naming is rejected by the carrier of \autoref{def:theorem-dna-complexity-scoring-carrier}. In particular, proof shortening, statement length, recognizer overhead, or score comparison alone is not an accepted theorem-DNA complexity score.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read a scoring request through $C$. The replay route reaches $T,R,D,M,W,Q,B,H,P,N$ before any scalar score can be exported.

If a role locus is missing, then $D$ is not the eight-locus theorem-DNA row required by the carrier. If a mass or weight coordinate is missing, then $Q$ has no displayed input from which to compute the scalar score.

If the recognizer, boundary, transport, replay, provenance, or local naming row is missing, then the score is detached from the finite NameCert packet. Each case is listed by $B$ as a refusal case.

Therefore the accepted score read requires the full displayed route and cannot be obtained from any single theorem-locus or scalar comparison alone.
\end{proof}
