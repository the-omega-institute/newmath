\begin{definition}[CauchyReblocking carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-reblocking-carrier}
A $\CauchyReblockingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(R,K,T,S,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the source $\RegSeqRatUp$ row whose regular rational readings supply the Cauchy tail data to be reblocked.
\item $K$ is the finite block partition row, listing the displayed consecutive blocks and their endpoints.
\item $T$ is the monotone tail selector row, assigning each requested tolerance to the block from which all later reads are taken.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, exposing exactly the finite observations consumed by the selected block.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, recording the dyadic radii and comparison cells attached to the selected tail.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the regular source, block partition, tail selector, finite window, dyadic tolerance row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tail request through $R,K,T,S,D,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyReblockingUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\FiniteStreamUp$, $\LocatedLimitUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyReblockingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the rows $R,K,T,S,D,H,C,P,N$. No coordinate stores an independent limit, quotient completion, choice-selected subsequence, trichotomy branch, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyReblocking NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-reblocking-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyReblockingUp$ carrier $B=(R,K,T,S,D,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyReblockingUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed packet, block-schedule exactness for the finite partition $K$, tail selector monotonicity for $T$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback preservation for $R$, and Real-seal nonescape for every downstream $\RealUp$ consumer. The finite-window and tolerance rows $S,D$ are consumed only after $K$ and $T$ have been displayed, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-reblocking-carrier}. The displayed rows are exactly the regular rational source, finite block partition, monotone selector, finite window, dyadic tolerance row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Block-schedule exactness is read from $K$. A downstream consumer may inspect only the finite blocks listed there, and the selected tail is recorded by $T$ rather than by a hidden subsequence or an external choice function.

The regular readback is preserved because $R$ remains the only source row. The finite window $S$ and tolerance row $D$ can be reached only after $K$ and $T$ expose the selected block, so the reblocked read is still a read of the same $\RegSeqRatUp$ source.

Finally, the rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, name, and cite the same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for an independent limit, quotient completion, trichotomy branch, choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle, no downstream $\RealUp$ seal can escape the displayed packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyReblocking regular tail selection]
\label{thm:cauchy-reblocking-regular-tail-selection}

$$

$$
Thus the reblocked tail is a finite reindexing of the source $\RegSeqRatUp$ row through $K$ and $T$, followed by the $\StreamNameUp$ window, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-reblocking-carrier}. The only rows available before a tail-facing consumer reaches a finite window or tolerance comparison are the source row $R$, the block partition $K$, and the selector $T$.

Use \autoref{thm:cauchy-reblocking-namecert-obligations}. Block-schedule exactness fixes the finite partition, and tail selector monotonicity forces later tail requests to remain inside the scheduled reblocking route.

After the selected block is visible, the consumer may read the finite window $S$ and the dyadic tolerance row $D$. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the same route, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming.

Therefore every reblocked tail read factors through $R,K,T,S,D,H,C,P,N$ before any $\RealUp$ seal. The packet supplies finite reindexing data only, not a separate completion or host limit.
\end{proof}
