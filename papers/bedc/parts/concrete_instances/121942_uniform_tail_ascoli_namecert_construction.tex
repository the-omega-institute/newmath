It consumes the compact-uniform-continuity route of \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity}, the compact-metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the metric-space and continuous-map surfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-space-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the finite-net equicontinuity certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-net-equicontinuity-namecert}, and the L10 readback rows $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. $$

\item $K$ is the compact finite-net source row.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ row for the source and target distance observations.
\item $C$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ row that makes the finite-net read available.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ family row.

\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule used for the diagonal read.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform-tail Ascoli NameCert]\label{thm:uniform-tail-ascoli-namecert}

$$

the local $\NameCert_{\UniformTailAscoliUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact finite-net admission, metric and compact-metric exposure, continuous-map family exposure, finite-net equicontinuity, shared uniform-modulus extraction, uniform-tail control, StreamName diagonal windows, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance control, RealUp seal boundary, diagonal-subfamily ledger rows, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.

\begin{proof}

Read the modulus and tail rows next. The shared modulus $U$ is computed from the displayed compact-net and equicontinuity data, and the uniform-tail row $T$ records a finite stage for the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$.

The diagonal part is windowed. The row $L$ records only finite diagonal-subfamily data at each displayed $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$, and $R$ reads those windows as regular rational data before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $S$ is exposed.

Finally $H,Q,P,N$ preserve the same finite route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier has no coordinate for a choice-selected infinite subsequence, quotient equality of maps, compact-open host topology, or ambient completeness, the stated rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
