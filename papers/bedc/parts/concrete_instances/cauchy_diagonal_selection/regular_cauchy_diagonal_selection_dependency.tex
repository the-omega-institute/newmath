\begin{theorem}[CauchyDiagonalSelection regular-Cauchy dependency]
\label{thm:cauchy-diagonal-selection-regular-cauchy-dependency}
Every accepted $\CauchyDiagonalSelectionUp$ read that exports a selected diagonal window first consumes the regular-Cauchy diagonal route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-diagonal-meet-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the finite stream-window row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. The exported diagonal window is therefore downstream of the RegularCauchyDiagonalMeet, RegSeqRat, and StreamName sibling surface, not a detached selector or host-side choice function.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the local $\CauchyDiagonalSelectionUp$ route through the existing diagonal-selection carrier. The selected window is read only after the stream schedule and regular readback rows have been exposed.

The regular-Cauchy sibling route supplies the diagonal-meet dependency. Once the consumer has passed through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-diagonal-meet-namecert}, the RegSeqRat row fixes the displayed rational readback and the StreamName row fixes the finite window schedule.

Thus the exported selected diagonal window is a named sibling handoff. No row in the packet supplies a detached selector, quotient stream equality, countable-choice schedule, or host completion representative.
\end{proof}
