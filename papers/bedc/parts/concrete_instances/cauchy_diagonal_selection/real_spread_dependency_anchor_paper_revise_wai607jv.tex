\begin{theorem}[Cauchy diagonal selection RealSpread dependency anchor]
\label{thm:cauchy-diagonal-selection-real-spread-anchor}
Any $\CauchyRealSpreadUp$ or Real-separability consumer reading a $\CauchyDiagonalSelectionUp$ diagonal route must pass through the $\StreamNameUp$ windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the $\CauchyDiagonalSelectionUp$ diagonal schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-diagonal-selection-namecert}, and the $\CauchyRealSpreadUp$ dependency route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-real-spread-namecert} before it consumes the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The route is a finite projection of displayed dependency rows, not a compactness principle or choice principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the diagonal-selection carrier through \autoref{thm:cauchy-diagonal-selection-streamname-regseqrat-route}. The visible rows are the finite StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger, selected diagonal schedule, and terminal Real seal.

Read the real-spread dependency route before the Real-facing consumer. That sibling route receives the same displayed window, readback, tolerance, and schedule rows; it does not add a branch selector or a countable-choice schedule.

Finally pass to the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. Since every row in the path is already displayed by the diagonal-selection and real-spread packets, the anchor only projects a finite dependency route and does not assert new compactness, quotient equality, host real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
