\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniformAbsoluteConvergenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-absolute-convergence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Weierstrass and Bishop--Bridges, the $\UniformAbsoluteConvergenceUp$ packet records the textbook route from an absolute majorant series to uniform convergence of a real-valued series. It is adjacent to the M-test surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-weierstrassmtest-namecert}, the uniform Cauchy criterion of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformcauchycriterion-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The carrier exposes only finite majorant, absolute-value, tail-modulus, readback, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows; it does not import host completeness, countable choice, or a selected limit function.

\begin{definition}[Uniform absolute convergence carrier]
\label{def:uniform-absolute-convergence-carrier}
A $\UniformAbsoluteConvergenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(A,W,T,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the majorant-series row, displaying finitely inspected nonnegative rational or real-name bounds and the summable tail ledger consumed by the packet.
\item $W$ is the absolute-value window row, recording the displayed inequalities $|f_n(x)|\leq a_n$ for the inspected indices, source probes, and tolerance requests.
\item $T$ is the shared uniform-tail modulus row, read once from the majorant tail and reused for every inspected source probe.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row listing the inspected indices, probes, partial sums, and requested tail tolerances.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the partial-sum and tail windows generated by $S,W,T$.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the regular rational readback has been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for majorant, absolute-value, tail-modulus, stream-window, regular-readback, real-seal, replay, provenance, and local-name rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through $A,W,T,S,Q,R$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformAbsoluteConvergenceUp$, $\WeierstrassMTestUp$, $\UniformCauchyCriterionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformAbsoluteConvergenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts a packet only when the majorant tail is displayed before the shared uniform modulus is consumed, every absolute-value window is bounded by the same majorant row, every stream window reads that common tail modulus, and the regular rational and real-name rows preserve the same finite route. It has no coordinate for host completeness of function spaces, selected pointwise limits, countable-choice schedules, quotient equality of series, or detached absolute-convergence facts.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform absolute convergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-absolute-convergence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformAbsoluteConvergenceUp$ carrier $U=(A,W,T,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UniformAbsoluteConvergenceUp}$ obligations are exactly carrier admission for the majorant series, absolute-value windows, shared uniform-tail modulus, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, real-name seal, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming; majorant exactness requiring each displayed absolute-value window to read the same $A$ row; tail-modulus exactness requiring $T$ to be produced from the majorant tail before any stream window is consumed; readback exactness through $Q$; real-seal boundary through $R$; and non-escape from host completeness, selected limits, countable choice, quotient series equality, and detached absolute-convergence predicates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-absolute-convergence-carrier}. The majorant row $A$, the absolute-value windows $W$, the shared tail modulus $T$, the stream windows $S$, the regular readback $Q$, and the real seal $R$ are the only analytic rows visible to the classifier.

Majorant exactness is read before any uniform tail request. Each displayed inequality in $W$ points to the same majorant row $A$, so the packet cannot replace the majorant by a probe-dependent bound or by an external convergence assertion.

The shared tail modulus $T$ is then consumed by the finite windows in $S$. Those windows determine the regular rational readback $Q$, and the $\RealUp$ row $R$ is reached only after the displayed stream and readback rows have been exposed.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no row for host completeness, selected pointwise limits, countable choice, quotient equality of series, or detached absolute-convergence facts, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
