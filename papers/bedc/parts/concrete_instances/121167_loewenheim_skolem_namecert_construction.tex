\begin{definition}[Loewenheim-Skolem carrier]
\label{def:loewenheim-skolem-carrier}
A $\LoewenheimSkolemUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(G,F,S,M,V,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the finite language row, read through $\FirstOrderUp$, listing the function, relation, and constant symbols inspected by the packet.
\item $F$ is the formula-family row, also read through $\FirstOrderUp$, exposing the formulas whose witnesses and truth values are tracked.
\item $S$ is the generated Skolem-closure row. It records the finite closure steps that add named witness terms for the displayed formulas rather than a host-level closure operator.
\item $M$ is the submodel-witness row, read through $\ModelTheoryUp$ and $\SetLikeUp$, displaying membership and interpretation compatibility for the generated carrier fragment.
\item $V$ is the satisfaction ledger, read through $\ModelTheoryUp$, recording that every inspected formula family keeps the same displayed truth row when evaluated inside the generated fragment.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the language, formula-family, Skolem-closure, submodel, satisfaction, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a submodel request through language exposure, formula exposure, closure generation, membership inspection, satisfaction readback, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LoewenheimSkolemUp$, $\FirstOrderUp$, $\ModelTheoryUp$, $\SetLikeUp$, $\TypeLikeUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LoewenheimSkolemUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an ambient set-theoretic universe, a selected infinite elementary chain, quotient equality of models, or a cardinal arithmetic theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Loewenheim-Skolem NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:loewenheim-skolem-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LoewenheimSkolemUp$ carrier
$$
L=(G,F,S,M,V,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LoewenheimSkolemUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite language exposure through $G$, formula-family exposure through $F$, generated Skolem-closure exposure through $S$, submodel-witness exposure through $M$, satisfaction-ledger exposure through $V$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Every consumer of the generated submodel boundary must read those rows before citing a Loewenheim-Skolem conclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:loewenheim-skolem-carrier}. The first two rows $G,F$ fix the first-order language and the formula family whose witnesses are being generated; they are read only through the $\FirstOrderUp$ certificate surface.

Next inspect the generated closure and submodel rows. The row $S$ records the finite Skolem steps, while $M$ records membership and interpretation compatibility through the $\ModelTheoryUp$ and $\SetLikeUp$ siblings. These rows supply the displayed submodel boundary without selecting an ambient chain or quotient model.

Now read the satisfaction ledger $V$. It ties each inspected formula in $F$ to the same displayed truth row before and after the generated-fragment read, so the packet exports only the finite satisfaction boundary that has been named in the carrier.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. Since the carrier has no coordinate for an external cardinal theorem, host universe, chosen elementary chain, or quotient model equality, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
