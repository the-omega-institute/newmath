\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\WellFoundedTreeRankUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-well-founded-tree-rank-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Brouwer-style finite-bar arguments and Bishop's use of induction over finitely generated construction trees, the $\WellFoundedTreeRankUp$ packet records the bounded tree-rank surface needed by prefix-tree reasoning in the L10 stream and completion route. It sits after the finitely branching tree carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finitely-branching-tree-namecert} and the Brouwer bar-induction boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-brouwer-bar-induction-namecert}. The packet names only a finite rank row for displayed prefix trees; it does not import choice, infinite branch selection, host well-founded recursion, or quotient tree equality.

\begin{definition}[Well-founded tree-rank carrier]
\label{def:well-founded-tree-rank-carrier}
A $\WellFoundedTreeRankUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(A,R,L,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the finite alphabet or constructor row for prefix labels;
\item $R$ is the rank row, listing a natural height bound for each displayed node in the finite prefix tree;
\item $L$ is the leaf row, recording the frontier nodes where the rank is $0$;
\item $W$ is the window row, recording the finite $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ prefix windows served by the tree;
\item $H$ is the componentwise $\hsame$ transport row for $A,R,L,W$;
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay row that extends a prefix window without changing the already exposed rank data;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\WellFoundedTreeRankUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\WellFoundedTreeRankUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for choosing an infinite branch, identifying distinct tree presentations by quotient equality, or invoking an ambient well-founded-recursion principle outside the displayed finite rank row.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Well-founded tree-rank NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:well-founded-tree-rank-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:well-founded-tree-rank-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\WellFoundedTreeRankUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $T=(A,R,L,W,H,C,P,N)$, rank-row exposure through $R$, leaf-frontier exposure through $L$, finite prefix-window readback through $W$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The finitely branching predecessor surface is supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finitely-branching-tree-namecert}, while the bar-facing boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-brouwer-bar-induction-namecert} explains why a finite frontier may be read without selecting an infinite branch.

Induct on the displayed finite rank row $R$. At rank $0$, the leaf row $L$ supplies the frontier certificate, and the window row $W$ reads only the finite $\StreamNameUp$ or $\RegSeqRatUp$ prefix already present at that leaf.

For the successor rank, each immediate subtree has a strictly smaller displayed rank. Apply the induction hypothesis to those rows, then repack the parent by $H,C,P,N$. Since every step reads only the finite coordinates $A,R,L,W,H,C,P,N$, the NameCert obligations are exhausted by the stated surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Well-founded tree-rank induction route]
\label{thm:well-founded-tree-rank-induction-route}
Every $\StreamNameUp$ or $\RegSeqRatUp$ prefix argument served by an accepted $\WellFoundedTreeRankUp$ packet may use induction only over the displayed finite rank row $R$. The route first exposes the finite branching data, then the leaf frontier, then the prefix window $W$, and only then replays continuation through $C$; it exports no choice of an infinite branch and no host recursion over an ambient tree type.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with \autoref{def:well-founded-tree-rank-carrier}. The only induction coordinate is $R$, and every consumer-visible prefix window is recorded in $W$ before continuation replay is available.

For the base rank, the leaf-frontier row $L$ gives the terminal prefix window. The consumer reads the finite $\StreamNameUp$ or $\RegSeqRatUp$ prefix directly from $W$, so no branch selection or quotient tree equality is needed.

For the successor rank, use the induction hypothesis on each displayed predecessor row. Finitely branching exposure supplies the finite list of predecessors, and the Brouwer-style bar boundary supplies the reason that the read remains frontier-local. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the same packet, while $P$ and $N$ keep the route inside the local NameCert.
\end{proof}
