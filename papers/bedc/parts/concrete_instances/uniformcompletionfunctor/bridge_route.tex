\begin{theorem}[UniformCompletionFunctor bridge route]
\label{thm:uniform-completion-functor-bridge-route}
Every accepted $\UniformCompletionFunctorUp$ carrier
$$
\mathcal{F}=(U,F,E,R,W,D,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
packages a bridge route from its public completion-facing $\NameCert$ export to the L10 completion schema through exactly the displayed rows
$$
\UniformCompletionUp,\ \CauchyCompletionFunctorUp,\ \CompletionUniversalPropertyUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RealUp,\ \BHist,\ \hsame,\ \Cont,\ \Pkg,\ \NameCert .
$$
The bridge first exposes the uniform-completion source $U$, then the Cauchy-completion handoff $F$, the universal-property row $E$, the regular readback $R$, the finite window row $W$, the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, and the terminal Real seal $S$ before any transport, replay, provenance, or local naming row is exported.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.UniformCompletionFunctorUp.UniformCompletionFunctorBridgeRoute}
\begin{proof}
Start with the public export of \autoref{thm:uniform-completion-functor-public-namecert-export}. It already binds every completion-facing consumer to the displayed source, functorial handoff, universal-extension, readback, finite-window, dyadic-tolerance, Real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Apply the bridge schema of \autoref{thm:uniformcompletionfunctor-bridge-schema}. That schema reads the same public route as a standard bridge surface: $\UniformCompletionUp$ supplies the source, $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$ supplies the handoff, and $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ supplies the extension ledger before L10 readback rows are consumed.

Use the target interface of \autoref{thm:uniform-completion-functor-bridge-target-interface}. It keeps the target-side bridge ordered through $R,W,D,S$ and confines $H,C,P,N$ to componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.

Thus the bridge route is exactly the finite package named in the statement. The carrier contains no quotient completion equality, selected limit, host complete uniform space, open-cover compactness theorem, or choice-selected representative, so none can be exported by the bridge.
\end{proof}
