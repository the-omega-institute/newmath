Following Bishop and Bridges, an Archimedean interval mesh is read as finite dyadic data: a requested scale is met by an explicit depth, listed endpoints, and displayed cell incidence before any Real-facing completion argument can inspect it. The $\ArchimedeanDyadicIntervalMeshUp$ packet places this textbook construction between the dyadic arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite-cell surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}, the stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[Archimedean dyadic interval mesh carrier]
\label{def:archimedean-dyadic-interval-mesh-carrier}
An $\ArchimedeanDyadicIntervalMeshUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(D,A,J,I,W,R,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint ledger, listing the left and right dyadic endpoints, radius comparisons, and tolerance arithmetic used by the mesh.
\item $A$ is the Archimedean depth witness: for the requested positive dyadic tolerance it displays a natural depth whose dyadic mesh size is below that tolerance.
\item $J$ is the finite cell list, including the ordered dyadic subintervals at depth $A$ and their endpoint-incidence rows.
\item $I$ is the $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ handoff row that exposes $J$ as a finite interval-net surface rather than as an open cover.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row through which endpoint and cell observations are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the displayed windows and dyadic tolerance ledger.
\item $Q$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,A,J,I,W,R$ have been replayed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoint, depth, cell, window, readback, seal, and local classifier rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay routes from a mesh request through $D,A,J,I,W,R,Q$.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ArchimedeanDyadicIntervalMeshUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ArchimedeanDyadicIntervalMeshUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient interval equality, selected real representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Archimedean dyadic interval mesh NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:archimedean-dyadic-interval-mesh-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ArchimedeanDyadicIntervalMeshUp$ carrier $M=(D,A,J,I,W,R,Q,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ArchimedeanDyadicIntervalMeshUp}$ surface is exhausted by the finite dyadic endpoint ledger, Archimedean depth witness, finite cell list, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ cell handoff, $\StreamNameUp$ observation window, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming row. Any consumer of the mesh must read those rows in that order before it may use the packet as interval or completion data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:archimedean-dyadic-interval-mesh-carrier}. The endpoint arithmetic, depth, listed cells, interval-net handoff, finite windows, readback, and terminal seal are the only analytic rows.

The Archimedean row $A$ is not a completeness claim. It is a displayed finite depth witness whose dyadic mesh size is compared against the requested tolerance inside the ledger $D$.

The cell row $J$ becomes interval-net data only through $I$. Thus a consumer receives listed dyadic cells and endpoint incidence from the $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ surface before any regular-name or Real-facing read is exposed.

Finally read the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. They transport, replay, source, and locally name the same finite packet. Since the carrier contains no row for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient interval equality, selected representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
