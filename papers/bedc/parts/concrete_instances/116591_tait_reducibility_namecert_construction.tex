\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\TaitReducibilityUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-tait-reducibility-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Tait and Girard on reducibility candidates, the $\TaitReducibilityUp$ packet records the traditional candidate method as finite BEDC NameCert data for the MetaCIC strong-normalization route. It sits beside the critical-path packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metaciccriticalpath-namecert}, the normalization-budget packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicnormalizationbudget-namecert}, the bounded candidate frontier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-normalization-candidate-namecert}, the logical-relation surface $\LogicalRelationUp$, and the normalization-by-evaluation surface $\NormalizationByEvaluationUp$. The packet names the closed typed term row, reducibility-candidate row, beta, application, and Pi closure rows, finite replay and provenance rows, and a local naming row before any MetaCIC consumer may read a candidate-mediated strong-normalization handoff.

\begin{definition}[Tait reducibility carrier]
\label{def:tait-reducibility-carrier}
A $\TaitReducibilityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(T,E,B,A,\Pi,S,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the closed typed MetaCIC term row, recording the term, its type, its context boundary, and the closedness evidence consumed by the MetaCIC route.
\item $E$ is the reducibility-candidate row, recording the candidate predicate, candidate membership evidence, and the closed-candidate or candidate-family witness used for the inspected type.
\item $B$ is the beta-closure row, recording that the candidate read is stable under the displayed beta step window admitted by the route.
\item $A$ is the application-closure row, recording the function-side candidate evidence, argument candidate evidence, and the application handoff row.
\item $\Pi$ is the Pi-closure row, recording binder-side candidate data, dependent-family routing, and the displayed adequacy boundary for Pi-shaped terms.
\item $S$ is the candidate-mediated strong-normalization handoff row, downstream of $E,B,A,\Pi$ and still attached to the closed typed term row $T$.
\item $Q$ records the refusal boundary for evidence-free closed-term normalization, unrestricted subject reduction, full Church--Rosser, global beta-conversion decidability, and completed consistency.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $T,E,B,A,\Pi,S,Q,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records finite $\Cont$ replay from the MetaCIC critical-path and normalization-budget consumers through the displayed candidate route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TaitReducibilityUp$, $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$, $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$, $\BoundedNormalizationCandidateUp$, $\LogicalRelationUp$, $\NormalizationByEvaluationUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TaitReducibilityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,E,B,A,\Pi,S,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for unconditional strong normalization, full subject reduction, full Church--Rosser, beta-conversion decidability, or consistency.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tait reducibility NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:tait-reducibility-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\TaitReducibilityUp$ carrier
$$
R=(T,E,B,A,\Pi,S,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\TaitReducibilityUp}$ surface consists exactly of closed typed term exposure through $T$, reducibility-candidate exposure through $E$, beta-closure exposure through $B$, application closure through $A$, Pi closure through $\Pi$, candidate-mediated strong-normalization handoff through $S$, refusal exactness through $Q$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:tait-reducibility-carrier}. The closed typed term row $T$ supplies the inspected endpoint, while $E$ is the only positive candidate row.

Read the closure rows $B,A,\Pi$ as stability data for the same candidate. Beta stability, application closure, and Pi closure do not introduce independent normalization theorems; they keep the candidate route well formed for the displayed term and type rows.

The handoff row $S$ is downstream of $E,B,A,\Pi$. Requests for evidence-free normalization, full subject reduction, full Church--Rosser, beta-conversion decidability, or consistency are sent to the refusal row $Q$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly the displayed rows. Hence the NameCert surface is the finite Tait-candidate route, not a global metatheorem about every MetaCIC term.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Tait reducibility candidate-SN handoff]
\label{thm:tait-reducibility-candidate-sn-handoff}
Every strong-normalization read exported from an accepted $\TaitReducibilityUp$ carrier must first display the route
$$
T \longmapsto E \longmapsto (B,A,\Pi) \longmapsto S
$$
and must keep the refusal row $Q$ visible when it is consumed by $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$, $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$, or $\BoundedNormalizationCandidateUp$ routes. The handoff may be compared with $\LogicalRelationUp$ and $\NormalizationByEvaluationUp$ as neighboring method surfaces, but it does not claim full subject reduction, Church--Rosser, beta-conversion decidability, arbitrary strong normalization, or consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the accepted carrier. The closed term row $T$ and reducibility-candidate row $E$ are both visible before any normalization handoff appears.

Apply \autoref{thm:tait-reducibility-namecert-obligations}. Its beta, application, and Pi closure clauses place $B,A,\Pi$ between candidate evidence and the strong-normalization handoff $S$.

Now read the downstream MetaCIC consumers. The critical-path, normalization-budget, and bounded-candidate chapters may inspect the displayed candidate route as dependency data, while the refusal row $Q$ keeps the open metatheoretic principles outside the packet.

The neighboring logical-relation and normalization-by-evaluation surfaces can compare methods only through displayed provenance and replay rows. They cannot replace $E,B,A,\Pi,S,Q$ by a hidden global theorem, so the exported handoff remains candidate-mediated.
\end{proof}
