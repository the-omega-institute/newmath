$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $I$ is the coherent inclusion row, recording the displayed stage-to-stage embeddings from the $p^n$ window to the $p^{n+1}$ window.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a torsion endpoint request through its finite window, inclusion route, abelian operation readback, classifier comparison, divisibility handoff, transport, provenance, and local naming.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Pruefer group NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:pruefer-group-namecert-obligations}

$$
G=(p,W,I,A,T,D,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PrueferGroupUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the fixed-prime row, finite $p$-power torsion windows, coherent inclusion ledger, abelian operation row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item $p$-power compatibility, requiring each operation and inverse read in $A$ to remain inside a displayed window and to commute with the inclusion rows of $I$;
\item torsion-window classifier stability, requiring $T$ to compare endpoints only after transporting them to a common displayed $p^m$ window;

\item ledger refusal excluding quotient groups, choice-selected roots, and ambient infinite colimits from the accepted packet.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

For compatibility, inspect $A$ together with $I$. The classifier row $T$ compares two accepted endpoints only after a common displayed window has been named. The divisibility row $D$ is then a next-window handoff for that displayed endpoint, not a selection principle over all roots.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. No row supplies quotient-group equality, a choice-selected root chain, or an ambient colimit carrier, so the five listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
