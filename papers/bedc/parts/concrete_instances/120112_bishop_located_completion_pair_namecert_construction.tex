\begin{definition}[Bishop located completion-pair carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-completion-pair-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $M$ is the metric-source row for the pre-completion space.
\item $D$ is the dense-route row tying source points to the Bishop completion-pair route.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row inspected by completion and Real consumers.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-Cauchy readback row over the windows in $W$.

\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal handoff, available only after $L,M,D,W,R,T$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the located, metric-source, dense-route, window, readback, tolerance, Real seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from each located completion-pair request through $L,M,D,W,R,T,E,H,P,N$.

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located completion-pair NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-completion-pair-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedCompletionPairUp$ carrier $K=(L,M,D,W,R,T,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedCompletionPairUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the located-real row $L$, metric-source row $M$, dense-route row $D$, $\StreamNameUp$ windows $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback $R$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger $T$, and $\RealUp$ seal handoff $E$;
\item located-source routing, requiring every completion-facing read to display $L$ before the dense completion-pair route is consumed;
\item dense-completion routing, requiring $M$ and $D$ to be read before any window, readback, tolerance, or seal row is public;
\item regular-Cauchy exactness, requiring $W,R,T$ to be read as one finite window-and-tolerance packet before $E$ is exported;
\item Real-seal non-escape, requiring every Real-facing read to pass through $L,M,D,W,R,T$ before it reaches $E$;
\item transport and replay through $H$ and $C$, preserving the same rows componentwise;
\item ledger refusal through $P$ and $N$, excluding quotient completion, selected representatives, host real equality, countable choice, located trichotomy, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-completion-pair-carrier}. Its analytic rows are exactly $L,M,D,W,R,T,E$, and its structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

The located-source route begins at $L$. A completion-facing consumer may use located comparison data only after the finite located-real row is displayed, so the consumer does not obtain a host trichotomy or quotient-real comparison.

The completion-pair route then reads $M$ and $D$, followed by the finite windows $W$, regular readback $R$, and dyadic tolerance ledger $T$. The Real seal row $E$ is downstream of that ordered route.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for the refused host objects, the seven listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located completion-pair finite means]
\label{thm:bishop-located-completion-pair-finite-means}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
