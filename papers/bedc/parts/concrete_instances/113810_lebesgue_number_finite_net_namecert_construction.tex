\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LebesgueNumberFiniteNetUp$}

\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\LebesgueNumberFiniteNetUp$ packet records the finite-net Lebesgue-number data needed before compact-metric finite-modulus consumers read a cover-stability tolerance. It sits over the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the total-bounded refinement surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the finite epsilon-net handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, the finite-net compactness route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-heine-borel-finite-net-namecert}, and the Real-facing tolerance surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet records finite centers, cover-stability rows, tolerance comparisons, and replay provenance without claiming open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Lebesgue-number finite-net carrier]
\label{def:lebesgue-number-finite-net-carrier}
A $\LebesgueNumberFiniteNetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(X,U,\delta,E,P,C,D,T,H,R,S,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, exposing the point classifier, metric-distance classifier, compact-metric ledger, and finite-modulus consumer surface read by the packet.
\item $U$ is the cover-stability request row. It records the finite family of named cover cells inspected by the packet, not an arbitrary host open cover.
\item $\delta$ is the positive tolerance row, read through $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, or $\RealUp$ only as displayed tolerance data.
\item $E$ is the imported $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ handoff row, exposing center rows, membership rows, dyadic or rational tolerance rows, and the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ refinement surface.
\item $P$ is the closed $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ of finite-net centers read from $E$.
\item $C$ records center admission and cover-cell assignment for every center in $P$.
\item $D$ records the finite coverage ledger from each inspected point to an admitted center, together with the displayed distance comparison bounded by $\delta$.
\item $T$ records the finite cover-stability tolerance comparison: if an inspected point is $\delta$-close to an admitted center, the point remains inside the cover cell assigned by $C$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,U,\delta,E,P,C,D,T,R,S,N$.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for finite cover-stability requests.
\item $S$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LebesgueNumberFiniteNetUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\HeineBorelFiniteNetUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LebesgueNumberFiniteNetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these finite rows. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary open cover, a selected finite subcover, a host compactness theorem, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lebesgue-number finite-net NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lebesgue-number-finite-net-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LebesgueNumberFiniteNetUp$ carrier $L=(X,U,\delta,E,P,C,D,T,H,R,S,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LebesgueNumberFiniteNetUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the compact metric source, cover-stability request, positive tolerance, finite epsilon-net handoff, center bundle, center-cover assignment ledger, coverage-distance ledger, stability-tolerance ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item finite-net support, requiring the center rows, membership rows, tolerance rows, and $\TotallyBoundedUp$ refinement surface of $E$ to be exposed before any cover-stability tolerance is read;
\item center assignment, requiring every center used by the stability ledger to be admitted through $P$ and assigned a displayed cover cell by $C$;
\item coverage transport, requiring every inspected point to reach an assigned center through $D$ and to preserve the same tolerance row under $H$;
\item local stability, requiring $T$ to certify the displayed cover-cell containment after the inspected point is compared with its admitted center;
\item ledger nonescape, excluding arbitrary open covers, selected finite subcovers, host compactness theorems, countable choice, host $\RealUp$ equality, and ambient completeness as local certificate data.
\end{enumerate}
No Lebesgue-number finite-net obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lebesgue-number-finite-net-carrier}. The analytic surface consists of the compact metric source $X$, cover-stability request $U$, tolerance row $\delta$, finite epsilon-net handoff $E$, center bundle $P$, assignment ledger $C$, coverage ledger $D$, and stability ledger $T$.

Use \autoref{thm:finite-epsilon-net-lebesgue-number-handoff}. The imported row $E$ exposes center rows, membership rows, rational or dyadic tolerance rows, and the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ refinement surface before this packet reads a cover-stability tolerance.

The center assignment and coverage transport rows are then forced locally. A center can be used only after it is visible in $P$ and assigned by $C$, and an inspected point can use the assigned cell only after $D$ supplies a displayed distance comparison to that center.

Finally apply the stability ledger $T$ and replay it through $H$ and $R$. The replay remains inside the finite request surface, and $S,N$ record provenance and local naming without adding any selected subcover, open-cover compactness theorem, countable-choice row, host Real equality, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Lebesgue-number finite-net nonescape]
\label{thm:lebesgue-number-finite-net-nonescape}
Every $\CompactMetricUp$, $\FiniteModulusRealUp$, or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\LebesgueNumberFiniteNetUp$ carrier reads a finite route
$$
X,U,\delta,E,P,C,D,T \longmapsto H,R,S,N
$$
before it may use a cover-stability tolerance. The route may be combined with the finite-net compactness surface of $\HeineBorelFiniteNetUp$, but it does not export open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, countable choice, host Real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a downstream consumer of $L$. By \autoref{def:lebesgue-number-finite-net-carrier}, the consumer enters through $X,U,\delta$ and the finite epsilon-net row $E$, not through an ambient cover or host compactness theorem.

Apply \autoref{thm:lebesgue-number-finite-net-namecert-obligations}. The packet opens the center bundle, center-cover assignment, coverage-distance ledger, stability-tolerance ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows in that order.

The optional $\HeineBorelFiniteNetUp$ sibling route is only a finite-net compactness surface. It can align named finite-net rows with compact-metric consumers, but it supplies no selected subcover and no open-cover compactness principle to this packet.

Thus every consumer read factors through the displayed finite route before any cover-stability tolerance is available. Since the carrier lacks coordinates for selected subcovers, countable choice, host Real equality, and ambient completeness, none of those objects escapes through the consumer boundary.
\end{proof}
