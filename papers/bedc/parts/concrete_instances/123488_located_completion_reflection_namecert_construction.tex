Following Bishop and Bridges on located metric completion, the $\LocatedCompletionReflectionUp$ packet records the boundary where a located metric name is admitted to the completion reflector through finite observed balls and regular rational tolerance rows. It sits beside the located completion surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-completion-namecert}, the universal completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completionuniversalproperty-namecert}, the stream-name windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite reflection boundary, not a quotient completion primitive, selected representative, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness assertion for $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[LocatedCompletionReflection carrier]
\label{def:located-completion-reflection-carrier}
A $\LocatedCompletionReflectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(L,U,S,R,D,E,H,C,K,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the source $\LocatedMetricCompletionUp$ row. It records located ball tests, metric distance reads, and finite Cauchy-window admission before any completion reflection is exported.
\item $U$ is the $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ handoff. It is the only row that turns the located completion request into a universal completion-facing route.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row opened before regular rational readback.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the displayed stream windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the located ball and regular rational rows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, available only after $L,U,S,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the located completion, universal handoff, stream-window, regular rational, dyadic, real-seal, continuation, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a reflection request through $L,U,S,R,D,E,H,K,N$ in that order.
\item $K$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedCompletionReflectionUp$, $\LocatedMetricCompletionUp$, $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedCompletionReflectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, a selected completed representative, a selected limit, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedCompletionReflection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-completion-reflection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedCompletionReflectionUp$ carrier $A=(L,U,S,R,D,E,H,C,K,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedCompletionReflectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the located metric completion row, universal completion handoff, stream windows, regular rational readback, dyadic ledger, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item reflection ordering, requiring the universal completion handoff $U$ to be displayed before any completion consumer reads a reflected object;
\item numerical readback ordering, requiring $S,R,D,E$ to be displayed before the terminal real seal is exported;
\item transport and replay stability, requiring $H$ and $C$ to preserve the same row order under componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay;
\item provenance exactness, requiring $K$ and $N$ to name exactly the displayed BEDC dependencies and the local certificate row;
\item non-escape, excluding quotient completion equality, selected representatives, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, and bridge checking.
\end{enumerate}
No located completion reflection NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-completion-reflection-carrier}. Its rows list the located completion source, universal handoff, stream windows, regular rational readback, dyadic ledger, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data.

The reflection ordering is the displayed route $L,U$. A completion-facing consumer first reads the located metric completion row and then passes through the universal handoff before any reflected object can be exported.

The numerical readback is the displayed route $S,R,D,E$. Stream windows are opened first, regular rational data and dyadic tolerances are read next, and the terminal real seal is reached only after those rows have appeared.

Transport, replay, and provenance do not add a second route. The rows $H,C,K,N$ move, replay, and name the same finite packet, so the local NameCert surface is exactly the listed obligation inventory.

The excluded endpoints are absent from the carrier coordinates. Hence no quotient completion equality, selected representative, selected limit, choice schedule, trichotomy row, compactness row, or ambient real-completeness assertion can be recovered from an accepted packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedCompletionReflection universal route]
\label{thm:located-completion-reflection-universal-route}
Let $A=(L,U,S,R,D,E,H,C,K,N)$ be an accepted $\LocatedCompletionReflectionUp$ packet. Every completion consumer that reads a located reflection factors through
$$
\LocatedMetricCompletionUp\to\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp\to\StreamNameUp\to\RegSeqRatUp\to\DyadicRatCoreUp\to\RealUp .
$$
The route exposes the located metric completion source, universal handoff, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, terminal real seal, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before the consumer receives completion-facing data. It exports no quotient completion primitive, selected representative, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted carrier. The source row $L$ supplies only located metric completion evidence, and the row $U$ is the unique displayed handoff to the universal completion route.

After $U$, a numerical read request must pass through $S,R,D,E$. These rows expose finite windows, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerances, and the terminal real seal in the order shown in the carrier.

The structural rows $H,C,K,N$ preserve the same chain under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the excluded completion and choice endpoints are not carrier coordinates, no consumer can bypass the displayed route to obtain them.
\end{proof}
