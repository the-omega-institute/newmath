\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FiniteImageStreamWindowUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-finite-image-stream-window-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on finite means in constructive analysis, the $\FiniteImageStreamWindowUp$ packet records the finite image of a displayed $\StreamNameUp$ observation window before any Real or Completion consumer reads it as a completed set of values. It sits after the finite stream-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the continuous-map interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only finite source rows, finite image rows, readback ledgers, classifier transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not select a limit, form a host image set, quotient stream equality, or import ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Finite image stream-window carrier]
\label{def:finite-image-stream-window-carrier}
A $\FiniteImageStreamWindowUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(S,J,F,I,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ source row whose inspected entries are named by the packet;
\item $J$ is the finite window-index row, recording the displayed positions of $S$ that are inspected;
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ or certified readback row applied to the inspected source entries;
\item $I$ is the finite image ledger, listing the image endpoints obtained from the entries indexed by $J$;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the finite rational approximants attached to the image ledger;
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row visible only after the stream window and regular rational rows have been read;
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, index, map, image, readback, and seal rows;
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ continuation from the finite source window through the image ledger and real seal;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteImageStreamWindowUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an infinite image set, a selected limiting image, a quotient of streams, or a completed compactness object.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite image stream-window NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-image-stream-window-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-image-stream-window-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteImageStreamWindowUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier exposure for
$$
W=(S,J,F,I,Q,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
finite source-window exposure through $S$ and $J$, certified map exposure through $F$, finite image ledger exposure through $I$, regular rational readback through $Q$, Real-facing seal visibility through $R$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The rows $S,J,F,I$ expose the finite source window and its image before any rational readback or Real-facing seal is available.

The rows $Q$ and $R$ are read only as handoff rows. They do not create new image endpoints; they record the regular rational and Real-facing surfaces attached to the finite ledger already displayed by $I$.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no row contains an infinite image set or selected limit, the NameCert obligations are exactly the displayed finite rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite image stream-window classifier stability]
\label{thm:finite-image-stream-window-classifier-stability}
If two accepted $\FiniteImageStreamWindowUp$ packets are related by the displayed classifier, then their source window rows, certified map rows, finite image ledgers, regular rational readbacks, Real-facing seals, transport rows, replay rows, provenance rows, and local names are matched componentwise. In particular, classifier transport cannot replace a finite image ledger by an ambient host image set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the classifier through the carrier rows of \autoref{def:finite-image-stream-window-carrier}. A classified replacement must preserve the entries named by $S$ and $J$, the applied readback row $F$, and the finite image ledger $I$.

The readback and seal rows $Q$ and $R$ are downstream of that same finite ledger, so they transport only with the displayed image entries. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ then repack the transported packet as another finite image window, not as a host image object.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite image stream-window RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:finite-image-stream-window-regseqrat-handoff}
Every $\RegSeqRatUp$ consumer of a $\FiniteImageStreamWindowUp$ packet must first read the finite source window $S,J$, the certified map row $F$, and the finite image ledger $I$. The consumer may then read $Q$ as the regular rational handoff for those displayed image entries, but it cannot infer a stream quotient or a selected limit from the handoff alone.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The carrier places $Q$ after the source window, certified map row, and finite image ledger. A regular rational consumer therefore receives $Q$ only together with the displayed finite image rows that generated it.

Since $Q$ records readback for the entries of $I$, it does not identify all future stream entries, select a convergent image, or quotient two streams. Those conclusions would require rows absent from the carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite image stream-window Real seal nonescape]
\label{thm:finite-image-stream-window-real-seal-nonescape}
Every Real-facing read of an accepted $\FiniteImageStreamWindowUp$ packet factors through
$$
S,J,F,I,Q,R,H,C,P,N
$$
before the $\RealUp$ seal row is visible. The seal exports only the finite image-window readback already certified by the packet; it does not broaden the packet to ambient Real completeness or selected image limits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By \autoref{thm:finite-image-stream-window-namecert-obligations}, the Real-facing row $R$ is part of one packet with the source window, image ledger, and regular rational readback rows.

A Real-facing consumer must therefore read $S,J,F,I,Q$ before $R$ can be interpreted. The classifier stability theorem prevents replacing $I$ by a host image set during transport, so the seal remains attached to the displayed finite image window.
\end{proof}
