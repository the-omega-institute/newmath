It sits after the lambda-calculus carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lambdacalc-namecert} and beside the MetaCIC parallel-diamond frontier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Parallel reduction carrier]
\label{def:parallel-reduction-carrier}
A $\ParallelReductionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(T,B,A,S,R,J,H,C,K,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\LambdaCalcUp$ term-packet row supplying the source and target term syntax.
\item $B$ is the one-step beta row whose redex witnesses may be contracted simultaneously.
\item $A$ is the alpha-stability row keeping binder names and capture-avoidance ledgers visible during the contraction.
\item $S$ is the simultaneous-contraction schedule, including reflexive constructor cases for variables, abstraction, and application.
\item $R$ is the residual ledger recording which redex rows survive, disappear, or are copied across the displayed constructor route.
\item $J$ is the local join candidate row used by downstream diamond consumers, without asserting that every peak has already been joined.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the term, beta, alpha, schedule, residual, join, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same rows.
\item $K$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ParallelReductionUp$, $\LambdaCalcUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ParallelReductionUp}$ evidence row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these ten rows. It has no coordinate for quotient term equality, subject reduction, evaluator equality, global normalization, full Church--Rosser, or MetaCIC kernel consistency.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Parallel reduction NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:parallel-reduction-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ParallelReductionUp$ carrier $P=(T,B,A,S,R,J,H,C,K,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ParallelReductionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, lambda-term exposure, one-step beta row visibility, alpha-stable simultaneous contraction, residual ledger preservation, local join-candidate exposure, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. A MetaCIC confluence consumer may read this packet as the traditional parallel-reduction surface feeding \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-namecert}, but it cannot read it as full Church--Rosser, subject reduction, quotient equality of terms, global normalization, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:parallel-reduction-carrier}. The rows $T,B,A,S,R,J,H,C,K,N$ are the whole visible surface, and the lambda-calculus source row keeps the term syntax inside the existing carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lambdacalc-namecert}.

The simultaneous-contraction obligation reads $B,A,S$ together. The beta row supplies the redex witnesses, the alpha row keeps binder transport and capture-avoidance visible, and the schedule row records the constructorwise reflexive and contraction cases.

The residual and join-facing obligations then read $R$ and $J$. These rows expose the data a parallel-diamond consumer may inspect, while retaining the conditional frontier: the packet names candidate residuals and local joins without asserting that all closed peaks have been joined.

Finally, $H,C,K,N$ transport, replay, source, and certify exactly the displayed rows. Since quotient equality, subject reduction, evaluator equality, global normalization, full Church--Rosser, and kernel consistency are absent from the carrier, none of those conclusions is exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
