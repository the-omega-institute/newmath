Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\UniformCauchyCompleteMetricLimitUp$ packet records the finite route by which a uniformly Cauchy family of regular rational names is read as a complete-metric limit without importing a host completion point. It consumes the sequence-space surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchysequencespace-namecert}, the regular rational name route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the stream-window route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the complete-metric uniqueness route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite L10 completion object; it is not quotient stream equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host metric completion, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Uniform Cauchy complete-metric limit carrier]
\label{def:uniform-cauchy-complete-metric-limit-carrier}
A $\UniformCauchyCompleteMetricLimitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(S,R,W,U,E,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the accepted $\CauchySequenceSpaceUp$ row naming the common finite Cauchy-window surface;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular rational names inspected by the packet;
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule that exposes the finite observations used by the uniform Cauchy test;
\item $U$ is the uniform tail ledger, recording one displayed tolerance budget for the inspected family rather than a separately chosen tail for each consumer;
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $S,R,W,U$ have been displayed;
\item $Q$ is the $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ row that compares any second displayed limit using the same complete-metric limit surface;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the sequence-space, regular-name, stream-window, uniform-tail, real-seal, uniqueness, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from uniform Cauchy inspection to the complete-metric limit read;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformCauchyCompleteMetricLimitUp$, $\CauchySequenceSpaceUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformCauchyCompleteMetricLimitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,R,W,U,E,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient Cauchy names, selected limit representatives, countable-choice schedules, host metric completion, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform Cauchy complete-metric limit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-cauchy-complete-metric-limit-obligations}
The local $\NameCert_{\UniformCauchyCompleteMetricLimitUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier exposure for $L=(S,R,W,U,E,Q,H,C,P,N)$, sequence-space exposure through $S$, regular rational readback through $R$, stream-window inspection through $W$, uniform tail exactness through $U$, terminal real-seal access through $E$, complete-metric uniqueness comparison through $Q$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-cauchy-complete-metric-limit-carrier}. The displayed rows are exactly the sequence-space, regular-name, stream-window, uniform-tail, real-seal, uniqueness, transport, replay, provenance, and naming coordinates.

The sibling route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchysequencespace-namecert} keeps the inspected family on finite Cauchy windows. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\StreamNameUp$ rows record the regular readback and scheduled observations before any $\RealUp$ seal can be consumed.

The row $U$ is the only uniform-tail ledger. The row $Q$ reads uniqueness through $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ after the same finite rows have been exposed. Since the carrier contains no quotient stream, selected representative, choice schedule, or host completion coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform Cauchy complete-metric limit uniqueness]
\label{thm:uniform-cauchy-complete-metric-limit-uniqueness}
Let $L=(S,R,W,U,E,Q,H,C,P,N)$ and $L'=(S',R',W',U',E',Q',H',C',P',N')$ be accepted $\UniformCauchyCompleteMetricLimitUp$ packets. If their $\CauchySequenceSpaceUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, uniform-tail, and $\RealUp$ seal rows agree componentwise under the displayed transport and replay rows, then their public complete-metric limit reads are identified only through the $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ rows $Q,Q'$. No host real equality, quotient stream equality, selected representative, or ambient completeness principle participates in the comparison.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the two carriers. Their public limit reads are available only after the sequence-space rows, regular-name rows, stream schedules, uniform-tail ledgers, and terminal real seals have been exposed.

Use \autoref{thm:uniform-cauchy-complete-metric-limit-obligations}. It confines comparison of accepted packets to the displayed rows $S,R,W,U,E,Q,H,C,P,N$ and $S',R',W',U',E',Q',H',C',P',N'$.

The uniqueness comparison is therefore routed through $Q$ and $Q'$, the rows named by $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay preserve the same finite route. Because the carrier has no quotient, selected representative, host equality, choice schedule, or ambient completeness coordinate, the comparison exports only the finite complete-metric uniqueness read.
\end{proof}
