It sits over the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the integer carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-int-namecert}, and the rational carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}. \begin{definition}[RealArchimedeanFloor carrier]
\label{def:real-archimedean-floor-carrier}
A $\RealArchimedeanFloorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(R,Q,D,Z,A,L,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the sealed $\RealUp$ row whose inspected name is being read.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational readback attached to the same displayed Real name.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the floor comparison windows.
\item $Z$ is the $\IntUp$ candidate bracket row.
\item $A$ is the $\RatUp$ Archimedean comparison row relating the integer candidate to rational windows.
\item $L$ records the displayed lower bound $Z \leq R$ through the rational readback route.
\item $U$ records the displayed upper bound $R < Z+1$ through the same route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Real seal, rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, integer bracket, rational comparison row, displayed bounds, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same finite floor-readback route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealArchimedeanFloorUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\IntUp$, $\RatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealArchimedeanFloorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier compares accepted packets only through these rows. No coordinate stores a host floor function, a trichotomy decision for arbitrary Real names, a quotient-real representative, a selected regular sequence, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RealArchimedeanFloor NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-archimedean-floor-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\RealArchimedeanFloorUp$ carrier $F=(R,Q,D,Z,A,L,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealArchimedeanFloorUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, sealed $\RealUp$ exposure, $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational readback, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance accounting, $\IntUp$ candidate exposure, $\RatUp$ Archimedean comparison, displayed lower and upper floor bounds, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-archimedean-floor-carrier}. The only visible rows are $R,Q,D,Z,A,L,U,H,C,P,N$, so every floor-facing consumer must read the Real seal through the rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, integer candidate, and rational comparison rows before it can inspect the displayed bounds.

The lower and upper rows are tied to the same finite route. Row $L$ records the lower comparison, while row $U$ records the successor-side comparison; both use $Q,D,Z,A$ rather than an external order oracle.

Finally read $H,C,P,N$. These rows transport, replay, source, and locally name exactly the displayed packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for host floor, trichotomy, quotient-real representatives, selected representatives, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the local NameCert obligations cannot export any of those principles.
\end{proof}
