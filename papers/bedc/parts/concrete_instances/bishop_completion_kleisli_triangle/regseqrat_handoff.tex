\label{thm:bishop-completion-kleisli-triangle-regseqrat-handoff}

Finally the terminal seal is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, while the completion source is anchored by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}. The structural rows $J,H,C,P,N$ replay and name that same handoff. Since the carrier has no row for selected limits, quotient stream equality, $\mathsf{Classical.choice}$, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, row exhaustion keeps every public triangle equation on the displayed finite route.
\end{proof}
