Following Kelley and Bishop--Bridges on filters and constructive Cauchy approximation, the $\CofinalCauchyFilterUp$ packet records the finite BEDC route by which a filter-style Cauchy carrier is read through cofinal finite windows rather than through a quotient filter or a selected limit. It sits after the Cauchy carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-namecert}, the finite stream-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet lets Completion and Real consumers ask for cofinal Cauchy evidence without importing host filter equality, countable choice, selected limits, or ambient Real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Cofinal Cauchy filter carrier]
\label{def:cofinal-cauchy-filter-carrier}
A $\CofinalCauchyFilterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(C,S,R,Q,W,E,H,A,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\CauchyUp$ source row exposing the Cauchy request interface.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row used to inspect named stream segments.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational readback row for tolerance comparisons.
\item $Q$ is the cofinal refinement row, recording which later windows refine a requested Cauchy witness.
\item $W$ is the finite-window projection row, sending a filter request to an inspected stream window and a rational tolerance.
\item $E$ is the completion-facing exactness row, exposed only after $C,S,R,Q,W$ are visible.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Cauchy, stream, rational, cofinal, projection, and exactness rows.
\item $A$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CofinalCauchyFilterUp$, $\CauchyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CofinalCauchyFilterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient filters, selected limits, countable choice, host equality of filters, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal Cauchy filter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cofinal-cauchy-filter-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CofinalCauchyFilterUp$ carrier $F=(C,S,R,Q,W,E,H,A,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CofinalCauchyFilterUp}$ surface consists exactly of Cauchy source exposure through $C$, stream-window exposure through $S$, regular rational readback through $R$, cofinal refinement through $Q$, finite-window projection through $W$, completion-facing exactness through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $A$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The source row $C$ supplies the Cauchy request, while $S$ and $R$ expose the finite stream and rational tolerance data on which the request is inspected.

The cofinal refinement row $Q$ records how later windows answer the same request, and $W$ projects each filter-style demand to the finite window actually read by the packet. The exactness row $E$ is therefore reached through finite-window projection and cofinal refinement, not through a quotient filter.

The structural rows $H,A,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same route. Since no further coordinate is present, the local NameCert cannot export selected limits, countable choice, host filter equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal Cauchy filter RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:cofinal-cauchy-filter-regseqrat-handoff}
Every Completion or Real consumer of an accepted $\CofinalCauchyFilterUp$ carrier must read the regular rational handoff through the displayed route
$$
C,S,R,Q,W,E \longmapsto H,A,P,N .
$$
Consequently a cofinal Cauchy filter request reaches $\RegSeqRatUp$ only after the StreamName finite window and cofinal refinement rows are visible, and it reaches $\CompletionUp$ or $\RealUp$ only through the completion-facing exactness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the Cauchy source row $C$ and the stream window row $S$. They determine which finite segment of the name is inspected for the current request.

The regular rational row $R$ then supplies the tolerance comparison. Cofinal refinement through $Q$ and projection through $W$ ensure that later filter-style requests reuse a displayed finite-window route rather than a host-level filter representative.

Only after those rows are exposed may the exactness row $E$ hand data to Completion or Real consumers. The rows $H,A,P,N$ preserve the same handoff under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming, so the consumer obtains no bypass around StreamName, RegSeqRat, cofinal refinement, or the Real seal.
\end{proof}
