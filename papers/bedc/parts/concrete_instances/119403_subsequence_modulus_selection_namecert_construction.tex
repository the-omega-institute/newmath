Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\SubsequenceModulusSelectionUp$ packet records the constructive act of extracting a displayed subsequence schedule together with the modulus rows used by later Cauchy and compactness arguments. It sits after the common-modulus sequence-space surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-cauchy-sequence-space-namecert}, the finite stream schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Subsequence modulus selection carrier]
\label{def:subsequence-modulus-selection-carrier}
A $\SubsequenceModulusSelectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(S,R,D,U,K,M,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule for the source sequence names under inspection.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row attached to the same windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare source windows, selected windows, and tail budgets.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformCauchySequenceSpaceUp$ common-modulus row that supplies the ambient family discipline.
\item $K$ is the finite subsequence-index schedule, listing only inspected source positions and their order relation inside the displayed windows.
\item $M$ is the modulus-selection row assigning each accepted tolerance request to a displayed tail budget in $D,S,R,U,K$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after the source schedule, readback, dyadic ledger, uniform Cauchy row, subsequence schedule, and selected modulus row have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream schedule, readback, dyadic ledger, uniform Cauchy row, subsequence schedule, modulus row, terminal seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through $S,R,D,U,K,M,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SubsequenceModulusSelectionUp$, $\UniformCauchySequenceSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SubsequenceModulusSelectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a selected limit, an infinite choice function, trichotomy, quotient sequence equality, host sequence equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Subsequence modulus selection obligations]
\label{thm:subsequence-modulus-selection-obligations}
For every accepted $\SubsequenceModulusSelectionUp$ carrier $X=(S,R,D,U,K,M,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SubsequenceModulusSelectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite source-window exposure, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance accounting, common-modulus sequence-space admission, finite subsequence-index scheduling, selected-modulus exactness, terminal Real seal handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every compactness-facing or completion-facing read must pass through
$$
S,R,D,U,K,M,E,H,C,P,N
$$
and through no selected limit, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy, quotient sequence equality, host sequence equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:subsequence-modulus-selection-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly $S,R,D,U,K,M,E$, and the structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

The source schedule $S$ exposes only finite inspected windows, while $R$ records regular rational readback and $D$ records the tolerance cells used by the comparison. The uniform sequence-space row $U$ imports the common-modulus discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-cauchy-sequence-space-namecert}.

The subsequence-index row $K$ and modulus row $M$ are the additional selection data. They name displayed positions and displayed tail budgets before the terminal seal $E$ can be consumed; neither row stores an infinite selector or a completed limit.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only this finite packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores countable choice, trichotomy, quotient equality, host sequence equality, a selected limit, or ambient completeness, the displayed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Subsequence modulus selection nonescape]
\label{thm:subsequence-modulus-selection-nonescape}
No accepted $\SubsequenceModulusSelectionUp$ packet exports data outside $S,R,D,U,K,M,E,H,C,P,N$. In particular, a compactness-facing, Cauchy-extraction-facing, or $\RealUp$ consumer cannot read a selected limit, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy principle, quotient sequence equality, host sequence equality, or ambient completeness theorem from the subsequence/modulus surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the carrier. Its only analytic coordinates are the finite source schedule, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, uniform Cauchy sequence-space row, subsequence-index schedule, modulus-selection row, and terminal Real seal.

Apply \autoref{thm:subsequence-modulus-selection-obligations}. Every local obligation is a projection of those rows or a structural transport, replay, provenance, or naming row for the same packet.

Thus any downstream compactness, Cauchy extraction, completion, or $\RealUp$ consumer reaches the seal only through the displayed subsequence/modulus route. The packet contains no coordinate from which a selected limit, infinite selector, trichotomy branch, quotient equality, host sequence equality, or ambient completeness object could be exported.
\end{proof}
