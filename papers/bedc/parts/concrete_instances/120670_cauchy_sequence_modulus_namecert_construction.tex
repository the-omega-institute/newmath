\begin{definition}[Cauchy sequence modulus carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-sequence-modulus-carrier}
A $\CauchySequenceModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(E,R,W,Q,L,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the $\EffectiveCauchySequenceUp$ source row, exposing the effective Cauchy reading before a tail budget is exported.
\item $R$ is the $\RegularCauchySequenceUp$ regularity row, recording the regular Cauchy sequence discipline that the modulus serves.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, displaying the inspected sequence windows and index requests.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, binding the displayed stream windows to regular rational approximation data.
\item $L$ is the finite tail-bound ledger row, storing the explicit threshold and tolerance histories used to compare later sequence windows.
\item $S$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $E,R,W,Q,L$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, regularity, window, readback, ledger, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

$$

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchySequenceModulusUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $E,R,W,Q,L,S,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected limit, quotient stream equality, countable-choice tail selector, trichotomy, host real equality, or ambient completeness.

\label{thm:cauchy-sequence-modulus-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-sequence-modulus-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the effective source, regular sequence, stream-window, readback, tail-ledger, Real-seal, and structural rows.

Read the source route first. Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier contains no selected limit, quotient equality, countable-choice selector, trichotomy, host equality, or ambient completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
