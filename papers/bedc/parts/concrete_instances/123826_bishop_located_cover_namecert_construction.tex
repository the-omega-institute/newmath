\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BishopLocatedCoverUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-located-cover-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopLocatedCoverUp$ packet records the finite located-cover surface used by Bishop-style real and completion arguments. It sits over the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite stream-name windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the locatedness budget of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedness-modulus-namecert}. The packet gives finite cover and completion-budget consumers a displayed located-cover route; it is not open-cover compactness, a selected subcover functional, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Bishop located-cover carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-cover-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(E,Q,S,D,M,C,W,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row being covered.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row that exposes the regular rational name used by the cover request.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row inspected before the cover decision is consumed.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger naming the dyadic radius and rational cells used by the cover.
\item $M$ is the $\LocatednessModulusUp$ budget row that turns each positive tolerance request into displayed endpoint and window data.
\item $C$ is the finite located-cover row listing the displayed cover cells that meet the requested real-name window.
\item $W$ is the completion-budget row recording which finite cover cells may be handed to a Real or Completion consumer.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the real seal, regular readback, stream window, dyadic ledger, locatedness budget, cover row, completion-budget row, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from real-name request through regular readback, stream observation, dyadic tolerance, locatedness budget, cover row, completion-budget row, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopLocatedCoverUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\LocatednessModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts exactly packets carrying these eleven rows. It exports no open-cover compactness principle, selected subcover functional, quotient stream equality, quotient real equality, countable-choice schedule, hidden limit, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located-cover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-cover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedCoverUp$ carrier
$$
L=(E,Q,S,D,M,C,W,H,R,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedCoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the $\RealUp$ seal row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, $\LocatednessModulusUp$ budget row, finite located-cover row, completion-budget row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. A cover-facing consumer must display $Q,S,D,M$ before it can consume $C$, and it must display $C$ before it can consume the completion-budget row $W$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-cover-carrier}. The analytic rows available to the local classifier are exactly $E,Q,S,D,M,C,W$.

Read the route in order. The regular rational readback $Q$ and stream window $S$ expose the finite name data, the dyadic ledger $D$ fixes the tolerance scale, and the locatedness row $M$ supplies the budget by which that tolerance becomes displayed endpoint and window data.

Only after those rows are visible may the finite located-cover row $C$ be consumed. The completion-budget row $W$ is downstream of the displayed cover row, so it records which already displayed cover cells may be handed to a Real or Completion consumer.

The structural rows $H,R,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no excluded principle is a coordinate of $L$, the local NameCert obligations cannot export open-cover compactness, a selected subcover functional, quotient equality, countable choice, hidden limits, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located-cover Real-completion handoff]
\label{thm:bishop-located-cover-real-completion-handoff}
Every Real or Completion consumer of an accepted $\BishopLocatedCoverUp$ packet receives located-cover data only through the displayed route
$$
\RealUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \LocatednessModulusUp .
$$
The handoff may consume the finite located-cover row $C$ and completion-budget row $W$ after this route has been displayed, but it exports no open-cover compactness principle, selected subcover functional, quotient stream equality, quotient real equality, countable-choice schedule, hidden limit, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:bishop-located-cover-namecert-obligations}. They force a cover-facing consumer to read the regular rational, stream-window, dyadic, and locatedness-budget rows before the cover row is available.

The Real-facing side therefore receives only displayed real-name data and the finite cover row attached to that data. The Completion-facing side receives the same route through $W$, which records a completion budget over already displayed cover cells rather than a selected limit or quotient object.

Replay through $H,R,P,N$ preserves the same finite packet. Because the carrier contains no row for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient equality, countable choice, hidden limits, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, no Real or Completion consumer can project those endpoints from the handoff.
\end{proof}
