\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate-discharge matrix]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-discharge-matrix}
Let
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)
$$
be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. A discharge-socket read through $K$ may be consumed only after the candidate-mediated strong-normalization rows, residual diamond budget rows, bounded checker rows, and the four L10 faces
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\qquad
\StreamNameUp,\qquad
\RegSeqRatUp,\qquad
\RealUp
$$
are all displayed on the same packet route. The read therefore factors through the candidate-mediated SN frontier, the residual-substitution diamond budget, the visible residual route, and the L10 normalization discharge budget before any consumer reads $D$ as a discharge socket.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the candidate-mediated SN frontier of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-mediated-sn-frontier}. It places the candidate rows and the four L10 faces on the accepted route before the confluence-facing or decidability-facing handoff can be consumed.

Attach the residual-substitution diamond budget of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-substitution-diamond-budget}. That route forces the residual budget, diamond checker, candidate-mediated frontier, and L10 source rows to remain on the same packet before bounded conversion reads $U$.

Now use the visible residual route of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-discharge-visible-residual-route} together with the L10 normalization discharge budget of \autoref{thm:metaciccriticalpath-l10-normalization-discharge-budget}. These rows keep the bounded checker, obstruction row $O$, discharge socket $D$, transport row $H$, continuation row $C$, provenance row $P$, local naming row $L$, and the four L10 faces visible before the socket is read.

Since the displayed matrix contains no coordinate for closed subject reduction, full consistency, global confluence, beta-conversion decidability, quotient term equality, selected limits, or $\RealUp$ completeness, the discharge read is exactly the finite candidate-residual-L10 socket surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC L10 candidate socket exhaustion]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-l10-candidate-socket-exhaustion}
For an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet $K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)$, omitting any candidate-mediated row, residual-budget row, bounded-checker row, or any one of the L10 faces $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ leaves the candidate-discharge socket unmet. In that case no consumer may read the missing surface as closed subject reduction, full consistency, global confluence, beta-conversion decidability, quotient term equality, selected limits, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the matrix theorem above. It identifies the whole discharge-socket surface as the joint display of candidate-mediated rows, residual diamond budget rows, bounded checker rows, and the four L10 faces on one packet route.

If a candidate row is absent, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-mediated-sn-frontier} supplies no frontier for the handoff. If a residual row or checker row is absent, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-substitution-diamond-budget} and \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-discharge-visible-residual-route} supply no visible residual route to the socket.

If an L10 face is absent, \autoref{thm:metaciccriticalpath-l10-normalization-discharge-budget} does not authorize a discharge read through the source budget. Thus each omission leaves the socket unmet and keeps all global metatheoretic, quotient, selected-limit, and Real-completeness readings outside the accepted packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate-discharge matrix frontier]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-discharge-matrix-frontier}
Let $K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)$ be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. If the candidate-mediated strong-normalization rows, residual-substitution diamond rows, bounded checker rows, and the four L10 faces $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ are all displayed on the same packet route, then $K$ supplies only a bounded formal-target frontier for the discharge socket $D$. The frontier authorizes the visible candidate-residual-L10 handoff and does not close subject reduction, full consistency, global confluence, beta-conversion decidability, quotient term equality, selected limits, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the matrix theorem \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-discharge-matrix}. It identifies a discharge-socket read with the joint display of candidate-mediated rows, residual diamond budget rows, bounded checker rows, and the four L10 faces on the same accepted route.

Next apply the socket-exhaustion theorem \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-l10-candidate-socket-exhaustion}. It shows that any missing candidate row, residual row, checker row, or L10 face leaves the socket unmet, so the displayed matrix is exactly the finite boundary that a consumer may use.

The carrier $K$ contains no row that asserts closed subject reduction, full consistency, global confluence, beta-conversion decidability, quotient term equality, selected limits, or $\RealUp$ completeness. Hence the accepted route supplies a bounded formal-target frontier for $D$ and no global metatheoretic theorem beyond that frontier.
\end{proof}
