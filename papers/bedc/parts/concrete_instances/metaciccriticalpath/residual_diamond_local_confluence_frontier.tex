\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC residual-diamond local-confluence frontier]\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-diamond-local-confluence-frontier}
Let
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)
$$
be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. Any local-confluence read for $K$ must expose the residual-substitution frontier, candidate-mediated strong-normalization frontier, residual-budget socket, bounded confluence checker, and the four L10 source faces $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ before it reads $U$. The read is therefore a finite residual-diamond frontier over the displayed packet rows, not a global confluence theorem, beta-conversion decidability result, quotient equality of terms, selected limit, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-substitution-diamond-budget}. It already binds residual substitution, candidate-mediated normalization, residual budget, bounded checker data, and L10 source rows to the same accepted packet route before any confluence-facing consumer can use the handoff.

Use residual diamond source totality, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-diamond-source-totality}. The local-confluence read may inspect $U$ only after the closed-substitution boundary, residual-budget socket, candidate-mediated frontier, and source rows are all displayed on $S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L$.

Finally apply the L10 source separation theorem, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-diamond-l10-source-separation}. The four source faces remain distinct rows throughout the residual-diamond route, so a checker that merges them, reconstructs them after reading $U$, or detaches the residual proof from the bounded budget leaves the accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet.
\end{proof}

Finally use the L10 source separation theorem, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-diamond-l10-source-separation}. \begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate residual-diamond lock]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-diamond-lock}
Let
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)
$$
be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. Any local-confluence read that uses both candidate-mediated strong-normalization data and residual-diamond data must keep the candidate rows, residual-budget rows, obstruction row $O$, discharge socket $D$, and the four L10 faces $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ visible before $U$ is consumed. The candidate frontier may support the handoff, but it cannot fill $D$ or export closed subject reduction, global confluence, beta-decidability, quotient term equality, selected limits, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

It places residual substitution, the residual-budget socket, bounded checker data, and the four L10 source faces before any local-confluence consumer may read $U$.

Next apply the candidate-SN confluence handoff, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-sn-confluence-handoff}, and residual-diamond source determinacy, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-diamond-source-determinacy}. These results force the candidate-mediated and residual-diamond rows to remain attached to the same packet $S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L$ while preserving the four L10 faces as separate source rows.

Now use candidate residual stability and frontier nonescape, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-stability} and \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-frontier-nonescape}. The candidate rows may carry the finite handoff, but they remain a displayed frontier over the same residual budget and cannot erase $O$, fill $D$, or detach the L10 source route.

Finally apply subject-reduction socket separation, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-subject-reduction-socket-separation}. Since $D$ is a separate socket row and no displayed candidate or residual row is a subject-reduction certificate, the local-confluence read cannot export any of the excluded global metatheoretic, quotient, selected-limit, host-equality, or Real-completeness principles.
\end{proof}
