\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC critical-path diamond-frontier consumer]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-diamond-frontier-consumer}
Any $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ read that consumes
$\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ must keep the residual-substitution
premise, critical-pair, candidate-join, bounded-check, obstruction,
discharge-socket, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows
visible before the normal-form consistency consumer may inspect the
handoff. The read is therefore a candidate-mediated frontier consumer,
not a proof of subject reduction, full Church--Rosser, evaluator
equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First keep the critical-path boundary visible. Apply
\autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-consistency-handoff} together with
\autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-obstruction-boundary} and
\autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-discharge-socket-nonescape}. These
theorems route a consistency-facing consumer through the displayed
critical-path packet while preserving the obstruction row and the
discharge socket before the normal-form endpoint can be read.

Next attach the parallel-diamond frontier. Use
\autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-residual-substitution-scope},
\autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-critical-pair-exhaustion},
\autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-local-join-scope}, and
\autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-closed-checker-retention}. These
results keep the residual-substitution premise, critical pair,
candidate join, and bounded checker rows inside the
$\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$ packet.

Finally compose the two consumer routes. The frontier handoff of
\autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-parallel-diamond-frontier-handoff}
and the candidate-mediated strong-normalization socket of
\autoref{thm:metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-candidate-mediated-sn-socket}
place the frontier rows on the same critical-path read before the
normal-form consistency consumer inspects the handoff. Since neither
component route supplies subject reduction, full Church--Rosser,
evaluator equality, quotient term equality, or kernel consistency, the
composed consumer exports none of those claims.
\end{proof}
