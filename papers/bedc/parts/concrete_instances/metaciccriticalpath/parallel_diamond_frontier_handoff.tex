\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC critical-path parallel-diamond frontier handoff]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-parallel-diamond-frontier-handoff}
Let
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)
$$
be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. Suppose a parallel-diamond discharge request for $K$ consumes the candidate-mediated strong-normalization frontier, the residual substitution budget, the local diamond witness, and the L10 four-object source ledger for $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$. Then the request factors through the displayed candidate frontier before it can be read as a confluence consumer or a normalization consumer.

The handoff is a finite same-packet route through $S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L$. It does not export subject reduction, global Church--Rosser, host beta equality, evidence-free strong normalization, quotient term equality, selected limits, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First read the candidate side. The candidate-mediated SN frontier of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-mediated-sn-frontier} and the candidate discharge frontier of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-discharge-frontier} place the normalization candidate and its discharge rows on the displayed critical-path packet before any public consumer can use them.

Next attach the residual and diamond budget. The residual substitution diamond budget, the residual-diamond local-confluence frontier, and the residual diamond budget scope keep the residual substitution rows, local join witness, checker factorization, and residual readback on the same finite route.

Finally attach the L10 ledger. The four-object exit certificate and the residual completion exactness handoff force every completion-facing read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ before a confluence or normalization endpoint can be read.

Thus a parallel-diamond discharge request reaches its endpoint only through the displayed candidate frontier followed by the residual, local-diamond, and L10 rows of $K$. Since the cited route has no coordinate for subject reduction, global Church--Rosser, host beta equality, evidence-free strong normalization, quotient term equality, selected limits, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or kernel consistency, none of those claims is exported.
\end{proof}
