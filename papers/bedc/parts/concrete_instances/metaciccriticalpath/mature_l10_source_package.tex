The visible residual route of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-discharge-visible-residual-route} and the residual diamond budget scope of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-diamond-budget-scope} then bind residual substitution, local diamond data, bounded checker rows, obstruction, socket, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming to the same finite route.
