\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate residual budget interface]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-budget-interface}
For every accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L),
$$
any candidate-mediated residual substitution, bounded confluence, or decidability read that reaches the critical-path normal-form endpoint must first expose the four L10 source rows
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\qquad
\StreamNameUp,\qquad
\RegSeqRatUp,\qquad
\RealUp
$$
through the open-phase source ledger of \autoref{def:metacic-critical-path-open-phase-source-ledger}. The candidate rows, residual substitution rows, SN confluence-decidability budget, residual-budget socket, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row remain visible before any critical-path consumer reads the endpoint $N$. The interface exports no closed subject reduction, global confluence, beta-conversion decidability, full consistency, quotient term equality, selected limits, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the open-phase source ledger. Definition~\ref{def:metacic-critical-path-open-phase-source-ledger} lists the packet rows $S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L$ together with the four L10 source rows $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$.

Attach the candidate residual stability surface, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-stability}. It keeps candidate-mediated residual substitution attached to the same four source faces and prevents the residual handoff from becoming a global confluence or decidability theorem.

Use the residual-confluence budget bridge, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-confluence-budget-bridge}, together with the SN confluence-decidability budget of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-sn-confluence-budget}. These routes force residual substitution, bounded confluence, and decidability consumers through one finite ledger before any normal-form endpoint read is exported.

Finally apply the frontier nonescape route, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-frontier-nonescape}. It leaves only the displayed candidate rows, residual rows, socket budget, L10 source faces, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since none of those rows carries the excluded metatheoretic or real-completion principles, the budget interface exposes exactly the stated source rows before $N$ can be read.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC critical-path candidate residual discharge budget]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-discharge-budget}
For every accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L),
$$
a bounded residual-substitution discharge may consume the critical-path normal-form endpoint $N$ only after the open-phase source ledger of \autoref{def:metacic-critical-path-open-phase-source-ledger}, the candidate residual stability surface of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-stability}, the residual-confluence budget bridge of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-confluence-budget-bridge}, the SN confluence-decidability budget of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-sn-confluence-budget}, and the frontier nonescape route of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-frontier-nonescape} are all visible. The discharge read remains a bounded packet route through the displayed source, residual, obstruction, socket, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it exports no global confluence, beta-conversion decidability, quotient term equality, host equality, selected limits, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First apply \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-budget-interface}. It requires any candidate-mediated residual substitution, bounded confluence, or decidability read reaching $N$ to expose the four L10 source rows through the open-phase source ledger before the endpoint is consumed.

Next use the candidate residual stability surface and the residual-confluence budget bridge. The stability theorem keeps residual substitution attached to the same candidate and L10 source faces, while the bridge keeps confluence-facing reads inside the finite residual ledger rather than turning them into global confluence or beta-conversion decidability.

Then add the SN confluence-decidability budget. That budget forces the strong-normalization, confluence, and decidability rows to remain a bounded handoff through the packet coordinates $S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L$, so quotient term equality and host equality have no separate source row.

Finally apply the frontier nonescape route. Its coordinate exhaustion leaves only the displayed candidate, residual, source, obstruction, socket, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows, so selected limits and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness cannot be recovered from the discharge read.
\end{proof}
