\label{def:metacic-critical-path-candidate-mediated-frontier}

$$

$$
the candidate-mediated strong-normalization frontier is the finite subroute that displays the strong-normalization row $S$, the normal-form endpoint $N$, the handoff row $U$, the discharge socket $D$, the componentwise transport row $H$, the continuation row $C$, the provenance row $P$, and the local naming row $L$, together with the four local source faces $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ already recorded for the open-phase source ledger.
