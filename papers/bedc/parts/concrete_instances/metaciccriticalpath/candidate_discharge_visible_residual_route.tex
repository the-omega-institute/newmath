\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate-discharge visible residual route]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-discharge-visible-residual-route}
Let
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)
$$
be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. Any candidate-mediated strong-normalization, confluence, or decidability consumer that reads the critical-path handoff through $K$ must first expose the candidate row, residual-substitution row, normalization-budget row, bounded checker row, obstruction row $O$, discharge socket $D$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and local $\NameCert$ row $L$. The route also keeps the four L10 faces $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ visible until the consumer reaches the handoff.

The visible-residual route exports no unconditional strong normalization, full Church--Rosser theorem, closed subject reduction, global conversion decidability, quotient syntax equality, selected limit, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or kernel consistency claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with candidate residual stability, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-stability}. It attaches candidate-mediated evidence to residual-substitution rows, leaves $O,D$ visible, and keeps the four L10 faces on the same finite packet route.

Apply the candidate residual budget interface of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-budget-interface}. This exposes the normalization-budget row, residual-budget socket, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before any normal-form endpoint or critical-path handoff is read.

Finally use the residual diamond budget scope of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-diamond-budget-scope} together with the bounded checker factorization of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-sn-confluence-checker-factorization}. These supply the bounded checker and local residual-diamond route while preserving the same candidate, obstruction, socket, L10, transport, replay, provenance, and NameCert rows. Since the cited surfaces contain none of the excluded global metatheoretic, quotient, completion, or consistency coordinates, the candidate-discharge consumer can read only the displayed visible-residual route.
\end{proof}
