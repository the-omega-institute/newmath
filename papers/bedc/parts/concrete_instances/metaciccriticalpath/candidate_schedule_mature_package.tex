\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate schedule mature package]
\label{thm:metaciccriticalpath-candidate-schedule-mature-package}
Let
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)
$$
be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. A mature candidate schedule package is readable only after the candidate schedule discharge of \autoref{thm:metacic-criticalpath-candidate-schedule-discharge}, the residual-budget discharge frontier of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-budget-discharge-frontier}, and the L10 source package of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-mature-package} have all been exposed on the same packet. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the candidate schedule discharge. It forces the candidate-mediated schedule to display candidate-set normalization, critical-pair ledger, residual-substitution boundary, finite observation confluence, and subject-reduction socket rows before the schedule is treated as discharged.

Next attach the residual-budget frontier. The residual route requires residual-budget rows, substitution-boundary rows, closed-premise ledger rows, and local-diamond rows on the same packet rows $S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L$ before the discharge socket can be read.

Finally attach the mature L10 source package. That route keeps $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ as the four visible source faces while residual, confluence, and candidate-mediated strong-normalization rows remain budget data attached to the packet.

The three routes share the same carrier and structural rows. Their combined read is therefore a candidate schedule mature package, not a detached metatheorem. Since none of the component routes has a coordinate for subject reduction, global Church--Rosser, quotient term equality, kernel consistency, selected limits, or ambient completeness, the package exports none of those claims.
\end{proof}
