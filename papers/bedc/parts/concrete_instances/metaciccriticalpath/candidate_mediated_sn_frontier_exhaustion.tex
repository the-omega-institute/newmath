\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate-mediated SN frontier exhaustion]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-mediated-sn-frontier-exhaustion}
Let
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)
$$
be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. Every candidate-mediated strong-normalization frontier read that may be consumed by the public $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ route must factor through the existing candidate-mediated frontier, residual-budget discharge surface, confluence-decidability handoff, and decidability budget rows before it is exported. Concretely, the read displays $S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L$, the residual-budget and socket rows of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-budget-discharge-frontier}, the handoff route of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-sn-confluence-decidability-handoff}, and the decidability budget of \autoref{thm:metaciccriticalpath-confluence-decidability-budget}; it cannot export full normalization, full confluence, global beta-conversion decidability, closed subject reduction, quotient term equality, host equality, selected limits, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.MetaCICCriticalPathUp.MetaCICCriticalPathCandidateMediatedSNFrontierExhaustion}
\begin{proof}
Start from the candidate-mediated SN frontier \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-mediated-sn-frontier}. It places the strong-normalization read on the finite packet rows $S,N,U,D,H,C,P,L$ together with the surrounding obstruction row $O$ and the four L10 source faces.

Next attach the residual-budget discharge surface. The residual frontier and socket route, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-budget-discharge-frontier} and \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-budget-socket-route}, require the residual-budget, substitution-boundary, closed-premise, local-diamond, socket, normalization, and audit-ledger rows before the discharge-facing consumer may proceed.

Now pass through the confluence and decidability side. The handoff theorem \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-sn-confluence-decidability-handoff} and the confluence-decidability budget \autoref{thm:metaciccriticalpath-confluence-decidability-budget} keep every confluence-facing or decidability-facing read downstream of the same finite packet route and open L10 source ledger.

The public route receives only this assembled finite boundary: the candidate-mediated frontier, residual-budget discharge surface, handoff rows, decidability budget, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local $\NameCert$ data. Since none of the cited rows supplies a coordinate for full normalization, full confluence, global beta-conversion decidability, closed subject reduction, quotient equality, host equality, selected limits, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or kernel consistency, no such claim can be exported by the frontier read.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate-mediated SN frontier public scope]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-mediated-sn-frontier-exhaustion-scope}
Let $K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)$ be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. The candidate-mediated strong-normalization frontier exhaustion of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-mediated-sn-frontier-exhaustion} is public only when the same packet displays the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ source rows together with the candidate-mediated frontier, residual-budget socket, confluence-decidability handoff, and decidability budget rows.

Under that scope, the public read is the finite L10 source route plus the displayed MetaCIC boundary rows. It does not export full normalization, full confluence, beta-conversion decidability, host consistency, selected limits, quotient stream equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.MetaCICCriticalPathUp.MetaCICCriticalPathCandidateMediatedSNFrontierExhaustionScope}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-mediated-sn-frontier-exhaustion}. It already confines the candidate-mediated strong-normalization frontier to the displayed packet rows and to the residual-budget, handoff, and decidability-budget surfaces.

Now attach the public interface route. The $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ source rows are the L10 four-object rows through which the public read may enter the MetaCIC packet. The residual-budget socket and public handoff rows keep that read in the same finite carrier rather than in an ambient normalization theorem.

Exhaust the displayed rows. The candidate-mediated frontier supplies only the frontier boundary, the residual-budget socket supplies only the budgeted discharge route, and the confluence-decidability handoff with its decidability budget supplies only the named finite decision surface.

Therefore the public export is scoped to the four L10 source rows and the displayed MetaCIC boundary rows. Full normalization, full confluence, beta-conversion decidability, host consistency, selected limits, quotient stream equality, and ambient Real completeness remain outside the packet.
\end{proof}
