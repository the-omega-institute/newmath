\begin{theorem}[MetaCICCriticalPath mature consumer synthesis]
\label{thm:metaciccriticalpath-mature-consumer-synthesis}
Let
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)
$$
be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. A mature consumer of $K$ receives exactly the finite synthesis of the confluence frontier, normalization frontier, substitution boundary, typed-example row, and bounded checker routes already displayed by the critical-path child files. The consumer surface is therefore the same packet route through $S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L$ together with the four L10 faces $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$.

This synthesis does not claim full normalization, full confluence, closed subject reduction, arbitrary Church--Rosser, global beta-conversion decidability, quotient term equality, selected limits, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.MetaCICCriticalPathUp.MetaCICCriticalPathMatureConsumerSynthesis}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the normalization and confluence dependency rows. Theorems~\ref{thm:metacic-critical-path-normalization-frontier-dependency-exposure} and~\ref{thm:metacic-critical-path-confluence-audit-dependency-exposure} place the normalization frontier on $S,N$ and the confluence audit route on $U$, while the obstruction row $O$ and discharge socket $D$ remain visible.

Next attach the substitution boundary. Theorem~\ref{thm:metacic-critical-path-closed-substitution-boundary-exposure} keeps closed-substitution support as boundary data routed through $U$ and $D$, not as a filled subject-reduction row.

Now add the typed-example and bounded checker surfaces. Theorems~\ref{thm:metaciccriticalpath-l10-candidate-discharge-axis}, \ref{thm:metacic-typed-reduction-candidate-sn-handoff}, and~\ref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-sn-confluence-checker-factorization} require typed-example coverage, candidate-mediated strong-normalization evidence, bounded checker data, and the four L10 source faces to remain on the same displayed route.

Finally apply the mature routes. Theorems~\ref{thm:metacic-critical-path-parallel-diamond-frontier-handoff}, \ref{thm:metaciccriticalpath-candidate-schedule-mature-package}, and~\ref{thm:metacic-criticalpath-mature-closure-witness} package the residual diamond frontier, candidate schedule, and mature L10 source rows as one consumer surface. None of the cited routes contains a coordinate for the excluded global metatheoretic or real-completion principles, so the synthesis exports only the finite mature consumer route stated above.
\end{proof}
