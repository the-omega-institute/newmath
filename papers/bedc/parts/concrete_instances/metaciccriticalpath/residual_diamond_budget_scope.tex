\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC critical-path residual diamond budget scope]
\label{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-diamond-budget-scope}
Let
$$
K=(S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L)
$$
be an accepted $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ packet. A candidate-mediated residual diamond read may enter the public critical-path route only after the packet exposes the candidate schedule, residual substitution budget, local join ledger, confluence checker factorization, and L10 source rows on the same route through $S,N,O,U,D,H,C,P,L$. The read is therefore a finite budget scope for the displayed packet; it is not a global strong-normalization theorem, global confluence theorem, beta-conversion decision procedure, quotient term equality, selected limit, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle, or kernel consistency claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the residual-substitution diamond budget of \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-substitution-diamond-budget}. It places residual substitution, candidate-mediated strong normalization, residual-budget socket data, and L10 source rows on one accepted packet route before a diamond or decidability consumer may read the handoff.

Next use the candidate residual budget interface, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-residual-budget-interface}. This forces the candidate rows and the four L10 source faces $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ to remain visible before a critical-path endpoint is read.

Apply the residual-diamond local-confluence frontier together with the candidate-SN confluence checker factorization, \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-residual-diamond-local-confluence-frontier} and \autoref{thm:metacic-critical-path-candidate-sn-confluence-checker-factorization}. These theorems supply the local join ledger and checker factorization while keeping the checker inside the same finite packet route.

The displayed rows exhaust the residual diamond budget scope. Since no row carries global SN, global confluence, beta-decidability, quotient equality, selected limits, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or full consistency, the public MetaCICCriticalPath route can consume only the shown finite budget.
\end{proof}
