Following Bishop-style finite-cover proofs of uniform continuity on compact metric data, the $\FiniteCoverUniformModulusEnvelopeUp$ packet records the finite envelope needed by constructive compact-continuous consumers. It reads compact metric finite-net data from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, total-bounded schedules from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, continuous-map pointwise rows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, and formal-ball tolerances from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-formalball-namecert}. The envelope is a displayed finite cover and modulus route, not an open-cover compactness theorem and not a host continuity principle.

\begin{definition}[Finite-cover uniform modulus envelope carrier]
\label{def:finite-cover-uniform-modulus-envelope-carrier}
A $\FiniteCoverUniformModulusEnvelopeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(K,T,F,B,M,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite compact source row.
\item $T$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite schedule row selecting the inspected cover cells.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ pointwise continuity row read at the selected cells.
\item $B$ is the $\FormalBallUp$ tolerance and containment row for the cover cells.
\item $M$ is the displayed uniform modulus envelope extracted from $K,T,F,B$.
\item $W$ is the finite window ledger tying the cover cells, pointwise moduli, and output modulus to one read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the displayed rows.
\item $C$ records the $\Cont$ replay from compact source to finite cover, pointwise continuity, formal-ball tolerance, and modulus envelope.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactMetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\FormalBallUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and the local naming row.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverUniformModulusEnvelopeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, countable choice, selected subsequences, host semantic continuity, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-cover uniform modulus envelope NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cover-uniform-modulus-envelope-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-cover-uniform-modulus-envelope-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverUniformModulusEnvelopeUp}$ surface consists of carrier admission for $U$; finite cover visibility through $\CompactMetricUp$ and $\TotallyBoundedUp$ rows; pointwise continuity and formal-ball tolerance visibility through $\ContinuousMapUp$ and $\FormalBallUp$ rows; componentwise transport and replay stability through $H,C,P$; and nonescape from open-cover compactness, countable choice, selected-subsequence, host-continuity, and completeness routes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission exposes the finite packet $U=(K,T,F,B,M,W,H,C,P,N)$. The compact and total-bounded rows supply only displayed finite cover data, and the continuous-map and formal-ball rows supply only pointwise and tolerance reads.

The modulus envelope $M$ is read through the window ledger $W$ and replay row $C$. Transport in $H$ and provenance in $P$ keep that read attached to the same finite packet and local name.

No excluded principle has a carrier coordinate. Thus open-cover compactness, countable choice, selected subsequences, host semantic continuity, and $\RealUp$ completeness are outside the exported NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier rows of \autoref{def:finite-cover-uniform-modulus-envelope-carrier}. The compact source, total-bounded schedule, continuous-map row, and formal-ball tolerance are the only inputs to the displayed envelope.

Apply \autoref{thm:finite-cover-uniform-modulus-envelope-namecert-obligations}. It binds those rows to $M,W,H,C,P,N$ as one local NameCert route.

Therefore a consumer can use the packet as a uniform-modulus source only by following the displayed route. Any appeal to open-cover compactness, countable choice, selected subsequences, host semantic continuity, or completeness has no exported row.
\end{proof}
