\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HedgehogSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-hedgehog-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Engelking on hedgehog spaces, the $\HedgehogSpaceUp$
packet names a finite-spine point-set topology surface rather than an
arbitrary-cardinal wedge. It sits after the metric carrier of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the topology carrier of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the finite real-distance
readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the visible
basepoint discipline already used by finite point-set packets. The
packet records a common basepoint, finitely displayed spines, branch
coordinates, metric and topology source rows, real distance rows, transport,
replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not claim arbitrary-cardinal
hedgehogs, choice-selected spine families, or a host topology of all wedges.

\begin{definition}[Hedgehog space carrier]
\label{def:hedgehog-space-carrier}
A $\HedgehogSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(S,b,X,Q,M,T,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the finite spine-label ledger. Each accepted spine label names one visible branch and no off-packet branch may be read.
\item $b$ is the common basepoint row shared by all displayed spines.
\item $X$ is the branch-coordinate row, recording for each displayed point either the basepoint or a visible spine label together with a finite interval coordinate.
\item $Q$ is the branch-equality and basepoint-collapse ledger. It identifies points from different spines only at $b$ and otherwise compares coordinates inside the same visible spine.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row for the finite wedge metric read.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row generated by the same finite branch and basepoint data.
\item $D$ is the $\RealUp$ distance row, exposing basepoint distance, same-spine coordinate distance, and different-spine wedge distance only through finite Real-facing readback.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for spine labels, basepoint rows, branch coordinates, equality collapse, metric source, topology source, Real distance, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a point request through finite spine inspection, basepoint collapse, metric and topology reads, Real distance readback, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HedgehogSpaceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HedgehogSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through
$S,b,X,Q,M,T,D,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary set of spines,
a choice-selected family of branches, a completed host wedge topology, or an
unrecorded quotient equality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hedgehog space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hedgehog-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HedgehogSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
J=(S,b,X,Q,M,T,D,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\HedgehogSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite
spine-label admission, common-basepoint admission, branch-coordinate
admission, basepoint-collapse exactness, $\MetricUp$ wedge-metric source
admission, $\TopologyUp$ generated-open source admission, $\RealUp$ distance
readback, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$
provenance, and local naming over the rows of
\autoref{def:hedgehog-space-carrier}. No obligation row asserts an
arbitrary-cardinal hedgehog, choice-selected spine family, host quotient
topology, or completed collection of all open neighbourhoods.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hedgehog-space-carrier}. The accepted
surface first displays the finite spine labels $S$, the common basepoint $b$,
the branch coordinates $X$, and the basepoint-collapse ledger $Q$.

The metric and topology rows are then read from the same visible data. The row
$M$ supplies only the finite wedge-metric source, while $T$ supplies only the
open-row provenance generated from the displayed branches and their shared
basepoint.

The distance row $D$ is terminal Real-facing readback for that same wedge
route. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the displayed rows
componentwise, and $P,N$ source and name them locally. Since no row stores an
arbitrary spine family, selected branch choice, host quotient topology, or
completed open lattice, none of those objects can become a NameCert
obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hedgehog metric wedge route]
\label{thm:hedgehog-space-metric-wedge-route}
Every $\MetricUp$-facing, $\TopologyUp$-facing, or $\RealUp$-distance consumer
of an accepted $\HedgehogSpaceUp$ packet must read the finite route
$$
S,b,X,Q \to M,T,D \to H,C,P,N .
$$
Same-spine reads compare branch coordinates inside one visible spine;
different-spine reads pass through the common basepoint row $b$ before
reaching the $\RealUp$ distance row. The route exports no arbitrary-cardinal
spine family, choice-selected branch set, host wedge topology, or quotient
space external to the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the visible point request. The classifier admits a point only when
the finite spine ledger, basepoint row, and branch-coordinate row display its
spine and coordinate data.

Use the collapse ledger $Q$ to separate the two metric cases. Same-spine
requests remain inside a single displayed branch, while different-spine
requests are forced through the common basepoint row before any distance
readback is exposed.

The rows $M,T,D$ then give the metric source, topology source, and Real-facing
distance for exactly that finite wedge route. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$
transport, replay, source, and name the same packet, so a consumer cannot
replace it by a host hedgehog with an arbitrary spine set or by a selected
family of branches.
\end{proof}
