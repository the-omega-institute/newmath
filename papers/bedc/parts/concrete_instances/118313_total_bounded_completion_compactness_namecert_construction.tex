\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\TotalBoundedCompletionCompactnessUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-total-bounded-completion-compactness-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on compact metric completions, the $\TotalBoundedCompletionCompactnessUp$ packet records the finite handoff by which a totally bounded completion route may be read by compact-metric consumers while retaining explicit net and Cauchy rows. It sits after the total-bounded completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallyboundedcompletion-namecert}, the precompact completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-precompact-completion-namecert}, and the constructive metric completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-constructive-metric-completion-namecert}, and it supplies a compactness-facing boundary for \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}. The packet is a finite NameCert surface; it is not an open-cover compactness theorem, a countable-choice principle, a quotient completion, or an ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[TotalBoundedCompletionCompactness carrier]
\label{def:total-bounded-completion-compactness-carrier}
A $\TotalBoundedCompletionCompactnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(M,T,F,C,Q,S,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the source metric carrier row, including the point histories, metric-distance witnesses, and classifier transport data used by the completion route.
\item $T$ is the total-bounded finite-net ledger inherited from $\TotallyBoundedCompletionUp$ and $\PrecompactCompletionUp$, naming centers, membership rows, rational radius bounds, and refinement reads for each displayed tolerance request.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterBasisUp$ or regular Cauchy basis row generated from the same finite-net data.
\item $C$ is the $\ConstructiveMetricCompletionUp$ route through dense embedding, regular Cauchy readback, dyadic tolerance control, and the terminal $\RealUp$ naming boundary.
\item $Q$ is the compact-metric reading row, allowing a downstream $\CompactMetricUp$ consumer to inspect only the displayed total-bounded completion route.
\item $S$ is the seal row recording that every compactness-facing read factors through $T,F,C,Q$ before any completion point, net refinement, or real-name boundary is consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, net ledger, basis row, constructive completion route, compact-metric reading row, seal row, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same row order.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TotalBoundedCompletionCompactnessUp$, $\TotallyBoundedCompletionUp$, $\PrecompactCompletionUp$, $\ConstructiveMetricCompletionUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TotalBoundedCompletionCompactnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $M,T,F,C,Q,S,H,R,P,N$. No coordinate stores open-cover compactness, a countable-choice selector, a quotient completion, selected completion representatives, detached host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[TotalBoundedCompletionCompactness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:total-bounded-completion-compactness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\TotalBoundedCompletionCompactnessUp$ carrier
$$
K=(M,T,F,C,Q,S,H,R,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\TotalBoundedCompletionCompactnessUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed metric row, finite-net ledger, Cauchy basis row, constructive completion route, compact-metric reading row, seal row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row;
\item finite-net persistence, requiring every compactness-facing tolerance request to expose the centers, membership constructors, rational radius bounds, and refinement reads stored in $T$;
\item completion-route admission, requiring the basis row $F$ and constructive route $C$ to be displayed before the compact-metric reader reaches $Q$;
\item compactness-facing seal discipline, requiring $S$ to record the same $T,F,C,Q$ order for every downstream compact-metric or uniform-continuity consumer;
\item nonescape from open-cover compactness, countable choice, quotient completion, selected representatives, host equality, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside these displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:total-bounded-completion-compactness-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the metric row, total-bounded finite-net ledger, Cauchy basis row, constructive completion route, compact-metric reading row, seal row, and structural rows.

Finite-net persistence is read from $T$. A compactness-facing tolerance request first opens the same finite centers, membership rows, radius bounds, and refinement data already named by $\TotallyBoundedCompletionUp$ and $\PrecompactCompletionUp$; it cannot replace them by an open cover or by a selected convergent subnet.

The completion route is then forced through $F$ and $C$. The basis row is generated from the displayed finite-net ledger, and the constructive completion row exposes only dense embedding, regular Cauchy readback, dyadic tolerance, and terminal real-name data before $Q$ is visible.

Finally $S,H,R,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the excluded principles have no carrier coordinate and no replay edge, the five rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[TotalBoundedCompletionCompactness compact handoff]
\label{thm:total-bounded-completion-compactness-compact-handoff}
Every accepted $\TotalBoundedCompletionCompactnessUp$ carrier $K=(M,T,F,C,Q,S,H,R,P,N)$ hands a compact-metric or uniform-continuity consumer only the displayed finite route
$$
M,T \longmapsto F \longmapsto C \longmapsto Q \longmapsto S,H,R,P,N .
$$
The route reads $\TotallyBoundedCompletionUp$ or $\PrecompactCompletionUp$ net data before it reads $\ConstructiveMetricCompletionUp$ completion data, and it reads the $\CompactMetricUp$ boundary only as a consumer of that route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the finite-net ledger $T$ over the source metric row $M$. These rows provide the only tolerance and center data available to the packet.

The basis and completion rows $F$ and $C$ are downstream of that ledger. They expose the constructive completion route without adding quotient representatives, selected limits, countable choice, or an ambient completeness theorem.

The compact-metric row $Q$ is then a reader of the displayed route, not an independent compactness proof. The seal and structural rows $S,H,R,P,N$ preserve the same order, so every downstream consumer receives the finite handoff stated above.
\end{proof}
