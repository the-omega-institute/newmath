\begin{theorem}[RieszBasis NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:riesz-basis-namecert-obligations}

$$
H,V,N,I,L,U \longrightarrow Q,\Gamma \longrightarrow T,C,P,Z .
$$
It receives no host Hilbert equality, no selected infinite series, no quotient vector equality, no countable-choice selector, and no ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:riesz-basis-carrier}. The rows $H,V,N$ expose the Hilbert, vector, and norm sources, while $I$ exposes the only index windows on which coefficient reads may be requested.

The frame-bound rows $L$ and $U$ are displayed beside the same index windows. The lower row records finite stability ledgers, and the upper row records finite bounded-synthesis ledgers, so neither bound can be read as an external Hilbert-space theorem.

The reconstruction row $Q$ is accepted only after those source and bound rows are visible. The classifier $\Gamma$ compares packets by the same displayed rows, and the structural rows $T,C,P,Z$ preserve that route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Since the carrier contains no host basis equality, selected infinite synthesis series, quotient vector equality, countable-choice selector, or ambient completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local naming surface.
\end{proof}
