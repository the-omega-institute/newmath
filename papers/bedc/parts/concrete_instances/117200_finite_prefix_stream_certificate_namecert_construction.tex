\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$ packet records the finite prefix of a countable name inspected at a displayed unary depth. It sits between the countable-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert} and the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, while keeping its dyadic tolerance row anchored in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, its terminal real-facing seal anchored in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and its completion-facing use anchored in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-regular-cauchy-completion-namecert}. The packet gives the Real and Completion route a named finite observation-prefix object, not quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Finite prefix stream certificate carrier]
\label{def:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-carrier}
A $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(k,W,D,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $k$ is the unary depth row. It fixes the largest inspected index and contains only the finite successor spine needed to reach that depth.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row. It displays exactly the stream-name observations at indices $0,\ldots,k$ and no infinite representative.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row. It records the finite dyadic radii, slack bounds, and comparison budgets used by the displayed windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row. It reads the same finite windows and dyadic budgets as regular rational data before any real-facing seal can be consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the unary-depth, stream-window, dyadic, regular-rational, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite prefix request through $k,W,D,R,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FinitePrefixStreamUp}$ row naming exactly this finite prefix certificate.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $k,W,D,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite prefix stream NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$ carrier $F=(k,W,D,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FinitePrefixStreamUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the unary depth, finite StreamName windows, dyadic tolerance rows, RegSeqRat handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. The obligation surface may cite the StreamName source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the RegSeqRat source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}; it cannot add quotient stream equality, a selected limit, countable choice, host real equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or a bridge certificate.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.FinitePrefixStreamUp.FinitePrefixStreamCarrier\_obligation\_surface}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-carrier}. The visible analytic rows are exactly the unary depth $k$, finite stream window $W$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, and regular rational handoff $R$.

Induct on the finite successor spine displayed by $k$. At depth $0$, the certificate reads only the initial window cell, its dyadic budget, and the matching regular rational observation. In the successor step, append one displayed window row and one matching dyadic budget, then transport the already-read prefix through $H$ and replay it through $C$.

The rows $P$ and $N$ source and name only that finite packet. Since the excluded principles are not fields of the carrier and are not produced by the induction, no local NameCert obligation can export quotient stream equality, a selected limit, countable choice, host real equality, ambient real completeness, or bridge checking.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite prefix stream window readback]
\label{thm:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-window-readback}
Every RegSeqRat-facing consumer of an accepted $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$ carrier reads the displayed prefix by the finite route
$$
k \longmapsto W \longmapsto D \longmapsto R \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
The exported read is a prefix-bounded regular-rational handoff, not an equality class of streams, not a chosen infinite tail, not a countable-choice schedule, not host equality of real names, and not ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.FinitePrefixStreamUp.FinitePrefixStreamCarrier\_window\_readback}
\begin{proof}
Fix a consumer of the accepted carrier. The first row available to the consumer is the unary depth $k$, so the consumer receives a finite bound before any stream observation is inspected.

The window read is then bounded by $W$. For each displayed index at or below $k$, the consumer reads the corresponding dyadic budget from $D$ and the matching regular rational row from $R$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve this same finite route under transport, replay, provenance, and naming. They do not add an infinite tail, an equality class of streams, a choice schedule, host equality of real names, or completeness data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite prefix stream observation induction]
\label{thm:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-observation-induction}
Let $F=(k,W,D,R,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$ carrier. For every displayed unary index $i \leq k$, the observation at $i$ is obtained by induction on the successor spine of $k$: the route reads exactly the corresponding $\StreamNameUp$ window row in $W$, the matching $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row in $D$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row in $R$, and the structural $H,C,P,N$ rows. The induction exports a finite observation proof only; it does not export quotient stream equality, a selected tail, countable choice, host real equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or a bridge certificate.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.FinitePrefixStreamUp.FinitePrefixStreamCarrier\_observation\_induction}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{def:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-carrier} to project the unary depth row $k$ and the finite rows $W,D,R,H,C,P,N$.

The base depth reads the initial window entry from $W$, the matching dyadic tolerance entry from $D$, and the corresponding regular-rational handoff from $R$. The structural rows then record the displayed transport, replay, provenance, and local naming for that initial observation.

For the successor step, extend the already-read finite route by one displayed observation. Apply \autoref{thm:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-window-readback} to force the new observation to pass through the same $W,D,R$ rows before it reaches the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Thus every index $i \leq k$ is read by finite induction on the displayed spine. Since no excluded object is a carrier coordinate or a product of the induction, the observation proof remains a finite StreamName-to-RegSeqRat handoff.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite prefix stream nonescape]
\label{thm:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-nonescape}
Every Real or Completion-facing use of an accepted $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$ carrier remains downstream of the displayed prefix window and the RegSeqRat handoff. \end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.FinitePrefixStreamUp.FinitePrefixStreamCarrier\_nonescape}
\begin{proof}

Apply \autoref{thm:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-window-readback}. The consumer obtains only prefix-bounded regular rational readback, and the structural rows keep the same prefix route visible.

\end{proof}
