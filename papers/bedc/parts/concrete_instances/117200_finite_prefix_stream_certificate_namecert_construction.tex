Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$ packet records the finite prefix of a countable name inspected at a displayed unary depth. It sits between the countable-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert} and the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, while keeping its dyadic tolerance row anchored in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, its terminal real-facing seal anchored in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and its completion-facing use anchored in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-regular-cauchy-completion-namecert}. The diagonal-selection corridor of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-diagonal-selection-namecert} consumes these prefix windows before selecting a regular Cauchy diagonal readback, so the diagonal handoff is fed by displayed finite observations rather than by a detached tail selector. The packet gives the Real and Completion route a named finite observation-prefix object, not quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Finite prefix stream certificate carrier]
\label{def:finite-prefix-stream-certificate-carrier}
A $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(k,W,D,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $k$ is the unary depth row. It fixes the largest inspected index and contains only the finite successor spine needed to reach that depth.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row. It displays exactly the stream-name observations at indices $0,\ldots,k$ and no infinite representative.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row. It records the finite dyadic radii, slack bounds, and comparison budgets used by the displayed windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row. It reads the same finite windows and dyadic budgets as regular rational data before any real-facing seal can be consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the unary-depth, stream-window, dyadic, regular-rational, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite prefix request through $k,W,D,R,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FinitePrefixStreamUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FinitePrefixStreamUp}$ row naming exactly this finite prefix certificate.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $k,W,D,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}
