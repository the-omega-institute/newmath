It sits beside the tagged finite-sum surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannsum-namecert}, the upper and lower Stieltjes-sum boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-darboux-stieltjes-sum-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Riemann--Stieltjes sum carrier]
\label{def:riemann-stieltjes-sum-carrier}
A $\RiemannStieltjesSumUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(T,F,G,L,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the tagged partition row, exposing ordered cells, endpoints, tags, and tag-containment witnesses through the finite $\RiemannSumUp$ surface.
\item $F$ is the integrand window row, recording exactly the finitely many displayed integrand reads required at the carried tags.
\item $G$ is the integrator-difference row, recording for each carried cell the displayed endpoint difference of the integrator between the cell endpoints.
\item $L$ is the finite product-sum ledger, pairing each integrand read in $F$ with the matching integrator difference in $G$ and recording the carried arithmetic sum over $T$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, fixing the dyadic budget used to compare the product-sum ledger with later regulated-integral or Darboux--Stieltjes consumers.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the displayed finite product-sum and tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, exposed only after the tagged partition, integrand window, integrator differences, product-sum ledger, dyadic budget, and regular readback are displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the tagged partition, integrand window, integrator differences, product-sum ledger, tolerance row, readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the tagged partition through
$$
T,F,G \longmapsto L \longmapsto D \longmapsto R \longmapsto E
$$
and then through the structural rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RiemannStieltjesSumUp$, $\RiemannSumUp$, $\DarbouxStieltjesSumUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RiemannStieltjesSumUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,F,G,L,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a completed integral, a limit over all refinements, bounded-variation completeness, quotient function equality, a selected tag family outside $T$, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Riemann--Stieltjes sum NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:riemann-stieltjes-sum-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RiemannStieltjesSumUp$ carrier $S=(T,F,G,L,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RiemannStieltjesSumUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the tagged partition, integrand window, integrator-difference row, finite product-sum ledger, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming rows. Every accepted read follows the finite route
$$
T,F,G \longmapsto L \longmapsto D \longmapsto R \longmapsto E
$$
together with $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:riemann-stieltjes-sum-carrier}. Its displayed rows are exactly the tagged partition, integrand reads, integrator differences, finite product-sum ledger, dyadic tolerance, regular readback, Real seal, and structural rows.

The arithmetic route is cellwise. Each summand is admitted only when the tag comes from $T$, the value comes from $F$, and the matching integrator difference comes from $G$ on the same displayed cell.

The ledger $L$ records only the finite product-sum built from those rows. The tolerance row $D$ and readback row $R$ make the numerical comparison visible before the terminal seal $E$ can be consumed.

Transport through $H$, replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$ preserve the same route. Since no carrier coordinate names a completed integral, refinement limit, quotient function, hidden tag selector, countable-choice principle, or ambient completeness theorem, the listed rows exhaust the obligation surface.
\end{proof}

The seal exports only the displayed finite Riemann--Stieltjes sum route, not an integral value, a refinement limit, bounded-variation completeness, quotient function equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Therefore the terminal seal is only a Real-facing seal for the displayed finite sum route. All completed integration, refinement-limit, quotient-function, choice, and ambient-completeness readings remain outside the packet.
