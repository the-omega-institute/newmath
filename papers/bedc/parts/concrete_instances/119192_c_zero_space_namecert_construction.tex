\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CZeroSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-c-zero-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Banach-space treatments of $c_0$, the $\CZeroSpaceUp$ packet presents a real-valued sequence with an explicit tail-to-zero modulus as finite NameCert data rather than as a quotient subspace of a completed sequence algebra. It is grounded in the sequence carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-sequence-space-namecert}, the bounded-sequence surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-bounded-sequence-namecert}, the regular-rate route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-sequence-space-namecert}, the finite stream naming of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the complete-metric boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-complete-metric-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[CZeroSpace carrier]
\label{def:c-zero-space-carrier}
A $\CZeroSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(S,R,B,T,K,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\RealSequenceSpaceUp$ source row, read only through the finite $\StreamNameUp$ schedule naming inspected sequence windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ real-coordinate seal, supplying regular rational readback for every coordinate requested by $S$.
\item $B$ is the $\RealBoundedSequenceUp$ boundedness witness, displayed before any norm-facing or complete-metric consumer may read the packet.
\item $T$ is the dyadic tail modulus: for each displayed tolerance request, $T$ gives a finite tail window after which the inspected coordinates are forced inside that tolerance.
\item $K$ is the $\RegularCauchySequenceSpaceUp$ readback row, carrying the same tail ledger as a regular-rate sequence-space route.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the coordinate readback, boundedness witness, and tail modulus.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the sequence source, real-coordinate seal, boundedness witness, tail modulus, regular Cauchy readback, dyadic ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from each finite coordinate, boundedness, tail, and complete-metric request through $S,R,B,T,K,D$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CZeroSpaceUp$, $\RealSequenceSpaceUp$, $\RealBoundedSequenceUp$, $\RegularCauchySequenceSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BishopCompleteMetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CZeroSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for arbitrary choice of subsequences, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, host real equality, selected limits, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CZeroSpace tail-modulus obligation]
\label{thm:c-zero-space-tail-modulus-obligation}
For every accepted $\CZeroSpaceUp$ carrier $Z=(S,R,B,T,K,D,H,C,P,N)$, the tail-to-zero claim is exhausted by the displayed $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ coordinate readback, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, $\RealBoundedSequenceUp$ boundedness witness, and $\RegularCauchySequenceSpaceUp$ rate row. In particular, every tail-zero consumer must first read the finite route
$$
S \longrightarrow R \longrightarrow D \longrightarrow T \longrightarrow K
$$
with $B$ displayed as the boundedness side condition, and no consumer may replace that route by arbitrary choice of subsequences, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, selected limits, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:c-zero-space-carrier}. The sequence source is the $\RealSequenceSpaceUp$ row $S$, the coordinate readback is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$, and the tolerance information for each coordinate and each tail request is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$.

The displayed tail modulus $T$ is then read over those same finite windows. The boundedness witness $B$ records the norm-facing side condition, while the regular Cauchy readback $K$ exposes the tail ledger to the regular-rate route described in \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-sequence-space-tail-zero-route}.

Finally use the sequence-space subspace route of \autoref{thm:real-sequence-space-tail-zero-subspace}. It identifies the tail-zero surface as an extra displayed ledger over the existing real-sequence route, so the excluded host-side objects never become classifier coordinates of $Z$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CZeroSpace metric seal]
\label{thm:c-zero-space-metric-seal}
Every $\BishopCompleteMetricUp$ or norm-facing consumer of an accepted $\CZeroSpaceUp$ carrier reads the packet through the bounded real sequence row $B$, the tail modulus $T$, and the regular Cauchy route $K$ after the sequence source, coordinate seal, and dyadic ledger have been displayed. The exported metric surface is therefore the finite packet
$$
S,R,B,T,K,D,H,C,P,N
$$
rather than an ambient complete subspace obtained from quotient stream equality, trichotomy, selected limits, arbitrary subsequence choice, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a complete-metric or norm-facing read. The carrier exposes no such read before $S,R,D$ name the inspected real-valued coordinates and before $B$ supplies the boundedness witness.

The tail-to-zero data are supplied by $T$ and replayed through $K$. By \autoref{thm:c-zero-space-tail-modulus-obligation}, the tail read is a dyadic ledger obligation over the displayed real-sequence route, and by \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-sequence-space-tail-zero-route} the same obligation remains visible to regular-rate consumers.

The complete-metric boundary can therefore inspect only the rows $S,R,B,T,K,D,H,C,P,N$. Since neither the definition nor the preceding obligation theorem contains quotient stream equality, trichotomy, selected limits, arbitrary subsequence choice, host real equality, or ambient Real completeness, the metric seal exports none of those objects.
\end{proof}
