\begin{definition}[ControllabilityRank carrier]
\label{def:controllability-rank-carrier}
A $\ControllabilityRankUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(X,U,A,B,W,V,L,R,O,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the finite state $\VecSpaceUp$ row and $U$ is the finite input $\VecSpaceUp$ row.
\item $A$ is the transition $\MatrixUp$ row and $B$ is the input $\MatrixUp$ row, both read as $\LinearMapUp$ data when the reachability columns are generated.
\item $W$ is the finite rank-window row listing the displayed columns $B,AB,\ldots,A^{n-1}B$ and the row-reduction comparisons used to test whether the window spans the displayed state dimension.
\item $V$ is the vector-space dimension row against which the rank window is measured.
\item $L$ is the $\LinearMapUp$ reachability-iterate ledger recording each multiplication and column append route.
\item $R$ is the $\ControlControllabilityUp$ reachability ledger consumed by downstream controllability readers.
\item $O$ is the $\ControlObservabilityUp$ dual-window row available to dual tests and observability-facing consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for state rows, input rows, matrix rows, reachability iterates, rank windows, dimension rows, dual windows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the plant rows through the reachability iterates into the rank-window certificate.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ControllabilityRankUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\ControlControllabilityUp$, $\ControlObservabilityUp$, $\HautusTestUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ControllabilityRankUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only this finite rank-window surface. It has no coordinate for a host rank theorem detached from $W,V,L,R,O,H,C,P,N$, and it has no quotient subspace through which reachability could be identified externally.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ControllabilityRank NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:controllability-rank-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ControllabilityRankUp$ carrier
$$
K=(X,U,A,B,W,V,L,R,O,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ControllabilityRankUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed packet, finite rank-window exactness for $W$, matrix and vector-space readback through $A,B,X,U,V$, reachability-ledger exactness through $L$ and $R$, dual-window exposure through $O$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. The certificate never escapes to a host rank predicate, a quotient state subspace, a Kalman decomposition, a spectral test outside the Hautus consumer row, or an infinite-dimensional controllability claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:controllability-rank-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the finite state and input rows, transition and input matrices, rank-window row, dimension row, reachability iterates, controllability ledger, dual observability window, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

Finite rank-window exactness is the row $W$ measured against $V$. The matrix and vector-space readback rows $A,B,X,U,V$ are already visible before $W$ can be consumed, and the reachability-iterate ledger $L$ records how each displayed column is generated.

The downstream control surface is $R,O$ together with the $\HautusTestUp$ provenance named by $P$. A controllability consumer reads $R$, an observability or dual-test consumer reads $O$, and a Hautus consumer may inspect the same finite rank-window data without receiving an external spectral decomposition.

Finally transport and replay are exactly $H$ and $C$. Since the carrier contains no host rank predicate, quotient state subspace, Kalman decomposition object, or infinite-dimensional theorem, the local NameCert obligations are exhausted by the finite packet $K$.
\end{proof}
