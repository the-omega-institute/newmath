\begin{definition}[Pointwise bounded Real-family carrier]
\label{def:pointwise-bounded-real-family-carrier}
A $\PointwiseBoundedRealFamilyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(I,S,R,E,B,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the index-window ledger. It lists the finite family positions currently inspected and contains no ambient set of all indices.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation ledger. For each displayed index in $I$, it records the finite stream windows read from the corresponding component.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row attached to the observed stream windows, limited to the precisions named in $I$ and $S$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for the components whose stream and regular rational rows have already been displayed.
\item $B$ is the per-index dyadic bound witness row. Each entry pairs one inspected index with a dyadic upper-bound witness for the corresponding displayed $\RealUp$ seal.
\item $U$ is the finite supremum ledger. It displays the upper-bound rows obtained from the finite list of dyadic witnesses in $B$, using $\LocatedSupremumUp$ only as a local finite-row consumer.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the index ledger, stream observations, regular handoffs, Real seals, dyadic bound witnesses, finite supremum rows, continuation rows, provenance rows, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from $I$ to $S$, from $S$ to $R$, from $R$ to $E$, from $E$ to $B$, and from the finite dyadic witness list $B$ to the finite upper-bound ledger $U$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PointwiseBoundedRealFamilyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\LocatedSupremumUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PointwiseBoundedRealFamilyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for infinite families, open covers, choice-selected subsequences, a host supremum, quotient-stream equality, or completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Pointwise bounded Real-family finite supremum obligation]
\label{thm:pointwise-bounded-real-family-finite-supremum-obligation}
Every accepted $\PointwiseBoundedRealFamilyUp$ packet exposes its bound only through displayed finite supremum rows over the inspected index window. The consumer may read the route
$$
I,S,R,E,B\longmapsto U
$$
and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$, but it receives no host supremum, no choice-selected index family, and no completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:pointwise-bounded-real-family-carrier}. The visible rows are the finite index window $I$, the $\StreamNameUp$ observations $S$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoffs $R$, the $\RealUp$ seals $E$, the dyadic bound witnesses $B$, the finite upper-bound ledger $U$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Induct over the displayed index-window ledger $I$. The empty window exposes only the displayed empty upper-bound row. In the successor step, the next component is read through its $\StreamNameUp$ observation row, then through its $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff, and only then through its $\RealUp$ seal.

For each inspected index, consume the corresponding dyadic witness in $B$. The componentwise $\hsame$ row preserves the witness under transport, and the $\Cont$ row replays the handoff without adding an ambient supremum coordinate.

Finally, read $\LocatedSupremumUp$ only at the finite upper-bound ledger $U$. This ledger displays the finite supremum rows already generated from the inspected dyadic witnesses, so the exported bound is a finite packet row rather than a host supremum, a choice-selected family, or a consequence of $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
