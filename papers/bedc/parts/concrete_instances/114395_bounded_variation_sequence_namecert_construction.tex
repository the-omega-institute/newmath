Following Bishop and Bridges, a sequence of bounded variation is a regular real sequence whose finite variation ledgers are uniformly controlled by displayed rational data. The $\BoundedVariationSequenceUp$ packet records this classical constructive object on the L10 Real and Completion route. It sits after the stream-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational sequence interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic rational core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the terminal real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the Cauchy route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-namecert}. The packet names finite sequence-variation control without importing host sequence equality, selected limits, countable choice, completed sequence spaces, or full real completeness.

\begin{definition}[BoundedVariationSequence carrier]
\label{def:bounded-variation-sequence-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ row for the observation stream and finite prefix windows.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row reading each displayed sequence value by regular rational names.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row for adjacent dyadic difference observations and finite variation sums.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for the accepted value names and variation-budget name.
\item $C$ is the $\CauchyUp$ route row exposed only after the finite variation ledger supplies a tail-control modulus.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for stream windows, regular rational reads, dyadic difference ledgers, Real seals, Cauchy handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $K$ stores $\Cont$ replay from prefix windows to adjacent differences, from finite variation sums to tail budgets, and from tail budgets to the Cauchy consumer route.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedVariationSequenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares packets only through $S,Q,D,R,C,H,K,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host sequence equality, a selected limiting real, quotient sequence spaces, countable choice, or full $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[BoundedVariationSequence variation ledger]
\label{thm:bounded-variation-sequence-variation-ledger}
For every accepted $\BoundedVariationSequenceUp$ carrier $V=(S,Q,D,R,C,H,K,P,N)$ and every displayed finite prefix window, the public variation read factors through
$$
\StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The row $D$ accounts for adjacent dyadic differences and finite variation sums, while $R$ seals only the displayed regular rational values and their variation-budget read. No consumer receives a supremum over all partitions, host sequence equality, selected limit, or ambient completed sequence space from this ledger.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bounded-variation-sequence-carrier}. A variation request first chooses a finite prefix window from $S$ and then reads the corresponding value names through the regular rational row $Q$.

The dyadic row $D$ is the only place where adjacent differences and finite variation sums are recorded. Its data are finite: each read is tied to the displayed prefix and to the regular names already exposed by $Q$.

The Real row $R$ seals those displayed value names and the displayed variation-budget name. Because the carrier has no coordinate for a supremum over all prefixes, host sequence equality, quotient sequence space, selected limit, or full real completeness, the public variation ledger is exactly the finite route through $S,Q,D,R$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BoundedVariationSequence Cauchy modulus route]
\label{thm:bounded-variation-sequence-cauchy-modulus-route}
Let $V=(S,Q,D,R,C,H,K,P,N)$ be an accepted $\BoundedVariationSequenceUp$ carrier. Any downstream $\CauchyUp$ or completion consumer that cites a modulus from $V$ must read the finite variation ledger $S,Q,D,R$ first and then consume the displayed handoff row $C$ with the same structural rows $H,K,P,N$. The route exports a tail-control obligation, not a selected limit or quotient equality of sequences.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the variation ledger of \autoref{thm:bounded-variation-sequence-variation-ledger}. It confines every finite variation read to stream windows, regular rational names, dyadic adjacent-difference observations, and the Real seal.

The Cauchy row $C$ is downstream of that ledger. A consumer may read $C$ only after the finite variation sums and tail-budget comparisons have been replayed through $K$ and transported through $H$.

The rows $P$ and $N$ source and name the same route for downstream completion-facing consumers. Since no coordinate of the carrier is a selected limit, quotient sequence equality, completed sequence-space object, countable-choice principle, or full real-completeness row, the Cauchy handoff is a modulus route over displayed finite data.
\end{proof}
