\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\EffectiveCompactnessUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-effectivecompactness-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive compactness, the $\EffectiveCompactnessUp$ packet records compactness by displayed finite $\varepsilon$-nets and located metric coverage rather than by an ambient open-cover principle. It sits over the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the total-bounded schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and the rational tolerance rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}. The packet isolates the finite-net compactness object used by the compact-uniform-continuity route of \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity}: a consumer receives located finite nets, rational tolerance bookkeeping, and a tolerance-only $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff, not a selected open subcover, quotient point equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Effective compactness carrier]
\label{def:effective-compactness-carrier}
An $\EffectiveCompactnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(M,T,F,L,R,Q,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, exposing only the distance comparisons and located balls admitted by the named metric carrier.
\item $T$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ schedule row, assigning a finite-net request to each positive rational tolerance.
\item $F$ is the finite center bundle returned by the schedule for the current tolerance request.
\item $L$ is the located coverage row, recording for each inspected point the displayed center and metric comparison witnessing membership in the requested ball.
\item $R$ is the $\RatUp$ tolerance ledger, carrying positivity, halving, slack, and comparison rows used by the finite-net read.
\item $Q$ is the compact metric provenance row tying $M,T,F,L,R$ to the displayed $\CompactMetricUp$ source.
\item $U$ is the tolerance-only $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row consumed by compact-uniform-continuity arguments after the finite-net coverage has been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,T,F,L,R,Q,U$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through the finite-net schedule, located coverage row, rational comparison ledger, and uniform-modulus handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EffectiveCompactnessUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RatUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EffectiveCompactnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover compactness, selected subcovers outside $F$, quotient equality of points, countable choice, host completeness, or an external modulus selector.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Effective compactness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:effective-compactness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EffectiveCompactnessUp$ carrier
$$
E=(M,T,F,L,R,Q,U,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\EffectiveCompactnessUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric-source exposure, total-bounded schedule exposure, finite center-bundle admission, located coverage readback, rational tolerance comparison, compact-metric provenance, tolerance-only uniform-modulus handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every compactness-facing read must pass through the displayed finite-net rows $T,F,L,R$ before it may consume the handoff row $U$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:effective-compactness-carrier}. Its visible rows are exactly the metric source, total-bounded schedule, finite center bundle, located coverage row, rational tolerance ledger, compact-metric provenance, uniform-modulus handoff, and structural rows.

Finite-net exposure is supplied by $T,F,L,R$. The schedule row $T$ receives the rational tolerance, the finite bundle $F$ lists the centers, the located row $L$ records coverage, and the rational ledger $R$ records the positive slack and comparisons used by that read.

The compact source and downstream handoff are confined to $Q$ and $U$. The row $Q$ ties the read to the compact metric source, and $U$ exposes only the tolerance-only modulus output available after the finite-net rows have been displayed.

Transport, replay, provenance, and naming are supplied by $H,C,P,N$. Since none of the excluded open-cover, choice, quotient, completeness, or external-selector objects occurs in the packet, the displayed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Effective compactness finite-net modulus]
\label{thm:effective-compactness-finite-net-modulus}
Every compact-uniform-continuity consumer of an accepted $\EffectiveCompactnessUp$ carrier obtains its tolerance-only $\UniformModulusUp$ row through the finite route
$$
\MetricUp \longmapsto \TotallyBoundedUp \longmapsto \CompactMetricUp \longmapsto \RatUp \longmapsto \UniformModulusUp .
$$
Concretely, the consumer first reads the metric source row $M$, the finite-net schedule $T$, the center bundle $F$, the located coverage row $L$, the rational tolerance ledger $R$, and the compact-metric provenance row $Q$ before it may use the modulus handoff $U$. The route exports no open-cover compactness, selected subcover outside the bundle, quotient point equality, countable choice, host completeness, or external modulus selector.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $E=(M,T,F,L,R,Q,U,H,C,P,N)$. The only metric-facing source is $M$, and the only finite-net schedule is $T$.

Read the finite-net data. The center bundle $F$ is produced for the requested tolerance, the located coverage row $L$ records the displayed metric comparisons, and $R$ carries the rational slack needed to compare the requested tolerance with the finite coverage radius.

The compact-metric provenance row $Q$ ties those rows to the compact metric source used in the route of \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity}. Only after that finite coverage read is displayed does the handoff row $U$ expose the tolerance-only uniform-modulus datum.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same packet. Because the carrier contains no open-cover, selected-subcover, quotient, countable-choice, completeness, or external-selector coordinate, the finite-net route is the whole modulus-facing export.
\end{proof}
