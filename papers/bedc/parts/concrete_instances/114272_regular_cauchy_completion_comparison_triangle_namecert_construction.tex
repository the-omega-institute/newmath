\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchyCompletionComparisonTriangle NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-completion-comparison-triangle-namecert-obligations}
The local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyCompletionComparisonTriangleUp}$ surface consists of a finite route-exhaustion statement: every accepted triangle packet first exposes the $\CauchyModulusUp$ threshold ledger, then the $\RegularCauchyLimitModulusUp$ diagonal selector, then the $\CauchyCompletionComparisonUp$ and $\CompletionComparisonUp$ comparison legs, and only then the $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ consumer rows. The same componentwise $\hsame$, displayed $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ rows transport the whole triangle.

Consequently a Real or Completion consumer cannot bypass the regular-Cauchy leg by reading $E$ as a host real, cannot bypass the completion-comparison leg by treating $C$ as quotient stream equality, and cannot bypass the dyadic leg by replacing $D$ with an ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-completion-comparison-triangle-carrier}. The rows $M,L,C,G,S,R,D,E,H,Q,P,N$ are all coordinates of one finite packet, so any consumer route must begin by choosing one of those displayed coordinates.

The regular-Cauchy leg is exhausted by $M$, $L$, $S$, $R$, and $D$. The threshold ledger supplies the modulus row, the limit-modulus row supplies the diagonal selector, the stream row supplies the finite window, the regular rational row supplies readback, and the dyadic row records the tolerance arithmetic.

The completion leg is exhausted by $C$ and $G$. These rows compare the same displayed observation against the Cauchy-completion and completion-comparison interfaces; they do not add a quotient representative or a selected completion point.

Finally $E$ is downstream of those rows. The transport, replay, provenance, and naming coordinates $H,Q,P,N$ preserve the same triangle, so route exhaustion leaves no coordinate for quotient stream equality, countable choice, trichotomy, host selected limits, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
