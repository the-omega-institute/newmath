\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ContinuityModulusLatticeUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-continuity-modulus-lattice-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on compact metric continuity and constructive moduli, the $\ContinuityModulusLatticeUp$ packet records the finite order surface on which displayed local moduli can be compared, combined by finite meet, and handed to a uniform-modulus consumer. It sits after the continuous-map rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the uniform-modulus consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, the compact metric finite-net source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the metric source rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and rational tolerance data from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}. The packet names a finite lattice of modulus rows; it is not open-cover compactness, a selected modulus functional, quotient equality of functions, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Continuity modulus lattice carrier]
\label{def:continuity-modulus-lattice-carrier}
A $\ContinuityModulusLatticeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(X,D,F,U,R,O,M,J,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the compact metric source row, read through $\CompactMetricUp$ over the same $\MetricSpaceUp$ carrier.
\item $D$ is the metric-distance comparison row used to compare source balls and target tolerances.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row whose pointwise modulus histories are being ordered.
\item $U$ is the downstream $\UniformModulusUp$ row that consumes a tolerance-only modulus history.
\item $R$ is the $\RatUp$ tolerance and lower-bound comparison row.
\item $O$ is the finite preorder row on displayed modulus histories.
\item $M$ is the finite meet row, recording the common lower precision history for finitely many comparable modulus rows.
\item $J$ is the join-readback row used only to report compatibility of already displayed modulus histories.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,D,F,U,R,O,M,J$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through compact-source, metric, continuous-map, rational, order, meet, and uniform-modulus reads.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ContinuityModulusLatticeUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\RatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ContinuityModulusLatticeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,D,F,U,R,O,M,J,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected moduli, quotient equality, trichotomy, countable choice, host function equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Continuity modulus lattice NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:continuity-modulus-lattice-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ContinuityModulusLatticeUp$ carrier
$$
L=(X,D,F,U,R,O,M,J,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ContinuityModulusLatticeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, compact-metric source exposure through $X,D$, continuous-map modulus exposure through $F$, rational tolerance comparison through $R$, finite preorder readback through $O$, finite meet readback through $M$, compatibility readback through $J$, uniform-modulus handoff through $U$, and structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:continuity-modulus-lattice-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the compact metric source, metric comparison row, continuous-map row, uniform-modulus consumer row, rational tolerance row, finite order rows, and structural rows.

The order obligations are read through $O$ and $R$. A comparison between two modulus histories must expose the rational tolerance comparisons used by the preorder row, so the packet cannot replace finite order by an ambient order theorem.

The meet obligations are read through $M$. The meet row records only a finite common lower precision history for already displayed modulus histories, and $J$ reports compatibility without adding an independent selector.

Finally $U,H,C,P,N$ keep the handoff local. The uniform-modulus consumer receives only the displayed finite meet route, while the structural rows transport, replay, source, and name the same finite data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Continuity modulus lattice finite-meet stability]
\label{thm:continuity-modulus-lattice-finite-meet-stability}
Every finite meet read exported by an accepted $\ContinuityModulusLatticeUp$ packet remains stable under $\hsame$ transport of the displayed rows $X,D,F,U,R,O,M,J,H,C,P,N$. A downstream compact-uniform or completion-modulus consumer may cite the meet as a finite order object over continuous-map and rational tolerance rows, but it may not read the meet as open-cover compactness, a selected global modulus, quotient equality, trichotomy, countable choice, host function equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows. The finite meet row $M$ is stored together with the rational comparison row $R$, the preorder row $O$, the compatibility row $J$, and the structural transport row $H$.

Transport first acts componentwise on $X,D,F,U,R,O,M,J$. Since $M$ is not a detached selector, a transported meet read must carry along the same compact-source, metric, continuous-map, rational, order, and compatibility rows.

The replay and provenance rows $C,P,N$ then repack the transported data as the same local NameCert surface. No refused object is introduced by this repacking, so finite-meet stability is exactly stability of the displayed carrier rows.
\end{proof}
