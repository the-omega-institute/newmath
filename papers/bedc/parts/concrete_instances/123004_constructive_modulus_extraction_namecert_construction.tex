\begin{definition}[Constructive modulus extraction carrier]
\label{def:constructive-modulus-extraction-carrier}
A $\ConstructiveModulusExtractionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(P,Q,C,W,D,R,U,B,E,H,K,S,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the proof-data row, recording the displayed constructive derivation whose modulus is being mined.
\item $Q$ is the tolerance-request row received from the Real or Completion consumer.
\item $C$ is the $\CauchyModulusUp$ discipline row, binding $Q$ to the Cauchy threshold shape demanded by the consumer.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row inspected by the extraction.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger used to compare the requested precision with the inspected finite windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from $W$ under the ledger $D$.
\item $U$ is the $\ComputableUniformContinuityUp$ row whenever the proof data supplies a uniform-continuity route for the same request.
\item $B$ is the $\BarRecursiveModulusUp$ row whenever the proof data supplies a bar-recursive modulus route for the same request.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ or Completion-facing seal, available only after $P,Q,C,W,D,R$ and the applicable $U$ or $B$ route have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for proof data, tolerance requests, Cauchy thresholds, stream windows, dyadic ledgers, regular readback, uniform-continuity rows, bar-recursive rows, terminal seals, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from proof data and tolerance request through the finite modulus rows to the terminal seal.
\item $S$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ConstructiveModulusExtractionUp$, $\CauchyModulusUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\ComputableUniformContinuityUp$, $\BarRecursiveModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveModulusExtractionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $P,Q,C,W,D,R,U,B,E,H,K,S,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected limit, quotient real equality, countable choice, an oracle modulus, host proof irrelevance, ambient completeness, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive modulus extraction NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:constructive-modulus-extraction-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ConstructiveModulusExtractionUp$ carrier
$$
M=(P,Q,C,W,D,R,U,B,E,H,K,S,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveModulusExtractionUp}$ surface consists exactly of proof-data admission through $P$, tolerance-request admission through $Q$, Cauchy-modulus discipline through $C$, finite $\StreamNameUp$ window exposure through $W$, dyadic tolerance exposure through $D$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, computable-uniform-continuity exposure through $U$ when present, bar-recursive modulus exposure through $B$ when present, terminal sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $K$, provenance through $S$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:constructive-modulus-extraction-carrier}. The rows $P,Q,C,W,D,R$ are the common finite spine: proof data is read under a concrete tolerance request, the Cauchy-modulus discipline gives the threshold shape, the stream row fixes the inspected windows, the dyadic ledger supplies comparisons, and the regular rational row records the visible readback.

The optional analytic routes are still displayed rows. A computable-uniform-continuity extraction must pass through $U$, while a bar-recursive extraction must pass through $B$; neither route can be replaced by an external modulus oracle because the carrier has no such coordinate.

The terminal row $E$ is downstream of the displayed extraction route. The support rows $H,K,S,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite data, so the listed rows exhaust the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveModulusExtractionUp}$ surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive modulus extraction L10 handoff]
\label{thm:constructive-modulus-extraction-l10-handoff}
Every L10 Real or Completion consumer of an accepted $\ConstructiveModulusExtractionUp$ packet factors its modulus read through the finite route
$$

$$
Thus Real-completeness and Completion-facing modulus claims are consumed only after displayed proof data, a tolerance request, Cauchy threshold discipline, stream windows, dyadic comparison data, regular rational readback, and the applicable uniform-continuity or bar-recursive extraction row have been exposed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a consumer request $Q$. The proof-data row $P$ is not read abstractly: it is paired with the Cauchy-modulus discipline $C$ so the requested threshold shape is already finite.

Next the extraction opens the observable rows. The $\StreamNameUp$ row $W$ fixes the finite windows, the dyadic ledger $D$ supplies the comparison budget, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ records the regular rational readback on those windows.

The route then takes the displayed extraction branch. If the proof data supplies computable uniform continuity, the row $U$ carries that modulus handoff; if it supplies a bar-recursive route, the row $B$ carries that handoff. In both cases the terminal seal $E$ is reached only after the branch row is present.

The support rows $H,K,S,N$ preserve the same route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier has no coordinate for selected limits, quotient equality, countable choice, oracle moduli, host proof irrelevance, or ambient completeness, the L10 handoff exports only the displayed finite extraction data.
\end{proof}
