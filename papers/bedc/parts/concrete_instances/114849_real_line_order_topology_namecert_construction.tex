\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RealLineOrderTopologyUp$}

Following Kelley and Bishop--Bridges, the $\RealLineOrderTopologyUp$ packet names the finite open-interval topology surface induced by the displayed $\RealUp$ order classifier. It consumes the endpoint and seal rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the endpoint-cell boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}. The chapter records only a finite open-interval basis boundary; it does not assert trichotomy, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, selected points, or completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Real line order-topology carrier]
\label{def:real-line-order-topology-carrier}
A $\RealLineOrderTopologyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T_{\Real}=(E,O,S,Q,D,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the finite endpoint row. Each endpoint is read as a $\RealUp$ order-classifier boundary together with the adjacent $\LocatedIntervalUp$ cell in which the endpoint is inspected.
\item $O$ is the open-interval probe row. A probe records a finite request of the form $(a,b,w)$, where $a$ and $b$ are displayed endpoint names and $w$ is the inspected finite window.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row attached to every endpoint and interval probe.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the retained endpoint windows and probe windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row used to compare endpoint windows and to bound the open-interval probe.
\item $A$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $E,O,S,Q,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoint rows, open-interval probes, windows, readbacks, dyadic tolerances, the Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an endpoint request through the finite open-interval probe, the window row, the readback row, the dyadic tolerance row, and the Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealLineOrderTopologyUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealLineOrderTopologyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $E,O,S,Q,D,A,H,C,P,N$. The carrier has no coordinate for trichotomy, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, selected points, host real equality, or completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real line order-topology NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-line-order-topology-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\RealLineOrderTopologyUp$ carrier $T_{\Real}=(E,O,S,Q,D,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealLineOrderTopologyUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item endpoint admission for the displayed $\RealUp$ order-classifier endpoints and the adjacent $\LocatedIntervalUp$ cells;
\item open-interval probe admission for the finite requests in $O$;
\item finite-window and readback admission through $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$;
\item dyadic tolerance admission through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ before any endpoint comparison is replayed;
\item Real-seal admission through $A$ after the endpoint, probe, window, readback, and tolerance rows have been displayed;
\item structural preservation by $H,C,P,N$;
\item non-escape for trichotomy, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, selected points, host real equality, and $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No local obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:real-line-order-topology-carrier}. The finite packet displays exactly the endpoint row, open-interval probe row, window row, readback row, dyadic tolerance row, Real seal, and structural rows.

The interval-topology part is read next. An open-interval probe in $O$ is admitted only with endpoint data from $E$, and those endpoint rows are the same Real and located-interval rows cited in the carrier. Thus the topology surface is generated by displayed open intervals rather than by an ambient open-set lattice.

The numerical part is finite. The rows $S,Q,D$ provide inspected windows, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance bounds before $A$ can be read as a Real seal.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the excluded principles have no carrier coordinate, the local certificate cannot export them as obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real line open-interval basis]
\label{thm:real-line-order-topology-open-interval-basis}
Every accepted $\RealLineOrderTopologyUp$ packet presents its topology-facing read as a finite open-interval basis generated from the rows
$$
E,O,S,Q,D,A,H,C,P,N .
$$
A downstream $\MetricSpaceUp$ or $\ContinuousMapUp$ consumer may inspect the basis only by first reading the displayed $\RealUp$ endpoints, $\LocatedIntervalUp$ cell boundary, $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows, and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerances. The basis exports no trichotomy, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, selected point, host real equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-line-order-topology-carrier}. The only topology-facing row is $O$, and it is a finite list of open-interval probes attached to endpoint rows $E$.

Read the endpoint route. The endpoints are sealed as $\RealUp$ rows and inspected through the located interval cells already named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}. They do not arrive as host real points or quotient classes.

Read the finite numerical route. The rows $S,Q,D$ supply windows, regular rational readbacks, and dyadic tolerances for the interval probes before the Real seal $A$ is available.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ keep the same finite route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Therefore any metric-space or continuous-map consumer receives a finite open-interval basis, not a classical topology with the excluded principles attached.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real line order-topology Real-seal nonescape]
\label{thm:real-line-order-topology-real-seal-nonescape}
In every accepted $\RealLineOrderTopologyUp$ carrier $T_{\Real}=(E,O,S,Q,D,A,H,C,P,N)$, the Real seal $A$ is downstream of the endpoint row $E$, the open-interval probe row $O$, the $\StreamNameUp$ window row $S$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $Q$, and the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row $D$. Reading $A$ cannot produce trichotomy, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, selected points, host real equality, or completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier definition. The row $A$ is listed as the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $E,O,S,Q,D$ have been displayed.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:real-line-order-topology-namecert-obligations}. They force endpoint admission, open-interval probe admission, finite-window readback, and dyadic tolerance admission before the Real-seal row is inspected.

The open-interval basis theorem, \autoref{thm:real-line-order-topology-open-interval-basis}, keeps every topology-facing consumer on that same displayed route. Thus a read of $A$ remains attached to finite endpoint, interval, window, readback, and tolerance data.

The carrier has no row for the excluded principles. Consequently the terminal Real seal cannot be used to export trichotomy, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, selected points, host equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
