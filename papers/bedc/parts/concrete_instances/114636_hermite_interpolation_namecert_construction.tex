\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HermiteInterpolationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-hermite-interpolation-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Hermite interpolation, the $\HermiteInterpolationUp$ packet records the finite route from displayed nodes, values, and derivative jets to a polynomial whose formal derivatives match those displayed jets at the same nodes. It refines the node-and-value surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lagrange-interpolation-namecert}, consumes the coefficient-list carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-namecert}, and routes Real-facing reads through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}. It is also separate from the orthogonal-family recurrence packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hermite-polynomial-namecert}: the present certificate names interpolation jets, not the Hermite polynomial family.

The BEDC carrier is finite. It stores the node list, the per-node jet-height list, the displayed derivative values, the confluent basis construction, the assembled polynomial, the matching and uniqueness checks, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and a local $\NameCert$ row. Every consumer must see those rows before reading a Hermite interpolant as a Real-facing polynomial approximation or calculus datum.

\begin{definition}[Hermite interpolation carrier]
\label{def:hermite-interpolation-carrier}
A $\HermiteInterpolationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(X,J,V,D,L,E,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is a nonempty finite node row, with pairwise-distinctness data for the displayed nodes.
\item $J$ is the jet-height row, assigning each node a finite number of formal derivative levels to be matched.
\item $V$ is the displayed jet-value table, recording one scalar value for every pair $(x_i,k)$ with $0\leq k<J_i$.
\item $D$ is the denominator and confluent-limit ledger, routing the nonzero node differences and repeated-node derivative factors through $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal.
\item $L$ is the Lagrange spine row imported from $\LagrangeInterpolationUp$, recording the ordinary node basis before the confluent jet rows are attached.
\item $E$ is the confluent basis row, displaying the finite $\PolynomialUp$ coefficient lists whose formal derivatives realize the Kronecker jet pattern on $X$.
\item $U$ is the assembly, jet-matching, and degree-bounded uniqueness row for the finite sum of displayed jet values times the basis polynomials.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for node, jet-height, jet-value, denominator, Lagrange-spine, basis, assembly, replay, provenance, and local-name rows.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ routes that replay denominator formation, confluent basis construction, polynomial differentiation, jet evaluation, and uniqueness comparison from the same finite packet.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HermiteInterpolationUp$, $\PolynomialUp$, $\LagrangeInterpolationUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HermiteInterpolationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate is
$\mathsf{HermiteInterpolationLedger}(X,J,V,D,L,E,U,H,C,P,N)$. Two accepted packets classify together exactly when the eleven displayed rows are transported by $H$ and replay through the same routes named by $C$ and $P$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hermite interpolation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hermite-interpolation-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\HermiteInterpolationUp$ packet $K=(X,J,V,D,L,E,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HermiteInterpolationUp}$ obligation surface consists exactly of the finite carrier and classifier rows of \autoref{def:hermite-interpolation-carrier}, the node and jet-height rows $X,J$, the jet-value table $V$, the denominator and confluent-limit ledger $D$, the Lagrange spine row $L$, the confluent $\PolynomialUp$ basis rows $E$, the assembly and uniqueness row $U$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and the local naming row $N$. No host differentiability oracle, quotient polynomial equality, infinite jet family, selected Real representative, or ambient completeness datum is exported.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier of \autoref{def:hermite-interpolation-carrier}. The rows $X,J,V$ supply the finite interpolation table: nodes, the number of derivatives requested at each node, and the displayed values for those derivative levels.

The construction rows are $D,L,E,U$. The denominator ledger $D$ supplies the finite nonzero and confluent data, $L$ supplies the ordinary Lagrange spine, $E$ displays the polynomial coefficient lists for the confluent basis, and $U$ assembles the interpolant and records the degree-bounded comparison.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed packet. Since the carrier has no additional coordinate for a host derivative operation, selected Real representative, quotient equality, or completion argument, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hermite interpolation jet matching]
\label{thm:hermite-interpolation-jet-matching}
Every jet-matching read exported by an accepted $\HermiteInterpolationUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
X,J,V,D \longmapsto L \longmapsto E \longmapsto U \longmapsto N .
$$
The route first verifies the distinct node row and the finite jet-height schedule, then builds the confluent basis from the Lagrange spine inside $\PolynomialUp$, evaluates the required formal derivatives at the displayed nodes, assembles the interpolating polynomial, and exposes the local $\NameCert_{\HermiteInterpolationUp}$ row. A consumer cannot bypass this route by appealing to ambient smooth functions, an external divided-difference table, quotient polynomial equality, or a Real-completion selector.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted packet $K=(X,J,V,D,L,E,U,H,C,P,N)$. The rows $X$ and $J$ determine a finite set of jet positions, and $V$ supplies exactly one displayed value for each such position.

Next inspect $D$ and $L$. The denominator and confluent-limit ledger makes repeated-node factors explicit, while the Lagrange spine supplies the ordinary interpolation basis from which the confluent basis is built.

Now use $E$ and $U$. The row $E$ records the finite polynomial coefficient lists whose formal derivatives have the required Kronecker jet pattern, and $U$ forms the finite linear combination using the displayed jet values. The same row records the degree-bounded uniqueness comparison, so a second accepted polynomial with the same jet table has the same displayed coefficient-list read.

Finally transport and replay through $H,C,P,N$. These rows preserve the same node, jet, denominator, basis, assembly, provenance, and local name. Thus every exported jet-matching read follows the displayed finite route and has no coordinate from which an ambient analytic or completion argument could be read.
\end{proof}
