\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ProperMetricGroupActionUp$}

Following Bourbaki and Bridson--Haefliger on group actions on proper metric spaces, the $\ProperMetricGroupActionUp$ packet records a displayed group action by isometries on a proper metric carrier as finite BEDC NameCert data. It consumes the group source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}, the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and the closed-ball compactness discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-propermetric-namecert}. The packet names only action rows, orbit-window rows, compact ball witnesses, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not assert a host quotient orbit space, a global choice of representatives, or a full geometric group theory package.

\begin{definition}[Proper metric group action carrier]
\label{def:proper-metric-group-action-carrier}
A $\ProperMetricGroupActionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(G,M,P,\alpha,I,O,W,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the consumed $\GroupUp$ source row, including the displayed carrier, multiplication, unit, inverse, and classifier rows used to index action words.
\item $M$ is the consumed $\MetricUp$ source row for the acted-on points, distance observations, and metric-law ledger.
\item $P$ is the consumed $\ProperMetricUp$ row, exposing compact closed-ball witnesses for the finite center-radius windows read by the action packet.
\item $\alpha$ is the action endpoint ledger. For each displayed group row $g$ and point row $x$, it records the endpoint row $\alpha(g,x)$ as a carried metric point.
\item $I$ is the isometry ledger, recording that each displayed action endpoint preserves the metric-distance rows inspected by the packet.
\item $O$ is the finite orbit-window ledger, listing the displayed group words and point rows inspected by a downstream consumer.
\item $W$ is the properness and cocompactness window ledger. It records compact closed-ball witnesses from $P$ and finite orbit-window coverage rows for the displayed windows only.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the group, metric, proper-metric, action, isometry, orbit-window, coverage-window, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from group multiplication and inverse rows through action endpoints, isometry comparisons, compact closed-ball witnesses, and finite coverage windows.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ProperMetricGroupActionUp$, $\GroupUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\ProperMetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ProperMetricGroupActionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through the displayed rows $G,M,P,\alpha,I,O,W,H,C,Q,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient orbit-space equality, selected orbit representatives, an ambient isometry group, open-cover compactness, or a global geometric group theory structure.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Proper metric group action NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:proper-metric-group-action-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ProperMetricGroupActionUp$ carrier $A=(G,M,P,\alpha,I,O,W,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ProperMetricGroupActionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, group-source exposure, metric-source exposure, proper-metric closed-ball exposure, action endpoint admission, isometry-ledger admission, finite orbit-window admission, properness and cocompactness window admission, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, local naming, and refusal of quotient orbit spaces, global representative choice, and full geometric group theory.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:proper-metric-group-action-carrier}. Its public rows are exactly $G,M,P,\alpha,I,O,W,H,C,Q,N$, with the group and metric inputs read before any action endpoint is accepted.

The action endpoint and isometry ledgers are finite rows. The endpoint row $\alpha(g,x)$ is inspected only for displayed group rows and point rows, and $I$ compares only the metric-distance observations already exposed by $M$.

The properness and cocompactness boundary is the window ledger $W$. It may read compact closed-ball witnesses from $P$ and the displayed orbit-window rows from $O$, but it has no field that selects orbit representatives or forms a host quotient space.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite surface. Since no other row is present in the carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Proper and cocompact finite-window handoff]
\label{thm:proper-metric-group-action-finite-window-handoff}
Every properness-facing or cocompactness-facing consumer of an accepted $\ProperMetricGroupActionUp$ packet factors through
$$
G,\quad M,\quad P,\quad \alpha,\quad I,\quad O,\quad W,\quad H,\quad C,\quad Q,\quad N .
$$
For each displayed orbit window, the consumer first reads the $\GroupUp$ word rows, the $\MetricUp$ point and distance rows, the $\ProperMetricUp$ closed-ball compactness witness, the action endpoint ledger, and the isometry ledger; only then may it read the finite coverage row in $W$. The handoff therefore supplies finite window data for proper and cocompact action reads, not a quotient orbit space, a selected representative system, or a general geometric group theory theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a displayed orbit window. The group rows in $G$ supply the finite words being tested, and the metric rows in $M$ supply the point and distance observations for the same window.

Read the proper-metric row $P$ next. The compact closed-ball witness used by the window is one of the displayed proper-metric witnesses, so the route is inherited from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-propermetric-namecert} and not from an open-cover compactness principle.

The action ledger $\alpha$ and isometry ledger $I$ then attach the group words to the metric rows. The orbit-window ledger $O$ records which endpoint rows are inspected, and $W$ records the finite properness or cocompactness coverage row attached to that window.

Finally use $H,C,Q,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. None of these rows contains a quotient orbit-space carrier, a representative selector, or a global geometric group theory structure, so the consumer receives only the displayed finite-window handoff.
\end{proof}
