\begin{theorem}[Dini uniform-monotone compact dependency route]
\label{thm:dini-uniform-monotone-compact-monotone-dependency-route}
Every accepted $\DiniUniformMonotoneUp$ consumer must expose the compact finite-window dependency through the $\DiniTheoremUp$ and $\DiniUniformLimitModulusUp$ rows, the monotone-tail dependency through $\LocatedMonotoneConvergenceUp$, and the compact source discipline already cited by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dini-theorem-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dini-uniform-limit-modulus-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert} before the route reaches its $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance row and terminal $\RealUp$ seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project an accepted carrier from \autoref{def:dini-uniform-monotone-carrier}. The compact-facing rows are $D,M,G,W$, while the monotone-tail handoff is $L$ and the tolerance-to-real route is $Q,E,R$.

Apply \autoref{thm:dini-uniform-monotone-namecert-obligations}. Its compact finite-modulus and located-monotone clauses require every uniform-modulus request to read the Dini finite-window surface, the extracted modulus row, and the located monotone-convergence row before a real-facing consumer inspects $R$.

Finally use \autoref{thm:dini-uniform-monotone-sibling-lattice-route}. The sibling lattice fixes the compact, monotone, stream, regular-rational, and real rows in one finite packet, so no accepted route can bypass the compact source discipline or replace the monotone dependency by a host convergence theorem.
\end{proof}
