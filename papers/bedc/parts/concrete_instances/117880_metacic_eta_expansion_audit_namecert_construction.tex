\begin{definition}[MetaCIC eta-expansion audit carrier]
\label{def:metacic-eta-expansion-audit-carrier}
A $\MetaCICEtaExpansionAuditUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(A,B,N,F,O,T,R,V,H,C,P,Q)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the $\MetaCICBetaAuditUp$ source row naming the audited beta
boundary.
\item $B$ is the finite beta-conversion-boundary row over the displayed
$\mathrm{BetaStep}$ and $\mathrm{BetaConv}$ relations.
\item $N$ is the $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$ row naming the bounded
normalization frontier inspected by the packet.
\item $F$ is the $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$ row recording which syntax boundary or
typing route is being audited.
\item $O$ is the $\MetaCICClosedBetaTraceEnvelopeUp$ obstruction-retention row.
\item $T$ is the eta-row ledger, recording the source term, target term, binder
shape, and finite eta-expansion request.
\item $R$ is the replay row that rechecks the eta ledger against the beta
boundary without turning it into a global conversion rule.
\item $V$ is the provenance row tying the packet to the beta audit map and the
normalization frontier.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all displayed rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the eta-row,
beta-boundary, normalization-frontier, obstruction-retention, transport,
replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetaCICEtaExpansionAuditUp$,
$\MetaCICBetaAuditUp$, $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$,
$\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$, $\MetaCICClosedBetaTraceEnvelopeUp$, $\BHist$,
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $Q$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetaCICEtaExpansionAuditUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for unrestricted eta conversion, subject
reduction, confluence, normalization, or host conversion equality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC eta-expansion audit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metacic-eta-expansion-audit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetaCICEtaExpansionAuditUp$ carrier
$$
E=(A,B,N,F,O,T,R,V,H,C,P,Q),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MetaCICEtaExpansionAuditUp}$ surface consists exactly of
beta-audit source exposure, beta-conversion-boundary exposure,
normalization-frontier exposure, critical-path exposure,
obstruction-retention exposure, finite eta-row exposure, replay exposure,
provenance, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay,
$\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-eta-expansion-audit-carrier}. The
source audit row $A$, beta boundary $B$, normalization frontier $N$, critical
path $F$, and closed trace envelope $O$ are displayed before the eta ledger can
be consumed.

Read the eta row locally. The row $T$ names a finite source, target, binder
shape, and eta request, while $R$ replays that request against the displayed
beta boundary.

The obstruction row remains present. Since $O$ is carried with $T$ and $R$, a
consumer cannot erase the boundary cases that separate eta-row auditing from
global conversion equality.

Finally $H,C,P,Q$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly that packet. No
row supplies unrestricted eta conversion, subject reduction, confluence,
normalization, or host conversion equality, so the local certificate is limited
to the finite audit obligations named above.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC eta-expansion audit boundary]
\label{thm:metacic-eta-expansion-audit-boundary}
Every consumer of an accepted $\MetaCICEtaExpansionAuditUp$ packet reads eta
expansion only through finite eta rows, the displayed beta-conversion boundary,
the normalization-frontier row, the critical-path row, the retained obstruction
row, transport, replay, provenance, and local $\NameCert$ data. The packet
does not authorize unrestricted eta conversion, subject reduction, confluence,
normalization, or host conversion equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the beta audit row. It fixes the audited $\mathrm{BetaStep}$ and
$\mathrm{BetaConv}$ boundary before any eta row is inspected.

The normalization frontier and critical path narrow the read to a finite syntax
and typing boundary. The retained obstruction row stays in the packet, so the
consumer must keep the cases where conversion, typing preservation, and
normalization are not globally available.

The eta ledger is therefore a replayable audit row, not a conversion rule. The
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\mathsf{Pkg}$, and $\NameCert$ rows preserve and name that
finite route without adding an external equality principle.

Thus the packet exports only the finite eta/beta audit boundary stated above.
It cannot prove or imply unrestricted eta conversion, subject reduction,
confluence, normalization, or host conversion equality.
\end{proof}
