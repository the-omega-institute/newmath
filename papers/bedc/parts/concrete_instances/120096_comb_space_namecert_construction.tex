\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CombSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-comb-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Steen and Seebach and Engelking, the $\CombSpaceUp$ packet records the topologist's comb as finite BEDC topology and metric boundary data. It sits after the indexed-open surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the distance-law carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. The packet names real-axis base windows, vertical tooth rows, an accumulation-spine row, topology classifier rows, and metric handoff rows without importing a host subspace, quotient point equality, selected infinite teeth, or ambient compactness.

\begin{definition}[CombSpace carrier]
\label{def:comb-space-carrier}
A $\CombSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(A,V,S,T,M,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the real-axis base-window row. It lists the inspected rational intervals on the base segment and records the $\RealUp$ endpoint seals used by those windows.
\item $V$ is the vertical-tooth row. It stores finitely many tooth indices, their rational height windows, and the displayed attachment point on the base axis.
\item $S$ is the accumulation-spine row. It records the finite evidence that teeth with displayed indices approach the spine boundary through the visible $\StreamNameUp$ windows.
\item $T$ is the topology classifier row. It names the basic-neighbourhood tests for base-axis points, tooth points, and spine-near observations through the $\TopologyUp$ carrier.
\item $M$ is the metric handoff row. It records only the finite $\MetricUp$ distance windows that compare displayed base, tooth, and spine observations after their $\RealUp$ readbacks are present.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the base-window, tooth, spine, topology, metric, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$$
A,V,S,T,M \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CombSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CombSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,V,S,T,M,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host subset of the plane, quotient point equality, an infinite selected family of teeth, open-cover compactness, sequential compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CombSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:comb-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CombSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
K=(A,V,S,T,M,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CombSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the real-axis base windows, vertical tooth rows, accumulation-spine rows, topology classifier rows, metric handoff rows, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:comb-space-carrier}. The rows $A,V,S$ display the base axis, the inspected teeth, and the accumulation-spine evidence before any topology or metric consumer can read the packet.

Next read $T$ and $M$. The topology classifier is finite because it only inspects the displayed neighbourhood tests, and the metric handoff is finite because every distance row is tied to the shown real-name and stream-window data.

Finally use $H,C,P,N$. Transport is componentwise, replay follows the displayed rows, provenance names the source packets, and the local NameCert row closes the finite carrier surface. Since no carrier coordinate stores a host plane subset, quotient equality, selected infinite teeth, compactness theorem, or ambient completeness witness, those objects cannot enter the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CombSpace topology-metric boundary]
\label{thm:comb-space-topology-metric-boundary}
Every topology-facing or metric-facing read of an accepted $\CombSpaceUp$ packet factors through
$$
\TopologyUp,\quad
\MetricUp,\quad
\RealUp,\quad
\StreamNameUp
$$
as displayed by $A,V,S,T,M$. The topology consumer receives only basic-neighbourhood classifier rows, and the metric consumer receives only finite distance-window handoffs. Neither consumer receives host point-set equality, a selected infinite tooth family, compactness, sequential compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the base and tooth rows $A,V$. They expose the finite real-axis windows and the inspected vertical teeth, so both topology and metric reads are tied to displayed observations.

Use the spine row $S$ before reading either classifier. The accumulation behaviour is visible only through the finite stream windows recorded in the packet.

The topology row $T$ then supplies neighbourhood tests, while the metric row $M$ supplies distance-window handoffs. Both rows depend on the same displayed base, tooth, spine, real-name, and stream data.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name this route. Because the carrier has no coordinate for host point-set equality, a selected infinite tooth family, compactness, sequential compactness, or ambient completeness, the topology-metric boundary remains exactly the finite one stated above.
\end{proof}
