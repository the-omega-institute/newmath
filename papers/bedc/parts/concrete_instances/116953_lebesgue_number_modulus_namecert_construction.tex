\begin{theorem}[Lebesgue-number modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lebesgue-number-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LebesgueNumberModulusUp$ carrier
$$
L=(K,M,U,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LebesgueNumberModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the compact finite-net source, metric distance row, finite cover-admission ledger, dyadic tolerance ledger, finite StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming row; scale exactness requiring every radius read to pass through $D,S,R,E$; cover exactness requiring every cover read to pass through $U$ after the compact finite-net row $K$; and nonescape from the named rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lebesgue-number-modulus-carrier}. The rows $K,M,U,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$ are exactly the inventory available to an accepted packet.

The compact route first reads the finite-net row $K$ and the metric row $M$. The cover ledger $U$ is then attached to those displayed net cells, so a cover read never becomes an external open-cover compactness principle.

The radius route is finite. The dyadic tolerance row $D$ fixes the mesh and slack comparisons, $S$ lists the inspected windows, $R$ performs regular rational readback, and $E$ seals the comparison as a $\RealUp$ row.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no additional carrier coordinate is present, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

\end{proof}
