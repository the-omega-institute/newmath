\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CoreCompactUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-core-compact-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Isbell, Johnstone, and constructive locale practice, the $\CoreCompactUp$ packet records a core-compact neighbourhood relation as finite BEDC data. It sits between the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the local compact-neighbourhood certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locallycompact-namecert}, the compact metric witness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, and the located compact handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedcompact-namecert}. The packet names one displayed way-below neighbourhood route and its compact witness support; it does not assert open-cover compactness, a host basis selector, countable choice, quotient opens, or ambient Real completeness.

\begin{definition}[CoreCompact carrier]
\label{def:core-compact-carrier}
A $\CoreCompactUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(X,U,V,W,K,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row whose opens and incidence data are already admitted as finite BEDC carrier rows.
\item $U$ is the inspected open-neighbourhood row requested by a consumer.
\item $V$ is the displayed core row, read as a neighbourhood that is way below $U$ in the finite inspection order carried by the packet.
\item $W$ is the way-below witness row, recording the finite containment and directed-cover test by which the packet justifies $V \ll U$ for the displayed request.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ or compact-window witness attached to the displayed core neighbourhood when a metric compact witness is available.
\item $L$ is the optional $\LocatedCompactUp$ handoff row for the same core neighbourhood, or an explicit empty handoff row when no located boundary is claimed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for topology source, inspected open, core row, way-below witness, compact witness, located handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite neighbourhood request through $X,U,V,W,K,L,H$ and into local compactness, compact metric, located compact, or completion-facing consumers.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CoreCompactUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\LocallyCompactUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\LocatedCompactUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CoreCompactUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these ten rows. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, a host-selected basis, quotient-open equality, countable choice, all-neighbourhood local compactness, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CoreCompact way-below neighbourhood route]
\label{thm:core-compact-way-below-neighbourhood-route}
Let $Q=(X,U,V,W,K,L,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CoreCompactUp$ packet. Every $\LocallyCompactUp$ or $\TopologyUp$ consumer that reads a core-compact neighbourhood must first display the topology source $X$, the inspected open row $U$, the core row $V$, and the way-below witness $W$. Only after those rows are present may the consumer inspect the compact witness $K$, the optional located compact handoff $L$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. The route supplies a finite way-below neighbourhood certificate and does not export arbitrary open-cover compactness, a host basis selector, quotient-open equality, countable choice, all-centres/all-radii properness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:core-compact-carrier}. The first public rows are the topology source, the inspected open, the displayed core neighbourhood, and the way-below witness.

Read the local compactness sibling through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locallycompact-namecert}. That sibling supplies compact neighbourhood data only after an inspected point and displayed compact window are named; here the analogous route is $U,V,W$ before compact support is read.

The compact support is therefore downstream. The row $K$ records the compact metric or compact-window witness for the displayed core, and the optional row $L$ can refine the same neighbourhood only when located compact data are shown.

Finally use the structural rows. Componentwise transport and replay preserve the same finite route, while provenance and local naming identify the sibling surfaces. Since no excluded open-cover, basis-selector, quotient, choice, properness-family, or ambient-completeness coordinate occurs in $Q$, no consumer can recover such data from the way-below route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoreCompact located compact handoff]
\label{thm:core-compact-located-compact-handoff}
Let $Q=(X,U,V,W,K,L,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CoreCompactUp$ packet. A $\LocatedCompactUp$ or $\CompactMetricUp$ consumer may read $L$ or $K$ only as support for the same displayed core neighbourhood $V \ll U$. The handoff factors through the $\TopologyUp$ source row, the $\LocallyCompactUp$ compact-neighbourhood route, the $\CompactMetricUp$ witness row, the optional $\LocatedCompactUp$ row, and then the $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows $H,C,P,N$. It exports no independent located compact object, no open-cover compactness theorem, no countable choice, and no ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with \autoref{thm:core-compact-way-below-neighbourhood-route}. It forces every consumer to expose $X,U,V,W$ before compact or located support is available.

Use the compact metric sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}. The compact row $K$ is read only for the displayed core neighbourhood; it is not a host compactness theorem and not a compact object detached from $V \ll U$.

Use the located compact sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedcompact-namecert}. The row $L$ is optional and exact: when present it refines the same compact neighbourhood, and when absent the packet makes no locatedness claim.

The remaining rows bind the handoff. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming pass through $H,C,P,N$, so the located or compact consumer receives one displayed core-neighbourhood route and none of the excluded open-cover, choice, quotient, independent located-object, or ambient-completeness data.
\end{proof}
