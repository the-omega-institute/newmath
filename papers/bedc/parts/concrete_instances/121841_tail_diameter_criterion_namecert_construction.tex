Following Bishop and Bridges constructive real analysis, the $\TailDiameterCriterionUp$ packet records the finite criterion that a regular rational source is Cauchy exactly when every requested dyadic tolerance can be served by a displayed tail-diameter witness. It refines the tail witness of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-tail-diameter-namecert} and the real-facing gauge of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-tail-diameter-gauge-namecert}, while depending on the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet supplies a criterion-level finite tail-diameter object for Real and Completion arguments; it does not assert quotient real equality, selected limits, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Tail-diameter criterion carrier]
\label{def:tail-diameter-criterion-carrier}
A $\TailDiameterCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(S,D,T,K,E,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ or stream source whose Cauchy behavior is being tested.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance request.
\item $T$ is the $\CauchyTailDiameterUp$ tail-bound row that exposes a threshold, two visible tail indices, and the dyadic diameter bound for $D$.
\item $K$ is the criterion row saying that each accepted tolerance request is served by such a displayed tail-bound row.
\item $E$ records the finite equivalence direction between the criterion row and the ordinary Cauchy tail-diameter reading of the same source.
\item $R$ is the $\RealTailDiameterGaugeUp$ or $\RealUp$ handoff row used only after $S,D,T,K,E$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, tolerance, tail-bound, criterion, equivalence, handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a criterion request through $S,D,T,K,E,R,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TailDiameterCriterionUp$, $\CauchyTailDiameterUp$, $\RealTailDiameterGaugeUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TailDiameterCriterionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,D,T,K,E,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient real equality, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or a standard bridge.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tail-diameter criterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:tail-diameter-criterion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\TailDiameterCriterionUp$ carrier $Q=(S,D,T,K,E,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\TailDiameterCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the finite carrier rows of \autoref{def:tail-diameter-criterion-carrier} and the following obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the source, tolerance, tail-bound, criterion, equivalence, handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item tolerance exactness, requiring each criterion request to read the displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$ before any tail-bound row is consumed;
\item tail-bound exactness, requiring $T$ to expose the threshold, two visible tail indices, and dyadic comparison row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-tail-diameter-namecert};
\item criterion exactness, requiring $K$ to bind the same source and tolerance rows used by $T$;
\item equivalence confinement, requiring $E$ to express only the two finite readings of Cauchy tail-diameter data, not quotient real equality or selected limits;
\item Real-seal handoff confinement, requiring $R$ to be downstream from the displayed criterion and compatible with \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-tail-diameter-gauge-namecert};
\item componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming for exactly the same rows.
\end{enumerate}
No tail-diameter criterion obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic rows are $S,D,T,K,E,R$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

Read the tolerance route first. The dyadic request $D$ is exposed before the tail-bound row $T$, so the threshold and visible tail-index comparison are tied to the requested tolerance.

The criterion row $K$ then packages the universal tolerance reading as finite NameCert data. The equivalence row $E$ compares this criterion only with the ordinary tail-diameter Cauchy reading of the same source and tolerance surface.

The handoff row $R$ is downstream. It may serve the real-facing gauge or terminal real seal only after the source, tolerance, tail-bound, criterion, and equivalence rows have been displayed.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same row list under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since no excluded object occurs in the carrier, no additional NameCert obligation can be read from the packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Tail-diameter criterion Cauchy equivalence]
\label{thm:tail-diameter-criterion-cauchy-equivalence}
Every accepted $\TailDiameterCriterionUp$ carrier presents the finite route
$$
S,D \longmapsto T \longmapsto K \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,C,P,N
$$
between the ordinary Cauchy tail-diameter reading of $S$ and the criterion that every displayed dyadic tolerance request is served by a matching tail-bound witness. The route exports only the finite rows of \autoref{def:tail-diameter-criterion-carrier}; it exports no quotient real equality, selected limit, trichotomy decision, countable-choice schedule, open-cover compactness witness, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem, or standard bridge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the source and tolerance rows. The packet exposes $S$ and $D$ before any criterion claim is available.

Apply the tail-diameter witness route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-tail-diameter-namecert}. The row $T$ supplies the threshold, two visible tail indices, and dyadic bound for the same source and tolerance surface.

The criterion row $K$ records that this displayed service is available for each accepted dyadic request, while $E$ records the finite comparison between that criterion and the ordinary tail-diameter Cauchy reading.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same rows. Because the carrier contains no quotient, choice, trichotomy, compactness, completeness, or bridge coordinate, the equivalence route cannot export any of those objects.
\end{proof}
