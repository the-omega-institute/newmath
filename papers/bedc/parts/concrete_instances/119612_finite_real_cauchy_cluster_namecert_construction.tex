\begin{definition}[Finite real Cauchy cluster carrier]
\label{def:finite-real-cauchy-cluster-carrier}
A $\FiniteRealCauchyClusterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(S,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row. It records the inspected stream names, finite index windows, and observation cells available to the cluster route.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row. It exposes only the rational observations scheduled by $S$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row. It records the finite dyadic radius, comparison slack, and arithmetic budget used to compare the readbacks.
\item $L$ is the cluster-window ledger. It records the accepted cluster cells, their finite membership evidence, the dyadic comparison used for each cell, and the finite overlap checks between cells.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row. It records the terminal real-facing seals that may be read after $S,R,D,L$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream windows, regular rational readbacks, dyadic tolerances, cluster ledger, Real seals, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
S,R,D \longmapsto L \longmapsto E .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteRealCauchyClusterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteRealCauchyClusterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient representatives, selected limits, countable choice, host completeness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite real Cauchy cluster obligations]
\label{thm:finite-real-cauchy-cluster-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteRealCauchyClusterUp$ carrier
$$
K=(S,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteRealCauchyClusterUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the stream-window, regular-readback, dyadic-tolerance, cluster-ledger, Real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item finite window coverage, requiring every cluster cell in $L$ to cite only windows displayed by $S$ and readbacks displayed by $R$;
\item cluster ledger exactness, requiring each membership, overlap, and separation comparison in $L$ to be checked against the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row $D$;
\item Real-seal nonescape, requiring every $\RealUp$ seal in $E$ to be reachable only after the finite route $S,R,D,L$ has been displayed;
\item transport stability, requiring componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$ and displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$ to preserve the same cluster-window route, with provenance and local naming exhausted by $P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier $K=(S,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N)$. The source rows are $S$ for finite stream windows, $R$ for regular rational readback, and $D$ for dyadic tolerance data.

Finite window coverage follows because the cluster ledger $L$ has no observation coordinate of its own. Each listed cluster cell must cite an inspected window from $S$ and a readback from $R$ before it can be accepted.

Ledger exactness follows from the placement of $D$. The membership, overlap, and separation entries in $L$ are compared only against the displayed dyadic tolerance row, so a cluster claim cannot switch to a host metric or hidden real representative.

The Real-seal row $E$ is downstream of $L$. Hence a Real or Completion consumer reaches $E$ only after the finite stream, readback, tolerance, and cluster-ledger rows have already been displayed.

Finally, $H$ and $C$ transport and replay the same route, while $P,N$ source and name the packet. Since the carrier contains no excluded coordinate, the five obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
