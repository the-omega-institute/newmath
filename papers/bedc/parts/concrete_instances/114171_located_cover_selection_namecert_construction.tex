\begin{theorem}[Located cover selection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-cover-selection-obligations}

the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedCoverSelectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of real-seal exposure through $R$, regular readback through $Q$, finite schedule exposure through $W$, dyadic radius and tolerance admission through $D$, compact metric source exposure through $K$, finite real cover exposure through $F$, selected located subrow exposure through $S$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-cover-selection-carrier}. The analytic rows are $R,Q,W,D,K,F,S$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The finite selection is internal to the displayed packet. Row $S$ is read only after the compact metric source $K$, the finite cover row $F$, the finite schedule $W$, and the dyadic ledger $D$ have exposed the located membership checks.
