\label{def:located-upper-lower-pair-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ enclosure row, carrying the displayed lower endpoint, upper endpoint, dyadic tolerance, and gap comparison for the requested precision.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which the consumer requests and observes the enclosure stage.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row that turns the same finite window into regular rational lower and upper observations.

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedUpperLowerPairUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

\begin{theorem}[Located upper-lower pair NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-upper-lower-pair-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedUpperLowerPairUp$ carrier $L=(D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedUpperLowerPairUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, dyadic enclosure admission, StreamName window visibility, RegSeqRat lower-upper readback, Real seal nonescape, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming. No NameCert obligation may replace these rows by quotient-cut equality, selected representative equality, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-upper-lower-pair-carrier}. The rows $D,S,R,E$ expose the dyadic enclosure, finite window, regular rational readback, and terminal seal in that order, while $H,C,P,N$ expose the transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The classifier is stable because every accepted comparison reads the same tolerance window through $D$ and the same finite observation window through $S$. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport in $H$ preserves the displayed lower endpoint, upper endpoint, tolerance, and gap comparison before the readback reaches $R$.

Finally, the terminal seal $E$ is downstream of $D,S,R$. Thus the NameCert surface exports only the finite located enclosure route and cannot supply quotient-cut equality, selected host representatives, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
