Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\PrecompactMetricSpaceUp$ packet records finite-net precompactness as displayed BEDC rows before any compact or complete metric consumer reads a completion boundary. It sits after the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the total-boundedness finite-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totally-bounded-metric-namecert}, the compact metric boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the complete metric boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the terminal real-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the Cauchy-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totally-bounded-cauchy-net-namecert}. The packet names a finite-net route from metric data toward compact and complete consumers; it is not an open-cover compactness theorem, a selected completion point, a quotient of Cauchy names, or an ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Precompact metric space carrier]
\label{def:precompact-metric-space-carrier}
A $\PrecompactMetricSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(M,T,K,C,R,W,Q,H,A,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row, exposing the metric observations and metric-law ledger used by every finite-net request.
\item $T$ is the $\TotallyBoundedMetricUp$ finite-net row, displaying for each inspected positive tolerance a finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ of centers and coverage histories.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ compact-boundary row consumed only as a finite-net target surface.
\item $C$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ completion-boundary row reached only after the finite-net row has been exposed.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for displayed distance and tolerance reads.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ or finite-window row through which Cauchy-facing consumers inspect the selected tolerance schedule.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the finite windows before any real seal is exported.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, finite-net, compact, complete, real-seal, window, readback, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $A$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from finite-net inspection through compact and complete metric consumers.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PrecompactMetricSpaceUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\TotallyBoundedMetricUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PrecompactMetricSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,T,K,C,R,W,Q,H,A,G,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected completion points, quotient equality of Cauchy names, countable choice, host compactification, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Precompact metric space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:precompact-metric-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PrecompactMetricSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
P=(M,T,K,C,R,W,Q,H,A,G,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PrecompactMetricSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the eleven displayed rows; metric-source admission through $M$; finite-net admission through $T$; compact-boundary admission through $K$; completion-boundary admission through $C$; real tolerance sealing through $R$; finite-window and regular-rational readback through $W,Q$; componentwise transport through $H$; displayed replay through $A$; provenance through $G$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:precompact-metric-space-carrier}. The analytic rows are the metric source $M$, finite-net row $T$, compact boundary $K$, complete boundary $C$, real seal $R$, finite window $W$, and regular readback $Q$.

The obligation order follows the finite-net route. A consumer first reads the metric source, then the displayed finite net, then any compact or complete metric boundary, and only then the window, readback, and real-seal rows.

The structural rows $H,A,G,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same route. Since no excluded object appears in the carrier, the obligation surface cannot export open-cover compactness, selected completion points, quotient Cauchy equality, countable choice, host compactification, or ambient real completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Precompact metric space finite-net refinement]
\label{thm:precompact-metric-space-finite-net-refinement}
Let
$$
P=(M,T,K,C,R,W,Q,H,A,G,N)
$$
be an accepted $\PrecompactMetricSpaceUp$ carrier. Any compact, complete, or real-facing consumer that reads $P$ must first expose the finite-net route
$$
\MetricSpaceUp \longrightarrow \TotallyBoundedMetricUp \longrightarrow \CompactMetricUp,\CompleteMetricUp \longrightarrow \RealUp .
$$
The row $T$ refines every tolerance request into a displayed finite probe bundle before $K$, $C$, $W$, $Q$, or $R$ can be consumed, and the structural rows $H,A,G,N$ keep that refinement attached to the same packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read $M$ as the source metric row. It supplies only displayed distance observations and metric-law ledgers, so a compact or complete metric consumer has no access to a host completion boundary at this stage.

Next read $T$. The total-boundedness row supplies the finite probe bundles and coverage histories for the inspected tolerances. This is the only row that can feed the downstream compact and complete boundaries $K$ and $C$.

Finally read $W,Q,R$ and the structural rows $H,A,G,N$. The finite windows and regular readback carry the tolerance schedule to the real seal, while transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite-net route. Any consumer that bypasses $T$ would use a coordinate absent from \autoref{def:precompact-metric-space-carrier}.
\end{proof}
