\begin{definition}[Located monotone sequence carrier]
\label{def:located-monotone-sequence-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $B$ is the accepted $\BoundedMonotoneSequenceUp$ row, exposing the same sequence through displayed upper-bound and lower-bound evidence.
\item $S$ is the $\LocatedSupremumUp$ handoff row, carrying upper-bound and lower-approximation data for the source sequence.
\item $W$ is the shared $\StreamNameUp$ precision-window row through which sequence observations and located-supremum requests are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row assembled from the windowed observations and the located-supremum approximation ledger.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording dyadic precision requests, comparison radii, and tail thresholds.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, boundedness, located-supremum, window, readback, tolerance, real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedMonotoneSequenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through
$$
M,B,S,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N.

It has no coordinate for ambient real completeness, a selected limit, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, countable choice, or a host-level monotone convergence theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located monotone sequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-monotone-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedMonotoneSequenceUp$ carrier
$$
L=(M,B,S,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedMonotoneSequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, monotone convergence source exposure, bounded monotone source exposure, located-supremum handoff, shared finite-window exposure, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance replay, terminal Real-seal ordering, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming for the same finite packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-monotone-sequence-carrier}. Carrier admission exposes exactly $M,B,S,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$, so the only source-facing rows are the monotone convergence source $M$ and the bounded monotone source $B$.

Read the order and approximation rows next. The located-supremum handoff $S$ is the only row that can expose upper-bound and lower-approximation data, while $W,R,D$ provide the shared finite windows, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance accounting.

The Real-facing seal is downstream of these rows. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ can only transport, replay, source, and name the same displayed packet, so the local NameCert surface has no coordinate for a selected limit, quotient equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located monotone sequence located-supremum handoff]
\label{thm:located-monotone-sequence-located-supremum-handoff}

Thus the L10 monotone-sequence-to-located-supremum route is a displayed finite handoff, not an appeal to arbitrary $\RealUp$ completeness or a choice-selected limit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted packet $L=(M,B,S,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$. By \autoref{def:located-monotone-sequence-carrier}, the terminal real row is $E$, and it appears only after the source, boundedness, located-supremum, window, readback, and tolerance rows have been displayed.

\end{proof}
