\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DecimalExpansionUniquenessUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges together with the standard convention that decimal streams are read in normalized form, the $\DecimalExpansionUniquenessUp$ packet records the finite route by which a decimal real has at most one normalized decimal stream presentation. It sits after the decimal readback carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-decimal-real-namecert}, the dual-tail boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-decimal-endpoint-ambiguity-namecert}, the endpoint-normalization route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-decimal-endpoint-normalization-namecert}, and the real decimal normal-form route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-decimal-normal-form-namecert}. The packet names uniqueness for normalized tails only; it does not quotient all decimal streams, choose representatives at endpoints, or expose host equality of real numbers.

\begin{definition}[Decimal expansion uniqueness carrier]
\label{def:decimal-expansion-uniqueness-carrier}
A $\DecimalExpansionUniquenessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(D,E,N,W,Q,R,Y,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DecimalRealUp$ readback row whose signed decimal prefix, ambiguity boundary, finite window, dyadic tolerance, regular rational readback, and terminal real seal are being inspected.
\item $E$ is the $\DecimalEndpointAmbiguityUp$ boundary row, exposing whether a displayed tail is ordinary, trailing-nine-facing, or trailing-zero-facing before any uniqueness consumer proceeds.
\item $N$ is the $\DecimalEndpointNormalizationUp$ row, replacing an endpoint-facing tail by the displayed normalized tail convention without choosing a quotient representative for arbitrary streams.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row listing the decimal cells and precision requests actually read by the comparison.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same finite decimal windows and tolerance requests.
\item $R$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare normalized decimal tails at the requested precision.
\item $Y$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,E,N,W,Q,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for decimal readback, endpoint ambiguity, endpoint normalization, stream windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the normalized comparison route
$$
D,E,N,W,Q,R \longmapsto Y .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DecimalExpansionUniquenessUp$, $\DecimalRealUp$, $\DecimalEndpointAmbiguityUp$, $\DecimalEndpointNormalizationUp$, $\RealDecimalNormalFormUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DecimalExpansionUniquenessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,E,N,W,Q,R,Y,H,C,P,L$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected endpoint representatives, arbitrary decimal-stream uniqueness, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Decimal expansion uniqueness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:decimal-expansion-uniqueness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DecimalExpansionUniquenessUp$ carrier
$$
U=(D,E,N,W,Q,R,Y,H,C,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DecimalExpansionUniquenessUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for decimal readback, endpoint ambiguity, endpoint normalization, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item endpoint visibility, requiring $E$ to be read before a trailing-nine-facing or trailing-zero-facing tail is normalized;
\item normalization exactness, requiring $N$ to display the normalized tail convention consumed by the uniqueness comparison;
\item finite-window comparison, requiring $W,Q,R$ to expose the inspected decimal cells, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance ledger before the $\RealUp$ seal $Y$ is available;
\item nonescape, requiring $H,C,P,L$ to transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed normalized-tail route and to export no quotient stream equality, selected endpoint representative, arbitrary decimal-stream uniqueness, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient completeness row.
\end{enumerate}
No DecimalExpansionUniqueness obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:decimal-expansion-uniqueness-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the decimal readback, endpoint ambiguity, endpoint normalization, finite windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, real seal, and structural rows.

Endpoint visibility is the row $E$. It is inspected before normalization, so a dual-tail boundary is not silently treated as an equality of unnormalized streams.

Normalization exactness is the row $N$. It records the standard normalized-tail convention as packet data, while refusing a selected representative for arbitrary decimal presentations.

The comparison then passes through $W,Q,R$. These rows display the finite decimal cells, the regular rational readback, and the dyadic tolerance ledger before the real seal $Y$ can be consumed.

Finally $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier contains no quotient stream equality, endpoint choice, arbitrary uniqueness, trichotomy, choice, or ambient completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Decimal expansion uniqueness normal-form route]
\label{thm:decimal-expansion-uniqueness-normal-form-route}
Every uniqueness read exported by an accepted $\DecimalExpansionUniquenessUp$ carrier factors through
$$
\DecimalRealUp,\ \DecimalEndpointAmbiguityUp,\ \DecimalEndpointNormalizationUp,\ \RealDecimalNormalFormUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RealUp
$$
as the displayed route $D,E,N,W,Q,R,Y,H,C,P,L$. Thus the comparison concerns normalized decimal tails reaching the same terminal real seal, not quotient equality of all decimal streams and not a choice of endpoint representative.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a uniqueness consumer of the carrier. Projection from \autoref{def:decimal-expansion-uniqueness-carrier} gives the route $D,E,N,W,Q,R,Y,H,C,P,L$ and no additional stream-equality object.

Read $D$ first. This supplies the decimal real readback route through finite prefixes, ambiguity boundary data, dyadic tolerance, regular rational readback, and a terminal real seal.

Read $E$ and $N$ next. The endpoint ambiguity row exposes the dual-tail boundary, and the endpoint normalization row records the normalized-tail convention used by the comparison.

The finite comparison then runs through $W,Q,R$. The stream windows and regular rational readback are inspected at a displayed dyadic tolerance before the real seal $Y$ is reached.

The structural rows $H,C,P,L$ keep the consumer on the same route. Therefore the packet exports uniqueness only for normalized decimal tails and cannot export quotient equality of all streams or selected endpoint representatives.
\end{proof}
