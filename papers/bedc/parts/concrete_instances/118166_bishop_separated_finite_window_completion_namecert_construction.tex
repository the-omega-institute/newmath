\begin{definition}[Bishop separated finite-window completion carrier]
\label{def:bishop-separated-finite-window-completion-carrier}
A $\BishopSeparatedFiniteWindowCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(H,F,S,D,R,E,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $H$ is the $\HausdorffCompletionUp$ separated-completion boundary, read as finite seal data rather than as a quotient class.
\item $F$ is the $\MetricCompletionFiniteUp$ finite-window completion row that fixes the completion-facing request.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule opened by the finite request.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for the radii and slack comparisons attached to the visible windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row consumed after the windows and dyadic tolerances have been displayed.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, exposed only after $H,F,S,D,R$ have been replayed.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the separated boundary, finite-window completion row, stream schedule, dyadic ledger, regular readback, real seal, continuation, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for refinements that remain inside the same separated finite-window packet.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopSeparatedFiniteWindowCompletionUp$, $\HausdorffCompletionUp$, $\MetricCompletionFiniteUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopSeparatedFiniteWindowCompletionUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $H,F,S,D,R,E,T,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, host equality of completed points, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop separated finite-window completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-separated-finite-window-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopSeparatedFiniteWindowCompletionUp$ carrier
$$
B=(H,F,S,D,R,E,T,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopSeparatedFiniteWindowCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the separated completion boundary $H$, finite-window completion row $F$, stream schedule $S$, dyadic ledger $D$, regular readback $R$, terminal real seal $E$, and structural rows $T,C,P,N$;
\item separated-window compatibility, requiring $F$ to consume the separated boundary $H$ before any stream window or rational readback is public;
\item finite coverage, requiring every visible completion request in $F$ to have a matching window in $S$, dyadic tolerance in $D$, and regular readback in $R$;
\item stability under transport and replay, requiring refinements to move through $T$ and $C$ without replacing the displayed separated boundary or finite-window row;
\item real-seal nonescape, requiring the $\RealUp$ row $E$ to export only the named seal reached through $H,F,S,D,R$.
\end{enumerate}
No local obligation exports quotient representatives, host equality of completed points, countable choice, or an ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-separated-finite-window-completion-carrier}. Its visible coordinates are exactly $H,F,S,D,R,E,T,C,P,N$, so carrier admission can expose only those rows.

Separated-window compatibility reads $H$ before $F$. The finite-window row is therefore attached to a displayed Hausdorff completion boundary, not to a quotient representative or host completion object.

Finite coverage then reads $S,D,R$ from the same packet. Each completion request visible in $F$ has its stream window, dyadic tolerance, and regular rational readback present before the terminal seal is reachable.

Transport and replay use $T$ and $C$ componentwise. Since those rows move the displayed packet rather than replacing it, the route remains inside $H,F,S,D,R,E,T,C,P,N$.

The real-seal clause is the final projection. The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, host equality, or ambient completeness, so none of those objects can be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop separated finite-window completion handoff]
\label{thm:bishop-separated-finite-window-completion-handoff}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:bishop-separated-finite-window-completion-namecert-obligations}. It forces any completion-facing consumer to read the separated boundary $H$ and finite-window row $F$ before inspecting the downstream readback.

The excluded completion principles are absent from the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-separated-finite-window-completion-carrier}. Therefore the handoff can export only the separated finite-window completion surface named by the packet.
\end{proof}
