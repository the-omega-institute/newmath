\begin{definition}[LocatedApartnessInterval carrier]
\label{def:located-apartness-interval-carrier}
A $\LocatedApartnessIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(I,L,U,E,S,R,D,G,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the accepted $\LocatedIntervalUp$ source row, exposing lower and upper endpoint reads, containment rows, interval-cell ledgers, and endpoint-window routes.
\item $L$ and $U$ are the lower and upper $\RealUp$ endpoint seal rows of the interval source.
\item $E$ is the exterior real-name row whose separation from the interval is being witnessed.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row naming the inspected endpoint and exterior-name observations.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to $S$, supplying regular rational endpoint and exterior observations.
\item $D$ is the positive $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row used for endpoint slack and apartness radius comparisons.
\item $G$ is the $\ApartnessRealUp$ gap row, displaying either separation of $E$ from the lower endpoint side or separation of $E$ from the upper endpoint side through the same finite windows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval source, endpoint seals, exterior row, stream windows, readbacks, dyadic tolerance row, apartness gap row, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from interval membership or exterior-separation requests through $I,L,U,E,S,R,D,G,H$.

\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for real trichotomy, quotient equality of real names, chosen endpoint representatives, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedApartnessInterval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-apartness-interval-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedApartnessIntervalUp$ carrier $A=(I,L,U,E,S,R,D,G,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedApartnessIntervalUp}$ surface consists exactly of these finite rows: carrier admission for the displayed packet; source-interval exactness through $I,L,U$; exterior-name admission through $E$; finite-window and regular-readback exactness through $S,R$; positive dyadic budget exactness through $D$; apartness-gap exactness through $G$; and structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-apartness-interval-carrier}. The interval source, endpoint seals, exterior real row, finite windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance, apartness gap, and structural rows are precisely the displayed coordinates of the packet.

Read the interval side first. The located interval source $I$ exposes endpoint and containment data, while $L$ and $U$ seal those endpoints as $\RealUp$ rows before the exterior row can be compared to them.

Read the numerical evidence next. The finite window $S$ and regular rational readback $R$ give the endpoint and exterior observations, and the dyadic row $D$ supplies the positive tolerance used by the separation ledger.

Finally read the apartness and structural rows. The row $G$ is the only apartness coordinate, and $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no excluded host-order, quotient, choice, compactness, selected-endpoint, or completeness coordinate occurs in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedApartnessInterval RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:located-apartness-interval-regseqrat-handoff}
Let $A=(I,L,U,E,S,R,D,G,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\LocatedApartnessIntervalUp$ carrier. Every downstream $\RegSeqRatUp$ consumer of the interval-apartness read must obtain its rational data from the same finite window row $S$ and readback row $R$ before consuming the dyadic tolerance row $D$ or the apartness gap row $G$. The handoff reaches $\ApartnessRealUp$ and $\RealUp$ only through the displayed endpoint, exterior-name, window, readback, tolerance, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a downstream read of rational endpoint or exterior-name data. The carrier stores all such observations in the finite window row $S$ and the regular rational readback row $R$, so a consumer has no second rational source.

Attach the dyadic budget after the readback. The tolerance row $D$ measures endpoint slack and separation radius only for the observations already exposed by $S$ and $R$.

Now read the apartness handoff. The gap row $G$ consumes the same endpoint and exterior observations together with the positive dyadic tolerance; it does not create a new real comparison or a host interval order.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve this route when it is replayed toward $\ApartnessRealUp$ or $\RealUp$. Thus the handoff is exactly the finite RegSeqRat route attached to the located interval packet, with no quotient real equality, selected endpoint, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness export.
\end{proof}
