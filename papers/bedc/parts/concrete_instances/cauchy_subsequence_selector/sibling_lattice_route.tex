The $\CauchySubsequenceSelectorUp$ route is consumed by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fastcauchysubsequence-namecert} when a cofinal fast subsequence schedule must be displayed before a completion-facing read, and by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-gluing-namecert} when selected finite windows are glued back into a regular Cauchy route.
