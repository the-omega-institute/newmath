\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LebesgueConstantUp$}

Following Lebesgue and Zygmund on Fourier partial sums, the $\LebesgueConstantUp$ packet records the finite norm-growth data of trigonometric partial-sum operators as BEDC NameCert rows. It sits over the Fourier coefficient windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fourier-namecert}, the Dirichlet-kernel row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dirichlet-kernel-namecert}, the circle phase samples of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-s1-namecert}, the integral normalization route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-integral-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the downstream approximation boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformlimit-namecert}. The packet names a finite harmonic-analysis growth surface, not a host $L^{1}$ operator norm, not Haar measure, and not a quotient function-space object.

\begin{definition}[LebesgueConstant carrier]
\label{def:lebesgue-constant-carrier}
A $\LebesgueConstantUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(F,D,S,I,N,W,R,E,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the Fourier coefficient-window row naming the finite frequency interval whose partial sum is inspected.
\item $D$ is the Dirichlet-kernel row supplying the finite convolution kernel for that window.
\item $S$ is the $\SOneUp$ phase-sample row used to evaluate the displayed kernel and partial-sum reads.
\item $I$ is the $\IntegralUp$ normalization row for the finite absolute-kernel read.
\item $N$ is the finite norm-growth ledger recording the inspected Lebesgue-constant value window.
\item $W$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance window bounding the displayed norm read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the finite rational approximations.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for the displayed norm-growth datum.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $F,D,S,I,N,W,R,E,C,P,L$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a partial-sum request through the Dirichlet-kernel and integral rows to the norm ledger.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over the Fourier, Dirichlet-kernel, circle, integral, dyadic, regular-rational, Real, transport, replay, and NameCert rows.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LebesgueConstantUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets displaying all twelve rows. It has no coordinate for Haar measure, host $L^{1}$ spaces, quotient function equality, selected Fourier representatives, ambient completeness, or an unrestricted Fourier-convergence theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[LebesgueConstant NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lebesgue-constant-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:lebesgue-constant-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\LebesgueConstantUp}$ surface is exhausted by five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for $K=(F,D,S,I,N,W,R,E,H,C,P,L)$;
\item Fourier-window and Dirichlet-kernel exposure through $F$ and $D$;
\item phase-sample and integral-normalization exposure through $S$ and $I$;
\item norm-growth, dyadic tolerance, regular rational readback, and Real sealing through $N,W,R,E$;
\item componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,L$.
\end{enumerate}
Every accepted norm-growth read must display these rows before a downstream approximation consumer may cite a Lebesgue-constant datum.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lebesgue-constant-carrier}. The displayed tuple contains exactly the Fourier window, Dirichlet kernel, phase samples, integral normalization, norm ledger, dyadic tolerance, regular rational readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert rows.

The first two obligations bind the source of the partial-sum read. The Fourier window $F$ names the finite frequency interval, and the Dirichlet row $D$ supplies the only kernel through which the partial-sum norm can be inspected.

The phase and normalization rows $S,I$ then make the finite absolute-kernel read visible before any norm-growth entry is accepted. Rows $N,W,R,E$ keep the numeric read inside the dyadic, regular-rational, and Real sealing route.

Finally $H,C,P,L$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no row for Haar measure, host $L^{1}$ spaces, quotient function equality, selected Fourier representatives, or ambient completeness, the five listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LebesgueConstant Dirichlet norm handoff]
\label{thm:lebesgue-constant-dirichlet-norm-handoff}
Every Fourier partial-sum norm read exported by an accepted $\LebesgueConstantUp$ packet factors through the Dirichlet-kernel and integral rows
$$
F,D,S,I \longmapsto N,W,R,E
$$
before any $\UniformLimitUp$ handoff can consume the norm-growth datum. The handoff does not export Haar measure, host $L^{1}$ spaces, quotient function equality, selected Fourier representatives, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a consumer of the norm ledger $N$. Carrier admission places $N$ downstream of the Fourier window $F$, Dirichlet kernel $D$, circle phase samples $S$, and integral normalization $I$.

The replay row $C$ records the only displayed route from a partial-sum request to the norm-growth read. Thus the request must first expose the finite coefficient window, then read the Dirichlet-kernel row and the integral-normalization row before the dyadic tolerance window $W$ and regular-rational readback $R$ are available.

The Real seal $E$ is reached only after $N,W,R$ have been displayed. Transport and provenance through $H$ and $P$ keep that same route attached to the local name $L$, so a $\UniformLimitUp$ consumer receives only the finite norm-growth datum produced by the Dirichlet-kernel and integral rows.
\end{proof}
