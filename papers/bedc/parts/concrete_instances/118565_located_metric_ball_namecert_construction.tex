\begin{definition}[LocatedMetricBall carrier]
\label{def:located-metric-ball-carrier}
A $\LocatedMetricBallUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(M,X,C,R,Q,L,T,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, recording the displayed metric relation and the finite distance windows available to the packet.
\item $X$ is the inspected point row, naming the point or finite point-window whose membership in the ball is being read.
\item $C$ is the center row, naming the displayed center and its transport data inside the same metric source.
\item $R$ is the positive radius row, read through $\RealUp$ and carried only as a displayed real-name seal.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row that supplies rational tolerance windows for the radius and distance comparison.
\item $L$ is the located comparison row, recording the finite alternatives $d_M(X,C)<R$ and $R<d_M(X,C)$ at the requested precision.
\item $T$ is the radius-transport row, recording how the same center and radius are preserved when the metric source is transported by $\hsame$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,X,C,R,Q,L,T,P,N$.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the metric, point, center, radius, rational-readback, located-comparison, and radius-transport rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedMetricBallUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RegularCauchyBallBasisUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedMetricBallUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $M,X,C,R,Q,L,T,H,K,P,N$. It therefore records one located metric-ball packet, not a host open set, a completed metric space, a quotient distance equality, a selected finite cover, or an ambient compactness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{proof}

\label{thm:located-metric-ball-radius-transport}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
