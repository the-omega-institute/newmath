\begin{definition}[Darboux partition ledger carrier]
\label{def:darboux-partition-ledger-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $C$ is the finite cell ledger, recording the ordered cells, endpoint adjacency, and membership boundary for each carried cell.
\item $E$ is the endpoint-coverage row, stating that the displayed cells cover exactly the interval source named by $I$.
\item $R$ is the refinement ledger, recording the finite parent-child relation between the carried cell ledger and any displayed refinement read by a consumer.
\item $W$ is the width ledger, recording the cell-width rows used by lower, upper, Darboux, and Riemann finite sums.
\item $S$ is the sum-consumer exposure row for $\DarbouxSumUp$, $\UpperDarbouxSumUp$, $\LowerDarbouxSumUp$, and $\RiemannSumUp$.
