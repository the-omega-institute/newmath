\begin{definition}[Bishop Cauchy rate completion carrier]
\label{def:bishop-cauchy-rate-completion-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $M$ is the Bishop rate row, recording the displayed modulus request by which later windows are compared with earlier windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger attached to the same rate request and window family.

\begin{theorem}[Bishop Cauchy rate completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-cauchy-rate-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopCauchyRateCompletionUp$ carrier
$$

the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCauchyRateCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite-window exposure through $S$, rate exposure through $M$, dyadic tolerance exposure through $D$, regular-rational readback through $R$, terminal Real seal exposure through $E$, Bishop regular completion handoff through $B$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-cauchy-rate-completion-carrier}. Its content rows are the finite stream window, rate, dyadic tolerance, regular-rational readback, terminal Real seal, and Bishop completion handoff.

Read the L10 dependency route in order. The $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$ exposes the finite observations, $M$ fixes the rate request against those observations, and the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$ supplies the tolerance ledger used by that rate request.

The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ is downstream of $S,M,D$, and the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is downstream of $R$. The Bishop regular completion row $B$ names the completion-facing handoff of the same route, so it cannot replace the displayed windows, rates, tolerances, or readback by a selected limit.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no carrier coordinate remains outside $S,M,D,R,E,B,H,C,P,N$, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
