Following Bourbaki, Kelley, and Bishop--Bridges, the $\CauchyFilterCompletenessUp$ packet records the finite L10 route by which a located Cauchy filter may be read toward a Real-facing completion seal. It sits after the Cauchy-filter carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfilter-namecert}, the located-filter boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-cauchy-filter-namecert}, the Real-induced uniformity of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-uniform-structure-namecert}, the finite StreamName windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Cauchy filter completeness carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-filter-completeness-carrier}
A $\CauchyFilterCompletenessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(F,L,U,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ row, exposing the filter base, directed refinements, and Cauchy request ledger.
\item $L$ is the located-tail row, read through $\LocatedCauchyFilterUp$ and carrying the finite interval or apartness evidence needed by a completion-facing request.
\item $U$ is the $\RealUniformStructureUp$ uniform-entourage row used to compare the displayed tails by Real-induced metric balls.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row for the inspected stages.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows and uniform requests.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $F,L,U,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport of $F,L,U,S,R,E$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyFilterCompletenessUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\LocatedCauchyFilterUp$, $\RealUniformStructureUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyFilterCompletenessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $F,L,U,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient filters, selected limits, countable choice, host equality of completed points, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy filter completeness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-filter-completeness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyFilterCompletenessUp$ carrier $Q=(F,L,U,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyFilterCompletenessUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, Cauchy-filter exposure through $F$, located-tail exposure through $L$, Real-uniform entourage exposure through $U$, finite StreamName windows through $S$, RegSeqRat readback through $R$, terminal Real sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-filter-completeness-carrier}. The available analytic rows are exactly $F,L,U,S,R,E$, and the available structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The source side starts with $F,L,U$. The Cauchy-filter row supplies the displayed filter requests, the located-tail row supplies finite interval or apartness evidence, and the Real-uniform row supplies the entourage comparison used by the completion-facing read.

The numerical readback is downstream. Rows $S$ and $R$ expose only finite windows and regular rational observations, and the seal row $E$ is available only after those rows have been displayed.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same packet. Since quotient filters, selected limits, countable choice, host equality, and ambient Real completeness are absent from the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:cauchy-filter-completeness-located-real-extraction}

$$

$$
The extraction may expose a terminal Real seal only as the end of this displayed route; it does not provide a quotient filter, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host equality of completed points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:cauchy-filter-completeness-namecert-obligations}. It orders every Real-facing read through the Cauchy-filter row, located-tail row, Real-uniform entourage row, finite windows, regular rational readback, and terminal seal.

The located-tail and uniform-entourage rows are both visible before the seal. Thus the consumer can inspect how the filter request is located and how the Real-induced uniformity compares the inspected tails, but it cannot bypass those rows.

The seal row $E$ is terminal. Rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route, so the Real-facing extraction remains a displayed packet rather than a host completion construction.
