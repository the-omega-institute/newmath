$$

\item $W$ is the shared $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-sequence readback row attached to those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used for positive-radius comparison.

\begin{proof}

With the source, readback, dyadic scale, projections, and separation witnesses displayed, the uniform pullback row $U$ is a BEDC replay row rather than an external completeness principle. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming keep the export inside the finite carrier.
\end{proof}
