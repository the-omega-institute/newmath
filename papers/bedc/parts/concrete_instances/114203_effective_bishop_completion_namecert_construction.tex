$$

\label{thm:effective-bishop-completion-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}

The source and window rows are finite. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ supplies the regular rational source, while the $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$ supplies only displayed finite windows for the readback.
