\begin{definition}[Chain-rule carrier]
\label{def:chain-rule-carrier}
A $\ChainRuleUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(D_0,C_i,D_o,W,Q,E,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D_0$ is the source $\DerivativeUp$ row naming the derivative observation for the inner displayed map at the inspected source window.
\item $C_i$ is the inner $\ContinuousMapUp$ row that carries the graph window needed to feed the outer derivative row.
\item $D_o$ is the outer $\DerivativeUp$ row naming the derivative observation at the displayed image window.
\item $W$ is the composition-window row recording the finite source, image, tolerance, and endpoint requests common to $D_0,C_i,D_o$.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational derivative and error values inspected through $W$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $D_0,C_i,D_o,W,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the derivative, continuous-map, composition-window, readback, real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a chain-rule request through $D_0,C_i,D_o,W,Q,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ChainRuleUp$, $\CalculusUp$, $\DerivativeUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChainRuleUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D_0,C_i,D_o,W,Q,E,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host function equality, quotient equality of maps, a selected representative, countable choice, a global derivative operator, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Chain-rule NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:chain-rule-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ChainRuleUp$ carrier $G=(D_0,C_i,D_o,W,Q,E,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ChainRuleUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the source derivative row, inner continuous-map row, outer derivative row, composition-window row, regular rational readback, real-name seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every derivative-composition read must pass through the $\CalculusUp$, $\DerivativeUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ sibling rows before a consumer reads the resulting Real-facing value.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:chain-rule-carrier}. The only analytic rows available to the classifier are $D_0,C_i,D_o,W,Q,E$: the inner derivative observation, inner graph window, outer derivative observation, shared composition window, regular rational readback, and terminal Real seal.

The calculus root surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-calculus-namecert} supplies the L10 boundary in which those rows are inspected. The source and outer derivative rows remain displayed $\DerivativeUp$ observations, and the inner graph row remains a $\ContinuousMapUp$ observation; none of them is replaced by a host function-space equality or a global derivative operator.

Readback and sealing are downstream of the same composition window. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $Q$ records the finite rational values and tolerances exposed by $W$, and the $\RealUp$ row $E$ is consumed only after those values have been displayed.

Finally, $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier contains no row for quotient map equality, selected representatives, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:chain-rule-l10-handoff}

$$

The handoff exports no host function equality, quotient function space, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, global derivative operator, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Transport and replay preserve the same finite route through $H$ and $T$, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming. \end{proof}
