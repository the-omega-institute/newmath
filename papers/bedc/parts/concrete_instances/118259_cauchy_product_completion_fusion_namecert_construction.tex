\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CauchyProductCompletionFusionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-product-completion-fusion-namecert}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\CauchyProductCompletionFusionUp$ packet
records the constructive fusion interface between the product-completion
certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-product-completion-namecert}
and the regular Cauchy coordinate projection route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-product-projection-namecert}. It
reads finite windows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert},
regular rational readback from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert},
dyadic tolerance rows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert},
and the terminal seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The
packet names only displayed product-completion rows; it does not quotient
streams, select limits, or invoke ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[CauchyProductCompletionFusion carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-product-completion-fusion-carrier}
A $\CauchyProductCompletionFusionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(Q,J,S,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the accepted $\CauchyProductCompletionUp$ product-completion
packet whose fused route is being consumed.
\item $J$ is the $\RegularCauchyProductProjectionUp$ coordinate projection
packet exposing the left and right regular Cauchy readbacks of $Q$.
\item $S$ is the shared $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window family read before either
coordinate projection reaches a real-facing consumer.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger governing both coordinate
windows and the product budget.
\item $R$ is the paired $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained after $S$ and $D$
are displayed.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row available only after the product-completion
packet, projection packet, finite windows, dyadic ledger, and regular readback
have all been exposed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $Q,J,S,D,R,E$.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same product-completion
and projection route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyProductCompletionFusionUp$,
$\CauchyProductCompletionUp$, $\RegularCauchyProductProjectionUp$,
$\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$,
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyProductCompletionFusionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through
$Q,J,S,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It contains no quotient stream equality, selected
completion representative, host product equality, trichotomy, or ambient
completeness row.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyProductCompletionFusion laws]
\label{thm:cauchy-product-completion-fusion-laws}
Every $\RealUp$-facing read exported by an accepted
$\CauchyProductCompletionFusionUp$ carrier factors through
$\CauchyProductCompletionUp$, $\RegularCauchyProductProjectionUp$,
$\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ in that
order, with $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ rows preserving the
same finite route. The fusion therefore exposes product-completion and
regular-Cauchy projection data as one displayed interface without adding
quotient streams, selected limits, host product equality, or ambient
completeness.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.CauchyProductCompletionFusionUp.CauchyProductCompletionFusion\_laws}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:cauchy-product-completion-fusion-carrier}. The rows $Q$ and $J$
are the only product-completion and projection packets available to the
consumer, so a Real-facing read cannot start from an external product object.

Read the finite route next. The shared window row $S$ and dyadic ledger $D$
precede the regular readback row $R$, and the terminal seal $E$ is downstream
of those displayed rows. Thus the route has exactly the dependency order
claimed in the statement.

Finally inspect $H,C,P,N$. They transport, replay, source, and name the same
finite packet, but introduce no coordinate for quotient equality, selected
limits, host product equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyProductCompletionFusion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-product-completion-fusion-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted carrier $F=(Q,J,S,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\CauchyProductCompletionFusionUp}$ surface consists exactly of
carrier admission, product-completion admission for $Q$, projection-route
admission for $J$, stream-window exposure for $S$, dyadic ledger compatibility
for $D$, regular readback handoff for $R$, Real-seal nonescape for $E$,
componentwise transport through $H$, continuation replay through $C$,
provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is the projection of the finite packet
$F=(Q,J,S,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$. The product-completion and projection obligations
are exactly the rows $Q$ and $J$, so the fused certificate does not recreate
their sibling carriers.

The window, ledger, and readback obligations are the rows $S,D,R$. They force
each coordinate read to expose a StreamName window and DyadicRatCore tolerance
before the regular rational handoff is available.

The remaining obligations are structural. The seal row $E$ is the only
Real-facing export, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same
route. Since the carrier has no other row, the local NameCert surface is
exhausted by the listed obligations.
\end{proof}
