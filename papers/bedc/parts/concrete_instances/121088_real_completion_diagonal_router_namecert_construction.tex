Following Bishop and Bridges, \emph{Constructive Analysis}, the $\RealCompletionDiagonalRouterUp$ packet records the finite diagonal handoff used by the Real and Completion route of \autoref{ch:visions-real-completeness}. It sits after the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the stream-window carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular-rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the minimal completion service boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionminimality-namecert}. Its role is to route one finite precision window through those rows before a completion-facing consumer reads the $\RealUp$ seal; it is not a quotient-stream construction, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, or ambient completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[RealCompletionDiagonalRouter carrier]
\label{def:real-completion-diagonal-router-carrier}
A $\RealCompletionDiagonalRouterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(W,D,R,E,M,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite precision-window row requested by the diagonal consumer.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ dyadic tolerance and readback row for the same window.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-rational handoff row built from the stream window and dyadic readback.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached by that regular-rational handoff.
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyCompletionMinimalityUp$ service row that records dense-image coverage, universal factorization, separated comparison, and replay non-escape for the completion-facing read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the window, dyadic, regular-rational, Real seal, minimality, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay through $W,D,R,E,M,H,P,N$ in the order in which a diagonal consumer may read the packet.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealCompletionDiagonalRouterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyCompletionMinimalityUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionDiagonalRouterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient streams, selected diagonal limits, host real equality, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RealCompletionDiagonalRouter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-completion-diagonal-router-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealCompletionDiagonalRouterUp$ carrier $Q=(W,D,R,E,M,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionDiagonalRouterUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the finite StreamName window, dyadic readback, RegSeqRat handoff, Real seal, CauchyCompletionMinimality route, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Every completion-facing diagonal read must expose the route
$$
W\longmapsto D\longmapsto R\longmapsto E\longmapsto M\longmapsto H\longmapsto C\longmapsto P\longmapsto N
$$
before it can present the Real seal as a finite completion handoff. The obligation surface exports no quotient stream, selected limit, host equality, countable-choice schedule, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:real-completion-diagonal-router-carrier}. The tuple displays exactly the finite window, dyadic readback, regular-rational handoff, terminal Real seal, minimal completion service, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The diagonal route is forced by the order of the displayed carrier. A consumer first reads the StreamName window $W$, then the DyadicRatCore tolerance row $D$, then the RegSeqRat handoff $R$, and only then reaches the Real seal $E$.

The completion-facing part is controlled by $M$. By \autoref{thm:cauchy-completion-minimality-obligation-export}, the minimality row exports dense-image coverage, universal factorization, separated reflection, replay non-escape, and finite-row induction without quotient completion, selected limits, host equality, choice, compactness, or ambient real-completeness data.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since none of the carrier rows contains an off-route completion principle, the local NameCert obligations are exhausted by the stated finite diagonal handoff.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RealCompletionDiagonalRouter precision-window route]
\label{thm:real-completion-diagonal-router-precision-window-route}
Every accepted $\RealCompletionDiagonalRouterUp$ packet routes a finite precision-window request from $\StreamNameUp$ through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ readback and $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff to the $\RealUp$ seal before the $\CauchyCompletionMinimalityUp$ service row can be consumed. The route is finite, componentwise transported by $H$, replayed by $C$, sourced by $P$, and named by $N$.

The packet does not identify Real names by quotient streams, choose a diagonal limit representative, assert host real equality, call on countable choice, or close ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:real-completion-diagonal-router-namecert-obligations}. They require any diagonal read to pass through $W,D,R,E,M,H,C,P,N$ in the displayed order.

The StreamName, DyadicRatCore, and RegSeqRat rows provide only a finite precision-window route. The Real seal row is therefore reached as a named finite handoff rather than as a selected representative of a quotient stream.

Apply \autoref{thm:cauchy-completion-minimality-obligation-export} to the minimality row. It keeps the completion-facing consumer inside dense-image, universal-factorization, separated-reflection, replay, and finite-row obligations, so the packet cannot import host equality, choice, compactness, or ambient completeness after the Real seal is read.
\end{proof}
