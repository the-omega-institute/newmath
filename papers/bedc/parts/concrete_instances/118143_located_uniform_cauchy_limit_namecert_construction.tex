\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedUniformCauchyLimitUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive uniform convergence and regular real completions, the $\LocatedUniformCauchyLimitUp$ packet records the finite located-modulus route by which a uniformly Cauchy family yields a Real-facing limit certificate. It sits after the stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the uniform Cauchy limit packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-cauchy-limit-namecert}, and the completion-modulus handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-cauchy-modulus-limit-namecert}. The packet names a located limit route with an explicit common modulus; it does not assert quotient equality of limits, select a host real, use countable choice, or import ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Located uniform Cauchy limit carrier]
\label{def:located-uniform-cauchy-limit-carrier}
A $\LocatedUniformCauchyLimitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(U,M,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the $\UniformCauchyLimitUp$ source row, including the inspected function-window family and the uniform Cauchy comparison ledger.
\item $M$ is the located common modulus row, read through the completion-modulus surface and binding each tolerance request to a finite tail threshold before any limit seal is exposed.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row selected by the same tolerance and modulus request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row extracted from the displayed windows and tail threshold.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger that compares the requested radius, the selected threshold, and the displayed readback.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for the located limit row, available only after $U,M,S,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the uniform Cauchy source, located modulus, windows, readback, dyadic ledger, Real seal, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a located tolerance request through $U,M,S,R,D$ and then to $E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedUniformCauchyLimitUp$, $\UniformCauchyLimitUp$, $\BishopCauchyModulusLimitUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedUniformCauchyLimitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these ten rows. It has no coordinate for host limit selection, quotient equality of functions or reals, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniform Cauchy limit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-uniform-cauchy-limit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedUniformCauchyLimitUp$ carrier $L=(U,M,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedUniformCauchyLimitUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, located-modulus exposure, finite-window readback, dyadic tolerance accounting, terminal Real sealing, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming for the rows of \autoref{def:located-uniform-cauchy-limit-carrier}. Every completion-facing consumer must read $U$ and $M$ before $S,R,D,E$ are available, and no obligation exports quotient limits, selected host reals, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:located-uniform-cauchy-limit-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the uniform Cauchy source row, located common modulus, finite windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, Real seal, and structural rows.

The located-modulus obligation is the first analytic route. A tolerance request reads the uniform Cauchy source $U$ and then the common modulus row $M$, so the later window and readback rows are tied to a displayed threshold rather than to a host-selected limit.

Finite-window readback and dyadic accounting then proceed through $S,R,D$. The stream window is opened at the selected threshold, the regular rational readback is extracted from that window, and the dyadic ledger records the tolerance comparison before the terminal Real seal can be consumed.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed packet. Since quotient limits, selected host reals, countable choice, open-cover compactness, and ambient completeness are absent from the carrier, no local obligation can export them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniform Cauchy limit completion handoff]
\label{thm:located-uniform-cauchy-limit-completion-handoff}

$$

\longmapsto
\BishopCauchyModulusLimitUp
\longmapsto

\longmapsto

$$
The handoff supplies a located modulus, finite windows, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance evidence, terminal Real sealing, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it supplies no quotient completion, selected representative, host equality of limits, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Open the carrier of \autoref{def:located-uniform-cauchy-limit-carrier}. Its analytic rows are $U,M,S,R,D,E$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve only that displayed order.

Apply the NameCert obligations above. They force the uniform Cauchy source and located modulus to be read before the finite window, regular readback, dyadic tolerance, and Real-seal rows are available.

The stated route is exactly the row order of the packet. Because the refused objects have no carrier coordinate and no provenance row, every completion-facing read remains a finite located-modulus handoff.
\end{proof}
