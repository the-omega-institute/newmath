\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DyadicCauchyCompletionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-cauchy-completion-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive real completion by regular Cauchy data, the $\DyadicCauchyCompletionUp$ packet records the dyadic-window completion surface as finite BEDC naming data. It sits over the signed dyadic arithmetic rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational Cauchy rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the dense-unit completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-dense-unit-namecert}. The packet names dyadic approximants, tolerance ledgers, regular-Cauchy readback, terminal real sealing, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not quotient sequences, choose representatives, assert ambient real completeness, or import a host metric completion theorem.

\begin{definition}[Dyadic Cauchy completion carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-cauchy-completion-carrier}
A $\DyadicCauchyCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(D,W,E,R,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ arithmetic row for signed dyadic endpoints, shared denominators, and finite comparison ledgers.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row listing the inspected dyadic stream segment and its source indices.
\item $E$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger, recording finite radius bounds and common-scale refinements for the displayed windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-Cauchy readback row obtained from the same dyadic windows and tolerance ledger.
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal naming the completed real endpoint reached by the regular-Cauchy readback.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transports for dyadic arithmetic, stream windows, tolerance ledgers, regular-Cauchy readback, terminal real sealing, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ routes from dyadic windows to tolerance ledgers, from tolerance ledgers to regular-Cauchy readback, and from regular-Cauchy readback to the terminal real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicCauchyCompletionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyCompletionDenseUnitUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicCauchyCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,W,E,R,S,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient sequence equality, selected Cauchy representatives, arbitrary real limits, host metric completion, or analytic completeness outside the displayed finite rows.
\end{definition}
\leandef{BEDC.Derived.DyadicCauchyCompletionUp}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic Cauchy completion obligation surface]
\label{thm:dyadic-cauchy-completion-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\DyadicCauchyCompletionUp$ carrier $K=(D,W,E,R,S,H,C,P,N)$, the public obligation surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the dyadic arithmetic row $D$, stream-window row $W$, tolerance ledger $E$, regular-Cauchy readback row $R$, terminal real seal $S$, componentwise transport row $H$, displayed replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-cauchy-completion-carrier}. Its nine coordinates are the only rows available to a consumer of the packet.

Read $D,W,E$ first. These rows expose finite dyadic arithmetic, finite stream windows, and the tolerance ledger that compares the inspected windows at common dyadic scales.

Next read $R$ and $S$. The row $R$ is the regular-Cauchy readback obtained from the displayed dyadic data, and $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for that readback. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the same route.

Since the carrier contains no quotient equality of sequences, selected representative, unrestricted limit operator, host metric completion, or analytic completeness theorem, the listed rows exhaust the obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic completion sibling bridge]
\label{thm:dyadic-cauchy-completion-sibling-bridge}
Every accepted $\DyadicCauchyCompletionUp$ packet factors its completion read through the sibling lattice
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp,\ \CauchyCompletionDenseUnitUp .
$$
The dyadic arithmetic row is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite window row through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular-Cauchy readback through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real seal through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the dense-unit completion boundary through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-dense-unit-namecert} before the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicCauchyCompletionUp}$ row is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:dyadic-cauchy-completion-obligation-surface}. The rows $D,W,E,R,S$ are visible before the structural rows can expose the local certificate.

The row $D$ supplies the signed dyadic arithmetic and common-scale comparison surface. The row $W$ supplies the finite stream-window request, and $E$ records the tolerance ledger that lets those windows be read at a shared dyadic scale.

The row $R$ then routes the same data through $\RegSeqRatUp$, while $S$ records the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The dense-unit completion chapter supplies the completion boundary that keeps this read as a displayed finite completion packet rather than a quotient or host limit.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name that sibling route. Therefore any completion consumer receives the dyadic, stream, regular-Cauchy, real-seal, and dense-unit rows before the local name certificate is public.
\end{proof}
