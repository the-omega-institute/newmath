Following Blakers and Massey on homotopy excision, the $\BlakersMasseyUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which two highly connected subobject rows and their intersection row are handed to an excision consumer. It sits after the $\HomotopyUp$ deformation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homotopy-namecert}, the $\SimplicialSetUp$ face-and-degeneracy ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-simplicialset-namecert}, the $\HomologyUp$ chain-read surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homology-namecert}, the van Kampen finite pushout route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-van-kampen-namecert}, and the suspension connectivity handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-freudenthal-suspension-namecert}. The packet names a displayed homotopy-excision handoff only; it does not import ambient model-category factorization, quotient topology, selected homotopy groups, a spectral sequence, or a host Blakers--Massey theorem.

\begin{definition}[Blakers--Massey carrier]
\label{def:blakers-massey-carrier}
A $\BlakersMasseyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(X,A,C,I,S,H_*,V,F,T,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the ambient gluing row, recording the finite union or pushout window whose two subobject faces are inspected.
\item $A$ and $C$ are the two subobject rows, read through $\HomotopyUp$ deformation data and through the local face ledger of the ambient packet.
\item $I$ is the intersection row, recording the displayed overlap, endpoint compatibility, and basepoint or boundary rows shared by $A$ and $C$.
\item $S$ is the $\SimplicialSetUp$ row carrying the finite face, horn, and degeneracy itinerary used to display the excision square.
\item $H_*$ is the $\HomologyUp$ read window used only as a finite chain-level sanity check for the inspected degrees.
\item $V$ is the $\VanKampenUp$ sibling row, recording the already displayed pushout route for fundamental group style reads before higher homotopy-excision vocabulary is consumed.
\item $F$ is the $\FreudenthalSuspensionUp$ sibling row, recording the stable-range connectivity handoff used when a suspension-facing consumer asks for the same excision boundary.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the ambient, subobject, overlap, simplicial, homotopy, homology, van Kampen, Freudenthal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an excision request through $X,A,C,I,S,H_*,V,F,T,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BlakersMasseyUp$, $\HomotopyUp$, $\SimplicialSetUp$, $\HomologyUp$, $\VanKampenUp$, $\FreudenthalSuspensionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BlakersMasseyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,A,C,I,S,H_*,V,F,T,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host homotopy pushout theorem, selected homotopy-group element, quotient space, model-category cofibration replacement, spectral-sequence convergence claim, or unbounded connectivity proof.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Blakers--Massey NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:blakers-massey-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BlakersMasseyUp$ carrier
$$
B=(X,A,C,I,S,H_*,V,F,T,R,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BlakersMasseyUp}$ surface consists exactly of ambient gluing admission through $X$, subobject exposure through $A$ and $C$, intersection exposure through $I$, finite simplicial itinerary exposure through $S$, homotopy deformation reads through the displayed sibling rows, homology-window sanity checks through $H_*$, van Kampen pushout handoff through $V$, Freudenthal suspension handoff through $F$, componentwise transport through $T$, displayed replay through $R$, provenance through $P$, local naming through $N$, and non-escape from those displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The rows $X,A,C,I$ supply the ambient square, the two subobject faces, and the overlap boundary, while $S$ supplies the finite simplicial itinerary used to inspect that square.

Read the sibling rows in dependency order. The homotopy-facing data is consumed through the displayed $\HomotopyUp$ rows attached to $A,C,I$; the $\HomologyUp$ row $H_*$ supplies only a finite chain-read window; the $\VanKampenUp$ row $V$ supplies the lower pushout route; and the $\FreudenthalSuspensionUp$ row $F$ supplies the suspension connectivity handoff.

Finally apply the structural rows. Transport $T$ and replay $R$ preserve exactly the rows already listed, and $P,N$ only source and name the packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for ambient quotient topology, chosen homotopy groups, model-category replacement, spectral-sequence convergence, or a host Blakers--Massey theorem, none of those objects can be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:blakers-massey-excision-handoff}

The handoff is therefore row-preserving. The rows $H_*,V,F$ are read as displayed dependency anchors, not as ambient theorem oracles; $T,R,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet.
