It sits between the $\LpSpaceUp$ carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lp-space-namecert}, the modulus route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-modulus-of-convexity-namecert}, the uniformly convex consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformly-convex-space-namecert}, and the terminal scalar comparisons of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only finite norm-window rows, conjugate-exponent comparison rows, midpoint-drop rows, Real tolerance seals, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data; it does not import host Banach-space equality, selected extremizers, measure-theoretic quotient equality, or a completed ambient Clarkson theorem.

\begin{definition}[Clarkson inequality carrier]
\label{def:clarkson-inequality-carrier}
A $\ClarksonInequalityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(L,N,E,M,D,R,T,Q,P,A)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the inspected $\LpSpaceUp$ row, including the finite norm windows for the two displayed vectors.
\item $N$ is the norm-comparison row that reads $\|x+y\|$, $\|x-y\|$, $\|x\|$, and $\|y\|$ only through the local $L^p$ windows.
\item $E$ is the exponent ledger separating the $1<p\leq 2$ and $2\leq p<\infty$ Clarkson regimes as finite case data.
\item $M$ is the midpoint-drop row that packages the inequality output as a modulus-facing convexity witness.
\item $D$ is the $\ModulusOfConvexityUp$ handoff row that records the extracted tolerance budget.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for the scalar comparisons and tolerance inequalities.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $L,N,E,M,D,R$.
\item $Q$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the norm-window, exponent, midpoint, modulus, and Real-seal reads.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClarksonInequalityUp$, $\LpSpaceUp$, $\ModulusOfConvexityUp$, $\UniformlyConvexSpaceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $A$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClarksonInequalityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows and has no coordinate for host norm equality, selected extremizers, quotient classes of functions, or an ambient Banach-space completion theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Clarkson inequality NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:clarkson-inequality-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClarksonInequalityUp$ carrier
$$
C=(L,N,E,M,D,R,T,Q,P,A),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ClarksonInequalityUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the $L^p$ row, norm-window comparison, exponent-regime ledger, midpoint-drop witness, modulus handoff, Real tolerance seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local naming. No obligation row is stored outside the displayed packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:clarkson-inequality-carrier}. The rows $L,N,E,M,D,R,T,Q,P,A$ are the complete inspection surface, so admission and classifier comparison are componentwise reads from the finite packet.

Read the analytic content through the sibling rows. The $\LpSpaceUp$ row supplies the inspected norm windows, $E$ selects the finite Clarkson regime, $N$ records the displayed norm comparisons, and $M$ packages the resulting midpoint drop.

The modulus and scalar routes are downstream. Row $D$ hands the drop to $\ModulusOfConvexityUp$, row $R$ seals the scalar tolerance through $\RealUp$, and $T,Q,P,A$ only transport, replay, source, and name this displayed route. Since none of the rows contains a host equality of functions, a selected extremizer, a quotient norm class, or an ambient completion theorem, the listed clauses exhaust the local obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Clarkson inequality uniform-convexity route]
\label{thm:clarkson-inequality-uniform-convexity-route}
Every $\ClarksonInequalityUp$ packet that exports a uniform-convexity read must first expose the route
$$
\LpSpaceUp,\quad \ClarksonInequalityUp,\quad \ModulusOfConvexityUp,\quad \UniformlyConvexSpaceUp,\quad \RealUp

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:clarkson-inequality-namecert-obligations}. They force the consumer to expose the $L^p$ norm windows, exponent-regime row, midpoint-drop row, modulus handoff, and Real tolerance seal before local naming.

The uniformly convex read is therefore downstream of the displayed Clarkson packet. The $\ModulusOfConvexityUp$ row receives the finite drop witness, and the $\UniformlyConvexSpaceUp$ row consumes that witness only after the Real tolerance comparisons have been sealed.

Nothing in the carrier provides host norm equality, selected extremizers, quotient function equality, or an ambient completed Banach theorem. The route exports exactly the finite modulus-facing witness displayed by the packet.
\end{proof}
