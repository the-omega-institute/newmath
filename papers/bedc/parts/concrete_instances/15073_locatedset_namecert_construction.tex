\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedSetUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-locatedset-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, a located subset of a metric space is not merely a subset predicate: it is a constructive distance interface from ambient points to the set, readable at finite rational or real tolerances. The $\LocatedSetUp$ packet records this standard object on the L10 real and completion frontier. It sits over the parent metric surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the admission discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-subspace-namecert}, the located-distance surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedmetric-namecert}, and the real terminal seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The carrier gives compactness, total-boundedness, completion, and constructive-analysis consumers a displayed located-subset dependency without importing open-cover compactness, choice-selected nearest points, quotient subset equality, or full $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[LocatedSet carrier]
\label{def:located-set-carrier}
A $\LocatedSetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(X,A,Q,R,E,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the parent $\MetricUp$ row, including the point classifier, distance witness, metric-law ledger, and finite observation histories.
\item $A$ is the $\MetricSubspaceUp$ admission row. It records which parent observations are admitted as members of the displayed set and exposes no host subset equality or quotient membership.
\item $Q$ is the located-distance ledger. For each accepted ambient point and positive rational tolerance request, it records the finite evidence used to locate the distance from the point to the admitted set.
\item $R$ is the rational distance row. It packages the finite lower and upper rational comparison windows supplied by $Q$ and keeps them tied to the same parent metric observations from $X$ and admission data from $A$.
\item $E$ is the real distance row. It seals the compatible rational windows as a $\RealUp$ distance name only after the parent metric, subspace admission, located-distance ledger, and rational rows have all been displayed.
\item $T$ is the located-metric handoff row. It records that any consumer requiring a located metric route reads the parent metric witness, located-distance ledger, rational windows, and real seal through the interface named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedmetric-namecert}.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the parent metric row, admission row, located-distance ledger, rational and real distance rows, located-metric handoff, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming row.
\item $C$ stores $\Cont$ replay from parent point observations to subspace admission, from admission to located-distance comparisons, from rational windows to the real distance seal, and from the seal to located-metric, completion, compactness, or total-boundedness consumers.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedSetUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\MetricSubspaceUp$, $\LocatedMetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedSetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares located-set packets only through the displayed tuple $X,A,Q,R,E,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, choice-selected nearest points, quotient subset equality, an independently completed ambient space, or full $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}
