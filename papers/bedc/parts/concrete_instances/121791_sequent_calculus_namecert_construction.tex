\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SequentCalculusUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sequent-calculus-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Gentzen's sequent calculi for structural proof theory, the $\SequentCalculusUp$ packet records sequents, inference rows, structural rules, proof trees, and cut-free boundary data as finite BEDC naming evidence. It sits beside the first-order syntax surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-firstorder-namecert}, the model-theory satisfaction packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-modeltheory-namecert}, and the MetaCIC critical-path route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metaciccriticalpath-namecert}. The packet supplies a proof-calculus substrate for later proof-theoretic consumers; it is not host proof syntax, a quotient of derivations, a global cut-elimination theorem, or a semantic completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Sequent calculus carrier]
\label{def:sequent-calculus-carrier}
A $\SequentCalculusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(F,\Gamma,\Delta,R,T,K,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the formula-source row, read through the first-order syntax surface before any sequent endpoint is public.
\item $\Gamma$ is the antecedent-context row, listing the finite left-hand formula window of each displayed sequent.
\item $\Delta$ is the succedent-context row, listing the finite right-hand formula window of each displayed sequent.
\item $R$ is the rule-ledger row, naming the initial, logical, and structural rule entries accepted by the packet.
\item $T$ is the proof-tree row, recording finite inference trees whose leaves and conclusions are sequents formed from $F,\Gamma,\Delta,R$.
\item $K$ is the cut-boundary row, recording which displayed trees are read as cut-free and which trees still contain an explicit cut occurrence.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for formula, context, rule, tree, cut-boundary, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a proof-calculus request through $F,\Gamma,\Delta,R,T,K,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SequentCalculusUp$, $\FirstOrderUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SequentCalculusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for host derivation equality, proof-term quotation, quotienting of derivations, semantic validity, completeness, global cut elimination, or normalization.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sequent calculus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sequent-calculus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SequentCalculusUp$ carrier $S=(F,\Gamma,\Delta,R,T,K,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SequentCalculusUp}$ surface consists exactly of formula-source admission, antecedent-context display, succedent-context display, rule-ledger admission, proof-tree readability, cut-boundary visibility, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every proof-calculus consumer must read the rows $F,\Gamma,\Delta,R,T,K$ before exporting any sequent derivation route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sequent-calculus-carrier}. The formula and context rows $F,\Gamma,\Delta$ determine the sequents that may be inspected, while $R$ determines the finite rule ledger available to proof-tree consumers.

Read $T$ only after those rows. A proof tree is public as a displayed finite inference tree over the visible sequents and rule entries, not as host syntax or an unrecorded derivation object.

Finally read $K,H,C,P,N$. The cut-boundary row keeps cut occurrences explicit, while transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same packet. Since semantic validity, completeness, quotient derivation equality, and global cut elimination are absent coordinates, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sequent calculus cut-free scope]
\label{thm:sequent-calculus-cut-free-scope}
For every accepted $\SequentCalculusUp$ carrier $S=(F,\Gamma,\Delta,R,T,K,H,C,P,N)$, a cut-free read is scoped to the displayed proof-tree row $T$ and the cut-boundary row $K$. If $K$ marks a tree as cut-free, downstream consumers may use that tree only through the same formula, context, rule, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows $F,\Gamma,\Delta,R,H,C,P,N$. The packet does not transform arbitrary cut-containing trees into cut-free trees and does not assert global cut elimination.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:sequent-calculus-namecert-obligations}. They force every proof-calculus read to pass through the formula source, antecedent and succedent contexts, rule ledger, proof-tree row, and cut-boundary row.

The cut-free claim is therefore a scope claim about an already displayed row of the carrier. A consumer may replay the cut-free marking only together with the same tree and rule ledger; it cannot infer a cut-free replacement for another tree.

Transport and continuation replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the displayed rows componentwise, while $P,N$ source and name the same packet. Since the carrier contains no global cut-elimination coordinate, no semantic completeness row, and no quotient of derivations, the public cut-free surface remains exactly the finite boundary recorded by $K$ over $T$.
\end{proof}
