\chapter{The Lucas-Cube Disjoint-Pair Silver Certificate}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-lucas-cube-disjoint-pair-silver}
\origin{ai}

The Lucas cube $\Lambda_m$ is the set of cyclic binary words $x=(x_0,\ldots,x_{m-1})$ with no adjacent $1$s, including the wraparound adjacency between $x_{m-1}$ and $x_0$. Its vertex count is the Lucas number $L_m$. This chapter records a second finite count on the same golden-mean carrier: the ordered disjoint-pair count
$$
D_m=\#\{(x,y)\in\Lambda_m^2:\ x_i y_i=0\ \text{for every }i\}.
$$
The disjoint condition removes the local state $(1,1)$, so each coordinate pair has one of the three states $00,01,10$. In the row and column order $00,01,10$, adjacency around the cycle is governed by the transfer matrix
$$
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&1\\
1&0&1\\
1&1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The trace of $M^m$ counts closed walks of length $m$ in this three-state automaton, which is exactly the same thing as a cyclic pair of disjoint Lucas-cube words. Thus
$$
D_m=\operatorname{tr}(M^m).
$$

\section{Trace And Silver Spectrum}
\label{sec:lucas-cube-disjoint-pair-silver-trace}

The characteristic polynomial is
$$
\det(tI-M)=(t+1)(t^2-2t-1)=t^3-t^2-3t-1.
$$
The eigenvalues are
$$
\{-1,\ 1+\sqrt2,\ 1-\sqrt2\}.
$$
The two roots $1\pm\sqrt2$ are the silver/Pell spectral pair, with the positive root $\delta=1+\sqrt2$ satisfying $\delta^2=2\delta+1$. Therefore the trace count has the closed form
$$
D_m=(-1)^m+(1+\sqrt2)^m+(1-\sqrt2)^m,
$$
and Cayley--Hamilton forces the order-three recurrence
$$
D_{m+3}=D_{m+2}+3D_{m+1}+D_m.
$$
The first ten checked values are
$$
D_1,\ldots,D_{10}=1,7,13,35,81,199,477,1155,2785,6727.
$$
