\label{def:regular-cauchy-cofinal-modulus-carrier}

$$

\item $K$ is the cofinal schedule row, recording the finite monotone route along which the source is read.
\item $U$ is the modulus row that assigns a displayed request bound to the cofinal schedule.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row used by the modulus.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row opened by the cofinal schedule.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row on those windows.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,K,U,D,W,R,E$ and the structural rows.

\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchyCofinalModulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-cofinal-modulus-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-cofinal-modulus-carrier}. Its analytic route is $S,K,U,D,W,R,E$, and its structural route is $H,C,P,N$.

The source row $S$ is read only through the cofinal schedule $K$. The modulus row $U$ then binds each displayed request to the same cofinal route, while the dyadic tolerance row $D$ records the requested error surface.

The window and readback rows $W$ and $R$ expose the finite StreamName and RegSeqRat data before the terminal Real seal $E$ is consumed. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve this same packet through $H,C,P,N$.

Since the carrier contains no row for quotient stream equality, representatives, choice, host equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligation surface.
