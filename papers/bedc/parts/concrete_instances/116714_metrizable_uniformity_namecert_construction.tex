\label{thm:metrizable-uniformity-entourage-basis}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no host topology theorem, quotient completion equality, selected infinite schedule, countable-choice selector, or ambient completeness coordinate occurs in $K$, the exported uniformity basis is exactly the finite metric-ball basis named by the carrier.
\end{proof}
