\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\MatroidDualUp$}

Following Whitney, the $\MatroidDualUp$ packet records matroid duality as finite complement-basis and rank-nullity NameCert data. It reads the independent-set and exchange rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matroid-namecert}, the meet/join dependency surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lattice-namecert}, and the finite algebraic transport discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}. The packet names a dual carrier over the same finite ground; it does not import a host duality theorem, quotient ground equality, selected bases outside the displayed finite rows, or an ambient rank oracle.

\begin{definition}[Matroid dual carrier]
\label{def:matroid-dual-carrier}
A $\MatroidDualUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(E,M,B,Q,R,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the finite ground row, read through the same $\FinSetUp$ ground discipline used by $\MatroidUp$.
\item $M$ is the source $\MatroidUp$ row, including independent-set, hereditary, and exchange data.
\item $B$ is the finite basis row for the source matroid, recording maximal independent subsets over the displayed ground.
\item $Q$ is the complement-basis row, assigning to each displayed source basis its finite complement in $E$ and reading that complement as a dual basis.
\item $R$ is the rank/nullity ledger, recording the finite rank of source basis rows and the nullity read used by the dual packet.
\item $U$ is the dual independence row, generated from finite subsets of complement-basis rows and transported through the source exchange route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for ground, source matroid, basis, complement-basis, rank/nullity, dual independence, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for every consumer that reads the dual packet.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MatroidDualUp$, $\MatroidUp$, $\FinSetUp$, $\LatticeUp$, $\GroupUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and local naming rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MatroidDualUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host dual matroid, a quotient ground set, a selected global basis family, an external rank function, or an ambient linear representation.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Matroid dual NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:matroid-dual-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MatroidDualUp$ carrier
$$
D=(E,M,B,Q,R,U,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MatroidDualUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $D$, finite ground exposure through $E$, source $\MatroidUp$ exposure through $M$, source-basis exposure through $B$, complement-basis exposure through $Q$, rank/nullity ledger exposure through $R$, dual independence exposure through $U$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The rows $E,M,B,Q,R,U$ are the complete finite duality inventory: ground, source matroid, source bases, complement bases, rank/nullity data, and dual independence.

Read the source matroid through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matroid-namecert}. The hereditary and exchange rows are available only as displayed $\MatroidUp$ certificate rows, while the ground and finite subset reads remain inside $\FinSetUp$ and $\LatticeUp$ dependency surfaces.

The complement-basis row $Q$ is then checked against $E$ and $B$. Each dual basis is the finite complement of a displayed source basis, and the rank/nullity row $R$ records the finite handoff by which source rank and complement size determine the dual rank read.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no coordinate stores a host dual matroid, quotient ground set, selected global basis family, external rank oracle, or ambient representation, none of those objects can discharge a local $\NameCert_{\MatroidDualUp}$ obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Matroid dual rank-nullity handoff]
\label{thm:matroid-dual-rank-nullity-handoff}
Every rank-facing consumer of an accepted $\MatroidDualUp$ packet must read the finite route
$$
E,M,B,Q,R \longmapsto U,H,C,P,N
$$
before exporting a dual rank or independence claim. The source rank row is read from the displayed $\MatroidUp$ basis data, the nullity row is read from finite complements inside $E$, and the dual independence row is admitted only after the complement-basis and rank/nullity rows have been displayed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the source $\MatroidUp$ row. The basis row $B$ is meaningful only after the finite ground $E$ and the source independent-set certificate $M$ have been displayed.

Next open the complement-basis row. The row $Q$ compares each displayed basis with its finite complement in $E$, so the dual basis data is a $\BHist$ complement ledger rather than a selected basis outside the packet.

The rank/nullity row $R$ is downstream of this complement read. It records the finite handoff from source rank to dual rank by counting displayed basis and complement rows, not by invoking an ambient rank theorem.

The dual independence row $U$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve exactly this route. Thus a rank-facing consumer cannot export a dual rank or independence claim before reading the source basis, complement basis, rank/nullity, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert rows.
\end{proof}
