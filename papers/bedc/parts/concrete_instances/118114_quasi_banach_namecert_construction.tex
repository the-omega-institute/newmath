\item $Z$ is the scalar action row when scalar multiplication is part of the displayed quasi-normed structure.

\begin{theorem}[QuasiBanach NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:quasi-banach-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:quasi-banach-carrier}. The rows $X,A,Z,M,T,F,E,C,H,R,P,N$ are the only available coordinates of the accepted packet.

The algebraic and metric rows are displayed before any completion-facing read. Addition, zero, and scalar action are read through $A$ and $Z$, while the quasi-norm distance ledger is read through $M$.

The non-Banach obligation is concentrated in $T$. Each inspected triple carries the displayed quasi-triangle constant and rational bound rows, so a consumer may use quasi-triangle control but cannot replace it by a norm triangle inequality or local convexity theorem.

The completion-facing rows are downstream. The Cauchy-filter basis $F$, embedding row $E$, and constructive completion route $C$ may be read only after the quasi-norm metric and quasi-triangle rows are visible.

Finally $H,R,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no row stores a Banach norm, Hahn--Banach separation, quotient completion, selected limit, or ambient real-completeness datum, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
