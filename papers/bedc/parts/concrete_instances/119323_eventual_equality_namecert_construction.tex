\begin{theorem}[Eventual equality NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:eventual-equality-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EventualEqualityUp$ carrier
$$
E=(L,R,T,A,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\EventualEqualityUp}$ surface consists exactly of two stream-window admissions, one finite tail-cut ledger, one post-cut agreement row, regular rational readback, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every equality-facing read must pass through
$$

$$
and through no quotient stream equality, selected representative, hidden host equality, countable-choice selector, or ambient completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:eventual-equality-carrier}. The rows $L,R,T,A,Q$ expose the two finite windows, the cofinite tail cut, the agreement row, and regular rational readback before any equality-facing consumer can read the packet.

Thus the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface. Since the carrier contains no quotient, selector, choice, host-equality, or ambient-completeness field, no downstream read can obtain such data from the $\EventualEqualityUp$ packet.
\end{proof}
