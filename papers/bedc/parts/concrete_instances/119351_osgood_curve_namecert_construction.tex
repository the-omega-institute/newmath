\begin{definition}[OsgoodCurve carrier]
\label{def:osgood-curve-carrier}
An $\OsgoodCurveUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(K,I,S,R,M,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the compact metric source row, read through the $\CompactMetricUp$ interface;
\item $I$ is the interval or curve-parameter window row, stored as finite $\StreamNameUp$ observations;
\item $S$ is the regular rational sampling schedule for the curve coordinates, read through $\RegSeqRatUp$ before any terminal $\RealUp$ seal is exposed;
\item $R$ is the displayed $\RealUp$ coordinate-readback row for the continuous image;
\item $M$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ modulus and graph-window row tying the samples in $S$ to the compact source $K$;
\item $A$ is the finite positive-area witness row, consisting of dyadic rectangle windows, lower-area ledger entries, and the displayed cover-overlap exclusions used by the packet;
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transports for compact-source, parameter-window, regular-sample, real-readback, continuity, positive-area, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ stores the displayed $\Cont$ routes for reading the compact source, replaying the coordinate samples, checking the continuity window, and exposing the positive-area witness;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BHist$, $\msame$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, compact metric rows, stream rows, regular rational rows, real rows, continuous-map rows, positive-area ledgers, and the surrounding $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\OsgoodCurveUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no host planar measure space, no ambient completeness theorem for $\RealUp$, no open-cover compactness principle, no quotient equality of curves, and no selected parametrization beyond the displayed stream and regular-rational rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[OsgoodCurve NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:osgood-curve-namecert-obligations}
The local $\NameCert_{\OsgoodCurveUp}$ surface for the carrier of \autoref{def:osgood-curve-carrier} consists of six finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item compact source: the accepted packet displays the compact metric source row $K$ and the parameter-window row $I$ through the cited compact and stream interfaces;
\item regular coordinate readback: every coordinate read passes through the regular rational schedule $S$ before the terminal real row $R$ is consumed;
\item continuity window: the row $M$ supplies the finite modulus and graph-window evidence required by the $\ContinuousMapUp$ route;
\item positive-area witness: the row $A$ lists the dyadic rectangles, lower-area ledger entries, and overlap exclusions that witness the displayed positive-area claim at the certificate level;
\item transport and replay: reads of $K,I,S,R,M,A$ transport through $H$ and replay only along the displayed continuation routes $C$;
\item provenance: the package row $P$ and local naming row $N$ bind the preceding rows as one $\OsgoodCurveUp$ NameCert packet.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the packet offers a finite Osgood-curve certificate for Real and Completion consumers, not a host theorem that every classical positive-area curve has been reconstructed inside BEDC.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:osgood-curve-carrier}. The compact source, parameter windows, regular coordinate samples, real readback rows, continuity evidence, positive-area ledger, transports, continuation routes, provenance, and local name are all displayed coordinates of the same finite packet.

The coordinate-readback obligation is finite because the stream and regular-rational rows $I,S$ are the only schedules through which $R$ may be read. The continuity obligation is finite because $M$ stores the displayed modulus and graph-window rows rather than a host function-space equality.

The positive-area obligation reads only the dyadic rectangles and lower-area ledger entries stored in $A$. Overlap exclusions and lower bounds are replayed through $C$ and transported through $H$, so the certificate never imports an ambient measure algebra or selected measurable representative.

Finally $P$ and $N$ bind the displayed rows as one local NameCert surface. The excluded host objects have no coordinate in $O$, so every Real or Completion consumer receives only the finite Osgood-curve data stated above.
\end{proof}
