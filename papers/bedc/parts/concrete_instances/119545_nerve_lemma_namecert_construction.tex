\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\NerveLemmaUp$}

Following Borsuk and the algebraic-topology formulation in Hatcher, the $\NerveLemmaUp$ packet records the finite good-cover route by which a compact metric cover, its finite nerve, a simplicial-complex carrier, and a homology consumer are read as one NameCert bridge. It sits after the compact source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite cover nerve of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cover-nerve-namecert}, the finite face inventory of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-simplicialcomplex-namecert}, the homology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homology-namecert}, and the dimension-control route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-coveringdimension-namecert}. The packet names only displayed finite good-cover rows, nerve-face rows, chain-handoff rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not import open-cover choice, quotient homotopy classes, selected partitions of unity, or an ambient homotopy equivalence theorem.

\begin{definition}[Nerve lemma carrier]
\label{def:nerve-lemma-carrier}
A $\NerveLemmaUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(K,G,N,S,H,D,T,R,C,P,Q)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, displaying the inspected compact metric carrier and the finite cover window used by the packet.
\item $G$ is the good-cover ledger, recording for each displayed finite intersection the contractibility witness row accepted by the packet.
\item $N$ is the $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$ row, exposing cover membership, overlap incidence, and accepted finite nerve faces.
\item $S$ is the $\SimplicialComplexUp$ finite face carrier read from the accepted nerve faces.
\item $H$ is the $\HomologyUp$ chain-and-cycle handoff row consumed after the simplicial face carrier is displayed.
\item $D$ is the $\CoveringDimensionUp$ dimension-control row, bounding the inspected finite intersection depth and face dimension read by the consumer.
\item $T$ is the finite chain-transport row from cover-intersection data to nerve-chain data.
\item $R$ records shortcut refusals for arbitrary open covers, selected subcovers, quotient homotopy classes, partitions of unity, and ambient host equality.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through good-cover admission, finite nerve formation, simplicial face inspection, homology handoff, and dimension control.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NerveLemmaUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$, $\SimplicialComplexUp$, $\HomologyUp$, $\CoveringDimensionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $Q$ is the local $\NameCert_{\NerveLemmaUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,G,N,S,H,D,T,R,C,P,Q$. It has no coordinate for an unrestricted open cover, a selected refinement, a quotient homotopy type, a chosen partition of unity, or equality in an ambient topological host.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Nerve lemma NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:nerve-lemma-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\NerveLemmaUp$ carrier
$$
L=(K,G,N,S,H,D,T,R,C,P,Q),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\NerveLemmaUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact metric source admission through $K$, finite good-cover intersection exposure through $G$, finite nerve readback through $N$, simplicial face admission through $S$, homology handoff through $H$, covering-dimension control through $D$, finite chain transport through $T$, shortcut refusal through $R$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $Q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:nerve-lemma-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the compact metric source, the finite good-cover ledger, the finite cover nerve, the simplicial face carrier, the homology handoff, the dimension-control row, finite transport, refusal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The cover and nerve obligations are exposed through $K,G,N,S$. The packet first fixes the compact metric source and finite cover window, then records the good-cover intersection rows, then reads the finite nerve faces as a simplicial-complex carrier.

The consumer obligations are exposed through $H,D,T$. Homology is reached only after the finite face carrier is displayed, and the dimension-control row records the finite intersection depth inspected by the same packet.

The structural obligations are exposed through $R,C,P,Q$. These rows keep the route inside displayed finite data and block any rereading through arbitrary open covers, quotient homotopy classes, selected partitions of unity, or host equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Nerve lemma finite-cover homology handoff]
\label{thm:nerve-lemma-finite-cover-homology-handoff}
Every $\HomologyUp$ row exported by an accepted $\NerveLemmaUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
K,G \longmapsto N,S \longmapsto H,D,T,R,C,P,Q .
$$
Thus the homology-facing consumer first reads the compact metric source and good-cover ledger, then the finite cover nerve and simplicial face carrier, and only then receives the homology handoff with dimension control, finite transport, shortcut refusal, replay, provenance, and local naming. The route exports no open-cover choice principle, selected refinement theorem, quotient homotopy equality, partition-of-unity construction, or ambient homotopy-equivalence theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with $K,G$. The compact metric row supplies the finite cover window, and the good-cover ledger records only the displayed finite intersections accepted by the packet.

Next read $N,S$. The finite cover nerve supplies cover membership, overlap incidence, and accepted faces; the simplicial-complex row turns those accepted faces into the finite face carrier consumed downstream.

Finally read $H,D,T,R,C,P,Q$. The homology handoff is downstream of the finite nerve and simplicial carrier, while dimension control, finite transport, refusal, replay, provenance, and local naming keep the consumer attached to the same finite packet.
\end{proof}
