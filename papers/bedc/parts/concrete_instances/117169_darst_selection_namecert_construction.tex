\begin{theorem}[Darst selection regulated compact handoff]
\label{thm:darst-selection-regulated-compact-handoff}
Every compact selection read from an accepted $\DarstSelectionUp$ packet factors through the ordered route
$$
\RegulatedRealFunctionUp \longmapsto \BoundedRealVariationUp \longmapsto \RegulatedFunctionCompactOscillationUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
Concretely, the consumer must first expose the regulated source row $G$, then the bounded-variation budget $V$, then the compact oscillation handoff $O$, then the selector windows $W$, then the regular rational readback $Q$, and only then the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ with $H,C,P,N$ attached.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the source side. The regulated source row $G$ supplies finite step-window and endpoint-control data, while the bounded-variation budget $V$ supplies only the displayed partition budget needed by the selection route.

The compactness handoff is the row $O$. It binds the regulated source and variation budget to compact oscillation windows before any selector window can be consumed.

The selected output is then read through $W,Q,E$ in order. The selector row $W$ records retained finite windows, $Q$ gives the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and $E$ seals the corresponding $\RealUp$ windows.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are supplied by $H,C,P,N$. Because the carrier has no additional analytic coordinate, every compact selection read stays inside the displayed finite route.
\end{proof}
