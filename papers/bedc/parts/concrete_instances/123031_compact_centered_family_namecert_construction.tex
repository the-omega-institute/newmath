Following Kelley and Bishop--Bridges compact metric practice, the $\CompactCenteredFamilyUp$ packet records a compact-metric closed-family read by finite centered witnesses rather than by a host powerset of closed subsets. It sits beside the compact metric certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite-intersection property row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-intersection-property-namecert}, and the finite-intersection compactness packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-intersection-compactness-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Compact centered family carrier]
\label{def:compact-centered-family-carrier}
A $\CompactCenteredFamilyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(K,F,I,W,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the compact-metric source row, read through $\CompactMetricUp$ before any closed-family row is consumed;
\item $F$ is the finite closed-family window, listing the inspected closed rows and their compact source coordinate;
\item $I$ is the centeredness ledger, recording that every displayed finite subfamily in the window has a displayed nonempty meet row;
\item $W$ is the finite-subfamily witness row returned to a consumer that asks for one inspected meet;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport across $K,F,I,W,R,P,N$;
\item $R$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay route from compact-metric source data to the closed-family window, centeredness ledger, and witness row;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactMetricUp$, $\FiniteIntersectionPropertyUp$, $\FiniteIntersectionCompactnessUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactCenteredFamilyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an arbitrary closed subset of the host space, no open-cover compactness theorem, no selected global intersection point, no choice functional, and no ambient completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact centered family NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-centered-family-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-centered-family-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactCenteredFamilyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for
$$
C=(K,F,I,W,H,R,P,N),
$$
compact-metric source visibility through $K$, closed-family window visibility through $F$, centeredness readback through $I$, finite-subfamily witness readback through $W$, componentwise transport through $H$, replay through $R$, sibling provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the finite packet $C$. The rows $K,F,I,W$ are the only mathematical data rows exposed to a compact-family consumer, and they appear in the order compact source, displayed closed-family window, centeredness ledger, and finite witness.

Read the structural rows next. The transport row $H$ compares the displayed coordinates componentwise, while $R$ replays only the route from compact source data through the closed-family window into the centeredness and witness rows.

Finally package the same rows with $P$ and $N$. Since the provenance cites the compact metric, finite-intersection property, and finite-intersection compactness packets, the NameCert surface contains no additional coordinate for host powersets, open-cover compactness, selected global intersection points, choice functionals, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact centered family finite-subfamily witness]
\label{thm:compact-centered-family-finite-subfamily-witness}
Every accepted $\CompactCenteredFamilyUp$ carrier that exposes a finite inspected subfamily must return its witness through the displayed route
$$
K,F,I,W,H,R,P,N.
$$
The route reads the compact-metric row $K$, the finite closed-family window $F$, and the centeredness ledger $I$ before the witness row $W$ can be consumed. It exports only the displayed finite witness for the inspected subfamily, not a selected point in the full intersection of an arbitrary family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the carrier projection. A requested inspected subfamily is visible only as part of the finite window $F$, and its compact source is visible only through $K$.

Apply the NameCert obligations. The centeredness ledger $I$ supplies the meet evidence attached to the displayed subfamily, and the witness row $W$ is read only after that ledger has been exposed.

The replay and provenance rows keep the witness inside the compact-metric and finite-intersection siblings. No row records a host family of all closed subsets, a choice-selected point, or a global intersection theorem, so the exported witness remains the finite inspected-subfamily witness.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:compact-centered-family-nonescape}
For every accepted $\CompactCenteredFamilyUp$ carrier, every compact-family consumer remains inside the displayed rows $K,F,I,W,H,R,P,N$. In particular, the packet cannot promote finite centeredness into arbitrary open-cover compactness, a full-family intersection point, quotient equality of closed sets, a choice principle, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Use the carrier classifier from \autoref{def:compact-centered-family-carrier}. The rows $K,F,I,W$ are separate coordinates, while $H$ and $R$ preserve those coordinates through transport and replay rather than adding new mathematical data.

Finally inspect the omitted coordinates. The carrier has no row for arbitrary host closed sets, no open-cover compactness row, no selected point for the full family, no quotient equality row, no choice row, and no ambient completeness row. \end{proof}
