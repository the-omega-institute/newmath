\begin{theorem}[Modulus-to-regular-Cauchy NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:modulus-to-regular-cauchy-namecert-obligations}

$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ModulusToRegularCauchyUp}$ surface consists exactly of Cauchy-modulus schedule admission through $S$, finite window exposure through $W$, dyadic tolerance checking through $D$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal real-name sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:modulus-to-regular-cauchy-carrier}. The schedule row $S$ is the only coordinate that assigns requested precisions to finite source-window requests, and the window row $W$ is the only coordinate that exposes the corresponding source observations.

The dyadic row $D$ checks those observations against the requested tolerance. The regular rational row $R$ is therefore downstream of $S,W,D$, and the terminal real-name seal $E$ is downstream of $R$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no quotient-equality coordinate, selected-limit coordinate, countable-choice schedule, host completion operator, or ambient completeness field, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Modulus-to-regular-Cauchy window induction]
\label{thm:modulus-to-regular-cauchy-window-induction}

The induction exports no quotient stream equality, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

For a successor window, read the next request from the modulus schedule row $S$. The stream window row $W$ supplies exactly the finite observations demanded by that request.

Now consume the dyadic tolerance row $D$. Once the scheduled observations meet the requested dyadic budget, the regular rational readback row $R$ is extended for that finite stage.

Finally use $H,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name the same stage before the terminal seal $E$ is read. Because no excluded principle is a row of the carrier, the induction remains a finite schedule-to-regular-name construction rather than a quotient, selector, choice, or completeness argument.
\end{proof}
