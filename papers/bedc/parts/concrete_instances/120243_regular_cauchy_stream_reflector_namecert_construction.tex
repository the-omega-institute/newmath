$$

$$

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected representatives, hidden cofinal tails, host completion equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
