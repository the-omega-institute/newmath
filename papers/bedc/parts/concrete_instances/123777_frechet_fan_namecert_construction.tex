\label{def:frechet-fan-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the spoke-index row, naming the finitely inspected natural-number spokes and the apex marker.
\item $T$ is the tail-window row, recording which finite initial segment of each inspected spoke is excluded before a neighbourhood read accepts the tail.
\item $A$ is the apex-neighbourhood row, exposing the finite schedule by which all displayed spokes are eventually admitted near the apex.
\item $Q$ is the sequential readback row, routing convergence and non-closure reads through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sequential-space-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-frechet-urysohn-namecert}.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a topology or sequence request through $S,T,A,Q$ and then through the structural rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FrechetFanUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\SequentialSpaceUp$, $\FrechetUrysohnUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

\begin{theorem}[Frechet fan NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:frechet-fan-namecert-obligations}

Every topology- or sequence-facing consumer must expose these rows before reading an apex neighbourhood, a spoke tail, a convergent fan sequence, or a sequential-closure handoff.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Read the apex-neighbourhood and sequential rows next. The row $A$ records the displayed tail schedule near the apex, while $Q$ routes sequence-facing reads through the existing sequential-space and Frechet--Urysohn sibling chapters.

Finally inspect $H,C,P,N$. These rows transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no row stores quotient sequence equality, a selected representative, countable choice, a completed open lattice, or host topology semantics, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
