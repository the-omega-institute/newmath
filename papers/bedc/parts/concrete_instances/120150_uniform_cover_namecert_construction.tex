\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniformCoverUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-cover-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki and Kelley on uniform spaces and uniform covers, the $\UniformCoverUp$ packet records the finite cover surface that an L10 uniform-space consumer may read before passing to star refinements or completion. It sits after the uniform-space surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert} and the entourage-base surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-entourage-base-namecert}, before the star-refinement surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-star-refinement-uniform-cover-namecert} and the uniform-completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformcompletion-namecert}. The packet names only displayed cover, entourage, membership, refinement, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it does not assert open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, paracompact refinement, ambient completeness, or a host powerset cover principle.

\begin{definition}[UniformCover carrier]
\label{def:uniform-cover-carrier}
A $\UniformCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(S,E,C,M,R,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ source row naming the underlying uniform-space interface.
\item $E$ is the $\EntourageBaseUp$ row from which the cover is generated.
\item $C$ is the finite cover row, listing the displayed members that a consumer may inspect.
\item $M$ is the membership-window row, recording which named points or finite probes are admitted to each cover member.
\item $R$ is the refinement-read row, exposing how an entourage-basis request may refine the cover row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, entourage base, cover, membership, refinement, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
S,E \longmapsto C,M,R \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformCoverUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\EntourageBaseUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate admits exactly these rows. Its classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,E,C,M,R,H,T,P,N$ and has no coordinate for compactness of all open covers, choice-selected subcovers, paracompactness, a star-refinement witness, or completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[UniformCover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-cover-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\UniformCoverUp$ carrier
$$
U=(S,E,C,M,R,H,T,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UniformCoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the uniform-space source, entourage-base, cover, membership, refinement, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item cover generation, requiring $C$ to be read from the displayed uniform-space and entourage-base rows $S,E$;
\item membership exposure, requiring every cover-member read to pass through the finite window row $M$;
\item refinement exposure, requiring every refinement-facing consumer to pass through $R$ before citing an entourage-base refinement;
\item classifier transport, requiring $H$ and $T$ to preserve the same rows componentwise under $\hsame$ and displayed continuation;
\item non-escape, excluding open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, paracompact refinements, star-refinement witnesses, and completion claims.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside $S,E,C,M,R,H,T,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-cover-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the uniform-space, entourage-base, cover, membership, refinement, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The cover generation and membership clauses are row reads. The cover row $C$ is downstream of $S,E$, while each member inspection is mediated by the finite membership-window row $M$.

The refinement clause is separate. A consumer may request an entourage-base refinement only through $R$, so the cover packet does not silently become a star-refinement or completion theorem.

Finally $H,T,P,N$ preserve the same finite route by componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the excluded host principles are not coordinates of the carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[UniformCover entourage refinement route]
\label{thm:uniform-cover-entourage-refinement}
Let
$$
U=(S,E,C,M,R,H,T,P,N)
$$
be an accepted $\UniformCoverUp$ carrier. Every consumer that treats the cover as generated by a uniform entourage factors through the finite route
$$
\UniformSpaceUp \longmapsto \EntourageBaseUp \longmapsto C,M,R \longmapsto H,T,P,N .
$$
The route may feed the star-refinement surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-star-refinement-uniform-cover-namecert} and the completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformcompletion-namecert}, but it exports no selected refinement, no compactness principle, and no ambient completion object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the accepted carrier. The source and entourage-base rows $S,E$ are the only entries from which the cover row can be generated.

Apply \autoref{thm:uniform-cover-namecert-obligations}. Its membership and refinement obligations force every cover read through $C,M,R$ before any downstream refinement or completion consumer can cite the cover.

The star-refinement and completion consumers therefore receive only the displayed cover route. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that route through $H,T,P,N$, and the absent compactness, selection, and completion coordinates cannot be projected from the packet.
\end{proof}

\falsifiablePrediction{A uniform-space consumer that cites a cover without displaying the $\UniformSpaceUp$ source row, the $\EntourageBaseUp$ generator row, and the finite membership window is not reading a UniformCoverUp carrier.}

\independenceWitness{uniformspace, entouragebase, starrefinementuniformcover, uniformcompletion: none is bijective because $\UniformCoverUp$ stores the cover and membership route between the uniform-space source and later refinement or completion consumers. UniformSpaceUp supplies the uniformity but not the finite cover row; EntourageBaseUp supplies basis refinement but not cover membership; StarRefinementUniformCoverUp consumes a cover with an extra star-containment row; UniformCompletionUp consumes Cauchy and completion data rather than a bare uniform-cover carrier.}
