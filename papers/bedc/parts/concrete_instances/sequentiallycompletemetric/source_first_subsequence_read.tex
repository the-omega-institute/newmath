\begin{theorem}[SequentiallyCompleteMetric source-first subsequence read]
\label{thm:sequentially-complete-metric-source-first-subsequence-read}
Let $Q=(X,S,M,L,D,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\SequentiallyCompleteMetricUp$ carrier. Every subsequence, tail, or cofinal-window consumer that is later used by a $\CompleteMetricUp$ or $\MetricCompletionUp$ handoff must first expose the $\MetricUp$ source row $X$, the $\StreamNameUp$ window row $S$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback attached to those windows, the modulus row $M$, the displayed limit row $L$, and the late-distance ledger $D$. The handoff cannot begin from quotient completion, selected representatives, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or an independent completed-point carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the source-first tail read of \autoref{thm:sequentially-complete-metric-source-first-tail-read}. It forces every completion-facing tail packet to expose the metric source, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, and modulus row before any displayed limit or late-distance row can be inspected.

Pass from tails to subsequences by tail induction. The base tail has the displayed route $X,S,M$ before $L,D$, and the successor step preserves the same source rows while tightening only the late-window request. Apply the limit-ledger coverage of \autoref{thm:sequentially-complete-metric-limit-ledger-coverage}; the displayed limit row and late-distance ledger are therefore read after the regular-source route rather than as free completion data.

Finally use the completion-factorization ledger of \autoref{thm:sequentially-complete-metric-completion-factorization-ledger}. Any $\CompleteMetricUp$ or $\MetricCompletionUp$ consumer receives $X,S,M,L,D,H,C,P,N$ as the visible route, so quotient completion, selected representatives, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, and independent completed carriers remain outside the handoff.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sequentially complete metric source-first subsequence readback]
\label{thm:sequentially-complete-metric-source-first-subsequence-readback}
Let $Q=(X,S,M,L,D,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\SequentiallyCompleteMetricUp$ packet. Every source-first subsequence read that is later inspected by a completion-facing consumer must replay through the displayed $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows before the consumer may inspect the subsequence. The replay exports no selected subsequence, countable-choice schedule, quotient limit, host equality proof, or ambient Real completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the packet rows. The source-first subsequence read of \autoref{thm:sequentially-complete-metric-source-first-subsequence-read} already places the metric source $X$, stream windows $S$, regular rational readback, modulus row $M$, displayed limit row $L$, and late-distance ledger $D$ before a completion handoff.

Replay the displayed route through the completion-facing rows. The completion-factorization ledger sends the same $X,S,M,L,D,H,C,P,N$ packet to $\CompleteMetricUp$ and $\MetricCompletionUp$, while the StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real rows remain the finite readback corridor through which the subsequence is observed.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same data. Because no selected subsequence, countable-choice function, quotient limit, host equality proof, or ambient completeness row appears in the carrier, the completion-facing consumer cannot inspect the subsequence outside the displayed readback route.
\end{proof}
