$$

\item $X$ is the $\MetricUp$ source-distance row, recording the finite distance comparisons used by the cover.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ pointwise-modulus row, assigning local precision windows to the displayed compact-cover centers.
\item $R$ is the $\RatUp$ subordinate radius row, recording positive rational radii below the local pointwise-modulus budgets.

\begin{theorem}[Subordinate modulus uniform handoff NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:subordinate-modulus-uniform-handoff-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SubordinateModulusUniformHandoffUp$ carrier $U=(K,X,F,R,Q,V,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SubordinateModulusUniformHandoffUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact finite-cover admission through $K$, metric comparison admission through $X$, continuous pointwise-modulus admission through $F$, subordinate positive-radius exposure through $R$, finite rational lower-bound folding through $Q$, uniform-modulus export through $V$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:subordinate-modulus-uniform-handoff-carrier}. The compact-cover row $K$ and metric row $X$ provide the finite source geometry available to the handoff.

Read the pointwise-modulus row $F$ and subordinate radius row $R$. Each radius is attached to a displayed cover center and is certified below the corresponding pointwise precision budget.

The fold row $Q$ is a finite $\RatUp$ lower-bound computation over those radii. The export row $V$ may be read only after that fold has produced a tolerance-only precision for the uniform-modulus consumer.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Because no carrier coordinate names an infinite subcover, host minimum, external modulus selector, quotient equality, countable choice, Lean verification, or bridge credentials, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Subordinate modulus uniform handoff uniform-modulus export]

\begin{proof}

Read $F,R,Q$ in order. The pointwise-modulus row attaches a local precision to each displayed cover center, the subordinate radius row keeps that radius below the local budget, and the rational fold converts the finite family of radii into one precision history.
