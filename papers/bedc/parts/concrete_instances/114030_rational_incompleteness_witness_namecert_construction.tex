Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\RationalIncompletenessWitnessUp$ packet records the constructive finite evidence that $\RatUp$ is not already completion-closed before the $\RealUp$ completion route is inspected. It sits after the dyadic arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational sequence interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}; it also reuses the irrationality obstruction style of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-irrational-witness-namecert}. The nested dyadic contradiction route is a direct consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-irrational-witness-namecert}, and its completion-facing refusal later feeds the $\RealUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ completion boundary through the displayed square-bound and tail-modulus rows. The packet names only a displayed nested-interval contradiction route, not a quotient real, not a selected limit, not countable choice, not host completeness of $\RealUp$, and not a classical theorem imported outside the BEDC carrier rows.

\begin{definition}[RationalIncompletenessWitness carrier]
\label{def:rational-incompleteness-witness-carrier}
A $\RationalIncompletenessWitnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(I,M,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is a finite nested dyadic-interval ledger, recording left and right $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoints, strict containment rows, and the finite square-bound comparisons attached to each inspected stage.
\item $M$ is the Cauchy tail modulus row, exposing the displayed stage bound after which all rational candidates must remain inside the currently inspected dyadic interval.
\item $S$ is the square-bound contradiction row, storing the finite parity, square comparison, and interval exclusion data used to reject a rational limit candidate for the nested route.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, naming the regular rational sequence surface whose finite windows are inspected by $I,M,S$.
\item $R$ is the $\StreamNameUp/\RealUp$ handoff row, carrying the terminal stream-name and real-name seal only after the dyadic nesting and rational-candidate contradiction rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for interval nesting, modulus, contradiction, regular rational readback, stream-name handoff, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion-facing request through $M$, then through the nested intervals $I$, the square-bound contradiction row $S$, the regular rational readback row $Q$, and finally the terminal handoff row $R$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RationalIncompletenessWitnessUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RationalIncompletenessWitnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows, their componentwise transports, their displayed replay routes, their provenance, and their local names. It has no coordinate for quotient rational completion, selected irrational limits, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RationalIncompletenessWitness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:rational-incompleteness-witness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RationalIncompletenessWitnessUp$ carrier $W=(I,M,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RationalIncompletenessWitnessUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, nested dyadic-interval ledger exposure, Cauchy tail modulus exposure, square-bound contradiction visibility, regular rational readback, stream-name and $\RealUp$ handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. No obligation is stored outside those finite rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:rational-incompleteness-witness-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the nested dyadic interval ledger $I$, the tail modulus $M$, the contradiction row $S$, the regular rational readback $Q$, and the terminal handoff $R$ before any structural row is used.

The mathematical obligations are row-local. The interval ledger supplies only dyadic endpoints and containment comparisons, the modulus row supplies only the finite late-tail bound, and the contradiction row supplies only the square-bound exclusion attached to those displayed intervals.

The handoff and structural rows do not add a second source. The rows $Q$ and $R$ are downstream of $I,M,S$, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Hence the listed rows exhaust the local certificate and leave no coordinate for quotient completion, selected limits, host equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RationalIncompletenessWitness dyadic nesting]
\label{thm:rational-incompleteness-witness-dyadic-nesting}
Every completion-facing read of an accepted $\RationalIncompletenessWitnessUp$ packet must first inspect a finite nested dyadic-interval route through $I$ and $M$. The route displays dyadic endpoints from $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, strict containment rows, the late-tail modulus budget, and the square-bound comparison windows before any $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, or $\RealUp$ handoff is exposed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the packet $W=(I,M,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$. The only rows available before the regular rational and real-name handoff are the nested interval ledger $I$, the tail modulus $M$, and the square-bound contradiction row $S$.

The continuation row $C$ fixes their order. A completion request first reads the displayed modulus budget, then the nested dyadic interval cells and their containment comparisons, and then the square-bound window attached to the same cells.

Only after this finite route is displayed can $Q$ and $R$ be inspected. Thus the completion-facing handoff cannot bypass dyadic endpoints, containment, and the late-tail budget by invoking a selected limit or host completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RationalIncompletenessWitness no-rational-limit route]
\label{thm:rational-incompleteness-witness-no-rational-limit}
For every accepted $\RationalIncompletenessWitnessUp$ carrier $W=(I,M,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, any claim that the displayed nested route has a rational limit is routed back to the finite square-bound contradiction row $S$ after the dyadic nesting and Cauchy tail modulus rows have been exposed. The rejection is therefore a BEDC finite-history refusal: it does not assert quotient $\RealUp$ equality, choose an irrational representative, use countable choice, or assume $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a rational-limit candidate read. The carrier forces the read through $M$ and $I$, so the candidate is compared only after the finite tail budget and the inspected dyadic interval window are visible.

The contradiction row $S$ then supplies the square-bound exclusion for that same window. Since $S$ is part of the packet rather than an external theorem coordinate, the refusal is tied to the displayed dyadic comparisons and not to host real equality.

Finally use the downstream rows. The regular sequence readback $Q$, handoff row $R$, transport $H$, replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local name $N$ preserve the same finite route. None of these rows can introduce quotient completion, a selected limit, countable choice, or ambient completeness, so every rational-limit claim is refused inside the packet.
\end{proof}
