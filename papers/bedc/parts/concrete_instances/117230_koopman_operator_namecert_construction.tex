The packet names a finite observable pullback route; it is not a host Hilbert-space operator, an invariant measure theorem, a selected spectral decomposition, quotient function equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Koopman operator carrier]
\label{def:koopman-operator-carrier}
A $\KoopmanOperatorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(X,T,F,O,W,R,E,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\DynSystemUp$ state-evolution row, naming the displayed source states and the single-step or finite-step transition being inspected.
\item $T$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ transition row attached to the same displayed dynamical system.
\item $F$ is the observable row, recording the finite graph values of the displayed observable on the target of $T$.
\item $O$ is the Koopman pullback row, whose displayed value at a source state is read as the observable row $F$ after the transition row $T$.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation-window row through which the pulled-back observable is inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-rational readback row attached to those finite windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $X,T,F,O,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $A$ is the averaging or transfer-neighbour row, recording which reads may be compared with $\TransferOperatorUp$, $\PerronFrobeniusUp$, and $\MeanErgodicUp$ consumers without identifying the packet with any one of them.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the dynamical-system, transition, observable, pullback, window, readback, seal, neighbour, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$$
X,T,F,O,W,R \longmapsto E
$$
and through the neighbour row $A$ when an averaging or density-facing consumer is present.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KoopmanOperatorUp$, $\DynSystemUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\TransferOperatorUp$, $\PerronFrobeniusUp$, $\MeanErgodicUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KoopmanOperatorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,T,F,O,W,R,E,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an ambient function space, selected invariant measure, spectral theorem, quotient observable equality, host integration, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Koopman operator NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:koopman-operator-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KoopmanOperatorUp$ carrier
$$
K=(X,T,F,O,W,R,E,A,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\KoopmanOperatorUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the dynamical-system row, continuous transition row, observable row, pullback row, observation windows, regular-rational readback, Real seal, neighbour route, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item pullback exactness, requiring each $O$ read to be the displayed observable row $F$ consumed after the transition row $T$ on the same $\DynSystemUp$ state row $X$;
\item readback ordering, requiring $W$ and $R$ to be exposed before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is available;
\item neighbour separation, requiring the $\TransferOperatorUp$, $\PerronFrobeniusUp$, and $\MeanErgodicUp$ rows in $A$ to remain comparison or averaging consumers rather than replacements for the observable pullback row;
\item structural stability under the displayed $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ rows.
\end{enumerate}
No Koopman-operator obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The tuple lists the dynamical-system row, transition row, observable row, pullback row, observation-window and readback rows, terminal seal, neighbour row, and structural rows.

Pullback exactness is local. Row $O$ is admitted only as the observable row $F$ read after the displayed transition $T$ on the same state-evolution row $X$, so the packet has no coordinate for an ambient function-space equality.

Readback ordering then follows the StreamName and RegSeqRat rows. The finite window $W$ is inspected before the regular-rational row $R$, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is downstream of both.

Finally read the neighbour row $A$. It permits comparison with transfer, Perron--Frobenius, and mean-ergodic consumers while keeping the observable-side pullback distinct. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that same finite route and add no invariant-measure theorem, spectral decomposition, host integration, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Koopman operator dynamics-observable route]
\label{thm:koopman-operator-dynamics-observable-route}
Every observable-facing read of an accepted $\KoopmanOperatorUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\DynSystemUp,\ \ContinuousMapUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp .
$$
More explicitly, the consumer must expose the state-evolution row $X$, transition row $T$, observable row $F$, pullback row $O$, observation window $W$, and readback row $R$ before the terminal seal $E$ or any neighbour comparison row $A$ is consumed. The packet exports no invariant measure, density pushforward, selected spectral datum, quotient observable equality, host integration theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:koopman-operator-namecert-obligations}. They place every accepted consumer read inside the displayed carrier rows $X,T,F,O,W,R,E,A,H,C,P,N$.

Read the dynamics-to-observable route first. The $\DynSystemUp$ row $X$ and $\ContinuousMapUp$ row $T$ determine where the observable is sampled, and the row $O$ records that the observable value is pulled back from $F$ along that transition.

The numerical readback is downstream. The finite $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$ feeds the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$, and only then can the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ be read.

The neighbour row $A$ cannot reverse the route. It may point to transfer, Perron--Frobenius, or mean-ergodic consumers, but those rows do not replace $X,T,F,O,W,R,E$ by a measure-theoretic, spectral, quotient, or host-completeness object. Therefore the displayed dynamics-observable route exhausts the accepted packet.
\end{proof}
