\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback attached to $S$.

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy window selector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-window-selector-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-window-selector-carrier}. The rows $D,S,R,T,E,H,C,P,N$ are the whole selector packet, so the local naming surface is finite and displayed.

The selection route is read in order. The dyadic row fixes the demanded tolerance, the stream row exposes the inspected finite window, and the regular rational row reads the associated rational data.

The tail-window row $T$ then records that this finite window is admitted by the existing $\RegularCauchyTailWindowSelectorUp$ route. The rows $E,H,C,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route, leaving no separate coordinate for a choice schedule or hidden limit.
