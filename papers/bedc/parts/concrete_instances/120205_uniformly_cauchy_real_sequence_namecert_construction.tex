\begin{theorem}[Uniformly Cauchy real sequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniformly-cauchy-real-sequence-namecert-obligations}

$$

the local $\NameCert_{\UniformlyCauchyRealSequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the real sequence source $S$, $\StreamNameUp$ windows $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger $D$, $\CauchyConvergenceModulusUp$ schedule $M$, $\RealUp$ seal $L$, componentwise transport $H$, displayed replay $C$, package provenance $P$, and local naming row $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniformly-cauchy-real-sequence-carrier}. The analytic rows are the real sequence source $S$, finite windows $W$, regular rational readback $R$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, convergence modulus $M$, and Real seal $L$.

The uniformity obligation is read from $W,D,M$. For a requested tolerance, $M$ supplies one displayed tail threshold, $W$ supplies the finite windows at and beyond that threshold, and $D$ supplies the dyadic comparison budget used by those windows.

The real-valued readback is downstream of the same finite route. The row $R$ records regular rational data for the displayed windows, while $L$ seals the corresponding real-name boundary only after the source, windows, readback, tolerance, and modulus rows are visible.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no excluded quotient, choice, selected-representative, selected-limit, host-equality, or ambient-completeness coordinate occurs in $U$, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
