\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LinearRecurrenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-linear-recurrence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Stanley and Graham--Knuth--Patashnik on linear recurrence sequences, the $\LinearRecurrenceUp$ packet records a finite recurrence-stream NameCert for the L10 $\StreamNameUp$--$\RegSeqRatUp$--$\RealUp$ route. It sits after the unary index discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nat-namecert}, the rational coefficient rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names finite lawlike stream data only; it does not import host sequence equality, selected infinite streams, closed-form solutions, characteristic roots, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Linear recurrence carrier]
\label{def:linear-recurrence-carrier}
A $\LinearRecurrenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(C,I,N,S,Q,R,H,P,A)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the coefficient row over $\RatUp$ and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, listing the finite recurrence order, rational coefficients, and dyadic tolerance tags used by later reads.
\item $I$ is the initial finite window, giving the seed values needed before the recurrence rule can be replayed.
\item $N$ is the unary $\NatUp$ index schedule naming which finite indices and successor steps are inspected.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row, exposing the inspected stream entries and recurrence replay windows.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational values generated by the coefficient row and initial window.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for those readback values when a Real or Completion consumer asks for terminal scalar output.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for coefficient, initial-window, index, stream, readback, and seal rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NatUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, and $\Cont$ rows.
\item $A$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LinearRecurrenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $C,I,N,S,Q,R,H,P,A$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Linear recurrence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:linear-recurrence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LinearRecurrenceUp$ carrier
$$
L=(C,I,N,S,Q,R,H,P,A),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LinearRecurrenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of coefficient-row exposure, initial-window exposure, unary index scheduling, finite stream observation, regular rational readback, Real sealing, componentwise transport, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:linear-recurrence-carrier}. The recurrence rule is not an ambient function on host sequences; it is the finite row $C$ together with the initial window $I$ and the unary index schedule $N$.

Read the numeric rows through the sibling chapters. The index schedule is supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nat-namecert}, the rational coefficients by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, and the dyadic tolerance tags by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}.

Finally route observations through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, readback through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and terminal sealing through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The remaining rows $H,P,A$ only transport, source, and name the same finite packet, so the listed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Linear recurrence stream handoff]
\label{thm:linear-recurrence-stream-handoff}
Every stream-facing read of an accepted $\LinearRecurrenceUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
C,I,N \longmapsto S \longmapsto Q .
$$
The consumer first sees the recurrence coefficients, initial window, and unary index schedule, then the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row, and only then the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row. No stream read may replace this route by host sequence equality, an uninspected tail, a selected closed-form solution, or an infinite recurrence oracle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the coefficient, initial-window, and index rows. By \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the coefficient row is finite rational-and-dyadic data; by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nat-namecert}, the schedule row names only unary inspected indices.

Pass next to the stream observation row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. It exposes the requested entries and replay windows, not a quotient of infinite streams.

The regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} consumes exactly those displayed windows. Since the route has no coordinate for host sequence equality, uninspected tails, selected closed forms, or infinite recurrence oracles, every stream-facing read factors through $C,I,N,S,Q$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Linear recurrence Real seal boundary]
\label{thm:linear-recurrence-real-seal-boundary}
Every Real or Completion consumer of an accepted $\LinearRecurrenceUp$ packet may read the terminal scalar row only through
$$
C,I,N,S,Q,R,H,P,A .
$$
The $\RealUp$ seal $R$ records a terminal boundary for displayed recurrence readback values; it does not assert convergence of the whole recurrence stream, completeness of the ambient real line, characteristic-root solvability, or equality with a host sequence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:linear-recurrence-stream-handoff} to force the consumer through the finite coefficient, initial-window, index, observation, and regular-readback rows. The construction therefore reaches the real-facing surface only after $\NatUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, and $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows have been exposed through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nat-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}.

Apply the terminal seal boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The $\RealUp$ row $R$ seals displayed readback values; it is not a convergence theorem for all indices, a completeness principle, or a host-sequence equality test.

The transport, provenance, and local naming rows $H,P,A$ preserve only the same route. Hence the Real or Completion consumer receives exactly the displayed recurrence-readback boundary and no stronger analytic structure.
\end{proof}
