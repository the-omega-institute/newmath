\begin{definition}[Closed observation proof-trace carrier]
\label{def:closed-observation-proof-trace-carrier}
A $\ClosedObservationProofTraceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(O,Q,H,C,P,N,R,K,D)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the closed finite-observation row, recording the observed proof-facing object as finite data.
\item $Q$ is the termination row, displaying the normal-form or totality evidence required before the trace is accepted.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for observation, termination, refusal, kernel, trace, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from observation through termination, kernel acceptance, trace replay, refusal checks, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClosedObservationProofTraceUp$, closed observation rows, total proof records, kernel acceptance witnesses, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedObservationProofTraceUp}$ row.
\item $R$ is the divergence-refusal row, rejecting any reduction row that is not displayed as terminating.
\item $K$ is the kernel-acceptance witness row for the total proof record.
\item $D$ is the proof-trace row, recording the finite derivation trace that is replayed by $C$.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $O,Q,H,C,P,N,R,K,D$. It has no coordinate for divergent reduction, quotient proof equality, erased proof-term identity, host derivation equality, or an unbounded computation trace.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Closed observation proof-trace obligations]
\label{thm:closed-observation-proof-trace-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClosedObservationProofTraceUp$ carrier
$$
T=(O,Q,H,C,P,N,R,K,D),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedObservationProofTraceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for finite observation, termination, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, local naming, divergence refusal, kernel acceptance, and proof-trace rows. The packet admits no proof trace unless $Q$ and $R$ jointly display totality and refusal of divergent reduction before the kernel row $K$ and trace row $D$ are replayed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:closed-observation-proof-trace-carrier}. The observation row is $O$, the termination row is $Q$, the refusal row is $R$, the kernel row is $K$, and the trace row is $D$.

The totality condition is local. A trace can be replayed only after $Q$ displays termination and $R$ rejects the divergent alternative, so a nonterminating reduction row has no route into $K$ or $D$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this finite surface. Since no carrier coordinate stores quotient proof equality, erased proof identity, host derivation equality, or an unbounded computation trace, none becomes a NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Closed observation proof-trace totality route]
\label{thm:closed-observation-proof-trace-totality-route}
Every accepted $\ClosedObservationProofTraceUp$ proof record factors through
$$
O \longmapsto Q,R \longmapsto K,D \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
The route exposes finite observation, termination, divergent-row refusal, kernel acceptance, proof-trace replay, transport, provenance, and local naming before a proof record is accepted. It exports no divergent reduction row, quotient proof identity, proof-term erasure equivalence, host derivation equality, or unbounded trace.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier rows. The finite observation row $O$ is the only source row, and $Q,R$ are the only totality and refusal gates.

The kernel row $K$ and proof-trace row $D$ are downstream of those gates. Thus the accepted trace is a total proof record with displayed termination and refusal data, not an erased or quotient proof object.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route. A consumer that asks for an unbounded trace or a divergent reduction row leaves the accepted carrier.
\end{proof}
