\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DyadicUniformCoverUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-uniform-cover-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on compact intervals and finite covers, the $\DyadicUniformCoverUp$ packet records the dyadic finite-cover side of the L10 compactness route described in \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity}. It sits over the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular-rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the compact interval source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-compact-interval-namecert}. The packet names finite cover rows only; it is not an open-cover compactness theorem, a selected infinite subcover, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Dyadic uniform cover carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-uniform-cover-carrier}
A $\DyadicUniformCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(I,D,W,Q,R,C,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\BishopCompactIntervalUp$ source interval row.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger naming dyadic endpoints, radii, overlaps, and tolerance comparisons.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ inspection window used to read interval endpoints and cover cells.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for dyadic cell centers and endpoint approximations.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $W,Q,D$ have been displayed.
\item $C$ is the finite cover row listing the displayed dyadic cells, their membership checks, and the local coverage certificate for the interval row $I$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval, dyadic, stream, regular-rational, real-seal, cover, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite cover route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicUniformCoverUp$, $\BishopCompactIntervalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicUniformCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, infinite subcover selection, quotient stream equality, host real equality, trichotomy, countable choice, selected limits, or ambient compactness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic uniform cover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-uniform-cover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicUniformCoverUp$ carrier $U=(I,D,W,Q,R,C,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicUniformCoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the compact interval source, dyadic ledger, finite StreamName window, regular-rational readback, Real seal, finite cover row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every compactness or completion consumer of the packet must read the cover row $C$ through $D,W,Q,R$ before it consumes the interval row $I$ as a finite-cover source.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-uniform-cover-carrier}. Its analytic rows are exactly $I,D,W,Q,R,C$, with $H,K,P,N$ providing structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The dyadic ledger $D$ names the cell endpoints and tolerance comparisons. The stream window $W$ and regular-rational row $Q$ expose only finitely many endpoint and center reads before the terminal real seal $R$ is visible.

The cover row $C$ is therefore consumed as a finite dyadic cover over the interval row $I$. Since arbitrary open covers, infinite subcover selection, quotient equality, host real equality, trichotomy, countable choice, selected limits, and ambient compactness are absent from the carrier, no obligation can export them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic uniform cover L10 handoff]
\label{thm:dyadic-uniform-cover-l10-handoff}
Every L10 compactness or completion consumer of an accepted $\DyadicUniformCoverUp$ packet factors through the dyadic ledger $D$, the finite StreamName window $W$, the regular-rational readback $Q$, the Real seal $R$, and the finite cover row $C$ before it consumes the $\BishopCompactIntervalUp$ source $I$. Thus the finite-cover direction is a displayed dyadic NameCert route, not an open-cover compactness principle or a choice-selected subcover.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the accepted carrier $U=(I,D,W,Q,R,C,H,K,P,N)$. The interval source $I$ is not read in isolation; the carrier pairs it with the dyadic ledger and finite observation rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:dyadic-uniform-cover-namecert-obligations}. It requires every public compactness or completion read to expose $D,W,Q,R,C$ before treating $I$ as a finite-cover source.

Finally read the structural rows $H,K,P,N$. They transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed dyadic cover route, so the handoff cannot be recast as arbitrary open-cover compactness, infinite subcover choice, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
