The $\ConstructiveCauchyCompletionSelectorUp$ packet records that selector surface as finite BEDC NameCert data over the regular rational source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the equality route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-difference-criterion-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Constructive Cauchy completion selector carrier]
\label{def:constructive-cauchy-completion-selector-carrier}
A $\ConstructiveCauchyCompletionSelectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(R,S,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source row whose regular rational observations are to be sealed as a completion-facing real name.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row exposing exactly the inspected source entries at the requested precision.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, including the dyadic radii, slack comparisons, and modulus budgets used by the window read.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, reachable only after $R,S,D$ have been displayed.
\item $Q$ is the $\CauchyDifferenceCriterionUp$ equality route used when two selector packets with displayed regular sources are compared.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the regular source, windows, dyadic ledger, Real seal, equality route, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion request through
$$
R,S,D \longrightarrow E \longrightarrow Q \longrightarrow H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ConstructiveCauchyCompletionSelectorUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyDifferenceCriterionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveCauchyCompletionSelectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,S,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, a selected host limit, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive Cauchy completion selector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:constructive-cauchy-completion-selector-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ConstructiveCauchyCompletionSelectorUp$ carrier $K=(R,S,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveCauchyCompletionSelectorUp}$ surface consists exactly of these finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source row, $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, $\RealUp$ seal, $\CauchyDifferenceCriterionUp$ equality route, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item source-window exactness, requiring every completion-facing read to expose $S$ as the finite inspection window for $R$;
\item tolerance-ledger exactness, requiring $D$ to supply the dyadic budget consumed by the window read and the terminal seal;
\item Real-seal nonescape, requiring $E$ to be read only after $R,S,D$ have been displayed and replayed through $H,C,P,N$;
\item equality-route discipline, requiring any comparison of accepted selector packets to factor through the displayed $\CauchyDifferenceCriterionUp$ row $Q$ rather than through quotient streams or host real equality.
\end{enumerate}
No completion selector obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:constructive-cauchy-completion-selector-carrier}. The source row is $R$, its finite observations are $S$, its precision budget is $D$, and its terminal seal is $E$.

The source-window and tolerance clauses are row projections. A completion-facing consumer receives the source only through the displayed window and dyadic ledger, so no unbounded stream representative or selected limit can enter the local certificate.

The equality clause is supplied by the $\CauchyDifferenceCriterionUp$ row $Q$. When two accepted selector packets are compared, $Q$ routes the comparison through displayed regular sources, difference data, null evidence, zero-distance handoff, and the Real seal boundary.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$. These structural rows preserve the displayed packet componentwise and record its provenance and local name; they do not add quotient stream equality, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive Cauchy completion selector RegSeqRat--Real handoff]
\label{thm:constructive-cauchy-completion-selector-regseq-real-handoff}

\begin{proof}

The handoff to $\RealUp$ is therefore the displayed route $R,S,D,E$. The transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows keep the same route visible to downstream Real and Completion consumers.
