\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ProofMiningMetastabilityUp$}

Following Kohlenbach proof mining and Tao metastability, the $\ProofMiningMetastabilityUp$ packet records the finite-bound extraction route by which a convergence or compactness argument is read as explicit metastable data. It sits beside the finite tail-window packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-metastability-namecert}, the Cauchy carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the compact finite-subcover boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet packages a human-standard proof-mining mechanism as finite NameCert data: proofs may export bounds, control windows, and compactness handoffs, but they do not import selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Proof-mining metastability carrier]
\label{def:proof-mining-metastability-carrier}
A $\ProofMiningMetastabilityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(F,B,W,D,S,Q,C,K,R,H,T,L,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the formal proof input row, naming the proof object or proof skeleton whose quantitative content is inspected.
\item $B$ is the extracted bound row, recording the finite counterfunction or majorant data read from $F$.
\item $W$ is the metastable window row, displaying the finite interval or finite cover segment controlled by $B$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used when the window is Cauchy-facing or real-facing.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation row for inspected stream positions.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-rational tail row for rational readback inside the window.
\item $C$ is the $\CauchyUp$ consumer row, recording when the mined data is read as a Cauchy-facing tail statement.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactUp$ consumer row, recording when the mined data is read as a compact finite-subcover or finite-choice boundary.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, exposed only after $B,W,D,S,Q,C,K$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for proof input, bound, window, tolerance, stream, regular-rational, Cauchy, compact, real-seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from proof input to extracted finite data and then to the Cauchy, compact, and Real-facing consumers.
\item $L$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ProofMiningMetastabilityUp$, $\CauchyMetastabilityUp$, $\CauchyUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ProofMiningMetastabilityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $F,B,W,D,S,Q,C,K,R,H,T,L,N$. It has no coordinate for selected limits, countable choice, quotient equality, host real equality, excluded-middle search, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Proof-mining metastability NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:proof-mining-metastability-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ProofMiningMetastabilityUp$ carrier $P=(F,B,W,D,S,Q,C,K,R,H,T,L,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ProofMiningMetastabilityUp}$ surface consists exactly of the proof input row $F$, extracted bound row $B$, finite metastable window $W$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, StreamName observations $S$, RegSeqRat readback $Q$, Cauchy consumer row $C$, Compact consumer row $K$, Real seal row $R$, componentwise transport $H$, displayed replay $T$, provenance $L$, and local naming $N$. Every public read of a mined convergence or compactness consequence factors through those rows before it can cite a metastable bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:proof-mining-metastability-carrier}. Its only proof-facing coordinates are $F,B,W$, so the proof input may be read only through the extracted finite bound and the window it controls.

Read the analytic rows next. The tolerance ledger $D$, StreamName row $S$, and RegSeqRat row $Q$ provide the finite comparison data consumed by Cauchy and Real-facing readers, while the Cauchy row $C$ and compact row $K$ record the two public consumer surfaces.

Finally inspect $R,H,T,L,N$. The real seal is downstream of the extracted finite data, and the structural rows transport, replay, source, and name that same packet. Since no other row is present, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Proof-mining metastability compact Cauchy handoff]
\label{thm:proof-mining-metastability-compact-cauchy-handoff}
Every accepted $\ProofMiningMetastabilityUp$ read that is consumed by a $\CompactUp$ or $\CauchyUp$ route exports only the finite bound $B$, metastable window $W$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, $\StreamNameUp$ observations $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $Q$, the displayed consumer rows $C,K$, and the downstream $\RealUp$ seal $R$. The handoff exports no selected limit, no countable choice object, no host compactness selector, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from \autoref{thm:proof-mining-metastability-namecert-obligations}. A public consumer must read the extracted finite data $B,W$ before it can inspect any analytic endpoint.

For a Cauchy-facing read, use the sibling route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-metastability-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-namecert}. The rows $D,S,Q$ provide tolerance accounting, finite observations, and regular-rational readback before $C$ reaches the $\RealUp$ seal.

For a compact-facing read, use the $\CompactUp$ row $K$. It receives the same finite window as a finite-subcover or finite-choice boundary, not as a host selector or countable choice principle.

The remaining rows $H,T,L,N$ preserve the same handoff under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Therefore the compact and Cauchy consumers receive a finite proof-mined metastability packet and no stronger completion or choice principle.
\end{proof}
