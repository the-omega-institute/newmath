The $\UniformlyContinuousSequenceUp$ route consumes \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-modulus-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert} as load-bearing rows before any sequence endpoint is public. The compact-uniform-modulus sibling supplies the finite compact-window fold, the uniform-modulus sibling supplies the shared tolerance-to-precision row, and the continuous-map sibling supplies the pointwise graph and modulus readback that the sequence packet transports along $\hsame$ and replays through $\Cont$.

Thus a sequence-level uniform-continuity packet reads compact cover data, finite modulus rows, tolerance readbacks, and terminal Real-facing seals through the sibling modulus lattice rather than through a host continuity theorem or an external compactness oracle.
