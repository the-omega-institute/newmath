\begin{theorem}[UniformlyContinuousSequence equicontinuous dependency]
\label{thm:uniformly-continuous-sequence-equicontinuous-dependency}
Every accepted $\UniformlyContinuousSequenceUp$ shared-modulus packet supplies the common modulus row consumed by $\EquicontinuousSequenceUp$. The dependency is read through $\StreamNameUp$ finite windows, the $\CompactMetricUp$ source rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} before the equicontinuity consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-equicontinuous-sequence-namecert} may inspect the shared sequence modulus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:uniformly-continuous-sequence-carrier}. Its finite sequence route displays the source and target metric rows, continuous-map entries, one shared $\UniformModulusUp$ row, finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows, regular rational readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The compact-metric dependency route keeps the same shared modulus attached to compact source rows before downstream compact-uniform consumers read it. Thus an $\EquicontinuousSequenceUp$ consumer receives a common modulus only after the compact source, stream window, rational readback, and real seal rows have been displayed.

Finally use the equicontinuous-sequence carrier of \autoref{def:equicontinuous-sequence-carrier}. Its common schedule and equicontinuity rows consume exactly the kind of shared finite modulus exported by the $\UniformlyContinuousSequenceUp$ packet, while selected subsequences, quotient stream equality, countable choice, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness remain outside both carriers.
\end{proof}
