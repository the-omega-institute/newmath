\begin{theorem}[Uniformly continuous sequence equicontinuity handoff]
\label{thm:uniformly-continuous-sequence-cites-equicontinuity}
Every accepted $\UniformlyContinuousSequenceUp$ packet read over a displayed finite sequence window may be consumed as a finite family-modulus route for \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-equicontinuity-namecert}. The read first exposes the shared $\UniformModulusUp$ row, the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window, the compact-metric source boundary, regular rational readback, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row; only then may an $\EquicontinuityUp$ consumer inspect the same window as a finite family indexed by the displayed sequence positions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the uniformly continuous sequence carrier through \autoref{def:uniformly-continuous-sequence-carrier}. Its shared modulus is already attached to the displayed sequence window, so the finite family read does not choose a new radius schedule.

Read the equicontinuity carrier through \autoref{def:equicontinuity-carrier}. That carrier asks for a finite family ledger, a common tolerance-radius handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. The sequence window supplies exactly that route when its displayed indices are treated as the family index set.

Thus the dependency is a forward consumer edge from the sequence packet to $\EquicontinuityUp$. It does not add selected subsequences, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
