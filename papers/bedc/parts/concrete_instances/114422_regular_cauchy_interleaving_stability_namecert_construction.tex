Following Bishop and Bridges, \emph{Constructive Analysis}, the $\RegularCauchyInterleavingStabilityUp$ packet isolates the finite stability surface for interleaved regular Cauchy names. It reads the regular rational rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite stream schedules of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the interleaving carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchyinterleaving-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives Real and Completion consumers a named stability certificate between selected even--odd windows and the shared modulus ledger; it does not identify quotient reals, choose host limits, or invoke ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy interleaving stability carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-interleaving-stability-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyInterleavingStabilityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(A,B,S_A,S_B,\sigma,M,W,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ and $B$ are the two accepted $\RegSeqRatUp$ source rows whose finite regular Cauchy windows are interleaved.
\item $S_A$ and $S_B$ are the selected $\StreamNameUp$ schedules for the even and odd sides.
\item $\sigma$ is the alternating selector row inherited from the $\RegularCauchyInterleavingUp$ carrier.
\item $M$ is the shared $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ modulus ledger, carrying the matched dyadic budgets for selected even and odd requests.
\item $W$ records the selected even and odd $\RegSeqRatUp$ windows after the schedules and shared modulus have been displayed.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row exposed only after $A,B,S_A,S_B,\sigma,M,W$ have been read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source rows, schedules, selector, shared modulus ledger, selected windows, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite route.
\item $P$ records the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyInterleavingStabilityUp$, $\RegularCauchyInterleavingUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyInterleavingStabilityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,B,S_A,S_B,\sigma,M,W,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient-real equality, selected host limits, ambient completeness, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy interleaving stability obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-interleaving-stability-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyInterleavingStabilityUp$ carrier
$$
T=(A,B,S_A,S_B,\sigma,M,W,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyInterleavingStabilityUp}$ surface is exhausted by five obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission: the accepted object is exactly the displayed packet $A,B,S_A,S_B,\sigma,M,W,E,H,C,P,N$;
\item selected-window stability: the even and odd rows in $W$ are read from $A$ and $B$ through $S_A,S_B,\sigma$ without changing the selected finite regular Cauchy windows;
\item shared-modulus exactness: every selected even or odd request uses the corresponding component of the single dyadic ledger $M$;
\item componentwise transport: $H$ carries $\hsame$ transport for each displayed row and $C$ replays the same finite route through $\Cont$;
\item Real seal nonescape: $E$ exports no quotient real, selected host limit, ambient completeness principle, or bridge-checked schema beyond the displayed rows.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy interleaving stability classifier]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-interleaving-stability-classifier}
Accepted $\RegularCauchyInterleavingStabilityUp$ packets classify together exactly when the selected $\RegSeqRatUp$ windows and shared modulus rows transport componentwise through the displayed $\hsame$ and $\Cont$ routes. Equivalently, the classifier sees the same two source rows $A,B$, the same schedules $S_A,S_B$, the same alternating selector $\sigma$, the same shared dyadic ledger $M$, the same selected-window row $W$, and the same Real seal boundary $E$ up to the displayed transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
