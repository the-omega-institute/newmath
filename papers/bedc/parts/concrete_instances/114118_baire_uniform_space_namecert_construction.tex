\begin{definition}[Baire uniform-space carrier]
\label{def:baire-uniform-space-carrier}
A $\BaireUniformSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(B,M,E,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BaireSpaceUp$ finite-prefix row, naming two inspected streams and the common prefix window.
\item $M$ is the $\BaireMetricUp$ common-prefix distance row, recording the dyadic radius attached to the first displayed disagreement or to agreement on the inspected window.
\item $E$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ entourage row generated by finite prefix agreement requests.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which prefix coordinates and requested entourage depths are read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Baire prefix, metric, entourage, window, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same prefix-entourage route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BaireUniformSpaceUp$, $\BaireSpaceUp$, $\BaireMetricUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BaireUniformSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,M,E,S,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected infinite branches, quotient stream equality, countable choice, ambient Real completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Baire uniform-space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:baire-uniform-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BaireUniformSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
U=(B,M,E,S,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BaireUniformSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed Baire prefix, common-prefix metric, uniform entourage, finite window, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; prefix-entourage stability under componentwise $\hsame$; displayed $\Cont$ locality; and non-escape for open-cover compactness, selected branches, quotient stream equality, countable choice, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:baire-uniform-space-carrier}. The only analytic rows are the finite prefix row $B$, common-prefix metric row $M$, generated entourage row $E$, and finite window row $S$.

The uniformity is read through $E$ after $B,M,S$ have displayed the finite prefix request. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same prefix-entourage route.

Finally $P$ and $N$ source and name the accepted packet. Since the excluded compactness, branch-selection, quotient-equality, choice, and completeness objects have no carrier coordinate, no NameCert projection can export them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Baire uniform-space complete-metric handoff]
\label{thm:baire-uniform-space-complete-metric-handoff}
Every $\CompleteBaireSpaceUp$ or $\CompleteMetricBaireSurfaceUp$ consumer of an accepted $\BaireUniformSpaceUp$ packet must first read the finite prefix row $B$, common-prefix metric row $M$, generated entourage row $E$, finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row $S$, componentwise transport row $H$, displayed replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local name $N$. The handoff is a finite prefix-entourage route, not a selected limit branch, quotient stream equality, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations. They force every uniform-space read to pass through $B,M,E,S,H,C,P,N$.

A completion-facing consumer can therefore inspect only finite prefix agreement and the common-prefix metric radius before it reaches any complete-Baire row. The generated entourage row $E$ is downstream of those finite observations.

The structural rows preserve the route. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming do not add selected branches, quotient equality, countable choice, compactness, or ambient completeness, so the handoff remains finite.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:baire-uniform-space-nonescape}
Every public read from an accepted $\BaireUniformSpaceUp$ packet factors through

$$
The packet exports finite prefix-entourage data and no open-cover compactness theorem, chosen branch, quotient stream equality, countable choice schedule, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.

\begin{proof}

The refused objects have no coordinate in the carrier and no continuation route, so no public read can export them.
