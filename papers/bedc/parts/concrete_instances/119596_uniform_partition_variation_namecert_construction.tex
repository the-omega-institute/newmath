Following Jordan's bounded-variation ledgers and the Bishop--Bridges treatment of finite mesh integration, the $\UniformPartitionVariationUp$ packet records the variation read available on a displayed uniform partition. It sits after the equal-mesh carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-partition-namecert}, the finite variation partition surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-variation-partition-namecert}, the Darboux-sum route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-darboux-sum-namecert}, the Riemann-sum chapter of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannsum-namecert}, and the $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ numeric rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}. The packet names a finite uniform-mesh variation ledger; it does not claim a supremum over all partitions, selected refinement families, measure completion, quotient equality of functions, or ambient real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Uniform partition variation carrier]
\label{def:uniform-partition-variation-carrier}
A $\UniformPartitionVariationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(I,M,V,D,R,S,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\RealUp$ interval and endpoint source row.
\item $M$ is the accepted $\UniformPartitionUp$ equal-mesh row over that interval.
\item $V$ is the $\FiniteVariationPartitionUp$ ledger row obtained from the same ordered cells.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ mesh-width and adjacent-difference budget row.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the finite adjacent-difference and tolerance data.
\item $S$ is the $\DarbouxSumUp$ or Riemann-sum consumer handoff row, present only after $M,V,D,R$ have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the finite variation ledger, listing the adjacent absolute-difference reads and their finite sum along the uniform mesh.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $I,M,V,D,R,S,L$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the interval and uniform mesh through the variation ledger, numeric readback, Darboux or Riemann handoff, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformPartitionVariationUp$, $\UniformPartitionUp$, $\FiniteVariationPartitionUp$, $\DarbouxSumUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformPartitionVariationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $I,M,V,D,R,S,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host variation supremum, a chosen refinement sequence, quotient equality of functions, measure completion, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform partition variation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-partition-variation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformPartitionVariationUp$ carrier $U=(I,M,V,D,R,S,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UniformPartitionVariationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, uniform-mesh exposure through $M$, finite variation-partition exposure through $V$, dyadic budget exposure through $D$, regular-rational readback through $R$, Darboux or Riemann consumer handoff through $S$, finite variation-ledger exactness through $L$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The only visible rows are the interval source, uniform mesh, finite variation partition, dyadic budget, regular-rational readback, consumer handoff, finite ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

The mesh and variation rows are tied to the same finite cells. Row $M$ supplies the equal-width partition, and row $V$ reads that partition as a finite variation ledger rather than as an arbitrary partition family.

The numeric side is finite. The dyadic row $D$ stores mesh widths and adjacent-difference budgets, while $R$ turns those displayed budgets into regular-rational readback before a real-facing consumer may use the ledger.

The handoff and structural rows do not add hidden analytic data. Row $S$ exposes only the Darboux or Riemann consumer boundary, row $L$ stores the finite variation sum, and $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly that packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform partition variation Darboux route]
\label{thm:uniform-partition-variation-darboux-route}
Every Darboux-facing or Riemann-sum-facing read of an accepted $\UniformPartitionVariationUp$ packet factors through
$$
\UniformPartitionUp \longmapsto \FiniteVariationPartitionUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \DarbouxSumUp
$$
before the finite ledger $L$ is public. The route gives consumers a uniform-mesh variation ledger over displayed real endpoints and numeric readback rows, not a host supremum over all partitions or a selected refinement process.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the uniform partition row $M$. It fixes the equal-width cells over the interval row $I$, so the variation packet has a displayed mesh before any adjacent difference is read.

Next apply the finite variation partition row $V$. It reads the same ordered cells as a finite adjacent-difference ledger and keeps the construction inside the sibling surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-variation-partition-namecert}.

The numeric rows $D$ and $R$ then expose dyadic budgets and regular-rational readback for the same finite differences. The Darboux or Riemann handoff $S$ is available only after those rows have been displayed.

Finally the ledger row $L$ records the finite variation sum, and the rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier contains no supremum, refinement selector, quotient function equality, measure completion, or ambient completeness coordinate, none can be exported by the route.
\end{proof}
