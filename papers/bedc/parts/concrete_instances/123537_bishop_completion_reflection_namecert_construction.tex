\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BishopCompletionReflectionUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopCompletionReflectionUp$ packet records the L10 completion-reflection interface as a finite NameCert route. It reads the regular rational source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the completion schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionmonad-namecert}, the extension row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completionuniversalproperty-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet exposes reflection data only through displayed rows; it does not form quotient completions, select limits, assert countable choice, compare host equality, or claim ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Bishop completion reflection carrier]
\label{def:bishop-completion-reflection-carrier}
A $\BishopCompletionReflectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(R,S,D,M,U,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the regular-Cauchy row over rational readback, inherited from $\RegSeqRatUp$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row used by the reflection consumer before any Real seal is read.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row controlling the displayed Cauchy and window comparisons.
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyCompletionMonadUp$ schedule row naming the completion request without quotienting stream names.
\item $U$ is the $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ row through which extension consumers replay the same request.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ terminal seal row exposed after $R,S,D,M,U$ are displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the regular-Cauchy, stream-window, dyadic, monad, universal-property, Real-seal, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion-reflection request through
$$
R,S,D,M,U,E \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopCompletionReflectionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\CauchyCompletionMonadUp$, $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCompletionReflectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,S,D,M,U,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected limit data, countable-choice schedules, host equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop completion reflection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-completion-reflection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopCompletionReflectionUp$ carrier
$$
B=(R,S,D,M,U,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCompletionReflectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five finite rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed packet $B=(R,S,D,M,U,E,H,C,P,N)$;
\item L10 source exposure through the regular-Cauchy, stream-window, and dyadic rows $R,S,D$;
\item completion-reflection exposure through the monad schedule $M$, universal-property row $U$, and terminal Real seal $E$;
\item transport and replay stability through $H$ and $C$, requiring every reflected consumer read to pass through the displayed rows;
\item provenance and local naming through $P$ and $N$, with no coordinate for quotient completions, selected limits, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-completion-reflection-carrier}. The first obligation is immediate because every public row is displayed in the finite tuple before the local name row is read.

The L10 source rows are $R,S,D$. They expose regular rational readback, finite stream windows, and dyadic tolerance before any completion-facing row can be consumed.

The reflection rows are $M,U,E$. The monad schedule names the completion request, the universal-property row replays extension consumption, and the Real seal is read only after the source and reflection rows have been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier contains no quotient, selected-limit, choice, host-equality, or ambient-completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop completion reflection finite route]
\label{thm:bishop-completion-reflection-finite-route}
Every reflection consumer of an accepted $\BishopCompletionReflectionUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\RegSeqRatUp,\quad \StreamNameUp,\quad \DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad \CauchyCompletionMonadUp,\quad \CompletionUniversalPropertyUp,\quad \RealUp,\quad \BHist,\quad \hsame,\quad \Cont,\quad \Pkg,\quad \NameCert .
$$
The factorization obtains exactly the displayed rows $R,S,D,M,U,E,H,C,P,N$. It obtains no quotient completion, selected limit, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:bishop-completion-reflection-namecert-obligations}. They force every reflected consumer to expose the regular-Cauchy, stream-window, dyadic, monad, universal-property, and Real-seal rows before the local certificate can be consumed.

Use the replay row $C$ and transport row $H$ to keep the consumer on the displayed route. These rows move only among the listed coordinates, so they cannot introduce a quotient carrier, a selected limit, a countable-choice schedule, host equality, or ambient completeness.

The provenance and local naming rows $P,N$ then package the same finite route as $\NameCert_{\BishopCompletionReflectionUp}$. Thus every reflection consumer factors through exactly the displayed L10 completion-reflection surface and nothing outside it.
\end{proof}
