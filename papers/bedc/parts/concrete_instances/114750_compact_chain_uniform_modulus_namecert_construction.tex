It realizes the finite cover-refinement branch of \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity}, sitting between the compact carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, the metric-distance rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the continuous-map local-radius ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the rational lower-bound readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the uniform-modulus consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Compact chain uniform-modulus carrier]
\label{def:compact-chain-uniform-modulus-carrier}
A $\CompactChainUniformModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U_{\mathrm{cc}}=(K,M,F,C,L,Q,R,E,H,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $K$ is the $\CompactUp$ finite-cover row, exposing the compact source only through a displayed finite cover or finite net ledger.
  \item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ distance row used to compare source cells, centers, and refinement radii.
  \item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ local-modulus row, attaching a positive local radius to each displayed center and tolerance read.
  \item $C$ is the cover-chain row, ordering the finite refinement from compact cover cells to local-radius cells before any uniform-modulus handoff is exported.
  \item $L$ is the finite lower-bound row over the displayed positive rational radii.
  \item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ readback row for lower-bound comparisons, positivity, and finite minimum inspection.
  \item $R$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row, available only after $K,M,F,C,L,Q$ have been displayed.
  \item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for real-valued consumers of the same modulus packet.
  \item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact cover, metric, continuous-map, chain, lower-bound, rational readback, uniform-modulus, seal, provenance, and naming rows.
  \item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactChainUniformModulusUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RatUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
  \item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactChainUniformModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these eleven rows. There is no coordinate for selected subcovers outside $K$, quotient equality of covers, a choice-selected modulus function, hidden point witnesses, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact chain uniform-modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-chain-uniform-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactChainUniformModulusUp$ carrier $U_{\mathrm{cc}}=(K,M,F,C,L,Q,R,E,H,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactChainUniformModulusUp}$ surface is exhausted by five finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item compact cover-chain admission for $K$ and $C$;
  \item metric and continuous-map local-radius compatibility for $M$ and $F$;
  \item finite lower-bound exactness for $L$ and $\RatUp$ readback $Q$;
  \item uniform-modulus and real-seal handoff through $R$ and $E$;
  \item refusal, transport, provenance, and local naming through $H,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is discharged by choice-selected moduli, quotient cover equality, hidden subcover selection, external point equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier into the displayed rows. The compact and cover-chain rows $K,C$ expose the only finite source cover inspected by the packet, while $M,F$ attach the metric and local-radius data to that same chain.

The lower-bound rows are then read finitely. The row $L$ records the finite positive-radius comparison, and $Q$ supplies the rational readback used to keep the selected tolerance inside every displayed local radius.

Finally, the handoff rows $R,E$ are downstream of those cover-chain and lower-bound rows. Componentwise transport, provenance, and local naming preserve the same packet, so the excluded choice, quotient, hidden-selection, point-equality, and completeness principles cannot enter the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite cover-chain handoff]
\label{thm:compact-chain-uniform-modulus-finite-cover-chain-handoff}
Every consumer that reads an accepted $\CompactChainUniformModulusUp$ packet as a $\UniformModulusUp$ witness must first display the compact cover row $K$, metric row $M$, continuous local-radius row $F$, cover-chain row $C$, finite lower-bound row $L$, and $\RatUp$ readback row $Q$. The exported object is the handoff row $R$ together with the optional $\RealUp$ seal $E$; it is not a selected modulus function or a quotient class of covers.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The carrier definition places $R$ downstream of $K,M,F,C,L,Q$. A consumer cannot inspect the uniform-modulus row without first reading the finite compact cover, the metric comparisons, the local continuous-map radii, and the rational lower-bound ledger.

The structural rows $H,P,N$ keep that read componentwise inside the same $\BHist$ packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores selected modulus functions or quotient cover classes, the handoff exports only the displayed finite cover-chain route.
\end{proof}
