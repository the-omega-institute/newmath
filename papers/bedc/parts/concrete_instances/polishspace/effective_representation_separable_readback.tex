\label{thm:polish-space-effective-representation-separable-readback}
Let $P=(M,K,D,S,R,W,H,C,G,N)$ be an accepted $\PolishSpaceUp$ packet. Any represented-space, admissible-representation, effective Polish, or computable Polish consumer of $P$ must display the complete-separable metric row, the dense schedule, the $\StreamNameUp$ windows, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, and the local $\NameCert_{\PolishSpaceUp}$ support before it reads an effective representation. Thus the represented or computable vocabulary is downstream of the same finite route
$$
M,K,D,S,R,W,H,C,G,N
$$
rather than a detached name oracle, selected dense sequence, host equality, countable-choice schedule, quotient completion, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness datum.
\end{theorem}
