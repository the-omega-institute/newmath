The classifier compares accepted packets only through these ten rows.
\end{definition}

Every accepted read must expose $P,R,W,C,B,E,H,T,Q,N$ in that order before a finite-window convergence consumer receives data, and no obligation is stored outside those rows.

Project the carrier of \autoref{def:strong-law-finite-window-carrier}. The rows $P,R,W$ expose the probability source, random-variable family, and finite schedule before any tail estimate can be read.

The rows $C,B,E$ then route the finite-window estimate through the displayed Chebyshev tail table, the Borel--Cantelli finite-event budget, and the positive real tolerance seal. Each row is finite and remains tied to the visible windows.

$$
P\longmapsto R\longmapsto W\longmapsto C\longmapsto B\longmapsto E

has been displayed. \begin{proof}
