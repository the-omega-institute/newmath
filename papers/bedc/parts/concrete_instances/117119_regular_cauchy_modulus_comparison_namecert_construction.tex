\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RegularCauchyModulusComparisonUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-modulus-comparison-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\RegularCauchyModulusComparisonUp$ packet records the finite comparison object that lets a completion-facing modulus row be checked against a regular Cauchy readback before any Real or Completion consumer reads a terminal seal. It sits between the completion-modulus discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishopcompletionmodulus-namecert}, the generic Cauchy threshold surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchymodulus-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a comparison route for L10 Real and Completion work; it does not select limits, quotient streams, host representatives, countable-choice schedules, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy modulus comparison carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-modulus-comparison-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyModulusComparisonUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(B,M,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BishopCompletionModulusUp$ row, recording the completion-facing modulus budget that later consumers may reuse.
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyModulusUp$ threshold row, recording the finite tail request attached to the same tolerance.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, recording the finite observations inspected after $B$ and $M$ have fixed the requested budget.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, recording the regular rational data obtained from those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, recording the dyadic comparison ledger shared by $B,M,W,R$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after the displayed completion-modulus, threshold, window, readback, and dyadic rows have been read.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the analytic rows and the structural rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through $B,M,W,R,D,E$.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyModulusComparisonUp$, $\BishopCompletionModulusUp$, $\CauchyModulusUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ data.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyModulusComparisonUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
Accepted carriers are compared only through $B,M,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. No coordinate stores a quotient stream, selected limit, host real equality, ambient completion object, or $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy modulus comparison NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-modulus-comparison-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyModulusComparisonUp$ carrier $K=(B,M,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyModulusComparisonUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the completion modulus $B$, Cauchy threshold $M$, finite windows $W$, regular readback $R$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, Real seal $E$, transport $H$, replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$;
\item modulus compatibility, requiring $B$ and $M$ to answer the same displayed dyadic tolerance request before any window row is inspected;
\item readback comparison, requiring $R$ to be obtained only from the $\StreamNameUp$ windows $W$ under the dyadic ledger $D$ and the threshold discipline supplied by $B$ and $M$;
\item Real-seal nonescape, requiring the terminal row $E$ to be downstream of $B,M,W,R,D$ and to export no selected limit, quotient stream, host equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness;
\item structural preservation, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name the same finite comparison packet.
\end{enumerate}
No additional NameCert obligation is available from the comparison carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is the projection of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-modulus-comparison-carrier}. The tuple lists exactly the completion-modulus, Cauchy-threshold, finite-window, readback, dyadic, Real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

For modulus compatibility, the only tolerance rows are $B$, $M$, and $D$. A consumer must therefore read the dyadic request through $D$ and then expose both the completion-facing modulus row and the Cauchy-threshold row before it can inspect any StreamName window.

Readback comparison is downstream of the same rows. The row $W$ supplies the finite observations, and $R$ repacks those observations as regular rational data under the displayed threshold and tolerance discipline.

Finally $E$ is the terminal Real-facing row, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same packet. Since no quotient stream, selected limit, host equality, choice schedule, or ambient completeness coordinate occurs in the carrier, those objects cannot be exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy modulus comparison stability]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-modulus-comparison-stability}
Let $K=(B,M,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RegularCauchyModulusComparisonUp$ carrier. If a later tolerance request is discharged by the same displayed $\BishopCompletionModulusUp$ row $B$ and $\CauchyModulusUp$ row $M$ with a refined $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger still recorded in $D$, then every accepted Real-facing read factors through the same $\StreamNameUp$ window route, the same $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, and the same terminal $\RealUp$ seal row. Thus refinement preserves the comparison between the completion modulus and the regular Cauchy readback.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the refined tolerance request. By the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-modulus-comparison-namecert-obligations}, the request is still read through $D$ and must expose the same completion-modulus and Cauchy-threshold rows $B$ and $M$.

The finite window row is downstream of those rows. Hence the refined request reaches observations only through $W$, and the regular rational readback can only be the displayed row $R$ attached to those windows.

The terminal Real-facing consumer then reads $E$ after $B,M,W,R,D$. Componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same packet, so the refined request stays on the original comparison route.
\end{proof}
