\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\NamiokaJointContinuityUp$}

Following Namioka and Kelley, the $\NamiokaJointContinuityUp$ packet records the finite window in which separate continuity over a Baire source and compact target becomes a displayed joint-continuity read. It sits after the continuous-map carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the compact-metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the compact fallback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, the Baire source row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bairespace-namecert}, the real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}. The packet names a finite separate-to-joint continuity window; it does not assert a host product theorem, selected dense $G_\delta$ set, open-cover compactness, pointwise choice principle, or ambient equality of real-valued functions.

\begin{definition}[Namioka joint-continuity carrier]
\label{def:namioka-joint-continuity-carrier}
A $\NamiokaJointContinuityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(X,K,T_X,T_K,F,S,W,M,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\BaireSpaceUp$ source row, exposing only the finite nonempty-open and residual-window data read by the packet;
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ target row, or the $\CompactUp$ target row when the metric window is not part of the local read;
\item $T_X$ and $T_K$ are the two $\TopologyUp$ rows through which source and target neighbourhoods are named;
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row for the separately continuous map into the $\RealUp$ seal;
\item $S$ records the two separate-continuity ledgers, one for source sections and one for compact-target sections;
\item $W$ is the finite product-window ledger naming the inspected source neighbourhood, compact-target window, and real tolerance;
\item $M$ is the compact finite-radius or finite-subcover row used to keep the target window finite;
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ oscillation seal reached only after $X,K,T_X,T_K,F,S,W,M$ have been displayed;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, target, topology, continuity, window, compactness, real seal, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from separate section reads to the product-window read;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NamiokaJointContinuityUp$, $\BaireSpaceUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\NamiokaJointContinuityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host product topology theorem, selected dense $G_\delta$ subset, open-cover compactness principle, arbitrary choice of sections, quotient equality of real-valued maps, or ambient function extensionality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Namioka joint-continuity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:namioka-joint-continuity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\NamiokaJointContinuityUp$ carrier
$$
J=(X,K,T_X,T_K,F,S,W,M,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\NamiokaJointContinuityUp}$ obligations are exactly carrier admission, Baire source exposure, compact or compact-metric target exposure, source and target topology exposure, separately continuous $\ContinuousMapUp$ exposure, finite product-window admission, compact finite-radius or finite-subcover control, $\RealUp$ oscillation-seal factorization, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:namioka-joint-continuity-carrier}. The rows $X,K,T_X,T_K,F,S,W,M,R,H,C,P,N$ are the complete displayed packet.

The Baire and topology rows supply the source windows, while the compact or compact-metric row supplies the target finite window. The continuous-map row $F$ and separate-continuity ledger $S$ expose only sectionwise continuity before any product window is read.

The rows $W,M,R$ then record the joint finite window, compact control, and real oscillation seal. Transport, replay, provenance, and naming are exactly $H,C,P,N$, so no further obligation row is available.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Namioka joint-continuity finite-window scope]
\label{thm:namioka-joint-continuity-finite-window-scope}
Every accepted $\NamiokaJointContinuityUp$ consumer that obtains a joint-continuity read must first expose the finite route
$$
X,K,T_X,T_K,F,S,W,M,R,H,C,P,N.
$$
The read is scoped to a displayed Baire source window, compact target window, separate-continuity ledger, product-window ledger, and $\RealUp$ oscillation seal. It does not export a global host Namioka theorem, selected residual set, arbitrary product topology, open-cover compactness, quotient equality of maps, or function extensionality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligation surface of \autoref{thm:namioka-joint-continuity-namecert-obligations}. It forces every consumer to expose the source, target, topology, continuous-map, section, product-window, compact-control, real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The product-window row $W$ can be read only after the section ledger $S$ and compact-control row $M$ are visible. Thus the joint-continuity read is a finite window over the displayed $\BaireSpaceUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$ or $\CompactUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows.

Because the carrier contains no row for a selected residual set, host product theorem, arbitrary product topology, open-cover compactness, quotient map equality, or function extensionality, none of those claims can escape from the finite-window read.
\end{proof}
