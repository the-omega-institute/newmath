\begin{theorem}[Padic external-scope constructive-boundary exhaustion]
\label{thm:padic-external-scope-boundary-exhaustion}
For the declared mathlib p-adic scope consisting of $\mathbb{Z}_p$ ring and
completeness, $\mathbb{Q}_p$ valued-field structure with valuation, norm, and
ultrametric comparison, Hensel existence and uniqueness, FTA as
unique-factorisation arithmetic used by p-adic valuations, mathlib
\texttt{PadicInt}/\texttt{Padic}
quotient-completion objects with their standard Real-valued norm reading, and
the global product-formula package, \autoref{tab:padic-decomposition}
exhausts the scope as
$$
S_{\mathrm{padic}}
=
S_{\mathrm{BEDC\ kernel}}
\sqcup
S_{\mathrm{measured/refused\ classical\ boundary}}.
$$
There is no hidden third region in this declared scope.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first four rows of \autoref{tab:padic-decomposition} name the BEDC
constructive kernel: the $\mathbb{Z}_p$ ring and completeness rows, the
$\mathbb{Q}_p$ field/valuation/norm/completeness summary, the Hensel
existence-and-uniqueness rows, and the FTA / unique-factorisation arithmetic
used by valuation reads. The last two rows name the refused classical boundary: the
quotient-completion \texttt{PadicInt}/\texttt{Padic} objects with their
Real-valued norm reading, and the global product-formula package with its
archimedean and all-places footprint. These rows are pairwise classified by
their status column, and the scope sentence lists exactly these six items.
\end{proof}
