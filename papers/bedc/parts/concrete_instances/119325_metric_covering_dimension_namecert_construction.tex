\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\MetricCoveringDimensionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-metric-covering-dimension-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Hurewicz and Wallman on covering dimension and Bishop--Bridges finite metric compactness, the $\MetricCoveringDimensionUp$ packet records finite-order metric covers over an explicit L10 compact-metric substrate. It sits after the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the compact finite-net rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite refinement route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lebesgue-covering-dimension-namecert}, and the dyadic mesh ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicmesh-namecert}. The packet names only finite cover rows, order ledgers, star-refinement or mesh handoff rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it is not an arbitrary open-cover compactness theorem, a choice-selected refinement principle, a paracompactness assertion, or an ambient $\RealUp$ completeness claim.

\begin{definition}[Metric covering dimension carrier]
\label{def:metric-covering-dimension-carrier}
A $\MetricCoveringDimensionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(M,K,U,O,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the metric source row, exposing the accepted $\MetricUp$ or $\MetricSpaceUp$ distance interface used by the cover cells.
\item $K$ is the compact-metric substrate row, exposing the finite-net and finite-cover boundary over the same metric source.
\item $U$ is the finite metric-cover row, listing named center-radius cells or named finite cover cells over $M,K$.
\item $O$ is the finite order-bound row, recording that every displayed point-history inspection meets at most the chosen number of cover cells.
\item $S$ is the star-refinement or mesh row, routing the finite cover through the dyadic mesh handoff and the sibling covering-dimension refinement surface.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,K,U,O,S$ and the certificate rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from metric source and compact finite-net data through finite cover inspection, order checking, star-refinement or mesh handoff, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetricCoveringDimensionUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\LebesgueCoveringDimensionUp$, $\DyadicMeshUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetricCoveringDimensionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only by transporting these rows. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, host paracompactness, selected subcovers outside the displayed finite bundle, quotient compacta, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Metric covering dimension NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metric-covering-dimension-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetricCoveringDimensionUp$ carrier
$$
D=(M,K,U,O,S,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MetricCoveringDimensionUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric source exposure through $M$, compact-metric finite-net exposure through $K$, finite cover admission through $U$, order-bound checking through $O$, star-refinement or dyadic-mesh handoff through $S$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metric-covering-dimension-carrier}. The coordinates $M,K,U,O,S$ are the only rows that expose metric source data, compact finite-net data, finite cover cells, overlap order, and mesh or star-refinement handoff.

The structural coordinates $H,C,P,N$ preserve that route. Transport is componentwise, replay follows the displayed finite cover inspection, provenance names the same upstream packets, and the local naming row certifies no additional object.

Since arbitrary open covers, selected refinements, paracompactness data, quotient compacta, and ambient completeness are absent from the carrier, no local NameCert obligation can export them. The listed rows therefore exhaust the certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Metric covering dimension refinement stability]
\label{thm:metric-covering-dimension-refinement-stability}
Let $D=(M,K,U,O,S,H,C,P,N)$ and $D'=(M',K',U',O',S',H',C',P',N')$ be accepted $\MetricCoveringDimensionUp$ carriers. If the displayed $\hsame$ transport identifies the metric source rows, compact-metric rows, finite cover rows, order ledgers, and star-refinement or mesh rows componentwise, then every finite-order metric-cover consumer of $D$ replays as a finite-order metric-cover consumer of $D'$ through the same bound and the same mesh handoff route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the consumer through the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:metric-covering-dimension-namecert-obligations}. The consumer may inspect only $M,K,U,O,S$ before structural replay and provenance.

Apply the assumed componentwise $\hsame$ transport to those rows. The metric source, compact substrate, finite cover, order ledger, and star-refinement or mesh handoff are carried to the corresponding rows of $D'$ without adding a new cover selector.

Replay through $C'$ and source the result through $P',N'$. The order bound remains the displayed bound carried by the transported order ledger, and the mesh handoff remains the displayed handoff carried by $S'$. Thus refinement stability is internal to the finite packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Metric covering dimension finite mesh handoff]
\label{thm:metric-covering-dimension-finite-mesh-handoff}
Every $\CompactMetricUp$, $\LebesgueCoveringDimensionUp$, $\DyadicMeshUp$, or $\MetricUp$ consumer of an accepted $\MetricCoveringDimensionUp$ packet must traverse the finite route
$$
M,K,U,O,S,H,C,P,N
$$
before it can use a dimension bound. In particular, the dimension read is a finite metric-cover and mesh handoff over the compact substrate, not a general open-cover compactness principle, an arbitrary paracompactness result, a choice-selected cover refinement, or an ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the accepted carrier. The source metric row $M$ and compact-metric row $K$ must be exposed before any finite cover cell in $U$ can be read.

The order ledger $O$ and the handoff row $S$ then place the cover inside the finite covering-dimension and dyadic-mesh interfaces. A consumer that omits those rows has not displayed the finite order bound or the mesh route.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Because the carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover compactness, choice, paracompactness, quotient compacta, or ambient Real completeness, the finite mesh handoff is the entire exported surface.
\end{proof}
