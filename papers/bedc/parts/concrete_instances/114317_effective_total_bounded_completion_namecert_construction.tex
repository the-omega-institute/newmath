It sits after the finite stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the complete-metric seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, and the finite-net carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}. \begin{definition}[EffectiveTotalBoundedCompletion carrier]
\label{def:effective-total-bounded-completion-carrier}
An $\EffectiveTotalBoundedCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(K,E,Q,M,C,A,W,R,S,H,L,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the bounded metric source row whose distance and diameter requests are read by the packet.
\item $E$ is the finite-net row, displaying for each dyadic tolerance a finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ of centers and coverage witnesses.
\item $Q$ is the Cauchy-net refinement row, recording that later finite nets refine earlier tolerances by displayed containment and proximity witnesses.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ route row consumed by completion-facing readers.
\item $C$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ seal row, available only after $E,Q,M$ have been displayed.
\item $A$ is the total-bounded approximation ledger, binding finite-net centers to the Cauchy-net refinement route.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row used to inspect source and center data.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row for the same finite windows.
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached from the displayed readback route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,E,Q,M,C,A,W,R,S,L,P,N$.
\item $L$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of finite-net, Cauchy-net, completion, and readback requests.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EffectiveTotalBoundedCompletionUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EffectiveTotalBoundedCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, quotient classes of Cauchy sequences, selected representatives, host complete metric objects, or choice.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[EffectiveTotalBoundedCompletion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:effective-total-bounded-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EffectiveTotalBoundedCompletionUp$ carrier $T=(K,E,Q,M,C,A,W,R,S,H,L,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\EffectiveTotalBoundedCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of source admission through $K$, finite-net admission through $E$, Cauchy-net refinement through $Q$, MetricCompletion routing through $M$, CompleteMetric sealing through $C$, total-bounded approximation through $A$, StreamName window inspection through $W$, RegSeqRat readback through $R$, Real sealing through $S$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $L$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

Every completion-facing consumer must therefore display the finite-net and Cauchy-net rows before it can read the MetricCompletion or CompleteMetric rows, and every Real-facing consumer must display $W,R,S$ on the same route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic rows are $K,E,Q,M,C,A,W,R,S$, and the structural rows are $H,L,P,N$.

The total-bounded part is finite. The finite-net row $E$ supplies explicit centers and coverage witnesses, while $Q$ records refinement from one tolerance to the next. The approximation ledger $A$ binds those two rows before the completion rows $M,C$ are visible.

The readback part stays on the same packet. The window row $W$, regular rational row $R$, and Real seal row $S$ are downstream of $E,Q,A$ and are replayed through $L$. Componentwise transport $H$, package provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ do not add a second completion object. Since the carrier contains no open-cover, quotient, selected-limit, host-completeness, or choice coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[EffectiveTotalBoundedCompletion Cauchy-net induction]
\label{thm:effective-total-bounded-completion-cauchy-net-induction}
Let $T=(K,E,Q,M,C,A,W,R,S,H,L,P,N)$ be an accepted $\EffectiveTotalBoundedCompletionUp$ carrier. A completion-facing induction over Cauchy-net approximants may advance from tolerance $n$ to tolerance $n+1$ only by reading the finite-net row $E$, the refinement row $Q$, and the approximation ledger $A$ before it consumes the MetricCompletion row $M$, the CompleteMetric seal $C$, or the Real-facing rows $W,R,S$.

Consequently the induction exports the finite route
$$
K,E,Q,A \longmapsto M,C \longmapsto W,R,S \longmapsto H,L,P,N,
$$
not an open-cover compactness proof, a quotient class of Cauchy sequences, a choice-selected subsequence, or an ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the displayed finite-net row. At each tolerance, $E$ supplies the finite center list and coverage witnesses used by the current approximation.

Advance the induction through $Q$ and $A$. The refinement row identifies how the next tolerance is attached to the previous finite net, and the approximation ledger records that the same displayed witnesses remain within the total-bounded packet. No external cover selection is consulted.

Only after this finite step may a consumer read $M$ and $C$. The MetricCompletion and CompleteMetric rows are therefore seals over the displayed Cauchy-net route. The rows $W,R,S$ translate that route into StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real-facing readback, while $H,L,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name it without adding quotient equality or choice.
\end{proof}
