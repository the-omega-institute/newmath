\begin{definition}[Packet exponential-readout carrier]
\label{def:packet-exponential-readout-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the packet-operation row, recording the self-similar operation or flow operation as packet data.
\item $F$ is the scalar readout row, read as a displayed $\ContinuousMapUp$-style map from packet data to a scalar carrier.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row, recording the finite readout samples opened before a scalar shadow is inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ scalar row used by the readout value and by the displayed exponential comparison.
\item $A$ is the analytic normalization row, recording the finite ledger for unit value and self-change equations at the readout level.
\item $E$ is the exponential-shadow row, recording that the scalar readout has the displayed exponential value.
