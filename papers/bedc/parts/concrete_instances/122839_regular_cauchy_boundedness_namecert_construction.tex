\section{Regular Cauchy boundedness carrier}
\label{sec:regular-cauchy-boundedness-carrier}

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy boundedness carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-boundedness-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyBoundednessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(S,R,D,M,I,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation window naming the inspected rational values and the finite prefix whose maximum is used by the bound;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback row, exposing the regular Cauchy inequality used after the prefix window;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for the dyadic budgets and rational comparisons appearing in the prefix and tail estimates;
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyModulusUp$ threshold row telling which depth places every later value within the displayed dyadic tolerance of the chosen prefix value;
\item $I$ is the finite induction budget row, recording the prefix-length induction and the displayed bound assembled from the prefix maximum and the tail tolerance ledger;
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal handoff row reached only after $S,R,D,M,I$ have been read;
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream window, regular readback, dyadic ledger, modulus threshold, induction budget, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a boundedness request through the stream window, regular readback, dyadic ledger, modulus threshold, induction budget, and real seal;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyBoundednessUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\CauchyModulusUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyContinuousMapUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyBoundednessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports no host supremum, no quotient stream equality, no selected Cauchy limit, no global real completeness principle, and no hidden bound beyond the displayed finite induction budget.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy boundedness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-boundedness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyBoundednessUp$ carrier $B=(S,R,D,M,I,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyBoundednessUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for these ten rows, stream-window exposure through $S$, regular readback through $R$, dyadic tolerance exactness through $D$, threshold exposure through $M$, finite induction-budget exactness through $I$, real-seal routing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-boundedness-carrier}. The rows $S,R,D,M,I$ are the whole boundedness surface: the prefix is visible in $S$, the tail comparison is visible in $R$, dyadic arithmetic is visible in $D$, the threshold is visible in $M$, and the finite induction budget is visible in $I$.

The remaining rows do not add analytic content. The seal $E$ receives the displayed route, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet.

Since no coordinate stores a supremum, quotient stream equality, selected limit, or ambient completeness theorem, the stated rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy boundedness finite induction bound]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-boundedness-finite-induction-bound}
Every accepted $\RegularCauchyBoundednessUp$ carrier $B=(S,R,D,M,I,E,H,C,P,N)$ exposes a boundedness witness by the finite route
$$
S,R,D,M \longmapsto I .
$$
The induction budget $I$ first compares the finitely many values in the $\StreamNameUp$ prefix named by $S$, then uses the $\CauchyModulusUp$ threshold $M$ and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$ with the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger $D$ to cover every later displayed value. Thus the bound is a finite induction certificate, not a selected supremum, quotient-stream representative, host limit, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the prefix window in $S$. The finitely many inspected values admit an induction over the displayed prefix length, and the budget row $I$ records the resulting prefix bound.

Next read the tail route. The modulus threshold $M$ tells where the regular Cauchy comparison begins, $R$ supplies the regular rational readback after that depth, and $D$ supplies the dyadic tolerance arithmetic used to compare later values with the prefix witness.

Combine the prefix induction with the tail comparison in $I$. Because each step names only rows already present in $B$, the exported bound is the displayed finite induction certificate and not an off-packet supremum or completeness argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy boundedness Real handoff]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-boundedness-real-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$ or $\CauchyContinuousMapUp$ consumer of an accepted $\RegularCauchyBoundednessUp$ packet must read the route
$$
S,R,D,M,I,E,H,C,P,N
$$
before using the bounded regular Cauchy name. The handoff exposes the stream window, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, modulus threshold, finite induction budget, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows, and it refuses host functions, quotient streams, selected limits, detached suprema, and ambient real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-boundedness-namecert-obligations}. They require the stream, regular readback, dyadic, threshold, and induction rows to be present before the seal row can be consumed.

Use the finite induction bound of \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-boundedness-finite-induction-bound}. It identifies the boundedness witness as the displayed $S,R,D,M,I$ route, so a Real-facing or Cauchy-continuous-map-facing consumer has no separate bound to inspect.

Finally pass through $E,H,C,P,N$. These rows seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for host functions, quotient streams, selected limits, detached suprema, or completeness, the consumer receives only the displayed boundedness certificate.
\end{proof}
