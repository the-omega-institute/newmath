\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DirectedCofinalSubsetUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-directed-cofinal-subset-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Moore--Smith and Kelley directed subsets, the $\DirectedCofinalSubsetUp$ packet records a cofinal retained index surface inside an already named directed-index carrier. It refines the finite upper-window data of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-directedset-namecert} for downstream net and completion arguments that need to restrict an index substrate without quotienting indices, selecting a cofinal sequence, or importing a choice-indexed thinning function.

The packet is a sibling handoff for the net routes of $\CauchyNetUp$, $\CauchyNetLimitUp$, $\MooreSmithConvergenceUp$, and the completion-facing Cauchy chapters. It keeps the ambient directed packet and the retained cofinal packet visible at the same time, so a consumer can prove that every ambient finite request is met inside the retained surface by displayed rows rather than by an external cofinality theorem.

\begin{definition}[Directed cofinal subset carrier]
\label{def:directed-cofinal-subset-carrier}
A $\DirectedCofinalSubsetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(I,J,\preceq,W_I,W_J,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the ambient directed-index row read from a $\DirectedSetUp$ packet.
\item $J$ is the retained index row naming the finite cofinal subset surface.
\item $\preceq$ is the displayed comparison row inherited from the ambient directed packet and restricted to the retained rows when both endpoints lie in $J$.
\item $W_I$ is the ambient finite-window row for downstream net or completion requests.
\item $W_J$ is the retained finite-window row whose entries lie in $J$.
\item $U$ is the cofinal-witness row, assigning to every displayed ambient request in $W_I$ a retained address in $J$ above that request by $\preceq$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the ambient row, retained row, comparison row, ambient and retained windows, cofinal witnesses, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an ambient finite-window request through the retained cofinal witness route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DirectedCofinalSubsetUp$, $\DirectedSetUp$, the downstream net and completion consumers, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DirectedCofinalSubsetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no quotient of directed indices, no selected cofinal sequence, no countable choice schedule, no total-order refinement, and no host-level completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Directed cofinal subset NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:directed-cofinal-subset-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\DirectedCofinalSubsetUp$ carrier $S=(I,J,\preceq,W_I,W_J,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DirectedCofinalSubsetUp}$ surface consists of carrier admission for the displayed rows, ambient directed-index readability through $\DirectedSetUp$, retained-row stability for $J$ and $W_J$, cofinal-window coverage through $U$, componentwise transport and replay through $H$ and $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Every obligation is discharged by the finite rows of $S$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:directed-cofinal-subset-carrier}. The ambient index row, retained index row, comparison row, window rows, cofinal-witness row, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row are the complete visible packet.

Ambient directed-index readability is inherited from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-directedset-namecert}: comparisons and upper-window requests are read through the displayed $\DirectedSetUp$ rows before the retained subset is consulted.

For retained-row stability and cofinal-window coverage, inspect $J,W_J,U$. The retained window row names only addresses in $J$, and the cofinal-witness row supplies, for each ambient request in $W_I$, a retained address certified above the request by the displayed comparison row.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no coordinate stores quotient indices, a cofinal sequence, countable choice, a total-order refinement, or ambient completeness, the NameCert surface is exhausted by the rows of $S$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Directed cofinal subset net handoff]
\label{thm:directed-cofinal-subset-net-handoff}
Every $\CauchyNetUp$, $\CauchyNetLimitUp$, $\MooreSmithConvergenceUp$, or completion-facing consumer that reads a directed index through an accepted $\DirectedCofinalSubsetUp$ packet receives only the ambient directed row $I$, retained row $J$, comparison row $\preceq$, ambient and retained finite windows $W_I,W_J$, cofinal-witness row $U$, transport row $H$, replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$. The handoff may restrict a net read to the retained cofinal surface, but it does not provide a quotient net, selected subnet sequence, countable-choice selector, host directed set, or Real completeness datum.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a downstream finite-window request. The request is first read as an ambient directed-window row in $W_I$ over the $\DirectedSetUp$ substrate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-directedset-namecert}.

The cofinal witness row $U$ then names a retained address in $J$ above the requested ambient window, and $W_J$ records the retained window through which the downstream net or completion consumer continues.

Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ keep the same ambient row, retained row, comparison row, window rows, and cofinal witness. The provenance and naming rows package exactly that route, so the consumer cannot replace it by a quotient net, selected subnet sequence, countable-choice selector, host directed set, or Real completeness datum.
\end{proof}
