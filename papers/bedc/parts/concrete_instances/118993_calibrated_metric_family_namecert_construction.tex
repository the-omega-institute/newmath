\begin{definition}[Calibrated metric-family carrier]
\label{def:calibrated-metric-family-carrier}
A $\CalibratedMetricFamilyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(M,K,B,C,Pc,E,U,H,T,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row naming the common metric classifier, distance observations, and finite tolerance comparisons used by the family.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row for the displayed compact-metric domain or finite compact-metric witness consumed by calibration readers.
\item $B$ is the finite bracket row. It records a displayed list of lower and upper metric-family brackets and the finite tolerance at which the list is admitted.
\item $C$ is the $\MetricCoveringNumberUp$ row read at the same tolerance and metric source as $B$.
\item $Pc$ is the $\PackingNumberUp$ row read against the same metric source and tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the $\FiniteMetricEntropyUp$ row comparing the displayed bracket count, covering number, and packing lower bound as one finite entropy budget.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row. It exposes the calibration tolerance, entropy budget, and finite bracket envelope before any uniform-modulus consumer may inspect the family.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, compact-metric, bracket, covering, packing, entropy, uniform-modulus, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from any calibration request through $M,K,B,C,Pc,E,U,H$.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CalibratedMetricFamilyUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricCoveringNumberUp$, $\PackingNumberUp$, $\FiniteMetricEntropyUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CalibratedMetricFamilyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for host function spaces, open-cover compactness, measurable envelope selection, countable bracket families, quotient maps, selected limits, or choice.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Calibrated metric-family NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:calibrated-metric-family-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CalibratedMetricFamilyUp$ carrier
$$
F=(M,K,B,C,Pc,E,U,H,T,Q,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CalibratedMetricFamilyUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed metric source, compact-metric source, finite bracket list, covering row, packing row, entropy budget, uniform-modulus handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item bracket visibility, requiring every calibration read to inspect the finite bracket row $B$ and its tolerance before it reads an entropy or modulus consequence;
\item covering and packing exactness, requiring $C$ and $Pc$ to be read against the same $M,K,B$ tolerance surface;
\item entropy compatibility, requiring $E$ to compare only the displayed bracket count, covering upper bound, and packing lower-bound rows;
\item uniform-modulus handoff, requiring $U$ to consume the finite entropy and bracket envelope before exporting any modulus-facing data;
\item ledger nonescape, excluding host function-space compactness, selected brackets, quotient families, measurable envelopes, countable choice, selected limits, and ambient completeness as local certificate data.
\end{enumerate}
No calibrated metric-family obligation is stored outside $M,K,B,C,Pc,E,U,H,T,Q,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:calibrated-metric-family-carrier}. The packet displays the metric source, compact-metric source, finite bracket list, covering and packing rows, entropy ledger, modulus handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Bracket visibility comes next. A calibration reader reaches neither $E$ nor $U$ until it has inspected the finite bracket row $B$ and the tolerance shared with the metric and compact-metric sources.

The covering and packing rows are rowwise comparisons. They are read against the same $M,K,B$ surface, so their budgets cannot be replaced by a host covering argument, a selected packing, or a compactness principle outside the packet.

Finally $E$ and $U$ account for entropy and modulus-facing consumption, while $H,T,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier contains none of the excluded host or choice coordinates, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Calibrated metric-family entropy handoff]
\label{thm:calibrated-metric-family-entropy-handoff}
Every $\FiniteMetricEntropyUp$ or $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer of an accepted $\CalibratedMetricFamilyUp$ carrier factors through
$$
M,K,B \longmapsto C,Pc,E \longmapsto U,H,T,Q,N .
$$
The consumer receives a finite bracketing and entropy calibration ledger before any uniform-modulus read is exposed. It receives no host function-space compactness theorem, no selected bracket family, no quotient-family equality, no measurable envelope, no selected limit, and no countable-choice schedule.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier projection. The metric and compact-metric rows $M,K$ supply the only source surface, and the bracket row $B$ is the only place where a family envelope can be read.

The entropy-facing rows then open in order. The covering row $C$ and packing row $Pc$ are read at the displayed tolerance, and the finite entropy row $E$ compares those budgets with the finite bracket list.

Only after this entropy route is visible may the handoff row $U$ be consumed by a $\UniformModulusUp$ reader. The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows keep the same route attached under $\hsame$ and $\Cont$.

The refused host principles have no carrier coordinate. Therefore every entropy or modulus-facing consumer factors through the displayed finite route and cannot replace it by function-space compactness, selected brackets, quotient families, measurable envelopes, selected limits, or countable choice.
\end{proof}
