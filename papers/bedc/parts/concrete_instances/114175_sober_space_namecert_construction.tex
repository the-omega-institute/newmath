\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SoberSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sober-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Johnstone on sober spaces and locale theory, the $\SoberSpaceUp$ packet records the finite point-reconstruction boundary through which displayed open observations determine points without importing host powersets or point equality. It sits after the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the observable-open classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert}, the separation discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tzero-reflection-namecert}, and the point-free open route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locale-space-namecert}. The packet names the standard sobriety handoff as finite NameCert data; it does not assert spatiality for every locale, quotient point reconstruction, host equality of points, excluded middle over opens, or choice over completely prime filters.

\begin{definition}[Sober space carrier]
\label{def:sober-space-carrier}
A $\SoberSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(T,O,L,Z,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ row, recording finite open-index and neighbourhood observations for the space under inspection.
\item $O$ is the $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ observable-open truth row, exposing open membership only through displayed Bool truth, Topology open, and finite StreamName windows.
\item $L$ is the $\LocaleSpaceUp$ comparison row, recording finite frame-of-opens and cover-refinement reads that can be compared with $T$ and $O$.
\item $Z$ is the $\TZeroReflectionUp$ compatibility row, requiring specialization readback to separate displayed point names only through observable opens.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for topology rows, Sierpinski truth rows, locale comparison rows, T0 compatibility, replay, provenance, and the local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a sobriety request through $T,O,L,Z,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SoberSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, $\LocaleSpaceUp$, $\TZeroReflectionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SoberSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,O,L,Z,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host powersets of opens, quotient point spaces, selected generic points, global locale spatiality, excluded middle over opens, choice over completely prime filters, or ambient point equality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sober space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sober-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SoberSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
S=(T,O,L,Z,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SoberSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of topology-row admission, Sierpinski observable-open truth admission, locale/frame-of-opens comparison admission, T0 specialization compatibility, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every point-reconstruction consumer must inspect those rows before treating a displayed completely prime open profile as a point-facing sober-space read.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sober-space-carrier}. The topology row $T$ supplies the finite open-index observations, while the Sierpinski row $O$ supplies observable-open truth without turning membership into a host proposition.

The locale row $L$ compares the same open observations with finite frame and cover-refinement data. The T0 row $Z$ then constrains specialization readback so that displayed point names are separated only by observable opens already visible through $T,O,L$.

The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no carrier coordinate stores host powersets, quotient point spaces, selected generic points, global locale spatiality, excluded middle, choice over completely prime filters, or ambient point equality, the listed rows exhaust the local obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sober space Sierpinski locale handoff]
\label{thm:sober-space-sierpinski-locale-handoff}
Any $\TopologyUp$, $\LocaleSpaceUp$, $\TZeroReflectionUp$, or observable-open consumer that reads a $\SoberSpaceUp$ packet must route the read through
$$
T,O \longmapsto L \longmapsto Z \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
The handoff compares open membership using the $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ truth row and compares the corresponding open frame using the $\LocaleSpaceUp$ row before specialization compatibility is consumed. It exports no host proposition for open membership, no powerset of opens, no quotient point space, no selected generic point, and no excluded middle over opens.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $S=(T,O,L,Z,H,C,P,N)$. A topology-facing read first exposes $T$, and an observable-open read exposes $O$ as the displayed Sierpinski truth route.

The locale comparison row $L$ is read only after those open observations are visible. It aligns the topology open rows with finite frame and cover-refinement data rather than replacing them by a host powerset.

Specialization compatibility is then the row $Z$. It may separate displayed point names through observable opens, but it cannot select a hidden generic point or quotient point representative.

The support rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route by transport, replay, provenance, and naming. Therefore every consumer receives the Sierpinski and locale handoff as finite packet data, not as an external proposition or choice principle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sober space point reconstruction nonescape]
\label{thm:sober-space-point-reconstruction-nonescape}
Every point-reconstruction read exported by an accepted $\SoberSpaceUp$ packet factors through the displayed topology row, Sierpinski observable-open row, locale comparison row, and T0 specialization row before it reaches transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Thus the packet can witness the sober-space boundary for displayed open profiles, but it cannot export host point equality, quotient spatial reflection, selected completely prime filters, choice-indexed generic points, excluded middle over opens, or global spatiality for arbitrary locales.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:sober-space-namecert-obligations}. The local NameCert surface stores every accepted sobriety obligation in $T,O,L,Z,H,C,P,N$ and stores none outside those rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:sober-space-sierpinski-locale-handoff}. It identifies the consumer route from topology and observable-open data through locale comparison and T0 specialization compatibility.

Since the carrier inventory has no coordinate for host point equality, quotient spatial reflection, selected completely prime filters, choice-indexed generic points, excluded middle over opens, or global locale spatiality, no point-reconstruction consumer can obtain those objects from the packet.
\end{proof}
