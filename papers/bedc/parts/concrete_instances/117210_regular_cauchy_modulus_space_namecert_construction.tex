\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RegularCauchyModulusSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-modulus-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, \emph{Constructive Analysis}, the
$\RegularCauchyModulusSpaceUp$ packet records the regular-Cauchy schedule
surface used when a Cauchy modulus family is read back as rational windows and
then sealed as a real-facing name. It refines the schedule route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-modulus-space-namecert} through the
finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert},
the regular rational readback of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger
of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal
real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The chapter names
the finite handoff from abstract schedule rows to regular rational readback; it
does not select a limit, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient
$\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Regular-Cauchy modulus space carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-modulus-space-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyModulusSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(M,S,W,Q,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyModulusSpaceUp$ schedule-family row that names the
precision requests available to the packet.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window ledger inspected by those
schedules.
\item $W$ is the regular-window alignment row, pairing each requested schedule
entry with the stream window used for readback.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the aligned windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger that records the dyadic
bound compared against the regular readback.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row consumed only after $S,W,Q,D$ have
been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,S,W,Q,D,E,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a schedule request through the
stream window, regular readback, dyadic tolerance, and terminal seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyModulusSpaceUp$,
$\CauchyModulusSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$,
$\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyModulusSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. It
has no coordinate for an unscheduled Cauchy oracle, quotient stream equality, a
selected limit, countable choice, or an ambient completeness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular-Cauchy modulus space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-modulus-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyModulusSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
R=(M,S,W,Q,D,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyModulusSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly
of CauchyModulusSpace schedule admission, finite StreamName window admission,
regular-window alignment, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance
compatibility, terminal Real seal compatibility, componentwise hsame transport,
displayed Cont replay, Pkg provenance, and local naming. Thus the regular
Cauchy schedule may be consumed by Real and Completion routes only as finite
readback data over $\CauchyModulusSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$,
$\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of
\autoref{def:regular-cauchy-modulus-space-carrier}. The analytic rows are
$M,S,W,Q,D,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The schedule row $M$ names only the displayed requests already available from
$\CauchyModulusSpaceUp$. The stream row $S$ and alignment row $W$ attach each
request to a finite window before any rational readback is inspected.

The readback and tolerance rows are then forced through $Q$ and $D$. The real
seal row $E$ is available only after those regular and dyadic ledgers have been
displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no
excluded host object is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local
NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular-Cauchy RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-modulus-space-regseqrat-handoff}
Every Real or Completion consumer of an accepted
$\RegularCauchyModulusSpaceUp$ carrier receives the regular Cauchy schedule
only through the finite handoff
$$
M,S,W,Q,D,E,H,C,P,N .
$$
In particular, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row is consumed before the
$\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance comparison and before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal,
so the handoff exports no quotient stream equality, selected limit, countable
choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a Real or Completion read of the accepted packet. The carrier
definition exposes the CauchyModulusSpace schedule row $M$ before the finite
stream window row $S$ and the regular-window alignment row $W$.

The regular rational readback $Q$ is the only row from which the consumer may
read the scheduled windows. The dyadic ledger $D$ compares those readings with
displayed tolerances, and the terminal seal $E$ is consumed only after that
comparison has been named.

Componentwise transport $H$ and displayed replay $C$ preserve the same order of
rows, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming. Hence the consumer
receives a finite regular readback handoff, not any quotient, choice, selected
limit, or ambient completeness object.
\end{proof}
