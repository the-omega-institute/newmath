\label{thm:omega-rigidity-ledger-namecert-obligations}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:omega-rigidity-ledger-carrier}. The tower body is visible only as $O$, and the bridge and standard rows are visible only as $B$ and $S$.

Read the dependency and compatibility rows next. The row $N$ lists the lower-layer NameCert packets already required by the ledger, while $K$ checks that bridge reports and standard readouts agree with those internal rows.

Since no carrier coordinate stores an external model as proof term, host RH theorem, selected zero witness, hidden bridge replacement, or detached standard readout, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.

\begin{theorem}[Omega rigidity ledger no-replacement boundary]
\label{thm:omega-rigidity-ledger-no-replacement-boundary}

Use the compatibility row $K$. It compares bridge reports and standard readouts against the internal tower body $O$ and the already named lower-layer certificates.

\end{proof}
