\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompletionExactnessUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-completion-exactness-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop-style completion exactness, the $\CompletionExactnessUp$ packet records the finite boundary where a completion-facing route is accepted only when its displayed coverage, soundness, completeness, and classifier equivalence rows agree. It sits over the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite StreamName windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the two-read comparison boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-equalizer-namecert}. The packet names an exactness predicate for the L10 completion route; it does not select limits, quotient Cauchy names, identify host reals, invoke countable choice, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Completion exactness carrier]
\label{def:completion-exactness-carrier}
A $\CompletionExactnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(D,W,R,S,V,K,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance coverage row, recording the dyadic budgets through which completion reads are inspected.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation-window row scheduled by $D$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row produced from the displayed windows.
\item $S$ is the soundness row, asserting that each accepted completion read is backed by the displayed dyadic, stream, regular-rational, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal data.
\item $V$ is the completeness row, asserting that every displayed dyadic-window and regular-rational route admitted by the classifier reaches an accepted completion read.
\item $K$ is the classification-iff row, recording that the public classifier accepts exactly the reads satisfying the coverage, soundness, and completeness rows.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay for $D,W,R,S,V,K$.
\item $P$ is the provenance row over $\CompletionExactnessUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyCompletionEqualizerUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompletionExactnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier has no coordinate for a chosen limit, quotient representative, host equality of real names, countable-choice schedule, or unrestricted completion theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Completion exactness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:completion-exactness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompletionExactnessUp$ carrier
$$
E=(D,W,R,S,V,K,T,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompletionExactnessUp}$ surface consists exactly of dyadic coverage through $D$, StreamName observation through $W$, regular-rational readback through $R$, soundness through $S$, completeness through $V$, classifier equivalence through $K$, transport and replay through $T$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:completion-exactness-carrier}. The rows $D,W,R$ expose the inspected dyadic budgets, finite windows, and regular-rational readback before a completion-facing read is accepted.

Read the exactness rows next. Soundness $S$ prevents an accepted read from escaping the displayed dyadic, stream, regular-rational, and terminal Real data, while completeness $V$ forces every classifier-admitted displayed route to reach a read. The row $K$ binds these two directions as the public classification equivalence.

Finally use the structural rows. Transport and replay $T$ preserve the same finite route, provenance $P$ records the sibling sources, and $N$ names only that surface. Since the packet contains no row for selected limits, quotient representatives, host equality, countable choice, or unrestricted completion, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Completion exactness real route]
\label{thm:completion-exactness-real-route}
Every Real-facing completion read accepted by a $\CompletionExactnessUp$ carrier factors through the displayed route
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp,\ \CauchyCompletionEqualizerUp .
$$
The classifier equivalence row $K$ accepts such a read exactly when the coverage row $D$, soundness row $S$, and completeness row $V$ hold over the same finite windows and readback data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the sibling route. The dyadic tolerance row $D$ schedules StreamName windows $W$, and those windows produce the regular-rational readback $R$ before any terminal $\RealUp$ seal is available.

Use the equalizer boundary. The $\CauchyCompletionEqualizerUp$ surface compares completion reads only after the common dyadic, stream, regular-rational, and Real-facing rows have been displayed, so the completion exactness packet has no separate quotient-equality source.

Apply the classifier equivalence row. The row $K$ reads coverage, soundness, and completeness over the same finite route, and $T,P,N$ merely transport, replay, source, and name that route. Hence an accepted Real-facing completion read is exact precisely along the displayed L10 completion path, without selected limits or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
