$$

\begin{theorem}[Compact cover radius monotonicity obligations]
\label{thm:compact-cover-radius-monotonicity-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactCoverRadiusMonotonicityUp$ carrier
$$
K=(C,M,Q,U,W,G,H,T,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactCoverRadiusMonotonicityUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact finite-cover admission, metric-distance exposure, rational radius comparison, uniform-modulus handoff discipline, StreamName window replay, RegSeqRat readback, componentwise transport, displayed continuation, provenance, and local naming. A radius shrinkage or refinement read is monotone only through the displayed finite cover rows $C$, metric rows $M$, rational radius rows $Q$, and readback rows $W,G$ before $U,H,T,P,N$ may export it.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-cover-radius-monotonicity-carrier}. The compact finite-cover row $C$, metric row $M$, rational-radius row $Q$, stream window $W$, and regular readback row $G$ are the only source rows for a refinement comparison.

The uniform-modulus consumer row $U$ is downstream of those rows. It may read the smaller radius only after the finite cover and rational comparison have been displayed.

Componentwise transport $H$, continuation replay $T$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ preserve the same finite route under classifier-stable comparison.

Because arbitrary open-cover compactness, selected Lebesgue numbers, quotient cover equality, countable choice, and ambient compactness have no carrier coordinates, the stated rows exhaust the monotonicity obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact cover radius monotonicity consumer handoff]
\label{thm:compact-cover-radius-monotonicity-consumer-handoff}

It cannot be read as open-cover compactness, selected Lebesgue-number choice, quotient equality of covers, countable choice, or ambient compactness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:compact-cover-radius-monotonicity-obligations}. It places every accepted monotonicity read on the finite route $C,M,Q,W,G,U,H,T,P,N$.

The source side is finite. The compact row $C$ supplies the displayed cover and refinement witness, the metric row $M$ supplies the distance comparison, and the rational rows $Q,W,G$ supply the radius readback.

The consumer side is forced through $U$ and the support rows $H,T,P,N$. These rows can transport, replay, package, and name the displayed refinement, but they cannot introduce an open-cover oracle or a chosen Lebesgue number.
