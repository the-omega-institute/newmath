\begin{definition}[Compact-uniform local-modulus nerve carrier]
\label{def:compact-uniform-local-modulus-nerve-carrier}
A $\CompactUniformLocalModulusNerveUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(B,L,O,Q,T,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the finite cover-nerve row, recording the $\ProbeBundle$ cover names, center rows, membership rows, and finite incidence data.
\item $L$ is the local-modulus row, recording pointwise modulus data attached to each bundled cover center.
\item $O$ is the overlap compatibility row, recording the common-radius and common-tolerance checks on nonempty cover intersections.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ lower-bound row, recording the finite rational fold that selects a positive bound below the displayed local radii.
\item $T$ is the metric-triangle row, recording source and target triangle estimates used to move from local center data to neighboring points.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row exposed after $B,L,O,Q,T$ have been read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for cover, local-modulus, overlap, lower-bound, triangle, uniform-modulus, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through cover membership, local modulus, overlap compatibility, rational lower-bound fold, metric-triangle estimate, and uniform-modulus handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\ContinuousFunctionUp$, $\RatUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\ProbeBundle$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformLocalModulusNerveUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an external cover choice, a host minimum, a global modulus selected outside the finite nerve, or a completed Heine--Cantor theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompactUniformLocalModulusNerve NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-local-modulus-nerve-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-uniform-local-modulus-nerve-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformLocalModulusNerveUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for
$$
K=(B,L,O,Q,T,U,H,C,P,N),
$$
finite cover-nerve display through $B$, local-modulus display through $L$, overlap compatibility through $O$, $\RatUp$ lower-bound display through $Q$, metric-triangle display through $T$, $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff through $U$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows. The cover nerve, local modulus, overlap compatibility, rational lower bound, triangle estimate, and uniform-modulus handoff are displayed before the structural rows are read.

The overlap row $O$ binds local modulus data across intersecting cover entries. The lower-bound row $Q$ is therefore a finite rational fold over displayed rows, and the metric-triangle row $T$ is the only route from local center estimates to arbitrary nearby source points.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name this same packet. They do not add an external cover selector or host minimum, so the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CompactUniformLocalModulusNerve uniform-modulus handoff]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-local-modulus-nerve-uniform-modulus-handoff}
Every accepted $\CompactUniformLocalModulusNerveUp$ packet exposes its $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff only through the finite route
$$
B,L,O,Q,T,U,H,C,P,N.
$$
A compact-uniform-continuity consumer may read the handoff only after cover membership, local pointwise moduli, overlap compatibility, rational lower-bound data, and metric-triangle estimates have been displayed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:compact-uniform-local-modulus-nerve-obligations}. They place the handoff row $U$ downstream of $B,L,O,Q,T$.

The finite cover row $B$ supplies the ProbeBundle nerve, while $L$ and $O$ attach local moduli and overlap compatibility to the same finite incidence data. The rational lower-bound row $Q$ reads only these displayed radii, and the metric-triangle row $T$ is the displayed passage from local estimates to the uniform read.

Transport and replay preserve the same route through $H$ and $C$. Hence any consumer that reads $U$ without the prior rows leaves the accepted carrier.
\end{proof}
