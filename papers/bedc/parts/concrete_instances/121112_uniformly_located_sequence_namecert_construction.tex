The $\UniformlyLocatedSequenceUp$ packet sits after the located sequence modulus route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-sequence-modulus-namecert}, the stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Uniformly located sequence carrier]
\label{def:uniformly-located-sequence-carrier}
A $\UniformlyLocatedSequenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(L,M,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the located-sequence window row, recording the finite index windows and located value reads exposed to the packet.
\item $M$ is the shared monotone modulus schedule, sending each displayed tolerance request to a finite window bound reused by all completion-facing consumers.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance scale that records the positive dyadic requests and comparison rows used by $M$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row naming the inspected stream prefixes.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the displayed stream windows and dyadic tolerance scale.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $L,M,D,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for located windows, the shared modulus schedule, dyadic tolerances, stream windows, regular rational readbacks, Real seals, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a uniform locatedness request through $L,M,D,S,R,E,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformlyLocatedSequenceUp$, $\LocatedSequenceModulusUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformlyLocatedSequenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice over windows, trichotomy, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniformly located sequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniformly-located-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformlyLocatedSequenceUp$ carrier $U=(L,M,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UniformlyLocatedSequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of located-window admission through $L$, shared monotone modulus exposure through $M$, dyadic tolerance accounting through $D$, $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window admission through $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniformly-located-sequence-carrier}. The only rows that expose analytic data are the located windows $L$, the shared schedule $M$, the dyadic tolerance scale $D$, the stream window $S$, the regular rational readback $R$, and the terminal seal $E$.

The monotonicity requirement is read from the displayed schedule $M$. Reusing a smaller tolerance or a later completion request therefore reuses the same finite schedule row and the same dyadic tolerance scale rather than selecting a new stream representative.

The readback route then passes through $S$ and $R$ before the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is available. Rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same route, so a consumer cannot bypass the displayed finite packet.

Since no carrier coordinate records quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, or ambient Real completeness, row projection and monotone reuse of the displayed modulus schedule exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
