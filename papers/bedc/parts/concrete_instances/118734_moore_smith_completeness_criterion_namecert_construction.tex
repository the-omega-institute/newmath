Following Moore--Smith and Kelley-style net convergence, the $\MooreSmithCompletenessCriterionUp$ packet records the standard completeness criterion that every displayed Cauchy net on the metric surface has a displayed convergence endpoint. It sits beside the Moore--Smith net carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-moore-smith-net-namecert}, the Cauchy-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchynet-namecert}, the convergence interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-netconvergence-namecert}, the completeness row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, and the Cauchy-subnet criterion of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-moore-smith-cauchy-subnet-criterion-namecert}. The packet is a finite completion-facing NameCert route: it names directed tails, Cauchy evidence, endpoint convergence, regular-rational readback, and the terminal real seal without asserting countable choice, quotient equality, or an ambient host-completeness oracle.

\begin{definition}[Moore--Smith completeness criterion carrier]
\label{def:moore-smith-completeness-criterion-carrier}
A $\MooreSmithCompletenessCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(D,M,A,N,E,K,S,G,R,Y,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the directed Moore--Smith index row, exposing finite comparison windows and common upper-window witnesses before any Cauchy request is read.
\item $M$ is the $\MooreSmithNetUp$ row over $D$, recording net values, tail windows, and the directed route consumed by the criterion.
\item $A$ is the $\CauchyNetUp$ evidence row, recording the common-window Cauchy estimates on the same Moore--Smith directed surface.
\item $N$ is the $\NetConvergenceUp$ endpoint row, exposing the displayed candidate endpoint and the convergence windows that the Cauchy evidence hands to the consumer.
\item $E$ is the endpoint-admission row, recording that the endpoint inspected by $N$ is the same completion-facing object read by the net consumer.
\item $K$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ completeness row, used only as the displayed service that accepts the Cauchy-net evidence and returns the endpoint convergence row.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule for finite observation windows along the Cauchy and convergence reads.
\item $G$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the stream windows and the displayed convergence estimates.
\item $R$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row used to compare the scheduled Cauchy and endpoint estimates.
\item $Y$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the stream, regular-rational, and dyadic rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the directed surface, Moore--Smith net row, Cauchy evidence, convergence endpoint, completeness row, readback rows, routes, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same Cauchy-net-to-convergence route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MooreSmithCompletenessCriterionUp$, $\MooreSmithNetUp$, $\CauchyNetUp$, $\NetConvergenceUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MooreSmithCompletenessCriterionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for countable choice, quotient equality of nets, a selected family of limits, an ambient host-completeness oracle, or completeness of $\RealUp$ outside the displayed readback route.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Moore--Smith completeness criterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:moore-smith-completeness-criterion-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted carrier $Q=(D,M,A,N,E,K,S,G,R,Y,H,C,P,L)$ of \autoref{def:moore-smith-completeness-criterion-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\MooreSmithCompletenessCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, directed Moore--Smith index exposure, net-value and tail exposure, CauchyNet evidence, NetConvergence endpoint admission, endpoint compatibility, CompleteMetric service exposure, StreamName scheduling, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance comparison, Real sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission projects the finite packet of \autoref{def:moore-smith-completeness-criterion-carrier}. The directed and net rows $D,M$ must be visible before the Cauchy row $A$ can be inspected.

The endpoint-facing rows are $N,E,K$. The $\CompleteMetricUp$ service row $K$ accepts the displayed CauchyNet evidence and exposes the $\NetConvergenceUp$ endpoint row $N$, while $E$ records that the same endpoint is read by the convergence consumer.

The numerical readback rows are $S,G,R,Y$. They provide finite stream windows, regular-rational readback, dyadic tolerance comparisons, and the terminal real-name seal for the displayed convergence estimates.

Finally $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. Because no other coordinate occurs in the carrier, no NameCert obligation can appeal to countable choice, quotient equality, a selected family of limits, an ambient host-completeness oracle, or a separate $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Moore--Smith completeness criterion Cauchy-net handoff]
\label{thm:moore-smith-completeness-criterion-cauchy-net-handoff}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\MooreSmithCompletenessCriterionUp$ packet that starts from a displayed $\CauchyNetUp$ row must factor through
$$
\MooreSmithNetUp,\;\CauchyNetUp
\longmapsto
\CompleteMetricUp
\longmapsto
\NetConvergenceUp
\longmapsto
\StreamNameUp,\;\RegSeqRatUp,\;\DyadicRatCoreUp,\;\RealUp .
$$
The handoff supplies endpoint convergence as finite NameCert data and exports no countable-choice selector, quotient equality of nets, selected family of limits, ambient host-completeness oracle, or standalone $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the Moore--Smith and Cauchy rows. The row $M$ records the directed net windows, and $A$ records the common-window Cauchy estimates on the same directed surface.

The completeness service is the displayed row $K$. It can be read only after $M$ and $A$ are present, and it returns the endpoint-facing convergence row $N$ together with the endpoint compatibility row $E$.

The readback route is finite. Rows $S,G,R,Y$ record the observation windows, regular-rational estimates, dyadic tolerance comparisons, and terminal real-name seal attached to the endpoint convergence data.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,L$ preserve this route. The excluded choice, quotient, selected-limit-family, host-completeness, and standalone real-completeness principles have no export row in the accepted packet.
\end{proof}
