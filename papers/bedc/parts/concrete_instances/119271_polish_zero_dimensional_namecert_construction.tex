It sits after the Polish carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polishspace-namecert}, the Baire prefix surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bairespace-namecert}, the Cantor coding surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cantorset-namecert}, the topological neighbourhood route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the metric row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Polish zero-dimensional carrier]
\label{def:polish-zero-dimensional-carrier}
A $\PolishZeroDimensionalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the $\PolishSpaceUp$ source row, exposing the separable complete metric name that the packet refines.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ neighbourhood row read from the source topology.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ observation row used to compare finite neighbourhood requests.
\item $B$ is the finite clopen-basis ledger. Each admitted basic neighbourhood request names a clopen row below the requested open row, and finite intersections are recorded by displayed ledger entries.
\item $C$ is the Cantor/Baire coding boundary, tying basic clopen rows to $\BaireSpaceUp$ and $\CantorSetUp$ cylinder reads without selecting infinite branches.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row used when a consumer probes the basis by names.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the finite metric and cylinder windows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Polish source, topology row, metric row, clopen ledger, coding boundary, window row, readback row, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $E$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Polish zero-dimensional NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:polish-zero-dimensional-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PolishZeroDimensionalUp$ carrier $Z=(P,T,M,B,C,W,R,H,E,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\PolishZeroDimensionalUp}$ surface consists exactly of source admission for the Polish row, topology row, metric observations, finite clopen-basis ledger, Baire/Cantor cylinder boundary, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. The certificate refuses host powersets, arbitrary subset equality, global choice of bases, open-cover compactness, selected infinite branches, quotient stream equality, and ambient classical descriptive set theory.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:polish-zero-dimensional-carrier}. Its rows expose exactly the Polish source, topology and metric observations, clopen-basis ledger, Baire/Cantor boundary, finite windows, rational readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

The zero-dimensional obligation is the ledger row $B$. For each admitted neighbourhood request, $B$ names a displayed clopen refinement and records the finite intersection rows needed by downstream basis consumers. The route reads $P,T,M$ before $B$, so the clopen row is tied to the displayed Polish topology rather than to a host subset universe.

The coding boundary $C$ and the observation rows $W,R$ keep later Baire or Cantor consumers inside finite cylinder and rational-readback evidence. Transport $H$ and replay $E$ repeat only those rows, while $Q$ and $N$ source and name the same packet.

Since the carrier contains no row for host powersets, arbitrary subset equality, global basis choice, open-cover compactness, selected infinite branches, quotient stream equality, or ambient classical descriptive set theory, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
