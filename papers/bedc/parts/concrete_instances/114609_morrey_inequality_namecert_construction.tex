\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\MorreyInequalityUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-morrey-inequality-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Morrey's classical regularity theorem, the $\MorreyInequalityUp$ packet records the finite certificate by which Sobolev control above the dimension threshold is read as a displayed Hölder modulus rather than as an ambient embedding theorem. It depends on the Sobolev carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sobolev-space-namecert}, the embedding handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sobolev-embedding-namecert}, the local radius and oscillation control of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-inequality-namecert}, and the Hölder-target surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-morrey-space-namecert}. The packet is a finite NameCert route from weak derivative data and local averages to a concrete regularity modulus; it is not a host compactness theorem, not a choice of representative outside the displayed carrier, and not a global smoothness principle.

\begin{definition}[Morrey inequality carrier]
\label{def:morrey-inequality-carrier}
A $\MorreyInequalityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(S,E,P,Y,R,O,A,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\SobolevSpaceUp$ source row, including the named domain, integrability exponent, weak derivative budget, and admitted Sobolev representative.
\item $E$ is the $\SobolevEmbeddingUp$ threshold row recording the dimension/exponent comparison that permits a pointwise regularity read.
\item $P$ is the $\PoincareInequalityUp$ local handoff row, containing the radius ledger, average comparison, and oscillation estimate consumed before the modulus is formed.
\item $Y$ is the $\MorreySpaceUp$ target row, naming the Hölder seminorm classifier and the destination regularity surface.
\item $R$ is the finite radius ledger, with each accepted radius tied to the same source domain and exponent data in $S,E,P$.
\item $O$ is the oscillation ledger, reading local average differences and derivative budgets through $P$ before any pointwise claim is exported.
\item $A$ is the Hölder modulus row, recording the displayed exponent and constant produced from $E,R,O$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Sobolev source, embedding threshold, Poincare handoff, Morrey target, radius, oscillation, modulus, continuation, provenance, and local NameCert rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a regularity consumer through $S,E,P,Y,R,O,A,H$.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SobolevSpaceUp$, $\SobolevEmbeddingUp$, $\PoincareInequalityUp$, $\MorreySpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MorreyInequalityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a representative selected by quotient choice, an external compact embedding theorem, a smooth approximation oracle, or an unbounded-domain regularity conclusion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Morrey inequality NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:morrey-inequality-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MorreyInequalityUp$ carrier $M=(S,E,P,Y,R,O,A,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MorreyInequalityUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for those rows, threshold visibility through $E$, local Poincare replay through $P,R,O$, Hölder-target visibility through $Y,A$, componentwise transport through $H$, continuation replay through $C$, provenance through $Q$, and local naming through $N$. The certificate exports no regularity conclusion unless the Sobolev source, embedding threshold, Poincare handoff, and Morrey target rows are all displayed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:morrey-inequality-carrier}. The only source of analytic data is the Sobolev row $S$ together with the threshold row $E$, the Poincare handoff row $P$, and the Morrey target row $Y$.

The local estimate is not read directly from $S$. The radius ledger $R$ and oscillation ledger $O$ force the consumer through the same local averages and derivative budgets that the Poincare certificate supplies in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-inequality-namecert}.

The Hölder conclusion is then packaged only through $A,H,C,Q,N$. Since representative choice, compact embedding, smooth approximation, and unbounded-domain regularity have no carrier row, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Morrey inequality Sobolev modulus]
\label{thm:morrey-inequality-sobolev-modulus}
Let $M=(S,E,P,Y,R,O,A,H,C,Q,N)$ be an accepted $\MorreyInequalityUp$ packet. Any exported Hölder modulus factors through
$$
\SobolevSpaceUp \longmapsto \SobolevEmbeddingUp \longmapsto \PoincareInequalityUp \longmapsto \MorreySpaceUp .
$$
Concretely, the Sobolev source row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sobolev-space-namecert} supplies the derivative budget, the embedding threshold of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sobolev-embedding-namecert} supplies the exponent comparison, the Poincare handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-inequality-namecert} supplies the finite oscillation control, and the Morrey target of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-morrey-space-namecert} receives the displayed Hölder exponent and constant.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a regularity consumer asking for a modulus. Carrier admission exposes $S$ and $E$ before any target row is available, so the derivative budget and dimension/exponent comparison are fixed at the source.

Replay then passes through $P,R,O$. These rows turn local average comparisons and radius budgets into oscillation estimates; the packet has no direct edge from Sobolev membership to a pointwise conclusion.

Finally $Y$ and $A$ receive the Hölder exponent and constant, while $H,C,Q,N$ preserve the same dependency order under transport and packaging. The factorization therefore records a concrete BEDC route rather than an ambient analytic theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Morrey inequality Poincare handoff]
\label{thm:morrey-inequality-poincare-handoff}
For an accepted $\MorreyInequalityUp$ packet $M=(S,E,P,Y,R,O,A,H,C,Q,N)$, the Poincare row $P$ is the unique certificate boundary at which local derivative control is converted into finite oscillation data. If $P$, $R$, or $O$ is absent, the packet can still display Sobolev and embedding rows, but it cannot export a Morrey-space modulus row $A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the source and threshold rows first. The Sobolev and embedding data name the admissible derivative budget and exponent comparison, but neither row stores local radius averages or oscillation witnesses.

The conversion happens at $P,R,O$. The Poincare certificate supplies the local estimate, $R$ names the radii at which it is inspected, and $O$ records the oscillation comparisons consumed by the modulus row.

If any of these rows is missing, continuation replay stops before $A$. The structural rows can still name the partial Sobolev/embedding surface, but the Morrey modulus is not exported because the local oscillation boundary has not been displayed.
\end{proof}
