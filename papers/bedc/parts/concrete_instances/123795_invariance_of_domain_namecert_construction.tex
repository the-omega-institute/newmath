\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\InvarianceOfDomainUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-invariance-of-domain-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Brouwer and Hurewicz--Wallman, the $\InvarianceOfDomainUp$ packet records the finite local-injectivity and open-image ledger that a BEDC topology consumer may inspect before it treats a displayed chart map as domain-preserving data. It sits between the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the compact-metric support windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the graph and modulus rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the manifold chart surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-manifold-namecert}, the boundary ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-brouwer-degree-namecert}, and the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is limited to finite triangulated neighbourhood, local injectivity, boundary-separation, open-image ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and NameCert rows.

\begin{definition}[Invariance of domain carrier]
\label{def:invariance-of-domain-carrier}
An $\InvarianceOfDomainUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(T,K,F,M,D,L,O,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ neighbourhood row carrying source and target open-window names.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ support row for the finite triangulated neighbourhood and its boundary shell.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row exposing the graph, modulus, and restriction data of the displayed chart map.
\item $M$ is the $\ManifoldUp$ chart row that ties the source and target windows to finite coordinate neighbourhoods.
\item $D$ is the $\BrouwerDegreeUp$ boundary-separation ledger, used only as a finite obstruction row attached to the displayed boundary shell.
\item $L$ is the local-injectivity row, recording that two displayed vertices or finite cells inside the chosen neighbourhood are separated by the carrier's equality classifier whenever their images are separated.
\item $O$ is the open-image ledger row, recording the target neighbourhood certificates forced by $T,K,F,M,D,L$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology, compact-metric, map, chart, degree, local-injectivity, and open-image rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the local data to the open-image ledger and then to local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\InvarianceOfDomainUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\ManifoldUp$, $\BrouwerDegreeUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\InvarianceOfDomainUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for full classical invariance of domain, arbitrary Euclidean topology, quotient equality of points, selected points, excluded middle, choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Invariance of domain local-injectivity window]
\label{thm:invariance-of-domain-local-injectivity-window}
For every accepted $\InvarianceOfDomainUp$ carrier
$$
I=(T,K,F,M,D,L,O,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local-injectivity read is confined to the finite window $T,K,F,M,D,L$. Any consumer that compares two displayed source cells must first read the topology neighbourhood row, compact-metric triangulation support, continuous-map restriction, manifold chart row, Brouwer-degree boundary ledger, and local-injectivity row. No comparison may appeal to host point selection, quotient equality, excluded middle, choice, or an ambient Euclidean theorem outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:invariance-of-domain-carrier}. The rows $T,K,F,M$ fix the source and target neighbourhood names, the triangulated support, the displayed map restriction, and the chart context.

Read the boundary row $D$ and the local-injectivity row $L$. The boundary ledger supplies only the finite separation data attached to the chosen shell, and $L$ records the permitted local comparison inside that shell.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores selected points, quotient point equality, excluded middle, choice, arbitrary Euclidean topology, or a host invariance-of-domain theorem, the local-injectivity read remains inside the displayed window.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Invariance of domain open-image ledger]
\label{thm:invariance-of-domain-open-image-ledger}
Every open-image read exported by an accepted $\InvarianceOfDomainUp$ carrier factors through
$$
T,K,F,M,D,L\longmapsto O\longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the open-image ledger is available only after the finite topology window, compact-metric triangulation, continuous-map row, manifold chart row, boundary-separation ledger, and local-injectivity window have been displayed.

The ledger exports only target neighbourhood rows certified by the carrier. It does not claim full classical invariance of domain, arbitrary Euclidean topology, quotient equality of points, a selected image point, excluded middle, choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the local-injectivity window of \autoref{thm:invariance-of-domain-local-injectivity-window}. It places the topology, compact-metric, continuous-map, manifold, boundary-separation, and local-injectivity rows before any open-image claim can be read.

The row $O$ records the target neighbourhood ledger forced by that finite route. It is therefore an output row of the packet, not a host-level openness theorem and not a selector for points in the image.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$. These rows preserve the same displayed route under componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming, so the exported surface is exactly the finite open-image ledger.
\end{proof}
