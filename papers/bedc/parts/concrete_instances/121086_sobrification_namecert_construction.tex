\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SobrificationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sobrification-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Johnstone and Gierz--Hofmann--Keimel--Lawson--Mislove--Scott, the $\SobrificationUp$ packet records the sober-point completion of a topological carrier as finite NameCert data. It consumes the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the Sierpinski classifier surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert}, the order-facing route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-scott-topology-namecert}, the localic point boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locale-space-namecert}, and the compact-open comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stone-space-namecert}. The chapter names sober completion without importing host point-set topology, choice of generic points, or a spectral-space representation theorem.

\begin{definition}[Sobrification carrier]
\label{def:sobrification-carrier}
A $\SobrificationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(T,I,\Sigma,\preceq,G,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the source $\TopologyUp$ carrier row whose closed and open predicates are read only through the accepted topology packet.
\item $I$ is the irreducible-closed predicate row, recording nonempty closedness and the finite meet-test boundary for irreducibility.
\item $\Sigma$ is the $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ classifier row that reads opens as characteristic maps into the Sierpinski carrier.
\item $\preceq$ is the specialization preorder row induced by the topology surface.
\item $G$ is the generic-point witness row for irreducible closed data, stored as local witness data rather than as a global point-selection principle.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology, irreducibility, classifier, specialization, witness, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ routes from topology opens through Sierpinski classifiers and specialization reads to the generic-point row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SobrificationUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, $\ScottTopologyUp$, $\LocaleSpaceUp$, $\StoneSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SobrificationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The packet has no coordinate for choice of all generic points, ambient spectral-space duality, quotient point creation, classical prime-filter selection, or host topological reflection.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sobrification NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sobrification-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:sobrification-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\SobrificationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for
$$
Q=(T,I,\Sigma,\preceq,G,H,C,P,N),
$$
irreducible-closed exposure through $I$, Sierpinski classifier exposure through $\Sigma$, specialization-order exposure through $\preceq$, generic-point witness locality through $G$, componentwise $\hsame$ stability through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ routing through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:sobrification-carrier}. The topology row $T$ is the only source of open and closed data, so every irreducibility read must pass through the displayed topology carrier.

The classifier row $\Sigma$ supplies the Sierpinski-facing open classifier, and the specialization row $\preceq$ records the induced preorder. The witness row $G$ is local to the displayed irreducible closed row $I$ and does not select generic points globally.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, route, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier contains no further coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sobrification specialization sober-point surface]
\label{thm:sobrification-specialization-sober-point-surface}
Every accepted $\SobrificationUp$ read that exposes a sober point factors through the specialization preorder row $\preceq$, the Sierpinski classifier row $\Sigma$, and the generic-point witness row $G$ of
$$
Q=(T,I,\Sigma,\preceq,G,H,C,P,N).
$$
It cannot replace those rows by a host sober-reflection theorem, a chosen generic-point function on all irreducible closed sets, or a spectral-space representation claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:sobrification-carrier}. A sober-point read has access to topology data only through $T$, to irreducible closed data only through $I$, and to open classifiers only through $\Sigma$.

The specialization preorder $\preceq$ is then the displayed order surface connecting topology opens to point comparison. The witness row $G$ is read through that same finite route, so it supplies only the local generic point named by the packet.

Apply \autoref{thm:sobrification-namecert-obligations}. Transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming remain inside $H,C,P,N$, and the excluded global principles have no carrier coordinate.
\end{proof}
