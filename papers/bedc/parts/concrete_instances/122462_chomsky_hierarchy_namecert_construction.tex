\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ChomskyHierarchyUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-chomsky-hierarchy-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Chomsky and Hopcroft--Ullman, the $\ChomskyHierarchyUp$ packet records the standard formal-language strata as one finite BEDC carrier. It links the regular-language surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularlanguage-namecert}, the context-free language surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-context-free-language-namecert}, the context-sensitive language surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-context-sensitive-language-namecert}, the linear-bounded automaton surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-linear-bounded-automaton-namecert}, and the Turing-machine surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-turingmachine-namecert}. The packet names the hierarchy classifier before any consumer may treat the strata as host powersets of languages or as an unrestricted semantic inclusion theorem.

\begin{definition}[Chomsky hierarchy carrier]
\label{def:chomsky-hierarchy-carrier}
A $\ChomskyHierarchyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(R,F,S,L,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the regular-language stratum, anchored by the finite automaton carrier and accepted-word classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularlanguage-namecert}.
\item $F$ is the context-free language stratum, anchored by the grammar, derivation, yield, and pushdown-handoff rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-context-free-language-namecert}.
\item $S$ is the context-sensitive language stratum, anchored by the noncontracting grammar and linear-bounded acceptance rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-context-sensitive-language-namecert}.
\item $L$ is the linear-bounded automaton stratum, anchored by the bounded-tape computation rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-linear-bounded-automaton-namecert}.
\item $T$ is the unrestricted Turing-machine stratum, anchored by the finite trace, source-classifier, ledger-exactness, and public certificate rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-turingmachine-namecert}.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the five strata and the local hierarchy name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a hierarchy query through $R,F,S,L,T,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularLanguageUp$, $\ContextFreeLanguageUp$, $\ContextSensitiveLanguageUp$, $\LinearBoundedAutomatonUp$, $\TuringMachineUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChomskyHierarchyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier reads the hierarchy only through the ordered stratum packet $R,F,S,L,T$. It has no coordinate for a host language powerset, quotient grammar equality, selected universal machine, complement closure, strictness theorem, or ambient decidability principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Chomsky hierarchy NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:chomsky-hierarchy-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ChomskyHierarchyUp$ carrier
$$
K=(R,F,S,L,T,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ChomskyHierarchyUp}$ surface consists exactly of regular-stratum admission through $R$, context-free admission through $F$, context-sensitive admission through $S$, linear-bounded admission through $L$, Turing-machine admission through $T$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

The sibling anchors are the regular-language obligation surface of \autoref{thm:regularlanguage-namecert-obligation-surface}, the context-free language obligation surface of \autoref{thm:context-free-language-namecert-obligations}, the context-sensitive language obligation surface of \autoref{thm:context-sensitive-language-namecert-obligations}, the linear-bounded automaton obligation surface of \autoref{thm:linear-bounded-automaton-namecert-obligations}, and the Turing-machine obligation surface of \autoref{thm:turingmachine-namecert-surface-obligation}. The hierarchy exports this finite ordered carrier, not a host-language inclusion theorem or a strictness classification.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:chomsky-hierarchy-carrier}. The rows $R,F,S,L,T$ are the only stratum rows, and each row points to an existing finite NameCert surface.

Read the sibling obligations. The regular row is grounded by deterministic finite-run data, the context-free row by grammar and derivation data, the context-sensitive row by noncontracting grammar and bounded acceptance data, the linear-bounded row by bounded-tape computation data, and the Turing-machine row by finite trace and public certificate data.

The transport and continuation rows keep hierarchy queries inside the same ordered packet. Provenance and local naming then record exactly this dependency surface.

Since no row stores host powersets, quotient grammar equality, a selected universal machine, complement closure, strictness, or global decidability, the local NameCert obligations close only the finite hierarchy carrier.
\end{proof}

\falsifiablePrediction{Removing any one of the five stratum rows $R,F,S,L,T$ makes the $\ChomskyHierarchyUp$ carrier unable to answer the corresponding hierarchy query without importing an external language semantics.}

\independenceWitness{regularlanguage, context-free-language, context-sensitive-language, linear-bounded-automaton, turingmachine: none is bijective to $\ChomskyHierarchyUp$ because the hierarchy packet jointly carries the five ordered stratum anchors, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming as one finite classifier surface.}
