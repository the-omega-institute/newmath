\begin{definition}[Constructive IVT modulus carrier]
\label{def:constructive-ivt-modulus-carrier}
A $\ConstructiveIVTModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(I,A,B,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the located interval source row. It records endpoint names, a displayed interval orientation, locator reads, and the finite bracket over which the modulus request is made.
\item $A$ is the opposite-sign witness row. It records the two endpoint sign certificates and the retained sign-change condition used by the bisection schedule.
\item $B$ is the finite bisection schedule row. It records the requested depth, midpoint inspections, retained child intervals, and branch evidence.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger. It records the tolerance request, diameter budget, midpoint denominator data, and finite contraction rows used by $B$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row opened by the retained endpoint and midpoint reads.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the same windows and tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $I,A,B,D,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval source, sign witnesses, bisection schedule, tolerance ledger, StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a modulus request through $I,A,B,D,S,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ConstructiveIVTModulusUp$, $\LocatedRealIntervalUp$, $\ConstructiveIntermediateValueUp$, $\DyadicBisectionTreeUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveIVTModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate asserts exactly that these rows form one finite sign-change modulus history. It has no coordinate for host root selection, quotient real equality, trichotomy, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive IVT modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:constructive-ivt-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ConstructiveIVTModulusUp$ carrier
$$
Q=(I,A,B,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveIVTModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the located interval source $I$, opposite-sign witness row $A$, finite bisection schedule $B$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$, terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$, componentwise transport $H$, displayed replay $C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:constructive-ivt-modulus-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes the located interval source, sign witnesses, bisection schedule, tolerance ledger, finite windows, regular readback, terminal seal, and structural rows.

The analytic rows are $I,A,B,D,S,R,E$. The interval row fixes the displayed bracket, the sign row supplies the endpoint and retained sign-change certificates, and the bisection and tolerance rows bind each midpoint inspection to a finite diameter budget. The stream, regular-readback, and Real-seal rows are downstream of those displayed inspections.

The structural rows are $H,C,P,N$. They transport the same finite data, replay the same route, record provenance, and bind the local name. Since the carrier contains no further coordinate, every local NameCert obligation is one of the listed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive IVT modulus bisection-window handoff]
\label{thm:constructive-ivt-modulus-bisection-window-handoff}
Every accepted $\ConstructiveIVTModulusUp$ request that asks for a sign-change modulus must factor through the finite route
$$
I,A \longmapsto B,D \longmapsto S,R \longmapsto E,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the retained bisection window is exposed only after the located interval source, opposite-sign witnesses, bisection schedule, and dyadic tolerance ledger have been read; the route cannot provide a host-selected root, quotient real equality, trichotomy, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted carrier $Q=(I,A,B,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$. The source stage is $I,A$: the located interval row fixes the bracket and the opposite-sign row records the endpoint and retained sign evidence.

The bisection stage is $B,D$. Each midpoint inspection and retained child interval is paired with a dyadic tolerance entry, so the request opens only the finite schedule specified by the carrier.

The window and readback stage is $S,R$. The $\StreamNameUp$ windows are opened from the retained endpoint and midpoint reads, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row repacks exactly those windows against the same tolerance ledger.

Finally $E,H,C,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Because no row carries a root selector, quotient equality, trichotomy split, choice schedule, open-cover compactness proof, or completeness principle, the displayed handoff is exhaustive.
\end{proof}
