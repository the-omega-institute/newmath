\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedLowerDedekindRealUp$}

Following Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis, the $\LocatedLowerDedekindRealUp$ packet records the constructive lower-cut real boundary where a lower Dedekind observation is read with explicit located comparison data before a regular-Cauchy or real-seal consumer treats it as real evidence. It is the lower-side sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-upper-dedekind-real-namecert}; it also depends on the dyadic arithmetic ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational handoff surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet displays a finite L10 lower-cut interface; it does not assume quotient cuts, selected representatives, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Located lower Dedekind real carrier]
\label{def:located-lower-dedekind-real-carrier}
A $\LocatedLowerDedekindRealUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(D,B,U,O,Y,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DedekindCutUp$ row, read only as rounded lower-and-upper rational observation data;
\item $B$ is the $\LowerRealUp$ row, recording the lower-side rational or dyadic observations that the consumer may inspect;
\item $U$ is the $\UpperRealUp$ comparison row, used only to display lower-upper separation when a locatedness request asks for it;
\item $O$ is the locatedness row: for each displayed rational interval $a<b$, it records either a lower-side observation below $b$ or an upper-side observation above $a$ inside the same finite history;
\item $Y$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row carrying the dyadic comparison budgets used when lower observations are sampled at finite precision;
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row recording which lower-cut observations are inspected before any sequence readback occurs;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row attached to the same windows and dyadic tolerances;
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,B,U,O,Y,W,R$ have been displayed;
\item $H$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the cut row, lower row, upper comparison row, locatedness row, dyadic budget, stream window, regular handoff, Real seal, and structural rows;
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay route for rereading the same lower-cut observations;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedLowerDedekindRealUp$, $\LocatedUpperDedekindRealUp$, $\DedekindCutUp$, $\LowerRealUp$, $\UpperRealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedLowerDedekindRealUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports no quotient class of cuts, no selected cut representative, no order trichotomy branch, no countable-choice selector, no Dedekind completeness theorem, and no completeness claim for $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located lower Dedekind real NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-lower-dedekind-real-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\LocatedLowerDedekindRealUp$ carrier $L=(D,B,U,O,Y,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedLowerDedekindRealUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed $\DedekindCutUp$ row, $\LowerRealUp$ row, $\UpperRealUp$ comparison row, locatedness row, dyadic budget, stream windows, regular handoff, $\RealUp$ seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item lower cut observation, stating that every lower-side read factors through $B$ together with the lower side of $D$;
\item located comparison, stating that every requested rational interval $a<b$ is answered by the displayed row $O$ together with the lower row $B$ and comparison row $U$;
\item finite sampling discipline, stating that every lower observation used for a sequence handoff passes through the dyadic budget $Y$ and the stream window row $W$;
\item Real-seal boundary, stating that a $\RealUp$-facing consumer reaches $E$ only after the cut, lower, upper-comparison, locatedness, dyadic, stream-window, and regular-handoff rows have been inspected;
\item representation restraint, stating that the lower packet may be paired with the upper packet only as a finite cut-facing comparison surface.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation introduces quotient stream equality, quotient cut equality, selected representatives, trichotomy, countable choice, Dedekind completeness, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:located-lower-dedekind-real-carrier}. The displayed packet contains the cut row, lower row, upper comparison row, locatedness row, dyadic budget, stream windows, regular handoff, Real seal, and structural rows as finite $\BHist$ data.

The lower cut observation obligation reads $D$ and $B$ together. The cut row supplies rounded lower-and-upper rational observations, while the lower row exposes only the lower-side candidates and their finite comparison budgets.

The located comparison obligation is the role of $O$. For a displayed interval $a<b$, the packet answers through the same finite lower and upper rows, so a consumer receives located evidence without a trichotomy branch or an external order oracle.

The finite sampling discipline is the route through $Y$ and $W$. A lower observation that will feed a regular sequence is first tied to dyadic precision data and then to a displayed finite window, so the handoff cannot be read from an unbounded stream or a quotient cut.

The Real-seal boundary is $E$. Since $E$ is downstream of $D,B,U,O,Y,W,R$ and is transported only by $H$ and replayed only by $C$, a $\RealUp$-facing read cannot bypass the finite lower-cut route.

Finally the provenance row $P$ names $\LocatedUpperDedekindRealUp$ only as the sibling comparison surface. The packet therefore supports later Cauchy-Dedekind comparison while adding no quotient equality, selected representative, countable-choice selector, Dedekind completeness theorem, or completeness claim for $\RealUp$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located lower Dedekind real regular-sequence handoff]
\label{thm:located-lower-dedekind-real-regseqrat-handoff}
Let $L=(D,B,U,O,Y,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\LocatedLowerDedekindRealUp$ carrier. Every downstream $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ consumer read of the handoff row $R$ factors through the finite route
$$

$$
inside the same $\BHist$ packet, and the terminal seal $E$ may be read only after that route has been displayed. Consequently the handoff exposes lower-cut sampling data, dyadic precision budgets, and finite stream windows, but it exposes no quotient cut, no selected real representative, no ambient completeness principle, and no order-trichotomy decision.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:located-lower-dedekind-real-namecert-obligations}. They force every lower-side observation to read the lower row $B$ together with the cut row $D$, and they force every located comparison to use the displayed row $O$ rather than an external order oracle.

Next use the finite sampling discipline in the same obligation list. Before $R$ can be consumed as a $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff, the dyadic budget $Y$ and the finite window row $W$ must already have been displayed. Thus the sequence-facing read is tied to finite lower-cut observations rather than to an unbounded stream.

Finally read the Real-seal boundary. The seal row $E$ is downstream of $D,B,U,O,Y,W,R$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, name, and package the same displayed route. The listed quotient, representative, completeness, and trichotomy readings are outside the carrier inventory and cannot be consumed here.
\end{proof}
