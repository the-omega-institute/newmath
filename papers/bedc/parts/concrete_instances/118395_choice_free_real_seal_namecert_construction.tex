\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ChoiceFreeRealSealUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive real names, the $\ChoiceFreeRealSealUp$ packet records the terminal finite seal for a real-name route that stays inside displayed name data. It reads stream windows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, regular rational readback from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, dyadic tolerances from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite NameCert seal for accepted real-name consumers, not a completeness theorem for $\RealUp$ and not a quotient stream presentation.

\begin{definition}[Choice-free real seal carrier]
\label{def:choice-free-real-seal-carrier}
A $\ChoiceFreeRealSealUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(W,R,D,E,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window used by the consumer read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback attached to that window.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row controlling the inspected precision.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row exposed only after $W,R,D$ have been read.
\item $L$ is the nonescape ledger refusing countable-choice, quotient-stream, trichotomy, host-real-equality, and completeness routes.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $W,R,D,E,L,P,N$.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay from stream window to rational readback, dyadic tolerance, and terminal real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and the local naming row.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChoiceFreeRealSealUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a countable-choice selector, quotient stream equality, trichotomy on real names, host real equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free real seal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:choice-free-real-seal-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:choice-free-real-seal-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\ChoiceFreeRealSealUp}$ surface consists of five finite rows: carrier admission for $S$; stream-window, regular-rational, and dyadic-tolerance visibility before the terminal real seal; componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the displayed rows; $\Cont$ replay and $\Pkg$ provenance for the same route; and nonescape from countable-choice, quotient-stream, trichotomy, host-real-equality, and completeness routes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is the displayed packet $S=(W,R,D,E,L,H,C,P,N)$. The first read exposes the stream window, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance rows before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal can be used.

The transport, replay, and provenance rows are $H,C,P$. They keep every accepted read on the same finite packet and attach the local naming row $N$ to that route.

The ledger row $L$ supplies the refusal boundary. Since the carrier lists no selector, quotient equality, trichotomy, host equality, or completeness coordinate, those principles cannot be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free real seal no-choice route]
\label{thm:choice-free-real-seal-no-countable-choice-route}
An accepted $\ChoiceFreeRealSealUp$ packet exports no countable-choice selector, no quotient stream equality, no trichotomy row for real names, no host real equality, and no $\RealUp$ completeness row. Every real-facing consumer must read the finite stream window $W$, regular rational row $R$, dyadic tolerance row $D$, terminal real seal $E$, nonescape ledger $L$, transport $H$, replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local name $N$ as its complete surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the carrier rows in \autoref{def:choice-free-real-seal-carrier}. The only real-facing rows are $W,R,D,E$ together with the ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:choice-free-real-seal-namecert-obligations}. It forces every local naming read to keep the same five-row obligation surface and the same nonescape ledger.

The excluded principles have no row in $S$. A consumer asking for countable choice, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, host real equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness therefore leaves the accepted packet rather than reading an exported coordinate.
\end{proof}
