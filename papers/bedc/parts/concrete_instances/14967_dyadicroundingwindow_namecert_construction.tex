Following Bishop and Bridges, \emph{Constructive Analysis}, the $\DyadicRoundingWindowUp$ packet records the finite precision-window surface used when a real name or regular rational name is inspected at one displayed unary precision and then rounded through adjacent dyadic endpoints. It sits between the finite windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the adjacent endpoint packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicrounding-namecert}, the readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicapproximation-namecert}, the regular rational handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic arithmetic ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The certificate names precision-window rounding as finite BEDC route data, without quotient real equality, selected limits, trichotomy, host rounding, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[DyadicRoundingWindow carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-rounding-window-carrier}
A $\DyadicRoundingWindowUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window opened for the inspected real or regular rational name.
\item $q$ is the unary precision request that fixes the finite observation scale.
\item $D$ is the adjacent endpoint $\DyadicRoundingUp$ packet at precision $q$, including its $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint rows and tolerance ledger.
\item $A$ is the $\DyadicApproximationUp$ readback row exposing the rounded finite observation produced from $S,q,D$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row that packages the same rounded window as regular rational data.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the stream window, unary precision, adjacent endpoint packet, dyadic readback, and regular rational handoff are displayed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream window, unary precision, adjacent endpoint packet, dyadic readback, regular rational handoff, real seal, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from the precision request to the stream window, from the window to the adjacent endpoint packet, from the endpoint packet to the dyadic readback, and from the readback to the regular rational and real-facing rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicRoundingWindowUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRoundingUp$, $\DyadicApproximationUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicRoundingWindowUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,q,D,A,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient real equality, selected limits, trichotomy, host rounding, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}
