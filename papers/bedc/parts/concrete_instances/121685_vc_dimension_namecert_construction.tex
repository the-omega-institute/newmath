\begin{definition}[VC dimension finite classifier carrier]
\label{def:vc-dimension-carrier}
A $\VCDimensionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
V=(X,K,S,L,F,T,G,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the finite sample-domain row, read through the displayed
membership discipline and the finite-set surface rather than through a host
powerset.
\item $K$ is the inspected cardinality row, a $\NatUp$ ledger recording the
sample size and the growth-boundary size request.
\item $S$ is the finite sample row, a $\ListUp$ spine of displayed sample
points with an explicit support boundary in $X$.
\item $L$ is the Boolean label-row ledger, listing only the inspected
$\BoolUp$ label patterns on the sample row $S$.
\item $F$ is the concept-class row, recording the finite family of displayed
classifier rows that are tested against $S$ and $L$.
\item $T$ is the shattering-pattern ledger: for each displayed label pattern
in $L$, it names a concept row in $F$ whose readback agrees with that pattern
on $S$.
\item $G$ is the growth-boundary row, recording the first displayed size at
which the packet refuses a full shattering ledger and exposes only the counted
pattern boundary.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for sample-domain,
cardinality, sample, label, concept-class, shattering, growth-boundary,
replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$X,K,S,L,F,T,G,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\VCDimensionUp$, $\BoolUp$,
$\ListUp$, $\NatUp$, $\SetLikeUp$, $\KuratowskiFiniteSetUp$,
$\HoeffdingFiniteSumUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\VCDimensionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through
$X,K,S,L,F,T,G,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host concept class,
quotient finite set, powerset of labels, probability measure, risk functional,
ambient growth theorem, or selected infinite sample family.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[VC dimension shattering obligation]
\label{thm:vc-dimension-shattering-obligation}
For every accepted $\VCDimensionUp$ carrier
$$
V=(X,K,S,L,F,T,G,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\VCDimensionUp}$ shattering surface consists exactly of
carrier admission for the sample-domain row, inspected cardinality row,
finite sample row, Boolean label-row ledger, concept-class row,
shattering-pattern ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
The shattering ledger $T$ must be read after $S,L,F$ are displayed, and each
entry of $T$ may cite only a displayed concept row whose Boolean readback on
$S$ matches the corresponding label row in $L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:vc-dimension-carrier}. The rows
$X,K,S,L,F,T$ are the full finite classifier surface before any growth
boundary is inspected.

First read the sample route. The row $X$ supplies the displayed membership
boundary, $K$ records the relevant size request, and $S$ supplies the finite
list of sample points whose labels can be tested.

Next read the classifier route. The row $L$ lists the Boolean patterns being
requested, while $F$ lists the displayed concept rows available for the test.
The ledger $T$ is accepted only after both rows are visible, so a shattering
read cannot be replaced by a host powerset, quotient finite set, or ambient
concept-class equality.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet.
Since no carrier coordinate stores a probability measure, risk functional,
infinite sample family, or host classifier semantics, the listed rows exhaust
the local shattering obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[VC dimension growth-boundary obligation]
\label{thm:vc-dimension-growth-boundary-obligation}
Let
$$
V=(X,K,S,L,F,T,G,H,C,P,N)
$$
be an accepted $\VCDimensionUp$ carrier. Any read of the growth-boundary row
$G$ is downstream of the displayed sample-domain, cardinality, sample,
Boolean label, concept-class, and shattering-pattern rows. Thus a
statistical-learning or finite-combinatorics consumer may use $G$ only as the
finite refusal boundary attached to the displayed shattering ledger, not as a
PAC guarantee, probability space, empirical-risk convergence theorem,
complete Sauer--Shelah proof, host finite-set quotient, or ambient growth
function oracle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:vc-dimension-shattering-obligation}. It orders the finite
classifier rows so that the sample, label, concept, and shattering ledgers are
read before a consumer can inspect any boundary row.

The growth-boundary row $G$ records only the displayed size at which the
packet refuses a full shattering ledger and exposes a counted pattern
boundary. It is therefore tied to the same $X,K,S,L,F,T$ route rather than to
an external Sauer--Shelah theorem or host powerset calculation.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that finite route under transport,
replay, provenance, and local naming. Because the carrier has no coordinate
for PAC probability, empirical risk, quotient finite sets, or ambient growth
semantics, no consumer can read such data from $G$.
\end{proof}
