Following Bourbaki and Kelley on completion and Hausdorff reflection, the $\HausdorffCompletionAdjointnessUp$ packet records the finite unit-counit surface by which a displayed completion source is routed through separated reflection before any regular-rational or Real-facing seal is consumed. It sits over the completion carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}, the metric-completion carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the separated-completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedcompletion-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the adjointness route as displayed BEDC data; it does not identify quotient completion classes, choose representatives, import countable choice, compare host completed objects, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Hausdorff completion adjointness carrier]
\label{def:hausdorff-completion-adjointness-carrier}
A $\HausdorffCompletionAdjointnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(C,H,U,V,S,R,E,T,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the completion source row, recording the $\CompletionUp$ or $\MetricCompletionUp$ request, its inspected source carrier, and the finite completion-facing branch exposed before any separated reflection is read.
\item $H$ is the Hausdorff or separated reflection row, tying the completion source to the $\SeparatedCompletionUp$ boundary used to compare completed names.
\item $U$ is the unit row, sending each displayed source observation into the completion-reflection packet through $C$ and $H$.
\item $V$ is the counit row, returning any completion-facing separated consumer to the same displayed completion-reflection surface.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row. It records the finite observation windows that must be open before the unit or counit rows can be consumed by downstream readers.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, exposing regular rational data only after $C,H,U,V,S$ have been displayed.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only downstream of the completion source, separated reflection, unit row, counit row, StreamName windows, and RegSeqRat readback.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the completion source, Hausdorff reflection, unit, counit, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the completion source through reflection, unit, counit, finite windows, rational readback, and Real sealing.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HausdorffCompletionAdjointnessUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\SeparatedCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HausdorffCompletionAdjointnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $C,H,U,V,S,R,E,T,K,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, host equality of completed objects, detached Real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}
\begin{theorem}[Hausdorff completion adjointness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hausdorff-completion-adjointness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HausdorffCompletionAdjointnessUp$ carrier $A=(C,H,U,V,S,R,E,T,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HausdorffCompletionAdjointnessUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item completion-source admission, requiring the displayed $\CompletionUp$ or $\MetricCompletionUp$ row $C$ before any adjointness data are read;
\item Hausdorff-reflection admission, requiring the $\SeparatedCompletionUp$ row $H$ before completed names may be compared;
\item unit-counit visibility, requiring the unit row $U$ and counit row $V$ to be exposed as finite rows over the same $C,H$ route;
\item finite readback routing, requiring $\StreamNameUp$ windows through $S$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$ before the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is available;
\item nonescape under structure, requiring $T,K,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed route, with no quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, detached Real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hausdorff-completion-adjointness-carrier}. The completion source is $C$, the separated-reflection boundary is $H$, and the unit-counit rows are exactly $U,V$.

The readback rows are downstream of that route. The finite StreamName windows are stored in $S$, the regular rational readback is stored in $R$, and the Real-facing seal is stored in $E$.

The structural rows cannot add new analytic content. The row $T$ transports the displayed components by $\hsame$, $K$ replays the same route by $\Cont$, $P$ records provenance, and $N$ names the local packet.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for the excluded quotient, selector, choice, host-equality, detached-Real, or ambient-completeness objects, the five listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
\begin{theorem}[Hausdorff completion adjointness unit-counit route]
\label{thm:hausdorff-completion-adjointness-unit-counit-route}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\HausdorffCompletionAdjointnessUp$ carrier must expose the unit row $U$ and counit row $V$ over the finite route
$$
C \longmapsto H \longmapsto U,V \longmapsto S \longmapsto R \longmapsto E \longmapsto T,K,P,N
$$
before any $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback or $\RealUp$ seal is read. In particular, the packet permits downstream completion, regular-rational, and Real consumers to see the adjointness boundary only as displayed unit-counit data over the same completion and Hausdorff-reflection rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a completion-facing consumer. Carrier admission exposes no rational readback or Real seal until the completion source $C$ and Hausdorff-reflection row $H$ have been read.

The unit and counit rows are the next public rows on the route. The row $U$ sends source observations into the completion-reflection packet, while $V$ returns separated completion consumers to that same packet.

Only after $U,V$ are visible can the consumer inspect finite observations and rational readback. These are the rows $S$ and $R$, and the terminal Real seal $E$ is downstream of both.

Finally $T,K,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the displayed route. They cannot introduce quotient classes, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, detached Real equality, or ambient completeness because no such coordinate is carried.
\end{proof}
