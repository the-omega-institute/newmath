\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion pullback NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-pullback-namecert-obligations}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-pullback-carrier}. Carrier admission exposes exactly the two source rows, the two leg rows, the two finite windows, the two regular-rational readbacks, the shared seal, and the four structural rows.

Leg compatibility is read from $f$ and $g$ together with $W_A,W_B,R_A,R_B$. The left leg must pass from the $\CauchyCompletionMonadUp$ row through its displayed window and readback; the right leg must pass from the $\MetricCompletionUp$ row through its displayed window and readback.

The shared target seal is the single row $T$. Since both legs name the same terminal $\RealUp$ coordinate only after the windows and readbacks have been displayed, the packet supplies a finite compatibility surface rather than a host pullback or quotient-completion equality.

Finally $H,C,P_0,N$ provide componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming for that same finite route. No remaining coordinate can export selected limits, countable choice, detached representatives, or ambient completeness, so the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
