\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LefschetzFixedPointUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-lefschetz-fixed-point-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Lefschetz fixed point theory, the $\LefschetzFixedPointUp$ packet records only the finite chain-level trace ledger attached to a displayed endomap. It reconstructs the theorem's computable accounting surface inside BEDC by naming chain groups, boundary compatibility, induced homology rows, alternating traces, and fixed-point ledger rows, while leaving global topological fixed-point existence outside the packet.

\begin{definition}[Lefschetz fixed point finite-chain carrier]
\label{def:lefschetz-fixed-point-finite-chain-carrier}
A $\LefschetzFixedPointUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(X,K,F,A,T,H,C,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the consumed $\NameCert_{\CompactUp}$ row for the finite compact source visible to the construction.
\item $K$ is the finite cellular or simplicial chain ledger, including boundary rows and degree indices.
\item $F$ is the consumed $\NameCert_{\ContinuousMapUp}$ row for the self-map whose chain-map shadow is being read.
\item $A$ records the finite chain-map compatibility rows, including boundary commutation and transport of cells or basis rows.
\item $T$ is the finite trace ledger over the induced homology or chain quotient rows, expressed through displayed $\MatrixUp$ or $\LinearMapUp$ trace data.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact source, chain ledger, continuous-map row, chain-map row, trace ledger, continuation, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from $X,K,F$ into $A$, from $A$ into $T$, and from $T$ into the local fixed-point ledger row.
\item $R$ is the fixed-point accounting row: it may record a nonzero displayed Lefschetz trace, a zero trace, or a refusal that the displayed finite rows do not support a fixed-point read.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LefschetzFixedPointUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\HomologyUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LefschetzFixedPointUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only the finite trace and fixed-point accounting ledger. It contains no ambient topological fixed-point theorem, no host singular homology machinery, no quotient space construction beyond the displayed finite rows, and no global existence claim.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[LefschetzFixedPoint NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lefschetz-fixed-point-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LefschetzFixedPointUp$ carrier $L=(X,K,F,A,T,H,C,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LefschetzFixedPointUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the compact source, finite chain ledger, continuous self-map, chain-map compatibility, trace ledger, transport, replay, fixed-point accounting, provenance, and naming rows;
\item chain-map route, requiring every trace read to pass through $X,K,F,A$ before any $\HomologyUp$, $\MatrixUp$, or $\LinearMapUp$ trace row is selected;
\item Lefschetz trace ledger, recording the displayed alternating trace rows and their finite homology or chain-quotient sources in $T$;
\item fixed-point accounting row, exposing only the local ledger conclusion $R$ obtained from the displayed trace rows;
\item no-host-existence boundary, excluding global fixed-point existence, host compactness theorems, host singular homology, and undeclared topological degree machinery.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is stored outside these finite rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:lefschetz-fixed-point-finite-chain-carrier}. The available sources are exactly $X,K,F,A,T,H,C,R,P,N$.

The chain-map route forces trace reads through the displayed compact source, finite chain ledger, continuous-map row, and chain-map compatibility row. Thus the trace ledger $T$ can cite only finite $\HomologyUp$, $\MatrixUp$, or $\LinearMapUp$ data already reached by that route.

The fixed-point accounting row $R$ is downstream from $T$. It records the local outcome named by the packet, but it does not assert a global fixed-point theorem for arbitrary compact spaces or arbitrary continuous maps.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite data. Since none of these rows contains host singular homology, an ambient quotient construction, or a global existence theorem, those claims remain outside the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Lefschetz fixed point finite-trace ledger]
\label{thm:lefschetz-fixed-point-finite-trace-ledger}
Let $L=(X,K,F,A,T,H,C,R,P,N)$ be an accepted $\LefschetzFixedPointUp$ carrier. Every fixed-point read exported by $L$ factors through the finite route
$$
X,K,F \longmapsto A \longmapsto T \longmapsto R,C,P,N .
$$
The exported read may cite the displayed alternating trace rows, but it cannot bypass $A$ or $T$ to import a host fixed-point theorem, a host homology theory, or an undeclared topological-degree computation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an exported fixed-point request. The only visible source rows are the compact source $X$, the finite chain ledger $K$, and the self-map row $F$.

The request must then pass through the chain-map compatibility ledger $A$. Only after boundary compatibility and basis transport are displayed can the continuation route select the finite trace ledger $T$.

The row $R$ records the local fixed-point accounting result attached to those traces. The structural rows $C,P,N$ expose this result as named BEDC data, while $H$ preserves it under accepted transport. No coordinate in the carrier supplies the excluded host machinery.
\end{proof}
