\label{def:cauchy-apartness-modulus-carrier}

$$

\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the two regular rational names on that window.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row carrying the dyadic positive gap and the comparison budget used to read it.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy apartness modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-apartness-modulus-namecert-obligations}

$$

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy apartness modulus regular window]
\label{thm:cauchy-apartness-modulus-regular-window}

$$

\RegSeqRatUp,\quad
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad
\RealUp,\quad
\CauchyRealApartnessUp
$$
and not an external order oracle or quotient-real comparison.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The regular rational readback and dyadic tolerance budget determine the public positive gap. The terminal Real seal is visible only after those rows have been opened, so the apartness row is a finite regular-window witness.

Replay through $H,C,P,N$ preserves the same route. A consumer that omits any of $S,Q,D,R$ has not reached the named apartness modulus and cannot read $J$ as public evidence.
\end{proof}
