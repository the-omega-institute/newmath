\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:oscillation-seminorm-carrier}. The analytic rows are $F,W,O,M,D,R,E,B$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The oscillation side is finite. The row $O$ supplies the bounded oscillation ledger for the displayed window, while $M$ records the modulus route by which a tolerance request opens only the corresponding finite windows.
