\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\StratifiedSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-stratified-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Whitney and Thom, the $\StratifiedSpaceUp$ packet records a finite stratum ledger for a displayed topological space whose local pieces are read through manifold, simplicial, or cellular charts. It sits after the indexed-open surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the deformation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homotopy-namecert}, the finite face inventory of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-simplicialcomplex-namecert}, and the cellular attachment surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cw-complex-namecert}. The packet names incidence, frontier, local chart, and consumer handoff rows without importing host stratification theory, selected tubular neighborhoods, quotient spaces, or ambient smooth transversality.

\begin{definition}[Stratified space carrier]
\label{def:stratified-space-carrier}
A $\StratifiedSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(T,L,F,C,A,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row, carrying the named open indices and finite membership reads for the ambient observable space;
\item $L$ is the finite stratum-label row, listing the displayed strata and their dimension or depth tags;
\item $F$ is the frontier-incidence row, recording which stratum closures may meet lower-depth strata;
\item $C$ is the local chart row, read through $\SimplicialComplexUp$ or $\CWComplexUp$ finite cells when a consumer needs combinatorial support;
\item $A$ is the deformation-admissibility row, read through $\HomotopyUp$ for stratum-preserving deformation requests;
\item $H$ is the componentwise $\hsame$ transport row for $T,L,F,C,A$;
\item $R$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay row for refining a finite chart or frontier read without changing the named stratum ledger;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\StratifiedSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\HomotopyUp$, $\SimplicialComplexUp$, $\CWComplexUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\StratifiedSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host Whitney condition theorem, a selected normal slice, quotient topological equality, global triangulation choice, or ambient smooth transversality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Stratified space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:stratified-space-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:stratified-space-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\StratifiedSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of ambient topology exposure through $T$, finite stratum inventory through $L$, frontier incidence through $F$, local chart support through $C$, stratum-preserving deformation admissibility through $A$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $R$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The topology source supplies open-membership rows, the stratum row lists only finitely many inspected pieces, and the frontier row records only the displayed incidence relation among those pieces.

Read the sibling chart rows next. The simplicial-complex and CW-complex chapters supply finite face and cell inventories, while the homotopy chapter supplies only the deformation route attached to those displayed strata.

Finally attach transport, replay, provenance, and naming. Since every consumer-visible read is one of $T,L,F,C,A,H,R,P,N$, the packet cannot export host stratification theory, selected tubular neighborhoods, quotient spaces, global triangulation choice, or transversality data outside the stated NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Stratified space cellular handoff]
\label{thm:stratified-space-cellular-handoff}
Every homology, homotopy, or topology consumer of an accepted $\StratifiedSpaceUp$ packet must expose the finite route
$$
\TopologyUp \longmapsto L,F \longmapsto \SimplicialComplexUp,\CWComplexUp \longmapsto \HomotopyUp
$$
before it reads a stratum-preserving boundary. The route is a finite chart-and-frontier handoff, not a host Whitney stratification theorem or a quotient realization of a stratified space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{def:stratified-space-carrier}. The ambient topology row $T$ is read before any stratum label, and the frontier row $F$ is attached to the same finite stratum inventory $L$.

Next read the chart row $C$. Any simplicial or cellular support is supplied by the sibling $\SimplicialComplexUp$ and $\CWComplexUp$ packets, so the handoff has finite face or cell data before the deformation row is inspected.

The row $A$ then records only stratum-preserving deformation admissibility through $\HomotopyUp$. Transport and replay preserve the same route through $H,R,P,N$, so the consumer receives the displayed packet and no additional global stratification principle.
\end{proof}

\falsifiablePrediction{A $\StratifiedSpaceUp$ consumer that deletes the frontier row $F$ can no longer distinguish a stratum-preserving chart handoff from an arbitrary topological decomposition.}

\independenceWitness{topology, homotopy, simplicialcomplex, cw-complex: TopologyUp supplies open-membership rows but not a stratum frontier ledger; HomotopyUp supplies deformation rows but not finite strata; SimplicialComplexUp and CWComplexUp supply finite face or cell inventories but not the ambient topology and deformation handoff together. None is bijective to $\StratifiedSpaceUp$ under NameCert-row preservation.}
