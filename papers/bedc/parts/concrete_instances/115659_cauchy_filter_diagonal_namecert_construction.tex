\begin{definition}[Cauchy-filter diagonal carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-filter-diagonal-carrier}

$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ source row whose base requests and finite Cauchy evidence are available before any completion consumer reads the packet.
\item $K$ is the $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ handoff row, recording the completion-facing use of the displayed filter source.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ boundary row, visible only after $F$ and $K$ have exposed the diagonal request.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row that supplies the diagonal observation schedule.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback attached to the stream windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached after the regular rational readback.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $F,K,M,S,Q,R,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite Cauchy-filter request through the completion handoff, the metric-completion boundary, the diagonal stream window, the regular rational readback, and the Real seal.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy-filter diagonal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-filter-diagonal-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyFilterDiagonalUp$ carrier $D=(F,K,M,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyFilterDiagonalUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed Cauchy-filter source, Cauchy-filter completion handoff, metric-completion boundary, stream-window schedule, regular rational readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item diagonal source routing, requiring every completion-facing request to read the same $F$ row before the rows $K$ and $M$ are available;
\item finite-window diagonalization, requiring the stream row $S$ to supply the observed diagonal windows before any regular rational row $Q$ or Real seal row $R$ is consumed;
\item classifier stability, requiring componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$ and displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$ to preserve the route $F,K,M,S,Q,R$;
\item ledger non-escape, requiring $P$ and $N$ to export no quotient filter, selected representative, chosen limit, countable-choice schedule, quotient stream equality, host real equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:cauchy-filter-diagonal-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the rows $F,K,M,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N$, so every local obligation must be read from those rows.

The diagonal source route is forced by the carrier order. A completion-facing request cannot reach $K$ or $M$ without first reading the displayed Cauchy-filter source $F$.

The finite-window part then uses the diagonal observation schedule. The stream row $S$ supplies the inspected windows, $Q$ reads them as regular rational data, and $R$ seals only the resulting Real-facing name.

Transport and replay are structural. Componentwise $\hsame$ in $H$ preserves each displayed coordinate, while $\Cont$ replay in $C$ repeats the same route $F,K,M,S,Q,R$ for each accepted request.

Finally, the provenance rows are exhausted by $P$ and $N$. Since quotient filters, selected representatives, chosen limits, countable choice, quotient stream equality, host real equality, and ambient Real completeness are not carrier rows, the local NameCert surface cannot export them.
\end{proof}
