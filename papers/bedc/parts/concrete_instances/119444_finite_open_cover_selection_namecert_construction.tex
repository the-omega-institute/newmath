\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FiniteOpenCoverSelectionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-finite-open-cover-selection-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges and Kelley on compactness via finite subcovers, the $\FiniteOpenCoverSelectionUp$ packet records only selection from already displayed finite open-cover rows. It sits after the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite cover cells of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-real-cover-namecert}, the stream schedules of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular-rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite selected subrow already visible in the cover ledger; it does not choose an arbitrary open cover, import countable choice, identify quotient real names, appeal to host compactness, select subcovers outside displayed finite rows, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Finite open cover selection carrier]
\label{def:finite-open-cover-selection-carrier}
A $\FiniteOpenCoverSelectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(K,O,S,R,D,E,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row exposing the compact metric carrier and the finite cover request already admitted by that source.
\item $O$ is the $\FiniteRealCoverUp$ row, or equivalently the displayed finite cover-cell ledger, whose cells are the only candidates from which selection may read.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule row attached to the cover cells and to the finite observation windows used by the consumer.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational observations visible in those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius and tolerance row attached to the cover cells, stream windows, and readback comparisons.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, reachable only after $K,O,S,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $U$ is the selected finite subrow of $O$, recorded as a displayed sublist or subbundle of cover cells rather than as an external selector.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact-source, finite-cover, stream, readback, dyadic, Real-seal, selected-subrow, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite cover-selection request through $K,O,S,R,D,E,U,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteOpenCoverSelectionUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\FiniteRealCoverUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteOpenCoverSelectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed finite cover row and its selected finite subrow, transported together with the stream, readback, tolerance, Real-seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite open cover selection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-open-cover-selection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteOpenCoverSelectionUp$ carrier $F=(K,O,S,R,D,E,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteOpenCoverSelectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, compact metric source exposure, displayed finite cover-cell exposure, $\StreamNameUp$ schedule exposure, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback exposure, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius and tolerance exposure, terminal $\RealUp$ seal exposure, selected finite subrow exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-open-cover-selection-carrier}. The only cover source is $O$, and the only selected object is the finite subrow $U$ already carried inside that displayed cover ledger.

Read the analytic rows in order. The compact metric source $K$ admits the finite cover request, $O$ displays the finite cover cells, $S$ schedules the finite observations, $R$ reads them back as regular rational data, $D$ attaches dyadic radius and tolerance comparisons, and $E$ supplies the terminal real-name seal.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover compactness, countable choice, quotient real equality, host compactness, selected subcovers outside $O$, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, those objects are not local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite open cover selection compact-uniform route]
\label{thm:finite-open-cover-selection-compact-uniform-route}
Every compact-uniform or Real-completion consumer of an accepted $\FiniteOpenCoverSelectionUp$ packet reads the selected subrow only through the finite corridor
$$
\CompactMetricUp\longmapsto\FiniteRealCoverUp\longmapsto\StreamNameUp\longmapsto\RegSeqRatUp\longmapsto\DyadicRatCoreUp\longmapsto\RealUp .
$$
The corridor exposes the selected finite subrow $U$ as part of the displayed cover row $O$ before any downstream uniform-modulus, finite-cover, or real-name consumer may use it.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:finite-open-cover-selection-namecert-obligations}. They force the compact source, finite cover cells, schedule, readback, dyadic tolerance, Real seal, selected subrow, and structural rows to be visible before the packet is consumed.

The selected subrow is read from $O$ through $U$. Thus a downstream compact-uniform route receives a finite cover-cell subrow already displayed in the packet, not an open-cover selector and not a host compactness principle.

Finally replay the stream, regular-rational, dyadic, and Real-seal rows. They provide the finite observation and tolerance corridor through which the selected cover cells can feed a uniform-modulus or Real-completion consumer. The route has no coordinate for countable choice, quotient real equality, subcovers outside the displayed finite rows, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
