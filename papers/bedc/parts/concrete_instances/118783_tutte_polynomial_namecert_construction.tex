\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\TuttePolynomialUp$}

Following Tutte's deletion-contraction treatment of finite graph invariants, the $\TuttePolynomialUp$ packet records the polynomial ledger that a graph or matroid consumer may inspect before using the two-variable Tutte polynomial. It sits over the finite graph carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-graph-namecert}, the rank and independence surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matroid-namecert}, the coefficient-list discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-namecert}, and the edge-ledger comparison route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-networkflow-namecert}. The packet names only finite deletion, contraction, base-case, coefficient, classifier, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it does not assert graph-minor structure, universal invariant uniqueness, or an ambient host polynomial quotient.

\begin{definition}[Tutte polynomial carrier]
\label{def:tutte-polynomial-carrier}
A $\TuttePolynomialUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(G,M,E,D,C,B,L,K,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the finite $\GraphUp$ row, including vertex, edge, incidence, loop, bridge, and endpoint readback data.
\item $M$ is the optional $\MatroidUp$ rank-dependence row used when the deletion-contraction read is routed through a displayed matroid carrier.
\item $E$ is the inspected edge row naming the finite edge whose deletion or contraction branch is being read.
\item $D$ is the deletion row, carrying the graph or matroid packet obtained by removing $E$ from the same displayed support.
\item $C$ is the contraction row, carrying the graph or matroid packet obtained by contracting $E$ with its endpoint or rank bookkeeping displayed.
\item $B$ is the base-case row for empty support, loop, bridge, and isolated component cases.
\item $L$ is the finite coefficient ledger in the $\PolynomialUp$ surface, with the two variables and all inspected coefficient cells named as rows.
\item $K$ is the classifier-stability row requiring deletion, contraction, base-case, and coefficient reads to remain stable under the displayed $\hsame$ witnesses.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transports for $G,M,E,D,C,B,L,K,R,P,N$.
\item $R$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay through graph or matroid inspection, deletion, contraction, base cases, coefficient extraction, classifier stability, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TuttePolynomialUp$, $\GraphUp$, $\MatroidUp$, $\PolynomialUp$, $\NetworkFlowUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TuttePolynomialUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these finite rows. It has no coordinate for a host graph-minor theorem, a universal invariant proof, quotient equality of polynomials, arbitrary edge-set selection, or an ambient deletion-contraction oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tutte polynomial deletion-contraction obligations]
\label{thm:tutte-polynomial-deletion-contraction-obligations}
For every accepted $\TuttePolynomialUp$ carrier $T=(G,M,E,D,C,B,L,K,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\TuttePolynomialUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the finite graph row, optional matroid row, inspected edge row, deletion row, contraction row, base-case row, coefficient ledger, classifier-stability row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every polynomial-facing consumer must expose the deletion branch, contraction branch, base cases, coefficient ledger, and classifier-stability row before it reads the Tutte-polynomial packet as a finite invariant.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:tutte-polynomial-carrier}. The source rows are $G,M,E$, the recursive polynomial rows are $D,C,B,L$, and the structural rows are $K,H,R,P,N$.

Read the deletion and contraction branches first. The inspected edge row $E$ determines which finite support is removed or contracted, while $D$ and $C$ keep both resulting packets inside the displayed $\GraphUp$ or $\MatroidUp$ dependency surface.

Next read the base and coefficient rows. The row $B$ records the empty-support, loop, bridge, and isolated-component cases, and $L$ records the two-variable coefficient ledger through $\PolynomialUp$ rather than through host polynomial equality.

Finally apply the classifier and structural rows. The stability row $K$ makes the deletion, contraction, base-case, and coefficient data invariant under the displayed $\hsame$ transports, while $H,R,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no coordinate stores a graph-minor theorem, a universal invariant proof, or an ambient polynomial quotient, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate obligations.
\end{proof}
