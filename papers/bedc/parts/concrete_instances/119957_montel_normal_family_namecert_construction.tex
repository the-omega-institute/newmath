\begin{definition}[Montel normal-family carrier]

$$

\item $T$ is the $\MontelTheoremUp$ handoff row, restricted to the same family ledger and compact complex window.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactUp$ finite-window row for the displayed compact subset of the complex domain.
\item $L$ is the local boundedness ledger for the family on $K$.
\item $F$ is the $\HolomorphicUp$ readback row for the displayed family entries on the complex window.
\item $Z$ is the $\ComplexUp$ coordinate row for source points, target values, and the visible complex bounds used by $L$ and $F$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule that lists the family members, compact-window probes, and tolerance requests actually inspected.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows and their displayed transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Montel normal-family NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:montel-normal-family-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MontelNormalFamilyUp$ carrier $M=(N,T,K,L,F,Z,S,R,H,C,P,A)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MontelNormalFamilyUp}$ surface consists exactly of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item normal-family admission for $N$ over the same displayed family ledger;
\item Montel handoff admission for $T$ over the same compact complex window;
\item compact finite-window admission for $K$;
\item local boundedness admission for $L$ over $K$;
\item holomorphic and complex readback through $F$ and $Z$;
\item finite stream-window and regular-rational tolerance admission through $S$ and $R$;
\item componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,A$.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation introduces countable choice, arbitrary subsequence selection, open-cover compactness, ambient complex completeness, quotient complex equality, or a host compact-open function space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier in \autoref{def:montel-normal-family-carrier}. The normal-family row $N$ and Montel row $T$ are the only family-compactness handoffs, and both are tied to the same compact window $K$ and family window schedule $S$.

The analytic input is finite. Local boundedness is the row $L$, holomorphic readback is the row $F$, complex coordinate exposure is the row $Z$, and regular rational tolerance data is the row $R$. Each of these rows is named before the packet can be consumed as a Montel normal-family handoff.

Finally, $H,C,P,A$ preserve exactly the displayed row inventory under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the packet has no coordinate for a choice-selected subsequence, open-cover compactness proof, ambient complex completeness theorem, quotient equality principle, or compact-open host function space, the local certificate cannot export any of those objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
