\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\PrincipalBundleUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-principal-bundle-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Steenrod and Kobayashi--Nomizu, the $\PrincipalBundleUp$ packet records principal-bundle data as finite BEDC NameCert rows. It sits over the displayed bundle carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bundle-namecert}, the charted base surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-manifold-namecert}, the smooth structure-group rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-liegroup-namecert}, the algebraic action boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}, and the continuous operation ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topgroup-namecert}. The packet names only the visible total space, base manifold, projection, local trivializations, transition cocycle, group action, transport, replay, provenance, and local certificate rows; it does not construct quotient bundle equality, choose a global section, assert a host smooth structure, or provide bridge checking.

\begin{definition}[Principal bundle carrier]
\label{def:principal-bundle-carrier}
A $\PrincipalBundleUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(E,B,\pi,G,U,\tau,\gamma,\alpha,H,C,\rho,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the total-space row, read through the upstream $\BundleUp$ carrier surface.
\item $B$ is the base-manifold row, read through the chart carrier of $\ManifoldUp$.
\item $\pi$ is the projection row from $E$ to $B$, displayed as bundle data rather than as a host map equality.
\item $G$ is the structure-group row, read through the $\LieGroupUp$, $\GroupUp$, and $\TopGroupUp$ operation surfaces.
\item $U$ is the finite local-trivialization cover row, including the displayed local product windows used by the bundle packet.
\item $\tau$ is the transition-cocycle row over overlapping local trivialization windows.
\item $\gamma$ is the group-action row, naming the right action of the displayed structure group on the displayed total-space fibres.
\item $\alpha$ is the $\hsame$ transport row for the total space, base, projection, structure group, local trivializations, transition cocycle, and group action.
\item $H$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay row for reading the same local data after transport.
\item $C$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PrincipalBundleUp$, $\BundleUp$, $\ManifoldUp$, $\LieGroupUp$, $\GroupUp$, $\TopGroupUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $\rho$ records the finite dependency order in which the upstream rows are exposed to a principal-bundle consumer.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PrincipalBundleUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient bundle equality, selected global sections, ambient smooth structures, external atlas maximality, or detached host principal-bundle objects.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Principal bundle NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:principal-bundle-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:principal-bundle-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\PrincipalBundleUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following displayed obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the packet $P=(E,B,\pi,G,U,\tau,\gamma,\alpha,H,C,\rho,N)$;
\item bundle exposure through the total-space, projection, and local-trivialization rows $E,\pi,U$;
\item base exposure through the $\ManifoldUp$ row $B$;
\item structure-group exposure through the $\LieGroupUp$, $\GroupUp$, and $\TopGroupUp$ readings of $G$;
\item action and transition exposure through $\gamma$ and $\tau$;
\item componentwise transport, replay, dependency provenance, and local naming through $\alpha,H,C,\rho,N$.
\end{enumerate}
Ledger exactness says that every accepted principal-bundle read exposes precisely these rows before a principal-bundle consumer may inspect the transition or action data. The obligations do not add quotient bundle equality, selected global sections, host smooth structures, atlas maximality, or bridge checking.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Principal bundle transition cocycle]
\label{thm:principal-bundle-transition-cocycle}
Let $P=(E,B,\pi,G,U,\tau,\gamma,\alpha,H,C,\rho,N)$ be an accepted $\PrincipalBundleUp$ carrier. Every transition-cocycle read from $P$ factors through the displayed base-manifold row $B$, the local-trivialization row $U$, and the structure-group row $G$ before the cocycle row $\tau$ is public:
$$
\ManifoldUp,\ \BundleUp,\ \LieGroupUp,\ \GroupUp,\ \TopGroupUp \longrightarrow \tau .
$$
On triple overlaps, the cocycle comparison is recorded only as finite NameCert data transported by $\alpha$ and replayed by $H$. It cannot identify quotient bundles, choose a section, import a maximal smooth atlas, or replace the displayed structure-group row by an ambient host group.
\end{theorem}
