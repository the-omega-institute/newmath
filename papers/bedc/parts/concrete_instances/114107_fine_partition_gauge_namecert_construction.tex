\begin{theorem}[FinePartitionGauge NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:fine-partition-gauge-namecert-obligations}

Every $\GaugeIntegralUp$, $\RiemannSumGaugeUp$, or $\DarbouxSumUp$ consumer must read these rows before using the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:fine-partition-gauge-carrier}. The rows $I,G,T,L,M,U,R,E,H,C,P,N$ are the entire displayed packet, so any local certificate obligation must be one of the listed interval, gauge, tagged-cell, fineness, mesh, handoff, readback, seal, transport, replay, provenance, or naming rows.

The fineness obligation is read before any sum or integral row. The tagged-cell ledger $T$ supplies only finite cells and tags, while $L$ checks each cell against the positive radius supplied by $G$.

The Cousin handoff and readback rows keep downstream consumers on the same finite route. The row $U$ is the only route to the Cousin existence surface, $R$ is the only regular rational readback, and $E$ is visible only after $R$. Since the carrier has no selected-partition, host-compactness, or ambient integration coordinate, the NameCert surface is exactly the displayed packet.
\end{proof}
