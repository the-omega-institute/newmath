\label{def:cauchy-complete-space-carrier}

$$

\item $D$ is the distance or apartness-control row used by Cauchy consumers.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row for inspected Cauchy names.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy complete space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-complete-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompleteSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
S=(X,D,W,Q,R,E,L,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompleteSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of space-source exposure through $X$, distance-control exposure through $D$, finite Cauchy-window exposure through $\StreamNameUp$ row $W$, regular rational readback through $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $Q$, Real sealing through $R$, completion-envelope exposure through $E$, regular Cauchy limit handoff through $L$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-complete-space-carrier}. Its visible analytic rows are $X,D,W,Q,R,E,L$, and its structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The Cauchy source is finite at every read. A consumer first sees stream windows through $W$ and regular rational estimates through $Q$; only then can it inspect the Real seal $R$ or the completion envelope $E$.

The handoff row $L$ keeps this order attached to the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient streams, selected representatives, countable choice, or host completion, the listed rows exhaust the complete-space obligation surface.
\end{proof}
