$$

\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, carrying dyadic requests and arithmetic comparisons for the convergence budget.
\item $M$ is the Bishop modulus row: for each displayed tolerance request it supplies a finite stage bound, together with the statement that all later scheduled windows lie inside the requested dyadic tolerance.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from $S$, $D$, and $M$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, exposed only after the stream schedule, dyadic tolerance row, modulus row, and regular rational readback have been displayed.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopModulusConvergenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[BishopModulusConvergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-modulus-convergence-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\BishopModulusConvergenceUp$ carrier $(S,D,M,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopModulusConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of these finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the stream schedule, dyadic tolerance row, Bishop modulus row, regular rational readback, real seal, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming row;
\item tolerance discipline, requiring every convergence request to enter through $D$ before a stage bound in $M$ is read;
\item modulus exactness, requiring the bound supplied by $M$ to control all later windows exposed by $S$ at the requested tolerance;
\item regular readback, requiring $R$ to be obtained from the displayed stream, dyadic, and modulus rows before the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is public;
\item transport and replay, requiring $H$ and $C$ to preserve the same ordered route under $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay;
\item ledger non-escape, excluding quotient streams, selected host limits, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, and ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted packet. The carrier inventory is exactly the stream schedule $S$, dyadic tolerance row $D$, Bishop modulus row $M$, regular readback $R$, real seal $E$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Tolerance discipline follows from the order of the packet. A convergence request first becomes a dyadic row in $D$, and only then can the displayed modulus row $M$ supply the finite stage bound.

Modulus exactness is the content of $M$: after the bound is read, every later scheduled window supplied by $S$ remains within the requested dyadic tolerance. The regular readback row $R$ packages that controlled tail as a $\RegSeqRatUp$ name before $E$ seals it as a $\RealUp$ row.

The rows $H$ and $C$ transport and replay the same route, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming. Since no remaining coordinate stores quotient stream equality, a selected host limit, countable choice, trichotomy, host equality, or ambient completeness, those data cannot be projected from the packet.
\end{proof}
