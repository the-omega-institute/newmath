\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RationalFloorBoundUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-rational-floor-bound-namecert}
\origin{human}

Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis treats integer-part extraction for rationals as bounded search once an explicit Archimedean bound has been displayed. The $\RationalFloorBoundUp$ packet records that finite bounded-search floor witness as a NameCert route between the rational carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the signed-integer interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-int-namecert}, the dyadic scaling ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream and regular-rational readbacks of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only the displayed rational floor route; it does not import a host floor function, least-number operator, quotient representative, choice principle, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[RationalFloorBound carrier]
\label{def:rational-floor-bound-carrier}
A $\RationalFloorBoundUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(Q,U,B,F,D,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ input row, naming the rational whose integer part is being bounded and inspected.
\item $U$ is a unary Archimedean bound row, displaying a finite natural bound large enough for the bounded search interval around $Q$.
\item $B$ is the bounded-search trace, recording the inspected integer candidates and the first adjacent pair where the rational input changes from being below the upper candidate to being at least the lower candidate.
\item $F$ is the $\IntUp$ floor witness row, storing the integer candidate certified by the search trace.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ scaling ledger, recording denominator clearing, dyadic comparison cells, and tolerance-free rational comparison data used by the trace.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the rational input, unary bound, search trace, integer witness, dyadic scaling ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite route
$$
Q,U,D \longmapsto B \longmapsto F .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RationalFloorBoundUp$, $\RatUp$, $\IntUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RationalFloorBoundUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $Q,U,B,F,D,T,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host floor, least-number search, quotient representative equality, countable choice, hidden real completeness, or an unbounded integer-part oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RationalFloorBound NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:rational-floor-bound-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RationalFloorBoundUp$ carrier $L=(Q,U,B,F,D,T,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RationalFloorBoundUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the $\RatUp$ input, unary bound, bounded-search trace, $\IntUp$ witness, dyadic scaling ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item bounded-search visibility, requiring every inspected candidate and stopping comparison in $B$ to remain displayed under the unary bound $U$;
\item floor inequality, requiring the witness row $F$ to certify $F \leq Q < F+1$ through rational comparisons read from $Q$ and $D$;
\item dyadic scale compatibility, requiring denominator clearing and comparison cells in $D$ to agree with the rational input and the integer candidates inspected by $B$;
\item ledger nonescape, stating that no host floor function, least-number operator, quotient representative, choice principle, hidden real-completeness row, or unbounded search oracle is exported by the packet.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:rational-floor-bound-carrier}. The rows $Q,U,B,F,D,T,C,P,N$ are the full visible surface of the packet, so a consumer must read the rational input and unary bound before it can inspect any search trace or integer witness.

Read the bounded search trace under the dyadic ledger. The row $B$ records only finitely many inspected integer candidates bounded by $U$, while $D$ supplies the rational comparison data used to verify the adjacent inequalities around $F$.

Finally replay through $T,C,P,N$. Transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite route and introduce no coordinate for host floor, least-number selection, quotient representatives, choice, hidden completeness, or unbounded search. The listed rows therefore exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RationalFloorBound Archimedean step]
\label{thm:rational-floor-bound-archimedean-step}
Every accepted $\RationalFloorBoundUp$ packet exports a finite unary bound and an $\IntUp$ floor witness for the displayed rational input $Q$. The export is the route
$$
Q,U,D \longmapsto B \longmapsto F
$$
inside $L=(Q,U,B,F,D,T,C,P,N)$: $U$ supplies the finite search limit, $B$ records the bounded trace, $D$ supplies the dyadic rational comparison ledger, and $F$ is the displayed integer satisfying $F \leq Q < F+1$. The route uses no host floor function, least-number operator, quotient representative, choice principle, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier admission row. It displays the rational input $Q$, unary bound $U$, dyadic scaling ledger $D$, bounded-search trace $B$, and integer witness $F$ as coordinates of the same finite packet.

Use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:rational-floor-bound-namecert-obligations}. Bounded-search visibility keeps every inspected candidate inside the unary bound, and dyadic scale compatibility makes the rational comparisons in the trace read from $Q$ and $D$ rather than from a host floor oracle.

The floor inequality row then identifies the adjacent certified candidates. The witness $F$ is exported only with the displayed inequalities $F \leq Q < F+1$, while $T,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same finite route. Since no excluded floor, least-number, quotient, choice, or real-completeness coordinate exists in the carrier, the Archimedean step is constructively bounded.
\end{proof}
