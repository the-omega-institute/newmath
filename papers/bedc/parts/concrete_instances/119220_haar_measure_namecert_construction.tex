\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HaarMeasureUp$}

Following Haar and Weil, the $\HaarMeasureUp$ packet records the finite NameCert surface for an invariant measure on a displayed locally compact group without taking the host existence theorem as a primitive. It sits after the topological-group rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topgroup-namecert}, the measure carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-measure-namecert}, the Radon--Nikodym comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-radon-nikodym-namecert}, the character-duality boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pontryaginduality-namecert}, and the finite-group averaging packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-haar-integral-finite-group-namecert}. The chapter names only the local rows that an invariant-measure consumer may inspect; it does not construct the ambient Haar existence theorem, quotient measure equality, selected representatives, modular-function theory, or completed locally compact group analysis.

\begin{definition}[HaarMeasure carrier]
\label{def:haar-measure-carrier}
A $\HaarMeasureUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(G,T,M,L,R,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the $\TopGroupUp$ source row, naming the group operation, identity, inverse, neighbourhood, compact-window, and translation data read before an invariant-measure row may be consumed.
\item $T$ is the local compactness and regularity window row, exposing the compact-test and open-neighbourhood data through which measure values are inspected.
\item $M$ is the $\MeasureUp$ row, recording the displayed event family and measure-value readback available to the packet.
\item $L$ and $R$ are the left- and right-translation invariance rows, recording how translated compact or measurable windows replay the same measure read.
\item $W$ is the comparison and refusal row, tying Radon--Nikodym and Pontryagin-duality consumers to the displayed invariant-measure surface while excluding quotient measure equality, selected representatives, modular-function claims, and host existence theorems.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topological-group, local-compactness, measure, invariance, comparison, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route from group rows through local compactness, measure readback, translation invariance, comparison, refusal, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HaarMeasureUp$, $\TopGroupUp$, $\MeasureUp$, $\RadonNikodymUp$, $\PontryaginDualityUp$, $\HaarIntegralFiniteGroupUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HaarMeasureUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host proof of Haar-measure existence, quotient equality of measures, selected compact approximants, an unrestricted modular function, or an ambient completed locally compact group.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[HaarMeasure NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:haar-measure-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HaarMeasureUp$ carrier $K=(G,T,M,L,R,W,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HaarMeasureUp}$ surface consists exactly of these five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed topological-group, local-compactness, measure, translation-invariance, comparison, refusal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item locally compact measure exposure, requiring $G,T,M$ to be visible before any invariant-measure consumer reads a value;
\item left and right invariance, requiring $L$ and $R$ to replay the same displayed measure row under translated windows;
\item sibling comparison, requiring Radon--Nikodym, Pontryagin-duality, and finite-group averaging consumers to enter through the displayed comparison row $W$;
\item ledger non-escape, excluding ambient Haar existence, quotient measure equality, selected representatives, unrestricted modular-function claims, and completed locally compact group analysis.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:haar-measure-carrier}. The only group, compactness, and measure data available to a consumer are the displayed rows $G,T,M$, so invariant-measure reading cannot bypass the local compactness and event-value windows.

Next inspect the translation rows. Rows $L$ and $R$ replay measure values through displayed left and right translates of the same group source row, while $H$ and $C$ preserve this replay componentwise and publicly.

Finally use the comparison and refusal row $W$ together with provenance $P$ and local naming $N$. The Radon--Nikodym, Pontryagin-duality, and finite-group averaging siblings can consume only the displayed comparison route, and no row supplies host existence, quotient equality, selected representatives, modular-function theory, or completed locally compact group analysis.
\end{proof}
