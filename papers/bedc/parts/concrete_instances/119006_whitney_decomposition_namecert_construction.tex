\begin{definition}[Whitney decomposition carrier]
\label{def:whitney-decomposition-carrier}
A $\WhitneyDecompositionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(X,U,D,Q,A,L,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row with the metric ball and located-distance reads available to the decomposition window;
\item $U$ is the displayed open-region row, naming the finite membership tests whose admitted points may be covered;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and dyadic-grid ledger, recording the dyadic scales and coordinate cells used by the window;
\item $Q$ is the finite cell inventory, listing the displayed dyadic cells and their parent-child refinement relations;
\item $A$ is the admission row, requiring each listed cell to lie inside $U$ through metric-ball certificates;
\item $L$ is the local-finiteness ledger, recording finite overlap checks for the displayed cell inventory;
\item $B$ is the boundary-distance comparison row, relating each admitted cell diameter to the located distance from that cell to the displayed complement boundary;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, dyadic, cover, overlap, and boundary-distance rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for cell admission, finite-overlap reads, and boundary-distance comparisons;

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\WhitneyDecompositionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,U,D,Q,A,L,B,H,C,P,N$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Whitney decomposition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:whitney-decomposition-namecert-obligations}
Every accepted $\WhitneyDecompositionUp$ packet binds exactly the finite NameCert obligation surface of \autoref{def:whitney-decomposition-carrier}: metric-source exposure through $X$, open-region membership through $U$, dyadic scale and cell data through $D,Q$, cell admission through $A$, finite local-overlap checks through $L$, boundary-distance control through $B$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $W=(X,U,D,Q,A,L,B,H,C,P,N)$. The metric and region rows supply the source carrier and the displayed open-region membership tests, while the dyadic rows supply only the finite cell inventory named by the packet.

The admission, overlap, and boundary rows then own the analytic checks. Cell containment is read through $A$, local finiteness is read through the finite overlap ledger $L$, and boundary-distance control is read through $B$.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, cite, and name the same displayed rows. Since the carrier has no additional coordinate, the NameCert surface cannot expose an infinite decomposition, selected boundary points, open-cover compactness, quotient cubes, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whitney decomposition local finite cover]
\label{thm:whitney-decomposition-local-finite-cover}
For every accepted $\WhitneyDecompositionUp$ carrier $W=(X,U,D,Q,A,L,B,H,C,P,N)$, every covered point of the displayed open row $U$ is read through a finite subinventory of $Q$, and every local-overlap query is answered by the finite ledger $L$. Thus the public cover read is a finite dyadic-window cover over $X$ and $U$, not an infinite locally finite family, selected enumeration, open-cover compactness proof, or host topological cover.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a point admitted by $U$. The cover read must inspect the metric source $X$, the dyadic scale ledger $D$, and the finite cell inventory $Q$ before it can name a cell containing the point.

Use the admission row $A$ to keep every selected displayed cell inside the open-region row. The local-overlap ledger $L$ then answers neighbourhood and overlap queries by finite inspection of the same inventory $Q$.

Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the displayed finite inventory. The provenance and local naming rows record no additional cells, so the read remains a finite dyadic cover window and cannot become a global host cover principle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whitney decomposition boundary-distance control]
\label{thm:whitney-decomposition-boundary-distance-control}
For every accepted $\WhitneyDecompositionUp$ carrier $W=(X,U,D,Q,A,L,B,H,C,P,N)$, each public cell read from $Q$ exposes a dyadic diameter estimate and a located-real comparison in $B$ relating that diameter to the displayed distance from the cell to the complement boundary of $U$. The estimate is read only through $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\MetricBallUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, dyadic cover rows, and located-real comparison rows; it exports no selected nearest boundary point, no quotient cube equality, no ambient real completeness theorem, and no global Whitney decomposition theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The boundary-distance comparison is the row $B$. It compares the displayed dyadic diameter with the located boundary-distance window and records the comparison as part of the finite carrier, not as a selected nearest-boundary construction.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ do not add an analytic endpoint. They preserve, replay, source, and name the same metric, dyadic, cover, and comparison rows, so quotient cube equality, ambient completeness, and a global Whitney theorem remain outside the packet.
\end{proof}
