\begin{theorem}[Brouwer continuity modulus bridge NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:brouwer-continuity-modulus-bridge-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BrouwerContinuityModulusBridgeUp$ carrier $B=(C,Q,S,R,E,M,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BrouwerContinuityModulusBridgeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the Brouwer-continuity row, choice-sequence finite-prefix row, StreamName window row, RegSeqRat readback row, Real seal row, finite modulus ledger, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Each accepted output-window request must pass through the displayed finite-prefix route
$$
C,Q \longmapsto M \longmapsto S,R \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,K,P,N .
$$
No local obligation is stored outside these displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The rows $C,Q,S,R,E,M$ are the only computational rows, and the rows $H,K,P,N$ are the only structural rows.

The Brouwer-continuity row $C$ supplies the finite-prefix dependence surface, while $Q$ records the choice-sequence cells actually inspected by the route. The StreamName row $S$ cannot answer a window request until the modulus ledger $M$ displays the corresponding input-prefix budget.

Once the window is scheduled, $R$ gives the regular rational readback and $E$ records the endpoint seal. The transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows preserve that order and add no hidden selector or completion theorem.

Thus the local NameCert obligations are exactly the displayed bridge rows. A consumer seeking countable choice, quotient stream equality, a completed host functional, or ambient Real completeness must obtain it outside this packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
