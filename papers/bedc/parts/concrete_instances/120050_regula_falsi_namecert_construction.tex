Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\RegulaFalsiUp$ packet records the false-position root-bracketing route as finite NameCert data. It sits after the constructive intermediate-value route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-constructive-intermediate-value-namecert}, beside the bisection surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bisection-method-namecert} and the secant surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-secant-method-namecert}, and before the dyadic, stream, regular-rational, and real-name handoff through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the finite bracketing rows consumed by root-finding consumers; it does not assert classical completeness, trichotomy, a selected zero, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ equality.

\begin{definition}[RegulaFalsi carrier]
\label{def:regula-falsi-carrier}
A $\RegulaFalsiUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(I,S,Q,D,W,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the sign-changing interval row supplied through $\ConstructiveIntermediateValueUp$;
\item $S$ is the false-position secant row, recording the endpoint values and the chord intersection requested at the current bracket;
\item $Q$ is the bracket-retention row, selecting the retained side only through displayed sign data rather than through a host trichotomy;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ arithmetic ledger for endpoint values, chord denominators, and tolerance budgets;
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row recording which endpoint and function-value reads are inspected;
\item $E$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row handed to the terminal $\RealUp$ seal;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval, secant, bracket, dyadic, window, readback, and seal rows;
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ routes from intermediate-value bracketing through bisection-compatible retention and secant interpolation;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegulaFalsiUp$, $\ConstructiveIntermediateValueUp$, $\BisectionMethodUp$, $\SecantMethodUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegulaFalsiUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a selected root, a global convergence theorem, an ambient real equality test, countable choice, or a classical completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RegulaFalsi NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regula-falsi-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegulaFalsiUp$ carrier $R=(I,S,Q,D,W,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegulaFalsiUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, constructive intermediate-value bracket exposure through $I$, false-position secant exposure through $S$, retained-bracket exposure through $Q$, dyadic arithmetic exactness through $D$, finite-window replay through $W$, regular-rational readback and real-seal handoff through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed continuation replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regula-falsi-carrier}. Its rows list precisely the sign-changing bracket, false-position chord, retained side, dyadic ledger, finite observation window, regular-rational readback, transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming coordinates.

Read the neighbouring certificates in their displayed roles. The constructive intermediate-value packet supplies the sign-changing bracket, the bisection packet supplies the retained-bracket discipline, and the secant packet supplies the chord-intersection row.

The remaining rows control arithmetic and replay. The dyadic ledger, stream window, RegSeqRat readback, and Real seal expose only finite inspected data, so the NameCert obligations are exhausted by the rows of $R$ and cannot add a selected root or host completeness claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RegulaFalsi bracket-secant handoff]
\label{thm:regula-falsi-bracket-secant-handoff}
Every accepted $\RegulaFalsiUp$ consumer that reads the false-position secant row $S$ must retain the same sign-changing interval row $I$, bracket-retention row $Q$, dyadic arithmetic row $D$, finite-window row $W$, regular-rational readback row $E$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$. The handoff from bracketing to secant interpolation therefore exports a finite root-bracketing route, not a selected zero or an unrestricted secant iteration.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted packet $R=(I,S,Q,D,W,E,H,C,P,N)$. The carrier makes $S$ visible only as one row of the same finite bracketing packet.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:regula-falsi-namecert-obligations}. They require the consumer to replay the constructive bracket, retained side, dyadic arithmetic, finite windows, readback, transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows whenever the secant row is cited.

Thus a consumer cannot detach the chord intersection from the bracket that licenses it. A detached secant iteration, selected root, or convergence claim would require an additional coordinate not present in \autoref{def:regula-falsi-carrier}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RegulaFalsi Real seal non-escape]
\label{thm:regula-falsi-real-seal-nonescape}
The terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached by an accepted $\RegulaFalsiUp$ packet is exposed only after the route through $\ConstructiveIntermediateValueUp$, $\BisectionMethodUp$, $\SecantMethodUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, and $\RegSeqRatUp$ has been replayed. It exports no host real equality, selected completed root, ambient completeness theorem, or countable-choice schedule.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Follow the carrier route from $I$ through $S,Q,D,W,E$. The intermediate-value row starts the bracket, the bisection-compatible row records retention, the secant row records the false-position chord, and the dyadic and stream rows determine the finite inspections.

The RegSeqRat row $E$ is the only route to the terminal Real seal. By \autoref{thm:regula-falsi-namecert-obligations} and \autoref{thm:regula-falsi-bracket-secant-handoff}, that route remains attached to the same bracket, chord, arithmetic, and replay rows.

Since the carrier has no selected-root coordinate, no quotient-real equality coordinate, no countable-choice schedule, and no ambient completeness row, the Real seal cannot export any of those objects.
\end{proof}
