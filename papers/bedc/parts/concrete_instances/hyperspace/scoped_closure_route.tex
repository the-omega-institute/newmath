\begin{theorem}[Hyperspace scoped closure route]
\label{thm:hyperspace-scoped-closure-route}
Every accepted $\HyperspaceUp$ consumer that reaches a compact-subset, directed Hausdorff, Vietoris, or completion-facing read is exhausted by the displayed carrier rows of \autoref{def:hyperspace-carrier}, the compact source route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the metric-distance witnesses of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the Hausdorff comparison route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hausdorff-metric-namecert}, the Vietoris handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vietoris-topology-namecert}, and the structural $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows already named by the packet.

Thus the scoped $\HyperspaceUp$ surface is the finite dependency lattice over compact-source admission, finite subset-net schedules, bidirectional Hausdorff windows, Vietoris hit--miss windows, completion-boundary handoffs, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. It exports no host powerset topology, selected compact subset, quotient compactum equality, open-cover compactness proof, selected nearest point, choice-selected subcover, or ambient complete-hyperspace object.
\end{theorem}
