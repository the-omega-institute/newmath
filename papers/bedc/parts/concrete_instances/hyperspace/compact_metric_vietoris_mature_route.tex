\begin{theorem}[Compact-metric Vietoris maturity route]
\label{thm:hyperspace-mature-compact-metric-vietoris-route}
Every mature $\HyperspaceUp$ Vietoris or Hausdorff-distance consumer first reads $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net data, $\HausdorffMetricUp$ distance rows, and $\KuratowskiHyperspaceUp$ closure rows before it exports compact-subset data. More precisely, the consumer route factors through the compact-metric dependency route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the Vietoris handoff of \autoref{thm:hyperspace-vietoris-handoff}, the compact-metric Vietoris unblock of \autoref{thm:hyperspace-compactmetric-vietoris-unblock}, the bridged interface route of \autoref{thm:hyperspace-bridged-interface-route}, the Hausdorff metric chapter of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hausdorff-metric-namecert}, and the Kuratowski hyperspace chapter of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-hyperspace-namecert}. The route exports no host powerset, quotient compactum, selected nearest point, open-cover compactness proof, or ambient complete hyperspace object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compact-metric dependency route. It forces every Vietoris or Hausdorff-distance consumer to expose the compact source, finite-net schedules, directed coverage ledgers, and tolerance readback before the local $\HyperspaceUp$ rows are inspected.

Next apply the Vietoris handoff and the compact-metric Vietoris unblock. These routes identify the hit--miss rows and finite compact-subset windows that may be consumed, while keeping the compact-source row visible through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}.

Finally apply the bridged interface route. The Hausdorff metric rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hausdorff-metric-namecert} and the Kuratowski closure rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-hyperspace-namecert} are read before any compact-subset export, so the mature consumer remains a finite dependency route rather than a host powerset or completion principle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hyperspace Vietoris mature envelope]
\label{thm:hyperspace-vietoris-mature-envelope}
The compact-metric finite-net row, Hausdorff metric row, Kuratowski hyperspace row, and Vietoris handoff together form the mature exported envelope for $\HyperspaceUp$. Every top-window consumer of compact-subset data must read those four rows after the local carrier and before any public compact-subset export, and the route exports no host powerset, quotient compactum, selected nearest point, open-cover compactness proof, or ambient complete hyperspace object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the compact-metric Vietoris maturity route of \autoref{thm:hyperspace-mature-compact-metric-vietoris-route}. It already forces Vietoris and Hausdorff-distance consumers through compact-metric finite-net data before any compact-subset export.

Read the two sibling rows next. The Hausdorff metric chapter supplies the finite distance ledger, and the Kuratowski hyperspace chapter supplies the closure-facing row consumed by the same compact-source route.

Finally use the Vietoris handoff of \autoref{thm:hyperspace-vietoris-handoff}. The handoff keeps hit--miss and compact-subset window data attached to the displayed finite-net route, so the four rows jointly form the mature envelope without adding any refused host object.
\end{proof}
