Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\TotallyBoundedReflectionUp$ packet records the finite precompact-reflection surface by which a uniform carrier exposes total-bounded schedules before any completion or compactness consumer reads it. It sits over the uniform source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the entourage basis of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-entourage-base-namecert}, the finite-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the stream observation rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite reflection carrier for later completion and compact-metric routes; it does not assert open-cover compactness, a selected Cauchy limit, quotient equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Totally bounded reflection carrier]
\label{def:totally-bounded-reflection-carrier}
A $\TotallyBoundedReflectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(U,E,T,S,Q,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ source row whose uniform carrier is being reflected.
\item $E$ is the $\EntourageBaseUp$ row naming the finite entourage requests available to downstream consumers.
\item $T$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite-net schedule for each displayed entourage request.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row recording the finite stream windows by which net centers and coverage witnesses are inspected.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same observation windows when rational radii or distances must be read.
\item $A$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the entourage, net, stream, and regular-rational rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $U,E,T,S,Q,A,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a precompact-reflection request through $U,E,T,S,Q,A$ and then through the support rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TotallyBoundedReflectionUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\EntourageBaseUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TotallyBoundedReflectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed tuple $U,E,T,S,Q,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, a selected completion point, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient complete uniform space data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Totally bounded reflection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:totally-bounded-reflection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\TotallyBoundedReflectionUp$ carrier
$$
R=(U,E,T,S,Q,A,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\TotallyBoundedReflectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of uniform-source admission, entourage-basis exposure, total-bounded finite-net scheduling, stream-window inspection, regular-rational readback, Real seal non-escape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every completion-facing or compact-metric consumer must read $U,E,T,S,Q,A,H,C,P,N$ before it treats the source as precompact.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:totally-bounded-reflection-carrier}. The rows $U,E,T$ expose the uniform source, the entourage basis, and the finite total-bounded schedules before any reflected route can be consumed.

The stream and regular-rational rows $S,Q$ provide the only observation and radius readback interface. The Real seal row $A$ is downstream of those rows, so a consumer cannot replace the displayed net schedule by quotient real equality or by a selected completion point.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no carrier coordinate stores open-cover compactness, a choice-selected subcover, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
