\begin{definition}[MoorePlane carrier]
\label{def:moore-plane-carrier}

$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the upper-half-plane point row, listing the inspected points with positive second coordinate.
\item $B$ is the boundary-line point row, listing the inspected real-axis points.
\item $T$ is the tangent-disc neighbourhood row, recording finite tangent-disc requests over $U$ and $B$.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ coordinate seal for all displayed coordinates and radii.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the upper-half-plane, boundary-line, tangent-disc, real-seal, replay, provenance, and local-name rows.

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MoorePlaneUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MoorePlaneUp}$ row.

\begin{theorem}[MoorePlane NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:moore-plane-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MoorePlaneUp$ carrier $M=(U,B,T,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MoorePlaneUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for upper-half-plane points, boundary-line points, tangent-disc neighbourhood requests, real-coordinate seals, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every neighbourhood consumer must read the topology surface through the displayed tangent-disc row $T$ and the real-coordinate seal $R$ before transport, replay, provenance, or local naming is consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The topology-facing read is therefore finite. The tangent-disc row records the neighbourhood request, while the real-coordinate seal records the inspected center and radius data before the structural rows are available.
