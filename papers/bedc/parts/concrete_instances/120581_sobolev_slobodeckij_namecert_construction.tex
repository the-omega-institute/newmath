\begin{definition}[Sobolev-Slobodeckij carrier]
\label{def:sobolev-slobodeckij-carrier}
A $\SobolevSlobodeckijUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(U,F,p,s,Q,G,R,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the source $\SobolevSpaceUp$ or $\FractionalSobolevUp$ row whose weak or fractional regularity window is being inspected.
\item $F$ records the finite function-value windows used by the seminorm read.
\item $p$ and $s$ are rational exponent and smoothness rows, with $0<s<1$ recorded as displayed order data.
\item $Q$ is the finite pair ledger naming the inspected point-pair or dyadic cell-pair requests.
\item $G$ is the Gagliardo-seminorm ledger, recording the displayed difference quotient budget over $Q,F,p,s$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the finite seminorm estimates.
\item $B$ is the handoff row to $\HolderSpaceUp$ or $\BesovSpaceUp$ consumers when their scale boundary is read.
\item $H,C,P,N$ record componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert_{\SobolevSlobodeckijUp}$ evidence.

The classifier compares only $U,F,p,s,Q,G,R,B,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient function equality, almost-everywhere representatives, selected completions, unrestricted integration theory, or ambient Banach-space completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Sobolev-Slobodeckij NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sobolev-slobodeckij-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sobolev-slobodeckij-carrier}. The analytic rows are precisely $U,F,p,s,Q,G,R,B$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The source regularity is read from $U$, while the finite windows and pair requests are read from $F$ and $Q$. The exponent rows $p,s$ and the ledger $G$ record the Gagliardo-seminorm budget over exactly those displayed requests.

The readback row $R$ transports the finite estimates to regular rational data, and the handoff row $B$ exposes only the Holder or Besov boundary named by the sibling chapters. Since every remaining operation is componentwise transport, replay, provenance, or naming, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.

\begin{proof}

The ledger $G$ is indexed by the displayed pair requests in $Q$ and by the finite function windows $F$. Thus a seminorm read cannot replace those rows by an ambient integral domain, an almost-everywhere representative, or an unrestricted quotient function.
