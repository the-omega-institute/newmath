\begin{theorem}[Fourier-Plancherel NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:fourier-plancherel-namecert-obligations}

These obligations do not include inversion, convergence, quotient function equality, selected representatives, or ambient Hilbert-space completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:fourier-plancherel-carrier}. The definition lists exactly the finite rows $T,A,E,J,I,Q,R,S,H,C,V,N$ and assigns the transform, Parseval, energy, observation, pairing, integral, readback, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming roles.

The analytic rows are read only through their sibling certificates. The transform surface comes from $\FourierTransformUp$, the finite identity from $\ParsevalUp$, the energy ledger from $\FiniteFourierEnergyUp$, and the finite observation source from $\FourierUp$.

The scalar comparison remains sealed. The rows $I,Q,R,S$ carry inner-product, integral, regular-rational, and Real-seal data, while $H,C,V,N$ preserve and name the same route. Since no additional coordinate is present, the excluded inversion, convergence, quotient, representative, and ambient-completeness readings cannot be projected.

\begin{proof}
