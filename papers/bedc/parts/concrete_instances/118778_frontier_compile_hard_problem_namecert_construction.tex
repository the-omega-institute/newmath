\begin{definition}[FrontierCompileHardProblem carrier]
\label{def:frontier-compile-hard-problem-carrier}
A $\FrontierCompileHardProblemUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(S,H,L,K,O,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the frontier-source row, naming the visible problem family and the finite syntax or computation window from which the frontier is read.
\item $H$ is the hard-problem classifier row. It records the exact blocked problem surface and the local reason it is not discharged by the current compiled route.
\item $L$ is the layered-relation row, separating source relation, compiled relation, witness relation, and refusal relation before any cook-style read is attempted.
\item $K$ is the cook-compile route row. It records the displayed reduction schedule from the source frontier to the compiled check surface.
\item $O$ is the obstruction ledger. It records which decision, normalization, subject-reduction, confluence, or computability row is still exposed as a boundary rather than as a solved theorem.
\item $T$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,H,L,K,O,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay from a frontier request through the layered relation, cook-compile route, and obstruction ledger.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FrontierCompileHardProblemUp$, $\MetaCICDecidabilityWitnessUp$, $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$, $\ComputableUp$, $\LambdaCalcUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FrontierCompileHardProblemUp}$ row.

\begin{theorem}[FrontierCompileHardProblem NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:frontier-compile-hard-problem-namecert-obligations}

A consumer that changes any exported frontier read must change one of these displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection of \autoref{def:frontier-compile-hard-problem-carrier}. The frontier source is $S$, the hard-problem classifier is $H$, the layered-relation surface is $L$, and the compile route is $K$.

The obstruction ledger is the row $O$. It records the remaining boundary as a finite row, so the packet does not silently turn an exposed problem into a solved theorem.

Transport and replay are exactly $T$ and $C$, while provenance and local naming are $P$ and $N$. Since the carrier contains no oracle, global normalization, global confluence, subject-reduction discharge, or hidden compiler theorem, these rows exhaust the obligation surface.

\begin{theorem}[FrontierCompileHardProblem consumer boundary]
\label{thm:frontier-compile-hard-problem-consumer-boundary}

$$

$$
The consumer may inspect the source frontier, the hard-problem classifier, the layered relation, and the compile route, but it cannot export a solved theorem unless the obstruction ledger itself displays the required discharge.

The consumer then reads $L$ and $K$. The layered-relation row separates the source, compiled, witness, and refusal relations; the cook-compile row records only the displayed reduction schedule.

If the obstruction ledger does not contain a discharge row, the carrier has no other place from which a solved theorem, oracle decision procedure, global normalization theorem, confluence theorem, subject-reduction theorem, or hidden compiler theorem could be recovered.
\end{proof}
