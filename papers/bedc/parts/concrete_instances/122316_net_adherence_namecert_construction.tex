Following Kelley and Bourbaki on nets and filters, the $\NetAdherenceUp$ packet records the finite boundary at which a displayed net, a generated filterbase, and neighbourhood requests meet an adherence or cluster-point test. It sits downstream of the convergence interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-netconvergence-namecert}, the Cauchy-net window discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchynet-namecert}, the filterbase carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-filterbase-namecert}, the neighbourhood language of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, and the completion-facing seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}. The packet names finite hit evidence; it is not a selected subnet, a choice principle, a quotient completion, or ambient real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Net adherence carrier]
\label{def:net-adherence-carrier}
A $\NetAdherenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(D,N,F,U,A,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the directed-index row, naming the finite index window and the tail requests available to the net-side reader.
\item $N$ is the net-sample row, recording the inspected values of the net on the displayed directed window.
\item $F$ is the $\FilterBaseUp$ row generated from the displayed tail family.
\item $U$ is the neighbourhood-request row, read through the local $\TopologyUp$ surface and only at the displayed candidate boundary.
\item $A$ is the adherence-hit row, recording the finite evidence that a tail or filterbase member meets the requested neighbourhood.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for directed indices, samples, filterbase rows, neighbourhood requests, hits, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for any consumer that asks for the same hit evidence.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NetAdherenceUp$, $\NetConvergenceUp$, $\CauchyNetUp$, $\FilterBaseUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\NetAdherenceUp}$ row, whose source, pattern, classifier, stability, and ledger fields are read from $D,N,F,U,A,H,C,P$.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a selected subnet, cofinal choice over the directed set, quotient equality of filters, a completed ambient point, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Net adherence FilterBase obligations]
\label{thm:net-adherence-filterbase-obligations}
For every accepted $\NetAdherenceUp$ carrier $A=(D,N,F,U,A,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\NetAdherenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the directed-index row, net-sample row, generated filterbase row, neighbourhood-request row, adherence-hit row, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row;
\item directed-tail discipline, requiring every hit read to expose the finite tail request in $D$ before it inspects a sample in $N$;
\item filterbase generation, requiring the $\FilterBaseUp$ row $F$ to be read from the same displayed tail family and not from a host filter equality;
\item neighbourhood discipline, requiring the request row $U$ to be interpreted through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert};
\item hit stability, requiring the adherence-hit row to transport along the componentwise $\hsame$ row $H$ and replay through $C$;
\item provenance and local naming through $P$ and $L$.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation for selected subnet construction, choice over tails, quotient completion, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness is stored in this packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:net-adherence-carrier}. The source-side rows are $D,N,F,U,A$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,L$.

The directed-tail evidence is displayed first. A hit read has to name the finite index window in $D$ and the inspected net samples in $N$ before any filterbase or neighbourhood row can be consumed.

The filterbase and topology rows then fix the analytic boundary. The row $F$ is generated from the same displayed tails, while $U$ records only the finite neighbourhood requests supplied by the topology-side surface. Thus the adherence hit is a finite meet between a displayed tail family and a displayed neighbourhood request.

Finally $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no coordinate stores a selected subnet, a choice operator, quotient filter equality, a completed ambient point, or real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local $\NameCert_{\NetAdherenceUp}$ obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Net adherence Cauchy-net cluster route]
\label{thm:net-adherence-cauchy-net-cluster-route}
Every completion-facing or cluster-point-facing consumer of an accepted $\NetAdherenceUp$ carrier factors through
$$
D,N \longmapsto F,U \longmapsto A \longmapsto H,C,P,L .
$$
The route may be read after the $\NetConvergenceUp$ and $\CauchyNetUp$ rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-netconvergence-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchynet-namecert}, may hand its filterbase row to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-filterbase-namecert}, and may be consumed by completion arguments in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}. It exports finite adherence evidence only; it does not select a subnet, choose a cluster point, identify quotient filters, or import ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a completion-facing request. The carrier first exposes the directed-index and sample rows $D,N$, so the request sees a finite net window rather than a host net predicate.

The next boundary is the filterbase and neighbourhood interface. The generated row $F$ gives the filterbase side, and $U$ records the topology-side neighbourhood request. Their common display is exactly the finite surface needed by adherence and cluster arguments.

The hit row $A$ is then read as evidence that the displayed tail or filterbase member meets the displayed neighbourhood request. The structural rows $H,C,P,L$ preserve that evidence under transport, replay it for downstream consumers, record provenance, and bind the local name.

Thus a Cauchy-net or completion consumer receives only the finite route through $D,N,F,U,A,H,C,P,L$. The packet cannot add subnet selection, cluster-point choice, quotient-filter equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness because none of those data appear among its rows.
\end{proof}
