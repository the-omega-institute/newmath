\begin{theorem}[Complete metric limit uniqueness separated route]
\label{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated-route}
When a $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ packet reads uniqueness through the regular Cauchy Real route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-regular-route}, separatedness and zero-distance reflection force a unique classifier-compatible terminal seal. The consumer must pass through the shared Cauchy source, the two complete-metric limit rows, the $\MetricUp$ distance witness, the $\RealUp$ zero-distance row, the $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ classifier, the $\RegularCauchyUniquenessUp$ tolerance route, the $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ readback rows, and the structural $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ rows before uniqueness is exported.

The route adds no host equality of metric points, selected representatives, quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or separate completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the regular-Cauchy Real route \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-regular-route}. It forces every regular-Cauchy consumer through $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\RegularCauchyUniquenessUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, and the structural rows before a uniqueness read is available.

Now apply zero-distance reflection and separatedness, \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-zero-distance-reflection} and \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separatedness}. These routes bind the two complete-metric limit rows to one Cauchy source, then require the metric distance witness and Real zero-distance row before the separated classifier can export uniqueness.

Finally use the separated reflection route \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated-reflection-route}. It places the separated classifier after the displayed distance and zero-distance rows and before the downstream handoff. Hence the terminal seal is classifier-compatible and unique inside the packet, while host equality, selected representatives, quotient equality, selected limits, choice, ambient Real completeness, and separate completeness data remain off-surface.
\end{proof}
