\begin{theorem}[Complete metric limit uniqueness public handoff route]
\label{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-public-handoff-route}
Every public uniqueness handoff exported by an accepted $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ carrier must first consume the complete-metric limit rows, the $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ source route, the $\RealUp$ zero-distance readback, and the $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ classifier. In carrier notation the handoff factors through
$$
A,L_{0},L_{1},M,Z,S,H,C,P,N
$$
with $L_{0},L_{1}$ supplied by the $\CompleteMetricUp$ limit surface, $A$ carrying the Cauchy-filter completion source route, $M,Z$ carrying the metric and Real zero-distance readback, and $S$ carrying the separated-reflection classifier before $H,C,P,N$ may replay or name the handoff. The route exports no selected limit, quotient completion point, countable-choice schedule, ambient completeness principle, or host equality of metric points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the sibling lattice route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-sibling-route}. It exposes the paired $\CompleteMetricUp$ limit rows over the Cauchy-filter completion source before any public uniqueness handoff is available.

Use the Cauchy-filter Real seal route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-cauchy-filter-real-seal}. This forces the metric witness and the $\RealUp$ zero-distance readback to remain downstream of the same source route.

Finally apply the separated-reflection route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated-reflection-route}. The classifier is read only after the zero-distance row is visible, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite route without adding selected limits, quotient points, countable choice, ambient completeness, or host equality.
\end{proof}
