\begin{theorem}[Complete metric limit uniqueness regular-sequence-space route]
\label{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-regular-sequence-space-route}
Any $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ read of equality of limits must expose the $\RegularSequenceSpaceUp$ schedule and readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-sequence-space-namecert}, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row, and the terminal $\RealUp$ zero-distance seal before separatedness is consumed. In carrier notation the public route remains
$$
A,L_{0},L_{1},M,Z,S,H,C,P,N,
$$
with the regular-sequence-space schedule feeding the regular rational readback, the readback feeding the Real seal $Z$, and the separated classifier $S$ read only after that seal is visible. The route exports no quotient equality of limits, selected endpoint, countable-choice schedule, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or host equality of metric points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-carrier}. The two limit rows $L_{0}$ and $L_{1}$ are admitted only over the displayed source row $A$, and the comparison is unavailable until the metric witness $M$ and zero-distance row $Z$ have been exposed.

Next apply the regular-sequence forward route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-regular-sequence-forward-route}. It forces a regular-sequence completion consumer through $\RegularSequenceSpaceUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ before public uniqueness can be read.

Finally use the separated reflection route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated-reflection-route}. The separated classifier $S$ is consumed only after the Real zero-distance seal $Z$, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same displayed packet. Therefore equality of limits is read through the regular-sequence-space schedule, regular rational readback, Real seal, and separatedness route, with no off-surface quotient, endpoint selector, choice schedule, ambient completeness, or host point equality.
\end{proof}
