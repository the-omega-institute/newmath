\begin{theorem}[Complete metric limit uniqueness CauchyLimitSeal route]
\label{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-cauchy-limitseal-route}
Every separated complete-metric limit-uniqueness read factors through the displayed $\CauchyLimitSealUp$ boundary before public uniqueness is available. More precisely, an accepted $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ consumer must expose the finite route
$$
\CauchyLimitSealUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RealUp,\ \MetricCompletionUp
$$
together with the carrier rows $A,L_{0},L_{1},M,Z,S,H,C,P,N$ before the separated classifier may publish uniqueness. The route exports no quotient stream equality, host selected limit, ambient completeness principle, or detached completion witness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier and sibling lattice routes of \autoref{def:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-carrier} and \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-sibling-route}. They place the two complete-metric limit rows over the same displayed Cauchy source and keep the metric-completion face inside the same finite packet.

Next attach the regular readback rows. The regular route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-regular-route} and the regular-sequence forward route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-regular-sequence-forward-route} force every zero-distance read through $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal before separatedness is inspected.

Now pass through the Cauchy-limit seal. The Cauchy-filter Real seal route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-cauchy-filter-real-seal} and the completion sibling route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-completion-sibling-route} keep the same source, readback, Real seal, and completion face attached to the packet before the separated classifier is visible.

Finally apply the separated reflection route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated-reflection-route}. Since $S$ is read only after $M$ and $Z$, and $Z$ is reached through the displayed window, readback, Real, CauchyLimitSeal, and MetricCompletion rows, the public uniqueness export cannot introduce quotient stream equality, a host selected limit, ambient completeness, or a second completion witness.
\end{proof}
