\label{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated-completion-limit-route}

\end{theorem}

Finally project the $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ export. Because the export is downstream of those sibling rows, it remains a finite dependency route through displayed completion evidence rather than host equality, quotient representatives, selected limits, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
