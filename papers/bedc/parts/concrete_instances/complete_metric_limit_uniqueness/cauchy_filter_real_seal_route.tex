Every uniqueness read from a $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ source must expose the Cauchy-filter completion row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfiltercompletion-namecert}, the paired $\CompleteMetricUp$ limit rows, the $\MetricUp$ distance witness, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ zero-distance seal, and the $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ classifier before any uniqueness handoff is public. $$

$$
and exports no quotient-filter equality, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or host equality of metric points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the Cauchy-filter comparison route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-cauchy-filter-comparison}. It ties the two complete-metric limit rows to the same $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ source row before any equality-like comparison is exposed.

Apply the common-tail contraction and zero-distance reflection routes, \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-common-tail-contraction} and \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-zero-distance-reflection}. They force the paired limit rows through one metric witness $M$ and one $\RegSeqRatUp$-backed $\RealUp$ zero-distance row $Z$.

Finally use separatedness and the separated zero-distance route, \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separatedness} and \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated-zero-distance-route}. The separated classifier is visible before the uniqueness handoff, while $H,C,P,N$ replay and name the same Cauchy-filter comparison route.
\end{proof}
