\begin{theorem}[Complete metric limit uniqueness RegSeqRat forward route]
\label{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-regseqrat-forward-route}
Any $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ read through the separated classifier must first preserve the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, the $\StreamNameUp$ window rows, the dyadic tolerance ledger, and the $\RealUp$ zero-distance seal already exposed by the child routes. Thus the public uniqueness read cannot consume the separated classifier before the regular-rational, stream-window, dyadic, and Real-seal route has been displayed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the common-tail and zero-distance theorems \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-common-tail-contraction} and \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-zero-distance-reflection}. They force the uniqueness comparison to pass through a shared tail read and then through the Real zero-distance seal.

Use the separated classifier route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated}. The separated read is public only after the carrier has exposed the limit comparison route, so it cannot bypass the regular-rational and stream-window data that feed the zero-distance test.

Finally apply the sibling route theorem \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-sibling-route}. The sibling lattice records the RegSeqRat, StreamName, dyadic tolerance, and Real-facing rows as outgoing dependencies of the same packet. Therefore every accepted uniqueness read preserves those child routes before it reaches the separated classifier.
\end{proof}
