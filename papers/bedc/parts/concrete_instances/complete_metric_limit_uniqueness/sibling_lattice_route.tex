\begin{theorem}[Complete metric limit uniqueness sibling route]
\label{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-sibling-route}
Every $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ read that exports a public uniqueness handoff must expose the sibling lattice route consisting of the $\CompleteMetricUp$ limit rows, the $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ source route, the $\MetricUp$ distance witness, the $\RealUp$ zero-distance row, and the $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ classifier before the handoff is public. In carrier notation this route factors through
$$
A,L_{0},L_{1},M,Z,S,H,C,P,N
$$
with $A$ carrying the Cauchy-filter completion source data, $L_{0},L_{1}$ carrying the complete-metric limit rows, $M$ carrying the metric comparison, $Z$ carrying the Real zero-distance read, and $S$ carrying the separated-reflection classifier. The route exports no selected limit, quotient representative equality, countable-choice schedule, ambient completeness principle, or host point equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier obligations of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-namecert-obligations}. They expose the Cauchy-source row $A$, the paired complete-metric limit rows $L_{0},L_{1}$, the metric distance witness $M$, the Real zero-distance row $Z$, and the separated classifier $S$ before any local naming row can publish uniqueness.

Apply \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated-reflection-route}. The separated-reflection classifier is consumed only after the metric witness and zero-distance read are visible, so the public handoff must pass through the same sibling route.

The support rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name this displayed route. They add transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert data, but they do not add selected limits, quotient representative equality, countable choice, ambient completeness, or host point equality.
\end{proof}
