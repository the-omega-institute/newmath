Following Bishop and Bridges on separated complete metric spaces, the $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ packet records the finite route by which two complete-metric limit rows for one displayed Cauchy source are compared through distance zero and then through the separated metric classifier. It sits over the complete-metric service of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the separated-limit uniqueness boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separated-limit-uniqueness-namecert}. The packet names limit uniqueness only as displayed finite NameCert data; it does not identify quotient representatives, choose a limit, invoke countable choice, import ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or export host point equality.

Before the two candidate limits are compared, the uniqueness packet reads a displayed $\CompleteMetricUp$ limit carrier from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert} and the completion route supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfiltercompletion-namecert}. The uniqueness packet consumes $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ as the source completion route and $\MetricCompletionUp$ as the optional metric-completion face before the separated classifier can export uniqueness. The optional $\MetricCompletionUp$ face is used only as a sibling completion route when the child row already names it; the uniqueness boundary itself still waits for the complete-metric limit rows, Cauchy-filter completion data, distance-zero readback, and separated classifier before exposing a comparison.

\begin{definition}[Complete metric limit uniqueness carrier]
\label{def:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-carrier}
A $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(A,L_{0},L_{1},M,Z,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the Cauchy-source row, exposing the finite request and tail data whose limit is being compared.
\item $L_{0}$ and $L_{1}$ are complete-metric limit rows for the same displayed source $A$.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ distance-witness row comparing the two displayed limit reads.
\item $Z$ is the $\RealUp$ zero-distance row reached from the distance witness through the regular rational readback surface.
\item $S$ is the separated-metric classifier row saying that the zero-distance read is the only admissible route from the two limit rows to a uniqueness handoff.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, limit rows, distance witness, zero-distance row, separated classifier, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the Cauchy-source request through the two limit rows, metric distance witness, Real zero-distance row, and separated classifier.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\SeparatedMetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,L_{0},L_{1},M,Z,S,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient representatives, selected limits, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or host equality of metric points.
\end{definition}
