\begin{theorem}[CompleteMetricLimitUniqueness downstream separated-completion link]
\label{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-downstream-link}
Every downstream separated-completion or metric-completion uniqueness consumer that cites $\CompleteMetricLimitUniquenessUp$ must cite it through the displayed route
$$
\CompleteMetricUp,\ \MetricUp,\ \RealUp,\ \SeparatedMetricReflectionUp .
$$
The consumer first reads the complete-metric limit rows supplied by $\CompleteMetricUp$, then the $\MetricUp$ distance witness, then the $\RealUp$ zero-distance row, and only then the $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ classifier row. The route exports no quotient-limit equality, selected endpoint, countable-choice schedule, ambient completeness principle, or host equality of metric points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-carrier}. Its limit rows are accepted only as complete-metric limit rows over the same displayed Cauchy source.

Apply the zero-distance reflection route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-zero-distance-reflection}. It places the $\MetricUp$ distance witness and the $\RealUp$ zero-distance read before any uniqueness handoff is available.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated} and the separated-reflection route of \autoref{thm:complete-metric-limit-uniqueness-separated-reflection-route}. They force the downstream uniqueness consumer to read the $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ classifier after the zero-distance row, so a separated-completion or metric-completion consumer can cite only the displayed finite route.
\end{proof}
