Following Kolmogorov's separation axiom and the Bourbaki--Kelley presentation of topological identification by indistinguishability, the $\KolmogorovReflectionUp$ packet records the $T_0$ separation-reflection route as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits beside the topological carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the separated metric reflector of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedmetricreflection-namecert}, and the metric-space carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-space-namecert}, while keeping quotient topology and host equality outside the certificate.

\begin{definition}[Kolmogorov reflection carrier]
\label{def:kolmogorov-reflection-carrier}
A $\KolmogorovReflectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(X,T,M,S,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source point-classifier row, carrying only the observable names whose topological indistinguishability is inspected.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ open-observation row, recording finite open-neighbourhood tests and their BEDC classifier readings.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ optional metric neighbourhood row used when a metric presentation supplies the same observation boundary.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ comparison row, available only as a neighbouring separated-reflection route and not as a quotient object.
\item $R$ is the Kolmogorov-reflected classifier row, identifying exactly the finite observation profile that distinguishes points up to $T_0$ separation.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, topological, metric, separated-reflection, reflected-classifier, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite observation query through $X,T,M,S,R,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KolmogorovReflectionUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KolmogorovReflectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exposes a $T_0$ reflection classifier only as finite observation data. It has no quotient-topology coordinate, no host equality coordinate, no sober-space reconstruction, no choice principle, and no completeness or separated-metric theorem beyond the displayed sibling route.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Kolmogorov reflection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kolmogorov-reflection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KolmogorovReflectionUp$ carrier $K=(X,T,M,S,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\KolmogorovReflectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier exposure, finite $\TopologyUp$ observation, optional $\MetricSpaceUp$ neighbourhood reading, neighbouring $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ comparison, reflected $T_0$ classifier output, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. A consumer may read the observation profile and the reflected classifier row, but it may not infer quotient topology or host equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The rows $X,T,M,S,R$ are the only point, topology, metric-neighbourhood, separated-reflection, and reflected-classifier rows available to a consumer.

The $T_0$ observation route is finite. A consumer reads open-neighbourhood evidence through $T$, may compare a metric presentation through $M$, and may inspect the neighbouring separated-reflection route through $S$ before reaching the classifier output $R$.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are supplied by $H,C,P,N$. Since quotient topology and host equality are not fields of the carrier, no NameCert obligation can export either object.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kolmogorov reflection separation route]
\label{thm:kolmogorov-reflection-separation-route}
Every accepted $\KolmogorovReflectionUp$ packet factors any $T_0$ separation read through the displayed route
$$
\TopologyUp\to\MetricSpaceUp\to\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp\to\BHist\to\hsame\to\Cont\to\Pkg\to\NameCert .
$$
The route may inspect finite open observations, metric neighbourhood rows, separated-reflection classifier comparison, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. It does not construct a quotient topology, identify host points, or claim a separated metric reflection as an ambient equality theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier projection of \autoref{def:kolmogorov-reflection-carrier}. The topological observation row $T$, metric neighbourhood row $M$, separated-reflection row $S$, and reflected classifier row $R$ are ordinary finite rows in one $\BHist$ packet.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:kolmogorov-reflection-namecert-obligations}. They force every local read to preserve the observation, comparison, classifier, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows before the reflected classifier is consumed.

The separated metric reflector is used only as a sibling dependency. Its row $S$ supplies comparison data, while the local packet still refuses quotient topology, host equality, and any ambient equality theorem. Thus the route remains a finite $T_0$ reflection certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kolmogorov reflection no-quotient boundary]
\label{thm:kolmogorov-reflection-no-quotient-boundary}
Let $K=(X,T,M,S,R,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\KolmogorovReflectionUp$ packet. Any consumer that reads the reflected classifier row $R$ must still display the original observation rows $X,T,M,S$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. Consequently the packet supplies no quotient topology, no host equality of points, no selected representatives, and no global separation theorem beyond the finite classifier route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted packet. The reflected classifier row $R$ is not independent of the observation rows; it is listed after $X,T,M,S$ in the carrier and is transported and replayed only through $H$ and $C$.

Use the separation route theorem. It keeps any $T_0$ read on the displayed route through topology, optional metric neighbourhoods, neighbouring separated reflection, history, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The provenance row $P$ names no quotient topology, host equality, selected representative, or global separation theorem. Therefore a consumer of $R$ receives only the finite classifier route and cannot repack the packet as any of those stronger objects.
\end{proof}
