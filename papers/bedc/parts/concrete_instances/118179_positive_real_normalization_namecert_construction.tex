\begin{theorem}[Positive-real normalization NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:positive-real-normalization-namecert-obligations}

$$

\item carrier admission for the positive-real source, apartness row, dyadic radius ledger, stream window, regular readback, normalized dyadic minorant, downstream scale row, transport, replay, provenance, and local name;
