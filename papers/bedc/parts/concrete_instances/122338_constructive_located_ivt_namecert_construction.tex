\begin{definition}[ConstructiveLocatedIVT carrier]
\label{def:constructive-located-ivt-carrier}
A $\ConstructiveLocatedIVTUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row, recording the finite continuity and modulus surface through which endpoint and midpoint image reads are requested.
\item $S$ is the sign-change ledger, recording only displayed endpoint and retained-window sign reads for the map row $F$ over the interval row $I$.
\item $B$ is the $\SignChangeBisectionUp$ row, read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sign-change-bisection-namecert}, which supplies the retained branch route from the displayed sign-change data.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ dyadic modulus and tolerance row for midpoint, endpoint, and image-window requests.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation-window row through which the bisection and image reads are inspected.

\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $I,F,S,B,D,W,R$ have been displayed.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveLocatedIVTUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
