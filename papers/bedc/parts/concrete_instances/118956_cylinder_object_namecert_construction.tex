\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CylinderObjectUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cylinder-object-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Quillen's model-category cylinder objects, the $\CylinderObjectUp$ packet records the finite BEDC row that factors the fold map from two displayed copies of one object through a weak-equivalence face. It sits after the model-category carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-modelcat-namecert}, the category dependency of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-category-namecert}, the cofibrant-replacement witness of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cofibrantreplacement-namecert}, and the homotopy ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homotopy-namecert}. The packet names one displayed cylinder for one object; it does not export functorial cylinders, mapping cylinders, homotopy-category quotients, derived mapping spaces, or a global replacement choice.

\begin{definition}[Cylinder object carrier]
\label{def:cylinder-object-carrier}
A $\CylinderObjectUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(M,X,I_0,I_1,C,W,F,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\ModelCatUp$ row supplying the category, cofibration, fibration, weak-equivalence, lifting, and factorization surfaces.
\item $X$ is the displayed object row over the $\CategoryUp$ surface of $M$.
\item $I_0$ and $I_1$ are the two endpoint inclusion rows from $X$ into the candidate cylinder row.
\item $C$ is the cylinder-object row through which the fold request on the two copies of $X$ factors.
\item $W$ is the weak-equivalence ledger marking the projection from $C$ back to the displayed object row.
\item $F$ is the finite factorization row read from the $\ModelCatUp$ factorization surface.
\item $H$ is the $\HomotopyUp$ ledger that records the homotopy-facing interpretation of the displayed weak-equivalence row.
\item $R$ is the $\CofibrantReplacementUp$ dependency row when the object is read through a displayed cofibrant witness.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CylinderObjectUp$, $\ModelCatUp$, $\CategoryUp$, $\CofibrantReplacementUp$, $\HomotopyUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CylinderObjectUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,X,I_0,I_1,C,W,F,H,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a functorial cylinder assignment, mapping-cylinder construction, host homotopy category, derived mapping space, quotient weak-equivalence calculus, or global replacement choice.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cylinder object NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cylinder-object-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CylinderObjectUp$ carrier $Z=(M,X,I_0,I_1,C,W,F,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CylinderObjectUp}$ surface consists exactly of five finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item endpoint admission, requiring $I_0$ and $I_1$ to be displayed as the two copies of $X$ entering the cylinder row $C$;
\item model-category factorization, requiring $F$ to read the factorization surface supplied by $M$ before $C$ can be consumed;
\item weak-equivalence projection, requiring $W$ to mark the projection from $C$ back to $X$ inside the weak-equivalence row of $M$;
\item homotopy and cofibrant-replacement compatibility, requiring $H$ and $R$ to be read only as the displayed homotopy and cofibrant-witness dependencies of the same packet;
\item ledger non-escape, requiring $P$ and $N$ to source and name only the displayed rows, with no functorial cylinder assignment, mapping-cylinder construction, homotopy-category quotient, derived mapping space, quotient weak-equivalence calculus, or global replacement choice.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cylinder-object-carrier}. The endpoint rows $I_0$ and $I_1$ are stored as displayed inputs to $C$, so a cylinder read starts from the two named copies of $X$ rather than from an ambient coproduct or host fold map.

Next read the model-category row $M$ and its factorization row $F$. These rows supply the finite factorization surface on which $C$ is admitted and prevent the certificate from importing an external weak-equivalence calculus.

The weak-equivalence ledger $W$ and homotopy ledger $H$ are consumed only after the cylinder row is visible. The cofibrant-replacement row $R$ is a dependency row for the displayed object witness, not a global replacement operator.

Finally inspect $P$ and $N$. They source and name the same finite packet, and none of the refused objects appears as a carrier coordinate. The five displayed obligation families therefore exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cylinder object factorization route]
\label{thm:cylinder-object-factorization-route}
Every downstream homotopy or cofibrant-replacement consumer citing an accepted $\CylinderObjectUp$ carrier must factor its read through
$$
M,X,I_0,I_1,C,W,F,H,R,P,N .
$$
The route exports one displayed cylinder object for one displayed object and does not export a functorial cylinder construction, mapping-cylinder construction, homotopy-category quotient, derived mapping space, quotient weak-equivalence calculus, or global replacement choice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the endpoint and cylinder rows of \autoref{def:cylinder-object-carrier}. A consumer reaches the cylinder row $C$ only after the object row $X$ and the two endpoint inclusions $I_0,I_1$ have been displayed.

Use the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:cylinder-object-namecert-obligations}. The model-category factorization row $F$ and weak-equivalence ledger $W$ bind the cylinder read to $M$, while the homotopy and cofibrant-replacement rows $H$ and $R$ keep the homotopy-facing and cofibrant-facing uses inside the same finite packet.

The provenance and local naming rows $P,N$ preserve that route for downstream consumers. Since the carrier lacks every refused coordinate, the consumer receives only the displayed cylinder route and cannot promote it to a functorial, quotient, derived, or global-choice construction.
\end{proof}
