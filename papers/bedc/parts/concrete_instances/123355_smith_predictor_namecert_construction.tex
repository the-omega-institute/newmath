Following Smith's predictor for delayed feedback systems and standard control-theory treatments, the $\SmithPredictorUp$ packet records the finite delay-compensation surface used by a controller that predicts through a displayed delay model before feeding back the plant row. It sits over \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-statespacemodel-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riccati-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lqr-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kalman-filter-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-controlobservability-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}. The chapter names only finite BEDC rows for the predictor packet; it does not import analytic stability, infinite-horizon optimality, stochastic convergence, or host delay differential equations.

\begin{definition}[Smith predictor carrier]
\label{def:smith-predictor-carrier}
A $\SmithPredictorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(P,D,C,K,E,H,R,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the $\StateSpaceModelUp$ plant row, exposing the finite state, input, output, transition, input-map, and observation-map data consumed by the predictor.
\item $D$ is the delay-model row, read as a finite ledger of delayed plant-output windows and matrix rows through $\MatrixUp$.
\item $C$ is the controller row, consuming the $\LQRUp$ and $\RiccatiUp$ control ledger when a displayed feedback law is available.
\item $K$ is the prediction row, reading the finite delayed-output estimate through $\KalmanFilterUp$ and the observable-output surface of $\ControlObservabilityUp$.
\item $E$ is the error-compensation ledger, recording the finite difference between delayed measured output and predicted output before the controller row is consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $P,D,C,K,E,R,Q,N$.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from plant and delay rows to prediction, compensation, and controller handoff.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SmithPredictorUp$, $\StateSpaceModelUp$, $\RiccatiUp$, $\LQRUp$, $\KalmanFilterUp$, $\ControlObservabilityUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SmithPredictorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for an analytic delay equation, an asymptotic closed-loop theorem, a stochastic convergence assertion, a selected infinite prediction horizon, or an external transfer-function identity.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Smith predictor NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:smith-predictor-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SmithPredictorUp$ carrier $S=(P,D,C,K,E,H,R,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SmithPredictorUp}$ surface consists exactly of plant admission through $P$, delay-model admission through $D$, controller admission through $C$, prediction admission through $K$, error-compensation exposure through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, replay through $R$, provenance through $Q$, and local naming through $N$. Any accepted predictor read must display these rows before treating delayed feedback compensation as available.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:smith-predictor-carrier}. The plant, delay, controller, prediction, and compensation rows are the only control-facing rows available to the packet.

The structural rows then preserve the same finite surface. Transport through $H$ aligns corresponding plant, delay, controller, prediction, compensation, replay, provenance, and naming rows; replay through $R$ records the displayed route from plant and delay data to prediction and compensation; provenance through $Q$ names the sibling certificates consumed by the packet.

Since no carrier coordinate stores an analytic delay equation, asymptotic theorem, stochastic convergence proof, infinite prediction horizon, or external transfer-function identity, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Smith predictor delay-compensation handoff]
\label{thm:smith-predictor-delay-compensation-handoff}
Every controller-facing read exported by an accepted $\SmithPredictorUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
P \longmapsto D \longmapsto K \longmapsto E \longmapsto C.
$$
The controller row $C$ may consume the compensation ledger only after the plant row, delay-model row, prediction row, and error-compensation row have been displayed and replayed through $H,R,Q,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:smith-predictor-namecert-obligations}. They require plant admission, delay-model admission, prediction admission, compensation exposure, and controller admission inside the same finite packet.

The handoff route is recorded by $R$. A prediction row $K$ is downstream of the plant and delay rows, and the compensation ledger $E$ is downstream of the delayed-output prediction. Only then can the controller row $C$ consume the compensated signal as displayed data.

Transport and provenance add no second route. The rows $H,Q,N$ preserve and name the same finite packet, while the carrier has no coordinate for host delay differential equations, analytic stability, infinite-horizon optimality, or stochastic convergence. Thus the controller-facing handoff is exactly the displayed route.
\end{proof}
