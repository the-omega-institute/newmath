\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\AsymptoticCompactnessUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-asymptotic-compactness-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Hale and Temam, the $\AsymptoticCompactnessUp$ packet records the dynamical compactness condition that long-time tails of a dissipative evolution admit compact finite-window capture. In BEDC form it consumes the dynamical-system certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dynsystem-namecert}, the metric certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the compactness certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, and the stream-window naming surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. The packet is a finite NameCert carrier for tail capture; it is not an ambient compactness theorem for the whole phase space and not a hidden choice principle for selecting convergent subsequences.

\begin{definition}[Asymptotic compactness packet carrier]
\label{def:asymptotic-compactness-packet-carrier}
An $\AsymptoticCompactnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(D,M,K,S,T,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DynSystemUp$ flow row whose long-time orbit tail is being read;
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ phase-distance row used to compare tail windows;
\item $K$ is the $\CompactUp$ candidate capture row for the selected finite tail window;
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule naming the finite sequence of requested tail times;
\item $T$ is the absorbing-tail row, recording that all sufficiently late windows selected by $S$ are routed into the compact candidate row $K$;
\item $W$ is the finite-window attractor ledger, storing the compact capture window, its metric tolerance rows, and the associated dynamical endpoints;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the dynamical, metric, compact, stream, tail, and window rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an orbit-tail request through $D,M,K,S,T,W,H$;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DynSystemUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AsymptoticCompactnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,M,K,S,T,W,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a compact whole phase space, a selected limit point, a host-level sequential compactness principle, or a global attractor object outside the displayed finite ledger.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Asymptotic compactness absorbing tail carrier]
\label{thm:asymptotic-compactness-absorbing-tail-carrier}
Every accepted $\AsymptoticCompactnessUp$ carrier exposes an absorbing-tail row $T$ before any compact read is consumed. Concretely, a downstream consumer may read the compact row $K$ only as a capture surface for tail windows selected by the stream schedule $S$ and produced by the dynamical row $D$ under the metric row $M$. Thus the carrier records finite tail capture rather than compactness of the entire phase carrier.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Asymptotic compactness finite-window attractor ledger]
\label{thm:asymptotic-compactness-finite-window-attractor-ledger}
For every accepted $\AsymptoticCompactnessUp$ carrier
$$
A=(D,M,K,S,T,W,H,C,P,N),
$$
the finite-window attractor ledger $W$ is exhausted by the compact capture row $K$, the stream-selected tail windows from $S$, the dynamical endpoints produced by $D$, the metric tolerance comparisons from $M$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. Agreement on those rows cannot be repacked as a selected limit, a full global attractor, a compact phase-space certificate, or a choice-backed subsequence selector.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Asymptotic compactness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:asymptotic-compactness-namecert-obligations}
The local $\NameCert_{\AsymptoticCompactnessUp}$ obligation surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $D,M,K,S,T,W,H,C,P,N$, classifier transport through the displayed $\hsame$ rows, continuation replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, absorbing-tail exposure through $T$, finite-window attractor ledger exposure through $W$, and local naming through $N$. No consumer-visible row claims a selected orbit limit, a compact ambient phase space, or a global attractor outside the finite BEDC packet.
\end{theorem}
