\begin{theorem}[CompactIntervalChoiceSequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-interval-choice-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-interval-choice-sequence-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactIntervalChoiceSequenceUp}$ surface is exhausted by five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier exposure for $K=(I,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$;
\item located interval admission through $I$ and its endpoint order;
\item dyadic tolerance and regular rational readback through $D,S,R$;
\item endpoint and enclosure exactness through $E$ and componentwise transport through $H$;
\item finite replay, provenance, and naming through $C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
Every accepted compact-interval choice-sequence consumer must read those rows before using the packet. No consumer may replace them by an arbitrary open-cover compactness theorem, a selected infinite branch, quotient stream equality, a hidden choice principle, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The rows $I,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$ are the complete local inventory, so the certificate cannot read an endpoint, tolerance, stream prefix, rational approximant, or enclosure fact outside that finite tuple.

The located interval admission is carried by $I$. The dyadic and rational data are then read through $D,S,R$, with $S$ exposing only a finite choice-sequence prefix and $R$ attaching regular rational readback to the observations already visible in that prefix.

Next inspect endpoint and enclosure exactness. The row $E$ binds every displayed sample to the located interval named by $I$ under the tolerance budget in $D$, and $H$ transports exactly those rows componentwise.

Finally $C,P,N$ replay, source, and name the same finite data. Since no carrier coordinate supplies an open cover, infinite branch selector, quotient stream witness, or completeness theorem for $\RealUp$, the five obligation rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
