\begin{theorem}[FiniteLimitSeal regular Cauchy gluing route]
\label{thm:finite-limit-seal-regular-cauchy-gluing-route}
Every $\FiniteLimitSealUp$ consumer that reads a glued regular-Cauchy limit must expose the finite-limit seal rows $M,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, and the sibling $\RegularCauchyGluingUp$ route before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal is public. The route exports no selected representatives, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the finite-limit seal carrier of \autoref{def:finite-limit-seal-carrier}. Its public limit-facing route is the displayed sequence from the modulus schedule through stream windows, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance, and terminal Real seal, together with transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Apply the real-limit dependency route and the Cauchy-filter forward route, \autoref{thm:finite-limit-seal-real-dependency-route} and \autoref{thm:finite-limit-seal-cauchy-filter-forward-route}. These require every completion-facing seal read to pass through the same $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows before the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteLimitSealUp}$ row is public.

For a glued regular-Cauchy limit, cite the substantive sibling dependency \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-gluing-namecert}. The gluing packet supplies the compatible-window route, but the finite-limit seal still exposes its own $M,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$ rows before the terminal seal can be consumed.

The carrier and the cited sibling route contain no coordinate for selected representatives, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness. Thus a glued regular-Cauchy limit is read only through the finite seal route and the $\RegularCauchyGluingUp$ dependency.
\end{proof}
