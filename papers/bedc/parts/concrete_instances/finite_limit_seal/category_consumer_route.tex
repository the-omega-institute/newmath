\begin{theorem}[Finite limit seal categorical consumer route]
\label{thm:finite-limit-seal-category-consumer-route}
Every categorical or completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\FiniteLimitSealUp$ carrier reads the seal through the finite-window route
$$
M \longmapsto W \longmapsto R \longmapsto D \longmapsto E
$$
of \autoref{thm:finite-limit-seal-route-nonescape}, then through downstream completion siblings such as \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-limit-seal-envelope-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-limit-classifier-lock-namecert} before it can use the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The consumer receives a finite categorical handoff over the displayed StreamName, RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, Real, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert rows; it receives no selected limits, quotient stream equality, countable choice, host real equality, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-limit-seal-carrier}. Its only completion-facing route places the modulus schedule, finite stream windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, and terminal Real seal in the ordered rows $M,W,R,D,E$.

Apply \autoref{thm:finite-limit-seal-route-nonescape}. This prevents a categorical consumer from bypassing the finite-window rows or reading a selected limit directly from the seal.

Use \autoref{thm:finite-limit-seal-bounded-regular-cauchy-route} to align bounded regular Cauchy data with the same route. The bounded threshold and late-window rows are repacked as $M,W,R,D$ before the Real seal $E$ is visible.

Finally pass through the downstream envelope and classifier-lock chapters. \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-limit-seal-envelope-namecert} records the finite envelope consumed by Real and Completion routes, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-limit-classifier-lock-namecert} keeps the diagonal limit classifier on a single displayed seal route. Since none of these rows adds a selected limit, quotient stream equality, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, trichotomy branch, or ambient completeness theorem, the categorical consumer remains on the finite-window lattice.
\end{proof}
