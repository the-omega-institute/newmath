\begin{definition}[Frechet--Urysohn space carrier]\label{def:frechet-urysohn-space-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $S$ is the $\SequentialSpaceUp$ source row, exposing the sequence-indexed convergence interface over the same topology surface.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation-window row for the sequence names used by a closure request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ convergence-readback row for numeric or completion-facing sequence observations.

\begin{theorem}[Frechet--Urysohn space NameCert obligations]\label{thm:frechet-urysohn-space-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}

The topology row supplies the open and closed vocabulary; the sequential row supplies the sequence-indexed convergence interface; the stream and regular-rational rows expose finite windows and convergence readback before the terminal Real seal can be consumed.

Finally replay $A,H,C,P,N$. The admission row binds topological closure reads to displayed sequence windows, while transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming preserve the same route.
