\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniformCompletionIdempotenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-completion-idempotence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges and Kelley on uniform-space completion, the $\UniformCompletionIdempotenceUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which a completed uniform route is read back through the same uniform-completion boundary. It sits after the uniform-completion carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformcompletion-namecert}, the general completion-idempotence row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-idempotence-namecert}, the metric fixed-point row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-completion-idempotence-namecert}, the separated-completion idempotence boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separated-completion-idempotence-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is an idempotence certificate for a displayed uniform completion route; it is not quotient completion equality, a selected representative, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Uniform completion idempotence carrier]
\label{def:uniform-completion-idempotence-carrier}
A $\UniformCompletionIdempotenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(U,C,M,S,W,R,D,E,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the first $\UniformCompletionUp$ boundary row whose completed route is being inspected.
\item $C$ is the $\CompletionIdempotenceUp$ row recording the repeated completion request as a finite idempotence surface.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionIdempotenceUp$ comparison row available when a metric presentation of the same uniform route is consumed.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$ row recording the separated classifier boundary for the completed object.
\item $W$ is the shared $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which completion-facing reads are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row exposed before any real-facing seal is consumed.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger controlling the finite comparison budget of the repeated read.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $U,C,M,S,W,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the uniform boundary, idempotence rows, finite window, regular readback, dyadic ledger, real seal, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same ordered route.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformCompletionIdempotenceUp$, $\UniformCompletionUp$, $\CompletionIdempotenceUp$, $\MetricCompletionIdempotenceUp$, $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformCompletionIdempotenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality of completed points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform completion idempotence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-completion-idempotence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformCompletionIdempotenceUp$ carrier
$$
I=(U,C,M,S,W,R,D,E,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UniformCompletionIdempotenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the uniform-completion boundary $U$, repeated-request exposure through $C$, metric-presentation compatibility through $M$, separated-boundary compatibility through $S$, shared finite-window exposure through $W$, regular rational readback through $R$, dyadic tolerance exposure through $D$, real-seal exposure through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $K$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-completion-idempotence-carrier}. The first four rows expose the uniform-completion boundary and the three idempotence witnesses that can be consumed by completion, metric, and separated presentations.

The finite analytic rows then force a single readback route. The window $W$, regular rational row $R$, and dyadic ledger $D$ are displayed before the terminal seal $E$ is available, so the repeated completion read cannot jump directly to a completed point.

Finally read $H,K,P,N$ as structural rows for the same packet. They transport, replay, source, and locally name only the displayed route, and no coordinate remains from which quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness could be exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform completion idempotence unit handoff]
\label{thm:uniform-completion-idempotence-unit-handoff}
Every unit-facing consumer of an accepted $\UniformCompletionIdempotenceUp$ carrier reads the second uniform-completion request through
$$
U,C,M,S,W,R,D,E,H,K,P,N
$$
in that order. The consumer may use the $\UniformCompletionUp$ boundary only together with the completion-idempotence, metric-idempotence, and separated-idempotence rows already displayed by the packet. It may not substitute quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality of completed points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness for that route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:uniform-completion-idempotence-namecert-obligations}. They place the uniform-completion boundary $U$ before the idempotence rows $C,M,S$ and before the finite readback rows $W,R,D,E$.

Use the sibling idempotence surfaces in order. The completion row $C$ supplies the repeated-request boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-idempotence-namecert}; the metric row $M$ supplies the metric fixed-point comparison of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-completion-idempotence-namecert}; and the separated row $S$ supplies the separated classifier boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separated-completion-idempotence-namecert}.

The regular and real-facing part of the handoff is still finite. The shared window comes from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular readback from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the tolerance ledger from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal seal from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}.

The structural rows $H,K,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name that same ordered route. Hence a unit-facing consumer receives a displayed uniform-completion idempotence handoff rather than a quotient equality, representative selector, choice schedule, host equality, or detached completeness principle.
\end{proof}
