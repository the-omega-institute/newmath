\begin{theorem}[Stream regular completeness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:stream-regular-completeness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\StreamRegularCompletenessUp$ carrier $C=(S,R,D,E,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\StreamRegularCompletenessUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item window coverage, requiring each completion request to expose the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observations in $S$ before any rational readback is consumed;
\item regularity coverage, requiring $R$ to exhibit the regular rational Cauchy route over the same windows;
\item dyadic tolerance coverage, requiring $D$ to account for the precision and refinement cells used by the regular readback;
\item Real-seal nonescape, requiring every $\RealUp$ consumer to read $E$ only after $S,R,D$ and the structural rows $H,T,P,N$ have been displayed.
\end{enumerate}
No local obligation supplies quotient representatives, selected limits, countable-choice schedules, trichotomy, host equality, or ambient completeness.

\begin{proof}

The first three substantive rows expose the constructive completion data. $S$ supplies finite stream windows, $R$ reads them as regular rational Cauchy data, and $D$ records the dyadic budgets used to compare those readings.

The terminal row $E$ is downstream of that route. Transport through $H$, replay through $T$, provenance through $P$, and naming through $N$ preserve the same displayed packet and do not add a quotient, selected representative, choice schedule, trichotomy, host equality, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{proof}
