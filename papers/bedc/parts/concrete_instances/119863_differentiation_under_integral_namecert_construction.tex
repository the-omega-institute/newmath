It sits beside the Riemann-integral route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannintegral-namecert}, the uniform convergence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-convergence-namecert}, the completion-modulus route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemann-integral-completion-modulus-namecert}, the finite stream interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the finite window in which a parameter derivative and an integral readback share the same displayed modulus ledger before a Real-facing consumer may compare the two rows.

\begin{definition}[Differentiation under integral carrier]
\label{def:differentiation-under-integral-carrier}
A $\DifferentiationUnderIntegralUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(A,I,R,\Delta,M,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the parameter row, naming the finite parameter window and the parameter probe at which the derivative is requested.
\item $I$ is the interval row, naming the compact interval over which the Riemann integral is read.
\item $R$ is the $\RiemannIntegralUp$ row, containing the finite tagged-partition and integral-readback data.
\item $\Delta$ is the derivative row, naming the finite difference-window data for the parameter derivative of the displayed integrand.
\item $M$ is the shared modulus row governing both the integral approximation and the derivative-window comparison.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row listing the inspected parameter, interval, partition, and tolerance windows.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to $R,\Delta,M,S$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the readback row $Q$ and the shared modulus row $M$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for parameter, interval, Riemann-integral, derivative, shared-modulus, stream-window, readback, real-seal, replay, provenance, and local-name rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite parameter-tolerance request through $A,I,R,\Delta,M,S,Q,E$.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DifferentiationUnderIntegralUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,I,R,\Delta,M,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for measure-theoretic dominated convergence, host function extensionality, quotient equality of functions or streams, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or a detached derivative theorem outside the finite window.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Differentiation under integral NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:differentiation-under-integral-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DifferentiationUnderIntegralUp$ carrier $D=(A,I,R,\Delta,M,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DifferentiationUnderIntegralUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the parameter, interval, Riemann-integral, derivative, shared-modulus, stream-window, regular-readback, real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; shared-modulus exactness requiring $R$ and $\Delta$ to consume the same $M$ row; finite-window exactness through $S$; readback exactness through $Q$; and non-escape from measure-theoretic dominated convergence, host extensionality, quotient equality, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, and detached derivative data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The only analytic rows visible to the classifier are the parameter row $A$, interval row $I$, Riemann-integral row $R$, derivative row $\Delta$, shared-modulus row $M$, stream-window row $S$, readback row $Q$, and real-seal row $E$.

The shared-modulus clause is read before the derivative and integral rows can be compared. The packet therefore treats the requested Leibniz-style equality as a finite window route through $R,\Delta,M,S,Q,E$, not as an ambient theorem about all functions.

Rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for dominated convergence, host extensionality, quotient equality, countable choice, ambient completeness, or detached derivative data, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
