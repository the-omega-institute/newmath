\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KelleyficationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-kelleyfication-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Steenrod compactly generated topology, the $\KelleyficationUp$ packet records the compactly generated reflection of a topology as finite BEDC data. It sits after the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the core-compact neighbourhood route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-core-compact-namecert}, and the compact-open topology ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-open-topology-namecert}. The packet names only the compact-window tests through which a topology is reflected; it does not import a host category of spaces, arbitrary open-cover compactness, quotient-open equality, or choice-selected compact witnesses.

\begin{definition}[Kelleyfication carrier]
\label{def:kelleyfication-carrier}
A $\KelleyficationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(T,C,O,W,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row whose carrier, open-incidence rows, and classifier are already admitted as finite topology data.
\item $C$ is the $\CoreCompactUp$ row, or an equivalent compactly generated test row, recording which compact-window probes may be used to inspect the source topology.
\item $O$ is the $\CompactOpenTopologyUp$ finite compact-window ledger. It lists the compact-domain windows and open-target tests used to decide reflected openness.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ inspection window, recording the finite observations through which a consumer reads the compact-window ledger.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source topology, compact-generation tests, compact-open ledger, inspection window, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a reflected-open request through $T,C,O,W,H$ before any downstream topology or function-space consumer reads the result.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KelleyficationUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\CoreCompactUp$, $\CompactOpenTopologyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KelleyficationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $T,C,O,W,H,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary host opens, quotient-open equality, an unrestricted category of compactly generated spaces, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or selected compact tests outside the displayed ledger.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Kelleyfication NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kelleyfication-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KelleyficationUp$ carrier $K=(T,C,O,W,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\KelleyficationUp}$ surface consists exactly of source-topology exposure through $T$; compactly generated test exposure through $C$; compact-open finite-window exposure through $O$; finite inspection through $W$; componentwise transport through $H$; displayed replay through $R$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$. Every reflected-open read must first pass through the topology source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the core-compact route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-core-compact-namecert}, and the compact-open topology ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-open-topology-namecert}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier in \autoref{def:kelleyfication-carrier}. The displayed rows are exactly $T,C,O,W,H,R,P,N$, so any accepted read begins with the source topology, the compact-generation test row, the compact-window ledger, and the finite inspection window.

The compactly generated reflection is read through sibling rows rather than through a host topology predicate. The topology source supplies the original open-incidence data, the core-compact row supplies compact-neighbourhood tests, and the compact-open topology row supplies the finite compact-window ledger.

Transport and replay preserve those rows componentwise. Provenance and local naming then bind the same packet as a $\KelleyficationUp$ certificate. Since no quotient-open equality, arbitrary open-cover compactness, selected compact-test family, or ambient category row occurs in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
