\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ParticularPointTopologyUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-particular-point-topology-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Alexandrov and standard finite-topology examples, the $\ParticularPointTopologyUp$ packet records the included-point topology on a finite displayed carrier as BEDC NameCert data. It sits after the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the two-point truth classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert}, and the separation boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tzero-reflection-namecert}. The packet names a distinguished point, the open-set classifier that accepts exactly the empty set and the displayed subsets containing that point, and the finite boundary where specialization may fail.

\begin{definition}[Particular point topology carrier]
\label{def:particular-point-topology-carrier}
A $\ParticularPointTopologyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(p,O,S,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $p$ is the distinguished point row on the finite carrier.
\item $O$ is the open classifier row, accepting the empty displayed subset and every displayed subset whose membership row contains $p$.
\item $S$ is the Sierpinski truth handoff row that reads membership in a particular-point open as a two-point truth observation.
\item $T$ is the $T_0$-boundary row, recording which displayed point pairs are separated by the particular-point classifier and which pairs remain specialization-indistinguishable.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the distinguished point, open classifier, Sierpinski handoff, separation boundary, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through topology, Sierpinski truth, and $T_0$-reflection consumers.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ParticularPointTopologyUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, $\TZeroReflectionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ParticularPointTopologyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $p,O,S,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host powersets, arbitrary union closure, quotient equality of opens, selected specialization classes, or completed separation reflection.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Particular point topology NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:particular-point-topology-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ParticularPointTopologyUp$ carrier
$$
X=(p,O,S,T,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ParticularPointTopologyUp}$ surface consists exactly of distinguished-point admission, open-classifier exposure through $O$, Sierpinski truth handoff through $S$, $T_0$-boundary exposure through $T$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:particular-point-topology-carrier}. The distinguished point row, open classifier row, Sierpinski handoff, separation boundary, transport, replay, provenance, and local name are all visible coordinates of one finite packet.

The topology-facing obligation reads $O$ only as a displayed classifier over finite subsets. The accepted open rows are the empty subset row and the rows that contain $p$, so no host powerset or arbitrary-union theorem is imported.

The truth and separation obligations are then read through $S$ and $T$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite rows and do not add quotient equality of opens or completed reflection data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Particular point topology Sierpinski handoff]
\label{thm:particular-point-topology-sierpinski-handoff}
Every accepted $\ParticularPointTopologyUp$ open-membership read factors through the Sierpinski truth row $S$ before a topology consumer may use it as a classifier. The handoff reads the sibling $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert} and returns only displayed truth data for the finite open row $O$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the open classifier row $O$. A displayed subset is accepted as open either because it is the empty row or because its membership row contains the distinguished point $p$.

The row $S$ repacks that membership test as a Sierpinski truth observation. Since $S$ is a carrier row, the handoff is finite and visible rather than a host characteristic-function theorem.

Transport and replay through $H,C$ keep the read attached to the same finite classifier. Thus topology consumers receive only the displayed truth handoff and cannot infer host powerset extensionality or arbitrary open-set equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Particular point topology T0 boundary]
\label{thm:particular-point-topology-tzero-boundary}
Every $T_0$-facing read of an accepted $\ParticularPointTopologyUp$ packet must expose the boundary row $T$ before it reaches \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tzero-reflection-namecert}. The row records the finite point pairs separated by opens containing $p$ and the pairs that remain indistinguishable by the displayed classifier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the rows $p,O,T$ from the carrier. Separation information is not stored as a global theorem about all opens; it is the displayed finite ledger $T$ attached to the particular-point classifier.

The topology and Sierpinski rows supply the inspected opens and truth observations. The $T_0$-boundary row records which point pairs those displayed observations separate.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only that ledger. A $T_0$ consumer may use the boundary row as input to reflection, but the packet does not export completed reflection, quotient specialization classes, or ambient topological separation.
\end{proof}
