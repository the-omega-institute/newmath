\begin{definition}[MetricSeparatedReflectorUnit carrier]
\label{def:metric-separated-reflector-unit-carrier}
A $\MetricSeparatedReflectorUnitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(M,Z,S,U,F,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the metric or pseudometric source row, exposing point admission, distance readback, symmetry, triangle, and zero-distance inspection.
\item $Z$ is the zero-distance witness row consumed from $M$, recording exactly which source pairs are identified by the separated reflection.
\item $S$ is the separated target row, carrying the reflected separated metric classifier and its public point surface.
\item $U$ is the unit arrow row from the source row $M$ into the separated target $S$.
\item $F$ is the factorization ledger. It records that any separated metric consumer receiving a compatible arrow from $M$ must read the arrow through $U$ and then through a displayed target arrow out of $S$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source metric row, zero-distance witness, separated target, unit arrow, factorization ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a source metric request through $M,Z,S,U,F,H$ before any completion or Hausdorff consumer reads the result.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetricSeparatedReflectorUnitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,Z,S,U,F,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient representatives, host equality of reflected points, selected section maps, completed limits, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MetricSeparatedReflectorUnit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metric-separated-reflector-unit-namecert-obligations}

Every separated or completion-facing consumer must read
$$
M,Z,S,U,F,H,C,P,N
$$
before treating the separated reflection as public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metric-separated-reflector-unit-carrier}. The rows $M,Z,S,U,F,H,C,P,N$ are exactly the displayed coordinates available to the local certificate.

The source metric and zero-distance rows are read first. The row $Z$ is the only place where source points become identified, so a consumer cannot obtain reflected equality from a hidden quotient representative or host equality predicate.

The unit and factorization rows then expose the reflector-unit surface. The arrow row $U$ supplies the map into the separated target $S$, and the ledger $F$ records how every compatible arrow into a separated metric consumer factors through that unit.

Transport and replay preserve the same route. Componentwise $\hsame$ in $H$ aligns each displayed coordinate, $C$ replays the request through those coordinates, and $P,N$ source and name the packet. Since quotient representatives, selected sections, completed limits, and ambient real completeness are absent from the carrier, no NameCert obligation can export them.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
