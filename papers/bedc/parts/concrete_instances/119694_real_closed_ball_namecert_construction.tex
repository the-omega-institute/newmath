\begin{definition}[Real closed ball carrier]
\label{def:real-closed-ball-carrier}
A $\RealClosedBallUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(c,r,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $c$ is the center $\RealUp$ seal row, exposed through finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback before any boundary comparison is read.
\item $r$ is the positive $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius row, including its positivity witness and tolerance ledger.
\item $W$ is the finite family of $\StreamNameUp$ windows opened from the center and tested real names at the requested precision.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the displayed windows.
\item $D$ is the non-strict dyadic distance ledger, recording the finite rational comparison that certifies the tested name at distance at most the radius row $r$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the tested name after $W,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for center, radius, windows, readback, non-strict comparison, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a closed-neighbourhood query through $c,r,W,R,D,E,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealClosedBallUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealOpenBallUp$, $\RealBallUp$, $\MetricClosedBallUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealClosedBallUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $c,r,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected limits, host topology closure, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real closed ball NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-closed-ball-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealClosedBallUp$ carrier $B=(c,r,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealClosedBallUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, positive dyadic radius admission, finite-window non-strict membership, classifier transport, Real-seal handoff, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming for the rows $c,r,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission projects the finite packet of \autoref{def:real-closed-ball-carrier}. The only Real-facing source rows are the center seal $c$, the tested seal $E$, and the window-readback route $W,R$ connecting them.

The radius obligation is the positive dyadic row $r$. Its positivity and tolerance ledger are visible before the non-strict comparison row $D$ can certify closed-ball membership.

Finite-window membership is exhausted by $W,R,D$. The StreamName windows expose finite observations, the RegSeqRat row reads those observations as regular rational data, and the DyadicRatCore row records the at-most-radius comparison.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no further coordinate is present, the packet cannot export open-cover compactness, trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected limits, host topology closure, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real open and closed boundary duality]
\label{thm:real-closed-ball-open-boundary-duality}
Every boundary consumer that compares an accepted $\RealClosedBallUp$ carrier with a strict $\RealOpenBallUp$ or finite-window $\RealBallUp$ carrier must read both sides through the shared route
$$
\StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The closed side exports only the non-strict dyadic distance row $D$, while the open side exports the strict radius row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-open-ball-namecert}. A metric-facing consumer may pass this boundary to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metricclosedball-namecert}, but it receives no open-cover compactness, trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected limit, host topology closure, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the closed carrier. Its rows $c,r,W,R,D,E$ display the center, radius, finite windows, rational readback, non-strict comparison, and tested Real seal before any boundary read is available.

Read the open side through the same finite source route. The $\RealOpenBallUp$ and $\RealBallUp$ packets expose StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore radius data, and terminal Real seals; they differ from the closed packet only in the strict comparison row exported to the consumer.

The duality is therefore a boundary routing statement. Closed reads keep the non-strict ledger $D$, open reads keep the strict radius ledger, and both are replayed through $H,C,P,N$ rather than through host real topology.

Finally, a metric boundary consumer can consume the non-strict row through the $\MetricClosedBallUp$ surface, but the row remains the displayed finite comparison. No carrier coordinate selects limits, decides trichotomy, quotients real names, proves open-cover compactness, or imports ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
