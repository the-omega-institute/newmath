The $\BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp$ packet realizes a cross-layer relation claim for the conjecture named q6.translation-mediated.protein-abundance.cross-layer. Its carrier is a finite statistical ledger: per-protein codon residual coordinates are compared with measured protein abundance, and the association is decomposed after conditioning on a modeled per-protein translation-adaptation readout. The ledger is intentionally narrower than a mechanism claim. It certifies that the decomposition was computed on matched coding-sequence and protein-abundance rows, with rank checks and in-unit-interval mediation fractions, not that codon choice causally determines abundance.

\begin{definition}[Q6 translation-mediation abundance ledger]
A $\BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp$ instance consists of the following finite data.

First, the codon-residual side $X_Q$ has nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{gathered}
K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{gathered}
$$
Second, the response $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts. Third, the mediator $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_{c} n_c w_c}{\sum_{c} n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count and $w_c$ is the modeled tAI weight obtained from all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. Fourth, the control block $Z$ is the finite residualization basis used before the $X_Q,T,Y$ comparisons. Fifth, the ledger stores, for each organism, the joined row count, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$, the ranks of the residual design blocks, the explained-energy witnesses, and the mediation decomposition numbers.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Organism rows and tAI witnesses]
The ledger contains exactly three organism rows. For Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, the joined table has $3739$ proteins, the tRNA organism is Escherichia coli, the all-tRNA copy total is $90$, and one zero raw tAI weight is filled by the geometric mean. For Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, the joined table has $4129$ proteins, the all-tRNA copy total is $49$, and one zero raw tAI weight is filled by the geometric mean. For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the joined table has $5815$ proteins, the all-tRNA copy total is $275$, and no zero raw tAI weight is filled by the geometric mean.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Residualized mediation fraction]
For each organism row, let $R^2_{QP}$ be the residualized total association from the nine-dimensional $X_Q$ block to $Y$ after the control block $Z$, and let $R^2_{QP\mid T}$ be the direct residualized association after also conditioning on the mediator $T$. The recorded statistical mediation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T}}{R^2_{QP}},
\end{aligned}
$$
when the finite arithmetic of the row supplies the displayed numerator and denominator. The ledger requires the computed value to lie in the unit interval for every included organism row.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Rank-powered Q6 translation-mediation abundance ledger]
The $\BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp$ data certify a rank-powered statistical mediation decomposition for the three organism rows. In every row, the nine residual codon coordinates retain rank $9$, the residual mediator retains rank $1$, the joint residual design has rank $10$, and the powered-rank-sufficient witness is true.

The Escherichia coli row has residual degrees of freedom $3716$, control-block rank $23$, $Y$ residual energy $4020.333671802061$, $T$ residual energy $0.4728468725814029$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.

The Mycobacterium row has residual degrees of freedom $4106$, control-block rank $23$, $Y$ residual energy $5729.194538214237$, $T$ residual energy $0.041191576348982066$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.03209009170649934$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.029395293228368185$, indirect difference $0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.

The Saccharomyces row has residual degrees of freedom $5792$, control-block rank $23$, $Y$ residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, $T$ residual energy $6.90537888244409$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The proof is a finite ledger check. The coordinate list supplies a nine-column $X_Q$ block. The residualized-control certificate supplies $24$ control columns with rank $23$ in each organism row, and the three residual degrees of freedom are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$. The same certificate records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_Q) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T) &= 1,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_Q,\widetilde T) &= 10
\end{aligned}
$$
for all three rows. Hence the mediator column is not rank-collapsed into the residualized $X_Q$ block, and the joint design has the recorded powered rank.

The energy witnesses are also finite row entries. For Escherichia coli, the recorded $T$ incremental improvement given $X_Q$ is $317.26302294928666$, while the direct $X_Q$ improvement given $T$ is $167.56227978068944$. For Mycobacterium, these two witnesses are $0.37452260494680445$ and $168.41135341317295$. For Saccharomyces, they are $104.11162139652299$ and $44.87838061554362$. These numbers are not promoted to causal effects; they only witness that the stated residualized comparisons were computed.

Finally, the mediation decomposition entries give the three displayed values of $M^{QTP}$, and each row explicitly records that this fraction lies in the unit interval. Therefore the carrier contains the claimed rank-powered statistical mediation ledger for the cross-layer relation.
\end{proof}
