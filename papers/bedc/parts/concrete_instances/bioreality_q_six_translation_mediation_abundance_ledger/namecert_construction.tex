The carrier \BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp records a cross-layer observational decomposition in which a nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-residual panel is compared with measured protein abundance after conditioning on a modeled per-protein translation-adaptation readout.  The claim is deliberately narrow: the packet certifies a finite statistical mediation decomposition for matched coding-sequence, tRNA-copy, and protein-abundance rows; it does not promote the mediated fraction into a causal translation mechanism.

The response variable $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts.  The mediation readout $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI weight divided by total codon count, using all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The codon panel $X_Q$ has exactly nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{gathered}
K\_{AAA},\quad Arg\_{AGR},\quad Ile\_{AUA},\quad Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\quad Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\quad Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\quad Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\quad f3\_{stress}.
\end{gathered}
$$
The control block $Z$ has twenty-four columns before rank reduction and includes the non-mediation adjustment surface used for the residualized comparisons.  The decomposition mode is a joint nine-dimensional $X_Q$ mediation scalar with per-coordinate decomposition audit.

\begin{definition}[Q-six translation-mediation abundance ledger]
A \BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp{} instance consists of a finite organism list, a nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel $X_Q$, a real coding-sequence codon-count table, a measured protein-abundance table, a tRNA-copy table, a modeled per-protein tAI vector $T$, a response vector $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and a residualized linear-decomposition certificate.  For each organism the certificate stores the joined protein count, the tRNA organism used for the modeled tAI readout, the total all-tRNA copy count, the zero raw-weight replacement count, the ranks of $Z$, $X_Q$ after residualization, $T$ after residualization, and $[X_Q,T]$ after residualization, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$, the residual energies of $Y$ and $T$, the direct and incremental explained energies, and the mediated fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{R^2(X_Q\to Y)-R^2(X_Q\to Y\mid T)}{R^2(X_Q\to Y)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This fraction is accepted by the carrier only as a statistical decomposition in the unit interval.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Powered residual mediation surface]
For an organism $o$, the translation-mediation surface is powered when the residualized nine-coordinate block has rank $9$, the residualized mediation scalar has rank $1$, the joint residualized block has rank $10$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are positive, and $M^{QTP}(o)$ lies in $[0,1]$.  The stored certificate has the shape
$$
\begin{aligned}
&rank(X_{\widetilde{Q}}(o))=9,\\
&rank(T_{\widetilde{Z}}(o))=1,\\
&rank([X_Q,T]_{\widetilde{Z}}(o))=10,\\
&df_{Z}(o)>0,\\
&0\leq M^{QTP}(o)\leq 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
The definition is statistical.  It does not assert perturbation, rescue, synonymous-edit, ribosome-profiling, or mechanism-realization evidence.
\end{definition}
