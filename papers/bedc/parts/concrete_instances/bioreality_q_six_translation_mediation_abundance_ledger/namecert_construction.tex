\section{Carrier}

\begin{definition}[Translation-mediation abundance ledger]
The carrier $\mathsf{BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedger}$ is the finite package consisting of:

\begin{enumerate}
\item a nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual matrix $X_Q$;
\item a modeled per-protein translation-adaptation scalar $T$;
\item a measured protein-abundance response $Y$;
\item a control matrix $Z$ with residualization before the mediation decomposition;
\item organism-indexed rank, energy, row-count, and mediated-fraction witnesses;
\item an explicit non-promotion boundary forbidding causal or mechanistic promotion.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

The nine residual coordinates are exactly $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. Their source is the imported $q$-vector computation for the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival residual system; this chapter treats those coordinates as the fixed residual basis, not as a newly estimated coordinate family.

The translation readout $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_p &= \frac{\sum_c n_{p,c} w_c}{\sum_c n_{p,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_{p,c}$ is the CDS codon count for protein $p$ and $w_c$ is the codon tAI weight derived from all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The abundance readout $Y$ is $\log_{10}$ of abundance in ppm from measured protein-abundance rows joined to real CDS codon counts.

\section{Residualized design}

Let $X$ denote the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual design, let $T$ denote the modeled-tAI scalar, and let $Y$ denote measured protein abundance. The control matrix $Z$ has $24$ columns in each organism row and residualizes the design before the powered-rank audit. In each row the residualized control rank is $23$, the residualized $X$ rank is $9$, the residualized $T$ rank is $1$, and the joint residualized $[X,T]$ rank is $10$.

The mediation decomposition is the joint nine-dimensional $X_Q$ mediation scalar with per-coordinate decomposition audit. The certificate records the total $X$-to-$Y$ association, the direct association after conditioning on $T$, and the indirect difference:
$$
\begin{aligned}
c &= R^2(Y \sim X \mid Z),\\
c' &= R^2(Y \sim X \mid Z,T),\\
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The value $M^{QTP}$ is accepted here only as a statistical decomposition fraction. It is not a causal effect and is not promoted to a translation mechanism.

\section{Rank sufficiency}

\begin{theorem}[Powered residualized rank sufficiency]
For each organism row in the ledger, the residualized design has enough rank to support the stated joint nine-dimensional mediation decomposition after the $Z$ controls are removed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The certificate records the same rank pattern in each organism row: $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, $rank(\widetilde T)=1$, and $rank([\widetilde X,\widetilde T])=10$. The coordinate count is $9$, and the powered-rank-sufficient flag is true in all three rows. The residual degrees of freedom are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively, so none of the three decompositions is a zero-degree residual fit. The degenerate $R^2$ threshold is $0.999999$, and each recorded $M^{QTP}$ fraction is in the unit interval. These finite witnesses establish rank sufficiency for the stated statistical decomposition.
\end{proof}

\section{Non-promotion boundary}

\begin{principle}[No mechanism promotion]
The carrier certifies a statistical mediation decomposition only. It does not certify translation causality, synonymous-edit response, perturbation rescue, ribosome occupancy, or mechanism realization.
\end{principle}

The modeled tAI scalar is computed from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. It is not ribo-seq. Conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional matched CDS/protein-abundance rows is not a proven translation mechanism. Protein abundance in this ledger is not isolated from mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context. Positive mediated fractions therefore do not establish synonymous-edit, perturbation, rescue, or mechanism-realization claims.
