\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp$}

The $\BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp$ carrier records a cross-layer statistical decomposition between per-protein B$^*_{Q6}$ codon residual coordinates and measured protein abundance after conditioning on a modeled translation-adaptation readout. Its scope is deliberately narrow: it certifies the finite ledger rows, the residualized regression ranks, and the reported mediation fractions for three organism-matched CDS/protein-abundance panels. It does not certify a perturbational translation mechanism.

\begin{definition}[Q6 translation-mediation abundance ledger]
\label{def:bioreality-q-six-translation-mediation-abundance-ledger}
A Q6 translation-mediation abundance ledger is a finite certificate with the following fields.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A declared layer tag $\mathrm{cross\_layer\_relation}$ and a conjecture tag $\mathrm{q6.translation\mbox{-}mediated.protein\mbox{-}abundance.cross\mbox{-}layer}$.
  \item A nine-coordinate B$^*_{Q6}$ residual vector $X_Q$ with coordinate names
  $$
  \begin{gathered}
  K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
  Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
  \end{gathered}
  $$
  \item A protein abundance readout $Y$, defined as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.
  \item A translation readout $T$, defined as the per-protein mean modeled tAI
  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  T &= \frac{\sum_{c} \mathrm{codon\_count}(c)\,\mathrm{tAI\_weight}(c)}{\sum_{c} \mathrm{codon\_count}(c)},
  \end{aligned}
  $$
  using all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.
  \item For each organism panel, a control-residualized linear audit of $Y$, $T$, $X_Q$, and the joint design $(X_Q,T)$ after the same control block $Z$.
  \item For each organism panel, a scalar mediation decomposition
  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},
  \end{aligned}
  $$
  where $c$ is the total B$^*_{Q6}$ residual association with protein abundance and $c'$ is the direct B$^*_{Q6}$ residual association after conditioning on modeled tAI.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Certified organism panels]
\label{def:bioreality-q-six-translation-mediation-organism-panels}
The ledger contains exactly three organism panels.  The Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 panel joins $3739$ CDS/protein rows and uses $90$ all-tRNA copy counts, with one zero raw weight filled by a geometric mean.  The Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 panel joins $4129$ CDS/protein rows and uses $49$ all-tRNA copy counts, with one zero raw weight filled by a geometric mean.  The Saccharomyces cerevisiae panel joins $5815$ CDS/protein rows and uses $275$ all-tRNA copy counts, with no zero raw weight filled by a geometric mean.
\end{definition}
