\section{Carrier}
\label{sec:bioreality-q-six-translation-mediation-abundance-ledger-carrier}

The carrier $\BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp$ records a cross-layer statistical relation between a per-protein $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-residual coordinate block, a modeled translation readout, and measured protein abundance.  Its intended layer is cross-layer relation: the object relates codon residuals to abundance through a conditioning readout, without promoting that conditioning readout to a causal mechanism.

The response coordinate $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts.  The mediator coordinate $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(p) &= \frac{\sum_{c} N_{p,c} w_c}{\sum_{c} N_{p,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $N_{p,c}$ is the codon count for protein $p$ and $w_c$ is the tAI weight induced by all-tRNA copy counts and wobble weights.  The predictor block $X_Q$ is the joint nine-dimensional $Q6$ residual block, with coordinate names
$$
\begin{gathered}
K_{AAA},\quad \mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\quad \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\quad
\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\quad \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\quad \mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\quad
\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\quad f3_{stress}.
\end{gathered}
$$
The carrier therefore binds the claim to a concrete nine-coordinate residual alphabet rather than to an unspecified codon-bias score.

\section{NameCert Packet}
\label{sec:bioreality-q-six-translation-mediation-abundance-ledger-namecert-packet}

\begin{definition}[BioReality $Q6$ translation-mediation abundance ledger]
\label{def:bioreality-q-six-translation-mediation-abundance-ledger}
A $\BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp$ packet consists of the following finite data.

\begin{enumerate}
\item A joined per-protein table for each of three organisms: $3739$ Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 proteins, $4129$ Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 proteins, and $5815$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae proteins.
\item A nine-coordinate predictor block $X_Q$ whose coordinates are exactly the $Q6$ residual coordinates listed above.
\item A modeled translation coordinate $T$ computed as per-protein mean tAI from all-tRNA copy counts and wobble weights.
\item A response coordinate $Y$ computed as log protein abundance from the joined abundance and codon-count rows.
\item A control matrix $Z$ with $24$ control columns before residualization and rank $23$ in each organism-specific audit.
\item A residualized mediation decomposition in which $X_Q$, $T$, and $Y$ are first conditioned against $Z$ and then compared through total, direct, and indirect explained-energy components.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

The joined rows are not symbolic placeholders.  The packet contains $3739$ joined and used proteins for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $4129$ joined and used proteins for Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, and $5815$ joined and used proteins for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding all-tRNA copy totals used by the modeled tAI readout are $90$, $49$, and $275$.  One zero raw tAI weight is filled by a geometric mean in each of the first two organisms, and none is filled in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

\section{Residualized Mediation Audit}
\label{sec:bioreality-q-six-translation-mediation-abundance-ledger-mediation-audit}

\begin{theorem}[Powered residualized abundance mediation ledger]
\label{thm:bioreality-q-six-translation-mediation-abundance-ledger-powered}
For the three organism-specific joined tables carried by $\BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedgerUp$, the residualized design has enough rank to evaluate the joint nine-dimensional $X_Q$ block, the scalar modeled tAI readout $T$, and their joint conditioning against abundance $Y$.  In each organism the mediated fraction $M^{QTP}$ lies in the unit interval.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The claim is a finite ledger assertion about the recorded residualized matrices.  In all three organisms, the audit records $q=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(X_Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X_Q,T)=10$ after residualization by $Z$.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively, so the powered-rank certificate is not obtained from a degenerate design.

The recorded mediated fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Ecoli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Msmegmatis}} &= 0.08397602919861291,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Scerevisiae}} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each displayed number is between $0$ and $1$, matching the explicit unit-interval audit flag in each organism.  The total, direct, and indirect $R^2$ ledger entries also match this statement: Escherichia coli has total $0.10767526677985896$, direct-given-$T$ $0.04167869969498872$, and indirect $0.06599656708487024$; Mycobacterium smegmatis has total $0.03209009170649934$, direct-given-$T$ $0.029395293228368185$, and indirect $0.0026947984781311544$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae has total $0.29837624994140827$, direct-given-$T$ $0.0151709093608293$, and indirect $0.28320534058057895$.  These entries witness the finite decomposition claimed by the theorem.
\end{proof}

The energy audit gives an additional non-template check on the packet.  For Escherichia coli, $X_Q$ explains $432.89050065533684$ energy units on $Y$, $T$ explains $582.5912438239341$ on $Y$, and adding $T$ after $X_Q$ improves the fit by $317.26302294928666$.  For Mycobacterium smegmatis, the corresponding $T$ increment after $X_Q$ is $0.37452260494680445$, while the direct $X_Q$ improvement given $T$ is $168.41135341317295$.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $X_Q$ explains $882.6526210803958$ on $Y$, $T$ explains $941.8858618613751$ on $Y$, and the indirect $R^2$ entry is $0.28320534058057895$.  These values are deliberately kept as ledger values rather than converted into a causal interpretation.

\section{Non-Promotion Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q-six-translation-mediation-abundance-ledger-non-promotion}

\begin{principle}[Statistical mediation only]
\label{prin:bioreality-q-six-translation-mediation-abundance-ledger-statistical-only}
The packet asserts a statistical mediation decomposition of the per-protein $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual to protein-abundance association after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI.  It does not assert a causal effect, a perturbation result, a synonymous-edit result, a rescue result, or a mechanism-realization result.
\end{principle}

The non-promotion boundary is part of the NameCert content.  The modeled tAI coordinate is computed from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights; it is not a ribosome-profiling measurement.  Conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional matched coding-sequence and protein-abundance rows is not a proof of a translation mechanism.  Protein abundance remains entangled with mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, and pathway context.  Positive mediated fractions therefore certify only the recorded decomposition, not biological intervention validity.

\falsifiablePrediction{If a later finite joined ledger with the same three-coordinate roles $X_Q$, $T$, and $Y$, the same nine $Q6$ residual coordinates, and the same residualization discipline records $M^{QTP}$ outside $[0,1]$ for any carried organism, or records insufficient powered rank for $X_Q$ and $(X_Q,T)$, then this NameCert packet no longer certifies the powered mediation claim for that organism.}

\independenceWitness{The certificate is independent of causal promotion: replacing the biological interpretation of $T$ by a purely modeled conditioning coordinate leaves the recorded total, direct, and indirect $R^2$ ledger entries meaningful as statistical decomposition data, while still blocking mechanism-realization, perturbation, rescue, and synonymous-edit conclusions.}
