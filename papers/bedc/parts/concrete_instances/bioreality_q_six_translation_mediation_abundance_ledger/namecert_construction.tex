This chapter constructs the NameCert ledger for a cross-layer relation in which a nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-residual readout is decomposed against measured protein abundance after conditioning on a modeled per-protein translation-adaptation scalar.  The carrier is named BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedger.  It is not a mechanism certificate: it records a statistical mediation decomposition for matched CDS, tRNA-copy, and protein-abundance rows.

The ledger has three organism rows.  The joined CDS/protein-abundance row counts are $3739$ for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $4129$ for \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, and $5815$ for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual vector has exactly nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{gathered}
\texttt{K\_AAA},\ \texttt{Arg\_AGR},\ \texttt{Ile\_AUA},\ \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \texttt{f3\_stress}.
\end{gathered}
$$
The abundance response $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\text{abundance ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The translation readout $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_{c} \operatorname{codon\_count}(c)\operatorname{tAI\_weight}(c)}{\sum_{c} \operatorname{codon\_count}(c)},
\end{aligned}
$$
using all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The control-residualized design uses $24$ control columns in each organism row, with residual degrees of freedom $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$ respectively after residualization.

\begin{definition}[Q-Six translation-mediation abundance ledger]
The BioRealityQSixTranslationMediationAbundanceLedger consists of the following finite data.

First, it contains a three-row organism table whose row names are \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, and \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}.  Each row stores a matched CDS/protein-abundance count, a tRNA organism tag, a total all-tRNA copy count, the zero-raw-weight fill count used by the modeled tAI computation, and the residualized design ranks.

Second, it contains the nine named $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates listed above, treated jointly as the predictor block $X_Q$.

Third, it contains the scalar readouts $T$ and $Y$ described above, together with a residualized mediation decomposition in which the total $X_Q \to Y$ association is compared with the direct $X_Q \to Y$ association after conditioning on $T$.

Fourth, it contains a guard saying that every mediated fraction in this chapter is a statistical decomposition value and not a causal-effect, translation-mechanism, perturbation, rescue, or synonymous-edit certificate.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Residualized mediation fraction]
For one organism row, let $c$ denote the total residualized association score from the nine-coordinate predictor block $X_Q$ to the abundance response $Y$, and let $c'$ denote the direct association score from $X_Q$ to $Y$ after conditioning on the modeled translation scalar $T$.  The ledger records
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}
\end{aligned}
$$
only for rows where the computed value lies in the unit interval.  The indirect score is $c-c'$.  The symbol $M^{QTP}$ is therefore a decomposition summary for the residualized tables in this chapter, not a biological intervention parameter.
\end{definition}
