Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\RegularCauchyBisimulationUp$ packet records equality-facing comparison of two regular Cauchy real names by finite paired windows rather than by quotient real equality. It sits after the regular rational source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite stream-window route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the uniqueness route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-uniqueness-namecert}. The packet names a non-quotient comparison surface for Real and Completion consumers; it does not select representatives, collapse names by quotient equality, import countable choice, or appeal to ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy bisimulation carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-bisimulation-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyBisimulationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(X,Y,W_X,W_Y,D_X,D_Y,M,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ and $Y$ are the two $\RegSeqRatUp$ source rows whose regular rational approximants are being compared.
\item $W_X$ and $W_Y$ are paired $\StreamNameUp$ windows exposing the finite observations of $X$ and $Y$ at the same inspected precisions.
\item $D_X$ and $D_Y$ are $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledgers recording the error budgets and comparison radii used for the two source rows.
\item $M$ is the finite matching relation pairing inspected windows whose dyadic distance is below the displayed joint tolerance.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,Y,W_X,W_Y,D_X,D_Y,M,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of each paired-window comparison through the source rows, stream windows, tolerance ledgers, matching relation, provenance, and local naming row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyBisimulationUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegularCauchyUniquenessUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyBisimulationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate asserts exactly that these rows form one symmetric paired-window comparison certificate. It has no coordinate for quotient real equality, selected representatives, infinite branch selection, countable choice, host equality of real numbers, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy bisimulation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-bisimulation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyBisimulationUp$ carrier $B=(X,Y,W_X,W_Y,D_X,D_Y,M,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyBisimulationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, paired-window exposure, dyadic tolerance comparison, symmetric step matching, $\RealUp$ seal nonescape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. In particular, every comparison read remains inside the displayed route
$$
X,Y \longmapsto W_X,W_Y \longmapsto D_X,D_Y \longmapsto M
$$
before a Real-facing consumer may use the comparison.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-bisimulation-carrier}. The rows $X,Y,W_X,W_Y,D_X,D_Y,M$ expose exactly two regular rational source rows, their paired stream windows, their dyadic tolerance ledgers, and the finite matching relation.

The matching relation is symmetric because every admitted comparison step is stored as a paired-window relation between the two displayed sources. Reading the pair in the opposite direction uses the same windows and the same dyadic tolerance data, so no quotient-real equality or selected representative is introduced.

The Real-facing seal is downstream of the displayed source rows. The $\RealUp$ consumer can only receive a comparison after the $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ rows have been replayed through $C$ and transported through $H$.

Finally $P$ and $N$ source and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for host equality, branch selection, choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
