\begin{theorem}[RegularRealTailSelector tail-window stability]
\label{thm:regular-real-tail-selector-tail-stability}
Let $S=(T,W,D,Q,A,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RegularRealTailSelectorUp$ carrier. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-real-tail-selector-carrier}. The tail read is exhausted by $T,W,D,Q,A,H,C,P,N$, with $T$ naming the threshold and $W$ naming the finite window actually inspected.

Use the componentwise transport row $H$. It moves the threshold, window, tolerance, regular rational readback, and real seal together, so a transported read cannot keep the seal while changing the displayed tail data.

Replay is displayed through $C$ and sourced by $P$. These rows keep the route inside the local $\NameCert_{\RegularRealTailSelectorUp}$ packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for countable choice, quotient real equality, selected representatives, or ambient completeness, the transported tail-window read remains stable only as finite packet data.
\end{proof}
