\begin{theorem}[ProjectiveLimitMetric NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:projective-limit-metric-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ProjectiveLimitMetricUp$ carrier $M=(D,W,\Pi,B,\Delta,K,Q,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ProjectiveLimitMetricUp}$ surface consists of exactly these obligation rows: carrier admission for the finite diagram and projection windows, bonding-map compatibility, $\MetricSpaceUp$ distance-row visibility, projective-limit distance readback, $\CompleteMetricUp$ and $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ completion handoff, $\InverseLimitMetricUp$ sibling dependency, $\StreamNameUp$ window routing, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:projective-limit-metric-carrier}. The rows $D,W,\Pi$ expose the finite diagram, projection windows, and bonding-map checks before any distance or completion row is read.

Next read the metric rows. The rows $B,\Delta,Q,R$ keep every distance comparison inside the displayed $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ data, while $E$ records the sibling dependency on \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-inverse-limit-metric-namecert}.

Finally use $K,H,C,P,N$. The completion handoff is a displayed row, transport is componentwise, replay is routed through $C$, and provenance and naming are local. No omitted quotient, representative family, ambient projective limit, chooser, or completeness oracle can enter the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
