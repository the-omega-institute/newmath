Following Hochster and the scheme-topology tradition, the $\SpectralSpaceUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface of quasi-compact open bases, finite intersections, and sober prime-facing observation rows. It sits between the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the point-free open ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locale-space-namecert}, the clopen compact boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stone-space-namecert}, and the affine-site consumer surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-scheme-namecert}. The packet names the compact-open certificate data available to later scheme, locale, and Stone routes; it does not import host prime spectra, selected prime ideals, quotient topology equality, spatiality for every locale, or an open-cover compactness theorem outside the displayed rows.

\begin{definition}[Spectral space carrier]
\label{def:spectral-space-carrier}
A $\SpectralSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(T,B,K,I,L,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the ambient $\TopologyUp$ indexed-open row.
\item $B$ is the compact-open basis row, recording displayed basis indices and membership reads.
\item $K$ is the quasi-compactness witness row for each displayed basis open, read only as finite cover-refinement data.
\item $I$ is the finite-intersection row, requiring the intersection of two displayed compact-open basis rows to be routed through another displayed basis row.
\item $L$ is the $\LocaleSpaceUp$ handoff row, exposing the same compact-open data as a point-free finite meet and cover-refinement ledger.
\item $A$ is the $\SchemeUp$ affine-site row, used only when scheme consumers read distinguished-open or affine-cover data through the compact-open basis surface.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology, basis, compactness, intersection, locale, scheme, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the route
$$
T,B,K,I \longmapsto L,A,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SpectralSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\LocaleSpaceUp$, $\StoneSpaceUp$, $\SchemeUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SpectralSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,B,K,I,L,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host set of points, selected prime ideals, arbitrary open-cover compactness, quotient topology equality, or a global spatiality theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:spectral-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SpectralSpaceUp$ carrier $S=(T,B,K,I,L,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SpectralSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of topology-source exposure through $T$, compact-open basis exposure through $B$, finite cover-refinement compactness through $K$, compact-open finite-intersection stability through $I$, locale handoff through $L$, affine-site scheme handoff through $A$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. A $\StoneSpaceUp$, $\LocaleSpaceUp$, $\SchemeUp$, or $\TopologyUp$ consumer may read only those rows from the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:spectral-space-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the topology row, compact-open basis row, compactness row, finite-intersection row, locale handoff, scheme handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row.

The basis row $B$ is read before compactness or intersection data. The row $K$ then records only finite cover-refinement witnesses for displayed basis opens, while $I$ records only finite intersections that remain inside the displayed compact-open basis.

The locale handoff $L$ and scheme handoff $A$ consume that same basis route. The locale consumer receives finite meet and cover-refinement data, and the scheme consumer receives affine-site compact-open reads, but neither receives a detached host point set or a chosen family of prime ideals.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, quotient topology equality, selected prime ideals, or a global spatiality theorem, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}
