\begin{definition}[Uniform Cauchy net bridge carrier]
\label{def:uniform-cauchy-net-bridge-carrier}
A $\UniformCauchyNetBridgeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(N,E,F,R,S,Z,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $N$ is the directed Cauchy-net source row, naming the directed index data, net observations, and displayed Cauchy-entourage requests.
\item $E$ is the entourage row, recording finite uniform entourages and their monotone refinement route.
\item $F$ is the filter row, recording the Cauchy filter data read from the entourage route and the compatibility witness with the net source.
\item $R$ is the regular-Cauchy readback row, exposing the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational name obtained from the displayed net/filter windows.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, recording the finite observations by which the directed net and regular readback are inspected.
\item $Z$ is the completion-seal row, recording the $\UniformSpaceCompletionUp$ handoff and the terminal $\RealUp$ zero-distance or classifier row available to completion consumers.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for directed-net inspection, entourage refinement, filter compatibility, regular-Cauchy readback, stream-window inspection, completion sealing, and zero-distance or classifier consumption.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformCauchyNetBridgeUp$, $\RegularCauchyNetUp$, $\CauchyEntourageFilterUp$, $\UniformSpaceCompletionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform Cauchy net bridge NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-cauchy-net-bridge-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-cauchy-net-bridge-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\UniformCauchyNetBridgeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $K=(N,E,F,R,S,Z,H,C,P,L)$, directed-entourage stability through $N,E$, filter/net compatibility through $F$, regular readback through $R$, stream-window inspection through $S$, completion sealing and zero-distance or classifier exposure through $Z$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $K=(N,E,F,R,S,Z,H,C,P,L)$. Carrier admission supplies the directed net source before any entourage or filter row is consumed.

Read $E$ and $F$ together. The entourage row displays finite uniform refinements, while the filter row records that the same displayed route is compatible with the directed Cauchy-net source.

Next read $R,S,Z$. The regular-Cauchy readback is inspected through finite stream windows before the completion seal and the $\RealUp$ zero-distance or classifier row can be consumed.

Finally use $H,C,P,L$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve only the displayed packet, so the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface and add no quotient net, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, ambient Real completeness theorem, or checked bridge.
\end{proof}
