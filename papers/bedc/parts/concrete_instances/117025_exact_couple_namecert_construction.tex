\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ExactCoupleUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-exact-couple-namecert}

Following Cartan--Eilenberg and McCleary, the $\ExactCoupleUp$ packet records the finite precursor by which graded-object data, two degree-preserving maps, and one connecting map generate the page handoff later consumed by $\SpectralSeqUp$. It sits over the abelian-category surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-abeliancat-namecert}, the homology and cohomology rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homology-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cohomology-namecert}, and before the spectral-sequence packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-spectralseq-namecert}. The certificate names the finite exact-couple ledger only; it does not export an ambient derived-category calculus, quotient page object, or unrecorded convergence theorem.

\begin{definition}[Exact-couple carrier]
\label{def:exact-couple-carrier}
An $\ExactCoupleUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(A,G,D,I,J,K,W,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the $\AbelianCatUp$ source row in which the displayed graded objects and maps are read.
\item $G$ is the graded-object row, carrying the inspected bidegree window and its neighboring degree shifts.
\item $D$ is the degree-preserving self-map row on the displayed graded object data.
\item $I$ is the second degree-preserving map row, recording the visible inclusion or transition map used by the exact couple.
\item $J$ is the connecting-map row from the companion graded object into the shifted inspected object.
\item $K$ is the $\HomologyUp$ or $\CohomologyUp$ kernel-image readback row attached to the same degree window.
\item $W$ records the finite exactness windows for the rows $D,I,J,K$, with every kernel and image comparison read through the displayed certificate rows.
\item $R$ is the derived-page handoff row, exposing the finite page data later consumed by $\SpectralSeqUp$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the graded-object row, the two degree-preserving maps, the connecting map, exactness windows, derived-page handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an exact-couple read through $A,G,D,I,J,K,W,R,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ExactCoupleUp$, $\AbelianCatUp$, $\HomologyUp$, $\CohomologyUp$, $\SpectralSeqUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ExactCoupleUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for quotient page equality, host exactness outside the finite windows, derived-category localization, selected representatives, countable choice, or convergence data beyond the handoff row $R$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exact-couple NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:exact-couple-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ExactCoupleUp$ carrier $Q=(A,G,D,I,J,K,W,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ExactCoupleUp}$ surface is exhausted by the abelian-category source row, graded-object data, two degree-preserving maps, one connecting map, kernel-image readback, finite exactness windows, derived-page handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. A consumer may not infer an ambient exact-couple object, quotient page equality, host exactness theorem, selected cycle representative, hidden derived-category localization, or spectral-sequence convergence claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:exact-couple-carrier}. The rows $A,G,D,I,J$ expose the abelian source, graded data, two degree-preserving maps, and the connecting map before any exactness or page read is available.

Next read $K$ and $W$. These rows attach kernel-image comparisons to the same finite degree window and keep exactness inside the displayed $\HomologyUp$ or $\CohomologyUp$ readback rather than in a host theorem.

Finally $R,H,C,P,N$ package the derived-page handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Since none of these rows adds quotient page equality, a selected representative, derived localization, countable choice, or convergence data, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact-couple spectral-sequence handoff]
\label{thm:exact-couple-spectral-sequence-handoff}
Every $\SpectralSeqUp$ consumer that reads an accepted $\ExactCoupleUp$ packet must reach the page-generation boundary through the finite route
$$
\AbelianCatUp,\;G,\;D,\;I,\;J,\;K,\;W \longmapsto R \longmapsto \SpectralSeqUp .
$$
The route exports the derived-page handoff row $R$ together with $H,C,P,N$, not an ambient exact-couple object, not a quotient page primitive, and not convergence data outside the spectral-sequence packet that consumes the handoff.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier rows $A,G,D,I,J,K,W$. They display the exact-couple data and the finite exactness windows before any page-generation read can be replayed.

The handoff is then the row $R$. By definition $R$ exposes only finite page data derived from the same graded-object and exactness-window ledger, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport and replay that same handoff.

Therefore a $\SpectralSeqUp$ consumer receives a finite precursor route rather than a primitive page oracle. Any quotient page equality, ambient exact-couple object, hidden representative choice, or convergence assertion lies outside the accepted $\ExactCoupleUp$ carrier.
\end{proof}
