Following Bishop and Bridges, located comparison of real names is read through explicit rational windows and dyadic tolerance data rather than through trichotomy or quotient-real equality. The $\BishopLocatedComparisonUp$ packet sits after the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the located interval route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}. It names a finite comparison interface for L10 real and completion consumers without importing selected representatives, ambient real order, or completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Bishop located-comparison carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-comparison-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedComparisonUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(L,R,D,W,S,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the left regular real-name row, including the inspected $\StreamNameUp$ windows and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readbacks used by the comparison.
\item $R$ is the right regular real-name row, with the same kind of displayed stream windows and regular rational readbacks.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and enclosure ledger, recording the positive dyadic requests, enclosure widths, and finite comparison rows used to separate or overlap the two displayed names.
\item $W$ is the located-window comparison ledger, listing the finite pairs of left and right windows inspected at each dyadic tolerance.
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal surface for the left and right names after the displayed windows and readbacks have been fixed.
\item $E$ records the comparison outcome rows available to downstream consumers: separated-left, separated-right, or retained-overlap data, each tied to a displayed tolerance and window pair.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the left and right real-name rows, dyadic tolerance ledger, window ledger, seal rows, comparison outcome rows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from each comparison request through $L,R,D,W,S,E,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopLocatedComparisonUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedComparisonUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate says that these rows form one finite located-comparison packet. It has no coordinate for real trichotomy, quotient real equality, a selected representative stream, a host order oracle, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located-comparison NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-comparison-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedComparisonUp$ carrier $K=(L,R,D,W,S,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedComparisonUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the finite packet of \autoref{def:bishop-located-comparison-carrier};
\item left and right source visibility through the displayed $\StreamNameUp$ windows and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readbacks in $L$ and $R$;
\item dyadic located-window comparison coverage through the tolerance and enclosure ledger $D$ together with the paired window ledger $W$;
\item comparison stability under componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay through $H$ and $C$;
\item $\RealUp$ seal nonescape through $S$, requiring every real-facing consumer to pass through the displayed left and right window rows before reading a seal;
\item provenance and local naming through $P$ and $N$.
\end{enumerate}
No further order, equality, choice, or completeness obligation is part of the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-comparison-carrier}. The left and right sources expose only their displayed stream windows and regular rational readbacks, while the dyadic ledger $D$ supplies the tolerance and enclosure rows used by the comparison.

The paired window ledger $W$ records exactly which left and right windows were inspected at each tolerance. A separated or retained-overlap row in $E$ is therefore read only after the corresponding entries of $L,R,D,W$ have been displayed.

Transport and replay are componentwise. Rows $H$ and $C$ move the same left source, right source, dyadic tolerance, window, seal, and outcome rows together, so a consumer cannot replace the finite comparison route with an external order oracle.

Finally, $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming for that same route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for trichotomy, quotient equality, selected representatives, or completeness of $\RealUp$, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located-comparison Real seal nonescape]
\label{thm:bishop-located-comparison-real-seal-nonescape}
Every comparison consumer of an accepted $\BishopLocatedComparisonUp$ carrier routes through the displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, and $\LocatedIntervalUp$ rows before it can read a comparison outcome or a real-facing seal. Such a consumer cannot read real trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected stream representatives, a host order oracle, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the consumer route from the carrier. The source rows $L$ and $R$ expose the stream windows and regular rational readbacks, and the dyadic ledger $D$ supplies the tolerance scale on which the comparison is staged.

The window ledger $W$ ties those rows to the located interval boundary inherited from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}. A comparison outcome in $E$ is therefore attached to a finite pair of displayed windows and a displayed dyadic tolerance before the terminal seal $S$ is available.

Rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. None of these rows contains trichotomy, quotient equality, a selected representative, an external order oracle, or completeness data for $\RealUp$. Hence every real-seal or comparison read remains inside the displayed finite packet.
\end{proof}
