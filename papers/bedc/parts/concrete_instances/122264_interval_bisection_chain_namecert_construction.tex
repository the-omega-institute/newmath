\begin{definition}[Interval bisection chain carrier]
\label{def:interval-bisection-chain-carrier}
An $\IntervalBisectionChainUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(I_0,\ldots,I_n,E,K,W,S,R,Q,H,T,P,N)
$$
where each $I_i=[a_i,b_i]$ is a displayed dyadic interval row and the packet exposes the following data:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the endpoint ledger, listing the dyadic endpoints $a_i,b_i$ and their $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ admission rows.
\item $K$ is the containment ledger, recording $I_{i+1}\subseteq I_i$ for every displayed successor step.
\item $W$ is the width-halving ledger, recording $b_{i+1}-a_{i+1}\leq (b_i-a_i)/2$ through dyadic arithmetic rows.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule generated from the endpoint rows and successor depth $n$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the midpoint or endpoint selector read from the finite windows.
\item $Q$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal available only after $E,K,W,S,R$ have all been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval rows, endpoint ledger, containment ledger, width ledger, window schedule, regular readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an interval-chain request through $I_0,\ldots,I_n,E,K,W,S,R,Q,H,P,N$.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\IntervalBisectionChainUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for a chosen infinite branch, Dedekind cut, interval choice principle, Heine--Borel cover argument, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Interval bisection chain NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:interval-bisection-chain-namecert-obligations}

$$

$$

\begin{proof}

The successor rows are read in order. Endpoint admission supplies the dyadic arithmetic inputs, containment keeps each retained child inside its predecessor, and the width ledger records the displayed halving budget for the same successor step.

The value-facing rows are downstream. The StreamName schedule is generated from the finite endpoint rows, the RegSeqRat readback reads the finite windows, and the Real seal is available only after those rows have been displayed.
