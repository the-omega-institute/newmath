Following Hopf's fibration theorem, the $\HopfFibrationUp$ packet records the circle-fibre projection from the displayed three-sphere source to the displayed two-sphere target as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits over the circle carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-s1-namecert}, the bundle surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bundle-namecert}, the Lie-group endpoint rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-liegroup-namecert}, the vector-bundle bookkeeping of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vectorbundle-namecert}, and the Riemann-sphere chart target of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemann-sphere-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Hopf fibration carrier]
\label{def:hopf-fibration-carrier}
A $\HopfFibrationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
H=(S^{3},S^{2},F,\Pi,C,L,V,T,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S^{3}$ is the source sphere row whose circle-phase boundary is read through $\NameCert_{\SOneUp}$.
\item $S^{2}$ is the target sphere row, read through the $\RiemannSphereUp$ chart surface.
\item $F$ is the circle-fibre row, displaying the $\SOneUp$ fibre over each inspected target observation.
\item $\Pi$ is the projection row from the source packet to the target packet.
\item $C$ is the componentwise classifier for source, target, fibre, projection, chart, and fibre-action rows.
\item $L$ is the local trivialization ledger supplied through the $\BundleUp$ dependency surface.
\item $V$ records the vector-bundle and tangent-window rows used only to compare displayed local coordinates.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S^{3},S^{2},F,\Pi,C,L,V$ and their dependency rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a fibre request through projection, chart readback, circle-fibre readback, and local trivialization.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HopfFibrationUp$, $\SOneUp$, $\BundleUp$, $\LieGroupUp$, $\VectorBundleUp$, $\RiemannSphereUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HopfFibrationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient equality of spheres, selected global section, ambient smooth-map extensionality, or a host principal-bundle object outside the displayed packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hopf fibration NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hopf-fibration-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HopfFibrationUp$ carrier $H=(S^{3},S^{2},F,\Pi,C,L,V,T,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HopfFibrationUp}$ surface consists exactly of source-sphere admission, target-chart admission, circle-fibre exposure, projection exposure, classifier stability, local trivialization ledger exposure, vector-window comparison, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hopf-fibration-carrier}. The geometric rows are $S^{3},S^{2},F,\Pi,L,V$, while $C,T,R,P,N$ are the classifier, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows attached to the same packet.

The source and target rows are read only through their displayed dependencies. The source circle phase reaches \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-s1-namecert}; the target chart reaches \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemann-sphere-namecert}; and the local trivialization reaches \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bundle-namecert}.

The fibre row $F$ is then compared through the $\SOneUp$ and $\LieGroupUp$ rows, while $V$ supplies only the finite vector-window comparison used by local coordinates. Componentwise transport through $T$ and replay through $R$ preserve exactly those rows.

Since no carrier coordinate names a host quotient sphere, selected global section, smooth-map extensionality principle, or external principal bundle, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hopf fibration circle-fibre handoff]
\label{thm:hopf-fibration-circle-fibre-handoff}
Every $\BundleUp$, $\LieGroupUp$, or $\RiemannSphereUp$ consumer that reads an accepted $\HopfFibrationUp$ packet must expose the source row $S^{3}$, target row $S^{2}$, circle-fibre row $F$, projection row $\Pi$, local trivialization ledger $L$, vector-window row $V$, transport row $T$, replay row $R$, provenance row $P$, and local name row $N$ before the circle-fibre handoff can be consumed. The handoff exports only the displayed $\SOneUp$ fibre over the displayed target row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:hopf-fibration-namecert-obligations}. It makes the source sphere, target chart, circle fibre, projection, local trivialization, and vector-window comparison rows first-class parts of the accepted packet.

A bundle consumer can therefore read a fibre only after the projection and local trivialization rows have been displayed. A Lie-group consumer reaches only the circle action already present in the $\SOneUp$ fibre row, and a Riemann-sphere consumer reaches only the target chart row $S^{2}$.

Finally the structural rows $T,R,P,N$ keep the same packet boundary under transport, replay, provenance, and naming. Thus the handoff is the displayed circle-fibre route itself, with no selected global section, quotient sphere equality, ambient smooth-map equality, or completed principal-bundle theorem.
\end{proof}
