\label{def:bishop-located-filter-base-carrier}

$$

\item $L$ is the $\CauchyFilterBaseUp$ and located-real bracket row. It records the Cauchy tail budget, lower and upper located brackets, and the handoff discipline inherited from $\BishopLocatedRealFilterUp$.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window ledger, displaying the finite source windows read by the bracket and tail requests.

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located filter-base NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-filter-base-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedFilterBaseUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedFilterBaseUp}$ surface consists exactly of FilterBase admission for $F$, CauchyFilterBase and located-real bracket admission for $L$, finite StreamName window coverage for $W$, dyadic tolerance visibility for $D$, RegSeqRat readback for $R$, Real seal routing for $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, sibling provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

No Bishop located filter-base obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-filter-base-carrier}. The accepted packet is exactly the tuple $F,L,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$, so every local naming obligation must be read from one of those rows.

\label{thm:bishop-located-filter-base-real-handoff}
