\begin{definition}[Grothendieck spectral sequence carrier]
\label{def:grothendieck-spectral-sequence-carrier}
A $\GrothendieckSpectralSequenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the $\AbelianCatUp$ source row in which exactness, injective or acyclic resolutions, and homology reads are interpreted.
\item $C$ is the $\CategoryUp$ dependency row for the source, middle, and target categories of the composite construction.
\item $F$ and $G_0$ are the two $\FunctorUp$ rows whose composite is being inspected.
\item $\eta$ is the $\NatTransUp$ comparison row recording the displayed compatibility data used by the composite-functor route.
\item $D$ is the $\DerivedFunctorUp$ row for the inner and outer derived-functor reads, including the resolution, degree, cycle-boundary, and $\Cont$ ledgers visible to downstream consumers.
\item $E$ is the $\SpectralSeqUp$ page row, carrying the displayed $E_2$ page, differential rows, successor-page route, and filtration readback boundary.
\item $P$ is the finite acyclicity and resolution-compatibility ledger that allows the inner derived-functor row to feed the outer derived-functor row.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the abelian, category, functor, natural-transformation, derived-functor, spectral-sequence, acyclicity, target-filtration, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,C,F,G_0,\eta,D,E,P,T,H,R,Q,N$. It has no coordinate for host derived-category equality, a global convergence theorem, chosen resolutions outside the displayed ledger, exactness beyond the acyclicity row, or quotient equality of pages.

\begin{theorem}[Grothendieck spectral sequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:grothendieck-spectral-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\GrothendieckSpectralSequenceUp$ carrier $G=(A,C,F,G_0,\eta,D,E,P,T,H,R,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\GrothendieckSpectralSequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for those thirteen rows; source exposure through $\AbelianCatUp$ and $\CategoryUp$; functorial exposure through the two $\FunctorUp$ rows and the $\NatTransUp$ comparison row; derived-functor exposure through $D$; spectral-sequence page exposure through $E$; acyclicity and resolution compatibility through $P$; target-filtration exposure through $T$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through $R$; $\Pkg$ provenance through $Q$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:grothendieck-spectral-sequence-carrier}. The only source rows are $A,C,F,G_0,\eta$, and the only homological rows are $D,E,P,T$.

Read the functorial source first. The category and abelian-category rows type the two functors and the natural-transformation comparison, so the composite route is a displayed BEDC route rather than an ambient functor category.

Next read the homological ledger. The derived-functor row $D$ supplies the inner and outer derived reads, $P$ records the acyclicity and resolution-compatibility budget, $E$ records the spectral-sequence page ledger, and $T$ records the target filtration boundary.

Finally use $H,R,Q,N$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve exactly those displayed rows. Since no carrier coordinate stores a host convergence theorem, chosen external resolution, derived-category equality, or quotient page equality, those data are not NameCert obligations of the packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Grothendieck spectral sequence derived-functor route]
\label{thm:grothendieck-spectral-sequence-derived-functor-route}

The route exposes the composite source, the acyclicity ledger, the page ledger, and the target-filtration boundary before any derived-functor output is read. It does not export a host Grothendieck spectral sequence theorem, global convergence assertion, selected resolution outside $P$, or quotient equality of spectral-sequence pages.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The spectral-sequence handoff is the row $E$, followed by the target filtration row $T$. These rows are read through the existing $\SpectralSeqUp$ page ledger and do not add a separate convergence theorem.

Finally $H,R,Q,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same route. Thus the accepted consumer sees a finite composite-derived-functor packet and no unlisted host-level exactness or convergence data.
\end{proof}
