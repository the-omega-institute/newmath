\begin{definition}[Ask--Sig--Pkg--Gap route carrier]
\label{def:ask-sig-gap-route-carrier}

$$
R=(B,D,A,S,E,P,G,X,N,H,C,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the probe-bundle row, naming the finite $\Bundle$ of probes whose answers generate the route.
\item $D$ is the admitted-domain row over source histories.
\item $A$ is the $\Ask$ row, recording each probe request, emitted mark, and event evidence for admitted histories.
\item $S$ is the $\Sig$ row, recording the generated answer history and evidence ledger assembled from $A$.
\item $E$ is the $\sameSig$ row, comparing generated answer histories only by internal $\hsame$ witnesses.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ row, naming visible package tokens for generated answer histories.
\item $G$ is the $\Gap$ row, recording package-token membership for admitted source histories.
\item $X$ is the $\ExactGlobalizeBase$ row, carrying coverage, base soundness, and base completeness for the displayed gap-signature route.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a route request through $B,D,A,S,E,P,G,X,N,H$.

\begin{theorem}[Ask--Sig--Pkg--Gap route NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:ask-sig-gap-route-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AskSigGapRouteUp$ carrier $R=(B,D,A,S,E,P,G,X,N,H,C,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\AskSigGapRouteUp}$ surface consists exactly of these finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for bundle, domain, ask, signature, signature-sameness, package, gap, exactness, local naming, transport, replay, and provenance rows;
\item ask totality and determinacy only for admitted histories in $D$ and probes in $B$;
\item signature construction and determinacy through the generated $\Sig$ row $S$ and the $\sameSig$ row $E$;
\item package-token visibility and reflection through $P$;
\item gap coverage and same-source separation through $G$;
\item exact local classification through the coverage, base-soundness, and base-completeness fields of $X$;
\item source, pattern, classifier, stability, ledger, transport, replay, and provenance discipline through $N,H,C,L$.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation for a downstream mature object is stored in this route packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:ask-sig-gap-route-carrier}. The rows $B,D,A$ display exactly the finite probe bundle, admitted source domain, and ask-event ledger, so an ask read has no access to an external oracle.

The signature and package rows are downstream. The row $S$ assembles answer histories from ask events, $E$ compares those answer histories by $\hsame$, and $P$ exposes only visible package tokens backed by generated signatures.

The gap and exactness rows complete the route. The row $G$ records package-token membership for admitted histories, while $X$ supplies precisely the coverage, base-soundness, and base-completeness fields of $\ExactGlobalizeBase$.

Finally $N,H,C,L$ name, transport, replay, and source the same finite data. Since the carrier has no semantic oracle, hidden quotient, external classifier, or downstream mature-object coordinate, the listed obligations exhaust the local $\NameCert_{\AskSigGapRouteUp}$ surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Ask--Sig--Pkg--Gap route exactness]
\label{thm:ask-sig-gap-route-gap-exactness}

$$

$$

The route exports no quotient package equality, semantic source equality, external global classifier, or mature downstream object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The signature side is then forced through $S$ and $E$. The generated answer histories are assembled by the signature row and compared only by the internal $\hsame$ witness carried by $\sameSig$.

The exactness row $X$ supplies coverage, base soundness, and base completeness for exactly those displayed memberships. Therefore local classification reaches package-token sameness only through the route already named, and the structural rows $N,H,C,L$ cannot add a quotient, oracle, external classifier, or downstream carrier.
\end{proof}
