\begin{theorem}[TotalOrder lattice sibling route]
\label{thm:totalorder-lattice-sibling-route}
The $\TotalOrderUp$ packet consumes the inherited $\PreorderUp$ and $\POSetUp$
comparison spine and supplies the comparable-pair row needed by finite
$\LatticeUp$ meet and join routes. A lattice consumer may read the
TotalOrder comparison branch as an order-front input for the displayed finite
meet-or-join construction, but it cannot extract a separate lattice law, host
order equality, quotient carrier equality, or a global completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the inherited order rows from the TotalOrder root obligation surface.
They expose the $\PreorderUp$ reflexive-transitive spine together with the
$\POSetUp$ antisymmetry and classifier-exactness rows already used by the
unary-prefix package.

The TotalOrder-specific row adds only comparability for the displayed pair.
That is exactly the order-front datum a finite $\LatticeUp$ meet or join
route needs before it can choose the lower or upper branch inside its own
certificate.

The route is therefore horizontal: TotalOrder supplies comparable-pair input
to Lattice, while Lattice retains its own meet, join, absorption, and
classifier rows. No host order equality, quotient carrier equality, separate
lattice law, or completeness endpoint is introduced by the sibling edge.
\end{proof}
