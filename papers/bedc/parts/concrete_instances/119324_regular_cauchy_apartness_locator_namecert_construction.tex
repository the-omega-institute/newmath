$$

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy apartness locator NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-apartness-locator-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyApartnessLocatorUp$ carrier
$$
K=(S,R,D,A,L,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyApartnessLocatorUp}$ surface consists exactly of paired stream-window exposure, paired regular-rational readback, dyadic-tolerance exactness, positive apartness-locator exposure, Cauchy located-apartness handoff, Real-seal routing, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-apartness-locator-carrier}. The analytic rows are $S,R,D,A,L,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The stream row $S$ fixes the finite observations, the readback row $R$ records the regular rational data read from those observations, and the dyadic row $D$ fixes the positive tolerance at which apartness is inspected.

The apartness locator row $A$ and the Cauchy located-apartness handoff row $L$ are downstream of the same $S,R,D$ route. The Real seal $E$ is reached only after those rows are displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since none of the excluded principles is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
