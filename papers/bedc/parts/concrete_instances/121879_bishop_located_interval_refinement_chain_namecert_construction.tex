Following Bishop and Bridges on located intervals, regular Cauchy names, and constructive real completion, the $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementChainUp$ packet records a finite chain of located interval refinements before a Real or Completion consumer may read a terminal real-name seal. It sits between the located-interval source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}, the real-interval endpoint surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-realinterval-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance descent of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The certificate gives the L10 Real and Completion route a displayed refinement-chain object; it does not assert compactness, trichotomy, selected representatives, countable choice, quotient equality of names, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Bishop located interval refinement-chain carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-interval-refinement-chain-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementChainUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(L,I,D,W,Q,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedIntervalUp$ source row naming the initial located interval and its apartness-facing boundary data.
\item $I$ is the $\RealIntervalUp$ endpoint and enclosure row, displaying lower and upper real-interval endpoints together with the enclosure relation consumed by the chain.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance-descent row, recording a finite decreasing dyadic budget for the refinement steps.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row inspected at each displayed tolerance stage.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same finite windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $S$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the located interval source, endpoint-enclosure row, tolerance descent, windows, and readback have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the located source, endpoint-enclosure row, dyadic descent, finite windows, regular readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a refinement request through $L,I,D,W,Q,S,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementChainUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\RealIntervalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementChainUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $L,I,D,W,Q,S,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for interval compactness, trichotomy of reals, a selected representative of a completion class, countable choice, quotient equality of interval names, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located interval refinement-chain NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-interval-refinement-chain-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementChainUp$ carrier
$$
R=(L,I,D,W,Q,S,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementChainUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the located interval source, RealInterval endpoint and enclosure row, DyadicRatCore tolerance descent, StreamName finite windows, RegSeqRat readback, RealUp seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-interval-refinement-chain-carrier}. The tuple displays the located source $L$, the endpoint-enclosure row $I$, the tolerance descent $D$, the finite windows $W$, the regular readback $Q$, and the terminal seal $S$ before any structural row is consumed.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name only those displayed rows. They do not add a hidden interval compactness theorem, a selected completion representative, or an ambient completeness principle.

Thus the local NameCert surface is exhausted by the ten displayed rows, and every accepted consumer must read the refinement chain through that finite packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located interval refinement-chain sibling route]
\label{thm:bishop-located-interval-refinement-chain-sibling-route}
The $\BishopLocatedIntervalRefinementChainUp$ carrier is not bijective to the located interval, real interval, dyadic rational core, stream-name, regular rational readback, or real-name sibling packets. It binds all six sibling surfaces into one ordered refinement-chain packet whose endpoint, tolerance, window, readback, and seal rows are consumed together.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compare row inventories. The located interval sibling supplies $L$, but it does not include the endpoint-enclosure row, dyadic tolerance descent, finite window schedule, regular readback, or terminal Real seal.

The real interval and dyadic rational siblings supply $I$ and $D$ respectively. Neither one records the StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, and structural rows as a single chain.

The stream-name, regular-rational, and real-name siblings supply $W,Q,S$ as downstream readings. They do not contain the located source and endpoint-enclosure data that make those readings a refinement chain. Therefore no listed sibling preserves the accepted packet $R=(L,I,D,W,Q,S,H,C,P,N)$ component by component.
\end{proof}
