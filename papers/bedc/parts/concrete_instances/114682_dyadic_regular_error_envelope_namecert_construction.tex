\item $Q$ is the requested regular tail tolerance presented by the downstream Real-or-completion consumer.
\item $B$ is the $\DyadicErrorBudgetUp$ row that splits the visible dyadic slack for the two regular reads.
\item $W_0$ and $W_1$ are the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window rows opened before the two approximants are inspected.

The carrier has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, host real equality, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, detached completion evidence, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic regular error-envelope stability]
\label{thm:dyadic-regular-error-envelope-stability}
For every accepted $\DyadicRegularErrorEnvelopeUp$ carrier $E=(Q,B,W_0,W_1,D_0,D_1,D_\Delta,R_0,R_1,T,A,H,C,P,N)$, any refinement of the two finite windows that preserves the displayed budget row $B$, dyadic endpoint rows $D_0,D_1$, envelope row $D_\Delta$, regular readbacks $R_0,R_1$, and tail-estimate row $T$ preserves the same error-envelope route under $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-regular-error-envelope-carrier}. The only analytic coordinates are the request, budget, two windows, three dyadic rows, two regular readbacks, tail estimate, and Real seal.

Window refinement can therefore be read only through $W_0,W_1$ and their attached rows $R_0,R_1$. The dyadic comparison still passes through $D_0,D_1,D_\Delta$ against the same request $Q$ and budget row $B$.

The row $T$ records that this displayed dyadic envelope is the tail-estimate surface consumed by the regular Cauchy route. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same route, so no hidden selector, quotient equality, or completion principle can change the envelope while the packet remains accepted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic regular error-envelope Real readback]
\label{thm:dyadic-regular-error-envelope-real-readback}
Every $\RealUp$ or completion-facing read of an accepted $\DyadicRegularErrorEnvelopeUp$ packet must factor through
$$
Q,B \longmapsto W_0,W_1 \longmapsto D_0,D_1,D_\Delta \longmapsto R_0,R_1,T \longmapsto A,H,C,P,N .

Thus the Real seal is downstream of the displayed dyadic budget, finite windows, regular readbacks, and Cauchy-estimate envelope; it is not a quotient-real equality, selected limit, countable-choice, or ambient-completeness export.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Next open the two finite windows and their dyadic rows. The rows $W_0,W_1,D_0,D_1,D_\Delta$ expose the endpoint errors and the comparison envelope, while $R_0,R_1$ attach the regular-rational readbacks required by the Cauchy tail route.
