\begin{theorem}[Equicontinuous sequence uniform lattice route]
\label{thm:equicontinuous-sequence-uniform-lattice-route}
Every compact-uniformity consumer of an accepted $\EquicontinuousSequenceUp$ carrier reads the same displayed rows as the shared-modulus route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformly-continuous-sequence-namecert}: the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, $\ContinuousMapUp$ sequence-entry row, $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, $\EquicontinuityUp$ common schedule, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The $\UniformlyContinuousSequenceUp$ sibling supplies the shared-modulus boundary, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} fix the compact, rational, and real-facing rows. Thus the route exports a finite compact-uniform sequence lattice, not a host function-space compactness theorem, quotient equality of maps, selected subsequence, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:equicontinuous-sequence-carrier}. Its compact-uniformity data are the compact metric row $K$, displayed map row $F$, finite stream-window row $S$, shared schedule $W$, equicontinuity row $Q$, regular readback row $R$, finite-net selector $T$, and Arzela--Ascoli handoff $A$.

The $\UniformlyContinuousSequenceUp$ route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformly-continuous-sequence-namecert} names the sibling boundary where a sequence of displayed maps shares one modulus row. The present packet reads that boundary through $W,Q$ over the same $K,F,S$ window, so a compact-uniformity consumer cannot detach the modulus from the compact source or from the inspected sequence entries.

Finally, the compact source, rational readback, and real seal remain anchored in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite route, and none of those rows adds a host function space, quotient equality, selected subsequence, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{proof}
