\chapter{Zero-Dimensional Compact Space NameCert Construction}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-zero-dimensional-compact-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Engelking and Stone, the $\ZeroDimensionalCompactSpaceUp$ packet records the compact zero-dimensional interface as a finite clopen-basis carrier rather than as a host point-set primitive. It consumes the Stone-space route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stone-space-namecert}, the topology rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, and the clopen algebra ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-clopen-algebra-namecert}. The compact Hausdorff readback is the displayed $\CompactHausdorffUp$ row already exposed by the Stone-space carrier, so the packet is a compact zero-dimensional surface over existing BEDC rows, not a duplicate Stone-space construction.

\begin{definition}[Zero-dimensional compact space carrier]
\label{def:zero-dimensional-compact-space-carrier}
A $\ZeroDimensionalCompactSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(S,T,K,L,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StoneSpaceUp$ source row. It supplies the Boolean-to-clopen route and the compact Hausdorff readback already certified by the Stone-space packet.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ public-open row read from the same source. It exposes only the indexed opens generated by the displayed clopen ledger.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactHausdorffUp$ compact separation row. It is consumed as a readback obligation over $S$ and $T$, not as a host compactification theorem.
\item $L$ is the $\ClopenAlgebraUp$ ledger of clopen rows whose finite Boolean operations generate the public-open basis.
\item $B$ is the zero-dimensional basis row. It records that every accepted public neighbourhood request is answered by a displayed clopen row from $L$ before it reaches $T$ or $K$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Stone source, topology row, compact Hausdorff row, clopen ledger, basis row, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the route
$$
S,L,B \longmapsto T,K .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ZeroDimensionalCompactSpaceUp$, $\StoneSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\CompactHausdorffUp$, $\ClopenAlgebraUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ZeroDimensionalCompactSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,T,K,L,B,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for selected ultrafilters, arbitrary host opens, quotient topology equality, open-cover compactness, selected clopen refinements, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Zero-dimensional compact space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:zero-dimensional-compact-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ZeroDimensionalCompactSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
Z=(S,T,K,L,B,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ZeroDimensionalCompactSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of Stone-space source exposure through $S$, topology public-open exposure through $T$, compact Hausdorff readback through $K$, clopen-algebra ledger exposure through $L$, clopen-basis response through $B$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Every compact-topology consumer receives the zero-dimensional compact interface only through those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:zero-dimensional-compact-space-carrier}. The packet exposes exactly the Stone source, topology row, compact Hausdorff readback, clopen ledger, basis row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row.

The Stone-space source supplies the Boolean-to-clopen route, while $L$ and $B$ keep the clopen generator visible before any public topology read is exported. Thus a consumer cannot replace the basis row by a host open set or by an unrecorded clopen selector.

The compact Hausdorff row is read only as $K$ over the same displayed topology row $T$. Since the carrier has no selected ultrafilter, arbitrary-open completion, quotient topology equality, selected refinement, or bridge witness coordinate, those objects remain outside the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Zero-dimensional compact space clopen-basis stability]
\label{thm:zero-dimensional-compact-space-clopen-basis-stability}
Every transported or replayed $\ZeroDimensionalCompactSpaceUp$ public-open request preserves the same clopen-basis route
$$
\StoneSpaceUp \longmapsto \ClopenAlgebraUp \longmapsto \TopologyUp .
$$
If two accepted packets are compared by $H$ or replayed by $C$, the basis row $B$ still answers each public neighbourhood request through the same clopen ledger $L$ before the compact Hausdorff row $K$ is consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a public-open request in an accepted carrier. The carrier forces the request to pass through $B$, and $B$ names only clopen rows from $L$ as its basis responses.

Componentwise $\hsame$ transport preserves the displayed rows $S,T,K,L,B$. Displayed $\Cont$ replay uses the same route $S,L,B \longmapsto T,K$, so replay cannot insert an arbitrary open, a selected refinement, or a quotient-topology witness.

Therefore every transported or replayed request still has the displayed clopen-basis origin before it is consumed by the compact Hausdorff readback.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Zero-dimensional compact space compact Hausdorff route]
\label{thm:zero-dimensional-compact-space-compact-hausdorff-route}
Every compact-facing read of an accepted $\ZeroDimensionalCompactSpaceUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\StoneSpaceUp,\ \ClopenAlgebraUp,\ \TopologyUp,\ \CompactHausdorffUp,\ H,C,P,N .
$$
The route exports compact zero-dimensional structure as displayed clopen-basis and compact Hausdorff rows; it exports no host compact space, open-cover compactness proof, selected subcover, ultrafilter choice, quotient topology equality, or checked standard bridge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The compact-facing read first exposes the Stone-space source row $S$. The clopen algebra ledger $L$ and basis row $B$ then determine the public topology row $T$ before any compact Hausdorff readback is available.

The compact readback is exactly $K$. It is tied to the same $S,T,L,B$ rows by componentwise transport and displayed replay, and $P,N$ record only that finite package and local name.

Since the accepted carrier has no field for host compact spaces, open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, ultrafilter choice, quotient topology equality, or a standard bridge, the displayed route exhausts the compact Hausdorff handoff.
\end{proof}
