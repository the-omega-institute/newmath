\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BoundedCauchyFilterUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-cauchy-filter-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Bishop--Bridges on Cauchy filters and bounded filters, the $\BoundedCauchyFilterUp$ packet records the bounded source surface used by the L10 completion corridor. It sits between the Cauchy-filter carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfilter-namecert}, the bounded base face of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-filterbase-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the completion handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-cauchy-filter-completion-namecert}. The packet exposes bounded Cauchy-filter source data only as finite NameCert rows; it is not a selected limit, quotient filter equality, countable-choice selector, or ambient completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Bounded Cauchy filter carrier]
\label{def:bounded-cauchy-filter-carrier}
A $\BoundedCauchyFilterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(F,B,D,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ source row whose requests and meets are read as displayed Cauchy-filter data.
\item $B$ is the bounded $\FilterBaseUp$ face naming the finite base requests and the bound certificate used by every source read.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row attached to bounded filter requests.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window family opened from the bounded Cauchy-filter source.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the stream windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row exposed only after $F,B_0,D,S,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Cauchy-filter source, bounded base face, dyadic tolerance, stream window, regular rational readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same bounded source route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BoundedCauchyFilterUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\FilterBaseUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedCauchyFilterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports no coordinate for selected limits, quotient filter equality, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded Cauchy filter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bounded-cauchy-filter-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted carrier $X=(F,B,D,S,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$ of \autoref{def:bounded-cauchy-filter-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedCauchyFilterUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, $\CauchyFilterUp$ source admission, bounded $\FilterBaseUp$ face admission, dyadic tolerance accounting, finite $\StreamNameUp$ window exposure, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal nonescape, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The Cauchy-filter source row is $F$, the bounded base face is $B$, and the dyadic tolerance ledger is $D$.

The route can open a finite window only after those source rows have been displayed. The window row $S$ then determines the regular rational readback row $Q$ available to Real-facing source consumers.

The seal row $R$ is downstream of $F,B_0,D,S,Q$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are exhausted by $H,C,P,N$, so the listed rows are precisely the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded Cauchy filter L10 handoff]
\label{thm:bounded-cauchy-filter-l10-handoff}
Every L10 completion consumer of an accepted $\BoundedCauchyFilterUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\CauchyFilterUp \longmapsto \FilterBaseUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp \longmapsto \BoundedCauchyFilterCompletionUp .
$$
The handoff exports only the displayed source, bounded-base, tolerance, window, readback, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an L10 completion consumer of $X$. By \autoref{thm:bounded-cauchy-filter-namecert-obligations}, the consumer cannot reach the completion-facing source route until it has read the Cauchy-filter source $F$, the bounded base face $B$, and the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$.

The finite readback is then forced through $S,Q,R$. These rows are the displayed stream schedule, regular rational readback, and Real seal, so the bounded completion packet receives only the bounded Cauchy-filter source route.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ replay and name that same route. Since the carrier contains no selected limit, quotient-filter equality, host real equality, countable-choice selector, or ambient completeness witness, no other L10 handoff is available.
\end{proof}
