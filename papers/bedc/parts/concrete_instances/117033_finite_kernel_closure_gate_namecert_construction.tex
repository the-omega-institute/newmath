The $\FiniteKernelClosureGateUp$ packet concretizes the closure-admission discipline of \autoref{ch:visions-theory-stability-taste-gate}. It binds a candidate kernel surface to the finite rows that decide whether an AI-origin chapter may report closure: a TasteGate admission row, a closure-status reference row, an axiom-purity audit row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. The packet is a gate over displayed evidence, not a semantic approval oracle, not a proof of the candidate theorem, and not a bridge checker.

The finite gate consumes the admission row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tastegateadmission-namecert}, the closure-ledger row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closure-status-reference-namecert}, and the dependency-purity row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-axiom-purity-gate-namecert} before a closure-admission decision is visible. Those siblings supply the displayed evidence read by $A,S,Q$; this packet only binds them into the finite kernel gate surface.

\begin{definition}[Finite kernel closure gate carrier]
\label{def:finite-kernel-closure-gate}
A $\FiniteKernelClosureGateUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(X,A,S,Q,F,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the candidate kernel-surface row, recording the chapter label, origin row, finite carrier surface, and requested closure grade.
\item $A$ is the consumed $\TasteGateAdmissionUp$ row, exposing the candidate's ChapterTasteGate admission evidence and refused-case ledger.
\item $S$ is the consumed $\ClosureStatusReferenceUp$ row, recording the current theory-closure grade, formal-status grade, bridge status, not-claimed surface, and next-grade route.
\item $Q$ is the consumed $\AxiomPurityGateUp$ row, recording the finite dependency audit and forbidden-dependency refusal surface for the candidate target.
\item $F$ is the field ledger for the gate itself, listing which visible fields of $X,A,S,Q$ are read by closure admission and which fields remain outside the gate.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,A,S,Q,F$ and for replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route from candidate surface through admission evidence, closure-status reference, axiom-purity audit, and field ledger.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteKernelClosureGateUp$, $\TasteGateAdmissionUp$, $\ClosureStatusReferenceUp$, $\AxiomPurityGateUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteKernelClosureGateUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $X,A,S,Q,F,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for hidden prompt state, host-side approval, theorem truth, bridge checking, global horizon admission, or a closure grade stronger than the displayed status row.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite kernel closure gate NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-kernel-closure-gate-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteKernelClosureGateUp$ carrier
$$
K=(X,A,S,Q,F,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteKernelClosureGateUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed packet; classifier stability under componentwise transport of $X,A,S,Q,F,H,C,P,N$; TasteGate-admission exposure through $A$; closure-status reference exposure through $S$; axiom-purity exposure through $Q$; field-ledger exactness through $F$; displayed replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; local naming through $N$; and nonescape for hidden prompt state, host-side approval, theorem truth, bridge checking, and unreported closure strength.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-kernel-closure-gate}. The finite packet exposes exactly the candidate surface, admission row, closure-status row, axiom-purity row, field ledger, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row.

The admission, status, and audit obligations are read from $A,S,Q$. The row $A$ supplies the TasteGate admission surface, $S$ supplies the two-axis closure-status reference, and $Q$ supplies the forbidden-dependency audit surface.

The field ledger $F$ records which visible fields the gate reads. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve those fields componentwise, while $P$ and $N$ record source and local naming.

Since the carrier has no hidden prompt, host approval, theorem-truth, bridge-checking, global-admission, or stronger-status coordinate, no consumer can extract one from the accepted gate packet.
\end{proof}
