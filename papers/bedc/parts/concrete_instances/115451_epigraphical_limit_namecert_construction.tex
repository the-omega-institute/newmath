\begin{definition}[Epigraphical limit carrier]
\label{def:epigraphical-limit-carrier}
An $\EpigraphicalLimitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(E,G,M,X,R,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the $\EpigraphUp$ source row, recording inspected epigraph windows, threshold cuts, and lower-set readbacks for the functional family under comparison.
\item $G$ is the $\GammaConvergenceUp$ recovery-window row, recording the liminf and recovery requests that are read through the same epigraph windows.
\item $M$ is the $\MoscoConvergenceUp$ weak-strong boundary row, recording the weak lower-bound side and the strong recovery side exposed to the packet.
\item $X$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, recording the displayed distance windows through which epigraph neighborhoods and recovery approximants are compared.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ scalar seal for functional values, thresholds, tolerance budgets, and terminal comparison rows.
\item $W$ is the finite epigraph-window ledger that binds each displayed threshold and metric request to the corresponding Gamma or Mosco read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $E,G,M,X,R,W$ and the local certificate rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay along the route
$$
E,G,M,X,R,W \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EpigraphicalLimitUp$, $\EpigraphUp$, $\GammaConvergenceUp$, $\MoscoConvergenceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EpigraphicalLimitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $E,G,M,X,R,W,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host epi-limit theorem, selected minimizers, quotient equality of functionals, unbounded compactness, countable choice, or ambient completion of the metric or Real carriers.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Epigraphical limit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:epigraphical-limit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EpigraphicalLimitUp$ carrier $L=(E,G,M,X,R,W,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\EpigraphicalLimitUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, epigraph-window exposure through $E$, Gamma recovery-window exposure through $G$, Mosco weak-strong boundary exposure through $M$, metric-window comparison through $X$, Real threshold sealing through $R$, finite window-ledger exactness through $W$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:epigraphical-limit-carrier}. The rows $E,G,M,X,R,W$ are the only places where epigraph windows, convergence requests, metric comparisons, scalar thresholds, and finite ledger data may be read.

The Gamma and Mosco rows are tied to the same finite epigraph ledger. Row $G$ can expose recovery and liminf requests only after $E$ and $W$ have displayed the relevant windows, while row $M$ can expose its weak-strong boundary only through the same metric and Real rows $X,R$.

Finally attach $H,C,P,N$. Transport and replay preserve the displayed rows componentwise, provenance names the sibling sources, and local naming adds no off-packet coordinate. Thus the listed obligations exhaust the NameCert surface and cannot be widened to selected minimizers, quotient functional equality, compactness by choice, or host epigraphical-limit theorems.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:epigraphical-limit-gamma-mosco-boundary}
