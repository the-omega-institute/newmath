The $\RingUp$ carrier consumes the additive route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-abgroup-namecert}, and the multiplicative route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-semigroup-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-monoid-namecert}, before distributivity becomes public. Thus the ring certificate is read as the common-carrier lattice joining additive group structure, additive commutativity, multiplicative associativity, multiplicative unit laws, and the two distributive rows, rather than as an isolated algebraic primitive.
