\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedCauchyLimitUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-located-cauchy-limit-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\LocatedCauchyLimitUp$ packet records the finite located-limit boundary that sits between regular Cauchy readback and the terminal real-name seal. It reads StreamName windows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, RegSeqRat readback from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, dyadic tolerance ledgers from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the terminal $\RealUp$ seal from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and uniqueness discipline from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-limit-uniqueness-namecert}. The packet names a located candidate-limit route; it does not assert quotient completion, selected representatives, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[LocatedCauchyLimit carrier]
\label{def:located-cauchy-limit-carrier}
A $\LocatedCauchyLimitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(S,R,D,T,L_0,U,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row for the Cauchy source stream.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row extracted from the displayed windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare requested precision with the displayed rational windows.
\item $T$ is the located tail schedule, recording the finite threshold and neighbourhood data that make the candidate limit inspectable.
\item $L_0$ is the candidate-limit row consumed by the terminal real-name seal.
\item $U$ is the uniqueness boundary, read through $\RegularCauchyLimitUniquenessUp$ before any two candidate-limit rows are classified together.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the displayed candidate limit.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all displayed rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from stream windows through readback, tolerance, located tail schedule, candidate limit, uniqueness boundary, real seal, provenance, and naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedCauchyLimitUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegularCauchyLimitUniquenessUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedCauchyLimitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient limits, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, host real equality, arbitrary completion objects, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedCauchyLimit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-cauchy-limit-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\LocatedCauchyLimitUp$ carrier $L=(S,R,D,T,L_0,U,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedCauchyLimitUp}$ surface consists exactly of StreamName window exposure, RegSeqRat readback exposure, DyadicRatCore tolerance comparison, located tail schedule admission, candidate-limit row admission, uniqueness-boundary routing, terminal RealUp sealing, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. No obligation is stored outside $S,R,D,T,L_0,U,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic rows are $S,R,D,T,L_0,U,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The source route is forced by the carrier order: stream windows are read by $S$, regular rational data by $R$, and dyadic tolerance comparison by $D$. The located tail schedule $T$ is the only row that can expose the neighbourhood threshold needed by the candidate-limit row $L_0$.

The uniqueness row $U$ and the terminal seal $E$ are downstream of the candidate-limit row. Transport, replay, provenance, and naming preserve exactly this finite route.

Since the excluded objects have no carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedCauchyLimit RegSeqRat route]
\label{thm:located-cauchy-limit-regseqrat-route}
Every accepted $\LocatedCauchyLimitUp$ read reaches a candidate-limit row only through the finite route
$$
\StreamNameUp(S) \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp(R) \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp(D) \longmapsto T \longmapsto L_0.
$$
The route then passes through the uniqueness boundary $U$ and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ before any consumer reads $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read $S$ first. It is the only stream-window source in the carrier, so a regular rational readback cannot bypass it.

The row $R$ is then compared against the requested precision by the dyadic ledger $D$. The located schedule $T$ records the finite tail threshold that lets the candidate-limit row $L_0$ be inspected.

The remaining analytic rows $U$ and $E$ place uniqueness and terminal real-name sealing after the candidate-limit read. The structural rows preserve the same route and add no second source.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedCauchyLimit nonescape]
\label{thm:located-cauchy-limit-nonescape}
No accepted $\LocatedCauchyLimitUp$ packet exports quotient completion, selected representatives, countable choice, host real equality, arbitrary completion data, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness. Every completion-facing consumer receives only $S,R,D,T,L_0,U,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:located-cauchy-limit-namecert-obligations}. It identifies the full local obligation surface as the displayed finite rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:located-cauchy-limit-regseqrat-route}. It orders the analytic source rows before the candidate-limit, uniqueness, and RealUp seal rows.

The carrier definition has no field for the excluded objects. Thus transport, replay, provenance, and naming cannot synthesize them for a completion-facing consumer.
\end{proof}
