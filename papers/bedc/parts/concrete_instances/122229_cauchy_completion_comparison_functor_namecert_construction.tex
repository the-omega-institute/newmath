\label{def:cauchy-completion-comparison-functor-carrier}

$$

\item $K$ is the $\CauchyCompletionKleisliCategoryUp$ source row, displaying completion objects, unit arrows, Kleisli arrows, bind composition, and its real-completion handoff.

\item $F$ is the displayed $\FunctorUp$ comparison row from $K$ to $E$.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,K,E,B,F,A,T,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion, host equality of completed objects, choice-selected representatives, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion comparison functor NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-comparison-functor-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompletionComparisonFunctorUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionComparisonFunctorUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier exposure, completion-monad exposure, Kleisli source exposure, Eilenberg--Moore target exposure, base-category exposure, comparison-functor exposure, naturality and monad-compatibility exposure, L10 readback exposure, comparison-ledger exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-comparison-functor-carrier}. The monad row $M$, the Kleisli row $K$, the Eilenberg--Moore row $E$, and the base row $B$ are displayed before any comparison can be consumed.

The functor row $F$ and naturality row $A$ then record the comparison itself. They read the unit and bind schedules from $M$, the source composition route from $K$, and the algebra-law route from $E$.

The L10 readback row $T$ keeps every completion-facing request on the displayed $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ route. The ledger $L$ records that object, arrow, unit, bind, and algebra-law comparisons are all made through those visible rows.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier contains no quotient-completion, host-equality, representative-choice, or ambient-completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion comparison functor handoff]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-comparison-functor-handoff}

$$

$$
The consumer may inspect the displayed unit, bind, Kleisli composition, algebra structure, functorial action, and naturality rows, but it cannot read a quotient completion, host equality, choice-selected representative, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem from the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the completion monad row $M$. It supplies the unit and bind schedules together with finite stream windows, regular-rational readback, dyadic tolerance rows, and the terminal Real seal route.

Read the source and target category rows next. The Kleisli row $K$ exposes objects, arrows, and bind composition, while the Eilenberg--Moore row $E$ exposes algebra objects, structure maps, law rows, homomorphisms, and composition ledgers over the same base row $B$.

The comparison rows $F$ and $A$ are then available as displayed functor and naturality data. Their consumer route is recorded by $L$ and must pass through the L10 readback row $T$ before any Real-facing seal is consumed.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ keep the handoff inside the displayed packet. Because the excluded objects are absent from the carrier inventory, no consumer can obtain them from the comparison functor route.
