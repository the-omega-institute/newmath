\begin{theorem}[Pringsheim-Tauberian NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:pringsheim-tauberian-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PringsheimTauberianUp$ carrier $Q=(S,C,A,B,T,M,R,E,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\PringsheimTauberianUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for that twelve-row packet, positive-series exposure through $S$, Pringsheim convergence exposure through $C$, Abel summation exposure through $A$, Abel boundary exposure through $B$, Tauberian side exposure through $T$, comparison exposure through $M$, regular rational readback through $R$, Real sealing through $E$, and transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,K,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:pringsheim-tauberian-carrier}. Its analytic rows are precisely $S,C,A,B,T,M,R,E$, and its structural rows are precisely $H,K,P,N$.

Read the source rows first. The positive-series row $S$ supplies the coefficients and finite partial sums, while $C$ supplies the ordinary Pringsheim-facing convergence readback. The Abel row $A$ and boundary row $B$ then expose only radial and summation-by-parts data for the same source.

Next inspect the Tauberian rows. The side row $T$ and comparison row $M$ are the only coordinates that relate the Abel boundary to the ordinary readback, and $R,E$ are the only endpoint coordinates. Componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming therefore preserve the same finite surface and supply no hidden host theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Pringsheim-Tauberian Abel handoff]
\label{thm:pringsheim-tauberian-abel-handoff}

$$
S,C \longmapsto A,B \longmapsto T,M \longmapsto R,E \longmapsto H,K,P,N .
$$
Thus an Abel-facing read becomes an ordinary positive-series read only after the displayed Tauberian side row and comparison row have been replayed through the same $\hsame$ and $\Cont$ surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the positive source and ordinary convergence rows $S,C$. They display the partial-sum window and the Pringsheim-facing readback that any endpoint consumer must cite.

Then apply the Abel summation and Hardy--Littlewood boundary routes. The rows $A,B$ expose Abel data, but they do not by themselves export ordinary convergence; they are boundary data for the Tauberian stage.

Finally use the Tauberian side row $T$ and comparison row $M$. These rows connect the Abel boundary to the regular rational readback $R$ and terminal Real seal $E$, while $H,K,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Hence the endpoint cannot be obtained from quotient series equality, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
