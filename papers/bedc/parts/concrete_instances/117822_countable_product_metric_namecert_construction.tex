Following Bourbaki and Kelley on product uniformities and Bishop--Bridges constructive metric practice, the $\CountableProductMetricUp$ packet records the finite-prefix product metric used when certified metric streams are read by Real and Completion consumers. It sits over the finite schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the metric carrier surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the finite-prefix weighted route only; it is not a host countable product, a selected family of points, a quotient completion, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Countable product metric carrier]
\label{def:countable-product-metric-carrier}
A $\CountableProductMetricUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(S,M,D,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-prefix schedule. It records which coordinate windows of the certified stream are visible to the consumer.
\item $M$ is the finite family of $\MetricSpaceUp$ rows indexed by the visible prefix of $S$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ weighted finite-prefix tolerance ledger. It records coordinate weights, requested coordinate tolerances, the finite product-radius budget, and the comparison allowance used by the packet.
\item $W$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ to $\RealUp$ seal handoff that reads the weighted finite-prefix sum as a regular rational name before any terminal real seal is consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream schedule, metric rows, dyadic ledger, regular-real handoff, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a product-metric request through $S,M,D,W$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CountableProductMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CountableProductMetricUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,M,D,W,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host countable products, a choice-selected stream of points, infinite sums, quotient completion equality, detached real carriers, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Countable product metric NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:countable-product-metric-namecert-surface}
For every accepted $\CountableProductMetricUp$ carrier $Q=(S,M,D,W,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CountableProductMetricUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite-prefix schedule admission, metric-row admission for the visible coordinates, weighted dyadic tolerance accounting, regular-rational readback, terminal Real seal handoff, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, local naming, and refusal of host countable products, choice-selected point streams, infinite sums, quotient completion equality, detached real carriers, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, and bridge checking.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:countable-product-metric-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the finite schedule $S$, the metric rows $M$ over the visible prefix, the dyadic ledger $D$, the regular-real handoff $W$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

The finite-prefix route reads $S$ before it reads $M$ or $D$. Thus the product metric read is bounded by the displayed prefix and cannot import a host countable product or a choice-selected infinite point stream.

The weighted ledger $D$ is then read before $W$. It supplies the coordinate weights, coordinate tolerances, and product-radius budget that the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback turns into the terminal $\RealUp$ seal handoff.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no carrier coordinate stores infinite sums, quotient completion equality, detached real carriers, ambient Real completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking, the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Countable product metric finite-prefix stability]
\label{thm:countable-product-metric-finite-prefix-stability}
Let $Q=(S,M,D,W,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CountableProductMetricUp$ packet. If $Q'=(S',M',D',W',H',C',P',N')$ is obtained by componentwise $\hsame$ transport of the finite schedule, visible metric rows, weighted dyadic ledger, regular-real handoff, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows, then $Q'$ preserves the same finite-prefix product metric read. The transported packet may rename the displayed prefix and rows, but it may not add an unseen coordinate, an infinite product metric, quotient completion equality, a selected limit, or an ambient $\RealUp$ completeness witness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with carrier admission for $Q$. The finite schedule $S$ determines exactly which coordinate metric rows can be inspected, and the ledger $D$ is attached to that same prefix.

Apply componentwise $\hsame$ transport. The transported schedule $S'$ names the same visible prefix up to the history classifier, while $M'$ and $D'$ are the transported metric rows and weighted ledger over that prefix.

Replay the displayed continuation. The row $C'$ reads the transported route through $S',M',D',W'$ in the same order, so the regular-real handoff preserves the product metric value exposed by the finite prefix.

The exclusions follow from the carrier shape. No coordinate of \autoref{def:countable-product-metric-carrier} stores unseen coordinates, infinite product metrics, quotient completion equality, selected limits, or ambient Real completeness, and transport cannot create rows absent from the packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Countable product metric Cauchy consumer route]
\label{thm:countable-product-metric-cauchy-consumer-route}
Every $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ completion consumer of an accepted $\CountableProductMetricUp$ packet must first expose the finite-prefix weighted ledger $D$ together with the schedule $S$ and metric rows $M$ before reading the handoff row $W$. The Cauchy-facing seal is therefore a routed finite-prefix weighted read, not a host completion of a countable product, a quotient of streams, a selected limit, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a completion-facing read of $Q=(S,M,D,W,H,C,P,N)$. Carrier admission makes the schedule and visible metric rows available before any regular-rational or Real-facing seal can be read.

The dyadic ledger $D$ records the weights and tolerance budgets for that displayed prefix. A consumer that omits $D$ has no certified product-radius budget and therefore has no $\CountableProductMetricUp$ metric read to pass to $\RegSeqRatUp$.

Now read the handoff row $W$. It turns the finite weighted ledger into the regular rational route and then into the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, while $H,C,P,N$ keep the readback tied to the same displayed rows.

Thus every Cauchy or Real completion consumer factors through $S,M,D,W,H,C,P,N$ in order. The packet exports only that finite-prefix weighted route and none of the host product, quotient, selected-limit, choice, or ambient-completeness exits.
\end{proof}
