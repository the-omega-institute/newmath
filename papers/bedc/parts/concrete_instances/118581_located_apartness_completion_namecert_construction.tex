\begin{definition}[Located apartness completion carrier]
\label{def:located-apartness-completion-carrier}
A $\LocatedApartnessCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(A,B,U,V,G,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the $\ApartnessCompletionUp$ boundary row exposing the separated completion route.
\item $B$ is the $\LocatedApartnessRealBridgeUp$ row binding the completion boundary to located real apartness data.
\item $U$ is the source $\LocatedRealUp$ row whose finite windows are inspected by the completion read.
\item $V$ is the $\ApartnessRealUp$ positive-separation row used to keep equality information apartness-facing.
\item $G$ is the located gap row recording that the inspected endpoint windows have a displayed positive dyadic separation whenever apartness is consumed.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule row for the finite completion and apartness windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for positive gaps, radii, and comparison cells.
\item $E$ is the optional $\RealUp$ seal row, present only after the located, apartness, stream, regular-rational, and dyadic rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all source, bridge, window, readback, tolerance, seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedApartnessCompletionUp$, $\ApartnessCompletionUp$, $\LocatedApartnessRealBridgeUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\ApartnessRealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $A,B,U,V,G,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for Real completeness, trichotomy, quotient equality, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or host-selected limits.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located apartness completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-apartness-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedApartnessCompletionUp$ carrier $L=(A,B,U,V,G,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedApartnessCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of apartness-completion boundary exposure through $A$; located-apartness real bridge exposure through $B$; located real source exposure through $U$; positive-separation exposure through $V$; located gap exposure through $G$; finite window, regular readback, and dyadic tolerance exposure through $S,R,D$; optional Real sealing through $E$; componentwise transport through $H$; displayed replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The displayed tuple contains precisely the apartness-completion boundary, located-apartness bridge, located real source, positive-separation row, located gap row, finite windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, optional seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

The NameCert obligations are read in the same order. Rows $A$ and $B$ expose the completion boundary and bridge before $U,V,G$ make the located real and apartness evidence available. Rows $S,R,D$ then bind every consumer observation to finite windows, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance data before the optional Real seal $E$ can be inspected.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only those displayed rows. Since the carrier has no coordinate for Real completeness, trichotomy, quotient equality, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or host-selected limits, the listed rows exhaust the local obligation surface.
\end{proof}
