\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy equality-modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-equality-modulus-namecert-obligations}

The regular-equivalence handoff is the row $E$, and the classifier readback is the row $C$. Thus a Real-facing consumer reaches the classifier only after the source regular-Cauchy equivalence boundary has been displayed.

Finally the structural rows $H,K,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and locally name the same packet. Because the carrier omits host real equality, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, countable choice, selected limits, and ambient completeness, none of those principles can be exported by the local NameCert surface.
