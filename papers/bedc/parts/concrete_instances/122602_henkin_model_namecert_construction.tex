\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HenkinModelUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-henkin-model-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Henkin and the standard completeness proof tradition, the
$\HenkinModelUp$ packet reconstructs the witness construction as finite BEDC
NameCert data. It is a local consumer of the first-order syntax surface of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-firstorder-namecert} and the satisfaction
surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-modeltheory-namecert}; it does not
assert a host compactness theorem or a global completeness theorem.

\section{Finite Henkin packet}
\label{sec:henkin-model-finite-packet}

A carried $\HenkinModelUp$ row is a displayed $\mathsf{BHist}$ tuple
$(S,\Theta,C,W,T,M,\mathsf{Sat},\rho,\lambda)$. The row $S$ is the
$\FirstOrderUp$ signature and formula endpoint source. The row $\Theta$ is a
finite theory window, consisting only of the named formula endpoints currently
admitted by the packet. The row $C$ lists the Henkin constants exposed inside
that window, and the row $W$ records the witness obligations tying existential
formula endpoints to their displayed witness constants.

The row $T$ is the finite term-model table. Its entries are raw term endpoints
read through $S$, together with the congruence comparisons already visible in
the local packet. The row $M$ packages the term-model interpretation rows for
relation and function symbols over those endpoints. The row $\mathsf{Sat}$ is
the satisfaction ledger: each entry names a formula endpoint, an assignment or
closed-term environment endpoint, and the truth row read from the finite
Henkin window.

Transport is part of the carrier. The comparison ledger $\lambda$ records the
$\hsame$ and $\Cont$ rows that move the finite theory window, Henkin
constants, witness obligations, term-model rows, and satisfaction entries
between classifier-related packets. The provenance row $\rho$ records the
$\Pkg$ source of each displayed row, so a downstream consumer can see whether
a formula endpoint came from the first-order syntax certificate, a witness
extension row, or a term-model readback.

\section{Local certificate surface}
\label{sec:henkin-model-local-certificate}

The local $\NameCert_{\HenkinModelUp}$ accepts exactly the rows exposed by the
finite packet. A witness obligation is accepted when its existential formula
endpoint lies in $\Theta$, its witness constant lies in $C$, and its
instantiated formula endpoint is recorded in the same finite window. A
term-model row is accepted when every term endpoint and interpretation entry
is read through $S$, $T$, and $M$, with provenance still attached to $\rho$.
A satisfaction entry is accepted when it is one of the displayed
$\mathsf{Sat}$ rows and its formula endpoint is carried by the same
$\FirstOrderUp$ source.

Classifier stability is rowwise. If two $\HenkinModelUp$ packets are related
by the displayed comparison ledger, then the finite theory windows,
constants, witness rows, term-model entries, interpretation rows, satisfaction
entries, and provenance rows transport together. No comparison appeals to
ambient syntax equality, quotient equality on terms, or a chosen infinite
model outside the packet.

The certificate is deliberately local. It records how a finite Henkin window
can be named, transported, and consumed as a term-model witness surface. It
does not choose a completed infinite theory, take a quotient by a host
equivalence relation, invoke countable choice to select witnesses, or derive
the global model-existence theorem.
