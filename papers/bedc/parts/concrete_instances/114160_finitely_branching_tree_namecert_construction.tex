\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FinitelyBranchingTreeUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-finitely-branching-tree-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Koenig's lemma, Brouwer spreads, and Bishop-style finite evidence, the $\FinitelyBranchingTreeUp$ packet records a traditional finite-prefix tree as a BEDC NameCert carrier. It sits between the path-sensitive boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-konig-lemma-namecert}, the binary-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cantorspace-namecert}, and the bar-modulus route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert}. The packet exposes finite branching and prefix admission data for L10 countable-name and compactness consumers without selecting an infinite branch, asserting fan compactness, or turning a prefix ledger into host tree equality.

\begin{definition}[FinitelyBranchingTree carrier]
\label{def:finitely-branching-tree-carrier}
A $\FinitelyBranchingTreeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(P,B,A,W,K,F,S,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the finite prefix-row family. Each row stores a finite node code, its length, and the immediate predecessor readback used by prefix consumers.
\item $B$ is the branching ledger. For each admitted prefix row it displays a finite child bundle and the rows witnessing which child extensions remain in the tree.
\item $A$ is the admissibility row, saying that the root prefix is present and that every admitted non-root prefix has its predecessor admitted.
\item $W$ is the finite window row read by $\StreamNameUp$ and by Cantor-style binary or finite-alphabet probes before a path consumer can inspect longer prefixes.
\item $K$ is the $\KonigLemmaUp$ handoff row. It records the finite data needed by a Koenig-facing theorem without adding a selected infinite branch.
\item $F$ is the $\FanTheoremUp$ handoff row. It records finite bar and modulus requests only through displayed prefix bundles.
\item $S$ is the $\CantorSpaceUp$ and $\SpreadSpaceUp$ handoff row, used when binary cylinders or spread laws read the same prefix ledger.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for prefix rows, branching rows, admissibility, window reads, handoff rows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay from prefix admission to branching exposure, finite-window reads, CantorSpace and FanTheorem handoffs, KoenigLemma consumers, and StreamName-facing prefix observations.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FinitelyBranchingTreeUp$, $\KonigLemmaUp$, $\CantorSpaceUp$, $\FanTheoremUp$, $\SpreadSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FinitelyBranchingTreeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a chosen infinite branch, detached open-cover compactness, host tree equality, countable choice, bar induction, or a completed path space.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FinitelyBranchingTree NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finitely-branching-tree-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FinitelyBranchingTreeUp$ carrier $T=(P,B,A,W,K,F,S,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FinitelyBranchingTreeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed prefix rows, finite branching ledger, predecessor-closure row, finite window row, KoenigLemma handoff, FanTheorem handoff, CantorSpace and SpreadSpace handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every consumer must read finite prefix data through
$$
P\longmapsto B\longmapsto A\longmapsto W\longmapsto K,F,S\longmapsto H,C,Q,N .
$$
Thus the packet supplies finite branching evidence and its standard handoffs, not an infinite branch selector, a fan compactness theorem, an open-cover compactness principle, or an ambient path-completion object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows from \autoref{def:finitely-branching-tree-carrier}. The prefix family, branching ledger, predecessor-closure row, finite-window row, three handoff rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local name are exactly the displayed coordinates of the packet.

The handoff rows are read after prefix admission. A KoenigLemma consumer sees the finite branching ledger and predecessor closure before it can ask for a branch-facing conclusion; a FanTheorem consumer sees only finite bar and modulus requests over the same prefix rows; CantorSpace and SpreadSpace consumers read finite cylinders and spread-law windows through $S$.

Componentwise stability is forced by $H$. If an accepted replacement of $T$ is compared with another packet, the local classifier first transports $P,B,A,W,K,F,S,C,Q,N$ by $\hsame$ and then replays the same route through $C$.

Finally inspect the absent coordinates. Since the tuple has no row for a chosen infinite branch, detached open-cover compactness, host tree equality, countable choice, bar induction, or completed path space, the local NameCert cannot export any of those claims.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FinitelyBranchingTree finite-window handoffs]
\label{thm:finitely-branching-tree-finite-window-handoffs}
Let $T=(P,B,A,W,K,F,S,H,C,Q,N)$ be an accepted $\FinitelyBranchingTreeUp$ packet. Any downstream $\StreamNameUp$, $\CantorSpaceUp$, $\FanTheoremUp$, $\SpreadSpaceUp$, or $\KonigLemmaUp$ read of $T$ factors through the same finite prefix rows $P$, the same branching ledger $B$, and the same predecessor-closure row $A$ before a handoff row is visible.

Consequently the downstream read is stable under $\hsame$ transport and $\Cont$ replay of $H$ and $C$, and its provenance remains the finite $\Pkg$ row $Q$ with local naming by $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a downstream read. The carrier has no direct consumer coordinate, so the read must open the prefix rows $P$, the branching ledger $B$, and the predecessor-closure row $A$ before any finite window or theorem-facing handoff can be inspected.

The window row $W$ supplies the StreamName-facing finite prefix observation. The row $S$ repacks the same observations for CantorSpace and SpreadSpace cylinders, while $F$ and $K$ expose only the finite bar-modulus and KoenigLemma boundary rows tied to those prefixes.

Transport and replay do not add further data. The row $H$ moves all displayed rows componentwise by $\hsame$, $C$ replays the same finite route, and $Q,N$ attach provenance and local naming. The packet therefore gives handoff stability without selecting an infinite branch or asserting compactness beyond the displayed finite ledger.
\end{proof}
