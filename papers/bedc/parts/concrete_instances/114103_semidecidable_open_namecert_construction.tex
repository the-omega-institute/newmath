\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SemidecidableOpenUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-semidecidable-open-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges and the constructive-topology reading of observable opens, the $\SemidecidableOpenUp$ packet records positive open evidence as finite BEDC observation data. It sits over the finite StreamName windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the Sierpinski observable-open classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert}, and the indexed open-row discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}. The packet names the interface by which a positive open observation may be reported without importing host open sets, quotient topology, excluded middle over membership, selected witnesses, or ambient real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Semidecidable open carrier]
\label{def:semidecidable-open-carrier}
A $\SemidecidableOpenUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(S,T,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ classifier row, exposing the observable truth mark for a positive open report.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row, recording the indexed open under inspection as displayed topology data.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite window row, listing the observed request prefixes available to the open report.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, recording the regular rational witness data used when the open report is read by a real-name consumer.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, attaching the report to the displayed real-name boundary rather than to a host real.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for classifier, topology, window, rational readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from the positive observation request through $S,T,W,Q,R,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SemidecidableOpenUp$, $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SemidecidableOpenUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a negative open-membership decision, a host powerset open, quotient equality of opens, selected neighbourhood witnesses, countable choice, or a bridge credential.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Semidecidable open NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:semidecidable-open-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SemidecidableOpenUp$ carrier $O=(S,T,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SemidecidableOpenUp}$ surface consists exactly of Sierpinski classifier admission, Topology open-row admission, StreamName finite-window readback, RegSeqRat rational witness readback, Real seal attachment, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. A positive open consumer must read $S,T,W,Q,R$ before it consumes $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:semidecidable-open-carrier}. The rows $S$ and $T$ supply the observable classifier and indexed open source; neither row is a host proposition about arbitrary subsets.

The rows $W,Q,R$ make the positive evidence finite and real-facing. $W$ records the observed StreamName windows, $Q$ records the regular rational readback used by completion consumers, and $R$ seals that readback at the displayed $\RealUp$ boundary.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same displayed packet. Since the carrier has no negative-decision row, selected-neighbourhood row, host powerset row, quotient-open row, countable-choice row, or bridge credential, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Semidecidable open real observation route]
\label{thm:semidecidable-open-real-observation-route}
Every real-facing positive open read exported by an accepted $\SemidecidableOpenUp$ packet factors through the displayed route
$$
\SierpinskiSpaceUp \longrightarrow \TopologyUp \longrightarrow \StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \RealUp .
$$
Thus L10 Real and Completion consumers receive positive open evidence through finite observable windows and regular rational readback, not through host open sets, quotient topology, excluded middle over membership, selected witnesses, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read $O=(S,T,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$. The Sierpinski row $S$ classifies the positive observation before the topology row $T$ supplies the indexed open source.

The StreamName row $W$ then fixes the finite observation window. The RegSeqRat row $Q$ supplies the rational readback attached to that window, and the Real row $R$ seals the result at the displayed real-name boundary.

Transport, replay, provenance, and naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve this order. A real-facing consumer therefore receives only the finite positive-evidence route and cannot bypass it by importing a host open-set predicate, quotient topology, excluded middle, selected witnesses, or ambient real completeness.
\end{proof}
