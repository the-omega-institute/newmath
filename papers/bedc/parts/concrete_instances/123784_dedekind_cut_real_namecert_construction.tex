\begin{theorem}[Dedekind cut real NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dedekind-cut-real-namecert-obligations}

Any real-facing or completion-facing consumer must read those rows before treating the packet as Dedekind-cut real evidence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dedekind-cut-real-carrier}. The only cut data are the displayed lower and upper rows $L,U$, and the only located separation data are the finite gap witnesses in $G$.

The boundary-window and readback rows are downstream of those cut rows. A consumer first opens $B$, then reads regular rational data through $R$, and only then reaches the terminal seal row $E$ or the located-apartness boundary row $A$.

\end{proof}
