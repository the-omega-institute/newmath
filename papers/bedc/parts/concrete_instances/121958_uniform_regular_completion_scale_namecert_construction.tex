Following Bourbaki's uniform-space completion interface and Bishop-style regular Cauchy completion, the $\UniformRegularCompletionScaleUp$ packet records a finite tolerance-scale route from uniform entourages to regular rational names. It sits after the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the scale used by completion-facing consumers without quotienting Cauchy filters, selecting representatives, importing ambient completeness, or deciding equality of completed points.

\begin{definition}[Uniform regular completion scale]
\label{def:uniform-regular-completion-scale-carrier}
A $\UniformRegularCompletionScaleUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(E,D,W,R,T,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the finite uniform-entourage request ledger, indexed by the precision cells actually inspected by a consumer.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, carrying dyadic radii, slack conversions, and monotone refinement data for the inspected cells.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ pointwise certification row, exposing finite observations at the requested tolerance scale.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tail-stability row, reading the same observations as a regular rational Cauchy name.
\item $T$ is the scale-coherence ledger, requiring finer dyadic requests to replay through compatible stream windows and regular rational tails.
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ sealing row reached only after $D,W,R,T$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the uniform request ledger, dyadic tolerance row, stream windows, regular rational tail, scale-coherence ledger, terminal seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the ordered route $E,D,W,R,T,S$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformRegularCompletionScaleUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformRegularCompletionScaleUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for quotient completion, selected Cauchy representative, ambient uniform space equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform regular completion scale NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-regular-completion-scale-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformRegularCompletionScaleUp$ carrier
$$
U=(E,D,W,R,T,S,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UniformRegularCompletionScaleUp}$ obligations are exactly carrier admission for the displayed ten-row packet, dyadic tolerance-scale admission through $D$, pointwise stream certification through $W$, regular rational tail stability through $R$, coherence of finer requests through $T$, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $S$, and transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$. These obligations export a uniform regular completion scale only as finite BEDC data and do not assert quotient equality of completed points, a selected representative, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:uniform-regular-completion-scale-carrier}. The uniform request ledger, dyadic tolerance row, pointwise stream windows, regular rational tail, scale-coherence ledger, terminal seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name are explicit coordinates of $U$.

The tolerance route is dyadic. The row $D$ supplies the inspected radii and slack conversions, while $W$ reads only the finite stream observations licensed by those cells. Thus pointwise certification is a $\StreamNameUp$ read attached to a $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ scale, not a selected infinite representative.

Tail stability is then read through $R$ and $T$. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row supplies the regular rational Cauchy handoff, and the scale-coherence ledger forces finer requests to replay through compatible windows and tails before any terminal seal is available.

Finally $S$ exposes the $\RealUp$ boundary only after the ordered route $E,D,W,R,T$ has been displayed. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport and replay that same route, so the NameCert surface has no path to quotient completion, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient real completeness.
\end{proof}
