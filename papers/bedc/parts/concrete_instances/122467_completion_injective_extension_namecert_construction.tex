\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompletionInjectiveExtensionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-completion-injective-extension-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive completions and Kelley on separated completions, the $\CompletionInjectiveExtensionUp$ packet records the finite surface by which a densely defined map into a separated target has at most one completion extension. It sits after the dense-extension surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-dense-extension-namecert}, the extension-uniqueness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-extension-uniqueness-namecert}, the metric-completion dense embedding of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-completion-dense-embedding-namecert}, and the dense embedding carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dense-embedding-namecert}. The packet names injective extension determinacy only as finite NameCert data; it does not identify quotient completions, host functions, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[CompletionInjectiveExtension carrier]
\label{def:completion-injective-extension-carrier}
A $\CompletionInjectiveExtensionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(D,E,I,U,S,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the dense source row, reading the $\DenseEmbeddingUp$ and $\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$ data used to place the source inside its completion.
\item $E$ is the completion dense-extension row supplied by $\CompletionDenseExtensionUp$.
\item $I$ is the injective image-reflection row, recording that two extension reads with the same dense-source image have the same target-facing image row.
\item $U$ is the completion-extension uniqueness row supplied by $\CompletionExtensionUniquenessUp$ and the universal-property boundary of $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$.
\item $S$ is the separated target row, read through $\CompleteMetricUp$ only as the classifier needed to turn zero-distance target reads into a unique target-facing comparison.
\item $R$ is the StreamName, RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, and Real readback row used to expose the finite completion-facing comparison before the separated classifier consumes it.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for dense-source, dense-extension, injective-reflection, uniqueness, separated-target, readback, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from dense-source reads through the completion extension, image reflection, uniqueness route, separated classifier, and readback rows.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompletionInjectiveExtensionUp$, $\CompletionDenseExtensionUp$, $\CompletionExtensionUniquenessUp$, $\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$, $\DenseEmbeddingUp$, $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompletionInjectiveExtensionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier accepts exactly those finite packets whose injective-extension read can be replayed through $D,E,I,U,S,R,H,C,P,N$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompletionInjectiveExtension NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:completion-injective-extension-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompletionInjectiveExtensionUp$ carrier
$$
J=(D,E,I,U,S,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompletionInjectiveExtensionUp}$ surface consists exactly of dense-source admission for $D$, completion dense-extension admission for $E$, injective image-reflection exactness for $I$, completion-extension uniqueness for $U$, separated target classification for $S$, StreamName/RegSeqRat/DyadicRatCore/Real readback for $R$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The dense source row and dense-extension row give the only input surface from which an extension read can start.

Read the image-reflection and uniqueness rows next. The reflection row $I$ keeps image comparison internal to the displayed dense source, while $U$ supplies the completion-extension uniqueness boundary already named by the sibling chapter.

The target-facing comparison then passes through $R$ and $S$. The readback row exposes the finite completion comparison, and the separated target row is the only classifier allowed to turn it into a uniqueness read.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. No quotient completion equality, host function equality, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, or ambient Real completeness row occurs among these coordinates.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CompletionInjectiveExtension uniqueness route]
\label{thm:completion-injective-extension-uniqueness-route}
Every accepted injective-extension comparison exported by a $\CompletionInjectiveExtensionUp$ packet factors through the dense source row $D$, the completion dense-extension row $E$, the injective image-reflection row $I$, the completion-extension uniqueness row $U$, the StreamName/RegSeqRat/DyadicRatCore/Real readback row $R$, the separated target row $S$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. The route is therefore a finite completion-facing determinacy path, not an equality of host functions or quotient completion representatives.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:completion-injective-extension-carrier}. It lists $D,E,I,U,S,R,H,C,P,N$ as the only accepted coordinates.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:completion-injective-extension-namecert-obligations}. They force the comparison through the dense source, dense extension, image reflection, completion-extension uniqueness, readback, and separated-target rows before a consumer can read injective-extension determinacy.

The structural rows preserve that route. Since no host function row, quotient equality row, representative selector, choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle is present in the carrier, no downstream read can obtain one from the packet.
\end{proof}
