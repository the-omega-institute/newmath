\label{thm:critical-strip-oscillation-zero-window-nonescape}

Finally apply the exclusion ledger $E$. It refuses RH decisions, selected off-window zeros, global zero-free regions, unrestricted contour evaluation, and oracle routes, so the zero-window read cannot escape the finite oscillation packet.
