\begin{definition}[CompleteMetricFixedPoint carrier]
\label{def:complete-metric-fixed-point-carrier}
A $\CompleteMetricFixedPointUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ source row, including the metric carrier, distance readback, regular Cauchy limit service, and local complete-metric naming route.
\item $T$ is the $\ContractionMappingUp$ graph row, with a displayed self-map route, graph admission, rational contraction ledger, and strict unit-bound row.
\item $I$ is the $\PicardIterationUp$ finite orbit row generated from an initial point by repeated reads of $T$.
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyModulusUp$ tail budget extracted from the residual distances carried by $I$ and the contraction ledger of $T$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row reading the Picard orbit as a regular rational tail route before any endpoint-facing seal is consumed.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal boundary available only after $X,M,R$ have displayed the complete-metric limit request and the regular readback route.

The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for a host fixed-point object, quotient equality of functions, a countable-choice selector for iterates, or an ambient real-completeness oracle outside the carried $\CompleteMetricUp$ source.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompleteMetricFixedPoint NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:complete-metric-fixed-point-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompleteMetricFixedPointUp$ carrier $F=(X,T,I,M,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompleteMetricFixedPointUp}$ surface consists exactly of complete-metric source admission, contraction-graph admission, Picard-orbit visibility, Cauchy-modulus tail budgeting, $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff visibility, $\RealUp$ seal separation, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming for the carrier of \autoref{def:complete-metric-fixed-point-carrier}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:complete-metric-fixed-point-carrier}. The displayed tuple exposes $X,T,I,M,R,E,H,C,P,N$ before any endpoint-facing consumer may read the packet.

Use the sibling interfaces. The $\CompleteMetricUp$ row supplies the carried limit-service surface, the $\ContractionMappingUp$ row supplies the graph and contraction-bound data, and the $\PicardIterationUp$ row supplies the finite orbit and residual-distance ledger.

The rows $M,R,E$ form the completion-facing tail. The Cauchy-modulus row packages the finite tail budget, the regular-rational row packages the readback route, and the real seal remains downstream of both.

Finally attach $H,C,P,N$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same carrier, so the NameCert surface contains exactly the listed rows and none of the excluded host fixed-point, quotient, choice, or ambient-completeness coordinates.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the complete metric and contraction rows. The source row $X$ is the only complete-metric surface available to the packet, and the graph row $T$ is the only route for applying the contraction.

Read the Picard orbit next. The row $I$ is finite and is generated by repeated visible applications of $T$, so it records a displayed orbit rather than a chosen infinite sequence or a selected endpoint.
