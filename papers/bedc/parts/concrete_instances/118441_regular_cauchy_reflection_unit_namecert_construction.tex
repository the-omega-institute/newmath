$$

\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which the source sequence is inspected.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger bounding every inspected window.

\label{thm:regular-cauchy-reflection-unit-namecert-obligations}

The Real-seal nonescape follows from the carrier inventory. No row supplies quotient-stream equality, a selected representative, a selected limit, countable choice, host real equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, so none of these can be exported by the certificate.

Finally, $H$ and $C$ preserve and replay the same row order, and $P,N$ record its provenance and local name. Transport or replay therefore cannot replace the unit-law packet by any object outside the displayed finite surface.
\end{proof}
