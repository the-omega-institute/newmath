\label{thm:dyadic-cauchy-criterion-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The criterion row $T$ is therefore local to the displayed finite data. It says that a requested dyadic tolerance has a displayed window witness, not that a global stream quotient or a selected limit has been chosen.

The readback row $R$ is consumed through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. The equality-facing part of $E$ is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-equivalence-setoid-namecert}, and its terminal seal is read only through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}.
