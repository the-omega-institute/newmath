\begin{definition}[Prime-adic local slicing carrier]
\label{def:prime-adic-local-slicing-carrier}
A $\PrimeAdicLocalSlicingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(p,V,U,E,F,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $p$ is the $\PrimeUp$ row naming the local prime slice.
\item $V$ is the valuation-depth row, recording the displayed exponent direction in the $p$-adic slice.
\item $U$ is the unit-phase row, recording the local unit coordinate after the valuation depth has been separated.
\item $E$ is the Euler-local row for the centered factor $p^{1/2-s}$.
\item $F$ is the finite prime-window row in which this local slice is read.
\item $D$ is the normalized finite-window defect row, read from valuation depth and unit phase rather than from an ambient global zero predicate.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $p,V,U,E,F,D$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from prime admission through valuation depth, unit phase, Euler-local readback, finite-window defect, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PrimeAdicLocalSlicingUp$, $\PrimeUp$, $\PadicUp$, $\ZetaUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PrimeAdicLocalSlicingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a completed $p$-adic field, a $p$-adic zero predicate for $\ZetaUp$, a quotient local factor, an infinite prime product, or a global RH conclusion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Prime-adic local slicing NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:prime-adic-local-slicing-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PrimeAdicLocalSlicingUp$ carrier
$$
L=(p,V,U,E,F,D,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PrimeAdicLocalSlicingUp}$ surface consists exactly of prime-row admission through $p$, valuation-depth exposure through $V$, unit-coordinate exposure through $U$, Euler-local readback through $E$, finite-window admission through $F$, normalized defect readback through $D$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier to the tuple of \autoref{def:prime-adic-local-slicing-carrier}. The local analytic data are the prime row $p$, valuation row $V$, unit row $U$, Euler-local row $E$, finite-window row $F$, and defect row $D$.

The valuation and unit rows are separate. The row $V$ records depth in the local $p$-slice, while $U$ records the unit-phase readout after that depth is exposed. The Euler-local row $E$ may read both rows, but it cannot replace them with a global zero predicate.

The finite-window defect row $D$ is downstream of $p,V,U,E,F$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same dependency order. Since the carrier contains none of the excluded endpoints, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:prime-adic-local-slicing-finite-window-defect-route}

Removing $V$ leaves no carrier coordinate from which to recover the normalized local defect of \autoref{def:unified-reality-prime-adic-finite-window-defect}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the accepted carrier $L=(p,V,U,E,F,D,H,C,P,N)$. The only row that records radial local depth is $V$, and the unit row $U$ is explicitly downstream of separating that depth from the local slice.

The Euler-local row $E$ and finite-window row $F$ then provide the displayed context in which the defect row $D$ is read. By the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:prime-adic-local-slicing-namecert-obligations}, $D$ is not an independent coordinate; it is the readback of the local valuation/unit packet inside the finite window.

If $V$ is removed, the carrier still names a prime and may still display unit-phase or window rows, but it has no radial-depth data. Therefore the normalized finite-window defect cannot be recovered from the remaining rows.
\end{proof}
