\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RealCompletionReflectorUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-real-completion-reflector-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges and Kelley on metric completion, the $\RealCompletionReflectorUp$ packet names the finite boundary by which a displayed metric source reaches a Real-sealed completion reflector without quotienting streams, selecting limits, invoking trichotomy, or using countable choice. It sits after the minimality route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionminimality-namecert}, the reflector-unit packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-reflector-unit-namecert}, the metric-completion service of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the regular rational name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a reflector boundary for Real and Completion consumers; it is not Real completeness, a selected limit theorem, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, or a countable-choice principle.

\begin{definition}[Real completion reflector carrier]
\label{def:real-completion-reflector-carrier}
A $\RealCompletionReflectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(M,Q,E,U,F,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the source metric row, exposing the source carrier, source classifier, distance row, zero-distance, symmetry, triangle, and distance-transport ledgers read by the reflector.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ seal row. It records the regular rational readback route, the finite dyadic tolerance requests inherited from $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, the inspected $\StreamNameUp$ windows, and the terminal Real seal exposed to consumers.
\item $E$ is the completion object row, displaying the $\MetricCompletionUp$ object together with the minimality and reflector-unit references supplied by the sibling completion packets.
\item $U$ is the unit row from the source metric into the displayed completion object. It records only the finite graph of inspected source points and their completion names.
\item $F$ is the universal arrow row. For each inspected uniformly continuous source-to-complete-target route, it records the finite factorization through $U$ and the target's displayed Real-sealed completion readback.
\item $T$ is the transport row, carrying componentwise $\hsame$ comparison for the source metric, regular rational and Real seal, completion object, unit, factorization route, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $H$ records the reflector handoff to the existing $\CauchyCompletionMinimalityUp$, $\CauchyCompletionReflectorUnitUp$, and $\MetricCompletionUp$ packets.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from source metric inspection through $Q,E,U,F,T,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealCompletionReflectorUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyCompletionMinimalityUp$, $\CauchyCompletionReflectorUnitUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionReflectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,Q,E,U,F,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for Real completeness, selected limits, quotient streams, trichotomy, countable choice, or host equality of completion representatives.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion reflector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-completion-reflector-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealCompletionReflectorUp$ carrier
$$
R=(M,Q,E,U,F,T,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionReflectorUp}$ obligation surface consists exactly of source metric admission through $M$, regular rational and Real-seal readback through $Q$, completion-object exposure through $E$, unit exposure through $U$, universal-arrow factorization through $F$, componentwise transport through $T$, completion-packet handoff through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-completion-reflector-carrier}. The source metric data is confined to $M$, while the Real-facing readback is confined to $Q$ through displayed regular rational names, finite stream windows, dyadic tolerance requests, and the terminal seal.

The completion object and unit are the rows $E$ and $U$. Their meaning is read through the sibling minimality, reflector-unit, and metric-completion packets recorded in $H$, not through an ambient completion theorem.

The universal-arrow surface is exactly $F$. It records a finite factorization route for each inspected source-to-complete-target map after $M,Q,E,U$ have been displayed. The structural rows $T,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this same route.

Since the carrier contains no coordinate for Real completeness, selected limits, quotient streams, trichotomy, countable choice, or host equality of representatives, those principles cannot be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Universal arrow factorization]
\label{thm:real-completion-reflector-universal-arrow-factorization}
Let $R=(M,Q,E,U,F,T,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RealCompletionReflectorUp$ packet. Every completion-facing consumer that reads a uniformly continuous source route from $M$ to a displayed complete target must factor its read through the unit row $U$ and the universal-arrow row $F$ before it may inspect the Real-sealed completion row $Q$ or the completion handoff row $H$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:real-completion-reflector-namecert-obligations} force any consumer to expose the source metric row $M$ before a completion-facing route is available. The unit row $U$ is the only row that connects the source metric to the displayed completion object.

The factorization row $F$ is downstream of $U$. It stores the inspected arrow through the displayed completion object and no other coordinate stores such a route. The replay row $C$ and transport row $T$ preserve this same factorization under displayed continuity and componentwise $\hsame$ comparison.

The handoff row $H$ then records the relationship with the existing minimality, reflector-unit, and metric-completion packets. Thus every completion-facing read passes through $U$ and $F$ before reaching $Q$ or $H$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real-seal nonescape]
\label{thm:real-completion-reflector-real-seal-nonescape}
Every Real-facing consumer read exported by an accepted $\RealCompletionReflectorUp$ packet factors through the displayed route
$$
M,Q,E,U,F,T,H,C,P,N .
$$
The packet supplies a Real-sealed completion reflector boundary and exports no Real completeness theorem, no selected limit operation, no quotient stream equality, no trichotomy principle, and no countable-choice schedule.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The route begins at $M$ and $Q$. These rows expose the source metric and the regular rational, stream-window, dyadic-tolerance, and terminal Real-seal data that the consumer may inspect.

The completion object, unit, and universal-arrow rows $E,U,F$ then determine how the completion-facing read is routed. The handoff row $H$ connects this route to the existing completion packets without adding any additional analytic coordinate.

Finally $T,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Because Real completeness, selected limits, quotient streams, trichotomy, and countable choice are absent from the carrier, the Real-seal read cannot escape the displayed route.
\end{proof}
