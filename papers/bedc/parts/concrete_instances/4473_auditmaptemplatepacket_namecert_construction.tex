$$

\item $P$ is the positive row listing facts, carrier rows, or checked local inventory that the map may expose directly;
\item $C$ is the conditional row listing setup fields, routes, or certificate obligations that must remain explicit before their conclusions are read;
\item $O$ is the obstruction row naming blocked routes, missing inhabitants, shape restrictions, or no-go facts;
\item $F$ is the frontier row naming the strongest nearby claim visible to consumers but not supplied as closed content by the packet;
\item $S$ is the sibling-use row naming concrete audit-map neighbours, including the substitution audit map of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-substitutionauditmap-namecert}, that may consume or supply the displayed rows;

\item $K$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over \autoref{ch:audit-map-family-methodology}, the sibling concrete packets, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AuditMapTemplatePacketUp}$ row.

The classifier accepts exactly packets carrying these ten rows. It exports no proof of $F$, no erased obstruction, no discharged conditional row, no hidden sibling route, no closed MetaCIC theorem, and no host semantic equality coordinate.
