\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FittingIdealUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-fitting-ideal-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Eisenbud and Bourbaki, the $\FittingIdealUp$ packet records the finite presentation-matrix route by which a finitely presented module exposes its Fitting ideals through generated minor ledgers. The certificate reads rows for the chosen presentation, displayed matrix entries, finite minors, generated ideal handoff, module transport, componentwise $\hsame$, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ naming. It is not a host commutative-algebra oracle, a hidden determinant computation, or an equality principle for all presentations.

\begin{definition}[FittingIdeal carrier]
\label{def:fitting-ideal-carrier}
A $\FittingIdealUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(R,M,P,A,I,G,T,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the coefficient-ring row used only as displayed scalar data;
\item $M$ is the finitely presented module row;
\item $P$ is the finite presentation row $R^{m}\to R^{n}\to M$;
\item $A$ is the displayed presentation matrix with finite row and column ledgers;
\item $I$ is the generated-minor ledger, listing the minors of the required size and their finite determinant expansions;
\item $G$ is the ideal handoff row generated by the minor ledger $I$;
\item $T$ is the module-transport row recording how a permitted presentation change is read before the ideal handoff is reused;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for ring, module, presentation, matrix, minor, ideal, and transport rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from presentation rows to minors and from minors to the ideal handoff;
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over the algebraic rows, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FittingIdealUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an ambient ideal-equality oracle, a presentation-independent quotient proof outside $T,H,C,Q,N$, an infinite determinant family, or a host module category.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FittingIdeal minor-ledger obligations]
\label{thm:fitting-ideal-minor-ledger-obligations}
For every accepted $\FittingIdealUp$ carrier $F=(R,M,P,A,I,G,T,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FittingIdealUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite presentation exposure, generated-minor ledger exposure, ideal handoff through $G$, module transport through $T$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $Q$, and local naming through $N$. A public Fitting-ideal read must pass through those rows before it may cite the generated ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted packet. The ring, module, presentation, matrix, generated-minor ledger, ideal handoff, and module-transport rows are displayed coordinates of the finite carrier.

The generated ideal is read only from $G$ after the minor ledger $I$ has been exposed. The determinant expansions in $I$ are finite rows over the matrix $A$, so the handoff never invokes an unbounded determinant family.

Presentation transport is confined to $T,H,C,Q,N$. These rows record how the presentation change is transported, replayed, sourced, and named before the ideal handoff is reused by a consumer.

Since the carrier has no ambient ideal-equality oracle, no hidden presentation-independent quotient proof, and no host module category coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
