\begin{definition}[Bishop uniform-structure carrier]
\label{def:bishop-uniform-structure-carrier}
A $\BishopUniformStructureUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(U,E,F,R,C,D,S,Q,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ source row naming the finite entourage basis, diagonal requests, symmetry requests, and composition requests accepted by the uniform surface.
\item $E$ is the entourage-filter row, recording finite upward-closure and finite-intersection witnesses for the displayed entourage family.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ compatibility row for consumers that read Cauchy data through the entourage filter.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUniformStructureUp$ comparison row, used only when a Real-induced uniformity supplies the source entourages.
\item $C$ is the completion handoff row that names $\MetricCompletionUp$ or sibling completion consumers as readers of the already displayed uniform data.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger when the source uniformity is metric or Real-induced.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row inspected by sequential or completion-facing consumers.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the uniform, entourage-filter, Cauchy-filter, Real-uniform, completion, tolerance, window, readback, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopUniformStructureUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\RealUniformStructureUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopUniformStructureUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $U,E,F,R,C,D,S,Q,H,K,P,N$. The carrier has no coordinate for Real completeness, selected limits, quotient uniformities, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or quotient equality of completion points.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop uniform-structure NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-uniform-structure-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopUniformStructureUp$ carrier
$$
B=(U,E,F,R,C,D,S,Q,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopUniformStructureUp}$ surface consists exactly of uniform-source admission, entourage-filter exposure, Cauchy-filter compatibility, optional Real-induced comparison, completion handoff ordering, tolerance-ledger exposure, finite-window readback, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming through the rows of $B$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-uniform-structure-carrier}. The uniform data are exactly $U,E$: a finite entourage source and the filter witnesses that make those entourages usable by downstream requests.

The Cauchy and completion rows are downstream of that source. A Cauchy-filter consumer reads $F$ only after the entourage-filter row $E$ is displayed, and a completion consumer reads $C$ only as a named reader of the same route.

When the source is Real-induced, the comparison row $R$ and tolerance ledger $D$ supply the finite bridge to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-uniform-structure-namecert}. The window and readback rows $S,Q$ expose sequential observations only after the uniform route has been displayed.

The structural rows $H,K,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this finite route. Since no listed row stores Real completeness, selected limits, quotient uniformities, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or quotient equality of completion points, none of those objects can discharge a local NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop filter-entourage induction]
\label{thm:bishop-uniform-structure-filter-entourage-induction}
Let $B=(U,E,F,R,C,D,S,Q,H,K,P,N)$ be an accepted $\BishopUniformStructureUp$ carrier. Any finite induction over accepted entourage-filter requests preserves the route
$$
\UniformSpaceUp,\;E,\;\CauchyFilterUp
\longmapsto
\MetricCompletionUp
$$
and, when the source row is Real-induced, preserves the comparison route through $\RealUniformStructureUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, and $\RegSeqRatUp$. The induction exports no Real completeness, selected limit, quotient uniformity, open-cover compactness, or quotient equality of completion points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The base case is a single displayed entourage request. Carrier admission gives the request through $U$ and its filter witness through $E$, so the Cauchy row $F$ and the completion row $C$ can only read the already displayed uniform surface.

For upward closure, the accepted refinement is another row of the same entourage-filter packet $E$. Transport through $H$ and replay through $K$ preserve the source row $U$ and keep the Cauchy-filter handoff downstream of the displayed refinement.

For finite intersections, the induction step reads both prior requests from $E$ and records the intersected request as another finite filter witness. The handoff to $F$ and $C$ is therefore still a handoff from the same uniform source, not a new quotient uniformity.

If $R$ is present as the Real-induced comparison row, each inductive step also keeps the tolerance ledger $D$ and the finite observations $S,Q$ attached to the same route. Thus Real-uniform consumers receive only the displayed comparison and readback data.

No induction case creates a coordinate outside $U,E,F,R,C,D,S,Q,H,K,P,N$. Real completeness, selected limits, quotient uniformities, open-cover compactness, and quotient equality of completion points are absent throughout the induction.
\end{proof}
