\item $R_0$ and $R_1$ are the corresponding $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows exposed through $S_0$ and $S_1$.

\begin{theorem}[Regular sequence apartness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-sequence-apartness-namecert-obligations}

No NameCert obligation may replace the positive witness by quotient real equality, real trichotomy, selected representatives, hidden completion limits, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted packet. The carrier exposes exactly two finite stream windows, two regular-rational readbacks, the dyadic positive-tolerance witness, two witness-window records, the terminal Real seals, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

The witness row $D$ is read before either terminal Real seal can be consumed. Its positivity is displayed inside the dyadic ledger and is attached to inspected regular-rational approximants through $W_0$ and $W_1$.

Classifier stability follows because the classifier has coordinates only for the listed rows. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport preserves the same displayed windows, witness, seals, replay, provenance, and local naming data.

The $\Cont$ row replays the route from finite windows to readbacks, then to the positive witness and witness windows, and only then to the Real seals. The package and local NameCert rows source and name that route.

Since the packet has no coordinate for quotient real equality, trichotomy, representative selection, hidden limits, countable choice, or ambient completeness, every accepted consumer remains inside the displayed positive-separation route.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:regular-sequence-apartness-positive-separation}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
