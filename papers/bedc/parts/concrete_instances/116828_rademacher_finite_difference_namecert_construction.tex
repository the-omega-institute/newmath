\label{thm:rademacher-finite-difference-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}

The finite-difference route is row-bounded. The dyadic row supplies endpoint radii and denominator tolerances, the metric row supplies the neighborhoods in which the distance comparisons are read, and the Lipschitz row supplies the graph and bound ledger for the same window.
