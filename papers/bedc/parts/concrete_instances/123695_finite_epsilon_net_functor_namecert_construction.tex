\begin{definition}[Finite epsilon-net functor carrier]
\label{def:finite-epsilon-net-functor-carrier}
A $\FiniteEpsilonNetFunctorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(X,E,N,R,D,M,U,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row whose finite nets are being refined.
\item $E$ is the positive $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row for the source scale.
\item $N$ is the finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ epsilon-net row, read through $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ and the compact metric cover surface.
\item $R$ is the refinement-map row, listing for each displayed target center the source center, membership constructor, and coverage witness used by the refined net.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger comparing the source tolerance, refined tolerance, and radius rows used by the functorial refinement.
\item $M$ is the mesh or finite-cover fold row consumed by compact-uniform-modulus and Heine--Cantor readers.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row reached after the refined finite net and mesh rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact source, tolerance, net, refinement, dyadic ledger, mesh, and modulus rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through finite-net refinement, mesh folding, and the uniform-modulus handoff.

\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteEpsilonNetFunctorUp}$ row.

The carrier stores only displayed finite ProbeBundle nets, refinement maps, dyadic tolerance rows, mesh folds, and the uniform-modulus handoff. It stores no selected center outside the finite rows, no open-cover compactness object, no quotient cover equality, no countable choice, and no ambient compactness coordinate.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite epsilon-net functor NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-epsilon-net-functor-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteEpsilonNetFunctorUp$ carrier
$$
F=(X,E,N,R,D,M,U,H,C,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteEpsilonNetFunctorUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact source exposure, dyadic tolerance admission, finite ProbeBundle net exposure, refinement-map readability, dyadic comparison accounting, finite mesh or cover-fold exposure, $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-epsilon-net-functor-carrier}. The analytic rows are $X,E,N,R,D,M,U$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,L$.

The finite-net row $N$ is read before the refinement map $R$. The dyadic ledger $D$ records the tolerance comparison that makes the refined row usable by downstream mesh and cover-fold consumers, while $M$ packages the finite mesh route consumed before $U$ is exported.

The structural rows transport, replay, source, and name exactly those displayed rows. Since no carrier coordinate stores selected centers outside the finite net, open-cover compactness, quotient cover equality, countable choice, or ambient compactness, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite epsilon-net functor uniform-modulus handoff]
\label{thm:finite-epsilon-net-functor-uniform-modulus-handoff}
Every Heine--Cantor or compact-uniform-modulus consumer of an accepted $\FiniteEpsilonNetFunctorUp$ packet reaches the $\UniformModulusUp$ row $U$ only through the finite route
$$
X,E,N,R,D,M \longmapsto U,H,C,P,L .
$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a compact-uniform consumer requesting $U$. By the carrier definition, the handoff row is stored after the compact source, tolerance, finite-net, refinement, dyadic ledger, and mesh rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:finite-epsilon-net-functor-namecert-obligations}. It forces the consumer to display the finite ProbeBundle net $N$ before using $R$, to display the dyadic comparison row $D$ before the mesh fold $M$, and to read $M$ before exporting $U$.

The rows $H,C,P,L$ preserve that displayed route. Because the excluded compactness, choice, quotient, and ambient-completeness surfaces are absent from the carrier, the handoff is exactly the finite functorial refinement package.
\end{proof}
