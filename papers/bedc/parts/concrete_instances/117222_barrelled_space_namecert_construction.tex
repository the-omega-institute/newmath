Following Schaefer and Bourbaki on locally convex spaces, the $\BarrelledSpaceUp$ packet records the traditional barrel condition as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits after the locally convex carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locally-convex-space-namecert}, the finite seminorm-family surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-seminormed-space-namecert}, the Banach-space boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banachspace-namecert}, and the theorem-level uniform boundedness route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-steinhaus-namecert}. The packet gives Banach--Steinhaus and locally convex consumers a named barrelledness certificate without importing full Banach--Steinhaus, countable choice, Hahn--Banach separation, quotient dual equality, or bridge checking.

\begin{definition}[BarrelledSpace carrier]
\label{def:barrelled-space-carrier}
A $\BarrelledSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(L,S,A,D,V,C,K,F,U,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ source row supplying the topological-vector, convex-neighbourhood, and local-convexity surface.
\item $S$ is the $\SeminormedSpaceUp$ finite seminorm-family row through which boundedness probes and neighbourhood reads are inspected.
\item $A$ is the absorbing test row for candidate barrels in the displayed locally convex source.
\item $D$ is the balanced test row.
\item $V$ is the convex test row.
\item $C$ is the closed test row.
\item $K$ is the barrel predicate row, admitting a candidate precisely when $A,D,V,C$ are displayed for the same neighbourhood carrier.
\item $F$ is the bounded-family test row, reading pointwise bounded operator-family windows through the same locally convex and seminorm rows.
\item $U$ is the $\BanachSteinhausUp$ consumer row that may receive a uniform-boundedness handoff only after the barrel predicate and bounded-family rows have been exposed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $L,S,A,D,V,C,K,F,U$.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from barrel reads and bounded-family requests through the same rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BarrelledSpaceUp$, $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$, $\SeminormedSpaceUp$, $\BanachSpaceUp$, $\BanachSteinhausUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BarrelledSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host topological-vector-space equality, a selected dual, a quotient completion, a full Banach--Steinhaus theorem, a Hahn--Banach separator, or a choice-selected family of barrels.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BarrelledSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:barrelled-space-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\BarrelledSpaceUp$ carrier $B=(L,S,A,D,V,C,K,F,U,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BarrelledSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the locally convex source row $L$ and finite seminorm-family row $S$;
\item barrel recognition through the absorbing, balanced, convex, and closed tests $A,D,V,C$ and the predicate row $K$;
\item bounded-family compatibility through $F$, with all pointwise bounded windows read over the same $L,S$ surface;
\item Banach--Steinhaus consumer routing through $U$, available only after $K$ and $F$ have been displayed;
\item nonescape through $H,T,P,N$, excluding host Banach--Steinhaus, countable choice, Hahn--Banach strong forms, selected duals, quotient completions, and bridge checking.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:barrelled-space-carrier}. The tuple $L,S,A,D,V,C,K,F,U,H,T,P,N$ is the whole accepted packet, so carrier admission and local naming are finite row reads.

The barrel predicate is not primitive. It is assembled from the absorbing, balanced, convex, and closed tests $A,D,V,C$ over the locally convex source $L$ and seminorm-family row $S$, so a consumer cannot detach barrelledness from the displayed locally convex carrier.

The bounded-family test $F$ uses the same $L,S$ surface. The $\BanachSteinhausUp$ row $U$ is therefore a downstream consumer of the barrel and bounded-family rows rather than a proof of the full Banach--Steinhaus theorem.

Finally the rows $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no row names choice, Hahn--Banach separation, quotient completion, selected dual data, or bridge checking, the five listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}
