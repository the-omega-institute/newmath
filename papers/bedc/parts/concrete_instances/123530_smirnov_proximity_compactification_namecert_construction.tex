Following Smirnov and Efremovic on proximity compactifications, the $\SmirnovProximityCompactificationUp$ packet records a finite BEDC naming surface for the compactification generated by proximity separation rows. It sits between the proximity-space carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-proximity-space-namecert}, the uniform-completion handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformcompletion-namecert}, the compact metric boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, and the L10 readback route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only a finite proximity-to-completion carrier; it does not select compactification points, import ultrafilters, quotient equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Smirnov proximity compactification carrier]
\label{def:smirnov-proximity-compactification-carrier}
A $\SmirnovProximityCompactificationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(X,D,U,K,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\ProximitySpaceUp$ source row, carrying the separated proximity classifier and its finite apartness requests.
\item $D$ is the finite proximity-disjointness ledger recording which closed-neighbourhood requests have been inspected.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformCompletionUp$ handoff row generated from the proximity uniformity requests.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ compactness boundary row available only as displayed finite metric evidence.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which completion-facing names are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same finite windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $X,D,U,K,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, ledger, completion, compactness, window, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
X,D \longmapsto U,K \longmapsto W,R \longmapsto E,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SmirnovProximityCompactificationUp$, $\ProximitySpaceUp$, $\UniformCompletionUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SmirnovProximityCompactificationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for ultrafilter selection, selected compactification points, quotient equality of completed names, host compact Hausdorff completion, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Smirnov proximity compactification NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:smirnov-proximity-compactification-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SmirnovProximityCompactificationUp$ carrier
$$
S=(X,D,U,K,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SmirnovProximityCompactificationUp}$ surface consists exactly of proximity-source exposure, finite disjointness-ledger exposure, uniform-completion handoff, compact-metric boundary exposure, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal ordering, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every consumer must read the route through those rows and cannot replace them by an ultrafilter, a selected compactification point, quotient equality, or a host compactification theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier through \autoref{def:smirnov-proximity-compactification-carrier}. The rows $X,D,U,K,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$ are the complete public packet.

The proximity side is read first. The source row $X$ supplies the $\ProximitySpaceUp$ classifier, while $D$ records the finite separated-neighbourhood and disjointness requests inspected by the packet.

The completion side is downstream of those rows. The $\UniformCompletionUp$ row $U$ and $\CompactMetricUp$ row $K$ are exposed only as displayed handoff data, and the $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows $W,R,E$ keep every completion-facing read finite and sealed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier definition lists no ultrafilter coordinate, no selected compactification point, no quotient equality row, and no ambient completeness theorem, the NameCert obligations are exhausted by the displayed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Smirnov proximity compactification uniform handoff]
\label{thm:smirnov-proximity-compactification-uniform-handoff}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\SmirnovProximityCompactificationUp$ packet must pass through the displayed route
$$
\ProximitySpaceUp,\ D,\ \UniformCompletionUp,\ \CompactMetricUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp
$$
before any compactification read is public. The handoff gives the consumer finite proximity-disjointness data, a uniform-completion row, compact-metric boundary evidence, finite windows, regular rational readback, and a terminal Real seal, but no ultrafilter, selected limit point, quotient stream equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:smirnov-proximity-compactification-namecert-obligations}. They force every public read through the proximity source $X$ and finite disjointness ledger $D$ before the completion and compactness rows are consumed.

Next read the completion rows $U$ and $K$. These rows are handoff coordinates inside the same packet, so a consumer receives only the displayed $\UniformCompletionUp$ and $\CompactMetricUp$ evidence generated from the proximity ledger.

The final readback is finite. The $\StreamNameUp$ row $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$, and $\RealUp$ seal $E$ preserve the L10 window discipline before any compactification endpoint is named. The structural rows then replay and source that same route, excluding ultrafilters, selected compactification points, quotient equality, countable choice, and ambient Real completeness from the public handoff.
\end{proof}
