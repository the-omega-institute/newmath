It sits after the compact metric rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the topology rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the finite epsilon-net rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, the stream-name rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[CompactOpenExhaustion carrier]
\label{def:compact-open-exhaustion-carrier}
A $\CompactOpenExhaustionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(K,O,F,S,W,R,E_{\RealUp},H,C,P,N).
$$
Here $K$ is the displayed $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, $O$ is the $\TopologyUp$ open-membership ledger, $F$ is the finite $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ cover row, $S$ is the finite subcover schedule, $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ inspection window, $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational readback row, $E_{\RealUp}$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached through the displayed windows, $H$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $C$ is displayed $\Cont$ replay, $P$ is $\Pkg$ provenance over the listed rows, and $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactOpenExhaustionUp}$ row.

The carrier predicate says that every accepted read exposes the finite rows $K,O,F,S,W,R,E_{\RealUp},H,C,P,N$ before any exhaustion consumer may inspect a subcover claim. The packet has no coordinate for an arbitrary open cover, an infinite cover selection rule, ambient compactness, or a host completeness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompactOpenExhaustion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-open-exhaustion-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ do not add a new cover principle. They only transport the displayed rows, replay the accepted read, record provenance, and name the same local packet.

Thus the NameCert surface is the finite exhaustion packet itself, with no hidden open-cover compactness, selected infinite subcover, host topological compactness, or ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CompactOpenExhaustion finite-subcover route]
\label{thm:compact-open-exhaustion-finite-subcover-route}
Every $\CompactOpenExhaustionUp$ finite-subcover exhaustion read factors through the displayed route
$$
\CompactMetricUp,\TopologyUp,\FiniteEpsilonNetUp,\StreamNameUp,\RegSeqRatUp,\RealUp,H,C,P,N .
$$
The route reads $K$ before $O$, reads the finite-net cover row $F$ before the subcover schedule $S$, and reads the finite windows $W,R,E_{\RealUp}$ before the exhaustion claim is exported to an L10 completion consumer.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:compact-open-exhaustion-namecert-obligations}. It fixes the local NameCert surface as the rows displayed by the carrier.

The finite-net row $F$ is downstream of the compactmetric and topology rows $K,O$, so the subcover schedule $S$ is read only after the finite cover entries are visible. The stream, rational readback, and Real seal rows then provide the finite inspection boundary consumed by completion-facing routes.

Finally, the transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows preserve the same packet. Therefore the exhaustion export is a finite route through the displayed BEDC rows rather than a general open-cover compactness theorem.
\end{proof}
