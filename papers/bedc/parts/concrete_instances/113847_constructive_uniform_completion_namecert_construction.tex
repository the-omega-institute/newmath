\begin{definition}[Constructive uniform completion carrier]
\label{def:constructive-uniform-completion-carrier}
A $\ConstructiveUniformCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite packet
$$
U=(S,F,T,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the uniform-source row naming the accepted $\UniformSpaceUp$ surface, entourage ledger, and separatedness data visible to the completion request.
\item $F$ is the Cauchy-filter row read through $\CauchyFilterUp$, including the finite filter-basis observations used by the completion route.
\item $T$ is the Cauchy-net row read through $\CauchyNetUp$, including the directed finite comparison windows paired with the filter row.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row exposed before any regular readback is consumed.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row attached to the finite stream windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger that records the positive dyadic scales used by the uniform Cauchy comparison.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $S,F,T,W,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, filter, net, window, readback, tolerance, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the uniform source through the Cauchy rows, finite windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, Real seal, transport, provenance, and local naming.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,F,T,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion classes, selected representatives, arbitrary choice of Cauchy subdata, host real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive uniform completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:constructive-uniform-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ConstructiveUniformCompletionUp$ carrier $U=(S,F,T,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveUniformCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the uniform-source row, Cauchy-filter row, Cauchy-net row, finite stream-window row, regular rational readback row, dyadic tolerance ledger, terminal Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.

The classifier agreement obligation requires two accepted packets to agree precisely when these displayed rows agree componentwise through $\hsame$. The stability obligation requires $\hsame$ transport and $\Cont$ replay to preserve the same row order before any completion-facing or Real-facing consumer reads the terminal seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:constructive-uniform-completion-carrier}. The analytic completion route is $S,F,T,W,R,D,E$, and the structural certificate route is $H,C,P,N$.

The uniform-source row fixes the entourage surface. The Cauchy-filter and Cauchy-net rows then expose the two finite Cauchy presentations that the completion request may compare.

The finite stream window, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance ledger form the computational readback route. The terminal Real seal is downstream of those rows, so it cannot be consumed before the finite Cauchy and tolerance data have been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. Since no excluded object is present as a coordinate, no quotient completion class, selected representative, arbitrary choice schedule, host equality, or ambient completeness theorem is a local obligation.
\end{proof}
