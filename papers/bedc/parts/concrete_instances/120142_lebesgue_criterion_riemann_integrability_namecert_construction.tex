\begin{theorem}[Lebesgue criterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lebesgue-criterion-riemann-integrability-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LebesgueCriterionRiemannIntegrabilityUp$ carrier
$$
L=(I,F,O,D,J,U,V,G,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LebesgueCriterionRiemannIntegrabilityUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the interval, bounded-function observation, oscillation test, discontinuity ledger, Darboux routing, Darboux sums, gap, regular readback, Riemann handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item discontinuity-ledger exposure, requiring every discontinuity candidate consumed by the criterion to appear in the finite ledger $D$ with a displayed interval window;
\item off-ledger continuity routing, requiring $J$ to send every non-ledger cell through the oscillation row $O$ before any Darboux comparison is read;
\item Darboux gap control, requiring $U,V,G$ to compare upper and lower finite sums only over the cells exposed by $D,J$;
\item Riemann handoff nonescape, requiring $E$ to be read only after the finite discontinuity, Darboux, and regular readback rows are visible.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the packet supplies a finite BEDC surface for the Lebesgue-style discontinuity criterion without exporting measure-zero data or an ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The discontinuity side is read first. The ledger $D$ lists only displayed discontinuity windows, while $J$ records how the remaining cells are routed through the oscillation row $O$. No countable exceptional family or measure object is present in this route.

The Darboux side then reads the same cell route. The upper and lower rows $U,V$ compute finite sums over the displayed cells, and the gap row $G$ compares them before the regular readback $R$ is consumed.

Finally the Riemann handoff $E$ is downstream of those rows. Since the carrier omits measure completion, null-set equality, selected refinement limits, quotient function equality, countable choice, host integration, and ambient completeness, the listed obligations exhaust the local $\NameCert_{\LebesgueCriterionRiemannIntegrabilityUp}$ surface.
