\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy gluing L10 forward route]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-gluing-l10-forward-route}
Every accepted $\RegularCauchyGluingUp$ consumer reaches Real-facing gluing only through the displayed $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readbacks, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledgers, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of the local carrier. The route first reads the name certificate construction, then the L10 sibling route, and finally the Bishop-completion consumer route before any gluing conclusion is exported. It refuses quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness as coordinates of the gluing read.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the local carrier and obligations from \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-gluing-carrier} and \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-gluing-namecert-obligations}. The rows available to a consumer are the two compatible windows, the common dyadic ledger, finite observations, glued regular readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Apply the L10 sibling route of \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-gluing-l10-sibling-route}. It orders the consumer through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\CauchyReindexingUp$, and $\RealUp$ before a local gluing certificate is exported.

Apply the Bishop-completion consumer theorem \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-gluing-bishop-completion-consumer}. A Real-facing or Bishop-completion consumer must display the same $D,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N$ route, so the forward path remains finite and local to the accepted packet.

The refused objects are absent from every cited step. The name certificate construction supplies no quotient stream equality; the L10 sibling route supplies no selected limit or countable-choice schedule; and the Bishop-completion consumer route supplies no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate. Hence every accepted forward gluing read factors through the displayed windows, readbacks, tolerance ledgers, and Real seal.
\end{proof}
