\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy gluing Bishop completion consumer]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-gluing-bishop-completion-consumer}
Every $\BishopRealCompletionUp$ consumer that reads an accepted $\RegularCauchyGluingUp$ packet must first expose the same finite gluing carrier rows already displayed by \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-gluing-carrier}. In particular, before the Bishop completion route may read the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, the consumer must display the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ refinement ledger $D$, the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row $S$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ glued readback $Q$, the $\RealUp$ seal $E$, the $\CauchyReindexingUp$ handoff recorded in $P$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and the local $\NameCert$ row $N$.

Thus the Bishop completion consumer receives the gluing packet only as a finite L10 route over dyadic, stream, regular-rational, Real-seal, reindexing, transport, replay, provenance, and naming data. It receives no quotient stream equality, selected representative, host real equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $G=(L,R,D,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$ from \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-gluing-carrier}. The rows that a completion-facing consumer can inspect are exactly the two local windows, the common dyadic ledger, the finite observations, the glued readback, the Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Apply the L10 sibling route of \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-gluing-l10-sibling-route}. It orders the gluing packet through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, and $\CauchyReindexingUp$ before a NameCert row is exported.

Now read the Bishop completion carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-real-completion-namecert}. Its completion-facing route consumes dyadic precision, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, a Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. The gluing rows $D,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N$ are therefore the only rows that can feed such a consumer from $\RegularCauchyGluingUp$.

The exclusions follow from the same carrier inventory. Since neither \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-gluing-carrier} nor the Bishop completion route supplies quotient stream equality, selected representatives, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness as a coordinate, the consumer cannot export any of those objects from the gluing packet.
\end{proof}
