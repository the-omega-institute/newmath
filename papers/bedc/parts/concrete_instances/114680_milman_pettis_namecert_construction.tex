Following Milman and Pettis, the $\MilmanPettisUp$ packet records the finite route by which a uniformly convex Banach-space surface may be handed to a reflexive Banach consumer. It sits over the Banach carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banachspace-namecert}, the modulus surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformly-convex-space-namecert}, the weak-observation interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-weak-topology-namecert}, the scalar seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the reflexive handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-reflexive-banach-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Milman--Pettis carrier]
\label{def:milman-pettis-carrier}
A $\MilmanPettisUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(B,U,W,R,D,H,C,P,N,E)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BanachSpaceUp$ row whose complete normed-space route is read before any convexity conclusion is inspected.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformlyConvexSpaceUp$ row, carrying the displayed modulus of convexity over the same Banach surface.
\item $W$ is the $\WeakTopologyUp$ observation row, restricted to finite scalar-functional windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ scalar-budget row used for norm, midpoint, separation, and modulus tolerances.
\item $D$ is the $\ReflexiveBanachUp$ handoff row, available only after $B,U,W,R$ have been replayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Banach, uniform-convexity, weak-topology, scalar-budget, reflexive-handoff, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Milman--Pettis request through $B,U,W,R,D,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MilmanPettisUp$, $\BanachSpaceUp$, $\UniformlyConvexSpaceUp$, $\WeakTopologyUp$, $\RealUp$, $\ReflexiveBanachUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MilmanPettisUp}$ row.
\item $E$ is the ledger-refusal row excluding host weak compactness, selected weak limits, quotient double-dual equality, and choice over bounded nets.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,U,W,R,D,H,C,P,N,E$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Milman--Pettis NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:milman-pettis-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MilmanPettisUp$ carrier $M=(B,U,W,R,D,H,C,P,N,E)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MilmanPettisUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the Banach row, uniformly convex row, weak-topology row, Real scalar-budget row, reflexive handoff row, transport, continuation, provenance, local naming, and refusal ledger. A consumer may read the Milman--Pettis route only by exposing $\BanachSpaceUp$, $\UniformlyConvexSpaceUp$, $\WeakTopologyUp$, $\RealUp$, $\ReflexiveBanachUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows in that order.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:milman-pettis-carrier}. The analytic rows are $B,U,W,R,D$: Banach completeness, uniform convexity, weak finite observations, scalar budgets, and the reflexive handoff.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name those analytic rows as one finite packet. The refusal row $E$ has no carrier slot for host weak compactness, selected weak limits, quotient double-dual equality, or choice over bounded nets.

Thus the local NameCert surface is exhausted by the displayed rows. Any accepted consumer must replay the Banach, uniformly convex, weak-topology, scalar-budget, and reflexive-handoff route before reading the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Milman--Pettis reflexive handoff]
\label{thm:milman-pettis-reflexive-handoff}
Every reflexivity-facing consumer of an accepted $\MilmanPettisUp$ packet factors through
$$
\BanachSpaceUp,\ \UniformlyConvexSpaceUp,\ \WeakTopologyUp,\ \RealUp
\longmapsto
\ReflexiveBanachUp .
$$
In carrier notation, the row $D$ can be read only after the Banach row $B$, the uniform-convexity row $U$, the weak-observation row $W$, and the Real scalar-budget row $R$ are visible. The handoff exports a displayed $\ReflexiveBanachUp$ route and refuses host weak compactness, selected weak cluster points, quotient double-dual equality, and choice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the Banach and uniform-convexity rows. The Banach row $B$ supplies the complete normed-space surface, while $U$ supplies the modulus-of-convexity row over that same surface.

Read weak observations and scalar budgets next. The row $W$ restricts weak-topology access to finite scalar-functional windows, and $R$ records the Real-valued norm, midpoint, and tolerance budgets used by the convexity route.

Only after those rows are present does the reflexive handoff row $D$ become visible. Transport through $H$, replay through $C$, and provenance through $P,N$ keep the handoff inside the finite packet, while $E$ records that no host compactness theorem, selected weak limit, quotient double-dual equality, or choice principle is exported.
\end{proof}
