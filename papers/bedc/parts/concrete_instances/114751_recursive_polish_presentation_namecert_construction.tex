\begin{theorem}[Recursive Polish presentation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:recursive-polish-presentation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RecursivePolishPresentationUp$ carrier $P_{\mathrm{rec}}=(M,C,D,T,S,R,A,E,V,H,G,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RecursivePolishPresentationUp}$ surface is exhausted by five finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item recursive dense-sequence admission through $D$ over the common metric row $M$;
  \item dyadic distance-table exactness through $T,S,R$;
  \item dense coverage of the complete-separable and Polish rows $C,A$ through the same scheduled names;
  \item represented-space and effective-Polish handoff through $E$ and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $V$;
  \item refusal, componentwise transport, provenance, replay, and local naming through $H,G,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is discharged by a selected limit, quotient completion equality, external dense-point choice, host equality of names, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows. The metric and complete-separable rows $M,C$ supply the analytic source, while the recursive dense row $D$ and stream row $S$ restrict all dense-name access to finite scheduled windows.

The table rows are read next. The dyadic table $T$ and regular-rational readback $R$ certify the inspected distance comparisons for the same dense indices, so no unscheduled table query or selected dense point is available.

Finally, $A,E,V,H,G,N$ package the Polish, effective, represented-space, seal, transport, provenance, and local naming boundaries. Since the carrier excludes quotient completions, selected limits, host equality of names, countable-choice schedules, and ambient completeness coordinates, those objects cannot become local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Recursive dense-table handoff]
\label{thm:recursive-polish-presentation-dense-table-handoff}

Componentwise transport and provenance preserve the same finite packet. Because no excluded coordinate is present in the carrier, the consumer receives the scheduled dense-table route and no quotient, selected, choice-like, or host-equality object.
\end{proof}
