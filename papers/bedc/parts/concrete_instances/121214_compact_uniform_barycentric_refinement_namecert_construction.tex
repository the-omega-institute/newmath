Following Bishop and Bridges finite-cover refinement practice, the $\CompactUniformBarycentricRefinementUp$ packet records the compact-uniform-continuity use of dyadic barycentric subdivision as finite NameCert data. It consumes the subdivision primitive of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-barycentric-refinement-namecert}, the compact source rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the tolerance-only modulus surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, the finite interval-cell net of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}, and the sequence/readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a compact-uniform consumer handoff; it does not assert open-cover compactness, selected finite subcovers, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Compact uniform barycentric refinement carrier]\label{def:compact-uniform-barycentric-refinement-carrier}
A $\CompactUniformBarycentricRefinementUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(B,K,M,I,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\DyadicBarycentricRefinementUp$ source row, exposing parent cells, barycenters, child cells, and mesh-decrease data.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row that supplies the compact finite-net boundary read by the uniform-continuity consumer.
\item $M$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row, recording the tolerance-only modulus produced after the compact source and subdivision rows are inspected.
\item $I$ is the $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ interval-cell row used to align parent cells, child cells, and compact finite-net probes.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation-window row for endpoint, barycenter, and finite-net probe reads.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for child-cell radii, compact-net tolerances, and modulus budgets.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $B,K,M,I,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all displayed refinement, compact, modulus, interval, window, readback, seal, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the compact-uniform route
$$
B,K,I,S,R \longmapsto M,E,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactUniformBarycentricRefinementUp$, $\DyadicBarycentricRefinementUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformBarycentricRefinementUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,K,M,I,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected finite subcovers, host compactness, quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform barycentric refinement NameCert obligations]\label{thm:compact-uniform-barycentric-refinement-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactUniformBarycentricRefinementUp$ carrier
$$
U=(B,K,M,I,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformBarycentricRefinementUp}$ surface consists exactly of barycentric-refinement source admission through $B$, compact-metric source admission through $K$, uniform-modulus handoff through $M$, dyadic interval-cell alignment through $I$, finite observation through $S$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal Real-seal ordering through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-uniform-barycentric-refinement-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the dyadic barycentric source, compact metric source, uniform-modulus handoff, interval-cell alignment, observation windows, readback row, and Real seal before the structural rows are inspected.

Read the compact-uniform route in order. The barycentric row supplies parent and child cells, the compact row supplies finite-net source data, and the interval-cell row aligns the subdivision with the compact probes. The StreamName and RegSeqRat rows then expose finite endpoint windows and tolerance budgets before the terminal Real seal is available.

Finally replay $M,H,C,P,N$. The modulus row is a tolerance-only handoff generated inside the displayed route, while transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite rows. Since no open-cover, selected-subcover, quotient, selected-limit, choice, host-compactness, or completeness coordinate is present, none is exported by the certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform barycentric refinement modulus route]\label{thm:compact-uniform-barycentric-refinement-modulus-route}
Every compact-uniform-continuity consumer of an accepted $\CompactUniformBarycentricRefinementUp$ packet must read
$$
B,K,I,S,R \longmapsto M,E,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the $\UniformModulusUp$ row is available only after the $\DyadicBarycentricRefinementUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, and $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows have been displayed, and the $\RealUp$ seal remains downstream of the same finite route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the barycentric and compact source rows. The row $B$ exposes the parent cells, barycenters, child cells, and mesh-decrease data, while $K$ supplies only the compact finite-net boundary used by the consumer.

Next align the source rows through $I,S,R$. The dyadic interval-cell row ties the subdivision cells to compact probes; the stream row exposes finite endpoint and barycenter windows; and the regular-rational row records the tolerance budgets consumed by the modulus handoff.

Only then may the consumer read $M$ and $E$. The UniformModulus row is produced as a tolerance-only consumer row, and the terminal Real seal remains downstream of the displayed readback. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that order, excluding open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, and ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
