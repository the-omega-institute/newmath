\begin{definition}[Ishihara finite-test boundary carrier]
\label{def:ishihara-finite-test-boundary-carrier}
An $\IshiharaFiniteTestBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(B,P,N,W,Q,D,E,H,C,S)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the branch-window row displaying the finite observation schedule inspected by the Ishihara-style argument;
\item $P$ is the positive-test row naming the finite evidence that the inspected branch has crossed the displayed positive threshold;
\item $N$ is the negative-test row naming the finite evidence that the inspected branch remains inside the displayed negative threshold;
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row through which the inspected sequence coordinates are read;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same finite window;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row carrying the threshold budget used by the two finite tests;
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $B,P,N,W,Q,D$ have been displayed;
\item $H$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the branch, test, window, readback, tolerance, and seal rows;
\item $C$ is displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same finite inspection route;
\item $S$ is the local $\NameCert_{\IshiharaFiniteTestBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets whose public read factors through the displayed finite branch window and exactly one of the two test rows before the Real or Completion consumer reads $E$. It has no coordinate for LPO, WLPO, trichotomy of real names, a selected completed limit, quotient equality of streams, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{proof}
