\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DyadicCauchyStaircaseUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-cauchy-staircase-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, \emph{Constructive Analysis}, the $\DyadicCauchyStaircaseUp$ packet records the finite precision staircase by which dyadic tolerance rows are opened in monotone order before a regular rational name is read back and sealed as a Real-facing row. It sits between the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the effective-rate schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyrate-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite precision-schedule certificate; it is not a quotient completion, a selected limit, a countable-choice operator, trichotomy, host real equality, or a completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Dyadic Cauchy staircase carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-cauchy-staircase-carrier}
A $\DyadicCauchyStaircaseUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(S,D,L,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row naming the inspected windows of the source rational stream.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ precision row ledger, listing the dyadic tolerance rows requested by the consumer.
\item $L$ is the monotone staircase ledger, recording that later displayed rows of $D$ refine earlier rows and that each read of $S$ is attached to the current step.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row exposed only after the relevant staircase step and stream window have been displayed.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached after the staircase and regular readback route has been consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream windows, dyadic precision rows, staircase ledger, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a precision request through $D,L,S,R,E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicCauchyStaircaseUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\CauchyRateUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicCauchyStaircaseUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, hidden stream equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic Cauchy staircase NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-cauchy-staircase-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicCauchyStaircaseUp$ carrier $K=(S,D,L,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicCauchyStaircaseUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ precision ledger, monotone staircase ledger, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item precision visibility, requiring each consumed tolerance request to appear as a displayed row of $D$ before $L$ advances the staircase;
\item monotone-step admission, requiring $L$ to record that later displayed precision rows refine earlier displayed rows and that no step is skipped by the readback route;
\item window-readback routing, requiring $S$ to be opened at the current staircase step before $R$ is consumed;
\item real-seal order, requiring $E$ to be read only after $D,L,S,R$ have all been displayed in the same finite route;
\item transport and replay stability, requiring $H$ and $C$ to preserve the same precision, staircase, window, readback, and seal rows componentwise;
\item ledger nonescape from quotient completion, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, hidden stream equality, and $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:dyadic-cauchy-staircase-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the rows $S,D,L,R,E,H,C,P,N$.

Precision visibility and monotone-step admission are the role of $D$ and $L$. The dyadic rows in $D$ name the requested tolerances, while $L$ records their displayed refinement order and attaches each opened stream window to the current step.

Window-readback routing follows from the route order. A readback row $R$ can be consumed only after the current dyadic precision row and its staircase step have opened the finite window $S$.

The real seal is downstream. The row $E$ is read after $D,L,S,R$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same finite path.

The nonescape clause follows because none of the excluded objects is a coordinate of the carrier. The local certificate therefore exports only the displayed precision-staircase surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic Cauchy staircase L10 handoff]
\label{thm:dyadic-cauchy-staircase-l10-handoff}
Every accepted $\DyadicCauchyStaircaseUp$ carrier $K=(S,D,L,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ hands any L10 Real or Completion consumer the ordered finite route
$$
D \longrightarrow L \longrightarrow S \longrightarrow R \longrightarrow E .
$$
The route first displays the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ precision rows, then the monotone staircase ledger, then the $\StreamNameUp$ window, then the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and only then the $\RealUp$ seal. A consumer receives no quotient completion, selected limit, countable-choice selector, trichotomy datum, host real equality, hidden stream equality, or ambient Real-completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the accepted carrier $K=(S,D,L,R,E,H,C,P,N)$. Projection gives the dyadic precision ledger $D$, the staircase ledger $L$, the stream-window row $S$, the regular readback row $R$, and the terminal seal $E$.

The precision rows are consumed first. The staircase ledger $L$ records their monotone refinement order and fixes which window of $S$ is attached to each requested tolerance.

The regular readback $R$ is then read through the opened window, and $E$ is reached only after that readback has been displayed. The rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same finite route.

Since the route has no carrier coordinate beyond $D,L,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$, the handoff cannot export quotient completion, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, hidden stream equality, or a completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.
\end{proof}
