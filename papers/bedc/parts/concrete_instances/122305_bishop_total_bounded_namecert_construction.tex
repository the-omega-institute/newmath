\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BishopTotalBoundedUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-total-bounded-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive metric spaces, the $\BishopTotalBoundedUp$ packet names total boundedness as a tolerance-indexed finite-net interface rather than as open-cover compactness. It sits beside the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the Real-induced uniformity route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-uniform-structure-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite net row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, and the compact-metric consumer boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}. The chapter records the L10 finite-net carrier that downstream completion and compact-metric routes may consume without importing open-cover compactness, countable choice, or selected limits.

\begin{definition}[Bishop total-bounded carrier]
\label{def:bishop-total-bounded-carrier}
A $\BishopTotalBoundedUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(M,U,D,E,W,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row whose point and distance classifiers are inspected before any net request is read.
\item $U$ is the $\RealUniformStructureUp$ row that records the generated entourage surface used by completion-facing consumers.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ positive-tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ row; for each displayed positive tolerance it carries a finite center bundle, center-membership rows, and coverage-distance witnesses.
\item $W$ is the finite observation-window row used to inspect source points, center names, and coverage witnesses.
\item $Q$ is the rational readback row attached to metric distances, dyadic tolerances, and finite-net comparisons.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,U,D,E,W,Q,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$$
M,U,D \longmapsto E \longmapsto W,Q .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopTotalBoundedUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RealUniformStructureUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopTotalBoundedUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,U,D,E,W,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, countable choice, a selected finite subcover, a selected Cauchy limit, quotient completion, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop total-bounded NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-total-bounded-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopTotalBoundedUp$ carrier
$$
B=(M,U,D,E,W,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopTotalBoundedUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for those rows, metric-source exposure through $M$, uniform-entourage compatibility through $U$, positive tolerance visibility through $D$, finite-net exposure through $E$, finite observation and rational readback through $W,Q$, classifier transport through $H,C$, and provenance plus local naming through $P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:bishop-total-bounded-carrier}. The displayed tuple is the whole packet, so no obligation can be read outside $M,U,D,E,W,Q,H,C,P,N$.

The metric and uniform rows give the source boundary. A finite-net request first opens the $\MetricUp$ row $M$, then reads the $\RealUniformStructureUp$ row $U$ when the request is passed to completion-facing or compact-metric consumers.

The tolerance and net rows are finite. The positive-radius data must appear in the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger $D$, and the centers, membership rows, and coverage-distance witnesses must appear in the $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ row $E$.

Finally, $W,Q,H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route by finite observation, rational readback, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since none of the carrier rows stores open covers, selected subcovers, selected limits, quotient completion, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness, the NameCert obligations export none of those objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop total-bounded finite-net route]
\label{thm:bishop-total-bounded-finite-net-route}
Every $\CompleteMetricUp$ or $\CompactMetricUp$ consumer of an accepted $\BishopTotalBoundedUp$ packet reads total boundedness through the displayed finite route
$$
\MetricUp,\RealUniformStructureUp,\DyadicRatCoreUp
\longmapsto
\FiniteEpsilonNetUp
\longmapsto
W,Q,H,C,P,N .
$$
The route supplies finite centers and coverage witnesses at each displayed positive tolerance, but it does not supply open-cover compactness, countable choice, selected limits, quotient completion, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:bishop-total-bounded-namecert-obligations}. It forces every consumer to read the metric source $M$, uniform row $U$, positive tolerance ledger $D$, finite-net row $E$, and structural rows before a total-bounded claim is available.

The finite-net row is the only place where centers and coverage witnesses occur. For a displayed tolerance from $D$, the row $E$ supplies a finite bundle of centers, their membership data, and the coverage-distance witnesses over the metric and uniform source rows.

The remaining rows keep the route finite and local. The observation window $W$ and readback row $Q$ record only finite inspections, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet.

Therefore a completion or compact-metric consumer receives exactly the finite-net route stated above. None of the excluded compactness, choice, quotient, limit-selection, or ambient-completeness principles has a carrier coordinate, so the consumer cannot read it from $\BishopTotalBoundedUp$.
\end{proof}
