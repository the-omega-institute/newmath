\begin{definition}[Dirichlet uniform test carrier]
\label{def:dirichlet-uniform-test-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\RealSeriesUp$ source row, exposing only finite function-series windows and their inspected partial sums.
\item $B$ is the bounded-partial-sum ledger, giving a displayed bound for every inspected source window.
\item $A$ is the coefficient monotonicity ledger, recording the finite order comparisons used by the coefficient row on the inspected tail.
\item $V$ is the coefficient variation ledger, recording the finite total-variation budget that replaces any appeal to host monotone convergence.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule naming the source indices and parameter windows visible to the test.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformCauchyCriterionUp$ handoff row, storing the uniform tail-window request produced by $B,A,V,W$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational estimates exported by the tail-window ledger.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, boundedness, coefficient, variation, window, handoff, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from $S,W$ into $B,A,V$, from those ledgers into $U$, and from $U$ through $R$ into $E$.

The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $S,B,A,V,W,U,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected infinite sum, host uniform convergence, quotient equality of functions, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dirichlet uniform test NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dirichlet-uniform-test-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DirichletUniformTestUp$ carrier $K=(S,B,A,V,W,U,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DirichletUniformTestUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, bounded-partial-sum exposure, coefficient monotonicity exposure, coefficient-variation exposure, StreamName window admission, UniformCauchyCriterion handoff readiness, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal non-escape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. A downstream consumer may read a uniform Cauchy tail certificate only through those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows of \autoref{def:dirichlet-uniform-test-carrier}. The source row $S$ gives only finite function-series windows, while $B,A,V$ give the bounded partial-sum, monotonicity, and variation ledgers visible on those windows.

The window schedule $W$ fixes which tails may be inspected before the handoff row $U$ is read. Thus a consumer cannot reach a uniform tail claim until the same finite source, boundedness, coefficient, and variation rows have already been displayed.

Finally read $R,E,H,C,P,N$. The regular readback and Real seal are downstream of $U$, and the structural rows preserve exactly the same displayed route. No selected infinite sum, quotient function equality, host uniform convergence theorem, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness witness is introduced.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dirichlet uniform test uniform Cauchy handoff]
\label{thm:dirichlet-uniform-test-uniform-cauchy-handoff}

$$
\DirichletUniformTestUp \longmapsto \RealSeriesUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \UniformCauchyCriterionUp \longmapsto \RealUp .

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Next use $B,A,V$ to form the Dirichlet tail estimate on the displayed window. The bounded partial sums provide the uniform bound, the monotonicity ledger orders the coefficient row, and the variation ledger supplies the finite loss budget needed to route the estimate into $U$.
