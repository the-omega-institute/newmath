\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BishopCauchyTestUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-cauchy-test-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopCauchyTestUp$ packet records the finite test surface by which a displayed sequence is accepted as Cauchy before any real-facing completion output is consumed. It sits over the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the Bishop Cauchy criterion of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-cauchy-criterion-namecert}. The packet names the textbook Cauchy-test route as finite BEDC NameCert data; it does not assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, quotient equality, trichotomy, selected limits, or countable choice.

\begin{definition}[Bishop Cauchy test carrier]
\label{def:bishop-cauchy-test-carrier}
A $\BishopCauchyTestUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(F,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the test-family row, recording the finite list of tested sequence presentations and the displayed comparison requests.
\item $W$ is the finite tail-window row, recording the inspected tail indices for each requested tolerance.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording the dyadic radii, slack entries, and comparison budgets used by the test.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, assembled from the tail windows and dyadic tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row exposed only after $F,W,D,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the test family, finite tail windows, tolerance ledger, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same finite test route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopCauchyTestUp$, $\BishopCauchyCriterionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCauchyTestUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The accepted carrier is exactly the displayed packet $T$. It has no coordinate for ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, quotient equality, trichotomy, selected limits, countable choice, or an unrecorded host Cauchy test.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop Cauchy test NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-cauchy-test-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopCauchyTestUp$ carrier $T=(F,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCauchyTestUp}$ obligations are exhausted by carrier admission, test-family exposure, finite tail-window exposure, dyadic tolerance-ledger exposure, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-cauchy-test-carrier}. The packet exposes only the rows $F,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$, so every accepted read starts from the displayed test family, finite tail windows, and dyadic tolerance ledger.

The analytic part is finite. The row $F$ supplies the tested presentations, $W$ supplies the inspected tails, and $D$ supplies the dyadic budgets. The readback row $R$ can therefore be assembled only from already displayed tail and tolerance data.

The endpoint rows are downstream. The terminal seal $E$ is available only after $F,W,D,R$ have been read, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier contains no excluded coordinate, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop Cauchy test finite tail window]
\label{thm:bishop-cauchy-test-finite-tail-window}
Every public $\BishopCauchyTestUp$ read of an accepted carrier $T=(F,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ must pass through the finite tail-window row $W$ before the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$ or terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is interpreted. The read exports only the displayed test-family row, window row, dyadic ledger, readback, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a public read of $T$. Carrier projection exposes the row $F$ of tested presentations and the row $W$ of inspected tail indices before any readback row is visible.

The tolerance route is next. The dyadic ledger $D$ records the requested radii and slack entries for the same finite windows, so the regular readback $R$ has no source other than $F,W,D$.

Finally read $E,H,C,P,N$. The seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve the same finite route, so the test cannot become a quotient equality, selected limit, trichotomy claim, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop Cauchy test Real seal nonescape]
\label{thm:bishop-cauchy-test-real-seal-nonescape}
Let $T=(F,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\BishopCauchyTestUp$ carrier. Any terminal $\RealUp$ seal read through $E$ remains downstream of the displayed test-family row, finite tail-window row, dyadic tolerance ledger, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. The seal cannot supply ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, quotient equality, trichotomy, selected limits, countable choice, or a detached host Cauchy-test theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project \autoref{def:bishop-cauchy-test-carrier}. The only terminal real-facing coordinate is $E$, and it is displayed inside the same finite packet as $F,W,D,R,H,C,P,N$.

Apply \autoref{thm:bishop-cauchy-test-finite-tail-window}. The theorem places the finite test windows and dyadic ledger before the regular readback and before any terminal seal exposure.

The structural rows finish the nonescape claim. Componentwise transport in $H$, continuation replay in $C$, provenance in $P$, and local naming in $N$ preserve the displayed packet. Since no excluded row exists in the carrier, the terminal seal cannot export completeness, quotient equality, trichotomy, selected limits, countable choice, or a detached host theorem.
\end{proof}
