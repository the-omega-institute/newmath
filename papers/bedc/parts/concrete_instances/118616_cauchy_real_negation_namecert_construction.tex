\begin{theorem}[CauchyRealNegation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-real-negation-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Bind the source route to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchynegation-namecert}. That sibling already names the regular sign-flip surface; this packet only records the Real-seal consumer route that reads it through $S,D,Q,E$.

The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve exactly that finite route. Since no remaining carrier coordinate exists, the excluded quotient, host-equality, selected-limit, trichotomy, completeness, and completed inverse objects cannot be read from the certificate.

\label{thm:cauchy-real-negation-real-seal-route}

Apply the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:cauchy-real-negation-namecert-obligations}. It binds every consumer route to $R,S,D,Q$ before $E$ is visible, and it keeps $H,C,P,N$ as transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows for the same data.
