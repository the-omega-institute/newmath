\begin{theorem}[CauchyNameTruncation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-name-truncation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyNameTruncationUp$ carrier $K=(S,Q,D,T,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyNameTruncationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite StreamName prefix exposure, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance accounting, truncation-index and tail-budget admission, optional RealUp sealing, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. No accepted local read may export quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-name-truncation-carrier}. The rows $S,Q,D,T$ are the finite prefix schedule, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, and truncation budget inspected before any terminal seal is available.

The optional seal row $E$ is downstream of that displayed route. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are exactly the rows $H,C,P,N$, so the local NameCert surface is exhausted by the nine-row packet.

The excluded outputs have no carrier coordinates. Since the packet contains no quotient row, selected-limit row, countable-choice schedule, open-cover compactness witness, or ambient-completeness certificate, an accepted local read cannot produce one.
\end{proof}
