Following Bishop--Bridges Cauchy tail estimates, the $\AnalyticTailBudgetUp$ packet records the finite tolerance budget by which analysis-facing arguments hand a tail claim to Real and Completion consumers. It realizes the analysis-interface route of \autoref{ch:visions-analysis-interface-spine} as concrete NameCert data and sits over the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the Cauchy modulus route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchymodulus-namecert}, and the completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-regular-cauchy-completion-namecert}. The packet names a finite tail-control row only; it does not assert ambient completeness, choose a limit, quotient streams, decide trichotomy, or identify host real equality.

\begin{definition}[Analytic tail-budget carrier]
\label{def:analytic-tail-budget-carrier}
An $\AnalyticTailBudgetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(\varepsilon,D,M,S,R,E,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\varepsilon$ is the requested finite tolerance row, presented as dyadic precision data and not as a detached host-real parameter.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budget ledger. It records the dyadic comparison cells, tail radius, and tolerance arithmetic used by the request.
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyModulusUp$ row that turns the tolerance request into a displayed finite tail index.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation-window row opened at and beyond that index.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the inspected windows and their regular rational tail estimates.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,M,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $B$ is the $\BishopRegularCauchyCompletionUp$ completion-facing boundary row; it records that the same tail data may be consumed by completion arguments without selecting a completed limit.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $\varepsilon,D,M,S,R,E,B,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tail request through dyadic budget, Cauchy modulus, finite windows, regular readback, Real seal, and completion boundary.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\AnalyticTailBudgetUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\CauchyModulusUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BishopRegularCauchyCompletionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AnalyticTailBudgetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares packets only through $\varepsilon,D,M,S,R,E,B,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected limit, quotient stream equality, ambient completeness of $\RealUp$, trichotomy, countable choice, or host real equality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Analytic tail-budget NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:analytic-tail-budget-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AnalyticTailBudgetUp$ carrier
$$
A=(\varepsilon,D,M,S,R,E,B,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\AnalyticTailBudgetUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed packet; dyadic tolerance exposure through $\varepsilon$ and $D$; tail-index extraction through $M$; finite observation through $S$; regular rational readback through $R$; terminal Real sealing through $E$; completion-facing boundary exposure through $B$; componentwise transport through $H$; displayed replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:analytic-tail-budget-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes precisely the tolerance, dyadic budget, modulus, finite-window, readback, Real-seal, completion-boundary, and structural rows.

The tolerance obligation is exhausted by $\varepsilon,D,M$. The request is read as dyadic data, the ledger fixes the comparison cells and radius, and the Cauchy modulus row turns that budget into the displayed tail index.

The analytic readback obligation is exhausted by $S,R,E,B$. The StreamName row opens the finite windows, the RegSeqRat row reads the regular rational estimates, the Real seal remains downstream of that readback, and the Bishop regular completion row records the completion-facing boundary without selecting a limit.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed rows. Since no carrier coordinate stores quotient stream equality, selected limits, trichotomy, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient completeness, none of those objects can become a local NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Analytic tail-budget Real completion route]
\label{thm:analytic-tail-budget-real-completion-route}
Every accepted Real-facing or Completion-facing read of an $\AnalyticTailBudgetUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \CauchyModulusUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp,\BishopRegularCauchyCompletionUp .
$$
The route makes a tail request public only after the dyadic budget, modulus index, finite observation windows, and regular rational readback have been displayed. It exports no selected limit, quotient stream equality, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy decision, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted Real-facing or Completion-facing consumer. By \autoref{def:analytic-tail-budget-carrier}, the consumer must read the dyadic budget row $D$ and the Cauchy modulus row $M$ before any tail window is available.

Apply \autoref{thm:analytic-tail-budget-namecert-obligations}. It places $S$ and $R$ between the modulus index and the terminal rows $E$ and $B$, so neither the Real seal nor the completion-facing boundary has an independent entry point.

The sibling chapters supply the listed interfaces only as finite rows: dyadic tolerances, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, Cauchy modulus indices, Real seals, and Bishop regular completion boundaries. None adds a quotient stream, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy decision, host real equality, or ambient completeness coordinate. Therefore the accepted consumer remains on the displayed Real completion route.
\end{proof}
