Following Bishop and Bridges and the categorical reflector formulation of completion, the $\IdempotentCompletionReflectorUp$ packet records the finite L10 surface by which a completion request is reflected back through displayed completion rows without quotienting Cauchy data. It sits beside the completion carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}, the reflection route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completionreflection-namecert}, the monadic completion corridor of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionmonad-namecert}, the metric completion surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, and the terminal real-name boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a narrow reflector certificate, not a quotient completion, a host equality of Cauchy classes, a selected representative family, or an ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Idempotent completion reflector carrier]
\label{def:idempotent-completion-reflector-carrier}
An $\IdempotentCompletionReflectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(C,F,M,T,E,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the source $\CompletionUp$ request row, exposing only the completion-facing name and its finite dependency boundary.
\item $F$ is the $\CompletionReflectionUp$ row, recording the reflector readback from the completion request to the displayed source packet.
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyCompletionMonadUp$ row, naming the unit and multiplication discipline only as finite monad-facing bookkeeping.
\item $T$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ row, recording the metric completion handoff consumed by the reflector.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, readable only after $C,F,M,T$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for completion, reflection, monad, metric completion, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a reflector request through $C,F,M,T,E$ and then through the structural rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\IdempotentCompletionReflectorUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\CompletionReflectionUp$, $\CauchyCompletionMonadUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\IdempotentCompletionReflectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $C,F,M,T,E,H,K,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completions, host equality of Cauchy classes, selected representatives, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Idempotent completion reflector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:idempotent-completion-reflector-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\IdempotentCompletionReflectorUp$ carrier
$$
R=(C,F,M,T,E,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\IdempotentCompletionReflectorUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the $\CompletionUp$ request row $C$, reflector readback through $\CompletionReflectionUp$ in $F$, monad-facing bookkeeping through $\CauchyCompletionMonadUp$ in $M$, metric-completion handoff through $\MetricCompletionUp$ in $T$, terminal real sealing through $\RealUp$ in $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $K$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:idempotent-completion-reflector-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly $C,F,M,T,E$, and the structural rows are exactly $H,K,P,N$.

The completion request is read first through $C$. The reflector row $F$ can only read back that displayed request, and the monad row $M$ records unit and multiplication discipline as finite bookkeeping over the same request.

The metric-completion row $T$ is downstream from that reflector route. The real seal $E$ is readable only after $C,F,M,T$ have been displayed, so it cannot supply an independent host completeness claim.

Finally inspect $H,K,P,N$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completions, host equality of Cauchy classes, selected representatives, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Idempotent reflector completion route]
\label{thm:idempotent-completion-reflector-route}
Every completion-reflection consumer of an accepted $\IdempotentCompletionReflectorUp$ packet must factor its read through
$$
\CompletionUp \longmapsto \CompletionReflectionUp \longmapsto \CauchyCompletionMonadUp \longmapsto \MetricCompletionUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
using the rows $C,F,M,T,E,H,K,P,N$. The route is idempotent only at this displayed packet surface: a second reflector read reuses the same $C,F,M,T,E$ rows through $H,K,P,N$ and introduces no quotient completion, host Cauchy-class equality, selected representatives, or ambient real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:idempotent-completion-reflector-namecert-obligations}. They force every completion-facing read to expose $C,F,M,T,E$ before the structural rows name the packet.

Apply the reflector row $F$ to the displayed completion request $C$. A repeated reflector read has no additional coordinate to inspect; it can only replay the same completion request, monad bookkeeping, metric completion handoff, and real seal through $H,K,P,N$.

The route therefore behaves idempotently at the level of visible BEDC rows. Since quotient classes, selected representatives, host equality, choice, and ambient completeness are absent from the classifier, no consumer can turn the reflected packet into a host quotient completion theorem.
\end{proof}
