\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FiniteOpenCoverIntervalUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-finite-open-cover-interval-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive compactness of closed intervals, the $\FiniteOpenCoverIntervalUp$ packet records a finite open-cover certificate by displaying endpoints, cover rows, and dyadic refinements rather than importing compactness as a primitive. It sits beside the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite dyadic interval-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}, and the dyadic mesh refinement route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-mesh-refinement-namecert}. The packet names only finite interval-cover data; it does not assert arbitrary Heine--Borel compactness, choose a Lebesgue number, select a limiting point, or provide ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Finite open-cover interval carrier]
\label{def:finite-open-cover-interval-carrier}
A $\FiniteOpenCoverIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(E,U,M,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the interval endpoint row, recording the left and right endpoint reads through $\RealUp$ together with their dyadic boundary approximations through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$.
\item $U$ is the finite $\ProbeBundle$ open-cover row, listing the displayed open probes, their interval-hit predicates, and the finite cover ledger inspected by the packet.
\item $M$ is the dyadic mesh refinement row, read through $\DyadicMeshRefinementUp$ and the finite dyadic interval-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}.
\item $W$ is the membership-witness row assigning every displayed mesh cell to a cover probe whose openness witness contains that cell.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoint rows, cover probes, mesh refinements, membership witnesses, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an interval-cover request through endpoint inspection, finite cover inspection, dyadic mesh refinement, and membership witnessing.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteOpenCoverIntervalUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\DyadicMeshRefinementUp$, $\ProbeBundle$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteOpenCoverIntervalUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $E,U,M,W,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary open cover over an unbounded domain, a chosen Lebesgue number, a selected interval point, a quotient real representative, or ambient compactness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FiniteOpenCoverInterval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-open-cover-interval-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteOpenCoverIntervalUp$ carrier $O=(E,U,M,W,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteOpenCoverIntervalUp}$ surface consists exactly of five certificate rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed endpoint and finite-cover packet;
\item endpoint exposure through $E$ along the $\RealUp$ and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ sibling routes;
\item finite open-cover exposure through the $\ProbeBundle$ row $U$;
\item dyadic mesh refinement and membership witnessing through $M$ and $W$;
\item structural stability through componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and local naming $N$.
\end{enumerate}
Every downstream compact-uniform, density-net, or interval consumer must read those five rows before treating the packet as a finite interval-cover certificate. The route exports no arbitrary compactness principle, no selected Lebesgue number, no point selector, no quotient real representative, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted packet of \autoref{def:finite-open-cover-interval-carrier}. The endpoint row $E$, finite cover row $U$, mesh row $M$, membership row $W$, transport row $H$, replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local name row $N$ are exactly the displayed coordinates.

Read the sibling routes in order. The real-name seal supplies the endpoint read, the dyadic arithmetic core supplies finite boundary approximations, the dyadic interval-net route supplies the finite cell surface, and the dyadic mesh refinement route supplies the refinement ledger.

The cover row is finite because it is a displayed $\ProbeBundle$ row, and the membership row assigns each displayed mesh cell to one displayed open probe. Thus the certificate is consumed through finite rows rather than through an unrecorded compactness theorem.

Finally replay the structural rows. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming preserve the same route and add no coordinate for an arbitrary compactness principle, a selected Lebesgue number, a point selector, quotient real representatives, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
