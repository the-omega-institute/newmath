\begin{theorem}[BishopFanCompactUniform NameCert obligations]\label{thm:compact-uniform-bishop-fan-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopFanCompactUniformUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopFanCompactUniformUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for compact-source observations, continuous-map rows, finite fan-bar data, local pointwise moduli, uniform-modulus handoff, dyadic tolerance comparison, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item compact-source exposure, requiring every source observation to enter through $K$ before any modulus row is read;
\item continuous-map exposure, requiring the pointwise modulus ledger $L$ to be tied to the displayed $\ContinuousMapUp$ row $M$;
\item fan-bar coverage, requiring the finite bar $B$ to bound all accepted compact-source cases before $U$ is exposed;
\item modulus-route exactness, requiring $U$ to be produced only from $K,M,B,L,D$ and excluding arbitrary open covers, selected branches, selected external radii, quotient map equality, countable choice, Lean verification, and bridge checking.
\end{enumerate}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The source and map exposure obligations are forced because the carrier has no alternate compact-space or function coordinate. Every local modulus row is therefore read as a row over the displayed $K,M$ boundary.

The finite bar row $B$ supplies the coverage boundary. The dyadic ledger $D$ compares the finitely many local rows in $L$ and records the tolerance handoff consumed by $U$.

Transport $H$ and replay $C$ preserve the same finite route before provenance and local naming. Since the carrier has no row for arbitrary open covers, branch selectors, external radius selection, quotient map equality, choice, Lean verification, or bridge data, the stated obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
