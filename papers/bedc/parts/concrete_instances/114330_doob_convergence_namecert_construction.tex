\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DoobConvergenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-doob-convergence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Doob, the $\DoobConvergenceUp$ packet names the finite convergence-readout surface that sits beside the martingale convergence packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-convergence-namecert}. It consumes the probability, random-variable, conditional-expectation, martingale, stopping-time, optional-stopping, upcrossing, Doob-upcrossing, martingale-convergence, rational, and real certificates of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-randomvar-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-condexp-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stoppingtime-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-optionalstopping-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-upcrossing-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-doob-upcrossing-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-convergence-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet records the bounded finite NameCert obligations needed to read a convergence-admission row; it does not introduce measure completion, almost-sure quotient equality, unbounded stopping choice, or a completed limiting-event carrier.

\begin{definition}[Doob convergence carrier]
\label{def:doob-convergence-carrier}
A $\DoobConvergenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(\Omega,X,E,M,T,O,U,B,A,Q,R,L,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\Omega$ is the consumed $\ProbSpaceUp$ row.
\item $X$ is the $\RandomVarUp$ endpoint-family row for the displayed martingale window.
\item $E$ is the $\CondExpUp$ successor-face row used by the martingale law.
\item $M$ is the finite $\MartingaleUp$ adapted-sequence packet.
\item $T$ is the $\StoppingTimeUp$ bounded index row.
\item $O$ is the $\OptionalStoppingUp$ stopped-window row.
\item $U$ is the $\UpcrossingUp$ crossing-count ledger.
\item $B$ is the $\DoobUpcrossingUp$ bound row consumed by the convergence readout.
\item $A$ is the $\MartingaleConvergenceUp$ finite convergence-admission row.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ rational tolerance ledger used to read finite windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ boundary and bounded-window row.
\item $L$ is the Doob convergence readout ledger recording which finite endpoint windows, rational tolerances, real bounds, upcrossing rows, and admission rows may be inspected together.
\item $W$ records the local source alignment among $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\RandomVarUp$, $\CondExpUp$, $\MartingaleUp$, $\StoppingTimeUp$, $\OptionalStoppingUp$, $\UpcrossingUp$, $\DoobUpcrossingUp$, $\MartingaleConvergenceUp$, $\RatUp$, and $\RealUp$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all displayed rows.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ routes from finite endpoint windows through bounded stopping, upcrossing, Doob-bound, martingale-convergence, rational, real-boundary, and convergence-readout rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over the displayed rows, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DoobConvergenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier is exactly this finite packet. It has no coordinate for measure completion, almost-sure quotient classes, unbounded stopping-time selection, pathwise limiting events, Brownian specialization, or an ambient convergence theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Doob convergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:doob-convergence-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:doob-convergence-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\DoobConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for
$$
\Omega,X,E,M,T,O,U,B,A,Q,R,L,W,H,C,P,N,
$$
probability and endpoint exposure through $\Omega,X,E,M$, bounded stopping exposure through $T,O$, upcrossing and Doob-bound exposure through $U,B$, martingale-convergence admission through $A$, rational and real readback through $Q,R$, convergence-readout exactness through $L$, source alignment through $W$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:doob-convergence-carrier}. Its rows list the consumed probability, random-variable, conditional-expectation, martingale, stopping-time, optional-stopping, upcrossing, Doob-upcrossing, martingale-convergence, rational, real, readout, source-alignment, transport, replay, provenance, and naming data.

The martingale-facing rows are $\Omega,X,E,M,T,O$. They are consumed only as finite probability, endpoint, conditional-expectation, martingale, and bounded stopped-window packets.

The crossing-facing rows are $U,B,A$. The upcrossing row and Doob-bound row must be displayed before the martingale-convergence admission row can be read, matching the sibling route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-convergence-namecert}.

The readback rows are $Q,R,L,W,H,C,P,N$. They record rational tolerance, real boundary, finite convergence readout, source alignment, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since no remaining coordinate names measure completion, almost-sure quotient equality, unbounded stopping choice, or a limiting-event carrier, the NameCert obligations are exactly the displayed finite rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Doob convergence bounded readout nonescape]
\label{thm:doob-convergence-bounded-readout-nonescape}
Every accepted $\DoobConvergenceUp$ carrier reads a convergence-facing row only through the finite handoff from $\MartingaleConvergenceUp$: bounded stopped windows, lower and upper $\RealUp$ boundary rows, $\RatUp$ tolerance rows, consumed $\UpcrossingUp$ and $\DoobUpcrossingUp$ ledgers, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ naming. A consumer that replaces this route by measure completion, almost-sure quotient equality, unbounded stopping choice, or an ambient limiting event leaves the accepted packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Open the accepted packet and project $A,L,Q,R,U,B$. The convergence-admission row $A$ is read only together with the readout ledger $L$, rational tolerance row $Q$, real boundary row $R$, crossing-count row $U$, and Doob-bound row $B$.

Use \autoref{thm:martingale-convergence-namecert-obligations} and \autoref{thm:martingale-convergence-finite-cauchy-admission}. They identify the martingale-convergence handoff as a finite bounded-window route over stopping, upcrossing, rational-readback, and real-boundary rows, not as an ambient limiting theorem.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:doob-convergence-namecert-obligations}. It adds the local source-alignment, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows $W,H,C,P,N$ and confirms that the $\DoobConvergenceUp$ carrier has no coordinate for measure completion, almost-sure quotient equality, unbounded stopping choice, or a limiting-event carrier.
\end{proof}
