The golden-mean subshift of finite type, the space of two-sided binary sequences with no two adjacent $1$s, has a single weighted dynamical zeta
$$
\zeta(t,y)=\frac{1}{1-t-yt^2},
$$
whose reciprocal is the spectral determinant of the weighted transfer matrix $T_y=\begin{pmatrix}1&y\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$. This chapter is the organizing summary of the finite, axiom-free certificate program built around that one object. It records the central algebraic identity, the table of finite facets that are shadows of it, and a frank statement of the program's boundary. It proves no new theorem; it names the completed envelope.

\section{The Central Norm Identity}
\label{sec:window6-zeta-program-central}

The organizing algebra is the rank-two quadratic ring
$$
Q_y=\mathbb Z[y][x]/(x^2-x-y),\qquad \bar x=1-x,
$$
whose regular representation of multiplication by $x$ in the basis $(x,1)$ is exactly $T_y$. The single identity from which the determinant face of the whole program descends is the quadratic norm
$$
N_{Q_y/\mathbb Z[y]}(1-xt)=(1-xt)\bigl(1-\bar x t\bigr)=1-t-yt^2,
$$
equivalently the matrix form $(I-tT_y)\bigl(I+t(T_y-I)\bigr)=(1-t-yt^2)I$, resting on the root relation $x^2=x+y$ (that is $T_y^2=T_y+yI$). The trace stream $C_n(y)=\operatorname{tr}(T_y^n)=\operatorname{Tr}_{Q_y}(x^n)$ and the Fibonacci-polynomial power law $x^{n+1}=F_{n+1}(y)\,x+yF_n(y)$ are the finite carriers that the individual facets read off in different ways.

\begin{theorem}[Organizing norm identity]
\label{thm:window6-zeta-program-norm}
In $Q_y[t]$ the quadratic norm of $1-xt$ equals the golden-mean zeta denominator $1-t-yt^2$, and its regular representation is the finite matrix identity $(I-tT_y)(I+t(T_y-I))=(1-t-yt^2)I$. Every determinant-side fact of the program is a reading of this one norm.
\end{theorem}

\section{The Program Boundary}
\label{sec:window6-zeta-program-boundary}

This is a finite algebraic and dynamical-zeta certificate program for the golden-mean subshift of finite type. It is complete as such: the shift-of-finite-type identity $\zeta(t)=1/\det(I-tT_y)$ already controls the zeta, and the facets exhaust the main finite meanings of that determinant. It does not claim, and this program does not contain, any Riemann-Hypothesis-type analytic depth, any general subshift or sofic zeta classification, any prime-orbit asymptotics or analytic continuation, or a thermodynamic-formalism library.
