\chapter{Compact Completion Dense Image Name Certificate}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-compact-completion-dense-image-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\CompactCompletionDenseImageUp$ packet records the finite route by which compact metric source data enters a completion-facing dense-image certificate. It sits between the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the Cauchy-thread and limit-service surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives completion consumers one displayed NameCert route for compactness, density, regular readback, and real sealing; it does not choose completion representatives, identify quotient classes, prove open-cover compactness, or import ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Compact completion dense image carrier]
\label{def:compact-completion-dense-image-carrier}
A $\CompactCompletionDenseImageUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(K,M,E,T,S,R,F,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is a $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, including the finite metric and compactness-facing certificate data available before completion is read.
\item $M$ is a $\CompleteMetricUp$ handoff row for the completion-facing metric service consumed by the dense-image route.
\item $E$ is the dense embedding row from the compact source into the completion surface; it records only displayed image membership and dense-window access.
\item $T$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by finite density requests.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation-window row attached to the dense-image request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback row consumed before any real-facing seal is inspected.
\item $F$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for completion-facing distance and limit-name requests.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, completion handoff, dense embedding, tolerance ledger, finite windows, regular readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a compact-source request through $K,M,E,T,S,R,F,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactCompletionDenseImageUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactCompletionDenseImageUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. No coordinate stores an open-cover compactness proof, a selected completion representative, quotient equality of completion names, a host dense-image equality theorem, countable choice, or full $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompactCompletionDenseImage NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-completion-dense-image-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactCompletionDenseImageUp$ carrier
$$
D=(K,M,E,T,S,R,F,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactCompletionDenseImageUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item compact-source admission: the source row $K$ is the only compact metric data made visible to the dense-image certificate;
\item completion handoff: the row $M$ is the only completion-facing metric service read before the dense image can be consumed;
\item dense-image exposure: the row $E$ exposes image membership and finite density windows without selecting representatives in the completion;
\item tolerance-window readback: the rows $T,S,R$ force every density request to pass through the dyadic tolerance ledger, finite stream window, and regular rational readback before the real seal $F$;
\item provenance and name exhaustion: the rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same route and introduce no open-cover compactness proof, selected completion representative, quotient equality, host equality, countable choice, or full $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-completion-dense-image-carrier}. The visible completion route begins at the compact metric row $K$, reaches the completion handoff $M$, and exposes density only through the displayed embedding row $E$.

The finite readback rows are then forced in order. A density request first reads the dyadic tolerance ledger $T$, then the finite observation window $S$, then the regular rational readback row $R$, and only after those rows may it inspect the terminal real seal $F$.

Finally use the structural rows. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport in $H$ and replay in $C$ preserve the same compact source, completion handoff, dense embedding, tolerance, window, readback, and real-seal rows, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected completion representatives, quotient equality, host equality, countable choice, or full $\RealUp$ completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
