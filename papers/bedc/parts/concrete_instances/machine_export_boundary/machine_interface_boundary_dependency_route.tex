Use the existing registry route of \autoref{thm:machine-export-boundary-registry-route} and the registry export dependency of \autoref{thm:machine-export-boundary-registry-export-dependency}. Together they force the consumer through $\RegistryExportSurfaceUp$, $\MachineReadableAuditInterfaceUp$, $\CannotClaimExportGateUp$, and $\NameCertMorphismUp$ before the local export name is read.

Since no carrier coordinate skips from $\MachineInterfaceBoundaryUp$ directly to export, the accepted route cannot introduce unregistered export, unchecked theorem evidence, refusal erasure, hidden transport, or off-packet replay.
\end{proof}
