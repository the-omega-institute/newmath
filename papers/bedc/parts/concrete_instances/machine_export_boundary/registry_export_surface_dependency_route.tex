Every $\MachineExportBoundaryUp$ export acceptance consumes the registry export surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-registry-export-surface-namecert} for public export-row visibility and the audit transcript envelope of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-audit-transcript-envelope-namecert} for replayable transcript evidence before a machine-facing export is accepted. The registry row keeps the exported, blocked, horizon-facing, or formal-target status visible, while the audit envelope keeps theorem-evidence readback tied to a replay checksum. Thus an accepted machine export cannot be read as a bare machine target, an audit-only transcript, or an unregistered registry row; it is the joint route through $\RegistryExportSurfaceUp$, $\AuditTranscriptEnvelopeUp$, transport, replay, provenance, and local $\NameCert$ data.
