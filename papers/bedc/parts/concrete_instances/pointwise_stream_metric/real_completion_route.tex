\begin{theorem}[Pointwise stream metric real-completion route]
\label{thm:pointwise-stream-metric-real-completion-route}
For every accepted $\PointwiseStreamMetricUp$ packet, each completion-facing read factors through finite $\StreamNameUp$ coordinate windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows, $\RealUp$ distance seals, $\MetricCompletionUp$ limit witnesses, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row. The route reads the coordinate windows before the rational readback, the rational readback before the Real distance seal, and the Real seal before any metric-completion handoff.

The packet exports no quotient stream equality, no selected limit representative, and no ambient completeness theorem. It is the same finite L10 route used by the StreamName chapter of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the RegSeqRat chapter of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the Real chapter of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the metric-completion interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted $\PointwiseStreamMetricUp$ packet onto its coordinate-window, regular-rational readback, real-distance, completion-facing, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. The coordinate windows are finite $\StreamNameUp$ data, so a completion read cannot start from an ambient stream equality.

The $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows then expose the rational comparisons used by each displayed coordinate distance. The terminal $\RealUp$ seal is downstream of those readbacks, not a host real equality detached from them.

Only after those rows are fixed may a $\MetricCompletionUp$ consumer inspect a limit witness. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport and $\Cont$ replay preserve the same displayed route, while $\Pkg$ provenance and local $\NameCert$ naming export no selected representative or ambient completeness principle.
\end{proof}
