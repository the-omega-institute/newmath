\begin{theorem}[Pointwise stream metric completion sibling anchor]
\label{thm:pointwise-stream-metric-sibling-anchor}
Every $\MetricCompletionUp$ consumer of an accepted $\PointwiseStreamMetricUp$ packet must first read the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window rows, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, the $\RealUp$ seal, and the $\MetricSpaceUp$ distance rows before any completion-facing convergence data are available. The forward lattice route exports only this displayed pointwise metric packet; it does not supply host stream equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, or an ambient completion principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:pointwise-stream-metric-carrier}. The completion-facing analytic rows are the certified stream source $S$, finite pointwise windows $W$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, regular-rational readback $R$, terminal Real seal $E$, and displayed metric row $M$.

Apply the pointwise classifier and completion-separation routes in \autoref{thm:pointwise-stream-metric-pointwise-classifier} and \autoref{thm:pointwise-stream-metric-completion-separation}. They force a distance read to consume $S,W,D,R,E$ before $M$ is inspected, and they exclude completion data not carried by the same finite packet.

The $\MetricCompletionUp$ consumer therefore receives convergence-facing data only after the sibling chain
$$
\StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \DyadicRatCoreUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \RealUp \longrightarrow \MetricSpaceUp
$$
has been displayed. Since the packet contains no host stream equality, selected representative, choice schedule, or ambient completion coordinate, the forward lattice route is exhausted by those rows.
\end{proof}
