\begin{theorem}[Pointwise stream metric orphan lattice route]
\label{thm:pointwise-stream-metric-orphan-lattice-route}
Every $\MetricCompletionFiniteUp$ consumer of $\PointwiseStreamMetricUp$ must first expose the $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, and $\MetricSpaceUp$ rows of \autoref{def:pointwise-stream-metric-carrier} and \autoref{thm:pointwise-stream-metric-namecert-obligations}. Only after those rows are visible may the consumer enter the finite-window completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletionfinite-namecert}. The route exports no quotient stream equality, no selected completion representative, no ambient completeness theorem for $\RealUp$, and no host metric completion object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the pointwise stream metric carrier. The stream windows, dyadic tolerance ledger, regular rational readback, Real seal, and metric-space row are all displayed before a completion-facing read has a source.

Apply the NameCert obligations for the same packet. They assign the completion-facing dependency to the visible stream and metric rows rather than to an external completion object.

Thus a $\MetricCompletionFiniteUp$ consumer can read only the finite row lattice already exposed by $\PointwiseStreamMetricUp$, and the refused quotient, representative, completeness, and host-completion objects remain outside the route.
\end{proof}
