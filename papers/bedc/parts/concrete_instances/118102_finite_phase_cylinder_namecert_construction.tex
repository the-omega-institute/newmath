$$

\begin{theorem}[FinitePhaseCylinder NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-phase-cylinder-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FinitePhaseCylinderUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FinitePhaseCylinderUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, complex phase readout, boundary-circle exposure, disk or Poincare-metric readback, reversible transport, finite phase-window stability, refusal-row preservation, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Transport by $H$ is componentwise across $Z,S,D,U,W,R,C,P,N$. Replay through $C$ and provenance through $P,N$ preserve the same finite packet, exhausting the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
