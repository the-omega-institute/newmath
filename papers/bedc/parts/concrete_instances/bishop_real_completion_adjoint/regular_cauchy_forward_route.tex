\begin{theorem}[Bishop real completion adjoint regular Cauchy forward route]
\label{thm:bishop-real-completion-adjoint-regular-cauchy-forward-route}
Every public $\BishopRealCompletionAdjointUp$ read exposes the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row and the $\RealCompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ completion-facing ledger before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal is consumed. The route cannot collapse into quotient completion equality, selected limits, host real equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or an equality-only completion quotient.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-real-completion-adjoint-carrier}. The displayed route includes the regular readback row $R$, the universal-property row $U$, the terminal seal $E$, and the structural rows $J,T,H,C,P,N$.

Use the unit-extension handoff of \autoref{thm:bishop-real-completion-adjoint-unit-extension-handoff}. It places the completion-facing ledger upstream of readback and seal exposure.

Apply triangle-ledger exactness, \autoref{thm:bishop-real-completion-adjoint-triangle-ledger-exactness}. The exactness row keeps the readback leg and terminal seal leg in the same finite route, so the seal cannot be consumed before the regular Cauchy and universal-property rows have appeared.

Finally replay readback nonescape and seal nonescape. The carrier contains no quotient equality, selected limit, host equality, ambient completeness, or equality-only quotient row, and the structural rows preserve that absence.
\end{proof}
