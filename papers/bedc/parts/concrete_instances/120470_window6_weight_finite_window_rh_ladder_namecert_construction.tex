\chapter{The Window6 Weight Polynomial: A Finite-Window Positivity Ladder on a Fibonacci Carrier}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-window6-weight-finite-window-rh-ladder}
\origin{ai}

The Fibonacci-cube weight polynomial $W_m(x)=\sum_k b(m,k)x^k$, with $b(m,k)$ the number of length-$m$ no-adjacent-$1$ words of weight $k$ and $\sum_k b(m,k)=F_{m+2}$, is a finite carrier for the positivity conditions that the finite-window formulation of the Riemann Hypothesis asks of the Riemann $\xi$ function. The Laguerre--Polya route reads $\mathrm{RH}$ as hyperbolicity of finite Jensen windows; its degree-two shadow is coefficient log-concavity, and its stronger shadows are the Newton inequalities, Toeplitz total positivity, and full real-rootedness. The finite positivity ladder consumes the Laguerre--Polya carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-laguerre-polya-namecert} as the sibling hyperbolicity route while keeping this chapter's own claims finite-window only. The same finite Fibonacci-cube positivity ladder is a finite-window source packet consumed by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-criticalstripzetazerowitness-namecert}, while the present chapter still makes no analytic transport claim to $\mathrm{RH}$. This chapter records four rungs of that ladder as axiom-free finite certificates on the Fibonacci carrier, together with the structural fact that ties them to this branch's spectral-determinant constants. None of this is a proof of $\mathrm{RH}$; each rung is a finite positivity witness on a Fibonacci-cube surface.

\section{The Ladder}
\label{sec:window6-weight-rh-ladder}

The degree-two rung, coefficient log-concavity $b(m,k)^2\ge b(m,k-1)b(m,k+1)$, is recorded separately as the degree-two log-concavity rung. The present chapter adds three sharper rungs and the recurrence that forces them.

\begin{theorem}[Newton ultra-log-concavity]
\label{thm:window6-weight-newton-ultra}
For $m=6,\ldots,10$ the weight coefficients satisfy the binomial-normalised Newton inequalities
$$
b(m,k-1)\,b(m,k+1)\,(k+1)\,(n-k+1)\ \le\ b(m,k)^2\,k\,(n-k),
$$
with $n$ the degree of $W_m$. This is the coefficient shadow of real-rootedness that is strictly sharper than plain log-concavity.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Toeplitz total positivity]
\label{thm:window6-weight-toeplitz}
For $m=6,\ldots,10$ the weight-coefficient sequence has nonnegative $2\times2$ and $3\times3$ Toeplitz minors, the discrete Polya-frequency strengthening that a real-rooted polynomial's coefficient sequence carries.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Fibonacci recurrence and real-rootedness]
\label{thm:window6-weight-fibonacci-recurrence}
The weight coefficients obey the Fibonacci polynomial recurrence
$$
b(m+2,k+1)=b(m+1,k+1)+b(m,k),
$$
that is $W_m(x)=W_{m-1}(x)+x\,W_{m-2}(x)$, with generating function $\sum_m W_m(x)z^m=(1-z-xz^2)^{-1}$. At $x=1$ this is the golden-mean generating function $(1-z-z^2)^{-1}$. The recurrence forces interlacing, hence real-rootedness of every $W_m$; the linear-times-quadratic factorisation of $W_6$ is recorded explicitly.
\end{theorem}

\section{The Spectral-Determinant Identification}
\label{sec:window6-weight-spectral-identification}

The generating function of the ladder is not a new object. With the weighted golden-mean transfer matrix
$$
T(y)=\begin{pmatrix}1&y\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
$$
this branch's necklace spectral determinant already records
$$
\det\!\left(I-xT(y)\right)=1-x-yx^2,
$$
which at $y=1$ is the Fibonacci generating function $1-x-x^2$ whose reciprocal produces $F_{m+2}=\mathbf 1^{\top}T(1)^{m-1}\mathbf 1$ and $L_m=\operatorname{tr}T(1)^m$. Thus $W_m$ is the coefficient stream of $\det(I-xT(y))^{-1}$, and the branch's fine constants are the same data viewed spectrally: the golden ratio and its conjugate $\{\varphi,-1/\varphi\}$ are the eigenvalues of $T(1)$, equivalently the reciprocal poles of $1-x-x^2$, equivalently the roots governing the metallic recurrences $t^2-t-1$, $t^3-t-1$, $t^2-2t-1$, $t^3-t^2-1$ that this branch has certified elsewhere.

\begin{theorem}[Three readings of one determinant]
\label{thm:window6-weight-three-readings}
The finite objects
$$
\text{fine constants}\quad=\quad\operatorname{spec}T(y),\qquad
\text{RH-carrier}\quad=\quad\text{coefficients of }\det(I-xT(y))^{-1},
$$
are two readings of the single spectral determinant $\det(I-xT(y))=1-x-yx^2$; the positivity rungs of \autoref{sec:window6-weight-rh-ladder} are finite-window positivity conditions on the second reading, and the metallic constants are the first reading. The reflection-positivity link deficit of a golden self-substitution word, $\Delta_d=D_d-|\mu_d|^2\ge0$, is the same $2\times2$ Gram determinant shape as the degree-two rung, on the same golden constants.
\end{theorem}
