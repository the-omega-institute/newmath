\begin{theorem}[Regular-Cauchy finite product downstream projection route]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-finite-product-downstream-projection-route}
Every downstream $\RegularCauchyProductProjectionUp$ consumer of an accepted $\RegularCauchyFiniteProductUp$ packet reads the finite product packet's index row, component source rows, synchronized window family, dyadic product-budget ledger, final readback row, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal through the projection certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-product-projection-namecert}. The projection route exports no host product object, selected component representative, quotient stream equality, countable choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the finite product carrier through its displayed index, source, synchronized window, dyadic budget, readback, and Real seal rows. These are the rows already consumed by the product projection dependency route.

Now read the downstream projection packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-product-projection-namecert}. Its left and right coordinate reads are admitted only after the finite product route has supplied the shared product index, component sources, windows, budget, and final readback.

The terminal $\RealUp$ seal remains downstream of that route and upstream of the local projection naming rows. Since no coordinate in the finite product packet carries a host product, selected component representative, quotient stream equality, choice schedule, or ambient completeness object, the downstream projection consumer cannot export any of them.
\end{proof}
