It sits over the dyadic arithmetic surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite request windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Dyadic rational dense-embedding carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-rat-dense-embedding-carrier}
A $\DyadicRatDenseEmbeddingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(D,S,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row naming the dyadic rational input and its signed numerator, denominator exponent, order, and arithmetic witness rows.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ row that opens the finite precision windows through which the dyadic input is observed.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ constant-readback row whose every inspected rational entry is the dyadic value named by $D$.
\item $L$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the dyadic input, stream windows, regular-rational readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay route from a finite precision request through $D,S,R$ to $L$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicRatDenseEmbeddingUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicRatDenseEmbeddingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets whose real-name consumer reads the same displayed rows $D,S,R,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary rational approximation, quotient equality of real names, a selected limit, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic rational dense-embedding NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-rat-dense-embedding-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicRatDenseEmbeddingUp$ carrier $E=(D,S,R,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicRatDenseEmbeddingUp}$ surface consists exactly of five finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed dyadic input row $D$ and its finite arithmetic witnesses;
\item constant-readback exactness saying that every inspected entry of $R$ reads the same dyadic value named by $D$;
\item window stability saying that $S$ exposes only finite precision requests and transports them through the displayed $\hsame$ row $H$;
\item Real seal handoff saying that $L$ is consumed only after $D,S,R$ have been replayed through $C$;
\item provenance and naming exactness through $P$ and $N$ over the same finite packet.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-rat-dense-embedding-carrier}. The dyadic row $D$ is the only numeric source, so carrier admission has no hidden rational or real coordinate.

Read the constant regular-rational name through $S$ and $R$. Each finite precision request opens a StreamName window, and the readback row repeats the dyadic value already named by $D$ at that window.

The Real seal $L$ is downstream of those rows. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same dyadic, stream, and regular-rational coordinates before any real-name consumer may read $L$.

Finally $P$ and $N$ record package provenance and local naming for exactly the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient real equality, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the five rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic rational dense-embedding Real seal handoff]
\label{thm:dyadic-rat-dense-embedding-real-seal-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$ seal consumer of an accepted $\DyadicRatDenseEmbeddingUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp
\longmapsto
\StreamNameUp
\longmapsto
\RegSeqRatUp
\longmapsto
\RealUp .
$$
The consumer first reads the dyadic rational row $D$, then the finite StreamName windows $S$, then the constant regular-rational readback $R$, and only then reaches the terminal Real seal $L$. The handoff exports no quotient stream, host real equality, selected limit, countable choice, or ambient real-completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:dyadic-rat-dense-embedding-namecert-obligations}. It places the dyadic input row before the finite window and readback rows.

The StreamName row $S$ controls which finite precision requests are inspected. The RegSeqRat row $R$ answers those requests by the constant dyadic value supplied by $D$.

The Real seal row $L$ is therefore a terminal consumer row. It is reached only through the displayed replay $C$ and transported only by the componentwise row $H$.

The excluded objects are absent from the carrier. Hence every accepted Real-facing read remains inside the displayed dyadic-to-regular-Cauchy route.
\end{proof}
