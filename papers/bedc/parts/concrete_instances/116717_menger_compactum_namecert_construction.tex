\begin{theorem}[Menger compactum NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:menger-compactum-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The readback row $R$ is downstream of those displayed rows. It seals only the distance and radius data already exposed by the metric, compactness, dimension, and entropy surfaces.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve that finite route.
