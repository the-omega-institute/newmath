\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniformCompletionUnitModulusUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-completion-unit-modulus-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis and the uniform-completion
practice of Bourbaki and Kelley, the $\UniformCompletionUnitModulusUp$ packet
records the finite unit-map modulus route used by L10 completion consumers. It
sits over the uniform-completion carrier of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformcompletion-namecert}, the completion
universal-property route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completionuniversalproperty-namecert}, the
finite StreamName windows of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational
readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic
tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and
the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The
packet names the displayed modulus by which the unit map enters completion; it
does not quotient Cauchy names, select representatives, import countable
choice, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Uniform completion unit modulus carrier]
\label{def:uniform-completion-unit-modulus-carrier}
A $\UniformCompletionUnitModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(X,I,M,D,W,R,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source uniform-space row whose unit map is being read by a
completion consumer.
\item $I$ is the unit-map row from $X$ into the displayed
$\UniformCompletionUp$ boundary.
\item $M$ is the modulus ledger assigning each requested uniform tolerance a
finite dyadic control row.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row used by the ledger $M$.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row selected by $M$ and checked
against $D$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row extracted from the displayed
window and tolerance data.
\item $S$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the readback row $R$ is
visible.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for
$X,I,M,D,W,R,S,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a unit-map request through the
modulus ledger, dyadic tolerance, finite window, readback, seal, provenance,
and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformCompletionUnitModulusUp$,
$\UniformCompletionUp$, $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$,
$\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$,
and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local
$\NameCert_{\UniformCompletionUnitModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no
coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, host
uniform-space equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform completion unit modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-completion-unit-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformCompletionUnitModulusUp$ carrier
$$
U=(X,I,M,D,W,R,S,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UniformCompletionUnitModulusUp}$ surface consists
exactly of source uniform admission, unit-map admission, modulus-ledger
exactness, dyadic tolerance admission, StreamName window admission, RegSeqRat
readback, Real seal nonescape, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed
$\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:uniform-completion-unit-modulus-carrier}. The analytic rows are
$X,I,M,D,W,R,S$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The source row $X$ and unit-map row $I$ place the read inside the
$\UniformCompletionUp$ and $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ corridor. The
modulus row $M$ is the only coordinate that answers a tolerance request, and it
answers only by displaying the dyadic row $D$ and the finite window row $W$.

The readback row $R$ is downstream of $M,D,W$. Thus a completion consumer may
read regular rational data only after the finite tolerance and window ledger is
visible.

Finally the seal row $S$ is downstream of $R$, while $H,C,P,N$ transport,
replay, package, and name the same route. Since no excluded host object appears
as a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert
obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform completion unit modulus RegSeqRat route]
\label{thm:uniform-completion-unit-modulus-regseqrat-route}
Every $\RegSeqRatUp$ read exported by an accepted
$\UniformCompletionUnitModulusUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
X,I,M,D,W,R,H,C,P,N .
$$
The route first displays the unit map, the modulus ledger, the dyadic tolerance
row, and the StreamName window row; it exports no quotient stream equality,
selected Cauchy representative, countable-choice schedule, or host equality of
completion points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:uniform-completion-unit-modulus-namecert-obligations} to
identify the accepted obligation surface. The only row with regular rational
readback content is $R$.

The rows $M,D,W$ are ordered before $R$ by the carrier itself. They provide the
displayed modulus, dyadic tolerance, and finite window needed for the readback,
so a consumer cannot reach $R$ from an unlisted stream coordinate or selected
representative.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that same route for transport, replay,
provenance, and naming. Hence the RegSeqRat-facing read is exactly the displayed
finite unit-modulus route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform completion unit modulus Real seal]
\label{thm:uniform-completion-unit-modulus-real-seal}
Every $\RealUp$-facing read exported by an accepted
$\UniformCompletionUnitModulusUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
X,I,M,D,W,R,S,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the Real seal is reached only after the unit map, modulus ledger,
DyadicRatCore tolerance, StreamName window, and RegSeqRat readback rows are
visible. It does not export host real equality, quotient completion equality,
selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the RegSeqRat route of
\autoref{thm:uniform-completion-unit-modulus-regseqrat-route}. It places the
readback row $R$ after the displayed unit-map, modulus, tolerance, and window
rows.

The seal row $S$ is the only Real-facing coordinate in the carrier, and it is
downstream of $R$. Therefore the Real read cannot bypass the finite
unit-modulus ledger.

Componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming
preserve the same finite chain. Since the carrier has no row for the excluded
objects, the Real-facing endpoint is a seal boundary, not an ambient
completeness principle.
\end{proof}
