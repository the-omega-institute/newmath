\begin{definition}[Real uniform Cauchy-filter completion carrier]
\label{def:real-uniform-cauchy-filter-completion-carrier}
A $\RealUniformCauchyFilterCompletionUp$ carrier is a packet
$$
Q=(R,U,F,W,Q_R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
where $R$ is the displayed $\RealUniformStructureUp$ source row, $U$ is the induced uniform-entourage row, $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ completion-facing row reached through a displayed $\CauchyFilterUp$ basis, $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row, $Q_R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row over the same window, $E$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius and error ledger ending in a $\RealUp$ seal, $H$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $C$ is displayed $\Cont$ replay, $P$ is $\Pkg$ provenance over the listed rows, and $N$ is the local $\NameCert$ row. The carrier predicate asserts that every completion read reaches $F$ only after the rows $R,U,W,Q_R,E,H,C,P,N$ have been displayed in one finite $\BHist$ packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real uniform completion entourage route]
\label{thm:real-uniform-cauchy-filter-completion-entourage-route}
Every accepted $\RealUniformCauchyFilterCompletionUp$ packet $Q=(R,U,F,W,Q_R,E,H,C,P,N)$ routes a completion-facing entourage request through the $\RealUniformStructureUp$ source row $R$, the generated uniform row $U$, the $\CauchyFilterUp$ basis displayed inside the $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ row $F$, the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row $W$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $Q_R$, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius ledger $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row before it is offered to any completion consumer.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows. The only row that can expose a Real-induced entourage is $R$, and the only row that can present the uniform basis to completion consumers is $U$.

The Cauchy-filter completion row $F$ is downstream of that basis. A completion read must therefore pass through the displayed $\CauchyFilterUp$ basis and then through the finite window and readback rows $W,Q_R$ before it can inspect the completion-facing row.

The dyadic radius ledger $E$ supplies the finite tolerance data and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. Transport, replay, provenance, and naming are confined to $H,C,P,N$, so the route remains one finite $\BHist$ handoff.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real uniform completion window handoff]
\label{thm:real-uniform-cauchy-filter-completion-window-handoff}
Let $Q=(R,U,F,W,Q_R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RealUniformCauchyFilterCompletionUp$ carrier. Any completion-facing tail comparison over the Real-induced uniformity reads the $\StreamNameUp$ window row $W$ and its $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $Q_R$ before it can consume the $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ row $F$ or the terminal $\RealUp$ seal recorded by $E$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a tail comparison addressed to the completion route. The carrier exposes the stream coordinates only through $W$, so no comparison can inspect the Real source before the finite window is displayed.

The readback row $Q_R$ is attached to the same window. It converts the inspected coordinates into the regular rational data consumed by the dyadic radius ledger $E$ and by the Cauchy-filter basis inside $F$.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this same handoff. Thus the completion consumer receives a windowed $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ route rather than an ambient sequence, selected representative, or quotient-completion equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real uniform completion nonescape]
\label{thm:real-uniform-cauchy-filter-completion-nonescape}
Every consumer of an accepted $\RealUniformCauchyFilterCompletionUp$ packet is confined to the displayed $\RealUniformStructureUp$, $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows. The packet exports no quotient filter, quotient real equality, selected limit, countable choice schedule, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the coordinates of the carrier. They list a Real-induced uniform source, a generated uniform row, a completion-facing Cauchy-filter row, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance data, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Apply the entourage route and the window handoff. Together they force each completion-facing request through the same finite rows before any completion read is available.

There is no carrier coordinate for quotient filters, quotient real equality, selected limits, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness. Hence those claims cannot be exported by the local packet.
\end{proof}
