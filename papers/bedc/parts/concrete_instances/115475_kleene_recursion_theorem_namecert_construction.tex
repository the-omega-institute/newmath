\begin{theorem}[Kleene recursion theorem NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kleene-recursion-theorem-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KleeneRecursionTheoremUp$ carrier $K=(E,Q,W,F,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\KleeneRecursionTheoremUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed computable transformer row $E$, finite syntax and index quotation through $Q$, self-application exposure through $W$, fixed-index witnessing through $F$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:kleene-recursion-theorem-carrier}. The only computability-facing row is $E$, the only quotation row is $Q$, and the only self-reference rows are $W$ and $F$.

The structural rows do not add semantic content. Transport through $H$ compares the displayed rows componentwise, while replay through $C$ exposes the same finite transformer, quotation, self-application, and witness route to consumers.

Finally $P$ and $N$ source and name the packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores host partial-function equality, quotient program equality, oracle choice, Rice classification, or Myhill minimization, the NameCert surface is exhausted by the listed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kleene recursion theorem fixed-index window]
\label{thm:kleene-recursion-theorem-fixed-index-window}
Every fixed-index read from an accepted $\KleeneRecursionTheoremUp$ packet must expose the finite route
$$
E \longmapsto Q \longmapsto W \longmapsto F
$$
before the witness is consumed. The route reads the $\ComputableUp$ transformer, the $\LambdaCalcUp$ and $\FirstOrderUp$ quotation row, the self-application window, and the fixed-index witness as one $\BHist$ packet. It cannot be cited as semantic equality of partial functions, quotient equality of program indices, unrestricted partiality, oracle choice, Rice theorem, or Myhill theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:kleene-recursion-theorem-namecert-obligations}. It makes $E,Q,W,F$ the consumer-visible recursion-theorem rows and keeps $H,C,P,N$ structural.

The witness row $F$ is therefore not detached from the transformer. It is read only after the finite quotation row $Q$ and self-application window $W$ have been displayed over the same computable transformer row $E$.

The exclusions follow by coordinate exhaustion. The carrier contains no row for host semantic equality, quotient program equality, unrestricted partiality, oracle selection, Rice classification, or Myhill minimization, so none of those endpoints can be exported by the fixed-index window.
\end{proof}
