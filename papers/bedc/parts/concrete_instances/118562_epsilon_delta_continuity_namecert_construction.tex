\begin{definition}[Epsilon-delta continuity carrier]
\label{def:epsilon-delta-continuity-carrier}

$$

\item $Y$ is the target $\MetricSpaceUp$ row, including the target-distance witness surface and its terminal $\RealUp$ distance seal.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ graph row for the named map, exposing only the finite graph and target-window reads used by the continuity certificate.

\begin{theorem}[Epsilon-delta continuity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:epsilon-delta-continuity-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:epsilon-delta-continuity-carrier}. The source metric row $X$, target metric row $Y$, displayed graph row $F$, and point row $a$ are explicit coordinates, so a continuity consumer cannot begin from an ambient function or an unshown host point.

The tolerance part is local. The target request $E$ and source-radius witness $D$ are paired before the finite window row $W$ is consumed. Thus the target-distance read reaches the $\RealUp$ seal of $Y$ only after the source-neighbourhood window and the graph cells of $F$ have been displayed.

The two sibling handoffs keep the packet connected to the surrounding metric route. The $\ModulusContinuityUp$ row $M$ exposes the same tolerance-to-window data to modulus-facing consumers, while the $\EpsilonDeltaLimitUp$ row $L$ records compatibility with the limit packet at $a$ without adding a selected limit or quotient real equality.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for classical open-cover compactness, host function equality, quotient real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
