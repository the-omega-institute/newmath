$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $U$ is the upper endpoint row accepted by $\DyadicRatCoreUp$.
\item $R$ is the roundedness and refinement row, recording endpoint order, directed refinement, and the outward-rounding witnesses used when a later interval narrows the present one.
\item $\Delta$ is the diameter and tolerance row, recording the dyadic width comparison and the requested enclosure tolerance.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row through which downstream consumers inspect the endpoint observations.

Next read $\Delta,W,Q,E$. Finally apply $H,C,P,N$. The carrier inventory contains no separate coordinate for completed-real selection, quotient equality, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, hidden host intervals, or ambient completeness. The route above is therefore the whole regular-sequence handoff.
