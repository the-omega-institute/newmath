\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SheafificationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sheafification-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Grothendieck's SGA 4 site formalism and the Mac Lane--Moerdijk account of sheaves in geometry and logic, the $\SheafificationUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which a displayed presheaf is routed to a sheaf-facing carrier. It sits after the categorical source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-category-namecert}, the topological and site carriers of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-grothendieck-topology-namecert}, the presheaf restriction surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-presheaf-namecert}, and the gluing target of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sheaf-namecert}. The packet names the separated-refinement, cover-local matching, plus-style gluing, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it is not a host sheafification functor, not a quotient of compatible families, and not a topos-level reflector.

\begin{definition}[Sheafification carrier]
\label{def:sheafification-carrier}
A $\SheafificationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(C,T,J,P,L,G,S,H,R,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\CategoryUp$ source row naming the displayed objects and arrows over which the presheaf is read.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ or site-open row supplying the indexed open windows used by cover requests.
\item $J$ is the $\GrothendieckTopologyUp$ coverage row, including covering sieves, pullback-stability rows, and local-transitivity rows.
\item $P$ is the $\PreSheafUp$ restriction row over $C,T,J$, with section carriers and restriction comparison data.
\item $L$ is the locality-refinement ledger, recording matching families after every displayed cover refinement.
\item $G$ is the gluing ledger, recording the accepted glued section row for each compatible local family.
\item $S$ is the resulting $\SheafUp$ target row, exposed only through the same locality and gluing data.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the category, topology, site, presheaf, locality, gluing, sheaf, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from presheaf restriction reads through the locality and gluing ledgers to the sheaf target.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SheafificationUp$, $\CategoryUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\GrothendieckTopologyUp$, $\PreSheafUp$, $\SheafUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SheafificationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $C,T,J,P,L,G,S,H,R,Q,N$. It has no coordinate for a host reflector, quotient of compatible families, global choice of covers, topos object, ambient powerset equality, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sheafification NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sheafification-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SheafificationUp$ carrier
$$
A=(C,T,J,P,L,G,S,H,R,Q,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SheafificationUp}$ surface consists exactly of category-source exposure through $C$, topology-window exposure through $T$, Grothendieck coverage through $J$, presheaf restriction exposure through $P$, locality-refinement rows through $L$, gluing rows through $G$, the sheaf target row through $S$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $R$, provenance through $Q$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sheafification-carrier}. The source side is the displayed category, topology or site window, Grothendieck coverage row, and presheaf restriction row.

The locality ledger $L$ records the cover-refinement comparisons that make matching families visible. The gluing ledger $G$ records the resulting glued section row only after those matching-family rows have been displayed.

The sheaf target $S$ is therefore read from $P,L,G$ over the same $C,T,J$ source. The structural rows $H,R,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same route. Since no host reflector, quotient family, selected cover, topos object, or powerset equality is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sheafification sheaf handoff]
\label{thm:sheafification-sheaf-handoff}
Every downstream $\SheafUp$ consumer of an accepted $\SheafificationUp$ packet receives only the displayed sheaf row $S$ together with its dependency route
$$
\CategoryUp,\TopologyUp,\GrothendieckTopologyUp,\PreSheafUp
\longmapsto L \longmapsto G \longmapsto \SheafUp .
$$
The handoff exposes the category source, topology or site windows, coverage row, presheaf restriction row, locality ledger, gluing ledger, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row; it does not expose a host sheafification functor, quotient of compatible families, selected cover system, topos object, or ambient powerset equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier projection. A sheaf-facing consumer can read $S$ only after $C,T,J,P,L,G$ have been displayed, because $S$ is the target row of that finite route.

Use the topology and site rows to interpret covers as displayed $\TopologyUp$ windows and $\GrothendieckTopologyUp$ covering sieves. The presheaf row $P$ supplies restriction data on those windows, while $L$ records locality under cover refinement and $G$ records the glued section row.

The handoff to $\SheafUp$ is therefore the same row package, transported by $H$ and replayed by $R$. The provenance and local naming rows $Q,N$ name the consumed siblings without adding a host reflector or quotient object.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sheafification coverage exactness]
\label{thm:sheafification-coverage-exactness}
Every cover-local read of an accepted $\SheafificationUp$ packet
$$
A=(C,T,J,P,L,G,S,H,R,Q,N)
$$
must expose the category row $C$, topology-window row $T$, Grothendieck-coverage row $J$, and presheaf-restriction row $P$ before any locality data in $L$ may be consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier tuple. The only cover source coordinates are $C,T,J,P$: $C$ supplies the $\CategoryUp$ objects and arrows, $T$ supplies the $\TopologyUp$ windows, $J$ supplies the $\GrothendieckTopologyUp$ covering and pullback-stability rows, and $P$ supplies the $\PreSheafUp$ restriction data over that source.

The locality ledger $L$ is typed by those displayed cover and restriction rows. A read that reaches $L$ without first exposing $C,T,J,P$ has no BEDC row from which cover-locality can be interpreted.

The structural rows $H,R,Q,N$ preserve this order. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row can carry the exposed source route, but none of them supplies an independent coverage coordinate. Thus coverage exactness forces every locality read through $C,T,J,P$ first.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sheafification locality refinement]
\label{thm:sheafification-locality-refinement}
For an accepted $\SheafificationUp$ packet, every matching-family row recorded in $L$ is stable under displayed $\GrothendieckTopologyUp$ refinement and componentwise $\hsame$ transport: replacing a cover by a displayed refinement in $J$ and transporting the component section rows by $H$ yields another locality row still recorded by $L$ over the same presheaf row $P$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read $L$ from the carrier tuple after $C,T,J,P$ have been exposed. By definition, $L$ is the locality-refinement ledger, so its entries are indexed by the displayed cover-refinement comparisons carried by the Grothendieck-topology row $J$.

Now apply the transport row $H$. It records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the category, topology, site, presheaf, locality, gluing, sheaf, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Transporting the component sections therefore preserves the same $P$-restriction comparisons rather than changing to a host family.

The replay row $R$ and provenance row $Q$ keep this refinement inside the same $\SheafificationUp$ package, and $N$ names it as part of the local $\NameCert_{\SheafificationUp}$ surface. Hence matching-family rows in $L$ are stable under the displayed refinement and transport data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sheafification gluing exactness]
\label{thm:sheafification-gluing-exactness}
The sheaf target row $S$ of an accepted $\SheafificationUp$ packet is consumed only after the locality ledger $L$ and gluing ledger $G$ have displayed compatible local sections for the presheaf row $P$ over the cover rows $T,J$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the route theorem \autoref{thm:sheafification-sheaf-handoff}. It places the sheaf-facing row $S$ downstream of the displayed category, topology, Grothendieck coverage, presheaf, locality, and gluing rows.

The ledger $L$ records the matching-family and refinement checks. The ledger $G$ records the accepted glued section row for compatible local families. Therefore $S$ has no independent coordinate from which a sheaf target can be read before $L$ and $G$ have been displayed.

Finally use $H,R,Q,N$. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ naming preserve the same route from $P$ through $L$ and $G$ to the $\SheafUp$ target row. They do not introduce a quotient of compatible families or a host sheafification functor, so the gluing read is exact at the displayed BEDC surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sheafification separated refinement exactness]
\label{thm:sheafification-separated-refinement-exactness}
Let
$$
A=(C,T,J,P,L,G,S,H,R,Q,N)
$$
be an accepted $\SheafificationUp$ carrier. Every separated-refinement read of $A$ must pass through the displayed category row $C$, topology-window row $T$, Grothendieck-coverage row $J$, presheaf-restriction row $P$, and locality ledger $L$ before any separated sheaf-facing row may be consumed. The route is transported by $\hsame$ through $H$, replayed by $\Cont$ through $R$, sourced by $\Pkg$ through $Q$, and named only by the local $\NameCert_{\SheafificationUp}$ row $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The carrier definition exposes separated refinement only as a locality entry in $L$. That row is indexed by the displayed source data $C,T,J,P$, so a separated read with no prior source exposure has no cover, no restriction comparison, and no matching-family ledger on which to stand.

Now apply the structural rows. The $\hsame$ transport $H$ moves the same displayed components, $\Cont$ replay $R$ records the same route, $\Pkg$ provenance $Q$ identifies the sibling packets, and $N$ names the local certificate. None of these rows creates an independent host reflector or quotient-compatible-family object.

Thus every separated-refinement read factors through $C,T,J,P,L$ before reaching the sheaf-facing surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sheafification plus gluing replay]
\label{thm:sheafification-plus-gluing-replay}
For an accepted $\SheafificationUp$ carrier
$$
A=(C,T,J,P,L,G,S,H,R,Q,N),
$$
every plus-style gluing replay must pass through the locality ledger $L$, gluing ledger $G$, sheaf row $S$, componentwise transport row $H$, and displayed replay row $R$. The replay remains a finite $\BHist$ route using $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ data; it is not a quotient construction of compatible families.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First read $L$. It records the compatible matching-family rows over the displayed cover and restriction data. Then read $G$, which records the accepted glued section row for those compatible families.

The sheaf row $S$ is downstream of that locality-and-gluing pair. Transport through $H$ and replay through $R$ carry the same finite route into a sheaf-facing consumer without adding a separate plus object.

Finally $Q$ and $N$ package and name the route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient families or selected covers, the plus-style replay is exactly the displayed path through $L,G,S,H,R$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sheafification source obligation package]
\label{thm:sheafification-source-obligation-package}
For an accepted $\SheafificationUp$ packet
$$
A=(C,T,J,P,L,G,S,H,R,Q,N),
$$
every sheaf-facing consumer must expose the $\CategoryUp$ source $C$, the $\TopologyUp$ or $\GrothendieckTopologyUp$ cover rows $T,J$, the $\PreSheafUp$ restriction row $P$, locality rows $L$, gluing rows $G$, the $\SheafUp$ target row $S$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $R$, $\Pkg$ provenance $Q$, and the local $\NameCert_{\SheafificationUp}$ row $N$ before it reads $S$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First apply \autoref{thm:sheafification-coverage-exactness}. It forces every locality read through $C,T,J,P$ before the ledger $L$ is available.

Next use \autoref{thm:sheafification-locality-refinement} and \autoref{thm:sheafification-gluing-exactness}. Locality rows in $L$ are stable under displayed refinement, and the sheaf target $S$ is consumed only after $L$ and $G$ have displayed compatible local sections over the presheaf row $P$.

The structural rows complete the package. The tuple projection supplies $H,R,Q,N$, and these rows transport, replay, source, and name the same route rather than adding a host reflector, quotient family, selected cover, or topos object.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sheafification local gluing induction]
\label{thm:sheafification-local-gluing-induction}
Finite induction over displayed cover-refinement rows in an accepted $\SheafificationUp$ packet preserves the route from locality to gluing: the base cover reads $C,T,J,P,L$, and each displayed refinement transports matching-family data by $H$ and replays the same $\Cont$ route $R$ before the gluing row $G$ and sheaf target $S$ are consumed. The induction exports no quotient of compatible families, selected cover system, host reflector, or ambient powerset equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the base cover, use \autoref{thm:sheafification-coverage-exactness}. The locality ledger is typed only after $C,T,J,P$ have been displayed, so the base matching-family row is already inside the finite carrier.

For a refinement step, apply \autoref{thm:sheafification-locality-refinement}. The displayed $\GrothendieckTopologyUp$ refinement row and the transport row $H$ preserve matching-family data over the same presheaf row $P$.

Finally use \autoref{thm:sheafification-gluing-exactness} together with \autoref{thm:sheafification-plus-gluing-replay}. The gluing ledger $G$ and target row $S$ are read only after the preserved locality route has been replayed by $R$, so finite induction stays inside $L,G,S,H,R,Q,N$ and never produces an external quotient or choice coordinate.
\end{proof}
