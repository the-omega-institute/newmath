The $\MetacicConfluenceForkCertificateUp$ packet realizes the parallel-diamond and substitution-compatibility fork of \autoref{ch:metacic-confluence-audit-map} as finite source, branch, candidate-join, residual-boundary, and NameCert rows. It sits beside the typed parallel-diamond boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-typed-parallel-diamond-boundary-namecert}, the confluence residual checker of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-confluence-residual-checker-namecert}, the residual envelope of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-confluence-residual-envelope-namecert}, the local-join ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-local-join-ledger-namecert}, and the residual frontier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-confluence-residual-frontier-namecert}. The packet names the finite fork certificate only; it does not discharge full parallel diamond, residual substitution compatibility, arbitrary closed-star confluence, or Church--Rosser for every closed source.

\begin{definition}[MetaCIC confluence fork certificate carrier]
\label{def:metacic-confluence-fork-certificate-carrier}
A $\MetacicConfluenceForkCertificateUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(S,L,R,J,B,D,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the typed MetaCIC source row whose local peak is inspected by the audit map.
\item $L$ and $R$ are the left and right branch rows of the fork, recording the two visible $\mathrm{BetaParallel}$ or $\mathrm{BetaStarStep}$ directions under inspection.
\item $J$ is the candidate-join row proposed for the two branches.
\item $B$ is the residual-boundary row naming the substitution-compatibility premise still required when the candidate join is not closed by a shape-limited fragment.
\item $D$ is the closed-fragment discharge row for normal, atom, and other already checked shapes.
\item $A$ is the audit-map readback row tying $S,L,R,J,B,D$ to the confluence fork named in \autoref{ch:metacic-confluence-audit-map}.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, branch, candidate-join, residual-boundary, discharge, audit, and local certificate rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from source through the two branches, candidate join, residual boundary, closed-fragment discharge, and audit readback.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance row over $\MetacicConfluenceForkCertificateUp$, the MetaCIC confluence audit map, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetacicConfluenceForkCertificateUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for erasing the substitution boundary, promoting a conditional bridge to an unconditional theorem, or turning closed-fragment discharge into global Church--Rosser.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC confluence fork certificate NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metacic-confluence-fork-certificate-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\MetacicConfluenceForkCertificateUp$ carrier $F=(S,L,R,J,B,D,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MetacicConfluenceForkCertificateUp}$ surface consists exactly of typed-source visibility, left-branch visibility, right-branch visibility, candidate-join visibility, residual-boundary visibility, closed-fragment discharge visibility, audit-map readback, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming for the finite packet of \autoref{def:metacic-confluence-fork-certificate-carrier}.

The certificate instructs future $\mathsf{Nontrivial}\ \MetacicConfluenceForkCertificateUp$ instances to separate a fork retaining $B$ from a fork claiming full discharge. It instructs future $\mathsf{FieldFaithful}\ \MetacicConfluenceForkCertificateUp$ instances to preserve $S,L,R,J,B,D,A,H,C,P,N$ as distinct rows, especially separating the candidate join $J$ from the residual-boundary row $B$ and the closed-fragment row $D$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The confluence-facing rows are $S,L,R,J,B,D,A$, and the support rows are $H,C,P,N$.

Read the source and branch rows first. They display the local peak under inspection and keep the two fork branches visible before any join is cited.

Read the candidate join, residual boundary, and closed-fragment discharge next. The candidate row proposes the join, the residual-boundary row retains the substitution-compatibility premise when needed, and the discharge row records only shape-limited closed cases already available to the audit map.

Finally use the audit readback, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows. They keep the fork certificate finite and source-traceable, but they do not erase $B$, promote a conditional bridge to an unconditional theorem, or convert $D$ into arbitrary closed-source confluence.
\end{proof}
