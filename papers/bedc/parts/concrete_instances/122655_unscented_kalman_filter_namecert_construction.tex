\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UnscentedKalmanFilterUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-unscented-kalman-filter-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Julier and Uhlmann on the unscented transform, the $\UnscentedKalmanFilterUp$ packet records a nonlinear filtering certificate whose sigma-point generation, propagation, covariance ledger, and update row are all displayed as finite BEDC data before a downstream estimator reads the filter. It sits beside the continuous-time $\KalmanBucyFilterUp$ packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kalman-bucy-filter-namecert} and the finite Gramian ledger of $\ObservabilityGramianUp$ in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-observability-gramian-namecert}. Its carrier is read through the $\MatrixUp$ rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, the $\VecSpaceUp$ rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, the $\ProbSpaceUp$ rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert}, and the $\RandomVarUp$ rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-randomvar-namecert}. The packet names only the finite unscented-filter ledger; it does not claim an ambient stochastic process, analytic convergence theorem, selected nonlinear model class, or optimality theorem.

\begin{definition}[UnscentedKalmanFilter carrier]
\label{def:unscented-kalman-filter-carrier}
An $\UnscentedKalmanFilterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(X,Y,\mu,\Sigma,S,W,F,G,\widehat{\mu},\widehat{\Sigma},Z,K,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ and $Y$ are the state and observation rows, each displayed through $\VecSpaceUp$ and $\MatrixUp$ coordinates;
\item $\mu$ and $\Sigma$ are the prior mean and covariance rows read through $\ProbSpaceUp$ and $\RandomVarUp$ provenance;
\item $S$ is the finite sigma-point row and $W$ is the corresponding weight row;
\item $F$ is the propagated state-sigma row and $G$ is the propagated observation-sigma row;
\item $\widehat{\mu}$ and $\widehat{\Sigma}$ are the predicted mean and covariance rows, and $Z,K,R$ are the innovation, gain, and residual-ledger rows;
\item $E$ records sibling readback through the $\KalmanBucyFilterUp$ and $\ObservabilityGramianUp$ surfaces without identifying the nonlinear sigma-point packet with either sibling;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport of state, observation, covariance, sigma-point, weight, propagation, innovation, gain, and residual rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for accepted prediction and update routes;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance naming $\UnscentedKalmanFilterUp$, $\KalmanBucyFilterUp$, $\ObservabilityGramianUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\RandomVarUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UnscentedKalmanFilterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
No external nonlinear dynamics oracle, host probability space, selected noise process, analytic approximation bound, or optimality certificate is part of the carrier.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[UnscentedKalmanFilter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:unscented-kalman-filter-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:unscented-kalman-filter-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\UnscentedKalmanFilterUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed packet $U=(X,Y,\mu,\Sigma,S,W,F,G,\widehat{\mu},\widehat{\Sigma},Z,K,R,E,H,C,P,N)$;
\item sigma-point classifier stability, saying that the accepted classifier depends only on the displayed $S,W,F,G$ rows and their stated state, observation, mean, and covariance sources;
\item covariance and update ledger exactness, saying that $\widehat{\mu}$, $\widehat{\Sigma}$, $Z$, $K$, and $R$ are read only through the displayed sigma-point, weight, propagated-observation, innovation, and residual rows;
\item componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$ for state, observation, covariance, sigma-point, weight, propagation, innovation, gain, residual, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$ for every prediction, update, and sibling-consumer route exported by the packet.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:unscented-kalman-filter-carrier}. Carrier admission is coordinate inspection: every row used by the certificate is a displayed $\BHist$ coordinate of $U$.

The sigma-point classifier is stable because it reads the finite sigma-point row $S$, weight row $W$, and propagated rows $F,G$ together with their displayed state, observation, mean, and covariance sources. Componentwise replacement by $\hsame$-matched rows preserves that finite shape.

The covariance and update ledger is exact by the same projection. The predicted mean, predicted covariance, innovation, gain, and residual rows have no route except through $S,W,F,G,\widehat{\mu},\widehat{\Sigma},Z,K,R$.

Finally, $H$ and $C$ are the only transport and replay rows. A downstream Kalman, Gramian, matrix, vector-space, probability, or random-variable consumer must therefore read the same finite packet through the package provenance $P$ and the local name row $N$, not through an undisplayed stochastic or analytic object.
\end{proof}
