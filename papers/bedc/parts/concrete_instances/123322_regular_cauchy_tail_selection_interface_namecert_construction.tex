$$

\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchyTailSelectionInterface NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-tail-selection-interface-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyTailSelectionInterfaceUp$ carrier
$$
I=(S,Q,T,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyTailSelectionInterfaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the source regular name, requested tolerance, accepted tail-selection criterion, finite StreamName window, dyadic tolerance ledger, regular rational readback, Real boundary row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Every public tail-selection read must pass through
$$
(S,Q)\longmapsto T\longmapsto(W,D,R)\longmapsto(E,H,C,P,N).
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-tail-selection-interface-carrier}. The tuple displays the source regular name, tolerance request, threshold row, window row, dyadic ledger, regular readback, Real boundary, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows as finite $\BHist$ data.

The threshold row $T$ is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-tail-selection-criterion-namecert}. It cannot be reconstructed from the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, because the displayed route exposes $T$ before $W,D,R,E$.

The sibling rows then determine the handoff. The $\StreamNameUp$ row supplies finite observations, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ supplies tolerance accounting, $\RegSeqRatUp$ supplies the regular readback, and $\RealUp$ supplies only the terminal boundary row. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this same packet, so the listed coordinates exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchyTailSelectionInterface Real boundary]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-tail-selection-interface-real-boundary}

$$

$$
Consequently the interface supplies a reusable finite tail-selection handoff for $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ consumers, while exporting no quotient stream equality, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-tail-selection-interface-namecert-obligations}. They place the threshold row $T$ before the finite window, dyadic ledger, regular readback, and Real boundary rows.

Now read the sibling anchors in order. The criterion chapter supplies the displayed threshold; the StreamName, DyadicRatCore, and RegSeqRat chapters supply $W,D,R$; and the Real chapter supplies only the terminal boundary row $E$.

Since the carrier has no quotient-stream, selected-limit, choice, trichotomy, host-equality, or ambient-completeness coordinate, a consumer cannot bypass $T,W,D,R$ and still remain a read of the same packet. Thus the Real boundary is a finite handoff boundary, not a completed Real construction principle.
