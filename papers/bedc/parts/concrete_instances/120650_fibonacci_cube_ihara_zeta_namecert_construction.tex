\chapter{Fibonacci-Cube Ihara Zeta}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-fibonacci-cube-ihara-zeta}
\origin{ai}

This chapter records a finite Ihara zeta computation for the Fibonacci-cube graphs. For $m\ge 1$, let $\Gamma_m$ be the graph whose vertices are the binary words of length $m$ with no adjacent $1$s, with an edge between two words exactly when their Hamming distance is $1$. Thus $|V(\Gamma_m)|=F_{m+2}$, and the first non-tree case is $\Gamma_3$.

Let $A_m$ be the adjacency matrix of $\Gamma_m$, and let $D_m$ be the diagonal degree matrix. Bass' determinant formula gives the reciprocal Ihara zeta expression
$$
Z_{\Gamma_m}(u)^{-1}
=(1-u^2)^{|E|-|V|}
\det\!\bigl(I-A_mu+(D_m-I)u^2\bigr).
$$
The Lean certificate works at the polynomial determinant layer:
$$
\det\!\bigl(I-A_mu+(D_m-I)u^2\bigr).
$$
It avoids encoding negative powers of $(1-u^2)$ as rational functions. For $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$, the graphs are trees; the determinant is $1-u^2$, and the Bass prefactor cancels it in the reciprocal zeta. For $\Gamma_3$, the exponent $|E|-|V|$ is $0$, so the reciprocal zeta is exactly the determinant.

\section{Finite Bass Determinants}
\label{sec:fibonacci-cube-ihara-finite-bass-determinants}

The verified vertex orders are the lexicographic no-adjacent-$1$ orders
$$
\Gamma_1:\ 0,1,
$$
$$
\Gamma_2:\ 00,01,10,
$$
and
$$
\Gamma_3:\ 000,001,010,100,101.
$$
For these orders, the certificate evaluates the concrete Bass matrices with entries
$$
\bigl(I-A_mu+(D_m-I)u^2\bigr)_{ij}
=\delta_{ij}-A_{ij}u+(\deg(i)-1)\delta_{ij}u^2.
$$
The determinant identities are
$$
\det\!\bigl(I-A_1u+(D_1-I)u^2\bigr)=1-u^2,
$$
$$
\det\!\bigl(I-A_2u+(D_2-I)u^2\bigr)=1-u^2,
$$
and
$$
\det\!\bigl(I-A_3u+(D_3-I)u^2\bigr)=1-2u^4+u^8.
$$
Equivalently, the first non-tree case factors as
$$
1-2u^4+u^8=(u-1)^2(u+1)^2(u^2+1)^2.
$$

\begin{theorem}[Fibonacci-cube Ihara determinant certificate]
\label{thm:fibonacci-cube-ihara-zeta-certificate}
For the Fibonacci-cube graphs $\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2,\Gamma_3$, the polynomial Bass determinants are
$$
\det\!\bigl(I-A_1u+(D_1-I)u^2\bigr)=1-u^2,
$$
$$
\det\!\bigl(I-A_2u+(D_2-I)u^2\bigr)=1-u^2,
$$
and
$$
\det\!\bigl(I-A_3u+(D_3-I)u^2\bigr)=1-2u^4+u^8=(u-1)^2(u+1)^2(u^2+1)^2.
$$
For $\Gamma_3$, since $|E|-|V|=0$, this is also $Z_{\Gamma_3}(u)^{-1}$.
\end{theorem}

\section{Honest Scope}
\label{sec:fibonacci-cube-ihara-honest-scope}

This packet is a finite Ihara (graph) zeta of the Fibonacci-cube graphs computed by the Bass determinant; it belongs to graph and dynamical zeta theory and to the graph-RH (Ramanujan) analogy, not to the actual Riemann xi or its Jensen criterion, and it is not a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. The Lean object is a concrete polynomial determinant calculation over integer coefficient lists. It does not encode analytic continuation, the critical strip, the nontrivial zeros of $\zeta(s)$, the Riemann $\xi$ function, or any Jensen-polynomial criterion for RH.

The finite analogy is still mathematically legitimate. Ihara zeta functions attach Euler-product-style zeta data to finite graphs, and their pole geometry is governed by graph spectra. In regular graph families, the Ramanujan condition is commonly expressed as a graph-theoretic RH analogue. The Fibonacci-cube calculation here stays inside that finite graph-zeta world: it verifies the Bass determinant data through $\Gamma_3$ and marks the first non-tree determinant explicitly, while leaving any uniform spectral theorem for all $\Gamma_m$ outside the closed scope.

\independenceWitness{riemann-hypothesis, riemann-xi-jensen-criterion: this packet is a finite Ihara graph zeta computation for Fibonacci-cube graphs, not a statement about the actual Riemann $\xi$ function, not a Jensen criterion certificate, and not a proof of RH.}
