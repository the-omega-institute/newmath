\begin{definition}[Compact metric modulus realizer carrier]
\label{def:compact-metric-modulus-realizer-carrier}
A $\CompactMetricModulusRealizerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(K,F,B,L,M,T,E,U,H,C,P,N)

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net row, including the displayed center bundle and located half-radius cover witnesses.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row, carrying the pointwise-modulus rows attached to the centers named by $K$.
\item $B$ is the finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ spine consumed by the lower-bound fold.
\item $L$ is the rational lower-bound fold over the positive center radii, read through $\RatUp$ order data.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ triangle ledger joining source-point, center, and target-image estimates.
\item $T$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row for halving, positivity, and lower-bound transport.

\end{definition}
