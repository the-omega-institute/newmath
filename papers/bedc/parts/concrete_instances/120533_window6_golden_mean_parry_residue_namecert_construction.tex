\section{Cylinder-Resolved Transfer Powers}
\label{sec:window6-parry-residue-cylinder-powers}

Let $F_0(y)=0$, $F_1(y)=1$, and
$$
F_{n+2}(y)=F_{n+1}(y)+yF_n(y).
$$
For $n\ge1$, the finite matrix-power identity is
$$
T_y^n=
\begin{pmatrix}
F_{n+1}(y)&yF_n(y)\\
F_n(y)&yF_{n-1}(y)
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Thus the scalar trace is only the sum of two cylinder channels,
$$
C_n(y)=\operatorname{tr}(T_y^n)=F_{n+1}(y)+yF_{n-1}(y),
$$
while the two diagonal cylinders are retained separately as
$$
N_0(n,y)=(T_y^n)_{00}=F_{n+1}(y),
\qquad
N_1(n,y)=(T_y^n)_{11}=yF_{n-1}(y).
$$
The Lean certificate checks the order-eight window by direct polynomial matrix multiplication, including the entrywise data through the same recursive polynomial arithmetic used by the finite zeta packet.

\section{Adjugate and Pressure Shadow}
\label{sec:window6-parry-residue-adjugate-pressure}

The finite resolvent identity is the adjugate calculation
$$
(I-tT_y)
\begin{pmatrix}
1&yt\\
t&1-t
\end{pmatrix}
=(1-t-yt^2)I_2.
$$
This is more refined than the determinant alone: the adjugate remembers how the two cylinder coordinates propagate before taking the scalar determinant.

The same finite transfer data also carries the pressure derivative shadow. Differentiating the trace polynomial formally in $y$ gives
$$
\frac{d}{dy}C_n(y)=nF_{n-1}(y),
$$
and therefore
$$
y\,C_n'(y)=n(T_y^n)_{11}.
$$
In the checked window, the identities include
$$
C_4'(y)=4(1+y),\qquad
C_5'(y)=5(1+2y),\qquad
C_6'(y)=6(1+3y+y^2),
$$
and continue through the order-eight finite certificate.

\section{Golden Parry Residue}
\label{sec:window6-parry-residue-golden-projector}

At $y=1$, work in $\mathbb Z[\varphi]$ with $\varphi^2=\varphi+1$, put
$$
\psi=1-\varphi,\qquad s=2\varphi-1,
$$
and define
$$
P=
\begin{pmatrix}
\varphi&1\\
1&\varphi-1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Q=
\begin{pmatrix}
1-\varphi&1\\
1&-\varphi
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The denominator-cleared spectral projector statement is
$$
sT^n=\varphi^nP-\psi^nQ.
$$
On diagonal entries this gives the exact finite Parry-equidistribution error, with $L_n=C_n(1)=\varphi^n+\psi^n$,
$$
s(T^n)_{00}=\varphi L_n-\psi^n,
\qquad
s(T^n)_{11}=(\varphi-1)L_n+\psi^n.
$$
The Lean certificate checks the golden spectral projector window through $n=6$ and the two diagonal error formulas through the same window over the concrete ring $\mathbb Z[\varphi]$.
