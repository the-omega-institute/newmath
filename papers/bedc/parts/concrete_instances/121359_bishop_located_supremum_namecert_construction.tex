Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopLocatedSupremumUp$ packet records a finite located-supremum witness for a displayed Bishop-style family of real names. It consumes the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular-rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the located-supremum route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedsupremum-namecert}. The chapter names only the finite witness packet and its approximation handoffs; it does not claim classical least-upper-bound completeness, trichotomy, countable choice, or a completed quotient-real supremum.

\begin{definition}[BishopLocatedSupremum carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-supremum-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedSupremumUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(F,W,D,Q,R,L,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the finite Bishop family row whose members are read only as displayed $\RealUp$ names.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row used to inspect the family at the requested precision.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row recording the dyadic gap budget for upper and lower approximation tests.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row that repacks the displayed stream windows as regular rational names.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $F,W,D,Q$ have stayed visible.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedSupremumUp$ row recording the finite located upper-bound and lower-approximation tests visible in the same window.
\item $A$ is the approximation ledger pairing each requested dyadic tolerance with a displayed family element and an upper-bound witness.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the family, window, tolerance, readback, real seal, located-supremum row, approximation ledger, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a supremum request through $F,W,D,Q,R,L,A,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopLocatedSupremumUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\LocatedSupremumUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedSupremumUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The accepted carrier has no coordinate for a classical least upper bound, quotient-real equality, trichotomy, countable choice, selected maximizing sequence, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BishopLocatedSupremum NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-supremum-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedSupremumUp$ carrier $S=(F,W,D,Q,R,L,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedSupremumUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed family row, stream window, dyadic tolerance ledger, regular-rational readback, terminal real seal, located-supremum row, approximation ledger, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming. Every accepted read factors through
$$
F,W,D,Q \longmapsto R,L,A,H,C,P,N
$$
and refuses classical least-upper-bound completeness, quotient-real equality, trichotomy, countable choice, selected maximizing sequences, and ambient completeness data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-supremum-carrier}. The visible rows are exactly $F,W,D,Q,R,L,A,H,C,P,N$, so the local NameCert can admit only those finite rows.

The approximation part begins with the family row $F$, the stream window $W$, and the dyadic tolerance row $D$. The regular-rational row $Q$ repacks the displayed windows before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $R$ is available.

The located-supremum row $L$ and approximation ledger $A$ then record the upper-bound and lower-approximation witnesses in the same displayed window. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this route without adding a classical least upper bound, quotient equality, trichotomy, countable choice, selected maximizing sequence, or ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BishopLocatedSupremum approximation nonescape]
\label{thm:bishop-located-supremum-approximation-nonescape}
Every approximation read from an accepted $\BishopLocatedSupremumUp$ carrier factors through the displayed route
$$
\StreamNameUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp
  \longrightarrow \RealUp
  \longrightarrow \LocatedSupremumUp .
$$
The read receives only the finite witness rows $F,W,D,Q,R,L,A,H,C,P,N$ and cannot export a classical least upper bound, quotient-real equality, trichotomy, countable choice, selected maximizing sequence, or an ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an approximation request and project the accepted carrier. The stream window $W$ and dyadic tolerance row $D$ are the only rows that determine which finite precision test is in scope.

The regular-rational readback $Q$ and terminal real seal $R$ are downstream of those displayed rows. Thus a consumer cannot inspect a real-facing approximation before the finite StreamName and DyadicRatCore route has been displayed.

Finally apply the located-supremum row $L$ and approximation ledger $A$. They bind the upper-bound and lower-approximation witnesses to the same finite window, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve the route. Since the carrier omits quotient reals, selected maximizing sequences, trichotomy, countable choice, and ambient completeness, the approximation route cannot escape the displayed packet.
\end{proof}
