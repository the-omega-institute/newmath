\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\OscillationRankUp$}

Following classical oscillation-rank analyses of real-valued functions, the $\OscillationRankUp$ packet records the finite window by which a real-valued map on a displayed metric source exposes a bounded oscillation-stage comparison before any descriptive-analysis consumer reads rank data. It sits over the $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the $\MetricSpaceUp$ route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the $\CompactMetricUp$ compact-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the $\ContinuousMapUp$ source-map carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the Dini-window sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dini-derivative-window-namecert}, and the finite rank-comparison sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-oscillation-rank-function-namecert}. The packet names the finite inspection surface for a bounded oscillation-rank request; it is not a completed transfinite hierarchy, a quotient of functions, a selected ordinal rank for every map, or a host descriptive-set-theoretic completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[OscillationRank carrier]
\label{def:oscillation-rank-carrier}
An $\OscillationRankUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(X,K,F,M,R,D,W,S,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row naming the inspected metric carrier and its visible distance witnesses.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-window row naming the compact subwindow on which the oscillation request is inspected.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row naming the real-valued source map visible on $K$.
\item $M$ is the finite mesh row listing the sampled source points and tolerance scale used by the request.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal through which all value and bound reads pass after $X,K,F,M$ have been displayed.
\item $D$ is the $\DiniDerivativeWindowUp$ or oscillation-derivative sibling row supplying the finite upper/lower comparison window used by the rank request.
\item $W$ is the oscillation-threshold row recording the displayed rational bound and the two value observations compared at that bound.
\item $S$ is the finite stage row naming the inspected derivative stage and its immediate predecessor window.
\item $T$ is the rank-survival row saying whether the displayed oscillation set survives the named finite stage inside the visible window.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, compact window, continuous map, mesh, real seal, derivative window, threshold, stage, survival, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a bounded oscillation-rank request through $X,K,F,M,R,D,W,S,T$ and then through the structural rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\OscillationRankUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DiniDerivativeWindowUp$, $\OscillationRankFunctionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\OscillationRankUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a completed descriptive-set-theoretic rank hierarchy, countable or dependent choice, a host supremum over all stages, quotient function equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[OscillationRank NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:oscillation-rank-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\OscillationRankUp$ carrier $O=(X,K,F,M,R,D,W,S,T,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\OscillationRankUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed rows; metric-source compatibility for $X$; compact-window compatibility for $K$; continuous-map exposure through $F$; mesh and tolerance admission through $M$; terminal real sealing through $R$; Dini or oscillation-derivative window exposure through $D$; threshold exactness through $W$; finite-stage admission through $S$; rank-survival exactness through $T$; componentwise transport stability through $H$; continuation replay through $C$; provenance and local naming through $P,N$; and ledger refusal for completed rank hierarchies, choice principles, host suprema over all stages, quotient function equality, and ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:oscillation-rank-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the rows $X,K,F,M,R,D,W,S,T,H,C,P,N$, so each local NameCert obligation must be read from one of those rows.

The source obligations are the route through $X,K,F,M,R$. The metric row fixes the visible source carrier, the compact row fixes the finite inspected window, the continuous-map row fixes the real-valued observations, the mesh row fixes the sampled tolerance schedule, and the real row seals every value read after those data have been displayed.

The rank-facing obligations are the route through $D,W,S,T$. The Dini or oscillation-derivative row supplies only the finite comparison window, the threshold row records the displayed oscillation bound, the stage row names the inspected derivative step, and the survival row records the finite yes/no comparison at that step.

Transport and replay preserve the same route. The rows $H$ and $C$ cannot add a completed hierarchy, a choice schedule, a host supremum, quotient function equality, or an ambient completeness principle, while $P$ and $N$ source and name only the listed coordinates. Hence the displayed obligations exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[OscillationRank finite-window handoff]
\label{thm:oscillation-rank-finite-window-handoff}
Every descriptive-analysis consumer of an accepted $\OscillationRankUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
X,K,F,M,R,D,W,S,T \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the consumer may inspect the metric source, compact window, continuous real-valued map, finite mesh, terminal real seal, Dini or oscillation-derivative window, oscillation threshold, displayed finite stage, and survival row, but it receives no full descriptive-set-theoretic rank completeness, no choice principle, no host supremum over all stages, no quotient function equality, and no ambient real-completion theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a consumer of the survival row $T$. Carrier admission makes $T$ available only after the metric source $X$, compact window $K$, continuous map $F$, mesh $M$, real seal $R$, derivative window $D$, threshold row $W$, and finite stage row $S$ have been displayed.

Replay the continuation row $C$. The request opens the source and compact-window data, reads the continuous-map observations through the real seal, consults the finite Dini or oscillation-derivative comparison window, and only then exposes the stage-survival row.

The handoff is therefore the stated finite route. Componentwise transport through $H$ preserves the displayed rows, and $P,N$ source and name only that packet. None of the excluded host objects can be recovered from the carrier.
\end{proof}
