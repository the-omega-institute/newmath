\begin{definition}[Real order-completion boundary carrier]
\label{def:real-order-completion-carrier}
A $\RealOrderCompletionBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(L,U,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the located lower rational-cut row. It records finite rational lower witnesses and located comparison requests.
\item $U$ is the located upper rational-cut row. It records finite rational upper witnesses and the displayed separation boundary from $L$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ bracket-window ledger. It stores dyadic lower and upper brackets, positive dyadic widths, and the finite refinement cells used by order reads.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row. It lists the finite requests through which a consumer asks for bracket refinement.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row. It records the regular rational approximants obtained from the displayed brackets and stream windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $L,U,D,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $L,U,D,S,R,E,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite route
$$

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealOrderCompletionBoundaryUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealOrderCompletionBoundaryUp}$ row.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real order-completion boundary obligations]
\label{thm:real-order-completion-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealOrderCompletionBoundaryUp$ carrier
$$
B=(L,U,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RealOrderCompletionBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the located lower row, located upper row, dyadic bracket ledger, finite stream observations, regular rational readback, terminal Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item located cut exposure, requiring every order request to read both $L$ and $U$ before it may inspect a bracket window;
\item dyadic bracket refinement, requiring every finite order window to pass through $D$ before it reaches $\StreamNameUp$ or $\RegSeqRatUp$;
\item readback compatibility, requiring $S$ and $R$ to expose the same finite tolerance windows before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is available;
\item nonescape, requiring downstream Real, separability, stream, regular-sequence, and dyadic consumers to read only $L,U,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No $\RealOrderCompletionBoundaryUp$ obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-order-completion-carrier}. The displayed tuple contains the located lower and upper cut rows, dyadic bracket ledger, finite stream observations, regular rational readback, terminal Real seal, and structural rows.

The located cut exposure obligation uses $L$ and $U$ together. A consumer cannot turn one side into a selected supremum or a trichotomy branch because the packet only displays finite lower and upper witnesses.

The dyadic bracket obligation uses $D$ before any stream or regular-rational read. Thus every finite order-completion request is mediated by the same dyadic window route that already governs \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for Dedekind-cut completeness, ambient Real completeness, trichotomy, quotient real equality, or choice-selected suprema, the obligation surface is exactly the displayed boundary.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real order-completion located supremum window]
\label{thm:real-order-completion-located-supremum-window}

$$

$$

It may not infer Dedekind-cut completeness, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, trichotomy, quotient real equality, or a choice-selected supremum.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the located rows $L$ and $U$. They expose the lower and upper rational-cut evidence as finite rows rather than as a completed cut quotient.

Read the dyadic bracket ledger $D$ next. Its finite brackets and positive widths are the only windows through which the order-completion request can be refined.

Pass from $D$ to $S$ and $R$. The finite stream observations determine which regular rational approximants are read back, and the terminal Real seal $E$ is visible only after those rows have been displayed.

The support rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name this route. Hence the located supremum window is a boundary certificate over finite BEDC rows, not a proof of Dedekind-cut completeness, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, trichotomy, quotient real equality, or choice-selected suprema.
\end{proof}
