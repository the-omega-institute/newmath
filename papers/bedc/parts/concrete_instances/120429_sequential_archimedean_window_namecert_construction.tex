It sits over the finite stream surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the sequential Archimedean handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sequential-archimedean-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Sequential Archimedean window carrier]
\label{def:sequential-archimedean-window-carrier}
A $\SequentialArchimedeanWindowUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(R,i,Q,D,A,G,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ name whose bounded sequential observation is being prepared.
\item $i$ is the unary $\NatUp$ request row naming the observation scale.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ Archimedean witness row visible at the request.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row presenting the same witness for window consumers.
\item $A$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window opened by the request and witness rows.
\item $G$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback extracted from the same window.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, available only after $i,Q,D,A,G$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the real name, request, witness rows, finite window, regular readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the request through the finite window, regular readback, witness rows, and terminal seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SequentialArchimedeanWindowUp$, $\SequentialArchimedeanRealUp$, $\RealUp$, $\NatUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialArchimedeanWindowUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $R,i,Q,D,A,G,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for trichotomy, quotient stream equality, a selected completed representative, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential Archimedean window NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sequential-archimedean-window-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SequentialArchimedeanWindowUp$ carrier
$$
W=(R,i,Q,D,A,G,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialArchimedeanWindowUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed real name, unary request, rational witness, dyadic tolerance, finite stream window, regular readback, Real seal, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming rows; request-window compatibility binding $i,Q,D$ to the same window $A$ that produces $G$; witness coherence binding $Q$ and $D$ to one finite Archimedean bound; seal ordering requiring $E$ to be read only after $i,Q,D,A,G$; and non-escape excluding trichotomy, quotient stream equality, selected completed representatives, countable choice, ambient Real completeness, Lean verification, and bridge checking.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sequential-archimedean-window-carrier}. The rows $R,i,Q,D,A,G,E,H,C,P,N$ are the complete packet inventory, so carrier admission and local naming introduce no hidden object.

The request-window compatibility clause follows from the displayed order. The unary request $i$ and witness rows $Q,D$ open the finite window $A$, and the readback $G$ is extracted from that same window before the seal $E$ can be consumed.

Witness coherence is componentwise. The rational witness $Q$ and dyadic tolerance row $D$ describe one Archimedean bound for the same request, and $H$ and $C$ transport and replay that bound without allocating another witness row.

Finally the seal-ordering and non-escape clauses use the absence of further coordinates. Since $E$ is the only terminal $\RealUp$ seal and the carrier has no row for trichotomy, quotient stream equality, selected completed representatives, countable choice, ambient completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking, every accepted consumer remains inside the displayed finite route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential Archimedean window Real handoff]
\label{thm:sequential-archimedean-window-real-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\SequentialArchimedeanWindowUp$ carrier
$$
W=(R,i,Q,D,A,G,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
must read the unary request $i$, the rational witness $Q$, the dyadic tolerance row $D$, the finite window $A$, and the regular readback $G$ before it may read the terminal seal $E$. The handoff is therefore a finite Archimedean witness-window route from $\SequentialArchimedeanRealUp$ to $\RealUp$ consumers, not a completeness principle or a selected representative of a completed stream.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the accepted carrier. The only Real-facing seal row is $E$, and it is stored after the request, witness, window, and readback rows $i,Q,D,A,G$.

Apply \autoref{thm:sequential-archimedean-window-namecert-obligations}. Its seal-ordering clause requires the request-window compatibility and witness-coherence rows before any consumer-visible seal read.

The provenance row $P$ names the sibling route through $\SequentialArchimedeanRealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$, while $H$ and $C$ preserve the same displayed rows under transport and replay. Hence the Real handoff exports exactly the finite witness window and not trichotomy, quotient stream equality, selected completed representatives, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
