Following Heine, Borel, and Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis, the $\HeineBorelFiniteIntervalUp$ packet records the finite-subcover boundary available for a displayed located real interval after the cover has already been reduced to finite dyadic interval data. It sits beside the compact interval route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-compact-interval-namecert}, the dyadic interval-cover packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-cover-namecert}, the finite closed-bounded interval compactness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closed-bounded-interval-compact-namecert}, and the L10 Real and Completion route through $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$. The packet names a constructive finite interval subcover interface; it does not claim general open-cover compactness, a choice-selected subcover, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Heine--Borel finite-interval carrier]
\label{def:heine-borel-finite-interval-carrier}
A $\HeineBorelFiniteIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(I,D,B,C,S,Q,T,E,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\LocatedRealIntervalUp$ source row, exposing lower and upper endpoint names, locatedness data, and interval-radius requests;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicIntervalCoverUp$ finite cover row, recording dyadic cells, mesh scale, membership checks, and cover-read witnesses;
\item $B$ is the $\BishopCompactIntervalUp$ compact-interval boundary row used only as the finite constructive interval route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-compact-interval-namecert};
\item $C$ is the $\ClosedBoundedIntervalCompactUp$ row, supplying the closed-bounded interval compactness handoff after finite cover data is displayed;
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row attached to endpoint and cover observations;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the same observed windows;
\item $T$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and mesh-refinement ledger;
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the interval, cover, mesh, window, and readback rows have been projected;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all displayed rows;
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for interval admission, cover readback, dyadic refinement, finite subcover witness, and Real-seal handoff;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HeineBorelFiniteIntervalUp$, $\LocatedRealIntervalUp$, $\DyadicIntervalCoverUp$, $\BishopCompactIntervalUp$, $\ClosedBoundedIntervalCompactUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HeineBorelFiniteIntervalUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, countable choice, selected host representatives, quotient equality of real names, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Heine--Borel finite-interval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:heine-borel-finite-interval-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HeineBorelFiniteIntervalUp$ carrier $K=(I,D,B,C,S,Q,T,E,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HeineBorelFiniteIntervalUp}$ surface consists exactly of five finite rows: interval-source admission through $I$; finite cover admission through $D$; dyadic mesh refinement through $T$ with the $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows $S,Q$; finite subcover witness readback through the $\BishopCompactIntervalUp$ and $\ClosedBoundedIntervalCompactUp$ rows $B,C$; and Real-seal nonescape through $E,H,R,P,N$. No row exports general open-cover compactness, a choice-selected subcover, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The source interval row is exactly $I$, so endpoint, radius, locatedness, and containment requests begin with the displayed $\LocatedRealIntervalUp$ packet rather than with a host interval.

The finite cover and mesh rows are $D$ and $T$. The cover cells, mesh scale, and membership checks are therefore read through $\DyadicIntervalCoverUp$ and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, with $S,Q$ supplying only the finite window and regular-rational readback needed to inspect the same observations.

The finite subcover witness is obtained only after the compact-interval boundary rows $B,C$ have been displayed. These rows are the constructive compact interval surfaces cited above; they do not add an arbitrary open cover or a choice functional.

Finally read the seal and structural rows. The terminal $\RealUp$ row $E$ is reached only after $I,D,B,C,S,Q,T$ have been projected, and $H,R,P,N$ transport, replay, provenance, and name that same finite packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for the excluded principles, the five listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
