The $\PhysicalInductionGateUp$ packet realizes the physical-induction certificate of \autoref{sec:physical-induction-open-continuation} as finite NameCert data. It sits after the reality-facing audit surfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-physical-namecert-audit-namecert}, the open-fit rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-reality-constrained-open-fit-namecert}, the continuation countermodel boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuationcountermodel-namecert}, and the failure-surface sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-realityconstrainedfailuresurface-namecert}. \begin{definition}[PhysicalInductionGate carrier]
\label{def:physical-induction-gate-carrier}
A $\PhysicalInductionGateUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(S,O,M,\Pi,F,Cn,B,Y,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source finite observation history row, including the prefix $H_n$ and the observation classifier used by the fitted model.
\item $O$ is the open continuation row. It names the admissible continuation class inspected by the packet and exposes only finite continuation requests.
\item $M$ is the displayed model-signature row read from the fitted model over the same finite history.
\item $\Pi$ is the probe-bundle and comparison row governing which signatures are compared.
\item $F$ is the finite-fit certificate row for the current prefix.
\item $Cn$ is the continuation-stability row. It records the local stability basis licensing reuse over the displayed continuation class.
\item $B$ is the failure-surface row. It records the observations or continuation refinements that would separate model-generated and observed signatures.
\item $Y$ is the predictive-descent row, requiring the prediction operation to descend through the classifier named by $\Pi$ before reuse is public.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,O,M,\Pi,F,Cn,B,Y,R,P,N$.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite fit through continuation request, stability basis, failure-surface test, descent check, provenance, and local name.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PhysicalInductionGateUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$, together with the reality-facing siblings named above.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PhysicalInductionGateUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $S,O,M,\Pi,F,Cn,B,Y,H,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a completed future observation carrier, universal physical necessity, a final-fit oracle, hidden continuation choice, or predictive reuse without a displayed failure surface.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[PhysicalInductionGate NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:physical-induction-gate-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PhysicalInductionGateUp$ carrier $G=(S,O,M,\Pi,F,Cn,B,Y,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\PhysicalInductionGateUp}$ surface consists exactly of source-prefix admission, continuation-class admission, model-signature readback, probe-comparison compatibility, finite-fit certification, continuation-stability display, failure-surface display, predictive-descent display, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming for the rows $S,O,M,\Pi,F,Cn,B,Y,H,R,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:physical-induction-gate-carrier}. The reality-facing rows are exactly the finite source prefix, continuation class, model signature, probe comparison, finite-fit certificate, stability basis, failure surface, and predictive-descent row.

The obligation order follows the physical-induction certificate. A reuse read first displays the finite fit $F$ over $S,M,\Pi$, then displays the continuation class $O$, then reads the stability basis $Cn$, and only then exposes the failure-surface row $B$ and descent row $Y$.

The structural rows $H,R,P,N$ keep that route stable under transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier has no completed future, final oracle, hidden continuation selector, or failure-free law row, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[PhysicalInductionGate failure surface]
\label{thm:physical-induction-gate-failure-surface}
Every accepted $\PhysicalInductionGateUp$ prediction read must expose a finite failure-surface row before it can use continuation stability:
$$
S\longmapsto O\longmapsto M\longmapsto \Pi\longmapsto F\longmapsto Cn\longmapsto B\longmapsto Y\longmapsto H,R,P,N .
$$
If the row $B$ is absent, or if $B$ is detached from the same continuation class $O$ and probe comparison $\Pi$, the packet exports no licensed physical-induction gate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{def:physical-induction-gate-carrier}. The only route from finite fit to prediction reuse passes through the displayed continuation class, stability basis, failure-surface row, and predictive-descent row.

Apply \autoref{thm:physical-induction-gate-namecert-obligations}. It makes failure-surface display a local NameCert obligation, not an optional annotation. A consumer therefore cannot consume $Cn$ as a lawlike stability basis unless it also consumes $B$ over the same $O$ and $\Pi$.

The rows $H,R,P,N$ preserve this route under transport, replay, provenance, and naming. Hence a read with no $B$ row, or with a failure surface over a different continuation class or classifier, remains outside $\PhysicalInductionGateUp$.
\end{proof}
