Following Lagrange and the classical finite coefficient form of the inversion theorem, the $\LagrangeInversionUp$ packet records the coefficient-reversion route that extracts finitely many inverse-series coefficients from one displayed Taylor jet with a nonzero linear coefficient. It sits after the finite Taylor theorem route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-taylor-theorem-namecert}, the derivative observation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-derivative-namecert}, the polynomial carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-namecert}, the dyadic rational arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular-rational readback rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seals of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Lagrange inversion carrier]
\label{def:lagrange-inversion-carrier}
A $\LagrangeInversionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(c,J,a_1,R,B,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $c$ is the displayed center row, read as a named $\RealUp$ endpoint seal together with the finite local window in which the Taylor jet is interpreted.
\item $J$ is the finite $\TaylorTheoremUp$ jet row. It lists the Taylor coefficients used by the inverse-coefficient extraction and records the associated $\DerivativeUp$ and $\PolynomialUp$ provenance.
\item $a_1$ is the nonzero linear coefficient witness, represented as a displayed apartness or inverse row over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and sealed by the same $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ readback route as $J$.
\item $R$ is the finite coefficient-reversion ledger. Each inverse coefficient requested by the packet is obtained by a displayed recurrence using only earlier rows of $R$, the finite jet $J$, and the linear witness $a_1$.
\item $B$ records dyadic rational coefficient bounds for the finite coefficient-reversion ledger and the polynomial truncations consumed by the packet.
\item $Q$ lists the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows for the displayed coefficients, the linear witness, the recurrence denominators, and the finite polynomial values.
\item $E$ records the terminal $\RealUp$ endpoint seals for the center, coefficient reads, and finite inverse-polynomial handoff.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the center, Taylor jet, nonzero linear witness, reversion ledger, coefficient bounds, readback rows, endpoint seals, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from every inverse-coefficient read through $c,J,a_1,R,B,Q,E,H,P,N$.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LagrangeInversionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed rows $c,J,a_1,R,B,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an infinite inverse series, analytic convergence, selected inverse branches, quotient equality of real names, countable choice, or an ambient inverse-function theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lagrange inversion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lagrange-inversion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LagrangeInversionUp$ carrier
$$
L=(c,J,a_1,R,B,Q,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LagrangeInversionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed center, finite Taylor jet, nonzero linear coefficient witness, coefficient-reversion ledger, coefficient bounds, readback rows, endpoint seals, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item Taylor-jet routing, requiring every source coefficient read to pass through $J$ and the finite $\TaylorTheoremUp$, $\DerivativeUp$, and $\PolynomialUp$ rows it names;
\item linear-coefficient apartness, requiring every division used by the reversion recurrence to cite the displayed nonzero witness $a_1$ over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$;
\item finite reversion exactness, requiring every row of $R$ to be computed from earlier displayed rows, the finite jet $J$, and the witness $a_1$;
\item coefficient-bound readback, requiring $B$ and $Q$ to expose dyadic rational bounds and $\RegSeqRatUp$ reads before any terminal $\RealUp$ seal in $E$ is consumed;
\item structural stability, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name only the rows already listed.
\end{enumerate}
No $\LagrangeInversionUp$ obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lagrange-inversion-carrier}. The theorem-facing rows are exactly $c,J,a_1,R,B,Q,E$, and the structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

The source data are finite. The row $J$ is a $\TaylorTheoremUp$ jet and therefore already factors through derivative observations, polynomial assembly, dyadic coefficient arithmetic, regular rational readback, and real endpoint sealing. The row $a_1$ is displayed separately, so every recurrence division cites a named nonzero linear coefficient witness.

The coefficient extraction is the finite ledger $R$. Each row of $R$ reads only earlier inverse-coefficient rows, the listed source coefficients in $J$, and the dyadic arithmetic attached to $a_1$; hence no analytic inverse branch or infinite series coordinate is introduced.

Finally $B,Q,E$ bind the numeric output to finite readback. Dyadic coefficient bounds are displayed before regular rational reads, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seals are reached only after those rows are visible. The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve this same packet, so the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
