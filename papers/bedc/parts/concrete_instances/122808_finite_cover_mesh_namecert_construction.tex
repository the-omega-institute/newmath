\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FiniteCoverMeshUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cover-mesh-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive compactness and finite covers, the $\FiniteCoverMeshUp$ packet records the finite mesh carrier consumed by compact-completion handoffs. It sits after the compact-metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the total-bounded finite-net row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the metric-space source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-space-namecert}, the Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}. The packet names the finite mesh data that compact and completion routes can consume; it is not the mesh-chain uniform-modulus theorem and it does not assert open-cover compactness.

\begin{definition}[Finite cover mesh carrier]
\label{def:finite-cover-mesh-carrier}
A $\FiniteCoverMeshUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(C,T,M,R,Q,D,L,U,H,E,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ compact-metric source row whose finite-cover data are being meshed.
\item $T$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite-net row supplying the finite mesh cells.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ row used for radius comparison, triangle transport, and mesh containment checks.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row consumed by completion-facing readers.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational readback row for the inspected mesh radii.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row controlling mesh size, cover radius, and completion budget.
\item $L$ is the finite mesh ledger, recording which displayed mesh cells refine which displayed cover rows.
\item $U$ is the compact-completion handoff row exposed only after $C,T,M,R,Q,D,L$ have been read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact, total-bounded, metric, real, rational, dyadic, mesh-ledger, and handoff rows.
\item $E$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact finite-cover reads through dyadic mesh bounds into the handoff row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteCoverMeshUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverMeshUp}$ registration row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these finite rows. It has no coordinate for an open-cover compactness principle, a least Lebesgue number, hidden centre choice, quotient equality of Real names, countable choice, or ambient completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite cover mesh NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cover-mesh-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteCoverMeshUp$ carrier $F=(C,T,M,R,Q,D,L,U,H,E,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverMeshUp}$ obligations are exactly compact-metric source admission, total-bounded finite-net admission, metric-space comparison admission, Real seal exposure, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance accounting, finite mesh-ledger exactness, compact-completion handoff exposure, componentwise stability transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. The obligations do not include open-cover compactness, a host Lebesgue-number theorem, hidden mesh-centre selection, quotient Real equality, countable choice, or ambient completion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-cover-mesh-carrier}. Its displayed compact data are $C,T,M$, so compact-metric, total-bounded, and metric comparison reads must enter through those rows.

Read the tolerance side next. The Real seal $R$, regular rational readback $Q$, and dyadic tolerance row $D$ provide the numerical budget consumed by the finite mesh ledger $L$.

The handoff row $U$ is downstream of the mesh ledger. Componentwise transport $H$, replay $E$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ preserve the same finite route, so none of the excluded compactness, hidden-choice, quotient, choice, or ambient-completion principles has a carrier coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite cover mesh compact handoff]
\label{thm:finite-cover-mesh-compact-handoff}
Every compact or completion consumer of an accepted $\FiniteCoverMeshUp$ packet must read the route
$$
C,T,M,R,Q,D,L,U,H,E,P,N
$$
before using the exported mesh handoff. Thus the packet supplies concrete finite mesh NameCert data between compact finite-net sources and Real, RegSeqRat, and DyadicRatCore tolerance consumers; it does not duplicate the finite-cover mesh-chain uniform-modulus route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:finite-cover-mesh-namecert-obligations}. It identifies the complete local surface of an accepted packet, so a compact or completion consumer cannot bypass $C,T,M,R,Q,D,L,U,H,E,P,N$.

The compact side is read through $C,T,M$. These rows expose compact-metric data, total-bounded finite cells, and the metric comparisons needed to relate finite mesh cells to cover rows.

The tolerance side is read through $R,Q,D,L$. The Real seal, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance row feed the finite mesh ledger, and the handoff row $U$ becomes available only after that ledger is displayed.

Finally $H,E,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same route. Since the packet exports only this finite path, downstream compact and completion readers receive a mesh carrier rather than a host compactness theorem or the separate mesh-chain uniform-modulus certificate.
\end{proof}
