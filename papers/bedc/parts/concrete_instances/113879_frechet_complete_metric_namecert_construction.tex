\begin{definition}[Cauchy-modulus shared-tail carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-modulus-shared-tail-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row exposing the inspected tail windows of the candidate Cauchy name.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ positive tolerance ledger consumed by the tail comparison.
\item $T$ is the shared-tail modulus row, recording that every displayed tolerance request is answered by one finite tail window accepted by both the stream and regular-readback faces.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy-modulus shared-tail NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-modulus-shared-tail-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FrechetCompleteMetricUp$ carrier $F=(M,S,R,D,T,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FrechetCompleteMetricUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the metric row, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, shared-tail modulus row, terminal Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.

The classifier agreement obligation requires two accepted packets to agree precisely when these displayed rows agree componentwise through $\hsame$. The stability obligation requires $\hsame$ transport and $\Cont$ replay to preserve the same row order before any completion-facing or Real-facing consumer reads the terminal seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-modulus-shared-tail-carrier}. The analytic route is $M,S,R,D,T,E$, and the structural certificate route is $H,C,P,N$.

The metric row fixes the distance surface. The stream and regular-readback rows expose the finite windows on which the Cauchy request is inspected, while the dyadic tolerance ledger supplies the positive comparison rows.

The shared-tail row is downstream of those rows and upstream of the terminal Real seal. It therefore records one displayed tail window for the two consuming faces without adding quotient equality, selected limits, choice, host equality, or ambient completeness as local certificate data.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly the same rows. Hence the listed fields exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:cauchy-modulus-shared-tail-cofinality}

The common refinement is cofinal among the finite tolerance demands visible to the packet: every listed request factors through its displayed tail window before any completion-facing consumer reaches the Real seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Enumerate the finite StreamName requests and RegSeqRat checks. Starting from the positive dyadic tolerance row, repeatedly read the shared-tail row $T$ of \autoref{def:cauchy-modulus-shared-tail-carrier} to enlarge the displayed tail window until all listed finite demands are covered.

The final window is therefore common to the finite family and is cofinal for the requests named inside the carrier. Since the construction uses only displayed carrier rows, it cannot appeal to quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
