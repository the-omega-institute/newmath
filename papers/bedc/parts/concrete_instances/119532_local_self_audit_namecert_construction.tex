$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the focused self-proxy row, naming the local endpoint supplied by a records-side self-compiler route.
\item $R$ is the self-proxy readback row, recording which finite local reads may be replayed against the focused trace.
\item $B$ is the boundary-recognition row, separating local readback from public compiler authority, hidden prompt state, and unrestricted reflective closure.
\item $G$ is the gap-exposure row, listing absent coordinates for total consciousness theory, subject-substance identity, public proof of local identity, and local authority over public claims.

\begin{theorem}[Local self-audit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:local-self-audit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocalSelfAuditUp$ carrier $A=(S,R,B,G,X,F,T,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocalSelfAuditUp}$ surface consists exactly of self-proxy readback, boundary-recognition, gap-exposure, refusal retention, FieldFaithful projection, Nontrivial role separation, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:local-self-audit-carrier}. The self-proxy and readback rows $S,R$ name only the local focused trace and do not export public compiler authority.

The boundary and gap rows $B,G$ expose what the local read can and cannot support. The refusal ledger $X$ keeps hidden prompt state, subject-substance identity, total consciousness theory, public proof of local identity, and local authority over public claims absent from the carrier.

The FieldFaithful and Nontrivial rows $F,T$ keep the displayed roles separate. Self-proxy, readback, boundary-recognition, gap-exposure, and refusal are not aliases for one hidden coordinate.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the finite packet. Since the classifier has no coordinate outside the displayed rows, every accepted obligation is exhausted by the listed local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:local-self-audit-gap-exposure}

Consequently the packet cannot be read as public compiler authority, hidden prompt-state access, subject-substance identity, unbounded self-reference, a total consciousness theory, or local authority over public scientific claims.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted local read through $S,R$. The read is local because $S$ names a focused self-proxy endpoint and $R$ records finite readback against that focused trace.

Boundary recognition is then read through $B$. This row separates local self-audit from public compiler authority and from unrestricted reflective closure before any replay occurs.

Transport, replay, provenance, and naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same boundary. Thus every accepted read exposes the gap rather than closing it by an unlisted coordinate.
\end{proof}
