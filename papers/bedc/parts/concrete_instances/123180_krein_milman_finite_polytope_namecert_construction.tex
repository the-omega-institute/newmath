$$

\item $X$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ row for the displayed convex subset.
\item $P$ is the $\PolytopeUp$ row containing the finite half-space, vertex, edge, and face ledgers.
\item $E$ is the $\KreinMilmanFiniteExtremeUp$ sibling row for finite exposed-face and candidate-extreme readback.

\begin{theorem}[Krein--Milman finite-polytope NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:krein-milman-finite-polytope-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KreinMilmanFinitePolytopeUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\KreinMilmanFinitePolytopeUp}$ surface consists exactly of vector-space admission for $V$, convex-set admission for $X$, finite polytope face-ledger admission for $P$, finite extreme-point readback for $E$, real scalar sealing for $R$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, package provenance through $G$, and local naming through $N$. A finite polytope consumer may read hull or face data only through the displayed route
$$
V,X,P \longmapsto E,R \longmapsto H,C,G,N.
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:krein-milman-finite-polytope-carrier}. The vector-space row $V$ supplies affine operations, the convex-set row $X$ supplies convex-membership reads, and the polytope row $P$ supplies finite half-space, vertex, edge, and face ledgers.

Read the finite extreme-point sibling through $E$ and the scalar comparisons through $R$. By \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-krein-milman-finite-extreme-namecert}, the extreme route is a finite exposed-face and candidate-extreme witness, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} seals the scalar comparisons used by the finite face ledger.

Finally $H,C,G,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no row for compact-convex hull equality, unrestricted supporting-functional choice, an external face lattice, quotient convex-body equality, countable choice, or ambient real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
