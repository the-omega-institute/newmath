\begin{theorem}[CauchySequenceRing NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-sequence-ring-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchySequenceRingUp$ carrier $K=(X,Y,W,D,A,M,Z,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchySequenceRingUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, regular-rational source exposure through $X$ and $Y$, synchronized-window exposure through $W$, dyadic operation-budget exposure through $D$, additive operation exposure through $A$, multiplicative operation exposure through $M$, constant and compatibility exposure through $Z$, terminal real-boundary exposure through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-sequence-ring-carrier}. The rows $X,Y,W,D,A,M,Z,E,H,C,P,N$ are the only coordinates available to the local certificate.

The source and window obligations read $X$ and $Y$ only through the synchronized schedule $W$. The dyadic ledger $D$ supplies the finite tolerance and rational arithmetic cells needed by the displayed operation rows.

The additive row $A$ exposes zero, addition, and negation, while the multiplicative row $M$ exposes one and multiplication. The compatibility row $Z$ keeps all five ring operations on the same regular-Cauchy source surface before the terminal $\RealUp$ boundary $E$ is available to a downstream consumer.

Rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly that displayed packet. Since the carrier contains no quotient row, selected representative row, countable-choice row, trichotomy row, host-real row, or ambient-completeness row, the NameCert obligations cannot export any of those stronger objects.
\end{proof}
