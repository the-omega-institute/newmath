\begin{definition}[Birkhoff-Kakutani carrier]
\label{def:birkhoff-kakutani-carrier}
A $\BirkhoffKakutaniUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(G,B,S,P,I,U,M,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the $\TopGroupUp$ source row, exposing multiplication, unit, inverse, translation, and neighborhood-continuity data.
\item $B$ is the countable neighborhood-basis ledger, represented by finite prefix windows rather than a choice-selected infinite basis.
\item $S$ is the symmetric shrinkage ledger, recording the displayed sequence of neighborhoods whose squares stay inside the previous neighborhood.
\item $P$ is the product-compatibility row, showing that multiplication consumes the shrinkage rows through the $\TopGroupUp$ continuity surface.
\item $I$ is the inverse-compatibility row, showing that inversion preserves the displayed symmetric neighborhood rows.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ handoff row generated from left translates of the displayed neighborhood data.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ handoff row, read through the $\FrinkMetrizationUp$ finite shrinkage-to-distance route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topological-group source, basis, shrinkage, product, inverse, uniform, metric, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a metrization request through $G,B,S,P,I,U,M,H,Q,N$.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BirkhoffKakutaniUp$, $\TopGroupUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\FrinkMetrizationUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BirkhoffKakutaniUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $G,B,S,P,I,U,M,H,C,Q,N$. It has no coordinate for a quotient group, selected infinite basis, host left-uniformity object, ambient completion, or unlogged metrization oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Birkhoff-Kakutani NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:birkhoff-kakutani-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BirkhoffKakutaniUp$ carrier $K=(G,B,S,P,I,U,M,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BirkhoffKakutaniUp}$ surface consists exactly of topological-group source exposure through $G$, countable neighborhood-basis exposure through $B$, symmetric shrinkage through $S$, product compatibility through $P$, inverse compatibility through $I$, left-uniform handoff through $U$, left-invariant metric handoff through $M$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $Q$, and local naming through $N$.

Every metric-facing consumer therefore reads the $\TopGroupUp$ source, the finite basis prefixes, the symmetric shrinkage rows, product and inverse compatibility, and the $\UniformSpaceUp$ handoff before the $\MetricUp$ row can be consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The source row $G$ is the only topological-group data available, and $B$ is the only basis data exposed to consumers.

The shrinkage and compatibility rows are downstream from those sources. The row $S$ records the symmetric neighborhood chain, $P$ records that multiplication respects the chain, and $I$ records inverse stability on the same displayed rows.

The uniform and metric rows then read the same finite route. The row $U$ packages the left-translate entourage handoff, while $M$ records the Frink-style metric handoff from the displayed shrinkage ledger.

Finally, $H,C,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that route. Since the carrier contains no quotient group, selected infinite basis, host uniformity object, ambient completion, or metrization oracle, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}
