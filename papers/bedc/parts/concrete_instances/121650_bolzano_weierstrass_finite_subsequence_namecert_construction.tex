\begin{theorem}[Bolzano--Weierstrass finite-subsequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bolzano-weierstrass-finite-subsequence-namecert-obligations}

$$
B=(X,K,W,I,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BolzanoWeierstrassFiniteSubsequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the bounded $\RealUp$ sequence source $X$ and the finite-window compactness row $K$;
\item classifier stability under componentwise $\hsame$ transport of $X,K,W,I,D,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$;
\item finite-window extraction of the displayed increasing index list $I$ from the inspected stream observations $W$ and dyadic tolerance ledger $D$;
\item the no-choice boundary asserting that $I$ is a finite row and does not name an infinite selected subsequence, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy decision, quotient stream representative, or ambient completeness proof for $\RealUp$;
\item local $\NameCert$ packaging through $S,R,E,H,C,P,N$, with the compact regular Cauchy subsequence sibling read only after the finite window has been exposed.
\end{enumerate}
No Bolzano--Weierstrass obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bolzano-weierstrass-finite-subsequence-carrier}. The bounded sequence source $X$ and finite-window compactness row $K$ are the only rows that can introduce the inspected window.

Read $W,I,D$ next. The stream row $W$ exposes the finite sequence observations, the finite index list $I$ records the positions selected inside that displayed window, and the dyadic ledger $D$ checks the tolerance comparison against the chosen mesh cell.

The sibling row $S$ is therefore downstream of a finite extraction. It can hand the displayed data toward the compact regular Cauchy subsequence surface, but it cannot replace the finite index list with a selected infinite subsequence.

Finally route the readback and seal through $R,E,H,C,P,N$. Since the carrier has no coordinate for countable choice, trichotomy, quotient stream equality, host sequential compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, the local NameCert surface is exactly the finite-window package listed in the theorem.
\end{proof}
