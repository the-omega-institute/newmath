\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CauchyExponentialUp$}

\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\CauchyExponentialUp$ packet records the classical exponential Cauchy series as a finite real-name constructor for the L10 Real and Completion route. It sits over the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the product-completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-product-completion-namecert}. The packet names a displayed exponential-series constructor; it does not import a host exponential function, quotient real equality, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[CauchyExponential carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-exponential-carrier}
A $\CauchyExponentialUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(X,W,A,F,D,Q,S,M,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source $\RealUp$ row whose exponential is being named through inspected finite windows.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule selecting finitely many source and partial-sum windows.
\item $A$ is the coefficient ledger for the factorial denominators of the exponential series, stored as rational and dyadic rows rather than as a host analytic function.
\item $F$ is the finite partial-sum family for the Cauchy series $\sum_n X^n/n!$ over the displayed windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger bounding the tail and product-budget requests visible in the packet.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the partial sums.
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal produced from the regular readback.
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyProductCompletionUp$ handoff row used whenever a consumer asks for product compatibility of the series constructor.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, windows, coefficients, partial sums, dyadic tolerance rows, regular readback, Real seal, product-completion handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay along the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyExponentialUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\CauchyProductCompletionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyExponentialUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier stores no host exponential operator, no quotient equality of real names, no chosen limit of an infinite series, no unrestricted product algebra, no countable-choice schedule, and no ambient completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyExponential NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-exponential-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyExponentialUp$ carrier $E=(X,W,A,F,D,Q,S,M,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyExponentialUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, source-window exposure, factorial coefficient accounting, finite partial-sum display, dyadic tail control, regular rational readback, terminal Real sealing, product-completion handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-exponential-carrier}. The visible analytic data are the source row $X$, the window schedule $W$, the coefficient ledger $A$, the finite partial sums $F$, the dyadic tolerance row $D$, the regular readback $Q$, and the terminal seal $S$.

The coefficient and partial-sum rows are downstream of the finite windows. Thus a consumer first reads $\StreamNameUp$ windows and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerances before it can inspect any factorial denominator, power product, or partial-sum value.

The readback and seal rows keep the same route visible. The row $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback attached to the displayed partial sums, and $S$ is the resulting $\RealUp$ seal; neither row selects a host limit or identifies quotient real names.

Finally read $M,H,C,P,N$. The product-completion handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local name record only the displayed packet. Since no other coordinate exists, the NameCert surface cannot export a host exponential, chosen series limit, unrestricted product algebra, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyExponential Cauchy-product handoff]
\label{thm:cauchy-exponential-cauchy-product-handoff}
Every product-facing or completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\CauchyExponentialUp$ packet must first read the source $\RealUp$ row, finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows, factorial coefficient ledger, finite partial-sum family, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tail budget, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal before the $\CauchyProductCompletionUp$ handoff row is available. The handoff exports no host exponential, quotient real equality, selected series limit, unrestricted product algebra, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a product-facing read of the carrier in \autoref{def:cauchy-exponential-carrier}. The row $M$ is present only as a handoff from the displayed series constructor, so it cannot be consumed before the source, window, coefficient, partial-sum, tolerance, readback, and seal rows have been opened.

The finite series route is ordered by the windows and tolerance ledger. The rows $W,A,F,D$ expose exactly the finite Cauchy-series data needed by the consumer, while $Q$ and $S$ route that data through regular rational readback and the terminal Real seal.

Now use the sibling product-completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-product-completion-namecert}. That boundary accepts a product read only after displayed stream windows, dyadic observations, regular readback, and Real sealing remain visible.

Composing the two routes gives the stated handoff. Since both routes refuse host analytic functions, quotient real equality, selected limits, unrestricted product algebra, countable choice, and ambient Real completeness, the $\CauchyExponentialUp$ product-facing read exports none of those objects.
\end{proof}
