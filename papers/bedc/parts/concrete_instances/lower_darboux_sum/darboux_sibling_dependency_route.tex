The $\LowerDarbouxSumUp$ packet consumes the shared $\DarbouxSumUp$ partition ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-darboux-sum-namecert} and is paired with $\UpperDarbouxSumUp$ through the same finite interval, cell-width, and partition rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-upper-darboux-sum-namecert}. A lower-sum consumer therefore reads the lower envelope as one half of the displayed Darboux partition lattice rather than as an isolated kernel row.
