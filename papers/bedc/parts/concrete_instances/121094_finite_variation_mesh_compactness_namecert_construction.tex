\begin{definition}[Finite variation mesh compactness carrier]
\label{def:finite-variation-mesh-compactness-carrier}
A $\FiniteVariationMeshCompactnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\RealUp$ interval source row naming the displayed endpoints.
\item $\Pi$ is the displayed finite partition row from the bounded-variation surface.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budget row recording the finite dyadic comparison and endpoint-arithmetic data.
\item $V$ is the $\FiniteVariationPartitionUp$ ledger row read from the same partition data.
\item $M$ is the finite mesh row recording the displayed subdivision points and adjacent cell comparisons.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the mesh and partition data.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row that supplies the finite observation route before any Real-facing consumer reads the packet.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactIntervalUp$ handoff row reached only after $I,\Pi,D,V,M,R,S$ are displayed.

The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $I,\Pi,D,V,M,R,S,K,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host variation supremum, a selected open-cover subfamily, quotient equality of functions, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite variation mesh compactness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-variation-mesh-compactness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteVariationMeshCompactnessUp$ carrier $V=(I,\Pi,D,V,M,R,S,K,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteVariationMeshCompactnessUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed interval, finite partition, dyadic budget, finite variation ledger, finite mesh, regular-rational readback, StreamName window, compact-interval handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-variation-mesh-compactness-carrier}. The analytic rows are $I,\Pi,D,V,M,R,S,K$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The interval and partition rows are the starting point. The row $I$ displays the interval source, while $\Pi$ and $V$ read the same finite partition as a bounded-variation ledger rather than as an arbitrary partition family.

The dyadic row $D$ and mesh row $M$ then record the finite comparison data used by the packet. The regular-rational readback $R$ and StreamName window $S$ keep the mesh route finite before the compact-interval handoff $K$ is visible.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same displayed route. Since the carrier has no row for open-cover compactness, a selected subcover, quotient equality of functions, countable choice, or ambient completeness, those principles cannot be exported by the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
