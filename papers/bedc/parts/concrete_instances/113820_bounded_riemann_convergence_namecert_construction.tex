\begin{definition}[Bounded Riemann convergence carrier]
\label{def:bounded-riemann-convergence-carrier}
A $\BoundedRiemannConvergenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(D,F,A,T,S,B,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the common compact interval and Darboux partition row, read through the accepted $\DarbouxSumUp$ comparison surface.
\item $F$ is the regulated-function family row, recording the displayed approximating functions over the same interval.
\item $A$ is the pointwise approach row, listing the finite observation windows by which the family is compared with its named limit row.
\item $T$ is the tagged Riemann readback row for the displayed members of the family.
\item $S$ is the lower/upper Darboux bracket ledger associated to the same partition and tolerance rows.
\item $B$ is the uniform bound row, exposing a common finite bound before any convergence consumer reads the integral handoff.
\item $E$ is the terminal real-equality row used only to compare the named integral readbacks after $D,F,A,T,S,B$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval, family, approach, Riemann readback, Darboux bracket, bound, real-equality, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from regulated-function reads through pointwise approach windows, tagged sums, Darboux brackets, the uniform bound, and terminal real-equality comparison.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BoundedRiemannConvergenceUp$, $\RegulatedFunctionUp$, $\RiemannIntegralUp$, $\DarbouxSumUp$, $\BoundedVariationRealFunctionUp$, $\RealEqualityUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedRiemannConvergenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
Accepted packets are compared only by these displayed rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded Riemann convergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bounded-riemann-convergence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BoundedRiemannConvergenceUp$ carrier $R=(D,F,A,T,S,B,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedRiemannConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of common interval and Darboux partition admission through $D$, regulated-family exposure through $F$, pointwise approach windows through $A$, tagged Riemann readback through $T$, lower/upper Darboux bracket visibility through $S$, uniform boundedness through $B$, terminal real-equality comparison through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bounded-riemann-convergence-carrier}. The interval and partition data are opened by $D$, the regulated family by $F$, and the finite approach windows by $A$.

The Riemann/Darboux rows then supply the analysis handoff. Tagged Riemann reads pass through $T$, lower and upper Darboux brackets pass through $S$, and the common boundedness coordinate is visible as $B$ before any terminal comparison is read.

Finally $E,H,C,P,N$ compare, transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier contains no measure-integration row, no quotient-function equality row, no selected limit row, no choice row, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded Riemann convergence Darboux handoff]
\label{thm:bounded-riemann-convergence-darboux-handoff}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The Darboux handoff is finite. A consumer may inspect the tagged Riemann row $T$ only together with the lower/upper bracket row $S$ over the same displayed interval and partition row $D$, and it may use the common bound only through $B$.
