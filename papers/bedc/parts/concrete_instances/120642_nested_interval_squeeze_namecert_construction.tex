\begin{definition}[NestedIntervalSqueeze carrier]
\label{def:nested-interval-squeeze-carrier}
A $\NestedIntervalSqueezeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(I,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the nested dyadic interval row, listing the retained interval cells, endpoint rows, containment witnesses, and diameter-refinement checks through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row through which endpoint, containment, and dyadic-radius observations are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readout row obtained from the midpoint or endpoint witnesses of the same finite windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $I,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for interval rows, window rows, readout rows, Real sealing, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Real or Completion-facing squeeze request through $I,S,R,E,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NestedIntervalSqueezeUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\NestedIntervalSqueezeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for Real completeness, Heine-Borel compactness, open-cover compactness, countable choice, trichotomy, host equality, quotient reals, or a selected intersection point.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[NestedIntervalSqueeze NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:nested-interval-squeeze-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\NestedIntervalSqueezeUp$ carrier $Z=(I,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\NestedIntervalSqueezeUp}$ surface is exhausted by carrier admission, nested dyadic refinement admission, finite $\StreamNameUp$ window coverage, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readout admission, $\RealUp$ seal nonescape, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every obligation is read through the finite route
$$
I,S,R \longrightarrow E \longrightarrow H,C,P,N .
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:nested-interval-squeeze-carrier}. The nested interval source is $I$, and it already contains the dyadic endpoint, containment, and diameter-refinement rows.

The observation route is finite. The row $S$ displays the endpoint and radius windows that are inspected, and the row $R$ reads the same windows as a regular rational sequence surface.

The Real-facing seal $E$ is downstream of $I,S,R$. Hence a Real or Completion consumer receives only the displayed squeeze corridor before the terminal seal is read.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and locally name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for completeness, compactness, choice, trichotomy, host equality, quotient reals, or a selected intersection point, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[NestedIntervalSqueeze dyadic-refinement stability]
\label{thm:nested-interval-squeeze-dyadic-refinement-stability}
Let $Z=(I,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ and $Z'=(I',S',R',E',H',C',P',N')$ be accepted $\NestedIntervalSqueezeUp$ carriers whose nested dyadic interval rows agree by componentwise $\hsame$ on endpoint rows, containment witnesses, and diameter-refinement checks. If their finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows inspect the same dyadic refinement rows, then the induced $\RegSeqRatUp$ readout rows expose the same public squeeze route through $I,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$ and $I',S',R',E',H',C',P',N'$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct over the finite list of nested dyadic interval rows in $I$ and $I'$. The initial row is transported by the displayed endpoint and containment $\hsame$ witnesses.

For the refinement step, the dyadic diameter and containment checks are rows of the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ corridor. The shared finite windows $S$ and $S'$ inspect exactly those rows, so the next readout entry is transported to the corresponding entry of $R'$.

After the finite induction, the regular rational readout rows agree on the displayed squeeze route. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ and $H',C',P',N'$ only transport, replay, package, and name that route, so no host equality, quotient real, choice, compactness, or completeness endpoint is introduced.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:nested-interval-squeeze-regseqrat-readout}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The $\RegSeqRatUp$ readout row $R$ is built from the endpoint and radius witnesses inspected by $S$. Thus any regular-rational consumer must read $I$ and $S$ before it can inspect $R$.

The terminal seal $E$ is available only after that finite readout has been displayed. The remaining rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route by transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Since none of the carrier coordinates stores a completed intersection point, quotient real, host equality, compactness theorem, choice schedule, trichotomy witness, or ambient completeness principle, the RegSeqRat readout cannot export any such endpoint.
\end{proof}
