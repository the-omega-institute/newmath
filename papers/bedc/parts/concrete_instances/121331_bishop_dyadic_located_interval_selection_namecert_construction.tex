\begin{theorem}[Bishop dyadic located-interval selection obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-dyadic-located-interval-selection-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopDyadicLocatedIntervalSelectionUp$ carrier $S=(Q,D,M,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopDyadicLocatedIntervalSelectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the finite packet of \autoref{def:bishop-dyadic-located-interval-selection-carrier};
\item located-request admission through the displayed $\LocatedIntervalUp$ and $\RealIntervalUp$ rows in $Q$;
\item dyadic bracket admission through the outward rounding and endpoint comparison rows of $D$;
\item midpoint and refinement-budget admission through the finite dyadic midpoint and comparison rows of $M$;
\item regular-Cauchy handoff through the $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows in $R$;
\item Real-seal non-escape through $E$ after the request, bracket, midpoint, and handoff rows have been displayed;

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The midpoint row $M$ refines that bracket by finite dyadic comparisons. It neither selects a host real endpoint nor appeals to an open-cover argument; each refinement step is one displayed midpoint or comparison row.

The handoff row $R$ then exposes the selected bracket as stream windows and regular rational readback, and $E$ seals the resulting endpoint reads as $\RealUp$ data. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Since every consumer read factors through those rows, the local NameCert has no remaining coordinate from which compactness, quotient equality, host choice, or hidden endpoint representatives could be exported.
\end{proof}
