\begin{definition}[Stream reindexing carrier]
\label{def:stream-reindexing-carrier}
A $\StreamReindexingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(X,I,J,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source $\StreamNameUp$ row whose finite observations are being reread;
\item $I$ is the displayed input schedule window listing the source indices available to the request;
\item $J$ is the displayed reindexing schedule, assigning each requested output window to a finite input-window read;
\item $W$ is the synchronized finite window ledger certifying that every output request has a displayed source read;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row attached only to the observed window requests;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row reached after $X,I,J,W,D$ have been displayed;
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal available to real-facing consumers of the reindexed name;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream row, schedules, windows, dyadic tolerance row, readback, and seal;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite reindexing route;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\StreamReindexingUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyReindexingUp$, $\RegularCauchyReindexingUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\StreamReindexingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected tails, unbounded schedule choice, host stream equality, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Stream reindexing NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:stream-reindexing-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\StreamReindexingUp$ carrier $S=(X,I,J,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\StreamReindexingUp}$ surface consists exactly of source-stream admission through $X$, input-window admission through $I$, reindexing-schedule admission through $J$, synchronized finite-window coverage through $W$, dyadic tolerance compatibility through $D$, regular-rational readback through $R$, terminal real sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:stream-reindexing-carrier}. The source stream, input schedule, reindexing schedule, synchronized windows, dyadic tolerance ledger, readback row, and terminal seal are all displayed coordinates.

Carrier admission reads only those coordinates. The synchronized window row $W$ accounts for every output request by a finite source-window read, so the local certificate has no detached schedule object to compare.

Transport and replay are then the structural rows $H$ and $C$, with provenance and local naming supplied by $P$ and $N$. Since no excluded object is a carrier coordinate, the obligation surface cannot export quotient stream equality, selected tails, countable choice, host stream equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Stream reindexing window stability]
\label{thm:stream-reindexing-window-stability}
Every $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyReindexingUp$, or $\RegularCauchyReindexingUp$ consumer of an accepted $\StreamReindexingUp$ packet must first read the displayed rows
$$

$$
In particular, the consumer receives the reindexed stream only through finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows, the synchronized schedule ledger, the dyadic tolerance row, the regular-rational readback, and the terminal real seal. It receives no quotient stream, selected tail, countable-choice schedule, host stream equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:stream-reindexing-namecert-obligations}. They place the source stream row $X$, schedule rows $I,J$, synchronized window row $W$, and dyadic tolerance row $D$ before any readback row can be consumed.

Next read the regular-rational and real-facing rows. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff $R$ is downstream of the finite schedule window, and the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is downstream of that handoff.

Finally apply componentwise transport and displayed replay through $H$ and $C$. The provenance and local naming rows keep the same finite packet attached to the downstream $\CauchyReindexingUp$ route and its $\RegularCauchyReindexingUp$ specialization, so no hidden quotient, selected tail, countable-choice schedule, host equality, or ambient completeness coordinate is available.
\end{proof}
