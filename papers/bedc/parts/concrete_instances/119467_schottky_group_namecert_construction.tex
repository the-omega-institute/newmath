\begin{proof}

The ping-pong obligation is read through $D,B$. The row $D$ displays the paired source and target boundary domains, while $B$ records the finite shadow relation saying how each generator moves the complement of one domain into its paired target domain.

The metric, limit-set, and action obligations are then read through $M,L,F$. These rows keep hyperbolic distance comparisons, boundary accumulation observations, and word-action replay attached to the same finite automorphism data.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for a host Kleinian group, quotient surface, selected fundamental domain, hidden compactification, choice principle, or ambient completion, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}
