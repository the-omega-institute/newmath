$$

\item $n$ is the positive window-length row fixing the length of each inspected word.
\item $W$ is the cyclic finite word row, represented as a $\ListUp$ spine with a named wrap boundary.
\item $S$ is the successor-rotation row, read as a directed $\GraphUp$ edge from each length-$n$ window to the next cyclic window.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the alphabet, length, cyclic word, successor rotation, coverage ledger, uniqueness ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
