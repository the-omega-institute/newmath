Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopRealArithmeticUp$ packet records the constructive arithmetic interface for regular Cauchy real names as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits after the dyadic rational ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular-Cauchy addition route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-addition-namecert}, and the regular-Cauchy multiplication route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-multiplication-namecert}. The packet gives $\RealAlgOrderUp$ and $\MetricSpaceUp$ consumers one public arithmetic route over displayed regular Cauchy names, not separate host operations.

\begin{definition}[Bishop real arithmetic carrier]
\label{def:bishop-real-arithmetic-carrier}
A $\BishopRealArithmeticUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(X,Y,S_X,S_Y,R_X,R_Y,D,+_A,\cdot_A,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ and $Y$ are the two source $\RealUp$ name rows, each read only through a displayed regular Cauchy route.
\item $S_X$ and $S_Y$ are the finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows requested for the two source names.
\item $R_X$ and $R_Y$ are the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows obtained from those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and error ledger used by both arithmetic operations.
\item $+_A$ is the $\RegularCauchyAdditionUp$ row, available only after $X,Y,S_X,S_Y,R_X,R_Y,D$ have been displayed.
\item $\cdot_A$ is the $\RegularCauchyMultiplicationUp$ row, available only after the same source rows and the displayed product-bound entries in $D$ have been displayed.
\item $E$ is the output $\RealUp$ seal for the requested arithmetic read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source names, windows, readbacks, dyadic ledger, addition row, multiplication row, output seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an arithmetic request through the source rows, finite windows, readbacks, dyadic budget, operation row, output seal, transport, package provenance, and local name.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopRealArithmeticUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegularCauchyAdditionUp$, $\RegularCauchyMultiplicationUp$, $\RealAlgOrderUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRealArithmeticUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate asserts exactly that these rows form one regular Cauchy arithmetic packet. It has no coordinate for quotient real equality, selected limits, trichotomy, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, host real addition, or host real multiplication.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop real arithmetic NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-real-arithmetic-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopRealArithmeticUp$ carrier
$$
A=(X,Y,S_X,S_Y,R_X,R_Y,D,+_A,\cdot_A,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRealArithmeticUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the two displayed $\RealUp$ source rows, finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readbacks, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, the $\RegularCauchyAdditionUp$ row, the $\RegularCauchyMultiplicationUp$ row, the output $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every $\RealAlgOrderUp$ or $\MetricSpaceUp$ consumer of arithmetic on regular Cauchy real names must read through these rows before it consumes an operation result.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-real-arithmetic-carrier}. The source data are the two real-name rows, their finite stream windows, their regular rational readbacks, and the shared dyadic tolerance ledger.

The addition and multiplication rows are downstream of those finite rows. The addition row cites the regular-Cauchy addition chapter, while the multiplication row also uses the displayed product-bound entries in the dyadic ledger before the output seal is available.

The output seal $E$ is therefore reached only through the finite regular Cauchy arithmetic route. Componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same displayed rows for downstream $\RealAlgOrderUp$ and $\MetricSpaceUp$ consumers.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient real equality, selected limits, trichotomy, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, host real addition, or host real multiplication, those objects cannot become local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop real arithmetic downstream route]
\label{thm:bishop-real-arithmetic-downstream-route}

$$

$$
The route supplies regular Cauchy addition and multiplication over displayed real-name rows, and exports no quotient real equality, selected limits, trichotomy, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or host arithmetic operation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an arithmetic consumer. The consumer first sees the displayed real-name sources and their finite windows, not host representatives of completed real numbers.

The regular rational readbacks and dyadic tolerance ledger are read before either arithmetic row. Thus the addition and multiplication entries are finite regular Cauchy operation rows rather than detached operations on an ambient real carrier.

Finally the output seal and structural rows package the same finite route for $\RealAlgOrderUp$ and $\MetricSpaceUp$. No carrier row supplies quotient equality, selected limits, trichotomy, ambient completeness, or host arithmetic.
\end{proof}
