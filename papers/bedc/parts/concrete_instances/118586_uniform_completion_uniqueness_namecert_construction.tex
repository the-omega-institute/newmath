\begin{definition}[Uniform completion uniqueness carrier]
\label{def:uniform-completion-uniqueness-carrier}
A $\UniformCompletionUniquenessUp$ carrier is a finite packet
$$
K=(U,C,F,S,M,R,E,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ source row, exposing the entourage ledger and separatedness data read by both completion presentations.
\item $C$ stores the two $\UniformCompletionUp$ presentation rows over the same displayed source.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ route by which each presentation reads uniform Cauchy data.
\item $S$ is the separated comparison row, read through $\CompletionUniquenessUp$ after the two presentations and the Cauchy-filter route have been displayed.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ comparison row used only as a metric handoff for consumers that enter through metric completion.
\item $R$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row visible before regular readback.
\item $E$ combines the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row and terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached from the same finite windows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $U,C,F,S,M,R,E$.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the uniform source through the two presentations, Cauchy-filter route, separated comparison, metric handoff, finite windows, readback, and Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformCompletionUniquenessUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\UniformCompletionUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\CompletionUniquenessUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformCompletionUniquenessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $U,C,F,S,M,R,E,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient-completion equality, selected limits, countable choice, host equality of completed points, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform completion uniqueness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-completion-uniqueness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformCompletionUniquenessUp$ carrier $K=(U,C,F,S,M,R,E,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UniformCompletionUniquenessUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the uniform source row, two uniform-completion presentation rows, Cauchy-filter route, separated comparison row, metric-completion comparison row, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback and Real seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-completion-uniqueness-carrier}. The comparison data are the uniform source $U$, the two presentation rows in $C$, the Cauchy-filter route $F$, and the separated comparison row $S$.

The metric and Real-facing rows are downstream handoffs. Row $M$ lets metric-completion consumers enter the same comparison surface, while $R$ and $E$ expose finite windows, regular readback, and the terminal Real seal.

Finally $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly those rows. Since the carrier contains no quotient-completion equality, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host equality, or ambient completeness row, none of those objects is a local NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform completion uniqueness separated comparison]
\label{thm:uniform-completion-uniqueness-separated-comparison}
Every uniqueness-facing comparison exported by an accepted $\UniformCompletionUniquenessUp$ packet first reads the common $\UniformSpaceUp$ source row $U$, the two $\UniformCompletionUp$ presentation rows $C$, the $\CauchyFilterUp$ route $F$, and then the separated comparison row $S$ through $\CompletionUniquenessUp$. The comparison is therefore a finite separated-comparison route, not a quotient-completion equality or host equality of completed points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:uniform-completion-uniqueness-namecert-obligations}. It orders carrier admission through the source, presentation, Cauchy-filter, and separated comparison rows before any downstream handoff can be read.

Read the separated comparison row $S$. Its role is to compare the two displayed completion presentations after the shared uniform Cauchy route is visible, so it cannot be replaced by equality of quotient representatives.

The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ preserve and replay that order. Thus every uniqueness-facing consumer receives a displayed separated comparison and no host equality witness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform completion uniqueness Real metric handoff]
\label{thm:uniform-completion-uniqueness-real-metric-handoff}
Every metric-facing or Real-facing consumer of an accepted $\UniformCompletionUniquenessUp$ packet factors through the same comparison route
$$
\UniformSpaceUp,\ \UniformCompletionUp,\ \CauchyFilterUp,\ \CompletionUniquenessUp,\ \MetricCompletionUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp .

The handoff exposes the metric-completion comparison row, finite StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, terminal Real seal, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming, but it does not export selected limits, quotient completion equality, countable choice, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The row $M$ is only a comparison handoff for metric-completion consumers. Rows $R$ and $E$ then expose finite windows, regular rational readback, and the Real seal attached to the same comparison route.

Use \autoref{thm:uniform-completion-uniqueness-namecert-obligations} for the structural rows. Since transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite route and the excluded objects are absent from the carrier, the handoff remains inside the displayed NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
