\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DyadicRealDensityUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges-style constructive real analysis, the $\DyadicRealDensityUp$ packet records the finite route by which dyadic rational approximants become visible to a displayed $\RealUp$ name through regular rational readback, finite stream windows, dyadic tolerance data, transport, replay, provenance, and a local naming row. It sits after the dyadic arithmetic surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite-window naming route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a density handoff for Real and Completion consumers; it does not choose a representative stream, quotient real names, invoke trichotomy, use countable choice, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[DyadicRealDensity carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-real-density-carrier}
A $\DyadicRealDensityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(D,W,R,E,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ source row carrying the dyadic rational approximant and its arithmetic certificate.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule at which the Real name is inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row consumed only after the dyadic source, window, and readback rows have been displayed.
\item $T$ is the dyadic tolerance row, read from the same dyadic ledger as $D$, that bounds the approximation request.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,W,R,E,T$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Real approximation request through $D,W,R,E,T,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and local $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicRealDensityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier stores only displayed finite rows. It has no coordinate for selected representatives, quotient equality of real names, trichotomy, countable choice, host least-upper-bound data, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[DyadicRealDensity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-real-density-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicRealDensityUp$ carrier
$$
Z=(D,W,R,E,T,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicRealDensityUp}$ surface consists of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the dyadic source, finite window, regular readback, Real seal, dyadic tolerance, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item tolerance compatibility, requiring the tolerance row $T$ to be read from the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger as the source row $D$;
\item window-readback exactness, requiring every Real approximation request to inspect the finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows $W$ and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$ before the Real seal $E$ is consumed;
\item transport stability, requiring componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay to preserve the route $D,W,R,E,T$;
\item ledger non-escape, stating that $P$ and $N$ export no selected representative stream, quotient real equality, trichotomy, countable-choice selector, host least-upper-bound object, or ambient Real completeness principle.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-real-density-carrier}. The rows $D,W,R,E,T,H,C,P,N$ are exactly the available coordinates, so carrier admission is finite.

The tolerance clause reads $D$ and $T$ together. Since both rows come from the dyadic ledger, an approximation request cannot import a separate host radius or a selected rational outside the displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ source.

The Real-facing clause then reads $W,R,E$. A consumer reaches the Real seal only after the finite windows and regular rational readback have been displayed, so the approximation route remains a finite read rather than a quotient stream judgement.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. None of the refused host-side objects is a coordinate of $Z$, and therefore the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DyadicRealDensity Real approximation]
\label{thm:dyadic-real-density-real-approximation}
Every Real-facing approximation exported by an accepted $\DyadicRealDensityUp$ carrier factors through the displayed route
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\StreamNameUp,\RegSeqRatUp,\RealUp
$$
with its dyadic tolerance, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row. The exported approximation cannot inspect selected limits, quotient equality of real names, countable choice, trichotomy, host least-upper-bound data, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $Z=(D,W,R,E,T,H,C,P,N)$. The dyadic source row $D$ and tolerance row $T$ provide the only approximation scale visible to the packet.

The finite observation then passes through $W$ and $R$. The StreamName row opens only the displayed windows, and the RegSeqRat row supplies the regular rational readback attached to those windows.

The Real seal $E$ is downstream of that finite route. Componentwise transport and replay preserve the same coordinates through $H$ and $C$, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming.

Thus every Real-facing approximation is a read of $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ in the displayed order. Since no other coordinate exists in the carrier, the refused host-side principles are unavailable to the approximation surface.
\end{proof}
