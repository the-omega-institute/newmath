Following Bishop and Bridges constructive completion, the Real-completeness limit route of \autoref{ch:visions-real-completeness} is read through finite moduli, selected windows, regular rational readback, and seal rows rather than through quotient streams or a host completeness theorem. The $\RealCompletionWitnessTransducerUp$ packet records the diagonal-limit witness of \autoref{thm:real-completeness-limit-exists-unique} as one NameCert carrier whose rows are visible before any Real-facing consumer reads the completed witness. It consumes the witness-ledger sibling \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-completion-witness-ledger-namecert} and compares its extracted finite route with the completion extractor \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-extractor-namecert} before the diagonal-limit row is public.

\begin{definition}[Real completion witness transducer carrier]
\label{def:real-completion-witness-transducer-carrier}
A $\RealCompletionWitnessTransducerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(S,M,W,D,R,L,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the regular Cauchy source row over $\RegSeqRatUp$;
\item $M$ is the explicit modulus row assigning each requested dyadic tolerance a displayed late threshold;
\item $W$ is the selected finite-window row over $\StreamNameUp$ at that threshold;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger attached to the same request;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row from the selected windows;
\item $L$ is the diagonal-limit row naming the candidate produced from $S,M,W,D,R$;
\item $U$ is the uniqueness row saying that any competing accepted witness with the same displayed convergence route is identified by the Real seal classifier;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,M,W,D,R,L,U,C,P,N$;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the source and modulus rows through the selected windows, dyadic tolerance ledger, readback, diagonal-limit row, uniqueness row, and local name;
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealCompletionWitnessTransducerUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionWitnessTransducerUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient real equality, selected host limits, countable choice, ambient completion, global stream equality, or a uniqueness proof outside the displayed finite route.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion witness transducer NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-completion-witness-transducer-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\RealCompletionWitnessTransducerUp$ carrier $T=(S,M,W,D,R,L,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionWitnessTransducerUp}$ surface consists exactly of source admission, explicit modulus exposure, selected finite-window exposure, dyadic tolerance ledger exposure, RegSeqRat readback, diagonal-limit row admission, uniqueness-row admission, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, local naming, and non-escape for quotient real equality, selected host limits, countable choice, ambient completion, global stream equality, and undisplayed uniqueness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:real-completion-witness-transducer-carrier}. The packet exposes the regular Cauchy source, explicit modulus, selected windows, dyadic tolerance ledger, regular rational readback, diagonal-limit candidate, uniqueness row, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

The limit witness route is constructive and finite. A tolerance request reaches the modulus row $M$, then the selected windows $W$ and dyadic ledger $D$, then the readback row $R$, and only then the diagonal-limit row $L$.

The uniqueness row $U$ is read through the same displayed route. Componentwise transport, route replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that inventory, while the carrier lacks quotient, choice, host-limit, ambient-completion, global-stream, and hidden-uniqueness coordinates.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion witness transducer diagonal route]
\label{thm:real-completion-witness-transducer-diagonal-route}
Every accepted $\RealCompletionWitnessTransducerUp$ packet realizes the diagonal-limit witness route of \autoref{thm:real-completeness-limit-exists-unique} through the finite spine
$$
S,M,W,D,R \longmapsto L,U,H,C,P,N .
$$
A Real-facing consumer may read the completed witness only after the regular Cauchy source, explicit modulus, selected windows, dyadic tolerance ledger, and RegSeqRat readback rows have been displayed. The route does not export quotient real equality, a selected host limit, countable choice, ambient completion, global stream equality, or a uniqueness proof outside $U$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the Real-completeness target \autoref{thm:real-completeness-limit-exists-unique}. In the transducer packet, the abstract diagonal candidate is represented by the displayed row $L$, reached only after $S,M,W,D,R$ have been exposed.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:real-completion-witness-transducer-namecert-obligations}. They force every accepted consumer to read the same finite modulus, window, tolerance, and readback spine before the diagonal-limit row and uniqueness row become public.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name the same witness route. Since the excluded host and quotient objects are absent from the carrier, the diagonal witness is exported only as this finite transducer route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion witness transducer uniqueness handoff]
\label{thm:real-completion-witness-transducer-uniqueness-handoff}
If two accepted $\RealCompletionWitnessTransducerUp$ packets agree on the regular Cauchy source row $S$, explicit modulus row $M$, selected window row $W$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, RegSeqRat readback row $R$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$, then any Real-facing comparison of their diagonal candidates is read through their displayed uniqueness rows $U$. No comparison may replace those rows by quotient real equality, global stream equality, or a host completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project both packets. Agreement on $S,M,W,D,R,H,C,P,N$ fixes the finite route that reaches the diagonal-limit row in each carrier.

The NameCert obligations make the uniqueness row $U$ the only comparison-facing coordinate after the diagonal-limit row is displayed. Thus a Real-facing consumer must inspect $U$ before treating the two candidates as the same completed witness.

The carrier has no quotient-equality, global-stream, or ambient-completion coordinate. Therefore any comparison that bypasses the displayed uniqueness row is not an accepted read of the transducer packet.
\end{proof}
