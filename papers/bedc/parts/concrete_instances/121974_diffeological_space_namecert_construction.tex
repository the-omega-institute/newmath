\chapter{Diffeological Space NameCert Construction}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-diffeological-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Souriau and Iglesias-Zemmour, the $\DiffeologicalSpaceUp$ packet reconstructs a diffeological-space interface as finite plot-observation data. The carrier records point rows, plot rows, domain-window rows, locality and gluing checks, pullback stability, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it gives topology and manifold consumers a visible smooth-space neighbour without quotienting all plots into a global smooth object.

The packet sits between the open-set reading of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the chart-facing manifold surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-manifold-namecert}, the stream-name interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the $\BHist$ and $\NameCert$ surfaces used throughout the concrete-instance ledger. It does not claim quotient smooth spaces, selected atlases, global colimits, or full manifold reconstruction.

\begin{definition}[Diffeological-space carrier]
\label{def:diffeological-space-carrier}
A $\DiffeologicalSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(X,P,U,L,G,S,T,R,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the point row, listing the observed points whose plot memberships are named by the packet.
\item $P$ is the finite plot row, storing selected parameterized observations into $X$.
\item $U$ is the domain-window row, recording the open-domain fragments on which the displayed plots are inspected.
\item $L$ is the locality row, stating that plot admission is checked on the named domain windows.
\item $G$ is the gluing row, recording compatible finite local plots before a glued plot is read.
\item $S$ is the pullback-stability row, showing that a displayed smooth reparameterization keeps the read inside the same plot surface.
\item $T$ records componentwise transports for point, plot, domain, locality, gluing, and pullback rows.
\item $R$ is the replay ledger that exposes only $X,P,U,L,G,S,T$ to downstream consumers.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DiffeologicalSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\ManifoldUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DiffeologicalSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient smooth-space construction, global colimits, selected atlases, full manifold reconstruction, or host smoothness semantics.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Diffeological-space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:diffeological-space-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\DiffeologicalSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
D=(X,P,U,L,G,S,T,R,Q,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DiffeologicalSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of point-row admission, finite plot-row admission, domain-window visibility, locality checking, gluing checking, pullback stability, componentwise transport, replay exactness, provenance, and local naming. Every topology, manifold, or stream-name consumer of the packet must read the same rows before using a plot observation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier in \autoref{def:diffeological-space-carrier}. The rows $X,P,U$ give the observed points, the selected plots, and the domain windows on which those plots are inspected.

Next read the diffeological closure data. The locality row $L$ keeps plot admission local to the displayed windows, the gluing row $G$ records finite compatible local plots, and the pullback row $S$ keeps smooth reparameterization inside the observed plot surface.

Transport and replay are then structural. The row $T$ preserves each displayed coordinate componentwise, while $R$ exposes only the finite plot-observation inventory to downstream reads.

Finally, provenance and naming are $Q$ and $N$. Since no quotient smooth space, selected atlas, global colimit, full manifold reconstruction, or host smoothness object appears as a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local $\NameCert$ obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Diffeological-space plot-observation nonescape]
\label{thm:diffeological-space-plot-observation-nonescape}
Every public read exported from an accepted $\DiffeologicalSpaceUp$ packet factors through $X,P,U,L,G,S,T,R,Q,N$. The read may be consumed by $\TopologyUp$, $\ManifoldUp$, and $\StreamNameUp$ only as finite plot-observation data; it is not a quotient smooth space, a selected atlas, a global colimit, or a proof of full manifold reconstruction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the NameCert-obligation inventory of \autoref{thm:diffeological-space-namecert-obligations}. It requires every accepted read to expose the point, plot, domain-window, locality, gluing, pullback, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The topology, manifold, and stream-name consumers therefore receive a finite observation interface. The packet can say which displayed plot rows are available and how they survive locality, gluing, and pullback checks.

No refused global object is present in the carrier. Hence quotient smooth spaces, selected atlases, global colimits, host smoothness semantics, and full manifold reconstruction cannot be extracted from the $\DiffeologicalSpaceUp$ packet.
\end{proof}
