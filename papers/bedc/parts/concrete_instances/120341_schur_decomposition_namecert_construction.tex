\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SchurDecompositionUp$}

Following Schur and standard finite-dimensional spectral theory, the $\SchurDecompositionUp$ packet records the finite route by which a displayed square matrix is read through a unitary change-of-basis ledger, an upper-triangular row, and a diagonal spectral readback. It consumes the matrix carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, the linear-map surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-linearmap-namecert}, the inner-product rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-innerproduct-namecert}, the unitary-row discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-unitarygroup-namecert}, and the real-spectrum boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-spectrum-namecert}. The packet names only finite matrix, unitary, triangular, spectral, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert data; it does not import host diagonalization, quotient matrix equality, a selected eigenbasis outside the displayed rows, or ambient spectral theory.

\begin{definition}[Schur decomposition carrier]
\label{def:schur-decomposition-carrier}
A $\SchurDecompositionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(A,F,U,T,\Delta,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the source square-matrix row, read through $\MatrixUp$ and the underlying $\LinearMapUp$ action surface.
\item $F$ is the scalar-field or complex-real spectrum boundary row, exposing the finite scalar readback interface used by the diagonal entries.
\item $U$ is the unitary change-of-basis ledger, read through $\InnerProductUp$ and $\UnitaryGroupUp$.
\item $T$ is the upper-triangular matrix ledger obtained after the displayed unitary change of basis.
\item $\Delta$ is the diagonal spectral readback row, listing the diagonal scalar reads of $T$ through the spectrum boundary $F$.
\item $E$ is the residual triangular exactness row saying that the displayed source matrix is replayed by the $U,T$ route and that no off-ledger remainder is exported.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source matrix, scalar boundary, unitary ledger, triangular ledger, diagonal readback, exactness row, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the source matrix through the scalar boundary, unitary change of basis, triangular ledger, diagonal spectral readback, and residual exactness row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SchurDecompositionUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\InnerProductUp$, $\UnitaryGroupUp$, $\RealSpectrumUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SchurDecompositionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets displaying all ten rows. It has no coordinate for a host eigenbasis, quotient matrix equality, normal diagonalization, selected spectral ordering outside $\Delta$, or an ambient theorem asserting that every host matrix has been decomposed.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Schur decomposition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:schur-decomposition-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SchurDecompositionUp$ carrier
$$
D=(A,F,U,T,\Delta,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SchurDecompositionUp}$ surface consists exactly of source matrix admission for $A$, scalar or spectrum-boundary admission for $F$, unitary-ledger admission for $U$, upper-triangular ledger admission for $T$, diagonal spectral readback for $\Delta$, residual triangular exactness for $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, sibling provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

No Schur decomposition obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The source row $A$ is the only matrix being named, and $F$ is the only scalar or spectrum boundary through which diagonal entries may be read.

The unitary and triangular data are displayed separately. The ledger $U$ records the change-of-basis route, while $T$ records the upper-triangular matrix obtained on that route.

The diagonal readback $\Delta$ and exactness row $E$ then tie the spectrum-facing entries and triangular residual to the same replay path. The support rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this finite route, so the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Schur decomposition unitary triangular handoff]
\label{thm:schur-decomposition-unitary-triangular-handoff}
Every finite-dimensional spectral, matrix-factorization, or linear-map consumer of an accepted $\SchurDecompositionUp$ carrier must first read
$$
A,F,U,T,\Delta,E,H,C,P,N.
$$
The unitary handoff is visible only through $U$ after the source matrix row $A$ and scalar boundary $F$ have been displayed, and the triangular handoff is visible only through $T,\Delta,E$ after that unitary ledger has been read. The handoff exports no host eigenbasis, quotient matrix equality, unrestricted diagonalization theorem, or selected ordering of spectrum entries outside the displayed diagonal ledger.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted carrier. The consumer first reads $A$ and $F$, so the matrix and scalar boundary are fixed before any change-of-basis row is available.

The unitary row $U$ then supplies the displayed change-of-basis ledger. Since the carrier has no other change-of-basis coordinate, any spectral or factorization consumer must pass through this ledger.

Finally $T,\Delta,E$ expose the upper-triangular matrix, the diagonal spectral readback, and the residual exactness row on the same route. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve the route and add no host-level diagonalization or quotient equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Schur decomposition spectrum non-escape]
\label{thm:schur-decomposition-spectrum-nonescape}
Every spectrum-boundary read from an accepted $\SchurDecompositionUp$ carrier factors through the displayed diagonal ledger $\Delta$ and the residual triangular exactness row $E$ after the source matrix, scalar boundary, unitary ledger, and upper-triangular ledger have already been read. A consumer cannot detach a diagonal entry from $A,F,U,T,\Delta,E,H,C,P,N$ and treat it as an ambient host eigenvalue, a quotient representative, or a hidden spectral selector.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier rows in order. The diagonal spectral ledger $\Delta$ appears only after the source matrix, scalar boundary, unitary ledger, and upper-triangular ledger have been displayed.

The exactness row $E$ keeps the diagonal readback attached to the residual triangular route. It does not provide a second spectral interface or a host eigenvalue selector.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that same finite route. Hence every spectrum-facing consumer remains inside the displayed packet and cannot obtain quotient representatives, hidden eigenvalue choices, or an ambient spectral theorem from the carrier.
\end{proof}
