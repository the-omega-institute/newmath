\begin{definition}[Real completion budget-router carrier]
\label{def:real-completion-budget-router-carrier}
A $\RealCompletionBudgetRouterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(o,s,W,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $o$ is the observation-budget row. It bounds which StreamName windows may be inspected.
\item $s$ is the selector-budget row. It records the finite selector requests allowed before a completion-facing route is admitted.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row available under $o$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row produced from the displayed windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger shared by the request, windows, readback, limit-seal handoff, and terminal seal.
\item $L$ is the CauchyLimitSeal-compatible handoff row consuming only $o,s,W,R,D$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal, exposed only after the budgeted window and readback route has passed through $L$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the budget, window, readback, tolerance, seal, replay, provenance, and local name rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay along the finite route from $o,s,W,R,D$ through $L$ to $E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealCompletionBudgetRouterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionBudgetRouterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets whose completion-facing reads are routed through these rows. It has no coordinate for unbounded observation, an external selector, quotient stream equality, selected limits, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion budget-router NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-completion-budget-router-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealCompletionBudgetRouterUp$ carrier $B=(o,s,W,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionBudgetRouterUp}$ surface consists exactly of budget admission, selector admission, StreamName window locality, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance accounting, CauchyLimitSeal-compatible handoff, RealUp seal ordering, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-completion-budget-router-carrier}. Its coordinates expose the two budget rows, the window and readback rows, the dyadic ledger, the seal handoff, the terminal seal, and the structural transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The route order is forced by the carrier shape. A StreamName read is admitted only under $o$, a selector read only under $s$, a RegSeqRat row only as readback from $W$, and a tolerance comparison only through $D$.

The handoff and endpoint rows add no second source. The row $L$ consumes the same budgeted $W,R,D$ route, and $E$ is exposed only after that route has been replayed through $H,C,P,N$. Hence the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion budget-router non-escape]
\label{thm:real-completion-budget-router-nonescape}
Every $\RealUp$ or CauchyLimitSeal-facing consumer of an accepted $\RealCompletionBudgetRouterUp$ packet reads only the observation budget, selector budget, $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, CauchyLimitSeal-compatible handoff, $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row. It cannot bypass the budget rows or import an unbounded observation service, external selector, quotient stream equality, selected limit, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:real-completion-budget-router-namecert-obligations} to identify the only rows available to any accepted consumer. The window row $W$ is behind $o$, the readback row $R$ is behind $W$, and all tolerance comparisons are behind $D$.

Now read the completion-facing rows. The CauchyLimitSeal-compatible handoff $L$ and terminal seal $E$ consume the same displayed budgeted route; neither row carries a detached selector or completed real object.

Finally replay through $H,C,P,N$. These structural rows preserve the displayed route and name its provenance, so a consumer has no remaining coordinate from which an unbudgeted observation, quotient, chosen limit, host equality, choice principle, or ambient completeness theorem could be read.
\end{proof}
