\begin{theorem}[Wronskian NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:wronskian-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\WronskianUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\WronskianUp}$ surface consists exactly of continuous-family admission through $F$, derivative-row admission through $D$, finite jet exposure through $J$, determinant-ledger exposure through $\Omega$, finite-window exposure through $S$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal real sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:wronskian-carrier}. The family row, derivative row, jet table, determinant ledger, window row, readback row, and Real seal row are visible before the structural rows are read.

Read the analytic route in order. The row $F$ supplies the continuous-map family surface, $D$ supplies derivative admission, $J$ records only the displayed finite derivative jets, and $\Omega$ assembles those jets into the Wronskian determinant ledger.

The value-facing route then passes through $S,R,E$. The $\StreamNameUp$ row fixes the inspected finite requests, $\RegSeqRatUp$ reads the determinant values as regular rational data, and $\RealUp$ seals only that displayed readback.

The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for the excluded host principles, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
