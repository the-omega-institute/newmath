\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ClosedInductionTraceUp$}

The $\ClosedInductionTraceUp$ packet realizes the closed-observation and induction-boundary surface of \autoref{ch:visions-transcendental-input-and-induction} as finite NameCert data. It sits beside the finite trace GAP packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-trace-induction-gap-namecert}, the inscription point packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-inscriptionpoint-namecert}, and the typed GAP socket taxonomy of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-typedgapsockettaxonomy-namecert}. The packet records observation rows, input-boundary rows, inscription rows, replay rows, and non-escape rows without installing a substrate-internal induction rule.

\begin{definition}[Closed induction trace carrier]
\label{def:closed-induction-trace-carrier}
A $\ClosedInductionTraceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(O,F,G,B,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the closed observation row: a finite family of records already present in the observational substrate.
\item $F$ is the finite verification trace row, recording observed base and successor checks only as bounded data.
\item $G$ is the induction GAP row, marking the missing universal closure without treating it as an accepted substrate rule.
\item $B$ is the input-boundary row identifying where a forward-binding belief would be supplied from outside the closed observation surface.
\item $S$ is the inscription row connecting that boundary to an inscription-point consumer without adding a new proof rule.
\item $R$ is the replay row that shows which finite observations can be read again by a downstream audit consumer.
\item $E$ is the non-escape row blocking a read from $O,F,G,B,S,R$ to a universal induction theorem inside the substrate.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the observation, trace, GAP, boundary, inscription, replay, non-escape, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClosedInductionTraceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, $\NameCert$, and the sibling GAP and inscription packets.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedInductionTraceUp}$ evidence row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for substrate-internal induction, unbounded universal quantification, countable choice, host natural-number recursion beyond displayed rows, or a hidden source of mathematical belief.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Closed induction trace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:closed-induction-trace-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClosedInductionTraceUp$ carrier $I=(O,F,G,B,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedInductionTraceUp}$ surface consists exactly of closed observation exposure, finite trace admission, induction-GAP retention, input-boundary naming, inscription-row visibility, replay confinement, non-escape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. The obligations realize closed observation, input boundary, inscription, replay, and non-escape rows as finite data; they do not provide a substrate-internal induction schema.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:closed-induction-trace-carrier}. The observation row and finite trace row provide only bounded records already present in the closed observational surface.

The boundary step reads $G,B,S$ together. The GAP row keeps the missing universal closure visible, the boundary row names the outside-supply site, and the inscription row records the local reception point without adding a rule to the substrate.

Replay and non-escape then read $R,E$. A downstream audit may replay displayed observations and trace rows, but the non-escape row blocks replacement of those finite records by a universal induction theorem.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite rows. Since substrate-internal induction, unbounded universal quantification, countable choice, host recursion beyond displayed rows, and hidden belief supply are absent from the carrier, none can be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\falsifiablePrediction{An accepted $\ClosedInductionTraceUp$ packet is refuted if a consumer erases the induction GAP row or reads the input-boundary row as an internal induction rule while preserving the same finite observation trace.}

\independenceWitness{finite-trace-induction-gap, inscriptionpoint, typedgapsockettaxonomy, closedobservationsystem: none is bijective to $\ClosedInductionTraceUp$ because this carrier jointly binds closed observation, finite trace, induction GAP, input boundary, inscription, replay, non-escape, transport, provenance, and local naming rows.}
