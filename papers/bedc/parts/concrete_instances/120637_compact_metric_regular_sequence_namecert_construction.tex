\begin{definition}[Compact metric regular sequence carrier]
\label{def:compact-metric-regular-sequence-carrier}

$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row carrying finite-net, located-distance, and compact inspection data.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ row for the source sequence windows inspected by the compact metric consumer.
\item $W$ is the finite window ledger pairing requested precisions with inspected compact-net cells and source sequence windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for compact mesh radii, window distances, and regular Cauchy budgets.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row produced from the displayed windows and dyadic budgets.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, exposed only after the compact source, finite windows, dyadic tolerances, and regular rational readback have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,S,W,D,R,E$ and for the structural rows.

\begin{theorem}[Compact metric regular sequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-metric-regular-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactMetricRegularSequenceUp$ carrier $M=(K,S,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactMetricRegularSequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact-metric source exposure through $K$, source-window exposure through $S$, finite-window ledger exposure through $W$, dyadic tolerance accounting through $D$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, $\RealUp$ seal handoff through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-metric-regular-sequence-carrier}. The finite packet exposes exactly the compact metric source, source stream windows, inspected finite-window ledger, dyadic tolerance ledger, regular rational readback, Real seal, and structural rows.

Read the analytic rows in order. The row $K$ supplies only displayed compact metric inspection data, while $S$ and $W$ record the finite source windows and compact-net cells inspected at each requested precision.

The handoff is then forced through $D,R,E$. Dyadic budgets are recorded in $D$, regular rational readback is recorded in $R$, and the Real seal $E$ is reached only after those rows are visible. Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet, so no additional NameCert obligation is available outside the displayed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact metric regular sequence finite-net handoff]
\label{thm:compact-metric-regular-sequence-finite-net-handoff}
Every $\CompactMetricRegularSequenceUp$ consumer that requests regular sequence data must first read the finite route
$$
\CompactMetricUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp .
$$
The compact metric source supplies finite-net cells, the stream row supplies inspected windows, the dyadic ledger supplies mesh and tail budgets, and the regular rational row supplies the displayed readback before any $\RealUp$ seal is exposed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted carrier $M=(K,S,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$. The compact source row $K$ is the only compact metric coordinate, and the source observations are read through $S$ and the finite ledger $W$.

Use the finite-net data in $K$ together with the displayed windows in $W$. For a requested precision, the consumer receives a compact-net cell and the source observations inspected in that cell rather than an unrecorded compactness theorem.

The dyadic and regular rows complete the handoff. The ledger $D$ records the mesh radius and tail budget, and $R$ repacks the inspected windows as $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback data. Transport and replay through $H,C,P,N$ preserve this route, so the consumer reaches $E$ only after the finite-net handoff has been displayed.
\end{proof}
