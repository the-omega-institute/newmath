\begin{definition}[Henstock gauge partition carrier]
\label{def:henstock-gauge-partition-carrier}

$$

$$
whose rows expose:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$, the interval source row read through $\LocatedIntervalUp$ and sealed against the endpoint order available in $\RealUp$;
\item $G$, the gauge-radius row assigning a positive local radius to every admitted tag over the displayed interval;
\item $T$, the finite tagged-interval ledger listing tags, left and right endpoints, cell order, and cell membership;
\item $E$, the endpoint readback row carrying the $\LocatedIntervalUp$ boundary data and the terminal $\RealUp$ endpoint seal for each tagged cell;
\item $F$, the fineness ledger stating that every tagged interval of $T$ lies inside the radius supplied by $G$ at its tag;
\item $C$, the coverage ledger stating that the finite tagged intervals cover the displayed interval without leaving a named gap;
\item $D$, the disjointness ledger stating that distinct interiors in the tagged-interval list do not overlap;
\item $R$, the displayed $\Cont$ replay row preserving the route from interval and gauge data to the same finite partition packet;
\item $Q$, the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HenstockGaugePartitionUp$, $\GaugeIntegralUp$, $\FinePartitionGaugeUp$, $\CousinLemmaUp$, $\HenstockLemmaUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$, the local $\NameCert_{\HenstockGaugePartitionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,G,T,E,F,C,D,R,Q,N$. It has no coordinate for a choice-selected partition family, open-cover compactness, quotient equality of functions, an external integral theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Henstock gauge partition fineness ledger]
\label{thm:henstock-gauge-partition-fineness-ledger}
Every accepted $\HenstockGaugePartitionUp$ carrier exposes a single finite route
$$
I,G,T,E \longmapsto F \longmapsto C,D
$$
from located endpoint data and positive gauge radii to the coverage and disjointness ledgers. The row $F$ is the only fineness witness visible to $\FinePartitionGaugeUp$ and $\HenstockLemmaUp$ consumers, and it is read from the same tags and endpoints displayed by $T$ and $E$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The fineness row $F$ consumes exactly those rows. It cannot change the tag list or endpoints because it is a ledger row over the tagged intervals already listed in $T$ and read back in $E$.

The coverage and disjointness rows $C,D$ are downstream finite checks on the same list. Thus any fine-partition or Henstock-lemma consumer that reads the packet receives the displayed fineness ledger before it receives coverage or disjointness as usable partition data.
\end{proof}
