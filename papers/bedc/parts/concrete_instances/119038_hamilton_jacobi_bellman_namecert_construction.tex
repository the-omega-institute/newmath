\begin{definition}[Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman carrier]
\label{def:hamilton-jacobi-bellman-carrier}
A $\HamiltonJacobiBellmanUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
H=(X,U,T,C,V,G,E,D,J,Q,L,R,A,M,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the displayed state row and $U$ is the admissible control row consumed by the finite control request.
\item $T$ is the transition or dynamics row, and $C$ is the running-cost row sealed only through displayed $\RealUp$ data.
\item $V$ is the value-row name, $G$ is the finite slope or gradient-read row, and $E$ is the terminal or boundary value row.
\item $D$ is the $\DynamicProgrammingUp$ recursion row that supplies finite Bellman table readback.
\item $J$ is the $\HamiltonJacobiUp$ Hamiltonian readback row, and $Q$ is the $\PdeUp$ relation row tying the value surface to a displayed PDE request.
\item $L$ is the $\LQRUp$ quadratic-control dependency row, and $R$ is the $\ControlRiccatiUp$ Riccati comparison row available for the linear-quadratic face.
\item $A$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,U,T,C,V,G,E,D,J,Q,L,R$.
\item $M$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the route
$$
D,J,Q,L,R \longmapsto V .
$$
\item $K$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HamiltonJacobiBellmanUp$, $\DynamicProgrammingUp$, $\HamiltonJacobiUp$, $\PdeUp$, $\ControlRiccatiUp$, $\LQRUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,U,T,C,V,G,E,D,J,Q,L,R,A,M,K,P,N$. It has no coordinate for selected optimal controls, classical solution existence, viscosity uniqueness, unbounded PDE regularity, host minimizers, quotient value-function equality, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hamilton-jacobi-bellman-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HamiltonJacobiBellmanUp$ carrier
$$
H=(X,U,T,C,V,G,E,D,J,Q,L,R,A,M,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\HamiltonJacobiBellmanUp}$ surface is the following five-row obligation surface:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission exposes the displayed state, control, transition, running-cost, value, slope, and terminal rows $X,U,T,C,V,G,E$;
\item dynamic-programming exposure routes finite Bellman recursion through $D$ and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dynamic-programming-namecert};
\item Hamilton-Jacobi and PDE exposure routes Hamiltonian and relation reads through $J,Q$ and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hamilton-jacobi-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pde-namecert};
\item quadratic-control exposure routes the LQR and Riccati faces through $L,R$ and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lqr-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-control-riccati-namecert};
\item structural exposure routes componentwise transport, replay, provenance, refusal, and local naming through $A,M,K,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No listed row exports selected optimal controls, classical solution existence, viscosity uniqueness, unbounded PDE regularity, host minimizers, quotient value-function equality, or ambient real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hamilton-jacobi-bellman-carrier}. The rows $X,U,T,C,V,G,E$ give the visible finite control and value surface before any Bellman or PDE request is read.

Read the sibling rows next. The dynamic-programming row $D$ supplies finite Bellman recursion, the Hamilton-Jacobi row $J$ supplies Hamiltonian readback, and the PDE row $Q$ supplies the displayed relation surface.

For the quadratic-control face, the LQR row $L$ and Riccati row $R$ are dependency rows inside the same packet. A consumer may read them only after the finite value, Hamiltonian, and relation rows have been displayed.

Finally use $A,M,K,P,N$ for transport, replay, provenance, refusal, and local naming. Since the carrier has no further coordinate, none of the refused analytic or choice-theoretic objects can be exported by the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
