\begin{definition}[Bishop located compactness carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-compactness-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedCompactnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row, exposing the distance classifier and metric-law ledger used by every finite-net request.
\item $C$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ service row, available only as the displayed Cauchy-readback boundary for completion-facing consumers.
\item $T$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite-net row, storing finite center bundles, coverage-distance rows, and rational tolerance comparisons.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedSetUp$ row for the compact-facing located subset or whole-space admission surface.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row that names the finite observation schedule for net and distance reads.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for regular rational distance and tolerance data.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger row for dyadic radius and coverage comparisons.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, complete, finite-net, located-set, stream, rational, dyadic, real, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $Q$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a compactness tolerance request through $M,C,T,L,W,R,D,E,H,P,N$.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedCompactnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected subsequences, host compact subsets, quotient completion, or chosen limit points.
\end{definition}

\label{thm:bishop-located-compactness-net-obligations}

\begin{proof}

The support rows $H,Q,P,N$ preserve this route by componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, choice-free sequential compactness, or selected host limit points, the obligation surface is exhausted by the displayed finite-net projection.
\end{proof}
