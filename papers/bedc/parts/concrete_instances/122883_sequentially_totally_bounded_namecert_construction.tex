\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SequentiallyTotallyBoundedUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sequentially-totally-bounded-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\SequentiallyTotallyBoundedUp$ packet records the L10 finite-window compactness route by which a bounded regular Cauchy source is inspected through finite nets before a Real or Completion consumer asks for a cluster-facing read. It sits beside the bounded source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-regular-cauchy-namecert}, the bounded-subsequence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-regular-cauchy-subsequence-namecert}, the Cauchy sequence space of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchysequencespace-namecert}, the stream-window source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a sequential finite-window total-boundedness carrier; it is not open-cover compactness, a selected subsequence, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[SequentiallyTotallyBounded carrier]
\label{def:sequentially-totally-bounded-carrier}
A $\SequentiallyTotallyBoundedUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(B,Q,X,W,D,E,F,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BoundedRegularCauchyUp$ source row;
\item $Q$ is the $\BoundedRegularCauchySubsequenceUp$ downstream route available only as displayed bounded-subsequence provenance;
\item $X$ is the $\CauchySequenceSpaceUp$ ambient sequence-space row;
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule inspected at the requested precision;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for the finite window;
\item $E$ is the finite net row covering the inspected window at the displayed dyadic tolerance;
\item $F$ is the sequential finite-window classifier saying that every inspected prefix element is assigned to one displayed net cell;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ readback row reached only after $B,X,W,D,E,F$ have been displayed;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport over the source, sequence-space, finite-window, dyadic, net, classifier, readback, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same finite-window route;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SequentiallyTotallyBoundedUp$, $\BoundedRegularCauchyUp$, $\BoundedRegularCauchySubsequenceUp$, $\CauchySequenceSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SequentiallyTotallyBoundedUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an open-cover compactness proof, a selected infinite subsequence, countable choice, quotient stream equality, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SequentiallyTotallyBounded NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sequentially-totally-bounded-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SequentiallyTotallyBoundedUp$ carrier $S=(B,Q,X,W,D,E,F,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SequentiallyTotallyBoundedUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, bounded regular Cauchy source exposure, bounded-subsequence provenance, Cauchy sequence-space exposure, finite stream-window scheduling, dyadic tolerance exactness, finite-net assignment, sequential finite-window classifier stability, regular rational and Real readback non-escape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:sequentially-totally-bounded-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes only the rows $B,Q,X,W,D,E,F,R,H,C,P,N$.

The finite-window route reads the bounded source $B$, the sequence-space row $X$, the stream schedule $W$, and the dyadic ledger $D$ before the finite net $E$ and classifier $F$ are available. Thus total boundedness is inspected through displayed finite windows rather than through an open-cover or host compactness principle.

Finally $R,H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route for regular rational readback, Real sealing, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since selected subsequences, countable choice, quotient stream equality, host real equality, and ambient completeness are absent from the carrier, no accepted consumer can project them from the certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[SequentiallyTotallyBounded L10 finite-window compactness route]
\label{thm:sequentially-totally-bounded-l10-finite-window-route}
Every L10 compactness-facing consumer of an accepted $\SequentiallyTotallyBoundedUp$ packet factors through
$$
\BoundedRegularCauchyUp,\ \BoundedRegularCauchySubsequenceUp,\ \CauchySequenceSpaceUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp .
$$
The consumer first reads bounded regular Cauchy data, bounded-subsequence provenance, and the Cauchy sequence-space row; then it inspects a finite stream window and dyadic tolerance ledger; then it reads the finite net classifier; only then may it reach the regular rational and Real seal rows. The route does not claim open-cover compactness, selected subsequences, countable choice, quotient stream equality, host real equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:sequentially-totally-bounded-namecert-obligations}. They require the bounded source, bounded-subsequence provenance, sequence-space row, stream schedule, dyadic ledger, finite net, and classifier to be displayed before readback.

Use the carrier ordering from \autoref{def:sequentially-totally-bounded-carrier}. The readback row $R$ is available only after $B,X,W,D,E,F$, and the bounded-subsequence row $Q$ is provenance rather than a selected infinite subsequence.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite-window packet. Because the forbidden compactness and choice coordinates are absent, the exported L10 route is a finite-window total-boundedness certificate, not a classical compactness or completeness theorem.
\end{proof}
