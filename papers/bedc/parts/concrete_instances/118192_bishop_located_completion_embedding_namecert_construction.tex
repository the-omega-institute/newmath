Following Bishop and Bridges on metric completions, located subsets, and regular real names, the $\BishopLocatedCompletionEmbeddingUp$ packet records the finite route by which a metric source is embedded into a displayed located completion without quotienting Cauchy sequences or selecting host limits. It sits beside the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the completion packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}, the metric-completion service of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the separated-metric reflection of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedmetricreflection-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the embedding as finite NameCert data; it is not a quotient completion, a host equality principle, a choice-selected limit, trichotomy, or an appeal to ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Bishop located completion embedding carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-completion-embedding-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedCompletionEmbeddingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
(M,L,D,E,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the metric source row, exposing the $\MetricUp$ distance, symmetry, triangle, and zero-distance comparison used by the embedding.
\item $L$ is the located positive-radius row, recording the finite rational-radius evidence that a source point has a displayed neighbourhood before any completion read is made.
\item $D$ is the dense completion route, carrying the $\CompletionUp$ or $\MetricCompletionUp$ approach map from the source metric into the completion packet.
\item $E$ is the embedding graph row, pairing each inspected source point with its completion name and recording graph membership before equality is read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ readback row, exposing only regular rational distance reads and the terminal real seal of the embedded point.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, located-radius row, dense route, embedding graph, readback, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a source-point request through located-radius inspection, dense-route lookup, embedding-graph readback, regular rational read, and real sealing.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopLocatedCompletionEmbeddingUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedCompletionEmbeddingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for quotient completions, host equality of representatives, choice-selected limits, trichotomy, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located completion embedding NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-completion-embedding-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedCompletionEmbeddingUp$ carrier $(M,L,D,E,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedCompletionEmbeddingUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the metric source, located positive-radius evidence, dense completion route, embedding graph, regular rational and real readback, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, provenance, and local naming. Every embedding read must pass through $M,L,D,E,R,H,C,P,N$, and no obligation is stored outside those finite rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The displayed rows are precisely the metric source $M$, located-radius evidence $L$, dense completion route $D$, embedding graph $E$, regular rational and real readback $R$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

The located part is read before the completion part. A source-point request first enters the metric row and the positive-radius ledger, so the later dense-route lookup is tied to displayed local evidence rather than to an external completion representative.

The embedding graph row then records the finite source-to-completion relation, and the readback row exposes only regular rational distance reads and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. Componentwise transport and displayed replay preserve this same order.

Since the carrier contains no quotient completion row, host equality coordinate, selected-limit coordinate, trichotomy split, or ambient real-completeness row, none of those objects can be projected from the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
