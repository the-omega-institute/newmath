\begin{definition}[Dyadic completion functor carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-completion-functor-carrier}

$$
F=(D,S,R,E,U,M,H,C,P,N)
$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which each dyadic source and map request is inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row reached after $D$ and $S$ have been displayed.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the completed source and target names.
\item $U$ is the $\CompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ extension row, read only after the dyadic, stream, regular-rational, and Real-seal rows have been projected.
\item $M$ is the map ledger, recording identity preservation and composition preservation for displayed dyadic maps through the same $D,S,R,E,U$ route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for dyadic data, stream windows, regular rational readback, Real seals, universal-property rows, map ledgers, replay, provenance, and local naming.

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic completion functor NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-completion-functor-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicCompletionFunctorUp$ carrier
$$
F=(D,S,R,E,U,M,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicCompletionFunctorUp}$ surface consists exactly of dyadic source admission through $D$, finite window inspection through $S$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal Real sealing through $E$, universal-property extension through $U$, identity and composition preservation through $M$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Every map-level completion consumer must read
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp \longmapsto \CompletionUniversalPropertyUp
$$
before treating the completed map as public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-completion-functor-carrier}. The source and observation rows are $D$ and $S$, so a map consumer first sees dyadic tolerance data and finite stream windows.

Read $R$ and $E$ next. The regular rational row is the only readback route from the finite windows, and the Real seal is downstream of that readback rather than a selected host limit.

The universal-property row $U$ and map ledger $M$ then record the functorial part. Identity and composition are preserved as finite ledger reads over the same dyadic, stream, regular, and Real-seal route; no host function equality or quotient completion is introduced.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the displayed packet. Since the carrier has no coordinates for quotient streams, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic completion functor map route]
\label{thm:dyadic-completion-functor-map-route}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
