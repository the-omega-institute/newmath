\chapter{The Golden-Mean Quadratic-Algebra Capstone}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-window6-golden-mean-quadratic-capstone}
\origin{ai}

The golden-mean zeta facets have a closest honest master identity, but not a single finite identity generating every certificate in the surrounding family. The organizing object is the quadratic algebra
$$
Q_y=\mathbb Z[y][x]/(x^2-x-y),
$$
with every element written as $a x+b$ over $\mathbb Z[y,t]$ and conjugation $\overline{x}=1-x$. The root equation is $x^2=x+y$, so multiplication reduces by
$$
(a x+b)(c x+d)=(ac+ad+bc)x+(acy+bd).
$$
The trace and norm are
$$
\operatorname{Tr}(a x+b)=a+2b,\qquad
N(a x+b)=b^2+ab-ya^2,
$$
and hence $\operatorname{Tr}(x)=1$ and $N(x)=-y$.

\section{The Master Norm Identity}
\label{sec:window6-quadratic-capstone-master}

The central identity is the norm computation
$$
(1-tx)(1+t(x-1))=1-t-yt^2.
$$
It is a norm identity in $Q_y[t]$: the second factor is the conjugate of $1-tx$, because $\overline{x}=1-x$. Equivalently, with the regular representation
$$
\rho(a x+b)=
\begin{pmatrix}
a+b&ya\\
a&b
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\rho(x)=T_y=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&y\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix},
$$
one has $\det\rho(a x+b)=N(a x+b)$ and $\operatorname{tr}\rho(a x+b)=\operatorname{Tr}(a x+b)$. The matrix form of the same identity is
$$
(I-tT_y)(I+t(T_y-I))=(1-t-yt^2)I,
$$
which is the Cayley--Hamilton reading of $T_y^2=T_y+yI$.

\begin{theorem}[Quadratic-algebra capstone certificate]
\label{thm:window6-quadratic-capstone-certificate}
In the finite $\mathbb Z[y,t]$ model of $Q_y$, the root identity $x^2=x+y$, the conjugation identity $\overline{x}=1-x$, the norm and trace values $N(x)=-y$ and $\operatorname{Tr}(x)=1$, the master norm identity $(1-tx)(1+t(x-1))=1-t-yt^2$, the regular representation value $\rho(x)=T_y$, concrete determinant and trace readings of $\rho(x)$, six Fibonacci power identities, six Hankel--Cassini identities, and two finite truncated resolvent identities are all checked by Lean without axioms.
\end{theorem}

\section{Powers, Cassini, and Truncated Resolvents}
\label{sec:window6-quadratic-capstone-consequences}

Let $F_0(y)=0$, $F_1(y)=1$, and
$$
F_{n+2}(y)=F_{n+1}(y)+yF_n(y).
$$
The quadratic relation gives the finite power law
$$
x^{n+1}=F_{n+1}(y)x+yF_n(y),
$$
verified in Lean for the first six nontrivial instances. Taking traces gives the cyclic trace polynomials $C_n(y)=\operatorname{Tr}(x^n)$, whose specialization at $y=1$ is the Lucas trace sequence.

The same conjugation gives the Hankel--Turan discriminant without a separate determinant argument:
$$
C_n(y)C_{n+2}(y)-C_{n+1}(y)^2=(x\overline{x})^n(x-\overline{x})^2=(-y)^n(1+4y).
$$
Here $x\overline{x}=-y$ and
$$
(x-\overline{x})^2=(2x-1)^2=1+4y.
$$
The capstone certificate checks this Cassini identity for $n=1,\ldots,6$ as polynomial data.

The finite resolvent identity is also a direct consequence of the same denominator:
$$
(1-t-yt^2)\sum_{i=0}^{N}x^i t^i
=1+t(x-1)-t^{N+1}x^{N+1}-y t^{N+2}x^N.
$$
The Lean certificate records the concrete $N=1$ and $N=2$ truncations as finite polynomial computations. These truncations are not a replacement for the primitive-orbit product; they are the quadratic-algebra denominator viewed inside $Q_y[t]$.

\section{Honest Scope Across the Six Facets}
\label{sec:window6-quadratic-capstone-scope}

There is no single finite identity generating all six facets; the closest honest master is the quadratic-algebra norm identity. This is the honest organizing master of the golden-mean SFT zeta facets, not a grand unification and not a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis; the independent facets remain separate certificates.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.32\linewidth}p{0.56\linewidth}}
\textbf{Facet} & \textbf{Generation by the quadratic master} \\
A determinant/zeta/trace-recurrence & \textbf{YES directly}. The determinant, resolvent denominator, and trace recurrence come from $T_y^2=T_y+yI$ and $\det(I-tT_y)=1-t-yt^2$; the $\mathbb Z[\varphi]$ splitting still needs $\varphi^2=\varphi+1$. \\
B Euler-product/primitive-orbit & \textbf{PARTLY}. The master gives $C_n(y)=\operatorname{Tr}(x^n)$ and therefore the input to the Mobius transform, but positivity $P_n\in\mathbb N[y]$ is an independent necklace-counting theorem. \\
C Parry/RPF/cylinder & \textbf{MOSTLY}. The power law, cylinder recurrences, and derivative identity $C_n'=nF_{n-1}$ are governed by $x^2=x+y$; the golden spectral projector belongs to the $\mathbb Z[\varphi]$ specialization. \\
D Hankel--Turan/discriminant & \textbf{YES}. Conjugation gives $x\overline{x}=-y$ and $(x-\overline{x})^2=1+4y$, hence the Cassini--Hankel identity. \\

Thus the capstone is an organizing certificate, not a total generator. It explains why the determinant, trace recurrence, resolvent denominator, power law, and Hankel discriminant repeatedly share the same polynomial $1-t-yt^2$, while marking the primitive-necklace positivity, golden-ring projector, pentagon geometry, and presentation data as independent inputs.

The zeta-program hub of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-window6-golden-mean-zeta-program-hub} consumes this capstone as the finite denominator and norm-identity source feeding the determinant, trace-recurrence, resolvent, and Hankel-facing facets. The hub retains the primitive-orbit, Euler-product, cyclotomic, and presentation data that the capstone explicitly does not generate, so the dependency is a bounded algebraic input rather than a replacement for the surrounding finite zeta program.
