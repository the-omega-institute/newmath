\begin{definition}[Real regular-subsequence cluster carrier]
\label{def:real-regular-subsequence-cluster-carrier}
A $\RealRegularSubsequenceClusterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(S,R,D,T,W,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the subsequence schedule row: a finite $\StreamNameUp$-indexed list of selected positions, monotone schedule comparisons, and source-window names.
\item $R$ is the regular rational readback row: the $\RegSeqRatUp$ endpoint data read at the scheduled positions, with the source classifier and regular readback ledger still visible.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger: a $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ precision row recording the tolerances used to compare scheduled readbacks inside a finite cluster window.
\item $T$ is the tail-estimate row: a $\RegularCauchyTailEstimateUp$ packet certifying that the scheduled window is read through displayed finite tail data before any Real-facing seal is consumed.
\item $W$ is the cluster-window witness row: a finite family of scheduled positions whose regular rational readbacks are pairwise inside the displayed dyadic tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row: the Real-facing endpoint exposed only after $S,R,D,T,W$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the schedule, readback, tolerance, tail, window, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
S,R,D,T,W\longmapsto E .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealRegularSubsequenceClusterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegularCauchyTailEstimateUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealRegularSubsequenceClusterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for selecting a cluster point, quotienting streams, invoking countable choice, deciding trichotomy, or replacing the finite tail route by ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real regular-subsequence cluster NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-regular-subsequence-cluster-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealRegularSubsequenceClusterUp$ carrier $K=(S,R,D,T,W,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealRegularSubsequenceClusterUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the subsequence schedule $S$, regular rational readback $R$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, tail-estimate row $T$, cluster-window witness $W$, terminal Real seal $E$, transport row $H$, replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$;
\item schedule-readback compatibility, requiring every selected position in $S$ to be read through the same displayed $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$;
\item dyadic-window stability, requiring the cluster-window comparisons in $W$ to use only the tolerance ledger $D$ and the finite tail surface $T$;
\item seal-route exactness, requiring the $\RealUp$ row $E$ to be consumed only after the schedule, readback, dyadic tolerance, tail estimate, and cluster window have been displayed;
\item ledger non-escape, requiring $P$ and $N$ to source no Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem, selected cluster point, quotient stream equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No cluster obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $K=(S,R,D,T,W,E,H,C,P,N)$. The analytic data available to a consumer are the subsequence schedule, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, regular Cauchy tail estimate, finite cluster window, and terminal Real seal.

The schedule-readback clause follows by reading $S$ through the StreamName route and then through the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$. A selected position has no independent value row unless the corresponding regular rational readback is displayed.

The dyadic-window clause uses $D,T,W$ together. The dyadic ledger records the tolerance, the tail-estimate row records the finite Real-facing route, and the cluster-window witness records only pairwise comparisons inside that displayed tolerance.

The seal-route clause places $E$ after $S,R,D,T,W$. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay preserve the same finite packet, while $\Pkg$ provenance and the local $\NameCert$ name only those rows.

Finally inspect the non-escape clause. Since the carrier has no row for a selected cluster point, quotient stream equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}
