\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CauchyBinetUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-binet-namecert}
\origin{human}

Cauchy--Binet's classical finite matrix-minor identity gives the $\CauchyBinetUp$ packet its linear-algebra surface: a displayed product matrix minor is expanded as a finite sum over intermediate column subsets, with every determinant, permutation sign, and scalar multiplication read through already named rows. It sits after the finite matrix carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, the determinant packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-determinant-namecert}, the permutation-sign route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-permutation-namecert}, and the scalar ring source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ring-namecert}. The packet names only finite minor data and its ledger; it does not assert a host linear-algebra theorem, quotient matrix equality, infinite-dimensional determinant, selected basis completion, or ambient algebraic closure.

\begin{definition}[Cauchy--Binet carrier]\label{def:cauchy-binet-carrier}
A $\CauchyBinetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(R,A,C,I,J,K,P_{\mathrm{row}},P_{\mathrm{col}},D_A,D_C,S,H,T,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the consumed $\RingUp$ scalar row.
\item $A$ and $C$ are the two $\MatrixUp$ factors over $R$, with compatible displayed inner dimension.
\item $I$ and $J$ are the displayed row and column index lists selecting the product minor.
\item $K$ is the finite list of intermediate column subsets of the required cardinality.
\item $P_{\mathrm{row}}$ and $P_{\mathrm{col}}$ are $\PermutationUp$ rows used to order the selected rows, selected columns, and intermediate subsets before determinant signs are read.
\item $D_A$ records the $\DeterminantUp$ minors of $A$ indexed by $(I,K)$, and $D_C$ records the $\DeterminantUp$ minors of $C$ indexed by $(K,J)$.
\item $S$ is the finite scalar sum row
$$
\sum_{K} \det(A_{I,K})\,\det(C_{K,J})
$$
read through the ring operations and the determinant rows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the scalar, matrix, index-list, permutation, determinant, finite-sum, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a product-minor request through
$$
R,A,C,I,J,K,P_{\mathrm{row}},P_{\mathrm{col}},D_A,D_C,S \longmapsto H,Q,N .
$$
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyBinetUp$, $\RingUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\DeterminantUp$, $\PermutationUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyBinetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for host matrix equality, quotient row spaces, infinite determinant algorithms, selected bases, exterior algebra, or ambient field closure.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy--Binet minor expansion]\label{thm:cauchy-binet-minor-expansion}
For every accepted $\CauchyBinetUp$ carrier
$$
B=(R,A,C,I,J,K,P_{\mathrm{row}},P_{\mathrm{col}},D_A,D_C,S,H,T,Q,N),
$$
the product-minor read of the displayed matrix product factors through exactly the finite route
$$
\MatrixUp,\PermutationUp,\DeterminantUp,\RingUp \longmapsto D_A,D_C,S \longmapsto H,T,Q,N .
$$
The exported identity is the displayed finite sum of paired minors indexed by $K$; it is not a host theorem about all matrices and not an equality outside the named carrier rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-binet-carrier}. The matrix rows $A$ and $C$, the selected index rows $I,J,K$, and the permutation rows $P_{\mathrm{row}},P_{\mathrm{col}}$ are visible before any determinant is read.

The determinant rows are then finite. The row $D_A$ reads each minor of $A$ selected by $(I,K)$, and $D_C$ reads each minor of $C$ selected by $(K,J)$. Both rows are routed through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-determinant-namecert} and keep the needed order data attached to the permutation packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-permutation-namecert}.

Finally the scalar sum row $S$ is read through the $\RingUp$ source. Since $S$ contains only the displayed products $\det(A_{I,K})\det(C_{K,J})$ and their finite sum, the exported expansion remains inside the named matrix, determinant, permutation, ring, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy--Binet ledger refusal]\label{thm:cauchy-binet-ledger-refusal}
Every accepted $\CauchyBinetUp$ packet refuses host matrix equality, quotient row or column spaces, infinite-dimensional determinants, selected basis completion, exterior-algebra semantics, ambient field closure, and any determinant algorithm not displayed by the consumed $\DeterminantUp$ rows. A consumer requesting one of those objects must leave the carrier route
$$
R,A,C,I,J,K,P_{\mathrm{row}},P_{\mathrm{col}},D_A,D_C,S,H,T,Q,N .
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the carrier in \autoref{def:cauchy-binet-carrier}. Its analytic rows are the scalar ring, the two displayed matrices, the finite index lists, the finite intermediate-subset list, the permutation order rows, the determinant-minor rows, and the finite scalar sum.

The minor expansion of \autoref{thm:cauchy-binet-minor-expansion} uses only those rows and the structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. No coordinate stores a quotient row space, an ambient equality of matrices, a selected basis completion, an infinite determinant, exterior-algebra semantics, or field-closure data.

Therefore such requests cannot be answered by the packet. They are refused at the ledger boundary before a linear-algebra consumer can treat the finite minor expansion as a host-level theorem.
\end{proof}
