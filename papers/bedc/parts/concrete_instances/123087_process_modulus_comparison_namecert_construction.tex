\begin{theorem}[ProcessModulusComparison NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:process-modulus-comparison-namecert-obligations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item endpoint-stream admission, requiring both displayed endpoints to be read through $\StreamNameUp$ windows before comparison data is consumed;
\item endpoint-modulus admission, requiring the two endpoint moduli and the comparison modulus in $M$ to be visible before any close or apart row is read;
\item dyadic tolerance exactness, requiring $D$ to record the requested precision and the rational threshold used by the comparison;
\item apartness-window exposure, requiring $A$ to remain a finite witness row rather than a total trichotomy branch;

\item transport and replay stability, requiring $H$ and $C$ to preserve the same endpoint, modulus, dyadic, rational, apartness, and real rows under accepted reads;

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for exact trichotomy, quotient representative equality, completed host points, or global order decisions, the displayed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
