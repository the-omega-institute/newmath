Following Alexandrov and Burago--Burago--Ivanov, the $\AlexandrovSpaceUp$ packet records curvature-bounded metric geometry as a finite BEDC comparison-triangle surface. It sits over the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the path-length ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-length-space-namecert}, the displayed segment witness of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-geodesic-metric-namecert}, the CAT(0) comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cat-zero-metric-namecert}, and the terminal scalar readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a local comparison-triangle carrier for curvature-bounded consumers; it does not assert global Toponogov comparison, selected geodesics, quotient path equality, smooth structure, local compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Alexandrov space carrier]
\label{def:alexandrov-space-carrier}
An $\AlexandrovSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(M,L,G,Z,R,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, exposing only the distance observations and endpoint identifiers inspected by the packet.
\item $L$ is the $\LengthSpaceUp$ row, recording the finite path-length ledger that makes comparison requests length-facing.
\item $G$ is the $\GeodesicMetricUp$ segment row, displaying the inspected geodesic windows used by the comparison triangle.
\item $Z$ is the $\CatZeroMetricUp$ or curvature-comparison row, carrying the signed comparison orientation and the finite triangle inequalities read by the packet.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ scalar-readback row for comparison distances, angle surrogates, and tolerance budgets; it is terminal and exports no real-completeness principle.
\item $T$ records the displayed triangle vertex, side, and comparison-side incidence data visible before any Alexandrov consumer may read the curvature bound.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, length, geodesic, comparison, scalar, triangle, continuation, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a local comparison request through the metric source, length ledger, geodesic segment rows, comparison ledger, scalar readback, and triangle incidence rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\AlexandrovSpaceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\LengthSpaceUp$, $\GeodesicMetricUp$, $\CatZeroMetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AlexandrovSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,L,G,Z,R,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host path space, a selected global geodesic, quotient equality of paths, smooth tangent cones, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Alexandrov space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:alexandrov-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AlexandrovSpaceUp$ carrier $A=(M,L,G,Z,R,T,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\AlexandrovSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the metric source, length-space ledger, geodesic segment windows, comparison ledger, scalar readback, displayed triangle incidence, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, provenance, and local naming. Every curvature-bounded read must pass through $M,L,G,Z,R,T,H,C,P,N$, and no obligation is stored outside those finite rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic route is precisely the metric source $M$, the path-length ledger $L$, the geodesic segment rows $G$, the comparison row $Z$, the scalar readback $R$, and the triangle-incidence row $T$.

The structural route is then forced. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same displayed rows, while $P$ and $N$ only record provenance and local naming for the accepted packet.

Since the carrier has no row for a host path space, selected global geodesic, quotient path equality, smooth tangent cone, open-cover compactness, or ambient real completeness, the local NameCert cannot export any of those objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Alexandrov comparison-triangle scope]
\label{thm:alexandrov-space-comparison-triangle-scope}
Every $\CatZeroMetricUp$, $\GeodesicMetricUp$, $\LengthSpaceUp$, or $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\AlexandrovSpaceUp$ carrier receives a comparison-triangle route only through
$$
\MetricUp,\quad \LengthSpaceUp,\quad \GeodesicMetricUp,\quad \CatZeroMetricUp,\quad \RealUp
$$
and the displayed rows $M,L,G,Z,R,T,H,C,P,N$. The handoff may expose finite curvature-bound comparison data, but it cannot be read as global confluence of geodesics, a selected-geodesic operator, quotient path equality, smooth manifold structure, local compactness, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the metric and length rows. They make endpoint distances and path-length requests visible before any comparison triangle can be inspected.

Next use the geodesic and comparison rows. The segment windows in $G$ and the comparison ledger in $Z$ bind the curvature-bounded read to displayed triangle data $T$ before scalar readback through $R$ is available.

Finally inspect the structural rows. The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite comparison route, so a downstream consumer cannot replace it by a host path object, selected geodesic, quotient equality, smooth structure, compactness theorem, or real-completeness principle.
\end{proof}
