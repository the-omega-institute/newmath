\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DynkinPiLambdaUp$}

Following Dynkin and Halmos, the $\DynkinPiLambdaUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface of the pi-lambda theorem as a handoff from displayed pi-system generators to a Dynkin-system closure route. It sits after the monotone-class carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-monotone-class-namecert}, the measurable-event rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-measure-namecert}, the generated-set boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-borel-set-namecert}, and the displayed membership discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-setlike-namecert}. The packet names generator admission, finite intersection closure, complement closure, disjoint-union closure, monotone-class handoff, measure-facing seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not import a host sigma-algebra completion, measure-extension theorem, quotient subset equality, or selected countable family.

\begin{definition}[Dynkin pi-lambda carrier]
\label{def:dynkin-pi-lambda-carrier}
A $\DynkinPiLambdaUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(G,I,C,U,M,S,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the displayed pi-system generator family, read through $\SetLikeUp$ rows and through the $\BorelSetUp$ generated-set boundary whenever the source is Borel-facing.
\item $I$ records finite intersection closure for generator rows that are jointly visible in $G$.
\item $C$ records complement closure relative to the named universe row exposed by the same displayed family.
\item $U$ records disjoint countable-union requests only through finite observed stages and their named continuation rows.
\item $M$ is the $\MonotoneClassUp$ handoff row that turns the displayed Dynkin closure route into the monotone-class closure surface already named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-monotone-class-namecert}.
\item $S$ is the $\MeasureUp$ seal row reached only after the generator, finite-intersection, complement, disjoint-union, and monotone-class rows have been exposed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $G,I,C,U,M,S$ and the structural rows.
\item $R$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite route
$$
G,I \longmapsto C,U \longmapsto M \longmapsto S,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DynkinPiLambdaUp$, $\MonotoneClassUp$, $\MeasureUp$, $\BorelSetUp$, $\SetLikeUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DynkinPiLambdaUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $G,I,C,U,M,S,H,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an ambient sigma-algebra, host pi-lambda theorem, selected countable family, measure extension, quotient equality of subsets, or external equality of events.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dynkin pi-lambda NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dynkin-pi-lambda-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:dynkin-pi-lambda-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\DynkinPiLambdaUp}$ surface consists exactly of generator admission through $G$, finite-intersection closure through $I$, complement closure through $C$, disjoint-union continuation through $U$, monotone-class handoff through $M$, measure-facing seal through $S$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $R$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Every accepted $\MeasureUp$, $\BorelSetUp$, or $\SetLikeUp$ consumer that reads a pi-lambda conclusion must expose those rows before it treats the generated family as measure-visible.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $D=(G,I,C,U,M,S,H,R,P,N)$. The generator row $G$ is the only entry point for displayed subset requests, and the finite-intersection row $I$ is the only pi-system closure coordinate.

Read the Dynkin closure rows next. Complement requests pass through $C$, while disjoint-union requests pass through the finite observed stages and continuation rows stored in $U$. These rows stay attached to the same displayed generator family.

The handoff and seal rows then determine every downstream consumer. The monotone-class row $M$ is the only route to the already named $\MonotoneClassUp$ surface, and the measure-facing row $S$ is reached only after that route has been exposed.

Finally replay $H,R,P,N$. Transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming preserve exactly the same finite packet, so no host sigma-algebra completion, selected countable family, measure-extension theorem, quotient subset equality, or external event equality can enter the certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dynkin pi-lambda MonotoneClass handoff]
\label{thm:dynkin-pi-lambda-monotone-class-handoff}
Every accepted $\DynkinPiLambdaUp$ packet exports the handoff route
$$
G,I,C,U \longmapsto M \longmapsto H,R,P,N
$$
to $\MonotoneClassUp$. A downstream consumer may cite monotone closure of the displayed generator family only after the pi-system intersection row, Dynkin complement row, disjoint-union continuation row, monotone-class handoff row, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local name row have all been exposed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the displayed generator row $G$. The pi-system condition is not a host family assertion; in the packet it is the finite-intersection row $I$ over the visible subset rows.

Pass through the Dynkin-system rows. Complement closure is read through $C$, and disjoint-union closure is read through the finite observed stages stored in $U$. Both rows keep the same displayed generator family and do not choose an external countable family.

Apply the monotone-class handoff row $M$. It is the only coordinate that lets a consumer enter the $\MonotoneClassUp$ surface, so the consumer receives the existing monotone closure route rather than an ambient pi-lambda theorem.

The structural rows $H,R,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same handoff. Therefore the route exports no sigma-algebra completion, measure extension, quotient subset equality, or selected countable chain.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dynkin pi-lambda measure seal nonescape]
\label{thm:dynkin-pi-lambda-measure-seal-nonescape}
For every accepted $\DynkinPiLambdaUp$ carrier

In particular, the seal cannot be used as a proof of host sigma-algebra completion, measure extension, quotient equality of subsets, selected countable families, external event equality, or an ambient pi-lambda theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

\end{proof}
