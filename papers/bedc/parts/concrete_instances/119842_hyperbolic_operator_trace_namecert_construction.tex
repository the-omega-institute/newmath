\begin{definition}[Hyperbolic operator trace carrier]
\label{def:hyperbolic-operator-trace-carrier}
A $\HyperbolicOperatorTraceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(D,O,U,L,B,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the finite disk-transport row recording the inspected Poincare-disk or hyperbolic disk windows and the reversible route between them.
\item $O$ is the operator row whose finite action is read only on the displayed disk-transport window.
\item $U$ is the reversible classifier-preservation row, recording the transport witness that preserves the relevant phase or inner-product classifier.
\item $L$ is the trace ledger row, recording the finite trace read, normalization, visible source ledger, and comparison budget.
\item $B$ is the boundary-refusal row excluding unlogged boundary collapse, hidden ambient Hilbert-space operators, global spectral measures, and unbounded functional calculus.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal readout row for the trace value or trace comparison exposed by $L$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for disk transport, operator, reversible classifier preservation, trace ledger, boundary refusal, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through disk transport, operator read, classifier preservation, trace ledger inspection, boundary refusal, Real-seal readout, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HyperbolicOperatorTraceUp$, $\HyperbolicOperatorCayleyUp$, $\HyperbolicPhaseHolonomyUp$, $\HyperbolicMetricUp$, $\UnitaryGroupUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HyperbolicOperatorTraceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The Lean-side follow-up for this carrier must provide \texttt{Nontrivial HyperbolicOperatorTraceUp} and \texttt{FieldFaithful HyperbolicOperatorTraceUp} TasteGate instances in \texttt{lean4/BEDC/Derived/HyperbolicOperatorTraceUp/TasteGate.lean}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hyperbolic operator trace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hyperbolic-operator-trace-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HyperbolicOperatorTraceUp$ carrier $T=(D,O,U,L,B,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HyperbolicOperatorTraceUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite disk-transport exposure, operator-row admission, reversible classifier-preservation exposure, trace-ledger readout, boundary-refusal exposure, $\RealUp$ seal readout, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. A trace read that omits $U$, $L$, or $B$ is not an accepted trace read of this packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hyperbolic-operator-trace-carrier}. The rows $D,O,U,L,B,R$ expose the disk transport, operator window, reversible classifier-preservation witness, trace ledger, boundary refusal, and Real-seal readout.

The trace row is not read in isolation. It is admitted only after the reversible transport row $U$ preserves the displayed classifier over the disk route $D$, and the ledger row $L$ records the finite trace value together with its source and comparison budget.

The boundary-refusal row $B$ blocks unlogged operator reads, boundary collapse, global spectral measures, and host Hilbert-space constructions. The Real-seal row $R$ is therefore a readout of the displayed trace ledger, not a substitute for the transport and refusal rows.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no additional coordinate stores an ambient operator trace, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
