\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UlmInvariantUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-ulm-invariant-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kaplansky and Fuchs, the $\UlmInvariantUp$ packet records the classical height-filtration invariant for countable reduced $p$-primary abelian groups as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits over the commutative operation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-abgroup-namecert}, the filtered scalar bookkeeping of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-module-namecert}, and the torsion-window route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pruefer-group-namecert}. The packet names finite height slices, quotient multiplicity rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming evidence; it does not assert the full Ulm classification theorem, choose representatives of quotient slices, construct an ambient Prüfer envelope, or compare external groups by an isomorphism selector.

\begin{definition}[Ulm invariant carrier]
\label{def:ulm-invariant-carrier}
An $\UlmInvariantUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(p,A,H,F,Q,M,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $p$ is the prime row naming the fixed $p$-primary torsion coordinate and its visible arithmetic ledger.
\item $A$ is the $\AbGroupUp$ source row, exposing the abelian operation, unit, inverse, commutativity, and classifier rows read by the invariant.
\item $H$ is the height-filtration row, listing the displayed finite height stages and the rowwise membership tests used at each stage.
\item $F$ is the successor-filtration row, recording the displayed passage from a height stage to its next $p$-multiple boundary.
\item $Q$ is the quotient-slice row, naming the finite comparison surface for height-stage elements modulo the next displayed boundary.
\item $M$ is the multiplicity row, recording the finite cardinal ledger of independent quotient-slice generators inspected by the packet.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the prime, abelian source, height filtration, successor filtration, quotient slices, multiplicities, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a torsion-height query through $p,A,H,F,Q,M,T,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UlmInvariantUp$, $\AbGroupUp$, $\ModuleUp$, $\PrueferGroupUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UlmInvariantUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for a full external abelian group classification, a selected basis of quotient slices, a hidden completion of the height filtration, or an ambient Prüfer-group embedding.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Ulm invariant height-filtration obligation]
\label{thm:ulm-invariant-height-filtration-obligation}
For every accepted $\UlmInvariantUp$ carrier
$$
U=(p,A,H,F,Q,M,T,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UlmInvariantUp}$ surface first reads the $\AbGroupUp$ source row $A$, then the displayed height-filtration row $H$, then the successor-filtration row $F$, and only then the quotient-slice and multiplicity rows $Q,M$. Thus every invariant-facing consumer receives height data as a finite $\BHist$ filtration route rather than as an ambient ordinal-indexed subgroup lattice or a choice-selected basis.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:ulm-invariant-carrier}. The only algebraic source row is $A$, and the only height-facing rows are $H,F,Q,M$.

The abelian source is read first. It supplies the operation, inverse, unit, commutativity, and classifier surface needed to speak about $p$-multiples and torsion-height comparisons.

The height filtration is then exposed through $H$ and $F$. Row $H$ lists the displayed stages, while $F$ records the successor boundary used to compare a height stage with its next $p$-multiple layer.

Finally $Q$ and $M$ read quotient slices and multiplicities only after those boundaries are visible. The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows $T,C,P,N$ preserve this order, so no hidden subgroup lattice or selected quotient basis enters the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Ulm invariant Pruefer dependency route]
\label{thm:ulm-invariant-pruefer-dependency-route}
Every Prüfer-facing or classification-facing consumer of an accepted $\UlmInvariantUp$ packet must retain the displayed route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-abgroup-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-module-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pruefer-group-namecert}. The $\PrueferGroupUp$ dependency is read only as a torsion-window comparison surface for $p$-primary height behavior; it is not a construction of an ambient divisible hull, a selected compatible root chain, or the full Ulm classification isomorphism.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the provenance row $P$ of \autoref{def:ulm-invariant-carrier}. It lists the abelian-group, module-style filtration, and Prüfer torsion-window surfaces as named dependencies of the same packet.

The abelian-group row supplies the operation and classifier. The module-style row supplies filtered scalar bookkeeping for the $p$-multiple boundary, and the Prüfer-group row supplies the finite torsion-window comparison surface used to keep height behavior visible.

The quotient-slice and multiplicity rows remain downstream of those dependencies. Because $T,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route, no consumer can replace it by a divisible hull, a compatible root selector, or an external classification isomorphism.
\end{proof}
