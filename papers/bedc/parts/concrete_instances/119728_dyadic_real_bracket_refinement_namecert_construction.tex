\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DyadicRealBracketRefinementUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-real-bracket-refinement-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive real approximation by nested rational intervals, the $\DyadicRealBracketRefinementUp$ packet records the finite bracket-refinement step by which a displayed real-name read is replaced by a smaller dyadic bracket before any completion-facing consumer reads a terminal Real seal. It sits after the Archimedean window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-archimedean-window-namecert}, the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a local radius-refinement certificate; it is not Real completeness, trichotomy, quotient stream equality, a selected limit, or open-cover compactness.

\begin{definition}[DyadicRealBracketRefinement carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-real-bracket-refinement-carrier}
A $\DyadicRealBracketRefinementUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(R,S,Q,D,E,E',\rho,\rho',I,J,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row whose finite observation is being bracketed.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row listing the finite requests through which the current bracket may inspect the real-name data.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger carrying endpoint arithmetic, midpoint arithmetic, containment checks, and the displayed halving comparison.
\item $E$ and $E'$ are the current and refined dyadic bracket rows; $E'$ keeps the same real-name source and is read only after $E,S,Q,D$ are visible.
\item $\rho$ and $\rho'$ are positive radius rows over $\PositiveRealUp$, with $\rho'$ displayed as the halved radius before the refined bracket may be exported.
\item $I$ is the inclusion row saying that the inspected regular readback stays inside the current bracket $E$.
\item $J$ is the refinement row saying that the refined bracket $E'$ stays inside $E$ and uses the halved radius $\rho'$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Real seal, StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, dyadic ledger, both brackets, both radius rows, inclusion, refinement, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a bracket request through finite window inspection, regular rational readback, dyadic arithmetic, radius halving, bracket inclusion, bracket refinement, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicRealBracketRefinementUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\PositiveRealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicRealBracketRefinementUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for Real completeness, trichotomy, quotient stream equality, selected limits, open-cover compactness, or an ambient host order oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[DyadicRealBracketRefinement NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-real-bracket-refinement-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicRealBracketRefinementUp$ carrier
$$
B=(R,S,Q,D,E,E',\rho,\rho',I,J,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicRealBracketRefinementUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite window exposure, regular readback exposure, dyadic arithmetic exposure, positive-radius exposure, inclusion of the inspected readback in $E$, halved-radius refinement from $\rho$ to $\rho'$, containment of $E'$ in $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-real-bracket-refinement-carrier}. The rows $R,S,Q,D,E,E',\rho,\rho',I,J,H,C,P,N$ are the only rows made public by the packet.

The window and readback obligations are the route through $S$ and $Q$. A bracket request first opens the finite StreamName window and then reads the regular rational data supplied by $\RegSeqRatUp$.

The dyadic and radius obligations are the route through $D,\rho,\rho'$. The ledger $D$ records the endpoint, midpoint, containment, and halving checks, while $\rho'$ is exposed as a positive halved radius before the refined bracket is exported.

The inclusion and refinement obligations are the rows $I$ and $J$. They keep the inspected readback inside $E$ and the refined bracket $E'$ inside $E$. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly this route, so none of the refused endpoints is introduced.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DyadicRealBracketRefinement radius halving]
\label{thm:dyadic-real-bracket-refinement-radius-halving}
For every accepted $\DyadicRealBracketRefinementUp$ carrier $B=(R,S,Q,D,E,E',\rho,\rho',I,J,H,C,P,N)$, any consumer that reads the refined bracket row $E'$ must first read the positive radius rows $\rho,\rho'$, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ halving ledger $D$, and the containment row $J$. Thus the radius-halving step is a finite dyadic ledger route, not a completeness argument or a selected limiting process.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the carrier definition. The refined bracket row $E'$ is not standalone; it is paired with the current bracket $E$, the two positive radius rows, the dyadic ledger, and the refinement row $J$.

Use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:dyadic-real-bracket-refinement-namecert-obligations}. They force radius exposure and dyadic arithmetic exposure before the refined bracket may be consumed.

Finally read $J$. This row is the displayed containment and halving handoff from $E$ to $E'$. Since no row stores a limit selector, quotient stream equality, trichotomy proof, or completeness theorem, the halving step remains a finite bracket-refinement route.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:dyadic-real-bracket-refinement-scope}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Because the carrier has no further coordinate, downstream Real or Completion consumers receive only the finite bracket-refinement packet.
