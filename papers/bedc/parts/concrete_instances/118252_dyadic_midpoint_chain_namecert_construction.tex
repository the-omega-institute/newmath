\begin{definition}[Dyadic midpoint chain carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-midpoint-chain-carrier}

$$

\item $Q$ is the midpoint row, recording the dyadic midpoint computed from the displayed endpoint pair.
\item $B$ is the retained-side ledger, listing the finite left-or-right midpoint decisions accepted by the refinement.
\item $O$ is the finite chain-order row, asserting that the retained intervals are read in the displayed midpoint-refinement order.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row exposing exactly the stages inspected by a downstream consumer.
\item $A$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same finite windows and midpoint decisions.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic midpoint chain NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-midpoint-chain-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicMidpointChainUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicMidpointChainUp}$ surface consists exactly of endpoint-pair admission through $L,R$, midpoint exposure through $Q$, retained-side ledger exactness through $B$, finite chain-order exposure through $O$, $\StreamNameUp$ window exposure through $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $A$, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-midpoint-chain-carrier}. The endpoint, midpoint, retained-side, chain-order, stream-window, readback, real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows are the whole packet.

Read the analytic rows in order. The endpoint rows $L,R$ supply the dyadic interval, the midpoint row $Q$ computes the displayed midpoint, and the retained-side ledger $B$ records which side survives each finite refinement.

The chain-order row $O$ then keeps those retained intervals in one displayed midpoint-refinement sequence. The window and readback rows $W,A$ expose only finite observations of that sequence, and the seal row $E$ is downstream of those reads.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same data. Since no carrier coordinate stores quotient stream equality, selected limits, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, Heine--Borel, or ambient real completeness, none of those principles belongs to the local obligation surface.
