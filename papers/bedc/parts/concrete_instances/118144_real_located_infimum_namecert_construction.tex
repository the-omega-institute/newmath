\begin{definition}[Real located infimum carrier]
\label{def:real-located-infimum-carrier}
A $\RealLocatedInfimumUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(B,D,R,M,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the finite $\RealUp$ lower-bound-candidate row, listing the inspected real-name lower surfaces and the comparison observations that certify them as lower-bound candidates in the current window.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ approximation-window row, recording dyadic precisions, lower-window widths, and the finite tolerance comparisons used to read the candidates.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, repacking the dyadic windows and candidate comparisons as regular rational observations before the terminal real seal is consumed.
\item $M$ is the consumed $\LocatedInfimumModulusUp$ row, supplying the displayed modulus discipline that turns lower-window requests into finite dyadic approximation obligations.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the lower-bound candidates, dyadic windows, regular readback, and located-infimum modulus have all been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $B,D,R,M,E,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for lower-bound-candidate reads, dyadic approximation reads, regular rational readback, modulus replay, real-seal access, and transported consumer return.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealLocatedInfimumUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\LocatedInfimumModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealLocatedInfimumUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exposes only the finite route $B,D,R,M,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for Dedekind cuts, trichotomy, quotient equality of real names, a host infimum operator, countable choice, selected witnesses from completeness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real located infimum NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-located-infimum-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealLocatedInfimumUp$ carrier
$$
J=(B,D,R,M,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RealLocatedInfimumUp}$ surface consists exactly of Real lower-bound candidate exposure through $B$, dyadic approximation-window exactness through $D$, regular rational readback through $R$, located-infimum modulus replay through $M$, terminal $\RealUp$ seal nonescape through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-located-infimum-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly the Real lower-bound-candidate row, dyadic approximation-window row, regular rational readback row, located-infimum modulus row, and terminal real seal row.

The lower-bound read is forced through $B,D,R,M$. Candidate observations begin as displayed $\RealUp$ lower surfaces, are inspected only at the dyadic windows stored in $D$, are repacked by $R$, and are governed by the modulus route $M$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no row stores Dedekind cuts, trichotomy, quotient equality, host infimum selection, countable choice, or ambient real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:real-located-infimum-l10-handoff}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The request first reads $B$ through the dyadic approximation windows in $D$. The row $R$ then turns those finite observations into regular rational readback, while $M$ supplies the located-infimum modulus discipline that keeps the lower-window request finite.

Finally $C,P,N$ replay, source, and name the same route before the consumer sees $E$. No step has a coordinate for Dedekind cuts, trichotomy, quotient equality, selected infima, or ambient completeness, so the L10 handoff remains inside the displayed packet.
\end{proof}
