\begin{proposition}[Vitali measure-Cauchy dependency route]
\label{prop:vitali-convergence-measure-cauchy-dependency-route}
Every $\VitaliConvergenceUp$ read that exposes a convergence seal first consumes the uniform-integrability route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-integrability-namecert} and the Cauchy sequential route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-sequential-criterion-namecert}. The read therefore keeps the measure and integral rows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vitali-convergence-namecert}, the budget row of \autoref{thm:vitali-convergence-uniform-integrability-dependency-route}, and the tolerance readback of \autoref{thm:vitali-convergence-cauchy-sequential-route} visible before any terminal $\RealUp$ or dominated-convergence handoff is exposed.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:vitali-convergence-carrier}. The rows $M,I,F,U,L,R,E,D,H,C,P,N$ are the only available inputs to a convergence-facing read.

Use \autoref{thm:vitali-convergence-uniform-integrability-dependency-route} to keep $U$ attached to the displayed measure and integral surface before any terminal seal is reached. Use \autoref{thm:vitali-convergence-cauchy-sequential-route} to route $L$ and $R$ through the Cauchy sequential criterion before $E$ is consumed.

The two routes share the same carrier rows, so their combination is still a finite Vitali dossier edge. It exports no measure completion, almost-everywhere quotient equality, selected subsequence, countable-choice schedule, or detached host convergence theorem.
\end{proof}
