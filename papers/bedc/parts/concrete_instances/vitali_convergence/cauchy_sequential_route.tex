\begin{theorem}[Vitali convergence Cauchy sequential route]
\label{thm:vitali-convergence-cauchy-sequential-route}
Any $\VitaliConvergenceUp$ consumer that reaches the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance row $R$ of an accepted carrier
$$
V=(M,I,F,U,L,R,E,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
must route the convergence-in-measure window $L$ through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-sequential-criterion-namecert} before consuming the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$. The route exposes the measure source $M$, integral row $I$, integrand family $F$, uniform-integrability budget $U$, convergence window $L$, rational tolerance row $R$, structural rows $H,C,P,N$, and only then the terminal real seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:vitali-convergence-carrier}. The convergence side is the displayed chain $L,R,E$, while $M,I,F,U$ supply the measure, integral, integrand-family, and uniform-integrability rows that make the window meaningful.

Before $E$ can be read as a real-facing endpoint, the tolerance row $R$ must be interpreted as a sequential Cauchy tolerance surface. The required neighboring packet is \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-sequential-criterion-namecert}, whose carrier records the finite stream-window, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance, and Real-seal handoff used by Real and Completion consumers.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve this route componentwise. Thus a Vitali consumer may reach the terminal $\RealUp$ seal only after the convergence-in-measure window has passed through the Cauchy sequential criterion surface; no hidden selected subsequence, countable-choice row, quotient equality, or ambient convergence theorem is introduced.
\end{proof}
