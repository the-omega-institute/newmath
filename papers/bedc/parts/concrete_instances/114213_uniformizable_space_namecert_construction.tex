It sits after the uniform entourage surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the metric-to-topology route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-topology-namecert}, the complete-uniform-space boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-complete-uniform-space-namecert}, and the directed entourage discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-quasi-uniform-space-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Uniformizable-space carrier]
\label{def:uniformizable-space-carrier}
A $\UniformizableSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(T,U,E,N,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ row whose neighbourhood and open-set reads are induced from the displayed uniformity data.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ row carrying the finite uniformity interface used by the packet.
\item $E$ is the entourage-base row, listing the displayed entourages and the finite intersection, diagonal, and composition requests that generate neighbourhood reads.
\item $N$ is the induced-neighbourhood evidence row, recording how each inspected point-neighbourhood request is read from an entourage section.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for topology, uniformity, entourage-base, induced-neighbourhood, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from topology requests through $U,E,N,H$ and back to the same topology row $T$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformizableSpaceUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\MetricTopologyUp$, $\CompleteUniformSpaceUp$, $\QuasiUniformSpaceUp$, $\SeparatedUniformSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformizableSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,U,E,N,H,C,P,L$. It has no coordinate for choice-based metrization, quotient topology, a host-selected uniform base, open-set extensionality, ambient separatedness, or completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniformizable-space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniformizable-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformizableSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
Z=(T,U,E,N,H,C,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UniformizableSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of five obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed topology, uniformity, entourage-base, induced-neighbourhood, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item entourage-base coverage, requiring every topology-facing neighbourhood read to be generated from a displayed entourage in $E$ over the uniformity row $U$;
\item induced-neighbourhood stability, requiring $N$ to preserve the same point and entourage section under componentwise $\hsame$ transport;
\item displayed $\Cont$ replay, requiring every topology request to return through the same $U,E,N,H$ rows before it is consumed as $T$;
\item non-escape, requiring $P,L$ to expose no choice-based metrization, quotient topology, host-selected base, open-set extensionality, ambient separatedness theorem, or completeness theorem.
\end{enumerate}
No Uniformizable-space obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The packet exposes exactly the topology row $T$, uniformity row $U$, entourage-base row $E$, induced-neighbourhood evidence $N$, transport row $H$, replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local name $L$.

Read the uniformity data before the topology data. The entourage-base row $E$ supplies the finite diagonal, intersection, and composition information used by neighbourhood requests, and $N$ records the pointwise section through which such a request reaches $T$.

Transport and replay preserve the same route. Componentwise $\hsame$ acts through $H$, while $\Cont$ replay through $C$ returns topology-facing reads to the same displayed entourage and neighbourhood rows.

Finally, provenance and local naming do not add external objects. Since the carrier has no coordinate for choice-based metrization, quotient topology, selected bases, open-set extensionality, separatedness, or completeness, the five listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniformizable-space entourage topology scope]
\label{thm:uniformizable-space-entourage-topology-scope}
For every accepted $\UniformizableSpaceUp$ carrier $Z=(T,U,E,N,H,C,P,L)$, every topology readback from $T$ factors through the displayed uniformity row $U$, entourage-base row $E$, induced-neighbourhood row $N$, transport row $H$, replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local name $L$. The readback is the topology induced by displayed entourages; it does not use choice-based metrization, quotient topology, host open-set extensionality, a selected base, ambient separatedness, or completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a topology readback request. The carrier definition makes $T$ readable only together with the uniformity, entourage-base, and induced-neighbourhood rows $U,E,N$.

Apply the NameCert obligations. Entourage-base coverage forces the neighbourhood request through a displayed entourage, and induced-neighbourhood stability keeps the point and entourage section fixed under transport.

Use the replay and provenance rows next. The row $C$ replays the same route back to $T$, while $P$ and $L$ source and name only the displayed packet.

The excluded topology principles are absent from the carrier. Therefore the topology readback factors through displayed entourages and cannot appeal to metrization by choice, quotient topology, host open-set extensionality, selected bases, separatedness, or completeness.
\end{proof}
