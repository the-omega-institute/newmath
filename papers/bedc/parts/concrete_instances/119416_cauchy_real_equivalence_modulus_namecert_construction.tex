\begin{definition}[Cauchy real equivalence modulus carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-real-equivalence-modulus-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ and $B$ are the two $\RegSeqRatUp$ representative rows whose stream names are compared by the packet.
\item $T$ is the shared dyadic tail-modulus row. For each requested dyadic tolerance, $T$ names a finite tail bound after which the two representatives are read in the same tolerance window.
\item $D_A$ and $D_B$ are the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance readback rows used to decode the two representative values at the requested dyadic precision.
\item $Q_A$ and $Q_B$ are the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window rows exposing only the retained prefixes and tail cells needed by $T,D_A,D_B$.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the pair of representatives through the shared tail-modulus row and dyadic readbacks to the real-name seal:

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyRealEquivalenceModulusUp}$ row.

The carrier exports a finite modulus witness for equality of the two real names. It has no coordinate for quotient equality of streams, a selected representative of an equivalence class, real trichotomy, a host Cauchy-completion object, or an ambient completeness principle.
\end{definition}

Finally inspect $P,N$. Provenance and local naming record only the displayed carrier rows, so a downstream Real consumer has no projection from which to read quotient stream equality, selected representatives, real trichotomy, host completion, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
