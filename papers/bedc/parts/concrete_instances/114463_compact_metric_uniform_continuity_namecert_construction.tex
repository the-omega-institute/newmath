\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompactMetricUniformContinuityUp$}

Following Bishop--Bridges and the Heine--Cantor finite-net proof, the $\CompactMetricUniformContinuityUp$ packet records the uniform-continuity theorem for a continuous map on a compact metric source as finite NameCert data. It sits over the compact finite-net source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the pointwise map rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the downstream modulus interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and the rational real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the finite route from local continuity radii and compact nets to a tolerance-only modulus; it does not import open-cover compactness, sequential compactness, host function equality, or a choice-selected center family.

\begin{definition}[Compact metric uniform continuity carrier]
\label{def:compact-metric-uniform-continuity-carrier}
A $\CompactMetricUniformContinuityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(K,F,M,Q,D,R,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net row, including the displayed center bundle, cover radii, and located membership witnesses for the source metric space.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row, carrying the graph read and the pointwise continuity modulus rows attached to the centers named by $K$.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ triangle-estimate ledger used to compare a point, a selected displayed center, and their images under the map row.
\item $Q$ is the finite $\RatUp$ lower-bound fold over the centerwise source radii supplied by $F$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for halving, positivity, lower-bound comparison, and final source-tolerance transport.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ seal boundary used only to read the rational and real comparison rows already displayed by $Q,D,M$.
\item $T$ is the resulting tolerance-only $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, continuous-map rows, metric estimates, rational fold, dyadic ledger, real seal, handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a target tolerance through $K,F,M,Q,D,R,H,P,N$ to the handoff row $T$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactMetricUniformContinuityUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactMetricUniformContinuityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover compactness, sequential compactness, choice-selected centers, quotient equality of functions, ambient Real completeness, or host equality of maps.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact metric uniform continuity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-metric-uniform-continuity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactMetricUniformContinuityUp$ carrier
$$
U=(K,F,M,Q,D,R,T,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactMetricUniformContinuityUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact finite-net exposure, continuous-map pointwise-modulus exposure, metric triangle-estimate replay, rational lower-bound fold exactness, dyadic tolerance transport, RegSeqRat and Real seal discipline, tolerance-only UniformModulus handoff, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every theorem-facing read must display $K,F,M,Q,D,R$ before it may consume the handoff row $T$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-metric-uniform-continuity-carrier}. Its visible rows are precisely the compact finite-net source, continuous-map pointwise rows, metric comparison ledger, rational fold, dyadic tolerance ledger, Real-facing seal, uniform-modulus handoff, and structural rows.

The finite-net and pointwise-modulus obligations are supplied by $K$ and $F$. The row $K$ displays the centers and coverage witnesses, while $F$ attaches a pointwise continuity radius to each displayed center.

The theorem-facing estimate is routed through $M,Q,D,R$. The metric ledger $M$ records the triangle comparisons, the rational fold $Q$ selects the displayed lower bound, the dyadic ledger $D$ transports positivity and halving data, and the seal row $R$ permits only the rational and Real reads already displayed by those rows.

Finally $T,H,C,P,N$ hand the result to the public certificate. Since no excluded compactness, choice, function-equality, or ambient-completeness coordinate occurs in the carrier, the stated rows exhaust the NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-net modulus route]
\label{thm:compact-metric-uniform-continuity-finite-net-modulus-route}

$$

\ContinuousMapUp
\longmapsto
\MetricUp
\longmapsto
\RatUp
\longmapsto
\DyadicRatCoreUp
\longmapsto
\RegSeqRatUp
\longmapsto

\longmapsto

$$
Concretely, the consumer reads the compact finite-net row $K$, the pointwise modulus row $F$, the metric triangle-estimate ledger $M$, the rational lower-bound fold $Q$, the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, and the RegSeqRat and Real seal row $R$ before it may use the modulus handoff $T$. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted packet $U=(K,F,M,Q,D,R,T,H,C,P,N)$. The compact source row $K$ provides the displayed centers and coverage witnesses, while the continuous-map row $F$ provides the pointwise radius rows over those same centers.

Use the metric triangle ledger $M$ to compare an arbitrary inspected point with its displayed center and then compare the corresponding image values. The rational fold $Q$ reads the finite family of source radii and records the lower bound used by the uniform tolerance.

The dyadic ledger $D$ transports the requested target tolerance through halving, positivity, and lower-bound comparisons. The RegSeqRat and Real seal row $R$ is then read only as a boundary for those already displayed numerical comparisons.

The handoff row $T$ is therefore a tolerance-only $\UniformModulusUp$ output. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same finite route, so no excluded compactness, choice, function-equality, or external map-equality object can enter the export.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact metric uniform-continuity explicit modulus scope]
\label{thm:compact-metric-uniform-continuity-explicit-modulus-scope}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-metric-uniform-continuity-carrier}. The compact row $K$ displays the finite centers and coverage witnesses, while the continuous-map row $F$ supplies only centerwise or pointwise continuity data. Without the finite-net row, those pointwise rows have no tolerance-only export surface.

Compose the compact, metric, and continuous-map rows. The point under inspection is first related to a displayed center by $K$ and the metric comparison ledger $M$; the row $F$ is then read at that displayed center. The rational lower-bound fold $Q$ and dyadic ledger $D$ turn the finite family of centerwise source radii into the radius budget used by the modulus handoff $T$.

The structural rows keep the export constructive. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$ preserves the compact witness, metric distance witness, radius budget, and handoff, while displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$ records the same route for each requested tolerance. Hence the exported uniform-continuity claim is the finite-net route $K,F,M,Q,D,R,T,H,C,P,N$, not open-cover compactness, a choice-selected subcover, or pointwise continuity detached from a displayed modulus.
\end{proof}
