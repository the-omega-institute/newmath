\label{thm:normalization-candidate-socket-metacic-candidate-dependency-route}

\end{theorem}

Project the carrier of \autoref{def:normalization-candidate-socket-carrier}. The candidate rows $K,D,E$, adequacy rows $A,B$, no-infinite-reduction row $I$, and frontier refusal row $F$ are all visible in the same finite socket.

They keep normalization-facing reads downstream of the candidate evidence rows and attach the socket to the MetaCIC critical-path consumer.

Finally read the normalization frontier and critical path chapters as the candidate-mediated parents. The accepted socket route therefore depends on those MetaCIC rows rather than floating as a closed-term or kernel-only surface.
\end{proof}
