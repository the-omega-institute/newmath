\begin{theorem}[FanFunctional scoped consumer route]
\label{thm:fanfunctional-scoped-consumer-route}
Every scoped consumer of an accepted $\FanFunctionalUp$ carrier
$$
\Phi=(C,F,\varepsilon,B,D,W,M,H,K,P,N)
$$
must read the finite $\CantorSpaceUp$ prefix row $C$, the $\FanTheoremUp$ finite-bar row $F$, the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-window row encoded by $B,D,W$, the $\UniformContinuityUp$ tolerance request $\varepsilon$, and the exported $\UniformModulusUp$ row $M$ on the same $\BHist$ route before it may use the modulus. The consumer surface is transported only by the displayed $\hsame$ rows $H$, replayed only by $K$, sourced only by $P$, and named only by $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:fan-functional-carrier-surface}. The rows $C,F,\varepsilon,B,D,W,M$ are the only analytic rows visible to a consumer, and $H,K,P,N$ are the only structural rows.

Apply the local obligation surface of \autoref{thm:fanfunctional-obligation-closure-scope}. Carrier admission and classifier stability keep the Cantor-prefix and finite-fan rows in scope before any compact or uniform consumer reads $M$.

Use the precedence theorem \autoref{thm:fanfunctional-obligation-bar-depth-coverage}. It requires the bar-depth and witness-window rows to be consumed before the modulus row is exported. Thus every scoped consumer sees the finite $\CantorSpaceUp$, $\FanTheoremUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\UniformContinuityUp$, and $\UniformModulusUp$ route in the displayed order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FanFunctional scoped choice nonescape]
\label{thm:fanfunctional-scoped-choice-nonescape}
The scoped $\FanFunctionalUp$ consumer route of \autoref{thm:fanfunctional-scoped-consumer-route} exports no selected branch, no host functional $2^{\NatUp}\to\RealUp$, no open-cover compactness proof, no countable-choice schedule, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness witness. Any accepted read of $M$ factors through the finite rows $C,F,\varepsilon,B,D,W,M,H,K,P,N$ and through the local $\NameCert_{\FanFunctionalUp}$ obligations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The modulus row $M$ is a coordinate of the displayed carrier, not a host functional. Projection of \autoref{def:fan-functional-carrier-surface} gives no coordinate for selected branches, open covers, choice schedules, or ambient real completeness.

Apply \autoref{thm:fanfunctional-obligation-modulus-nonescape}. Its nonescape clause already exhausts the handoff through $M,H,K,P,N$ and excludes the same host objects from the public surface.

The scoped consumer route adds only the destination names $\CompactMetricUp$, $\UniformContinuityUp$, and $\UniformModulusUp$. Since those destinations receive the same finite packet, they cannot receive any omitted host datum.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FanFunctional scoped finite-modulus export]
\label{thm:fanfunctional-scoped-finite-modulus-export}
For every accepted $\FanFunctionalUp$ packet, the row $M$ exported to $\UniformModulusUp$ is a finite modulus extracted from the displayed Cantor prefix, fan-bar, compact-window, and tolerance rows. Any $\CompactMetricUp$ or $\UniformContinuityUp$ consumer receives $M$ together with its route through $C,F,\varepsilon,B,D,W,H,K,P,N$, and receives no separate compactness, function-space, branch-selection, choice, or Real-completion principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read $C$ and $F$ first. They expose the finite Cantor-prefix and finite fan-bar surfaces that bound the branch-prefix window.

Next read $\varepsilon,B,D,W$. The tolerance request is attached to the finite witness windows, and the bar-depth row is already fixed before the witness rows are used.

Finally export $M$ through $H,K,P,N$. Componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and the local name preserve the same finite route, so a downstream compact or uniform-continuity consumer receives only the finite modulus packet.
\end{proof}
