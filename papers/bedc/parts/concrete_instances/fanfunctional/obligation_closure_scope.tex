\begin{theorem}[FanFunctional local obligation surface]
\label{thm:fanfunctional-obligation-closure-scope}
For the carrier
$$
\Phi=(C,F,\varepsilon,B,D,W,M,H,K,P,N)
$$
of \autoref{def:fan-functional-carrier-surface}, the rows
$C,F,\varepsilon,B,D,W,M,H,K,P,N$ jointly form the complete local obligation
surface for $\FanFunctionalUp$. Carrier admission is read from the displayed
$\BHist$ packet, classifier stability is read from the Cantor-prefix and
finite-fan rows, and the remaining public obligations are exactly bar-depth
exactness, witness-window coverage, uniform-modulus handoff, and nonescape.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The coordinates $C,F,\varepsilon,B,D,W,M$
name the finite Cantor-prefix, finite fan, tolerance, branch-window,
bar-depth, witness-window, and modulus rows.

The structural coordinates $H,K,P,N$ then close the local reading surface:
$H$ supplies componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $K$ supplies displayed $\Cont$
replay, $P$ records package provenance, and $N$ records the local NameCert
name. No further carrier coordinate is available to the chapter.
\end{proof}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.FanfunctionalUp.FanFunctionalLocalObligationSurface}

\begin{theorem}[FanFunctional bar-depth and witness-window precedence]
\label{thm:fanfunctional-obligation-bar-depth-coverage}
Every $\FanFunctionalUp$ obligation read consumes the finite bar-depth row $D$
and the witness-window row $W$ before any modulus row $M$ is exported to a
$\UniformModulusUp$, $\UniformContinuityUp$, or $\CompactMetricUp$ consumer.
Thus finite bar-depth exactness and witness-window coverage are prerequisites
for modulus consumption, not consequences of an already accepted modulus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the finite rows in carrier order. The branch window $B$ is cut from the
Cantor-prefix row $C$ by the finite fan row $F$, and $D$ certifies the displayed
bar depth over that finite window.

Only after this depth row is fixed can $W$ attach the pointwise tolerance
witnesses that feed the modulus row $M$. Consequently a consumer of $M$ sees
the already displayed $D,W$ surface and cannot use the modulus row to choose or
repair the preceding bar-depth and witness-window data.
\end{proof}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.FanfunctionalUp.FanFunctionalBarDepthWitnessWindowPrecedence}

\begin{theorem}[FanFunctional modulus handoff and nonescape]
\label{thm:fanfunctional-obligation-modulus-nonescape}
The uniform-modulus handoff $M,H,K,P,N$ of
\autoref{def:fan-functional-carrier-surface} and
\autoref{thm:fan-functional-namecert-obligations} excludes host functionals,
selected branches, open-cover compactness, countable choice, and Real
completeness from the $\FanFunctionalUp$ public surface. Every accepted read
factors through the finite carrier rows, componentwise transport, displayed
continuation replay, package provenance, and the local NameCert row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The handoff row $M$ is produced from the finite windows $B,D,W$ and is
transported only by the displayed structural rows $H,K,P,N$.

Since the carrier contains no coordinate for a host functional
$2^{\NatUp}\to\RealUp$, selected branch, open-cover compactness proof,
countable-choice schedule, or $\RealUp$ completeness witness, there is no
projection through which such data can enter a public read. Nonescape is
therefore a finite-coordinate statement about the displayed carrier.
\end{proof}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.FanfunctionalUp.FanFunctionalModulusHandoffNonescape}

\begin{theorem}[FanFunctional Real and RegSeqRat scope]
\label{thm:fanfunctional-real-regseqrat-scope}
Every $\FanFunctionalUp$ modulus read consumed by a $\RealUp$ or $\RegSeqRatUp$ route must first pass through $\CantorSpaceUp$ finite prefixes, $\FanTheoremUp$ decidable finite-bar depth, $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net data, $\UniformContinuityUp$ local tolerance rows, and the extracted $\UniformModulusUp$ row. The exported route contains no host function space, selected branch, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier surface of \autoref{def:fan-functional-carrier-surface}. The visible route begins with the finite Cantor-prefix row $C$, the finite fan row $F$, the tolerance row $\varepsilon$, the branch-window row $B$, the bar-depth row $D$, the witness-window row $W$, and the extracted modulus row $M$.

Apply the local obligation surface of \autoref{thm:fanfunctional-obligation-closure-scope}. It fixes carrier admission and classifier stability before any downstream consumer can read a modulus value.

Now use the bar-depth coverage theorem and the modulus nonescape theorem. The row order is
$$
C,F,\varepsilon,B,D,W,M,H,K,P,N .
$$
Thus a Real or regular-rational consumer receives a finite modulus route through Cantor prefixes, decidable fan depth, compact-metric finite nets, uniform-continuity tolerance rows, and the extracted UniformModulus row.

Because no carrier coordinate stores a host function space, selected branch, open-cover compactness proof, countable-choice schedule, or $\RealUp$ completeness witness, those objects cannot be exported by the scoped consumer route.
\end{proof}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.FanfunctionalUp.FanFunctionalRealRegSeqRatScope}
