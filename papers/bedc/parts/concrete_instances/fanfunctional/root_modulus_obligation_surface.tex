\begin{definition}[FanFunctional finite-modulus carrier]
\label{def:fan-functional-finite-modulus-carrier}
A $\FanFunctionalUp$ finite-modulus carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
\Theta=(C,F,K,U,M,W,D,H,R,P,N)
$$
whose rows are as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\CantorSpaceUp$ finite-prefix source row, including the bounded prefix windows inspected by the packet.
\item $F$ is the $\FanTheoremUp$ finite-bar row that supplies the displayed depth ledger for those windows.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net row consumed by compact-metric downstream routes.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformContinuityUp$ tolerance request row.
\item $M$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ extracted modulus row.
\item $W$ is the finite witness-window family linking each inspected prefix to the modulus read.
\item $D$ is the depth ledger that records the bounded fan depth used before $M$ may be exported.
\item $H,R,P,N$ are the componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert_{\FanFunctionalUp}$ rows.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these finite rows and has no coordinate for a host functional, selected branch, open-cover compactness object, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FanFunctional NameCert obligation surface]
\label{thm:fan-functional-namecert-obligation-surface}
For an accepted $\FanFunctionalUp$ finite-modulus carrier $\Theta=(C,F,K,U,M,W,D,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FanFunctionalUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $\Theta$, $\CantorSpaceUp$ prefix exposure through $C$, $\FanTheoremUp$ finite-bar exactness through $F,D$, $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net handoff through $K$, $\UniformContinuityUp$ tolerance admission through $U$, $\UniformModulusUp$ extraction through $M,W$, and structural transport, replay, provenance, and naming through $H,R,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:fan-functional-finite-modulus-carrier}. Its displayed rows are exactly the Cantor prefix source, fan bar and depth ledger, compact finite-net handoff, uniform-continuity tolerance request, extracted uniform-modulus row, witness windows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row.

The NameCert obligations are therefore the admission and exactness checks for those rows. Prefix exposure reads $C$; finite-bar exactness reads $F,D$; the compact-metric handoff reads $K$; the tolerance request reads $U$; and modulus extraction reads $M,W$.

The rows $H,R,P,N$ account for componentwise transport, displayed consumer replay, package provenance, and local naming. Since no host functional, selected branch, open-cover compactness object, choice schedule, or Real-completeness row appears in $\Theta$, the displayed list exhausts the local obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FanFunctional Real uniform-modulus handoff]
\label{thm:fan-functional-real-uniform-modulus-handoff}
Every Real-facing uniform-modulus consumer of an accepted $\FanFunctionalUp$ finite-modulus carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\CantorSpaceUp \longrightarrow \FanTheoremUp,\CompactMetricUp,\UniformContinuityUp \longrightarrow \UniformModulusUp
$$
as represented by the rows $C,F,K,U,M,W,D,H,R,P,N$. The consumer receives only finite prefix windows, a finite fan depth, a compact finite-net row, a uniform-continuity tolerance row, witness windows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it receives no host functional, selected branch, open-cover compactness proof, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness assertion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted finite-modulus carrier. The only Cantor-facing coordinate is $C$, and the only fan-depth coordinates are $F,D$, so a consumer must read finite prefixes and the finite-bar ledger before any modulus row is visible.

Next the compact and uniform-continuity rows are read. Row $K$ supplies the compact finite-net handoff, while row $U$ supplies the tolerance request that the modulus must answer.

Finally the modulus export reads $M$ through the witness windows $W$ and the structural rows $H,R,P,N$. These rows preserve the same finite packet, so a Real-facing consumer cannot bypass the displayed route through a host functional, selected branch, open-cover compactness proof, choice schedule, or Real-completeness principle.
\end{proof}
