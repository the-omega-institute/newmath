\begin{definition}[InfoGenerationTrace carrier]
\label{def:info-generation-trace-carrier}
An $\InfoGenerationTraceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(S,V,L,Q,R,J,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source-carrier row, naming the displayed $\mathcal I_n$ layer or tower source from which the trace is read.
\item $V$ is the visible-record row, recording the generated record exposed to the current consumer.
\item $L$ is the ledger row, carrying provenance, gap, audit, and classifier-boundary entries that license $V$.
\item $Q$ is the residue row, recording the remaining source fiber over $V$ rather than collapsing it into the visible record.
\item $R$ is the compatible refinement row, naming any readback from a later visible surface to the present one.
\item $J$ is the visible-refinement join row, displaying how $V,Q,R$ are consumed together by a downstream generated-information read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, visible record, ledger, residue, refinement, join, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$$
S\longrightarrow V\longrightarrow L\longrightarrow Q\longrightarrow R\longrightarrow J .
$$
\item $P$ records provenance over $\InfoGenerationTraceUp$, $\RealityConstrainedTowerPacketUp$, $\ReflectionGapAuditUp$, $\ClassifierBoundaryUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\InfoGenerationTraceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted traces only through $S,V,L,Q,R,J,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a bare information object, hidden source equality, final physical truth, tower-external information, host semantic completeness, or an unrecorded generation step.
\end{definition}

\label{thm:info-generation-trace-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:info-generation-trace-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the rows $S,V,L,Q,R,J,H,C,P,N$, so source admission and visible-record exposure are finite displayed data.

The licensing obligation is supplied by $L$. It records the provenance, gap, audit, and classifier-boundary entries through which the visible row is admitted, so no bare information object can enter the trace.

The non-exhaustion obligation is supplied by $Q$ and $R$. The residue row keeps the source fiber visible, while the refinement row records any compatible readback from a later surface to the current visible record.

Finally, $J,H,C,P,N$ join, transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier contains no row for hidden source equality, final physical truth, tower-external information, host semantic completeness, or unrecorded generation, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
