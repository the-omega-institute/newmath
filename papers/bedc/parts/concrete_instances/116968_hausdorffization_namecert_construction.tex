\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HausdorffizationUp$}

Following Bourbaki and Bishop--Bridges on separated reflection and constructive completion, the $\HausdorffizationUp$ packet records the finite non-quotient boundary that turns a pseudometric name into the separated metric route consumed by completion. It sits between the separated-reflection surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separated-reflection-namecert}, the pseudometric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pseudometric-namecert}, the separated metric consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedmetric-namecert}, and the completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}. Its readback is exposed through the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the constructive separated-reflection boundary; it does not assert host quotient equality, representative selection, ambient Hausdorff completion, trichotomy, countable choice, or a completed Real object.

\begin{definition}[Hausdorffization carrier]
\label{def:hausdorffization-carrier}
A $\HausdorffizationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
H=(P,S,M,C,W,R,E,T,K,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the consumed $\PseudometricUp$ source row, including the displayed zero-distance witness surface and finite distance comparisons.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparatedReflectionUp$ boundary row that records which source names are identified by displayed zero-distance evidence.
\item $M$ is the $\SeparatedMetricUp$ target row available only after the separated-reflection boundary has been read.
\item $C$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ consumer handoff row, recording that completion can read only the separated metric route.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which pseudometric and separated-reflection observations are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the finite distance and zero-distance windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for the displayed distance readbacks.
\item $T$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $P,S,M,C,W,R,E,K,G,N$.
\item $K$ is displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same boundary route.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HausdorffizationUp$, $\SeparatedReflectionUp$, $\PseudometricUp$, $\SeparatedMetricUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HausdorffizationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $P,S,M,C,W,R,E,T,K,G,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient-set equality, selected representatives, ambient Hausdorff reflection, host metric completion, trichotomy, countable choice, or external Real completeness.
\end{definition}
