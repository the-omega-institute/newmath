\begin{theorem}[Hausdorffization NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hausdorffization-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HausdorffizationUp$ carrier
$$
H=(P,S,M,C,W,R,E,T,K,G,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\HausdorffizationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the pseudometric source, separated-reflection boundary, separated metric target row, metric-completion consumer handoff, finite StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, terminal Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming; zero-distance exactness requiring every separated identification to pass through $P,S,W,R,E$; completion-boundary exactness requiring every completion read to pass through $M,C$ after the separated reflection; and nonescape from the named rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hausdorffization-carrier}. Its rows are precisely $P,S,M,C,W,R,E,T,K,G,N$, so carrier admission supplies the full local inventory.

The zero-distance route begins in the pseudometric row $P$. A separated identification is admitted only when the boundary row $S$ displays the corresponding finite evidence, and that evidence is inspected through the windows $W$, read back by $R$, and sealed by $E$.

The separated metric and completion handoff are downstream. The row $M$ is exposed only after the separated-reflection boundary, and the completion row $C$ can consume only that separated metric route.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are the rows $T,K,G,N$. Because no further carrier coordinate exists, the listed obligations exhaust the $\NameCert_{\HausdorffizationUp}$ surface.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:hausdorffization-separated-reflection}

It may not read host quotient equality, a selected representative, an ambient Hausdorff reflection object, a quotient completion, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The separated-reflection boundary $S$ records how that evidence is admitted by the BEDC packet. It is a finite boundary row, not a quotient set or a selector for representatives.

Finally $W,R,E,T,K,G,N$ give the window, readback, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming surface for the same packet. The absence of quotient, selector, trichotomy, choice, and ambient-completeness coordinates forces every consumer to remain on the displayed route.
