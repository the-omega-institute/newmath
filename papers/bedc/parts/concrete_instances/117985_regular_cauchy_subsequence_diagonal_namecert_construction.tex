\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy subsequence diagonal carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-subsequence-diagonal-carrier}

$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the bounded sequence source row, read as a $\BoundedRealSequenceUp$ packet and exposing only the inspected sequence indices, finite bounds, and local source route.
\item $S$ is the subsequence schedule row, listing the finite initial segment of the increasing index schedule used by the diagonal read.
\item $J$ is the diagonal window selector, assigning each requested regular-Cauchy tolerance to the finite source window and subsequence index already displayed in $S$.
\item $W$ is the family of $\StreamNameUp$ windows inspected along the displayed schedule and selector rows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the selected windows.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the bounded source, schedule, selector, windows, and regular readback have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the bounded source, schedule, selector, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, continuation row, provenance row, and local naming row.

$$

The classifier compares accepted packets exactly through $B,S,J,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for countable choice, a host subsequence selector, quotient equality of streams, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy subsequence diagonal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-subsequence-diagonal-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchySubsequenceDiagonalUp$ carrier $D=(B,S,J,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchySubsequenceDiagonalUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the ten displayed rows; bounded-source visibility through $B$; finite subsequence scheduling through $S$; diagonal window selection through $J$; StreamName window exposure through $W$; RegSeqRat readback through $R$; Real seal handoff through $E$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$; package provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-subsequence-diagonal-carrier}. The bounded source row $B$ is the only source-side input, so every sequence read starts from the finite $\BoundedRealSequenceUp$ surface.

Read the schedule and selector next. The row $S$ lists the finite inspected subsequence indices, while $J$ ties each requested diagonal tolerance to one of the displayed windows. No row can request an unlisted tail or an externally selected subsequence.

The analytic handoff then passes through $W,R,E$. StreamName windows are inspected before the regular rational readback, and the Real seal is downstream of that readback.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for countable choice, a host subsequence selector, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy subsequence diagonal Real handoff]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-subsequence-diagonal-real-handoff}

The handoff exports no countable choice, selected host subsequence, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The readback row $R$ is therefore the only regular rational route to the terminal seal. A $\RealUp$ consumer reaches $E$ only after this displayed readback has been named.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route under transport and replay. Because no excluded selector, quotient, trichotomy, choice, or completeness row occurs in the carrier, every Real-facing read remains inside the displayed diagonal packet.
