\begin{definition}[Rational cut carrier]
\label{def:rational-cut-carrier}
A $\RationalCutUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(L,U,D,S,R,E,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the lower rational row, recording finite rational witnesses below the inspected boundary.
\item $U$ is the upper rational row, recording finite rational witnesses above the inspected boundary.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row that names the dyadic slack and comparison radius used by the located request.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which a consumer inspects the lower and upper rational observations.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row reached after the rational rows and the dyadic tolerance row are visible.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $L,U,D,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the lower row, upper row, dyadic tolerance, stream window, readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from each located cut request through the same finite rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RationalCutUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RationalCutUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through those displayed rows and their transports. It has no coordinate for quotient cut equality, trichotomy, selected real representatives, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Rational cut NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:rational-cut-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RationalCutUp$ carrier $C=(L,U,D,S,R,E,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RationalCutUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the lower and upper $\RatUp$ rows, dyadic tolerance exposure, finite $\StreamNameUp$ inspection, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every located cut consumer must read $L,U,D,S$ before it can read $R$ or $E$, and the packet exports no Dedekind construction of $\RealUp$, quotient cut equality, selected representative, trichotomy, countable-choice selector, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:rational-cut-carrier}. Its analytic rows are exactly the lower rational row, upper rational row, dyadic tolerance row, finite stream window, regular rational readback, and terminal Real seal.

Read the finite route in order. A located cut request first exposes the rational rows $L,U$, then the dyadic tolerance row $D$, and then the finite observation window $S$. Only after those rows are visible can the regular rational readback $R$ and the Real seal $E$ be consumed.

The structural rows $H,K,P,N$ do not add a completed cut. They transport, replay, source, and name the same finite rows. Since the carrier contains no quotient equality, selected representative, trichotomy, choice, or completeness coordinate, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Rational cut L10 handoff boundary]
\label{thm:rational-cut-lten-handoff-boundary}

$$
\RatUp,\quad \DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad \StreamNameUp,\quad \RegSeqRatUp,\quad \RealUp,\quad \BHist,\quad \hsame,\quad \Cont,\quad \Pkg,\quad \NameCert .

\begin{proof}
