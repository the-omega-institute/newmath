\begin{definition}[Bishop separated real completion carrier]
\label{def:bishop-separated-real-completion-carrier}
A $\BishopSeparatedRealCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(S,R,M,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-observation row, exposing only the stream windows requested by the completion consumer.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational row read from those stream windows.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ completion row, recording the completion-facing metric handoff before separation is applied.
\item $Q$ is the $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$ separation row, restricting comparison to displayed indistinguishability data instead of quotient equality.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $S,R,M,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream, regularity, metric-completion, separation, real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Real or Completion request through $S,R,M,Q,E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopSeparatedRealCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\SeparatedMetricReflectionUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopSeparatedRealCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,R,M,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, host real equality, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

The completion-facing part of the route reads finite observations through $S$, regular rational data through $R$, and the metric completion handoff through $M$.
