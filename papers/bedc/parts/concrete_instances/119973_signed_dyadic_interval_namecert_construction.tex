\begin{definition}[Signed dyadic interval carrier]
\label{def:signed-dyadic-interval-carrier}
A $\SignedDyadicIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(L,U,\sigma_L,\sigma_U,O,W,I,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ and $U$ are lower and upper endpoint rows accepted by $\DyadicRatCoreUp$.
\item $\sigma_L$ and $\sigma_U$ are the signed-endpoint rows, recording the visible sign data attached to the two dyadic endpoints.
\item $O$ is the lower-before-upper order ledger, read only through dyadic comparison rows over $L$ and $U$.
\item $W$ is the finite window row exported to $\StreamNameUp$ consumers.
\item $I$ is the interval-arithmetic handoff row, exposing the same endpoints and containment comparisons through $\DyadicIntervalArithmeticUp$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for consumers that turn the displayed interval into a regular rational enclosure.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the endpoint, sign, window, interval-arithmetic, and regular-readback rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the endpoint, sign, order, window, containment, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SignedDyadicIntervalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DyadicIntervalArithmeticUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SignedDyadicIntervalUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate is exactly the assertion that these rows form one signed dyadic interval packet. It has no coordinate for quotient real equality, host interval equality, trichotomy, selected representatives, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Signed dyadic interval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:signed-dyadic-interval-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SignedDyadicIntervalUp$ carrier $S=(L,U,\sigma_L,\sigma_U,O,W,I,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SignedDyadicIntervalUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the endpoint rows, signed-endpoint rows, endpoint-order ledger, StreamName window, interval-arithmetic handoff, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item endpoint sign exactness, requiring $\sigma_L$ and $\sigma_U$ to remain attached to the displayed dyadic endpoint rows $L$ and $U$;
\item order stability, requiring $O$ to transport along $\hsame$ and displayed $\Cont$ replay without replacing dyadic comparison by host real order;
\item containment handoff, requiring $I$ to expose every interval containment row through $\DyadicIntervalArithmeticUp$ using the same displayed endpoints;
\item completion boundary, requiring $W,R,E$ to route the finite window through $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ in that order;
\item ledger non-escape, excluding quotient real equality, host interval equality, trichotomy, selected representatives, countable choice, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:signed-dyadic-interval-carrier}. The projection exposes exactly the two $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoints, the signed-endpoint rows, the dyadic order ledger, the StreamName window, the interval-arithmetic handoff, the RegSeqRat readback, the Real seal, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Endpoint sign exactness and order stability are read from $\sigma_L,\sigma_U,O,H,C$. Componentwise transport and displayed replay move the named endpoint histories together with their sign and order ledgers, so no host real order or quotient equality row is introduced.

Containment handoff is the row $I$. It points to the existing $\DyadicIntervalArithmeticUp$ enclosure surface and uses the same displayed endpoint rows $L,U$ rather than a detached interval object.

Finally the completion boundary is the route $W \longmapsto R \longmapsto E$. A completion-facing consumer receives finite windows, regular rational readback, and the terminal real seal only in that order. The excluded ambient principles are absent from the carrier inventory.
\end{proof}
