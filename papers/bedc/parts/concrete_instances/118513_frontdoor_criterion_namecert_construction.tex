\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FrontdoorCriterionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-frontdoor-criterion-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Pearl's front-door criterion, the $\FrontdoorCriterionUp$ packet records the finite mediation certificate used to identify an intervention-facing query through observed mediator rows. It sits after the intervention-row packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-causal-intervention-namecert}, the normalized probability surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert}, and the carried random-variable rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-randomvar-namecert}. The packet names the front-door surface as BEDC data; it does not claim full do-calculus, back-door completeness, counterfactual semantics, or probability-space completion.

\begin{definition}[Front-door criterion carrier]
\label{def:frontdoor-criterion-carrier}
A $\FrontdoorCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(T,M,Y,I,B,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the treatment $\RandomVarUp$ row whose intervention-facing value is read by the query.
\item $M$ is the mediator $\RandomVarUp$ row through which the treatment effect must pass.
\item $Y$ is the outcome $\RandomVarUp$ row whose response is queried.
\item $I$ is the $\CausalInterventionUp$ row that names the finite intervention on $T$.
\item $B$ is the observed-blocking ledger, recording that the mediator row blocks every displayed directed treatment-to-outcome path admitted by the packet.
\item $A$ is the adjustment ledger, recording the observed rows used to read the treatment-to-mediator and mediator-to-outcome probability factors.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the treatment, mediator, outcome, intervention, blocking, adjustment, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
T,I \longmapsto M \longmapsto Y
$$
together with the blocking and adjustment ledgers.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FrontdoorCriterionUp$, $\CausalInterventionUp$, $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\RandomVarUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FrontdoorCriterionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host structural causal model, selected counterfactual universe, unbounded graph search, hidden back-door completeness proof, or completed probability space.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Front-door criterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:frontdoor-criterion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FrontdoorCriterionUp$ carrier $F=(T,M,Y,I,B,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FrontdoorCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed treatment, mediator, outcome, intervention, observed-blocking, adjustment, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Any consumer that reads a front-door claim must display the treatment-to-mediator route, the mediator-to-outcome route, the observed blocking ledger, the adjustment ledger, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local name before the claim is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:frontdoor-criterion-carrier}. Its rows expose $T,M,Y,I,B,A,H,C,P,N$ and no other coordinate.

Read the causal route first. The intervention row $I$ is attached to the treatment row $T$, the mediator row $M$ is the only displayed handoff between treatment and outcome, and the outcome row $Y$ is consumed only after that handoff is visible.

Read the two ledgers next. The blocking row $B$ records the displayed path-blocking surface, while the adjustment row $A$ records the observed probability-facing rows used by the two front-door factors. These rows are supported by the $\ProbSpaceUp$ and $\RandomVarUp$ siblings rather than by a host model.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for full do-calculus, back-door completeness, counterfactual semantics, or probability-space completion, those objects are not local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Front-door criterion causal handoff]
\label{thm:frontdoor-criterion-causal-handoff}
Every intervention-facing consumer of an accepted $\FrontdoorCriterionUp$ packet reaches its causal query only after reading the finite route
$$
T,I,B,A,H,C,P,N \longmapsto M \longmapsto Y .
$$
The handoff is a displayed mediator route from $\CausalInterventionUp$ through $\ProbSpaceUp$ and $\RandomVarUp$ rows. It exports no host causal graph, full do-calculus derivation, back-door completeness theorem, selected counterfactual, or completed probability space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the intervention row $I$ and treatment row $T$. The carrier supplies no intervention-facing conclusion until the blocking ledger $B$ and adjustment ledger $A$ have been inspected with the same transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The mediator row $M$ is then the only displayed bridge to the outcome row $Y$. Thus the consumer can reach the outcome-facing query only through the finite mediator handoff and not by projecting a hidden causal graph or back-door theorem.

Because the probability-facing rows are cited through $\ProbSpaceUp$ and $\RandomVarUp$, the route stays inside existing NameCert siblings. The absence of any counterfactual or probability-completion coordinate gives the claimed nonescape boundary.
\end{proof}
