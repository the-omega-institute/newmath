\item $F$ is the $\OpenPhysicalFitUp$ row exposing finite trace, observed signature, model-generated signature, classifier, refutation row, open-continuation row, ledger, replay, and local fit naming.

The classifier compares packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for bare equation authority, record-only empirical adequacy, unchecked host-model bridges, hidden theorem preservation, final physical truth, or a law read without an exposed falsification surface.
