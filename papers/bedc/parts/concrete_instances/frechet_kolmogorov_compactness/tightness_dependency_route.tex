\begin{theorem}[Frechet--Kolmogorov tightness route]
\label{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-tightness-route}
Every $\FrechetKolmogorovCompactnessUp$ compactness read must display the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite-net row, the $\UniformEquicontinuityModulusUp$ control row, the $\CompleteMetricUp$ boundary row, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational readback row, the $\RealUp$ seal, and the local tightness and boundedness rows before it exports compactness of the family. The route is a finite $\BHist/\Cont/\Pkg$ ledger over compact metric, total-bounded, equicontinuity, completion, regular-rational, real, tightness, boundedness, transport, replay, provenance, and local $\NameCert$ data; it is not an ambient compactness oracle, a selected subsequence, a selected subcover, or quotient completion equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier of \autoref{def:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-carrier}. Its source rows already expose $\CompactMetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\UniformEquicontinuityModulusUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ as the visible compactness route.

Now combine the total-bounded handoff of \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-total-bounded-handoff} with the sibling route of \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-orphan-route}. Those results force the finite-net, equicontinuity, and completion-facing rows to remain visible before any compactness export is read.

The tightness and boundedness rows are therefore local dependency rows in the same ledger. They do not introduce a hidden subsequence choice, a hidden subcover, or an equality in a quotient completion, because the structural rows transport and replay only the displayed finite packet.
\end{proof}
