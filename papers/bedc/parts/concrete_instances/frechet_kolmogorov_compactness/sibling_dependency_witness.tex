Read the total-bounded surface next. The row $T$ supplies the finite-net budget consumed by the criterion, and the theorem \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-total-bounded-handoff} forces that budget to pass through the same compact metric source before any readback rows are inspected.

The sibling route of \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-sibling-route} binds the Arzela--Ascoli finite-modulus chapter to the same finite compactness route, so the equicontinuity row cannot be replaced by an ambient function-space compactness theorem.

Finally the local transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows $H,A,P,N$ keep those sibling dependencies on the same packet. Thus the Frechet--Kolmogorov chapter records a dependency lattice over compact metric, total boundedness, and Arzela--Ascoli finite modulus rather than a kernel-only compactness assertion.
\end{proof}
