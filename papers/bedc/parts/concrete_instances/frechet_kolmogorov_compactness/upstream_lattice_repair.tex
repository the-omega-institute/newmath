\begin{theorem}[Frechet--Kolmogorov upstream dependency lattice]
\label{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-upstream-lattice-repair}
Every $\FrechetKolmogorovCompactnessUp$ compactness read must pass through the compact metric, totally bounded, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, Arzela--Ascoli finite-modulus, and Lp-tail localization rows before any compactness conclusion is exported. The exported route is therefore a finite dependency lattice over the displayed carrier rows, not an ambient compactness oracle, selected subsequence, selected subcover, quotient completion equality, or host completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the local carrier and the compact metric total-bounded dependency of \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compact-metric-total-bounded-dependency}. It forces the compact metric source and total-bounded finite-net budget to appear before later StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real readbacks are inspected.

Next read the Arzela--Ascoli side through \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-arzela-ascoli-dependency-route} and \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-arzela-ascoli-compact-handoff}. Those routes keep finite-modulus and finite-window function-family data downstream of the compact metric and total-bounded rows.

Now apply the completion and rational-readback routes, \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-metric-completion-route} and \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-sibling-route}. They keep the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ rows attached to the same displayed finite lattice.

Finally use the Lp-tail routes, \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-lp-tail-route} and \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-lp-tail-localization}. These place tail localization after compact-metric, total-bounded, completion-facing, and rational-readback rows. The stated lattice follows because no cited carrier row supplies a hidden compactness principle or selected limiting object.
\end{proof}
