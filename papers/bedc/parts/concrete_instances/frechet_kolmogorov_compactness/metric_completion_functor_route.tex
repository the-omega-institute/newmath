\begin{theorem}[Frechet--Kolmogorov metric-completion functor route]
\label{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-metric-completion-route}
Every $\MetricCompletionFunctorUp$ consumer of an accepted $\FrechetKolmogorovCompactnessUp$ carrier must first read the compact-metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the total-bounded finite-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the uniform-equicontinuity modulus of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-equicontinuity-modulus-namecert}, the complete-metric boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the StreamName window rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the RegSeqRat readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The route exports a displayed compactness-to-completion handoff only; it exports no quotient completion equality, selected compact limit, selected subsequence, selected subcover, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-carrier}. Its compactness-facing source rows are the compact metric row, the total-bounded finite-net row, the uniform-modulus row, and the completion boundary row.

Apply the sibling route of \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-sibling-route}. It keeps the compact metric, total-bounded, uniform-equicontinuity, RegSeqRat, and Real-seal rows visible as one dependency lattice before any compactness conclusion is exported.

Finally apply the completion boundary of \autoref{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-compactness-completion-boundary}. A $\MetricCompletionFunctorUp$ consumer can only repackage that displayed boundary through StreamName and RegSeqRat readbacks, so quotient completion equality, selected compact limits, and ambient completeness are outside the exported surface.
\end{proof}
