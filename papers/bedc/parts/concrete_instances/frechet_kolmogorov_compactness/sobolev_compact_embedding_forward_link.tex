\begin{theorem}[Frechet-Kolmogorov Sobolev compact-embedding forward link]
\label{thm:frechet-kolmogorov-sobolev-compact-embedding-forward-link}
Every Sobolev-family consumer that reaches the $\FrechetKolmogorovCompactnessUp$ packet through a compact-embedding route must first display the $\SobolevUp$ source rows, the $\CompactEmbeddingUp$ handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-embedding-namecert}, and the compact-metric and total-bounded rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}. The Frechet--Kolmogorov packet may then consume the finite StreamName, RegSeqRat, completion-boundary, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert rows exposed by those siblings, but it does not replace the compact-embedding handoff with selected subsequences, open-cover compactness, quotient function equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the compact-embedding side. The $\CompactEmbeddingUp$ chapter exposes a finite handoff from Sobolev source windows into compact and complete target rows, so a Sobolev-family read arrives with displayed source, target, readback, transport, replay, provenance, and naming data.

Use the Frechet--Kolmogorov compactness carrier and its sibling route. The rows $M,T,E,Q,S,R,H,A,P,N$ already require compact-metric source exposure, total-bounded finite nets, completion boundary, finite-window readback, rational comparison, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before compactness-facing data are exported.

The forward link is therefore a dependency route, not a new compactness principle. The Frechet--Kolmogorov consumer reads the Sobolev and compact-embedding siblings as displayed rows and then reuses the compact-metric and total-bounded surfaces, leaving selected subsequences, open-cover compactness, quotient function equality, and ambient Real completeness outside the packet.
\end{proof}
