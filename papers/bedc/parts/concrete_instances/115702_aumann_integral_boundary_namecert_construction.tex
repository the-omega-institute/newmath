Following Aumann on integrals of set-valued maps, the $\AumannIntegralBoundaryUp$ packet records the finite NameCert boundary at which a set-valued integral can be read without importing a host measurable-selection principle. It sits after the measurable ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-measure-namecert}, the scalar readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-integral-namecert}, the convex-value carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, the observable random input surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-randomvar-namecert}, and the target normed carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-namecert}. The packet names a finite boundary for downstream probability and optimization readers: it exposes displayed selection rows and their integrals, but it does not claim a global measurable-selection theorem, quotient equality of selections, countable choice, or ambient measure completion.

\begin{definition}[Aumann integral boundary carrier]
\label{def:aumann-integral-boundary-carrier}
An $\AumannIntegralBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(M,R,X,V,G,S,K,L,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MeasureUp$ source row whose measurable-event ledger bounds every domain-side read.
\item $R$ is the $\RandomVarUp$ observation row carrying the displayed random input, measurable preimage tests, and observable classifier.
\item $X$ is the source measurable-space row on which the set-valued map is inspected.
\item $V$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ value row, recording nonempty convex fibres and their finite membership tests.
\item $G$ is the measurable graph row, displaying the finite graph tests that relate source observations in $X,R$ to value candidates in $V$.
\item $S$ is the displayed finite selection ledger. Its entries are finite rows
$$
(s_i,\gamma_i,\mu_i,\iota_i)_{i\in I}
$$
where each $s_i$ is a named selection candidate, $\gamma_i$ is its graph-admission witness through $G$, $\mu_i$ is its measurable-read witness through $M,R,X$, and $\iota_i$ is its integral-read token.
\item $K$ is the convex-value stability row, recording that every displayed selection value remains inside the named convex fibre of $V$ and that finite convex combinations preserve the same value boundary.
\item $L$ is the integral readback row, reducing each $\iota_i$ to the finite scalar or vector integral data already accepted by $\IntegralUp$.
\item $B$ is the $\BanachUp$ target row in which the displayed integral endpoints and norm comparisons are read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,R,X,V,G,S,K,L,B$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
M,R,X,V,G\longmapsto S\longmapsto K,L\longmapsto B .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\AumannIntegralBoundaryUp$, $\MeasureUp$, $\IntegralUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$, $\RandomVarUp$, $\BanachUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AumannIntegralBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed measure, random-variable, source, convex-value, measurable-graph, finite-selection, convex-stability, integral-readback, Banach-target, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Aumann integral boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:aumann-integral-boundary-obligations}
For every accepted $\AumannIntegralBoundaryUp$ carrier
$$
A=(M,R,X,V,G,S,K,L,B,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\AumannIntegralBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the measure row, random-variable row, source row, convex-value row, measurable graph row, displayed finite selection ledger, convex-value stability row, integral readback row, Banach target row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming through $N$. Every public set-valued integral read must pass through $G,S,K,L,B$ before it names an endpoint or boundary verdict.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:aumann-integral-boundary-carrier}. The dependency rows $M,R,X,V$ provide the measure, random-variable, source, and convex-value data on which the boundary is interpreted.

The theorem-facing rows are $G,S,K,L,B$. The graph row $G$ admits only displayed graph tests, the finite ledger $S$ lists the selection candidates that are actually inspected, the convex row $K$ keeps their values inside the displayed fibres, the integral row $L$ reads their accepted integrals, and the Banach row $B$ names the endpoint and norm comparisons.

Transport and replay do not add analytic content. The rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name exactly the same finite route, so the local certificate has no projection to a global measurable-selection theorem, quotient equality of selections, countable choice, or ambient measure completion.
\end{proof}
