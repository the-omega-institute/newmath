\begin{definition}[Finite cover star-refinement modulus carrier]
\label{def:finite-cover-star-refinement-modulus-carrier}
A $\FiniteCoverStarRefinementModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(K,f,U,V,L,R,D,M,H,C,P,N)

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row whose compact evidence is read as finite cover data.
\item $f$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row carrying the local modulus data attached to the same source.
\item $U$ is the finite cover row and $V$ is the star-refinement row over that cover.
\item $L$ is the $\FiniteLebesgueNumberUp$ or $\CompactCoverLebesgueLedgerUp$ lower-bound row for the star refinement.
\item $R$ is the positive $\RatUp$ tolerance row requested by the target consumer.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger comparing the requested tolerance with the finite lower-bound data.
\item $M$ is the resulting $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row, available only after $K,f,U,V,L,R,D$ have been displayed.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverStarRefinementModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, host-function equality, quotient covers, countable choice, or ambient completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite cover star-refinement modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cover-star-refinement-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteCoverStarRefinementModulusUp$ carrier $S=(K,f,U,V,L,R,D,M,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverStarRefinementModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact metric admission, continuous-map local-modulus admission, finite cover exposure, star-refinement exactness, finite Lebesgue-number or compact-cover ledger admission, positive rational tolerance admission, dyadic tolerance comparison, uniform-modulus output exactness, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming for the rows of \autoref{def:finite-cover-star-refinement-modulus-carrier}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic rows are $K,f,U,V,L,R,D,M$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The compact source $K$ and continuous-map row $f$ are read before the finite cover is used. The cover row $U$ and star-refinement row $V$ then give the finite local geometry inspected by the modulus construction.

The lower-bound row $L$, rational tolerance row $R$, and dyadic comparison row $D$ determine the tolerance-only output row $M$. This order prevents the uniform-modulus consumer from replacing the finite star-refinement data by an open-cover compactness theorem or selected subcover.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same packet. Since no forbidden principle is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
