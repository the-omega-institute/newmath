It sits after the metric source row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Uniformly located set carrier]
\label{def:uniformly-located-set-carrier}
A $\UniformlyLocatedSetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(M,L,D,W,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row, including the named point carrier and displayed metric comparison data.
\item $L$ is the located-set row or locator-modulus row, recording the finite witness that answers distance-to-the-subset requests uniformly over the displayed metric source.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row that bounds each locator request by a dyadic precision and slack comparison.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which the locator answer is observed.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row induced by the displayed window and tolerance rows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the distance readback reached through $D,W,Q$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for metric source rows, locator rows, dyadic tolerances, finite windows, regular readbacks, Real seals, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a distance-to-set request through $M,L,D,W,Q,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformlyLocatedSetUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformlyLocatedSetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,L,D,W,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host subset membership, selected nearest points, countable choice over approximants, quotient completion, or ambient Real-completeness principles.
\end{definition}
