It sits over the ambient affine carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-affinespace-namecert}, the vector-space dependence rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, the convex membership surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, the finite face and witness rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polytope-namecert}, the finite support route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-caratheodory-convex-hull-namecert}, and the finite-intersection route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-helly-theorem-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Radon partition carrier]
\label{def:radon-partition-carrier}
A $\RadonPartitionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(A,V,F,C,P,I,H,T,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the finite-dimensional $\AffineSpaceUp$ ambient row through which all point histories and affine combinations are read;
\item $V$ is the $\VecSpaceUp$ translation row that records the vector-dependence read underlying the affine relation;
\item $F$ is the finite support family, listing the displayed point histories whose affine dependence is being split;
\item $C$ is the signed coefficient ledger, recording positive-side and negative-side coefficient rows, zero-sum balance, and equal normalized total mass;
\item $P$ is the partition row, assigning each displayed support history to the positive side, the negative side, or a zero-coefficient boundary row;
\item $I$ is the convex-hull intersection witness, stored as finite replay data over the two convex combinations and read through $\ConvexSetUp$, $\PolytopeUp$, $\CaratheodoryConvexHullUp$, and $\HellyTheoremUp$ sibling routes;
\item $H$ is the componentwise $\hsame$ transport for ambient, vector, support, coefficient, partition, and intersection rows;
\item $T$ is the displayed $\Cont$ route that replays the affine-dependence split without adding host convexity data;
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RadonPartitionUp$, $\AffineSpaceUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$, $\PolytopeUp$, $\CaratheodoryConvexHullUp$, $\HellyTheoremUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and local $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RadonPartitionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exposes no ambient theorem that every large finite point family admits such a split, no quotient equality of convex hulls, no selected host point, and no standard bridge row.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Radon partition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:radon-partition-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:radon-partition-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\RadonPartitionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the affine ambient row, vector-dependence row, finite support family, signed coefficient ledger, partition row, convex-hull intersection witness, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming row. Classifier stability is tested only by replaying the same support family, coefficient balance, side assignment, and intersection witness through transported affine, vector, convex, polytope, Caratheodory, and Helly rows. Ledger exactness says that every accepted Radon-partition read exposes precisely $A,V,F,C,P,I,H,T,Q,N$ and no external convex-geometry datum.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Radon affine-dependence route]
\label{thm:radon-partition-affine-dependence-route}
Every accepted $\RadonPartitionUp$ packet routes a finite convex-geometry consumer through
$$
\AffineSpaceUp,\ \VecSpaceUp,\ \ConvexSetUp,\ \PolytopeUp,\ \CaratheodoryConvexHullUp,\ \HellyTheoremUp .
$$
The affine and vector rows fix the displayed dependence source, the finite support and signed coefficient rows record the zero-sum balance, the partition row separates the two convex sides, and the intersection witness is readable only after the convex-set, polytope, Caratheodory, and Helly sibling routes have been exposed. A consumer that omits any of those rows is outside the accepted $\RadonPartitionUp$ carrier.
\end{theorem}
