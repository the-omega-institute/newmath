\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FanBarInductionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-fan-bar-induction-namecert}

Following Brouwer and Heyting, the $\FanBarInductionUp$ packet records the finite induction surface available for bars on the binary fan before any open-cover compactness, branch selection, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient Real-completeness claim is read. It sits after the finite fan certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert}, the Cantor-space prefix carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cantorspace-namecert}, the barred-spread replay of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-brouwer-bar-induction-namecert}, and the Bool/List prefix carriers of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bool-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-list-namecert}. The packet names a finite bar-induction handoff for downstream compact fan-modulus and total-bounded routes; it is not an unrestricted induction principle over arbitrary spreads.

\begin{definition}[Fan bar induction carrier]
\label{def:fan-bar-induction-carrier}
A $\FanBarInductionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(T,B,I,W,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\CantorSpaceUp$ binary-prefix tree row, retaining exactly the visible $\BoolUp$ endpoint rows and $\ListUp$ prefix spines under inspection;
\item $B$ is the $\FanTheoremUp$ finite-bar row, recording decidable bar decisions and the unary depth ledger for the retained fan window;
\item $I$ is the $\BrouwerBarInductionUp$ local backwards-replay row, restricted to displayed children of $T$ and barred prefixes of $B$;
\item $W$ is the finite witness window listing the prefixes where the replay reads bar data, successor rows, and terminal decisions;
\item $Q$ is the prefix-closure ledger, asserting that every prefix of a displayed witness prefix is again a displayed $\ListUp$ row with its $\BoolUp$ endpoint data still visible;
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the tree, bar, induction replay, witness-window, prefix-closure, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for root inspection, left and right successor inspection, prefix restriction, and induction replay;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FanBarInductionUp$, $\FanTheoremUp$, $\CantorSpaceUp$, $\BrouwerBarInductionUp$, $\BoolUp$, $\ListUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FanBarInductionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these finite rows. It has no coordinate for selected infinite branches, open-cover compactness, quotient stream equality, countable choice, arbitrary spread induction, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Fan bar induction NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:fan-bar-induction-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FanBarInductionUp$ carrier $F=(T,B,I,W,Q,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FanBarInductionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the binary-prefix tree, finite-bar row, local backwards-replay row, witness window, prefix-closure ledger, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local name; finite fan-bar exactness requiring $B$ and $W$ to expose the same retained prefixes; induction-replay exactness requiring $I$ to use only displayed successors of $T$; prefix-closure exactness requiring $Q$ to keep every inspected prefix inside the Bool/List carrier surface; and non-escape of open-cover compactness, branch selection, quotient stream equality, countable choice, arbitrary spread induction, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:fan-bar-induction-carrier}. Carrier admission exposes exactly $T,B,I,W,Q,H,C,P,N$, so every certificate read starts with the finite binary-prefix, bar, replay, window, closure, transport, continuation, provenance, and naming rows.

Read finite fan-bar exactness through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert}. The row $B$ supplies the decidable bar decisions and depth ledger, while $W$ lists the prefixes actually inspected.

Read the backwards-replay row through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-brouwer-bar-induction-namecert}. Because $I$ is restricted to the displayed children of $T$, the replay remains local to the retained Cantor-space prefix tree and does not select an infinite branch.

Finally use the Bool/List carrier route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bool-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-list-namecert} for $Q$. Prefix closure keeps endpoint and spine data inside finite carriers, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route without adding compactness, quotient, choice, arbitrary induction, or Real-completeness coordinates.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Fan bar induction finite prefix closure]
\label{thm:fan-bar-induction-finite-prefix-closure}
Let $F=(T,B,I,W,Q,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\FanBarInductionUp$ carrier. Every accepted replay of the fan-bar induction row is prefix-closed in the displayed window: if a replay reads a barred prefix from $W$, then each earlier prefix of that same $\ListUp$ spine is read through $Q$, with its $\BoolUp$ endpoint rows and its $\CantorSpaceUp$ tree coordinate still visible. Thus a downstream compact fan-modulus or total-bounded consumer may consume a finite prefix-closed induction handoff, but it receives no selected branch, open-cover compactness theorem, quotient stream equality, countable-choice functional, arbitrary spread-induction rule, or ambient Real-completeness claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:fan-bar-induction-namecert-obligations}. They require every induction replay to expose $T,B,I,W,Q$ before the structural rows are consumed.

The window row $W$ identifies the displayed barred prefixes. The prefix-closure ledger $Q$ then reads each earlier prefix through the same $\ListUp$ spine and the same $\BoolUp$ endpoint discipline, so the replay never leaves the finite Cantor-space tree row $T$.

Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve those displayed prefix rows, while $P$ and $N$ source and name the same finite packet. Since the accepted carrier has no coordinate for branch selection, open covers, quotient streams, countable choice, arbitrary spread induction, or Real completeness, the finite prefix-closed handoff exports none of those objects.
\end{proof}
