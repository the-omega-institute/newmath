\begin{theorem}[Membership and apartness boundary]
\label{thm:bishop-located-subset-membership-apartness}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedSubsetUp$ carrier
$$
L=(M,A,R,D,E,G,H,C,P,N),
$$
each public membership or apartness read factors through the displayed $\LocatedSetUp$ row $A$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row $D$, the $\RealUp$ seal row $E$, and the located-apartness radius row $G$. A negative membership read exposes a positive witness radius only as a finite $\LocatedMetricUp$ distance comparison, not as a host complement predicate, a trichotomy decision, or a selected nearest point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-subset-carrier}. The rows $M,A,R,D,E,G,H,C,P,N$ are the full inventory available to the local certificate.

The membership side is supplied by $A$ and read through $R,D,E$. Thus an inspected real name reaches the subset row only after its regular-rational window, dyadic tolerance ledger, and terminal real seal have been displayed.

The apartness side is the row $G$. Its witness radius is positive because $D$ supplies the dyadic positivity and refinement ledger, and it is a subset-facing witness because $M$ supplies the located-metric distance comparison against the admitted rows in $A$.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same finite route. Since no row stores a host complement, trichotomy split, nearest-point selector, quotient subset, or ambient completeness theorem, the stated boundary exhausts the public membership and apartness reads.
\end{proof}
