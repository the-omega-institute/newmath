Following Weibel and McCleary on spectral-sequence comparison theorems, the $\SpectralSequenceComparisonUp$ packet records the finite NameCert surface by which displayed page data in $\SpectralSeqUp$ can be compared before any downstream derived-functor spectral-sequence consumer reads it. It sits after the spectral-sequence page carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-spectralseq-namecert}, the homology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homology-namecert}, the abelian-category carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-abeliancat-namecert}, the derived-functor spine of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-derivedfunctor-namecert}, and the composite-derived-functor packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-grothendieck-spectral-sequence-namecert}. The packet names only the displayed source pages, comparison rows, page-stability ledger, filtration boundary, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data; it does not claim convergence, exactness beyond the recorded rows, or a host-level comparison theorem.

\begin{definition}[Spectral sequence comparison carrier]
\label{def:spectral-sequence-comparison-carrier}
A $\SpectralSequenceComparisonUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(S_0,S_1,H_0,A,D,F,L,T,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S_0$ and $S_1$ are the two $\SpectralSeqUp$ page carriers being compared, including their page objects, differential rows, successor-page routes, and filtration readback boundaries.
\item $H_0$ is the $\HomologyUp$ readback row for the pagewise cycles, boundaries, and induced homology rows visible in the comparison.
\item $A$ is the $\AbelianCatUp$ source row in which the page objects, differentials, and homology reads are interpreted.
\item $D$ is the $\DerivedFunctorUp$ consumer row that records how the compared pages feed derived-functor data.
\item $F$ is the pagewise comparison row, listing the displayed maps or rowwise $\hsame$ transports used between corresponding page entries.
\item $L$ is the finite page-stability ledger. It records the range of pages, bidegrees, differential compatibility checks, and filtration-boundary agreements that are actually inspected.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S_0,S_1,H_0,A,D,F,L$ and the structural rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the source page rows through the comparison row and the page-stability ledger.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SpectralSequenceComparisonUp$, $\SpectralSeqUp$, $\HomologyUp$, $\AbelianCatUp$, $\DerivedFunctorUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SpectralSequenceComparisonUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S_0,S_1,H_0,A,D,F,L,T,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for global convergence, an ambient equality of spectral sequences, quotient equality of pages, selected resolutions, exact-couple data not displayed in $L$, or a host comparison theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral sequence comparison NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:spectral-sequence-comparison-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SpectralSequenceComparisonUp$ carrier $C=(S_0,S_1,H_0,A,D,F,L,T,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SpectralSequenceComparisonUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for those eleven rows; page exposure through the two $\SpectralSeqUp$ rows; homology readback through $H_0$; abelian source exposure through $A$; derived-functor consumer exposure through $D$; displayed pagewise comparison through $F$; page-stability and filtration-boundary exposure through $L$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $T$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through $R$; $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:spectral-sequence-comparison-carrier}. Its only spectral-sequence inputs are the displayed page rows $S_0$ and $S_1$, and its only ambient source rows are $H_0,A,D$.

Read the pagewise comparison next. The row $F$ lists the inspected maps or transports between corresponding page entries, while $L$ records the finite page range, bidegree range, differential compatibility checks, and filtration-boundary agreements used by the comparison.

Finally use $T,R,P,N$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve exactly the listed rows. Since no coordinate stores a global convergence theorem, ambient equality of spectral sequences, quotient page equality, selected resolution, or host comparison theorem, none of those data is a NameCert obligation of the packet.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
