\begin{definition}[Finite modulus compaction carrier]\label{def:finite-modulus-compaction-carrier}
A $\FiniteModulusCompactionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(D,S,R,E,M,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger. It stores only the dyadic radii, comparison rows, and finite tolerance requests that the compaction read may inspect.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window family. Each row is a displayed observation window attached to one tolerance request in $D$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row reached from the windows in $S$ and the dyadic ledger $D$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, consumed only after $D,S,R$ have been replayed.
\item $M$ is the finite modulus compaction row. It records a finite association from displayed dyadic requests to displayed windows and readbacks, with no hidden cover, subsequence, or completed real-line coordinate.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,S,R,E,M$ and for the replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a modulus request through
$$
D,S,R,E,M \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteModulusCompactionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteModulusCompactionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only by transporting the displayed dyadic ledger, finite stream windows, regular readback, Real seal, finite modulus compaction row, replay, provenance, and local name to a common finite surface. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, Heine--Borel for arbitrary subsets, sequential compactness without choice, quotient-stream equality, selected representatives, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite modulus compaction NameCert obligations]\label{thm:finite-modulus-compaction-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteModulusCompactionUp$ carrier
$F=(D,S,R,E,M,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteModulusCompactionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the dyadic tolerance ledger, finite stream-window family, regular rational readback, Real seal, finite modulus compaction row, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local name;
\item modulus readability, requiring every modulus request to pass through a displayed row of $D$ and the matching finite window row in $S$;
\item regular readback, requiring the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ and $\RealUp$ seal row $E$ to be exposed after the finite window family has been displayed;
\item compaction-row stability, requiring $M$ to transport componentwise with $D,S,R,E$ under the $\hsame$ data recorded in $H$;
\item replay exactness, requiring every public consumer route to replay $D,S,R,E,M$ through $C$ before provenance and local naming are read;
\item refusal rows excluding open-cover compactness, Heine--Borel for arbitrary subsets, sequential compactness without choice, quotient-stream equality, selected representatives, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-modulus-compaction-carrier}. The displayed analytic rows are exactly $D,S,R,E,M$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name that finite packet.

Modulus readability is finite. A tolerance request first reads the dyadic ledger $D$, then the corresponding stream-window row in $S$, and then the regular readback $R$ and Real seal $E$ attached to that displayed window.

The compaction row $M$ does not add a compactness principle. It records only the finite association already visible in $D,S,R,E$, and componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$ preserves those same rows.

Finally $C$ replays the route before $P$ and $N$ are inspected. Since none of the refused objects is a carrier coordinate, no NameCert obligation can export open-cover compactness, Heine--Borel for arbitrary subsets, sequential compactness without choice, quotient-stream equality, selected representatives, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

The Cauchy-name and uniform-modulus sibling routes receive this finite package as displayed input data. They do not turn the package into a host cover argument, a chosen subsequence, a quotient stream, or a completed real-line principle.
