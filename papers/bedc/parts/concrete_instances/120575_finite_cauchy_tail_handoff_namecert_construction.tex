\begin{definition}[Finite Cauchy tail handoff carrier]
\label{def:finite-cauchy-tail-handoff-carrier}
A $\FiniteCauchyTailHandoffUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(R,W,D,M,S,Z,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-rational readback row whose tail is being offered to a Real-facing consumer;
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite request window over which the tail is inspected;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger carrying the displayed radius, precision, and comparison budget for the window;
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyModulusArithmeticUp$ row computing the tail bound from the request window and dyadic ledger;
\item $S$ is the $\RegularCauchyTailSelectorUp$ row selecting the finite tail segment licensed by $M$;
\item $Z$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal that may consume the displayed tail handoff;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transports for the regular readback, window, dyadic ledger, modulus arithmetic, tail selector, real seal, provenance, and local name;
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ routes from $R,W,D$ through $M,S$ to $Z$ and from the structural rows back to the local certificate;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\CauchyModulusArithmeticUp$, $\RegularCauchyTailSelectorUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCauchyTailHandoffUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, a host-selected Cauchy tail, a selected real limit, trichotomy, countable choice, or an ambient completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite Cauchy tail handoff NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cauchy-tail-handoff-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteCauchyTailHandoffUp$ carrier $T=(R,W,D,M,S,Z,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCauchyTailHandoffUp}$ surface consists exactly of four public obligation rows: regular-rational tail source through $R$, finite window and dyadic budget through $W,D$, computed modulus and tail selector through $M,S$, and terminal real sealing through $Z$. The structural rows $H,K,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and locally name only those displayed rows, so no other tail witness or Real-facing evidence is available.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the finite packet of \autoref{def:finite-cauchy-tail-handoff-carrier}. Its analytic rows are exactly the regular readback $R$, window $W$, dyadic ledger $D$, modulus arithmetic row $M$, tail selector $S$, and real seal $Z$.

The first obligation exposes the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tail source. The second obligation fixes the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budget. The third obligation computes the displayed tail bound and selector through $M,S$. The fourth obligation seals the resulting handoff through $Z$.

The rows $H,K,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same packet. Since the carrier omits quotient equality, selected limits, choice schedules, trichotomy, and ambient completeness, the local certificate has no other public obligation row.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite Cauchy tail handoff Real seal route]
\label{thm:finite-cauchy-tail-handoff-real-seal-route}
Every Real-facing read of an accepted $\FiniteCauchyTailHandoffUp$ carrier reaches the $\RealUp$ seal $Z$ only after passing through the displayed $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$, the common $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger $D$, the $\CauchyModulusArithmeticUp$ row $M$, the $\RegularCauchyTailSelectorUp$ row $S$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $K$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCauchyTailHandoffUp}$ row $N$. \end{theorem}

The route to $Z$ must therefore pass through $R,W,D,M,S$ first. The $\Cont$ row $K$ records that order, while the $\hsame$ row $H$ can only transport the displayed components of the same carrier.

Finally, $P$ and $N$ provide provenance and local naming for that carrier.
