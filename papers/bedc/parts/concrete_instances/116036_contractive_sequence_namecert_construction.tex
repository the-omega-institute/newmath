\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ContractiveSequenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-contractive-sequence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on contractive iteration, the $\ContractiveSequenceUp$ packet records the finite route by which contraction-stage budgets become displayed regular rational sequence data before any Real-facing seal is consumed. It sits between the interval-contraction surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-interval-contraction-namecert}, the contraction and iteration sources of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-contraction-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-picard-iteration-namecert}, the dyadic budget ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite window source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal seal boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite sequence certificate; it is not a selected fixed point, a quotient stream, countable choice, an endpoint existence theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Contractive sequence carrier]
\label{def:contractive-sequence-carrier}
A $\ContractiveSequenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(I,F,L,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the source interval or metric-domain row read from $\BishopIntervalContractionUp$ and the surrounding contraction setup.
\item $F$ is the contraction or Picard-step row, read through $\BanachContractionUp$ or $\PicardIterationUp$ as finite BEDC data.
\item $L$ is the stage-budget ledger recording the contraction depth, retained prefix, and shrinkage request.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the contraction budgets and error windows.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row exposing the inspected prefixes of the sequence.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal available only after $I,F,L,D,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval, contraction, stage-budget, dyadic, stream, regular-rational, Real-seal, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a contractive-sequence request through the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ContractiveSequenceUp$, $\BishopIntervalContractionUp$, $\BanachContractionUp$, $\PicardIterationUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ContractiveSequenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,F,L,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected fixed point, selected limit, quotient stream equality, countable choice, endpoint existence, host iteration equality, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Contractive sequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:contractive-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ContractiveSequenceUp$ carrier
$$
S=(I,F,L,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ContractiveSequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed interval or domain row, contraction-step row, stage-budget ledger, dyadic tolerance ledger, finite windows, regular rational readback, terminal Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Every completion-facing consumer must read contraction stability through $I,F,L,D$, finite prefix exposure through $W$, regular rational handoff through $R$, and the structural route $E,H,C,P,N$ before treating the sequence as Real-facing data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:contractive-sequence-carrier}. The tuple lists the complete finite inventory available to a contractive-sequence consumer, beginning with the source interval or domain row and the displayed contraction-step row.

Read the stage budget through $L,D$. The contraction depth and dyadic tolerance ledger are part of the packet, so a consumer cannot replace them by a host convergence theorem or by an implicit infinite schedule.

Next use the sibling routes. The interval-contraction certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-interval-contraction-namecert} supplies the shrinkage discipline, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} expose only finite windows and regular rational readback.

Finally the rows $E,H,C,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no row stores a selected fixed point, quotient stream, countable-choice schedule, endpoint theorem, or ambient completeness principle, the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Contractive sequence RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:contractive-sequence-regseqrat-handoff}
Every Real-facing consumer of an accepted $\ContractiveSequenceUp$ packet factors through
$$
\BishopIntervalContractionUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
using the displayed rows $I,F,L,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. The handoff may use $\BanachContractionUp$ or $\PicardIterationUp$ to read the contraction-step row $F$, but it cannot claim a selected limit, quotient stream equality, endpoint existence, countable choice, a completed fixed point, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the accepted packet. The contraction data is read through $I,F,L$, while the dyadic tolerance budget is read through $D$ before any finite sequence window is exposed.

Use the finite-window route through $W$. Each inspected prefix is a displayed $\StreamNameUp$ read, not an implicit infinite stream or a selected tail.

Use the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$ and then the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row $E$. This places the regular rational handoff after the same contraction and dyadic budget rows named by the carrier.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the route for downstream consumers. Thus the handoff produces a finite RegSeqRat-to-Real seal path and leaves selected limits, quotient streams, countable choice, fixed-point existence, and ambient Real completeness outside the packet.
\end{proof}
