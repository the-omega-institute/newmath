\begin{theorem}[Separated root-choice NameCert surface]
\label{thm:root-choice-separation-namecert}
For every accepted $\RootChoiceSeparationUp$ carrier
$$
R=(F,I,A,B,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RootChoiceSeparationUp}$ surface consists exactly of function-read exposure through $F$, located interval exposure through $I$, sign-change and apartness exposure through $A$, retained bisection scheduling through $B$, finite stream-window readout through $S$, regular rational readback through $Q$, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:root-choice-separation-carrier}. The rows $F,I,A,B$ are the only pre-read rows available to the choice request, and they jointly display the function inspections, the located interval source, the separation witness, and the retained sign-change bisection schedule.

Read the neighboring bisection surfaces next. The located interval route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-real-interval-bisection-namecert} supplies finite interval refinement, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bisectionrootisolation-namecert} supplies the retained sign-changing window. The apartness row $A$ keeps the retained window separated from excluded windows before any regular readback is consumed.

The downstream read is then forced through $S,Q,E$. The $\StreamNameUp$ row records the finite endpoint windows, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row repacks those windows as regular rational readback, and the $\RealUp$ row seals only that displayed route.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no carrier coordinate contains a general intermediate-value theorem, Heine--Borel compactness, quotient real equality, unseparated root choice, countable choice, or ambient real completeness, none of those objects can be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
