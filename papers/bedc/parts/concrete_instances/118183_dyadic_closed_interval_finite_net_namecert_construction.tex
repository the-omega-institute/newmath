$$

\begin{proof}

Endpoint-grid coverage and finite-net row coverage are then finite packet checks. The row $G$ lists the displayed dyadic grid, while $F$ names the finite selected grid points and their metric-radius witnesses.

The readback and seal clauses are factorization clauses. The rows $S,Q$ carry the stream and regular rational readings of the same endpoint-grid packet, and $R$ is reached only through those rows.
