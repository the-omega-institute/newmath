\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FreeTopologicalGroupUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-free-topological-group-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Markov and Graev on free topological groups, the $\FreeTopologicalGroupUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which a displayed topological source generates reduced-word group rows equipped with Graev uniformity data. It sits over the open-row discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the entourage discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the continuous operation ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topgroup-namecert}, the metric witness route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and the algebraic operation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}. The certificate names finite reduced-word rows, Graev entourage rows, continuity rows for multiplication and inverse, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not import a host quotient, a universal-property oracle, or an ambient free-object primitive.

\begin{definition}[Free topological group carrier]
\label{def:free-topological-group-carrier}
A $\FreeTopologicalGroupUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(X,T,U,G,W,E,M,I,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source-object row whose points are read only through the displayed $\TopologyUp$ surface.
\item $T$ is the topology row from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, exposing the open-neighbourhood requests used by the source.
\item $U$ is the uniformity row from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, exposing entourage requests used to compare reduced words.
\item $G$ is the algebraic group row from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}, naming multiplication, unit, inverse, and reduced-word comparison rows.
\item $W$ is the reduced-word ledger, listing finite alternating source letters and formal inverse tags together with the local cancellation record.
\item $E$ is the Graev entourage ledger, recording the finite word-level entourages generated from source entourages and their compatibility with reduction.
\item $M$ is the optional $\MetricUp$ handoff row, read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert} when the source uniformity is supplied by a displayed metric.
\item $I$ is the $\TopGroupUp$ continuity row from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topgroup-namecert}, requiring multiplication and inverse to be continuous for the topology generated by $E$.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,T,U,G,W,E,M,I,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from source rows through reduced words, Graev uniformity, group operations, metric handoff, and topological-group continuity.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FreeTopologicalGroupUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\TopGroupUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\GroupUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FreeTopologicalGroupUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,T,U,G,W,E,M,I,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host quotient equality of words, selected representatives, ambient universal arrows, untracked topologies, external completion, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Free topological group NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:free-topological-group-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FreeTopologicalGroupUp$ carrier
$$
F=(X,T,U,G,W,E,M,I,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FreeTopologicalGroupUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed source topology, source uniformity, algebraic group row, reduced-word ledger, Graev entourage ledger, optional metric handoff, topological-group continuity row, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. A consumer may read a free topological group only after these rows have been displayed together.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier through \autoref{def:free-topological-group-carrier}. The source data are $X,T,U$, the algebraic data are $G,W$, and the Graev uniform route is stored in $E$ with optional metric support in $M$.

The topological-group obligation is read through $I$. This forces the multiplication and inverse rows named by $G$ to be consumed together with the topology generated by the Graev entourage ledger, using the sibling topological-group surface rather than an ambient operation theorem.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ exhaust transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the classifier has no further coordinate, the packet cannot export host quotient equality of words, selected representatives, ambient universal arrows, untracked topologies, external completion, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Free topological group Graev uniform route]
\label{thm:free-topological-group-graev-uniform-route}
Every Graev-uniform read exported by an accepted $\FreeTopologicalGroupUp$ packet factors through the displayed source route
$$
\TopologyUp,\ \UniformSpaceUp,\ \GroupUp \longrightarrow \TopGroupUp
$$
and, when metric data are present, through the optional $\MetricUp$ row before the word-level entourage is consumed. The reduced-word ledger $W$ and the Graev entourage ledger $E$ remain visible at the point of use, so no consumer can replace the packet by a host quotient group, an external free topological group, or an untracked universal map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with $F=(X,T,U,G,W,E,M,I,H,C,P,N)$. The topology row $T$ and uniformity row $U$ expose the source neighbourhood and entourage requests before any generated word can be compared.

The reduced-word and group rows are $W$ and $G$. They provide the finite word ledger, cancellation record, unit, multiplication, and inverse rows that the Graev route may inspect.

The Graev-uniform route is $E$ together with the continuity row $I$. If a metric source is available, $M$ records the displayed metric handoff before the word-level entourage is used; otherwise no metric coordinate is exported. Transport and replay through $H,C,P,N$ preserve exactly these rows, so the consumer receives only the finite NameCert route.
\end{proof}
