\begin{theorem}[Profinite metric completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:profinite-metric-completion-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The transport, provenance, and naming rows $H,P,N$ preserve and source that same finite route. Since no coordinate supplies quotient completion equality, a selected infinite representative, Haar measure, host group completion, or ambient compactness outside $K$, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
