Following Bishop and Bridges on compact metric arguments by finite covers, the $\FiniteOpenCoverNumberUp$ packet records the finite numerical boundary used when a compact cover is consumed through a displayed $\ProbeBundle$ rather than through an external enumeration. It realizes the finite-cover route of \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity}: compactness supplies finitely many cover rows, the $\RatUp$ and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledgers record positive radius comparisons, and the resulting number row is consumed by uniform-modulus arguments without choosing a host finite set. The packet sits beside the compact carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, the finite-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-finite-net-namecert}, and the rational lower-bound fold of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probe-bundle-finite-minimum-fold-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[Finite open-cover number carrier]
\label{def:finite-open-cover-number-carrier}
A $\FiniteOpenCoverNumberUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(K,B,R,L,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the compact source row whose cover is being read.
\item $B$ is the $\ProbeBundle$ row carrying the finite cover names.
\item $R$ is the $\RatUp$ radius row attached to each bundled cover element.
\item $L$ is the lower-bound ledger row recording a positive rational number below the displayed cover radii.
\item $U$ is the uniform-modulus handoff row consumed by compact-uniform-continuity arguments.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,B,R,L,U,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite-cover read.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteOpenCoverNumberUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\ProbeBundle$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteOpenCoverNumberUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,B,R,L,U,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an externally numbered cover, a host finite set, a selected minimum, open-cover compactness outside the displayed compact row, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite open-cover number NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-open-cover-number-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteOpenCoverNumberUp$ carrier $O=(K,B,R,L,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteOpenCoverNumberUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the compact source row, $\ProbeBundle$ finite cover row, $\RatUp$ radius rows, positive lower-bound ledger, uniform-modulus handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every consumer route must expose the finite cover through $B$ and the rational radius ledger through $R,L$ before it may read the uniform-modulus row $U$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-open-cover-number-carrier}. The only rows available to the local classifier are $K,B,R,L,U,H,C,P,N$, so the cover number is named by finite packet data.

The compact row $K$ supplies the source of the cover, while $B$ supplies the displayed finite $\ProbeBundle$ cover names. The radius row $R$ and lower-bound ledger $L$ are the only numerical rows from which the cover number can be read.

The uniform-modulus handoff $U$ is downstream of those cover and radius rows. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that route. Since the carrier contains no external enumeration, host finite set, selected minimum, choice coordinate, open-cover compactness outside $K$, or ambient Real completeness, these rows exhaust the local $\NameCert$ obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite open-cover number compact-uniform route]
\label{thm:finite-open-cover-number-compact-uniform-route}
Every compact-uniform-continuity consumer of an accepted $\FiniteOpenCoverNumberUp$ carrier factors its finite-cover read through
$$
K \to B \to R \to L \to U \to H \to C \to P \to N .
$$
Thus the consumer reads the compact source, $\ProbeBundle$ finite cover, rational radius rows, positive lower-bound ledger, uniform-modulus handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before treating the packet as a finite open-cover number.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Open the handoff row $U$. By \autoref{def:finite-open-cover-number-carrier}, it appears only after the compact source row, finite cover bundle row, rational radius row, and lower-bound ledger row have been displayed.

Apply \autoref{thm:finite-open-cover-number-namecert-obligations}. It forces any compact-uniform consumer to read the finite cover as $\ProbeBundle$ data and the numerical cover bound as a $\RatUp$ ledger before using the handoff.

The compact-uniform-continuity vision cited above consumes exactly this finite-cover route. Because the carrier has no external numbering, selected minimum, host finite set, countable choice, ambient completeness, or open-cover compactness outside the displayed compact row, the finite BEDC route is the complete boundary.
\end{proof}
