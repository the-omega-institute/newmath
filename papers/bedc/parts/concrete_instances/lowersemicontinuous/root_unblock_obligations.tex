\begin{theorem}[LowerSemicontinuous lower-real epigraph classifier]
\label{thm:lowersemicontinuous-lowerreal-epigraph-classifier}
For the root packet
$L_{\mathrm{root}}=(X,F,E,W,R,O,H,C,P,N)$, every $\LowerRealUp$ epigraph
classifier read is determined by the displayed source row $X$, graph row $F$,
finite stream schedule $W$, regular rational value row $R$, and epigraph
ledger $E$. The classifier may be transported by $H$ and replayed by $C$, but
it cannot be replaced by a host liminf predicate, quotient real equality,
countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the root carrier from \autoref{def:lowersemicontinuous-root-carrier}.
The only lower-real coordinate available to a consumer is the epigraph ledger
$E$ attached to the source, graph, schedule, and readback rows.

Use \autoref{thm:lower-semicontinuous-lowerreal-epigraph-route}. It orders the
classifier read through $X,F,W,R,E$ before the located comparison, transport,
replay, provenance, and local naming rows are consumed.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:lower-semicontinuous-real-seal-window-nonescape}. Since
the excluded host liminf, quotient equality, choice, and completeness
principles have no carrier coordinate and no replay row, the epigraph
classifier remains finite $\LowerRealUp$ data.
\end{proof}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.LowerSemicontinuousUp.LowerSemicontinuousLowerRealEpigraphClassifier}

\begin{theorem}[LowerSemicontinuous regular readback transport]
\label{thm:lowersemicontinuous-regular-readback-transport}
Every transported lower-semicontinuous readback of
$L_{\mathrm{root}}=(X,F,E,W,R,O,H,C,P,N)$ carries the same $\StreamNameUp$
schedule row $W$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ value row $R$ into the located comparison
ledger $O$. The transport exports a finite readback route through
$W,R,E,O,H,C,P,N$ and not a semantic liminf operation, selected representative,
quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient Real-completeness
witness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the transported readback. The root carrier gives $H$ as the only
componentwise transport row and $C$ as the only displayed replay row, so the
readback cannot leave the carrier surface.

Apply \autoref{thm:lower-semicontinuous-located-readback-stability}. It keeps
the transported epigraph comparison on the same scheduled window $W$ and
regular rational readback $R$ before the located route $O$ is available.

Combine this with \autoref{thm:lowersemicontinuous-real-handoff}. The Real
handoff reads the transported route only after $W,R,E,O,H,C,P,N$ are visible,
so the consumer receives no host liminf, selector, quotient equality, choice,
or ambient completeness data.
\end{proof}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.LowerSemicontinuousUp.LowerSemicontinuousRegularReadback\_transport}

\label{thm:lowersemicontinuous-root-epigraph-located-transport}

$L_{\mathrm{root}}=(X,F,E,W,R,O,H,C,P,N)$ factors through the same finite
schedule $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$, epigraph ledger $E$, comparison
row $O$, $\hsame$ transport row $H$, $\Cont$ replay row $C$, provenance row
$P$, and local NameCert row $N$. The transported route may compare
$\LowerRealUp$ and $\UpperRealUp$ evidence, but it cannot replace the displayed

\autoref{thm:lowersemicontinuous-lowerreal-epigraph-classifier}. It pins the
classifier to the displayed source, graph, finite stream schedule, regular
rational readback, and epigraph ledger rows.

Next apply \autoref{thm:lowersemicontinuous-regular-readback-transport}. The
transport row keeps the located comparison on the same $W,R,E,O,H,C,P,N$
surface, so a $\LowerRealUp$ or $\UpperRealUp$ comparison has no independent
readback source.

Finally use \autoref{thm:lower-semicontinuous-real-seal-window-nonescape}. The

those rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:lowersemicontinuous-lowerreal-epigraph-classifier} and
