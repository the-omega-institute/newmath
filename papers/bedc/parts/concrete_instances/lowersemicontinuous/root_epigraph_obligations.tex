\begin{theorem}[LowerSemicontinuous root epigraph carrier]
\label{thm:lowersemicontinuous-root-epigraph-carrier}
For an accepted $\LowerSemicontinuousUp$ root packet
$$
L_{\mathrm{root}}=(X,F,E,W,R,O,H,C,P,N),
$$
there is a finite root epigraph subcarrier
$$
L_{\mathrm{epi}}=(X,F,W,R,E,O,H,C,P,N)
$$
whose rows are read in that order. The row $W$ displays the finite $\StreamNameUp$ inspection window, $R$ displays the $\RegSeqRatUp$ value readback, $E$ displays the lower-real epigraph ledger, $O$ displays the $\LowerRealUp$--$\UpperRealUp$ comparison and located $\RealUp$ seal boundary, $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay, $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance, and $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LowerSemicontinuousUp}$ row. The carrier has no host liminf predicate, quotient real equality, selected approximating sequence, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the rows of the root packet. The carrier definition already displays $X,F,E,W,R,O,H,C,P,N$, and the reordered subcarrier only makes the consumer route explicit: source and graph first, then finite window, rational readback, epigraph evidence, comparison, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

The finite epigraph window is obtained by induction on the displayed window rows. The base read sees the source and graph rows; the successor read adds one $\StreamNameUp$ schedule row, one $\RegSeqRatUp$ value row, and the corresponding epigraph witness in $E$.

Transport and replay do not add analytic data. They carry the same rows through $H$ and $C$, with $P$ and $N$ sourcing and naming the accepted packet.

The exclusions follow from the carrier inventory. Since no coordinate contains a host liminf predicate, quotient equality, selector, choice row, compactness theorem, or completeness witness, no consumer can extract one from the subcarrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LowerSemicontinuous regular readback handoff]
\label{thm:lowersemicontinuous-root-regular-readback-handoff}
Every $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback consumed from $L_{\mathrm{epi}}$ reaches a $\LocatedRealUp$ handoff only through the displayed route
$$
W \longrightarrow R \longrightarrow E \longrightarrow O \longrightarrow H \longrightarrow C \longrightarrow P \longrightarrow N .
$$
The comparison row $O$ keeps the lower-real and upper-real boundary attached to the same finite readback, so the handoff exports located comparison data and not semantic topology, hidden lower envelopes, quotient $\RealUp$ equality, countable choice, or a detached completion principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the finite schedule row $W$ and the regular rational value row $R$. These are the only readback rows displayed before the epigraph ledger is inspected.

Apply the lower-real epigraph route and located readback stability already present in the chapter. The epigraph row $E$ is reached from the scheduled value read, and $O$ then compares the same lower-real and upper-real boundary before the located handoff is visible.

The remaining rows preserve that route. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and the local $\NameCert$ row $N$ keep the handoff attached to the same readback.

Thus the consumer receives only the finite located comparison surface. The absent semantic topology, envelope, quotient equality, choice, and completion coordinates cannot be supplied by this handoff.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LowerSemicontinuous Real seal boundary]
\label{thm:lowersemicontinuous-root-real-seal-boundary}
For every accepted root epigraph subcarrier $L_{\mathrm{epi}}$, a terminal $\RealUp$ seal can be read only after the finite epigraph window, $\LowerRealUp$--$\UpperRealUp$ comparison row, $\LocatedRealUp$ handoff, $\BHist$ transport, $\hsame$ ledger, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row have all been displayed. The seal boundary exports no Baire-one pointwise limit, quotient real equality, selected representative, countable choice schedule, open-cover compactness proof, or ambient Real-completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the subcarrier of \autoref{thm:lowersemicontinuous-root-epigraph-carrier}. Its Real-facing route is finite because every row is already part of the accepted $\BHist$ packet.

Use the regular readback handoff of \autoref{thm:lowersemicontinuous-root-regular-readback-handoff}. It places the terminal seal after the finite window $W$, rational readback $R$, epigraph row $E$, comparison row $O$, transport row $H$, replay row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local name $N$.

The seal is therefore a boundary row, not an analytic limit principle. It confirms that the displayed located comparison is ready for Real-facing consumers while refusing the absent pointwise-limit, quotient-equality, selection, choice, compactness, and completeness data.
\end{proof}
