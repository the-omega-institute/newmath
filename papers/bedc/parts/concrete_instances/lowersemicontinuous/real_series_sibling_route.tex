\begin{theorem}[LowerSemicontinuous real-series sibling route]
\label{thm:lower-semicontinuous-real-series-sibling-route}
Every $\RealSeriesUp$ consumer of an accepted $\LowerSemicontinuousUp$ packet in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-realseries-namecert} must read the lower-semicontinuous endpoint through the displayed $\RealUp$ seal after the finite rows
$$
X,F,W,R,E,O,H,C,P,N
$$
remain visible. The series-facing route may consume lower-bound information only after the $\StreamNameUp$ window, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, $\LowerRealUp$ and $\UpperRealUp$ epigraph rows, $\LocatedRealUp$ comparison ledger, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert_{\LowerSemicontinuousUp}$ row have all been exposed.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.LowerSemicontinuousUp.LowerSemicontinuousRealSeriesSiblingRoute}
\begin{proof}
Apply the obligation closure package. It fixes $X,F,W,R,E,O,H,C,P,N$ as the complete lower-semicontinuous inventory and keeps semantic liminf data, host topology, quotient equality, selected approximants, countable choice, open-cover compactness, and ambient completeness outside the carrier.

Use the located epigraph route and located-real obligation route. They force the lower-bound read through $W,R,E,O$ before the endpoint can be handed to a located or real-facing consumer.

Finally apply Real-seal nonescape and public route export. The terminal $\RealUp$ seal is downstream of the same finite route, so a real-series consumer receives only the displayed endpoint rows and cannot bypass the lower-semicontinuous packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LowerSemicontinuous epigraph dependency route]
\label{thm:lowersemicontinuous-epigraph-dependency-route}
Every $\EpigraphUp$ or $\RealSeriesUp$ consumer using lower-bound information from an accepted $\LowerSemicontinuousUp$ packet must read the same route
$$
X,F,W,R,E,O,H,C,P,N
$$
through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-epigraph-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-realseries-namecert} before the lower-bound row can be consumed. The route displays the finite domain row, function row, $\StreamNameUp$ window, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, lower and located-real endpoint rows, comparison ledger, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert_{\LowerSemicontinuousUp}$ row. It exports no semantic liminf data, host topology, quotient equality, selected approximants, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.LowerSemicontinuousUp.LowerSemicontinuousEpigraphDependencyRoute}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:lower-semicontinuous-real-series-sibling-route}. It already forces every real-series read through $X,F,W,R,E,O,H,C,P,N$ before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal is consumed.

Use the obligation closure package for the same carrier. The package fixes the lower-semicontinuous inventory as the displayed finite route and keeps liminf semantics, topology, quotient equality, choice-selected approximants, open-cover compactness, and ambient completeness outside the packet.

The epigraph consumer reads the lower-bound row through the same $E,O$ endpoint and comparison rows before passing to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-epigraph-namecert}. Hence both epigraph and real-series consumers depend on the identical sibling route rather than on an external lower-bound principle.
\end{proof}
