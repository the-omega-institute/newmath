\chapter{UniversalCoefficientUp NameCert Construction}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-universal-coefficient-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Eilenberg--Steenrod and Mac Lane, the $\UniversalCoefficientUp$ packet records the finite homological-algebra interface by which a chain-complex homology read and the corresponding cohomology read share coefficient data through displayed module or abelian-group rows. It sits beside the homology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homology-namecert}, the cohomology substrate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cohomology-namecert}, the module source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-module-namecert}, the abelian-group carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-abgroup-namecert}, and the Mayer--Vietoris sequence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-mayer-vietoris-sequence-namecert}. The packet names coefficient, chain-complex, exact-sequence, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it does not assert a host derived functor, selected chain representatives, quotient-class equality, spectral-sequence convergence, or ambient exactness outside the displayed finite window.

\begin{definition}[Universal coefficient carrier]
\label{def:universal-coefficient-carrier}
A $\UniversalCoefficientUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(C,A,M,H_*,H^*,E,T,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\ChainComplexUp$ source row, naming the inspected boundary windows and the displayed square-zero compatibility rows.
\item $A$ is the $\AbGroupUp$ or $\ModuleUp$ coefficient row, read as the only coefficient carrier available to both homology and cohomology consumers.
\item $M$ is the finite coefficient-action ledger, recording the module action or additive group operation rows consumed by the inspected degree.
\item $H_*$ is the $\HomologyUp$ row for the displayed chain-complex degree window.
\item $H^*$ is the $\CohomologyUp$ row for the matching cochain degree window.
\item $E$ is the $\ExactSequenceUp$ row that records the displayed boundary order, kernel-image contact, and connecting slot for the inspected universal-coefficient window.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for chain-complex, coefficient, homology, cohomology, exact-sequence, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for consumers that ask for the same coefficient window and degree route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniversalCoefficientUp$, $\ChainComplexUp$, $\HomologyUp$, $\CohomologyUp$, $\ModuleUp$, $\AbGroupUp$, $\ExactSequenceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniversalCoefficientUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a global derived functor, a selected cycle or cocycle representative, quotient-class equality, an unrestricted splitting, spectral-sequence data, or ambient exactness beyond $E$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Universal coefficient NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:universal-coefficient-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniversalCoefficientUp$ carrier $U=(C,A,M,H_*,H^*,E,T,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UniversalCoefficientUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed chain-complex source $C$, coefficient row $A$, coefficient-action ledger $M$, homology row $H_*$, cohomology row $H^*$, exact-sequence row $E$, componentwise transport $T$, displayed replay $R$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$. Every consumer must read the coefficient row before either the $\HomologyUp$ or $\CohomologyUp$ face, and must read the displayed $\ExactSequenceUp$ row before using the homology-to-cohomology handoff.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:universal-coefficient-carrier}. The tuple lists the only rows available to the classifier: chain-complex source, coefficient carrier, coefficient action, homology, cohomology, exact-sequence, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The coefficient row is shared. A homology-facing read and a cohomology-facing read both pass through $A$ and $M$, so the packet cannot silently change coefficient systems between $H_*$ and $H^*$.

The exact-sequence row is then the unique handoff route. The row $E$ records the displayed kernel-image contact and connecting slot for the inspected degree window, while $T$ and $R$ transport and replay that same finite route. Thus no selected representative, unrestricted splitting, quotient equality, or ambient exactness principle can be exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Universal coefficient homology-cohomology handoff]
\label{thm:universal-coefficient-homology-cohomology-handoff}
Let $U=(C,A,M,H_*,H^*,E,T,R,P,N)$ be an accepted $\UniversalCoefficientUp$ packet. Any downstream consumer that moves from the $\HomologyUp$ row $H_*$ to the $\CohomologyUp$ row $H^*$ must first expose the common coefficient row $A$, the coefficient-action ledger $M$, the $\ChainComplexUp$ source $C$, and the $\ExactSequenceUp$ row $E$. The handoff supplies a finite displayed universal-coefficient route for the inspected degree window, not a chosen splitting, selected chain representative, quotient-class equality, host derived-functor computation, or spectral-sequence convergence claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the shared coefficient surface. The rows $A$ and $M$ are the only coefficient data named by the packet, so both homology and cohomology reads must use the same displayed $\AbGroupUp$ or $\ModuleUp$ source.

Read the chain-complex and exact-sequence rows next. The row $C$ fixes the inspected boundary windows, and $E$ records the finite exact-sequence contact through which the homology and cohomology rows are related.

The structural rows preserve this route for downstream consumers. Componentwise transport $T$, replay $R$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ carry the displayed $C,A,M,H_*,H^*,E$ surface without adding a selected representative, a split sequence, quotient equality, a host derived functor, or spectral-sequence data.
\end{proof}
