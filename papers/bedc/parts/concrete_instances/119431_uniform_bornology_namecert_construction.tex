\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniformBornologyUp$}

Following Bourbaki and Kelley on uniform spaces and bornologies, the $\UniformBornologyUp$ packet records the finite interface in which a displayed uniform-space entourage row controls bounded-family reads without reducing them to metric-ball boundedness. It sits after the bornology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bornology-namecert}, the uniform-space entourage discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the bounded-set row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedset-namecert}, the Cauchy bounded-family route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-bornology-namecert}, and the proper-metric completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-proper-metric-space-namecert}. The packet gives L10 completion, properness, and Cauchy-bornology consumers a non-quotient bounded-uniform interface with no selected entourage scale, host bounded subset, metric-ball collapse, quotient completion, or ambient compactness principle.

\begin{definition}[Uniform bornology carrier]
\label{def:uniform-bornology-carrier}
A $\UniformBornologyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q_{\mathrm{ub}}=(U,B,E,H_f,F,K,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ row naming the finite entourage requests, refinements, inverse requests, and composition windows that may be inspected by a bounded-family consumer.
\item $B$ is the bounded-family source row, read through the $\BornologyUp$ and $\BoundedSetUp$ carriers, and containing only the displayed bounded families admitted by the packet.
\item $E$ is the entourage-boundedness ledger. For each displayed family in $B$, it records the finite entourage windows of $U$ that keep that family inside the same bounded source row after a uniform thickening request.
\item $H_f$ is the hereditary ledger, requiring every displayed subfamily or smaller bounded-set window inherited from $B$ to remain accepted after the same entourage-boundedness read in $E$.
\item $F$ is the finite-union ledger, requiring finite unions of displayed bounded families to be accepted by the same source row and to replay through the same finite entourage windows.
\item $K$ is the Cauchy and proper-metric handoff row. It exposes the route by which $\CauchyBornologyUp$ consumers read bounded Cauchy data and by which $\ProperMetricSpaceUp$ consumers read closed-ball boundedness, always after $U,B,E,H_f,F$ have been replayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the uniform-space row, bounded-family row, entourage-boundedness ledger, hereditary ledger, finite-union ledger, handoff row, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a uniform-bornology request through $U,B,E,H_f,F,K,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformBornologyUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\BornologyUp$, $\BoundedSetUp$, $\CauchyBornologyUp$, $\ProperMetricSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformBornologyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for a selected entourage scale, quotient completion class, host bounded subset, metric-ball-only boundedness theorem, open-cover compactness proof, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform bornology NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-bornology-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformBornologyUp$ carrier
$$
Q_{\mathrm{ub}}=(U,B,E,H_f,F,K,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UniformBornologyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, uniform-space row exposure, bounded-family source exposure, entourage-boundedness exactness, hereditary stability, finite-union stability, Cauchy and proper-metric handoff ordering, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every completion-facing consumer that asks for bounded-uniform data must read these rows before it can consume a Cauchy-bornological or proper-metric boundary.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:uniform-bornology-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly $U,B,E,H_f,F,K,H,C,P,N$, so the uniform, bornological, bounded-set, ledger, handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows are finite BEDC data.

Entourage-boundedness is read from $U,B,E$. The uniform row supplies the finite entourage windows, the bounded-family row supplies the displayed source families, and the ledger $E$ records that each admitted thickening request stays within the same bounded-family source.

Hereditary and finite-union stability are separate ledger reads. The row $H_f$ handles displayed subfamilies and smaller bounded-set windows, while $F$ handles finite unions. Both ledgers replay through $E$, so neither can introduce a host bounded subset or a metric-ball-only boundedness theorem.

Finally inspect $K,H,C,P,N$. The handoff row is available only after the bounded-uniform ledger has been replayed, and the structural rows preserve the same finite packet. Since no coordinate stores a selected entourage scale, quotient completion, open-cover compactness proof, or ambient completeness theorem, the NameCert obligations are exhausted by the displayed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform bornology uniform-space handoff]
\label{thm:uniform-bornology-uniform-space-handoff}
Every $\CauchyBornologyUp$ or $\ProperMetricSpaceUp$ consumer of an accepted $\UniformBornologyUp$ packet reads bounded-family data through the displayed route
$$
\UniformSpaceUp,\quad \BornologyUp,\quad \BoundedSetUp,\quad \CauchyBornologyUp,\quad \ProperMetricSpaceUp,\quad \BHist,\quad \hsame,\quad \Cont,\quad \Pkg,\quad \NameCert .
$$
The handoff row $K$ may expose Cauchy boundedness or proper closed-ball boundedness only after the uniform entourage row $U$, bounded-family row $B$, entourage-boundedness ledger $E$, hereditary ledger $H_f$, and finite-union ledger $F$ have been replayed. Thus the exported object is a bounded-uniform NameCert route, not a metric-ball collapse, selected radius, quotient completion, host subset, or ambient compactness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the uniform-space row $U$ and the bounded-family source row $B$. These rows fix the entourage requests and the bounded families before any downstream consumer can inspect the handoff.

Use the entourage-boundedness ledger $E$ to replay uniform thickenings of displayed bounded families. Then use $H_f$ and $F$ to close the same source row under displayed subfamilies and finite unions.

Only after those ledger reads is the row $K$ available. Its Cauchy side routes bounded Cauchy reads to $\CauchyBornologyUp$, while its proper-metric side routes closed-ball boundedness reads to $\ProperMetricSpaceUp$; both sides share the same $U,B,E,H_f,F$ prefix.

The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve that prefix. Because the carrier has no coordinate for the excluded host principles, a consumer receives exactly the bounded-uniform handoff and nothing stronger.
\end{proof}
