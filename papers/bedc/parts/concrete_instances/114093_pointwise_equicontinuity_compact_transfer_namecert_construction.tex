Following Bishop and the Arzela--Ascoli compact equicontinuity route, the $\PointwiseEquicontinuityCompactTransferUp$ packet records the finite handoff from pointwise family moduli on a compact metric source to a uniform-modulus consumer. It sits after the compact source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the continuous-map rows consumed by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the family-modulus surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-equicontinuityfamily-namecert}, and the uniform-modulus boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}. The packet is a finite compact-net and family-radius transfer; it is not a selected global modulus function, open-cover compactness theorem, function-space compactness assertion, countable-choice schedule, or ambient equality of maps.

\begin{definition}[Pointwise equicontinuity compact transfer carrier]
\label{def:pointwise-equicontinuity-compact-transfer-carrier}
A $\PointwiseEquicontinuityCompactTransferUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(K,F,E,B,R,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, exposing only the finite compact-net and metric inspection data used by the transfer.
\item $F$ is the finite $\ContinuousMapUp$ family ledger whose graph rows are inspected by the equicontinuity request.
\item $E$ is the $\EquicontinuityFamilyUp$ pointwise family-modulus row over the same compact source and target metric data.
\item $B$ is the finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ cover row of compact-net centers.
\item $R$ is the $\RatUp$ radius and tolerance fold ledger that lowers the pointwise family radii over the finite bundle.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row receiving the tolerance-only family modulus extracted from $K,F,E,B,R$.
\item $H$ is the componentwise $\hsame$ transport row for compact source, continuous-map, equicontinuity, bundle, rational fold, uniform-modulus, replay, provenance, and naming data.
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact finite-net reads and pointwise family-modulus rows to the uniform-modulus handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PointwiseEquicontinuityCompactTransferUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\EquicontinuityFamilyUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\RatUp$, $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PointwiseEquicontinuityCompactTransferUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,F,E,B,R,U,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected global modulus, an arbitrary open-cover compactness theorem, function-space compactness, host equality of maps, countable choice, or an external family selector.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Pointwise equicontinuity compact transfer NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:pointwise-equicontinuity-compact-transfer-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PointwiseEquicontinuityCompactTransferUp$ carrier $T=(K,F,E,B,R,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\PointwiseEquicontinuityCompactTransferUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row $K$, finite $\ContinuousMapUp$ family ledger $F$, $\EquicontinuityFamilyUp$ pointwise modulus row $E$, finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ compact-net row $B$, $\RatUp$ fold ledger $R$, $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row $U$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and local naming row $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:pointwise-equicontinuity-compact-transfer-carrier}. The rows $K,F,E,B,R,U,H,C,P,N$ are the whole packet, so carrier admission and local naming are finite row checks.

The transfer route is finite. The compact source row $K$ supplies the finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ cover $B$; the family ledger $F$ and pointwise equicontinuity row $E$ attach a displayed local radius to each inspected center; the $\RatUp$ fold $R$ lowers those radii over $B$ before the uniform-modulus row $U$ is consumed.

Transport and replay remain componentwise. The rows $H,C,P,N$ keep the compact-net, family-modulus, rational-fold, and uniform-modulus data inside one $\BHist$ packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores a selected global modulus, open-cover theorem, function-space compactness object, host map equality, countable-choice schedule, or external family selector, no local NameCert obligation can export such data.
\end{proof}
