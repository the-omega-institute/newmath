It sits between the Cauchy-filter surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfilter-namecert}, the real-uniformity rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-uniform-structure-namecert}, the stream-window naming of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the public real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the completion handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}. \begin{definition}[CauchyNetCriterion carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-net-criterion-carrier}
A $\CauchyNetCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(D,A,U,T,F,S,R,E,M,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the directed-index row naming the finite preorder comparisons, upper-window witnesses, and tail requests inspected by the criterion.
\item $A$ is the net-value row, exposing the finite values whose common-tail comparisons are read by downstream consumers.
\item $U$ is the $\RealUniformStructureUp$ entourage row, displaying the uniform tolerance requests used to compare late net values.
\item $T$ is the Cauchy-tail ledger, recording the displayed common-tail witnesses for each requested entourage in $U$.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ basis row obtained from the directed tails and their common-window evidence.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation-window row extracted from finite directed tails without choosing a global cofinal sequence.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row reached from $D,A,U,T,S$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row available only after regular-rational readback has been displayed.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ handoff row that lets completion-facing consumers read the same Cauchy evidence as a completion criterion.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for directed indices, net values, uniformity rows, Cauchy-tail ledgers, filter bases, stream windows, regular readback, real seals, completion handoffs, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the directed-tail, filter-basis, regular-sequence, real-seal, and completion routes.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyNetCriterionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets whose criterion consumer first displays $D,A,U,T,F,S,R,E,M,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected cofinal sequence, quotient-net equality, countable choice, host completion, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyNetCriterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-net-criterion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyNetCriterionUp$ carrier
$$
Q=(D,A,U,T,F,S,R,E,M,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyNetCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of directed-index admission, net-value exposure, $\RealUniformStructureUp$ entourage exposure, Cauchy-tail ledger exposure, $\CauchyFilterUp$ basis handoff, $\StreamNameUp$ window readback, $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff, $\RealUp$ seal readback, $\MetricCompletionUp$ completion handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming through the rows of $Q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-net-criterion-carrier}. The directed analytic data are exactly $D,A,U,T$: indices, values, entourages, and common-tail witnesses.

The filter-basis row $F$ is read only after those directed rows have been displayed. It records the finite basis generated by tails and common-window evidence, not a quotient of nets or a completed limit object.

The sequence-facing rows $S,R,E$ are then reached in order. Stream windows are extracted from displayed finite tails, the regular-rational handoff repacks those windows, and the real seal is read only after the regular row is present.

The completion-facing row $M$ reads the same Cauchy evidence. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the displayed route, so no hidden cofinal selector, quotient equality, choice principle, host completion, or ambient completeness witness can discharge a NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}
