\begin{definition}[Quartic spectral common carrier]
\label{def:quartic-spectral-common-carrier}
A $\QuarticSpectralCommonCarrierUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(R,M,P,A,E,L,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the recurrence-source row, storing the numerator $N(t,y)$, denominator $D(t,y)$, coefficient readback, and order-four recurrence schedule of \autoref{def:unified-reality-quartic-recurrence-source}.
\item $M$ is the companion-matrix row, storing the displayed $4\times4$ transfer matrix and its characteristic-polynomial readout.
\item $P$ is the spectral-polynomial row, storing the quartic $\Pi(\lambda,y)$ as both the reciprocal denominator face and the matrix characteristic face.
\item $A$ is the affine spectral-curve row, storing the finite equation $\Pi(\lambda,y)=0$ and the projection coordinates $(\lambda,y)$.
\item $E$ is the elliptic-normalization row, storing the balanced-quartic normalization route to the displayed Weierstrass model.
\item $L$ is the projection ledger, storing the generating-function, recurrence, companion-matrix, spectral-polynomial, affine-curve, elliptic-normalization, real-projection, and complex-coordinate reads.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the recurrence, matrix, polynomial, curve, normalization, ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay for each projection in $L$.
\item $G$ stores $\Pkg$ provenance over $\QuarticSpectralCommonCarrierUp$, $\PolynomialUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\EllipticCurveUp$, $\RealPolynomialUp$, $\ComplexUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\QuarticSpectralCommonCarrierUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an arbitrary quartic family, external eigenvalue selection, analytic continuation, global branch choice, or a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Quartic spectral carrier NameCert obligations]
\label{def:quartic-spectral-carrier-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\QuarticSpectralCommonCarrierUp$ carrier $Q=(R,M,P,A,E,L,H,C,G,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\QuarticSpectralCommonCarrierUp}$ surface consists exactly of six finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the recurrence source, companion matrix, spectral polynomial, affine curve, elliptic normalization, projection ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data;
\item recurrence-matrix compatibility, requiring the recurrence row $R$ and matrix row $M$ to expose the same order-four transfer law;
\item matrix-polynomial compatibility, requiring $M$ and $P$ to expose the same characteristic face $\Pi(\lambda,y)$;

No obligation row stores a host spectral theorem, arbitrary branch selection, analytic continuation, or global zeta-zero claim.
\end{definition}

\label{thm:quartic-spectral-projection-ledger}

Finally read the structural rows $H,C,G,N$. Componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite carrier. Since the carrier omits arbitrary polynomial families, free spectra, branch selectors, analytic continuation, and global zeta-zero data, the public projection ledger is exactly the displayed quartic spectral surface.
\end{proof}
