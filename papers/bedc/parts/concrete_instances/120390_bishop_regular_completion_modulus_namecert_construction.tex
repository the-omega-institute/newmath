The $\BishopRegularCompletionModulusUp$ packet records that finite modulus surface between the dyadic arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the effective Cauchy completion carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-effective-cauchy-completion-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. $$

\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, exposing exactly the inspected entries of the regular Cauchy name.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the same stream window and dyadic tolerance ledger.
\item $C$ is the $\EffectiveCauchyCompletionUp$ admission row, recording that the displayed windows and tolerances form the completion-facing Cauchy route.

\begin{theorem}[Bishop regular completion-modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-regular-completion-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopRegularCompletionModulusUp$ carrier
$$
M=(D,S,R,C,E,B,H,T,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCompletionModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the dyadic tolerance ledger, stream window, regular readback, effective-completion admission, Real seal, Bishop modulus, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item finite-window adequacy, requiring every precision request in $B$ to open the displayed $S$ window before the regular readback row $R$ is consumed;
\item dyadic tolerance exactness, requiring the row $D$ to supply the budget used by both $R$ and the completion admission row $C$;
\item Real seal nonescape, requiring $E$ to be read only after $D,S,R,C,B$ have been displayed and replayed through $H,T,P,N$;
\item exclusion of quotient stream equality, selected completed representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-regular-completion-modulus-carrier}. Its analytic rows are exactly $D,S,R,C,E,B$, with $H,T,P,N$ supplying transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The modulus row $B$ is the only row assigning a finite inspection bound to a requested precision. Once $B$ is read, the consumer must open the corresponding stream window $S$ and dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ before it can consume the regular readback row $R$.

The completion-facing row $C$ is downstream of $D,S,R,B$. Thus the packet presents an effective Cauchy completion route through displayed finite data rather than through a quotient of Cauchy names.

Finally, the terminal seal $E$ is reached only after the same rows have been replayed. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for quotient equality, a selected host representative, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness, those principles are outside the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
