\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KhalimskyLineUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-khalimsky-line-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Khalimsky and digital topology, the $\KhalimskyLineUp$ packet records the integer line with its alternating finite-open topology as NameCert data rather than as a host topological space. It sits after the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the observable-open classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert}, and the specialization-order carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-order-namecert}. The packet exposes the finite local stars around integer rows, their Sierpinski truth reads, and the induced specialization comparison as one displayed surface.

\begin{definition}[Khalimsky line carrier]
\label{def:khalimsky-line-carrier}
A $\KhalimskyLineUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(Z,T,S,O,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Z$ is the integer-site row, read as finite named windows of adjacent sites rather than as an external set of integers.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ row whose basic opens are the Khalimsky stars: odd sites are open singletons, while even sites are read through the three-site window consisting of the site and its two neighbours.
\item $S$ is the $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ observable-open row recording membership of a displayed site in a displayed basic open.
\item $O$ is the $\OrderUp$ specialization row generated by the finite rule that an even site specializes to its adjacent odd sites and every site specializes to itself.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the site, topology, Sierpinski, specialization-order, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from each local-open or specialization request through $Z,T,S,O,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KhalimskyLineUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, $\OrderUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KhalimskyLineUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for an ambient integer topology, host subset equality, excluded middle over arbitrary opens, selected neighbourhood filters, quotient specialization equality, or Lean verification.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Khalimsky line NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:khalimsky-line-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KhalimskyLineUp$ carrier
$$
K=(Z,T,S,O,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\KhalimskyLineUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the integer-site row, the Khalimsky-star topology row, the Sierpinski observable-open row, the specialization-order row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming; local-star exactness for odd and even sites; Sierpinski membership exactness for displayed basic opens; specialization exactness for the reflexive and adjacent even-to-odd comparisons; and non-escape of all topology and order reads through the same finite packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:khalimsky-line-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the site row, topology row, observable-open row, specialization-order row, and structural rows.

The local-star clauses are finite. Odd sites expose a singleton basic open, while even sites expose the three-site adjacent window. The Sierpinski row reads only those displayed opens, so open membership is a truth row in the packet rather than a host proposition.

The specialization row is then read from the same local stars. Reflexivity is supplied at every displayed site, and the nontrivial comparisons are precisely the adjacent even-to-odd incidences forced by containment of observable neighbourhoods.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve the site, topology, Sierpinski, and order rows together. Since no carrier coordinate stores arbitrary open subsets, selected neighbourhood filters, host subset equality, quotient specialization equality, or excluded middle, none becomes a NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Khalimsky line specialization handoff]
\label{thm:khalimsky-line-specialization-handoff}
Every $\OrderUp$ or digital-topology consumer of an accepted $\KhalimskyLineUp$ carrier reads specialization through the displayed route
$$
Z \longrightarrow T \longrightarrow S \longrightarrow O.
$$
Thus the exported order surface is the finite Khalimsky specialization ledger generated by local stars and Sierpinski membership rows, not an ambient order on integers, an arbitrary Alexandrov topology, a selected neighbourhood filter, host subset equality, or quotient specialization equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an order-facing read. The carrier exposes no specialization comparison before the site row $Z$ and topology row $T$ display the relevant local stars.

Read the Sierpinski row $S$ next. It turns membership in each displayed basic open into observable truth data, so the consumer can test only the finite local-star rows carried by the packet.

The specialization row $O$ is downstream of those observable-open reads. It records reflexive comparisons and adjacent even-to-odd comparisons exactly when every displayed open observation around the upper site is respected by the lower site.

Therefore any order or digital-topology consumer receives the route $Z,T,S,O,H,C,P,N$. The absent host objects are never coordinates of the packet and cannot be exported by transport, replay, provenance, or local naming.
\end{proof}
