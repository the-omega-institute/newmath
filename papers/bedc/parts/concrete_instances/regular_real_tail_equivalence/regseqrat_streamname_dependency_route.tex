\begin{theorem}[Regular real tail equivalence RegSeqRat StreamName route]
\label{thm:regular-real-tail-equivalence-regseqrat-streamname-route}
Every accepted $\RegularRealTailEquivalenceUp$ packet consumes $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window scheduling, and $\RealUp$ sealing before comparing tails. The dependency route is the finite carrier read
$$
R_0,R_1,W,D,Z,Q,E,H,C,P,N,
$$
where the two regular rational source rows are exposed through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the shared observation window is scheduled through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the equality-facing endpoint is sealed only through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. Tail equivalence has no selected limit, host equality, quotient class, or countable-choice coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier and NameCert obligations of \autoref{def:regular-real-tail-equivalence-carrier} and \autoref{thm:regular-real-tail-equivalence-namecert-obligations}. They place $R_0,R_1$ before the shared window $W$ and place the Real seal $E$ after the finite tolerance, zero-tail, and comparison rows.

Apply the RegSeqRat route of \autoref{thm:regular-real-tail-equivalence-regseqrat-route}. It forces the two source rows to be read as regular rational names before any tail comparison can be exported.

Next use Real seal nonescape, \autoref{thm:regular-real-tail-equivalence-real-seal-nonescape}. The seal row is available only after the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window, dyadic ledger, zero-tail row, and comparison route have been displayed.

Thus the packet compares tails only through the displayed RegSeqRat, StreamName, and Real route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for selected limits, host equality, quotient classes, or countable choice, none can enter the tail-equivalence read.
\end{proof}
