\begin{theorem}[Heine-Cantor finite-net modulus extraction]
\label{thm:heine-cantor-finite-net-modulus-extraction}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Finally replay the route through $H,C,P,N$. These structural rows transport, continue, package, and name the same displayed packet, so every uniform-modulus consumer reaches $U$ only through $X,K,F,L,Q$.
