\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\GroupCohomologyUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-group-cohomology-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Brown and Cartan--Eilenberg on group cohomology, the $\GroupCohomologyUp$ packet names the finite BEDC surface by which a group action on abelian or module coefficients is read as cocycle and boundary data before any cohomology group is inspected. It depends on the action carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}, the coefficient rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-abgroup-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-module-namecert}, and the cocycle-boundary interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cohomology-namecert}. The packet does not choose representatives in a quotient, import derived-functor machinery, assume projective resolutions, invoke countable choice, or claim an ambient exact-category theorem.

\begin{definition}[Group cohomology carrier]
\label{def:group-cohomology-carrier}
A $\GroupCohomologyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(A,M,W,D,Z,B,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the $\GroupUp$ action row naming the finite group-operation surface and its action on coefficient reads.
\item $M$ is the coefficient row, read either through an $\AbGroupUp$ additive surface or through a compatible $\ModuleUp$ row.
\item $W$ is the finite cochain-window ledger listing the displayed cochain arities and argument windows being inspected.
\item $D$ is the coboundary row, recording the finite alternating action-and-face route from one cochain window to the next.
\item $Z$ is the cocycle row, asserting that the displayed coboundary route sends the inspected cochain window to the zero coefficient row.
\item $B$ is the boundary row, recording the finite predecessor window whose coboundary route yields a displayed cocycle.
\item $T$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the group action, coefficient, cochain window, coboundary, cocycle, boundary, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay for reading the action, coefficient module, cochain window, coboundary row, cocycle row, and boundary row in that order.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\GroupCohomologyUp$, $\GroupUp$, $\AbGroupUp$, $\ModuleUp$, $\CohomologyUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\GroupCohomologyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for quotient representatives, selected resolutions, derived functors, global exactness, countable choice, or an ambient category of modules.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Group cohomology NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:group-cohomology-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:group-cohomology-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\GroupCohomologyUp}$ surface consists of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item action exposure: the $\GroupUp$ row $A$ is read before any cochain, cocycle, or boundary row is available;
\item coefficient exposure: the $\AbGroupUp$ or $\ModuleUp$ coefficient row $M$ is displayed before the coboundary route is inspected;
\item cochain-window exactness: the ledger $W$ lists the finite argument windows and arities used by the cochain read;
\item coboundary routing: the row $D$ displays the finite alternating action-and-face route between the relevant cochain windows;
\item cocycle admission: the row $Z$ is admitted only after $D$ has exposed a zero coboundary read in the coefficient surface;
\item boundary admission: the row $B$ is admitted only through a displayed predecessor cochain window and its coboundary route;
\item stability and replay: $T$ and $C$ preserve the same action, coefficient, cochain-window, coboundary, cocycle, boundary, provenance, and naming rows under $\hsame$ transport and $\Cont$ replay;
\item non-escape: $P$ and $N$ expose the package provenance and local name without adding quotient representatives, selected resolutions, derived-functor objects, global exactness, countable choice, or ambient module-category data.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from the displayed tuple $G=(A,M,W,D,Z,B,T,C,P,N)$. The first three obligations expose the action, coefficient row, and finite cochain-window ledger before any cohomology read is possible.

The coboundary and cocycle obligations are local to the displayed windows. The row $D$ supplies the alternating action-and-face route, and $Z$ records zero coboundary only after that route has been read in the coefficient surface.

The boundary obligation is likewise finite. The row $B$ names a predecessor window and its displayed coboundary route; it does not select a representative in a quotient or appeal to an external resolution.

Finally $T,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same rows. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient representatives, selected resolutions, derived functors, global exactness, countable choice, or ambient module-category data, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Group cohomology cocycle-boundary route]
\label{thm:group-cohomology-cocycle-boundary-route}
Every accepted consumer of a $\GroupCohomologyUp$ packet must expose the group action row $A$, coefficient row $M$, cochain-window ledger $W$, cocycle row $Z$, and boundary row $B$ before any cohomology read is available. In particular, no consumer can inspect a cohomology class by bypassing the displayed $\GroupUp$ action, $\AbGroupUp$ or $\ModuleUp$ coefficient surface, finite cochain windows, coboundary route, cocycle row, and boundary row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:group-cohomology-carrier}. Its analytic rows appear as the action $A$, coefficient $M$, cochain window $W$, coboundary route $D$, cocycle row $Z$, and boundary row $B$ before the transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows are read.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:group-cohomology-namecert-obligations}. Action exposure and coefficient exposure make $A$ and $M$ prerequisites for every downstream read, while cochain-window exactness and coboundary routing place $W$ and $D$ before either $Z$ or $B$.

Cocycle admission and boundary admission then force the two cohomology-facing rows to be displayed. The replay and non-escape clauses keep the consumer inside $A,M,W,D,Z,B,T,C,P,N$, so there is no route through quotient representatives, selected resolutions, derived functors, global exactness, countable choice, or ambient module-category data.
\end{proof}
