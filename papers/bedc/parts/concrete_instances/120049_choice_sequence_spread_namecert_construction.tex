\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-choice-sequence-spread-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Brouwer on choice sequences and spreads, and Bishop--Bridges on
lawlike finite approximations to constructive sequences, the
$\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp$ packet names the finite prefix surface through which a
choice sequence is admitted by a spread law before any infinite branch, choice
function, or completed real object is exposed. It sits after the
$\SpreadLawUp$ admission row of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-spread-law-namecert}, the finite observation
schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the list spine
of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-list-namecert}, and the Boolean endpoint
discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bool-namecert}. The packet is a
finite BEDC carrier for prefix inspection; it is not countable choice, not a
selected infinite branch, and not an ambient fan theorem.

\begin{definition}[Choice-sequence spread carrier]
\label{def:choice-sequence-spread-carrier}
A $\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(B,L,A,S,W,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BoolUp$ endpoint row used by each displayed binary choice.
\item $L$ is the $\ListUp$ finite-prefix row, listing the inspected Boolean
prefixes and their parent-prefix inclusions.
\item $A$ is the $\SpreadLawUp$ admission row, recording that every displayed
prefix in $L$ is accepted by the same spread law and that accepted extensions
remain in the displayed prefix tree.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule row naming the finite moments at which
the choice sequence is observed.
\item $W$ is the prefix-window ledger aligning the StreamName observations with
the finite List prefixes admitted by $A$.
\item $R$ is the finite readback row, saying which Boolean endpoint in $B$ is
read at each displayed position of the schedule.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for
$B,L,A,S,W,R,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite route
$$
B \longmapsto L \longmapsto A \longmapsto S \longmapsto W
\longmapsto R .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp$,
$\BoolUp$, $\ListUp$, $\SpreadLawUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$,
$\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through
$B,L,A,S,W,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected infinite branch,
countable choice, quotient equality of streams, fan compactness, or ambient
$\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-sequence spread NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:choice-sequence-spread-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp$ carrier
$$
Q=(B,L,A,S,W,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp}$ surface consists exactly of
carrier admission for the Boolean endpoint row, List prefix row, SpreadLaw
admission row, StreamName schedule row, prefix-window ledger, finite readback
row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$
provenance, and local naming. Every public read of the packet factors through
those displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:choice-sequence-spread-carrier}. The
accepted tuple exposes exactly $B,L,A,S,W,R,H,C,P,N$, so the local certificate
has no hidden row from which to read a branch selector or a host-level sequence.

The prefix data are finite. The $\BoolUp$ row supplies the endpoints, the
$\ListUp$ row lists the inspected prefixes, and the $\SpreadLawUp$ row admits
only those displayed prefixes and their displayed extensions.

The schedule data are finite as well. The $\StreamNameUp$ row names the observed
positions, while $W$ and $R$ align those positions with the displayed prefix
tree and endpoint reads.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the
carrier has no coordinate for countable choice, quotient stream equality, fan
compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, the listed rows exhaust the
NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-sequence spread prefix closure]
\label{thm:choice-sequence-spread-prefix-closure}
Every accepted $\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp$ carrier is closed under displayed
prefix restriction: if a consumer reads a prefix from the ledger $L$ through the
window $W$, then each earlier prefix on the same displayed branch is read
through the same $\BoolUp$, $\ListUp$, $\SpreadLawUp$, $\StreamNameUp$,
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the displayed prefix from the $L$ coordinate. The List row stores the
parent-prefix inclusions for the finite prefixes currently under inspection.

Apply the admission row $A$. Because $A$ is the same $\SpreadLawUp$ row for the
whole packet, restricting to an earlier displayed prefix keeps the read inside
the same spread-law admission surface.

The window and schedule rows do not introduce a second branch. They only align
the earlier prefix with already displayed $\StreamNameUp$ observations and
Boolean endpoint reads.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve the
same finite packet. Thus prefix restriction remains inside the accepted
$\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp$ carrier and exports no infinite selector.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-sequence spread StreamName handoff]
\label{thm:choice-sequence-spread-streamname-handoff}
Every $\StreamNameUp$ consumer of an accepted $\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp$ packet
must first expose the finite route
$$
\BoolUp \longmapsto \ListUp \longmapsto \SpreadLawUp \longmapsto
\StreamNameUp \longmapsto W \longmapsto R .
$$
The schedule is therefore a finite observation handoff from spread-admitted
prefixes to StreamName reads, not a selected branch or countable family of
choices.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from \autoref{thm:choice-sequence-spread-namecert-obligations}. A
StreamName-facing read can cite the packet only after the Boolean endpoint row,
List prefix row, and SpreadLaw admission row have been displayed.

The schedule row $S$ is downstream of that admission data. The window ledger
$W$ records which displayed prefixes are observed, and the readback row $R$
records the Boolean endpoints read at the displayed positions.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve this same route. They do not add a
selector for a full infinite branch, a countable-choice function, or quotient
equality of streams.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-sequence spread nonescape]
\label{thm:choice-sequence-spread-nonescape}
No consumer of an accepted $\ChoiceSequenceSpreadUp$ carrier can extract
anything beyond the displayed finite packet
$$
B,L,A,S,W,R,H,C,P,N .
$$
In particular the packet does not export a chosen infinite branch, countable
choice, quotient equality of streams, fan compactness, bar induction, or
ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{def:choice-sequence-spread-carrier} to enumerate the carrier
coordinates. The only analytic surface available to consumers is the finite
prefix route through $B,L,A,S,W,R$.

Use \autoref{thm:choice-sequence-spread-prefix-closure} to keep every prefix
restriction inside that same displayed route. No restricted read creates a new
coordinate outside the packet.

Use \autoref{thm:choice-sequence-spread-streamname-handoff} for
StreamName-facing consumers. They receive only the finite observation handoff
from spread-admitted prefixes to Boolean endpoint readback.

The remaining rows $H,C,P,N$ are structural. They transport, replay, source, and
name the finite packet, but they do not contain a branch selector, a
countable-choice schedule, quotient stream equality, fan compactness, bar
induction, or a completeness witness for $\RealUp$.
\end{proof}
