\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniformlyDiscreteSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-uniformly-discrete-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki and Kelley on metric and uniform-space terminology, the $\UniformlyDiscreteSpaceUp$ packet records the finite certificate by which a metric space with a displayed positive separation radius presents a uniform-space entourage base. It sits between the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the uniform-space interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the entourage-base route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-entourage-base-namecert}, and the dyadic and real-name corridor of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The chapter names a finite metric-to-uniformity certificate; it does not assert quotient equality, countable choice, selected completions, or ambient Real completeness.

\begin{definition}[UniformlyDiscreteSpace carrier]
\label{def:uniformly-discrete-space-carrier}
A $\UniformlyDiscreteSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(M,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row whose distance comparisons and point rows are exposed before any uniform-space read is public.
\item $R$ is a positive dyadic separation radius read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and sealed by the displayed $\RealUp$ tolerance route.
\item $E$ is the $\EntourageBaseUp$ and $\UniformSpaceUp$ handoff row generated by the condition that distinct displayed points are separated by the radius $R$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, radius, entourage-base handoff, and structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from metric comparisons through dyadic radius exposure and the uniformity handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformlyDiscreteSpaceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\EntourageBaseUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformlyDiscreteSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient metric equality, a hidden selector of apart points, countable choice, selected completion, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[UniformlyDiscreteSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniformly-discrete-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformlyDiscreteSpaceUp$ carrier $U=(M,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UniformlyDiscreteSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, positive-radius exposure, metric separation, uniformity handoff, transport and replay stability, provenance, local naming, and nonescape from the displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniformly-discrete-space-carrier}. Carrier admission exposes the metric source $M$, the positive dyadic radius $R$, the entourage-base handoff $E$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Positive-radius exposure is read from $R$. Since $R$ is a dyadic tolerance row sealed through the Real-name route, the packet exposes a finite separation budget rather than a host real parameter.

Metric separation and the uniformity handoff are read in order. The metric row $M$ supplies the displayed distance comparisons, and $E$ packages the radius-separated diagonal boundary as an $\EntourageBaseUp$ row for $\UniformSpaceUp$ consumers.

Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same route, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming. No further row stores quotient equality, countable choice, selected completions, or ambient Real completeness, so the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[UniformlyDiscreteSpace uniformity handoff]
\label{thm:uniformly-discrete-space-uniformity-handoff}
Every $\UniformSpaceUp$-facing read of an accepted $\UniformlyDiscreteSpaceUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\MetricUp \to \DyadicRatCoreUp \to \EntourageBaseUp \to \StreamNameUp \to \RegSeqRatUp \to \RealUp
$$
together with the displayed $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows. The handoff exports only the radius-separated metric entourage base and never supplies quotient equality, countable choice, selected completion, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier rows $M,R,E,H,C,P,N$. A uniform-space consumer can read $E$ only after the metric row $M$ and the positive dyadic radius row $R$ have been displayed.

The dyadic tolerance route named by $R$ is the only numerical boundary available to the packet. Its StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real seals provide the finite readback path for the radius, while $E$ packages that readback as an entourage-base handoff.

The structural rows preserve the same path for downstream consumers. Thus every uniformity-facing read factors through the metric source, dyadic radius, entourage base, finite name route, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows, with no hidden completion or quotient principle attached.
\end{proof}
