Following Bishop and Bridges and Kelley on constructive metric completion and product metrics, the $\CompleteProductMetricUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface where a binary product metric is already displayed and the completion-facing Cauchy windows are accepted as complete-product data. It sits after the product-distance packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-productmetric-namecert}, the metric-completion carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the separated-completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedcompletion-namecert}, the finite stream interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives Banach, Polish, compact, and completion routes a complete product-metric certificate without adding host product metrics, quotient completion equality, selected product limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Complete product-metric carrier]
\label{def:complete-product-metric-carrier}
A $\CompleteProductMetricUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(X,Y,\Pi,\Delta,M,S,W_X,W_Y,R_X,R_Y,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ and $Y$ are the two accepted component metric-completion source rows.
\item $\Pi$ is the accepted $\ProductMetricUp$ product-distance row, including the displayed product carrier, projection reads, and product-distance ledger.
\item $\Delta$ is the completion-facing metric row read from $\Pi$ rather than from a host product metric.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ route that accepts paired Cauchy windows as completion-facing data.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparatedCompletionUp$ comparison boundary for identifying completed product reads only through displayed separated data.
\item $W_X$ and $W_Y$ are the paired $\StreamNameUp$ window rows inspected on the component Cauchy names.
\item $R_X$ and $R_Y$ are the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows attached to those windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for the displayed product-distance estimate.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the component rows, product metric, completion route, separated boundary, stream windows, regular readbacks, Real seal, continuation, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a complete-product request through $X,Y,\Pi,\Delta,M,S,W_X,W_Y,R_X,R_Y,E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompleteProductMetricUp$, $\ProductMetricUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\SeparatedCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompleteProductMetricUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for host product metrics, quotient completion classes, selected representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, detached real carriers, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Complete product-metric NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:complete-product-metric-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompleteProductMetricUp$ carrier $Q=(X,Y,\Pi,\Delta,M,S,W_X,W_Y,R_X,R_Y,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompleteProductMetricUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the component completion sources, the $\ProductMetricUp$ product-distance row, the $\MetricCompletionUp$ completion route, the $\SeparatedCompletionUp$ comparison boundary, paired $\StreamNameUp$ windows, paired $\RegSeqRatUp$ readbacks, the $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, local naming, and refusal of host product metrics, quotient completion equality, selected product limits, countable choice, trichotomy, detached real carriers, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, and bridge checking.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:complete-product-metric-carrier}. The displayed packet exposes exactly the two component source rows, the product-distance row $\Pi$, the completion route $M$, the separated boundary $S$, paired windows, regular readbacks, the Real seal, and the structural rows.

The product metric is read before the completion route. Thus $\Delta$ and $M$ can consume only the $\ProductMetricUp$ ledger carried by $\Pi$, together with the finite windows and regular readbacks, and cannot import a host product metric.

The separated-completion boundary is then forced through $S$. It compares completed product reads only after the paired Cauchy windows and product-distance estimate have been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, detached real carriers, ambient completeness, or bridge checking, the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}
