\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DetachableBarUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-detachable-bar-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Troelstra--van Dalen on bars over spreads and choice sequences, the $\DetachableBarUp$ packet records a finite-prefix bar predicate whose inspected prefixes are decidable inside the displayed BEDC rows. It sits beside the finite fan surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert}, the barred-spread induction route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-brouwer-bar-induction-namecert}, the choice-sequence prefix rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-choice-sequence-namecert}, the Baire-space cylinder surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bairespace-namecert}, and the spread-law admission route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-spread-law-namecert}. Downstream analytic consumers reach it only through the finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular-rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}; they receive no countable-choice selector, quotient stream equality, open-cover compactness, selected branch, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[DetachableBar carrier]
\label{def:detachable-bar-carrier}
A $\DetachableBarUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(F,I,Q,B,A,S,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\FanTheoremUp$ finite fan row or finite fan-boundary row through which bounded prefix inspection is requested;
\item $I$ is the $\BrouwerBarInductionUp$ barred-spread row, exposing only the finite bar decisions and induction replay visible at the inspected prefixes;
\item $Q$ is the $\ChoiceSequenceUp$ prefix-answer row, recording the finite answers whose prefixes are being tested;
\item $B$ is the $\BaireSpaceUp$ prefix-cylinder row that names the ambient countable-prefix window without naming a completed branch;
\item $A$ is the $\SpreadLawUp$ admission row, deciding which displayed prefixes are legal spread extensions;
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule read by downstream analytic consumers;
\item $W$ is the detachable bar-decision row, recording for each displayed prefix whether the bar predicate accepts it;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row available only after the finite window schedule and bar-decision row have been displayed;
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for real-facing consumers that pass through $S,W,R$;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the fan, bar-induction, choice-sequence, Baire, spread-law, StreamName, bar-decision, regular-rational, and Real rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite-prefix route;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DetachableBarUp$, $\FanTheoremUp$, $\BrouwerBarInductionUp$, $\ChoiceSequenceUp$, $\BaireSpaceUp$, $\SpreadLawUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DetachableBarUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier compares accepted packets only through these displayed finite-prefix rows. It has no coordinate for a host function on completed streams, a selected infinite branch, countable choice, quotient stream equality, an open-cover subcover, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[DetachableBar NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:detachable-bar-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DetachableBarUp$ carrier
$$
D=(F,I,Q,B,A,S,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DetachableBarUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for those rows, fan-boundary exposure through $F$, barred-spread replay through $I$, choice-sequence prefix exposure through $Q$, Baire-cylinder exposure through $B$, spread-law admission through $A$, finite StreamName scheduling through $S$, detachable bar-decision exposure through $W$, regular-rational readback through $R$, Real sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:detachable-bar-carrier}. The projection lists exactly the fan, bar-induction, choice-sequence, Baire, spread-law, finite-window, bar-decision, readback, Real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The bar predicate is detachable because $W$ is a displayed decision row over the finite prefixes already exposed by $F,I,Q,B,A,S$. A consumer cannot ask about an unseen tail or a completed branch before those rows have been displayed.

The analytic handoff is downstream. Rows $R$ and $E$ may be read only after the finite StreamName schedule and bar-decision row have been exposed, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route without adding a choice principle or quotient equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DetachableBar finite-prefix decision]
\label{thm:detachable-bar-finite-prefix-decision}
Every decision exported by an accepted $\DetachableBarUp$ packet is attached to a displayed finite prefix admitted through
$$
\FanTheoremUp,\ \BrouwerBarInductionUp,\ \ChoiceSequenceUp,\ \BaireSpaceUp,\ \SpreadLawUp,\ \StreamNameUp.
$$
If two accepted packets agree componentwise on those prefix rows and on their detachable bar-decision rows under the displayed $\hsame$ transport, then they expose the same accepted-or-refused bar decision to every downstream consumer.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the prefix-facing rows $F,I,Q,B,A,S$. They determine the finite branch window, barred-spread ancestry, choice-sequence answers, Baire cylinder, spread-law admission, and StreamName schedule available to the packet.

The detachable decision row $W$ is indexed only by that displayed window. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport therefore identifies the visible prefix and the visible decision together, so no consumer can distinguish transported packets by asking about an unlisted tail.

Replay through $C$ and provenance through $P,N$ preserve the same finite decision surface. Thus the exported classifier is a finite-prefix decision, not an extensional equality test on completed streams.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DetachableBar real-completion handoff]
\label{thm:detachable-bar-real-completion-handoff}
Every $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, or completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\DetachableBarUp$ packet must factor through the finite route
$$
\StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \DetachableBarUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \RealUp
$$
together with the sibling fan, bar-induction, choice-sequence, Baire, and spread-law rows. The handoff exports finite bar evidence for real-name readback, not countable choice, open-cover compactness, quotient stream equality, selected branch data, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:detachable-bar-namecert-obligations}. They force the StreamName window $S$ and detachable decision row $W$ to be displayed before $R$ or $E$ is available.

The regular-rational row $R$ reads the same finite window, and the Real row $E$ seals only that readback. The fan, bar-induction, choice-sequence, Baire, and spread-law rows remain prerequisites for the prefix to which $W$ attaches.

Since no carrier coordinate stores a selected branch, open-cover subcover, quotient stream equality, countable-choice functional, or ambient completeness theorem, the analytic consumer receives only the displayed finite handoff.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DetachableBar prefix transport]
\label{thm:detachable-bar-prefix-transport}
Let $D=(F,I,Q,B,A,S,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ and $D'=(F',I',Q',B',A',S',W',R',E',H',C',P',N')$ be accepted $\DetachableBarUp$ packets. If the fan, barred-spread, choice-sequence, Baire, spread-law, StreamName, bar-decision, regular-rational, and Real rows transport componentwise under $H,H'$ and replay through $C,C'$, then every consumer sees the same detachable-bar prefix route from $D$ and $D'$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The componentwise transport identifies the prefix-facing rows $F,I,Q,B,A,S$ with $F',I',Q',B',A',S'$. Hence both packets expose the same finite prefix window and the same legal spread or Baire admission surface.

The same transport identifies $W$ with $W'$ over that window. Consequently the accepted-or-refused bar decision is transported together with its prefix, not detached from it as a host predicate on completed streams.

Finally $R,E,P,N$ and $R',E',P',N'$ are replayed through $C,C'$. Any real-facing or completion-facing consumer therefore reads the same transported finite-prefix route.
\end{proof}

The preceding carrier, NameCert, finite-prefix decision, real-completion handoff, and prefix-transport clauses assemble the seed obligation package for $\DetachableBarUp$. The package is exhausted by the finite rows

\begin{proof}
The carrier definition supplies exactly the listed rows, and \autoref{thm:detachable-bar-namecert-obligations} makes them the local NameCert surface.

Thus the package names the finite-prefix bar predicate needed by downstream Real and Completion work while refusing countable choice, open-cover compactness, quotient stream equality, selected branch data, and ambient real completeness.
\end{proof}
