\begin{theorem}[Dedekind-complete ordered-field NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dedekind-complete-ordered-field-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Ordered-field and algebraic-order reads are forced through $F$ and $A$. Located upper-bound and lower-approximation reads are forced through $L$ and the finite window $W$, with the inspected lower-family and upper-bound rows recorded in $G$ before the terminal Real seal $R$ is available.

\end{proof}
