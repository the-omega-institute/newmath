\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompactOpenCoverBoundaryUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-compact-open-cover-boundary-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Bishop--Bridges compactness practice, the
$\CompactOpenCoverBoundaryUp$ packet records the finite BEDC boundary at which
an open-cover compactness reading is refused and replaced by compact-metric,
totally-bounded, finite-net, radius-ledger, and uniform-modulus rows. It sits
beside the compact metric source of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the total-boundedness
surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the finite
$\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ witness of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, the compact-uniform
route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactuniformcontinuity-namecert},
the $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, and the terminal
$\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is
an L10 boundary: it names the finite rows available to later compact-uniform
and Heine--Cantor routes, while it does not name an arbitrary open cover, a
selected subcover, or an ambient compactness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Compact open-cover boundary carrier]
\label{def:compact-open-cover-boundary-carrier}
A $\CompactOpenCoverBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(K,O,N,L,U,H,C,P,Q)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the compact-metric source row. It exposes only the
$\CompactMetricUp$ source data and the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite-probe
discipline used by the route.
\item $O$ is the finite cover-request row. It records the cover-shaped request
as finite $\BHist$ data rather than as an arbitrary open-cover family.
\item $N$ is the $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ witness row, including the centers and
finite membership checks visible to the compact-metric source.
\item $L$ is the Lebesgue-number or radius ledger row. It records the rational
radius lower-bound data consumed after the finite net has been displayed.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row used by compact-uniform and
Heine--Cantor consumers after $K,O,N,L$ have been replayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source,
finite cover request, finite net witness, radius ledger, uniform-modulus
handoff, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the cover-shaped request
through the compact-metric source, finite net witness, radius ledger, and
uniform-modulus handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactOpenCoverBoundaryUp$,
$\CompactMetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$,
$\CompactUniformContinuityUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$,
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $Q$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactOpenCoverBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $K,O,N,L,U,H,C,P,Q$.
It has no coordinate for an arbitrary open-cover object, selected subcover,
choice functional, quotient equality of cover families, host set equality,
semantic compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}
\begin{theorem}[Compact open-cover boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-open-cover-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactOpenCoverBoundaryUp$ carrier
$B=(K,O,N,L,U,H,C,P,Q)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\CompactOpenCoverBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of the
following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the compact-metric source, finite cover request,
finite $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ witness, Lebesgue-number or radius ledger,
$\UniformModulusUp$ handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local name;
\item classifier transport to the common finite surface shared by
$\CompactMetricUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$,
$\CompactUniformContinuityUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, and $\RealUp$;
\item stability of $K,O,N,L,U$ under the componentwise $\hsame$ rows recorded
in $H$;
\item replay exactness requiring each compact-uniform consumer to pass through
$K,O,N,L,U$ in that order;
\item refusal rows excluding arbitrary open covers, selected subcovers, choice
functionals, quotient cover equality, host set equality, semantic compactness,
and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:compact-open-cover-boundary-carrier}. The projection exposes the
compact-metric source, finite cover request, finite epsilon-net witness,
radius ledger, uniform-modulus handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and
local naming rows.

Classifier transport is componentwise. The rows in $H$ move the mathematical
coordinates $K,O,N,L,U$ and the administrative coordinates $C,P,Q$ to the same
finite comparison surface used by the cited compact, net, modulus, and Real
chapters.

Replay exactness is read from $C$. A cover-shaped request reaches the compact
source row, then the finite-net witness, then the radius ledger, and only then
the downstream $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff.

The refusal clauses are exhausted by carrier inspection. Since the carrier has
no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected subcovers, choice, quotient
cover equality, host set equality, semantic compactness, or Real completeness,
none of those data can be exported by the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact open-cover boundary finite-net refusal]
\label{thm:compact-open-cover-boundary-finite-net-refusal}
For every accepted $\CompactOpenCoverBoundaryUp$ carrier
$B=(K,O,N,L,U,H,C,P,Q)$, every compact-uniform or Heine--Cantor read that asks
for open-cover compactness factors instead through the finite route
$$
K,O,N,L \longmapsto U .
$$
Here $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, $O$ is the finite cover-request
row, $N$ is the $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ witness row, $L$ is the
Lebesgue-number or radius ledger, and $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff.
The route may be transported through $H$, replayed through $C$, packaged by
$P$, and named by $Q$, but it exports no arbitrary open cover, selected
subcover, host compactness object, choice schedule, quotient equality of
covers, semantic continuity theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a compact-uniform read of the accepted carrier. By
\autoref{thm:compact-open-cover-boundary-namecert-obligations}, every such
read must expose the carrier rows $K,O,N,L,U,H,C,P,Q$ and no row outside that
finite surface.

The finite cover-request row $O$ is not an open-cover object. It is admitted
only as the cover-shaped request displayed inside the compact-metric source
discipline, so the next available witness row is the finite
$\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ coordinate $N$.

The radius ledger $L$ records the Lebesgue-number or rational-radius lower
bound used after the finite net is visible. The replay row $C$ then sends this
finite radius package to the downstream $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff $U$.

Transport, provenance, and local naming do not add analytic content. The rows
$H,P,Q$ preserve and record the same finite route rather than providing a
selected subcover, host compactness theorem, semantic continuity principle, or
Real completeness row.

Thus the accepted open-cover-shaped request is refused as a general
open-cover compactness read and is replaced by the displayed finite-net route
$K,O,N,L \longmapsto U$.
\end{proof}
