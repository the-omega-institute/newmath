It sits over the located endpoint carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Located interval separation carrier]
\label{def:located-interval-separation-carrier}
A $\LocatedIntervalSeparationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(J_0,J_1,G,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $J_0$ and $J_1$ are the two $\LocatedIntervalUp$ source rows whose exposed endpoint windows are being separated.
\item $G$ is the dyadic positive-gap row. It displays a positive $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance together with the endpoint-side choice saying which visible face of $J_0$ lies at least that gap before the corresponding visible face of $J_1$.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ arithmetic ledger for positivity, subtraction, comparison, and slack retention used by the gap row.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window family for the endpoint reads of $J_0$, the endpoint reads of $J_1$, and the gap-check observations.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the two endpoint windows and the dyadic gap tolerance.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, exposed only after $J_0,J_1,G,D,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source intervals, positive gap, dyadic arithmetic, finite windows, readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from each finite separation request through $J_0,J_1,G,D,W,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedIntervalSeparationUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedIntervalSeparationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $J_0,J_1,G,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for endpoint trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, a host interval order, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located interval separation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-interval-separation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedIntervalSeparationUp$ carrier
$$
S=(J_0,J_1,G,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedIntervalSeparationUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the two displayed $\LocatedIntervalUp$ source rows, positive dyadic gap, dyadic arithmetic ledger, finite windows, regular readback, terminal real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item source-row exposure, requiring every interval endpoint read to be projected from $J_0$ or $J_1$ as in \autoref{def:located-interval-carrier};
\item positive-gap exactness, requiring $G$ to display a positive $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ gap and $D$ to display the arithmetic route that witnesses the retained separation;
\item finite-window coverage, requiring every endpoint and gap-check observation to be named by $W$ and read back by $R$ before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is consumed;
\item non-escape, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to export no endpoint trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, host interval order, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No located interval separation obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:located-interval-separation-carrier}. The displayed tuple $J_0,J_1,G,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$ is the whole packet surface.

The source-row clause first reads the two interval packets through the located interval carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}. Thus every endpoint row available to the separation test is already one of the finite endpoint-window rows of $J_0$ or $J_1$.

The positive-gap clause is then dyadic. Row $G$ names the positive gap, and row $D$ supplies the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ positivity, subtraction, comparison, and slack data needed to replay the displayed separation check.

The window and readback rows control the handoff. Every endpoint observation and every gap-check observation passes through $W$, then through the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$, before the terminal real-name seal $E$ can be consumed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same route. Since the carrier contains no further coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate surface and cannot export trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host interval order, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
