\begin{definition}[MetricFanTheorem carrier]
\label{def:metric-fan-theorem-carrier}
A $\MetricFanTheoremUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(F,B,M,C,R,U,E,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\FanTheoremUp$ finite-bar row, recording a displayed depth that covers the finite branching source.
\item $B$ is the $\FanSpaceUp$ or $\CantorSpaceUp$ prefix row whose inspected binary branches are bounded by $F$.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, exposing only the metric comparisons needed to read branch radii.
\item $C$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ pointwise continuity row, assigning each inspected prefix a displayed local radius and target tolerance response.
\item $R$ is the finite radius ledger, listing the branch radii that survive the fan-depth inspection.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row obtained by folding the finite radius ledger into one displayed modulus depth.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only for the finite metric and modulus observations already exposed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for fan, prefix, metric, continuity, radius, modulus, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a metric fan request through $F,B,M,C,R,U,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetricFanTheoremUp$, $\FanTheoremUp$, $\FanSpaceUp$, $\CantorSpaceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetricFanTheoremUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate asserts that these rows form one finite metric fan history. Its classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed fan depth, inspected prefix rows, metric observations, pointwise continuity rows, finite radius ledger, uniform-modulus handoff, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MetricFanTheorem NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metric-fan-theorem-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetricFanTheoremUp$ carrier $T=(F,B,M,C,R,U,E,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MetricFanTheoremUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite fan-depth admission, prefix-row exposure, metric comparison exposure, pointwise continuity exposure, finite radius ledgering, uniform-modulus handoff, terminal Real seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No NameCert obligation is stored outside those finite rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metric-fan-theorem-carrier}. The rows $F$ and $B$ are the only fan-side rows, so fan admission and branch inspection are confined to the displayed finite depth and prefix surface.

Read the analytic rows next. The metric row $M$ supplies the branch-distance comparisons, $C$ supplies pointwise continuity data at the inspected prefixes, and $R$ records the finite list of radii that remain after the fan-depth inspection.

Finally use $U,E,H,K,P,N$. The uniform-modulus handoff, Real seal, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local name preserve the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no further coordinate, open-cover compactness, host uniform continuity, selected infinite branches, quotient stream equality, countable choice, and ambient Real completeness cannot become local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
