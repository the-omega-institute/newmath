\begin{theorem}[Bohr-Mollerup NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bohr-mollerup-namecert-obligations}

$$

Finally attach $K,H,C,P,N$. These rows stabilize the classifier, transport the same finite coordinates, replay the displayed route, record provenance, and name the local certificate. \end{proof}
