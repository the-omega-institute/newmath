\begin{theorem}[LowerUpperRealSeparator NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lower-upper-real-separator-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LowerUpperRealSeparatorUp$ carrier
$$
G=(L,U,S,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LowerUpperRealSeparatorUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the lower ledger, upper ledger, separator row, stream window, regular rational readback, Real seal, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row;
\item componentwise classifier compatibility, requiring accepted comparison to be read row by row from $L,U,S,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$;
\item lower/upper compatibility, requiring the separator row $S$ to display the boundary by which lower and upper reads remain ordered under the same windowed request;
\item Real-route stability, requiring the terminal read through $E$ to preserve the preceding $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows;
\item separator-ledger nonescape, requiring the provenance and local NameCert rows to source no trichotomy, quotient equality, selected supremum, countable-choice, or host-completeness coordinate.
\end{enumerate}
No separator obligation is stored outside these finite rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lower-upper-real-separator-carrier}. The visible one-sided real coordinates are exactly the lower ledger $L$, the upper ledger $U$, the separator row $S$, the stream window $W$, the regular rational readback $R$, and the Real seal $E$.

Classifier compatibility is componentwise. A transported packet keeps each ledger on the same displayed field, and replay through $C$ can only revisit the rows already named by $L,U,S,W,R,E$.

Lower/upper compatibility is supplied by the separator row. It is read after the one-sided ledgers and before the readback window, so a consumer cannot replace it by a host trichotomy decision or by an unlisted supremum selector.

Finally inspect $P$ and $N$. Their source list contains only the displayed packet dependencies and local naming data. Since no coordinate stores quotient real equality, choice, selected suprema, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
