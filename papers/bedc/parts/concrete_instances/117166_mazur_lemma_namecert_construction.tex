\begin{theorem}[Mazur lemma NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:mazur-lemma-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:mazur-lemma-carrier}. The normed source $X$, sequence row $N$, convex row $C$, tail row $T$, and weight ledger $W$ are the only rows from which a convex approximant can be built.
