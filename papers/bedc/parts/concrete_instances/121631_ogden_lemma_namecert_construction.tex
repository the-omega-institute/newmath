\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\OgdenLemmaUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-ogden-lemma-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Hopcroft--Ullman and Sipser, the $\OgdenLemmaUp$ packet records the
marked-position strengthening of the context-free pumping lemma as finite
NameCert data. It sits beside the grammar carrier of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-contextfreegrammar-namecert} and the
unmarked pumping route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-context-free-pumping-lemma-namecert}; its role
is to expose which marked terminal positions force the repeated nonterminal
route, not to import host language equality, infinite parse trees, grammar
quotients, or an ambient semantic pumping theorem.

\begin{definition}[Ogden lemma carrier]
\label{def:ogden-lemma-carrier}
An $\OgdenLemmaUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(G,W,M,D,A,E,I,Y,H,C,R,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the finite $\ContextFreeGrammarUp$ grammar packet, including the
terminal alphabet, nonterminal carrier, start row, production ledger, and
terminal-yield window.
\item $W$ is the terminal word row, read as a $\ListUp$ spine with a displayed
length bound supplied by $\NatUp$.
\item $M$ is the finite marked-position ledger over $W$, recording only the
displayed positions chosen for the Ogden read.
\item $D$ is the finite derivation ledger whose prefix rows are read through
the grammar packet and whose marked leaves are aligned with $M$.
\item $A$ is the repeated-nonterminal exposure row forced by the marked
derivation route.
\item $E$ is the decomposition row naming the five displayed word pieces and
the marked-position split attached to them.
\item $I$ is the pump-index row.
\item $Y$ is the yielded-word row obtained by replaying the marked pumping
route at the displayed index.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the grammar, word,
marked-position ledger, derivation ledger, repeated-nonterminal row,
decomposition, pump index, yielded word, replay, provenance, and local naming
rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$G,W,M,D,A,E,I,Y,H,R,Q,N$.
\item $R$ is the sibling route to the $\ContextFreePumpingLemmaUp$ packet,
forgetting only the marked-position ledger while preserving the displayed
derivation-prefix route.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\OgdenLemmaUp$,
$\ContextFreeGrammarUp$, $\ContextFreePumpingLemmaUp$, $\ListUp$, $\NatUp$,
$\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\OgdenLemmaUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through
$G,W,M,D,A,E,I,Y,H,C,R,Q,N$. The carrier contains no host language set,
grammar quotient, infinite derivation tree, selected parse tree, or ambient
semantic pumping principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Ogden lemma NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:ogden-lemma-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\OgdenLemmaUp$ carrier
$$
O=(G,W,M,D,A,E,I,Y,H,C,R,Q,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\OgdenLemmaUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier
admission for the grammar packet, terminal word row, marked-position ledger,
finite derivation ledger, repeated-nonterminal exposure row, decomposition,
pump index, yielded word, transport, replay, sibling pumping route,
provenance, and local naming. The marked-position ledger $M$ must be displayed
before $A$ and $E$ are read, and the sibling route $R$ may forget only that
ledger after the finite derivation-prefix path has been exposed.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.OgdenLemmaUp.OgdenLemmaCarrier\_namecert\_obligations}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:ogden-lemma-carrier}. The rows
$G,W,M,D,A,E,I,Y$ are the whole formal-language surface, while $H,C,R,Q,N$
transport, replay, compare with the unmarked pumping sibling, source, and name
the same packet.

First read the grammar and terminal word rows. The finite production ledger
and terminal-yield window of $G$ supply the derivation-prefix route, and $W$
supplies the bounded terminal spine on which marked positions can be displayed.

Next expose $M$ before the pumping witness. The repeated-nonterminal row $A$
and decomposition row $E$ are accepted only as reads of the marked derivation
ledger $D$, so no unmarked host pumping argument can replace them.

Finally replay the chosen pump index through $I$ and $Y$, then package the
route through $H,C,R,Q,N$. The sibling row $R$ erases only the marked-position
annotation after the finite derivation-prefix data has been read, and no
carrier coordinate stores a host language set, grammar quotient, infinite
derivation tree, selected parse tree, or ambient semantic pumping principle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Ogden lemma marked-position pumping route]
\label{thm:ogden-lemma-marked-position-pumping-route}
Let
$$
O=(G,W,M,D,A,E,I,Y,H,C,R,Q,N)
$$
be an accepted $\OgdenLemmaUp$ carrier. Every marked pumping read of $O$
factors through the finite route
$$
G \longmapsto W \longmapsto M \longmapsto D \longmapsto A
\longmapsto E \longmapsto I \longmapsto Y
$$
before it reaches transport, replay, provenance, local naming, or the
$\ContextFreePumpingLemmaUp$ sibling route. A consumer may therefore recover a
standard pumping-lemma surface only through the displayed forgetful row $R$,
not by replacing the marked route with host language equality, quotient grammar
semantics, an infinite parse tree, or an ambient membership theorem.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.OgdenLemmaUp.OgdenLemmaCarrier\_marked\_position\_pumping\_route}
\begin{proof}
Begin with \autoref{thm:ogden-lemma-namecert-obligations}. It orders the
formal-language rows so that the grammar packet and terminal word are read
before the marked-position ledger and derivation ledger.

The marked ledger then controls the pumping witness. The repeated-nonterminal
row $A$ and decomposition row $E$ are downstream of $M$ and $D$, and the pump
index $I$ can be replayed to the yielded row $Y$ only along that displayed
finite path.

The structural rows do not add a second pumping argument. Componentwise
transport $H$ and continuation replay $C$ preserve the same path, while $Q$ and
$N$ source and name it. The sibling row $R$ gives the only route to the
unmarked $\ContextFreePumpingLemmaUp$ surface, and that route forgets the mark
ledger only after the derivation-prefix witness has been displayed.
\end{proof}
