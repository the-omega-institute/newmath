\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ChineseRemainderUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-chinese-remainder-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Gauss and the standard algebraic Chinese remainder theorem, the $\ChineseRemainderUp$ packet records finite congruence ledgers over displayed integer, ring, ideal, prime, coprime, and unary-index rows. It sits after the integer carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-int-namecert}, the operation rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ring-namecert}, the modulus rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ideal-namecert}, the prime and coprime ledger rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-prime-namecert}, and the finite index discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nat-namecert}. The packet names only the displayed residue computation route; it is not a quotient-ring primitive, a selected representative oracle, an ambient ideal factorization theorem, or a host-side modular arithmetic black box.

\begin{definition}[Chinese remainder carrier]
\label{def:chinese-remainder-carrier}
A $\ChineseRemainderUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(I,A,M,C,U,L,V,T,H,Q,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\IntUp$ source row for the displayed residue values and reconstructed integer candidate.
\item $A$ is the $\RingUp$ operation row used for addition, multiplication, subtraction, and unit reads during recombination.
\item $M$ is the finite modulus row, read through $\IdealUp$, listing the displayed ideals or integer-modulus rows against which congruences are checked.
\item $C$ is the $\PrimeUp$ and coprime ledger row, recording the pairwise coprimality witnesses required by the displayed moduli.
\item $U$ is the $\NatUp$ finite index row, naming the inspected modulus positions and the finite recursion order.
\item $L$ is the congruence ledger, storing each residue row, its modulus row, and the local proof that the candidate agrees with the residue modulo that row.
\item $V$ is the recombination row, displaying the finite Bezout or unit coefficients used to assemble the candidate from the listed residues.
\item $T$ is the transport row, recording the componentwise $\hsame$ movement of residues, moduli, coefficients, congruence checks, and reconstructed candidate data.
\item $H$ is the replay row, storing displayed $\Cont$ replay from the residue request through $I,A,M,C,U,L,V,T$.
\item $Q$ is the classifier row, comparing accepted packets only by the displayed residue ledger, coprime ledger, recombination row, transport, and replay data.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ChineseRemainderUp$, $\IntUp$, $\RingUp$, $\IdealUp$, $\PrimeUp$, $\NatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChineseRemainderUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier has no coordinate for quotient-ring equality, a hidden representative selector, an infinite family of moduli, a non-displayed ideal decomposition, or a detached host proof of the Chinese remainder theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Chinese remainder NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:chinese-remainder-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ChineseRemainderUp$ carrier
$$
R=(I,A,M,C,U,L,V,T,H,Q,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ChineseRemainderUp}$ surface consists exactly of integer residue admission, ring-operation admission, finite modulus-row admission, coprime ledger admission, unary index budgeting, congruence ledger exposure, recombination coefficient exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, classifier comparison through displayed rows, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:chinese-remainder-carrier}. The rows $I,A,M,C,U$ expose the arithmetic source, operation surface, modulus list, coprime witnesses, and finite index budget before any residue computation can be read.

Read the ledger and recombination rows next. The row $L$ stores the finite congruence checks, and $V$ stores the displayed coefficient data used to assemble the reconstructed candidate; neither row can be replaced by a quotient-ring equality or a selected representative.

Finally $T,H,Q,P,N$ transport, replay, compare, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier contains no other coordinate, these rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Chinese remainder congruence ledger]
\label{thm:chinese-remainder-congruence-ledger}
Let $R=(I,A,M,C,U,L,V,T,H,Q,P,N)$ be an accepted $\ChineseRemainderUp$ carrier. For every modulus position displayed by $U$, the reconstructed candidate named by $I$ and $V$ agrees with the corresponding residue row of $L$ modulo the corresponding modulus row of $M$, and the agreement is replayed through the same coprime ledger $C$ and transport row $T$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct over the finite index row $U$. In the base case, the first displayed modulus row is read from $M$, its residue row is read from $L$, and the recombination row $V$ supplies the coefficient data whose local congruence is checked by the ring operations in $A$.

For the induction step, assume the already-inspected prefix has been replayed through $H$ and transported by $T$. The next index exposes its modulus row and coprime witnesses through $M$ and $C$, and the next congruence row of $L$ checks the reconstructed candidate against that modulus using only displayed operations.

Because the induction ranges only over the finite row list, it cannot introduce quotient-ring representatives, an infinite modulus family, or an external CRT oracle. The replay row $H$ and provenance row $P$ then package the same finite congruence ledger as the public local certificate.
\end{proof}
