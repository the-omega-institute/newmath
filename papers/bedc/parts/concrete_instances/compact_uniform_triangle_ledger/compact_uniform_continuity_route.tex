\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform triangle ledger compact-uniform-continuity route]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-triangle-ledger-compact-uniform-continuity-route}
Every $\CompactUniformContinuityUp$ or $\CompactUniformContinuityModulusUp$ consumer of an accepted $\CompactUniformTriangleLedgerUp$ packet must read the same $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net row, $\ContinuousMapUp$ pointwise-modulus row, $\MetricSpaceUp$ source and target rows, $\RatUp$ tolerance row, $\FiniteNetMinimumFoldUp$ lower-bound row, $\MetricTriangleUniformEstimateUp$ triangle row, $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows before it uses the uniform-continuity output.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier from \autoref{def:compact-uniform-triangle-ledger-carrier}. The rows $K,X,Y,F,\varepsilon,B,R,\mu,S,T,U,H,C,P,N$ are the only displayed route from compact finite-net data and pointwise modulus data to the uniform-modulus handoff.

Apply \autoref{thm:compact-uniform-triangle-ledger-namecert-obligations} and \autoref{thm:compact-uniform-triangle-ledger-handoff}. The obligation theorem forces the consumer to expose compact, continuous-map, metric, rational, finite-fold, triangle, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; the handoff theorem identifies the downstream output as the $\UniformModulusUp$ row produced by the same route.

Thus a $\CompactUniformContinuityUp$ consumer, as in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactuniformcontinuity-namecert}, and a $\CompactUniformContinuityModulusUp$ consumer, as in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-continuity-modulus-namecert}, both inherit the same ledger route before reading their compact-uniform-continuity surface.
\end{proof}
