Following Alexandroff and Urysohn's split-interval construction and Kelley-style order topology, the $\DoubleArrowSpaceUp$ packet records the lexicographically ordered two-copy real interval as finite NameCert data. It sits beside the endpoint seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the order-topology surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-order-topology-namecert}, the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the order-facing row family of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-order-namecert}, and the observable-open classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sierpinski-space-namecert}. \begin{definition}[DoubleArrowSpace carrier]
\label{def:double-arrow-space-carrier}
A $\DoubleArrowSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(R,T,L,W,O,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ endpoint row, carrying the displayed left and right real endpoints currently inspected by the packet.
\item $T$ is the split-tag row, with each inspected endpoint marked by a left or right copy of the interval.
\item $L$ is the lexicographic order row: real endpoints are compared first, and equal endpoints are then compared by the split tag.
\item $W$ is the generated order-topology interval-window row, listing the finite open rays and bounded order intervals actually exposed to topology consumers.
\item $O$ is the observable-open row, read through $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, that records truth values for membership in the displayed interval windows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoint rows, split tags, lexicographic order rows, interval windows, observable-open truth rows, continuation rows, provenance rows, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from $R,T$ to $L$, from $L$ to the generated windows $W$, and from $W$ to the observable-open row $O$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DoubleArrowSpaceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RealOrderTopologyUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\OrderUp$, $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DoubleArrowSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,T,L,W,O,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host point equality on a classical split interval, open-cover compactness, a quotient by adjacent endpoints, selected suprema, or an ambient topology.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[DoubleArrowSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:double-arrow-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DoubleArrowSpaceUp$ packet $D=(R,T,L,W,O,H,C,P,N)$, the local NameCert surface consists exactly of endpoint admission through $\RealUp$, split-tag admission, lexicographic order admission through $\OrderUp$, generated order-topology window exposure through $\RealOrderTopologyUp$ and $\TopologyUp$, observable-open truth exposure through $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row. A consumer may read a double-arrow open only after those finite rows are displayed; it cannot replace them by a host split-interval topology, quotient point equality, open-cover compactness, selected cuts, or ambient real-order completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:double-arrow-space-carrier}. The rows $R,T,L,W,O,H,C,P,N$ are the whole accepted packet, so every double-arrow read must expose endpoints, split tags, lexicographic order, interval windows, and observable-open truth before the structural rows are consumed.

The order-topology route is finite. The row $L$ is built from the displayed real endpoint comparison and split-tag comparison; $W$ then lists only the generated order intervals currently inspected; and $O$ records their observable truth values through $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$.

The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores host point equality, compactness, selected cuts, quotient identification, or an unrestricted open-set lattice, none of those objects can appear among the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DoubleArrowSpace order-topology route]
\label{thm:double-arrow-space-order-topology-route}

$$
\RealUp,\ T,\ L,\ W,\ O,\ H,\ C,\ P,\ N .
$$
The endpoint row supplies the real comparison boundary, the split-tag row separates the two copies at equal endpoints, the lexicographic order row supplies the order-facing classifier, and the interval-window row supplies the generated order-topology opens. The route exports no host compactness theorem, quotient equality between adjacent split points, selected endpoint cuts, or ambient open-set lattice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Thus every order-topology consumer receives the double-arrow data through the finite route above. No additional host topology, quotient point equality, compactness proof, selected cut, or open-set lattice is available from the carrier.
\end{proof}
