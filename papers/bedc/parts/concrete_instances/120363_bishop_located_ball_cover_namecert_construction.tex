\begin{definition}[Bishop located ball cover carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-ball-cover-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedBallCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(M,Q,W,R,E,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the displayed $\MetricUp$ or completion-facing metric source whose ball membership comparisons are inspected.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius ledger, carrying the positive dyadic radii, tolerance refinements, and overlap budgets for the cover rows.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ observation window through which points, centers, and requested radii are exposed.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational distance and radius comparisons used by the locatedness test.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the dyadic radius row, finite window row, and regular rational readback have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the located cover ledger. For each inspected window it names a finite ball entry, its center row, its dyadic radius, and the comparison showing that the inspected point is either inside the displayed ball or separated from the complementary inspected window.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, radius ledger, finite windows, readback, seal, located cover ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a ball-cover request through $M,Q,W,R,E,L,H$.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedBallCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,Q,W,R,E,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary open cover, a host compactness theorem, a selected subcover, countable choice, quotient real equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located ball cover radius locality]
\label{thm:bishop-located-ball-cover-radius-locality}

A consumer may refine a displayed radius by a finite dyadic tolerance row, but it cannot replace $Q$ by a global real-order decision, trichotomy branch, quotient real equality, or ambient completeness principle.

\begin{proof}

The regular rational readback $R$ interprets the dyadic radius comparisons before the located cover ledger $L$ is read. Therefore every radius-facing consumer receives a finite dyadic route rather than an ambient real-order oracle.

The seal $E$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ remain downstream of $Q,W,R,L$. They preserve the displayed route and add no trichotomy, quotient equality, selected subcover, choice principle, or ambient completeness witness.
