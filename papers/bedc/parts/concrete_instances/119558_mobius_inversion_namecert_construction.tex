It sits after the relation skeletons of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-preorder-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poset-namecert}, the meet-and-join comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lattice-namecert}, the finite enumeration discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finset-namecert}, and the membership-facing carrier route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-setlike-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Mobius inversion carrier]
\label{def:mobius-inversion-carrier}
A $\MobiusInversionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(P,I,Z,K,W,Q,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the finite poset source row, recording the $\PosetUp$ comparison data and the underlying $\PreorderUp$ reflexive-transitive ledger.
\item $I$ is the interval ledger. For every displayed comparable pair $x\leq y$, it lists the finite interval members as a $\FinSetUp$ row with membership readback through $\SetLikeUp$.
\item $Z$ is the zeta row, assigning the displayed unit value to every accepted interval entry in $I$ and refusing non-comparable pairs.
\item $K$ is the Mobius row, recording the recursively checked alternating interval coefficients attached to the same finite interval ledger.
\item $W$ is the finite convolution window, listing exactly the intermediate $z$ with $x\leq z\leq y$ that may be used in a zeta-Mobius product.
\item $Q$ is the inverse-check ledger, recording both finite convolution checks: the zeta-then-Mobius and Mobius-then-zeta rows collapse to the displayed diagonal row on each accepted interval.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the poset source, interval ledger, zeta row, Mobius row, convolution window, inverse-check ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an inversion request through $P,I,Z,K,W,Q,H,G,N$.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MobiusInversionUp$, $\PreorderUp$, $\PosetUp$, $\LatticeUp$, $\FinSetUp$, $\SetLikeUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MobiusInversionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $P,I,Z,K,W,Q,H,C,G,N$. The carrier has no coordinate for an infinite incidence algebra, host matrix inversion, selected global inverse, unbounded summation, quotient equality of functions, or ambient algebraic completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Mobius inversion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:mobius-inversion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MobiusInversionUp$ carrier $M=(P,I,Z,K,W,Q,H,C,G,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MobiusInversionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the finite poset source, interval ledger, zeta row, Mobius row, finite convolution window, inverse-check ledger, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming rows. A consumer may read inversion data only after it displays the route
$$
P,I \longmapsto Z,K \longmapsto W,Q \longmapsto H,C,G,N .
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:mobius-inversion-carrier}. The poset source $P$ supplies the comparison row, while $I$ supplies the finite interval members through the Finset and SetLike routes.

The algebra-facing rows are then read from the same interval ledger. The zeta row $Z$ and the Mobius row $K$ attach coefficients only to displayed comparable pairs, and $W$ lists the finite intermediates allowed in a convolution.

Finally inspect $Q,H,C,G,N$. The inverse-check ledger $Q$ records the two finite convolution identities, and the structural rows transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no accepted row stores infinite incidence algebra data, host matrix inversion, selected inverse matrices, unbounded sums, quotient equality of functions, or ambient completion, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Mobius inversion incidence ledger]
\label{thm:mobius-inversion-incidence-ledger}
Every accepted $\MobiusInversionUp$ incidence consumer receives the inverse relation only through the displayed finite ledger
$$

\begin{proof}

Use the interval ledger $I$ and the convolution window $W$. They list the only intermediate elements available to the consumer, with membership read through the Finset and SetLike rows. The zeta and Mobius rows are therefore attached to the same finite interval.

\end{proof}
