It sits after the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the Cauchy product source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyproduct-namecert}, the regular product route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchyproduct-namecert}, and the product-window route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-product-commutativity-namecert}. \begin{definition}[CauchyProductCompletionSeal carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-product-completion-seal-carrier}

$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ and $R$ are the left and right $\RegSeqRatUp$ source rows admitted for product formation.
\item $P$ is the $\CauchyProductUp$ product-source row.
\item $B$ is the finite product-budget row exposing which tail windows and dyadic tolerances are consumed.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ product-tolerance row.
\item $T$ is the dyadic tail-stability ledger for the product row.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the source rows through product budget, dyadic tolerance, tail stability, and seal handoff.

\begin{theorem}[CauchyProductCompletionSeal classifier]
\label{thm:cauchy-product-completion-seal-classifier}
Two accepted $\CauchyProductCompletionSealUp$ carriers are classified by the displayed rows $L,R,P,B,Q,T,S,H,C,K,N$. A classifier comparison may identify the product-completion seal only when the source regular names, product-source row, product budget, dyadic tolerance, tail-stability ledger, seal handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows are all compared through the same finite packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-product-completion-seal-carrier}. The classifier-visible data are exactly the listed rows.

The structural rows $H,C,K,N$ preserve and name the same source, product, budget, tolerance, stability, and seal coordinates. Since the carrier has no quotient-stream or host-real-equality coordinate, no classifier comparison can use one.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
