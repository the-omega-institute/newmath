\begin{proof}

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for selected pointwise limits, countable-choice enumeration, host equality, or ambient completeness, the five listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
