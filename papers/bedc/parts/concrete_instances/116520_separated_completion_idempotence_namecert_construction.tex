\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-separated-completion-idempotence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on completion of separated metric spaces, the $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which an already separated completion is recognized as fixed by the completion classifier. It sits between the completion interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the metric-completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, and the functorial completion handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionfunctor-namecert}. The packet names the idempotence boundary for separated completions; it is not a quotient-completion equality, a selected representative, countable choice, host real equality, or ambient completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Separated completion idempotence carrier]
\label{def:separated-completion-idempotence-carrier}
A $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(C,S,M,F,R,E,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\CompletionUp$ source row naming the completion-facing request.
\item $S$ is the separated-classifier row, recording that zero-distance comparisons are already identified by the displayed separated completion surface.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ row through which the same request reaches a metric-completion consumer.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$ functorial handoff row.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback row attached to the inspected completion windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the separated classifier, metric completion route, functorial handoff, and regular readback have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $C,S,M,F,R,E,T,P,N$.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a second completion request back through the same separated classifier route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier for accepted packets compares only the displayed rows $C,S,M,F,R,E,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, host equality of completed points, countable choice, or an ambient completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Separated completion idempotence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:separated-completion-idempotence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$ carrier $I=(C,S,M,F,R,E,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the completion source row, separated-classifier row, metric-completion route, Cauchy-completion functorial handoff, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. The second completion read must return to the same separated classifier $S$ before any $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, or $\RealUp$ consumer can be served.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:separated-completion-idempotence-carrier}. The rows $C,S,M,F,R,E,H,T,P,N$ are the complete accepted inventory.

The separated classifier is the lock. A second completion-facing request first reads $C$ and $S$; only after the zero-distance classifier has been exposed may it continue to the metric route $M$ and functorial handoff $F$.

The readback and terminal seal remain downstream of that route. Rows $R$ and $E$ record regular rational windows and the Real seal after the separated completion boundary has been displayed, while $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet.

Since no other coordinate is present, the local certificate cannot export quotient completion equality, a selected representative, countable choice, host equality of completed points, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Separated completion RegSeqRat route]
\label{thm:separated-completion-idempotence-real-regseqrat-route}
Every $\RealUp$-facing or $\RegSeqRatUp$-facing read exported by an accepted $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
C,S,M,F \longmapsto R \longmapsto E .
$$
Thus applying completion again to an already separated completion exposes the same separated classifier before the regular rational readback and terminal Real seal are available. The packet supplies idempotence as a finite NameCert route, not as quotient equality of completion objects or a selected completed representative.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a consumer of $R$ or $E$. By \autoref{thm:separated-completion-idempotence-namecert-obligations}, the consumer first opens the completion source row $C$ and the separated classifier row $S$.

The metric and functorial rows $M$ and $F$ keep the request on the sibling completion route. They show that the second completion read is served by the displayed metric-completion and Cauchy-completion handoff, not by an ambient equality of completion objects.

Only then can $R$ and $E$ be read. The regular rational row records the inspected windows, and the Real seal remains terminal to those windows. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve this route without adding a selected representative or choice principle.
\end{proof}
