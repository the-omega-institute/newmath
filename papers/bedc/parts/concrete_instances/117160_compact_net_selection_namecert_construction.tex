\begin{definition}[CompactNetSelection carrier]
\label{def:compact-net-selection-carrier}
A $\CompactNetSelectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K_0$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net source row, carrying the compact metric source, the displayed center list, the net radius, and the finite coverage witness table.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ distance and hit row, recording the inspected point names, center names, distance comparisons, and hit witnesses used by the selected net.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row exposing exactly the finite observations of source points and centers read by the compact-net query.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational distance and tolerance data opened by $M$ and $S$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for distance or tolerance comparisons, visible only after $K_0,M,S,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, metric hit ledger, stream windows, regular readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a requested finite-net read through the center list, hit witnesses, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, and Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactNetSelectionUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactNetSelectionUp}$ row.

The classifier accepts a packet only when the center list is displayed in $K_0$, every hit row is read through $M$, every observation window is exposed by $S$, every rational tolerance readback factors through $Q$, and every Real-facing comparison reaches $E$ after the finite rows are visible. \end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompactNetSelection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-net-selection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactNetSelectionUp$ carrier $K=(K_0,M,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactNetSelectionUp}$ obligations consist exactly of carrier admission for the compact finite-net source, metric distance and hit ledger, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows; center-list visibility through $K_0$; hit-row exactness through $M$; finite-window exactness through $S$; rational readback through $Q$; Real-seal nonescape through $E$; and preservation of the same route through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-net-selection-carrier}. Its visible rows are precisely $K_0,M,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N$, so every local obligation must be read from one of those rows.

The compact finite-net source row $K_0$ displays the center list and coverage table before the metric hit row is inspected. The metric row $M$ then records the distance comparisons and hit witnesses attached to those displayed centers.

The Real-facing side is downstream. StreamName windows $S$ expose the finite observations, RegSeqRat readback $Q$ records the rational tolerance data, and the Real seal $E$ is terminal.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no further carrier coordinate exists, host selectors, open-cover compactness, choice-selected centers, quotient equality, selected limits, and ambient Real completeness cannot be local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
