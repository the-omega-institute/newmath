\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompactDerivativeBoundUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges on differentiable functions over compact intervals, the $\CompactDerivativeBoundUp$ packet records the finite derivative-control surface that a compact-uniform-continuity consumer may inspect before a Lipschitz or mean-value packet reads any bound. It sits over the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the continuous-map rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the derivative carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-derivative-namecert}, the uniform-modulus route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a compact interval derivative-bound interface as displayed rows, not as a general mean-value theorem or a host compactness principle.

\begin{definition}[CompactDerivativeBound carrier]
\label{def:compact-derivative-bound-carrier}
A $\CompactDerivativeBoundUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(K,F,G,U,N_r,R,E,H,C,P,Q)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ compact interval source row, or an equivalent compact interval source row already admitted through the compact metric NameCert surface.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row for the displayed function whose derivative is being bounded.
\item $G$ is the finite $\DerivativeUp$ observation row, listing the derivative endpoints and local difference-quotient windows visible to the packet.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ row that synchronizes the compact interval source with the continuous-map and derivative observation windows.
\item $N_r$ is the finite compact-net radius row, storing the selected net radii, local derivative-control windows, and rational comparison budgets.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for those rational budgets.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the displayed derivative bound.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,F,G,U,N_r,R,E,C,P,Q$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a derivative-bound request through the compact source, continuous-map row, derivative row, uniform-modulus row, compact-net radius row, regular rational readback, real seal, transport, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactDerivativeBoundUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\DerivativeUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $Q$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactDerivativeBoundUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a general mean-value theorem, open-cover compactness, choice-selected extrema, quotient equality of functions, a host derivative norm, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompactDerivativeBound NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-derivative-bound-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactDerivativeBoundUp$ carrier
$$
D=(K,F,G,U,N_r,R,E,H,C,P,Q),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactDerivativeBoundUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact interval source admission through $K$, continuous-map exposure through $F$, derivative-observation exposure through $G$, uniform-modulus synchronization through $U$, finite compact-net radius accounting through $N_r$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, $\RealUp$ seal nonescape through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $Q$.
\end{theorem}
