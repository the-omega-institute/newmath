\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-convergence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kuratowski and Painleve--Kuratowski hyperspace convergence, the $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$ packet records finite BEDC convergence data for displayed closed-set sequences in a metric hyperspace. It sits over the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the located-set source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedset-namecert}, the projection boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metricprojection-namecert}, the compact metric hyperspace surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-metric-hyperspace-namecert}, the Kuratowski hyperspace carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-hyperspace-namecert}, and the hit--miss convergence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-painleve-kuratowski-convergence-namecert}. The packet names only displayed lower-hit, upper-miss, projection, and Real-seal rows; it does not quotient closed sets, select hyperspace representatives, invoke choice, or import ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Kuratowski convergence carrier]
\label{def:kuratowski-convergence-carrier}
A $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(M,A,Q,H,R,L,U,W,E,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row carrying the finite distance observations used by all hit, miss, and projection reads.
\item $A$ is the $\LocatedSetUp$ closed-set source row, naming each inspected set by located-radius observations rather than by a host powerset.
\item $Q$ is the $\MetricProjectionUp$ boundary row that supplies point-to-set distance and nearest-boundary evidence only through displayed projection data.
\item $H$ is the $\CompactMetricHyperspaceUp$ row carrying finite Hausdorff and Vietoris windows for compact hyperspace reads.
\item $R$ is the $\KuratowskiHyperspaceUp$ row that compares hyperspace and Kuratowski-profile classifiers through finite metric data.
\item $L$ is the lower-hit ledger: each entry names a displayed source point, a finite stage window, and a tolerance row witnessing that the inspected closed-set sequence meets the requested neighborhood.
\item $U$ is the upper-miss ledger: each entry names a finite cover or finite complement test witnessing that, beyond the displayed stage window, no inspected set escapes the requested metric neighborhood.
\item $W$ is the $\PainleveKuratowskiConvergenceUp$ hit--miss window row tying $L$ and $U$ to the sibling convergence classifier.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ seal row used by Real and completion consumers to read tolerances without quotienting closed sets.
\item $T,C,P,N$ are respectively componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert_{\KuratowskiConvergenceUp}$ naming row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an ambient powerset of closed subsets, quotient equality of closed sets, a selected limit set, countable choice, or an untracked hyperspace completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Kuratowski convergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kuratowski-convergence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$ carrier
$$
K=(M,A,Q,H,R,L,U,W,E,T,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\KuratowskiConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric source admission, located-set admission, metric-projection boundary admission, compact metric hyperspace admission, Kuratowski hyperspace profile comparison, lower-hit ledger admission, upper-miss ledger admission, Painleve--Kuratowski hit--miss synchronization, Real and regular-rational tolerance sealing, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every convergence-facing consumer must read through these rows before it may inspect a limit claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:kuratowski-convergence-carrier}. The metric source, located-set source, projection row, compact hyperspace row, Kuratowski profile row, lower ledger, upper ledger, hit--miss window, and Real seal are the only mathematical coordinates.

The lower-hit and upper-miss obligations are separate rows. A lower request reads a point, stage window, and tolerance from $L$; an upper request reads a finite cover or complement test from $U$. The synchronization row $W$ is the only place where those two ledgers are compared.

The structural rows $T,C,P,N$ preserve and name that same route. Since the carrier has no powerset, quotient, selected-limit, or choice coordinate, no local NameCert obligation can be stored outside the listed finite rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kuratowski convergence metric hyperspace handoff]
\label{thm:kuratowski-convergence-metric-hyperspace-handoff}
Every compact-hyperspace or projection-facing read exported by an accepted $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$ carrier factors through the displayed route
$$
\MetricUp,\LocatedSetUp,\MetricProjectionUp,\CompactMetricHyperspaceUp,\KuratowskiHyperspaceUp,\PainleveKuratowskiConvergenceUp .
$$
The handoff exposes finite Hausdorff windows, Kuratowski-profile comparisons, and projection-distance rows before any Real or completion consumer reads the convergence packet. It exports no quotient closed-set equality, selected hyperspace representative, selected limit set, or ambient compactness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a compact-hyperspace read. The carrier forces the consumer to inspect the metric source $M$, located-set row $A$, projection boundary $Q$, compact hyperspace row $H$, and Kuratowski profile row $R$ before the convergence windows $L,U,W$ are available.

The lower-hit ledger and upper-miss ledger then provide the finite convergence evidence. Their comparison is read only through $W$, which is already tied to the sibling Painleve--Kuratowski hit--miss surface.

Finally the tolerance readback is sealed by $E$ and replayed through $T,C,P,N$. Thus the handoff is a finite BEDC route through the named sibling anchors, not a route through quotient closed sets or host hyperspace objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kuratowski convergence Real-seal nonescape]
\label{thm:kuratowski-convergence-real-seal-nonescape}
Every $\RealUp$ or $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance read of an accepted $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$ carrier is downstream of the lower-hit ledger $L$, the upper-miss ledger $U$, and the hit--miss synchronization row $W$. The Real seal records only the displayed tolerance values used by those finite windows; it cannot be used to recover a host limit set, quotient equality of closed sets, countable-choice schedule, or ambient hyperspace completion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the Real-facing coordinate $E$. By \autoref{def:kuratowski-convergence-carrier}, it is attached to the convergence packet only after the lower-hit and upper-miss ledgers have been displayed and synchronized.

Apply \autoref{thm:kuratowski-convergence-namecert-obligations}. The local naming surface sends every tolerance read through $M,A,Q,H,R,L,U,W,E,T,C,P,N$, so the Real row cannot be detached from the finite convergence evidence.

The nonescape claim follows from absence of carrier coordinates. There is no field for a selected closed-set limit, quotient equality, choice schedule, or ambient hyperspace completion, hence the Real seal can certify only the displayed tolerance route.
\end{proof}
