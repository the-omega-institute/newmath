\begin{theorem}[Poincare disk geodesic projection hyperbolic route]
\label{thm:poincare-disk-geodesic-projection-hyperbolic-route}
Every public $\PoincareDiskGeodesicProjectionUp$ projection read factors
through the hyperbolic geodesic and phase-transport siblings before export.
For an accepted carrier
$$
G=(D,B,M,T,J,H,C,P,N),
$$
the consumer first reads the boundary-geodesic data through
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-geodesic-boundary-namecert}, then the
metric comparison row through
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-metric-namecert}, and then the
phase-transport route through
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-disk-phase-transport-namecert}. Only
after those sibling rows have been exposed may the local projection join $J$ be
exported as projected disk-geodesic data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier through
\autoref{def:poincare-disk-geodesic-projection-carrier}. The rows $B$, $M$, and
$T$ are visible coordinates of the same finite packet, and the projection join
$J$ is downstream of those rows.

Apply
\autoref{thm:poincare-disk-geodesic-projection-namecert-obligations}. Its
NameCert surface requires every projection consumer to read
$$
D,B,M,T \longmapsto J \longmapsto H,C,P,N
$$
before phase-transport or metric export.

The boundary-geodesic row $B$ is therefore read through
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-geodesic-boundary-namecert}; the
metric row $M$ is read through
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-metric-namecert}; and the outgoing
transport consumer is read through
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-disk-phase-transport-namecert}. Since
$J$ is not available before those sibling rows are displayed, the projection
read cannot float independently of the hyperbolic and phase-transport route.
\end{proof}

The same projection route is consumed by the hyperbolic geodesic convex-hull
packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-geodesic-convex-hull-namecert}.
There the displayed side ledger reads $\PoincareDiskGeodesicProjectionUp$ only
after the hyperbolic metric and boundary-geodesic rows have been exposed, so
the projection child remains a finite geodesic-side service rather than a
standalone global hull operation.
