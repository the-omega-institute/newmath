\begin{theorem}[Poincare disk geodesic projection convex-hull consumption]
\label{thm:poincare-disk-geodesic-projection-convex-hull-consumption}
Every $\HyperbolicGeodesicConvexHullUp$ consumer reads $\PoincareDiskGeodesicProjectionUp$ only after the $\UnitDiskUp$ carrier, $\HyperbolicMetricUp$ comparison row, $\HyperbolicGeodesicBoundaryUp$ endpoint row, and $\PoincareBoundaryFixedTransportUp$ row have been exposed. The convex-hull packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-geodesic-convex-hull-namecert} therefore consumes the projection as a downstream finite geodesic-side route, not as an independent projection primitive.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the projection route through the child carrier of \autoref{def:poincare-disk-geodesic-projection-carrier}. The public projection row is downstream of the disk point, hyperbolic metric, geodesic-boundary, and boundary-fixed transport rows.

Apply the sibling lattice of \autoref{thm:poincare-disk-geodesic-projection-sibling-lattice}. It fixes the order $\UnitDiskUp$, $\HyperbolicMetricUp$, $\HyperbolicGeodesicBoundaryUp$, and $\PoincareBoundaryFixedTransportUp$ before the projection join is available.

The convex-hull consumer cited above reads the same join only after that route is visible. Hence the convex-hull use is a downstream consumption edge, while the hub contributes no extra carrier row.
\end{proof}
