\begin{definition}[Compact pointwise bounded modulus carrier]

\item $F$ is the $\PointwiseBoundedRealFamilyUp$ row, displaying the family entries and pointwise boundedness data read at the probes of $K$.
\item $L$ is the finite $\LocatedSupremumUp$ comparison row used only over the displayed probe bounds.

\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the finite rational bounds and slack comparisons.

\begin{theorem}[Compact pointwise bounded modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-pointwise-bounded-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactPointwiseBoundedModulusUp$ carrier
$$
B=(K,F,L,S,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactPointwiseBoundedModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the compact finite-net source, pointwise bounded family, finite located-supremum row, slack ledger, stream windows, regular rational readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item compact-net exposure, requiring $K$ to display the inspected probes and overlap witnesses before any family bound is folded;
\item pointwise-bound exposure, requiring $F$ and $W$ to read family bounds at the displayed probes and never as quotient equality of families;
\item finite bound-modulus fold, requiring $L,S,R$ to carry the finite maximum, lower-bound, slack, and rational readback comparisons used to produce the shared bound;
\item ledger refusal, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed rows, with no open-cover compactness, host supremum, countable-choice selector, quotient family equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, or bridge credential.
\end{enumerate}
No compact pointwise bounded modulus NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:compact-pointwise-bounded-modulus-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the compact source, pointwise family row, finite supremum comparison, slack ledger, stream windows, regular readback, Real seal, and structural rows.

Compact-net exposure is read through $K$. The inspected probes and overlaps are finite data, so the packet never replaces them by an arbitrary open cover or selected subcover.

Pointwise-bound exposure is read through $F$ and $W$. Each family bound is attached to a displayed probe window, not to host equality of families or quotient real equality.

The finite bound-modulus fold is the shared role of $L,S,R$. The located-supremum row is used only over the finite displayed probe bounds, the slack ledger records the lower-bound comparisons, and the regular rational row records the finite readback.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same displayed packet. Since the carrier contains no open-cover compactness row, host supremum, countable-choice selector, quotient family equality, ambient completeness principle, verification row, or bridge credential, the five listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact pointwise bounded modulus finite-net bound]
\label{thm:compact-pointwise-bounded-modulus-finite-net-bound}
Every bound-modulus output exported by an accepted $\CompactPointwiseBoundedModulusUp$ carrier
$$
B=(K,F,L,S,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
factors through the finite route
$$
\CompactMetricUp,\ \PointwiseBoundedRealFamilyUp,\ \LocatedSupremumUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp
$$
displayed by $K,F,L,S,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. The compact finite net is read first, pointwise family bounds are read only at its displayed probe windows, the finite located-supremum and slack rows compute the shared bound, and the regular rational readback precedes the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The route exports no open-cover compactness, host suprema, countable choice, quotient family equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the carrier projection. The compact-facing data are exactly the finite-net row $K$, and the family-facing data are exactly $F$ and the stream windows $W$.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:compact-pointwise-bounded-modulus-namecert-obligations}. They force the compact probes to be displayed before the family bounds are folded and force every pointwise bound to remain attached to a visible probe window.

The shared bound is then computed only through $L,S,R$. The row $L$ reads a finite located-supremum comparison over the displayed probe bounds, $S$ records the lower-bound and slack budget, and $R$ turns that finite ledger into regular rational readback.

Only after this route is visible can the Real-facing seal $E$ be consumed. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite route, so there is no coordinate through which open-cover compactness, a host supremum, countable choice, quotient family equality, or ambient Real completeness can enter the exported certificate.
\end{proof}
