\begin{definition}[Ostrowski completion uniqueness carrier]
\label{def:ostrowski-completion-uniqueness-carrier}
An $\OstrowskiCompletionUniquenessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(F,A,V,D,S,R,E,U,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the source field row, exposing the finite arithmetic operations and equality classifier visible to the completion request.
\item $A$ is the absolute-value row, displaying positivity, multiplicativity, and triangle-control data as packet history.
\item $V$ is the visible Archimedean or non-Archimedean branch row inherited from the $\OstrowskiCompletionUp$ boundary.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger read by the regular completion names.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule shared by the compared names.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-rational source row routed through the schedule and tolerance ledger.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached from the displayed $D,S,R$ spine.
\item $U$ is the $\CompletionUniquenessUp$ separated ledger comparing completion-facing presentations over the same $F,A,V,D,S,R,E$ packet.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, absolute-value, branch, dyadic, stream, regular, Real, separated-ledger, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an absolute-value completion comparison through $F,A,V,D,S,R,E,U,T,P,N$.

\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion classes, selected Cauchy representatives, ambient completed fields, global branch trichotomy, countable choice, or host equality of completed points.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Ostrowski completion uniqueness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:ostrowski-completion-uniqueness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\OstrowskiCompletionUniquenessUp$ carrier
$$
K=(F,A,V,D,S,R,E,U,T,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\OstrowskiCompletionUniquenessUp}$ surface consists exactly of source-field exposure, absolute-value exposure, visible branch exposure, regular-name spine exposure, separated uniqueness ledger admission, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming. A consumer may read the separated ledger $U$ only after the absolute-value branch rows $F,A,V$ and the regular real-name rows $D,S,R,E$ have both been displayed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:ostrowski-completion-uniqueness-carrier}. The source-field, absolute-value, branch, dyadic, stream, regular-rational, Real-seal, separated-ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows are precisely the coordinates of the finite packet.

The branch-facing obligations are supplied by $F,A,V$. These rows identify the source field visible to the completion request, the absolute-value data that controls Cauchy admission, and the Archimedean or non-Archimedean branch visible to the packet.

The uniqueness-facing obligations are supplied by $D,S,R,E,U$. The rows $D,S,R,E$ give the regular real-name spine, and $U$ compares completion-facing presentations only over that displayed spine together with the same absolute-value branch data.

Finally $T,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no quotient completion, selected representative, host completed field, hidden branch choice, or host equality coordinate occurs in the carrier, the stated rows exhaust the local NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}
