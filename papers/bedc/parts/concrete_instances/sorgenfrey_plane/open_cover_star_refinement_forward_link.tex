\begin{theorem}[Sorgenfrey plane open-cover star-refinement forward lattice link]\label{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-open-cover-code-forward-lattice-link}
Every open-cover-code consumer of an accepted $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ packet that is later used by compact-uniform or product-topology downstream rows must pass through the finite open-cover and star-refinement machinery before the downstream read is public. In the carrier notation of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}, the route first exposes the lower-limit component rows $L_X,L_Y$, component topology rows $O_X,O_Y$, product row $Q$, rectangle ledger $B$, and observable-open row $S$; it then hands the displayed cover fragment to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-open-cover-code-namecert} and the finite star-refinement modulus route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cover-star-refinement-modulus-namecert}. Only after those cover-incidence rows are visible may a compact-uniform or product-topology consumer reuse the cover code. The route exports no selected subcover, ambient powerset cover, metrizability theorem, normality theorem, quotient equality on $\RealUp\times\RealUp$, or standalone compactness principle for the Sorgenfrey plane.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows listed in \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}. The lower-limit component rows, component topology rows, product row, rectangle ledger, and observable-open row are the only open-cover data available inside the packet.

Apply \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-open-cover-code-boundary-route}. It routes any coded-cover consumer through the displayed lower-limit product rows before the open-cover-code sibling can inspect the cover fragment.

Now read the finite star-refinement modulus sibling. Its finite cover and star-refinement rows are the required cover-incidence machinery for compact-uniform reuse, so a downstream compact-uniform or product-topology row cannot bypass them while remaining a consumer of the displayed cover code.

The excluded endpoints are absent from this row inventory. Since neither a selected subcover nor a host powerset topology occurs among the displayed coordinates, the theorem records only the horizontal dependency edge through open-cover code and finite star refinement.
\end{proof}
