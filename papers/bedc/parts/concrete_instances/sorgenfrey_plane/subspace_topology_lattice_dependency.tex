\begin{theorem}[Sorgenfrey plane subspace forward lattice]
\label{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-subspace-forward-lattice}
Every $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ read that restricts the lower-limit product topology to a coordinate axis, diagonal trace, rectangle boundary, or separation-facing subrectangle consumes the $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subspace-topology-namecert} before it exports the separation-space or product-lattice read. The dependency is read after the two lower-limit line rows, product-topology row, rectangle ledger, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}, so the lattice route remains a sibling dependency and not an ambient host-topology assertion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows named in \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}. The lower-limit line rows, product-topology row, rectangle ledger, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row are the only coordinates visible to a coordinate-axis, diagonal-trace, rectangle-boundary, or separation-facing subrectangle consumer.

The subspace read is therefore downstream from those rows. It reaches the sibling $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ certificate through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subspace-topology-namecert} before any separation-space or product-lattice export is visible, and no ambient host-topology assertion is introduced.
\end{proof}
