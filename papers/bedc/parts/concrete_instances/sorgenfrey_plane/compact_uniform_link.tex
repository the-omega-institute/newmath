\begin{theorem}[Sorgenfrey plane compact-uniform link]
\label{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-compact-uniform-link}
For every accepted $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ packet
$$
P=(L_X,L_Y,Q,O_X,O_Y,B,S,H,C,R,N),
$$
the lower-limit product rows $L_X,L_Y,Q,O_X,O_Y,B,S$ feed $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ and $\ProductTopologyUp$ consumers through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subspace-topology-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-product-topology-namecert}. The same rows do not satisfy the compact-uniform-continuity finite-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-continuity-realizer-namecert} unless an explicit finite-net row or compact-metric certificate is supplied outside the Sorgenfrey plane carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}. Its topology-facing rows are the two lower-limit line rows $L_X,L_Y$, the product row $Q$, the component topology rows $O_X,O_Y$, the rectangle ledger $B$, and the observable-open row $S$.

For product and subspace consumers, these are enough: \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-product-topology-link} reads $Q$ before any plane-level observation, and \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-subspace-topology-refinement} hands rectangle restrictions to the $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ sibling after the ambient product row is displayed.

For the compact-uniform consumer, compare rows. The finite-net route requires a finite-net or compact-metric certificate before uniform-continuity realization can be read. No such coordinate occurs in $P$, and the structural rows $H,C,R,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the displayed lower-limit product rows. Hence the compact-uniform route is blocked until the missing finite-net or compact-metric certificate is supplied explicitly.
\end{proof}
