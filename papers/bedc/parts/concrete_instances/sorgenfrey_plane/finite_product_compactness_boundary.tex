\begin{theorem}[Sorgenfrey plane finite-product compactness boundary]
\label{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-finite-product-compactness-boundary}
Every accepted $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ lower-limit product packet routes product compactness questions through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sorgenfrey-line-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-product-topology-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-product-compactness-namecert} before any compact product consumer may read a finite product-net certificate. Since the $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ carrier has no compact finite-net row, it cannot export $\FiniteProductCompactnessUp$, compact-metric product compactness, metrizability, normality, selected bases, or host powerset topology.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}. The lower-limit line rows, product topology row, component topology rows, rectangle ledger, observable-open row, transport, replay, provenance, and local name are displayed, but no compact finite-net row occurs among them.

The topology-facing route is therefore a product-topology route. It reads the Sorgenfrey line and product topology chapters before any product question is answered, but that does not manufacture a finite-net certificate.

Apply \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-compact-uniform-link}. The compact-uniform route is blocked unless a finite-net or compact-metric certificate is supplied outside the Sorgenfrey plane carrier.

The same boundary applies to finite-product compactness. Without the finite product-net data supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-product-compactness-namecert}, the accepted plane packet cannot export compact product compactness or any of the listed host-side product-topology consequences.
\end{proof}
