\begin{theorem}[Sorgenfrey plane SubspaceTopologyUp forward link]
\label{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-subspace-topology-forward-link}
Every accepted $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ lower-limit product read that exports a separation-facing or product-topology consumer first consumes the $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ carrier route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subspace-topology-namecert}. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier from \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}. The lower-limit component rows and product-topology row are visible before any plane-level separation-facing read.

Apply the lower-limit product dependency route and the product-topology forward link. They force each such read through the component lower-limit sources, paired rectangle ledger, and observable-open route.

Now read the $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subspace-topology-namecert}. Its ambient-topology and admission rows provide the displayed subspace route consumed before separation or product-topology consumers inspect the plane packet. Since the Sorgenfrey-plane carrier has no host powerset topology, quotient equality of real pairs, or selected ambient plane coordinate, the forward link is a finite sibling dependency rather than an external topology assertion.
\end{proof}
