\begin{theorem}[Sorgenfrey plane lower-limit product dependency route]
\label{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-lower-limit-product-dependency-route}
Every accepted $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ read that exposes plane-level separation, product-open, or nonnormality-facing data must first consume the two lower-limit line rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sorgenfrey-line-namecert} and the product-topology row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-product-topology-namecert}. In the carrier notation of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}, the rows $L_X,L_Y,O_X,O_Y,Q,B$ are dependency data for the plane-level read; they are not replaceable by a host plane, a selected family of open rectangles, or a powerset topology on $\RealUp\times\RealUp$.

The required lower-limit product route is the dependency chain through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sorgenfrey-line-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-product-topology-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}: every plane-level open, separation, or nonnormality-facing read factors through the two lower-limit line rows and the product-topology rectangle ledger before the ambient topology row is available.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}. The two one-dimensional source rows $L_X,L_Y$ are read before any component topology rows $O_X,O_Y$ or rectangle ledger row $B$ is available.

Next read the product-topology row $Q$. It packages paired lower-limit windows as product evidence, so a plane-level product-open or separation-facing consumer receives the rectangular route only after both component lower-limit sources have been displayed.

The topology dependency is not separate from that rectangle ledger. The component lower-limit topology rows $O_X,O_Y$ and the generated plane topology read are consumed through the $\TopologyUp$ surface named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, while the product rectangle ledger is supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-product-topology-namecert}.

The observable-open, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows $S,H,C,R,N$ preserve this dependency order. Since the carrier has no coordinate for an ambient host plane, selected rectangle family, powerset topology, or quotient equality of real pairs, every accepted plane-level read remains downstream of the lower-limit line and product-topology rows.
\end{proof}
