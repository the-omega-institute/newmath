\begin{theorem}[Sorgenfrey plane downstream separation route]
\label{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-downstream-link}
Every downstream separation-space consumer of an accepted $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ product lower-limit packet must first pass through the already displayed rows for $\SorgenfreyLineUp$, $\ProductTopologyUp$, $\TopologyUp$, and $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$. In the carrier notation of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}, the required route is
$$
L_X,L_Y,O_X,O_Y,Q,B,S,H,C,R,N .
$$
The route may serve as a separation-facing contrast with \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-perfectly-normal-space-namecert}, but it supplies no normality, perfect normality, metrizability, selected basis, host powerset topology, or ambient plane object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}. The component rows $L_X,L_Y$ expose the two $\SorgenfreyLineUp$ sources before any plane-level read is available.

Next read the topology and product rows. The rows $O_X,O_Y$ are the component $\TopologyUp$ exports, while $Q$ is the $\ProductTopologyUp$ row and $B$ is the lower-limit rectangle ledger. By \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-topology-sibling-route}, \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-product-topology-forward-link}, and \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-topology-sibling-lattice-repair}, every topology or product-open read remains on this sibling lattice.

The observable-open row $S$ supplies the $\SierpinskiSpaceUp$ readback for separation-facing observations. The structural rows $H,C,R,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name the same route.

The comparison with \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-perfectly-normal-space-namecert} is only downstream contrast. Since the carrier has no row for normality, perfect normality, metrizability, selected bases, host powerset topology, or an ambient plane object, no separation-space consumer may read any of those claims from the $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ packet.
\end{proof}
