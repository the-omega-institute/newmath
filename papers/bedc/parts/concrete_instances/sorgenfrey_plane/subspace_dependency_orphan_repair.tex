\begin{theorem}[Sorgenfrey plane subspace dependency route]
\label{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-subspace-dependency-route}
Every coordinate-axis, diagonal-trace, or rectangle-boundary read exported by an accepted $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ carrier consumes the $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ dependency of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subspace-topology-namecert} only after the two $\SorgenfreyLineUp$ rows and the $\ProductTopologyUp$ row have been displayed. In the carrier notation of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}, the rows $L_X,L_Y$ are read before the component topology rows, the product row $Q$ is read before the rectangle ledger $B$, and the subspace dependency reads only that already displayed lower-limit product surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}. The two one-dimensional sources are the displayed $\SorgenfreyLineUp$ rows $L_X,L_Y$, and the product-facing row is $Q$ from the product-topology sibling.

The coordinate-axis, diagonal-trace, and rectangle-boundary consumers have no independent host plane coordinate. They can inspect only the lower-limit source rows, component topology rows, product row, rectangle ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows already exposed by the carrier.

Thus the subspace-topology chapter is consumed as a sibling dependency after the two line rows and the product row are visible. The route cannot be replaced by a host powerset topology, selected rectangle basis, quotient equality on $\RealUp\times\RealUp$, or standalone plane topology.
\end{proof}
