\begin{proposition}[SorgenfreyPlane product-topology dependency]
\label{prop:sorgenfrey-plane-product-topology-dependency}
Every accepted $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ packet consumes the lower-limit component rows and the $\ProductTopologyUp$ row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-product-topology-namecert} before any separation, subspace, or lattice consumer reads the plane. In the notation of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}, the route first displays the lower-limit sibling dependency of \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-lower-limit-product-dependency-route}, then the product row $Q$, rectangle ledger $B$, transport, replay, provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows; it exports no standalone host plane topology or selected rectangle basis.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The lower-limit dependency theorem supplies the two component line rows before a plane-level read is public. The product-topology chapter supplies the product row consumed by $Q$, and the carrier then records the rectangle ledger $B$.

Any separation, subspace, or lattice consumer reads only after those rows have been transported, replayed, sourced, and named. Since the packet has no coordinate for an independent host plane topology or selected rectangle basis, those objects are not exported.
\end{proof}
