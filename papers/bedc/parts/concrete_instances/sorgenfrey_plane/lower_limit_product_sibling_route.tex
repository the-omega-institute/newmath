\begin{theorem}[Sorgenfrey plane lower-limit product sibling route]
\label{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-lower-limit-product-sibling-route}
The $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ carrier consumes the lower-limit topology rows, product-topology rows, and subspace-topology forward links through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-product-topology-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subspace-topology-namecert}. In the notation of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}, every plane-level read of $B,S,H,C,R,N$ is downstream of the component lower-limit rows $L_X,L_Y$, component topology rows $O_X,O_Y$, product row $Q$, and the displayed subspace restriction route; no hidden product plane, host powerset topology, or quotient real-pair route is available.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sorgenfrey-plane-carrier}. The component lower-limit rows $L_X,L_Y$ and topology rows $O_X,O_Y$ are the only sources from which the rectangle ledger $B$ can be generated.

Apply the lower-limit product dependency route of \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-lower-limit-product-dependency-route} and the product-topology forward link of \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-product-topology-forward-link}. They force every product-open or separation-facing read through the $\TopologyUp$ and $\ProductTopologyUp$ siblings before the observable-open row $S$ is inspected.

Finally read the subspace forward link of \autoref{thm:sorgenfrey-plane-subspace-topology-forward-link}. It keeps the restriction-facing consumer inside the same lower-limit product packet, so the split sibling routes jointly expose the lattice path without adding a host plane, selected rectangle family, powerset topology, or quotient equality of real pairs.
\end{proof}
