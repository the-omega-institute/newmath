The $\ClaimAuditBoundaryUp$ packet realizes the legitimate-claim audit surface of \autoref{ch:visions-legitimate-claim-audit} as a finite NameCert boundary. It sits beside the writing-item packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-writing-item-audit-packet-namecert}, the named externality boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apophaticname-namecert}, the registry layer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-registrylayer-namecert}, and the object-knowledge certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-objectknowledgecertificate-namecert}. Those siblings regulate item-level claim text, apophatic externality, registry admission, and licensed object knowledge; this packet binds the finite expression, claim-status row, refusal or gap/socket row, and permitted-claim row before a theorem-shaped consumer may publish the claim.

\begin{definition}[Claim audit boundary carrier]
\label{def:claim-audit-boundary-carrier}
A $\ClaimAuditBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(E,S,G,R,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the finite communicable-expression row, recording the encoded expression being audited.
\item $S$ is the claim-status row, read from the theorem, conditional, route, evidence, non-evidence, meta, refusal, $\mathsf{GAP}$, or socket statuses of \autoref{def:claim-status}.
\item $G$ is the gap or socket row, recording the boundary position whenever the expression requests supply not produced by the closed substrate.
\item $R$ is the refusal row, recording the visible refusal that prevents hidden supply from being read as theorem content.
\item $Q$ is the permitted-claim row, recording the strongest claim text licensed after $E,S,G,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for expression, status, gap or socket, refusal, permitted claim, and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from expression admission through status assignment, boundary exposure, refusal readback, and permitted-claim publication.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClaimAuditBoundaryUp$, $\ClaimStatusAuditUp$, $\WritingItemAuditPacketUp$, $\ApophaticNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClaimAuditBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for a theorem row that bypasses the displayed status, refusal, gap or socket, and permitted-claim rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Claim audit boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:claim-audit-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:claim-audit-boundary-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\ClaimAuditBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite-expression readback, claim-status readback, gap or socket exposure, refusal exposure, permitted-claim readback, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the packet $B=(E,S,G,R,Q,H,C,P,N)$. The rows $E,S,G,R,Q$ expose the expression under audit, the status assigned to it, the gap or socket boundary, the refusal row, and the permitted claim.

The rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that displayed boundary by transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier has no hidden theorem, status-free claim, refusal-free read, or gap-erasing coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Claim audit boundary status nonescape]
\label{thm:claim-audit-boundary-status-nonescape}
No accepted $\ClaimAuditBoundaryUp$ packet permits a theorem row, route row, evidence row, or object-knowledge claim to escape the displayed claim-status row $S$, refusal row $R$, gap or socket row $G$, and permitted-claim row $Q$. If a consumer publishes a claim stronger than $Q$ or bypasses $G$ or $R$, the consumer is no longer reading the accepted packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:claim-audit-boundary-namecert-obligations}. Every accepted read exposes $S,G,R,Q$ before local naming, so the status assignment, boundary row, refusal row, and permitted text are part of the public packet.

Read the replay row $C$. It orders expression admission, status assignment, boundary exposure, refusal readback, and permitted-claim publication; therefore claim publication cannot be separated from the displayed status and boundary rows.

Finally inspect the carrier inventory. There is no row for a hidden theorem, unmarked object-knowledge export, gap-free theorem upgrade, or refusal-free claim. The structural rows $H,P,N$ only preserve and name the same packet, so any stronger or bypassing read exits $\ClaimAuditBoundaryUp$.
\end{proof}
