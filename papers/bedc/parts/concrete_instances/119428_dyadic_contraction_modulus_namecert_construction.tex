Following Bishop and Bridges on regular Cauchy estimates, the $\DyadicContractionModulusUp$ packet records the finite dyadic contraction budget used by the diagonal regularity route of \autoref{sec:real-completeness-regular-cauchy}. \begin{definition}[Dyadic contraction modulus carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-contraction-modulus-carrier}
A $\DyadicContractionModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(Q,L,M,U,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the precision-request row naming the diagonal regularity comparison.
\item $L$ is the left endpoint-leg dyadic contraction row.
\item $M$ is the middle modulus row comparing the two selected regular Cauchy stages.
\item $U$ is the right endpoint-leg dyadic contraction row.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger that sums the three contraction rows.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row opened by the comparison request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained after the three dyadic legs are displayed.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $Q,L,M,U,D,W,R$ have been replayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the request, three contraction rows, tolerance ledger, window, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a diagonal regularity request through $Q,L,M,U,D,W,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicContractionModulusUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicContractionModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only by transporting these finite rows to a common tolerance surface.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic contraction modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-contraction-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicContractionModulusUp$ carrier
$$
K=(Q,L,M,U,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicContractionModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, precision-request exposure, left endpoint-leg contraction, middle-modulus contraction, right endpoint-leg contraction, DyadicRatCore tolerance summation, StreamName window replay, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal handoff, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The three analytic contraction rows are $L,M,U$, the shared tolerance ledger is $D$, and the Real-completion readback rows are $W,R,E$.

The precision request $Q$ determines which diagonal regularity comparison is being read. The left endpoint leg, middle modulus leg, and right endpoint leg are visible before the DyadicRatCore ledger $D$ sums their tolerances.

StreamName and RegSeqRat consumption is downstream of the dyadic ledger. The finite window $W$ is opened for the same request, $R$ records the regular rational readback, and $E$ seals the resulting Real row only after those displays.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no quotient stream, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, open-cover compactness row, or ambient completeness coordinate, no local obligation can export those objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic contraction modulus real-completeness route]
\label{thm:dyadic-contraction-modulus-real-completeness-route}
The diagonal regularity estimate of \autoref{thm:real-completeness-diagonal-regular} is consumed through a $\DyadicContractionModulusUp$ packet only by the finite route
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp\longmapsto\StreamNameUp\longmapsto\RegSeqRatUp\longmapsto\RealUp .
$$
The route displays the left endpoint leg, middle modulus leg, right endpoint leg, dyadic tolerance ledger, StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before any Real-completion consumer may read the estimate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier obligations of \autoref{thm:dyadic-contraction-modulus-namecert-obligations}. They expose the three contraction rows $L,M,U$ and the DyadicRatCore ledger $D$ before any readback or seal row is available.

Use the source vision theorem \autoref{thm:real-completeness-diagonal-regular}. Its proof route has exactly the left endpoint leg, middle modulus comparison, and right endpoint leg named by $L,M,U$.

The finite window and readback rows are then forced by $W$ and $R$. The Real seal $E$ is downstream of those rows, so a Real-completion consumer reads the estimate only after the dyadic and regular-name data have been displayed.

Therefore the public route is the finite DyadicRatCore-to-StreamName-to-RegSeqRat-to-RealUp handoff. Quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, open-cover compactness, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness are absent from the carrier and cannot replace that handoff.
\end{proof}
