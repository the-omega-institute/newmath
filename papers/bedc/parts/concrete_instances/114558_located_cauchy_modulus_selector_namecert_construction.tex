\begin{theorem}[Located Cauchy modulus selector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-cauchy-modulus-selector-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly that route. Since the carrier contains none of the excluded coordinates, no quotient stream, host limit, choice schedule, unlocated witness, ambient completion theorem, or bypass seal can be exported by the local certificate.
