Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, finite dyadic approximation work is read through explicit meshes and tolerances rather than through an ambient dense-subset theorem. The $\DyadicCofinalGridUp$ packet records the finite cofinal grid surface used when rational sources are approximated by dyadic windows before reaching regular rational sequences and real-name seals. It sits after the rational source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the dyadic ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite request windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Dyadic cofinal grid carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-cofinal-grid-carrier}
A $\DyadicCofinalGridUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(Q,D,M,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ source row whose finite rational samples are the only inputs inspected by the grid.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ scale row recording dyadic denominators, endpoint comparisons, and tolerance budgets.
\item $M$ is the cofinal mesh row: for each displayed tolerance in $D$, it lists a finite dyadic grid whose spacing refines that tolerance on the displayed rational window.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row that records which precision requests and grid cells are opened.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the rational source, dyadic scale, cofinal mesh, and stream window.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $Q,D,M,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the rational source, dyadic scale, mesh, windows, readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a grid request through $Q,D,M,W,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicCofinalGridUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicCofinalGridUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $Q,D,M,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary real-density completion, quotient equality of real names, countable choice, selected limiting grids, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic cofinal grid NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-cofinal-grid-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicCofinalGridUp$ carrier
$$
G=(Q,D,M,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicCofinalGridUp}$ surface consists exactly of rational source exposure, dyadic scale exposure, cofinal mesh admission, StreamName window exposure, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal nonescape, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation may be discharged by an ambient density theorem, quotient real equality, countable choice, a selected limiting grid, or ambient real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-cofinal-grid-carrier}. The displayed analytic rows are exactly $Q,D,M,W,R,E$, and the structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

Read the approximation data in dependency order. The rational samples enter through $Q$, the dyadic denominators and tolerances enter through $D$, and the cofinal mesh row $M$ records only finite dyadic grids attached to those displayed tolerances.

The window and readback rows are downstream. The row $W$ opens only the requested precision cells, and $R$ reads those cells as a regular rational sequence before the terminal real seal $E$ is visible.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no additional coordinate exists, the excluded density, quotient, choice, selected-limit, and completeness principles are not part of the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic cofinal grid real readback route]
\label{thm:dyadic-cofinal-grid-real-readback-route}
Let $G=(Q,D,M,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\DyadicCofinalGridUp$ carrier. Every Real-facing consumer of the grid must read the rational source row $Q$, dyadic scale row $D$, cofinal mesh row $M$, StreamName window row $W$, and RegSeqRat readback row $R$ before it reaches the Real seal row $E$. The route supplies finite dyadic approximation data for displayed tolerances; it does not supply real completeness, quotient equality of real names, a selected limit, or countable choice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted consumer read of $G$. Carrier admission exposes the tuple $Q,D,M,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$ and no other analytic row.

The Real-facing route is ordered by the displayed rows. The source row $Q$ supplies finite rational samples, $D$ supplies dyadic scales, $M$ supplies the cofinal finite grids for those scales, and $W$ records the finite precision requests being opened.

Only after those rows are visible can $R$ be read as a regular rational handoff. The terminal seal $E$ is downstream of that handoff, so a consumer cannot reach $\RealUp$ through a hidden density or limit principle.

The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve the same route. Therefore every Real-facing read factors through the displayed finite grid surface and leaves real completeness, quotient equality, selected limits, and countable choice outside the packet.
\end{proof}
