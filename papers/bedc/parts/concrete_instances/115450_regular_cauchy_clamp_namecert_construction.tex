\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RegularCauchyClampUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-clamp-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\RegularCauchyClampUp$ packet records the finite regular-Cauchy route for clamping a regular rational Cauchy name between two regular rational endpoint rows before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal is inspected. It sits below the Real-facing clamp of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-clamping-namecert} and reads its source surface through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the lower-bound factorization of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-minimum-namecert}, the upper-bound factorization of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-maximum-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives completion-facing interval, oscillation, and boundedness consumers a pre-seal regular-Cauchy clamp route without host trichotomy, quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy clamp carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-clamp-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyClampUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(X,L,U,W,D,M,N,R,E,H,C,P,A)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source row whose regular rational Cauchy readback is being clamped.
\item $L$ and $U$ are the $\RegSeqRatUp$ endpoint rows for the displayed lower and upper bounds.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row opened at the precision requested by the clamp consumer.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and readback ledger; it records the requested dyadic tolerance, the endpoint slack, and the comparison scale used by the two factorization rows.
\item $M$ is the $\RegularCauchyMaximumUp$ factorization row implementing the lower projection $\max(X,L)$ on the displayed regular-Cauchy surface.
\item $N$ is the $\RegularCauchyMinimumUp$ factorization row implementing the upper projection $\min(M,U)$ after the lower projection has been exposed.
\item $R$ is the exported $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular-Cauchy clamp value.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $R$ has been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, endpoints, windows, dyadic ledger, maximum row, minimum row, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a clamp request through $X,L,U,W,D,M,N,R,E,H,P,A$.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyClampUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegularCauchyMaximumUp$, $\RegularCauchyMinimumUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $A$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyClampUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier reads only the displayed tuple $K=(X,L,U,W,D,M,N,R,E,H,C,P,A)$. It has no coordinate for host order trichotomy, quotient stream equality, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, ambient real completeness, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy clamp NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-clamp-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyClampUp$ carrier
$$
K=(X,L,U,W,D,M,N,R,E,H,C,P,A),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyClampUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the source row, endpoint rows, finite-window row, dyadic tolerance ledger, maximum factorization row, minimum factorization row, exported readback row, Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. The certificate also requires factorization order: $M$ must be read as the displayed $\RegularCauchyMaximumUp$ route for $\max(X,L)$ before $N$ is read as the displayed $\RegularCauchyMinimumUp$ route for $\min(M,U)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-clamp-carrier}. Its displayed rows are exactly the source regular-rational readback, two endpoint rows, the finite observation window, dyadic tolerance ledger, two factorization rows, exported readback, Real seal, and structural rows.

The factorization order is part of the row inventory. The maximum row $M$ can inspect only $X,L,W,D$ and the structural rows available at that stage; the minimum row $N$ is downstream of $M$ and the upper endpoint row $U$.

The exported readback $R$ and seal $E$ have no independent numeric source. They can therefore expose only the regular-Cauchy clamp route already displayed by $M$ and $N$, transported by $H$ and replayed by $C$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy clamp min--max factorization]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-clamp-min-max-factorization}
Every accepted $\RegularCauchyClampUp$ carrier $K=(X,L,U,W,D,M,N,R,E,H,C,P,A)$ presents its clamp value through the finite route
$$
\begin{aligned}
X,L,U,W,D
&\longmapsto M=\max(X,L),\\
M,U,D
&\longmapsto N=\min(M,U),\\
N
&\longmapsto R \longmapsto E .
\end{aligned}
$$
No consumer receives a direct host clamp operation, a trichotomy of the three regular Cauchy rows, quotient stream equality, a selected limit, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted carrier and read the maximum row $M$. By \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-clamp-carrier}, this row is the only lower-projection coordinate and it factors through the displayed $\RegularCauchyMaximumUp$ route.

Next read the minimum row $N$. It is downstream of $M$ and $U$, so its only upper-projection source is the displayed $\RegularCauchyMinimumUp$ route applied to the lower-projected row and the upper endpoint.

Finally read $R$ and then $E$. Since the carrier has no coordinate for any excluded host or quotient object, the exported route is exactly the displayed min--max factorization.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy clamp Real seal boundary]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-clamp-real-seal-boundary}
Every $\RealUp$ consumer of an accepted $\RegularCauchyClampUp$ packet must first expose the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source row $X$, endpoint rows $L,U$, finite $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger $D$, $\RegularCauchyMaximumUp$ row $M$, $\RegularCauchyMinimumUp$ row $N$, and exported $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$. The terminal seal $E$ records only the displayed regular-Cauchy clamp boundary and cannot be used as an ambient Real-level clamp theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The Real seal row $E$ is downstream of the exported readback row $R$ in the carrier tuple. Thus a Real-facing consumer cannot inspect $E$ until the regular-Cauchy readback has been displayed.

The readback row $R$ is downstream of the two factorization rows. Those factorization rows force the route through $\RegularCauchyMaximumUp$ and $\RegularCauchyMinimumUp$ before any Real-facing seal is available.

The structural rows $H,C,P,A$ transport, replay, source, and name only that route. Hence $E$ seals the pre-seal regular-Cauchy clamp boundary and does not add host order, quotient equality, selected limits, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
