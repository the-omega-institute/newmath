\label{def:metacic-residual-substitution-socket-carrier}

\item $W$ is the substitution-window row, listing the binder depth, occurrence window, and substitution request visible before residual transport.
\item $B$ is the candidate-boundary row, exposing the candidate branch or normalization boundary that will consume the socket.
\item $J$ is the confluence socket row, recording the finite join or conditional-diamond handoff available after $R,W,B$ have been displayed.

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC residual-substitution socket NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metacic-residual-substitution-socket-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetacicResidualSubstitutionSocketUp$ carrier
$$

the local $\NameCert_{\MetacicResidualSubstitutionSocketUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for residual source, substitution-window compatibility, candidate-boundary exposure, confluence socket handoff, refusal and provenance rows, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Any normalization or confluence audit consumer must read
$$

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-residual-substitution-socket-carrier}. The residual source, substitution window, candidate boundary, confluence socket, refusal, provenance, and structural rows are exactly the displayed coordinates.

Substitution-window compatibility is the pair $R,W$. The source residual data and the binder-window request are read together before a candidate branch is exposed.

The refusal and provenance rows $F,V$ keep the audit boundary explicit. They record which large MetaCIC claims are absent and which normalization or confluence audit surfaces supplied the data.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only those rows. Since the carrier has no coordinate for full subject reduction, confluence, normalization, conversion typing, quotient syntax equality, hidden substitution admissibility, or consistency, the listed rows exhaust the local obligation surface.
\end{proof}
