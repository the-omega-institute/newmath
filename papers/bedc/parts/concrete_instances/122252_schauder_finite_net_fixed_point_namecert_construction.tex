\begin{definition}[Schauder finite-net fixed-point carrier]
\label{def:schauder-finite-net-fixed-point-carrier}
A $\SchauderFiniteNetFixedPointUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, exposing the finite compact-metric inspection windows used by the net.
\item $Q$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ row, recording membership and finite affine-combination stability for the compact convex carrier.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ self-map row, carrying graph access, source membership, target membership, and displayed continuity data.
\item $M$ is the $\SchauderFiniteCompactMapUp$ residual route, binding compact metric, convex, continuous-map, modulus, finite-net, displacement, and residual reads into one compact-map packet.
\item $A$ is the $\FiniteApproximateFixedPointUp$ handoff row, listing the accepted finite mesh, coverage, displacement tolerance, and approximate fixed-point read.
\item $E$ is the finite-net exposure row, naming the centers, cells, tolerance requests, and projection reads actually inspected by the packet.
\item $D$ is the displacement ledger comparing each inspected net point with its image through $F$ at the displayed tolerance.
\item $R$ is the fixed-point residual row exported toward $\SchauderFixedPointUp$ after $K,Q,F,M,A,E,D$ have been displayed.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SchauderFiniteNetFixedPointUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,Q,F,M,A,E,D,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected fixed point, host compactness, open-cover compactness, quotient equality of approximants, countable choice, or ambient completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Schauder finite-net fixed-point NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:schauder-finite-net-fixed-point-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SchauderFiniteNetFixedPointUp$ carrier
$$
T=(K,Q,F,M,A,E,D,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SchauderFiniteNetFixedPointUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact metric admission through $K$, convex carrier admission through $Q$, continuous self-map admission through $F$, compact-map residual routing through $M$, finite approximate fixed-point handoff through $A$, finite-net exposure through $E$, displacement-ledger comparison through $D$, fixed-point residual export through $R$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, package provenance, local naming, and non-escape from host fixed-point selection, open-cover compactness, quotient approximants, countable choice, and ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:schauder-finite-net-fixed-point-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the rows $K,Q,F,M,A,E,D,R,H,C,P,N$, so no consumer can read compactness, convexity, continuity, finite-net data, displacement, residual evidence, transport, replay, provenance, or naming outside this list.

Read the geometric source rows first. The compact metric row $K$ supplies the inspected finite windows, the convex row $Q$ supplies membership and affine-combination stability, and the continuous-map row $F$ supplies the self-map data over that same displayed carrier.

The approximation route then passes through $M$ and $A$. The compact-map row $M$ packages the residual route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-schauder-finite-compact-map-namecert}, while the approximate fixed-point row $A$ supplies the finite mesh and tolerance handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-approximate-fixed-point-namecert}.

Finally $E,D,R,H,C,P,N$ expose the inspected net, compare displacements, export the residual row, transport the same components, replay the displayed request, record provenance, and register the local name. Since none of those rows names a host fixed-point selector, open-cover compactness theorem, quotient approximant, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness object, the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}
