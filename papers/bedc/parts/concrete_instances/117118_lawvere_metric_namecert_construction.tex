\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LawvereMetricUp$}

Following Lawvere's enriched-category account of generalized metric spaces, the $\LawvereMetricUp$ packet records a directed distance interface as finite BEDC NameCert data rather than as an ambient completed metric object. It sits between the directed comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-quasi-metric-namecert}, the Yoneda-facing route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-yoneda-completion-namecert}, the formal-ball surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-formalball-namecert}, the symmetric metric sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the dyadic and regular rational readbacks of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the completion consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lawvere-metric-completion-namecert}. The packet names the base directed-distance ledger; it does not claim Real completeness, Cauchy completion, selected colimits, quotient equality of points, symmetry, or separatedness.

\begin{definition}[Lawvere metric carrier]
\label{def:lawvere-metric-carrier}
A $\LawvereMetricUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(X,Z,T,D,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the directed source row naming the finite object-window on which directed distances are read.
\item $Z$ is the zero/self ledger, recording that each displayed source entry carries the directed self-distance row expected by a Lawvere-style metric interface.
\item $T$ is the triangle ledger, recording finite composable directed-distance triples before any completion or formal-ball consumer sees them.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ distance budget row used for finite tolerance reads.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row converting displayed dyadic distance windows into regular rational observations.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal for directed distance values after $D$ and $Q$ have been exposed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,Z,T,D,Q,R$ and for the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the source row through
$$
X,Z,T,D,Q \longmapsto R \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LawvereMetricUp$, $\QuasiMetricUp$, $\YonedaCompletionUp$, $\FormalBallUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\LawvereMetricCompletionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LawvereMetricUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for symmetry, separatedness, selected Yoneda limit, completed formal-ball object, quotient point equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification row, or bridge credential.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lawvere metric triangle ledger stability]
\label{thm:lawvere-metric-triangle-ledger-stability}
For every accepted $\LawvereMetricUp$ carrier $L=(X,Z,T,D,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, any $\QuasiMetricUp$, $\FormalBallUp$, $\YonedaCompletionUp$, $\MetricUp$, or $\LawvereMetricCompletionUp$ consumer of a displayed directed-distance comparison must read the same directed source row $X$, zero/self ledger $Z$, triangle ledger $T$, dyadic budget $D$, regular rational readback $Q$, and real seal $R$ before it treats the comparison as stable under $\hsame$ transport and $\Cont$ replay.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lawvere-metric-carrier}. The directed source row $X$ fixes the finite window of points, while $Z$ and $T$ are the only rows naming self-distance and triangle data.

The numeric side is not read directly as host real equality. The dyadic row $D$ supplies finite tolerance data, $Q$ repacks it as regular rational readback, and $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal visible to distance consumers.

The structural rows preserve that route. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport in $H$ carries the same displayed rows, and $\Cont$ replay in $C$ permits the consumer to replay the path from directed source data to real-sealed distance data.

Thus any sibling consumer must factor through $X,Z,T,D,Q,R,H,C,P,N$. The carrier has no additional coordinate from which to extract symmetry, separatedness, selected completion data, quotient point equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
