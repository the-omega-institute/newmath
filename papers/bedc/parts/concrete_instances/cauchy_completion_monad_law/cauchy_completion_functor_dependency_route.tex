\begin{theorem}[CauchyCompletionMonadLaw functor dependency route]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-monad-law-functor-dependency-route}
Every accepted $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$ packet consumes the functorial completion surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionfunctor-namecert} before its multiplication, unit, idempotence, or associativity rows can be read as law data. Concretely, the monad-law carrier $L=(M,U,I,B,S,D,R,H,C,P,N)$ first reads the $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$ source-metric, regular-Cauchy, completion-seal, unit-and-bind, universal-property, classifier, transport, provenance, and local NameCert rows, and only then replays $M,U,I,B,S,D,R,H,C,P,N$ as the law surface. Thus the law packet records coherence over the displayed functorial completion route rather than a kernel-only island or a host-level completion equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-monad-law-carrier}. Its monad, unit, idempotence, bind, stream, dyadic, regular-rational, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows are law-facing rows, but they do not by themselves identify the completion transformer as a functorial public route.

Read \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletionfunctor-namecert} together with the sibling universal-property certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completionuniversalproperty-namecert}. The functor packet supplies the source-metric row, regular-Cauchy family row, completion seal, unit-and-bind schedule, universal-property row, classifier ledger, transport row, provenance row, and local NameCert row consumed by completion-facing clients; the unit-and-bind law rows consume the universal-property witness before associativity is read as monad law data.

Now apply \autoref{thm:cauchy-completion-monad-law-namecert-obligations} and \autoref{thm:cauchy-completion-monad-law-unit-associativity-boundary}. They replay the unit, idempotence, bind, and associativity rows through the displayed stream, dyadic, regular-rational, transport, and provenance data. Since the functor route is the public completion surface being coordinated, the monad-law packet depends substantively on $\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$ before the law rows are read.
\end{proof}
