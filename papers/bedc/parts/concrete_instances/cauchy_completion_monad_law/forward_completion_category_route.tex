$$
\CauchyCompletionMonadUp,\ \CauchyCompletionUnitUp,\ \CompletionIdempotenceUp,\ \CauchyCompletionBindUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp .

Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-monad-law-carrier}. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, selected limits, host equality, countable choice, hidden stream witnesses, or ambient completeness, the stated route is exhaustive.
\end{proof}
