\begin{theorem}[CauchyCompletionMonadLaw finite-approximation consumer route]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-monad-law-finite-approximation-consumer-route}
Every finite-approximation or totally bounded completion consumer of an accepted $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$ packet reads the monad-law surface through the displayed completion rows before using it as approximation data. The route passes through the finite window, dyadic tolerance, regular-rational readback, unit, multiplication, idempotence, bind, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-monad-law-carrier}, then through the finite approximation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-approximation-space-namecert}, and finally through the totally bounded completion consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallyboundedcompletion-namecert}. It exports a finite completion-law route for approximation consumers, not quotient completion equality, selected representatives, selected limits, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp.CauchyCompletionMonadLawFiniteApproximationConsumerRoute}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-monad-law-carrier}. The law rows $M,U,I,B,S,D,R,H,C,P,N$ expose unit, multiplication, idempotence, bind, stream-window, dyadic-tolerance, regular-readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data.

Use the local NameCert obligations and the unit-associativity boundary, \autoref{thm:cauchy-completion-monad-law-namecert-obligations} and \autoref{thm:cauchy-completion-monad-law-unit-associativity-boundary}. They keep every law read attached to the same finite completion-facing rows before a consumer can treat the law as available approximation data.

Now route the read through the approximation and total-boundedness consumers. The finite approximation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-approximation-space-namecert} supplies the windowed approximation interface, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallyboundedcompletion-namecert} supplies the totally bounded completion consumer edge.

Since the carrier and both consumer routes display only finite rows, the resulting read cannot introduce quotient completion equality, selected representatives, selected limits, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
