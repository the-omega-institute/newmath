\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KalmanDecompositionUp$}

Following Kalman, the $\KalmanDecompositionUp$ packet records the finite block-splitting rows that separate a linear control system into controllable/observable, controllable/unobservable, uncontrollable/observable, and residual components. It sits after the finite reachability surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-controlcontrollability-namecert}, the observation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-controlobservability-namecert}, the matrix-ledger rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, the vector-space carrier rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, and the regulator-facing rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lqr-namecert}. The packet names only finite plant, basis, block, transition, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data; it does not claim a canonical host quotient, analytic minimal realization, transfer-function equality, or stochastic filtering law.

\begin{definition}[KalmanDecomposition carrier]
\label{def:kalman-decomposition-carrier}
A $\KalmanDecompositionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(X,U,Y,A,B,C,S,Q,O,Z_{co},Z_{cu},Z_{uo},Z_{ur},M,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the finite state-space row, $U$ is the input row, and $Y$ is the output row, each read through displayed $\VecSpaceUp$ and $\MatrixUp$ coordinates.
\item $A:X\to X$, $B:U\to X$, and $C:X\to Y$ are the finite transition, input, and output rows of the plant.
\item $S$ is the displayed change-of-basis ledger whose columns are finite state rows and whose inverse-readback is recorded through $\MatrixUp$.
\item $Q$ is the controllability row consumed from $\ControlControllabilityUp$, and $O$ is the observability row consumed from $\ControlObservabilityUp$.
\item $Z_{co}$ is the controllable/observable block, $Z_{cu}$ is the controllable/unobservable block, $Z_{uo}$ is the uncontrollable/observable block, and $Z_{ur}$ is the residual block.
\item $M$ is the matrix transition ledger recording the displayed block reads of $S^{-1}AS$, $S^{-1}B$, and $CS$ together with the zero-pattern entries that witness the decomposition route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for state, input, output, plant, basis, controllability, observability, block, matrix-transition, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the finite route
$$
X,U,Y,A,B,C,S,Q,O \longmapsto Z_{co},Z_{cu},Z_{uo},Z_{ur},M,H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KalmanDecompositionUp$, $\ControlControllabilityUp$, $\ControlObservabilityUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\LQRUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KalmanDecompositionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,U,Y,A,B,C,S,Q,O,Z_{co},Z_{cu},Z_{uo},Z_{ur},M,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host quotient state space, a canonical ambient basis, analytic transfer-function equality, stochastic covariance data, or an infinite-dimensional realization theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[KalmanDecomposition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kalman-decomposition-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KalmanDecompositionUp$ carrier
$$
K=(X,U,Y,A,B,C,S,Q,O,Z_{co},Z_{cu},Z_{uo},Z_{ur},M,H,T,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\KalmanDecompositionUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite state, input, and output admission through $X,U,Y$, plant admission through $A,B,C$, basis-ledger admission through $S$, controllability admission through $Q$, observability admission through $O$, block admission through $Z_{co},Z_{cu},Z_{uo},Z_{ur}$, matrix transition readback through $M$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $T$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:kalman-decomposition-carrier}. The rows $X,U,Y,A,B,C,S$ supply the finite plant and basis data, and the rows $Q,O$ attach the displayed controllability and observability certificates.

The four block rows $Z_{co},Z_{cu},Z_{uo},Z_{ur}$ are read only after $Q,O$ and $S$ have exposed the finite splitting ledger. The row $M$ then records the block transition, input, and output matrices over that same basis route rather than over an ambient quotient or selected canonical form.

Finally $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly the displayed rows. Since the carrier has no coordinate for host quotient semantics, analytic transfer equality, stochastic covariance, or an infinite-dimensional realization theorem, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[KalmanDecomposition controllable-observable split]
\label{thm:kalman-decomposition-controllable-observable-split}
Every downstream control consumer that reads an accepted $\KalmanDecompositionUp$ packet as a controllable/observable splitting must pass first through the displayed finite route
$$
\VecSpaceUp,\MatrixUp,\ControlControllabilityUp,\ControlObservabilityUp
\longmapsto
Z_{co},Z_{cu},Z_{uo},Z_{ur},M .
$$
The exported splitting consists only of the finite state, input, output, plant, basis, controllability, observability, block, transition-ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows of \autoref{def:kalman-decomposition-carrier}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:kalman-decomposition-namecert-obligations}. It exposes the plant rows and the basis ledger before either the controllability or observability rows can be used.

The controllable side is read through $Q$ and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-controlcontrollability-namecert}, while the observable side is read through $O$ and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-controlobservability-namecert}. Both rows are finite control certificates over the same $\MatrixUp$ and $\VecSpaceUp$ surface, so the consumer cannot replace them by an unlogged reachable quotient or unobservable quotient.

The four block rows and the matrix transition ledger then provide the only public splitting data. The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ preserve that finite route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming, so the consumer receives the displayed Kalman decomposition packet rather than a host canonical-form theorem.
\end{proof}
