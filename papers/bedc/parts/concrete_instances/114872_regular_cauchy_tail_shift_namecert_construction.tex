It sits between the regular-rational certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the stream-window naming surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy tail-shift carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-tail-shift-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyTailShiftUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(W,Q,D,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, listing the source stream name, the discarded prefix length, the shifted request window, and the finite overlap witnesses that compare the original and shifted reads.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tail row, recording the regular Cauchy rational data visible on both sides of the shift and the tail index from which the two windows are read.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for the original window, shifted window, overlap comparison, and tail-index budget.
\item $L$ is the $\RealUp$ seal available only after $W,Q,D$ have all been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream window, regular-rational tail data, dyadic tolerance ledger, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a shifted-tail request through $W,Q,D$ and then to $L,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyTailShiftUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyTailShiftUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $W,Q,D,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected limit, quotient representative equality, a host sequence equality proof, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy tail-shift NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-tail-shift-namecert-obligations}

It also requires overlap exactness for the original and shifted windows, tail-index stability for the regular rational rows, dyadic tolerance accounting for the finite overlap comparison, and Real seal nonescape for the terminal read.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-tail-shift-carrier}. The rows $W,Q,D,L,H,C,P,N$ are the whole accepted inventory, so every local certificate obligation must read from those rows.

The window and tail obligations are finite. The row $W$ displays the original and shifted stream windows, while $Q$ displays the regular Cauchy rational tail data that both windows consume.

The numerical comparison is read through $D$. Its dyadic tolerance rows account for the original window, shifted window, overlap comparison, and tail-index budget before the seal $L$ is available.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no carrier row names selected limits, quotient equality, host sequence equality, or ambient completeness, the certificate cannot export any of those claims.
\end{proof}

Thus the shifted read reaches the same finite Real-facing route as the original tail read, without using selected limits, quotient representative equality, host sequence equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

The dyadic row $D$ supplies the requested tolerance comparison for the shifted window. Once that comparison is displayed, the seal $L$ and the transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route.
