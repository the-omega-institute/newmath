\begin{definition}[RegularCauchyExponential carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-exponential-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyExponentialUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the source $\RegSeqRatUp$ row naming the regular Cauchy input.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule used to inspect source values, powers, factorial denominators, partial sums, and tail budgets.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for the requested precision window.
\item $A$ is the coefficient row for the rational factorial denominators and dyadic comparison budgets of the exponential series.
\item $F$ is the finite partial-sum row for the displayed exponential series over the inspected regular Cauchy windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row produced from the finite partial sums and tail ledger.
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the regular Cauchy exponential name.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, windows, dyadic budgets, coefficients, partial sums, readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchyExponential NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-exponential-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyExponentialUp$ carrier $E=(X,W,D,A,F,R,S,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyExponentialUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, regular Cauchy source exposure, finite StreamName window exposure, dyadic tolerance accounting, factorial coefficient accounting, finite partial-sum display, regular rational readback, terminal Real sealing, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. No local obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-exponential-carrier}. The available analytic data are the source row $X$, the window schedule $W$, the dyadic budget $D$, the coefficient row $A$, the finite partial-sum row $F$, the readback row $R$, and the seal row $S$.

The finite series rows are ordered by the inspected windows. A consumer first reads the regular Cauchy input through $X,W,D$, then the factorial coefficients and partial sums through $A,F$, and only then the regular readback and Real seal through $R,S$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name exactly that route. Since no coordinate contains a host exponential, quotient stream equality, selected infinite limit, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle, none of those objects belongs to the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchyExponential series window]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-exponential-series-window}
Every series-facing consumer of an accepted $\RegularCauchyExponentialUp$ carrier must read the displayed route
$$
\RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto F \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp
$$
before any terminal $\RealUp$ seal is exported. The finite row $F$ contains only the partial sums, factorial denominator ledger, and tail estimates visible in the requested window.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the source and window rows $X,W$. They determine which regular Cauchy values and which partial-sum stages are available for the request.

Next read the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ and coefficient row $A$. These rows bound the inspected factorial denominators and tail estimates before the finite partial-sum row $F$ is exposed.

Finally the readback row $R$ repacks the displayed partial sums as regular rational data. The terminal seal can only consume that readback, so the consumer receives a finite windowed series route rather than a hidden host exponential or selected infinite limit.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The seal row $S$ is downstream from $R$. Thus a $\RealUp$ consumer cannot inspect a terminal value until the regular readback has already exposed the finite series route.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming then preserve the same packet through $H,C,P,N$. These rows do not add ambient Real completeness, quotient stream equality, selected limits, or host exponential functions, so the exported seal is exactly the displayed regular Cauchy exponential name.
