\begin{definition}[Finite substrate map carrier]
\label{def:finite-substrate-map-carrier}
A $\FiniteSubstrateMapUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(B,E,S,W,L,O,M,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the source $\BHist$ audit row to be read as substrate data.
\item $E$ is the $\CellularCarrierEncodingUp$ row translating finite audit
entries into cellular carrier entries.
\item $S$ is the $\CellularSubstrateUp$ row that receives the encoded carrier.
\item $W$ is the $\CellularWindowTraceUp$ finite trace window on which the map
is observed.
\item $L$ is the $\CellularLocalUpdateRuleUp$ local rule row used by each
displayed successor read.
\item $O$ is the $\CellularOrbitSubstrateUp$ finite orbit row generated by the
encoded carrier and local rule over the displayed window.
\item $M$ is the finite map ledger pairing each accepted $\BHist$ source entry
with its encoded carrier cell, local update read, orbit entry, and exported
substrate observation.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $B,E,S,W,L,O,M$ and the
local certificate data.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a source audit request through
the encoding, substrate, window, local update, orbit, and map-ledger rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteSubstrateMapUp$, $\BHist$,
$\CellularCarrierEncodingUp$, $\CellularSubstrateUp$,
$\CellularWindowTraceUp$, $\CellularLocalUpdateRuleUp$,
$\CellularOrbitSubstrateUp$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteSubstrateMapUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier reads accepted packets only through these finite rows. It has no
coordinate for an unbounded substrate, a detached machine evaluator, quotient

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite substrate map NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-substrate-map-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteSubstrateMapUp$ carrier
$F=(B,E,S,W,L,O,M,H,C,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\FiniteSubstrateMapUp}$ surface consists exactly of source audit
admission, cellular carrier encoding, cellular substrate admission, finite
window trace exposure, local update exposure, finite orbit exposure, map-ledger
exactness, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$
provenance, and local naming. Every exported substrate observation factors
through the finite route
$$
B,E,S,W,L,O,M,H,C,P,N .
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-substrate-map-carrier}. The only
substrate-facing coordinates are the source audit row, encoding row, substrate
row, finite window trace, local update rule, orbit row, and map ledger.

The map ledger $M$ pairs source entries with encoded carrier cells and the
finite orbit observations obtained through $W,L,O$. Thus an exported substrate
observation cannot be read before the source, encoding, local update, and orbit
rows have been displayed.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this same
finite route. Since the classifier has no coordinate outside the tuple, the
listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
