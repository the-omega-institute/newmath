Following Bishop-style compact uniform continuity, the $\CompactUniformCoverReadUp$ packet names the finite cover-read route that turns pointwise modulus rows on a compact metric source into a tolerance-only uniform-modulus handoff. It consumes compact, metric, continuous, rational, ProbeBundle, and uniform-modulus surfaces from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuous-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}. The chapter records finite cover data and rational lower-bound readback; it does not choose an external finite subcover, invoke open-cover compactness, select a global modulus by choice, or import ambient metric completion.

\begin{definition}[Compact-uniform cover-read carrier]
\label{def:compact-uniform-cover-read-carrier}
A $\CompactUniformCoverReadUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(Cpt,M,F,Q,B,U,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Cpt$ is the $\CompactUp$ finite-cover row exposing a located finite net.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ distance row used to read source and target triangle estimates.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousUp$ pointwise-modulus row attached to the displayed map or graph.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ positive-radius and lower-bound readback row.
\item $B$ is the $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ finite cover row whose probes index the compact net centers and local modulus radii.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer row receiving the tolerance-only precision ledger after the finite fold.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact, metric, continuous, rational, ProbeBundle, uniform-modulus, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a compact finite cover through pointwise moduli, rational lower-bound fold, metric triangle reads, and the uniform-modulus consumer row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactUniformCoverReadUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\ContinuousUp$, $\RatUp$, $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformCoverReadUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $Cpt,M,F,Q,B,U,H,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an external subcover selector, open-cover compactness, quotient point equality, countable choice, or ambient metric completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact-uniform cover-read NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-cover-read-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactUniformCoverReadUp$ carrier $K=(Cpt,M,F,Q,B,U,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformCoverReadUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the compact finite-cover row, metric distance row, continuous pointwise-modulus row, rational radius and lower-bound row, ProbeBundle finite-cover row, UniformModulus consumer row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row; cover-read compatibility through $Cpt,B$; pointwise-modulus exposure through $F$; rational-fold accountability through $Q$; metric-triangle compatibility through $M$; and non-escape through $H,R,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:compact-uniform-cover-read-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly $Cpt,M,F,Q,B,U,H,R,P,N$, so the local NameCert cannot refer to data outside those rows.

The cover-read obligations are $Cpt,B$. The compact row supplies the located finite net, while the ProbeBundle row indexes the centers and local radius rows that are actually folded.

The modulus obligations are $F,Q,U$. The continuous row exposes pointwise moduli at bundled centers, the rational row supplies positive-radius comparisons and the finite lower-bound fold, and the UniformModulus row receives only the resulting tolerance-only precision ledger.

Metric compatibility is read through $M$. Source and target triangle estimates are finite metric rows attached to the same cover-read route, not ambient metric-space theorems.

Finally $H,R,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier lacks an external subcover selector, open-cover compactness, quotient point equality, countable choice, and ambient metric completion, none of those objects is exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact finite cover to uniform-modulus handoff]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-cover-read-uniform-modulus-handoff}
Every $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer of an accepted $\CompactUniformCoverReadUp$ packet receives data only after the compact finite-net row, pointwise-modulus rows, rational lower-bound fold, ProbeBundle cover index, metric triangle rows, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row have all been displayed. The handoff is a finite ProbeBundle read, not a selected open-cover subcover, external modulus function, quotient point equality, countable choice principle, or ambient compactness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compact finite-cover row of \autoref{def:compact-uniform-cover-read-carrier}. The row $Cpt$ exposes located finite-net data, and $B$ records the ProbeBundle index through which bundled centers and radius rows are read.

At each bundled center, the continuous row $F$ supplies a pointwise-modulus read. The rational row $Q$ then folds the finite family of positive radii into a displayed lower-bound ledger, so the precision handed to the consumer is a BEDC history rather than an external selector.

Use the metric row $M$ for the source and target triangle estimates. These estimates are finite distance reads attached to the bundled center and lower-bound ledger, and they are the only route by which the pointwise data become a uniform estimate.

Finally the structural rows $H,R,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite cover-read. Thus the $\UniformModulusUp$ row $U$ receives only the compact finite-net, pointwise-modulus, rational-fold, ProbeBundle, metric, and structural data already displayed, with no open-cover selector, quotient point equality, countable choice, or ambient compactness principle.
\end{proof}
