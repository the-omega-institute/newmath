\item $B$ is the boundedness row, exposing the $\BoundedRealSequenceUp$ bound ledger attached to the same windows.

\begin{theorem}[Bishop monotone convergence boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-monotone-convergence-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopMonotoneConvergenceBoundaryUp$ carrier
$$
K=(S,B,M,Q,D,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopMonotoneConvergenceBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation families:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the sequence schedule, boundedness row, monotone ledger, Bishop Cauchy convergence criterion row, dyadic tolerance row, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item bounded-monotone exposure, requiring $B$ and $M$ to be read from the finite windows scheduled by $S$ before any convergence criterion is consumed;
\item criterion handoff, requiring $Q$ to consume the displayed boundedness and monotone rows rather than an external monotone convergence theorem;
\item finite L10 seal nonescape, requiring $D,R,E$ to expose dyadic tolerances, regular rational readback, and the terminal Real seal in order;
\item transport, replay, provenance, and local naming, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to preserve and name the same finite route without adding selected limits, quotient stream equality, countable choice, host real completeness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:bishop-monotone-convergence-boundary-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the schedule, boundedness, monotone, criterion, tolerance, readback, seal, and structural rows.

Bounded-monotone exposure is read from $S,B,M$. The boundedness row and monotone ledger are attached to the same finite windows, so no consumer can replace them by a detached convergence theorem.

The criterion handoff is the role of $Q$. It receives only the displayed boundedness and monotone data, then passes through the dyadic tolerance row $D$ and the regular readback row $R$ before any Real-facing seal is available.

Finally $E,H,C,P,N$ add only the terminal seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier has no selected-limit, quotient-stream, countable-choice, host-completeness, or ambient-completeness coordinate, the listed families exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
