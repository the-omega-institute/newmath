Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\BoundedContinuousImageUp$ packet records the finite BEDC carrier for the familiar fact that a continuous real-valued map on a compact metric source has a displayed bound. It sits over the compact metric finite-net source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the continuity-radius surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the bounded source exactness of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedrealsequence-namecert}, the stream-name handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Bounded continuous image carrier]
\label{def:bounded-continuous-image-carrier}
A $\BoundedContinuousImageUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(K,F,W,Q,R,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, read through finite net centers, cover radii, and located membership witnesses.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row from the compact metric source to the real-name target surface.
\item $W$ is the finite source-window ledger listing the compact-source centers and local continuity windows consumed by the bound search.
\item $Q$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row for the finitely many real-name samples produced by $F$ on the displayed windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational approximations of those finitely many samples.
\item $B$ is the $\BoundedRealSequenceUp$ bound row asserting that the displayed finite readback family lies under a rational bound budget.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, map row, source windows, stream observations, readback, bound row, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a requested tolerance through the same finite-window route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BoundedContinuousImageUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BoundedRealSequenceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

The carrier contains no coordinate for an open-cover compactness proof, a selected supremum, quotient equality of maps, countable choice, ambient real completeness, or a host-level bounded-image theorem outside the displayed finite rows.

\begin{theorem}[Bounded continuous image NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bounded-continuous-image-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BoundedContinuousImageUp$ carrier $I=(K,F,W,Q,R,B,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedContinuousImageUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact source admission through $K$, continuous-map admission through $F$, finite source-window coverage through $W$, stream observation through $Q$, regular rational readback through $R$, bounded-source exactness through $B$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bounded-continuous-image-carrier}. The source and map rows are $K$ and $F$, and the finite window ledger $W$ records the only compact-source observations available to the packet.

Read the observation rows next. The stream row $Q$ records the finitely many real-name samples produced by the map row on the displayed windows, and $R$ is the regular rational readback for those samples.

The bound row $B$ is downstream of $R$, so boundedness is asserted only for the displayed finite readback family. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route; no excluded compactness, supremum, quotient, choice, or ambient completeness principle has a carrier coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded continuous image compact-bound route]
\label{thm:bounded-continuous-image-compact-bound-route}
Every accepted $\BoundedContinuousImageUp$ packet routes a compact metric source and a continuous real-valued map through the finite chain
$$
\CompactMetricUp \to \ContinuousMapUp \to \StreamNameUp \to \RegSeqRatUp \to \BoundedRealSequenceUp .
$$
The bound row $B$ may be read only after the finite source-window ledger $W$, stream observation row $Q$, and regular rational readback row $R$ have been displayed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with $K$ and $F$. Compactness is used only through the finite net and located membership data recorded in the source row, while continuity is used only through the displayed map row and the windows listed in $W$.

The image samples are then named by $Q$ and read back by $R$. Since $B$ is a bounded real sequence row for that finite readback family, a consumer cannot read the bound before the compact-window and continuity observations have been exposed.

Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ keep the same route under componentwise equality and tolerance requests. Provenance and local naming through $P$ and $N$ seal the route as the stated compact-bound surface.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The bound is recorded by $B$ after the readback. Therefore a $\RealUp$ consumer can read an image value or bound only through the displayed stream, regular-rational, and bounded-sequence route.

The remaining coordinates $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name that route. They add no coordinate for a completeness principle, supremum selector, quotient map equality, or host equality, so the real seal cannot escape the finite packet.
