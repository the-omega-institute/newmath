\label{def:channel-termination-boundary-carrier}

$$
B=(A,E,T,D,R,F,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the active binary alphabet row, recording the source fragments $0,1$ and channel fragments $0,10,11$;
\item $E$ is the legal channel-event row, recording membership in $(0\mid 10)^{*}11$ for the inspected finite event;
\item $T$ is the terminator row, recording that the first adjacent $11$ is the local endpoint;
\item $D$ is the decoder-state row, recording the finite scan state before and after the local endpoint;
\item $R$ is the refusal row separating local delimiter termination from certificate-level halting;
\item $F$ is the finite trace row, recording the inspected event prefix and the endpoint witness;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $A,E,T,D,R,F,P,N$;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the active alphabet, legal-event, terminator, decoder, refusal, finite trace, provenance, and local naming rows;

\end{enumerate}
