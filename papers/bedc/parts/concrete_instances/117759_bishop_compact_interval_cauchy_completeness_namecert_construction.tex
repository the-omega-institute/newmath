\begin{theorem}[Bishop compact-interval Cauchy-completeness obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-compact-interval-cauchy-completeness-obligations}

\item dyadic mesh visibility, requiring every tolerance and endpoint comparison to pass through the displayed row $D$;
\item nested interval locking, requiring $L$ to bind the Cauchy stream and mesh rows to the shrinking interval route before any limit readback is exposed;
\item regular rational readback, requiring $R$ to be read only after $I,S,D,L$ are displayed;
\item terminal Real sealing, requiring $E$ to factor through the same compact interval, stream, mesh, nested lock, and readback rows;
