Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive completions and the standard dense-embedding comparison used in metric completion, the $\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingComparisonUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface for comparing two dense presentations of the same Cauchy completion boundary. It sits over the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the Cauchy dense embedding of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-dense-embedding-namecert}, and the metric dense embedding of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-completion-dense-embedding-namecert}. The packet compares displayed dense rows only; it does not quotient completion points, select representatives, identify host limits, invoke countable choice, or import ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Cauchy completion dense-embedding comparison carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-completion-dense-embedding-comparison-carrier}
A $\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingComparisonUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(S,D,M,E,E',Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the shared source row, binding the compared dense presentations to the same displayed Cauchy-completion source and its finite $\StreamNameUp$ observation schedule.
\item $D$ is the $\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$ row, exposing the Cauchy dense-source image, completion route, dyadic tolerance ledger, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal used by the first presentation.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$ row, exposing the metric dense-source image, separated comparison route, uniform-completion handoff, and regular rational readback used by the second presentation.
\item $E$ is the first dense embedding into the completion presentation, read through $D$ before any comparison row is consumed.
\item $E'$ is the second dense embedding into the completion presentation, read through $M$ before any comparison row is consumed.
\item $Q$ is the comparison row, recording finite agreement of the two dense-image reads on the shared source windows and their $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledgers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,D,M,E,E',Q$ and for the replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion-facing comparison request through the source, both dense embeddings, the comparison row, and the structural rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingComparisonUp$, $\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$, $\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingComparisonUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,D,M,E,E',Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, a selected dense representative, host equality of limits, countable choice, or an ambient completion theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion dense-embedding comparison NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-dense-embedding-comparison-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingComparisonUp$ carrier
$$
K=(S,D,M,E,E',Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingComparisonUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the shared source row, the Cauchy dense-embedding row, the metric dense-embedding row, the two completion-presentation embeddings, the comparison row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every completion-facing comparison read must expose $S,D,M,E,E'$ before it can consume $Q,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-dense-embedding-comparison-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the source row, two dense-embedding rows, two completion-presentation embedding rows, one comparison row, and the structural rows.

The entry route is $S,D,M,E,E'$. The source row fixes the common finite windows; $D$ and $M$ expose the Cauchy and metric dense-embedding presentations; $E$ and $E'$ are therefore read only after their corresponding dense rows have been displayed.

The comparison route is $Q,H,C,P,N$. The row $Q$ compares only the displayed dense-image reads over the shared windows and dyadic tolerance ledgers. The rows $H$ and $C$ transport and replay the same comparison, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and the local certificate.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, host limit equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness, those endpoints cannot be exported by the local $\NameCert$ surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion dense-embedding comparison handoff]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-dense-embedding-comparison-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$ or completion consumer of an accepted $\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingComparisonUp$ packet factors through
$$
\StreamNameUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto
\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp,\ \MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp \longmapsto
Q \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
Thus the consumer receives a finite comparison between two dense presentations feeding the same completion boundary. It receives no quotient equality of completion points, selected representative, host limit equality, countable-choice schedule, total completion object, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:cauchy-completion-dense-embedding-comparison-namecert-obligations}. They force every comparison read to expose the shared source row and both dense-embedding rows before the comparison row $Q$ is consumed.

Read the numerical side through the sibling rows. The source windows are governed by $\StreamNameUp$, their tolerance ledgers by $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and their regular readback by $\RegSeqRatUp$ before either dense presentation reaches a $\RealUp$ seal.

The Cauchy presentation is supplied by $\CauchyCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$, while the metric presentation is supplied by $\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$. The comparison row $Q$ only relates those displayed dense-image reads over the shared windows.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Because none of the excluded host-level objects is a carrier coordinate, the handoff remains a comparison surface rather than a quotient completion theorem.
\end{proof}
