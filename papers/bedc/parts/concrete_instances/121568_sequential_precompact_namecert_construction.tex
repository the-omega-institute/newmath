\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SequentialPrecompactUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sequential-precompact-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, sequential precompactness is witnessed by finite nets and regular readback windows rather than by selected convergent subsequences or completed quotient streams. The $\SequentialPrecompactUp$ packet sits after the finite density-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cauchy-density-net-namecert}, the dyadic interval net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}, the stream-window interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. It gives L10 compactness and completion consumers a finite inspection route for a displayed sequence source; it is not open-cover compactness, countable choice, selected subsequence extraction, quotient stream equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Sequential precompact carrier]
\label{def:sequential-precompact-carrier}
A $\SequentialPrecompactUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(X,N,S,R,E,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the displayed sequence source row, naming the finite observation interface through which a consumer asks for sequence data.
\item $N$ is the finite-net extractor row, read through $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ and $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ before any completion-facing density claim is exposed.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, recording the inspected finite prefixes of the displayed sequence source and net cells.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained only after the finite window and dyadic tolerance data have been displayed.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for real-facing consumers of the finite-net readback.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for sequence source, finite net, windows, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a sequential-precompactness request through $X,N,S,R,E,H,P,L$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SequentialPrecompactUp$, $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialPrecompactUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $X,N,S,R,E,H,C,P,L$. It has no coordinate for quotient streams, selected subsequences, countable choice, open-cover compactness, host sequence equality, or a general completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential precompact NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sequential-precompact-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SequentialPrecompactUp$ carrier $Q=(X,N,S,R,E,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialPrecompactUp}$ surface consists exactly of sequence-source admission, finite-net extraction, StreamName window display, RegSeqRat readback, terminal Real seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation is stored outside those finite rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sequential-precompact-carrier}. The displayed packet has exactly the rows $X,N,S,R,E,H,C,P,L$, so carrier admission and local naming must be read from those rows.

The sequence source row $X$ supplies only inspected sequence data. The finite-net row $N$ supplies the finite density and dyadic-cell route, while $S$ and $R$ record the finite window and regular rational readback through which the sequence is compared to those cells.

The terminal seal $E$ is downstream of the net, window, and readback rows. The rows $H,C,P,L$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no further coordinate, selected subsequences, quotient stream equality, countable choice, open-cover compactness, and ambient completeness cannot become local certificate obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential precompact finite-net handoff]
\label{thm:sequential-precompact-finite-net-handoff}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\SequentialPrecompactUp$ packet reads the finite-net row $N$ through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cauchy-density-net-namecert} and the dyadic interval cell surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert} before it consumes the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, or terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The handoff is the displayed route
$$
X,N \longmapsto S \longmapsto R \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,C,P,L .
$$
It exports finite dyadic density rows for L10 completion consumers, not quotient streams, selected subsequences, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:sequential-precompact-namecert-obligations}. They force the consumer to expose the sequence source, finite net, stream window, readback, and seal rows in the same accepted packet.

Next read $N$ through the finite density-net and dyadic interval-net sibling chapters. The density route supplies the finite Cauchy density witness, and the dyadic interval-net route supplies the finite cell and endpoint-incidence surface used by the requested tolerance.

Finally use the carrier exhaustion in \autoref{def:sequential-precompact-carrier}. The packet has no row for selected subsequence extraction, quotient stream representatives, countable-choice schedules, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness. Thus the completion-facing handoff is exactly the displayed finite-net route into $S,R,E,H,C,P,L$.
\end{proof}
