Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopCauchyLocatedLimitUp$ packet records the finite route by which a located regular Cauchy source exposes a completion-facing limit window before any quotient or selected representative is available. It sits over the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite observation interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names located Cauchy limit extraction as displayed BEDC data; it does not assert quotient completion, choose a representative, invoke countable choice, or prove ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[BishopCauchyLocatedLimit carrier]
\label{def:bishop-cauchy-located-limit-carrier}
A $\BishopCauchyLocatedLimitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(R,W,D,O,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the regular Cauchy source row, read through the $\RegSeqRatUp$ rational sequence surface.
\item $W$ is the located window row, recording the finite interval and modulus request under which the source is inspected.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger supplied by $\DyadicRatCoreUp$.
\item $O$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation row used to read the source only through named windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row exposed after $R,W,D,O$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the regular source, located window, dyadic tolerance, finite observation, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
R,W,D,O \longmapsto E .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopCauchyLocatedLimitUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCauchyLocatedLimitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,W,D,O,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion, a selected representative, countable choice, a host limit object, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BishopCauchyLocatedLimit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-cauchy-located-limit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopCauchyLocatedLimitUp$ carrier
$$
L=(R,W,D,O,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCauchyLocatedLimitUp}$ surface consists exactly of regular Cauchy source exposure through $R$, located-window exposure through $W$, dyadic tolerance exposure through $D$, StreamName finite observation through $O$, Real sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-cauchy-located-limit-carrier}. The rows $R,W,D,O$ expose the regular source, located window, dyadic tolerance, and finite observation data before any limit-facing read can occur.

The Real seal row $E$ is downstream of those displayed rows. A consumer therefore receives a named finite extraction route rather than a quotient completion object, a selected representative, or a countable-choice schedule.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no host limit object or ambient completeness theorem is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
