\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\QuasiPolishSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-quasi-polish-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following de Brecht and the Brattka--Gherardi treatment of represented countably based spaces, the $\QuasiPolishSpaceUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface in which countable open information and sober point reconstruction are displayed without requiring a compatible complete metric. It sits between the complete-separable metric boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polishspace-namecert}, the point-reconstruction route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sober-space-namecert}, the point-free open route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locale-space-namecert}, the indexed-open source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, and the finite observation schedules of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. The packet supplies a non-metric countably based sober-space bridge; it does not import host powersets, choice-indexed generic points, quotient spatial reflection, or a selected Polish presentation.

\begin{definition}[Quasi-Polish space carrier]
\label{def:quasi-polish-space-carrier}
A $\QuasiPolishSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(T,B,S,L,P,W,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row, exposing indexed open observations and their finite refinement tests.
\item $B$ is the countable-base ledger, recording finite windows of basic opens and their cover-refinement reads.
\item $S$ is the $\SoberSpaceUp$ handoff row, available only after the topology and base rows have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the $\LocaleSpaceUp$ comparison row, reading the same open data point-free before any point-facing reconstruction is consumed.
\item $P$ is the optional $\PolishSpaceUp$ comparison row, present only when a complete-separable metric presentation has already been displayed by a sibling packet.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation schedule for finite windows of basic-open and neighborhood reads.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology, base, sober, locale, Polish-comparison, stream, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from base-window requests through topology rows, locale comparison, sober reconstruction, and optional Polish comparison.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\QuasiPolishSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\LocaleSpaceUp$, $\SoberSpaceUp$, $\PolishSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\QuasiPolishSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,B,S,L,P,W,H,C,G,N$. It has no coordinate for host powersets, arbitrary open-set quantification, selected completely prime filters, quotient spatial reflection, choice-indexed generic points, or a forced compatible complete metric.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Quasi-Polish space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:quasi-polish-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\QuasiPolishSpaceUp$ carrier $Q=(T,B,S,L,P,W,H,C,G,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\QuasiPolishSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of topology-source admission, countable-base window exposure, locale comparison, sober reconstruction handoff, optional Polish comparison, finite stream scheduling, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every consumer must read these rows before treating the packet as a quasi-Polish space boundary.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:quasi-polish-space-carrier}. The rows $T,B,S,L,P,W,H,C,G,N$ are the only classifier-visible coordinates.

The open-information side is finite. The topology row $T$ and base ledger $B$ expose indexed opens through finite windows, while the stream row $W$ records the schedule by which those windows are read.

The reconstruction side is routed. The locale row $L$ compares the same open data point-free, the sober row $S$ supplies the point-reconstruction handoff, and the Polish row $P$ is only a comparison surface when a sibling complete-separable metric presentation is already displayed.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are exhausted by $H,C,G,N$. Since no row stores host powersets, selected generic points, quotient spatial reflection, or a forced metric presentation, the listed rows are exactly the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Quasi-Polish sober-Polish handoff]
\label{thm:quasi-polish-space-sober-polish-handoff}
Every accepted $\QuasiPolishSpaceUp$ consumer that compares sober reconstruction with Polish-style readback factors through
$$
\TopologyUp,\ B,\ \LocaleSpaceUp,\ \SoberSpaceUp,\ \StreamNameUp
$$
before it may inspect the optional $\PolishSpaceUp$ comparison row. The handoff lets a complete-separable metric sibling feed a quasi-Polish consumer, and lets a sober countably based route feed a non-metric consumer, but it does not identify the packet with a host powerset topology, a quotient spatial reflection, a selected generic point, or a chosen compatible complete metric.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the topology and base rows. They expose the countably based open information as finite indexed windows rather than as a host powerset.

Next read the locale and sober rows. The locale comparison keeps the frame-of-opens route visible, and the sober handoff reads point reconstruction only after those open rows are displayed.

The stream schedule preserves finiteness of the comparison. Any Polish row is downstream from these windows and appears only as a sibling comparison surface, so it cannot force a metric presentation for the whole packet.

Thus the quasi-Polish handoff separates the sober countably based route from the Polish complete-separable comparison while preserving both as displayed BEDC rows.
\end{proof}
