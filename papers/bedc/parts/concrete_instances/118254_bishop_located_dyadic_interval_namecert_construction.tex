Following Bishop and Bridges, a located dyadic interval is useful when a finite bracketing object keeps both endpoints, the positive gap, and the comparison route visible before any Real-facing consumer reads a point or completion seal. The $\BishopLocatedDyadicIntervalUp$ packet sits over the dyadic arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the bisection route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-binary-search-namecert}, and the located dyadic real packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-dyadic-real-namecert}. It names the interval-level carrier between endpoint data and Real-facing located completion; it does not select a midpoint limit, quotient Cauchy names, or import ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Bishop located dyadic interval carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-dyadic-interval-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedDyadicIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(L,U,G,S,Q,B,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the lower endpoint row, read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and retained as a displayed dyadic rational.
\item $U$ is the upper endpoint row, read through the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger.
\item $G$ is the positive located-gap row, recording the finite evidence that the lower endpoint lies apart from or below the upper endpoint by a displayed dyadic margin.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row exposing only the finite endpoint windows requested by a comparison or bisection consumer.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for those endpoint windows.
\item $B$ is the $\DyadicBinarySearchUp$ bisection row, recording the retained left or right subinterval and the dyadic midpoint read used to make that decision.
\item $R$ is the $\LocatedDyadicRealUp$ or $\RealUp$ handoff row, available only after $L,U,G,S,Q,B$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoints, gap evidence, stream windows, regular readback, bisection, handoff, provenance, replay, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from endpoint reads through gap checks, finite windows, regular readbacks, bisection decisions, and the Real-facing handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopLocatedDyadicIntervalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicBinarySearchUp$, $\LocatedDyadicRealUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedDyadicIntervalUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $L,U,G,S,Q,B,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a hidden midpoint selector, quotient real equality, countable choice, detached trichotomy, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located dyadic interval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-dyadic-interval-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopLocatedDyadicIntervalUp$ carrier
$$
I=(L,U,G,S,Q,B,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedDyadicIntervalUp}$ surface consists exactly of lower endpoint exposure, upper endpoint exposure, positive located-gap accounting, finite StreamName window inspection, RegSeqRat endpoint readback, dyadic bisection readback, Real-facing handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation row may erase an endpoint, replace the gap by detached order trichotomy, select a midpoint limit, quotient Cauchy names, or export ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-dyadic-interval-carrier}. The endpoint rows $L,U$ and the located-gap row $G$ are the only source data for the interval boundary.

The finite observation route is $S$ followed by $Q$. A comparison or bisection consumer may inspect endpoint windows and regular rational readbacks, but it cannot replace them by quotient real names or by a hidden global order oracle.

The bisection row $B$ is downstream of the same endpoint and gap rows. It records the displayed midpoint read and retained subinterval, so it supplies readback data rather than a selected limit point.

Finally $R,H,C,P,N$ hand off, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite interval packet. Since no excluded coordinate appears in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
