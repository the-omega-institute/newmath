\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UpwindFluxUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-upwind-flux-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following LeVeque and Eymard--Gallouet--Herbin, the $\UpwindFluxUp$ packet records finite-volume flux orientation as BEDC-visible NameCert data. It sits over the conservative balance packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-volume-namecert}, the dyadic mesh interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicmesh-namecert}, the PDE relation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pde-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only the finite upstream orientation rows used by a numerical flux; it does not import a host PDE solution, unrestricted convergence theorem, quotient weak-solution object, selected trace, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Upwind-flux carrier]
\label{def:upwind-flux-carrier}
An $\UpwindFluxUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(V,M,Q,A,O,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V$ is the $\FiniteVolumeUp$ carrier row whose face-incidence and balance ledgers supply the finite-volume context.
\item $M$ is the $\DyadicMeshUp$ row naming the inspected cell, face, and neighbour windows at dyadic precision.
\item $Q$ is the $\PdeUp$ relation row whose local derivative or conservation relation supplies the direction-sensitive PDE side.
\item $A$ is the advection or sign classifier row, read only as a finite orientation comparison on each displayed face.
\item $O$ is the upstream-choice row, selecting for each oriented face the left, right, or boundary value row licensed by $V,M,Q,A$.
\item $W$ is the numerical-flux window row, recording the finite stencil values consumed after the upstream side has been selected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the inspected flux values and residual windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row comparing paired orientation and residual budgets.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $V,M,Q,A,O,W,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the finite-volume, mesh, PDE, orientation, upstream-choice, flux-window, readback, tolerance, real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay from an upwind flux request through $V,M,Q,A,O,W,R,D,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UpwindFluxUp$, $\FiniteVolumeUp$, $\DyadicMeshUp$, $\PdeUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UpwindFluxUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for a host PDE solution, unrestricted convergence of a scheme, quotient weak solution, selected trace, hidden solver state, or ambient real completeness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Upwind-flux NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:upwind-flux-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UpwindFluxUp$ carrier
$$
U=(V,M,Q,A,O,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UpwindFluxUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite-volume admission through $V$, dyadic mesh admission through $M$, PDE relation admission through $Q$, orientation classifier admission through $A$, upstream-choice exactness through $O$, flux-window exposure through $W$, regular rational readback through $R$, dyadic tolerance exposure through $D$, real-seal handoff through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed continuation replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:upwind-flux-carrier}. The rows $V,M,Q$ expose the finite-volume balance packet, dyadic mesh window, and PDE relation before any orientation-sensitive flux read is public.

The row $A$ is the only source of direction information. The upstream-choice row $O$ is therefore exact only when it selects a displayed left, right, or boundary value from the finite rows already licensed by $V,M,Q,A$.

The flux-window row $W$ consumes the selected side from $O$ before the readback and tolerance rows are inspected. Thus $R,D,E$ certify only the resulting finite flux and residual windows, not a host solution or a scheme-wide convergence theorem.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no excluded host solution, unrestricted convergence proof, quotient weak-solution object, selected trace, solver state, or ambient completeness coordinate occurs in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Upwind-flux ledger stability]
\label{thm:upwind-flux-ledger-stability}
Let $U=(V,M,Q,A,O,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\UpwindFluxUp$ carrier. Any componentwise $\hsame$ transport that preserves the displayed finite-volume incidence in $V$, the dyadic mesh window in $M$, the PDE relation row in $Q$, and the orientation classifier row in $A$ also preserves the upstream-choice ledger $O$ and the numerical-flux window $W$ as $\UpwindFluxUp$ data. The transported packet remains inside the same finite ledger boundary and exposes no host PDE solution, unrestricted convergence theorem, quotient weak solution, selected trace, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the componentwise transport rows in $H$. They move the finite-volume incidence row, dyadic mesh window, PDE relation row, and orientation classifier row without adding any coordinate outside the displayed packet.

Because $O$ is defined only as the upstream side selected from $V,M,Q,A$, preserving those four rows preserves the source of every selection in $O$. Boundary faces remain boundary reads, left-facing interior choices remain left reads, and right-facing interior choices remain right reads.

The numerical-flux window $W$ is downstream of $O$. Once the selected side is transported with the same classifier data, the regular readback $R$, dyadic tolerance row $D$, and real seal $E$ replay the same finite flux route.

The continuation, provenance, and local name rows $C,P,N$ then repack the transported data as an $\UpwindFluxUp$ carrier. No excluded analytic or quotient object can enter, since none is a field of \autoref{def:upwind-flux-carrier}.
\end{proof}
