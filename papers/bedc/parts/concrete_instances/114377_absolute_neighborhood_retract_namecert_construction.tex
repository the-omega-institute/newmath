\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\AbsoluteNeighborhoodRetractUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-absolute-neighborhood-retract-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Borsuk and Dugundji, the $\AbsoluteNeighborhoodRetractUp$ packet records the neighbourhood-local retract-extension surface used by compact and located metric analysis. It refines the retract route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-absolute-retract-namecert}, reads compact source data through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, reads distance comparison through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedmetric-namecert}, and consumes the closed-extension and separation anchors of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tietze-extension-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-urysohn-lemma-namecert}. The packet names only displayed neighbourhood, extension, and retraction rows; it does not assert a selected neighbourhood-retraction principle.

\begin{definition}[Absolute neighbourhood retract carrier]\label{def:absolute-neighborhood-retract-carrier}
An $\AbsoluteNeighborhoodRetractUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(X,K,L,U,T,V,E,R,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
where:
\begin{itemize}
\item $X$ is the displayed source carrier row;
\item $K$ is the compact-metric source row read through $\CompactMetricUp$;
\item $L$ is the located-metric comparison row read through $\LocatedMetricUp$;
\item $U$ is the displayed neighbourhood row around the closed source packet;
\item $T$ is the $\TietzeExtensionUp$ closed-extension row;
\item $V$ is the $\UrysohnLemmaUp$ separating-function row used to expose $U$ as a displayed neighbourhood boundary;
\item $E$ records the finite extension request over the closed source row;
\item $R$ records the candidate retraction from the displayed neighbourhood row back to the source row;
\item $S$ records stability of the retraction after the extension and separation rows are visible;
\item $H,C,P,N$ are componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert_{\AbsoluteNeighborhoodRetractUp}$ row.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Absolute neighbourhood retract NameCert obligations]\label{thm:absolute-neighborhood-retract-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AbsoluteNeighborhoodRetractUp$ carrier $A=(X,K,L,U,T,V,E,R,S,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\AbsoluteNeighborhoodRetractUp}$ surface consists exactly of source admission, compact-metric readback, located-metric comparison, displayed neighbourhood exposure, Tietze extension exposure, Urysohn separation exposure, finite extension request, neighbourhood retraction row, retraction-stability row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. A consumer that omits one of $K,L,U,T,V,E,R,S,H,C,P,N$ is outside the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:absolute-neighborhood-retract-carrier}. Each coordinate is finite $\BHist$ data, and the local naming row $N$ names only the displayed packet.

The compact and located-metric rows provide the source scope. The neighbourhood row $U$ is then exposed through the separation route, and the closed-extension row $T$ supplies the extension side of the retract-extension packet.

Finally read $E,R,S,H,C,P,N$. These rows package the extension request, neighbourhood retraction, stability, transport, replay, provenance, and name without adding a selected neighbourhood-retraction theorem, quotient compactum, open-cover compactness argument, or choice of extension operators.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Absolute neighbourhood extension-retraction route]\label{thm:absolute-neighborhood-retract-extension-retraction-route}
Every accepted $\AbsoluteNeighborhoodRetractUp$ packet whose retraction row $R$ is consumed must first expose the compact-metric row $K$, located-metric row $L$, displayed neighbourhood row $U$, Tietze extension row $T$, Urysohn separation row $V$, and finite extension request $E$. The retraction read is then transported through $S,H,C,P,N$ as a neighbourhood-local BEDC route, not as an ambient absolute retract theorem or a selected family of retractions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:absolute-neighborhood-retract-namecert-obligations}. They make $K,L,U,T,V,E,R,S,H,C,P,N$ the visible surface for any accepted packet.

The extension side is read before the retraction. The compact-metric and located-metric rows bound the source, the Urysohn row exposes the neighbourhood boundary, and the Tietze row records the closed-extension data over that boundary.

The retraction row $R$ is therefore a neighbourhood-local coordinate of the same finite packet. The stability, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows keep the route inside $S,H,C,P,N$, so no arbitrary selected neighbourhood retraction, quotient compactum, open-cover compactness principle, or chosen extension operator is exported.
\end{proof}
