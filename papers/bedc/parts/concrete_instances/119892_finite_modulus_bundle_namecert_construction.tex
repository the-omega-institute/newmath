\begin{definition}[FiniteModulusBundle carrier]
\label{def:finite-modulus-bundle-carrier}

$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ row carrying the requested uniform modulus schedule.
\item $Q$ is the query ledger that lists the finite precision requests and source-window requests served by $U$.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row exposing exactly the observations named by $Q$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback assembled from the displayed modulus requests and stream windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after the finite readback row has been displayed.
\item $S$ is the $\UniformlyContinuousSequenceUp$ handoff row that records which sequence-facing consumer may reuse the same modulus bundle.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the modulus, query, window, readback, seal, handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an L10 consumer request through $U,Q,W,R,E,S,H$.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $U,Q,W,R,E,S,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host function, quotient equality of streams, a selected infinite witness family, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
