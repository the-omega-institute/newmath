$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source-state row, recording the prior finite observation history and the visible obligation or socket before the event is accepted.
\item $A$ is the accepted-record row, naming the public record entered at the transition.
\item $R$ is the route row, exposing the finite generator-closure route, refusal route, or mixed route by which the accepted record is audited.
\item $Q$ is the check row, recording the visible acceptance, validation, refusal, or bounded review state attached to $A$ and $R$.
\item $G$ is the GAP-ledger row, preserving the non-generated residue, compression residue, consumer accountability, and non-escape boundary of the event.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ continuation from $S$ to the accepted event surface and from that surface to downstream consumers.

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EventInstanceUp$, \autoref{ch:visions-inscription-event-as-concrete-instance}, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.

\begin{theorem}[Event instance NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:event-instance-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:event-instance-carrier}. The event-facing rows are $S,A,R,Q,G,U$, while $T,C,P,N$ are structural rows.

The source and accepted-record rows are read first. Without $S$ there is no prior public observation state; without $A$ there is no named record whose acceptance can be audited.

The route, check, and GAP-ledger rows then determine the event boundary. The route row $R$ displays what is generator-closed, refused, or mixed; the check row $Q$ records the visible acceptance state; and the ledger row $G$ keeps the non-generated and compression residue from becoming hidden authority.

Finally $T,C,U,P,N$ transport, continue, expose the consumer, source the packet, and name the same finite transition. Since no private intention, host causal completion, psychological equality, hidden generator authority, or global agency predicate is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

The inscription-facing part is the triple $R,Q,G$. The route row separates generator-closed material from refused or residual material, the check row records the visible acceptance state, and the GAP ledger records the non-escape boundary.

\end{proof}
