\begin{theorem}[Finite cover gauge-radius choice NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cover-gauge-radius-choice-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteCoverGaugeRadiusChoiceUp$ carrier $G=(K,Q,F,L,S,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverGaugeRadiusChoiceUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact-source exposure through $K$, Cousin gauge-source admission through $Q$, finite gauge-cover admission through $F$, finite subcover-ledger coverage through $L$, displayed radius-choice exactness through $S$, $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff through $U$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. No obligation is discharged by host open-cover compactness, countable choice, a detached subcover selector, quotient cover equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-cover-gauge-radius-choice-carrier}. The tuple displays the compact source, Cousin gauge source, finite gauge-cover row, finite subcover ledger, radius-choice ledger, uniform-modulus handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The radius-choice row is downstream of the finite cover data. The rows $Q$ and $F$ expose the available positive gauge radii and finite cells, while $L$ restricts the compact read to the displayed finite coverage ledger.

The row $S$ then records only finitely many positive radius histories, one for each displayed ledger entry that survives the finite cover route. Because this row is part of the carrier, the construction uses no external selector and no host minimum over an open cover.

Finally $U,H,C,P,N$ export, transport, replay, source, and name exactly this route. Since the excluded compactness, choice, quotient, and completeness objects are absent from the carrier, they cannot appear in the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
