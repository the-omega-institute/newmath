\begin{definition}[Dyadic ball base carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-ball-base-carrier}
A $\DyadicBallBaseUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(Y,Q,M,W,S,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Y$ is the $\DyadicBallUp$ source row, exposing the finite center-radius ball request and its dyadic containment vocabulary.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ center and radius ledger, recording the inspected dyadic centers, positive dyadic radii, halved-radius comparisons, and finite refinement budgets.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricBallUp$ comparison row, tying the dyadic ball request to the metric-ball inclusion and overlap checks that a later metric topology consumer may read.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, displaying the finite name-window reads used to expose centers and endpoint requests without selecting an infinite enumeration.
\item $S$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance readback row attached to the displayed StreamName windows and dyadic-radius ledger.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for the inspected center and radius windows, reached only after $Y,Q,M,W,S$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the dyadic ball, dyadic rational ledger, metric-ball comparison, stream window, regular rational readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a dyadic-ball-basis request through $Y,Q,M,W,S,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicBallBaseUp$, $\DyadicBallUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\MetricBallUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicBallBaseUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $Y,Q,M,W,S,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected dense enumeration, an ambient open-set lattice, host compactness, Real completeness, quotient equality of names, or choice of centers.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic ball base NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-ball-base-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicBallBaseUp$ carrier
$$
B=(Y,Q,M,W,S,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicBallBaseUp}$ surface consists exactly of five analytic rows followed by structural support: dyadic ball admission through $Y$; dyadic center-radius ledger exactness through $Q$; metric-ball comparison through $M$; finite StreamName window exposure through $W$; RegSeqRat tolerance readback through $S$; terminal Real sealing through $E$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-ball-base-carrier}. The analytic surface is the finite row sequence $Y,Q,M,W,S,E$, and the structural surface is $H,C,P,N$.

The dyadic source row is read first. It supplies the ball request, while the dyadic rational ledger records the center and radius data that make the request finite and checkable.

The metric comparison row then places the same center-radius request inside the metric-ball vocabulary. The StreamName and RegSeqRat rows expose only finite windows and tolerance readbacks, so the packet does not select an infinite basis enumeration.

Finally the Real seal and structural rows preserve the displayed sequence. Since no other coordinate occurs in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface and no ambient basis, compactness, completeness, quotient, or choice datum is exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic ball base finite radius stability]
\label{thm:dyadic-ball-base-finite-radius-stability}
Let
$$
B=(Y,Q,M,W,S,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
and
$$
B'=(Y',Q',M',W',S',E',H',C',P',N')
$$
be accepted $\DyadicBallBaseUp$ carriers. If $Y,Y'$, $Q,Q'$, $M,M'$, $W,W'$, $S,S'$, and $E,E'$ are componentwise $\hsame$ as dyadic ball rows, dyadic center-radius ledgers, metric-ball comparison rows, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readbacks, and Real seals, then every finite basis read exported through $H,C,P,N$ and $H',C',P',N'$ displays the same center-radius window and the same metric-ball comparison route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project both accepted carriers. The only rows that can feed a basis read are $Y,Q,M,W,S,E$, with $H,C,P,N$ and $H',C',P',N'$ only transporting, replaying, sourcing, and naming the same packet shapes.

Apply the NameCert obligation surface of \autoref{thm:dyadic-ball-base-namecert-obligations}. It forces every consumer read through the dyadic ball source, dyadic rational center-radius ledger, metric-ball comparison, finite window, tolerance readback, and Real seal.

The componentwise $\hsame$ assumptions identify each analytic row in the two packets. Replay through $C$ and $C'$ therefore follows the same finite sequence, and transport through $H$ and $H'$ preserves the displayed center-radius and comparison data. Provenance and naming add no new coordinate.

Thus the exported basis read is stable under the displayed finite radius data. The theorem does not assert an ambient topology basis, a chosen dense sequence, a compact net, Real completeness, or quotient equality of names.
\end{proof}
