\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\MengerCurvatureUp$}

Following Menger's metric curvature tradition, the $\MengerCurvatureUp$
packet records the finite three-point curvature readout as BEDC NameCert data.
It sits after the metric carrier of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the dyadic rational ledger of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational
readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the
terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The
packet names a finite metric-geometry object for downstream comparison and
completion routes; it does not claim global curvature bounds, quotient metric
geometry, selected circumcentres, trichotomy of distances, or completeness.

\begin{definition}[Menger curvature carrier]
\label{def:menger-curvature-carrier}
A $\MengerCurvatureUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(M,T,D,Q,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the source $\MetricUp$ carrier and distance classifier.
\item $T$ is a displayed ordered triple of carried metric points.
\item $D$ is the finite distance ledger for the three pairwise distances of
$T$.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ comparison ledger recording rational
triangle-window tests for the three distances.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the reciprocal perimeter and
area-budget approximants used by the curvature numerator and denominator.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal of the named curvature value.
\item $H$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source points, distance
rows, rational ledgers, readback rows, and seal.
\item $C$ is displayed $\Cont$ replay for the finite curvature computation.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MengerCurvatureUp$, $\MetricUp$,
$\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$,
and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MengerCurvatureUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Menger curvature NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:menger-curvature-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MengerCurvatureUp$ carrier
$K=(M,T,D,Q,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\MengerCurvatureUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric source
exposure through $M$; finite triple exposure through $T$; three-distance ledger
exposure through $D$; dyadic triangle-window comparison through $Q$; regular
rational readback through $R$; terminal Real sealing through $E$;
componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$C$; $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The metric source row supplies only carried
points and displayed distances, while the triple row selects the three finite
endpoints that the curvature packet may inspect.

The dyadic and regular rational rows keep the computation finite. The
$\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger records triangle-window comparisons for the three
distances, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row records the reciprocal perimeter and
area-budget readbacks before the $\RealUp$ seal is formed.

Transport and replay preserve these same rows componentwise. Provenance and
local naming therefore certify exactly the displayed curvature packet and do
not add quotient equality, selected circumcentres, distance trichotomy, global
curvature bounds, or completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Menger curvature dyadic triangle handoff]
\label{thm:menger-curvature-dyadic-triangle-handoff}
Every $\MetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, or $\RealUp$ consumer
of an accepted $\MengerCurvatureUp$ packet must read the finite route
$$
M,T,D,Q,R,E
$$
in order: first the metric source and carried triple, then the three-distance
ledger, then the dyadic triangle-window ledger, then the regular rational
readback, and finally the Real seal. No consumer can bypass $Q$ and recover a
curvature value directly from $M$ and $E$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The distance ledger $D$ is indexed by the displayed triple $T$, so the metric
source $M$ is only a source of finite carried distances. It does not by itself
name a curvature row.

The handoff to $Q$ records the triangle-window checks in the
$\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger. These checks are the finite rational boundary that
precedes the reciprocal perimeter and area-budget readbacks.

The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ reads those budgets before the $\RealUp$ seal $E$
is admitted. Hence any downstream consumer that reaches $E$ has already
crossed the dyadic triangle handoff, and the packet provides no alternate
route through quotient metric geometry, chosen circumcentres, trichotomy, or
ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
