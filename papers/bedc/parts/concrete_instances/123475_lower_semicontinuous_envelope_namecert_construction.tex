\begin{theorem}[Lower-semicontinuous envelope NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lower-semicontinuous-envelope-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lower-semicontinuous-envelope-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly $F,W,A,D,T$, and the structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

The lower-window obligation is read from $W$ and $A$. A consumer must inspect rational lower probes and the displayed $\LowerSemicontinuousUp$ approximants before the envelope row is available.

The domination obligation is read from $D$. It relates the approximant rows to the displayed $\RealUp$-valued function row on the same finite windows, so the packet never appeals to a host supremum or an ambient function-space order.

Finally $T,H,C,P,N$ route the read through stream windows, regular rational readback, real sealing, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since no other carrier coordinate exists, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
