\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KrasnoselskiiFixedPointUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-krasnoselskii-fixed-point-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Krasnoselskii and Browder on compact-plus-contractive fixed point
principles, the $\KrasnoselskiiFixedPointUp$ packet records the finite BEDC
surface for a displayed self-map decomposed into a contraction row and a compact
continuous row on a convex carrier. It sits beside the endpoint contraction
route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-fixed-point-namecert}, the
compact-convex handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-schauder-fixed-point-namecert},
the convex carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, and
the compact completion service of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-metric-completion-namecert}. The packet
names only the displayed decomposition, compactness, convexity, iteration, and
endpoint-seal rows; it does not import a host fixed-point selector, quotient
point equality, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient
$\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Krasnoselskii fixed point carrier]
\label{def:krasnoselskii-fixed-point-carrier}
A $\KrasnoselskiiFixedPointUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(X,V,B,S,A,C,I,E,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the ambient metric or Banach source row on which the fixed-point
request is read.
\item $V$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ admissible carrier row, recording the convex
subpacket in which the fixed point must remain.
\item $B$ is the $\BanachFixedPointUp$ contraction endpoint row for the
contractive summand.
\item $S$ is the $\SchauderFixedPointUp$ compact-convex endpoint row for the
compact continuous summand.
\item $A$ records the displayed algebraic decomposition of the self-map into
contractive and compact-continuous rows.
\item $C$ is the $\CompactMetricCompletionUp$ compactness or completion-facing
seal read before compactness is consumed.
\item $I$ is the finite iteration-stability ledger joining the contraction
budget, compact image window, convex membership row, and endpoint request.
\item $E$ records the fixed-point endpoint seal visible to downstream Real or
completion consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,V,B,S,A,C,I,E$ and
the local certificate data.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a fixed-point request through
the decomposition, compact-convex, contraction, iteration, and endpoint rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\KrasnoselskiiFixedPointUp$,
$\BanachFixedPointUp$, $\SchauderFixedPointUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$,
$\CompactMetricCompletionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KrasnoselskiiFixedPointUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It
has no coordinate for a selected host fixed point, an external compactness
principle, quotient point equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Krasnoselskii fixed point NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:krasnoselskii-fixed-point-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KrasnoselskiiFixedPointUp$ carrier
$K=(X,V,B,S,A,C,I,E,H,R,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\KrasnoselskiiFixedPointUp}$ surface consists exactly of the
following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for $X$ and the admissible convex row $V$;
\item contraction endpoint exposure through $B$;
\item compact-convex endpoint exposure through $S$;
\item decomposition exactness for the displayed self-map row $A$;
\item compact completion or compactness handoff through $C$;
\item iteration-stability routing through $I$;
\item endpoint-seal nonescape through $E$;
\item componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$;
\item displayed $\Cont$ replay through $R$;
\item $\Pkg$ provenance and local naming through $P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is stored outside the finite carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:krasnoselskii-fixed-point-carrier}. The
rows $X,V,B,S,A,C,I,E$ are the only mathematical data available before the
transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows are read.

The fixed-point request then splits along the displayed decomposition row $A$.
The contraction endpoint is read through the Banach fixed-point row $B$, while
the compact continuous endpoint is read through the Schauder row $S$ after the
convex carrier $V$ and compactness handoff $C$ are visible.

The iteration ledger $I$ binds those two endpoint surfaces to the same
fixed-point request. The rows $H,R,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name
that finite route, so the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Krasnoselskii fixed point iteration stability]
\label{thm:krasnoselskii-fixed-point-iteration-stability}
Every transported $\KrasnoselskiiFixedPointUp$ fixed-point read preserves the
same decomposition row $A$, the same contraction endpoint row $B$, the same
compact-convex endpoint row $S$, and the same convex carrier row $V$ before the
endpoint seal $E$ is consumed. In particular, the iteration ledger cannot be
detached into an independent Banach route, an independent Schauder route, or a
host-selected fixed point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the componentwise transport row $H$. It moves the ambient carrier,
convex admissible row, Banach endpoint row, Schauder endpoint row,
decomposition row, compact handoff, iteration ledger, and endpoint seal
together.

Apply the NameCert obligations of
\autoref{thm:krasnoselskii-fixed-point-namecert-obligations}. The fixed-point
read must expose $A$ before either endpoint row is consumed, and it must expose
$V$ and $C$ before the compact-convex side is read.

Replay through $R$ then follows the same decomposition and iteration route.
Since $I$ has no coordinate outside the carrier and $E$ is only the downstream
seal for this route, transport cannot replace the packet by a separate Banach
fixed point, a separate Schauder fixed point, or a selected host endpoint.
\end{proof}
