Following Heine--Cantor and Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis, the $\HeineCantorFiniteModulusUp$ packet records the finite-modulus route by which compact metric continuity becomes a uniform-modulus read without importing an open-cover compactness theorem. It sits beside the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the continuous-map pointwise rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the finite-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-heine-cantor-finite-net-namecert}, the finite lower-bound fold of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-lower-bound-fold-namecert}, the $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, and the L10 readback boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The certificate names the finite rows that a compact-uniform-continuity consumer may inspect; it does not select a host modulus or identify quotient functions.

\begin{definition}[Heine-Cantor finite-modulus carrier]
\label{def:heine-cantor-finite-modulus-carrier}
A $\HeineCantorFiniteModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(K,F,N,J,L,U,W,R,E,H,C,P,Z)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row displaying the compact metric carrier and its finite-net coverage surface.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row whose pointwise modulus data are read only at the centers exposed by $K$ and $N$.
\item $N$ is the compact finite-net selector row, using the same finite center bundle and coverage ledger as $\HeineCantorFiniteNetUp$.
\item $J$ is the pointwise modulus ledger pairing each center in $N$ with the corresponding local continuity radius and target tolerance row from $F$.
\item $L$ is the $\FiniteLowerBoundFoldUp$ row that folds the finitely many displayed lower-bound candidates in $J$ into one positive precision row.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer row reached only after $K,F,N,J,L$ have been displayed.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ readback window opened for the source and target values used by the uniform-modulus consumer.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the observations in $W$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the finite-net, fold, uniform-modulus, stream-window, and regular-rational rows have been consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, continuous-map row, finite-net selector, pointwise ledger, fold, uniform-modulus consumer, L10 readback rows, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a target-tolerance request through the finite-net selector, pointwise modulus ledger, finite fold, uniform-modulus consumer, and L10 readback route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HeineCantorFiniteModulusUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\HeineCantorFiniteNetUp$, $\FiniteLowerBoundFoldUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $Z$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HeineCantorFiniteModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, a host-selected modulus, quotient function equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Heine-Cantor finite-modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:heine-cantor-finite-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HeineCantorFiniteModulusUp$ carrier $M=(K,F,N,J,L,U,W,R,E,H,C,P,Z)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HeineCantorFiniteModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed packet, compact-net exposure through $K,N$, pointwise-to-uniform modulus handoff through $F,J,L,U$, L10 readback nonescape through $W,R,E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $Z$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:heine-cantor-finite-modulus-carrier}. The analytic rows are the compact source $K$, continuous-map row $F$, finite-net selector $N$, pointwise ledger $J$, fold row $L$, uniform-modulus consumer $U$, and readback rows $W,R,E$.

Read the compact-net exposure first. The selector row $N$ uses only the finite centers and coverage ledger supplied by $\CompactMetricUp$ and the sibling $\HeineCantorFiniteNetUp$ route, so no open-cover compactness or host-selected cover is present.

Next pass from pointwise continuity to a uniform modulus. The ledger $J$ pairs each displayed center with its pointwise modulus row from $F$, and the fold row $L$ is the finite lower-bound fold consumed by $U$.

Finally attach the L10 readback route. The rows $W,R,E$ expose StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, and the terminal Real seal only after the finite-modulus route has been displayed, while $H,C,P,Z$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Thus the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate surface and export no host modulus selection, quotient function equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
