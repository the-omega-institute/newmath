\begin{theorem}[Real completion universal-property forward link]
\label{thm:real-completion-universal-property-forward-link}
Every $\RealCompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ read consumed by $\FastCauchyCompletionUp$ must expose the dyadic source row, the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback row, the Bishop completion handoff, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal route before a modulus-controlled completion consumer may read it. In carrier notation the forward link is the displayed route
$$
D,S,R,B,E,U,H,C,P,N
$$
of \autoref{def:real-completion-universal-property-carrier}, consumed before the $\FastCauchyCompletionUp$ route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fast-cauchy-completion-namecert} opens its $\CauchyModulusUp$ threshold, $\StreamNameUp$ window, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and $\RealUp$ seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-completion-universal-property-carrier}. The universal-property packet exposes the dyadic source, stream window, regular readback, Bishop regular completion handoff, completion-interface row, universal-property row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows as its entire public route.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:real-completion-universal-property-namecert-obligations}. Dense-source exposure, stream-window exactness, regular-readback handoff, extension existence, extension uniqueness, and non-escape all occur before a downstream consumer can inspect the universal-property row.

Now use the sibling route of \autoref{thm:real-completion-universal-property-sibling-route} and the completion-facing route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fast-cauchy-completion-namecert}. A modulus-controlled completion consumer can only consume the universal-property packet after the dyadic source, $\StreamNameUp$ row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ row, Bishop completion handoff, and $\RealUp$ seal route have been displayed, so the forward link exports no quotient stream, selected limit, countable choice, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
