\begin{theorem}[Real completion universal-property extension stability]
\label{thm:real-completion-universal-property-extension-stability}
Let $G=(D,S,R,B,E,U,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RealCompletionUniversalPropertyUp$ carrier. If two accepted extension reads share the displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ source row $D$, $\StreamNameUp$ window $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$, Bishop regular completion handoff $B$, completion-interface row $E$, and universal-property row $U$, then every downstream completion-facing consumer must read the same $H,C,P,N$ route before uniqueness is public. The stability claim exports no quotient stream, selected limit, host equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-completion-universal-property-carrier}. The extension route enters through $D,S,R,B,E,U$ and has no other entrance coordinate for a completion-facing read.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:real-completion-universal-property-namecert-obligations}. The extension existence and uniqueness clauses are recorded only over the displayed dyadic source, stream window, regular readback, Bishop completion handoff, completion-interface, and universal-property rows.

Use Real-seal uniqueness and extension-uniqueness factorization, \autoref{thm:real-completion-universal-property-real-seal-uniqueness} and \autoref{thm:real-completion-universal-property-extension-uniqueness-factorization}. They force two reads sharing the displayed extension route to pass through the same structural route before a downstream consumer may read uniqueness.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$. Transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the common route and provide no coordinate for quotient streams, selected limits, host equality, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
