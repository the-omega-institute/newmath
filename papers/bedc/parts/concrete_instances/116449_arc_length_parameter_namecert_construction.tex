\begin{definition}[Arc-length parameter carrier]
\label{def:arc-length-parameter-carrier}
An $\ArcLengthParameterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(G,M,D,V,R,L,J,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the curve row, naming the displayed source interval, target points, and the curve graph through the $\ContinuousMapUp$ surface.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ distance row used to measure adjacent curve samples.
\item $D$ is the finite Darboux or tagged partition row, recording the ordered sample ledger from which polygonal lengths are read.
\item $V$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ variation-bound row, carrying dyadic upper and lower budgets for partition-length comparison.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the resulting length approximants.
\item $L$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row naming the accumulated length read.
\item $J$ is the located-integral handoff row, available when the same partition ledger is consumed through $\LocatedRiemannIntegralUp$.
\item $S$ is the local parameter-scale row, pairing initial segment requests with their displayed length readbacks.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for curve, metric, partition, dyadic variation, regular readback, Real seal, integral handoff, parameter-scale, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a parameter request through $G,M,D,V,R,L,J,S$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ArcLengthParameterUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\LocatedRiemannIntegralUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ArcLengthParameterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $G,M,D,V,R,L,J,S,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host limits of partition sums, quotient curves, chosen inverse parameters, global geodesic structure, countable-choice schedules, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Arc-length parameter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:arc-length-parameter-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:arc-length-parameter-carrier}. The rows $G,M,D,V,R,L,J,S$ are the whole numerical and geometric surface before any structural row is read.

The curve and length data are exhausted by $G,M,D,V$. The curve row gives the displayed continuous map, the metric row gives adjacent-sample distances, the partition row records the finite ledger of samples, and the dyadic variation row stores the comparison budgets for polygonal length reads.

The readback and parameter data are exhausted by $R,L,J,S$. Regular rational readback turns the partition ledger into a finite Cauchy-style name, the Real seal records the terminal length read, the located-integral handoff supplies the sibling integral route, and the parameter-scale row pairs initial segment requests with those displayed length reads.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly the same displayed rows. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for host partition limits, quotient curves, selected inverse parameters, global geodesic structure, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, the listed rows are precisely the NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}
