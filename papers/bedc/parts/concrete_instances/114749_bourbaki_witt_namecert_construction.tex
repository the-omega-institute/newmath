Following Bourbaki and Witt, the $\BourbakiWittUp$ packet records the constructive order-theoretic fixed-point route for a progressive monotone self-map on a displayed chain-complete poset source. It sits after the separated order carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poset-namecert}, the certified-family bound surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-complete-lattice-namecert}, and the complete-lattice monotone fixed-row route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tarski-fixed-point-namecert}. The packet names only finite chain rows, displayed upper-bound witnesses, approximation ledgers, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not assert Zorn's lemma, full choice, unrestricted chain completeness, quotient order equality, or an ambient maximal-chain theorem.

\begin{definition}[Bourbaki-Witt carrier]
\label{def:bourbaki-witt-carrier}
A $\BourbakiWittUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(O,F,G,U,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the $\PosetUp$ source row equipped with the displayed chain-completeness boundary accepted by this packet.
\item $F$ is the progressive monotone self-map row, recording both monotonicity and the displayed comparison $x \leq F(x)$ for carried elements.
\item $G$ is the finite chain ledger, listing the inspected chain windows and their inherited $\PosetUp$ comparison rows.
\item $U$ is the displayed chain-bound witness row assigning an upper-bound name to each inspected chain window.
\item $A$ is the finite approximation ledger, recording the iterated or successor-stage rows used by a fixed-point consumer before the bound row is read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $O,F,G,U,A,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the poset source and progressive monotone map through chain windows, upper-bound witnesses, approximation rows, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BourbakiWittUp$, $\PosetUp$, $\CompleteLatticeUp$, $\TarskiFixedPointUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BourbakiWittUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $O,F,G,U,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a choice-selected maximal chain, Zorn principle, unrestricted chain completeness, quotient order equality, host ordinal recursion, or ambient lattice semantics.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bourbaki-Witt NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bourbaki-witt-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BourbakiWittUp$ carrier $B=(O,F,G,U,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BourbakiWittUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the chain-complete $\PosetUp$ source row, progressive monotone map exposure through $F$, chain-window exposure through $G$, displayed chain-bound witnesses through $U$, finite approximation coverage through $A$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bourbaki-witt-carrier}. The displayed rows are exactly $O,F,G,U,A,H,C,P,N$, so every local obligation must be read from one of those rows.

Read the order-theoretic rows first. The source row $O$ supplies the separated partial-order carrier and the displayed chain-completeness boundary, while $F$ supplies the progressive monotone self-map row consumed by the fixed-point route.

The chain rows $G,U,A$ then account for the constructive content. Each inspected chain window is named before its upper-bound witness is used, and each approximation step is recorded before a consumer may read a fixed-row handoff.

Finally use $H,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no carrier coordinate stores a maximal-chain selector, Zorn principle, unrestricted chain-completeness theorem, quotient order equality, host ordinal recursion, or ambient complete-lattice semantics, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bourbaki-Witt chain handoff]
\label{thm:bourbaki-witt-chain-handoff}
Every fixed-point consumer of an accepted $\BourbakiWittUp$ packet must read the route
$$
\PosetUp(O),F,G,U,A,H,C,P,N
$$
before it may use the exported fixed-row surface. The route hands finite chain windows and their displayed upper-bound witnesses to $\CompleteLatticeUp$ and $\TarskiFixedPointUp$ consumers without selecting a maximal chain, invoking Zorn's lemma, asserting full choice, or claiming unrestricted chain completeness outside the displayed source row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the $\PosetUp$ source row. It supplies the only order comparison surface visible to the packet, and the chain-completeness boundary is read as part of that displayed source rather than as an ambient theorem.

Apply the progressive monotone map row. The row $F$ records both monotonicity over the carried order and the progressive comparison used to extend each inspected approximation window.

Use the chain ledger and bound rows next. The row $G$ names the finite chain windows, $U$ supplies their displayed upper-bound witnesses, and $A$ records the approximation ledger that a fixed-point consumer may replay.

The handoff to $\CompleteLatticeUp$ or $\TarskiFixedPointUp$ is therefore mediated by $H,C,P,N$. Transport and replay preserve the same finite rows, while provenance and local naming prevent the endpoint from importing Zorn, full choice, unrestricted chain completeness, quotient order equality, or a detached maximal-chain construction.
\end{proof}
