\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SemiAlgebraicSetUp$}

Following Tarski and Seidenberg, the $\SemiAlgebraicSetUp$ packet records a real-algebraic set classifier as finite polynomial sign data rather than as an ambient subset oracle. It sits between the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the polynomial carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-namecert}, the set-like membership surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-setlike-namecert}, the first-order formula row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-firstorder-namecert}, and the ordered comparison row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-order-namecert}. The packet names the constructive BEDC carrier for finite Boolean combinations of polynomial sign tests over displayed real coordinates.

\begin{definition}[SemiAlgebraicSet carrier]
\label{def:semi-algebraic-set-carrier}
A $\SemiAlgebraicSetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(X,R,P,S,F,O,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the finite coordinate arity and variable-context row for the ambient real affine space.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ coordinate-seal row. It records the real-name data through which coordinate comparisons are read.
\item $P$ is the finite $\PolynomialUp$ ledger. Its entries list the displayed polynomial terms used by the local classifier.
\item $S$ is the polynomial sign ledger. For each displayed polynomial row and each visible coordinate tuple, it records whether the public test reads $<$, $=$, or $>$ through the ordered real surface.
\item $F$ is the $\FirstOrderUp$ formula row generated from the finite sign atoms by Boolean operations and bounded displayed quantifier windows.
\item $O$ is the $\OrderUp$ comparison row used by sign tests before the formula row is evaluated.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,R,P,S,F,O,C,G,N$ and for the local classifier.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from coordinate readback through polynomial evaluation, sign comparison, formula evaluation, transport, provenance, and local naming.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SemiAlgebraicSetUp$, $\RealUp$, $\PolynomialUp$, $\SetLikeUp$, $\FirstOrderUp$, $\OrderUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SemiAlgebraicSetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $X,R,P,S,F,O,H,C,G,N$. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary powerset predicate, a quotient of polynomial formulas, a selected decomposition into cells, an undecided host real comparison, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SemiAlgebraicSet NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:semi-algebraic-set-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SemiAlgebraicSetUp$ carrier $A=(X,R,P,S,F,O,H,C,G,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SemiAlgebraicSetUp}$ surface consists exactly of coordinate-context admission, real-coordinate seal accountability, polynomial-ledger admission, polynomial-sign ledger admission, first-order formula formation, ordered sign comparison, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming for the rows $X,R,P,S,F,O,H,C,G,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:semi-algebraic-set-carrier}. The algebraic rows are exactly the coordinate context, real-coordinate seals, finite polynomial ledger, sign ledger, formula row, and ordered comparison row.

The obligation order follows the displayed classifier. A membership read first exposes the real coordinate rows $R$, then evaluates a polynomial from $P$, then reads its sign through $S$ and $O$, and only then evaluates the formula row $F$.

The rows $H,C,G,N$ preserve this route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the carrier contains no arbitrary subset predicate, cell-decomposition choice, quotient formula coordinate, host comparison oracle, or ambient completeness row, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[SemiAlgebraicSet polynomial sign ledger]
\label{thm:semi-algebraic-set-polynomial-sign-ledger}
Every membership read from an accepted $\SemiAlgebraicSetUp$ carrier factors through the displayed polynomial sign ledger
$$
R\longmapsto P\longmapsto S\longmapsto O\longmapsto F\longmapsto H,C,G,N .
$$
Thus a consumer receives only finite sign tests for displayed $\PolynomialUp$ rows over displayed $\RealUp$ coordinates before the $\SetLikeUp$ membership surface is public. It receives no arbitrary subset predicate, no selected cell decomposition, no quotient formula equality, no undecided host trichotomy, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier projection. The only route from a coordinate tuple to membership is the finite row sequence through real coordinate seals, polynomial rows, sign comparisons, ordered tests, and first-order formula evaluation.

The sign ledger $S$ is attached to the displayed polynomial list $P$ and the ordered comparison row $O$. It therefore cannot be replaced by an external predicate on real tuples or by a host-level decision procedure.

The structural rows $H,C,G,N$ transport and name the same finite route. After those rows are read, the public $\SetLikeUp$ surface receives a membership value generated by the displayed sign ledger alone.
\end{proof}
