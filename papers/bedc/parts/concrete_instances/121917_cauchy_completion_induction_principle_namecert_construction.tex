\label{thm:cauchy-completion-induction-principle-namecert-obligations}

\item completion-boundary routing, requiring $B$ to expose the quotient-free $\BishopRegularCauchyCompletionUp$ boundary before any universal-property handoff is read;

\end{theorem}

The induction ledger is local. It stores the base row, successor row, and terminal handoff row over the same displayed route, and $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that route without adding another analytic coordinate.
