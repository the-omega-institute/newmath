\begin{definition}[Cauchy subsequence limit seal carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-subsequence-limit-seal-carrier}

$$

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $I$ is the cofinal subsequence index row, recording monotonicity, cofinal reach, and the finite selected indices used by the route.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance data, including the requested precision and the finite comparison ledger that justifies the selected tail window.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,I,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host-selected infinite subsequence, quotient stream equality, host real equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy subsequence limit seal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-subsequence-limit-seal-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchySubsequenceLimitSealUp$ carrier $L=(S,I,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchySubsequenceLimitSealUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the source sequence window $S$, cofinal subsequence index row $I$, dyadic tolerance data $D$, $\StreamNameUp$ windows $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$, $\RealUp$ seal $E$, componentwise transport $H$, displayed replay $C$, package provenance $P$, and local naming $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-subsequence-limit-seal-carrier}. Each row named in the statement is a displayed coordinate of the finite packet, and the carrier lists no hidden coordinate for a selected infinite subsequence, quotient stream, or ambient completion theorem.

The source and index obligations are $S$ and $I$. The dyadic tolerance and finite-window obligations are $D$ and $W$, and the Real-facing route reaches $R$ before it reaches the terminal seal $E$.

Finally use the structural rows. Componentwise transport $H$, replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ preserve the same route for the selector, regular-subsequence-limit, subsequence-limit, StreamName, RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, and Real siblings named above.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy subsequence limit seal nonescape]
\label{thm:cauchy-subsequence-limit-seal-nonescape}
Every completion-facing or Real-facing consumer of an accepted $\CauchySubsequenceLimitSealUp$ packet must read the route
$$

$$
before it consumes any subsequence-limit seal. The route exports no host-selected infinite subsequence, quotient stream equality, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:cauchy-subsequence-limit-seal-namecert-obligations}. They force every consumer to display the source window, cofinal index row, dyadic tolerance data, finite StreamName windows, regular rational readback, and Real seal as one carrier-local route.

The sibling handoffs preserve the same order. The selector boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bolzanoweierstrassselector-namecert}, the regular Cauchy subsequence limit packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-subsequence-limit-namecert}, and the subsequence limit boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subsequence-limit-namecert} can feed the row $I$ or the readback row $R$, but none supplies a hidden quotient stream or host choice coordinate.

Since the classifier has only $S,I,D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$, any completion-facing read that asks for host real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness asks for data outside the packet. The Real seal is therefore a terminal finite seal for the displayed subsequence route, not an escape hatch into host completion.
\end{proof}
