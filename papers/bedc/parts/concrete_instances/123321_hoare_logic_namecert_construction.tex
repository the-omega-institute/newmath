\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HoareLogicUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-hoare-logic-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Hoare's axiomatic basis for computer programming, the $\HoareLogicUp$ packet records a Hoare triple proof as finite assertion, command-trace, rule-ledger, replay, transport, provenance, and local naming data. It sits beside the first-order assertion surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-firstorder-namecert}, the computability boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-computable-namecert}, the machine-trace carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-turingmachine-namecert}, the proof-trace normal form of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-proof-trace-normal-form-namecert}, and the Kripke forcing surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kripke-semantics-namecert}. The packet names finite program-logic certificate rows only; it does not import host semantic validity, a global weakest-precondition oracle, total correctness, or an ambient program equivalence theorem.

\begin{definition}[Hoare logic NameCert carrier]
\label{def:hoare-logic-carrier}
A $\HoareLogicUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(A,T,R,L,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the assertion-source row, exposing precondition, postcondition, and intermediate assertion formulas through the $\FirstOrderUp$ surface.
\item $T$ is the command-step trace row, recording the finite operational trace inspected through $\ComputableUp$ and $\TuringMachineUp$ rows.
\item $R$ is the pre/postcondition row ledger, listing the Hoare triple boundary rows whose source and target assertions are read from $A$.
\item $L$ is the proof-rule ledger, containing exactly the displayed rule instances used by the certificate: assignment, sequencing, conditional, loop-invariant, consequence, and structural transport rows.
\item $E$ is the proof-trace replay row, reading $L$ through the $\ProofTraceNormalFormUp$ sibling before any consumer may cite the resulting triple.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for assertion rows, command-step rows, pre/postcondition rows, rule-ledger rows, replay rows, provenance rows, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ routes from assertion readback to command-step replay, from command-step replay to rule-ledger replay, from rule-ledger replay to proof-trace normal form, and from the resulting triple to downstream program-logic consumers.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HoareLogicUp$, $\FirstOrderUp$, $\ComputableUp$, $\TuringMachineUp$, $\ProofTraceNormalFormUp$, $\KripkeSemanticsUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HoareLogicUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,T,R,L,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host semantic validity, a global weakest-precondition transformer, total correctness, unbounded proof search, hidden machine semantics, or ambient program equivalence.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hoare logic NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hoare-logic-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HoareLogicUp$ carrier
$$
J=(A,T,R,L,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\HoareLogicUp}$ surface consists exactly of assertion-source admission through $A$, command-step trace admission through $T$, pre/postcondition ledger exposure through $R$, proof-rule ledger exactness through $L$, proof-trace replay through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hoare-logic-carrier}. The visible proof-theoretic rows are precisely the assertion source $A$, command-step trace $T$, pre/postcondition ledger $R$, rule ledger $L$, and proof-trace replay $E$.

Read the sibling rows in order. $\FirstOrderUp$ supplies the assertion formulas, $\ComputableUp$ and $\TuringMachineUp$ supply the finite command-step trace boundary, and $\ProofTraceNormalFormUp$ supplies the replay discipline for the rule ledger.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name that same finite packet. Since the carrier has no row for host semantic validity, weakest-precondition search, total correctness, hidden machine semantics, or ambient program equivalence, no local NameCert obligation can be stored outside the displayed route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hoare logic rule-soundness ledger]
\label{thm:hoare-logic-rule-soundness-ledger}
Every public rule-soundness read of an accepted $\HoareLogicUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
A \to T \to R \to L \to E .
$$
The rule ledger may cite $\KripkeSemanticsUp$ only as a displayed forcing-style accountability row for assertion transport; it does not export host semantic validity, a global weakest-precondition transformer, total correctness, hidden proof search, or ambient program equivalence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with carrier admission. The precondition and postcondition formulas are read from $A$, the command behavior visible to the certificate is read from $T$, and the triple boundary is recorded in $R$.

Next read the proof-rule ledger $L$. Each rule instance is a displayed finite row and is replayed through $E$ before the triple is public. The proof-trace normal form prevents a consumer from replacing the ledger by an unstated host derivation.

Kripke-style accountability is only a sibling row for assertion transport. It can record that assertion movement is read through displayed forcing data, but it does not add semantic validity or weakest-precondition computation to the carrier.

Finally use $H,C,P,N$. These rows preserve the same route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Therefore every public rule-soundness read is confined to $A,T,R,L,E,H,C,P,N$ and exports none of the excluded host principles.
\end{proof}
