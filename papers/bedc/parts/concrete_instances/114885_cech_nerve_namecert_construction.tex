\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CechNerveUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cech-nerve-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Cech, Leray, and the classical cover-nerve construction, the $\CechNerveUp$ packet records the finite simplicial object determined by a displayed finite topological cover. It sits after the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the finite-radius cover ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-lebesgue-number-namecert}, and the finite face inventory of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-simplicialcomplex-namecert}. The packet names cover members, intersection witnesses, nerve faces, stability, ledger, transport, provenance, and local naming rows; it does not assert the nerve theorem, selected points in arbitrary intersections, open-cover compactness, quotient realization equality, or ambient homology computation.

\begin{definition}[Cech nerve carrier]
\label{def:cech-nerve-carrier}
A $\CechNerveUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(T,L,U,F,I,S,G,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row, carrying the indexed-open membership classifier used by the cover.
\item $L$ is the $\FiniteLebesgueNumberUp$ or finite-cover radius row, recording the finite cover scale when the cover is served by compact or metric data.
\item $U$ is the finite cover-member row, listing the displayed cover indices and their topology-open witnesses.
\item $F$ is the nerve-face classifier row: a finite subset of cover indices is accepted exactly when its displayed intersection witness is present.
\item $I$ is the intersection-witness ledger, recording the finite meet or overlap data consumed by $F$.
\item $S$ is the $\SimplicialComplexUp$ row whose faces are the accepted finite nerve faces.
\item $G$ is the stability row, carrying componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology, cover, intersection, nerve-face, simplicial-complex, ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $H$ is the nonescape ledger, refusing selected intersection points, arbitrary subcovers, host nerve realizations, quotient simplicial equality, and ambient homology computation.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a cover request through $T,L,U,I,F,S,G,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CechNerveUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\FiniteLebesgueNumberUp$, $\SimplicialComplexUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CechNerveUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed cover members, intersection witnesses, nerve-face classifier, simplicial-complex row, stability row, ledger row, replay, provenance, and local naming data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cech nerve NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cech-nerve-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CechNerveUp$ carrier $K=(T,L,U,F,I,S,G,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CechNerveUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission through $T,L,U,F,I,S,G,H,C,P,N$; classifier admission for the finite nerve-face row $F$; stability under componentwise transport through $G$; ledger exposure through $I,H$; replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; local naming through $N$; and nonescape for selected intersection points, arbitrary subcover choice, host nerve realizations, quotient simplicial equality, and ambient homology computation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cech-nerve-carrier}. The topology row $T$, finite-radius or finite-cover row $L$, cover-member row $U$, intersection ledger $I$, nerve-face classifier $F$, and simplicial-complex row $S$ are the only geometric coordinates in the packet.

The classifier is finite. A face is accepted only through $F$, and $F$ reads the displayed intersection witnesses in $I$ over the listed cover members $U$. Thus the nerve is the finite face inventory delivered to $\SimplicialComplexUp$, not a host simplicial realization.

Stability is componentwise. The row $G$ transports topology, cover, intersection, face, and simplicial-complex data together, while $C$ replays the same route and $P,N$ source and name the packet.

Finally read the ledger row $H$. Since the carrier has no coordinate for selected intersection points, arbitrary subcover choice, quotient realization equality, or ambient homology computation, those objects cannot be exported by an accepted $\CechNerveUp$ read.
\end{proof}
