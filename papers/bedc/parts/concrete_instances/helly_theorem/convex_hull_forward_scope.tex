\begin{theorem}[Helly convex-hull forward scope]
\label{thm:helly-theorem-convex-hull-forward-scope}
The finite-intersection row of an accepted $\HellyTheoremUp$ packet is a forward scope for the $\CaratheodoryConvexHullUp$ consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-caratheodory-convex-hull-namecert}. A convex-hull membership request may consume the Helly row only after the convex-set and polytope sibling packets have displayed their finite family, face-boundary, support, and coefficient ledgers. The handoff exports a finite subfamily certificate for convex-hull reasoning; it does not export ambient separation, selected minimal supports, quotient hull equality, host convexity, or Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
