\begin{theorem}[Helly convex-set dependency route]\label{thm:helly-convex-set-dependency-route}
Every accepted $\HellyTheoremUp$ finite convex-intersection read first consumes the convex carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert} and the finite polytope witness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polytope-namecert}. The route exports no host separation theorem, selected intersection point, quotient convex-set equality, unrestricted finite-cover oracle, or ambient standard-model convexity.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the Helly carrier of \autoref{def:helly-theorem-carrier}. The convex-family row is consumed through the convex-set certificate, and the finite witness row is consumed through the polytope certificate.

After those sibling rows are visible, the Helly subfamily and intersection rows can be read as finite $\BHist$ data. The structural rows transport, replay, source, and name the same route, so the carrier has no coordinate for a host separation theorem, selected point, quotient convex-set equality, or unrestricted cover oracle.
\end{proof}
