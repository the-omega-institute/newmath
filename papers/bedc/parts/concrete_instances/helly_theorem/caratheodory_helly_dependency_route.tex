\begin{lemma}[Caratheodory--Helly finite dependency route]
\label{lem:helly-theorem-caratheodory-helly-dependency-route}
Every $\HellyTheoremUp$ consumer that uses Caratheodory-style convex support data must first expose the finite convex-family row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, the finite witness and face rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polytope-namecert}, and the bounded-support handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-caratheodory-convex-hull-namecert}. Only after those rows are visible may the Helly packet read its subfamily-intersection ledger and export the full-family intersection row through $\NameCert_{\HellyTheoremUp}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Project the $\HellyTheoremUp$ carrier from \autoref{def:helly-theorem-carrier}. The convex-family coordinate is displayed before the subfamily ledger can be inspected, and the finite witness rows are carried separately from the ambient affine data.

The Caratheodory convex-hull packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-caratheodory-convex-hull-namecert} then supplies only bounded support rows. It does not select an ambient intersection point or add a host separation theorem.

Thus the dependency lattice is finite: convex-family rows and polytope witnesses feed the Caratheodory support route, and that route feeds the Helly subfamily ledger before the full-family row is exported.
\end{proof}
