The finite-intersection certificates used by $\HellyTheoremUp$ enter through the same convex-row surface as \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert} and the finite hull route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-caratheodory-convex-hull-namecert}. A Helly carrier can therefore read its displayed family of convex sets, witness subfamilies, and intersection rows as finite $\BHist$ data before it exports the Helly conclusion, rather than importing an ambient convexity theorem or an unrestricted finite-cover oracle.
