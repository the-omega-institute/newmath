\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CantorBarModulusUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cantor-bar-modulus-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Brouwer and Troelstra--van Dalen on bars in choice-sequence spaces, the $\CantorBarModulusUp$ packet records the finite prefix certificate by which a decidable bar on Cantor space yields a displayed modulus for downstream Real and Completion consumers. It sits beside the Cantor-space carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cantorspace-namecert}, the fan-theorem packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fantheorem-namecert}, the finite stream interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite NameCert route for prefix-depth extraction; it is not countable choice, not an open-cover compactness theorem, not a selected branch principle, and not ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Cantor bar modulus carrier]
\label{def:cantor-bar-modulus-carrier}
A $\CantorBarModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(C,B,F,S,Q,R,T,H,E,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\CantorSpaceUp$ row, recording binary choice streams by finite prefix observations.
\item $B$ is the decidable-bar row, recording the finite test that recognizes when a prefix has met the bar.
\item $F$ is the $\FanTheoremUp$ row, recording the finite fan bound consumed by the packet.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ row, naming the inspected stream windows and their prefix schedule.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to rational data extracted from the accepted prefix window.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row consumed by Real-facing and Completion-facing readers after the prefix modulus has been displayed.
\item $T$ is the transport row, preserving Cantor prefixes, bar decisions, fan bounds, stream windows, regular-rational readback, and Real seals componentwise under $\hsame$.
\item $H$ is the replay row, recording displayed $\Cont$ replay from prefix inspection through bar acceptance to modulus export.
\item $E$ is the export row, naming the finite prefix depth available to downstream consumers.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CantorBarModulusUp$, $\CantorSpaceUp$, $\FanTheoremUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CantorBarModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an infinite branch search, an undecidable bar, a choice-selected depth, a quotient stream equality, a hidden Real limit, or ambient compactness outside the packet $M$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cantor bar modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cantor-bar-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:cantor-bar-modulus-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\CantorBarModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of Cantor-space admission through $C$, decidable-bar exposure through $B$, fan-bound exposure through $F$, stream-window naming through $S$, regular-rational readback through $Q$, Real sealing through $R$, componentwise transport through $T$, displayed replay through $H$, modulus export through $E$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The rows $C,B,F,S,Q,R,T,H,E,P,N$ are the only coordinates available to the local certificate.

The source rows give the constructive bar surface. Row $C$ supplies finite Cantor prefixes, row $B$ makes bar membership decidable on those prefixes, and row $F$ supplies the fan-bound row that converts local bar decisions into a uniform finite depth.

The downstream rows then bind the exported modulus. Row $S$ names the finite stream windows, $Q$ records the regular-rational readback available after those windows, and $R$ records the Real-facing seal. Rows $T,H,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name exactly that route, so no hidden branch search or choice-selected depth remains available.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cantor bar modulus finite-branch handoff]
\label{thm:cantor-bar-modulus-finite-branch-handoff}
Every Real-facing or Completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\CantorBarModulusUp$ packet must read a finite Cantor prefix route
$$
C,B,F,S,Q,R,T,H,E,P,N
$$
before using the exported depth row $E$. The handoff exposes CantorSpace, DecidableBar, FanTheorem, StreamName, RegSeqRat, Real, transport, replay, provenance, and local-name rows, and it exports no countable-choice schedule, selected infinite branch, quotient stream equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:cantor-bar-modulus-carrier}. A consumer can reach the export row $E$ only through the displayed Cantor-space source, decidable bar, fan-bound, stream-window, regular-rational readback, and Real-seal rows.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:cantor-bar-modulus-namecert-obligations}. They force the bar-depth read to pass through $C,B,F$ before the stream and Real-facing rows $S,Q,R$ can be consumed.

Finally use the structural rows. Transport $T$ preserves the displayed rows componentwise, replay $H$ follows the same finite route, provenance $P$ names the sibling chapters, and local name $N$ seals the packet. Since the carrier has no row for an infinite search or an external selector, the public handoff is exactly the finite-prefix route.
\end{proof}
