\begin{definition}[ZTransform carrier]
\label{def:z-transform-carrier}
A $\ZTransformUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(W,S,F,R,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is the finite one-sided coefficient-window row, exposing only inspected nonnegative indices and their coefficient reads.
\item $S$ is the shift row, recording how a finite delay or advance request reindexes the displayed one-sided window.
\item $F$ is the formal-power readback row, reading the inspected coefficients as a finite $\FormalPowerSeriesUp$ or $\GeneratingFunctionUp$ prefix.
\item $R$ is the radius ledger, recording the displayed convergence-radius or subradius data available to downstream $\RealPowerSeriesUp$ consumers.
\item $T$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for coefficient budgets, radius budgets, and endpoint readback requests.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for coefficient windows, shift rows, formal-power readback, radius and tolerance ledgers, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a transform request through $W,S,F,R,T,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ZTransformUp$, $\GeneratingFunctionUp$, $\LaplaceTransformUp$, $\DirichletSeriesUp$, $\RealPowerSeriesUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\FormalPowerSeriesUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ZTransformUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $W,S,F,R,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for analytic convergence, inverse-transform reconstruction, bilateral negative-index data, selected infinite sums, quotient equality of series, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ZTransform NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:z-transform-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ZTransformUp$ carrier $Z=(W,S,F,R,T,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ZTransformUp}$ surface consists exactly of one-sided coefficient-window admission, shift-row admission, formal-power readback, radius-ledger exposure, dyadic tolerance exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every transform read factors through the finite route
$$
W,S,F,R,T \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
It exports no analytic convergence theorem, inversion theorem, bilateral transform, host infinite sum, quotient series equality, or Lean-checked target.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:z-transform-carrier}. The one-sided coefficient window $W$, shift row $S$, formal-power readback $F$, radius ledger $R$, and tolerance ledger $T$ are the only transform-facing rows in the packet.

The structural rows preserve that visible route. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport can identify only displayed rows, and $\Cont$ replay can move only along the finite route from coefficient windows through local naming.

The package and local NameCert rows cite the same packet. Since no excluded coordinate occurs in $Z$, the certificate cannot export analytic convergence, inversion, bilateral negative-index data, selected infinite sums, quotient series equality, or Lean verification.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:z-transform-generating-function-handoff}
