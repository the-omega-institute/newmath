\item $I$ is the $\ListUp$ finite input row, recording the ordered finite family of $\RealUp$ names to be aggregated.

\item $A$ is the finite averaging row, carried through the same finite-sum discipline as the sum surfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-darboux-sum-namecert}.
