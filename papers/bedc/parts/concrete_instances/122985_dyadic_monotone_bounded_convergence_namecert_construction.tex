Following Bishop and Bridges on bounded monotone convergence, the $\DyadicMonotoneBoundedConvergenceUp$ packet records the finite dyadic route by which a monotone bounded rational approximation ledger is sealed as a Real-facing convergence name. It sits over the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the dyadic supremum boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-supremum-namecert}, and the broader monotone convergence packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-monotone-convergence-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Dyadic monotone bounded convergence carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-monotone-bounded-convergence-carrier}
A $\DyadicMonotoneBoundedConvergenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(D,L,B,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row naming the dyadic precision ledger used by every convergence read.
\item $L$ is the monotone ledger recording that the displayed rational approximation rows are read in nondecreasing order.
\item $B$ is the bounded dyadic envelope row, exposing the lower source and upper dyadic bound before a supremum-facing read is allowed.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window family inspected by the convergence consumer.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the displayed finite windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached from the regular rational readback.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the tolerance, monotone, bounded-envelope, window, readback, Real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from any convergence-facing request through $D,L,B,W,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicMonotoneBoundedConvergenceUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DyadicSupremumUp$, $\MonotoneConvergenceRealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicMonotoneBoundedConvergenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate asserts only that these rows form one finite dyadic monotone convergence route. It has no coordinate for an ambient completeness theorem, a quotient stream, a selected limiting real, trichotomy, countable choice, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic monotone bounded convergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-monotone-bounded-convergence-namecert-obligations}

$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicMonotoneBoundedConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed finite packet, dyadic tolerance exposure through $D$, monotone-ledger exposure through $L$, bounded-envelope exposure through $B$, finite-window exposure through $W$, regular-rational readback through $R$, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-monotone-bounded-convergence-carrier}. Its rows are precisely the dyadic tolerance row, monotone ledger, bounded envelope, finite window family, regular rational readback, Real seal, and structural certificate rows listed in the statement.

Finally replay through $H,C,P,N$. These rows transport, replay, source, and name the displayed route, so the local certificate cannot add quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, or host real equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic monotone bounded convergence Cauchy route]
\label{thm:dyadic-monotone-bounded-convergence-cauchy-route}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Use the window and readback siblings. The row $W$ supplies the displayed finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows, and the row $R$ is the only regular rational readback attached to those windows and dyadic tolerances.

Then pass to the Real seal. The terminal row $E$ is read only after $R$ is displayed, so the public convergence object is a finite regular-rational seal route rather than a selected host limit.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same route. Hence the exported Cauchy-style handoff has no coordinate for quotient streams, ambient completeness, trichotomy, countable choice, or hidden supremum selection.
