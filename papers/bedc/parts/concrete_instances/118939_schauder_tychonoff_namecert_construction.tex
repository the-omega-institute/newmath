\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SchauderTychonoffUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-schauder-tychonoff-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Schauder and Tychonoff, the $\SchauderTychonoffUp$ packet records the finite handoff surface used when a compact convex source, a continuous self-map, and an approximate fixed-point mesh are displayed together. It sits after the Schauder fixed-point route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-schauder-fixed-point-namecert}, the finite approximate fixed-point packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-approximate-fixed-point-namecert}, the compact-metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the convex membership surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}, the locally convex ambient source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locally-convex-space-namecert}, and the continuous-map graph row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}. The chapter names a compact-convex fixed-point handoff as finite NameCert data; it does not claim arbitrary product compactness, choice-selected fixed points, quotient equality, or a host Schauder--Tychonoff theorem.

\begin{definition}[Schauder--Tychonoff handoff carrier]
\label{def:schauder-tychonoff-carrier}
A $\SchauderTychonoffUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(K,V,X,f,M,A,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ or compact-source row that supplies the finite inspection windows for the domain.
\item $V$ is the $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ ambient row carrying the finite seminorm or weak-neighborhood windows available to the packet.
\item $X$ is the $\ConvexSetUp$ row for the same displayed subset, including the affine-combination and membership reads used by fixed-point consumers.
\item $f$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ self-map row whose graph reads stay inside the displayed compact convex source.
\item $M$ is the finite mesh and radius ledger consumed from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-approximate-fixed-point-namecert}.
\item $A$ is the approximate fixed-point displacement row, recording the inspected points and tolerance windows through which $f(x)$ is compared with $x$.
\item $R$ is the fixed-point handoff row to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-schauder-fixed-point-namecert}, naming only the finite compact-convex route exposed by the packet.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, locally convex source, convex-set membership, continuous-map graph, mesh, displacement, handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact and convex source reads through $f,M,A,R$ to the structural rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SchauderTychonoffUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$, $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, finite approximate fixed-point rows, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SchauderTychonoffUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier compares accepted packets only through $K,V,X,f,M,A,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary product compactness, a choice-selected exact fixed point, quotient equality of maps or points, a completed locally convex dual, or a host Schauder--Tychonoff theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Schauder--Tychonoff NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:schauder-tychonoff-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SchauderTychonoffUp$ carrier
$$
S=(K,V,X,f,M,A,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SchauderTychonoffUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact-source admission for $K$, locally convex ambient admission for $V$, convex-set admission for $X$, continuous self-map visibility for $f$, finite mesh and radius admission for $M$, approximate displacement readback for $A$, fixed-point handoff through $R$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

Every compact-convex fixed-point consumer must read the dependency route
$$
K,V,X,f \longmapsto M,A \longmapsto R \longmapsto H,C,P,N
$$
before using the packet as a Schauder--Tychonoff handoff. The route exports finite certificate rows only; it exports no arbitrary product compactness theorem, no selected fixed point, no quotient equality, and no host theorem beyond the displayed compact-convex fixed-point handoff.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:schauder-tychonoff-carrier}. The compact source is $K$, the locally convex ambient source is $V$, the convex subset source is $X$, and the self-map graph is $f$.

Read the finite approximation data next. The mesh and radius row $M$ is supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-approximate-fixed-point-namecert}, and the displacement row $A$ records only the inspected point and tolerance comparisons needed for the handoff.

Route the fixed-point-facing read through $R$ and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-schauder-fixed-point-namecert}. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the same displayed rows, while $P$ and $N$ name the resulting packet.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for product compactness, an exact selected fixed point, quotient equality, a completed dual, or a host Schauder--Tychonoff theorem, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
