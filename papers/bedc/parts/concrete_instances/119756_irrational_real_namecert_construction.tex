\label{def:irrational-real-carrier}

$$

\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ candidate row, listing the inspected rational names that a consumer asks to compare with $R$.
\item $Z$ is the $\IntUp$ numerator and denominator row attached to the displayed rational candidates.

\item $W$ is the $\IrrationalWitnessUp$ obstruction row, recording the finite witness packet that refuses the displayed rational candidate route.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for quotient real equality, trichotomy, selected representatives, a host rationality decision, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[IrrationalReal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:irrational-real-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\IrrationalRealUp$ carrier
$$
I=(R,Q,Z,D,W,T,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\IrrationalRealUp}$ surface consists exactly of Real-source admission, rational-candidate exposure, integer numerator and denominator readback, dyadic tolerance accounting, $\IrrationalWitnessUp$ obstruction routing, $\RegSeqRatUp$ approximation readback, terminal rational-exclusion sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:irrational-real-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the Real source $R$, rational candidate row $Q$, integer row $Z$, dyadic ledger $D$, witness row $W$, regular rational readback $T$, terminal seal $E$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

The rational-candidate side is finite. The rows $Q$ and $Z$ expose only the inspected rational names and their integer numerator-denominator data, while $D$ records the dyadic comparison budgets used by those inspections.

The exclusion side is routed through $W$ and $T$. The witness row supplies the finite obstruction packet of $\IrrationalWitnessUp$, and the regular rational readback row supplies only the $\RegSeqRatUp$ observations visible before the terminal seal.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no row stores quotient equality, trichotomy, selected representatives, a host rationality decision, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
