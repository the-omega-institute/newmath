\begin{definition}[Serre--Swan finite-projective carrier]
\label{def:serre-swan-finite-projective-carrier}
A $\SerreSwanFiniteProjectiveUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(X,R,M,E,V,L,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the compact-base naming row over which the section and vector-bundle reads are scoped.
\item $R$ is the scalar $\RingUp$ row for continuous scalar functions on the displayed base.
\item $M$ is the $\ModuleUp$ row carrying the finite-projective section module with its displayed scalar action.
\item $E$ is the idempotent or finite-summand ledger witnessing finite projectivity through matrix rows over $R$.
\item $V$ is the $\VectorBundleUp$ row carrying fibres, local trivializations, transition maps, and overlap checks.
\item $L$ is the local section ledger, displaying the finite windows in which module generators read as bundle sections and bundle sections read back as module elements.
\item $Q$ is the comparison row tying the projective-module idempotent surface to the vector-bundle transition surface.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the base, scalar ring, module, idempotent, vector-bundle, section, comparison, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Serre--Swan request through $X,R,M,E,V,L,Q,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SerreSwanFiniteProjectiveUp$, $\VectorBundleUp$, $\ModuleUp$, $\RingUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SerreSwanFiniteProjectiveUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $X,R,M,E,V,L,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host category equivalence, a selected basis, quotient equality of modules, an ambient sheaf of sections, or external compactness data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Serre--Swan finite-projective NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:serre-swan-finite-projective-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SerreSwanFiniteProjectiveUp$ carrier
$$

the local $\NameCert_{\SerreSwanFiniteProjectiveUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed base row, scalar ring row, finite-projective module row, idempotent ledger, vector-bundle row, local section ledger, comparison row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every consumer of the Serre--Swan handoff must expose $X,R,M,E,V,L,Q,H,C,P,N$ before reading either the finite-projective module side or the vector-bundle side as public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:serre-swan-finite-projective-carrier}. The finite packet contains only the base, ring, module, idempotent, vector-bundle, section, comparison, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The module-facing read is routed through $R,M,E$. The scalar ring row supplies the displayed coefficients, the module row supplies the carrier and scalar action, and the idempotent ledger supplies the finite-projective witness rather than a selected basis or quotient-module equality.

The bundle-facing read is routed through $V,L,Q$. The vector-bundle row exposes fibres and transition data, the local section ledger records the finite section windows, and the comparison row is the only bridge between those windows and the finite-projective module surface.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite rows. Since no host category equivalence, ambient section sheaf, external compactness assertion, or selected global basis is present in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the public NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
