\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FordCircleUp$}

Following Hardy and Wright on Farey fractions and the classical Ford-circle construction, the $\FordCircleUp$ packet records a reduced rational boundary point together with its tangent upper-half-plane circle as finite Farey and horoball NameCert data. It realizes the Farey-horoball branch of \autoref{ch:visions-hyperbolic-phase-geometry-observer-expansion} beside the finite Farey sequence rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-farey-sequence-namecert}, the Farey approximation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-farey-approximation-namecert}, and the hyperbolic horoball carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-horoball-namecert}. The packet names only displayed rational, radius, tangency, horoball, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it is not a quotient real circle, not a host Euclidean tangent theorem, and not a global hyperbolic-plane completion.

\begin{definition}[FordCircle carrier]\label{def:ford-circle-carrier}
A $\FordCircleUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(Q,D,R,T,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the reduced rational center row, naming coprime integer numerator and positive denominator data over the rational boundary line.
\item $D$ is the positive denominator row, recording the denominator witness used by the circle radius and by Farey-neighbour tests.
\item $R$ is the dyadic reciprocal-square radius row, storing the displayed radius corresponding to the classical value $1/(2D^2)$ as rational and dyadic readback data.
\item $T$ is the tangency ledger. It records the finite Farey determinant tests that witness equality of the boundary tangency point and exact tangency between neighbouring Ford circles.
\item $U$ is the upper-half-plane and horoball row. It places the circle as a boundary-tangent horoball read whose base is $Q$ and whose height is read from $R$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the reduced rational center, denominator, radius, tangency ledger, upper-half-plane or horoball row, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Farey or hyperbolic-boundary consumer through $Q,D,R,T,U,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance row over $\FareySequenceUp$, $\HyperbolicHoroballUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RatUp$, $\IntUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FordCircleUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares two accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for ambient Euclidean circle equality, real quotient centers, selected real representatives, a completed hyperbolic plane, or a host geometry theorem about all tangent circles.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FordCircle NameCert obligations]\label{thm:ford-circle-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FordCircleUp$ carrier
$$
F=(Q,D,R,T,U,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FordCircleUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier formation for the reduced rational center, positive denominator, dyadic reciprocal-square radius, Farey tangency ledger, upper-half-plane or horoball row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data;
\item radius stability, requiring the radius row $R$ to be transported together with the same denominator row $D$ and not recomputed from an external real-circle representative;
\item Farey tangency exactness, requiring the tangency ledger $T$ to expose the determinant and adjacency rows consumed by neighbouring-circle reads;
\item horoball embedding route, requiring the upper-half-plane row $U$ to be read downstream of $Q,D,R,T$ and upstream of the structural rows $H,C,P,N$;
\item nonescape from host real-circle equality, excluding ambient Euclidean circle equality, quotient real centers, selected representatives, global hyperbolic-plane completion, and an unlogged tangent-circle theorem.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:ford-circle-carrier}. The rows $Q,D,R,T,U,H,C,P,N$ display the reduced rational center, denominator, radius, tangency data, horoball placement, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

Radius stability is componentwise. The denominator row $D$ and the dyadic reciprocal-square radius row $R$ move together under $H$, and any consumer replay through $C$ must read $R$ as part of the same packet.

Farey tangency exactness is supplied by the ledger $T$. The packet records the determinant and adjacency data before a neighbouring-circle read can be consumed, so tangency is not imported from an ambient real-circle theorem.

Finally the horoball row $U$ is downstream of the rational and radius rows. The package and NameCert rows $P,N$ name this displayed route, while the absent host objects have no coordinate in the carrier.
\end{proof}
