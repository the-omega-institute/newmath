\begin{theorem}[Henstock lemma NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:henstock-lemma-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}

The partition admission obligation is the route through $F$. It is already a $\FinePartitionGaugeUp$ row, so the Cousin/fine tagged partition is displayed before the oscillation or mesh ledgers can be consumed.
