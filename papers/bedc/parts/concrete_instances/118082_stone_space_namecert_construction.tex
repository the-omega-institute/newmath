\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\StoneSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-stone-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Stone, Birkhoff, and Kelley, the $\StoneSpaceUp$ packet records the finite BEDC carrier by which a Boolean algebra is read as a topological clopen ledger. It sits beside the Boolean algebra carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-booleanalgebra-namecert}, the clopen algebra carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-clopen-algebra-namecert}, the Stone-duality bridge of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stoneduality-namecert}, the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, and the local compact Hausdorff surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locally-compact-hausdorff-namecert}. The packet names the Stone-space observation surface available to later clopen-ledger and bridge work; it does not assert ultrafilter choice, host powersets, quotient topology equality, or a checked standard bridge.

\begin{definition}[Stone space carrier]
\label{def:stone-space-carrier}
A $\StoneSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(B,L,T,K,Z,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BooleanAlgebraUp$ source row. It records the displayed zero, one, meet, join, complement, order, and distributivity observations that generate the Stone-space interface.
\item $L$ is the $\ClopenAlgebraUp$ ledger. It records the clopen rows named by the Boolean observations and keeps their finite Boolean operations visible as clopen operations.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ public-open row. It exposes the indexed open rows generated by the clopen ledger, including the displayed empty, whole, finite-meet, complement-open, and finite-union reads.
\item $K$ is the compact Hausdorff readback row. It records only the displayed $\CompactHausdorffUp$ separation and compactness obligations available from the finite clopen ledger, not an external point-set compactification theorem.
\item $Z$ is the zero-dimensional readback row. It says that the public topology rows read by $T$ are generated through clopen rows from $L$, so the topology-facing basis remains the displayed clopen ledger.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Boolean source, clopen ledger, topology rows, compact Hausdorff readback, zero-dimensional readback, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the route
$$
B \longmapsto L \longmapsto T,K,Z .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\StoneSpaceUp$, $\BooleanAlgebraUp$, $\ClopenAlgebraUp$, $\StoneDualityUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\CompactHausdorffUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\StoneSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,L,T,K,Z,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for selected ultrafilters, host powersets, quotient topology equality, arbitrary opens outside the displayed $\TopologyUp$ rows, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Stone space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:stone-space-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:stone-space-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\StoneSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of Boolean-algebra source exposure through $B$, clopen-ledger exposure through $L$, topology public-open exposure through $T$, compact Hausdorff and zero-dimensional readback through $K$ and $Z$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Every Stone-duality or clopen-ledger consumer reads the Stone space only through those rows and receives no ultrafilter choice, host powerset, quotient topology equality, arbitrary-open completion, or checked bridge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:stone-space-carrier}. The packet displays exactly the Boolean source, clopen ledger, topology public rows, compact Hausdorff readback, zero-dimensional readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row.

The topology surface is generated before any point-space readback is considered. The row $L$ turns Boolean observations into clopen observations, and $T$ exposes only the indexed open rows generated from that clopen ledger.

The compact Hausdorff and zero-dimensional rows are readbacks from the same displayed route. They can certify the finite separation, compactness, and clopen-basis obligations visible in $K$ and $Z$, but they do not choose ultrafilters or equate an external quotient topology with the internal classifier.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same route. Since no field of the carrier stores host powersets, selected ultrafilters, arbitrary opens, quotient topology equality, or a standard bridge witness, no consumer can extract such data from the local $\NameCert_{\StoneSpaceUp}$ surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Stone space clopen-ledger boundary]
\label{thm:stone-space-clopen-ledger-boundary}
Every accepted $\StoneSpaceUp$ packet factors its topology-facing observations through the finite route
$$
\BooleanAlgebraUp
\longrightarrow
\ClopenAlgebraUp
\longrightarrow
\TopologyUp
\longrightarrow
\CompactHausdorffUp
\longrightarrow
H,C,P,N .
$$
The route is compatible with the $\StoneDualityUp$ clopen classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stoneduality-namecert}: a Boolean row may be replayed as a clopen topology row and then read back as compact Hausdorff and zero-dimensional structure. The boundary gives later bridge work a concrete $\StoneSpaceUp$ target while excluding ultrafilter selection, host powerset extensionality, quotient topology equality, and bridge-checked equivalence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the Boolean source row $B$. The clopen ledger $L$ admits only the finite clopen observations generated from the Boolean operations, so each topology-facing read has a displayed clopen origin.

The topology handoff is $T$. It records the public opens generated by the clopen rows, while the compact Hausdorff and zero-dimensional readbacks $K$ and $Z$ inspect only the same public rows and the same clopen generator provenance.

Compatibility with $\StoneDualityUp$ is therefore a ledger comparison, not a point-space construction. The Stone-duality classifier already sends Boolean observations to clopen topology rows, and the present carrier exposes that route as the named $\StoneSpaceUp$ packet.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming keep the route finite. Since every accepted read factors through the displayed chain, the excluded ultrafilter, powerset, quotient-equality, and bridge-checking objects remain outside the carrier boundary.
\end{proof}
