\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\GoodCoverUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-good-cover-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Leray's good-cover method and the Bott--Tu and Hatcher presentations of cover nerves, the $\GoodCoverUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface of a cover whose visible nonempty finite intersections are contractible along displayed rows. It sits between the open-cover carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-open-cover-namecert}, the finite open-cover nerve of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-open-cover-nerve-namecert}, the finite cover nerve of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cover-nerve-namecert}, the compact cover nerve rows used by $\CompactCoverNerveUp$, and the Cech nerve packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cech-nerve-namecert}. The packet is a finite good-cover ledger; it is not the nerve theorem, not a selected homotopy equivalence, not an ambient triangulation theorem, and not host contractibility of arbitrary intersections.

\begin{definition}[Good-cover carrier]
\label{def:good-cover-carrier}
A $\GoodCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(T,O,F,I,K,N,S,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row naming the open-set ambient surface.
\item $O$ is the $\OpenCoverUp$ row giving the displayed cover members.
\item $F$ is the $\FiniteOpenCoverUp$ or finite cover row fixing the finite index window.
\item $I$ is the finite-intersection ledger for every nonempty displayed subwindow of $F$.
\item $K$ is the good-intersection row recording contractible-cell evidence for each nonempty entry of $I$.
\item $N$ is the nerve-face row shared with $\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp$, $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$, $\CompactCoverNerveUp$, and $\CechNerveUp$ consumers.
\item $S$ is the $\SimplicialComplexUp$ export row for the finite nerve faces.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology, cover, finite index, intersection, good-intersection, nerve, simplicial, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from finite cover reads through the intersection and nerve rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\GoodCoverUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\OpenCoverUp$, $\FiniteOpenCoverUp$, $\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp$, $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$, $\CechNerveUp$, $\SimplicialComplexUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\GoodCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $T,O,F,I,K,N,S,H,C,P,L$. It has no coordinate for selected points in intersections, ambient homotopy equivalence, quotient realization equality, a global triangulation, open-cover compactness, or the nerve theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Good-cover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:good-cover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\GoodCoverUp$ carrier $G=(T,O,F,I,K,N,S,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\GoodCoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of topology-source exposure, open-cover exposure, finite index exposure, nonempty finite-intersection ledger exposure, good-intersection contractible-cell exposure, nerve-face exposure, simplicial-complex handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:good-cover-carrier}. Rows $T,O,F$ expose the topological source, displayed cover, and finite index window before any intersection can be read.

The finite-intersection ledger $I$ lists only displayed nonempty subwindows. The good-intersection row $K$ attaches contractible-cell evidence to those ledger entries, and the nerve row $N$ records the corresponding finite faces.

The simplicial export $S$ is downstream of $I,K,N$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are supplied by $H,C,P,L$, so no host nerve theorem, selected point, quotient realization, or global triangulation is part of the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Good-cover finite-intersection nerve route]
\label{thm:good-cover-finite-intersection-nerve-route}
Every $\FiniteOpenCoverNerveUp$, $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$, $\CompactCoverNerveUp$, or $\CechNerveUp$ consumer that reads an accepted $\GoodCoverUp$ packet must factor through the finite route
$$
\TopologyUp \longmapsto \OpenCoverUp \longmapsto \FiniteOpenCoverUp \longmapsto I \longmapsto K \longmapsto N \longmapsto \SimplicialComplexUp .
$$
The consumer receives only the displayed cover rows, finite-intersection rows, contractible-cell rows, nerve-face rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local $\NameCert$ evidence. It does not receive the nerve theorem, an ambient homotopy equivalence, a selected realization map, arbitrary open-cover compactness, quotient point equality, or global homology computation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite cover source. A nerve-facing consumer can read $N$ only after the cover member rows $O$ and finite index row $F$ have been exposed over the topology row $T$.

Next read the intersection data. The ledger $I$ records exactly the displayed nonempty finite intersections, while $K$ records the good-cover evidence attached to those intersections. Thus the nerve face row is determined by finite ledger data rather than by a host compactness or choice principle.

Finally apply the structural rows $H,C,P,L$. They transport, replay, source, and name the same route into the finite-open-cover, finite-cover, compact-cover, and Cech nerve siblings. Since the carrier has no coordinate for a nerve theorem or ambient homotopy equivalence, the route stops at the displayed finite nerve and simplicial-complex surface.
\end{proof}
