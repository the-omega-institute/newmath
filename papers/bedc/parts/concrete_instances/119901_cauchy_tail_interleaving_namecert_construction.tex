\begin{definition}[Cauchy tail interleaving carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-tail-interleaving-carrier}
A $\CauchyTailInterleavingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R_0$ and $R_1$ are the two $\RegSeqRatUp$ source rows whose Cauchy tails are being fused.
\item $S_0$ and $S_1$ are the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window rows used to inspect the two tails.
\item $M$ is the $\CauchySubsequenceSelectorUp$ monotone corridor that records the displayed tail schedules used by the fusion.
\item $W$ is the interleaving window row, recording for each inspected output window whether it reads the left tail, the right tail, or the shared comparison window.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare the two tail reads at the displayed windows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal exposed only after $R_0,R_1,S_0,S_1,M,W,D$ have been read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the two sources, two window rows, selector corridor, interleaving row, tolerance ledger, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a fusion request through $R_0,R_1,S_0,S_1,M,W,D,E,H,P,N$.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through these finite rows. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected representatives, a host limit, countable choice, real trichotomy, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy tail interleaving NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-tail-interleaving-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyTailInterleavingUp$ carrier $T=(R_0,R_1,S_0,S_1,M,W,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyTailInterleavingUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, left-tail and right-tail source admission, finite-window admission, selector-corridor monotonicity, interleaving-window exactness, dyadic tolerance compatibility, $\RealUp$ seal non-escape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every obligation is read through the finite route
$$
R_0,R_1,S_0,S_1,M,W,D \longrightarrow E \longrightarrow H,C,P,N .
$$
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:cauchy-tail-interleaving-carrier}. The two regular rational sources, the two finite window rows, the selector corridor, the interleaving window, the dyadic tolerance ledger, and the terminal seal are all visible coordinates of the same packet.

The selector-corridor obligation is supplied by $M$. It keeps the two tail schedules monotone and finite, while $W$ records which source tail each output window reads before any fused consumer receives a seal.

The tolerance obligation is read from $D$ over the displayed windows. It compares only the inspected regular rational rows $R_0,R_1$ through $S_0,S_1,M,W$, so the certificate has no need for a quotient-tail equality or a selected representative.

Transport through $H$, replay through $C$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ preserve the same route. Since the excluded quotient, choice, limit, trichotomy, host equality, and completeness objects are absent from the carrier coordinates, no NameCert obligation can discharge through them.
\end{proof}
