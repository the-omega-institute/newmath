The $\PseudotheoryRefutationGateUp$ packet realizes the refusal surface of \autoref{ch:pseudotheory-refutation-gate-map} as finite NameCert data. The packet is apophatic: it blocks overclaimed discovery reads before any positive admission judgment is exported.

\begin{definition}[Pseudotheory refutation gate carrier]
\label{def:pseudotheory-refutation-gate-carrier}
A $\PseudotheoryRefutationGateUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(D,K,C,A,U,T,H,Q,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the structural-DNA collision row, recording a finite payload comparison with a prior grounded carrier.
\item $K$ is the classifier-neutral padding row, recording that a proposed surface changes no accepted classifier bit before a discovery assertion is read.
\item $C$ is the axiom-crossing dependency row, recording a forbidden source token or transitive dependency surface for a claimed BEDC theorem.
\item $A$ is the unpaid discovery-data row, recording missing semantic naming, row evidence, ledger membership, debt, or scope-seal data for a positive-discovery claim.
\item $U$ is the taste-boundary row, recording that stability, usefulness, and fit with the surrounding theory remain admission judgments and are not automatic demotion results.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the five refusal rows and for replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $H$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the refusal route from a candidate read to exactly one exposed finite refusal row before any conclusion is exported.
\item $Q$ is the audit-query provenance row tying the read to axiom-dependency maps, query ledgers, audit boundaries, audit-map families, and ground-compiler audit maps.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BHist$, refusal rows, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, audit-query data, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PseudotheoryRefutationGateUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate that turns refusal into a positive discovery proof, carrier repair recipe, or taste decision.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Pseudotheory refutation gate NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:pseudotheory-refutation-gate-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\PseudotheoryRefutationGateUp$ carrier
$$
R=(D,K,C,A,U,T,H,Q,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PseudotheoryRefutationGateUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the structural-DNA collision row, classifier-neutral padding row, axiom-crossing dependency row, unpaid discovery-data row, taste-boundary row, transport, replay, audit-query provenance, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from the ten displayed rows. The five refusal rows are all visible before the local name is read.

The transport and replay rows preserve refusal-row identity. A structural collision cannot be replayed as an axiom dependency, and a taste-boundary row cannot be replayed as a mechanical discovery failure.

The provenance rows $Q$ and $P$ name the audit surfaces used by the read, while $N$ names only the local packet. Since the carrier has no positive-admission coordinate, no NameCert obligation can turn the refusal surface into a discovery proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite refusal soundness]
\label{thm:pseudotheory-refutation-gate-finite-refusal-soundness}
Every accepted $\PseudotheoryRefutationGateUp$ read exposes at least one of the finite refusal rows $D,K,C,A,U$ before any refusal conclusion is exported. The exported conclusion is therefore a row-local refusal over the displayed audit surface, not a global proof that the candidate has no repair or no future value.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier. The replay row $H$ sends a candidate read to the finite refusal surface, and the carrier predicate admits only the five displayed refusal rows as conclusions.

If the read is structural, it factors through $D$; if it is classifier-neutral, through $K$; if it crosses an axiom boundary, through $C$; if it lacks discovery data, through $A$; and if it reaches taste-sensitive stability, through $U$.

The audit-query and provenance rows record where the refusal was read. They do not add a positive discovery certificate or a global impossibility theorem, so the conclusion remains finite and row-local.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Taste-boundary nonescape]
\label{thm:pseudotheory-refutation-gate-taste-boundary-nonescape}
The taste-boundary row $U$ of an accepted $\PseudotheoryRefutationGateUp$ carrier may refuse automatic demotion or automatic admission, but it cannot export a positive discovery judgment, carrier replacement, or theorem-worthiness proof. Any consumer that cites $U$ must keep the read inside the displayed taste-boundary row, its $\hsame$ transport, its $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local $\NameCert$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the row $U$ from the accepted carrier. It is a boundary row: it records where stability and fit require judgment rather than a mechanical gate.

Transport through $T$ preserves that boundary status, and replay through $H$ keeps the row inside the same refusal route. The provenance rows $Q$ and $P$ record the audit source without changing the kind of the row.

Because the carrier contains no positive-admission coordinate, a consumer cannot obtain a discovery proof, carrier replacement, or theorem-worthiness result from $U$. The only exported data are the displayed boundary row and its transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{proof}
