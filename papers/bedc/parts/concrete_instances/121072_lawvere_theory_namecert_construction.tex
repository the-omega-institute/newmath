From Lawvere's finite-product algebraic theories, the $\LawvereTheoryUp$ packet records a categorical presentation of finitary algebraic syntax as finite NameCert data. It reads the small category surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-category-namecert}, the product-preserving comparison route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-functor-namecert}, the finite-product rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-prod-namecert}, and the syntactic arity ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-firstorder-namecert}. The packet therefore exposes algebraic operations by their finite-product arities and equations, not by importing a host algebraic theory, quotient syntax, or an external clone object.

\begin{definition}[Lawvere theory carrier]
\label{def:lawvere-theory-carrier}
A $\LawvereTheoryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(C,A,O,I,E,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the category source row, recording the small categorical carrier, identities, composition, and finite-product comparison surface.
\item $A$ is the finite-product arity row, recording the distinguished finite powers of the generic object and their projections.
\item $O$ is the operation-symbol row, recording each inspected operation as a morphism out of one displayed finite product.
\item $I$ is the product-preserving interpretation row, recording the functorial read that carries arities and operations through finite-product preserving maps.
\item $E$ is the equation ledger, recording only finite diagrams between displayed operation composites and projection composites.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the category source, arity row, operation-symbol row, interpretation row, equation ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming row.
\item $R$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from categorical composition and product projections to operation interpretation and equation checking.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LawvereTheoryUp$, $\CategoryUp$, $\FunctorUp$, $\ProdUp$, $\FirstOrderUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LawvereTheoryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The accepted carrier has no coordinate for host quotient syntax, a selected term algebra, an external variety of algebras, or a global equality principle beyond the displayed finite equation ledger.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lawvere theory NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lawvere-theory-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted packet $L=(C,A,O,I,E,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LawvereTheoryUp}$ obligations are exhausted by carrier admission for the category source $C$, finite-product arities $A$, operation symbols $O$, product-preserving interpretation row $I$, equation ledger $E$, componentwise transport $H$, displayed replay $R$, package provenance $P$, and local naming row $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:lawvere-theory-carrier}. The categorical source and finite-product arities are exactly the rows $C$ and $A$, so every operation read must pass through a displayed morphism in $O$ whose domain is one of the finite products recorded by $A$.

The interpretation row $I$ is read only after the functorial and product-preserving data have been displayed. Equation checks are then finite ledger reads from $E$: each equation compares two displayed composites built from projections, operations, identities, and composition in $C$.

Transport, replay, provenance, and naming are componentwise through $H,R,P,N$. Since the carrier has no host term quotient, selected term algebra, external variety coordinate, or global equality principle, the local NameCert surface cannot export any of those objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Lawvere theory finite-product clone route]
\label{thm:lawvere-theory-finite-product-clone-route}
Every clone-facing or algebraic-syntax consumer of an accepted $\LawvereTheoryUp$ packet must first read the category source $C$, finite-product arities $A$, operation symbols $O$, product-preserving interpretation row $I$, and finite equation ledger $E$. Only after those rows are displayed may the consumer reuse the packet as a finite-product clone route through $H,R,P,N$; no read bypasses the displayed products by invoking host syntax, quotient terms, ambient algebras, or a choice of representatives.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite-product arity row. It identifies each operation by a morphism from a displayed finite power of the generic object, so the consumer has only the arities and projections recorded in $A$ and $O$.

Next read the interpretation and equation rows. Product-preserving interpretation through $I$ keeps arities tied to the same finite-product structure, while the ledger $E$ verifies only the finite composites that have been displayed.

Finally repack the route through $H,R,P,N$. Componentwise transport and replay preserve the same categorical rows, so the resulting clone-facing surface is a finite-product route and not an imported syntax quotient, a selected term model, or an ambient algebraic-theory object.
\end{proof}
