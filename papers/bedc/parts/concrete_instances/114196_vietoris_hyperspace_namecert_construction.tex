\begin{definition}[Vietoris hyperspace carrier]
\label{def:vietoris-hyperspace-carrier}
A $\VietorisHyperspaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
V=(K,H,T,D,C,F,S,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, exposing the point carrier, metric distance rows, finite-net schedules, and completion witnesses used by compact-subset requests.
\item $H$ is the $\HyperspaceUp$ compact-subset row, naming the nonempty compact-subset presentations over $K$.
\item $T$ is the $\VietorisTopologyUp$ row, carrying the lower-hit and upper-miss generators attached to the compact-subset presentations.
\item $D$ is the $\HausdorffDistanceUp$ row, recording finite scalar distance reads between displayed compact-subset names.
\item $C$ is the $\KuratowskiHyperspaceUp$ row, keeping completion-facing hyperspace consumers inside compact finite-net and Hausdorff-distance data.
\item $F$ is the finite compatibility ledger tying Vietoris hit-and-miss reads to the Hausdorff finite-distance and compact-metric net schedules.
\item $S$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,H,T,D,C,F,R,P,N$.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route from compact metric source through hyperspace, Vietoris topology, Hausdorff distance, Kuratowski boundary, and finite compatibility rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\VietorisHyperspaceUp$, $\HyperspaceUp$, $\VietorisTopologyUp$, $\HausdorffDistanceUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\KuratowskiHyperspaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\VietorisHyperspaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,H,T,D,C,F,S,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host powersets, quotient compacta, selected nearest points, ambient equality of closed subsets, open-cover compactness, or a hidden completion representative.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Vietoris hyperspace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:vietoris-hyperspace-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\VietorisHyperspaceUp$ carrier
$$
V=(K,H,T,D,C,F,S,R,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\VietorisHyperspaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the compact metric source, hyperspace compact-subset row, Vietoris topology row, Hausdorff distance row, Kuratowski hyperspace row, finite compatibility ledger, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The obligation surface requires compact-metric source visibility through $K$, compact-subset admission through $H$, lower-hit and upper-miss topology reads through $T$, finite scalar distance reads through $D$, completion-facing boundary control through $C$, Vietoris-Hausdorff compatibility through $F$, and structural transport, replay, provenance, and naming through $S,R,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:vietoris-hyperspace-carrier}. The analytic rows are $K,H,T,D,C,F$, and the structural rows are $S,R,P,N$.

The source row $K$ is read first because each compact-subset, topology, distance, and completion-facing row is over the same compact metric carrier. The hyperspace row $H$ then supplies the compact-subset presentations consumed by the Vietoris and Hausdorff sides.

Read $T,D,C$ through the finite compatibility ledger $F$. The Vietoris row contributes only lower-hit and upper-miss generators, the Hausdorff distance row contributes only finite scalar distance reads, and the Kuratowski row contributes only completion-facing hyperspace boundary data.

Finally $S,R,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the excluded host powerset, quotient, nearest-point, open-cover, ambient-equality, and hidden-representative objects do not occur in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Vietoris hyperspace compact-metric handoff]
\label{thm:vietoris-hyperspace-compact-metric-handoff}
Every compact-metric, Vietoris-facing, Hausdorff-facing, or Kuratowski-facing consumer of an accepted $\VietorisHyperspaceUp$ carrier must factor through the displayed route
$$
K \longmapsto H \longmapsto T,D,C \longmapsto F \longmapsto S,R,P,N.
$$
Thus the consumer receives compact metric source rows, compact-subset hyperspace rows, Vietoris hit-and-miss rows, finite Hausdorff-distance rows, Kuratowski completion-facing rows, finite compatibility, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. It receives no host powerset, quotient compactum, selected nearest point, open-cover compactness theorem, ambient equality of closed subsets, or hidden completion representative.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:vietoris-hyperspace-namecert-obligations}. It requires $K$ before any compact-subset or hyperspace read, and it requires $H$ before the Vietoris, Hausdorff, or Kuratowski rows can be exposed.

The three consumer directions then share the same finite source. The row $T$ reads lower-hit and upper-miss data from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vietoris-topology-namecert}; the row $D$ reads finite scalar distance data from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hausdorff-distance-namecert}; and the row $C$ reads the completion-facing boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-hyperspace-namecert}.

The compatibility ledger $F$ ties these rows back to the compact metric finite-net schedules in $K$ and the compact-subset presentations in $H$. The structural rows $S,R,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name that route for downstream consumers.

No excluded host or quotient object is a coordinate of $V$. Hence the public handoff is exactly the displayed compact-metric hyperspace route.
\end{proof}
