\begin{definition}[Banach indicatrix carrier]
\label{def:banach-indicatrix-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the accepted $\BoundedVariationRealFunctionUp$ source row on a displayed real interval.
\item $L$ is a finite list of displayed level rows read through $\RegSeqRatUp$ names.
\item $G$ is the finite interval ledger that records which displayed subintervals are inspected for each level row.
\item $P$ is the finite preimage-count ledger; each entry records the counted crossings or endpoint hits for one displayed level row and one displayed interval window.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for function values, level names, and endpoint comparisons.

\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $B,L,G,P,R,D$ have been displayed.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Banach indicatrix NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:banach-indicatrix-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BanachIndicatrixUp$ carrier $I=(B,L,G,P,R,D,E,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BanachIndicatrixUp}$ surface consists exactly of bounded-variation real-function source admission, finite level-row exposure, finite interval-ledger exposure, finite preimage-count exposure, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance accounting, Real seal nonescape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:banach-indicatrix-carrier}. The source is the single row $B$, and every level inspected by the packet is already listed in the finite row $L$.

The counting surface is finite. The interval ledger $G$ selects the displayed interval windows, and the preimage-count ledger $P$ records only the crossings or endpoint hits supported by those windows.

Readback and tolerance remain downstream. The row $R$ supplies regular rational names for values and levels, $D$ supplies dyadic sign and endpoint tests, and $E$ seals only the Real-facing boundary reached from those rows.

Finally $H,C,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and locally name the same packet. Since no row stores a host quotient of functions, a measurable representative, a choice family over all levels, a coarea measure, or ambient Real completeness, none of those objects is exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
