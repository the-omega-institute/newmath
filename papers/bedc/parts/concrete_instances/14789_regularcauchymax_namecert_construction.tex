Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\RegularCauchyMaxUp$ packet reads the maximum of two regular Cauchy names constructively through the identity $max(x,y)=(x+y+|x-y|)/2$. The certificate factors that identity through the finite pointwise-sum surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchysum-namecert}, the absolute-value surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchyabs-namecert}, the rational scaling surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchyscale-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. It does not compare completed reals by trichotomy, choose a larger host real, quotient streams, or appeal to ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[RegularCauchyMax carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-max-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyMaxUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(A,B,W_A,W_B,D_A,D_B,S,\Delta,V,T,Q,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible coordinates:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ and $B$ are the two source $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational Cauchy name rows.
\item $W_A$ and $W_B$ are the aligned $\StreamNameUp$ windows used to observe the two sources at the same precision requests.
\item $D_A$ and $D_B$ are the displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint rows read from those windows.
\item $S$ is the $\RegularCauchySumUp$ row for the source sum $A+B$.
\item $\Delta$ is the displayed $\RegularCauchySumUp$ difference row for $A+(-B)$, with the negation supplied by the same rational scaling route used by the sibling operation surface.
\item $V$ is the $\RegularCauchyAbsUp$ row for $|\Delta|$.
\item $T$ is the $\RegularCauchySumUp$ row for $S+V$.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegularCauchyScaleUp$ row scaling $T$ by the displayed rational one-half.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row assembled from the scaled regular Cauchy name.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the readback row $R$ has been exposed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the two sources, aligned windows, dyadic endpoints, sum, difference, absolute-value, second sum, half-scale, readback, seal, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ routes from the source windows to the dyadic endpoint rows, through the sum-absolute-value factorization, through the half-scale row, and onward to the readback and real seal.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegularCauchySumUp$, $\RegularCauchyAbsUp$, $\RegularCauchyScaleUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyMaxUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for trichotomy on real names, host real order, quotient stream equality, selected limits, or a $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{definition}
