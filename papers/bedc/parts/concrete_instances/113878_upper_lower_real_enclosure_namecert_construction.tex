\begin{definition}[Upper/lower real enclosure carrier]
\label{def:upper-lower-real-enclosure-carrier}
An $\UpperLowerRealEnclosureUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(L,U,W,S,R,Z,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the lower rational bound row, read through the rational and dyadic tolerance surface used by $\DyadicRatCoreUp$;
\item $U$ is the upper rational bound row for the same inspected real-name window;
\item $W$ is the dyadic width row, displaying a tolerance certificate that $U-L$ is bounded by the named dyadic width and can be refined along the same finite schedule;
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule row naming exactly the finite observation windows used to inspect the enclosure;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row translating those observations into regular rational approximants;
\item $Z$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the lower bound, upper bound, width, schedule, and readback rows have been displayed;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $L,U,W,S,R,Z$;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for bound inspection, width refinement, regular readback, located-name handoff, and Real sealing;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UpperLowerRealEnclosureUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UpperLowerRealEnclosureUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host real representative, quotient equality of names, selected limits, trichotomy of real order, or unrestricted completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Upper/lower real enclosure NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:upper-lower-real-enclosure-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UpperLowerRealEnclosureUp$ carrier $E=(L,U,W,S,R,Z,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UpperLowerRealEnclosureUp}$ surface consists exactly of five finite rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item lower-bound admission, where $L$ is displayed as a rational bound over the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by the inspected real-name window;
\item upper-bound admission, where $U$ is displayed over the same ledger and is compared with $L$ only through dyadic rational order data;
\item width control, where $W$ records the finite dyadic enclosure width and its refinement schedule;
\item schedule/readback coherence, where $S$ and $R$ tie the bound rows to the same $\StreamNameUp$ windows and $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational observations;
\item Real-seal nonescape, where $Z,H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the displayed enclosure without exporting host representatives, quotient equality, selected limits, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The first two rows are precisely the rational bound rows $L$ and $U$, and both are read through the displayed dyadic tolerance ledger rather than through a host order on completed reals.

The width row $W$ is part of the same finite packet. Its dyadic comparison is therefore a displayed tolerance certificate attached to $L$ and $U$, not an external metric completeness assertion.

Next read the schedule and readback rows. The finite windows in $S$ determine which observations may be inspected, and $R$ translates only those observations into regular rational approximants. This keeps the enclosure on the L10 path through $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$.

Finally project the seal and structural rows. The $\RealUp$ row $Z$ is reached only after $L,U,W,S,R$ have been displayed, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite data. Since the carrier has no coordinate for the excluded principles, the five rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Upper/lower real enclosure width shrink]
\label{thm:upper-lower-real-enclosure-width-shrink}
Let $E=(L,U,W,S,R,Z,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\UpperLowerRealEnclosureUp$ carrier. Any refinement of the displayed $\StreamNameUp$ schedule $S$ that is replayed by $C$ and read back by $R$ may replace $W$ by a smaller displayed dyadic width $W'$ only through the same lower-bound row $L$, upper-bound row $U$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ comparison ledger, and $\RealUp$ seal row $Z$. The shrink route exports a narrower finite enclosure row, but it does not export a selected limit point, quotient real equality, order trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier definition. The only width coordinate is $W$, and every permitted inspection of $W$ is tied to the bound rows $L,U$, the finite schedule $S$, and the regular readback row $R$.

A refinement of $S$ supplies a smaller finite observation window only when the replay row $C$ displays that refinement inside the same packet. The readback row $R$ then compares the resulting rational approximants through the dyadic ledger, so the proposed $W'$ is a new displayed width row rather than a semantic limit.

The structural rows $H,P,N$ keep the refined enclosure attached to the original packet, and the terminal seal $Z$ is not reread as completeness. Therefore width shrink is a finite dyadic enclosure operation over the displayed rows and cannot yield any of the excluded host principles.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Upper/lower real enclosure Real-seal nonescape]
\label{thm:upper-lower-real-enclosure-real-seal}

The seal may certify that the displayed enclosure is Real-facing, but it does not identify quotient real representatives, select a completed limit, prove arbitrary locatedness, invoke trichotomy, or supply ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the packet in the order forced by \autoref{def:upper-lower-real-enclosure-carrier}. The seal row $Z$ is one coordinate of the carrier and is not available before the bound, width, schedule, and readback rows have been displayed.

The transport and replay rows keep the consumer on the same finite enclosure. They allow the listed rows to be moved and replayed componentwise, but they do not create a new representative of the real name or a completion operation.

Finally inspect the provenance and local certificate.
