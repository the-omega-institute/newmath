\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BingNagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$}

Following Bing, Nagata, Smirnov, and Engelking on metrization criteria, the $\BingNagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$ packet records the finite BEDC route by which a regular topological source exposes both a Bing-style development route and a Nagata--Smirnov locally finite cover route before a metric-space consumer reads the handoff. It sits over the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, beside the Bing packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bing-metrization-namecert}, beside the Nagata--Smirnov packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nagata-smirnov-metrization-namecert}, and downstream of the paracompactness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-paracompact-namecert} when displayed local finiteness is supplied. The packet names only the combined finite criterion route through $\BingMetrizationUp$, $\NagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, and $\ParacompactUp$ anchors; it does not assert host open-cover compactness, selected covers, quotient topology equality, countable choice, or an ambient point-set metrization oracle.

\begin{definition}[Bing--Nagata--Smirnov metrization carrier]
\label{def:bing-nagata-smirnov-metrization-carrier}
A $\BingNagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(T,B,N,P,L,S,R,E,Q,M,F,H,C,G,A)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row, carrying the open-row classifier, point-neighbourhood requests, and regular separation reads.
\item $B$ is the $\BingMetrizationUp$ development route row, exposing the displayed star-refinement and development-level checks read from the source topology.
\item $N$ is the $\NagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$ cover-family route row, exposing the displayed locally finite layer schedule and base-refinement checks.
\item $P$ is the $\ParacompactUp$ dependency row, used only when the packet has an explicitly displayed local-finiteness supply.
\item $L$ is the common locally finite ledger through which the Bing and Nagata--Smirnov routes are compared.
\item $S$ is the combined schedule row, recording the finite development levels and cover layers inspected by the packet.
\item $R$ is the regularity and refinement row, binding closed-neighbourhood separation to the displayed cover refinements.
\item $E$ is the exactness row asserting that every accepted criterion request is read through both $B$ and $N$ before any metric handoff is consumed.
\item $Q$ is the dyadic radius schedule shared by the combined criterion route.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ handoff row, available only after $T,B,N,P,L,S,R,E,Q$ have been exposed.
\item $F$ records finite compatibility between metric balls from $M$ and topology windows from $T$ along the two sibling metrization routes.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all displayed rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a topology request through Bing development data, Nagata--Smirnov cover data, paracompact local-finiteness data, regularity, exactness, dyadic radii, metric handoff, compatibility, provenance, and local naming.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BingNagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$, $\BingMetrizationUp$, $\NagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\ParacompactUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $A$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BingNagataSmirnovMetrizationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for host open-cover compactness, selected covers, quotient topology equality, arbitrary refinements, countable choice, or an ambient metric oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bing--Nagata--Smirnov metrization NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bing-nagata-smirnov-metrization-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BingNagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$ carrier $K=(T,B,N,P,L,S,R,E,Q,M,F,H,C,G,A)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BingNagataSmirnovMetrizationUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the topology source, Bing development route, Nagata--Smirnov cover route, paracompact local-finiteness dependency, common locally finite ledger, combined schedule, regularity-refinement row, exactness row, dyadic radius schedule, metric-space handoff, compatibility, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row;
\item sibling route readability, requiring every accepted criterion request to start at $T$ and pass through the displayed $\BingMetrizationUp$ and $\NagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$ rows before $M$ is inspected;
\item local-finiteness compatibility, requiring $P,L,S,R$ to expose only finite cover-layer and separation checks supplied inside the packet;
\item metric handoff exactness, requiring $E,Q,M,F$ to keep the $\MetricSpaceUp$ row downstream of the combined criterion route and compatible with the original topology windows;
\item refusal, requiring $G$ and $A$ to name only this finite packet and to refuse host open-cover compactness, selected covers, quotient topology equality, arbitrary refinements, countable choice, and ambient metric oracles.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bing-nagata-smirnov-metrization-carrier}. The projection exposes exactly the topology source, Bing route, Nagata--Smirnov route, paracompact local-finiteness dependency, common ledger, combined schedule, regularity-refinement row, exactness row, dyadic radius schedule, metric handoff, compatibility row, and the structural rows $H,C,G,A$.

Sibling route readability is forced by row order. A criterion request starts at $T$, reads the development data in $B$, reads the locally finite cover-family data in $N$, and then compares both through the common ledger $L$ and schedule $S$.

The metric handoff is downstream of the finite compatibility route. Rows $P,L,S,R,E$ record the local-finiteness supply, cover-layer checks, regularity separation, and exactness witness, while $Q,M,F$ record the dyadic schedule, metric-space handoff, and finite topology compatibility.

Since the tuple contains no host open-cover compactness coordinate, selected-cover coordinate, quotient topology equality coordinate, arbitrary refinement coordinate, countable-choice coordinate, or ambient metric oracle, the local NameCert cannot export those principles.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bing--Nagata--Smirnov metrization handoff]
\label{thm:bing-nagata-smirnov-metrization-handoff}
Every $\MetricSpaceUp$ consumer of an accepted $\BingNagataSmirnovMetrizationUp$ packet receives the finite route
$$
\TopologyUp,\ \BingMetrizationUp,\ \NagataSmirnovMetrizationUp,\ \ParacompactUp,\ L,\ S,\ R,\ E,\ Q,\ M,\ F,\ H,\ C,\ G,\ A .
$$
The handoff row $M$ is consumed only as a downstream metric-space interface tied to the displayed Bing development rows, Nagata--Smirnov locally finite cover rows, paracompact local-finiteness dependency row, regularity-refinement rows, and topology compatibility windows. It is not a host metric, not a selected-cover construction, and not a claim that the source topology equals a quotient topology.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier rows in order. The topology source $T$, Bing route $B$, Nagata--Smirnov route $N$, paracompact dependency $P$, common ledger $L$, combined schedule $S$, and regularity row $R$ are displayed before the exactness row can license a metric-facing read.

The rows $E,Q,M,F$ then supply the downstream interface: exactness binds the two sibling criteria to the same accepted request, the dyadic schedule prepares the metric handoff, and the compatibility row ties metric balls back to the topology source windows.

Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve that same route, while $G$ and $A$ record provenance and local naming. Because no row stores host open-cover compactness, selected covers, quotient topology equality, countable choice, or an ambient metric oracle, the $\MetricSpaceUp$ consumer receives only the finite BEDC handoff named above.
\end{proof}
