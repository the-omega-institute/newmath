It sits beside the compact source certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, the uniform-continuity carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactuniformcontinuity-namecert}, the finite star-refinement route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cover-star-refinement-modulus-namecert}, the mesh-chain cover route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cover-mesh-chain-namecert}, the local-modulus nerve of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-local-modulus-nerve-namecert}, and the tolerance-only handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}. \begin{definition}[CompactCoverOrderBound carrier]
\label{def:compact-cover-order-bound-carrier}
A $\CompactCoverOrderBoundUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(K,C,S,M,B,U,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactUp$ source row exposing the finite cover only through displayed compact data.
\item $C$ is the finite cover row, read as bundled centers, radii, or cells rather than as an ambient open-cover object.
\item $S$ is the star-refinement and mesh-chain row that records which cover cells may meet after refinement.
\item $M$ is the pointwise local-modulus row consumed by compact-uniform-continuity arguments.
\item $B$ is the finite cover-order bound row: for each displayed cell, it lists the finite family of neighboring cells allowed to overlap it.
\item $U$ is the downstream $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row reached through the bound $B$ and the local-modulus row $M$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, cover, refinement, local-modulus, bound, and uniform-modulus rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same finite cover-order route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactCoverOrderBoundUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\CompactUniformContinuityUp$, $\FiniteCoverStarRefinementModulusUp$, $\FiniteCoverMeshChainUp$, $\CompactUniformLocalModulusNerveUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactCoverOrderBoundUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an optimal subcover, a least Lebesgue number, an infinite locally finite family, countable choice, or an external compactness oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompactCoverOrderBound NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-cover-order-bound-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactCoverOrderBoundUp$ carrier $O=(K,C,S,M,B,U,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactCoverOrderBoundUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed packet, compact source exposure through $K$, finite cover exposure through $C$, star-refinement and mesh-chain exposure through $S$, local-modulus exposure through $M$, finite cover-order bound exposure through $B$, uniform-modulus handoff through $U$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $T$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The rows of \autoref{def:compact-cover-order-bound-carrier} are precisely the compact source, finite cover, refinement mesh, local-modulus, order-bound, uniform-modulus, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The cover-order row is finite because $B$ records only the displayed neighboring cells allowed by $C$ and $S$. No proof step ranges over an arbitrary open cover or over an infinite locally finite family.

The uniform-modulus handoff $U$ is downstream of the local-modulus row $M$ and the finite overlap bound $B$. The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ preserve the same bounded-overlap route, so the local certificate contains exactly the listed obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CompactCoverOrderBound uniform-modulus scope]
\label{thm:compact-cover-order-bound-uniform-modulus-scope}
Every uniform-modulus consumer of an accepted $\CompactCoverOrderBoundUp$ carrier reads the finite route
$$
\CompactUp \longmapsto \FiniteCoverStarRefinementModulusUp \longmapsto \FiniteCoverMeshChainUp \longmapsto \CompactUniformLocalModulusNerveUp \longmapsto \UniformModulusUp .
$$
The cover-order bound row $B$ is consumed before the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff $U$, and it confines the handoff to bounded-overlap finite cover rows. The route exports no optimal cover, least Lebesgue number, infinite cover selection, countable-choice schedule, or external compactness oracle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the compact source row $K$ and the finite cover row $C$. These rows expose a displayed finite family, not an arbitrary open-cover quantifier.

Use \autoref{thm:compact-cover-order-bound-namecert-obligations}. The obligation surface places the star-refinement row $S$, local-modulus row $M$, and cover-order bound row $B$ before any uniform-modulus read is available.

Finally read $U$ through $H,T,P,N$. The transport and replay rows preserve the same finite neighboring-cell list, while provenance and naming keep the route tied to the displayed carrier. Since no other cover-selection coordinate exists, the uniform-modulus scope is exactly the bounded-overlap finite cover route.
\end{proof}
