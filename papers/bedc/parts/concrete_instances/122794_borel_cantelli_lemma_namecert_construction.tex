\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BorelCantelliLemmaUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-borel-cantelli-lemma-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Borel and Cantelli, the $\BorelCantelliLemmaUp$ packet records the finite summable-event handoff used by first-lemma consumers before any repeated-hit conclusion is visible. It sits after the probability source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert}, the event-value surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-measure-namecert}, the finite union-bound ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-union-bound-namecert}, the event-sequence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-borel-cantelli-namecert}, and the bounded-window packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-borel-cantelli-namecert}. The packet names only displayed event rows, finite tail windows, partial probability sums, and the local summable-budget ledger; it does not import countable choice, almost-sure quotient equality, selected null sets, or the full infinite theorem.

\begin{definition}[Borel--Cantelli lemma carrier]
\label{def:borel-cantelli-lemma-carrier}
A $\BorelCantelliLemmaUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(\Omega,M,E,W,U,F,S,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\Omega$ is the $\ProbSpaceUp$ source row naming the normalized probability carrier.
\item $M$ is the $\MeasureUp$ event-value row through which each displayed event probability is read.
\item $E$ is the finite event-sequence packet, listing the inspected event names and measurable-event witnesses.
\item $W$ is the finite tail-window row, recording the bounded index range seen by the consumer.
\item $U$ is the $\FiniteUnionBoundUp$ ledger row for the union of events in the displayed window.
\item $F$ is the $\FiniteBorelCantelliUp$ bounded-window handoff row.
\item $S$ is the local summable-event budget row, recording the finite partial-sum bound available to the window.
\item $B$ is the Borel--Cantelli first-lemma route row read through $\BorelCantelliUp$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $\Omega,M,E,W,U,F,S,B,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the probability and measure rows through the event window, finite union-bound ledger, bounded-window handoff, summable-budget row, and first-lemma route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BorelCantelliLemmaUp$, $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\MeasureUp$, $\FiniteUnionBoundUp$, $\BorelCantelliUp$, $\FiniteBorelCantelliUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BorelCantelliLemmaUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for countable choice, almost-sure quotient equality, selected null-event witnesses, an independence converse, or ambient measure completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Borel--Cantelli lemma NameCert surface]
\label{thm:borel-cantelli-lemma-namecert-surface}
For every accepted $\BorelCantelliLemmaUp$ carrier $L=(\Omega,M,E,W,U,F,S,B,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BorelCantelliLemmaUp}$ surface consists exactly of probability-source admission through $\Omega$, event-value exposure through $M$, finite event-sequence admission through $E$, finite tail-window exposure through $W$, finite union-bound ledger exposure through $U$, bounded-window handoff through $F$, summable-budget exposure through $S$, first-lemma route exposure through $B$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:borel-cantelli-lemma-carrier}. The projection exposes exactly the probability source, measure event-value row, finite event-sequence packet, tail-window row, finite union-bound ledger, bounded-window handoff, summable-budget row, first-lemma route, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The continuation row fixes the order of inspection. A consumer first reads the probability and measure rows, then the finite event packet and tail window, then the finite union-bound ledger and bounded-window Borel--Cantelli handoff, and only then the local summable budget and first-lemma route.

Since the carrier contains no further coordinate, no countable-choice selector, almost-sure quotient equality, selected null-event witness, independence converse, or completed-measure row can be exported from this surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Borel--Cantelli lemma finite-sum handoff]
\label{thm:borel-cantelli-lemma-finite-sum-handoff}
Every accepted $\BorelCantelliLemmaUp$ consumer that reads a summable-event request must pass through the finite route
$$
\ProbSpaceUp,\ \MeasureUp,\ \FiniteUnionBoundUp,\ \FiniteBorelCantelliUp,\ \BorelCantelliUp .
$$
The event-sequence row $E$, tail-window row $W$, finite union-bound ledger $U$, bounded-window handoff $F$, and summable-budget row $S$ are visible before the first-lemma route $B$ is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the probability and measure rows $\Omega$ and $M$. They supply the source normalization and event-value reads for the displayed event sequence $E$.

Next restrict the request to the finite tail window $W$. The finite union-bound ledger $U$ applies only to that displayed window, so the probability estimate is attached to a visible finite event family.

Read the bounded-window handoff $F$ through \autoref{thm:finite-borel-cantelli-union-bound-route}. This places the finite Borel--Cantelli estimate downstream of $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\MeasureUp$, and $\FiniteUnionBoundUp$.

Finally the local summable-budget row $S$ and the first-lemma route row $B$ consume that finite estimate. Transport through $H$, replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$ preserve the same route, so the handoff remains finite BEDC data rather than a full infinite theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Borel--Cantelli lemma no-choice escape]
\label{thm:borel-cantelli-lemma-no-choice-escape}
An accepted $\BorelCantelliLemmaUp$ packet cannot expose countable choice, almost-sure quotient equality, a selected null-event witness, an independence-side converse, or ambient measure completion. Every public read of the packet is exhausted by the finite rows $\Omega,M,E,W,U,F,S,B,H,C,P,N$ of \autoref{def:borel-cantelli-lemma-carrier}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \autoref{thm:borel-cantelli-lemma-namecert-surface}. The NameCert surface is exactly the listed probability, measure, event, window, union-bound, bounded-window, summable-budget, first-lemma, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:borel-cantelli-lemma-finite-sum-handoff}. The only public route to the first-lemma row passes through the finite union-bound and bounded-window handoff before reading the summable budget.

The refused objects are absent from the carrier and from the handoff route. Therefore no consumer can recover them as hidden projections of the $\BorelCantelliLemmaUp$ packet.
\end{proof}
