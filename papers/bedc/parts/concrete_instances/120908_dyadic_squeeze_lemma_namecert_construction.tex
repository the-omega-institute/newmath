Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\DyadicSqueezeLemmaUp$ packet isolates the finite dyadic tolerance sandwich used when a middle regular-rational observation row is trapped between lower and upper dyadic rows before an L10 Real or Completion consumer can see the terminal seal. It sits over the dyadic endpoint core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the regular Cauchy enclosure of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-squeeze-namecert}, the dyadic lower/upper route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-sandwich-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a local dyadic tolerance lemma; it is not quotient stream equality, host trichotomy, a selected limit, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

$$

\item $L$ is the lower dyadic row, listing the inspected lower endpoints and their finite comparison witnesses.
\item $M$ is the middle $\RegSeqRatUp$ observation row whose displayed rational values are being squeezed.
\item $U$ is the upper dyadic row, listing the inspected upper endpoints and their matching comparison witnesses.
\item $W$ is the shared $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row on which $L,M,U$ are jointly observed.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance budget, recording the dyadic radius, split slack, and endpoint arithmetic used by the sandwich.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff row that exports the middle observations only after the dyadic bounds and shared window have been displayed.

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic squeeze lemma NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-squeeze-lemma-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicSqueezeLemmaUp$ carrier
$$
S_{\mathrm{dy}}=(L,M,U,W,D,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicSqueezeLemmaUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the lower dyadic row, middle regular-rational observation row, upper dyadic row, shared finite window, dyadic tolerance budget, RegSeqRat handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item lower-to-middle dyadic boundedness, requiring each displayed middle observation in $M$ to lie above the matching lower endpoint in $L$ on the shared window $W$ at the budget in $D$;
\item middle-to-upper dyadic boundedness, requiring the same displayed middle observation to lie below the matching upper endpoint in $U$ on $W$ at the budget in $D$;
\item shared-window stability, requiring all lower, middle, and upper reads to use the same finite observation row $W$;
\item tolerance exactness, requiring $D$ to be the sole dyadic budget consumed by both comparison sides and by the exported handoff;
\item RegSeqRat handoff, requiring $R$ to expose the middle regular-rational route only after $L,M,U,W,D$ have been read;
\item Real-seal nonescape, requiring every $\RealUp$ consumer to pass through the same dyadic and regular-rational rows before any terminal seal is available;
\item componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$;
\item refusal of quotient stream equality, host trichotomy, selected limits, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, and bridge checking as local certificate data.
\end{enumerate}
No dyadic squeeze lemma obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted packet $S_{\mathrm{dy}}=(L,M,U,W,D,R,H,C,P,N)$. The carrier exposes exactly the lower dyadic row, middle observation row, upper dyadic row, shared window, dyadic tolerance budget, RegSeqRat handoff, and the structural rows, so carrier admission and provenance are exhausted by those coordinates.

The two boundedness clauses are the two faces of the displayed dyadic sandwich. The lower-to-middle comparison and the middle-to-upper comparison both read the same middle row $M$, the same finite window $W$, and the same budget $D$.

Shared-window stability and tolerance exactness follow from the absence of any alternate schedule or radius coordinate. The packet cannot name a second window for one side of the squeeze, and it cannot replace the displayed dyadic budget with an external real-order comparison.

The handoff row $R$ is downstream of $L,M,U,W,D$. The sibling routes of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-squeeze-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-sandwich-namecert} consume exactly such displayed regular-rational and dyadic sandwich data before a Real-facing read is exposed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and locally name the same route. Since the carrier has no quotient stream, trichotomy, selected-limit, countable-choice, ambient-completeness, Lean-verification, or bridge-checking coordinate, the listed obligations exhaust the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicSqueezeLemmaUp}$ surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic squeeze lemma Real handoff]
\label{thm:dyadic-squeeze-lemma-real-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$-facing squeeze read from an accepted $\DyadicSqueezeLemmaUp$ carrier factors through
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp \longrightarrow \StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow
\left(\RegularCauchySqueezeUp,\ \DyadicSandwichUp\right) \longrightarrow \RealUp
$$
before the seal is visible. The exported read is a finite dyadic tolerance sandwich for the middle regular-rational row; it exports no quotient stream equality, host trichotomy, selected limit, countable-choice principle, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted carrier $S_{\mathrm{dy}}=(L,M,U,W,D,R,H,C,P,N)$. The dyadic data are read through $L,U,D$, so every endpoint comparison and tolerance request first enters the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger.

The finite observations are fixed by $W$. The middle row $M$ and the handoff row $R$ can therefore expose only the $\RegSeqRatUp$ observations scheduled by the displayed $\StreamNameUp$ window.

The regular Cauchy and dyadic sandwich consumers read the same data in the same order. The route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-squeeze-namecert} uses the middle regular-rational row and shared windows, while the route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-sandwich-namecert} uses the lower and upper dyadic bounds with the dyadic budget.

Only after those rows have been replayed through $C$ and transported through $H$ can $P,N$ package the result for the terminal $\RealUp$ read. No remaining coordinate supplies a quotient stream equality test, a host trichotomy case split, a selected limit, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness, so the displayed factorization is exhaustive.
\end{proof}
