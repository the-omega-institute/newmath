\begin{theorem}[Bolzano--Weierstrass cluster-source factorization]
\label{thm:bolzano-weierstrass-cluster-source-factorization}
Every public cluster read from an accepted $\BolzanoWeierstrassUp$ packet factors through the finite source route
$$
\BoundedRealSequenceUp,\ \RealSequenceClusterPointUp,\ \RegularCauchySubsequenceUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp
$$
before any Bolzano--Weierstrass seal is consumed. Equivalently, the public read first exposes the bounded sequence source, then the cluster-point carrier, then the regular Cauchy subsequence row, then the regular rational readback, and only then the terminal Real seal attached to the Bolzano--Weierstrass packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the public selector and cluster export surface of the Bolzano--Weierstrass chapter. The carrier and public export theorems expose the bounded source row $S$, the regular-subsequence row $R$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $Q$, and the terminal $\RealUp$ cluster seal $E$ before any public consumer may read the packet.

Now apply \autoref{thm:real-sequence-cluster-point-public-export}. It identifies the cluster-point consumer route through the bounded source row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedrealsequence-namecert}, the cofinal subsequence row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-subsequence-namecert}, selected windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance, and the Real cluster seal.

Composing these routes gives the stated factorization. The Bolzano--Weierstrass seal is downstream of the bounded source, cluster-point carrier, regular Cauchy subsequence, RegSeqRat readback, and Real seal, so no public cluster read can bypass them through selected subsequences, quotient stream equality, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cluster-source factorization formal target]
\label{thm:cluster-source-factorization-formal-target}
The formal target for \autoref{thm:bolzano-weierstrass-cluster-source-factorization} is the BEDC kernel statement that an accepted $\BolzanoWeierstrassUp$ cluster export factors through four displayed rows before the terminal seal is consumed: a bounded real sequence carrier over $\BHist$ and $\RealUp$; a closed-bounded interval net whose endpoints and refinements are recorded by $\Cont$ replay; a regular Cauchy subsequence row governed by $\RegularCauchySubsequenceUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback; and a cluster-point witness in the $\RealSequenceClusterPointUp$ surface. The statement names only this route and does not claim full sequential compactness for arbitrary real sequences.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The bounded real sequence carrier supplies the source row and the interval net records finite closed-bounded windows. Both are ordinary BEDC rows, so the formalization can expose them by carrier projection and displayed $\Cont$ replay.

The regular Cauchy subsequence row then gives the constructive subsequence route, while $\RegSeqRatUp$ supplies rational readback of the same row. The cluster-point witness is read only after these rows have been exposed.

Thus the formal target is a factorization theorem about the accepted packet's named rows. It does not need a host compactness principle, an open-cover argument, or a choice-selected subsequence outside the displayed regular Cauchy data.
\end{proof}
