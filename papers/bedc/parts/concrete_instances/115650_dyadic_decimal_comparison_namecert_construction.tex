\begin{definition}[Dyadic decimal comparison carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-decimal-comparison-carrier}
A $\DyadicDecimalComparisonUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(D,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DecimalExpansionUp$ finite-prefix row, displaying the inspected digit block, its place-value ledger, and the decimal window from which the comparison is read.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, recording the dyadic comparison radius, slack budget, and finite rational normalization used for the displayed prefix.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row, listing exactly the prefix positions and tolerance requests inspected by the comparison.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same decimal prefix and dyadic tolerance data.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,Q,W,R$ have all been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,Q,W,R,E$ and for the replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a comparison request through the decimal prefix, dyadic tolerance, finite window, regular readback, and real seal rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicDecimalComparisonUp$, $\DecimalExpansionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicDecimalComparisonUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an infinite decimal expansion, quotient real equality, endpoint selection, host trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic decimal comparison NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-decimal-comparison-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicDecimalComparisonUp$ carrier $K=(D,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicDecimalComparisonUp}$ surface consists exactly of five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the finite packet $K$;
\item classifier locality for the $\DecimalExpansionUp$ prefix row, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal row;
\item stability under componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$ and displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$;
\item exactness of the ordered route $D,Q,W,R,E$ from decimal prefix to dyadic tolerance, finite window, regular readback, and real seal;
\item ledger control through $\Pkg$ provenance $P$ and the local naming row $N$.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation may replace these rows by an infinite decimal stream, quotient Cauchy equality, endpoint convention selection, host trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-decimal-comparison-carrier}. The analytic rows are $D,Q,W,R,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The locality obligation reads only displayed finite data. The decimal row $D$ exposes the inspected finite prefix, the dyadic row $Q$ fixes the tolerance and normalization budget, the window row $W$ records which prefix positions are inspected, and the readback and seal rows $R,E$ are downstream of those rows.

The stability obligation is structural. Componentwise transport $H$ preserves each displayed row, and replay $C$ repeats the same route from a comparison request to the terminal seal.

The exactness and ledger obligations are then exhausted by $D,Q,W,R,E,P,N$. Since the carrier has no row for infinite streams, quotient equality, endpoint selection, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the five listed rows are the whole local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
