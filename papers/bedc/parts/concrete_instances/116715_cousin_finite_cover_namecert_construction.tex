\begin{definition}[Cousin finite-cover carrier]
\label{def:cousin-finite-cover-carrier}
A $\CousinFiniteCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(I,G,C,T,M,W,R,E,H,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the located closed interval row.
\item $G$ is the positive gauge row over $I$.
\item $C$ is the finite gauge-cover row read from $\CousinCoverUp$.
\item $T$ is the finite tagged-subinterval extraction row consumed by $\CousinLemmaUp$.
\item $M$ is the mesh and adjacency ledger for the extracted finite cover cells.
\item $W$ is the located bisection witness row, recording the finite search tree used when a displayed cell is refined.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational radii and mesh bounds.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for endpoint and radius comparisons.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for interval, gauge, cover, tagged-cell, mesh, bisection, readback, Real-seal, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CousinFiniteCoverUp$, $\CousinCoverUp$, $\CousinLemmaUp$, $\FiniteLebesgueNumberUp$, $\LocatedIntervalBisectionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CousinFiniteCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,G,C,T,M,W,R,E,H,P,N$. It contains no open-cover compactness theorem, selected subcover oracle, quotient cover equality, ambient completeness assertion, or evidence-erased passage from local gauge data to a tagged partition.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cousin finite-cover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cousin-finite-cover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CousinFiniteCoverUp$ carrier
$$
K=(I,G,C,T,M,W,R,E,H,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CousinFiniteCoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of located interval admission, positive gauge admission, finite $\CousinCoverUp$ cover exposure, finite tagged-cell extraction toward $\CousinLemmaUp$, mesh and adjacency exactness, located bisection support, $\RegSeqRatUp$ radius readback, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay through the named rows, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation is discharged by open-cover compactness, hidden subcover selection, quotient cover equality, host real completeness, or a direct lemma-level subdivision endpoint.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cousin-finite-cover-carrier}. The only visible analytic rows are $I,G,C,T,M,W,R,E$, followed by the structural rows $H,P,N$ and displayed replay through the same packet.

The cover row $C$ supplies the finite local cover data, while the extraction row $T$ records the finite tagged cells that can be handed to $\CousinLemmaUp$. The mesh ledger $M$ and bisection row $W$ keep the extraction finite and interval-local.

The readback and seal rows $R,E$ attach rational radius comparisons and endpoint comparisons to the same packet. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming then preserve the row order. Since the carrier has no coordinate for compactness, selected subcovers, quotient cover equality, host completeness, or a direct subdivision endpoint, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cousin finite-cover gauge-refinement route]
\label{thm:cousin-finite-cover-gauge-refinement-route}
Let $K=(I,G,C,T,M,W,R,E,H,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CousinFiniteCoverUp$ carrier. Any consumer that refines a gauge cover toward a Cousin subdivision must expose the $\CousinCoverUp$ cover row $C$, the finite bisection row $W$, the mesh and adjacency ledger $M$, the rational readback row $R$, the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$, and the tagged extraction row $T$ before it may cite the $\CousinLemmaUp$ handoff. The route exports a finite extraction packet and cannot replace it by open-cover compactness, an arbitrary dense real argument, host subcover choice, quotient cover equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite cover row $C$ and the displayed gauge row $G$. These rows identify the local cover cells over the located interval $I$, but they do not by themselves export a tagged partition.

Use the bisection row $W$ together with the mesh ledger $M$. The finite bisection search refines failed cells, records adjacency and mesh comparisons, and keeps each accepted cell attached to the same gauge row.

The rational readback $R$ and Real seal $E$ verify the radius and endpoint comparisons used by the refinement. The tagged extraction row $T$ is therefore a finite handoff row rather than a selected cover family.

Finally replay the packet through $H,P,N$. Componentwise transport and provenance keep the consumer on the same $I,G,C,T,M,W,R,E$ rows, so no compactness theorem, dense real argument, host selector, quotient cover equality, or ambient completeness coordinate is available.
\end{proof}
