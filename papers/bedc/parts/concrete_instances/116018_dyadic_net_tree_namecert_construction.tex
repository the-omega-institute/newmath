Following Christ's dyadic cubes and finite metric net-tree practice in constructive metric analysis, the $\DyadicNetTreeUp$ packet records a multiscale finite-net skeleton as BEDC NameCert data. It sits over the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the doubling control of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-doubling-metric-namecert}, the levelwise finite-net rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, the total-bounded coverage surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the covering-number budget of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-covering-number-namecert}, and the dyadic scale ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}. The packet names the finite multiscale route only; it does not import a host cover tree, quotient equality of trees, selected centers outside the displayed net rows, or ambient compactness.

\begin{definition}[DyadicNetTree carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-net-tree-carrier}
A $\DyadicNetTreeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(M,S,E,T,B,L,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, recording the carrier, distance-reading route, and local metric naming surface.
\item $S$ is the $\DoublingMetricUp$ scale-control row, recording the finite doubling budget used to compare adjacent dyadic levels.
\item $E$ is the $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ family row, exposing the center bundle selected at each displayed dyadic level.
\item $T$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ coverage row, asserting only the displayed finite coverage witnesses read from the same levelwise nets.
\item $B$ is the $\MetricCoveringNumberUp$ budget row, storing the finite upper bound consumed by the level ledger.
\item $L$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ scale ledger, storing level indices, dyadic radii, mesh comparisons, and late-radius requests.
\item $R$ is the parent-child refinement row, recording for every displayed child center the parent center, scale comparison, containment witness, and nonescape certificate visible at the next coarser level.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, doubling row, finite-net family, total-bounded coverage, covering budget, dyadic ledger, parent-child refinement, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a multiscale net-tree request through $M,S,E,T,B,L,R,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicNetTreeUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\DoublingMetricUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\MetricCoveringNumberUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicNetTreeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,S,E,T,B,L,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected host tree, an unbounded center oracle, minimal cover cardinality, open-cover compactness, quotient equality of trees, or ambient completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[DyadicNetTree NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-net-tree-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicNetTreeUp$ carrier
$$
D=(M,S,E,T,B,L,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicNetTreeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission through $M$, scale visibility through $S$ and $L$, levelwise coverage through $E$ and $T$, parent-child refinement through $R$, budget nonescape through $B$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-net-tree-carrier}. The metric source row $M$ is the only carrier admission surface, while $S$ and $L$ expose the doubling and dyadic scale data before any level is read.

The levelwise coverage obligations are then forced through $E$ and $T$. The finite-net row displays the centers at each inspected level, and the total-bounded row supplies only the coverage witnesses attached to those displayed centers.

The budget and refinement rows are separate. The row $B$ keeps the covering-number budget visible, and the row $R$ records the parent of each displayed child center together with the containment and nonescape witnesses needed for the next coarser level.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier contains no host-tree, minimal-cardinality, open-cover, quotient-tree, center-oracle, or ambient-completion coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
