\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SionMinimaxUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sion-minimax-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Sion's minimax theorem and Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis,
the $\SionMinimaxUp$ packet records finite compact-convex minimax data as
NameCert rows rather than as a host saddle-point selection principle. It sits
between the finite payoff-table route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-game-minimax-namecert}, the KKM-facing
convex route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ky-fan-minimax-namecert}, the
primal-dual convex packet of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fenchelduality-namecert}, and the compact
interval source of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convex-compact-interval-namecert}. Its scalar
readback is routed through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[SionMinimax finite compact-convex carrier]
\label{def:sion-minimax-carrier}
A $\SionMinimaxUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(X,Y,F,A,T,R,W,D,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the compact convex source row, read through displayed
finite-window membership and finite convex-combination checks rather than
through an ambient compactness theorem.
\item $Y$ is the opposite convex source row, with the same finite-window
membership and convex-combination discipline for the second variable.
\item $F$ is the convex-concave payoff row, recording only displayed
two-variable payoff comparisons and the finite slice checks needed by
downstream minimax consumers.
\item $A$ is the finite affine-spine ledger for the inspected convex
combinations in $X$ and $Y$.
\item $T$ is the finite saddle-window approximant row, listing paired lower
and upper comparison windows before any scalar value is sealed.
\item $R$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger, recording the requested comparison
precision through the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ route.
\item $W$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row that turns the finite tolerance
ledger into regular rational comparison data.
\item $D$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule that orders the finite
inspection windows consumed by $T,R,W$.
\item $Q$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, available only after the finite
saddle-window, tolerance, rational-readback, and stream-schedule rows are
displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact-convex source
rows, payoff rows, affine-spine rows, saddle-window approximants, tolerance
ledgers, readback rows, schedule rows, value seals, replay, provenance, and
local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same finite compact-convex
minimax route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SionMinimaxUp$,
$\FiniteGameMinimaxUp$, $\KyFanMinimaxUp$, $\FenchelDualityUp$,
$\ConvexCompactIntervalUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$,
$\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SionMinimaxUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through
$X,Y,F,A,T,R,W,D,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host suprema or infima,
choice-selected saddle points, quotient equality, full compactness, or ambient
real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SionMinimax NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sion-minimax-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SionMinimaxUp$ carrier
$$
S=(X,Y,F,A,T,R,W,D,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SionMinimaxUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier
admission, classifier comparison, saddle-window exactness, dyadic tolerance
ledger readback, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, componentwise $\hsame$ transport,
displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. A minimax
equality read is admitted only after the compact-convex source rows, payoff
row, affine-spine ledger, finite saddle-window approximants, tolerance ledger,
rational readback, stream schedule, value seal, transport, replay,
provenance, and local certificate are visible in the same packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of
\autoref{def:sion-minimax-carrier}. The source rows $X,Y$, the payoff row
$F$, and the affine-spine ledger $A$ display the compact-convex minimax
surface before any scalar comparison is read.

The finite approximation route is $T,R,W,D$. The row $T$ supplies the lower
and upper saddle windows, $R$ records their dyadic tolerance budget, $W$
exports regular rational readback, and $D$ fixes the stream-window schedule
that orders those finite reads.

The value seal $Q$ is downstream of that route. Componentwise transport,
replay, provenance, and local naming are then supplied by $H,C,P,N$.

Since no row stores host suprema, host infima, a selected saddle pair, quotient
equality, full compactness, or ambient real completeness, the listed rows
exhaust the local naming obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[SionMinimax finite saddle-window route]
\label{thm:sion-minimax-finite-saddle-window}
Every minimax equality consumer of an accepted $\SionMinimaxUp$ packet must
pass through the finite saddle-window row $T$ before the $\RealUp$ seal $Q$ is
public. Thus the scalar equality exported by the packet is read only after the
displayed compact-convex rows, convex-concave payoff row, affine-spine ledger,
dyadic tolerance ledger, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and $\StreamNameUp$ schedule
have all been inspected.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:sion-minimax-namecert-obligations}. It orders the accepted
packet so that the source, payoff, and affine-spine rows are displayed before
the finite saddle-window approximants are read.

The only path from those approximants to a scalar equality is through
$R,W,D,Q$. The dyadic tolerance ledger $R$, regular rational readback $W$, and
stream schedule $D$ are all visible before the $\RealUp$ seal $Q$ can serve a
consumer.

Therefore a minimax equality read cannot skip directly to an ambient scalar
principle. It factors through the finite saddle-window route carried by the
same packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[SionMinimax nonescape boundary]
\label{thm:sion-minimax-nonescape}
An accepted $\SionMinimaxUp$ packet exports no host supremum, host infimum,
choice-selected saddle point, quotient equality of points or functions, full
compactness theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle. Every public
minimax read is confined to the displayed rows
$X,Y,F,A,T,R,W,D,Q,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the carrier definition. Its coordinates are exactly
$X,Y,F,A,T,R,W,D,Q,H,C,P,N$, and the classifier compares packets only through
those rows.

The obligation theorem identifies the same rows as the complete local
NameCert surface. The finite saddle-window theorem then forces every scalar
equality read through $T,R,W,D$ before the value seal $Q$.

Because none of these rows contains a host extremum operator, a selected
saddle pair, a quotient-equality witness, full compactness data, or ambient
real-completeness data, no consumer can extract such a principle from the
packet.
\end{proof}
