\begin{theorem}[Heron square-root Newton sibling lattice]
\label{thm:heron-square-root-newton-sibling-lattice}
Every accepted $\HeronSquareRootUp$ read that exposes a square-root-facing value first consumes the $\NewtonIterationUp$ route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-newtoniteration-namecert}, the Newton interval route already attached to the Heron window, the regular Cauchy dependency through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the reciprocal Real route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-reciprocal-namecert}. For a carrier
$$
H=(A,S,R,L,W,D,K,Q,E,T,C,P,N),
$$
the read projects the positive radicand and seed rows, the reciprocal ledger, the finite Heron window, the contraction estimate, the Newton provenance row $K$, the regular-rational readback $Q$, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} before any square-root read is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows $A,S,R,L,W,D,K,Q,E,T,C,P,N$. The finite Heron ledger is visible before any consumer-facing read, so the Newton provenance row $K$ is read while the positive radicand, reciprocal ledger, window, and contraction estimate are still part of the displayed packet.

Apply \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-newton-dependency-route} and \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-newton-interval-dependency}. These sibling routes attach the Newton iteration surface and the interval-shrink evidence to the same Heron window rather than to a separate square-root object.

Next apply \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-regular-cauchy-dependency} and \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-real-reciprocal-forward-link}. The regular Cauchy route supplies the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $Q$, while the reciprocal route forces the reciprocal ledger through the existing Real reciprocal packet before $Q$ and $E$ are public.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-real-seal-boundary}. The terminal $\RealUp$ row $E$ is available only after the Newton, regular Cauchy, and reciprocal rows have all been displayed, so no host square-root function, quotient-real equality, selected limit, trichotomy, or ambient completeness coordinate can bypass the sibling lattice.
\end{proof}
