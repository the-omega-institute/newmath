\begin{theorem}[Heron square-root Newton regular-Cauchy bridge]
\label{thm:heron-square-root-newton-regular-cauchy-bridge}
Every accepted $\HeronSquareRootUp$ read factors its Newton update route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-newtoniteration-namecert} and its convergence and seal route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-square-root-namecert} before reaching the Heron square-root $\NameCert$ surface. For a carrier
$$
H=(A,S,R,L,W,D,K,Q,E,T,C,P,N),
$$
the rows $A,S,R,L,W,D,K$ expose the displayed Heron recurrence as a finite $\NewtonIterationUp$ update, while $Q,E,T,C,P,N$ expose the $\RegularCauchySquareRootUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. No accepted consumer may read the Heron square-root packet from the Newton row alone or from the regular-Cauchy seal alone.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The rows $A,S,R,L,W,D$ give the positive radicand, seed, reciprocal ledger, recurrence ledger, visible window, and contraction estimate, while $K$ records that this finite recurrence is the displayed $\NewtonIterationUp$ route.

Apply \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-newton-dependency-route}. It places every square-root-facing read downstream of the finite Newton update ledger and before the regular rational readback and terminal seal are exposed.

Apply \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-regular-cauchy-square-root-forward-route}. It routes the same packet through the $\RegularCauchySquareRootUp$ convergence service, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $Q$, and the $\RealUp$ seal row $E$ before export.

Finally use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-namecert-obligations}. The rows $T,C,P,N$ preserve transport, replay, provenance, and local naming for the joint route, so neither the Newton update nor the regular-Cauchy seal can be consumed as an isolated square-root source.
\end{proof}
