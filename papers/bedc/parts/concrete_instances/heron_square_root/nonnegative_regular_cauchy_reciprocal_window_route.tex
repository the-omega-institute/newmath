\begin{theorem}[Heron square-root nonnegative regular-Cauchy reciprocal-window route]
\label{thm:heron-square-root-nonnegative-regular-cauchy-reciprocal-window-route}
Every accepted $\HeronSquareRootUp$ read consumed by either \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-reciprocal-namecert} or \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-square-root-namecert} must display the same nonnegative $\RealUp$ radicand row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, and reciprocal-window budget before the square-root-facing seal is public. For a carrier
$$
H=(A,S,R,L,W,D,K,Q,E,T,C,P,N),
$$
the dependency route first exposes the positive radicand and seed rows $A,S$, then the reciprocal ledger $R$ through the reciprocal-window rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyinversebudget-namecert}, then the finite Heron window $L,W,D,K$, and only then the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $Q$ and terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$ used by the regular-Cauchy square-root route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the Heron carrier. The rows $A,S$ display the positive radicand and seed, while $R$ is the reciprocal ledger consumed by the Heron recurrence.

Read the reciprocal-window dependency through \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-real-reciprocal-forward-link}. The Real reciprocal chapter can consume the Heron packet only after the reciprocal ledger has passed through the finite inverse-budget and guarded-division rows named there.

Next read the regular-Cauchy square-root dependency through \autoref{thm:heron-square-root-regular-cauchy-square-root-forward-route}. The same nonnegative radicand route must expose $L,W,D,K$ before the regular-rational readback $Q$ and terminal Real seal $E$ are visible.

The structural rows $T,C,P,N$ preserve the shared route. Thus the reciprocal and regular-Cauchy square-root consumers meet at the displayed nonnegative Real row, RegSeqRat readback, and reciprocal-window rows, with no host square-root function, quotient real equality, selected completed limit, trichotomy, or ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{proof}
