\begin{theorem}[Heron square-root Newton interval dependency]
\label{thm:heron-square-root-newton-interval-dependency}
Every accepted $\HeronSquareRootUp$ read that exports a $\RealUp$-facing square-root approximation consumes either the $\NewtonIterationUp$ provenance row or the nested-interval shrink route before the regular-rational and Real rows are public. For a carrier
$$
H=(A,S,R,L,W,D,K,Q,E,T,C,P,N),
$$
the route first exposes the positive dyadic radicand and seed rows $A,S$, the reciprocal ledger $R$, the Heron recurrence window $L,W$, and the contraction estimate $D$. It then reads the Newton provenance row $K$ through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-newtoniteration-namecert}, or the interval-shrink evidence through the nested route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-real-nested-intervals-namecert}, before reaching the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the Heron carrier. The rows $A,S,R,L,W,D$ are the finite radicand, seed, reciprocal, recurrence-window, and contraction rows; none of them is a completed square-root value.

The iteration witness is consumed next. If the packet follows the Newton route, the row $K$ identifies the Heron recurrence as the displayed $\NewtonIterationUp$ step for $x^2-A$. If it follows the interval route, the same contraction ledger is read as retained nested interval shrink data through the located nested-interval chapter.

Only after one of those finite iteration routes is visible may the Real-facing rows be inspected. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $Q$ records the regular rational readback of the displayed approximation window, and the $\RealUp$ row $E$ seals the terminal read. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $T,C,P,N$ preserve that dependency order and add no host square-root operation, selected limit, quotient-real equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{proof}
