\begin{theorem}[Morse lemma critical-chart ledger]
\label{thm:morse-lemma-critical-chart-ledger}
For every accepted $\MorseLemmaUp$ carrier $M=(X,C,Q,S,L,NF,H,R,P,A)$, the critical-chart ledger exposes the chart-source row $X$, the derivative-vanishing critical-point row $C$, and the Hessian-signature row $Q$ before any local-coordinate row $S$ or quadratic normal-form row $L$ may be read. A consumer that omits one of $X,C,Q$ receives no accepted Morse-lemma packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:morse-lemma-namecert-carrier}. The chart-source, critical-point, and Hessian-signature data are separate rows, and no later coordinate stores a replacement for them.

The coordinate row $S$ is downstream of these rows. It records a local transformation only after the chart window, derivative-vanishing witness, and nondegenerate Hessian signature have been displayed.

The quadratic row $L$ is downstream again. Since $L$ reads the signed square decomposition determined by $Q$ inside the chart named by $X$ at the critical point named by $C$, the packet cannot admit a normal-form read with any of those rows missing.
\end{proof}
