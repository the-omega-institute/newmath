\label{thm:bishop-diagonal-cauchy-telescope-regular-readback-forward-link}

\end{theorem}

Thus the regular rational component is not an optional sibling citation. It is a forward dependency carried by every accepted diagonal-tail packet before any terminal $\RealUp$ seal or Completion-facing consequence may be consumed.
\end{proof}
