\label{thm:bishop-diagonal-cauchy-telescope-regseqrat-route}
Every Real-and-Completion consumer of an accepted $\BishopDiagonalCauchyTelescopeUp$ packet must read the rational telescope route, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance rows, $\StreamNameUp$ finite windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and $\RealUp$ seal in that order before the diagonal tail is public. $$
R,\quad D,\quad W,\quad G,\quad E
$$
inside $T=(R,D,W,G,M,L,E,H,C,P,N)$, with the diagonal modulus $M$, tail-link ledger $L$, transport $H$, replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local $\NameCert$ row $N$ preserving the same route.
\end{theorem}

Project the carrier from \autoref{def:bishop-diagonal-cauchy-telescope-carrier}. The rational telescope row $R$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, finite-window row $W$, regular rational readback $G$, and terminal real seal $E$ are the only Real-facing analytic rows available to the packet.

Apply the NameCert obligation surface of \autoref{thm:bishop-diagonal-cauchy-telescope-namecert-obligations}. It forces any Real or Completion consumer to expose the rational telescope, dyadic tolerance, finite tail windows, RegSeqRat readback, diagonal modulus, and finite tail-link composition before using the completed real-name seal.

Finally read the tail route of \autoref{thm:bishop-diagonal-cauchy-telescope-tail-route}. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that ordered route, so no quotient stream equality, selected host limit, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness is available to the consumer.
\end{proof}
