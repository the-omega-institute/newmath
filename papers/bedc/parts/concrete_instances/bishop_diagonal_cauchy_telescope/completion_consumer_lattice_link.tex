\begin{theorem}[Bishop diagonal Cauchy telescope completion-consumer lattice link]
\label{thm:bishop-diagonal-cauchy-telescope-completion-consumer-lattice-link}
The downstream packets $\CauchyCompletenessRateUp$ and $\RealConvergenceRateExtractorUp$ consume the $\BishopDiagonalCauchyTelescopeUp$ route through the same finite Real-and-Completion lattice: dyadic tolerance rows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal rows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. In particular, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completeness-rate-namecert} reads the telescope as a rate-extraction input, while \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-convergence-rate-extractor-namecert} reads it as the displayed route that prevents selected limits, quotient representatives, countable choice, and ambient completeness from entering the Real-facing convergence-rate surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier and tail-route obligations of \autoref{thm:bishop-diagonal-cauchy-telescope-namecert-obligations} and \autoref{thm:bishop-diagonal-cauchy-telescope-tail-route}. They force every consumer to pass through the rational telescope row, dyadic tolerance ledger, finite stream window, regular rational readback, diagonal-modulus row, finite tail-link ledger, and Real seal before the diagonal tail is public.

The $\CauchyCompletenessRateUp$ chapter cites this route as one of its displayed rate-extraction inputs, and the $\RealConvergenceRateExtractorUp$ chapter cites it as the convergence-rate readback route. Both consumers therefore inherit the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ lattice rather than replacing it by a host limit or quotient stream.
\end{proof}
