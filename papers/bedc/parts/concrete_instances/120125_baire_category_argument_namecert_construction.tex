\begin{theorem}[Baire category argument NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:baire-category-argument-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BaireCategoryArgumentUp$ carrier $A=(B,K,M,I,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BaireCategoryArgumentUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the eleven displayed rows; dense-open prefix exposure through $B$; Bishop complete metric service through $K$; shrinking-ball metric comparison through $M$; nested-ball induction through $I$; finite-window and regular-rational readback through $W,R$; terminal Real sealing through $E$; componentwise transport through $H$; displayed replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:baire-category-argument-carrier}. Its analytic rows are exactly $B,K,M,I,W,R,E$, and its structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

The dense-open schedule row $B$ supplies only finite prefixes. The complete-metric service row $K$ and metric shrinking row $M$ turn each request in such a prefix into a successor ball inside the predecessor ball, with all radii and distance comparisons displayed as carrier data.

The nested-ball row $I$ records the induction spine for the same prefix. The finite-window row $W$ and regular-rational readback row $R$ expose the centers, radii, and tolerances before the terminal Real seal $E$ can be inspected.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same finite route. Since the carrier contains no further coordinate, these rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations and block host intersections, countable-choice selectors, quotient real equality, open-cover compactness, and ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Baire category argument nested-ball induction]
\label{thm:baire-category-argument-nested-ball-induction}
Let $A=(B,K,M,I,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\BaireCategoryArgumentUp$ carrier. Every finite dense-open prefix in $B$ determines a nested predecessor/successor ball chain whose successor at each stage is obtained through the complete-metric service $K$, checked in the metric shrinking row $M$, recorded in the induction row $I$, displayed through the finite-window and regular-rational rows $W,R$, and sealed by $E$ only after the finite chain has been built. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same chain under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the displayed finite dense-open prefix in $B$.

For the empty prefix, the carrier provides the initial metric context through $M$ and the complete-metric service boundary through $K$. The induction row $I$ records the empty nested-ball spine, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that base route.

For the successor step, assume the predecessor prefix has already produced a nested chain whose last ball is displayed in $M$ and whose window data have been read through $W,R$. Consume the next dense-open request from $B$ and use the complete-metric service $K$ to obtain the successor-ball obligation inside the predecessor ball.

Record the metric part of the step in $M$: center, radius, containment, and shrinkage comparisons are all displayed there. Append the step to the nested-ball induction row $I$, then expose the corresponding finite windows and regular rational readback through $W$ and $R$.

The terminal seal $E$ is read only after those rows are visible. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$, and provenance and local naming through $P$ and $N$, preserve the same finite chain without adding a host intersection point, countable-choice selector, quotient real equality, or ambient completeness witness.
\end{proof}
