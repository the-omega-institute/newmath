Following Bishop and Bridges and Kelley-style completion theory, the $\CauchyCompletionIntersectionUp$ packet records the finite BEDC route by which two completion-facing Cauchy reads are compared through a shared window before any terminal real seal is consumed. It sits after the regular Cauchy completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-regular-cauchy-completion-namecert}, the completion monad law surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-monad-law-namecert}, and the L10 readback spine through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[CauchyCompletionIntersection carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-completion-intersection-carrier}
A $\CauchyCompletionIntersectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(A,B,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the first completion-facing Cauchy route, including its source name, regularity request, and completion consumer boundary.
\item $B$ is the second completion-facing Cauchy route, with the same kind of source, regularity, and consumer data.
\item $W$ is the shared $\StreamNameUp$ window row through which both routes must expose finite observations before either route reaches a completion seal.
\item $D$ is the shared $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording the dyadic radius, slack, comparison budget, and refinement data used by both window reads.
\item $R$ is the shared $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained after $A$ and $B$ have both passed through $W$ and $D$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after the two displayed routes have reached the same finite window, tolerance, and regular-rational readback surface.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $A,B,W,D,R,E,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the route order $A,B,W,D,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyCompletionIntersectionUp$, $\BishopRegularCauchyCompletionUp$, $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionIntersectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,B,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$. No coordinate stores quotient completion classes, selected stream representatives, a countable-choice selector, detached host equality of completed points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyCompletionIntersection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-intersection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompletionIntersectionUp$ carrier $I=(A,B,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionIntersectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the two completion-facing Cauchy routes, shared $\StreamNameUp$ window exposure, shared $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance exposure, shared $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal admission, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.

Every consumer of the packet must read $A$ and $B$ before opening the shared window row $W$, must read $D$ before comparing regular-rational readbacks through $R$, and must reach the terminal seal $E$ only after the same finite rows have been displayed for both routes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-intersection-carrier}. The two completion-facing Cauchy routes are exactly $A$ and $B$, and the shared analytic readback surface is exactly $W,D,R,E$.

The window and tolerance obligations are forced by the carrier order. A consumer must first display both Cauchy routes, then open the common $\StreamNameUp$ window and the shared $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger before any $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback can be compared.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since there is no coordinate for quotient classes, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyCompletionIntersection shared-window route]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-intersection-window-route}
Let $I=(A,B,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CauchyCompletionIntersectionUp$ packet. Any completion-facing read that claims the two Cauchy routes meet must factor through the finite route
$$
A,B\longmapsto W\longmapsto D\longmapsto R\longmapsto E\longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the intersection is witnessed by shared StreamName windows, DyadicRatCore tolerances, RegSeqRat readback, and the Real seal, not by quotienting the two routes or selecting a representative completion point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the two route rows $A$ and $B$. They are separate coordinates of the carrier, so neither one can be erased or replaced by the other before the shared readback surface is opened.

The common window row $W$ and tolerance ledger $D$ provide the finite comparison surface. The regular-rational row $R$ is read only after both routes have passed through those shared rows, and the real seal $E$ is downstream of that readback.

Finally the transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows preserve the displayed order. The carrier therefore supplies a finite shared-window route and no quotient, selected representative, choice schedule, host equality, or ambient-completeness route.
\end{proof}
