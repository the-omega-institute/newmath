Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive completions by regular Cauchy data, the $\CauchyRepletionUp$ packet names the finite repletion corridor between displayed stream observations and the $\RealUp$ seal. It sits beside the finite windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the completion-admission surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycompletioncriterion-namecert}, and the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a non-quotient repletion object for L10 completion consumers; it does not import selected limits, quotient equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Cauchy repletion carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-repletion-carrier}
A $\CauchyRepletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(S,R_0,D,Q,U,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation schedule through which the repletion request reads stream data.
\item $R_0$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback attached to those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger recording tolerance scales, comparison bounds, and dyadic threshold data.
\item $Q$ is the $\CauchyCompletionCriterionUp$ admission row, read only after $S,R_0,D$ have been displayed.
\item $U$ is the separated comparison row, recording the finite apartness and equality-refusal data used before any seal-facing comparison is exported.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached from the criterion and separated comparison rows.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream schedule, readback, dyadic ledger, criterion row, separated comparison, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from finite windows to regular readback, from readback through the dyadic and criterion rows, and from the separated comparison row to the seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyRepletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\CauchyCompletionCriterionUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyRepletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $S,R_0,D,Q,U,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient streams, selected host limits, countable-choice schedules, host real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy repletion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-repletion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyRepletionUp$ carrier $R=(S,R_0,D,Q,U,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyRepletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite-window readback, dyadic tolerance routing, completion-criterion admission, separated comparison, Real seal exposure, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every repletion consumer must display the $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R_0$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$, $\CauchyCompletionCriterionUp$ row $Q$, separated comparison row $U$, $\RealUp$ seal $E$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$ before it reads the packet as a completion neighbour.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-repletion-carrier}. The ten displayed rows are the only classifier-visible coordinates, so carrier admission and local naming are immediate row projections.

The analytic obligations are also row projections. The stream schedule $S$ precedes the regular readback $R_0$, the dyadic ledger $D$ records the tolerance data used by that readback, and the criterion row $Q$ is the only completion-admission coordinate available before a seal-facing read.

The separated comparison row $U$ and seal row $E$ complete the finite route. Transport $H$, replay $C$, and provenance $P$ preserve the same packet, while the absence of quotient, selected-limit, countable-choice, host-equality, and ambient-completeness coordinates gives the non-escape clause.
\end{proof}
