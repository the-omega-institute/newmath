\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ClopenAlgebraUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-clopen-algebra-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Stone and Birkhoff on Boolean algebras of clopen sets, the $\ClopenAlgebraUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which a displayed topology row supplies clopen rows together with Boolean operations on those rows. It sits beside the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the Boolean algebra carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-booleanalgebra-namecert}, the Stone-duality clopen ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stoneduality-namecert}, the connectedness refusal surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-connected-space-namecert}, and the total-disconnectedness separation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totally-disconnected-space-namecert}. The packet names a local finite clopen algebra ledger; it does not assert a global powerset algebra, selected clopen basis, ultrafilter representation, host subset extensionality, or excluded-middle separation.

\begin{definition}[Clopen algebra carrier]
\label{def:clopen-algebra-carrier}
A $\ClopenAlgebraUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(T,O,\bar O,B,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row. It records the open-set classifier, displayed empty and whole opens, and finite inclusion requests inspected by the packet.
\item $O$ is the displayed open row for each clopen candidate under inspection.
\item $\bar O$ is the displayed complement-open row attached to the same candidate. A row is admitted as clopen only when both $O$ and $\bar O$ are present over the same $T$.
\item $B$ is the $\BooleanAlgebraUp$ operation row. It records finite union, intersection, complement, zero, one, and order reads on the admitted clopen rows.
\item $L$ is the $\StoneDualityUp$ clopen ledger row. It records the same clopen observations as the Stone-duality surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stoneduality-namecert} without adding ultrafilter points or a completed Stone space.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the topology row, open row, complement-open row, Boolean operation row, Stone ledger row, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the route
$$
T,O,\bar O \longmapsto B,L .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClopenAlgebraUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\BooleanAlgebraUp$, $\StoneDualityUp$, $\ConnectedSpaceUp$, $\TotallyDisconnectedSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClopenAlgebraUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,O,\bar O,B,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary subsets, a selected clopen basis, ultrafilter choice, quotient topology equality, or host powerset Boolean operations.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Clopen algebra NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:clopen-algebra-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:clopen-algebra-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\ClopenAlgebraUp}$ surface consists exactly of topology-source exposure through $T$, displayed open-row exposure through $O$, displayed complement-open exposure through $\bar O$, Boolean operation exposure through $B$, Stone-duality clopen-ledger exposure through $L$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Every connectedness or total-disconnectedness consumer reads the clopen algebra only through those rows and receives no excluded-middle split, selected clopen basis, host subset equality, arbitrary powerset algebra, or ultrafilter representation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:clopen-algebra-carrier}. The packet displays exactly the topology source, open row, complement-open row, Boolean operation row, Stone clopen ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row.

The clopen condition is local to $T,O,\bar O$. A consumer may call a row clopen only after both the open row and its complement-open row have been displayed over the same topology classifier.

The Boolean operations are then read through $B$. Union, intersection, complement, zero, one, and order requests are operations on the displayed clopen rows, not on an ambient powerset.

Finally $L,H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same route. The Stone-duality ledger records the clopen observations already visible in the packet, while transport and replay cannot create a selected basis, ultrafilter point, host subset equality, or external Boolean algebra.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Clopen algebra Boolean-topology handoff]
\label{thm:clopen-algebra-boolean-topology-handoff}
Every Boolean-topology consumer of an accepted $\ClopenAlgebraUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
\TopologyUp
\longrightarrow
O,\bar O
\longrightarrow
\BooleanAlgebraUp
\longrightarrow
\StoneDualityUp
\longrightarrow
H,C,P,N .
$$
The handoff gives connectedness consumers the displayed clopen-refusal boundary used by $\ConnectedSpaceUp$ and gives total-disconnectedness consumers the displayed clopen-separation ledger used by $\TotallyDisconnectedSpaceUp$. It gives neither open-cover compactness, nor host subset extensionality, nor an ultrafilter representation, nor a selected global clopen basis.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:clopen-algebra-namecert-obligations}. They require every clopen read to expose the topology source together with both the open row and the complement-open row before the Boolean operation row is consumed.

The Boolean handoff is finite. The row $B$ supplies only the displayed operations on admitted clopen rows, and $L$ identifies those rows with the Stone-duality clopen ledger rather than with a completed point space.

Connectedness and total-disconnectedness consume different faces of the same packet. The connectedness side reads clopen attempts as refusal rows, while the total-disconnectedness side reads clopen separations as displayed separation rows.

Since $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the displayed rows, every Boolean-topology consumer remains inside the route shown above. The excluded powerset, basis-selection, ultrafilter, and host-extensionality objects are absent from the carrier.
\end{proof}
