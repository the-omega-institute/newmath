\chapter{The Finite-Window Fibonacci--RH Program}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-finite-window-fib-rh-program-hub}
\origin{ai}

The Riemann Hypothesis has several equivalent finite-window formulations: hyperbolicity of the Jensen polynomials of $\xi$, positivity of Li's coefficients, positivity of Weil's explicit-formula quadratic form, and cancellation bounds such as $M(x)=O(x^{1/2+\varepsilon})$ for the Mertens function. This chapter is the organizing summary of a finite, axiom-free program that places Fibonacci and Zeckendorf structure inside these finite-window criteria. It proves no new theorem; it records which finite certificates were built, how each touches a genuine RH criterion, and where the exact-arithmetic frontier honestly ends.

\section{The Certificates}
\label{sec:fib-rh-program-certificates}

Each certificate below is a finite, $0$-axiom object verified in the Lean development. None is a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis; each is a finite shadow of, or a finite carrier for, a genuine RH-equivalent criterion.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\textbf{Certificate} & \textbf{Finite content} & \textbf{Genuine RH criterion it touches}\\
\hline
Fibonacci--P\'olya Jensen kernel & $\operatorname{disc}F_{m,2,n}=4[(\gamma_{n+1}^2-\gamma_n\gamma_{n+2})+(3m-2)\gamma_n^2]$ & the actual $\xi$ Jensen/P\'olya hyperbolicity criterion\\
Jensen kernel deformation & $\operatorname{disc}F^{(\lambda)}_{m,2,n}$, $\lambda=0$ the exact $\xi$ Jensen window & Jensen stability margin under a Fibonacci P\'olya--Schur kernel\\
Zeckendorf--M\"obius sieve & $\bigl(\sum_{N<F_{m+2}}\mu(N)\bigr)^2\le F_{m+2}$ & the Mertens bound $M(x)=O(x^{1/2})$ (RH-equivalent)\\
Fibonacci-cube Ihara zeta & Bass $\det(I-Au+(D-I)u^2)$, $\Gamma_3=(u-1)^2(u+1)^2(u^2+1)^2$ & the graph-theoretic RH / Ramanujan analogy\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The first two are the hyperbolicity flavor: the Fibonacci-cube weight polynomial $W_m$ is real-rooted, so $W_m(D)$ is a finite P\'olya--Schur hyperbolicity preserver, and the Griffin--Ono--Rolen--Zagier theorem gives unconditional large-$n$ hyperbolicity of the smoothed windows. The third is the arithmetic flavor: it places M\"obius and squarefree arithmetic inside Fibonacci windows and records a finite Mertens cancellation certificate. The fourth is the graph-zeta flavor: it computes the Ihara zeta of the Fibonacci-cube graphs through the Bass determinant and marks the first non-tree case.

\begin{theorem}[Program summary]
\label{thm:fib-rh-program-summary}
The four finite certificates above are verified in Lean as axiom-free, propext-free objects. Together they realize the hyperbolicity, arithmetic, and graph-zeta flavors of the finite-window Fibonacci--RH program on exact integer and polynomial data.
\end{theorem}

\section{The Program Boundary}
\label{sec:fib-rh-program-boundary}

This program is complete as a finite, exact-arithmetic certificate program. The three flavors above exhaust the finite-window RH criteria that reduce to exact integer, polynomial, or $\mathbb Z[\varphi]$ arithmetic: Jensen hyperbolicity through symbolic $\xi$ coefficients, Mertens cancellation through exact M\"obius sums, and the graph zeta through an exact Bass determinant.

The one genuinely distinct criterion not reduced here is Weil's explicit-formula positivity: the finite Galerkin quadratic form of the Weil functional on Fibonacci-windowed test functions. That object is real and central, but its matrix entries are transcendental, involving the Euler--Mascheroni constant, $\log 4\pi$, archimedean integrals, and von Mangoldt evaluations at logarithms of integers. It is therefore not an exact-arithmetic $0$-axiom object; a certificate would require certified interval arithmetic together with a rational positive-semidefinite (Cholesky) certificate, a different and substantially harder formalization. It is recorded here as the honest open direction, not as a completed certificate.
