\begin{definition}[Mollifier carrier]
\label{def:mollifier-carrier}
A $\MollifierUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(S,R,N,P,C,H,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the smooth-kernel row, naming the Schwartz-test or smooth compact-support profile together with the visible derivative-order windows consumed by regularization.
\item $R$ is the support-radius row, recording the dyadic radius, local support inclusion, and finite locality budget.
\item $N$ is the normalization row, recording the unit-mass ledger as displayed $\RealUp$ and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ comparisons rather than as a host integral.
\item $P$ is the convolution-window row, recording the finite source window, target window, translation request, and scale request read by a Sobolev, weak-derivative, distribution-derivative, or continuous-map consumer.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a regularization request through the kernel, support, normalization, convolution-window, real, dyadic, and local naming rows.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,R,N,P,C,L$ and the sibling rows named by the request.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MollifierUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $S,R,N,P,C,H,L$. It has no coordinate for host integration, a selected approximate identity, quotient equality of functions or distributions, completed test-function topology, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}
