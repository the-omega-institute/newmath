It sits on the L10 Real and Completion spine: bounded source evidence is read through the finite window interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, dyadic tail budgets through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, regular rational readback through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, displayed extraction through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-subsequence-extractor-namecert}, and the terminal seal through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[BoundedRegularCauchyCluster carrier]
\label{def:bounded-regular-cauchy-cluster-carrier}
A $\BoundedRegularCauchyClusterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(S,W,D,R,E,X,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the bounded source row, recording the displayed lower and upper rational bounds for the inspected regular-Cauchy source.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which the source is observed.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tail-budget row used to compare requested tolerances with the displayed windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to $W$ and $D$.
\item $E$ is the $\CauchySubsequenceExtractorUp$ row, exposing the finite extraction schedule consumed by cluster reads.
\item $X$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback and the displayed extraction row have been read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source bounds, window row, tail-budget row, regular-rational readback, extraction row, and real seal.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay routes by which Real-facing and completion-facing consumers read the packet.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BoundedRegularCauchyClusterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\CauchySubsequenceExtractorUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedRegularCauchyClusterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports no countable-choice selector, no quotient equality of Cauchy names, no host real equality, and no ambient completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BoundedRegularCauchyCluster NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bounded-regular-cauchy-cluster-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BoundedRegularCauchyClusterUp$ carrier $B=(S,W,D,R,E,X,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedRegularCauchyClusterUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the bounded source row, $\StreamNameUp$ window row, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tail-budget row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, $\CauchySubsequenceExtractorUp$ row, terminal $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming row. The classifier is stable only through $H$, and the ledger admits no selected subsequence outside $E$, no quotient real equality, no countable choice, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project an accepted read through \autoref{def:bounded-regular-cauchy-cluster-carrier}. The bounded source, finite window, dyadic tail budget, regular-rational readback, extraction row, terminal real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows are all visible coordinates of the same packet.

Classifier stability is componentwise because the only transport coordinate is $H$. The continuation row $C$ can replay the displayed source, readback, extraction, and Real-facing routes, but it has no coordinate for a selector or quotient object.

Thus the local certificate is exhausted by the displayed finite carrier. The excluded rows cannot be recovered from the packet because no coordinate stores them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BoundedRegularCauchyCluster RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:bounded-regular-cauchy-cluster-regseq-handoff}
Every cluster read accepted by a $\BoundedRegularCauchyClusterUp$ packet reaches the terminal $\RealUp$ seal only through the displayed $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$ and the $\CauchySubsequenceExtractorUp$ row $E$. In particular, a Real-facing consumer must first read the bounded source row $S$, the $\StreamNameUp$ window row $W$, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tail-budget row $D$, and the regular-rational readback row $R$ before it may consume the extraction row $E$ and the seal $X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier coordinates in order. The real seal $X$ is stored as the terminal row of the same packet that contains $S,W,D,R,E$, and the displayed replay row $C$ exposes no route to $X$ that bypasses the regular-rational readback.

The extraction row $E$ is not an independent choice functional. It is a finite row of the carrier, transported by $H$ and replayed by $C$ after the window and tail-budget rows have already fixed the RegSeqRat handoff.

Therefore every Real-facing cluster read factors through the displayed $S,W,D,R,E,X$ route. The packet contains no quotient equality of streams, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness row that could supply another route.
\end{proof}
