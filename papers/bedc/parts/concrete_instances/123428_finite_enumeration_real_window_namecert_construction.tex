Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\FiniteEnumerationRealWindowUp$ packet records a finite enumeration of inspected real-name windows before any consumer reads a terminal real-facing seal. It sits between the finite stream-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the $\RealUp$ naming boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the Archimedean real-window packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-archimedean-window-namecert}, and the Bishop located dyadic interval route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-located-dyadic-interval-namecert}. \label{def:finite-enumeration-real-window-carrier}

$$

\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row attached to the enumerated real-name observations;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, recording the dyadic budgets used for each enumerated window;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, exposing the rational observations read from the finite windows;

\begin{theorem}[FiniteEnumerationRealWindow NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-enumeration-real-window-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-enumeration-real-window-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteEnumerationRealWindowUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $K=(E,S,D,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, enumeration exposure through $E$, stream-window exposure through $S$, dyadic-tolerance exactness through $D$, regular-rational readback through $Q$, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $R$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Every accepted real-window read must factor through the finite route
$$
E \longmapsto S \longmapsto D \longmapsto Q \longmapsto R
$$
before a consumer may treat it as a Real-facing observation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:finite-enumeration-real-window-carrier}. The displayed tuple gives exactly the enumeration, stream-window, dyadic-tolerance, regular-rational readback, real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming coordinates.

The route first inspects the finite row $E$, then reads only the corresponding $\StreamNameUp$ windows $S$. The dyadic budgets $D$ control the tolerance of those windows, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $Q$ exposes the rational observations allowed by the budgets.

The terminal row $R$ is therefore reached only after the finite enumeration and its readback data have been displayed. Since no row of $K$ stores quotient real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FiniteEnumerationRealWindow readback]
\label{thm:finite-enumeration-real-window-readback}
Every $\RealUp$ consumer reading an accepted $\FiniteEnumerationRealWindowUp$ packet receives the regular rational readback only through the displayed route
$$
\StreamNameUp,\quad \DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad \RegSeqRatUp,\quad \RealUp .
$$
Concretely, the finite enumeration row $E$ selects the inspected windows, $S$ displays their stream-name observations, $D$ supplies the dyadic tolerance ledger, $Q$ supplies the regular-rational readback, and $R$ seals the observation for Real-facing use. The route is compatible with the Archimedean window discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-archimedean-window-namecert} and the located dyadic interval boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-located-dyadic-interval-namecert}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the enumeration row $E$. It is finite, so a consumer can inspect each displayed request without invoking a selector for a countable family of real names.

For each listed request, the stream row $S$ supplies the actual finite window and the dyadic row $D$ supplies the requested tolerance. The regular rational row $Q$ is read only after those two rows are present, so the readback cannot bypass the displayed window or its budget.

Finally the $\RealUp$ seal row $R$ records the Real-facing endpoint of the same finite route. The cited Archimedean and located-interval chapters supply the surrounding finite-window discipline, but no row here adds a quotient equality theorem, representative choice, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{proof}
