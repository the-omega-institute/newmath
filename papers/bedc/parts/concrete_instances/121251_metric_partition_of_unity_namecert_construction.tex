It sits after the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the locally finite cover surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locally-finite-cover-namecert}, the paracompact refinement ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-paracompact-namecert}, and the partition surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-partition-of-unity-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Metric partition of unity carrier]
\label{def:metric-partition-of-unity-carrier}
A $\MetricPartitionOfUnityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(M,T,L,Q,S,W,Z,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row, exposing the displayed distance, ball, and metric-cover requests whose subordinate weights are being named.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ handoff row induced by the metric balls, recording the open-set interpretation used by every cover and support read.
\item $L$ is the $\LocallyFiniteCoverUp$ row, recording the locally finite cover cells, finite neighbourhood witnesses, and assignment back to the metric-cover request.
\item $Q$ is the $\ParacompactUp$ refinement ledger that keeps the locally finite cover route visible without selecting an arbitrary host subcover.
\item $S$ is the subordinate support row, listing for each displayed cover cell the support window in which the corresponding weight row may be nonzero.
\item $W$ is the finite weight row, listing the finitely many nonzero weights visible at each displayed metric neighbourhood read.
\item $Z$ is the normalization row, recording that the displayed finite weights sum to the unit row on the covered metric region.
\item $H$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,T,L,Q,S,W,Z,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ is displayed $\Cont$ replay from the metric-cover request through the locally finite cover, support, weight, and normalization rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetricPartitionOfUnityUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\LocallyFiniteCoverUp$, $\ParacompactUp$, $\PartitionOfUnityUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetricPartitionOfUnityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,T,L,Q,S,W,Z,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host-selected function family, arbitrary subcover selection, open-cover compactness, unlogged support choice, or extensional equality of real-valued functions.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Metric partition of unity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metric-partition-of-unity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetricPartitionOfUnityUp$ carrier
$$
U=(M,T,L,Q,S,W,Z,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MetricPartitionOfUnityUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the metric source row, topology handoff, locally finite cover row, paracompact refinement ledger, subordinate support rows, finite weight rows, normalization row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every partition consumer must read the finite route
$$
M \longmapsto T \longmapsto L,Q \longmapsto S,W,Z \longmapsto H,C,P,N
$$
before it treats subordinate weights as metric analytic data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metric-partition-of-unity-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the metric source, topology handoff, locally finite cover, paracompact refinement, support, weight, normalization, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The metric source row $M$ is read first, and $T$ records the open-set interpretation of the displayed metric balls. The locally finite cover row $L$ and paracompact refinement row $Q$ then supply the cover cells and finite neighbourhood witnesses without adding a host subcover selector.

The subordinate support, weight, and normalization rows are $S,W,Z$. They expose support containment, the finite list of nonzero weights visible at a displayed metric neighbourhood, and the corresponding sum-to-one row on the covered region.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary function selection, open-cover compactness, or extensional equality of functions, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Metric cover partition route]
\label{thm:metric-partition-of-unity-cover-route}

Thus the metric partition certificate is a finite cover route, not a host construction of real-valued functions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the dependency route in \autoref{thm:metric-partition-of-unity-namecert-obligations}. It places the metric source $M$ and topology handoff $T$ before the locally finite and refinement rows $L,Q$.

The rows $S,W,Z$ are downstream of that cover route. A support row is subordinate to a displayed cover cell, a weight row is visible only through the finite neighbourhood witnesses, and the normalization row sums only the displayed finite support.

The final rows $H,C,P,N$ authenticate the same route. Therefore a consumer reaches the partition surface only through the metric and topological cover ledger already named by the packet, with no hidden function family or subcover selection.
\end{proof}
