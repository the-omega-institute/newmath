\item $M$ is the $\HyperbolicMetricUp$ metric-read row restricted to the displayed half-plane window.

\begin{theorem}[Poincare half-plane NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:poincare-half-plane-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PoincareHalfPlaneUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PoincareHalfPlaneUp}$ surface consists exactly of half-plane coordinate exposure through $H$, boundary readback through $B$, hyperbolic metric exposure through $M$, optional disk comparison through $D$, visual-boundary readback through $V$, Mobius transport through $T$, Real-seal handoff through $E$, and structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $C,R,Q,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:poincare-half-plane-carrier}. The half-plane coordinate row $H$ and boundary row $B$ establish the displayed upper-half-plane window before any metric or transport read is inspected.

The metric and boundary rows are then read through $M,V,E$. The optional disk-comparison row $D$ remains a comparison row inside the same packet, not a proof that every disk model object has been globally identified with a half-plane object.

Mobius transport is supplied by $T$ and preserved by $C,R,Q,N$. Since the carrier has no further coordinate, the packet cannot export quotient boundary dynamics, complete geodesic selection, physical observer dynamics, analytic completeness, or bridge checking.
\end{proof}
