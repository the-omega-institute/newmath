It sits after the topological source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the finite cover nerve of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cover-nerve-namecert}, the simplicial complex interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-simplicialcomplex-namecert}, the homology rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homology-namecert}, and the categorical carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-category-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Finite cover incidence realizer carrier]
\label{def:finite-cover-incidence-realizer-carrier}
A $\FiniteCoverIncidenceRealizerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(T,K,M,O,F,S,A,G,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row for the covered object.
\item $K$ is the $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$ cover row, including the finite cover index list and cover-member exposure.
\item $M$ is the finite membership-incidence ledger pairing displayed source rows with displayed cover-index rows.
\item $O$ is the overlap-incidence ledger for nonempty finite intersections.
\item $F$ is the simplicial face ledger induced by $M$ and $O$.
\item $S$ is the $\SimplicialComplexUp$ handoff row that reads the finite face ledger as a simplicial carrier.
\item $A$ is the $\HomologyUp$ handoff row for finite chains generated from the accepted simplicial faces.
\item $G$ is the $\CategoryUp$ provenance row for functorial comparison of cover maps, face maps, and homology consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for topology, cover, membership, overlap, face, simplicial, homology, category, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a cover-incidence request through $T,K,M,O,F,S,A,G,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteCoverIncidenceRealizerUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$, $\SimplicialComplexUp$, $\HomologyUp$, $\CategoryUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverIncidenceRealizerUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,K,M,O,F,S,A,G,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host open covers, selected refinements, ambient simplicial sets, quotient chain complexes, or global homology representatives.
\end{definition}
