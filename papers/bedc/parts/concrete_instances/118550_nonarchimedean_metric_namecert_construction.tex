\begin{definition}[Non-Archimedean metric carrier]
\label{def:nonarchimedean-metric-carrier}

$$

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare distances by finite dyadic bounds.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ distance-seal row reached only after the metric and dyadic rows have been displayed.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row listing the point and distance observations read by a consumer.

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NonArchimedeanMetricUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

\begin{theorem}[Non-Archimedean metric NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:nonarchimedean-metric-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\NonArchimedeanMetricUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\NonArchimedeanMetricUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed rows; metric admission through the common $\MetricSpaceUp$ row $M$; dyadic tolerance admission through $D$; finite observation scheduling through $S$; Real-distance sealing through $R$; strong-triangle comparison through $V$; componentwise transport and replay through $H,C$; and provenance with local naming through $P,N$. No additional obligation stores a quotient metric construction, selected representatives, host real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:nonarchimedean-metric-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the metric row, dyadic tolerance ledger, Real-distance seal, stream-window schedule, strong-triangle ledger, and the structural rows.

Metric and dyadic admission are read before any strong-triangle comparison. A finite triple request first opens the observation window $S$, then reads the common metric row $M$ and dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ used to compare the three displayed distances.

The strong-triangle row is $V$. It records only the finite maximum comparison between the two displayed legs and the third distance, and it reaches the Real-distance seal $R$ only after those rows are public.

Finally inspect $H,C,P,N$. They transport, replay, source, and name the same displayed route, while the excluded quotient, representative, trichotomy, choice, host-equality, and completeness data have no projection from the packet.
