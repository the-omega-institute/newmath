\begin{theorem}[BrowderFixedPoint NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:browder-fixed-point-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\BrowderFixedPointUp$ carrier $B=(K,V,Q,F,A,M,R,W,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BrowderFixedPointUp}$ surface consists of exactly five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item compact-convex domain admission through $K,V,Q$;
\item continuous self-map admission through $F$;
\item finite affine-approximation and monotone residual admission through $A,M$;
\item Brouwer-Schauder reduction and fixed-window exposure through $R,W$;
\item structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation may call on a host fixed-point selector, unrestricted compactness, ambient weak convergence, quotient equality of approximation ledgers, or an uncarried nonlinear-analysis theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:browder-fixed-point-carrier}. The compact-convex domain data are exactly $K,V,Q$, so compact support, ambient compatibility, and convex membership enter as displayed packet rows.

The self-map route is then read only through $F$. The approximation and residual obligations consume $A$ and $M$, which expose finite affine faces, retractions, comparison maps, and monotone residual windows without exporting a selected limiting point.

The reduction row $R$ may cite the Brouwer and Schauder sibling surfaces, but only as finite handoffs attached to the displayed approximation and residual rows. The fixed-window row $W$ records the boundary that downstream consumers can inspect.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier contains no other coordinate, the listed five rows exhaust the local NameCert surface and exclude the host principles named in the statement.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BrowderFixedPoint compact-convex handoff]
\label{thm:browder-fixed-point-compact-convex-handoff}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

\end{proof}
