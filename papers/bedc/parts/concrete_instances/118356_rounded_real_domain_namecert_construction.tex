\begin{definition}[Rounded real domain carrier]
\label{def:rounded-real-domain-carrier}

$$

\item $I$ is the $\IntervalDomainUp$ row, displaying the current interval-domain cell to which the formal ball is attached.
\item $W$ is the $\IntervalDomainWayBelowUp$ row, recording the strict containment margin by which the rounded cell refines a coarser interval-domain observation.
\item $K$ is the $\LowerUpperRealCutUp$ row, keeping the rounded interval adjacent to the lower-upper cut boundary used by Real-facing consumers.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger, storing the dyadic center, radius, slack, endpoint, and comparison data used by $F,I,W,K$.

\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, reachable only after the formal ball, interval-domain, way-below, cut-boundary, dyadic, stream, and regular readback rows have been displayed.

\begin{theorem}[Rounded real domain NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:rounded-real-domain-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\RoundedRealDomainUp$ carrier
$$
D=(F,I,W,K,Q,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RoundedRealDomainUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, formal-ball rounding visibility, interval-domain attachment, way-below margin stability, lower-upper cut boundary visibility, dyadic exactness, finite-window scheduling, regular rational readback, terminal real-seal non-escape, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:rounded-real-domain-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the formal-ball row, interval-domain row, way-below row, lower-upper cut row, dyadic ledger, stream window, regular readback, real seal, and structural rows.

The rounded approximation rows are read in order. The formal-ball row $F$ supplies the radius request, $I$ attaches it to the interval-domain carrier, and $W$ records the strict way-below refinement that keeps the rounded cell inside the displayed approximation route.

The cut and readback rows then prevent escape. The lower-upper cut row $K$ keeps the approximant adjacent to the two-sided located boundary, while $Q,S,R$ provide the dyadic, finite-window, and regular rational reads consumed before the seal row $E$.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no quotient, host-supremum, trichotomy, choice, selected-representative, or completeness coordinate, the local certificate cannot export those principles.
\end{proof}
