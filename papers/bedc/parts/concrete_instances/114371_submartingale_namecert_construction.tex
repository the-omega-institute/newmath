\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SubmartingaleUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-submartingale-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Doob and Meyer, a submartingale is the inequality-direction sibling of a martingale: the conditional expectation of the next visible value is required to dominate the current value rather than equal it. The $\SubmartingaleUp$ packet records the finite NameCert surface for this one-sided adapted-sequence object. It sits between the finite martingale carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-namecert}, the bounded stopping windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-optionalstopping-namecert}, the crossing ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-upcrossing-namecert}, the Doob upcrossing inequality packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-doob-upcrossing-namecert}, and the convergence-preparation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-martingale-convergence-namecert}. The chapter names only the finite one-sided conditional-expectation carrier and its handoff to finite upcrossing rows; it does not assert almost-sure convergence, measure completion, or a quotient of stochastic processes.

\begin{definition}[Submartingale finite adapted carrier]
\label{def:submartingale-carrier}
A $\SubmartingaleUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(\Omega,R,C,F,X,E,I,T,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\Omega$ is the displayed $\ProbSpaceUp$ row naming the probability context.
\item $R$ is the consumed $\RandomVarUp$ source packet for the finite endpoint rows.
\item $C$ is the consumed $\CondExpUp$ packet supplying conditional-expectation endpoints.
\item $F$ is the finite filtration and adaptedness ledger over a unary time spine $0,\ldots,T$.
\item $X$ is the finite endpoint family $X_0,\ldots,X_T$ read through $\RandomVarUp$ rows and visible in the corresponding filtration rows.
\item $E$ is the finite conditional-expectation family $E_{n+1|n}$ whose source row is $X_{n+1}$ and whose conditioning visibility row is the $n$th filtration row.
\item $I$ is the one-sided comparison ledger recording
$$
X_n\leq_R E_{n+1|n}
$$
for every displayed successor $n<T$, with the order comparison read through the carried $\RealUp$ endpoint classifier.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $\Omega,R,C,F,X,E,I,T,K,P,N$.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for endpoint exposure, conditional-expectation exposure, filtration visibility, one-sided comparison, bounded stopping-window reads, and finite upcrossing handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance row naming exactly $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\RandomVarUp$, $\CondExpUp$, $\MartingaleUp$, $\OptionalStoppingUp$, $\UpcrossingUp$, $\DoobUpcrossingUp$, $\MartingaleConvergenceUp$, and the local submartingale rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SubmartingaleUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted carriers only through the displayed probability, random-variable, conditional-expectation, filtration, endpoint, one-sided comparison, transport, continuation, package, and local certificate rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Submartingale NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:submartingale-namecert-obligations}
The seed $\NameCert_{\SubmartingaleUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier exposure for $S=(\Omega,R,C,F,X,E,I,T,H,K,P,N)$, classifier transport of every displayed row of that carrier, endpoint exposure through $\RandomVarUp$, conditional-expectation exposure through $\CondExpUp$, filtration and adaptedness exposure through $F$, one-sided comparison exposure through $I$, bounded stopped-window routing through $\OptionalStoppingUp$, finite crossing-ledger handoff through $\UpcrossingUp$, finite Doob-bound handoff through $\DoobUpcrossingUp$, convergence-preparation handoff through $\MartingaleConvergenceUp$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $K$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier exposure is \autoref{def:submartingale-carrier}. It supplies the full finite row list and fixes the local classifier to those rows.

The analytic rows are $R,C,F,X,E,I$. The endpoint rows are read through $\RandomVarUp$, the conditional-expectation faces are read through $\CondExpUp$, and the inequality ledger $I$ records the one-sided comparison $X_n\leq_R E_{n+1|n}$ at each displayed successor.

The downstream rows are bounded and finite. Optional stopping can inspect only the displayed time window, the upcrossing ledger can count only lower-to-upper passages inside that window, and the Doob-upcrossing packet receives only the crossing count and expectation-bound rows.

The structural rows $H,K,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no row contains an almost-sure quotient, an unbounded stopping index, a limiting event, or a completed measure carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the seed NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
