\begin{definition}[Peter--Weyl carrier]
\label{def:peter-weyl-carrier}

$$
P=(G,M,R,H,F,A,D,T,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the $\TopGroupUp$ compact-group source row, exposing multiplication, inversion, unit, continuity, and compact-readback windows used by the coefficient route.
\item $M$ is the $\HaarMeasureUp$ row, recording the invariant integration surface and finite measure readbacks consumed by coefficient pairing.
\item $R$ is the $\RepresentationRingUp$ row, listing the finite representation endpoints, direct-sum rows, tensor rows, and Grothendieck ledger entries available to the packet.
\item $H$ is the $\HilbertUp$ row carrying the finite inner-product and norm windows over which coefficient functions are compared.
\item $F$ is the $\FourierUp$ finite-observation row for coefficient windows, transform samples, truncation bounds, and integration witnesses.
\item $A$ is the $\MatrixUp$ coefficient row, naming the inspected matrix entries of the displayed representation endpoints.
\item $D$ is the density ledger, recording the finite coefficient span, requested tolerance row, approximation witness, and error readback.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for group, measure, representation, Hilbert, Fourier, matrix, density, replay, provenance, and local-name rows.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $G,M,R,H,F,A,D,T,C,Q,N$. It has no coordinate for a host compact group, a selected complete irreducible basis, quotient function equality, completed $L^2$ space, ambient Haar integration theorem, or external density proof.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Peter--Weyl NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:peter-weyl-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PeterWeylUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PeterWeylUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the compact topological-group row, Haar-measure row, representation-ring row, Hilbert row, finite Fourier-observation row, matrix-coefficient row, density ledger, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:peter-weyl-carrier}. The analytic source rows are $G,M,R,H,F,A,D$: compact-group operations, invariant measure readback, finite representation endpoints, Hilbert comparison windows, Fourier finite observations, matrix entries, and the coefficient-density ledger.

Read the rows in dependency order. The representation row $R$ is interpreted over the group source $G$, the coefficient row $A$ is read through the displayed representation endpoints, and the Hilbert and Fourier rows $H,F$ provide only finite pairing and finite-observation windows.

The density ledger $D$ is downstream from those rows. It records a finite coefficient span, a tolerance request, an approximation witness, and an error readback, without adding an ambient basis or a completed $L^2$ object.

Finally the structural rows $T,C,Q,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same finite surface. Since no excluded coordinate occurs in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a density request. The carrier first exposes the compact-group row $G$ and the Haar-measure row $M$, so every coefficient comparison is attached to the displayed topological-group and invariant-measure surfaces.

Next read the representation and coefficient rows. The finite endpoints in $R$ determine the representation data under inspection, and the matrix row $A$ names only the selected coefficient entries of those endpoints.
