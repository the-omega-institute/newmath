\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The cancellation step is finite. The coefficient row $K$ is computed only from the displayed dyadic scale row $D$ and order row $O$, and the denominator guard $Q$ is read before any accelerated value in $B$ is exported.

The analytic handoff is downstream of that finite stencil. Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same visible route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since no carrier coordinate stores host asymptotic equality, quotient real equality, a selected rate theorem, countable-choice extraction, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
