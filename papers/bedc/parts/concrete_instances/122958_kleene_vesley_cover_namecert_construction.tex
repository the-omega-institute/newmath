\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\KleeneVesleyCoverUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-kleene-vesley-cover-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kleene and Vesley constructive analysis, the $\KleeneVesleyCoverUp$ packet records a finite spread-cover surface for choice-sequence neighborhoods before any bar-induction or completed-real consumer reads it. It sits after the choice-sequence carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-choice-sequence-namecert}, the spread-law rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-spread-law-namecert}, the Brouwer continuity boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-brouwer-continuity-principle-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only finite cover evidence over displayed prefixes; it does not assert full bar induction, countable choice, quotient sequence equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Kleene--Vesley cover carrier]
\label{def:kleene-vesley-cover-carrier}
A $\KleeneVesleyCoverUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(S,Q,B,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the finite $\SpreadLawUp$ row, recording the admitted spread-law nodes and immediate prefix extensions;
\item $Q$ is the $\ChoiceSequenceUp$ prefix row, recording the inspected finite choice-sequence prefixes over the spread;
\item $B$ is the bar and cover row, recording which displayed prefixes have cover evidence and which finite successors remain open;
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window family through which prefix observations are scheduled for downstream consumers;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for rational observations attached to the displayed windows;
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the spread-law, prefix, cover, window, and readback rows have been displayed;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,Q,B,W,R,E$ and the local naming row;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from spread-law rows through prefix rows, cover rows, stream windows, regular-rational readback, and the real-name seal;
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SpreadLawUp$, $\ChoiceSequenceUp$, $\BrouwerContinuityPrincipleUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\KleeneVesleyCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for a global bar-induction principle, countable-choice schedule, quotient equality of infinite sequences, selected branch, host open-cover semantics, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Kleene--Vesley cover NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:kleene-vesley-cover-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KleeneVesleyCoverUp$ carrier
$$
K=(S,Q,B,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\KleeneVesleyCoverUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, spread-law row exposure, choice-sequence prefix readback, bar and cover row exposure, finite $\StreamNameUp$ window scheduling, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal boundary, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The only data rows are the spread-law row $S$, choice-sequence prefix row $Q$, cover row $B$, window family $W$, readback row $R$, and seal row $E$.

Read the structural rows next. Transport is componentwise through $H$, replay is displayed through $C$, provenance is recorded by $P$, and the packet is named by $N$.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for global bar induction, countable choice, quotient sequence equality, selected branches, host cover semantics, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, the NameCert obligations are exhausted by the displayed finite rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kleene--Vesley cover choice-sequence scope]
\label{thm:kleene-vesley-cover-choice-sequence-scope}
Every accepted $\KleeneVesleyCoverUp$ consumer that reads a choice-sequence prefix must first expose the spread-law row $S$, then the prefix row $Q$, and then the cover row $B$. The consumer may continue through $W,R,E,H,C,P,N$, but it cannot replace the finite prefix surface by a selected infinite branch or by quotient equality of choice sequences.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from \autoref{def:kleene-vesley-cover-carrier}. The choice-sequence data appears only as the displayed prefix row $Q$, and that row is downstream of the spread-law row $S$ and upstream of the cover row $B$.

Use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:kleene-vesley-cover-namecert-obligations}. They bind prefix readback to the same finite packet and route all later observations through $W,R,E,H,C,P,N$.

The excluded objects are not coordinates of the carrier. Therefore a consumer can read finite prefixes and their cover evidence, but it cannot select an infinite branch or identify quotient sequence representatives.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kleene--Vesley cover real-completion boundary]
\label{thm:kleene-vesley-cover-real-completion-boundary}
Every $\RealUp$ or completion-facing read exported by an accepted $\KleeneVesleyCoverUp$ packet factors through
$$
S \longrightarrow Q \longrightarrow B \longrightarrow W \longrightarrow R \longrightarrow E
$$
with transport, replay, provenance, and local naming supplied by $H,C,P,N$. The real-facing boundary is a finite spread-cover route over displayed choice-sequence prefixes, not a proof of $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The only $\RealUp$ coordinate is the seal row $E$, and it appears after the spread-law, prefix, cover, stream-window, and regular-rational readback rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:kleene-vesley-cover-choice-sequence-scope}. Any prefix consumer is already forced through $S,Q,B$, and later observation windows are the displayed $W$ rows.

Finally use the NameCert obligations to pass through $R,E,H,C,P,N$. Because no full bar-induction, countable-choice, quotient-sequence, selected-limit, or ambient-completeness coordinate exists in the packet, the exported real-facing surface is only the finite route stated above.
\end{proof}
