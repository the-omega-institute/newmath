\begin{theorem}[Cauchy pasting regular-gluing density route]
\label{thm:cauchy-pasting-regular-gluing-density-route}
Every $\CauchyPastingUp$ consumer first reads the $\RegularCauchyGluingUp$ source row. It may then feed $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ only through the displayed $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows of \autoref{def:cauchy-pasting-carrier}; no density-net read may bypass the gluing source, finite window, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, or terminal real seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the carrier and NameCert obligations of \autoref{def:cauchy-pasting-carrier} and \autoref{thm:cauchy-pasting-namecert-obligations}. They put the $\RegularCauchyGluingUp$ source row before the $\StreamNameUp$ window, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, and the $\RealUp$ seal.

The finite density-net consumer receives only that displayed route. Since the pasting carrier has no coordinate for quotient streams, selected representatives, host equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness, feeding $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ cannot add any row outside the same finite route.
\end{proof}
