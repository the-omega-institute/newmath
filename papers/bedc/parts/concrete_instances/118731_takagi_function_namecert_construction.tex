\begin{theorem}[Takagi function NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:takagi-function-namecert-obligations}

$$
D,S \longmapsto A \longmapsto B \longmapsto U \longmapsto G \longmapsto R
$$

\end{theorem}

Transport through $H$, replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$ preserve the same route.
