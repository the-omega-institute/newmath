\begin{definition}[Cauchy subsequence selection modulus carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-subsequence-selection-modulus-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $T$ is the finite $\TotallyBoundedUp$ net ledger read from $K$, including visible positive radii, center histories, and coverage witnesses.
\item $A$ is the source $\StreamNameUp$ sequence row, recording the finite windows of the sequence inspected by the selection procedure.
\item $M$ is the subsequence selection modulus row. For each displayed dyadic tolerance it records a monotone index schedule, a retained finite-net cell, and the finite cofinality checks used to compare later selected terms.
\item $W$ is the selected-window row, listing the source windows retained by $M$ together with their dyadic comparison budgets.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row opened only after $K,T,A,M,W$ have been displayed.

\label{thm:cauchy-subsequence-selection-modulus-carrier-admission}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

No further coordinate is present. Thus carrier admission is exhausted by the compact source, finite nets, source stream, selection modulus, selected windows, regular readback, real seal, and the four structural rows.

\begin{theorem}[Selection modulus finite-net extraction]
\label{thm:cauchy-subsequence-selection-modulus-finite-net-extraction}
Every subsequence-selection read from an accepted $\CauchySubsequenceSelectionModulusUp$ carrier factors through the finite-net row $T$ and the monotone schedule row $M$. For each displayed tolerance, $M$ retains only a finite-net cell supplied by $T$, a finite list of inspected source windows from $A$, and the selected-window comparison budget recorded in $W$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with one displayed tolerance request. The finite-net data available to the packet are exactly the centers, coverage witnesses, and radius ledger in $T$.

The schedule row $M$ can therefore choose only among the finite cells exposed by $T$ and the source windows exposed by $A$. The retained windows and comparison budgets are then written in $W$.

Induct over the displayed tolerance list. The empty list asks for no selection, while the next request appends one retained finite cell and one finite source-window comparison. The induction never appeals to a global choice function.
\end{proof}
