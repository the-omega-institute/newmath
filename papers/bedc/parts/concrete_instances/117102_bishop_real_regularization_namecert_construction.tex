\begin{theorem}[Bishop real regularization NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-real-regularization-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopRealRegularizationUp$ carrier $B=(Q,T,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRealRegularizationUp}$ surface consists exactly of raw Cauchy source exposure, Bishop threshold exposure, finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance accounting, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No consumer receives quotient streams, selected host limits, countable-choice schedules, trichotomy decisions, host sequence equality, or ambient Real completeness from the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-real-regularization-carrier}. Its analytic rows are $Q,T,W,R,D,E$, so every accepted regularization read must expose the raw Cauchy source, threshold, finite windows, rational readback, tolerance ledger, and terminal seal.

The regularization obligations are read from $T,W,R,D$. The threshold row supplies the finite stage, the window row displays the source and output observations at that stage, the readback row packages those observations as a regular rational name, and the dyadic ledger records the tolerances used by the whole route.

The structural rows preserve the same route. Transport $H$ is componentwise, replay $C$ consumes the displayed rows in dependency order, and $P,N$ record provenance and local naming.

Since the carrier contains no quotient-stream, selected-limit, choice, trichotomy, host-equality, or completeness coordinate, none of those objects can be exported through the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop real regularization Real-seal handoff]
\label{thm:bishop-real-regularization-real-seal-handoff}
Every Real-facing consumer of an accepted $\BishopRealRegularizationUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
Q \longmapsto T \longmapsto W,D \longmapsto R \longmapsto E,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the consumer reads the raw Cauchy source, then the Bishop regularization threshold, then finite stream windows and dyadic tolerance data, then the regular rational readback, and only then the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The handoff exports no quotient stream, selected limit, countable-choice selector, trichotomy row, host sequence equality, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted carrier $B=(Q,T,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$. The source row $Q$ is the only raw Cauchy source coordinate, so a Real-facing read starts there.

Use the threshold and window rows next. The row $T$ supplies the finite Bishop regularization stage, while $W$ and $D$ record the finite observations and tolerances checked at that stage.

The readback row $R$ is downstream of the threshold and window data. It is the only regular rational name available before the terminal seal is inspected.

Finally read $E,H,C,P,N$. These rows seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. They do not add quotient, selection, choice, trichotomy, host-equality, or completeness data.
