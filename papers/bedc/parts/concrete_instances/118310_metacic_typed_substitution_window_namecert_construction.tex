\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC typed substitution window NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metacic-typed-substitution-window-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MetaCICTypedSubstitutionWindowUp$ carrier $T=(\Gamma,A,U,V,C,S,O,B,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MetaCICTypedSubstitutionWindowUp}$ surface consists exactly of typed-context admission through $\Gamma$; source and inserted-term typing through $A$ and $U$; closedness boundary exposure through $V$; closed type-checker sealing through $C$; shift, substitution, closed-composition, and generator-closure routing through $S$; obstruction retention through $O$; binder-window transport through $B$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; displayed replay through $R$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-typed-substitution-window-carrier}. The typed-window rows are $\Gamma,A,U,V,C,S,O,B$, and the structural certificate rows are $H,R,P,N$.

Read the context rows first. The finite context window $\Gamma$, typed source data $A$, inserted-term data $U$, and closedness row $V$ determine the boundary at which substitution is inspected.

Read the audit rows next. The closed type-checker seal $C$ scopes the window, while $S$ records the shift, substitution, closed-composition, and generator-closure route. The binder row $B$ is the only row that advances the typed window across binders.

Finally $O,H,R,P,N$ retain refused endpoints and structural certificate data. Since the carrier has no row for full subject reduction, definitional equality, evaluator equality, quotient syntax equality, or kernel consistency, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
