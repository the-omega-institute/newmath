Following Prohorov on tight probability families and weak compactness, the $\ProhorovUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface where a probability family may be read through displayed tightness ledgers, metric compactness witnesses, and weak-convergence test windows. It sits over the probability source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert}, the distribution readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-distribution-namecert}, the metric distance vocabulary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and the compact metric witness route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}. The packet names only the finite dependency route for tight probability data; it does not import ambient weak compactness, select subsequences, quotient measures, countable choice, or an external topology of measures.

\begin{definition}[Prohorov carrier]
\label{def:prohorov-carrier}
A $\ProhorovUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
P=(S,D,T,K,W,M,R,V,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\ProbSpaceUp$ probability source row, naming the total-mass and measurable-event surface from which the family is read.
\item $D$ is the $\DistributionUp$ readback row, recording the pushed-forward probability endpoints inspected by the packet.
\item $T$ is the tightness ledger, displaying the finite list of compact windows and tolerance requests that must be met before any weak-convergence read is public.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ witness row for the compact windows named by $T$.
\item $W$ is the weak-convergence test-window row, containing the finite family of bounded continuous tests and tolerance comparisons currently admitted.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ distance row used to compare test-window endpoints and compact-window neighborhoods.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ readback row for the displayed probabilities, tolerances, and test-window endpoint comparisons.
\item $V$ records transport of probability mass through the displayed distribution rows and compact windows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,D,T,K,W,M,R,V,C,Q,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Prohorov request through probability source, distribution readback, tightness ledger, compact metric witness, weak-convergence tests, metric comparison, Real readback, and transport.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ProhorovUp$, $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\DistributionUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ProhorovUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,D,T,K,W,M,R,V,H,C,Q,N$. It has no coordinate for selected subsequences, quotient probability measures, ambient weak compactness, countable choice, or a host topology on all measures.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Prohorov NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:prohorov-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ProhorovUp$ carrier
$$
P=(S,D,T,K,W,M,R,V,H,C,Q,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ProhorovUp}$ surface consists exactly of probability-source admission through $S$, distribution readback through $D$, tightness-ledger exposure through $T$, compact metric witness exposure through $K$, weak-convergence test-window admission through $W$, metric comparison through $M$, Real readback through $R$, transport through $V$ and $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $Q$, and local naming through $N$.

No Prohorov obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The probability source row $S$ and the distribution row $D$ are the only measure-facing data available to the packet, so every probability endpoint is read through the displayed source and pushforward surfaces.

The tightness ledger $T$ must be displayed before a compact-window witness can be consumed. The compact metric row $K$ supplies only the finite compact windows named by the ledger, not an ambient compactness theorem or a selected subsequence.

The weak-convergence test row $W$, metric row $M$, and Real readback row $R$ then record the finite comparisons that are public. The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows $V,H,C,Q,N$ preserve the same route, so the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Prohorov tightness metric handoff]
\label{thm:prohorov-tightness-metric-handoff}
Every consumer that reads a weak-convergence or compactness-facing conclusion from an accepted $\ProhorovUp$ carrier must first pass through the probability source row $S$, distribution readback row $D$, tightness ledger $T$, compact metric witness $K$, weak-convergence test window $W$, metric comparison row $M$, and Real readback row $R$. The handoff is the finite route
$$
S,D,T,K,W,M,R,V,H,C,Q,N
$$
and exports no selected subsequence, quotient measure equality, countable-choice schedule, ambient weak compactness principle, or host topology of probability measures.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:prohorov-namecert-obligations}. They place source admission, distribution readback, tightness exposure, compact metric witnessing, weak test windows, metric comparison, and Real readback inside one accepted packet.

Read the tightness row before the compact metric row. This forces every compactness-facing consumer to inspect the displayed compact windows and tolerance requests before using any compact metric witness.

Next read the weak-convergence test window and metric comparison rows. These rows expose only finite tests and metric endpoint comparisons; they do not choose a subsequence or introduce an external topology of measures.

Finally replay $V,H,C,Q,N$. Transport, componentwise equality, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite handoff, so the consumer cannot replace the route by quotient measure equality, countable choice, or ambient weak compactness.
\end{proof}
