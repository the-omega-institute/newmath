\begin{theorem}[Channel/genetic-code bridge MetaCIC forward route]
\label{thm:channel-genetic-code-bridge-metacic-forward-route}
Every MetaCIC-facing read of an accepted $\ChannelGeneticCodeBridgeUp$ packet passes through the displayed codon-stop, endpoint, bioreality, and MetaCIC rows before the read may cite \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-namecert}. Concretely, the route first exposes the finite endpoint dependency of \autoref{lem:channel-genetic-code-bridge-finite-endpoint-dependency-route}, then the codon-window stop sibling route of \autoref{thm:channel-genetic-code-bridge-codon-window-sibling-route}, then the MetaCIC forward link of \autoref{thm:channel-genetic-code-metacic-forward-link}, and finally the bioreality forward lattice route of \autoref{lem:channel-genetic-code-bridge-bioreality-forward-lattice-route}. The exported read is only a routed frontier annotation over $D,B,P,A,T,S,L,H,C,R,N$; it exports no channel-token identity, biological theorem, confluence theorem, normalization theorem, subject-reduction theorem, or MetaCIC proof object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier of \autoref{def:channel-genetic-code-bridge-carrier}. The decoded channel row $D$, endpoint row $B$, product row $P$, codon atlas row $T$, classifier row $S$, and stop-role ledger $L$ are visible before any biological or MetaCIC-facing read is available.

Apply the finite endpoint dependency route and the codon-window sibling route. These force the endpoint and product rows to precede the codon-stop comparison, so a stop-role read remains tied to the displayed channel termination budget and codon-window stop-face classifier.

Next apply the MetaCIC forward link. It allows a MetaCIC consumer to cite the packet only as a frontier annotation after $D,B,P,L$ and the finite endpoint route have been displayed.

Finally use the bioreality forward lattice route. It composes the codon-stop and MetaCIC rows with the biological-reality route while preserving the same transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Since no carrier coordinate stores a biological theorem, channel-token identity, confluence, normalization, subject reduction, or proof object, the read remains a finite horizontal lattice edge into the MetaCIC candidate-normalization confluence handoff.
\end{proof}
