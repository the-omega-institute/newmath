\begin{lemma}[Channel/genetic-code finite endpoint dependency route]
\label{lem:channel-genetic-code-bridge-finite-endpoint-dependency-route}
Every accepted $\ChannelGeneticCodeBridgeUp$ read exposes the $\BoolUp$ endpoint row and the $\ProdUp$ phase row before any DNA four-phase atlas row or codon atlas row is consumed. The dependency route is the finite endpoint surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bool-namecert} followed by the product-phase surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-prod-namecert}; only after those two rows are visible may the bridge read DNA four-phase or codon atlas data. Termination-role reads also pass through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-channel-termination-budget-certificate-namecert} before the stop-role bridge compares channel termination with codon stop roles. The codon stop-face read also factors through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-codon-window-stop-face-classifier-namecert}, so the bridge compares channel termination with biological stop classes only after the finite codon-window classifier has exposed the stop face.
\end{lemma}
