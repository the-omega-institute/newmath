\begin{definition}[FiberFarEndDigestBoundary carrier]
\label{def:fiber-far-end-digest-boundary-carrier}
A $\FiberFarEndDigestBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(D,F,T,G,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the visible digest-value row;
\item $F$ is the source-fiber row behind $D$;
\item $T$ is the apophatic fiber far-end row, read as boundary role rather than digest value;
\item $G$ is the $\mathsf{GAP}$ provenance row keeping the source side and far-end boundary auditable;
\item $R$ is the refusal row blocking any accepted read from equating $D$ with $T$;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for digest, fiber, far-end, GAP, refusal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same digest, fiber, far-end, GAP, and refusal route;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiberFarEndDigestBoundaryUp$, $\DigestFiberLedgerUp$, $\ApophaticFixedPointUp$, $\HashApophaticSealUp$, $\HashDigestFiberBoundaryUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiberFarEndDigestBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a global universe digest, source equality from digest equality, selected preimage, internal $\mathsf{T}$ object, or quotient collapse of fiber membership.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FiberFarEndDigestBoundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:fiber-far-end-digest-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiberFarEndDigestBoundaryUp$ carrier
$$
B=(D,F,T,G,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiberFarEndDigestBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of visible digest exposure through $D$, source-fiber exposure through $F$, apophatic far-end boundary exposure through $T$, GAP provenance through $G$, digest/far-end equality refusal through $R$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:fiber-far-end-digest-boundary-carrier}. The visible row $D$ and the source-fiber row $F$ are distinct coordinates, and the far-end row $T$ is exposed only as an apophatic boundary role.

Read the refusal route through $G$ and $R$. The GAP provenance row keeps the fiber and far-end boundary attached to the digest, while the refusal row blocks the digest value from being read as the far-end identity.

The transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve the same boundary. Since no carrier coordinate stores source equality from digest equality, a selected preimage, an internal far-end object, a global digest, or quotient collapse, those readings are absent from the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Fiber far-end digest nonescape]
\label{thm:fiber-far-end-digest-boundary-nonescape}
Every accepted $\FiberFarEndDigestBoundaryUp$ consumer that reads the visible digest row $D$ must keep the source-fiber row $F$, apophatic far-end row $T$, GAP provenance row $G$, refusal row $R$, componentwise transport $H$, continuation replay $C$, package provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ in the same packet. Therefore digest equality cannot be used as fiber far-end identity, source equality, selected preimage, internal $\mathsf{T}$ object, global universe digest, or quotient fiber collapse.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:fiber-far-end-digest-boundary-namecert-obligations}. It makes $D,F,T,G,R,H,C,P,N$ the complete inventory for any accepted read.

The nonescape claim follows by inspecting that inventory. The digest row is only the visible value; the far-end row is only the apophatic boundary role; and the refusal row keeps those readings apart for every transported or replayed consumer.

No additional coordinate remains from which a consumer could construct source equality, a selected preimage, an internal far-end object, a global digest, or a quotient collapse. The packet therefore preserves the digest/fiber/far-end distinction of \autoref{prin:hash-like-fiber-fixedness}.
\end{proof}
