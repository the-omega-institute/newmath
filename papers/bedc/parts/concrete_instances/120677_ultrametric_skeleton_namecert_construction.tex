\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UltrametricSkeletonUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-ultrametric-skeleton-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following the metric embedding and ultrametric skeleton literature, the $\UltrametricSkeletonUp$ packet records a finite tree-scale approximation to displayed metric data as NameCert rows rather than as a host-selected infinite tree. It sits over the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the doubling control route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-doubling-metric-namecert}, the finite epsilon-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, and the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}. The packet gives compactness, finite-net, entropy, and embedding-facing consumers a named finite ultrametric approximation object; it does not import quotient metric equality, open-cover compactness, a selected infinite hierarchy, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Ultrametric skeleton carrier]
\label{def:ultrametric-skeleton-carrier}
An $\UltrametricSkeletonUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(M,E,T,D,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, exposing only the displayed distance observations consumed by the skeleton.
\item $E$ is the finite scale-net row, read through $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ at the displayed dyadic radii.
\item $T$ is the cluster-tree row, recording the finite parent, child, and leaf incidence data induced by the scale-net inclusions.
\item $D$ is the ultrametric distance ledger, assigning the displayed dyadic merge scale to each pair of retained leaves.
\item $B$ is the distortion budget row, comparing source metric observations from $M$ with ultrametric ledger entries from $D$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, finite scale net, cluster tree, distance ledger, distortion budget, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a metric read through the scale-net row, cluster-tree row, ultrametric distance ledger, and distortion budget.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UltrametricSkeletonUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\DoublingMetricUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UltrametricSkeletonUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $M,E,T,D,B,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host quotient of metric spaces, a selected infinite cluster tree, open-cover compactness, a choice principle, or ambient metric completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Ultrametric skeleton NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:ultrametric-skeleton-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UltrametricSkeletonUp$ carrier $U=(M,E,T,D,B,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UltrametricSkeletonUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric-source exposure, finite scale-net exposure, cluster-tree incidence exposure, ultrametric distance ledger exposure, distortion-budget exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every embedding-facing or compactness-facing consumer must read the finite route
$$
\MetricUp,\quad
\DoublingMetricUp,\quad
\FiniteEpsilonNetUp,\quad
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad
\BHist,\quad
\hsame,\quad
\Cont,\quad
\Pkg,\quad
\NameCert .
$$
No NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:ultrametric-skeleton-carrier}. The only analytic rows are the metric source $M$, finite scale-net row $E$, cluster-tree incidence row $T$, ultrametric distance ledger $D$, and distortion budget $B$.

Read the finite approximation rows in order. The metric source supplies displayed distances, the finite scale-net row supplies retained representatives at dyadic radii, the cluster-tree row supplies only finite incidence data, and the ultrametric ledger assigns the merge scale recorded by that finite tree.

The distortion obligation is then exactly the row $B$. Componentwise transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same finite rows, while provenance $P$ and local naming $N$ cite the packet surface.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for host choice, quotient metric equality, a selected infinite tree, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness, none of those principles can be exported by the local $\NameCert_{\UltrametricSkeletonUp}$ obligations.
\end{proof}
