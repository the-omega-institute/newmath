\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-archimedeanapproximation-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, \emph{Constructive Analysis}, the $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$ packet records the finite Archimedean approximation boundary used before a rational or dyadic scale computation may be sealed as a real-name observation. It sits over the natural-number bound rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nat-namecert}, the rational source rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite name windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readbacks of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names approximation as displayed BEDC route data, not as quotient real equality, a selected host limit, countable choice, trichotomy, or a completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[ArchimedeanApproximation carrier]
\label{def:archimedean-approximation-carrier}
An $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(N,Q,D,\epsilon,S,R,E,H,C,P,M)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $N$ is the $\NatUp$ bounding row that records the finite index or scale bound used by the approximation request.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ rational source row naming the displayed lower and upper rational bounds read at that scale.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row that refines the rational bounds into a dyadic tolerance ledger.
\item $\epsilon$ is the displayed tolerance request, read only through $D$ and the $\NatUp$ bound row $N$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row opened at the bound and tolerance named by $N,D,\epsilon$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row extracted from the same finite window $S$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row that exposes the approximation to a Real-facing consumer after the finite window and readback have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the natural bound, rational source, dyadic tolerance, stream window, regular readback, real seal, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay that opens the finite window, reads the rational and dyadic rows, extracts the regular rational readback, and hands it to the $\RealUp$ seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NatUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $M$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ArchimedeanApproximationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for quotient real equality, selected representatives, host limits, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ArchimedeanApproximation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:archimedean-approximation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$ carrier
$A=(N,Q,D,\epsilon,S,R,E,H,C,P,M)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\ArchimedeanApproximationUp}$ surface consists exactly of the
following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed $\NatUp$ bound, $\RatUp$ rational source, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance, $\StreamNameUp$ window, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal, transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item bound compatibility, stating that the requested finite approximation scale is read from the same $N$ row that controls the tolerance request $\epsilon$;
\item rational-bound exposure, stating that the lower and upper rational rows exported to the consumer are the displayed $\RatUp$ data in $Q$;
\item dyadic tolerance exactness, stating that every tolerance comparison used by the packet is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger named by $D$;
\item window and readback compatibility, stating that $S$ and $R$ are the $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows opened at the bound and tolerance already displayed by $N,D,\epsilon$;
\item real-seal handoff, stating that the only Real-facing route is the row $E$ after the displayed finite window and regular rational readback;
\item non-escape, stating that quotient real equality, selected host limits, countable choice, trichotomy, and ambient completeness are absent from the carrier.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.ArchimedeanApproximationUp.ArchimedeanApproximationCarrier\_namecert\_obligations}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:archimedean-approximation-carrier}. The
only available coordinates are the natural bound, rational source, dyadic
tolerance, finite stream window, regular rational readback, real seal, and the
structural support rows.

Bound compatibility and rational-bound exposure are immediate from the rows
$N,Q,\epsilon$. The tolerance request is attached to the displayed natural
bound, and the lower and upper rational readings are the $\RatUp$ data named
by $Q$ rather than host rational choices outside the packet.

Dyadic tolerance exactness is the role of $D$. The finite window $S$ can be
opened only at the bound and tolerance already named by $N,D,\epsilon$, and
the readback $R$ is the corresponding $\RegSeqRatUp$ extraction from that same
window.

The Real-facing consumer then sees only the seal row $E$ transported and
replayed by $H$ and $C$. The provenance $P$ and local naming row $M$ name the
same route and add no further coordinate, so the listed obligation rows
exhaust the local $\NameCert_{\ArchimedeanApproximationUp}$ surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ArchimedeanApproximation real-seal handoff]
\label{thm:archimedean-approximation-real-seal-handoff}
Every Real-facing read exported by an accepted $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$
carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\NatUp,\ \RatUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp .
$$
Concretely, a consumer first reads the natural bound and rational source rows,
then the dyadic tolerance row, then the finite stream window and regular
rational readback, and only then the $\RealUp$ seal. The handoff exports no
quotient real equality, selected host limit, countable-choice schedule,
trichotomy, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted carrier and project its Real-facing read. The row $E$
is the only $\RealUp$ seal coordinate, so the read cannot begin from a host
real or a quotient class.

Before $E$ can be consumed, the continuation row $C$ displays the bound,
rational source, tolerance, window, and readback rows. Thus the route first
passes through $N$ and $Q$, then through $D$, then through $S$ and $R$.

The transport row $H$ preserves the same components, while $P$ and $M$ record
only provenance and local naming. Since the carrier has no additional
coordinate, every Real-facing approximation read factors through the stated
finite route and cannot obtain any of the excluded principles.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ArchimedeanApproximation window obligations]
\label{thm:archimedean-approximation-window-obligations}
For every accepted $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$ carrier
$$
A=(N,Q,D,\epsilon,S,R,E,H,C,P,M),
$$
the $\StreamNameUp$ window row $S$ opens only after the natural bound row $N$, dyadic tolerance row $D$, and tolerance request $\epsilon$ have been displayed. A consumer that reads $S$ before those rows is not reading the accepted packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:archimedean-approximation-carrier}. The definition places $S$ as the finite-window row opened at the bound and tolerance named by $N,D,\epsilon$.

Use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-namecert-obligations}. Their bound-compatibility and window-readback compatibility clauses require the same $N,D,\epsilon$ rows before $S$ can be consumed.

The handoff theorem \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-real-seal-handoff} then keeps every Real-facing read downstream of the same finite window. Thus a packet that opens $S$ without first displaying $N,D,\epsilon$ leaves the accepted ArchimedeanApproximation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ArchimedeanApproximation dyadic cofinal window]
\label{thm:archimedean-approximation-dyadic-cofinal-window}
For every accepted $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$ carrier
$$
A=(N,Q,D,\epsilon,S,R,E,H,C,P,M),
$$
any later unary bound refining $N$ and any smaller displayed dyadic tolerance refining $D$ open a $\StreamNameUp$ window whose $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback remains on the same $\RealUp$ seal route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the carrier definition and the NameCert obligations. The rows $N,D,\epsilon$ control the window row $S$, and the obligation surface keeps the bound-compatibility, dyadic tolerance exactness, and window-readback compatibility rows in the same finite packet.

Apply \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-window-obligations}. A later unary bound and a smaller dyadic tolerance refine the displayed gate for opening a StreamName window; they do not create a separate host approximation source.

The readback then uses the same $R$ row named by \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-dyadic-readback}, and the real-seal handoff of \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-real-seal-handoff} keeps the resulting read downstream of $E$. Thus the cofinal window remains a refinement of the accepted $\NatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ArchimedeanApproximation dyadic readback]
\label{thm:archimedean-approximation-dyadic-readback}
For every accepted $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$ carrier $A=(N,Q,D,\epsilon,S,R,E,H,C,P,M)$, rational bounds from the $\RatUp$ row $Q$ become Real-facing only through the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$ and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$. No consumer may expose the rational bounds directly as a $\RealUp$ observation.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.ArchimedeanApproximationUp.ArchimedeanApproximationCarrier\_dyadic\_readback}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier definition. The rational source row $Q$ is a displayed source of lower and upper rational bounds, while $D$ records dyadic tolerance exactness and $R$ records the regular rational readback from the finite window.

Apply the NameCert obligations. Rational-bound exposure, dyadic tolerance exactness, and window-readback compatibility are separate rows of the same certificate, so the rational data in $Q$ must pass through $D$ and $R$ before the real seal row is available.

Finally use \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-real-seal-handoff}. The Real-facing route runs through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ before $\RealUp$, excluding any direct host read of rational bounds as real observations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ArchimedeanApproximation real seal nonescape]
\label{thm:archimedean-approximation-real-seal-nonescape}
For every accepted $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$ carrier $A=(N,Q,D,\epsilon,S,R,E,H,C,P,M)$, the $\RealUp$ seal row $E$ exports no quotient equality, selected host limit, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness. The seal is only the terminal boundary of the displayed bound, dyadic, window, and readback route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The carrier definition lists the only coordinates available to a consumer: the natural bound, rational source, dyadic tolerance, tolerance request, StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.

The NameCert obligations identify non-escape as part of the local surface, and \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-real-seal-handoff} fixes the route into $E$ through the displayed finite rows.

Since no coordinate stores quotient equality, a selected host limit, a choice schedule, trichotomy, or completeness data, the seal row cannot export any of those principles.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic upper ladder for Archimedean approximation]
\label{thm:archimedean-approximation-dyadic-upper-ladder}
Every accepted $\ArchimedeanApproximationUp$ packet over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RatUp$, $\NatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ exposes a finite unary exponent ladder whose dyadic radius eventually lies below any displayed positive $\RealUp$ tolerance row. The ladder is transported only through the displayed $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\Pkg$ rows of the same carrier, and it exports no trichotomy, no selected host limit, and no ambient choice of a real representative.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the carrier of \autoref{def:archimedean-approximation-carrier} and the obligation rows of \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-namecert-obligations}. The rows $N,D,\epsilon,S,R,E,H,C,P,M$ display the natural bound, dyadic tolerance ledger, positive tolerance request, finite window, regular readback, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming route.

Induct on the displayed unary ladder attached to the $\NatUp$ bound row. The base step reads the initial dyadic scale from $D$, and the successor step applies the dyadic scale monotonicity rows to replace the current radius by the next smaller dyadic radius while preserving the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger.

The positive tolerance readback is then consumed through the $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ route already fixed by \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-dyadic-cofinal-window} and \autoref{thm:archimedean-approximation-dyadic-readback}. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, and $\Pkg$ provenance keep the ladder inside the accepted packet, so no trichotomy, host limit selection, or external real representative is introduced.
\end{proof}
