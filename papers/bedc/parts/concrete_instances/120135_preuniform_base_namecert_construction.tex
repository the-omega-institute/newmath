\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\PreuniformBaseUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-preuniform-base-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki and Willard, the $\PreuniformBaseUp$ packet names the finite entourage-base data that can feed uniformity and completion consumers before a full uniformity object has been closed. It sits below the filter route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-entourage-filter-namecert}, the regular-Cauchy uniformity route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchyuniformity-namecert}, and the Real-specific entourage basis of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-uniform-entourage-base-namecert}. The packet is a displayed preuniform basis surface; it is not a quotient uniformity, not a selected completion, not host equality, and not an ambient Real completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Preuniform-base carrier]
\label{def:preuniform-base-carrier}
A $\PreuniformBaseUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(E,D,S,M,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the entourage-base row whose entries name only displayed binary-neighborhood requests.
\item $D$ is the diagonal witness row, recording that each admitted base entourage contains the displayed diagonal request.
\item $S$ is the symmetry witness row, sending each admitted base request to a displayed symmetric refinement.
\item $M$ is the finite-meet and refinement witness row, displaying a common smaller base request for each accepted finite pair.
\item $R$ is the consumer route to $\EntourageFilterUp$, $\RegularCauchyUniformityUp$, and $\RealUniformEntourageBaseUp$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the base row, diagonal row, symmetry row, finite-meet row, consumer route, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a downstream entourage request through $E,D,S,M,R,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PreuniformBaseUp$, $\EntourageFilterUp$, $\RegularCauchyUniformityUp$, $\RealUniformEntourageBaseUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PreuniformBaseUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $E,D,S,M,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a completed uniformity, quotient filter, selected limit, host equality, countable-choice selector, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Preuniform-base NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:preuniform-base-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PreuniformBaseUp$ carrier
$$
B=(E,D,S,M,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PreuniformBaseUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed entourage-base row $E$, diagonal exposure through $D$, symmetry exposure through $S$, finite-meet and refinement exposure through $M$, downstream consumer routing through $R$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, local naming through $N$, and non-escape for completed uniformities, quotient filters, selected limits, host equality, countable-choice selectors, and ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:preuniform-base-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly one entourage-base row, one diagonal row, one symmetry row, one finite-meet refinement row, one consumer route, and four structural rows.

The basis obligations are local to $E,D,S,M$. The diagonal row supplies the displayed diagonal containment, the symmetry row supplies a displayed symmetric refinement, and the meet row supplies a displayed common refinement for each finite pair before any downstream filter or uniformity consumer is opened.

The consumer route is downstream. The row $R$ permits $\EntourageFilterUp$, $\RegularCauchyUniformityUp$, and $\RealUniformEntourageBaseUp$ readers to consume the same basis data, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that data without adding a completed uniformity or a quotient filter.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for selected limits, host equality, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Preuniform-base downstream handoff]
\label{thm:preuniform-base-downstream-handoff}
Every $\EntourageFilterUp$, $\RegularCauchyUniformityUp$, or $\RealUniformEntourageBaseUp$ consumer of an accepted $\PreuniformBaseUp$ carrier must first read the displayed route
$$
E\longmapsto D,S,M\longmapsto R\longmapsto H,C,P,N.
$$
The handoff exports a preuniform basis only as finite entourage-base data, not as a completed uniformity, quotient filter, selected limit, host equality, countable-choice selector, or ambient Real completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:preuniform-base-namecert-obligations}. It places every accepted consumer read on the displayed base row $E$ and then on the diagonal, symmetry, and finite-meet rows $D,S,M$.

Only after those rows are visible may the route $R$ be read. The route records the three downstream consumers named in the statement, and each of them receives the same finite basis surface rather than a private completion object.

The remaining rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route under transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming. Because the carrier lacks completed-uniformity, quotient-filter, selected-limit, host-equality, choice, and Real-completeness coordinates, the downstream handoff cannot export any of those endpoints.
\end{proof}
