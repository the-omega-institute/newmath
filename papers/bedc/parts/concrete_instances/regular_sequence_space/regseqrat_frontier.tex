\begin{theorem}[RegularSequenceSpace RegSeqRat frontier]
\label{thm:regular-sequence-space-regseqrat-frontier}
Let $S=(X,T,Q,D,R,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RegularSequenceSpaceUp$ carrier. Any Real-completion consumer of $S$ reaches the terminal $\RealUp$ seal only through the displayed $\StreamNameUp$ schedule $T$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ regularity rows $Q$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance surface $D$, and $\RealUp$ seal $R$, with transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows $H,C,P,N$ still visible. The packet exports no quotient of sequences, choice-selected subsequence, hidden representative selector, or ambient Real completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-sequence-space-carrier}. The value-facing route exposes the schedule row $T$, the regular rational readback row $Q$, the dyadic tolerance row $D$, the terminal seal $R$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Use the sibling route of \autoref{thm:regular-sequence-space-sibling-route}. It forces completion-facing consumers to read the StreamName, RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, and Real rows in that order before any value-facing data are public.

Next use the Real and Completion dependency route of \autoref{thm:regular-sequence-space-real-completion-dependency-route}. It keeps the finite-tail and tail-filter rows upstream of the same $T,Q,D,R$ route and blocks any detour through quotient stream equality or selected limits.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$. Componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the displayed frontier and add no coordinate for sequence quotients, choice-selected subsequences, hidden representatives, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness. Hence the RegSeqRat frontier is the only route from the accepted packet to Real-completion consumers.
\end{proof}
