\begin{theorem}[RegularSequenceSpace product-window route]
\label{thm:regular-sequence-space-product-window-route}
Let $S=(X,T,Q,D,R,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RegularSequenceSpaceUp$ carrier. Every finite product, paired-tail, or product-completion consumer of $S$ must expose the two displayed $\StreamNameUp$ finite windows, the corresponding $\RegSeqRatUp$ readbacks, the shared $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance rows, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seals before any product completion read is public. The route exports only the common finite-window product surface of the accepted packet; it exports no quotient stream equality, selected representative pair, countable-choice selector, or ambient completeness coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-sequence-space-carrier}. A product-facing read has access only to the displayed schedule row $T$, regular rational readback $Q$, dyadic tolerance row $D$, Real seal $R$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Use the sibling and tail routes already attached to the hub: \autoref{thm:regular-sequence-space-sibling-route}, \autoref{thm:regular-sequence-space-tail-filter-handoff}, and \autoref{thm:regular-sequence-space-common-window-completion-route}. They force each coordinate of a paired-tail read to pass through a displayed StreamName window before the RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, and Real rows are consumed.

Use the shift-stability route of \autoref{thm:regular-sequence-space-shift-closure}. Finite shifts of either coordinate remain inside the same $T,Q,D,R,H,C,P,N$ route, so a product window is assembled from two finite windows rather than from a selected representative pair.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$. Transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the paired finite route and supply no coordinate for quotient stream equality, countable choice, selected representatives, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
