\label{def:martingale-convergence-finite-upcrossing-carrier}

\item $M$ is the $\MartingaleUp$ finite adapted-sequence source.

\begin{theorem}[Martingale convergence finite-upcrossing NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:martingale-convergence-finite-upcrossing-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted carrier
$$
F=(\Omega,X,M,U,D,S,V,R,T,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MartingaleConvergenceFiniteUpcrossingUp}$ surface
consists exactly of probability exposure, endpoint exposure, martingale-source
exposure, finite upcrossing-count exposure, Doob-bound exposure, bounded
stopping-window exposure, bounded-variation or tolerance-ledger exposure,
dyadic rational tolerance exposure, convergence-route admission, componentwise
$\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local
naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:martingale-convergence-finite-upcrossing-carrier}. The stochastic
source rows are $\Omega,X,M$, while the convergence-facing finite evidence is
$U,D,S,V,R,T$.

Read the upcrossing side first. The row $U$ supplies a finite completed-count
ledger, $D$ supplies the Doob bound for that ledger, and $S$ records the bounded
stopping windows that make the count inspectable.

The tolerance side is also finite. The rows $V$ and $R$ display the bounded
variation and dyadic tolerance evidence before the convergence-route row $T$
may be consumed.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly that
packet. Since no row stores an almost-sure quotient, selected exceptional set,
completed filtration, unbounded stopping family, selected limit, or measure
completion, the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite upcrossing route to martingale convergence]
\label{thm:martingale-convergence-finite-upcrossing-route}

$\DyadicRatCoreUp$ rational tolerance row, and the structural
$\hsame/\Cont/\mathsf{Pkg}/\NameCert$ rows. The route exports finite
convergence-preparation data only.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the martingale source. The rows $\Omega,X,M$ display the probability
context, endpoint family, and adapted finite window before any crossing count is
read.

Apply the upcrossing and Doob-upcrossing routes. The finite count $U$ is read

Now inspect the convergence-facing rows. The bounded stopping window $S$ and

limiting event.

Transport and replay are componentwise through $H$ and $C$, with $P,N$ recording
provenance and local naming. Therefore the route cannot export an almost-sure

\end{proof}
