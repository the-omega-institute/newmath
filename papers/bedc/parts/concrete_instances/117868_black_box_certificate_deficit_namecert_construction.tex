\begin{definition}[Black-box certificate deficit carrier]
\label{def:black-box-certificate-deficit-carrier}
A $\BlackBoxCertificateDeficitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(T,S,M,L,F,X,R,V,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the behavior-target row;
\item $S$ is the support-classifier row witnessing that a classifier candidate supports $T$;
\item $M$ is the missing-row inventory for source, pattern, classifier, stability, ledger, or verification rows;
\item $L$ is the deficit ledger that records which NameCert rows are absent or incomplete;
\item $F$ is the closed-explanation refusal row;
\item $X$ is the classifier transport row from $\ClassifierBoundaryUp$ and $\ClassifierMorphismUp$ surfaces;
\item $R$ is the replay row for behavior, support, missing-row, ledger, and refusal reads;
\item $V$ is the provenance row tying the packet to the black-box missing-certificate vision and the classifier-facing siblings;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $T,S,M,L,F,X,R,V,C,P,N$;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through the behavior target, support classifier, missing-row inventory, deficit ledger, refusal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BlackBoxCertificateDeficitUp$, $\ClassifierBoundaryUp$, $\ClassifierMorphismUp$, $\ClassifierShiftLedgerResidueUp$, $\AuditGateBoundaryUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BlackBoxCertificateDeficitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for unexplained behavior with no support classifier, ledger-complete certified explanation, global classifier correctness, hidden causal proof, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Black-box certificate deficit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:black-box-certificate-deficit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BlackBoxCertificateDeficitUp$ carrier $B=(T,S,M,L,F,X,R,V,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BlackBoxCertificateDeficitUp}$ surface consists exactly of behavior-target exposure, support-classifier exposure, missing-row inventory, deficit-ledger exposure, closed-explanation refusal, classifier transport, displayed replay, provenance, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:black-box-certificate-deficit-carrier}. The behavior target $T$ and support classifier $S$ are visible before any deficit claim can be read.

Read the missing-row inventory and ledger rows next. The inventory $M$ identifies which NameCert rows are absent or incomplete, while $L$ records the deficit as finite ledger data and $F$ refuses a closed-explanation read until those rows are supplied.

The classifier-facing siblings constrain transport. Classifier boundary and classifier morphism rows supply the support route, classifier-shift ledger residue records whether a classifier disagreement is present, and audit gate boundary records the local admission route. The structural rows $R,V,H,C,P,N$ preserve and name exactly that surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Black-box certificate deficit closed-explanation refusal]
\label{thm:black-box-certificate-deficit-closed-explanation-refusal}
An accepted $\BlackBoxCertificateDeficitUp$ packet may be read as black-box behavior only when the support-classifier row $S$ is displayed and the missing-row inventory $M$ or deficit ledger $L$ is nonempty. It may not be read as unexplained behavior, because $S$ is present, and it may not be read as ledger-complete explanation, because $F$ records the closed-explanation refusal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the behavior target and support classifier. The carrier stores $T$ and $S$ as distinct rows, so the packet is not an unexplained-behavior row.

Use the deficit rows $M,L,F$. Missing or incomplete certificate rows are recorded before the refusal row is consumed, and $F$ blocks any closed-explanation read that lacks the named NameCert rows.

Finally inspect the transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. They preserve the supported-but-uncertified status and provide no hidden certificate row, classifier-correctness proof, or ledger-complete explanation outside the displayed packet.
\end{proof}
