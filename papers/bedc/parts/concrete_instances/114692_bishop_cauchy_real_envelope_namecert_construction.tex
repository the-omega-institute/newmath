\begin{definition}[Bishop Cauchy real envelope carrier]
\label{def:bishop-cauchy-real-envelope-carrier}
A $\BishopCauchyRealEnvelopeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ stream-window row exposing exactly the finite observation windows opened by the Real-facing request.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance-budget row controlling endpoint scale, tail radius, comparison precision, and rational readback budget.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-Cauchy witness row read only through the displayed stream windows and dyadic budget.
\item $A$ is the $\RealRationalApproximationUp$ rational approximation row attached to the same $S,D,R$ data.
\item $B$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after the stream, dyadic, regular-Cauchy, and rational approximation rows have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the ledger projection row. It records the finite completion-interface read through $\CauchyRateRealizationUp$ and $\CauchyRealCompletionInterfaceUp$, and it stores no chosen limit or quotient representative.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream window, dyadic budget, regular-Cauchy witness, rational approximation, Real seal, ledger projection, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCauchyRealEnvelopeUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,D,R,A,B,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient reals, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or bridge credentials.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop Cauchy real envelope obligation surface]
\label{thm:bishop-cauchy-real-envelope-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\BishopCauchyRealEnvelopeUp$ carrier $E=(S,D,R,A,B,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopCauchyRealEnvelopeUp}$ surface consists exactly of five obligation families:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier display, requiring the stream-window, dyadic-budget, regular-Cauchy witness, rational approximation, Real seal, ledger projection, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows to be present;
\item classifier stability, requiring accepted packets to be compared only through the displayed rows $S,D,R,A,B,L,H,C,P,N$;
\item envelope transport, requiring every $\hsame$ transport and every $\Cont$-compatible finite observation replay to preserve the same stream windows, dyadic budget, regular witness, rational approximation, Real seal, and ledger projection;
\item ledger exactness, requiring the projection row $L$ to factor through the Cauchy-rate realization and four-face completion-interface rows before the Real seal can be consumed;
\item no quotient or choice use, requiring the certificate to export no quotient real, selected limit, countable-choice selector, trichotomy datum, host real equality proof, ambient completeness theorem, or bridge credential.
\end{enumerate}
No local obligation is stored outside these five families.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic rows are exactly $S,D,R,A,B,L$, and the structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

Carrier display and classifier stability are immediate from the displayed packet: every accepted comparison has access only to those ten rows. The regular-Cauchy witness $R$ is downstream of the stream window and dyadic budget, the rational approximation $A$ is attached to the same finite data, and the Real seal $B$ appears only after those rows have been read.

Transport and replay use the structural rows. The $\hsame$ row $H$ moves each displayed coordinate componentwise, while the $\Cont$ row $C$ replays the same finite observation route for later consumers.

The ledger row $L$ is the only completion-facing projection. It records the finite Cauchy-rate and completion-interface handoff, so ledger exactness cannot add a second Real coordinate or a selected limit.

The excluded principles have no carrier row and no continuation route. Hence quotient reals, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, host real equality, ambient completeness, and bridge credentials cannot become local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
