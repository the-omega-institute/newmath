$$

\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row used to inspect named tails of the net.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for rational tolerance comparisons.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budget row, recording precision cells and refinement slack for each request.
\item $W$ is the window-eventuality row, sending a requested tolerance to a displayed finite tail window.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, window, readback, dyadic, eventuality, coherence, and seal-eligibility rows.

\end{enumerate}

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy net convergence criterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-net-convergence-criterion-namecert-obligations}

$$
K=(C,S,R,D,W,T,E,H,A,P,N),

the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyNetConvergenceCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of Cauchy-net source exposure through $C$, stream-window inspection through $S$, regular rational readback through $R$, dyadic budget exposure through $D$, eventual tail-window selection through $W$, tail coherence through $T$, Real-seal eligibility through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed continuation replay through $A$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The dyadic budget row $D$ fixes the tolerance cell. The eventuality row $W$ then records which displayed tail window answers the request, and the coherence row $T$ records that later displayed windows keep the same tolerance decision.

Only after those rows are visible may the eligibility row $E$ reach the Real seal.
