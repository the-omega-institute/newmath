The $\EffectiveRealCompactIntervalSearchUp$ search packet consumes the closed-bounded interval net of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedintervalnet-namecert}, the finite epsilon-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, the compact-net modulus selector of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactnetmodulusselector-namecert}, and the located interval bisection route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-interval-bisection-namecert} before exporting its extremum handoff.
