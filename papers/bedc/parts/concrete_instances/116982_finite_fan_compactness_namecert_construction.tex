\begin{definition}[Finite fan compactness carrier]\label{def:finite-fan-compactness-carrier}
A $\FiniteFanCompactnessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(B,K,M,A,U,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ fan row, recording the bundled probe names, center histories, and coverage witnesses.
\item $K$ is the consumed $\CompactUp$ row, restricted to the displayed finite fan and its located cover evidence.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ row used to read source distances from points to fan centers and between source points.
\item $A$ is the $\ContinuousUp$ pointwise-modulus row at each bundled center.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row produced only after the finite fan and centerwise modulus rows have been read.
\item $R$ is the $\RatUp$ tolerance and positive-radius ledger attached to the same fan.
\item $L$ is the $\FiniteNetMinimumFoldUp$ lower-bound fold row, recording primitive recursion over the finite fan and the certified minimum radius used by the uniform-modulus route.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through finite cover recursion, centerwise modulus reads, rational lower-bound folding, metric triangle estimates, and uniform-modulus handoff.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteFanCompactnessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,K,M,A,U,R,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, countable choice, quotient metric equality, or ambient completion of the metric space.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite fan compactness uniform-modulus route]\label{thm:finite-fan-compactness-uniform-modulus-route}
Let $F=(B,K,M,A,U,R,L,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\FiniteFanCompactnessUp$ carrier. Every compact-uniform-continuity consumer must read the finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ fan $B$, the compact and metric rows $K,M$, the centerwise continuous rows $A$, the rational tolerance ledger $R$, and the finite lower-bound fold $L$ before the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff $U$ is exported. The route is constructive finite ProbeBundle recursion and exports no open-cover compactness, selected subcover, countable choice, quotient metric equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the finite fan row $B$. It lists the probe names and center histories consumed by the compact row $K$, so the cover data are already finite before any modulus is read.

For each bundled probe, replay $C$ through the metric row $M$ and continuous row $A$. These rows produce only centerwise pointwise-modulus data attached to the displayed fan.

The rational ledger $R$ and finite lower-bound fold $L$ then perform primitive recursion over the probes. The fold keeps a certified positive lower bound for the displayed center radii and supplies the tolerance-only precision needed by $U$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no row stores an arbitrary open cover, selected subcover, choice principle, quotient metric equality, or ambient completion, the uniform-modulus handoff can only be read through the finite fan route.
\end{proof}
