\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC confluence audit bridge boundary]
\label{thm:metacic-confluence-audit-bridge-boundary}
The public $\MetaCICConfluenceAuditPacketUp$ audit packet exports a bridged boundary only through the candidate-normalization sibling route, the residual-diamond handoff, the candidate SN discharge boundary, and the sibling lattice route displayed by the hub. No bridge-facing consumer may bypass those sibling rows or read a host confluence certificate outside the finite $\BHist$ route named by the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the public audit packet through its displayed siblings. The candidate-normalization route fixes the normal-form boundary, the residual-diamond handoff records the local confluence comparison, the candidate SN discharge boundary records the terminating candidate side, and the sibling lattice route records the shared dependency order.

A bridged consumer therefore receives only the route that has passed through these four rows. Since the hub exposes no additional host certificate coordinate, the bridge boundary is exactly the displayed finite route through those siblings.
\end{proof}
