Following Bishop and Bridges constructive metric practice, the $\ApartnessMetricUp$ packet records the finite separation interface by which a displayed $\MetricUp$ distance row can be read as constructive apartness through positive $\RealUp$ evidence and $\LocatedRealUp$ witness data. It sits beside the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the located-real witness packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-real-namecert}, and the real-separation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-separation-via-uniformity-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Apartness metric carrier]
\label{def:apartness-metric-carrier}
An $\ApartnessMetricUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(M,D,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, recording the accepted carrier, distance read, and metric transport window.
\item $D$ is the distance-read row, recording the displayed value $d(x,y)$ before any separation consumer reads it.
\item $R$ is the positive $\RealUp$ readback row, recording that the distance read is separated from zero by a positive real-name witness.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedRealUp$ witness row, recording the finite located data used to compare the distance read with the positive boundary.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, distance read, positive-real readback, located-real witness, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the metric source through the positive-distance readback and located witness rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ApartnessMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\RealSeparationViaUniformityUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ApartnessMetricUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,D,R,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for classical trichotomy of reals, quotient equality of metric points, selected representatives, or completion of the ambient real carrier.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Apartness metric NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:apartness-metric-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ApartnessMetricUp$ carrier $A=(M,D,R,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ApartnessMetricUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, metric-source exposure through $M$, distance-read exposure through $D$, positive $\RealUp$ readback through $R$, located-real witness exposure through $L$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:apartness-metric-carrier}. The rows $M,D,R,L,H,C,P,N$ are the whole accepted packet, so every NameCert obligation is a projection from one of those rows.

Read the constructive separation data in order. The metric source row $M$ supplies the distance context, the row $D$ exposes the distance value, and the rows $R,L$ record positive real readback together with located witness data.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no row for trichotomy, quotient equality, selected representatives, or real completion, those objects are outside the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Apartness metric symmetry cotransport]
\label{thm:apartness-metric-symmetry-cotransport}
Every accepted $\ApartnessMetricUp$ read transports symmetry of constructive separation only through the displayed metric distance row, positive $\RealUp$ readback, and located-real witness row. If the packet reads $x\# y$ from $d(x,y)$, then the reverse read $y\# x$ must replay the same $\MetricUp$ symmetry source, the same positive-distance boundary in $\RealUp$, and componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; it cannot appeal to classical inequality trichotomy or quotient equality of points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the metric source row of \autoref{def:apartness-metric-carrier}. The distance read $D$ is downstream of $M$, so a reverse separation read must reuse the displayed metric symmetry source rather than an external equality comparison.

The positive witness remains constructive. Rows $R$ and $L$ keep the positive $\RealUp$ readback and $\LocatedRealUp$ witness visible before either direction of apartness is public.

Finally the transport row $H$ carries the reverse read through the same finite packet. The excluded classical and quotient objects have no carrier coordinate, so they cannot supply the reverse direction.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

Read the real-separation consumer route next. The sibling $\RealSeparationViaUniformityUp$ may consume the positive-distance boundary only after $R$ and $L$ have been displayed over the same distance read.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route for completion-facing consumers. Because the carrier has no coordinate for completion, trichotomy, quotient equality, or selected representatives, the consumer receives only the finite apartness metric surface.
\end{proof}
