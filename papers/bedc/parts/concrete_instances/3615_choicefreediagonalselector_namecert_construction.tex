\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-choicefreediagonalselector-namecert}
\origin{ai}

The $\ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp$ packet records the finite selector boundary used by the Real completeness diagonal route. It binds a precision request, a selected cofinal window, StreamName observations, a RegSeqRat witness, the RealUp seal handoff, and the BEDC transport rows as one inspectable naming certificate. The packet is a finite boundary between cofinal-window schedules and selector-budget packets; it is not a host choice principle, quotient stream, selected host limit, or ambient completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.

The selected cofinal window is consumed through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, then routed through the existing $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ seal surfaces before the local selector row is read. The selector does not own the window semantics; it schedules the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window consumed by the diagonal handoff.

\begin{definition}[Choice-free diagonal selector carrier]
\label{def:choice-free-diagonal-selector-carrier}
A $\ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\varepsilon$ is the positive precision request made by the diagonal-limit consumer;
\item $W$ is the selected cofinal window row, displayed as a finite family of admissible StreamName observation indices;
\item $S$ is the finite family of $\StreamNameUp$ observation rows opened by the selected cofinal window;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ witness row assembled from those observations for the diagonal handoff;
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the RegSeqRat witness has been assembled;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transports for the request, window, StreamName observations, RegSeqRat witness, RealUp seal, and structural rows;
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ routes for request inspection, cofinal-window selection, observation readback, RegSeqRat handoff, RealUp seal delivery, and non-escape inspection;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted carriers only through the visible rows $\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free diagonal selector window route]
\label{thm:choice-free-diagonal-selector-window-route}
Every accepted $\ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp$ carrier routes a diagonal precision request through the displayed finite cofinal window before any RegSeqRat or RealUp data can be consumed. For
$$
D=(\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
each StreamName observation in $S$ is opened only inside the selected cofinal window $W$, the RegSeqRat witness $R$ is assembled only from those observations, and the RealUp seal row $E$ is reached only through that witness route.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp.ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorCarrier\_window\_route}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier coordinates. The request row $\varepsilon$ and selected cofinal window $W$ are displayed before any StreamName, RegSeqRat, or RealUp row is available.

The observation rows $S$ are listed as the finite family opened by $W$. Hence a downstream diagonal consumer can read StreamName data only from the selected cofinal window.

The witness row $R$ appears after those observations, and the seal row $E$ appears after $R$. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same finite route, so the selector boundary is exactly $\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free diagonal selector RegSeqRat seal factorization]
\label{thm:choice-free-diagonal-selector-regseqrat-seal-factorization}
For an accepted $\ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp$ carrier
$$
D=(\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
any $\RealUp$ seal read from the selector factors first through the finite $\StreamNameUp$ observations $S$ and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ witness row $R$. The seal row $E$ is therefore available only as the terminal handoff of the displayed route
$$
S\longmapsto R\longmapsto E,
$$
with $H,C,P,N$ preserving that same factorization by transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:choice-free-diagonal-selector-carrier}. The only observation rows before the Real-facing endpoint are the finite StreamName family $S$ and the RegSeqRat witness row $R$.

Apply \autoref{thm:choice-free-diagonal-selector-window-route}. It forces each observation in $S$ to be opened inside the selected cofinal window $W$, then assembles the RegSeqRat witness $R$ from those observations before the RealUp seal row $E$ can be reached.

The structural rows do not add another seal route. Componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$ preserve the same $S\longmapsto R\longmapsto E$ route, so every selector RealUp read factors through the RegSeqRat handoff first.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Choice-free diagonal selector stage carrier]
\label{def:choice-free-diagonal-selector-stage-carrier}
For an accepted $\ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp$ carrier
$$
D=(\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
a stage carrier is a finite row
$$
D_j=(\varepsilon,W_j,S_j,R_j,E_j,H_j,C_j,P,N)
$$
displayed inside the selected cofinal window $W$. The row $W_j$ is a finite prefix of $W$, $S_j$ is the corresponding finite $\StreamNameUp$ observation family, $R_j$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff assembled from $S_j$, and $E_j$ is the $\RealUp$ seal boundary reached from $R_j$. The structural rows $H_j,C_j,P,N$ are the restrictions of the same transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows to that finite stage.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free diagonal selector stage induction]
\label{thm:choice-free-diagonal-selector-stage-induction}
Let $D=(\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp$ carrier whose selected cofinal window $W$ is displayed as finite stage rows. Induction over those rows preserves the route
$$
\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N
$$
and proves that every successor stage remains inside the displayed path
$$
\StreamNameUp\longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp\longmapsto \RealUp
$$
before the selector boundary is sealed by $E$. No successor stage may introduce a hidden cofinal-window selector, quotient stream equality, host choice rule, selected host limit, unbounded StreamName scan, or ambient completeness principle for $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp.StageInduction.ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorCarrier\_stage\_induction}
\begin{proof}
For the base row, inspect the first displayed finite prefix $W_0$ of the selected cofinal window. Its observation family $S_0$ is read through $\StreamNameUp$, the witness $R_0$ is assembled as the corresponding $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff, and the boundary $E_0$ is reached only after that handoff.

For the successor row, assume the stage carrier
$$
D_j=(\varepsilon,W_j,S_j,R_j,E_j,H_j,C_j,P,N)
$$
has preserved the displayed route. The next row extends the finite prefix of $W$ by one displayed window entry, so the new observation is appended inside the same $\StreamNameUp$ family, the RegSeqRat witness is rebuilt from the enlarged finite family, and the RealUp seal remains downstream of that witness.

The transport and replay restrictions $H_j,C_j$ are inherited from the original carrier rows $H,C$, while $P,N$ keep the same provenance and local naming. Thus the induction invariant is exactly the finite route $\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$, and every successor stage stays inside $\StreamNameUp\longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp\longmapsto \RealUp$ before sealing.

Since each step only inspects a displayed finite row of $W$, the induction has no coordinate for hidden selection, quotient stream equality, host choice, selected host limits, unbounded observation scans, or ambient RealUp completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free diagonal selector non-escape]
\label{thm:choice-free-diagonal-selector-nonescape}
An accepted $\ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp$ carrier cannot be used to recover data outside the displayed tuple $\varepsilon,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. In particular, it exports no host choice rule, quotient stream equality, selected host limit, hidden cofinal-window selector, unbounded StreamName scan, or ambient completeness principle for $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorUp.Nonescape.ChoiceFreeDiagonalSelectorNonescape}
\begin{proof}
Read the displayed tuple in \autoref{def:choice-free-diagonal-selector-carrier}. The content rows are the request, cofinal window, StreamName observations, RegSeqRat witness, and RealUp seal; the remaining rows only provide transport, route replay, provenance, and local naming for those entries.

Apply \autoref{thm:choice-free-diagonal-selector-window-route}. It confines every diagonal read to the selected finite cofinal window and forces the RegSeqRat and RealUp rows to be downstream of that route.

Since the carrier contains no coordinate for host choice, quotient stream equality, selected host limits, hidden selectors, unbounded observation scans, or RealUp completeness, no such object can be recovered from an accepted packet.
\end{proof}
