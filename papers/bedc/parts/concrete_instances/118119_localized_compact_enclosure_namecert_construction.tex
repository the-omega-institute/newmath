Following Bishop--Bridges style located compact interval enclosures, the $\LocalizedCompactEnclosureUp$ packet records a finite boundary around one displayed located interval cell before any compactness or continuity consumer reads it. It sits after the endpoint-cell certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}, the finite-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closed-interval-compact-net-namecert}, and the bisection handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-intervalbisection-namecert}. The packet is a local compact-enclosure boundary over finite observation rows; it is not open-cover compactness, a selected subcover, a host interval object, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Localized compact enclosure carrier]
\label{def:localized-compact-enclosure-carrier}
A $\LocalizedCompactEnclosureUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(I,K,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\LocatedIntervalUp$ source row, carrying the located endpoint-cell boundary to be enclosed.
\item $K$ is the $\ClosedIntervalCompactNetUp$ finite-net row for the displayed interval cell.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row through which downstream names request finite windows inside the enclosure.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row used by Real and completion-facing consumers of the same enclosure.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the finite interval and readback rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $I,K,S,Q,E$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route from the located interval row through the finite-net row to stream, regular-rational, and real-seal consumers.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocalizedCompactEnclosureUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\ClosedIntervalCompactNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocalizedCompactEnclosureUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $I,K,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, host interval equality, quotient real equality, arbitrary choice of a mesh, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Localized compact enclosure NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:localized-compact-enclosure-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocalizedCompactEnclosureUp$ carrier
$L=(I,K,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\LocalizedCompactEnclosureUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, located interval boundary exposure, finite-net admission, stream-window exposure, regular-rational readback, Real seal factorization, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, or $\RealUp$ consumer of the enclosure must read these rows before it can consume the terminal seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:localized-compact-enclosure-carrier}. The rows $I,K,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N$ expose the located interval source, finite-net enclosure, stream windows, regular readbacks, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

Use the sibling endpoint-cell route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}. It supplies the finite located interval boundary read through $\LocatedIntervalUp$ rather than through a host interval or selected endpoint.

Apply the finite-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closed-interval-compact-net-namecert}. The row $K$ is the only compact-enclosure row, and it is displayed before any stream, regular-rational, or real-seal consumer can read the packet.

Finally replay the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. They transport, route, source, and name the same finite enclosure surface. Since the carrier contains no open-cover, selected-subcover, quotient-real, mesh-choice, or ambient-completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Localized compact enclosure finite-net handoff]
\label{thm:localized-compact-enclosure-finite-net-handoff}
Every accepted $\LocalizedCompactEnclosureUp$ carrier
$L=(I,K,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$ hands a downstream compactness or uniform-continuity consumer the same finite route
$$
\LocatedIntervalUp,\ \ClosedIntervalCompactNetUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp,\ \BHist,\ \hsame,\ \Cont,\ \Pkg,\ \NameCert .
$$
The consumer receives the located interval row $I$ and the finite-net row $K$ before it may read the stream windows $S$, regular readbacks $Q$, or seal row $E$. The handoff supplies a finite enclosure boundary only; it does not claim open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, host function equality, quotient real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the located interval row $I$. It is the same endpoint-cell boundary governed by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinterval-namecert}, so the enclosure begins with displayed finite interval data.

Read the compact-net row $K$ next. By \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closed-interval-compact-net-namecert}, this row is an explicit finite-net handoff for the displayed interval cell, not a general open-cover compactness principle.

The stream and regular-rational rows $S,Q$ then expose the finite observation windows consumed by downstream names. The seal row $E$ is reachable only after those finite rows are displayed.

Transport and replay through $H,C$, then attach $P,N$. The resulting L10 dependency route is the same boundary used by interval bisection in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-intervalbisection-namecert}, but the present packet exports only the localized enclosure surface and not a branch selector or selected subcover.
\end{proof}
