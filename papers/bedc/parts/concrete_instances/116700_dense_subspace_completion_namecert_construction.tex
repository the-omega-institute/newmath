\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DenseSubspaceCompletionUp$}

Following Bourbaki and Engelking on completion from a dense subspace, the $\DenseSubspaceCompletionUp$ packet records the finite BEDC handoff by which a dense subspace carrier supplies completion-facing names without replacing them by quotient representatives or host equality. It sits beside the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the Cauchy-filter completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfiltercompletion-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dense embedding boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completionembedding-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a dense-subspace completion handoff as finite rows; it is not a selected completion point, not an ambient complete metric space, and not a detached Real-completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Dense-subspace completion carrier]
\label{def:dense-subspace-completion-carrier}
A $\DenseSubspaceCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(X,Y,M,S,F,W,R,E,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the ambient $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row whose distance and classifier data are read by the completion request.
\item $Y$ is the dense subspace row, including the displayed inclusion into $X$, the dense witness ledger, and the subspace classifier transport.
\item $M$ is the metric restriction row on $Y$, read through the same metric witnesses as $X$.
\item $S$ is the subspace approximation row: each ambient tolerance request is met by a displayed subspace witness.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$ route generated from the dense subspace data.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window ledger used to observe finite completion-facing reads.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row extracted from the same windows and metric tolerances.
\item $E$ is the $\CompletionEmbeddingUp$ row that embeds subspace observations into the completion boundary.
\item $A$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $M,S,F,W,R,E$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the ambient metric, dense subspace, restricted metric, approximation, filter completion, windows, readbacks, embedding, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an ambient completion request through the dense subspace row, Cauchy-filter route, stream windows, regular readback, completion embedding, and Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DenseSubspaceCompletionUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\CauchyFilterCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\CompletionEmbeddingUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DenseSubspaceCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $X,Y,M,S,F,W,R,E,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, host metric equality, countable choice, or an ambient completeness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dense-subspace completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dense-subspace-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DenseSubspaceCompletionUp$ carrier $D=(X,Y,M,S,F,W,R,E,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DenseSubspaceCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed ambient metric, dense subspace, restricted metric, approximation ledger, Cauchy-filter completion route, finite stream windows, regular rational readbacks, completion embedding, Real seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming rows. No NameCert obligation is supplied by quotient completions, selected representatives, host equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dense-subspace-completion-carrier}. The rows $X,Y,M,S,F,W,R,E,A,H,C,P,N$ are exactly the ambient metric, dense subspace, restricted metric, approximation, Cauchy-filter, window, readback, embedding, Real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming coordinates.

The dense-subspace part is $Y,M,S$. It reads the metric row $X$, restricts that metric to the displayed subspace, and records the finite approximation ledger by which ambient tolerance requests are met inside the subspace.

The completion-facing part is $F,W,R,E,A$. The Cauchy-filter completion route consumes the dense-subspace data, the stream windows and regular readbacks expose finite observations, the embedding row inserts the subspace observations into the completion boundary, and the Real seal is reached only after those rows are present.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion, selected representatives, host equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dense-subspace completion dense handoff]
\label{thm:dense-subspace-completion-dense-handoff}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\DenseSubspaceCompletionUp$ packet factors through the finite handoff
$$
X,Y,M,S \longmapsto F,W,R \longmapsto E \longmapsto A \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the consumer reads the dense subspace row, metric restriction, Cauchy-filter completion row, finite windows, regular rational readbacks, $\CompletionEmbeddingUp$ boundary, $\RealUp$ seal, $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row before accepting the completed result.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted completion-facing request. Carrier admission exposes the ambient metric $X$ and the dense subspace row $Y$ before any completion row is available.

The restricted metric and approximation ledger $M,S$ then produce the dense handoff. Each ambient tolerance read must be witnessed by a subspace approximation before it can enter a Cauchy-filter completion route.

Read the completion rows next. The row $F$ is the Cauchy-filter completion route, $W$ supplies finite stream observations, $R$ supplies regular rational readback, and $E$ is the dense completion embedding boundary.

The Real seal $A$ is downstream of that route, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name it. Hence every dense-subspace completion consumer receives the displayed finite handoff and cannot extract quotient equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
