Following Bishop and Bridges on apartness spaces and Kelley on uniform
entourages, the $\UniformApartnessSpaceUp$ packet records the constructive
interface where uniform smallness and positive apartness are read from the
same finite carrier. It sits after the entourage rows of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the positive-gap rows
of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apartnessspace-namecert}, the metric
apartness handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-apartness-namecert},
the completion transport of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-completion-functor-namecert}, and the
filter criterion of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-filter-completion-criterion-namecert}.
The packet supplies a separated uniform apartness interface for constructive
completion consumers; it does not introduce quotient completion, choice,
ambient host equality, real trichotomy, or a selected completion point.

\begin{definition}[UniformApartnessSpace carrier]
\label{def:uniform-apartness-space-carrier}
A $\UniformApartnessSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(X,E,A,M,F,K,S,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the common carried-object row and local point classifier.
\item $E$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ entourage source row over $X$.
\item $A$ is the $\ApartnessSpaceUp$ positive-apartness source row over the
same carried observations.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricApartnessUp$ compatibility row, reading positive
metric distance as apartness evidence without importing host inequality.
\item $F$ is the $\MetricCompletionFunctorUp$ handoff row for uniformly
continuous maps between completion packets.
\item $K$ is the $\CauchyFilterCompletionCriterionUp$ filter-criterion row
used by completion consumers that read uniform Cauchy data.
\item $S$ is the separated-uniform boundary row: if a consumer has only
uniform smallness and no displayed apartness evidence, it must remain inside
the separated classifier boundary before treating two names as identified.
\item $R$ is the row that relates an entourage request, a positive-apartness
request, and the metric-apartness witness to one finite readback route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the carried object,
uniform source, apartness source, metric-apartness compatibility,
completion-functor handoff, filter criterion, separated boundary, readback,
provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion-facing request
through the rows $E,A,M,F,K,S,R,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformSpaceUp$,
$\ApartnessSpaceUp$, $\MetricApartnessUp$, $\MetricCompletionFunctorUp$,
$\CauchyFilterCompletionCriterionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and
$\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformApartnessSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier is finite and row-visible. It carries no quotient of Cauchy
filters, no selected representative, no choice principle, no host equality
predicate, and no route that turns absence of apartness into equality outside
the displayed separated-uniform boundary.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[UniformApartnessSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-apartness-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformApartnessSpaceUp$ carrier
$U=(X,E,A,M,F,K,S,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\UniformApartnessSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier
admission for the displayed rows, classifier stability under componentwise
$\hsame$ transport, entourage-apartness readback through $R$, completion
handoff through $F$ and $K$, separated-uniform nonescape through $S$, visible
$\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming
through $N$. Every consumer read is forced through the finite route
$$
X,E,A,M,F,K,S,R\longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
No NameCert row exports quotient completion, choice, ambient host equality,
real trichotomy, selected representatives, or equality from mere absence of
positive apartness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:uniform-apartness-space-carrier}. The rows $X,E,A,M,F,K,S,R$ are
the only mathematical rows available to a consumer, while $H,C,P,N$ record
transport, replay, provenance, and naming.

Classifier stability is componentwise. The stored $\hsame$ rows move the
uniform source, apartness source, metric-apartness witness, completion handoff,
filter criterion, separated boundary, and readback row together, and the
$\Cont$ row replays the same finite request after transport.

The excluded principles cannot be read because the carrier has no coordinate
for a quotient completion, selected representative, choice function, host
equality predicate, real trichotomy, or equality-by-non-apartness rule. Thus
the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[UniformApartnessSpace completion-filter handoff]
\label{thm:uniform-apartness-space-completion-filter-handoff}
Every $\MetricCompletionFunctorUp$ or
$\CauchyFilterCompletionCriterionUp$ consumer of an accepted
$\UniformApartnessSpaceUp$ packet must first read the route
$$

$$
The functor row $F$ supplies only the displayed map and extension handoff

\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-completion-functor-namecert}; the
criterion row $K$ supplies only the displayed Cauchy-filter completion test
named by
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-filter-completion-criterion-namecert}.

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a completion-facing request. The uniform source row $E$ identifies
the entourage data being consumed, and the apartness source row $A$ records

The metric-apartness row $M$ translates positive metric distance into the
apartness interface, while $F$ and $K$ expose the two completion-facing
handoffs. These handoffs are read as finite rows of the same packet, not as
external quotient objects.

The separated boundary $S$ and readback row $R$ keep the consumer inside the
displayed uniform-apartness interface. Transport, replay, provenance, and
naming then pass through $H,C,P,N$, so no hidden completion quotient,
selected point, choice function, or host equality is exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[UniformApartnessSpace separated uniform nonescape]
\label{thm:uniform-apartness-space-separated-uniform-nonescape}
Let $U=(X,E,A,M,F,K,S,R,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted
$\UniformApartnessSpaceUp$ carrier. If a downstream consumer has only a
uniform smallness read from $E$ and lacks a displayed positive-apartness read
through $A$ and $M$, then every equality-facing read remains confined to the
separated boundary row $S$. The packet therefore prevents a completion or
filter consumer from treating uniform nearness, absence of apartness, or
zero-distance notation as host equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the rows of \autoref{def:uniform-apartness-space-carrier}. Uniform
smallness is stored in $E$, positive apartness is stored through $A$ and $M$,
and equality-facing access is represented only by the separated boundary
row $S$.

A consumer that lacks the positive-apartness route cannot use $R$ to produce a
positive separation witness, and it cannot bypass $S$ because the packet has
no host equality row. The completion handoff rows $F$ and $K$ are downstream
readers of the same boundary, not constructors of ambient equality.

Thus every equality-facing read remains inside $S$ and is transported or
replayed only by $H$ and $C$ with provenance $P$ and local naming $N$.
\end{proof}
