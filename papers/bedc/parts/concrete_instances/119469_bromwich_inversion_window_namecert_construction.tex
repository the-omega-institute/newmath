\begin{definition}[Bromwich inversion window carrier]
\label{def:bromwich-inversion-window-carrier}
A $\BromwichInversionWindowUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(L,A,W,Q,S,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the $\LaplaceTransformUp$ input row whose transform data are being read.
\item $A$ is the abscissa row naming the finite vertical line $\operatorname{Re}(s)=a$ inspected by the window.
\item $W$ is the height-window row naming the finite interval $[-T,T]$ on that vertical line.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the finite quadrature samples attached to $W$.
\item $S$ is the $\ContourIntegralSocketUp$ row that supplies the contour-window socket used by the read.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for the windowed inversion value returned to Real-facing consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $L,A,W,Q,S,R$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
L \longmapsto A \longmapsto W \longmapsto Q \longmapsto S \longmapsto R .

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BromwichInversionWindowUp$, $\LaplaceTransformUp$, $\ContourIntegralSocketUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BromwichInversionWindowUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed transform, vertical-line, height-window, rational readback, contour socket, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows. \end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BromwichInversionWindow NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bromwich-inversion-window-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\BromwichInversionWindowUp$ carrier $B=(L,A,W,Q,S,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BromwichInversionWindowUp}$ surface consists exactly of six finite obligations: carrier admission for the ten displayed rows; transform-source compatibility binding $L$ to the same window; vertical-line stability binding $A$ and $W$ under componentwise transport; regular readback exactness for the quadrature row $Q$; contour-socket handoff through $S$; and local provenance plus naming over $H,C,P,N$. These obligations export no host Bromwich inversion theorem, analytic-continuation theorem, selected improper integral, quotient contour equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:bromwich-inversion-window-carrier}. The packet exposes exactly the transform row, abscissa row, height window, regular rational readback, contour socket, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Transform-source compatibility reads $L,A,W$. The window may inspect only the finite transform data attached to the displayed abscissa and height rows, so it cannot call an external inverse-transform operator.

Vertical-line stability reads $A,W,H,C$. A transported abscissa row and height row remain the same finite vertical-line window through the displayed replay route.

Regular readback exactness reads $Q$ after $W$. The finite samples attached to the height window are returned only through the displayed $\RegSeqRatUp$ row, not through a selected improper integral.

The contour-socket handoff reads $S$ and then the Real seal $R$. The socket row supplies the contour-window boundary, and the terminal seal is the only Real-facing output coordinate.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no carrier coordinate supplies a host inversion theorem, analytic continuation, quotient contour equality, selected improper integral, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the six obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

$$

\begin{proof}
