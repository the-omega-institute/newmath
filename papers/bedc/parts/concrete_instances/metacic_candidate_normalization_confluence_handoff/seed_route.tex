\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate-normalization confluence seed route]
\label{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-seed-route}
Every seed read of an accepted $\MetacicCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ packet passes through the finite candidate-normalization handoff route: the candidate row, the normalization row, the local confluence witness row, the residual budget row, the replay row, the provenance row, and the local $\NameCert$ row. The route is therefore a displayed MetaCIC handoff packet rather than a global confluence principle, quotient syntax equality, evaluator equality, or unrestricted conversion decision.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-carrier}. Its visible rows include candidate evidence, bounded normal endpoint data, frontier data, residual envelope data, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Use the boundary and obligation surface theorems. They keep candidate evidence and normalization-frontier rows visible before a local confluence read can be consumed, so the seed route cannot skip directly to an endpoint equality.

Apply residual-budget factorization and route factorization. These rows place the residual budget and local confluence witness inside the same finite handoff surface as the replay and provenance rows.

Finally use the scoped surface theorem. It records the finite packet surface consumed by MetaCIC frontier readers and excludes full Church--Rosser, quotient syntax equality, evaluator equality, global conversion decidability, and kernel-consistency claims.
\end{proof}
