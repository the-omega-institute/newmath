\begin{theorem}[Normalization-confluence bridge obligations]
\label{thm:normalization-confluence-bridge-obligations}
Every accepted normalization-confluence bridge carrier exposes candidate-mediated strong-normalization evidence and bounded local confluence rows only through
$$
A,K,N,J,Q,D,O,H,C,P,L .
$$
The candidate row $K$ may feed the bounded endpoint row $N$, and the residual local-join budget $J$ may feed the confluence frontier only after the closed checker $Q$ and obstruction row $D$ remain visible. The bridge does not prove full confluence, unrestricted subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient syntax equality, global conversion decidability, host evaluator soundness, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the bridge carrier. The only normalization-facing rows are the audit anchor $A$, candidate evidence $K$, bounded endpoint $N$, residual local-join budget $J$, closed checker $Q$, and obstruction row $D$.

Use the candidate-normalization handoff boundary of \autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-boundary}. It keeps candidate evidence and frontier data visible before bounded endpoint or residual rows can be consumed.

Apply residual-diamond retention and decider-boundary routing from \autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-residual-diamond-retention} and \autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-decider-boundary}. These force $J,Q,D$ to remain finite frontier rows rather than global confluence or subject-reduction evidence.

Finally read the structural rows $O,H,C,P,L$. They transport, replay, package, route, and name the same bounded bridge budget, so a consumer receives candidate-mediated normalization and local confluence frontier data without extracting any refused global MetaCIC endpoint.
\end{proof}
