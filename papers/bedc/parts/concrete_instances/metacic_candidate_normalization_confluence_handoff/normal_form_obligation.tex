\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate normalization confluence handoff normal-form obligation]
\label{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-normal-form-obligation}
Any normal-form read exported by an accepted
$\MetacicCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ carrier must first expose
the candidate-normalization row, the residual-diamond row, and the sibling
confluence route. Concretely, the read must pass through the candidate gate and
frontier rows of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-boundary}, the
premise-exposure and normal-endpoint rows of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-premise-exposure}
and
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-normal-endpoint-readback},
the residual-diamond retention row of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-residual-diamond-retention},
the bounded candidate-diamond route of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-diamond-budget-route}, and the
sibling confluence route of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-sibling-route}
before any normal-form endpoint is available. The obligation is a finite
premise-exposure row only; it does not assert host normalization, global
confluence, unrestricted subject reduction, quotient syntax equality, evaluator
soundness, or closed consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier from
\autoref{def:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-carrier}. A
normal-form read can see only the candidate, frontier, residual, decidable,
blocked-edge, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows displayed in
that carrier.

Apply the boundary and premise-exposure theorems. They force the candidate gate
and normalization frontier to appear before the normal endpoint is consumed,
and they keep the residual envelope, decidable boundary, and blocked-edge rows
visible for confluence-facing reads.

Next apply normal-endpoint readback and residual-diamond retention. These rows
show that an endpoint can be handed to confluence consumers only while
candidate evidence, residual data, decidable-boundary data, and blocked edges
remain present in the same packet.

Finally use the diamond-budget dependency and sibling confluence route. The
candidate diamond supplies bounded local peak and join-budget rows, and the
sibling route exposes the critical-path, normalization-frontier,
confluence-audit, and parallel-diamond residual surfaces before the normal-form
read is available. Since no carrier coordinate contains a host-level
normalization or global confluence theorem, the obligation remains the finite
handoff row stated above.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC retained-premise normal-form handoff]
\label{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-retained-premise-normal-form}
Every strong-normalization-facing consumer of the normal-form endpoint exported
by an accepted $\MetacicCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ packet must
retain the candidate gate, the residual-diamond retention row, the
decidable-boundary row, and the sibling confluence route. The consumer may read
the bounded normal-form endpoint only as a finite handoff through those rows; it
does not receive candidate-free normalization, quotient syntax equality, host
evaluator soundness, unrestricted subject reduction, global confluence, or
closed consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the normal-form obligation of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-normal-form-obligation}.
It forces a normal-form read through the candidate-normalization row, residual
row, and sibling confluence route before the endpoint is available.

Next use residual-diamond retention from
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-residual-diamond-retention}.
That row keeps the residual envelope, decidable boundary, and blocked-edge data
visible while the endpoint is treated as a confluence-facing read.

The diamond-budget route of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-diamond-budget-route} supplies the
bounded local peak and join-budget rows. These are the finite rows that a
strong-normalization-facing consumer may inspect; they do not discharge the
candidate gate or replace it with a global normalization theorem.

Finally apply the sibling confluence route of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-sibling-route}.
It keeps the critical-path, normalization-frontier, confluence-audit, and
parallel-diamond residual surfaces visible before the handoff reaches a
consumer. Hence the endpoint remains a retained-premise normal-form handoff and
no candidate-free normalization or quotient syntax equality is exposed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate-normalization residual budget]
\label{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-residual-budget}
Any normal-form endpoint exported by an accepted
$\MetacicCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ packet to a
strong-normalization-facing consumer must retain, before the endpoint is read,
the candidate evidence, residual-diamond retention row, decidable-boundary data,
blocked-edge rows, bounded local peak rows, join-budget rows, and the sibling
confluence route. The endpoint is therefore a finite residual-budget read, not
candidate-free normalization, global confluence, unrestricted subject reduction,
quotient syntax equality, evaluator soundness, or closed consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the normal-form obligation of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-normal-form-obligation}
and the retained-premise handoff of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-retained-premise-normal-form}.
Together they force candidate evidence and the sibling confluence route to
remain visible before a normal-form endpoint is exported.

Use residual-diamond retention from
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-residual-diamond-retention}.
It keeps the residual envelope, decidable-boundary data, and blocked-edge rows
attached to the endpoint read rather than allowing a detached normal-form token.

Finally apply the diamond-budget route of
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-diamond-budget-route}. Its bounded
local peak and join-budget rows are the finite budget a
strong-normalization-facing consumer may inspect. Since the cited rows exhaust
the exported endpoint surface, none of candidate-free normalization, global
confluence, unrestricted subject reduction, quotient syntax equality, evaluator
soundness, or closed consistency is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate-normalization residual exhaustion]
\label{thm:metacic-normal-form-residual-budget-exhaustion}
Every accepted $\MetacicCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ handoff row
whose candidate set, bounded local peak, join-budget row, residual envelope,
decidable-boundary row, blocked-edge row, transport, replay, provenance, and
local $\NameCert$ are displayed has an exhaustive finite endpoint surface. It
either forwards a retained premise through the candidate and normalization
frontier rows, or emits the blocked residual edge as an obstruction. No
consumer may erase that obstruction and read global confluence, unrestricted
subject reduction, quotient syntax equality, closed consistency, or a
candidate-free normal-form theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed handoff carrier from the parent chapter. The candidate
set, frontier rows, residual envelope, decidable boundary, blocked-edge row,
transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data are the only rows available
to the consumer.

Apply the residual budget theorem
\autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-residual-budget}. It requires the
candidate evidence, residual-diamond retention, decidable-boundary data,
blocked-edge rows, bounded local peak rows, join-budget rows, and sibling
confluence route to remain visible before any endpoint is exported.

Now read the displayed forward-or-blocked row as finite data. In the forward
case the retained premise is carried through the candidate and frontier rows. In
the blocked case the residual edge remains an obstruction attached to the
endpoint read. The carrier's refused endpoints exclude candidate-free
normalization, global confluence, unrestricted subject reduction, quotient
syntax equality, closed consistency, and any consumer route that erases the
obstruction.
\end{proof}
