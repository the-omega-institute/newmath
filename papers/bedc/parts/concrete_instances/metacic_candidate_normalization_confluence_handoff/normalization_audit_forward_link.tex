\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate handoff normalization-audit forward link]
\label{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-normalization-audit-forward-link}
Every confluence-facing consumer of an accepted $\MetacicCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ packet must first read the $\MetacicNormalizationAuditUp$ packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicnormalizationaudit-namecert}. In particular, the handoff consumes the audit carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-normalization-audit-carrier}, the candidate evidence row of \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-audit-candidate-boundary}, the closed projection route of \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-audit-closed-term-projection-route}, the finite strong-normalization scope of \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-audit-sn-scope}, the application boundary of \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-audit-candidate-mediated-sn-closure}, and the confluence refusal row of \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-audit-confluence-refusal-split} before candidate normalization is routed to any confluence-facing endpoint.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the normalization-audit carrier. The rows $R,C,S,A,N,F,H,T,P,L$ separate candidate evidence, closed projection, finite strong-normalization scope, application boundary, confluence refusal, replay, transport, provenance, and local naming.

Apply the candidate boundary and closed-term projection route. They force any normalization read to expose candidate evidence and checked closed projection before the finite SN inventory can be consumed.

Next apply the finite SN scope and candidate-mediated SN closure theorems. These rows keep the SN export inside the candidate ledger and retain the application boundary, so the handoff cannot convert candidate normalization into an evidence-free endpoint.

Finally use the confluence refusal split. The refusal row $N$ remains visible when the handoff approaches a confluence-facing consumer, excluding global confluence, unrestricted subject reduction, quotient syntax equality, and host evaluator evidence from the forwarded route.
\end{proof}
