\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate normalization confluence handoff public export]
\label{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-public-export}
The $\MetacicCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ packet may be exported publicly only as the finite route through $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$, $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$, $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$, the residual join budget, $\MetaCICDecidableBoundaryUp$, blocked-edge retention, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-carrier}. Its public rows are $A,K,N,F,C,D,B,T,R,P,L$, so a public export starts from the audit anchor, candidate evidence, bounded endpoint, frontier row, residual confluence row, decidable boundary, blocked edge, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

Next apply the residual budget and route-factorization surfaces, \autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-residual-budget} and \autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-route-factorization}. They force every residual join or parallel-diamond read to retain the bounded budget, decidable boundary, and blocked-edge rows rather than replacing them by a global confluence theorem.

Finally read $T,R,P,L$. Componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming preserve the finite route already displayed by the sibling theorems. Since no carrier coordinate stores global strong normalization, full Church--Rosser, unrestricted subject reduction, quotient syntax equality, evaluator equality, global conversion decidability, or closed consistency, the only public export is the stated finite route.
\end{proof}
