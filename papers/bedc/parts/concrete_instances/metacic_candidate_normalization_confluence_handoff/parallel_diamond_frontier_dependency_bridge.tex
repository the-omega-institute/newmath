\begin{theorem}[Parallel-diamond frontier dependency bridge]
\label{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-parallel-diamond-frontier-dependency-bridge}
Every accepted $\MetaCICCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ carrier reads the residual join route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-namecert} before it exports a normalization-confluence budget to a downstream candidate-normalization consumer.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The handoff carrier already exposes candidate-normalization, residual-join, and confluence-budget rows through its sibling route files. The parallel-diamond frontier is the only displayed sibling that licenses the residual join as a diamond-facing dependency rather than as an isolated normalization claim.

Thus the confluence budget is exported only after the residual join has passed through the parallel-diamond frontier route. No kernel-only or local-normalization row can stand in for that horizontal dependency.
\end{proof}
