\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate residual-budget discharge frontier]
\label{thm:metacic-candidate-residual-budget-discharge-frontier}
For every accepted $\MetacicCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ carrier
$$
H=(A,K,N,F,C,D,B,T,R,P,L),
$$
candidate evidence $K$, residual envelope $C$, decidable boundary $D$, and blocked-edge row $B$ remain visible before the endpoint row $N$ is treated as a residual join by $\MetaCICParallelDiamondFrontierUp$. The discharge frontier is therefore the finite route from the audit anchor $A$ through $K,F,C,D,B,T,R,P,L$ into the parallel-diamond consumer, not full Church--Rosser, unrestricted subject reduction, evaluator equality, quotient syntax equality, residual-substitution discharge, or kernel consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-carrier}. The rows $A,K,N,F,C,D,B,T,R,P,L$ display the audit anchor, candidate evidence, endpoint row, normalization frontier, residual envelope, decidable boundary, blocked edge, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Apply the boundary theorem \autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-boundary} and the premise-exposure theorem \autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-premise-exposure}. Together they keep $K,F,C,D,B$ attached to the same accepted packet before any confluence-facing endpoint read can treat $N$ as residual join data.

Finally read the parallel-diamond frontier sibling of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-namecert}. Its consumer may inspect the residual join only after the candidate, residual-envelope, decidable-boundary, and blocked-edge rows have remained visible through $T,R,P,L$.

The refused global conclusions are absent from the carrier and from this consumer route. Thus the discharge frontier records a candidate-mediated finite handoff rather than a global MetaCIC theorem.
\end{proof}
