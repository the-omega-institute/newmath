\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC candidate-normalization handoff forward link]
\label{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-forward-link}
Every $\MetaCICCandidateNormalizationConfluenceHandoffUp$ consumer that exports a residual join to MetaCIC normalization consumers must first display the parallel-diamond frontier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-namecert} or the residual-frontier route carried by that frontier. Only after the candidate evidence, residual-envelope row, decidable-boundary row, blocked-edge ledger, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local $\NameCert$ row have been read through that frontier may the confluence handoff be public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the handoff carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-carrier}. Its confluence-facing rows are candidate evidence, bounded endpoint data, residual envelope, decidable boundary, blocked-edge retention, and the structural transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

Use the residual-diamond retention theorem \autoref{thm:metacic-candidate-normalization-confluence-handoff-residual-diamond-retention}. It keeps candidate evidence, residual envelope, decidable boundary, and blocked-edge rows visible before any bounded endpoint is treated as joinable.

The sibling routes in this hub then point the retained residual data to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-parallel-diamond-frontier-namecert}. That frontier is the public place where the residual-frontier route and local parallel-diamond budget are displayed.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for a public confluence handoff detached from those rows, any consumer exporting a residual join must pass through the frontier route before the handoff is public.
\end{proof}
