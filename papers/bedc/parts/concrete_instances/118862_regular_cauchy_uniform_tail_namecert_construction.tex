\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy uniform tail carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-uniform-tail-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the finite family of precision requests whose common tail window is being certified;
\item $M$ is the displayed modulus-threshold row, naming one index bound that serves every request in $Q$;
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ tail-window row, listing exactly the stream observations at and beyond the threshold $M$ that are consumed by the packet;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for the dyadic comparison budgets attached to the requests in $Q$;
\item $T$ is the $\RegularCauchyTailEstimateUp$ row witnessing that the same threshold $M$ controls all later windows in $W$;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row reached from the uniform tail window and dyadic ledger;

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy uniform tail NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-uniform-tail-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-uniform-tail-carrier}. The projection exposes the finite request family $Q$, the common threshold $M$, the tail-window row $W$, the dyadic ledger $D$, the tail-estimate row $T$, the regular readback $R$, the real seal $E$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Classifier transport is componentwise. The rows in $H$ compare the request family, common threshold, tail window, dyadic ledger, tail estimate, readback, and seal on the same finite surface, while $C,P,N$ only replay, source, and name that surface.

Exactness is read from the displayed route. A precision request first enters $Q$, uses the shared threshold $M$, reads only the displayed windows in $W$, consumes the dyadic budget $D$, reaches the tail-estimate row $T$, then the regular readback $R$, and only then the real seal $E$.

The refusal rows are exhausted by the carrier inventory. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected representatives, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness, no local NameCert obligation can export those data.
\end{proof}

$$

$$
Thus the package stays inside the displayed $\RealUp$ seal route and does not assert ambient completeness, quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, or open-cover compactness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $U=(Q,M,W,D,T,R,E,H,C,P,N)$. The rows $Q$ and $M$ supply the finite precision family and the common threshold; no real-facing read is available at this stage.

Finally inspect $E,H,C,P,N$. The real seal $E$ is downstream of the displayed route, and the structural rows only transport, replay, source, and name that route.
