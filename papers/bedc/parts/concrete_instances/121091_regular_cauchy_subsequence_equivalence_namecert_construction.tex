\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchySubsequenceEquivalence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-subsequence-equivalence-namecert-obligations}

\item tolerance stability, requiring the dyadic ledger $D$ to witness the same requested precision for both windows;
