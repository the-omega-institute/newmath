Following Bishop and Bridges' finite approximation discipline for constructive completions, the $\FiniteSectionCompletionInterfaceUp$ packet records the finite request-section boundary at which a dyadic endpoint and tolerance row, a stream-name section, a regular rational readback, and a terminal Real seal can be cited together. It refines the four-face completion surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-real-completion-interface-namecert} by making the section pullback explicit over \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite NameCert interface for L10 completion consumers; it is not a quotient-stream construction, a selected limit, a countable-choice principle, host real equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or a checked bridge.

\begin{definition}[Finite section completion interface carrier]
\label{def:finite-section-completion-interface-carrier}
A $\FiniteSectionCompletionInterfaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(D,S,R,B,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint and tolerance row, recording the finite dyadic endpoint cells, scale bounds, and slack conversions inspected by the section.
\item $S$ is the finite request-section row over $\StreamNameUp$, listing the requested stream windows and the section coordinates through which a completion consumer reads them.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, exposing the regular rational data obtained from the section after the dyadic tolerance row and stream windows have been displayed.
\item $B$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, recording only the finite seal-facing boundary reached after $D,S,R$.
\item $Q$ is the section pullback and exactness row, requiring the requested stream section, dyadic tolerance cells, regular rational readback, and terminal seal row to agree componentwise on every displayed finite request.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for dyadic, section, readback, seal, exactness, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite completion request through $D,S,R,B,Q,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteSectionCompletionInterfaceUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\CauchyRealCompletionInterfaceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteSectionCompletionInterfaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier compares accepted packets only through $D,S,R,B,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite section completion interface NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-section-completion-interface-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteSectionCompletionInterfaceUp$ carrier
$$
I=(D,S,R,B,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteSectionCompletionInterfaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed finite $\BHist$ packet of \autoref{def:finite-section-completion-interface-carrier};
\item finite section coverage, requiring each requested $\StreamNameUp$ window in $S$ to be read against the displayed dyadic endpoint and tolerance row $D$;
\item $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff, requiring the regular rational readback $R$ to be exposed only through the already displayed $D,S$ section route and the exactness row $Q$;
\item $\RealUp$ seal nonescape, requiring the terminal seal row $B$ to be consumed only after $D,S,R,Q$ and refusing quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, and ambient completeness;
\item structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$, with no additional completion, quotient, choice, or bridge coordinate.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:finite-section-completion-interface-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the dyadic endpoint and tolerance row, finite request-section row, regular rational readback row, terminal Real seal row, section exactness row, and structural rows.

Finite coverage is rowwise. A completion request first reads $D$ and $S$; only then can $Q$ certify that the selected finite section agrees with the requested dyadic tolerance cells and stream windows.

The handoff to $R$ and the seal read $B$ are downstream of that exactness row. Since the carrier has no quotient-stream, selected-limit, choice, host-equality, ambient-completeness, or bridge coordinate, the terminal seal cannot escape the displayed finite section route.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. These structural rows preserve the finite route through $D,S,R,B,Q$ and add no further completion principle.
\end{proof}
