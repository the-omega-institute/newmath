\begin{theorem}[Cauchy real multiplication NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-real-multiplication-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyRealMultiplicationUp$ carrier
$$
M_{\Real,\times}=(R_0,R_1,W_0,W_1,D_0,D_1,D_\times,C_\times,S,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyRealMultiplicationUp}$ surface consists exactly of five finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the two factor rows, paired windows, dyadic ledgers, $\CauchyProductUp$ product row, product readback, $\RealUp$ seal, and structural rows;
\item factor classifier stability, requiring the two $\RegSeqRatUp$ factor rows and their $\StreamNameUp$ windows to transport with the same displayed $\hsame$ data;
\item $\CauchyProductUp$ regularity, requiring the product row to supply the product $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback before any Real-facing seal is visible;
\item Real-seal nonescape, requiring the multiplication seal $E$ to be downstream of $C_\times$ and $S$;
\item ledger exactness, requiring $D_0,D_1,D_\times$ to account for every source tolerance, product observation, and output tolerance used by the read.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-real-multiplication-carrier}. Its analytic rows are the two factor rows, two window rows, dyadic ledgers, product row, readback row, and seal row; its structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The factor rows are read first. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows $R_0,R_1$ and $\StreamNameUp$ rows $W_0,W_1$ fix the finite factor observations, while $D_0,D_1$ record the factor tolerance ledgers that travel with those observations.

The product route is downstream of that data. The row $C_\times$ is a $\CauchyProductUp$ read through the displayed windows and dyadic product ledger $D_\times$, and it supplies the product $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $S$ before the seal row $E$ can be consumed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no other coordinate appears in the carrier, the listed five obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface and exclude host multiplication, quotient equality, selected limits, trichotomy, and ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
