\begin{definition}[Apartness metric completion carrier]
\label{def:apartness-metric-completion-carrier}
An $\ApartnessMetricCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(M,Q,W,D,E,S,L,T,C,P,N).
$$
The rows have the following meanings:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row, carrying the displayed metric object and its local naming data.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-Cauchy name row used as the completion input.
\item $W$ is the finite stream-window row through which the Cauchy name is inspected.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger for metric distance, Cauchy regularity, apartness comparison, and limit-seal readback.
\item $E$ is the apartness witness row, recording the positive separatedness evidence available for the displayed comparison.
\item $S$ is the separated limit seal, reached only after $M,Q,W,D,E$ have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedRealUp$ readback row that exposes the Real-facing boundary of the separated seal.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, name, window, tolerance, apartness, seal, readback, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from any completion consumer through the same finite rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ApartnessMetricCompletionUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ApartnessMetricCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for quotient completion, equivalence classes of Cauchy sequences, selected representatives, metric trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Apartness metric completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:apartness-metric-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ApartnessMetricCompletionUp$ carrier
$$
A=(M,Q,W,D,E,S,L,T,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ApartnessMetricCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric source admission, regular-Cauchy name exposure, finite window readback, dyadic tolerance accounting, displayed apartness evidence, separated limit sealing, located real readback, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Any completion-facing consumer must read $M,Q,W,D,E$ before $S$ or $L$ can be treated as a completed boundary.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:apartness-metric-completion-carrier}. The rows $M,Q,W,D,E,S,L,T,C,P,N$ are the only rows available to the local certificate, so the obligation surface cannot contain hidden quotient, selection, or trichotomy data.

The completion source is displayed before the endpoint is sealed. Row $M$ supplies the metric source, row $Q$ supplies the regular-Cauchy name, row $W$ supplies finite observations, and row $D$ supplies the dyadic budget used by the Cauchy and distance comparisons.

The apartness and limit rows then preserve separation. Row $E$ records the positive apartness witness, $S$ seals the separated limit boundary, and $L$ exposes only the located real readback attached to that seal.

Finally $T,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no carrier coordinate stores quotient completion, selected representatives, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Apartness Cauchy limit seal]
\label{thm:apartness-metric-completion-apartness-cauchy-limit-seal}
Every accepted $\ApartnessMetricCompletionUp$ packet hands a regular-Cauchy completion read to downstream $\CompletionUp$, $\RealUp$, or $\LocatedRealUp$ consumers only through the finite route
$$

$$
The route preserves the displayed apartness witness and separated limit seal, but it does not export quotient equality of Cauchy names, selected limits, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient metric completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted carrier $A=(M,Q,W,D,E,S,L,T,C,P,N)$. By \autoref{thm:apartness-metric-completion-namecert-obligations}, every completion-facing read first exposes the metric source, regular-Cauchy name, finite windows, dyadic tolerance ledger, and apartness witness.

The separated endpoint is therefore reached only at $S$, with the located real readback $L$ downstream of the same rows. This is a finite seal: it is generated from displayed Cauchy and apartness evidence rather than from an equivalence class of all Cauchy names.

The structural rows $T,C,P,N$ preserve that seal under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Any request for quotient equality, a selected limit, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient completeness asks for a row absent from the packet, so it leaves the certified route.
\end{proof}
