\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CofiniteFilterUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cofinite-filter-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki and Kelley on filters, the $\CofiniteFilterUp$ packet records the tail-facing filter on a displayed stream by finite complement ledgers rather than by a host powerset object. It sits after the finite window route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the Cauchy seed carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the Frechet filter-basis neighbour of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-frechet-filter-basis-namecert}. The carrier is a traditional tail filter reconstructed as BEDC data: every accepted read displays the finite excluded prefix, the surviving tail window, the Cauchy and readback rows that consume the tail, structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

\begin{definition}[Cofinite filter carrier]
\label{def:cofinite-filter-carrier}
A $\CofiniteFilterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(S,B,T,C,R,H,Q,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ source whose finite windows are inspected.
\item $B$ is the finite complement ledger naming the excluded prefix.
\item $T$ is the surviving tail window generated by removing the entries listed in $B$.
\item $C$ is the $\CauchyUp$ consumer row that may read only the displayed tail window.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same tail.
\item $H$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,B,T,C,R$.
\item $Q$ is displayed $\Cont$ replay for the tail-filter route.
\item $P$ is $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CofiniteFilterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\CauchyUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\FrechetFilterBasisUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CofiniteFilterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinite filter tail stability]
\label{thm:cofinite-filter-tail-stability}
Let $F=(S,B,T,C,R,H,Q,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CofiniteFilterUp$ carrier. Any downstream read that refines the excluded finite prefix must produce a tail window still routed through the same $\StreamNameUp$, $\CauchyUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ rows. Thus cofinite-tail stability is a finite refinement of $B$ and $T$, not a host assertion about arbitrary complements or a quotient filter equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier in \autoref{def:cofinite-filter-carrier}. The only data that can change under tail refinement are the finite complement ledger $B$ and the displayed survivor window $T$.

The stream, Cauchy, and regular-readback rows remain fixed as coordinates of the same packet. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport keeps the refined prefix and tail inside the original $\BHist$ route, while $\Cont$ replay and $\Pkg$ provenance expose the same local naming row. Since no coordinate carries a host powerset complement, quotient filter, selected cofinal subsequence, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness principle, the refinement is exactly the displayed tail-stability witness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinite filter Cauchy-window handoff]
\label{thm:cofinite-filter-cauchy-window-handoff}
Every $\CauchyUp$ or $\RegSeqRatUp$ consumer of an accepted $\CofiniteFilterUp$ carrier receives the finite handoff
$$
\StreamNameUp,\ \CofiniteFilterUp,\ \CauchyUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \FrechetFilterBasisUp,\ \BHist,\ \hsame,\ \Cont,\ \Pkg,\ \NameCert .
$$
The handoff identifies the cofinite tail with the displayed StreamName window consumed by Cauchy and regular-rational rows. It does not export a quotient of filters, a selected eventual index, a completed stream, countable choice, host real equality, or an ambient Real-completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the tail row through \autoref{thm:cofinite-filter-tail-stability}. The finite complement ledger and surviving tail window are already downstream of the $\StreamNameUp$ source and upstream of the Cauchy and regular-readback rows.

The Frechet filter-basis neighbour supplies the traditional filter reading, but the present packet exposes only the finite complement and tail rows. The Cauchy and RegSeqRat consumers therefore receive $S,B,T,C,R$ with structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming attached. The excluded quotient, selected-index, completed-stream, choice, host-equality, and completeness objects have no carrier coordinate from which to enter the handoff.
\end{proof}
