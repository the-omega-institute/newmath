\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy convex combination carrier]

\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the coefficient row, recording a rational coefficient $q$ together with the dyadic window in which the complementary coefficient $1-q$ is computed.
\item $X$ and $Y$ are the two $\RegSeqRatUp$ source rows.
\item $W_X$ and $W_Y$ are synchronized $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedules for the two source names, read at the same precision request and observation index.
\item $D_Q$ and $D_{\bar Q}$ are the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint rows for $q$ and $1-q$ at the common exponent used by the packet.
\item $S_X$ is the $\RegularCauchyScaleUp$ row for the scaled source $q x$, reading $Q,X,W_X,D_Q$ and its scale-error ledger.
\item $S_Y$ is the $\RegularCauchyScaleUp$ row for the scaled source $(1-q)y$, reading the complementary coefficient row, $Y,W_Y,D_{\bar Q}$, and its scale-error ledger.
\item $\Sigma$ is the $\RegularCauchySumUp$ row that adds the two scaled regular names.
\item $E$ is the finite error-budget ledger combining the two scale ledgers, the sum tolerance, the complementary-coefficient tolerance, and the final output tolerance assigned to the convex readback.

\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transports for the coefficient, source rows, schedules, dyadic endpoint rows, scale rows, sum row, ledger, readback, Real seal, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay routes for coefficient exposure, source-window exposure, the two scale reads, the sum read, the ledger read, the regular readback, and the Real seal handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyConvexCombinationUp$, $\RegularCauchyAffineCombinationUp$, $\RegularCauchyMidpointConvexUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyConvexCombinationUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed rows $Q,X,Y,W_X,W_Y,D_Q,D_{\bar Q},S_X,S_Y,\Sigma,E,R,Z,H,C,P,N$.
