Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\RegularCauchyMajorantUp$ packet names the finite majorant certificate used when a regular Cauchy rational route needs a displayed bound before entering the L10 completion readback. It sits between the dyadic tolerance arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation schedules of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the explicit majorant siblings \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-majorant-sequence-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-tail-majorant-namecert}, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet records a finite majorant row for regular Cauchy completion consumers; it does not import quotient completion, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy majorant carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-majorant-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyMajorantUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule naming the inspected regular Cauchy requests;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger against which the requested error bounds are checked;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source row whose rational tail is read only through $S$ and $D$;
\item $J$ is the majorant row assigning a finite bound to each displayed tolerance window;
\item $T$ is the tail-majorant comparison row, relating $J$ to the sibling tail-majorant service of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-tail-majorant-namecert};
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ completion-seal row available only after the regular tail and its majorant row have been displayed;

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,D,Q,J,T,E,H,C,P,N$ and their displayed transport and replay rows. It has no coordinate for a quotient-completion object, a selected limit, a hidden tail selector, countable choice, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy majorant NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-majorant-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyMajorantUp$ carrier
$$
M=(S,D,Q,J,T,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyMajorantUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite-window exposure through $S$, dyadic tolerance exposure through $D$, regular rational source exposure through $Q$, majorant readability through $J$, tail-majorant comparison through $T$, Real-seal ordering through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-majorant-carrier}. The displayed tuple is exactly $S,D,Q,J,T,E,H,C,P,N$, so every accepted packet exposes the finite windows, dyadic tolerances, regular rational source, majorant row, tail comparison, Real seal, and structural rows before a completion consumer can read it.

Read the analytic rows in order. The schedule row $S$ names the inspected windows, the dyadic row $D$ supplies the tolerance data, and the regular rational source row $Q$ is read only through those finite rows.

The majorant rows then close the estimate route. The row $J$ gives the finite bound, while $T$ compares it to the sibling tail-majorant service; the seal row $E$ is downstream of that displayed comparison and not an independent limit.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion, selected limits, hidden tail choice, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy majorant completion handoff]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-majorant-completion-handoff}

$$

\end{theorem}
