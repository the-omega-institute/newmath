$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $K$ is the classifier row assigning admitted changes to the same-force-pattern surface.
\item $V$ is the preserved-structure row, recording the relation data stable under the admitted classifier.
\item $W$ is the not-preserved row, recording empirical constants, numerical laws, global-field readings, and physical semantics not carried by the records-side packet.

\item $E$ is the exactness row stating when classifier sameness reflects the displayed inter-$\Hist$ relation rather than object identity.

\label{thm:force-like-relation-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The certificate then reads preservation and refusal together. The preserved row $V$ is bounded by the not-preserved row $W$, while the layer map $M$ and exactness row $E$ are bounded by the failure row $B$.

Transport, replay, provenance, and naming are exhausted by $H,C,P,N$. Thus the local certificate has exactly the displayed relation rows and no hidden physics coordinate.
