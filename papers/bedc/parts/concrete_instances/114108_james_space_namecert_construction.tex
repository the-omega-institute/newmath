\begin{definition}[James-space carrier]
\label{def:james-space-carrier}
A $\JamesSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(B,L,Q,R,V,M,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the basis-block ledger. Each row names a finite interval of the canonical unit-vector basis, its ordered endpoints, and the finite coefficient support inspected inside that block.
\item $L$ is the block-link ledger. It records which displayed blocks are read consecutively and which rows remain disjoint, so a consumer can replay the James block pattern without an ambient infinite choice of blocks.
\item $Q$ is the norm-budget ledger. Each row records a finite family of consecutive block sums, a rational or dyadic budget for the squared block-sum magnitudes, and the comparison needed for the James norm inspection.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the coefficients and norm budgets.
\item $V$ is the $\VecSpaceUp$ handoff row, recording finite support addition and scalar multiplication over the displayed coefficient rows.
\item $M$ is the $\NormedSpaceUp$ and $\BanachUp$ handoff row. It names the normed carrier, the Cauchy-completion route, and the Banach-facing certificate surface consumed by downstream space arguments.
\item $A$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for every displayed norm-budget comparison reached from $Q$ through $R$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the basis-block ledger, block-link ledger, norm-budget ledger, rational readback, vector-space handoff, normed/Banach handoff, real seals, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the carrier through the same finite block rows and norm-budget rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\JamesSpaceUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\NormedSpaceUp$, $\BanachUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\JamesSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $B,L,Q,R,V,M,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an external Banach-space parameter, an infinite block selector, a dual-space presentation, reflexivity analysis, compactness, quotient equality, or host completeness beyond the displayed $\BanachUp$ handoff.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[James-space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:james-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\JamesSpaceUp$ carrier $J=(B,L,Q,R,V,M,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\JamesSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of basis-block admission through $B$, consecutive-block coverage through $L$, norm-budget admission through $Q$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, $\VecSpaceUp$ finite-support handoff through $V$, $\NormedSpaceUp$ and $\BanachUp$ handoff through $M$, $\RealUp$ norm-seal admission through $A$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

No James-space obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:james-space-carrier}. The accepted packet is exhausted by the displayed basis-block rows, the consecutive-block ledger, the norm-budget rows, the rational readback, the vector/normed/Banach handoffs, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

The block side is finite. The rows $B$ and $L$ name only inspected basis intervals and their displayed adjacency data, so a consumer can replay the block pattern without selecting an infinite block family.

The norm side is also finite. The row $Q$ records each inspected family of consecutive block sums together with its rational budget, while $R$ reads those budgets through $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $A$ seals the resulting comparisons through $\RealUp$.

Finally $V$ and $M$ carry the same rows to $\VecSpaceUp$, $\NormedSpaceUp$, and $\BanachUp$ consumers. The transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows preserve the displayed packet componentwise and cannot introduce any excluded coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[James-space Banach route]
\label{thm:james-space-banach-route}
Every $\BanachUp$-facing read of an accepted $\JamesSpaceUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
B,L,Q \longmapsto R,A \longmapsto V,M,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the Banach-space construction is presented by finite basis-block rows, norm-budget rows, rational readback, real norm seals, vector/normed/Banach handoffs, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming, rather than by an opaque ambient Banach parameter.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a Banach-facing consumer. The carrier first exposes $B$ and $L$, so the consumer sees the inspected canonical-basis blocks and their displayed consecutive-link data before any normed-space handoff is available.

Read the norm route next. The row $Q$ supplies exactly the finite James norm-budget comparisons under inspection, $R$ translates the coefficient and budget data through $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $A$ seals the resulting real-valued norm comparisons through $\RealUp$.

The vector and Banach handoffs are downstream of those rows. The row $V$ supplies the finite-support $\VecSpaceUp$ operations, while $M$ records the $\NormedSpaceUp$ and $\BanachUp$ handoff over the same displayed norm data. Since $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the already displayed packet, the route cannot replace the finite ledger by an external Banach parameter.
\end{proof}
