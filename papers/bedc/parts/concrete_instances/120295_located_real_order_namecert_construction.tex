\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedRealOrderUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-located-real-order-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on located order for constructive real numbers, the $\LocatedRealOrderUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which order-facing real comparisons are read from displayed regular Cauchy windows. It sits over the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the comparison row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchysequenceequivalence-namecert}. The packet exposes located interval and apartness evidence as finite L10 data; it does not import trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or bridge checking.

\begin{definition}[Located real order carrier]
\label{def:located-real-order-carrier}
A $\LocatedRealOrderUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(D,W,S,R,Q,I,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ observation and tolerance ledger used by all order-facing comparisons.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule that selects the displayed rational approximants.
\item $S$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row over the same windows and dyadic budget.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,W,S$ have been displayed.
\item $Q$ is the $\CauchySequenceEquivalenceUp$ comparison row used to keep paired real names on the same finite comparison route.
\item $I$ is the located interval row, recording a finite dyadic bracket or finite separation of brackets for the requested order comparison.
\item $A$ is the apartness row, recording displayed positive separation when a strict order read is exported.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,W,S,R,Q,I,A$ and for the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an order-facing request through the dyadic, window, readback, seal, comparison, interval, apartness, transport, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedRealOrderUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchySequenceEquivalenceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedRealOrderUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier reads accepted packets only through $D,W,S,R,Q,I,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for real trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, ambient real completeness, or an external bridge object.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located real order NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-real-order-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedRealOrderUp$ carrier
$$
O=(D,W,S,R,Q,I,A,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedRealOrderUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the dyadic tolerance ledger, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, terminal real seal, Cauchy-sequence comparison row, located interval evidence, apartness evidence, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every order-facing consumer must expose $D,W,S,R,Q$ before it can consume $I,A,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-real-order-carrier}. The admitted packet contains exactly the numerical rows $D,W,S,R,Q,I,A$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

The entry route is $D,W,S,R,Q$. The dyadic ledger fixes the tolerance budget, the stream schedule fixes the finite windows, the regular rational row supplies the readback, the real row supplies the terminal seal, and the Cauchy-sequence comparison row keeps paired names on one finite route.

The order route is $I,A,H,C,P,N$. The located interval row and apartness row are consumed only after the real and comparison rows are visible; the transport and replay rows preserve the same displayed route; the provenance and naming rows record the local certificate.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, ambient real completeness, or bridge checking, those endpoints cannot be exported by the local $\NameCert$ surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located real order apartness handoff]
\label{thm:located-real-order-apartness-handoff}
Let
$$
O=(D,W,S,R,Q,I,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
be an accepted $\LocatedRealOrderUp$ carrier. Any strict order or apartness read exported from $O$ must pass through the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger $D$, $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $S$, terminal $\RealUp$ seal $R$, and $\CauchySequenceEquivalenceUp$ comparison row $Q$ before the apartness row $A$ is consumed. The read exports the finite packet $D,W,S,R,Q,I,A,H,C,P,N$ and no trichotomy decision, quotient equality, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, ambient completeness theorem, or bridge certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:located-real-order-namecert-obligations}. They force every order-facing consumer to expose the dyadic, window, readback, real-seal, and comparison rows before any interval or apartness evidence is read.

The strict order witness is the row $A$. It is not an independent sign oracle: it is attached to the located interval row $I$ and therefore to the same displayed dyadic budget and finite windows used by $D,W,S,R,Q$.

The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that same packet. Because none of the excluded host-level objects is a carrier coordinate, the apartness handoff remains a finite L10 route rather than a trichotomy or quotient-equality principle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located real order interval route]
\label{thm:located-real-order-interval-route}
Every interval comparison consumed through an accepted $\LocatedRealOrderUp$ packet factors through
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto
\RealUp \longmapsto \CauchySequenceEquivalenceUp \longmapsto I,A .
$$
Thus the consumer receives a located interval or displayed apartness read over one finite comparison route. It receives no quotient real equality, selected representative, trichotomy split, countable-choice stream, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness object, or checked bridge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the numerical side through \autoref{def:located-real-order-carrier}. The only tolerance source is $D$, the only finite schedule is $W$, and the only regular rational readback is $S$.

The terminal real row $R$ is reached only after that finite route is displayed. The comparison row $Q$ then aligns paired Cauchy presentations through the same finite windows before an interval read is accepted.

The rows $I$ and $A$ supply the interval and apartness evidence. They are therefore downstream of $D,W,S,R,Q$, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve the route as one packet.

Consequently the interval route is exactly the displayed finite chain. The excluded objects would require additional carrier coordinates, so they cannot be read from an accepted $\LocatedRealOrderUp$ packet.
\end{proof}
