Following Neyman and Pearson, the $\LikelihoodRatioTestUp$ packet records a finite decision ledger for comparing a displayed null hypothesis against a displayed alternative through likelihood-score rows. It sits after the hypothesis surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hypothesis-namecert}, the estimator ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-estimator-namecert}, the Fisher-information score route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fisherinfo-namecert}, the Bayesian likelihood row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bayesian-namecert}, and the probability source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Likelihood-ratio test carrier]\label{def:likelihood-ratio-test-carrier}
A $\LikelihoodRatioTestUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(H_0,H_1,L,\tau,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $H_0$ is the null-hypothesis row, read through the $\HypothesisUp$ carrier and attached to the displayed $\ProbSpaceUp$ source.
\item $H_1$ is the alternative-hypothesis row, read through the same hypothesis and probability-source surface.
\item $L$ is the finite likelihood-score ledger, carrying the inspected observation windows, likelihood comparisons, estimator readbacks, Fisher-information score rows when present, and Bayesian likelihood row when present.
\item $\tau$ is the threshold row, displaying the finite critical comparison requested by the test.
\item $D$ is the decision row, recording the accepted local decision after $H_0,H_1,L,\tau$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the null hypothesis, alternative hypothesis, likelihood ledger, threshold, decision, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the probability source and hypothesis rows through the likelihood ledger and threshold to the decision row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LikelihoodRatioTestUp$, $\HypothesisUp$, $\EstimatorUp$, $\FisherInfoUp$, $\BayesianUp$, $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LikelihoodRatioTestUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $H_0,H_1,L,\tau,D,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for asymptotic power, uniformly most powerful optimality, posterior odds, selected rejection regions, ambient measure completion, or host probability choice.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Likelihood-ratio test NameCert obligations]\label{thm:likelihood-ratio-test-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LikelihoodRatioTestUp$ carrier $T=(H_0,H_1,L,\tau,D,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LikelihoodRatioTestUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the null hypothesis, alternative hypothesis, likelihood-score ledger, threshold, decision, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item hypothesis-source stability, requiring $H_0$ and $H_1$ to remain attached to the same $\ProbSpaceUp$ source and to the hypothesis carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hypothesis-namecert};
\item likelihood-ledger stability, requiring every score comparison in $L$ to be read through the displayed estimator, Fisher-information, Bayesian likelihood, or probability-source row named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-estimator-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fisherinfo-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bayesian-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert};
\item threshold stability, requiring $\tau$ to be displayed before the decision row $D$ is consumed;
\item decision nonescape, requiring $D$ to export only the finite likelihood-ratio decision and not asymptotic optimality, posterior odds, selected critical regions, host measure completion, or measure-theoretic choice;
\item componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
These obligations certify only the finite statistical decision ledger.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:likelihood-ratio-test-carrier}. Its visible rows are exactly the two hypothesis rows, likelihood-score ledger, threshold, decision, and structural rows.

Hypothesis-source stability is read from $H_0$ and $H_1$. Both rows point back to the displayed hypothesis carrier and probability source, so the test does not compare hypotheses from unrelated sources.

Likelihood-ledger stability is read from $L$. Each score comparison is attached to displayed observation, estimator, Fisher-information, Bayesian likelihood, or probability-source rows before the threshold can be applied.

The threshold and decision rows are downstream. The row $\tau$ supplies the finite comparison boundary, and $D$ records the resulting local decision only after the ledger has been displayed.

Rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no additional coordinate stores asymptotic optimality, posterior odds, selected critical regions, host measure completion, or choice, the listed obligations exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Likelihood-ratio test finite-ledger threshold stability]\label{thm:likelihood-ratio-test-finite-ledger-threshold-stability}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Use componentwise $\hsame$ transport. The null and alternative hypotheses are compared through the displayed hypothesis and probability-source rows, the likelihood ledgers are compared through their finite score entries, and the threshold rows are compared before either decision row is read.

The replay and provenance rows preserve the same route to the local decision. Because neither carrier contains an asymptotic optimality theorem, posterior-odds row, selected critical region, host measure completion, or choice principle, the transported threshold decision is the same finite ledger decision.
\end{proof}
