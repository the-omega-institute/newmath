\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy repletion boundary carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-repletion-boundary-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyRepletionBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(S,R,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation schedule read by the boundary.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback attached to those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger recording precision scales, comparison bounds, and displayed dyadic thresholds.
\item $Q$ is the $\CauchyRepletionUp$ corridor row through which the boundary receives completion-facing repletion data.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row exposed only after $S,R,D,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the stream schedule, regular readback, dyadic ledger, repletion corridor, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from finite windows to regular readback, from readback through the dyadic ledger, from the dyadic ledger into the repletion corridor, and from the corridor to the terminal seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyRepletionBoundaryUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\CauchyRepletionUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyRepletionBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $S,R,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected host limits, countable-choice schedules, trichotomy, host real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy repletion boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-repletion-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyRepletionBoundaryUp$ carrier $B=(S,R,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyRepletionBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, finite-window exposure, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance routing, Cauchy repletion corridor admission, terminal Real seal exposure, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every boundary consumer must display the $\StreamNameUp$ row $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row $D$, $\CauchyRepletionUp$ row $Q$, $\RealUp$ seal row $E$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$ before it reads the packet as repleted regular Cauchy data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-repletion-boundary-carrier}. The nine displayed rows are the only classifier-visible coordinates, so carrier admission and local naming are row projections.

The analytic obligations are the four displayed source steps. The stream schedule $S$ supplies finite observations, $R$ reads those observations as regular rational data, $D$ records the dyadic tolerances used by the readback, and $Q$ is the only repletion-corridor coordinate available before the seal-facing row can be read.

The structural rows preserve the same packet. The transport row $H$ moves each displayed coordinate by $\hsame$, the replay row $C$ records the $\Cont$ route through the window, readback, dyadic, repletion, and seal rows, and $P$ records the $\Pkg$ provenance for the local $\NameCert$ row $N$. Since no other classifier coordinate exists, the listed obligations exhaust the local surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Window repletion boundary]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-repletion-boundary-window-repletion}
Let $B=(S,R,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RegularCauchyRepletionBoundaryUp$ carrier. Any completion-facing read of the terminal row $E$ factors through the finite window row $S$, the regular readback row $R$, the dyadic tolerance row $D$, and the $\CauchyRepletionUp$ corridor row $Q$; no consumer can read $E$ from $S$ or $R$ alone.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the displayed replay row $C$. Its route is ordered from $S$ to $R$, from $R$ through $D$, from $D$ into $Q$, and from $Q$ to $E$, so the seal-facing read is a replay of the whole boundary rather than a direct projection from a stream or readback row.

Transport does not change the dependency order. The row $H$ only supplies componentwise $\hsame$ transport of the same displayed packet, and $P,N$ only record provenance and naming. Thus a consumer reading $E$ through the local $\NameCert$ must pass through the repletion row $Q$ after the finite window, readback, and dyadic rows have been displayed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy repletion boundary non-escape]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-repletion-boundary-nonescape}
Every accepted $\RegularCauchyRepletionBoundaryUp$ carrier is insensitive to quotient equality of streams, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness. The only seal-facing information exported to a consumer is the displayed route through $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and the local $\NameCert$ rows named in \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-repletion-boundary-carrier}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the carrier coordinates. The tuple contains $S,R,D,Q,E,H,C,P,N$ and no row for quotient stream equality, a chosen host limit, a countable-choice schedule, trichotomy, or an ambient completeness principle.

It remains to check that the structural rows cannot add a hidden source. The $\hsame$ row $H$ transports only displayed coordinates, the $\Cont$ row $C$ replays only the boundary route already present in the carrier, and the $\Pkg$ row $P$ records provenance for the local $\NameCert$ row $N$. Therefore any exported seal-facing fact is confined to the finite boundary rows.
\end{proof}
