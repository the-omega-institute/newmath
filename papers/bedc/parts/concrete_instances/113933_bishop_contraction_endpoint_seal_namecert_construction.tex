It sits after the interval-contraction route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-interval-contraction-namecert}, the general contraction-mapping surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-contractionmapping-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the stability consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-fixed-point-stability-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Bishop contraction endpoint-seal carrier]
\label{def:bishop-contraction-endpoint-seal-carrier}
A $\BishopContractionEndpointSealUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(I,F,Q,R,X,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the source $\BishopIntervalContractionUp$ row exposing the located interval, contraction witness, dyadic shrinkage ledger, finite windows, regular-rational readback, and Real seal already certified by the interval-contraction packet.
\item $F$ is the $\ContractionMappingUp$ row recording that the same displayed map is read as a contraction before an endpoint consumer may inspect it.
\item $Q$ is the endpoint-request row, naming the requested fixed-point endpoint handoff and the shrinkage budget that routes it to the readback row.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the interval-contraction windows and endpoint request.
\item $X$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ endpoint seal consumed by fixed-point consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $I,F,Q,R,X,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the endpoint request through $I,F,Q,R,X,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopContractionEndpointSealUp$, $\BishopIntervalContractionUp$, $\ContractionMappingUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BanachFixedPointStabilityUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopContractionEndpointSealUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a selected fixed point, quotient real equality, a host convergence theorem, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop contraction endpoint-seal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-contraction-endpoint-seal-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopContractionEndpointSealUp$ carrier $E=(I,F,Q,R,X,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopContractionEndpointSealUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed interval-contraction row, contraction-mapping row, endpoint request, regular rational readback, Real endpoint seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; contraction-route compatibility between $I$ and $F$; endpoint-budget exactness through $Q$ and $R$; Real-seal ordering through $X$; and downstream stability handoff for $\BanachFixedPointStabilityUp$ consumers. No NameCert obligation is stored outside $I,F,Q,R,X,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:bishop-contraction-endpoint-seal-carrier}. The tuple lists the interval-contraction source, contraction-mapping row, endpoint request, regular readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name as the only available coordinates.

Contraction-route compatibility reads $I$ and $F$ together. The $\BishopIntervalContractionUp$ row supplies the located interval, dyadic shrinkage ledger, finite windows, and readback route, while the $\ContractionMappingUp$ row records the displayed map as the contraction consumed by the endpoint request.

Endpoint-budget exactness is then read through $Q$ and $R$. The request row names the endpoint budget, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row receives only the scheduled readback exported by the interval-contraction packet.

Finally the Real handoff row $X$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route for $\BanachFixedPointStabilityUp$ consumers. Since the carrier contains no selected fixed point, quotient real equality, host convergence theorem, countable-choice row, or ambient Real-completeness coordinate, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop contraction endpoint-seal Real handoff]
\label{thm:bishop-contraction-endpoint-seal-real-handoff}
Every fixed-point stability consumer that reads the terminal $\RealUp$ endpoint seal $X$ from an accepted $\BishopContractionEndpointSealUp$ carrier $E=(I,F,Q,R,X,H,C,P,N)$ must first read
$$
\BishopIntervalContractionUp \longmapsto \ContractionMappingUp \longmapsto Q \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
through the displayed rows $I,F,Q,R,X$, and then read the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ for $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ naming. The handoff may be consumed by $\BanachFixedPointStabilityUp$, but it exports no selected fixed point, quotient completion, host Banach theorem, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the endpoint request. The carrier exposes the source interval-contraction route only through $I$ and the displayed contraction row only through $F$, so a consumer cannot begin from an external fixed-point theorem or an unrecorded self-map.

Read the endpoint budget through $Q$. By the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:bishop-contraction-endpoint-seal-namecert-obligations}, this budget is the only route from the contraction data to the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$.

The terminal Real endpoint seal is then $X$. It is downstream of $I,F,Q,R$, so a fixed-point stability consumer receives a displayed regular-rational-to-Real handoff rather than a chosen limit or quotient real equality.

Finally replay the route through $H,C,P,N$. Componentwise transport, displayed continuation, provenance, and local naming preserve the same endpoint-seal packet before $\BanachFixedPointStabilityUp$ consumes it, and no excluded host fixed-point or completeness data is present in the carrier.
\end{proof}
