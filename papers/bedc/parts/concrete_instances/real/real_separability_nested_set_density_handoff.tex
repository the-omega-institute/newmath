\begin{theorem}[RealSeparability nested-set density handoff]
\label{thm:real-separability-nested-set-density-handoff}
Every nested located closed-set packet over $\RealUp$ that consumes rational density must expose the $\RealSeparabilityUp$ countable dense row, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ approximation row, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal before it passes diameter-modulus data to completion consumers. In particular, the handoff to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-nested-closed-set-principle-namecert} is routed through the L10 source chain of \autoref{thm:real-separability-l10-source-route} and the countable-dense handshake of \autoref{thm:real-separability-countable-dense-cauchy-handshake}; it exports no sequential compactness, open-cover compactness, countable-choice selector, quotient stream equality, or ambient completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First read the density source through \autoref{thm:real-separability-l10-source-route}. It forces a density consumer to expose finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows before the rational density row is consumed.

Next use \autoref{thm:real-separability-countable-dense-cauchy-handshake}. The handshake routes the countable dense surface through $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal before any downstream consumer treats density as Real-facing data.

For a nested located closed-set packet, the diameter-modulus row is downstream of the same finite density surface. Thus the completion-facing handoff can pass only the displayed countable dense row, dyadic approximation row, regular rational readback row, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Since none of these rows contains sequential compactness, open-cover compactness, countable choice, quotient stream equality, or ambient completeness, the nested-set consumer cannot export any of those claims.
\end{proof}
