\begin{theorem}[RealSeparability countable dense choice-free filter]
\label{thm:real-separability-countable-dense-choice-free-filter}
An accepted $\RealSeparabilityUp$ dense read is exported as a countable dense filter only after the dyadic rational candidate row, the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regularity readback, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal are all displayed. The route exports the countable dense filter ledger carried by those rows and exports no selected representative, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, countable-choice selector, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted separability carrier to its finite density rows. The dyadic-density dependency of \autoref{thm:real-separability-dyadic-density-dependency} supplies the dyadic rational candidate and tolerance ledger through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, so the dense read begins from displayed rational data rather than from a selected host representative.

Next use the countable dense route of \autoref{lem:real-separability-countable-dense-route}. Its schedule is finite-window data, and the $\StreamNameUp$ row records exactly the windows inspected by the density ledger.

Finally apply the RegSeqRat pullback of \autoref{thm:real-separability-regseqrat-density-pullback} and the countable-dense seal route immediately above this theorem. These bind the regularity readback and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal before the filter is public. Since no row in the chain carries quotient stream equality, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the exported object is precisely the displayed countable dense filter ledger.
\end{proof}
