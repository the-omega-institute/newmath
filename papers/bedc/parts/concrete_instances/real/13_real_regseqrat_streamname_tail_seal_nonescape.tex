\begin{theorem}[RealUp RegSeqRat StreamName tail seal nonescape]
\label{thm:realup-regseqrat-streamname-tail-seal-nonescape}
Let a finite $\RealUp$ seal source be accepted by the RegSeqRat--StreamName
handoff. Every tail, reindexing, completion-facing, or stream-facing consumer
of that source still factors through the same finite packet of rows:
the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ observation rows, the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window
classifier rows, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regularity spine rows, the $\RealUp$
endpoint and readback rows, and the displayed $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$,
$\mathsf{Pkg}$, and $\NameCert$ coordinates.

The tail-seal consumer therefore has no coordinate for quotient carriers,
host equality, ambient completeness, arithmetic or order packages,
metric-completeness rows, or selected representatives. Any accepted tail or
completion read is a read of the same finite seal source surface, not a route
to an external completion object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First identify the accepted finite seal source. Apply
\autoref{thm:realup-regseqrat-streamname-seal-handoff}. It exposes exactly the
stream-name classifier row, transported unary classifier row, certified
endpoint carriers, finite-window ledger, and seal/readback provenance that a
tail or completion-facing consumer can read.

Next apply \autoref{thm:realup-open-phase-seal-exactness}. Exactness factors
that accepted source through one finite $\mathsf{BHist}$ packet with the four
faces: dyadic observations, stream-name finite-window rows, RegSeqRat
regularity-spine rows, and RealUp endpoint/readback rows. The displayed
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\mathsf{Pkg}$, and $\NameCert$ coordinates are the only
structural coordinates attached to the factorization.

Finally use
\autoref{thm:realup-open-phase-four-face-consumer-totality}. Tail consumers,
reindexing consumers, completion-facing consumers, and stream-facing consumers
are accepted only when their reads pass through this same four-face packet.
Since the packet contains no quotient carrier, host equality, ambient
completeness, arithmetic or order package, metric-completeness row, or
selected representative, none of those coordinates can be exported by a
tail-seal read.
\end{proof}
