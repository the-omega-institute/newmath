\begin{theorem}[Real separability dyadic density scope]
\label{thm:real-separability-dyadic-density-scope}
Every $\RealSeparabilityUp$ density read that consumes a dyadic rational witness factors through the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ numerator and exponent rows, the finite observation windows of $\StreamNameUp$, the regular readback surface of $\RegSeqRatUp$, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The route exports a scoped finite density witness only after these four surfaces have been read, and it exports no selected representative, quotient stream, host-countable enumeration, nearest-dyadic selector, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the dyadic-density dependency and induction routes. Theorem~\ref{thm:real-separability-dyadic-density-dependency} places each density request behind the dyadic tolerance ledger, while \autoref{thm:real-separability-dyadic-density-induction} keeps each refinement step inside a finite dyadic window.

Next read the stream and regular-rational routes. The finite $\StreamNameUp$ observation window records which prefixes are inspected, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ dependency route supplies the regular rational readback before the separability ledger is exported.

Finally compose with the forward dyadic-density link and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The resulting route is the single finite chain
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp
$$
inside the $\RealSeparabilityUp$ packet, so no selected representative, quotient stream, host-countable enumeration, nearest-dyadic selector, or ambient completeness theorem can be recovered from the accepted density read.
\end{proof}
