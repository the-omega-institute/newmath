\label{thm:finite-cauchy-density-net-real-separability-link}
Let $N=(D_{\mathrm{net}},W_{\mathrm{net}},R_{\mathrm{net}},E_{\mathrm{net}},M_{\mathrm{net}},T_{\mathrm{net}},C_{\mathrm{net}},P_{\mathrm{net}},L_{\mathrm{net}})$ be an accepted $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ packet over dyadic and regular-Cauchy windows, and let
$$
S=(Q,D,W,R,E,T,H,C,P,N_S)
$$
be the $\RealSeparabilityUp$ density carrier whose finite observation request reads the same dyadic mesh, finite stream window, regular-Cauchy readback, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal. Then the finite density-net certificate induces the finite observation row needed by $\RealSeparabilityUp$: $D_{\mathrm{net}},W_{\mathrm{net}},R_{\mathrm{net}},E_{\mathrm{net}},M_{\mathrm{net}}$ supply the dyadic witness family, finite window, regular readback, Real seal, and precision budget for $Q,D,W,R,E$, while $T_{\mathrm{net}},C_{\mathrm{net}},P_{\mathrm{net}},L_{\mathrm{net}}$ transport, replay, source, and name the same separability route. Classifier stability is inherited from regular-Cauchy tail equivalence and dyadic mesh refinement, so the induced observation row exports no host countability assumption, selected dense point, quotient stream equality, trichotomy branch, or ambient completeness witness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First project the finite density-net packet. Its rows display the dyadic net ledger, finite StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, and precision budget before any density read is exported.

Next align those rows with the $\RealSeparabilityUp$ carrier. The rational enumeration and dyadic tolerance rows $Q,D$ consume the net ledger and budget, the finite window $W$ consumes $W_{\mathrm{net}}$, and the regular readback and Real seal rows $R,E$ consume $R_{\mathrm{net}},E_{\mathrm{net}}$.

The classifier comparison is stable because regular-Cauchy tail equivalence preserves the readback row, while dyadic mesh refinement preserves the finite net ledger and its tolerance budget. The transport and replay rows of the density-net packet therefore induce the transport and replay required by the separability carrier.

Finally apply the finite-density exhaustion route for $\RealSeparabilityUp$. The separability read receives only the displayed finite observation rows and their provenance. Since neither packet contains host countability, a selected dense point, quotient stream equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness, the induced observation row cannot export any of those surfaces.
\end{proof}
