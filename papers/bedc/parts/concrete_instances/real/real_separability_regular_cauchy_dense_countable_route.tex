\begin{theorem}[RealSeparability regular Cauchy dense countable route]
\label{thm:real-separability-regular-cauchy-dense-countable-route}
Every accepted $\RealSeparabilityUp$ density request that reaches a countable dense row first factors through the finite route
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The request reads dyadic tolerance rows, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, the Cauchy-equivalence classifier, and the terminal real-name seal before the countable dense row is consumed. The route exports no quotient $\RealUp$ equality, selected dense enumeration, countable-choice branch, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted density request for $\RealSeparabilityUp$. The hub route already places such a request downstream of the dyadic rational core, stream-name window, regular-rational readback, Cauchy-equivalence classifier, and terminal real-name seal.

The dyadic and stream rows provide the finite observation data. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback attaches those observations to a regular Cauchy name, and the Cauchy-equivalence classifier is the only equality classifier inspected before the name reaches the $\RealUp$ seal.

Only after that seal is fixed may the countable dense row be read. Since none of the displayed rows contains quotient real equality, a selected dense enumeration, countable choice, or an ambient completeness coordinate, the countable-density export remains exactly the finite regular-Cauchy route stated above.
\end{proof}
