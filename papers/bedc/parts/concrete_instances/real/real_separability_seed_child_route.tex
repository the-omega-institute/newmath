\begin{theorem}[Real separability seed child route]
\label{thm:real-separability-seed-child-route}
Every $\RealSeparabilityUp$ density request factors through the displayed child route consisting of a $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ approximation row, $\StreamNameUp$ finite windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\CauchyEquivalenceSetoidUp$ comparison, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal before any countable-dense row is consumed. The route is the seed-level child of \autoref{thm:real-separability-seed-status}, \autoref{thm:real-separability-scoped-density-route}, and \autoref{thm:real-separability-seed-closure-route}; it exports no quotient Real equality, selected dense enumeration, countable choice, host dense subset, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the seed status \autoref{thm:real-separability-seed-status}. It identifies the separability seed as the displayed child-route family rather than theorem content stored on the hub.

Apply the scoped density route \autoref{thm:real-separability-scoped-density-route}. The dyadic approximation row, finite StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Cauchy-equivalence comparison, and Real seal are exposed before the countable-dense row can be read.

Finally use the seed closure route \autoref{thm:real-separability-seed-closure-route}. It records the same child ledger as the seed closure surface, so the density request remains inside the finite route and cannot supply quotient Real equality, selected enumeration, countable choice, a host dense subset, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
