\label{def:hales-jewett-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $W$ is the finite word-window row. Each word is read as a $\ListUp$ spine over $A$, and the window records the inspected length and membership rows.
\item $V$ is the variable-word row. It records a finite word spine over $A$ together with at least one displayed variable position.
\item $L$ is the combinatorial-line row obtained by substituting each displayed alphabet symbol into every variable position of $V$ while leaving the constant positions fixed.
\item $C$ is the finite coloring row on the word window, exposing colors only for the words listed by $W$ and the line entries listed by $L$.
