\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\IntervalExchangeUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-interval-exchange-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Keane and Veech on interval exchange transformations, the $\IntervalExchangeUp$ packet records the finite interval-map surface used when a displayed partition of a real interval is rearranged by finitely many translations. It sits over the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite cell net of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The chapter names only the finite partition, permutation, and translation ledger by which later symbolic-dynamics or interval-map certificates may inspect an interval exchange; it does not assert invariant measures, minimality, unique ergodicity, host interval functions, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[IntervalExchange carrier]
\label{def:interval-exchange-carrier}
An $\IntervalExchangeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(D,I,B,\Pi,T,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row containing the located interval endpoints, dyadic cut points, endpoint comparisons, and translation budgets.
\item $I$ is the $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ row listing the finite source cells and target cells of the partition.
\item $B$ is the boundary-incidence row recording which neighboring source cells share displayed endpoints and which target cells share displayed endpoints.
\item $\Pi$ is the finite permutation row matching each source cell to exactly one target cell.
\item $T$ is the finite translation row; for each displayed source cell it records the dyadic displacement that carries the source endpoints to the corresponding target endpoints.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row used whenever a source endpoint, target endpoint, or translated endpoint is inspected.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row turning the displayed endpoint and translation windows into regular rational comparisons.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $D,I,B,\Pi,T,W,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,I,B,\Pi,T,W,Q,R$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from partition cells through permutation, translation, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, and the Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance row over $\IntervalExchangeUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\IntervalExchangeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted carriers only through the displayed endpoint, cell, incidence, permutation, translation, window, readback, seal, transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[IntervalExchange NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:interval-exchange-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\IntervalExchangeUp$ carrier $E=(D,I,B,\Pi,T,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local NameCert surface consists exactly of carrier admission for those rows; classifier stability under common dyadic refinement of the source and target cells; interval-cell transport through $I,B,\Pi,T$; finite orbit replay by iterating only the displayed permutation and translation rows for a displayed number of steps; $\StreamNameUp$ window exposure through $W$; $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $Q$; terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $R$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$; $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$; and ledger nonescape through the local certificate $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is \autoref{def:interval-exchange-carrier}. It fixes the finite row inventory and makes the local classifier a comparison of displayed endpoint, cell, permutation, translation, window, readback, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming data.

Common refinement is finite. A refined carrier replaces listed cells in $I$ by displayed dyadic subcells whose endpoint arithmetic is still read in $D$, whose incidences are still read in $B$, and whose source-to-target matching is still read through $\Pi,T$.

Finite orbit replay is also a row operation. A displayed number of steps iterates the permutation and translation ledgers already present in $\Pi,T$, opening StreamName windows through $W$ and rational readback through $Q$ before the Real seal $R$ is visible.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$. Transport, continuation, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite ledger, so the packet cannot export invariant measures, minimality, unique ergodicity, host interval functions, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[IntervalExchange L10 handoff]
\label{thm:interval-exchange-l10-handoff}
Every downstream consumer of an accepted $\IntervalExchangeUp$ carrier must read the $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ cell row, the $\StreamNameUp$ endpoint windows, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal before treating the packet as an interval map. The handoff order is
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\DyadicIntervalNetUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp,\RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp,
$$
with the permutation and translation rows displayed between the interval-net read and the terminal seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $E=(D,I,B,\Pi,T,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N)$. The dyadic endpoint arithmetic and interval-cell rows $D,I,B$ are the only partition data visible at the start of an interval-map read.

The consumer then opens $\Pi,T$. These rows give a finite source-to-target matching and a finite translation ledger, so no host interval function or quotient real equality is used to interpret the rearrangement.

The L10 numeric route remains downstream. Endpoint observations pass through $W$, tolerance and regular rational comparisons pass through $Q$, and the Real-facing boundary is exposed only by $R$.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$. The same finite dependency order is preserved under transport, continuation, provenance, and naming, so the handoff reaches an interval-map surface without importing invariant measures, minimality, unique ergodicity, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
