\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ChuSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-chu-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Chu and Barr on duality for spaces of states and observations, the $\ChuSpaceUp$ packet records a finite state-observation-evaluation surface as NameCert data. It sits beside the categorical object and transport rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-category-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-functor-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nattrans-namecert}, and it reads its state and observation carriers through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-setlike-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-typelike-namecert}. The certificate names a displayed Chu-style duality route, not a host category of all Chu spaces, an ambient profunctor calculus, a selected adjunction, or an external linear-logic semantics.

\begin{definition}[ChuSpace carrier]
\label{def:chu-space-carrier}
A $\ChuSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(S,O,E,D,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the state row, read as a finite $\SetLikeUp$ or $\TypeLikeUp$ carrier display.
\item $O$ is the observation row, read through the same set-like or type-like naming discipline.
\item $E$ is the evaluation relation row, displaying which state-observation incidences are accepted by the packet.
\item $D$ is the duality flip row, exchanging the state and observation faces while reversing the evaluation read.
\item $T$ is the category and profunctor transport row, recording the $\CategoryUp$, $\FunctorUp$, and $\NatTransUp$ handoff used by a categorical consumer.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,O,E,D,T,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for evaluation, duality flip, category transport, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ChuSpaceUp$, $\CategoryUp$, $\FunctorUp$, $\NatTransUp$, $\SetLikeUp$, $\TypeLikeUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChuSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,O,E,D,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an unbounded class of all Chu spaces, a host profunctor oracle, a quotient evaluation relation, a selected dualizing object, or an ambient linear-logic interpretation.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ChuSpace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:chu-space-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:chu-space-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\ChuSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $K=(S,O,E,D,T,H,C,P,N)$, state-row exposure, observation-row exposure, evaluation-relation exposure, duality-flip exposure, category and profunctor transport through $T$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every accepted evaluation read must pass through
$$
S,O \longmapsto E \longmapsto D
$$
before a categorical transport consumer may read $T,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:chu-space-carrier}. The packet exposes the state row $S$, observation row $O$, evaluation relation row $E$, duality flip row $D$, categorical transport row $T$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

The evaluation route is finite. A consumer first reads the displayed state and observation carriers, then the evaluation relation row, and only then the duality flip that reverses the two faces of the packet.

The categorical handoff is downstream of those rows. The $\CategoryUp$, $\FunctorUp$, and $\NatTransUp$ dependencies appear only through $T$, while the carrier rows $S$ and $O$ are read through $\SetLikeUp$ or $\TypeLikeUp$ before the handoff is available.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for a host category of all Chu spaces, an ambient profunctor oracle, quotient evaluation, a selected dualizing object, or linear-logic semantics, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ChuSpace duality handoff]
\label{thm:chu-space-duality-handoff}
Every accepted $\ChuSpaceUp$ packet exposes its duality consumer through the finite handoff
$$
(S,O,E)\longmapsto D\longmapsto T\longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
The flip row $D$ may exchange state and observation faces only after the state row, observation row, and evaluation relation row have been displayed, and the category/profunctor transport row $T$ may be consumed only after that flip row is available. The handoff exports no host-level Chu category, no implicit profunctor equality, no quotient of evaluations, and no selected external dualizing object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier from \autoref{def:chu-space-carrier}. The rows $S,O,E$ are the only state, observation, and evaluation coordinates available to a consumer.

Use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:chu-space-namecert-obligations}. They force every accepted evaluation read through the displayed state and observation rows before the duality flip row can be interpreted.

Read the transport row $T$ after the flip row. The categorical dependencies on $\CategoryUp$, $\FunctorUp$, and $\NatTransUp$ are therefore attached to the displayed duality route rather than to a host profunctor calculus.

The support rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the same route. Because no carrier row stores a host category of all Chu spaces, quotient evaluation equality, or an external dualizing object, the duality handoff is exactly the displayed finite packet.
\end{proof}
