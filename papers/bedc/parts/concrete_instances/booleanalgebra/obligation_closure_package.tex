\begin{theorem}[BooleanAlgebra obligation closure package]
\label{thm:booleanalgebra-obligation-closure-package}
The accepted $\BooleanAlgebraUp$ packet has an obligation-closure package over the displayed rows
$$
J,M,K,Z,O,R,H,C,P,N .
$$
Carrier admission is the finite $\BHist$ surface of \autoref{def:booleanalgebra-bhist-carrier}; complement stability is the pair of endpoint reads $x \wedge Kx$ through $Z$ and $x \vee Kx$ through $O$ from \autoref{thm:booleanalgebra-namecert-obligation-surface}; finite meet and join laws are consumed through the $\LatticeUp$ route of \autoref{thm:boolean-algebra-lattice-dependency-route}; and the Stone-source dependency is exported through \autoref{thm:booleanalgebra-stone-duality-forward-route} and \autoref{thm:booleanalgebra-stone-source-lattice-determinacy}. Thus the obligation package contains exactly carrier admission, complement stability, finite meet/join laws, Stone-source route, lattice dependency rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming, and no host powerset algebra, ultrafilter selector, quotient lattice, locale carrier, or ambient topology row.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.BooleanalgebraUp.BooleanAlgebraCarrier\_obligation\_closure\_package}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier from \autoref{def:booleanalgebra-bhist-carrier}. The rows $J,M,K,Z,O,R,H,C,P,N$ are the complete finite inventory available to a Boolean-algebra consumer.

Use \autoref{thm:booleanalgebra-namecert-obligation-surface} for carrier admission and complement stability. It forces the complement row $K$ to meet the zero endpoint $Z$ and join the one endpoint $O$ inside the displayed route, with transport and replay still attached.

Use \autoref{thm:boolean-algebra-lattice-dependency-route} for the finite meet and join laws. The lattice dependency reads $J$ and $M$ together with complement, endpoint, order, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows before any lattice or Stone-duality consumer can use the packet.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:booleanalgebra-stone-duality-forward-route} and \autoref{thm:booleanalgebra-stone-source-lattice-determinacy}. They identify the Stone-source route with the same Boolean rows and prevent divergence through host Boolean algebras, powerset representations, ultrafilter spaces, quotient lattices, locale carriers, or topology bridges. These cited rows are exactly the obligation-closure package.
\end{proof}
