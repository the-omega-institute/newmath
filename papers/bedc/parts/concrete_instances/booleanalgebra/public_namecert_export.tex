\begin{theorem}[BooleanAlgebra public NameCert export]
\label{thm:booleanalgebra-public-namecert-export}
The finite $\BooleanAlgebraUp$ NameCert surface exported from \autoref{def:booleanalgebra-bhist-carrier} is public for $\StoneDualityUp$ and $\RegularOpenAlgebraUp$ consumers. A public read keeps the $\BoolUp$ endpoint rows, $\PosetUp$ order row, $\LatticeUp$ join and meet rows, complement row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row visible before either downstream consumer may treat the packet as a Boolean source.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier $B=(J,M,K,Z,O,R,H,C,P,N)$. The rows $Z$ and $O$ are the visible $\BoolUp$ endpoints, $R$ is the $\PosetUp$ order route, and $J,M,K$ are the $\LatticeUp$ join, meet, and complement-facing rows.

Use the local obligation surface and the obligation package. They bind complement endpoint exactness, order-lattice compatibility, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming to the same finite packet, so the public export cannot drop $H,C,P,N$ while retaining a Boolean source.

The outgoing consumers read this same surface in two directions. $\StoneDualityUp$ consumes it as the Boolean-source side of Stone duality, while $\RegularOpenAlgebraUp$ consumes it as the algebraic surface behind regular-open meet, join, and complement. No host Boolean algebra, powerset representation, selected ultrafilter, quotient lattice, locale carrier, or ambient topology row is exported.
\end{proof}
