\begin{theorem}[BooleanAlgebra dossier lattice route]
\label{thm:booleanalgebra-dossier-lattice-route}
Every top-level $\BooleanAlgebraUp$ dossier read first consumes the $\BoolUp$
endpoint rows and the $\LatticeUp$ meet, join, and complement rows of the
local carrier before it is available as a Boolean source for $\StoneDualityUp$.
The route adds no Stone space, ultrafilter, powerset representation, quotient
lattice, or host Boolean-algebra equality row.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.BooleanalgebraUp.BooleanAlgebraDossierLatticeRoute}

\begin{proof}
Project the carrier surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-booleanalgebra-namecert}.
The endpoint coordinates are the displayed zero and one rows inherited from
$\BoolUp$, while the algebraic coordinates are the meet, join, complement,
and order rows inherited through the $\LatticeUp$ dependency route.

The Stone-duality handoff can read a Boolean source only after those local
Boolean-algebra rows have been exposed. Since the dossier has no coordinate
for Stone spaces, ultrafilters, powerset representatives, quotient lattices,
or host equality, the outgoing route remains the existing Bool--Lattice--Stone
dependency surface.
\end{proof}
