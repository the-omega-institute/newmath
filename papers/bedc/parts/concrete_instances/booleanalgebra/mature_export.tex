\begin{theorem}[Boolean algebra mature export]
\label{thm:boolean-algebra-mature-export}
The finite $\BooleanAlgebraUp$ rows routed through this hub provide the mature export consumed by the $\LatticeUp$ and $\StoneDualityUp$ sibling routes. The export consists exactly of the join row $J$, meet row $M$, complement row $K$, endpoint rows $Z,O$, order row $R$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport row $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay row $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance row $P$, and local $\NameCert$ row $N$ of \autoref{def:booleanalgebra-bhist-carrier}. It is the same packet exposed by the lattice dependency route, the Stone-duality handoff, the regular-open forward lattice route, the obligation closure package, and the public NameCert export.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:booleanalgebra-bhist-carrier}. The rows $J,M,K,Z,O,R,H,C,P,N$ are the full finite Boolean-algebra inventory, so every export to a lattice or Stone-facing consumer must use those rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:boolean-algebra-lattice-dependency-route}. It makes the meet and join rows visible together with complement, endpoint, order, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before any $\LatticeUp$ consumer uses the packet.

Apply \autoref{thm:booleanalgebra-stone-duality-handoff} and \autoref{thm:booleanalgebra-public-namecert-export}. They send the same finite packet to $\StoneDualityUp$ and regular-open consumers after the public obligation surface has been exposed.

Finally use \autoref{thm:booleanalgebra-obligation-closure-package}. It identifies carrier admission, complement endpoint exactness, finite meet and join laws, Stone-source routing, transport, replay, provenance, and naming as one package. Thus the mature export has no coordinate for a second host Boolean algebra, powerset representation, selected ultrafilter, quotient lattice equality, detached locale frame, or ambient topology.
\end{proof}
