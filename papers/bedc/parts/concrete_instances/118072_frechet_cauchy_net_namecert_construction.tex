\begin{definition}[Frechet Cauchy net carrier]
\label{def:frechet-cauchy-net-carrier}
A $\FrechetCauchyNetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(I,T,F,M,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the directed-index row, recording only the finite portion of the Moore--Smith direction inspected by the packet.
\item $T$ is the tail-cofinal ledger, listing the displayed upper-bound and tail-neighborhood requests attached to $I$.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ row obtained by reading the tail ledger as a Cauchy-filter boundary.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ handoff row that receives the displayed Cauchy-filter route.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule used to inspect the net tail at requested tolerances.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the same finite windows and tail budgets.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $I,T,F,M,S,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for directed-index, tail-cofinal, Cauchy-filter, metric-completion, window, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion request through the directed tail, Cauchy-filter row, metric-completion handoff, finite windows, regular readback, and Real seal.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FrechetCauchyNetUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $I,T,F,M,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient nets, selected subnets, selected limits, countable-choice tail selection, host equality of completed objects, or ambient real completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Frechet Cauchy net NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:frechet-cauchy-net-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FrechetCauchyNetUp$ carrier
$$

Every completion-facing obligation is read through those rows and no obligation is stored in quotient-net equality, selected subnets, selected limits, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:frechet-cauchy-net-carrier}. The tuple displays the directed-index source, tail-cofinal ledger, Cauchy-filter boundary, metric-completion handoff, window schedule, regular rational readback, terminal real seal, and structural rows.

The directed-net side is exhausted by $I$ and $T$. The index row exposes only the finite directed fragment being inspected, and the tail ledger records only the cofinal tail requests attached to that fragment.

The completion side is exhausted by $F,M,S,R,E$. Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no other coordinate is present, the excluded quotient, subnet, selector, choice, host-equality, and ambient-completeness data cannot become NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
