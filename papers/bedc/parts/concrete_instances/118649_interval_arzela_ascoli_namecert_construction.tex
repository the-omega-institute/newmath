$$

\begin{theorem}[Interval Arzela--Ascoli NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:interval-arzela-ascoli-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\IntervalArzelaAscoliUp$ carrier $A=(I,K,F,W,R,D,T,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\IntervalArzelaAscoliUp}$ surface consists exactly of interval-source admission through $I$, compact-metric finite-net admission through $K$, continuous-map family admission through $F$, finite-window exposure through $W$, regular rational readback through $R$, dyadic tolerance exposure through $D$, metric-triangle uniform-estimate exposure through $T$, compact-uniform handoff through $U$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:interval-arzela-ascoli-carrier}. The source rows are the interval row $I$, compact-metric row $K$, and continuous-map family row $F$.

Read the finite numerical surface next. The window row $W$, readback row $R$, and dyadic tolerance row $D$ attach each interval point and each inspected map value to finite data before any uniform estimate is considered.

The row $T$ supplies the only metric-triangle uniform estimate, and $U$ is downstream of that row. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same packet.

Since the carrier has no open-cover, selected-subsequence, quotient-equality, countable-choice, compact-open host, or ambient completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
