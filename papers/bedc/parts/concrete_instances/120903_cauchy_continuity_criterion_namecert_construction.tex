\begin{definition}[Cauchy continuity criterion carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-continuity-criterion-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row for the source regular real name and for the target observations requested by the criterion.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular rational names exposed by those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row used to compare source and target rational cells.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy continuity criterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-continuity-criterion-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-continuity-criterion-carrier}. The accepted tuple displays exactly the continuity row, stream windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance data, Cauchy-modulus schedule, Real seal, and structural rows.

The continuous/modulus row $F$ supplies the map-facing input, while $M$ supplies the finite schedule that turns each target tolerance request into a source-window demand. The rows $S,R,D$ then display the windows, regular rational readbacks, and dyadic comparisons served by that schedule.

\end{proof}
