Following Horn and Johnson's finite-dimensional matrix analysis, the packet records spectral projectors as displayed polynomial idempotent rows over a matrix carrier. It sits after the finite matrix carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, the displayed polynomial surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-namecert}, the field-extension rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fieldext-namecert}, the vector-space carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, and the finite spectral-radius boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-spectral-radius-namecert}. The chapter names only the finite polynomial projector packet; it does not import host spectral calculus, contour integrals, quotient eigenspaces, selected eigenbases, or analytic completeness.

\begin{definition}[Spectral projection carrier]
\label{def:spectral-projection-carrier}
A $\SpectralProjectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(M,P,L,I,R,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MatrixUp$ source row, including the finite square matrix and its entry carrier.
\item $P$ is the displayed $\PolynomialUp$ projector row whose coefficients are read over the same scalar field as $M$.
\item $L$ is the eigenvalue or factor row, exposed through $\FieldExtUp$ when the projector is displayed after scalar extension.
\item $I$ is the idempotence row recording that the evaluated polynomial projector satisfies $P(M)P(M)=P(M)$ inside the displayed matrix carrier.
\item $R$ is the range/kernel visibility row: it records the visible image boundary, the visible kernel boundary, and the finite $\VecSpaceUp$ rows consumed by downstream invariant-subspace readers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the matrix source, polynomial projector, eigenvalue or factor row, idempotence row, range/kernel visibility, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a spectral-projection request through $M,P,L,I,R,H,Q,N$.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SpectralProjectionUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\PolynomialUp$, $\FieldExtUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\SpectralRadiusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SpectralProjectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,P,L,I,R,H,C,Q,N$. It has no coordinate for a host functional calculus, an implicit contour integral, a quotient eigenspace, a selected eigenbasis, or a completed spectral decomposition.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral projection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:spectral-projection-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SpectralProjectionUp$ carrier
$$
S=(M,P,L,I,R,H,C,Q,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SpectralProjectionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed matrix source, polynomial projector, eigenvalue or factor row, idempotence row, range/kernel visibility row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Any consumer that reads a spectral projector must expose the matrix-polynomial route, the field-extension boundary when used, the finite vector-space visibility row, and the spectral-radius boundary before it may consume the projector as a named finite row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:spectral-projection-carrier}. The matrix and polynomial inputs are $M,P$, the scalar or factor boundary is $L$, the idempotence witness is $I$, and the visible range/kernel boundary is $R$.

The transport and replay rows $H,C$ preserve exactly those displayed rows. The provenance row $Q$ records the consumed MatrixUp, PolynomialUp, FieldExtUp, VecSpaceUp, and SpectralRadiusUp siblings, while $N$ names the local packet.

No additional spectral object is available to the classifier. Hence the NameCert obligations are exhausted by the displayed carrier rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral projection matrix-polynomial handoff]
\label{thm:spectral-projection-matrix-polynomial-handoff}
Every accepted $\SpectralProjectionUp$ packet exports its projector only through the finite handoff
$$
\MatrixUp \times \PolynomialUp \times \FieldExtUp \to \mathsf{IdempotentMatrixRow} \to \VecSpaceUp.
$$
The $\SpectralRadiusUp$ row can bound the finite matrix side of the handoff, but it cannot replace the displayed polynomial projector, idempotence row, or range/kernel visibility row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the handoff from the carrier rows. The matrix source and polynomial projector produce the displayed matrix row $P(M)$, while the field-extension row records the scalar boundary used to display the eigenvalue or factor data.

The idempotence row $I$ is the only row that lets the packet act as a projector. The range/kernel visibility row $R$ is the only row exported to vector-space consumers.

The spectral-radius row appears only through provenance and finite matrix-side estimates. It therefore cannot supply the projector, the idempotence witness, or the visible subspace boundary by itself.
\end{proof}
