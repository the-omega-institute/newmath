\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\NuclearSpaceUp$}

Following Grothendieck and Treves on nuclear locally convex spaces, the $\NuclearSpaceUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which a locally convex source is inspected through seminorm windows, bounded-set ledgers, nuclear factorization data, and tensor-test rows before a functional-analysis consumer may cite nuclearity. It sits after the locally convex surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locally-convex-space-namecert}, the topological-vector source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topvecspace-namecert}, the tensor-product carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tensorproduct-namecert}, the functional-analysis consumer frontier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-functionalanalysis-namecert}, and the scalar readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a displayed nuclearity test route, not a completed locally convex space, a strong duality theorem, Grothendieck's theorem, choice-selected bases, or an ambient function-space quotient.

\begin{definition}[Nuclear space carrier]
\label{def:nuclear-space-carrier}
A $\NuclearSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(L,S,B,F,T,R,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ source row, read through its topological-vector, finite-seminorm, and convex-neighbourhood data.
\item $S$ is the finite seminorm-family row selected for the nuclearity test window.
\item $B$ is the bounded-set ledger, recording the displayed bounded probes against which the seminorm window is read.
\item $F$ is the nuclear-map factorization row: it records the finite source seminorm, target seminorm, scalar coefficient readback, and intermediate finite-rank witness used by the local test.
\item $T$ is the $\TensorProductUp$ tensor-test row, available only after $L,S,B,F$ have been displayed.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ scalar-readback row for coefficient bounds, summability budgets, and seminorm estimates.
\item $Q$ is the $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$ consumer row that may replay nuclearity only through $L,S,B,F,T,R$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for locally convex source, seminorm, bounded-set, factorization, tensor-test, scalar-readback, consumer, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a seminorm or bounded-set query through the factorization and tensor-test rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NuclearSpaceUp$, $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$, $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$, $\TopVecSpaceUp$, $\TensorProductUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\NuclearSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $L,S,B,F,T,R,Q,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for topological-vector-space completion, strong duality, Grothendieck theorem, choice-selected bases, ambient function-space quotients, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Nuclear space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:nuclear-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\NuclearSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
U=(L,S,B,F,T,R,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\NuclearSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the locally convex source, finite seminorm-family window, bounded-set ledger, nuclear-map factorization row, tensor-test row, real scalar readback, functional-analysis consumer row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every consumer that treats $U$ as a nuclear-space certificate must expose $L,S,B,F,T,R$ before $Q,H,C,P,N$ are public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:nuclear-space-carrier}. The locally convex source is the row $L$, and the only local probes available to it are the finite seminorm-family row $S$ and bounded-set ledger $B$.

Read the factorization surface next. The row $F$ stores the finite nuclear-map factorization witness for the displayed test window, while the scalar budgets used by that witness are read through the $\RealUp$ row $R$.

The tensor-test row $T$ is downstream of the same data. It receives the locally convex source, the seminorm and bounded-set windows, the factorization row, and the scalar readback before a functional-analysis consumer may inspect $Q$.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly those rows. Since the carrier contains no completion object, strong-duality row, Grothendieck theorem, choice-selected basis, or quotient function-space carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Nuclear space finite tensor-test handoff]
\label{thm:nuclear-space-finite-tensor-test-handoff}
Every accepted $\NuclearSpaceUp$ read that is consumed by $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$ must factor through
$$
\LocallyConvexSpaceUp,\ \TopVecSpaceUp,\ \TensorProductUp,\ \RealUp
$$
by way of the displayed rows $L,S,B,F,T,R,Q,H,C,P,N$. The handoff exposes finite seminorm probes, bounded-set tests, nuclear factorization data, tensor-test evidence, and scalar readback; it does not export completed locally convex spaces, strong duals, Grothendieck theorem, selected bases, or quotient function spaces.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:nuclear-space-namecert-obligations}. They force a nuclearity consumer to read the locally convex source row, finite seminorm window, bounded-set ledger, factorization row, tensor-test row, and real scalar readback before the local certificate can be used.

The $\TopVecSpaceUp$ dependency is already inside the $\LocallyConvexSpaceUp$ source route, so a consumer cannot detach the seminorm window from the underlying topological-vector operations and continuity data.

The $\TensorProductUp$ row then receives only the displayed factorization data. Its tensor test is a finite BEDC handoff from $F$ and $R$, not a completed projective tensor product or a host theorem about all continuous linear maps.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that finite route into $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$. Because no coordinate stores strong duality, a Grothendieck theorem, a selected basis, or an ambient quotient function space, none of those endpoints can be exported by the packet.
\end{proof}
