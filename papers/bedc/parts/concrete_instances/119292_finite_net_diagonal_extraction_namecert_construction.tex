\begin{definition}[Finite net diagonal extraction carrier]
\label{def:finite-net-diagonal-extraction-carrier}
A $\FiniteNetDiagonalExtractionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(G,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the finite net-row ledger. Each row records the inspected net
stage, its finite entries, and the diagonal index selected from displayed
finite data.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window ledger whose finite windows read the
entries of $G$ needed by the diagonal row.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance-budget row governing the error
allowed for each displayed net row and diagonal comparison.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the finite windows
and dyadic tolerance budget.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $G,W,D,R$
have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the net-row ledger,
window ledger, tolerance budget, readback, seal, continuation, provenance, and
local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a diagonal request through
$G,W,D,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteNetDiagonalExtractionUp$,
$\CauchyDiagonalSelectionUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$,
$\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteNetDiagonalExtractionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through the displayed rows. It
has no coordinate for selected representatives, countable choice, open-cover
compactness, quotient streams, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$
completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite net diagonal extraction NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-net-diagonal-extraction-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteNetDiagonalExtractionUp$ carrier
$$
F=(G,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteNetDiagonalExtractionUp}$ surface consists
exactly of carrier admission for the finite net rows, StreamName windows,
DyadicRatCore tolerance budget, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport,
continuation, provenance, and local naming; classifier stability under the
componentwise $\hsame$ rows; route exactness from $G,W,D,R$ to $E$; and
refusal rows excluding selected representatives, countable choice, open-cover
compactness, quotient streams, host real equality, and ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:finite-net-diagonal-extraction-carrier}. The projection exposes
only the finite net-row ledger, finite windows, dyadic tolerance budget,
regular readback, Real seal, transport, continuation, provenance, and local
naming rows.

Classifier stability is componentwise. The rows in $H$ transport $G,W,D,R,E$
and the administrative rows $C,P,N$ to a common finite comparison surface.

Route exactness is read from the continuation row $C$. A diagonal request first
displays the net rows $G$, then the finite StreamName windows $W$, then the
DyadicRatCore tolerance row $D$, then the RegSeqRat readback $R$, and only then
the Real seal $E$.

The refusal rows are exhausted by the carrier inventory. Since no listed row
contains selected representatives, countable choice, open-cover compactness,
quotient streams, host equality, or ambient completeness, no local NameCert
obligation can export those objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite net diagonal extraction window route]
\label{thm:finite-net-diagonal-extraction-window-route}
Every diagonal-limit consumer of an accepted
$\FiniteNetDiagonalExtractionUp$ carrier must read the route
$$

$$
before consuming the $\RealUp$ seal. Thus the diagonal extraction passes

$\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance budgets, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback,
componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$
provenance, and the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteNetDiagonalExtractionUp}$ row.

No selected representative, countable-choice schedule, open-cover compactness
row, quotient stream, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier projection. The terminal $\RealUp$ seal is the row $E$,
and it appears only after $G,W,D,R$ in the displayed packet.

Replay the continuation row. Its route takes a diagonal request through the
finite net-row ledger, then through the StreamName windows, the dyadic
tolerance budget, and the RegSeqRat readback before the seal row is visible.

Transport and provenance do not add another seal. The row $H$ moves only the
displayed coordinates, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming for
the same finite packet.

Consequently every diagonal-limit consumer reaches $\RealUp$ through the
displayed finite route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for selected
representatives, countable choice, open-cover compactness, quotient streams,
host equality, or ambient completeness, none can replace the route.
\end{proof}
