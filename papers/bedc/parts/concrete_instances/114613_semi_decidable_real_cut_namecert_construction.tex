\begin{definition}[Semi-decidable real cut carrier]
\label{def:semi-decidable-real-cut-carrier}
A $\SemiDecidableRealCutUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(W,R,L,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row listing the inspected precision requests and rational lower-cut queries.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the scheduled windows.
\item $L$ is the $\LowerRealUp$ lower-observation ledger. Each accepted entry records a rational or dyadic candidate together with finite evidence that the candidate is admitted by the lower side.
\item $Q$ is the $\LocatedRealUp$ comparison row, used only when the lower evidence is checked against a located enclosure already visible through $W$ and $R$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, consumed only after the stream, regular-rational, lower-ledger, and located-comparison rows are displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $W,R,L,Q,E$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same lower-cut query route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SemiDecidableRealCutUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\LowerRealUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SemiDecidableRealCutUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed tuple $W,R,L,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an upper cut, quotient real equality, trichotomy, selected witnesses for all positive entries, a Dedekind-completeness theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Semi-decidable real cut obligation surface]
\label{thm:semi-decidable-real-cut-obligation-surface}
For an accepted $\SemiDecidableRealCutUp$ carrier $S=(W,R,L,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SemiDecidableRealCutUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, stream-window visibility, regular-rational readback, lower-ledger admission, located-comparison compatibility, terminal $\RealUp$ seal ordering, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. These obligations are all read through
$$
\StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \LowerRealUp \longmapsto \LocatedRealUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
with the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is the tuple displayed in \autoref{def:semi-decidable-real-cut-carrier}. The only observation entry point is the finite window row $W$, and every regular readback is attached to that row through $R$.

The lower-cut evidence is the row $L$. It records positive lower information as finite admitted entries, while the located comparison row $Q$ can only compare those entries against the already displayed located-real route.

The terminal seal $E$ is downstream of $W,R,L,Q$. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the same finite route, and $P,N$ source and name it. Since no carrier row stores an upper cut, quotient equality, trichotomy, selected global enumeration, Dedekind completeness, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Semi-decidable real cut window stability]
\label{thm:semi-decidable-real-cut-window-stability}
Let $S=(W,R,L,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\SemiDecidableRealCutUp$ carrier. Replacing a displayed lower-cut query by a later displayed finite window changes only the inspected entries of $W$ and the corresponding regular-rational readback in $R$; the admitted lower-ledger row $L$, located comparison row $Q$, terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$, componentwise transport $H$, replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local name $N$ remain attached to the same semi-decidable cut route. Thus window refinement does not create Dedekind completeness, quotient equality of real names, trichotomy, a selected dense enumeration, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite observation row $W$. A later displayed window is still a finite $\StreamNameUp$ read, so the corresponding regular-rational data are obtained by rereading $R$ along the same schedule discipline.

The lower evidence is not recomputed by a host predicate. It remains the displayed lower ledger $L$, and any located check must use the visible $Q$ row before the terminal seal $E$ is consumed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ keep the refined window in the same carrier. Componentwise transport preserves the rows, replay repeats the displayed route, provenance records the same dependencies, and the local name remains the same NameCert surface. No excluded completeness, quotient, trichotomy, selection, or choice principle is introduced by refining the finite window.
\end{proof}
