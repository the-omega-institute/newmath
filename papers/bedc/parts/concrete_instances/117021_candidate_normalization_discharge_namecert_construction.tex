\begin{definition}[Candidate-normalization discharge carrier]
\label{def:candidate-normalization-discharge-carrier}
A $\CandidateNormalizationDischargeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(A,K,E,S,O,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the audit-map anchor row for \autoref{ch:metacic-normalization-audit-map};
\item $K$ is the candidate-evidence row, reading candidate, closed-candidate, or candidate-set evidence through the existing MetaCIC normalization frontier;
\item $E$ is the endpoint-seal row, recording the normal endpoint that may be consumed only after $K$ is displayed;
\item $S$ is the discharge row connecting the candidate evidence in $K$ to the endpoint use in $E$;
\item $O$ is the obstruction-retention row, keeping absent candidate evidence, subject-reduction obstruction, and evidence-free endpoint requests visible;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $A,K,E,S,O$ and for the local naming row;
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay routes for audit, candidate, endpoint, discharge, and obstruction reads;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CandidateNormalizationDischargeUp$, $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$, $\MetaCICCandidateNormalizationWitnessUp$, $\MetaCICNormalEndpointBudgetUp$, $\MetaCICSnCandidateInterfaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CandidateNormalizationDischargeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Candidate discharge obligation surface]
\label{thm:candidate-normalization-discharge-obligation-surface}
Every accepted $\CandidateNormalizationDischargeUp$ packet exposes exactly the candidate-evidence row $K$, endpoint-seal row $E$, discharge row $S$, obstruction-retention row $O$, displayed $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows needed before a normalization-facing consumer may use a strong-normalization endpoint. No consumer can reach the endpoint seal through the packet while omitting the candidate evidence or the discharge row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:candidate-normalization-discharge-carrier}. The endpoint row $E$ is stored beside $K$ and $S$, not as an independent normalization theorem.

Replay the read through $C$. A normalization-facing consumer must display the audit anchor, candidate evidence, endpoint seal, discharge link, and obstruction row in the carrier order before using the endpoint.

Transport and provenance through $H$ and $P$ preserve those same rows. Thus the local NameCert surface is exhausted by the displayed finite packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Endpoint seal nonescape]
\label{thm:candidate-normalization-discharge-endpoint-seal-nonescape}
An accepted $\CandidateNormalizationDischargeUp$ packet is refuted as a discharge certificate if a normalization-facing consumer obtains a strong-normalization endpoint without displaying candidate evidence, endpoint seal, obstruction retention, $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows. The packet does not export evidence-free strong normalization, host evaluation soundness, unrestricted subject reduction, quotient syntax equality, or closed consistency.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:candidate-normalization-discharge-obligation-surface}. It places candidate evidence and the discharge row before endpoint use.

The obstruction row $O$ records exactly the refused bypasses. If the consumer reaches a strong-normalization endpoint while erasing $K,S,O,H,C,P,N$, then the read no longer factors through the accepted carrier.

Because the carrier has no row for evidence-free strong normalization, host evaluation soundness, unrestricted subject reduction, quotient syntax equality, or closed consistency, those endpoints remain outside the packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sibling separation]
\label{thm:candidate-normalization-discharge-sibling-separation}
The $\CandidateNormalizationDischargeUp$ carrier is not bijective, row for row, with $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$, $\MetaCICCandidateNormalizationWitnessUp$, $\MetaCICNormalEndpointBudgetUp$, or $\MetaCICSnCandidateInterfaceUp$. It binds the discharge row connecting candidate evidence to endpoint use, while the siblings respectively expose frontier, witness, endpoint budget, or candidate interface rows without the same discharge packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compare first with $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$. The frontier exposes candidate and endpoint boundary rows, but it does not by itself store the local discharge row $S$ together with this packet's obstruction-retention and endpoint-use contract.

Compare next with $\MetaCICCandidateNormalizationWitnessUp$ and $\MetaCICNormalEndpointBudgetUp$. The witness records evidence for a candidate-facing route, and the budget records endpoint consumption constraints; neither is the single carrier that binds both sides through $S$.

Finally compare with $\MetaCICSnCandidateInterfaceUp$. The interface describes candidate admission, while $\CandidateNormalizationDischargeUp$ records the finite discharge packet through which an endpoint read is allowed. A row-preserving bijection would have to preserve $A,K,E,S,O,H,C,P,N$ simultaneously, which no listed sibling carries.
\end{proof}
