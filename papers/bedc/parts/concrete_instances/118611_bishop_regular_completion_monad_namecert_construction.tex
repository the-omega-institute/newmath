\begin{definition}[Bishop regular completion monad carrier]
\label{def:bishop-regular-completion-monad-carrier}
A $\BishopRegularCompletionMonadUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(D,S,R,E,L,U,J,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger. It records the dyadic scales, triangle-budget entries, and composition slack used by every regular completion request.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window family. It records source windows, unit windows, multiplication windows, and seal-facing observation cuts.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback family attached to the displayed stream windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $D,S,R$ and the monad-law rows have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$ law ledger naming the finite unit, multiplication, left-unit, right-unit, and associativity comparisons.
\item $U$ is the unit row. It admits a displayed regular rational name by copying the relevant stream, dyadic, readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows into the completion surface.
\item $J$ is the join or multiplication row. It flattens a displayed completion-of-completion request by replaying the outer and inner windows through the same dyadic ledger before exposing the Real seal.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the dyadic ledger, stream windows, regular readbacks, Real seal, law ledger, unit row, multiplication row, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
D,S \longmapsto R \longmapsto L,U,J \longmapsto E .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopRegularCompletionMonadUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCompletionMonadUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,S,R,E,L,U,J,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, quotient stream equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, trichotomy, host real equality, or full completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop regular completion monad NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-regular-completion-monad-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopRegularCompletionMonadUp$ carrier
$$
B=(D,S,R,E,L,U,J,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCompletionMonadUp}$ surface consists of exactly five finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission: the accepted object is the displayed $\BHist$ packet over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, and $\CauchyCompletionMonadLawUp$;
\item source routing: every request reads the finite stream-window row $S$, the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, and the regular rational readback row $R$ before a Real seal is available;
\item unit ledger: the row $U$ copies displayed regular rational names into the completion surface without changing $D,S,R,H,C,P,N$;
\item multiplication ledger: the row $J$ flattens nested completion requests only by replaying the law ledger $L$ and the displayed $D,S,R$ rows;
\item structural closure: $H,C,P,N$ provide componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, package provenance, and local naming for the same rows.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is stored in quotient completion, quotient stream equality, countable choice, trichotomy, selected host limits, or full $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-regular-completion-monad-carrier}. The projection exposes exactly the dyadic ledger, stream windows, regular readbacks, Real seal, monad-law ledger, unit row, multiplication row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The source-routing row is finite because $S$ lists the inspected windows and $D$ lists the dyadic budgets before $R$ repacks the observations. The seal row $E$ is downstream of those rows and has no independent host-limit coordinate.

The unit and multiplication obligations are read from $U,J$ together with $L$. The unit row copies the displayed source data, while the multiplication row replays the nested request through the same finite ledger.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the displayed packet. Since the excluded principles have no carrier coordinate, the five listed rows exhaust the local $\NameCert$ obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop regular completion monad unit and multiplication ledger]
\label{thm:bishop-regular-completion-monad-unit-multiplication-ledger}
Every unit, multiplication, left-unit, right-unit, and associativity comparison exported by an accepted $\BishopRegularCompletionMonadUp$ carrier is a comparison of the displayed ledger
$$
D,S,R,E,L,U,J,H,C,P,N .
$$
The unit row $U$ embeds source regular rational data through the same $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, and $\RegSeqRatUp$ rows, and the multiplication row $J$ flattens nested completion requests only after the law ledger $L$ has replayed the relevant finite windows and dyadic tolerances. The comparison exports no quotient completion, quotient stream equality, countable choice, trichotomy, selected host limit, or full $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the unit side first. The row $U$ is available only after the displayed dyadic ledger $D$, stream-window row $S$, and regular readback row $R$ have been projected, so source data enter the completion surface by copying those rows.

For multiplication, read the law ledger $L$ and then the row $J$. A nested completion request supplies outer and inner finite windows; $J$ flattens them by replaying the same dyadic budget and regular readback route rather than by selecting a quotient representative.

The left-unit and right-unit comparisons are therefore comparisons of copied finite rows. Associativity compares the two finite replays of a three-stage request, and both replays enumerate the same dyadic tolerances, stream windows, readbacks, law ledger rows, and Real-seal handoff.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming close the ledger through $H,C,P,N$. Since the carrier has no row for the excluded principles, none of the exported monad comparisons can depend on them.
\end{proof}
