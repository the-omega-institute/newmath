Following Kelley and Kuratowski on hit--miss hyperspace convergence, the $\HitMissConvergenceUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which a hyperspace or cluster-set consumer reads hit and miss convergence data without importing an ambient host hyperspace topology. It sits between the hit--miss classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperspace-namecert}, the Vietoris route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vietoris-topology-namecert}, the Kuratowski convergence consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kuratowski-convergence-namecert}, the compact-metric support of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the regular-rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a finite convergence interface only; it is not a selected closed-set limit, a quotient hyperspace equality, a host Vietoris topology, or a compactness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Hit--miss convergence carrier]
\label{def:hit-miss-convergence-carrier}
A $\HitMissConvergenceUp$ carrier is a tuple
$$
\begin{gathered}
H=(L,M,V,K,D,R,E,T,C,P,N)
\end{gathered}
$$
with the following finite rows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the finite hit ledger, listing the displayed source cells, target neighbourhood probes, and witnessed hits used by the convergence read.
\item $M$ is the finite miss ledger, listing the displayed closed or compact avoidance probes and their witnessed exclusions.
\item $V$ is the $\VietorisTopologyUp$ route through which hit and miss rows are interpreted before any hyperspace-facing consumer can read them.
\item $K$ is the $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$ route that consumes the same hit and miss rows as finite convergence evidence.
\item $D$ records compact and metric support from $\CompactMetricUp$ and $\MetricUp$, including the finite ball or compact-source names on which the hit and miss ledgers are checked.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance readback row for displayed radius and stage bounds.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal for real-valued tolerance endpoints reached only after $R$ has been displayed.
\item $T$ is componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $L,M,V,K,D,R,E$.
\item $C$ is displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same finite route.
\item $P$ is $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HitMissConvergenceUp$, $\HyperspaceUp$, $\VietorisTopologyUp$, $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HitMissConvergenceUp}$ row naming exactly this carrier surface.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host powerset, selected closed-set limit, quotient equality of hyperspace points, open-cover compactness object, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hit--miss convergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hit-miss-convergence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HitMissConvergenceUp$ carrier $H=(L,M,V,K,D,R,E,T,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HitMissConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite hit-ledger admission through $L$; finite miss-ledger admission through $M$; Vietoris routing through $V$; Kuratowski convergence routing through $K$; compact and metric support through $D$; regular-rational tolerance readback through $R$; real tolerance sealing through $E$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $T$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$; $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hit-miss-convergence-carrier}. The rows $L,M,V,K,D,R,E,T,C,P,N$ are exactly the hit, miss, Vietoris, Kuratowski, support, tolerance, transport, replay, provenance, and naming coordinates.

The hit and miss ledgers are finite before either route can be read. The Vietoris route $V$ supplies the hyperspace-facing interpretation, while the Kuratowski route $K$ consumes the same finite evidence as convergence data.

The support and tolerance rows $D,R,E$ keep the route inside compact-metric, metric, regular-rational, and real-seal data already displayed in the packet. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that same finite route, so the listed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hit--miss convergence Kuratowski route]
\label{thm:hit-miss-convergence-kuratowski-route}
Every $\KuratowskiConvergenceUp$ or Kuratowski cluster-set consumer that reads an accepted $\HitMissConvergenceUp$ packet must first expose the finite hit ledger $L$, the finite miss ledger $M$, the $\VietorisTopologyUp$ route $V$, the compact and metric support row $D$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance readback $R$, the $\RealUp$ seal $E$, and the structural rows $T,C,P,N$. The consumer receives a finite hit--miss convergence route and not a host hyperspace topology, selected closed-set limit, quotient equality, open-cover compactness proof, or ambient real completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with \autoref{thm:hit-miss-convergence-namecert-obligations}. It fixes the accepted read to the displayed finite rows and leaves no coordinate for a host hyperspace object or selected limit.

The Kuratowski route $K$ can consume hit--miss data only after $L$ and $M$ have been displayed and interpreted through $V$. The compact-metric and metric support row $D$ supplies the named source cells, while $R$ and $E$ seal the tolerance endpoints.

Finally the structural rows $T,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Thus every Kuratowski-facing read factors through the finite hit--miss interface and cannot export any of the refused host, quotient, compactness, or completeness endpoints.
\end{proof}
