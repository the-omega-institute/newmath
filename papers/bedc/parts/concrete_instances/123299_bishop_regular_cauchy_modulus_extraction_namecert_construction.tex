Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopRegularCauchyModulusExtractionUp$ packet records the finite boundary by which a regular Cauchy rational name exposes a tail modulus to a Real-facing or completion-facing consumer. It sits over the $\StreamNameUp$ window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the $\RealUp$ naming seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the sibling modulus-extraction packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-modulus-extraction-namecert}. The chapter isolates the Bishop-facing boundary: a modulus is exported only as displayed finite tail data, not as a selector, quotient representative, or ambient completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Bishop regular Cauchy modulus extraction carrier]
\label{def:bishop-regular-cauchy-modulus-extraction-carrier}
A $\BishopRegularCauchyModulusExtractionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row opened by the regular Cauchy rational source and by each requested precision window.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback row, binding source windows to rational observations and regularity bounds.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, displaying dyadic scales, comparisons, half-radius cells, and endpoint observations.
\item $E$ is the Bishop tail-modulus row, assigning each displayed tolerance request to a finite stage justified by $S,R,D$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for window, readback, tolerance, tail-modulus, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
S,R,D \longmapsto E .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopRegularCauchyModulusExtractionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegularCauchyModulusExtractionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCauchyModulusExtractionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected limit, quotient equality of Cauchy streams, countable choice, trichotomy, detached modulus selector, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or bridge credential.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop regular Cauchy modulus extraction NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-regular-cauchy-modulus-extraction-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopRegularCauchyModulusExtractionUp$ carrier
$$
B=(S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCauchyModulusExtractionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the StreamName window, RegSeqRat regular readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance, Bishop tail-modulus, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows; regular-readback exactness through $S,R$; tolerance exactness through $D$; tail-modulus exactness through $E$; and structural stability through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-regular-cauchy-modulus-extraction-carrier}. The rows $S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$ are the only coordinates of an accepted packet.

The readback and tolerance obligations are componentwise. The regular rational observations are read through $S,R$, while dyadic scales and comparison cells are read through $D$.

The tail-modulus obligation is the row $E$. It is downstream of the displayed route $S,R,D$, so it cannot be replaced by a detached selector or by a completed Real object.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no remaining coordinate stores quotient equality, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, ambient completeness, or bridge data, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop regular Cauchy tail modulus route]
\label{thm:bishop-regular-cauchy-modulus-extraction-tail-modulus}
Let
$$
B=(S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
be an accepted $\BishopRegularCauchyModulusExtractionUp$ carrier. Every $\RealUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, or $\CompleteMetricUp$ consumer that reads a tail modulus from $B$ must factor through
$$
S,R,D \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Consequently the consumer receives a displayed StreamName window row, RegSeqRat regular readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger, Bishop tail-modulus row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ data, and receives no selected limit, quotient stream equality, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or bridge credential.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier and project $S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. Before any tail modulus is exported, the consumer can read only the displayed window, regular readback, and dyadic tolerance rows.

The row $E$ is then read at the same displayed tolerance request. It records the finite stage justified by the regular readback and dyadic tolerance data.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. The sibling packet \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-modulus-extraction-namecert} gives the general modulus-extraction surface, while this carrier binds the Bishop regular-Cauchy boundary without adding selector, quotient, choice, order, completeness, or bridge coordinates.
\end{proof}
