\begin{definition}[Cauchy diagonal uniform modulus carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-diagonal-uniform-modulus-carrier}
A $\CauchyDiagonalUniformModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(S,U,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the diagonal schedule row, read through $\StreamDiagonalSelectorUp$ and naming the finite precision-to-window assignments consumed by the diagonal read.
\item $U$ is the uniform modulus row, assigning each displayed tolerance request one finite bound that serves all selected regular Cauchy rows visible to the schedule.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance budget row, recording the dyadic request, slack conversion, radius ledger, and comparison cells used by $U$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source row, displaying the regular rational readbacks inspected by the diagonal schedule.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $S,U,D,R$ and the $\RegularCauchyDiagonalUp$ source route have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the schedule, uniform modulus, dyadic budget, regular readback, Real seal, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Real or Completion tolerance request through $S,U,D,R,E$ and the structural rows.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyDiagonalUniformModulusUp$, $\StreamDiagonalSelectorUp$, $\RegularCauchyDiagonalUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyDiagonalUniformModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts exactly packets whose consumer-visible route is $S,U,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a countable-choice selector, a family of hidden moduli, quotient stream equality, a selected completed limit, host real equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy diagonal uniform modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-diagonal-uniform-modulus-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:cauchy-diagonal-uniform-modulus-carrier}. The packet displays one diagonal schedule, one uniform modulus row, one dyadic tolerance ledger, the regular rational readbacks inspected by that schedule, and the terminal Real seal.

The diagonal schedule is read first. It fixes the finite StreamName windows and selector coordinates supplied by $\StreamDiagonalSelectorUp$ and $\RegularCauchyDiagonalUp$. The uniform row $U$ then answers a displayed tolerance request by a single bound over those scheduled reads rather than by choosing a separate hidden modulus for each source.

The dyadic budget $D$ and regular readback row $R$ make the bound auditable. They display the precision, slack, comparison, and rational readback data consumed before the Real seal $E$ is available.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no countable-choice selector, quotient-stream equality, selected completed limit, host equality, or ambient completeness coordinate appears in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
