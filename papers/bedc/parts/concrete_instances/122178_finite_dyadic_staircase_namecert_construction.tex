\begin{theorem}[Finite dyadic staircase NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-dyadic-staircase-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteDyadicStaircaseUp$ carrier
$$

the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteDyadicStaircaseUp}$ surface consists exactly of partition admission through $P$, dyadic cell-net exposure through $I$, finite rational value exposure through $V$, lower and upper Darboux ledgers through $L,U$, Darboux handoff through $D$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal real sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $G$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The Darboux route is fixed by $L,U,D,R,E$. The lower and upper ledgers feed the Darboux handoff row, the regular rational row reads the finite sums and heights, and the Real seal exposes only the terminal value-facing name.

The rows $H,C,G,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for the excluded host principles, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
