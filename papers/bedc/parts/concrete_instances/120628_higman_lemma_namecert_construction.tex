\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HigmanLemmaUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-higman-lemma-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Higman 1952 and Nash-Williams' well-quasi-order method, the $\HigmanLemmaUp$ packet records finite word-embedding data over a well-quasi-ordered source as a NameCert carrier. It sits after the finite-product induction packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dickson-lemma-namecert}, the finite list rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-list-namecert}, the ordered comparison rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poset-namecert}, and the candidate-boundary rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicnormalizationfrontier-namecert}. The packet is a finite subsequence-embedding certificate for words; it is not a selected infinite subsequence theorem, not a quotient of streams, and not a host proof of MetaCIC normalization.

\begin{definition}[Higman lemma carrier]
\label{def:higman-lemma-carrier}
A $\HigmanLemmaUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
\mathsf{Higman}(S,W,E,B,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source well-quasi-order row, exposing only finite comparisons and bad-prefix refusals for the underlying alphabet.
\item $W$ is the finite-word row, storing word windows as $\ListUp$ packets over $S$.
\item $E$ is the subsequence-embedding row, recording monotone index maps and pointwise source comparisons for a pair of displayed word windows.
\item $B$ is the bad-sequence-prefix row, storing the finite obstruction prefix that must be reduced when no embedding row is displayed.
\item $D$ is the Dickson-style product-order dependency row read through $\DicksonLemmaUp$ for the finite vector of front letters and residual word lengths.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, word, embedding, obstruction, dependency, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a bad-prefix read to a shorter residual-word read and then to an embedding read.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HigmanLemmaUp$, $\DicksonLemmaUp$, $\ListUp$, $\PosetUp$, $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HigmanLemmaUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for countable choice, quotient word equality, a selected infinite subsequence, excluded-middle case selection, or an unconditional MetaCIC termination theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Higman lemma NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:higman-lemma-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HigmanLemmaUp$ carrier $\mathsf{Higman}(S,W,E,B,D,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HigmanLemmaUp}$ surface consists exactly of source well-quasi-order exposure through $S$, finite-word exposure through $W$, subsequence-embedding exposure through $E$, bad-sequence-prefix exposure through $B$, Dickson dependency exposure through $D$, componentwise transport through $H$, continuation replay through $C$, provenance nonescape through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The source row $S$, word row $W$, embedding row $E$, obstruction row $B$, and dependency row $D$ are the only mathematical rows available before the structural rows are read.

The source and word rows fix the alphabet and finite windows. The embedding row records only monotone index maps together with pointwise source comparisons, while the bad-prefix row records the finite obstruction prefix that remains when no such embedding is displayed.

The dependency row is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dickson-lemma-namecert}. It supplies a finite product-order route for the front-letter and residual-length bookkeeping, but it does not add a selected infinite subsequence or a quotient word equality.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for countable choice, excluded-middle selection, quotient words, or an unconditional MetaCIC termination theorem, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Higman lemma finite subsequence embedding route]
\label{thm:higman-lemma-subsequence-embedding-route}
Every downstream consumer of an accepted $\HigmanLemmaUp$ packet that asks for a word-embedding certificate must read the finite route
$$
\ListUp\to\PosetUp\to\DicksonLemmaUp\to\HigmanLemmaUp\to\NameCert .
$$
The consumer receives finite word windows, source comparisons, monotone index data, bad-prefix reduction data, Dickson-style product bookkeeping, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. It receives no selected infinite subsequence, no quotient stream equality, no countable-choice selector, and no global normalization theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:higman-lemma-namecert-obligations}. They force a consumer to expose the source row $S$, word row $W$, embedding row $E$, obstruction row $B$, and dependency row $D$ before it reads the local naming row.

The word row is a finite $\ListUp$ window over the source comparison surface. The embedding row is a displayed monotone index map with pointwise comparisons, so it is a finite certificate rather than an infinite subsequence selection.

The dependency row routes the finite obstruction reduction through $\DicksonLemmaUp$. This places the front-letter and residual-length bookkeeping on the same finite product-order surface already named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dickson-lemma-namecert}.

Finally the structural rows carry the route to normalization-facing consumers. A $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$ reader may consume the packet only as a finite residual-word evidence row, because $P$ names no quotient stream equality, countable-choice selector, or global normalization theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Higman lemma normalization-frontier handoff]
\label{thm:higman-lemma-normalization-frontier-handoff}
A normalization-frontier consumer may cite $\HigmanLemmaUp$ only after the finite word packet exposes $S,W,E,B,D,H,C,P,N$ and after the consumer has identified which residual-word window is being handed to the $\MetaCICNormalizationFrontierUp$ boundary. The handoff is a finite subsequence-embedding certificate for residual windows; it does not close subject reduction, confluence, strong normalization, quotient syntax equality, or host evaluator soundness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the finite subsequence route of \autoref{thm:higman-lemma-subsequence-embedding-route}. It already places the word windows, source comparisons, embedding data, bad-prefix reduction, Dickson dependency, and structural rows before any downstream handoff.

Now compare with the MetaCIC normalization frontier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicnormalizationfrontier-namecert}. That frontier separates candidate evidence, finished-normal evidence, and obstruction rows; the Higman packet may feed only the finite residual-word evidence side of that separation.

Thus the handoff preserves a finite certificate boundary. It can record that a residual-word window has an embedding route, but it cannot erase the MetaCIC obstruction rows or turn the finite combinatorial packet into a global normalization theorem.
\end{proof}
