\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SuslinTreeUp$}

Following Suslin's tree problem and standard set-theoretic order theory, the $\SuslinTreeUp$ packet records the visible order-theoretic surface of a height-$\omega_1$ tree with no displayed uncountable chain and no displayed uncountable antichain. It sits beside the $\AronszajnTreeUp$ obstruction of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-aronszajn-tree-namecert}, the $\TreeUp$ source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tree-namecert}, the $\POSetUp$ comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poset-namecert}, the unary $\OrderUp$ read of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-order-namecert}, the membership discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-set-namecert}, and the ordinal-analysis ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ordinal-analysis-namecert}. The packet names the finite BEDC route through tree source, partial-order comparison, level accounting, chain-boundary refusal, antichain-boundary refusal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not assert Suslin's hypothesis, construct a Suslin tree in any host universe, choose a club, or decide independence from the usual axioms of set theory.

\begin{definition}[Suslin tree carrier]
\label{def:suslin-tree-carrier}
A $\SuslinTreeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(T,O,L,C,A,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TreeUp$ source row, naming the tree nodes, predecessor route, root discipline, and displayed level map.
\item $O$ is the $\POSetUp$ and $\OrderUp$ comparison row, recording the tree order as a partial-order read with finite predecessor comparison data.
\item $L$ is the level-accounting row, exposing the countable displayed levels and the ordinal-height interface used by $\OrdinalAnalysisUp$.
\item $C$ is the chain-boundary row, recording that no displayed uncountable chain route is exported by the packet.
\item $A$ is the antichain-boundary row, recording that no displayed uncountable antichain route is exported by the packet.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the tree source, order comparison, level ledger, chain boundary, antichain boundary, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from order and level requests through $T,O,L,C,A$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SuslinTreeUp$, $\AronszajnTreeUp$, $\TreeUp$, $\POSetUp$, $\OrderUp$, $\SetUp$, $\OrdinalAnalysisUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SuslinTreeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $T,O,L,C,A,H,K,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host construction of $\omega_1$, a selected club, a forcing extension, a proof or refutation of Suslin's hypothesis, Kurepa splitting, a maximal antichain selector, or a detached set-theoretic universe.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Suslin tree NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:suslin-tree-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SuslinTreeUp$ carrier $S=(T,O,L,C,A,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SuslinTreeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed tree source, order comparison, level ledger, chain boundary, antichain boundary, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; tree-source visibility through $T$; partial-order and unary-order visibility through $O$; level accounting through $L$; chain and antichain boundary visibility through $C,A$; componentwise transport through $H$; displayed continuation through $K$; package provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:suslin-tree-carrier}. The accepted inventory is exactly $T,O,L,C,A,H,K,P,N$, so every consumer must read the tree source, order comparison, level ledger, and two boundary rows before the local name is available.

The sibling anchors supply only their displayed surfaces. The Aronszajn-tree chapter \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-aronszajn-tree-namecert} supplies the height-$\omega_1$ branch-boundary comparison, while the tree, poset, order, set, and ordinal-analysis chapters supply source, comparison, membership, and level-accounting rows.

Rows $H,K,P,N$ preserve and replay that same finite route. Since no carrier coordinate names a host universe, forcing construction, selected club, maximal antichain selector, or decision of Suslin's hypothesis, none of those endpoints can be exported by the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Suslin tree chain--antichain boundary]
\label{thm:suslin-tree-chain-antichain-boundary}
Every order, set, or ordinal-analysis consumer of an accepted $\SuslinTreeUp$ carrier must read both boundary rows
$$
C,A
$$
after the displayed tree source, partial-order comparison, and level ledger rows $T,O,L$. The row $C$ blocks an exported uncountable chain route, and the row $A$ blocks an exported uncountable antichain route. The packet therefore records the Suslin-tree chain--antichain boundary as finite NameCert data beside $\AronszajnTreeUp$, without deciding Suslin's hypothesis, constructing a host Suslin tree, choosing a club, selecting a maximal antichain, or importing a detached set-theoretic universe.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:suslin-tree-namecert-obligations}. It requires the source row $T$, the order row $O$, and the level row $L$ before either boundary can be read.

Read the two boundary rows separately. The chain boundary $C$ refuses an exported uncountable branch or chain route, while the antichain boundary $A$ refuses an exported uncountable antichain route. Keeping both rows visible is what distinguishes the Suslin-tree packet from the sibling $\AronszajnTreeUp$ route.

Finally apply the structural rows of \autoref{def:suslin-tree-carrier}. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve only the displayed boundary packet. They cannot add a host construction, force a set-theoretic decision, choose a club, select a maximal antichain, or import any detached universe data.
\end{proof}
