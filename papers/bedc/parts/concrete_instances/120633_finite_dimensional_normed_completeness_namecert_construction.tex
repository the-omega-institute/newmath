\begin{theorem}[Finite coordinate Cauchy extraction]
\label{thm:finite-dimensional-normed-completeness-cauchy-coordinate}

$$

any Cauchy read of the normed vector sequence factors through the finite basis ledger $B$ and the coordinate-evaluation row $K$ before it reaches the regular rational readback $R$ or the real seal $E$. Thus the extracted evidence consists of finitely many coordinate Cauchy streams, their RegSeqRat readbacks, the displayed norm-control estimates, and the structural rows $H,C,P,L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-dimensional-normed-completeness-carrier}. The only route from the normed source rows $V,N$ to scalar data passes through the finite basis ledger $B$ and the coordinate-evaluation row $K$.

The coordinate Cauchy schedule $S$ is read after those rows are displayed. It supplies one finite-window Cauchy record for each coordinate named by $B$, so the extraction is finite rather than a choice over an external basis.

The rows $R$ and $E$ then read the same coordinate streams as regular rational data and terminal real seals. The norm-control estimates remain attached to those scalar rows because $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the displayed packet.

Since the carrier contains no selected basis, quotient normed space, arbitrary Banach-space completeness field, or choice schedule, no downstream consumer can replace the coordinate extraction by a stronger ambient principle.
\end{proof}
