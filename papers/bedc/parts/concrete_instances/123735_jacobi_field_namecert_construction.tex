Following do Carmo and Kobayashi--Nomizu, the $\JacobiFieldUp$ packet records the finite NameCert surface for a variational vector field along a displayed geodesic. It sits over the Riemannian metric row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannianmetric-namecert}, the connection row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-connection-namecert}, the curvature row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-curvature-namecert}, the geodesic metric surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-geodesic-metric-namecert}, and the vector-bundle bookkeeping of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vectorbundle-namecert}. The packet names only the displayed finite variation ledger; it does not assert a host geodesic-deviation theorem, selected variation family, quotient equality of vector fields, conjugate-point theorem, or ambient smooth solution space.

\begin{definition}[Jacobi field carrier]
\label{def:jacobi-field-carrier}
A $\JacobiFieldUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(G,V,\nabla,R,E,H,C,P,N,A)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the geodesic row, read through $\GeodesicMetricUp$, exposing the base path, endpoint window, tangent-speed readback, and finite subdivision ledger.
\item $V$ is the vector-field row along $G$, read through $\VectorBundleUp$, exposing the fibre row, section row, and fibrewise transport ledger over the same endpoint window.
\item $\nabla$ is the $\ConnectionUp$ row carrying the two covariant-derivative readbacks of $V$ along the geodesic row.
\item $R$ is the $\CurvatureUp$ row exposing the curvature action on the tangent row of $G$ and the vector-field row $V$.
\item $E$ is the endpoint and variation row, recording the displayed endpoint values, variation-family boundary data, and the finite ledger that relates the second covariant derivative row to the curvature row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $G,V,\nabla,R,E$ and their dependency rows.
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay from geodesic and vector-bundle data through connection readback, curvature readback, endpoint variation, and Jacobi-equation inspection.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\JacobiFieldUp$, $\GeodesicMetricUp$, $\VectorBundleUp$, $\ConnectionUp$, $\CurvatureUp$, $\RiemannianMetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\JacobiFieldUp}$ row.
\item $A$ is the audit row stating that every accepted Jacobi read remains inside the displayed geodesic, vector-field, connection, curvature, endpoint, transport, replay, provenance, and local-name packet.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a selected smooth variation, a global solution operator, quotient equality of vector fields, a conjugate-point theorem, or a host-level geodesic-deviation identity outside the displayed finite ledger.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[JacobiField NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:jacobi-field-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\JacobiFieldUp$ carrier $J=(G,V,\nabla,R,E,H,C,P,N,A)$, the local $\NameCert_{\JacobiFieldUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed geodesic row $G$, vector-field row $V$, connection row $\nabla$, curvature row $R$, endpoint and variation row $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, local naming $N$, and audit row $A$. Every downstream conjugate-point or geodesic-deviation consumer must expose these rows before reading the packet as a Jacobi-field certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:jacobi-field-carrier}. The geodesic, vector-field, connection, curvature, endpoint, transport, replay, provenance, local naming, and audit rows are the whole displayed packet.

Read the dependency surfaces in order. The geodesic row is supplied by the geodesic metric certificate, the vector-field row by the vector-bundle certificate, the derivative rows by the connection certificate, and the curvature action by the curvature certificate over the same displayed Riemannian metric source.

Finally replay the endpoint and variation ledger through $H,C,P,N,A$. Since the carrier has no coordinate for selected smooth variations, quotient vector-field equality, global solution operators, conjugate-point theorems, or host geodesic-deviation identities, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
