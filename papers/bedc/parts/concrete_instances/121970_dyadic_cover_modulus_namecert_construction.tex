\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DyadicCoverModulusUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges finite-cover arguments, the $\DyadicCoverModulusUp$ packet records the mesh-control data used when a regular-Cauchy limit route must pass through finitely many dyadic cells. It concretizes the regular-Cauchy vision of \autoref{sec:real-completeness-regular-cauchy} by naming the finite cover modulus that sits between the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular-rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the finite epsilon-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, and the dyadic interval-net cells of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert}. The packet exports only finite mesh coverage and modulus bookkeeping; it does not assert compactness of a host space, selected limits, quotient equality of real names, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Dyadic cover modulus carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-cover-modulus-carrier}
A $\DyadicCoverModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(D,I,E,R,L,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, carrying dyadic radii, mesh widths, half-radius comparisons, and finite refinement requests.
\item $I$ is the $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ cell row naming the finite dyadic interval cells inspected by the cover request.
\item $E$ is the $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ row that records the finite net points or cells at the requested tolerance.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row whose regular-Cauchy windows are controlled by the displayed mesh.
\item $L$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after the dyadic mesh and regular-rational readback have been exposed.
\item $W$ is the cover-modulus row: for each requested dyadic tolerance it records the finite mesh level and cover cells sufficient for the regular-Cauchy read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,I,E,R,L,W,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same dyadic-cover route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicCoverModulusUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicCoverModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $D,I,E,R,L,W,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host compactness, selected limiting covers, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic cover modulus obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-cover-modulus-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicCoverModulusUp$ carrier
$$
K=(D,I,E,R,L,W,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicCoverModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of dyadic tolerance admission through $D$, dyadic interval-cell exposure through $I$, finite epsilon-net exposure through $E$, regular-rational readback through $R$, Real seal delivery through $L$, finite cover-modulus exactness through $W$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-cover-modulus-carrier}. Its rows are exactly the dyadic tolerance ledger, interval-net cells, finite epsilon-net surface, regular-rational readback, Real seal, cover-modulus row, and structural naming rows.

The dyadic mesh is read before any regular-Cauchy consumer reaches the Real seal. The rows $D,I,E$ expose the requested tolerance, the finite interval cells, and the finite net data.

The cover modulus is then the row $W$. It binds each tolerance request to a mesh level and a finite list of cover cells before $R$ and $L$ can be consumed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since none of the excluded host objects is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular-Cauchy finite mesh control]
\label{thm:dyadic-cover-modulus-regular-cauchy-control}
Every regular-Cauchy limit consumer reading an accepted $\DyadicCoverModulusUp$ packet must pass through the finite route
$$
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \DyadicIntervalNetUp,\ \FiniteEpsilonNetUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp
$$
together with the cover-modulus, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. The route supplies finite mesh control for the regular-Cauchy limit read and supplies no host compactness theorem, selected limit, quotient equality of real names, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:dyadic-cover-modulus-obligations}. They force the dyadic tolerance ledger, interval-net row, finite epsilon-net row, and cover-modulus row to be exposed before the regular-rational readback or terminal Real seal is available.

The regular-Cauchy consumer therefore reads mesh control as finite data. A requested tolerance first opens $D$, then chooses the displayed cell level in $I$ and finite cover in $E$, and only then reaches $R$ and $L$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that route without adding a compactness theorem or a selected limiting object. Thus the packet realizes finite mesh control for the regular-Cauchy limit route and nothing stronger.
\end{proof}
