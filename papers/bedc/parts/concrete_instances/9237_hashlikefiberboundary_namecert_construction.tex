\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-hashlikefiberboundary-namecert}
\origin{ai}

The $\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp$ packet realizes the hash-like apophatic
fixed-point discipline of
\autoref{ch:visions-hash-like-apophatic-fixed-point} as a composite finite
NameCert packet. It sits beside the hash certificate of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hash-namecert}, the digest-fiber ledger of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-digestfiberledger-namecert}, the
apophatic-fixed-point fiber of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apophatic-fixed-point-fiber-namecert}, and the
apophatic fixed-point packet of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apophaticfixedpoint-namecert}. Its role is to
bind digest visibility, hidden fiber membership, GAP provenance, apophatic
far-end refusal, and consumer non-escape as one concrete boundary surface.

\begin{definition}[HashLikeFiberBoundary carrier]
\label{def:hash-like-fiber-boundary-carrier}
A $\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(D,F,G,T,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the visible digest row read through the $\HashUp$ certificate;
\item $F$ is the hidden fiber ledger row recording source-side membership
behind $D$ without identifying the source with the digest;
\item $G$ is the $\mathsf{GAP}$ provenance row for the same fiber ledger;
\item $T$ is the apophatic far-end boundary row read through the
$\ApophaticFixedPointFiberUp$ and $\ApophaticFixedPointUp$ sources;
\item $R$ is the refusal row blocking any consumer from treating $T$ as a
digest value, source payload, selected preimage, kernel object, or ambient
universe object;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transports for $D,F,G,T,R,C,P,N$;
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ routes for digest, fiber, GAP,
far-end, refusal, transport, provenance, and local naming reads;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HashUp$, $\DigestFiberLedgerUp$,
$\ApophaticFixedPointFiberUp$, $\ApophaticFixedPointUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$,
$\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for digest-as-source equality, selected
preimage recovery, an internal far-end object, a global universe digest, or an
unledgered fiber consumer.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[HashLikeFiberBoundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:hash-like-fiber-boundary-carrier}, the local
$\NameCert_{\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier
admission for
$$
X=(D,F,G,T,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
visible digest exposure through $D$, hidden fiber-ledger exposure through $F$,
GAP provenance through $G$, apophatic far-end boundary naming through $T$,
far-end refusal through $R$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$,
displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local
naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The rows $D,F,G,T,R$ are the complete concrete
boundary content, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same
finite packet.

Read the source chapters in their displayed order. The $\HashUp$ certificate
supplies digest visibility, $\DigestFiberLedgerUp$ supplies hidden fiber and
GAP provenance, $\ApophaticFixedPointFiberUp$ supplies the fiber-to-far-end
boundary route, and $\ApophaticFixedPointUp$ supplies the refusal discipline
that prevents far-end internalization.

The NameCert surface is exhausted by these rows. Since the carrier has no
coordinate for digest-as-source equality, selected preimage recovery, an
internal far-end object, global universe digest, or unledgered fiber consumer,
no accepted read can obtain any of those objects from the packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[HashLikeFiberBoundary sibling route]
\label{thm:hashlikefiberboundary-sibling-route}
Every accepted $\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp$ packet reads visible digest exposure
from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hash-namecert}, hidden fiber ledger rows
from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-digestfiberledger-namecert}, and far-end
boundary rows from
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apophatic-fixed-point-fiber-namecert} before it
exports the local boundary certificate. Concretely, the rows $D,F,G,T,R,H,C,P,N$
must be displayed as digest visibility, hidden fiber membership, GAP
provenance, apophatic far-end boundary, refusal, transport, replay, provenance,
and local naming rows for the same finite route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project $D$ first. The hash certificate supplies only the visible digest
exposure; it does not expose a hidden fiber or far-end boundary by itself.

Next project $F$ and $G$. The digest-fiber ledger supplies hidden membership
and GAP provenance behind the visible digest, keeping the source-side row
ledgered rather than identified with $D$.

Finally project $T,R,H,C,P,N$. The apophatic-fixed-point fiber supplies the
far-end boundary row, while the refusal, transport, replay, provenance, and
local naming rows keep that boundary attached to the displayed digest-fiber
route before the local certificate is exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[HashLikeFiberBoundary fiber-digest stability]
\label{thm:hashlikefiberboundary-fiber-digest-stability}
Any accepted transport of a $\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp$ packet that preserves the
visible digest row $D$ and the hidden fiber ledger row $F$ must preserve the
same GAP provenance row $G$, apophatic boundary row $T$, and refusal row $R$
through the displayed packet
$$
D,F,G,T,R,H,C,P,N.
$$
Thus the fiber-digest route cannot be replayed as a digest-only route, a
selected preimage route, or a far-end object route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows of
\autoref{def:hash-like-fiber-boundary-carrier}. The rows $D$ and $F$ are not
free-standing coordinates; they are displayed together with $G,T,R,H,C,P,N$ in
one finite packet.

Apply the NameCert obligations of
\autoref{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-namecert-obligations}. They identify the
accepted surface with digest exposure, hidden fiber-ledger exposure, GAP
provenance, apophatic far-end boundary naming, refusal, transport, replay,
provenance, and local naming for the same rows.

Use noninternalization
\autoref{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-noninternalization}. Once transport
preserves $D$ and $F$, the far-end boundary can still be read only through
$G,T,R,H,C,P,N$, so the transport preserves the GAP, apophatic boundary, and
refusal rows rather than creating a digest-only or selected-preimage route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[HashLikeFiberBoundary noninternalization]
\label{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-noninternalization}
Every accepted $\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp$ consumer that reads the far-end row
$T$ receives it only as the apophatic boundary of the displayed fiber behind
the visible digest row $D$. The consumer may replay $T$ through $R,H,C,P,N$,
but it cannot read $T$ as a digest value, source payload, selected preimage,
kernel object, global universe digest, host equality, or ambient completeness
principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier inventory in
\autoref{def:hash-like-fiber-boundary-carrier}. The far-end coordinate is the
boundary row $T$, and it is displayed together with digest, fiber, GAP, and
refusal rows.

Apply the NameCert obligations of
\autoref{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-namecert-obligations}. Any consumer of
$T$ must preserve the route through $D,F,G,T,R,H,C,P,N$, so the far-end read
cannot detach from the fiber ledger that made it visible.

The refusal row $R$ excludes the forbidden interpretations. A read that treats
$T$ as a digest value, payload, selected preimage, object, global digest, host
equality, or completeness principle has no coordinate in the carrier and
therefore no accepted route.
\end{proof}

The far-end refusal row is consumed only after the digest-fiber ledger of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-digestfiberledger-namecert} and the
apophatic-fixed-point fiber of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apophatic-fixed-point-fiber-namecert} have both
been displayed. Thus a consumer reaches the boundary through the fiber ledger
and apophatic fiber before it can read the refusal row.

\begin{theorem}[HashLikeFiberBoundary consumer route]
\label{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-consumer-route}
Every accepted $\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp$ consumer reads the displayed rows
$$
D,F,G,T,R,H,C,P,N

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hash-like-fiber-boundary-carrier}. The rows $D,F,G,T,R$ are ordered as digest, hidden fiber, GAP provenance, far-end boundary, and refusal data, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name that same route.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-namecert-obligations}. They identify local admission with precisely the rows $D,F,G,T,R,H,C,P,N$, so a consumer has no accepted entry point outside the displayed packet.

Finally use noninternalization \autoref{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-noninternalization}. The far-end row can be consumed only after the digest and fiber ledger have been read and only with the refusal row attached, so the consumer route is exactly the ordered route stated above.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[HashLikeFiberBoundary transport stability]
\label{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-transport-stability}
$\hsame$ transport and $\Cont$ replay preserve the same digest, fiber, GAP, far-end, and refusal route of an accepted $\HashLikeFiberBoundaryUp$ packet. Transport may move the rows componentwise, and replay may expose the route again, but neither operation adds selected preimage recovery, digest-as-source equality, internal far-end objects, global universe digests, host equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the consumer route of \autoref{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-consumer-route}. It fixes the ordered path through $D,F,G,T,R,H,C,P,N$ before any endpoint is consumed.

The carrier row $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for those same rows, and the row $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay. Therefore transporting or replaying an accepted packet preserves the digest row, the hidden fiber ledger, GAP provenance, far-end boundary, refusal row, provenance, and local naming route.

Apply noninternalization \autoref{thm:hash-like-fiber-boundary-noninternalization}. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for selected preimage recovery, digest-as-source equality, internal far-end objects, global universe digests, host equality, or ambient completeness, neither transport nor replay can create such a coordinate.
\end{proof}
