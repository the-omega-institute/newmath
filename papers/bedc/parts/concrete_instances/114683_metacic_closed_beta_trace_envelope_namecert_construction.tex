\begin{definition}[MetaCIC closed beta trace-envelope carrier]
\label{def:metacic-closed-beta-trace-envelope-carrier}

$$
E=(S,T,B,S_b,K,R,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source closed term row, including the displayed ClosedAt boundary.
\item $T$ is the target term row reached by the accepted beta-star route.
\item $B$ is the finite beta-chain row, listing the one-step beta moves and congruence positions used by the route.
\item $S_b$ is the substitution replay row used by beta-redex contractions.
\item $K$ is the ClosedAt preservation row for the source, every intermediate beta-chain node, and the target.
\item $R$ records the replay order of shift, variable-substitution, full-substitution, beta-step, and beta-star closedness witnesses.
\item $A$ is the kernel-acceptance trace row saying that the displayed replay is the accepted audit route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, target, beta-chain, substitution, closedness, replay, acceptance, route, provenance, and local naming rows.

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetacicClosedBetaTraceEnvelopeUp$, MetaCIC closure trace, closed-term substitution compiler, TasteGate audit trace, kernel acceptance trace, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetacicClosedBetaTraceEnvelopeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for subject reduction, typed conversion, dependent codomain transport, hidden beta normalization, quotienting of traces, or acceptance outside the displayed kernel route.

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC closed beta trace-envelope preservation]
\label{thm:metacic-closed-beta-trace-envelope-preservation}

The result cannot be obtained from an unrecorded subject-reduction step, typed conversion rule, hidden normalization oracle, or quotient trace identity.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The source and target rows are connected by the finite beta-chain row $B$, and beta-redex contractions can read substitution data only through $S_b$.

The closedness claim is then the row $K$ replayed in the order $R$. It follows the shift, variable-substitution, full-substitution, beta-step, and beta-star preservation witnesses named in the audit map, without inspecting typing judgments.

Finally $A,H,C,P,N$ accept, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier omits typed conversion, dependent codomain transport, hidden normalization, and quotient trace identity, the accepted envelope preserves only the displayed closedness route.
\end{proof}
