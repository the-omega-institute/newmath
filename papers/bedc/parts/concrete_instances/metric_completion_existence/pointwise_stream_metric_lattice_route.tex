\begin{theorem}[MetricCompletionExistence pointwise-stream lattice route]
\label{thm:metric-completion-existence-pointwise-stream-metric-lattice-route}
Any $\MetricCompletionExistenceUp$ read consumed by $\PointwiseStreamMetricUp$ or by a regular Cauchy completion consumer first exposes the finite lattice
$$
\StreamNameUp,\RegSeqRatUp,\RealUp,\MetricSpaceUp,\PointwiseStreamMetricUp
$$
before it reaches the completion-existence boundary. Thus the read travels through displayed stream windows, regular rational readback, terminal real-name sealing, the metric carrier, and the pointwise stream metric row; it is not a quotient completion, a selected limit, an ambient completeness theorem, or a host equality of real streams.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the pointwise-stream metric consumer first. Its distance observations are stream-indexed, so the consumer must display the $\StreamNameUp$ row and the $\PointwiseStreamMetricUp$ row before any completion-existence seal can be used.

For a regular Cauchy completion consumer, the same route exposes the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal on top of the ambient $\MetricSpaceUp$ row. These rows are the finite completion-facing lattice visible to the consumer.

The $\MetricCompletionExistenceUp$ boundary is therefore reached only after the displayed lattice has been opened. Since none of those rows carries quotient completion, selected limits, ambient completeness, or host stream equality, the handoff cannot export those conclusions.
\end{proof}
