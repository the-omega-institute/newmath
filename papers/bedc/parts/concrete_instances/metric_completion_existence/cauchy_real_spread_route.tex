\label{thm:metric-completion-existence-cauchy-real-spread-route}

\begin{proof}

Since the carrier has no quotient-completion coordinate, selected representative, ambient completeness theorem, host equality oracle, trichotomy row, or choice schedule, the route cannot export any of the excluded objects.
\end{proof}
