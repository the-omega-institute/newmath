\begin{theorem}[Metric completion existence separated forward route]
\label{thm:metric-completion-existence-separated-forward-route}
Every $\MetricCompletionExistenceUp$ completion-seal row $U$ is consumed by the separated-completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedcompletion-namecert} only after the $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\CauchyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, and $\CompleteMetricUp$ rows are visible. The forward link exports a displayed completion-existence seal into $\SeparatedCompletionUp$ as finite route evidence, not as quotient completion equality, selected representatives, host point equality, countable choice, or an ambient complete metric space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:metric-completion-existence-carrier}. Its completion-seal row is not isolated; it is read downstream of the metric-space source, Cauchy request, StreamName observation schedule, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance row, Real seal, MetricCompletion row, CompleteMetric row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:metric-completion-existence-namecert-obligations}. The obligation surface forces the seal consumer to expose those prerequisite rows before the completion-existence claim can be replayed or named.

The separated-completion sibling then reads the displayed completion-existence seal as route evidence for \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedcompletion-namecert}. Since the carrier and obligation theorem have no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, host point equality, countable choice, or an ambient complete metric space, the forward route exports none of those endpoints.
\end{proof}
