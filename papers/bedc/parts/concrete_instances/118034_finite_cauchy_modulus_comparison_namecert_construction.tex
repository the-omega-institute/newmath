\begin{definition}[Finite Cauchy modulus comparison carrier]
\label{def:finite-cauchy-modulus-comparison-carrier}
A $\FiniteCauchyModulusComparisonUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(M_0,M_1,W_0,W_1,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)

\begin{enumerate}

$$

\item $M_1$ is the second displayed modulus schedule on the same precision list:
$$
n_0 \mapsto k^1_{n_0},\quad n_1 \mapsto k^1_{n_1},\quad \ldots,\quad n_s \mapsto k^1_{n_s}.

\item $W_0$ and $W_1$ are the shared $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window rows. They expose exactly the stream observations demanded by $M_0$ and $M_1$ on the displayed precision list.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance-comparison ledger. It records the dyadic tolerance cells in which the two schedules are compared and the positive margin used by the comparison route.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row. It records the regular rational approximants obtained from the two windows after the dyadic tolerance comparison has been displayed.

$$

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteCauchyModulusComparisonUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCauchyModulusComparisonUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only by the two displayed schedules, their shared finite windows, the dyadic tolerance-comparison ledger, regular rational readback, the terminal Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local names. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, ambient completeness, or host real equality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite Cauchy modulus comparison NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cauchy-modulus-comparison-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteCauchyModulusComparisonUp$ carrier
$$
B=(M_0,M_1,W_0,W_1,D,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCauchyModulusComparisonUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the two modulus schedules, shared $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance comparison, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item common-window exposure, requiring both schedules to be read on the same finite precision list before any tolerance comparison is available;
\item dyadic comparison exactness, requiring the tolerance ledger $D$ to mediate every comparison between schedule bounds;
\item readback compatibility, requiring the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ to use exactly the stream windows and dyadic tolerance cells displayed by $W_0,W_1,D$;
\item Real-seal nonescape, requiring any downstream Real consumer to pass through $E,H,C,P,N$ after the comparison rows have been displayed.
\end{enumerate}
No finite Cauchy-modulus comparison obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The common-window obligation reads $W_0$ and $W_1$ on the same displayed precision list. This keeps the comparison inside the StreamName windows rather than moving to a quotient stream.

The dyadic comparison row $D$ is then the only route from schedule data to tolerance data. The readback row $R$ receives exactly those windows and tolerance cells, matching the regular rational reconstruction discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}.

Finally the Real seal $E$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the displayed route. Since no carrier coordinate supplies selected limits, ambient completeness, or host real equality, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
