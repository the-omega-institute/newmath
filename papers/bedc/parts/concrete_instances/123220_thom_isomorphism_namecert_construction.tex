\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ThomIsomorphismUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-thom-isomorphism-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Thom and Bott--Tu, the $\ThomIsomorphismUp$ packet records the finite NameCert surface for reading a displayed Thom-class handoff from an oriented vector-bundle window into a cohomology consumer. It sits over the vector-bundle rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vectorbundle-namecert}, the cohomology substrate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cohomology-namecert}, the bundle carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bundle-namecert}, the manifold chart source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-manifold-namecert}, and the compact support boundaries supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}. The packet names the finite carrier route by which an oriented bundle read reaches a Thom-class cup and pullback ledger; it is not a host isomorphism theorem, selected fundamental class, quotient cohomology equality, ambient compact-support construction, or global orientation principle.

\begin{definition}[Thom isomorphism carrier]
\label{def:thom-isomorphism-carrier}
A $\ThomIsomorphismUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(B,V,O,K,U,L,A,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\BundleUp$ source row, read through the finite bundle carrier and projection data.
\item $V$ is the $\VectorBundleUp$ refinement row. It records the displayed fiber rank, local trivialization windows, and transition checks consumed by the Thom read.
\item $O$ is the orientation row. It stores only the finite orientation ledger for the displayed bundle windows and carries no global host orientation theorem.
\item $K$ is the compact-support window, read through $\CompactUp$ and $\CompactMetricUp$ support rows before any cohomology output is exported.
\item $U$ is the $\CohomologyUp$ source row for the base-side class and compact-support target window.
\item $L$ is the Thom-class ledger. It records the local class rows, support compatibility, and overlap agreement inspected by the packet.
\item $A$ is the cup and pullback handoff row. It names the ordered read from $U$ and $L$ into the displayed target cohomology window.
\item $R$ is the transport row from bundle charts, manifold charts, compact support, and cohomology rows into the same packet.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for bundle, vector-bundle, orientation, compact-support, cohomology, Thom-class, cup/pullback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Thom read through $B,V,O,K,U,L,A,R,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ThomIsomorphismUp$, $\VectorBundleUp$, $\CohomologyUp$, $\BundleUp$, $\ManifoldUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ThomIsomorphismUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,V,O,K,U,L,A,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host isomorphism, quotient cohomology equality, selected fundamental classes, ambient compact support, global orientation choice, Lean verification, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Thom isomorphism NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:thom-isomorphism-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ThomIsomorphismUp$ carrier
$$
T=(B,V,O,K,U,L,A,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ThomIsomorphismUp}$ surface consists exactly of bundle source exposure through $B$, vector-bundle refinement through $V$, orientation-ledger exposure through $O$, compact-support and compact-metric support exposure through $K$, cohomology source and target-window exposure through $U$, Thom-class ledger exactness through $L$, cup and pullback handoff through $A$, chart and support transport through $R$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.ThomIsomorphismUp.ThomIsomorphismTasteGate\_single\_carrier\_alignment}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:thom-isomorphism-carrier}. The projections display the bundle, vector-bundle, orientation, compact-support, cohomology, Thom-class, cup/pullback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The source rows are finite. Rows $B$ and $V$ expose only the displayed bundle and vector-bundle windows, while $O$ records the orientation ledger attached to those windows. Row $K$ forces compact support to be read through the compact and compact-metric sibling surfaces before any target cohomology window is consumed.

The cohomology handoff is also row-local. Row $U$ supplies the source and target windows, $L$ supplies the Thom-class ledger, and $A$ records the cup and pullback handoff in that order. Thus no consumer can replace the packet by a host Thom isomorphism, quotient cohomology equality, selected fundamental class, or ambient compact-support construction.

Finally $R,H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly those rows. Since the carrier contains no additional isomorphism or choice coordinate, the listed projections exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
