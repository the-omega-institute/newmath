Following Bishop and Bridges on regular Cauchy completion and Kelley--Bourbaki reflection terminology, the $\RegularCauchyReflectionCounitUp$ packet records the counit route from the completed reflector surface back to the displayed $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ seal rows. It is paired with the unit packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-reflection-unit-namecert} and reads the reflector boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-completion-reflector-namecert}, the regular source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy reflection counit carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-reflection-counit-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyReflectionCounitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(E,R,S,D,F,U,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the completed reflector and $\RealUp$ seal row from which a Real-facing consumer begins.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row exposed by the counit.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which the readback is inspected.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger attached to every displayed window.
\item $F$ is the $\RegularCauchyCompletionReflectorUp$ row that supplies the completion-reflector source of the counit read.
\item $U$ is the unit and counit compatibility row pairing this packet with the displayed $\RegularCauchyReflectionUnitUp$ route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the completed seal, readback, finite windows, dyadic ledger, reflector row, compatibility row, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$

$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyReflectionCounitUp$, $\RegularCauchyCompletionReflectorUp$, $\RegularCauchyReflectionUnitUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyReflectionCounitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows and their replay order. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, a selected representative, countable choice, host equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy reflection counit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-reflection-counit-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyReflectionCounitUp$ carrier $C=(E,R,S,D,F,U,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyReflectionCounitUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the completed-source and $\RealUp$ seal row, regular readback row, finite window row, dyadic tolerance ledger, completion-reflector row, unit and counit compatibility row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. The counit handoff is visible only as the finite route from $E$ through $R,S,D$ and then through $F,U,H,K,P,N$; it does not export quotient stream equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, trichotomy, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-reflection-counit-carrier}. The completed-source row $E$ is the only Real-facing entry point, while $R,S,D$ expose the regular readback, finite observation windows, and dyadic tolerance ledger that the counit may return.

The handoff is ordered by the displayed rows. A consumer first reads the completed seal, then the RegSeqRat readback and its inspection data, and only then reaches the reflector and compatibility rows $F$ and $U$.

The Real-seal nonescape follows from the carrier inventory. No row supplies quotient stream equality, a selected representative, countable choice, host real equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, so none of these can be exported by the local certificate.

Finally, $H$ and $K$ preserve and replay the same route, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming. Transport, replay, and naming therefore cannot replace the counit packet by any object outside the displayed finite surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy reflection counit Real handoff]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-reflection-counit-real-handoff}
Every Real-facing counit read from an accepted $\RegularCauchyReflectionCounitUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
E \longmapsto R,S,D \longmapsto F,U \longmapsto H,K,P,N .
$$
The handoff returns from the completed reflector surface to displayed $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ rows before any reflector or unit-compatibility row may be consumed. It exports no quotient stream equality, selected representative, countable choice, host equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the Real-facing row $E$. By definition of the carrier, the counit has no other completed-source coordinate and no hidden host completion object.

The readback rows $R,S,D$ are next in the displayed route. They expose the regular rational name, the finite windows through which it is read, and the dyadic tolerance ledger attached to those windows.

Only after that readback may the reflector row $F$ and compatibility row $U$ be consumed. The structural rows $H,K,P,N$ then transport, replay, record, and name the same finite route.

Since the carrier contains no quotient equality, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, host equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness row, the Real-facing handoff cannot export any of those principles.
\end{proof}
