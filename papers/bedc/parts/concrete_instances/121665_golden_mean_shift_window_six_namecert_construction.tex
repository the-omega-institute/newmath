Following Lind and Marcus, the golden-mean subshift is the finite-type no-adjacent-one shift. The $\GoldenMeanShiftWindowSixUp$ packet realizes the window-six kernel of \autoref{def:axiszeckendorf-golden-mean-shift-kernel} as finite $\BHist$ data: a six-cell binary window, a two-state transition classifier, a shift-stability row, a finite-window growth row, Zeckendorf carry-facing provenance through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-zeckendorf-normal-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-zcarry-namecert}, and local NameCert evidence. The packet names the finite symbolic-dynamics carrier used by the Axis-Zeckendorf vision; it is not an invariant measure, a two-sided completion, a host entropy theorem, or a claim that all Zeckendorf carries have been bridged.

\begin{definition}[Golden-mean window-six carrier]
\label{def:golden-mean-shift-window-six-carrier}
A $\GoldenMeanShiftWindowSixUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(W,K,T,S,F,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is a six-symbol binary window row.
\item $K$ is the no-adjacent-one transition-kernel classifier of \autoref{def:axiszeckendorf-golden-mean-shift-kernel}, restricted to adjacent pairs inside $W$.
\item $T$ is the transition ledger recording which adjacent pairs are admitted by $K$.
\item $S$ is the one-step shift-stability row: deleting the first cell and reading the remaining five-cell suffix preserves the admitted adjacent-pair rows.
\item $F$ is the finite-window growth row, recording the Fibonacci recurrence of \autoref{prop:axiszeckendorf-golden-growth-finite-windows} at the inspected window length.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the window, transition classifier, transition ledger, shift row, growth row, replay row, provenance row, and local name.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the window read through $K,T,S,F,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\GoldenMeanShiftWindowSixUp$, the Axis-Zeckendorf kernel of \autoref{def:axiszeckendorf-golden-mean-shift-kernel}, $\ZeckendorfNormalUp$, $\ZCarryUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\GoldenMeanShiftWindowSixUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $W,K,T,S,F,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host subshift object, invariant measure, entropy limit, two-sided completion, unrestricted carry propagation, or bridge credential.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Golden-mean transition-kernel obligations]
\label{thm:golden-mean-shift-window-six-transition-kernel-obligations}
For every accepted $\GoldenMeanShiftWindowSixUp$ carrier $G=(W,K,T,S,F,H,C,P,N)$, the local transition-kernel obligations are exactly pair admission by the no-adjacent-one classifier, transport of those pair rows under $\hsame$, and replay of the admitted finite window through $C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:golden-mean-shift-window-six-carrier}. The only transition coordinates are $K$ and $T$, so every adjacent-pair decision is read through the displayed no-adjacent-one classifier.

The transport row $H$ carries the same pair decisions under accepted renaming of the finite window. Replay and provenance through $C,P,N$ can name the route but cannot add another transition classifier.

Since no host subshift, measure, entropy, or unrestricted carry coordinate belongs to the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local transition obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Golden-mean window-six shift stability]
\label{thm:golden-mean-shift-window-six-shift-stability}
Every accepted $\GoldenMeanShiftWindowSixUp$ carrier exports shift stability only by reading $S$ after $W,K,T$ have displayed the no-adjacent-one pair rows. The shifted five-cell suffix remains inside the same transition-kernel classifier and the same $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the finite window $W$ and transition rows $K,T$. They display every adjacent pair whose preservation matters after the first cell is dropped.

The shift row $S$ is then a suffix read of the same finite window. It preserves all adjacent-pair rows that remain in the suffix and does not introduce a second classifier.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that suffix route. Thus shift stability is a finite NameCert row, not a host dynamical-system completion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Golden-mean window-six finite growth route]
\label{thm:golden-mean-shift-window-six-finite-growth-route}
Any finite-window growth read from an accepted $\GoldenMeanShiftWindowSixUp$ packet factors through $F$ after the transition classifier $K$ and ledger $T$ have been displayed. The read may cite the recurrence of \autoref{prop:axiszeckendorf-golden-growth-finite-windows}, but it exports only the inspected finite growth row and not a host entropy limit or a completed inverse-limit carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the kernel obligations of \autoref{thm:golden-mean-shift-window-six-transition-kernel-obligations}. They ensure that the no-adjacent-one grammar has been displayed as finite adjacent-pair data.

The growth row $F$ reads the same grammar at the inspected window length. It records the Fibonacci recurrence locally, using the vision proposition only as the named source of the finite recurrence.

The refusal inventory in \autoref{def:golden-mean-shift-window-six-carrier} excludes host entropy and completed inverse-limit coordinates. Therefore the growth route remains a finite NameCert row.
\end{proof}
