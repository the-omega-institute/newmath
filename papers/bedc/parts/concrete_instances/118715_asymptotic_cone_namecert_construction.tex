\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\AsymptoticConeUp$}

Following Gromov's asymptotic-cone large-scale metric construction, the $\AsymptoticConeUp$ packet records the finite scaling-window surface by which a pointed metric family is read at increasing scales. It replaces nonprincipal ultrafilters and selected limiting cones by explicit scale ledgers, finite metric-ball windows, dyadic tolerance rows, regular rational readback, and a terminal Real seal. The packet sits beside the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the ball-window surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metricball-namecert}, the dyadic ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[AsymptoticCone carrier]
\label{def:asymptotic-cone-carrier}
An $\AsymptoticConeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(M,o,S,B,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, naming only the displayed distance comparisons available to the packet.
\item $o$ is the basepoint row attached to the same metric source.
\item $S$ is the scaling ledger, a finite list of scale requests and comparison budgets.
\item $B$ is the $\MetricBallUp$ window row, recording which bounded metric balls are inspected after rescaling.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row for the finite window schedule.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row used to compare rescaled distances.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational distance families.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $S,B,W,D,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, basepoint, scale, ball-window, stream, dyadic, regular-rational, Real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a scaling-window request through the same rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\AsymptoticConeUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\MetricBallUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AsymptoticConeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a nonprincipal ultrafilter, a selected metric limit, a quotient cone, host real equality, or ambient completeness of the source metric or $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[AsymptoticCone NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:asymptotic-cone-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AsymptoticConeUp$ carrier
$$
A=(M,o,S,B,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\AsymptoticConeUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric-source admission through $M,o$, scaling-window exposure through $S$, bounded ball-window exposure through $B$, finite stream observation through $W$, dyadic tolerance comparison through $D$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal Real sealing through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:asymptotic-cone-carrier}. The metric and basepoint rows $M,o$ are the only source rows, and the scaling ledger $S$ names exactly the scale requests visible to the packet.

The bounded windows are displayed before any Real-facing read. The row $B$ supplies the metric balls, $W$ lists the finite observation schedule, $D$ supplies dyadic tolerance comparisons, and $R$ supplies the regular rational readback used by the terminal seal $E$.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same displayed packet. Since no coordinate contains an ultrafilter, selected limit, quotient cone, host equality, or ambient completeness theorem, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[AsymptoticCone scaling window]
\label{thm:asymptotic-cone-scaling-window}
Every coarse-metric consumer of an accepted $\AsymptoticConeUp$ carrier must read the finite route
$$
\MetricUp,\ \MetricBallUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp
$$
through the scale ledger $S$ before treating a rescaled distance family as cone-facing data. The route exposes only displayed scale requests, bounded ball windows, finite stream observations, dyadic tolerances, regular rational readbacks, and terminal Real seals; it exports no nonprincipal ultrafilter, selected limiting point, quotient cone equality, host real equality, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:asymptotic-cone-namecert-obligations}. They force every accepted read through the metric source, basepoint, scaling ledger, ball-window row, finite observation row, dyadic tolerance row, regular readback row, Real seal, and structural rows.

The scale ledger $S$ controls the order of the read. A consumer can inspect a rescaled distance only after the matching $\MetricBallUp$ window has been displayed and the dyadic tolerance row has attached a finite comparison budget to that window.

The StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real rows then provide readback rather than a completed cone object. Because the carrier has no ultrafilter, selected-limit, quotient, host-equality, or completeness coordinate, the scaling-window route cannot export any of those endpoints.
\end{proof}
