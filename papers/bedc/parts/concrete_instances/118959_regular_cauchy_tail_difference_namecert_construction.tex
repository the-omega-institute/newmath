\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RegularCauchyTailDifferenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-tail-difference-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges' constructive-analysis treatment of regular
Cauchy names, the $\RegularCauchyTailDifferenceUp$ packet records the finite
two-tail comparison surface visible before a $\RealUp$ seal is consumed. It
sits over the one-tail estimate route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchytailestimate-namecert}, the
dyadic tolerance surface of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream
window of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular
rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and
the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The
packet compares two displayed regular Cauchy tails; it is not a quotient
stream equality, a selected limit, a countable-choice schedule, host real
equality, or an ambient completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy tail difference carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-tail-difference-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyTailDifferenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
\Delta=(M_0,M_1,W_0,W_1,D,R_0,R_1,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M_0$ and $M_1$ are the two tail-threshold rows, one for each accepted
regular Cauchy tail estimate.
\item $W_0$ and $W_1$ are the two $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window rows inspected
at and beyond the displayed thresholds.
\item $D$ is the shared $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row against which the two
tail windows are compared.
\item $R_0$ and $R_1$ are the two $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback rows reached from
$W_0,D$ and $W_1,D$.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal boundary that may be consumed only after both
threshold, window, tolerance, and readback routes have been replayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the two threshold rows,
the two windows, the shared dyadic tolerance, the two readbacks, the seal
boundary, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a two-tail comparison request
through $M_0,M_1,W_0,W_1,D,R_0,R_1$ and then $E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyTailEstimateUp$,
$\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$,
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyTailDifferenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only by transporting these displayed
rows to a common finite two-tail surface. It has no coordinate for quotient
streams, selected limits, host real equality, countable choice, trichotomy,
open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy tail difference NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-tail-difference-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyTailDifferenceUp$ carrier
$\Delta=(M_0,M_1,W_0,W_1,D,R_0,R_1,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyTailDifferenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of five
obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the two thresholds, two stream windows, shared
dyadic tolerance, two regular readbacks, real seal boundary, transport,
continuation, provenance, and local name;
\item classifier transport to a common finite surface over
$\RegularCauchyTailEstimateUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$,
$\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$;
\item stability of $M_0,M_1,W_0,W_1,D,R_0,R_1,E$ under the componentwise
$\hsame$ rows recorded in $H$;
\item seal-route exactness requiring every Real-facing two-tail comparison to
pass through both displayed threshold-window-readback routes before $E$ is
public;
\item refusal rows excluding quotient streams, selected limits, host real
equality, countable choice, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, and ambient
completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:regular-cauchy-tail-difference-carrier}. The projection exposes
exactly the two threshold rows, two finite windows, shared dyadic tolerance,
two regular readbacks, real seal boundary, transport, continuation,
provenance, and local naming rows.

Use \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-tail-difference}. The sibling theorem in the
one-tail estimate chapter says that two accepted tail-estimate packets sharing
transported threshold and dyadic rows compare through their displayed
$\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ seal rows.

The structural rows do not create another comparison surface. The row $H$
transports only the displayed coordinates, $C$ replays the same two-tail route,
and $P,N$ record provenance and local naming for the finite packet.

Since no quotient stream, selected limit, host equality, countable-choice
schedule, trichotomy, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness
coordinate occurs in the carrier, none can appear as a local obligation.
\end{proof}
