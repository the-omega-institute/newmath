\label{thm:bishop-located-complete-metric-namecert-obligations}

\item carrier admission for the displayed metric source, locatedness requests, complete-metric service, completion boundary, stream windows, regular rational readback, terminal real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;

Finite readback exactness follows from the order of $W,R,E$. The stream windows expose the inspected approximants and tolerances, the regular rational row records the distance readbacks, and only then can the real seal be consumed.
