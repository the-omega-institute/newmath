\begin{definition}[Dini modulus carrier]
\label{def:dini-modulus-carrier}
A $\DiniModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(K,F,M,T,U,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite compact-source row that supplies the inspected net and tolerance cells.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ family row, read only through the displayed compact-source windows.
\item $M$ is the monotone comparison row recording the finite descent of the family toward the terminal real row.
\item $T$ is the uniform-tail request row, recording the requested dyadic tolerance and the finite tail index visible to the packet.
\item $U$ is the exported modulus row: it names the tail index and compact-window budget that every uniform-tail consumer must read.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same compact window and tolerance request.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $K,F,M,T,U,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, continuous family, monotone comparison, tail request, modulus, readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a uniform-tail request through $K,F,M,T,U,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DiniModulusUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DiniModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,F,M,T,U,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, a selected finite subcover, a host supremum, quotient equality of functions, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dini modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dini-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DiniModulusUp$ carrier $D=(K,F,M,T,U,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DiniModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, compact-source exposure, continuous-family exposure, monotone comparison visibility, uniform-tail request admission, modulus-row export, regular rational readback, Real-seal non-escape, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming. Every obligation is read from $K,F,M,T,U,R,E,H,C,P,N$ and no obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dini-modulus-carrier}. The analytic rows are the compact source $K$, continuous family $F$, monotone comparison $M$, uniform-tail request $T$, exported modulus $U$, readback row $R$, and terminal Real seal $E$.

The obligation order is finite. A tolerance request first exposes the compact-source row and continuous-family row, then the monotone comparison row, and only then the uniform-tail request and exported modulus rows.

The readback and seal rows are downstream. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ reads the same compact window and tolerance request, while the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ is visible only after $K,F,M,T,U,R$ have been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name exactly that route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, host suprema, quotient function equality, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness, the local NameCert surface cannot export any of them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dini modulus uniform-tail route]
\label{thm:dini-modulus-uniform-tail-route}
Every uniform-tail consumer of an accepted $\DiniModulusUp$ carrier must factor through the displayed route
$$
\CompactMetricUp,\quad \ContinuousMapUp,\quad \RealUp,\quad
\RegSeqRatUp,\quad \BHist,\quad \hsame,\quad \Cont,\quad \Pkg,\quad \NameCert .
$$
Concretely, the consumer first reads the compact metric source and continuous family, then the monotone comparison and tail-request rows, then the exported modulus row and regular rational readback, and only then the terminal $\RealUp$ seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier rows of \autoref{def:dini-modulus-carrier}. The uniform-tail output is the row $U$, and the definition places it downstream of the compact source $K$, continuous family $F$, monotone comparison $M$, and tail request $T$.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:dini-modulus-namecert-obligations}. They force every consumer of $U$ to expose the compact-source and continuous-family rows before the tail request can be inspected.

The terminal read is the regular-readback and Real-seal route $R,E$. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name that same route, so the consumer receives only the displayed finite uniform-tail modulus interface and no host compactness, selected subcover, quotient function equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{proof}
