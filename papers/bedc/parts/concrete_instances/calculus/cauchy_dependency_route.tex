\begin{theorem}[Calculus Cauchy dependency route]
\label{thm:calculus-cauchy-dependency-route}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the calculus local-linearization carrier. Its derivative, integral, and limit rows are interpreted only together with the regular rational readback row and the terminal real seal.

Apply the root local-linearization Cauchy handoff. That theorem keeps the derivative-facing calculus window behind the displayed Cauchy rows, dyadic tolerance ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before any $\RealUp$ endpoint is public.

Finally read the sibling route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-limit-namecert}. These chapters supply the finite readback, real boundary, and limit consumer rows consumed by calculus; none supplies an ambient host completeness or quotient-function equality coordinate.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:calculus-cauchy-dependency-real-completion-readback}
