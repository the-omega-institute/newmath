\begin{theorem}[Calculus located-compact dependency route]
\label{thm:calculus-locatedcompact-dependency-route}
Every $\LocatedCompactUp$ consumer of a $\CalculusUp$ derivative, limit, or local-linearization row factors through the finite calculus route displayed by \autoref{def:calculus-local-linearization-carrier}, \autoref{thm:calculus-root-local-linearization-cauchy-handoff}, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, and $\RealUp$ before it reaches the located-compact packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedcompact-namecert}. The handoff exports a finite graph-and-readback window for compactness use, not open-cover compactness, a selected finite subcover, quotient function equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the calculus local-linearization carrier. Its derivative and limit rows expose graph data through $\ContinuousMapUp$, regular rational readback through $\RegSeqRatUp$, and endpoint values through the $\RealUp$ boundary.

Use \autoref{thm:calculus-root-local-linearization-cauchy-handoff} for the Cauchy-facing derivative window. That route displays the derivative row, graph row, readback row, dyadic tolerance ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before the Real boundary is consumed.

Only after those rows are visible may the consumer enter \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedcompact-namecert}. The located-compact packet therefore receives finite calculus data suitable for its finite-net and locatedness rows, with no host compactness theorem or selected subcover coordinate added by the calculus route.
\end{proof}
