Following Kelley, Moore--Smith, and Bourbaki, the $\NetConvergenceCriterionUp$ packet records the directed-net convergence test as finite L10 NameCert data rather than as an ambient filter or topological limit assertion. It reads directed indices from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-directedset-namecert}, net windows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-moore-smith-net-namecert}, eventual-tail comparison through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-filterbase-namecert}, uniform entourage control through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, and Cauchy-tail consumers through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchynet-namecert}. The packet is a nonquotient criterion surface for directed convergence; it is not a selected subnet, a host limit point, countable choice, quotient net equality, or ambient compactness.

\begin{definition}[Net convergence criterion carrier]
\label{def:net-convergence-criterion-carrier}
A $\NetConvergenceCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(D,N,F,U,T,E,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DirectedSetUp$ index row, carrying the finite comparison and upper-bound witnesses that a tail request may inspect.
\item $N$ is the $\MooreSmithNetUp$ observation row, recording the source net values opened at the displayed directed indices.
\item $F$ is the $\FilterBaseUp$ eventual-tail row, translating a neighborhood or entourage request into a displayed tail base.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ control row, exposing the entourage ledger against which the net values are compared.
\item $T$ is the convergence-test row, pairing every accepted entourage request with a tail base and the finite net windows required on that tail.
\item $E$ is the $\CauchyNetUp$ handoff row, available only after the directed, net, filter-base, uniform-space, and convergence-test rows have all been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $D,N,F,U,T,E$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a convergence request through directed indices, net windows, filter-base tails, uniform entourages, and the Cauchy-net handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\NetConvergenceCriterionUp$, $\DirectedSetUp$, $\MooreSmithNetUp$, $\FilterBaseUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\CauchyNetUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\NetConvergenceCriterionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
Accepted packets are compared only through $D,N,F,U,T,E,H,C,P,L$. No coordinate stores a host limit, a selected subnet, quotient equality of nets, countable choice, or an ambient compactness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Net convergence criterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:net-convergence-criterion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\NetConvergenceCriterionUp$ carrier $Q=(D,N,F,U,T,E,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\NetConvergenceCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of directed-index admission, Moore--Smith net window exposure, filter-base eventual-tail exposure, uniform-space entourage accounting, convergence-test exactness, Cauchy-net handoff admission, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming for the same displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:net-convergence-criterion-carrier}. The convergence request first exposes the directed index row $D$ and the Moore--Smith net row $N$, so every later tail read is tied to displayed directed data.

The eventual and uniform parts are then fixed. The filter-base row $F$ supplies the admitted tail base, while $U$ supplies the entourage ledger against which the net windows are compared. The test row $T$ records only those paired tail and entourage checks.

The handoff $E$ is downstream of those rows and may be consumed by Cauchy-net readers only after the criterion surface is visible. The structural rows $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no carrier row stores a host limit, selected subnet, quotient equality, choice schedule, or compactness witness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Net convergence criterion filter handoff]
\label{thm:net-convergence-criterion-filter-handoff}
Every $\FilterBaseUp$ or $\CauchyNetUp$ consumer of an accepted $\NetConvergenceCriterionUp$ packet must read the route
$$
D,N \longmapsto F,U \longmapsto T \longmapsto E,H,C,P,L
$$
before it may inspect the convergence result. Thus the criterion hands off a displayed eventual-tail test over directed Moore--Smith net data, not a quotient filter, selected convergent subnet, host limit point, countable-choice schedule, or ambient compactness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the directed and net rows. The consumer can inspect only the index comparisons and finite net windows exposed by $D$ and $N$.

Next read the filter and uniform rows. The row $F$ gives the tail base used for the request, and $U$ gives the entourage ledger. The convergence-test row $T$ binds those two rows to the same net windows.

Finally the Cauchy-net handoff $E$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,L$ preserve that route for downstream readers. Because every public read factors through the displayed packet, the handoff cannot be replaced by a quotient filter, selected subnet, host limit, choice principle, or ambient compactness assertion.
\end{proof}
