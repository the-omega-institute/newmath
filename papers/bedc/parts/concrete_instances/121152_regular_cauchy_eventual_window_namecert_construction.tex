\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RegularCauchyEventualWindowUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-eventual-window-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on regular Cauchy sequences and explicit moduli, the $\RegularCauchyEventualWindowUp$ packet records the finite eventual-window route by which a regular Cauchy request exposes a StreamName window, a DyadicRatCore tolerance row, a RegSeqRat readback, a Real seal, and a DiagonalLimitRepresentative handoff. It sits beside the finite-window discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic arithmetic core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal Real carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the diagonal-limit representative packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-diagonallimitrepresentative-namecert}. The packet is a choice-free finite tail-window interface; it is not quotient stream equality, a selected host limit, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy eventual-window carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-eventual-window-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyEventualWindowUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(S,D,R,E,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ eventual-window request row naming the finite tail stage and the precision at which the window is read.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row recording the dyadic comparison bound used inside the tail window.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the displayed stream window and tolerance data.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $S,D,R$ have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the $\DiagonalLimitRepresentativeUp$ handoff row that consumes the same finite window before any diagonal-limit consumer reads a representative.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the window, tolerance, readback, seal, handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a regular Cauchy precision request through the eventual window, dyadic tolerance, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, and diagonal-limit handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyEventualWindowUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DiagonalLimitRepresentativeUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyEventualWindowUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,D,R,E,L,H,C,P,N$. It stores no coordinate for a quotient of streams, a chosen limit object, a countable-choice selector, or an ambient completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy eventual-window NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-eventual-window-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyEventualWindowUp$ carrier $W=(S,D,R,E,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyEventualWindowUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, eventual-window exposure, dyadic tolerance compatibility, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal handoff, diagonal-limit representative handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. A consumer must read the tail stage through $S$, the tolerance through $D$, and the regular rational row through $R$ before the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ or the diagonal-limit row $L$ is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-eventual-window-carrier}. The rows $S,D,R,E,L$ are the only analytic rows available before the structural support rows are read.

The eventual-window exposure starts with $S$. A downstream consumer receives a displayed finite tail stage and precision request, not a global quotient of streams or a selected limit.

Next read the dyadic tolerance row $D$ and the regular rational readback row $R$. Compatibility means that the tolerance data belongs to the same tail window and that the RegSeqRat row is obtained from that displayed window.

Finally read $E$ and $L$. The Real seal and diagonal-limit representative handoff are both downstream of the same finite window. The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only this displayed packet, so the NameCert surface cannot import countable choice or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy eventual-window tail handoff]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-eventual-window-tail-handoff}
Every diagonal-limit or Real-completion consumer of an accepted $\RegularCauchyEventualWindowUp$ packet reads through the finite tail-window route
$$
\StreamNameUp,\quad
\DyadicRatCoreUp,\quad
\RegSeqRatUp,\quad
\RealUp,\quad
\DiagonalLimitRepresentativeUp .
$$
In carrier notation, the consumer must expose $S,D,R,E,L$ before it treats the packet as a limit-facing handoff, while $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:regular-cauchy-eventual-window-namecert-obligations}. They place every Real-facing or diagonal-limit-facing read downstream of the same displayed tail window and dyadic tolerance row.

Read the StreamName and DyadicRatCore rows together. The tail stage is usable only with its tolerance witness, so the route cannot jump directly from a regular Cauchy request to a completed real or a diagonal representative.

The RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, and diagonal-limit handoff close the finite route. Since the support rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same packet, no consumer can replace the handoff by quotient stream equality, a selected limit, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
