\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CartanDecompositionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cartan-decomposition-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Helgason and Knapp, the $\CartanDecompositionUp$ packet records the finite involution and eigenspace surface used to read a Lie algebra or Lie group through its compact and noncompact rows. It is grounded in the Lie-group route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-liegroup-namecert}, the Lie-algebra carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-liealgebra-namecert}, the root-system and Weyl-group consumers of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rootsystem-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-weylgroup-namecert}, and the bilinear-form row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bilinform-namecert}. The chapter names the displayed decomposition as finite NameCert data; it does not assert classification of real reductive groups, global symmetric-space theory, selected Cartan subalgebras, or analytic integration.

\begin{definition}[Cartan decomposition carrier]
\label{def:cartan-decomposition-carrier}
A $\CartanDecompositionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(G,L,\Theta,K,P,E,B,R,W,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the $\LieGroupUp$ source row when a group-level source is present; otherwise it is the explicit absence row for group-level data.
\item $L$ is the $\LieAlgebraUp$ row whose bracket and additive vector-space operations are the only algebraic operations read by the packet.
\item $\Theta$ is the involution row. It records an endomap of the displayed Lie-algebra carrier with $\Theta^2$ classified with the identity by the local carrier relation.
\item $K$ is the compact eigenspace row, recording the displayed fixed endpoints of $\Theta$.
\item $P$ is the noncompact eigenspace row, recording the displayed negated endpoints of $\Theta$.
\item $E$ is the eigenspace stability ledger. It records bracket routing for $[K,K]$ into $K$, $[K,P]$ into $P$, and $[P,P]$ into $K$ whenever those brackets are displayed.
\item $B$ is the $\BilinFormUp$ compatibility row used by consumers that inspect orthogonality, sign, or Killing-form-style pairings.
\item $R$ is the $\RootSystemUp$ consumer row for restricted-root reads of the displayed $P$-side direction data.
\item $W$ is the $\WeylGroupUp$ consumer row for reflection reads that are sourced through the same root-system row $R$.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the group source, Lie algebra, involution, eigenspace, bracket-stability, bilinear-form, root, Weyl, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a decomposition request through the rows $G,L,\Theta,K,P,E,B,R,W,H$.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CartanDecompositionUp$, $\LieGroupUp$, $\LieAlgebraUp$, $\RootSystemUp$, $\WeylGroupUp$, $\BilinFormUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CartanDecompositionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host symmetric space, a selected maximal abelian subspace, analytic integration, or classification of real forms.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cartan decomposition involution eigenspace stability]
\label{thm:cartan-decomposition-involution-eigenspace-stability}
For every accepted $\CartanDecompositionUp$ carrier
$$
D=(G,L,\Theta,K,P,E,B,R,W,H,C,Q,N),
$$
any consumer that reads the public decomposition must first retain the involution row $\Theta$, the compact row $K$, the noncompact row $P$, and the bracket-stability ledger $E$. The public read may then pass to the $\BilinFormUp$, $\RootSystemUp$, and $\WeylGroupUp$ rows, but it cannot replace the finite rows by a host symmetric-space theorem, a selected Cartan subspace, analytic integration, or classification of real forms.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cartan-decomposition-carrier}. The Lie-algebra source is the row $L$, and the decomposition data are exactly the involution $\Theta$ with the displayed fixed and negated rows $K$ and $P$.

The stability claim is the row $E$. It keeps the three bracket routes $[K,K]$, $[K,P]$, and $[P,P]$ attached to the same involution and eigenspace rows before any downstream consumer can inspect the decomposition.

The bilinear-form and root-theoretic consumers are downstream from this retained surface. Rows $B,R,W$ may read only the pairing, restricted-root, and reflection data named by the carrier, while $H,C,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no excluded symmetric-space, selected-subspace, analytic, or classification row is present, the consumer is confined to the displayed NameCert route.
\end{proof}
