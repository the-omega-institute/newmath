\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DieudonneCompletionUp$}

Following Dieudonne, Bourbaki, and Kelley on completion of uniform spaces, the $\DieudonneCompletionUp$ packet records the finite BEDC boundary by which a displayed uniform source hands Cauchy-filter and Cauchy-net evidence to the separated-completion corridor. It sits beside the uniform-space completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-space-completion-namecert}, the separated-completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separatedcompletion-namecert}, the finite stream-name windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives Real and Completion consumers a traditional uniform-completion interface without quotient completion objects, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality of completed points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Dieudonne completion carrier]
\label{def:dieudonne-completion-carrier}
A $\DieudonneCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(U,F,T,S,W,R,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ source row, recording the displayed entourage base, finite refinement reads, and uniform source data admitted by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniform-space-completion-namecert}.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ row, exposing finite filter-base demands and Cauchy eventuality checks over the same uniform source.
\item $T$ is the $\CauchyNetUp$ row, exposing directed Cauchy windows when the same completion request is presented in Moore--Smith form.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparatedCompletionUp$ handoff row, recording the separated boundary consumed before a completion-facing point can be public.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which the filter or net observations are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, translating the inspected windows into regular rational observations.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording dyadic radii, refinement slack, and comparison budgets used by the Cauchy-filter or Cauchy-net rows.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $U,F,T,S,W,R,Q$ have all been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the uniform source, Cauchy-filter row, Cauchy-net row, separated handoff, finite windows, regular readback, dyadic ledger, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a uniform-completion request through
$$
U,F,T,S,W,R,Q,E \longmapsto \NameCert_{\DieudonneCompletionUp}.
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DieudonneCompletionUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\CauchyNetUp$, $\SeparatedCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ dependency rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DieudonneCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $U,F,T,S,W,R,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion classes, selected Cauchy representatives, countable choice, host equality of completed points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dieudonne completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dieudonne-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DieudonneCompletionUp$ carrier
$$
D=(U,F,T,S,W,R,Q,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DieudonneCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the uniform source row, Cauchy-filter row, Cauchy-net row, separated-completion handoff, finite StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger, terminal Real seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Any completion-facing read must keep these rows visible before it consumes the separated boundary or the Real seal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dieudonne-completion-carrier}. The rows $U,F,T,S,W,R,Q,E$ are the only analytic coordinates through which a consumer can read uniform source data, Cauchy evidence, separated completion data, finite windows, rational readback, dyadic tolerance, and the terminal Real seal.

The uniform side begins at $U$. Filter-style evidence is read through $F$, while directed-net evidence is read through $T$; both remain attached to the same displayed entourage and refinement rows rather than to a quotient of Cauchy presentations.

The separated and numerical side then passes through $S,W,R,Q,E$. The separated-completion handoff is displayed before the public completion boundary is consumed, and the finite windows, regular rational readback, dyadic ledger, and Real seal keep the route inspectable by Real and Completion consumers.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and locally name the same packet. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for quotient completion classes, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, or ambient Real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dieudonne completion uniform handoff]
\label{thm:dieudonne-completion-uniform-handoff}
Every Real or Completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\DieudonneCompletionUp$ carrier factors through the displayed route
$$
\UniformSpaceUp \longmapsto (\CauchyFilterUp,\CauchyNetUp) \longmapsto \SeparatedCompletionUp \longmapsto \StreamNameUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The consumer first reads the uniform source, then the displayed filter or net Cauchy evidence, then the separated-completion handoff, and only then the finite-window, rational-readback, dyadic-ledger, and Real-seal rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:dieudonne-completion-namecert-obligations}. They place every public read inside the carrier rows $U,F,T,S,W,R,Q,E,H,C,P,N$.

The first step is uniform. A filter presentation uses $F$, and a net presentation uses $T$, but both are read over the same displayed source row $U$ and share the same downstream packet boundary.

The separated handoff is the next public boundary. The row $S$ must be displayed before a completion-facing consumer can treat the Cauchy evidence as separated data, so the route cannot skip to a completed point by quotienting filters or nets.

The final read is numerical and structural. The windows $W$, regular readback $R$, dyadic ledger $Q$, and Real seal $E$ are replayed through $H,C,P,N$, leaving no route for selected representatives, countable choice, host equality of completed points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness to enter the packet.
\end{proof}
