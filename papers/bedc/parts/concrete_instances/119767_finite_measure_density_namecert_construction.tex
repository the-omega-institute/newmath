\begin{definition}[Finite measure density carrier]
\label{def:finite-measure-density-carrier}
A $\FiniteMeasureDensityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(P,M,A,Z,Q,R,E,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the finite partition row, listing the cells and refinement witnesses inspected by the density request.
\item $M$ is the $\MeasureUp$ base-measure row, displaying the base value of each cell of $P$.
\item $A$ is the absolutely-continuous measure row, displaying the second measure on the same cells and recording zero-base cells as zero-target cells.
\item $Z$ is the density ledger, with one finite row for each cell carrying the rational ratio or the zero-cell witness used by the packet.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the finite base, target, and density values listed by $M,A,Z$.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for the displayed cell measures and cellwise densities.
\item $E$ is the finite expectation or distribution handoff row consumed by $\ProbSpaceUp$ and $\DistributionUp$ requests.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for partition, measure, absolute-continuity, density, readback, seal, handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite density query through $P,M,A,Z,Q,R,E,H$.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteMeasureDensityUp$, $\MeasureUp$, $\ProbSpaceUp$, $\DistributionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteMeasureDensityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for sigma-algebra completion, quotient measure classes, countable choice, non-finite partitions, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite measure density NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-measure-density-namecert-obligations}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-measure-density-carrier}. The rows $P,M,A,Z,Q,R,E,H,C,G,N$ are the complete visible packet.

The finite partition row $P$ is the only route to cells. The base-measure row $M$ and absolutely-continuous row $A$ are read on the same cells, so a zero-base cell can only be consumed through the displayed zero-target witness in $A$.

The density ledger $Z$ is finite and cellwise. Each nonzero base cell carries its displayed ratio, while each zero-base cell carries the corresponding zero-target witness rather than a quotient or completion argument.

The readback and seal rows $Q,R$ are downstream of $P,M,A,Z$, and the probability or distribution handoff $E$ consumes only that sealed finite route. The structural rows $H,C,G,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet, so no sigma-completion, quotient measure class, countable choice, non-finite partition, or ambient completeness coordinate is available.
\end{proof}
