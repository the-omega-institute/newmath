\begin{definition}[Complete metric separability carrier]
\label{def:complete-metric-separability-carrier}
A $\CompleteMetricSeparabilityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ row, carrying the point-history carrier, distance classifier, visible metric comparisons, and local metric naming data.
\item $K$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ row over the same metric carrier, exposing only the displayed Cauchy-window and limit-seal service available to downstream completion requests.
\item $D$ is the countable dense-index row. It records finite inspected dense names, their index tags, and the admission rule that every requested ball must be answered through an indexed dense name rather than through an external choice function.
\item $W$ is the finite dense-window row, displaying the requested center, radius, tolerance, inspected dense indices, and the first accepted witness window for the request.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ distance-and-tolerance row, sealing the displayed metric distances and positive radii used by $W$.
\item $T$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance readback row, recording the regular rational windows by which the dense witness and the completion row are compared.
\item $E$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ readback row for dense names, Cauchy windows, and finite observation requests made by $D,W,K,T$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,K,D,W,R,T,E$ and for the structural rows of the packet.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for metric-ball inspection, dense-index lookup, finite dense-window replay, completion-service replay, StreamName readback, RegSeqRat readback, and Real sealing.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompleteMetricSeparabilityUp}$ row.

The local classifier compares accepted carriers only through $M,K,D,W,R,T,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host countability, selected infinite dense sequences outside the displayed index row, quotient equality of completions, open-cover compactness, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Complete metric separability NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:complete-metric-separability-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompleteMetricSeparabilityUp$ carrier
$$
S=(M,K,D,W,R,T,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompleteMetricSeparabilityUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric carrier exposure through $M$, complete-metric service exposure through $K$, dense-index admission through $D$, finite dense-window coverage through $W$, Real-valued distance and tolerance sealing through $R$, regular rational readback through $T$, StreamName readback through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. It exports no host countability principle, no selected global dense sequence beyond the displayed index row, no quotient completion equality, no open-cover compactness theorem, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows from \autoref{def:complete-metric-separability-carrier}. The metric row $M$ fixes the distance surface, while the complete-metric row $K$ supplies only the Cauchy-window and limit-seal service available on that same surface.

Read the separability-specific rows next. The dense-index row $D$ records the only accepted source of dense names, and the finite dense-window row $W$ records the requested ball, inspected indices, tolerance, and accepted witness window. The distance and tolerance values in $W$ are sealed through $R$, while $T$ and $E$ expose the regular rational and stream readbacks used to inspect the witness.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no row stores a host countability principle, a hidden global sequence selector, quotient equality of completions, open-cover compactness, or ambient real completeness, none is exported by the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
