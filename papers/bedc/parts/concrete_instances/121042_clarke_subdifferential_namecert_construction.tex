$$

\item $F$ is the locally Lipschitz functional row, including the finite $\LipschitzModulusUp$ windows and value-comparison rows actually inspected by the packet.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClarkeSubdifferentialUp}$ row.

\begin{theorem}[ClarkeSubdifferential NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:clarke-subdifferential-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClarkeSubdifferentialUp$ carrier $G=(X,F,x,W,D,T,S,V,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ClarkeSubdifferentialUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the ambient vector, metric, norm, and real rows; the locally Lipschitz functional row; the base point; the finite window ledger; the directional-slope ledger; the tangent-cone row; the sibling support row; the convex-hull provenance row; the stationarity and duality readback row; transport; replay; provenance; and local naming;
\item Lipschitz-window exactness, requiring every exported slope comparison to pass through the displayed windows $W$ and the value-comparison rows of $F$;
\item directional-slope exposure, requiring every generalized-gradient read to display the direction row and the finite upper-slope comparison stored in $D$;
\item tangent and support compatibility, requiring constrained reads to pass through $T$ and support-facing reads to pass through $S$ before the convex-hull row $V$ may be inspected;
\item convex-hull provenance, requiring each generalized-gradient candidate to list the displayed slope and support rows used by its finite convex combination;
\item non-escape from selected limiting gradients, quotient dual spaces, measurable selections, host differentiability theorems, ambient compactness, and global nonsmooth calculus rules.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:clarke-subdifferential-carrier}. The analytic rows are $X,F,x,W,D,T,S,V,R$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

Lipschitz-window exactness is read first. A slope comparison can be exported only when the relevant norm, real, and value-comparison rows are visible through $X,F,W$.

The directional row then passes through $D$. Constrained consumers also expose the tangent-cone row $T$, and support-facing consumers expose the subdifferential row $S$, before the convex-hull provenance row $V$ may name a generalized-gradient candidate.

Finally $R$ hands the same finite route to KKT and Fenchel-duality consumers, while $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the packet. Since no coordinate of $G$ stores a selected limiting gradient, quotient dual space, measurable selection, host differentiability theorem, compactness theorem, or global nonsmooth calculus rule, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}
