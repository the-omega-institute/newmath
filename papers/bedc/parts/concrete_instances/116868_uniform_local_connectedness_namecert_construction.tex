\begin{definition}[Uniform local connectedness carrier]
\label{def:uniform-local-connectedness-carrier}
A $\UniformLocalConnectednessUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(M,L,A,K,F,C,\sigma,R,Q,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row, carrying the point classifier, distance classifier, and metric-distance ledger used by every local window.
\item $L$ is the $\LocallyConnectedUp$ local basis row, naming the displayed connected neighborhood family around each inspected point.
\item $A$ is the $\ChainConnectedMetricUp$ epsilon-chain row, storing the finite chain witnesses available inside the displayed metric ball.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-window compatibility row, restricting compact reads to finite net cells and bounded metric windows.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ consumer row, recording how a continuous-map request may read the local basis and chain rows without selecting a hidden modulus.
\item $C$ is the compact local-window row binding the local basis row $L$, chain row $A$, and compact window row $K$ to the same inspected metric scale.
\item $\sigma$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule for visible local-window, chain, compact-window, and consumer reads.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for metric radii, chain tolerances, local-basis thresholds, and consumer moduli.
\item $Q$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for distance and tolerance comparisons after the finite rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for metric source, local basis, chain row, compact window, continuous-map consumer, compact local-window row, stream schedule, regular readbacks, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformLocalConnectednessUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,L,A,K,F,C,\sigma,R,Q,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover local connectedness, selected components, path connectedness, choice-selected moduli, quotient equality of neighborhoods, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform local connectedness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-local-connectedness-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformLocalConnectednessUp$ carrier
$$
U=(M,L,A,K,F,C,\sigma,R,Q,H,T,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UniformLocalConnectednessUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric-source admission, local-basis admission, epsilon-chain admission, compact finite-window compatibility, continuous-map consumer compatibility, compact local-window binding, stream-schedule readback, regular rational comparison, Real seal handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming for the same finite packet. It also contains the refusal ledger excluding arbitrary open-cover local connectedness, selected components, path connectedness, choice-selected moduli, quotient neighborhood equality, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:uniform-local-connectedness-carrier}. The metric source, local basis, chain, compact-window, continuous-map consumer, and compact local-window rows are explicit entries of the finite packet.

The uniformity requirement is witnessed by shared rows. The same schedule $\sigma$, regular rational readback $R$, and Real seal $Q$ are used for metric radii, local-basis thresholds, chain tolerances, compact windows, and consumer moduli.

The structural rows preserve the same route. The transport row $H$ identifies corresponding displayed entries, $T$ replays the route through $\Cont$, and $P,N$ record provenance and local naming.

Since no additional coordinate appears in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface. The omitted open-cover, component-selection, path, choice-modulus, quotient, and ambient-completeness exits are therefore not available to a consumer.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform local connectedness chain-modulus handoff]
\label{thm:uniform-local-connectedness-chain-modulus-handoff}
Every continuous-map or compact-metric consumer that reads an accepted $\UniformLocalConnectednessUp$ packet factors its local connectedness request through the finite route
$$
\MetricUp,\ \LocallyConnectedUp
\longmapsto
\ChainConnectedMetricUp,\ \CompactMetricUp
\longmapsto
\StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp
\longmapsto
\ContinuousMapUp .
$$
Thus the consumer sees a displayed local basis, an epsilon-chain witness, a compact finite-window compatibility row, a stream schedule, regular rational modulus readbacks, and a Real-facing comparison seal before it can export a uniform local-connectedness modulus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the metric and local rows. The source row $M$ supplies distances, while the local basis row $L$ supplies the connected neighborhood family visible at the inspected point.

Next use the chain and compact rows. The epsilon-chain row $A$ records finite chain witnesses inside the displayed metric ball, and the compact row $K$ restricts the read to finite windows compatible with \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}.

The readback rows then bind the modulus. The schedule $\sigma$, regular rational row $R$, and Real seal $Q$ expose only displayed thresholds and comparisons, matching the chain-connected handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-chain-connected-metric-namecert}.

Finally the consumer row $F$ may replay the same data through $\ContinuousMapUp$. The rows $H,T,P,N$ preserve transport, replay, provenance, and local naming, so the exported modulus is finite packet data rather than an open-cover, component-selection, path, choice, quotient, or ambient-completeness principle.
\end{proof}
