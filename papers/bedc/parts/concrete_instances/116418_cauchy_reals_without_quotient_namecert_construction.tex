\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CauchyRealsWithoutQuotientUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-reals-without-quotient-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\CauchyRealsWithoutQuotientUp$ packet records the constructive Cauchy-real reading used at the current L10 Real and Completion boundary without forming quotient classes of streams. It binds the dyadic tolerance source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite window schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the pointwise locator induction route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-real-locator-pointwise-induction-namecert}. The certificate is a displayed finite-row interface for Real-facing consumers; it is not a quotient construction, a selected limit operator, or an ambient completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[Quotient-free Cauchy real carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-reals-without-quotient-carrier}
A $\CauchyRealsWithoutQuotientUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(D,W,Q,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, carrying signed mantissa rows, positive-unary scale rows, denominator rows, distance rows, radius rows, and local dyadic naming data.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite schedule that tells each consumer which finite observation windows of the Cauchy stream are public.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-rational readback row obtained from the displayed windows and dyadic tolerance data.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row consumed after the dyadic, stream, and regular-rational faces have been displayed.
\item $L$ is the $\CauchyRealLocatorPointwiseInductionUp$ route that records pointwise locator reads over the same displayed finite windows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the dyadic, stream, readback, seal, locator, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for finite-window tolerance reads.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CauchyRealLocatorPointwiseInductionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyRealsWithoutQuotientUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
Carrier comparison is rowwise over $D,W,Q,R,L,H,C,P,N$. The carrier has no coordinate for quotient real equality, quotient streams, countable choice, selected limits, trichotomy, metric completeness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Quotient-free Cauchy real classifier stability]
\label{thm:cauchy-reals-without-quotient-classifier-stability}
Let
$$
X=(D,W,Q,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
be an accepted $\CauchyRealsWithoutQuotientUp$ carrier. Replacing the dyadic tolerance ledger, finite stream schedule, regular-rational readback, Real seal, and pointwise locator induction rows by displayed componentwise $\hsame$ rows, and replaying the replacement through $C$, preserves the public Cauchy-real classifier read. The classifier remains the finite route
$$
D,W \longmapsto Q \longmapsto R,L \longmapsto H,C,P,N
$$
and does not inspect quotient real equality, quotient stream equality, countable choice, selected limits, trichotomy, metric completeness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-reals-without-quotient-carrier}. The visible classifier source is exactly the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ together with the finite stream schedule $W$.

The readback row $Q$ is downstream of those two rows, and the Real seal $R$ is downstream of the readback. The locator induction route $L$ reads the same displayed window and tolerance surface through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-real-locator-pointwise-induction-namecert}.

The transport row $H$ records the componentwise replacement, and $C$ replays that replacement through the displayed route. Since $P$ only records provenance and $N$ only names the local packet, no additional classifier coordinate is introduced.

Thus the public classifier is stable under the displayed transport. The excluded quotient, choice, order, and completeness objects have no row in the carrier, so they cannot affect the classifier read.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Quotient-free Cauchy real finite-window tolerance readback]
\label{thm:cauchy-reals-without-quotient-finite-window-tolerance-readback}
For every accepted $\CauchyRealsWithoutQuotientUp$ carrier
$$
X=(D,W,Q,R,L,H,C,P,N),
$$
each Real-facing tolerance request is answered by a finite window of $W$, the corresponding $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row in $D$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $Q$, and the terminal seal row $R$. The answer is a BEDC-visible finite row readback; it is not a representative of a quotient stream, a selected limit, a countable-choice selection, a trichotomy decision, or an appeal to ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a Real-facing tolerance request at the accepted carrier. The only public schedule for inspecting the Cauchy stream is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ row $W$, so the request first selects a displayed finite window rather than an infinite representative.

Read the dyadic bound for that window from $D$. This uses the signed mantissa, scale, denominator, distance, and radius rows supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}.

Now pass to $Q$. The regular-rational readback consumes the same finite window and dyadic ledger, matching the route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}. The Real-facing seal $R$ is then read only after those rows are displayed.

The remaining rows $L,H,C,P,N$ record pointwise locator compatibility, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since none of these rows stores quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, metric completeness, or ambient Real completeness, the tolerance answer is exactly the stated finite-window readback.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Quotient-free Cauchy real locator-induction compatibility]
\label{thm:cauchy-reals-without-quotient-locator-induction-compatibility}
Let
$$
X=(D,W,Q,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
be an accepted $\CauchyRealsWithoutQuotientUp$ carrier. Every pointwise locator induction read in $L$ is compatible with the same finite-window tolerance route used by $D,W,Q,R$: the branch ledger opens a displayed $\StreamNameUp$ window, reads the matching $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, passes through the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and reaches the same $\RealUp$ seal before the local $\NameCert$ row is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the locator row $L$. By \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-real-locator-pointwise-induction-namecert}, each accepted pointwise branch is read through a displayed StreamName window, DyadicRatCore tolerance row, RegSeqRat readback, and Real seal.

Compare those rows with the carrier rows $W,D,Q,R$. The $\CauchyRealsWithoutQuotientUp$ packet stores no second stream schedule or second dyadic ledger, so the branch ledger in $L$ must use the same finite-window tolerance route already exposed by the classifier and readback rows.

The componentwise transport $H$ and replay $C$ preserve this route, while $P$ and $N$ source and name it locally. Therefore the locator-induction branch becomes public only through the same displayed finite rows.

The compatibility claim uses no quotient real equality, quotient streams, countable choice, selected limits, trichotomy, metric completeness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, because none of those objects occurs in the accepted carrier.
\end{proof}
