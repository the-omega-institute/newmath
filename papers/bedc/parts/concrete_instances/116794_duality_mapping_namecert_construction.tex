\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DualityMappingUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-duality-mapping-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Browder and Cioranescu, the $\DualityMappingUp$ packet records the normalized duality mapping of a displayed normed or Hilbert-facing carrier as finite NameCert data. It sits over the Banach carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banachspace-namecert}, the dual-row interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-banach-dual-namecert}, the norm readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-norm-namecert}, the Hilbert specialization of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hilbert-namecert}, and convex admissible domains named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-convexset-namecert}. The packet names the pairing and norm-equality obligations without importing a Hahn--Banach selector or a host dual-space theorem.

\begin{definition}[Duality mapping carrier]
\label{def:duality-mapping-carrier}
A $\DualityMappingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(X,X^\ast,N,J,E,Q,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\BanachSpaceUp$ source row, or the $\HilbertUp$ source row when the normalized duality map is read through the Hilbert inner-product specialization.
\item $X^\ast$ is the $\BanachDualUp$ row naming the admitted dual observations available to the packet.
\item $N$ is the $\NormUp$ row for source and dual norms, including the Real-valued norm endpoints used by the normalized condition.
\item $J$ is the graph row of the normalized duality mapping, relating each displayed source point to the admitted dual row that witnesses the mapping read.
\item $E$ is the norm-pairing exactness row, recording the displayed equalities between the pairing, the source norm square, and the dual norm product.
\item $Q$ is the admissible-domain row, read through $\ConvexSetUp$ when the mapping is consumed by monotonicity, variational-inequality, or convex-analysis packets.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, dual, norm, graph, exactness, admissible-domain, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the source and dual rows through the normalized graph and exactness checks.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DualityMappingUp$, $\BanachSpaceUp$, $\BanachDualUp$, $\NormUp$, $\HilbertUp$, $\ConvexSetUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DualityMappingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a choice-selected norming functional, a global Hahn--Banach extension, quotient dual equality, weak-star compactness, or an ambient Banach-dual existence theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Duality mapping NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:duality-mapping-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DualityMappingUp$ carrier $D=(X,X^\ast,N,J,E,Q,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DualityMappingUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, dual-row admission, norm-pairing exactness, transport, replay, provenance, and non-escape of Hahn--Banach choice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission projects the source row $X$ and the norm row $N$. The packet may be consumed through $\BanachSpaceUp$ or through the $\HilbertUp$ specialization, but the source endpoint and norm readback remain displayed rows of the same finite carrier.

Dual-row admission projects $X^\ast$ and the graph row $J$. A consumer reads only the dual observations already named in the $\BanachDualUp$ row, and the normalized mapping graph records which admitted dual row is paired with the displayed source point.

Norm-pairing exactness is the role of $E$. The pairing value, source norm, and dual norm are all read through $N$ and sealed as displayed Real-valued data, so exactness is a packet row rather than a host dual-space equality.

Transport, replay, and provenance are carried by $H,C,P,L$. They preserve the same source, dual, norm, graph, exactness, and admissible-domain rows when a $\ConvexSetUp$ or Hilbert-facing consumer reuses the mapping.

Finally, Hahn--Banach choice is outside the carrier. Since no row of $D$ stores a selected norming functional, an extension selector, quotient dual equality, weak-star compactness, or an ambient Banach-dual existence theorem, the local $\NameCert$ surface cannot export any of those objects.
\end{proof}
