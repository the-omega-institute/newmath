Every $\GromovHausdorffDistanceUp$ correspondence and distortion read consumes $\MetricSpaceUp$ distance rows together with compact or finite-net rows from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert} before it can export a distance comparison. The route therefore treats metric distance, compact coverage, finite-net inspection, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming as prerequisites of the public Gromov--Hausdorff distance surface, not as consequences of the distance comparison itself.
