\begin{theorem}[Gromov--Hausdorff distance density-net forward route]
\label{thm:gromov-hausdorff-distance-density-net-forward-route}
Every public $\GromovHausdorffDistanceUp$ read must expose the compact-metric endpoint rows, finite correspondence or density-net rows, dyadic distortion bounds, $\MetricUp$ distance witnesses, $\RealUp$ scalar comparison rows, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming before exporting a distance comparison. Concretely, the read passes through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-gromov-hausdorff-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cauchy-density-net-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. Thus the distance packet is consumed as a finite compact-metric comparison route, not as a quotient of compact spaces, selected optimal correspondence, cover-choice principle, or ambient completion theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:gromov-hausdorff-distance-carrier}. Its endpoint rows are compact metric rows, so any public scalar read starts by opening the displayed $\CompactMetricUp$ and $\MetricUp$ surfaces of the two endpoints.

The comparison rows are finite. Either the consumer reads a finite correspondence through the $\GromovHausdorffUp$ route, or it reads a finite density-net table through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cauchy-density-net-namecert}. In both cases the local distortion row supplies the dyadic bound attached to the displayed table.

The scalar comparison is downstream of those rows. The $\RealUp$ read is available only after the metric endpoint distances, finite table, and dyadic distortion budget have been exposed.

Finally the transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve exactly that finite route. Since the carrier has no row for quotient compact spaces, selected optima, cover choice, or ambient completion, the exported distance comparison cannot bypass the stated dependency route.
\end{proof}
