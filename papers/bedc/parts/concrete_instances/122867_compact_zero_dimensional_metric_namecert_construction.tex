\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompactZeroDimensionalMetricUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-compact-zero-dimensional-metric-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on compact metric data and Kelley's zero-dimensional compact spaces, the $\CompactZeroDimensionalMetricUp$ packet records the finite BEDC surface by which a compact metric source also exposes a clopen-basis schedule. It sits over the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the total-bounded finite-net ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, and the real-valued diameter and tolerance readback rows supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite naming certificate for compact zero-dimensional metric presentations; it is not an open-cover compactness theorem, not a selected arbitrary clopen refinement, and not a quotient compactum.

\begin{definition}[CompactZeroDimensionalMetric carrier]
\label{def:compact-zero-dimensional-metric-carrier}
A $\CompactZeroDimensionalMetricUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(K,M,T,B,F,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, exposing the compact-metric finite-net and completion-facing boundary inherited from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert};
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ row, keeping the distance read, triangle ledger, and metric observation surface visible through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert};
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ row, recording the open-set and finite-observation topology carrier used by the zero-dimensional read;
\item $B$ is a finite clopen-basis schedule: for each displayed tolerance window and observed point row, it gives a finite list of clopen neighborhoods whose union covers the relevant finite-net window and whose members refine the requested open row;
\item $F$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite-net ledger, binding each clopen-basis window to a finite net, mesh row, and covering schedule before compact-metric data is exported;
\item $D$ is the $\RealUp$-valued diameter and tolerance readback row, recording the displayed bounds used to compare clopen windows with metric balls;
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, metric row, topology row, clopen-basis schedule, finite-net ledger, diameter readback, provenance, and local naming row;
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay for reading the compact metric source, the metric and topology rows, the clopen-basis schedule, the finite-net ledger, the diameter row, and the local certificate row;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactZeroDimensionalMetricUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier names zero-dimensional compact-metric data only through the displayed finite rows. It does not add an ambient open-cover compactness proof, quotient equality of compact spaces, arbitrary clopen choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompactZeroDimensionalMetric NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-zero-dimensional-metric-namecert-obligations}
The local $\NameCert_{\CompactZeroDimensionalMetricUp}$ surface for the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-zero-dimensional-metric-carrier} consists of seven finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission: the accepted object is the displayed packet $Z=(K,M,T,B,F,D,H,C,P,N)$;
\item compact-source visibility: the $\CompactMetricUp$ row $K$ is read before any compact-metric conclusion is exported;
\item metric-topology compatibility: the $\MetricUp$ row $M$ and $\TopologyUp$ row $T$ remain visible when a clopen window is inspected;
\item finite clopen coverage: each row of $B$ supplies only the finite clopen windows displayed for the current finite-net and tolerance request;
\item total-bounded net binding: the ledger $F$ ties every clopen-basis read to a finite-net schedule and never to an unbounded cover search;
\item real-valued readback: the diameter and tolerance comparisons pass through the $\RealUp$ row $D$ before any metric-ball refinement is exposed;
\item non-escape: every downstream read factors through $K,M,T,B,F,D,H,C,P,N$ and has no route to open-cover compactness, arbitrary clopen choice, quotient equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is the projection of the displayed packet in \autoref{def:compact-zero-dimensional-metric-carrier}. The compact source, metric carrier, topology carrier, clopen-basis schedule, finite-net ledger, real-valued readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local name are exactly the coordinates of that packet.

The compact-source and metric-topology obligations are componentwise reads. A consumer must first expose the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row and then keep the $\MetricUp$ and $\TopologyUp$ rows visible when a clopen window is inspected.

The finite coverage and net-binding obligations use the rows $B$ and $F$. The clopen-basis schedule is indexed by displayed finite-net and tolerance requests, so it is a finite schedule for the current packet rather than a selected family of all clopen refinements.

The real-valued comparison obligation is the row $D$. Diameter and tolerance readback pass through the displayed $\RealUp$ seal before any metric-ball refinement is consumed.

The remaining rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since no coordinate stores open-cover compactness, arbitrary clopen choice, quotient equality, or ambient Real completeness, no accepted read can export those endpoints.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CompactZeroDimensionalMetric clopen-basis route]
\label{thm:compact-zero-dimensional-metric-clopen-basis-route}
Every clopen-basis read exported by an accepted $\CompactZeroDimensionalMetricUp$ carrier factors through
$$
K,M,T,B,F,D,H,C,P,N .
$$
It must expose the compact metric source, metric row, topology row, finite clopen-basis schedule, total-bounded finite-net ledger, real-valued diameter and tolerance readback, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, package provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row before any zero-dimensional compact-metric consumer can inspect the basis. The route exports finite clopen windows only, not open-cover compactness, unrestricted clopen choice, quotient equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a clopen-basis request. By the carrier definition, the request can inspect only the displayed rows $K,M,T,B,F,D,H,C,P,N$.

Read the source rows first. The compact-metric row $K$ supplies the compact source, while $M$ and $T$ keep the metric and topology carriers visible before any clopen window is read.

Next consume the finite schedule. The clopen-basis row $B$ is indexed by the total-bounded finite-net ledger $F$, and the diameter or tolerance comparison is read through the real-valued row $D$.

Finally replay the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. These rows transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet, so the exported basis remains a displayed zero-dimensional compact-metric schedule and cannot be reread as the refused compactness, choice, quotient, or completeness endpoints.
\end{proof}
