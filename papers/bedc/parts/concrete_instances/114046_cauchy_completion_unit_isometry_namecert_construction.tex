\begin{definition}[Cauchy completion unit-isometry carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-completion-unit-isometry-carrier}
A $\CauchyCompletionUnitIsometryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(S,R,D,U,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source metric row, including the displayed source points,
metric-distance requests, and finite point-classifier observations consumed by
the unit map.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the constant Cauchy names
associated to the displayed source points.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording the requested
dyadic radius, the comparison budget, and the finite descent used to compare
source distances with completion distances.
\item $U$ is the unit-admission row supplied by the
$\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$ route, saying that each displayed source point is
sent to its constant completion name before any completion consumer can read
it.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached by the distance comparison
after the source metric row, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance
ledger, and unit-admission row have been exposed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source row,
readback row, dyadic ledger, unit-admission row, Real seal, replay,
provenance, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from source distance requests
through $S,R,D,U,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyCompletionUnitIsometryUp$,
$\CauchyCompletionFunctorUp$,
$\MetricCompletionDenseEmbeddingUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$,
$\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionUnitIsometryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate asserts exactly that these rows form one finite
unit-isometry history. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality,
selected representatives, host complete spaces, open-cover compactness,
countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion unit-isometry NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-unit-isometry-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompletionUnitIsometryUp$ carrier
$$
J=(S,R,D,U,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionUnitIsometryUp}$ surface consists
exactly of carrier admission for the source metric row $S$, regular rational
readback row $R$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, unit-admission row $U$,
terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$, componentwise transport $H$, displayed replay
$C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$. The certificate is local to
the constant-Cauchy-name unit route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:cauchy-completion-unit-isometry-carrier}. The accepted packet
exposes the source metric row, regular readback, dyadic ledger, unit admission,
terminal seal, and structural support rows.

The analytic rows are $S,R,D,U,E$. The source row names the points and their
distance requests, the readback row names the constant regular Cauchy
observations, and the dyadic ledger binds the requested comparison tolerance.
The unit row admits the constant completion names before the Real seal is read.

The structural rows are $H,C,P,N$. They transport the same displayed data,
replay the same route, record provenance, and bind the local name. Since the
carrier contains no additional coordinate, every local NameCert obligation is
one of the listed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion unit-isometry distance route]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-unit-isometry-distance-route}
Every accepted $\CauchyCompletionUnitIsometryUp$ carrier routes a displayed
source-distance comparison through the finite chain
$$
S \longmapsto U \longmapsto R,D \longmapsto E,H,C,P,N .
$$
The completion distance read is therefore the same finite regular-rational and
dyadic comparison attached to the source metric row; it is not a quotient
equality of completion classes and not a selected limiting representative.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted carrier $J=(S,R,D,U,E,H,C,P,N)$. The source stage is
$S$: the displayed metric row fixes the two source points and the distance
request being compared.

Use the unit-admission row $U$ next. It sends each displayed source point to
its constant Cauchy completion name before any distance readback is available,
so the completion-side comparison is attached to the same source observations.

Read $R,D$ as the finite comparison ledger. The regular rational row supplies
the constant-name observations, and the dyadic row supplies the tolerance
budget for comparing those observations with the source distance request.

Finally $E,H,C,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, source, and name the displayed
route. No quotient class, selected representative, host complete space, or
choice schedule appears in the packet, so the distance route is exhausted by
the listed finite rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion unit-isometry Real-seal consumer]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-unit-isometry-real-seal}
Every Real-facing consumer of an accepted $\CauchyCompletionUnitIsometryUp$
packet must reach the terminal $\RealUp$ seal by first reading the source
metric row $S$, the unit-admission row $U$, the regular rational readback $R$,
and the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$. The consumer receives the unit-isometry
witness only as a finite Real-seal route over constant Cauchy names, with
support rows $H,C,P,N$ preserving the same packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted packet. The terminal row $E$ is not a detached real
object; it is displayed only after $S,U,R,D$ have fixed the source distance
request, the constant completion names, and the dyadic comparison budget.

Use \autoref{thm:cauchy-completion-unit-isometry-distance-route}. It orders
the completion-facing distance read through the same source, unit, readback,
and tolerance rows before the Real seal is available.

The support rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that route under componentwise transport,
displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. A Real-facing consumer
therefore obtains the unit-isometry witness through finite NameCert data only,
not through quotient completion equality, selected limits, ambient
completeness, or choice.
\end{proof}
