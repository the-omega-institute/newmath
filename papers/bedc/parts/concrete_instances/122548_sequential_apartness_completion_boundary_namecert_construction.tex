Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\SequentialApartnessCompletionBoundaryUp$ packet records the finite boundary where sequential completion reads, apartness evidence, and Real-facing seals meet without being allowed to collapse into quotient completions or host-selected limits. It sits after the apartness source surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apartnessspace-namecert}, the generic apartness completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apartness-completion-namecert}, the located apartness completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-apartness-completion-namecert}, the finite window schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the Real seal discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a Real and Completion seed boundary; it does not assert quotient equality of completion names, selected limits, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Sequential apartness completion boundary carrier]
\label{def:sequential-apartness-completion-boundary-carrier}
A $\SequentialApartnessCompletionBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(A,Q,L,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the $\ApartnessSpaceUp$ source row exposing positive separation before any completion-facing read is consumed.
\item $Q$ is the $\ApartnessCompletionUp$ boundary row recording the separated completion route attached to the same apartness source.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedApartnessCompletionUp$ row displaying located completion windows and apartness evidence before Real-facing inspection.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite window schedule for the sequential observations seen by the completion boundary.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback extracted from those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger recording positive gaps, dyadic radii, and comparison cells used by the readback.
\item $E$ is the optional $\RealUp$ seal row, available only downstream of $A,Q,L,S,R,D$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the apartness-space row, apartness-completion row, located-apartness-completion row, stream windows, regular readback, dyadic ledger, optional seal, replay row, provenance row, and local NameCert row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from apartness inspection through apartness completion, located apartness completion, stream windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance, and the optional Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SequentialApartnessCompletionBoundaryUp$, $\ApartnessSpaceUp$, $\ApartnessCompletionUp$, $\LocatedApartnessCompletionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialApartnessCompletionBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion classes, host equality of completed points, selected limits, countable-choice schedules, trichotomy rows, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential apartness completion boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sequential-apartness-completion-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SequentialApartnessCompletionBoundaryUp$ carrier $B=(A,Q,L,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialApartnessCompletionBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed apartness-space source, apartness-completion boundary, located-apartness-completion route, stream windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, optional Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item source-first exactness, requiring $A$ to be displayed before the completion rows $Q$ and $L$ are consumed;
\item sequential readback discipline, requiring every completion-facing observation to pass through $S$, $R$, and $D$ before the optional seal row $E$ is available;
\item componentwise stability, requiring $H$ and $C$ to transport and replay only the displayed rows $A,Q,L,S,R,D,E,P,N$;
\item provenance exhaustion, requiring $P$ and $N$ to export no quotient completion class, host equality of completed names, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy row, or ambient-completeness principle.
\end{enumerate}
No local obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:sequential-apartness-completion-boundary-carrier}. The tuple displays the apartness source, apartness-completion boundary, located-apartness-completion route, finite windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, optional Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

Source-first exactness follows from the row order. A completion-facing read can inspect $Q$ or $L$ only after the positive separation row $A$ has been named, so the completion boundary cannot hide its apartness source.

The sequential readback discipline is carried by $S,R,D,E$. Each observation first opens a finite stream window, then reads the regular rational row, then consults the dyadic tolerance ledger; the optional Real seal is downstream of that finite route.

Componentwise stability and provenance exhaustion are the roles of $H,C,P,N$. These rows transport, replay, package, and name only the displayed packet, and therefore cannot add quotient completion classes, host equality, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential apartness completion boundary Real seal nonescape]
\label{thm:sequential-apartness-completion-boundary-real-seal-nonescape}
Let $B=(A,Q,L,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\SequentialApartnessCompletionBoundaryUp$ packet. Every Real-facing or completion-facing read of $B$, when the optional seal row $E$ is present, factors through the finite route
$$
A,Q,L,S,R,D \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the Real seal receives only the displayed apartness-space source, apartness-completion boundary, located-apartness-completion route, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger, transport rows, continuation replay, provenance, and local NameCert row. It receives no quotient completion, host equality of completed points, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, trichotomy row, or ambient-completeness coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a downstream completion or Real-facing read. Projection of the carrier gives a single ordered route from the apartness-space source row $A$ through the apartness-completion row $Q$ and the located-apartness-completion row $L$.

The finite observation rows then exhaust the readback path. The window schedule $S$, regular readback $R$, and dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ must be displayed before the Real seal row $E$ can be inspected.

Finally, the transport, replay, provenance, and local name rows $H,C,P,N$ carry the same route without adding coordinates. Row exhaustion therefore blocks quotient completions, host equality, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, and ambient completeness from entering the Real seal boundary.
\end{proof}
