\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy minimum-modulus CauchyModulus dependency]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-min-modulus-cauchy-modulus-dependency}
Every $\RegularCauchyMinModulusUp$ consumer reads the request-normalization rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchymodulus-namecert} before it may normalize a source tail request into the minimum-modulus row. The same route reads the regular-sequence handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} before any $\RealUp$ completion-facing seal is consumed.

Thus the minimum-modulus row depends on $\CauchyModulusUp$ for source request normalization and on $\RegSeqRatUp$ for regular readback, while it supplies no quotient stream equality, selected limit, hidden completion object, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the minimum-modulus request. A consumer cannot form the minimum row until the modulus comparison schedule has displayed how source tail requests are normalized, so the $\CauchyModulusUp$ request-normalization rows are read first.

Next read the regular-sequence handoff. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row supplies the regular readback used by the completion-facing route, and this readback occurs only after the modulus schedule has already fixed the request shape.

Finally inspect the $\RealUp$ seal. Since the seal is downstream of the displayed request schedule and regular readback, it cannot introduce quotient stream equality, a selected limit, a hidden completion object, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
