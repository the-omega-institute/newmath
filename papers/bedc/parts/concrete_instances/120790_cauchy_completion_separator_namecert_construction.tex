\begin{definition}[Cauchy completion separator carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-completion-separator-carrier}
A $\CauchyCompletionSeparatorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(K,W,R,D,U,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CauchyCompletionCriterionUp$ row. It records the criterion that a completion-facing Cauchy name must satisfy before any separator is read.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ row. It exposes the finite observation window through which the criterion is inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row. It records the regular rational readback used by the separator at the displayed precision.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ row. It supplies the dyadic tolerance ledger used to compare the two inspected completion names.
\item $U$ is the $\HausdorffCompletionUniversalUp$ row. It records the separated universal-property boundary consumed after the criterion, window, readback, and tolerance rows have been displayed.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegularCauchyCompletionSeparationUp$ row. It records the quotient-free regular Cauchy separation handoff used by downstream completion consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,W,R,D,U,Q,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
K,W,R,D \longmapsto U,Q \longmapsto H,P,N .

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyCompletionSeparatorUp$, $\CauchyCompletionCriterionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\HausdorffCompletionUniversalUp$, $\RegularCauchyCompletionSeparationUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.

The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, selected limits, host sequence equality, countable-choice schedules, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion separator NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-separator-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompletionSeparatorUp$ carrier $S=(K,W,R,D,U,Q,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompletionSeparatorUp}$ surface consists exactly of Cauchy criterion admission, finite StreamName window exposure, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance comparison, HausdorffCompletionUniversal separated-boundary routing, RegularCauchyCompletionSeparation handoff, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming through the displayed rows $K,W,R,D,U,Q,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-completion-separator-carrier}. The Cauchy criterion row $K$ is the only admission point, and the finite window $W$ is the only route by which the criterion is inspected.

The regular rational row $R$ and dyadic tolerance row $D$ provide the displayed comparison data. A separator read therefore stays inside the finite regular Cauchy and dyadic ledgers before any separated completion boundary is consumed.

The rows $U$ and $Q$ then route the same inspected data to the Hausdorff universal boundary and the regular Cauchy separation handoff. Componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$ preserve that route without adding quotient equality, selected limits, host equality, choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy completion separator Hausdorff route]
\label{thm:cauchy-completion-separator-hausdorff-route}
Every completion consumer that reads an accepted $\CauchyCompletionSeparatorUp$ carrier must pass through the finite route
$$
\CauchyCompletionCriterionUp,\StreamNameUp,\RegSeqRatUp,\DyadicRatCoreUp
\longmapsto

$$
Thus the separator can be consumed as a displayed Hausdorff or separated completion boundary, but not as quotient completion equality, selected-limit data, host sequence equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted carrier $S=(K,W,R,D,U,Q,H,C,P,N)$. The source side is fixed by $K,W,R,D$: criterion admission, finite window exposure, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance comparison.

Read the separated boundary rows next. The $\HausdorffCompletionUniversalUp$ row $U$ supplies the universal separated route, while the $\RegularCauchyCompletionSeparationUp$ row $Q$ supplies the quotient-free separation handoff for regular Cauchy names.

Finally inspect the structural rows. The $\hsame$ transport row $H$, continuation row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$ replay the same finite packet, so downstream completion consumers receive only the displayed separator route and no stronger host-side completion principle.
\end{proof}
