Following Cousin and Bishop--Bridges gauge integration, the $\CousinPartitionUp$ packet records the finite gauge-partition object that sits between the existence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cousin-lemma-namecert} and the tagged-sum consumers of $\TaggedRealPartitionUp$, $\FinePartitionGaugeUp$, and $\RiemannSumGaugeUp$. The packet does not assert open-cover compactness, an arbitrary host partition object, selected representatives outside the displayed ledger, quotient real equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Cousin partition carrier]
\label{def:cousin-partition-carrier}
A $\CousinPartitionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(I,G,T,M,F,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the located closed interval row, including endpoint names, interval containment, and the finite source boundary inherited from the Cousin route.
\item $G$ is the positive gauge row, recording local positive-radius data as displayed rational or regular-rational comparisons rather than as an ambient open cover.
\item $T$ is the finite tagged-subinterval ledger. Each entry carries a cell, a tag, endpoint reads, tag-in-cell containment, and its position in the ordered partition.
\item $M$ is the mesh and refinement ledger, recording adjacency, common refinements, and the comparison route to the tagged-real-partition mesh.
\item $F$ is the gauge-fineness row, certifying for every displayed tagged cell that the cell lies inside the local gauge radius attached to its tag.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window family opened for endpoints, tags, gauge-radius reads, mesh comparisons, and fineness checks.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback family for the same finite windows and positive-radius comparisons.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal family reached only after the interval, gauge, tagged-cell, mesh, fineness, window, and regular readback rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $I,G,T,M,F,S,R,E$ and for replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Cousin-partition request through
$$
I,G,T,M,F,S,R,E \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CousinPartitionUp$, $\CousinLemmaUp$, $\TaggedRealPartitionUp$, $\FinePartitionGaugeUp$, $\RiemannSumGaugeUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CousinPartitionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only by these displayed interval, gauge, tagged-subinterval, mesh, fineness, readback, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cousin partition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cousin-partition-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CousinPartitionUp$ carrier $K=(I,G,T,M,F,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CousinPartitionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, located interval exposure through $I$, positive gauge admission through $G$, finite tagged-subinterval exposure through $T$, mesh and refinement accountability through $M$, gauge-fineness certification through $F$, finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows through $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, terminal $\RealUp$ seals through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cousin-partition-carrier}. The tuple lists the only rows available to a Cousin-partition consumer: interval source, positive gauge, tagged cells, mesh and refinement data, gauge-fineness witnesses, finite windows, regular readback, Real seals, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Read the partition-facing rows in order. The Cousin lemma chapter supplies the existence-facing route to finite tagged subintervals, but the present packet records the displayed ledger $T$ together with the mesh row $M$ and fineness row $F$ before any tagged-sum consumer can inspect it.

Finally read $S,R,E,H,C,P,N$. These rows keep endpoint, tag, and gauge-radius observations inside finite StreamName windows and regular-rational readback before the Real seals are exposed. Since no carrier coordinate names an open cover, host partition, selected representative family, quotient equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cousin partition gauge-refinement handoff]
\label{thm:cousin-partition-gauge-refinement-handoff}
Let $K=(I,G,T,M,F,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CousinPartitionUp$ carrier. Any consumer that refines a Cousin subdivision toward $\FinePartitionGaugeUp$, $\TaggedRealPartitionUp$, or $\RiemannSumGaugeUp$ must first expose the located interval row $I$, positive gauge row $G$, finite tagged-subinterval ledger $T$, mesh and refinement ledger $M$, gauge-fineness row $F$, regular readback row $R$, and terminal Real seal row $E$. The handoff exports a finite gauge-fine partition route only; it does not export an ambient open-cover theorem, an arbitrary host mesh, quotient equality of partitions, selected tag values outside $T$, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the Cousin source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cousin-lemma-namecert}. It supplies the finite subdivision route over the displayed interval and positive gauge, but the consumer still receives only the rows named in $I,G,T,M,F$.

Next route the tagged cells through the sibling tagged-partition and fine-gauge surfaces. The tagged row $T$ is the only source for $\TaggedRealPartitionUp$ reads, while $F$ is the row that permits $\FinePartitionGaugeUp$ to see the partition as gauge-fine.

Finally route a Riemann-sum or gauge-integration consumer through $\RiemannSumGaugeUp$. The mesh row $M$, readback row $R$, Real seal row $E$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite partition ledger, so the downstream consumer receives a displayed gauge-refinement handoff rather than hidden compactness, quotient equality, selected tags, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
