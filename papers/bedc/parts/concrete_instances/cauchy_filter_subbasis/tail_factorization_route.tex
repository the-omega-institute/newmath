\begin{theorem}[Cauchy filter subbasis tail-base factorization]
\label{thm:cauchy-filter-subbasis-tail-base-factorization}
Every $\CauchyFilterSubbasisUp$ read specialized to cofinite sequence tails factors through $\FrechetFilterUp$ and $\FilterBaseUp$ before $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ are read. For an accepted carrier
$$
S=(U,I,E,G,B,F,W,T,R,L,H,C,P,N),
$$
the tail-specialized route first displays the finite subbasis tokens, finite intersections, generated events, and generation ledger $U,I,E,G$; then reads the cofinite-tail threshold discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-frechetfilter-namecert}; then returns through the generated $\FilterBaseUp$ row $B$ before the Cauchy row, stream windows, tolerance ledger, regular readback, and Real seal $F,W,T,R,L$ can be consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the generated-base cofinality theorem, \autoref{thm:cauchy-filter-subbasis-generated-base-cofinality}. It places $U,I,E,G,B$ before every completion-facing read, so a tail-specialized consumer cannot reach $F,W,T,R,L$ before the generated base has been displayed.

Specialize the generated requests to eventual sequence tails. The Frechet tail dependency row in this chapter identifies the cofinite-tail discipline supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-frechetfilter-namecert}; this row is the only tail-filter surface available to the specialized read.

Compose the specialization with the completion handoff of \autoref{thm:cauchy-filter-subbasis-completion-handoff}. The handoff routes the resulting base through the $\CauchyFilterUp$ row, finite stream windows, dyadic tolerance ledger, regular rational readback, and terminal Real seal.

The carrier exclusions then apply to the whole composition. Quotient filters, selected limits, countable choice, host equality of generated filters, and ambient Real completeness are absent from the displayed rows, so none can replace the Frechet and FilterBase stages of the tail-base factorization.
\end{proof}
