\begin{definition}[Transcendental GAP ledger carrier]
\label{def:transcendental-gap-ledger-carrier}
A $\TranscendentalGapLedgerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
\Gamma=(S,T,B,G,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the socket-site row naming the proof, paper claim, or construction site at which an induction-facing universal request is exposed.
\item $T$ is the finite observation-trace row, recording the displayed initial cases, bounded successors, or finite prefixes that have actually been inspected.
\item $B$ is the forward-binding belief socket row. It names the present commitment that would treat not-yet-observed cases as discharged without storing that commitment as an internal theorem.
\item $G$ is the $\Gap$ exposure row, keeping the missing universal supply visible to audit consumers.
\item $L$ is the supply-boundary ledger row. It records the relation to $\TranscendentalSupplyLedgerUp$ and refuses to turn supply position into substrate data.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the socket site, finite trace row, belief socket, GAP exposure, supply-boundary ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the socket site through the finite traces, belief socket, GAP exposure, supply boundary, provenance, and local certificate.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TranscendentalGapLedgerUp$, $\TranscendentalSupplyLedgerUp$, $\BHist$, $\Gap$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TranscendentalGapLedgerUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an unrestricted induction rule, universal quantifier over future observations, hidden witness, completed supply source, quotient equality, self-certifying truth row, or bridge-checked theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Transcendental GAP ledger NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:transcendental-gap-ledger-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\TranscendentalGapLedgerUp$ carrier $\Gamma=(S,T,B,G,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\TranscendentalGapLedgerUp}$ surface consists exactly of socket-site admission, finite observation-trace exposure, forward-binding belief-socket display, $\Gap$ exposure, supply-boundary ledger routing, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Every consumer factors through
$$
S \to T \to B \to G \to L \to H \to C \to P \to N
$$
before it can cite the ledger.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:transcendental-gap-ledger-carrier}. The rows $S,T,B,G,L$ are the only substantive coordinates, and $H,C,P,N$ are the only structural coordinates.

The finite trace row $T$ is read before the belief socket $B$. Thus the packet records exactly what has been observed before exposing the request that would bind all not-yet-observed cases.

The GAP row $G$ and supply-boundary row $L$ prevent discharge. They route the request to the transcendental supply ledger surface without adding the requested universal rule to the substrate.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that same finite sequence. Since no other coordinate is present, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Transcendental GAP ledger non-discharge]
\label{thm:transcendental-gap-ledger-nondischarge}
An accepted $\TranscendentalGapLedgerUp$ packet may expose a forward-binding belief socket, but it never supplies the missing universal claim named by that socket. The finite observation trace $T$ remains finite, the $\Gap$ row $G$ remains visible, and the supply-boundary ledger $L$ records non-internality rather than importing an induction principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the finite trace row $T$. It contains only displayed observations and bounded replay data, so it cannot already contain the universal discharge.

Read the belief socket $B$ and GAP row $G$. The socket names the commitment that would be needed, while the GAP row keeps the missing discharge exposed to audit.

Finally read $L,H,C,P,N$. These rows transport, replay, source, and name the non-discharge boundary. None adds an unrestricted induction rule, hidden witness, completed supply source, quotient equality, self-certifying truth row, or bridge-checked theorem.
\end{proof}
