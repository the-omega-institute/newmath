\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RealArchimedeanWindowUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\RealArchimedeanWindowUp$ packet records the finite inspected window by which a displayed $\RealUp$ name is bracketed between dyadic lower and upper rows at a positive radius before any Real or Completion consumer reads an order-facing statement. It sits over the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic endpoint ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the positive-radius surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-positivereal-namecert}, and the Archimedean property route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-archimedean-property-namecert}. The packet names a displayed bracketing carrier; it is not a completeness theorem, trichotomy principle, quotient-stream equality test, selected-limit operator, or open-cover compactness claim.

\begin{definition}[RealArchimedeanWindow carrier]
\label{def:real-archimedean-window-carrier}
A $\RealArchimedeanWindowUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(R,S,Q,D,L,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the sealed $\RealUp$ row whose inspected name is being bracketed;
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation schedule selecting the finite windows consumed by the read;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same stream windows;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ dyadic-core ledger for the bracket arithmetic;
\item $L$ is the lower dyadic bracket row;
\item $U$ is the upper dyadic bracket row;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the real seal, schedule, readback, dyadic core, lower and upper brackets, provenance, and local naming row;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the same inspected bracketing route;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealArchimedeanWindowUp$, $\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\PositiveRealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealArchimedeanWindowUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The positive radius is carried as part of the local dyadic-window read through $D,L,U$ and the sibling $\PositiveRealUp$ provenance. No coordinate stores a quotient real representative, selected limit point, host trichotomy decision, or ambient completeness witness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RealArchimedeanWindow NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-archimedean-window-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\RealArchimedeanWindowUp$ carrier $W=(R,S,Q,D,L,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealArchimedeanWindowUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, sealed $\RealUp$ exposure, finite $\StreamNameUp$ observation scheduling, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ bracket arithmetic, lower and upper dyadic endpoint exposure, positive-radius provenance, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.RealArchimedeanWindowUp.RealArchimedeanWindowNameCertObligations}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:real-archimedean-window-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes only the rows $R,S,Q,D,L,U,H,C,P,N$.

The observation route reads $S$ before $Q$, and reads $D$ before the lower and upper endpoint rows. Thus the bracket is an inspected dyadic window over a displayed regular rational readback rather than a host comparison of real numbers.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no quotient-stream equality coordinate, selected-limit coordinate, trichotomy coordinate, or completeness coordinate, the local certificate cannot export any of those principles.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RealArchimedeanWindow dyadic bracket]
\label{thm:real-archimedean-window-dyadic-bracket}
Every accepted $\RealArchimedeanWindowUp$ packet exposes its lower and upper bracket only through the shared dyadic-core row $D$, the inspected stream schedule $S$, and the regular readback row $Q$. The lower row $L$ and upper row $U$ are consumed as one finite bracketing surface for the sealed real row $R$ and the positive-radius provenance in $P$; they do not supply Real completeness, trichotomy, quotient stream equality, selected limits, or open-cover compactness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Open the carrier $W=(R,S,Q,D,L,U,H,C,P,N)$. The two endpoint rows $L$ and $U$ are not independent order witnesses; they are named after the schedule, readback, and dyadic-core rows have been displayed.

Use the NameCert obligations above. They make the bracketing route pass through $R,S,Q,D,L,U$ before transport, replay, provenance, or local naming is read.

The exclusion follows by coordinate inspection. A completeness theorem, trichotomy decision, quotient stream equality test, selected-limit operator, and open-cover compactness witness are absent from the carrier, so no consumer of the window can project them from the dyadic bracket.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RealArchimedeanWindow bound consumer exactness]
\label{thm:real-archimedean-window-bound-consumer-exactness}
Every order-facing or completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\RealArchimedeanWindowUp$ packet must read the $\PositiveRealUp$ radius provenance and the $\RealArchimedeanPropertyUp$ bound route together with the displayed rows $R,S,Q,D,L,U,H,C,P,N$ before treating the bracket as a bound. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $W=(R,S,Q,D,L,U,H,C,P,N)$ from \autoref{def:real-archimedean-window-carrier}. The only radius-facing entry available to a consumer is the provenance row $P$, where the $\PositiveRealUp$ dependency is recorded together with the local $\NameCert_{\RealArchimedeanWindowUp}$ row.

Use the NameCert obligations from \autoref{thm:real-archimedean-window-namecert-obligations}. They force the order-facing read to keep $R,S,Q,D,L,U,H,C,P,N$ in the same finite packet, so the lower and upper endpoints cannot be separated from their stream schedule, regular readback, dyadic core, transport, replay, provenance, or local name.

Apply the dyadic-bracket handoff of \autoref{thm:real-archimedean-window-dyadic-bracket}. The bracket is consumed only after $D,S,Q,L,U$ and the positive-radius provenance have been displayed. Therefore an order or completion consumer that treats the bracket as a bound must also carry the sibling $\RealArchimedeanPropertyUp$ route named in the chapter interface, rather than projecting a host order axiom, trichotomy decision, quotient-real equality test, selected-limit operator, or ambient completeness witness from the window alone.
\end{proof}
