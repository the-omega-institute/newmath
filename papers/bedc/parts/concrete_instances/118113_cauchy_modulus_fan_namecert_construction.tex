\item fan coverage, requiring each index named by $I$ to expose a $\CauchyModulusUp$ schedule, a shared dyadic tolerance read, a finite $\StreamNameUp$ or $\ProbeBundle$ window, and a $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback in the same packet;
\item shared-threshold stability, requiring tolerance refinements in $D$ to replay componentwise through every schedule in $M$ and through the meet and arithmetic rows in $A$;

For shared-threshold stability, replay a refinement of $D$ through $C$ and transport it through $H$. The meet and arithmetic rows in $A$ keep the request componentwise, so no schedule can leave the finite fan.

For RegSeqRat readback and the Real seal, every component reaches $Q$ before $E$ is visible. Since $E$ is terminal and the carrier has no row for a selected common limit, countable family, quotient stream equality, choice selector, or ambient completeness, the five obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
