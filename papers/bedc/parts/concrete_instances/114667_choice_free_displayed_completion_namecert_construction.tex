\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ChoiceFreeDisplayedCompletionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-choice-free-displayed-completion-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis and Kelley completion language, the $\ChoiceFreeDisplayedCompletionUp$ packet records the displayed limit interface used by completion arguments that inspect finite Cauchy data without quotienting names or selecting representatives. It sits over the $\CauchyUp$ carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-namecert}, the $\StreamNameUp$ finite windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the sequence-completeness service of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sequentiallycompletemetric-namecert}, the complete-metric boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the metric-completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives L10 Real and Completion consumers a finite displayed-completion landing surface while refusing quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, ambient completeness, and any claim that $\RealUp$ is complete.

\begin{definition}[Choice-free displayed completion carrier]
\label{def:choice-free-displayed-completion-carrier}
A $\ChoiceFreeDisplayedCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(X,S,R,M,L,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row naming the admitted carrier, distance observations, and finite metric request histories.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row for the Cauchy name under inspection.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same stream windows.
\item $M$ is the modulus-threshold row assigning each requested tolerance a displayed late index.
\item $L$ is the displayed limit row offered to $\SequentiallyCompleteMetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, and $\RealUp$ consumers only after $X,S,R,M$ have been read.
\item $E$ is the late-distance ledger. For each displayed tolerance and every sufficiently late window, it records the metric-distance comparison from the readback row to $L$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, stream windows, regular readback, modulus threshold, displayed limit, late-distance ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request through $X,S,R,M,E,L$ and then through the structural rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ChoiceFreeDisplayedCompletionUp$, $\CauchyUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\SequentiallyCompleteMetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChoiceFreeDisplayedCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, a selected representative of a Cauchy class, a countable-choice schedule, ambient completeness, or a proof that $\RealUp$ is complete.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free displayed completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:choice-free-displayed-completion-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\ChoiceFreeDisplayedCompletionUp$ carrier $D=(X,S,R,M,L,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ChoiceFreeDisplayedCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the metric source, stream-window, regular-rational readback, modulus-threshold, displayed-limit, late-distance, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows; classifier stability under componentwise $\hsame$ transport; modulus coverage for every displayed tolerance request; late-distance exactness for the displayed limit; consumer handoff to $\SequentiallyCompleteMetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, and $\RealUp$ through the same rows; and ledger refusal for quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, ambient completeness, and completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier definition. The packet exposes exactly the rows $X,S,R,M,L,E,H,C,P,N$, so carrier admission and the local naming row are read from the displayed tuple.

Classifier stability is componentwise. Any accepted transport first identifies the metric source, stream windows, regular readback, modulus threshold, displayed limit, late-distance ledger, replay, provenance, and local name through $H$ before the packet can be compared.

Modulus coverage and late-distance exactness are read in sequence. A tolerance request reaches the displayed threshold row $M$, then inspects the windows $S$ and readback $R$, and only then reads the ledger $E$ comparing late values with $L$ inside the metric source $X$.

The consumer handoff is the same finite route replayed by $C$ and sourced by $P,N$. Since none of the rows stores quotient equality, a selected representative, a choice schedule, an ambient complete carrier, or a completeness theorem for $\RealUp$, the ledger refusal follows from carrier exhaustion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free displayed completion limit handoff]
\label{thm:choice-free-displayed-completion-limit-handoff}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\ChoiceFreeDisplayedCompletionUp$ packet $D=(X,S,R,M,L,E,H,C,P,N)$ receives the displayed limit only through the route
$$
X,S,R,M,E \longmapsto L \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
The handoff supplies metric source data, stream windows, regular rational readback, a modulus threshold, a displayed limit, a late-distance ledger, $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and a local $\NameCert$ row. It does not supply quotient completion equality, selected representatives, countable-choice schedules, ambient completeness, or a claim that $\RealUp$ is complete.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a completion-facing request. The metric source row $X$ fixes the distance observations available to the request, and the stream-window row $S$ fixes the inspected Cauchy data.

Read $R$ and $M$ next. The regular rational row supplies the finite readback of the displayed windows, while the modulus row supplies the late threshold for the requested tolerance.

The late-distance ledger $E$ is then the only row that compares late readbacks with the displayed limit row $L$. Thus a $\SequentiallyCompleteMetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricCompletionUp$, or $\RealUp$ consumer can reach $L$ only after the metric, stream, readback, threshold, and distance-ledger rows are visible.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly that route. The carrier has no further coordinate, so the handoff cannot be read as quotient completion equality, a representative selector, a countable-choice schedule, ambient completeness, or completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{proof}
