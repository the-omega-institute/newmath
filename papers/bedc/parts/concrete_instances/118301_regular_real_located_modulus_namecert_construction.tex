\begin{theorem}[Regular real located modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-real-located-modulus-namecert-obligations}

\item carrier admission for the stream schedule, dyadic ledger, regular readback, located bracket, modulus, Real-seal window, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item modulus admission, requiring each request in $M$ to consume a positive tolerance row already displayed in $D$;

\item tail localization, requiring the depth supplied by $M$ to bind all later $Q$-reads to the bracket row $E$ before $W$ is available;

The modulus and bracket obligations are row-local. The modulus row $M$ has no tolerance coordinate other than the positive rows of $D$, and the located bracket row $E$ reads the same stream schedule and regular rational source through $S$ and $Q$.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are structural rows. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient equality, selected representatives, selected limits, host equality, trichotomy, choice, or ambient completeness, the listed obligations exhaust the local certificate surface.
