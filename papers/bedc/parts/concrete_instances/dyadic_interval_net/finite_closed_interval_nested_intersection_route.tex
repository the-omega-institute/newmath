\begin{theorem}[DyadicIntervalNet finite closed-interval nested-intersection route]
\label{thm:dyadic-interval-net-finite-closed-interval-nested-intersection-route}
Every $\FiniteClosedIntervalNestedIntersectionUp$ consumer that reads an accepted $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ packet must inspect the finite interval-cell refinement rows before it reaches terminal $\RealUp$ seals. The route passes through the local DyadicIntervalNet carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-net-namecert} and then through the finite closed-interval nested-intersection packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-closed-interval-nested-intersection-namecert}; it exports no selected intersection point, quotient real equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the DyadicIntervalNet carrier of \autoref{def:dyadic-interval-net-carrier}. The rows $D,J,E$ display dyadic endpoint arithmetic, finite cells, and endpoint incidence before any StreamName, RegSeqRat, or Real-facing seal is visible.

Apply the finite closed-interval nested-intersection carrier of \autoref{def:finite-closed-interval-nested-intersection-carrier}. Its cell row reads the same interval-net data before bounded-sequence or nested-intersection consumers receive a Real-facing handoff.

Thus the downstream nested-intersection route is a consumer of displayed finite cell refinement, not a source of completed intersections, selected points, quotient equality, choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
