\begin{theorem}[DyadicIntervalNet sibling dependency route]
\label{thm:dyadic-interval-net-orphan-route}
Every finite-cell read of a $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ carrier consumed by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedintervalnet-namecert} or \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-interval-uniform-modulus-namecert} must first expose DyadicRatCore endpoint arithmetic, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicIntervalRefinement retained-cell rows, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. Thus no compact or uniform-modulus consumer can use the interval-net carrier as an ambient compactness principle, a host completion theorem, or an untracked real endpoint object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the dependency route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-refinement-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. These sibling chapters supply endpoint arithmetic, regular rational readback, retained-cell refinement, and terminal sealing before the finite-cell row is visible to any compact or uniform-modulus consumer. Therefore the DyadicIntervalNet packet remains a finite interval-net route rather than an ambient compactness or host-completion principle.

The closed-bounded interval net consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedintervalnet-namecert} reads the same DyadicRatCore endpoint arithmetic, RegSeqRat tolerance readback, DyadicIntervalRefinement retained-cell rows, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal before it treats the finite cells as a bounded interval net.
\end{proof}
