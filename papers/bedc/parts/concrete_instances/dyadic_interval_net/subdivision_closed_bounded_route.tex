\begin{theorem}[Dyadic interval net subdivision closed-bounded route]
\label{thm:dyadic-interval-net-subdivision-closed-bounded-route}
Every accepted $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ carrier
$$
I=(D,J,E,S,R,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
used by an interval-lattice consumer factors through the dyadic cell rows of \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-net-cover-refinement}, the subdivision carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-interval-subdivision-namecert}, and the closed-bounded interval net rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedintervalnet-namecert}. The route sends $D,J,E$ to the subdivision scale, grid, and coverage rows, then sends the resulting finite cells to the closed-bounded interval mesh before $S,R,Q$ are read. It exports only displayed dyadic cells, subdivision rows, finite mesh coverage, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, and the Real seal; it exports no open-cover compactness theorem, selected finite subcover, quotient stream equality, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the local carrier and cover-refinement rows. The rows $D,J,E$ display dyadic endpoint arithmetic, the listed interval cells, and endpoint incidence, so every interval-net read starts as a finite cell read.

Next pass through dyadic interval subdivision. The subdivision chapter reads closed interval endpoints, dyadic scale, grid rows, cell coverage, and mesh bounds as displayed finite rows. Therefore a lattice consumer can refine $J,E$ only by exposing those subdivision rows, not by invoking an ambient cover or hidden grid selector.

Now attach the closed-bounded interval net. Its carrier reads the located endpoint, mesh, rational and dyadic cell, center, and coverage rows before any Real-facing handoff is public, so the interval net becomes part of the closed-bounded finite mesh route.

Finally read $S,R,Q,H,C,P,N$. The StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows preserve the same finite route. Since none of the three carriers contains an open-cover compactness, selected-subcover, quotient-stream, countable-choice, host-equality, or ambient-completeness coordinate, the lattice route cannot export one.
\end{proof}
