\begin{theorem}[DyadicIntervalNet forward lattice route]
\label{thm:dyadic-interval-net-forward-lattice-route}
Every compactness, finite-net, or Real-completion consumer of an accepted $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ carrier must expose $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ endpoint and radius arithmetic, the listed interval-cell row, endpoint incidence, $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal before reading compactness or completion rows. The forward lattice is therefore the displayed route
$$
D,J,E \longmapsto S,R \longmapsto Q,H,C,P,N
$$
of \autoref{def:dyadic-interval-net-carrier}, not an open-cover compactness theorem, selected finite subcover, quotient stream equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier $I=(D,J,E,S,R,Q,H,C,P,N)$ of \autoref{def:dyadic-interval-net-carrier}. Endpoint arithmetic and radius comparisons live in $D$, the listed cells live in $J$, endpoint incidence lives in $E$, and the completion-facing rows are downstream in $S,R,Q$.

Apply the finite-mesh Real-completion route and compact interval forward route, \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-net-finite-mesh-real-completion-route} and \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-net-compact-interval-forward-route}. They force mesh, compact-interval, and completion consumers to display finite cells, endpoint arithmetic, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, and the terminal Real seal in that order.

Apply the regular-Cauchy Real forward route and finite-net sibling dependency route, \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-net-regular-cauchy-real-forward-route} and \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-net-finite-net-sibling-dependency-route}. These routes keep regular-Cauchy, finite-net, and compactness consumers on the same dyadic endpoint, finite-cell, window, readback, and Real-seal lattice.

Finally replay $H,C,P,N$. Componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the finite lattice route and provide no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
