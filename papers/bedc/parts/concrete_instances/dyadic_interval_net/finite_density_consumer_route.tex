\begin{theorem}[Dyadic interval net finite-density consumer route]
\label{thm:dyadic-interval-net-finite-density-consumer-route}
Every completion-facing consumer that reads a $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ packet as finite dyadic cell data may pass that read to $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$ only after the displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ rows have been exposed. The handoff cites the finite density packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cauchy-density-net-namecert} only through dyadic cell incidence, stream windows, regular rational readback, terminal Real sealing, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the dyadic interval-net hub through its L10 sibling routes. The dyadic cells and endpoint incidence are read first through the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ rows, so the consumer has no immediate completion-facing density claim.

The route then exposes the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row. These rows are the visible bridge from finite dyadic cells to regular rational observations, and they match the stream and readback requirements used by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-cauchy-density-net-namecert}.

Only after those rows are displayed may the consumer read a terminal $\RealUp$ seal and hand the packet to $\FiniteCauchyDensityNetUp$. The same $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows preserve the displayed route, so no quotient stream, selected representative, ambient completeness theorem, or unrecorded density selector is exported by the dyadic interval net itself.
\end{proof}
