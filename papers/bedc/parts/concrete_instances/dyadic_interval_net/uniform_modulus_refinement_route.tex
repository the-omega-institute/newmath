\begin{theorem}[Dyadic interval net uniform-modulus refinement route]
\label{thm:dyadic-interval-net-uniform-modulus-refinement-route}
Every $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$ mesh-refinement read consumed by $\CompactIntervalUniformModulusUp$ or $\FiniteEpsilonNetUniformModulusUp$ must first pass through the finite route
$$
D,J,E \longmapsto S,R \longmapsto Q,H,C,P,N
$$
of the accepted carrier $I=(D,J,E,S,R,Q,H,C,P,N)$. The mesh-refinement induction, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ route, and the $\StreamNameUp$ handoff therefore expose dyadic endpoint arithmetic, listed interval cells, endpoint incidence, finite observation windows, regular rational readback, the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before any uniform-modulus ledger is read.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the mesh-refinement induction of \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-net-mesh-refinement-induction}. It keeps every refined finite cell inside the displayed rows $D,J,E$ and preserves the downstream route through $S,R,Q,H,C,P,N$ at each finite refinement depth.

Next apply the $\RegSeqRatUp$ route of \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-net-regseqrat-route} and the $\StreamNameUp$ handoff of \autoref{thm:dyadic-interval-net-streamname-regseqrat-handoff}. These rows force endpoint observations through $S$ and rational tolerance readback through $R$ before the terminal Real seal $Q$ is opened.

Now read the consumer rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-interval-uniform-modulus-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-uniform-modulus-namecert}. A compact-interval or finite-epsilon-net uniform-modulus consumer receives only the routed dyadic packet, so its ledger is downstream of the displayed sequence $D,J,E \longmapsto S,R \longmapsto Q,H,C,P,N$.

The route has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient stream equality, countable choice, trichotomy, host equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness. Thus every uniform-modulus read remains a finite dyadic interval-net refinement read before the modulus ledger is consumed.
\end{proof}
