\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\InducedCharacterUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-induced-character-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Serre on finite-group characters and Fulton--Harris on induction and restriction, the $\InducedCharacterUp$ packet records the character-level coset-sum construction as finite NameCert data. It consumes the finite group source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}, the subgroup source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subgroup-namecert}, the coset ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-coset-space-namecert}, the finite action rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-group-representation-namecert}, the trace and class-function surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-charactertheory-namecert}, the value-row consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-character-table-namecert}, the induction--restriction packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-inducedrep-namecert}, and the reciprocity provenance of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-frobenius-reciprocity-namecert}. The packet names only the displayed coset-sum character row, its transport, its class-function stability, and its handoff to induction and reciprocity consumers; it does not assert irreducible decomposition, Schur orthogonality, a completed character table, or a host category of representations.

\begin{definition}[InducedCharacter carrier]
\label{def:induced-character-carrier}
An $\InducedCharacterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
\mathcal{C}=(G,H,Q,V,\chi,\iota,\sigma,\kappa,\rho,\lambda,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the consumed finite $\NameCert_{\GroupUp}$ source row.
\item $H$ is the consumed $\NameCert_{\SubgroupUp}$ row naming the subgroup inclusion inside the same finite group source.
\item $Q$ is the consumed $\NameCert_{\CosetSpaceUp}$ row listing the finite left-coset representatives used only as display indices.
\item $V$ is the consumed $\NameCert_{\FiniteGroupRepresentationUp}$ row whose restricted trace row supplies the source character on $H$.
\item $\chi$ is the consumed $\NameCert_{\CharacterTheoryUp}$ row naming the source class-function and trace surface.
\item $\iota$ is the displayed $\NameCert_{\InducedRepUp}$ induction row.
\item $\sigma$ is the finite coset-sum ledger assigning to each displayed group row the finite sum of the source-character values over the accepted representative rows of $Q$.
\item $\kappa$ is the class-function stability row, recording conjugation transport of $\sigma$ through $G$, $H$, $Q$, and $\chi$.
\item $\rho$ is the $\NameCert_{\FrobeniusReciprocityUp}$ provenance row tying the character-level handoff to the induction--restriction comparison.
\item $\lambda$ is the downstream consumer ledger for $\CharacterTableUp$, $\InducedRepUp$, and $\FrobeniusReciprocityUp$ reads.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over the consumed rows, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\InducedCharacterUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no host induced representation, no selected representative object beyond the displayed coset ledger, no irreducible-character decomposition, and no ambient character algebra outside these rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[InducedCharacter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:induced-character-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\InducedCharacterUp$ packet
$$
\mathcal{C}=(G,H,Q,V,\chi,\iota,\sigma,\kappa,\rho,\lambda,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\InducedCharacterUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission exposes the displayed group, subgroup, coset-space, finite-representation, character-theory, induced-representation, coset-sum, class-stability, reciprocity, consumer, provenance, and local naming rows of \autoref{def:induced-character-carrier};
\item coset-representative invariance requires the value of $\sigma$ to be unchanged under $\hsame$ transport of the displayed representative rows in $Q$, with every replacement read still sourced by $H$ and $G$;
\item class-function stability requires the conjugation transport row $\kappa$ to preserve the induced value row through the trace and class-function surface $\chi$;
\item restriction/induction handoff requires every $\InducedRepUp$ read to pass through the displayed induction row $\iota$ and the same subgroup, representation, and coset ledgers used by $\sigma$;
\item reciprocity provenance requires every Frobenius comparison read to pass through $\rho$ and the character-pairing boundary of $\FrobeniusReciprocityUp$, with no bypass through a host adjunction or semisimple decomposition.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows of \autoref{def:induced-character-carrier}. Carrier admission is the displayed tuple $\mathcal{C}$ itself, so every source and consumer row is already visible in finite $\BHist$ form.

For coset-representative invariance, compare two accepted representative ledgers in $Q$. The transport is componentwise $\hsame$ over the consumed group and subgroup rows, and each summand is still read through the source character row $\chi$. Reindexing the finite ledger therefore changes only the display of the summation, not the named character row.

Class-function stability is the row $\kappa$. It transports the coset-sum value along conjugation in $G$ and checks that the source trace row remains inside the class-function surface named by $\CharacterTheoryUp$.

For the induction handoff, route every representation-level read through $\iota$ and the public boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-inducedrep-namecert}. For reciprocity provenance, route every comparison read through $\rho$ and the finite character-pairing boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-frobenius-reciprocity-namecert}. Repacking these projected rows with $\lambda$, $P$, and $N$ gives exactly the five listed obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[InducedCharacter table consumer boundary]
\label{thm:induced-character-table-consumer-boundary}
Any $\CharacterTableUp$ consumer of an accepted $\InducedCharacterUp$ packet may read only the finite group row $G$, subgroup row $H$, coset ledger $Q$, source character row $\chi$, coset-sum row $\sigma$, class-stability row $\kappa$, and consumer ledger $\lambda$. It may not import an irreducible decomposition, Schur orthogonality row, completed character table, host representative selector, or ambient representation category unless that row is already present in the displayed packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the consumer request through the displayed carrier. The character-table consumer receives the group and class-function rows only through $G$ and $\chi$, and it receives coset indexing only through $Q$.

The induced value row is $\sigma$, while its class-function stability is $\kappa$. The consumer ledger $\lambda$ records exactly the downstream reads exposed to $\CharacterTableUp$ together with the representation-induction and reciprocity handoff rows.

No remaining coordinate of $\mathcal{C}$ names an irreducible decomposition, a Schur row, a completed table, a representative selector, or a host representation category. Hence the table-facing boundary is precisely the finite packet described above.
\end{proof}
