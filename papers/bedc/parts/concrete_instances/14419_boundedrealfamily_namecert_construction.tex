\begin{definition}[Bounded real family carrier]
\label{def:bounded-real-family-carrier}
A $\BoundedRealFamilyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(I,W,Q,R,B,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the finite index-window ledger. Each row names an inspected family index together with the finite observation window requested at that index.
\item $W$ is the family of $\StreamNameUp$ windows displayed for the inspected index-window rows of $I$.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those StreamName windows and their visible dyadic tolerance observations.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ family-seal row. A family member is visible to a Real-facing consumer only after its index row, StreamName window, and RegSeqRat readback have been displayed.
\item $B$ is the $\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp$ bound row carrying the located lower and upper interval data used to witness a common bound for the displayed family rows.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the index-window ledger, StreamName windows, regular readback, Real family seals, bound row, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from index-window rows to StreamName windows, from StreamName windows to RegSeqRat readback, from RegSeqRat readback to Real family seals, and from Real family seals to the common bound ledger.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BoundedRealFamilyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedRealFamilyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The bound row is displayed as finite located interval data
$$
\begin{gathered}
B:\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp,\\
\forall i\in I_{\mathrm{shown}}\quad W_i\longmapsto Q_i\longmapsto R_i\in B.
\end{gathered}
$$
Here $I_{\mathrm{shown}}$ denotes only the inspected finite index-window rows carried by the packet. The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,W,Q,R,B,H,C,P,N$ and has no coordinate for trichotomy, quotient equality of real names, countable choice over indices, an ambient supremum, open-cover compactness, or host completeness.
\end{definition}
