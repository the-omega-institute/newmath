\begin{definition}[RH route closure fixed statement separation carrier]
\label{def:rh-route-closure-fixed-statement-separation-carrier}
A $\RhRouteClosureFixedStatementSeparationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(R,F,U,B,T,C,H,Q,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the route-layer row, recording a finite route-family classifier request over route codes and theorem targets.
\item $F$ is the fixed-statement row, recording the single $\mathsf{RH}$ proposition as a fixed constructive statement rather than a family of routes.
\item $U$ is the route-undecidability row, importing only the family-level obstruction from the route-halting boundary.
\item $B$ is the row-split boundary, requiring any independence-facing consumer to display which data belong to $R,U$ and which data belong to $F$.
\item $T$ is the refusal row, storing failed attempts to reuse a route-family classifier obstruction as a fixed-RH independence witness.
\item $C$ is the TasteGate-facing row requiring later Lean files to provide $\Nontrivial\ \RhRouteClosureFixedStatementSeparationUp$ and $\FieldFaithful\ \RhRouteClosureFixedStatementSeparationUp$ instances for the displayed route-layer and statement-layer fields.

\item $Q$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a route-family obstruction request through $R,U,B,T$ without rewriting it into a proof predicate for the fixed row $F$.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,F,U,B,T,C,H,Q,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a fragment-specific non-provability theorem, a proof or refutation of $\mathsf{RH}$, an identification of route closure with fixed-statement truth, or a total classifier for route-family closure.

\begin{theorem}[RH route closure fixed statement separation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:rh-route-closure-fixed-statement-separation-namecert-obligations}

Every independence-facing consumer must read the row-split boundary $B$ and refusal row $T$ before treating route undecidability as evidence about the fixed $\mathsf{RH}$ statement.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:rh-route-closure-fixed-statement-separation-carrier}. The route-layer row $R$ and obstruction row $U$ are family-level data, while $F$ is the single fixed proposition row.

The row-split boundary $B$ is read before any independence-facing consumer receives the packet. If a consumer attempts to identify the route-family obstruction with fragment-specific independence for the fixed row, the failed row is stored in $T$ rather than accepted as a NameCert obligation.

The remaining rows preserve the displayed separation. The TasteGate-facing row $C$ names the later $\Nontrivial$ and $\FieldFaithful$ obligations for the same carrier fields, while $H,Q,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only the split route-layer and statement-layer data.
\end{proof}
