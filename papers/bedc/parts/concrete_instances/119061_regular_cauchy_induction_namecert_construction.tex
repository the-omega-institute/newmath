$$

\item $S$ is the successor and tail-step row, recording the rule that extends an accepted displayed window to the next regular Cauchy tail request.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budget row used by both $B$ and $S$ for radius comparison and tail contraction.
\item $W$ is the finite request-window ledger listing every stream coordinate read by the base and successor rows.

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy induction step soundness]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-induction-step-soundness}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyInductionUp$ carrier
$$
I=(B,S,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the successor row $S$ may advance a regular Cauchy construction only along request windows already displayed by $W$ and budgeted by $Q$. After componentwise transport through $H$ and replay through $C$, the same finite route exposes the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$ before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$. No step exports countable choice, quotient equality of names, host real comparison, or a selected limit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier onto its displayed rows. The only induction data are the base window $B$, the successor and tail-step row $S$, the dyadic budget $Q$, and the finite request ledger $W$.

Induct on the displayed $\BHist$ request window. In the base case, the request is already listed by $B$ and budgeted by $Q$, so the route reaches $R$ without reading any hidden tail coordinate.

For the successor case, the induction hypothesis gives the prior displayed window. The row $S$ appends exactly the next tail request named in $W$, while $Q$ supplies the dyadic comparison budget needed for the regularity readback.

Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve that order. The rows $P$ and $N$ only record provenance and local naming, and the seal $E$ is downstream from $R$, so no unlisted choice sequence, quotient-equality test, host real comparison, or selected limit can enter the step.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:regular-cauchy-induction-finite-window}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

At each successor stage, the row $S$ extends the displayed window and consumes the same dyadic budget row $Q$. The induction hypothesis supplies all earlier reads, so the extended packet remains finite and visible in $W$.
