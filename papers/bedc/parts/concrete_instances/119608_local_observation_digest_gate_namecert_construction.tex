$$

\item $F$ is the source-fiber row that names the local observation fiber to which $V$ belongs.
\item $Q$ is the GAP provenance row recording the visible source route and the refused hidden-source route.

\begin{theorem}[Local observation digest gate obligations]
\label{thm:local-observation-digest-gate-obligations}

$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocalObservationDigestGateUp}$ surface consists exactly of visible token admission through $V$, source-fiber admission through $F$, GAP provenance through $Q$, exactness status through $E$, apophatic far-end status through $A$, hidden-source refusal through $R$, and structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:local-observation-digest-gate-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the visible token, source fiber, GAP provenance, exactness status, far-end status, hidden-source refusal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Read the digest side through $V,F,Q,E$. A consumer may use the visible token only together with the displayed fiber, the GAP provenance route, and the exactness-status row.

Read the refusal side through $A,R$. The far-end status and hidden-source refusal rows block any conversion of digest equality into source equality or hidden source recovery.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same local packet. Since no global hash, object-level $\mathsf{T}$ value, quotient equality, host collision row, theorem-input parameter, or detached certificate name occurs in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
