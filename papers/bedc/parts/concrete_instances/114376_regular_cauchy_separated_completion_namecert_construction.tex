\begin{definition}[Regular-Cauchy separated-completion carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-separated-completion-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchySeparatedCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(R,S,D,Z,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational Cauchy name row.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule through which $R$ is inspected.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger controlling the displayed Cauchy budgets.
\item $Z$ is the separated zero-distance row asserting that two completed reads agree only through the displayed regular-Cauchy windows and dyadic budgets.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available after $R,S,D,Z$ have been exposed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport across the regular name, stream windows, tolerance ledger, separatedness row, Real seal, and certificate rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion request to $R,S,D,Z,E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchySeparatedCompletionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchySeparatedCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,S,D,Z,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected representatives, host real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regular-Cauchy separated-completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-separated-completion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchySeparatedCompletionUp$ carrier $Q=(R,S,D,Z,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchySeparatedCompletionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source row, the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, the separated zero-distance row, the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No NameCert obligation may replace these rows by quotient stream equality, selected representatives, host real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-separated-completion-carrier}. The only visible coordinates are $R,S,D,Z,E,H,C,P,N$.

The completion-facing rows are ordered. The regular rational name is read through finite stream windows, those windows are bounded by the dyadic ledger, and the separated zero-distance row is downstream of both.

The terminal Real seal is therefore not a quotient of names. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the displayed rows, and the excluded equality, selector, choice, trichotomy, and ambient-completeness coordinates cannot be projected from the carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regular-Cauchy separated-completion zero-distance route]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-separated-completion-zero-distance}
Every zero-distance read accepted by a $\RegularCauchySeparatedCompletionUp$ packet factors through the displayed route
$$
\RegSeqRatUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RealUp .
$$
In particular, two Real-facing completion reads may be identified only after the regular rational source row, finite stream-window schedule, dyadic tolerance ledger, separatedness row, and terminal Real seal have all been displayed in the same finite packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Read the tolerance row $D$ and separatedness row $Z$ next. They bind zero-distance agreement to finite windows and dyadic budgets rather than to a host quotient or selected representative.

Finally consume the Real seal $E$ through the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. Since the carrier has no further equality coordinate, the accepted zero-distance read cannot escape the displayed route.
\end{proof}
