\begin{theorem}[Precompact function family NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:precompact-function-family-namecert-obligations}

$$

the local $\NameCert_{\PrecompactFunctionFamilyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the source row $X$, target row $Y$, compact-open topology row $T$, precompact uniform handoff $U$, bounded-family row $B$, equicontinuity row $E$, finite-net row $N$, compact-open window ledger $W$, componentwise transport $H$, displayed replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local naming $L$.

\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:precompact-function-family-carrier}. The compact-open read starts from $X,Y,T$, so every family request is already tied to displayed source, target, and compact-window rows.

The precompactness data are then forced through $U,B,E,N,W$. The precompact uniform handoff supplies the total-bounded family surface, the bounded-family and equicontinuity rows provide the Arzela--Ascoli side conditions, and the finite-net row records the inspected approximants at the requested tolerances.
