\begin{definition}[Finite variation partition carrier]
\label{def:finite-variation-partition-carrier}
A $\FiniteVariationPartitionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
V=(I,E,P,M,D,S,T,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the interval or domain row on which variation is measured, exposing the endpoint type and order comparison used by the packet.
\item $E$ is the endpoint row, naming the left and right boundary points and their admitted order.
\item $P$ is the finite partition row, listing the accepted ordered points between the endpoints.
\item $M$ is the mesh-comparison row, recording adjacent-point order and the finite successor relation inside $P$.
\item $D$ is the edge-difference row, naming the finite family of adjacent absolute differences or metric differences attached to $P$.
\item $S$ is the finite sum or ledger row that accumulates the entries of $D$ without taking an ambient supremum.
\item $T$ is the sibling handoff row to $\BoundedVariationUp$, $\TotalVariationFunctionUp$, $\RiemannSumUp$, or $\RegulatedIntegralUp$, with an explicit empty row when no such consumer is claimed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $I,E,P,M,D,S,T$ and the local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from endpoint admission through partition ordering, edge-difference summation, sibling handoff, provenance, and local naming.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteVariationPartitionUp$, $\BoundedVariationUp$, $\TotalVariationFunctionUp$, $\RiemannSumUp$, $\RegulatedIntegralUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteVariationPartitionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these eleven rows. It has no coordinate for a host-selected partition family, a supremum over all partitions, open-cover compactness, countable choice, quotient equality, or ambient real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite variation partition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-variation-partition-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteVariationPartitionUp$ carrier $V=(I,E,P,M,D,S,T,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteVariationPartitionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, endpoint exposure, finite partition ordering, mesh comparison, edge-difference exposure, finite-sum ledger exposure, sibling handoff to the bounded-variation, total-variation, Riemann-sum, or regulated-integral rows when $T$ is present, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation in the packet asserts a host partition selector, a global total-variation supremum, countable choice, quotient equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. Its visible rows are the domain row, endpoint row, finite partition row, mesh-comparison row, edge-difference row, finite-sum ledger, sibling handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

The partition obligation is finite. The row $P$ supplies only the displayed list of partition points, while $M$ records adjacent order and successor comparisons inside that list. No row ranges over all possible partitions.

The variation obligation is ledgered through $D$ and $S$. A bounded-variation or total-variation consumer may read the adjacent edge differences and their finite sum, but the packet does not identify this finite ledger with a supremum over every refinement.

The sibling handoff is exact. When $T$ cites $\BoundedVariationUp$, $\TotalVariationFunctionUp$, $\RiemannSumUp$, or $\RegulatedIntegralUp$, the read passes through $H,C,Q,N$; when $T$ is empty, the packet makes no downstream variation or integration claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite variation partition bounded-variation handoff]
\label{thm:finite-variation-partition-bounded-variation-handoff}
Every $\BoundedVariationUp$, $\TotalVariationFunctionUp$, $\RiemannSumUp$, or $\RegulatedIntegralUp$ consumer of an accepted $\FiniteVariationPartitionUp$ packet must first read
$$
I,E,P,M,D,S,T,H,C,Q,N.
$$
It receives the displayed endpoint, ordered finite partition, mesh-comparison, edge-difference, finite-sum, sibling handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert rows. It receives no host-level partition choice, no hidden variation supremum, no unledgered refinement family, no countable choice, and no ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-variation-partition-carrier}. The bounded-variation and integration-facing data occur only in the finite rows $P,M,D,S,T$ over the endpoint data $I,E$.

Apply the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:finite-variation-partition-namecert-obligations}. They require every downstream handoff to expose the finite partition ordering, mesh comparison, edge-difference ledger, and finite sum before the sibling row $T$ is read.

The structural rows preserve the same finite route. Transport $H$ is componentwise, replay $C$ follows the displayed ledger, provenance $Q$ names the sibling certificates, and $N$ names the local packet.

Therefore a bounded-variation, total-variation, Riemann-sum, or regulated-integral consumer receives only the finite partition ledger. The absent coordinates for host partition choice, global supremum, refinement-family choice, and ambient completeness cannot be recovered from the packet.
\end{proof}
