Following Heine--Cantor and Bishop's finite-net reading of compact uniform continuity, the $\CompactModulusEvaluationUp$ packet records how a compact metric source, a continuous-map row, and pointwise modulus data are evaluated into a finite uniform-modulus output without invoking open-cover compactness or a selected subcover theorem. It concretizes the compact uniform continuity direction of \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity} beside the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the continuous-map surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the uniform-modulus consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite NameCert evaluation route, not an external compactness theorem, choice principle, host equality of functions, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness claim.

\begin{definition}[Compact modulus evaluation carrier]
\label{def:compact-modulus-evaluation-carrier}
A $\CompactModulusEvaluationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(K,F,P,D,N,U,W,R,S,H,C,G,Q)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net source row, displaying the inspected probes, mesh radius, and compact-metric provenance.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row whose pointwise continuity reads are being evaluated on the compact source.
\item $P$ is the pointwise modulus row attached to the inspected probes and tolerance request.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, carrying the requested output radius, finite mesh slack, and lower-bound comparisons.
\item $N$ is the finite net and overlap row, recording which inspected probe covers each source request before the modulus is read.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row produced after the finite lower-bound fold.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule for point observations, probe reads, and requested tolerances.
\item $R$ is the regular rational readback row attached to the inspected windows and dyadic tolerance comparisons.
\item $S$ is the $\RealUp$ seal boundary reached only after $K,F,P,D,N,U,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, continuous map, pointwise modulus, dyadic ledger, finite net, uniform handoff, window, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of every compact modulus evaluation through
$$
K,F,P,D,N,W,R \longmapsto U,S .
$$
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactModulusEvaluationUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $Q$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactModulusEvaluationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,F,P,D,N,U,W,R,S,H,C,G,Q$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected subcovers, host equality of functions, quotient real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, Lean verification, or a standard bridge.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact modulus evaluation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-modulus-evaluation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactModulusEvaluationUp$ carrier
$$
E=(K,F,P,D,N,U,W,R,S,H,C,G,Q),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactModulusEvaluationUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the compact metric source, continuous map, pointwise modulus data, dyadic tolerance ledger, finite net and overlap rows, uniform-modulus handoff, stream windows, regular rational readback, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item compact-source stability, requiring every transported or replayed evaluation to keep the same finite-net source $K$ and finite net row $N$ before any modulus handoff can be consumed;
\item pointwise-modulus stability, requiring the continuous-map row $F$ and pointwise modulus row $P$ to be read at the displayed probe windows and never as a host-level function predicate;
\item dyadic fold exactness, requiring the tolerance ledger $D$ and readback row $R$ to carry all lower-bound, slack, and radius comparisons used to produce $U$;
\item ledger refusal, requiring $H,C,G,Q$ to transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed rows, with no arbitrary open cover, selected subcover, host function equality, quotient real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, Lean verification, or bridge credential.
\end{enumerate}
No compact modulus evaluation NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:compact-modulus-evaluation-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the compact source, continuous map, pointwise modulus, dyadic ledger, finite net, uniform handoff, stream windows, regular readback, real seal, and structural rows.

Compact-source stability is componentwise. Transport and replay keep the same finite-net source and cover row, so the packet never replaces the displayed compact metric data by an arbitrary open cover.

Pointwise-modulus stability is read through $F,P,W$. The continuous map and its pointwise modulus are inspected only at displayed probe windows, not through a host-level function equality predicate.

Dyadic fold exactness is the shared role of $D,N,R,U$. The finite net supplies the inspected probes, the dyadic ledger records the lower-bound and slack comparisons, the regular rational readback records the finite observations, and the uniform-modulus row is downstream of those data.

Finally, $H,C,G,Q$ only transport, replay, source, and name the displayed rows. Since the carrier contains no open-cover theorem, selected subcover, choice row, host equality coordinate, Lean verification, or bridge credential, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact modulus evaluation finite net route]
\label{thm:compact-modulus-evaluation-finite-net-route}
Every $\UniformModulusUp$ output exported by an accepted $\CompactModulusEvaluationUp$ carrier
$$
E=(K,F,P,D,N,U,W,R,S,H,C,G,Q)
$$
factors through the finite route
$$
K,F,P,D,N,W,R \longmapsto U,S
$$
with structural support $H,C,G,Q$. The route first exposes the compact metric finite-net source, then the continuous-map and pointwise-modulus reads, then the dyadic tolerance fold and regular rational readback, and only then the uniform-modulus handoff and terminal $\RealUp$ seal. It exports no arbitrary open-cover compactness, selected subcover, host equality of functions, quotient real equality, countable choice, trichotomy, Lean verification, or standard bridge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier projection. The compact metric source $K$ and finite net row $N$ are the only compact-facing rows available to the evaluation.

The continuous-map and pointwise-modulus data are read through $F,P,W$. Each inspected probe and tolerance request is attached to a displayed stream window, so no host-level function predicate is introduced.

The dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ and regular rational readback $R$ then perform the finite lower-bound and slack comparisons. These rows are upstream of the uniform-modulus handoff $U$.

Only after those rows have been displayed can the output row $U$ and terminal seal $S$ be consumed. Transport, replay, provenance, and naming through $H,C,G,Q$ preserve the same order, and the excluded open-cover, selection, choice, equality, verification, and bridge data are absent from the accepted packet.
\end{proof}
