\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CauchyTailSpaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-tail-space-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on Cauchy sequences and Kelley on filters, the $\CauchyTailSpaceUp$ packet records the finite tail-stability carrier that sits between the stream-name schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the real-seal boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. It is the local object read by the Cauchy-name map surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchycontinuousmap-namecert} when a consumer needs tail truncation, not selected limits or host equality of reals.

\begin{definition}[CauchyTailSpace carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-tail-space-carrier}
A $\CauchyTailSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(R,K,D,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the base regular Cauchy name row, read through $\RegSeqRatUp$ and its underlying $\StreamNameUp$ window schedule;
\item $K$ is the tail cutoff schedule, listing the finite windows after which the carrier permits a tail read;
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger attached to the displayed tail windows;
\item $Q$ is the tail-equivalence classifier, comparing two accepted packets only by the regular-name windows and dyadic tolerances visible past the displayed cutoffs;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $R,K,D,Q$ and for the local naming row;
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay routes by which a consumer reopens the same regular-name, cutoff, tolerance, and classifier rows;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over the stream-name, regular-rational, dyadic-tolerance, real-seal boundary, transport, replay, and local naming rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyTailSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier has no coordinate for quotient streams, selected limits, host real equality, completeness, countable choice, or a hidden real-valued representative.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tail truncation stability]
\label{thm:cauchy-tail-space-tail-truncation-stability}
For every accepted $\CauchyTailSpaceUp$ carrier $T=(R,K,D,Q,H,C,P,N)$, any consumer that discards an initial finite prefix must still expose the base regular Cauchy name row $R$, the cutoff schedule $K$, and the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ before it may read the tail-equivalence classifier $Q$. The truncation therefore preserves the finite $\StreamNameUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ tail surface, without choosing a limit or quotient-stream representative.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-tail-space-carrier}. The only rows that can witness a tail read are the base regular name $R$, the cutoff schedule $K$, and the tolerance ledger $D$.

After a finite prefix is ignored, the displayed continuation route $C$ still reopens $R,K,D$ before $Q$ is visible. The prefix removal changes which finite windows are inspected, but it does not add a new carrier coordinate.

Transport through $H$ preserves the same rows componentwise. Hence the accepted tail read remains a finite regular-Cauchy read and cannot export a selected limit, quotient stream, or host real value.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Tail classifier transport]
\label{thm:cauchy-tail-space-tail-classifier-transport}
If two accepted $\CauchyTailSpaceUp$ carriers are related by the displayed $\hsame$ rows of their packets, then every tail-equivalence read through $Q$ transports along the same $R,K,D$ rows. A transported classifier cannot replace those rows by host real equality, ambient completeness, or an unrecorded filter equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the transport row $H$. It stores componentwise transport for the base regular name, cutoff schedule, dyadic ledger, classifier, and local naming row.

Replay the classifier through $C$. The classifier row $Q$ is available only after the transported $R,K,D$ rows are displayed, so its comparison remains tied to the finite packet.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for host real equality, filter equality, quotient streams, completeness, or choice, the transported classifier cannot use any of those objects as a substitute for the displayed tail rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real seal nonescape]
\label{thm:cauchy-tail-space-real-seal-nonescape}
Every $\RealUp$-facing consumer of an accepted $\CauchyTailSpaceUp$ packet must factor its read through the $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$, the tail cutoff schedule $K$, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger $D$, the classifier row $Q$, displayed $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row. The read does not create a selected limit, a host real equality proof, a completeness theorem, a quotient-stream function, or a choice principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier inventory. The only route from a tail packet toward $\RealUp$ is the displayed regular-rational and dyadic-tolerance route through $R,K,D,Q$.

The replay row $C$ forces a consumer to reopen that route before any real-seal boundary can be cited. The transport row $H$ and provenance row $P$ keep the same finite data attached to the local naming row $N$.

The refused objects are absent from the carrier. Therefore a $\RealUp$-facing read may consume the finite tail-stability packet, but it cannot manufacture selected limits, host real equality, completeness, quotient-stream functions, or choice.
\end{proof}
