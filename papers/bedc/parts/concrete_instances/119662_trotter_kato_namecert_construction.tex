\begin{definition}[Trotter-Kato generator-window carrier]
\label{def:trotter-kato-generator-window-carrier}
A $\TrotterKatoUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(S,F,O,R,Q,G,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\SemigroupUp$ composition row for the approximating evolution family and the limiting evolution row inspected by the consumer.
\item $F$ is the $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$ source row, exposing the bounded-operator, domain-window, and graph-readback coordinates available to the approximation request.
\item $O$ is the $\OperatorIdealUp$ or compact-operator observation row used when the approximation is read through finite-rank, compact, or ideal-controlled windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the inspected rational time, resolvent, and graph-window tolerances.
\item $Q$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal for the scalar error bounds and convergence budgets read from $R$.
\item $G$ records the generator-window ledger: finite source-domain rows, finite graph comparison rows, and the displayed compatibility of approximating generators with the limiting generator window.
\item $W$ records the resolvent-sequence window: finite resolvent probes, rational error budgets, and the handoff order by which resolvent convergence may be consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the semigroup, functional-analysis, operator-ideal, rational-readback, Real-seal, generator-window, resolvent-window, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Trotter-Kato approximation request through $S,F,O,R,Q,G,W,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TrotterKatoUp$, $\SemigroupUp$, $\FunctionalAnalysisUp$, $\OperatorIdealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TrotterKatoUp}$ row naming exactly this finite approximation packet.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host graph-limit theorem, selected core, quotient operator equality, global PDE well-posedness, or ambient Banach-space completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Trotter-Kato generator-window NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:trotter-kato-generator-window-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\TrotterKatoUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\TrotterKatoUp}$ surface consists exactly of semigroup composition visibility, functional-analysis source visibility, operator-window visibility, regular rational readback, Real-seal error-budget visibility, generator-window compatibility, resolvent-sequence ordering, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, package provenance, and local naming. Every downstream semigroup, PDE, or operator approximation consumer must pass through $S,F,O,R,Q,G,W,H,C,P,N$ before it reads a Trotter-Kato approximation claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:trotter-kato-generator-window-carrier}. The rows $S,F,O$ expose the semigroup source, functional-analysis boundary, and operator-window observations before any approximation claim is read.

The numeric side is read through $R$ and $Q$. The regular rational windows provide the inspected tolerances and graph or resolvent budgets, and the Real seal records the scalar error bounds available to the consumer.

The approximation-specific rows are $G$ and $W$. The generator-window ledger keeps finite source-domain and graph comparisons visible, while the resolvent-sequence window fixes the order in which resolvent probes may be handed to downstream consumers.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier contains no row for a host graph-limit theorem, selected core, quotient operator equality, global PDE well-posedness, or ambient completeness, these displayed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
