\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RationalBallCoverUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-rational-ball-cover-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on rational covers and constructive real neighborhoods, the $\RationalBallCoverUp$ packet records finite rational-ball cover evidence for Real and Completion consumers. It sits after the request route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rational-ball-filter-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names finite cover evidence through displayed rows only; it does not select a subcover by choice, quotient real names, invoke trichotomy, assert compactness, or import ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Choice-free RationalBallCover source]
\label{def:choice-free-rational-ball-cover-source}
A $\RationalBallCoverUp$ source packet is a finite $\BHist$ record
$$
U=(F,L,D,W,R,M,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\RationalBallFilterUp$ request row that supplies the rational center and ball-request surface.
\item $L$ is the finite cover ledger, listing the displayed rational-ball entries and their finite indices.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius-refinement and tolerance ledger attached to the cover entries.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row opened by the cover request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row that reads the stream windows as regular rational approximation data.
\item $M$ is the cover-modulus handoff row, recording the finite modulus by which a consumer reads enough cover entries for the requested window.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after the filter request, cover ledger, dyadic refinement, stream window, regular readback, and modulus rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the filter request, finite cover ledger, dyadic ledger, stream windows, readback, modulus, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the rational-ball filter request through $F,L,D,W,R,M,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RationalBallCoverUp$, $\RationalBallFilterUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RationalBallCoverUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The five NameCert rows are carrier admission, finite cover ledger, dyadic refinement stability, cover-modulus handoff, and Real-seal nonescape. The carrier has no coordinate for countable choice, selected subcovers, open-cover compactness, quotient real equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free RationalBallCover finite subcover]
\label{thm:choice-free-rational-ball-cover-finite-subcover}
Every accepted $\RationalBallCoverUp$ packet $U=(F,L,D,W,R,M,E,H,C,P,N)$ exposes a finite subcover surface by reading the $\RationalBallFilterUp$ request row $F$, the finite ledger $L$, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ refinement row $D$, the $\StreamNameUp$ window $W$, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$ before any terminal $\RealUp$ seal is consumed. The subcover is the displayed finite ledger in $L$ transported through $H,C,P,N$; it is not selected by countable choice, compactness, quotient equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the source packet of \autoref{def:choice-free-rational-ball-cover-source}. Carrier admission exposes only $F,L,D,W,R,M,E,H,C,P,N$.

The finite cover ledger $L$ is downstream of the $\RationalBallFilterUp$ request $F$. Thus a cover-facing consumer first reads the rational-ball request route and then the finite list of displayed rational-ball entries.

Dyadic refinement and window coverage are supplied by $D,W,R$. The cover entries are read through the same dyadic tolerance, stream-window, and regular-rational readback surface used by the Real and Completion route.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite ledger. Since the carrier has no coordinate for choice, compactness, quotient equality, trichotomy, selected subcovers, or ambient completeness, the exported subcover surface is exactly the displayed finite ledger.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Choice-free RationalBallCover modulus]
\label{thm:choice-free-rational-ball-cover-modulus}
Every $\RealUp$ or Completion consumer of an accepted $\RationalBallCoverUp$ packet reads the cover-modulus handoff through
$$
\RationalBallFilterUp,\;\DyadicRatCoreUp,\;\StreamNameUp,\;\RegSeqRatUp
\longmapsto
\RealUp .
$$
The modulus row $M$ may be consumed only after the finite cover ledger $L$, dyadic refinement row $D$, stream window $W$, and regular rational readback $R$ have been displayed, and before the terminal seal $E$ is read. The handoff exports no countable-choice schedule, compactness theorem, selected limit, quotient real equality, trichotomy branch, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted $\RealUp$ or Completion consumer of $U$. The consumer receives the rational-ball request from $F$ and the finite cover entries from $L$.

Next consume $D,W,R$. The dyadic ledger controls radius refinement, the stream window fixes the finite observation surface, and the regular rational readback interprets that window as approximation data.

Only then can the modulus row $M$ be read. It records how much of the displayed finite cover ledger is needed for the requested window, without adding a selector or choice schedule.

The terminal seal $E$ is downstream of $F,L,D,W,R,M$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming remain confined to $H,C,P,N$, so the Real and Completion handoff reaches only the displayed finite-cover route and none of the excluded compactness, quotient, trichotomy, choice, selected-limit, or completeness principles.
\end{proof}
