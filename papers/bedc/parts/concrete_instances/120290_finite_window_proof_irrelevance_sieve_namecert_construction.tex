\begin{definition}[FiniteWindowProofIrrelevanceSieve carrier]
\label{def:finite-window-proof-irrelevance-sieve-carrier}
A $\FiniteWindowProofIrrelevanceSieveUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row, recording the observable prefixes used by the consumer.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare the finite reads.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ or Completion-facing seal row, available only after $W,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $Q$ is the proof-token ledger retained inside the packet as evidence for how the finite windows were justified; $Q$ is not a coordinate of the public classifier.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the window, readback, dyadic, seal, proof-token, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite read request through $W,R,D,E,Q,H$.

The public classifier compares accepted packets only through $W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. The row $Q$ may be inspected by the local proof ledger, but it cannot create a public distinction after the same finite windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance data, and seal row have been displayed.

\begin{theorem}[FiniteWindowProofIrrelevanceSieve NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-window-proof-irrelevance-sieve-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteWindowProofIrrelevanceSieveUp$ carrier
$$
S=(W,R,D,E,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteWindowProofIrrelevanceSieveUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the finite window, regular readback, dyadic tolerance, Real or Completion seal, proof-token ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; public-readback preservation requiring every public consumer to expose the same $W,R,D,E$ rows before classification; proof-token erasure requiring the classifier to ignore identity changes in $Q$ once $W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$ are fixed; ledger nonescape requiring $Q$ to remain a retained evidence row rather than a public classifier coordinate; componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all displayed rows; displayed $\Cont$ replay through the same finite route; $\Pkg$ provenance over the listed sibling carriers; and the local naming obligation for $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:finite-window-proof-irrelevance-sieve-carrier}. The packet displays the finite observation row $W$, the regular rational readback $R$, the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, the terminal seal row $E$, and the retained proof-token ledger $Q$ before transport, replay, provenance, and naming are inspected.

Public-readback preservation is read componentwise. A public consumer sees $W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$ in the same order as the carrier, so two accepted packets with the same visible rows offer the same StreamName, RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, and Real-facing data.

Proof-token erasure and ledger nonescape are the same finite sieve boundary. The row $Q$ remains present as evidence for local reconstruction, but it is not an argument of the public classifier. Replacing $Q$ while preserving the visible rows therefore cannot change the public readback class.

Transport and replay are handled row by row. The $\hsame$ row $H$ transports each displayed component without turning $Q$ into a public coordinate, and the $\Cont$ row $C$ replays finite read requests through the same window, readback, dyadic, seal, and retained-ledger route.

Finally, $P$ and $N$ source and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no extra public coordinate beyond $W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$, the listed clauses exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
