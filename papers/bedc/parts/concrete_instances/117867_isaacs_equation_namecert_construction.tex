\label{thm:isaacs-equation-namecert-obligations}

No local obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Read the dynamic-programming and viscosity boundaries through the named siblings. The dynamic-programming row supplies the finite min-max handoff, while the viscosity-solution, PDE, and ODE rows keep the Hamilton-Jacobi-Isaacs expression attached to displayed evolution data.
