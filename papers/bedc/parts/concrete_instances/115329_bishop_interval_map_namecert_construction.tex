Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive functions over located intervals, and Scott's interval-domain reading of approximation, the $\BishopIntervalMapUp$ packet records the finite map surface used when interval-domain approximants are handed to Real-facing continuous-map consumers. It sits after the interval-domain carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-intervaldomain-namecert}, the continuous-map graph and modulus surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the finite stream windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names a displayed interval-domain map certificate only; it does not define quotient functions, identify host extensional equality, select completed representatives, or assert ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[BishopIntervalMap carrier]
\label{def:bishop-interval-map-carrier}
A $\BishopIntervalMapUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(D,F,W,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\IntervalDomainUp$ approximation-domain row, exposing located cells, rational refinements, directed nesting, endpoint windows, regular readbacks, and the downstream $\RealUp$ seal only in that order.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ graph-and-modulus row whose source reads from the displayed interval-domain approximants and whose target is a Real-facing naming surface.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window family through which finite endpoint and image-approximation requests are inspected.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback family attached to the same windows, carrying the regular rational observations consumed before the Real seal.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ handoff row reached only after $D,F,W,Q$ and the structural rows are displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval-domain, continuous-map, stream-window, regular-readback, Real-handoff, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay routes for interval-domain reads, continuous-map reads, stream requests, regular rational readbacks, and Real-facing handoffs.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopIntervalMapUp$, $\IntervalDomainUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopIntervalMapUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only this finite map packet. It has no coordinate for quotient functions, host function extensionality, selected Real representatives, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BishopIntervalMap NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-interval-map-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopIntervalMapUp$ carrier $M=(D,F,W,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopIntervalMapUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $D,F,W,Q,E,H,C,P,N$; source-domain visibility through the $\IntervalDomainUp$ packet; graph and modulus visibility through the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row; finite endpoint and image-window replay through $W$ and $Q$; Real-handoff non-escape through $E$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:bishop-interval-map-carrier}. The displayed inventory is exactly the interval-domain row, continuous-map row, stream windows, regular rational readbacks, Real handoff, transports, replay routes, provenance, and local naming row.

Read the source through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-intervaldomain-namecert}. The interval-domain packet already orders located cells, rational refinements, directed nesting, stream windows, regular readbacks, and the Real seal, so the map certificate cannot consume a Real-facing approximation before the source approximant rows are visible.

Read the graph through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}. The continuous-map row supplies only displayed graph and modulus data tied to the same finite windows, and $H,C$ preserve those rows under classifier transport and replay.

Finally inspect the handoff row $E$. Since the packet has no coordinate for quotient functions, host extensional equality, selected representatives, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness, the local certificate can expose only the finite map route named by $D,F,W,Q,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[BishopIntervalMap Real handoff]
\label{thm:bishop-interval-map-real-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$-facing consumer of an accepted $\BishopIntervalMapUp$ packet first reads the finite route
$$
\IntervalDomainUp\longmapsto\ContinuousMapUp\longmapsto\StreamNameUp\longmapsto\RegSeqRatUp\longmapsto\RealUp\longmapsto(H,C,P,N).
$$
The handoff therefore supplies an interval-domain map surface for Real and Completion consumers, not a quotient function, not host extensional equality, not a selected completed value, and not ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the source approximation. The row $D$ is the displayed interval-domain packet, so any consumer must first read its located cells, rational refinements, directed nesting, endpoint windows, regular readbacks, and downstream Real seal discipline.

Next read the map row. The row $F$ is a continuous-map certificate attached to those source approximants, while $W$ and $Q$ provide the finite request windows and regular rational readbacks used to inspect both endpoint and image data.

The Real-facing row $E$ is downstream of $D,F,W,Q$. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route, so the consumer receives only the displayed finite packet and not a host-level function object.

Coordinate exhaustion then gives the exclusions. Quotient functions, host extensional equality, selected completed values, and ambient Real completeness would each require a row absent from \autoref{def:bishop-interval-map-carrier}.
\end{proof}
