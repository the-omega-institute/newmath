Following Bourbaki and standard set-theoretic algebra, the $\EquivalenceRelationUp$ packet records the reusable finite classifier-law surface for reflexive, symmetric, and transitive binary rows. It sits beside the membership discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-setlike-namecert}, the order-law contrast of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-preorder-namecert}, the regular Cauchy comparison packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchysequenceequivalence-namecert}, the equality-facing setoid boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-equivalence-setoid-namecert}, and the rational source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}. The packet gives L10 comparison chapters one named BEDC object for classifier laws; it is not a quotient carrier, a representative selector, or a host equality principle.

\begin{definition}[Equivalence relation carrier]
\label{def:equivalence-relation-carrier}
An $\EquivalenceRelationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(S,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the carried source data on which the classifier row is displayed; it may be a set-like row, a rational-history row, or an L10 comparison source, but it is read only through its own NameCert surface;
\item $R$ is the displayed binary classifier row on accepted source observations;
\item $L$ stores the reflexive, symmetric, and transitive law witnesses for $R$ on the accepted displayed rows;
\item $H$ transports the source, classifier, law, replay, provenance, and local name rows componentwise by $\hsame$;
\item $C$ replays the same finite classifier-law packet by displayed $\Cont$ routes;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EquivalenceRelationUp$, the source packet named by $S$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EquivalenceRelationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotienting $S$, selecting canonical representatives, choosing class witnesses, or importing host equality of source objects.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Equivalence relation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:equivalence-relation-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\EquivalenceRelationUp$ carrier $E=(S,R,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\EquivalenceRelationUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the source, binary classifier, law witnesses, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item law-row exactness, requiring $L$ to expose precisely reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity for $R$ on accepted displayed rows;
\item classifier transport, requiring $H$ and $C$ to move the same source, classifier, and law rows without changing their public read;
\item non-quotient boundary, excluding quotient carriers, representative selectors, host equality, and selected equivalence classes;
\item downstream classifier handoff, allowing comparison packets such as regular Cauchy and rational-history readers to cite $S,R,L,H,C,P,N$ as one finite classifier-law substrate.
\end{enumerate}
No equivalence-relation NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic data are exactly the source row $S$, the displayed binary classifier $R$, and the law row $L$.

The law row is read without leaving the carrier. Its three fields state reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity only for displayed accepted rows, so the public classifier law is not a host relation on an ambient set.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and locally name the same classifier-law packet. Since no quotient, selector, class representative, or host equality row occurs in $E$, the listed five obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Equivalence relation classifier laws]
\label{thm:equivalence-relation-classifier-laws}
Every public classifier read of an accepted $\EquivalenceRelationUp$ packet exposes exactly the reflexive, symmetric, and transitive laws stored in $L$ for the displayed classifier row $R$ on accepted source rows from $S$. The exported read may be handed to downstream comparison packets through $H,C,P,N$, but it exports no quotient carrier, representative selector, selected equivalence class, host equality of source objects, or ambient extensionality principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a public classifier read. Carrier admission forces the read to pass through $S$ and then through the displayed binary classifier row $R$.

Next read the law row $L$. The only laws available there are reflexivity on each accepted displayed row, symmetry for accepted displayed pairs, and transitivity for accepted displayed triples.

Finally transport the read through $H$ and replay it through $C$, with provenance and local naming supplied by $P$ and $N$. These rows preserve the displayed classifier-law packet but do not add a quotient carrier or a representative-selection coordinate.
\end{proof}
