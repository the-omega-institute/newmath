\begin{definition}[Located limit-operator carrier]
\label{def:located-limit-operator-carrier}
A $\LocatedLimitOperatorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(I,J,D,W,Q,R,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the source $\NestedIntervalUp$ row, exposing the retained nested interval evidence.
\item $J$ is the $\LocatedRealNestedIntervalsUp$ handoff row, recording the located containment and diameter route used by the source.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for endpoint widths, requested precisions, and rational enclosure checks.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row selected by each precision request.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row extracted from the displayed finite windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row that may be consumed only after $I,J,D,W,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $A$ is the operator-admission row, recording that the input route $I,J,D,W,Q$ has been assembled before the Real-facing handoff $R$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $I,J,D,W,Q,R,A,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a precision request through the nested interval, window, readback, and seal rows.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedLimitOperatorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,J,D,W,Q,R,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host completeness, quotient representatives, exact trichotomy, countable choice, or a selected limit outside the displayed rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located limit-operator NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-limit-operator-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedLimitOperatorUp$ carrier $L=(I,J,D,W,Q,R,A,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedLimitOperatorUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the nested interval row $I$, located nested-interval handoff $J$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, finite window row $W$, regular rational readback $Q$, terminal Real seal $R$, operator-admission row $A$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and local naming $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-limit-operator-carrier}. The rows $I,J,D,W,Q$ expose the input evidence from retained nested intervals to regular rational readback, and $R$ is the only Real-facing row.

The operator-admission row $A$ records that the displayed input route has already been assembled before $R$ is consumed. Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the same rows and cannot add a hidden selected point or quotient representative.

Finally, provenance and naming through $P,N$ bind the packet to the sibling surfaces cited above. Since no excluded coordinate is present in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
