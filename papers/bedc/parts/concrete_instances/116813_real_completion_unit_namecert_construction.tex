\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\RealCompletionUnitUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-real-completion-unit-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive real completion, the packet records the unit map from dyadic and rational data into the $\RealUp$ seal as finite BEDC data. It sits over the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completion-namecert}. The packet names the unit-map surface available to Real and Completion consumers without quotienting Cauchy names, selecting representatives, importing countable choice, or asserting ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Real completion unit carrier]
\label{def:real-completion-unit-carrier}
A $\RealCompletionUnitUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the dyadic constant row, read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, that supplies the rational or dyadic source data for the unit map.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row listing the finite observations by which the source data are presented to a regular-name consumer.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row generated from $D$ and $W$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the regular readback has been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the dyadic row, stream-window row, regular readback, and Real seal.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite unit-map route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealCompletionUnitUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionUnitUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient completion, selected Cauchy representatives, host real equality, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion unit NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-completion-unit-obligations}
For an accepted $\RealCompletionUnitUp$ carrier $U=(D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionUnitUp}$ surface consists of five obligation rows: dyadic source admission through $D$, finite stream-window admission through $W$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal Real sealing through $E$, and structural certification through $H,C,P,N$. Every Real or Completion consumer of the unit map must read those rows on the same finite BEDC surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-completion-unit-carrier}. The dyadic source row $D$, stream-window row $W$, regular readback row $R$, and terminal seal row $E$ are displayed coordinates, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly that route.

The first four obligations are therefore the analytic face of the unit map: source admission, window admission, readback, and sealing. The fifth obligation is the structural face binding those reads into one local NameCert surface.

No excluded shortcut is a coordinate of the carrier. Hence a consumer receives the unit map only through the displayed rows and cannot replace the route by quotient completion, selected representatives, host equality, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion unit nonescape]
\label{thm:real-completion-unit-nonescape}
Any $\RealUp$ endpoint consumer of an accepted $\RealCompletionUnitUp$ carrier $U=(D,W,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ that drops one of $D$, $W$, $R$, $E$, $H$, $C$, $P$, or $N$ leaves the accepted unit-map surface. In particular, after dropping any one of those rows, the consumer cannot read the $\RealUp$ endpoint, cannot replace the finite route with quotient equality of completed names, cannot choose a representative Cauchy name, cannot import external real completeness, and cannot appeal to countable choice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier projection of \autoref{def:real-completion-unit-carrier}. The endpoint row $E$ is downstream of the dyadic source row $D$, the stream-window row $W$, and the regular readback row $R$.

Apply the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:real-completion-unit-obligations}. It binds the analytic rows $D,W,R,E$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ into one local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionUnitUp}$ surface, so a consumer that drops any row no longer has the displayed certificate route.

The excluded replacements are not rows of $U$. Thus dropping a coordinate cannot be repaired by quotient equality, a selected representative, external completeness, or countable choice; it simply removes the consumer from the accepted finite unit-map packet.
\end{proof}
