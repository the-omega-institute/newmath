\begin{theorem}[Completely regular space compact Hausdorff Urysohn route]
\label{thm:completely-regular-space-compact-hausdorff-urysohn-route}
Every $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ read that exports the local classifier to a compact or Hausdorff consumer first consumes the $\TopologyUp$ carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the $\HausdorffSpaceUp$ separation boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hausdorffspace-namecert}, the compact metric sibling route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, and the Urysohn separation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-urysohn-lemma-namecert}. The exported classifier is therefore the finite row route $T,R,C,F,H,K,P,N$ of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}, not a host compactness theorem, a selected separating function, an unrestricted powerset, or an ambient normal-space principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the local carrier and NameCert obligations of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier} and \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-namecert-obligations}. They expose the topology row, the regular-space row, the point-versus-closed-set request, the real-valued continuous-map separator, and the structural rows before any classifier is public.

Apply the Urysohn sibling route of \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-urysohn-sibling-route}. It forces the separator to pass through the displayed Urysohn separation row rather than through a chosen host function family.

Now read the compact and Hausdorff siblings. The Hausdorff row supplies the separation boundary, and the compact metric row supplies only a displayed compact metric dependency; neither row adds a powerset, a selected separator, or an ambient compactness theorem.

The structural rows $H,K,P,N$ preserve the same route for downstream compact or Hausdorff consumers. Hence every exported classifier remains tied to the Urysohn and compact-Hausdorff sibling lattice before it is read.
\end{proof}
