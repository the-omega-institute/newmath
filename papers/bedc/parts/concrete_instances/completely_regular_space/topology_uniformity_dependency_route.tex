\begin{theorem}[Completely regular space topology--uniformity route]
\label{thm:completely-regular-space-topology-uniformity-route}
Every $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ packet that feeds a compact-open or uniformity consumer first exposes the $\TopologyUp$ source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the $\UrysohnLemmaUp$-style separation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-urysohn-lemma-namecert}, and the finite separator rows of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}. A $\CompactOpenTopologyUp$, $\CompactOpenUniformConvergenceUp$, or $\UniformConvergenceSpaceUp$ consumer therefore receives the row sequence $T,R,C,F,H,K,P,N$ through local naming, not a host family of real-valued maps or an ambient uniformity oracle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}. The topology row $T$ supplies the open-set vocabulary, while $R,C,F$ record the regularity request and the displayed real-valued separator.

Apply the topology--Urysohn sibling lattice of \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-topology-urysohn-sibling-lattice}. It places the separator after the topology source and Urysohn-style separation route before any compact-open read.

The same finite row sequence is then available to compact-open uniformity consumers. Since $H,K,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the displayed separator packet, no downstream consumer obtains an unrestricted function space, a selected family of separators, or a detached uniformity surface.
\end{proof}
