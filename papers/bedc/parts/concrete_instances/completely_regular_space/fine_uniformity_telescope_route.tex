\begin{theorem}[Completely regular space fine-uniformity telescope route]
\label{thm:completely-regular-space-fine-uniformity-telescope-route}
Every $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ carrier consumed by a $\FineUniformityUp$ reader passes through the topology-uniformity route already exposed in \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-topology-uniformity-route} and the fine-uniformity carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fine-uniformity-namecert}. The telescope read keeps the topology source, real-valued separator, continuous-map probe, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows visible before an entourage-base row is public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}. The topology row, request row, real-valued continuous separator, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows are the only visible source for a uniformity-facing consumer.

Apply the topology-uniformity dependency route. It keeps the separator attached to the same topology and continuous-map rows before any uniformity consumer can read the exported surface.

Finally read the fine-uniformity carrier. Its entourage-base row is generated from the displayed complete-regularity and real-valued probe data, so the telescope route exports no ambient family of all probes, selected entourage filter, host powerset uniformity, metrization theorem, or completion object.
\end{proof}
