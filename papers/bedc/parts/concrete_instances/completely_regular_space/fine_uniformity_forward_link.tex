\begin{theorem}[Completely regular space fine-uniformity forward link]
\label{thm:completely-regular-space-fine-uniformity-forward-link}
Every $\FineUniformityUp$ consumer of a $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ packet must first read the displayed real-valued separation row together with the shared $\TopologyUp$ and $\ContinuousMapUp$ rows before exporting an entourage base. The forward link is the finite route
$$
\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp,\TopologyUp,\ContinuousMapUp,\RealUp
\longmapsto
\FineUniformityUp ,
$$
with the separator, topology source, real-valued probe, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows all visible before the fine-uniformity base is public.

Consequently the fine-uniformity consumer receives only the displayed separator route. It does not receive a host family of all real-valued maps, a selected entourage filter, an ambient powerset uniformity, a metrization theorem, a completion object, or a choice principle for probes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ carrier rows of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}. They expose the topology source, the regularity request, the displayed $\RealUp$-valued $\ContinuousMapUp$ separator, and the structural rows used by downstream consumers.

Now read the $\FineUniformityUp$ carrier of \autoref{def:fine-uniformity-carrier}. Its complete-regularity coordinate is consumed before the real-valued probe row generates a finite entourage-base ledger, so the fine-uniformity base cannot be exported before the separator route is visible.

The same $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows transport and replay the displayed separator into the fine-uniformity packet. Since no carrier field ranges over all real-valued maps or selected filters, the forward link exports only the finite entourage base generated by the displayed probes.
\end{proof}
