\begin{theorem}[Completely regular space topology-uniformity consumer lattice]
\label{thm:completely-regular-space-topology-uniformity-consumer-lattice}
Every $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ real-valued separator read reaches compact-open, Kelleyfication, Urysohn, and uniformity consumers only after the displayed topology source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the Urysohn-style separator route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-urysohn-lemma-namecert}, the compact-open route of \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-compact-open-forward-route}, and the uniformity-forward route of \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-uniformity-forward-route} have all remained visible. Thus downstream consumers read the finite row sequence $T,R,C,F,H,K,P,N$ of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}; they do not receive a host family of real-valued functions, selected complements, normality, metrization, or ambient compactness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the topology--Urysohn sibling lattice, \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-topology-urysohn-sibling-lattice}. It places the separator after the displayed topology carrier and the Urysohn-style route before compact-open or Kelleyfication consumers may inspect it.

Apply the compact-open forward route and Kelleyfication forward route, \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-compact-open-forward-route} and \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-kelleyfication-forward-route}. These keep compact-open and Kelleyfication reads downstream of the same topology, regular-space, Real, continuous-map, and Urysohn rows.

Finally use the uniformity-forward route of \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-uniformity-forward-route}. Uniformity consumers receive only the displayed separator chain $T,R,C,F,H,K,P,N$, so the sibling lattice exports a concrete dependency edge rather than a stronger host topological theorem.
\end{proof}
