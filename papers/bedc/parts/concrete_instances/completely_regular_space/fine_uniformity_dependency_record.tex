\begin{theorem}[Completely regular space fine-uniformity dependency record]
\label{thm:completely-regular-space-fine-uniformity-dependency-record}
Every accepted $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ packet consumed as a $\FineUniformityUp$ source must expose the topology row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the Urysohn-style separation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-urysohn-lemma-namecert}, the lattice-facing regular-open rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lattice-namecert}, and the fine-uniformity carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fine-uniformity-namecert} before any entourage-base consumer reads the exported uniformity row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}. Its separator row is attached to the same topology source, regular-space source, real-valued continuous-map row, transport row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row used by the sibling dependency routes.

Apply the forward link of \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-fine-uniformity-forward-link}. It sends the displayed separator through the fine-uniformity carrier only after the topology, Urysohn-style separation, and lattice-facing regular-open dependencies have been made public.

Thus the uniformity consumer receives a finite dependency record over the displayed rows. It does not receive an ambient family of all real-valued maps, a selected entourage filter, a host powerset lattice, or a metrization theorem.
\end{proof}
