\begin{theorem}[Completely regular space regular-open sibling route]
\label{thm:completely-regular-space-regular-open-sibling-route}
Every accepted $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ separator route consumed by $\RegularOpenSetUp$, $\CozeroSetUp$, or $\ZeroSetUp$ factors through the displayed topology row, the real-valued separator row, and the continuous-map row of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}. The sibling consumer may read the open or zero-locus boundary only through those rows; it receives no host powerset, selected complement, detached open-set lattice, or classical complement operation from the completely regular packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier $Q=(T,R,C,F,H,K,P,N)$ of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}. The topology row $T$ fixes the open and closed vocabulary, while the request row $C$ and separator row $F$ keep the point-versus-closed-set separation attached to a displayed $\RealUp$-valued $\ContinuousMapUp$ route.

Read the regular-open sibling through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-open-set-namecert}. It can consume only the topology-facing open and closure-interior information already exposed by $T$ and the separator route; it does not obtain a host powerset or a detached Boolean algebra of opens.

Read the cozero and zero-set siblings through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cozero-set-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-zero-set-namecert}. Their positive-apartness and zero-locus routes must use the displayed real-valued continuous-map row $F$, transported by $H$ and replayed by $K$, before provenance and local naming are inspected.

Thus the sibling route is exactly the finite topology, real-valued separator, and continuous-map path carried by $T,C,F,H,K,P,N$. Since no carrier coordinate supplies selected complements, host powersets, or a classical complement operation, none is exported to the regular-open, cozero, or zero-set consumers.
\end{proof}
