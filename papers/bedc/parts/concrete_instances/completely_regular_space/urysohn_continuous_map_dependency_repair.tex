\begin{theorem}[Completely regular Urysohn and continuous-map route]
\label{thm:completely-regular-space-urysohn-continuous-map-route}
Every $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ separator row consumed by an Urysohn-facing or continuous-map-facing chapter must pass through the separator route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-urysohn-lemma-namecert} and the map graph route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuous-namecert}. The consumer reads the topology source, point-versus-closed-set request, real-valued separator, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows of the completely regular packet; it receives no host powerset, selected function space, ambient family of all real-valued maps, or untracked separator.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:completely-regular-space-carrier}. The separator row $F$ is already a $\RealUp$-valued continuous-map row, and the request rows $T,R,C$ bind it to the displayed topology and closed-set data.

Apply the Urysohn consumer route of \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-urysohn-consumer-route}. It forces any Urysohn-style read to consume the topology source, real-valued separation window, uniformity-facing route, provenance, and local naming before a function-separation packet is public.

Finally read the continuous-map route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuous-namecert}. Since the carrier has no coordinate for host powersets, selected function spaces, or ambient families of real-valued maps, the separator remains the displayed finite route through $T,R,C,F,H,K,P,N$.
\end{proof}
