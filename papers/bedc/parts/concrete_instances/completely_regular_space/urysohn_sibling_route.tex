\label{thm:completely-regular-space-urysohn-sibling-route}

\end{theorem}

These expose the displayed topology carrier, the point-versus-closed-set regularity row, the $\RealUp$-valued $\ContinuousMapUp$ separator, and the Urysohn-facing route before the completely regular row is available to downstream consumers.

Any compactification or uniformity reader therefore receives only the finite separation certificate already tied to $\TopologyUp$ and $\UrysohnLemmaUp$. The route has no coordinate for a hidden compactification, a uniformity generated by an ambient family, host function equality, selected complements, or choice.
\end{proof}
