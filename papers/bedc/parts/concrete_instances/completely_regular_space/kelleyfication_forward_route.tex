\begin{theorem}[Completely regular space Kelleyfication forward route]
\label{thm:completely-regular-space-kelleyfication-forward-route}
Every $\KelleyficationUp$ reader of a $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ separator must first pass through the displayed $\TopologyUp$ source, the $\RegularTopologicalSpaceUp$ point-versus-closed-set boundary, the $\RealUp$-valued $\ContinuousMapUp$ separator, and the Urysohn-facing route before the compact-open ledger consumes the separator. The Kelleyfication window therefore reads the same compact-open consumer path named in \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-compact-open-forward-route}; it receives no host separator family, quotient-open equality, selected real-valued map, or external compactness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compact-open forward route \autoref{thm:completely-regular-space-compact-open-forward-route}. It already places every compact-open or Kelleyfication consumer after the topology source, regular topological source, real-valued continuous-map separator, and Urysohn-facing separation route.

The Kelleyfication reader can only consume that separator after the compact-open window has displayed the finite graph and target-open data. Since the hub packet supplies no additional carrier field, the Kelleyfication handoff preserves the same topology, regularity, Real, ContinuousMap, and Urysohn rows.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming remain attached to the original $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ packet. Thus the forward route reaches $\KelleyficationUp$ only through the compact-open consumer path and cannot export a selected separator family, quotient-open equality, or ambient compactness principle.
\end{proof}
