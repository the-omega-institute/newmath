\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchyUniformModulus dependency route]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-uniform-modulus-cauchy-uniformmodulus-dependency-route}
Every accepted $\RegularCauchyUniformModulusUp$ read consumes the substantive dependency route from the $\CauchyUp$ tail-window and modulus rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-namecert}, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal $\RealUp$ seal data of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the tolerance-only ledger rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}. The packet therefore belongs to the L10 Cauchy and uniform-modulus lattice, not to an isolated orphan carrier surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the Cauchy side first. The tail-window and modulus rows fix the finite observation and tolerance route before any regular rational or Real-facing consumer can inspect the packet.

The regular-rational and Real rows then provide readback and sealing. The uniform-modulus side contributes only the tolerance ledger needed by the same route, so no independent host modulus, selected limit, quotient real equality, or ambient completeness object enters the dependency.
\end{proof}
