Following Bishop and Bridges on finite-cover proofs of Heine--Cantor, the $\FiniteCoverMeshChainUp$ packet records the mesh-chain route from compact finite-cover data to a tolerance-only uniform-modulus handoff. It sits over the compact source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, continuous-map rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, rational lower-bound reads from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, and the downstream $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Finite cover mesh-chain carrier]
\label{def:finite-cover-mesh-chain-carrier}
A $\FiniteCoverMeshChainUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(K,M,G,B,D,S,L,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the consumed $\CompactUp$ finite-cover source row, exposed as finite $\ProbeBundleUp$ cover data.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row used for mesh-radius comparisons and triangle transport.
\item $G$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ pointwise-modulus row over the displayed compact source.
\item $B$ is the dyadic mesh-bound row, recording the finite mesh scale and cover-radius comparisons.
\item $D$ is the finite cover-chain ledger connecting each mesh cell to a displayed cover member.
\item $S$ is the star-refinement transport row, carrying local pointwise moduli along neighbouring mesh cells.
\item $L$ is the $\RatUp$ rational lower-bound fold over the finitely many displayed radii.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row consumed only after $B,D,S,L$ have been read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,M,G,B,D,S,L,U$ and the local certificate row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact-cover reads through mesh bounds, refinement transport, rational folding, and uniform-modulus handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactUp$, $\ProbeBundleUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RatUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverMeshChainUp}$ registration row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these finite rows. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, a host Lebesgue number, semantic continuity, quotient metric equality, selected centres outside the displayed cover, or an external modulus oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite cover mesh-chain NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cover-mesh-chain-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteCoverMeshChainUp$ carrier $F=(K,M,G,B,D,S,L,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverMeshChainUp}$ obligations are exactly compact cover exposure, metric source exposure, continuous-map row admission, dyadic mesh-bound admission, finite cover-chain routing, star-refinement transport, rational lower-bound folding, UniformModulus handoff, componentwise stability transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. The obligations do not include open-cover compactness, a host Lebesgue number, semantic continuity, quotient metric equality, hidden centre selection, or an external modulus oracle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-cover-mesh-chain-carrier}. Its compact-uniform data are the finite cover row $K$, metric source $M$, continuous-map row $G$, mesh bound $B$, cover chain $D$, star-refinement row $S$, rational fold $L$, and uniform-modulus handoff $U$.

The finite-cover route is ordered. Mesh bounds are inspected before cover-chain transport, star refinement moves only along the displayed finite chain, and the rational lower-bound fold reads only the finitely many radii exposed by that chain.

The UniformModulus handoff is downstream of those rows. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same displayed route, so none of the excluded host compactness, semantic-continuity, quotient, hidden-selection, or oracle principles can enter the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite cover mesh-chain UniformModulus handoff]
\label{thm:finite-cover-mesh-chain-uniform-modulus-handoff}
Every consumer of an accepted $\FiniteCoverMeshChainUp$ packet must read the compact finite-cover row $K$, metric row $M$, continuous-map row $G$, dyadic mesh bound $B$, cover-chain ledger $D$, star-refinement transport $S$, rational lower-bound fold $L$, and $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff $U$ before using the exported modulus. Thus the packet realizes the finite-cover mesh route as concrete NameCert data rather than as a host compactness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compact and metric sources. The row $K$ supplies finite cover data, $M$ supplies mesh comparisons, and $G$ supplies the pointwise-modulus rows that will be transported.

The mesh chain is then read through $B,D,S$. These rows expose the dyadic mesh scale, the finite cover member attached to each mesh cell, and the star-refinement transport that keeps local moduli valid along neighbouring cells.

Finally the rational lower-bound fold $L$ produces the tolerance-only value consumed by $U$. The rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name this same finite path, so the exported modulus has no bypass through open-cover compactness, a host Lebesgue number, hidden centre selection, quotient metric equality, or an external oracle.
\end{proof}
