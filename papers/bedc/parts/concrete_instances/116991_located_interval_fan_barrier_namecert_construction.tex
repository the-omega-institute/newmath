\begin{definition}[Located interval fan barrier carrier]
\label{def:located-interval-fan-barrier-carrier}
A $\LocatedIntervalFanBarrierUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(I,K,F,D,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\LocatedIntervalUp$ source row carrying located endpoint comparison and interval-membership data.
\item $K$ is the closed bounded interval fan row that supplies the finite binary-prefix and bar boundary.
\item $F$ is the finite fan compactness row read only as a bounded fan of cover probes, not as an open-cover compactness oracle.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare barrier prefixes and interval endpoints.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row for endpoint names, barrier prefixes, and finite cover probes.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows and dyadic tolerance checks.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $I,K,F,D,S,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for interval, fan, dyadic, stream, rational, real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a located-interval request through the finite fan barrier and then through $D,S,Q,E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedIntervalFanBarrierUp$, $\LocatedIntervalUp$, $\ClosedBoundedIntervalFanUp$, $\FiniteFanCompactnessUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedIntervalFanBarrierUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,K,F,D,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected infinite branches, quotient streams, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located interval fan barrier NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-interval-fan-barrier-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedIntervalFanBarrierUp$ carrier
$$
B=(I,K,F,D,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedIntervalFanBarrierUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed rows, located interval admission through $I$, closed bounded interval fan admission through $K$, finite fan compactness admission through $F$, dyadic tolerance admission through $D$, stream-window admission through $S$, regular rational readback through $Q$, terminal real sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ stability through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-interval-fan-barrier-carrier}. Its public rows are exactly $I,K,F,D,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N$, so every local obligation is attached to one displayed row.

The located interval row is read before the fan boundary rows, and the fan boundary rows are read before dyadic tolerance, finite stream, regular rational, and real-seal rows. Thus a finite-cover consumer cannot skip from an interval request directly to a completed cover theorem.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same packet route. Since no excluded coordinate occurs in the carrier, the NameCert surface cannot call on open-cover compactness, selected branches, quotient streams, countable choice, or ambient real completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located interval fan barrier finite-cover handoff]
\label{thm:located-interval-fan-barrier-finite-cover-handoff}
Every L10 finite-cover consumer of an accepted $\LocatedIntervalFanBarrierUp$ packet factors through the route
$$
\LocatedIntervalUp,\ \ClosedBoundedIntervalFanUp,\ \FiniteFanCompactnessUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp .
$$

The handoff exports only the displayed finite barrier route and exports no open-cover compactness, branch selector, quotient stream equality, countable-choice row, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.

\begin{proof}

Therefore the finite-cover handoff remains inside the displayed packet and cannot export any excluded compactness or choice principle.
