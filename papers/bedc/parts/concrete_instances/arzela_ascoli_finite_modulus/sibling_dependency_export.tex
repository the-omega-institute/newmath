\label{thm:arzela-ascoli-finite-modulus-sibling-dependency}
Every selector-facing read exported from an accepted $\ArzelaAscoliFiniteModulusUp$ packet factors through the displayed horizontal dependency lattice
$$

\EquicontinuityUp,\quad
\CompactUniformContinuityUp,\quad

Since the export is obtained only after these rows are visible, every selector-facing read factors through the stated lattice.
\end{proof}
