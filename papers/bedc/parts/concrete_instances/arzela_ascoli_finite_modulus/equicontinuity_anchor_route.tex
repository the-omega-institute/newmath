\begin{theorem}[Arzela--Ascoli finite-modulus Equicontinuity anchor]
\label{thm:arzela-ascoli-finite-modulus-equicontinuity-anchor}
Every $\ArzelaAscoliFiniteModulusUp$ finite-modulus read first consumes the shared-family modulus and compact-source route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-equicontinuity-namecert} before any finite subfamily, diagonal, compactness-facing, or selector-facing handoff is public. The anchor route exposes the compact source, continuous-family ledger, tolerance row, shared radius row, uniform-modulus handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows supplied by $\EquicontinuityUp$, and only then may the finite-modulus packet read the compact-uniform-continuity, finite-window, regular-rational, dyadic-tolerance, and terminal Real rows of the Arzela--Ascoli lattice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the $\EquicontinuityUp$ carrier first through \autoref{def:equicontinuity-carrier}. Its rows expose the compact metric source, continuous-family ledger, tolerance, shared radius, uniform-modulus handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before any family selector is visible.

Apply \autoref{thm:equicontinuity-compact-metric-route} and \autoref{thm:equicontinuity-uniform-modulus-handoff}. These force compact-source and shared-radius consumption before a compact-uniform or finite-net consumer can use the family.

Now pass to the finite-modulus lattice through \autoref{thm:arzela-ascoli-finite-modulus-equicontinuity-dependency-route} and \autoref{thm:arzela-ascoli-equicontinuity-family-dependency-route}. Those routes identify the downstream Arzela--Ascoli rows as consumers of the equicontinuity family ledger, so finite subfamilies, diagonal data, compactness-facing handoffs, and selector outputs remain downstream of the $\EquicontinuityUp$ anchor.
\end{proof}
