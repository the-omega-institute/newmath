\begin{theorem}[Arzela--Ascoli finite modulus compact-metric sibling readback]
\label{thm:arzela-ascoli-finite-modulus-compactmetric-sibling-readback}
Any finite-modulus selector read through an accepted $\ArzelaAscoliFiniteModulusUp$ packet must first consume the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert} and the $\ContinuousMapUp$ family rows before the selector row can be exported. The route passes through the compact-uniform-continuity finite-modulus handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-continuity-finite-modulus-namecert}; it therefore exposes finite covers, continuity witnesses, and the shared modulus ledger before a downstream consumer may read the $\ArzelaAscoliFiniteModulusUp$ selector.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Read the compact-metric sibling first. The finite-net rows named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert} provide the bounded finite cover surface on which a modulus selector can be evaluated.

Next read the $\ContinuousMapUp$ family rows. These rows keep the maps in the family visible as continuous data rather than as an external collection of functions.

Finally pass through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-uniform-continuity-finite-modulus-namecert}. Its finite-modulus handoff is the point where compactness and continuity are combined, so the $\ArzelaAscoliFiniteModulusUp$ selector row can be exported only after those sibling rows have been consumed.
\end{proof}
