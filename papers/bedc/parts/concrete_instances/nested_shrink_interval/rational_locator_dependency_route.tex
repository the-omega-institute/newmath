\begin{theorem}[Nested shrink interval rational locator route]
\label{thm:nested-shrink-interval-rational-locator-route}
Every accepted $\NestedShrinkIntervalUp$ route whose endpoint localization is read through rational endpoints first consumes the rational locator carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rational-interval-locator-namecert}. Only after that locator row is displayed may the consumer read the nested shrink rows and then the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ handoff rows. The route exports no selected completed point and no Heine--Borel compactness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the accepted packet through the carrier theorem of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-nested-shrink-interval-namecert}. The only endpoint localization rows available to the nested shrink route are finite displayed rows, so a rational-endpoint consumer must obtain its rational interval locator row before the shrink ledger can be interpreted.

The sibling chapter \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rational-interval-locator-namecert} supplies exactly that locator surface. Once it is consumed, the nested shrink packet may proceed through its displayed shrink ledger, finite windows, regular rational readback, and terminal real seal.

No selected completed point or Heine--Borel compactness row is present in either carrier. The dependency route therefore records only the rational locator read before the nested shrink and $\RegSeqRatUp/\RealUp$ handoff rows are read.
\end{proof}
