\begin{definition}[MacNeille completion boundary carrier]
\label{def:macneille-completion-boundary-carrier}
A $\MacneilleCompletionBoundaryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(L,U,O,J,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ is the lower-cut row, recording the finite downward-closed observations and their closure under displayed lower bounds.
\item $U$ is the upper-cut row, recording the finite upward-closed observations and their closure under displayed upper bounds.
\item $O$ is the $\OrderUp$ comparison support row shared by the two cuts.
\item $J$ is the $\LatticeUp$ meet--join support row used to test the visible lower--upper adjunction.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for Real-facing approximants attached to the cut pair.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached from $R$ only after the lower and upper cut rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for lower cuts, upper cuts, order support, lattice support, regular-rational readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from cut comparison through the regular-rational and Real readback route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MacneilleCompletionBoundaryUp$, $\OrderUp$, $\LatticeUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MacneilleCompletionBoundaryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $L,U,O,J,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host complete lattice, quotient completion, choice-selected lower or upper cut, arbitrary supremum, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MacNeille completion boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:macneille-completion-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MacneilleCompletionBoundaryUp$ carrier
$$
M=(L,U,O,J,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MacneilleCompletionBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed lower-cut, upper-cut, order-support, lattice-support, regular-rational readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; lower--upper adjunction testing through $O$ and $J$; Real-facing readback through $R$ and $E$; and nonescape from host complete lattices, quotient completions, arbitrary suprema, selected cuts, and ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The rows $L,U,O,J,R,E,H,C,P,N$ display the cut pair, the order and lattice support that test it, the Real-facing readback route, and the structural rows that transport and name that route.

The lower-cut row $L$ and upper-cut row $U$ are not compared by host inclusion. Their comparison is read through the displayed $\OrderUp$ row $O$ and the meet--join support row $J$.

The Real-facing route then passes through $R$ before it reaches $E$. Thus a consumer sees a regular-rational readback and a terminal Real seal, not an external completion object.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed rows. Since the carrier contains no host complete lattice, quotient completion, selected cut, arbitrary supremum, or ambient completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
