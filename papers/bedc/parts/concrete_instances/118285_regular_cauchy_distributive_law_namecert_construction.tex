\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy distributive-law carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-distributive-law-carrier}

\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$, $B$, and $C$ are three $\RegSeqRatUp$ source rows for regular rational Cauchy real names.
\item $W_A$, $W_B$, and $W_C$ are synchronized $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window rows opened at the same requested precision scale.
\item $S_L$ is the $\RegularCauchySumUp$ row for the source sum $B+C$.
\item $P_L$ is the $\RegularCauchyProductUp$ row for the left route $A\cdot(B+C)$.
\item $P_B$ and $P_C$ are the $\RegularCauchyProductUp$ rows for the two right-route products $A\cdot B$ and $A\cdot C$.
\item $S_R$ is the $\RegularCauchySumUp$ row for the right route $(A\cdot B)+(A\cdot C)$.

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy distributive-window induction]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-distributive-law-window-induction}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the empty window ledger. No source endpoint has been read, so both routes expose only the trivial displayed tolerance row in $E$ and no real-facing equality claim is available.

For the induction step, open the next synchronized windows $W_A,W_B,W_C$. The sum row $S_L$ computes the endpoint for $B+C$, and the product row $P_L$ computes the left-route endpoint from $A$ and that displayed sum.

On the same window, the rows $P_B$ and $P_C$ compute the two product endpoints $A\cdot B$ and $A\cdot C$. The row $S_R$ then sums those two displayed endpoints. The dyadic arithmetic entries in $E$ record the finite distributive identity at this precision and the accumulated error budget for both routes.

The difference row $\Delta$ consumes the two route readbacks and records that their displayed tolerance gap is absorbed by the same ledger. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay preserve the induction state, so the finite calculation remains inside the named BEDC packet at every step.
\end{proof}
