\label{def:frechet-cauchy-filter-carrier}

\item $B$ is the $\FilterBaseUp$ meet row, recording the common-tail refinement required before two tail requests are read as one filter event.
\item $Q$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ handoff row receiving the displayed cofinite-tail filter route.

\label{thm:frechet-cauchy-filter-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:frechet-cauchy-filter-carrier}. The visible rows are precisely $T,B,Q,W,R,E,H,C,P,N$.

The tail side is exhausted by $T$ and $B$. The Frechet row exposes the finite complement and tail request, while the FilterBase row records the meet that makes several tail requests a single displayed filter event.
