\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SequentialCompactIntervalUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-sequential-compact-interval-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges' constructive treatment of compact intervals by finite approximation and subsequence extraction, the $\SequentialCompactIntervalUp$ packet records the sequential compactness surface for a displayed closed real interval. It consumes the finite-net route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-closed-interval-finite-net-namecert}, the terminal real-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}. The packet supplies explicit finite-net and subsequence data for interval sequences; it does not export open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, countable choice, quotient real equality, host subsequence selectors, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Sequential compact interval carrier]
\label{def:sequential-compact-interval-carrier}
A $\SequentialCompactIntervalUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(I,F,Q,W,R,A,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the located closed interval source row, displaying the endpoint names and interval membership boundary consumed by sequence readers.
\item $F$ is the $\LocatedClosedIntervalFiniteNetUp$ finite-net row, listing the mesh scale, finite interval net, and coverage checks used to classify sequence tails.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the sequence observations and endpoint comparisons attached to the same interval boundary.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row opened for the sequence prefixes, endpoint names, and finite-net membership tests already displayed by $I,F,Q$.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for the interval endpoints and the retained subsequence candidate, available only after the finite windows and regular rational readbacks have been displayed.
\item $A$ is the accumulation-cell row, naming a descending finite-net cell chain and a retained infinite-tail witness as finite BEDC data.
\item $D$ is the subsequence-decision ledger, recording the finite choices of cells, tail indices, and comparison checks used to produce the displayed subsequence route.
\item $E$ is the extraction row, exposing the selected finite prefixes and regular rational convergence budgets for the retained subsequence.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval source, finite net, sequence readback, stream windows, real seal, accumulation-cell row, subsequence ledger, extraction row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a sequence request through the interval source, finite-net row, finite observation windows, regular rational readback, real seal, accumulation-cell ledger, and extraction row.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SequentialCompactIntervalUp$, $\LocatedClosedIntervalFiniteNetUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialCompactIntervalUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $I,F,Q,W,R,A,D,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, unrestricted Heine--Borel compactness, countable choice, host real equality, quotient interval representatives, an external subsequence selector, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential compact interval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sequential-compact-interval-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SequentialCompactIntervalUp$ carrier
$$
S=(I,F,Q,W,R,A,D,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialCompactIntervalUp}$ surface consists exactly of located interval admission, finite-net exposure through $\LocatedClosedIntervalFiniteNetUp$, sequence readback through $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\StreamNameUp$, terminal $\RealUp$ seal admission, accumulation-cell formation, subsequence-decision recording, extraction-row exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. The $\CompactUp$ dependency in $P$ is readable only as a finite-net compatibility row and not as open-cover compactness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The interval source is $I$, and the only compactness-facing row is the displayed finite-net row $F$; therefore every compactness read starts with finite interval coverage rather than with an open cover.

The sequence data are finite at each stage. The rows $W$ and $Q$ expose only the inspected stream windows and regular rational readbacks, while $R$ seals those observations as $\RealUp$ data after the finite windows have been displayed.

The extraction rows are also internal to the packet. The accumulation row $A$ records a finite chain of net cells with retained tail witnesses, $D$ records the finite subsequence-decision ledger, and $E$ exposes the resulting subsequence prefixes and convergence budgets.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no carrier coordinate stores an arbitrary open cover, selected subcover, countable-choice functional, quotient representative, host subsequence selector, or ambient completeness principle, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential compact interval finite-net subsequence extraction]
\label{thm:sequential-compact-interval-finite-net-subsequence-extraction}
Every accepted $\SequentialCompactIntervalUp$ packet supplies subsequence data only through the finite route
$$
\LocatedClosedIntervalFiniteNetUp \longrightarrow \StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \RealUp .
$$
Concretely, the packet first reads the finite interval net $F$, then opens the finite sequence windows $W$, then checks the regular rational readback $Q$, then reaches the terminal $\RealUp$ seal $R$, and only then exposes the accumulation-cell row $A$, subsequence ledger $D$, and extraction row $E$. Thus the route supplies explicit finite-net and subsequence data rather than open-cover compactness, a selected subcover principle, countable choice, or an external subsequence selector.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite-net row $F$. Its coverage data come from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-closed-interval-finite-net-namecert}, so the sequence is inspected against displayed interval cells and mesh ledgers before any extraction row is available.

Next read $W$ and $Q$. These rows expose finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readbacks for the sequence values and endpoint comparisons attached to the same interval source.

The terminal seal $R$ is downstream of those finite observations. It provides the $\RealUp$ boundary for the retained subsequence candidate but does not create a host real representative or an ambient completeness argument.

Finally inspect $A,D,E$. The accumulation-cell chain and subsequence ledger are finite BEDC rows, and the extraction row reports the retained prefixes and convergence budgets produced by those rows. No row in the carrier exports open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, countable choice, or an external selector, so the finite-net subsequence route is the only available extraction surface.
\end{proof}
