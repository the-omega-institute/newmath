\begin{theorem}[Cofinal monotone subsequence extraction ledger]
\label{thm:cofinal-monotone-subsequence-extraction-ledger}
For every accepted $\CofinalMonotoneSubsequenceUp$ carrier $M=(B,D,I,C,W,R,E,H,T,P,N)$, every extraction-facing read must first display the bounded source row $B$, the direction row $D$, the cofinal index schedule $I$, the finite windows $C$, and the monotone-window ledger $W$. The schedule $I$ answers each inspected source threshold by a later selected window, while $D$ and $W$ record the monotone comparisons for that displayed window. No consumer can enlarge this finite ledger into a selected infinite subsequence, quotient stream representative, countable-choice functional, or ambient compactness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cofinal-monotone-subsequence-carrier}. The only source row is $B$, the only direction row is $D$, and the only cofinal schedule row is $I$.

The finite windows are then read through $C$ and $W$. The row $C$ records which source and selected windows are inspected, while $W$ supplies the monotone comparisons inside those windows and keeps them tied to the direction row.

The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ preserve and replay this route without adding another selector coordinate. Since the carrier has no selected infinite subsequence, quotient stream representative, countable-choice functional, or compactness row, the extraction ledger is exhausted by the displayed finite data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cofinal monotone subsequence Real readback]
\label{thm:cofinal-monotone-subsequence-real-readback}
Every $\RealUp$-facing read from an accepted $\CofinalMonotoneSubsequenceUp$ packet factors through
$$
B,D,I,C,W \longmapsto R \longmapsto E
$$
together with $H,T,P,N$. Thus Bolzano--Weierstrass selector and compact interval subsequence consumers may reuse the bounded source, cofinal schedule, monotone-window ledger, regular-rational readback, and terminal Real seal, but they receive no independent completeness theorem, selected limit, quotient real equality, host subsequence selector, or countable-choice principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with \autoref{thm:cofinal-monotone-subsequence-extraction-ledger}. It places the bounded source, direction, cofinal schedule, finite windows, and monotone ledger before any readback row can be consumed.

The regular-rational readback row $R$ is downstream of that extraction ledger, and the terminal Real seal $E$ is downstream of $R$. Therefore a Real-facing consumer sees only the displayed route from finite windows to regular-rational readback and then to the Real seal.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same packet. The excluded rows are absent from the carrier, so the readback cannot become a completeness principle, selected limit, quotient equality, host subsequence selector, or countable-choice rule.
\end{proof}
