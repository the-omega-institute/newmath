It sits after the Real-name surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite observation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and the topology handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Erdos space carrier]
\label{def:erdos-space-carrier}
An $\ErdosSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(Q,S,R,A,M,O,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the rational-coordinate schedule row. It lists the finite coordinate requests, rational entries, support flags, and denominator guards that the packet exposes.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row opened for the coordinate schedule, support flags, metric requests, topology basic-neighbourhood requests, and terminal Real seals.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row. It repacks exactly the scheduled rational observations from $S$ as regular rational sequence data.
\item $A$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for coordinate values and distance budgets. It is terminal after $Q,S,R$ and exports no independent completeness principle.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ handoff row. It records the finite paired-coordinate distance checks consumed by metric readers of the Erdos-space packet.
\item $O$ is the $\TopologyUp$ handoff row. It records the basic-neighbourhood and finite-cover membership requests that may be read after the metric route is displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the coordinate schedule, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seals, Metric handoff, Topology handoff, replay rows, provenance, and local naming row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a local Erdos-space request through $Q,S,R,A,M,O,H,P,N$ without adding a selected infinite coordinate sequence or quotient sequence equality.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ErdosSpaceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ErdosSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $Q,S,R,A,M,O,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host Hilbert subspace, quotient sequence equality, selected infinite coordinate family, open-cover compactness theorem, classical separability argument, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Erdos space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:erdos-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ErdosSpaceUp$ carrier $E=(Q,S,R,A,M,O,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ErdosSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the rational-coordinate schedule, finite $\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ seals, induced $\MetricUp$ handoff, induced $\TopologyUp$ handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every metric or topology read of the packet must pass through $Q,S,R,A,M,O,H,C,P,N$, and no obligation is stored outside those finite rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic rows are precisely the rational-coordinate schedule $Q$, finite window row $S$, regular rational readback $R$, Real seal $A$, metric handoff $M$, and topology handoff $O$.

The structural rows are then forced. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same displayed schedule, readback, metric, and topology rows, while $P$ and $N$ only source and name the accepted packet.

Since the carrier has no row for a host Hilbert subspace, quotient sequence equality, selected infinite coordinate family, open-cover compactness theorem, classical separability argument, or ambient real completeness, the local NameCert cannot export any of those objects.
\end{proof}

Next use the readback and Real rows. The row $R$ repacks exactly the scheduled observations as regular rational data, and $A$ seals the coordinate and distance budgets as terminal $\RealUp$ rows.
