The $\RegularCauchyDiagonalMeetUp$ interface packages the finite shared-threshold diagonal route used by the Real-completeness vision of \autoref{ch:visions-real-completeness}. It consumes the tail-meet surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-tail-meet-namecert}, the shared-modulus surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchymodulusmeet-namecert}, the witness envelope of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-tail-witness-envelope-namecert}, and the diagonal window coverage row used by the completion route. The packet records the diagonal threshold comparison as data, not as a host-selected limit, quotient stream, or ambient completeness theorem.

\begin{definition}[RegularCauchyDiagonalMeet carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-diagonal-meet-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyDiagonalMeetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(T,M,E,W,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed coordinates:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\RegularCauchyTailMeetUp$ row that exposes the paired regular-Cauchy tails through one tail threshold;
\item $M$ is the $\CauchyModulusMeetUp$ row that supplies the shared bound consumed by the tail route;
\item $E$ is the $\CauchyTailWitnessEnvelopeUp$ row that stores the selected window, finite-window envelope, tail comparison, tail-agreement seal, and RealUp readback;
\item $W$ is the $\RealDiagonalWindowCoverageUp$ row that records the diagonal window coverage available to the Real-completion route;
\item $Q$ is the shared diagonal threshold comparison row, read only after $T,M,E,W$ have been displayed;
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transports for the tail-meet row, modulus-meet row, witness envelope, diagonal coverage row, comparison row, provenance, and local name;
\item $C$ stores the displayed $\Cont$ routes for reading the shared modulus, applying it to the tail meet, passing through the witness envelope, checking diagonal coverage, and exposing the comparison row;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchyTailMeetUp$, $\CauchyModulusMeetUp$, $\CauchyTailWitnessEnvelopeUp$, $\RealDiagonalWindowCoverageUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyDiagonalMeetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows, their transports, their continuation routes, package provenance, and local naming provenance.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchyDiagonalMeet shared-threshold factorization]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-diagonal-meet-shared-threshold-factorization}
Every Real-completion consumer of an accepted $\RegularCauchyDiagonalMeetUp$ carrier reads the diagonal threshold comparison through the finite route
$$
M \longmapsto T \longmapsto E \longmapsto W \longmapsto Q.
$$
The route uses the shared-bound packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchymodulusmeet-namecert}, the shared-tail packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-tail-meet-namecert}, the witness envelope of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-tail-witness-envelope-namecert}, and the diagonal-window coverage row described by \autoref{ch:visions-real-completeness}.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.RegularCauchyDiagonalMeetUp.RegularCauchyDiagonalMeetSharedThresholdFactorization}
\begin{proof}
Start with a Real-completion read of the comparison row. The carrier has exactly one comparison coordinate $Q$, and the continuation route to it passes through the rows $M,T,E,W$.

The shared-bound route is first read through $M$ and then consumed by $T$. This is the finite meet route: the source moduli and tail windows are synchronized before any diagonal comparison is available.

Next the witness route is exposed through $E$, and the diagonal window coverage is exposed through $W$. Only after these rows have been displayed can $Q$ be consumed. Thus every comparison read factors through the stated finite route, with $H,C,P,N$ carrying transport, replay, provenance, and naming.
\end{proof}
