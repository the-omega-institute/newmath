\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\EntourageCompletionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-entourage-completion-namecert}

Following Bishop and Bridges' constructive completion discipline, Kelley's
uniform-space entourages, and Bourbaki's filter-facing uniformity language, the
$\EntourageCompletionUp$ packet names the finite route by which an entourage
base can be read as completion data without quotienting Cauchy filters or
selecting limits. It sits between the Cauchy-filter surface of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfilter-namecert}, the finite stream
windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular
rational sequence route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic ledger of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal Real
seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[Entourage completion carrier]
\label{def:entourage-completion-carrier}
An $\EntourageCompletionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(U,F,B,S,R,D,W,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is a displayed uniformity row whose entourages are read as finite
neighborhood relations rather than as a completed quotient space.
\item $F$ is a $\CauchyFilterUp$ source row whose Cauchy condition is opened
only through named entourage requests.
\item $B$ is an entourage-base row, assigning each accepted request a finite
base entourage and its monotone refinement ledger.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-observation row used to expose
observable names before a completion-facing consumer reads the route.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback row and $D$ is the
$\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used by Real-facing consumers.
\item $W$ is a common-window row tying the Cauchy-filter read and the stream
read to the same displayed entourage budget.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the uniformity, filter,
entourage-base, stream, regularity, dyadic, window, replay, provenance, and
local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an entourage request through
$B,W,S,R,D$ and then toward the terminal $\RealUp$ seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EntourageCompletionUp$,
$\CauchyFilterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$,
$\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EntourageCompletionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows.
The carrier has no coordinate for quotient filters, host uniform spaces,
selected limits, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Entourage completion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:entourage-completion-namecert-obligations}
The local $\NameCert_{\EntourageCompletionUp}$ surface for an accepted carrier
$E=(U,F,B,S,R,D,W,H,C,P,N)$ consists exactly of carrier admission, entourage-base
exposure, Cauchy-filter source exposure, finite StreamName observation,
RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance control, common-window routing,
componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance,
and local naming. No NameCert clause is stored outside the displayed rows
$U,F,B,S,R,D,W,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:entourage-completion-carrier}. Its listed
coordinates are the whole local packet, so carrier admission and local naming
are read directly from the displayed tuple.

The entourage-base row $B$ opens the uniform request before the filter source
row $F$ is consumed. The common-window row $W$ then binds that filter read to
the same finite stream, regularity, and dyadic tolerance route used by
$S,R,D$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name this route without adding a
new object. Since quotient filters, selected limits, countable choice, host
uniform equality, and ambient Real completeness are absent from the carrier,
they cannot occur as local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Entourage completion Cauchy-filter handoff]
\label{thm:entourage-completion-cauchy-filter-handoff}
Every $\CauchyFilterUp$ consumer of an accepted $\EntourageCompletionUp$ packet
must first expose the displayed uniformity row, entourage-base row, common
window, StreamName observations, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance
ledger, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$
provenance, and local $\NameCert$ row. The handoff exports Cauchy-filter data
only as the finite route
$$
U,F,B,W,S,R,D,H,C,P,N \longmapsto \CauchyFilterUp .
$$
It exports no quotient filter, selected limit, cofinal-choice schedule, host
uniform equality, or detached completion point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the source row $F$ in the carrier. The filter is not inspected by an
ambient limit operation; it is opened only after the uniformity row $U$ and
entourage-base row $B$ have named the finite request.

Next bind the read to the common window. The row $W$ routes the same request
through the finite observation row $S$, the regular readback row $R$, and the
dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ before any completion-facing consumer can reuse the
filter data.

Finally replay the route through $H,C,P,N$. These rows transport, continue,
source, and name the displayed packet, and no excluded quotient, selected
limit, choice schedule, host uniform equality, or detached completion point is
available in the carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Entourage completion Real-route nonescape]
\label{thm:entourage-completion-real-route-nonescape}
Every $\RealUp$-facing read of an accepted $\EntourageCompletionUp$ packet
passes through the same displayed route
$$
\CauchyFilterUp,\ \StreamNameUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RealUp,
\ \BHist,\ \hsame,\ \Cont,\ \Pkg,\ \NameCert .
$$
The terminal Real seal is reached only after the entourage request, Cauchy
filter source, finite stream window, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger,
and common-window rows have been displayed. The read exports no quotient
filters, selected limits, countable choice, host real equality, host uniform
space equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the Cauchy-filter handoff of
\autoref{thm:entourage-completion-cauchy-filter-handoff}. It forces the
entourage and Cauchy-filter source rows to remain visible before the
completion-facing route is consumed.

Then read the L10 rows. StreamName observations, RegSeqRat regularity, and the
DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger form the finite bridge from the displayed
entourage request to the terminal Real seal.

The structural rows keep the bridge inside the packet. Componentwise $\hsame$
transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay preserve the same route, while $\Pkg$
and $\NameCert$ record its provenance and local name. Since the carrier
contains none of the excluded host-level or choice-level data, the Real-facing
read cannot escape the displayed route.
\end{proof}
