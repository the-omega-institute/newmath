\begin{definition}[CandidateFrontierQueue carrier]
\label{def:candidate-frontier-queue-carrier}
A $\CandidateFrontierQueueUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(S,A,F,D,B,U,L,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the certified source-state row, recording the current theory state, accepted theorem rows, and active boundary conditions read from the discovery calculus.
\item $A$ is the automation-record row, naming the generator, admissible moves, replay seed, and bounded search surface used to propose candidates.
\item $F$ is the $\NormalizationCandidateFrontierUp$ row, supplying candidate-frontier evidence rather than an evidence-free theorem.
\item $D$ is the $\ClassifierShiftLedgerResidueUp$ row, exposing the before-and-after classifier comparison used to decide whether the queue entry can leave record growth.
\item $B$ is the score-budget row, recording public cost, score, and resource bounds for the queued candidate.
\item $U$ is the queue-order row, recording the displayed priority, dependency predecessor, and admission status of each candidate.
\item $L$ is the ledger row, recording vision coverage, classifier-shift status, score account, replay obligations, and candidate rejection reasons.
\item $R$ is the replay row, recording the deterministic route by which the source state, automation record, frontier row, classifier-shift row, budget, and queue order are rechecked.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,A,F,D,B,U,L,R,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the source state through automation, frontier, classifier-shift, score, queue, ledger, and replay rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CandidateFrontierQueueUp$, $\NormalizationCandidateFrontierUp$, $\ClassifierShiftLedgerResidueUp$, $\VisionCoverageLedgerUp$, $\MetaCICCriticalPathUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CandidateFrontierQueueUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,A,F,D,B,U,L,R,H,C,P,N$. It therefore records finite queue admission for candidate-frontier work, not a host search process, an unledgered discovery verdict, a hidden semantic novelty score, or unchecked theorem soundness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CandidateFrontierQueue NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:candidate-frontier-queue-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CandidateFrontierQueueUp$ carrier
$$
Q=(S,A,F,D,B,U,L,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CandidateFrontierQueueUp}$ surface consists exactly of source-state admission, automation-record admission, candidate-frontier exposure, classifier-shift ledger exposure, score-budget exposure, queue-order exposure, ledger exposure, replay exposure, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:candidate-frontier-queue-carrier}. The accepted tuple displays the source state $S$, automation record $A$, candidate frontier $F$, classifier-shift ledger $D$, score budget $B$, queue order $U$, ledger $L$, replay row $R$, transport $H$, continuation replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$.

The queue read starts from the source state and automation record. These rows fix which candidate was proposed and which bounded search surface produced it.

The frontier and classifier rows then decide admissibility. Row $F$ keeps the candidate attached to a displayed frontier, while row $D$ records the before-and-after classifier evidence required by the discovery calculus.

The budget, queue, ledger, and replay rows make the admission auditable. Since the carrier contains no further coordinate for semantic novelty, theorem soundness, or positive-discovery certification, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CandidateFrontierQueue classifier-shift nonescape]
\label{thm:candidate-frontier-queue-classifier-shift-nonescape}
Let $Q=(S,A,F,D,B,U,L,R,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CandidateFrontierQueueUp$ carrier. A queued candidate cannot be read as discovery-class work unless the same packet exposes the $\ClassifierShiftLedgerResidueUp$ row $D$, the score-budget row $B$, the ledger row $L$, and the replay row $R$ over the source-state and automation rows $S,A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The queue-order row $U$ only records priority and dependency placement; it does not decide claim strength.

The source-state and automation rows $S,A$ fix the candidate's origin. The candidate-frontier row $F$ supplies admissible frontier evidence, but it still does not show classifier change.

Classifier-shift evidence must therefore be read through $D$. The score-budget row $B$ records the public cost account, the ledger row $L$ records the coverage and rejection boundary, and the replay row $R$ records the deterministic recheck route over the same source and automation surface.

If any of $D,B,L,R$ is absent, the queue entry remains record growth or candidate work inside this packet. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that boundary and do not create a hidden discovery verdict.
\end{proof}
