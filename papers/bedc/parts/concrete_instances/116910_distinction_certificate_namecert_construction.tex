\begin{definition}[DistinctionCertificate carrier]
\label{def:distinction-certificate-carrier}
A $\DistinctionCertificateUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(S,P_0,K,\Sigma,L,H,C,R,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source row naming the generated marks, histories, traces, event flows, or supply records whose distinction is being inspected.
\item $P_0$ is the pattern row, recording the finite feature, trace, boundary, or certificate pattern proposed as evidence for the distinction.
\item $K$ is the candidate classifier row that compares source records through the displayed pattern.
\item $\Sigma$ is the stability row, recording transport of the classifier under the admitted $\hsame$ movements.
\item $L$ is the ledger row, naming hidden material, refusal cases, and not-claimed readings.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source, pattern, classifier, stability, ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for distinction-certification consumers.
\item $R$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DistinctionCertificateUp$, $\PrimitiveDistinctionLedgerUp$, $\ObserverDistinctionTraceUp$, $\HaltingDistinctionTraceUp$, $\CertifiedEventFlowUp$, $\SupplyTaxonomyUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DistinctionCertificateUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for raw distinction objecthood, uncertified semantic assignment, hidden classifier shift, global explanation, or verifier acceptance detached from the five displayed certificate rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[DistinctionCertificate NameCert boundary]
\label{thm:distinction-certificate-namecert-boundary}
Every accepted $\DistinctionCertificateUp$ packet certifies a candidate distinction as a NameCert boundary only through
$$
S,P_0,K,\Sigma,L,H,C,R,N .
$$
The source, pattern, classifier, stability, and ledger rows must all be present before the local naming row can be consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:distinction-certificate-carrier}. Rows $S,P_0,K,\Sigma,L$ are the five certificate rows, and $H,C,R,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name that same surface.

The candidate classifier row $K$ is not read by itself. It is tied to the source row $S$ and pattern row $P_0$, then stabilized through $\Sigma$ and audited through $L$.

Only after those rows are visible can the local $\NameCert$ row $N$ be consumed. Thus the accepted packet is a certificate boundary, not a raw distinction or a bare classifier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DistinctionCertificate sibling separation]
\label{thm:distinction-certificate-sibling-separation}
The $\DistinctionCertificateUp$ packet is not bijective to $\PrimitiveDistinctionLedgerUp$, $\ObserverDistinctionTraceUp$, $\HaltingDistinctionTraceUp$, $\CertifiedEventFlowUp$, or $\SupplyTaxonomyUp$. Each listed sibling supplies a distinction source, trace, event-flow, or audit route, while $\DistinctionCertificateUp$ additionally binds the candidate classifier to the five-field NameCert boundary $S,P_0,K,\Sigma,L$ with transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compare the displayed rows. Primitive distinction, observer trace, halting trace, certified event flow, and supply taxonomy each provide a concrete source surface or audit path for distinctions.

The present packet has a different role. It requires the source row, pattern row, classifier row, stability row, and ledger row to be visible together before certification is licensed.

Therefore none of the listed siblings can replace $\DistinctionCertificateUp$ while preserving all fields of \autoref{def:distinction-certificate-carrier}. A consumer that needs certification must read the full packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DistinctionCertificate TasteGate requirements]
\label{thm:distinction-certificate-tastegate-requirements}
The Lean TasteGate surface for $\DistinctionCertificateUp$ must provide explicit $\mathsf{Nontrivial}\ \DistinctionCertificateUp$ and $\mathsf{FieldFaithful}\ \DistinctionCertificateUp$ instances. The nontriviality witness must separate a certified classifier packet from a bare candidate distinction, and the field-faithfulness witness must keep $S,P_0,K,\Sigma,L,H,C,R,N$ as distinct rows under transport and replay.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Nontriviality follows from the intended separation between raw distinction material and a certified NameCert boundary. A bare candidate distinction lacks at least one of $S,P_0,K,\Sigma,L$ and therefore cannot be the same accepted packet as the full carrier.

Field faithfulness is the preservation requirement for the displayed coordinates. If source, pattern, classifier, stability, ledger, transport, replay, provenance, or naming were collapsed, the packet would no longer expose the certificate boundary of \autoref{thm:distinction-certificate-namecert-boundary}.

Thus the TasteGate must preserve both the distinction between bare and certified objects and the componentwise rows of the accepted packet.
\end{proof}
