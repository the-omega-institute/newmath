\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ConstructiveRealModulusUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-constructive-real-modulus-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive real moduli, the
$\ConstructiveRealModulusUp$ packet records the finite modulus surface that a
Real and Completion consumer reads before treating a regular rational stream as
a completed real name. It sits over the dyadic tolerance rows of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite observation
windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular
rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the
terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The
packet names a displayed modulus schedule only; it does not assert quotient
stream equality, select a limit, import countable choice, or close ambient
$\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Constructive real modulus carrier]
\label{def:constructive-real-modulus-carrier}
A $\ConstructiveRealModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(S,D,Q,R,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule naming the inspected
source positions.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger recording the dyadic
radii requested by the consumer.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular rational name
inside the displayed window.
\item $R$ is the modulus-response row, assigning each requested dyadic radius a
displayed tail threshold inside $S$.
\item $A$ is the visible $\RealUp$ seal available only after $S,D,Q,R$ have
been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,D,Q,R,A,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
\StreamNameUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp
\longmapsto R \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ConstructiveRealModulusUp$,
$\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$,
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveRealModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $S,D,Q,R,A,H,C,P,N$.
It has no coordinate for quotient equality of real names, a selected limit,
countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}
\leanstmt{BEDC.Derived.ConstructiveRealModulusUp}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive real modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:constructive-real-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ConstructiveRealModulusUp$ carrier
$$
M=(S,D,Q,R,A,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ConstructiveRealModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of
finite StreamName window exposure, DyadicRatCore tolerance readback, RegSeqRat
regular-rational readback, modulus-response threshold exposure, visible Real
seal admission, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay,
$\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation is stored outside these
displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:constructive-real-modulus-carrier}. The
packet exposes exactly the window, tolerance, readback, response, seal,
transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows.

The modulus obligations are finite because $S,D,Q,R$ are displayed rows. The
window row lists the inspected positions, the dyadic ledger lists the requested
tolerances, the regular-rational readback row supplies the inspected values,
and the response row records the threshold used by the consumer.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same packet. Since the carrier has no
further coordinate, the local certificate cannot store quotient equality of real
names, a selected limit, countable choice, open-cover compactness, or ambient
$\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive real modulus Real-completion route]
\label{thm:constructive-real-modulus-real-completion-route}
Every Real and Completion consumer reading an accepted
$\ConstructiveRealModulusUp$ packet must first expose the finite route
$$
\StreamNameUp \longmapsto \DyadicRatCoreUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp
\longmapsto R \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
Thus the packet supplies a displayed modulus window for the completion exit
route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert},
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}; it does not supply Real
completeness, quotient equality, selected limits, or countable choice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier projection. The consumer first reads the finite source
window $S$, then the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, and then the regular rational
readback row $Q$.

The modulus-response row $R$ is available only over the displayed $S,D,Q$
surface. The Real seal $A$ is therefore downstream of a finite modulus response,
not a host completion principle.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route.
Because the route is exhausted by $S,D,Q,R,A,H,C,P,N$, it cannot export quotient
equality of real names, selected limits, countable choice, open-cover
compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
