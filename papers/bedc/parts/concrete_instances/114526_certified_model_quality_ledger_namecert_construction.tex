\begin{definition}[Certified model quality ledger carrier]
\label{def:certified-model-quality-ledger-carrier}
A $\CertifiedModelQualityLedgerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(S,B,W,C,D,T,A,V,G,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the declared model-quality scope row, naming the evaluation domain, model state, admitted intervention family, critical behavior family, and public protocol.
\item $B$ is the behavior-coverage row, separating certified critical behaviors from uncovered or partially certified behaviors.
\item $W$ is the public weight-profile row used to compare benefit, cost, and debt.
\item $C$ is the cost row, recording model, data, training, certificate, ledger, intervention, and verification costs.
\item $D$ is the debt row, recording black-box, ledger, stability, generalization, safety, shortcut, semantic, and verification debts.
\item $T$ is the stability and transfer row for the certified behavior classifiers.
\item $A$ is the safety and shortcut-boundary row, recording failure cases and not-claimed scope.
\item $V$ is the verification row, separating checked, target, and unverified certificate obligations.
\item $G$ is the quality-score gate row. It reads benefit, cost, and debt only through $B,W,C,D,T,A,V$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the scope, coverage, weights, costs, debts, stability, safety, verification, score, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from scope and behavior coverage to score comparison, gate acceptance, and refusal boundaries.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CertifiedModelQualityLedgerUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CertifiedModelQualityLedgerUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted ledgers only through $S,B,W,C,D,T,A,V,G,H,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for private benchmark preference, hidden weight selection, unledgered classifier gain, performance-only quality, or verification laundering.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Certified model quality ledger NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:certified-model-quality-ledger-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:certified-model-quality-ledger-carrier}. The scope, coverage, weight, cost, debt, stability, safety, verification, score, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows are the entire displayed inventory.

The accounting rows are read before any score comparison. Behavior coverage supplies certified and uncovered critical behavior families, the public weight profile states how benefit, cost, and debt are compared, and the cost and debt rows record the negative terms.

The stability, safety, and verification rows restrict what may count as certified benefit. A classifier gain without those rows remains a debt-bearing or target-only entry rather than a closed quality gain.

Finally $G,H,R,P,N$ replay, transport, source, and name the same finite ledger. Since the carrier has no coordinate for hidden weights, private benchmark preference, performance-only quality, unledgered classifier gain, or verification laundering, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Certified model quality score nonescape]
\label{thm:certified-model-quality-ledger-score-nonescape}
Every accepted quality-score comparison in a $\CertifiedModelQualityLedgerUp$ packet must display the behavior-coverage row $B$, public weight-profile row $W$, cost row $C$, debt row $D$, stability and transfer row $T$, safety row $A$, verification row $V$, and score gate row $G$ before the comparison is read. Two accepted ledgers with identical behavior-coverage rows but different public weight profiles cannot be compared without displaying the weight-profile row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the score gate row $G$. By \autoref{def:certified-model-quality-ledger-carrier}, $G$ reads benefit, cost, and debt only through $B,W,C,D,T,A,V$.

If two ledgers agree on behavior coverage but differ in public weights, the row $B$ alone does not determine the score comparison. The row $W$ specifies how certified benefit, cost, and debt are weighed, so it must be displayed before the comparison is public.

The remaining rows keep the score inside the ledger. Costs and debts are read from $C,D$, stability and safety qualifications from $T,A$, verification status from $V$, and transport and replay from $H,R,P,N$.

Thus score comparison cannot escape to a private benchmark, hidden weight protocol, performance-only metric, unledgered classifier gain, or verification laundering artifact.
\end{proof}
