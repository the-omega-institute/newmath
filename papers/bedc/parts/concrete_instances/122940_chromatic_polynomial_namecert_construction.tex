Following Birkhoff and Whitney, the $\ChromaticPolynomialUp$ packet records the finite graph-colouring data used to compute the chromatic polynomial through deletion and contraction. It sits over the finite graph carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-graph-namecert}, the finite-set rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finset-namecert}, the spanning-tree witness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-spanningtree-namecert}, the deletion-contraction neighbour \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tutte-polynomial-namecert}, and the matroid boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matroid-namecert}. The packet names only finite colour palettes, proper-colouring ledgers, and deletion-contraction rows; it does not import an infinite colouring principle, a host graph isomorphism oracle, an unrestricted polynomial ring, or a Tutte-polynomial theorem outside the displayed packet.

\begin{definition}[ChromaticPolynomial carrier]
\label{def:chromatic-polynomial-carrier}
A $\ChromaticPolynomialUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(G,Q,C,D,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the finite $\GraphUp$ row naming the vertex list, edge list, incidence readback, loop refusal, and simple-graph boundary used by the packet.
\item $Q$ is the $\FinSetUp$ colour-palette row, including the finite colour list and membership ledger visible to the colouring read.
\item $C$ is the proper-colouring ledger, listing the accepted vertex-to-colour rows and the edgewise inequality checks for adjacent vertices.
\item $D$ is the deletion-contraction row for a displayed edge, exposing the deletion graph, contraction graph, and the boundary cases for loops and empty edge lists.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the graph, colour palette, colouring ledger, deletion-contraction row, replay, provenance, and local name.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from graph admission through palette lookup, proper-colouring verification, deletion-contraction comparison, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ChromaticPolynomialUp$, $\GraphUp$, $\FinSetUp$, $\SpanningTreeUp$, $\TuttePolynomialUp$, $\MatroidUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ChromaticPolynomialUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $G,Q,C,D,H,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an infinite colour set, host graph equality, unrestricted polynomial algebra, a hidden deletion-contraction oracle, or a matroid/Tutte theorem not displayed as finite sibling data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ChromaticPolynomial NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:chromatic-polynomial-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ChromaticPolynomialUp$ carrier $X=(G,Q,C,D,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ChromaticPolynomialUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the finite graph row, finite colour-palette exposure, proper-colouring ledger exactness, deletion-contraction boundary exposure, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:chromatic-polynomial-carrier}. The row $G$ is the only graph source, and $Q$ is the only colour-palette source.

Read the colouring data through $C$. Properness is checked edge by edge against the finite graph row, so the ledger records only displayed vertex-colour assignments and displayed adjacency refusals.

Read deletion and contraction through $D$. The row names the selected edge, the deletion graph, the contraction graph, and the loop or empty-edge boundary cases before any recurrence-style comparison is exposed.

Finally use $H,R,P,N$. These rows transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier contains no infinite palette, host graph equality, unrestricted polynomial algebra, or hidden Tutte/matroid theorem, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ChromaticPolynomial deletion-contraction boundary]
\label{thm:chromatic-polynomial-deletion-contraction-boundary}
Every deletion-contraction read accepted by a $\ChromaticPolynomialUp$ carrier factors through the displayed route
$$
G \longmapsto D \longmapsto C \longmapsto Q \longmapsto H,R,P,N .
$$
The deletion graph and contraction graph are read as finite graph rows tied to the same colour-palette and proper-colouring ledger. Boundary cases for loops, empty edge lists, and palette exhaustion remain ledger rows, not external polynomial identities.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the graph row $G$. It supplies the finite vertices, finite edges, and incidence readback used by the selected edge in $D$.

The deletion-contraction row $D$ keeps both derived graphs inside the same packet. Their colouring reads are not independent host graphs; each is compared through the palette row $Q$ and the proper-colouring ledger $C$.

The structural rows $H,R,P,N$ preserve the same finite route and record its provenance through the graph, finite-set, spanning-tree, Tutte-polynomial, and matroid neighbours. They do not introduce an unrestricted polynomial identity or a hidden recurrence theorem.

Thus deletion-contraction is a boundary of the displayed NameCert packet: a downstream consumer may read the finite comparison route, but not an ambient chromatic-polynomial algebra beyond it.
\end{proof}
