Following Hewitt and Kelley on realcompact spaces, the $\HewittRealcompactificationUp$ packet records the finite real-valued-function completion boundary seen by BEDC consumers before any ambient realcompactification theorem is available. It sits after the completely regular source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completely-regular-space-namecert}, the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the continuous-map surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the bounded-probe comparison of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-stone-cech-compactification-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Hewitt realcompactification carrier]
\label{def:hewitt-realcompactification-carrier}
A $\HewittRealcompactificationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
H=(C,T,F,R,B,Z,E,L,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp$ source row, exposing the point-versus-closed-set real-valued separation surface.
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ row naming the open-set carrier on which the real-valued observations are read.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ row listing the visible real-valued probes used by the completion boundary.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ value-seal row for the displayed probe evaluations.
\item $B$ is the $\StoneCechCompactificationUp$ comparison row, used only as a bounded-probe sibling surface.
\item $Z$ is the zero-set and cozero-set ledger row recording the finite real-function tests seen by the packet.
\item $E$ is the realcompactification envelope row, exposing the completion-facing request without selecting z-ultrafilters or points.
\item $L$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, topology, continuous-map, Real, Stone--Cech comparison, zero-set ledger, envelope, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HewittRealcompactificationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $C,T,F,R,B,Z,E,L,K,P,N$. It contains no coordinate for a selected z-ultrafilter, a chosen realcompactification point, host compactification equality, a full ring of all real-valued functions, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hewitt realcompactification NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hewitt-realcompactification-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HewittRealcompactificationUp$ carrier
$$
H=(C,T,F,R,B,Z,E,L,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\HewittRealcompactificationUp}$ surface consists exactly of completely regular source exposure through $C$, topology exposure through $T$, continuous real-valued probe exposure through $F$, Real value sealing through $R$, Stone--Cech comparison through $B$, zero-set ledger exposure through $Z$, realcompactification-envelope exposure through $E$, componentwise transport through $L$, displayed replay through $K$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hewitt-realcompactification-carrier}. Rows $C,T,F,R$ expose the completely regular source, its topology, the displayed continuous real-valued probes, and their Real value seals.

Rows $B,Z,E$ then record only the comparison and envelope data. The Stone--Cech row is a sibling bounded-probe comparison, the zero-set ledger lists finite real-function tests, and the envelope row names the completion-facing request without adding a selected z-ultrafilter or point.

Finally $L,K,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly those rows. Since no excluded object is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hewitt realcompactification real-function handoff]
\label{thm:hewitt-realcompactification-real-function-handoff}
Every completion-facing read of an accepted $\HewittRealcompactificationUp$ carrier first exposes the route
$$
\CompletelyRegularSpaceUp \longmapsto \TopologyUp \longmapsto \ContinuousMapUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
and only then reads the $\StoneCechCompactificationUp$ comparison row, the zero-set ledger, and the realcompactification envelope. Thus the packet exports a finite real-function handoff, not z-ultrafilter choice, host compactification equality, selected points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a completion-facing query. The query must read $C$ and $T$ before any real-valued probe is visible, so the completely regular source and its topology remain explicit.

The probe stage is $F$ and $R$. It displays the continuous real-valued observations and their Real value seals, but it does not supply an ambient function space or a completed realcompact host.

Only after those rows are present can the consumer read $B,Z,E$. The comparison, zero-set ledger, and envelope therefore remain downstream of displayed real-function data, and the structural rows $L,K,P,N$ preserve that same order.
\end{proof}
