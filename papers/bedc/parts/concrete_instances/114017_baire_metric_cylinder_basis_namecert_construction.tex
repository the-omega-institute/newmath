\begin{proof}

$$

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Read the window and radius rows $W,D$. These rows locate the cylinder by finite length and dyadic radius, so the metric consumer row $C$ cannot begin from a completed stream or a quotient name.

Finally pass through $F,H,T,P,N$. The FanTheorem row receives the displayed finite cylinder route, while transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that route without adding an arbitrary topology or compactness oracle.
