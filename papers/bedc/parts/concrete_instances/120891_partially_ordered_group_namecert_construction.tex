Following Bourbaki on ordered algebraic structures and Fuchs on partially ordered groups, the $\PartiallyOrderedGroupUp$ packet records the finite NameCert surface for a group equipped with a translation-invariant partial order. It sits between the group carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}, the order surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-order-namecert}, the poset carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poset-namecert}, the monoid source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-monoid-namecert}, and the local $\NameCert$ transport rows used throughout the concrete-instance layer. The packet names order-compatible multiplication and inversion data; it does not assert totality, lattice operations, Archimedean comparison, ordered completion, or host equality of group functions.

\begin{definition}[Partially ordered group carrier]
\label{def:partially-ordered-group-carrier}
A $\PartiallyOrderedGroupUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(A,O,P,M,I,K,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the $\GroupUp$ row, naming multiplication, inverse, neutral element, and the finite group-law surface.
\item $O$ is the $\OrderUp$ row on the same carrier, recording reflexive, antisymmetric, and transitive comparison data.
\item $P$ is the $\PosetUp$ readback row, exposing the order as a partial-order classifier rather than a preorder-only relation.
\item $M$ is the $\MonoidUp$ source row for the multiplication law before inverses are inspected.
\item $I$ records order transport under left and right multiplication by displayed group elements.
\item $K$ records positive-cone data: neutral membership, multiplication closure, inverse reflection, and reconstruction of the order from the cone.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $A,O,P,M,I,K$ and the structural rows below.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from group-law reads to order reads, translation reads, positive-cone reads, provenance, and local naming.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PartiallyOrderedGroupUp$, $\GroupUp$, $\OrderUp$, $\PosetUp$, $\MonoidUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PartiallyOrderedGroupUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,O,P,M,I,K,H,C,Q,N$. It has no coordinate for total order, lattice meet or join, Archimedean law, ordered completion, quotient representative, or host-level group function equality.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Partially ordered group NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:partially-ordered-group-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PartiallyOrderedGroupUp$ carrier $G=(A,O,P,M,I,K,H,C,Q,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\PartiallyOrderedGroupUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $G$, group exposure through $A$, order exposure through $O$, poset readback through $P$, monoid multiplication exposure through $M$, translation-invariance accountability through $I$, positive-cone accountability through $K$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $Q$, local naming through $N$, and nonescape excluding totality, lattice operations, Archimedean comparison, ordered completion, quotient representatives, and host group-function equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:partially-ordered-group-carrier}. The algebraic rows are $A$ and $M$, while the order rows are $O$ and $P$.

The compatibility rows are separate. Translation invariance is read from $I$, and positive-cone stability is read from $K$; neither row is forced by merely naming a group or a poset.

Finally $H,C,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no further coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface and block the excluded stronger structures.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:partially-ordered-group-positive-cone-stability}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The reconstruction is not detached from algebra. It uses the group operation from $A$, the multiplication law from $M$, and the partial-order classifier exposed through $O$ and $P$.

Thus cone stability remains a finite positive-cone obligation inside the packet. It does not add totality, meets, joins, Archimedean comparison, or completion rows.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:partially-ordered-group-order-translation}

Provenance and local naming record the route as part of the $\PartiallyOrderedGroupUp$ certificate. No coordinate identifies host functions or supplies comparison data outside the finite packet.
