\begin{theorem}[Dedekind located interval NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dedekind-located-interval-namecert-obligations}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:dedekind-located-interval-carrier}. The located interval row $L$ and rational bracket row $Q$ expose the displayed endpoints before any separator read is admitted.

The dyadic ledger $D$, finite windows $S$, and regular readback $R$ supply the finite inspection route. They determine the separator evidence only at the displayed scale and do not introduce a cut over all rational tests.
