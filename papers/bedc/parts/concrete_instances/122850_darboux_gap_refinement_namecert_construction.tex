\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DarbouxGapRefinementUp$}

Following Darboux and Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis, the $\DarbouxGapRefinementUp$ packet records the finite partition refinement surface that turns an upper-minus-lower Darboux gap into a displayed refinement certificate before a Real-facing consumer reads a regular rational window. It sits after the Darboux-sum refinement route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-darboux-sum-refinement-namecert}, the tagged or dyadic partition surfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tagged-partition-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-partition-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives Darboux and Riemann-facing consumers a finite gap-refinement certificate without importing selected partition sequences, quotient equality of functions, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[DarbouxGapRefinement carrier]
\label{def:darboux-gap-refinement-carrier}
A $\DarbouxGapRefinementUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(R,L,U,T,D,W,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the $\DarbouxSumRefinementUp$ source row, carrying the source partition, refined partition, finite cell-incidence map, and Darboux handoff.
\item $L$ is the $\LowerDarbouxSumUp$ row on the displayed refined partition.
\item $U$ is the $\UpperDarbouxSumUp$ row on the same displayed refined partition.
\item $T$ is either the $\TaggedPartitionUp$ row or the $\DyadicPartitionUp$ row that supplies the finite refinement cells read by $R,L,U$.
\item $D$ is the finite upper-minus-lower gap row comparing $U$ and $L$ over exactly the displayed cells of $T$.
\item $W$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the displayed gap budget.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $R,L,U,T,D,W$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the refinement, lower row, upper row, partition row, gap row, readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from Darboux, Riemann, Real, or Completion requests through the same finite packet.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DarbouxGapRefinementUp$, $\DarbouxSumRefinementUp$, $\DarbouxSumUp$, $\LowerDarbouxSumUp$, $\UpperDarbouxSumUp$, $\TaggedPartitionUp$, $\DyadicPartitionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DarbouxGapRefinementUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,L,U,T,D,W,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a selected sequence of partitions, quotient equality of functions, a host supremum or infimum, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[DarbouxGapRefinement NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:darboux-gap-refinement-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DarbouxGapRefinementUp$ carrier $G=(R,L,U,T,D,W,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DarbouxGapRefinementUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the Darboux-sum refinement row, lower and upper Darboux-sum rows, tagged or dyadic partition row, finite gap row, regular rational readback, Real seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every obligation is read through the same finite partition-gap packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:darboux-gap-refinement-carrier}. The admitted packet displays the Darboux-sum refinement source, the lower and upper rows, the tagged or dyadic partition row, the gap comparison, the regular rational readback, and the Real seal.

The partition-gap obligations are finite. The row $T$ supplies the displayed cells, $R$ supplies the refinement route, and $D$ compares the lower and upper Darboux rows only over those cells.

The readback and seal rows are downstream. The row $W$ turns the displayed gap into a regular rational window, and $E$ is reached only after $R,L,U,T,D,W$ have been opened. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly the same packet, so the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DarbouxGapRefinement partition route]
\label{thm:darboux-gap-refinement-partition-route}
Every Darboux, Riemann, Real, or Completion consumer of an accepted $\DarbouxGapRefinementUp$ carrier reaches the gap certificate only through the finite route
$$
\DarbouxSumRefinementUp \longmapsto (\TaggedPartitionUp \ \mathrm{or}\ \DyadicPartitionUp) \longmapsto (\LowerDarbouxSumUp,\UpperDarbouxSumUp) \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The consumer first reads the refinement row $R$, the displayed partition row $T$, the lower and upper rows $L,U$, and the gap row $D$; only then may it consume the regular rational readback $W$ and terminal Real seal $E$. It receives no selected partition sequence, quotient-function equality, host integral value, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $G=(R,L,U,T,D,W,E,H,C,P,N)$. The carrier definition makes $R$ the Darboux-sum refinement source and $T$ the only displayed partition row available to the consumer.

Read $L$ and $U$ over the same cells supplied by $T$. The gap row $D$ then compares the upper and lower rows over exactly that displayed finite partition route.

Only after this comparison is displayed can the consumer reach $W$ and $E$. The regular rational readback and Real seal are therefore downstream of the finite partition-gap route, while $H,C,P,N$ merely transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for a selected partition sequence, quotient-function equality, host integral value, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness theorem, none of those objects is exported.
\end{proof}
