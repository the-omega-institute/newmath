\begin{definition}[Real completion diagonal budget carrier]
\label{def:real-completion-diagonal-budget-carrier}
A $\RealCompletionDiagonalBudgetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(Q,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the precision-request row naming the diagonal index and requested
regular-Cauchy tolerance.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row opened for the requested
diagonal index.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance-budget row that accounts for the
endpoint, middle-modulus, and readback error allowances.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the window and
tolerance budget.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row consumed only after $Q,W,D,R$ are
displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the precision, window,
tolerance, readback, seal, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a diagonal-limit request
through $Q,W,D,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealCompletionDiagonalBudgetUp$,
$\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$,
$\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionDiagonalBudgetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only by transporting the displayed
precision, window, tolerance, readback, Real seal, continuation, provenance,
and local naming rows to a common finite surface.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion diagonal budget NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-completion-diagonal-budget-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealCompletionDiagonalBudgetUp$ carrier
$$
B=(Q,W,D,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RealCompletionDiagonalBudgetUp}$ surface consists
exactly of carrier admission for the precision request, StreamName window,
DyadicRatCore budget, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, continuation,
provenance, and local naming; classifier stability under the displayed
$\hsame$ rows; route exactness from $Q,W,D,R$ to $E$; and refusal rows
excluding quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, host real
equality, open-cover compactness, and ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:real-completion-diagonal-budget-carrier}. The listed coordinates
are exactly the precision request, finite window, dyadic budget, regular
readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Classifier stability is componentwise. Transport through $H$ moves those rows
to a common finite surface without adding another representative or quotient
coordinate.

Route exactness follows from $C$. A diagonal-limit request first exposes the
precision row $Q$, then the finite window $W$, then the dyadic tolerance budget
$D$, then the regular readback $R$, and only then the Real seal $E$.

The refusal rows are not extra data. They state that no coordinate of the
packet contains quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, host
equality, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness, so no NameCert
obligation can export them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Real completion diagonal budget vision route]
\label{thm:real-completion-diagonal-budget-vision-route}
The diagonal-limit representative of
\autoref{def:real-completeness-diagonal-limit} is consumed through an accepted
$\RealCompletionDiagonalBudgetUp$ carrier only by the route
$$

$$
Thus the real-completion vision route factors each precision request through a

$\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$
transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local
$\NameCert_{\RealCompletionDiagonalBudgetUp}$ naming.

No quotient stream, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host real
equality, open-cover compactness row, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the source vision section
\autoref{sec:real-completeness-regular-cauchy}. Its diagonal representative
uses a requested precision to choose a displayed diagonal readback; the carrier
records that request as $Q$.

Replay the finite route. The request opens the StreamName window $W$, spends
the DyadicRatCore budget $D$, reads the RegSeqRat row $R$, and only then
reaches the Real seal $E$.

Transport, replay, provenance, and naming stay local to the same packet. The
rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name the displayed route rather than adding a
second completion principle.

Therefore the vision route lands as finite NameCert data. Since the carrier
has no quotient stream, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host
equality, open-cover compactness row, or ambient completeness coordinate, none
can replace the finite diagonal budget.
\end{proof}
