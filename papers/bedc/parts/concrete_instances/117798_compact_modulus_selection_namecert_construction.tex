\item $N$ is the finite-net row, recording the displayed compact net, tolerance budget, and cover membership checks used by the selector.
\item $L$ is the local pointwise continuity ledger, naming the inspected centers and local continuity budgets before any uniform row is exposed.

\begin{theorem}[Compact modulus selection finite-net selector]
\label{thm:compact-modulus-selection-net-selector}

\begin{proof}

Next read the local continuity ledger $L$. The uniform handoff $U$ is available only after the finite centers and local budgets have been replayed through $C$ and transported through $H$.

Finally pass to the stream, regular-rational, and Real rows. They record finite request replay and readback only, so the selector remains a finite-net route rather than any of the refused choice, open-cover, quotient, or completeness principles.
\end{proof}
