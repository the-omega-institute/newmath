Following Riemann, Stieltjes, and Bishop--Bridges, the $\DarbouxStieltjesSumUp$ packet records the finite upper-lower Stieltjes sum boundary for a regulated integrand read against a bounded-variation integrator. It sits beside the Riemann--Stieltjes packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannstieltjes-namecert}, the bounded-variation ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedvariation-namecert}, the regulated-function surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regulated-function-namecert}, the tagged finite-sum row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannsum-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only finite partition cells, local oscillation rows, bounded-variation cell contributions, dyadic comparison budgets, rational readback, and the Real-facing seal handoff; it does not import measure integration, quotient functions, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Darboux--Stieltjes sum carrier]
\label{def:darboux-stieltjes-sum-carrier}
A $\DarbouxStieltjesSumUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(F,A,T,U,L,Q,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\RegulatedFunctionUp$ integrand row, exposing only the finite value windows and one-sided oscillation reads requested by the displayed partition.
\item $A$ is the $\BoundedVariationUp$ integrator row, carrying the finite partition-variation ledger and transported endpoint changes of the integrator.
\item $T$ is the tagged partition row, read through the $\RiemannSumUp$ mesh and tag-containment surface.
\item $U$ is the upper Darboux--Stieltjes cell row, recording for each carried cell a displayed upper oscillation bound for $F$ multiplied by the corresponding positive variation contribution of $A$.
\item $L$ is the lower Darboux--Stieltjes cell row, recording for each carried cell a displayed lower oscillation bound for $F$ multiplied by the corresponding signed variation contribution of $A$ when that row is licensed by $A$.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ oscillation budget, fixing the dyadic tolerances used to compare upper and lower cell ledgers.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the finite upper sum, lower sum, and gap comparison ledgers.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal handoff reached only after $F,A,T,U,L,Q,R$ have all been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the integrand, integrator, tagged partition, upper and lower cell rows, dyadic budget, rational readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a partition request through the regulated-function windows, bounded-variation cell contributions, upper and lower cell ledgers, dyadic budget, rational readback, and Real seal handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DarbouxStieltjesSumUp$, $\RiemannStieltjesUp$, $\BoundedVariationUp$, $\RegulatedFunctionUp$, $\RiemannSumUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DarbouxStieltjesSumUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed rows $F,A,T,U,L,Q,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for measure integration, a quotient of functions, a selected refinement limit, countable choice, or an ambient Real-completeness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Darboux--Stieltjes NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:darboux-stieltjes-sum-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DarbouxStieltjesSumUp$ carrier $D=(F,A,T,U,L,Q,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\DarbouxStieltjesSumUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the regulated integrand, bounded-variation integrator, tagged partition, upper and lower Darboux cell rows, dyadic oscillation budget, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming rows. Every accepted read follows the finite route
$$
F,A,T \longrightarrow U,L \longrightarrow Q \longrightarrow R \longrightarrow E
$$
together with $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:darboux-stieltjes-sum-carrier}. The displayed rows list the regulated integrand windows, bounded-variation cell contributions, tagged partition, upper and lower cell ledgers, dyadic budget, rational readback, Real seal, and structural provenance.

For each carried cell, the upper and lower rows are formed from the same partition row $T$. The integrand contribution is read through $F$, while the Stieltjes cell contribution is read through $A$, so both Darboux rows are finite cellwise records rather than host-level extrema or a measure integral.

The dyadic budget $Q$ and readback row $R$ compare the two finite ledgers before the seal row $E$ is visible. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same route, and $P,N$ only source and name it.

Since no carrier coordinate names measure integration, quotient functions, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Darboux--Stieltjes RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:darboux-stieltjes-sum-regseqrat-handoff}
Every $\DarbouxStieltjesSumUp$ read that is consumed by a $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ route factors through a finite partition refinement before the numerical seal is inspected. For an accepted carrier $D=(F,A,T,U,L,Q,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the consumer must first read the common refinement of the tagged partition, the regulated integrand windows, the bounded-variation cell contributions, the upper and lower Darboux cell rows, and the dyadic oscillation budget; only then may it read $R$ and the terminal seal row $E$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite tagged partition row $T$ and refine it inside the displayed partition surface. The refinement changes only carried cells and their tag-containment data, so it remains a finite $\BHist$ calculation.

Read $F$ and $A$ on that refinement. The regulated integrand supplies the local value windows and oscillation bounds, while the bounded-variation integrator supplies the endpoint changes and variation ledger needed by each Stieltjes cell.

Rebuild the upper and lower ledgers $U,L$ on the refined cells and compare them through the dyadic budget $Q$. The $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $R$ reads back exactly those finite sums and their gap, after which the $\RealUp$ seal $E$ may be consumed.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same refinement route. Therefore the handoff never uses measure integration, quotient functions, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
