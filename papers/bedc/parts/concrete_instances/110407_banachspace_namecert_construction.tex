\label{def:banach-space-carrier}

$$

\item $N$ is the $\NormUp$ row over $V$, exposing norm evaluation, triangle, zero, and scalar-compatibility obligations only as displayed finite rows.
\item $M$ is the metric comparison row induced by $N$, used by completion-facing consumers before any completeness read is public.
\item $Q$ is the $\CauchyNetCompletionUp$ or regular Cauchy request ledger listing the finite tail and net windows inspected by a completion read.
\item $S$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ handoff row for the displayed Cauchy requests over $M$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance readback row attached to the same Cauchy windows and norm budgets.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ tolerance and distance seal reached after $R$ has been displayed.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for an external complete metric space, quotient Cauchy classes, selected representatives, host vector equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
