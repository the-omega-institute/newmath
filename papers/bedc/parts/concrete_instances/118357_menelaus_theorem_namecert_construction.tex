\begin{theorem}[MenelausTheorem NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:menelaus-theorem-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MenelausTheoremUp$ carrier $M=(A,P,L,I,R,Q,H,C,G,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MenelausTheoremUp}$ surface consists exactly of these obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed affine triangle, projective incidence boundary, transversal line, side-intersection witnesses, directed-ratio ledger, real readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local name;
\item incidence exactness, requiring the three side intersections in $I$ to be read through $A,P,L$ before any ratio row is consumed;
\item ratio exactness, requiring every directed ratio in $R$ to expose its $\RatUp$ numerator, denominator, sign, and nonzero-side rows before the product comparison is read;
\item real-seal handoff, requiring the $\RealUp$ row $Q$ to be downstream of $R$ and never a replacement for the rational ratio ledger;
\item Ceva companion separation, recording that \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ceva-namecert} supplies a concurrency route while this packet supplies the transversal-collinearity route;
\item ledger non-escape, excluding hidden projective models, selected charts, host orientation, quotient real equality, countable choice, and detached ambient completeness.
\end{enumerate}
No Menelaus obligation is stored outside $A,P,L,I,R,Q,H,C,G,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:menelaus-theorem-carrier}. The ten rows list the triangle, projective boundary, transversal, intersections, directed ratios, real readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data available to the certificate.

Incidence exactness is forced before ratio formation. The transversal row $L$ and side-intersection row $I$ can be read only against the affine triangle $A$ and the projective incidence boundary $P$, so no ratio row is formed from an untracked host line.

The ratio ledger is then local to $R$. Each directed ratio exposes rational numerator, denominator, sign, and nonzero-side rows before the terminal product comparison reaches $Q$.

Finally compare with the Ceva companion. The provenance row $G$ points to the concurrency certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ceva-namecert}, while the present packet keeps the transversal-collinearity route separate. Since $Q,H,C,G,N$ only read, transport, replay, source, and name the displayed route, the certificate exports none of the refused hidden projective, chart-selection, quotient-real, choice, or ambient-completeness data.
\end{proof}
