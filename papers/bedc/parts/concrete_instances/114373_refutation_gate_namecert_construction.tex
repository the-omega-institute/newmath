The $\RefutationGateUp$ packet realizes finite pseudotheory refusal as a concrete NameCert carrier. It reads audit-map rows, claimed discovery rows, structural collision rows, classifier-bit rows, axiom-dependency rows, certificate-debt rows, and taste-boundary rows, then exposes only finite refusal classes rather than a positive admission oracle.

The packet is the finite refusal surface consumed before a pseudotheory or cannot-claim export is read. The pseudotheory refusal packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pseudotheory-refutation-gate-namecert} and the checked-target export block of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cannot-claim-export-gate-namecert} consume the row-local refusal classes recorded here: $\RefutationGateUp$ supplies the claim, collision, classifier, axiom-dependency, certificate-debt, and taste-boundary classes, while those sibling packets read the same classes in pseudotheory and export-grade contexts.

\begin{definition}[RefutationGate carrier]
\label{def:refutation-gate-carrier}
A $\RefutationGateUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(Q,S,B,A,D,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the claim row naming the asserted discovery or theorem-facing packet under audit.
\item $S$ is the structural-DNA collision row, recording synonym or reconstruction evidence against the asserted novelty of $Q$.
\item $B$ is the classifier-bit row, recording classifier-neutral, smoke, or unsupported disagreement evidence.
\item $A$ is the axiom-dependency row, recording source-token axiom use or forbidden transitive kernel dependency.
\item $D$ is the certificate-debt row, recording missing semantic NameCert, ledger membership, scope seal, or row evidence.
\item $T$ is the taste-boundary row, recording that taste-sensitive stability is an admission boundary and not the complement of finite refusal.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for claim, refusal, taste-boundary, continuation, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from $Q$ through $S,B,A,D,T$ into the public refusal class.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RefutationGateUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RefutationGateUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports finite refusal classes only. It contains no positive discovery oracle, no hidden taste evaluator, and no permission to infer admission from the absence of displayed failures.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RefutationGate NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:refutation-gate-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RefutationGateUp$ carrier $R=(Q,S,B,A,D,T,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RefutationGateUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the claim, structural collision, classifier-bit, axiom-dependency, certificate-debt, taste-boundary, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows;
\item refusal coverage, requiring every public refusal class to be read from one of $S,B,A,D$ or from the taste-boundary row $T$;
\item non-admission boundary, requiring the packet to distinguish finite refusal from positive taste admission;
\item transport stability, requiring $H$ and $C$ to preserve the same refusal class under accepted source transport;
\item no-hidden-oracle boundary, excluding global discovery certification, host taste judgment, and unledgered proof repair as packet fields.
\end{enumerate}
No local obligation is stored outside these finite rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:refutation-gate-carrier}. The visible rows are exactly $Q,S,B,A,D,T,H,C,P,N$.

Refusal coverage is a case split over the displayed refusal rows. Structural collision reads from $S$, classifier-bit failure reads from $B$, forbidden axiom dependency reads from $A$, and unpaid certificate data reads from $D$.

The taste-boundary row $T$ supplies the remaining distinction. It says that stability and value admission are not computed by negating the finite refusal classes, so the packet cannot export a positive discovery judgment merely because $S,B,A,D$ are silent.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this same finite route. Since no coordinate contains a positive admission oracle or a hidden proof repair mechanism, such data remain outside the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RefutationGate finite refusal routing]
\label{thm:refutation-gate-finite-refusal-routing}
Let $R=(Q,S,B,A,D,T,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\RefutationGateUp$ carrier. Every refusal exported by $R$ factors through the displayed route
$$
Q \longmapsto S,B,A,D,T \longmapsto C,P,N .
$$
A downstream audit consumer may read a refusal class only from these rows, and may not replace the taste-boundary row by a positive admission rule.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a downstream audit request. The claim under audit is the single row $Q$.

The continuation route then exposes the finite refusal family $S,B,A,D$ together with the taste-boundary row $T$. These rows determine whether the packet reports structural collision, classifier-bit failure, axiom dependency, certificate debt, or taste-boundary non-admission.

The structural rows $C,P,N$ export the selected class, and $H$ preserves it under accepted transport. Because the route never leaves the displayed packet, no consumer can smuggle in an external positive-discovery oracle.
\end{proof}

The Lean TasteGate development for this packet must provide explicit $\mathsf{Nontrivial}\ \RefutationGateUp$ and $\mathsf{FieldFaithful}\ \RefutationGateUp$ instances. Nontriviality separates a claim with a displayed refusal row from the empty audit surface, and field faithfulness requires the classifier to preserve $Q,S,B,A,D,T,H,C,P,N$ as distinct fields before any checked carrier-alignment theorem can certify the gate.
