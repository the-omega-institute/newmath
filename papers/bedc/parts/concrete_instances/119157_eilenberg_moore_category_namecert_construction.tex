\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\EilenbergMooreCategoryUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-eilenberg-moore-category-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Mac Lane's categorical account of monads and the Eilenberg--Moore construction, the $\EilenbergMooreCategoryUp$ packet names the category of displayed algebra objects for a displayed monad. It sits after the categorical carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-category-namecert}, the functorial transport surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-functor-namecert}, the monad packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-monad-namecert}, the Kleisli completion category of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-kleisli-category-namecert}, and the completion-algebra surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-eilenberg-moore-completion-algebra-namecert}. The chapter records the finite NameCert rows by which monad algebras and their homomorphisms become a category-level consumer interface.

The packet is the algebra-object companion to the Kleisli category route. It does not identify algebras by quotient equality, select representatives, construct a host category of algebras, or import ambient equality of algebra maps.

\begin{definition}[Eilenberg--Moore category carrier]
\label{def:eilenberg-moore-category-carrier}
An $\EilenbergMooreCategoryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(B,T,A,S,U,M,H,C,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the base $\CategoryUp$ row, exposing object and hom histories before any algebra row is read.
\item $T$ is the $\MonadUp$ row on the base category, with its endofunctor, unit, and multiplication data displayed as finite BEDC carrier rows.
\item $A$ is the algebra-object row, listing the displayed object on which the monad acts.
\item $S$ is the structure-map row from the monad-applied object back to the algebra object.
\item $U$ is the unit-law row, comparing the unit route followed by $S$ with the displayed identity route on the algebra object.
\item $M$ is the multiplication-law row, comparing the multiplication route followed by $S$ with the route that first applies the monad to $S$ and then applies $S$.
\item $H$ is the algebra-homomorphism row, recording maps between displayed algebra objects whose square with the two structure maps is certified by the same finite carrier route.
\item $C$ is the composition ledger for algebra homomorphisms, including identity homomorphisms and associative composition as displayed $\CategoryUp$ and $\MonadUp$ route comparisons.
\item $R$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport and displayed $\Cont$ replay for $B,T,A,S,U,M,H,C$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EilenbergMooreCategoryUp$, $\CategoryUp$, $\FunctorUp$, $\MonadUp$, $\NatTransUp$, $\CauchyCompletionKleisliCategoryUp$, $\EilenbergMooreCompletionAlgebraUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EilenbergMooreCategoryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,T,A,S,U,M,H,C,R,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient algebras, selected representatives, host equality of algebra maps, ambient category objects, or a collapse of the Eilenberg--Moore route into the Kleisli route.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Eilenberg--Moore category NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:eilenberg-moore-category-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EilenbergMooreCategoryUp$ carrier
$$
E=(B,T,A,S,U,M,H,C,R,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\EilenbergMooreCategoryUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the base category, monad, algebra object, structure map, unit law, multiplication law, algebra homomorphism, composition ledger, transport, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item monad-source exposure, requiring the base category and monad rows $B,T$ before any algebra object or homomorphism can be inspected;
\item algebra-object exposure, requiring $A$ and $S$ to display the object and its structure map before either law row is public;
\item law exactness, requiring $U$ and $M$ to compare only the displayed unit and multiplication routes of the same monad row;
\item homomorphism and composition exactness, requiring $H$ and $C$ to keep algebra maps, identities, and composites inside the displayed algebra rows;
\item structural exhaustion, requiring $R,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name the listed rows without adding quotient algebras, selected representatives, host equality of algebra maps, ambient category objects, or a Kleisli-category identification.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:eilenberg-moore-category-carrier}. The accepted packet exposes exactly the base category, monad, algebra-object, structure-map, law, homomorphism, composition, transport, provenance, and local naming rows.

Monad-source exposure is read from $B$ and $T$. Since the monad is displayed over the same base category row, an algebra consumer cannot begin from a separate host category or from an unrecorded endofunctor.

Algebra-object exposure is read from $A$ and $S$. The object row is available before the structure-map row, and the structure map is the only coordinate that turns the monad-applied object back into the algebra object.

Law exactness is read from $U$ and $M$. The unit law compares the displayed unit route with the identity route, while the multiplication law compares the displayed multiplication route with the two-step action route; both comparisons use the same $B,T,A,S$ rows.

Finally $H,C,R,P,N$ bind maps, composites, transport, provenance, and local naming to the same finite packet. Because no other coordinate occurs in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Eilenberg--Moore category algebra route]
\label{thm:eilenberg-moore-category-algebra-route}
Every consumer that reads an accepted $\EilenbergMooreCategoryUp$ packet as a category of algebra objects factors through the route
$$
\CategoryUp,\ \FunctorUp,\ \MonadUp,\ \NatTransUp,\ \EilenbergMooreCompletionAlgebraUp,\ \CauchyCompletionKleisliCategoryUp .
$$
The object side reads $B,T,A,S,U,M$ before any algebra homomorphism is public, and the arrow side reads $H,C,R,P,N$ before identities or composites can be exported. Thus the packet gives a finite category-level algebra interface without quotient categories, selected algebra representatives, host equality of algebra maps, ambient category equality, or identification with the Kleisli category.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the object side. Projection of the carrier supplies $B$ and $T$ as the base category and monad rows, then $A$ and $S$ as the displayed algebra object and structure map.

The law rows are then forced in order. The unit row $U$ compares the unit action against the displayed identity route, and the multiplication row $M$ compares the two monad-action schedules against the same structure map.

Turn to arrows. The homomorphism row $H$ reads maps only after their source and target algebra rows have exposed the same $B,T,A,S,U,M$ data, and the composition ledger $C$ records identities and composites as displayed category and monad route comparisons.

The dependency route is therefore the advertised sibling route: the base categorical rows come from $\CategoryUp$, $\FunctorUp$, $\MonadUp$, and $\NatTransUp$, while the completion-facing algebra and Kleisli consumers cite \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-eilenberg-moore-completion-algebra-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-completion-kleisli-category-namecert}. The structural rows $R,P,N$ add transport, replay, provenance, and local naming only, so no quotient or host equality coordinate is exported.
\end{proof}
