\begin{theorem}[Effective Cauchy real completion obligation surface]
\label{thm:effective-cauchy-real-completion-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\EffectiveCauchyRealCompletionUp$ carrier
$$
E=(M,W,D,R,S,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\EffectiveCauchyRealCompletionUp}$ surface consists
exactly of five rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed $\BHist$ packet $E$;
\item modulus-window exactness, requiring $M$ to choose the same finite windows read by $W$ and checked by $D$;
\item $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback discipline, requiring $R$ to be produced only from the displayed $M,W,D$ route;
\item $\RealUp$ seal nonescape, requiring the $\CauchyLimitSealUp$ and $\RealUp$ row $S$ to be read only after $R$ is visible;
\item ledger exhaustion through componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ naming.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:effective-cauchy-real-completion-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly the explicit modulus $M$, finite stream window $W$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, regular rational readback $R$, and terminal seal row $S$.

The modulus-window row is exact because $W$ exposes only the entries selected by $M$, while $D$ checks those entries against the requested dyadic tolerance. Thus no Cauchy comparison can be imported from an unlisted stream coordinate.

Next read the $\RegSeqRatUp$ handoff. The row $R$ is downstream of $M,W,D$, so the regular rational readback discipline is the same finite route used in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} and cannot be replaced by host sequence equality.

The seal row is then consumed through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchylimitseal-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. Since $S$ appears only after $R$, the $\RealUp$ endpoint is a routed seal surface rather than a selected limit or a completeness theorem.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ exhaust the ledger. They transport, replay, package, and name the same displayed rows, and the carrier has no coordinate for quotient streams, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, independent limits, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
