\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BoundedVariationRiemannSumUp$}

Following Jordan's bounded-variation theory and the Riemann finite-sum construction, the $\BoundedVariationRiemannSumUp$ packet records the finite partition route already used when integration-facing chapters read a variation ledger and a tagged sum in the same breath. It sits between the bounded-variation certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedvariation-namecert}, the tagged finite-sum certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannsum-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only the common finite partition, variation ledger, tagged summation row, dyadic observations, regular readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming data; it does not assert an integral limit, a supremum over all partitions, a host function quotient, selected tags outside the displayed packet, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Bounded-variation Riemann-sum carrier]
\label{def:bounded-variation-riemann-sum-carrier}
A $\BoundedVariationRiemannSumUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(I,\Pi,V,T,S,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the finite interval row over which all partition, endpoint, variation, and tagged-sum reads are made.
\item $\Pi$ is the finite interval-partition row, carrying ordered endpoints, subinterval cells, and the common mesh ledger used by both the variation and tagged-sum faces.
\item $V$ is the $\BoundedVariationUp$ ledger, read through the finite endpoint and edge-difference rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-boundedvariation-namecert}.
\item $T$ is the tag-containment row, assigning a displayed tag to each cell of $\Pi$ without exporting a choice-selected tag family outside the packet.
\item $S$ is the $\RiemannSumUp$ row, computing the finite tagged sum from $\Pi$, $T$, the displayed value reads, and the cell-width ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-riemannsum-namecert}.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ edge-observation row, recording endpoint differences, tag-value observations, widths, and finite variation or sum arithmetic at the dyadic level.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, repacking the displayed dyadic variation and tagged-sum data as regular rational observations.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, exposed only after $I,\Pi,V,T,S,D,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval, partition, variation, tag, sum, dyadic, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the common partition through
$$
I,\Pi \longmapsto V,T,S,D \longmapsto R \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BoundedVariationRiemannSumUp$, $\BoundedVariationUp$, $\RiemannSumUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedVariationRiemannSumUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for host integrability, a limiting process over all refinements, a supremum variation value, quotient equality of functions, a choice-selected tag family, an ambient measure, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded-variation Riemann-sum NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bounded-variation-riemann-sum-obligations}
For every accepted $\BoundedVariationRiemannSumUp$ carrier $J=(I,\Pi,V,T,S,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BoundedVariationRiemannSumUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed interval, partition, bounded-variation ledger, tag-containment row, finite Riemann-sum row, dyadic observation row, regular readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item common finite-partition exposure, requiring both the $\BoundedVariationUp$ ledger $V$ and the $\RiemannSumUp$ row $S$ to read the same interval row $I$ and partition row $\Pi$ before either face is consumed;
\item dyadic edge and tag compatibility, requiring the edge-difference observations, tag-value reads, widths, variation arithmetic, and finite-sum arithmetic in $D$ to be attached to the same cells of $\Pi$;
\item $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, requiring $R$ to be obtained only from the displayed variation and tagged-sum dyadic rows rather than from a hidden sequence or external function;
\item $\RealUp$ seal non-escape, requiring $E$ to be downstream from $R$ and to export no host integrability theorem, limiting process over refinements, supremum over partitions, quotient function equality, choice-selected tag family, ambient measure, or completeness principle.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bounded-variation-riemann-sum-carrier}. The displayed rows are exactly the interval, common partition, bounded-variation ledger, tag-containment data, finite Riemann sum, dyadic observations, regular readback, terminal Real seal, and structural rows.

The common-partition obligation is forced by the order of the carrier. The rows $V$ and $S$ are both downstream from $I$ and $\Pi$, so a consumer cannot read the variation budget from one partition and the tagged sum from another hidden mesh.

The dyadic compatibility obligation is the row $D$. It attaches endpoint differences, tag values, widths, variation arithmetic, and finite-sum arithmetic to the same displayed cells before any regular rational readback is available.

Finally the readback and seal obligations are downstream. The row $R$ repacks only the displayed dyadic data, and the row $E$ is exposed only after $R$. Since no carrier coordinate stores integrability, refinement limits, variation suprema, quotient function equality, external tag choice, measures, or completeness, the five obligations exhaust the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded-variation Riemann-sum finite interface]
\label{thm:bounded-variation-riemann-sum-finite-interface}
Every integration-facing consumer of an accepted $\BoundedVariationRiemannSumUp$ packet reads the finite route
$$
\BoundedVariationUp,\ \RiemannSumUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp
\longmapsto
\RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp
$$
through one displayed interval-partition row $\Pi$. The bounded-variation face supplies the finite endpoint and edge ledger, the Riemann-sum face supplies tags, widths, and finite summands on the same cells, and the regular rational readback and Real seal are downstream from both faces.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the accepted carrier $J=(I,\Pi,V,T,S,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$. The rows $I$ and $\Pi$ are displayed before the variation ledger $V$ and before the tag-containment and finite-sum rows $T,S$.

Read the two analytic faces over that same partition. The bounded-variation face contributes endpoint and edge-difference rows through $V$, while the Riemann-sum face contributes the tag, value, width, and summand rows through $T$ and $S$.

The dyadic row $D$ then records both faces at the finite arithmetic level. Only after that common dyadic ledger has been displayed may the packet expose the regular rational readback $R$ and the terminal Real seal $E$.

Thus integration-facing consumers receive a named finite interface between variation control and tagged summation. The interface contains no hidden limiting process, no ambient integrability theorem, and no external tag or partition selection principle.
\end{proof}
