\begin{definition}[Sequential Cauchy filter carrier]
\label{def:sequential-cauchy-filter-carrier}
A $\SequentialCauchyFilterUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F_{\seq}=(S,B,Q,D,G,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ sequence-window row, exposing exactly the finite index windows inspected by the sequential Cauchy request.
\item $B$ is the $\CauchyFilterBasisUp$ row generated from the displayed tail windows and their common-refinement witnesses.
\item $Q$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ saturation row reached from $B$ before any completion-facing consumer may read filter data.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording the dyadic radius, tail comparison budget, and endpoint scale used by the displayed Cauchy test.
\item $G$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-rational readback row attached to the same sequence windows and dyadic tolerances.
\item $R$ is the sequential route ledger, ordering $S,D,G,B,Q$ so the filter read is visibly generated from sequential evidence.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal boundary available only after the sequential route ledger and filter rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for sequence windows, filter bases, filter saturation, dyadic tolerances, regular readback, route ledger, terminal seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a sequential Cauchy request through $S,D,G,B,Q,R,E$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SequentialCauchyFilterUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\CauchyFilterBasisUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,B,Q,D,G,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, host equality, trichotomy, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or bridge credentials.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Sequential Cauchy filter NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:sequential-cauchy-filter-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SequentialCauchyFilterUp$ carrier $F_{\seq}=(S,B,Q,D,G,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SequentialCauchyFilterUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed eleven-row packet; sequence-window exposure through $S$; filter-basis generation through $B$; Cauchy-filter saturation through $Q$; dyadic tolerance exposure through $D$; regular-rational readback through $G$; sequential route ordering through $R$; terminal Real-seal boundary through $E$; componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$; displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:sequential-cauchy-filter-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly $S,B,Q,D,G,R,E$, and the structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

The sequence data are read first. The window row $S$ exposes the inspected indices, the dyadic row $D$ fixes the tolerance budget, and the regular readback row $G$ records the rational evidence attached to those windows.

The filter rows are downstream of that route. The basis row $B$ is generated from the displayed sequential data, and the saturation row $Q$ is read only after the basis has been shown.

Finally, $R,E,H,C,P,N$ order, seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since quotient streams, selected limits, countable choice, host equality, trichotomy, ambient completeness, and bridge credentials are absent from the carrier, none is a local NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

The filter handoff then passes through $B$ and $Q$. A completion-facing consumer therefore receives the Cauchy-filter row as a saturation of the displayed sequential basis, not as an external filter or quotient stream.
