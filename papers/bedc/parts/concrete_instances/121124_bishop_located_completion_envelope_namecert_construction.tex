\begin{definition}[Bishop located completion envelope carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-completion-envelope-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for rational radii, slack witnesses, and refusal rows.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window schedule read by every Cauchy-name request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback obtained from the displayed stream windows.
\item $L$ is the located classifier row, recording the finite apartness and enclosure answers used to admit a Cauchy name to the envelope.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ completion-service row exposed only after $D,S,R,L$ have been displayed.
\item $I$ is the $\DirectedSetUp$ request-index row for net-style or filter-style completion consumers that need a finite common-window witness.

\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the dyadic, stream, regular-rational, located, completion, directed-index, Real-seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located completion envelope NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-completion-envelope-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\BishopLocatedCompletionEnvelopeUp$ carrier $E=(D,S,R,L,M,I,A,T,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedCompletionEnvelopeUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following five obligation families:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier envelope admission through the displayed packet $D,S,R,L,M,I,A,T,C,P,N$;
\item located Cauchy-name classification through the finite dyadic, stream-window, regular-rational, and located rows $D,S,R,L$;
\item componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $T$ for every displayed row;
\item finite completion ledger exposure through $M,I,A,C,P,N$;
\item a no-choice boundary excluding quotient completion, selected limits, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, countable-choice schedules, trichotomy, and host equality.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is the tuple of \autoref{def:bishop-located-completion-envelope-carrier}. Every row consumed by a completion-facing reader is one of $D,S,R,L,M,I,A,T,C,P,N$, so the carrier envelope is finite and visible.

The located Cauchy-name classifier is confined to $D,S,R,L$. The dyadic ledger supplies tolerance requests, the stream row supplies finite observations, the regular rational row supplies readback, and the located row records the finite apartness or enclosure answer.

The completion ledger is then read through $M,I,A,C,P,N$. The metric-completion row and directed-index row expose only the finite request surface that reaches the terminal Real seal, while replay, provenance, and local naming keep the route inspectable.

Finally the carrier contains no coordinate for quotient completion, selected limits, ambient Real completeness, countable choice, trichotomy, or host equality. Componentwise transport through $T$ preserves the displayed rows only, so none of those excluded principles can be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located Cauchy-name classifier]

$$

The structural rows $T,C,P,N$ transport and replay the same route. They do not add a hidden selector, quotient representative, or ambient completion proof.
