\begin{theorem}[PinGroup Clifford-Spin sibling route]
\label{thm:pin-group-clifford-sibling-route}
Every accepted $\PinGroupUp$ carrier consumes the $\CliffordUp$ multiplication and quadratic-form rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-clifford-namecert}, then consumes the $\SpinGroupUp$ even-subgroup route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-spingroup-namecert}, before it exports the reflection-cover row of the local $\NameCert_{\PinGroupUp}$ surface. Thus the Pin packet is a finite Clifford reflection-cover extension of the Spin route, not an independent orthogonal-group carrier or a host matrix representation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier to its Clifford-facing rows. The multiplication row and quadratic-form row are read first, so reflection products are typed inside the displayed $\CliffordUp$ sibling surface.

Next project to the Spin-facing rows. The even-subgroup route is the displayed $\SpinGroupUp$ handoff, and it is consumed before any reflection-cover row is exported.

The local $\NameCert_{\PinGroupUp}$ row packages these sibling reads with the parity classifier, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, and $\Pkg$ provenance. Since no carrier coordinate stores an ambient orthogonal group, a host matrix model, or a quotient Clifford algebra, every accepted Pin read factors through the Clifford rows and the Spin route before the reflection-cover row becomes public.
\end{proof}
