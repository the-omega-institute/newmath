$$

\item $J$ is the $\RiemannIntegralUp$ row for the approximants, exposing only tagged-mesh, Darboux-gap, and integral-readback data already present in the Riemann packet;

\begin{theorem}[Uniform limit integral NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-limit-integral-namecert-obligations}
