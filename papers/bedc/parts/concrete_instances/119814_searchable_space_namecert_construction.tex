\begin{definition}[Searchable space carrier]
\label{def:searchable-space-carrier}
A $\SearchableSpaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(W,Q,F,K,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is the search schedule row, read through $\StreamNameUp$ finite windows and bounded fan-functional branches when the source is fan-like.
\item $Q$ is the predicate readback row, opening only the displayed decidable predicate observations requested by $W$.
\item $F$ is the finite witness row, recording the retained candidate list, the accepted witness when one is displayed, and the refusal of a hidden global selector.
\item $K$ is the compact-search handoff row, allowing $\CompactMetricUp$, $\SearchableCompactUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ consumers to read the same finite search route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $W,Q,F,K$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from schedule to predicate readback, from predicate readback to finite witness, and from finite witness to compact-search handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SearchableSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\FanFunctionalUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\SearchableCompactUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SearchableSpaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $W,Q,F,K,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a detached selection functional, an unbounded search over an ambient set, quotient equality of predicates, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Searchable space NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:searchable-space-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SearchableSpaceUp$ carrier
$$
S=(W,Q,F,K,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SearchableSpaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of five obligation rows: schedule exposure through $W$, predicate readback through $Q$, finite witness or refusal exposure through $F$, compact-search handoff through $K$, and structural certification through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier of \autoref{def:searchable-space-carrier}. The schedule row $W$ is the only source of search windows, so every predicate read is bounded by the displayed stream or fan-functional route.

Read the predicate and witness rows next. The predicate row $Q$ exposes only the decidable observations requested by $W$, while $F$ records either the retained finite witness boundary or the refusal of a hidden selector.

Now hand the finite search route to compact and real-facing consumers through $K$. This handoff may cite $\CompactMetricUp$, $\SearchableCompactUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$, but it carries only the same schedule, predicate, and witness rows.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the packet. Since the carrier has no coordinate for detached selection, unbounded ambient search, quotient predicate equality, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient real completeness, the five listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
