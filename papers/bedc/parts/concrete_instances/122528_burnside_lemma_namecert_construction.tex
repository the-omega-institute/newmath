\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\BurnsideLemmaUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-burnside-lemma-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Burnside's finite group-action counting theorem, the $\BurnsideLemmaUp$ packet records the orbit-count formula as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits over the finite group source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert} and consumes the stabilizer and fixed-orbit accounting route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-orbit-stabilizer-namecert}. The certificate names fixed-point tables and orbit counts as displayed finite ledgers, not as quotient orbit sets, selected representatives, or an ambient enumeration theorem.

\begin{definition}[Burnside lemma carrier]
\label{def:burnside-lemma-carrier}
A $\BurnsideLemmaUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(G,X,A,L,F,O,K,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is the finite $\GroupUp$ action source row, exposing the group operation, unit, inverse, and finite multiplication table used by the action.
\item $X$ is the acted finite set/list carrier row, exposing a duplicate-controlled finite list of acted points together with its local classifier.
\item $A$ is the finite action row, displaying the unit-action and multiplication-action replay laws over $G$ and $X$.
\item $L$ is the finite group-list ledger, listing the group elements whose fixed-point rows are inspected.
\item $F$ is the fixed-point table, assigning to each displayed element of $L$ the finite sublist of points of $X$ fixed by that element.
\item $O$ is the orbit-count row, recording the displayed count of action orbits as the averaged fixed-point ledger named by $F$ and $L$.
\item $K$ is the Nat/List count ledger, exposing finite lengths, duplicate control, summation, divisibility, and the final natural-number orbit count.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the group, acted list, action, group-list ledger, fixed-point table, orbit-count row, Nat/List count ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from $\GroupUp$ and $X$ through $A,L,F,O,K,H,P,N$ into $\BurnsideLemmaUp$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\GroupUp$, $\OrbitStabilizerUp$, finite action rows, fixed-point rows, Nat/List ledgers, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BurnsideLemmaUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate admits exactly these rows. It has no coordinate for quotient orbit sets, selected orbit representatives, external cardinal arithmetic, an infinite group action, cycle-index polynomials, Polya enumeration, or host equality of quotient types.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Burnside lemma NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:burnside-lemma-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\BurnsideLemmaUp$ carrier
$$
T=(G,X,A,L,F,O,K,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BurnsideLemmaUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed finite $\GroupUp$ source, finite set/list carrier, action row, group-list ledger, fixed-point table, orbit-count row, Nat/List count ledger, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted packet onto its eleven displayed rows. The group source, acted finite list, action law, group-list ledger, fixed-point table, orbit-count row, and Nat/List count ledger are present before the local name can be read.

The fixed-point table is indexed by the displayed group-list ledger $L$, so it cannot be replaced by an external class function. The orbit-count row is read only after the fixed-point table and Nat/List ledger have exposed the finite average, so it cannot be replaced by a quotient orbit object or selected representative list.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed rows. Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient orbit sets, external cardinal arithmetic, infinite actions, cycle-index polynomials, Polya enumeration, or host equality of quotient types, no local NameCert obligation can depend on those objects.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Burnside lemma orbit-count route]
\label{thm:burnside-lemma-orbit-count-route}
Let $T=(G,X,A,L,F,O,K,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\BurnsideLemmaUp$ carrier. Every orbit-count read exported by $T$ factors through the finite route
$$
\GroupUp \longmapsto X \longmapsto A \longmapsto L \longmapsto F \longmapsto K \longmapsto O \longmapsto (H,C,P,N).
$$
The stabilizer and fixed-orbit accounting consumed from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-orbit-stabilizer-namecert} remains a finite ledger over the same action row from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-group-namecert}; it is not a quotient orbit carrier, selected representative family, cycle-index polynomial, Polya enumeration surface, or ambient finite-action category.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the group and acted-list rows $G$ and $X$, then read the action row $A$. The group-list ledger $L$ enumerates only the displayed elements whose fixed points are inspected, and the table $F$ records those fixed points as finite sublists of $X$.

The Nat/List ledger $K$ is the only row where lengths, summation, divisibility, and the averaged fixed-point count are exported. The orbit-count row $O$ is therefore downstream of the fixed-point table and the finite count ledger.

The dependency on \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-orbit-stabilizer-namecert} supplies stabilizer and fixed-orbit accounting for the same action surface. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve that row order before $P$ and $N$ package the local certificate. Thus every consumer sees the finite Burnside ledger, while quotient orbit carriers, selected representatives, cycle-index polynomials, Polya enumeration, and ambient action categories remain outside the packet.
\end{proof}
