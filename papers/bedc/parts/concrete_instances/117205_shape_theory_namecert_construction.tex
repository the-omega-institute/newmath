\chapter{Shape Theory NameCert Construction}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-shape-theory-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Borsuk and Mardesic--Segal, the $\ShapeTheoryUp$ packet encodes compact-metric shape through finite pro-homotopy expansion rows. It sits beside the compact metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the hyperspace packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperspace-namecert}, the absolute-neighborhood-retract packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-absolute-neighborhood-retract-namecert}, and the homotopy carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-homotopy-namecert}. The chapter records the finite expansion, comparison, and nonescape rows that a shape consumer may inspect, not a host pro-category, quotient compactum, selected inverse system, or ambient homotopy classifier.

\begin{definition}[Shape theory carrier]
\label{def:shape-theory-carrier}
A $\ShapeTheoryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(K,H,A,T,E,M,R,U,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the compact metric source row, read through the $\CompactMetricUp$ surface.
\item $H$ is the hyperspace row recording finite compact-subset windows used by expansion stages.
\item $A$ is the absolute-neighborhood-retract row naming the finite ANR approximants admitted by the packet.
\item $T$ is the homotopy row recording finite homotopy comparisons between adjacent approximants.
\item $E$ is the expansion-index row, listing the finite prefix of pro-homotopy stages visible to the consumer.
\item $M$ is the bonding-map row, recording the displayed maps and homotopy commutation checks inside that prefix.
\item $R$ is the refinement row, pairing later expansion prefixes with earlier displayed stages.
\item $U$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact-metric, hyperspace, ANR, homotopy, expansion, bonding-map, refinement, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a compact-metric read through hyperspace, ANR, and homotopy rows before a shape comparison is exposed.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ShapeTheoryUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\HyperspaceUp$, $\AbsoluteNeighborhoodRetractUp$, $\HomotopyUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ShapeTheoryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no coordinate for an unbounded pro-object, host compactum quotient, arbitrary shape category, selected inverse-system representative, or ambient homotopy equivalence outside the displayed prefix.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Shape theory NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:shape-theory-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ShapeTheoryUp$ carrier $S=(K,H,A,T,E,M,R,U,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ShapeTheoryUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact-metric admission, hyperspace-window admission, ANR approximant admission, finite homotopy comparison, expansion-prefix exposure, bonding-map exactness, refinement-row visibility, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local naming through the displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:shape-theory-carrier}. The compact metric row $K$ and hyperspace row $H$ name the compact source and finite compact-subset windows before any shape read is available.

The ANR row $A$ and homotopy row $T$ supply only displayed approximants and finite comparison data. The expansion row $E$, bonding row $M$, and refinement row $R$ bind those comparisons into a finite pro-homotopy prefix.

Transport, replay, provenance, and naming through $U,C,P,N$ preserve that same prefix. Since no carrier coordinate stores a host pro-category, quotient compactum, selected inverse system, or ambient homotopy classifier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Shape theory pro-homotopy handoff]
\label{thm:shape-theory-pro-homotopy-handoff}
Every shape comparison read from an accepted $\ShapeTheoryUp$ packet factors through the compact-metric source, hyperspace windows, ANR approximant rows, homotopy comparison rows, expansion prefix, bonding-map row, refinement row, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. The handoff is finite pro-homotopy NameCert data, not an ambient pro-category theorem or an unrestricted shape-equivalence principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the source through $K$ and $H$, then expose ANR approximants through $A$. Homotopy comparisons are visible only through $T$.

The expansion prefix $E$ determines which stages are in scope. The bonding and refinement rows $M,R$ then route every comparison through a displayed finite row before it reaches a consumer.

The structural rows $U,C,P,N$ transport and replay the same data. Thus the exported handoff remains the finite pro-homotopy prefix named by the packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Shape theory nonescape]
\label{thm:shape-theory-nonescape}
An accepted $\ShapeTheoryUp$ carrier exports no host compactum quotient, selected inverse system, arbitrary pro-object equality, open-cover compactness theorem, or ambient homotopy classifier. Any consumer that cites a shape comparison must exhibit the rows $K,H,A,T,E,M,R,U,C,P,N$ of the same packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The only comparison rows in \autoref{def:shape-theory-carrier} are $T,E,M,R$ together with the compact, hyperspace, and ANR sources $K,H,A$.

No row stores a host quotient, selected inverse system, arbitrary pro-object equality, open-cover compactness proof, or ambient classifier. Therefore any such read is outside the carrier unless it is first reduced to the displayed rows and replayed through $U,C,P,N$.
\end{proof}
