\begin{theorem}[Compactly generated weak Hausdorff NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compactly-generated-weak-hausdorff-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compactly-generated-weak-hausdorff-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly $T,K,O,D,W$, and the structural rows are exactly $H,C,P,N$.

The compact-generation side is therefore read from $K$ and $O$. The first row records compact probes over the source topology, while the second records the compact-open window ledger used by function-space consumers.

The separation side is read from $D$. It is a diagonal-separation row attached to the same compact-source tests, so it cannot provide host equality of points or an ambient Hausdorff theorem outside the carrier.

Finally, transport, replay, provenance, and naming are supplied by $H,C,P,N$ for those same rows. Since the packet has no further coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

The diagonal-separation datum is then forced through $D$. Because $D$ is a row of the same packet, it is read with the finite inspection window $W$ rather than by consulting an external diagonal predicate.

The remaining rows bind the read to the accepted packet. Componentwise transport and displayed replay preserve the route through $H$ and $C$, while provenance and local naming record the same dependency through $P$ and $N$.
