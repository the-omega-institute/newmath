\begin{definition}[Local-global zero exclusion certificate carrier]
\label{def:local-global-zero-exclusion-certificate-carrier}
A $\LocalGlobalZeroExclusionCertificateUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(C,F,A,Z,K,T,G,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the critical-strip carrier row being tested.
\item $F$ is the fixed-half comparison row naming the mirror-fixed surface.
\item $A$ is the apartness witness row separating $C$ from $F$ by displayed positive rational data.
\item $Z$ is the zero-ledger request row whose consumption is blocked until $A$ has been exposed.
\item $K$ is the zeta-continuation application row used to read the completed zeta carrier.
\item $T$ is the prime-window recursive tower row supplying local source compatibility.
\item $G$ is the global compatibility ledger tying the local window rows to the completed carrier.

\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the critical-strip request through the apartness witness, zero-ledger gate, zeta-continuation application, tower row, and compatibility ledger.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocalGlobalZeroExclusionCertificateUp$, $\FixedHalfZeroWitnessUp$, the zeta-continuation application route, the prime-window recursive tower, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocalGlobalZeroExclusionCertificateUp}$ row.

The carrier has no coordinate for a zero row consumed before apartness, a selected off-fixed zero, a hidden completed-carrier equality, host analytic continuation, or ambient RH completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Local-global zero exclusion carrier certificate]
\label{thm:local-global-zero-exclusion-carrier-certificate}
For every accepted $\LocalGlobalZeroExclusionCertificateUp$ carrier $E=(C,F,A,Z,K,T,G,H,R,P,N)$, a consumer may consume the zero-ledger request row $Z$ only after the apartness witness row $A$ is displayed against the fixed-half comparison row $F$. The accepted route then reads the zeta-continuation application row $K$, prime-window recursive tower row $T$, compatibility ledger $G$, componentwise transport $H$, replay $R$, provenance $P$, and local $\NameCert_{\LocalGlobalZeroExclusionCertificateUp}$ row $N$ before any local-global zero exclusion claim is public. The packet exports no selected off-fixed zero, hidden carrier equality, host analytic continuation theorem, or ambient RH completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:local-global-zero-exclusion-certificate-carrier}. The rows $C,F,A,Z,K,T,G,H,R,P,N$ are the entire public surface of the packet.

The apartness row $A$ is the gate for the zero-ledger request $Z$. Thus a consumer that attempts to read $Z$ without first displaying $A$ and the fixed-half comparison row $F$ has left the accepted carrier.

After that gate, replay through $R$ fixes the remaining order: the zeta-continuation application row $K$, prime-window recursive tower row $T$, and compatibility ledger $G$ are read before transport, provenance, and local naming. Since no carrier coordinate stores a selected off-fixed zero, hidden completed-carrier equality, host analytic continuation theorem, or ambient RH completeness, the certificate is exactly the finite non-escape route stated above.
\end{proof}
