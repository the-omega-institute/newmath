Following Bourbaki and Mac Lane, the $\SymmetricAlgebraUp$ packet records the free commutative algebra generated by a displayed module or vector-space carrier through finite tensor words modulo displayed permutation-stability rows. It is grounded in the scalar ring rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ring-namecert}, the module carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-module-namecert}, the vector-space specialization of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-vecspace-namecert}, the finite word source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tensor-algebra-namecert}, the alternating companion of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-exterior-algebra-namecert}, and the coefficient-list consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-namecert}. The packet is a BEDC NameCert carrier for finite symmetric words and polynomial handoff rows; it is not a host quotient algebra, selected monomial basis, infinite direct sum, or ambient commutative algebra object.

\begin{definition}[Symmetric algebra carrier]
\label{def:symmetric-algebra-carrier}
A $\SymmetricAlgebraUp$ carrier over a displayed $\ModuleUp$ row $V$ and scalar $\RingUp$ row $R$ is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(R,V,T,W,\Pi,M,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the scalar $\RingUp$ source row, and $V$ is the $\ModuleUp$ source row; when the scalar row is a field, the same endpoint source may be read through $\VecSpaceUp$.
\item $T$ is the consumed $\TensorAlgebraUp$ finite-word row over $R$ and $V$.
\item $W$ is the finite symmetric-word ledger. Each entry is a displayed tensor word together with the finite multiset of carried letters it represents.
\item $\Pi$ is the finite permutation-stability ledger. For every listed adjacent swap or finite permutation of a listed word, $\Pi$ records the classifier comparison that keeps the word in the same symmetric class.
\item $M$ is the commutative product row. It multiplies two displayed symmetric words by tensor-word concatenation through $T$ and then reads the result through $W$ and $\Pi$, so the two product orders are classified by the same permutation-stability surface.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for scalar rows, source letters, tensor words, symmetric-word rows, permutation-stability rows, commutative product rows, and local classifiers.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from source endpoints through tensor-word formation, symmetric-word admission, permutation stability, and commutative multiplication.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SymmetricAlgebraUp$, $\TensorAlgebraUp$, $\ExteriorAlgebraUp$, $\PolynomialUp$, $\ModuleUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\RingUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SymmetricAlgebraUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only finite symmetric tensor-word data and the displayed commutative product handoff. It does not add a quotient primitive, selected monomial basis, host polynomial syntax, infinite direct sum, or ambient algebra-library object.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Symmetric algebra NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:symmetric-algebra-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SymmetricAlgebraUp$ carrier
$$
S=(R,V,T,W,\Pi,M,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SymmetricAlgebraUp}$ surface consists exactly of scalar admission through $R$, module or vector-space endpoint admission through $V$, finite tensor-word admission through $T$, symmetric-word admission through $W$, finite permutation-stability admission through $\Pi$, commutative product admission through $M$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows of \autoref{def:symmetric-algebra-carrier}. The scalar, source-letter, tensor-word, symmetric-word, permutation-stability, and product rows are displayed before a consumer receives a commutative algebra operation.

The tensor source is read through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-tensor-algebra-namecert}. Thus every symmetric word first appears as a finite tensor word, and only then passes through the symmetric-word ledger $W$ and the permutation-stability ledger $\Pi$.

The commutative product row $M$ is also visible data. Multiplying two symmetric words means concatenating their tensor representatives and applying the listed permutation-stability rows, so commutativity is recorded by the packet rather than supplied by a host quotient algebra.

Transport by $H$, replay by $C$, provenance by $P$, and naming by $N$ preserve exactly the displayed packet. Because the carrier has no quotient coordinate, selected monomial basis, host polynomial syntax, infinite direct sum, or ambient commutative algebra endpoint, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Symmetric algebra universal polynomial route]
\label{thm:symmetric-algebra-universal-polynomial-route}
Every polynomial-style consumer accepted by a $\SymmetricAlgebraUp$ packet must read a finite symmetric word through
$$
R,V,T,W,\Pi,M,H,C,P,N.
$$
The route first consumes the scalar $\RingUp$ row and the $\ModuleUp$ or $\VecSpaceUp$ source row, then the $\TensorAlgebraUp$ word row, then the finite symmetric-word and permutation-stability ledgers, and finally the commutative product row. The consumer receives a finite polynomial handoff compatible with \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-namecert}; it does not receive quotient algebra equality, selected monomial coordinates, host polynomial syntax, arbitrary symmetric powers, exterior alternating rows, or ambient algebra completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:symmetric-algebra-namecert-obligations}. It places $T,W,\Pi,M$ after the scalar and module or vector endpoints and before any polynomial-style read is exported.

For a degree-one word, the route is the source row $V$ with transport and replay supplied by $H$ and $C$. For a longer word, read the finite tensor word through $T$, then read all listed adjacent swaps and finite permutations through $\Pi$ before the word is admitted by $W$.

Finally inspect the product row $M$. The two possible orders of multiplying displayed symmetric words differ only by a permutation row already present in $\Pi$, so the polynomial consumer receives a commutative finite-word route rather than a quotient object. The exterior companion of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-exterior-algebra-namecert} uses signed transposition and repeated-letter refusal; this packet instead keeps ordinary permutation stability and exports no alternating boundary.
\end{proof}
