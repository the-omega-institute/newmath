Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\RealNameGaugeUp$ packet records finite precision gauges for real names before a completed real object is exposed. It sits beside the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the located comparison service of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-locator-namecert}. The packet names the tolerance-window discipline a consumer must inspect before treating a finite read as Real-facing data; it does not construct a quotient real, decide host equality, choose a completed representative, or assume ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[RealNameGauge carrier]
\label{def:real-name-gauge-carrier}
A $\RealNameGaugeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(T,D,S,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following visible rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the tolerance-window ledger, listing the finite precision requests currently admitted by the gauge.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ radius row attached to each displayed tolerance window, including the dyadic comparison and refinement data used by the request.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ request row through which the named real is queried at those finite tolerances.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row serving the same stream requests with regular rational observations before any $\RealUp$ seal is visible.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for tolerance windows, dyadic radii, stream requests, regular readbacks, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a tolerance request to its dyadic radius, stream read, regular rational readback, and Real-facing seal boundary.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealNameGaugeUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\RealLocatorUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealNameGaugeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for quotient-real equality, host equality of real names, trichotomy, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RealNameGauge window obligation]
\label{thm:real-name-gauge-window-obligation}
For every accepted $\RealNameGaugeUp$ carrier $G=(T,D,S,Q,H,C,P,N)$, a gauge read must expose the monotone tolerance windows in $T$, their dyadic radii in $D$, the stream requests in $S$, and the regular rational readbacks in $Q$ before any $\RealUp$ seal may be consumed. The exported surface is the finite gauge route through $T,D,S,Q,H,C,P,N$; it exports no quotient real, host equality, trichotomy, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-name-gauge-carrier}. The tolerance windows, dyadic radii, stream requests, regular readbacks, transports, replay routes, provenance, and local naming rows are the whole visible packet.

Read the precision discipline through $T$ and $D$. A later tolerance window may refine an earlier one only by displaying the dyadic-radius row attached to the same gauge request, so monotonicity is part of the finite window ledger rather than a host property of real names.

Next read $S$ and $Q$. The stream request and regular rational readback are consumed before the Real-facing seal boundary, and the replay row $C$ preserves that order under continuation.

Finally $H,P,N$ transport, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no quotient-real, host-equality, trichotomy, selected-limit, choice-schedule, or completeness coordinate, none can be exported by the accepted gauge read.
\end{proof}
