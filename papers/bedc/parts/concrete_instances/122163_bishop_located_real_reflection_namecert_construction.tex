\begin{definition}[Bishop located real reflection carrier]
\label{def:bishop-located-real-reflection-carrier}
A $\BishopLocatedRealReflectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(A,B,F,S,Q,D,R,U,V,H,C,P,N)
$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the $\LocatedRealUp$ source row, exposing only the located real-name observations admitted by the packet.
\item $B$ is the $\ApartnessRealUp$ comparison row used when reflection consumers request positive separation data.
\item $F$ is the $\CompletionReflectionUp$ boundary row, recording that located data are read through the displayed completion-reflection surface.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row through which all source observations are scheduled.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row attached to those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row that bounds the located and reflected reads.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $A,B,F,S,Q,D$ remain visible.
\item $U$ is the unit row, recording the displayed entry of located real data into the reflection route.
\item $V$ is the counit row, recording the displayed return from the completion-reflection boundary to the Real seal route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the located, apartness, reflection, stream, readback, tolerance, seal, unit, counit, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopLocatedRealReflectionUp$, $\LocatedRealUp$, $\ApartnessRealUp$, $\CompletionReflectionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopLocatedRealReflectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The accepted carrier is exactly the displayed packet $L$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected completed points, host real equality, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or an external reflection theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located real reflection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-located-real-reflection-namecert-obligations}

A consumer can therefore read the reflection packet only through
$$
A,B,F,S,Q,D,R,U,V,H,C,P,N .
$$
No local obligation may call on quotient stream equality, a selected limit, host real equality, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or a detached reflection principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-located-real-reflection-carrier}. The accepted tuple displays exactly the located-real source, apartness comparison, completion-reflection boundary, stream-window, regular-readback, dyadic-tolerance, seal, unit, counit, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Read the analytic rows first. The located row $A$ and apartness row $B$ are scheduled through $S$, interpreted by the regular readback row $Q$, and bounded by the dyadic tolerance row $D$ before the terminal seal $R$ is public.

The reflection row $F$ and the unit-counit rows $U,V$ are attached to that same finite route. Componentwise transport in $H$, replay in $C$, provenance in $P$, and local naming in $N$ preserve the displayed carrier, so no excluded completion, equality, choice, or host-side principle is exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop located real reflection unit-counit route]
\label{thm:bishop-located-real-reflection-unit-counit-route}

\begin{proof}

Next read the reflection boundary. The row $F$ is the only completion-reflection coordinate in the packet, and the unit row $U$ records entry into that route without choosing a representative or completed point.
