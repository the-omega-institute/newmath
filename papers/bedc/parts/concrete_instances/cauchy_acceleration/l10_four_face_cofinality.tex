\begin{theorem}[Cauchy acceleration L10 four-face cofinality]
\label{thm:cauchy-acceleration-l10-four-face-cofinality}
Every accepted $\CauchyAccelerationUp$ carrier that exports an accelerated $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback or terminal $\RealUp$ seal preserves the ordered L10 four-face route
$$
\StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \DyadicRatCoreUp \longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp \longrightarrow \RealUp .
$$
The cofinal acceleration ledger may refine which displayed source window is read for a requested precision, but it cannot commute, omit, or replace the $\StreamNameUp$ source face, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance face, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback face, or the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. The resulting route remains finite and displayed, with no chosen subsequence outside the ledger, quotient representative, ambient completion row, host real equality, countable-choice schedule, Lean verification, or standard bridge.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:cauchy-acceleration-l10-handoff}. It names the only exported analytic route of an accepted carrier as the ordered sequence from the stream window through the dyadic tolerance ledger and regular rational readback to the Real-facing seal.

Next apply \autoref{thm:cauchy-acceleration-rectangular-window-route}. The cofinal ledger $\alpha$ refines precision requests inside the displayed rectangle of source windows and dyadic tolerances, so it cannot move the $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ rows ahead of the upstream faces.

Finally use the sibling dependency route of \autoref{thm:cauchy-acceleration-cauchy-subsequence-route}. The rate-dominance and fast-modulus surfaces are consumed before the accelerated readback is exported, and they leave the same $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$ faces in order.

The carrier and dependency route have no row for a hidden subsequence, quotient representative, ambient completion proof, host Real equality, choice schedule, Lean verification, or bridge credential. Hence cofinal acceleration preserves exactly the displayed L10 four-face route.
\end{proof}
