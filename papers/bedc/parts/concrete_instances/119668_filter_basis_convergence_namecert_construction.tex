\begin{definition}[FilterBasisConvergence carrier]
\label{def:filter-basis-convergence-carrier}
A $\FilterBasisConvergenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(B,F,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\FilterLimitBasisUp$ row exposing the finite basis route already accepted by limit and completion consumers.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ source row whose accepted reads remain tied to displayed finite basis data.
\item $L$ is the $\CauchyFilterLimitUp$ convergence-boundary row, read only after $B$ and $F$ have been displayed.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row inspected by the convergence request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the displayed convergence windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used to compare the readback at the requested precision.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $B,F,L,W,R,D$ have been replayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for basis, filter, limit, window, readback, tolerance, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a convergence request through $B,F,L,W,R,D,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FilterBasisConvergenceUp$, $\FilterLimitBasisUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\CauchyFilterLimitUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FilterBasisConvergenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $B,F,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$. There is no coordinate for quotient filters, ultrafilters, selected limits, countable-choice schedules, host filter equality, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[FilterBasisConvergence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:filter-basis-convergence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FilterBasisConvergenceUp$ carrier $K=(B,F,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FilterBasisConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of these obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed filter-limit basis row, Cauchy-filter source row, convergence-boundary row, finite stream window, regular rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, terminal Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming row;
\item basis exposure, requiring every convergence-facing read to consume $B$ and $F$ before it consults $L,W,R,D$, or $E$;
\item convergence-window compatibility, requiring the $\CauchyFilterLimitUp$ row $L$ to use the same displayed basis, window, readback, and dyadic tolerance route;
\item Real handoff, requiring the terminal seal $E$ to remain downstream of $B,F,L,W,R,D$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$;
\item ledger refusal, excluding quotient filters, ultrafilters, selected limits, countable-choice schedules, host filter equality, host real equality, ambient completeness, and bridge certificates.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:filter-basis-convergence-carrier}. The displayed rows are exactly $B,F,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$, so carrier admission exposes only finite basis, filter, convergence-boundary, window, readback, tolerance, seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming data.

The convergence route starts with $B$ and $F$. The filter-limit basis row and the Cauchy-filter row force the request to pass through accepted finite basis data before any convergence-facing row can be inspected.

Next read $L,W,R,D$. The convergence boundary consumes the same finite stream windows, regular rational readback, and dyadic tolerance ledger, so the packet has one displayed basis-convergence route rather than two unrelated filter presentations.

Finally the terminal seal $E$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route. Since quotient filters, ultrafilters, selected limits, countable-choice schedules, host equality, ambient completeness, and bridge certificates are absent from the carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the local surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[FilterBasisConvergence L10 handoff]
\label{thm:filter-basis-convergence-l10-handoff}
Every accepted convergence-facing consumer of a $\FilterBasisConvergenceUp$ carrier factors through the finite route
$$
\FilterLimitBasisUp,\;\CauchyFilterUp,\;\CauchyFilterLimitUp
\longmapsto
\StreamNameUp,\;\RegSeqRatUp,\;\DyadicRatCoreUp
\longmapsto
\RealUp
$$
before terminal Real evidence is read. The route uses the displayed rows $B,F,L,W,R,D,E,H,C,P,N$ of the same packet and cannot import quotient filters, ultrafilters, selected limits, countable choice, host filter equality, host real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a convergence-facing consumer of $K$. The carrier admits the $\FilterLimitBasisUp$ row $B$ only together with the source filter row $F$ and the convergence-boundary row $L$.

Replay the displayed continuation row $C$. The request consumes $B$ and $F$, then passes through $L$, the finite windows $W$, the regular readback $R$, and the dyadic tolerance ledger $D$ before the terminal seal $E$ is available.

The structural rows do not create an additional convergence object. Transport through $H$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$ record the same finite route, so every accepted consumer receives only that route before the $\RealUp$ seal.

The excluded objects are not carrier rows. Consequently any consumer using quotient filters, ultrafilters, selected limits, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness has left the accepted $\FilterBasisConvergenceUp$ packet rather than reading its L10 handoff surface.
\end{proof}
