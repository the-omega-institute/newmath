\begin{definition}[CompactMetricEquicontinuity carrier]
\label{def:compact-metric-equicontinuity-carrier}
A $\CompactMetricEquicontinuityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(K,Y,F,R,M,V,S,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, read through finite net centers, cover radii, and located membership witnesses;
\item $Y$ is the target $\MetricUp$ row whose distance comparisons are used by the family ledger;
\item $F$ is the finite $\ContinuousMapUp$ family row over the source and target rows;
\item $R$ records pointwise continuity radius rows for the displayed family members and source centers;
\item $M$ is the shared equicontinuity modulus candidate read through $\EquicontinuityFamilyUp$;
\item $V$ is the finite-net cover ledger that records which compact-source centers and radii are consumed by the handoff;
\item $S$ is the $\ArzelaAscoliFiniteModulusUp$ handoff row that receives the compact finite-net and shared-modulus data;
\item $A$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ tolerance seal for the displayed target-distance budget;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact source, target metric, family rows, radius rows, modulus candidate, cover ledger, handoff row, tolerance seal, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a requested tolerance through $K,Y,F,R,M,V,A,H,P,N$ to the finite-modulus handoff $S$;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactMetricEquicontinuityUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\EquicontinuityFamilyUp$, $\ArzelaAscoliFiniteModulusUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactMetricEquicontinuityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only these finite rows. It contains no arbitrary open-cover compactness proof, no selected center family outside $V$, no host function-space topology, no selected subsequence operator, no quotient equality of maps, and no ambient $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompactMetricEquicontinuity Arzela--Ascoli handoff]
\label{thm:compact-metric-equicontinuity-ascoli}
Every accepted $\CompactMetricEquicontinuityUp$ carrier $E=(K,Y,F,R,M,V,S,A,H,C,P,N)$ supplies the finite-modulus handoff consumed by $\ArzelaAscoliFiniteModulusUp$. The handoff exposes the compact metric source row $K$, target metric row $Y$, finite family row $F$, pointwise radius rows $R$, shared modulus candidate $M$, finite-net cover ledger $V$, terminal $\RealUp$ tolerance seal $A$, and structural rows $H,C,P,N$ before the consumer reads $S$. It supplies no open-cover compactness, selected centers outside the displayed ledger, host function-space compactness, selected subsequences, quotient equality of maps, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compact source row $K$ and the finite-net cover ledger $V$. Finite induction over the displayed net centers reduces a requested tolerance to finitely many center reads, and each step keeps the membership witness and cover radius inside the same $\BHist$ packet.

Use the equicontinuity rows next. The pointwise continuity radius rows $R$ are transported through the shared modulus candidate $M$, so every family member in $F$ reads the same source-radius budget for the displayed tolerance. The target metric row $Y$ supplies only the distance comparisons attached to those finite rows.

Finally compose the tolerance ledger. The $\RealUp$ seal $A$ records the target-distance budget after the finite-net and equicontinuity-radius transports have been displayed, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same packet. Therefore the handoff row $S$ gives $\ArzelaAscoliFiniteModulusUp$ exactly the finite modulus data it expects, with no hidden compactness, choice, subsequence, quotient, function-space, or ambient-completeness coordinate.
\end{proof}
