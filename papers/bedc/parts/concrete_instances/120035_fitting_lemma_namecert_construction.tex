\chapter{Fitting Lemma Name Certificate Construction}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-fitting-lemma-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Fitting and Atiyah--Macdonald, the $\FittingLemmaUp$ packet records the finite decomposition surface for an endomorphism of a Noetherian module. It sits over the module carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-module-namecert}, the Noetherian finiteness surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-noetherian-module-namecert}, the linear-map rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-linearmap-namecert}, and the Fitting-ideal packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-fitting-ideal-namecert}; it does not import an ambient abelian-category splitting theorem, quotient equality, or choice of complements.

\begin{definition}[Fitting lemma carrier]
\label{def:fitting-lemma-carrier}
A $\FittingLemmaUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(M,N,E,I,K,T,S,R,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the underlying $\ModuleUp$ row, including the visible additive and scalar-operation classifier data.
\item $N$ is the $\NoetherianModuleUp$ row for the same module carrier, recording the finite ascending-chain and generator witnesses used by the packet.
\item $E$ is the $\LinearMapUp$ endomorphism row, with source and target both read from $M$.
\item $I$ is the $\FittingIdealUp$ row generated by the displayed determinantal data of $E$ on the finite Noetherian presentation.
\item $K$ is the finite kernel-stabilization ledger listing the inspected rows for $\ker(E^n)$ and $\ker(E^{n+1})$.
\item $T$ is the finite image-stabilization ledger listing the inspected rows for $\operatorname{im}(E^n)$ and $\operatorname{im}(E^{n+1})$.
\item $S$ is the decomposition row asserting that the stabilized kernel and image rows are disjoint on the displayed module coordinates and jointly cover the accepted carrier.
\item $R$ records the replay routes from $E,N,I,K,T$ to $S$, including the finite powers of the endomorphism actually inspected.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the module, Noetherian, linear-map, Fitting-ideal, stabilization, decomposition, replay, provenance, and local-name rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay for kernel reads, image reads, Fitting-ideal reads, stabilized decomposition reads, and consumer handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FittingLemmaUp$, $\ModuleUp$, $\NoetherianModuleUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\FittingIdealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FittingLemmaUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts exactly these finite rows. It has no coordinate for an external complement selector, quotient-space equality, classical primary decomposition, or a host theorem proving the Fitting lemma outside the displayed packet.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Fitting lemma NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:fitting-lemma-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FittingLemmaUp$ carrier $Q=(M,N,E,I,K,T,S,R,H,C,P,L)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FittingLemmaUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed packet, module-row exposure through $M$, Noetherian-row exposure through $N$, endomorphism exposure through $E$, Fitting-ideal exposure through $I$, kernel and image stabilization through $K$ and $T$, decomposition replay through $S$ and $R$, componentwise transport through $H$, continuation replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier and read the structural rows. The module row, Noetherian row, linear endomorphism row, and Fitting-ideal row are the only algebraic coordinates available to the classifier.

Inspect the stabilization ledgers next. The kernel ledger $K$ and image ledger $T$ list the finite powers of $E$ whose stabilized rows are used by the decomposition surface.

Finally replay the decomposition row. The route $R$ witnesses that $S$ is consumed only after the displayed stabilization rows and their transports have been read, while $C,P,L$ replay, source, and name the same packet. No other NameCert obligation is exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Fitting lemma kernel-image splitting route]
\label{thm:fitting-lemma-kernel-image-splitting-route}
Every accepted $\FittingLemmaUp$ carrier exposes a finite route from the $\ModuleUp$, $\NoetherianModuleUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, and $\FittingIdealUp$ rows to the stabilized kernel-image decomposition. A consumer of the decomposition row must first read
$$
M,N,E,I\longmapsto K,T\longmapsto S
$$
together with the replay, transport, provenance, and local-name rows $R,H,C,P,L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the algebraic base rows $M,N,E,I$. These rows identify the module, its Noetherian finiteness witness, the displayed endomorphism, and the Fitting-ideal data attached to that endomorphism.

The stabilization rows are downstream from this base. The packet records only the finite kernel rows in $K$ and finite image rows in $T$ that are reached by replaying powers of $E$ through the Noetherian and Fitting-ideal ledgers.

The splitting row $S$ is then consumed through $R,H,C,P,L$. Since no complement selector or host quotient equality is a coordinate of $Q$, the consumer obtains only the displayed kernel-image route and no stronger ambient decomposition principle.
\end{proof}
