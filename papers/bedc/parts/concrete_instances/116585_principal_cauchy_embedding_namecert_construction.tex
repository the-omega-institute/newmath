\begin{definition}[Principal Cauchy embedding carrier]
\label{def:principal-cauchy-embedding-carrier}

$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the source point row, exposing one displayed $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ point together with its dyadic endpoint ledger.
\item $S$ is the constant $\StreamNameUp$ window row, recording that every finite observation requested by the completion route reads the same source point row $Q$.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regularity row for the constant sequence generated by $S$.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance row, recording that every requested tail budget is discharged by the equality of the repeated dyadic readback.
\item $A$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, exposed only after $Q,S,R,D$ have been read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source point, constant window, regularity, tolerance, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a completion request through $Q,S,R,D$ and then into the terminal Real seal row $A$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PrincipalCauchyEmbeddingUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-regular-cauchy-completion-namecert}, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PrincipalCauchyEmbeddingUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $Q,S,R,D,A,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, host real equality, or detached equality of Real names.

\begin{theorem}[Principal Cauchy embedding finite route]
\label{thm:principal-cauchy-embedding-finite-route}
Every accepted $\PrincipalCauchyEmbeddingUp$ carrier
$$
E=(Q,S,R,D,A,H,C,P,N)
$$
exports a completion-facing read only after the source $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ point row, constant $\StreamNameUp$ window, $\RegSeqRatUp$ regularity row, dyadic tolerance row, and terminal $\RealUp$ seal row have been displayed in that order. The exported route may be consumed by the $\BishopRegularCauchyCompletionUp$ packet as point-generated regular Cauchy data, but it exports no $\RealUp$ completeness, quotient stream equality, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, or hidden equality of Real names.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:principal-cauchy-embedding-carrier}. The only source data available to a completion-facing consumer is the dyadic point row $Q$ together with the constant finite observation row $S$.

Read the regularity and tolerance rows next. Since $S$ repeats the same dyadic point through each finite window, the regular sequence row $R$ and dyadic tolerance row $D$ are both downstream of the displayed point ledger, not of a selected stream representative.

The Real seal row $A$ is reached only after $Q,S,R,D$ are visible. Componentwise transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve that order, while $P$ and $N$ attach provenance and the local NameCert boundary.

The carrier inventory contains no quotient stream equality, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, ambient Real completeness, or detached Real-name equality coordinate. Therefore the point-generated completion route is exactly the displayed finite route consumed by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-regular-cauchy-completion-namecert}.
\end{proof}
