Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\CauchyRealAdditionUp$ packet records addition of Cauchy real names as a finite BEDC naming surface before any $\RealUp$ consumer is allowed to read a sum. It sits over the synchronized addition route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-addition-namecert}, the regular rational sequence surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite window schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives Real-facing algebra consumers a finite route from two Cauchy names to one sealed sum name; it does not assert quotient equality of real names, selected host limits, trichotomy, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or a completed field operation.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy real addition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-real-addition-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyRealAdditionUp$ carrier
$$
A_{\Real,+}=(A,R_0,R_1,W,D,S,E,Z,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyRealAdditionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the $\RegularCauchyAdditionUp$ source certificate $A$, the two $\RegSeqRatUp$ input-name rows $R_0,R_1$, the paired $\StreamNameUp$ window row $W$, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and endpoint row $D$, the summed readback row $S$, the tail ledger $E$, the $\RealUp$ seal row $Z$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-real-addition-carrier}. Its analytic rows are $A,R_0,R_1,W,D,S,E,Z$, and its structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

Read the source certificate first. The $\RegularCauchyAdditionUp$ row $A$ supplies the synchronized-window addition route, while $R_0,R_1,W,D$ expose the two regular rational names, the paired window schedule, and the dyadic endpoint arithmetic used by that route.

The summed readback and seal rows are downstream. The ledger $E$ records the finite tail accounting before $S$ is consumed, and the $\RealUp$ row $Z$ is exposed only after the summed regular rational name has been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since no other coordinate appears in the carrier, the local certificate has no hidden quotient-equality, host-arithmetic, selected-limit, trichotomy, or ambient-completeness obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy real addition tail stability]
\label{thm:cauchy-real-addition-tail-stability}
Let two accepted $\CauchyRealAdditionUp$ packets have matching $\RegularCauchyAdditionUp$ source certificates, input-name rows, paired finite-window schedules, dyadic tolerance rows, summed readbacks, tail ledgers, and $\RealUp$ seal rows through their displayed $\hsame$ data. Then every Real-facing sum read of the first packet transports to the corresponding sum read of the second packet through the same finite route $A,R_0,R_1,W,D,S,E,Z,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the componentwise transport row $H$. It matches the source addition certificate $A$, the two input rows $R_0,R_1$, the window row $W$, and the dyadic tolerance row $D$.

Use the synchronized route supplied by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-addition-namecert}. The row $A$ keeps the pointwise sum tied to the paired windows and dyadic tolerance arithmetic, so transport cannot replace the finite tail route by host real addition.

The tail ledger and readback rows are preserved next. The ledger $E$ remains the only displayed place where source and output tolerances are combined before the summed readback row $S$ and the seal row $Z$ are consumed.

Replay through $C$ and repack through $P,N$. Every accepted Real-facing sum read is one of these displayed routes, so no quotient equality, selected limit, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle can enter under transport.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
