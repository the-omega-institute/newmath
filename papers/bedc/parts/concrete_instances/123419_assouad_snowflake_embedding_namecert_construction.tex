\begin{definition}[Assouad snowflake embedding carrier]
\label{def:assouad-snowflake-embedding-carrier}
An $\AssouadSnowflakeEmbeddingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Z=(M,D,A,S,E,B,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the source $\MetricUp$ or metric-space row, including the point-history carrier and displayed distance requests.
\item $D$ is the $\DoublingMetricSpaceUp$ finite-cover row exposing the relevant parent balls, smaller balls, and finite center lists.
\item $A$ is the $\AssouadDimensionUp$ scale-control row, recording the radius-ratio, cover-count, and scale-bound accountability used by the embedding window.
\item $S$ is the $\SnowflakeMetricUp$ row carrying the snowflaked distance route read from the source metric and the selected exponent ledger.
\item $E$ is the finite embedding-window ledger. It names only the finite family of scales, centers, coordinate windows, and comparison requests consumed by downstream embedding readers.
\item $B$ is a $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ of centers drawn from the finite nets exposed by $D,A,E$.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ exponent and tolerance row, carrying the displayed snowflake exponent and the finite error budgets for the embedding window.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ distance readback row for source and snowflaked distances used by the finite comparison requests.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $M,D,A,S,E,B,Q,R$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the metric and scale rows through
$$
M,D,A,S,E,B,Q,R \longmapsto H,P,N .

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\AssouadSnowflakeEmbeddingUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\DoublingMetricSpaceUp$, $\AssouadDimensionUp$, $\SnowflakeMetricUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AssouadSnowflakeEmbeddingUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for a host embedding theorem, an optimal target dimension, selected Euclidean coordinates, a Hilbert-space target, quotient metric equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Assouad snowflake embedding NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:assouad-snowflake-embedding-namecert-obligations}

$$
Z=(M,D,A,S,E,B,Q,R,H,C,P,N),

the local $\NameCert_{\AssouadSnowflakeEmbeddingUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission through $M$; doubling exposure through $D$; Assouad scale accountability through $A$; snowflake-distance stability through $S$; finite embedding-window handoff through $E$; center-bundle admission through $B$; dyadic exponent and tolerance readback through $Q$; Real-valued distance readback through $R$; and componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:assouad-snowflake-embedding-carrier}. The distance obligations are read from $S,Q,R$. The snowflake row supplies the transformed distance route, the dyadic row carries the exponent and tolerance budget, and the Real row seals the source and snowflaked distance reads needed by the finite comparison requests.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since no row stores a host embedding theorem, optimal dimension, selected coordinates, Hilbert-space target, quotient metric equality, or ambient real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
