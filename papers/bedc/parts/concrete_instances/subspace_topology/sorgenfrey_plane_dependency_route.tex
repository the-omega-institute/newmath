\begin{proposition}[SubspaceTopology Sorgenfrey-plane dependency route]
\label{prop:subspace-topology-sorgenfrey-plane-dependency-route}
Every $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ consumer that restricts product lower-limit windows through a displayed subspace first reads the ambient $\TopologyUp$ carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert} and then the $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ admission, relative-open, and restriction-exactness rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subspace-topology-namecert}. The downstream product window of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-sorgenfrey-plane-namecert} is therefore a topology-restriction read, not a detached host product topology or an independent lower-limit basis.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ carrier. Its relative-open row is read only after the ambient $\TopologyUp$ indexed-open ledger and the displayed subspace admission row have both been exposed.

Apply the topology forward route and topology sibling route. They keep every subspace-open read anchored in the ambient topology carrier before any downstream topology consumer can use the restricted row.

The $\SorgenfreyPlaneUp$ consumer then reads its product lower-limit windows from that restricted topology surface. Since neither the topology carrier nor the subspace packet stores a detached host product topology or independent lower-limit basis, the route factors through $\TopologyUp$ and $\SubspaceTopologyUp$ exactly as displayed.
\end{proof}
