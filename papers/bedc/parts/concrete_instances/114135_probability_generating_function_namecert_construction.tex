\begin{definition}[Probability generating function carrier]
\label{def:probability-generating-function-carrier}
A $\ProbabilityGeneratingFunctionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the $\ProbSpaceUp$ row that names the finite probability context for the displayed event and mass reads.
\item $D$ is the $\DistributionUp$ row whose integer-valued mass table supplies the inspected coefficient window.
\item $I$ is the $\NatUp$ index row naming the finite natural-number slots whose probabilities are exposed.
\item $S$ is the $\SeriesUp$ coefficient-stream row, reading the displayed masses as the corresponding formal coefficient window.
\item $W$ is the finite coefficient-window ledger connecting $D,I$ to $S$ without selecting an infinite stream.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the inspected probability coefficients and finite partial evaluations.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row exported only after $P,D,I,S,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the probability, distribution, index, series, window, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a coefficient or evaluation request through $P,D,I,S,W$ to $R$ and $E$.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ProbabilityGeneratingFunctionUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $P,D,I,S,W,R,E,H,C,K,N$. It has no coordinate for a host analytic function, a completed probability law outside the displayed distribution row, selected infinite coefficients, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Probability generating function NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:probability-generating-function-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ProbabilityGeneratingFunctionUp$ carrier $G=(P,D,I,S,W,R,E,H,C,K,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ProbabilityGeneratingFunctionUp}$ surface consists exactly of probability-space admission through $P$; distribution-row admission through $D$; natural-index exposure through $I$; coefficient-stream exposure through $S$; finite coefficient-window exhaustion through $W$; $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$; $\RealUp$ seal ordering through $E$; and componentwise transport, continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming through $H,C,K,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:probability-generating-function-carrier}. The rows $P,D,I,S,W$ display the probability context, the integer-valued distribution, the finite natural-index slots, and the coefficient-stream window before any scalar readback is available.

The regular rational row $R$ is downstream from that displayed coefficient window, and the Real seal $E$ is downstream from $R$. Thus a scalar consumer can only read probabilities and partial evaluations along the route already exposed by the finite coefficient data.

Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same displayed route. Provenance and local naming through $K,N$ source and name it without adding host analytic semantics, selected infinite coefficients, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:probability-generating-function-coefficient-window-handoff}

\begin{proof}

Read the scalar handoff through $R$ and $E$. The regular rational readback is obtained from the same finite coefficient windows, and the Real seal is visible only after that readback has been displayed.
