\begin{theorem}[Nested interval sequence NameCert obligation surface]
\label{thm:nested-interval-sequence-obligation-surface}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:nested-interval-sequence-carrier}. The only displayed coordinates are $I,E,L,D,S,R,Z,H,C,P,A$, so interval admission, endpoint comparison, nesting evidence, diameter control, finite observation, readback, Real sealing, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are all direct readings of the packet.

Read the interval data first. The row $I$ supplies the located interval rows, $E$ supplies endpoint comparison, and $L$ supplies the displayed containment relation between later and earlier inspected intervals.

Read the shrinkage and observation rows next. Finally $H,C,P,A$ preserve the same finite route under transport, replay, source, and local naming.
