\begin{definition}[Beurling--Lax carrier]
\label{def:beurling-lax-carrier}
A $\BeurlingLaxUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(H,O,S,I,W,R,E,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $H$ is the $\HardySpaceUp$ source row, exposing the finite coefficient prefix, boundary window, Hardy-control, summation, readback, tolerance, and real-seal rows used by the invariant-subspace read.
\item $O$ is the $\BoundedLinearOperatorUp$ row naming the displayed shift or multiplication action as bounded operator data.
\item $S$ is the shift-stability row, recording the finite witnesses that the inspected subspace rows are stable under the displayed operator action.
\item $I$ is the inner-divider ledger row. It names a finite divisor or multiplier certificate when such a row is displayed, but it is not a selected inner function.
\item $W$ is the finite window row for inspected basis, boundary, or coefficient probes.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the coefficient, boundary, operator, and stability probes.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row available only after the Hardy-space, operator, shift-stability, window, and readback rows are visible.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $H,O,S,I,W,R,E$.

The carrier has no coordinate for a host Hilbert quotient, a chosen orthogonal projection, a selected inner function, full spectral resolution, quotient equality of boundary functions, or ambient Hilbert-space completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Beurling--Lax NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:beurling-lax-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BeurlingLaxUp$ carrier $B=(H,O,S,I,W,R,E,T,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BeurlingLaxUp}$ surface consists exactly of these five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed Hardy-space source, bounded-operator action, shift-stability ledger, optional divider ledger, finite windows, readback, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local name;
\item Hardy boundary exposure, requiring every invariant-subspace read to consume the $\HardySpaceUp$ coefficient, boundary-window, Hardy-control, summation, tolerance, and seal rows before it can inspect $S$;
\item bounded shift exposure, requiring the operator row $O$ and the finite stability row $S$ to be read before any invariant-subspace claim is exported;
\item readback and seal nonescape, requiring $W,R,E$ to supply the visible coefficient and boundary reads without host quotient equality or selected representatives;
\item structural transport, replay, provenance, and naming nonescape through $T,C,P,N$.
\end{enumerate}
No obligation is discharged by a host Hilbert-space quotient, a selected inner function, a spectral theorem, orthogonal projection choice, or ambient closed-subspace completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:beurling-lax-carrier}. The displayed analytic rows are $H,O,S,I,W,R,E$, and the structural rows are $T,C,P,N$.

The Hardy-space source $H$ is read first, so the coefficient and boundary data remain those of the finite $\HardySpaceUp$ packet. The bounded operator row $O$ and shift-stability row $S$ then record the displayed invariant-subspace action.

The divider ledger $I$ is only a finite certificate row. It cannot choose an inner function because the carrier contains no selector for one. The window, readback, and real-seal rows $W,R,E$ keep the boundary reads inside the L10 surface.

Finally $T,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no excluded host object appears among the carrier coordinates, the five rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:beurling-lax-hardy-shift-route}

Therefore every Hardy-space shift consumer factors through the listed sibling rows, while the excluded Hilbert quotient, selected inner function, projection choice, spectral theorem, and ambient completeness data remain outside the packet.
