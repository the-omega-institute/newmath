\begin{definition}[Zeta zero type factorization carrier]
\label{def:zeta-zero-type-factorization-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $V$ is the zeta-value relation row, displaying a finite witness that the source row has a value readout.
\item $F$ is the zero-fiber row, displaying the value witness together with the zero-complex classifier row of \autoref{thm:rh-zeta-zero-type-zero-fiber}.
\item $G$ is the legal-generator factorization row, recording that every admitted generator read factors through the same displayed zero fiber.
\item $T$ is the non-trivial strip row, recording the critical-strip witness required before the fixed-half interface is meaningful.
\item $J$ is the fixed-half ledger row, recording the critical-line witness supplied to the sufficiency consumer.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Zeta zero type factorization zero-fiber boundary]
\label{thm:zeta-zero-type-factorization-zero-fiber}
Every accepted $\ZetaZeroTypeFactorizationUp$ packet exposes the zero-type route through the displayed row $F$ before any generator or fixed-half consumer can read the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:zeta-zero-type-factorization-carrier}. The source row $D$ and value row $V$ are not yet a zero-type packet; the zero-fiber row $F$ is the displayed witness that the value readout is classified with the zero complex history.

The generator row $G$ is downstream of $F$. A legal generator is accepted only after its output is routed through the same zero-fiber row, so it cannot bypass the zero-type carrier described in \autoref{ch:visions-rh-zeta-zero-type-factorization}.

The fixed-half rows $T,J$ are also downstream of $F$. They refine a non-trivial zero packet by strip and critical-line ledgers, but they do not create the zero witness. Thus the zero-fiber row is the boundary through which every accepted packet must pass.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Zeta zero type factorization ledger]
\label{thm:zeta-zero-type-factorization-ledger}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:zeta-zero-type-factorization-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\ZetaZeroTypeFactorizationUp}$ surface consists exactly of the finite zero-fiber row, generator-factorization row, fixed-half ledger rows, TasteGate-facing row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the rows $D,V,F$ first. They display the source, the value relation, and the zero-fiber witness. Without $F$, the packet is merely a zeta-value readout and cannot feed the fixed-half sufficiency interface.

Next read $G,T,J$. The generator row records factorization through the displayed zero fiber, while the non-trivial strip row and fixed-half row record the two ledgers required by the RH-facing subtype route. These are finite ledger rows, not an analytic proof of the Riemann hypothesis.

Finally read $K,H,C,P,N$. The TasteGate row names the future $\Nontrivial$ and $\FieldFaithful$ obligations for the same carrier fields, and the transport, replay, provenance, and local-name rows exhaust the BEDC certificate surface. No Euler-product, primality, finite-place-product, residual-diamond, host-equality, or analytic-continuation coordinate is available to a consumer.
\end{proof}
