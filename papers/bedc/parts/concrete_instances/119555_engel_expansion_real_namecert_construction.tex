\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\EngelExpansionRealUp$}

Following Engel and the constructive real-number treatment in Bishop and Bridges, the $\EngelExpansionRealUp$ packet names the finite route by which an Engel denominator schedule becomes a displayed real-name surface. It sits after the Engel expansion carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-engel-expansion-namecert}, the rational row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, the dyadic tail ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream window discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet records an Engel-generated real presentation as finite BEDC NameCert data; it does not identify quotient representatives, choose an infinite sum, invoke trichotomy, or claim ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[EngelExpansionReal carrier]
\label{def:engel-expansion-real-carrier}
An $\EngelExpansionRealUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
X=(E,Q,D,W,R,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the source $\EngelExpansionUp$ packet, including its denominator schedule, rational partial sums, dyadic remainder bounds, stream windows, regular readback, and terminal Real seal rows.
\item $Q$ is the $\RatUp$ partial-sum row read from the Engel denominator schedule.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tail-control row used to bound every unread Engel remainder visible to the packet.
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window row selecting the denominator prefixes, partial sums, and tail bounds under inspection.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational sequence row produced from $Q,D,W$.
\item $L$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row attached only after the regular rational route has been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Engel source, rational partial sums, dyadic tail bounds, stream windows, regular readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a real-name request through
$$
E,Q,W \longmapsto D \longmapsto R \longmapsto L .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\EngelExpansionUp$, $\RatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\EngelExpansionRealUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for quotient representatives of reals, a selected value of an infinite Engel sum, trichotomy, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[EngelExpansionReal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:engel-expansion-real-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EngelExpansionRealUp$ carrier $X=(E,Q,D,W,R,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\EngelExpansionRealUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the Engel source row, rational partial-sum exposure, dyadic remainder control, finite stream-window selection, regular rational readback, terminal Real sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming through the displayed rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the finite carrier $X=(E,Q,D,W,R,L,H,C,P,N)$. The projection exposes the Engel source packet, the rational partial-sum row, the dyadic tail-control row, the stream-window row, the regular readback row, and the real-name seal.

Use \autoref{thm:engel-expansion-namecert-obligations}. The source row $E$ already displays the Engel denominator schedule and its finite NameCert obligations, so the real-facing packet does not introduce a separate infinite series object.

Read $Q,D,W$ before any completion-facing data. The rational partial sums are visible only through the finite windows, and every unread tail claim passes through the dyadic remainder-control row before $R$ or $L$ can be consumed.

Finally form the regular readback and the Real seal. The rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly this route, so quotient representatives, trichotomy, selected sums, ambient completeness, and bridge checking remain outside the local obligation surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[EngelExpansionReal regular-Cauchy route]
\label{thm:engel-expansion-real-regular-cauchy-route}
Every accepted $\EngelExpansionRealUp$ carrier exposes its real-name readback only through the finite route
$$
E,Q,W \longmapsto D \longmapsto R \longmapsto L
$$
with structural support $H,C,P,N$. Thus every real-facing consumer first reads an $\EngelExpansionUp$ source, $\RatUp$ partial sums, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tail bounds, $\StreamNameUp$ windows, a $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular readback, and a terminal $\RealUp$ seal before any $\RealUp$ observation is available.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from a consumer of the Real seal $L$. By the carrier definition, $L$ is not an independent completed object; it is downstream of the regular rational row $R$ and the finite Engel data displayed by $E,Q,D,W$.

Apply the real readback boundary of \autoref{thm:engel-expansion-real-readback-boundary}. That theorem forces the Engel source to pass through rational partial sums, dyadic tail bounds, stream windows, regular readback, and the terminal Real seal before any real-facing interpretation is available.

Use the sibling L10 route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. These chapters supply the dyadic bound, finite-window, regular-sequence, and real-seal rows consumed by the route.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name the same finite passage. Since no carrier coordinate stores quotient representatives, selected real limits, trichotomy, ambient completeness, Lean verification, or a checked bridge, none of those claims is exported by the route.
\end{proof}
