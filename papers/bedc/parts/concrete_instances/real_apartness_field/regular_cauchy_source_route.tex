\begin{theorem}[Real apartness field regular-Cauchy source route]
\label{thm:real-apartness-field-regular-cauchy-source-route}
Every accepted $\RealApartnessFieldUp$ inverse read or apartness read first consumes the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the located and order sibling rows already named by this chapter before the $\FieldUp$ inverse data are exposed. In carrier notation, a read of
$$
F=(R,A,O,K,D,H,C,P,N)
$$
passes through the regular-rational source behind $R$, the terminal real seal $R$, the apartness row $A$, located-order row $O$, dyadic ledger $D$, transport $H$, replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local naming row $N$ before the field row $K$ can export an inverse branch.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:real-apartness-field-carrier}. The field row $K$ is stored together with the real seal row $R$, apartness row $A$, located-order row $O$, dyadic ledger $D$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$.

Use the StreamName dependency theorem \autoref{thm:real-apartness-field-streamname-dependency}. It forces every comparison-facing read to expose the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} before the terminal $\RealUp$ route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} is consumed.

Now apply the located and order child routes already carried by the chapter: \autoref{thm:real-apartness-field-located-source-route}, \autoref{thm:real-apartness-field-located-real-route}, and \autoref{thm:real-apartness-field-real-order-forward-link}. These routes place the located-real, apartness-order, and Real/order dependencies before any field-facing inverse read is exported.

Finally combine the NameCert obligations with inverse-guard stability. Theorems~\ref{thm:real-apartness-field-namecert-obligations} and \ref{thm:real-apartness-field-inverse-guard-stability} require the apartness guard and dyadic reciprocal budget to be displayed before $K$ is consumed. Thus the inverse data are downstream of the RegSeqRat source, Real terminal seal, and located/order sibling rows, and the route adds no trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected representative, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}
