\begin{theorem}[Real apartness field product-topology dependency]
\label{thm:real-apartness-field-product-topology-dependency}
Every product-coordinate apartness-field read of an accepted $\RealApartnessFieldUp$ packet must expose the finite $\ProductTopologyUp$ product window before the inverse, order, and field rows of the apartness-field carrier are consumed. The route first reads the $\RealUp$ seal, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\StreamNameUp$ finite windows, $\FieldUp$ operation row, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, and $\RealApartnessOrderBridgeUp$ apartness-order bridge, and only then permits the product-coordinate consumer to use the $\RealApartnessFieldUp$ inverse or order-facing field surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted $\RealApartnessFieldUp$ carrier through \autoref{def:real-apartness-field-carrier}. Its inverse and order-facing reads are stored in the displayed real seal, apartness, located-order, field, dyadic, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows, so a product-coordinate consumer has no separate coordinate from which to read a field operation.

Read the product surface through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-product-topology-namecert}. The finite $\ProductTopologyUp$ window supplies the coordinate route before either coordinate can be consumed as a real apartness-field input.

Use the source rows already required by the chapter: the $\StreamNameUp$ windows and $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback feed the $\RealUp$ seal, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ ledger supplies tolerance and positivity data, the $\FieldUp$ row carries the operation boundary, and the $\RealApartnessOrderBridgeUp$ route ties apartness evidence to order-facing comparison over the same real seal. The product-coordinate read therefore reaches the inverse or order-facing field rows only after the product-topology dependency has been displayed.
\end{proof}
