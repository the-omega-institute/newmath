\begin{definition}[Cauchy compactness criterion carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-compactness-criterion-carrier}
A $\CauchyCompactnessCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(S,Q,D,R,M,I,F,G,H,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row that records the inspected name windows.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the regular rational data exposed by those windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row controlling mesh size, Cauchy radius, and comparison budget.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $S,Q,D$ have been displayed.
\item $M$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ compact-metric row read by the finite-net consumer.
\item $I$ is the $\HeineBorelIntervalUp$ interval-net row supplying bounded interval net cells.
\item $F$ is the $\FiniteNetSelectionPrincipleUp$ row selecting the displayed finite net from the available compact-metric and interval data.
\item $G$ is the $\RegularCauchyCompactnessCriterionUp$ handoff row that records compatibility with regular Cauchy compactness data.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $S,Q,D,R,M,I,F,G,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the displayed $\Cont$ replay and $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CauchyCompactnessCriterionUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\HeineBorelIntervalUp$, $\FiniteNetSelectionPrincipleUp$, $\RegularCauchyCompactnessCriterionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompactnessCriterionUp}$ row.

The carrier predicate asserts exactly that compactness is read through these finite rows. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, sequential compactness without explicit finite-window data, quotient equality of streams, countable choice, selected representatives, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy compactness criterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-compactness-criterion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyCompactnessCriterionUp$ carrier $K=(S,Q,D,R,M,I,F,G,H,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyCompactnessCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, $\RealUp$ seal row, $\CompactMetricUp$ compact-metric row, $\HeineBorelIntervalUp$ interval-net row, $\FiniteNetSelectionPrincipleUp$ finite-net selection row, $\RegularCauchyCompactnessCriterionUp$ handoff row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay with $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation row may be discharged by host open-cover compactness, sequential compactness without displayed data, quotient-stream equality, countable choice, selected representatives, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-compactness-criterion-carrier}. Its displayed coordinates are exactly $S,Q,D,R,M,I,F,G,H,P,N$, so carrier admission and local naming can inspect only those rows.

Read the Cauchy side first. The finite windows $S$ expose the regular rational readback $Q$, the dyadic tolerance row $D$ controls the Cauchy and mesh budgets, and the terminal seal $R$ is downstream of those displayed rows.

Now read the compactness side. The compact-metric row $M$, interval-net row $I$, finite-net selection row $F$, and regular Cauchy handoff $G$ provide the complete finite-net route consumed by the criterion. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite packet, so the excluded compactness and choice principles have no carrier coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy compactness criterion finite-net handoff]
\label{thm:cauchy-compactness-criterion-finite-net-handoff}
Every compactness read of an accepted $\CauchyCompactnessCriterionUp$ carrier factors through the finite-net route
$$
S,Q,D,R,M,I,F,G,H,P,N .
$$
In particular, the read consumes StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance, the Real seal, CompactMetric finite data, Heine--Borel interval nets, FiniteNetSelectionPrinciple selection data, the RegularCauchyCompactnessCriterion handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before it can expose compactness data. It is therefore a finite-net handoff, not open-cover compactness, not sequential compactness without explicit data, and not an appeal to ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:cauchy-compactness-criterion-namecert-obligations}. They identify the complete local surface of an accepted carrier, so a compactness read cannot leave the rows $S,Q,D,R,M,I,F,G,H,P,N$.

Use the Real-facing route next. Rows $S,Q,D$ provide finite observation, regular rational readback, and tolerance accounting before the terminal seal $R$ is available.

Finally apply the finite-net rows $M,I,F,G$. The compact-metric and interval rows provide the net cells, the selection-principle row records the finite choice already displayed in the packet, and the regular Cauchy handoff keeps the read compatible with regular Cauchy compactness data. Since none of these rows contains an open-cover subcover choice, a sequence-only compactness principle, quotient stream equality, countable choice, or an ambient completeness theorem, the compactness read is exactly the stated finite-net handoff.
\end{proof}
