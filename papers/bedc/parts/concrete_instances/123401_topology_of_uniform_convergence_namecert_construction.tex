\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\TopologyOfUniformConvergenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-topology-of-uniform-convergence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Bourbaki, the $\TopologyOfUniformConvergenceUp$ packet records the finite uniform-window surface by which a family of function reads is admitted to the topology of uniform convergence before any completed function space or quotient equality is available. It sits over the topology carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the uniform-space rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the continuous-map surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}. The packet names a finite neighbourhood basis for uniform convergence; it does not assert compact-open equivalence, quotient function equality, selected suprema, countable choice, ambient completeness, or bridge checking.

\begin{definition}[Topology of uniform convergence packet]
\label{def:topology-of-uniform-convergence-packet}
A $\TopologyOfUniformConvergenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(T,X,Y,F,E,W,R,C,H,D,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ row naming the target neighbourhood surface for function-family reads.
\item $X$ is the source-window row whose points are inspected only through displayed finite observations.
\item $Y$ is the $\UniformSpaceUp$ target row supplying entourages for comparing function values.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ family row, exposing the displayed maps whose uniform convergence is being read.
\item $E$ is the finite entourage-budget row, recording the requested uniform tolerance over the displayed source window.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation schedule used to open point, function, and entourage windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ value-read seal reached by the displayed function values before comparison.
\item $C$ is the convergence-neighbourhood row, naming the basic uniform-convergence neighbourhood determined by $X,Y,F,E,W,R$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for topology, source-window, uniform-space, continuous-map, entourage, stream, real-value, convergence-neighbourhood, replay, provenance, and naming rows.
\item $D$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through
$$
T\longrightarrow X\longrightarrow Y\longrightarrow F\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow W\longrightarrow R\longrightarrow C .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TopologyOfUniformConvergenceUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RealUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TopologyOfUniformConvergenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host function-space quotient, selected supremum norm, compact-open comparison theorem, countable-choice schedule, ambient completeness principle, or bridge credential.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Topology of uniform convergence obligation surface]
\label{thm:topology-of-uniform-convergence-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\TopologyOfUniformConvergenceUp$ carrier
$$
U=(T,X,Y,F,E,W,R,C,H,D,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\TopologyOfUniformConvergenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of topology admission for $T$, source-window admission for $X$, uniform-space admission for $Y$, continuous-map family admission for $F$, finite entourage-budget exactness for $E$, stream-window observation through $W$, real-value sealing through $R$, convergence-neighbourhood formation through $C$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $D$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

Every uniform-convergence neighbourhood exported by the packet must therefore pass through the displayed finite route $T,X,Y,F,E,W,R,C,H,D,P,N$ and exports no compact-open equivalence, quotient equality of maps, selected supremum, countable-choice schedule, ambient completeness principle, or bridge credential.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:topology-of-uniform-convergence-packet}. The rows $T,X,Y,F,E,W,R,C,H,D,P,N$ are the only coordinates of an accepted packet, so the local NameCert surface can read only topology, source-window, uniform-space, continuous-map, entourage, stream, real-value, convergence-neighbourhood, transport, replay, provenance, and naming data.

The finite entourage-budget row $E$ is read together with the displayed source window $X$ and target uniform-space row $Y$. The stream row $W$ opens only the finite observations required for that budget, and the real-value row $R$ seals the displayed function values before the convergence-neighbourhood row $C$ is formed.

Finally, $H,D,P,N$ preserve and name this route without adding analytic content. Since the carrier has no row for compact-open comparison, quotient equality of maps, selected suprema, countable choice, ambient completeness, or bridge checking, none of those surfaces can be exported by an accepted $\TopologyOfUniformConvergenceUp$ packet.
\end{proof}
