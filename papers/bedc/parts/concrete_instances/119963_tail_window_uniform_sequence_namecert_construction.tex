\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\TailWindowUniformSequenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-tail-window-uniform-sequence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\TailWindowUniformSequenceUp$ packet records the finite tail-window interface used when a uniformly continuous sequence is inspected only through displayed windows and a shared modulus row. It refines the sequence route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformly-continuous-sequence-namecert} through the modulus discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet gives Real and Completion consumers a tail-window surface without selected limits, quotient streams, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Tail-window uniformly continuous sequence carrier]
\label{def:tail-window-uniform-sequence-carrier}
A $\TailWindowUniformSequenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(I,M,W_0,W_1,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the sequence-index window row naming the finite tail interval being inspected.
\item $M$ is the shared $\UniformModulusUp$ row used by every read in the window.
\item $W_0$ and $W_1$ are the paired $\StreamNameUp$ window reads cut from the same tail interval.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the two window reads and their shared modulus budget.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row available only after $I,M,W_0,W_1,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the index window, modulus, stream windows, regular readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the index window through the shared modulus, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, and Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TailWindowUniformSequenceUp$, $\UniformlyContinuousSequenceUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TailWindowUniformSequenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a selected limiting value, quotient equality of streams, a private modulus per side of the window, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tail-window uniformly continuous sequence obligations]
\label{thm:tail-window-uniform-sequence-obligations}
For every accepted $\TailWindowUniformSequenceUp$ carrier
$$
T=(I,M,W_0,W_1,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\TailWindowUniformSequenceUp}$ surface consists of the following five obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed sequence-index window, shared modulus, paired StreamName reads, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item shared-modulus exposure, requiring $M$ to be read before either window can be consumed as a uniformly continuous sequence tail;
\item tail-window readback, requiring $W_0,W_1$ and $R$ to expose only the finite inspected tail interval named by $I$;
\item Real-seal handoff, requiring $E$ to appear only after the shared modulus and regular readback rows have been displayed;
\item nonescape for selected limits, quotient streams, private side moduli, countable choice, trichotomy, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:tail-window-uniform-sequence-carrier}. The analytic coordinates are exactly the index window, shared modulus, two StreamName reads, regular readback, and terminal Real seal.

Read the shared modulus row before the window rows. Since $M$ is common to $W_0$ and $W_1$, the two reads cannot choose separate tail thresholds or private uniform-continuity data.

The RegSeqRat row repacks only the displayed window data. Thus a Real-facing consumer reaches $E$ through $I,M,W_0,W_1,R$ rather than through a selected limit or quotient stream.

Finally the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. They do not add countable choice, trichotomy, private moduli, or ambient completeness, so the five listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
