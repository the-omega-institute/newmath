\label{def:hyperbolic-shadow-boundary-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $O$ is the observer window row, recording the displayed basepoint, aperture, and finite visible-shadow request.
\item $W$ is the $\HyperbolicBoundaryShadowUp$ row that supplies the boundary-shadow window.
\item $V$ is the $\HyperbolicVisualBoundaryUp$ row that reads the same shadow window as visible boundary data.
\item $S$ is the $\HyperbolicVisualShadowMetricUp$ row that records visual-shadow metric comparison before observer expansion consumes the boundary read.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Hyperbolic shadow boundary NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hyperbolic-shadow-boundary-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\HyperbolicShadowBoundaryUp$ carrier $B=(M,O,W,V,S,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\HyperbolicShadowBoundaryUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric source admission, observer-window exposure, $\HyperbolicBoundaryShadowUp$ window admission, $\HyperbolicVisualBoundaryUp$ readback, $\HyperbolicVisualShadowMetricUp$ comparison, $\HyperbolicObserverExpansionUp$ handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:hyperbolic-shadow-boundary-carrier}. The rows $M,O,W,V,S,R$ are the only analytic rows, and $H,C,P,N$ are the only structural rows.

The boundary side is finite. The observer row $O$ exposes the window request, the boundary-shadow row $W$ supplies the visible shadow, and the visual-boundary row $V$ reads that same window as boundary data.

The observer side is downstream. The visual-shadow metric row $S$ compares the visible shadow before the observer-expansion handoff $R$ is available. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve this route without adding a completed ideal boundary, quotient ray equality, selected geodesic, compactification theorem, or physical observer path.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Hyperbolic shadow boundary observer handoff]

$$
\HyperbolicBoundaryShadowUp \longmapsto \HyperbolicVisualBoundaryUp \longmapsto \HyperbolicVisualShadowMetricUp \longmapsto \HyperbolicObserverExpansionUp

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Next read the visual-shadow metric row. It compares only the displayed shadow and visible-boundary rows, so the observer-expansion handoff receives finite metric readback data rather than a global compactification.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for quotient ray equality, selected geodesics, completed boundary objects, or physical observer paths, the observer handoff remains exactly the displayed finite route.
