\begin{definition}[MetaCIC audit matrix carrier]
\label{def:metacic-audit-matrix-carrier}
A $\MetaCICAuditMatrixUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(A,N,B,S,E,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the available-row matrix, recording closed-normal consistency, candidate-mediated normalization, local decidable checking, closed substitution, and conditional confluence as positive audit rows;
\item $N$ is the non-claim row matrix, recording full closed consistency, arbitrary closed Church--Rosser, candidate-free normalization, unrestricted beta-conversion decidability, and host substitution semantics as non-exported endpoints;
\item $B$ is the blocked-edge matrix, with the subject-reduction lift as the critical edge from arbitrary closed proof terms to the closed-normal consistency branch;
\item $S$ is the synthesis-consumer row, naming the consumers that may read the matrix before using closed-normal packets, discharge ledgers, or audit-map dependency routes;
\item $E$ is the non-escape ledger, recording that every consumer route must preserve $A,N,B,S$ as displayed rows;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transports for $A,N,B,S,E$ and the structural rows;
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ routes by which a consumer reads available rows, non-claim rows, blocked edges, synthesis consumers, and non-escape ledgers in matrix order;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over \autoref{ch:metacic-audit-synthesis}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicnormalizationfrontier-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-subjectreductiondischargeledger-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closednormalconsistencypacket-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-auditmapdependencyweave-namecert};
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetaCICAuditMatrixUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for removing the blocked subject-reduction edge, no hidden full-consistency theorem, no host reduction semantics, and no quotient equality of MetaCIC terms.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC audit matrix non-escape]
\label{thm:metacic-audit-matrix-nonescape}
Every consumer-facing route out of an accepted $\MetaCICAuditMatrixUp$ carrier factors through
$$
A,N,B,S,E,H,C,P,L.
$$
If the subject-reduction blocked edge $B$ is removed from the packet, the non-escape ledger $E$ no longer has a row through which to refuse the full closed-consistency read. Such a packet therefore fails the non-escape theorem for $\MetaCICAuditMatrixUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier inventory. The available rows, non-claim rows, blocked edge, synthesis consumers, and non-escape ledger are all named coordinates of one finite packet.

The continuation and provenance rows $C$ and $P$ can only replay and package those displayed coordinates. They do not create a second route to full closed consistency, host substitution semantics, or quotient equality of MetaCIC terms.

Now remove $B$ from the packet. The row $E$ is defined as the ledger that preserves $A,N,B,S$ under consumer reads, so without the blocked-edge coordinate it cannot certify refusal of the full closed-consistency route. Hence any accepted packet missing $B$ is outside the stated non-escape boundary.
\end{proof}
