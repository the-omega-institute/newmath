\item $K$ is the completion seal row for the Cauchy-completion reflector over $X$.
\item $\eta$ is the unit map row sending the displayed dense source into the completion seal.

\item $T$ is the triangle identity row that compares the unit handoff with the reflector replay inside the same finite packet.

\label{thm:cauchy-completion-reflection-unit-namecert-obligations}
