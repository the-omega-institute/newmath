\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-metacicnormalizationbudget-namecert}
\origin{ai}

The $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ packet turns the normalization audit map of \autoref{ch:metacic-normalization-audit-map} into a finite NameCert surface. It is the normalization-side sibling of the conditional confluence witness in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicconfluenceauditwitness-namecert}, the subject-reduction obligation packet in \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-subject-reduction-obligation-namecert}, and the closed-normal consistency boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closed-normal-consistency-boundary-namecert}. The packet records a candidate-mediated budget route; it does not assert unconditional strong normalization for every closed typed MetaCIC term.

\begin{definition}[MetaCIC normalization budget carrier]
\label{def:metacic-normalization-budget-carrier}
A $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(T,C,E,S,A,P,R,H,Q,L,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the closed typed MetaCIC term row whose normalization read is requested;
\item $C$ is the reducibility-candidate evidence row attached to the same closed term;
\item $E$ is the closed-candidate or candidate-set membership row consumed by the checked normalization lemmas;
\item $S$ is the candidate-implies-strong-normalization row, naming the Lean route $\mathsf{BEDC.MetaCIC.candidate\_implies\_sn}$ as a consumed theorem surface rather than as a global closed-term theorem;
\item $A$ is the application and Pi adequacy boundary row that prevents componentwise normalization data from becoming unconditional application normalization;
\item $P$ is the proof-replay budget row recording the finite candidate projections, adequacy reads, and no-infinite-reduction consequences available to the consumer;
\item $R$ is the refusal row for requests that omit candidate evidence, closed-candidate evidence, or candidate-set membership;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for term, candidate, membership, theorem, adequacy, budget, refusal, route, provenance, and naming rows;
\item $Q$ records the displayed $\Cont$ routes by which consumers read normalization only through $C,E,S,A,P$ or receive the refusal row $R$;
\item $L$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over the normalization audit map, the confluence audit witness, the subject-reduction obligation packet, the closed-normal consistency boundary, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and the local certificate;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains no coordinate for a host evaluator, an unconditional closed-term strong-normalization theorem, a subject-reduction discharge, a hidden application-normalization rule, or a conversion principle outside the candidate-mediated rows.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC normalization budget NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ carrier $B=(T,C,E,S,A,P,R,H,Q,L,N)$, the local $\NameCert$ surface exposes exactly the closed typed term row $T$, reducibility-candidate evidence $C$, closed-candidate or candidate-set membership $E$, candidate-to-strong-normalization row $S$, application and Pi adequacy boundary $A$, finite proof-replay budget $P$, refusal row $R$, componentwise transport $H$, displayed continuation routes $Q$, package provenance $L$, and local naming $N$. A consumer may read strong normalization only along the candidate-mediated route displayed by $C,E,S,A,P$.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetTasteGate\_single\_carrier\_alignment}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-normalization-budget-carrier}. The rows $T,C,E,S,A,P$ display the positive route: a closed term is paired with candidate evidence, the evidence is read through the checked candidate-to-SN row, and the adequacy boundary records where Pi and application reasoning is allowed.

The refusal row $R$ accounts for requests that skip the candidate evidence or ask for unconditional closed-term normalization. It is part of the same packet, so the negative answer is not an external convention.

The support rows $H,Q,L,N$ transport, route, package, and name the same finite inventory. Since the carrier has no second theorem coordinate, the local NameCert obligations are exhausted by the displayed candidate-mediated budget surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC normalization budget obligation surface]
\label{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-obligation-surface}
Every accepted $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ carrier exposes exactly the positive rows
$$
T,C,E,S,A,P,
$$
the refusal row $R$, and the support rows $H,Q,L,N$. The positive rows are read only as the candidate-mediated normalization budget, while $R$ records requests for unconditional closed-term strong normalization or for normalization without the displayed candidate evidence.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetCarrier\_obligation\_surface}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-normalization-budget-carrier}. Its tuple has the six positive coordinates $T,C,E,S,A,P$, the single refusal coordinate $R$, and the four structural support coordinates $H,Q,L,N$; no other row is present.

Apply \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-candidate-route-scope}. The positive surface is therefore candidate-mediated: the closed term row $T$ reaches a strong-normalization read only through the candidate evidence $C,E$, the candidate-to-SN row $S$, the adequacy boundary $A$, and the replay budget $P$.

Apply \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-nonescape}. Requests that ask for unconditional closed-term strong normalization, subject reduction, hidden application normalization, or a host evaluator have no positive coordinate in the carrier, so they are routed to $R$ and to the support rows that transport, replay, source, and name the refusal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC normalization budget candidate-route scope]
\label{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-candidate-route-scope}
Every positive normalization read from an accepted $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ packet factors through
$$
T,C,E,S,A,P.
$$
The row $S$ may be consumed as the candidate-to-strong-normalization route for the displayed evidence, and the row $A$ may be consumed as the displayed adequacy boundary, but neither row erases the candidate evidence or supplies closed subject reduction. The sibling packets of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicconfluenceauditwitness-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-subject-reduction-obligation-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closed-normal-consistency-boundary-namecert} therefore receive a budgeted normalization surface, not an unconditional theorem.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetCarrier\_candidate\_route\_scope}
\begin{proof}
Read the continuation coordinate $Q$. Its positive route reaches the strong-normalization read only after the term row $T$ has been paired with the candidate rows $C,E$ and the candidate-to-SN row $S$.

The adequacy row $A$ records the Pi and application boundary named in \autoref{ch:metacic-normalization-audit-map}. It lets consumers compare the route with confluence and subject-reduction siblings while preserving the guard that application normalization is not obtained from parts alone.

Finally inspect the provenance row $L$. It sends downstream readers to the confluence audit witness, the subject-reduction obligation packet, and the closed-normal consistency boundary as named consumers of the same finite budget. Those consumers inherit the candidate route; they do not receive a stronger closed-term normalization principle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC normalization budget non-escape]
\label{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-nonescape}
An accepted $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ packet cannot yield an unconditional theorem that every closed typed MetaCIC term is strongly normalizing. It cannot yield subject reduction, a hidden host evaluator, compiler semantics, quotient conversion, or application strong normalization without the displayed candidate evidence. Every such request is routed to the refusal row $R$ and the public rows $H,Q,L,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier inventory. The only positive normalization route is the displayed candidate-mediated route through $T,C,E,S,A,P$.

If a consumer asks for normalization without $C$ or $E$, the request has no positive route in $Q$. The packet therefore routes the request to the refusal row $R$, which records the missing evidence rather than filling it.

The remaining rows preserve the boundary. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport, continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming can carry the accepted packet to downstream chapters, but none of them adds a host evaluator, a subject-reduction theorem, a quotient conversion, compiler semantics, or an unconditional closed-term normalization coordinate.
\end{proof}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetCarrier\_nonescape}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC normalization budget candidate SN boundary]
\label{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-candidate-sn-boundary}
Every accepted $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ packet can export a strong-normalization read only when the displayed rows $T,C,E,S,A,P$ are read together. Dropping the candidate evidence row $C$ or the closed-candidate membership row $E$ routes the request to the refusal row $R$, and dropping the adequacy boundary row $A$ prevents application and Pi consumers from reading the route as closed subject reduction.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetCarrier\_candidate\_sn\_boundary}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-normalization-budget-carrier}. The positive read is not a single closed-term coordinate; it is the six-row route $T,C,E,S,A,P$ together with the structural rows that transport and replay it.

Apply the candidate-route scope theorem, \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-candidate-route-scope}. It forces every positive normalization read through the candidate evidence and adequacy boundary before downstream confluence, subject-reduction, or closed-normal consistency consumers may inspect the packet.

Finally apply non-escape, \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-nonescape}. Missing $C$ or $E$ has no positive continuation and is recorded by $R$, while missing $A$ leaves application and Pi reads at the boundary rather than turning the candidate route into closed subject reduction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC normalization budget falsifiable boundary]
\label{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-falsifiable-boundary}
Every accepted $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ packet that is queried for a normalization read while missing the candidate evidence row $C$ or the closed-candidate membership row $E$ must route the query to the refusal row $R$. In particular, an accepted packet whose public read exports strong normalization without both $C$ and $E$ is not a valid instance of the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-normalization-budget-carrier}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the carrier tuple. Its only positive normalization route is the displayed sequence $T,C,E,S,A,P$, while $R$ is the unique row assigned to requests that omit candidate evidence or closed-candidate membership.

Apply \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-candidate-sn-boundary}. The theorem states that a strong-normalization read is exported only when $T,C,E,S,A,P$ are read together. Therefore a request missing $C$ or $E$ has no positive continuation.

It remains to identify the public destination of such a request. By \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-nonescape}, missing candidate data is recorded by $R$ rather than supplied by a hidden evaluator, subject-reduction theorem, or unconditional closed-term normalization coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC normalization budget refusal exactness]
\label{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-refusal-exactness}
Every accepted $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ packet queried for a normalization read while missing $C$ or $E$ has exactly one public destination: the refusal row $R$. The request is transported by the componentwise row $H$ and replayed by the displayed continuation row $Q$, and it has no alternative route through $T,S,A,P$ or through any structural row outside $H,Q,L,N$.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp.MetaCICNormalizationBudgetCarrier\_refusal\_exactness}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-normalization-budget-carrier}. Its visible rows are $T,C,E,S,A,P,R,H,Q,L,N$, so the packet contains one refusal coordinate and no hidden evaluator, subject-reduction coordinate, host normalization coordinate, or second structural destination.

Apply \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-candidate-route-scope}. The positive route reaches a strong-normalization read only through the full candidate-mediated chain $T,C,E,S,A,P$. If $C$ or $E$ is missing, that route is not available as a public read.

Apply \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-nonescape} and \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-falsifiable-boundary}. They identify the missing-candidate query with the refusal row $R$ rather than with a positive normalization row.

Finally read the support rows. The row $H$ transports the same refusal judgment componentwise, while $Q$ replays the displayed refusal route to consumers and $L,N$ record provenance and local naming only. Hence the public destination is exactly $R$, with transport and replay but without an alternative positive or structural route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC normalization budget refusal transport]
\label{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-refusal-transport}
Let $B=(T,C,E,S,A,P,R,H,Q,L,N)$ be an accepted $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ packet. If a normalization query is missing the candidate evidence row $C$ or the closed-candidate membership row $E$ and is therefore routed to $R$, then componentwise $\hsame$ transport along $H$ and displayed $\Cont$ replay along $Q$ preserve the same refusal destination. No transported or replayed read creates a positive route through $T,S,A,P$, and the provenance and naming rows $L,N$ record only the same refusal boundary.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier through \autoref{def:metacic-normalization-budget-carrier}. The only displayed rows are $T,C,E,S,A,P,R,H,Q,L,N$, with $R$ the unique refusal coordinate.

Apply \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-refusal-exactness}. A query missing $C$ or $E$ has exactly one public destination, namely $R$, and it has no alternative route through $T,S,A,P$ or through structural rows outside $H,Q,L,N$.

Apply \autoref{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-nonescape}. Componentwise transport cannot supply the absent candidate evidence, and replay cannot manufacture an unconditional strong-normalization route. The rows $H$ and $Q$ therefore carry the same refusal judgment rather than changing its destination.

Finally read $L$ and $N$ as provenance and local naming rows. They source and name the transported refusal packet, but they do not add candidate evidence, membership evidence, a second refusal destination, or a positive normalization coordinate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC normalization budget independence boundary]
\label{thm:metacic-normalization-budget-independence-boundary}
The $\MetaCICNormalizationBudgetUp$ packet is not bijective to the confluence audit witness of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicconfluenceauditwitness-namecert}, the subject-reduction obligation packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-subject-reduction-obligation-namecert}, the closed-normal consistency boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closed-normal-consistency-boundary-namecert}, or the normalization frontier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacicnormalizationfrontier-namecert}. The reason is row-level: this packet alone combines the closed term row $T$, candidate evidence $C$, closed-candidate membership $E$, candidate-to-strong-normalization row $S$, adequacy boundary $A$, replay budget $P$, refusal row $R$, transport row $H$, continuation row $Q$, provenance row $L$, and local naming row $N$ as one finite carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compare the row inventory with the named siblings. The confluence audit witness records a confluence-facing route; the subject-reduction obligation packet records preservation-facing data; the closed-normal consistency boundary records consistency-facing data; and the normalization frontier records an open boundary for normalization claims.

The carrier of \autoref{def:metacic-normalization-budget-carrier} has a different public signature. It jointly stores $T,C,E,S,A,P,R,H,Q,L,N$, so it contains both the positive candidate-mediated route and the refusal row in the same NameCert packet.

A bijection to any listed sibling would have to preserve those rows and their route order. Since none of the siblings exposes exactly this combined candidate, adequacy, replay, refusal, transport, continuation, provenance, and naming surface, no listed sibling is the same packet under renaming.
\end{proof}
