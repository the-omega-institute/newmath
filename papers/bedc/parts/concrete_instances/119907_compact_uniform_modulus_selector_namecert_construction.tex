Following Bishop and Bridges compact metric analysis, the $\CompactUniformModulusSelectorUp$ packet records the finite selector that turns compact finite-net data and pointwise continuity rows into a tolerance-only uniform-modulus ledger. It realizes the compact-uniform-continuity route of \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity} through the compact source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, the metric rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the continuity surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuous-namecert}, the rational finite-minimum readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totalorder-namecert}, the $\RatUp$ arithmetic of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, and the downstream $\UniformModulusUp$ packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}. The packet names only a displayed finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ fold; it is not an open-cover compactness theorem, a host modulus function, a selected Lebesgue number, a sequential compactness argument, or an ambient completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Compact uniform-modulus selector carrier]
\label{def:compact-uniform-modulus-selector-carrier}
A $\CompactUniformModulusSelectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(X,K,F,T,B,R,L,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the shared $\MetricUp$ source row.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactUp$ finite-net row, exposing the bundled centers and coverage radii inspected by the selector.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousUp$ pointwise-modulus row over the same source.
\item $T$ is the positive $\RatUp$ tolerance request.
\item $B$ is the finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ ledger whose entries contain centers, local pointwise-modulus rows, subordinate radius rows, and half-radius cover data.
\item $R$ is the $\RatUp$ lower-bound fold row, using the finite-order selector from $\TotalOrderUp$ to produce a displayed radius below every bundled radius.
\item $L$ is the metric-triangle ledger joining compact coverage, pointwise moduli, and the selected radius.
\item $U$ is the terminal $\UniformModulusUp$ row exposed only after $B,R,L$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric, compact, continuous, tolerance, bundle, rational fold, triangle ledger, uniform-modulus output, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the finite selector route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactUniformModulusSelectorUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\ContinuousUp$, $\RatUp$, $\TotalOrderUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformModulusSelectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate compares accepted selectors only through these rows. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary open cover, selected point outside the bundle, host minimum, sequence extraction, quotient metric equality, countable choice, or ambient compactness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform-modulus selector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-modulus-selector-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactUniformModulusSelectorUp$ carrier $S=(X,K,F,T,B,R,L,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformModulusSelectorUp}$ obligations are exactly carrier admission, shared metric-source lock, compact finite-net exposure, pointwise-modulus exposure, positive rational tolerance exposure, finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ admission, rational lower-bound fold exactness, metric-triangle ledger replay, $\UniformModulusUp$ output admission, componentwise $\hsame$ stability, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, local naming, and non-escape from open-cover, choice, quotient, or ambient-completeness principles.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission projects the displayed rows of \autoref{def:compact-uniform-modulus-selector-carrier}. The source side is $X,K,F,T$, so the compact and continuous rows are locked to one metric carrier before the finite bundle is opened.

The selector stage is $B,R,L$. The bundle row lists the inspected centers and local radius data, the rational row folds those finitely many radii through $\RatUp$ and $\TotalOrderUp$, and the ledger row records the metric-triangle replay needed by the uniform-modulus consumer.

The output and structural stage is $U,H,C,P,N$. These rows expose, transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the excluded open-cover, host-minimum, choice, quotient, and completeness objects are absent from the carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform-modulus selector finite-fold handoff]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-modulus-selector-finite-fold-handoff}
Every $\UniformModulusUp$ read exported by an accepted $\CompactUniformModulusSelectorUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
X,K,F,T \longmapsto B,R,L \longmapsto U,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the selector first reads the common metric source, compact finite-net data, pointwise-modulus rows, and rational tolerance; then folds the displayed $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ radius ledger; and only then exposes the tolerance-only uniform-modulus output.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the source rows $X,K,F,T$. They fix the metric carrier, compact finite-net coverage, pointwise-modulus entries, and tolerance request available to the selector.

The finite fold is carried by $B$ and $R$. The row $B$ displays every bundled radius and center, while $R$ records the rational lower-bound selected by the finite order readback. No entry outside the bundle is available to the fold.

The metric-triangle ledger $L$ is then replayed before $U$ is public. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that same route, so a $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer receives only the finite selector package and no host compactness, selected Lebesgue number, arbitrary open cover, quotient equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness row.
\end{proof}
