\begin{theorem}[Finite cover bar modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cover-bar-modulus-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The compact source and bar-cover rows $K,F$ list the finite cover data before any local modulus is read. The row $L$ then attaches pointwise modulus data to the displayed cover members, while $D$ folds the resulting dyadic lower bounds inside the same finite packet.

The readback row $R$, terminal seal $E$, and uniform-modulus row $U$ are downstream of that finite fold.
