\begin{theorem}[Separated completion universal-arrow NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:separated-completion-universal-arrow-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}

Project the carrier of \autoref{def:separated-completion-universal-arrow-carrier}. Its rows display the separated reflection, completion object, universal arrow, finite window, regular rational readback, Real seal, factorization, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

The separatedness obligation is held in $S$ before completion is consumed. The completion row $M$ and universal-arrow row $A$ are downstream of that separated boundary, so a consumer cannot read a non-separated quotient representative as the source of the factorization.

The Real-facing part is held in $W,Q,E$. These rows expose finite windows, regular rational readback, and the terminal seal before any completion-facing consumer cites the packet.

Finally $F,H,C,P,N$ factor, transport, replay, source, and name only those displayed rows. Since no excluded principle appears in the carrier, the local NameCert surface is exhausted by the finite route above.
