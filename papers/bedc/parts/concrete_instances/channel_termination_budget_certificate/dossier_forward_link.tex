\begin{theorem}[Channel termination budget-certificate dossier forward link]
\label{thm:channel-termination-budget-certificate-dossier-forward-link}
Every accepted $\ChannelTerminationBudgetCertificateUp$ packet forwards its local delimiter budget and global-halting refusal rows to the sibling surfaces \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-channel-termination-halting-boundary-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-terminationrefusalboundary-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-certificatecompiler-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-typechecking-budget-namecert}. The forward link reads exactly the rows
$$
A,L,Z,F,G,R,H,C,P,N
$$
from \autoref{def:channel-termination-budget-certificate-carrier}: $A,L,Z,F$ certify the local channel delimiter budget, $G$ preserves the refusal of global certificate-level halting, $R$ exposes the sibling route, and $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and locally name the same dossier.
\end{theorem}

Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:channel-termination-budget-certificate-carrier}. The rows $A,L,Z$ display the active alphabet, legal-event row, and finite scan budget, while $F$ records the local delimiter endpoint.

Apply \autoref{thm:channel-termination-budget-certificate-local-global-boundary}. It keeps the global-halting refusal row $G$ and sibling route row $R$ in scope whenever a consumer tries to read the local delimiter budget as certificate-level halting data.

Read the sibling route. The row $R$ points to the channel termination halting boundary, termination refusal boundary, certificate compiler, and bounded typechecking budget surfaces, and the rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that same finite dossier by componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming.

Thus every accepted packet forwards the delimiter and refusal data to the stated sibling surfaces without adding a total halting classifier, unbounded parser oracle, host machine semantics theorem, proof-irrelevant equality, or bridge field.
\end{proof}
