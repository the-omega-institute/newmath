\chapter{Bishop Separated Cauchy Completion Idempotence NameCert Construction}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopSeparatedCauchyCompletionIdempotenceUp$ packet records the finite certificate by which a separated regular Cauchy completion read remains on the same displayed route when completion is requested again. It sits after the separated completion idempotence row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separated-completion-idempotence-namecert}, the regular Cauchy Hausdorff reflection of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-hausdorff-reflection-namecert}, and the Bishop separated real completion packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-separated-real-completion-namecert}. The packet names idempotence of the displayed separated regular Cauchy completion route; it is not quotient equality of completed objects, not a selected completed representative, not countable choice, and not ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.

\begin{definition}[Bishop separated Cauchy completion idempotence carrier]
\label{def:bishop-separated-cauchy-completion-idempotence-carrier}
A $\BishopSeparatedCauchyCompletionIdempotenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
J=(C,S,R,Q,B,E,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $C$ is the $\CompletionUp$ source row for the repeated completion request.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$ row, exposing the separated classifier before any repeated readback.
\item $R$ is the $\RegularCauchyHausdorffReflectionUp$ row, recording the Hausdorff-separated regular Cauchy comparison surface.
\item $Q$ is the $\BishopSeparatedRealCompletionUp$ row, giving the Bishop separated real completion route consumed by Real-facing readers.
\item $B$ is the regular Cauchy readback row, carrying the displayed finite windows and regular rational data for the completion request.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after $C,S,R,Q,B$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the completion, separated-idempotence, Hausdorff-reflection, Bishop separated completion, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a second separated regular Cauchy completion request through the same rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopSeparatedCauchyCompletionIdempotenceUp$, $\CompletionUp$, $\SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp$, $\RegularCauchyHausdorffReflectionUp$, $\BishopSeparatedRealCompletionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopSeparatedCauchyCompletionIdempotenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier for accepted packets compares only $C,S,R,Q,B,E,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient equality of completions, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality of completed points, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop separated Cauchy completion idempotence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-separated-cauchy-completion-idempotence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopSeparatedCauchyCompletionIdempotenceUp$ carrier $J=(C,S,R,Q,B,E,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopSeparatedCauchyCompletionIdempotenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the repeated completion source, separated completion idempotence row, regular Cauchy Hausdorff reflection row, Bishop separated real completion row, regular Cauchy readback, terminal Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming. A repeated separated regular Cauchy completion read must expose $C,S,R,Q,B$ before any $\RealUp$-facing consumer may inspect $E$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:bishop-separated-cauchy-completion-idempotence-carrier}. The rows $C,S,R,Q,B,E,H,T,P,N$ are the complete accepted inventory.

The separated idempotence row $S$ is read before the Hausdorff-reflection row $R$ and the Bishop separated real completion row $Q$. Thus the second completion request returns to the displayed separated classifier and regular Cauchy comparison surface before any Real-facing boundary is reached.

The regular Cauchy readback $B$ and terminal seal $E$ are downstream of those rows. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same finite route and add no coordinate for quotient equality, selected representatives, countable choice, host equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop separated Cauchy completion idempotent seal route]
\label{thm:bishop-separated-cauchy-completion-idempotent-seal-route}
Every terminal $\RealUp$ seal read exported by an accepted $\BishopSeparatedCauchyCompletionIdempotenceUp$ packet factors through
$$
\CompletionUp \longmapsto \SeparatedCompletionIdempotenceUp \longmapsto \RegularCauchyHausdorffReflectionUp \longmapsto \BishopSeparatedRealCompletionUp \longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
Consequently, applying separated regular Cauchy completion again returns to the same finite displayed route before the terminal seal is visible. The route is an idempotence certificate for repeated separated regular Cauchy completion reads, not quotient equality, not a representative selector, not countable choice, and not ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:bishop-separated-cauchy-completion-idempotence-namecert-obligations}. They force every seal-facing read through $C,S,R,Q,B$ before $E$ can be consumed.

Use the sibling separated idempotence surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-separated-completion-idempotence-namecert}. It supplies the repeated-completion boundary as finite row data, so the second request is not interpreted as equality of completion objects.

Now read the regular Cauchy Hausdorff reflection and Bishop separated real completion siblings, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-hausdorff-reflection-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-separated-real-completion-namecert}. These keep the Hausdorff-separated comparison and Bishop separated completion route visible before the terminal $\RealUp$ seal.

Finally use $H,T,P,N$ only as transport, replay, provenance, and local naming for the same displayed rows. Since the carrier has no selected representative, quotient equality, countable-choice schedule, host equality, or ambient completeness coordinate, none of those endpoints is exported by the seal route.
\end{proof}
