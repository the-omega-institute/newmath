\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompactoidUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-compactoid-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki and Bishop-style constructive uniform-space terminology, the $\CompactoidUp$ packet records a subset whose public reads are controlled by explicit finite entourage nets. It sits after the located uniform-space rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-uniform-space-namecert}, the finite-net metric surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-precompactmetric-namecert}, the compact metric consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the Cauchy-filter convergence boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-complete-uniform-space-namecert}, and the Real-uniform carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-uniform-space-namecert}. The packet names total-bounded uniform finite-net data for a displayed subset; it does not assert open-cover compactness, choice-selected finite subcovers, full locally-convex compactness, host powersets, or ambient completion.

\begin{definition}[Compactoid carrier]
\label{def:compactoid-carrier}
A $\CompactoidUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(U,L,T,M,K,E,B,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the ambient $\UniformSpaceUp$ entourage row, exposing the finite entourage filterbase, diagonal, refinement, symmetry, and composition data consumed by the compactoid request.
\item $L$ is the $\LocatedUniformSpaceUp$ row, recording the located membership and separation readback through which the displayed subset is inspected.
\item $T$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ finite-net schedule: for each displayed entourage or metric tolerance, it records a finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ of center names, membership rows, and coverage witnesses.
\item $M$ is the $\PrecompactMetricUp$ comparison row used when the compactoid subset is read through metric finite-net data rather than through a general open-cover statement.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ consumer boundary, available only as a finite-net compact-metric route and not as a selected subcover theorem.
\item $E$ is the $\CompleteUniformSpaceUp$ Cauchy-filter boundary through which completion-facing consumers may test whether the displayed finite nets remain inside the uniform completion route.
\item $B$ is the $\RealUniformSpaceUp$ handoff row for real-uniform consumers of compactoid finite-net data.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the uniform, located, finite-net, metric, compact-metric, complete-uniform, real-uniform, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a compactoid request through $U,L,T,M,K,E,B,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactoidUp$, $\UniformSpaceUp$, $\LocatedUniformSpaceUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\PrecompactMetricUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\CompleteUniformSpaceUp$, $\RealUniformSpaceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactoidUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $U,L,T,M,K,E,B,H,R,P,N$. No coordinate stores an arbitrary open cover, a selected finite subcover, a locally convex hull theorem, a host powerset, quotient equality of subsets, countable choice, or ambient completion.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compactoid NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compactoid-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactoidUp$ carrier
$$
C=(U,L,T,M,K,E,B,H,R,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactoidUp}$ surface consists exactly of ambient uniform-space admission, located subset visibility, finite entourage-net exposure, precompact metric comparison, compact-metric consumer handoff, complete-uniform completion boundary, real-uniform handoff, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.CompactoidUp.CompactoidCarrier\_namecert\_obligations}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compactoid-carrier}. The rows $U,L,T$ provide the ambient uniform data, located subset inspection, and finite entourage-net schedule.

Read the consumer boundaries next. The precompact metric row $M$, compact metric row $K$, complete uniform-space row $E$, and real-uniform row $B$ are the only downstream routes available to a compactoid reader.

Finally read $H,R,P,N$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same displayed rows, so the local NameCert surface has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover compactness, selected finite subcovers, locally-convex compactness, host powersets, quotient subset equality, countable choice, or ambient completion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compactoid finite-net absorption]
\label{thm:compactoid-finite-net-absorption}
Every finite-net read exported by an accepted $\CompactoidUp$ packet factors through
$$
\LocatedUniformSpaceUp \longmapsto \TotallyBoundedUp \longmapsto \PrecompactMetricUp \longmapsto \CompactMetricUp \longmapsto \CompleteUniformSpaceUp \longmapsto \RealUniformSpaceUp .
$$
The finite centers and coverage witnesses are absorbed through the displayed $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ row $T$ before any compact-metric, completion-facing, or real-uniform consumer may inspect the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the located uniform-space row $L$ and the ambient entourage row $U$. These rows determine which displayed subset and which entourage request the compactoid packet is answering.

The finite-net row $T$ then supplies the only centers, membership rows, and coverage witnesses available to the request. The precompact metric row $M$ can compare those witnesses with metric finite-net data, but it cannot replace them by an external cover.

The consumer rows $K,E,B$ are downstream of this finite-net schedule. Compact-metric, complete-uniform, and real-uniform consumers therefore see the same displayed centers and coverage witnesses, transported and replayed through $H,R,P,N$.

Since no row contains an arbitrary open cover, a selected finite subcover, a locally convex compactness proof, a host powerset, quotient equality, countable choice, or ambient completion, the absorption route exports only finite-net compactoid data.
\end{proof}
