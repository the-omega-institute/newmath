Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\BishopApartnessFieldEnvelopeUp$ packet records the finite envelope by which a regular real-name surface is admitted to constructive apartness-field use without turning the ambient Real object into a quotient field or a trichotomous ordered field. It sits after the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the apartness-real interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apartnessreal-namecert}. The envelope also exposes the neighboring apartness-budget discipline used by $\RegularCauchyApartnessBudgetUp$ consumers, but it names only the finite handoff rows visible to BEDC.

\begin{definition}[Bishop apartness field envelope carrier]\label{def:bishop-apartness-field-envelope-carrier}
A $\BishopApartnessFieldEnvelopeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(Q,R,A,B,F,M,T,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ source row, carrying the regular rational name that later field-facing consumers inspect.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row, available only after the regular rational readback route has been displayed.
\item $A$ is the $\ApartnessRealUp$ positive-separation row used when nonzero or inequality evidence is consumed constructively.
\item $B$ is the finite apartness-budget row, recording the dyadic or regular-Cauchy tolerance window required before $A$ may be read.
\item $F$ is the field-operation envelope row for addition, multiplication, negation, and reciprocal requests that already carry displayed apartness evidence for the reciprocal case.
\item $M$ is the mixed handoff row joining regular names, Real seals, apartness evidence, and field-operation requests.
\item $T$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for source names, Real seals, apartness evidence, budgets, field-operation rows, mixed handoff rows, continuation rows, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a regular name and a positive apartness budget through $R,A,B,F,M,T$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopApartnessFieldEnvelopeUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\ApartnessRealUp$, $\RegularCauchyApartnessBudgetUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopApartnessFieldEnvelopeUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $Q,R,A,B,F,M,T,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for trichotomy, quotient fields, selected reciprocal representatives, host field equality, countable choice, or full $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop apartness field envelope NameCert obligations]\label{thm:bishop-apartness-field-envelope-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopApartnessFieldEnvelopeUp$ carrier $E=(Q,R,A,B,F,M,T,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BishopApartnessFieldEnvelopeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, regular rational source readback, Real seal nonescape, apartness-positive evidence, finite apartness-budget exactness, field-operation envelope admission, mixed handoff replay, componentwise transport, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:bishop-apartness-field-envelope-carrier}. The source row $Q$ is read first, and the Real seal $R$ remains downstream of the regular rational route.

The apartness row $A$ is not a global order decision. It can be consumed only together with the finite budget $B$, so reciprocal and inequality-facing uses enter through displayed positive-separation evidence rather than trichotomy or host real equality.

The field-operation row $F$ and mixed handoff row $M$ collect only the operations whose inputs have already exposed the required regular and apartness rows. The structural rows $T,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no other coordinate is present, the certificate has exactly the stated obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bishop apartness field envelope regular real handoff]\label{thm:bishop-apartness-field-envelope-regular-real-handoff}
Every regular-real consumer of an accepted $\BishopApartnessFieldEnvelopeUp$ carrier reaches the $\RealUp$ seal only through the displayed route
$$
Q \longmapsto R \longmapsto M,T,C,P,N .
$$
Thus Real and Completion consumers receive a regular rational source row and a finite replay route before the Real-facing seal is visible, and they receive no quotient-field equality, selected representative, countable-choice schedule, or full $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $E=(Q,R,A,B,F,M,T,C,P,N)$. The row $Q$ is the only regular rational source coordinate, and $R$ is the only Real seal coordinate.

The mixed handoff row $M$ records how the Real-facing read is joined to the field and apartness envelope, while $T$ and $C$ transport and replay that same route. Provenance and local naming are carried by $P$ and $N$.

Because no quotient-field object, selected representative, choice schedule, or completeness witness is a coordinate of the carrier, every regular-real handoff reaches the seal only through the displayed finite rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

The carrier contains no trichotomy coordinate, quotient-field row, global equality decision, selected inverse, or completeness witness. Apartness-field consumers therefore receive only the displayed constructive handoff.
