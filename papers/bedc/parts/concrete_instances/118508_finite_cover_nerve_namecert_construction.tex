\begin{definition}[Finite cover nerve carrier]
\label{def:finite-cover-nerve-carrier}
A $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
N=(K,V,O,F,R,B,U,H,C,P,Q)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite cover source row, displaying a finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ of centers and cover-radius histories.
\item $V$ is the cover-member row, recording which displayed probe covers which inspected source point.
\item $O$ is the overlap-incidence row, recording finite pairwise and higher overlap reads among displayed probes.
\item $F$ is the nerve-face row, accepting exactly the finite probe lists whose overlap rows are displayed by $O$.
\item $R$ is the $\RatUp$ radius and lower-bound comparison row attached to the displayed cover radii.
\item $B$ is the rational-fold row that computes a finite positive lower-bound ledger from the accepted face and radius rows.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row consumed after the rational fold.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $K,V,O,F,R,B,U$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay through cover membership, overlap incidence, nerve faces, rational folding, and uniform-modulus handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\RatUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $Q$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverNerveUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,V,O,F,R,B,U,H,C,P,Q$. It has no coordinate for arbitrary open covers, selected subcovers, quotient equality of covers, trichotomy, countable choice, host set equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite cover nerve NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-cover-nerve-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$ carrier
$$
N=(K,V,O,F,R,B,U,H,C,P,Q),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteCoverNerveUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, cover-member exposure through $K,V$, overlap-incidence readback through $O$, nerve-face admission through $F$, rational radius comparison through $R$, finite rational folding through $B$, uniform-modulus handoff through $U$, and structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,Q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:finite-cover-nerve-carrier}. The accepted packet displays the finite cover source, cover-member row, overlap-incidence row, nerve-face row, rational comparison row, finite fold, uniform-modulus handoff, and structural rows.

Cover membership is exposed through $K,V$. Every membership read is tied to a displayed probe from the finite $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$, so the packet has no route to an arbitrary open cover.

Overlap and face obligations are exposed through $O,F$. A nerve face is admitted only when its finite overlap rows are already displayed, and the carrier has no separate coordinate for host-set intersection equality.

The radius and output obligations are exposed through $R,B,U$. The rational fold reads only the displayed radius comparisons before the uniform-modulus handoff is consumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite cover nerve compact-uniform handoff]
\label{thm:finite-cover-nerve-compact-uniform-handoff}
Every $\UniformModulusUp$ row exported by an accepted $\FiniteCoverNerveUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
K,V,O,F \longmapsto R,B \longmapsto U,H,C,P,Q .
$$
The route first reads compact-metric cover membership and overlap incidence, then accepts finite nerve faces, then folds rational radius data, and only then exposes the uniform-modulus handoff. It exports no open-cover compactness theorem, selected subcover, quotient cover equality, trichotomy, countable choice, host set equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier rows $K,V,O,F$. The compact metric source supplies the finite probe list, the member row records coverage reads, the incidence row records finite overlaps, and the face row records exactly the accepted finite nerve faces.

Next read $R,B$. The rational comparison row exposes radius histories, and the finite fold row records the lower-bound ledger used by the compact-uniform construction.

Finally read $U,H,C,P,Q$. The uniform-modulus row is downstream of the fold, while transport, replay, provenance, and local naming repack only the same finite cover-nerve data.
\end{proof}
