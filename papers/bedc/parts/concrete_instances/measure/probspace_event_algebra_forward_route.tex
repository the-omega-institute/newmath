\begin{theorem}[Measure ProbSpace event-algebra forward route]
\label{thm:measure-probspace-event-algebra-forward-route}
Every probability-space mass-normalization or event-algebra consumer reading $\ProbSpaceUp$ must first display the $\MeasureUp$ finite event-value surface, finite additivity readback, sigma-algebra public basis, and real-stream premeasure bridge before the probability normalization row is read. The route factors through the ProbSpace certificate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert} only after the Measure sibling routes \autoref{def:measure-root-sigma-algebra-public-basis} and \autoref{sec:measure-root-real-stream-premeasure-bridge} have exposed their event-basis and premeasure rows.

Thus a $\ProbSpaceUp$ consumer may normalize an event ledger only as a downstream read of the displayed $\MeasureUp$ event-value and premeasure surface, not as a direct host probability object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the $\MeasureUp$ finite event-value surface. Its carrier rows expose the finite event ledger and the value readback before any probability-space normalization is available.

Apply the finite additivity readback and then the sigma-algebra public basis of \autoref{def:measure-root-sigma-algebra-public-basis}. These rows identify which event algebra a downstream $\ProbSpaceUp$ reader is allowed to inspect.

Finally use the real-stream premeasure bridge of \autoref{sec:measure-root-real-stream-premeasure-bridge} and its carrier obligation \autoref{thm:measure-root-real-stream-premeasure-carrier-obligation}. It places the real-stream premeasure row between the event algebra and the probability normalization row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert}, so the ProbSpace consumer inherits only the displayed Measure route.
\end{proof}
