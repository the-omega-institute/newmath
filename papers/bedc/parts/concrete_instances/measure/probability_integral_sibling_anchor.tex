The $\MeasureUp$ hub supplies measurable-event rows and $\RealUp$-valued mass rows to \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-integral-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-probspace-namecert} only after the value row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} and the local $\NameCert_{\MeasureUp}$ route have been displayed. The probability and integral consumers therefore read the same finite event-value packet, rather than a detached measure, an independent probability primitive, or an ambient real-valued selector.
