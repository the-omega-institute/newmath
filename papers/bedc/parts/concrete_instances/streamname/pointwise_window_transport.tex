\begin{theorem}[StreamName pointwise finite-window transport]
\label{thm:streamname-pointwise-window-transport}
Let $s,t,s',t':\mathsf{BHist}\to\mathsf{BHist}$ be displayed $\StreamNameUp$ rows and let $\mathcal{P}:\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ be a finite certified observation window. Suppose the source window exposes
$$
\mathsf{RatStreamNameCarrier}(s),\quad
\mathsf{RatStreamNameCarrier}(t),\quad
\mathsf{RatStreamNameClassifier}(s,t),\quad
\mathsf{RatStreamNameFiniteWindowClassifier}(s,t,\mathcal{P}),
$$
together with the finite request ledger rows $\mathsf{UnaryHistory}(n)$ and $\mathsf{InBundle}(n,\mathcal{P})$ for every selected observation. If each selected observation carries pointwise transports $\hsame(s(n),s'(n))$ and $\hsame(t(n),t'(n))$, and the same $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert_{\StreamNameUp}$ rows replay the transported window, then the transported packet preserves StreamCarrier membership, StreamSame classification, and the finite request ledger:
$$
\mathsf{RatStreamNameCarrier}(s'),\quad
\mathsf{RatStreamNameCarrier}(t'),\quad
\mathsf{RatStreamNameClassifier}(s',t'),\quad
\mathsf{RatStreamNameFiniteWindowClassifier}(s',t',\mathcal{P}).
$$
The handoff exports exactly the same finite bundle, unary-history ledger, membership rows, pointwise $\hsame$ transports, replay, provenance, and local naming rows to downstream $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ consumers. It exports no quotient stream equality, host sequence equality, selected real value, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the source finite window. Carrier membership for $s$ and $t$, the StreamSame classifier, the finite-window classifier, and the ledger rows are all displayed before any downstream consumer reads the packet.

Apply the pointwise transport rows at each certified observation of $\mathcal{P}$. The rows $\hsame(s(n),s'(n))$ and $\hsame(t(n),t'(n))$ transport the opened $\BHist$ observations without changing the bundle membership or unary-history ledger.

Use the finite-window transport obligations of \autoref{thm:streamname-window-pointwise-transport} together with the RegSeqRat pointwise handoff of \autoref{thm:streamname-regseqrat-pointwise-handoff}. They identify the transported carrier and classifier rows over the same finite bundle and keep regular readback and Real sealing on the consumer side.

Finally replay the transported packet through the displayed $\Cont$ row and record its $\Pkg$ provenance and local $\NameCert_{\StreamNameUp}$ row. Since every exported coordinate is one of the displayed finite rows, no quotient stream equality, host sequence equality, selected real value, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness datum can enter the handoff.
\end{proof}
