\section{StreamName finite-window diagonal section}
\label{sec:streamname-finite-window-diagonal-section}

\begin{theorem}[StreamName finite-window diagonal section]
\label{thm:streamname-finite-window-diagonal-section}
Every finite diagonal section read by a $\RegSeqRatUp$ consumer and then by a $\RealUp$ seal factors through the same $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window request. The displayed route is the finite window row, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ budget row, the $\RealUp$ seal row, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ rows. The section exports no quotient stream equality, quotient equality of Cauchy presentations, selected limit, host real equality, countable-choice row, metric-completeness datum, or ambient completion coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite-window budget handoff of \autoref{thm:streamname-real-regseq-finite-window-budget-handoff}. It places every $\RegSeqRatUp$ regularity row, dyadic tolerance row, and $\RealUp$ seal row behind the same displayed StreamName finite observation-budget surface.

Next apply the finite-request section packet of \autoref{thm:streamname-real-endpoint-finite-request-section} and \autoref{thm:streamname-regseqrat-window-budget-section}. These results package the endpoint, readback, budget, StreamName window, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ rows as one finite request section rather than as separate diagonal sources.

Use the RegSeqRat handoff of \autoref{thm:streamname-regseqrat-real-seal-handoff}. The diagonal read reaches the $\RealUp$ seal only after the finite StreamName window and the RegSeqRat readback have been transported by $\hsame$ and replayed by $\Cont$ through the displayed support rows.

Finally apply the Real seal non-escape boundary of \autoref{thm:streamname-real-seal-request-section-nonescape} and the finite request seal lock of \autoref{thm:streamname-finite-request-section-real-seal-lock}. They exclude quotient streams, quotient Cauchy-presentation equality, selected limits, host real equality, countable choice, metric completeness, and ambient completion from the route. Thus the diagonal section is exactly the finite StreamName request transported and replayed through the listed BEDC rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[StreamName finite-window nested-refinement induction]
\label{thm:streamname-finite-window-nested-refinement-induction}
Let
$$
W_0,W_1,\ldots,W_k
$$
be an accepted finite chain of $\StreamNameUp$ windows such that each successor row $W_{i+1}$ refines the previous window $W_i$ by retaining the displayed pointwise rational observations and only appending later finite requests. Then the whole chain preserves the displayed pointwise rational observations, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert_{\StreamNameUp}$ row exposed by $W_0$. The induction exports no quotient stream equality, selected limit, host sequence equality, countable-choice schedule, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Proceed by induction on the finite refinement chain.

The base chain has only $W_0$. The conclusion is exactly the local finite-window diagonal section of \autoref{thm:streamname-finite-window-diagonal-section}: the displayed observations, transport, replay, provenance, and local NameCert rows are already present, and no excluded coordinate is available.

For the successor step, assume the claim for the chain ending at $W_i$ and read the accepted refinement row from $W_i$ to $W_{i+1}$. The finite-window pullback of \autoref{thm:streamname-regseqrat-window-budget-section} keeps the old rational observations visible inside the later window, while the reindexing route of \autoref{thm:streamname-real-regseq-finite-window-budget-handoff} places the same $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ consumers behind that retained window surface.

Transport is componentwise. The $\hsame$ row of the successor refinement agrees with the previously displayed observations on the old window and only names the appended finite requests separately. The displayed $\Cont$ replay and $\Pkg$ provenance therefore compose along the same finite route, and the local $\NameCert_{\StreamNameUp}$ row remains the one read by the refined window chain.

Finally, \autoref{thm:streamname-real-seal-request-section-nonescape} and \autoref{thm:streamname-finite-request-section-real-seal-lock} exclude quotient streams, selected limits, host sequence equality, countable choice, and ambient Real completeness at each successor stage. The finite induction therefore preserves exactly the listed StreamName rows for the whole chain.
\end{proof}
