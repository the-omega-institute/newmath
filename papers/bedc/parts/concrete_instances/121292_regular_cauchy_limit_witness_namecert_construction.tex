\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy limit witness NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-limit-witness-namecert-obligations}

$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyLimitWitnessUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the regular-Cauchy source family, diagonal modulus, dyadic tolerance and triangle ledger, finite windows, regular readback, terminal real seal, completion boundary, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item diagonal-stage exactness, requiring each output read to use the displayed modulus row $M$ and no selected representative outside the packet;
\item finite-window coverage, requiring every source and output observation to be named by $W$ and compared through the same dyadic ledger $D$;

\begin{proof}

The diagonal-stage clause reads the source family through $X$ and the stage selector through $M$. Because $M$ is a displayed row, the output representative is obtained by the finite diagonal recipe rather than by choosing a representative from a host completion.

The window and dyadic clauses then control the estimate. Every source read and output read is named by $W$, while $D$ supplies the tolerance, triangle, and slack data used by the diagonal regularity route of \autoref{thm:real-completeness-diagonal-regular}.

The regular readback row $R$ is therefore downstream of $X,M,D,W$. The terminal seal $E$ and completion boundary $B$ can be consumed only after that route has been replayed and transported by $H,C,P,N$. Since the carrier contains no quotient-stream, selected-limit, countable-choice, host-equality, or ambient-completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
