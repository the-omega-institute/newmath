\label{def:upperhemicontinuity-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ domain row whose finite probes supply the source windows.
\item $Y$ is the $\RealUp$ or finite-dimensional real codomain seal through which value coordinates are read.
\item $G$ is a finite graph-window ledger recording inspected source probes, value probes, and membership rows for the correspondence graph.
\item $K$ is the compact-value row naming the finite cover, located radius, and total-bounded witness for each displayed value fibre.
\item $R$ is the closed-graph readback row: whenever a source window and value window are replayed through $G$, the accepted value remains inside the displayed compact-value fibre.
\item $V$ is the polytope or convex-set dependency row used by optimization and control consumers when value fibres are exposed as $\PolytopeUp$ or $\ConvexSetUp$ packets.

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Upper hemicontinuity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:upperhemicontinuity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UpperHemicontinuityUp$ packet $U=(X,Y,G,K,R,V,O,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UpperHemicontinuityUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, compact-metric source exposure through $X$, real codomain sealing through $Y$, graph-window inspection through $G$, compact-value certification through $K$, closed-graph readback through $R$, convex/polytope dependency exposure through $V$, finite optimization-window handoff through $O$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:upperhemicontinuity-carrier}. The projected rows are exactly the domain, codomain, graph-window, compact-value, closed-graph, convex/polytope, optimization, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The analytic part is finite. A source probe reads $X$, a value probe reads $Y$, graph membership is inspected through $G$, and compactness of the fibre is read only through the displayed row $K$.

The upper-hemicontinuity boundary is the closed-graph readback row $R$ together with the same compact-value row $K$. The convex/polytope dependency $V$ and optimization window $O$ are downstream consumers of those rows, not independent value selectors.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this route. Since the carrier contains no coordinate for a host correspondence, selected value, open-cover proof, ambient closure theorem, quotient real equality, or optimizer, the listed rows exhaust the seed NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact-valued correspondence handoff]
\label{thm:upperhemicontinuity-compact-valued-correspondence-handoff}

$$
X \longmapsto Y \longmapsto G \longmapsto K \longmapsto R \longmapsto V \longmapsto O \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $U=(X,Y,G,K,R,V,O,H,C,P,N)$. The carrier places every consumer-facing row after the graph-window and compact-value rows, so no consumer can inspect $V$ or $O$ before reading $X,Y,G,K,R$.

The structural rows keep the same route under componentwise sameness and replay. Since $\ModelPredictiveControlUp$ reads only the displayed optimization window $O$ together with its provenance and local naming rows, it receives no selected optimizer, no ambient correspondence, and no open-cover compactness principle from the packet.
\end{proof}
