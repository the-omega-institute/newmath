\begin{definition}[Schur property carrier]
\label{def:schur-property-carrier}
A $\SchurPropertyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V$ is the $\VecSpaceUp$ row naming the finite vector expressions and scalar-action windows inspected by the packet.
\item $N$ is the $\NormUp$ row naming the norm observations on the same displayed sequence windows.
\item $L$ is the $\LinearMapUp$ row naming the finite family of linear-functional reads used to expose weak convergence.
\item $W$ is the weak-sequence ledger, recording the displayed functional tests and their finite tail bounds.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ or norm-induced distance row that reads the displayed norm tails as a metric convergence claim.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $V,N,L,W,M$ and for the replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from vector and functional windows through weak-tail checks into the norm-distance readout.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SchurPropertyUp$, $\VecSpaceUp$, $\NormUp$, $\LinearMapUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $A$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SchurPropertyUp}$ row naming exactly this finite weak-to-norm packet.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host Banach-space theorem, dual-space representation, selected subsequence, quotient equality of sequences, countable choice, or ambient completeness of the normed space.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Schur property NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:schur-property-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SchurPropertyUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SchurPropertyUp}$ surface consists exactly of vector-space exposure through $V$, norm observation through $N$, finite linear-functional tests through $L$, weak-tail bookkeeping through $W$, norm-distance readout through $M$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:schur-property-carrier}. The rows $V,N,L,W,M$ are the only mathematical rows visible before a sequence consumer can read weak convergence or norm convergence.

The structural rows $H,C,P,A$ preserve that same route. Transport is componentwise, replay follows the displayed functional tests and norm-distance readout, provenance names the same dependency packet, and local naming registers no extra analytic object.

Thus the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface. Since the carrier contains no host Banach-space theorem, dual representation, selected subsequence, quotient sequence equality, choice schedule, or ambient completeness field, no downstream consumer can obtain such data from the $\SchurPropertyUp$ packet.
\end{proof}
