Following Ahlfors and Beardon on disk automorphisms, the
$\PoincareDiskAutomorphismUp$ packet records the finite disk-map and inverse
rows by which a Poincare disk self-map is read as an automorphism before
hyperbolic metric, boundary, Schwarz--Pick, or composition consumers inspect
it. It sits after the disk metric packet of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-poincare-disk-metric-namecert}, the fractional
linear carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-mobius-transform-namecert},
the hyperbolic metric surface of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-metric-namecert}, and the boundary
transport packet of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hyperbolic-boundary-transport-namecert}. The
certificate names only displayed disk-map, boundary-transport,
hyperbolic-metric-preservation, inverse, composition, provenance, and
NameCert rows; it does not import host complex analysis, a completed
automorphism group, quotient boundary equality, or an unrecorded analytic
classification theorem.

\begin{definition}[Poincare disk automorphism carrier]
\label{def:poincare-disk-automorphism-carrier}
A $\PoincareDiskAutomorphismUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(D,M,U,V,B,K,I,J,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\PoincareDiskMetricUp$ disk-metric source row for the finite domain and codomain observations.
\item $M$ is the $\MobiusTransformUp$ coefficient and disk-action row whose determinant guard and disk-local action are visible in the packet.
\item $U$ is the disk-self-map row, recording that the displayed action sends admitted disk rows to admitted disk rows.
\item $V$ is the $\HyperbolicMetricUp$ preservation row, exposing equality of the displayed hyperbolic metric reads only through the finite disk-metric and Real-facing rows.
\item $B$ is the $\HyperbolicBoundaryTransportUp$ row, carrying the boundary readback induced by the same displayed disk map.
\item $K$ is the inverse row. It displays a second disk-map packet whose two composites replay to the identity rows visible on the admitted disk observations.
\item $I$ is the composition row, recording finite replay for composing two admitted automorphism packets before any group-style consumer may read the result.
\item $J$ is the refusal ledger excluding host analytic continuation, completed automorphism groups, quotient boundary equality, selected global normal forms, and unrecorded Schwarz--Pick equality.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for disk-metric, Mobius, disk-map, hyperbolic-metric, boundary-transport, inverse, composition, refusal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a disk-map request through Mobius coefficient inspection, disk admission, metric preservation, boundary transport, inverse and composition checks, refusal, provenance, and local naming.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PoincareDiskAutomorphismUp$, $\PoincareDiskMetricUp$, $\MobiusTransformUp$, $\HyperbolicMetricUp$, $\HyperbolicBoundaryTransportUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PoincareDiskAutomorphismUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through
$D,M,U,V,B,K,I,J,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a host analytic
automorphism group, quotient boundary equality, selected normal form,
unbounded complex-analysis theorem, or off-packet inverse.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Poincare disk automorphism NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:poincare-disk-automorphism-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PoincareDiskAutomorphismUp$ carrier
$$
A=(D,M,U,V,B,K,I,J,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PoincareDiskAutomorphismUp}$ surface consists exactly of
Poincare disk metric admission through $D$, Mobius coefficient and disk-action
admission through $M$, disk self-map admission through $U$, hyperbolic metric
preservation through $V$, boundary transport through $B$, inverse replay
through $K$, composition replay through $I$, refusal exposure through $J$,
componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance,
and local naming over the rows of
\autoref{def:poincare-disk-automorphism-carrier}. No obligation row asserts a
host automorphism group, analytic classification theorem, quotient boundary
equality, selected normal form, or arbitrary complex-analysis principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of
\autoref{def:poincare-disk-automorphism-carrier}. The visible map data are the
disk-metric source $D$, the Mobius coefficient row $M$, and the disk self-map
row $U$.

The metric and boundary obligations are then read from the same displayed map.
The row $V$ compares only finite $\HyperbolicMetricUp$ reads, while $B$ records
the induced boundary transport without exposing a quotient boundary object.

The inverse and composition rows $K,I$ are replay obligations over displayed
disk observations. They make the automorphism surface reusable by downstream
consumers, but they do not construct a completed automorphism group or select
a global normal form.

Finally, $J,H,C,P,N$ attach refusal, transport, replay, provenance, and local
naming to the same finite rows. Since no carrier coordinate stores host
complex analysis, quotient boundary equality, selected normal forms, or an
off-packet inverse, none of those objects can become a NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Poincare disk automorphism metric-boundary route]
\label{thm:poincare-disk-automorphism-metric-boundary-route}
Every $\HyperbolicMetricUp$-facing, $\HyperbolicBoundaryTransportUp$-facing,
or composition-facing consumer of an accepted $\PoincareDiskAutomorphismUp$
packet must read the finite route
$$
\PoincareDiskMetricUp,\ \MobiusTransformUp,\ \HyperbolicMetricUp,\ \HyperbolicBoundaryTransportUp .
$$
The metric-preservation row and the boundary-transport row are downstream of
the displayed disk-map row and upstream of inverse and composition replay.
The route exports no host automorphism group, quotient ideal-boundary
equality, selected disk automorphism normal form, or analytic classification
outside the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the disk-map request. The packet first exposes the
$\PoincareDiskMetricUp$ disk observations and the $\MobiusTransformUp$
coefficient action, then records disk self-map admission through $U$.

The hyperbolic metric and boundary rows are read next. The row $V$ can compare
metric values only after the disk self-map is visible, and the row $B$ carries
only the boundary transport induced by that same displayed map.

The inverse and composition rows are therefore downstream replay data. Rows
$K$ and $I$ can be consumed only after the metric-preservation and
boundary-transport surface has been named, and the structural rows keep the
same finite carrier under transport and provenance. Thus the consumer route
remains internal to BEDC NameCert data rather than to host complex analysis.
\end{proof}
