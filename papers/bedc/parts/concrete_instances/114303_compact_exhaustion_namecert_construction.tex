\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CompactExhaustionUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-compact-exhaustion-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, a compact exhaustion is read constructively as displayed finite exhaustion data: each public request exposes a prefix of compact metric windows, finite epsilon-net witnesses, and the metric and real rows that make those windows usable. The $\CompactExhaustionUp$ packet sits between the compact metric witness of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite net interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, the completion boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. It gives later compactness, completion, and continuous-image chapters a finite compact-exhaustion dependency without importing open-cover compactness or a choice-selected cover family.

\begin{definition}[CompactExhaustion carrier]
\label{def:compact-exhaustion-carrier}
A $\CompactExhaustionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
E=(X,K,F,M,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the ambient $\MetricUp$ source row, including the point classifier, distance observations, and finite history entries inspected by the exhaustion prefix.
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ window row. Its displayed entries $K_0,\ldots,K_m$ are the compact windows currently exposed to downstream consumers.
\item $F$ is the $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$ row attached to the displayed windows. For each requested positive real or rational tolerance already visible in the packet, $F$ records the finite net witnesses used inside the matching compact metric window.
\item $M$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ compatibility row, read only as a handoff boundary for consumers that need completion-facing metric data over the same windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ tolerance and radius seal row. It records the finite real-name data used by the epsilon-net requests and by any radius comparison attached to the compact windows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $X,K,F,M,R,C,P,N$ and for the local classifier row.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an exhaustion-prefix request through the metric source, compact metric window, finite-net witness, completion handoff, real seal, transport, provenance, and local name.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CompactExhaustionUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\FiniteEpsilonNetUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CompactExhaustionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $X,K,F,M,R,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, a selected cover family, a completed countable exhaustion, quotient equality of window schedules, or ambient real completeness beyond the displayed $\RealUp$ seal.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CompactExhaustion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-exhaustion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactExhaustionUp$ carrier $E=(X,K,F,M,R,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactExhaustionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, ambient metric compatibility, compact-window admission, finite-net tolerance readback, completion-facing handoff, real-seal accountability, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming for the rows $X,K,F,M,R,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-exhaustion-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly the ambient metric source, compact metric windows, finite epsilon-net witnesses, completion handoff row, and real tolerance seal.

The compact-window and finite-net obligations are ordered by the displayed prefix. A consumer first reads the metric source $X$, then a compact window from $K$, then the corresponding finite-net witness in $F$, and only then the completion or real-facing rows $M,R$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier contains no open-cover, selected-cover, completed-exhaustion, quotient-schedule, or ambient-completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CompactExhaustion finite-net induction]
\label{thm:compact-exhaustion-finite-net-induction}
Every finite-prefix consumer of an accepted $\CompactExhaustionUp$ packet can inspect the exhaustion by induction over the displayed compact-window list
$$
K_0,\ldots,K_m .
$$
At each stage the consumer receives the same route
$$
X\longmapsto K_i\longmapsto F_i\longmapsto R_i\longmapsto M\longmapsto H,C,P,N ,
$$
where $F_i$ is the finite epsilon-net witness row and $R_i$ is the real tolerance seal attached to the $i$th window. The induction exports no open-cover compactness, choice-selected subcover, hidden window after $m$, quotient equality of exhaustion schedules, or completed countable family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the displayed prefix. The carrier exposes only finitely many compact-window entries, so ordinary finite induction over $K_0,\ldots,K_m$ is available inside the packet.

At the base stage, the consumer reads $X$, the first compact metric window, its finite epsilon-net witness, and the matching real tolerance seal. The rows $M,H,C,P,N$ are then attached as completion handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

At the successor stage, the same projection is repeated for the next displayed window. No constructor reveals an undisplayed window or a selected cover; the induction step is only a replay of the next finite row already present in $K$ and $F$.

Thus the public exhaustion read is finite-prefix induction through $X,K,F,R,M,H,C,P,N$. Anything requiring a completed countable exhaustion, open-cover compactness, or choice-selected cover data must be supplied by a different packet.
\end{proof}
