\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension root finite-cover refinement obligation]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-root-finite-cover-refinement-obligation}
Every root $\CoveringDimensionUp$ read that asks for a finite-cover refinement must expose its $\BHist$ carrier rows through the compact-metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite-net boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, and the local root finite-cover siblings already routed by the hub. The resulting $\NameCert_{\CoveringDimensionUp}$ row is transported by $\hsame$ and replayed by $\Cont$ before any dimension consumer may read an order-bound witness.
\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.CoveringdimensionUp.CoveringDimensionRootFiniteCoverRefinementObligation}
\begin{proof}
Project the root carrier rows recorded by the covering-dimension child files. A refinement request first reads the compact-metric source and its finite epsilon-net boundary, so the cover data are finite before a dimension classifier can inspect them.

Apply the existing root finite-cover refinement and stability siblings. They keep the refinement row inside the displayed $\BHist$ packet and make the local $\NameCert$ row the only public endpoint of the finite-cover refinement.

Finally transport and replay the same rows through $\hsame$ and $\Cont$. Since the route has no hidden open-cover compactness or selected subcover coordinate, the order-bound witness remains a finite-cover NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\leanchecked{BEDC.Derived.CoveringdimensionUp.CoveringDimensionRootLebesgueLedgerObligation}

The covering-dimension root siblings then consume that ledger as support for the order-bound classifier. This keeps the carrier, finite cover, Lebesgue radius, and local NameCert data on one displayed route.

Transport by $\hsame$ and replay by $\Cont$ preserve the finite ledger rows. Therefore a downstream dimension consumer receives the compact-cover Lebesgue ledger as a finite NameCert obligation, not as an unrestricted host compactness principle.
\end{proof}
