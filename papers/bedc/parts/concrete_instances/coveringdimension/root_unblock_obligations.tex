\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension finite cover carrier obligation]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-finite-cover-carrier-obligation}
Every root $\CoveringDimensionUp$ carrier exposes finite cover rows over the displayed $\CompactMetricUp$ and $\MetricUp$ witnesses before any dimension read may inspect an order or refinement conclusion. In particular, the consumer must first see the compact finite-net row, the metric distance ledger, the finite $\BHist$ cover row, the refinement membership row, and the local $\NameCert$ row carried by the existing compactmetric route.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the hub route through \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-finite-cover-admission} and \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-compact-net-dependency-route}. These place the compact metric source, finite-net row, metric distance ledger, finite cover row, refinement row, and order ledger in the displayed carrier before any dimension-facing consumer reads a bound.

The structural rows then preserve the same route. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport keeps the compact and metric witnesses attached to the finite cover rows, displayed $\Cont$ replay fixes the order of the read, and the local $\NameCert$ row names only that finite surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension Real separability scale obligation]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-real-separability-scale-obligation}
Every scale-refinement read in a $\CoveringDimensionUp$ packet that touches Real-facing data must pass through the displayed $\RealSeparabilityUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance rows before the finite cover order is consumed. The scale row may refine cover radii and rational dense windows, but it may not replace the finite cover carrier by a host separability theorem or an ambient Real completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the Real-separability route of \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-real-separability-refinement-bound} together with the metric-completion cover route of \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-real-separability-metric-completion-cover-route}. These routes display rational dense windows, dyadic tolerance comparisons, and regular-rational seal rows before cover refinement and order-bound rows are read.

The same finite carrier is preserved by the $\BHist$ and $\Cont$ rows. Since the scale rows are downstream of the metric ledger and upstream of the finite order read, they cannot introduce a separate separability oracle, selected dense subfamily, or ambient Real completeness export.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension compactmetric non-escape obligation]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-compactmetric-nonescape-obligation}
The $\CoveringDimensionUp$ packet does not export open-cover compactness, choice-selected refinements, or host topological dimension. Its compactmetric-facing export is limited to displayed compact finite-net rows, metric distance ledgers, finite $\BHist$ cover entries, refinement memberships, order rows, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the compactmetric and metric routes already named by the hub. The finite cover admission, compact-net dependency, and finite-cover order non-escape results keep compactness, refinement, and order data inside the displayed carrier rows.

No row in that carrier names arbitrary open-cover compactness, a selected refinement outside the finite $\BHist$ cover, or a host topological-dimension theorem. The structural rows transport and replay only the finite packet, so every downstream read remains inside the CoveringDimension NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension compact-net cover root route]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-root-compact-net-cover-route}
Every root $\CoveringDimensionUp$ cover read over an accepted carrier
$$
D=(K,E,C,R,O,L,H,T,P,N)
$$
must expose the compact finite-net route
$$
\CompactMetricUp,\MetricUp \longmapsto K,E \longmapsto C,R,O,L \longmapsto H,T,P,N
$$
before a dimension-bound consumer may inspect the order row. The rows $K,E,C,R,O,L,H,T,P,N$ are the complete finite cover route: $K,E$ expose compact metric and metric finite-net data, $C,R,O$ expose cover membership, refinement, and order, $L$ exposes the $\CompactCoverLebesgueLedgerUp$ support, and $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compactmetric non-escape obligation, \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-compactmetric-nonescape-obligation}. It keeps compactness and metric information inside the displayed finite carrier rows rather than in an ambient open-cover principle.

Then read the finite cover carrier obligation, \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-finite-cover-carrier-obligation}. It places $K,E$ before $C,R,O$, so the order row cannot be consumed before compact finite-net and metric evidence are visible.

Finally use the Lebesgue-ledger dependency already carried by $L$. Since $H,T,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name the packet, the resulting root route is the displayed finite chain through $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, and $\CompactCoverLebesgueLedgerUp$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension refinement-order root route]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-root-refinement-order-route}
For every accepted $\CoveringDimensionUp$ carrier $D=(K,E,C,R,O,L,H,T,P,N)$, the refinement-order read is available only along the displayed route
$$
K,E,C,R,\CompactCoverLebesgueLedgerUp,O,L,H,T,P,N .
$$
In particular, a consumer must read the finite cover row $C$, the refinement membership row $R$, the order-bound row $O$, and the compact-cover ledger $L$ as one packet after the $\CompactMetricUp$ and $\MetricUp$ source rows have been displayed, and before any $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\RealSeparabilityUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, or $\RealUp$ completion-facing route may reuse the bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier coordinates. The refinement data are not a separate theorem: they are the rows $C,R,O,L$ of the accepted packet, downstream of $K,E$ and upstream of the structural rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-root-compact-net-cover-route}. It supplies the compact finite-net and metric prefix required before $C,R,O,L$ can be read.

The completion-facing siblings may consume the order only after this local refinement route is fixed. Thus $\MetricCompletionUp$, $\RealSeparabilityUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, and $\RealUp$ receive a finite refinement-order packet, not an external order oracle or selected refinement.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension Real-separability completion root route]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-root-real-separability-completion-route}
Any root $\CoveringDimensionUp$ read that reaches Real-facing completion data must factor through
$$
K,E,C,R,O,L,H,T,P,N
\longmapsto
\MetricCompletionUp,\RealSeparabilityUp,\RegSeqRatUp,\DyadicRatCoreUp,\RealUp .
$$
The first segment is the finite cover-refinement packet over $\CompactMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, and $\CompactCoverLebesgueLedgerUp$; the second segment supplies only completion-facing metric rows, finite rational or dyadic density windows, regular-rational readback, dyadic tolerance checks, and terminal real seals.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the scale obligation, \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-real-separability-scale-obligation}. It places $\RealSeparabilityUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ rows before a Real-facing scale read may consume the finite cover order.

Next use \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-root-refinement-order-route}. That theorem fixes the local packet $K,E,C,R,O,L,H,T,P,N$ before any completion-facing consumer reuses the bound.

The $\MetricCompletionUp$ and $\RealUp$ rows therefore receive only the displayed finite route. They cannot introduce arbitrary open-cover compactness, selected dense points, quotient completion equality, selected refinements, host dimension data, or ambient real completeness.
\end{proof}
