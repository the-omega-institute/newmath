The $\CoveringDimensionUp$ lattice reads finite cover order only after the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite epsilon-net schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-finite-epsilon-net-namecert}, the compact-cover Lebesgue ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-cover-lebesgue-ledger-namecert}, the completion-facing rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, the rational dense windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-separability-namecert}, and the finite face rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-simplicialcomplex-namecert} have been displayed.
