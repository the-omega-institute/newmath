\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension Cauchy forward link]
\label{thm:covering-dimension-cauchy-forward-link}
Let $D=(K,E,C,R,O,L,H,T,P,N)$ be an accepted $\CoveringDimensionUp$ finite cover-refinement packet. Any metric-completion or Real-facing consumer that uses the dimension bound must pass through the $\CauchyUp$ and $\RegSeqRatUp$ finite windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, together with the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, before the $\RealUp$-facing bound is consumed.

Thus the cover-refinement read reaches completion consumers as a finite route through compact finite nets, Cauchy windows, regular-rational readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. It does not export open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient completion points, host multiplicity, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the existing cover-refinement routes of \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-compact-net-dependency-route} and \autoref{thm:coveringdimension-refinement-order-root-route}. They expose the compact metric source $K$, finite-net row $E$, finite cover row $C$, refinement row $R$, order-bound ledger $O$, compact Lebesgue row $L$, and structural rows $H,T,P,N$ before the dimension bound can be read.

Attach the Cauchy and regular-rational windows. A completion-facing consumer reads the Cauchy route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-namecert} and the regular-rational readback through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}; these rows supply finite windows and tolerance ledgers, not quotient completion points or selected limits.

Now use the compact metric finite-net source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}. The finite-net row determines the sampled cover data that the refinement and order ledgers may consume, so the Real-facing dimension bound remains downstream of compact finite data.

Finally exhaust the packet rows. The consumer has only compact finite nets, Cauchy windows, RegSeqRat readback, cover refinement, order ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, quotient completion points, host multiplicity, and ambient Real completeness have no row in the accepted carrier.
\end{proof}
