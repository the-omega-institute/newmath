\begin{proposition}[CoveringDimension metric separability dependency route]
\label{prop:coveringdimension-metric-real-separability-route}
Every $\CoveringDimensionUp$ consumer that uses $\RealSeparabilityUp$ data factors through the metric-ball surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}. The exported route is only the finite chain

\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension metric-real dependency]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-metric-real-dependency}
Every $\CoveringDimensionUp$ finite-refinement read that uses $\RealSeparabilityUp$ must expose, in order, the $\MetricUp$ ball rows, the $\RealSeparabilityUp$ dense-window rows, and the local order-bound ledger of the covering-dimension packet. \end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The order-bound ledger is then a downstream read of the covering-dimension packet. Since neither the carrier nor the route premise contains a coordinate for selected dense points, arbitrary open covers, or external refinements, the dependency order remains metric balls, dense windows, and the local order-bound ledger.
\end{proof}
