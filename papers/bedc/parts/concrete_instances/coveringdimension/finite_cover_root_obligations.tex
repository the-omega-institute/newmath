\begin{definition}[CoveringDimension finite cover carrier]
\label{def:coveringdimension-finite-cover-carrier}
For an accepted $\CoveringDimensionUp$ packet, the finite cover carrier is the displayed $\BHist$ subpacket
$$
F=(K,E,M,C,R,O,S,A,H,T,P,N)
$$
cut out of the local carrier of \autoref{def:covering-dimension-carrier}. Here $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, $E$ is the finite-net row, $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ distance-read ledger used by the cover tests, $C$ is the finite $\BHist$ cover row, $R$ is the refinement membership row, $O$ is the order-bound ledger, $S$ is the $\SimplicialComplexUp$ nerve row, $A$ records any $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ seal rows consumed by a metric read, and $H,T,P,N$ are componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local $\NameCert$ data.

The carrier is finite at every exposed face. It contains no arbitrary open cover, selected subcover, host multiplicity oracle, quotient completion point, or ambient compactness theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension finite cover admission]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-finite-cover-admission}
Every root $\CoveringDimensionUp$ read that admits finite cover data must first expose the finite cover carrier of \autoref{def:coveringdimension-finite-cover-carrier}. In particular, the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net rows $K,E$, the $\MetricUp$ distance reads $M$, the finite $\BHist$ cover row $C$, the refinement row $R$, the order ledger $O$, any $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ seal rows $A$, and the structural rows $H,T,P,N$ are all visible before a dimension-bound consumer may read an order conclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier through \autoref{def:covering-dimension-carrier}. The compact source, finite-net row, cover row, refinement row, order ledger, and structural rows are already part of the displayed CoveringDimension packet.

Insert the metric and Real-facing reads only as rows of the same finite carrier. The $\MetricUp$ ledger records distance tests for cover membership, while the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\RealUp$ seal rows are read only when those tests are Real-facing.

Since no arbitrary open-cover coordinate occurs in $F$, admission is a finite row projection. The dimension-bound consumer receives the displayed finite cover route and none of the excluded host principles.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension order refinement route]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-order-refinement-route}
For a finite cover carrier $F=(K,E,M,C,R,O,S,A,H,T,P,N)$, the order-bound row $O$ is readable only after each member counted by $O$ has been traced through the finite cover row $C$ and the refinement membership row $R$. The route uses the same $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net rows, $\MetricUp$ distance reads, and $\BHist$ cover entries that admitted the carrier, and it exports no selected refinement or host overlap oracle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite cover admission theorem. It places $K,E,M,C,R$ before $O$ in the public route.

For every refined member, the row $R$ names a parent in the finite cover row $C$ and records the containment witness used by the distance-read ledger $M$. Thus the overlap count in $O$ is computed over displayed $\BHist$ rows.

The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name that finite computation. They do not add a second order source, so the order refinement route remains a finite CoveringDimension obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension simplicial nerve handoff]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-simplicial-nerve-handoff}
Every $\SimplicialComplexUp$ nerve read consumed by a $\CoveringDimensionUp$ finite cover carrier must use the same finite cover and refinement data
$$
C,R,O
\longmapsto
S
$$
after the compact finite-net rows $K,E$ and metric distance ledger $M$ have been displayed. The nerve row $S$ receives vertices and incidence rows from the finite cover carrier; it receives no classical nerve theorem, selected locally finite cover, or ambient topological realization.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier $F$. The cover row $C$ supplies the finite vertex list for the nerve, while $R$ supplies the refinement memberships that determine incidence.

The order ledger $O$ bounds the displayed overlap count before the nerve row is consumed. Therefore the $\SimplicialComplexUp$ row $S$ is downstream of the same finite cover-refinement route, not a detached simplicial object.

The compact finite-net and metric rows $K,E,M$ remain attached to the cover tests. Hence the handoff to the nerve surface is constructive row replay over the accepted carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CoveringDimension Cauchy metric scope]
\label{thm:coveringdimension-cauchy-metric-scope}
When a $\CoveringDimensionUp$ finite cover carrier is read by a Cauchy, RegSeqRat, or Real-facing metric consumer, the consumer may use only the displayed route
$$
\CompactMetricUp
\longmapsto
\MetricUp
\longmapsto
\RegSeqRatUp
\longmapsto
\RealUp
$$
inside the carrier rows $K,E,M,A$. The finite cover, refinement, order, and nerve rows $C,R,O,S$ remain the only dimension-facing rows, and no quotient completion point, selected limit, host metric equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness is exposed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project $K,E,M,A$ from \autoref{def:coveringdimension-finite-cover-carrier}. These are the only rows through which metric or Real-facing reads enter the finite cover route.

The regular rational and Real seal data in $A$ are downstream of the metric distance ledger $M$. They can certify the numerical side of a cover test, but they do not replace the finite cover row $C$ or refinement row $R$.

Finally use the order refinement and nerve handoff routes. They keep $C,R,O,S$ as the dimension-facing surface, so completion or Real-facing metric data remain scoped to the displayed finite carrier.
\end{proof}
