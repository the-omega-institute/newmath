\begin{definition}[Covering dimension finite cover-order carrier]
\label{def:covering-dimension-finite-cover-order-carrier}
For an accepted $\CoveringDimensionUp$ packet
$$
D=(K,E,C,R,O,L,H,T,P,N),
$$
the finite cover-order carrier is the displayed root subpacket
$$
F_{\dim}=(K,E,C,R,O,L,M,S,Q,A,H,T,P,N).
$$
Here $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, $E$ is the finite compact metric cover-support row, $C$ is the displayed finite cover, $R$ is the finite refinement map, $O$ is the order-bound ledger, $L$ is the finite Lebesgue-number and compact-cover ledger, $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ completion-facing metric row, $S$ is the $\RealSeparabilityUp$ finite density row, $Q$ records the $\RegSeqRatUp$ and $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ readbacks used by cover tests, and $A$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal boundary. The rows $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet.

The carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover compactness, choice-selected subcovers, quotient equality, selected dense points, host dimension theory, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Covering dimension finite refinement obligations]
\label{thm:covering-dimension-finite-refinement-obligations}
Every accepted finite cover-order carrier $F_{\dim}$ discharges its refinement obligations through the displayed rows $K,E,C,R,O,L,M,S,Q,A,H,T,P,N$. A downstream cover consumer must read compact metric cover rows, finite Lebesgue-number or compact-cover ledger rows, MetricCompletion rows, RealSeparability rows, RegSeqRat and DyadicRatCore comparison rows, and the terminal Real seal before it exports the order-bound ledger.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:covering-dimension-finite-cover-order-carrier}. The rows $K,E,C,R,O,L$ are the compact source, finite cover, refinement, order, and finite Lebesgue-ledger coordinates already visible in the parent $\CoveringDimensionUp$ packet.

Read the completion and separability rows before the order bound is exported. The $\MetricCompletionUp$ row $M$ supplies only displayed completion-facing metric data, while the $\RealSeparabilityUp$ row $S$ supplies finite rational or dyadic density witnesses; neither row chooses a subcover or a dense point outside the packet.

The comparison rows $Q,A$ then attach the RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, and Real seal reads to the same finite cover tests. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,T,P,N$ preserve that route.

Thus the refinement obligation is exactly the finite row family named in the statement. Since no excluded open-cover, choice, quotient, host-dimension, or completeness coordinate appears in the carrier, none can be exported by a downstream consumer.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Covering dimension Real-separability refinement route]
\label{thm:covering-dimension-real-separability-refinement-route}
Every $\RealSeparabilityUp$ or $\MetricCompletionUp$ consumer of a $\CoveringDimensionUp$ finite refinement row must factor through
$$
\CompactMetricUp,\ C,\ R,\ \FiniteLebesgueNumberUp,\ \MetricCompletionUp,\ \RealSeparabilityUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \DyadicRatCoreUp,\ \RealUp .
$$
The route exposes compact metric cover rows, finite cover order rows, finite Lebesgue-number and refinement siblings, completion-facing metric rows, finite density rows, regular-rational and dyadic comparison rows, and the Real seal boundary before any dimension-bound order row is consumed. It exports no arbitrary open-cover compactness, choice-selected subcover, quotient equality, selected approximating sequence, selected dense point, host dimension theorem, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the finite refinement obligations of \autoref{thm:covering-dimension-finite-refinement-obligations}. They force the consumer to open the compact source $K$, finite cover $C$, refinement map $R$, order ledger $O$, and finite Lebesgue ledger $L$ as displayed cover-order rows.

Next read the completion and density dependencies. The $\MetricCompletionUp$ row is a finite completion-facing metric handoff, and the $\RealSeparabilityUp$ row is a finite density handoff; both are inserted before the order ledger is consumed, so neither can act as an external selector.

Then apply the RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, and Real seal rows. They provide the comparison and terminal seal data used by the cover tests, but they do not add quotient Real equality or ambient completeness.

The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ replay the same finite route for downstream cover and compactness chapters. Because each step is a displayed carrier row, the resulting route refuses open-cover compactness, choice-selected subcovers, quotient equality, selected approximating sequences, selected dense points, host dimension theorems, and ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
