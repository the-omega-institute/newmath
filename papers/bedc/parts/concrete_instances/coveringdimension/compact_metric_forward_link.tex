\begin{theorem}[Covering dimension compact metric forward link]
\label{thm:covering-dimension-compact-metric-forward-link}
Every compact-cover or finite-net read exported by $\CoveringDimensionUp$ first passes through the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row and the $\MetricUp$ ball-observation row before a dimension-bound consumer may inspect the finite cover row, refinement map, order-bound ledger, compact Lebesgue ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:covering-dimension-carrier}. The compact source row $K$ is the only entry point for compact-cover and finite-net data, and its pointwise observations are read through the metric ball surface before a cover member can be tested.

Only after this compact-metric and metric route is visible may the finite cover row $C$, refinement row $R$, order-bound row $O$, and compact Lebesgue ledger $L$ be consumed as a dimension-bound surface. The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name the same route without adding an arbitrary open-cover compactness principle.
\end{proof}
