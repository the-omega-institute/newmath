\chapter{Real Archimedean Cutoff NameCert Construction}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-real-archimedean-cutoff-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the
$\RealArchimedeanCutoffUp$ packet records the finite Archimedean cutoff row
for displayed $\RealUp$ names. It reads a real name only through finite
$\StreamNameUp$ windows, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, a $\DyadicRatCoreUp$
tolerance ledger, and a $\NatUp$ unary bound history, so the cutoff certificate
stays inside displayed BEDC data and does not claim trichotomy, quotient reals,
least upper bounds, choice, or ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[RealArchimedeanCutoff carrier]
\label{def:real-archimedean-cutoff-carrier}
A $\RealArchimedeanCutoffUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(R,S,G,D,U,L,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal whose displayed name is being cut off
at a finite Archimedean scale.
\item $S$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ window family opened from $R$ at the
requested precisions.
\item $G$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to those windows, so
every inspected rational value has a regular sequence source.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger comparing the inspected
readback values with the displayed dyadic cutoff scale.
\item $U$ is the $\NatUp$ and $\mathsf{UnaryHistory}$ bound row naming the
finite number of window extensions used by the cutoff read.
\item $L$ is the finite cutoff ledger pairing each requested precision with
the displayed stream window, regular readback, dyadic tolerance, and unary
bound row used for that precision.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the Real seal, stream
windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, unary bound row, cutoff
ledger, replay rows, provenance row, and local naming row.
\item $T$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a cutoff request through
$R,S,G,D,U,L,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RealArchimedeanCutoffUp$,
$\RealUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\NatUp$,
$\mathsf{UnaryHistory}$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealArchimedeanCutoffUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier contains only the displayed finite cutoff surface. It has no
trichotomy row, no quotient equality of real names, no selected representative
stream, no least-upper-bound object, no choice selector, and no completeness
coordinate.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RealArchimedeanCutoff NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-archimedean-cutoff-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealArchimedeanCutoffUp$ carrier
$C=(R,S,G,D,U,L,H,T,P,N)$, the local
$\NameCert_{\RealArchimedeanCutoffUp}$ surface consists exactly of the
following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the Real seal, stream windows, regular readback,
dyadic tolerance ledger, unary bound row, cutoff ledger, componentwise
transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item classifier stability, requiring every classified cutoff read to
transport $R,S,G,D,U,L$ through the stored $\hsame$ and $\Cont$ routes;
\item finite bound readback, requiring the unary row $U$ to expose the finite
number of window extensions used before the cutoff ledger $L$ is accepted;
\item Real seal nonescape, requiring every completion-facing consumer to read
$S,G,D,U,L,H,T,P,N$ before it receives the $\RealUp$ seal row $R$;
\item ledger exactness, requiring each accepted cutoff comparison to be the
displayed row in $L$ that pairs the relevant $\StreamNameUp$ window,
$\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance entry, and
$\NatUp$ or $\mathsf{UnaryHistory}$ bound.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from the tuple in
\autoref{def:real-archimedean-cutoff-carrier}. The Real seal, finite stream
windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, unary bound row, cutoff
ledger, transport table, replay table, provenance row, and local naming row
are all explicit coordinates of the accepted packet.

Classifier stability is componentwise. The stored $\hsame$ and $\Cont$ rows
transport the Real seal together with the stream windows and regular readback,
and they transport the dyadic tolerance and unary bound rows beside the same
cutoff ledger.

Finite bound readback then uses only $U$ and $L$. The unary row names how many
window extensions are opened, while the ledger records which displayed
window, readback, and dyadic tolerance entry is used at each requested
precision.

Finally inspect the carrier inventory. Since the packet has no coordinate for
trichotomy, quotient equality of real names, selected representatives,
least-upper-bound machinery, choice, or ambient completeness, every
completion-facing read remains inside the displayed finite cutoff surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RealArchimedeanCutoff sibling dependency]
\label{thm:real-archimedean-cutoff-sibling-dependency}
Every accepted $\RealArchimedeanCutoffUp$ read factors through the property-level Archimedean bound route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-archimedean-property-namecert} and the Cauchy-real Archimedean witness route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-real-archimedean-namecert} before the local cutoff ledger $L$ can be exported. The cutoff certificate is a bounded finite read of the same Archimedean witness surface through $R,S,G,D,U,L,H,T,P,N$; it is not a least-bound principle, trichotomy principle, quotient-real equality route, or ambient completeness claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:real-archimedean-cutoff-carrier}. The rows $R,S,G,D,U,L$ expose the Real seal, finite stream windows, regular readback, dyadic tolerance, unary bound history, and cutoff ledger.

The property-level sibling supplies the Archimedean bound route used to interpret the unary bound row. The Cauchy-real sibling supplies the witness route that makes the same bound readable from regular Cauchy real-name data. Thus the cutoff read cannot export $L$ until the property route and the Cauchy-real witness route have both been displayed.

The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ preserve that dependency through componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local naming. Since no row in $R,S,G,D,U,L,H,T,P,N$ is a least-bound object, trichotomy classifier, quotient-real selector, or Real-completeness witness, the dependency remains a finite bounded read of the Archimedean witness surface.
\end{proof}
