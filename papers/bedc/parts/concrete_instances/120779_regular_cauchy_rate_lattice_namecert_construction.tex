\begin{definition}[Regular Cauchy rate lattice carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-rate-lattice-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchyRateLatticeUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance and comparison row used by every rate entry.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row recording the inspected Cauchy schedules.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback attached to the same windows.
\item $A$ is the displayed rate-ledger row listing finitely many regular Cauchy rate entries.
\item $M$ is the meet row, storing the finite common-refinement rate.
\item $J$ is the join row, storing the finite coarser public rate bound.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the rate ledger, operations, readback rows, seal, replay, provenance, and local name.

\begin{theorem}[Regular Cauchy rate lattice obligation surface]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-rate-lattice-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchyRateLatticeUp$ carrier $L=(D,S,R,A,M,J,F,Q,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchyRateLatticeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed rate ledger; dyadic tolerance admission through $D$; finite window admission through $S$; regular rational readback through $R$; meet, join, and refinement exposure through $M,J,F$; terminal real sealing through $Q$; and structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,C,P,N$.

Every Real or Completion consumer that combines regular Cauchy rates through this packet must read the finite ledger row $A$, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row $D$, the $\StreamNameUp$ window row $S$, and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$ before the meet, join, refinement, or $\RealUp$ seal rows are public.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-rate-lattice-carrier}. The rate-facing data are exactly the finite ledger $A$, the dyadic tolerance row $D$, the stream window $S$, and the regular rational readback $R$.

The lattice operations are finite row operations. The meet row $M$ records a displayed common refinement, the join row $J$ records a displayed coarser public bound, and the refinement row $F$ records the comparison route between rate ledgers.

The consumer-facing read is delayed until the same finite route has been displayed. The seal row $Q$ is downstream of $D,S,R,A,M,J,F$, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name that route.

No remaining coordinate can choose a modulus functional, quotient streams, a limit, a trichotomy branch, or a completeness principle. The listed rows therefore exhaust the obligation surface.
\end{proof}
