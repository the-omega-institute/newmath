\begin{definition}[Ledger-mode classifier carrier]
\label{def:ledger-mode-classifier-carrier}
A $\LedgerModeClassifierUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(E,C,F,R,G,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $E$ is the evidence-mode row, recording for each classifier-relevant NameCert field whether its support is empirical, paper, formal, hybrid, ledger-only, or open.
\item $C$ is the mode-completeness row, asserting that every source, pattern, classifier, stability, and ledger field has a declared mode and that no accepted row remains open.
\item $F$ is the hardening-frontier row, listing the hardenable rows that are not yet hardened and separating them from the non-hardenable residue.
\item $R$ is the ledger-only residue row, recording the exact non-hardenable or not-yet-hardened residue that must remain visible to classifier-strength consumers.
\item $G$ is the gap-refusal row, refusing any classifier-strength read when a required evidence-mode row, hardening-frontier row, or ledger-only residue row is absent.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for evidence modes, mode completeness, hardening frontier, residue exactness, gap refusal, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a classifier-strength request through $E,C,F,R,G,H$ and back to the same ledger-mode surface.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LedgerModeClassifierUp$, the hard-certificate layer, certified model quality, positive interpretability classifier novelty, Tensor NameCert, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LedgerModeClassifierUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a hidden hardened proof, unledgered classifier strength, open-mode acceptance, certified model quality by itself, interpretability novelty by itself, or Tensor NameCert field by itself.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Ledger-mode classifier NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:ledger-mode-classifier-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LedgerModeClassifierUp$ carrier $M=(E,C,F,R,G,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LedgerModeClassifierUp}$ surface consists exactly of evidence-mode exposure through $E$, mode-completeness through $C$, hardening-frontier exposure through $F$, ledger-only residue exactness through $R$, gap refusal through $G$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $T$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. Any classifier-strength consumer must read those rows before it may inspect ledger-only status.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier in \autoref{def:ledger-mode-classifier-carrier}. The rows $E,C,F,R,G$ are the analytic rows: they display evidence modes, certify mode completeness, expose the hardening frontier, record the ledger-only residue, and refuse gap-bearing reads.

The structural rows $H$ and $T$ preserve and replay the same surface. A transported or replayed classifier-strength request therefore still carries the evidence-mode ledger and hardening-frontier exposure before ledger-only status is inspected.

The provenance and local naming rows $P,N$ attach the packet to its philosophy and model-quality siblings. Since the carrier has no hidden hardened proof, no unledgered strength row, and no open-mode acceptance coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Ledger-mode classifier consumer boundary]
\label{thm:ledger-mode-classifier-consumer-boundary}
Every accepted $\LedgerModeClassifierUp$ read exposes its evidence-mode ledger $E$, mode-completeness row $C$, hardening-frontier row $F$, ledger-only residue row $R$, and gap-refusal row $G$ before any certified model-quality, interpretability-novelty, Tensor NameCert, or classifier-strength consumer may inspect ledger-only status. A consumer that omits any of these rows is outside the accepted ledger-mode classifier surface.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the NameCert obligations. They require $E,C,F,R,G$ as the analytic rows of every accepted packet, with $H,T,P,N$ preserving, replaying, sourcing, and naming the same data.

The certified model-quality, interpretability-novelty, Tensor NameCert, and classifier-strength consumers named by $P$ receive only the exported packet. If such a consumer omits $E$, it cannot determine evidence mode; if it omits $C$, it cannot determine mode completeness; if it omits $F$ or $R$, it cannot separate hardenable frontier from ledger-only residue; and if it omits $G$, it cannot refuse a mode gap.

Thus ledger-only status is not a free-standing predicate. It is inspected only after the displayed evidence-mode, completeness, frontier, residue, and refusal rows have been read.
\end{proof}
