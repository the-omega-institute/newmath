\label{thm:luenberger-observer-state-space-dependency-route}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:luenberger-observer-carrier}. The plant row $A$ and output row $C$ are meaningful only after the state-space and linear-map substrates have been displayed.

Next read the observability dependency route. It exposes the output-map, residual, observer-gain, and error-ledger rows through the accepted observability and Gramian surfaces before the deterministic observer interface is public.

Finally compose the Kalman-filter boundary route and the observer-controller forward route. These sibling routes keep stochastic covariance and controller synthesis outside the observer carrier while allowing the estimator-error ledger $E$ to be consumed as deterministic state-space data.

The structural rows $H,T,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly this route. Since the carrier has no coordinate for hidden state trajectories, covariance recursion, pole placement, or host convergence, every estimator-error export factors through the displayed state-space dependency route.
\end{proof}
