\begin{definition}[MetaCIC residual-substitution comparison carrier]
\label{def:metacic-residual-substitution-comparison-carrier}
A $\MetaCICResidualSubstitutionComparisonUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(T_0,T_1,S_0,S_1,J,O,Q,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T_0$ and $T_1$ are the typed-window rows. They display the two finite MetaCIC term windows whose residual substitution traces are compared.
\item $S_0$ and $S_1$ are the residual-substitution trace rows. They record the two substituted residual branches and the binder windows used by the comparison.
\item $J$ is the local-join row. It records the bounded local join attempt between the two residual traces, without promoting the attempt to a full parallel diamond.
\item $O$ is the obstruction-retention row. It keeps the named substitution-compatibility boundary visible whenever the local join is conditional.
\item $Q$ is the bounded-checker row. It records the finite checker requests used to compare the typed windows, residual traces, local join, and obstruction row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the typed windows, residual traces, local join, obstruction retention, bounded checker, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
T_0,T_1 \longmapsto S_0,S_1 \longmapsto J,O,Q \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MetaCICResidualSubstitutionComparisonUp$, MetaCIC typed windows, residual substitution traces, local join data, obstruction rows, bounded checker data, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MetaCICResidualSubstitutionComparisonUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only by these displayed typed-window, residual-trace, local-join, obstruction-retention, bounded-checker, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows. It has no coordinate for global confluence, premise-free substitution compatibility, arbitrary closed Church--Rosser, quotient syntax, or host evaluator soundness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC residual-substitution comparison NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metacic-residual-substitution-comparison-namecert-obligations}

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MetaCICResidualSubstitutionComparisonUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the typed windows, residual-substitution traces, local join, obstruction retention, bounded checker, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item typed-window exposure, requiring $T_0$ and $T_1$ to be read before either residual trace is compared;
\item residual-trace comparison, requiring $S_0$ and $S_1$ to remain paired with their typed windows and binder windows;
\item local-join boundedness, requiring $J$ to record only the displayed local join attempt inspected by $Q$;
\item obstruction retention, requiring $O$ to keep the residual-substitution compatibility boundary visible whenever the comparison is conditional;
\item provenance exhaustion, requiring $H,C,P,N$ to transport, replay, source, and name exactly the displayed rows.
\end{enumerate}

\end{theorem}

The local join row $J$ is bounded by the checker row $Q$. Thus the packet can record a finite comparison between two residual traces without claiming a global parallel diamond.

The obstruction row $O$ remains visible whenever the join is conditional. This matches the premise-retention discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-residual-substitution-boundary-namecert} and keeps the confluence audit map from silently treating a named input as a theorem.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no coordinate supplies global confluence, premise-free substitution compatibility, arbitrary closed Church--Rosser, quotient syntax, or host evaluator soundness, the obligation surface is exactly the finite comparison packet displayed above.
\end{proof}
