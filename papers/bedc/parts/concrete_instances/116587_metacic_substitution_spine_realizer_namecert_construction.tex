\begin{theorem}[MetaCIC substitution spine realizer boundary]
\label{thm:metacic-substitution-spine-realizer-boundary}
For every accepted $\MetaCICSubstitutionSpineRealizerUp$ carrier
$$

\begin{proof}

Transport and replay through $H$ and $C$ preserve the same row order, while $P$ and $N$ attach provenance and local naming. Since no carrier coordinate stores unrestricted substitution commutation, beta preservation, closed subject reduction, quotient syntax equality, host evaluator soundness, erased derivation semantics, or kernel consistency, the consumer cannot export any of those endpoints from this packet.
\end{proof}
