\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DiscreteGronwallUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-discrete-gronwall-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bellman, Gronwall, and Bishop--Bridges finite induction practice, the $\DiscreteGronwallUp$ packet records the stepwise inequality ledger used when a Cauchy-rate or Picard-style error estimate is bounded by a finite product-and-sum majorant. It sits beside the continuous envelope route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-gronwall-inequality-namecert}, the Picard iteration surfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-picard-iteration-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-picard-error-estimate-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-picard-threshold-cauchy-handoff-namecert}, the Cauchy-rate criteria of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-criterion-equivalence-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-modulus-dominance-criterion-namecert}, the summable-tail ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-summable-tail-modulus-namecert}, and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite dyadic induction boundary; it is not a continuous differential inequality, not a selected infinite product, and not an ambient completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[DiscreteGronwall finite-step carrier]
\label{def:discrete-gronwall-finite-step-carrier}
A $\DiscreteGronwallUp$ finite-step carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
G=(I,U,A,B,T,Q,R,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is a finite initial segment of step indices with a distinguished first step and successor relation;
\item $U$ is the nonnegative error ledger recording the displayed value at each step of $I$;
\item $A$ is the dyadic multiplicative coefficient ledger, with each coefficient read through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$;
\item $B$ is the dyadic additive inhomogeneity ledger;
\item $T$ is the finite product-and-sum majorant row generated by primitive recursion along $I$;
\item $Q$ is the $\SummableTailModulusUp$ row used when the additive ledger is read as a Cauchy tail;
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the dyadic bounds;
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row exposed to Real or Completion consumers;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the index, error, coefficient, tail, readback, seal, continuation, package, and local naming rows;
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite induction;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DiscreteGronwallUp$, $\GronwallInequalityUp$, $\PicardIterationUp$, $\PicardErrorEstimateUp$, $\PicardThresholdCauchyHandoffUp$, $\SummableTailModulusUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DiscreteGronwallUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares two accepted carriers only through the finite index segment, displayed error ledger, dyadic coefficient rows, primitive-recursive majorant, summable-tail read, regular-rational readback, Real seal, transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[DiscreteGronwall NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:discrete-gronwall-namecert-obligations}
For a carrier $G=(I,U,A,B,T,Q,R,S,H,C,P,N)$ of \autoref{def:discrete-gronwall-finite-step-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\DiscreteGronwallUp}$ surface has six obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier exposure of the finite tuple $I,U,A,B,T,Q,R,S,H,C,P,N$;
\item step admission for the first step and successor reads in $I$;
\item dyadic coefficient admission for every row of $A$ and $B$;
\item primitive induction exactness for the majorant row $T$;
\item Cauchy-rate handoff through $Q,R,S$ when a consumer reads a tail estimate;
\item non-escape from continuous Gronwall arguments, selected infinite products, countable choice, quotient real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier exposure is coordinate projection from the displayed finite tuple. The classifier has no source outside $I,U,A,B,T,Q,R,S,H,C,P,N$.

Step admission and dyadic coefficient admission are read before the induction starts. The first-step row and successor relation bound the recursion to the finite segment $I$, while $A$ and $B$ force each multiplicative and additive coefficient through the dyadic ledger.

Primitive induction exactness is then obtained by induction over $I$. At the first step, $T$ records the displayed initial bound. At a successor step, the previous majorant is multiplied by the visible coefficient row and then enlarged by the visible additive row, so the next error entry of $U$ is bounded by the next entry of $T$.

For a Cauchy-rate consumer, the terminal read is through $Q,R,S$. The summable-tail row supplies the finite tail modulus, the regular-rational row repacks the dyadic estimates, and the Real seal exposes the same bound to Real and Completion consumers.

Since no other coordinate is present, the packet cannot export a continuous Gronwall theorem, a selected infinite product, countable choice, quotient real equality, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[DiscreteGronwall finite induction boundary]
\label{thm:discrete-gronwall-finite-induction-boundary}
Every accepted $\DiscreteGronwallUp$ packet bounds its displayed error ledger only by primitive induction over the finite step segment $I$. Picard-style iteration and L10 Cauchy-rate consumers must read the dyadic coefficient ledgers $A,B$, the recursive majorant $T$, the $\SummableTailModulusUp$ row $Q$, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$, and the $\RealUp$ seal $S$ before exporting a rate bound. The packet exports no continuous-time envelope, no hidden infinite product, no selected limit, and no ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier. The only rows available to the consumer are the finite step segment, error ledger, dyadic coefficient ledgers, primitive-recursive majorant, tail modulus, readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Induct over the finite segment $I$. The base step reads the initial error row and the initial majorant entry. The successor step applies the displayed dyadic multiplicative row and additive row to the previous majorant entry, producing the next entry of $T$ and bounding the corresponding entry of $U$.

Now route the bound to the downstream ledgers. Picard consumers read the same finite estimate as an iteration budget through $\PicardIterationUp$, $\PicardErrorEstimateUp$, and $\PicardThresholdCauchyHandoffUp$, while Cauchy-rate consumers read the tail through $\SummableTailModulusUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$.

The continuous $\GronwallInequalityUp$ chapter remains a sibling reference rather than a source of this proof. Because the finite packet has no coordinate for a continuous envelope, hidden infinite product, selected limit, or ambient completeness theorem, every exported bound stays inside the displayed induction route.
\end{proof}
