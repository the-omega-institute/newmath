\begin{definition}[Dini uniform Cauchy carrier]
\label{def:dini-uniform-cauchy-carrier}
A $\DiniUniformCauchyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
D=(S,M,L,T,Q,U,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ source row, listing the finite observation schedule for the regular real-name family.
\item $M$ is the monotone comparison row, recording that later sampled names lie below earlier sampled names on the inspected dyadic windows.
\item $L$ is the $\LowerSemicontinuousUp$ visibility row, keeping the lower epigraph reads displayed before any tail budget is extracted.
\item $T$ is the finite tail row, naming the inspected index threshold and the two tail positions compared against it.
\item $Q$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, storing the rational error bound used by the tail comparison and the later readback.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ row extracted from $S,M,L,T,Q$, exposing the same tail budget uniformly over the scheduled windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the displayed uniform tail and dyadic tolerance.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, consumed only after the regular-rational readback is visible.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source, monotone comparison, lower-epigraph visibility, tail budget, dyadic tolerance, uniform modulus, regular readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same monotone uniform Cauchy extraction route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DiniUniformCauchyUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\LowerSemicontinuousUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DiniUniformCauchyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S,M,L,T,Q,U,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for classical compactness, countable choice, quotient equality of real names, selected limiting values, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dini uniform Cauchy NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dini-uniform-cauchy-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DiniUniformCauchyUp$ carrier
$$
D=(S,M,L,T,Q,U,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DiniUniformCauchyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed stream source, monotone comparison, lower-epigraph visibility, finite tail row, dyadic tolerance, extracted uniform modulus, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The stream source $S$, monotone comparison $M$, lower-epigraph row $L$, tail row $T$, dyadic tolerance $Q$, extracted modulus $U$, regular readback $R$, and real seal $E$ are the only analytic coordinates available to a consumer.

The order of use is forced by the tuple. The tail row $T$ is read only after the observed stream and monotone comparisons are visible, while $Q$ supplies the dyadic tolerance against which that tail is judged.

The uniform Cauchy data enter through $U$. Since $U$ is extracted from $S,M,L,T,Q$, the readback row $R$ and the terminal seal $E$ cannot bypass the displayed monotone tail and tolerance budget.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. The excluded objects have no carrier coordinate, so the local NameCert surface cannot read compactness, choice, quotient equality, selected limits, or ambient real completeness from this packet.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dini monotone tail extraction]
\label{thm:dini-uniform-cauchy-monotone-tail-extraction}
Every $\DiniUniformCauchyUp$ read that exposes a uniform Cauchy tail factors through the finite route
$$
S,M,L,T,Q,U .
$$
In particular, the monotone comparison row and lower-epigraph visibility row are displayed before the tail threshold is used, and the dyadic tolerance row is displayed before the extracted $\UniformModulusUp$ row is consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:dini-uniform-cauchy-namecert-obligations}. It places the stream source, monotone comparison, lower-epigraph visibility, tail threshold, dyadic tolerance, and uniform modulus inside one accepted packet.

The monotone tail is not an external limit object. It is the row $T$ read against the order comparisons in $M$ and the lower-epigraph visibility in $L$.

The uniformity is also finite. The row $U$ is downstream of the same $S,M,L,T,Q$ data, so the consumer obtains a uniform Cauchy tail only by displaying those rows first.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:dini-uniform-cauchy-regular-real-handoff}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Next consume the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row $R$. This row is attached to the same tail threshold, dyadic tolerance, and uniform modulus, so it cannot be detached from the displayed Dini extraction route.

Finally read the terminal real-name seal $E$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. They transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the forbidden host objects are absent from the carrier, the $\RealUp$ consumer receives only the regular real-name Cauchy handoff.
