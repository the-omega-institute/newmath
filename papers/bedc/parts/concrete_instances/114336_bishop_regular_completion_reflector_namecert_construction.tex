It sits after the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the RegSeqRat reflector route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-completion-reflector-namecert}. \begin{definition}[BishopRegularCompletionReflector carrier]
\label{def:bishop-regular-completion-reflector-carrier}
A $\BishopRegularCompletionReflectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(D,S,R,E,F,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger used for every regular precision request and every comparison of inspected rational windows.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row exposing the currently inspected approximants of the Bishop regular name.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, certifying that the displayed windows satisfy the regular rational discipline required before completion is requested.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached from $D,S,R$ through the displayed completion surface rather than through a quotient of streams.
\item $F$ is the reflector row, recording that any completion-facing consumer must read the dyadic ledger, stream windows, regular readback, and real seal in that order.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for dyadic tolerances, stream windows, regular readbacks, real seals, reflector rows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a regular-name precision request through
$$
D,S \longmapsto R \longmapsto E \longmapsto F \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BishopRegularCompletionReflectorUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCompletionReflectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,S,R,E,F,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion equality, selected limit representatives, host equality of real values, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, or standard bridge checking.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[BishopRegularCompletionReflector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:bishop-regular-completion-reflector-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BishopRegularCompletionReflectorUp$ carrier
$$
B=(D,S,R,E,F,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BishopRegularCompletionReflectorUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for dyadic tolerance ledgers, finite stream windows, regular rational readback, real-name sealing, reflector routing, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; source exposure requiring every completion-facing read to inspect $D,S$ before $R$; reflector discipline requiring $E$ and $F$ to be read only through the same $D,S,R$ route; and nonescape excluding quotient completion, selected limits, host equality, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, and standard bridge checking.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. Its displayed coordinates are exactly $D,S,R,E,F,H,C,P,N$, so carrier admission, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are read from those rows.

The source side begins with $D$ and $S$. The dyadic ledger fixes the tolerances used by each regular request, while the stream-window row specifies the finite approximants available for the current read.

The regular readback row $R$ is downstream of those source rows. A completion-facing consumer therefore obtains a regular rational name only after the dyadic and stream data have been displayed.

The seal and reflector rows are $E$ and $F$. Both are indexed by the same $D,S,R$ route, so the carrier cannot replace them by a quotient completion, a selected limit, or a host-level equality witness.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no other coordinate is present, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface and the refused principles have no export path.
\end{proof}
