Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\LocatedInfimumModulusUp$ packet records the finite data by which a located lower-bound search exposes an infimum modulus before any ambient completeness principle for $\RealUp$ is available. It sits beside the located-infimum carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedinfimum-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet extracts lower-window brackets and dyadic tolerances; it does not assert trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected infima, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Located infimum modulus carrier]
\label{def:located-infimum-modulus-carrier}
A $\LocatedInfimumModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(I,L,U,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the consumed $\NameCert_{\LocatedInfimumUp}$ row, recording the lower-bound ledger and greatest-lower-approximation ledger that the modulus request may inspect.
\item $L$ is the $\LowerRealUp$ row, storing the displayed lower surface and its admitted lower-window observations.
\item $U$ is the $\UpperRealUp$ row, storing the displayed upper guard used to bracket the lower search window.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, listing dyadic precision requests, lower-window refinements, and width certificates.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, translating dyadic lower-window refinements into regular rational observations before the real seal is consumed.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, reached only after the located-infimum, lower-real, upper-real, tolerance, and readback rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the located-infimum row, lower and upper real rows, dyadic tolerance row, regular-rational readback, real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an infimum-modulus request through $I,L,U,D,R,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedInfimumModulusUp$, $\LocatedInfimumUp$, $\LowerRealUp$, $\UpperRealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedInfimumModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only the displayed modulus route $I,L,U,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for trichotomy, quotient equality of real names, selected infima, countable choice, a host infimum operator, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located infimum modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-infimum-modulus-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedInfimumModulusUp$ carrier
$$
M=(I,L,U,D,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedInfimumModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of located-infimum admission through $I$, lower-real window exposure through $L$, upper-real guard exposure through $U$, dyadic tolerance exactness through $D$, regular rational readback through $R$, terminal $\RealUp$ seal nonescape through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-infimum-modulus-carrier}. The analytic rows are exactly the located-infimum row, the lower and upper real rows, the dyadic tolerance row, the regular rational readback row, and the terminal real seal row.

The lower-window read is forced through $I,L,U$. The located-infimum row supplies the lower-bound and greatest-lower-approximation ledgers, while $L$ and $U$ display the bracketing surfaces used by the modulus request.

The dyadic tolerance row supplies the modulus data. It refines lower-window widths through $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ entries and exposes those refinements as $\RegSeqRatUp$ readbacks before the $\RealUp$ seal may be consumed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since no carrier row stores trichotomy, quotient equality, selected infima, countable choice, a host infimum operator, or ambient real completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located infimum modulus real seal]
\label{thm:located-infimum-modulus-real-seal}
Let $M=(I,L,U,D,R,E,H,C,P,N)$ be an accepted $\LocatedInfimumModulusUp$ carrier. Every $\RealUp$-facing read exported by $M$ factors through the displayed route
$$
I,L,U \longmapsto D \longmapsto R \longmapsto E \longmapsto C,P,N .
$$
Thus a consumer can read only the sealed regular-rational lower-window route, not a quotient real equality test, a selected infimum, a countable-choice witness, or an ambient completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a $\RealUp$-facing request. The carrier contains only one real seal row, namely $E$, and the definition makes $E$ reachable only after the located-infimum, lower-real, upper-real, dyadic tolerance, and regular-rational rows have been displayed.

The request first reads the lower-window source $I,L,U$. It then passes through the tolerance row $D$, which supplies the dyadic precision control, and through $R$, which repacks the same finite observations as regular rational readback.

The structural rows expose no additional real object. The continuation row $C$, provenance row $P$, and local naming row $N$ can only replay, source, and name the sealed route, so the exported read remains inside $I,L,U,D,R,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{proof}
