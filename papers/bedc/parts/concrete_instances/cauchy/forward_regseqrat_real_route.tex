\begin{theorem}[Cauchy forward RegSeqRat and Real route]
\label{thm:cauchy-forward-regseqrat-real-route}
Every completion-facing read of an accepted $\CauchyUp$ packet must pass through the displayed $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert} and then the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert} before any completion consumer may use it. The route carries only the finite stream-window, dyadic tolerance, regular-rational readback, real-seal, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and local $\NameCert$ rows exposed by the Cauchy sibling files; it exports no quotient stream equality, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-carrier}. Its public readback is organized by finite stream windows, dyadic tolerances, regular-rational readback, terminal real-name sealing, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Apply \autoref{thm:cauchy-displayed-regseqrat-route} and \autoref{thm:cauchy-core-streamname-regseqrat-window}. These routes place every Real-facing or completion-facing Cauchy read behind displayed finite windows and the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row before any terminal seal is consumed.

Finally apply \autoref{thm:cauchy-core-regseqrat-real-seal} together with \autoref{thm:cauchy-regseqrat-real-completion-lattice}. The Real seal is reached only after the regular-rational row has been displayed, and the completion-facing consumer receives the same finite route. Since the cited sibling routes provide no coordinate for quotient equality, selected limits, choice, host equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness, none can enter the forward route.
\end{proof}
