\begin{theorem}[Cauchy filter-base net-convergence route]
\label{thm:cauchy-filterbase-netconvergence-route}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\CauchyUp$ packet that reads Cauchy finite-window evidence through filter or net interfaces must first pass through the displayed routes of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-filterbase-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-filter-base-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-netconvergence-namecert}. The route exposes the same finite windows, tolerance ledger, tail comparisons, componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows before any filter-base or net-convergence completion consumer inspects the packet. It exports no selected limit, quotient completion equality, countable-choice schedule, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or detached net convergence theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted $\CauchyUp$ packet to its finite-window and tolerance rows. These are the only rows available to a downstream filter or net consumer.

The filter-base read is routed through the finite basis surface named by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-filterbase-namecert} and the Cauchy-specific basis of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-filter-base-namecert}. The net read then passes through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-netconvergence-namecert}, which consumes the same displayed rows rather than a separate completion theorem.

Since the route never adds a coordinate for selected limits, quotient completion equality, countable choice, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or detached convergence, those objects remain outside the exported Cauchy packet.
\end{proof}
