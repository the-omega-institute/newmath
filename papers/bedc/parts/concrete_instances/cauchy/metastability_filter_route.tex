\label{thm:cauchy-orphan-sibling-route}

The CauchyFilter packet exposes the finite filterbase carrier, directed refinement row, Cauchy modulus row, stream schedules, regular observations, dyadic neighbourhood witnesses, transport, replay, provenance, and local name.

Combining the two sibling packets gives the stated route. No completion-facing $\CauchyUp$ read can reach the StreamName, RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, or RealUp rows by bypassing both finite-tail stability and finite-filterbase data.
\end{proof}
