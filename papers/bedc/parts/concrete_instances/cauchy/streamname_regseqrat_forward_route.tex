\label{thm:cauchy-streamname-regseqrat-forward-route}

Finally apply the regular-fusion route of \autoref{thm:cauchy-orphan-regular-cauchy-fusion-forward-route}. Its fused-tail handoff preserves the same $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows, so the Cauchy hub reaches any later $\RegSeqRatUp$ consumer only through the finite StreamName and regular rational surface.
\end{proof}
