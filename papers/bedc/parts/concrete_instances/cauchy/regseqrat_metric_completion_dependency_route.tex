\begin{theorem}[Cauchy regular-source completion dependency route]
\label{thm:cauchy-regseqrat-metric-completion-dependency-route}
Every $\CauchyUp$ read consumed by an L10 completion-facing packet is public only through the displayed dependency route
$$
\RegSeqRatUp \longmapsto \MetricCompletionUp \longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The route first exposes the regular-rational tail budget of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, then the metric-distance comparison used by the $\MetricCompletionUp$ boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metriccompletion-namecert}, and only then the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. It exports no quotient stream, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness row.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the $\CauchyUp$ hub through its child carriers. The hub contributes only displayed finite rows, so a completion-facing consumer must first inspect the regular-rational tail budget that names the tail data and tolerance requests.

Next compare distances at the metric-completion boundary. The $\MetricCompletionUp$ consumer receives the same displayed regular-source route and metric-distance comparison; it does not receive a quotient representative or a selected completed point.

Finally consume the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. Since the seal is reached only after the regular tail budget and the metric-completion comparison have been displayed, the public route is a dependency edge through sibling chapters rather than an ambient completeness principle.
\end{proof}
