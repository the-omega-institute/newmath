\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\HardCertificateLayerUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-hard-certificate-layer-namecert}
\origin{ai}

The $\HardCertificateLayerUp$ packet realizes the verification layer of \autoref{sec:visions-philosophy-hard-certificate-layer} as finite NameCert data. It records backend-relative proof-object acceptance, trust-boundary exposure, classifier preservation, verification-ledger routing, and local naming without turning a checked theorem into a new classifier. The packet sits beside \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-auditgateboundary-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kernel-audit-witness-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-negativecertificateboundary-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-hostprimitiveleakage-namecert} as a certificate-hardening surface rather than as an audit gate, kernel witness, negative boundary, or host-primitive leak detector.

\begin{definition}[HardCertificateLayer carrier]
\label{def:hard-certificate-layer-carrier}
A $\HardCertificateLayerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(B,\Phi,\Pi,T,C,L,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the verification backend row, naming the formal language, checker, proof-object format, accepted dependencies, and trust boundary.
\item $\Phi$ is the formal statement row translated from an already displayed certificate row.
\item $\Pi$ is the proof-object row accepted by $B$ for $\Phi$.
\item $T$ is the trust-boundary row, recording checked components, trusted components, unchecked components, and portability limits.
\item $C$ is the classifier-preservation row, requiring the source classifier to remain unchanged by the hardening read.
\item $L$ is the verification-ledger row that records assumptions, dependencies, proof artifact, backend, uncovered certificate fields, and not-claimed endpoints.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for backend, statement, proof object, trust boundary, classifier, ledger, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the certificate row being hardened through backend acceptance and back to the unchanged classifier.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\HardCertificateLayerUp$, the source NameCert row, the verification backend, proof object, trust boundary, classifier-preservation row, ledger row, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\HardCertificateLayerUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The packet has no coordinate for classifier creation, backend-independent truth, transfer across proof assistants, replacement of a source NameCert, or discovery credit from verification alone.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[HardCertificateLayer NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:hard-certificate-layer-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:hard-certificate-layer-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\HardCertificateLayerUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for
$$
Q=(B,\Phi,\Pi,T,C,L,H,R,P,N),
$$
backend-relative proof acceptance through $B,\Phi,\Pi$, trust-boundary exposure through $T$, classifier preservation through $C$, verification-ledger routing through $L$, componentwise $\hsame$ stability through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $R$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:hard-certificate-layer-carrier}. The backend, statement, and proof-object rows are the only hard-certificate evidence rows, so every verification read passes through $B,\Phi,\Pi$.

The trust-boundary row $T$ and ledger row $L$ record what the proof object does and does not cover. The classifier-preservation row $C$ binds the hardening read to the same classifier surface that existed before the backend accepted the proof object.

Finally $H,R,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier omits classifier creation, backend-independent truth, proof-assistant transfer, source-NameCert replacement, and discovery credit, none of those endpoints is a local NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[HardCertificateLayer classifier preservation]
\label{thm:hard-certificate-layer-classifier-preservation}
Every accepted $\HardCertificateLayerUp$ transition preserves the classifier row it hardens. If a verification-only transition changes the classifier row, then it is not a $\HardCertificateLayerUp$ transition.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the carrier of \autoref{def:hard-certificate-layer-carrier}. The classifier row $C$ is preservation data, not an output slot for a new classifier.

Apply \autoref{thm:hard-certificate-layer-namecert-obligations}. Backend acceptance through $B,\Phi,\Pi$ and trust-boundary exposure through $T,L$ harden a displayed certificate row while $C$ keeps the classifier surface fixed.

Thus any transition that changes the classifier leaves the displayed carrier and cannot be cited as a $\HardCertificateLayerUp$ read.
\end{proof}
