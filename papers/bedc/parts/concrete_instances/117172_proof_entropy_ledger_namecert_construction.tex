\begin{definition}[Proof entropy ledger carrier]
\label{def:proof-entropy-ledger-carrier}
A $\ProofEntropyLedgerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(T,M,R,W,S,A,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the theorem-flow source row, exposing the canonical theorem candidate and its site before any scalar read is public.
\item $M$ is the theorem-DNA locus-mass row, recording the eight displayed masses for statement, dependencies, proof, certificates, ledger, status, canonical site, and closing seal.
\item $R$ is the recognizer-mass row attached to the generated theorem recognizer.
\item $W$ is the public weight-profile row, a nine-coordinate profile for the eight theorem-DNA loci together with recognizer mass.
\item $S$ is the score-profile row, recording the componentwise products and the displayed scalar total.
\item $A$ is the score-bound audit row, exposing the accepted bound, comparison result, and refusal of any scalar read that omits $M,R,W,S$.
\item $E$ is the closing seal row, tying the score-bound ledger to the theorem-DNA closing seal and audit status.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $T,M,R,W,S,A,E$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from theorem-flow exposure through locus masses, recognizer mass, public weights, score profile, score-bound audit, and closing seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ProofEntropyLedgerUp$, $\TheoremDnaScoreUp$, $\TheoremGenomeEvolutionUp$, $\ProofTraceNormalFormUp$, $\AuditGateBoundaryUp$, $\CertificateAuditGateUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ProofEntropyLedgerUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $T,M,R,W,S,A,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a private weight profile, hidden recognizer mass, score-only theorem quality, unledgered audit acceptance, or an external theorem-value oracle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Proof entropy ledger NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:proof-entropy-ledger-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ProofEntropyLedgerUp$ carrier
$$
L=(T,M,R,W,S,A,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ProofEntropyLedgerUp}$ surface consists exactly of theorem-flow exposure through $T$, theorem-DNA locus masses through $M$, recognizer mass through $R$, public weight profile through $W$, score profile through $S$, score-bound audit through $A$, closing seal through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:proof-entropy-ledger-carrier}. The theorem-flow source $T$, locus-mass row $M$, recognizer-mass row $R$, public weight profile $W$, score profile $S$, score-bound audit $A$, and closing seal $E$ are the only scoring rows.

The support rows then preserve the same route. Componentwise transport is $H$, displayed replay is $C$, provenance is $P$, and local naming is $N$.

Since the carrier has no private-weight, score-only, hidden-recognizer, or external theorem-value coordinate, every certified scalar read must expose the listed rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Proof entropy ledger weight-profile nonescape]
\label{thm:proof-entropy-ledger-weight-profile-nonescape}
Every scalar score read from an accepted $\ProofEntropyLedgerUp$ packet must factor through the ordered ledger route
$$
T,M,R,W \longmapsto S \longmapsto A \longmapsto E .
$$
In particular, the score profile $S$ is public only together with the theorem-DNA locus masses $M$, recognizer mass $R$, and public weight profile $W$, and the score-bound audit $A$ cannot be replayed as a scalar theorem-quality judgment after erasing the weight profile.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the theorem-flow source row $T$. It fixes the theorem candidate whose locus masses are being read, so the score cannot float free of a canonical theorem site.

Next expose $M,R,W$. These rows give the eight theorem-DNA masses, the recognizer mass, and the public nine-coordinate weight profile before the scalar score profile is formed.

The row $S$ is therefore downstream from the mass and weight rows. The audit row $A$ can compare the displayed total with the displayed bound only after that downstream score profile is present, and the seal row $E$ closes the same ledger.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve this order through $H,C,P,N$. A consumer that erases $W$ no longer has the row required to reconstruct or audit the scalar score, so no weight-detached score read escapes the packet.
\end{proof}
