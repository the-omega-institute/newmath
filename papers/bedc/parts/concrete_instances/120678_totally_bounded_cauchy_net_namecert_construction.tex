\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\TotallyBoundedCauchyNetUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-totally-bounded-cauchy-net-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop--Bridges constructive total boundedness and Kelley's filter and net language, the $\TotallyBoundedCauchyNetUp$ packet records the finite route by which a totally bounded source and a Cauchy filter or net source produce a completion-facing regular readback. It sits after the finite-net surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-totallybounded-namecert}, the Cauchy-filter surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfilter-namecert}, the complete-metric seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, and the basis-first completion handoff of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-filter-basis-completion-namecert}. The packet names a finite compactness-completion bridge only; it does not select a limit, identify quotient filters, use countable choice, or import ambient completeness outside the displayed rows.

\begin{definition}[Totally bounded Cauchy net carrier]
\label{def:totally-bounded-cauchy-net-carrier}
A $\TotallyBoundedCauchyNetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(B,C,F,D,W,R,S,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $B$ is the $\TotallyBoundedUp$ source row, exposing the finite-net coverage surface used by the inspected metric source.
\item $C$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ or Cauchy-net source row, naming the directed observation route whose tails must be read through finite covers.
\item $F$ is the finite net basis row, recording the selected cover indices, basis observations, and bundled centers displayed at each inspected tolerance.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger row, storing the positive tolerance histories and refinement comparisons used by the finite basis.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window family through which Cauchy observations and finite-net choices are inspected.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row extracted from the same windows and dyadic tolerance data.
\item $S$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp/\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $B,C,F,D,W,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the totally bounded source, Cauchy source, finite basis, tolerance ledger, windows, readback, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a Cauchy observation request through finite-net coverage, dyadic tolerance, stream-window inspection, regular rational readback, and the terminal seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\TotallyBoundedCauchyNetUp$, $\TotallyBoundedUp$, $\CauchyFilterUp$, $\CauchyFilterBasisCompletionUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\TotallyBoundedCauchyNetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports only the displayed finite completion route. It has no coordinate for quotient completion, a choice-selected subnet, host metric compactness, selected limit representatives, hidden tolerance comparison, or ambient completeness outside $S$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Totally bounded Cauchy net NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:totally-bounded-cauchy-net-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\TotallyBoundedCauchyNetUp$ carrier
$$
T=(B,C,F,D,W,R,S,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\TotallyBoundedCauchyNetUp}$ surface consists exactly of totally bounded source exposure, Cauchy filter or net source exposure, finite net basis exposure, dyadic tolerance-ledger exposure, StreamName window exposure, RegSeqRat readback, CompleteMetric or Real seal handoff, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:totally-bounded-cauchy-net-carrier}. The source rows are $B$ and $C$, the finite compactness rows are $F,D$, the observation rows are $W,R$, and the seal and structural rows are $S,H,K,P,N$.

Read the totally bounded row before the Cauchy observation is consumed. The finite basis $F$ is licensed by the finite-net coverage surface, while the Cauchy row $C$ fixes which directed tails or filter observations must be covered.

Next inspect $D,W,R$. The dyadic ledger records the tolerance budget, the stream windows display the finite observations, and the regular rational readback records the completion-facing value for the same inspected windows.

Finally consume $S,H,K,P,N$. The seal is downstream of the totally bounded source, Cauchy source, basis, tolerance, window, and readback rows, and the structural rows preserve exactly that route. Since no carrier coordinate stores quotient completion, a selected subnet, host compactness, selected limits, hidden tolerance comparison, or detached completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Totally bounded Cauchy net filter handoff]
\label{thm:totally-bounded-cauchy-net-filter-handoff}
Every $\CauchyFilterBasisCompletionUp$ or $\CompleteMetricUp$ consumer of an accepted $\TotallyBoundedCauchyNetUp$ packet must first read
$$
B,C,F,D,W,R,S,H,K,P,N.
$$
Thus a completion-facing Cauchy read from a totally bounded source passes through displayed finite-net coverage, Cauchy filter or net source data, dyadic tolerance control, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, the CompleteMetric or Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming before any downstream completion result is consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:totally-bounded-cauchy-net-namecert-obligations}. It requires the totally bounded source row $B$ and the Cauchy source row $C$ before the finite basis, tolerance, window, and readback rows can be read.

For a $\CauchyFilterBasisCompletionUp$ consumer, the row $C$ supplies the filter or net source and $F,D,W,R$ supply the basis, tolerance, window, and readback data needed for the basis-first route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchy-filter-basis-completion-namecert}.

For a $\CompleteMetricUp$ consumer, the seal row $S$ records only the displayed completion-facing handoff. The provenance row may cite the complete-metric vocabulary, but it does not add a selected limit, quotient filter, or choice-selected subnet.

The exclusions follow from \autoref{def:totally-bounded-cauchy-net-carrier}. Any downstream completion read must traverse the finite rows of $T$, so no hidden compactness or quotient-completion principle can be recovered from the packet.
\end{proof}
