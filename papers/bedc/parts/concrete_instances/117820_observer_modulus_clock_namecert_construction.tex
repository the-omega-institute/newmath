Finally, $H,C,P,N$ preserve and name exactly these rows. Since no global clock, synchronized Hist stream, hidden convergence oracle, host equality, or ambient completeness coordinate appears in the carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the certificate surface.
\end{proof}
