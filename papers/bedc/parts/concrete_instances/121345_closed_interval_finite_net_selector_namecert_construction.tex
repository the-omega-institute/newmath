\begin{definition}[ClosedIntervalFiniteNetSelector carrier]
\label{def:closed-interval-finite-net-selector-carrier}
A $\ClosedIntervalFiniteNetSelectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
Q=(L,U,M,F,V,W,R,S,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $L$ and $U$ are the lower and upper endpoint rows read from a $\RealIntervalUp$ packet, including the displayed endpoint-order and enclosure data.
\item $M$ is the dyadic mesh-depth row. It records the positive precision request, mesh denominator, and outward rounding budget used by the selector.
\item $F$ is the located finite-net row from $\LocatedClosedIntervalFiniteNetUp$; it displays the finite list of mesh cells, endpoint coverage checks, and locatedness witnesses visible at depth $M$.
\item $V$ is the finite-net witness row routed through $\HeineBorelFiniteNetUp$ and $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$; it records the exact finite candidate rows retained by the selector, not an arbitrary subcover.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row for the endpoint streams and the selected mesh cells.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row turning those windows and the mesh budget into regular rational observations.
\item $S$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row for every Real-facing endpoint or selected-net output.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoints, mesh depth, located finite-net rows, witness rows, stream windows, readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming.

The carrier predicate asserts exactly that these rows form one finite selector history for a displayed closed real interval. It has no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected arbitrary subcovers, quotient real equality, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ClosedIntervalFiniteNetSelector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:closed-interval-finite-net-selector-obligations}

No finite selector obligation is stored outside those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:closed-interval-finite-net-selector-carrier}. The endpoint rows $L,U$, mesh row $M$, located finite-net row $F$, and witness row $V$ are the only selection data in the packet.

Read the sibling finite-net route next. The located finite-net row $F$ supplies finite mesh-cell and endpoint-coverage data, while $V$ records the retained witness rows through $\HeineBorelFiniteNetUp$ and $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$. These rows name finite candidates; they do not choose an arbitrary subcover.

Finally route every Real-facing output through $W,R,S$ and every structural read through $H,C,P,N$. Since the excluded compactness, quotient equality, trichotomy, countable-choice, and completeness principles are not coordinates of the carrier, the local NameCert obligations are exhausted by the displayed rows.
\end{proof}
