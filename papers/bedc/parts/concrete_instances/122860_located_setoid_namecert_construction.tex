Following Bishop and Bridges on constructive equality, setoids, and located apartness, the $\LocatedSetoidUp$ packet records the finite BEDC naming surface for equality that is witnessed by displayed equivalence data and separated by displayed apartness data. It sits after the membership classifier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-setlike-namecert}, the displayed-family carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-typelike-namecert}, the located-subset route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedset-namecert}, the metric route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a constructive equality object for Real and RegSeqRat consumers; it does not quotient representatives, identify values by host equality, use trichotomy, or appeal to ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[LocatedSetoid carrier]
\label{def:located-setoid-carrier}
A $\LocatedSetoidUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(S,T,M,Q,A,D,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the $\SetLikeUp$ membership-classifier row for the underlying displayed collection;
\item $T$ is the $\TypeLikeUp$ displayed-family row carrying endpoints, fibers, and transport requests;
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ comparison row through which distance-shaped equality tests are read;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance-readback row attached to the displayed comparison schedule;
\item $A$ is the located apartness row, recording the finite positive-separation witnesses and the refusal branch for equality reads;
\item $D$ is the equality-decision ledger for the inspected finite window, storing reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity, and apartness incompatibility rows;
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $S,T,M,Q,A,D$ have been displayed;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the membership, family, metric, rational, apartness, equality-ledger, and seal rows;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from membership inspection through located apartness, rational readback, equality-ledger exposure, and Real sealing;
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedSetoidUp$, $\SetLikeUp$, $\TypeLikeUp$, $\LocatedSetUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedSetoidUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for $\mathsf{Quot}$, host equality, trichotomy, selected representatives, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedSetoid NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-setoid-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedSetoidUp$ carrier
$$
L=(S,T,M,Q,A,D,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedSetoidUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed $\SetLikeUp$ membership classifier, $\TypeLikeUp$ family endpoint route, $\LocatedSetUp$ locatedness context, $\MetricUp$ comparison row, $\RegSeqRatUp$ tolerance readback, located apartness row, finite equality ledger, terminal $\RealUp$ seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Equality may be consumed only as this displayed finite route, and apartness may be consumed only through the positive-separation row $A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-setoid-carrier}. The rows $S,T,M,Q,A,D,R$ are the complete equality and apartness sources, while $H,C,P,N$ are the structural rows that preserve, replay, source, and name the same packet.

The equality route is not independent of located apartness. Membership and displayed-family endpoints are read first through $S,T$, metric comparison enters through $M$, and rational tolerance readback enters through $Q$. The row $A$ records the positive-separation alternative, and the ledger $D$ records exactly the inspected equivalence and incompatibility rows.

Finally the Real-facing seal $R$ is reached only after those finite rows have been displayed. Since no row stores quotient representatives, host equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness, the local NameCert obligations are exhausted by the carrier rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[LocatedSetoid Real classifier handoff]
\label{thm:located-setoid-real-classifier-handoff}
Every $\RealUp$ or $\RegSeqRatUp$ consumer that reads equality or apartness from an accepted $\LocatedSetoidUp$ packet must factor through the displayed sequence
$$
\SetLikeUp,\ \TypeLikeUp,\ \LocatedSetUp,\ \MetricUp,\ \RegSeqRatUp,\ \RealUp .
$$
Concretely, the consumer first exposes the membership and displayed-family rows $S,T$, then the metric comparison row $M$, then the rational tolerance row $Q$, then the located apartness and finite equality ledger rows $A,D$, and only then reaches the Real seal $R$. The handoff supplies no $\mathsf{Quot}$ primitive, no host equality of representatives, no trichotomy split, and no ambient Real completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the carrier rows of \autoref{def:located-setoid-carrier}. A Real-facing consumer has only one seal row, namely $R$, and that row is packaged after the membership, family, metric, rational, apartness, and equality-ledger rows.

Apply the obligation surface of \autoref{thm:located-setoid-namecert-obligations}. It forces equality and apartness reads to remain inside $S,T,M,Q,A,D,H,C,P,N$ before $R$ is exposed.

Thus the classifier handoff is a finite route through the named sibling chapters. The excluded equality sources have no carrier coordinate, no replay edge, no provenance row, and no local NameCert row.
\end{proof}
