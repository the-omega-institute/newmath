\begin{proposition}[Compact real interval real-source route]
\label{prop:compact-real-interval-real-source-route}
Every $\CompactRealIntervalUp$ consumer that reads compact interval rows first passes through the endpoint $\RealUp$ source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, the metric reading surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-metric-namecert}, and the finite-net route recorded by \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-real-interval-namecert}. The compact interval packet therefore exposes real endpoints, metric comparison data, and finite-net compatibility before any compact interval row is exported.
\end{proposition}
