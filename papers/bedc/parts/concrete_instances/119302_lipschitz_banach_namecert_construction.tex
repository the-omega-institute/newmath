\begin{theorem}[LipschitzBanach NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:lipschitz-banach-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LipschitzBanachUp$ carrier $B=(L,F,K,U,V,M,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LipschitzBanachUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the LipschitzSpace row, bounded Real-valued map family row, shared Lipschitz constant row, uniformly Cauchy ledger, UniformLimit row, CompleteMetric row, StreamName windows, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming. The obligations require the same shared constant $K$ to control every listed map, the uniform Cauchy ledger $U$ to be displayed before the uniform-limit row $V$, the StreamName and RegSeqRat rows $S,R$ to account for the finite value and norm windows, and the Real seal $E$ to remain downstream of those rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. Its analytic rows are $L,F,K,U,V,M,S,R,E$, and its structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The Lipschitz route starts with $L,F,K$. The LipschitzSpace row supplies the displayed graph and inequality surface, the bounded family row lists the inspected maps and value bounds, and the shared Lipschitz row is read once for every listed member.

The completion-facing route then uses $U,V,M$. The uniformly Cauchy ledger records finite pairwise comparison budgets, the UniformLimit row is exposed only after those budgets are visible, and the CompleteMetric row names the displayed metric boundary without importing a host Banach-space theorem.

Finally $S,R,E,H,C,P,N$ keep the read inside finite BEDC data. Since no carrier coordinate stores quotient equality of functions, a selected limit, countable choice, ambient Real completeness, or a hidden complete function space, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
