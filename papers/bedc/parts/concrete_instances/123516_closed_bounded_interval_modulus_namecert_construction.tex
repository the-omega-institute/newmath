It sits after the interval carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedinterval-namecert}, the finite interval-net row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedintervalnet-namecert}, the finite StreamName windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the RegSeqRat readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal Real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Closed bounded interval modulus carrier]
\label{def:closed-bounded-interval-modulus-carrier}
A $\ClosedBoundedIntervalModulusUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(I,N,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,L)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the $\ClosedBoundedIntervalUp$ source row, exposing endpoints, endpoint order, containment, readback, and Real-seal boundary data.
\item $N$ is the $\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp$ interval-net row, naming the finite mesh cells and endpoint-visible grid used by the modulus request.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, recording the finite source and mesh windows inspected by the request.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger, recording the dyadic radii, mesh widths, and comparison budgets.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal boundary reached only after $I,N,S,R,D$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval source, net, windows, readback, dyadic ledger, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from every interval-modulus request through $I,N,S,R,D,E,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClosedBoundedIntervalModulusUp$, $\ClosedBoundedIntervalUp$, $\ClosedBoundedIntervalNetUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $L$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedBoundedIntervalModulusUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier compares accepted packets only through $I,N,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,L$. It contains no coordinate for open-cover compactness, selected subcovers, selected limits, quotient real equality, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Closed bounded interval modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:closed-bounded-interval-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClosedBoundedIntervalModulusUp$ carrier
$$
M=(I,N,S,R,D,E,H,C,P,L),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedBoundedIntervalModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, interval endpoint visibility, net-window exactness, dyadic modulus schedule, RegSeqRat handoff, Real-seal nonescape, provenance, and local naming.

Carrier admission displays the interval source $I$, finite net $N$, window row $S$, readback row $R$, dyadic ledger $D$, Real seal $E$, and structural rows $H,C,P,L$. Interval endpoint visibility requires every modulus read to expose the endpoints, endpoint order, and containment rows of $I$ before $N$ is consumed. Net-window exactness binds the mesh cells of $N$ to the windows of $S$. The dyadic modulus schedule reads only the radii and mesh budgets stored in $D$. RegSeqRat handoff routes rational observations through $R$ before $E$ is reached. Real-seal nonescape, provenance, and local naming keep all accepted reads inside $E,H,C,P,L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:closed-bounded-interval-modulus-carrier}. The analytic rows available to a modulus consumer are exactly the interval source $I$, interval net $N$, StreamName windows $S$, RegSeqRat readback $R$, dyadic tolerance ledger $D$, and Real seal $E$.

Read the interval source first. The endpoint and containment data of $I$ are inherited from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-closedboundedinterval-namecert}, so a modulus request cannot inspect a mesh or tolerance budget before the closed interval boundary is visible.

Next read $N,S,R,D$ in order. The net row supplies finite mesh cells, the StreamName row names the inspected windows, the RegSeqRat row provides rational readback on those windows, and the dyadic ledger records the requested tolerance schedule.

Finally consume $E,H,C,P,L$. The Real seal is downstream of the displayed finite route, while transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve that route. Since the carrier has no compactness, selected-limit, quotient-equality, trichotomy, choice, or ambient-completeness coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
