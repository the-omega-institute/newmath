\begin{theorem}[Banach contraction Cauchy NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:banach-contraction-cauchy-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BanachContractionCauchyUp$ carrier
$$
B=(X,T,L,I,M,R,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\BanachContractionCauchyUp}$ surface consists exactly of complete-metric source exposure through $X$, contraction-map exposure through $T$, contraction-ledger exactness through $L$, Picard-window exposure through $I$, Cauchy-modulus extraction through $M$, regular rational readback through $R$, Real-seal delivery through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic rows are $X,T,L,I,M,R,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

Read the contraction side first. The complete-metric row $X$ supplies the carried metric service, the contraction row $T$ supplies the displayed map, and the ledger $L$ supplies only the visible rational shrink comparisons.

The Picard and Cauchy rows then determine the tail evidence. The iteration row $I$ lists the finite orbit windows, while the modulus row $M$ records the threshold schedule extracted from those windows and the ledger $L$.

Finally read $R,E,H,C,P,N$. Regular rational readback and the Real seal are downstream of the Cauchy modulus, and the structural rows transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no excluded fixed-point or quotient object is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local certificate surface.
\end{proof}
