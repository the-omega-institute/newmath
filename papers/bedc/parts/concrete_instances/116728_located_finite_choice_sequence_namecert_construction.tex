\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedFiniteChoiceSequenceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-located-finite-choice-sequence-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges, the $\LocatedFiniteChoiceSequenceUp$ packet records a finite choice-sequence observation only through the located window currently displayed to a construction. It refines the finite-prefix carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-choice-sequence-namecert} by requiring a located readback route through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedsequence-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-brouwer-finite-choice-sequence-modulus-namecert}. The packet supports finite-window Real and completion arguments while refusing countable-choice, selected-tail, quotient-equality, and ambient-completeness coordinates.

\begin{definition}[Located finite choice-sequence carrier]
\label{def:located-finite-choice-sequence-carrier}
A $\LocatedFiniteChoiceSequenceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(I,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the finite choice index row, naming the inspected choice-sequence positions and no tail positions outside the packet.
\item $W$ is the located window row, recording the finite located interval, ball, or tolerance window attached to the indices of $I$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row opened by the same finite choice-index and located-window data.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the displayed $\StreamNameUp$ window and located tolerance data.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback has been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the finite choice index, located window, $\StreamNameUp$ window, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, $\RealUp$ seal, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay for the finite route
$$
I,W \longmapsto S \longmapsto R \longmapsto E .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedFiniteChoiceSequenceUp$, $\ChoiceSequenceUp$, $\LocatedSequenceUp$, $\BrouwerFiniteChoiceSequenceModulusUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedFiniteChoiceSequenceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts only packets whose finite choice index, located window, stream window, regular rational readback, and real seal describe one displayed finite route. It exports no countable-choice functional, no selected infinite sequence, no quotient real equality, no host real equality, and no $\RealUp$ completeness coordinate.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located finite choice-sequence NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-finite-choice-sequence-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedFiniteChoiceSequenceUp$ carrier $L=(I,W,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedFiniteChoiceSequenceUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite choice-index admission through $I$, located-window admission through $W$, $\StreamNameUp$ window exposure through $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, $\RealUp$ sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

Every consumer from the finite-window Real route or a completion-facing route must read those rows in the displayed order
$$
\ChoiceSequenceUp,\ \LocatedSequenceUp,\ \BrouwerFiniteChoiceSequenceModulusUp
\longmapsto I,W
\longmapsto \StreamNameUp
\longmapsto \RegSeqRatUp
\longmapsto \RealUp .
$$
The packet exports no countable-choice functional, selected infinite sequence, quotient real equality, host real equality, or $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-finite-choice-sequence-carrier}. The rows $I$ and $W$ are finite displayed rows, so the choice-sequence input is visible only at the named inspected positions and only through the located window recorded in the packet.

The stream and analytic readback rows are downstream of that finite surface. The row $S$ opens the $\StreamNameUp$ window from $I,W$, the row $R$ repacks the same displayed window as $\RegSeqRatUp$ data, and the row $E$ seals only that readback as $\RealUp$ data.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. They do not add a new analytic coordinate; they keep the finite choice index, located window, stream window, regular rational readback, and real seal in one packet.

The sibling chapters supply the boundary discipline. \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedsequence-namecert} fixes the located-window route, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-brouwer-finite-choice-sequence-modulus-namecert} fixes the finite choice-sequence modulus route into $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, and $\RealUp$.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for a countable-choice functional, a selected infinite sequence, quotient real equality, host real equality, or ambient real completeness, no local $\NameCert_{\LocatedFiniteChoiceSequenceUp}$ consumer can export one.
\end{proof}
