\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ClassifierInformationBudgetUp$}

\origin{ai}

The $\ClassifierInformationBudgetUp$ packet realizes the classifier-level information distinction of \autoref{ch:visions-classifier-level-information} as finite BEDC data. It separates record growth from classifier shift by displaying before/after classifier-state rows, a $\SigRel$ handoff row, ledger-delta rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. The packet is designed for discovery-classifier and TasteGate consumers that must know whether a route changes the classifier rather than merely extending a trace.

The budget packet consumes the morphism-level route discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-classifiermorphism-namecert} and the boundary-exposure discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-classifierboundary-namecert} before interpreting a $\SigRel$ ledger delta as classifier-level information.

It also consumes the before/after disagreement route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-classifier-shift-ledger-residue-namecert} and feeds the positive-claim boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-pipeline-discovery-classifier-namecert} together with the novelty ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-classifier-novelty-ledger-namecert}.

\begin{definition}[Classifier information budget carrier]
\label{def:classifier-information-budget-carrier}
A $\ClassifierInformationBudgetUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(S_0,S_1,G,D,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S_0$ is the source classifier-state row before the audited transition.
\item $S_1$ is the target classifier-state row after the audited transition.
\item $G$ is the $\SigRel$ handoff row, recording which signature relation compares the two classifier states.
\item $D$ is the ledger-delta row, separating external record growth from the classifier-shift bit read along $G$.
\item $L$ is the local budget row, recording which classifier-level information budget is consumed by a downstream route.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the source state, target state, $\SigRel$ handoff, ledger delta, budget row, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the classifier-budget route through $S_0,S_1,G,D,L,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClassifierInformationBudgetUp$, $\SigRel$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClassifierInformationBudgetUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $S_0,S_1,G,D,L,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for equating record growth with discovery, for treating a notation change as classifier shift, or for reading a positive discovery without the displayed ledger-delta row.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Classifier information budget NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:classifier-information-budget-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClassifierInformationBudgetUp$ carrier $I=(S_0,S_1,G,D,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ClassifierInformationBudgetUp}$ surface consists exactly of classifier-state admission for $S_0,S_1$, $\SigRel$ handoff admission for $G$, ledger-delta exposure for $D$, local budget exposure for $L$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. A downstream consumer may read classifier-level information only after it displays these rows and keeps record growth distinct from classifier shift.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The two classifier-state rows and the $\SigRel$ handoff are the only before/after comparison data available to a consumer.

The ledger-delta row $D$ separates record growth from classifier shift before the local budget row $L$ can be consumed. Thus a longer record is not itself a classifier-level information witness, and a notation-only presentation change is not accepted as a shift.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier has no hidden discovery bit or host classifier oracle, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Classifier information budget SigRel handoff]
\label{thm:classifier-information-budget-sigrel-handoff}
Every accepted $\ClassifierInformationBudgetUp$ read of classifier-level information factors through
$$
S_0\longmapsto S_1\longmapsto G\longmapsto D\longmapsto L\longmapsto H,C,P,N.
$$
In particular, the $\SigRel$ row $G$ is mandatory before the ledger-delta row $D$ may classify the transition as classifier-preserving, classifier-shifting, or budget-consuming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with a classifier-information request. The source and target classifier states $S_0,S_1$ are displayed before any comparison can be made.

The request next reads $G$. This row fixes the signature relation by which the two states are compared, so the ledger-delta row $D$ cannot be interpreted independently of the handoff.

Finally the budget row $L$ and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ present the accepted consumer surface. The route therefore records classifier-level information only through the displayed $\SigRel$ comparison and ledger delta.
\end{proof}
