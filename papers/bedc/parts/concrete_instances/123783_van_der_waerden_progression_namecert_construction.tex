\begin{definition}[Van der Waerden progression carrier]
\label{def:van-der-waerden-progression-carrier}
A $\VanDerWaerdenProgressionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $N$ is the $\NatUp$ interval-length row naming the finite prefix $\{0,\ldots,N-1\}$ on which the coloring is read.
\item $C$ is the finite color ledger, presented as a $\ListUp$ row of color observations rather than as a host function into an ambient set of colors.
\item $K$ is the $\NatUp$ progression-length row required by the local van der Waerden request.
\item $P$ is the progression-witness row, storing the finite start and step data whose displayed terms remain inside the interval named by $N$.
\item $A$ is the monochromatic arithmetic-progression row, binding the entries selected by $P$ to one visible color row in $C$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the interval, coloring ledger, length, witness, monochromatic row, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite coloring request through interval admission, witness readback, monochromatic-row inspection, transport, provenance, and local naming.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\VanDerWaerdenProgressionUp$, $\NatUp$, $\ListUp$, $\RamseyUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $S$ is the local $\NameCert_{\VanDerWaerdenProgressionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an infinite coloring, a host choice principle, a classical least-bound search, a quotient equality of colorings, or a real-valued arithmetic progression outside the finite interval.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Van der Waerden progression NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:van-der-waerden-progression-namecert-obligations}

\begin{proof}

The structural rows preserve this finite route. \begin{theorem}[Van der Waerden progression coloring handoff]
\label{thm:van-der-waerden-progression-coloring-handoff}

$$

\begin{proof}
