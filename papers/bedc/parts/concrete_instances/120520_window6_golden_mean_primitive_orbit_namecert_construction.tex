The golden-mean subshift has a primitive-orbit Euler product behind its dynamical zeta. This chapter records the finite weighted Mobius certificate for that Euler-product side: after the divisor substitution $y\mapsto y^d$, the primitive-orbit Mobius transform is divisible by the period and has a nonnegative integral quotient through period $12$. The primitive-orbit Mobius certificate consumes the golden-mean shift carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-golden-mean-shift-namecert} and sits alongside the determinant/zeta window of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-window6-golden-mean-ruelle-zeta}.

\section{The Weighted Primitive-Orbit Transform}
\label{sec:window6-primitive-orbit-transform}

Let $T_y=\begin{pmatrix}1&y\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ be the weighted golden-mean transfer matrix and let
$$
C_n(y)=\operatorname{tr}(T_y^n)
$$
be the weighted cyclic period-count polynomial. The primitive-orbit Mobius transform is
$$
A_n(y)=\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)\,C_{n/d}(y^d).
$$
The substitution $y\mapsto y^d$ is part of the object: an orbit with a weight contribution in the shorter quotient period is repeated $d$ times in the length-$n$ orbit, so its weight exponent is multiplied by $d$.

\begin{theorem}[Finite primitive-orbit integrality and positivity]
\label{thm:window6-primitive-orbit-positivity}
For each $1\le n\le12$, the weighted primitive-orbit Mobius transform satisfies
$$
A_n(y)=n\,P_n(y)
$$
for a polynomial $P_n(y)\in\mathbb N[y]$. The certified quotients are
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_1&=1,\\
P_2&=y,\\
P_3&=y,\\
P_4&=y,\\
P_5&=y+y^2,\\
P_6&=y+y^2,\\
P_7&=y+2y^2+y^3,\\
P_8&=y+2y^2+2y^3,\\
P_9&=y+3y^2+3y^3+y^4,\\
P_{10}&=y+3y^2+5y^3+2y^4,\\
P_{11}&=y+4y^2+7y^3+5y^4+y^5,\\
P_{12}&=y+4y^2+9y^3+8y^4+3y^5.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the Euler-product exponents in this finite window are integral and nonnegative after division by the period.
\end{theorem}

At $y=1$, the values $p_n=A_n(1)/n$ are the primitive golden-mean orbit counts of exact period $n$ in the certified window:
$$
1,\ 1,\ 1,\ 1,\ 2,\ 2,\ 4,\ 5,\ 8,\ 11,\ 18,\ 25.
$$
Equivalently, the corresponding Mobius numerators are
$$
A_1(1),\ldots,A_{12}(1)=1,2,3,4,10,12,28,40,72,110,198,300.
$$

\section{What This Certificate Claims}
\label{sec:window6-primitive-orbit-scope}

This is the finite Euler-product / primitive-orbit positivity side of the golden-mean subshift zeta; it is not a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and not about the Riemann xi. The determinant side and the trace-Hankel positivity side certify spectral polynomial identities and finite square-sum positivity. The Mobius certificate here is different finite content: it certifies the integrality and positivity of the Euler-product exponents after the primitive-orbit divisor transform, with the essential substitution $y\mapsto y^d$.

This certificate is therefore not reducible to the determinant identity. The determinant records a reciprocal zeta polynomial and its spectral transfer reading. The primitive-orbit Mobius transform records how periodic weighted words decompose into primitive cycles and checks that the quotient exponents are actual nonnegative orbit counts in the finite window $1\le n\le12$.
