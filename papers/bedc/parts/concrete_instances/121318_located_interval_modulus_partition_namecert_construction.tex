\label{def:located-interval-modulus-partition-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}

\item $A$ is the locatedness and endpoint-order row, binding the endpoints before any interval partition is read.
\item $Q$ is the finite dyadic partition-cell row supplied by $\DyadicIntervalNetUp$.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since the carrier has no row for host interval equality, selected representatives, trichotomy, choice, compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, none of those endpoints is exported.
