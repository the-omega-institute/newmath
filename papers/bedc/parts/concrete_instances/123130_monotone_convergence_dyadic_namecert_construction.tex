\begin{definition}[MonotoneConvergenceDyadic carrier]
\label{def:monotone-convergence-dyadic-carrier}
A $\MonotoneConvergenceDyadicUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(S,W,D,R,B,T,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source row naming the bounded monotone dyadic convergence request and the admitted finite observation window.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row, recording the finite prefixes of the displayed sequence inspected by the certificate.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ arithmetic row, recording dyadic tolerance budgets and the displayed lower and upper bound comparisons.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row attached to the same windows and dyadic tolerance data.
\item $B$ is the bounded-tail row, recording that all inspected values remain inside the displayed dyadic interval.
\item $T$ is the monotone-transport row, recording finite comparisons that move later windows above earlier windows without introducing trichotomy.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the StreamName, DyadicRatCore, RegSeqRat, bounded-tail, and monotone rows have been read.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from Real-facing or completion-facing requests through the same finite rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MonotoneConvergenceDyadicUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MonotoneConvergenceDyadicUp}$ row.

The classifier has no coordinate for a choice-selected subsequence, quotient stream equality, a supremum operator, trichotomy, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, or a bridge certificate.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[MonotoneConvergenceDyadic obligation surface]
\label{thm:monotone-convergence-dyadic-obligation-surface}
For every accepted $\MonotoneConvergenceDyadicUp$ carrier
$$
M=(S,W,D,R,B,T,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\MonotoneConvergenceDyadicUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the bounded monotone source row, classifier exposure through $S,W,D,R,B,T,E,H,C,P,N$, monotone transport through $T$, bounded-tail Cauchy extraction through $B,D,R$, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:monotone-convergence-dyadic-carrier}. The source, StreamName window, DyadicRatCore tolerance, and RegSeqRat readback rows are displayed before any Real-facing seal is available.

The monotone and bounded-tail obligations are separate rows. The row $T$ transports finite order comparisons forward along the displayed windows, while $B$ keeps those windows inside the named dyadic interval. The Cauchy extraction therefore reads only the finite dyadic budget and regular-rational readback rows.

The terminal seal is downstream. The row $E$ can be consumed only after $W,D,R,B,T$ have been displayed, and the structural rows $H,C,P,N$ replay that same route for every consumer. Since the excluded choice, quotient, supremum, trichotomy, completeness, and bridge data are not carrier coordinates, the listed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
