$$

\begin{theorem}[Koch snowflake NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:koch-snowflake-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\KochSnowflakeUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\KochSnowflakeUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed packet; sidewise $\KochCurveUp$ arc exposure through $A_0,A_1,A_2$; cyclic endpoint compatibility through $S$; dyadic stage and displacement exactness through $D$; finite window scheduling through $W$; regular rational readback through $R$; terminal $\RealUp$ coordinate sealing through $E$; componentwise transport through $H$; displayed replay through $C$; provenance through $P$; and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:koch-snowflake-carrier}. The only arc-facing rows are $A_0,A_1,A_2$, and each is already a sidewise $\KochCurveUp$ packet with its finite address, polygonal-stage, dyadic displacement, coordinate-window, regular-readback, Real-seal, and uniform-limit data.

The compatibility obligation is exactly the row $S$. It compares only the displayed endpoints of adjacent side packets, with the terminal endpoint of one side matched to the initial endpoint of the next side in cyclic order.

The Real-facing obligations are exhausted by $D,W,R,E$. The dyadic stage ledger aligns the side stages and tolerances, the StreamName windows schedule the finite endpoint reads, the RegSeqRat row performs the rational readback, and the terminal coordinate seals remain downstream of the same L10 route.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this finite loop. Since the carrier contains no host planar subset, area row, dimension row, quotient real equality row, selected branch row, or ambient compactness row, no such object can be a local $\NameCert_{\KochSnowflakeUp}$ obligation.
