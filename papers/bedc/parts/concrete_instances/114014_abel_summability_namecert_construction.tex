\begin{theorem}[Abel summability NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:abel-summability-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:abel-summability-carrier}. The tuple displays exactly the Abel summation row, radial-boundary row, Cesaro-facing mean row, dyadic tolerance ledger, finite windows, regular readback, Real seal, and structural rows.

The seal row $E$ is downstream from $R$. Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same displayed route, and $P,N$ only source and name it. Since no further coordinate exists in the carrier, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
