Following Barendregt and Coquand on typed lambda calculi and structural closure arguments, the $\FiniteBetaClosednessIterationUp$ packet records the finite NameCert route behind the MetaCIC theorem \texttt{betaStarStep\_preserves\_closed}. It concretizes the closedness-preservation audit map of \autoref{ch:metacic-closure-preservation-audit-map} beside the witness packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-closure-preservation-witness-namecert} and the subject-reduction obstruction of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metacic-subject-reduction-obstruction-namecert}. The packet is a closedness-only object: it does not assert subject reduction, confluence, normalization, conversion preservation, or a typing transport theorem.

\begin{definition}[Finite beta closedness iteration carrier]
\label{def:finite-beta-closedness-iteration-carrier}
A $\FiniteBetaClosednessIterationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(Z,B,O,S,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $Z$ is the zero-step row, recording that a term already closed at the displayed boundary remains closed without taking a beta step.
\item $B$ is the one-step beta closedness row, read from the \texttt{betaStep\_preserves\_closed} route.
\item $O$ is the finite order row listing the displayed beta steps of the accepted \texttt{BetaStarStep} chain.
\item $S$ is the successor-step row, recording that $B$ may be replayed at the head of the finite chain before the remaining tail is inspected.
\item $L$ is the terminal \texttt{ClosedAt} ledger for the endpoint term.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the chain, closedness boundary, endpoint ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the finite induction from $Z$ through repeated uses of $B$ and $S$ to $L$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FiniteBetaClosednessIterationUp$, \texttt{BetaStep}, \texttt{BetaStarStep}, \texttt{ClosedAt}, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteBetaClosednessIterationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a typing derivation, dependent-codomain transport, subject-reduction discharge, confluence diagram, normalization proof, or conversion rule.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite beta closedness route]
\label{thm:finite-beta-closedness-iteration-route}
Every accepted $\FiniteBetaClosednessIterationUp$ packet routes a finite \texttt{BetaStarStep} chain through the zero-step row, repeated one-step beta closedness rows, the displayed finite order of the chain, and the terminal \texttt{ClosedAt} ledger. Thus a closed source term remains inside the displayed closedness classifier along the finite beta chain, without adding a subject-reduction, confluence, normalization, or conversion claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-beta-closedness-iteration-carrier}. A zero-length beta chain reads $Z$ and immediately reaches the terminal closedness ledger $L$.

For a nonzero finite chain, read the displayed head step from $O$ and apply the one-step row $B$. The successor row $S$ records that the remaining finite tail is another accepted closedness-iteration request.

Iterate this finite route until the endpoint ledger $L$ is reached. The replay row $C$ records the induction as displayed continuation data, and $H$ transports only the closedness and chain rows.

The route never reads a typing derivation or codomain transport. The subject-reduction obstruction remains outside the carrier, so the theorem is a closedness-preservation statement only.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite beta closedness iteration NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-beta-closedness-iteration-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteBetaClosednessIterationUp$ carrier $I=(Z,B,O,S,L,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteBetaClosednessIterationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, zero-step closedness, one-step beta closedness, finite-chain ordering, successor replay, endpoint \texttt{ClosedAt} ledger, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. No obligation is stored for subject reduction, dependent-codomain transport, confluence, normalization, conversion preservation, or typing stability.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is tuple projection. The only visible rows are $Z,B,O,S,L,H,C,P,N$, so the NameCert surface starts and ends with those displayed fields.

The zero-step and successor cases are the two finite-chain constructors. The zero case reads $Z$; the successor case reads $B$ for the head beta step, then uses $S$ and $C$ to replay the same closedness route over the remaining finite tail.

The endpoint ledger $L$ records the final \texttt{ClosedAt} claim. Transport by $H$ is componentwise and cannot change the classifier from closedness to typing.

Finally, provenance and local naming do not enlarge the carrier. Since no subject-reduction, codomain-transport, confluence, normalization, conversion, or typing-stability coordinate exists, none can be exported as a local NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}
