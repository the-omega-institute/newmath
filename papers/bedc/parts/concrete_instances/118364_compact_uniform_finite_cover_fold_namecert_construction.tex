\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform finite-cover fold NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-finite-cover-fold-namecert-obligations}

$$
G=(K,F,L,B,R,U,W,Q,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformFiniteCoverFoldUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the compact finite cover, pointwise-modulus rows, subordinate positive-radius ledger, lower-bound fold, rational readbacks, uniform-modulus output, finite windows, regular readbacks, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming;
\item cover exactness, requiring every center, coverage, and nonempty-spine read to pass through the displayed compact finite-cover row $K$;
\item local-modulus exactness, requiring every pointwise precision row to pass through $F$ over the centers supplied by $K$;
\item radius-fold exactness, requiring every positive-radius comparison to pass through $L,R$ before the finite lower-bound fold $B$ is read;
\item uniform-output ordering, requiring the $\UniformModulusUp$ row $U$ to be downstream of $B$ and not an independent modulus selector;

Cover exactness is supplied by $K$. The bundle centers, coverage witnesses, and nonempty spine have no second cover coordinate, so every cover read enters the packet through the displayed compact finite-cover source.

The fold route is supplied by $L,R,B$. The positive-radius ledger and rational readback rows expose the finite comparisons before primitive recursion over the bundle records the lower-bound fold.

Finally $U,W,Q,H,C,P,N$ impose output order and local naming.
