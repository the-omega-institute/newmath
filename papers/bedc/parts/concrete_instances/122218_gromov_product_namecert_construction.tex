\label{def:gromov-product-carrier}

\item $D$ is the $\RealUp$ distance-readback row for $d(x,b)$, $d(y,b)$, and $d(x,y)$.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,b,x,y,D,T,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for ambient completeness, selected geodesics, quotient boundary points, host hyperbolicity, or external Real equality.

\begin{theorem}[Gromov product NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:gromov-product-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\GromovProductUp$ carrier
$$
G=(M,b,x,y,D,T,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\GromovProductUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric-source admission through $M$, basepoint and endpoint exposure through $b,x,y$, $\RealUp$ distance readback through $D$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance exposure through $T$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:gromov-product-carrier}. The metric row $M$ is the only source of distance observations, and the point rows $b,x,y$ are displayed before any product comparison is read.

Read the distance and budget rows next. The row $D$ exposes exactly the three $\RealUp$ distance reads needed for the basepointed comparison, and $T$ records the finite dyadic tolerances used by downstream inequality consumers.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same finite packet. Since no row supplies ambient completeness, selected geodesics, quotient boundary points, host hyperbolicity, or external Real equality, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
