Following Bishop and Bridges on regulated functions and regular Cauchy real names, the $\RegulatedCauchyStepApproximationUp$ packet records the finite approximation schedule by which displayed step-function windows over a regulated source produce regular-rational names for Real-facing consumers. It sits after the regulated-function carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regulated-function-namecert}, the dyadic step-function surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicstepfunction-namecert}, the uniform-limit route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformlimit-namecert}, the dyadic mesh gauge of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-mesh-gauge-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name chapter of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Regulated Cauchy step approximation carrier]
\label{def:regulated-cauchy-step-approximation-carrier}
A $\RegulatedCauchyStepApproximationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(R,S,U,G,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the $\RegulatedFunctionUp$ source row whose one-sided oscillation data are exposed on the displayed interval windows.
\item $S$ is the $\StepFunctionUp$ approximation ledger, listing finitely many step rows and their domains of constancy.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformLimitUp$ handoff binding the step rows to the regulated source at the requested tolerance.
\item $G$ is the $\DyadicMeshGaugeUp$ mesh row that supplies the finite dyadic subdivision used by the step ledger.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular-rational readback row for the sampled approximation values and tail budgets.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after $R,S,U,G,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the regulated source, step ledger, uniform-limit handoff, dyadic mesh, regular readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a regulated-function request through the finite mesh, step approximation ledger, regular readback, and Real-facing handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegulatedCauchyStepApproximationUp$, $\RegulatedFunctionUp$, $\StepFunctionUp$, $\UniformLimitUp$, $\DyadicMeshGaugeUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegulatedCauchyStepApproximationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $R,S,U,G,Q,E,H,C,P,N$. No quotient function equality, selected limit, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness coordinate is part of the carrier.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Regulated Cauchy step approximation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regulated-cauchy-step-approximation-namecert-obligations}
For an accepted $\RegulatedCauchyStepApproximationUp$ carrier $A=(R,S,U,G,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegulatedCauchyStepApproximationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier exposure for the regulated source, step-function ledger, uniform-limit handoff, dyadic mesh gauge, regular-rational readback, Real seal, componentwise transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming rows. Every accepted read first fixes the displayed regulated source $R$, then chooses only the finite mesh cells in $G$, then reads the step ledger $S$ and uniform-limit handoff $U$, and finally exports the regular-rational and Real-facing rows $Q,E,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:regulated-cauchy-step-approximation-carrier}. The rows $R,S,U,G,Q,E,H,C,P,N$ are the only displayed coordinates of the packet.

The source and mesh obligations read $R$ and $G$ before any step approximation is consumed. The step ledger $S$ is therefore tied to the displayed dyadic cells rather than to an external partition choice.

The uniform-limit and regular-readback obligations use $U$ and $Q$. They attach each tolerance request to the finite step ledger and regular rational tail data before the terminal Real seal $E$ is available.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Since the carrier has no row for quotient function equality, a selected limit object, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, none of those readings can enter the local certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Regulated Cauchy step approximation RegSeqRat handoff]
\label{thm:regulated-cauchy-step-approximation-regseqrat-handoff}
Every $\RegSeqRatUp$ or $\RealUp$ consumer reading an accepted $\RegulatedCauchyStepApproximationUp$ packet factors through
$$
R \longmapsto G \longmapsto S \longmapsto U \longmapsto Q \longmapsto E \longmapsto H,C,P,N .
$$
The consumer receives a regular-rational name assembled from finitely displayed step approximations and dyadic mesh gauges. It receives no quotient equality of regulated functions, selected uniform limit, countable-choice sequence, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:regulated-cauchy-step-approximation-namecert-obligations}. They force a consumer to read the regulated source $R$, the dyadic mesh row $G$, the step ledger $S$, and the uniform-limit handoff $U$ before $Q$ can be inspected.

The row $Q$ is the only regular-rational readback coordinate. Thus a $\RegSeqRatUp$ consumer receives the tail budget and sampled approximation values from the finite step schedule, not from an external limiting object.

The terminal seal $E$ is downstream of $Q$, while $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same route under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. The route therefore cannot export quotient function equality, a selected limit, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{proof}
