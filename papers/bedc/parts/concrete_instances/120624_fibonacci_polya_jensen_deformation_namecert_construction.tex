\chapter{The Fibonacci--Polya Jensen Deformation}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-fibonacci-polya-jensen-deformation}
\origin{ai}

This chapter records a finite lambda-deformation of the Fibonacci--Polya Jensen kernel. Let
$$
\widehat J^{d,n}(X)=X^dJ^{d,n}(1/X)
$$
be the reciprocal Jensen polynomial attached to the Taylor coefficients $\gamma_n$ of the Riemann $\xi$ function. For a Fibonacci cube of length $m$, set
$$
W_m(T)=\sum_k \binom{m-k+1}{k}T^k.
$$
The deformation is
$$
F^{(\lambda)}_{m,d,n}(X)=W_m(\lambda D)\widehat J^{d,n}(X),
$$
where $D=d/dX$. At $\lambda=0$ this is the actual reciprocal xi Jensen polynomial. At $\lambda=1$ this is the Fibonacci-smoothed window. The parameter therefore embeds the actual degree window into a finite Polya--Schur smoothing family without replacing the xi coefficients by a Fibonacci counting model.

\section{Degree-Two Lambda Discriminant}
\label{sec:fibonacci-polya-jensen-deformation-degree-two}

In degree two write
$$
\widehat J^{2,n}(X)=aX^2+2bX+c,
$$
where $a=\gamma_n$, $b=\gamma_{n+1}$, and $c=\gamma_{n+2}$. Since
$$
W_m(\lambda D)=1+m\lambda D+\frac{(m-1)(m-2)}{2}\lambda^2D^2
$$
on degree-two polynomials, the deformed window is
$$
F^{(\lambda)}_{m,2,n}(X)
=aX^2+(2am\lambda+2b)X+
\left(a\lambda^2m^2-3a\lambda^2m+2a\lambda^2+2bm\lambda+c\right).
$$
Its discriminant is the finite identity
$$
\operatorname{disc}F^{(\lambda)}_{m,2,n}
=4\left((b^2-ac)+(3m-2)\lambda^2a^2\right).
$$
Equivalently,
$$
\operatorname{disc}F^{(\lambda)}_{m,2,n}-4(b^2-ac)
=4(3m-2)\lambda^2a^2.
$$
For $m\ge 1$, the Fibonacci term is nonnegative after the usual shift $m\mapsto m+1$. Thus the deformation certifies a continuous hyperbolicity stability margin from the actual Jensen discriminant at $\lambda=0$ to the Fibonacci-smoothed margin at $\lambda=1$.

\begin{theorem}[Fibonacci--Polya lambda-deformation Jensen certificate]
\label{thm:fibonacci-polya-jensen-deformation-certificate}
The degree-two lambda-deformed Fibonacci--Polya Jensen window has the symbolic discriminant certificate
$$
\operatorname{disc}F^{(\lambda)}_{m,2,n}
=4\left((\gamma_{n+1}^2-\gamma_n\gamma_{n+2})+(3m-2)\lambda^2\gamma_n^2\right).
$$
The specialization $\lambda=0$ gives the actual reciprocal Jensen discriminant $4(\gamma_{n+1}^2-\gamma_n\gamma_{n+2})$, the specialization $\lambda=1$ gives the Fibonacci-smoothed degree-two window, and the difference from the actual Jensen discriminant is the explicit lambda-square margin.
\end{theorem}

\section{Honest Scope}
\label{sec:fibonacci-polya-jensen-deformation-honest-scope}

This is a finite Jensen stability-margin certificate under Fibonacci Polya--Schur deformation, interpolating the actual reciprocal xi Jensen polynomial at $\lambda=0$ and the Fibonacci-smoothed window at $\lambda=1$. It is a genuine finite RH-shadow, not a sharper attack on RH, not a Zeckendorf model of xi, and not a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. The coefficients $\gamma_n,\gamma_{n+1},\gamma_{n+2}$ remain symbolic throughout.

The Lean certificate proves the algebraic normal-form identity for the lambda discriminant, the $\lambda=0$ and $\lambda=1$ specializations, and the lambda-square margin identity. It does not evaluate xi coefficients, does not assert global hyperbolicity for all Jensen degrees, and does not discharge any analytic zero-location theorem. Its role is to place the actual degree-two Jensen discriminant inside a finite hyperbolicity-preserving deformation whose extra Fibonacci contribution is visible and nonnegative in the certified margin.
