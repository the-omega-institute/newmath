Following Heine--Cantor compact-uniform-continuity practice in Bishop--Bridges constructive analysis, the $\CompactUniformRadiusSelectorUp$ packet names the finite lower-bound selector that turns compact local-radius data into a tolerance-only uniform-radius row. It realizes the radius-selection branch of \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity} beside the compact metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the continuous-map rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the uniform-modulus consumer of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformmodulus-namecert}, the rational lower-bound ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-rat-namecert}, and the metric-space distance surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-space-namecert}. The packet records finite ProbeBundle data only; it is not an open-cover compactness theorem, an external choice of radii, or a host modulus function.

\begin{definition}[Compact uniform radius selector carrier]
\label{def:compact-uniform-radius-selector-carrier}
A $\CompactUniformRadiusSelectorUp$ carrier is a finite packet
$$
R=(K,F,B,L,U,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
whose rows have the following roles:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net source over the same metric carrier used by the consumer.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ pointwise-radius source exposing local radii for the requested tolerance.
\item $B$ is the finite $\ProbeBundle$ row of compact centers and source coverage witnesses.
\item $L$ is the $\RatUp$ positive lower-bound fold over the bundled local radii.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ target row receiving the selected radius.
\item $T$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ triangle and distance-transport row used to move from center radii to arbitrary source points.
\item $H,C,P,N$ are componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformRadiusSelectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for selected subcovers, countable choice, host infima over radii, quotient maps, or an external modulus selector.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform radius selector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-radius-selector-namecert-obligations}
Every accepted $\CompactUniformRadiusSelectorUp$ carrier $R=(K,F,B,L,U,T,H,C,P,N)$ exposes the compact metric finite-net row, continuous pointwise-radius row, finite ProbeBundle coverage row, rational lower-bound fold, uniform-modulus target, metric triangle transport, $\hsame$ transport, $\Cont$ replay, provenance, and local naming rows before a compact-uniform-continuity consumer reads a selected radius. The selection cannot be replaced by an ambient infimum, an unrestricted open-cover argument, countable choice, selected representatives, or a host modulus function.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:compact-uniform-radius-selector-carrier}. The compact source $K$, pointwise-radius source $F$, and finite bundle $B$ are the only rows from which a radius-selection consumer may start.

Read the lower-bound and target rows next. The rational fold $L$ is built over the finite bundle and supplies the radius consumed by the $\UniformModulusUp$ row $U$, while the metric row $T$ provides the triangle transport needed for arbitrary source points.

Finally apply the structural rows $H,C,P,N$. They replay the same finite route under transport and provenance, so no ambient infimum, open-cover compactness principle, countable choice, selected representative, or host modulus coordinate is exposed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform radius selector vision handoff]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-radius-selector-vision-handoff}
The finite lower-bound fold used in \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity} is realized by the $\CompactUniformRadiusSelectorUp$ route
$$
K,F,B,L \longmapsto U,T,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the compact-uniform-continuity consumer reads a radius through compact finite-net data, continuous pointwise-radius data, a finite $\ProbeBundle$ fold, rational positivity, metric triangle transport, and local NameCert rows, rather than through a host proof that a positive minimum of radii exists.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the compact finite-net and pointwise-radius rows. They supply the bundled centers and local radii that the compact-uniform-continuity construction needs before any global radius is visible.

Use the finite lower-bound fold row $L$. Because the fold is carried by the displayed $\ProbeBundle$ and $\RatUp$ positivity data, the selected radius is a named history, not an external minimum or choice.

Transport the selected row through $U,T,H,C,P,N$. The uniform-modulus target receives the radius only after metric triangle transport and structural replay, so the vision's lower-bound step is a concrete NameCert route.
\end{proof}
