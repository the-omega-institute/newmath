\begin{definition}[Backward differentiation formula carrier]
\label{def:backward-differentiation-formula-carrier}
A $\BackwardDifferentiationFormulaUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
B=(O,W,C,D,R,S,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the $\OdeUp$ source row naming the inspected right-hand side and the finite step request.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ history window containing the displayed past solution values used by the multistep stencil.
\item $C$ is the coefficient row for the selected backward differentiation stencil, including the current implicit coefficient and the finite list of past coefficients.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row for the step size, residual budget, and finite comparison windows.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for every rational coefficient, history value, and residual budget consumed by the step.
\item $S$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for the current implicit residual after $O,W,C,D,R$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the ODE source, history window, coefficient row, tolerance row, readback row, seal row, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite step request through the same history, coefficient, tolerance, readback, and seal rows.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BackwardDifferentiationFormulaUp$, $\OdeUp$, $\FiniteDifferenceUp$, $\RungeKuttaFiniteStepUp$, $\EulerMethodFiniteStepUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ records the local $\NameCert_{\BackwardDifferentiationFormulaUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for a chosen exact solution, a selected implicit root, global convergence, a host real-completeness theorem, or an infinite time grid.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Backward differentiation formula NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:backward-differentiation-formula-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\BackwardDifferentiationFormulaUp$ carrier $B=(O,W,C,D,R,S,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BackwardDifferentiationFormulaUp}$ surface consists exactly of ODE source admission, finite history-window coverage, backward-stencil coefficient exposure, dyadic step and residual budget exposure, regular rational readback, terminal Real seal admission, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier rows. The ODE source $O$, history window $W$, and coefficient row $C$ provide the finite implicit multistep stencil; no solution curve is present outside these rows.

Read the numerical boundary through $D,R,S$. The dyadic row supplies the step and residual budget, the RegSeqRat row supplies rational readback for the displayed data, and the Real row seals only the displayed residual comparison.

Transport and replay are then forced through $H$ and $K$. Provenance $P$ and local naming $N$ preserve the same finite packet, so the NameCert obligations are exactly the listed finite rows and not a convergence theorem or selected implicit solve.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Backward differentiation formula window stability]
\label{thm:backward-differentiation-formula-window-stability}
Let $B=(O,W,C,D,R,S,H,K,P,N)$ and $B'=(O',W',C',D',R',S',H',K',P',N')$ be accepted $\BackwardDifferentiationFormulaUp$ carriers. If the source, history, coefficient, tolerance, readback, and seal rows are componentwise transported by the displayed $\hsame$ data and replayed through the displayed $\Cont$ routes, then every finite-step consumer sees the same backward-stencil window and the same residual-budget ledger. The stable surface is the finite step packet; it exposes no selected exact solution, host implicit-root choice, infinite grid, global convergence theorem, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Use the displayed $\hsame$ data to identify the corresponding ODE source, history window, coefficient row, dyadic budget, regular readback, and Real seal rows. The replay rows $K$ and $K'$ then route the same step request through the transported finite packet.

Finally read $P,N$ and $P',N'$. These rows name and source the same finite backward-stencil window, so stability holds at the packet boundary and cannot be promoted to a selected solution, an implicit-root oracle, an infinite grid, convergence, or completeness.
\end{proof}
