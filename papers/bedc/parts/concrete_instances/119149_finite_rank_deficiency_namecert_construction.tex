The packet is a finite support-rank certificate; it is not a host rank oracle, a biological semantics claim, quotient support equality, an infinite support lattice, or a basis-selection theorem.

\begin{definition}[Finite rank-deficiency carrier]
\label{def:finite-rank-deficiency-carrier}
A $\FiniteRankDeficiencyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
F=(U,S,R,P,L,K,T,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $U$ is the finite support-universe row naming the inspected support coordinates.
\item $S$ is the finite support-family row whose members are compared with $U$.
\item $R$ is the support-rank row, recording the visible size or rank token of each displayed support.
\item $P$ is the proper-support classifier row, accepting exactly the displayed supports that remain below the full support named by $U$.
\item $L$ is the deficiency ledger separating proper-support rows from the full-support corner.
\item $K$ is the consumed $\CoverageRankAlgebraUp$ sibling row when the support family comes from a coverage or trigger algebra.
\item $T$ is the consumed $\RankNullityUp$ sibling row when the support-rank read is compared with a kernel-image accounting surface.
\item $C$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for support universes, support rows, rank rows, proper-support classifiers, ledgers, sibling algebra rows, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\item $G$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a finite rank-deficiency request through $U,S,R,P,L,K,T,C$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteRankDeficiencyUp}$ row, with $\Pkg$ provenance over the displayed rows, \autoref{thm:philosophy-bedc-rank-deficiency-prototype}, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier accepts a finite rank-deficiency read only through $U,S,R,P,L,K,T,C,G,N$. It has no coordinate for hidden full-support escape, host rank completion, quotient support equality, biological interpretation, stochastic dynamics, infinite support search, or selected bases.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite rank-deficiency NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-rank-deficiency-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FiniteRankDeficiencyUp$ carrier
$$
F=(U,S,R,P,L,K,T,C,G,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FiniteRankDeficiencyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the finite support universe, support family, rank row, proper-support classifier, deficiency ledger, optional coverage-rank sibling row, optional rank-nullity sibling row, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming; proper-support exactness for $P$ against the full support named by $U$; ledger separation of proper-support rows from the full-support corner; sibling preservation through $K$ and $T$ whenever those rows are present; and structural preservation through $C,G,N$. No finite rank-deficiency obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-rank-deficiency-carrier}. The rows $U,S,R,P,L$ display the finite support universe, support family, rank read, proper-support classifier, and deficiency ledger before any sibling algebra is consumed.

Proper-support exactness is the finite law of \autoref{thm:philosophy-bedc-rank-deficiency-prototype}: accepted deficient rows are those whose displayed support remains below the full support named by $U$. The ledger row $L$ keeps the full-support corner visible as a refused escape rather than erasing it.

The sibling rows $K$ and $T$ do not add hidden rank data. They only route already displayed coverage-rank and rank-nullity surfaces into the same finite support-rank packet.

Finally $C,G,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same rows. Since hidden support completion, quotient support equality, biological interpretation, infinite search, and selected bases are absent from the carrier, the listed obligations exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite rank-deficiency proper-support nonescape]
\label{thm:finite-rank-deficiency-proper-support-nonescape}
Every accepted $\FiniteRankDeficiencyUp$ packet that reads a proper-support row must keep the route
$$
U,S,R,P,L,K,T,C,G,N
$$
visible until the local $\NameCert$ row is consumed. In particular, a consumer cannot promote the proper-support classifier $P$ to the full-support corner of $U$, cannot replace the displayed rank row $R$ by a host rank oracle, and cannot identify two support packets by quotient support equality unless the required transport is already displayed in $C$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from an accepted proper-support read. Carrier projection exposes $U,S,R,P,L$, so the support universe, inspected support family, rank token, classifier, and deficiency ledger are all visible.

The nonescape claim follows from the separation row $L$. It records the full-support corner as a boundary distinct from the accepted proper-support rows, so a downstream consumer cannot collapse the boundary into the classifier without adding a row not present in the packet.

The coverage-rank and rank-nullity siblings are read only through $K$ and $T$. These rows preserve the same support-rank evidence and do not supply a host rank oracle or selected basis.

The structural rows $C,G,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name the displayed packet. Therefore any read that leaves $U,S,R,P,L,K,T,C,G,N$ has left the finite rank-deficiency NameCert route.
\end{proof}
