Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\LocatedSignStabilityUp$ packet records the finite sign-window stability boundary by which a located sign read remains stable under a displayed tolerance refinement. It sits between the sign read of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-apartness-sign-namecert}, the located positivity route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-located-positive-real-namecert}, the apartness source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-apartnessreal-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-window interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names the boundary at which a positive-side, negative-side, or zero-adjacent refusal read is preserved by shrinking the displayed window; it does not provide trichotomy, quotient real equality, selected limits, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Located sign-stability carrier]
\label{def:located-sign-stability-carrier}
A $\LocatedSignStabilityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(X,A,P,D,W,Q,L,R,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the inspected $\RealUp$ row, read only through its displayed real-name presentation.
\item $A$ is the $\ApartnessRealUp$ comparison row against the zero real-name boundary.
\item $P$ is the $\RealApartnessSignUp$ sign-read row exposing the current positive-side, negative-side, or zero-adjacent refusal alternative.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger for the requested sign window and its refinement.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation window opened for the sign read.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback attached to $W$ and to the dyadic comparison.
\item $L$ is the stability ledger asserting that the displayed sign alternative is preserved by the named refinement of $D,W,Q$.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached after the sign and stability rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the real row, apartness row, sign row, dyadic ledger, window, readback, stability ledger, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the sign request through apartness inspection, finite-window readback, dyadic refinement, stability, and the real seal.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedSignStabilityUp$, $\RealApartnessSignUp$, $\LocatedPositiveRealUp$, $\ApartnessRealUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedSignStabilityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for real trichotomy, total order, quotient stream equality, host real equality, selected representatives, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located sign-stability NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-sign-stability-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedSignStabilityUp$ carrier
$$
S=(X,A,P,D,W,Q,L,R,H,C,G,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedSignStabilityUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed real row, apartness row, sign-read row, dyadic tolerance ledger, stream window, regular readback, stability ledger, real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; apartness anchoring of $P$ through $A$; finite-window readback through $D,W,Q$; stability exactness through $L$; real-seal factorization through $R$; and ledger exhaustion excluding trichotomy, total order, quotient stream equality, selected limits, countable choice, and ambient completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-sign-stability-carrier}. The tuple displays exactly the real row, apartness row, sign-read row, dyadic ledger, stream window, readback, stability ledger, seal, and structural rows.

The sign row is anchored by $A$. Thus the positive-side, negative-side, or zero-adjacent refusal alternative in $P$ is read through apartness from the zero boundary rather than through an ambient order predicate.

The window-stability part is the route through $D,W,Q,L$. The dyadic tolerance, finite observation window, and regular readback are exposed before the stability ledger records preservation under the named refinement.

Finally $H,C,G,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only the displayed packet. Since no excluded object is a carrier coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located sign-stability window preservation]
\label{thm:located-sign-stability-window-preservation}
Every accepted $\LocatedSignStabilityUp$ packet preserves its displayed sign alternative only along the finite route
$$
X,A,P,D,W,Q \longmapsto L \longmapsto R \longmapsto H,C,G,N .
$$
A Real or Completion consumer may use the preserved sign read after the apartness comparison, dyadic tolerance ledger, StreamName window, and RegSeqRat readback are displayed, but cannot replace that route by quotient streams, selected limits, hidden completion rows, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the accepted carrier. Projection gives the route $X,A,P,D,W,Q,L,R,H,C,G,N$ and no second sign-stability coordinate.

Read $P$ through $A$. The sign alternative is the finite RealApartnessSign row attached to the displayed comparison with zero, so a consumer has no detached trichotomy decision.

Next read the window data through $D,W,Q$. The stability ledger $L$ can preserve only the alternative supported by those finite rows and their named refinement.

The terminal seal $R$ and structural rows $H,C,G,N$ then carry the same packet to downstream Real and Completion consumers. Since the carrier excludes quotient streams, selected limits, hidden completion rows, countable choice, and ambient completeness, window preservation remains inside the displayed route.
\end{proof}
