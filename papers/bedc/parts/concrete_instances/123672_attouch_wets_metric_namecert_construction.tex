Following Attouch--Wets hyperspace convergence, the $\AttouchWetsMetricUp$ packet records the metric-facing side of bounded-window variational convergence as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits between the observation topology of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-attouch-wets-topology-namecert} and the convergence packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-attouch-wets-convergence-namecert}, while retaining explicit links to $\WijsmanTopologyUp$, $\VietorisTopologyUp$, $\HausdorffMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, and $\RealUp$. The packet names the local distance surface induced by displayed bounded windows; it does not import a completed hyperspace, a quotient of closed figures, selected nearest points, or choice-based compactness.

\begin{definition}[Attouch-Wets metric carrier]
\label{def:attouch-wets-metric-carrier}
An $\AttouchWetsMetricUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A_{\mathrm{awm}}=(M,W,V,H,T,G,D,R,E,Q,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ source row for the ambient distance observations and bounded balls.
\item $W$ is the $\WijsmanTopologyUp$ point-distance row, read only through displayed witnesses and finite tolerance cells.
\item $V$ is the $\VietorisTopologyUp$ hit--miss row for the same bounded window schedule.
\item $H$ is the $\HausdorffMetricUp$ bounded-set distance row, restricted to the finite windows exposed by $M,W,V$.
\item $T$ is the $\AttouchWetsTopologyUp$ observation-topology row that orders the local balls, comparison radii, and tolerance requests.
\item $G$ is the $\AttouchWetsConvergenceUp$ handoff row recording which downstream convergence packet can read the metric surface.
\item $D$ is the dyadic tolerance ledger for window radius, local Hausdorff margin, Wijsman point-distance precision, Vietoris hit--miss precision, and late real-name precision.
\item $R$ is the regular rational readback row for the bounded-window distance comparisons obtained from $M,W,V,H,T,D$.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal reached only after the regular rational readback has been displayed.
\item $Q$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the metric source, Wijsman row, Vietoris row, Hausdorff metric row, Attouch--Wets topology row, convergence handoff, tolerance ledger, readback, real seal, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from an Attouch--Wets metric request through the same finite route.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\AttouchWetsMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\WijsmanTopologyUp$, $\VietorisTopologyUp$, $\HausdorffMetricUp$, $\AttouchWetsTopologyUp$, $\AttouchWetsConvergenceUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AttouchWetsMetricUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for a host hyperspace metric, quotient equality of closed figures, selected projections, choice-based compactness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness beyond the displayed terminal seal.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Attouch-Wets metric NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:attouch-wets-metric-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AttouchWetsMetricUp$ carrier
$$
A_{\mathrm{awm}}=(M,W,V,H,T,G,D,R,E,Q,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\AttouchWetsMetricUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission; ambient metric-source exposure through $M$; Wijsman point-distance exposure through $W$; Vietoris hit--miss exposure through $V$; bounded Hausdorff metric comparison through $H$; Attouch--Wets topology scheduling through $T$; Attouch--Wets convergence handoff through $G$; dyadic tolerance, regular rational readback, and terminal Real sealing through $D,R,E$; and componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming through $Q,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:attouch-wets-metric-carrier}. The rows $M,W,V,H,T,G,D,R,E,Q,C,P,N$ are the only displayed coordinates available to the local NameCert.

The metric-facing obligations are $M,W,V,H,T,G$. They expose the ambient metric observations, point-distance readings, hit--miss readings, bounded Hausdorff metric comparisons, local Attouch--Wets topology schedule, and convergence handoff in that order.

The numerical obligations are $D,R,E$. The dyadic ledger fixes the requested tolerances, the regular rational row records the finite comparisons, and the Real row seals the resulting local distance value.

Finally $Q,C,P,N$ preserve, replay, source, and name that route. Since no carrier coordinate stores a completed hyperspace metric, quotient equality of closed figures, selected projections, choice-based compactness, or ambient completeness beyond the displayed seal, those objects do not belong to the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Attouch-Wets metric interface route]
\label{thm:attouch-wets-metric-interface-route}
Every metric-facing consumer of an accepted $\AttouchWetsMetricUp$ packet reads the finite route
$$
\MetricUp,\ \WijsmanTopologyUp,\ \VietorisTopologyUp,\ \HausdorffMetricUp
\longmapsto
\AttouchWetsTopologyUp,\ \AttouchWetsConvergenceUp
\longmapsto
\RealUp,\ \hsame,\ \Cont,\ \Pkg,\ \NameCert_{\AttouchWetsMetricUp}.
$$
Thus later variational compactness consumers receive a bounded-window metric surface rather than a host hyperspace metric or a quotient carrier for closed figures.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the source row $M$. It supplies only the ambient distance observations and bounded balls displayed by the packet.

The next rows $W,V,H$ give the metric-facing hyperspace readings. Wijsman point distances, Vietoris hit--miss cells, and Hausdorff metric comparisons are all restricted to the same bounded-window schedule.

Rows $T$ and $G$ connect that metric surface to the existing Attouch--Wets topology and convergence packets. The numerical rows $D,R,E$ then record tolerance, rational readback, and the terminal real seal for the displayed comparisons.

The structural rows $Q,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite route. Therefore any downstream compactness or variational consumer sees the bounded-window metric interface, not an ambient completed hyperspace metric, quotient equality of closed figures, selected projections, or choice-based compactness.
\end{proof}
