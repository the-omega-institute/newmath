\begin{theorem}[Egoroff finite-measure NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:egoroff-finite-measure-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\EgoroffFiniteMeasureUp$ carrier
$$

No obligation is stored outside $M,F,S,B,W,R,U,H,C,P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:egoroff-finite-measure-carrier}. The analytic rows are $M,F,S,B,W,R,U$, while $H,C,P,N$ are the structural certificate rows.

The measure source and finite-measure ledger are admitted first. The exceptional-budget row $B$ can therefore speak only about displayed finite events and their recorded finite values, not about a hidden countable union or completed null ideal.

The stream row $S$ supplies the inspected pointwise tails. The rows $W$ and $R$ then expose a finite tail index and a regular rational readback for the accepted request.

Finally $U,H,C,P,N$ bind that budget and window to one public route. Since no excluded object is a carrier coordinate, none is a NameCert obligation of the packet.
\end{proof}
