\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\LocatedUniformityUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-located-uniformity-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bourbaki and Kelley on uniform structures, and Bishop--Bridges on
located metric comparison and regular real names, the $\LocatedUniformityUp$
packet records the finite interface where metric locatedness is read as a
uniform-entourage basis before any Cauchy-filter, stream-name, regular-rational,
or real-seal consumer may use it. It sits between the ambient uniform-space
surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-uniformspace-namecert}, the
real-entourage route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-uniform-structure-namecert},
the regular-Cauchy entourage route of
\autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regularcauchyuniformity-namecert}, the Cauchy
filter boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cauchyfilter-namecert}, the
finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert},
the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert},
the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the
located compact consumer surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-locatedcompact-namecert}.
The packet names a located uniformity as displayed finite data; it does not
select a completion point, quotient filters, import an open-cover compactness
principle, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Located uniformity carrier]
\label{def:located-uniformity-carrier}
A $\LocatedUniformityUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(M,A,E,F,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the metric source row, naming the $\MetricUp$ or real-facing metric
source whose entourage reads are admitted by the packet.
\item $A$ is the located comparison row, exposing finite inside-or-apart
comparison data for point-pairs and positive rational radii.
\item $E$ is the entourage-basis row, recording the displayed basis used by
$\UniformSpaceUp$, $\RealUniformStructureUp$, and $\RegularCauchyUniformityUp$
consumers.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ handoff row, available only after $M,A,E$
have been displayed.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window row read by filter and completion
consumers.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the windows named by $W$.
\item $R$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the located
comparison, entourage basis, Cauchy-filter handoff, stream windows, and regular
rational readback are visible.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for
$M,A,E,F,W,Q,R,C,P,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a metric locatedness request
through the entourage basis, Cauchy-filter handoff, StreamName windows,
RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocatedUniformityUp$, $\MetricUp$,
$\UniformSpaceUp$, $\RealUniformStructureUp$, $\RegularCauchyUniformityUp$,
$\CauchyFilterUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$,
$\LocatedCompactUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedUniformityUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through these rows. It has no
coordinate for a hidden completion point, quotient filter equality, host
uniform-space equality, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient
$\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniformity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:located-uniformity-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocatedUniformityUp$ carrier
$$
L=(M,A,E,F,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocatedUniformityUp}$ surface consists exactly of metric
source admission, located comparison admission, entourage-basis admission,
Cauchy-filter handoff, StreamName window admission, RegSeqRat readback, Real
seal nonescape, componentwise hsame transport, displayed Cont replay, Pkg
provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:located-uniformity-carrier}. The analytic
rows are exactly $M,A,E,F,W,Q,R$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The metric source and located comparison rows are read first. They expose the
finite point-pair and rational-radius comparisons from which the entourage
basis row $E$ may be inspected by $\UniformSpaceUp$,
$\RealUniformStructureUp$, and $\RegularCauchyUniformityUp$ consumers.

The Cauchy-filter and completion-facing rows are downstream. A consumer reaches
$F$ only after the located entourage basis is visible, then reads $W$ as finite
windows, $Q$ as regular rational readback, and $R$ as the terminal real seal.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same route. Since no
excluded host object is a carrier coordinate, these rows exhaust the local
NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniformity Cauchy-filter handoff]
\label{thm:located-uniformity-cauchy-filter-handoff}
Every Cauchy-filter or completion consumer of an accepted
$\LocatedUniformityUp$ carrier receives the located uniformity only through the
finite route
$$
M,A,E,F,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N .
$$
In particular, the $\CauchyFilterUp$ handoff row is consumed after the metric
located comparison and entourage basis rows, and before the StreamName,
RegSeqRat, and Real-seal rows. The handoff exports no quotient filter equality,
selected completion point, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness
principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a Cauchy-filter or completion read of the accepted packet. The
carrier first exposes the metric source $M$, the located comparison row $A$,
and the entourage-basis row $E$.

The handoff row $F$ is the only Cauchy-filter coordinate of the carrier. The
consumer may inspect $F$ only along the route already displaying $M,A,E$.

The read then proceeds through $W,Q,R$: finite stream windows, regular rational
readback, and the terminal real seal. Componentwise transport and continuation
replay preserve the same order, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local
naming.

Because no quotient filter, selected completion point, choice schedule, or
ambient completeness coordinate occurs in the packet, the consumer receives
exactly the displayed finite handoff.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Located uniformity Real seal nonescape]
\label{thm:located-uniformity-real-seal-nonescape}
Every $\RealUp$-facing read exported by an accepted $\LocatedUniformityUp$
carrier factors through the displayed located-uniformity route
$$
M,A,E,F,W,Q,R,H,C,P,N .
$$
Thus the terminal seal row $R$ is reached only after metric source admission,
located comparison, entourage-basis admission, Cauchy-filter handoff, finite
StreamName windows, and RegSeqRat readback. It does not export host real
equality, quotient stream equality, quotient filter equality, selected limits,
or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{thm:located-uniformity-namecert-obligations} to identify the
complete local obligation surface. The route to the real seal contains the
source, comparison, entourage, filter, window, and rational-readback rows before
$R$.

Next apply the handoff theorem
\autoref{thm:located-uniformity-cauchy-filter-handoff}. It fixes the order in
which Cauchy-filter and completion consumers may read the packet, so the
terminal real seal cannot be reached from an unlisted source.

The structural rows preserve this route without adding host data:
$H$ transports the displayed coordinates, $C$ replays the same finite path, $P$
records only the named sibling packets, and $N$ names the local certificate.
Hence the real-facing read is a finite seal boundary, not a quotient,
selection, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{proof}
