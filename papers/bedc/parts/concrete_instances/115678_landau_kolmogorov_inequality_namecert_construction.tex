It sits beside the asymptotic comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-landau-symbol-namecert}, the finite-window interpolation route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-gagliardo-nirenberg-finite-window-namecert}, the norm carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-norm-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Landau--Kolmogorov inequality carrier]
\label{def:landau-kolmogorov-inequality-carrier}

$$

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the derivative-window ledger, naming the finite orders $0<k<m$ and the inspected derivative windows used by the estimate.
\item $N_0,N_k,N_m$ are the $\NormUp$ rows for the base function, the intermediate derivative, and the highest displayed derivative.
\item $A$ is the interpolation-exponent row, storing the finite rational exponent data and coefficient budget used by the Landau--Kolmogorov comparison.
\item $W$ is the $\GagliardoNirenbergFiniteWindowUp$ handoff row that places the displayed derivative windows and norm ledgers in one finite interpolation packet.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational inequality budget and all finite tolerance requests.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row, reached only after $D,N_0,N_k,N_m,A,W,R$ have been displayed.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $D,N_0,N_k,N_m,A,W,R,E,H,C,P,Z$. \begin{theorem}[Landau--Kolmogorov inequality NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:landau-kolmogorov-inequality-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LandauKolmogorovInequalityUp$ carrier
$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LandauKolmogorovInequalityUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed derivative-window ledger, three norm ledgers, interpolation-exponent row, finite-window handoff, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item derivative-window exactness, requiring the intermediate derivative request and the highest derivative request to be read through the same finite row $D$ before any norm comparison is exposed;
\item norm-ledger accountability, requiring $N_0,N_k,N_m$ to be the only norm rows available to the inequality read;
\item interpolation-exponent exactness, requiring $A$ and $W$ to store the rational exponent data and finite interpolation budget before $R$ may read the inequality as regular rational data;
\item Real-seal nonescape, requiring every Real-facing conclusion to pass through $R$ and then $E$;

\item refusal of host function equality, selected derivative representatives, unbounded differentiability towers, measure completion, quotient norm equality, countable choice, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness as local certificate data.

The derivative and norm obligations are finite. The row $D$ fixes the orders and inspected windows, while $N_0,N_k,N_m$ are the only norm ledgers available to the comparison; no host derivative representative or quotient norm equality can be read outside them.

The interpolation step is routed through $A$ and $W$. These rows store the rational exponent data and the finite-window handoff already associated with $\GagliardoNirenbergFiniteWindowUp$, so the estimate reaches $R$ only as displayed regular rational data.

Finally, $E,H,C,P,Z$ seal and preserve the same route. Since the excluded host-function, choice, infinite-tower, measure-completion, quotient, and ambient-completeness objects are not carrier rows, the local NameCert surface cannot export them.
