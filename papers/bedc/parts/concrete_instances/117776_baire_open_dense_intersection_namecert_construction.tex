Following Baire and Bishop--Bridges, the $\BaireOpenDenseIntersectionUp$ packet records the complete-metric nested-ball construction for an open-dense intersection as finite BEDC NameCert data. It sits beside the theorem-level category route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-baire-category-theorem-namecert}, the schedule surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-open-dense-sequence-namecert}, the complete-metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the finite observation windows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only the displayed nested-ball route; it is not a host comeager set, a countable-choice selector, a quotient of Cauchy streams, or an ambient real-completeness principle.

\begin{definition}[Baire open-dense intersection carrier]
\label{def:baire-open-dense-intersection-carrier}
A $\BaireOpenDenseIntersectionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
I=(M,D,B,\rho,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp/\MetricUp$ source row, naming the complete metric service and the metric ball classifier used by the construction;
\item $D$ is the $\OpenDenseSequenceUp$ schedule row, listing the finite dense-open requests and predecessor-ball refinements visible to the packet;
\item $B$ is the nested-ball choice row, a displayed finite window of centers, radii, containment certificates, and dense-open admissions obtained from the rows $M$ and $D$;
\item $\rho$ is the radius-budget row, recording the positive rational bounds and shrinkage comparisons used to keep the nested balls Cauchy-facing;
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation row that names the finite prefix windows through which centers, radii, and dense-open requests are inspected;
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the Cauchy thread assembled from $B,\rho,S$;
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the regular rational readback has been displayed;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the complete-metric source, schedule, nested-ball choices, radius budget, stream observations, regular readback, real seal, continuation rows, provenance row, and local naming row;
\item $C$ records the displayed $\Cont$ replay routes by which a consumer rereads the complete-metric source, each dense-open request, each nested-ball refinement, the radius budget, the stream windows, the regular readback, and the real seal without changing the finite packet;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BaireOpenDenseIntersectionUp$, $\BaireCategoryTheoremUp$, $\OpenDenseSequenceUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\BaireOpenDenseIntersectionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a countable-choice selector, a host intersection object, a comeager-set predicate, quotient equality of names, an arbitrary completed point, open-cover compactness, or an ambient completeness theorem for $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Baire open-dense intersection NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:baire-open-dense-intersection-obligations}
For an accepted $\BaireOpenDenseIntersectionUp$ packet $I=(M,D,B,\rho,S,Q,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\BaireOpenDenseIntersectionUp}$ obligation surface is exhausted by five rows: source exposure through $M,D$, nested construction exposure through $B,\rho$, observation and readback exposure through $S,Q,E$, componentwise transport and replay through $H,C$, and provenance with local naming through $P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:baire-open-dense-intersection-carrier}. The complete-metric and dense-open source obligations are exactly the rows $M,D$.

The constructive route is the displayed nested-ball row $B$ together with the radius-budget row $\rho$. These rows record the finite predecessor/successor-ball data and the positive rational shrinkage comparisons needed by the open-dense intersection read.

The terminal observation surface is $S,Q,E$. The stream windows expose the inspected finite data, the regular rational row reads back the Cauchy thread, and the real seal is available only after those rows have been displayed.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no selector, host intersection, comeager predicate, quotient equality, selected completed point, open-cover compactness, or ambient real-completeness coordinate, the five listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Baire open-dense intersection nested-ball route]
\label{thm:baire-open-dense-intersection-nested-ball-route}
Every completion-facing consumer of an accepted $\BaireOpenDenseIntersectionUp$ packet must pass through the finite route
$$
\CompleteMetricUp,\quad
\OpenDenseSequenceUp,\quad
B,\rho,\quad
\StreamNameUp,\quad
\RegSeqRatUp,\quad

$$
The route is transported and replayed only by $H,C,P,N$. No accepted read may replace it by a countable-choice selector, host comeager set, quotient equality of Cauchy names, arbitrary completed point, open-cover compactness, or ambient real completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read the source rows first. The complete-metric and metric-ball classifier row $M$ supplies the ball service, while the schedule row $D$ supplies the finite open-dense requests.

The nested construction then proceeds through $B$ and $\rho$. Each visible refinement reads the next dense-open request, records the retained successor ball, and displays the positive rational radius comparison before any Cauchy-facing read is exported.

The consumer reaches the completion-facing surface only through $S,Q,E$. The finite stream row exposes the inspected window, the regular rational row reads the assembled Cauchy thread, and the real seal is the terminal value-facing row.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that same route under componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming. Therefore every completion-facing read passes through the complete-metric, dense-open, nested-ball, radius-budget, StreamName, RegSeqRat, and Real-seal rows, with no hidden selector or host topological object.
\end{proof}
