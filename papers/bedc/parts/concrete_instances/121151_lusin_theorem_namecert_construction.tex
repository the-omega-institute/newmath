\begin{theorem}[Lusin theorem NameCert obligations]\label{thm:lusin-theorem-namecert-obligations}

$$

$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LusinTheoremUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following obligation rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the $\MeasureUp$ source row $M$, the compact approximation row $K$, and the $\ContinuousMapUp$ representative row $C$;
\item compact approximation exposure through $K$, requiring the compact window and its complement budget to be read in the same measure ledger as $M$;
\item continuous representative exactness through $C$, requiring every public representative read to stay on the displayed compact window;
\item exceptional-set and error-ledger exposure through $E$ and the dyadic tolerance row $D$, requiring each theorem read to display its finite exceptional budget;

Finally $H,Q,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name only those displayed rows. Since the excluded host quotient, selected approximation, completed measure algebra, countable choice, and ambient real-completeness coordinates are absent, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
