\begin{definition}[RealUniformPartition carrier]
\label{def:real-uniform-partition-carrier}
A $\RealUniformPartitionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(A,B,M,I,E,S,R,Z,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ and $B$ are the lower and upper $\RealUp$ endpoint names, read only through their displayed real-seal rows.
\item $M$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ mesh-scale row, recording a positive dyadic step, its refinement budget, and the comparison cells used by the finite partition.
\item $I$ is the ordered finite window of partition indices, stored as constructor-generated finite data rather than as a host interval or hidden enumeration.
\item $E$ is the endpoint ledger assigning to each displayed index in $I$ the corresponding dyadic endpoint history between $A$ and $B$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-observation row used to inspect endpoint names, mesh rows, and the finite index window.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the endpoint and mesh observations displayed by $S$.
\item $Z$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after the dyadic mesh, ordered finite window, endpoint ledger, finite observations, and regular rational readback have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for endpoints, mesh scale, finite index window, endpoint ledger, StreamName observations, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local certificate.

\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RealUniformPartitionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier predicate requires the finite endpoint ledger $E$ to be ordered by the displayed dyadic mesh row $M$, to cover exactly the finite window $I$, and to be readable through $S,R,Z,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for an arbitrary open cover, a selected subcover, quotient real equality, host interval equality, countable choice, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RealUniformPartition NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:real-uniform-partition-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RealUniformPartitionUp$ carrier $U=(A,B,M,I,E,S,R,Z,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RealUniformPartitionUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the two $\RealUp$ endpoint names, the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ mesh-scale row, the ordered finite partition window, the endpoint ledger, $\StreamNameUp$ finite observations, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback, terminal $\RealUp$ sealing, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every partition consumer must expose $M,I,E,S,R$ before reading the Real seal $Z$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The rows before the Real seal are precisely the endpoint names $A,B$, the dyadic mesh $M$, the finite index window $I$, the endpoint ledger $E$, the finite observation row $S$, and the regular rational readback $R$.

The ordered partition obligation is finite. The mesh row supplies the dyadic step and comparison cells, while the finite window lists the partition indices and the endpoint ledger attaches each index to its displayed endpoint history.

Readback and sealing are downstream. The StreamName row records exactly the finite observations used by the RegSeqRat row, and the Real seal is then read from that regular rational boundary through $Z$.

Finally transport, replay, provenance, and local naming are supplied by $H,C,P,N$. Since the carrier contains no row for open-cover compactness, subcover choice, quotient real equality, host interval equality, countable choice, or completeness, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:real-uniform-partition-regseqrat-scope}

$$

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The RegSeqRat readback $R$ therefore cannot be read independently of the finite partition. Its observations are exactly those exposed by $S$, with the mesh comparisons supplied by $M$ and the endpoint rows supplied by $E$.

No excluded coordinate appears in the carrier inventory. Hence the scoped route cannot export an infinite stream, quotient real equality, selected limit, open-cover compactness, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}
