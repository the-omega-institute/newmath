\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\MinimizingMovementUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-minimizing-movement-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following De Giorgi, Ambrosio, Gigli, and Savare on minimizing movements, the $\MinimizingMovementUp$ packet records the finite descent surface by which an implicit time-step scheme is read before any gradient-flow or curve-of-maximal-slope consumer receives it. It sits after the variational descent ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ekeland-variational-principle-namecert}, the complete-metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, the metric source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the regular-rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the lower-semicontinuity packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lowersemicontinuous-namecert}. The packet names a displayed minimizing-step route only; it does not select minimizers, identify quotient metric points, invoke countable choice, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Minimizing-movement carrier]
\label{def:minimizing-movement-carrier}
A $\MinimizingMovementUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
M=(X,d,K,L,Q,R,E,S,T,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $X$ is the $\MetricSpaceUp$ source row for the inspected state addresses.
\item $d$ is the metric-comparison row naming the distances used by the step penalty.
\item $K$ is the $\CompleteMetricUp$ descent-readiness row for the finite Cauchy tails requested by the step scheme.
\item $L$ is the $\LowerSemicontinuousUp$ value-stability row for the energy inspected at each step.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for comparing displayed energy values and penalties.
\item $R$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for exported values after the regular-rational readback is visible.
\item $E$ is the $\EkelandVariationalPrincipleUp$ descent ledger used to certify the approximate minimizing step.
\item $S$ is the step-size and budget row, naming the finite time-step schedule, penalty coefficient, and admissible error budget.
\item $T$ is the finite step trace, recording the source state, trial state, descent comparison, and accepted next state for each inspected step.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for metric, complete-metric, lower-semicontinuity, readback, variational, schedule, and step-trace rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a minimizing-movement request through $X,d,K,L,Q,R,E,S,T$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\MinimizingMovementUp$, $\EkelandVariationalPrincipleUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\MetricSpaceUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\LowerSemicontinuousUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\MinimizingMovementUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier exports exactly these finite rows. It contains no selected minimizer, quotient equality of states, countable-choice scheduler, completed gradient flow, metric slope theorem, or independent proof that the ambient metric space is complete.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Minimizing-movement NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:minimizing-movement-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\MinimizingMovementUp$ carrier $M=(X,d,K,L,Q,R,E,S,T,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\MinimizingMovementUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed metric, distance, complete-metric, lower-semicontinuity, regular-rational, real-seal, variational, schedule, trace, transport, continuation, provenance, and naming rows;
\item metric-penalty readability, requiring every penalty comparison in $T$ to use the displayed $d$ row on addresses admitted by $X$;
\item complete-metric readiness, requiring every Cauchy-tail read requested by the step route to pass through $K$;
\item lower-semicontinuity and value readback, requiring every energy comparison to pass through $L,Q,R$ before any exported value is read;
\item variational-step exposure, requiring the approximate minimizing-step ledger to be read through $E$ before the accepted next-state row of $T$ is consumed;
\item schedule and budget coverage, requiring the time-step, penalty, and error data to be named in $S$ before the corresponding trace row is used;
\item transport, replay, provenance, and local naming stability through $H,C,P,N$;
\item ledger non-escape, excluding selected minimizers, quotient state equality, countable-choice schedules, completed gradient flows, metric-slope conclusions, and ambient real or metric completeness claims.
\end{enumerate}
No local NameCert obligation is stored outside these rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier row by row. The metric and distance rows $X,d$ expose exactly the inspected states and penalty comparisons, while $K,L,Q,R$ expose the complete-metric readiness, lower-semicontinuity reads, regular-rational comparisons, and real seals used by the step route.

The variational and schedule rows determine when the trace may be read. The row $E$ supplies the displayed Ekeland-style descent ledger for an approximate step, and $S$ supplies the finite time-step, penalty, and admissible error data before any coordinate of $T$ is accepted as a next-state record.

The trace row $T$ has no hidden coordinates beyond the source state, trial state, comparison, and accepted next-state data displayed in the carrier. Thus each minimizing-step read is exhausted by the metric, value, variational, and schedule rows already listed.

Finally, $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no coordinate stores a selected minimizer, quotient state equality, countable-choice schedule, completed gradient flow, metric slope theorem, or ambient completeness assertion, the listed obligations exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Minimizing-movement real-completion route]
\label{thm:minimizing-movement-real-completion-route}
Every Real-facing or Completion-facing consumer reading an accepted $\MinimizingMovementUp$ packet factors through the displayed route
$$
\MetricSpaceUp,\CompleteMetricUp,\LowerSemicontinuousUp,\RegSeqRatUp,\RealUp,\EkelandVariationalPrincipleUp
\longmapsto
S,T,H,C,P,N .
$$
The consumer therefore receives a finite implicit-step descent packet over displayed metric, value, and variational rows, not a selected minimizer, quotient completion object, countable-choice sequence, completed gradient flow, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted carrier $M=(X,d,K,L,Q,R,E,S,T,H,C,P,N)$. A consumer cannot read a distance penalty without first reading $X,d$, and cannot read a completion-facing tail request without first reading the complete-metric row $K$.

The value route is similarly forced. Energy comparisons pass through the lower-semicontinuity row $L$, the regular-rational readback $Q$, and the real seal $R$ before they can be exported to a Real-facing consumer.

The minimizing-step data then pass through $E,S,T$. The variational ledger $E$ supplies the displayed approximate descent route, $S$ names the finite time-step and budget data, and $T$ records only the inspected source, trial, comparison, and accepted next-state rows.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve and replay this same route. Because the carrier has no coordinate for selected minimizers, quotient completion, countable-choice schedules, completed flows, metric slopes, or independent completeness, no such datum can be obtained by a downstream Real or Completion consumer.
\end{proof}
