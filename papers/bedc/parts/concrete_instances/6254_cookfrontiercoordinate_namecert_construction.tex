The $\CookFrontierCoordinateUp$ packet turns the engineering path of \autoref{ch:visions-cook-compile-frontier-engineering-path} into a finite $\NameCert$ surface. It reads the cellular rule-table and finite orbit rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cellularautomaton-namecert}, the fuel-indexed readback discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bounded-universal-trace-namecert}, and the observation sieve of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-kernel-observation-sieve-namecert}; its own work is to bind a stage-frontier triple, Rule 110 scale row, audit replay row, and failure ledger into one coordinate packet. The carrier does not assert unbounded universality, does not decide that a halted target has been reached, and does not import a host evaluator oracle.

\falsifiablePrediction{If a claimed frontier coordinate can be replayed without the displayed stage-frontier, Rule 110 scale, audit-binary, or failure-ledger rows, then the carrier admission row is false.}

\independenceWitness{cellularautomaton, cellulartrustsubstrate, boundeduniversaltrace, kernelobservationsieve: these siblings expose cellular rows, trust routes, bounded traces, or observation sieves, but none is bijective to the coordinate packet because none carries the ordered stage-frontier triple with audit replay and failure-ledger rows.}

\begin{definition}[Cook frontier coordinate carrier]
\label{def:cook-frontier-coordinate-carrier}
A $\CookFrontierCoordinateUp$ carrier is a finite packet
$$
K=(F,R,S,A,L,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F=(F_{\mathrm{tag}},F_{\mathrm{compile}},F_{\mathrm{halt}})$ is the ordered stage-frontier triple naming the tag-system encoding frontier, the Rule 110 compilation frontier, and the halted-target frontier inspected by the packet;
\item $R$ is the finite Rule 110 scale row naming the cellular rule table, width budget, time budget, boundary convention, and row family used by the frontier read;
\item $S$ is the finite stage-sample ledger recording the tag-system word, compiled cellular window, and target readback rows displayed at the chosen frontier;
\item $A$ is the audit-binary row containing the replay executable name, input manifest, digest row, accepted output rows, and refusal rows for failed replay;
\item $L$ is the failure-ledger row recording absent encodings, scale overflows, replay mismatches, unread target rows, and refused universality reads;
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transports for $F,R,S,A,L,P,N$;
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ routes from the stage-frontier triple through the scale row, sample ledger, audit replay, and failure ledger;
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CookFrontierCoordinateUp$, \autoref{ch:visions-cook-compile-frontier-engineering-path}, $\CellularAutomatonUp$, $\BoundedUniversalTraceUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows;
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CookFrontierCoordinateUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The local classifier compares accepted coordinates only by these displayed rows. It has no coordinate for unbounded Rule 110 universality, a host-level proof that the target Turing machine halts, a hidden cellular evaluator, quotient stream equality, or a detached success claim outside the audit binary.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Cook frontier coordinate NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cook-frontier-coordinate-namecert-obligations}
For the carrier of \autoref{def:cook-frontier-coordinate-carrier}, the local $\NameCert_{\CookFrontierCoordinateUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for $K=(F,R,S,A,L,H,C,P,N)$, ordered stage-frontier accountability through $F$, Rule 110 scale accountability through $R$, finite sample exposure through $S$, audit replay exactness through $A$, failure-ledger non-escape through $L$, componentwise stability through $H$, displayed route replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:cook-frontier-coordinate-carrier}. The packet displays the stage-frontier triple, scale row, sample ledger, audit replay, failure ledger, transports, routes, provenance, and local naming row as its only coordinates.

Stage-frontier and scale accountability are read from $F$ and $R$. A consumer must name which encoding, compilation, and halted-target frontiers are being inspected, and must read the Rule 110 width, time, boundary, and row-family data from the finite scale row.

Sample exposure and audit replay are read from $S$ and $A$. The sample ledger records the tag-system, cellular, and target readback rows selected by the coordinate, while the audit-binary row supplies the replay manifest, digest, accepted outputs, and refused replay cases.

Finally use $L,H,C,P,N$. The failure ledger blocks success claims that bypass the replay rows, and the structural rows transport and package the same finite fields. Since the carrier has no unbounded universality, host halting, hidden evaluator, quotient stream, or detached success coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:cook-frontier-coordinate-failure-ledger-nonescape}

\begin{proof}

The sibling chapters do not supply a bypass. Therefore every rejected or incomplete read stays recorded as a failure-ledger row.

\begin{theorem}[Cook frontier coordinate scale-row faithfulness]
\label{thm:cook-frontier-coordinate-scale-row-faithfulness}
For every accepted $\CookFrontierCoordinateUp$ carrier
$$
K=(F,R,S,A,L,H,C,P,N),
$$
the scale row $R$ is the unique row from which the Rule 110 rule table, width budget, time budget, boundary convention, and row family of the frontier read may be projected. A consumer that changes any one of those five scale fields while preserving $F,S,A,L,H,C,P,N$ no longer reads the same $\NameCert_{\CookFrontierCoordinateUp}$ coordinate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cook-frontier-coordinate-carrier}. The row $R$ displays exactly the Rule 110 rule table, width budget, time budget, boundary convention, and row family used by the coordinate.

The stage-frontier triple $F$ names where the packet is read, but it does not store the cellular scale. The sample ledger $S$ records selected rows after the scale has already been fixed.

The audit row $A$ and failure ledger $L$ replay or refuse a finite read against the displayed scale; they do not define a second width, time, boundary, or row-family source. Transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same displayed field order.

Thus any alteration of the Rule 110 scale data changes the only row that can justify those projections. The accepted coordinate is therefore faithful to $R$ as the scale row.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cook frontier coordinate audit replay exactness]
\label{thm:cook-frontier-coordinate-audit-replay-exactness}
For every accepted $\CookFrontierCoordinateUp$ carrier $K=(F,R,S,A,L,H,C,P,N)$, the audit-binary row $A$ and failure-ledger row $L$ determine the replay boundary exactly: accepted output rows are those named by $A$, and absent encodings, scale overflows, replay mismatches, unread target rows, and refused universality reads remain ledger entries in $L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Read $A$ first. It carries the replay executable name, input manifest, digest row, accepted output rows, and refusal rows for failed replay.

Read $L$ next. The ledger names the failure classes attached to the same frontier and scale rows: absent encodings, scale overflows, replay mismatches, unread target rows, and refused universality reads.

The sample row $S$ supplies observed data, but it does not convert a ledger entry into an accepted output. Likewise $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, package, and name the same audit boundary without adding a hidden evaluator.

Therefore replay exactness is the joint reading of $A$ and $L$. A successful replay must be an accepted output row in $A$, and every failed or incomplete replay remains visible as a row of $L$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cook frontier coordinate field faithfulness]
\label{thm:cook-frontier-coordinate-field-faithfulness}
The fields $F,R,S,A,L,H,C,P,N$ of a $\CookFrontierCoordinateUp$ carrier cannot be merged while preserving the displayed $\NameCert_{\CookFrontierCoordinateUp}$ rows. Merging any two of the ordered stage-frontier triple, Rule 110 scale row, stage-sample ledger, audit-binary row, failure ledger, componentwise transport, displayed replay, package provenance, or local naming row loses at least one carrier admission, accountability, replay, non-escape, stability, provenance, or naming obligation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the roles of the nine fields. The row $F$ names the frontier stages, $R$ names the Rule 110 scale, and $S$ names the sampled stage data.

The rows $A$ and $L$ separate replay success from replay failure. If they are merged, the packet can no longer distinguish accepted output rows from refused or incomplete reads.

The structural rows $H$ and $C$ have different duties: one transports componentwise equality data and the other records displayed route replay. The package row $P$ and local naming row $N$ then record provenance and certificate naming rather than frontier, scale, sample, audit, or failure data.

Each field therefore discharges a distinct NameCert obligation listed in \autoref{thm:cook-frontier-coordinate-namecert-obligations}. A merge removes the witness for at least one listed obligation, so field faithfulness is forced by the displayed carrier itself.
\end{proof}
