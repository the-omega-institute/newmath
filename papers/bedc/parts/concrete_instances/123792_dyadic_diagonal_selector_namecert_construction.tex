\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\DyadicDiagonalSelectorUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-dyadic-diagonal-selector-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Bishop and Bridges constructive analysis, the $\DyadicDiagonalSelectorUp$ packet records the finite diagonal-window selector used when a displayed family of rational observations is handed to Real and Completion consumers. It sits over the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the finite stream-name window of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}, and the Bishop regular Cauchy completion route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-bishop-regular-cauchy-completion-namecert}. The packet names only a displayed finite selector surface; it does not choose an infinite subsequence, quotient streams, import countable choice, or assert ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[Dyadic diagonal selector carrier]
\label{def:dyadic-diagonal-selector-carrier}
A $\DyadicDiagonalSelectorUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(W,D,R,T,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is the finite $\StreamNameUp$ observation window in which the diagonal request is inspected.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row recording the requested radius, retained slack, and comparison budget for the selected window.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the rational observations exposed by $W$ and checked against $D$.
\item $T$ is the selected dyadic diagonal window, naming the finite indices and observations that survive the selector request.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ or Bishop-regular-completion handoff row consumed only after $W,D,R,T$ have been displayed.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $W,D,R,T,E$ and the structural rows.
\item $C$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from the stream observation, dyadic budget, regular rational readback, selector window, and completion-facing handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\DyadicDiagonalSelectorUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BishopRegularCauchyCompletionUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicDiagonalSelectorUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an infinite choice sequence, a selected tail outside the displayed window, quotient equality of stream names, host real equality, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic diagonal selector NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:dyadic-diagonal-selector-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\DyadicDiagonalSelectorUp$ carrier
$$
S=(W,D,R,T,E,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\DyadicDiagonalSelectorUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite observation exposure through $W$, dyadic tolerance exactness through $D$, regular rational readback through $R$, selected diagonal-window admission through $T$, terminal Real or Bishop-completion handoff through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier to the tuple of \autoref{def:dyadic-diagonal-selector-carrier}. The observation row $W$, tolerance row $D$, readback row $R$, selected window $T$, and terminal handoff row $E$ are the only analytic rows available to a consumer.

The selector is finite because $T$ is read after $W,D,R$ have already been displayed. It records the selected diagonal window and cannot create an unbounded choice sequence or quotient stream representative.

The handoff row $E$ is downstream of that finite selector. It may feed a $\RealUp$ or $\BishopRegularCauchyCompletionUp$ consumer, but only through the displayed stream, dyadic, rational, and selected-window rows.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ preserve the same packet under transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. Since the excluded endpoints do not occur as carrier coordinates, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dyadic diagonal selector regular-tail handoff]
\label{thm:dyadic-diagonal-selector-regular-tail-handoff}
Every Real or Completion-facing use of an accepted $\DyadicDiagonalSelectorUp$ carrier hands the selected window to the regular-tail route through
$$
\StreamNameUp\longrightarrow \DyadicRatCoreUp\longrightarrow \RegSeqRatUp\longrightarrow \RealUp .
$$
The completion consumer may inspect the selected diagonal window $T$ only after the finite observation row $W$, dyadic tolerance row $D$, and regular rational readback row $R$ have been displayed; the handoff exports no countable choice principle, no quotient stream equality, and no selected completed real outside the terminal row $E$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the accepted packet $S=(W,D,R,T,E,H,C,P,N)$. The only stream-facing data are the finite observations in $W$, and the only tolerance data are the dyadic budget rows in $D$.

Read the regular rational row next. The row $R$ exposes the rational observations checked by $D$, so the selector row $T$ is a finite diagonal window over already visible data.

The terminal handoff $E$ is therefore downstream of $W,D,R,T$. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming keep that dependency order fixed. Because the carrier has no coordinate for countable choice, quotient stream equality, or a selected completed real outside $E$, the regular-tail handoff remains finite.
\end{proof}
