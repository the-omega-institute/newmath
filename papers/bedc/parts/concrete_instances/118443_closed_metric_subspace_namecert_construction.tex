\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\ClosedMetricSubspaceUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-closed-metric-subspace-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Bishop--Bridges metric-space practice, the $\ClosedMetricSubspaceUp$ packet records the finite closed-subspace route by which a displayed subspace admission row inherits convergence from a parent metric carrier. It refines the subspace surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-subspace-namecert}, reads the parent metric packet of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, consumes the complete-metric limit boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-completemetric-namecert}, and keeps its convergence data visible through \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The resulting carrier is a closed-subspace certificate, not a host-subset quotient, selected-representative principle, arbitrary completeness theorem, or compactness assertion.

\begin{definition}[ClosedMetricSubspace carrier]
\label{def:closed-metric-subspace-carrier}
A $\ClosedMetricSubspaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
C=(M,A,D,L,S,R,E,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the parent $\MetricUp$ row, including its point classifier, distance witness, metric-law ledger, and finite observation histories.
\item $A$ is the displayed subspace admission row. A point is readable by the closed-subspace packet only after the parent point classifier of $M$ and the admission row $A$ both accept it.
\item $D$ is the inherited-distance row. It exports no independent distance; each closed-subspace distance read is the parent distance row of $M$ applied to two admitted points.
\item $L$ is the closed-under-limit witness. It states that a parent limit witness for an admitted Cauchy stream is admitted again by $A$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ Cauchy witness row naming the finite observation windows used by the convergence consumer.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row recording the regular rational comparison data attached to the stream.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal read only after the stream, regular-rational, inherited-distance, and closed-limit rows have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the parent metric row, admission row, inherited distance, closed-limit witness, stream witness, regular-rational readback, Real seal, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row.
\item $K$ records the displayed $\Cont$ route replaying point admission, subspace distance restriction, Cauchy-window admission, parent limit handoff, closedness admission, and Real-seal handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClosedMetricSubspaceUp$, $\MetricSubspaceUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\CompleteMetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedMetricSubspaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through the displayed rows $M,A,D,L,S,R,E,H,K,P,N$ and their replay paths. It has no coordinate for host subset equality, quotient membership, selected representatives, arbitrary completeness, open-cover compactness, countable choice, or ambient Real completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[ClosedMetricSubspace NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:closed-metric-subspace-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClosedMetricSubspaceUp$ carrier $C=(M,A,D,L,S,R,E,H,K,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ClosedMetricSubspaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, inherited metric restriction, closure under parent limit witnesses, convergence handoff through StreamName windows and RegSeqRat readback, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.

Equivalently, every completion-facing or Real-facing consumer must read
$$
M,\quad A,\quad D,\quad L,\quad S,\quad R,\quad E,\quad H,\quad K,\quad P,\quad N
$$
before it can use a closed-subspace conclusion, and no consumer can import host subsets, quotient equality, selected representatives, arbitrary completeness, open-cover compactness, or bridge data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:closed-metric-subspace-carrier}. The tuple lists the parent metric, displayed admission, inherited distance, closed-limit witness, StreamName Cauchy witness, RegSeqRat readback, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

The inherited metric restriction is forced by $D$ and $K$. A distance read first admits both endpoints through $M$ and $A$, then reads the parent distance row of $M$; the carrier contains no second metric coordinate.

Closure under parent limits is the role of $L$. A convergence consumer opens the finite StreamName row $S$, reads the RegSeqRat comparisons in $R$, uses the parent metric and complete-metric boundary named by $M$, and then applies $L$ to admit the parent limit back into $A$.

Finally $E,H,K,P,N$ seal, transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier has no row outside $M,A,D,L,S,R,E,H,K,P,N$, host subset equality, quotient equality, selected representatives, arbitrary completeness, open-cover compactness, and bridge checking cannot be exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
