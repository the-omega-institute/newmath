Following Heine--Cantor and Bishop's compactness proof, the $\UniformModulusLedgerUp$ packet records the finite ledger that turns compact metric coverage and pointwise continuity rows into a tolerance-only uniform-modulus handoff. It realizes the ledger named in \autoref{ch:visions-compact-uniform-continuity} as concrete NameCert data: a $\CompactMetricUp$ finite-net source, a $\ContinuousMapUp$ local-radius row, positive $\RatUp$ radius comparisons, a $\ProbeBundleUp$ compact-cover spine, metric-triangle transport, and a $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer row remain visible in one finite carrier.

\begin{definition}[UniformModulusLedger carrier]
\label{def:uniform-modulus-ledger-carrier}
A $\UniformModulusLedgerUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(K,F,R,B,T,U,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, including finite-net coverage data over the metric carrier.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ graph and pointwise local-radius row inspected on the compact source.
\item $R$ is the positive $\RatUp$ tolerance and local-radius comparison ledger.
\item $B$ is the $\ProbeBundleUp$ row naming the finite compact-net centers and their subordinate local-radius witnesses.
\item $T$ is the metric-triangle transport row that moves estimates from a bundled center to nearby source points.
\item $U$ is the $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff row from tolerance histories to precision histories.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for compact-net, continuous-map, rational-radius, bundle, triangle, and uniform-modulus rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from compact coverage through local-radius inspection, rational lower-bound comparison, triangle transport, and uniform-modulus handoff.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniformModulusLedgerUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RatUp$, $\ProbeBundleUp$, $\UniformModulusUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniformModulusLedgerUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an open-cover compactness theorem, a host Heine--Cantor theorem, a choice-selected center, a global modulus selector, quotient equality of maps, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[UniformModulusLedger NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:uniform-modulus-ledger-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniformModulusLedgerUp$ carrier $L=(K,F,R,B,T,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\UniformModulusLedgerUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier exposure, compact finite-net admission through $K,B$, continuous-map local-radius admission through $F$, positive rational comparison through $R$, metric-triangle transport through $T$, tolerance-only uniform-modulus handoff through $U$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The compact source and finite-cover spine are displayed as $K$ and $B$, while the pointwise continuity information is displayed as $F$ before any uniform handoff is available.

The rational comparison row $R$ records the local radii and the lower-bound comparison needed by the finite compact-net fold. The triangle row $T$ is then the only route that transports estimates from bundled centers to arbitrary source points.

The handoff row $U$ receives the resulting precision history as a tolerance-only $\UniformModulusUp$ row. Since $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, package, and name the same displayed route, the carrier cannot export host compactness, global choice, quotient map equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[UniformModulusLedger compact-cover handoff]
\label{thm:uniform-modulus-ledger-compact-cover-handoff}
Every $\UniformModulusUp$ row exported by an accepted $\UniformModulusLedgerUp$ carrier is read through $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\RatUp$, $\ProbeBundleUp$, and the metric-triangle row before the handoff reaches $U$. The exported ledger is finite compact-net, local-radius, metric-triangle, and NameCert data, not a host selector for a global modulus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compact-cover data $K,B$. These rows list the finite centers and subordinate local-radius witnesses available to the ledger.

Read the continuous-map row $F$ at each bundled center and compare the resulting positive rational radii through $R$. The metric-triangle row $T$ transports the local estimates to the source pair under inspection.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ replay and name this route before $U$ is exposed. Thus the public $\UniformModulusUp$ handoff is the displayed finite ledger, with no external center choice, open-cover compactness theorem, or host Heine--Cantor step.
\end{proof}
