\begin{definition}[Cauchy ideal carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-ideal-carrier}
A $\CauchyIdealUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterUp$ source row whose neighborhoods supply the admitted Cauchy observations.
\item $R$ is the rounded directed ideal row, listing finitely many observation rows closed under displayed coarsening and carrying a finite common-refinement witness for any two listed rows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, recording the dyadic radii and comparison budgets used by the displayed observations.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation-window row through which the ideal is inspected.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row assembled from the same stream windows and dyadic tolerance data.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal, exposed only after $F,R,D,S,Q$ have been displayed.
\item $O$ is the local rounded-obligation ledger, recording inhabitedness, upward closure under displayed weakening, directed refinement, and Cauchy compatibility inside the finite packet.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $F,R,D,S,Q,E,O,H,T,P,N$. It has no coordinate for a quotient of filters, equality of completed reals, selected representatives, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[CauchyIdeal NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-ideal-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyIdealUp$ carrier $I=(F,R,D,S,Q,E,O,H,T,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyIdealUp}$ surface consists exactly of CauchyFilter source exposure through $F$, rounded directed ideal exposure through $R$, DyadicRatCore tolerance accounting through $D$, StreamName window exposure through $S$, RegSeqRat readback through $Q$, terminal Real sealing through $E$, rounded-obligation coverage through $O$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $T$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$. A Real or Completion consumer may read the ideal only after the route
$$
F,R,D \longmapsto S,Q \longmapsto E,O,H,T,P,N
$$
has been displayed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:cauchy-ideal-carrier}. Its analytic rows are $F,R,D,S,Q,E,O$, and its structural rows are $H,T,P,N$.

The rounded ideal row $R$ is read together with the CauchyFilter source $F$ and dyadic tolerance row $D$. Thus inhabitedness, displayed weakening, directed refinement, and Cauchy compatibility are obligations of the same finite packet rather than properties imported from a quotient completion.

The readback rows remain downstream. The finite StreamName window $S$ and RegSeqRat row $Q$ expose only the listed observations, while the Real seal $E$ is available only after those observations and tolerances have been replayed.

Finally $O,H,T,P,N$ cover the local obligations, transport them componentwise, replay them, source them, and name them. Since no hidden coordinate stores quotient equality, representative choice, countable choice, or ambient completeness, the displayed rows exhaust the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The rounded-obligation ledger $O$ records exactly the finite ideal laws visible to the consumer: inhabitedness, weakening, directed refinement, and Cauchy compatibility. These laws are replayed through $T$ and transported through $H$ without adding a completed object.
