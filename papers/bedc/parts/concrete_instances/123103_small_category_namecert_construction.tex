\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\SmallCategoryUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-small-category-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Mac Lane's small-category convention, the $\SmallCategoryUp$ packet records the size-controlled category surface in which the object and morphism rows are displayed BEDC data before categorical composition is read. It sits beside the category carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-category-namecert}, the functor carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-functor-namecert}, the membership carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-setlike-namecert}, and the displayed-family carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-typelike-namecert}. The packet names one finite object ledger, one finite hom-ledger family, identities, composition, law transport, continuation replay, provenance, and local naming; it does not import a host universe of all objects, quotient equality of hom-sets, selected representatives, or an ambient large-category principle.

\begin{definition}[SmallCategory carrier]
\label{def:small-category-carrier}
A $\SmallCategoryUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
S=(O,M,I,C,L,A,F,T,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $O$ is the $\SetLikeUp$ object ledger, naming the accepted object rows and their membership classifier.
\item $M$ is the displayed hom-ledger family, read as $\TypeLikeUp$ fibers over ordered pairs from $O$.
\item $I$ is the identity row, assigning to each displayed object an admitted morphism row in the corresponding fiber.
\item $C$ is the composition row, assigning to each displayed composable pair a displayed composite morphism row.
\item $L$ is the categorical law ledger, recording associativity and left- and right-identity laws as carried $\hsame$ rows.
\item $A$ is the parent $\CategoryUp$ admission row, exposing the same object, hom, identity, composition, and law rows to the category interface.
\item $F$ is the $\FunctorUp$ handoff row, recording which object and morphism rows a functor-facing consumer may read.
\item $T$ is the size ledger, requiring every object row and every hom-fiber row to be read through the displayed $\SetLikeUp$ and $\TypeLikeUp$ surfaces before $A$ or $F$ is consumed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for object, hom, identity, composition, law, category-admission, functor-handoff, size, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a small-category request through $O,M,I,C,L,A,F,T,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SmallCategoryUp$, $\CategoryUp$, $\FunctorUp$, $\SetLikeUp$, $\TypeLikeUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SmallCategoryUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host universe of objects, unrestricted hom-set collection, quotient equality of morphisms, selected representatives, large-category closure, or an external category of all BEDC packets.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SmallCategory NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:small-category-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SmallCategoryUp$ carrier
$$
S=(O,M,I,C,L,A,F,T,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\SmallCategoryUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed object ledger $O$, hom-ledger family $M$, identity row $I$, composition row $C$, categorical law ledger $L$, parent $\CategoryUp$ admission $A$, $\FunctorUp$ handoff $F$, size ledger $T$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay $K$, $\Pkg$ provenance $P$, and local naming row $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:small-category-carrier}. The object side is the SetLike row $O$, and the morphism side is the TypeLike hom-ledger family $M$ over displayed ordered object pairs.

The categorical operations are finite displayed rows. The identity row $I$ and composition row $C$ are read before the law ledger $L$, and $L$ records the three category laws through carried $\hsame$ evidence rather than host equality of morphisms.

The parent and consumer rows are downstream. The Category admission $A$ and Functor handoff $F$ may be consumed only after the size ledger $T$ has exposed the SetLike and TypeLike routes. Componentwise transport $H$, continuation replay $K$, provenance $P$, and local naming $N$ preserve the same route.

Since no additional coordinate exists, the local NameCert obligations cannot ask for a host object universe, unrestricted hom-set collection, quotient equality, selected representatives, large-category closure, or an external category of all BEDC packets.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[SmallCategory Category handoff]
\label{thm:small-category-category-handoff}
Every $\CategoryUp$ or $\FunctorUp$ consumer of an accepted $\SmallCategoryUp$ packet must first expose the displayed route
$$
\SetLikeUp,\ \TypeLikeUp,\ O,M,I,C,L,T \longmapsto A,F,H,K,P,N .
$$
Thus the parent category interface receives object and morphism carriers already bounded by $\SetLikeUp$ and $\TypeLikeUp$, while the functor interface receives only the displayed object and morphism rows selected by $F$. The handoff cannot be reread as a large category, a host universe, an unrestricted hom-set family, quotient morphism equality, or a selected representative principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:small-category-namecert-obligations}. They force every categorical read to expose the object ledger $O$, hom-ledger family $M$, identity row $I$, composition row $C$, law ledger $L$, and size ledger $T$ before the parent or functor rows are available.

The sibling chapters supply the bounded carrier surfaces. $\SetLikeUp$ supplies the displayed object-membership ledger, $\TypeLikeUp$ supplies the displayed hom-fiber family, $\CategoryUp$ supplies the parent category interface, and $\FunctorUp$ supplies the consumer-facing object and morphism mapping route.

Finally read $A,F,H,K,P,N$. Category admission and functor handoff are preserved by componentwise $\hsame$ transport and replayed by $\Cont$, while provenance and local naming keep the route sourced inside the finite packet. The refused large-category and host-universe principles are absent from the carrier, so no consumer can recover them from the handoff.
\end{proof}
