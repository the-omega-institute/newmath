Following Hahn--Mazurkiewicz and Kelley on compact metric continua, the $\LocallyConnectedContinuumUp$ packet records the compact, connected, locally connected metric layer that Peano-continuum and dendrite consumers read before importing their richer endpoint or image data. It sits over the compact metric carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compactmetric-namecert}, the finite chain-connected route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-chain-connected-metric-namecert}, the continuous-map surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuous-namecert}, and the downstream continuum consumers of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-peano-continuum-namecert} and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dendrite-namecert}. The packet names the local connectedness refinement boundary without asserting a space-filling curve, unique-arc data, open-cover compactness, quotient equality of points, or ambient real completeness.

\begin{definition}[Locally connected continuum carrier]
\label{def:locally-connected-continuum-carrier}
A $\LocallyConnectedContinuumUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
L=(K,C,R,W,M,H,T,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CompactMetricUp$ source row, exposing the metric carrier, finite compact-window rows, total-boundedness ledger, and completion-facing comparisons used by continuum consumers.
\item $C$ is the connectedness source row. It either records a displayed connectedness witness over $K$ or a finite $\ChainConnectedMetricUp$ route whose chain ledgers are read as connectedness-facing data for the same compact metric source.
\item $R$ is the local connectedness refinement ledger. For each displayed compact window it records connected neighbourhood rows, inclusion refinements, overlap witnesses, radius comparisons, and the refusal of host open-set lattices outside the packet.
\item $W$ is the compact-window row, recording the finite family of windows through which later interval, image, and dendrite reads may inspect the continuum.
\item $M$ is the continuous-map consumer route, exposing only the $\ContinuousMapUp$ graph, modulus, and window-read rows allowed to consume $K,C,R,W$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the compact metric source, connectedness row, local connectedness refinement ledger, compact windows, continuous-map route, replay row, provenance row, and local naming row.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the route
$$
K,C,R,W \longmapsto M \longmapsto H,P,N .
$$
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LocallyConnectedContinuumUp$, $\CompactMetricUp$, $\ChainConnectedMetricUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LocallyConnectedContinuumUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,C,R,W,M,H,T,P,N$. The carrier has no coordinate for an ambient point-set continuum, a selected Peano parametrization, dendrite arc uniqueness, open-cover compactness, quotient point equality, countable choice, or completed real subset selection.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Locally connected continuum NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:locally-connected-continuum-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\LocallyConnectedContinuumUp$ carrier
$$
L=(K,C,R,W,M,H,T,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\LocallyConnectedContinuumUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact-metric source exposure through $K$, connected or chain-connected source exposure through $C$, local connectedness refinement through $R$, compact-window visibility through $W$, continuous-map consumer routing through $M$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $T$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The displayed tuple lists every row that may be consumed by a continuum-facing reader, so carrier admission and classifier exposure are confined to $K,C,R,W,M,H,T,P,N$.

The compact and connected source data are read first. The row $K$ supplies the compact metric source, and $C$ supplies either displayed connectedness evidence or the finite $\ChainConnectedMetricUp$ route over that same source. No second host space is introduced.

The local connectedness data are confined to $R$ and $W$. Each compact-window request is answered by finite connected-neighbourhood, inclusion, overlap, and radius-comparison rows, while $W$ records the windows through which downstream image and continuum readers may inspect the packet.

Finally $M,H,T,P,N$ route, transport, replay, source, and name exactly the displayed rows. Since the carrier has no coordinate for a host continuum, selected parametrization, dendrite arc, open-cover principle, quotient point equality, countable choice, or completed real subset, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Locally connected continuum Peano handoff]
\label{thm:locally-connected-continuum-peano-handoff}
Every $\PeanoContinuumUp$ or $\DendriteUp$ consumer that reads a compact metric continuum through $\LocallyConnectedContinuumUp$ must first pass through the finite route
$$
\CompactMetricUp,\ \ChainConnectedMetricUp,\ R,\ W,\ \ContinuousMapUp
\longmapsto

$$
Thus the consumer receives the compact metric source, connectedness route, local connectedness refinement ledger, compact windows, continuous-map route, componentwise transport, displayed replay, provenance, and local naming before reading Peano-continuum or dendrite-specific rows. It receives no host continuum, selected Peano parametrization, unique-arc dendrite structure, open-cover compactness, quotient point equality, countable-choice selector, or ambient real-completeness witness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the compact source row $K$. A Peano-continuum or dendrite consumer cannot read local connectedness, image, endpoint, or arc data before the metric carrier, compact windows, and finite compactness-facing rows have been displayed.

Next read connectedness through $C$. If the source is supplied by a chain-connected route, the consumer sees the finite endpoint, chain, radius, and tolerance ledgers of $\ChainConnectedMetricUp$ over the same compact metric row.

Now read the local connectedness and window rows $R,W$. These rows provide finite connected-neighbourhood refinements and compact-window boundaries before any $\ContinuousMapUp$ graph or modulus row in $M$ is made public.

The structural rows close the handoff. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and the local $\NameCert$ row preserve exactly this finite dependency order, so Peano-continuum and dendrite consumers receive the locally connected continuum layer without receiving any refused host-side object.
\end{proof}
