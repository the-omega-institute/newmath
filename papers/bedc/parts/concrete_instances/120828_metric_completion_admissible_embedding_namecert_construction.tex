\begin{definition}[Metric completion admissible embedding carrier]
\label{def:metric-completion-admissible-embedding-carrier}
A $\MetricCompletionAdmissibleEmbeddingUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(M,E,F,S,U,W,R,D,Z,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\MetricCompletionUp$ row, recording the completion-facing carrier, Cauchy admission surface, and completion readback boundary.
\item $E$ is the $\CompletionEmbeddingUp$ row, recording the source-to-completion graph before any completed point equality is read.
\item $F$ is the $\CauchyFilterBasisUp$ row, exposing the finite filter-basis requests whose images are tested for density.
\item $S$ is the $\SeparatedMetricUp$ row, exposing the zero-distance comparison that makes the embedding admissible into a separated target.

\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window row for the finite observations consumed by completion and extension readers.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for rational distance and modulus data.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance row, recording the dyadic radii and comparison cells used by density and separatedness checks.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $M,E,F,S,U,W,R,D,Z,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient completion classes, selected limits, host equality of completed points, countable choice, trichotomy, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{theorem}[Metric completion admissible embedding NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:metric-completion-admissible-embedding-namecert-obligations}

$$
A=(M,E,F,S,U,W,R,D,Z,H,C,P,N),

the local $\NameCert_{\MetricCompletionAdmissibleEmbeddingUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the metric-completion row, completion-embedding graph, Cauchy-filter basis, separated-metric comparison, uniform-continuous extension handoff, StreamName window, RegSeqRat readback, DyadicRatCore tolerance ledger, Real seal, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every extension-facing or completion-facing consumer must display $M,E,F,S,U,W,R,D,Z,H,C,P,N$ before reading the embedding as admissible.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic rows are $M,E,F,S,U,W,R,D,Z$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The embedding is read through the completion and filter-basis rows first. The rows $M,E,F$ expose a completion-facing object, its graph from the source, and the finite basis requests that witness density for the read under inspection.

The separated and extension rows are downstream. The row $S$ supplies the zero-distance comparison for the separated target, and $U$ can be consumed only as a named handoff after the embedding and density rows have been displayed.

The numerical readback rows $W,R,D,Z$ keep every distance, tolerance, and extension value inside the finite StreamName, RegSeqRat, DyadicRatCore, and Real seal route. Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same displayed packet, so no quotient completion, selected limit, host equality, countable-choice row, trichotomy branch, or ambient Real completeness coordinate can be projected from the NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
Start with the NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:metric-completion-admissible-embedding-namecert-obligations}. For a completion-universal consumer, the same rows give the completion and density route without quotienting or selecting a completed representative. The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ preserve that finite route, and the excluded global principles are absent from the carrier.
