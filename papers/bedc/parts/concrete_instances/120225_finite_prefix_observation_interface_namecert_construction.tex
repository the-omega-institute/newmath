\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\FinitePrefixObservationInterfaceUp$}

Following Bishop and Bridges on finite approximations to real data, the $\FinitePrefixObservationInterfaceUp$ packet records the finite read surface by which a $\StreamNameUp$ schedule is observed before a regular rational or real-facing consumer uses it. It sits after the countable-name gate of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet names only finite prefix indices, finite observation rows, handoff rows, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming; it is not a countable choice principle and not a host equality test for streams or completed reals.

\begin{definition}[Finite-prefix observation interface carrier]
\label{def:finite-prefix-observation-interface-carrier}
A $\FinitePrefixObservationInterfaceUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
O=(I,F,S,R,D,H,K,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I$ is the finite prefix index row naming the inspected initial segment of a $\StreamNameUp$ schedule.
\item $F$ is the finite observation row storing exactly the reads made at the indices named by $I$.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ handoff row whose public requests are restricted to the displayed prefix.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from the same finite stream observations.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger recording the precision, radius, and comparison budget attached to the prefix read.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the prefix index, observation row, stream handoff, regular readback, dyadic ledger, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $K$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the finite prefix index through $F,S,R,D$ to the consumer-facing boundary.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\FinitePrefixObservationInterfaceUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\FinitePrefixObservationInterfaceUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a countable-choice schedule, infinite stream equality, selected limit, host real equality, trichotomy, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-prefix observation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:finite-prefix-observation-interface-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\FinitePrefixObservationInterfaceUp$ carrier
$$
O=(I,F,S,R,D,H,K,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\FinitePrefixObservationInterfaceUp}$ surface consists exactly of finite prefix admission through $I$, finite observation exposure through $F$, $\StreamNameUp$ handoff through $S$, $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback through $R$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance accounting through $D$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $K$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:finite-prefix-observation-interface-carrier}. The prefix index $I$ determines the only inspected positions, and the observation row $F$ stores only the finite reads at those positions.

The stream handoff $S$ is consumed through that prefix before the regular rational readback $R$ is available. The dyadic ledger $D$ records the tolerance data attached to the same finite observation row, so a consumer cannot replace the readback by a selected infinite stream, host real value, or unbounded choice schedule.

The structural rows $H,K,P,N$ keep the route local. Componentwise $\hsame$ transport preserves the displayed prefix rows, $\Cont$ replay reuses the same finite route, $\Pkg$ provenance records the sibling packets, and the local $\NameCert$ names no data beyond the carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-prefix observation handoff boundary]
\label{thm:finite-prefix-observation-interface-handoff-boundary}
Every downstream read of an accepted $\FinitePrefixObservationInterfaceUp$ packet factors through the finite route
$$
I \longmapsto F \longmapsto S \longmapsto R \longmapsto D
$$
with structural transport, replay, provenance, and local naming through $H,K,P,N$. The read may be handed to $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, or $\RealUp$ consumers only as this displayed finite prefix boundary, not as countable choice, infinite stream equality, selected limit, host real equality, or completeness data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with an accepted packet $O=(I,F,S,R,D,H,K,P,N)$. The carrier definition gives a single route from the prefix index to the observation row and then to the stream, regular rational, and dyadic tolerance rows.

The NameCert obligations of \autoref{thm:finite-prefix-observation-interface-namecert-obligations} account for every accepted public read. Since those obligations list only $I,F,S,R,D,H,K,P,N$, a consumer has no separate coordinate from which to extract an infinite schedule, a host equality decision, or a completion principle.

Finally use the structural rows. Transport by $H$ and replay by $K$ preserve the same finite prefix route, while $P$ and $N$ record provenance and local naming without adding analytic content.
\end{proof}
