\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\UniqueFactorizationDomainUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-unique-factorization-domain-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Gauss's factorization lemma and the Atiyah--Macdonald commutative-algebra tradition, the $\UniqueFactorizationDomainUp$ packet records unique factorization as finite BEDC NameCert data over a displayed integral-domain source. It sits after the algebraic operation rows of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ring-namecert}, the ideal-facing surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-ideal-namecert}, and the arithmetic prime row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-prime-namecert}. The principal-ideal-domain route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-principal-ideal-domain-namecert} and the Euclidean-domain route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-euclidean-domain-namecert} are sufficient producer rows, but this packet names only the finite irreducible-factor ledger, associate comparison, prime-divisor check, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming. It does not import quotient ideals, selected representatives, a field of fractions, countable choice, Noetherian induction, or a host algebra theorem.

\begin{definition}[Unique factorization domain carrier]
\label{def:unique-factorization-domain-carrier}
A $\UniqueFactorizationDomainUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
U=(R,I,P,F,A,D,G,E,H,C,Q,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the consumed $\RingUp$ source row, including zero, one, addition, additive inverse, multiplication, distributivity, and the local equality classifier for the algebra carrier.
\item $I$ is the $\IdealUp$ dependency row used by ideal-facing consumers and by the principal-ideal producer route.
\item $P$ is the $\PrimeUp$ row that supplies prime-divisor and prime-factor readback inside the displayed carrier.
\item $F$ is the finite factorization ledger for a nonzero nonunit row, listing irreducible factor rows and the multiplication replay that returns the source row.
\item $A$ is the associate-comparison ledger for two displayed finite factorizations of the same source row.
\item $D$ is the prime-divisor handoff row requiring each irreducible factor comparison to pass through the displayed $\PrimeUp$ route.
\item $G$ is the optional $\PrincipalIdealDomainUp$ producer row.
\item $E$ is the optional $\EuclideanDomainUp$ producer row.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $R,I,P,F,A,D,G,E,C,Q,N$.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from factorization requests through finite multiplication, prime-divisor checks, associate comparison, producer rows, and downstream algebra consumers.
\item $Q$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\UniqueFactorizationDomainUp$, $\RingUp$, $\IdealUp$, $\PrimeUp$, $\PrincipalIdealDomainUp$, $\EuclideanDomainUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\UniqueFactorizationDomainUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier compares accepted packets only through these rows. It has no coordinate for quotient ideals, chosen factorization representatives outside $F$, countable choice, Noetherian induction, a field-of-fractions construction, bridge checking, or a host unique-factorization theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Unique factorization domain NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:unique-factorization-domain-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\UniqueFactorizationDomainUp$ carrier
$$
U=(R,I,P,F,A,D,G,E,H,C,Q,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\UniqueFactorizationDomainUp}$ surface consists exactly of ring-source admission, ideal dependency exposure, prime-row exposure, finite irreducible-factor ledger exposure, associate-comparison exposure, prime-divisor handoff, optional principal-ideal-domain producer evidence, optional Euclidean-domain producer evidence, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every factorization-facing consumer must read these rows before using the packet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:unique-factorization-domain-carrier}. The visible algebra source is $R$, ideal-facing dependency is $I$, prime readback is $P$, and the factorization data are the finite ledgers $F,A,D$.

The finite factorization ledger $F$ supplies existence only as displayed multiplication rows for a nonzero nonunit source. The associate ledger $A$ supplies uniqueness only by comparing two displayed ledgers through the same prime-divisor handoff $D$ and the same prime row $P$.

The producer rows $G$ and $E$ are dependencies, not ambient services. A principal-ideal-domain packet or a Euclidean-domain packet may supply the UFD surface, but the consumer still reads the displayed factorization, associate, and prime-divisor ledgers inside $U$.

Finally $H,C,Q,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since none of the carrier rows names quotient ideals, selected representatives outside $F$, countable choice, Noetherian induction, a field of fractions, bridge checking, or a host theorem, none is exported by the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Unique factorization domain PID and Euclidean route]
\label{thm:unique-factorization-domain-pid-euclidean-route}
Every accepted $\UniqueFactorizationDomainUp$ packet supplied by a $\PrincipalIdealDomainUp$ or $\EuclideanDomainUp$ producer factors that producer through the same finite route
$$
R,I,P,F,A,D,H,C,Q,N.
$$
The producer row may justify admission of the factorization surface, but any factorization consumer still receives only the displayed ring row, ideal row, prime row, factor ledger, associate ledger, prime-divisor handoff, transport, replay, provenance, and local name. In particular, the Euclidean route does not export an algorithm service, and the principal-ideal route does not export quotient ideals or selected generators by choice.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with an accepted packet and inspect its producer rows. If the producer is $G$, then \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-principal-ideal-domain-namecert} supplies the principal-generator ledger only as a dependency row over the same ring and ideal surface. If the producer is $E$, then \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-euclidean-domain-namecert} supplies division-with-remainder evidence only as a dependency row.

In either case, the UFD consumer route is read from \autoref{thm:unique-factorization-domain-namecert-obligations}. The consumer must pass through $R,I,P,F,A,D$ before it reaches transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Thus the producer row cannot replace the local factorization ledgers. It only witnesses that the displayed UFD packet is admissible, while the exported surface remains the same finite route and contains no quotient-ideal object, choice-selected generator, Euclidean algorithm service, field-of-fractions construction, countable choice, or Noetherian induction.
\end{proof}
