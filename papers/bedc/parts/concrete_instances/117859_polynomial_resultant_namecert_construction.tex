\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\PolynomialResultantUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-resultant-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Sylvester and van der Waerden, the $\PolynomialResultantUp$ packet records the finite elimination row by which two displayed univariate polynomial coefficient lists expose a determinant obstruction to a shared root. It sits after the coefficient carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-namecert}, the finite matrix carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, and the scalar arithmetic surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-field-namecert}. The packet names only the Sylvester matrix assembly, determinant readback, degree ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; it is not an algebraic closure theorem, a selected common root, a quotient-polynomial equality, a Euclidean division oracle, or a host elimination procedure.

\begin{definition}[Polynomial resultant carrier]
\label{def:polynomial-resultant-carrier}
A $\PolynomialResultantUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
R=(F,G,D,S,M,\Delta,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ and $G$ are $\PolynomialUp$ coefficient-list rows for the two input polynomials.
\item $D$ is the finite degree and leading-coefficient ledger that fixes the Sylvester block sizes.
\item $S$ is the Sylvester assembly row, listing the shifted coefficient rows used to build the matrix.
\item $M$ is the $\MatrixUp$ row that exposes the assembled Sylvester matrix as finite matrix data.
\item $\Delta$ is the $\FieldUp$ scalar determinant readback row for the matrix $M$.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for $F,G,D,S,M,\Delta$ and the local packet shape.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from polynomial coefficient reads through Sylvester assembly and determinant readback.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\PolynomialResultantUp$, $\PolynomialUp$, $\MatrixUp$, $\FieldUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\PolynomialResultantUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for algebraic closure, selected roots, quotient-polynomial equality, Euclidean division, subresultant chains, ambient field completion, or host elimination.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial resultant Sylvester determinacy]
\label{thm:polynomial-resultant-sylvester-determinacy}
For every accepted $\PolynomialResultantUp$ carrier
$$
R=(F,G,D,S,M,\Delta,H,C,P,N),
$$
the determinant row $\Delta$ is determined only after the two polynomial rows $F,G$, the degree ledger $D$, the Sylvester assembly row $S$, and the finite matrix row $M$ have all been displayed. A consumer may therefore read the resultant scalar only along the route
$$
\PolynomialUp,\PolynomialUp \longmapsto D \longmapsto S \longmapsto \MatrixUp \longmapsto \FieldUp,
$$
followed by $H,C,P,N$; no accepted packet reads $\Delta$ from a selected common root, a quotient-polynomial equality, a Euclidean algorithm transcript, or an ambient elimination theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:polynomial-resultant-carrier}. The scalar row $\Delta$ appears only after the two coefficient rows, the degree ledger, the Sylvester assembly row, and the matrix row have been named.

The Sylvester row $S$ is finite because it is built from shifted copies of the displayed coefficient rows with block sizes fixed by $D$. The matrix row $M$ is therefore a displayed $\MatrixUp$ read, not a host array or an implicit linear operator.

Finally $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same route. Since the carrier contains no selected root, quotient-polynomial equality, Euclidean transcript, or host elimination theorem, the determinant row is determined exactly by the displayed Sylvester route.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial resultant NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:polynomial-resultant-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\PolynomialResultantUp$ carrier
$$
R=(F,G,D,S,M,\Delta,H,C,P,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\PolynomialResultantUp}$ surface consists exactly of polynomial coefficient exposure through $F,G$, degree and leading-coefficient exposure through $D$, Sylvester block assembly through $S$, matrix readback through $M$, field determinant readback through $\Delta$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.

Thus every downstream elimination, discriminant, or algebraic-geometry consumer receives a finite resultant packet only through the sibling surfaces of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-polynomial-namecert}, \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-matrix-namecert}, and \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-field-namecert}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start from the carrier projection. The polynomial source rows $F,G$ and the ledger $D$ provide the only coefficient and degree data available to the packet.

Next apply \autoref{thm:polynomial-resultant-sylvester-determinacy}. It forces every determinant read to pass through $S$ and $M$ before the scalar row $\Delta$ is exposed.

The structural rows do not add algebraic content. They only transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet, so the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}
