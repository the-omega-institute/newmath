\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\WhitneyApproximationUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-whitney-approximation-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Whitney and standard smooth approximation theory, the $\WhitneyApproximationUp$ packet records a finite NameCert route for replacing a displayed continuous map on a finite manifold window by a smooth-row handoff at named tolerances. It sits over the finite-atlas source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-manifold-namecert}, the continuous-map source of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-continuousmap-namecert}, the local smoothing cover of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-partition-of-unity-namecert}, smooth-row consumers such as \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-smoothmanifold-namecert} or \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-diffform-namecert}, the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The chapter names only the finite approximation certificate; it does not assert a host smooth-function space, select a smoothing operator, identify quotient maps, or use ambient completeness.

\begin{definition}[Whitney approximation carrier]
\label{def:whitney-approximation-carrier}
A $\WhitneyApproximationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
W=(M,F,U,L,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $M$ is the $\ManifoldUp$ finite-atlas source row, exposing only the displayed chart domains, overlap rows, and local coordinate names.
\item $F$ is the $\ContinuousMapUp$ source map row to be approximated on the displayed finite window.
\item $U$ is the $\PartitionOfUnityUp$ local cover and weight row used to assemble chartwise smooth pieces.
\item $L$ is the smooth-row ledger, naming the local $\SmoothManifoldUp$ handoff or $\DiffFormUp$ consumer-facing row reached after the partitioned construction.
\item $S$ is the finite approximation schedule, listing the chart windows, requested tolerances, and overlap checks inspected by the packet.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ regular rational readback row for the displayed tolerances and chartwise comparison data.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row for the finite error bounds visible to downstream consumers.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the manifold, source map, partition, smooth ledger, schedule, readback, Real seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the continuous source through the finite cover, local smooth rows, rational readback, and terminal Real seal.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\WhitneyApproximationUp$, $\ManifoldUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\PartitionOfUnityUp$, $\SmoothManifoldUp$, $\DiffFormUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\WhitneyApproximationUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for a host smooth-function space, a choice-selected smoothing operator, quotient map equality, a global approximation theorem, or ambient completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Whitney approximation NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:whitney-approximation-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\WhitneyApproximationUp$ carrier $W=(M,F,U,L,S,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\WhitneyApproximationUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed manifold, continuous-map, partition, smooth-ledger, schedule, regular-readback, Real-seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows; finite-atlas exposure through $M$; source-map exposure through $F$; local smoothing cover exposure through $U$ and $L$; tolerance readback through $S,R,E$; componentwise stability through $H$; displayed replay through $C$; and ledger exhaustion through $P,N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:whitney-approximation-carrier}. The only analytic rows are the finite-atlas source, continuous source map, partition-of-unity cover, smooth ledger, approximation schedule, regular rational readback, and Real seal.

The local smoothing cover is visible before any smooth handoff is public. The partition row $U$ and smooth ledger $L$ name the chartwise route, while $S,R,E$ record the finite tolerance checks that a downstream smooth-row consumer may inspect.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ then transport, replay, source, and name the same finite packet. Since no host smooth-function space, selected smoothing operator, quotient map equality, global theorem, or ambient completeness row is present, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whitney approximation smooth handoff]
\label{thm:whitney-approximation-smooth-handoff}
Every $\SmoothManifoldUp$ or $\DiffFormUp$ consumer of an accepted $\WhitneyApproximationUp$ packet must first read
$$
M,F,U,L,S,R,E
$$
before it receives a smooth-row handoff. The consumer receives a finite atlas, a displayed continuous source map, a partition-of-unity assembly row, local smooth rows, finite tolerance windows, regular rational readback, and a terminal Real seal; it receives no host smooth-function space, selected smoothing operator, quotient map equality, global Whitney theorem, or ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with \autoref{thm:whitney-approximation-namecert-obligations}. It requires carrier admission and finite-atlas exposure before any approximation-facing consumer can inspect the packet.

The smooth handoff is downstream of $U$ and $L$. These rows display the partitioned local construction and the smooth-row ledger, so a $\SmoothManifoldUp$ or $\DiffFormUp$ consumer cannot enter through an untracked host smoothing operator.

The tolerance read is $S,R,E$. The finite schedule lists the inspected windows, the regular rational row supplies the readback, and the Real seal records the displayed error bound. The remaining rows $H,C,P,N$ only transport, replay, source, and name this route, leaving no coordinate for quotient equality, a global theorem, or ambient completeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whitney approximation nonescape]
\label{thm:whitney-approximation-nonescape}
Every accepted $\WhitneyApproximationUp$ public read factors through the displayed rows
$$
M,F,U,L,S,R,E,H,C,P,N.
$$
Thus the packet can expose only a finite NameCert approximation route over $\ManifoldUp$, $\ContinuousMapUp$, $\PartitionOfUnityUp$, $\SmoothManifoldUp$ or $\DiffFormUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, $\Pkg$, and $\NameCert$ rows. It cannot export a host smooth-function space, a choice-selected smoothing operator, quotient map equality, ambient completeness, or a completed global approximation theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use \autoref{def:whitney-approximation-carrier}. The carrier displays exactly the eleven rows $M,F,U,L,S,R,E,H,C,P,N$.

Apply \autoref{thm:whitney-approximation-namecert-obligations} and \autoref{thm:whitney-approximation-smooth-handoff}. They force every smooth-facing read to pass through the finite-atlas, continuous-map, partition, smooth-ledger, tolerance, readback, and Real-seal rows before structural transport and provenance are consumed.

The excluded host objects are not coordinates of the carrier and are not introduced by the structural rows. Therefore every public read remains inside the displayed finite NameCert route.
\end{proof}
