\begin{definition}[Cartan--Dieudonne carrier]
\label{def:cartan-dieudonne-carrier}

$$

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V$ is the displayed vector-space or module source row whose finite-dimensional boundary is being inspected.
\item $Q$ is the quadratic-form row read through the diagonal and symmetry data of $\BilinFormUp$.
\item $B$ is the nondegenerate bilinear-pairing row that keeps radical and orthogonality tests visible.
\item $R$ is the finite reflection tuple, listing the reflection rows that generate the inspected isometry.
\item $W$ is the Clifford word row that types the same reflection product inside $\CliffordUp$.
\item $O$ is the orthogonal-action boundary row, recording that the reflected endpoints preserve the displayed quadratic row $Q$.
\item $S$ records the Spin and Pin sibling reads needed to distinguish even reflection products from the full reflection-parity surface.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\BilinFormUp$, $\CliffordUp$, $\SpinGroupUp$, $\PinGroupUp$, $\FiniteReflectionTupleUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the vector source, quadratic row, bilinear row, reflection tuple, Clifford word, orthogonal boundary, parity row, provenance, replay, and local naming.
\item $T$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from an orthogonal-action request through $V,Q,B,R,W,O,S,P,H$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CartanDieudonneUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for an ambient orthogonal group, a host matrix representation, a selected basis, a quotient Clifford algebra, infinite reflection products, or analytic spectral diagonalization.

Since the carrier has no coordinate for an ambient orthogonal group, selected basis, host matrix action, quotient Clifford algebra, infinite product, or analytic diagonalization, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
