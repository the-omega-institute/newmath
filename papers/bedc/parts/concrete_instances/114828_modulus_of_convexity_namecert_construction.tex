\begin{theorem}[Modulus of convexity NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:modulus-of-convexity-namecert-obligations}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:modulus-of-convexity-carrier}. The analytic rows are $B,N,K,R,E,M$: Banach completeness, norm access, unit-ball convexity, Real tolerance budgets, displayed separation, and the midpoint-drop modulus.

The structural rows $H,C,P,L$ transport, replay, source, and name those analytic rows as one finite packet. They do not add host compactness, selected minimizers, quotient norm equality, or ambient separation.

The handoff row $U$ is therefore readable only after the displayed modulus rows are present. This exhausts the local NameCert surface and fixes the consumer order through the finite BEDC rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

Read the convexity and scalar rows next. The row $K$ restricts the midpoint operation to the displayed convex unit-ball surface, and $R$ supplies the Real-valued separation and midpoint-drop tolerances.
