\begin{definition}[Cauchy null-modulus carrier]
\label{def:cauchy-null-modulus-carrier}

$$

\begin{enumerate}
\item $D$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ difference row whose inspected observations are the rational difference stream being tested for zero convergence.
\item $T$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance request row, including the positive dyadic budget requested by a downstream equality or completion-facing consumer.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite observation window in which the difference row is inspected for the requested tolerance.
\item $B$ is the explicit $\ZeroTailModulusUp$ threshold row, binding each tolerance request in $T$ to a finite tail index and the corresponding window in $W$.
\item $Z$ is the $\NullSequenceUp$ row that consumes $D,T,W,B$ as the displayed zero-tail witness.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ equality seal, readable only after $Z$ has consumed the explicit threshold row.

\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from the consumer request to the tolerance row, from the tolerance row to the threshold and window rows, from those rows to the null-sequence classifier, and from the classifier to the equality seal.

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy null-modulus NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:cauchy-null-modulus-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CauchyNullModulusUp$ carrier $M=(D,T,W,B,Z,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CauchyNullModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission for the displayed difference, tolerance, window, threshold, NullSequence, Real seal, transport, replay, provenance, and naming rows; threshold exactness from each tolerance request in $T$ to the finite row $B$; window compatibility between $B$ and $W$; NullSequence consumption of $D,T,W,B$ through $Z$; and Real-seal nonescape through $E$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Carrier admission is projection from \autoref{def:cauchy-null-modulus-carrier}. The accepted packet displays exactly the difference row, tolerance request, finite window, explicit threshold, null-sequence consumer, Real equality seal, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

Threshold exactness is the next obligation. The row $T$ supplies the requested dyadic tolerance, while $B$ is the only coordinate that can answer that request with a finite tail index. The window row $W$ is then the finite observation surface attached to that threshold, so the route from tolerance to inspection is visible before zero-convergence is consumed.

Now apply the null-sequence row. The row $Z$ receives the difference row $D$ only together with the tolerance, window, and threshold rows $T,W,B$, and therefore records explicit zero-tail evidence rather than a host limit.

Finally inspect the carrier inventory. Since quotient stream equality, selected representatives, selected limits, countable choice, trichotomy, ambient $\RealUp$ completeness, Lean verification, and bridge checking are absent from $M$, none becomes a local NameCert obligation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cauchy null-modulus Real equality route]
\label{thm:cauchy-null-modulus-real-equality-route}

$$

$$
as replayed by $H,C,P,N$. The equality seal is therefore reached through an explicit null-modulus threshold and not through quotient stream equality, selected limits, trichotomy, countable choice, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the equality-facing consumer to the rows of $M$. The consumer tolerance is visible as the dyadic row $T$, and the difference stream under test is visible as the regular rational difference row $D$.

The explicit modulus route then supplies the threshold row $B$ and finite window row $W$. These rows bind the requested tolerance to a displayed tail index and to the inspected finite stream window.

The refused alternatives are absent from the carrier. Thus the $\RealUp$ equality seal is a consumer of the explicit null-modulus route, not a quotient equality, selected limit, trichotomy branch, countable-choice schedule, or ambient completeness theorem.
