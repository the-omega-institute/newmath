\begin{theorem}[Compact uniform dyadic radius ladder NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-dyadic-radius-ladder-namecert-obligations}

\item carrier admission for the compact metric source, finite-net ledger, continuous-map pointwise-radius row, finite Lebesgue-number row, dyadic radius ladder, uniform-modulus handoff, transports, continuations, provenance, and local name;
\item finite-net coverage, requiring every uniform-modulus read to begin with the compact $\mathsf{ProbeBundle}$ centers and coverage witnesses in $X$ and $F$;
\item pointwise-radius attachment, requiring each bundled center to carry its $\ContinuousMapUp$ local radius row $G$ before the lower-bound fold is consumed;
\item lower-bound exactness, requiring the folded positive radius to pass through the $\FiniteLebesgueNumberUp$ row $L$ and the dyadic comparison ladder $D$ before $U$ is exposed;
\item downstream handoff, requiring every $\UniformModulusUp$ consumer to read $X,F,G,L,D,U,H,C,P,N$ and no external compactness, selected minimum, or host modulus function.

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The lower-bound step is finite. The bundled radii are folded through the $\FiniteLebesgueNumberUp$ ledger, and the resulting positive radius is refined through the dyadic ladder $D$ before the $\UniformModulusUp$ row can be exposed.

Transport and continuation replay preserve the same row order. Since the carrier contains no external minimum, sequential compactness witness, or selected modulus function, the downstream consumer receives only the finite NameCert ladder.
\end{proof}
