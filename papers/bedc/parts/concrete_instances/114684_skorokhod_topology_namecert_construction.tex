It sits after the cadlag path carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cadlag-real-function-namecert}, the metric interface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-metric-namecert}, the finite observation schedule of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the regular rational readback surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance core of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, and the terminal real-name seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. \begin{definition}[Skorokhod topology carrier]
\label{def:skorokhod-topology-carrier}
A $\SkorokhodTopologyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
T=(K,M,S,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $K$ is the $\CadlagRealFunctionUp$ source row naming the path packet whose right-continuity and left-limit windows are already displayed.
\item $M$ is the $\MetricUp$ comparison row used only as a finite tolerance interface on path-window observations.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ observation schedule for domain windows, jump-neighborhood windows, and time-change probes.
\item $R$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for rational observations along the displayed schedule.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger controlling mesh size, time-change displacement, path-window error, and real-name readback precision.
\item $L$ is the Skorokhod-lag row. It records finite monotone time-change probes and their mesh/tolerance obligations, not a selected homeomorphism of the host interval.
\item $E$ is the terminal $\RealUp$ seal row exposed only after the cadlag source, metric comparison row, observation schedule, rational readback, dyadic tolerance ledger, and lag row have been displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for the cadlag source, metric comparison, schedule, readback, tolerance, lag, seal, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from a path-topology request through $K,M,S,R,D,L,E,H,P,N$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\SkorokhodTopologyUp$, $\CadlagRealFunctionUp$, $\MetricUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\SkorokhodTopologyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $K,M,S,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for host path equality, quotient path equality, selected time changes, selected limits, Borel sigma-algebras, compactness, tightness, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Skorokhod topology NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:skorokhod-topology-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\SkorokhodTopologyUp$ carrier $T=(K,M,S,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\SkorokhodTopologyUp}$ surface consists exactly of carrier admission, cadlag-source admission through $K$, metric comparison admission through $M$, finite observation schedule exposure through $S$, regular rational readback through $R$, dyadic tolerance control through $D$, finite Skorokhod-lag obligation through $L$, terminal real-seal ordering through $E$, componentwise transport through $H$, displayed replay through $C$, package provenance through $P$, local naming through $N$, and ledger refusal for host path equality, quotient path equality, selected time changes, selected limits, measure-theoretic structure, compactness, tightness, and ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier. The analytic rows are $K,M,S,R,D,L,E$, and the structural rows are $H,C,P,N$.

The cadlag source $K$ supplies right-continuity and left-limit window obligations from \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-cadlag-real-function-namecert}. The metric comparison row $M$, stream schedule $S$, rational readback $R$, and dyadic ledger $D$ provide the finite data on which a topology consumer may compare path-window observations.

The lag row $L$ is downstream of those data. It records finite time-change probes and their tolerance obligations, while the terminal seal $E$ is reached only after the displayed route has passed through $K,M,S,R,D,L$.

Componentwise transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve the same packet. Since no coordinate stores selected time changes, quotient path equality, measure-theoretic structure, compactness, tightness, or ambient real completeness, the NameCert obligations are exactly the listed finite rows.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Skorokhod topology cadlag modulus handoff]
\label{thm:skorokhod-topology-cadlag-modulus-handoff}
Every path-topology consumer of an accepted $\SkorokhodTopologyUp$ packet must first read the $\CadlagRealFunctionUp$ source row, then the $\MetricUp$ comparison row, the $\StreamNameUp$ schedule, the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row, and the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger before it can consume the Skorokhod-lag row and the terminal $\RealUp$ seal. Thus the handoff is a finite cadlag-modulus route rather than a host equality of paths, a quotient equality under time changes, a selected limiting path, a measure-theoretic compactness theorem, or an ambient completeness principle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $T=(K,M,S,R,D,L,E,H,C,P,N)$ be accepted. A path-topology request reaches the cadlag source row $K$ before any comparison row is available, so the request begins with displayed right-continuity and left-limit windows.

Next the request reads $M,S,R,D$. These rows give the metric tolerance interface, finite observation schedule, regular rational readback, and dyadic precision ledger needed to state a finite comparison between path windows.

Only then may the request consume the lag row $L$ and the terminal seal $E$. The lag row records time-change probes as displayed finite obligations, while the seal row remains downstream of cadlag, metric, schedule, readback, and tolerance data.

Transport through $H$ and replay through $C$ preserve the same route, and $P,N$ package and name it. The excluded host equality, quotient equality, selected-limit, measure-theoretic, compactness, and completeness conclusions have no carrier row to read.
\end{proof}
