\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\CountablyCompactUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-countably-compact-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Kelley and Bourbaki on countable open-cover compactness, the $\CountablyCompactUp$ packet records the traditional countably compact surface as finite NameCert data. It reads the $\TopologyUp$ source row of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the compactness boundary of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-compact-namecert}, the finite window discipline of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the countable-cover consumer surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-lindelof-space-namecert}, and the basis-counting route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-second-countable-namecert}. The packet names the countable-cover interface without making arbitrary open-cover compactness a primitive coordinate.

\begin{definition}[Countably compact carrier]
\label{def:countably-compact-carrier}
A $\CountablyCompactUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(T,C,W,S,H,R,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ is the $\TopologyUp$ source row, exposing the carrier, indexed open rows, finite-intersection readback, and open-membership classifier used by the packet.
\item $C$ is the countable open-cover enumeration row. It records the inspected StreamName schedule of open indices and the displayed statement that the named countable family covers the source row.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite cover-window row, listing finite prefixes and the pointwise cover checks visible inside each prefix.
\item $S$ is the finite-subcover ledger. It records the accepted prefix whose listed open rows cover the same source surface, together with the finite witness that no later enumeration row is needed for that cover read.
\item $H$ stores componentwise $\hsame$ transport for topology, countable-cover enumeration, finite windows, finite-subcover ledger, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $R$ stores displayed $\Cont$ replay from a countable-cover request through $T,C,W,S,H$.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\CountablyCompactUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\CompactUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\LindelofSpaceUp$, $\SecondCountableUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\CountablyCompactUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The carrier has no coordinate for arbitrary open-cover compactness, selected ultrafilters, countable-choice extraction of subcovers, host powersets, quotient open equality, or a standard bridge.
\end{definition}
\leanstmt{BEDC.Derived.CountablyCompactUp}

\begin{theorem}[Countably compact NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:countably-compact-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\CountablyCompactUp$ carrier $K=(T,C,W,S,H,R,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CountablyCompactUp}$ surface consists exactly of topology source admission, countable open-cover enumeration, StreamName finite cover windows, finite-subcover ledger readback, componentwise $\hsame$ transport, displayed $\Cont$ replay, $\Pkg$ provenance, and local naming. Every countable-cover consumer must read $T,C,W,S$ before the structural rows $H,R,P,N$ can be consumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:countably-compact-carrier}. The topology row $T$ is the only source surface, and the countable cover row $C$ is the only row that records an open-cover enumeration.

The finite evidence is downstream of that enumeration. Row $W$ exposes finite StreamName windows of the countable cover, and $S$ records the finite prefix whose displayed open rows cover the same source row.

Finally $H,R,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name exactly those rows. Since no field stores arbitrary open covers, ultrafilter convergence, selected subcovers, host powersets, quotient open equality, or a bridge credential, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Countably compact StreamName cover route]
\label{thm:countably-compact-streamname-cover-route}
Every accepted $\CountablyCompactUp$ cover read factors through the finite route
$$
\TopologyUp \longrightarrow \StreamNameUp \longrightarrow \CountablyCompactUp \longrightarrow \LindelofSpaceUp \longrightarrow \SecondCountableUp
$$
whenever a downstream countability or basis consumer asks for countable-cover compactness. The route exports only the displayed topology source row, countable open-cover enumeration, finite StreamName windows, finite-subcover ledger, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Start with the accepted packet $K=(T,C,W,S,H,R,P,N)$. A cover read first inspects $T$ and $C$, so the cover is a displayed countable family over the named topology row rather than an arbitrary host cover.

The StreamName handoff is the finite evidence layer. Row $W$ provides the finite prefix windows, and $S$ records the finite-subcover ledger consumed by countable-cover readers.

The downstream Lindelof and second-countable surfaces receive only that finite ledger through $H,R,P,N$. Thus the route is a displayed countable-cover compactness packet and not an open-cover compactness primitive.
\end{proof}
