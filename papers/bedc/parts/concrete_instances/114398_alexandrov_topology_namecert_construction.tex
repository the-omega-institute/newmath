\chapter{A Concrete Naming Certificate for $\AlexandrovTopologyUp$}
\label{ch:concrete-instances-alexandrov-topology-namecert}
\origin{human}

Following Alexandrov and the order-topology tradition, the $\AlexandrovTopologyUp$ packet records the topology generated by a finite preorder row through upward-closed observation windows. It sits after the preorder carrier of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-preorder-namecert}, the indexed-open surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-topology-namecert}, the open-neighbourhood implication route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-specialization-order-namecert}, and the domain-facing comparison surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-scott-topology-namecert}. The packet names only finite upper-set generation, finite union and intersection replay, specialization transport, and Scott-facing comparison data; it does not import a host powerset topology, quotient-open equality, arbitrary joins, selected directed suprema, excluded middle over opens, or Lean verification.

\begin{definition}[Alexandrov topology carrier]\label{def:alexandrov-topology-carrier}
An $\AlexandrovTopologyUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
A=(P,U,O,J,M,S,Q,H,C,G,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is the consumed $\PreorderUp$ row, listing the finite point names, reflexivity witnesses, transitivity witnesses, and comparison requests used by the generation.
\item $U$ is the upper-set row, recording the finite upward-closure checks: whenever $x \leq_P y$ and $x$ is accepted by the window, the same window accepts $y$.
\item $O$ is the open-window row, listing the finitely inspected Alexandrov opens and their membership probes.
\item $J$ records finite union replay for the displayed open windows, using only the listed membership probes.
\item $M$ records finite intersection replay for the displayed open windows, again restricted to the same finite preorder-generated rows.
\item $S$ is the specialization transport row, comparing the specialization preorder induced by the displayed open windows with the source preorder row $P$.
\item $Q$ is the Scott-comparison row, recording the finite handoff to $\ScottTopologyUp$ consumers when a Scott-facing upper-set window is displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for preorder rows, upper-set rows, open windows, union and intersection replay, specialization transport, Scott comparison, continuation, provenance, and local naming rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay from preorder requests through $U,O,J,M,S,Q$ and then to the public topology-facing read.
\item $G$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\AlexandrovTopologyUp$, $\PreorderUp$, $\TopologyUp$, $\SpecializationOrderUp$, $\ScottTopologyUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\AlexandrovTopologyUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted carriers only through $P,U,O,J,M,S,Q,H,C,G,N$. It has no coordinate for a host powerset of opens, arbitrary infinitary closure, quotient-open equality, selected basis completion, or directed-supremum theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[AlexandrovTopology NameCert obligations]\label{thm:alexandrov-topology-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\AlexandrovTopologyUp$ carrier $A=(P,U,O,J,M,S,Q,H,C,G,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\AlexandrovTopologyUp}$ surface consists exactly of the following finite obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item carrier admission for the displayed preorder, upper-set, open-window, finite union, finite intersection, specialization, Scott-comparison, transport, replay, provenance, and local naming rows;
\item upper-set exactness, requiring each accepted open-window membership read to be upward closed along the preorder row $P$ through the displayed row $U$;
\item finite topology replay, requiring $J$ and $M$ to certify only the displayed finite union and finite intersection windows;
\item specialization compatibility, requiring the specialization preorder read from $O,J,M$ to transport through $S$ back to the source preorder row $P$;
\item Scott handoff discipline, requiring $Q$ to expose only the displayed upper-set comparison rows before any $\ScottTopologyUp$ consumer reads the packet;
\item componentwise transport, displayed continuation replay, package provenance, and local naming through $H,C,G,N$.
\end{enumerate}
These obligations certify the finite preorder-generated Alexandrov topology surface. They do not close arbitrary open lattices, powerset extensionality, directed-supremum completeness, quotient-open equality, selected bases, excluded middle over opens, or Lean verification.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:alexandrov-topology-carrier}. The readable rows are exactly $P,U,O,J,M,S,Q,H,C,G,N$, so every topology-facing consumer starts from the displayed preorder and finite open-window data.

Upper-set exactness is read from $U$ together with the comparison witnesses in $P$. The finite topology operations are then replayed by $J$ and $M$, which inspect only the displayed open windows and therefore cannot introduce an ambient powerset of opens.

The specialization route is handled by $S$. It compares open-neighbourhood implication with the source preorder row already visible in $P$, so the packet records a transport of finite rows rather than a quotient equality of points.

The Scott-facing row $Q$ is downstream from the same upper-set and open-window rows. Thus any Scott comparison receives a finite Alexandrov handoff and not a directed-supremum theorem.

Finally, $H,C,G,N$ transport, replay, source, and name this finite route. Since no other coordinate is present, the excluded host-topological and infinitary principles cannot be exported.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[AlexandrovTopology preorder specialization route]\label{thm:alexandrov-topology-preorder-specialization-route}
Let $A=(P,U,O,J,M,S,Q,H,C,G,N)$ be an accepted $\AlexandrovTopologyUp$ carrier. Every specialization-order read exported by $A$ factors through the finite route
$$
P \longmapsto U \longmapsto O,J,M \longmapsto S .
$$
The exported read may compare preorder rows with open-neighbourhood implication rows, but it cannot bypass the displayed Alexandrov windows to import quotient point equality, host separation, or an undeclared topology theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a specialization-facing request. The only order source visible to the request is the preorder row $P$, and the only open data available for neighbourhood implication is the displayed upper-set and open-window route $U,O,J,M$.

The transport row $S$ reads those same rows before it exposes a specialization comparison. Hence the exported relation is obtained by finite preorder-generated windows rather than by host point equality or a separation axiom.

The structural rows $H,C,G,N$ preserve and name this route. They do not add any coordinate for quotient points, powerset opens, or an ambient topological theorem, so the specialization read remains inside the displayed carrier.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[AlexandrovTopology Scott handoff]\label{thm:alexandrov-topology-scott-handoff}
Let $A=(P,U,O,J,M,S,Q,H,C,G,N)$ be an accepted $\AlexandrovTopologyUp$ carrier. Every $\ScottTopologyUp$ comparison exported by $A$ must first read the preorder row $P$, the upper-set ledger $U$, the finite open-window row $O$, and the union and intersection replay rows $J,M$. The handoff row $Q$ therefore presents a finite upper-set comparison surface and does not claim directed-supremum inaccessibility, dcpo completeness, or equality with a host Scott topology.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier. The Scott-facing coordinate is $Q$, and it appears only after the preorder, upper-set, open-window, and finite topology replay rows.

Use the Scott handoff discipline in \autoref{thm:alexandrov-topology-namecert-obligations}. It requires $Q$ to expose the displayed upper-set comparison rows before a $\ScottTopologyUp$ consumer can read the packet.

Because directed suprema, dcpo completeness, and host Scott-open equality are absent from $P,U,O,J,M,S,Q,H,C,G,N$, the handoff remains a finite comparison route. It can feed domain-theoretic consumers without asserting the missing infinitary closure.
\end{proof}
