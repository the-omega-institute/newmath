Following Bishop and Bridges on regular Cauchy real names, the $\RegularCauchySubsequenceCriterionUp$ packet records the finite criterion by which a displayed monotone cofinal subsequence carries the same regular Cauchy and Real-facing data as its source. It sits over the regular rational readback of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regseqrat-namecert}, the finite observation surface of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-streamname-namecert}, the dyadic tolerance ledger of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-dyadicratcore-namecert}, the tail-shift route of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-regular-cauchy-shift-namecert}, and the terminal seal of \autoref{ch:concrete-instances-real-namecert}. The packet is a finite subsequence criterion for Real and Completion consumers; it does not choose a subsequence by countable choice, quotient streams, selected limits, host sequence equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.

\begin{definition}[RegularCauchySubsequenceCriterion carrier]
\label{def:regular-cauchy-subsequence-criterion-carrier}
A $\RegularCauchySubsequenceCriterionUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$
K=(S,M,W,D,T,R,E,H,C,P,N)
$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $S$ is the source $\RegSeqRatUp$ row carrying the regular rational readback of the original name.
\item $M$ is the monotone cofinal subsequence schedule, recording the finite source indices inspected for each subsequence window.
\item $W$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ window ledger comparing source windows with subsequence windows.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger transported from source windows to subsequence windows.
\item $T$ is the $\RegularCauchyShiftUp$ tail route that witnesses discarded prefixes before the subsequence readback is exposed.
\item $R$ is the resulting $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row for the subsequence window.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $S,M,W,D,T,R$ are displayed.
\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for all carrier rows.
\item $C$ records displayed $\Cont$ replay of the same finite route.
\item $P$ records $\Pkg$ provenance over $\RegularCauchySubsequenceCriterionUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegularCauchyShiftUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchySubsequenceCriterionUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier reads only $S,M,W,D,T,R,E,H,C,P,N$. It has no coordinate for quotient stream equality, a selected infinite subsequence, host sequence equality, a chosen limit, countable choice, or ambient completeness of $\RealUp$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchySubsequenceCriterion NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-subsequence-criterion-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\RegularCauchySubsequenceCriterionUp$ carrier $K=(S,M,W,D,T,R,E,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\RegularCauchySubsequenceCriterionUp}$ surface consists exactly of the source $\RegSeqRatUp$ row $S$, monotone cofinal schedule $M$, finite $\StreamNameUp$ comparison windows $W$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger $D$, $\RegularCauchyShiftUp$ tail route $T$, subsequence $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback $R$, terminal $\RealUp$ seal $E$, componentwise transport $H$, continuation replay $C$, provenance $P$, and local naming row $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the accepted carrier of \autoref{def:regular-cauchy-subsequence-criterion-carrier}. Each row named in the statement is a displayed coordinate, and the definition lists no hidden coordinate for quotient streams, selected subsequences, or completion objects.

The analytic part of the criterion is the finite chain $S,M,W,D,T,R,E$. The source regular readback $S$ is compared with subsequence windows by $M$ and $W$, dyadic tolerances are transported by $D$, discarded prefixes are recorded by $T$, and the subsequence readback $R$ is sealed by $E$.

The structural rows $H,C,P,N$ transport, replay, source, and name the same finite chain. Since the carrier has no row for countable choice, host sequence equality, selected limits, quotient stream equality, or ambient Real completeness, none of those endpoints can enter the local NameCert surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[RegularCauchySubsequenceCriterion Real seal handoff]
\label{thm:regular-cauchy-subsequence-criterion-real-seal-handoff}

Thus the criterion hands a cofinal subsequence to the Real route as finite displayed data, without quotient streams, selected subsequences, countable choice, selected limits, host real equality, or ambient $\RealUp$ completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Begin with a request for the Real seal. By the carrier definition, $E$ is not available directly from the source row; it is reached only after $S,M,W,D,T,R$ have been inspected.

The subsequence part is finite. The schedule $M$ names the source indices for the displayed windows, $W$ records the compared observations, and $D$ carries the dyadic tolerances needed to preserve regularity along those windows.

The tail route $T$ then records the finite shift discipline before $R$ is read as a regular rational subsequence row. Only after this route is displayed can $E$ be consumed as the terminal $\RealUp$ seal.

Transport, replay, provenance, and local naming preserve this same order. Because no carrier row stores quotient stream equality, a selected subsequence functional, a selected limit, host equality, countable choice, or Real completeness, the handoff cannot export those data.
\end{proof}
