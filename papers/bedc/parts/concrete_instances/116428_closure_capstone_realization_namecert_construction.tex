\begin{definition}[Closure capstone realization carrier]
\label{def:closure-capstone-realization-carrier}
A $\ClosureCapstoneRealizationUp$ carrier is a finite $\BHist$ packet
$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $A$ is the closure-axis row, recording the term, type, and proposition strata named by the capstone.
\item $G$ is the generator-induction row, recording the finite replacement route used when a witness, representative, or equality token is demanded.
\item $R$ is the refusal row, recording that unwitnessed choice, quotient representative selection, and proposition equality by extensionality are not accepted as kernel inputs.
\item $T$ is the transport row, recording explicit $\hsame$ and $\psame$ transport through displayed implications and classifier-respecting routes.
\item $E$ is the replay row, recording the $\Cont$ trace by which the replacement route is recomputed from the finite rows.
\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\ClosureCapstoneRealizationUp$, the closure-capstone vision route, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\psame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\ClosureCapstoneRealizationUp}$ row.

The classifier compares accepted packets only through $A,G,R,T,E,P,N$. It has no coordinate for $\mathsf{Classical.choice}$, $\mathsf{Quot.sound}$, $\mathsf{propext}$, hidden representatives, erased implication directions, or unwitnessed existential closure.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Closure capstone realization NameCert obligations]
\label{thm:closure-capstone-realization-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ClosureCapstoneRealizationUp$ carrier $K=(A,G,R,T,E,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\ClosureCapstoneRealizationUp}$ surface consists exactly of closure-stratum exposure, generator-induction replacement, refusal recording, explicit transport, replay, provenance, and local naming.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:closure-capstone-realization-carrier}. The row list $A,G,R,T,E,P,N$ is the whole packet, so no accepted read can draw on a hidden representative, erased proposition equality, or external choice function.

The closure-axis row $A$ names the three syntactic strata. The generator row $G$ supplies the finite replacement route at each stratum, while the refusal row $R$ records which unwitnessed closure move is not available.

Transport and replay are explicit. The row $T$ keeps each implication or classifier-respecting route visible, and the row $E$ records the $\Cont$ replay that recomputes the replacement route from the displayed rows.

Finally $P$ and $N$ source and name the same packet. Since the excluded axioms and oracle moves are not carrier coordinates, the local NameCert obligations are exactly the displayed rows.
\end{proof}

\end{proof}
