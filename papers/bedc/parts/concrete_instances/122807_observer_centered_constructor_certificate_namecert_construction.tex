\begin{definition}[Observer-centered constructor certificate carrier]
\label{def:observer-centered-constructor-certificate-carrier}

$$

$$
with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ is the adjacent frame-selection row naming the displayed frame edge and successor frame.
\item $B$ is the Hilbert-bundle row naming the source and successor Hilbert spaces used by the observer-centered step.
\item $S$ is the isometric reshaping row carrying the section transport between the two frames.
\item $L$ is the closed ledger row naming the prior ledger subspace and its rewritten closed image.
\item $P$ is the orthogonal projection row against the rewritten ledger.
\item $R$ is the projection residual row naming the vector subtracted from the projection row.

\item $H$ records componentwise $\hsame$ transport for frame, bundle, reshaping, ledger, projection, residual, handoff, replay, provenance, and local naming rows.

\begin{theorem}[Observer-centered constructor certificate obligations]
\label{thm:observer-centered-constructor-certificate-namecert-obligations}
For every accepted $\ObserverCenteredConstructorCertificateUp$ carrier
$$
O=(F,B,S,L,P,R,E,H,C,G,N),
$$
the local $\NameCert_{\ObserverCenteredConstructorCertificateUp}$ surface consists exactly of adjacent frame selection through $F$, Hilbert-bundle exposure through $B$, isometric reshaping through $S$, closed ledger retention through $L$, projection against the rewritten ledger through $P$, projection residual exposure through $R$, orthogonal section handoff through $E$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\TasteGate$ readiness through $G$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the carrier of \autoref{def:observer-centered-constructor-certificate-carrier}. The rows $F,B,S,L$ reproduce the typed inputs of the OCLSD constructor packet, and $P,R,E$ are the projection, residual, and accepted orthogonal section rows.

Apply \autoref{thm:observer-centered-projection-forces-orthogonal-step}. The theorem forces the residual row to be orthogonal to the rewritten ledger, so the handoff row $E$ cannot be derived if $R$ is absent.

The structural rows $H,C,G,N$ preserve, replay, reserve TasteGate readiness for, and name the same packet. Since the carrier has no hidden observer, detached projection theorem, or residual-free handoff coordinate, the listed rows exhaust the local NameCert obligations.
\end{proof}
