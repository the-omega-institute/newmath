\begin{definition}[Leibniz alternating test carrier]
\label{def:leibniz-alternating-test-carrier}

$$
T=(R,A,M,S,D,Q,W,E,H,C,P,N)

with the following displayed rows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $R$ is the accepted $\LeibnizAlternatingRemainderUp$ row, exposing the next-omitted-term remainder certificate already routed through alternating signs, monotone magnitudes, and dyadic budgets.
\item $A$ is the alternating partial-sum schedule, recording the inspected parity rows, finite partial sums, adjacent direction checks, and cutoff schedule used by the test.
\item $M$ is the monotone null-tail row, recording nonnegative magnitude reads, observed monotonicity comparisons, and the null-tail request that makes the next omitted magnitude smaller than any requested dyadic tolerance.
\item $S$ is the $\StreamNameUp$ finite-window family for summands, signs, magnitudes, partial sums, cutoffs, next omitted terms, dyadic comparisons, and endpoint-facing reads.
\item $D$ is the $\DyadicRatCoreUp$ tolerance ledger pairing every precision request with a cutoff, a next omitted magnitude, and a comparison row.
\item $Q$ is the $\RegSeqRatUp$ readback row obtained from $A,M,S,D$ and the accepted remainder row $R$.
\item $W$ is the test-level Cauchy handoff row: it records that the partial-sum schedule is Cauchy by reading only the displayed remainder certificate, monotone null tail, dyadic tolerance ledger, and regular rational readback.
\item $E$ is the $\RealUp$ seal row reached only after $R,A,M,S,D,Q,W$ have been displayed.

$$

\item $P$ is the $\Pkg$ provenance over $\LeibnizAlternatingTestUp$, $\LeibnizAlternatingRemainderUp$, $\DyadicRatCoreUp$, $\RegSeqRatUp$, $\StreamNameUp$, $\RealUp$, $\BHist$, $\hsame$, $\Cont$, and $\NameCert$ rows.
\item $N$ is the local $\NameCert_{\LeibnizAlternatingTestUp}$ row.
\end{enumerate}
The classifier compares accepted packets only through $R,A,M,S,D,Q,W,E,H,C,P,N$.
