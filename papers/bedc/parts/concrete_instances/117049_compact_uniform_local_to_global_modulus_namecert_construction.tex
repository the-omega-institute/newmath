\begin{theorem}[CompactUniformLocalToGlobalModulus obligations]
\label{thm:compact-uniform-local-to-global-modulus-obligations}
For every accepted $\CompactUniformLocalToGlobalModulusUp$ carrier $G=(K,F,L,R,T,U,H,C,P,N)$, the local $\NameCert_{\CompactUniformLocalToGlobalModulusUp}$ surface consists exactly of compact finite-net admission through $K$, local pointwise-modulus exposure through $F$, local-radius ledger visibility through $L$, rational lower-bound fold exactness through $R$, metric-triangle transport through $T$, uniform-modulus export through $U$, componentwise $\hsame$ transport through $H$, displayed $\Cont$ replay through $C$, $\Pkg$ provenance through $P$, and local naming through $N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Project the displayed carrier. The compact side is exhausted by $K$: it supplies the finite $\ProbeBundle$ coverage row over the compact metric source.

The local side is exhausted by $F$ and $L$. These rows record the pointwise modulus read at each bundled center and the radius ledger attached to that read. The global precision row is then computed only by $R$, the finite rational lower-bound fold over the displayed ledger.

The handoff to a consumer uses $T$ and $U$. The metric-triangle row transports center estimates to arbitrary source points, and the uniform-modulus row records the tolerance-only output. Since $H,C,P,N$ only preserve, replay, source, and name the displayed rows, the listed obligations exhaust the packet.
\end{proof}

\label{thm:compact-uniform-local-to-global-modulus-handoff}

\begin{proof}

The local radii do not become a global modulus until the rational fold row $R$ is displayed. This row is finite because it folds over the $\ProbeBundle$ ledger named by $K$ and the local-radius row $L$.
