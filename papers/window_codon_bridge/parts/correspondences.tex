\subsection{Cardinality Forcing}\label{sec:bridge-cardinality-forcing}
% CLAIM_DETAIL claim_id=bridge.window6_codon_q6.cardinality_forcing status=coincidence
\textbf{Statement.} The codon-Q6 boundary cardinality ($|R|=13=F_7$, 21 synonymous families) is FORCED by the Window6
Fibonacci horizon ($X_6=21=F_8$) via an explicit structural map standard-code-boundary -> width-6
Fibonacci words, not coincidence.

\textbf{Verdict.} \texttt{coincidence}.

\textbf{Why.} The counts 21 and 13 match the width-6 and width-5 Fibonacci cubes, but the tested canonical
biological structures do not yield a structure-respecting forcing map.

\textbf{Derivation script.} tools/window\_codon\_bridge/experiments/run\_bc1\_cardinality\_forcing.py

\subsection{Spectral Correspondence}\label{sec:bridge-spectral-correspondence}
% CLAIM_DETAIL claim_id=bridge.window6_codon_q6.spectral_correspondence status=refuted
\textbf{Statement.} The bio codon-Q6 spectral scalars ($\lambda_M$, $\lambda_R$, mu) equal the Window6 coarse Markov
kernel spectrum (high/low energy superposition).

\textbf{Verdict.} \texttt{refuted}.

\textbf{Why.} bio spectral scalars are not the coarse Markov kernel spectrum (subdominant eig do not match lambda;
mu>1 is not a stochastic eigenvalue).

\textbf{Derivation script.} tools/window\_codon\_bridge/experiments/run\_bc2\_markov\_kernel\_spectrum.py

\subsection{Foldbin Codon Positions}\label{sec:bridge-foldbin-codon-positions}
% CLAIM_DETAIL claim_id=bridge.window6_codon_q6.foldbin_codon_positions status=coincidence
\textbf{Statement.} The Window6 Foldbin tail-cube (3 free Boolean coords, seam-return $\rho_6-s_6=3$) corresponds to the
3 codon positions / wobble folding; the bio $Q^{(2)}$ second-order structure is the biological image
of the second fold.

\textbf{Verdict.} \texttt{coincidence}.

\textbf{Why.} foldbin tail-cube vs codon positions: 3=3 count match only; weight-order vs
value-window-marginal-order vs reverse-constraint give 3 different assignments (no forcing); 8-state
reading fails (only 4 tail states); $Q^{(2)}$ abundance reading failed (Route B).

\textbf{Derivation script.} tools/window\_codon\_bridge/experiments/run\_bc3\_foldbin\_codon\_correspondence.py

\subsection{Killed Walk Spectral Correspondence}\label{sec:bridge-killed-walk-spectral-correspondence}
% CLAIM_DETAIL claim_id=bridge.window6_codon_q6.killed_walk_spectral_correspondence status=refuted
\textbf{Statement.} The bio codon-Q6 spectral scalars ($\lambda_M$, $\lambda_R$, $\mu=6\lambda_M-1$) -- where lambda =
(1/6)*spectral radius of the induced 6-cube subgraph adjacency on R and on M=median-closure(R) --
are structurally forced by the Window6 Fibonacci-cube killed-walk operator (same induced-subgraph
spectrum / canonical map), not a numeric coincidence.

\textbf{Verdict.} \texttt{refuted}.

\textbf{Why.} BC2-R1 is refuted: the operators have the same induced-subgraph killed-walk form, but the bio sets
and Window Fibonacci cubes are not the same cube-embedded graph, their exact adjacency
characteristic polynomials differ, and the bio radial factor does not occur in the Window graph
spectra.

\textbf{Derivation script.} tools/window\_codon\_bridge/experiments/run\_bc2\_killed\_walk\_spectrum.py

\subsection{Cyclic Sector Frame Symmetry}\label{sec:bridge-cyclic-sector-frame-symmetry}
% CLAIM_DETAIL claim_id=bridge.window6_codon_q6.cyclic_sector_frame_symmetry status=refuted
\textbf{Statement.} Window6 cyclic Zeckendorf sector rotation corresponds to cyclic rotation of the 3 codon positions as
a code-label-preserving sector character.

\textbf{Verdict.} \texttt{refuted}.

\textbf{Why.} codon-position rotation preserves the amino-acid/stop label for only 4/64 codons (UUU,CCC,AAA,GGG)
and positional degeneracy is strongly asymmetric
(pos1\textasciitilde{}0.042,pos2\textasciitilde{}0.010,pos3\textasciitilde{}0.667), so no
code-label-preserving rotation image exists.

\textbf{Derivation script.} tools/window\_codon\_bridge/experiments/run\_bc4\_cyclic\_sector\_frame\_symmetry.py
