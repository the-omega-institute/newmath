\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates under a translation boundary}
\label{sec:q6-direct-protein-omics-survival-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object considered here is a CDS-by-organism table whose rows are coding sequences with three attached blocks of columns: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates computed after quotienting by amino-acid identity, covariates used to remove elementary compositional explanations, and matched protein-omics abundance readouts.  The residual coordinate block contains nine named coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These columns are not amino-acid usage itself; they are residual codon-topology coordinates left after the amino-acid quotient.  The protein readout used in the finite computation is measured PAXdb protein abundance, transformed as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The direct score $S^{QP}_{ik}$ is read as survival of a residual codon coordinate against a protein-omics column after baseline controls, not as an explanation of how translation occurred.

\paragraph{Rows and joins.}
The protein-omics table is large enough for within-organism controlled regression in each reported organism.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, 3739 proteins were used after a join hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$.  For \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, 4129 proteins were used with hit rate $1.0$.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, 5815 proteins were used with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The control matrix has 24 columns in each organism, with residual ranks $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  The remaining degrees of freedom after residualizing against $Z$ are 3716, 4106, and 5792 respectively, so the direct protein-omics calculation is not an $n=3$ organism-level saturation artifact.

\paragraph{Controlled direct survival.}
The control set removes amino-acid composition, organism-specific baseline structure, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and related composition covariates before measuring the contribution of $Q$.  The degenerate-fit cutoff was $R^2=0.999999$, and the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold was $10^{-12}$.  In the three finite row blocks, all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates survive the direct protein-omics test.  The aggregate direct $Q$-to-$P$ partial readouts are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli}\ \mathrm{K12})&=0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{M. smegmatis}\ \mathrm{MC2\ 155})&=0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae})&=0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding residual response energies after the control fit are 4020.333671802061, 5729.194538214237, and 2958.1865890925337.  The control-explained energies are 432.89050065533684, 183.85037813566998, and 882.6526210803958.  Thus the finite computation supports a direct cross-layer association: residual synonymous codon topology retains protein-abundance information after the declared baseline controls.

\paragraph{Translation readout boundary.}
A separate translation-side finite contact was also computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads and dos Reis wobble weights, yielding modeled tAI codon weights for 61 sense codons in each of three organisms.  The local tRNA payload contained all 20 standard amino-acid families for \textit{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast; the FASTA header counts were 89, 432, and 275, with matched header counts 87, 432, and 275. This is useful as a bounded codon-adaptation contact, but it is not the same experiment as measured ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, matched mRNA, or a same-CDS mediation fit.  The organism count there is only 3, and the recorded boundary explicitly refuses powered survival from that modeled tAI table without at least 40 matched species.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The result is therefore a direct protein-omics survival record with a translation boundary.  It says that the nine residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates carry measurable information about steady-state protein abundance in the reported finite CDS rows after strong compositional residualization.  It does not say that the association is mediated by translation rate, ribosome occupancy, elongation dynamics, or codon adaptation.  A translation-mediated conclusion would require joined rows containing the same CDS identifiers, a measured translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and a mediation comparison showing that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i\to P_k$ signal while the $q_i\to T_j$ step itself survives.

\paragraph{Local refusal of over-interpretation.}
No folded-structure conclusion, physical admissibility conclusion, biochemical-function conclusion, localization conclusion, stability conclusion, phenotype conclusion, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the present $S^{QP}$ values.  Protein abundance is a downstream omics measurement, not a structural assay, not a perturbation experiment, and not a translation-rate observation.  The finite rows justify the narrower statement: under the available PAXdb joins and the stated controls, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual codon coordinates remain predictive of measured protein abundance, while the route from synonymous residual geometry through translation to protein output remains unresolved until a separate matched translation contact supplies it.

\subsection{Rank-powered residual survival against modeled translation weights}
\label{subsec:q6-rank-powered-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Object.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-readout surface in which synonymous codon residual coordinates from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ are compared with organism-level modeled translation weights after quotienting away amino-acid sequence composition. The coordinate side consists of nine residual directions,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The readout side uses the same nine named codon-family directions after computing modeled per-codon tAI weights from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with the dos Reis wobble-weight rule. The resulting surface is therefore not a protein, phenotype, or ribosome-trace object. It is a controlled finite matrix asking whether any amino-acid-residual codon coordinate retains predictive mass against a bounded translation-weight readout.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation-weight readout $T_j$, the tested quantity is a controlled survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ computed only after residualizing against the declared control family $Z_T$. The controls include amino-acid composition, organism indicators, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines. In the powered run, the control design has $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$. The residualized coordinate side has rank $9$, matching the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ directions listed above. The matrix actually evaluated has shape $9\times 9$, and the global controlled readout fit reported for the residualized $Q\to T$ surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z_T) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared positive-entry threshold is $10^{-12}$ for partial-$R^2$ survival. The powered rank check is explicitly separated from the degenerate boundary by comparing this fit with the saturation cutoff $0.999999$; the reported $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ does not hit that saturation boundary.

\paragraph{Finite support.}
The powered run uses $40$ organisms, including vertebrate, plant, fungal, bacterial, cyanobacterial, and algal rows: $homo\_sapiens$, $arabidopsis\_thaliana$, $vitis\_vinifera$, $danio\_rerio$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, $escherichia\_coli$, $xenopus\_tropicalis$, $gallus\_gallus$, multiple $burkholderia$, $mycobacterium$, $pseudomonas$, and $leptospira$ rows, and additional microbial representatives. This matters because the earlier three-organism contact is formally inadequate for a powered survival claim. In that earlier finite contact, only $3$ organisms were used, even though all $81$ modeled-tAI coupling entries exceeded the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$ and the largest absolute reported value was $3801961.031607896$. Those $3$-row numbers are kept as a coupling sanity check, not as a population-level survival result. The powered record changes the status of the modeled-tAI readout by supplying $40$ matched organism rows, rank $23$ controls, rank $9$ residual coordinates, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The readout is modeled tAI, not observed ribosome occupancy. It is assembled from tRNA gene-copy annotations and wobble-weight rules, then projected onto the same nine residual coordinate directions. Thus the claim that survives here is narrower than a claim about measured translation dynamics. The finite-row result supports the existence of a rank-adequate, controlled modeled translation-weight surface for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates. It does not by itself assert elongation speed, ribosome density, translation efficiency measured by ribosome profiling, protein abundance, folding state, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Boundary condition.}
The break condition for this packet is local and explicit: after the controls $Z_T$ and the simpler codon-usage baselines are removed, the packet would fail at this layer if every entry satisfied $S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0$, or if the apparent signal were caused by rank saturation. The reported powered surface avoids the stated saturation artifact because $40$ organisms are present, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z_T)=23$, $\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}}=17$, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579<0.999999$. What is established is therefore a finite controlled modeled-readout survival boundary for the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel. What remains unestablished is any claim that the same coordinates survive into measured ribosome traces, protein output, folded structure, biochemical function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The codon-usage tables, tRNA gene-copy counts, and modeled tAI or stAI weights are admissible contacts only at their own resolution. They do not become direct measurements of translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality merely because they are organized by the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates. A stronger biological claim would require a separate matched contact at the relevant layer: ribosome profiling or another direct translation readout for measured translation, proteomics for protein abundance, structural assays or structure-prediction evidence for folding, perturbational or biochemical assays for function, and an independently powered comparative design for any proposed general law.
