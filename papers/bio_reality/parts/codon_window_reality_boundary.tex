\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}
\label{sec:cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record treats the leucine codon split between the $\mathrm{CUN}$ family and the $\mathrm{UUR}$ family as a bounded organism-level statistic.  The measured object is not a protein, a folded state, or a ribosomal trajectory.  It is a finite comparison between codon-usage summaries and organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy annotations.  The packet therefore asks a narrow question: after controlling for amino-acid composition through the synonymous leucine class, does the sign and magnitude of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ vary with the matching tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio?

\paragraph{Finite coordinate data.}
The code-window check fixes a twenty-element set $M$, with cardinality $20$ and the listed equality $M=\mathrm{WNR}\cup\mathrm{CUN}$ witnessed by
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\\
&\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Inside this finite row, the $\mathrm{CUN}$ side contributes $\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}$ and the $\mathrm{UUR}$ side contributes $\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}$.  This is only a codon-family coordinate.  Its biological use begins only when the coordinate is paired with organism labels and independent tRNA-Leu annotation rows.

\paragraph{Organism rows.}
The loaded organism rows are $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  For these three rows, the recorded values of $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ are

so the sign is not uniform across the measured organisms.  The row count for the controlled cross-organism statistic is $n=3$.

\paragraph{Controlled relation.}
The recorded cross-organism validation reports a positive relation between the leucine codon-family log-ratio and the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio.  The ordinary Pearson value is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value is also

Human and $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ rows have positive $\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ log-ratios, whereas the yeast row is negative.  The statistic is admissible here because it combines two distinct contacts: per-organism codon usage and organism-level tRNA-Leu copy spectra.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The result supports only a bounded translation-layer readback in the following restricted sense: a codon-usage coordinate changes sign across organisms, and the observed finite rows align positively with the paired tRNA-Leu copy-number coordinate after the stated amino-acid composition control.  It does not say that $\mathrm{CUN}$ is globally preferred, that $\mathrm{UUR}$ is globally suppressed, or that sign reversal is a universal rule.  It also does not identify a ribosomal mechanism.  The data summarize coding-sequence composition and genome-annotation counts; they do not observe ribosome dwell time, charged tRNA availability, protein output, or cellular fitness.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
No stronger translation process, folding conclusion, physical admissibility statement, biological function, mechanistic account, or universal cross-organism law follows from these rows unless another independent contact supplies that information.  In particular, the finite values $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, $1.0086640520051702$, the sign vector $(1,-1,1)$, and the partial correlation value $1.0$ are not protein-structure measurements and are not functional assays. \paragraph{Verdict.}
Within the available finite table, the packet is accepted as a narrow organism-level leucine codon-family relation.  The accepted content is exactly this: $M$ has size $20$ with the listed $\mathrm{WNR}\cup\mathrm{CUN}$ membership, three organism rows were loaded, the $\log(\#\mathrm{CUN}/\#\mathrm{UUR})$ signs are not uniform, and the controlled tRNA-Leu relation is recorded with partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$.
