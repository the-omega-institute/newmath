The claim does not identify the window-six geometry with translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality. \subsection{Cross-Organism CUN/UUR Leucine Gate}
\label{sec:cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Packet.}
This BioReality record concerns the organism-level leucine coding contrast between the CUN family and the UUR family. The admissible object is not a protein, a ribosome trajectory, or a cellular mechanism. It is a finite paired statistic: for each organism in the recorded table, take the codon-usage residual for leucine, form the signed log contrast $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$, and compare that contrast with the organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio after an amino-acid composition control. The carrier therefore has four named distinctions: the coordinate $\log(CUN/UUR)$, the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio, the external organism label, and the amino-acid-composition control. The internal structure used here is only coordinate-and-relation structure: it records a finite comparison, not a causal biological story.

\paragraph{Finite code-side contact.}
The companion code-table check fixes the relevant codon family without appealing to an unrecorded biological interpretation. The verified finite set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly the following list:
$$
\begin{aligned}

$$
This row records $M=WNR\cup CUN$ with size $20$. Its role in this chapter is narrow: it anchors the codon-label side of the packet and names the CUN block used in the leucine contrast. \paragraph{Organism rows.}
The measured cross-organism table loaded three organisms: $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$. The recorded log-ratio values for $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ are:

\paragraph{Paired statistic.}
The finite validation row records experiment $cross\_organism\_cun\_uur\_leu\_gate\_validation$ with run id $675fdddb3187$ and status $passed$. This is a realized finite contact between codon-usage summaries and genome-annotation summaries, not an observation of elongation events.

\paragraph{Negative scope.}
The contact is deliberately local. A CUN/UUR sign alone is not a translation-realization claim. A raw log-ratio without the amino-acid-composition control is also not enough. The attached tRNA-Leu gene-copy spectrum is a genome-annotation summary, and the codon-usage table is a sequence-composition summary. They do not establish ribosome-mediated translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal cross-organism law. Any one of those stronger claims would require its own contact: for example ribosome profiling for ribosome dynamics, protein-output evidence for translation products, structural evidence for folding, physical measurement for admissibility, or perturbation and phenotype data for function.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The scientific readback is therefore this: in the finite snapshot containing human, budding yeast, and the K-12 strain of Escherichia coli, the leucine CUN/UUR usage contrast changes sign across organisms and the controlled paired statistic with tRNA-Leu copy ratio is recorded as perfectly positive on $3$ rows. The packet is accepted as a narrow organism-level translation-realization record because it is tied to two external-style contacts, per-organism codon-usage summaries and organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number summaries. Its strength is the explicit finite pairing; its weakness is the small row count and the absence, in this record, of ribosome, protein, structural, functional, physical, or mechanistic measurements.

\paragraph{Conjectural survival statistic.}
For a readout $r$ in the set $\{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{protein\ abundance}\}$ and a residual coordinate $q\in B^*_{Q6}$, the proposed statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z)=J_0(Z)-J_Q(Z,q),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $J_0$ is the score of the controlled baseline and $J_Q$ is the score after adding the single residual coordinate $q$. The conditioning tuple $Z$ must include amino-acid sequence $y$, sequence context $c$, and simpler codon-feature baselines. In the stricter comparison against an $M$-density explanation, the tested statistic is $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)$, where $\Delta_M$ records the baseline contribution of the $M$-density variable. A positive value means only that the coordinate $q$ improves the specified readout under the stated controls and in the sampled organism. A nonpositive value leaves that coordinate unsupported for that readout.

\paragraph{Why the residual basis matters.}
An $M$-only description can express a local codon-topology optimum, but it cannot by itself distinguish all amino-acid-residual contrasts tested here. In particular, the finite basis includes contrasts such as $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ and $K_{AAA}$ against its synonymous alternative, which are invisible if the model has already collapsed to an insufficient codon-density summary. The conjecture therefore asks whether at least one residual coordinate carries information about DNA-to-protein realization after amino-acid composition has been held fixed. The current finite data justify running that test because $B^*_{Q6}$ has nine explicit coordinates and seven nonzero finite rows in the three-organism table. They do not justify claiming that such a survival score has already been observed.

\paragraph{Empirical contacts and current null state.}
Three contact classes are relevant. Per-organism codon-usage tables provide sequence-composition summaries. Organism-level tRNA gene-copy annotations provide anticodon-family spectra, including the Leu, Arg, Ile, Ser, and Thr families needed by the listed residual coordinates. Organism-scoped tAI or stAI weights provide a bounded translation-efficiency readout. These contacts are presently separate. The available memory does not contain an organism-matched table joining codon usage, tRNA copy number, and tAI or stAI weights with amino-acid-composition-controlled estimates of $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)$. For that reason, the correct status is a blocked null rather than weak support or weak refutation.

\paragraph{Break and support conditions.}
The conjecture is supported if some coordinate $q\in B^*_{Q6}$ satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one organism and one permitted readout after amino-acid composition and the $M$-density baseline have been controlled. A sharper failure of the $M$-only explanation would be an organism-dependent sign reversal in the controlled $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ log-ratio that is not captured by $\Delta_M$. The conjecture is not supported by a raw codon-count residual, by a residual variance number alone, or by an unpaired tAI table. The reported finite facts are sufficient to define the coordinates and motivate the comparison; they are not sufficient to decide it.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome-mediated causal story, protein folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function claim, or universal law follows from the finite residual basis. Even a future positive survival score would remain conditional on the organism, readout, and covariates used in $Z$. Higher biological layers require separate empirical contact with those layers. The present chapter records a precise testable residual-coordinate conjecture: after the amino-acid quotient, the only admissible surviving signal must live in $q^\perp$, and the current finite rows specify nine candidate directions for that test.
