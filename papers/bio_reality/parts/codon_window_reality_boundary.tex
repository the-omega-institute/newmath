\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case survival discriminant}
\label{subsec:q6-translation-conditioned-four-case-survival-discriminant}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and readouts.}
This BioReality record tests whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates remain informative after ordinary coding-sequence covariates have already been removed.  The finite carrier is a joined matrix whose rows are matched coding-sequence or protein identifiers and whose columns consist of residual coordinates $q_i$, a translation-layer readout $T$, a protein-omics readout $P$, and a control block $Z$.  In the computed finite rows, $T$ is the per-protein mean modelled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_{c} n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the CDS codon count and $w_c$ is the organism-scoped tAI weight derived from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance after joining to real CDS codon counts.  The control block contains amino-acid composition, organism handling, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA controls, and simpler baseline codon summaries.  The finite computation is therefore not a statement about abstract codon geometry alone; it is a residualized matrix test over organism-matched rows.

\paragraph{Controlled statistics.}
For each coordinate, the discriminant uses two controlled survival surfaces and one mediation check.  Let $\widetilde Q$, $\widetilde T$, and $\widetilde P$ denote residuals after projecting away $Z$.  The two sign tests are the translation-side score $S^{QT}$ and the protein-side score $S^{QP}$, with positive sign interpreted only as finite-row predictive survival under the declared controls.  The mediation check $M^{QTP}$ asks whether a direct residual association from $q_i$ to $P$ is reduced after the translation readout is included.  The four cases are then local matrix labels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{A} &: S^{QT} \leq 0,\quad S^{QP} \leq 0,\\
\mathrm{B} &: S^{QT} > 0,\quad S^{QP} \leq 0,\\
\mathrm{C} &: S^{QT} \leq 0,\quad S^{QP} > 0,\\
\mathrm{D} &: S^{QT} > 0,\quad S^{QP} > 0,\quad M^{QTP}\ \text{shows attenuation after conditioning on }T.
\end{aligned}
$$
Case A is compression of the coordinate away from both readouts.  Case B is translation-level survival without a protein-omics propagation call.  Case C is protein-omics prediction not mediated by the measured translation readout.  Case D is the only label in this chapter that records a translation-conditioned propagation pattern, and even then it remains a predictive finite-row classification rather than a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universal biological principle follows from these rows by itself.  A direct $q_i\to P$ association is not enough for a propagation claim unless the $q_i\to T$ step survives and conditioning on $T$ reduces the direct $q_i\to P$ association.  Case C, if it appears in later panels, must be read only as a protein-omics residual not mediated by the measured translation readout.  Bulk protein abundance is not a folding assay, a function assay, a localization measurement, a stability measurement, or a perturbation result; it is affected by transcript abundance, turnover, sample preparation, ionization efficiency, pathway context, and post-translational state.  A stronger biological claim would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as measured ribosome or translation-efficiency rows for translation realization, structural measurements or predictions for folding, physical assays for admissibility, functional assays for molecular function, and perturbation or phenotype data for system-level interpretation.
