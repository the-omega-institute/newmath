\paragraph{Finite carrier and readouts.}

The tested translation readout is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the coding sequence and $w_c$ is the organism-scoped tAI weight computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.
