\paragraph{Admissible reading.}

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
For a readout $r$ such as a bounded codon-adaptation weight, ribosome-occupancy statistic, or protein-abundance statistic, the intended comparison is conditional on amino-acid sequence $y$, local context $c$, and simpler codon-feature baselines.  Write this conditioning information as $Z$.  For each residual coordinate $q \in B^*_{Q6}$, the test statistic is the improvement of the model carrying $q$ over the baseline lacking it:

\begin{aligned}

\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
If this inequality holds for at least one organism, one readout, and one element of $B^*_{Q6}$, then an $M$-only description is not adequate for that bounded translation-layer readout.  If all such inequalities are nonpositive in a paired dataset, the conjectural residual-survival claim is not supported by that dataset.

\paragraph{Local scope.}
The only supported positive statement at this stage is finite and narrow: the nine-coordinate residual family has a non-null proxy coupling row, and the CUN/UUR leucine contact has a three-organism positive partial-correlation statistic with nonuniform signs.
