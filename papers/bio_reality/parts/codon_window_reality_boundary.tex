\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR Leu gate}\label{sec:cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Question.}
This chapter records a bounded cross-organism test for the leucine codon pair $({\rm CUN},{\rm UUR})$.  The object under inspection is not a protein, a ribosome trace, or a molecular mechanism.  It is a finite organism-indexed table in which the residual coordinate is the signed statistic $\log(\# {\rm CUN}/\# {\rm UUR})$, read together with an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio and an amino-acid-composition control.  The biological label is kept external: it names the organism whose codon-usage and tRNA-copy summaries are being compared, but it is not itself evidence.

\paragraph{Finite codon row.}
The code-level row used here first fixes the codon window $M$ as the union of the WNR row with the CUN leucine block.  The verified cardinality is $|M|=20$, and the listed elements are
$$\begin{aligned}

This row supplies the local codon-coordinate background.  It does not by itself say that a codon distribution is translated, nor does it supply a ribosomal, structural, functional, or physical-admissibility observation.

\paragraph{Organism-indexed measurement.}

\paragraph{Paired tRNA-Leu contact.}
The same finite run pairs the codon-usage residual with an organism-level tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio.  On the three loaded organism rows, the reported Pearson coefficient is $1.0$.  After the amino-acid-composition control specified for the probe, the reported partial Pearson statistic is also $1.0$ with $n=3$:
$$\begin{aligned}

The only claim supported by this row is that, within this finite table, the signed and sized CUN/UUR residual co-varies positively with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after the stated control.  The small row count is part of the content of the claim; it prevents treating the statistic as a population law.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter therefore admits a narrow translation-layer contact in the following sense: codon-usage summaries and tRNA-Leu copy-number summaries are both external organism-level sequence or annotation records, and their controlled pairing is a biologically meaningful constraint on codon-choice realization.  The observation is not a measurement of translation events in the cell.  It does not establish ribosome-mediated dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, biological mechanism, or universality.  Those conclusions would require separate contacts of the corresponding kind, such as ribosome-profiling evidence for translation dynamics or protein-output evidence for structure and function.

\paragraph{Negative boundary.}
A raw sign of $\log({\rm CUN}/{\rm UUR})$ is insufficient.  A packet identifier, including one that contains the words CUN, UUR, tRNA, or Leu, is also insufficient.  A standard genetic-code table can confirm codon labels, but it cannot repair an organism-matched usage/tRNA statistic that has not been measured.  In this chapter the admissible evidence is exactly the finite paired statistic above: three organism rows, non-uniform signs $(+1,-1,+1)$, Pearson $1.0$, controlled partial Pearson $1.0$, and the explicit refusal to promote the row to folding, function, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a global biological rule.

\subsection{Amino-acid-residual codon coordinates for translation-layer testing}
\label{sec:residual-basis-q6-topology-after-aa-quotient-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This record isolates a codon-level residual question that remains after amino-acid sequence has already been held fixed.  The object is not the full codon table and not amino-acid composition alone.  It is the per-organism codon-count profile projected onto the amino-acid-residual basis $B^{*}_{Q6}$, whose named coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
B^{*}_{Q6}=\{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN/UUR},\ Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\ Ser_{UCA/UCG},\ Thr_{ACR/ACY},\ f_{3}\text{-stress antisymmetric}\}.

$$
These nine coordinates ask whether synonymous choices retain translation-layer signal once the amino-acid chain $y$ has been conditioned on.  The readout is organism-scoped: for each $q\in B^{*}_{Q6}$ and each admissible readout $r$, estimate the conditional survival contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{Q}(r\mid Z)=J_{0}(Z)-J_{Q}(Z,q),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes at least the amino-acid sequence $y$, local context $c$, and simpler codon-feature baselines.  A positive value is meaningful only as a bounded improvement for that readout under that conditioning set; it is not a free-standing biological law.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate facts.}

\paragraph{Why the residual basis is not the same as $M$-density.}
The $M$ quotient compresses a useful local codon geometry, but the residual coordinates above are designed precisely to ask questions that $M$-density alone cannot ask.  In particular, a model that only sees $M$-membership cannot distinguish the Leu $CUN/UUR$ split or the $AAA/AAG$ Lys split as residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting.  The point of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ is therefore not to replace amino-acid control, but to test what survives after that control is imposed.  The finite facts already show that the residual basis is not empty: it has nine named coordinates and seven nonzero finite residuals in the three-organism contact.  What remains open is whether any of those residuals improves a translation-layer readout after paired organism-level controls are supplied.

\paragraph{Translation-layer test.}
The operational conjecture is conditional.  If the $Q6$ residual topology participates in DNA-to-protein realization beyond amino-acid sequence, then there should exist at least one $q\in B^{*}_{Q6}$ and at least one readout
$$
\begin{aligned}
r\in\{\mathrm{tAI},\ \mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\ \mathrm{protein\ abundance}\}
\end{aligned}
$$
for which the controlled survival score is positive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{Q}(r\mid Z,\Delta_{M})>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Delta_{M}$ denotes the simpler $M$-density baseline, and $Z$ must include amino-acid composition or the amino-acid sequence itself.  A useful failure mode is equally sharp: if every $q\in B^{*}_{Q6}$ has $S_{Q}(r\mid Z,\Delta_{M})\leq 0$ for every organism and every admitted readout, then the residual basis has not survived this translation-layer test.  A second possible break condition is a controlled organism-to-organism sign reversal in the Leu $CUN/UUR$ log-ratio that is not captured by $\Delta_{M}$.

\paragraph{Reality contacts and present blockage.}
Three contact classes are relevant.  Per-organism codon-usage tables can test sequence-composition and codon-distribution claims, but they do not observe ribosome dynamics.  Organism-level tRNA gene-copy annotations can supply anticodon-family spectra, including Leu, Arg, Ile, Ser, and Thr families, but copy number alone is still an annotation summary.  Organism-scoped tAI or stAI weights can supply a bounded translation-efficiency readout, but these weights must be kept separate from the coordinate construction itself.  The present finite row has those contact classes separated rather than paired.  It does not yet contain an organism-matched table combining codon usage, tRNA copy numbers, and tAI or stAI weights into controlled estimates of $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)$.  For that reason, the translation-realization assertion is a testable conjecture with a blocked statistic, not a confirmed biological result.

\paragraph{Local limits of interpretation.}
The finite residual computation permits a coordinate statement: in three organisms, the amino-acid-residual $Q6$ basis has nine elements and seven nonzero finite residuals above threshold $0.01$.  It also permits a test design: compare each residual coordinate against an $M$-density baseline under amino-acid and context controls.  It does not by itself establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, ribosome-mediated causation, protein abundance, or universality.  Any promotion to one of those levels requires a separate contact that measures that level directly, together with the same conditioning discipline.  Until the paired statistic is computed, absence of a positive survival score is not evidence for $M$-only sufficiency, and presence of nonzero codon-count residual variance is not evidence for translation-layer realization.

\subsection{A residual survival matrix for translation readouts}
\label{sec:bioreality-translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Coordinate object.}
The record considered here is a finite matrix question at the interface between synonymous codon geometry and organism-matched translation measurements.  Let $q_i$ range over the nine residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\quad Arg_{AGR},\quad Ile_{AUA},\quad Leu_{CUN/UUR},\quad Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\quad Ser_{UCA/UCG},\quad Thr_{ACR/ACY},\quad f3_{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are evaluated after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed, so the question is not whether amino-acid composition predicts a readout, but whether a remaining synonymous coordinate improves prediction after the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and simpler-codon-density covariates have already been charged to the null model.  For a translation-related readout $r_j$, the intended entry is a controlled survival score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\qquad
Z=(AA, organism, GC3, length, M\text{-}density),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $S_{ij}>0$ means that the residual coordinate $q_i$ gives a strict partial description-length improvement, equivalently a positive controlled partial-$R^2$ contribution under the declared baseline.  The readout family is restricted to bounded translation-layer contacts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, and matched protein abundance when that abundance is treated only as a downstream measured readout and not as evidence for folding or function.

\paragraph{Finite rows already computed.}
Two finite computations are available.  First, a proxy coupling computation was run in the $61$-codon count space using the nine listed residual coordinates against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The null break condition for that proxy row was that every coordinate satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  This condition was not met: all nine entries were nonzero, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
The loaded organisms for that finite row were $Homo\ sapiens$, $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, and $Escherichia\ coli\ K12$.  This computation is evidence that the residual coordinate system has a nontrivial proxy contact with a translation-adjacent tRNA-copy spectrum.  It is not yet the survival matrix $S_{ij}$, because no Z-controlled tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, or protein-abundance entry has been estimated for the full readout family.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A second finite row isolates the Leu CUN/UUR gate across the same three organisms.  The reported Pearson statistic is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The log-ratio row is

\paragraph{Survival criterion.}
The chapter-level support condition is deliberately stronger than proxy coupling.  The matrix is supported only if at least one pair $(q_i,r_j)$ satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the full control set $Z$ and after ablation of the simpler codon-density baseline.  The corresponding break condition is that every controlled entry satisfy $S_{ij}\leq 0$, or equivalently that none of the nine residual coordinates improves the controlled description of any permitted translation readout.  The proxy matrix has already passed a weaker nonzero-coupling test with threshold $0.01$, but the survival test remains open because the memory contains no completed organism-matched matrix of controlled partial-$R^2$ values against the direct readout family.

\paragraph{Contact discipline.}
Codon-usage tables supply per-organism sequence summaries.  tRNA gene-copy annotations supply genome-level anticodon-family counts.  tAI or stAI weights supply organism-scoped modelled adaptation weights.  Ribosome-profiling or matched RNA-seq contacts can supply translation-efficiency residuals when identifiers and conditions are paired.  Proteomics contacts can supply protein-abundance readouts when mapped to the same coding sequences.  These contacts have different evidential roles and cannot be interchanged.  In particular, tRNA gene copy is not tRNA abundance, not tRNA charging, and not ribosome occupancy.  Matched mRNA abundance is a covariate for translation-efficiency residualization, not itself a translation-readout survival entry.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present record does not establish translation realization from the proxy coupling alone.  It also does not establish folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, a ribosome-level mechanism, protein-output causality, or a universal biological law.  Any such conclusion requires a separate matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.  The only positive finite facts recorded here are the nonzero nine-coordinate proxy coupling row with maximum absolute value $1882464.1106377328$, and the three-organism Leu CUN/UUR row with Pearson and partial Pearson both equal to $1.0$ while the signs remain $(1,-1,1)$.  The survival-matrix conjecture therefore remains a precise open test: the residual coordinates are not numerically inert in the available proxy rows, but positive survival against direct translation readouts has not yet been computed.
