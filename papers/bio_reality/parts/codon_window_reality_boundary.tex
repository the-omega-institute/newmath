\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS readout surface in which synonymous residual coordinates from $B^*_{Q6}$ are compared with organism-scoped codon-adaptation readouts after amino-acid quotient residualization. The coordinate side is not the raw codon table. For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the relevant object is the amino-acid-orthogonal remainder $q_i-P_{\tau}q_i$, so any effect that is already explained by amino-acid sequence composition is removed before the cross-layer score is evaluated. The readout side is likewise not an unconditioned translation claim: each $T_j$ is evaluated only after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.

\paragraph{Powered modeled-readout row.}
The powered finite row uses $40$ organisms and computes a $9\times 9$ modeled $S^{QT}$ matrix between the $9$ residual coordinates and $9$ modeled tAI readout columns. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress .
\end{aligned}
$$
The same nine names occur as the modeled tAI readout columns. The control design has $24$ control columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $17$ after residualization. The residual coordinate design has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$. The reported modeled cross-layer fit is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive partial-$R^2$ threshold in the finite row is $10^{-12}$, while the rank-degeneracy cutoff is $0.999999$. Since $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ is below that degeneracy cutoff and the panel size is $40$, this row is rank-adequate for a modeled tAI or stAI readout boundary.

\paragraph{Rejected small-panel boundary.}
The earlier three-organism modeled-tAI row is retained only as a boundary case. It computed the same named $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual family over $61$ sense codons using modeled tAI weights from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The bounded coupling screen found $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. This does not count as powered survival, because $n=3$ can create the all-$1.0$ rank projection artifact. Its role is diagnostic: it shows that the modeled contact can be assembled, not that population-level translation survival has been established.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
For each coordinate-readout pair, the admissible statistic is a controlled entry $S^{QT}_{ij}=S_{q_i}(T_j\mid Z_T)$ after the declared residualization and baseline ablation. The local support condition is strictly finite:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists(i,j)\quad S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
with the positive entry exceeding the stated partial-$R^2$ threshold and with no rank saturation. The local break condition is the complementary finite outcome: every controlled entry satisfies $S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0$, or the apparent signal is exhausted by rank saturation or by the simpler codon-usage baselines. In that case, $B^*_{Q6}$ remains a code-read or bounded codon-count residual basis for this layer, rather than a surviving modeled translation-readout coordinate.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite row supports a bounded modeled translation-readout statement: under the available $40$-organism panel, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and modeled tAI readout columns admit a rank-adequate controlled $S^{QT}$ computation with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$. This is not ribosome profiling, not measured elongation speed, not measured ribosome occupancy, and not proteomics. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are organism-level codon-adaptation readbacks built from external tRNA gene-copy and wobble-weight information; they do not by themselves observe ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No statement about measured translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter alone. Such claims require separate matched contacts at the relevant layer and a new controlled statistic. In particular, a positive modeled $S^{QT}$ entry is not evidence for $S^{QP}$, is not a mediation claim $M^{QTP}$, and does not promote any single coordinate, including $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, into a population-level biological law. The chapter therefore closes only the finite modeled-readout boundary: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive this modeled tAI or stAI readout test only to the extent recorded by the controlled, rank-adequate $S^{QT}$ surface.
