\paragraph{Carrier.} The record is carried by a finite surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  Here $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}$; $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, in this finite run the per-protein mean modeled tAI; and $p_k$ is measured matched protein abundance, read as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb rows joined to real coding-sequence codon counts.  The tAI readout is not a ribosome-footprint measurement: it is computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights by
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(\mathrm{CDS}) &= \frac{\sum_{c} n_c(\mathrm{CDS})\,w_c}{\sum_{c} n_c(\mathrm{CDS})},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count and $w_c$ is the modeled codon-adaptation weight.

\paragraph{Finite rows.} The powered mediation run joined $3739$ proteins in \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $4129$ proteins in \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ proteins in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding all-tRNA copy totals used for the modeled tAI layer were $90$, $49$, and $275$, respectively.  The raw codon-weight construction needed one geometric-mean fill in the two bacterial rows and no fill in yeast.  A separate modeled-tAI contact check also confirmed all $61$ sense codons in the tAI weight table for \textit{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast, with zero raw-$W$ fallback count in each of those three contact rows.

\subsection{Controlled Decomposition}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-decomposition}

\paragraph{Control geometry.} The statistical test residualizes against a control block with amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and associated finite baseline columns.  In the three organism-level mediation rows the control design has $24$ columns and numerical rank $23$.  After this residualization the remaining degrees of freedom are $3716$ in \textit{E. coli}, $4106$ in \textit{M. smegmatis}, and $5792$ in yeast.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block has rank $9$ for both the protein-abundance residual and the translation-readout residual; the translation block has rank $1$; and the joint $(Q,T)$ block has rank $10$ in all three organisms.  Thus the reported mediation numbers are not rank artifacts of a collapsed design matrix.

\paragraph{Translation contact.} Before the protein-abundance decomposition, the modeled-tAI contact showed nonzero bounded coupling for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual family.  Across the finite modeled matrix there were $81$ entries above the $0.01$ coupling threshold, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$ and the same maximum on the diagonal coordinate summary.  This is a modeled codon-adaptation contact only.  Its organism count is $3$, so it is explicitly below the $40$-organism threshold required for a powered multi-organism survival statement.  The contact therefore supports a bounded translation-readout route, not a measured ribosome-occupancy or universal translation law.

\paragraph{Mediation scalar.} The cross-layer row computes a joint nine-dimensional $X_Q$ mediation scalar by comparing the direct $Q \to P$ association with the association left after conditioning on $T$.  The finite decomposition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $R^2(Q\to P)=0.10767526677985896$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.04167869969498872$, the indirect difference is $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  For \textit{M. smegmatis}, $R^2(Q\to P)=0.03209009170649934$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.029395293228368185$, the indirect difference is $0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.  For yeast, $R^2(Q\to P)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  All three mediated fractions lie inside the unit interval.

\paragraph{Energy audit.} The same rows give a scale check on the residualized regression problem.  In \textit{E. coli}, the $T$ residual energy is $0.4728468725814029$ and the protein-abundance residual energy is $4020.333671802061$; the $T$ block explains $582.5912438239341$ units of residual protein-abundance energy, while adding $T$ after $Q$ improves by $317.26302294928666$.  In \textit{M. smegmatis}, the corresponding values are $0.041191576348982066$, $5729.194538214237$, $15.813547327443837$, and $0.37452260494680445$.  In yeast they are $6.90537888244409$, $2958.1865890925337$, $941.8858618613751$, and $104.11162139652299$.  The mediated signal is therefore strongest in yeast, substantial in \textit{E. coli}, and weak but positive in \textit{M. smegmatis} under this modeled-tAI readout.

\subsection{Boundary of Interpretation}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-boundary}

\paragraph{Positive claim.} The finite record supports a controlled statistical cross-layer relation: for the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block, modeled per-protein tAI absorbs a positive fraction of the matched protein-abundance association in each of the three joined organism rows.  The claim is not that any single synonymous residual coordinate directly realizes protein abundance.  The admissible readout is the mediated matrix $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$, with the translation readout kept as an explicit middle layer and with direct $Q_i\to P_k$ evidence treated as insufficient unless the translation step is present.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the measured layer.} The calculation does not establish translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality.  Modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation annotation derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights, not an observed ribosome-profiling experiment.  Conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional coding-sequence and protein-abundance rows is a statistical decomposition, not a perturbation, rescue, causal intervention, or mechanism proof.  Protein abundance is also not isolated here from mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, or pathway context.  Any claim at those layers would require a separate matched contact and a new finite statistic at that layer.

\paragraph{Verdict.} The chapter is ready as a bounded BioReality record for the cross-layer protein-abundance boundary.  Its strongest finite facts are the joined row counts, the rank-sufficient residualized design, and the organism-specific mediated fractions $0.612922252793667$, $0.08397602919861291$, and $0.9491551041216973$.  Its local limitation is equally part of the result: the row proves neither a biological law nor a molecular mechanism.  It says only that, under the declared controls and this modeled translation readout, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a finite positive mediated protein-abundance decomposition.
