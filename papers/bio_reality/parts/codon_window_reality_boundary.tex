\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates under a translation boundary}
\label{sec:q6-direct-protein-omics-survival-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object considered here is a CDS-by-organism table whose rows are coding sequences with three attached blocks of columns: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates computed after quotienting by amino-acid identity, covariates used to remove elementary compositional explanations, and matched protein-omics abundance readouts.  The residual coordinate block contains nine named coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These columns are not amino-acid usage itself; they are residual codon-topology coordinates left after the amino-acid quotient.  The protein readout used in the finite computation is measured PAXdb protein abundance, transformed as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The direct score $S^{QP}_{ik}$ is read as survival of a residual codon coordinate against a protein-omics column after baseline controls, not as an explanation of how translation occurred.

\paragraph{Rows and joins.}
The protein-omics table is large enough for within-organism controlled regression in each reported organism.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, 3739 proteins were used after a join hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$.  For \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, 4129 proteins were used with hit rate $1.0$.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, 5815 proteins were used with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The control matrix has 24 columns in each organism, with residual ranks $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  The remaining degrees of freedom after residualizing against $Z$ are 3716, 4106, and 5792 respectively, so the direct protein-omics calculation is not an $n=3$ organism-level saturation artifact.

\paragraph{Controlled direct survival.}
The control set removes amino-acid composition, organism-specific baseline structure, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and related composition covariates before measuring the contribution of $Q$.  The degenerate-fit cutoff was $R^2=0.999999$, and the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold was $10^{-12}$.  In the three finite row blocks, all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates survive the direct protein-omics test.  The aggregate direct $Q$-to-$P$ partial readouts are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli}\ \mathrm{K12})&=0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{M. smegmatis}\ \mathrm{MC2\ 155})&=0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae})&=0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding residual response energies after the control fit are 4020.333671802061, 5729.194538214237, and 2958.1865890925337.  The control-explained energies are 432.89050065533684, 183.85037813566998, and 882.6526210803958. A separate translation-side finite contact was also computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads and dos Reis wobble weights, yielding modeled tAI codon weights for 61 sense codons in each of three organisms. \paragraph{Interpretation.}
The result is therefore a direct protein-omics survival record with a translation boundary.  It says that the nine residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates carry measurable information about steady-state protein abundance in the reported finite CDS rows after strong compositional residualization. \subsection{Rank-powered translation survival for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:q6-rank-powered-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
The record tests whether the synonymous residual coordinates in $B^{*}_{Q6}$ retain measurable contact with a bounded translation-readout layer after the amino-acid quotient has removed ordinary amino-acid-composition information.  The finite carrier is an organism-by-CDS readout surface: each row is evaluated through codon-usage summaries, organism-level tRNA gene-copy information, and modeled tAI or stAI weights, while each $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate is used only through its quotient residual $q_i-P_{\tau}q_i$.  The tested coordinates are the nine residual directions \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  The translation readout uses the same nine projected modeled-tAI columns, computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts together with dos Reis wobble weights.  Thus the matrix under review has shape $9\times 9$, with one coordinate axis and one modeled translation-weight axis for each listed residual direction.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation-readout coordinate $T_j$, the reported boundary statistic is a controlled survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$.  The controls are not optional: translation readout is evaluated only after residualizing against amino-acid composition, organism effects, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.  In the powered run, the control design has $24$ columns, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after control removal.  The rank of the residualized coordinate design is $9$, so the tested coordinate panel is not collapsed to a one-dimensional artifact.  The scalar coupled fit for the powered modeled readout is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{X}) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold recorded for partial-$R^2$ survival is $10^{-12}$, and the rank-degeneracy ceiling is $0.999999$.  Since $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ is below the degeneracy ceiling and the organism panel has $40$ rows, the powered modeled-tAI result escapes the all-perfect-fit boundary that invalidates very small panels.

Those numbers show that the modeled weight construction was populated, but they do not establish powered survival.  With only $3$ organisms, the projection problem is rank-saturated for this kind of cross-organism matrix and is therefore treated as a null boundary rather than as biological survival.
