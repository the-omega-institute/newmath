\subsection{Local Verdict}

\paragraph{Supported finite statement.}

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This chapter does not claim observed ribosome occupancy, direct translation efficiency, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation response, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is a translation annotation, not a ribosome-profiling experiment.  Conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional CDS and protein-abundance rows is a statistical mediation calculation, not a proof that translation dynamics caused the protein-abundance differences.  Protein abundance is also not isolated here from transcript abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or assay bias.  Any stronger biological statement would require a separate matched contact at the relevant layer and a new controlled finite-row statistic on the same coordinate route.
