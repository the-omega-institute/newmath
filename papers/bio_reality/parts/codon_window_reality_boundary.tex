$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall i,j\quad S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)\leq 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Under the null, the nine residual coordinates are absorbed by the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density baseline for the tested translation readouts.  Under support, at least one residual coordinate survives as a controlled readout predictor.
