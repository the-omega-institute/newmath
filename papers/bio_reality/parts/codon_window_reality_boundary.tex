\paragraph{Current coupling statistic.}
On the modeled-tAI surface, the computed bounded-coupling table has $81$ entries above threshold.  The threshold is $0.01$, the reported maximum absolute entry is $3801961.031607896$, and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is also $3801961.031607896$. Thus the present positive finite contact is a modeled codon-adaptation coupling contact, not a ribosome-seq, translation-efficiency, or protein-abundance validation.

\paragraph{Mediation rule.}
The cross-layer claim is intentionally stricter than a direct residual-to-protein association.  Let $Z$ denote the control block containing amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance.  The admissible mediated chain is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,\\
S_{ij}(q_i,t_j\mid Z) &> 0,\\
\Delta_{ijk}(q_i,t_j,p_k\mid Z) &> 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first inequality records survival of the residual coordinate at the translation layer.  The second records a downstream improvement for matched protein abundance after the translation readout is kept in the model.  A direct association $q_i\to p_k$ is not counted as support when the $q_i\to t_j$ step is null, unavailable, or only represented by a contact name rather than organism-matched rows.

\paragraph{Matched mRNA control.}
The mRNA contact is used only to separate transcription-layer abundance from translation-layer output.  In practice, this means that a ribosome or translation-efficiency readout must be evaluated after the corresponding mRNA abundance has been included as a control, or by using a translation-efficiency construction such as $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$ when the paired measurements justify that quotient.  Transcript abundance alone is therefore not a translation survival entry, not a protein-abundance entry, and not a mechanism assay.  Its role is to prevent a residual coordinate from being promoted because it is merely correlated with mRNA level.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance contact.}
The proteomics contact is a downstream abundance route keyed by matched protein identifiers, such as UniProt accessions mapped back to CDS identifiers.  A valid row must join the residual coordinate, the translation readout, the protein abundance value, and the control block in the same organism or matched experimental design.  Steady-state protein abundance can test downstream abundance association, but it cannot determine folding, mature molecular function, physical admissibility, or organismal phenotype.  Turnover, ionization efficiency, sample preparation, and isoform aggregation remain noise sources, so a positive abundance mediation row would still be an abundance result rather than a full mechanistic explanation.

\paragraph{Null condition.}
The finite mediation surface remains bounded at translation-readout or codon-count residual status if every controlled mediated improvement is nonpositive.  Equivalently, if all admissible rows satisfy
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}(q_i,t_j\mid Z) &\leq 0
\quad\text{or}\quad
\Delta_{ijk}(q_i,t_j,p_k\mid Z) \leq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
then no protein-realization fitting claim is made.  This null result would not show that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates are biologically irrelevant; it would only show that this particular cross-layer route has not survived the stated translation and protein-abundance tests.
