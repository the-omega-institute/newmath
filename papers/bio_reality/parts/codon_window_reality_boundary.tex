\paragraph{Mediation rule.}
The cross-layer claim is the following bounded mediation test.  For a candidate row $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, let $Z$ denote the declared control block: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance.  A protein-abundance mediation row is admissible only when the translation edge survives first:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow t_j \quad &\text{is estimated under } Z,\\
q_i \longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k \quad &\text{is then estimated with matched protein abundance},\\
q_i \longrightarrow p_k \quad &\text{alone is not counted as protein realization.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus a direct association between a residual coordinate and a protein-abundance readout is treated as incomplete unless the intervening translation readout is positive under the same control discipline.  Matched mRNA abundance separates translation-layer signal from transcription-only abundance.  Amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and M-density remain controls; they are not promoted to residual coordinates in this chapter.

\paragraph{Boundary of the present evidence.}
The current finite-row evidence stops at modeled tAI coupling.  The powered survival condition is explicitly unmet for measured translation survival because only $3$ organisms are present, while the recorded break rule requires more species before a powered survival claim is requested.  In particular, the existing finite table does not contain same-CDS joined rows for codon counts, ribosome profiling or translation efficiency, matched protein abundance, and matched mRNA abundance.  It also does not contain a controlled matrix showing that conditioning on $t_j$ reduces or absorbs a direct $q_i\to p_k$ association.

Consequently the local conclusion is conditional.  If a future finite table supplies organism-matched rows and at least one coordinate $q_i$ has a positive controlled translation edge $q_i\to t_j$ that also mediates a downstream matched protein-abundance edge $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$, then the row enters the translation-mediated protein-abundance layer. No translation realization follows from residual geometry alone.  No ribosome-occupancy claim follows from modeled tAI or stAI weights alone. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from this row store without a separate layer-specific measurement contact and its own controlled statistic. \subsection{Direct protein--omics survival with a translation boundary}\label{sec:q6-direct-protein-omics-survival-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}

\paragraph{Direct survival statistic.}
Let $X_Q$ denote the nine-column residual-coordinate matrix, let $Z$ denote the baseline control matrix, and let $y_P$ denote the measured protein-abundance vector.  The reported statistic is the protein--omics survival of $X_Q$ after $Z$ has been removed from both sides.  In finite-row form, the tested projection is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde X_Q &= (I-\Pi_Z)X_Q,\\
\widetilde y_P &= (I-\Pi_Z)y_P,\\
S^{QP} &= R^2(\widetilde y_P \sim \widetilde X_Q).
\end{aligned}
$$

For \emph{M. smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $R^2_{QP}=0.03209009170649934$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving. The residual protein-abundance energies after control projection were $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$, while the energies explained by the control block were $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$. \paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS readout surface on which the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are compared with translation-readout weights after amino-acid quotient residualization. The coordinate side consists of nine residual directions,

The intended statistic is the controlled translation-survival boundary
$S^{QT}_{ij}=S_{q_i}(T_j\mid Z_T)$, where $q_i$ is a residual codon coordinate, $T_j$ is a modeled translation-weight readout, and $Z_T$ contains amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.

\paragraph{Powered finite-row computation.}
The powered run uses $40$ organisms and a $9\times 9$ modeled translation-readout matrix. The residualization design has $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after projection away from $Z_T$. $$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z_T) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_Q) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The support threshold for a positive partial readout contribution is $10^{-12}$, while the rank-degeneracy guard is $R^2\geq 0.999999$. Since the powered run has $40$ organisms, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579<0.999999$, this finite row set is not the rank-saturated all-perfect projection artifact that invalidated the three-organism boundary case. \paragraph{Survival statement.}
On the powered $40$-organism surface, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis survives the amino-acid quotient and the declared controls as a modeled tAI or stAI readout boundary. In finite terms, the result is a controlled $9$-coordinate by $9$-readout comparison with sufficient rank support, nondegenerate global fit, and an explicitly recorded positive-entry threshold. The claim is narrower: after residualizing against amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines, the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate surface remains coupled to modeled codon-adaptation weights in a powered multi-organism panel.
