\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}

$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}

\paragraph{Blocked null.}

The absence of the joined statistic is not evidence that $M$-only models are sufficient, and it is not evidence that residual coordinates matter for translation.  It is a blocked test.  A decisive support event would be one organism and one readout for which some $q\in B^{*}_{Q6}$ has $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)>0$.  A decisive failure event for the stated probe would be a controlled table in which all nine residual coordinates have $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)\leq 0$ across the declared readout class, with the amino-acid quotient and context controls retained.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The finite codon-count calculation alone does not assert ribosome-mediated translation, protein-output causality, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or a universal biological law. Even if a positive $S_Q$ is later observed, its interpretation remains conditional on the chosen organisms, readout, loss, and conditioning variables; it must not be promoted to folding, function, physical admissibility, mechanism, or universality without an additional contact that directly tests that higher layer.
