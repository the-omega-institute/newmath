\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates under a translation boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object considered here is a CDS-by-organism table whose rows are coding sequences with three attached blocks of columns: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates computed after quotienting by amino-acid identity, covariates used to remove elementary compositional explanations, and matched protein-omics abundance readouts. $$
These columns are not amino-acid usage itself; they are residual codon-topology coordinates left after the amino-acid quotient.  The protein readout used in the finite computation is measured PAXdb protein abundance, transformed as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, joined to real CDS codon counts. The remaining degrees of freedom after residualizing against $Z$ are 3716, 4106, and 5792 respectively, so the direct protein-omics calculation is not an $n=3$ organism-level saturation artifact.

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}

Each coordinate is interpreted only through its amino-acid-quotient residual form. $$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
with a positive partial-$R^2$ threshold of $10^{-12}$ and a degenerate-rank cutoff at $0.999999$. These finite ranks are the essential guardrail: the claim is evaluated after controls for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.
