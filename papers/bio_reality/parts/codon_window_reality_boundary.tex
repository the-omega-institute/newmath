\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record fixes a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The first coordinate $q_i$ ranges over the computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, the middle coordinate $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and the final coordinate $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  A protein-abundance association is only read as cross-layer evidence after the same residual has first passed through a translation-readout coordinate.  In the present finite calculation the residual list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These nine coordinates are evaluated against a scalar per-protein modeled-tAI readout and a matched measured protein-abundance readout.  Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance remain in the control side of the calculation; they are not promoted into the residual coordinate set.

\paragraph{Translation-readout contact.}
The translation contact used here is modeled tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  In the bounded tAI calculation, the sense-codon count is $61$ in each of \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with zero raw-$W$ fallback count equal to $0$ for all three organisms.  The local GtRNAdb payload contains all standard amino-acid families: for \emph{E. coli} K12 it has $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, $2$ unmatched headers, and $20$ standard amino-acid families; for human it has $432$ headers, all $432$ matched, and $20$ families; for yeast it has $275$ headers, all $275$ matched, and $20$ families.  The modeled coupling table has $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This establishes a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not a ribosome-profiling observation and not a protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Joined protein-abundance rows.}
The downstream readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The joined finite rows are organism-specific: \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 contributes $3739$ joined CDS/protein rows using the \emph{E. coli} tRNA table with all-tRNA copy total $90$ and one zero raw-$W$ value filled by the geometric mean; \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 contributes $4129$ joined rows using its matched tRNA table with all-tRNA copy total $49$ and one geometric-mean fill; yeast contributes $5815$ joined rows with all-tRNA copy total $275$ and no geometric-mean fill.  The finite rank checks are nondegenerate in all three organisms: the control rank is $23$, the residualized $X$ rank is $9$, the residualized $T$ rank is $1$, and the joint $(X,T)$ rank is $10$.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively.

\paragraph{Mediation scalar.}
Let $X_Q$ denote the nine-dimensional residual block, $T$ the per-protein modeled-tAI scalar, and $Y$ the matched protein-abundance scalar after the stated controls.  The reported finite statistic is a statistical decomposition of $X_Q \to Y$ by conditioning on $T$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(X_Q\to Y)-R^2(X_Q\to Y\mid T)}{R^2(X_Q\to Y)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $R^2(X_Q\to Y)=0.10767526677985896$, $R^2(X_Q\to Y\mid T)=0.04167869969498872$, the indirect difference is $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  For \emph{M. smegmatis}, the corresponding values are $0.03209009170649934$, $0.029395293228368185$, $0.0026947984781311544$, and $0.08397602919861291$.  For yeast they are $0.29837624994140827$, $0.0151709093608293$, $0.28320534058057895$, and $0.9491551041216973$.  All three mediated fractions lie in the unit interval, so the finite decomposition is internally well formed for the joined rows.

\paragraph{Control-residual energy audit.}
The residual-energy audit separates the translation readout from a transcription-only or codon-count-only association.  In \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, the residual energy of $T$ after controls is $0.4728468725814029$ and the residual energy of $Y$ is $4020.333671802061$; $T$ explains $582.5912438239341$ energy on $Y$, while its incremental improvement given $X_Q$ is $317.26302294928666$.  The direct residual block still has $167.56227978068944$ improvement on $Y$ after conditioning on $T$.  In \emph{M. smegmatis}, the corresponding quantities are $0.041191576348982066$, $5729.194538214237$, $15.813547327443837$, $0.37452260494680445$, and $168.41135341317295$.  In yeast they are $6.90537888244409$, $2958.1865890925337$, $941.8858618613751$, $104.11162139652299$, and $44.87838061554362$.  Thus the strongest mediated fraction occurs in yeast, the weakest in \emph{M. smegmatis}, and the bacterial \emph{E. coli} row remains intermediate.

\paragraph{Scientific verdict.}
The finite-row result supports a bounded cross-layer relation: in three organism-specific joined tables, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a nonnegative statistical component that is absorbed by a modeled per-protein tAI coordinate before matched protein abundance is read.  The row counts and rank checks are sufficient for this finite decomposition, and the reported $M^{QTP}$ values are positive in all three organisms.  The result is not read as a causal effect, not read as ribosome occupancy, and not read as direct protein realization from synonymous residual geometry.  It is a controlled mediation matrix over finite rows: $q_i$ is admitted to the protein-abundance side only through a translation-readout coordinate $t_j$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No observed translation dynamics, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological rule follows from these rows alone.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts is not ribosome profiling, conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional CDS/protein rows is not perturbational mechanism evidence, and steady-state protein abundance is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context.  A stronger claim at any one of those layers would require a separate matched contact at that layer and a fresh controlled statistic on the same coordinate route.

\subsection{Gate Logic and Null Boundary}\label{sec:q6-translation-gated-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Translation gate.} The conjectural route is not a direct map $q_i \mapsto$ structure. Its admissible form is
$$\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,\\
(q_i,T_j) &\longrightarrow \mathrm{SO},
\end{aligned}$$
where $T_j$ is a translation readout and $\mathrm{SO}$ is a separately attached structural-order readback. A structural-order entry is therefore interpretable only after the same coordinate has survived a translation-readout test under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls, together with the relevant transcript and abundance controls when those layers are used. The present verified run supplies the structural-confidence association and rank evidence, but it does not provide a same-coordinate $q_i \to T_j$ survival matrix, a $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation table, or p-values for individual coordinates.

\subsection{Rank-powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ survival against modeled translation readouts}
\label{sec:q6-rank-powered-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This chapter records a bounded cross-layer test for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting.  The finite surface has organism rows, residual coordinates $q_i$, and translation-readout coordinates $T_j$ obtained from codon-adaptation weights.  The biological object is not a protein, not a ribosome trace, and not a phenotype.  It is a controlled organism-by-coordinate readout surface in which each candidate $q_i$ is first separated from amino-acid-sequence information by replacing it with its residual component $q_i-P_{\tau}q_i$, and each $T_j$ is evaluated only after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.

\paragraph{Finite-row construction.}
The powered run uses $40$ organism rows and $9$ $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The same nine labels are used as the modeled tAI readout columns after projection onto the synonymous-residual coordinate basis.  The control design has $24$ control columns, numerical rank $23$, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom after the control projection.  The residual coordinate block has rank $9$, so the powered panel is not the three-row saturation artifact.  The recorded aggregate controlled fit is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared positive-survival threshold for a partial-$R^{2}$ entry is $10^{-12}$, and the rank-degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$.  Since the aggregate $R^{2}_{QT}$ is below the degeneracy cutoff and the panel has $40$ rows, this run is admissible as a powered modeled-readout calculation rather than as a rank-filled three-row projection.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
For each coordinate-readout pair, the intended boundary statistic is $S^{QT}_{ij}=S_{q_i}(T_j\mid Z_T)$, where $Z_T$ contains the listed composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and baseline-codon controls.  The powered finite run reports a computed $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix together with the aggregate $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  This supports the local statement that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis has a non-null modeled translation-readout interface under rank-adequate organism support.  The supported layer is therefore the modeled codon-adaptation readout layer, not measured ribosome occupancy and not protein realization.  The break condition for this chapter is concrete: if every controlled entry satisfies $S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0$, or if the apparent signal reaches only through rank saturation, then the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ basis remains a code-read or bounded codon-count residual basis for this layer.

\paragraph{Underpowered comparison.}
A separate three-organism run used $61$ sense codons and modeled tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  In that finite table the three organisms were Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  The payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each organism, with $432$ matched FASTA headers out of $432$ for Homo sapiens, $275$ out of $275$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and $87$ matched headers out of $89$ for Escherichia coli K-12.  The modeled-tAI calculation had zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ for all three organisms.  It produced $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That run is retained only as a bounded coupling check: $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival, and an all-perfect or nearly perfect matrix at that size is not biological evidence.

\paragraph{Contacts used by the calculation.}
The finite calculation depends on organism-scoped codon-usage summaries, tRNA gene-copy annotations, and tAI or stAI weight tables.  These contacts are appropriate for a modeled translation-readout surface because they provide codon-count, anticodon-copy, and codon-adaptation quantities at organism resolution.  They do not observe elongation speed, ribosome density, translation efficiency in vivo, protein abundance, folding, function, physical feasibility, or phenotype.  The readout in the powered run is explicitly modeled per-codon tAI from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, projected onto $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter permits only the following claim: a rank-adequate $40$-organism modeled-tAI panel gives a controlled $B^{*}_{Q6}\to T$ readout surface with $9$ residual coordinates, $9$ readout columns, a $9\times 9$ computed survival matrix, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $17$ residual degrees of freedom, and aggregate $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  It does not permit promotion to measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Any such stronger statement requires a separate matched contact at that layer and a new controlled statistic on the joined rows.
