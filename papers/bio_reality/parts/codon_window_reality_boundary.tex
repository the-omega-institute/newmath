\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR leucine gate}

A raw $\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}$ sign by itself is not enough for this packet.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
No translation mechanism is established by codon counts or tRNA gene-copy ratios alone.  No tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output follows from a tRNA-copy row unless the corresponding direct contact is present.  Ribosome-profiling rows, when attached, would test only the translation readout they measure; they would not by themselves imply mature protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or universality.  Protein-abundance rows, when attached, would be downstream abundance contacts rather than folding or function contacts.  The three-organism leucine row also cannot support an eight-organism or global cross-organism claim.  Those stronger readings require separate measurements and separate statistics.
