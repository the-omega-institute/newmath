\subsection{Residual Codon Topology After the Amino-Acid Quotient}
\label{subsec:residual-basis-q6-topology-after-aa-quotient-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Conditional survival statistic.}
For a translation readout $r$ such as tAI, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance, the residual survival score for a coordinate $q\in B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ is read as the conditional improvement

\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z,q)=J_0(Z)-J_Q(Z,q),
