\subsection{Amino-acid residual coordinates for Q6 codon topology}
\label{sec:biorality-residual-basis-q6-topology-after-aa-quotient-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Object under test.}
The finite object considered here is the amino-acid-residual codon basis $B^*_{Q6}$ obtained after quotienting away amino-acid identity. Its coordinates are not whole-codon usage frequencies and are not amino-acid composition. They are residual contrasts left after the amino-acid sequence $y$ has been placed in the conditioning data. The nine named coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
B^*_{Q6}=\{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN/UUR},\ Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\ Ser_{UCA/UCG},\ Thr_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}_{\mathrm{anti}}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended biological question is whether any one of these residual coordinates survives as a translation-layer predictor once amino-acid composition and simpler codon-feature baselines have already been accounted for. For a readout $r$ such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance, the local score is treated as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z)=J_0(Z)-J_Q(Z,q),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains the amino-acid sequence $y$, organismal context, and the simpler baseline variables. A positive value is therefore not a free-standing biological law; it is a conditional improvement of a residual coordinate over a specified null model.

\paragraph{Finite codon-topology rows.}
The verified motif contact identifies the finite set $R$ exactly as the thirteen codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,\ AGA,\ AGG,\ AUA,\ CUA,\ CUC,\ CUG,\ CUU,\\
&UAA,\ UAG,\ UCA,\ UGA,\ UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite check reports no missing codons and no extra codons relative to the motif union. This matters because the residual basis is not inferred from an open-ended biological narrative; it is anchored in a closed codon list before any translation readout is attached.

\paragraph{Quotient geometry.}
The quotient record gives the $M$-complex face vector as $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and the amino-acid quotient face vector as $(10,14,6,1)$. The quotient image has size $10$, with rows

The finite organismal contact loads one codon-count table for each of three organisms: human, budding yeast, and \textit{E. coli} K-12. \paragraph{Translation readout criterion.}
The conjectural test is deliberately narrower than a general codon-usage claim. A support event requires an organism-matched table in which codon usage, tRNA-family copy spectra, and a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance are paired under the same conditioning design. $$
\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)\leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for every $Q\in B^*_{Q6}$, every organism in the paired dataset, and every admitted readout $r$. A break of this null occurs if at least one residual coordinate improves the score, or if a controlled leucine $CUN/UUR$ log-ratio reverses sign between organisms while the $M$-density baseline fails to account for the reversal.

\paragraph{Current status of the contact.}
The available contacts are sufficient to define the test classes: per-organism codon-usage summaries, organism-level tRNA gene-copy spectra, and organism-scoped tAI or stAI weights. They are not yet a single matched controlled statistic. Consequently, the present row supports a precise conjecture and a concrete experimental design, but it does not yet report a positive residual survival score. The absence of the paired statistic is a blocked null boundary rather than evidence for or against the $M$-only hypothesis.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
Nonzero residual variation in codon-count tables does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization. It also does not establish folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, protein abundance, or a universal biological rule. Those conclusions require separate contact at the relevant layer. The only claim made here is that the finite $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis is a well-specified amino-acid-controlled coordinate system, and that the next reality contact is the paired estimation of $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)$ on organism-matched translation readouts.
