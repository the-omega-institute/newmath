\subsection{Finite Coordinate Surface and Gate Definition}\label{sec:q6-translation-gated-structural-order-cross-layer-surface}

\paragraph{Coordinate carrier.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer test for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface on coding sequences. The carrier is the CDS-indexed table whose rows are encoded proteins and whose coordinate columns are the nine residual directions
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. Each row is paired with organism-specific codon-count information, a predicted structural-confidence readout when available, and a control block $Z$ containing CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline. The relevant object is not a free claim about protein shape. It is a finite regression surface asking whether these nine residual coordinates retain an association with a structural-order readback after the declared controls have already absorbed simpler sequence composition effects.

\paragraph{Gate.}
The intended biological route is ordered. A coordinate $q_i$ is first a synonymous residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting. It may be considered for a structural-order query only through a readout chain of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow SO,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout and $SO$ is a separately attached structural-order readback. The finite run summarized here directly evaluates the $SO$ association against AlphaFold mean pLDDT, interpreted only as predicted structural-confidence on the $0$ to $100$ scale. It therefore does not by itself certify an experimental folded structure, a translation mechanism, a function, a phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal biological law. Those stronger readings require their own layer-matched measurements.

\paragraph{Control model.}
For each organism, the control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors. With $X_Q$ denoting the nine-coordinate design matrix and $Y_{SO}$ denoting mean pLDDT, the tested residual association is the controlled projection
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde X_Q &= (I-P_Z)X_Q,\\
\widetilde Y_{SO} &= (I-P_Z)Y_{SO},\\
R^2_{SO} &= R^2(\widetilde Y_{SO}\,;\,\widetilde X_Q).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive value is read only as a finite cross-sectional association with predicted structural confidence after these controls. It is not read as causality.
