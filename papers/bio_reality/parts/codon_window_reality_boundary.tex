\subsection{Window-six codon tile at the code-read boundary}
\label{subsec:codon-window-six-local-tile-boundary}

\paragraph{Object and contact.}
The object considered here is the curated standard-code codon table, read only as a finite assignment from codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The coordinate carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, with the usual four-letter codon alphabet displayed as a binary window. Within that carrier, the relevant distinctions are a six-bit codon display, a local tile face, a singleton label corner, and a three-point label class. These distinctions organize coordinate, closure, spectrum, and relation data, but the labels themselves are supplied by the curated code table. Thus the empirical contact is a code-label contact: it says which codon has which standard-code label, and it does not by itself observe translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a mechanism, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Residual codon set.}
The finite residual set $R$ has cardinality $13$. In the verified row, it is exactly

The shape separates a singleton-like corner behavior from three-point and family-like label classes, but the separation remains a code-read phenomenon unless another empirical contact is added.

No translation event, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, or cross-organism law follows from this row system unless another measurement contact supplies that higher claim directly. The claim is ready only as a finite, organism-level Leu codon-usage/tRNA-copy realization statement. \paragraph{Conditional survival test.}
For a basis element $q\in B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ and a readout $r$, the test statistic is the conditional improvement of the residual coordinate over the baseline model:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z)=J_0(Z)-J_Q(Z,q).
\end{aligned}

When the local $M$-density baseline is explicitly included, the sharper counterexample search is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The null form says that, for every organism, every listed residual coordinate, and every readout under consideration, adding $q$ after $Z$ and $\Delta_M$ should fail to improve the score:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)\leq 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive value for at least one residual coordinate on at least one organism-matched readout would be a bounded translation-layer signal. A second admissible break would be a controlled sign reversal in the Leu $\mathrm{CUN/UUR}$ log-ratio between organisms when amino-acid composition is held fixed and the $M$-only baseline does not capture the reversal.

\paragraph{Why the $M$-only baseline is not enough by itself.}
The $M$ geometry is useful because it compresses a local codon topology into a finite quotient. However, the residual question is deliberately posed after amino-acid composition control. The $M$ table by itself cannot distinguish all synonymous residual contrasts appearing in $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$; in particular, it does not by itself test contrasts such as $\mathrm{CUN/UUR}$ inside leucine or $\mathrm{AAA/AAG}$ inside lysine. Therefore a model that only scores $\Delta_M$ can be locally adequate while still blind to the residual coordinates that survive the amino-acid quotient.

\paragraph{Empirical contacts.}
Three contact classes are relevant. Per-organism codon-usage tables supply codon-count or frequency summaries for coding sequences. Organism-level tRNA gene-copy annotations supply anticodon-family spectra, including the tRNA-Leu, tRNA-Arg, tRNA-Ile, tRNA-Ser, and tRNA-Thr families required by the proposed probe. Organism-scoped tAI or stAI weights can supply a bounded codon-adaptation readout. These contacts are not interchangeable: codon usage measures sequence composition, tRNA copy number measures genome annotation, and tAI or stAI is already a modelled translation-efficiency quantity. The chapter therefore treats them as separate inputs that must be matched by organism before any survival score is reported.

\paragraph{Current status of the conjecture.}
The internal residual geometry is finite and nontrivial: $13$ codons in $R$, quotient size $10$, $9$ residual basis elements, $7$ nonzero residuals at threshold $0.01$, and $3$ organisms in the present codon-count contact. This supports the existence of a concrete residual coordinate system after amino-acid quotienting. It does not yet supply the paired statistic needed for the translation-realization claim. The missing object is an organism-matched table combining codon usage, relevant tRNA copy-number spectra, and tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or protein-abundance readouts, followed by the controlled computation of $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)$ for each $q\in B^{\ast}_{Q6}$.

\paragraph{Local limits.}
A nonzero residual variance in codon-count tables is not evidence of translation dynamics by itself. A positive conditional score, if later obtained, would still be conditional on the chosen organisms, readout, controls, and baseline. It would not imply protein folding, structural admissibility, biochemical function, a molecular mechanism, or a global biological law. Any movement from codon residuals to those higher biological layers requires a separate measurement contact designed for that layer.

\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
with no evidential shortcut from $q_i$ to $p_k$.  A protein-abundance association is admissible for this chapter only after the same residual coordinate first survives a translation readout. Matched mRNA abundance is used to separate transcription-layer abundance from translation-layer signal, for instance through a translation-efficiency residual or a regression term for transcript level.

$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is the same number.  These scalars show that the finite modeled contact is nonzero under the declared threshold, but they do not by themselves establish measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
The intended cross-layer statistic compares a controlled direct model with a controlled mediated model.  Let $Z$ denote the controls consisting of amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance.  For a fixed triple $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, the positive mediation condition is that $q_i$ improves the translation readout after $Z$, and the downstream model through $t_j$ improves the protein-abundance readout after the same controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0,\\
S^{QTP}_{ijk} &> 0,\\
\Delta(Q_i\to P_k\mid Z,T_j) &\leq \Delta(Q_i\to P_k\mid Z).
\end{aligned}
$$
The last inequality records the absorption test: a direct residual-to-protein association is not treated as protein realization when the translation step is null, absent, or untested.  The chapter therefore distinguishes three outcomes.  First, a positive mediated outcome requires a surviving $Q_i\to T_j$ entry and a matched protein-abundance improvement downstream.  Second, a transcription-only outcome occurs when the apparent protein association disappears after matched mRNA abundance control or fails to pass through translation.  Third, a bounded residual outcome occurs when all mediated improvements are nonpositive; in that case $B^*_{Q6}$ remains a codon-count or translation-readout residual and is not advanced to protein-abundance realization.

The verified finite row currently supports only a modeled translation-weight contact. The computation is also not a ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  The break condition for the modeled contact is not met, because the finite modeled-tAI matrix does contain entries with $|S_{ij}|$ above $0.01$; however, the stronger mediation condition is still open because the record has no same-CDS rows joining codon counts, measured translation readout, matched protein abundance, and matched mRNA abundance. \paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The scientific limit is part of the statement.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are codon-adaptation annotations, not observations of ribosome dynamics.  A ribosome-profiling contact would test translation efficiency or occupancy, but not mature protein abundance by itself.  A proteomics contact would test steady-state protein abundance, but not folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control, not a translation readout.  Therefore no translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, or global biological law follows from the residual geometry or the modeled-tAI matrix alone.  Each of those claims requires its own matched measurement layer and its own controlled statistic after the translation step has survived.
