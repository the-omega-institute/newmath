\paragraph{Matrix form.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a readout vector $r_j$, the intended survival entry is computed only after projecting the readout away from the control block $Z$.  Here $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The coupling and survival quantities are therefore separated:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z).
\end{aligned}
$$
The first number asks whether the residual coordinate has a covariance-level contact with a projected readout weight.  The second number asks whether the same coordinate improves a controlled description of the readout after the declared null model has already used the available compositional and contextual covariates.  A positive $A_{ij}$, a negative $A_{ij}$, or a large absolute coupling is not by itself a positive survival score.  The survival claim requires a separately computed partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement for the organism-matched readout rows.

\paragraph{Present verdict.}
The current evidence keeps the conjecture alive but not settled as a translation-survival result.  The proxy matrix has nine nonzero residual couplings above the $0.01$ threshold, and the three-organism Leu row reports a partial Pearson value of $1.0$ after the amino-acid-composition control.  These facts show that the residual coordinate panel is not vacuous at the codon-usage/tRNA-copy interface.  They do not yet establish that any $S_{ij}$ entry is strictly positive for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance after the full control block and $M$-density ablation.  The survival-matrix support condition remains: at least one pair $(q_i,r_j)$ must satisfy $S_{ij}>0$ under the full control set and under the simpler-codon-baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The chapter records a bounded translation-readout target and nothing above it.  Codon-usage tables and tRNA gene-copy rows do not by themselves measure ribosome dynamics, tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law. A proteomics contact can test downstream abundance only when identifier-matched protein rows are attached and the translation step has survived first.  No assertion about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows here unless a separate contact supplies that layer and a recorded statistic is computed for it.

\paragraph{Data needed for closure.}
The next finite table must join per-CDS codon counts, organism labels, GC3, CDS length, amino-acid composition, the $M$-density baseline, and at least one direct translation readout.  Matched mRNA abundance is used only as a control for translation-efficiency residualization.
