\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For a finite joined table, write $Z$ for the control block consisting of amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance. $$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0,\\
\Delta^{QTP}_{ijk} &> 0\quad \text{after conditioning on } Z.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $S^{QT}_{ij}$ denotes the controlled translation-readout improvement for residual coordinate $q_i$ and readout $t_j$, and $\Delta^{QTP}_{ijk}$ denotes the downstream improvement for protein-abundance readout $p_k$ once the translation mediator has been retained.  A direct association $q_i \to p_k$ is not enough, because a protein-abundance row can reflect transcription level, protein turnover, ionization efficiency, sample preparation, identifier aggregation, or other abundance-layer variation that is not a translation-mediated residual effect.

\paragraph{Matched mRNA control.}
The matched mRNA contact has a restricted role.  It is used to separate translation-layer signal from transcription-layer abundance, for example through translation efficiency of the form $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$ when ribosome-profiling rows and RNA-seq rows are condition-matched.  It is not itself a translation readout, and it is not protein-output evidence.  In this chapter, mRNA abundance belongs to $Z$; it can block a false mediation claim, but it cannot create a positive $q_i \to t_j$ survival entry.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance contact.}
The protein layer requires matched proteomics rows, such as organism-matched PAXdb, PRIDE, or ProteomicsDB abundance estimates keyed through UniProt and mapped back to coding-sequence identifiers.  These rows can test downstream abundance after a translation mediator is present.  They cannot test folding pathway dynamics, molecular function, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, or universality.  A steady-state abundance measurement is downstream output, not a structural assay and not a functional assay.

\subsection{Rank-powered translation survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}\label{sec:q6-rank-powered-translation-survival-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-codon readout surface on which the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are compared with modeled translation-readout weights after amino-acid quotienting.  The carrier is not a protein, phenotype, or physical structure.  It is a controlled table whose rows are organisms and whose columns are residual codon-usage coordinates and translation-weight coordinates.  The tested residual basis contains nine coordinates,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The translation readout is also a nine-column modeled tAI surface obtained from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights and then projected onto the same synonymous-residual coordinate frame.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and translation readout $T_j$, the reported quantity is the controlled entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ after residualizing against amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.  The finite computation uses forty organisms and a control design with $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after the control projection.  The tested residual coordinate design has rank $9$.  The survival table is therefore a $9\times 9$ finite matrix rather than a single-coordinate anecdote.  In the run cited here, the powered fit reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n &= 40,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
 \operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17,\\
 R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared positivity threshold for a partial-$R^2$ entry is $10^{-12}$, and the rank-degeneracy guard rejects the all-saturated regime at $R^2\geq 0.999999$.  Since $0.9003849000036579<0.999999$ with forty rows and nonzero residual degrees of freedom, this table is not the earlier all-$1.0$ projection artifact.

\paragraph{What survives.}
The result supports a bounded modeled translation-weight survival claim: after the stated controls, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel retains positive contact with the modeled tAI readout surface at the level of the $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix.  The claim is deliberately table-level.  A coordinate such as $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ may participate in the finite residual panel, but no single coordinate is promoted to a population law.  The finite-row meaning is that the amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates are not exhausted by amino-acid composition, organism labels, GC3, length, $M$-density, or the simpler codon baselines when the target is this modeled tAI coordinate surface.

\paragraph{Underpowered boundary.}
The three-organism predecessor calculation is retained only as a boundary case.  In that smaller table, the modeled tAI readout covered the $61$ sense-codon space for Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12, and the local GtRNAdb payload contained all standard amino-acid tRNA families for those organisms.  It produced a nonzero bounded-coupling matrix with $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That is useful as a finite coupling check, but it is not a powered survival test.  With only $3$ organisms, rank saturation can make a survival matrix appear perfect for algebraic reasons.  The forty-organism run is the first cited table here that clears the explicit support requirement for rank-powered modeled-readout survival.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the contacted layer.}

Consequently, the positive $S^{QT}$ boundary does not imply protein abundance, folded structure, molecular function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, ribosome-mediated mechanism, or a global biological law.
