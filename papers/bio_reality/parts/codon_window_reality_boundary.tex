\subsection{Translation Survival Matrix for the Nine Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix-translation-realization}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object.}
This record isolates a translation-layer conjecture for the nine residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ after the amino-acid quotient has already been imposed. The proposed finite table is a survival matrix $S_{ij}$ whose rows are the residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress, and whose columns are translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance when those readouts are organism matched. For each pair $(q_i,r_j)$ the intended statistic first projects the readout away from amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline, and then asks whether the residual coordinate improves prediction or description length beyond that controlled model.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
The matrix entry is not the raw coupling between a codon residual and a readout. It is a controlled survival score. If $\ell_j^{\perp}$ denotes the readout weight after the declared controls have been removed, the proxy coupling used for screening has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},GC3,\text{ length},M\text{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive translation-survival entry requires $S_{ij}>0$ after the full control set and baseline ablation. A large or signed $A_{ij}$ entry is therefore only a routing signal unless the corresponding $S_{ij}$ row has been estimated from a direct translation or downstream abundance contact.

\paragraph{Finite rows already computed.}
The finite codon-usage screening row \texttt{h3.codon\_usage\_topology.translation\_survival.b\_star\_q6\_coupling\_matrix} computed all nine listed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates in the 61-codon count space against a proxy readout, namely the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. The run loaded three organisms: homo sapiens, saccharomyces cerevisiae, and escherichia coli K12. Its null screen was declared as $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for all nine residual coordinates against the proxy readout. That break condition was not met: all nine entries were nonzero above the coupling screen in the reported matrix, with maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{(i,j): |A_{ij}|\geq 0.01\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact, not a survival matrix. It shows that the residual basis is not numerically silent under the specified proxy spectrum, while leaving the translation-readout survival entries unfilled.

\paragraph{Survival conjecture.}
The chapter-level conjecture is that at least one controlled survival entry is positive once a direct translation-related readout is attached:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists i,j\quad S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual packet is conjectured not to be fully absorbed by a model containing amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, sequence length, and the simpler codon-density baseline when the target is a translation-layer readout. The current finite evidence supports the relevance of the residual coordinates for a tRNA-copy proxy and highlights a concrete leucine split, but it does not yet populate the declared $S_{ij}$ matrix for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Contacts required for promotion.}
The required data are organism-matched codon-usage rows, tRNA gene-copy numbers, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency measurements, matched mRNA abundance for transcript control, and matched proteomics if protein abundance is used as a downstream readout. The mRNA contact belongs inside $Z$ as a control for translation-efficiency residualization; by itself it is not a translation output. Likewise, tRNA gene-copy number is a genome-annotation quantity and must not be treated as tRNA abundance, charging state, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output without a separate measurement.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present record does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization from the proxy coupling alone, and it does not establish protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, pathway role, mechanism, or a universal biological law. Ribo-seq can support a bounded translation-efficiency row only after the stated controls are applied.
