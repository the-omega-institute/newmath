\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}

Let $\ell_j^{\perp}$ be the synonymous projection of the readout weight for column $j$, after the amino-acid quotient has removed the amino-acid composition component. $$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}

$$
\begin{aligned}

q_i \text{ improves the controlled model of } r_j \text{ beyond } Z.
\end{aligned}
$$
The conjectural support condition is that at least one entry in the nine-by-$K$ matrix is strictly positive after the declared controls and the $M$-density ablation.  The null condition is that every entry satisfies $S_{ij}\leq 0$ under the same fitting rule.
