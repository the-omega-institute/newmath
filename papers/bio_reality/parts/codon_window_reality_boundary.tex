\paragraph{Matrix definition.}

$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij}=q_i^T\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}>0
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
q_i\ \text{adds positive controlled partial }R^2\text{ or description-length gain for }r_j\text{ given }Z.
\end{aligned}
$$
The open support condition is that at least one entry in the $9\times K$ matrix satisfy $S_{ij}>0$ after amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, and the $M$-density baseline have all been applied.
