\subsection{A B-star Q6 residual survival matrix for translation readouts}

\paragraph{Object of record.}

\paragraph{Matrix statistic.}
For a genuine translation-survival entry, each readout vector $\ell_j$ must first be projected away from amino-acid composition and the stated covariates, giving a residual readout $\ell_j^{\perp}$.  The coupling row is then measured on the residual count covariance $\Sigma$ by
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} = q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell_j^{\perp} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The survival score $S_{ij}$ is a stronger object: it records whether adding $q_i$ to the controlled model of $r_j$ improves the residual explanation, equivalently whether the corresponding partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement is positive after $Z$ and after the $M$-density baseline has already been fitted.  A positive $A_{ij}$ is therefore only a coupling diagnostic.  It becomes a positive $S_{ij}$ only after the controlled survival calculation has been run on an organism-matched translation readout.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter records a translation-layer conjecture and its current finite contacts.  The three-organism tRNA-copy row and the nine-coordinate proxy coupling row do not by themselves establish ribosome-mediated translation realization.  They also do not establish protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or any universal biological law.  Ribosome profiling may test translation efficiency only when the matched mRNA abundance control is present; mRNA abundance alone remains a transcription-layer covariate; protein abundance is a downstream readout and does not certify folding or function.  tRNA gene-copy counts are genome-annotation summaries and must not be read as tRNA abundance, charging state, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output without the corresponding direct measurement.

\paragraph{Status of the conjecture.}
The current evidence leaves the survival-matrix claim open rather than confirmed.  The available facts show that all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero proxy coupling above the $0.01$ break threshold in a three-organism row, and they show a perfect finite-row Leu CUN/UUR partial correlation with sign reversal across organisms.  What remains missing is the actual $S_{ij}$ matrix against direct translation readouts under the full control block $Z$.  The conjecture survives as a precise finite test: it is supported exactly when at least one pair $(q_i,r_j)$ has $S_{ij}>0$ after amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and $M$-density baseline controls, and it is broken exactly when every such entry is nonpositive.
