\subsection{Window-six local codon tile boundary}
\label{sec:codon-window-six-local-tile-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The record considered here is a code-layer statement about the standard genetic-code table, not a statement about the biochemical act of translation.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a binary display for the $64$ codons.  Its labels are supplied by the curated standard-code table, whose role is only to assign each codon an amino-acid or stop label.  In this coordinate presentation the detected boundary row is the set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row count is $|R|=13$.  As a face complex inside the window-six cube, this same row has the measured vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4),
\end{aligned}
$$
so the local object is not merely a list of exceptional codons: it has a small punctured-cube shape with thirteen vertices, sixteen edges, and four two-faces.

\paragraph{Spectral and sampling checks.}
The associated spectral measurements separate the boundary row from a generic coordinate subset.  The recorded values are $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, the same numerical residual reported for the cubic polynomial check, so the row lies on the intended finite spectral constraint up to the stated numerical tolerance.  A null sampling run with $N=2000$ trials, seed $42$, $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, and $55377$ duplicate retries found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ samples with Jaccard overlap at least $0.9$.  Thus the empirical exact-shape rate is $0.0005$, while the high-overlap rate is $0.0125$.  Under the amino-acid-class-preserving null with the same $N=2000$ and seed $42$, the observed module size was $20$, the exact-match count was $0$, and the lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, giving lower-tail rate $0.0035$ and exact-shape rate $0.0$.  These numbers justify treating the tile as a finite local signal in the codon-label table, while still keeping the signal at the code-read layer.

\paragraph{Leave-one recovery.}
The code-table leave-one experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate after adjoining the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$, equal to the reported hit rate above baseline.  The codon leave-one experiment reported no anchor cases, $5$ boundary cases, $8$ predictable cases, and $8$ high-coverage closure cases, with recovery rate $1.0$.  By family, the hit rates above baseline were $1.0$ for bacterial/plastid, mitochondrial, nuclear-ciliate, and candidate-other families, and $0.8333333333333334$ for the alternative-yeast family.  These finite rows say that the local coordinate-and-label split has predictive traction on code-table readback, especially around known reassignment modules, but they do not identify a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Module partition.}
The thirteen codons in $R$ are completely assigned by the module classification.  The reported modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys}&=\{AAA\},\\

\mathrm{Ile\_Met}&=\{AUA\},\\

\end{aligned}
$$
The classification reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, and no missing, extra, or unassigned codons.  Every module has at least one predictable or boundary status.  This is the strongest finite-row form of the claim: the window-six coordinate system exposes a local tile whose codons match a complete codon-reassignment module partition in the curated label table.

\paragraph{Local biological reading.}
The supported biological reading is deliberately narrow.  The curated standard-code table supplies codon-to-label assignments; the six-coordinate display supplies a finite local geometry; the experiments above record that the boundary row has a rare punctured-cube shape, a stable spectral signature, and strong leave-one recovery behavior.  Therefore the claim may say that window-six codon coordinates expose local boundary-tile structure in code-label readback.  It may not say that this geometry is translation itself, a folded protein structure, a physical admissibility criterion, a biological function, a universal mechanism, or a law governing all DNA.  Amino-acid label sequences are still labels at the code layer.  Any assertion about ribosomal realization, structure formation, organism-level function, or cross-organism necessity would require a separate empirical contact measuring that higher layer.

\subsection{Cross-organism CUN/UUR leucine gate}\label{subsec:cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate-translation-realization}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record treats the leucine split between CUN and UUR codons as an organism-level statistic, not as a claim about a protein product.  The carrier is the residual pair consisting of a CUN-over-UUR codon-usage coordinate and an organism-matched tRNA-Leu copy-number coordinate, with the organism name retained as an external label and amino-acid composition treated as a control variable.  The finite code-side support used by \texttt{h0.M.equals.WNR.CUN} has cardinality $20$ and is exactly
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The four CUN entries in this finite set are $CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU$, and the two UUR leucine entries are $UUA,UUG$.  The biological readout is therefore the signed statistic $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$, paired with a same-organism tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio after the synonymous-composition control has been applied.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The organism rows reported by \texttt{h3.translation\_realization.cross\_organism.cun\_uur\_leu\_gate} are $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$.  Their recorded CUN-over-UUR log-ratios are
$$\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}$$
with signs
$$\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across the three measured organisms.  The same finite run records Pearson correlation $1.0$ for the paired statistic and a positive partial correlation, after amino-acid composition control, with
$$\begin{aligned}
n=3,\qquad r_{partial}=1.0.
\end{aligned}$$
The run status is $passed$.  No p-value, lambda parameter, hit-rate statistic, row-class module label, or larger row count is present in this finite packet, so the numerical assertion is limited to these three rows and these recorded scalar values.

\paragraph{Translation-layer reading.}
The supported statement is narrow: across the three available organism rows, the CUN/UUR leucine usage sign reverses rather than remaining constant, and the measured magnitude is positively aligned with the organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after the stated composition control.  This is enough to register a bounded translation-layer contact between synonymous leucine codon usage and an organism-matched tRNA gene-copy spectrum.  The contact is a statistical readback from sequence and genome-annotation summaries; it is not a direct observation of ribosome traffic, elongation kinetics, aminoacylation, protein abundance, or cellular phenotype.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
The CUN/UUR sign alone is not allowed to stand for translation in the mechanistic sense.  In particular, no folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, ribosome-mediated mechanism, organism-wide law, or universal direction of effect follows from the numbers above.  The reversal $+,-,+$ is a context-dependent row fact, not a rule that every organism must satisfy.  A stronger biological claim would require a separate contact, such as ribosome profiling, charged tRNA availability, peptide-output measurements, protein-structure evidence, or function assays.  Without such data, this chapter remains at the controlled codon-usage and tRNA-copy comparison: it licenses the finite cross-organism statistic and blocks promotion beyond that scope.

\subsection{Residual codon topology after amino-acid quotienting}\label{sec:bior-reality-residual-basis-q6-topology-after-aa-quotient-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Coordinate under test.}
The object considered here is the codon-level residual basis obtained after amino-acid identity has already been held fixed.  The carrier is the per-organism table of coding-sequence codon counts, projected away from the amino-acid composition coordinate and then read in the residual basis
$B^{*}_{Q6}$.  The nine named directions are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and an antisymmetric third-position stress-pair coordinate.  These directions are not amino-acid claims: they are synonymous or quotient-residual distinctions that remain after the amino-acid sequence coordinate $y$ is included in the conditioning data.

The finite contact used for the coordinate check contains three organism-level codon-count tables, for human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12.  On that finite contact the residual computation reports nine basis elements, with seven of them nonzero above variance threshold $0.01$.  Thus the observed nonzero fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \frac{7}{9} &= 0.7777777777777778 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This number is only a finite readout over the three named organisms.  It is not a population estimate, and it does not by itself identify a translation mechanism or a biological law.

\paragraph{Motif and quotient geometry.}
The finite motif compression check identifies the thirteen-codon set

In particular, an $M$-only model cannot directly test the $CUN/UUR$ leucine contrast or the $AAA/AAG$ lysine contrast after amino-acid quotienting, because those distinctions live in the residual directions rather than in the local $M$ membership summary alone.

\paragraph{Translation-layer survival statistic.}
The proposed biological test is conditional survival of a residual coordinate against an explicitly controlled baseline.  For a readout $r$ such as tAI, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance, and for conditioning data $Z$ including amino-acid sequence $y$, organism context, and simpler codon-feature baselines, the score for a residual coordinate $q\in B^{*}_{Q6}$ is read as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_Q(r\mid Z,q) &= J_0(Z)-J_Q(Z,q).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive value means only that the residual coordinate improves the specified controlled readout model on the specified contact.  The stricter comparison against an $M$-density baseline is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M,q) &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one residual direction, one organism, and one admissible readout.  The corresponding null states that every residual direction fails to improve over the $M$-density baseline once amino-acid composition and context have been held fixed.

The available finite rows establish the residual coordinate system and show that the nine residual directions are not all collapsed on the three-organism codon-count contact.  They do not yet supply the paired statistic $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M,q)$, because the codon-count tables, tRNA-copy summaries, and tAI or stAI weights have not been assembled into one organism-matched controlled readout table.  Therefore the present status is a bounded conjectural test: the coordinate is finite and nontrivial on the reported contact, while the translation-realization claim remains blocked until the controlled readout statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Reality contacts and local limit.}
Three contact classes are relevant.  Per-organism codon-usage tables supply coding-sequence composition summaries.  tRNA gene-copy annotations supply organism-level anticodon-family spectra, including the leucine, arginine, isoleucine, serine, and threonine families needed by the residual basis.  tAI or stAI resources can supply a bounded translation-layer readout when kept separate from the internal codon geometry.  Each contact has a narrower scope than the biological language it may tempt: codon counts are sequence summaries, tRNA copy numbers are genome-annotation summaries, and adaptation weights are modelled translation-efficiency annotations.

Consequently this section records no established claim of DNA-to-protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein-output causality, or universality.  Such conclusions would require a distinct matched contact at the relevant layer.  A positive residual score, if later obtained, would be a conditional organism-scoped readout result, not a general law.  A negative or absent score would likewise be a result about the specified controlled statistic, not a refutation of codon biology in general.

\subsection{Translation-Survival Matrix for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:biorreality-translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Claim being tested.}
This record concerns the proposed survival matrix
$S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates against translation-related readouts.  The coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the antisymmetric third-position stress coordinate.  The intended readouts include organism-scoped codon adaptation weights, ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, and protein abundance when matched to the same coding sequences.  The control block is
$Z=(\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{organism context},GC3,\hbox{length},\hbox{M-density baseline})$.
For each coordinate $q_i$ and readout $r_j$, the proposed statistic first removes the amino-acid quotient, then estimates the residual coupling and the controlled gain:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^T\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z).
\end{aligned}
$$
The conjectural support condition is that at least one entry has $S_{ij}>0$ after all controls and after ablation of the simpler codon-density baseline.  The null condition is that every entry is non-positive, equivalently that no $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate improves the controlled description of any translation readout.

\paragraph{Finite rows already attached.}
The present data contain two finite checks.  The first computed the nine residual coordinates in the $61$-codon count space and compared them with a proxy spectrum: the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy readout.  All nine residual coordinates were present in the calculation, the coupling threshold was $0.01$, the null rule was $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for every one of the nine coordinates, and the null rule was not met.  The recorded maximum absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $9$ nonzero entries above the proxy-coupling test.  This is a codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact, not a controlled survival estimate.  It shows that the nine-coordinate residual basis is not numerically silent against the attached proxy spectrum, but it does not supply any value of $S_{ij}$.

The second finite row used three organisms: \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12.  In that row, the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ codon-usage ratio was compared with the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact.  The recorded Pearson value was $1.0$, and the partial Pearson value after the available control was also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs were not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern $(+,-,+)$.  Thus the finite row supports a context-dependent association in a three-organism table, while explicitly refusing any universal direction for the sign.

\paragraph{Why this is not yet a survival matrix.}
The proxy coupling matrix and the three-organism Leu gate are weaker than the requested translation-survival matrix.  A positive $A_{ij}$ entry is only a residual covariance contact against the chosen proxy spectrum.  It is not the same object as a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length survival score.  The requested survival entry requires an organism-matched translation readout, the same coding-sequence identifiers, amino-acid composition residualization, organism context, $GC3$, length, and M-density ablation.  In particular, the current rows do not contain a ribosome-profiling matrix, a stAI or tAI survival table under the full control set, a matched proteomics survival table, or a computed list of positive and non-positive $S_{ij}$ entries.

The finite rows therefore place the conjecture at an open boundary.  They justify keeping the survival-matrix test active because the nine residual coordinates are measurable and because the proxy coupling is not identically zero.  They do not justify replacing the open test by the statement $S_{ij}>0$ for any particular pair $(q_i,r_j)$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation limits.}
The contacts used here are codon-usage tables, tRNA gene-copy summaries, and named routes to tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, mRNA, and proteomics measurements.  Gene-copy counts are not tRNA abundance, charging state, ribosome speed, or translation efficiency.  Codon-usage geometry is not protein folding evidence.  Ribosome profiling, when eventually attached, would test a translation-layer readout only under its own experimental biases and controls.  Protein abundance, when eventually attached, would be a downstream measurement rather than a folding, function, or physical-admissibility assay by itself.  Accordingly, no ribosome-mediated translation survival, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the present rows unless an additional matched contact supplies that specific measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Current finite verdict.}
The evidence currently supports the following bounded statement.  In a $61$-codon count space, all nine declared $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were computed; against the tRNA-Leu $CUN/UUR$ proxy spectrum, the all-small-coupling null at threshold $0.01$ failed, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ and $9$ nonzero proxy entries.  In a separate three-organism Leu contact, the recorded Pearson and partial Pearson values were both $1.0$ at $n=3$, while the sign pattern was $(+,-,+)$ rather than uniform.  These rows motivate the full survival-matrix experiment but leave the central survival claim unclosed until at least one fully controlled $S_{ij}>0$ entry is observed under the declared controls.
