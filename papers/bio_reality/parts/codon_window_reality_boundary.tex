No translation realization, folding statement, physical admissibility statement, biological function statement, mechanistic account, or universal biological rule follows from these rows unless an additional matched empirical contact supplies that specific layer and the controlled statistic is recomputed there.

The current data justify carrying the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates forward into a controlled translation-readout test, because the proxy coupling screen is nonzero in all nine residual rows. They do not justify reporting $S_{ij}>0$ for any translation readout yet. The next decisive computation is the full matrix over the nine residual coordinates and the matched readout columns, reporting for every entry the sign of $A_{ij}$, the controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement, and whether $S_{ij}$ is positive, zero, or negative after the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls.
