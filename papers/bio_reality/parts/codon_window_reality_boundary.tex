\paragraph{Conjectural survival statistic.}
The translation-level claim is deliberately conditional.  Let $r$ be one bounded readout chosen from tAI, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance.  Let $Z$ contain at least the amino-acid sequence $y$, organism context $c$, and simpler codon-feature baselines.  For a basis coordinate $q\in B^*_{Q6}$, the intended statistic compares the baseline information score to the score after adjoining $q$:
$$\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z) &= J_0(Z)-J_Q(Z,q).
\end{aligned}$$
A positive value is evidence only for survival of that residual coordinate against the specified readout and controls.  The stronger controlled test compares every residual coordinate against an $M$-density baseline $\Delta_M$:
$$\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M) &> 0.
\end{aligned}$$
The conjecture says that if Q6 codon topology participates in DNA-to-protein realization beyond amino-acid sequence alone, then at least one $q\in B^*_{Q6}$ should have a positive controlled survival score for at least one organism and at least one admissible readout.  A second possible break of the $M$-only explanation would occur if the controlled Leu $CUN/UUR$ log-ratio reverses sign between organisms while $\Delta_M$ does not account for the reversal.

\paragraph{What the finite rows already say.}
The existing finite rows support only the coordinate side of the record.  They show that the residual basis has nine computed elements, that seven are nonzero across the three loaded organism tables at threshold $0.01$, that the motif set $R$ matches the thirteen listed codons exactly in the checked comparison, and that the quotient geometry has the reported $M$ and quotient $f$-vectors.  These are enough to make the conjecture scientifically sharp: a paired external table can now ask whether a residual coordinate improves prediction of a translation-layer readout once amino-acid composition and simpler codon summaries are already present.  They are not enough to assert that such improvement has been observed.

\paragraph{Required reality contact.}
The next contact must be a paired organism-level dataset, not three unrelated summaries.  For each organism used in the statistic, the dataset must align codon-usage counts, relevant tRNA copy-number spectra for Leu, Arg, Ile, Ser, and Thr families, and an organism-scoped tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or protein-abundance readout.  The readout must be analyzed with $y$ in the control set, because amino-acid composition is the quotient variable that the residual basis is designed not to replace.  Without that paired statistic, the present state is a blocked null: the absence of a computed $S_Q(r\mid Z,\Delta_M)$ is neither evidence for nor evidence against $M$-only sufficiency.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
This chapter records a translation-readout conjecture, not a conclusion about higher biological layers.  A nonzero residual coordinate, or even a future positive controlled survival score, would not by itself imply folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or universality across organisms.  Translation realization itself is also not obtained from codon-count residual variance alone; it requires a separate bounded readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance under the stated controls.  The finite rows therefore license a precise test and a concrete residual coordinate system, while leaving all stronger biological claims outside the result until a separate contact supplies them.
