\subsection{ORF eligibility as a boundary-level biological contact problem}

\paragraph{Question isolated by the packet.}
The object treated here is not a translated gene product and not a biochemical mechanism.  It is the narrower question of whether a nucleotide interval may be admitted to the layer called \texttt{orf\_eligibility} when the only internal information available is a start--stop codon enumeration.  The packet therefore separates two operations.  The first operation is finite codon bookkeeping: the geometry can enumerate codons and windows.  The second operation is biological contact: an interval has to be supplied by sequence annotation, transcript evidence, or another curated biological record before the word ``eligible'' is allowed to attach to an interval.  The seed status records exactly this gap.  In the absence of such a contact, the packet is a testable boundary marker, not an ORF claim.

\subsection{Cross-organism Leu codon-gate statistic}
\label{sec:cross-organism-cun-uur-leu-gate-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record isolates one organism-level Leu coding statistic.  The finite coordinate is the signed residual comparing the CUN Leu codon block with the UUR Leu codon block, together with an organism label, a tRNA-Leu gene-copy ratio, and an amino-acid-composition control.  The intended observable comparison is not a protein-output assertion.  It asks whether the per-organism value $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ changes sign across organisms and whether its magnitude tracks the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum once the synonymous-class composition control has been applied.

\paragraph{Finite rows used.}
The code-side anchor records the finite set $M$ as a 20-element union of the WNR block with the CUN block:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
     &CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded cardinality is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|M| = 20.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the cross-organism Leu statistic, the finite loaded organism rows are
$$
\begin{aligned}
O=\{&\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\ \mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\ \\
   &\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded log-ratio vector for $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row explicitly rejects uniform sign over these three organisms.  The same run records $\mathrm{sign\_uniform}=\mathrm{false}$, Pearson correlation $1.0$, and a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu ratio of $1.0$ at $n=3$.

\paragraph{Admissible reading.}
Within this packet, the result is a bounded organism-level translation-layer contact: the CUN/UUR Leu usage residual is paired with the tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum, and the recorded three-row statistic is compatible with the claim that the sign and magnitude of the CUN-over-UUR preference are context-dependent rather than universal.  Human and \emph{E. coli} K12 have positive recorded signs, while yeast has a negative recorded sign.  The point of the row is therefore not that one direction is globally preferred, but that the finite table already contains a reversal and that the paired tRNA-Leu statistic is positive in the supplied controlled readback.

\paragraph{Local control condition.}
The statistic is only interpretable after controlling for amino-acid composition by a synonymous-class null.  A raw CUN/UUR log-ratio, taken without that control and without an organism-matched tRNA-Leu copy-number row, is only a codon-composition number.  It is not by itself evidence for a translation event, ribosome dynamics, or any downstream biological property.  The finite record is therefore best read as a constrained cross-organism association between codon-usage summaries and tRNA-Leu gene-copy summaries, not as a mechanistic account of how a ribosome realizes the relation.

\paragraph{What the row does not license.}
No folding conclusion follows from these data.  No protein structure, physical admissibility, function, ribosome-mediated mechanism, or universal biological law is established by the sign reversal or by the reported correlations.  The contacts used here are codon-usage tables and tRNA gene-copy annotations; both are genome- or sequence-summary contacts rather than direct measurements of translation kinetics, folding trajectories, biochemical activity, or organismal function.  Any stronger claim would require a separate contact of the relevant kind, such as matched ribosome-profiling, protein-output, structural, physical, or functional evidence.

\paragraph{Status.}
The finite row is ready as a narrow cross-organism Leu-gate statistic.  Its strongest local statement is that, over the three loaded organisms, $\log(\#CUN/\#UUR)$ is not sign-uniform and the supplied paired statistic reports a positive partial correlation with the tRNA-Leu copy-number ratio after the stated composition control.  Its weakest point is sample size: $n=3$ is enough to record a finite reversal and a concrete row-level association, but it is not enough to support broad extrapolation beyond the listed organisms.

\subsection{Residual Q6 Coordinates After the Amino-Acid Quotient}
\label{sec:residual-basis-q6-topology-after-aa-quotient-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Object of record.}
This record concerns codon-level synonymous residuals that remain visible after amino-acid identity has already been held fixed.  The carrier is the per-organism coding-sequence codon-count table projected onto the amino-acid-residual basis
$B^{*}_{Q6}$.  The nine recorded coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and an antisymmetric third-position stress-pair coordinate. They are residual contrasts inside synonymous fibres, so the relevant question is whether any one of them continues to predict a translation-layer readout after the amino-acid sequence $y$ and simpler codon-feature baselines have already been included in the conditioning data.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate evidence.}
The finite computation used here loaded one codon-count table for each of three organisms: human, budding yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12.  In that finite contact, the residual basis contained exactly $9$ elements, and $7$ of them had nonzero cross-organism residual variance above the stated threshold $0.01$.  The observed nonzero fraction was therefore $7/9 = 0.7777777777777778$.  This is only a finite organism-scoped contact, not a population estimate and not a biological law.  Its role is narrower: it shows that the proposed residual coordinates are not vacuous after quotienting by amino-acid identity in the three observed codon-count tables.

\paragraph{Conditional survival statistic.}
For a readout $r$ in $\{tAI,\text{ ribosome occupancy},\text{ protein abundance}\}$ and a residual coordinate $q \in B^{*}_{Q6}$, the intended statistic is
$$\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r \mid Z) = J_0(Z) - J_Q(Z,q),
\end{aligned}$$
where $Z$ contains the amino-acid sequence $y$, local context $c$, and simpler codon-feature baselines.  A positive value means only that, under the specified conditioning and readout, the residual coordinate improves the score relative to the baseline model.  The sharper controlled version compares against an $M$-density baseline and asks whether
$$\begin{aligned}
S_Q(r \mid Z,\Delta_M) > 0
\end{aligned}$$
for at least one organism, one residual coordinate, and one bounded translation readout.  The finite rows presently establish the residual coordinate geometry and the existence of relevant external contact classes; they do not yet supply the paired controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Reality contact and blocked null.}
Three contact classes are relevant: per-organism codon-usage summaries, organism-level tRNA gene-copy spectra, and organism-scoped tAI or stAI weights.  The current row set contains all three classes as separate contacts, but it does not yet contain an organism-matched table joining codon usage, tRNA copy number, and tAI or stAI readout into one controlled estimate of $S_Q(r \mid Z,\Delta_M)$.  For that reason, the translation-realization claim is not accepted as an empirical result here. \paragraph{Local boundary.}
A nonzero residual variance in codon-count tables does not by itself show translation efficiency, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or universality.  Even a future positive $S_Q$ value would remain conditional on the organism, the readout, and the conditioning variables.  Promotion beyond the bounded translation readout would require a separate contact measuring the higher layer directly.  Thus the present chapter records a finite coordinate result and a precisely stated controlled test, while withholding the stronger biological conclusions until the paired external statistic exists.

\subsection{Translation survival of the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual matrix}
\label{sec:bioreality-translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Object of test.}
This record concerns the survival matrix $S_{ij}$ obtained by placing the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates against translation-related readouts. The residual coordinates are
$\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $\mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}/\mathrm{UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}/\mathrm{UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}/\mathrm{AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}/\mathrm{ACY}}$, and an $f_3$ stress coordinate. The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates before the synonymous quotient is imposed. The proposed readouts are organism-scoped translation weights or measurements, including tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance when identifier-matched data are available. The control set is not optional: amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, sequence length, and a simpler $M$-density baseline are all part of the null model.

\paragraph{Matrix statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and readout $r_j$, the intended computation first projects the readout weight to its synonymous component $\ell_j^\perp$. The finite covariance contact then gives a coupling entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} = q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^\perp .
\end{aligned}
$$
The survival entry $S_{ij}$ is stricter than this coupling: it is the residual gain, measured for example as partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement, after the full control family $Z$ and the $M$-density ablation have been applied. Thus $A_{ij}\neq 0$ is only a routing signal toward a possible translation test, while $S_{ij}>0$ is the finite-row survival condition.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
Two finite contacts are currently attached. First, the nine residual coordinates were evaluated against a proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum across $3$ organisms: $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K12. In that proxy matrix all $9$ residual coordinates had nonzero coupling entries above the declared $0.01$ threshold, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding null condition for the proxy computation was that every one of the nine coordinates have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout. That condition was not met. This is evidence that the synonymous residual basis is not numerically silent against the proxy spectrum, but it is not yet a positive $S_{ij}$ entry for a direct translation readout.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A second finite row isolates the CUN-versus-UUR leucine coordinate against organism-level tRNA-Leu copy-ratio data. The row again uses the same $3$ organisms. The recorded Pearson correlation is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\,-0.24869078488829677,\,1.0086640520051702)
\quad\text{with signs}\quad
(+1,\,-1,\,+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The nonuniform sign pattern is part of the observation rather than an error term to erase. It says that the finite row is context-sensitive and too small to support a universal directional law. It also prevents the three-organism perfect correlation from being read as a population estimate.

\paragraph{Current survival status.}
The translation-survival conjecture asks whether at least one entry satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} = S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z) > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a direct translation-related readout after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls. The attached rows do not yet contain such a matrix. The proxy coupling row supplies $A_{ij}$ evidence against numerical silence, and the leucine row supplies a three-organism codon-usage/tRNA-copy contact. Neither row contains organism-matched ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, stAI/tAI survival, matched proteomics survival, or a computed partial-$R^2$ table under the full control set. The chapter therefore records a bounded open claim: the residual basis has finite proxy coupling, but the survival matrix remains uncomputed at the direct translation-readout layer.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No statement in this record establishes ribosome-mediated translation realization unless a direct ribosome, stAI/tAI, or equivalent translation contact is paired with the residual matrix and the declared controls. tRNA gene-copy ratios are not treated as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output. Ribosome-profiling rows, if later attached, still would not by themselves establish mature protein abundance. Proteomics rows, if later attached, still would not establish folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or a universal biological law. A positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to a positive survival score $S_{ij}$ without the controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length computation. Conversely, a zero or negative survival entry would only constrain this translation-readout interface; it would not prove that the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual geometry is biologically irrelevant in every other contact class.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The declared break condition for the survival matrix is that every $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate fail to improve any translation readout after the full controls, equivalently $S_{ij}\leq 0$ for all tested $(q_i,r_j)$ entries. The current finite record has not triggered that break condition, because the required $S_{ij}$ table has not yet been computed. The next decisive row is an organism-matched dataset containing codon usage, tRNA-copy or translation-weight information, a direct translation readout such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, and matched mRNA abundance for residualizing transcriptional signal. Only then can the nine-by-$K$ survival matrix be filled with positive, zero, or negative entries.
