\subsection{Mediation readback}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-readback}
\paragraph{Definition of the reported scalar.}
The recorded scalar is a statistical mediation decomposition. Let $X$ denote the nine-column $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block, let $T$ denote residualized modeled per-protein tAI, and let $Y$ denote residualized log protein abundance. The total residual-to-protein fit is summarized by $c=R^2(Y\sim X)$ after controls. The direct residual-to-protein fit after conditioning on the translation readout is summarized by $c'=R^2(Y\sim X\mid T)$. The mediated portion is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c}
\end{aligned}
$$
when the denominator is positive and the fraction lies in the unit interval. This number is descriptive: it measures how much of the residual-to-protein association is absorbed by the modeled tAI coordinate in the fitted rows. It is not a causal effect and is not a perturbation result.

The mediated fraction was $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$. \subsection{Interpretation boundary}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-boundary}
\paragraph{Positive content.}
The finite-row result supports a bounded cross-layer association: in all three joined organisms, a nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a protein-abundance association whose fitted direct component is reduced after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI. The fractions $0.612922252793667$, $0.08397602919861291$, and $0.9491551041216973$ are all in the unit interval, and the relevant residual ranks are sufficient for the reported decomposition. The strongest readback is yeast, where the conditioned direct fit falls from $0.29837624994140827$ total to $0.0151709093608293$ after the modeled translation readout is included. The weakest readback is \emph{M. smegmatis}, where the mediated fraction is positive but small.

\paragraph{What is not licensed.}
This chapter does not infer measured ribosome occupancy, observed translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality. Modeled tAI is a translation-layer annotation derived from gene-copy and wobble-weight inputs; it is not ribosome profiling. Protein abundance is a downstream steady-state measurement and remains entangled with mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, and pathway context. A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not accepted as protein realization unless the same coordinate first passes through a translation-readout step under the controls. The present result therefore occupies only the controlled statistical mediation layer: $Q_i\to T\to P$ is a finite fitted readback, not a causal biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The claim is ready at cross-layer statistical scope. It remains open at the measured-translation and mechanism scopes because no matched ribosome-profiling or perturbation contact is used in the reported finite rows. If a later row set replaces modeled tAI with measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency and preserves the same control discipline, then the same mediation surface can be reevaluated at a stronger translation-readout level. Until then, the admissible conclusion is exactly the bounded one: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has organism-matched protein-abundance associations that are substantially, but not uniformly, absorbed by modeled per-protein tAI after finite control residualization.

\subsection{Direct protein-omics survival of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates under a translation boundary}
\label{sec:q6-direct-protein-omics-survival-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object considered here is a CDS-by-organism table whose rows are coding sequences with three attached blocks of columns: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates computed after quotienting by amino-acid identity, covariates used to remove elementary compositional explanations, and matched protein-omics abundance readouts.  The residual coordinate block contains nine named coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These columns are not amino-acid usage itself; they are residual codon-topology coordinates left after the amino-acid quotient.  The protein readout used in the finite computation is measured PAXdb protein abundance, transformed as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The direct score $S^{QP}_{ik}$ is read as survival of a residual codon coordinate against a protein-omics column after baseline controls, not as an explanation of how translation occurred.

\paragraph{Rows and joins.}
The protein-omics table is large enough for within-organism controlled regression in each reported organism.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, 3739 proteins were used after a join hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$.  For \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, 4129 proteins were used with hit rate $1.0$.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, 5815 proteins were used with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The control matrix has 24 columns in each organism, with residual ranks $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  The remaining degrees of freedom after residualizing against $Z$ are 3716, 4106, and 5792 respectively, so the direct protein-omics calculation is not an $n=3$ organism-level saturation artifact.

\paragraph{Controlled direct survival.}
The control set removes amino-acid composition, organism-specific baseline structure, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and related composition covariates before measuring the contribution of $Q$.  The degenerate-fit cutoff was $R^2=0.999999$, and the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold was $10^{-12}$.  In the three finite row blocks, all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates survive the direct protein-omics test.  The aggregate direct $Q$-to-$P$ partial readouts are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli}\ \mathrm{K12})&=0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{M. smegmatis}\ \mathrm{MC2\ 155})&=0.03209009170649934,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae})&=0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding residual response energies after the control fit are 4020.333671802061, 5729.194538214237, and 2958.1865890925337.  The control-explained energies are 432.89050065533684, 183.85037813566998, and 882.6526210803958.  Thus the finite computation supports a direct cross-layer association: residual synonymous codon topology retains protein-abundance information after the declared baseline controls.

\paragraph{Translation readout boundary.}
A separate translation-side finite contact was also computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads and dos Reis wobble weights, yielding modeled tAI codon weights for 61 sense codons in each of three organisms.  The local tRNA payload contained all 20 standard amino-acid families for \textit{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast; the FASTA header counts were 89, 432, and 275, with matched header counts 87, 432, and 275. This is useful as a bounded codon-adaptation contact, but it is not the same experiment as measured ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, matched mRNA, or a same-CDS mediation fit.  The organism count there is only 3, and the recorded boundary explicitly refuses powered survival from that modeled tAI table without at least 40 matched species.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The result is therefore a direct protein-omics survival record with a translation boundary.  It says that the nine residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates carry measurable information about steady-state protein abundance in the reported finite CDS rows after strong compositional residualization.  It does not say that the association is mediated by translation rate, ribosome occupancy, elongation dynamics, or codon adaptation.  A translation-mediated conclusion would require joined rows containing the same CDS identifiers, a measured translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and a mediation comparison showing that conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct $q_i\to P_k$ signal while the $q_i\to T_j$ step itself survives.

\paragraph{Local refusal of over-interpretation.}
No folded-structure conclusion, physical admissibility conclusion, biochemical-function conclusion, localization conclusion, stability conclusion, phenotype conclusion, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the present $S^{QP}$ values.  Protein abundance is a downstream omics measurement, not a structural assay, not a perturbation experiment, and not a translation-rate observation.  The finite rows justify the narrower statement: under the available PAXdb joins and the stated controls, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual codon coordinates remain predictive of measured protein abundance, while the route from synonymous residual geometry through translation to protein output remains unresolved until a separate matched translation contact supplies it.
