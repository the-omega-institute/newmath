\subsection{What is already numerically grounded nearby}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-nearby-grounding}

\paragraph{Existing cross-layer calibration rows.}
The surrounding BioReality table already contains several finite cross-layer measurements, but none of them is allowed to substitute for the missing same-window $f_3$ ribosome-occupancy statistic.  A translation-conditioned structural-order test passed on two organisms with five folds, with joined structural-order row counts $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In that test the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ was positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean $0.005284160876443678$, and weighted value $0.004764742175882462$; the sign-flip probability for greater-than-zero full-control improvement was $0.0029296875$.  The corresponding permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ values were negative in both organisms, with mean $-0.00212186632044159$ and range from $-0.003463794378680385$ to $-0.000779938262202795$.  Those numbers show that the broader $B^\ast_{Q6}$ program has powered cross-layer examples, but they do not by themselves establish position-resolved translation survival for the $f_3$ member.

\paragraph{Readout precedents and row scales.}
Other verified finite rows give scale and noise context.  A G418 stop-context readthrough contact joined $7658$ rows before nonzero filtering and retained $4982$ nonzero RRTS rows, with nonzero fraction $0.65056$.  A cross-dataset stop-readthrough row used $55886$ rows and reported untreated Spearman correlation $\rho=0.032718$.  A 5-prime UTR translation-initiation row used $80000$ rows, while a random-promoter expression boundary used $75111$ designed sequences, $8027$ high-quality rows, and an analysis subsample of $2500$ with $200$ permutations.  CRISPR guide-efficiency rows add two independent position-resolved controls: the Doench row used $5310$ guides with GC main correlation $-0.0582917$, and the Luo cross-protocol row used $10543$ rows.  A pri-miRNA processing row supplied a matched-scaffold motif boundary with GC main correlation $-0.0479934$ and explicitly avoided de-novo structure inference.  These measurements are cited only as finite-row precedents: they demonstrate that the paper handles controlled sequence-to-readout tables, not that this chapter's $f_3$ statistic has already survived.
