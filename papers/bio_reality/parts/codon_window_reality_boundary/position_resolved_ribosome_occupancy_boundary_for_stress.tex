\subsection{Position-Resolved Ribosome-Occupancy Boundary for $f_3$-Stress}\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-resolved-ribosome-occupancy-translation-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate.}
This record isolates a single cross-layer test: whether an $f_3$-stress residual, computed only after the synonymous-codon quotient has removed amino-acid composition, has any bounded survival against a position-resolved translation readout. The row type is a finite CDS-window table indexed by organism, coding sequence, codon position modulo $3$, local counts of the $f_3$-paired codons, a bounded wobble-weight surface, a matched mRNA control, and a codon-indexed or local-window ribosome-occupancy readout. The response is not protein abundance, folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, or a global biological law. The only admissible response is a same-window ribosome-footprint occupancy statistic, or a translation-efficiency residual formed after the matched mRNA row has been joined.

\paragraph{Quotient support already present.}
The available finite coordinate facts determine the codon-side support but do not yet supply the ribosome-footprint rows. In the $f_3$ saturation run, the set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ contains $20$ codons:
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The corresponding saturated $f_3$ support removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves $18$ codons:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded boundary list for $f_3$ is empty, so no additional boundary codon is currently certified by that finite run. The quotient geometry run records an $M$ face vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$, quotient face vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and $|\pi_M|=10$. Thus the codon coordinate is finite and quotient-compressed before any translation readout is considered.

\paragraph{Required survival statistic.}
For a fixed organism, coding sequence, and window $w$, let $X_{f_3}(w)$ be the amino-acid-quotient-surviving $f_3$-stress coordinate, let $Y_{\mathrm{RPF}}(w)$ be the matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or TE residual, and let $Z(w)$ collect organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, position bin, amino-acid bin, coverage, and mappability controls. The only positive readback has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}}(Y_{\mathrm{RPF}}\mid Z)>0,
\end{aligned}$$
where the model matrix is full rank after the controls are attached and where simpler codon-count or wobble-weight baselines fail to remove the same signal. If the controlled increment is non-positive, rank-degenerate, or supported only by modelled tAI or stAI weights without ribosome-footprint rows, the coordinate remains a codon-topology residual and is not promoted to a translation claim.

\paragraph{Empirical contacts and present gap.}
The required empirical contacts are codon-usage rows per organism, tAI or stAI weights as bounded annotations, matched mRNA abundance for the same experimental condition, and codon-indexed ribosome-profiling rows. The codon-usage and wobble-weight contacts can define covariates, but they do not observe ribosome motion. The matched mRNA contact is needed to separate footprint occupancy from transcript abundance. The position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact is still marked as pending same-organism, same-CDS, codon-indexed curation; therefore this chapter records a finite test boundary rather than a completed positive survival result.

\paragraph{Calibration against nearby finite probes.}
The surrounding finite probes show why the boundary must be kept narrow. A separate structural-order ramp run used $5$ folds, minimum joined size $300$, and primary ramp length $50$. It reported a positive ramp increment, with weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$ and sign-flip probability $0.0478515625$, while the body increment was negative, weighted $f_3$ body held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$ with probability $0.54296875$. The paired ramp-minus-body increment was $0.001378137149572267$ with probability $0.05859375$, so the recorded classification was positive ramp evidence without passing the localization gate. That result is informative about a different readout and cannot substitute for position-resolved ribosome occupancy. Other cross-layer finite contacts likewise remain layer-specific: the alternative-polyadenylation table contains $24{,}740$ genes and $296{,}950$ PAS rows, while the exon-inclusion contact contains $1{,}037$ rows and records $\operatorname{collinearity}(\Delta\mathrm{ESEseq},\Delta\mathrm{MaxEnt})=-0.008429245619$. These scalar facts show that finite biological contacts can be joined and audited, but they do not measure this chapter's ribosome-occupancy response.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore has a precise negative discipline. From $f_3$-stress, quotient geometry, codon-window counts, or modelled translation weights alone, one may not infer translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Such statements require a separate matched contact at that layer. The only claim reserved here is conditional: if same-organism, same-CDS, same-window ribosome-footprint rows are joined to the codon coordinate and the controlled survival statistic remains positive under rank checks, baseline ablations, and null comparisons, then $f_3$-stress may be reported as a bounded predictor of the translation readout. If those rows are absent, underpowered, rank-degenerate, or non-positive after controls, the correct outcome is null or needs-data rather than downstream biological promotion.
