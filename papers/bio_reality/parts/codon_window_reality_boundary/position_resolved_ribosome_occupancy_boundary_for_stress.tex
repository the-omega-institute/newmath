\subsection{Position-resolved ribosome-occupancy boundary for $f_3$-stress}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-resolved-ribosome-occupancy-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate.}
This record isolates one narrow translation-layer question: whether an $f_3$-stress residual, computed after the amino-acid quotient has removed synonymous codon effects explainable by amino-acid composition, has any surviving association with position-resolved ribosome occupancy in matched coding-sequence windows. The row unit is finite: organism, coding sequence, codon position modulo $3$, local $f_3$-paired codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight surface, matched mRNA abundance, baseline covariates, and a codon-indexed or window-indexed ribosome-footprint readout. The coordinate is not a protein-output coordinate. It is a controlled window statistic whose admissible readback is the residual survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress}}(\mathrm{position\text{-}resolved\ occupancy}\mid Z)$, where $Z$ contains organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, and position-bin or amino-acid-bin controls.

\paragraph{Combinatorial support already present.}
The finite codon-side structure is populated. In the saturation run, the set reported as $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
so the two codons present in $M$ but absent from this saturation list are $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$. The corresponding boundary-zero table is empty in the recorded finite fact, so this packet should not be read as already possessing a zero-boundary certificate. A separate quotient-geometry run reports $|\pi_M|=10$, an $M$ face vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$, and a quotient face vector $(10,14,6,1)$. These values are sufficient to define the residual coordinate family and to require rank checks, but they do not themselves supply a ribosome-footprint measurement.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The proposed biological row is a same-organism and same-CDS join between ordered coding-sequence coordinates and ribosome-profiling footprints. A valid row must keep codon topology, modeled wobble weights, mRNA abundance, and ribosome occupancy as separate columns before any residual is estimated. The readout is position-resolved occupancy or a translation-efficiency residual derived from matched footprint and mRNA measurements. Modeled tAI or stAI weights can enter only as bounded annotation surfaces or baseline terms; they are not observed ribosome dynamics. A positive entry must survive simpler codon-composition baselines, amino-acid controls, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, and a rank test. If the design matrix is rank-degenerate, or if the controlled increment is non-positive, the proper output is a null or needs-data status, not a translation claim.

\paragraph{Nearby finite evidence.}
The closest powered cross-layer row currently present is not a ribosome-occupancy row. It concerns a positional structural-order ramp and reports five folds, a minimum joined count of $300$, and primary ramp length $50$. For ramp windows it gives weighted $f_3$ held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$ with sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$; for body windows it gives weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$ with sign-flip $p=0.54296875$; the paired ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$ with sign-flip $p=0.05859375$. The finite verdict is therefore ramp-positive but not localized. This is useful as a warning about scale and localization: a small positive residual can exist without licensing a stronger positional mechanism. Other finite rows show that the surrounding reality-contact machinery can carry large biological tables: the alternative-polyadenylation row records $24{,}740$ genes and $296{,}950$ PAS entries; an exon-inclusion row records $n=1{,}037$ with collinearity $-0.008429245619$; a cross-organism splice row records $n=50{,}633$ with all tissue-level signs positive; and the protein thermal-stability row records $n=8{,}092$ with UniProt sequence match rate $0.963104$. None of these rows is the required ribosome-occupancy join for this claim, but they show the expected finite-row standard: a named external table, explicit row counts, concrete covariates, and a scalar verdict.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is conditional. If matched ribosome-footprint windows are supplied and the controlled residual survival is strictly positive after the declared ablations, the chapter may report a bounded association between $f_3$-stress and a position-resolved translation readout. If the survival entry is non-positive, underpowered, or rank-degenerate, $f_3$-stress remains a codon-topology residual. No translation realization, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanistic explanation, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the codon-side coordinate, the quotient geometry, the wobble-weight surface, or the present structural-order ramp row without a separate contact at that layer.
