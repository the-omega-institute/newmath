\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{subsec:q6-translation-conditioned-protein-localization-boundary-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record isolates a narrow cross-layer question about synonymous residual coordinates in coding sequences.  The admissible table has one row per matched coding sequence or protein entry.  Each row contains a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate vector, a matched translation readout, an mRNA control, and a bounded protein-localization label or score.  The coordinate side is not an amino-acid sequence claim: it is the amino-acid-quotient-surviving synonymous remainder.  The localization side is not a structural, functional, trafficking-mechanism, or phenotype claim: it is only the externally attached localization readout present in the same finite row.  The intended readback is therefore a conditioned relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
  Q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Q_i$ is a residual synonymous coordinate, $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $L_k$ is a localization label, score, enrichment, or compartment distribution.  A direct association $Q_i \to L_k$ may be reported as a bounded protein-readout association only after the controls are stated; it is not a translation mechanism unless the same-row translation variable mediates the association under the matched design.

\paragraph{Available finite evidence.}
The presently verified finite computation supplies the residual-coordinate side and a separate cross-layer protein-readout precedent, not a localization result.  The computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual list has $9$ coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  &K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
  &Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the verified essentiality readout, the rows were real CDS records joined to measured protein abundance and lab knockout labels.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3737$ genes were used, with $285$ essential and $3452$ nonessential entries, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$.  The control design used $25$ columns, had $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, retained $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and had residual degrees of freedom $3713$.  Its reported residual response energy was $232.13814987247684$, explained energy was $2.243840654793728$, and the beyond-expression partial readout value was $R^2_{FE}=0.00966597112980509$.

For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5511$ genes were used, with $1168$ essential and $4343$ nonessential entries, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$.  The same $25$-column control family had $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, retained $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and had residual degrees of freedom $5487$.  Its residual response energy was $826.488384003322$, explained energy was $2.741892921952373$, and the beyond-expression partial readout value was $R^2_{FE}=0.003317521425614316$.  In both organisms the powered-rank condition was reported as sufficient, all $9$ residual coordinates survived the rank test, and the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold was $10^{-12}$.  These numbers establish that the $Q6$ coordinate matrix can remain full rank after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, M-density, and measured protein-abundance controls in a finite cross-layer protein-readout design.  They do not establish localization mediation.

\paragraph{Localization test form.}
A localization row is admissible only when the same coding sequence or protein identifier carries all required entries: codon counts for the $Q_i$ vector, a translation readout such as translation efficiency or another bounded translation statistic, a matched mRNA abundance control, and a protein-localization readout.  The localization readout may be a compartment label, imaging score, fractionation enrichment, proximity-labeling score, curated compartment distribution, or another explicitly bounded localization statistic keyed to the same protein or CDS-derived protein.  The finite test is the comparison between a direct residual-localization fit and a translation-conditioned fit.  In schematic residualized form, the admissible question is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  L_k &\sim Z + Q_i + T_j,\\
  T_j &\sim Z + Q_i,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes organism scope, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and the matched mRNA control, with additional study or batch covariates when the localization assay requires them.  A reported localization entry must state whether $Q_i$ retains signal after $T_j$ is included, whether $T_j$ carries the signal expected from $Q_i$, and whether the direct $Q_i \to L_k$ term collapses, weakens, or remains after translation conditioning.  The break condition is reached if the apparent localization association is promoted to a translation-mediated statement without the $Q_i \to T_j \to L_k$ same-row comparison.

\paragraph{Boundary against over-reading.}
The local claim is deliberately bounded.  A positive residual association with a localization label does not show ribosome dynamics unless the translation contact supplies that fact.  It does not show folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular function, protein trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, organism phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  A modeled tAI or stAI value can help define a translation-layer readout only when kept separate from observed localization data; by itself it is not a localization survival statistic.  Likewise, the verified essentiality computation is not a localization computation.  Its role here is limited to showing that the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual design has already been run in a finite protein-readout setting with real row counts, full residual coordinate rank, explicit controls, and nonzero bounded readout values.  The localization boundary remains a separate contact requirement.

\paragraph{Present status.}
The current record is ready as a boundary chapter because it specifies the finite row schema, the conditioning order, the mandatory controls, the break condition, and the non-promotion rule.  It is not a positive localization finding.  The missing reality contact is a same-organism, same-row localization table joined to the $9$ residual coordinates, a matched translation readout, and matched mRNA controls.  Until that table is attached and the conditioned statistic is computed, the strongest licensed statement is this: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates may be tested against protein localization only through a translation-conditioned finite-row design, and any direct residual-localization association remains a bounded protein-readout association rather than a mechanism, function, phenotype, or universal law.
