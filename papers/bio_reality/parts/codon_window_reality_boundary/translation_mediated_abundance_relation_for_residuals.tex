\subsection{Translation-mediated abundance relation for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinate ranges over the nine computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation coordinate $t_j$ is presently represented by per-protein modeled tAI, computed as the codon-count-weighted mean of organism-specific tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate $p_k$ is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from PAXdb abundance rows joined to real CDS codon counts.  The intended readout is therefore not a direct arrow $q_i \to p_k$, but the controlled chain $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$.

\paragraph{Controls and admissible rank.}
For each organism the residualization stage used a 24-column control block with rank $23$, consisting of an intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The $Q$ block had rank $9$, the translation block had rank $1$, and the joined $(Q,T)$ block had rank $10$ in every reported organism.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $1633$ in $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$ to $19372$ in $Mus\ musculus$, and every organism was marked powered-rank-sufficient for the reported matrix.  This supports a finite linear decomposition audit, but it does not by itself turn modeled tAI into observed ribosome motion or establish a perturbational translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-contact status.}
A smaller modeled-tAI survival calculation was also run on three organisms: $Escherichia\ coli\ K12$, $Homo\ sapiens$, and $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$.  The codon-count space contained $61$ sense codons.  The local GtRNAdb payload contained all standard amino-acid families: $87$ matched FASTA headers out of $89$ in $E.\ coli\ K12$, $432$ out of $432$ in human, and $275$ out of $275$ in yeast.  All three modeled tAI readouts had $61$ sense codons and zero raw-weight fallback count.  The bounded coupling audit found $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$.  Its own status remains underpowered for powered survival because the organism count is $3$, not at least $40$, and because the contact is modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling or measured translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation numbers.}
The powered cross-organism protein-abundance computation joined $12$ organism-specific CDS, tRNA, and abundance tables.  The joined row counts were $4042$ for $Bacillus\ subtilis$ subsp. $subtilis$ str. $168$, $11661$ for $Caenorhabditis\ elegans$, $14410$ for $Danio\ rerio$, $13295$ for $Drosophila\ melanogaster$, $3739$ for $Escherichia\ coli\ K12\ MG1655$, $12418$ for $Gallus\ gallus$, $19053$ for $Homo\ sapiens$, $19395$ for $Mus\ musculus$, $4129$ for $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$ str. $mc^2\ 155$, $13322$ for $Rattus\ norvegicus$, $5815$ for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, and $1656$ for $Sulfolobus\ solfataricus$.  The total $R^2$ and conditional $R^2$ decomposition gave positive mediated fractions in all twelve organisms:

Since all twelve fractions lie in $[0,1]$, the finite decomposition is numerically coherent as a cross-sectional variance partition.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-side comparison.}
A separate yeast five-way join used $4759$ rows linking CDS codon counts, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, protein abundance, and a 5-prime CDS folding-free-energy proxy.  The reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$ and the overall join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$.  In that yeast-only decomposition, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $S_Q$-to-structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$.  These numbers are relevant only as a boundary check: the structure proxy is sequence-derived 5-prime CDS MFE, not a folded protein structure assay.

\paragraph{Axis identity and earlier code-table contact.}
The structural-order axis audit computed the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ axes and found exactly one coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, and its one-sided sign-flip value was $0.03125$.  The other eight axes had larger sign-flip values: $0.142578125$ for $Arg\_AGR$, $0.0693359375$ for $Ile\_AUA$, $0.2294921875$ for $K\_AAA$, $0.779296875$ for $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $0.24609375$ for $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $0.4873046875$ for $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $0.2158203125$ for $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and $0.529296875$ for $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$.  The earlier confirmatory code-table audit supplied a separate finite sanity check: $40$ successes out of $40$ in the boundary probe, $40$ successes out of $40$ in the independent replication, one-sided binomial value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ in each case, and zero unexpected successes in $20$ negative controls and $20$ critical ablations.  Those table-level facts do not promote the present mediation surface beyond the layers actually measured here.

\paragraph{Verdict for this record.}
The numerical evidence supports a finite, controlled association pattern in which $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, modeled tAI, and matched protein abundance form a coherent mediation matrix over twelve organisms.  The result is nevertheless not ready as a full biological realization claim, because the declared conjecture requires a translation step that survives as an observed or otherwise separately validated translation readout and also requires matched mRNA abundance control for the protein-abundance step.  The powered matrix uses modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb abundance, while the matched mRNA-control route is not present in the twelve-organism protein-abundance computation.  Thus the admissible statement is a cross-sectional statistical decomposition through modeled tAI, not a causal translation mechanism, not ribosome occupancy, not folding, not physical admissibility, not molecular function, and not a universal biological law.  Promotion to a stronger claim requires same-CDS translation rows, matched mRNA abundance, and protein-abundance rows in the same controlled design, followed by the same $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$ attenuation test.
