\subsection{Translation survival of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual matrix}
\label{sec:bioreality-translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Object.}
The empirical object is the $9 \times K$ survival surface obtained by testing the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates against translation-adjacent readouts after the amino-acid quotient has already been imposed.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  For a coordinate $q_i$ and a readout $r_j$, the statistic is estimated after residualizing against the control block $Z$: amino-acid composition, organism context when present, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the row-level experiments this control block has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in the main CDS joins.  The reported entry is not just a raw covariance; it is a residual survival score, read as partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement after the simpler baseline has been removed.

\paragraph{Matrix statistic.}
The matrix calculation uses the residualized pairing
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell_j^{\perp}$ is the readout direction after the declared controls.  A positive $A_{ij}$ is only a coupling sign and is not promoted to survival unless the controlled $S_{ij}$ entry is computed.  The proxy coupling pass gives one finite check on geometry: in the three-organism contact using Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12, all nine residual coordinates have nonzero coupling against the tRNA-Leu CUN-vs-UUR log-copy spectrum, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ above the $0.01$ threshold.  This row is deliberately weaker than translation survival because it uses tRNA-copy and codon-count contacts rather than measured ribosome or protein-output readouts.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
The powered modeled-tAI experiment supplies the strongest bounded translation-layer row.  Across $40$ organisms, modeled per-codon tAI was computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the nine synonymous-residual coordinates.  The residual design has $rank(X_{\sim})=9$, $rank(Z)=23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The resulting $9 \times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the positive-entry threshold set at $10^{-12}$ and the degeneracy guard at $0.999999$.  A wider organism sweep computes modeled-tAI rows for $41$ organisms and reports an $R^2_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ under $1000$ permutations.  These numbers show that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals are not absorbed by amino-acid, GC3, length, organism, and $M$-density controls when the readout is modeled codon adaptation.  They do not, by themselves, say that ribosomes were observed.

\paragraph{Measured ribosome contact.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy row attaches a local measured contact.  Ordered CDS rows and ribosome occupancy windows were joined for $5754$ genes, with $5754$ gene-id overlaps, $6013$ ordered CDS genes available, and zero length mismatches.  The position confound is handled by exact file-provided window and codon-index bins together with amino-acid bins; the effect uses occupancy demeaned within bin, and the null shuffles synonymous labels only inside the same bin.  Nine families are computed, all with at least $200$ permutations.  With $DL=2$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and the support-alignment gate $C_H>0$, three families pass all gates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This is a measured ribosome-occupancy separation for synonymous choices in local windows, not a causal elongation model.

\paragraph{Leucine finite row.}
The CUN/UUR Leu contact is finite and context-sensitive.  In the three-organism table, the partial Pearson correlation between log codon ratio and log tRNA-Leu copy ratio is $1.0$ with $n=3$, while the organism signs are $+1,-1,+1$ for log ratios $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  The row is therefore a positive finite contact and also a warning: sign is not uniform across organisms, and $n=3$ cannot be read as a population-level direction.

\paragraph{Protein and mRNA side readouts.}
Downstream protein and transcript rows give additional bounded context without replacing the translation test.  In yeast growth-condition protein abundance, the shared GFP/PAXdb join has $2209$ proteins; $S^{QP}$ gives $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD and $0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD, with $f_3$-stress dominant in both and pairwise stability $0.9993775935182967$ by cosine.  In human tissue proteomics, Adult B-cells join $9244$ proteins with $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ and dominant $Arg_{AGR}$; Adult Heart joins $6468$ proteins with $R^2_{QP}=0.02320896321347296$ and dominant $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$; Adult Liver joins $8664$ proteins with $R^2_{QP}=0.026133456228248052$ and dominant $Ile_{AUA}$.  Pairwise tissue cosines are $0.6883655232187615$, $0.8990093450963941$, and $0.9176793373754919$.

The mRNA-stability rows show that some residual structure also reaches transcript stability, but this remains a cross-layer statistical association.  Yeast log half-life joins $5085$ rows and gives $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$, and dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$; the same join has $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ and cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$.  E. coli joins $2720$ rows at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$ and gives $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, $p=0.000999000999000999$, dominant $Arg_{AGR}$, and cosine $-0.7784923375569599$ to the same-join protein row.  Human consensus relative half-life joins $16165$ rows across $54$ dataset columns and gives $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, $p=0.000999000999000999$, dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and cosine $0.6351183883924848$ to the protein row.  The yeast mediation scalar is small but nonzero: $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, absorbed variance $0.027596042074056304$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Complement residuals.}
The modeled-tAI complement analysis separates the protein-abundance component explained by $B^{*}_{Q6}$ from the part aligned with modeled translation.  The complement fractions $d$ are organism-specific: Danio rerio $0.874628721049266$, Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 $0.6629960038578532$, Gallus gallus $0.9400341806302775$, Homo sapiens $0.9208949355679455$, Mycobacterium smegmatis str. mc2 155 $0.8972672018746489$, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae $0.21173219572706703$.  The corresponding modeled-translation fractions $m=1-d$ are $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  The support readback remains nine-dimensional in each organism; for example, yeast complement support is carried by $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, while human support is carried by $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite rows support a bounded translation-survival statement: after amino-acid quotienting and the declared $Z$ controls, the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface has positive survival against modeled translation-adaptation readouts, and separate measured ribosome-occupancy windows detect gated synonymous-family separation for $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Protein abundance, mRNA stability, and complement rows show additional cross-layer statistical structure, but they are not substitutes for a same-row measured translation-efficiency matrix.  No claim of translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal pathway, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that specific measurement and its own controlled statistic.
