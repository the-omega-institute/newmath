\subsection{Translation survival of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual matrix}
\label{sec:translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix-translation-realization}

\paragraph{Object under test.}
The object considered here is the $9 \times K$ survival matrix obtained by pairing the nine synonymous-residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress with translation-related readouts.  The intended statistic is not a raw codon-count association.  For a coordinate $q_i$ and a readout $r_j$, the readout is first residualized against the control block consisting of amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The survival entry is then the residual description gain, equivalently the controlled partial $R^2$, carried by $q_i$ after those controls have been removed.  In matrix notation the tested quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma \ell_{j}^{\perp},\\
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\hbox{amino-acid composition},\ \hbox{organism},\ GC3,\ \hbox{length},\ M\hbox{-density}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry means that the coordinate improves the controlled readout model; it is not treated as evidence for folding, physical admissibility, biological role, causal mechanism, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Finite contact geometry.}
The first finite contact used three organisms, Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12, to compare the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates with a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy.  All nine coordinates were computed in the $61$-codon count space, and the proxy coupling test did not meet its null condition: the maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, with $9$ nonzero entries above the $0.01$ threshold.  This is a strong coordinate-to-proxy coupling row, but it is not a survival row, since the proxy is tRNA gene-copy geometry rather than ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  The accompanying CUN/UUR Leu finite row gave Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ over $n=3$ organisms, with log-ratio signs $(1,-1,1)$ from values $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  The sign reversal is recorded as context dependence rather than as a population-level direction.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
A powered modeled-readout computation used $40$ organisms and formed modeled per-codon tAI from tRNA anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were paired with nine modeled tAI readout columns, producing a $9 \times 9$ $S^{QT}$ surface.  The control block had $24$ columns, rank $23$, and left $17$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ residualization.  The residualized coordinate matrix had rank $9$, and the modeled translation surface gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\perp Z} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared positive threshold was $10^{-12}$ and the degeneracy threshold was $0.999999$, so this finite row is powered and nondegenerate for modeled tAI.  It supports the statement that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual system is not absorbed by amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density when the readout is the bounded modeled-tAI panel.  The result remains below direct ribosome evidence: modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, measured occupancy, proteomics, folding, or protein realization.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance contacts and the modeled-tAI complement.}
Six organism-level abundance joins were used to read how much of the abundance component aligned with modeled tAI and how much remained outside it.  The joined row counts were $14410$ for Danio rerio, $3739$ for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for Gallus gallus, $19053$ for Homo sapiens, $4129$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae; each used a $24$-column control block with rank $23$.  The complement fractions $d$ for the modeled-tAI-complement part of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component were
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{Danio} &= 0.874628721049266,\\
d_{Ecoli} &= 0.6629960038578532,\\
d_{Gallus} &= 0.9400341806302775,\\
d_{Human} &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
d_{Mycobacterium} &= 0.8972672018746489,\\
d_{Yeast} &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding direct $R^2_{QP}$ values were $0.04613529805760884$, $0.10767526667768725$, $0.03980793595345331$, $0.04454565117192484$, $0.032090091625816136$, and $0.29837624987287115$.  These numbers show that the protein-abundance component is organism-dependent and that modeled tAI does not uniformly absorb the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance direction.  They do not establish a causal path from codon residuals to protein output.

\paragraph{Candidate readouts for the residual abundance direction.}
The direction-aware supplementary-readout ranking used the regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description length $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{col})$, while also reporting the unpenalized product $A_H C_H$.  In Danio rerio, measured translation efficiency was the leading candidate with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, score $0.001566051846174291$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, null $p95=0.0003297458343745959$, and $n=13140$ joined rows.  The same organism also placed mRNA second with score $0.000734588577977589$, $A_H=0.011973033879985887$, and $C_H=0.896563785384548$.  In Homo sapiens, the strongest ranked candidate was complex membership with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, $A_HC_H=0.006566030115340095$, score $-0.0034339698846599055$, threshold $0.0002646171322365322$, and $n=18335$; measured translation efficiency had $A_H=0.002894714925796667$, $C_H=-0.10813537216189718$, and score $-0.01031302107580362$ over $11554$ rows.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, mRNA was the top candidate with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, and score $-0.008853737772559931$, while measured translation efficiency had $A_H=0.0002762783754376191$, $C_H=0.22623719083092017$, and score $-0.009937495556453664$.  Escherichia coli K-12 had no selected $H_o^*$, with mRNA and measured translation efficiency both below the penalized selection line, and Mycobacterium smegmatis had no computable ranking entries.  Thus the residual-abundance contact is finite and informative, but it is not a universal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Condition, tissue, and mRNA-stability finite rows.}
Yeast protein abundance under two conditions gave stable $S^{QP}$ shape but not a causal interpretation.  For YEPD and SD, the shared join was $2209$ proteins; the controlled $R^2_{QP}$ values were $0.3280825603424665$ and $0.3050385538305022$, both dominated by $f3$-stress, with cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ across the two condition-specific surfaces using $1000$ permutations.  Human tissue proteomics gave three further finite rows: Adult B cells had $n=9244$, $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$, and dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$; Adult heart had $n=6468$, $R^2_{QP}=0.02320896321347296$, and dominant coordinate $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$; Adult liver had $n=8664$, $R^2_{QP}=0.026133456228248052$, and dominant coordinate $Ile_{AUA}$.  Pairwise tissue cosines were $0.6883655232187615$, $0.8990093450963941$, and $0.9176793373754919$ over $1000$ permutations.  Yeast mRNA half-life supplied a separate transcript-layer row: $n=5085$, $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, cosine $0.8040935468384264$, permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$, and dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ for mRNA stability versus $f3$-stress for abundance.  Its join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$ against the Neymotin half-life table, with $5379$ valid half-life records and payload hash cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite record supports a bounded conclusion: the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual system has a positive, powered survival surface against modeled tAI after the declared controls, and it has several nontrivial downstream abundance, condition, tissue, and transcript-stability contacts.  The direct measured-translation version of the matrix remains open unless same-organism or same-CDS ribosome-profiling, measured TE, or stAI rows are joined to codon counts and evaluated under the same $Z$ controls and simpler-baseline ablation.  The chapter therefore records modeled translation survival and adjacent finite contacts, while refusing promotion to measured ribosome-mediated translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality without a separate layer-matched contact and statistic.
