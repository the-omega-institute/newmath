\subsection{Translation Survival of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Matrix}
\label{sec:bioreality-translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Question.}
The object tested here is the survival matrix $S_{ij}$ obtained by pairing nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ with translation-adjacent readouts.  The coordinate list is
$\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  For each coordinate $q_i$ and each readout $r_j$, the intended statistic asks whether $q_i$ retains positive explanatory value after the controls $Z$ have removed amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the density of the simpler codon-support baseline.  In the finite computations below, survival is therefore not identified with raw codon usage, not identified with tRNA gene copy, and not identified with a sign of an unadjusted coupling.  It is a controlled residual readback.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
The finite-row target can be written as a residual projection test.  Let $X$ be the matrix whose columns are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and let $y_j$ be a translation or translation-adjacent readout.  With $P_Z$ denoting projection onto the control span, the tested part is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \widetilde X &= (I-P_Z)X, \\
  \widetilde y_j &= (I-P_Z)y_j, \\
  S_{\bullet j} &\simeq \operatorname{gain}(\widetilde y_j;\widetilde X),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the reported gain is a partial-$R^2$ or equivalent description-length improvement.  Positive support for the chapter requires at least one entry of this controlled matrix to be positive after the baseline ablation; a proxy covariance coupling $A_{ij}$ is only a screening measurement unless the residual survival score is also computed.

\paragraph{Finite rows supporting modeled translation readback.}
A powered modeled-tAI run used $40$ organisms and all nine residual coordinates.  The control design had $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The residual coordinate matrix retained rank $9$, and the modeled translation readout produced
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579, \\
  \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9, \\
  \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23, \\
  n_{\mathrm{organism}} &= 40.
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared positive-entry threshold was $10^{-12}$, and the computed survival surface had shape $9\times 9$.  A broader organism sweep reached $41$ organisms and reported a modeled-$tAI$ $R^2_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ over $1000$ permutations.  These rows show that the nine-coordinate residual system is not absorbed by the amino-acid, context, $GC3$, length, and codon-support baseline when the readout is the explicitly modeled tAI surface.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling and the Leu gate.}
Before the powered modeled-tAI rows, a three-organism proxy computation loaded human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12 codon-count contacts and a tRNA-Leu copy spectrum.  All nine $q_i$ residuals were computed in the $61$-codon count space, and the proxy coupling matrix had $9$ nonzero entries with
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328, \\
  \text{coupling threshold} &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding CUN/UUR Leu gate had $n=3$, Pearson correlation $1.0$, and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ after the available control, but its log-ratio signs were $(1,-1,1)$.  This row is therefore a finite contact with sign reversal across organisms, not a universal direction rule.  It motivates the matrix test but cannot substitute for a controlled $S_{ij}$ entry.

\paragraph{Measured ribosome-window contact.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy computation joined $5754$ genes after the length check, with $5754$ genes in the occupancy file and $6013$ ordered-CDS genes.  The analysis used file-provided codon-window and codon-index bins, demeaned occupancy within those bins, and shuffled synonymous labels only within the same bin.  All $9$ families had at least $200$ permutations.  With the rule $C_H>0$, description-length cost $DL=2$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, the families passing all gates were $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  This is the strongest direct ribosome-window contact in the finite record: it shows position-controlled synonymous separation in local occupancy windows.  It is still a descriptive occupancy separation, not a causal elongation mechanism.

\paragraph{Protein abundance and translation-complement rows.}
Several protein-abundance rows show that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ also survives in downstream abundance regressions under the same style of controls.  In yeast condition-specific abundance, the shared protein count was $2209$; the SD condition gave $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$, the YEPD condition gave $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$, and both had $\texttt{f3\_stress}$ as the dominant coordinate.  The condition comparison produced cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ over $1000$ permutations.  In human tissue-specific abundance, adult B cells had $n=9244$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ with dominant $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$; adult heart had $n=6468$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.02320896321347296$ with dominant $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$; adult liver had $n=8664$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.026133456228248052$ with dominant $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$.  Pairwise tissue cosines were $0.6883655232187615$, $0.8990093450963941$, and $0.9176793373754919$.

\paragraph{Complement decomposition.}
The modeled-translation complement calculation separates the abundance component explained by $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ from the part aligned with modeled tAI.  The finite list of complement fractions was
$$
\begin{aligned}
  d_{\mathrm{danio}} &= 0.874628721049266, \\
  d_{\mathrm{ecoli}} &= 0.6629960038578532, \\
  d_{\mathrm{gallus}} &= 0.9400341806302775, \\
  d_{\mathrm{human}} &= 0.9208949355679455, \\
  d_{\mathrm{mycobacterium}} &= 0.8972672018746489, \\
  d_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding modeled-translation-mediated fractions were $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  Thus yeast is the organism in this finite panel where the abundance component is most aligned with modeled translation, while several metazoan and bacterial rows retain a large modeled-translation complement.  A ranking table then tested candidate readouts for this complement: in danio, measured translation efficiency had $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, score $0.001566051846174291$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, and $n=13140$; in human, measured translation efficiency had $A_H=0.002894714925796667$, $C_H=-0.10813537216189718$, score $-0.01031302107580362$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0004670613303535708$, and $n=11554$; in yeast, measured translation efficiency had $A_H=0.0002762783754376191$, $C_H=0.22623719083092017$, score $-0.009937495556453664$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0015825636518639428$, and $n=5128$.  These signs and scores are heterogeneous, so they should be read as organism-scoped residual structure rather than a shared biological direction.

\paragraph{mRNA stability controls and mediation.}
The mRNA-stability rows give a second control axis.  In yeast, $5085$ rows joined through the Neymotin table with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, $R^2_{QP}$ on the same join was $0.2761140373962526$, the dominant stability coordinate was $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, and the $S^{QM}$ versus $S^{QP}$ cosine was $0.8040935468384264$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In \emph{E. coli}, $2720$ rows joined with reported CDS hit rate $0.9978649586335735$; $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, dominant coordinate $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In human, $16165$ rows joined with reported CDS hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ over $54$ consensus columns; $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, dominant coordinate $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  The yeast abundance-residual mediation row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526, \\
  R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.24851799532219632, \\
  R^2_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by\ }M} &= 0.027596042074056304, \\
  \text{mediation fraction} &= 0.09994436477872035,
\end{aligned}
$$
with null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Coordinate decompositions.}
Two decomposition rows support the claim that the matrix signal is not a single opaque aggregate.  A yeast per-coordinate reducibility run used $n=1258$, base $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, and $Z$-basis rank $24$; eight named coordinates had a real-source flag, with the table retaining all nine rows.  A family decomposition of $\texttt{f3\_stress}$ used $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, $n=1800$, base $n=4759$, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations.  It reported $P_{f3}$ norm squared $52.76911713166682$, target norm squared $430.42705205115925$, and $P_{f3}$ fraction of target $0.12259712041843235$; $19$ of the $21$ family subcoordinates were flagged as real sources under the held-out/permutation gate.  These rows localize part of the abundance-side signal to named synonymous families while preserving the same control discipline.

\paragraph{Verdict and scope.}
The finite evidence is sufficient to state a bounded translation-layer realization result for the modeled-tAI matrix and a bounded ribosome-window occupancy contact for selected synonymous families.  It is also sufficient to state that downstream protein-abundance and mRNA-stability residuals carry organism-specific $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ structure under the declared controls.  The strongest compact summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \exists i,j\; S_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{for the modeled translation readout panel}, \\
  \exists i\; \text{occupancy separation}_i &> \text{within-bin null}_{95} \quad \text{for measured ribosome windows}, \\
  \exists i\; S^{QP}_i &> 0 \quad \text{for several protein-abundance joins}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This does not say that tRNA gene copy is tRNA abundance or charging.  It does not say that modeled tAI is observed ribosome movement.  It does not say that protein abundance proves folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Each of those stronger readings requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  Within those limits, the chapter records a positive finite-row survival result for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ at the translation-adjacent interface and a set of explicit boundaries preventing promotion beyond the measured or modeled contacts.
