\subsection{Translation survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual matrix}
\label{subsec:translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix-translation-realization}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and quotient.}
The object tested here is the nine-coordinate residual family $B^*_{Q6}$ after the amino-acid quotient has already been imposed.  The rows are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The readout side is deliberately narrow: modeled tAI or stAI weights, measured translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy contacts when present, mRNA controls, and protein-abundance contacts used only as downstream readouts.  The controlling design is the same across the finite rows: amino-acid composition, organism context where applicable, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline are included before any residual coordinate is allowed to count as surviving.

\paragraph{Matrix statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a readout $r_j$, the survival entry is the controlled gain left after projecting away the declared controls $Z$.  In the computed matrix contact the relevant summary has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j \mid Z),\\
A_{ij} &= q_i^T \Sigma \ell_j^\perp,\\
Z &= (\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{organism},GC3,\hbox{length},M\hbox{-density}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The proxy coupling run used a 61-codon count space and the nine projected synonymous coordinates.  Its null was that every row would satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy Leu tRNA-copy readout.  That null did not occur: all nine entries were nonzero above the threshold, and the largest absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.  This is a coordinate-contact result, not a survival result by itself, because the proxy readout was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum rather than a directly measured translation-output matrix.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
The strongest finite translation-layer row is the powered modeled-tAI computation.  It used $40$ organisms, all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, a $9 \times 9$ modeled tAI readout matrix, $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The residualized design had full coordinate rank $9$ and produced
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{\perp Z}) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold was $10^{-12}$, so the modeled tAI surface is a powered positive $S^{QT}$ contact under the stated controls.  A wider organism sweep reached $41$ organisms.  In that sweep the modeled-tAI $R^2_{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using $1000$ permutations.  These numbers support a modeled translation-readout survival statement, while staying below measured ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Three-organism Leu contact.}
The CUN/UUR Leu contact loaded Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  The finite row reported Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ with $n=3$ for the log CUN/UUR usage ratio against the tRNA-Leu copy ratio.  The signs were not uniform across organisms: the log ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+,-,+$.  Thus the row is a useful contact for the Leu gate but cannot be read as a population-level or universal directional law.

\paragraph{Measured ribosome-occupancy contact.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy computation joined $5754$ genes after length checking, with $5754$ gene identifiers overlapping between the occupancy file and ordered CDS records, and no length mismatches.  The null shuffled synonymous labels only within the same file-provided window, codon-index, and amino-acid bins.  All nine families had at least $200$ permutations.  With $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, description-length cost $DL=2$, and the support rule $C_H>0$, the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This is a measured local ribosome-occupancy separation result with position confounding controlled.  It still remains descriptive: the statistic says that synonymous codon choices co-vary with local relative ribosome occupancy inside matched codon windows, not that a causal translation mechanism has been identified.

\paragraph{Protein and mRNA readouts below the translation boundary.}
Downstream protein and mRNA rows are retained as bounded cross-layer evidence rather than promoted to function.  In yeast abundance data, the two growth-condition rows used $2209$ shared proteins.  The rich-medium row had $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$, while the minimal-medium row had $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$; both had dominant coordinate $f_3$-stress and residual degrees of freedom $2186$.  Human tissue protein-abundance rows gave $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ in Adult B cells with dominant $Arg_{AGR}$ over $9244$ joined proteins, $R^2_{QP}=0.02320896321347296$ in Adult Heart with dominant $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$ over $6468$ joined proteins, and $R^2_{QP}=0.026133456228248052$ in Adult Liver with dominant $Ile_{AUA}$ over $8664$ joined proteins.  These are protein-abundance projections, not folding, function, or physical-admissibility contacts.

\paragraph{mRNA stability and mediation rows.}
The yeast mRNA-stability row joined $5085$ CDS records to the Neymotin half-life table with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$.  Its mRNA half-life statistic was $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, with dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  On the same join the protein-abundance statistic was $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, and the cosine between $S^{QM}$ and same-join $S^{QP}$ was $0.8040935468384264$.  The mediation decomposition gave $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, absorbed amount $0.027596042074056304$, and mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$ against a permutation null mean $0.00019674487584783138$ and null $95\%$ value $0.0014571003414947088$.  E. coli half-life data joined $2720$ rows with hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ and gave $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, dominant $Arg_{AGR}$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  Human consensus half-life data joined $16165$ rows with hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and gave $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These rows show that residual codon coordinates remain statistically visible in mRNA and abundance measurements, but they do not turn the translation matrix into a causal pathway.

\paragraph{Modeled-tAI complement and candidate readouts.}
The modeled-tAI complement decomposition records how much of the abundance component remains outside modeled tAI.  The $d$ values were $0.874628721049266$ for Danio rerio, $0.6629960038578532$ for E. coli K-12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ for Gallus gallus, $0.9208949355679455$ for Homo sapiens, $0.8972672018746489$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis, and $0.21173219572706703$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding $m$ values were $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  In the direction-aware candidate table, Danio rerio selected measured TE as $H_o^*$ with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, score $0.001566051846174291$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, null $95\%$ value $0.0003297458343745959$, and $13140$ joined rows.  Homo sapiens selected complex membership; Saccharomyces cerevisiae selected mRNA; Gallus gallus selected complex membership; E. coli and Mycobacterium smegmatis had no selected $H_o^*$ under the same scoring convention.  The outcome is heterogeneous, so it supports readout-specific residual structure rather than a single universal biological channel.

\paragraph{Coordinate-level checks.}
A yeast coordinate decomposition used $1258$ joined proteins, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, and target residualized log protein abundance.  Eight of the nine listed coordinates had a real-source flag in the held-out/permutation test: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The $f_3$ family decomposition used $1800$ joined rows, $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations.  Its total $f_3$ projection had squared norm $52.76911713166682$ and accounted for fraction $0.12259712041843235$ of the target energy; $19$ of the $21$ family subcoordinates were listed as real-source families.  These checks give a row-wise reason that the matrix signal is not only a pooled artifact.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite data support the bounded statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ is not absorbed by the amino-acid, context, GC3, length, and $M$-density baseline model on modeled translation readouts, and that at least some measured ribosome-occupancy and downstream abundance contacts are compatible with residual coordinate survival.  The chapter therefore records a positive translation-layer realization only in this restricted sense.  No translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  Each such stronger statement would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic, and the present matrix does not supply that higher-layer evidence.
