\subsection{Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Surface}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the finite surface whose rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout. The computed coordinate list has nine entries: K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress. The protein-abundance run used per-protein modeled tAI as $T$ and log10 PAXdb abundance in ppm as $Y$, with real CDS codon counts joined to organism-matched tRNA-copy data. The readout is therefore not a direct $q_i \to p_k$ assertion: the admissible statistical object is the controlled path $Q \to T \to P$, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline kept in the control block.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank diagnostics.}
The powered mediation audit joined twelve organism panels. The row counts were 4042 proteins for Bacillus subtilis subsp. subtilis str. 168, 11661 for Caenorhabditis elegans, 14410 for Danio rerio, 13295 for Drosophila melanogaster, 3739 for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, 12418 for Gallus gallus, 19053 for Homo sapiens, 19395 for Mus musculus, 4129 for Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, 13322 for Rattus norvegicus, 5815 for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and 1656 for Sulfolobus solfataricus. In every panel the residualized rank check was sufficient: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$, with 24 control columns before degeneracy screening. The residual degrees of freedom ranged from 1633 in Sulfolobus to 19372 in mouse, and every finite panel kept the mediation fraction inside the unit interval. The per-protein tAI calculation used all-tRNA copy totals ranging from 47 in Sulfolobus to 8874 in zebrafish; zero raw wobble weights required geometric-mean filling in Bacillus subtilis (3 codons), Escherichia coli (1), Mycobacterium smegmatis (1), and Sulfolobus (1), while the other eight organisms required no such filling.

\paragraph{Mediation readout.}
The joint nine-dimensional statistic reports positive finite mediation fractions in all twelve organism panels, but the magnitude is organism-specific. The strongest value was yeast, with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. Escherichia coli gave $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$. Bacillus gave $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$, Drosophila $0.6165167748739756$, Caenorhabditis $0.4872268357227724$, zebrafish $0.3157053953614641$, human $0.27206289160119157$, chicken $0.1976903964724546$, rat $0.17489681798543982$, mouse $0.15488799907323988$, Mycobacterium $0.08397602919861291$, and Sulfolobus $0.01850668564589326$. These finite values support a controlled cross-layer association in the registered sense: after residualizing the declared controls, modeled tAI attenuates part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ association with matched protein abundance.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival calculation found 81 entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$ across three organisms, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$ and the same value on the largest diagonal entry. The three-organism panel covered Escherichia coli K12, human, and yeast; all three had 61 sense codons with zero raw weight fallback count equal to 0 in that readout. The same audit explicitly classed the result as modeled and underpowered for a population survival claim, since $n=3$ organisms is below the requested $40$ matched-species level. The cross-layer mediation surface therefore uses modeled codon-adaptation information as a bounded translation covariate, not as an observed ribosome-profile measurement.

\paragraph{Related finite checks.}
Several neighboring audits constrain how much may be read from the positive mediation fractions. The yeast five-way structure mediation join had 4759 rows, a reported CDS join hit rate of 0.9156038419146592, a full join hit rate of 0.8818572656921754, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$; its structure-only fraction was only 0.003284564452662019 and its structure-incremental permutation p-value was 0.1258741258741259. A dynamic mass-balance audit on 120 genes and 718 intervals was negative for the stronger closure, with $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$. A turnover audit on 3290 genes was also negative beyond the declared expression, TE, pLDDT, and N-end controls, with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta$ description length $=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$. The cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit used 80 orthogroups, 240 rows, and 3 species; its positive gate failed because $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$ did not exceed the null maximum 0.05427157066522958 and leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2$ was $-0.0021477729853302424$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence is sufficient for a BioReality statement at the cross-layer relation level: the registered $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block has a controlled, translation-mediated association with matched protein abundance on the twelve-organism modeled-tAI/PAXdb surface. The result is best stated as a statistical decomposition of residual association, not as causal proof. It is also not a claim that a direct $Q \to P$ edge is biologically admissible without the $Q \to T$ step. The local exclusion is strict: no observed translation dynamics, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organism-level phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this surface unless an additional layer-matched measurement supplies that specific contact and its own controlled statistic.
