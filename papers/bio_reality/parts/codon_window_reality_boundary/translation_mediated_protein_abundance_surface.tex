\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance surface}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The claim is a finite mediation statement for synonymous residual coordinates in coding sequences.  Its rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The admissible readback is not the direct arrow $q_i\to p_k$.  The admissible readback is the controlled chain $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$, with amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance retained as controls.  In the finite run used here, the coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate $P$ is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance ppm joined to real CDS codon counts.  Across the twelve organism rows, the joined protein counts are 4042, 11661, 14410, 13295, 3739, 12418, 19053, 19395, 4129, 13322, 5815, and 1656 for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, \emph{Danio rerio}, \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, \emph{Gallus gallus}, human, mouse, \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, rat, yeast, and \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, respectively.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, let $X_Q$ be the residualized nine-dimensional $Q$ matrix, let $T$ be the residualized modeled-tAI vector, and let $Y$ be residualized protein abundance after the control matrix $Z$.  The reported scalar is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{R^2(Y\sim X_Q)-R^2(Y\sim X_Q\mid T)}{R^2(Y\sim X_Q)},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the direct term $c'=R^2(Y\sim X_Q\mid T)$ and the total term $c=R^2(Y\sim X_Q)$.  Every organism row passed the rank-sufficiency check: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X_Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X_Q,T)=10$ in each row, with residual degrees of freedom ranging from 1633 in \emph{S. solfataricus} to 19372 in mouse.  The mediated fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=&(0.6085,0.4872,0.3157,0.6165,0.6129,0.1977,\\
&0.2721,0.1549,0.0840,0.1749,0.9492,0.0185),
\end{aligned}
$$
in the organism order listed above.  The corresponding total $R^2$ values $c$ were
$$
\begin{aligned}
c=&(0.06191,0.10218,0.04614,0.07710,0.10768,0.03981,\\
&0.04455,0.04983,0.03209,0.02764,0.29838,0.06398),
\end{aligned}
$$
and the conditional direct values $c'$ were
$$
\begin{aligned}
c'=&(0.02424,0.05239,0.03157,0.02956,0.04168,0.03194,\\
&0.03243,0.04211,0.02940,0.02281,0.01517,0.06279).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite table contains positive translation-conditioned attenuation in all twelve organism rows, with the largest fraction in yeast, $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041$, and the smallest fraction in \emph{S. solfataricus}, $M^{QTP}=0.0185066856$.  The yeast row has $c=0.2983762499$, $c'=0.0151709094$, and indirect increment $0.2832053406$; the human row has $c=0.0445456512$, $c'=0.0324264325$, and indirect increment $0.0121192187$.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The same coordinate family also has a bounded translation-weight contact: three organisms were evaluated for modeled tAI coupling, with 61 sense codons in each organism and zero raw-weight fallback count equal to $0$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast in that run.  The finite modeled-tAI matrix had 81 entries above the $0.01$ coupling threshold and maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$.  This establishes that the coordinate list can be read against organism-scoped codon-adaptation weights, but it is explicitly not ribosome profiling and not proteomics.  The three-organism modeled-tAI run is underpowered for a powered survival law requiring at least 40 matched species, so it functions only as a bounded translation-readout check.

\paragraph{Separated controls and related rows.}
A yeast five-way abundance decomposition joined 4759 rows across CDS, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a 5-prime CDS MFE structure proxy.  In that row, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.0032845645$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507779$, and the right-tail permutation probabilities were $0.0279720280$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741259$ for the incremental structure term.  These numbers keep the protein-abundance mediation surface separate from a folded-structure claim.

Other finite rows place the same boundary in context.  The structural-order audit identified $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$ as the dominant axis, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502$ and sign-flip probability $0.03125$, while the other coordinate probabilities included $0.142578125$ for $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $0.0693359375$ for $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, and $0.779296875$ for $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint row used 80 orthogroups and 240 rows over 3 species; it returned $\Delta R^2=0.0325051502$, description-length change $-258.6010084302$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477730$, null 95-percent threshold $0.0542715707$, and no positive selection-imprint verdict.  The mass-balance row used 120 genes and 718 intervals; it reported $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628202$, $R^2_{L1}=0.9964106289$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443378$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0758443809$, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693$.  The turnover row used 3290 genes and gave $R^2_{L2}=0.0557408516$, $R^2_{L3}=0.0502788722$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0054619794$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These companion rows do not change the present claim; they constrain the interpretation of the protein-abundance mediation surface.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result is therefore a statistical cross-layer decomposition: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a positive modeled-translation-mediated component in matched protein-abundance rows after the declared controls.  The strongest row is yeast, but the table is not a universal law and no organism sign is promoted into a population-level direction.  A direct $Q\to P$ association is not treated as protein realization unless the $Q\to T$ step is present and separated from transcription by matched mRNA control.  No translation mechanism, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection claim, perturbation rescue, or universality follows from these rows alone.  Such claims require their own layer-matched measurements and their own controlled finite statistics.  The Phase-one table confirms only the earlier codon-geometry module classification surface against the NCBI genetic-code table, with 40 of 40 successes in both strong binomial checks, one-sided probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ in each, and zero unexpected successes in 20 negative-control and 20 ablation rows; it supplies no protein-output mechanism.  The current chapter is consequently ready as a bounded protein-abundance mediation record, not as a causal biological mechanism.
