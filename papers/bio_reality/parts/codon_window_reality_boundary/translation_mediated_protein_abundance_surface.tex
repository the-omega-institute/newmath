\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies a finite cross-layer table whose rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_{stress}$.  The translation coordinate used in the powered finite run is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as a codon-count-weighted mean from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance ppm joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control block is not optional: it contains an intercept, $\log(CDS\ length)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline, giving control rank $23$ and control-column count $24$ in each organism after residualization.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}

The total residual association is $c=R^2(P\sim X_Q\mid Z)$, the direct part after conditioning on translation is $c'=R^2(P\sim X_Q\mid Z,T)$, and the mediated share is $M^{QTP}=(c-c')/c$ when $c>0$. \paragraph{Translation contact status.}
A separate bounded modeled-tAI survival calculation supplies the translation-side contact rather than direct protein promotion.  In the three-organism modeled-tAI matrix, all standard amino-acid tRNA families are represented in human and yeast, and $20$ standard families are also represented in \textit{E. coli} K-12 after $87$ of $89$ FASTA headers match.  The modeled codon-weight calculation covers all $61$ sense codons with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ for human, yeast, and \textit{E. coli}.  The finite matrix has $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This is nevertheless a modeled codon-adaptation contact, not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement, and the three-organism count is explicitly below the powered survival standard requiring at least $40$ matched species.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite interpretation is therefore a controlled mediation matrix $Q_i\to T\to P$, not a direct residual-to-protein law.  The record supports the statement that, in this joined panel, the joint $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive residual association with matched protein abundance and that part of that association is attenuated by conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI.  It does not identify a causal translation mechanism.  It does not show that any single coordinate controls protein abundance.  It does not convert the CUN/UUR sign, any organism-specific sign, or any residual coordinate into a population law.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control needed to separate transcription-level abundance from translation-level readout; it is not itself a translation measurement.

\paragraph{Local claim discipline.}
The allowed chapter-level claim is narrow: a finite, controlled, cross-layer statistical mediation surface exists for the twelve-organism modeled-tAI and matched-protein-abundance panel, and every reported organism has a positive $c-c'$ with a mediated fraction in the unit interval.  No translation realization follows from this alone unless an observed translation contact supplies it.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation response, mechanism, evolutionary selection law, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this mediation table.  Those layers require their own matched contacts and their own controlled finite statistics.
