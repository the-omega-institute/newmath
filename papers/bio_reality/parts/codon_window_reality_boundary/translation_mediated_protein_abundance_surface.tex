\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance surface}
\label{subsec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-surface}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite mediation surface with rows of the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation slot $t_j$ is the per-protein modeled tAI readout
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= {\sum_c n_c w_c \over \sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the real CDS codon count and $w_c$ is computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein slot $p_k$ is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance ppm joined to the same real CDS codon-count rows.  The row object is therefore not a direct $Q \to P$ claim: a residual coordinate is allowed to touch protein abundance only through an explicitly named translation readout, and the resulting quantity is a statistical decomposition rather than a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
Each organism-specific matrix is residualized against the same control family: intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, where $M$ is the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The main powered run used twelve organism rows: $4042$ joined proteins in \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ in \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ in \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ in \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ in \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In every organism the control rank was $23$, the controlled residual table retained rank $9$ for the $Q$ block, rank $1$ for the $T$ block, and rank $10$ for the joint $(Q,T)$ block.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $1633$ in \emph{Sulfolobus} to $19372$ in mouse, so the reported mediation fractions are not artifacts of a rank-deficient design.
