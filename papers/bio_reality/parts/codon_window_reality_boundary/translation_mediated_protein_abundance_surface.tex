\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance surface}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The object studied here is a finite mediation surface whose rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinate is one of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The translation coordinate $t_j$ is a bounded readout of codon adaptation or translation efficiency; in the computed powered table it is the per-protein mean modeled tAI obtained from codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate $p_k$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The controls are not promoted into residual coordinates: the regression surface residualizes amino-acid composition, organism-specific rows, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline, and the finite design reports rank sufficiency rather than treating any direct $q_i \to p_k$ association as enough.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, let $X$ denote the nine-column residual-coordinate matrix, $T$ the modeled per-protein tAI readout, $Y$ the protein-abundance readout, and $Z$ the control matrix.  The reported scalar is the fraction of the $X \to Y$ residual association removed after conditioning on $T$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Y_Z\sim X_Z)-R^2(Y_Z\sim X_Z\mid T_Z)}{R^2(Y_Z\sim X_Z)},\\
0 &\leq M^{QTP}\leq 1
\end{aligned}
$$
whenever the denominator is positive and the rank diagnostics are nondegenerate.  This statistic is a finite variance decomposition, not an intervention.  It licenses the sentence that part of the residual-to-abundance association is routed through the modeled translation readout; it does not license a claim about causation, ribosome dynamics, folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Computed organism panel.}
The powered table contains twelve organism-level joins.  The joined protein counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each organism has rank $9$ for $X$, rank $1$ for $T$, rank $10$ for $(X,T)$, control rank $23$, and the reported nondegenerate threshold is $0.999999$.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse.  The tRNA copy totals used in the modeled tAI calculation are $86$, $581$, $8874$, $290$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $407$, $49$, $417$, $275$, and $47$ in the same organism order; geometric-mean filling of zero raw weights occurs only for \emph{B. subtilis} ($3$), \emph{E. coli} ($1$), \emph{M. smegmatis} ($1$), and \emph{S. solfataricus} ($1$).

\paragraph{Mediated fractions.}
All twelve rows have $M^{QTP}$ in the unit interval.  The strongest mediated fraction occurs in yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Large mediated fractions also occur in \emph{D. melanogaster} ($0.6165167748739756$), \emph{E. coli} ($0.612922252793667$), and \emph{B. subtilis} ($0.6084594553474505$).  The metazoan rows are not uniform: worm has $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$, zebrafish $0.3157053953614641$, human $0.27206289160119157$, chicken $0.1976903964724546$, rat $0.17489681798543982$, and mouse $0.15488799907323988$.  The smallest positive fractions are in \emph{M. smegmatis} ($0.08397602919861291$) and \emph{S. solfataricus} ($0.01850668564589326$).  These rows support a cross-layer statistical relation: a direct residual-to-protein association is not admitted as protein realization unless the same residual coordinates first pass through the translation-readout step.

\paragraph{Translation contact boundary.}
A smaller modeled-tAI contact computed from three organisms confirms that the codon-adaptation readout is finite and nonzero but underpowered as a broad survival claim.  In that contact, the sense-codon space has size $61$, the organism count is $3$, and the bounded-coupling table has $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$.  The same record states that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the calculation is modeled tAI coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  Thus the present chapter treats modeled tAI as a bounded translation-layer coordinate, while observed ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, and matched proteomics remain distinct measurement routes.

\paragraph{Cross-checks against overreading.}
The yeast structure-mediated abundance audit gives a useful comparison because it joins $4759$ genes and reports a five-way join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ with CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  Its residual protein-abundance association is $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability leaves $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$ and adding the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy leaves $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is only $0.003284564452662019$ and the structure-incremental permutation $p$-value is $0.1258741258741259$.  This agrees with the local rule of the chapter: abundance mediation is not folding evidence.  Other finite rows enforce the same boundary.  The mass-balance audit with $120$ genes and $718$ intervals has $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, and $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ with $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$ and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit over $3290$ genes gives $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$, so no turnover claim is imported into this chapter.

\paragraph{Relation to broader finite evidence.}
The observable execution decomposition row supplies one positive rate-proxy result in yeast, with $3289$ genes and the finite verdict that at least one observed rate slot passed both effective null classes.  The evolutionary selection-imprint audit is deliberately weaker: it uses $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and a negative gate for positive selection imprint.  The locked genetic-code-contact checks provide a separate boundary calibration: $40$ successes out of $40$ in each strong binomial probe, one-sided upper-tail $p=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, with $20$ negative controls and $20$ critical ablations showing zero unexpected successes.  These facts support finite contact discipline and module-classification coverage, not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite row evidence is sufficient for a chapter-level cross-layer statement: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual matrix has a computed, rank-sufficient, translation-mediated association with matched protein abundance across twelve organism joins, and every reported $M^{QTP}$ fraction lies in $[0,1]$.  The strongest row is yeast and the weakest positive row is \emph{S. solfataricus}; the panel is heterogeneous rather than a single-sign population law.  The admissible readback is therefore a controlled mediation matrix $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$.  No translation realization beyond the modeled or measured translation contact, no folding claim, no physical-admissibility claim, no molecular-function claim, no causal mechanism, and no universal rule follows without a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
