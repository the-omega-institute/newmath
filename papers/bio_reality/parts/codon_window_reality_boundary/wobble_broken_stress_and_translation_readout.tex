\subsection{Wobble-broken $f_3$ stress and translation readout}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates a single cross-layer question: whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress remains visible after amino-acid quotienting when it is tested against translation-layer readouts. The carrier is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to matched rows carrying codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation readout data. The relevant contrast is not raw codon identity. It is a paired synonymous imbalance after amino-acid projection, measured only where a context weight can distinguish a codon $c$ from its third-position partner $c^{f_3}$.

The finite saturation row fixes the combinatorial boundary. The median-$R$ union with the two ACA-family entries is
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ surface removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, giving the $18$-codon set
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.
The reported $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the finite boundary condition is the zero-boundary case rather than a residual list requiring additional codon exceptions.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The only promoted readback is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The intended test is a partial-survival comparison against amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls. In symbols, the entry is treated as positive only when the controlled readout improves beyond the baseline:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{ organism},\mathrm{GC3},\hbox{ length},M\hbox{-density},\hbox{ mRNA control}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix is therefore a null boundary. It does not count as survival, because it contains no row-wise ordering with which the $f_3$ coordinate can interact.

\paragraph{Mechanistic break condition.}
The conjecture is explicitly conditional on wobble or tRNA asymmetry. If every relevant codon pair has $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$, then third-position pairing gives no translation-layer asymmetry for this coordinate to read. The coordinate becomes a biological candidate only in organisms or contexts where
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c &\neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This condition is motivated by the powered tRNA-poverty row: in $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped, $R$ sense codons were reported as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That row used $1000$ permutations and $\alpha=0.05$, and its conclusion supports a tRNA-supply route for $R$ avoidance without by itself proving a translation-efficiency effect for the present coordinate.

\paragraph{Finite powered context.}
Several supporting rows delimit the background geometry. The axis-strength spectrum was computed for $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Its descriptive result was led by $d_{resid4}$ on average, followed by $d_{tRNA}$, with $d_{resid4}$ dominant in all $11$ computed organisms; the largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$. The two-axis closure row, using the same $11/16$ organism count, $5$ folds, $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ design columns, reported a third-axis-present conclusion because $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The three-axis sufficiency row, again at $11/16$ organisms and $41$ synonymous design columns, classified the system as having a fourth mechanism present.

The identity and mechanism rows sharpen but do not finish the biological interpretation. The $d_{\perp}$ identity row aligned the candidate with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The mechanism row classified the residual as selection-not-GC and not wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as requiring contact outside the residual geometry. The universal residual-axis row identified the non-tRNA residual optimum most strongly with $f3\_translation\_selection$, and the core tRNA adaptation row passed under the same $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns. These rows justify testing the translation interface, but they do not replace the survival test.

\paragraph{Local falsifier.}
The conjecture is broken if the powered panel keeps all controlled entries non-positive or rank-degenerate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall j,\quad S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &\leq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ ranges over the matched translation readouts available for the same CDS identifiers and organisms. A positive tAI or stAI association can nominate a bounded translation-weight row, but observed ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency requires the corresponding ribo-seq and matched mRNA contact. The strongest usable row is a sequence-matched TE estimate, $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$, because it separates translation-layer signal from transcript abundance.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
This chapter asserts only a testable relation between the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and translation readout rows under explicit controls. It does not infer protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, biochemical function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law from codon geometry, tRNA weights, or modeled adaptation weights alone. Each such layer would require its own matched contact and its own controlled survival entry. Until the translation row is non-vacuous and positive, the coordinate remains a finite residual candidate with a clear null condition, not a biological conclusion.
