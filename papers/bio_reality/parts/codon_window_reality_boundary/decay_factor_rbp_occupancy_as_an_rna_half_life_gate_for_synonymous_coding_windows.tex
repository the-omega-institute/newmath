\subsection{Decay-factor RBP occupancy as an RNA half-life gate for synonymous coding windows}\label{sec:bioreality-decay-factor-rbp-rna-half-life-translation-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim surface.} The proposed record concerns a finite matched panel of synonymously editable coding-RNA windows. Each row must carry a coordinate label for the synonymous window, an RNA half-life measurement, a same-row translation readout, measured local occupancy for a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, a target-RBP perturbation state, wild-type rescue, a binding-defective rescue or non-target control, transcription-rate and mRNA-abundance controls, isoform state, local RNA-structure covariates, motif-density covariates, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, batch, and an explicit declared failure condition. The intended object is not an organism-wide codon-usage law. It is a row-local test of whether a coordinate-associated RNA-stability difference can be promoted to a bounded cross-layer relation only after the measured RBP bridge and the translation boundary are both present in the same finite rows.

\paragraph{Finite readout required for a positive decision.} Let $i$ range over the assayed windows. A row sufficient for the proposed test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_i = (&c_i, T_i, H_i, O_i, A_i, S_i, D_i, G_i, P_i, I_i, B_i,\\
       &u_i, w_i, b_i, n_i, e_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $c_i$ is the high-coordinate or low-coordinate synonymous label after amino-acid composition control, $T_i$ is the matched translation readout, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $O_i$ is measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, $A_i$ is transcript abundance or mRNA abundance, $S_i$ is local structure, $D_i$ is motif density, $G_i$ records GC and dinucleotide composition, $P_i$ records codon-pair context, $I_i$ records splice or isoform state, $B_i$ records batch, $u_i$ records target-RBP presence or loss, $w_i$ records wild-type rescue, $b_i$ records binding-defective rescue, $n_i$ records non-target control, and $e_i$ is the row-level stop condition. A positive result would require the coordinate-associated half-life contrast to track $O_i$, to collapse under target-RBP loss, to return under wild-type rescue, and to fail to return under binding-defective or non-target controls, while remaining separated from transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local structure, motif density, isoform state, composition, codon-pair context, and batch.

\paragraph{Existing finite contacts.} The available evidence supports related RNA-stability and cross-layer boundary surfaces but does not instantiate the requested RBP-occupancy panel. A transcript-surveillance position-rule run retained $38576$ rows with $1000$ permutations and runtime $55.602$ seconds. A site-load m6A versus mRNA half-life run kept $5837$ genes and reported static Spearman $-0.388929$ for m6A load against DMSO log half-life, causal Spearman $0.245804$ for m6A load against $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, length-partial Spearman $0.227683$, and two-sided partial permutation $p=0.000999001$ under the stated coverage rule. A cross-organism m6A half-life run used $15845$ genes, $135300$ sites, $1000$ permutations, and found $3$ sign-concordant cases. An ARE deletion stability run used $226$ BEAS2B rows, $2242$ Jurkat rows, $1000$ actual permutations, and $2000$ grading permutations, with sign concordance reported under a pooled label-permutation procedure. An endogenous 3UTR half-life transfer run used $14933$ genes, $2000$ permutations, baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and $p=0.00049975$. A miRNA target-repression run used $55170$ rows and reported median aggregate per-miRNA partial $\rho=-0.224966$ with $7$ replicated gradients. These numbers show that finite RNA-stability contacts, motif-linked stability contacts, and translation-boundary-adjacent controls can be represented as measured row sets; they do not provide the missing decay-factor RBP occupancy and rescue structure for this specific claim.

\paragraph{Decision rule.} The proposed statistic should compare high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels only after conditioning on the mandatory covariates and controls. In schematic form, the permitted contrast is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H
  &= \operatorname{contrast}\bigl(H_i \mid c_i, O_i, T_i, A_i, S_i, D_i, G_i, P_i, I_i, B_i, u_i, w_i, b_i, n_i\bigr),\\
\Delta_O
  &= \operatorname{contrast}\bigl(O_i \mid c_i, T_i, A_i, S_i, D_i, G_i, P_i, I_i, B_i\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$
The bridge is licensed only if $\Delta_H$ follows the measured occupancy contrast $\Delta_O$, disappears under target-RBP loss, reappears under wild-type rescue, and is not reproduced by the binding-defective rescue, non-target control, transcription-rate shift, abundance shift, local-structure covariate, motif-density covariate, isoform mixture, codon-pair context, composition, or batch. If high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable for RNA half-life after these controls, the coordinate label remains a bounded descriptor. If RNA half-life changes without a corresponding measured occupancy change, the record remains an RNA-stability association rather than an RBP-mediated relation.

\paragraph{Boundary.} The present data do not authorize a positive chapter verdict for the decay-factor RBP mechanism bridge. The available facts include finite row counts, half-life statistics, permutation counts, effect signs, and partial associations for related RNA-stability settings, but they do not include same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target control behavior, or coordinate-occupancy decoupling for the synonymously editable coding windows. Consequently the claim remains a well-specified finite test awaiting its decisive rows. No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone. No protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or universal biological law follows from the coordinate label, the half-life readout, the mRNA control, the translation boundary, or the related verified RNA-stability contacts unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that content.
