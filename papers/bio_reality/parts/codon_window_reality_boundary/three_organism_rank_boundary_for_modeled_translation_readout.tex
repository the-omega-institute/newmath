\subsection{Three-organism rank boundary for modeled translation readout}
\label{sec:q6-three-organism-rank-degenerate-translation-survival-refusal-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a three-organism $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-readout surface built from residual synonymous coordinates and modeled tAI-style readouts.  The intended carrier is not a survival theorem.  It is a finite rank diagnostic: after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, organism, and baseline controls have been accounted for, the three-row surface can still record modeled coupling, but it cannot certify translation survival.  In this setting an all-perfect or saturated $S^{QT}$ fit is interpreted as a rank boundary, not as biological support.  The readback is therefore restricted to the statement that the present panel has insufficient residual rank for survival adjudication.

\paragraph{Coordinate basis and controls.}
The computed residual basis has nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The powered companion computation used six organism rows for the coordinate-support readout, with joined protein counts $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Each row used $24$ control columns with residual control rank $23$.  These numbers are enough to document the residualized coordinate contact, but they do not turn the three-organism survival matrix into a rank-powered survival claim.

\paragraph{Observed modeled coupling.}
The finite readout does contain nonzero modeled translation coupling.  In the powered companion readout, the $R^2_{QP}$ values are $0.04613529805760884$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.10767526667768725$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.03980793595345331$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $0.04454565117192484$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $0.032090091625816136$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $0.29837624987287115$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  The corresponding modeled-$T$-complement fractions $m$ are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&m_{Danio}=0.12537127895073397,\quad m_{Ecoli}=0.3370039961421468,\\
&m_{Gallus}=0.05996581936972245,\quad m_{Human}=0.07910506443205445,\\
&m_{Myco}=0.10273279812535108,\quad m_{Yeast}=0.788267804272933.
\end{aligned}
$$
The complementary fractions $d$ are $0.874628721049266$, $0.6629960038578532$, $0.9400341806302775$, $0.9208949355679455$, $0.8972672018746489$, and $0.21173219572706703$ in the same organism order.  These finite scalars support the weaker statement that modeled translation-readout geometry can be decomposed and read back in the nine-coordinate basis.  They do not support the stronger statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates survive as translation-realization predictors in a rank-degenerate three-organism panel.

\paragraph{Coordinate support of the modeled complement.}
The $a_{\perp}$ readout also remains coordinate-local.  \emph{Danio rerio} is dominated by $f3\_stress$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, and $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$.  \emph{E. coli} is dominated by $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, and $f3\_stress$.  \emph{Gallus gallus} is dominated by $Arg\_AGR$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and $K\_AAA$.  \emph{Homo sapiens} is dominated by $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $K\_AAA$, and $f3\_stress$.  \emph{M. smegmatis} is dominated by $K\_AAA$, $f3\_stress$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, and $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$.  \emph{S. cerevisiae} is dominated by $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The absolute support fractions sum to $1.0$ in all listed rows, up to the recorded floating-point value $1.0000000000000002$ for \emph{E. coli}.  This is a finite coordinate-support statement, not a mechanistic translation statement.

\paragraph{Mismatch ledger.}
The complement-projection $m$ values do not generally match the mediation reference values computed on the same residualized $Q_e/T_e/P_e/Z$ rows.  The absolute differences are $0.19033411641073011$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.27591825665152014$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.13772457710273214$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $0.19295782716913712$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $0.018756768926738165$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $0.16088729984876438$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  Only the \emph{M. smegmatis} comparison is within the stated $0.02$ tolerance.  This pattern reinforces the refusal: the modeled complement is a useful finite readout of residual coordinates, but it is not a stable survival certificate across the organism panel.

\paragraph{Rank refusal.}
For the three-organism $S^{QT}$ surface, saturated or all-$1.0$ entries are classified as rank-degenerate.  The correct status is therefore $needs\_data$: the result asks for a rank-powered multi-organism translation-readout surface before any survival interpretation is made.  A nonzero modeled coupling $A^{QT}$ may be reported as a contact with modeled tAI or stAI annotations, but it cannot be promoted to $S^{QT}$ survival without enough independent organism rows and without the specified baseline-controlled partial-$R^2$ survival test.

\paragraph{Local biological boundary.}
The only biological contacts used here are per-organism codon-usage summaries and organism-scoped modeled codon-adaptation weights.  Those contacts can test sequence-composition readback, translation-layer annotation, and cross-layer association under explicit controls.  They do not observe ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  No such downstream conclusion follows from the modeled-tAI coupling or from the residual coordinate geometry unless an additional contact at that layer is attached and separately tested.  The chapter therefore records a finite refusal rather than a translation-survival result: the present three-organism surface marks the boundary at which coupling is visible but survival is inadmissible.
