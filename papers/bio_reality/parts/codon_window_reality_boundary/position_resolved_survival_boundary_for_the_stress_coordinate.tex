\subsection{Position-Resolved Survival Boundary for the $f_3$-Stress Coordinate}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The object considered here is not a protein-level or phenotype-level assertion.  It is a finite table of coding-sequence windows.  Each admissible row must contain an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid identity, local synonymous-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA control for the same biological condition, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The proposed coordinate is therefore a rowwise relation between a synonymous residual direction and a same-window translation-layer readback, not a free-standing sequence statistic.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
For a window table $T$ and control block $Z$, the only admissible survival entry is the controlled quantity $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$.  In this chapter the control block includes amino-acid composition or amino-acid-bin effects, codon position or position-bin effects, GC3, coding length, methionine density, mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability filters, and simpler codon-usage baselines.  A positive call requires held-out improvement after these terms are attached.  In symbolic form the survival gate is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
with the further requirement that the gain persists under rank checks and simpler-baseline ablation.  If the improvement disappears after these controls, the row table remains a useful boundary specification but it does not support a cross-layer survival claim.

\paragraph{Asymmetry condition.}
The conjecture is intentionally restricted to contexts where wobble weights do not preserve the $f_3$ symmetry.  The local test must observe, rather than assume, an asymmetry of the form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This condition is not itself a ribosome-occupancy measurement.  It only marks the finite coordinate direction that is allowed to enter the controlled rowwise test.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight may contribute a bounded codon-adaptation surface, but it cannot substitute for the same-window footprint or translation-efficiency readout.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite evidence.}
The surrounding BioReality ledger already contains several cross-layer finite-row examples, but none of them is the requested $f_3$ same-window occupancy table.  The translation-conditioned structural-order boundary joined 3412 \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 entries and 5119 \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} entries, used 5 folds, and reported a weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ of 0.004764742175882462 with a sign-flip probability 0.0029296875.  Its aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ ranged from 0.002688284523492457 to 0.007880037229394898 over two organisms, while the permuted control ranged from -0.003463794378680385 to -0.000779938262202795.  That is evidence that bounded cross-layer survival tests can be made finite and controlled, not evidence that this $f_3$ position-resolved occupancy claim has passed.

Other verified finite panels give scale and calibration points for the same style of rowwise testing.  A random-promoter grammar run used 75111 designed promoters, retained 8027 high-quality rows, and analyzed a 2500-row subsample with 200 permutations.  A Doench sgRNA position-nucleotide run used $n=5310$ rows and gave a GC main correlation of -0.0582917, while the Luo cross-dataset run used $n=10543$ rows.  A pri-miRNA processing run reported a GC main correlation of -0.0479934 and anchored motif windows to fixed Drosha and mature-arm landmarks rather than to de-novo structure inference.  Stop-context and stop-readthrough panels contributed 4982 nonzero G418 readthrough rows and 55886 cross-dataset rows, respectively.  A NET-seq elongation-factor-deletion panel used 11668 windows, 1000 permutations, permutation $p=0.001$, held-out $R^2=0.3438$ for the control baseline, and held-out $R^2=0.3652$ after sequence terms, for an incremental positive-direction value of 0.0214.  These numbers justify treating finite controlled row tables as the appropriate evidential form, while also emphasizing that the present claim still lacks its specific joined ribosome-occupancy rows.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The local claim is only that an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate may survive as a bounded cross-layer coordinate when the same CDS windows show controlled position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency improvement.  No translation-layer realization, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from codon-window geometry, tAI or stAI weights, or $B^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ notation alone.  Each of those stronger statements would require its own matched contact and its own finite survival statistic.

\paragraph{Current verdict.}
The record is not ready as a positive survival chapter because the decisive rows are still missing: same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, wobble-weight asymmetry, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency readouts have not yet been joined into the required table.  The correct present classification is therefore a constrained open conjecture.  It specifies the carrier, the asymmetry condition, the controls, the rank-powered break condition, and the forbidden promotions, but it does not yet report a passed $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ entry.
