\subsection{Reality contacts and current finite evidence}\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival-evidence}

\paragraph{Required readout.} The central missing contact is the same-CDS, same-window ribosome-occupancy table.  Such a contact can be represented at codon-index or local-window resolution when ribosome-profiling footprints are joined to ordered coding-sequence coordinates.  Its known biases are large enough to matter for this claim: pausing, nuclease bias, fragment-length selection, frame assignment, mappability, edge effects near start and stop codons, normalization, condition specificity, and annotation mismatch can each change the local occupancy estimate.  Therefore the row cannot be treated as a translation-survival row unless the footprint readout, the mRNA control, the codon counts, and the wobble-weight context are joined before the statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Adjacent powered records.} Several neighboring cross-layer records show that the broader finite-row program has produced controlled numerical contacts, but they do not by themselves supply the missing position-resolved $f_3$ statistic.  The random-promoter expression boundary used $75111$ designed sequences, $8027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subset of $2500$ high-quality rows, $200$ permutations, seed $20260239$, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  The finite-state dwell transducer record is marked passed under run $26912bc3e41e$.  The translation-conditioned structural-order boundary used $5$ folds with a minimum join requirement of $300$ rows, joining $3412$ rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In that separate record, the full-control $\Delta R^2$ values were positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, median and mean $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, and weighted value $0.004764742175882462$; the corresponding sign-flip probability for being greater than zero was $0.0029296875$.  Its permuted full-control values were negative in both organisms, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, mean and median $-0.00212186632044159$, and maximum $-0.000779938262202795$.  A separate translation-initiation record reports $80000$ rows and runtime $172.864$ seconds, with no cross-sample value attached.  These numbers establish that related finite controls and readouts exist elsewhere in the data ledger, but none is the required position-resolved ribosome-occupancy survival entry for $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Why this remains open.} The present packet is therefore a precise conjectural boundary rather than a completed empirical call.  The available verified records include row counts, fold counts, permutation counts, runtimes, signed $\Delta R^2$ summaries, and a sign-flip probability for related cross-layer tasks.  They do not include the actual finite list of same-window $f_3$ residuals, codon counts, wobble-weight values, mRNA controls, ribosome-footprint readouts, rank outcomes, or held-out survival values for this claim.  Without those rows, no statement about position-resolved translation survival can be promoted from a well-formed test to a passed test.
