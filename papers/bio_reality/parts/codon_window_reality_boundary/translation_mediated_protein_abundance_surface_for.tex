\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance surface for $B^{*}_{Q6}$}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite mediation surface whose rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate used in the powered row table is a per-protein modeled tAI score, computed as the codon-count weighted mean of organism-specific tAI weights from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein-abundance coordinate is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The finite panel contains twelve organism-level joins: 4042 rows for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, 11661 for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, 14410 for \emph{Danio rerio}, 13295 for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, 3739 for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, 12418 for \emph{Gallus gallus}, 19053 for \emph{Homo sapiens}, 19395 for \emph{Mus musculus}, 4129 for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, 13322 for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, 5815 for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and 1656 for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In each organism the residualized design has rank $9$ for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate block, rank $1$ for the modeled translation coordinate, rank $10$ for the joint coordinate-plus-translation block, and rank $23$ for the control block after removing one degenerate direction from the nominal 24 controls.

\paragraph{Controls and readback.}
The control set contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an M-density baseline defined from the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  Thus amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and M-density are not promoted into the coordinate being tested; they are residualized before the translation-mediated protein-abundance table is read.  The statistical decomposition is the comparison between the total controlled association $c=R^2(Q,P)$ and the remaining direct association $c'=R^2(Q,P\mid T)$ after conditioning on the modeled translation readout $T$.  The mediated component is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= c-c',\\
F^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},
\end{aligned}
$$
reported only when the fraction lies in $[0,1]$ and the residual ranks are sufficient.  The run satisfies this rank condition in all twelve organisms.

\paragraph{Observed finite values.}
The mediated fraction is positive in every organism in the powered table, but it is highly uneven across clades and should be read as a finite statistical decomposition rather than as a law.  The largest value occurs in yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $M^{QTP}=0.28320534058057895$, and $F^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ on 5815 joined proteins.  The bacterial and animal rows show smaller but nonzero mediated components: for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, and $F^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ on 3739 rows; for \emph{C. elegans}, $c=0.10217906758013379$, $c'=0.05239468380596188$, and $F^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$ on 11661 rows; for human, $c=0.04454565118019448$, $c'=0.03242643251185274$, and $F^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ on 19053 rows.  The smallest mediated fractions remain positive: \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} has $F^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$ with $M^{QTP}=0.0011839678516385038$ on 1656 rows, and \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} has $F^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$ with $M^{QTP}=0.0026947984781311544$ on 4129 rows.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The same coordinate family already has a bounded modeled-tAI contact in a smaller three-organism translation-survival table.  That table computed the same nine residual directions, used a codon-count space of $61$, and found $81$ entries above the absolute coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled entry $3801961.031607896$.  The three organisms in that earlier table were \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast; their tRNA payloads covered all 20 standard amino-acid families, with matched FASTA header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively.  This contact is explicitly bounded: three organisms are not enough for powered survival, and modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling.  It can supply a translation-weight readout, not observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Comparison with adjacent rows.}
The yeast structure-mediation row gives a useful local calibration of how much of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance residual can be absorbed by other measured or sequence-derived quantities.  In that five-way yeast join, $n=4759$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the structure-incremental fraction after measured TE and mRNA-stability conditioning is $0.0005507778820193332$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.1258741258741259$.  This says that a 5-prime CDS MFE proxy is not the main explanation for the protein-abundance residual, even though the $S_Q$ structure association itself has $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ and permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$.

\paragraph{Negative higher-layer checks.}
Several attached audits prevent the translation-mediated abundance row from being promoted too far.  A chaperone-client absorption check in yeast used $n=3290$ analysis genes and found held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.025094043993987934$ for the 64-dimensional chaperone-client membership matrix, with $p=0.8059701492537313$ and verdict composition artifact.  A turnover residual audit on the same scale reported $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$ across 3290 genes.  A cross-ortholog selection-imprint run on 80 orthogroups and 240 rows reported $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null95 $0.05427157066522958$, and no positive selection-imprint gate.  These values leave the protein-abundance mediation surface as a controlled abundance decomposition, not as evidence for chaperone absorption, turnover prediction, or evolutionary mechanism.

\paragraph{Allowed conclusion.}
The finite result supports a narrow statement: after the specified sequence-composition controls and modeled tAI conditioning, the joint nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a nonzero statistical mediation component toward matched PAXdb protein abundance in all twelve powered organism rows, with mediated fractions ranging from $0.01850668564589326$ to $0.9491551041216973$.  This is enough to enter the standalone BioReality paper as a cross-layer finite-row record for modeled translation-mediated protein-abundance association.  It is not enough to say that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ realizes translation in the ribosome, causes protein abundance, changes folding, determines physical admissibility, explains molecular function, gives a phenotype, or states a universal biological rule.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: observed ribosome profiling for translation dynamics, matched mRNA abundance for transcriptional separation, perturbational proteomics for causal abundance response, structure or stability data for folding and physical state, and explicit multi-organism or intervention controls before any mechanism or universality language is licensed.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
Within the finite table actually computed, the break condition for the mediated protein-abundance surface is not met: all reported $M^{QTP}$ entries are positive and all reported mediated fractions are in the unit interval.  The exact biological interpretation remains bounded because the translation coordinate is modeled tAI rather than observed RPF or TE, and because the powered QTP table does not itself include matched mRNA abundance as a separate residualized denominator.  The chapter therefore records a positive modeled-translation mediation surface with a hard boundary against causal, mechanistic, folding, function, phenotype, and universal-law readings.
