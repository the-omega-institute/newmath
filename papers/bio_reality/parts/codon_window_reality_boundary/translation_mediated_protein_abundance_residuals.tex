\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance residuals}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the finite surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ is one of nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout. The operational readout used here is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as the codon-count weighted mean of GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm joined to real CDS codon counts. The nine residual coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. The controls are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $q$ vectors. Direct $q_i\to p_k$ interpretation is therefore not admitted as a protein-realization claim; the tested object is whether residual protein-abundance association is decomposed through a translation readout after these controls.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The finite organism panel contains twelve joined CDS--protein--tRNA surfaces: \textit{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. \textit{subtilis} str. 168 with $4042$ joined proteins, \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans} with $11661$, \textit{Danio rerio} with $14410$, \textit{Drosophila melanogaster} with $13295$, \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 with $3739$, \textit{Gallus gallus} with $12418$, \textit{Homo sapiens} with $19053$, \textit{Mus musculus} with $19395$, \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 with $4129$, \textit{Rattus norvegicus} with $13322$, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $5815$, and \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} with $1656$. In every row the residualized design has $9$ $q$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{XT})=10$, with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ under a $24$-column control table. Thus the mediation scalar is not a notational artifact of a collapsed design; the $q$ surface and the translation readout both remain present after residualization.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}

The result is therefore a positive finite mediation decomposition across the declared surface, not a statement that modeled tAI is an observed ribosome footprint or that the residual coordinate has become a protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation contact and modeled-readout boundary.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival run on three organisms computed all $61$ sense-codon weights for \textit{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each of these three cases. The local GtRNAdb payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each of those organisms, with matched FASTA header counts $87$ of $89$ for \textit{E. coli} K12, $432$ of $432$ for human, and $275$ of $275$ for yeast. The finite modeled-tAI coupling table had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold and maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, but that run explicitly remains underpowered for a powered survival law because it used $3$ organisms rather than at least $40$ matched species. This chapter therefore treats the twelve-organism protein-abundance mediation as a controlled statistical decomposition through modeled tAI, while the three-organism survival table is only a bounded translation-readout contact.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite checks.}
The yeast structure-mediation row supplies a useful local comparison. In the five-way yeast join, $4759$ rows were retained, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572657$. The controlled $Q\to P$ value was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, while conditioning on residualized measured TE plus mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, and adding the CDS MFE proxy changed this only to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121$. The structure-only fraction was $0.0032845645$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507779$, the $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.0037742485$, and the structure-increment permutation $p$-value was $0.1258741259$. These numbers support a narrow variance-partition statement, not a folded-structure claim. Additional checks also keep the scope bounded: the cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species, with $\Delta R^2=0.0325051502$ but leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477730$ and null $95\%$ maximum $0.0542715707$, so the strong selection-imprint gate was not supported. The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and gave $R^2_{L2}=0.0557408516$, $R^2_{L3}=0.0502788722$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0054619794$, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$, so turnover was not promoted. The mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, with $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628202$, $R^2_{L1}=0.9964106289$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443378$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0758443809$, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693$, again giving no stronger physical conclusion. A rate-slot decomposition did find an observable execution subblock in yeast with $3289$ genes, and the structural-order audit identified $f3_{stress}$ as the single fold-level sign-flip passing coordinate with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502$ and $p=0.03125$, but those records do not convert the present protein-abundance mediation into structure, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the following local statement: on the twelve-organism matched CDS--protein--tRNA panel, the joint nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface has positive controlled protein-abundance association whose direct component is reduced after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI in every organism row, with all reported $M^{QTP}$ fractions lying in the unit interval. The accepted reading is a controlled mediation matrix $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ at the statistical cross-layer relation level. It does not by itself establish observed ribosome occupancy, translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, perturbation rescue, organismal phenotype, adaptation, or a universal biological rule. Each of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched contact and a separate controlled statistic before being stated.
