\subsection{Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Residuals}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite collection of coding-sequence contrasts whose protein-abundance interpretation is allowed only through a translation readout.  The finite coordinate set used in the powered run has nine axes,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The observed rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ is one of these residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-side readout, and $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  The powered finite panel uses per-protein modeled tAI as $T$ and $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb as $P$, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The computation is therefore a controlled statistical decomposition of $Q \to T \to P$, not a direct assertion that a synonymous residual has become a protein-level mechanism.

\paragraph{Controlled regression form.}
For each organism, let $X$ be the $n\times 9$ matrix of B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, let $T$ be the per-protein modeled tAI column, let $Y$ be log protein abundance, and let $Z$ contain the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline.  After residualizing against $Z$, the finite statistic compares the total $Q$--protein association with the remaining direct association after conditioning on $T$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  c &= R^2(Y_Z\sim X_Z),\\
  c' &= R^2(Y_Z\sim X_Z\mid T_Z),\\
  M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mediated component is admitted only when the translation coordinate is present in the same finite row system and the ranks remain nondegenerate.  Across all twelve organisms in the powered run, the rank check reports $\operatorname{rank}(X_Z)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T_Z)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}([X,T]_Z)=10$, with $23$ control columns and the in-unit-interval condition for $M^{QTP}$ satisfied in every organism.  Thus the row system has enough finite rank to separate a joint nine-coordinate residual block from the single modeled-translation column.

\paragraph{Translation contact and its boundary.}
The translation readout in the powered protein-abundance panel is modeled per-protein tAI, computed as the codon-count-weighted mean of codon tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  This contact is finite and useful, but it is not ribosome profiling.  A separate three-organism modeled-tAI survival run computed the same nine residual coordinates over the $61$ sense-codon count space and found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$ across Escherichia coli K12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  That run explicitly remains underpowered for a species-level survival claim because $3$ organisms do not meet the stated $40$-species requirement.  The present chapter therefore reads the twelve-organism protein panel as a modeled-translation mediation surface, not as observed ribosome occupancy or measured translation-efficiency validation.

\paragraph{Matched-abundance context.}
A related yeast five-way join shows how a measured-TE and abundance panel behaves when mRNA and structure-side controls are attached.  In that join, $4759$ yeast genes were retained, the reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419$, and the overall join hit rate was $0.8818572657$.  After the same nine B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates and control residualization, the yeast structure/translation/mRNA decomposition had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.0032845645$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507779$, the right-tail permutation value for $S_Q$ structure was $0.0279720280$, and the right-tail permutation value for the structure-incremental statistic was $0.1258741259$.  These numbers do not replace the modeled-tAI mediation statistic above; they only show that same-row abundance, translation, mRNA, and auxiliary readouts can be kept finite without merging their biological meanings.

\paragraph{Negative controls on promotion.}
The cross-ortholog imprint audit gives a useful warning against reading every positive residual as selection or mechanism.  In $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species, the evaluated imprint gate had $\Delta R^2=0.0325051502$, description-length change $-258.6010084302$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477730$, null $95$ percent maximum $\Delta R^2=0.0542715707$, and the positive selection-imprint flag was false.  The appropriate conclusion there was codon-bias expression correlation, not a strong evolutionary mechanism.  The same discipline applies here: a positive $M^{QTP}$ fraction says that conditioning on modeled translation readout reduces the controlled $Q$--protein association on this finite panel; it does not by itself identify causation, adaptation, or a molecular pathway.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement.  On a twelve-organism matched CDS/protein-abundance panel, the joint nine-dimensional B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a nonnegative modeled-translation-mediated component in every organism tested, with $M^{QTP}$ ranging from $0.0185066856$ in Sulfolobus solfataricus to $0.9491551041$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and M-density controls.  This is enough to place the record at cross-layer statistical mediation through modeled tAI.  It is not enough to claim observed translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, perturbational rescue, selection, or universality.  Any such stronger reading requires a separate layer-matched contact, such as same-row ribosome profiling with matched mRNA abundance for observed translation, direct structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility, perturbation data for mechanism, and multi-organism controls before population-level laws are asserted.
