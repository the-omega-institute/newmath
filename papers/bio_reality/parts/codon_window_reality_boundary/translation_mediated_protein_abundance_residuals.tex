\subsection{Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Residuals}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the protein-abundance question as a finite mediation surface on triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation coordinate used in the powered cross-organism computation is per-protein modeled tAI,
$$\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}$$
where $n_c$ is the CDS codon count and $w_c$ is the codon adaptation weight computed from all-tRNA GtRNAdb gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The fit is not a direct $q_i\to p_k$ promotion.  It is a residualized comparison between the total $Q\to P$ association and the remaining $Q\to P$ association after conditioning on the translation coordinate $T$.

\paragraph{Controlled residualization.}
Each organism-specific design residualizes against a fixed control block $Z$ with 24 columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the union support of the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The rank diagnostics show full residual coordinate rank in every powered row: $\mathrm{rank}(X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(T)=1$, and $\mathrm{rank}(XT)=10$ after the control block, with residual degrees of freedom ranging from 1633 in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to 19372 in mouse.  The joined finite surface contains 12 organism panels: 4042 proteins for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, 11661 for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, 14410 for zebrafish, 13295 for fly, 3739 for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, 12418 for chicken, 19053 for human, 19395 for mouse, 4129 for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, 13322 for rat, 5815 for budding yeast, and 1656 for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The tRNA copy totals span 47 in \emph{Sulfolobus} to 8874 in zebrafish; zero raw weight fallback counts are 3 in \emph{Bacillus}, 1 in \emph{E. coli}, 1 in \emph{Mycobacterium}, 1 in \emph{Sulfolobus}, and 0 in the remaining eight panels.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism the finite statistic is
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)},\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T).
\end{aligned}$$
All 12 organism rows have $0\leq M^{QTP}\leq 1$ and all have positive indirect increments.  The largest mediation fraction is yeast, with $R^2(Q\to P)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}}=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Other high fractions occur in fly, $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$ with $\Delta R^2=0.047530538755721025$; \emph{E. coli}, $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ with $\Delta R^2=0.06599656708487024$; and \emph{Bacillus}, $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$ with $\Delta R^2=0.037667440654802556$.  Intermediate panels include worm, $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$; zebrafish, $0.3157053953614641$; human, $0.27206289160119157$; chicken, $0.1976903964724546$; rat, $0.17489681798543982$; and mouse, $0.15488799907323988$.  The weakest positive rows are \emph{Mycobacterium}, $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$, and \emph{Sulfolobus}, $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$.  Thus the finite row verdict is not a universal sign law and not an organism-independent mechanism; it is a powered statistical attenuation pattern on a specified modeled-translation coordinate.

\paragraph{Translation-contact boundary.}
A separate three-organism modeled-tAI survival computation confirms that the codon adaptation contact is nonempty but still bounded.  In \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and budding yeast, the local GtRNAdb payload covers all 20 standard amino-acid families; the matched FASTA header counts are 87 of 89, 432 of 432, and 275 of 275, respectively.  The modeled tAI readout covers all 61 sense codons in each of the three organisms, with zero raw weight fallback count 0 in that three-organism contact.  The bounded-coupling matrix has 81 entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This shows finite modeled translation contact, not observed ribosome occupancy and not proteomics validation.  The same record states that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival if a stronger species-wide survival claim requires at least 40 matched species.

\paragraph{Local comparison with RNA-structure mediation.}
The yeast five-way panel gives a useful scale check because it joins 4759 genes across CDS codon counts, modeled or measured translation information, mRNA half-life, protein abundance, and a 5-prime coding-region MFE structure proxy.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$ and the five-way join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  In that panel, $R^2(Q\to P)=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid S)=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T,M)=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2(Q\to P\mid T,M,S)=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, while the structure-incremental fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$ with permutation right-tail value $0.1258741258741259$.  The structure proxy itself has $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail value $0.027972027972027972$.  These finite numbers support the decision to keep the present chapter at translation-mediated abundance attenuation rather than turning it into a folded-structure or causal pathway statement.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite checks.}
The surrounding powered checks constrain over-reading.  The evolutionary selection-imprint panel has 80 orthogroups, 240 rows, and 3 species; it reports $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null 95 percent maximum $0.05427157066522958$, so the positive selection-imprint gate is false.  A mass-balance dynamic panel on 120 genes and 718 intervals reports $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$; it does not strengthen the present abundance mediation into a dynamic closure claim.  A separate rate-slot bridge in budding yeast uses 3289 genes and reaches the limited verdict that at least one observed rate proxy slot passes both effective null classes.  A structural-order identity computation over the same nine axes finds $f3\_stress$ as the sole fold-level sign-flip survivor, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, loading magnitude $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip value $0.03125$; the other eight axes have sign-flip values from $0.0693359375$ to $0.779296875$ and do not define the present protein-abundance claim.

\paragraph{Finite conclusion.}
The chapter-level conclusion is that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a reproducible finite attenuation pattern when the protein-abundance association is conditioned on modeled per-protein tAI across 12 joined organism panels.  The strongest single finite row is yeast, where almost all of the residual association measured by this statistic is absorbed by the modeled translation coordinate, while several bacterial, animal, and archaeal panels retain smaller but positive mediated fractions.  The result licenses only a controlled mediation-matrix reading $Q\to T\to P$ for the declared finite rows.  It does not by itself establish observed translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbational mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require an additional layer-matched contact: observed ribosome profiling for ribosome dynamics, matched mRNA abundance for transcript-level separation, direct protein-state or structure measurements for folded or post-translational claims, and perturbation or rescue data for mechanism.
