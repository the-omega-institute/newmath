\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance residuals}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether a finite family of synonymous residual coordinates in $B^*_{Q6}$ can pass through a translation-layer readout before appearing in matched protein-abundance measurements. The admissible object is not a direct residual-to-protein arrow. It is a finite mediation table whose rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout. The row is interpreted only after the residual coordinate, the translation readout, and the protein measurement have been put on the same finite organism-specific surface and after the baseline covariates have been removed.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The powered cross-layer run used nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $\mathrm{K}_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$. For each protein row, the translation coordinate was the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the CDS codon count and $w_c$ is the tAI weight computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout was $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts. The residualization controls were an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors. The rank diagnostics remained finite and nondegenerate in every organism: the control rank was $23$, the residualized $Q$ rank was $9$, the residualized modeled-tAI rank was $1$, and the joined $Q+T$ rank was $10$ in all twelve organism panels.

\paragraph{Observed finite panels.}
The mediation run joined CDS, tRNA, and abundance rows for twelve organisms. The usable protein counts were $4042$ in \textit{Bacillus subtilis} str. 168, $11661$ in \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ in \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ in \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ in \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ in \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ in \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ in \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, $13322$ in \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. The tRNA copy totals used in the modeled readout ranged from $47$ in \textit{S. solfataricus} to $8874$ in \textit{D. rerio}; zero raw-weight fallback counts were $0$ in most panels, with $3$ in \textit{B. subtilis}, $1$ in \textit{E. coli}, $1$ in \textit{M. smegmatis}, and $1$ in \textit{S. solfataricus}. These rows are therefore adequate for a finite statistical decomposition, but they are modeled adaptation-weight rows, not direct ribosome-profiling rows.

\paragraph{Mediation scalar.}
Let $X$ denote the nine-dimensional residual matrix after control residualization, $T$ the residualized modeled-tAI readout, and $Y$ the residualized protein-abundance readout. The reported scalar is the fraction of the total residual-to-protein association that is removed by conditioning on $T$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Y\sim X)-R^2(Y\sim X\mid T)}{R^2(Y\sim X)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Across the twelve panels, $M^{QTP}$ lay in the unit interval every time. The largest mediated fraction was in yeast, with $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect component $0.28320534058057895$. Large mediated fractions also appeared in \textit{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \textit{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, and \textit{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$. The intermediate cases were \textit{C. elegans} at $0.4872268357227724$, \textit{D. rerio} at $0.3157053953614641$, and human at $0.27206289160119157$. The smallest mediated fractions were $0.1976903964724546$ in chicken, $0.17489681798543982$ in rat, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ in \textit{M. smegmatis}, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{S. solfataricus}. Thus the finite panels support a cross-layer statistical mediation readout in the narrow sense that conditioning on modeled tAI attenuates the residual-to-protein association on the joined rows.

\paragraph{Energy diagnostics.}
The same run reports that the residualized modeled-tAI coordinate explains protein-abundance energy even after the baseline controls. The strongest $T$ explained energies on $Y$ were $1088.8137823273846$ in \textit{C. elegans}, $941.8858618613751$ in yeast, $923.4025869376133$ in mouse, $850.7864327764183$ in human, and $823.3791598584983$ in \textit{D. melanogaster}. The corresponding $X$ direct improvement energy after conditioning on $T$ remained nonzero, for example $44.87838061554362$ in yeast, $997.7463057114725$ in human, and $2055.2519066931027$ in mouse. This is important: the table does not say that translation absorbs all residual information. It says that the modeled translation coordinate accounts for a finite share of the protein-abundance association while leaving a residual direct component under the same controls.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival computation used three organisms, $61$ sense codons, and the same family of nine residual coordinates. It found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$ and maximum diagonal absolute score $3801961.031607896$. The three-organism calculation included all standard tRNA amino-acid families in \textit{E. coli}, human, and yeast, with FASTA header counts $89$, $432$, and $275$, respectively. The calculation also records its own limitation: three organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species, and modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling. This makes the mediation table usable as a bounded modeled-readout result, not as an observed translation-dynamics result.

\paragraph{Matched yeast structure comparison.}
A five-way yeast join gives an additional local check on whether the residual-to-protein relation is merely a structure proxy. That run joined $4759$ genes across CDS, measured TE, mRNA half-life, protein abundance, and a CDS-local structure proxy. The baseline residualized protein association for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$. Conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE structure proxy alone reduced it only to $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, giving a structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$. Conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability reduced it to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and adding the structure proxy after those readouts gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$ and permutation $p=0.1258741258741259$. The structure proxy itself had $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$. This supports the narrower reading that the yeast abundance association is strongly attenuated by translation/mRNA readouts and only weakly by the CDS-local MFE proxy.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded companion checks.}
The broader finite ledger keeps this chapter from being read as a universal mechanism. A mass-balance RPF-protein closure run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals reported $R^2_{L0,M\ only}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1,M,E}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TE\,bin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{time\,shuffle}=0.9306930693069307$. A turnover audit on $3290$ genes found $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matched\,bin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$. These negative finite checks block promotion from protein abundance to turnover, proteostasis, or dynamic mass-balance claims. A separate observed-rate-slot run in yeast used $3289$ genes and did find at least one rate-proxy slot passing both effective null classes, but that result is a subblock contact, not a license to treat modeled tAI mediation as a general translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Selection and structural-order limits.}
An evolutionary imprint check on $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species produced a codon-bias expression-correlation verdict but did not support the strong selection-imprint claim: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and positive-selection-imprint status false. A structural-order axis audit found a significant residual coordinate only for $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, sign-flip $p=0.03125$, and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$; other axes such as $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$ and $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$ had negative weighted held-out increments. These companion facts let the chapter report axis-specific finite behavior while forbidding a population-law, organism-independent direction, or selection narrative.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the chapter at the level of controlled statistical mediation by modeled tAI. The strongest concrete row is yeast, where $5815$ joined proteins give $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ and reduce the residual-to-protein association from $R^2=0.29837624994140827$ to $R^2=0.0151709093608293$ after conditioning on modeled tAI. Several other organisms show positive mediated fractions in the same finite decomposition, and all twelve panels satisfy the stated rank sufficiency checks. The correct reading is therefore: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a finite controlled association with matched protein abundance whose measured size is substantially attenuated by a modeled translation-adaptation coordinate in multiple organism panels.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
Nothing in this chapter by itself establishes observed translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law. Modeled tAI and stAI-style weights are codon-adaptation annotations, not ribosome-occupancy observations. PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein measurement and is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context. Direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the $q_i\to t_j$ step is present on the same controlled surface. Any stronger statement requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
