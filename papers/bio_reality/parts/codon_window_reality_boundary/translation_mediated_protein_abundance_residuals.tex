\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance residuals}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether a synonymous residual coordinate from the $B^*_{Q6}$ coding-sequence packet can enter a protein-abundance fit only through a translation-layer readout.  The finite surface is made of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over nine residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation annotation or translation-efficiency readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The admissible readback is not a direct $q_i \to p_k$ association.  It is the separated chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, an $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance held as controls whenever those measurements are present.  In the finite run described here, $t_j$ is modeled per-protein tAI and $p_k$ is measured protein abundance from joined coding-sequence and abundance rows.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates.}
The residual side contains the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are retained as coding-sequence residuals after the amino-acid quotient.  They are not promoted to protein-level objects merely because they are numerically defined.  The first finite translation contact used three organisms for modeled tAI weights, with $61$ sense codons per organism and no zero raw-weight fallback in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, or \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The bounded modeled-tAI coupling table had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$.  That table is evidence for a bounded modeled translation-readout contact, not for observed ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Matched finite rows.}
The cross-layer mediation run used $12$ organism-specific tables and $124{,}977$ joined coding-sequence/protein rows in total.  The joined row counts were $4{,}042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11{,}661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14{,}410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13{,}295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3{,}739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12{,}418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19{,}053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19{,}395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4{,}129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} MC2 155, $13{,}322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5{,}815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1{,}656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The translation scalar was the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T = \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the coding-sequence codon count and $w_c$ is the tAI weight from all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout was $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts.

\paragraph{Controlled ranks.}
For every organism, the residualized design had $9$ $Q6$ coordinates, one translation column, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$.  The control matrix had $24$ columns and residual control rank $23$.  The remaining degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $1{,}633$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19{,}372$ in \emph{Mus musculus}; each organism passed the powered-rank sufficiency check, and each mediation fraction lay in the unit interval.  This makes the finite matrix algebra well-posed for the reported decomposition.

\paragraph{Mediation readout.}
Let $X$ denote the joint nine-dimensional $Q6$ residual block, $T$ the modeled per-protein tAI scalar, $Y$ the protein-abundance readout, and $Z$ the control block.  The reported quantity is the loss of direct $X \to Y$ explanatory power after conditioning on $T$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} = \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Across the twelve organisms, the mediated fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
&0.6084594553,\ 0.4872268357,\ 0.3157053954,\ 0.6165167749,\ 0.6129222528,\ 0.1976903965,\\
&0.2720628916,\ 0.1548879991,\ 0.0839760292,\ 0.1748968180,\ 0.9491551041,\ 0.0185066856.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest mediated fraction occurred in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.2983762499$, direct conditioned $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709094$, and indirect difference $0.2832053406$.  The smallest fraction occurred in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, with $M^{QTP}=0.0185066856$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.0639751425$, direct conditioned $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0627911747$, and indirect difference $0.0011839679$.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The finite-row evidence supports the cross-layer relation at the level of statistical mediation through modeled translation readout.  Every organism has positive indirect $R^2$ difference, every $M^{QTP}$ value is between $0$ and $1$, and every residualized rank check is sufficient.  The claim is therefore retained as a controlled $Q6 \to T \to P$ matrix statement: at least one, and in this run all twelve organism tables, show a positive attenuation of the residual-to-protein association after the modeled translation scalar is inserted.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result does not show causal translation control, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is not ribosome profiling.  Conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional coding-sequence/protein-abundance rows is not a perturbation, synonymous-edit, rescue, or mechanistic assay.  Protein abundance is also not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context.  Any stronger biological reading requires a distinct contact at that layer and a same-coordinate controlled statistic before promotion beyond the mediated abundance matrix.
