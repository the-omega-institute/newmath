\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance boundary for the B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite cross-layer mediation surface rather than as direct protein-output evidence.  The rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$; $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout such as modeled per-protein tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency; and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The admissible readback is therefore not $q_i \to p_k$ by itself.  It is the controlled chain $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance have been accounted for.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI contact.}
The bounded tAI calculation supplied a first finite translation readout without turning the residual geometry into observed ribosome dynamics.  In the modeled-tAI survival run, the codon-count space had 61 sense codons and the residual coordinate list had the same nine entries as above.  Three organisms were loaded for the bounded contact: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, human, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local GtRNAdb payload covered all 20 standard amino-acid families in each organism, with 87 matched tRNA FASTA headers out of 89 in \emph{E. coli} K12, 432 out of 432 in human, and 275 out of 275 in yeast.  The dos Reis wobble-weight computation returned all 61 sense-codon weights in each organism with zero raw-weight fallbacks equal to 0.  At the bounded threshold $0.01$, the modeled matrix had 81 entries above threshold, and the largest absolute entry was $3801961.031607896$.  This is a strong finite coupling diagnostic for modeled codon-adaptation weights, but it remains underpowered for a population survival claim because the organism count is 3, not the declared 40-or-more matched-species regime.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation statistic.}
The powered cross-layer abundance run evaluated twelve organism-matched CDS/protein tables with modeled per-protein tAI as the middle readout.  The joined row counts were 4042 for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, 11661 for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, 14410 for \emph{Danio rerio}, 13295 for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, 3739 for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, 12418 for chicken, 19053 for human, 19395 for mouse, 4129 for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, 13322 for rat, 5815 for yeast, and 1656 for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In every organism the residualized design had nine $Q$ coordinates, rank $9$ for $X$, rank $1$ for $T$, and rank $10$ for the joint $X,T$ design after the 23-rank control block.  Thus the finite matrix is rank-sufficient for the declared statistical decomposition.

\paragraph{Mediated fractions.}
The scalar mediation readout is the fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q \to P)-R^2(Q \to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q \to P)} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed values were in the unit interval for all twelve organisms.  Yeast had the largest mediated fraction, $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with total $R^2(QP)=0.29837624994140827$, direct conditioned $R^2(QP\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  The other mediated fractions were $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ for \emph{C. elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ for zebrafish, $0.6165167748739756$ for fly, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.1976903964724546$ for chicken, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, $0.17489681798543982$ for rat, and $0.01850668564589326$ for \emph{S. solfataricus}.  These positive fractions support a finite statistical attenuation pattern: conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI removes a nonzero part of the B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ association with protein abundance.

\paragraph{Control-residual details.}
The control block used an intercept, log CDS length, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline.  The abundance readout was $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in ppm from PAXdb-style matched protein rows joined to real CDS codon counts.  The modeled middle readout was the per-protein mean tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{protein} &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the protein's codon count and $w_c$ is the organism-specific tAI weight from all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The tRNA-copy totals used by the per-protein tAI computation ranged from 47 in \emph{S. solfataricus} and 49 in \emph{M. smegmatis} to 8874 in zebrafish; zero raw-weight geometric-mean fill counts were 3 for \emph{B. subtilis}, 1 for \emph{E. coli}, 1 for \emph{M. smegmatis}, 1 for \emph{S. solfataricus}, and 0 for the remaining organisms.

\paragraph{Yeast structure and rate-side checks.}
A yeast five-way join supplied a separate abundance-residual decomposition with 4759 joined rows, a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$, and an overall join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$.  In that run, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation $p$-values were $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the structure-incremental statistic.  A separate yeast rate-slot run used 3289 genes and concluded that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  These finite rows are compatible with translation-linked execution substructure, but they do not convert the abundance mediation statistic into a causal pathway.

\paragraph{Axis and selection checks.}
The structural-order axis audit identified $f3\_stress$ as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate: it was both the dominant increment axis and the dominant loading axis, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  Other coordinates had positive or negative increments but did not pass the same gate: for example $Arg_{AGR}$ had weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, while $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint check had 80 orthogroups, 240 rows, and 3 species; it reported $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null 95 percent maximum $0.05427157066522958$.  Its positive-selection gate was false.  Thus the abundance mediation result should be read as codon-bias expression correlation and modeled translation attenuation, not as a powered evolutionary selection-imprint result.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The claim accepted here is narrow.  The finite rows support a controlled statistical matrix in which B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are associated with protein abundance and a nonzero share of that association is removed by conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI.  A residual coordinate alone does not establish translation; modeled tAI alone does not observe ribosome motion; protein abundance alone does not isolate translation from mRNA level, turnover, localization, post-translational state, folding, or pathway context.  No folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this matrix unless a distinct measurement contact at that layer supplies the relevant rows and the same-coordinate controlled statistic is then computed.  The finite code-table background is also only a lower-layer sanity check: the confirmatory genetic-code contact represented the locked module-classification targets and had 40 successes out of 40 in both the boundary and independent-replication binomial checks, with upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ and zero unexpected successes in 20 negative-control and 20 critical-ablation trials.  That supports the codon-geometry starting surface, not the higher biological layers by itself.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter can state a ready finite-row result: the B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface has a cross-layer statistical mediation pattern through modeled translation-adaptation readouts into matched protein-abundance rows.  The strongest organism-level mediated fraction is yeast at $0.9491551041216973$, and all twelve organism fractions are positive and lie in $[0,1]$.  The correct scientific status is therefore controlled modeled-translation mediation of protein-abundance association, with explicit refusal of causal, structural, mechanistic, and universal promotion.
