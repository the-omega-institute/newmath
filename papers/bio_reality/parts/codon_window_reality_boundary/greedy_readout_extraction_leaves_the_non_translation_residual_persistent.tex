\subsection{Greedy Readout Extraction Leaves the Non-Translation Residual Persistent}
\label{sec:q6-residual-basis-extraction-greedy-readout-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the modeled-translation-complement abundance residual $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ for the $B^*_{Q6}$ packet.  The finite object is a recursive residual-energy ledger.  For each organism with enough matched rows, the ledger starts from
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_0 &= P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}},\\
\rho_k &= \frac{\|R_k\|^2}{\|R_0\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
At step $k$, the available readout blocks $H$ are scored by the absorbed residual fraction penalized by a description-length term,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{score}_k(H)
  &= \frac{\|\Pi_H R_{k-1}\|^2}{\|R_{k-1}\|^2}
     - \lambda \operatorname{DL}(H),\\
\lambda &= 0.01,\\
\operatorname{DL}(H) &= \log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}}(H)).
\end{aligned}
$$
The selected block $H_k^*$ is the block with maximal score among the remaining candidates.  The ledger then records the absorbed increment, the retained fraction $\rho_k$, and a degree-of-freedom-matched deterministic Gaussian null comparison for the selected block.  This is a finite coordinate-and-relation readout: it reports how much of the residual can be linearly absorbed by the declared readout panel, not why the absorption occurs.

\paragraph{Readout classification.}
Using the declared persistence floor $\rho_{\mathrm{floor}}=0.97$, the computed classification is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{persistent}
&=\{\mathrm{danio\_rerio},\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655},\\
&\quad \mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}\},\\
\mathrm{compressible}&=\varnothing,\\
\mathrm{partially\_compressible}&=\varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the central negative result.  The available panel can identify small, sometimes null-exceeding projections, but it does not compress the residual in any computed organism.  The greedy readout is also not a global optimum certificate: it is a finite recursive extraction rule over the declared candidates.  A different candidate library, a richer matched data matrix, or a non-greedy objective could change the descriptive curve, but the present finite rows do not support a shared compressing readout.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The chapter records a bounded residual-compression statistic.  It does not establish translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality.  Projection by $\Pi_H$ is a statistical operation, so absorbed energy is not biological action.  A selected label such as $\mathrm{ptm\_density}$, $\mathrm{localization}$, $\mathrm{mrna}$, $\mathrm{measured\_te}$, or $\mathrm{measured\_trna\_tai}$ names a readout block in the finite panel; it is not by itself a protein-state mechanism.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are modeled adaptation annotations rather than direct ribosome occupancy.  Proteomics abundance is not folding, structure, function, or phenotype evidence without an additional layer-matched measurement and statistic.  The honest conclusion is therefore persistence: the non-translation abundance residual remains mostly unexplained by the current readout basis, and the missing organisms require data before they can be classified.
