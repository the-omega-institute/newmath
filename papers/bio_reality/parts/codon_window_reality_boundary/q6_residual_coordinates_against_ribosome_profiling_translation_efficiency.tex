\subsection{Q6 residual coordinates against ribosome-profiling translation efficiency}
\label{subsec:q6-ribosome-te-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the claim as a finite row problem over coding sequences.  The carrier is a CDS-by-residual-coordinate matrix for $B^{*}_{Q6}$, crossed with organism-matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency rows.  The intended residual coordinates are not amino-acid sequence claims: they are synonymous residual coordinates after quotienting by the amino-acid baseline and after controlling the same rows for organism, GC3, length, methionine density, modelled codon-adaptation baselines, and matched mRNA abundance.  A coordinate is therefore read only through a bounded survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, where $q_i$ is a residual synonymous coordinate and $T_j$ is a matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readout.  The positive case is $S^{QT}_{ij} > 0$ under the stated controls; the non-surviving case is $S^{QT}_{ij} \leq 0$ or a rank-saturated design in which the apparent gain is explained by the baseline.

\paragraph{Coordinate list and readout boundary.}
The checked finite experiment used nine residualized $Q$ coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For each fold, the residualization $Q \sim C$ was fit only on the training genes, and the held-out coordinates were obtained from the corresponding train-fit coefficients and train residual scaling, with ridge parameter $\lambda=1.0$.  The readout used $n=2206$ genes and the signed target
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta P_g &= z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{SD}) - z_{\mathrm{rank}}(P_{YEPD}),
\end{aligned}
$$
with mean $-1.1858399826713623\times 10^{-18}$ and standard deviation $0.4434928347257967$.  The sensitivity target was the z-scored log ratio $\log(P_{SD})-\log(P_{YEPD})$.  The five held-out gene folds had sizes $447$, $475$, $444$, $451$, and $389$.

\paragraph{Control matrix.}
The finite control set contained an intercept, log CDS sense-codon length, five train-fold PCA coordinates from the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from ordered CDS codons, a mean pLDDT structural-order proxy with a missingness indicator, and integrated PaxDb log10 baseline abundance.  The structural-order proxy had $4$ missing rows, and the complete-case count for the richer control stage was $1654$.  These controls are part of the finite design, not a license to infer structure or mature protein behavior from the ribosome-profiling response.

\paragraph{Held-out survival calculation.}
The main held-out comparison between the baseline model $M_0$ and the residual-coordinate model $M_1$ did not support a positive survival entry.  On $2206$ held-out test rows, the baseline had $R^2_{M_0}=0.008108769333748045$ and $SSE_{M_0}=430.68597066859365$, whereas the residual-coordinate model had $R^2_{M_1}=0.005545435711181601$ and $SSE_{M_1}=431.7989876963185$.  Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= -0.002563333622566444,\\
\Delta \log pD &= -2.936811337346853.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual code saving was $-2.8467939090648606$ nats.  After the $Q$-plus-$\lambda$ encoding cost of $38.494680999067235$ nats, the description-length drop was $-41.3414749081321$ nats, so the description-length gate was false.  In this finite panel, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates did not improve the controlled ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency description.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The null suite also did not support promotion of the coordinate block.  Under the condition-sign-flip null with $300$ draws, the 95 percent thresholds were $\Delta \log pD=-0.05373328681116618$, $\Delta R^2=0.00002359796412121362$, and description-length drop $-38.4687437759017$ nats; the actual main comparison failed both the $\Delta \log pD$ and $\Delta R^2$ gates.  Under the matched-$Q$ permutation null with $300$ draws, the 95 percent thresholds were $\Delta \log pD=3.7150577222696484$, $\Delta R^2=0.003501567522117832$, and description-length drop $-34.593988916260244$ nats; the observed held-out gain was below these thresholds.  Under the synonymous-recoding null with $300$ draws, amino-acid sequence was preserved, per-amino-acid GC3-ending counts were preserved exactly, the mean absolute GC3 error was $0.0$, and the mean relative ENC-proxy error was $0.09042494844341317$ with p95 relative error $0.4059574704858408$.  Its 95 percent thresholds were $\Delta \log pD=0.011353124037214002$, $\Delta R^2=0.00028102266970064793$, and description-length drop $-38.18213471025496$ nats.  The replicate placebo class was unavailable because the SD/YEPD wrappers exposed endpoint condition abundance rather than same-condition replicate abundance columns.

\paragraph{Side readouts.}
The auxiliary signed-only checks separate the main negative result from weaker side signals.  The absolute-$\Delta$ side readout had $\Delta \log pD=-3.8682101284448436$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0030746447001754884$, residual code saving $-3.5938977348833916$ nats, and description-length drop $-42.08857873395063$ nats.  The endpoint-mean-rank extra-control side readout had $\Delta \log pD=-2.7093968327387756$, $\Delta R^2=-0.002354084610949325$, residual code saving $-2.6130013210868746$ nats, and description-length drop $-41.10768232015411$ nats.  A mean-$P$ side readout had $\Delta \log pD=7.4632216658321795$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0026536465383733$, but after the same encoding cost its description-length drop was still negative at $-30.29464161255981$ nats.  The absolute-rank, mean-rank, GO, stress, high-$P$, and low-$P$ subgroups were not used for the main conclusion.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite verdict for this record is $\mathrm{no\_compressible\_q\_interaction}$.  The positive gate failed because the description-length drop was not positive and because the held-out $\Delta R^2$ and $\Delta \log pD$ were not above all applicable null-95 thresholds.  Consequently the present finite-row evidence keeps the Q6 residual coordinates at the level of code-read or model-coupling residuals for this readout, not observed translation-efficiency survivors.  The result is a bounded negative result for this panel and this controlled design, not a biological irrelevance theorem for Q6 coordinates.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter only licenses statements about controlled finite-row survival against matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readouts.  It does not establish translation realization in general, folded structure, physical admissibility, protein abundance, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Modelled tAI or stAI weights remain modelled codon-adaptation annotations unless a separate ribosome-profiling contact supplies observed occupancy or translation-efficiency data.  Likewise, ribosome-profiling translation efficiency alone does not supply protein folding, mature protein abundance, cellular function, or a global biological law; each of those layers would require its own matched contact and its own finite controlled survival calculation.
