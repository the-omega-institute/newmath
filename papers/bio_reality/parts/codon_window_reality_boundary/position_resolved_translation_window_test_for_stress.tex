\subsection{Position-resolved translation-window test for $f_3$-stress}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-window-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Claim form.}
This record isolates a narrow cross-layer conjecture: a synonymous third-position stress coordinate, computed on finite coding-sequence windows after amino-acid projection, is allowed to become biologically testable only when the same row also carries a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readout. The row object is therefore not a codon-geometry claim by itself. It is a table whose admissible entries are indexed by organism, coding sequence or protein identifier, codon-position bin, amino-acid-bin controls, GC3, local length, methionine density, a bounded wobble-weight surface, matched mRNA abundance, and a ribosome-footprint occupancy residual. The proposed signal is the residual component of the occupancy readout that remains associated with $f_3$-stress after these same-row controls have been applied.

\paragraph{Finite row.}
For a coding window $i$, the admissible datum has the form $(o_i,c_i,b_i,s_i,a_i,g_i,\ell_i,m_i,w_i,r_i,\beta_i)$, where $o_i$ is the organism, $c_i$ is the coding-sequence identifier, $b_i$ is the position or amino-acid bin, $s_i$ is the $f_3$-stress coordinate after synonymous projection, $a_i$ records amino-acid composition controls, $g_i$ is GC3, $\ell_i$ is window length, $m_i$ is local methionine density, $w_i$ records wobble asymmetry, $r_i$ is the position-resolved ribosome-occupancy residual, and $\beta_i$ is the break flag. The working contrast is not merely whether $s_i \neq 0$. It is whether $s_i$ improves a bounded translation-window residual specifically where the wobble surface is asymmetric, meaning that the third-position weight attached to a codon is not invariant under the $f_3$ partner operation.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{eligible}(i) = 1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
&\operatorname{rank}(X_i) \text{ is sufficient},\\
&r_i \text{ is joined to the same organism, same CDS, and same window},\\
&w_{c_i} \neq w_{c_i^{f_3}} \text{ or the row is kept in the symmetric-control stratum}.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The conjecture predicts a positive survival pattern only in the restricted stratum where codon topology, wobble weights, and ribosome occupancy are all present in the same finite row. A surviving association must beat simpler controls based on amino-acid bin or position, GC3, length, methionine density, codon count, matched mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, and rank adequacy. If the design matrix is rank-degenerate, if the ribosome-footprint readout is not joined to the same coding window, or if the same-row mRNA control is absent, the statistic does not graduate beyond a request for data. In that case $f_3$-stress remains a codon-topology coordinate rather than a translation-layer variable.

\paragraph{Contact surfaces.}
The ribosome-occupancy contact is the decisive readout surface. It permits codon-indexed or fixed-window footprint occupancy only after external ribosome-profiling data are joined to ordered coding-sequence coordinates. Its known distortions include pausing, nuclease and fragment-length effects, frame-assignment ambiguity, mappability, start and stop edge effects, library normalization, condition specificity, and coding-sequence annotation mismatch. The mRNA contact is required to separate translation-layer variation from transcript abundance; without it, an apparent footprint signal can be transcriptional. The tAI or stAI contact supplies bounded codon-adaptation weights, but those weights are modeled annotations. They can define the wobble-weight surface used by the test, but they are not themselves an observation of ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Finite evidence already in the packet.}
The present row bundle contains two linked verified cross-layer records, but neither is evidence for the $f_3$-stress translation-window conjecture. The human N-terminal acetylation record is identified by run $90\mathrm{ea}6\mathrm{b}5\mathrm{f}7\mathrm{fc}7$, with $n=1426$, runtime $5.372$ seconds, and abundance control recorded as false. The protein-solubility record is identified by run $8742\mathrm{a}224\mathrm{b}538$, with $n=3198$ and runtime $60.0$ seconds. These rows show that the surrounding BioReality table can carry finite cross-layer experiment metadata, but they do not provide the missing same-window ribosome-footprint residuals, wobble-asymmetry strata, rank checks, or controlled $f_3$-stress coefficients for this conjecture.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The record fails locally if review readiness, a contact name, modeled wobble weights, $S^Q_T$ notation, $f_3$-stress geometry, codon tables, or coding windows are treated as if they were already measured position-resolved translation statistics. It also fails if an organism-level codon table or a modeled tAI or stAI surface is promoted to ribosome occupancy, protein output, structure, function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law. Passing the row audit requires an actual same-organism, same-CDS, same-window join with mRNA control and a declared position-controlled occupancy statistic.

\paragraph{Local non-claim.}
No translation realization, protein abundance, folding state, structural order, physical admissibility, localization, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this row. Such conclusions require separate contacts at those layers and must also survive the prior translation-readout test. The only admissible present claim is narrower: the finite table specifies how an $f_3$-stress residual could be tested against a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy residual under wobble-asymmetry and amino-acid-composition controls, and it states the conditions under which the attempt must remain null.
