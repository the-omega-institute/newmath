\subsection{Survival statistic and break condition}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-survival-statistic}

\paragraph{Controlled score.}
For each organism and each admissible window panel, the test fits a baseline model using amino-acid composition or amino-acid bins, position bins, GC3, length, methionine density, local codon-count summaries, mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability filters, and simpler codon-usage predictors.  The $f_3$ term is then added only through the residual coordinate and its wobble-weight context.  A row panel supports the chapter when
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
  \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}(f_3;T_{\mathrm{pos}},Z) &> 0,\\
  \operatorname{rank}(f_3,T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z) &\text{ remains positive under ablation checks}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statistic fails if every controlled entry is nonpositive, if the sign is carried only by organism-level tAI or stAI weights, if the effect disappears after mRNA and position controls, or if the wobble-weight asymmetry $w_c\neq w_c^{f_3}$ is assumed rather than observed in the row table.

\paragraph{Rank and ablation requirements.}
The rank condition is essential because a single favorable coordinate can be produced by codon composition, local mappability, transcript abundance, or positional edge effects.  The admissible panel must therefore survive held-out evaluation after amino-acid quotienting, same-window mRNA adjustment, GC3 and length controls, methionine-density control, codon-count and simpler-baseline ablations, and position-bin or amino-acid-bin stratification.  If the $f_3$ residual improves the table only before these adjustments, the chapter records a boundary contact but not a powered survival entry.
