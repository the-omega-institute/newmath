\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance contact for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record studied here is the finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$. The $q_i$ coordinate ranges over the nine computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The translation coordinate $t_j$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI score, computed from real CDS codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights. The protein coordinate $p_k$ is $\log_{10}$ abundance ppm from measured PAXdb protein abundance, joined to the same CDS rows. The intended readout is not a direct $q_i \to p_k$ assertion. It is the controlled chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &\longrightarrow T \longrightarrow P,\\
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism},\ GC3,\ \log(\text{CDS length}),\ M\text{-density}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where the $Q$ block must first retain a translation-readout component before the protein-abundance association is allowed into this chapter's fitting surface.

\paragraph{Joined rows and controls.}
The powered mediation computation used twelve organism-specific finite joins: $4042$ proteins for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In every organism the residualized design had $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, $9$ residual coordinates, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(Q)=9$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Q,T)=10$. The residual degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, so the calculation is a finite-row regression surface rather than a symbolic extrapolation.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism the statistic compares the total controlled protein-abundance association $c=R^2_{QP}$ with the remaining direct association $c'=R^2_{QP\mid T}$ after conditioning on modeled tAI. The mediated component is $c-c'$, and the reported fraction is $M^{QTP}=(c-c')/c$ when the value lies in $[0,1]$. All twelve organism rows satisfied the unit-interval check. The largest fraction was in yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. Other strong bounded fractions occurred in \emph{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, \emph{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$, and \emph{C. elegans} with $0.4872268357227724$. The smallest fractions were still nonnegative: \emph{S. solfataricus} had $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$ with $c=0.06397514251295629$ and $c'=0.06279117466131778$, and \emph{M. smegmatis} had $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$ with $c=0.03209009170649934$ and $c'=0.029395293228368185$.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival audit computed the same nine residual coordinates in a $61$-sense-codon space for three organisms. The local GtRNAdb payloads contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \emph{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast. The bounded modeled-tAI coupling surface had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$. This supports a modeled codon-adaptation contact, but it does not by itself become ribosome profiling or proteomics validation; the same audit explicitly records that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival when $\geq 40$ matched species are requested.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The chapter therefore admits a narrow positive statement: within matched CDS/protein rows, and after amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, CDS length, and $M$-density controls, the joint nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a bounded statistical mediation decomposition through modeled per-protein tAI into protein abundance. The statement is strongest as a cross-layer association audit, not as a molecular mechanism. The yeast row is the most concentrated example, while the archaeal and mycobacterial rows show that the same finite test can return only a small mediated fraction.

\paragraph{Neighboring checks.}
Several adjacent computations keep the interpretation bounded. The yeast structure-mediation audit joined $4759$ genes and found $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$; its structure-only fraction was only $0.003284564452662019$, and the incremental structure fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$. The evolutionary-selection-imprint audit over $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows did not pass the strong selection gate: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$ was below the null-$95$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$. The mass-balance dynamic closure test on $120$ genes gave $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, while the turnover residual audit on $3290$ genes gave $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$. These negative or bounded neighbors prevent the mediation result from being read as a general law of protein output.

\paragraph{Perturbation and axis context.}
The same residual family has positive finite contacts elsewhere, but they do not enlarge this chapter's claim. The observable execution decomposition in yeast used $3289$ genes and reported that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes. The structural-order axis audit identified $f3\_stress$ as the only fold-level sign-flip-passing coordinate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$. Synonymous perturbation checks also remain layer-specific: the \emph{E. coli} mRFP protein-output panel had $1459$ variants, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null $p=0.004975124378109453$; the yeast synonymous-mutation panel had $1783$ mutants across $20$ genes, with real-CSC robustness and real-versus-proxy correlation $0.39717918221186976$. These are finite contacts at their own readout layers, not permissions to infer folding, phenotype, or universality from the present tAI-mediated abundance matrix.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result is a controlled statistical mediation record for $Q \to T \to P$ with modeled tAI as $T$ and PAXdb abundance as $P$. It does not show observed ribosome occupancy, translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary selection, causation, rescue, or a universal biological rule. Any such assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact: ribosome profiling for observed translation, structure or stability measurements for folding and physical state, perturbation or rescue data for mechanism, and phenotype assays for system-level output. Without that additional contact, the admissible conclusion remains the finite mediated association reported above.
