\subsection{Four-case translation-protein discriminant for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:q6-four-case-translation-protein-discriminant-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite panel and residual coordinate.}
The object considered here is the finite $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate tensor on matched coding-sequence, translation-readout, and protein-abundance rows.  The coordinate block has nine residual directions, namely K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress.  Every comparison below is residualized against the same finite control frame: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions from the observed CDS codon counts, GC3, and an M-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The resulting control matrix has 24 columns and residualized rank $23$ in the reported organism panels.  The discriminant is not a free biological interpretation of a codon pattern; it is a four-way finite classification of the signs and conditional drops of the residualized quantities $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$, with a separate check of the $Q \to T \to P$ decrease.

\paragraph{Discriminant rule.}
For each matched row set and each residual coordinate block, the four classes are assigned by the following controlled readout table:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &: S^{QT} \leq 0 \;\wedge\; S^{QP} \leq 0,\\
B &: S^{QT} > 0 \;\wedge\; S^{QP} \leq 0,\\
C &: S^{QT} \leq 0 \;\wedge\; S^{QP} > 0,\\
D &: S^{QT} > 0 \;\wedge\; S^{QP} > 0 \;\wedge\; R^{2}_{QP\mid T} < R^{2}_{QP}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Class $A$ says that the residual coordinate compresses away on both the measured or modelled translation surface and the protein-abundance surface.  Class $B$ says that the coordinate survives at the translation readout but does not survive to the selected protein readout.  Class $C$ says that a protein-abundance residual is present without support from the measured translation surface used in the panel.  Class $D$ is the only class in which the finite table supports a bounded translation-mediated protein-readout relation: the coordinate survives on $T$, survives on $P$, and its direct $Q$-to-$P$ statistic is reduced after conditioning on $T$.  This rule is intentionally narrower than a biological mechanism claim.  A residual coordinate alone does not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality; each such statement requires its own layer-matched measurement contact.

\paragraph{Measured translation readout.}
The measured-ribosome-efficiency panel uses $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$ with TE defined as footprint divided by mRNA abundance, and protein readout $P$ as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from matched proteomics.  Four organisms have powered finite rows.  The joined three-way row counts are $13140$ for danio, $3415$ for E. coli K12 MG1655, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for yeast.  The measured discriminant statistics are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{danio}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.04926578454614652,\; S^{QT}=0.04034217614654355,\; M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0,\\
\mathrm{E.coli}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.10941535951828074,\; S^{QT}=0.009394266754822262,\; M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.04091158340950673,\\
\mathrm{human}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.04288464996165745,\; S^{QT}=0.018837911344127926,\; M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.0027966485036786744,\\
\mathrm{yeast}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042,\; S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436,\; M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
All four panels have positive $S^{QT}$ and positive $S^{QP}$ after the stated controls, so their sign pattern enters class $D$ at the finite-table level.  The strength of the mediated drop is heterogeneous: yeast gives a large measured fraction $0.4793069656345167$, E. coli gives a smaller but positive fraction $0.04091158340950673$, human is close to zero at $0.0027966485036786744$, and danio is clipped to zero because the raw fraction is negative, $-0.04801486048721136$.  This means the class label records the supported readout geometry, while the scalar $M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}$ records how much of the protein-abundance association is reduced by conditioning on measured TE.

\paragraph{Modelled translation readout.}
The modelled translation panel uses per-protein mean tAI, computed from codon counts, organism-matched tRNA gene-copy totals, and dos Reis wobble weights.  It is a translation-layer annotation, not ribosome profiling.  The row counts span $1656$ proteins in Sulfolobus, $3739$ in E. coli, $5815$ in yeast, $19053$ in human, and $19395$ in mouse, with intermediate panels for Bacillus, worm, danio, fly, chicken, Mycobacterium, and rat.  The total $Q$-to-$P$ and $Q$-to-$P$ given $T$ statistics give the following modelled mediation fractions:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Bacillus} &: 0.6084594553474505, & \mathrm{worm} &: 0.4872268357227724,\\
\mathrm{danio} &: 0.3157053953614641, & \mathrm{fly} &: 0.6165167748739756,\\
\mathrm{E.coli} &: 0.612922252793667, & \mathrm{chicken} &: 0.1976903964724546,\\
\mathrm{human} &: 0.27206289160119157, & \mathrm{mouse} &: 0.15488799907323988,\\
\mathrm{Mycobacterium} &: 0.08397602919861291, & \mathrm{rat} &: 0.17489681798543982,\\
\mathrm{yeast} &: 0.9491551041216973, & \mathrm{Sulfolobus} &: 0.01850668564589326.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest modelled tAI-mediated fraction is the yeast value $0.9491551041216973$, with $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$.  E. coli is also high at $0.612922252793667$, with $R^{2}_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$.  Sulfolobus is near the lower edge, with a fraction $0.01850668564589326$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.06397514251295629$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.06279117466131778$.  These values support a finite modelled-readout ranking, not a claim that tAI is an observed ribosome state.

\paragraph{Joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition.}
The measured-TE-plus-stability panel supplies a second decomposition for E. coli, human, and yeast.  The finite joins have $2517$ E. coli rows, $11322$ human rows, and $4759$ yeast rows.  Reported join hit rates are $0.9133458143888741$ for E. coli, $0.6064136881331024$ for human, and $0.8818572656921754$ for yeast.  The residualized protein associations and conditional values are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{E.coli}:&\quad R^{2}_{QP}=0.1181743523064932,\; R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.11583979275713331,\; R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.09093273405121653,\; R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036,\\
\mathrm{human}:&\quad R^{2}_{QP}=0.04274178488511215,\; R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657,\; R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814,\; R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993,\\
\mathrm{yeast}:&\quad R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803,\; R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314,\; R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165,\; R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding joint fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for E. coli, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast.  Each has permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$ under the stated deterministic paired-permutation construction.  The measured-TE-only component is small in E. coli and human, $0.01975521340963268$ and $0.00549490864190343$, while yeast has a much larger TE-only component $0.4697188319693239$.  Stability-only fractions are $0.23052056324898387$, $0.15314679870183337$, and $0.0626618595344251$ respectively.  Thus the same class-$D$ sign pattern can have different internal decompositions across organisms.

\paragraph{Stability and structure checks.}
The single-organism yeast mRNA-stability panel has $5085$ joined rows, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, and absorbed value $0.027596042074056304$, giving mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  Cross-domain mRNA-stability panels give $2720$ E. coli rows and $16165$ human rows.  Their fractions are $0.24697202727892137$ for E. coli and $0.1411299863319291$ for human, again with right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  The yeast 5-prime CDS MFE check has $4759$ joined rows and reports $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is only $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental fraction beyond measured TE and stability is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the corresponding right-tail values are $0.027972027972027972$ for $S^{Q\!S}$ and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  These numbers are recorded as bounded residual associations with a CDS-derived folding-energy proxy; they do not promote the coordinate to a folding, initiation, or physical-state claim.

\paragraph{Classification readback.}
The finite readback is therefore a four-case boundary table over the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block after residualization by amino-acid composition, organism-specific sequence controls, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA abundance where available, and simpler codon-usage baselines.  The measured-TE panels place danio, E. coli, human, and yeast in the positive-positive discriminant region, with yeast carrying the strongest measured $Q \to T \to P$ drop.  The modelled-tAI panel also gives positive mediated fractions across the twelve reported organisms, but those values belong to a modelled translation annotation and must not be merged with ribosome profiling.  Protein-abundance survival without a matched translation step is only a protein-readout residual.  Translation support without protein survival is a translation-only residual.  Joint positivity with a conditional reduction is the bounded class-$D$ relation.  No entry in this chapter licenses claims about protein folding, mature protein state, localization, PTM, stability, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law unless an independent finite contact at that layer is supplied and reported with its own rank and residual diagnostics.
