\subsection{The $f_3$-Stress Coordinate Requires a Translation Readout}\label{subsec:q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates a cross-layer conjecture for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate in coding sequences. The coordinate of interest is the $f_3$-stress row after amino-acid projection: synonymous codons are compared only after the amino-acid identity has been quotiented away, and the remaining signal is allowed to persist only as a controlled translation-layer distinction. The finite saturation computation supplies the ambient codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $20$ listed codons. The same row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $18$ listed codons. The two codons present in $M$ but absent from the displayed saturation list are $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$. The recorded $f_3$ boundary list is empty, so the finite row by itself does not expose a nonzero boundary object; it only fixes the coordinate surface on which the later readout test must be evaluated.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The conjectural biological content is not that the $f_3$ coordinate is automatically dynamical. It becomes a translation candidate only when the organism or context assigns unequal effective weights to paired third-position alternatives. In notation, the operative asymmetry is $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$, where $c^{f_3}$ denotes the codon paired with $c$ by the third-position $f_3$ operation. If the relevant tRNA or wobble-weight layer is symmetric across this pairing, the coordinate remains a residual geometric distinction and should not be promoted to a translation statement. Thus the readout is deliberately conditional: amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and mRNA abundance are controls rather than optional covariates.

\paragraph{Mechanism rows already available.}
Two finite mechanism rows make the conjecture worth testing, while still not settling it. The tRNA-poverty computation for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ boundary set evaluated $46$ requested organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations, and used $\alpha=0.05$. Its recorded status is passed, with the call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN combined with dos Reis wobble weights. A separate universal-core adaptation computation requested $16$ organisms, computed $11$, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous design columns; its recorded status is also passed. These rows support the presence of tRNA-supply and codon-adaptation structure around the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surface, but they do not by themselves supply a survival score for the $f_3$-stress coordinate against a matched translation readout.

\paragraph{Required survival statistic.}
The bounded readout for this chapter is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is an organism-matched translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The admissible positive case is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and mRNA abundance, and after ablation against simpler codon baselines. In explanatory terms, the residual $f_3$-stress coordinate must improve the controlled description of the translation row. A three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-degenerate matrix is therefore not evidence of survival: it is the boundary case in which the readout has no useful rank for this question.

\paragraph{Falsification rule.}
The conjecture is falsified at the translation interface if the powered organism panel keeps every controlled survival entry non-positive, or if the readout matrix remains rank-degenerate after the controls are applied. The relevant negative conclusion is narrow. It would say that this $f_3$-stress coordinate has not survived into the specified translation readout under the stated controls. It would not say that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ is biologically irrelevant, nor that codon usage has no tRNA association, nor that the finite codon surface is mathematically empty.

\paragraph{Local evidential boundary.}
This chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, a mechanism, or a universal biological law from the codon coordinate, the saturation row, the tRNA-poverty row, the codon-adaptation row, or a modelled tAI or stAI weight. Each of those stronger claims would need its own matched contact at the corresponding layer. In particular, ribosome profiling may support a translation-efficiency row only when paired with condition-matched mRNA abundance, because transcript abundance alone is not a translation realization. Likewise, tRNA copy number and wobble weights can explain an asymmetry hypothesis, but they do not themselves observe ribosome motion or protein output.

\paragraph{Operational conclusion.}
The finite data justify a guarded cross-layer test. The coordinate surface is concrete: $20$ codons in $M$, $18$ in the $f_3$ saturation list, and no displayed $f_3$ boundary element. The mechanism background is nonempty: $40$ computed organisms in the tRNA-poverty row with $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and $11$ computed organisms in the codon-adaptation row with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous design columns. The chapter is therefore ready as a conjectural BioReality record, provided the claim remains limited to the following statement: $f_3$-stress may survive as a translation-readout distinction only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position symmetry, and only when the controlled survival statistic is positive and nondegenerate.
