$$
\begin{aligned}

 \operatorname{rank}(Z)&=23,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)&=9,\\

The positive-entry threshold for partial-$R^2$ scoring is $10^{-12}$, while the degeneracy cutoff is $0.999999$; the observed $R^2_{QT}$ lies below that degeneracy cutoff.  The row therefore supports a modeled codon-topology-to-translation-weight survival statement for $f_3$-stress inside a rank-powered multi-organism panel.  The statement is deliberately narrower than a claim about measured elongation speed, protein abundance, folding, localization, biochemical function, or universal causation.

\paragraph{Cross-organism stability and residual accounting.}

These numbers make the intended interpretation sharp: the $f_3$-stress coordinate survives selected translation-proximal readouts, while much of the organism-level projection remains unexplained by the measured dictionaries.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite rows support the following bounded claim.  The $f_3$-stress residual is a genuine codon-topology coordinate after amino-acid projection; it is nontrivial in finite codon-usage contacts, couples to modeled wobble-weight readouts when the paired codon weights are not symmetric, survives a rank-powered $40$-organism modeled-tAI panel, and appears among the families passing the position-controlled ribosome-occupancy gate.  The claim stops there.  No translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, localization, stability, or universal law follows from this chapter unless a separate same-row contact supplies that layer and passes its own controls.  If future rows make the paired wobble weights $f_3$-symmetric, or if the residual design becomes rank-saturated, the correct status of this coordinate reverts to a bounded codon-residual observation rather than a cross-layer readout.
