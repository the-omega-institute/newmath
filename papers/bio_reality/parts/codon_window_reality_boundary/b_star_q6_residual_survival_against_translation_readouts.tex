\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual survival against translation readouts}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and coordinate frame.}
This record isolates a nine-coordinate synonymous-residual block inside sense-codon count space and asks whether that block remains visible after ordinary sequence-composition explanations have been removed.  The coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and an $f_3$ stress coordinate.  Each coordinate is tested only after quotienting by amino-acid composition and after residualizing against a control matrix $Z$ containing an intercept, CDS length on a logarithmic scale, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The finite matrix under discussion is the survival surface
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j \mid Z),\\
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates, $r_j$ is a translation-related readout, $\ell_j^{\perp}$ is the readout weight after synonymous projection, and $S_{ij}$ is read as a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement, not as a causal coefficient.

\paragraph{Finite coupling contact.}
The first finite check used three organisms, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, and computed all nine synonymous residual coordinates against a proxy readout given by the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The proxy coupling gate was not null: the threshold was $0.01$, all $9$ entries were nonzero above the reporting surface, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes only that the residual coordinate system is not numerically orthogonal to the finite codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact.  It does not by itself give a positive $S_{ij}$ entry, because survival requires the residualized readout regression under $Z$ rather than a raw or proxy covariance entry.

\paragraph{Leucine gate and sign audit.}
The dedicated CUN/UUR leucine gate on the same three-organism panel reported Pearson correlation $1.0$ and an amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The log-ratio signs were not uniform: the observed log ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with sign pattern $(+,-,+)$.  Thus the finite row supports a nonzero contact between codon usage and tRNA-copy summaries, while explicitly refusing a universal sign law.  The three-row statistic is too small to carry population-level directionality.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-readout survival.}
A rank-powered modeled translation check used $40$ organisms and the same nine residual coordinates.  The readout was modeled per-codon tAI derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine-coordinate residual basis.  The residualized control design had $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The projected residual coordinate matrix had rank $9$, and the reported joint modeled translation survival was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{shape}(S^{QT}) &= 9\times 9,\\
\tau_{+} &= 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degenerate-fit cutoff was $0.999999$, so the reported $R^2_{QT}$ is large but not classified as a saturated all-fit surface.  This is the strongest positive finite row for the translation-facing boundary: after the amino-acid quotient and the listed $Z$ controls, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block retains substantial modeled-tAI survival.

\paragraph{Measured and downstream readouts.}
The measured-readout side is more heterogeneous.  In the six-organism translation-complement computation, abundance components explained by the residual coordinates had $R^2_{QP}$ values $0.04613529805760884$ for Danio rerio, $0.10767526667768725$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.03980793595345331$ for Gallus gallus, $0.04454565117192484$ for Homo sapiens, $0.032090091625816136$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, and $0.29837624987287115$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding modeled-tAI-complement fractions $d$ were $0.874628721049266$, $0.6629960038578532$, $0.9400341806302775$, $0.9208949355679455$, $0.8972672018746489$, and $0.21173219572706703$, respectively.  Yeast therefore has the largest reported abundance association in this finite row, while the modeled-tAI complement is smallest for yeast and largest for chicken.

\paragraph{Candidate residual readouts.}
The direction-aware residual ranking used $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{col})$.  For Danio rerio, measured translation efficiency was the selected readout $H_o^*$, with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, $DL_H=1.0$, score $0.001566051846174291$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, null $p95=0.0003297458343745959$, and $n=13140$ joined rows.  For Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 no $H_o^*$ was selected; its best listed mRNA row had score $-0.008470202379021127$ and did not exceed the Bonferroni threshold.  For Gallus gallus the selected readout was complex membership, with score $-0.00575848521855683$ despite exceeding the Bonferroni threshold $0.0055416578806643324$.  For Homo sapiens the selected readout was also complex membership, with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, $A_HC_H=0.006566030115340095$, score $-0.0034339698846599055$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0002646171322365322$, and $n=18335$.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae the selected readout was mRNA, with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, $A_HC_H=0.0011462622274400686$, score $-0.008853737772559931$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$, null $p95=0.0004745820831535835$, and $n=5128$.  Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 had no computable ranking row in this table.  These results show that residual support may be organism-specific and may be positive, weak, or absent depending on the downstream readout.

\paragraph{Protein abundance and condition structure.}
Yeast condition-specific protein-abundance checks joined $2209$ shared proteins across SD and YEPD.  Both conditions used $24$ control columns with rank $23$, full nine-coordinate residual rank, and dominant coordinate $f_3$ stress.  The reported values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(SD) &= 0.3050385538305022,\\
R^2_{QP}(YEPD) &= 0.3280825603424665,\\
\operatorname{corr}(S^{QP}_{SD},S^{QP}_{YEPD}) &= 0.9983880256003688,\\
\cos(S^{QP}_{SD},S^{QP}_{YEPD}) &= 0.9993775935182967.
\end{aligned}
$$
A human tissue-specific protein-abundance check used adult B cells, heart, and liver from Kim 2014 SEQUEST PAXdb files.  The joined row counts were $9244$, $6468$, and $8664$, respectively.  The reported $R^2_{QP}$ values were $0.03626117434944674$ for adult B cells with dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$, $0.02320896321347296$ for adult heart with dominant coordinate $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, and $0.026133456228248052$ for adult liver with dominant coordinate $Ile_{AUA}$.  The pairwise cosine values were $0.6883655232187615$ for B cells versus heart, $0.8990093450963941$ for liver versus B cells, and $0.9176793373754919$ for liver versus heart, using $1000$ permutations for the comparison audit.

\paragraph{mRNA stability and mediation audit.}
The yeast mRNA half-life check joined $5085$ rows against the Neymotin 2014 4tU metabolic-labeling table.  The CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, with $5815$ CDS rows reported joined before half-life matching and $5379$ valid half-life records.  On the same control scheme, the mRNA-stability surface reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.05179429044975085,\\
R^2_{QP,\,same\ join} &= 0.2761140373962526,\\
\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP}) &= 0.8040935468384264.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant mRNA-stability coordinate was $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, whereas the same-join abundance surface was dominated by $f_3$ stress.  The mediation decomposition on the same $5085$ rows reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, and absorbed variance $0.027596042074056304$, giving mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$.  The permutation audit used $1000$ permutations and reported right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$, null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, and null $p95=0.0014571003414947088$.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports a bounded statement: the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block survives strongly against a modeled translation-adaptation readout under the declared amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls, and it also leaves measurable residual traces in several protein-abundance, mRNA, and candidate-readout tables.  The exact measured ribosome-profiling survival matrix remains incomplete in this packet, and the proxy tRNA-copy coupling is not a replacement for that matrix.  No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from these numbers unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant observation and the same controlled survival or mediation statistic is computed there.
