\subsection{Rank-powered translation-readout survival for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:q6-rank-powered-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-coding-sequence readout surface in which synonymous codon geometry is first reduced to $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and translation-facing readouts are then evaluated only after explicit control residualization. The coordinate side has nine residual axes: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The readout side uses the same nine projected modeled-tAI columns, so the finite survival surface has shape $9 \times 9$. The carrier is not a claim about protein abundance or ribosome motion. It is a controlled tabulation of whether amino-acid-quotient codon residuals retain explanatory mass when compared with organism-scoped modeled translation weights.

\paragraph{Residualization rule.}
For each synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, the amino-acid projection is treated as a nuisance component. The coordinate allowed to enter the survival test is therefore the quotient residual $q_i-P_\tau q_i$, not the raw codon-count contrast. Translation readout $T_j$ is likewise interpreted only after removing the finite control block $Z_T$: amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, length, M-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines. In the powered run the control table had $24$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z_T)=23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$. The coordinate residual block retained $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ after the same control pass.

\paragraph{Powered finite panel.}
The rank condition is met by a matched panel of $40$ organisms. The panel includes human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, budding yeast, chicken, Escherichia coli, Xenopus tropicalis, Nostoc, Anabaena, multiple Burkholderia and Mycobacterium entries, and additional bacterial, fungal, algal, and plant genomes. This is the relevant boundary between a powered finite-row test and the earlier three-organism contact. The earlier surface used Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, and it produced a nonzero modeled-tAI coupling table, but $n=3$ is not enough to support a rank-powered survival statement. That smaller table is retained only as a bounded contact check: it had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ coupling threshold, maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, codon-count space $61$, and modeled tAI weights for all $61$ sense codons in each of the three organisms.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
The powered statistic records whether a residual coordinate improves a controlled translation-readout fit. In compact form, the reported boundary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one coordinate-readout pair after the control projection, while the break condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &\leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for every pair, or an apparent positive table arising only from rank saturation. The powered finite-row run computed $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ on the $9 \times 9$ survival matrix, with a positive partial-$R^2$ threshold of $10^{-12}$ and a degeneracy threshold of $0.999999$. Since the observed $R^2_{QT}$ is below the degeneracy cutoff and the control-residual degrees of freedom remain positive, the all-$1.0$ projection artifact is not the explanation of the powered table.

\paragraph{Finite contacts.}
The empirical contact allowed here is restricted to organism-level codon usage, tRNA gene-copy summaries, and modeled tAI or stAI weights. Codon-usage tables report coding-sequence composition; tRNA gene-copy annotations report anticodon-family copy spectra; modeled tAI weights combine anticodon copy counts with wobble-weight assumptions. In the three-organism tRNA payload, Homo sapiens had $432$ FASTA headers with $432$ matched, Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $275$ with $275$ matched, and Escherichia coli K12 had $89$ headers with $87$ matched and $2$ unmatched. All three covered the $20$ standard amino-acid families, and each modeled-tAI table had zero fallback count for raw codon weights.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The ready status is therefore narrow. The finite evidence supports a controlled modeled translation-readout survival entry for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ under the stated residualization and rank conditions. It does not say that $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, or any other single coordinate, is a population-level law. It also does not turn modeled tAI into ribosome profiling, measured elongation speed, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, biological function, pathway mechanism, or universality. Any such statement would require a separate contact that directly observes the corresponding layer. The present result is a cross-layer finite-row relation between synonymous residual codon coordinates and modeled organism-level translation weights, with the promotion boundary kept closed.
