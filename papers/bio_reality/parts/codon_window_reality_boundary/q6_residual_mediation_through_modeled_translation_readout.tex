\subsection{Q6 residual mediation through modeled translation readout}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.} This record studies a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation-side coordinate $t_j$ is not an observed ribosome trace in this finite run; it is the per-protein mean modeled tAI value computed from real CDS codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein-side coordinate $p_k$ is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to the same CDS rows.  Thus the allowed readback is a controlled matrix $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$, not a direct promotion from synonymous residual geometry to protein realization.

\paragraph{Matched rows and ranks.} The powered cross-layer run used six organism-scoped row sets.  The joined protein counts were $14410$ for danio rerio, $3739$ for escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $12418$ for gallus gallus, $19053$ for homo sapiens, $4129$ for mycobacterium smegmatis strain MC2 155, and $5815$ for saccharomyces cerevisiae.  In every organism the residualized design kept $9$ $Q$ coordinates, one residualized modeled-tAI column, and a $23$-rank control block coming from $24$ control columns.  The combined residualized $(X,T)$ design had rank $10$, with $rank(X)=9$ and $rank(T)=1$ after controls.  Residual degrees of freedom after the control block were $14387$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $4106$, and $5792$ in the same organism order, so this record is not a small-row anecdote.  The controls include amino-acid composition, organism scope, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance controls; these variables remain controls and are not promoted into $Q$ coordinates.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.} The upstream modeled-tAI contact had three organism examples before the powered protein-abundance run: escherichia coli K12, homo sapiens, and saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The modeled readout covered all $61$ sense codons in each of those organisms, with zero raw-weight fallback counts $0$, $0$, and $0$.  The local GtRNAdb payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families in all three organisms, with matched FASTA header counts $87$ of $89$ in escherichia coli K12, $432$ of $432$ in homo sapiens, and $275$ of $275$ in saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The bounded modeled-coupling matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This establishes a finite modeled translation-readout contact, but the run itself records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered translation survival; it is modeled-tAI bounded coupling, not ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Mediation decomposition.} For each organism, the finite statistic compares the total residual-to-protein association with the direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI.  The local quantity reported as $M^{QTP}$ is the fraction of the controlled $Q \to P$ association assigned to the modeled-T channel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},\\
c &= R^2(Q,P \mid Z),\\
c' &= R^2(Q,P \mid T,Z).
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed fractions all lie in the unit interval.  Danio rerio has $c=0.04613529807085213$, $c'=0.031570135553274765$, indirect difference $0.014565162517577362$, and $M^{QTP}=0.3157053953614641$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  Gallus gallus has $c=0.03980793596647631$, $c'=0.031938289322513524$, indirect difference $0.007869646643962787$, and $M^{QTP}=0.1976903964724546$.  Homo sapiens has $c=0.04454565118019448$, $c'=0.03242643251185274$, indirect difference $0.012119218668341744$, and $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$.  Mycobacterium smegmatis has $c=0.03209009170649934$, $c'=0.029395293228368185$, indirect difference $0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.

\paragraph{Energy audit.} The residualized energy numbers show that the mediated channel is organism-dependent rather than a universal sign law.  The $X$-explained energy on $T$ is $0.21928827576976329$ in danio rerio, $0.256128714706293$ in escherichia coli, $0.17482157737522303$ in gallus gallus, $0.3099459586758378$ in homo sapiens, $0.026187928294862578$ in mycobacterium smegmatis, and $5.671703404506426$ in saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding $T$-incremental improvements on protein abundance after $X$ are $342.6284544657774$, $317.26302294928666$, $133.55048991934166$, $477.88368900318414$, $0.37452260494680445$, and $104.11162139652299$.  The strongest mediated fraction is therefore saccharomyces cerevisiae, while the weakest is mycobacterium smegmatis; this spread is evidence for a finite cross-layer statistical decomposition, not for a population law.

\paragraph{Interpretation.} The record is ready at the cross-layer statistical-relation level because every organism has sufficient residualized rank, positive indirect $R^2$ difference, and $M^{QTP}$ within $[0,1]$.  The result supports the following narrow statement: after the declared controls, the joint nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a protein-abundance association that is partly attenuated by conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI.  It does not show that any single codon residual causes protein abundance, and it does not isolate protein abundance from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or experimental condition.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is therefore not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a translation-readout step under the matched controls.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} No observed translation dynamics, folding conclusion, physical admissibility claim, biological function, mechanistic explanation, phenotype statement, or universal biological law follows from this finite table by itself.  Modeled tAI is a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not ribosome occupancy.  PAXdb abundance is a matched protein-abundance measurement, not a folding, stability, localization, or function assay.  Any higher-layer statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic on the same finite rows.
