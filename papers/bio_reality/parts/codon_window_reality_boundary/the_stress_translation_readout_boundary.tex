\subsection{The $f_3$-stress translation-readout boundary}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record isolates the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate after amino-acid quotienting and asks whether it remains visible in matched translation-layer rows.  The finite carrier is the CDS-by-organism residual coordinate table restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation readouts of the same organism: codon adaptation weights such as tAI or stAI, or ribosome-occupancy and translation-efficiency rows when transcript controls are available.  The question is not whether codon geometry is biologically interesting in isolation.  It is whether the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ is positive after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls have been included.

\paragraph{Saturation datum.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation computation fixes the codon-level boundary of the carrier.  The mediated set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ has $20$ codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $R$ set removes exactly the two ACA/ACG rows and has $18$ codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary list is empty, so the finite codon surface itself supplies no nonzero boundary witness.  The surviving distinction must therefore enter through organism-scoped asymmetry in tRNA or wobble weights, not through a residual codon-boundary row alone.

\paragraph{Wobble-broken trigger.}
The trigger is the inequality $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ for a context codon $c$ and its third-position $f_3$ partner.  If the paired synonymous codons have equal context weight after amino-acid projection, the $f_3$ coordinate is read as a null contrast for translation.  If the weights differ, the coordinate becomes testable against a translation row.  The survival statistic is interpreted only as a controlled partial readout,
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\quad &\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{the $f_3$ residual improves the controlled translation model for row }T_j,\\
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}\leq 0
\quad &\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{the row gives no positive controlled translation survival for this coordinate.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Rank-degenerate all-one matrices are classified as a null boundary.  They do not count as survival, because they contain no finite row contrast on which an $f_3$ distinction could act.

\paragraph{Observed finite support.}
The powered $B^*_{Q6}$ panel gives several bounded reasons to treat the conjecture as testable rather than decorative.  In the tRNA-poverty assay, $40$ of $46$ requested organisms were computed, $6$ were skipped, and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$ supported the call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights.  In the axis-spectrum assay, $11$ of $16$ requested organisms were computed with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{resid4}$.  The same spectrum reported the largest GC3 rank effect as $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{usage}$, so GC3 remains a control rather than the claimed readout.

\paragraph{Residual-axis context.}
The residual coordinate sits inside a larger powered geometry but is not promoted beyond the translation row.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation assay used $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous columns.  The non-tRNA residual optimality assay identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant axis under the same organism, fold, protein-count, and column counts.  The two-axis closure assay returned a third-axis-present conclusion because $d_\perp$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed organism directions.  Its identity assay aligned $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism assay retained a selection-not-GC conclusion after GC orthogonalization, but it also recorded that the CSC or positional identity still needs an external row.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The fourth-axis tests narrow what the present record is allowed to say.  The three-axis sufficiency assay returned fourth-mechanism-present on $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous columns.  However, the codon-pair assay concluded not-codon-pair after $50$ shuffle-null replicates, the translation-accuracy assay concluded not-accuracy after $200$ permutations, and the mRNA-stability assay concluded that the dominant fourth-axis identity was not mRNA stability because the yeast half-life direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates.  The elongation-dwell assay in Saccharomyces cerevisiae was only partial.  Consequently, the present conjecture does not identify the fourth mechanism.  It only states that an $f_3$ residual may be tested against translation readouts when a wobble or tRNA asymmetry creates a finite contrast.

\paragraph{Local falsifier.}
For every organism and every translation row $T_j$, compute the controlled survival entry for the $f_3$-stress coordinate after the baseline controls have been applied.  The conjecture is supported only if at least one nondegenerate row satisfies $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0$ with the agreed partial-$R^2$ threshold and after simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  It is broken if all entries remain non-positive or rank-degenerate across the powered panel.  This falsifier is deliberately local: negative survival constrains the translation interface of the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate, but it does not prove that the coordinate lacks sequence-level or mathematical structure.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
The only permitted biological reading is a controlled translation-row survival claim.  Codon-usage tables, tRNA gene-copy counts, and tAI or stAI weights may define bounded contacts for sequence composition, tRNA supply, and modelled codon adaptation.  Ribosome profiling can supply a direct translation-efficiency row only when paired with matched mRNA abundance so that transcriptional signal is separated from translation signal.  No statement about mature protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, biochemical function, mechanism identity, phenotype, or universality follows from this record unless a separate finite contact at that layer is supplied and the corresponding controlled row survives.
