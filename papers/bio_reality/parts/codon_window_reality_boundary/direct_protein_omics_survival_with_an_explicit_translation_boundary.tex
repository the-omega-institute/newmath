\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record tests whether the synonymous residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ retain protein-omics information after the amino-acid quotient has already removed ordinary protein-sequence composition.  The finite rows are coding-sequence entries matched to organism-specific protein abundance measurements, with nine residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  Each organism uses the same baseline design: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition coordinates, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  In every organism listed below, the residualized design has $9$ retained $q$-coordinates, $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, and residual coordinate rank $9$ after projection away from the controls.

\paragraph{Boundary imposed by translation data.}
The direct protein-omics calculation does not by itself identify translation as the mediator.  The only attached translation-side finite calculation here is a modeled-tAI contact over three organisms, using GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and dos Reis wobble weights.  It computes all $61$ sense-codon weights for Escherichia coli, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each organism.  The local tRNA payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families in all three organisms; the FASTA header accounting is $89$ headers with $87$ matched in Escherichia coli, $432$ with $432$ matched in Homo sapiens, and $275$ with $275$ matched in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The modeled bounded-coupling matrix has $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$ and the same value on the maximum absolute diagonal entry.  Nevertheless, the organism count is only $3$, so this contact is an underpowered modeled translation annotation, not a measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency validation.  A positive $S^{QP}$ entry is therefore read only as DNA-to-protein-abundance predictive irreducibility after CDS-derived controls, unless a matched measured translation readout $T_j$ is attached and a joint fit shows that the path $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ accounts for the direct signal.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports a chapter-level claim of direct protein-omics survival for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual codon geometry after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls.  It does not support the stronger statement that the surviving signal is a translation-rate mechanism, a folding effect, a physical-admissibility constraint, a biochemical-function statement, a causal perturbation result, or a universal biological law.  Those readings require additional layer-matched contacts.  In particular, the translation boundary remains active: without joint measured translation readouts, mRNA abundance controls, and protein abundance in the same matched finite rows, the proper status of the mediation question is open even when the direct $S^{QP}$ matrix is positive.
