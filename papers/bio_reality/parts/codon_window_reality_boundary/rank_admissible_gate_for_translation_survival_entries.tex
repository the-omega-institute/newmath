\subsection{Rank-admissible gate for $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival entries}
\label{sec:q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats a proposed translation-survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ as admissible only after the synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, the translation readout $T_j$, and the control block $Z$ have all been placed in the same finite organism-indexed row table.  The carrier is a residualized design matrix whose $Q6$ side contains the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}$,
with controls for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, length, and M-density.  The gate does not ask whether an in-sample coupling can be fitted; it asks whether the post-control row space leaves enough independent residual variation to make held-out survival falsifiable.

\paragraph{Admissibility relation.}
For each candidate pair $(q_i,T_j)$, write $P_Z^\perp$ for residualization against the declared control columns and set
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= P_Z^\perp q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= P_Z^\perp T_j,\\
X_{ij} &= [\widetilde q_i,\widetilde T_j, P_Z^\perp Z].
\end{aligned}
$$
The entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is eligible for a survival score only when the residualized design is not rank-saturated and the remaining degrees of freedom are large enough to test the entry outside the fitted rows.  If $\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij})$ consumes the available row support, or if the held-out organism/readout split is too small to falsify a positive score, the entry is recorded as coupling-only or needs-data.  In particular, a fitted score equal to $1.0$ is not evidence of survival when it is produced by a saturated projection.

\paragraph{Observed coupling is below the survival boundary.}
The finite coupling audit for h3.codon\_usage\_topology.translation\_survival.b\_star\_q6\_coupling\_matrix loaded three organism rows, homo sapiens, saccharomyces cerevisiae, and escherichia coli k12, over a 61-codon count space.  Against the proxy readout ``tRNA-Leu CUN-vs-UUR log-copy spectrum,'' all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates had nonzero coupling entries above the stated threshold $0.01$, and the maximum absolute fitted coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.  The predeclared break condition for that coupling contact was null only if all nine $|A_{ij}|$ values were below $0.01$; that condition was not met.  This proves only that the proxy contact is not identically silent.  It does not establish $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$, because the available readout is a codon-count and tRNA-copy proxy rather than a matched translation readout with rank-audited held-out support.

\paragraph{Residual basis evidence.}
The separate residual-basis computation h3.cross\_layer\_relation.residual\_basis\_extraction.b\_star\_q6\_non\_translation\_residual\_powered gives a finite cross-organism picture of unresolved $Q6$ residual structure.  Four organisms were computed and two were marked needs-data.  The computed cases were danio rerio with $n=14410$, one candidate, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.0$, and final $\rho=0.9791828971490305$; escherichia coli k12 mg1655 with $n=3415$, two candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.00017488612263594217$, and final $\rho=0.9979478314995476$; homo sapiens with $n=16008$, six candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191321$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.018824203448813415$, and final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$; and saccharomyces cerevisiae with $n=3046$, nine candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$, and final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$.  Gallus gallus and mycobacterium smegmatis str mc2 155 were not computed and remain needs-data.  The regularizer used in the extraction was $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  The reported negative summary is that all four computed organisms are persistent, with no compressible or partially compressible case and $\min \rho_{\mathrm{final}}=0.9723341658092314$ at a persistence floor of $0.97$.

\paragraph{Held-out null comparisons.}
The residual-basis table also records degree-of-freedom matched deterministic Gaussian null checks for selected readout blocks.  Danio rerio exceeded its null at step 1 with measured\_trna\_tai, increment $0.02081710285096942$ against null p95 $0.0002306374780841125$.  Escherichia coli k12 mg1655 exceeded at step 1 for mrna, increment $0.0017706954554909888$ against $0.0013371669456573104$, but not at step 2 for measured\_te, increment $0.0002819723320884449$ against $0.0010272364744326006$.  Homo sapiens exceeded at steps 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6, with selected blocks ptm\_density, complex\_member, tm\_count, measured\_trna\_tai, and localization; the domain\_count step 5 did not exceed its null, with increment $0.00003840392718095609$ below null p95 $0.00021932006910265595$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae exceeded at steps 1, 2, 3, 4, and 9, while complex\_member, measured\_trna\_tai, domain\_count, and mrna at steps 5 through 8 did not exceed their nulls.  These finite checks show that some readout blocks carry residual structure, but they are not per-entry rank certificates for $S^{QT}_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Closure rule.}
The gate therefore closes as a refusal principle rather than as a positive survival theorem.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ may be reported only when residualized rank, remaining degrees of freedom, and held-out translation readout support are all present.  The current finite rows support a controlled distinction between proxy coupling and admissible survival: $A^{QT}_{ij}$ contact can be nonzero while $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is withheld.  No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from these rows unless a separate matched contact at that layer supplies the missing measurement and passes the same rank audit.
