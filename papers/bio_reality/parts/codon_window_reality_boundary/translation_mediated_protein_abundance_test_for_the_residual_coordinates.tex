\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance test for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
The test object is the finite surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The computed residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation readout used in the powered cross-layer computation is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted mean tAI using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $q$ vectors.  These controls are not promoted to residual coordinates; they remain the baseline against which the $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$ relation is measured.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-contact calibration.}
A bounded tAI-contact computation was first run on three organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local GtRNAdb payload contained all twenty standard amino-acid families in each case, with FASTA header counts $89$, $432$, and $275$ respectively; the matched header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$.  The modeled tAI codon weights covered all $61$ sense codons for all three organisms, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each organism.  The bounded coupling audit found $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |S_{ij}| = 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
This three-organism run is retained only as a modeled translation-readout contact.  It is not powered survival: organism count $3$ is below the declared $40$-matched-species powered-survival requirement.

\paragraph{Cross-layer row set.}
The powered mediation run joined real CDS codon counts, organism-matched tRNA-copy information, modeled per-protein tAI, and measured protein abundance for twelve organisms.  The finite row counts were: \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $4042$; \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $11661$; \emph{Danio rerio}, $14410$; \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $13295$; \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $3739$; \emph{Gallus gallus}, $12418$; \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19053$; \emph{Mus musculus}, $19395$; \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $4129$; \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $13322$; \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$; and \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, $1656$.  The total joined CDS/protein row count across these organism-scoped computations is $122935$.  In every organism the residualized $Q$ block had rank $9$, the residualized translation block had rank $1$, the joint $Q,T$ block had rank $10$, the control rank was $23$, and the rank sufficiency flag was true.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-side audit.}
A separate yeast five-way join supplies a boundary check rather than a replacement for the translation mediation claim.  It joined $4759$ rows, with reported join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The measured-TE source had $5128$ valid protein-keyed TE genes, the mRNA half-life contact had $5379$ valid systematic-ORF records, and the structure readout had $6027$ valid 5-prime CDS MFE records.  After the same $Z$-style residualization, the yeast structure partition gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542,\\
R^2(S_e\sim Q_e) &= 0.003774248455422586.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond measured TE plus mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, the right-tail permutation value for $R^2(S_e\sim Q_e)$ was $0.027972027972027972$, and the right-tail permutation value for the incremental structure term was $0.1258741258741259$.  This confirms that the protein-abundance association is not to be re-described as folding or structure realization: the MFE contact is a CDS-local proxy, single-organism, sequence-derived, and only a variance-partition companion.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The admissible readback is a controlled matrix $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$: $Q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates, $T_j$ is modeled tAI in the powered run or another explicitly attached translation readout in later data, and $P_k$ is matched protein abundance.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not counted as protein realization unless the translation step has been measured or modeled in the same finite row system and then used in the conditioned decomposition.  The result also does not establish translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, perturbation response, selection, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Each of those claims would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls.  Within the present data, the correct conclusion is narrower: the twelve-organism finite surface contains positive translation-conditioned statistical mediation fractions for the joint nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block, while the causal and higher-layer biological interpretations remain outside the measured scope.
