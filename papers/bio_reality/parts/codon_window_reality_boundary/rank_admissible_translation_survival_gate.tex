\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate}
\label{sec:q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Question.}
The object tested here is not a claim that synonymous codon geometry already realizes translation.  It is a gate placed before that promotion.  For a residual coordinate $q_i$ in the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous basis and a translation readout $T_j$, the proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is allowed to be called survival only when the controlled residual design still has usable row space after removing the baseline columns $Z$.  The controls include amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, and $M$-density.  Thus the gate is a finite-row admissibility test for a residualized design matrix, not a biological interpretation of the fitted coefficient.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The available finite carrier contains nine synonymous residual coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coupling computation used the $61$-codon count space and loaded three organism rows, Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12.  Against the proxy readout given by the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, all nine coordinates had nonzero entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with reported maximum absolute entry $1882464.1106377328$.  This only proves that the proxy coupling matrix was not identically null under that finite contact.  It does not prove a survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, because the readout is a tRNA-copy proxy rather than a held-out translation realization measurement.

\paragraph{Admissibility rule.}
Let $\widetilde q_i$ be $q_i$ after residualization against $Z$, and let $\widetilde T_j$ be the corresponding residualized translation readout.  The entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is admissible only if the post-control design has non-saturated rank and enough degrees of freedom for a held-out failure to be possible.  In schematic finite-row form the gate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Adm}(i,j)
  &= \bigl(\operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j]) > \operatorname{rank}(Z)\bigr)\\
  &\quad {}\wedge \bigl(n - \operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j]) > 0\bigr)\\
  &\quad {}\wedge \bigl(\text{held-out residual prediction can decrease under ablation}\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$
If this predicate fails, the entry is recorded as coupling-only or needing more rows, even when an in-sample score is positive or exactly $1.0$.  The all-$1.0$ pattern is especially dangerous in this setting: it can mean that the finite row space has been exhausted by the controls and fitted columns, so the apparent fit is no longer a falsifiable survival contact.

\paragraph{Residual evidence already present.}
The non-translation residual-basis computation supplies a second finite-row check on the size of the unresolved signal.  Four organisms were computed and all four were classified as persistent rather than compressible.  Homo sapiens used $16008$ joined rows, selected $6$ candidates, and had
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho &= (1.0,\ 0.9852198225375993,\ 0.9806696939851618,\ 0.9792537895870558,\\
&\qquad 0.9787609432955213,\ 0.9787233550315274,\ 0.9723341658092314),\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.9020707321191321,\qquad d_{\mathrm{difference}} = 0.018824203448813415.
\end{aligned}
$$
Saccharomyces cerevisiae used $3046$ joined rows, selected $9$ candidates, and had final residual level $0.9798667977250413$, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 used $3415$ joined rows, selected $2$ candidates, and ended at $0.9979478314995476$, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.00017488612263594217$.  Danio rerio used $14410$ joined rows, selected $1$ candidate, and moved from $1.0$ to $0.9791828971490305$, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.0$.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str MC2 155 were not computed and remain in the needs-data state.

\paragraph{Null comparison inside the residual run.}
The residual-basis run used the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  Its degree-of-freedom-matched deterministic Gaussian null shows why the present chapter must separate residual persistence from translation survival.  In Homo sapiens, measured tRNA-tAI appears at step $4$ with increment $0.0005032876020244136$ above null $p95=0.00020173994173736226$, but the largest recorded increments in that organism are attached to PTM density, complex membership, and localization rather than to a direct translation boundary.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, measured translation efficiency appears at step $1$ with increment $0.003750101033636422$ above null $p95=0.0011272729395622515$, while measured tRNA-tAI at step $6$ has increment $0.0008239697005207827$ below null $p95=0.0012089302784270603$.  In Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, measured translation efficiency at step $2$ has increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below null $p95=0.0010272364744326006$.  These finite rows support the existence of residual structure and mixed readout contacts, not an unconditional $S^{QT}_{ij}$ survival matrix.

\paragraph{Boundary before translation claims.}
The gate therefore has a strict local reading.  A positive proxy coupling $A^{QT}_{ij}$, a modeled tAI or stAI contact, or a perfect in-sample score is not yet a translation-survival certificate.  The certificate must name the residualized rank, the post-control degrees of freedom, the held-out translation readout, and the ablation under which the entry can fail.  Without those items the entry remains at the level of codon-topology contact or unresolved residual structure.  No ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway role, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this gate unless a separate finite contact supplies that layer.

\paragraph{Related finite contacts.}
Other verified rows show that the paper already has finite biological readouts at adjacent layers, but none replaces the rank gate.  The uORF-Kozak ribosome-load comparison reports $41862$ fixed rows and $16739$ varying rows with two-sample concordance.  The branchpoint motif usage run reports $10584$ rows with expression controlled by a UCSC hg19 GTEx-gene summary joined by same-strand intron overlap.  The mRNA-stability run reports $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, and a non-CSC tAI circularity guard.  These are useful controls for how finite readouts can be kept layer-specific: they demonstrate that a contact can be real while still not licensing claims outside its measured surface.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
For the present finite packet, the admissible conclusion is a refusal rule.  The nine-coordinate proxy coupling matrix is nonzero, and the larger residual-basis run shows persistent unresolved structure across four computed organisms with minimum final residual level $0.9723341658092314$.  However, the supplied facts do not list the residualized rank, post-control degrees of freedom, held-out translation-survival score, or partial-$R^2$ ablation for each $(q_i,T_j)$ entry.  Therefore the chapter records the gate as ready for use and classifies the current survival matrix as not yet certified: every proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ must pass the rank-admissibility test before it may be read as translation realization.
