\subsection{Translation-Gated Structural-Order Readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{subsec:q6-translation-gated-structural-order-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object treated here is a finite CDS-indexed table whose rows are encoded proteins and whose columns are residual codon-usage coordinates, translation-layer readouts, baseline controls, and, only when attached, a structural-order readback. The residual coordinate side is fixed by nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each row therefore records whether a coordinate $q_i\in Q$ remains informative after residualization against the control surface $Z$, and whether any later structural-order readout has been tested after the translation and abundance baselines have already been respected. The table is not a direct observation of folding, function, mechanism, or phenotype. It is a controlled finite association surface for asking whether a codon-residual coordinate survives the required gate before any structural-language claim is even admissible.

\paragraph{Control surface and rank adequacy.}
The supplied run used $Z$ consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts for $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$ from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The positive-entry threshold was $10^{-12}$ in partial-$R^2$ units, so a reported survival is not merely a sign convention; it is a finite residual improvement above the declared numerical boundary. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the joined codon table had $3739$ CDS rows, $3736$ matched proteins were used, the structural-order join hit rate was $0.999198$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $3713$. The same diagnostic surface in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} had $5815$ joined codon rows, $5804$ matched proteins, hit rate $0.998108$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and $5781$ residual degrees of freedom. Thus, for both organisms, the nine-coordinate residual block remained powered after the amino-acid, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls rather than collapsing into the baseline.

\paragraph{Readout actually tested.}
The available structural-order readback is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structural-confidence score. It is not experimental folding order, not thermodynamic admissibility, not pathway activity, not localization, not abundance, and not function. With that limitation fixed locally, the controlled cross-sectional association was finite in both organisms. For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the structural-confidence residual association was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
\#\{q_i\ \mathrm{surviving}\} &= 9,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $3736$ matched structural-confidence rows. For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the corresponding values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.0715885627979685,\\
\#\{q_i\ \mathrm{surviving}\} &= 9,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $5804$ matched rows. The larger yeast value is still only a controlled association against predicted confidence; it is not a promotion to a general biological rule. The finite observation is that, after the declared controls, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block retains nonzero explanatory content for this particular prediction-derived readout in these two organism-specific tables.

\paragraph{Gate logic.}
The admissible chain is $q_i\rightarrow T_j\rightarrow SO$, where $q_i$ is a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, $T_j$ is an attached translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy-derived translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance, and $SO$ is a separately supplied structural-order readback. A coordinate that does not first survive a translation-layer test under amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density controls cannot be used as an entry point for structural-order analysis. Conversely, a positive structural-confidence association after controls does not retroactively prove translation mediation unless the translation layer itself has been observed and conditioned in the same row system. The present finite run supplies codon residuals, controls, matched proteins, and AlphaFold mean pLDDT; it does not by itself supply a causal translation mechanism or an experimental folding measurement.

\paragraph{Local null boundary.}
The null boundary is part of the claim. Codon counts, $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry, $M$-density, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome profiling, protein abundance, and AlphaFold confidence each occupy distinct observational roles. None may be silently substituted for another. In particular, a modeled adaptation weight is not ribosome dynamics, ribosome profiling is not mature protein structure, steady-state protein abundance is not folding physics, and predicted confidence is not function. No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this table unless a separate contact at that layer supplies the corresponding observation. The present result is therefore narrow: in two organism-specific finite tables, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were rank-sufficient after $Z$ and showed finite association with a prediction-confidence readout, while every stronger biological interpretation remains outside the licensed readback.
