\subsection{Chapter verdict}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-verdict}

\paragraph{Conjectural status.}
The chapter is ready as a self-contained BioReality conjecture because the row carrier, readout field, asymmetry condition, survival statistic, and failure modes are all finite and explicit.  It is not ready as a demonstrated biological survival result.  The decisive missing object is the joined same-organism, same-CDS, same-window table containing $f_3$ residuals, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, position-resolved ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, and all declared controls.  Once that table exists, the chapter has a direct pass-fail rule: the $f_3$ member survives exactly when the controlled held-out entry $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ is strictly positive after the baseline and rank checks.
