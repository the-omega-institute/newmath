\subsection{mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-translation-efficiency-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
The record tests whether the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis leaves a measurable trace at the ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency layer after sequence-level and transcript-level separation.  The coordinate list is fixed as $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation-efficiency value is the matched ribosome-footprint read density divided by the matched mRNA read density in the same study condition, so the contact is not a raw transcript-abundance association.  The nuisance block contains an intercept, $\log$ CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an M-density baseline defined on the union support of the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  For each organism the reported control matrix has 24 columns and rank 23.

\paragraph{Observed organism rows.}
The matched translation-efficiency join contains four organism rows.  The joined row counts are $n=13140$ for $Danio\ rerio$, $n=3415$ for $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for $Homo\ sapiens$, and $n=5128$ for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$.  The corresponding CDS joins before the measured-TE restriction are 14410, 3739, 19053, and 5815, respectively.  The measured-TE availability therefore changes the readout surface in two large eukaryotic rows: the self-check against the full-abundance CDS complement differs by 0.03219747984141397 in $Danio\ rerio$ and by 0.06427590857052146 in $Homo\ sapiens$, both exceeding the 0.02 stability tolerance.  The same check is stable for $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, with absolute difference 0.00017488612263594217, and for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, with absolute difference 0.012133464712697745.

\paragraph{Translation-efficiency absorption numbers.}
Let $P\_Q$ denote the residual projection of the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block after the sequence-level nuisance controls, and let $P\_{Q\perp Tmod}$ denote the component left after the modeled-tAI direction is removed on the same organism-specific join.  On the measured-TE readout surface the squared-norm ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d\_{modeled}(Danio\ rerio) &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
 d\_{modeled}(Escherichia\ coli\ K\text{-}12) &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
 d\_{modeled}(Homo\ sapiens) &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
 d\_{modeled}(Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae) &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The descriptive absorption by measured translation efficiency is small in every row: 0.01759429001506974 for $Danio\ rerio$, 0.0010982956489726373 for $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, 0.0028947149257963897 for $Homo\ sapiens$, and 0.0002762783754375593 for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$.  The combined residual ratios after adding the measured-TE direction are 0.8276090159036916, 0.6620871850159149, 0.854133502300174, and 0.2237928123367028.  Thus the finite contact does not collapse the residual basis into measured translation efficiency; it records a weak, organism-bounded descriptive contact rather than a translation-layer explanation.

\paragraph{Controls against over-reading the contact.}
The matched mRNA component enters through the TE construction, and the reported contact is explicitly a statistical projection on a single measured condition, not a causal mediator test.  A positive absorption number does not license a claim about protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Conversely, the low measured-TE absorption values do not prove that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ basis is biologically irrelevant; they only say that this particular ribosome-profiling interface explains little of the modeled-tAI-complement residual component on the available joins.  Any claim at the level of folding, mature protein amount, degradation, localization, complex membership, or organism-wide law requires its own matched contact at that layer.

\paragraph{Neighboring checks.}
The measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI contact leaves the same full CDS joins intact for $Danio\ rerio$, $Homo\ sapiens$, and $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$: 14410, 19053, and 5815 rows.  Its absorption values are 0.020817102845637092, 0.00005426229300865148, and 0.001530747712165342, again small and descriptive.  In yeast, the turnover contact joins 3640 proteins, reports a CDS hit rate of 0.9156038419146592 and a turnover join hit rate of 0.8818572656921754, and absorbs only 0.0009086031889966327 of the modeled-tAI-complement component.  Protein-feature contacts show sparse and join-sensitive behavior: the $Danio\ rerio$ feature join has only 2242 rows and shifts the modeled-complement ratio by 0.1384608720516286 relative to the full-abundance CDS reference, while $Homo\ sapiens$ and yeast are stable by the 0.02 check.  Among feature candidates, Bonferroni excess appears for $Danio\ rerio$ PTM density, for $Homo\ sapiens$ complex membership, PTM density, and transmembrane count, and for none of the yeast feature candidates.  These neighboring contacts are useful guards: they show that the translation-efficiency readout is one bounded surface in a broader cross-layer map, not a mechanism by itself.

\paragraph{Cross-organism caution.}
The organism-specific meta table has 16 assembled rows but only 12 rows with the complete feature set used in the domain, GC3, tRNA-pool-size, and proteome-size comparisons.  The dominant-coordinate contingency has $n=12$, Pearson chi-square 9.399999999999999, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and right-tail permutation value 0.5814185814185814.  The $M\_{QTP}$ fraction tests report $r=-0.3062616500469447$ against GC3 with two-sided permutation value 0.35264735264735264, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ against log proteome size with value 0.913086913086913, and $r=0.06029405206037823$ against log tRNA-gene count with value 0.8621378621378621.  The domain variance ratio is 0.8889980452277374 with right-tail permutation value 0.4155844155844156.  A localization boundary check also exists for a human mitochondrial-propensity surface, with 8696 joined proteins, 657 mitochondrial entries, and 2000 permutations.  These numbers support only bounded descriptive heterogeneity.  They do not establish a phylogenetically corrected rule, and they do not turn the small measured-TE absorption values into a universal biological principle.
