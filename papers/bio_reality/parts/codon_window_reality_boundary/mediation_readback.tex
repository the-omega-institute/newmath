\subsection{Mediation readback}
\label{subsec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-readback}

\paragraph{Joint mediated fraction.}
For each organism the run reports the total controlled association $c=R^2(Q,P)$, the remaining direct association $c'=R^2(Q,P\mid T)$, and the mediated residual increment $c-c'$.  The finite mediation scalar is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= {c-c' \over c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve organism values lie in the unit interval.  The largest value is yeast, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, indirect increment $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The bacterial and animal rows also remain positive: \emph{E. coli} has $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ with $c=0.10767526677985896$ and $c'=0.04167869969498872$; \emph{Drosophila} has $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$ with $c=0.0770952887136557$ and $c'=0.02956474995793468$; \emph{C. elegans} has $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$ with $c=0.10217906758013379$ and $c'=0.05239468380596188$; human has $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ with $c=0.04454565118019448$ and $c'=0.03242643251185274$; mouse has $M^{QTP}=0.15488799907323988$ with $c=0.049827984062539195$ and $c'=0.04211022731323921$.  The smallest positive value is \emph{Sulfolobus}, with $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$, $c=0.06397514251295629$, $c'=0.06279117466131778$, and mediated increment $0.0011839678516385038$.

\paragraph{Translation slot strength.}
The controlled translation readout is finite but uneven across organisms.  Yeast has the strongest $Q \to T$ energy, with $X$ explaining $5.671703404506426$ units on $T$ and $T$ explaining $941.8858618613751$ units on protein abundance.  \emph{C. elegans} has $X$ explaining $2.3459016179925603$ units on $T$ and $T$ explaining $1088.8137823273846$ units on abundance.  Human has $X$ explaining $0.3099459586758378$ units on $T$, with $T$ explaining $850.7864327764183$ units on abundance.  The finite-row conclusion is therefore not a universal sign law for any individual synonymous axis; it is a controlled, organism-indexed decomposition in which the modeled tAI coordinate absorbs a positive fraction of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance association in every reported row.
