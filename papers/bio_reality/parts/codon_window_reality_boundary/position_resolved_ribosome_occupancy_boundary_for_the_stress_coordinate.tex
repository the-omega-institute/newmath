\subsection{Position-Resolved Ribosome Occupancy Boundary for the $f_3$-Stress Coordinate}\label{subsec:q6-f3-stress-position-resolved-ribosome-occupancy-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate.}
The object in this record is a finite CDS-window table whose rows are keyed by organism, coding sequence, codon index, codon position modulo $3$, local $f_3$-paired codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight surface, a matched mRNA control, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readout. The coordinate is not a raw codon-count imbalance. It is the amino-acid-quotient surviving residual obtained after synonymous codons that differ only inside the same amino-acid fiber have been compared under organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, and position or amino-acid-bin controls. The finite codon-side input fixes the relevant $f_3$ saturation list as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ambient $M$ list is larger by the two ACA-family entries retained in $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$, namely $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, so the recorded $M$ list has $20$ codons whereas the displayed $f_3$ saturation list has $18$ codons. The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. Thus the codon-side statistic has a sharply finite input surface, but the finite codon surface alone is not a translation measurement.

\paragraph{Quotient geometry.}
The amino-acid projection is part of the test, not a post hoc explanation. The verified quotient table records $\pi_M$ with $10$ rows and quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, while the corresponding $M$ complex has $f$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. This is the numerical reason that a window statistic must be computed after quotienting away amino-acid composition: any survival entry is only admissible if it is still visible after the synonymous-residual coordinate has passed through the amino-acid quotient and the standard covariates. In row notation, the target readout has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress}}(\mathrm{RPF}_{\mathrm{window}}\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, and position or amino-acid-bin controls. The sign of this entry is interpreted only after a rank check, simpler-baseline ablation, and a null comparison. A rank-degenerate design, a non-positive controlled increment, or a window table without same-organism and same-CDS joins returns a data insufficiency or null state rather than a biological promotion.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The required empirical contact is stricter than an organism-level codon-usage table or a modeled tAI/stAI surface. It must contain codon-indexed or fixed-window ribosome-footprint occupancy, identifier-matched mRNA abundance from the same condition, and ordered CDS coordinates. The contact is vulnerable to ribosome pausing, nuclease and fragment-length bias, frame-assignment ambiguity, mappability, edge effects near start and stop codons, library normalization, condition specificity, and CDS annotation mismatch. For that reason, the present record authorizes only a bounded window-level test. It does not convert the $f_3$ coordinate, the quotient table, or modeled wobble weights into observed ribosome dynamics. Protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, a translation mechanism, and universality do not follow from this row unless a separate matched contact at that layer supplies the relevant measurement.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite evidence.}
The closest powered cross-layer computation in the supplied finite data is not this ribosome-occupancy table. It is a structural-order ramp analysis with $5$ folds, minimum joined size $300$, and primary ramp length $50$. In that run the ramp-side weighted $f_3$ increment is positive, $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}}=0.0011165640103326878$, with sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, while the body-side increment is negative, $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}}=-0.00026157313923957926$, with sign-flip $p=0.54296875$. The paired ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$ with sign-flip $p=0.05859375$, so the finite verdict is ramp-positive but not localized. This adjacent result is useful as a warning: a positive $f_3$-linked signal can exist without licensing a stronger localization or mechanism claim. A separate APA contact table records $24{,}740$ genes and $296{,}950$ PAS rows, but that row count belongs to a different cross-layer surface and supplies no ribosome-occupancy statistic for the present claim.

\paragraph{Operational verdict.}
The conjecture therefore remains a translation-readout boundary. The finite codon geometry is explicit, the quotient residual is numerically constrained, and the admissible statistic is well specified, but the decisive table has not yet been supplied: there is no same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix joining $f_3$-stress residuals, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome occupancy under the declared controls. Once such a table exists, support requires a strictly positive controlled survival entry after rank sufficiency and simpler-baseline ablation. Failure of that condition leaves $f_3$-stress as a codon-topology or code-read residual rather than evidence for downstream translation behavior.
