\subsection{Rank-admissible translation survival gate for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Object under test.}
This record concerns a finite panel of synonymous residual coordinates $B^*_{Q6}$ and asks a narrower question than ordinary codon-usage association: when may an entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ be treated as a translation-survival contact rather than as an in-sample coupling or a rank artifact?  The carrier is a residualized design matrix whose rows are organism-indexed observations and whose columns include the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}$,
translation-layer readouts such as tRNA-copy, tAI or stAI weights, measured translation efficiency, or matched abundance, and control columns $Z$ for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, length, and $M$-density.  The rank gate is placed before any biological interpretation of protein output: the only admissible question is whether a residual coordinate still has falsifiable held-out contact with a translation readout after the controls have already absorbed the obvious sequence and organism effects.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For a proposed coordinate--readout pair $(q_i,T_j)$, write $q_i^\perp$ and $T_j^\perp$ for the residuals after projection away from $Z$.  The entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is admissible only when the post-control design has enough independent support to make failure observable.  In local notation the gate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z,q_i,T_j) &> \operatorname{rank}(Z),\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z,q_i,T_j) &< n_{ij},\\
\mathrm{df}_{ij} &= n_{ij}-\operatorname{rank}(Z,q_i,T_j)>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
If these inequalities fail, a fitted score, including a perfect in-sample fit, remains a projection event.  The entry is then recorded as coupling-only or as needing additional data.  This is the central refusal of the gate: a saturated row space cannot certify translation survival because there is no remaining degree of freedom on which the proposed survival contact could have failed.

\paragraph{Finite coupling evidence.}
The finite coupling computation loaded three organisms: $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  The codon-count ambient space had $61$ codon coordinates, and all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates produced nonzero coupling entries against the proxy readout.  The stated coupling threshold was $0.01$ and the maximum absolute coupling magnitude was $1882464.1106377328$, so the proxy coupling break condition, namely that every $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, was not met.  This supports the existence of a finite codon-topology contact, but it does not by itself support $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.  The readout used in that computation was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, explicitly described as a proxy rather than a true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, or protein-abundance measurement.

\paragraph{Residual readout panel.}
A separate finite computation evaluated residual-basis extraction on organisms with joined local readout data.  Four organisms were computed and two were marked as needing data.  The computed row counts were $16008$ joined rows for $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $3046$ for $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, $3415$ for $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}$, and $14410$ for $\mathrm{danio\_rerio}$; $\mathrm{gallus\_gallus}$ and $\mathrm{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}$ were not computed and remain needs-data cases.  The regularizer was $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  The residual curves remained high: for human the recorded $\rho$ sequence was $(1.0,0.9852198225375993,0.9806696939851618,0.9792537895870558,0.9787609432955213,0.9787233550315274,0.9723341658092314)$; for yeast it was $(1.0,0.9962498989663637,0.9929843170647626,0.9915730985485388,0.9903314906684438,0.9894054695002641,0.9885902293718664,0.9882386501212531,0.9881524582851271,0.9798667977250413)$; for $E.$ coli it was $(1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476)$; and for zebrafish it was $(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$.  With a residual floor of $0.97$, all four computed organisms were classified as persistent, with minimum final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$.  Thus the finite rows show remaining unexplained residual structure, not a completed translation-survival proof.

\paragraph{Dof-matched increments.}
The dof-matched null comparison gives useful local checks but still does not replace the rank-admissibility gate for each $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry.  In human, selected increments above their null thresholds included PTM density $0.014780177462400674$ versus $0.0002845846156909306$, complex membership $0.004618389163869967$ versus $0.000206488525646134$, transmembrane count $0.0014438137599134737$ versus $0.0002435268444123588$, measured tRNA/tAI $0.0005032876020244136$ versus $0.00020173994173736226$, and localization $0.006528084968494762$ versus $0.0011598998794309233$; the human domain-count step did not exceed its null threshold, with $0.00003840392718095609$ versus $0.00021932006910265595$.  In yeast, measured translation efficiency, PTM density, transmembrane count, turnover, and localization exceeded their thresholds, while complex membership, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, and mRNA did not.  In $E.$ coli, mRNA exceeded its null threshold with $0.0017706954554909888$ versus $0.0013371669456573104$, while measured translation efficiency did not, with $0.0002819723320884449$ versus $0.0010272364744326006$.  In zebrafish, measured tRNA/tAI exceeded its threshold with $0.02081710285096942$ versus $0.0002306374780841125$.  These numbers show that some readout blocks carry finite residual information, but they are block-level descriptive contacts rather than per-entry rank-certified survival claims.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The available finite rows justify retaining the conjecture as an explicit admissibility rule.  They do not justify promoting any particular $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry to translation survival.  The correct status is therefore needs-data: the coupling matrix has nonzero proxy contact, and the residual panel has large row counts for four organisms, but the packet has not supplied the residualized rank table, per-entry degrees of freedom after $Z$, held-out validation scores, or a matched translation-readout survival matrix.  Until those quantities are present, an entry with positive in-sample fit, or even an all-$1.0$ fitted score, is not a survival result.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This gate speaks only about rank-admissible contact between $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and bounded translation-layer readouts.  It does not establish ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal law.  Those claims require separate matched contacts at their own observational layer.  Codon-usage tables, tRNA-copy annotations, and tAI or stAI weights may be used here only as organism-scoped readouts with known modelling and sampling limitations; they are not direct observations of the downstream biological layers.
