\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Protein-Abundance Mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether synonymous residual coordinates in $B^*_{Q6}$ can enter a protein-abundance readout only through a translation-layer mediator.  The finite surface consists of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$; $t_j$ is a translation readout; and $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  In the powered protein-abundance run the translation readout was per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights, while the abundance readout was $\log_{10}$ PAXdb ppm joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control block contained an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The residual coordinates are not interpreted as protein measurements by themselves: a direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is admissible only as part of the conditioned comparison against $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$.

\paragraph{Model equation.}
For each organism, let $X$ be the $n\times 9$ matrix of $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T$ the modeled tAI column, $Y$ the protein-abundance vector, and $Z$ the control matrix.  All reported quantities are computed after residualizing against $Z$.  The mediated fraction is the finite scalar
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Y\sim X\mid Z)-R^2(Y\sim X\mid Z,T)}{R^2(Y\sim X\mid Z)},\\
0 &\leq M^{QTP}\leq 1
\end{aligned}
$$
when the computed rank diagnostics certify that the comparison is nondegenerate.  The run reports $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$ for every organism, with $23$ effective control columns and a degeneracy threshold of $0.999999$.  Thus the comparison is a finite matrix comparison, not an unrestricted regression narrative.

\paragraph{Powered organism panel.}
The joined protein panels contain $4042$ rows for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  All twelve panels passed the rank-sufficiency check.  The corresponding mediated fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{B.subtilis} &= 0.6084594553, & M^{QTP}_{C.elegans} &= 0.4872268357,\\
M^{QTP}_{D.rerio} &= 0.3157053954, & M^{QTP}_{D.melanogaster} &= 0.6165167749,\\
M^{QTP}_{E.coli} &= 0.6129222528, & M^{QTP}_{G.gallus} &= 0.1976903965,\\
M^{QTP}_{H.sapiens} &= 0.2720628916, & M^{QTP}_{M.musculus} &= 0.1548879991,\\
M^{QTP}_{M.smegmatis} &= 0.0839760292, & M^{QTP}_{R.norvegicus} &= 0.1748968180,\\
M^{QTP}_{S.cerevisiae} &= 0.9491551041, & M^{QTP}_{S.solfataricus} &= 0.0185066856.
\end{aligned}
$$
The total controlled $Q\to P$ values ranged from $0.0276399860$ in rat to $0.2983762499$ in yeast.  Yeast had $c=0.2983762499$, $c'=0.0151709094$, and indirect component $0.2832053406$; \emph{E. coli} had $c=0.1076752668$, $c'=0.0416786997$, and indirect component $0.0659965671$; \emph{C. elegans} had $c=0.1021790676$, $c'=0.0523946838$, and indirect component $0.0497843838$.  The smallest mediated component was observed in \emph{S. solfataricus}, where $c=0.0639751425$, $c'=0.0627911747$, and the indirect component was $0.0011839679$.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival calculation used three organisms and computed the same nine residual coordinates in a $61$-sense-codon space.  The tRNA payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast, with matched FASTA header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively.  The bounded coupling matrix had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold and maximum absolute value $3801961.031607896$, but the run explicitly remained underpowered for a strong multi-species translation-survival claim because only $3$ organisms were present where at least $40$ matched species would be required for that stronger status.  This matters for interpretation: the protein-abundance mediation run is a controlled cross-sectional decomposition using modeled tAI, not an observed ribosome-profiling validation.

\paragraph{Yeast mediation comparison.}
A yeast five-way join gives an internal check on how much of the $B^*_{Q6}$ to abundance association can be moved by additional biological covariates.  The join contained $4759$ genes, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419$ and global join hit rate $0.8818572657$.  In that panel $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.0032845645$, while the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507779$.  The $S_Q$ structure association had $R^2=0.0037742485$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.0279720280$, but the incremental structure mediation right-tail permutation was $p=0.1258741259$.  These numbers support a bounded variance partition, not a claim that folding, initiation, or abundance causation has been isolated.

\paragraph{Negative and adjacent checks.}
The mass-balance run used $120$ fixed-subsample genes and $718$ intervals.  It had $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628202$, $R^2_{L1}=0.9964106289$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443378$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.0758443809$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693$.  The turnover residual audit likewise stayed negative beyond expression, TE, pLDDT, N-end, and related controls: $R^2_{L2}=0.0557408516$, $R^2_{L3}=0.0502788722$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0054619794$, $\Delta DL=-13.6881970790$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$ over $3290$ genes.  The cross-ortholog imprint run over $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows also did not support the strong selection-imprint claim: $\Delta R^2=0.0325051502$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477730$, null $95\%$ maximum $0.0542715707$, and the positive-imprint gate was false.

\paragraph{Permitted conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports this chapter only at the level of a translation-conditioned protein-abundance association: in twelve organism panels, modeled tAI attenuation produced a positive mediated component in every panel, and all mediated fractions lay in the unit interval under the stated rank checks.  The strongest finite row is yeast, where the modeled-tAI-conditioned drop accounts for $0.2832053406$ of the $0.2983762499$ controlled $Q\to P$ association; several other organisms show smaller but positive decompositions.  The supported readback is therefore a controlled matrix statement about $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, modeled translation adaptation, and matched protein abundance.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological statement follows from these rows alone.  Modeled tAI is not ribosome occupancy, ribosome speed, or translation efficiency measured by sequencing.  PAXdb abundance is not a folding assay, a function assay, a physical-admissibility measurement, a perturbation rescue, or a universal mechanism.  A positive $M^{QTP}$ value does not establish causation, synonymous-edit realization, organism-wide selection, protein turnover, localization, post-translational state, or phenotype.  Translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality require their own matched contacts and their own finite statistics before they can be asserted.
