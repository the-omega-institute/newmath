\subsection{Translation-conditioned abundance mediation for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
The record is a finite cross-layer mediation table whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual axes
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate $t_j$ used in the powered protein-abundance mediation run is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as a codon-count-weighted mean from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate $p_k$ is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance ppm joined to real CDS codon counts.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct $Q\to P$ edge.  It is the controlled comparison between total residual-to-abundance association and the remaining direct association after conditioning on the modeled translation readout:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= R^2(P_Z\sim Q_Z),\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= R^2(P_Z\sim Q_Z\mid T_Z),\\
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T}}{R^2_{QP}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism-matched sequence controls through the joined row set, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The residual coordinate remains a sequence-derived predictor unless the translation row is present and the rank checks after $Z$ residualization are nondegenerate.

\paragraph{Powered joined rows.}
The powered mediation computation joined twelve organism-level CDS, tRNA, and protein-abundance surfaces.  The row counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} MC2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each organism had $q$-rank $9$ after residualization, $T$-rank $1$, joint $QT$-rank $10$, and $Z$-rank $23$ with $24$ control columns recorded; every organism passed the finite-rank sufficiency check.  The per-protein tAI construction used all-tRNA copy totals ranging from $47$ in \textit{S. solfataricus} to $8874$ in \textit{D. rerio}; zero raw wobble-weight entries were filled in only four cases, with counts $3$, $1$, $1$, and $1$ for \textit{B. subtilis}, \textit{E. coli}, \textit{M. smegmatis}, and \textit{S. solfataricus} respectively.

\paragraph{Mediation magnitudes.}
All twelve mediated fractions lay in the unit interval.  The strongest finite-row mediation was yeast, with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect increment $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Other high mediated fractions were \textit{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \textit{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, and \textit{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$.  Intermediate rows included \textit{C. elegans} at $0.4872268357227724$, \textit{D. rerio} at $0.3157053953614641$, and human at $0.27206289160119157$.  Smaller but still positive mediated fractions were chicken $0.1976903964724546$, rat $0.17489681798543982$, mouse $0.15488799907323988$, \textit{M. smegmatis} $0.08397602919861291$, and \textit{S. solfataricus} $0.01850668564589326$.

\paragraph{Translation-contact support and limits.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival computation confirmed that the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ axes were computable on the $61$-sense-codon space and that the finite modeled-tAI coupling matrix had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold, with maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$.  The same computation was explicitly underpowered for a population-wide translation-survival law because it used only three organisms, not the requested $\geq 40$ matched species.  Thus the chapter may report a modeled translation-conditioned abundance mediation table, but it may not report observed ribosome occupancy, a universal translation rule, or a mechanism.  The contact surface is strong enough to prevent a naked $Q\to P$ readback, and weak enough to keep all causal and molecular-process claims outside the present result.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite checks.}
The yeast five-way mediation record gives an internal comparison against an mRNA-structure proxy: $4759$ genes were joined, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  There $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$; the structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the two permutation right-tail values were $0.027972027972027972$ and $0.1258741258741259$.  These numbers support a narrow variance-partition interpretation rather than a folding claim.  A dynamic mass-balance check on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals was negative for the stronger closure, with $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  A turnover audit on $3290$ genes was also negative beyond expression, TE, pLDDT, and N-end controls, with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$.

\paragraph{Readback verdict.}
The finite evidence therefore supports a statistical statement: the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface has a measured association with matched protein abundance that is partly absorbed by modeled per-protein tAI after the declared sequence controls, and the mediation fraction is positive in each of the twelve organism rows.  It does not establish translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  The evolutionary-selection imprint run illustrates this boundary: across $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species, the positive selection-imprint gate was not met, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and a reported verdict of codon-bias expression correlation rather than mechanism.  Perturbation evidence is likewise layer-specific: the \textit{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-variant run had $1459$ variants, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and $p=0.004975124378109453$ under both matched and within controls, but that result is a separate perturbation surface, not a license to promote the present cross-sectional mediation table.  The confirmatory genetic-code table checks, with $40/40$ successes in both independent and boundary-probe tests and zero unexpected successes in $20$ negative controls and $20$ ablations, supply background codon-geometry discipline only; they do not add a protein-layer claim here.
