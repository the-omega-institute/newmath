\subsection{Local Null And Boundary Conditions}\label{sec:q6-ribosome-te-survival-boundary}

\paragraph{Null rule.}
The local null condition is explicit.  If every controlled $S^{QT}$ entry is non-positive, or if the residualized coordinate matrix is rank-degenerate after the $Z$ controls, then $B^*_{Q6}$ remains a codon-topology or code-read residual for this contact and is not promoted into measured translation-efficiency survival.  The same null handling applies to tiny panels, saturated designs, and all-$1.0$ fits, because those cases do not provide an identifiable residual coordinate contribution.  In the present finite rows the null is not triggered: each organism retained rank $9$ for the nine-coordinate block and reported nine surviving coordinates.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
The chapter asserts only a controlled association between synonymous residual coordinates and measured ribosome-profiling TE.  No claim of translation realization beyond the measured TE contact follows from the coordinate table alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, protein function, mechanism, phenotype, causal direction, or universal biological law follows unless a separate contact at that layer supplies the required observations.  The codon-usage contact supplies sequence composition, the ribosome-profiling contact supplies measured footprint-over-mRNA TE, and the matched mRNA contact separates the TE readout from transcription abundance.  Those contacts are sufficient for this finite measured-TE survival statement and insufficient for stronger biological conclusions.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the cross-layer contact in the following limited sense: the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive the controlled measured-TE test in the four powered organism panels, with organism-level $R^2_{QT,meas}$ values ranging from $0.009394266754822262$ to $0.13681931343730436$.  The result is therefore ready as a finite BioReality chapter about measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency under matched mRNA and sequence controls.  Its scope remains deliberately narrow: it is a measured association record for finite rows, not a mechanism, not a protein-level conclusion, and not a universal law.
