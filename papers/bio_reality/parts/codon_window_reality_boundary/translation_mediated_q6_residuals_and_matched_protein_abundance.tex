\subsection{Translation-mediated Q6 residuals and matched protein abundance}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates can pass through a translation readout before being compared with matched protein abundance. The finite surface consists of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ is one of nine residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance measurement. The nine coordinates used in the powered run were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The readout $T$ was the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The abundance readout $Y$ was $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.

\paragraph{Translation gate before protein readback.}
A direct residual-to-protein association is not admitted as protein realization by itself. The admissible comparison first asks whether the residual coordinates explain variation in the translation readout after controls, and only then asks whether conditioning on the translation readout changes the residual-to-protein association. In the earlier modeled-tAI bounded-contact check, the codon-count space had $61$ sense codons, three organisms were available, and all three organism-level modeled tAI tables had zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ for the sense-codon computation. That check found $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$. It was explicitly underpowered for population survival, because $3$ organisms are below the declared $40$-organism powered-survival threshold.

\paragraph{Matched rows and ranks.}
The cross-layer run joined CDS, modeled translation, tRNA-copy, and protein-abundance rows for ten organisms. The joined row counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. In every organism the residualized design retained rank $9$ for $X_Q$, rank $1$ for $T$, and rank $10$ for the joint $(X_Q,T)$ matrix after the control block. The control block had $24$ columns, with residual degrees of freedom $4019$, $11638$, $14387$, $13272$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $19372$, $4106$, and $5792$ in the same organism order. The rank sufficiency check therefore passed for the finite cross-layer decomposition in all ten organisms.

\paragraph{Controlled mediation scalar.}
For each organism, the reported scalar is a statistical decomposition of the residual-to-abundance association after controlling amino-acid composition, organism-level covariates, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance structure in the available finite rows. It is not a causal effect. The decomposition can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},\\
c &= R^{2}(Y \sim X_Q \mid Z),\\
c' &= R^{2}(Y \sim X_Q \mid Z,T),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ denotes the declared control block, $X_Q$ is the joint nine-dimensional residual matrix, $T$ is modeled per-protein tAI, and $Y$ is matched protein abundance. The mediated fractions were inside $[0,1]$ in every organism: $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ in \emph{C. elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ in \emph{D. rerio}, $0.6165167748739756$ in \emph{D. melanogaster}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.1976903964724546$ in \emph{G. gallus}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast.

\paragraph{Organism-level readback.}
The largest mediated fraction was observed in yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$. The smallest mediated fraction was observed in \emph{M. smegmatis}, where $c=0.03209009170649934$, $c'=0.029395293228368185$, and $c-c'=0.0026947984781311544$. Human and mouse showed positive but smaller mediated components: human had $c=0.04454565118019448$, $c'=0.03242643251185274$, and $c-c'=0.012119218668341744$, while mouse had $c=0.049827984062539195$, $c'=0.04211022731323921$, and $c-c'=0.007717756749299982$. In all ten cases the indirect component $c-c'$ was positive, so the finite rows support a bounded statistical statement: modeled tAI accounts for a nonzero portion of the joint $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual association with matched protein abundance under the specified controls.

\paragraph{Control-energy audit.}
The residualized control audit shows that the translation coordinate was not a free label. The $T$-explained energy on $Y$ ranged from $15.813547327443837$ in \emph{M. smegmatis} to $1088.8137823273846$ in \emph{C. elegans}. The incremental improvement of $T$ given $X_Q$ was positive in all ten organisms: $138.73766744025298$, $473.49104177158597$, $342.6284544657774$, $352.88511336078443$, $317.26302294928666$, $133.55048991934166$, $477.88368900318414$, $546.7260575899445$, $0.37452260494680445$, and $104.11162139652299$ in the organism order listed above. The direct improvement of $X_Q$ given $T$ also remained positive, from $44.87838061554362$ in yeast to $2055.2519066931027$ in mouse. Thus the rows do not collapse into a pure translation-only account or a pure residual-only account; they support a separated finite decomposition.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The result is a controlled cross-layer association through a modeled translation readout. It does not show observed ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, a perturbation mechanism, or a universal law. Modeled per-protein tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is not ribo-seq. Conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional CDS and abundance rows is not a demonstrated translation mechanism. Protein abundance remains entangled with mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, and pathway context unless separate layer-matched contacts measure those quantities. The admissible conclusion is therefore narrow: in the supplied finite rows, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual matrix has a positive modeled-translation-mediated component for matched protein abundance in all ten tested organisms, while every stronger biological interpretation remains outside this chapter unless a separate contact supplies it.
