\subsection{mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary for B*\_Q6 residual coordinates}\label{sec:bior-q6-mrna-controlled-translation-efficiency-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.} The record attached to claim \texttt{q6.mrna-controlled.translation-efficiency-boundary.cross-layer} is a finite CDS-by-organism table.  Each row carries a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^*\_{Q6}$ construction after the amino-acid quotient, a matched transcript-abundance measurement for the same biological condition, and a translation-side readout such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.  The intended comparison is not between codon geometry and protein output directly.  It is a three-stage comparison: first remove amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and methionine-density effects; then attach the same-row mRNA abundance surface; then ask whether any remaining $q_i$ signal survives in translation efficiency.  In row notation, the admissible readout has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
TE_r &= RPF_r / RNA_r,\\
\widehat{TE}^{\,res}_r &= TE_r \mid (AA_r, organism_r, GC3_r, length_r, Mdensity_r, RNA_r),\\
S_{ij} &> 0 \quad \hbox{only if } q_{i,r} \hbox{ improves the controlled translation readout } r_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $RNA_r$ is not an optional covariate.  It is the surface that separates a transcription-layer association from a translation-layer survival signal.

\paragraph{Local decision rule.} A $Q_i \to mRNA$ association and a $Q_i \to TE$ residual are different finite-row facts.  If a coordinate is associated with transcript abundance but loses its signal after the mRNA control is imposed, the row supports only a transcription-level association for this packet.  Such a row must not be rewritten as evidence for realized translation.  The survival matrix for translation readouts may receive an entry only from the residual part that remains after the transcript control and the standard sequence controls are present in the same row.  Equivalently, the packet refuses the shortcut from codon-usage geometry, tAI, stAI, or organism-level codon tables to ribosome dynamics unless a ribosome or translation-efficiency contact has been attached and the matched mRNA control has failed to absorb the effect.

\paragraph{Concrete finite-row anchor.} The linked translational-control run \texttt{842b739606af} supplies a useful scalar warning about cross-layer interpretation.  It retained $4419$ genes, split $883$ genes into the top group and $883$ genes into the bottom group, and used $10000$ permutations.  The observed displacement was $D=0.23767000000000027$, with replicate displacements $D_{rep,a}=0.19967000000000013$ and $D_{rep,b}=0.24280000000000035$, and the replicate signs agreed.  The matched RNA-side displacement was $D_{RNA}=-0.0224999999999973$, while the nominal threshold scalar was $D_{thresh}=0.32192809488736235$.  The two-sided permutation value was $p_{two}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$, and the recorded runtime was $20.019$ seconds.  These numbers do not certify the $B^*\_{Q6}$ translation-efficiency boundary by themselves, because they belong to the linked ER-proximity and ISRIB translational-rescue contact.  They do, however, demonstrate the exact kind of finite-row accounting this chapter requires: a translation-side displacement is reported together with an RNA-side displacement rather than being conflated with it.

\paragraph{Boundary enforced by the claim.} The chapter therefore permits only the following local conclusion.  A $B^*\_{Q6}$ coordinate may be tested against translation efficiency in a CDS-matched table, and an entry may be admitted into the translation-readout survival matrix only when the same entry remains nonzero after mRNA abundance and the baseline sequence controls are included.  No translation process, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, localization, phenotype, protein abundance, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter alone.  A tAI or stAI weight is a bounded annotation, not an observed ribosome trace.  A ribosome-profiling count is a translation-side contact, not a folding or function contact.  A transcript count is a control needed to isolate translation efficiency, not evidence that translation has been realized.  Any claim at those later layers requires a separate matched contact at that layer.

\paragraph{Break condition.} The conjectural support fails for the translation-readout interface if every controlled entry in $B^*\_{Q6}\times r_j$ has nonpositive survival after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, methionine density, mRNA abundance, and simpler codon baselines have been applied.  In symbols, the translation part remains unsupported when
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} \leq 0 \quad \hbox{for all tested } (q_i,r_j).
\end{aligned}
$$
This failure would not show that $Q6$ coordinates are biologically irrelevant.  It would show only that the current finite-row contact does not license promotion from synonymous residual geometry to translation-efficiency survival.  Positive survival is likewise narrow: one positive controlled entry is a row-level translation-readout survival fact, not a global law and not a protein-level conclusion.
