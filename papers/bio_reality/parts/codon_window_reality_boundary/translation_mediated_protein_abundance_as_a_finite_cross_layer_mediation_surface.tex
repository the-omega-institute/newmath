\subsection{Translation-mediated protein abundance as a finite cross-layer mediation surface}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation coordinate used in the powered matrix is per-protein modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(g)&=\frac{\sum_c n_g(c)\,w(c)}{\sum_c n_g(c)},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_g(c)$ is the CDS codon count for protein $g$ and $w(c)$ is the dos Reis wobble-weighted codon adaptation weight computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts.  The protein coordinate is $Y(g)=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm}(g))$ from PAXdb abundance rows matched to real CDS codon counts.  The surface is therefore read only as a controlled statistical chain $Q\to T\to Y$, not as a direct statement that synonymous residual geometry has become protein output.

\paragraph{Rows and controls.}
The powered finite table contains twelve organism panels: $4042$ joined proteins for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each panel residualizes against $24$ control columns, with rank $23$ for the control matrix and rank $9$ for the residualized $Q$ block.  The controls include CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the union support of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The translation block has rank $1$ in every organism, and the joint $Q,T$ block has rank $10$, so the mediation regression is not a rank artifact in these finite rows.  Residual degrees of freedom range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse.

\paragraph{Mediation numbers.}
The statistical decomposition writes $c=R^2(Y\sim Q\mid Z)$ and $c'=R^2(Y\sim Q\mid Z,T)$, with the bounded mediation fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTY}&=\frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve panels place $M^{QTY}$ in the unit interval.  The largest finite mediation fraction is observed in yeast, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTY}=0.9491551041216973$.  Other large panels remain positive but smaller: \emph{E. coli} has $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, $c-c'=0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTY}=0.612922252793667$; \emph{D. melanogaster} has $c=0.0770952887136557$, $c'=0.02956474995793468$, $c-c'=0.047530538755721025$, and $M^{QTY}=0.6165167748739756$; human has $c=0.04454565118019448$, $c'=0.03242643251185274$, $c-c'=0.012119218668341744$, and $M^{QTY}=0.27206289160119157$.  The weakest positive mediated fraction is still nonnegative: \emph{S. solfataricus} has $c=0.06397514251295629$, $c'=0.06279117466131778$, $c-c'=0.0011839678516385038$, and $M^{QTY}=0.01850668564589326$.  These values support a finite statistical attenuation pattern: after modeled tAI is conditioned on, part of the $Q$--protein-abundance association is removed in every organism panel.

\paragraph{Translation readout calibration boundary.}
A smaller modeled-tAI survival contact was computed for three organisms rather than twelve: \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast.  In that contact the codon-count space has $61$ sense codons, all three organisms have standard tRNA family coverage, and the modeled-tAI matrix has $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This confirms that the coordinate-to-modeled-tAI readout is not empty.  It does not, by itself, provide ribosome profiling, translation-efficiency measurement, or protein abundance measurement.  The powered claim therefore uses the modeled tAI contact as $T$, not as observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Relation to measured yeast rows.}
A separate yeast five-way join supplies a useful check on scale.  In $4759$ joined yeast rows, the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ block and controls give $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ for protein abundance, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$ after a 5-prime CDS MFE structure proxy, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$ after measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$ after all three.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, with permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $p=0.1258741258741259$ for the structure-incremental term.  This independent yeast decomposition is consistent with the main chapter's caution: attenuation through translation-related covariates is visible, while structure and mechanism cannot be inferred from the abundance mediation alone.

\paragraph{Negative and limiting checks.}
The finite record also contains important failures of stronger readings.  In the mass-balance dynamic closure check, $120$ genes and $718$ intervals gave $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  In the turnover audit, $3290$ genes gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$.  In the cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit, $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species gave $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and no positive selection-imprint verdict.  These checks prevent the mediation surface from being promoted to turnover, mass-balance mechanism, or evolutionary-selection law.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The admissible chapter-level conclusion is narrow.  A twelve-organism finite matrix shows that the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive modeled-tAI-mediated attenuation component for matched protein-abundance rows after the declared sequence controls.  The claim is therefore a statistical cross-layer relation over finite joined rows, with strongest attenuation in yeast and positive bounded fractions in all panels.  It is not a causal effect, not an observed ribosome-dynamics result, and not a protein-realization theorem.  No conclusion about translation in the mechanistic sense, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality follows from this surface unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding observations and the same residual coordinate survives the required controlled readout step.
