\subsection{The wobble-broken $f_3$-stress translation-readout conjecture}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
The object in this record is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained by restricting the $B^*_{Q6}$ coding-coordinate table to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to organism-matched translation readout rows.  The intended distinction is not a raw codon-count asymmetry.  It is the residual imbalance that remains after amino-acid projection, with organism, amino-acid composition, GC3, coding length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls held in the baseline.  The conjectural readout is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be a tAI row, an stAI row, or a ribosome-occupancy / translation-efficiency row.  Protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, biological function, phenotype, and general organismal law are outside this readout unless a separate measurement at that layer is supplied.

\paragraph{Closed finite rows.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation computation gives the following row-level support for the coordinate before any translation claim is made.  The mediated set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
so the two mediated codons $ACA$ and $ACG$ are present in $M$ but absent from this saturated row, and the reported $f_3$ boundary list is empty.  Thus the finite coordinate is sharp enough to define a residual third-position stress axis, but this fact alone is only internal codon geometry.  It does not yet say that a ribosome, a tRNA pool, a protein product, or an organism-level phenotype responds to the axis.

\paragraph{Mechanistic asymmetry condition.}
The conjecture becomes biologically testable only when the organism or local context breaks third-position $f_3$ symmetry through tRNA or wobble weights.  The relevant inequality is a local readout condition, not a theorem of the codon table:
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Where this inequality is absent, the paired synonymous codons can remain topologically separated while being dynamically indistinguishable for the selected translation row.  Where it is present, the residual coordinate may enter a controlled translation model.  A powered tRNA-poverty computation on the $R$ sense codons was evaluated on $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped, permutation count $1000$, and alpha level $0.05$; its call was that the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply route for asking the question, but it is still not itself a survival score $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$.

\paragraph{Relation to the powered residual-axis rows.}
Several powered rows constrain the shape of the conjecture.  In the third-axis identity computation, $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested were computed, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The identified candidate was usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  In the corresponding mechanism row, again with $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, and $41$ design columns, the conclusion was selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and that CSC or positional identity still needs an external contact.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation row used the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The residual-axis row reported that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure row reported a third axis present, because $d_\perp$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  These rows make the $f_3$-stress conjecture coherent as a finite cross-layer test, but they do not license a universal direction of effect.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
For an organism $o$, a coding sequence $g$, and a translation readout $T_j$, the admissible test is a controlled comparison between a baseline model and the same model augmented by the $f_3$-stress coordinate.  In schematic form, the queried survival entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}(o)
  ={}& \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{partial}}\bigl(T_j(g);\ q_{f3}(g)\mid Z(g,o)\bigr),\\
Z(g,o) ={}& \{\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism},\ GC3,\ \text{length},\ M\text{-density},\ \text{mRNA control}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The conjecture is supported only if at least one controlled entry is strictly positive and clears the agreed partial-$R^2$ threshold after the simpler-codon baseline ablation.  It is broken if every such entry remains non-positive, or if the translation readout matrix is rank-degenerate.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix is a null boundary rather than evidence of survival.

\paragraph{Local interpretation rule.}
The finite data justify the following restricted claim: after amino-acid quotienting, the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a candidate translation-readout distinction in organisms or contexts where tRNA or wobble weights separate $f_3$-paired synonymous codons.  The allowed evidence sources are organism codon-usage tables, organism-level tRNA gene-copy or wobble-weight annotations, tAI or stAI weights as bounded translation-layer annotations, and matched ribosome-profiling plus mRNA data for translation-efficiency residuals.  No translation-layer realization follows from the coordinate alone; no folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows from a positive residual unless an independent contact at that layer supplies the missing observation.  The chapter therefore records a finite, controlled conjecture: the coordinate is ready to be tested against translation rows, while every stronger biological reading remains blocked until its own measurement is present.
