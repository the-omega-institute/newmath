\subsection{Finite Coordinate Contact}\label{sec:q6-ribosome-te-survival-coordinate-contact}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer test for the synonymous residual coordinates denoted here by $B^*_{Q6}$.  The biological object is not a protein, a folded structure, or a phenotypic endpoint.  It is a finite table of coding-sequence rows in which each row carries residual codon coordinates and a matched measured translation-efficiency readout.  The readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$, with $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$ in the local ribosome-profiling files, so the mRNA abundance contact is already part of the measured response rather than an optional downstream interpretation.  The tested question is whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate block retains positive controlled explanatory power for measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, and matched mRNA controls have been imposed.

\paragraph{Coordinate table.}
The residual block has nine coordinates.  The finite coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\quad Arg\_AGR,\quad Ile\_AUA,\quad Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\quad Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\quad Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\quad Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\quad f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are read as residual synonymous-usage directions after the amino-acid quotient has removed ordinary amino-acid composition.  The finite contact therefore tests a codon-residual association with measured TE, not a direct molecular mechanism.  A positive entry says that a coordinate remains useful for predicting the measured TE response under the stated controls; it does not say that the coordinate causes ribosome motion, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, or organism-wide fitness.
