\subsection{Rank-Powered Translation-Conditioned Protein Mediation for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bior-q6-rank-powered-translation-conditioned-protein-mediation-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record is a finite cross-layer matrix experiment over nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, matched translation readouts, matched protein-abundance readouts, and transcript-level controls per coding sequence.  The active coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  Every reported survival or mediation number is read after residualizing against an intercept, coding-sequence length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  Across the powered abundance-only panel, the control design has $24$ columns, residual rank $23$, and residualized coordinate rank $9$ in every organism reported here.

\paragraph{Direct protein-omics survival.}
The direct $Q \to P$ layer is positive in the powered PAXdb abundance panel, but it is only a protein-omics association.  The joined CDS/protein rows include $4042$ rows in \emph{Bacillus subtilis} with hit rate $0.997532$, $11661$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans} with hit rate $0.891377$, $14410$ in \emph{Danio rerio} with hit rate $1.000000$, $3739$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 with hit rate $0.997865$, $19053$ in human with hit rate $0.977929$, $19395$ in mouse with hit rate $0.980833$, and $5815$ in yeast with hit rate $0.915604$.  The corresponding direct residual $R^2_{QP}$ values include
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.107675,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.298376,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.044546,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.046135,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{C. elegans}) &= 0.102179,\\
R^2_{QP}(\textit{M. musculus}) &= 0.049828.
\end{aligned}
$$
All nine coordinates survive the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$ in each direct abundance organism, including yeast, human, mouse, rat, chicken, fish, bacteria, archaea, and amoebozoan rows.  This establishes a finite direct association surface, not a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Measured translation survival.}
The measured ribosome-efficiency contact supplies a separate $Q \to T$ layer, where $T$ is log measured translation efficiency from ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA.  Four organisms have powered measured-TE rows: \emph{D. rerio} has $13140$ TE genes and join hit rate $0.911867$, \emph{E. coli} K-12 has $3415$ TE genes and hit rate $0.913346$, human has $11554$ TE genes and hit rate $0.606414$, and yeast has $5128$ TE genes and hit rate $0.881857$.  After the same residual controls, the measured translation survivals are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.040342,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.009394,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.018838,\\
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.136819.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each of these rows again reports nine surviving coordinates.  The TE contact is condition-specific and observational; it is not a universal translation law, but it is enough to distinguish translation-surviving residual coordinates from coordinates that only survive at the protein-abundance layer.

\paragraph{Measured $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition.}
The measured mediation fit joins CDS, measured TE, and protein abundance for $13140$ fish rows, $3415$ \emph{E. coli} rows, $11554$ human rows, and $5128$ yeast rows.  The finite mediation scalar is reported as the fraction of the direct $Q \to P$ signal reduced after conditioning on measured TE.  The measured mediation list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{meas}(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.000000\quad (\text{raw }-0.048015),\\
M^{QTP}_{meas}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.040912,\\
M^{QTP}_{meas}(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.002797,\\
M^{QTP}_{meas}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.479307.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast row is the strongest measured translation-conditioned mediation row: $S_{QP}=0.314682$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.136819$, and the conditioned fraction is $0.479307$.  The \emph{E. coli} and human rows are positive but small, while the fish row is suppressive and is clipped to zero in the unit-interval readback.  Thus the finite surface supports translation-conditioned mediation in yeast, weak positive measured mediation in \emph{E. coli} and human, and no positive measured mediation in fish.

\paragraph{Joint TE and mRNA-stability mediation.}
A four-way join adds mRNA-stability readouts and decomposes $Q \to P$ by conditioning on measured TE, mRNA stability, and both together.  The computed organism count is $3$: \emph{E. coli} uses $2517$ joined rows, human uses $11322$, and yeast uses $4759$.  The single and joint residual fits are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{E. coli}:\quad &R^2_{QP}=0.118174,\; R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.115840,\; R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.090933,\; R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.089757,\\
\textit{H. sapiens}:\quad &R^2_{QP}=0.042742,\; R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.042507,\; R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196,\; R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.035522,\\
\textit{S. cerevisiae}:\quad &R^2_{QP}=0.271552,\; R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.143999,\; R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.254536,\; R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.130476.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding joint mediation fractions are $0.240467$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.168907$ for human, and $0.519518$ for yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999$.  The single-mediator split shows that yeast is dominated by TE-only reduction, with TE-only fraction $0.469719$ and stability-only fraction $0.062662$, whereas \emph{E. coli} is dominated by mRNA-stability reduction, with TE-only fraction $0.019755$ and stability-only fraction $0.230521$.  Human has TE-only fraction $0.005495$ and stability-only fraction $0.153147$.  In all three rows the joint reduction is within $0.05$ of the sum of the two single-mediator fractions, so the overlap classification is approximately independent in the fixed descriptive tolerance.

\paragraph{Four-case discriminant.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminator was run only for \emph{E. coli} and yeast.  In \emph{E. coli}, the modeled tAI row has $R^2_{QT}=0.541674$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.107675$; seven coordinates fall in case $D$, and two have positive-positive status without a mediation drop.  In yeast, the modeled tAI row has $R^2_{QT}=0.821346$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.298376$; all nine coordinates fall in case $D$.  The case labels are finite sign-and-conditioning readbacks over modeled tAI and measured abundance.  They do not turn modeled tAI into ribosome profiling and do not identify a molecular pathway.

\paragraph{Residual readout dictionary and non-translation contacts.}
The yeast partial-mediation dictionary certificate has $n_{join}=1800$ on $4759$ base rows, dictionary penalty $\lambda_{dl}=0.01$, join penalty $\rho_{join}=0.05$, raw best coverage $0.274500$, selection-aware null $95$ percentile $0.029149$, certified coverage $C^{\ast}=0.245382$, and unexplained fraction $0.754618$.  A separate yeast $f3_{stress}$ dwell analysis reports $n_{join}=1257$, base $R^2=0.185558$, full $R^2=0.204123$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.018565$, null $95$ percentile $0.001312$, $f3$--dwell correlation $0.161544$, and approximate $p=7.873916\times 10^{-9}$.  The localization and turnover contacts are weaker or more specialized.  Localization absorbs $0.009255$ in human above a null $95$ percentile $0.001376$, $0.011225$ in yeast above $0.004958$, and $0.009509$ in fish below its null $95$ percentile $0.011321$.  Human protein turnover joins $8057$ rows and gives absorbed fraction $3.574076\times 10^{-6}$, below its null $95$ percentile $0.000434$.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite data support a stratified conclusion.  Direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual survival against measured protein abundance is widespread across the powered organism panel.  Measured TE survival is positive in fish, \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast.  Translation-conditioned reduction of the direct protein signal is substantial in yeast, small but positive in \emph{E. coli} and human, and absent in the fish row under the measured scalar used here.  Joint TE plus mRNA-stability conditioning strengthens the interpretation that the yeast row has a large translation component, while \emph{E. coli} and human carry more of the reduction through stability-like transcript contacts.  The correct readback is therefore not that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ universally determines protein abundance, but that the finite matrix separates direct protein-omics survival, measured translation survival, measured mediation, and residual non-translation candidates.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No conclusion about translation itself, protein folding, physical admissibility, protein function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from a $Q \to P$ survival score alone.  A translation claim requires the measured TE or tAI/stAI contact to be named as the translation readout; a mediation claim requires the conditioned $Q \to T \to P$ fit; and folding, function, physical admissibility, perturbation rescue, or evolutionary directionality would require separate measurements at those layers.  The entries above are finite observational variance partitions over matched rows, not causal intervention results.
