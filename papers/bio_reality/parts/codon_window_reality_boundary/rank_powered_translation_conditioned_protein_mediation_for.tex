\subsection{Rank-powered translation-conditioned protein mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:q6-rank-powered-translation-conditioned-protein-mediation-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The empirical object is a finite matrix surface whose columns are nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and whose rows are organism-matched coding-sequence units with translation, transcript, and protein readouts when those contacts are present.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  Each regression residualizes the coordinate matrix and the readout against the same sibling control family: an intercept, $\log$ coding length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  Across the protein-abundance scan the control matrix has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and the residualized $Q$ matrix has rank $9$ in every reported organism, so the observed survival is not the rank-degenerate all-one boundary.

\paragraph{Direct protein-abundance contact.}
The direct $Q \to P$ surface uses $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  Fifteen organisms are rank-powered, with joined row counts and hit rates including $4042$ Bacillus subtilis CDS at hit rate $0.997532$, $11661$ Caenorhabditis elegans at $0.891377$, $14410$ Danio rerio at $1.000000$, $6974$ Dictyostelium discoideum at $0.946911$, $13295$ Drosophila melanogaster at $0.995656$, $3739$ Escherichia coli K12 at $0.997865$, $12418$ Gallus gallus at $1.000000$, $1747$ Halobacterium salinarum at $1.000000$, $19053$ Homo sapiens at $0.977929$, $19395$ Mus musculus at $0.980833$, $4129$ Mycobacterium smegmatis at $1.000000$, $5006$ Pseudomonas aeruginosa at $0.994438$, $13322$ Rattus norvegicus at $0.823159$, $5815$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae at $0.915604$, and $1656$ Sulfolobus solfataricus at $1.000000$.  The corresponding direct residual $R^2_{QP}$ values are all positive: $0.061906$, $0.102179$, $0.046135$, $0.081418$, $0.077095$, $0.107675$, $0.039808$, $0.019257$, $0.044546$, $0.049828$, $0.032090$, $0.064647$, $0.027640$, $0.298376$, and $0.063975$, respectively; in each case all nine coordinates survive the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  This establishes a direct protein-omics association surface, not a translation-mediated interpretation.

\paragraph{Measured translation contact.}
Measured translation efficiency is separately attached through ribosome-profiling TE, defined in the local files as footprint divided by mRNA, and then used as $\log_{10}(TE)$.  Four organisms have powered TE joins: Danio rerio has $13140$ TE genes from $14410$ CDS with hit rate $0.911867$ and $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.040342$; Escherichia coli K12 has $3415$ TE genes from $3739$ CDS with hit rate $0.913346$ and $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394$; Homo sapiens has $11554$ TE genes from $19053$ CDS with hit rate $0.606414$ and $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.018838$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5128$ TE genes from $5815$ CDS with hit rate $0.881857$ and $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.136819$.  All four retain rank $9$ for residualized $Q$, rank $23$ for the control family, and all nine coordinates survive.  Thus the same coordinate surface reaches a measured translation readout, but the data remain condition-specific and cross-sectional.

\paragraph{Measured mediation.}
The measured $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition uses the three-way join of CDS, ribosome TE, and protein abundance.  Danio uses $13140$ joined rows; Escherichia coli uses $3415$; Homo sapiens uses $11554$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae uses $5128$.  The measured mediation fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{Danio} &= 0.000000 \quad (M^{raw}=-0.048015),\\
M^{QTP}_{E.coli} &= 0.040912,\\
M^{QTP}_{Human} &= 0.002797,\\
M^{QTP}_{Yeast} &= 0.479307.
\end{aligned}
$$
The paired $S_{QP}$ values on the three-way joins are $0.049266$, $0.109415$, $0.042885$, and $0.314682$, while the paired measured-TE survivals are $0.040342$, $0.009394$, $0.018838$, and $0.136819$.  The yeast row is therefore the strongest measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance row: it has positive $Q \to T$, positive $Q \to P$, and nearly half of the direct abundance association is absorbed by measured TE under this variance partition.  Escherichia coli and human give positive but small absorption; Danio gives positive translation and protein surfaces but a suppressive raw mediation value, so it is recorded as measured-readout survival without promotion to a mediated row.

\paragraph{Joint transcript-stability conditioning.}
A stricter four-way surface conditions on measured TE and mRNA stability together in Escherichia coli, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The joined sample sizes are $2517$, $11322$, and $4759$.  The single and joint residual abundance values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(E.coli) &= 0.118174,& R^2_{QP\mid T,M}(E.coli) &= 0.089757,\\
R^2_{QP}(Human) &= 0.042742,& R^2_{QP\mid T,M}(Human) &= 0.035522,\\
R^2_{QP}(Yeast) &= 0.271552,& R^2_{QP\mid T,M}(Yeast) &= 0.130476.
\end{aligned}
$$
The joint mediation fractions are $0.240467$, $0.168907$, and $0.519518$, respectively, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999$.  The TE-only fractions are $0.019755$, $0.005495$, and $0.469719$, while the stability-only fractions are $0.230521$, $0.153147$, and $0.062662$.  The module classification is approximately independent in all three rows: the joint-minus-single-sum differences are $-0.009809$, $0.010265$, and $-0.012862$ under a descriptive tolerance of $0.05$.  This shows that measured TE and transcript stability explain complementary parts of the $Q \to P$ association, with yeast dominated by TE and the bacterial and human rows dominated by stability.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant was computed for Escherichia coli and Saccharomyces cerevisiae using per-protein mean modeled tAI and measured PAXdb abundance.  Escherichia coli has $3739$ joined proteins, residual degrees after controls $3716$, $R^2_{QT}=0.541674$, $R^2_{QP}=0.107675$, and case counts $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=7$, with two positive-positive coordinates lacking a mediation drop.  Yeast has $5815$ joined proteins, residual degrees after controls $5792$, $R^2_{QT}=0.821346$, $R^2_{QP}=0.298376$, and case counts $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$, with no positive-positive coordinate lacking the modeled-tAI mediation drop.  The modeled-tAI labels are objective sign-and-conditioning labels; they do not replace the measured ribosome-TE mediation rows above.

\paragraph{Residual readout dictionary and nontranslation candidates.}
The yeast partial-mediation certificate uses $n=1800$ sampled rows from $4758$ available joined rows, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations.  The best selection-aware coverage is $C^*=0.245382$, above the selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149$ and above the naive fixed-subset null $95$th percentile $0.005984$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.754618$.  Nontranslation candidate readouts remain secondary probes.  Subcellular localization absorbs $0.009509$ in Danio, $0.009255$ in human, and $0.011225$ in yeast against null $95$th percentiles $0.011321$, $0.001376$, and $0.004958$; hence human and yeast exceed that null while Danio does not.  Human protein turnover, on $8057$ joined rows, has $R^2_{Q,Tturn}=0.004615$ but absorption $3.5741\times 10^{-6}$, below the null $95$th percentile $0.000434$.

\paragraph{Admissible interpretation.}
The finite rows support a rank-powered cross-layer statement: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface survives both direct protein-abundance and measured translation readouts, and yeast gives a strong measured TE-conditioned abundance absorption, with additional transcript-stability absorption in the joint rows.  The same rows also enforce the local limit.  A positive direct $Q \to P$ entry alone does not imply translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality.  Those interpretations require a separate measured contact at the relevant layer and a conditioning analysis showing that the proposed readout removes or materially reduces the direct $Q \to P$ signal.
