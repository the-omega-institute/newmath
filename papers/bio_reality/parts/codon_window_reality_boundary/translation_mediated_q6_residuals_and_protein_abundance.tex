\subsection{Translation-mediated Q6 residuals and protein abundance}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions computed from real coding sequences: \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  The $t_j$ readout used in the powered mediation panel is per-protein modeled tAI, computed from organism-specific GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights and averaged over the codons of each protein.  The $p_k$ readout is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in ppm from PAXdb, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The readback is therefore not a direct $Q \to P$ association: it is the controlled chain $Q \to T \to P$, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline held in the control block.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
The powered mediation run joined twelve organism panels.  The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In every organism panel the residualized control rank was $23$, the residualized $Q$ rank was $9$, the residualized $T$ rank was $1$, and the joint $Q,T$ rank was $10$.  Thus the finite matrix has the expected degrees of freedom for a nine-coordinate residual block plus one translation-readout coordinate after the control projection.

\paragraph{Mediation scalar.}
For each organism the reported statistic decomposes total residual protein-abundance fit into a direct term after conditioning on modeled tAI and an indirect term removed by that conditioning.  The reported quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T}}{R^2_{QP}},\\
R^2_{QP} &= \text{protein-abundance fit from the residual } Q \text{ block after controls},\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= \text{remaining } Q \text{ fit after the residual modeled-tAI coordinate is included}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve organism panels placed $M^{QTP}$ in the unit interval.  The strongest mediated fraction was in yeast, where $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect component was $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Other large mediated fractions occurred in \textit{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \textit{E. coli} with $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$, and \textit{B. subtilis} with $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$.  The smaller but still positive mediated fractions were $0.4872268357227724$ in \textit{C. elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ in zebrafish, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.1976903964724546$ in chicken, $0.17489681798543982$ in rat, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ in \textit{M. smegmatis}, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{S. solfataricus}.  The finite-row verdict is therefore positive for a statistical translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition, not for a causal molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout contact.}
A smaller modeled-tAI survival audit computed the same nine residual directions over three organisms.  It confirmed all standard amino-acid families in the local GtRNAdb payload for \textit{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast; the corresponding standard-family counts were $20$, $20$, and $20$.  The modeled tAI readout covered $61$ sense codons in each of the three organisms and had zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each case.  The bounded coupling table had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This earlier three-organism contact is deliberately described as modeled tAI coupling rather than powered ribosome-profiling validation; it is useful as a translation-layer readout only when it remains separated from claims about observed ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Controls against direct promotion.}
The chapter does not infer protein realization from a naked $Q \to P$ regression.  The direct association is admissible only after the $Q \to T$ step is represented, and matched mRNA abundance is treated as a transcription-layer control rather than a translation coordinate.  In a five-way yeast mediation comparison with protein abundance, measured TE, mRNA half-life, and a 5-prime CDS MFE proxy, the joined row count was $4759$.  The same nine $Q$ coordinates had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation value for the incremental structure fraction was $0.1258741258741259$.  This auxiliary row block supports the same boundary: abundance variance can be partitioned, but that partition is not a proof of folding path, translation initiation mechanism, or causal structure mediation.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded companion checks.}
The broader finite ledger also contains checks that prevent overreading the positive mediation scalar.  A cross-ortholog selection-imprint panel used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; its positive-selection gate was not met, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null95 maximum $0.05427157066522958$.  A dynamic RPF-protein mass-balance run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals moved from $R^2=0.9712443377743347$ at level $L2$ to $R^2=0.8953999568421264$ at level $L3$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits.  A turnover residual audit over $3290$ genes gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These rows keep the claim at the level of a controlled statistical mediation surface: they do not license selection, turnover, or mass-balance mechanism claims.

\paragraph{Perturbation and axis context.}
Independent perturbation-style rows show that some protein-output contexts can carry a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal while still requiring their own local contacts.  The \textit{E. coli} mRFP synonymous variant panel used $1459$ variants and reported a controls-only $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, a full $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null values both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  Separately, the structural-order axis audit identified \texttt{f3\_stress} as the dominant coordinate, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip value $0.03125$; the other listed coordinates did not pass the same fold-level gate.  These are not substitutions for the mediation row block.  They only show that the residual coordinates have finite-row contacts in neighboring readout regimes and that the present claim must keep its own $Q \to T \to P$ route explicit.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite evidence supports the following statement: in the available joined organism panels, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive modeled-tAI-mediated component in matched protein-abundance prediction after the declared controls, and each panel has sufficient rank for that decomposition.  Nothing stronger is asserted here.  Modeled tAI is not an observed ribosome-occupancy trace; PAXdb abundance is not an isolated protein-production rate; a positive mediated fraction is not a synonym-edit rescue experiment; and the row surface by itself does not establish translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, selection, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger conclusion requires a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled finite statistic.
