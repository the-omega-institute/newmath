\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-stability boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:q6-translation-conditioned-protein-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel and gate.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is first read as an amino-acid-quotient residual rather than as raw codon composition. The tested residual family has nine coordinates, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The admissible biological question is not whether these coordinates directly determine protein stability. The admissible question is narrower: after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, organismal context, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls have been charged to the model, does a translation readout remain strong enough that a downstream protein-stability or degradation-rate readout can be reported as a conditioned residual association?

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
The residualization used in the adjacent translation-to-structure computation gives the relevant finite rank check. The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 the diagnostic table has $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and residual readout energy $136607.52873622958$. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the same diagnostic has $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and residual readout energy $620506.1490869389$. Thus the nine-coordinate residual block is not rank-collapsed by the local controls in those finite panels.

\paragraph{Stability readout rows.}
The protein-stability boundary run is classified as \texttt{partial}, with five folds and a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$. Three organisms carry stability-side rows. For \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, the primary readout is $\mathrm{mRNA}$ half-life with $2517$ joined rows. For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the primary readout is protein turnover with $6697$ protein-turnover rows, while the matched $\mathrm{mRNA}$ half-life table has $11322$ rows. For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the primary readout is protein turnover with $3537$ protein-turnover rows, while the matched $\mathrm{mRNA}$ half-life table has $4759$ rows. Before translation conditioning, the summary statistic for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ predicting the stability-side readout is positive in all three organism entries, with minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$.

\paragraph{Conditioned verdict.}
The decisive quantity is the full-control change in explained variance after the translation gate is imposed. Across the three organism entries, the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ has minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, with two negative entries and one positive entry. The weighted full-control value is $-0.001572140012981015$. The sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect is $0.946441650390625$, so the finite table does not support a robust direct stability claim after conditioning. The permuted full-control comparison is also nonpositive in all three entries, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$. The organism-level classification has two \texttt{partial} calls and one \texttt{mediated\_blocked\_null} call. The proper reading is therefore a guarded boundary result: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals can be brought to the protein-stability surface only through the translation-conditioned matrix, and the available rows do not justify promoting that contact into a direct codon-to-stability mechanism.

\paragraph{Comparison with structural-order contact.}
A separate structural-order boundary supplies a useful contrast because it carries finite positive conditioned signal. In the structural-order run, \emph{E. coli} contributes $3412$ joined rows and \emph{S. cerevisiae} contributes $5119$ joined rows. The unconditional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$-to-order statistic is positive in both entries, with minimum $0.03664544696481631$, mean and median $0.09855549686555976$, and maximum $0.1604655467663032$. After full controls, the conditioned $\Delta R^2$ remains positive in both entries, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$. The permuted comparison is negative in both entries, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, mean and median $-0.00212186632044159$, and maximum $-0.000779938262202795$. This contrast matters because the same residual family can cross one downstream boundary while failing to support a stronger stability interpretation in the available finite rows.

\paragraph{Observed structural-order rows.}
The translation-gated structural-order contact is itself limited to predicted structure confidence. The readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the scale $0$ to $100$. In \emph{E. coli}, $3739$ codon rows were joined, $3736$ matched proteins were used, $3736$ structural-order proteins were present, and the join hit rate was $0.999198$; the observed $R^2_{SO}$ was $0.03059081709413562$ with all nine coordinates surviving. In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows were joined, $5804$ matched proteins were used, $5804$ structural-order proteins were present, and the join hit rate was $0.998108$; the observed $R^2_{SO}$ was $0.0715885627979685$ with all nine coordinates surviving. These numbers are included only to delimit the neighboring contact surface. Predicted structure confidence is not experimental folding, protein function, pathway activity, localization, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Residual compression check.}
The broader residual-compression computation also argues against treating $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ as already explained by the measured downstream readout dictionary. With
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{compressibility} &= 1-\frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2},
\end{aligned}
$$
the computed organism values are $0.024368095798914635$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.02635576736932188$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, and $0.13234136547055853$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}; the median is $0.026$. The corresponding final residual fractions are $0.9756319042010854$, $0.9942823785118229$, $0.9736442326306781$, and $0.8676586345294415$. Each listed permutation probability is $0.000999000999000999$. The retained downstream dictionaries were $\{\mathrm{measured\_te}\}$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$ for \emph{E. coli}, $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{ptm\_density}\}$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, and $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}. The retained dimensionalities were respectively $1$, $2$, $3$, and $2$, while the raw candidate dimensionalities were $17$, $2$, $19$, and $19$. The finite result is not a high-compression law; it says that these measured downstream readouts absorb only a small fraction of the $P_Q$ residual energy in this descriptive design.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a cross-layer boundary rather than a mechanism. A $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate may be discussed at the protein-stability surface only after it has passed through the matched translation readout and the stated controls. Even then, the stability-side statement is a residual association conditional on translation, not a claim about translation realization by itself, folding, physical admissibility, degradation mechanism, biological function, phenotype, or universality. Modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used as bounded translation annotations, and codon-usage tables may be used as sequence-composition contacts, but neither source observes ribosome dynamics, protein turnover, folding quality, or organism-wide law without a separate matched measurement at that layer.
