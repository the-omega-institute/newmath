\subsection{Translation-mediated protein abundance for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite cross-layer fitting object rather than as a direct biological cause.  The row surface consists of triples $(q_i,T,P)$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$; $T$ is the per-protein modeled tAI value computed from codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights; and $P$ is the matched protein-abundance readout $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, so the residual relation is not allowed to recycle amino-acid identity, organism identity, length, GC content, or the broad support of the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates as the claimed signal.

\paragraph{Mediated statistic.}
For each organism, the fitted quantities are the total residual association $c=R^2(Q,P\mid Z)$, the remaining direct association after the translation readout $c'=R^2(Q,P\mid Z,T)$, the indirect increment $c-c'$, and the mediated fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statistic is a finite variance decomposition on matched rows.  It is not a causal intervention and it is not a ribosome-dynamics measurement.  It only says whether the same controlled $Q$ residual surface that predicts protein abundance is attenuated when the modeled translation-readout coordinate is inserted between $Q$ and $P$.

\paragraph{Primary finite-row result.}
The powered cross-organism run computed the full nine-coordinate $Q$ block in twelve organisms with rank sufficiency after residualization.  The joined row counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In every organism the residualized $Q$ design retained rank $9$, the residualized translation column had rank $1$, the joint $Q,T$ design had rank $10$, and the control block had rank $23$ with $24$ control columns before rank reduction.  Thus the mediated fractions are not produced by a collapsed design matrix.

\paragraph{Mediated fractions.}
The mediated fractions were all inside the unit interval.  The strongest finite-row attenuation occurred in yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The bacterial and animal rows also gave positive mediated components: \textit{E. coli} had $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, $c-c'=0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$; \textit{D. melanogaster} had $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$ with $c-c'=0.047530538755721025$; \textit{B. subtilis} had $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$ with $c-c'=0.037667440654802556$; and \textit{C. elegans} had $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$ with $c-c'=0.04978438377417191$.  The smallest mediated component was still positive in the archaeal row, where \textit{S. solfataricus} had $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$, $c=0.06397514251295629$, $c'=0.06279117466131778$, and $c-c'=0.0011839678516385038$.

\paragraph{Translation-readout contact.}
The translation coordinate used in the primary run is modeled tAI, not observed ribosome profiling.  A separate finite modeled-tAI survival computation used three organisms and found $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$ and maximum absolute diagonal value $3801961.031607896$.  That three-organism run did not meet the standard for powered survival across species: its own boundary condition records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival when at least $40$ matched species are requested.  It is therefore admissible here only as a bounded codon-adaptation readout and as the modeled $T$ coordinate used in the twelve-organism protein-abundance decomposition.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance separation.}
The primary claim is a cross-layer relation because $P$ is matched protein abundance, but the protein contact remains steady-state abundance rather than folding, function, localization, turnover, or mechanism.  Related finite-row audits support this separation.  In the yeast structure-mediation audit, the five-way join contained $4759$ rows, with a CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and an overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; there the residual $Q$-to-protein association had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, while conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability reduced the residual value to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$.  Adding the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy changed it only to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, giving a structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$ with permutation $p=0.1258741258741259$.  This neighboring result is consistent with a translation-abundance decomposition and does not license a folded-structure interpretation.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded audits.}
Several companion checks prevent over-reading the positive mediated fractions.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; its positive selection-imprint gate was false, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits.  The turnover audit also stayed negative beyond its controls: $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$ over $3290$ genes.  The mass-balance audit likewise showed deterioration after the fuller block, with $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$ on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The allowed readback is the controlled mediation matrix $Q\to T\to P$ on finite matched rows.  The result says that, for the current matched CDS, modeled-tAI, and PAXdb abundance surfaces, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive protein-abundance association whose fitted component is partially absorbed by modeled per-protein tAI in all twelve organisms, with organism-specific mediated fractions ranging from $0.01850668564589326$ to $0.9491551041216973$.  The result does not by itself establish observed translation, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, selection, perturbation response, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger statement would require its own layer-matched measurement surface and a separate controlled statistic.  The Phase-one genetic-code contact remains even more limited: it reports exact one-sided binomial upper-tail values $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ for both boundary and independent-replication probes with $40$ successes out of $40$, plus zero unexpected successes in $20$ negative controls and $20$ critical ablations, but that table-level contact is not a translation or protein-realization measurement.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The claim is ready as a finite, controlled cross-layer mediation result: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are allowed to enter protein-abundance fitting only through the declared $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition, with modeled tAI serving as the bounded translation-readout coordinate and matched protein abundance serving as the downstream $P$ coordinate.  The positive mediated fractions support the narrow cross-layer relation; the surrounding negative audits and contact boundaries keep the interpretation confined to statistical mediation on finite matched rows.
