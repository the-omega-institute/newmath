\subsection{Translation-mediated protein abundance for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer test for the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates after amino-acid quotienting.  The coordinate block is the nine-dimensional list
$Q=\{K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, f3\_stress\}$.  For each organism, the readout $T$ is a per-protein modeled tAI value computed as the codon-count-weighted mean of codon adaptation weights from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream readout $P$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The finite rows are not allowed to compare $Q$ directly with $P$ as a protein-realization claim.  The admissible object is the controlled matrix $Q\to T\to P$, after residualizing against the local control design $Z$ with amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each organism $o$, let $X_o$ denote the residualized nine-column $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix, let $T_o$ be the residualized modeled-tAI column, and let $Y_o$ be the residualized protein-abundance vector.  The computed scalar is the attenuation of the $Q\to P$ association after conditioning on $T$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_o &= \frac{R^2_o(Q,P)-R^2_o(Q,P\mid T)}{R^2_o(Q,P)},\\
R^2_o(Q,P) &= c_{o,\mathrm{total}},\\
R^2_o(Q,P\mid T) &= c'_{o,\mathrm{direct}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statistic is read only as a variance-partition on finite joined rows.  It is not a causal effect and it does not identify a molecular mechanism.  Rank diagnostics were satisfactory in every organism: the residualized $Q$ block had rank $9$, the residualized $T$ block had rank $1$, the joined $Q,T$ block had rank $10$, the control rank was $23$, and the reported in-unit-interval flag for $M^{QTP}_o$ was true for every organism.

\paragraph{Organism-level rows.}
The strongest row was \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with $5815$ joined proteins, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, $c_{\mathrm{total}}=0.29837624994140827$, $c'_{\mathrm{direct}}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect increment $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The same organism had $X$ explained energy on $T$ equal to $5.671703404506426$, $X$ explained energy on $Y$ equal to $882.6526210803958$, $T$ explained energy on $Y$ equal to $941.8858618613751$, and $t$ residual energy $6.90537888244409$.

The bacterial and metazoan rows show that the statistic is not a single-organism artifact.  \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 had $3739$ joined proteins, $c_{\mathrm{total}}=0.10767526677985896$, $c'_{\mathrm{direct}}=0.04167869969498872$, indirect increment $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  \textit{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168 had $4042$ joined proteins, $c_{\mathrm{total}}=0.061906245886660105$, $c'_{\mathrm{direct}}=0.02423880523185755$, indirect increment $0.037667440654802556$, and $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$.  \textit{Drosophila melanogaster} had $13295$ joined proteins, $c_{\mathrm{total}}=0.0770952887136557$, $c'_{\mathrm{direct}}=0.02956474995793468$, indirect increment $0.047530538755721025$, and $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$.  \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans} had $11661$ joined proteins, $c_{\mathrm{total}}=0.10217906758013379$, $c'_{\mathrm{direct}}=0.05239468380596188$, indirect increment $0.04978438377417191$, and $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$.

The remaining rows remain positive but more bounded.  \textit{Danio rerio} had $14410$ joined proteins and $M^{QTP}=0.3157053953614641$; \textit{Homo sapiens} had $19053$ joined proteins and $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$; \textit{Gallus gallus} had $12418$ joined proteins and $M^{QTP}=0.1976903964724546$; \textit{Rattus norvegicus} had $13322$ joined proteins and $M^{QTP}=0.17489681798543982$; \textit{Mus musculus} had $19395$ joined proteins and $M^{QTP}=0.15488799907323988$; \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} strain MC2 155 had $4129$ joined proteins and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$; and \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} had $1656$ joined proteins and $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$.  Thus all twelve finite organism panels produce a nonnegative mediated fraction in the declared interval, but the magnitude varies sharply by organism.

\paragraph{Translation contact status.}
A smaller modeled-tAI survival computation was also present for three organisms.  It computed the same nine residual coordinates, used a $61$-sense-codon count space, and found $81$ modeled-tAI coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  The three-organism payload included all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \textit{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast; raw wobble-weight fallback count was $0$ for each of those three modeled readouts.  This contact supports bounded translation-readout coupling, not observed ribosome dynamics: the run itself records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that a stronger survival claim would need at least $40$ matched species or a measured translation panel.

\paragraph{Matched yeast comparison.}
A separate yeast five-way join gives an internal consistency check on the role of measured TE and mRNA stability.  In that panel, $4759$ genes were joined across CDS, protein abundance, TE, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE structure proxy.  The direct residual association was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  After conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability, the remaining $Q$ increment was $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$; after adding the structure proxy it was $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only attenuation fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, with permutation $p=0.1258741258741259$ for that incremental structure term.  This comparison is important because it shows that translation and mRNA controls absorb a large part of the abundance association in a measured yeast panel, while a CDS-only MFE proxy contributes very little additional attenuation.

\paragraph{Negative and limiting rows.}
The finite record also contains limiting checks that constrain interpretation.  A dynamic RPF/protein closure computation on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals had $R^2_{L0,M}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1,M,E}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  A turnover audit on $3290$ genes had $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint panel used $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows; it reported $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null95 $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and did not pass the positive selection-imprint gate.  These rows keep the present chapter at a controlled abundance-mediation level rather than promoting it to dynamics, turnover, or evolutionary mechanism.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The ready claim is narrow: finite joined panels support a controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-to-modeled-tAI-to-protein-abundance mediation statistic across twelve organisms, with all reported mediated fractions lying in $[0,1]$ and with rank sufficiency recorded.  The chapter does not infer observed translation from modeled tAI alone, and it does not infer folding, physical admissibility, biological function, perturbation rescue, selection, mechanism, phenotype, or universality from these rows.  A stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact: ribosome profiling for observed translation, matched mRNA abundance for transcriptional separation, structure or physical assays for folding and admissibility, perturbation rows for causal response, and multi-organism controlled panels before any population-level law is asserted.
