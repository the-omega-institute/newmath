\subsection{Translation-mediated protein abundance for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite cross-layer surface rather than as a direct assertion about protein biology.  The row type is a triple $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-side readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  In the powered protein-abundance run the residual coordinate list was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate used for the cross-organism mediation surface was per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed from codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate was $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control block $Z$ contained an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  Thus the admissible readback is not $q_i\to p_k$ in isolation; it is the controlled comparison between the total $Q\to P$ fit and the residual $Q\to P$ fit after $T$ has been included.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the finite computation used the joint nine-dimensional residual matrix $X_Q$, one modeled translation column $T$, and one protein-abundance column $Y$, all residualized against $Z$.  The reported mediation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Y\sim X_Q\mid Z)-R^2(Y\sim X_Q\mid Z,T)}{R^2(Y\sim X_Q\mid Z)},\\
 c &= R^2(Y\sim X_Q\mid Z),\qquad
 c' = R^2(Y\sim X_Q\mid Z,T).
\end{aligned}
$$
Every organism in the powered surface had sufficient rank after the control residualization: the control rank was $23$, the residual coordinate rank was $9$, the modeled translation rank was $1$, and the combined $X_Q,T$ rank was $10$.  The resulting finite organism list and joined protein counts were: \emph{Bacillus subtilis} $4042$, \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans} $11661$, \emph{Danio rerio} $14410$, \emph{Drosophila melanogaster} $13295$, \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 $3739$, \emph{Gallus gallus} $12418$, \emph{Homo sapiens} $19053$, \emph{Mus musculus} $19395$, \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} MC2 155 $4129$, \emph{Rattus norvegicus} $13322$, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} $5815$, and \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} $1656$.  These counts are part of the finite surface: no population-level law is inferred from them.

\paragraph{Observed finite values.}
The mediated fractions were positive and inside $[0,1]$ for all twelve organism rows.  The strongest row was yeast, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, indirect increment $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The bacterial and metazoan rows remained positive but varied substantially: \emph{E. coli} had $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, indirect increment $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$; \emph{Drosophila} had $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$; \emph{B. subtilis} had $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$; \emph{C. elegans} had $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$; \emph{Danio} had $M^{QTP}=0.3157053953614641$; human had $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$; chicken had $M^{QTP}=0.1976903964724546$; rat had $M^{QTP}=0.17489681798543982$; mouse had $M^{QTP}=0.15488799907323988$; \emph{M. smegmatis} had $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$; and \emph{S. solfataricus} had $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$.  The row with the smallest mediated fraction still had positive indirect increment, $0.0011839678516385038$, so the table supports a bounded statistical mediation surface rather than an all-or-nothing organism signature.

\paragraph{Translation readout boundary.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival computation checked the residual basis against organism-scoped tAI weights for three organisms.  It computed all nine residual coordinates over the $61$ sense-codon space, used a bounded-contact threshold of $0.01$, and found $81$ entries above threshold with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The same run explicitly reported that $3$ organisms are not enough for powered survival; it is modeled-tAI bounded coupling, not ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  This distinction is essential for the present chapter.  The mediated protein-abundance surface may use modeled tAI as a translation-side covariate, but modeled tAI is not itself an observation of ribosome occupancy, elongation dynamics, or protein production.

\paragraph{Control comparisons.}
The yeast structure-mediation run provides a local check that protein-abundance association can be decomposed under additional biological covariates without upgrading the claim to a causal pathway.  In the five-way yeast join, $4759$ rows were available, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  In that surface, the controlled $Q\to P$ value was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, the value after residualized 5-prime CDS MFE was $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, the value after measured TE plus mRNA stability was $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and the value after all three was $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, while the structure-incremental fraction beyond TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$ with permutation $p=0.1258741258741259$.  These numbers keep the chapter in the domain of variance partitioning: the controlled matrix can separate codon residuals, modeled or measured translation readouts, mRNA controls, and protein abundance, but it does not by itself identify a physical route.

\paragraph{Negative and neighboring checks.}
Several finite rows constrain how far the present conclusion may be read.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; it found $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$ but leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, below the null95 threshold $0.05427157066522958$, so the positive selection-imprint gate was false.  The dynamic mass-balance run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals reported $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $p_{TE\mathrm{bin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover residual audit on $3290$ genes likewise gave $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These rows prevent promotion from protein-abundance mediation to evolutionary selection, dynamic mass balance, or turnover prediction.

\paragraph{Scope of the claim.}
The chapter therefore records the following bounded claim.  A finite twelve-organism table supports a controlled statistical decomposition in which $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a protein-abundance association whose direct component is reduced after including a modeled translation readout.  The strongest mediated row is yeast, and all twelve rows have positive $M^{QTP}$ fractions within the unit interval.  This is not a statement that synonymous residual geometry directly realizes translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  A claim at any of those layers would require its own matched contact: observed ribosome profiling for translation dynamics, structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility, perturbation or rescue data for mechanism, and population-scale controls for any general law.  Without such layer-specific data, the present finite surface remains a controlled $Q\to T\to P$ mediation matrix over matched CDS, modeled tAI, and measured protein abundance.
