\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance relation for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer relation whose rows are triples $(q_i,T,P)$: a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ basis, a translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$. The admissible residual coordinates are the nine computed axes
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate used in the powered protein-abundance computation is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI from GtRNAdb all-tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The protein coordinate is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts. The control design residualizes amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline before the residual-to-protein and residual-to-translation comparisons are read.

\paragraph{Powered organism panel.}
The powered matrix joins twelve organism-specific CDS, tRNA, and protein-abundance tables. The joined row counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In every organism the residualized rank check is sufficient: $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$, and the control rank is $23$ with $24$ control columns. Thus the protein-abundance statement is not produced by an obvious rank collapse of the finite table.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the finite statistic compares the total residual-to-protein fit $c=R^2(Q\to P)$ against the direct fit $c'=R^2(Q\to P\mid T)$ after the translation readout has been conditioned. The mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c},
\end{aligned}
$$
whenever $c>0$. The observed finite values are all in the unit interval. The strongest entry is yeast, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. Other large mediated fractions occur in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 with $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$, \emph{Bacillus subtilis} with $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$, and \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans} with $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$. Smaller but positive mediated fractions occur in \emph{Danio rerio} ($0.3157053953614641$), \emph{Homo sapiens} ($0.27206289160119157$), \emph{Gallus gallus} ($0.1976903964724546$), \emph{Rattus norvegicus} ($0.17489681798543982$), \emph{Mus musculus} ($0.15488799907323988$), \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} ($0.08397602919861291$), and \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} ($0.01850668564589326$).

\paragraph{Readout scale and residual energies.}
The table is numerically finite at the readout layer. The per-protein modeled tAI computation uses $86$, $581$, $8874$, $290$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $407$, $49$, $417$, $275$, and $47$ all-tRNA copy totals across the twelve organisms in the order listed above. Zero raw codon-adaptation weights are filled by a geometric-mean fallback only in four cases: $3$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $1$ for \emph{Escherichia coli}, $1$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, and $1$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}; the remaining eight organisms have fallback count $0$. At the residualized comparison layer, yeast has $T$-residual energy $6.90537888244409$ and protein-readout residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, while human has $T$-residual energy $1.174468648693672$ and protein-readout residual energy $30769.5367150478$. These numbers matter because the mediated fractions are computed from concrete residual energies and ranks, not from a qualitative ordering of codons.

\paragraph{Translation-contact precursor.}
A smaller modeled-tAI precursor computation verifies that all three local GtRNAdb payloads contain the full twenty standard amino-acid families: \emph{E. coli} K12 has $89$ FASTA headers with $87$ matched and $2$ unmatched, human has $432$ headers with $432$ matched, and yeast has $275$ headers with $275$ matched. In each of the three organisms, modeled tAI is computed on all $61$ sense codons with zero raw $W$ fallback count $0$. The bounded modeled-tAI coupling matrix has $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. That precursor is not by itself a powered protein-abundance result, since $3$ organisms are insufficient for the powered survival request, but it gives the translation-readout coordinate used by the larger protein-abundance table.

\paragraph{Yeast structure and rate-side audits.}
The yeast five-way join for the structure-mediated abundance audit has $4759$ joined rows, a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and an overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$. Its residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ association to protein abundance is $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy gives $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction after TE and mRNA-stability conditioning is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation values are $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$-structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction. A separate yeast rate-slot audit uses $3289$ genes and reports an observable execution subblock: at least one observed rate proxy slot passes both effective null classes. These audits support the placement of the protein-abundance claim at the controlled cross-layer relation level, while keeping structure and mechanism claims outside the present conclusion.

\paragraph{Axis localization.}
The structural-order axis audit identifies $f3\_stress$ as the single fold-level significant coordinate. Its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, its held-out weighted $\Delta R^2$ is $0.0031744502294756994$, and its sign-flip right-tail value is $0.03125$. The remaining coordinate-level sign-flip values are $0.142578125$ for $Arg\_AGR$, $0.0693359375$ for $Ile\_AUA$, $0.2294921875$ for $K\_AAA$, $0.779296875$ for $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $0.24609375$ for $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $0.4873046875$ for $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $0.2158203125$ for $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and $0.529296875$ for $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$. This makes the protein-abundance chapter sharper: the cross-layer matrix is a nine-coordinate statistic, but one stress-oriented residual axis is the most visible route in the finite structural-order audit.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The admissible statement is a controlled statistical decomposition: a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-to-protein-abundance association is reduced after conditioning on a translation readout, and the reduction is positive in every organism in the powered panel. The statement is not a causal claim. Modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling, and the present protein-abundance readout does not isolate mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway role, or perturbation response. No claim of translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, or universality follows from these rows unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that evidence and the corresponding controlled statistic is evaluated. The confirmatory genetic-code-table checks remain at their own level: they have $40$ successes out of $40$ in the independent replication binomial test, right-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, $20$ negative controls with $0$ unexpected successes, and $20$ critical ablations with $0$ unexpected successes, but those table contacts do not promote the present protein-abundance relation beyond the measured and modeled readouts named here.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite-row conclusion is therefore positive at the cross-layer relation level. The carrier is the table of residual coordinate, modeled translation readout, and matched protein abundance, with the nine-coordinate $Q$ matrix residualized against sequence and composition controls. The strongest organism-specific relation is yeast, where $94.91551041216973\%$ of the residual-to-protein $R^2$ is removed by conditioning on modeled tAI, and the weakest positive relation is \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, where the same fraction is $1.850668564589326\%$. Because all twelve mediated fractions lie in $[0,1]$ and the rank checks are sufficient, the chapter enters the paper as a bounded protein-abundance mediation record, not as evidence for a molecular mechanism or a universal biological rule.
