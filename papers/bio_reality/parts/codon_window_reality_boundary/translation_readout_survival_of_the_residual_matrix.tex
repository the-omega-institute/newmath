\subsection{Translation-readout survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual matrix}\label{sec:bioreality-translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and tested statistic.}
The object tested here is the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual matrix against translation-adjacent readouts.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the aggregate $f_3$-stress coordinate.  For a coordinate $q_i$ and readout $r_j$, the score is read as a controlled survival entry rather than as an unadjusted association.  The covariate block contains amino-acid composition, organism context where applicable, $GC3$, coding length, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the notation of this chapter the tested matrix is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism},\ GC3,\ \text{length},\ M\text{-density}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry means that the residual coordinate improves the controlled description of the readout after the declared covariates and baseline ablation.  It does not mean that the codon coordinate is a mechanism, a universal direction, a folding signal, or a functional assay.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling before survival.}
The first finite computation used three organisms, $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K-12, with a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy.  All nine residual coordinates were computed in the $61$-codon count space.  The break rule for this row was that all nine couplings satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  The rule was not met: the matrix had $9$ nonzero entries above the threshold and a maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is only a coupling row.  It shows that the synonymous residual directions are not numerically silent against the proxy tRNA-copy spectrum, but it is not itself an $S_{ij}$ survival matrix and it is not a ribosome observation.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI survival surface.}
A separate powered modeled-translation readout used per-codon tAI derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The run covered $40$ organisms, used $9$ modeled readout columns matched to the same $9$ residual coordinates, and residualized a $24$-column control block of rank $23$.  After controls, the residual degrees of freedom were $17$ and the residualized coordinate rank was $9$.  The resulting modeled translation score was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\mathrm{shape}(S^{QT}) &= 9\times 9,\\
\mathrm{threshold}_{+} &= 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This row supports a bounded modeled-translation statement: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual directions survive strongly against a modeled tAI surface after the declared controls.  The readout is still gene-copy and wobble-weight based.  It is not ribosome occupancy, measured elongation, proteomics, folding, or protein realization.

\paragraph{Organism sweep.}
The modeled-tAI sweep extended the same family of residual coordinates across $41$ organisms, with a target of $40$ organisms reached.  The per-organism $S^{QT}$ distribution had minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using $1000$ permutations.  Thus the modeled signal is not a one-row anomaly of the three-organism proxy calculation.  The result remains descriptive because the organisms are not treated as phylogenetically independent units, and the readout remains modeled tAI rather than a measured ribosome trace.

\paragraph{Position-resolved ribosome contact.}
A direct ribosome-occupancy row was computed for ordered coding sequences and local occupancy windows.  The join contained $5754$ overlapping gene identifiers, $5754$ genes after the length check, $6013$ ordered-CDS genes, and zero length mismatches.  The analysis used the file-provided codon index and window positions together with amino-acid bins; occupancy effects were demeaned within those bins, and synonymous labels were permuted only inside the same bin.  The permutation request was $200$; all $9$ families reached at least $200$ permutations.  With $\lambda=0.01$, description-length penalty $DL=2$, and the sign gate $C_H>0$, the passing families were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ f_3\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supplies a measured local ribosome-occupancy contact for synonymous families under position controls.  It is a descriptive separation of local footprint occupancy, not a causal proof of slowing, pausing, translation mechanism, protein output, folding, or function.

\paragraph{Three-organism Leu gate.}
The CUN/UUR Leu gate used the same three organism labels as the proxy coupling row.  The reported Pearson coefficient and the controlled partial Pearson coefficient were both $1.0$ at $n=3$.  The log-ratio signs were $+1$, $-1$, and $+1$, with log-ratio values $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms.  This row is useful as a finite contact for the Leu CUN/UUR coordinate and tRNA-Leu copy ratio, but it is too small to be read as a population estimate, an invariant direction, or a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance contacts and modeled-tAI complements.}
The protein-abundance rows show that residual coordinate information remains visible in abundance-adjacent readouts under the same control vocabulary, but they do not replace a translation readout.  For six organisms, the modeled-tAI complement fraction $d$ of the abundance component was $0.874628721049266$ in $\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}$, $0.6629960038578534$ in $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ K-12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ in $\mathrm{Gallus\ gallus}$, $0.9208949355679455$ in human, $0.8972672018746484$ in $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis}$, and $0.21173219572706714$ in yeast.  The same table reported $P_Q$ norms and modeled-tAI-complement norms; for example yeast had $P_Q^2=882.6526208776509$ and $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}^2=186.88597748267557$, while human had $P_Q^2=1370.6490492302532$ and $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}^2=1262.2237678771598$.

The $H$-ranking row sharpened this complement analysis.  In $\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}$, the selected readout was measured translation efficiency, with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, score $0.001566051846174291$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, null $95$th percentile $0.0003297458343745959$, and $13140$ joined rows.  In yeast the selected readout was mRNA, with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, score $-0.008853737772559931$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$, null $95$th percentile $0.0004745820831535835$, and $5128$ joined rows.  Human and chicken selected complex membership in that ranking table, while the $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ and $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$ rows had no selected $H_o^*$.

\paragraph{Abundance survival in organism and tissue contexts.}
The yeast condition row compared YEPD rich medium and SD minimal medium on shared GFP-tagged abundance rows.  The shared count was $2209$ proteins; YEPD had $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ and SD had $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$.  In both conditions the dominant coordinate was $f_3$-stress, and the condition comparison had cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ over $1000$ permutations.  A human tissue row gave $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ in adult B cells with dominant $Arg_{AGR}$ over $9244$ joined proteins, $R^2_{QP}=0.02320896321347296$ in adult heart with dominant $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$ over $6468$ proteins, and $R^2_{QP}=0.026133456228248052$ in adult liver with dominant $Ile_{AUA}$ over $8664$ proteins.  The tissue pairwise cosines were $0.6883655232187615$, $0.8990093450963941$, and $0.9176793373754919$.  These rows show abundance association after controls, not a translation-mediated protein mechanism by themselves.

\paragraph{mRNA stability and mediation rows.}
The yeast mRNA half-life row used $5085$ joined records and reported $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, with dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ and cosine $0.8040935468384264$ against the same-join protein-abundance surface.  The $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ half-life row used $2720$ joined records, reported $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, dominant $Arg_{AGR}$, and cosine $-0.7784923375569599$ against the same-join abundance surface.  The human consensus half-life row used $16165$ joined records across $54$ dataset columns, reported $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and a positive cosine $0.6351183883924848$ against human abundance.

In yeast, conditioning abundance on mRNA half-life decomposed the joint residual relation as
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.24851799532219632,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by}\ M} &= 0.027596042074056304,\\
\mathrm{mediation\ fraction} &= 0.09994436477872035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mediation permutation null mean was $0.00019674487584783138$, the null $95$th percentile was $0.0014571003414947088$, and the right-tail permutation value was $0.000999000999000999$.  This is a variance-partition statement.  It is not a causal mRNA-stability pathway proof.

\paragraph{Per-coordinate and family decomposition.}
The coordinate decomposition row used a yeast join with $n=1258$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, and a fixed residualized readout dictionary of rank $3$.  Eight of the nine coordinates were listed as having a real source in the held-out/permutation gate: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The $f_3$-family decomposition used $1800$ joined yeast rows, $21$ family subcoordinates, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations.  The full $f_3$ projection norm was $52.76911713166682$, and its fraction of target energy was $0.12259712041843235$ against target norm $430.42705205115925$.  Nineteen synonymous-family subcoordinates were listed as real sources, including $F_{UUU\to UUC}$, $L_{UUA\to UUG}$, $K_{AAA\to AAG}$, $R_{AGA\to AGG}$, $E_{GAA\to GAG}$, and $G_{GGU\to GGG}$.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter supports a bounded positive reading: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not absorbed by amino-acid composition, coding length, $GC3$, organism context where used, and the $M$-density baseline on several translation-adjacent and abundance-adjacent finite rows.  The strongest translation-layer row is the powered modeled-tAI matrix with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ and a $9\times 9$ survival surface over $40$ organisms; the most direct ribosome row is the position-controlled occupancy separation in which $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress pass the $\lambda=0.01$, $DL=2$, $C_H>0$ gate.  Protein abundance, mRNA stability, tissue, condition, complement, and family decompositions provide additional controlled contacts, but they do not erase the distinction between modeled translation, measured ribosome occupancy, mRNA stability, and protein abundance.

The scope restriction is local and strict.  No translation mechanism, folding property, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal pathway, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that claim and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.  A positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to a positive survival entry without the controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation, and a modeled tAI row is not promoted to measured ribosome occupancy or protein output.
