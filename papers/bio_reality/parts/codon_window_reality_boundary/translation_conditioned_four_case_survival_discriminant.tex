\subsection{Translation-conditioned four-case survival discriminant}
\label{sec:q6-translation-conditioned-four-case-survival-discriminant-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as finite columns $q_i$ evaluated on matched coding-sequence rows, translation readouts $T_j$, and protein-omics readouts $P_k$. The carrier is not a symbolic claim about codon meaning by itself. It is the concrete matrix obtained after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, methionine density, mRNA, and simpler baseline controls have been removed from the tested readouts. The intended readback is therefore a controlled cross-layer classification of each matched cell $(q_i,T_j,P_k)$, not a direct biological mechanism.

For the powered run cited here, the coordinate source is the imported $q$-vector table from the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation. The translation readout is per-protein modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_{c} \operatorname{count}(c)\,\operatorname{tAI}(c)}{\sum_{c} \operatorname{count}(c)},
\end{aligned}
$$
where codon counts are combined with all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\operatorname{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts. This distinction is essential: the $T$ column is a modeled translation-efficiency proxy, while the $P$ column is a measured abundance contact.

\paragraph{Four cases.}
For every residual coordinate and matched pair of readouts, the classification uses the signs of the controlled survival scores $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$, followed by a mediation check through $M^{QTP}$. The local decision rule is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{A} &: S^{QT}\leq 0 \;\text{and}\; S^{QP}\leq 0,\\
\mathrm{B} &: S^{QT}>0 \;\text{and}\; S^{QP}\leq 0,\\
\mathrm{C} &: S^{QT}\leq 0 \;\text{and}\; S^{QP}>0,\\
\mathrm{D} &: S^{QT}>0 \;\text{and}\; S^{QP}>0 \;\text{with the direct } q_i\to P_k \text{ term reduced after conditioning on } T_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
Case A means that the controlled data compress the residual coordinate away for both tested layers. Case B means that the coordinate survives at the modeled-translation layer without detected propagation to the measured protein-abundance layer. Case C means that the coordinate predicts protein abundance without mediation by the measured translation readout; it is not a translation explanation. Case D means that the coordinate survives both tests and the protein association is reduced after translation conditioning, which is the local signature of translation-mediated propagation in this finite matrix.

\paragraph{Rank-powered contact.}
The run used a classification threshold of $10^{-12}$ and required rank sufficiency before any positive classification. For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the joined table contained $3739$ coding-sequence/protein rows. After the $24$ control columns were residualized, the control rank was $23$, the residual degrees of freedom were $3716$, and the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate rank was $9$ against both $T$ and $P$. The residual translation energy was $0.4728468725814029$, the residual protein-abundance energy was $4020.333671802061$, the coordinate energy explained on $T$ was $0.256128714706293$, and the coordinate energy explained on $P$ was $432.89050065533684$. The aggregate controlled fits were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.5416736993691318,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate-level classification was complete: $0$ coordinates in case A, $0$ in case B, $0$ in case C, $7$ in case D, and $2$ positive-positive coordinates without the required mediation drop. No coordinate was left unclassified.

For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined table contained $5815$ coding-sequence/protein rows. After the same $24$ control columns were residualized, the control rank was $23$, the residual degrees of freedom were $5792$, and the residualized coordinate rank was again $9$ against both $T$ and $P$. The residual translation energy was $6.90537888244409$, the residual protein-abundance energy was $2958.1865890925337$, the coordinate energy explained on $T$ was $5.671703404506426$, and the coordinate energy explained on $P$ was $882.6526210803958$. The aggregate controlled fits were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification was also complete in this organism: $0$ coordinates in case A, $0$ in case B, $0$ in case C, $9$ in case D, and $0$ positive-positive coordinates lacking the mediation drop. No coordinate was unclassified.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite observation is therefore not merely that some $Q6$ residual coordinate correlates with a protein-abundance column. The observation is that, after the stated controls and rank checks, both organisms retain full residual coordinate rank and place their classified coordinates in the positive translation-positive protein regime, with mediation-supported case D accounting for $7$ of $9$ coordinates in \textit{E. coli} and $9$ of $9$ coordinates in yeast. The two remaining \textit{E. coli} positive-positive coordinates are explicitly held outside case D because the mediation drop condition was not met.

This supports a bounded cross-layer discriminant: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry can be tested against a modeled translation readout and a measured protein-omics readout, and the result separates translation-only survival, protein prediction without measured translation mediation, and translation-conditioned propagation. The present run supplies a powered finite matrix for the two listed organisms; it does not promote the result to a population law over all organisms.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation dynamics, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from the residual coordinates alone. Modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling. Cross-sectional abundance is not a perturbation experiment and is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context. A direct $Q\to P$ association without the mandatory $T$ conditioning step is not admissible as a protein-function or mechanism claim. Case C, when it occurs, must be read only as protein-omics prediction not mediated by the measured translation readout; it may reflect unmeasured translation variables, transcriptional effects, selection, confounding, or non-translation paths. If future matched panels lose residual rank, collapse to case A, or lack matched translation, proteomics, and mRNA contacts, the discriminant becomes a null boundary result rather than evidence for downstream structure, function, physical admissibility, phenotype, or universality.
