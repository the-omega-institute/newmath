\subsection{Measured Rows And Controlled Rank}\label{sec:q6-ribosome-te-survival-controlled-rank}

\paragraph{Matched ribosome-profiling rows.}
Four organism-specific row sets were joined to the measured TE source.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ CDS rows were joined, $3415$ genes were used, $3415$ genes had measured TE, and the ribosome-TE join hit rate was $0.9133458143888741$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ CDS rows were joined, $5128$ genes were used, $5128$ genes had measured TE, and the hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19053$ CDS rows were joined, $11554$ genes were used, $11554$ genes had measured TE, and the hit rate was $0.6064136881331024$.  For \emph{Danio rerio}, $14410$ CDS rows were joined, $13140$ genes were used, $13140$ genes had measured TE, and the hit rate was $0.9118667591950035$.  These row counts make the test finite and inspectable: the result is not a statement about all organisms or all translation conditions.

\paragraph{Residualized design.}
Each organism used $24$ control columns, with residualized control rank $23$, rank tolerance $10^{-10}$, and degeneracy threshold $0.999999$ for excluding saturated fits.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ block had $9$ coordinates and residualized rank $9$ in every organism, so the coordinate panel was powered rather than collapsed into the control span.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3392$ in \emph{E. coli}, $5105$ in yeast, $11531$ in human, and $13117$ in zebrafish.  The corresponding residual response energies were $919.4854026798789$, $753.5781168800304$, $2052.594706241978$, and $3588.378859021949$.  The finite explained energies attributed to the residualized coordinate block were $8.637891149939946$, $103.10404057290246$, $38.66659710161269$, and $144.76301201119642$ in the same order.

\paragraph{Rank discipline.}
The rank audit is part of the claim.  A table with too few rows, a rank-saturated control design, or an all-perfect fit is not counted as survival.  Such a table is marked as requiring more data because it cannot separate a genuine residual coordinate contribution from a degenerate design.  In this contact all four organism panels passed the powered-rank check: $rank(X_{Q6}\mid Z)=9$ while $rank(Z)=23$ for a $24$-column control matrix.  Thus the finite data support a measured association test rather than only a notation-level or geometry-level comparison.
