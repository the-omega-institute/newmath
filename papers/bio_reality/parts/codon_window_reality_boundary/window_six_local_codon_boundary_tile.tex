\subsection{Window-six local codon boundary tile}
\label{sec:bioreality-codon-window6-local-tile-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object and contact.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the window-six codon cube $Q_6$, equipped with a six-bit codon display, local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The biological contact is a curated standard-code table assigning each codon to an amino-acid label or to a stop label.  That contact is sufficient for label readback and for checking a local split inside $Q_6$, but it is not an observation of translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Recovered boundary set.}
The distinguished boundary row $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The finite run identifies it as
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is reported as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the data do not merely give a count; they specify the full thirteen-codon boundary and the combinatorial surface statistics attached to it.  The row is local: it is a tile-level codon-label pattern in the six-bit cube, not a law asserted for all biological coding systems.

\paragraph{Spectral row.}
The spectral check reports $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$.  The radial polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, recorded also as the cubic-polynomial residual, so the numerical witness is at floating-point tolerance rather than exact symbolic equality.  The spectral radius row records $0.6752479308830213$, matching $\lambda_M$ in the supplied tolerance.  This supports a stable local numerical signature for the observed tile, while leaving its biological interpretation confined to codon-label readback.

\paragraph{Shape significance.}
In the random-shape comparison with $N=2000$ samples, seed $42$, the run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$.  The resulting empirical values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite rows therefore make the local shape unusual under that sampling regime.  A second null model preserving amino-acid classes used $N=2000$ and seed $42$, with observed $M$-size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and lower-tail count $7$.  Its reported values are $p=0.0$ for exact shape and $p=0.0035$ for the lower-tail event.  These numbers strengthen the claim that the local code-read tile is not a generic byproduct of the tested randomizations, but they still do not promote the tile to translation, structure, function, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out behavior.}
Across $23$ evaluated code tables and $63$ total targets, the leave-one-code-out row reports a baseline hit rate of $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary is $0.9841269841269841$.  The family-stratified row covers $5$ families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial/plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$.  These rows say that the local boundary information is predictive for the code-label perturbation test as measured here.  They do not say that the same geometry performs translation, selects a folded structure, or licenses a mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out behavior.}
The leave-one-codon-out run reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  The module classification is complete: $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons, and no unassigned codons.  The six modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has either predictable or boundary status in the supplied row.  This is the strongest finite support for the local-tile reading: all thirteen boundary codons are accounted for by named modules, and the leave-one-codon probe recovers its held-out codon in the reported test.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow.  Window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary tile whose codon labels can be checked against the curated standard-code table.  The table is a contact for codon-to-label assignment only.  Amino-acid label sequences obtained from that table are not protein structures, and codon-label regularities are not observations of folding, physical admissibility, function, translation realization, mechanism, or cross-organism universality.  Any claim crossing one of those layers requires a separate contact that measures that layer directly.  In the present record, the finite rows justify a local code-read claim and block the higher biological promotions.
