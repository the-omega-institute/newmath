\subsection{Q6 Translation--Protein Mediated Localization Readout}
\label{subsec:q6-translation-protein-mediated-localization-readout-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite row under examination.}
The record concerns the claim identifier \texttt{q6.translation-protein-mediated.localization-readout.cross-layer}.  Its carrier is a finite same-row coding-sequence panel in which a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate is not read alone: the same row must also carry a matched translation readout, a matched protein-abundance readout, an mRNA control, and an externally attached localization bin for the corresponding protein.  The available self-check is deliberately narrow.  The packet-level self-check \texttt{verified\_facts.self.claim} reports experiment run \texttt{06f57f988c93}, verified at \texttt{2026-06-24T19:58:25+00:00}, with \texttt{checked\_gate\_count}=3 and the boundary statement that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  The conjecture-level record \texttt{conjecture.verified\_facts.self.claim} repeats the same run identifier, timestamp, gate count, and boundary, so the finite row has an internal consistency check but not an external localization effect estimate.

\paragraph{Cross-layer tuple.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the admissible observation is a tuple $(q_i,T_j,P_k,L_l)$ formed on one matched biological row.  Here $T_j$ is the translation surface, typically ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency after the mRNA denominator is supplied; $P_k$ is the matched protein-abundance surface; and $L_l$ is a localization label, score, compartment distribution, fractionation enrichment, imaging readout, or proximity-labeling bin attached through the same organism and protein/CDS identifier chain.  The intended statistic is not a direct map from codon residual geometry to localization.  It is a mediation question: any localization readback must pass through the translation-to-protein sequence of contacts before it is interpreted as a bounded localization association.

\paragraph{Controls and rank condition.}
The finite row separates the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate from amino-acid composition, GC3, coding length, methionine-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance.  These controls are part of the row identity rather than optional annotations.  The rank condition asks whether the localization signal assigned to $q_i$ is removed by the measured translation and protein-abundance mediators, or whether a residual localization association remains after those mediators are included.  In schematic notation, the row is acceptable only if the coordinate, controls, mediators, and localization bin are simultaneously present:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{row}_r &= (q_i,\mathrm{AA}_r,\mathrm{GC3}_r,\ell_r,M_r,\mathrm{mRNA}_r,T_j,P_k,L_l),\\
\mathrm{admissible}(r) &\Rightarrow T_j\ \mathrm{matched\ to}\ q_i\ \mathrm{and}\ P_k\ \mathrm{matched\ to}\ T_j\ \mathrm{and}\ L_l\ \mathrm{matched\ to}\ P_k.
\end{aligned}
$$
The checked gate count of $3$ should therefore be read as an internal boundary audit of the row grammar, not as a count of biological confirmations.

\paragraph{Reality contacts.}
The usable contacts are heterogeneous and must not be collapsed.  Codon-usage tables provide sequence-composition summaries, while tAI or stAI weights provide modeled codon-adaptation annotations.  Ribosome-profiling contacts supply per-CDS ribosome protected fragments or translation efficiency when paired with mRNA abundance.  Proteomics contacts supply protein abundance by accession or mapped CDS-derived protein identifier.  Localization contacts add externally curated or measured compartment information, imaging signal, fractionation enrichment, or proximity-labeling readout.  Each contact has a different noise model: codon tables reflect sampling and annotation choices; tAI/stAI are modeled rather than directly observed dynamics; ribosome profiling carries stalling, library-preparation, isoform, and normalization biases; proteomics carries turnover, ionization, preparation, and isoform-aggregation biases; localization carries condition specificity, antibody or tag artifacts, compartment coarseness, contamination, labeling radius, and identifier loss.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
The row supports only a bounded localization-readout candidate.  It does not show translation realization unless the translation contact is present on the same row.  It does not show protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, fitness, trafficking mechanism, compartment identity mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Protein abundance is only a mediator surface, not a protein-state claim.  Localization labels are only localization readouts, not evidence of why a protein localizes or what it does after localization.  Modeled tAI or stAI coupling alone is also insufficient; it can help describe a translation-layer annotation, but it cannot replace measured translation, protein abundance, and localization contacts.

\paragraph{Break condition and null status.}
The packet breaks if it treats codon/window geometry, modeled adaptation weights, protein-abundance association, or gate-pass status as evidence for PTM, stability, localization mechanism, folding, structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, or global law.  It also breaks if the localization association is reported without the same-row translation readout, matched mRNA control, and protein-abundance mediator.  With the present finite data, the null status is not a biological failure; it is a disciplined non-promotion.  The row grammar is internally checked by run \texttt{06f57f988c93} with three checked gates, but no $R$ list, $M$ list, lambda value, p-value, hit rate, row count, or module classification is supplied here.  Thus the chapter records the admissible cross-layer test form and its prohibitions, while withholding any quantitative localization-survival claim.
