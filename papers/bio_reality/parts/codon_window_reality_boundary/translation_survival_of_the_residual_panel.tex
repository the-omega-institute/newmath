\subsection{Translation survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix-translation-realization}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim being tested.}
The object of this record is the survival matrix $S_{ij}$ obtained by placing the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates against translation-related readouts after quotienting away the amino-acid sequence itself.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For a readout $r_j$, the statistic is evaluated only after residualizing against the declared controls $Z$: amino-acid composition, organism context where applicable, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the baseline density on the union support of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  A positive entry means that the residual coordinate contributes description-length or partial-$R^2$ improvement after those controls; it does not mean that the coordinate is a molecular cause.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate and proxy-coupling contact.}
A three-organism codon-count and tRNA-copy contact was computed for Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12.  In the $61$-codon count space, all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals were present, and the proxy coupling test against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum did not meet the null condition.  The null was defined as $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for every coordinate; the observed maximum absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $9$ nonzero entries.  This establishes that the residual panel is not numerically silent against this translation-adjacent proxy.  The proxy row is deliberately weaker than a survival row: it uses codon counts and tRNA gene-copy annotations, not a measured ribosome-occupancy, elongation, or protein-output surface.

\paragraph{Powered modeled translation-adaptation survival.}
The strongest finite-row survival result in the current packet is the $40$-organism modeled tAI computation.  It uses per-codon modeled tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, projected into the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates.  The residualized control design had $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and $17$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$.  The residual coordinate block had full rank $9$, and the degeneracy threshold was fixed at $0.999999$.  The computed translation-adaptation survival surface has shape $9 \times 9$ and total controlled fit
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} = 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-entry threshold used by the run was $10^{-12}$.  Since the design is rank-sufficient and $R^2_{QT}$ is below the degeneracy cutoff, this supplies a finite, powered survival readout for modeled translation adaptation.  The organisms in this row include human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, yeast, Escherichia coli, chicken, multiple Burkholderia, Mycobacterium, Pseudomonas, and cyanobacterial entries, giving an organism count of $40$ rather than the earlier three-row Leu-only surface.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR finite-row gate.}
The CUN/UUR leucine gate is retained as a low-rank contact, not as population evidence.  Across Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, the recorded partial Pearson correlation between log CUN/UUR codon usage and log tRNA-Leu copy ratio was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{partial} = 1.0, \qquad n = 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The signs of the log ratios were $+1,-1,+1$, with values $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  Thus the gate is a useful finite contact and also a warning: the sign is not uniform across organisms, and no universal direction is being inferred from it.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance and complement readbacks.}
Separate abundance-facing computations show that the translation-adaptation component does not absorb all $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance-associated signal.  For six organism-level rows, the modeled-tAI-complement fraction $d$ of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component was
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{Danio} &= 0.874628721049266,\\
 d_{Ecoli} &= 0.6629960038578532,\\
 d_{Gallus} &= 0.9400341806302775,\\
 d_{Human} &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 d_{Mycobacterium} &= 0.8972672018746489,\\
 d_{Yeast} &= 0.21173219572706703.
\end{aligned}
$$
The complementary mediated fractions $m=1-d$ were correspondingly $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  The associated abundance fits $R^2_{QP}$ were $0.04613529805760884$ for zebrafish, $0.10767526667768725$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.03980793595345331$ for chicken, $0.04454565117192484$ for human, $0.032090091625816136$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis, and $0.29837624987287115$ for yeast.  These values show organism-dependent residual structure after modeled translation adaptation, but they do not turn the translation-adaptation row into a protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Direction-aware supplementary readout ranking.}
The supplementary readout ranking used a stated regularization parameter $\lambda=0.01$ and a description-length cost $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{col})$.  For single-column readouts this gives $DL_H=1.0$; for the twelve-column localization readout the cost was $3.700439718141092$.  In zebrafish, measured translation efficiency had $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, score $0.001566051846174291$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, null $95\%$ level $0.0003297458343745959$, and $13140$ joined rows.  Zebrafish mRNA was also positive by this score, with $A_HC_H=0.01073458857797759$, score $0.000734588577977589$, threshold $0.000587830414454545$, and the same $13140$ joined rows.  In Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 neither measured TE nor mRNA passed the same scoring rule: the measured-TE score was $-0.009446333814243976$ on $3415$ joined rows, and the mRNA score was $-0.008470202379021127$ on $3415$ joined rows.  In human, the measured-TE row exceeded its Bonferroni threshold but had negative coordinate-support sign, with $A_H=0.002894714925796667$, $C_H=-0.10813537216189718$, and score $-0.01031302107580362$ on $11554$ joined rows.  These rows are useful for prioritizing follow-up contacts, but they are not a replacement for the declared $S_{ij}$ survival matrix.

\paragraph{Condition and tissue abundance rows.}
The yeast condition-specific abundance computation joined $2209$ shared proteins in SD and YEPD conditions.  Both condition designs used $24$ control columns, rank $23$, full residual coordinate rank $9$, and $2186$ residual degrees of freedom after controls.  The controlled abundance fits were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(SD) &= 0.3050385538305022,\\
R^2_{QP}(YEPD) &= 0.3280825603424665,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $f3\_stress$ dominant in both conditions.  The condition comparison had cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ over $1000$ permutations.  In the human tissue abundance computation, the joined row counts were $9244$ for adult B cells, $6468$ for adult heart, and $8664$ for adult liver.  The corresponding controlled fits were $0.03626117434944674$, $0.02320896321347296$, and $0.026133456228248052$, with dominant coordinates Arg\_AGR, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, and Ile\_AUA.  Pairwise tissue cosines were $0.6883655232187615$ for B cells versus heart, $0.8990093450963941$ for liver versus B cells, and $0.9176793373754919$ for liver versus heart.  These abundance rows show that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual structure can survive in protein-abundance measurements under the same style of controls, but they sit downstream of the translation-readout claim.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite record supports the following bounded statement: the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel has a nondegenerate, rank-powered survival surface against modeled translation-adaptation readouts after amino-acid, organism, $GC3$, length, and baseline-density controls, with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ over $40$ organisms and a $9\times 9$ survival matrix.  The three-organism Leu gate and the proxy coupling matrix are retained as supporting contact geometry, not as population-level laws.  The abundance and complement computations indicate downstream residual structure and organism-specific heterogeneity, but they do not complete a ribosome-measured or protein-mediated mechanism chain.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
No statement in this subsection asserts measured ribosome-mediated translation unless a ribosome-profiling or measured-TE contact is joined to the same codon rows and evaluated under the declared controls.  No statement here asserts folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  tRNA gene-copy counts are not treated as tRNA abundance or charging.  A proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to a positive survival score $S_{ij}$ without the controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length computation.  Protein abundance rows are treated as downstream statistical readouts only, and any higher-layer interpretation requires its own matched contact and its own statistic.
