\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Protein-Stability Boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:q6-translation-conditioned-protein-stability-boundary-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The object considered here is a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each coding sequence carries residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates, matched translation-layer readouts where available, mRNA controls, protein abundance contacts where available, and a downstream protein-stability or degradation-rate readout only when such a readout is separately attached. The intended claim is not that synonymous residual geometry determines protein stability. The admissible statement is narrower: a coordinate $q_i$ may be discussed at a protein-stability boundary only after passing through a controlled translation readout $T_j$, and any downstream protein-stability signal must be reported as a residual association conditioned on that translation readout.

The computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis used by the adjacent finite tests has nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each coordinate is interpreted only after amino-acid quotient residualization. The control design therefore includes an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from observed CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors. These controls are part of the mathematical object, not later decoration: without them, a synonymous-coordinate statement can collapse into amino-acid composition, length, GC3, or support-density effects.

\paragraph{Rank gate before downstream interpretation.}
The residual coordinate block is rank-powered for the structural-order contact in the two organisms for which the finite calculation was reported. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the controlled design has $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, residual response energy $136607.52873622958$, and explained energy $4178.935925251874$. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the analogous numbers are $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, residual response energy $620506.1490869389$, and explained energy $44421.14342043593$. Thus the nine-coordinate residual block is not numerically erased by the stated controls in those panels.

The structural-order readout already computed is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0\ldots 100$, and it is used only as predicted structure-confidence score. For \emph{E. coli}, $3739$ codon-joined rows were available, $3736$ matched proteins were used, and the join hit rate was $0.999198$. For yeast, $5815$ codon-joined rows were available, $5804$ matched proteins were used, and the join hit rate was $0.998108$. After the same control family, the reported structural-order residual associations were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both organisms retained all nine residual coordinates under a positive partial-$R^2$ threshold of $10^{-12}$. These numbers show finite cross-sectional association with a predicted confidence readout. They do not establish translation mediation, protein folding, protein stability, degradation rate, biological function, phenotype, or a causal pathway.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned stability rule.}
Let $q_i$ denote one of the nine amino-acid-residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates, let $T_j$ denote a measured translation readout such as organism-matched translation efficiency, and let $P_k$ denote a protein-stability or degradation-rate readout when such a measurement exists for the same row universe. The admissible boundary test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,\\
P_k &\sim Z + T_j + q_i,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism scope, GC3, length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls. A downstream protein-stability entry is reportable only if the translation-layer entry has first survived the controlled test. The stability-side quantity is then the residual contribution of $q_i$ after $T_j$ is present in the model, not a direct codon-to-stability law.

The measured-readout compilation test supplies additional scale for this caution. In the four computed organisms, the residual source energy $\|P_Q\|^2$ and final residual fraction were reported as follows: zebrafish had $n=13140$, $\|P_Q\|^2=605.0092168669606$, final residual fraction $0.9756319042010854$, and compressibility $0.024368095798914635$; \emph{E. coli} had $n=2517$, $\|P_Q\|^2=225.84556319953032$, final residual fraction $0.9942823785118229$, and compressibility $0.005717621488177116$; human had $n=11250$, $\|P_Q\|^2=286.3673197177688$, final residual fraction $0.9736442326306781$, and compressibility $0.02635576736932188$; yeast had $n=4759$, $\|P_Q\|^2=229.86450794006544$, final residual fraction $0.8676586345294415$, and compressibility $0.13234136547055853$. Each organism used permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$ in that descriptive test. The median compressibility was $0.026$, so the available low-dimensional readout set did not strongly compress the $P_Q$ residual source. This supports a conservative boundary: the residual geometry remains largely unabsorbed by the measured readouts used there, and therefore a protein-stability claim needs its own matched stability contact rather than borrowing force from translation, modeled codon adaptation, or predicted structure confidence.

\paragraph{Observed contacts and non-observed layers.}
The attached contacts are sufficient to define sequence composition, modelled codon-adaptation weights, ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, and organism-matched protein abundance. Codon-usage resources provide per-organism coding-sequence summaries; tAI or stAI weights provide modelled translation-layer annotations; ribosome profiling with matched RNA-seq can define $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$; proteomics contacts can provide steady-state abundance keyed through protein identifiers. None of these contacts, by itself, observes protein-stability half-life, degradation rate, folding kinetics, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or a universal rule.

Consequently, this chapter records a boundary condition rather than a completed stability result. A protein-stability association is admissible only on rows where $q_i$, $T_j$, mRNA control, protein abundance when used, and an actual stability or degradation readout are jointly available. If all controlled $q_i \to T_j$ entries fail, no protein-stability interpretation is allowed downstream. If a translation entry survives but the direct $q_i \to P_k$ association remains unchanged after conditioning on $T_j$, the result is not a translation-mediated mechanism. If conditioning on $T_j$ reduces or reorganizes the $q_i \to P_k$ association, the reportable object is still a finite residual mediation pattern, not a folding mechanism or a phenotype.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence currently supports only a gated frontier claim. The panel has a concrete nine-coordinate residual basis, controlled rank diagnostics in two structural-confidence panels, cross-organism residual-compressibility numbers, and explicit contacts for codon usage, translation annotation, ribosome profiling, matched mRNA, and proteomics abundance. It does not yet contain a matched protein-stability or degradation-rate measurement. Therefore no translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ at this layer unless an independent contact supplies that layer and the controlled translation-conditioned test is passed on the same finite rows.
