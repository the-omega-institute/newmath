\subsection{Rank-Powered Translation-Readout Survival for B$^{*}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:q6-rank-powered-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The object studied here is a finite organism-by-coordinate readout table for the synonymous residual coordinates denoted $B^{*}_{Q6}$.  Each coordinate is interpreted only after amino-acid-sequence projection: if $q_i$ is a raw synonymous coordinate and $P_{\tau}$ is the amino-acid-composition projection, then the available coordinate is the residual component $q_i-P_{\tau}q_i$.  The tested readout is not protein output and not ribosome occupancy.  It is a modeled translation-weight table obtained from organism-level codon usage, tRNA gene-copy counts, and tAI or stAI-style codon adaptation weights.  The powered panel contains $40$ organisms, including human, Arabidopsis, grape, zebrafish, yeast, Escherichia coli, Xenopus, chicken, several Burkholderia and Mycobacterium entries, and additional bacterial and eukaryotic rows.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual side has $9$ coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The modeled tAI readout side uses the same $9$ projected columns, so the controlled survival array has shape $9\times 9$.

\paragraph{Controlled survival score.}
For a coordinate $q_i$ and a translation-weight readout $T_j$, the reported quantity is a controlled survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$.  It is evaluated only after removing the design matrix $Z_T$ containing amino-acid composition, organism-level controls, GC3, length, M-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.  In the powered run, $Z_T$ has $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and leaves $17$ residual degrees of freedom.  The residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block has rank $9$.  The scalar coupled fit reported for the residualized $Q\to T$ surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z_T) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_Q) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run therefore passes the rank screen used here: the organism count is $40$, the residual coordinate rank is not collapsed, and the coupled $R^2_{QT}$ is below the declared degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$.  The positive-entry threshold for partial-$R^2$ survival is $10^{-12}$.  Thus the chapter records a powered modeled-translation readout contact rather than the earlier three-organism boundary case.

\paragraph{Three-row boundary.}
A smaller finite check used only $3$ organisms: human, budding yeast, and Escherichia coli K-12.  That check had all standard amino-acid tRNA families represented in the local GtRNAdb-style payloads: $432$ matched human FASTA headers out of $432$, $275$ yeast headers out of $275$, and $87$ matched Escherichia coli headers out of $89$, with $2$ unmatched Escherichia coli headers.  For each of the three organisms the modeled tAI computation covered $61$ sense codons with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$.  The bounded coupling table had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This smaller table is not treated as survival evidence, because $n=3$ can project the residual design into an all-$1.0$ or otherwise saturated geometry.  It is retained only as a finite sanity check for codon-weight construction and for the break rule requiring at least $40$ matched organisms before powered survival is considered.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The powered result says that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ has nontrivial controlled contact with a modeled tAI translation-weight surface after amino-acid quotienting and baseline residualization.  It does not say that a particular coordinate is a universal law of translation.  In particular, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ may be listed as one of the nine residual coordinates, but it is not promoted to a population-level rule merely by appearing in the basis.  The support is matrix-level and control-dependent: the meaningful object is $S^{QT}_{ij}$ after $Z_T$ residualization, not an unadjusted codon-count association.

\paragraph{Local stopping rule.}
The claim would fail at this layer if every controlled survival entry satisfied $S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0$, or if the apparent signal required a saturated rank geometry.  It would also fail if the readout were used to infer a layer that it does not observe.  The present finite row gives modeled codon-adaptation contact only.  No measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological principle follows from this table unless an additional contact directly measures that layer.  Positive $S^{QT}$ behavior is therefore a bounded modeled-translation readout statement, not evidence for $S^{QP}$ protein survival and not evidence for any three-layer mechanism $M^{QTP}$.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
Within the powered $40$-organism panel, the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate block and the modeled tAI readout block form a rank-adequate finite surface: $9$ residual coordinates, $9$ readout columns, $23$-rank controls, $17$ residual degrees of freedom, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ under the declared nonsaturation cutoff.  The earlier $3$-organism coupling table remains a construction check and a warning against rank-degenerate survival.  The chapter therefore records a ready modeled translation-readout survival claim, with the biological scope limited to controlled codon-usage and tRNA-copy-derived adaptation weights.
