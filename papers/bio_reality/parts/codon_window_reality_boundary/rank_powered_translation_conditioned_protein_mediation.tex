\subsection{Rank-powered translation-conditioned protein mediation}
\label{sec:q6-rank-powered-translation-conditioned-protein-mediation-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record tests whether the nine residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ survive simultaneously against a measured translation readout and a matched protein-abundance readout, and whether the apparent $q_i \to P$ association is reduced when the measured translation coordinate is supplied.  The finite coordinate row is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  Each fit residualizes the codon-coordinate matrix, translation readout, and protein readout against an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the powered rows the control matrix has $24$ columns, effective control rank $23$, and the residualized $Q$ matrix has rank $9$ rather than a degenerate all-one boundary.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance survival.}
The direct protein-omics row uses $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ from PAXdb-style measured protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The direct $Q \to P$ survival is positive in every listed organism after the controls, with $15$ powered organism rows.  The finite $R^2_{QP}$ list is $0.061906$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $0.102179$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.046135$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.081418$ for \emph{Dictyostelium discoideum}, $0.077095$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.107675$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.039808$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $0.019257$ for \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}, $0.044546$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $0.049828$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $0.032090$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $0.064647$ for \emph{Pseudomonas aeruginosa} PAO1, $0.027640$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $0.298376$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $0.063975$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each of these rows reports all $9$ coordinates as surviving above the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  The corresponding joined row counts are $4042$, $11661$, $14410$, $6974$, $13295$, $3739$, $12418$, $1747$, $19053$, $19395$, $4129$, $5006$, $13322$, $5815$, and $1656$, respectively; the reported join hit rates range from $0.823159$ in rat to $1.000000$ in danio, chicken, halobacterium, mycobacterium, and sulfolobus.

\paragraph{Measured translation survival.}
A separate measured-translation row uses $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ defined from ribosome footprint divided by mRNA in local ribosome-profiling sources.  Four organisms have powered measured-TE rows: danio, \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast.  Their measured translation survivals are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.040342,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.009394,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.018838,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.136819.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured-TE joins use $13140$ danio genes out of $14410$ CDS rows, $3415$ \emph{E. coli} genes out of $3739$ CDS rows, $11554$ human genes out of $19053$ CDS rows, and $5128$ yeast genes out of $5815$ CDS rows.  The corresponding ribosome-TE hit rates are $0.911867$, $0.913346$, $0.606414$, and $0.881857$.  These translation entries are observational, condition-specific, and already mRNA-normalized by construction; a positive row therefore means residual association with a measured TE readout, not a universal translation law.

\paragraph{Measured mediation row.}
For the same four measured-TE organisms, the protein-abundance association is decomposed by conditioning on residualized measured $TE$.  The measured mediation row is summarized by
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
S_{QT} &= R^2(T_e \sim Q_e),\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= \frac{S_{QP}-S_{QP\mid T}}{S_{QP}},
\end{aligned}
$$
with truncation to the unit interval for the reported descriptive fraction.  The finite measured fractions are $0.000000$ in danio from a raw suppressive value $-0.048015$, $0.040912$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.002797$ in human, and $0.479307$ in yeast.  The paired $S_{QP}$ values in this three-way join are $0.049266$, $0.109415$, $0.042885$, and $0.314682$, while the matched $S_{QT}$ values are $0.040342$, $0.009394$, $0.018838$, and $0.136819$.  The three-way row counts are $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$.  Thus the strongest measured translation-conditioned absorption in this finite row is the yeast entry, where roughly $47.93\%$ of the direct abundance association is absorbed by the measured TE coordinate; the human and \emph{E. coli} rows are positive but small, and danio is suppressive before truncation.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The modeled-tAI discriminant provides a separate two-organism readback using modeled per-protein tAI rather than ribosome-profiling TE.  In \emph{E. coli}, the modeled-tAI row has $R^2_{QT}=0.541674$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.107675$ over $3739$ joined proteins; the case table is $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=7$, with $2$ positive-positive coordinates lacking a mediation drop.  In yeast, the modeled-tAI row has $R^2_{QT}=0.821346$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.298376$ over $5815$ proteins; the case table is $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$, with no positive-positive coordinate left without a mediation drop.  This table is a finite classification of sign and conditioning behavior.  It is not a claim that modeled tAI is ribosome occupancy, nor that a modeled-tAI absorption row identifies a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{mRNA-stability mediation.}
The joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability row separates two possible downstream absorbers of the $Q \to P$ signal.  In \emph{E. coli}, $R^2_{QP}=0.118174$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.115840$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.090933$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.089757$; the TE-only fraction is $0.019755$, the stability-only fraction is $0.230521$, and the joint fraction is $0.240467$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999$.  In human, $R^2_{QP}=0.042742$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.042507$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.035522$; the TE-only fraction is $0.005495$, the stability-only fraction is $0.153147$, and the joint fraction is $0.168907$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999$.  In yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.271552$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.143999$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.254536$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.130476$; the TE-only fraction is $0.469719$, the stability-only fraction is $0.062662$, and the joint fraction is $0.519518$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999$.  The four-way joined row counts are $2517$ for \emph{E. coli}, $11322$ for human, and $4759$ for yeast.  The overlap diagnostics are approximately additive within tolerance in all three rows, with joint-minus-single-sum values $-0.009809$, $0.010265$, and $-0.012862$.

\paragraph{Non-translation residual checks.}
Two downstream checks prevent the translation-conditioned result from being over-read.  The localization projection tests whether the modeled-tAI complement abundance residual is absorbed by multi-label subcellular localization columns.  Human has $16008$ joined rows and localization absorption $0.009255$ against a degree-of-freedom matched null $95\%$ value $0.001376$; yeast has $4656$ joined rows and absorption $0.011225$ against null $95\%$ value $0.004958$; danio has $1984$ joined rows and absorption $0.009509$ against null $95\%$ value $0.011321$.  The human turnover check joins $8057$ proteins, uses Mathieson-style half-life data, and gives turnover absorption $3.5741\times 10^{-6}$ with a null $95\%$ value $0.000434$; this does not exceed its matched null.  These rows show that residual protein-abundance structure can be queried outside translation, but only as descriptive projection evidence.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and boundary.}
The finite data support a rank-powered cross-layer readback: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive against direct protein abundance in $15$ organisms and against measured TE in four organisms, and the measured translation-conditioned absorption is substantial in yeast, weak in \emph{E. coli} and human, and absent after truncation in danio.  The strongest positive mediation row is yeast, where $M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.479307$ and the joint TE-plus-stability fraction is $0.519518$.  The row does not license any further biological promotion.  No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical admissibility statement, molecular function, causal pathway, organismal phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from these residual matrices unless an additional measured contact at that exact layer supplies it.  Direct $S^{QP}$ is therefore retained as protein-omics prediction, measured $S^{QT}$ as translation-readout survival, and $M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}$ as a finite observational conditioning statistic rather than a causal mechanism.
