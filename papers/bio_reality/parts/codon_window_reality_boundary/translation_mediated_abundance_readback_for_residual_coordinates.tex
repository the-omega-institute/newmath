\subsection{Translation-mediated abundance readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The claim is evaluated on a finite mediation surface whose rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate $t_j$ used in the powered abundance computation is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as the codon-count-weighted mean of codon tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The abundance coordinate $p_k$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The residualization controls are the intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the union support of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ directions.  Thus amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, length, and $M$-density stay in the control layer rather than becoming promoted explanatory coordinates.

\paragraph{Observed row support.}
The powered mediation run joined twelve organism panels: 4042 proteins for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, 11661 for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, 14410 for \emph{Danio rerio}, 13295 for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, 3739 for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, 12418 for \emph{Gallus gallus}, 19053 for \emph{Homo sapiens}, 19395 for \emph{Mus musculus}, 4129 for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, 13322 for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, 5815 for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and 1656 for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each organism had rank-sufficient residualized design matrices: the $Q$ block had rank $9$, the modeled-tAI block had rank $1$, the joint $Q,T$ block had rank $10$, and the control block had rank $23$ in every organism-specific panel.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, so the computation is not a single-row or single-organism anecdote.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the recorded scalar is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $Q$, $T$, and $P$ first residualized against the stated controls.  The numerator is the attenuation of the residual-to-protein association after the modeled translation readout is conditioned upon.  The denominator is the total residual-to-protein association before conditioning on $T$.  The resulting mediated fractions are all inside $[0,1]$: $0.608459$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.487227$ in \emph{C. elegans}, $0.315705$ in zebrafish, $0.616517$ in fly, $0.612922$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.197690$ in chicken, $0.272063$ in human, $0.154888$ in mouse, $0.083976$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, $0.174897$ in rat, $0.949155$ in yeast, and $0.018507$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.  The largest mediated fraction is therefore yeast, where $R^2(Q\to P)=0.298376$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.015171$, and the indirect component is $0.283205$.  The smallest mediated fraction is \emph{S. solfataricus}, where $R^2(Q\to P)=0.063975$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.062791$, and the indirect component is $0.001184$.

\paragraph{Translation contact before protein readback.}
A direct $Q\to P$ association is not accepted as protein-level realization unless the same finite surface contains a translation step.  The supporting translation boundary was independently checked on modeled tAI: three organisms had all standard amino-acid tRNA families represented, the modeled codon readout covered all $61$ sense codons in \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast, and the bounded-coupling matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$.  The maximum absolute modeled coupling was $3801961.031607896$.  This establishes only a bounded modeled-tAI contact; it is explicitly not ribosome profiling and not a measured translation-efficiency validation, because the organism count there was $3$ rather than a powered multi-species panel of at least $40$.

\paragraph{Relation to measured yeast structure and translation controls.}
A separate yeast five-way join gives a useful local check on the same family of residual coordinates.  That panel joined $4759$ genes, with $5128$ valid TE-by-protein records, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records, and $6027$ valid 5-prime coding-region MFE records.  In that yeast variance partition, $R^2(Q\to P)=0.271552$, while $R^2(Q\to P\mid S)=0.270660$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T,M)=0.137740$, and $R^2(Q\to P\mid T,M,S)=0.137590$.  The structure-only fraction was only $0.003285$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.000551$, and the right-tail permutation value for the structure increment was $0.125874$.  Thus the abundance signal is not being reinterpreted here as folded structure; the stronger attenuation in that yeast panel occurs after measured TE plus mRNA-stability conditioning, while the 5-prime MFE proxy remains a small and non-causal variance-partition component.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded companion checks.}
The chapter keeps negative contacts in the same ledger.  A mass-balance RPF/protein closure run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals had $R^2_{L0}=0.996063$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996411$, $R^2_{L2}=0.971244$, $R^2_{L3}=0.895400$, $\Delta R^2=-0.075844$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.804607$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.683168$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.930693$; this is not a positive mass-balance promotion.  A turnover audit on $3290$ genes similarly gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055741$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050279$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005462$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.688197$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.885572$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$, so no protein-turnover extension is licensed.  A cross-ortholog imprint check on $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows reported $\Delta R^2=0.032505$ but leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.002148$ and null95 $0.054272$, hence no strong selection-imprint claim is supported.  In contrast, an observed-rate-slot decomposition on $3289$ yeast genes did pass as an observable execution subblock, and a structural-order axis analysis found $f3\_stress$ as the only significant axis, with sign-flip $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.003174$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is therefore a bounded cross-layer relation: the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a reproducible statistical mediation decomposition through modeled per-protein tAI into matched protein abundance across twelve organism panels, with all recorded $M^{QTP}$ fractions lying in the unit interval and with rank-sufficient residualized matrices.  The strongest organism-level attenuation is yeast and the weakest is \emph{S. solfataricus}; neither extreme is promoted into a universal biological law.  The chapter does not infer measured ribosome occupancy from modeled tAI, does not infer folding or structural mechanism from protein abundance, does not infer physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality, and does not treat a direct $Q\to P$ association as sufficient unless a translation readout is explicitly present.  Those stronger readings require distinct layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.
