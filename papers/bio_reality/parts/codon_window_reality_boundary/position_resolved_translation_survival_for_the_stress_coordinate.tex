\subsection{Position-Resolved Translation Survival for the $f_3$-Stress Coordinate}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite row being tested.}
The object of this record is a finite coding-sequence window table, not a free-standing biological law.  Each admissible row must contain an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate computed after quotienting by amino-acid identity, the local synonymous codon counts in the same window, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA abundance control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates.  The proposed cross-layer entry is therefore not the statement that $f_3$-stress causes translation.  It is the weaker and testable statement that the residual coordinate may survive as a controlled predictor only when the same coding windows contain the translation-layer readback and when the wobble context breaks the $f_3$ symmetry.

\paragraph{Coordinate and survival statistic.}
For a window table $T$, let $q_{f_3}(x)$ denote the residual $f_3$ coordinate for row $x$ after amino-acid quotienting.  Let $Y_{\mathrm{pos}}(x)$ be the matched codon-indexed or local-window ribosome-occupancy readout, or a translation-efficiency readout formed from ribosome footprints with the corresponding mRNA control.  Let $Z(x)$ collect amino-acid composition, position bin, $\mathrm{GC3}$, length, methionine density, mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability controls, and simpler codon-usage baselines.  The survival entry is the held-out gain of the $f_3$ coordinate over the controlled baseline:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
  &= \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(Y_{\mathrm{pos}}; q_{f_3} \mid Z,\, \mathrm{baseline}\bigr),\\
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z) &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
with the strict inequality interpreted after the declared ablations.  A positive apparent effect without the same-row ribosome or translation-efficiency readout is not counted.  A positive apparent effect without the matched mRNA control is also not counted, because transcript abundance and translation-layer occupancy are different contacts.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry requirement.}
The coordinate is only meaningful for this claim when the wobble-weight surface is observed in a context where $w_c \ne w_c^{f_3}$.  This asymmetry is not a decoration on the model: it is the condition that prevents the $f_3$ residual from collapsing into a codon-composition or amino-acid-bin surrogate.  The admissible table must therefore contain both the residual coordinate and the wobble-weight context before the rank test is evaluated.  If the table has codon counts but no bounded wobble surface, the row can support a codon-usage analysis, but it cannot support this position-resolved $f_3$-stress survival claim.

\paragraph{Finite evidence already available.}
Two related finite checks are recorded, but neither is promoted into the missing position-resolved ribosome-occupancy test.  The finite-state dwell-transducer check for the $B^*_Q6$ order-memory execution signal has status $\mathrm{passed}$ under experiment run $26912bc3e41e$.  Separately, the translation-conditioned structural-order boundary check has status $\mathrm{passed}$ under run $1b97387835f7$ with $5$ folds and minimum joined count $300$.  Its joined structural-order rows were $3412$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In that check, the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ over the two organism calls had
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\mathrm{median} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\max &= 0.007880037229394898,\\
\mathrm{mean} &= 0.005284160876443678,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $2$ positive calls and $0$ negative calls.  The weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ was $0.004764742175882462$, and the sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect was $0.0029296875$.  The permuted full-control comparison was negative in both organism calls, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, median $-0.00212186632044159$, mean $-0.00212186632044159$, and maximum $-0.000779938262202795$.  The reported module classification for that check was $\mathrm{crosses\_to\_structural\_order\_layer}$.  These values justify keeping the $B^*_Q6$ family under active biological readout testing, while still leaving the $f_3$ position-resolved ribosome-occupancy row unproven until its own same-window statistic is supplied.

\paragraph{Readout contacts.}
The primary contact is a same-organism, same-CDS ribosome-footprint table keyed by transcript or coding-sequence identifier, codon position, profiling sample, and local window.  Such a table may represent codon-indexed occupancy or fixed-window occupancy after frame assignment, mappability filtering, coverage normalization, and start/stop-edge handling.  A matched mRNA abundance contact is required in the same experimental condition, because translation efficiency must separate ribosome footprint signal from transcript supply.  Organism-level tAI or stAI weights may be used as bounded codon-adaptation annotations, but they are not observations of ribosome movement.  Per-organism codon-usage summaries may describe sequence composition, but they do not by themselves observe translation, protein amount, folding, function, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Rank and ablation rule.}
The row passes only if the $f_3$ entry remains positive under rank-sensitive evaluation and after the controls named in the row are present.  The minimum practical break condition is that the improvement disappears, reverses, or becomes indistinguishable from the simpler baseline once amino-acid composition, position bin, $\mathrm{GC3}$, length, methionine density, mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, wobble weights, and local codon counts are included.  In symbols, the proposed row is rejected for this contact whenever
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z) \le 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the held-out and ablation checks.  A modeled tAI or stAI association cannot replace this inequality, because modeled adaptation weights are not the same datum as position-resolved footprint occupancy.

\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
This record does not claim translation realization from codon geometry alone.  It does not claim protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal directionality law.  Those statements would require their own matched contacts and their own survival rows.  The only admissible conclusion, if the missing window statistic later passes, is that the $f_3$-stress residual has a bounded cross-layer association with the specified position-resolved translation readout under the declared controls.  If the same-row ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency table is absent, the present record remains a disciplined conjectural boundary rather than an empirical survival result.
