\subsection{The $f_3$-stress Translation-Readout Conjecture}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This record isolates the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate as a finite residual surface on coding sequences, organisms, and matched translation readouts.  The coordinate is read only after amino-acid projection, so the comparison is not between amino-acid classes but between synonymous codons that remain separated by the $f_3$ operation.  The concrete saturation row fixes the relevant finite support: the mediated set $M = \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified $f_3$-saturation of $R$ removes exactly the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this mediated support:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The listed boundary outside this saturation row is empty.  Thus the conjectural stress is not an unbounded sequence property; it is a finite synonymous residual coordinate whose possible biological visibility must pass through measured translation-layer contacts.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The finite claim is that the $f_3$ coordinate can become visible to translation only when the empirical decoding context distinguishes an $f_3$-paired codon from its partner.  In notation, the local activation condition is the failure of symmetry
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $w_c$ denotes an organism-scoped tRNA, wobble, tAI, or stAI weight attached to codon $c$.  If the organism-level decoding weights are symmetric on the relevant $f_3$ pairs, the topological residual is only a coordinate distinction and has no licensed translation-readout force.  This is especially important because a rank-degenerate readout, including an all-constant three-organism matrix, is a null boundary rather than evidence for survival.

\paragraph{Finite empirical rows already supporting the mechanism screen.}
Several rows are already positive at the mechanism-screen level, while none yet supplies the mandatory translation-survival entry.  A tRNA-poverty computation over the $R$ sense codons passed at $\alpha=0.05$ with $1000$ permutations, using $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested and skipping $6$.  Its reported mechanism call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  A separate universal-core tRNA-adaptation row passed with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  A third powered row with the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $11$-of-$16$ organism, and $41$-column design reported the dominant residual axis as $f_3\_translation\_selection$, with the conclusion that non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most strongly with that axis.  These rows justify using decoding asymmetry as the next filter, but they do not by themselves establish a positive translation-readout survival score.

\paragraph{Readout definition.}
For each organism and each matched coding sequence row, the test residualizes the $f_3$-stress coordinate against amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, length, $M$-density, and mRNA-level controls.  Translation readouts may include tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, provided that the readout is kept separate from the coordinate definition.  The bounded survival entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is one translation-layer readout.  The conjecture is supported only when this entry is strictly positive after the stated controls and after ablation against simpler codon baselines.  It is falsified, for the available powered panel, if every controlled entry remains non-positive or if the rows collapse to a rank-degenerate matrix.

\paragraph{Empirical contacts required for interpretation.}
Codon-usage tables contribute organism-level codon counts or frequencies.  tRNA gene-copy annotations contribute anticodon-family supply summaries, especially for the Leu-relevant comparison families.  tAI or stAI weights contribute modelled translation-adaptation weights and may be used only as bounded translation annotations.  Ribosome-profiling data contribute per-CDS ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, ideally as $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$.  Matched mRNA measurements are required to separate a translation-layer residual from transcript abundance.  The contact layer therefore has two roles: it can make the wobble asymmetry condition non-vacuous, and it can provide the measured $T_j$ row on which $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ is computed.

\paragraph{Local negative scope.}
This chapter does not infer translation dynamics from codon geometry alone, and it does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or universality from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate.  A modelled tAI or stAI weight is not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement.  A tRNA-copy asymmetry is not by itself a protein-output measurement.  A positive mechanism-screen row is not a universal biological law.  Each stronger biological layer requires its own matched contact at that layer.  Until the controlled translation-survival entry is non-vacuous and positive, the record remains a precise conjecture about where the $f_3$ residual should and should not survive.
