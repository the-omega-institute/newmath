\paragraph{What is established.}
The ready claim is narrow.  The finite computation establishes a controlled statistical decomposition in which the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface has a nonzero $Q \to P$ association and a positive $Q \to T \to P$ attenuation component when $T$ is modeled per-protein tAI and $P$ is matched PAXdb protein abundance.  The twelve-organism matrix supplies the concrete row counts, ranks, residual degrees of freedom, and mediated fractions needed to treat this as a protein-abundance cross-layer relation at the level of controlled finite data.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter does not assert observed ribosome occupancy, causal translation control, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, organism-wide adaptation, or universality.  Modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation readout, not ribosome profiling.  PAXdb abundance is a protein-abundance measurement, not a direct assay of synthesis rate, turnover, localization, post-translational state, folding, or function.  A stronger statement at any one of those layers requires a separate matched contact at that layer and a fresh controlled statistic on the same finite-row surface.
