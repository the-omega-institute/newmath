\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ Translation-Survival Matrix}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object tested here is the $9\times K$ survival surface obtained by placing the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates against translation-facing readouts.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Each coordinate is evaluated only after quotienting by amino-acid composition and after residualizing against the declared controls: organism context where applicable, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  For a coordinate $q_i$ and a readout $r_j$, the reported quantity is the controlled improvement of the readout residual, not the raw codon-count contrast.  In the finite runs this is represented by the residual coupling $A_{ij}$ and by the survival score $S_{ij}$, with positive survival meaning that the $q_i$ direction improves the controlled readout model beyond the baseline.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling before survival.}
The first finite contact used a three-organism codon-count and tRNA-copy table for Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  In the $61$-codon count space, all nine synonymous residuals were computed, and the proxy coupling test against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum found $9$ nonzero entries above the stated $0.01$ threshold, with maximum absolute coupling
$1882464.1106377328$.  This breaks the all-small-coupling null for the proxy readout, but it is not itself a survival claim.  The same finite table recorded a CUN/UUR Leu gate with $n=3$, Pearson correlation $1.0$, and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$, while the log-ratio signs were $(1,-1,1)$.  Thus the finite row preserves a strong three-organism contact and, at the same time, refuses any universal sign law.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
The powered modeled-tAI computation supplies the strongest translation-layer survival evidence in this packet.  Across $40$ organisms, modeled per-codon tAI was computed from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The design used $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for the control space, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and full rank $9$ for the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ block.  The resulting $9\times 9$ modeled translation matrix had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{\sim Z}) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\mathrm{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positivity threshold for partial $R^2$ was $10^{-12}$, and the fit was below the degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$.  A wider organism sweep reached $41$ organisms and reported an $R^2_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$ over $1000$ permutations.  These numbers support a bounded translation-readout survival statement for modeled tAI/stAI-type weights, not a measured ribosome-occupancy or protein-output statement.

\paragraph{Measured ribosome-position contact.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy contact used ordered coding sequences and file-provided ribosome windows.  The join contained $5754$ overlapping genes, $5754$ genes after the length check, $6013$ ordered-CDS genes, and no length mismatches.  Occupancy effects were demeaned inside exact window/codon-index and amino-acid bins, and the null shuffled synonymous labels only inside the same bin.  All nine families had at least $200$ within-bin permutations.  With description-length penalty $DL=2$ and $\lambda=0.01$, the families passing the combined gate $C_H>0$, null exceedance, and penalty were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This is a direct finite contact with ribosome-position data, but it remains a descriptive synonymous-label separation in local occupancy windows rather than a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Protein and mRNA-side readouts.}
The downstream readouts show that the same nine-dimensional residual block is not merely a modeled-tAI artifact.  In yeast protein-abundance data, the YEPD and SD condition tables joined $2517$ and $2442$ rows respectively, with $2209$ shared proteins.  The controlled protein-abundance scores were $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD and $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD; both had $f_3$-stress as the dominant coordinate, rank $9$ for the residualized coordinate block, rank $23$ for the control block, and residual degrees of freedom $2186$.  The condition-level vectors were nearly collinear, with cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ over $1000$ permutations.

Human tissue proteomics gave three additional controlled surfaces: Adult B-cells joined $9244$ proteins with $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ and dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$; adult heart joined $6468$ proteins with $R^2_{QP}=0.02320896321347296$ and dominant coordinate $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$; adult liver joined $8664$ proteins with $R^2_{QP}=0.026133456228248052$ and dominant coordinate $Ile_{AUA}$.  Pairwise tissue cosines were $0.6883655232187615$, $0.8990093450963941$, and $0.9176793373754919$.  These protein-abundance contacts are downstream statistical readouts; they do not replace the translation step.

The mRNA-stability contacts provide controls and mediation diagnostics rather than translation by themselves.  Yeast Neymotin half-life rows joined $5085$ records with CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; the controlled mRNA half-life surface had $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and permutation right-tail probability $0.000999000999000999$.  In E. coli, the Esquerr\'e join used $2720$ rows with CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, selected growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$, and the same right-tail probability $0.000999000999000999$.  In human consensus half-life data, the Agarwal-Kelley contact used $54$ dataset columns, joined $16165$ rows with CDS hit rate $0.9779294769799313$, and gave $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$ with dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ and right-tail probability $0.000999000999000999$.  In the yeast abundance-mediation run, conditioning on residualized mRNA half-life changed $R^2_{QP}$ from $0.2761140373962526$ to $0.24851799532219632$, absorbing $0.027596042074056304$ of $R^2$ and giving mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$ against null mean $0.00019674487584783138$ and null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$.

\paragraph{Complement readback.}
The modeled-translation complement of the protein-abundance component was computed for six organisms.  The complement fraction $d$ was $0.874628721049266$ in Danio rerio, $0.6629960038578532$ in Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ in Gallus gallus, $0.9208949355679455$ in Homo sapiens, $0.8972672018746489$ in Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, and $0.21173219572706703$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding modeled-mediated fractions $m=1-d$ were $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  The readback vector $a_\perp$ remained nine-dimensional in each organism.  Dominant coordinates varied by organism: for example, yeast emphasized $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, while human emphasized $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $K_{AAA}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This organism dependence is part of the result: the matrix survives as a finite statistical surface, not as a universal direction vector.

\paragraph{Coordinate-level decomposition.}
A yeast decomposition of the abundance target used $n=1258$ joined rows, a five-fold held-out design, $120$ permutations, and control-rank $24$.  Eight of the nine named coordinates had real-source support under the reported held-out/permutation criterion: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The $f_3$-stress family decomposition used $n=1800$ joined rows, $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, five folds, and $120$ permutations.  Its full $f_3$ projection had norm square $52.76911713166682$ and accounted for fraction $0.12259712041843235$ of the target norm square $430.42705205115925$.  Nineteen family subcoordinates had real-source support, including $K_{AAA\to AAG}$, $R_{AGA\to AGG}$, $I_{AUU\to AUA}$, $T_{ACU\to ACG}$, and multiple two-codon third-position contrasts.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite matrix supports the following bounded conclusion.  After amino-acid quotienting and the declared controls, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block is not absorbed by the amino-acid, context, GC3, length, and $M$-density baseline model on translation-facing readouts.  The strongest direct survival statistic is the powered modeled-tAI surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ over $40$ organisms and a nondegenerate rank profile.  A separate measured ribosome-occupancy contact joins $5754$ genes and identifies $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress under position-bin shuffling with $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL=2$.  Protein abundance, mRNA stability, tissue, condition, and complement analyses show that the same residual family has downstream finite contacts, but those contacts are not allowed to stand in for translation unless the translation readout is explicitly present.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological claim is licensed by this chapter.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are not measured ribosome dynamics.  tRNA gene-copy counts are not tRNA abundance or charging.  Ribosome occupancy is not protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Protein abundance and mRNA half-life are separate downstream or control readouts, and their positive controlled scores do not retroactively create a translation-survival entry.  Any claim about folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
