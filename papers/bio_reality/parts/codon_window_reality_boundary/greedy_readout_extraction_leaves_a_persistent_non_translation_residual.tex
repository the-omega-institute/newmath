\subsection{Greedy Readout Extraction Leaves a Persistent Non-translation Residual}
\label{sec:q6-residual-basis-extraction-greedy-readout-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the modeled translation-complement abundance residual $R_0=P_{Q\perp T\mathrm{mod}}$ for the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate packet.  The finite object is a recursive ledger: at step $k$ the current residual $R_{k-1}$ is tested against the remaining readout blocks $H$, one block $H_k^*$ is chosen by a penalized absorption score, the projected component is removed, and the retained energy fraction $\rho_k=\|R_k\|^2/\|R_0\|^2$ is recorded.  The readout is deliberately modest: it is a compression trace for a fixed panel of available covariates, not a statement that any selected block is a cause of protein abundance or a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Selection rule.}
For each organism with sufficient matched rows, the chosen block maximizes the regularized projection score
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_k^* &= \arg\max_{H\in\mathcal H_{k-1}}\left(\frac{\|\Pi_H R_{k-1}\|^2}{\|R_{k-1}\|^2}-\lambda\,DL(H)\right),\\
\lambda &= 0.01,\\
DL(H) &= \log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}}(H)),\\
\rho_k &= \frac{\|R_k\|^2}{\|R_0\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each selected step is also compared with a degree-of-freedom matched deterministic Gaussian null.  That comparison asks whether the selected block absorbs more residual energy than a null block of the same dimension would typically absorb.  It does not convert a projection into causation.

\paragraph{Computed organism rows.}
Four organisms have sufficient local readout data.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=3046$ common rows and $9$ candidate blocks were available, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$.  The retained curve is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.0000000000,&\ 0.9962498990,\ 0.9929843171,\ 0.9915730985,\ 0.9903314907,\\
&0.9894054695,\ 0.9885902294,\ 0.9882386501,\ 0.9881524583,\ 0.9798667977).
\end{aligned}
$$
The selected blocks, in order, are measured translation efficiency, PTM density, transmembrane count, turnover, complex membership, measured tRNA adaptation, domain count, mRNA, and localization.  The first four selections exceed their matched nulls, steps five through eight do not, and the localization step exceeds its null with absorbed share $0.008385002223710514$ against null p95 $0.00708487840939486$.

\paragraph{Bacterial and vertebrate rows.}
In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=3390$ common rows and $6$ candidate blocks were available, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6789390310059091$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.015943027148055955$.  The retained curve is

&0.9951348881,\ 0.9949556469,\ 0.9947913329).
\end{aligned}
$$
The first two choices, PTM density and mRNA, exceed their degree-matched nulls: PTM density absorbs $0.002169763913160702$ against null p95 $0.0012766357121006147$, and mRNA absorbs $0.0017199221251025301$ against null p95 $0.0011573977552172463$.  The later selected blocks, transmembrane count, complex membership, measured translation efficiency, and domain count, do not exceed their null thresholds.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=16008$ common rows and $6$ candidate blocks were available, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191321$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.018824203448813415$.  The retained curve is

&0.9787609433,\ 0.9787233550,\ 0.9723341658).
\end{aligned}
$$
PTM density, complex membership, transmembrane count, measured tRNA adaptation, and localization exceed their matched nulls, while domain count does not.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=14410$ common rows and a single candidate block were available, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$ and $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.0$.  The only retained curve is $(1.0000000000,0.9791828971)$, and measured tRNA adaptation absorbs $0.02081710285096942$ against null p95 $0.0002306374780841125$.

\paragraph{Persistence verdict.}
The computed rows are uniformly persistent under the declared rule.  No organism is labeled compressible or partially compressible.  The final retained fractions are $0.9798667977250413$ for yeast, $0.9947913328743022$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.9723341658092314$ for human, and $0.9791828971490305$ for zebrafish.  The smallest final retained value is therefore $0.9723341658092314$, above the stated persistence floor $0.97$.  Thus the available readout panel leaves more than $97\%$ of the initial non-translation residual energy in every computed organism.  This is an informative negative outcome: the residual is not compressed away by the present readout basis, even though several individual selected blocks exceed degree-matched null behavior.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The only bounded readback supplied by this record is the retained-energy curve, the selected readout order, and the null comparison for the rows where the matrix is present.  No translation realization, folding statement, physical admissibility claim, functional claim, phenotype claim, mechanism, causation, or universal biological law follows from $\rho_k$, from $\Pi_H R_{k-1}$, from a selected readout label, from codon-window geometry, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, from translation-efficiency estimates, from mRNA abundance, from proteomics abundance, or from the fact that the computation completed.  Any such stronger statement would require its own layer-matched contact and statistic.  In the present finite record, the main conclusion is narrower and sharper: the available readout blocks do not provide a compressing basis for the $B^*_{Q6}$ modeled translation-complement abundance residual in any computed organism.
