\subsection{Position-resolved $f_3$ stress as a translation-survival question}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Claim geometry.}
The record concerns one member of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family: an $f_3$-stress coordinate computed on finite coding-sequence windows after amino-acid identity has been quotiented away.  The relevant table is not a codon-usage summary alone.  Each admissible row must carry the local $f_3$ residual coordinate, synonymous codon counts in the same window, a bounded wobble-weight surface, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates.  The proposed survival entry is therefore a controlled finite-row statistic, not a biological assertion about translation in the absence of the readout row.

\paragraph{Test statistic.}
For a window table $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ and controls $Z$, the intended readback is a rank-powered survival entry $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$.  The coordinate is allowed to matter only when the wobble surface separates the observed context from the $f_3$-symmetric baseline, written locally as $w_c \ne w^{f_3}_c$, and when a held-out statistic improves after the declared controls are attached.  A compact form of the survival comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
  &= \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{position},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M,\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{baseline}; f_3\bigr),\\
\mathrm{support} &\Longrightarrow S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)>0
\quad \text{under the same CDS, same window, and same organism join.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $M$ denotes the local methionine-density or matched density control named by the finite-row design.  A positive entry must survive simpler codon-count baselines and rank checks; a modeled codon-adaptation weight alone is not a position-resolved footprint measurement.

\paragraph{Observed neighboring finite rows.}
The present packet is motivated by nearby finite-row cross-layer records, but those records do not themselves instantiate this $f_3$ position-resolved table.  The strongest neighboring powered translation-conditioned structural-order run reports status \texttt{passed}, five folds, a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, and joined structural-order counts of $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Its full-control aggregate $\Delta R^2$ values were positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, and weighted value $0.004764742175882462$; the sign-flip probability for greater-than-zero full-control improvement was $0.0029296875$.  The permuted full-control comparison was negative in both organisms, with mean $-0.00212186632044159$, minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, and maximum $-0.000779938262202795$.  These numbers show that a finite controlled cross-layer statistic can be powered in the surrounding program, but they are not a substitute for the missing same-window ribosome-occupancy table for $f_3$ stress.

\paragraph{Why the present verdict is not ready.}
The required $f_3$ table is still absent.  The contacts name ribosome-footprint occupancy at codon or local-window resolution, matched mRNA abundance, organism-scoped tAI or stAI weights, and codon-usage summaries, but the supplied data do not yet provide a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window join containing all of them together with the controlled rank statistic.  The missing row is specifically the one that would let the chapter compare $f_3$ residual stress against amino-acid composition, position bin, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, codon-count baselines, wobble-weight asymmetry, mappability, coverage, and simpler predictor ablations.  Without that joined row, the record remains a precise request for a finite survival computation rather than a ready biological claim.

\paragraph{Local scope restriction.}
No translation realization, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal rule follows from the $f_3$ coordinate, the $B^*_{Q6}$ notation, modeled tAI or stAI weights, or organism-level codon tables.  Those conclusions require separate matched contacts at their own measurement layer.  Even a future positive $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ would only say that this bounded residual coordinate improves the declared position-resolved translation readout under the stated controls; it would not by itself establish protein output, folding, function, or a general law of codon directionality.
