\subsection{Finite-row translation readback for $B^\ast_{\mathrm{Q6}}$}\label{subsec:bstar-q6-translation-survival}
\origin{ai}
\paragraph{Residual basis and control geometry.}
This section records a bounded readback of the nine-coordinate $B^\ast_{\mathrm{Q6}}$ residual basis against translation-facing observables. The carrier is the ordered list
$$\begin{aligned}
Q &= \{K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}\},\\
Z &\in \mathbb{R}^{n\times 24},\qquad \operatorname{rank}(Z)=23.
\end{aligned}$$
The control block includes the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len}_{nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the union support of the nine projected $q$-vectors. On the modeled translation-adaptation surface, the residualized readout block is $9\times 9$, the organism count is $40$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $17$, and the score is $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$. The same finite row is not a blank projection: the proxy coupling matrix has $9$ nonzero entries, $\max |A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328$, the coupling threshold is $0.01$, and the break condition is not met. In other words, the basis remains visible after the amino-acid and context controls are imposed.

\paragraph{Local contacts and occupancy separation.}
The translation-contact readback is not restricted to the modeled-tAI surface. In the three-organism CUN/UUR gate, the partial Pearson correlation between $\log(\mathrm{CUN/UUR})$ and the tRNA-Leu ratio is $1.0$ with $n=3$, but the sign is not uniform across organisms: the log ratios are $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, so the signs are $(1,-1,1)$. This is a context-sensitive contact, not a universal direction rule. The same nine-coordinate support also reads back as a nine-dimensional complement in six organisms, with support fractions summing to $1$ up to floating error and with complement distances $d$ equal to $0.874628721049266$ in Danio rerio, $0.6629960038578534$ in Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ in Gallus gallus, $0.9208949355679455$ in Homo sapiens, $0.8972672018746484$ in Mycobacterium smegmatis, and $0.21173219572706714$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. For ribosome occupancy, the position-bin-controlled test uses the exact window and codon-index layout from the file, shuffles synonymous labels only within each bin, and reports $5754$ overlapping genes with zero length mismatch. With $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\mathrm{DL}=2$, $200$ requested permutations, and the gate $C_H>0$, the families $K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress pass all filters. This is a finite local occupancy separation, not a causal mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Abundance and decomposition.}
At the abundance layer, the yeast growth-condition packet joins $2209$ shared proteins between SD and YEPD. The support is stable across the two conditions: $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD and $0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD, with the same dominant coordinate $f_3$-stress in both, a condition-space cosine of $0.9993775935182967$, and Pearson correlation $0.9983880256003688$. The single-coordinate audit is also finite and nontrivial: the held-out decomposition uses $1258$ joined proteins, base $n=1800$, $5$ folds, and $D_e=3$, and it reports that at least one coordinate is a real source rather than a permutation artifact. The synonymous-family audit for $f_3$-stress is likewise explicit: there are $21$ additive family subcoordinates, $P_{f_3}\text{\,norm}^2=52.76911713166682$, target norm squared $430.42705205115925$, and $P_{f_3}$ carries fraction $0.12259712041843235$ of the target at this decomposition scale. A related mediation row on yeast abundance and mRNA half-life gives $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, absorbed variance $0.027596042074056304$, mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$, null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. That row is observational variance partitioning, not a causal pathway proof, but it does show that the abundance signal is not collapsed by the half-life mediator alone.

\paragraph{Boundary statement.}
The chapter therefore keeps a strict local boundary: these are finite rowwise contacts between the $B^\ast_{\mathrm{Q6}}$ residual basis and translation-facing observables under explicit controls, and they support a bounded translation-layer witness. They do not by themselves establish folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal validity. The tAI surface is modeled from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights; it is not ribo-seq, elongation speed, proteomics, or protein realization. Any upgrade from this bounded witness to a stronger biological statement requires a separate contact with its own joined rows and its own controlled statistic.
