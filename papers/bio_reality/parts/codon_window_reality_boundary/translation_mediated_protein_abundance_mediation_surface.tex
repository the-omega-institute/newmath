\subsection{Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Mediation Surface}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as a finite cross-layer mediation surface, not as a direct protein-output law. The coordinate side contains nine residual axes,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout side is a matched triple $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ is one of these residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance measurement. In the powered finite-row run used here, $t_j$ is per-protein modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the coding sequence and $w_c$ is the GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy-count and dos Reis wobble-weight codon score. The protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ from PAXdb-like measured abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts. The control block contains an intercept, CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, giving $23$ residualized control rank columns and $24$ control columns including the intercept in the per-organism diagnostics.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the reported scalar is the reduction in the residual $Q\to Y$ association after conditioning on the translation readout. With $X$ denoting the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual matrix and $T$ the modeled tAI readout, the finite statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{R^2(Y\sim X\mid Z)-R^2(Y\sim X\mid Z,T)}{R^2(Y\sim X\mid Z)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This number is read only as a controlled decomposition of variance on the joined rows. It is not a causal effect and it is not an observation of ribosome motion. The data nevertheless give a nonnegative mediated component in every one of the twelve organism panels, with all fractions inside $[0,1]$. The strongest row is yeast: $n=5815$ proteins joined, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, indirect $R^2=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. Other large mediated fractions occur in Drosophila with $n=13295$, $R^2_{QP}=0.0770952887136557$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.02956474995793468$, and $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$; Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 with $n=3739$, $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$; and Bacillus subtilis strain 168 with $n=4042$, $R^2_{QP}=0.061906245886660105$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.02423880523185755$, and $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$.

\paragraph{Rank and contact discipline.}
The organism panels have sufficient algebraic rank for the declared residualized design: $\mathrm{rank}(X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(T)=1$, and $\mathrm{rank}(X,T)=10$ after residualizing by the control block. Residual degrees of freedom range from $1633$ in Sulfolobus to $19372$ in mouse. The per-protein tAI weights also remain finite after codon-weight filling: the zero raw-weight fallback counts are $3$ in Bacillus, $1$ in Escherichia coli, $1$ in Mycobacterium, $1$ in Sulfolobus, and $0$ in the other eight organisms. A smaller modeled-tAI contact over three organisms gives $81$ entries above the bounded threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute score of $3801961.031607896$, but that run is explicitly underpowered for species-level survival because $3$ organisms do not meet the $40$-species powered condition. Thus the larger twelve-organism protein-abundance surface is the finite table used for the mediation claim, while the three-organism tAI table only confirms that the modeled readout can be computed over the standard sense-codon space.

\paragraph{Adjacent checks.}
Several neighboring finite-row records keep the interpretation narrow. In yeast structure mediation, a five-way join gives $4759$ rows, a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$, and a total $Q\to P$ residual association $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$. Conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability reduces this to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, while adding the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy changes it only to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$; the structure incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$ with permutation $p=0.1258741258741259$. A turnover audit on $3290$ genes reports $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$, so the current turnover surface does not supply a downstream stability claim. A mass-balance run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals reports $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, and $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, with $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$ and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$. These negative or attenuating controls prevent the protein-abundance mediation table from being silently promoted into turnover, mass-balance, or physical-state realization.

\paragraph{Perturbation and evolutionary boundaries.}
A separate Escherichia coli mRFP perturbation panel has $1459$ variants, $R^2_{L2}=0.12648386762689323$, $R^2_{L3}=0.1798829276988051$, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched as well as within-null $p=0.004975124378109453$. That is a useful protein-output perturbation contact for its own finite surface, but it does not by itself license a general $B^*_{Q6}$ mechanism. The companion 5-prime boundary audit on the same variant count has downstream $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$, downstream $\Delta LL=-2.082967365015851$, block $p=0.1691542288557214$, and a non-stable sign, so the start-window boundary is not promoted. The evolutionary selection-imprint audit over $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species reports $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and no positive selection-imprint gate. These records support a conservative reading: the present claim is a controlled cross-layer protein-abundance mediation table, not an evolutionary law.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The ready statement is therefore finite and conditional. At least one $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block survives as a controlled statistical mediation surface from residual coordinate to modeled translation readout to matched protein abundance, and the strongest table entry is yeast with $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ on $5815$ joined proteins. The broader organism list shows positive mediated fractions across all twelve joined panels, but the contact is modeled tAI plus matched abundance, not ribosome profiling. No translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from this table unless an additional layer-matched measurement supplies that layer and the same residual coordinate survives the corresponding controlled statistic.
