\subsection{Local interpretation boundary}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-local-boundary}

\paragraph{What the chapter may claim.}
A passed row table would say only this: within the tested organism, CDS-window definition, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency assay, and control family, the $f_3$-stress residual supplies a bounded cross-layer coordinate for position-resolved translation readout.  The claim is local to the finite rows and to the declared statistic.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may help define a wobble-weight surface, and codon-usage tables may help define background composition, but neither source is an observed ribosome-footprint array.

\paragraph{What it may not claim.}
No translation realization follows from codon geometry alone.  No protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from a positive $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ entry.  Such conclusions would need separate matched contacts at their own measurement layer.  Conversely, a negative entry would not show that $Q6$ residual geometry is biologically irrelevant; it would only show that this particular position-resolved translation interface has not retained controlled predictive power for the $f_3$ coordinate under the specified rows and controls.
