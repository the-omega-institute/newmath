\subsection{The $f_3$-stress translation-readout boundary in the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{subsec:q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The object considered here is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate called $f_3$-stress and to matched rows carrying codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation-layer readouts. The coordinate is not treated as a protein-level observable. It is a controlled synonymy residual whose admissible readback is a survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ against a translation readout $T_j$, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and mRNA-level controls already present in the comparison.

\paragraph{Saturation row.}
The finite saturation calculation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\notag\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)&=\{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\notag\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
M\setminus \operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)&=\{ACA,ACG\},\\
\partial_{f_3}&=\varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the $f_3$ boundary row is empty in this finite table, while the two codons $ACA$ and $ACG$ remain as the explicit complement of the saturated $R$-generated row inside $M$. This is a combinatorial fact about the coordinate table; by itself it does not report translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, causal explanation, or universality.

\paragraph{Wobble-broken reading.}
The conjectural biological reading is narrow. After quotienting by amino acid, the $f_3$-stress residual can become visible to translation only in those organism-context rows where tRNA supply or wobble weighting distinguishes a codon $c$ from its third-position partner $c^{f_3}$, so that $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$. If the weights are symmetric, or if the available readout matrix is rank-degenerate, the row is interpreted as a null boundary rather than survival. In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism $S^{QT}$ matrix is not evidence for translation-layer survival, because it contains no usable rank separation.

\paragraph{Powered finite evidence around the boundary.}
The adjacent powered rows support the existence of a nontrivial translation-adaptation neighborhood, but they do not close this conjecture. In the tRNA-poverty test, $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ were skipped, and $1000$ permutations were used at $\alpha=0.05$; the reported call says that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights. In the cross-organism optimality rows, several tests used $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The core tRNA-adaptation row passed under that design. The non-tRNA residual optimality row identified $f_3\_translation\_selection$ as the dominant residual axis. The two-axis closure row concluded that a third axis is present, with positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed $d_\perp$ directions across organisms. The third-axis identity row aligned $d_\perp$ with usage frequency within family, reporting mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The GC-orthogonalized row retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, with the finite conclusion summarized as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and needing a separate contact for CSC or positional identity. A further sufficiency row concluded that a fourth explanatory component is present under the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $11/16$ organism, $41$-column design.

\paragraph{Mandatory survival test.}
The local support condition is the existence of at least one controlled positive entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for $T_j$ equal to a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after the stated covariates and simpler-codon baselines are removed. The falsifying condition is equally finite: every controlled entry is non-positive, or the available matrix remains rank-degenerate, across a powered organism panel. A positive topology row, a tRNA-weight asymmetry, or a modeled adaptation score is therefore not promoted to survival unless the controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry is non-vacuous and positive.

\paragraph{Permitted contacts and local boundary.}
Codon-usage tables supply per-organism sequence-composition rows; tRNA gene-copy resources supply anticodon-family copy-number rows; tAI or stAI resources supply organism-scoped modeled adaptation weights. Ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, when paired to matched mRNA abundance, can supply a per-CDS translation row through $TE=RPF/mRNA$. These contacts are layer-specific. They do not, merely by being present, establish mature protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, biological function, a causal account, physical admissibility, or a universal law. Any such statement would require its own matched contact at that layer. The present chapter therefore records a cross-layer conjecture with a sharp readout gate: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate is allowed to survive only as a controlled translation-row distinction, and it is rejected at this interface when that finite survival row is absent, non-positive, or rank-degenerate.
