\subsection{Wobble-Broken Translation Readout for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-Stress Coordinate}\label{subsec:q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates a cross-layer conjecture for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate after amino-acid projection. The carrier is not the full codon cube and not a downstream phenotype table. It is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained by restricting each coding sequence to the $f_3$-stress residual and then pairing that coordinate with translation-layer rows measured in the same organismal setting. The relevant internal distinctions are: an $f_3$-paired synonymous codon imbalance after amino-acid quotienting; a context-dependent tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$; and survival of the residual coordinate after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, and mRNA controls have been included.

\paragraph{Verified finite saturation row.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation check gives an explicit codon row. The mediated set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The verified $f_3$-saturation row for $R$ removes exactly the two ACA/ACG entries:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the row has no additional finite boundary codons beyond this listed saturation difference:
$$\begin{aligned}
\partial_{f_3}=\varnothing.
\end{aligned}$$
These rows are used only as coordinate data. They do not by themselves measure ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, stability, localization, function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Organism-powered wobble contact.}
A separate finite organism panel supplies the readout-side biological contact for the wobble and tRNA-supply part of the conjecture. The powered boundary-set computation requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, and used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$. Its recorded status is passed. The finite biological reading of that row is narrow: $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights, giving a bounded tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance in that panel. This supports the use of tRNA or wobble weights as a candidate symmetry-breaking layer for $f_3$ pairs, but it is not yet a translation-efficiency survival result for the $f_3$-stress residual.

\paragraph{Translation survival variable.}
For each organism and each matched translation readout $T_j$, the intended bounded readback is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$. The admissible readouts are tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, with mRNA abundance used to separate translation-layer signal from transcript abundance. A positive entry is meaningful only after controls for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, and the relevant mRNA baseline have already been applied. In schematic form, the admissible test is
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}$$
under the full control set and after a simpler-codon-baseline ablation. If the controlled entry is non-positive, or if the readout matrix is rank-degenerate, the conjecture remains unsupported at the translation layer.

\paragraph{Support and break conditions.}
The conjecture is supported only if at least one controlled translation-readout row has strictly positive survival for the $f_3$-stress residual in a powered organism panel where $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ for the relevant codon context. It is broken, for this contact layer, if every controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ by translation-readout entry satisfies $S_{ij}\leq 0$, equivalently if no $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate improves the description of any translation readout after the stated controls. A three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-degenerate matrix is treated as a null boundary, not as evidence of survival.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The finite rows above justify a specific conjectural interface: an $f_3$-paired synonymous residual may become visible to translation readouts only where tRNA supply or wobble weights break third-position $f_3$ symmetry. No stronger conclusion is licensed here. Codon topology, tAI or stAI weights, organism-level codon tables, and $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry do not independently establish realized translation dynamics. Likewise, no folding, physical admissibility, mature protein abundance, function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism beyond the separately supplied tRNA/wobble contact, or universality follows unless an additional matched contact at that layer supplies it.
