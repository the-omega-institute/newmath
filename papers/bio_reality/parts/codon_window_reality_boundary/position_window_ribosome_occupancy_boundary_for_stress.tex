\subsection{Position-window ribosome-occupancy boundary for $f_3$-stress}\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-window-ribosome-occupancy-translation-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record considered here is a finite coding-sequence window table, not a statement about proteins or organisms as wholes.  A row is keyed by organism, coding-sequence identifier, codon-position bin, synonymous amino-acid fiber, $f_3$ partner class, residual $f_3$-stress coordinate, wobble or tAI weight surface, matched mRNA control, and a position-resolved ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency window readout.  The $f_3$ coordinate is admitted only after amino-acid quotienting and synonymous residualization: it is a residual coordinate inside a synonymous fiber, not an amino-acid composition effect and not a raw codon-count effect.  The dynamic comparison additionally requires a declared asymmetric weight surface, written locally as $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, and the same row must contain the codon information, the weight annotation, the matched mRNA control, and the ribosome-occupancy statistic before a survival statement is made.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
The intended readback is the per-window survival statistic $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{window}}\mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{window}}$ is a codon-indexed or local-window ribosome-occupancy statistic and $Z$ contains the declared controls.  In this chapter the controls are part of the claim, not optional decorations: organism, amino-acid composition or amino-acid bin, codon-position bin, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, wobble or tAI surface, matched mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, and simpler codon-usage baselines must be available on the same finite rows.  The null return is also part of the object.  If the organism panel is rank-degenerate, if same-CDS joins are absent, if the ribosome readout is not position-resolved, or if the modeled weight surface is present without footprint data, the packet returns a boundary-null result rather than a translation claim.

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{window}}\mid Z) &:= \operatorname{Survival}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{window}};\ f_3\text{-stress}, Z\bigr),\\
Z &:= (\text{organism},\text{AA bin},\text{position bin},\text{GC3},\text{length},M\text{-density},w,\text{mRNA},\text{coverage},\text{baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Finite upstream coordinates.}
The upstream $B^*_{Q6}$ computation supplies a reason to test $f_3$-stress as a residual coordinate, but it does not itself supply ribosome dynamics.  In the powered structural-order run, the computed axes were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Among these, $f_3$-stress was the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, rank $1$ by weighted increment, and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  The other reported weighted held-out increments were smaller or negative: $Arg_{AGR}$ gave $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile_{AUA}$ gave $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, $K_{AAA}$ gave $0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$ gave $0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$ gave $0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$ gave $0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$ gave $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$, and $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$ gave $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  This upstream fact licenses a narrow residual-coordinate question: whether the same $f_3$ axis survives after attachment to position-window translation readouts under the stated controls.

\paragraph{Permutation and collision checks.}
A separate target-permutation dictionary-ceiling run reported $200$ target-null permutations and classified the overall verdict as crossing the target-null boundary, with the reported conclusion that $24.5\%$ of the observed $C^*$ signal exceeded the matched target-null $95\%$ ceiling.  This supports the existence of nontrivial finite structure in the upstream dictionary setting, but it is still not a ribosome-occupancy measurement.  Conversely, the disome/monosome Hel2-resolution residual run gives an explicit negative example for downstream promotion: on $2997$ genes, the readout $Y_g=\Delta\log_2(\mathrm{disome}/\mathrm{mono})$ in $hel2\Delta$ minus wild type had held-out $R^2=0.1971$ for controls and $R^2=0.1955$ after adding the grammar variables, for incremental held-out $R^2=-0.0015$ with $1000$ permutations and permutation $p=0.4555$.  That finite row set therefore does not support a collision-persistence gain from the grammar variables.  The present chapter uses this as a guardrail: even a visible residual coordinate in one finite surface cannot be promoted into translation mechanism unless the requested position-window occupancy table itself passes the same-row survival test.

\paragraph{Contact requirements.}
The ribosome contact must be a codon-indexed or fixed-window footprint occupancy row joined to ordered coding-sequence coordinates.  The weight contact may be tAI, stAI, or a comparable bounded wobble surface, but a modeled weight is only an annotation surface until a footprint readout is joined.  The mRNA contact must be matched to the ribosome-profiling condition so that translation-efficiency style residualization can separate footprint enrichment from transcript abundance.  The codon-usage contact supplies organism-scoped codon counts or frequencies and is kept distinct from the footprint measurement.  The valid finite row is therefore a joined row, not a list of separately true biological facts.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The claim made here is a bounded cross-layer test of whether residual $f_3$-stress survives as a predictor of position-resolved ribosome occupancy after the declared controls.  No translation realization, no protein abundance, no folding, no physical admissibility, no localization, no biochemical function, no mechanism, no phenotype, and no universal biological rule follows from the coordinate, from the wobble or tAI surface, from codon usage, or from the upstream $B^*_{Q6}$ finite results.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate same-layer contact and its own finite test.  In the absence of same-organism, same-CDS, same-window footprint rows with matched mRNA controls and nondegenerate rank, the correct output is the null boundary for this chapter.
