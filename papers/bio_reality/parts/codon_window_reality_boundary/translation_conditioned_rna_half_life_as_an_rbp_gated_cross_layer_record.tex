\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life as an RBP-gated cross-layer record}
\label{sec:q6-translation-conditioned-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object of this chapter is a finite matched table whose rows join a coding transcript, its amino-acid quotient class, a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate label, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein at the editable RNA window, and matched controls for transcription rate, abundance, isoform state, local sequence composition, codon-pair context, local RNA structure, batch, perturbation, and rescue.  The coordinate label is not itself a biological mechanism.  It is a finite stratifier: rows may be compared as high-coordinate or low-coordinate only after amino-acid sequence has been quotiented away and the remaining synonymous variation has been placed in the same transcript-window frame.  A positive record therefore has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta_{\mathrm{HL}\mid \mathrm{TE}}(\mathrm{high},\mathrm{low}) \neq 0,\\
&\Delta_{\mathrm{RBP}}(\mathrm{window}) \neq 0,\\
&\Delta_{\mathrm{HL}\mid \mathrm{TE}} \to 0 \quad \text{under target-RBP loss},\\
&\Delta_{\mathrm{HL}\mid \mathrm{TE}} \quad \text{returns under wild-type target-RBP rescue},\\
&\Delta_{\mathrm{HL}\mid \mathrm{TE}} \not\text{ returns under binding-defective or non-target rescue}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Delta_{\mathrm{HL}\mid \mathrm{TE}}$ denotes the RNA half-life contrast after the same-row translation readout has been attached and conditioned, while $\Delta_{\mathrm{RBP}}$ denotes experimentally measured local occupancy of the named decay-factor RBP.  The bridge is accepted only when the half-life effect, the local occupancy effect, the loss collapse, and the rescue asymmetry are all present in the same finite panel.

\paragraph{Readout discipline.}
The row table is deliberately narrower than a general expression or protein-output claim.  RNA half-life alone supplies an RNA-stability surface; translation efficiency alone supplies a translation surface; RBP occupancy alone supplies a binding surface.  The proposed cross-layer record is the conjunction of these surfaces under matched-row bookkeeping.  The required half-life contact is per-gene or per-transcript RNA stability keyed by organism-specific identifiers.  The required abundance control is the mRNA measurement used to separate transcription-layer signal from translation-layer signal.  The required translation contact is a ribosome-profiling or equivalent translation-efficiency readout, paired to the same rows by identifiers and condition.  None of these three contacts is allowed to stand in for measured decay-factor occupancy, perturbation, or rescue.

\paragraph{Finite calibration ledger.}
Existing finite cross-layer checks show why the present claim is stated as a gated conjecture rather than as an automatic consequence of coordinate geometry.  A methylation flanking-context run used $20{,}000$ probes from $656$ individuals and reported a baseline coefficient $R^{2}=0.0129735$, with no cross-cohort scalar recorded in the row.  A PRDM9 hotspot contact carried $52{,}963$ hotspots.  A translation-response contact for the mTOR setting carried $n=6494$ rows from the declared GEO raw-data source and ran in $9.469$ seconds.  A protein N-terminal-acetylation contact carried $n=657$ proteins, including $426$ NatA rows, and reported AUROC $0.942081$ over $622$ high-low rows.  A polyadenylation-tail contact carried $8854$ human rows and $3990$ yeast rows, while its cross-species concordance flag was false.  An RBP-binding contact carried $900$ total peaks and only $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases.  These numbers are not evidence for the RNA half-life bridge by themselves.  They fix the intended empirical scale: finite row sets, explicit scalar summaries, and refusal to convert one layer's successful statistic into another layer's mechanism.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The local null says that, after conditioning on amino-acid composition, transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the mandatory same-row translation readout, high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life.  The stronger mechanistic null says that even a nonzero RNA half-life residual is not an RBP-mediated bridge unless the named decay-factor RBP changes occupancy at the same window, target-RBP loss collapses the residual, wild-type rescue restores it, and binding-defective or non-target rescue fails to restore it.  Thus the finite statistic is not simply a regression coefficient; it is a matched loss-and-rescue pattern attached to a named molecular contact.

\paragraph{Admissible positive reading.}
A positive panel licenses only the following bounded statement: in the sampled transcript windows, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate separates RNA half-life after translation conditioning, and that separation tracks an experimentally decoupled local occupancy pattern for the prespecified decay-factor RBP with the required perturbation and rescue asymmetry.  The statement remains local to the finite rows, the organism, the assay conditions, the coordinate construction, and the declared controls.  It does not assert that the coordinate causes translation, that translation realizes the half-life effect, or that the RBP bridge persists outside the sampled windows.

\paragraph{Exclusion boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life without the same-row translation readout.  No RBP-decay mechanism follows from half-life without measured local RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss collapse, wild-type rescue return, and binding-defective or non-target rescue failure.  No protein output, folding, physical admissibility, cellular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal biological law follows from this chapter.  RNA-structure-only, motif-density-only, transcription-rate, isoform-state, transcript-abundance, GC or dinucleotide, codon-pair-context, batch, binding-defective rescue, and non-target-control explanations remain live unless the finite panel separates them in the declared rows.  The chapter therefore records a testable cross-layer conjecture with a strict local acceptance rule, not a general sequence law.
