\subsection{Translation-mediated protein abundance for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,T,P)$: a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$.  The residual coordinate set has nine entries,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm after joining real CDS codon counts to measured protein-abundance rows.  The translation readout used in the powered mediation run is per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the CDS count of sense codon $c$ and $w_c$ is the organism-matched modeled tAI weight from all-tRNA GtRNAdb copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The control block contains an intercept, CDS length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline; in each organism the residualized design reports $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
Twelve organism-level panels are present and rank-sufficient: $4042$ joined proteins for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The tRNA-copy totals range from $47$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $8874$ in \emph{D. rerio}; geometric-mean filling of zero raw $W$ values occurs in only four panels, with counts $3$, $1$, $1$, and $1$ for \emph{B. subtilis}, \emph{E. coli}, \emph{M. smegmatis}, and \emph{S. solfataricus}, respectively.  These rows support a finite statistical decomposition, not a causal account of translation.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the total residual-to-protein association is measured as $c=R^2(Q,P)$ after residualizing controls, the direct remainder is $c'=R^2(Q,P\mid T)$, and the mediated component is $c-c'$.  The reported scalar is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve $M^{QTP}$ values lie in the unit interval.  The strongest mediated fraction is in yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The next largest fractions are \emph{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, \emph{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$, and \emph{C. elegans} with $0.4872268357227724$.  The vertebrate panels are smaller but still positive: $0.3157053953614641$ in zebrafish, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.1976903964724546$ in chicken, $0.17489681798543982$ in rat, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse.  The weakest positive fractions are $0.08397602919861291$ in \emph{M. smegmatis} and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.  Thus the finite-row claim is not that every organism has a large effect, but that the controlled $Q\to T\to P$ matrix has positive mediated entries after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Translation-readout status.}
The same nine residual coordinates were also evaluated against modeled tAI weights in a smaller three-organism bounded-contact run.  That run used the $61$ sense-codon count space, loaded all standard tRNA families for \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast, and computed modeled tAI readouts with zero raw-$W$ fallback count $0$ in all three organisms.  It found $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-coupling threshold and a maximum absolute score of $3801961.031607896$, but the organism count was only $3$.  Consequently this readout is evidence of modeled codon-adaptation contact and not a powered ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation by itself.

\paragraph{Negative and limiting contacts.}
The surrounding finite rows prevent over-reading the positive mediation matrix.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; it reported $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, so the strong selection-imprint gate is not supported.  The dynamic mass-balance run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals reports $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover residual audit similarly reports no positive extension beyond expression, TE, pLDDT, and N-end controls: $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$ over $3290$ genes.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The supported reading is therefore narrow.  A direct $Q_i\to P$ association is not admitted as protein realization unless the same surface first passes through a translation readout $T$.  The present data support a controlled statistical mediation matrix using modeled per-protein tAI and matched PAXdb abundance, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and matched transcript-layer separation retained as controls.  They do not establish observed ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, a mechanistic causal pathway, selection, phenotype, or universality.  Any such stronger statement requires an additional layer-matched contact and a separate finite statistic on the same coordinate rows.
