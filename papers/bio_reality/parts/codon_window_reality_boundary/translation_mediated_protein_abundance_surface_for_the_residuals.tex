\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance surface for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{subsec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record uses a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual axes
$\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  The translation readout $t_j$ in the powered abundance run is the per-protein modeled tAI average, computed as the codon-count-weighted mean of organism-specific tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout $p_k$ is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in ppm from PAXdb, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct $q_i\to p_k$ assertion: it is the controlled matrix route $Q\to T\to P$, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline kept in the control set.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank checks.}
The powered mediation table contains twelve organism-scoped joins.  The joined protein counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In every organism the residualized design retained rank $9$ for $X_Q$, rank $1$ for the modeled-tAI column, rank $10$ for the joint $X_Q,T$ block, and rank $23$ for the control block, with a degeneracy threshold of $0.999999$.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, so the mediation calculation is not a rank artifact of a collapsed design.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism the scalar mediation fraction is computed from the total controlled $Q\to P$ fit and the residual direct fit after conditioning on $T$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)},\\
R^2(Q\to P) &= c,\qquad R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=c'.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve finite rows had $0<M^{QTP}<1$.  The organism list of $M^{QTP}$ values is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&0.6084594553,\ 0.4872268357,\ 0.3157053954,\ 0.6165167749,\\
&0.6129222528,\ 0.1976903965,\ 0.2720628916,\ 0.1548879991,\\
&0.0839760292,\ 0.1748968180,\ 0.9491551041,\ 0.0185066856,
\end{aligned}
$$
ordered as \emph{B. subtilis}, worm, zebrafish, fly, \emph{E. coli}, chicken, human, mouse, \emph{M. smegmatis}, rat, yeast, and \emph{S. solfataricus}.  The strongest mediated fraction is yeast, where $c=0.2983762499$, $c'=0.0151709094$, and the indirect component is $0.2832053406$.  The weakest positive row is \emph{S. solfataricus}, where $c=0.0639751425$, $c'=0.0627911747$, and the indirect component is $0.0011839679$.  The result is therefore finite and heterogeneous: it supports a controlled statistical mediation surface, not a universal sign law.

\paragraph{Translation-readout contact.}
The earlier modeled-tAI survival computation used three organisms and all $61$ sense codons.  Its tRNA payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families in \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast; the FASTA header counts were $89$, $432$, and $275$, with matched header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$.  The finite modeled-tAI coupling matrix had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, and the maximum absolute entry was $3801961.031607896$.  That contact is useful only as a bounded modeled translation readout.  The three-organism run explicitly does not provide powered ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation, and it cannot by itself promote a residual coordinate to observed translation output.

\paragraph{Nearby finite controls.}
The yeast five-way structure-mediation join supplies an independent check on how quickly this surface can overstate layer movement.  In that table $4759$ genes were joined, the CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419$, and the overall join hit rate was $0.8818572657$.  The controlled abundance residual fit gave $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121$.  The structure-only fraction was only $0.0032845645$, and the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507779$, with right-tail permutation values $0.0279720280$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741259$ for the incremental structure term.  This nearby table supports the same discipline used here: a positive residual decomposition is a variance partition on measured rows, not a license to infer a causal path.

\paragraph{Negative and boundary rows.}
Several finite audits keep the present claim bounded.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; its positive-imprint gate failed with $\Delta R^2=0.0325051502$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477730$, and a null $95\%$ maximum of $0.0542715707$.  The mass-balance closure run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals had $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628202$, $R^2_{L1}=0.9964106289$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443378$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0758443809$, and permutation-style checks $p_{TE\,bin}=0.6831683168$, $p_{time\,shuffle}=0.9306930693$.  The turnover residual audit on $3290$ genes similarly gave $R^2_{L2}=0.0557408516$, $R^2_{L3}=0.0502788722$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0054619794$, $p_{matched\,bin}=0.8855721393$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$.  These rows prevent the translation-mediated abundance result from being read as turnover, stability, selection, or dynamic mass-balance closure.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity and module-level context.}
The structural-order axis audit found that the only coordinate passing its fold-level sign-flip gate was $\texttt{f3\_stress}$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502$ and $p=0.03125$.  Other axes remained below that gate: for example $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0020139271$ with $p=0.142578125$, and $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0013067593$ with $p=0.0693359375$.  The confirmatory genetic-code table checks also remain lower-layer: the locked module-classification and leave-one-code/codon tasks had $40$ successes in $40$ independent replication trials with exact upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, while $20$ negative controls and $20$ critical ablations produced zero unexpected successes.  These module facts constrain coordinate provenance and classification, but they do not supply protein-layer biology.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The claim is ready as a finite controlled mediation statement.  At least one, and in fact every organism row in the twelve-organism table, has a positive mediated component for the joint nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block after the declared controls.  The chapter may state that modeled-tAI conditioning separates a positive portion of the controlled $Q\to P$ association in these finite matched rows.  It may not state that the residuals cause translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or a universal biological rule.  No such stronger conclusion follows here unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and the same controlled finite-row test is passed at that layer.
