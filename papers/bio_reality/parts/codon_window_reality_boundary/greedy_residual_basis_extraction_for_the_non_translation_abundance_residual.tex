\subsection{Greedy residual-basis extraction for the non-translation abundance residual}
\label{sec:q6-residual-basis-extraction-greedy-readout-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the modeled-translation-complement abundance residual denoted here by $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$.  The finite object is a recursive residual-energy ledger.  For each organism with sufficient matched rows, the initial residual is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_0 &= P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}},\\
\rho_k &= \frac{\|R_k\|^2}{\|R_0\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
At step $k$, the available readout blocks are searched greedily.  The selected block $H_k^*$ is the remaining candidate that maximizes the regularized absorbed fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\|\Pi_H R_{k-1}\|^2}{\|R_{k-1}\|^2} - \lambda\,DL(H),
\qquad
\lambda &= 0.01,
\qquad
DL(H)=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}}(H)).
\end{aligned}
$$
The readback stored by the ledger is not a biological cause.  It is the ordered list of selected readout blocks, the absorbed increment at each step, the retained-energy curve $\rho_k$, and a degree-of-freedom-matched deterministic Gaussian null threshold for each selected step.  The internal distinctions are therefore coordinate, relation, and rank distinctions: coordinate because every row lives in a matched finite organism table; relation because each $\Pi_H$ is a statistical projection between residual coordinates and readout coordinates; and rank because each comparison is made against a null with the same effective size of readout block.

\paragraph{Compression verdict.}
The finite classification is persistent for every computed organism.  No computed row is classified as compressible or partially compressible.  The persistence floor is $0.97$, the computed count is $4$, and the minimum final retained fraction is $0.9723341658092314$.  The negative result is therefore not that the projections are numerically empty; several selected blocks exceed their matched null thresholds.  The negative result is that the available readout panel does not form a compact residual basis for $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$.  Even after the full greedy pass, more than $97\%$ of the starting residual energy remains in each computed organism.

This distinction is important.  A readout block can absorb more residual energy than a matched null and still fail to explain the residual as a whole.  Conversely, a late greedy block can absorb a small amount of energy without warranting a mechanistic interpretation.  The record therefore reads the $\rho_k$ curves as a bounded persistence review over the currently attached panel, not as a discovery of a shared cross-organism biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The chapter makes only the finite-row claim above.  It does not infer translation realization from the residual curve.  It does not infer folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or a universal biological law from $\Pi_H R_{k-1}$, from a selected readout label, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, from measured translation efficiency, from mRNA abundance, from proteomics abundance, from localization, from PTM density, from domain count, from complex membership, from turnover, or from codon-window geometry.  Such stronger claims would require a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic designed for that layer.  Within this record, the honest conclusion is narrower and sharper: the declared readout panel leaves a persistent non-translation abundance residual in yeast, \emph{E. coli}, human, and zebrafish, while chicken and \emph{M. smegmatis} remain outside the computed scope until the missing rows are supplied.
