\subsection{Translation-conditioned decay-factor occupancy and RNA half-life}
\label{sec:q6-translation-conditioned-decay-rbp-rna-half-life-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite claim surface.}
This record concerns synonymously editable coding-RNA windows equipped with residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate labels. The finite row unit is not a gene in isolation and not a free sequence string: it is a matched RNA-window row carrying a high-coordinate or low-coordinate synonymous label after amino-acid composition control, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, measured occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, transcription-rate controls, local RNA-structure controls, motif-density controls, GC and dinucleotide controls, codon-pair controls, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, perturbation state, rescue state, and batch labels. The intended statistic is bounded to those row fields. It may compare coordinate label, local decay-factor occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, rescue class, translation readout, and half-life, but it does not read back to protein output, mature phenotype, adaptation, folded structure, or a general law of sequence.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The matched null says that, after the declared controls, high-coordinate and low-coordinate RNA-window labels are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life. In particular, a positive coordinate-associated half-life contrast is not by itself a licensed cross-layer result. The finite test must condition on the same-row translation boundary and must show that the half-life contrast follows measured local occupancy of the chosen decay-factor RBP. The null is broken only when the coordinate contrast and the occupancy contrast can be experimentally separated, target-RBP loss collapses the half-life effect, wild-type rescue restores it, and binding-defective rescue or non-target control does not restore the same pattern.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $C_i \in \{0,1\}$ denote the low-versus-high residual coordinate label of RNA window $i$, $T_i$ the matched translation readout, $H_i$ the RNA half-life readout, $O_i$ the measured local occupancy of the specified decay-factor RBP, $P_i$ the perturbation state, $W_i$ the wild-type rescue indicator, and $B_i$ the binding-defective or non-target control indicator. Let $Z_i$ collect transcription rate, local structure, motif density, GC, dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, abundance, and batch. A minimal finite contrast has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H
  &= \widehat{E}[H_i \mid C_i=1,O_i=1,T_i,P_i,W_i,Z_i]
   - \widehat{E}[H_i \mid C_i=0,O_i=0,T_i,P_i,W_i,Z_i],\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{loss}}
  &= \Delta_H(\mathrm{target\ RBP\ present})
   - \Delta_H(\mathrm{target\ RBP\ loss}),\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{rescue}}
  &= \Delta_H(\mathrm{wild\ type\ rescue})
   - \Delta_H(\mathrm{binding\ defective\ or\ non\ target\ control}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The claim is supported only if the observed half-life contrast tracks $O_i$, if $\Delta_{\mathrm{loss}}$ shows collapse under target-RBP loss, and if $\Delta_{\mathrm{rescue}}$ separates wild-type rescue from binding-defective or non-target control. A coordinate-only regression, a half-life-only regression, or a translation-only regression is therefore insufficient.

\paragraph{Concrete finite evidence already available.}
The present repository evidence is adjacent rather than decisive for this RBP-decay claim. One verified cross-condition observation-ceiling run was carried out in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with organism key \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. It started at 2026-06-29T13:29:24+00:00, completed at 2026-06-29T13:37:41+00:00, and recorded runtime $497.03086400032043$ seconds. This establishes that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ cross-condition machinery has a finite organism-scoped execution record, but it is not an RBP-occupancy or RNA-half-life perturbation panel.

A second verified finite run measured a sequence-conditioned chromatin-remodeler repair-persistence readout in $12055$ one-kilobase windows. Its response was
$$
\begin{aligned}
Y_{\mathrm{RSC}}
  &= \operatorname{mean}_{\mathrm{rsc2},\mathrm{sth1}}
     \bigl([\log(1+\mathrm{CPD}_{0h})-\log(1+\mathrm{CPD}_{2h})]_{\mathrm{mutant}}\\
  &\qquad\qquad - [\log(1+\mathrm{CPD}_{0h})-\log(1+\mathrm{CPD}_{2h})]_{\mathrm{WT}}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
computed per window as an RSC-dependent repair-persistence change. The held-out control model using the $0h$ dipyrimidine and GC baseline had $R^2=0.1878$, while the sequence-augmented model had $R^2=0.1989$, giving incremental held-out $R^2=0.0111$ for RSC. The SWI/SNF specificity increment was $0.0246$, the permutation count was $1000$, and the permutation $p$-value was $0.003$. The module-specific flag was false, so this run should be read as evidence for a finite cross-layer sequence-to-readout contact under controls, not as a confirmed RSC-specific module law and not as evidence for the present decay-factor RBP mechanism.

\paragraph{Decision rule for this chapter.}
For the RNA half-life claim itself, the decisive finite panel must contain matched rows where the same synonymous window has coordinate label, translation readout, half-life, measured target-RBP occupancy, perturbation, rescue, and control annotations. Support is local and conditional: high-coordinate windows must differ from low-coordinate windows in half-life only along the measured decay-factor occupancy route, and that route must fail under target-RBP loss while returning under wild-type rescue. If binding-defective rescue, non-target control, local RNA structure, motif density, transcription rate, isoform stability, GC, dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, transcript abundance, translation readout, or batch reproduces the same pattern, the RBP-decay interpretation is rejected.

\paragraph{Boundaries.}
The mRNA half-life contacts provide organism-scoped transcript or gene rows with curated half-life estimates or normalized consensus half-life readouts. The ribosome-profiling contact can supply translation-efficiency information when paired to matched mRNA abundance. Neither contact alone supplies measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or non-target control behavior. Consequently, this chapter permits only a bounded conjectural cross-layer relation. No translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, cellular function, decay mechanism, adaptation, causation, universality, or global biological rule follows from the coordinate label or half-life readout unless an independent measured contact at that layer supplies the missing row fields.
