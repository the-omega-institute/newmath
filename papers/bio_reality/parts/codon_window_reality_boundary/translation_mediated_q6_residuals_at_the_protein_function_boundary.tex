\subsection{Translation-mediated Q6 residuals at the protein-function boundary}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-function-boundary-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record fixes a finite cross-layer table whose rows are indexed by quadruples $(q_i,T_j,P_k,F_l)$.  The first coordinate $q_i$ is a synonymous residual coordinate after the amino-acid quotient; the second coordinate $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout boundary; the third coordinate $P_k$ is a protein-omics intermediary; and the fourth coordinate $F_l$ is a downstream function or phenotype surface that is admitted only when a separate measurement supplies it.  The table is therefore not a direct map from codon geometry to biological function.  It is a bounded mediation ledger: a residual coordinate may be related to a protein readout only after the translation coordinate has first been tested, and it may be related to a function readout only after an additional function or phenotype contact is supplied.

The residual basis used in the finite run contains nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6} = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These nine rows were imported from the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual computation and were tested after residualizing against real coding-sequence covariates.  The control matrix contained an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, the M-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors, and measured $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ protein abundance.  Thus the finite table does not ask whether codon use is associated with essentiality in the raw data.  It asks whether the nine amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates retain a beyond-expression association after the listed controls have been removed.

Two organism rows are available in the verified finite contact.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the joined table used $3737$ genes, with $285$ essential genes and $3452$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.07626438319507627$.  The reported abundance join contained $3739$ proteins, and the unique essentiality label count was $3737$.  The residualized design had $25$ control columns, rank $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, residual residual-coordinate rank $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $2.243840654793728$, and response residual energy $232.13814987247684$.  The positive partial-$R^2$ threshold was $10^{-12}$, the degenerate-$R^2$ threshold was $0.999999$, and all nine residual coordinates survived the powered rank test.  The essentiality readout gave

R^2_{FE}(\textit{E. coli}) = 0.00966597112980509,
\qquad n_{\mathrm{genes}} = 3737,
\qquad n_{\mathrm{surviving}} = 9.
\end{aligned}
$$

For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined table used $5511$ genes, with $1168$ essential genes and $4343$ nonessential genes, giving essential fraction $0.2119397568499365$. No finite $\lambda$ values, p-values, hit-rate statistic, or module-classification labels are present in this row package.  The available scalar evidence is therefore rank, residual degrees of freedom, energy, essentiality counts, joined row counts, and the two small positive $R^2_{FE}$ values.

\paragraph{Mediation rule.}
The admissible relation has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k \dashrightarrow F_l,
\end{aligned}
$$
where the dashed arrow is intentionally inactive until a separate function or phenotype readout is attached.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight may be used only as a bounded translation-layer annotation.  It is not an observation of ribosome occupancy, mature protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or a general biological law.  A ribosome-profiling contact may test per-CDS ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, especially when paired with matched mRNA abundance through $TE=RPF/mRNA$.  A matched proteomics contact may test whether a protein-abundance association is absorbed after conditioning on the matched translation readout.  A matched mRNA contact separates transcript abundance from translation-layer signal.  None of these contacts alone licenses the whole chain.

The mediation test is therefore sequential.  First, the residual coordinate $q_i$ must survive amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, and mRNA or expression controls against a matched translation readout $T_j$.  Second, a protein-omics association $q_i \to P_k$ must be tested after including $T_j$; if the direct residual contribution is reduced or absorbed, the association may be recorded as translation-mediated protein-omics survival.  Third, the function coordinate $F_l$ remains null unless an independently measured phenotype, activity, localization, interaction, or other function contact is attached.  The present essentiality run supplies a bounded phenotype-adjacent readout after protein-abundance control, but it is cross-sectional and observational, so it does not by itself identify codon causality, synonymous-edit causality, translation mechanism, pathway mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, or organism-general law.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For each finite row $(q_i,T_j,P_k,F_l)$, the local outcome is one of four cases.  In the first case, no tested survival remains after the controls, and the row is silent beyond the failure of that contact.  In the second case, the residual coordinate survives only at the translation layer, so the row may be carried as a translation-bound association and no protein or function conclusion is made.  In the third case, a protein-omics association survives but is not absorbed by the measured translation readout; this is a protein-layer association without demonstrated translation mediation.  In the fourth case, the residual coordinate survives at translation and the downstream protein association is absorbed by the translation readout; this is the bounded translation-mediated protein-omics case.  Even in the fourth case, $F_l$ remains inactive until an additional function or phenotype measurement is attached and tested.

The two available organism rows instantiate the powered-rank side of this readback, not the full mediation chain.  Both organisms have both essentiality classes, both have residual-coordinate rank $9$ after controls, and both produce positive but small $R^2_{FE}$ values under a linear-probability residualization of knockout essentiality.  The honest reading is narrow: the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show beyond-expression association with a laboratory knockout binary phenotype in these two joined datasets.  This is not a claim of translation realization from the residuals alone, not a folding claim, not a physical-admissibility claim, not a mechanism claim, not a direct function-realization claim, and not a universal biological rule.  Each stronger interpretation requires its own matched measurement contact and its own finite survival test.
