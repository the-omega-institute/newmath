\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The seed row by itself has carrier status awaiting biological contact and no internal biological realization.  The finite codon rows justify only a controlled enumeration substrate: $R$ has $13$ listed codons, $M$ has $20$ listed codons, the saturated $f3$ row omits exactly $ACA$ and $ACG$, and the quotient summary has $10$ points. Consequently, this section asserts only contact-gated ORF eligibility for the finite tested setting.  It does not assert translation, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal biological law.  Any such stronger assertion would require a different observation and a separately recorded test.
