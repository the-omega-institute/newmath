\subsection{A survival matrix for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Object of measurement.}
The packet studies a $9\times K$ survival matrix whose rows are the nine synonymous-residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and the antisymmetric $f_3$ stress coordinate.  Each column is a translation-related readout after quotienting out amino-acid composition and ordinary sequence covariates.  For a row $q_i$ and a readout $r_j$, the intended statistic is the controlled survival entry $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline.  The finite record therefore asks whether any residual codon coordinate improves the description of a translation readout after those controls and after the simpler-codon baseline has already been given its chance.

\paragraph{Finite modeled-translation contact.}
The strongest finite entry in the record is the powered modeled-tAI computation.  It uses $40$ organisms, the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and a $9\times 9$ modeled readout surface formed from per-codon tAI weights derived from tRNA anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The residualized control design has $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$; the residualized coordinate block has rank $9$.  The total controlled modeled-translation fit is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a non-saturated powered finite-row result because the reported degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$, while the observed $R^2_{QT}$ is below that value.  The positive-entry threshold used for the modeled survival surface is $10^{-12}$, and the computation reports a full $9\times 9$ matrix rather than a single hand-picked coordinate.  The row names of the readout surface match the nine residual coordinates, so the statistic is coordinate-aligned rather than a post hoc aggregate.

\paragraph{Proxy coupling before survival.}
A separate three-organism coupling computation loads Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12 and works in the $61$-codon count space.  Against the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, all nine residual rows have nonzero proxy coupling above the declared threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated break condition, namely that every one of the nine rows should satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout, is false.  This is useful as a coordinate-contact signal, but it is not itself a survival score: the proxy contact uses codon counts and tRNA-copy information, not measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance.

\paragraph{Leucine gate and finite-row direction.}
The focused $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ gate records a three-organism partial-correlation surface with $n=3$ and partial Pearson value $1.0$ for the relation between the $CUN/UUR$ codon-usage log ratio and the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-ratio readout.  The finite row also reports that the signs are not uniform across organisms: the log-ratio list is $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1,-1,+1$.  Consequently the row supports neither a universal direction nor a population-level estimate.  Its role is narrower: it shows that the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ coordinate is not silent under a concrete organism-level codon/tRNA contact, while also showing that a larger panel is needed before treating the sign as stable.

\paragraph{Modeled complement and residual support.}
The protein-abundance complement computations are downstream diagnostics, not translation-survival proof.  Across six organisms, the modeled-tAI complement fraction $d$ and the remaining mediated fraction $m$ vary strongly:
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\mathrm{danio}} &= 0.874628721049266, & m_{\mathrm{danio}} &= 0.12537127895073397,\\
d_{\mathrm{ecoli}} &= 0.6629960038578532, & m_{\mathrm{ecoli}} &= 0.3370039961421468,\\
d_{\mathrm{gallus}} &= 0.9400341806302775, & m_{\mathrm{gallus}} &= 0.05996581936972245,\\
d_{\mathrm{human}} &= 0.9208949355679455, & m_{\mathrm{human}} &= 0.07910506443205445,\\
d_{\mathrm{mycobacterium}} &= 0.8972672018746489, & m_{\mathrm{mycobacterium}} &= 0.10273279812535108,\\
d_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.21173219572706703, & m_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.788267804272933.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation reports finite $R^2_{QP}$ values $0.04613529805760884$, $0.10767526667768725$, $0.03980793595345331$, $0.04454565117192484$, $0.032090091625816136$, and $0.29837624987287115$ for Danio rerio, Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, Gallus gallus, Homo sapiens, Mycobacterium smegmatis str. mc2 155, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae respectively.  These numbers identify where a modeled-tAI complement remains in the abundance component explained by $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates; they do not identify a causal biochemical path.

\paragraph{Readout ranking against the non-modeled remainder.}
The direction-aware readout ranking uses the regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, while also reporting the raw product $A_H C_H$.  In Danio rerio, measured translation efficiency has $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, score $0.001566051846174291$, $n=13140$, Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$, and null $95$th percentile $0.0003297458343745959$; mRNA is close behind with score $0.000734588577977589$ at the same joined row count.  In Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, the available mRNA and measured-TE rows have negative regularized scores, $-0.008470202379021127$ and $-0.009446333814243976$, with joined row count $3415$.  In Homo sapiens, the measured-TE row has $A_H=0.002894714925796667$, $C_H=-0.10813537216189718$, $A_HC_H=-0.00031302107580362116$, score $-0.01031302107580362$, joined row count $11554$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0004670613303535708$, and null $95$th percentile $0.0003390166378989019$.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, measured TE has score $-0.009937495556453664$ at $n=5128$, while mRNA is the highest ranked row with score $-0.008853737772559931$.  The classification is therefore organism-specific: a measured-TE row is the best residual readout in Danio rerio, no selected readout is positive after regularization in Escherichia coli, and mRNA or complex-related rows can outrank measured TE in other organisms.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The ready statement is intentionally narrow.  The finite record supports a modeled translation-readout survival surface for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ under explicit controls, and it records proxy coupling plus organism-specific downstream diagnostics.  It does not establish ribosome-mediated translation from tRNA gene-copy tables alone; it does not identify tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the controlled statistic is recomputed.  In particular, a positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not promoted to a positive survival entry $S_{ij}$ without the $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length comparison, and a modeled-tAI survival entry is kept separate from measured ribo-seq or proteomics claims.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The claim is ready as a BioReality chapter about a bounded survival-matrix interface: the $40$-organism modeled-tAI result gives a nondegenerate $9\times 9$ controlled survival surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, while the three-organism Leu gate and the six-organism complement/ranking rows explain why the contact should not be read as a universal biological rule.  The measured-ribosome and matched-proteomics branches remain open measurement requirements, not consequences of the modeled-tAI computation.
