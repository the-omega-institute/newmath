\subsection{Broken third-position symmetry and translation readout}\label{subsec:q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate on coding sequences, restricted to finite organism rows for which codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and a matched translation-layer readout can be placed on the same residual surface.  The carrier is not a protein-level object.  It is a finite CDS-by-organism table after amino-acid projection, with the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance retained only as a residual coordinate.  The observable question is whether this residual coordinate remains visible in a bounded translation row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after controlling amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, $M$-density, and mRNA abundance where the readout is TE.

\paragraph{Saturation row.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation check gives an explicit boundary for the coordinate.  The verified mediated set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding saturated $f_3$ image of the $R$ row removes exactly $ACA$ and $ACG$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
& UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary list is empty, so the finite topological row by itself supplies no surviving boundary codon.  This is why the conjecture is not a claim from codon topology alone.  The coordinate becomes biologically testable only when a context-dependent tRNA or wobble weight assigns different values to an $f_3$ pair, $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$, and the resulting asymmetry is then tested against a separate translation readout.

\paragraph{Powered finite contacts.}
The available finite rows give several controlled reasons to keep the conjecture open.  In the tRNA-poverty boundary test, $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped; the test used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$ and passed.  Its bounded conclusion is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a supply-side explanation for $R$ avoidance within that contact.  In the universal core tRNA-adaptation test, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; the run passed.  The residual-axis test on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel, again with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins, and $41$ synonymous columns, identified $f3\_translation\_selection$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual axis.  The two-axis closure test on the same powered design concluded that a third axis is present, because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
For an organism row $o$, a CDS row $g$, the third-position residual coordinate $q_{f3}(g,o)$, a translation readout $T_j(g,o)$, and a control block $Z(g,o)$ containing amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and mRNA abundance when available, the admissible survival entry is the controlled gain
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}
  &= \operatorname{score}\bigl(T_j \mid Z,q_{f3}\bigr)
     - \operatorname{score}\bigl(T_j \mid Z\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive conjectural instance requires $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0$ after the simpler codon-baseline ablation.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix across three organisms is not survival; it is a null boundary because it does not supply the contrast needed to estimate a controlled residual entry.  Conversely, negative or zero survival does not show that the $Q6$ coordinate is irrelevant to biology in general.  It only says that this coordinate has not crossed the specified translation-readout interface under the available controls.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The claim is therefore conditional and cross-layer: after amino-acid quotienting, the $f_3$-stress residual should survive as a translation-readout distinction only in organismal or sequence contexts where tRNA or wobble weights break third-position $f_3$ symmetry.  The finite powered rows make this plausible enough to test, because tRNA poverty, tRNA adaptation, a dominant $f3\_translation\_selection$ axis, and a same-signed third residual axis have all been observed in bounded panels.  They do not by themselves establish ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those conclusions require their own matched contacts.  In this chapter the only allowed readback is the controlled translation entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  If every such entry is non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered multi-organism panel, the conjecture fails at the translation interface.
