\subsection{Position-window $f_3$-stress and ribosome-occupancy boundary}\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-window-ribosome-occupancy-translation-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The object of this record is a finite coding-sequence window table.  A row is indexed by organism, coding-sequence identifier, codon-position bin, synonymous amino-acid fiber, $f_3$ partner class, residual $f_3$-stress coordinate, wobble or tAI weight surface, matched mRNA control, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readout.  The coordinate is not evaluated on raw codon frequencies.  It is evaluated only after amino-acid quotienting and baseline residualization, so that the row asks whether a remaining synonymous residual survives at the translation-readout layer.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
Let $x$ denote a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window row after amino-acid composition, position bin, GC3, length, M-density, and matched mRNA controls have been attached.  The tested statistic is a bounded survival readback
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{window}}\mid Z)
  &= \operatorname{Surv}\bigl(\text{position-resolved occupancy window};\; B^{*}_{Q6}(f_3), Z\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\text{amino-acid fiber},\text{position bin},\text{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\text{length},M\text{-density},\text{wobble/tAI surface},\text{mRNA control}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statistic is a window-level translation readout.  It is not a protein-abundance statistic, not a folding statistic, not a stability statistic, not a localization statistic, not a function statistic, and not a phenotype statistic.  The dynamic case is admissible only when the declared wobble-weight asymmetry satisfies $w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ and the same row also carries codon counts, readout values, and controls.

\paragraph{Finite evidence already present.}
The finite structural-order run computed nine coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,\mathrm{vs}\,AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,\mathrm{vs}\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The run used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, and identified exactly one coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  That coordinate was $f_3$-stress.  Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, its sign-flip probability for a positive increment was $0.03125$, and its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$.  The same run ranked $f_3$-stress first by weighted increment and first by loading.

\paragraph{Coordinate comparison.}
The remaining eight coordinates did not pass the same gate in that run.  Their weighted held-out increments and sign-flip probabilities were, respectively, $0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$ for $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$ for $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $0.0005307046179682497$ with $p=0.2294921875$ for $K_{AAA}$, $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$ for $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUR}$, $0.00017615626369389472$ with $p=0.24609375$ for $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUG}$, $0.00004390882136492317$ with $p=0.4873046875$ for $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UCG}$, $0.0010134090056701938$ with $p=0.2158203125$ for $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,\mathrm{vs}\,AGY}$, and $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$ for $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,\mathrm{vs}\,ACY}$.  These numbers license the local choice of $f_3$-stress as the candidate coordinate for the ribosome-window boundary, but they do not themselves measure ribosome pausing.

\paragraph{Target-null ceiling.}
A separate target-permutation dictionary-ceiling run used $B=200$ permutation draws and returned the verdict that the observed $C^{*}$ crossed the matched target-null $95\%$ ceiling.  The reported scalar conclusion was that $24.5\%$ of the signal survived the target-permutation comparison as structure rather than target noise.  The deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256}:\text{b\_star\_q6\_pq\_target\_permutation\_dictionary\_ceiling\_powered}|\text{seed}=70651|\text{organism}|\text{trial}|\text{subset}|\text{stratum}$, and the run time was $441.9897983074188$ seconds.  This ceiling result supports treating the coordinate as a finite candidate for a downstream readout test; it still does not supply the downstream readout.

\paragraph{Required contact rows.}
A row can support the translation-window question only if four contact surfaces are joined without changing row identity.  First, ordered codon-usage data must supply codon counts or frequencies for the same organism and coding sequence.  Second, a bounded tAI, stAI, or wobble-weight surface must state the codon-level translation-weight context without being treated as a direct observation of ribosome motion.  Third, matched mRNA abundance must be available in the same experimental condition, so that ribosome-footprint signal can be separated from transcript abundance.  Fourth, codon-indexed or fixed-window ribosome-footprint occupancy must be joined to the same ordered coding-sequence coordinates.  The intended readout row is therefore keyed by organism, transcript or CDS identifier, codon index or local window, and ribosome-profiling sample.

\paragraph{Support condition.}
The claim is supported only in the following narrow sense.  After amino-acid quotienting and residualization, $f_3$-stress may be evaluated as a bounded predictor of a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency window statistic in contexts where wobble or tAI weights break the $f_3$ symmetry.  The admissible positive statement is survival of $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{window}}\mid Z)$ under the declared controls and rank checks.  The record therefore concerns a translation-readout boundary: it asks whether the coordinate remains visible when an actual occupancy surface is attached.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The null answer must be returned whenever the organism panel is rank-degenerate, the same-CDS join is missing, the wobble or tAI surface is absent, the mRNA control is absent, the ribosome-footprint window is absent, or the row can be explained by simpler position, amino-acid-bin, GC3, length, M-density, coverage, or mappability baselines.  A modeled tAI or wobble asymmetry by itself is only a context annotation.  It is not an observed pause, not a measured occupancy window, and not a translation-efficiency measurement.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from codon geometry alone.  No folding, structural admissibility, physical admissibility, localization, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from $f_3$-stress survival.  No protein-abundance or protein-stability claim follows from the window statistic.  Each of those layers would require its own same-row measurement and its own control design.  This chapter records only the finite condition under which a residual synonymous coordinate may be tested against position-resolved ribosome occupancy, and the condition under which the answer must remain null.
