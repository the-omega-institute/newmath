\subsection{Position-resolved f\_3-stress as a translation-survival conjecture}
\label{sec:q6-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Claim record.}
This chapter records the conjecture that the $f_3$-stress coordinate in the $B^*_Q6$ family can survive as a bounded cross-layer statistic only after it is joined to position-resolved translation data on the same coding-sequence windows. The carrier is a finite table whose row is a CDS window together with an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, local synonymous-codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls. The proposed object is not a law about codons in isolation. It is a rowwise survival question: after quotienting away amino-acid identity, does the remaining $f_3$ direction still improve a position-resolved translation readout once the ordinary sequence, expression, and position covariates have already been charged to the baseline?

\paragraph{Finite row shape.}
For a row $r$ let $T_r$ denote the local ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, let $F_{3,r}$ denote the amino-acid-quotiented $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, and let $Z_r$ collect the required controls: amino-acid composition or position bin, GC3, CDS length, methionine density, local codon-count terms, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability filters, and any simpler codon-usage baseline. The conjectural survival entry is the controlled gain
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)
  &= \Delta_{\mathrm{held\ out}}\bigl(T; Z + F_3,\, Z\bigr),\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
where the inequality is evaluated on held-out rows and is not allowed to be replaced by an in-sample association. The wobble term is part of the same row: the conjecture is only meaningful in contexts where the observed or curated wobble-weight surface is asymmetric, written informally as $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$. If the row set lacks this asymmetry, the $f_3$ direction is treated as unpowered for this translation-survival test.

\paragraph{Attached contacts.}
The contact named by the conjecture is position-resolved ribosome occupancy: per-CDS ribosome-footprint occupancy can be represented at codon or local-window resolution when a ribosome-profiling dataset is joined to ordered CDS coordinates. The matching condition is stringent. Rows must be keyed by organism, transcript or CDS identifier, codon position, and ribosome-profiling sample before any $B^*_Q6$ statistic is computed. A matched mRNA abundance row is also required, so that a translation-efficiency residual can separate ribosome footprint signal from transcript abundance. Organism-scoped tAI or stAI weights and per-organism codon-usage summaries may supply bounded weight and composition surfaces, but those modeled or summary resources are not ribosome dynamics by themselves.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite evidence.}
The current finite evidence does not yet contain the same-window ribosome-occupancy table demanded by this conjecture. It does, however, give calibrated neighboring examples of the scale and control discipline expected for a cross-layer row test. One linked structural-order boundary was run with $5$ folds, a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, and joined structural-order counts of $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. In that run, the $B^*_Q6$ aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ was positive in both organisms, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2 &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\operatorname{median}\Delta R^2 &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\max \Delta R^2 &= 0.007880037229394898,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the weighted full-control value was $0.004764742175882462$. The sign-flip probability for a positive full-control value was $0.0029296875$. The matched permuted full-control comparison was negative in both organisms, with mean $-0.00212186632044159$, minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, and maximum $-0.000779938262202795$. A separate finite-state dwell-transducer run for the $B^*_Q6$ order-memory execution signal reported status $\mathrm{passed}$. A further promoter-expression boundary used $75111$ designed sequences, $8027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subsample of $2500$ high-quality rows, $200$ permutations, seed $20260239$, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds. These numbers are not evidence for the present $f_3$-stress translation conjecture; they are included to pin the surrounding record to finite computations rather than to verbal plausibility.

\paragraph{Survival and failure conditions.}
The conjecture passes only if a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window table shows positive held-out improvement after all declared controls and simpler-baseline ablations. The admissible positive call is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z) &> 0,\\
\text{with } Z &\supseteq \{\mathrm{AA/bin},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},\mathrm{M\ density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{coverage},\mathrm{mappability}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The conjecture breaks if the packet treats review status, notation, modeled wobble weights, codon-window geometry, or organism-level codon tables as if they were already a computed same-window ribosome-occupancy statistic. It also breaks if a positive association is reported before rank checks, coverage filters, mappability checks, position controls, and ablation against simpler codon baselines have been applied.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows from $f_3$-stress geometry alone. No protein abundance, folding statement, physical admissibility statement, functional claim, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter. A modeled tAI or stAI surface is a bounded codon-adaptation annotation, not an observed footprint profile. A codon-usage table is a sequence-composition summary, not a translation measurement. Any downstream assertion at the protein, structure, function, mechanism, or organismal layer must be supplied by a separate matched contact at that layer and cannot be inherited from the $f_3$ coordinate or from the survival notation.
