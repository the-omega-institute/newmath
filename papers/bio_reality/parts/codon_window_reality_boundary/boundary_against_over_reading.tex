\subsection{Boundary against over-reading}
\label{subsec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-boundary}

\paragraph{What the row proves.}
The strongest supported statement is a cross-layer statistical one: in a twelve-organism joined CDS, tRNA-copy, modeled-tAI, and PAXdb abundance table, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive mediated component through modeled per-protein tAI after the stated sequence controls.  This is a protein-abundance association conditioned through a translation annotation, not a demonstration that a ribosome measured the same quantity in vivo.  The modeled-tAI boundary is visible in the smaller survival run as well: only three organisms were present there, the readout was modeled from GtRNAdb and dos Reis weights, $81$ entries exceeded the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold, and the reported maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ was $3801961.031607896$; however, the run itself labels the result underpowered for powered survival because $3$ organisms are far below the declared $40$-species requirement.

\paragraph{Matched mRNA and structure checks.}
The yeast five-way structure-and-expression table gives a useful local check on the same family of rows.  It joined $4759$ genes, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and total join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  In that table $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, the right-tail permutation value for $S_Q$ structure was $0.027972027972027972$, and the right-tail value for the structure-incremental statistic was $0.1258741258741259$.  These numbers support a conservative reading: much of the yeast abundance residual association is attenuated by translation and mRNA-stability covariates, while the structure proxy contributes only a small sequence-derived partition and does not establish folded-state realization.

\paragraph{Independent negatives and narrow positives.}
Other finite-row probes keep the chapter from promoting the mediation statistic into a general biological law.  The mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; adding the final $B^*_{Q6}$ layer lowered held-out fit, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and found $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$, so the same coordinate family did not improve measured turnover beyond expression, TE, pLDDT, N-end, and related controls.  The evolutionary imprint audit on $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species found $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95$ threshold $0.05427157066522958$, and no positive selection-imprint gate.  By contrast, the observable execution decomposition did report a bounded subblock in yeast with $3289$ genes, and the structural-order axis audit identified $f3\_stress$ as the sole significant axis with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.

\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
The chapter therefore admits a precise readback and rejects larger interpretations.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not counted as protein realization unless the same row includes a translation readout and the controlled decomposition is evaluated.  Modeled tAI and stAI weights are translation annotations, not observed ribosome occupancy.  PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-abundance contact, not a folding, localization, turnover, post-translational modification, or functional assay.  Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control, not a translation measurement by itself.  No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal pathway, selection principle, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that contact and the corresponding controlled statistic is recomputed on the same finite rows.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence is sufficient to write the chapter as a bounded cross-layer relation: the twelve-organism table supports positive modeled-tAI mediation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual association, with all reported $M^{QTP}$ values positive and in $[0,1]$.  The same evidence is not sufficient for a stronger claim involving measured ribosome occupancy, matched-condition mRNA-isolated translation efficiency across all organisms, folded protein state, protein function, perturbational rescue, or evolutionary law.  The correct chapter status is therefore ready for a controlled statistical mediation statement and explicitly closed against mechanism or universality.
