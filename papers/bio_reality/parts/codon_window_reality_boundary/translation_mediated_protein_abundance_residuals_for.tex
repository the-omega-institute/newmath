\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance residuals for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-cross-layer}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual packet as a finite mediation table whose rows have the form $(q_i,T,P)$.  The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional residual list
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\texttt{K\_AAA},\texttt{Arg\_AGR},\texttt{Ile\_AUA},\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\texttt{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mediator $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream readout $P$ is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in ppm from PAXdb, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The regression surface residualizes amino-acid composition, organism-specific rows, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline before testing whether the $Q$ block is diminished after conditioning on $T$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The powered mediation run used twelve organism-scoped joins: 4042 proteins for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, 11661 for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, 14410 for \emph{Danio rerio}, 13295 for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, 3739 for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, 12418 for \emph{Gallus gallus}, 19053 for human, 19395 for mouse, 4129 for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, 13322 for rat, 5815 for yeast, and 1656 for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In every organism-specific residual design the control rank was 23, the residualized $Q$ rank was 9, the residualized $T$ rank was 1, and the combined $Q,T$ rank was 10.  Thus the reported mediation fractions are not produced by a rank collapse of the fitted design.  The residual degrees of freedom ranged from 1633 in \emph{S. solfataricus} to 19372 in mouse, with all organism rows marked rank-sufficient under the declared degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
The finite scalar reported for each organism is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)},\\
R^2(Q\to P)&=c,\qquad R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=c'.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest mediated fraction was yeast, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, indirect $R^2=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Several bacteria and metazoa also had positive finite mediation fractions: \emph{E. coli} had $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ with indirect $R^2=0.06599656708487024$; \emph{Drosophila} had $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$ with indirect $R^2=0.047530538755721025$; \emph{B. subtilis} had $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$ with indirect $R^2=0.037667440654802556$; \emph{C. elegans} had $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$ with indirect $R^2=0.04978438377417191$; zebrafish had $M^{QTP}=0.3157053953614641$; human had $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$; chicken had $M^{QTP}=0.1976903964724546$; rat had $M^{QTP}=0.17489681798543982$; mouse had $M^{QTP}=0.15488799907323988$; \emph{M. smegmatis} had $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$; and \emph{S. solfataricus} had $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$.  Every reported $M^{QTP}$ value lies in the unit interval, so the finite table supports a statistical attenuation statement rather than a sign-flipped or overfit mediation artifact.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The separate modeled-tAI survival run computed the same nine synonymous residual coordinates in a 61-sense-codon space for three organisms.  The GtRNAdb payload covered all 20 standard amino-acid families in \emph{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast; matched tRNA FASTA header counts were 87 of 89 for \emph{E. coli}, 432 of 432 for human, and 275 of 275 for yeast.  The modeled tAI matrix had 81 entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$.  This is a real finite coupling computation against tRNA-copy-derived weights, but its own audit states that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that this is modeled tAI coupling rather than ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Nearby biological checks.}
The yeast structure-mediation table gives a useful comparison.  In 4759 five-way joined yeast rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation value for the structure-incremental fraction was $0.1258741258741259$.  Thus the abundance residual is much more visibly attenuated by the measured-TE plus mRNA-stability block than by the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy alone.  A mass-balance audit on 120 genes and 718 intervals gave $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, and $\Delta$ description length $=-668.8046070381928$ bits, with time-shuffle $p=0.9306930693069307$ and TE-bin $p=0.6831683168316832$.  A turnover audit on 3290 genes similarly stayed negative beyond expression, TE, pLDDT, and N-end controls, with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta$ description length $=-13.68819707902732$ bits, matched-bin $p=0.8855721393034826$, and recoded $p=1.0$.  These checks keep the mediation record from being read as a general protein-state claim.

\paragraph{Permitted conclusion.}
The allowed statement is narrow.  In finite joined CDS, modeled-tAI, and protein-abundance rows, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive statistical attenuation through modeled per-protein tAI in every reported organism, with the strongest scalar in yeast and additional positive entries across bacteria, archaea, fungi, insects, fish, birds, nematodes, and mammals.  This licenses a controlled cross-layer association of the form $Q\to T\to P$ for the declared finite table.  It does not license a direct $Q\to P$ biological realization claim when the $Q\to T$ step is absent, null, or untested.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No measured translation dynamics, folding path, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this table.  Modeled tAI is not ribosome occupancy; PAXdb abundance is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or assay aggregation; and a positive mediated fraction is not a synonymous-edit rescue experiment.  A stronger claim would require a separate matched contact at the target layer, for example organism- and CDS-matched ribosome profiling with matched mRNA for observed TE, perturbation data for causal translation effects, structure data for folding or order, turnover assays for stability, localization assays for compartment state, and explicit cross-organism controls before any population-level rule is asserted.
