\subsection{Translation-bounded RNA half-life and decay-factor occupancy}
\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel whose rows carry $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate labels, a same-row translation readout when available, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and externally attached decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation columns when those columns exist. The intended biological question is narrow: whether a coordinate-associated RNA half-life difference can be treated as a bounded cross-layer relation only after it follows measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, survives coordinate and occupancy decoupling, collapses under target-RBP loss, and returns under wild-type rescue while failing under binding-defective or non-target controls. Without those columns, the coordinate is only a descriptor of a finite sequence window or, at most, a finite RNA-stability association.

\paragraph{Available finite evidence.}
The currently verified finite-row material is not yet the required RNA-decay/RBP perturbation panel. The locally checked cross-layer ubiquitylation occupancy run, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.ubiquitylation\_occupancy.ub\_occupancy\_acidic\_microenvironment\_steger2024}, reports $2883$ proteins, $10410$ ubiquitylation sites, and $2000$ actual permutations, with deterministic stdout and wall time measured by the harness. Two neighboring checked cross-layer controls are also present in the evidence bundle: \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_degron\_stability.degron\_terminal\_motif\_stability\_voutsinos2026} reports $5303$ C-terminal rows over $5303$ proteins with runtime $40.3029$ seconds, and \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_thermal\_stability.scd\_charge\_patterning\_thermal\_stability\_meltome\_human} reports $6961$ rows with a deterministic runtime bucket of $70.0$ seconds. These numbers certify that finite cross-layer row audits exist in the bundle, but they do not supply same-window RNA half-life, decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, rescue, or binding-defective controls for the RNA-decay claim.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
A positive RNA-decay bridge would require a declared finite statistic on rows of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i &= (q_i,T_i,A_i,H_i,O_i,L_i,R_i,C_i,Z_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout, $A_i$ is matched mRNA abundance, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $O_i$ is measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, $L_i$ marks target-RBP loss, $R_i$ marks wild-type rescue, $C_i$ marks binding-defective or non-target controls, and $Z_i$ contains transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, amino-acid composition, batch, rank-sufficiency diagnostics, and simpler-baseline controls. The support condition is not merely that $q_i$ predicts $H_i$. It is that the coordinate-associated half-life contrast is explained by measured occupancy after coordinate/occupancy decoupling, disappears under target-RBP loss, and is restored only by wild-type rescue.

\paragraph{Observed gap.}
The available contacts provide an RNA half-life boundary, a matched mRNA abundance control route, and a ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency route. They do not yet provide measured decay-factor RBP occupancy in the same organism and same local window; they also do not provide target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, or the finite RBP-decay half-life statistic. Consequently, the present row cannot be promoted from a bounded review record into a positive RNA-decay mechanism claim. The verified ubiquitylation, degron, and thermal-stability runs remain useful as finite-row precedents for cross-layer auditing, but their proteins, sites, and C-terminal or thermal rows are not substitute RNA-decay rows.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone. No protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal biological law follows from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate labels, codon/window geometry, matched mRNA abundance, or RNA half-life readout by themselves. No RNA-decay mechanism follows unless measured decay-factor RBP occupancy is present and experimentally separated from coordinate state. No RBP bridge follows unless target-RBP loss collapses the half-life effect and wild-type rescue restores it while binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it. The same-row translation readout is a gate on interpretation, not evidence of RNA decay by itself.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
This packet is therefore not ready as a positive cross-layer RNA-decay/RBP result. It is ready only as a bounded finite-row specification and as a negative audit of the missing columns. The next admissible data object is a same-organism, same-window panel joining $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate labels, translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and the declared controlled statistic.
