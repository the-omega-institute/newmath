\subsection{Translation-bounded RNA decay and measured RBP occupancy}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats the proposed bridge as a finite matched panel of synonymous RNA/CDS windows. Each row carries a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate label, a same-row translation readout flag or value, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and, only when supplied by an attached assay, prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation columns. The coordinate is not allowed to select its own mechanism after seeing the outcome. A high-coordinate versus low-coordinate contrast is therefore admissible only as a row-level descriptor until the panel also contains measured local RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.

A separate cross-layer finite row, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_thermal\_stability.scd\_charge\_patterning\_thermal\_stability\_meltome\_human}, reports experiment run \texttt{8873440304f9}, with $6961$ rows and a $70.0$ second deterministic runtime bucket, whose note states that the shell time was independently verified below $300$ seconds. These numbers are used here only as finite-row scale and audit-contact evidence: they show that the BioReality ledger contains bounded cross-layer assays with explicit row counts, but they do not by themselves supply RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, or RBP perturbation rows for the present RNA-decay question.

\paragraph{Admissible readback.}
The admissible statistic is a bounded finite readback of whether coordinate-associated RNA half-life differences follow measured decay-factor RBP occupancy after the same-row translation readout and matched mRNA controls are present. In symbols, the intended row family has at least the fields
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i = (&q_i, t_i, h_i, a_i, o_i, \ell_i, w_i, b_i, n_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the residual coordinate label, $t_i$ is the translation readout, $h_i$ is RNA half-life, $a_i$ is matched mRNA abundance, $o_i$ is measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, $\ell_i$ is target-RBP loss state, $w_i$ is wild-type rescue state, $b_i$ is binding-defective control state, and $n_i$ is non-target control state. A positive bridge requires the half-life contrast to track $o_i$ after decoupling $q_i$ from occupancy, to collapse under $\ell_i$, and to return under $w_i$ but not under $b_i$ or $n_i$. If any of these columns is absent, the coordinate remains a finite RNA-stability association at most.

\paragraph{Local null.}
The null model is row exchangeability of high- and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life after transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation boundary are controlled. The present payload does not contain same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective and non-target control rows. It also does not contain an explicit finite RBP-decay half-life statistic for this subject. Therefore the record is ready as a scoped negative or boundary statement, not as a positive RBP-mediated RNA-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
RNA half-life is not a translation measurement. Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control, and ribosome profiling or translation efficiency is a separate translation-layer readout. The local rule is consequently strict: no translation, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from RNA half-life alone. The same exclusion applies to the available ubiquitylation and thermal-stability finite rows: their counts certify bounded assays in other cross-layer locations, but they cannot be promoted into evidence for RNA decay, decay-factor RBP occupancy, perturbation rescue, or translation realization in this panel.

\paragraph{Chapter conclusion.}
This BioReality entry is a finite, row-scoped record for a proposed $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-bounded RNA-decay/RBP bridge. The row counts $2883$, $10410$, $2000$, and $6961$ are concrete audit anchors from the attached finite experiments, while the decisive RBP-decay columns remain absent. The correct current reading is therefore: a coordinate-associated RNA half-life contrast may be investigated, but it is not yet a cross-layer RBP bridge. It becomes one only after a same-organism, same-window panel supplies measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, decoupling of coordinate from occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and a declared finite statistic that survives the stated controls.
