\paragraph{Local carrier.}
The rows are read as triples consisting of a protein identifier, a site-local ubiquitylation coordinate, and a measured or derived acidic-neighborhood descriptor. The intended comparison is a bounded relation between occupancy at the ubiquitylation site and the acidic microenvironment around that site. In the row language of this chapter, a positive finite signal would mean only that the site labels and local acidic descriptors are not exchangeable under the declared permutation design. It would not by itself identify the enzyme, substrate-recognition route, degradation consequence, structural exposure state, or cellular phenotype.

\paragraph{Statistic meaning.}
For the ubiquitylation-acidic panel, the admissible finite statistic is a site-level contrast between occupied or annotated ubiquitylation coordinates and their local acidic context, evaluated against the declared permutation reference. In schematic form, the chapter only needs the following bounded reading:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{row} &= (\text{protein},\text{site},\text{acidic context},\text{occupancy label}),\\
\text{question} &= \text{whether the occupancy label remains associated with the acidic context under the finite null},\\
\text{budget} &= 10410\text{ sites},\ 2883\text{ proteins},\ 2000\text{ permutations}.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The packet must not be promoted from a site-level occupancy association into a statement about translation, RNA behavior, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, degradation mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality. A separate contact would be required for each such promotion. For example, a degradation mechanism would require perturbation, rescue, substrate-turnover, and relevant enzyme or reader controls; a folding or exposure claim would require a structure-level or accessibility-level measurement; a functional claim would require an assay at the functional layer. The acidic microenvironment statistic supplies none of those contacts by itself.

The broader finite-row bundle names RNA half-life, matched mRNA abundance, and ribosome-profiling contacts, but those contacts do not enter the ubiquitylation-acidic statistic unless a same-row panel explicitly joins them. In particular, an RNA half-life row, an mRNA-control row, or a translation-efficiency row cannot convert the $10410$ ubiquitylation-site panel into an RNA-decay, translation, or protein-output claim. Its concrete audited scale is $10410$ sites, $2883$ proteins, and $2000$ permutations. The chapter is silent about any layer not measured in that panel.
