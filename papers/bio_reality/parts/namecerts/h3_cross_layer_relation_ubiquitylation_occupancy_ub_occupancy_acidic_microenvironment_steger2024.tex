\subsection{Translation-bounded RNA half-life and decay-factor occupancy}\label{subsec:biorality-q6-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.} The record concerns a finite matched RNA/CDS-window panel in which each row carries a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate label, a synonymous-window description, a same-row translation readout when available, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and a reserved place for measured decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation controls. The admissible carrier is therefore not an unrestricted sequence law. It is a finite table whose rows can be partitioned by high-coordinate versus low-coordinate synonymous windows only after amino-acid composition and local sequence controls have been fixed. A positive cross-layer reading would require the half-life contrast to track a prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy signal after the coordinate label and the occupancy state have been experimentally separated.

\paragraph{Observed finite support.} The finite numerical support presently attached to the claim is indirect and bounded. The ubiquitylation-occupancy computation identified $10410$ sites over $2883$ proteins and used $2000$ actual permutations in run $0b96a8e4da3c$. Two neighboring cross-layer computations give scale checks for the broader finite-row environment: the terminal-degron stability run $1a932ef5684e$ used $5303$ C-terminal rows over $5303$ proteins in $40.3029$ seconds, and the charge-pattern thermal-stability run $8873440304f9$ used $6961$ rows with a deterministic runtime bucket of $70.0$ seconds. These numbers show that the surrounding BioReality packet is finite and row-indexed, but they do not by themselves instantiate the RNA half-life/RBP bridge.

\paragraph{Required statistic.} The intended finite statistic can be written as a controlled comparison between two conditional half-life contrasts. Let $q_i$ denote the residual-coordinate bin, $T_i$ the same-row translation readout, $H_i$ the RNA half-life readout, $A_i$ the matched mRNA abundance control, $O_i$ the measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, and $Z_i$ the declared nuisance block. The admissible contrast has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H &= \widehat{E}(H_i \mid q_i=\mathrm{high}, T_i, A_i, O_i, Z_i) - \widehat{E}(H_i \mid q_i=\mathrm{low}, T_i, A_i, O_i, Z_i),\\
\Delta_{H\mid O} &= \widehat{E}(H_i \mid O_i=\mathrm{occupied}, q_i, T_i, A_i, Z_i) - \widehat{E}(H_i \mid O_i=\mathrm{unoccupied}, q_i, T_i, A_i, Z_i).
\end{aligned}
$$
A bridge reading is permitted only if the coordinate-associated half-life effect follows $\Delta_{H\mid O}$, collapses when the target RBP is lost, and returns with wild-type rescue while failing under binding-defective or non-target controls. The control block $Z_i$ includes transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, translation readout, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler baselines.

\paragraph{Current boundary.} The present data do not supply same-organism, same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy. They also do not supply coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective and non-target rescue controls. Consequently the finite record can report only a bounded RNA half-life boundary together with matched translation and mRNA-control requirements. It cannot be promoted to an RNA-decay mechanism, an RBP mechanism, translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law. RNA half-life alone remains an RNA-stability readout; it is not evidence for downstream protein realization unless the same-row translation boundary is independently attached and passes its own controls.

\paragraph{Null reading.} The local null hypothesis is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life after the declared controls are applied. In finite-row form this says that the coordinate label should add no stable half-life information once transcription rate, RNA structure, motif density, composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, batch, and the same-row translation readout have been accounted for. Because the required RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue rows are missing, the present chapter records a constrained target rather than a positive bridge. The correct scientific status is therefore: finite row geometry and neighboring cross-layer computations are available, but the RNA half-life/RBP occupancy bridge is still unlicensed until the missing same-window occupancy and perturbation panel exists.
