\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate at the branchpoint boundary}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row subject.}
This record concerns the proposed cross-layer entry
$S^{QT}_{ij}$ from a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ to an organism-indexed translation readout $T_j$.  The admissible object is not a biological mechanism and not a protein-level interpretation.  It is a finite residualized design matrix whose rows must still contain enough independent post-control variation after the columns $Z$ have absorbed amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The row-level question is whether the proposed translation-survival entry remains testable after those controls, rather than whether an in-sample score can be made large.

The branchpoint-side finite contact currently has $n=10584$ joined human intron rows, with expression controlled by $\log(1+x)$ of the median across 54 GTEx tissue expression scores from the UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8 table.  The join is by same-strand intron overlap, and the recorded runtime for the expression-controlled branchpoint computation is $138.47155$ seconds.  This supplies a concrete finite-row, expression-controlled splice-boundary surface, but it is not itself a translation-readout matrix.  Its role here is to discipline the admissibility rule: finite contacts may be useful only at the layer they actually measure.

\paragraph{Residualization rule.}
For each candidate pair $(q_i,T_j)$, the gate first removes the declared controls from both sides:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i-\Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j-\Pi_Z T_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
The proposed survival entry is admissible only when the residualized row space is not rank-saturated and still has enough degrees of freedom to make a held-out failure possible.  A perfect fitted surface on too few rows, or on a design whose residual rank has consumed the available variation, is therefore a refusal signal rather than evidence for survival.  The gate separates three cases: a rank-admissible translation-survival entry, a coupling-only contact, and a projection artifact caused by saturation.

\paragraph{Present finite evidence.}
The finite $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coupling computation loaded three organisms, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  It used 61 codon-count coordinates and computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, two leucine contrasts, three serine/threonine contrasts, and $f_3$ stress.  Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the break condition was not met: not all nine $q_i$ had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  The largest absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, and all nine entries exceeded the stated nonzero threshold in the coupling matrix.  This is a coupling surface $A^{QT}$, not a controlled survival surface $S^{QT}$.

The broader residual-basis extraction supplies a negative finite-row context.  Homo sapiens had $16008$ joined rows and six selected readout blocks, with $\rho$ decreasing from $1.0$ to $0.9723341658092314$ and label persistent.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $3046$ joined rows and nine selected blocks, ending at $0.9798667977250413$ and also persistent.  Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 had $3415$ joined rows and two selected blocks, ending at $0.9979478314995476$.  Danio rerio had $14410$ joined rows and one selected block, ending at $0.9791828971490305$.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. mc2 155 remain needs-data cases.  The regularizer was recorded as $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.  The minimum final residual floor among computed organisms was $0.9723341658092314$, and all four computed organisms were classified as persistent rather than compressible.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The residual-basis packet also records degree-of-freedom matched Gaussian null checks.  In Homo sapiens, selected readout increments above their null $95\%$ levels include PTM density with increment $0.014780177462400674$ versus null $p95=0.0002845846156909306$, complex membership with $0.004618389163869967$ versus $0.000206488525646134$, transmembrane count with $0.0014438137599134737$ versus $0.0002435268444123588$, measured tRNA/tAI with $0.0005032876020244136$ versus $0.00020173994173736226$, and localization with $0.006528084968494762$ versus $0.0011598998794309233$; the domain-count step did not exceed its null, with $3.840392718095609\times 10^{-5}$ versus $0.00021932006910265595$.  In yeast, measured translation efficiency, PTM density, transmembrane count, turnover, and localization exceeded their local null checks, while complex membership, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, and mRNA did not.  In E. coli, the mRNA step exceeded its null but the measured-translation-efficiency step did not.  In zebrafish, measured tRNA/tAI exceeded its null.  These numbers show finite readout structure, but they do not by themselves create a rank-admissible $S^{QT}$ entry.

\paragraph{Related finite contacts.}
Two other cross-layer finite contacts help locate the boundary.  The uORF--Kozak ribosome-load sample contains $41862$ fixed rows and $16739$ varying rows, with two-sample concordance recorded and runtime $131.941$ seconds.  The mRNA-stability contact contains $16500$ human rows and $18924$ mouse rows, uses a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, and reports raw $\rho=0.126224$ with runtime $46.349$ seconds.  These contacts establish that translation-adjacent and RNA-stability surfaces can be represented by finite rows, but neither substitutes for a same-row, residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}\to T$ survival matrix under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Admissibility verdict.}
The local rule is therefore:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Admissible}(S^{QT}_{ij})
&\Longleftrightarrow
\operatorname{rank}\bigl([q_i^{\perp Z},T_j^{\perp Z}]\bigr)
< \operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{heldout}}\\
&\quad\text{and the held-out readout can falsify the proposed entry.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the controlled design is rank-saturated, underpowered, or fitted only through a proxy coupling, the entry remains coupling-only or needs-data.  In particular, a modeled tAI or stAI contact, a tRNA gene-copy spectrum, a positive proxy $A^{QT}$ entry, or an all-$1.0$ fitted surface does not count as translation survival unless the matched residualized design has a non-saturated rank and a held-out translation readout.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter makes no claim about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Codon-usage rows, tRNA-copy rows, and tAI or stAI weights may define bounded translation-adjacent readbacks, but they do not observe protein output or downstream biological realization.  The expression-controlled branchpoint panel is likewise a splice-boundary finite contact, not a translation matrix.  Any later promotion beyond this gate requires a separate layer-matched contact and a new statistic computed on the appropriate joined rows.
