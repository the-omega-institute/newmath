\subsection{Rank-admissible translation readout before branchpoint promotion}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-branchpoint-gate}

\paragraph{Finite-row carrier.}
The finite object in this chapter is a residualized design matrix whose rows are indexed by organism or matched coding-sequence readout units, whose coordinate columns are the synonymous residual coordinates $B^*_{Q6}$, and whose control block is denoted by $Z$. The intended readout is a bounded translation surface $T_j$, such as codon-adaptation weight, tRNA-copy-derived weight, ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, or another explicitly named translation-layer quantity. The branchpoint-usage record is not used here as a translation measurement; it supplies a same-discipline reminder that a finite biological contact may be large and controlled while still remaining layer-specific. In the Mercer-style branchpoint packet, the verified finite panel has $n=10584$ intron-overlap rows, expression is controlled, and the expression covariate is $\log(1+\operatorname{median}_{54}\,\mathrm{GTEx})$ from UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8, joined by same-strand intron overlap. The runtime of that finite computation is $138.47155$ seconds. Those numbers establish a controlled branchpoint-surface row count, not a translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Admissibility statistic.}
For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, the gate first residualizes the candidate coordinate $q_i$ and translation readout $T_j$ against $Z$, then asks whether the remaining design has non-saturated rank and enough row support for a held-out survival test. The intended finite predicate is not merely that an in-sample fit is positive. It is that the residualized design leaves degrees of freedom after the controls and still supports an out-of-sample or held-out falsification boundary. In symbols, the entry is eligible only when the post-control matrix has rank strictly below the number of effective rows and the candidate contribution is not completely absorbed by $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= (I-P_Z)q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= (I-P_Z)T_j,\\
\operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j]) &< n_{\mathrm{eff}},\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij} &= n_{\mathrm{eff}}-\operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j]) > 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
If this condition fails, the entry is assigned to coupling-only or needs-data status, even if the fitted in-sample score is positive or exactly $1.0$. This is the central refusal rule of the chapter: a rank-saturated projection cannot be promoted to translation survival.

\paragraph{Null-comparison readback.}

These mixed outcomes justify a per-entry certificate rather than a blanket promotion of all $B^*_{Q6}$ contacts.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent finite contacts.}
Two other verified finite contacts help locate the gate without changing its conclusion. A translation-initiation packet has $n_{\mathrm{fixed}}=41862$, $n_{\mathrm{varying}}=16739$, two-sample concordance marked true, and runtime $131.941$ seconds. An mRNA-stability packet has $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16500$, $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18924$, raw $\rho=0.126224$, runtime $46.349$ seconds, and a circularity guard using non-CSC tAI. These facts show that translation-adjacent and RNA-stability-adjacent finite panels exist, but neither supplies the missing rank-admissible $S^{QT}_{ij}$ matrix for the present $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival gate. The branchpoint panel with $10584$ controlled rows likewise remains a branchpoint and expression-controlled contact, not a codon-to-translation survival surface.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore a bounded gate statement. A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry may be considered only when the residualized design matrix over $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, translation readouts, and controls $Z$ has non-saturated post-control rank and enough remaining degrees of freedom to make held-out survival falsifiable. The existing finite facts support proxy coupling, persistent residual structure, controlled branchpoint row construction, and several adjacent translation or RNA panels. They do not yet prove measured translation survival for this exact gate. In particular, $A_{ij}$ coupling, tAI or stAI weights, tRNA-copy summaries, an all-$1.0$ fitted surface, or a review-eligible packet cannot be read as ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule. Any such promotion requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
