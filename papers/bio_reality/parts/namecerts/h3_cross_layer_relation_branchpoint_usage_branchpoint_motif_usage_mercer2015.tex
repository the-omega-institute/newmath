\subsection{Rank-admissible translation boundary for branchpoint-motif usage}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-branchpoint-usage-branchpoint-motif-usage-mercer2015}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the Mercer-style branchpoint-motif usage panel as a finite cross-layer audit surface rather than as a direct translation or protein-level claim. The checked row set contains $n=10584$ intron-overlap observations after joining the branchpoint-side rows to an expression control surface. The expression control is present: the control column is $\log(1+x)$ applied to the median of $54$ GTEx tissue expression scores from the UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8 source, joined by intron overlap on the same strand. The recorded finite run took $138.47155$ seconds. In this chapter the relevant question is not whether a branchpoint motif is biologically important in the broad sense, but whether a proposed cross-layer entry can be treated as a rank-admissible survival contact after the declared controls have been applied.

\paragraph{Carrier and boundary.}
Let $Q$ denote the finite family of synonymous residual coordinates over the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ basis, let $T$ denote a translation-readout surface, and let $Z$ collect the control columns. The admissibility test is performed after residualizing both the coordinate and the readout against $Z$. A proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is reportable only when the residual design retains non-saturated rank and enough degrees of freedom for a held-out failure to be possible. The local decision rule is therefore the following finite-row condition:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{is admissible only if } \operatorname{rank}(q_i^{\perp Z},T_j^{\perp Z}) \text{ is not saturated and the held-out score can fail.}
\end{aligned}
$$
When the residual rank is saturated, or when the row support cannot falsify the fitted relation, the entry remains a coupling or routing observation rather than a translation-survival statement. This is especially important for small organism panels, where a perfect in-sample score can be a symptom of rank exhaustion rather than biological survival.

\paragraph{Related finite-row calibration.}
The broader $B^{*}_{Q6}$ memory contains several finite checks that motivate the refusal rule. A three-organism codon and tRNA-copy proxy contact loaded Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12, used a codon count space of $61$, and computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. That proxy matrix had nonzero entries in all $9$ coordinate rows and maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ at threshold $0.01$, but it was explicitly a coupling matrix $A_{ij}$, not a survival matrix $S_{ij}$. Its own break condition was not met because not all nine $q_i$ had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout. The important lesson for the present branchpoint chapter is that positive or large fitted contact is insufficient: the rank gate asks whether the controlled residual surface can fail under held-out testing.

\paragraph{Residual-basis audit.}
The residual-basis extraction run gives a second calibration point. With the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, four organisms had computed residual-basis results and two were marked as needing data. Homo sapiens used $16008$ joined rows and $6$ candidate blocks, with residual curve $(1.0,0.9852198225375993,0.9806696939851618,0.9792537895870558,0.9787609432955213,0.9787233550315274,0.9723341658092314)$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae used $3046$ joined rows and $9$ candidate blocks, with residual curve $(1.0,0.9962498989663637,0.9929843170647626,0.9915730985485388,0.9903314906684438,0.9894054695002641,0.9885902293718664,0.9882386501212531,0.9881524582851271,0.9798667977250413)$. Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 used $3415$ joined rows and $2$ candidate blocks, with residual curve $(1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476)$. Danio rerio used $14410$ joined rows and $1$ candidate block, with residual curve $(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$. The minimum final residual among these computed cases was $0.9723341658092314$, so the finite result is a persistent-residual negative result, not a compression success. This makes the branchpoint record conservative: even substantial row counts do not license promotion unless the declared readout and controls yield a nondegenerate survival statistic.

\paragraph{Control discipline.}
The branchpoint panel is expression-controlled, but expression control alone is not a translation readout. Other finite packets show how row support can exist at neighboring layers without crossing this boundary. The uORF and Kozak ribosome-load packet recorded $41862$ fixed rows, $16739$ varying rows, two-sample concordance, and runtime $131.941$ seconds. The codon-composition mRNA half-life packet recorded $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, runtime $46.349$ seconds, and a non-CSC tAI circularity guard. These facts show that translation-initiation and RNA-stability surfaces can be finite and controlled in their own right, but they do not turn the Mercer-style branchpoint-motif usage rows into a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-survival entry. The present chapter therefore keeps the branchpoint-motif panel at the rank-admissibility and control-boundary level.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
For this finite record, the branchpoint-motif usage surface is ready as a bounded cross-layer audit object: it has $10584$ joined rows, a declared expression control source, and an explicit rule for rejecting rank-saturated or underpowered survival entries. It is not ready as a positive translation-survival claim. No statement here asserts ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanistic causation, phenotype, or a universal biological law. Such claims require their own matched contact surface and their own controlled statistic at the corresponding layer.
