\subsection{Rank-admissible translation survival at the branchpoint interface}
\label{sec:bior-eality-rank-admissible-translation-survival-branchpoint-interface}

\paragraph{Finite empirical surface.}
The record fixes a bounded cross-layer question rather than a biological promotion rule.  The finite branchpoint panel contains $n=10584$ intron-overlap rows and was computed with expression control active.  The expression covariate is the log-one-plus median of $54$ GTEx tissue expression scores from the UCSC hg19 \texttt{gtexGeneV8} track, joined to introns by same-strand overlap, and the recorded run time is $138.47155$ seconds.  This surface is therefore admissible only as an expression-controlled branchpoint-motif readout surface.  It is not, by itself, a translation-readout table, a ribosome table, a protein table, or a structure table.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate side.}
The coordinate side is the finite $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual design.  The computed residual list contains $9$ named coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  These coordinates were evaluated in a $61$-codon count space on the three-organism contact $\{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}$.  The proxy translation-side contact used in that run was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum rather than a direct ribosome or translation-efficiency measurement.  The coupling threshold was $0.01$; the break condition, that all $9$ entries have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, was not met, with $9$ nonzero entries and maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  The conclusion from this finite computation is only that a proxy coupling matrix has a nonzero contact.  It does not license an $S^{QT}$ survival claim.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For a proposed coordinate-readout pair $(q_i,T_j)$, the admissibility object is the residualized finite design after removing the declared controls $Z$: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and M-density baseline, with simpler-codon baselines included when the readout requires them.  Let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote the post-control residual columns and let $X_{ij}$ be the resulting held-out design.  The local gate is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{admissible}(i,j)=1
&\Longleftrightarrow
\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}) < n_{ij}
\ \text{and}\
\operatorname{df}(X_{ij}\mid Z)>0 \\
&\quad\text{and the held-out score for }\widetilde q_i\to \widetilde T_j\text{ is falsifiable.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the rank is saturated, or if the residual degrees of freedom collapse after $Z$, the entry is reported as coupling-only or needs-data even when the in-sample fit is positive or exactly $1.0$.  Thus the mathematical readback of the packet is a per-entry certificate containing rank, degrees of freedom, the readout boundary, and the explicit refusal condition.

\paragraph{Why the branchpoint panel does not promote translation survival.}
The branchpoint result and the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ proxy coupling result live on different finite surfaces.  The branchpoint run supplies an expression-controlled intronic motif-usage panel with $10584$ rows.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ run supplies codon-residual coordinates and a proxy tRNA-copy coupling matrix in three organisms.  A positive branchpoint association cannot be substituted for a same-row translation readout, and a nonzero proxy $A_{ij}$ cannot be substituted for $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.  The required survival statement would need the same finite rows, or an explicitly joined organism/CDS panel, carrying codon counts, a translation readout such as measured TE or ribosome occupancy, the relevant controls, and enough remaining rank to permit held-out failure.

\paragraph{Residual-basis audit.}
The broader residual-basis extraction supports the same caution.  With the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, four organisms were computed and two remained needs-data.  The computed rows were $n=16008$ for human, $n=3046$ for yeast, $n=3415$ for \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655, and $n=14410$ for zebrafish; chicken and \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 were not computable in this packet.  The final residual-ratio floors stayed high: human ended at $0.9723341658092314$, yeast at $0.9798667977250413$, \textit{E. coli} at $0.9979478314995476$, and zebrafish at $0.9791828971490305$.  The packet therefore classifies all four computed organisms as persistent rather than compressible, with minimum final residual ratio $0.9723341658092314$ and computed count $4$.  These are negative compression facts, not failures of the finite audit.

\paragraph{Per-organism selected readout increments.}
The dof-matched null comparison is also finite and bounded.  In human, the selected steps were PTM density with increment $0.014780177462400674$ above null $0.0002845846156909306$, complex membership with $0.004618389163869967$ above $0.000206488525646134$, transmembrane count with $0.0014438137599134737$ above $0.0002435268444123588$, measured tRNA/tAI with $0.0005032876020244136$ above $0.00020173994173736226$, domain count with $3.840392718095609\times 10^{-5}$ below $0.00021932006910265595$, and localization with $0.006528084968494762$ above $0.0011598998794309233$.  In yeast, measured TE, PTM density, transmembrane count, turnover, and localization exceeded their matched nulls, while complex membership, measured tRNA/tAI, domain count, and mRNA did not.  In \textit{E. coli}, mRNA exceeded its null at step $1$ but measured TE did not at step $2$.  In zebrafish, measured tRNA/tAI exceeded its null at the single computed step.  These comparisons identify finite readout contacts that absorb some residual energy; they do not establish a universal mechanism or a translation-survival theorem.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite checks.}
Two neighboring cross-layer records help delimit the boundary.  The translation-initiation panel reports $41862$ fixed rows, $16739$ varying rows, and two-sample concordance, with run time $131.941$ seconds.  The mRNA-stability panel reports human $n=16500$, mouse $n=18924$, raw $\rho=0.126224$, run time $46.349$ seconds, and a non-CSC tAI circularity guard.  These checks show that finite biological readout surfaces can be attached at other boundaries, but they do not fill the missing rank-admissible $B^{\ast}_{Q6}\to T$ survival matrix for the branchpoint subject.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
The chapter therefore records a gate, not a positive translation-survival result.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry is acceptable only when the residualized post-control design has non-saturated rank, positive residual degrees of freedom, and a held-out translation-readout test that could have failed.  If those conditions are absent, the correct output is needs-data or coupling-only.  In particular, neither an expression-controlled branchpoint row set, nor modeled tAI/stAI weights, nor tRNA gene-copy counts, nor an all-$1.0$ fitted surface can be read as measured translation survival without a rank-powered joined readout panel.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
No claim about ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality follows from this chapter.  Each of those statements would require its own layer-matched empirical contact and its own finite statistic after the declared controls.  The only claim made here is the finite mathematical boundary: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates may be proposed for a translation-readout survival test, but each entry must pass the rank-admissibility gate before it can be reported as $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.
