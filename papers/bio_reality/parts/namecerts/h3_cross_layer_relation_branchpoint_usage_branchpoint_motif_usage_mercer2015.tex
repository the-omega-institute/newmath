\subsection{Rank-admissible boundary for branchpoint and translation readbacks}
\label{sec:biorality-branchpoint-usage-mercer2015-rank-admissible-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite contact for the branchpoint-usage record is an expression-controlled human intron panel with $n=10584$ joined rows.  The expression covariate is not an inferred biological mechanism: it is the quantity obtained from UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8 as $\log(1+x)$, where $x$ is the median over $54$ tissue expression scores, and the join is performed by intron overlap on the same strand.  The computation attached to this record ran in $138.47155$ seconds and reports $\mathrm{expr\_controlled}=\mathrm{true}$.  Thus the row object available here is a controlled branchpoint-readout surface, not a protein, translation, folding, phenotype, or universal-law measurement.

\paragraph{Admissibility criterion.}
For a proposed residual coordinate $q_i$ and readout $T_j$, the chapter uses the following finite-row test boundary.  Let $Z$ denote the declared control columns, including amino-acid composition when codon coordinates are involved, organism, GC3, length, and the M-density baseline where it is available.  The admissible part of a proposed survival entry is the residualized problem after both sides have been projected away from $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j - \Pi_Z T_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
The entry may be called a survival entry only when the post-control row space has residual degrees of freedom, the fitted surface is not rank-saturated, and a held-out or otherwise falsifiable readout remains.  If the fit is all in-sample rank geometry, or if the controlled surface has too few independent rows to be broken by a held-out test, the finite record is retained as contact or coupling information only.

\paragraph{What the branchpoint panel establishes.}
The Mercer-style branchpoint record supplies a concrete controlled association surface at the branchpoint-usage layer.  Its row count, $10584$, is large enough to make the expression-control statement non-vacuous for that layer, and the explicit source of the control variable fixes the finite readout: same-strand intron overlap joined to the hg19 GTEx expression table.  The record therefore supports a bounded claim of the following form: branchpoint motif usage can be evaluated on an expression-controlled finite human intron panel.  It does not, by itself, contain any $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, codon-count matrix, tRNA copy spectrum, tAI/stAI readout, ribosome-footprint row, matched protein-abundance row, or translation-efficiency denominator.  Consequently it cannot be promoted into a $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ translation-survival entry.

\paragraph{Relation to the Q6 residual gate.}
The linked Q6 computations show why the rank gate is necessary.  A separate codon-usage computation used a $61$-codon count space and produced nine synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$.  Against a proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, all nine entries were nonzero at threshold $0.01$, with $\max |A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328$ and $9$ nonzero entries, so the coupling break condition was not met.  This remains only an $A_{ij}$ contact: the stated proxy is not a true ribosome or translation-efficiency readout, and the computation explicitly refuses protein folding, structural order, biological function, physical admissibility, population universality, and sign-direction interpretation.

\paragraph{Residual-basis row audit.}
The residual-basis extraction supplies additional finite controls over the same boundary.  With regularization $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, four organisms were computed and all four were classified as persistent rather than compressible.  The joined common-row counts and final residual floors were: human, $n=16008$ with final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$ after $6$ selected candidates; budding yeast, $n=3046$ with final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$ after $9$ selected candidates; Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $n=3415$ with final $\rho=0.9979478314995476$ after $2$ selected candidates; and zebrafish, $n=14410$ with final $\rho=0.9791828971490305$ after $1$ selected candidate.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 remain needs-data rows.  The persistent residual result is a negative constraint on overinterpretation: even when readout blocks are allowed to absorb residual energy, the floor $0.97$ is not crossed, so the computation does not create a mechanism, cause, or downstream biological realization.

\paragraph{Cross-layer comparison records.}
Other finite-row records are useful only as neighboring surfaces.  A uORF/Kozak ribosome-load computation reports $41862$ fixed rows, $16739$ varying rows, two-sample concordance, and runtime $131.941$ seconds.  An mRNA-stability computation reports a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, and runtime $46.349$ seconds.  These numbers show that translation-initiation and mRNA-stability contacts can be finite and controlled in their own settings, but they do not repair the missing same-row matrix for this branchpoint record.  No theorem in this section treats branchpoint motif usage as a codon-coordinate readout, and no row join in the stated facts combines the Mercer branchpoint panel with a measured $B^{\ast}_{Q6}\to T$ translation statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore conditional and bounded.  The branchpoint panel is ready as an expression-controlled cross-layer branchpoint-usage record with $10584$ finite human intron rows.  It is not ready as a translation-survival claim.  A positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry would require a same-organism or same-CDS panel joining the residual coordinate $q_i$, the translation readout $T_j$, and the control matrix $Z$, followed by a non-saturated rank audit and a breakable held-out statistic.  Until that panel exists, any all-1.0 fitted surface, modeled tAI/stAI contact, proxy coupling, or review eligibility is only a boundary marker.  No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, causation, or universal biological law follows from the present branchpoint record without a separate layer-matched contact and statistic.
