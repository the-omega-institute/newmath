\paragraph{Finite row surface.} The record is a finite cross-layer gate, not a declaration that a codon residual has already become a translation phenotype.  Its carrier consists of residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates $q_i$, organism-indexed translation readout surfaces $T_j$, and control columns $Z$ containing amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, and an $M$-density baseline.  The local Mercer branchpoint contact supplies a separate finite-row cross-layer panel: the branchpoint motif computation used $n=10584$ joined rows, controlled for expression, and used UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8 expression as $\log(1+\operatorname{median})$ over 54 tissue expression scores after same-strand intron overlap joining.  That run is recorded as experiment $\mathrm{a27e7c351f67}$, with runtime $138.47155$ seconds and verification time $2026$-$06$-$24\,09{:}42{:}38\,\mathrm{UTC}$.  These numbers identify a real finite design surface, but they do not themselves populate a $B^*_{Q6}$ translation survival matrix.

\paragraph{Admissibility statistic.} For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, the row-level test first residualizes both the coordinate and the readout against the controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= q_i-\Pi_Z q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= T_j-\Pi_Z T_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
The entry is eligible for survival language only when the post-control design has non-saturated rank and enough independent rows to make held-out failure possible.  A perfect in-sample fit is therefore not evidence by itself.  If the controlled row space is saturated, or if the remaining degrees of freedom cannot support held-out testing, the entry is recorded as coupling-only or needs-data even when its fitted score is positive.  In symbols, the gate checks the rank of the residualized finite design before reading a score as survival:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}\bigl([\widetilde q_i,\widetilde T_j]\bigr)&< n_{\mathrm{rows}}-\operatorname{rank}(Z),\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij}&=n_{\mathrm{rows}}-\operatorname{rank}(Z)-\operatorname{rank}\bigl([\widetilde q_i,\widetilde T_j]\bigr)>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
When this fails, $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is not promoted.  The admissibility certificate records the residualized rank, the remaining degrees of freedom after $Z$, the held-out translation readout boundary, and the break condition that rejects survival under rank saturation.

\paragraph{Nearby finite evidence and its limit.} The current $B^*_{Q6}$ memory contains computed synonymous residual coordinates for nine entries: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  A three-organism proxy coupling computation loaded $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K-12, used a 61-codon count space, and tested the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The declared break condition was that all nine $q_i$ have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout; the condition was not met, with nine nonzero entries and maximum absolute proxy coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  This is a contact with a modeled or proxy translation surface, not a controlled survival statement.  The coupling threshold $0.01$ is therefore read as a finite contact threshold for $A_{ij}$, while $S^{QT}_{ij}$ remains unavailable until the rank-admissible, held-out, $Z$-controlled readout is computed.

\paragraph{Residual persistence context.} A separate residual-basis computation used a declared regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and compared greedy readout selections with degree-of-freedom matched deterministic Gaussian nulls.  Four organisms were computed and all four were labelled persistent rather than compressible: $\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}$ with $n=14410$ joined rows and final $\rho=0.9791828971490305$; $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K-12 MG1655 with $n=3415$ and final $\rho=0.9979478314995476$; $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$ with $n=16008$ and final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$; and $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ with $n=3046$ and final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$.  The persistence floor was $0.97$, the computed count was four, and $\mathrm{Gallus\ gallus}$ and $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis}$ str. MC2 155 remained needs-data.  These persistent residuals are important negative structure: the readout blocks did not absorb the residual geometry enough to justify a simple downstream interpretation.  They support the need for a rank gate rather than bypassing it.

\paragraph{Cross-layer safeguards.} Other finite panels in the same memory clarify the boundary.  The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load sample has $n_{\mathrm{fixed}}=41862$, $n_{\mathrm{varying}}=16739$, a concordant two-sample result, and runtime $131.941$ seconds.  The codon-composition mRNA half-life computation has $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16500$, $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18924$, raw $\rho=0.126224$, runtime $46.349$ seconds, and a circularity guard recorded as non-CSC tAI.  These are row-bearing contacts at neighboring layers, but each remains attached to its own readout.  A branchpoint motif panel, a ribosome-load panel, an mRNA half-life panel, and a proxy tRNA-copy coupling surface cannot be pooled into a positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry unless the same declared coordinate, readout, and controls are joined on one admissible design.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.} This section permits only the following conclusion: the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival entry is testable only after residualized rank and held-out degrees of freedom are certified.  It does not assert translation realization from codon geometry, tAI, stAI, tRNA gene copy, branchpoint usage, mRNA abundance, or proxy coupling.  It does not infer ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  Until that exists, the scientifically correct outcome for a saturated or underpowered $S^{QT}_{ij}$ design is refusal: the entry remains coupling-only or needs-data rather than survival.
