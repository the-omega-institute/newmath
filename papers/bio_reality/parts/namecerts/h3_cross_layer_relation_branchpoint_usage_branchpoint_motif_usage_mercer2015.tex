\subsection{Rank-admissible translation survival as a finite-row gate}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-gate}

\paragraph{Question.}
The record asks when a synonymous residual coordinate in $B^{*}_{Q6}$ may be promoted from codon-topology memory to a translation-survival entry. The proposed entry is not the raw coupling coefficient between a coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$. It is a finite-row admissibility certificate for $S^{QT}_{ij}$ after the coordinate and the readout have both been residualized against the declared control columns $Z$. The controls named for this packet are amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and $M$-density baseline; later uses may add matched mRNA or simpler-codon baselines, but those additions do not change the first gate. The gate asks whether the remaining row space has enough independent support for a held-out translation-readout test. If the residualized design is rank-saturated, or if the remaining degrees of freedom are too small to make a failure observable, the entry stays at coupling-only or needs-data status even when an in-sample fit is positive.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The carrier is a finite residualized design matrix whose rows are organism- or sequence-indexed observations, whose coordinate columns are the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals, whose readout columns are bounded translation surfaces, and whose nuisance columns are $Z$. The currently computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate list contains nine residual directions in the coupling matrix packet: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f_3$-stress. The finite cross-organism proxy matrix used Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12, with codon-count space $61$. Its proxy readout was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a direct ribosome or protein measurement. The coupling threshold was $0.01$; all nine $q_i$ failed the all-small null because the maximum absolute proxy coefficient was $1882464.1106377328$ and the nonzero-entry count was $9$. This is enough to say that the proxy contact was not identically silent, but not enough to say that any $S^{QT}_{ij}$ survived.

\paragraph{Rank condition.}
For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, let $q_i^{\perp Z}$ and $T_j^{\perp Z}$ denote the residuals after removing the span of the finite control matrix $Z$. The admissible test surface is the residualized design, not the unadjusted pair. A minimal way to record the gate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_{ij} &= [\,q_i^{\perp Z},\ T_j^{\perp Z}\,],\\
\operatorname{dof}_{ij} &= n - \operatorname{rank}(Z) - \operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}),\\
\operatorname{admissible}(i,j) &= \bigl(\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}) < n - \operatorname{rank}(Z)\bigr)\ \wedge\ \bigl(\operatorname{dof}_{ij} > 0\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$
The equality $R^2=1.0$ on a three-organism controlled surface is therefore not a success state. It is the diagnostic shape of a saturated row space unless a separate held-out panel supplies enough independent rows. This is the central distinction of the packet: a nonzero $A^{QT}$ contact can be real as a bounded finite statistic while still failing the stricter survival predicate.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The branchpoint-motif companion contact is expression-controlled and contains $n=10584$ joined intron-overlap rows on the same strand. Its expression covariate is UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8, summarized as $\log(1+\text{median})$ over $54$ tissue expression scores; the run time was $138.47155$ seconds. That contact is useful as a cross-layer control example because it shows a finite, covariate-bearing biological join rather than an unadjusted motif count. It does not, however, provide a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation matrix: its rows are branchpoint and expression rows, not codon-residual and translation-readout rows. Two other nearby finite records show that matched biological panels can be large enough to avoid the three-row degeneracy: the uORF/Kozak ribosome-load sample had $41862$ fixed rows and $16739$ varying rows with two-sample concordance, and the codon-composition mRNA half-life contact had $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, and a non-CSC tAI circularity guard. These counts are evidence that the required row scale is realistic; they are not a completed $S^{QT}$ entry for the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates.

\paragraph{Residual-basis readback.}
The residual-basis extraction packet gives a second finite diagnostic for the same boundary. It used a stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with description length $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$. The computed organisms were all classified as persistent rather than compressible: Danio rerio, Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 were marked needs-data. The observed row counts were $14410$ for Danio rerio, $3415$ for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $16008$ for Homo sapiens, and $3046$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Their residual curves stayed high. Danio rerio had $R=(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$; Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 had $R=(1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476)$; Homo sapiens had $R=(1.0,0.9852198225375993,0.9806696939851618,0.9792537895870558,0.9787609432955213,0.9787233550315274,0.9723341658092314)$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $R=(1.0,0.9962498989663637,0.9929843170647626,0.9915730985485388,0.9903314906684438,0.9894054695002641,0.9885902293718664,0.9882386501212531,0.9881524582851271,0.9798667977250413)$. The minimum final residual value was $0.9723341658092314$, above the stated floor $0.97$, so the descriptive outcome is persistent residual signal, not a collapsed translation explanation.

\paragraph{Null comparisons.}
The dof-matched Gaussian-null checks further separate descriptive absorption from survival. Danio rerio selected measured\_trna\_tai at step $1$ with increment $0.02081710285096942$ over null $p95=0.0002306374780841125$. Escherichia coli selected mRNA at step $1$ with increment $0.0017706954554909888$ over null $p95=0.0013371669456573104$, then measured\_te at step $2$ with increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below null $p95=0.0010272364744326006$. Homo sapiens selected ptm\_density, complex\_member, tm\_count, measured\_trna\_tai, domain\_count, and localization over six steps; the fifth step was below its null with increment $0.00003840392718095609$ against $p95=0.00021932006910265595$, while the sixth localization step exceeded null with increment $0.006528084968494762$ against $p95=0.0011598998794309233$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae selected measured\_te, ptm\_density, tm\_count, turnover, complex\_member, measured\_trna\_tai, domain\_count, mRNA, and localization; steps $5$ through $8$ were below their null thresholds, while step $9$ exceeded null with increment $0.008385002223710514$ against $p95=0.00708487840939486$. These lists show that some finite readout blocks absorb residual variation more than their matched nulls, but the packet still refuses to identify those absorptions with translation survival unless the exact $q_i \to T_j$ surface is non-saturated and held-out testable.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The admissibility rule is therefore a refusal rule as much as a support rule. A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is reportable only when the residualized rank leaves degrees of freedom, the readout is a translation-layer surface rather than a proxy-only contact, and the support remains visible outside the fitted rows. The existing finite evidence contains nonzero proxy coupling, large adjacent biological panels, and persistent residual-basis diagnostics, but it does not contain a same-organism or same-CDS measured translation-readout matrix for this exact gate. The correct local classification is: proxy coupling present; rank-admissible measured survival not yet established.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
Nothing in this chapter licenses translation realization beyond the bounded readout being tested. A tAI or stAI weight is a modelled codon-adaptation surface, not observed ribosome occupancy. A ribosome or translation-efficiency contact, when merely named, is not a computed $S^{QT}$ matrix. Protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, causation, and universality require their own matched contacts and their own finite statistics. The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual geometry supplies coordinates for a test; it is not itself a biological measurement at those higher layers.
