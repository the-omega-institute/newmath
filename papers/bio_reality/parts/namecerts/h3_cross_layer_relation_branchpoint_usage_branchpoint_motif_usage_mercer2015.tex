\subsection{Rank-admissible translation readout gate for synonymous residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the proposed $B^*_{Q6}$ translation contact as a finite controlled matrix problem, not as a biological promotion rule.  The carrier is a residualized design matrix whose rows are organism-indexed or CDS-indexed observations, whose candidate columns are the synonymous residual coordinates $q_i$, whose readout columns are bounded translation surfaces $T_j$, and whose control block is $Z$.  The intended control block includes amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, and an $M$-density baseline; mRNA abundance and simpler codon baselines are added when the readout is a translation-efficiency surface.  A proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is admissible only after both $q_i$ and $T_j$ have been projected away from $Z$ and the remaining row space still has non-saturated rank.

The local finite branchpoint contact supplies one concrete row count: the Mercer-style branchpoint motif usage surface has $n=10584$ intron-overlap rows after joining to UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8 expression, with expression summarized as $\log(1+x)$ of the median across $54$ tissue scores on the same strand.  The run was expression-controlled and completed in $138.47155$ seconds.  These numbers are useful as a comparison point for a controlled finite-row surface: they show that the BioReality record can carry a real covariate-controlled row set, but they do not by themselves instantiate a $B^*_{Q6}$ translation survival matrix.

\paragraph{Admissibility condition.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and each translation readout $T_j$, the controlled entry is formed only if the residual design has enough independent support to make a held-out failure possible.  In symbols, write
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \widetilde q_i &= (I-P_Z)q_i,\\
  \widetilde T_j &= (I-P_Z)T_j,\\
  S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0
    \quad\text{only if}\quad
    \operatorname{rank}[Z,q_i,T_j] > \operatorname{rank}[Z]
    \quad\text{and the held-out score is non-vacuous.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the post-control rank is saturated, or if the remaining degrees of freedom are too small to let the fitted relation fail, the entry is recorded as coupling-only or needs-data.  In particular, an in-sample fitted value of $1.0$ is not treated as survival unless the residual row space has a falsifiable held-out boundary.  The gate therefore separates three cases: a rank-admissible translation-survival entry, a rank-saturated projection artifact, and a coupling-only contact that has not crossed into controlled survival.

\paragraph{Available $Q6$ finite rows.}
The present $Q6$ residual packet contains computed synonymous residual coordinates for Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f_3$-stress.  A three-organism codon-count and tRNA-copy proxy was evaluated over Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12 in the $61$-codon count space.  Against the proxy readout, the break condition requiring all $9$ coordinates to have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met: the matrix had $9$ nonzero entries and maximum absolute proxy coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  This is evidence of a finite coupling surface, not a survival result.  The readout was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, and the record explicitly lacks true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, or protein-abundance rows for a controlled $S^{QT}$ claim.

The broader residual-basis extraction also gives finite row support and residual persistence labels.  Homo sapiens used $16008$ joined rows and selected $6$ candidate blocks, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \rho &= (1.0, 0.9852198225375993, 0.9806696939851618, 0.9792537895870558,\\
  &\qquad 0.9787609432955213, 0.9787233550315274, 0.9723341658092314),
\end{aligned}
$$
and was labeled persistent.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae used $3046$ joined rows and $9$ candidate blocks, with final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$, also persistent.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 used $3415$ joined rows and $2$ candidate blocks, ending at $\rho=0.9979478314995476$.  Danio rerio used $14410$ joined rows and $1$ candidate block, ending at $\rho=0.9791828971490305$.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 remain needs-data.  Across the computed organisms, the residual packet reported $4$ persistent cases, no compressible cases, no partially compressible cases, and a residual floor of $0.97$; the minimum final $\rho$ was $0.9723341658092314$.  The extraction used the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$.

\paragraph{Null comparisons inside the residual packet.}
The row-level extraction was compared with degree-of-freedom matched deterministic Gaussian nulls.  Homo sapiens had positive null comparisons for ptm\_density at step $1$ with gain $0.014780177462400674$ over null p95 $0.0002845846156909306$, complex\_member at step $2$ with gain $0.004618389163869967$ over $0.000206488525646134$, tm\_count at step $3$ with gain $0.0014438137599134737$ over $0.0002435268444123588$, measured\_trna\_tai at step $4$ with gain $0.0005032876020244136$ over $0.00020173994173736226$, and localization at step $6$ with gain $0.006528084968494762$ over $0.0011598998794309233$; domain\_count at step $5$ did not exceed its null.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae exceeded the null for measured\_te, ptm\_density, tm\_count, turnover, and localization, but not for complex\_member, measured\_trna\_tai, domain\_count, or mrna.  Escherichia coli K12 exceeded the null for mrna at step $1$ but not for measured\_te at step $2$.  Danio rerio exceeded the null for measured\_trna\_tai at its only selected step.

These finite comparisons support the descriptive statement that residual coordinate energy remains present after selected readout blocks.  They do not supply an organism-matched, rank-audited $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry for the translation gate unless the specific $q_i$, $T_j$, and $Z$ design is recomputed with non-saturated rank and held-out evaluation.

\paragraph{Related finite contacts.}
Two neighboring controlled surfaces show that the project already records finite biological row sets without collapsing layers.  The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load sample had $41862$ fixed rows and $16739$ varying rows, with two-sample concordance and runtime $131.941$ seconds.  The codon-composition mRNA half-life contact used a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, with $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, and runtime $46.349$ seconds.  These contacts are bounded readout surfaces at their own layers.  They do not certify that any $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinate has survived into translation realization under the rank gate.

\paragraph{Verdict for this packet.}
The finite evidence is sufficient to state the gate and to attach concrete row counts and residual curves, but it is not sufficient to report a positive translation-survival entry.  The correct readback is therefore conditional: the packet is ready as a rank-admissibility and refusal rule.  A future positive entry must report the residualized rank, post-control degrees of freedom, held-out readout score, and the break condition under which saturated or underpowered designs are rejected.  Until those quantities are present for a specific $S^{QT}_{ij}$, the largest valid claim is bounded coupling or needs-data.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, system phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, the proxy $A_{ij}$ coupling matrix, the branchpoint row count, the expression-controlled intron surface, the tAI or stAI contact route, or an all-in-sample fitted score.  Each of those stronger claims requires its own matched biological readout and its own controlled statistic at the relevant layer.  This chapter therefore uses the finite rows only to define what must be checked before promotion, and to mark the present subject as blocked against translation-survival interpretation until a non-saturated measured translation-readout matrix is available.
