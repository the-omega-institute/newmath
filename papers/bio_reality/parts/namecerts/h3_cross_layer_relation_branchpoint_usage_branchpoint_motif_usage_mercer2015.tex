\subsection{Expression-Controlled Branchpoint Usage as a Rank Boundary for Translation Claims}
\label{sec:bioreality-branchpoint-usage-mercer2015-rank-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The Mercer-anchored branchpoint packet is used here as a finite cross-layer readout, not as a translation assay.  Its verified row set contains $n=10584$ intron-overlap observations after joining branchpoint-motif usage to an expression covariate on the same strand.  The expression covariate is the UCSC hg19 \texttt{gtexGeneV8} surface, encoded as $\log(1+\operatorname{median})$ over $54$ tissue expression scores, and the recorded run time for the finite join was $138.47155$ seconds.  The expression flag is true: the table is therefore not a bare motif-count surface, but a controlled branchpoint surface in which transcript-level abundance is explicitly present as a covariate.

\paragraph{Rank object.}
Let $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ denote the finite family of synonymous residual coordinates available to the surrounding Q6 analysis, and let $T_j$ denote a translation readout candidate.  A proposed survival entry is admissible only after both $q_i$ and $T_j$ have been residualized against a control matrix $Z$ containing amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline.  The branchpoint panel supplies a useful negative comparison because its verified contact is expression-controlled splicing geometry, not ribosome movement.  Thus the finite object for this chapter is the residualized design matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_{ij}^{\perp Z} &= \bigl(q_i^{\perp Z},\,T_j^{\perp Z}\bigr),\\
\operatorname{adm}(i,j) &= \mathbf{1}\{\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}^{\perp Z}) < n_Z \text{ and held-out loss is estimable}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The value $n=10584$ is large enough for the branchpoint join itself, but row count alone is not a survival certificate.  The relevant condition is non-saturated residual rank at the exact translation boundary being tested.  A fitted in-sample score equal to $1.0$, or a nonzero coupling coefficient on a proxy surface, is rejected as a translation-survival claim whenever the residualized row space has no independent hold-out degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Relation to the Q6 residual inventory.}
The Q6 residual inventory used by the adjoining finite-row computations contains nine named synonymous residual coordinates: $\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, $\operatorname{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $\operatorname{Leu}_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $\operatorname{Ser}_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $\operatorname{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In the proxy coupling computation, three organisms were loaded: \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Escherichia coli} K12.  The proxy contact was the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum; the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met, and the maximum recorded $|A_{ij}|$ was $1882464.1106377328$ with $9$ nonzero entries.  This is deliberately kept as coupling, not survival.  It shows that the coordinate family can touch a modeled translation-adjacent surface, but it does not certify any $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ entry.

\paragraph{Residual persistence check.}
The residual-basis extraction gives the same caution from a different finite-row angle.  With the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, four organisms were computable and all four were classified as persistent rather than compressible: \emph{Danio rerio}, \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The joined common-row counts were $14410$, $3415$, $16008$, and $3046$, respectively.  Their terminal residual fractions were $0.9791828971490305$, $0.9979478314995476$, $0.9723341658092314$, and $0.9798667977250413$, with a recorded persistence floor of $0.97$ and a minimum final value $0.9723341658092314$.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 remained in the needs-data class.  These numbers support the local reading that residual Q6 geometry is not automatically absorbed by available readout blocks; a branchpoint or expression-controlled contact cannot be promoted to a translation mechanism merely because it joins cleanly.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite controls.}
Two other finite panels fix the scale of the surrounding evidence without changing the status of this chapter.  The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load sample records $n_{\mathrm{fixed}}=41862$, $n_{\mathrm{varying}}=16739$, a two-sample concordance flag equal to true, and a run time of $131.941$ seconds.  The mRNA half-life codon-composition panel records $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16500$, $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18924$, raw $\rho=0.126224$, a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, and a run time of $46.349$ seconds.  These are useful comparison surfaces: one is closer to translation initiation and one to RNA stability.  Neither turns the Mercer branchpoint join into a protein-output, folding, function, or phenotype measurement.

\paragraph{Admissibility rule.}
For each candidate entry, the chapter uses the following finite decision rule.  If the post-control residual design has enough independent rows and non-saturated rank, the entry may be tested on a held-out translation readout.  If the design is rank-saturated, underpowered, or based only on modeled adaptation weights, proxy coupling, transcript abundance, or branchpoint overlap, the entry is assigned to coupling-only or needs-data status.  In symbols, the only admissible promotion has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} > 0
&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{adm}(i,j)=1,\\
\operatorname{adm}(i,j)=0
&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{no translation-survival assertion is made.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a row-space statement, not a biological universality statement.  It protects against the misleading case in which a small or saturated panel returns a perfect in-sample fit while leaving no falsifiable residual degrees of freedom.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The Mercer branchpoint result certifies only an expression-controlled branchpoint-usage surface with $10584$ finite rows.  It does not by itself measure translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, system phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those layers require their own matched contacts and their own post-control statistics.  The chapter's positive content is therefore the rank gate: before a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is read as translation survival, the finite row design must remain estimable after the declared controls, and the survival score must survive outside the saturated projection that produced it.
