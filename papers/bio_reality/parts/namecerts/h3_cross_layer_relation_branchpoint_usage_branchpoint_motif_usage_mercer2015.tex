\subsection{Branchpoint motif usage under a rank-audited translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-branchpoint-motif-usage-mercer2015}

\paragraph{Finite row and local question.}
The record named $\mathrm{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.branchpoint\_usage.branchpoint\_motif\_usage\_mercer2015}$ is a finite-row cross-layer check in which a branchpoint-motif surface is joined to an expression-controlled intron panel.  The verified row count is $n = 10584$.  The expression covariate is not left implicit: it is $\log(1+x)$ applied to the median of $54$ GTEx tissue expression scores from the UCSC hg19 $\mathrm{gtexGeneV8}$ table, joined by intron overlap on the same strand.  The run is marked expression-controlled, with runtime $138.47155$ seconds and experiment run id $\mathrm{a27e7c351f67}$.  Thus the object of this subsection is not a genome-wide story about splicing in general, but a finite design surface: branchpoint motif usage is read against matched intron rows while a measured expression summary is present as a control.

\paragraph{Rank gate for cross-layer promotion.}
The same local rule used for $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival entries is imposed here as a methodological guardrail.  Let $q_i$ denote a residual coordinate, let $T_j$ denote a proposed translation readout, and let $Z$ collect controls such as amino-acid composition, organism, $\mathrm{GC3}$, length, and $M$-density when the readout is translation-facing.  A proposed survival entry is admissible only after residualization leaves non-saturated rank and enough independent rows to make held-out failure possible.  In symbols, the relevant status is not an in-sample fitted score alone but the residualized design condition
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}\bigl((I-P_Z)X_{ij}\bigr) &< n_Z,\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij} &= n_Z - \operatorname{rank}\bigl((I-P_Z)X_{ij}\bigr) > 0,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &\text{ is reportable only when held-out loss can falsify it.}
\end{aligned}
$$
For the branchpoint-motif row, the finite support $n=10584$ makes the expression-controlled intron statistic inspectable, but it does not by itself create a $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-survival entry.  The branchpoint packet can serve as a cross-layer relation surface only at the layer it actually touches: motif usage, intron overlap, strand matching, and expression control.

\paragraph{Comparison with the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel.}
The finite memory also contains a separate residual-basis computation for $\mathrm{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.residual\_basis\_extraction.b\_star\_q6\_non\_translation\_residual\_powered}$.  In that computation, $\lambda = 0.01$ appears as the declared description-length regularizer through $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, and the organism-scope rows are explicitly mixed rather than universally closed.  Four organisms are computed and persistent: $\mathrm{danio\_rerio}$ has $n=14410$, one candidate, and $\rho=(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$; $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}$ has $n=3415$, two candidates, and final $\rho=0.9979478314995476$; $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$ has $n=16008$, six candidates, and final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$; $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$ has $n=3046$, nine candidates, and final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$.  Two organisms, $\mathrm{gallus\_gallus}$ and $\mathrm{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}$, remain $\mathrm{needs\_data}$.  The residual panel therefore supplies an instructive negative comparison: even when a coordinate family is computed over thousands of rows, the surviving label can be persistent unexplained residual rather than a promoted mechanism.

\paragraph{Coupling is not survival.}
A further bounded contact, $\mathrm{h3.codon\_usage\_topology.translation\_survival.b\_star\_q6\_coupling\_matrix}$, reports that nine synonymous residual coordinates were computed: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$.  The contact spans $61$ codon-count coordinates and three organisms, $\mathrm{homo\_sapiens}$, $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}$.  Its proxy readout is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum; the threshold is $0.01$; the break condition would be met if all nine $q_i$ had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against that proxy, and it is not met because the maximum absolute coupling is $1882464.1106377328$ with nine nonzero entries.  This is deliberately weaker than survival.  A large or nonzero $A_{ij}$ says that the finite proxy surface has coupling; it does not say that a held-out translation readout survives after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The branchpoint-motif packet is ready as a finite, expression-controlled cross-layer relation record.  Its strongest local statement is that $10584$ same-strand intron-overlap rows were evaluated with an explicit GTEx-derived expression control.  The surrounding $B^*_{Q6}$ evidence explains why the chapter refuses promotion from row contact to translation survival: residual panels can remain persistent after regularized extraction, and proxy coupling can be nonzero without satisfying a controlled survival gate.  Consequently, the branchpoint record supports an audited motif-usage surface, not translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, causation, or a universal biological law.  Any one of those stronger conclusions would require a separate layer-matched measurement table and a non-saturated held-out statistic at that layer.
