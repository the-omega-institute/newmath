\subsection{Rank-admissible translation survival at a branchpoint-scale boundary}\label{sec:biorality-rank-admissible-translation-survival-branchpoint-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.} This record treats the proposed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-survival entry as a finite-row admissibility question, not as a biological law. The branchpoint-side finite panel has $n=10584$ intron-overlap rows, expression control enabled, and expression read back from UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8 as $\log(1+\operatorname{median})$ over $54$ tissue expression scores. The computation was recorded in run $\mathrm{a27e7c351f67}$ with runtime $138.47155$ seconds. The expression join is therefore a real finite control surface for this chapter, but it is not by itself a translation readout, a protein readout, or a mechanistic assay.

\paragraph{Rank gate.} Let $q_i$ denote a residual synonymous coordinate in $B^{*}_{Q6}$ and let $T_j$ denote a candidate translation readout. The admissible statistic is not the raw coupling score. It is the post-control survival score obtained only after residualizing both sides against the declared control columns $Z$, including amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and an $M$-density baseline. The row test is:
$$\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= (I-P_Z)q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= (I-P_Z)T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{is reportable only when } \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_{ij}) < n_{ij} \text{ and held-out residual support remains non-vacuous.}
\end{aligned}$$
If the residualized design is saturated, then an apparent in-sample score, including a fitted value equal to $1.0$, is classified as a projection artifact. The entry is then left as coupling-only or as needing more rows. This is the central claim of the chapter: a proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry must first be falsifiable after controls before it can be named as survival.

\paragraph{Observed coupled coordinates.} The available codon-side computation contains $9$ named residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The codon count space has $61$ codons. A three-organism proxy panel loaded Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, and tested a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum as the proxy readout. The coupling threshold was $0.01$; the break condition requiring all $9$ entries to satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met. The maximum absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$, and all $9$ residual coordinates had nonzero entries above the threshold. This supports the weaker statement that a coupling contact exists in the finite proxy panel. It does not support $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$, because the readout is a tRNA-copy or adaptation proxy rather than a matched observed translation-survival surface.

\paragraph{Residual compression audit.} The residual-basis computation gives the local scale of the unsolved signal. With the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, four organisms were computed and two remained without enough data. Homo sapiens used $16008$ joined rows and $6$ selected candidates; its residual curve was $1.0,0.9852198225375993,0.9806696939851618,0.9792537895870558,0.9787609432955213,0.9787233550315274,0.9723341658092314$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae used $3046$ joined rows and $9$ selected candidates; its curve ended at $0.9798667977250413$. Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 used $3415$ joined rows and $2$ candidates, ending at $0.9979478314995476$. Danio rerio used $14410$ joined rows and $1$ candidate, ending at $0.9791828971490305$. Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. mc2 155 were marked as needing data. The computed organisms were all classified as persistent rather than compressible, with residual floor $0.97$ and minimum final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$. The chapter therefore reads persistence as a negative constraint: the existing readout blocks do not absorb the residual geometry enough to justify a translation-realization promotion.

\paragraph{Matched controls from neighboring finite records.} Two additional finite panels locate the boundary of admissibility. The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load sample recorded $41862$ fixed rows and $16739$ varying rows, with two-sample concordance true and runtime $131.941$ seconds. The codon-composition mRNA half-life panel recorded $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, runtime $46.349$ seconds, and a non-CSC tAI circularity guard. These records show that translation-related and RNA-stability row surfaces exist elsewhere in the finite memory, but they are not the same as a joined $B^{*}_{Q6}\times T$ survival matrix for the branchpoint-scale gate. They may help specify future controls, not replace the rank-admissibility test.

\paragraph{Local verdict.} The branchpoint panel contributes a controlled finite-row context, and the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ computations contribute residual coordinates, proxy coupling, and persistent unexplained residual structure. The correct chapter-level conclusion is therefore conditional and restrictive: proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entries are admissible only after a non-saturated residualized design and a held-out translation readout are present. The available facts do not yet report such an entry. No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, or universal rule follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the needed rows and statistic.
