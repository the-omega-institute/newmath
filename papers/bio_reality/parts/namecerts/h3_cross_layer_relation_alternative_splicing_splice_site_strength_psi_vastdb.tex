\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite record for this chapter is the run $1\mathrm{ce}9\mathrm{f}7\mathrm{f}16\mathrm{dbe}$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$25\mathrm{T}13{:}52{:}57{+}00{:}00$.  It contains $29742$ alternative-splicing event rows and completed in $36.4$ seconds.  Each row is treated as an externally keyed splicing observation rather than as a theorem about translation or protein state.  In the present chapter the row carrier is therefore the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
E &= \{e_1,\ldots,e_{29742}\},\\
\rho(e) &= (\mathrm{splice\ event\ id},\ \mathrm{splice\ site\ strength},\ \Psi\mathrm{/VastDB\ readout},\ Z_e),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z_e$ denotes the matched covariate record available for that event.  The role of $\rho(e)$ is modest: it fixes a counted panel on which a splice-site-strength coordinate may be compared with an inclusion readout.  It does not by itself attach codon residuals, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, localization, folding, molecular function, phenotype, or a general biological law.

\paragraph{Splicing statistic.}
For a splice event $e$, write $a(e)$ for its splice-site-strength coordinate and $y(e)$ for its PSI or VastDB-derived inclusion readout.  A bounded splicing association may be stated only as a finite-row residual comparison against controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{\mathrm{splice}} &= \Delta\mathcal{L}\bigl(y(e)\mid Z_e\bigr)
              - \Delta\mathcal{L}\bigl(y(e)\mid Z_e,a(e)\bigr),\\
\mathrm{support} &\Longrightarrow S_{\mathrm{splice}}>0\ \mathrm{on\ the\ declared\ finite\ panel}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Delta\mathcal{L}$ may be read as a held-out loss, description length, or equivalent finite-sample score, provided the same scoring rule is used on both sides.  The chapter records the existence and size of the tested event panel, not a positive effect size: the supplied finite-row facts give $29742$ rows and the run time, but they do not provide a coefficient, $p$-value, confidence interval, held-out loss delta, false-discovery rate, or module assignment.  Consequently the correct readback is a bounded splicing-row contact, not a promoted mechanistic conclusion.

\paragraph{Relation to $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.}
The accompanying conjectural object asks whether synonymous residual coordinates in coding sequence can approach higher-layer readouts through a translation gate.  That logic is stricter than an ordinary association fit.  If $q_i$ is a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, $T_j$ is a same-row translation readout, and $L_k$ is a protein-localization label or score, then the admissible route is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,\\
(q_i,T_j,\mathrm{mRNA\ control}) &\longrightarrow L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the first arrow required to be nondegenerate before the second arrow is interpreted.  A direct $q_i\to L_k$ fit is not enough.  Likewise, a splice-site-strength-to-PSI association on $29742$ event rows is not a translation readout.  It can motivate a finite cross-layer question, but it cannot replace same-CDS codon counts, measured or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA controls, and matched protein-localization rows.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The local stopping rule is part of the mathematical content.  If the only verified scalar facts are the run identifier, $29742$ event rows, $36.4$ seconds of runtime, and the verification timestamp, then the chapter may say that a finite PSI/VastDB splice panel exists for the named claim.  It may not say that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry survives translation controls, that translation mediates localization, that protein trafficking or compartment identity has been measured, or that a splice event implies folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Those conclusions require their own row-matched contacts and their own statistics.  In particular, any localization-facing statement remains blocked unless a non-rank-saturated $S^{QT}>0$ step is computed first and the localization association is then tested under conditioning on $T_j$ together with mRNA and baseline controls.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
This chapter is therefore ready only as a finite-row BioReality record for the splice-site-strength/PSI surface and as a carefully bounded boundary statement for the linked translation-mediated localization conjecture.  The verified event count gives a concrete panel size, and the run identifier makes the record auditable.  The absence of effect sizes and mediation statistics keeps the scientific claim intentionally narrow: the data support the existence of a finite tested splicing surface, while translation, localization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and organism-general directionality remain outside the demonstrated content.
