\subsection{Splice-Site-Strength and PSI Audit Boundary for a Translation-Gated Q6 Coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-splice-site-strength-psi-vastdb-q6-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite record considered here is the run $1ce9f7f16dbe$, which reports $29742$ alternative-splicing events and a wall-clock runtime of $36.4$ seconds. These numbers define the only measured row surface available for this BioReality entry: a VASTDB-facing event panel large enough to support a splice-event audit, but not yet carrying the joined coding-sequence, translation-readout, mRNA-control, and protein-localization columns required for a positive translation-mediated localization claim. Thus the present chapter treats the observed panel as a controlled boundary record rather than as evidence for protein localization, translation mechanism, folding, function, phenotype, or biological universality.

\paragraph{Objects and rows.}
Let $E$ be the finite set of audited splice events in the run, with $|E|=29742$. A row $e \in E$ may carry splice-site-strength or PSI information at the splicing layer. A separate coding-sequence row, when it exists, would carry a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ in the amino-acid quotient of the coding sequence. The present data do not state a completed identifier join from every splice event to a CDS-level $q_i$, nor do they state a completed same-row translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA abundance control, protein abundance control, or localization label. Consequently, the finite object actually certified by the run is the event-counted splice audit surface:
$$
\begin{aligned}
E &= \{e_1,\ldots,e_{29742}\},\\
\tau(E) &= 36.4\ \mathrm{s},\\
\mathrm{available}(e) &= \mathrm{splice\ event\ audit\ row},\\
\mathrm{not\ yet\ available}(e) &= (q_i,T_j,m_i,p_k,\ell_a)\ \mathrm{as\ a\ joined\ same\ row\ tuple}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $m_i$ denotes the matched mRNA-control coordinate, $p_k$ denotes a protein-abundance control when used, and $\ell_a$ denotes a protein-localization label or score. The notation is only a bookkeeping convention for the finite audit; it is not a claim that those columns have been observed in the supplied run.

\paragraph{Admissible cross-layer question.}
The admissible question is conditional. A Q6 residual coordinate may be brought into contact with a protein-localization interpretation only after a nondegenerate translation-readout step has first been measured on the same organism and same coding sequence. In finite-row form, a positive claim would require a joined matrix of rows of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
r &= (q_i,T_j,m_i,p_k,\ell_a,Z),\\
Z &= (\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},GC3,\mathrm{CDS\ length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{baseline\ terms}),\\
S^{QT}(q_i,T_j\mid Z,m_i) &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
followed by a localization statistic showing that the apparent $q_i \to \ell_a$ association is reduced, explained, or otherwise explicitly mediated after conditioning on $T_j$ and the declared controls. The run reported here does not contain such a joined matrix. It therefore supports the existence of a finite splicing audit surface, not the stronger translation-mediated localization relation.

\paragraph{Local null boundary.}
The null boundary is part of the mathematical content of the record. A direct association between a Q6 coordinate and a localization label, even if later observed, would not by itself establish a translation-mediated relation. The required order of interpretation is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\rightsquigarrow T_j \quad \mathrm{first},\\
(q_i,T_j,m_i,Z) &\rightsquigarrow S^{QT} \quad \mathrm{with\ nonzero\ finite\ rank},\\
(q_i,T_j,m_i,p_k,\ell_a,Z) &\rightsquigarrow \mathrm{conditioned\ localization\ readout}\quad \mathrm{second}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If the first step is absent, rank-saturated, underpowered, or explained entirely by baseline covariates, then localization remains outside the translation interpretation. If localization is present without a surviving translation step, the result is a bounded residual contact at most, not evidence for translation causation or protein trafficking.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the verified run.}
The verified run contributes two concrete scalars: $29742$ audited events and $36.4$ seconds of runtime. These values are enough to state that the splice-event panel was materialized and processed as a finite object. They are not enough to state a direction of effect, a survival score, a mediation drop, a p-value, a hit rate, a module class, or a biological mechanism. In particular, no $R$ list, $M$ list, $\lambda$ value, localization coefficient, controlled $S^{QT}$ value, or mediation statistic is present in the supplied finite-row record. The chapter therefore records a precise boundary: the splicing panel can be named as an audited surface, while the translation-localization bridge remains unlicensed until the missing same-row columns and statistics are supplied.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, trafficking mechanism, phenotype, adaptation claim, or universal biological rule follows from the present finite row data. Codon residual geometry, splice-site or PSI event counting, modeled codon-adaptation weights, ribosome-profiling contact names, mRNA-control contact names, protein-abundance contact names, and localization-contact names remain distinct kinds of evidence. A positive cross-layer statement would require its own layer-matched finite statistic; absent that statistic, the scientifically correct reading is a bounded audit record with an explicit refusal to promote beyond the observed row surface.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
This BioReality entry is therefore accepted only as a finite, audited boundary chapter. The event panel exists with $|E|=29742$ and runtime $36.4$ seconds under run $1ce9f7f16dbe$. The chapter does not certify that B$^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates affect splice-site strength, PSI, translation efficiency, protein abundance, protein localization, protein structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or a general biological law. It certifies the more limited mathematical statement that any future Q6-to-localization readback must pass through a same-row, nondegenerate translation-readout statistic before localization language is admissible.
