\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence panel in which each row is allowed to carry four kinds of fields: a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ amino-acid-quotient construction, a same-row translation readout $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and a matched protein-localization label or score $L_\ell$.  The verified run attached to this record is $\mathrm{run}=\mathrm{1ce9f7f16dbe}$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$27\,19{:}56{:}13+00{:}00$, with $29{,}742$ finite events and runtime $36.4$ seconds. Consequently the chapter treats the row count as a scope certificate for the finite audit surface, not as a positive biological effect estimate.

\paragraph{Coordinate-to-readout discipline.}
The object under test is not the amino-acid sequence itself and not a raw codon-count table.  It is a residual coordinate $q_i$ that survives the amino-acid quotient only after the ordinary protein sequence has already been held fixed at the relevant comparison level.  A permissible row has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x_r &= (\mathrm{CDS}_r,\mathrm{organism}_r,q_i(r),T_j(r),m_r,L_\ell(r),Z_r),\\
  Z_r &= (\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},\mathrm{M\mbox{-}density},\mathrm{mRNA\ control},\mathrm{baseline\ codon\ controls}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation statistic is the first gate.  A localization-facing reading is admissible only when the controlled translation surface is nondegenerate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S^{QT}_{i j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls.  If the translation surface is absent, rank-saturated, underpowered, or indistinguishable from a baseline-only fit, then the same row may still be kept as a bounded proposal, but it is not evidence for a translation-mediated localization relation.

\paragraph{Localization relation.}
After the translation step has survived, the localization question is a conditional question rather than a direct $q_i\to L_\ell$ association.  The finite panel must ask whether the association between $q_i$ and $L_\ell$ is reduced, absorbed, or otherwise explained when $T_j$ and the mRNA control are included.  In row-level notation, the admissible comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,\\
  (q_i,T_j,m_r,Z_r) &\longrightarrow L_\ell,
\end{aligned}
$$
not an isolated regression from $q_i$ to a compartment label.  This distinction matters because localization resources can be shaped by cell type, condition, antibody or tag artifacts, compartment granularity, isoform ambiguity, fractionation contamination, proximity-labeling radius, and identifier loss.  Those sources can define a localization boundary, but they do not by themselves supply a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Readback rule.}
The finite readback is therefore gated in two stages.  First, the synonymous residual coordinate must retain predictive content for a translation readout after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA abundance, and simpler codon baselines are accounted for.  Second, the localization term must be tested after conditioning on that same-row translation readout.  A positive chapter-level reading would require a row-joined matrix containing codon counts, $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy values, matched mRNA abundance, and protein-localization labels or scores for the same organism and matched CDS-derived protein identifiers.  The supplied audit record names the required contact classes and gives the finite event count, but it does not provide the joined statistic needed to assert survival.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The present finite evidence is therefore a controlled boundary record rather than a localization claim.  The $29{,}742$ events show that the run addressed a finite surface for the named claim, and the $36.4$ second runtime records the completed computational pass, but neither number states that $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is positive, that a localization association survives, or that conditioning on translation explains localization.  In particular, a contact name such as matched protein localization is not a measured localization statistic, a modeled tAI or stAI weight is not observed ribosome occupancy, and a ribosome-profiling route is not a joined CDS-level translation-efficiency matrix unless the actual row join and residual statistic are present.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this record alone.  Those claims require separate layer-matched measurements and their own finite statistics.  Protein localization may be discussed here only as a possible downstream readout after a nonzero translation step and a conditioning or mediation test.  If that step is missing, saturated, or not row-matched, the correct reading is a bounded cross-layer boundary: $q_i$ remains a synonymous residual coordinate with a proposed localization-facing test route, not a biological mechanism.
