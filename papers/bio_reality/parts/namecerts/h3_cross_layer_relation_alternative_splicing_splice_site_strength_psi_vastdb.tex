\subsection{Translation-Gated Localization Boundary for the Q6 Synonymous Residual}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-gated-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row status.}
This record concerns the conjecture identifier \texttt{q6.translation-mediated.protein-localization-readout.cross-layer}.  Its biological object is a finite table of coding-sequence rows in which a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family is kept separate from amino-acid sequence, organism, codon-composition controls, transcript controls, translation readouts, and protein-localization labels.  The only completed finite run attached to the present packet is the row-count audit for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_splicing.splice\_site\_strength\_psi\_vastdb}: run \texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}, verified at \texttt{2026-06-27T12:27:41+00:00}, inspected $29742$ events in $36.4$ seconds.  Thus the available numerical evidence establishes a concrete finite event surface, but it does not yet establish a joined coding-sequence, translation, mRNA, and localization matrix for this Q6 localization question.

\paragraph{Admissible row type.}
A row eligible for the proposed localization readback has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x &= (c, o, q_i, a, z, m, T_j, L_\ell),\\
  z &= (\mathrm{AA}, \mathrm{GC3}, \mathrm{length}, \mathrm{M\mbox{-}density}, \mathrm{organism}, \mathrm{baseline}),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $c$ is a coding-sequence or protein-mapped identifier, $o$ is the organism or matched condition, $q_i$ is the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, $a$ is the amino-acid sequence or amino-acid composition stratum, $m$ is the matched mRNA abundance control, $T_j$ is a same-row translation readout such as bounded translation efficiency or ribosome occupancy, and $L_\ell$ is a protein-localization label, score, compartment distribution, imaging intensity, fractionation enrichment, or proximity-labeling readout.  The coordinate $q_i$ is not itself a biological localization measurement.  It becomes eligible for localization-facing interpretation only after the same row contains a nondegenerate translation-readout step and a tested conditioning relation against $L_\ell$.

\paragraph{Gate statistic.}
The intended test is a two-stage finite-row comparison.  First, the residual coordinate is tested against the translation surface after the declared controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S^{QT}_{ij} &= \Delta\mathcal{L}(T_j \mid z,m,\mathrm{baseline})
    - \Delta\mathcal{L}(T_j \mid z,m,q_i,\mathrm{baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign convention is that $S^{QT}_{ij} > 0$ records a non-vacuous improvement for predicting or compressing the translation readout after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler codon baselines have already been accounted for.  A rank-saturated fit, an underpowered panel, or a model-only adaptation weight without a measured or explicitly bounded translation row is not sufficient for this step.  Only after this translation gate is passed may the localization surface be evaluated:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  D^{QTL}_{i j \ell} &=
  \mathrm{Assoc}(q_i,L_\ell \mid z,m)
  - \mathrm{Assoc}(q_i,L_\ell \mid z,m,T_j).
\end{aligned}
$$
The localization-facing reading is bounded by the behavior of $D^{QTL}_{i j \ell}$: a reduction of the direct $q_i$--$L_\ell$ term after conditioning on $T_j$ is compatible with a translation-gated explanation, while persistence without translation survival remains only a residual association boundary.

\paragraph{What the verified run does and does not supply.}
The verified finite run supplies a concrete event count, not a translation-localization mediation table.  The scalar facts $n=29742$ and runtime $36.4$ seconds show that the associated splice-site-strength event surface is finite and executable under the named run identifier.  They do not provide same-CDS codon counts, ribosome profiling rows, matched mRNA abundance, matched protein-localization scores, localization p-values, mediation coefficients, held-out ranks, hit rates, module labels, $R$ lists, $M$ lists, or $\lambda$ values for the Q6 localization claim.  Consequently, the current chapter records a testable boundary rather than a positive biological finding.

\paragraph{Local null rule.}
A direct fit from $q_i$ to $L_\ell$ is not interpreted as translation mediation.  The null region is entered whenever
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S^{QT}_{ij} \leq 0,
  \quad\text{or}\quad
  S^{QT}_{ij}\ \text{is rank-saturated},
  \quad\text{or}\quad
  T_j\ \text{is absent from the same finite row}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Inside that region, a localization association, if one is later observed, may motivate a further panel but cannot be reported as a translation-mediated localization readback.  The same rule blocks all stronger interpretations: no translation mechanism, protein folding statement, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, phenotype claim, trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the Q6 coordinate, from codon-window geometry, from a contact name, or from the completed $29742$-event run alone.  Each such layer would require its own measured contact and its own finite-row statistic.

\paragraph{Current readback.}
The present record is therefore a bounded cross-layer proposal.  It says that $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates may be brought to a protein-localization question only through a same-row translation gate, matched mRNA control, and a localization statistic conditioned on the translation readout.  With the available verified facts, the strongest admissible statement is the following: the packet has a concrete finite audit surface for the linked splice-site-strength event run, and it specifies the row structure needed for a later Q6 translation-localization test; it does not yet contain the joined matrix needed to assert localization survival, mediation, mechanism, function, structure, physical admissibility, or organism-general directionality.
