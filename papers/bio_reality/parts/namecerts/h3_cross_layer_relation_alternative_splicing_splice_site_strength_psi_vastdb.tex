\subsection{A translation-gated localization boundary for synonymous residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-translation-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record considered here is the finite-row packet
\texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_splicing.splice\_site\_strength\_psi\_vastdb}.  Its checked run identifier is \texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}.  The run contains $29742$ event rows and completed in $36.4$ seconds.  These two numbers are the only scalar measurements available in the present packet: they certify the size and execution surface of the finite event table, but they do not by themselves certify any codon-to-translation, codon-to-localization, or splice-to-localization biological effect.

\paragraph{Coordinate object.}
The mathematical object is a finite table of coding-sequence rows indexed by organism and by matched identifiers.  Each row may carry a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family, together with amino-acid composition, organism label, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, and any same-row molecular readouts that have actually been joined.  The residual coordinate is interpreted only after quotienting away amino-acid sequence information; it is not a protein sequence, not a structural descriptor, and not a localization annotation.  In this chapter the intended readout chain has three finite layers:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,\\
(q_i,T_j) &\longrightarrow L_k,\\
(q_i,T_j,\mathrm{mRNA},Z) &\longrightarrow \hbox{conditioned localization readback},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ denotes a translation readout, $L_k$ denotes a protein-localization label or score, and $Z$ denotes the declared controls: amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, methionine density, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler codon baselines.

\paragraph{Admissible claim.}
The admissible claim is deliberately narrow.  A residual coordinate $q_i$ may be treated as localization-facing only if the same finite row panel first supplies a nondegenerate translation-readout step.  Write this survival condition as $S^{QT}(q_i,T_j)>0$, where the score is evaluated after the declared controls and after rejecting rank-saturated or baseline-only explanations.  Only then may the row be tested for a localization relation, and the localization statement must be phrased as a conditioned readback rather than as a direct biological mechanism.  In particular, a direct association
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow L_k
\end{aligned}
$$
is insufficient unless it is compared with the conditioned route
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k.
\end{aligned}
$$
The decisive question is whether the apparent $q_i$--localization term is reduced, explained, or bounded once $T_j$, matched mRNA abundance, and the baseline variables are included.

\paragraph{Present finite evidence.}
The available checked fact is a finite event table with $29742$ rows from run \texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}, not a joined codon--translation--localization matrix.  The row count is large enough to make the packet worth retaining as a test target, but the recorded scalars do not include $R$ lists, $M$ lists, $\lambda$ values, $p$-values, module classifications, hit rates, or a computed $S^{QT}$ surface.  Therefore the current chapter records a bounded cross-layer proposal rather than a positive biological result.  The statement supported by the data is that the event table exists at the recorded scale; the statement not yet supported is that any $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate survives a translation-readout test or mediates a localization readout.

\paragraph{Localization contact.}
A localization row is acceptable only when it is keyed to a protein or coding-sequence identifier compatible with the codon and translation rows.  Such a row may be a compartment label, a compartment score, an imaging-derived localization assignment, a fractionation enrichment, or a proximity-labeling score.  The contact is noisy in predictable ways: cell type, condition, antibody or tag artifacts, compartment granularity, isoform ambiguity, contamination, labeling radius, and identifier loss can all alter the finite table.  For that reason, the localization layer cannot be read as a mechanism.  It is a measured or curated surface to be conditioned on a prior translation surface.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The local null condition is the following.  If $S^{QT}(q_i,T_j)$ is absent, zero, underpowered, or saturated by rank or baseline controls, then the coordinate $q_i$ remains a bounded residual proposal.  In that case, even a visible $q_i$--localization association is not a translation-mediated localization claim.  It is only a boundary event requiring more row joins.  The same refusal applies if the finite table contains localization annotations but lacks matched codon counts, matched translation readouts, or matched mRNA controls.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
Nothing in the present finite record establishes translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, trafficking mechanism, compartment identity mechanism, phenotype, causation, or universality.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight is not an observed ribosome footprint.  A ribosome-profiling contact name is not a joined translation-efficiency matrix.  A protein-localization contact name is not a computed localization statistic.  A workflow-ready event is not biological evidence.  Each stronger interpretation requires its own layer-matched rows and its own statistic.

\paragraph{Operational test.}
The next finite test is explicit.  Build a same-organism, same-coding-sequence table containing $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, codon counts, a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, optional protein-abundance controls, and matched localization labels or scores.  Estimate $S^{QT}(q_i,T_j)$ under the declared controls.  Only for entries with nondegenerate $S^{QT}>0$ should the table estimate whether conditioning on $T_j$ reduces or explains the $q_i$--localization association.  Until that computation exists, the chapter remains a finite, bounded proposal at the interface between synonymous residual geometry and localization readouts, not a completed localization result.
