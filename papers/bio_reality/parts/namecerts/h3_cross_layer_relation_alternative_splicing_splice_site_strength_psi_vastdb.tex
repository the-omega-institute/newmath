\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite event table for the claim identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_splicing.splice\_site\_strength\_psi\_vastdb}.  Its verified run identifier is \texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}; the checked surface contains $29742$ alternative-splicing events and the audit run took $36.4$ seconds, with verification time recorded as \texttt{2026-06-22T17:40:58+00:00}.  These numbers are the only numerical row facts used in this chapter.  The biological question is not whether those rows already establish a protein-localization mechanism.  Rather, the rows supply a finite splice-site and inclusion-readout surface on which a proposed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate can be tested only after it is joined to same-organism coding-sequence rows, matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and protein-localization labels or scores.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout roles.}
For a coding-sequence row $x$, let $q_i(x)$ denote an amino-acid-quotient-preserving synonymous residual coordinate.  The quotient condition is essential: $q_i$ is not an amino-acid sequence feature, and it is not allowed to inherit explanatory force from amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, or methionine-density baselines.  Let $T_j(x)$ be a same-row translation readout, such as a bounded translation-efficiency or ribosome-profiling surface after mRNA adjustment, and let $L(x)$ be a matched localization label, compartment score, enrichment score, imaging class, fractionation score, or proximity-labeling readout.  The finite splice-site table can only enter this packet as an observed splicing-side surface; it does not by itself supply $T_j$ or $L$.

\paragraph{Gated statistic.}
The admissible readback has two gates.  First, the residual coordinate must survive a translation-layer test under the declared controls.  Second, a localization association may be discussed only through a conditioning or mediation comparison against that translation readout.  In schematic form the permitted finite-row test is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0,\\
\Delta^{QL\mid T}_{i} &= \operatorname{Assoc}(q_i,L \mid Z,mRNA) - \operatorname{Assoc}(q_i,L \mid Z,mRNA,T_j),\\
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},\text{ GC3},\text{ CDS length},\text{ M-density baseline}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first line is mandatory and non-vacuous.  If the $q_i\to T_j$ surface is rank-saturated, absent, or too small to separate from the baseline fit, the localization row is not read as translation-facing evidence.  The second line is interpreted only as a finite conditioning check: a reduction of the direct $q_i\to L$ association after adding $T_j$ is compatible with a translation-mediated localization-facing boundary, while no reduction, no translation survival, or no joined localization matrix leaves the packet at a null boundary.

\paragraph{What the verified splice-site run contributes.}
The verified VASTDB-style splice-site surface contributes a finite count, $29742$ events, and a successful row-level computation trace, $36.4$ seconds in run \texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}.  This is enough to make the chapter concrete: the claim is not a free-floating biological slogan but a finite-row audit target with an event count and a run identity.  However, the verified fact is a splice-site and inclusion surface, not a same-CDS translation-efficiency matrix and not a protein-localization matrix.  Therefore the present record can state the admissible cross-layer test and the refusal rule, but it cannot report a positive localization statistic.  In particular, no $p$-value, hit rate, module class, $R$ list, $M$ list, or $\lambda$ value is present in the supplied finite-row data; none is invented here.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
A direct association between $q_i$ and localization, even if later observed on matched rows, is not enough.  It must pass through a nondegenerate same-row translation readout and then be re-estimated after the translation readout and mRNA control are included.  Likewise, codon-usage tables, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling contact names, mRNA abundance controls, and localization resource names are only bounded measurement routes until joined rows and explicit statistics exist.  From the present finite facts one may not infer translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, protein trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or an organism-general law.  Each of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched measurement and its own finite statistic.

The finite row surface is ready as a boundary chapter.  It records that $29742$ splice-site events were processed in a named run and that a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate may approach localization only through the ordered gate $q_i\to T_j\to L$.  The current evidence remains intentionally bounded: without same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, measured or explicitly bounded translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, matched protein-abundance controls where used, and matched localization rows, the correct scientific conclusion is a constrained cross-layer proposal, not a positive localization mechanism.
