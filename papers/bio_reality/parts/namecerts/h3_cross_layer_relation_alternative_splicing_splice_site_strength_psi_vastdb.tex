\subsection{A translation-gated boundary for Q6 residuals and localization-facing readouts}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite record considered here is indexed by the claim identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_splicing.splice\_site\_strength\_psi\_vastdb}.  Its only verified numerical contact in the present packet is the run \texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}, checked at \texttt{2026-06-24T05:41:53+00:00}, which reports $29742$ VASTDB splice-event rows and a runtime of $36.4$ seconds.  These numbers certify that a finite alternative-splicing surface was materialized for the named claim, not that a synonymous-codon residual, translation readout, protein abundance row, or protein-localization label has already been joined to those rows.  The biological reading is therefore deliberately narrow: the data provide a counted splice-event panel with a reproducible run identity, while the Q6 translation-localization interpretation remains conditional on additional same-row biological measurements.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout variables.}
Let $q_i$ denote a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate after quotienting away the amino-acid sequence.  Let $T_j$ denote a translation-facing readout, such as a ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency row, a bounded tAI or stAI readout, or another explicitly declared translation measurement.  Let $L_\ell$ denote a protein-localization label, localization score, compartment distribution, fractionation enrichment, imaging call, or proximity-labeling readout keyed to the same protein or coding sequence.  The admissible finite row is not merely $(q_i,L_\ell)$; it must contain the controlled chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
(q_i, Z_i, m_i, T_j, L_\ell),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z_i$ includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, and simpler codon baselines, and $m_i$ is the matched mRNA control when translation efficiency is computed as a separated translation-layer quantity.  The verified VASTDB panel supplies a row count for splice events, but the present packet does not yet report such joined Q6--translation--localization rows.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
A Q6 residual may face a localization interpretation only after a nondegenerate translation step has survived the declared controls.  In local notation, this means that the statistic $S^{QT}(q_i,T_j)$ must be positive in a rank-powered design, and it must remain positive after the baseline models absorb amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, methionine-density, mRNA abundance, and simpler codon features.  The required form is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}(q_i,T_j) > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
with enough finite rank that the estimate is not an all-saturated or underpowered fit.  If this inequality is absent, rank-saturated, or not computed on same-organism and same-coding-sequence rows, then a later $q_i$--localization association is not read as translation-mediated.  It remains a boundary signal or a null contact, even when a localization resource exists by name.

\paragraph{Localization conditioning.}
After the translation gate is met, the localization-facing question is not whether $q_i$ can be directly fitted to $L_\ell$ in isolation.  The relevant finite-row question is whether the apparent $q_i \to L_\ell$ relation is reduced, explained, or otherwise reorganized when $T_j$ and the mRNA control are included.  A minimal admissible comparison has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_\ell &\sim q_i + Z_i + m_i,\\
L_\ell &\sim q_i + T_j + Z_i + m_i.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation-facing reading is allowed only when the first model is not treated as sufficient by itself and the second model shows that the localization contact is mediated, attenuated, or otherwise conditioned by the nondegenerate translation readout.  Without that controlled comparison, localization remains a measured protein-layer label, not evidence for a codon-residual translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Role of the verified splice-event run.}
The run \texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe} contributes a concrete finite surface: $29742$ splice events processed in $36.4$ seconds.  This is useful because it fixes the scale of the splice-site panel attached to the named claim and prevents the chapter from treating the claim as purely verbal.  However, the recorded values do not include an effect size, $R$ list, $M$ list, lambda value, p-value, module classification, hit rate, or controlled Q6 survival score.  Consequently, the splice-event count cannot be promoted into evidence for translation efficiency, protein localization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, trafficking mechanism, phenotype, or organism-general law.  It is a finite contact point for the splice-site side of the record, while the Q6 translation-localization chain still requires a separately joined row matrix.

\paragraph{Local null boundary.}
The null case is part of the claim rather than a failure of the chapter.  If the finite panel contains localization labels but lacks a same-row translation readout, the result is a localization boundary only.  If it contains a translation readout but $S^{QT}$ is zero, rank-saturated, or not powered, the result is a translation-readout refusal.  If it contains a direct $q_i \to L_\ell$ association but conditioning on $T_j$ and matched mRNA does not reduce or explain that association, the result is a non-translation residual boundary.  In all three cases the record may still guide future data joining, but it does not claim that synonymous residual geometry determines localization.

\paragraph{Claim discipline.}
The chapter therefore supports a constrained statement: a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate can be proposed as localization-facing only through a successful same-row translation step and a subsequent conditioning test against localization.  The available finite numbers, $29742$ splice events and $36.4$ seconds of runtime for run \texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}, establish the size and reproducibility of the attached splice-event run but do not establish the translation-localization bridge.  No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, compartment mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from the present packet unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the missing rows and the declared statistics are computed on them.
