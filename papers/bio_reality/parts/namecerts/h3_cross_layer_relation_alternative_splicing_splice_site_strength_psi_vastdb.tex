\subsection{Translation-Gated Localization Boundary for the Splice-Site PSI Panel}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-translation-localization-psi-vastdb}

\paragraph{Finite panel and recorded contact.}
The record considered here is the finite-row claim
$\mathsf{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_splicing.splice\_site\_strength\_psi\_vastdb}$, verified under experiment run $\mathsf{1ce9f7f16dbe}$ at $2026$-$06$-$26$ $21{:}16{:}55+00{:}00$.  The available checked surface contains $29{,}742$ splice-event rows and completed in $36.4$ seconds.  These two numbers are the only scalar measurements supplied for the present chapter.  They are enough to identify a concrete finite panel and a bounded computation, but they are not enough to promote the row set into a measured translation, localization, folding, function, mechanism, or organism-general law.

\paragraph{Coordinate under review.}
The biological coordinate attached to this panel is a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate on coding sequences.  Each proposed row is meant to carry an amino-acid-quotient residual $q_i$, same-row translation readout candidates $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and protein-localization labels or scores when such rows are actually joined.  The intended distinction is not a direct codon-to-localization claim.  It is a gated cross-layer question: a residual codon coordinate may face a localization readout only after a nondegenerate translation-readout step has been established on the same matched entry.

\paragraph{Admissible finite-row form.}
For a row $e$ in the verified finite panel, the admissible data shape is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 e &=(q_i, T_j, A, Z, L),\\
 Z&=(\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},\mathrm{M\ density},\mathrm{mRNA\ control}),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $A$ denotes baseline or simpler-rank controls and $L$ denotes a matched localization label, compartment score, fractionation value, imaging call, or proximity-labeling score.  The row is interpretable only if $q_i$, $T_j$, $Z$, and $L$ are all keyed to compatible organism and CDS-derived protein identifiers.  A localization contact name by itself is not such a row; it is only the address of a possible measurement class.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The first test is survival of the residual coordinate against a translation readout under the stated controls.  In this chapter the gate is denoted
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}(q_i,T_j\mid Z,A)>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
A rank-saturated fit, an underpowered panel, a modeled codon-adaptation weight without measured support, or a missing same-row translation matrix counts as no positive gate.  The supplied finite facts report $29{,}742$ events for the splice-site PSI run, but they do not report any $S^{QT}$ value, held-out statistic, partial $R^2$, description-length reduction, $p$-value, or rank diagnostic.  Therefore the verified row count supports the existence of a computed event surface, not translation survival.

\paragraph{Localization readback.}
Only after the translation gate is nondegenerate may the localization readout be tested.  The admissible readback has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L,\\
\Delta_L &= \mathrm{assoc}(q_i,L\mid Z,A)-\mathrm{assoc}(q_i,L\mid Z,A,T_j).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive localization-facing interpretation requires that the direct $q_i$ to $L$ association be reduced, explained, or otherwise bounded after conditioning on $T_j$.  If the $q_i$ to $L$ term persists without translation survival, the row remains a residual association boundary rather than a translation-mediated localization claim.  If the localization row is present but the translation row is absent, the record is a localization-contact opportunity only.

\paragraph{Relation to the splice-site PSI surface.}
The verified finite surface belongs to an alternative-splicing splice-site strength and PSI claim.  Its $29{,}742$ rows can supply a row-indexed biological panel for testing cross-layer proposals only if the same rows are extended with compatible CDS residual coordinates, translation readouts, mRNA controls, and protein-localization labels.  PSI and splice-site strength do not by themselves measure ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, subcellular localization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  They may become covariates or stratification variables in a later joined panel, but the present verified facts do not contain that joined panel.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The local boundary is strict.  From the recorded run one may say that a finite VASTDB-facing splice-event surface with $29{,}742$ rows was computed in $36.4$ seconds.  One may also state the admissible design of a translation-gated localization test for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals.  One may not infer translation realization, protein localization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, trafficking mechanism, compartment mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, or a universal biological rule.  Any such assertion requires its own layer-matched contact and its own statistic, joined to the same finite rows or explicitly declared as a separate panel.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter is therefore a ready bounded record, not a positive biological mechanism.  The finite-row computation is real at the level recorded by the run metadata, and the cross-layer design is precise: $q_i$ may approach localization only through $S^{QT}>0$ and a conditioning or mediation test against $L$.  Since the supplied facts include no translation-survival scalar and no localization statistic, the current scientific content is a well-formed boundary statement for the standalone BioReality paper.
