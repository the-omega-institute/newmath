\paragraph{Finite contact record.}

Its measured table contains $29742$ alternative-splicing events, and the reported wall-clock runtime for the event pass is $36.4$ seconds. so the finite observation is an alternative-splicing and splice-site-strength contact over a VASTDB-style event panel, not a completed translation, protein-localization, or protein-state measurement.

\paragraph{Rows and coordinates.}
For the proposed cross-layer question, each admissible row would have to carry a coding-sequence identifier, an organism label, an amino-acid sequence or amino-acid-composition control, a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ in the $B^*_{Q6}$ quotient, a same-row translation readout $T_j$, a matched mRNA control, and a protein-localization label or score $L_\ell$.  The verified finite run supplies an event count for the splice-site panel, but it does not by itself assert that all of these fields have been joined on the same coding sequence.  The correct local carrier is therefore a bounded table of candidate rows, not a biological conclusion.  The intended admissible statistic has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathrm{row}_a &= (\mathrm{CDS}_a,\mathrm{organism}_a,q_i(a),T_j(a),mRNA(a),L_\ell(a),Z_a),\\
  Z_a &= (\mathrm{amino\ acid\ controls},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{baseline\ terms}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The VASTDB event count $29742$ may delimit the size of the splice-event contact used by the row-building procedure, but it is not yet a count of rows satisfying the full $q_i,T_j,mRNA,L_\ell$ join.

\paragraph{Gated interpretation.}
The admissible readback is sequential.  A synonymous residual coordinate may approach a protein-localization-facing interpretation only after the same row has a nondegenerate translation step.  In notation, the first required test is a controlled survival statistic
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S^{QT}(q_i,T_j\mid Z,mRNA)>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where the control set includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler codon baselines.  Only after this step is powered and non-saturated may one ask whether a localization association is attenuated, explained, or otherwise conditioned by the translation readout:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_\ell.
\end{aligned}
$$
If the direct $q_i\to L_\ell$ association exists without a surviving $q_i\to T_j$ step, the result remains a localization-side boundary signal rather than a translation-mediated claim.  If $S^{QT}$ is absent, rank-saturated, underpowered, or produced only by a baseline fit, the localization term is not promoted.

\paragraph{What the verified run does and does not establish.}
The verified facts establish that one finite event pass exists for the splice-site-strength and PSI/VASTDB contact: $29742$ events were processed in $36.4$ seconds under run \texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}.  They do not establish a joined codon-residual matrix, do not report $R$ lists, $M$ lists, $\lambda$ values, $p$-values, module labels, hit rates, or a controlled mediation coefficient for this subject.  Consequently, the chapter records a ready boundary statement: the finite splice-event contact is suitable as a named row source or row constraint, but the present evidence is insufficient for a positive translation-to-localization biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Local null boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows from the $29742$-event splice-site panel alone.  No protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, protein-trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from the codon residual coordinate, from the event count, from the runtime, or from the existence of a matched localization contact name.  Each such assertion would require its own layer-matched measured contact and its own finite statistic.  In particular, protein localization may be discussed here only as a conditional readout target after a nondegenerate same-row translation readout has survived the declared controls.

\paragraph{Resulting chapter claim.}
The chapter therefore treats
$\texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_splicing.splice\_site\_strength\_psi\_vastdb}$
as a finite cross-layer boundary record.  It records a verified splice-event surface of $29742$ events and a runtime of $36.4$ seconds, then fixes the admissible rule for any later Q6-to-localization analysis: first compute a controlled $q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout survival on joined same-row data; then test whether the localization association is explained by, reduced by, or otherwise conditioned on $T_j$; otherwise report only a bounded null or unresolved boundary.
