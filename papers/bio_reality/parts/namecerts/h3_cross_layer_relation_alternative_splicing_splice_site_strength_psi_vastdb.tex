\subsection{Translation-Gated Localization Boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-translation-gated-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The finite record considered here is keyed by the claim identifier
$\texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.alternative\_splicing.splice\_site\_strength\_psi\_vastdb}$ and by the conjectural coordinate name
$\texttt{q6.translation\mbox{-}mediated.protein\mbox{-}localization\mbox{-}readout.cross\mbox{-}layer}$.  Its row type is not a free biological assertion.  A row is admissible only when a coding sequence, an organism label, a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ in the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ quotient, an amino-acid-preserving baseline description, a same-row translation readout $T_j$, a matched mRNA control, and a matched protein-localization label or score are all present on one finite table.  The supplied finite run contains one verified scalar panel: experiment run $\texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}$ reports $29742$ events and a runtime of $36.4$ seconds, verified at $\texttt{2026\mbox{-}06\mbox{-}27T05:12:32+00:00}$.  These numbers certify the size and execution trace of the available event surface; they do not by themselves certify a translation or localization effect.

\paragraph{Coordinate and controls.}
For each admissible coding-sequence row, the coordinate $q_i$ is interpreted only after quotienting away the amino-acid sequence.  Thus $q_i$ is a synonymous residual coordinate, not a protein-sequence coordinate and not a localization annotation.  The intended conditioning set contains organism, amino-acid composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, an $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler codon baselines.  A direct fit from $q_i$ to a localization label is therefore not the tested object.  The tested object is a gated relation in which $q_i$ must first survive as a translation-facing distinction before any localization-facing readback is allowed.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
Let $Q$ denote the finite list of admissible $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and let $T$ denote the finite list of translation readouts available on the same rows.  For a coordinate $q_i$ and a translation surface $T_j$, write $S^{QT}(q_i,T_j)$ for the controlled survival score after the declared baselines are fitted.  The gate is nondegenerate only when the fitted statistic is not rank-saturated, not underpowered, and not supplied merely by a contact name.  In the notation of the finite panel, the necessary condition for any localization-facing interpretation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
& S^{QT}(q_i,T_j) > 0,\\
& S^{QT}(q_i,T_j) \text{ remains positive after amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls},\\
& \text{the row join identifies the same coding sequence across the codon, translation, mRNA, and localization surfaces.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If this condition fails, the residual coordinate remains a bounded coding-sequence distinction.  It is not promoted to translation, protein localization, trafficking, compartment identity, function, mechanism, or a general biological law.

\paragraph{Localization readout.}
The localization layer is represented by protein-level labels, compartment scores, imaging, fractionation, proximity-labeling, or curated localization measurements keyed back to UniProt, RefSeq protein, locus, or coding-sequence-derived identifiers.  This contact is a layer boundary, not a completed statistic.  The admissible localization test asks whether the apparent association between $q_i$ and a localization readout $L_\ell$ is reduced, absorbed, or explained after conditioning on the surviving translation readout $T_j$ and the matched mRNA control.  A local mediation-style summary may be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{i j \ell}
&= \operatorname{Fit}(L_\ell \mid q_i, Z)
   - \operatorname{Fit}(L_\ell \mid q_i, T_j, Z),\\
Z
&= \{\text{organism},\text{ amino-acid composition},\text{ GC3},\text{ length},M\text{-density},\text{ mRNA control},\text{ simpler baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This expression records the intended finite comparison only.  In the present supplied data, no numeric $S^{QT}$ matrix, no localization coefficient, no mediation drop $\Delta_{i j \ell}$, no $p$-value, no hit rate, and no module classification are provided.  The verified $29742$-event run is therefore usable as a finite event surface and audit anchor, but the localization-facing claim remains conditional on the missing joined statistic.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The chapter records a strict null boundary.  A localization association without a nondegenerate same-row translation step is not evidence for a translation-mediated localization mechanism.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight is not ribosome occupancy.  A ribosome-profiling route is not a joined translation-efficiency matrix until RPF rows and matched mRNA rows are actually attached to the same coding sequences.  A protein-localization contact name is not a computed localization survival statistic.  Bulk protein abundance, when present in related packets, is not a substitute for localization, folding, protein state, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Readback rule.}
The allowed readback is per entry and gated.  A row may approach a protein-localization interpretation only through a nondegenerate $q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step, followed by a localization statistic tested after conditioning on $T_j$, matched mRNA abundance, and the declared controls.  If the translation step is absent, saturated, underpowered, or merely named rather than computed, then the row remains a finite cross-layer boundary proposal.  With the present finite facts, the only positive statements are that the event surface has $29742$ events, was produced by run $\texttt{1ce9f7f16dbe}$ in $36.4$ seconds, and supplies an audit target for later row joining.  No translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, protein-localization survival, or universal biological law follows from these facts unless a separate layer-matched measurement and statistic supplies it.
