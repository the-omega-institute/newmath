\subsection{Modeled-tAI organism sweep for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-sqt-organism-sweep-b-star-q6-modeled-tai}

\paragraph{Finite matrix under audit.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates retain predictive mass against a modeled translation-adaptation readout after the amino-acid quotient and the standard sequence controls are imposed.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control block has 24 columns and includes the amino-acid composition terms, organism context where applicable, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  The finite run reports a modeled-tAI sweep over 41 organisms, exceeding the declared target of 40 organisms.  The readout is not a ribosome-footprint measurement: it is a modeled per-codon tAI surface built from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts together with dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
For each organism, the statistic is the controlled residual fit of modeled tAI against the nine-dimensional $Q$ panel after removing the declared covariates.  In the powered matrix run that supplies the local rank check, the organism count was 40, the control matrix had rank 23, the residual degrees of freedom after controls were 17, the $Q$ design retained rank 9, and the joint modeled-tAI survival value was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism-sweep run extends the boundary to 41 organisms and records the per-organism distribution
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{QT} &= 0.1579271837024072,\\
Q_{25}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.26353954700074694,\\
\operatorname{median}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.33934845811598124,\\
\operatorname{mean}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.38466555440501393,\\
Q_{75}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.5143951545826744,\\
\max R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row audit used 1000 permutations for the organism-sweep surface.  These numbers show that the modeled-tAI readout is not absorbed by the amino-acid, GC3, length, organism-context, and $M$-density control layer in the computed finite panel.  The evidence is therefore a positive modeled $S^{QT}$ contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, not merely a codon-count or tRNA-copy coupling table.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the sweep.}
The empirical point is cross-organism persistence of a residual modeled-adaptation signal.  The weakest organism-level fit in the sweep is still positive at $R^2_{QT}=0.1579271837024072$, while the median is $0.33934845811598124$ and the upper quartile is $0.5143951545826744$.  The spread matters: the result is not a claim that one coordinate has one universal sign or that all organisms share the same codon-adaptation geometry.  It says only that, after the declared controls and baseline ablation, the nine-coordinate residual panel carries nonzero modeled-tAI information across a 41-organism finite matrix.  The earlier powered run also confirms that the design is not the three-row saturated surface: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and the post-control residual degrees of freedom are 17.

\paragraph{Local refusal of over-promotion.}
This chapter does not report measured ribosome occupancy, measured elongation speed, ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, proteomics output, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy annotations is a bounded translation-adaptation proxy; it is not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, direct ribosome dynamics, or protein realization.  The sweep also uses CDS-only codon counts for the added organisms, so downstream protein and mRNA contacts are absent from this finite row.  Because the organisms are not phylogenetically independent, the cross-organism pattern is descriptive rather than a population-level evolutionary estimate.  Any later assertion about measured translation, protein abundance, folding, function, mechanism, or universality must be supported by a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The packet is ready as a modeled-tAI cross-layer record.  Its finite content is the 41-organism $S^{QT}$ organism sweep with 24-column controls, nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, 1000 permutations, target organism count reached, and positive organism-level $R^2_{QT}$ distribution bounded between $0.1579271837024072$ and $0.8213457220900503$.  The correct reading is narrow: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel survives the declared controlled modeled-tAI readout, while every measured-translation, protein, structure, function, mechanism, and universal-law interpretation remains outside the evidence supplied here.
