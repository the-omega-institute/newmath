\subsection{Modeled-tAI organism sweep for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-sqt-organism-sweep-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-powered}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate system leaves a measurable trace on a bounded translation-layer readout built from modeled tAI.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  For each organism passing the gene-count gate, coding-sequence codon counts are projected to these residual coordinates and residualized against a control family $Z$ with 24 columns: an intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the support of the nine projected coordinates.  The readout is modeled per-codon tAI obtained from GtRNAdb-style tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same synonymous-residual coordinate basis.  The resulting statistic is $S^{QT}$, reported as a controlled partial-$R^2$ surface rather than as a mechanistic statement.

\paragraph{Sweep result.}
The powered sweep computed modeled tAI for 41 organisms and reached the declared target of 40 organisms.  For the organism-wise $S^{QT}$ surfaces, the empirical distribution of controlled $R^2_{QT}$ values was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{QT} &= 0.1579271837024072,\\
Q_{25}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.26353954700074694,\\
\operatorname{median}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.33934845811598124,\\
\operatorname{mean}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.38466555440501393,\\
Q_{75}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.5143951545826744,\\
\max R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sweep used 1000 permutations for the organism-level survival summaries.  These numbers say that the nine residual coordinates are not absorbed by the amino-acid, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density control model when the readout is the bounded modeled-tAI surface; even the weakest organism-level entry in the sweep retains $R^2_{QT} \approx 0.158$, while the central organism has $R^2_{QT} \approx 0.339$.

\paragraph{Rank and coordinate admissibility.}
A separate powered modeled-tAI computation on 40 organisms gives the matrix-level diagnostic for the same nine coordinates.  In that run, the control matrix had 24 columns, rank $23$, and left residual degrees of freedom $17$ after $Z$ residualization.  The residual coordinate matrix retained rank $9$, so the nine-coordinate test was not a rank-zero artifact.  The whole $9 \times 9$ modeled-readout surface had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the nondegeneracy check comparing this value to the stated saturation threshold $0.999999$.  The positive-entry threshold used there was $10^{-12}$ for partial $R^2$.  Thus the organism sweep should be read together with a non-saturated rank diagnostic: the coordinate basis has enough residual dimension to be tested, and the large matrix-level value is still below the declared degeneracy ceiling.

\paragraph{Pre-survival coupling context.}
The earlier codon-usage coupling row supplies a weaker but useful sanity check.  It computed all nine synonymous residuals against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy on three organisms: \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12.  The break condition for that proxy row was that all nine $|A_{ij}|$ values should fall below $0.01$; the condition was false, with 9 nonzero entries and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |A_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
This proxy coupling is not itself survival, but it confirms that the residual-coordinate directions are not numerically silent before the controlled $S^{QT}$ test is imposed.  The Leu CUN/UUR finite row also showed the danger of overreading small organism sets: the three-organism partial Pearson value was $1.0$, but the log-ratio signs were $+1,-1,+1$ with values $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, so the sign was explicitly not uniform.

\paragraph{Position-level contact context.}
The ribosome-occupancy contact is a distinct, narrower check.  It joined 5754 genes with ordered coding sequences and local occupancy arrays, with no length mismatches.  Within position and amino-acid bins, 9 families were computed, all 9 had at least 200 within-bin permutations, and the families passing the stated gate were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The gate used $C_H>0$, description-length cost $DL=2$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$.  This supports the claim that some of the same residual families can be inspected at a local ribosome-readout boundary, but it remains separate from the 41-organism modeled-tAI sweep and does not upgrade modeled tAI into observed elongation dynamics.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The ready conclusion is limited and numerical.  Across 41 swept organisms, the bounded modeled-tAI readout leaves a positive controlled $S^{QT}$ signature for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate system after amino-acid composition, organismal context, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density controls are imposed.  The organism-wise $R^2_{QT}$ distribution is far from a single exceptional row, and the associated rank diagnostics show that the nine residual coordinates remain testable after control residualization.  The strongest statement licensed here is therefore a cross-layer statistical relation between synonymous residual coordinates and modeled tAI readbacks.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No measured ribosome-mediated translation process follows from this chapter by itself.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights is not ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, elongation speed, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger readings require their own matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  The present row is deliberately confined to the modeled-readout $S^{QT}$ surface and to the finite organism sweep described above.
