\subsection{Organism Sweep of Modeled tAI on the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Basis}
\label{subsec:bio-reality-sqt-organism-sweep-b-star-q6-modeled-tai}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Setup.} This record belongs to the cross-layer relation class. It uses the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual carrier
$$\begin{aligned}
Q = (&K_{AAA}, \mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}, \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}, \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}, \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}, \mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG}, \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY}, f_3\text{-stress})
\end{aligned}$$
and reads it back against modeled tAI, where the readout is built from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights rather than measured ribosome occupancy. The sweep uses a 24-column control block, applies an $n \ge 500$ gene gate, and skips sparse joins instead of forcing them.

\paragraph{Sweep summary.} Across 41 organisms, exceeding the target of 40, the per-organism $R^2_{QT}$ values were summarized by $\min = 0.1579271837024072$, $q_{25} = 0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, $q_{75} = 0.5143951545826744$, and $\max = 0.8213457220900503$, with 1000 permutations per organism. The record therefore captures a broad but finite alignment between the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and the modeled adaptation surface. It is descriptive cross-organism evidence, not a mechanism statement.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.} The fitted surface is not measured ribosome occupancy, not protein abundance, and not a claim about folding, physical admissibility, function, or a universal biological law. That boundary matters here because a separate three-organism CUN/UUR versus tRNA-Leu copy-ratio readback has partial Pearson 1.0 but nonuniform signs across organisms: the logged ratios are 1.3514980212896148, -0.24869078488829677, and 1.0086640520051702, so the sign pattern is +, -, +. This prevents the sweep from being read as a directionally universal translation rule. The section only says that the $B^*_{Q6}$ carrier remains visible on the modeled tAI readout after the stated controls; any stronger claim needs a separate contact that directly measures translation output, protein abundance, or another downstream layer under the same control logic.
