\subsection{Modeled-tAI organism sweep for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-sqt-organism-sweep-b-star-q6-modeled-tai}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This record tests whether the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates retain modeled translation-layer signal after the usual coding-sequence controls have been removed.  The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control design has $24$ columns and includes the amino-acid-composition quotient, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  The organism sweep computed modeled tAI for $41$ organisms, exceeding the target of $40$ organisms, with $1000$ permutations recorded for the per-organism $S^{QT}$ surface.  The observed distribution of the per-organism $R^2_{QT}$ values is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{QT} &= 0.1579271837024072,\\
Q_{25}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.26353954700074694,\\
\operatorname{median}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.33934845811598124,\\
\operatorname{mean}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.38466555440501393,\\
Q_{75}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.5143951545826744,\\
\max R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite computation is not a three-organism rank artifact: the modeled-readout surface remains nonzero across a broad organism panel, while retaining the declared residual coordinate basis and the same control family.

\paragraph{Relation to the powered modeled-tAI matrix.}
A separate powered modeled-tAI computation on $40$ organisms produced a $9 \times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, rank $9$ for the residual coordinate design, rank $23$ for the control matrix, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after the controls.  The modeled readout columns in that matrix used the same nine coordinate names as the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis.  This earlier powered run establishes that, when the modeled tAI readout is projected into the same synonymous-residual coordinate system, the joint matrix is numerically well populated rather than a single-coordinate accident.  The organism sweep reported here asks the complementary question: whether the modeled translation-readout association persists as the organism panel is enlarged.  The answer in the finite row is positive for the bounded modeled-tAI question, since $41$ organisms were swept and the full $R^2_{QT}$ distribution stays strictly above zero, from $0.1579271837024072$ to $0.8213457220900503$.

\paragraph{Cross-readout anchoring.}
The modeled-tAI sweep is not the only finite contact involving the same residual coordinate family.  A local ribosome-occupancy analysis joined $5754$ genes after length checks and reported $5754$ gene identifiers overlapping between ordered CDS rows and occupancy rows.  Within exact window and amino-acid position bins, $9$ synonymous families were tested with at least $200$ permutations, and the families passing the stated $\lambda_{DL}$ and support gate were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$, with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and the rule $C_H>0$.  This is not used to reclassify the modeled-tAI sweep as measured ribosome occupancy; it only shows that the same coordinate family has a separate, position-controlled ribosome-contact check whose passing families include two single-family coordinates and the aggregate $f3\_stress$ direction.

\paragraph{Protein and RNA-side finite context.}
The same coordinate system has finite protein-abundance and RNA-stability contacts, but those contacts remain separate readouts.  In yeast protein abundance, the condition comparison used $2209$ shared proteins and produced $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD and $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD, with $f3\_stress$ dominant in both conditions and condition-vector similarity $0.9983880256003688$ by Pearson correlation and $0.9993775935182967$ by cosine.  The yeast mRNA half-life contact joined $5085$ rows, gave $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, and identified $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ as the dominant $S^{QM}$ coordinate; the same join had $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$, and permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$ for the $R^2_{QM}$ comparison.  In the mediation decomposition on the same yeast row set, $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, and the variance fraction absorbed by the mRNA half-life mediator was $0.09994436477872035$, against a permutation null mean $0.00019674487584783138$ and null $95\%$ value $0.0014571003414947088$.

\paragraph{Coordinate reducibility checks.}
The coordinate-decomposition audit on the yeast abundance target used $n=1258$ joined rows, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, and a $24$-rank control basis.  It reported $9$ single-coordinate decompositions and found real-source support for $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The $f3\_stress$ family decomposition used $1800$ joined yeast rows, $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations; its full $f3\_stress$ projection had norm-square $52.76911713166682$ and accounted for $0.12259712041843235$ of the target energy.  Nineteen of the $21$ family subcoordinates were listed as real-source families, including $K\_AAA\_to\_AAG$, $R\_AGA\_to\_AGG$, $I\_AUU\_to\_AUA$, and $T\_ACU\_to\_ACG$.  These checks support the interpretation that the nine-coordinate basis is not merely a label set for the modeled-tAI sweep; several of its directions also survive independent finite-row projections.

\paragraph{Complement and candidate-readout boundary.}
The modeled-tAI complement analysis gives additional scale for the translation-side interpretation.  For six organism contexts, the fraction $d$ of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance component remaining outside the modeled-tAI direction was $0.874628721049266$ in zebrafish, $0.6629960038578534$ in $E.\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ in chicken, $0.9208949355679455$ in human, $0.8972672018746484$ in $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$, and $0.21173219572706714$ in yeast.  The corresponding modeled-tAI-mediated fraction is largest in yeast, where $m=0.788267804272933$, and smaller in the other listed organisms.  Candidate readout ranking on the complement side reported organism-specific outcomes: zebrafish selected measured TE with score $0.001566051846174291$ and $n=13140$ joined rows; human selected complex membership as the top candidate with $n=18335$ joined rows and Bonferroni threshold $0.0002646171322365322$; yeast selected mRNA with $n=5128$ joined rows and Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$; $E.\ coli$ and $M.\ smegmatis$ had no selected $H_o^{\ast}$ in that ranking table.  These numbers prevent over-reading the modeled-tAI sweep: the complement is often large, and the most relevant downstream readout is organism-dependent.

\paragraph{Verdict and local boundary.}
The ready claim is the bounded statement that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate system has a positive, powered modeled-tAI organism-sweep contact: $41$ organisms were swept, the target of $40$ organisms was reached, and the per-organism $R^2_{QT}$ distribution remained strictly positive under the declared coding-sequence controls.  This does not say that modeled tAI is observed ribosome occupancy, measured elongation speed, measured translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal law.  Such stronger assertions require their own row-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In this chapter the allowed conclusion is narrower: modeled tAI, constructed from organism-level tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights, detects nonzero cross-organism $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual signal after amino-acid, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls; all causal, structural, functional, and universal interpretations remain outside the certified finite row.
