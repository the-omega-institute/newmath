\subsection{Modeled tAI sweep for the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-sqt-organism-sweep-b-star-q6-modeled-tai}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite matrix under test.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ retain translation-readout information after the usual sequence-composition controls have been imposed.  The carrier is the residual score matrix $S^{QT}$ whose rows are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress, and whose readout side is a modeled codon adaptation surface computed from organism-level tRNA gene-copy contacts and wobble weights.  The fitted controls have $24$ columns and include length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the simpler $M$-density baseline, so the statistic is not allowed to explain amino-acid composition or a generic codon-density effect twice.

\paragraph{Organism sweep.}
The powered sweep reached its declared target: $41$ organisms passed the data and size filters against a target of $40$ organisms.  Each organism was evaluated only when the CDS join supplied at least the required gene support; sparse organisms were skipped rather than completed by imputation.  Across the $41$ organism-scoped modeled-tAI fits, the residual $R^{2}_{QT}$ distribution was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^{2}_{QT} &= 0.1579271837024072,\\
Q_{25}(R^{2}_{QT}) &= 0.26353954700074694,\\
\operatorname{median}(R^{2}_{QT}) &= 0.33934845811598124,\\
\operatorname{mean}(R^{2}_{QT}) &= 0.38466555440501393,\\
Q_{75}(R^{2}_{QT}) &= 0.5143951545826744,\\
\max R^{2}_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The sweep used $1000$ permutations for the organism-level null comparison.  The finite row therefore supports a bounded claim: after the amino-acid quotient and the explicit covariates are applied, the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface is not absorbed by the controlled modeled-tAI baseline across the swept organism panel.

\paragraph{Rank-powered reference fit.}
A related cross-organism modeled-tAI fit gives the scale of the joint nine-coordinate surface.  In that fit, $40$ organisms were loaded, the control matrix had $24$ columns with rank $23$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $17$, and the residualized coordinate matrix had rank $9$.  The resulting joint statistic was $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the degeneracy guard $0.999999$ and above the positive-entry threshold $10^{-12}$.  Thus the large modeled-tAI association is not being read from a rank-saturated three-row table.  It is a high-rank, controlled, modeled-readout calculation on a finite organism panel.

\paragraph{Coupling and coordinate sanity checks.}
Before the powered modeled-tAI readout was evaluated, the codon-count and tRNA-copy proxy coupling matrix had already shown that the nine residual coordinates were not numerically silent against a translation-adjacent spectrum: all $9$ entries crossed the $0.01$ coupling threshold, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  That proxy calculation was not itself a survival statistic, but it justifies treating the nine rows as live coordinates rather than empty labels.  The small Leu gate also gives a useful warning about direction: in the three-organism CUN/UUR Leu contact the partial Pearson value was $1.0$ for $n=3$, while the log-ratio signs were $(1,-1,1)$, so sign is reported as context-dependent rather than as a universal orientation.

\paragraph{Relation to measured and downstream contacts.}
The modeled-tAI result sits below stronger translation and protein claims.  In a six-organism protein-abundance complement calculation, modeled-tAI explained very different fractions of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance component across organisms: the complement fraction $d$ was $0.874628721049266$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.6629960038578532$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.9400341806302775$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $0.9208949355679455$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $0.8972672018746489$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, and $0.21173219572706703$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The complementary mediated fraction $m$ was correspondingly $0.12537127895073397$, $0.3370039961421468$, $0.05996581936972245$, $0.07910506443205445$, $0.10273279812535108$, and $0.788267804272933$.  Those values show that modeled tAI can align strongly with the residual abundance component in yeast while leaving most of that component outside modeled tAI in several metazoan and bacterial rows.

\paragraph{Non-translation readout contrast.}
Candidate readouts for the modeled-tAI complement were organism-specific.  For \emph{Danio rerio}, measured translation efficiency was the top scored candidate with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, and score $0.001566051846174291$, exceeding its Bonferroni threshold $0.000719994971498518$ on $13140$ joined rows.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, complex membership was the selected candidate with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, and $18335$ joined rows; for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, mRNA was selected with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, and $5128$ joined rows.  \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 had no selected $H^{*}_{o}$ in that ranking.  These contrasts keep the modeled-tAI sweep from being overstated as a single conserved mechanism.

\paragraph{Measured ribosome contact.}
The position-controlled ribosome-occupancy analysis is separate evidence and not a substitute for the modeled-tAI sweep.  It joined $5754$ genes after length checking, with zero length mismatches between ordered CDS and occupancy rows.  Its within-position-bin permutation used $200$ permutations over $9$ computable families, and the families passing the stated $C_H>0$ and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ gate with $DL=2$ were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  This supports the idea that some synonymous families can carry local occupancy separation under position controls, but it does not convert the organism-sweep modeled-tAI matrix into measured ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The finite conclusion is therefore narrow.  The ready claim is that a $41$-organism modeled-tAI sweep found positive controlled $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual survival on a modeled translation-layer readout, with $R^{2}_{QT}$ values ranging from $0.1579271837024072$ to $0.8213457220900503$ and with the declared $40$-organism power target exceeded.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights is not measured ribosome motion, not translation efficiency from RPF over mRNA, not protein abundance, not folding, not physical admissibility, not molecular function, not a biochemical mechanism, and not a universal biological law.  Each such stronger reading would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
