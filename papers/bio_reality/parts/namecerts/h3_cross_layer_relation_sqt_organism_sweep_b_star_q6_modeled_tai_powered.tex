\subsection{Modeled-tAI organism sweep for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-sqt-organism-sweep-b-star-q6-modeled-tai}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim tested.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer question: after quotienting away amino-acid composition and residualizing ordinary coding-sequence covariates, do the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates retain predictive mass against modeled translation-adaptation readouts across a multi-organism panel?  The tested coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The control design contains $24$ columns, including the intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline.  The finite-row run is not a protein-function claim; it asks only whether the residual codon coordinates remain visible to a modeled translation-adaptation layer.

\paragraph{Finite-row statistic.}
The organism sweep computed modeled tAI entries for $41$ organisms and exceeded the declared target of $40$ organisms.  For each organism with at least the required gene support, the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design was compared with modeled tAI readouts derived from tRNA gene-copy and wobble-weight information.  The sweep used $1000$ permutations for the per-organism statistic.  The observed $R^2_{QT}$ distribution over the accepted organisms was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{QT} &= 0.1579271837024072,\\
Q_1(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.26353954700074694,\\
\operatorname{median}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.33934845811598124,\\
\operatorname{mean}(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.38466555440501393,\\
Q_3(R^2_{QT}) &= 0.5143951545826744,\\
\max R^2_{QT} &= 0.8213457220900503.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus every retained organism in this sweep had a positive modeled-tAI residual projection, with the weakest retained organism still at $R^2_{QT}=0.1579271837024072$ and the strongest at $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$.  The distribution is wide enough to rule out a single universal effect size, but it is not compatible with complete absorption of the tested $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis by the listed controls inside this modeled readout design.

\paragraph{Rank and calibration context.}
A companion powered modeled-tAI calculation used $40$ organisms, the same nine residual coordinates, $24$ control columns, control-rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and a full residual-coordinate rank of $9$.  Its joint modeled-tAI fit gave $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the stated degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.  This calibration matters because the sweep is not being read as an $n=3$ saturated surface.  The earlier three-organism Leu CUN/UUR contact had Pearson and partial Pearson values equal to $1.0$ over $n=3$, with log-ratio signs $(1,-1,1)$ and therefore no uniform sign claim.  In contrast, the present sweep is a rank-powered modeled-readout scan over $41$ organisms, with sparse joins excluded rather than forced.

\paragraph{Relation to coupling rather than survival.}
The older proxy coupling matrix computed all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum in $3$ organisms and found $9$ nonzero entries, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$ above the $0.01$ coupling threshold.  That result showed that the residual basis can couple to a translation-adjacent proxy, but it did not by itself supply a controlled survival score.  The organism sweep reported here is stronger in one respect and weaker in another: it has a broad modeled-tAI panel and positive modeled $R^2_{QT}$ values across retained organisms, but its readout is still modeled adaptation from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights rather than directly measured ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, or translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite-row conclusion is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis survives as a modeled translation-adaptation signal under the declared controls in a $41$-organism sweep.  The statement supported here is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists o\; R^2_{QT}(o) &> 0,\\
\text{and in the retained sweep}\quad R^2_{QT}(o) &\in [0.1579271837024072,\,0.8213457220900503].
\end{aligned}
$$
Because the retained distribution is entirely positive and the target organism count was reached, the appropriate status is a ready modeled-readout support record for the cross-layer relation.  The phrase ``modeled-readout'' is essential: the statistic is about residual codon coordinates against a constructed tAI layer, not about direct ribosome measurements.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No direct translation mechanism follows from this row.  Modeled tAI from GtRNAdb-style gene-copy information is not measured ribosome occupancy, not measured translation efficiency, not proteomics, and not protein folding.  The result does not assert physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  The organisms are not phylogenetically independent replicates, so the cross-organism pattern is descriptive rather than a population-level estimate.  Any claim about measured translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
