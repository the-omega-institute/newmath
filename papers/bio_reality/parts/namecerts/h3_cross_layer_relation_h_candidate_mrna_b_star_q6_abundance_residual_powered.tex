\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ mRNA H-candidate abundance residual}\label{subsec:bior-hcandidate-mrna-bstar-q6-abundance-residual}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}  The record concerns a finite coding-sequence panel carrying nine amino-acid-quotient-surviving $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, organism-matched protein abundance, residualized modeled translation-weight information, and a matched $\log_{10}$ mRNA readout.  The coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The control matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $q$-vectors.  The realized row counts are large enough for a descriptive projection rather than a saturated toy fit: $n=13140$ for danio rerio, $n=3415$ for escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for homo sapiens, and $n=5128$ for saccharomyces cerevisiae.  In all four organisms the control design reports $24$ control columns and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}  The mRNA H-candidate test does not ask whether transcript abundance causes the protein residual.  It asks how much of the modeled-translation complement of the protein-abundance residual is absorbed by residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA after the same sequence controls have been imposed.  In notation, if $P_{Q}$ is the protein-abundance residual component associated with the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate block and $T_{\mathrm{mod}}$ is the modeled translation-weight readout, the first residual complement is $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$.  The reported descriptive quantities are
$$\begin{aligned}
  d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= \frac{\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^{2}}{\|P_{Q}\|^{2}},\\
  d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= \text{the remaining fraction after adding residualized mRNA},\\
  \mathrm{absorbed}_{mRNA} &= d_{\mathrm{modeled}}-d_{\mathrm{combined}}.
\end{aligned}$$
For danio rerio, $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$, $\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2=919.9445878158525$, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8424312412078521$, $d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.8322280386743373$, and the mRNA absorption is $0.011973033879985792$.  For escherichia coli K12 MG1655, the corresponding values are $393.78692095732885$, $261.0102870984463$, $0.6628211177352172$, $0.6615987644523418$, and $0.0017706954554908886$.  For homo sapiens they are $565.3850201235074$, $484.31956581711796$, $0.8566190269974241$, $0.8498079619223776$, and $0.007858222847072218$.  For saccharomyces cerevisiae they are $675.3275479192903$, $151.18264752811882$, $0.22386566043976477$, $0.223360891719316$, and $0.0018976054721605224$.

\paragraph{Join audit.}  The same projection is sensitive to which proteins survive the mRNA join.  The complement comparison against the full abundance-CDS join is nearly unchanged in escherichia coli K12 MG1655 and saccharomyces cerevisiae, but not in danio rerio or homo sapiens.  The absolute differences are $0.00017488612263594217$ for escherichia coli K12 MG1655 and $0.012133464712697745$ for saccharomyces cerevisiae, both within the stated $0.02$ tolerance.  The differences are $0.03219747984141397$ for danio rerio and $0.06427590857052146$ for homo sapiens, both outside that tolerance.  The row audit explains why this matters: danio rerio moves from $14410$ reported abundance-joined CDS rows to $13140$ rows with valid mRNA, homo sapiens from $19053$ to $11554$, escherichia coli K12 MG1655 from $3739$ to $3415$, and saccharomyces cerevisiae from $5815$ to $5128$.  Thus the mRNA projection is a condition- and identifier-dependent finite-panel statistic, not a portable biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to RNA-stability mediation rows.}  A separate finite-row computation tests RNA half-life or stability as a mediator-like readout in two organisms, and it gives useful scale for the H-candidate interpretation without turning the mRNA abundance projection into a causal story.  In that computation the stable joined row counts are $2720$ for escherichia coli and $16165$ for human, again with $24$ controls, $9$ $q$-coordinates, and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.  The mediation fractions are $0.24697202727892137$ for escherichia coli and $0.1411299863319291$ for human, with right-tail permutation values $0.000999000999000999$ in both cases.  The same diagnostic table reports $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$ for escherichia coli, and $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$ for human.  These rows support only a variance-partition reading for the attached RNA-stability readout; they do not prove that transcript abundance or half-life is the mechanistic path from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ to protein abundance.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}  The finite data justify a bounded cross-layer readback: residualized mRNA can be tested as an H-candidate by projecting $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto the residualized mRNA column under amino-acid composition, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, and organism-matched row restrictions.  The observed mRNA absorption is small in all four organisms, with the largest reported value $0.011973033879985792$ in danio rerio.  The computation therefore records a descriptive absorption surface and a join-sensitivity audit, not a causal transcript mechanism.  No translation-readout survival $S^{QT}$, no folding or structural order, no physical admissibility, no molecular function, no phenotype, no mechanism, and no universal biological rule follows from this row.  Any such promotion would require a separate layer-matched contact and a controlled statistic at that layer, beginning with a nondegenerate translation-readout matrix before downstream protein-omics interpretation is allowed.
