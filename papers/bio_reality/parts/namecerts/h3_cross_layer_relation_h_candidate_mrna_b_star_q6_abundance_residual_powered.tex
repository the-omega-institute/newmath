\subsection{B-star-Q6 mRNA absorption as a bounded abundance-residual readback}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-mrna-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record studies a finite panel of coding sequences in four organism-indexed rows, with each coding sequence carrying nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, a protein-abundance readout, and a matched transcript-abundance control. The residual coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-}stress$. The control block $Z$ has 24 declared columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline measured as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. In every organism row the reported control rank is $23$, so the finite computation is not a statement about unconstrained sequence geometry; it is a residual projection after these codon, composition, and length covariates have been removed.

\paragraph{Joined rows and ranks.}
The actual mRNA-controlled abundance panel has $n=13140$ rows for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K--12 MG1655, $n=11554$ rows for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The corresponding CDS-abundance joins before imposing the mRNA row condition were $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$. Thus the mRNA contact changes the admissible protein-id support in a species-dependent way. The mRNA-controlled projection must therefore be read on its own finite row set rather than transferred from the larger abundance-only join.

\paragraph{Modeled complement before transcript absorption.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the residual protein-abundance component associated with the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates after applying $Z$, and let $T_{mod}$ denote the modeled translation-weight component used in this computation. The experiment first records the remaining modeled-translation complement:
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{mod} &= \frac{\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite values are $D_{mod}=0.8424312412078521$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.6628211177352172$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.8566190269974241$ for human, and $0.22386566043976477$ for yeast. The underlying squared norms are, respectively, $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$ and $\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=919.9445878158525$; $393.78692095732885$ and $261.0102870984463$; $565.3850201235074$ and $484.31956581711796$; and $675.3275479192903$ and $151.18264752811882$. These numbers say only that a modeled translation-weight projection leaves a residual protein-abundance component of the displayed size; they do not identify a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{mRNA as an H-candidate projection.}
The transcript readout is then tested as an $H$-candidate by projecting $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA on the same joined rows. The computed transcript absorption fractions are small in all four organisms: $0.011973033879985792$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.0017706954554908886$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.007858222847072218$ for human, and $0.0018976054721605224$ for yeast. After adding this transcript projection, the remaining combined complement values are $0.8322280386743373$, $0.6615987644523418$, $0.8498079619223776$, and $0.223360891719316$. In this finite run, ordinary mRNA abundance absorbs only a small part of the modeled-translation complement abundance residual.

\paragraph{Join-sensitivity check.}
A negative self-check compares the current mRNA-conditioned $D_{mod}$ values against the complement experiment computed on the fuller abundance-CDS join. \emph{E. coli} changes by only $0.00017488612263594217$ and yeast by $0.012133464712697745$, both within the $0.02$ tolerance. \emph{D. rerio} changes by $0.03219747984141397$ and human by $0.06427590857052146$, both outside that tolerance. The failed rows are not biological counterexamples; they record that the mRNA availability condition changes the protein-id support enough that the two projections should not be silently identified.

\paragraph{Relation to RNA-stability mediation rows.}
A separate finite check replaces same-condition mRNA abundance by an RNA half-life or stability readout in two organisms. In \emph{E. coli}, the three-way join has $2720$ stable identifiers, a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, a mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, and a right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. In human, the analogous row has $16165$ stable identifiers, a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, a mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. These rows support a descriptive variance-partition statement for the attached finite panels, not a causal RNA-decay pathway.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded cross-layer relation: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance-residual panel admits a computed mRNA-absorption readback, but the absorption fractions are small and the transcript row condition changes the admissible support in two of the four organisms. The admissible statement is that $\log_{10}$ mRNA was tested as an $H$-candidate covariate for the modeled-translation complement residual and that its descriptive projection was computed under the stated controls. No translation survival $S^{QT}$, protein-output mechanism, folding conclusion, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, causal transcript mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and the controlled statistic is recomputed on the corresponding finite panel.
