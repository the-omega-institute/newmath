\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual abundance after mRNA control}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-mrna-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.} The record concerns a finite matched panel of coding sequences in which nine amino-acid-quotient-surviving synonymous residual coordinates are evaluated against protein abundance and transcript abundance after a common control design has been removed.  The coordinate list is $\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.  The shared control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In each organism this design has $24$ declared control columns and numerical rank $23$, so the residual projection is not an uncontrolled comparison of codon counts to abundance.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.} Four organism panels are present in the abundance--mRNA joined computation.  The zebrafish panel has $n=13140$ rows after mRNA joining, with $14410$ CDS rows reported in the abundance join, $14410$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $13140$ valid mRNA rows by protein.  The \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 panel has $n=3415$, with $3739$ CDS rows reported in the abundance join, $3747$ valid abundance rows, and $3415$ valid mRNA rows.  The human panel has $n=11554$, with $19053$ CDS rows reported, $19483$ valid abundance rows, and $11554$ valid mRNA rows.  The yeast panel has $n=5128$, with $5815$ CDS rows reported, $6351$ valid abundance rows, and $5128$ valid mRNA rows.  These row counts define the finite domain of the computation; changing the transcript-availability join changes the row set and therefore changes the residual readout.

\paragraph{Modeled translation complement.} The tested quantity is not a direct translation-survival matrix $S^{QT}$.  It is the descriptive projection of a modeled-translation complement abundance residual, written $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$, onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA.  The modeled complement ratios are organism-specific: zebrafish has $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$, $\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2=919.9445878158525$, and $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8424312412078521$; \emph{E. coli} has $393.78692095732885$, $261.0102870984463$, and $0.6628211177352172$; human has $565.3850201235074$, $484.31956581711796$, and $0.8566190269974241$; yeast has $675.3275479192903$, $151.18264752811882$, and $0.22386566043976477$.  The comparison to the full abundance-CDS complement is a join self-check rather than biological promotion: zebrafish differs by $0.03219747984141397$ and human by $0.06427590857052146$, both outside the $0.02$ tolerance, while \emph{E. coli} differs by only $0.00017488612263594217$ and yeast by $0.012133464712697745$.

\paragraph{mRNA absorption readback.} Adding residualized mRNA changes the complement only weakly in all four joined panels.  The computed transcript absorption values are $0.011973033879985792$ for zebrafish, $0.0017706954554908886$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.007858222847072218$ for human, and $0.0018976054721605224$ for yeast.  The corresponding combined residual ratios are $d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.8322280386743373$, $0.6615987644523418$, $0.8498079619223776$, and $0.223360891719316$.  Thus the finite-row statement is narrow: transcript abundance is evaluated as an $H$-candidate covariate for the modeled complement, and the resulting absorption is reported as a bounded projection statistic.  A large value would not establish a causal transcript mechanism, and a small value does not establish that the missing component has been found elsewhere.

\paragraph{Related stability-mediated finite check.} A separate cross-domain half-life computation supplies useful scale for the same nine-coordinate residual family.  In the \emph{E. coli} stability join there are $2720$ stable identifiers, residual degree of freedom $2697$ after the same rank-$23$ control design, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, and deterministic right-tail permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$, with $3739$ CDS rows and $2947$ half-life records before the three-way join.  In the human stability join there are $16165$ stable identifiers, residual degree of freedom $16142$, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and the same right-tail permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$; the CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$, with $19053$ CDS rows and $19480$ consensus half-life records before joining.  These numbers support only a statistical partition of abundance-associated residual variance through an mRNA-stability readout, not a pathway claim.

\paragraph{Ordered three-matrix boundary.} The admissible cross-layer interpretation is ordered.  A translation-readout survival surface $S^{QT}$ must first be estimated using organism-matched translation readouts and the rank-powered control design above.  Only after that step may a direct protein-abundance surface $S^{QP}$ or a translation-conditioned component $M^{QTP}$ be read as a downstream matrix.  The present finite row proves that the mRNA covariate and the modeled translation complement were computed on explicit joined panels; it does not prove that any measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency entry survives.  The modeled coupling surface $A^{QT}$, if present elsewhere, is therefore not interchangeable with $S^{QT}$.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.} No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy claim, protein folding statement, physical admissibility statement, molecular function claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  The data here are sequence-derived residual coordinates, abundance rows, transcript rows, modeled adaptation information, and finite projection diagnostics.  A stronger biological claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: measured translation rows for $S^{QT}$, matched protein rows for $S^{QP}$, and an explicit conditioning test for $M^{QTP}$.  If every rank-powered $S^{QT}$ entry is nonpositive after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls, then $B^*_{Q6}$ remains only a codon-usage residual basis for this purpose and no downstream protein-readout mediation claim is licensed.
