\subsection{B$^*_{Q6}$ mRNA absorption boundary for the abundance residual}\label{sec:b-star-q6-mrna-abundance-residual-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}  The record concerns a finite panel of coding sequences in which each row carries an organism label, one of nine B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, a protein-abundance readout, and a matched mRNA-abundance control.  The coordinate list is $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  These coordinates are evaluated only after quotienting away the amino-acid sequence component: the control design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from CDS codon counts, and an M-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected B$^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Each organism has $24$ control columns and rank $23$ for the control matrix $Z$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}  The four organism-specific panels are not equal in size, because the mRNA and protein-abundance identifiers impose different joins.  Danio rerio contributes $n=13140$ rows after mRNA matching, with $14410$ CDS rows reported in the protein-abundance join and $14410$ valid abundance entries.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 contributes $n=3415$ rows, with $3739$ reported CDS rows in the abundance join and $3747$ valid abundance entries.  Homo sapiens contributes $n=11554$ rows, compared with $19053$ reported CDS rows and $19483$ valid abundance entries.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae contributes $n=5128$ rows, compared with $5815$ reported CDS rows and $6351$ valid abundance entries.  The row loss is itself part of the observation: the H-candidate projection is evaluated on the all-readout join, not on a protein-only surface.

\paragraph{Modeled complement and mRNA projection.}  Let $P_Q$ denote the protein-abundance component associated with the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after the declared controls, and let $P_{Q\perp Tmod}$ denote the modeled-tAI complement of that component.  The computed descriptive statistic is the remaining normalized energy after the modeled translation readout is removed, followed by the amount additionally absorbed by residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA.  The four primary complement values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
 d_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
 d_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
 d_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding squared energies are $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$ and $\|P_{Q\perp Tmod}\|^2=919.9445878158525$ in Danio, $393.78692095732885$ and $261.0102870984463$ in E. coli, $565.3850201235074$ and $484.31956581711796$ in human, and $675.3275479192903$ and $151.18264752811882$ in yeast.  After adding the mRNA projection, the descriptive absorption by mRNA is small in all four panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \alpha_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 0.011973033879985792, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8322280386743373,\\
 \alpha_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &= 0.0017706954554908886, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.6615987644523418,\\
 \alpha_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 0.007858222847072218, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8498079619223776,\\
 \alpha_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 0.0018976054721605224, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.223360891719316.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the matched mRNA control does not explain away most of the modeled-tAI complement abundance residual in this finite panel.  The result is a bounded projection statement: high or low absorption is not promoted to a transcript-causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Join self-check.}  The modeled complement was compared with the protein-only complement experiment to detect whether the mRNA join changes the surface being measured.  The all-readout join differs from the protein-only reference by $0.03219747984141397$ in Danio and by $0.06427590857052146$ in human, exceeding the $0.02$ matching tolerance in both cases.  The same check passes in E. coli, where the difference is $0.00017488612263594217$, and in yeast, where the difference is $0.012133464712697745$.  These failures are not biological counterevidence; they mark that the mRNA availability filter changes the protein-id panel for Danio and human and therefore prevents treating the all-readout row set as identical to the protein-only one.

\paragraph{Cross-domain stability comparison.}  A separate mRNA-stability finite panel was computed for E. coli and human.  The E. coli join uses an Esquerre GeneId b-number key, has $2720$ stable joined rows, $3739$ reported CDS rows, $2947$ half-life records, and a reported CDS hit rate of $0.9978649586335735$.  The human join uses Ensembl gene identifiers with a gene-symbol fallback, has $16165$ stable joined rows, $19053$ reported CDS rows, $19480$ consensus records, and a reported CDS hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$.  In that stability decomposition, the B$^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance residual association has $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$ in E. coli, giving a mediation fraction of $0.24697202727892137$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In human the corresponding values are $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers support a descriptive variance partition involving RNA half-life or stability, not a causal route from codon geometry to protein abundance.

\paragraph{Ordered readout rule.}  The admissible cross-layer order is strict.  A direct protein-abundance association is not enough to claim a translation-mediated effect.  The translation-readout survival surface $S^{QT}$ must be estimated first under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and mRNA controls.  Only after a nondegenerate positive $S^{QT}$ entry is present may a direct protein surface $S^{QP}$ or a translation-conditioned component $M^{QTP}$ be interpreted as downstream of the same coordinate.  The present H-candidate mRNA panel supplies a controlled descriptive projection of $P_{Q\perp Tmod}$ onto residualized mRNA; it does not by itself provide a measured ribosome-footprint or TE survival matrix.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}  The finite computations above allow the following limited statement: in the joined panels, residualized mRNA absorbs only a small share of the modeled-tAI complement abundance residual, while an independent mRNA-stability comparison gives nonzero descriptive mediation fractions in E. coli and human.  Nothing in this chapter establishes translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  Each of those stronger readings requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, the matched mRNA abundance rows are controls for separating transcript abundance from translation and protein-abundance readouts; they are not themselves evidence for translation survival.
