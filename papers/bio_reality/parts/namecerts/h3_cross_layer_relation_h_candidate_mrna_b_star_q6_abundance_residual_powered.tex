\subsection{mRNA absorption boundary for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}
\label{subsec:bior-reality-q6-h-candidate-mrna-abundance-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests a narrow cross-layer question: after a protein-abundance residual has already been projected against the modeled translation readout, how much of the remaining $B^{*}_{Q6}$-indexed abundance residual is absorbed by matched mRNA abundance?  The finite carrier is the joined coding-sequence panel indexed by organism, protein identifier, nine synonymous residual coordinates, control covariates, modeled-translation complement, protein abundance, and matched transcript abundance.  The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The control design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Each organism has $24$ control columns and control rank $23$.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
The four organism-specific panels are not equal to the full protein-abundance joins because requiring matched mRNA abundance changes the available row set.  The finite row counts are: $n=13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, with $14410$ CDS/protein abundance rows before the mRNA filter; $n=3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, with $3739$ CDS/protein abundance rows and $3747$ valid abundance-by-protein rows; $n=11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, with $19053$ CDS/protein abundance rows and $19483$ valid abundance-by-protein rows; and $n=5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with $5815$ CDS/protein abundance rows and $6351$ valid abundance-by-protein rows.  Thus the transcript-matched panel is a real finite restriction, not a relabeling of the protein-abundance surface.

\paragraph{Residual statistic.}
Let $P_{Q}$ denote the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual after the stated controls, and let $T_{\mathrm{mod}}$ denote the modeled translation readout used for the complement projection.  The computed descriptive statistic first removes the modeled translation component and then asks whether residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA explains any further squared norm:
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}} &= P_{Q}-\Pi_{T_{\mathrm{mod}}}P_{Q},\\
\mathrm{absorbed\_by\_mRNA} &= d_{\mathrm{modeled}}-d_{\mathrm{combined}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The resulting modeled-complement fractions are $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8424312412078521$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.6628211177352172$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.8566190269974241$ for human, and $0.22386566043976477$ for yeast.  The corresponding mRNA absorption values are small: $0.011973033879985792$, $0.0017706954554908886$, $0.007858222847072218$, and $0.0018976054721605224$, respectively.  Equivalently, the combined residual fractions are $0.8322280386743373$, $0.6615987644523418$, $0.8498079619223776$, and $0.223360891719316$.

\paragraph{Norm readout.}
The norm accounting shows the scale of the calculation.  In \emph{D. rerio}, $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$ and $\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2=919.9445878158525$.  In \emph{E. coli}, the corresponding values are $393.78692095732885$ and $261.0102870984463$.  In human, they are $565.3850201235074$ and $484.31956581711796$.  In yeast, they are $675.3275479192903$ and $151.18264752811882$.  These rows therefore support a bounded descriptive statement: matched mRNA abundance accounts for only a small additional fraction of the modeled-translation-complement residual on this finite panel.

\paragraph{Join self-check.}
The complement comparison is also a warning about row identity.  Comparing the current all-readout join to the full abundance-CDS join gives absolute differences $0.03219747984141397$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.00017488612263594217$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.06427590857052146$ for human, and $0.012133464712697745$ for yeast.  With a $0.02$ tolerance, \emph{E. coli} and yeast pass the join-stability check, while \emph{D. rerio} and human fail it.  The failed rows are not biological contradictions; they say that adding the mRNA requirement changes the protein-identifier join enough that the projected abundance complement must be read on the restricted panel only.

\paragraph{Neighboring stability row.}
A separate RNA-stability mediation computation gives a useful comparison but not a replacement for this mRNA-abundance test.  On the stability panel, \emph{E. coli} has $2720$ stable joined rows, residual degrees of freedom $2697$, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  Human has $16165$ stable joined rows, residual degrees of freedom $16142$, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and the same reported right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The stability panel also reports finite join rates: $0.9978649586335735$ for the \emph{E. coli} CDS join and $0.9779294769799313$ for the human CDS join.  These numbers show that RNA half-life can carry a stronger variance-partition signal than same-condition mRNA abundance in a different finite panel, but they do not turn the mRNA-abundance projection into a causal transcript mechanism.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-specific conclusion is therefore negative in a precise sense and positive only as a bounded computation.  The tested mRNA abundance coordinate is an $H$-candidate covariate for the residual left after modeled translation readout; it does not absorb most of that residual in any of the four organism panels.  The row restriction is material for two organisms, so the reported quantities belong to the transcript-matched joins and should not be copied back to the larger abundance-only joins.  The computation is nevertheless usable as a cross-layer guardrail: if a later protein-readout claim uses the same $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals, it must report the translation-readout survival surface first, then the direct protein-abundance surface, and only then any mediation or attenuation by transcript abundance or RNA stability.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy result, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, phenotype claim, causal pathway, or universal biological rule follows from this mRNA projection.  Transcript abundance is a matched covariate and a possible absorber of residual variance; it is not a translation readout, not a protein-output mechanism, and not a substitute for a controlled $S^{QT}$ matrix.  Any later promotion requires a separate layer-matched contact and a finite statistic on the relevant rows.
