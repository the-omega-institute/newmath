\subsection{The mRNA H-candidate test for the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-h-candidate-mrna-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite matched panel of coding sequences carrying nine amino-acid-quotient-surviving $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a protein-abundance residual readout, a modeled translation component, and an mRNA abundance covariate.  The tested coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control block $Z$ has an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In each organism the raw control-column count is $24$ and the numerical rank is $23$, so the readback is not the rank-saturated $n=3$ surface that is excluded by the conjecture.

\paragraph{Joined rows and residual dimensions.}
The mRNA H-candidate projection is evaluated on the all-readout joins, not on the larger protein-only joins.  The joined row counts are $13{,}140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3{,}415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $11{,}554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5{,}128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding protein-abundance joins reported before imposing the mRNA availability constraint are $14{,}410$, $3{,}739$, $19{,}053$, and $5{,}815$.  Thus the finite row object is explicitly condition-sensitive: zebrafish and human lose many rows when the mRNA covariate is required, while the bacterial and yeast joins are closer to their protein-only panels.

\paragraph{Projection being tested.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the protein-abundance residual component associated with the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block after the declared $Z$ controls.  Let $T_{mod}$ denote the modeled translation component and let $m$ denote residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA abundance on the same joined rows.  The H-candidate statistic first removes the modeled translation component and then asks how much of the remaining abundance residual is absorbed by the mRNA coordinate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{Q\perp T_{mod}} &= P_Q - \Pi_{T_{mod}} P_Q,\\
d_{mod} &= \frac{\lVert P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2},\\
d_{combined} &= \frac{\lVert P_{Q\perp (T_{mod},m)}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2},\\
a_{mRNA} &= d_{mod}-d_{combined}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $a_{mRNA}$ is a descriptive absorption scalar.  It is not a causal effect, and a small value does not certify that the remaining residual has been explained by translation, turnover, localization, stability, or any downstream biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Numerical readback.}
The modeled-complement ratios are organism-specific.  For \emph{Danio rerio}, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=1092.0114815505467$, $\lVert P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\rVert^2=919.9445878158525$, and $d_{mod}=0.8424312412078521$.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the corresponding values are $393.78692095732885$, $261.0102870984463$, and $0.6628211177352172$.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, they are $565.3850201235074$, $484.31956581711796$, and $0.8566190269974241$.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, they are $675.3275479192903$, $151.18264752811882$, and $0.22386566043976477$.  Adding the residualized mRNA coordinate changes these ratios to $0.8322280386743373$, $0.6615987644523418$, $0.8498079619223776$, and $0.223360891719316$, respectively.  The absorbed portions are therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{mRNA}(\mathrm{zebrafish}) &= 0.011973033879985792,\\
a_{mRNA}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.0017706954554908886,\\
a_{mRNA}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.007858222847072218,\\
a_{mRNA}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.0018976054721605224.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite outcome is that the mRNA covariate absorbs a small part of the modeled-translation-complement abundance residual in all four organisms, with the largest value in zebrafish and the smallest value in \emph{E. coli}.  The statement is bounded to this projection geometry: the numbers say how the residual energy is partitioned under the chosen controls and joined rows.

\paragraph{Join self-check.}
The all-readout join changes the modeled-complement value relative to the protein-only abundance join.  In zebrafish, the comparison is $0.8424312412078521$ versus $0.874628721049266$, an absolute difference of $0.03219747984141397$, which fails the $0.02$ agreement band.  In \emph{E. coli}, the comparison is $0.6628211177352172$ versus $0.6629960038578532$, an absolute difference of $0.00017488612263594217$, which passes the band.  In human, the comparison is $0.8566190269974241$ versus $0.9208949355679455$, an absolute difference of $0.06427590857052146$, which fails the band.  In yeast, the comparison is $0.22386566043976477$ versus $0.21173219572706703$, an absolute difference of $0.012133464712697745$, which passes the band.  This makes row availability part of the mathematical object rather than a clerical detail: a protein-only panel and an all-readout panel need not induce the same residual geometry.

\paragraph{Related stability mediation check.}
A separate mRNA-stability mediation computation gives a useful contrast because it uses half-life or stability rows rather than abundance rows.  In the bacterial stability join, $2720$ stable identifiers are retained from $3739$ joined CDS records and $2947$ valid half-life records, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9978649586335735$.  Its joint nine-dimensional mediation fraction is $0.24697202727892137$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In the human stability join, $16165$ stable identifiers are retained from $19053$ joined CDS records and $19480$ valid consensus records, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9779294769799313$.  Its mediation fraction is $0.1411299863319291$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, and the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These stability numbers do not replace the abundance H-candidate projection; they show that transcript-layer readouts can be finite, joined, and statistically nontrivial while still remaining descriptive variance partitions.

\paragraph{Ordered cross-layer boundary.}
The admissible ordering is translation readout first, direct protein readout second, and translation-mediated protein interpretation only after the first two surfaces exist on a sufficiently powered panel.  The present H-candidate row does not contain an organism-matched, controlled $S^{QT}$ survival matrix from measured ribosome profiling or measured translation efficiency.  It also does not contain a controlled $M^{QTP}$ matrix showing that conditioning on a matched translation readout attenuates a direct $Q_i$ to protein-abundance component.  Therefore the result may be stated as a finite residual-energy decomposition involving modeled translation complement and residualized mRNA abundance, but it may not be promoted to translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.  Any one of those stronger readings requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic on the corresponding joined rows.
