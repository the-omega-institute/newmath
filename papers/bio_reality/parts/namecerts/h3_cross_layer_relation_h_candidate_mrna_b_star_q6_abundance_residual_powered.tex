\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mRNA-absorption boundary for protein-abundance residuals}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ as a finite synonymous-residual coordinate panel whose rows are coding sequences joined to organism labels, codon-count controls, modeled translation weights, protein abundance, and matched mRNA abundance.  The working coordinate list has nine entries:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The common control block is
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism the recorded control-column count is $24$ and the realized control rank is $23$, so the row panels are not being read as unconstrained sequence summaries.  The joined row counts are $n=13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding protein-abundance joins before the matched-mRNA restriction were $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$ coding sequences, while the valid abundance-by-protein counts were $14410$, $3747$, $19483$, and $6351$.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
The tested quantity is not a translation-survival entry.  It is a descriptive absorption check for an $H$-candidate: after forming the modeled-translation complement of the protein-abundance residual, the row vector $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ is projected against residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA abundance.  In finite-row notation the reported scalar is the drop from the modeled complement distance $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}$ to the combined distance $d_{\mathrm{combined}}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{absorbed\_by\_mRNA}
  &= d_{\mathrm{modeled}}-d_{\mathrm{combined}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The four organism values are small but finite: for \emph{Danio rerio}, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8424312412078521$, $d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.8322280386743373$, and absorption is $0.011973033879985792$; for \emph{E. coli}, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.6615987644523418$, and absorption is $0.0017706954554908886$; for human, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8566190269974241$, $d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.8498079619223776$, and absorption is $0.007858222847072218$; for yeast, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.22386566043976477$, $d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.223360891719316$, and absorption is $0.0018976054721605224$.  The associated squared norms before and after removal of the modeled translation component are $(1092.0114815505467,919.9445878158525)$ for zebrafish, $(393.78692095732885,261.0102870984463)$ for \emph{E. coli}, $(565.3850201235074,484.31956581711796)$ for human, and $(675.3275479192903,151.18264752811882)$ for yeast.

\paragraph{Join sensitivity.}
The mRNA restriction changes the finite panel, and the record therefore carries a negative self-check rather than hiding the join dependence.  Comparing the current all-readout join to the earlier full-abundance coding-sequence join gives absolute differences $0.03219747984141397$ for zebrafish, $0.00017488612263594217$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.06427590857052146$ for human, and $0.012133464712697745$ for yeast.  The $0.02$ tolerance is met for \emph{E. coli} and yeast and not met for zebrafish or human.  The biological interpretation is correspondingly bounded: mRNA availability changes the protein-identifier panel in two of the four organisms, so the finite-row statistic is a panel-conditioned projection result, not an invariant organism-level law.

\paragraph{Relation to RNA-stability mediation rows.}
A separate cross-domain half-life panel supplies useful scale information but does not turn the mRNA-abundance projection into mechanism.  In that panel the stable-id counts are $2720$ for \emph{E. coli} and $16165$ for human, with reported coding-sequence join hit rates $0.9978649586335735$ and $0.9779294769799313$.  The joint nine-coordinate mediation fractions are $0.24697202727892137$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.1411299863319291$ for human, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The corresponding $R^2$ pairs are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{E.coli}:\quad &R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134,\qquad R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319,\\
\mathrm{human}:\quad &R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418,\qquad R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those values classify the half-life packet as a statistical mediation readout over finite rows.  They do not supply a causal transcript mechanism for the abundance projection above; the rows differ in organism coverage, platform, and biological condition.

\paragraph{Local admissible conclusion.}
The admissible claim is therefore narrow.  On the matched finite panels, residualized mRNA abundance explains a small part of the modeled-translation-complement protein-abundance residual, after amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls.  This is enough to keep mRNA abundance as an $H$-candidate covariate for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance-residual boundary, especially because the calculation is finite, row-indexed, and rank-audited.  It is not enough to promote a direct $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-survival statement, a $Q_i\to P_k$ protein-output mechanism, or a mediated $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ chain.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No translation realization follows from these rows unless a direct translation readout supplies a controlled $S^{QT}$ surface.  No protein mechanism follows unless conditioning on a matched translation readout reduces the direct $Q_i$ to protein-readout component in a controlled $M^{QTP}$ calculation.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal pathway, or universal biological rule follows from the projection, from the modeled tAI or stAI complement, from the matched mRNA covariate, or from the protein-abundance residual alone.  Each of those higher claims requires its own layer-matched contact and its own finite-row statistic.
