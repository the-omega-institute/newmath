\subsection{A translation-first boundary for the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-mrna-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as finite coding-sequence covariates in a matched organism panel. The tested table is not a protein-function table and not a structure table. It is a controlled projection table over coding sequences with organism-specific joins to mRNA abundance, modeled translation weights, and protein-abundance identifiers. The residual coordinate list has nine entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. The control design used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The design therefore asks whether a synonymous residual component remains after amino-acid sequence, length, GC3, and the coarse $M$-support baseline have already been charged.

\paragraph{Row and rank support.}
The run identifier is $11a4d3e00b77$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$07\,05{:}12{:}29$ UTC. Four organisms entered the finite panel. For $Danio\ rerio$, the all-readout join used $n=13140$ rows, with $14410$ CDS rows joined to protein abundance, $13140$ valid mRNA-by-protein rows, $24$ control columns, and $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$. For $Escherichia\ coli$ K12 MG1655, the all-readout join used $n=3415$, with $3739$ CDS rows joined to protein abundance, $3415$ valid mRNA-by-protein rows, $24$ control columns, and $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$. For $Homo\ sapiens$, the all-readout join used $n=11554$, with $19053$ CDS rows joined to protein abundance, $11554$ valid mRNA-by-protein rows, $24$ control columns, and $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$. For $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, the all-readout join used $n=5128$, with $5815$ CDS rows joined to protein abundance, $5128$ valid mRNA-by-protein rows, $24$ control columns, and $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$. These ranks are large enough for the descriptive projection to avoid the trivial $n=3$ all-one residual surface; they do not by themselves create a translation-survival entry.

\paragraph{Ordered readout decomposition.}
The admissible order is $S^{QT}$ first, $S^{QP}$ second, and $M^{QTP}$ only after the translation step has survived. The modeled translation coupling matrix $A^{QT}$ is kept separate from a controlled translation-survival matrix $S^{QT}$: a modeled coupling can guide a projection, but it is not counted as survival unless a direct translation readout is residualized against the declared controls. The downstream protein readout is likewise separated into a direct residual-to-protein component and a translation-conditioned component. The intended finite decomposition is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_k &= P_k^{(Q\to T\to P)} + P_k^{(Q\to P\mid T)} + \varepsilon_k,\\
M^{QTP}_{ijk} &\text{ is interpretable only when } S^{QT}_{ij}>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
In the present finite run, the computed object is narrower: mRNA is tested as an $H$-candidate by projecting the modeled-tAI complement abundance residual $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA. This is a descriptive absorption calculation, not a causal transcript mechanism.

\paragraph{Modeled complement and mRNA absorption.}
The modeled complement quantities show strong organism-specific differences. In $Danio\ rerio$, $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$, $\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=919.9445878158525$, and $d_{modeled}=0.8424312412078521$. In $E.\ coli$ K12 MG1655, the corresponding values are $393.78692095732885$, $261.0102870984463$, and $0.6628211177352172$. In $Homo\ sapiens$, they are $565.3850201235074$, $484.31956581711796$, and $0.8566190269974241$. In $S.\ cerevisiae$, they are $675.3275479192903$, $151.18264752811882$, and $0.22386566043976477$. After adding residualized mRNA, the absorption fractions are small: $0.011973033879985792$ for $Danio\ rerio$, $0.0017706954554908886$ for $E.\ coli$, $0.007858222847072218$ for $Homo\ sapiens$, and $0.0018976054721605224$ for $S.\ cerevisiae$. The combined residual distances are respectively $0.8322280386743373$, $0.6615987644523418$, $0.8498079619223776$, and $0.223360891719316$. Thus mRNA accounts for only a small part of the modeled-tAI complement in this run, with the largest reported absorption still below $0.012$.

\paragraph{Join sensitivity.}
The run also checks whether adding mRNA availability changes the protein-identifier join relative to a complement-reference abundance join. The modeled distance difference is $0.03219747984141397$ for $Danio\ rerio$ and $0.06427590857052146$ for $Homo\ sapiens$, so these two fail the $0.02$ matching tolerance. $E.\ coli$ K12 MG1655 has difference $0.00017488612263594217$, and $S.\ cerevisiae$ has difference $0.012133464712697745$, so these two remain within the tolerance. The negative self-check matters: in the two failed organisms, the mRNA-available subset changes the protein-id join enough that the abundance residual must be read as a joined-panel quantity rather than a full-proteome quantity.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
This finite row supports a bounded statement: the $H$-candidate mRNA projection was computed for the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis under the declared $Z$ controls, and the observed mRNA absorption of the modeled-tAI complement is small in all four organisms. It does not establish a positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry, because the direct translation-readout survival matrix has not been reported here. It also does not establish a protein-mediated mechanism: no $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ matrix is promoted unless the same coordinate first survives on a translation readout and conditioning on that readout reduces the direct $Q_i\to P_k$ component. No conclusion about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universality follows from this row unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that evidence and is evaluated under its own controls.
