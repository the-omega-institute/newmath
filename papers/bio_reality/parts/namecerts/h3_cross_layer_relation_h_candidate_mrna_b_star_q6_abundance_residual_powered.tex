\subsection{The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mRNA absorption boundary for protein-abundance residuals}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-mrna-abundance-residual-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel and residual coordinates.}
The claim is evaluated on a finite coding-sequence panel indexed by organism, a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, a modeled translation readout, an mRNA-abundance readout, and a protein-abundance readout. The residual coordinate is not the amino-acid sequence itself: it is the part of a synonymous codon contrast that remains after the amino-acid quotient is taken. The computed coordinate family has nine columns,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. Each organism-level design uses the same control form: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The finite-row computation is rank-powered rather than anecdotal. After mRNA availability is imposed on the protein-indexed joins, the retained row counts are $13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. In all four organisms the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, so the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates are tested against a nontrivial controlled row space rather than against raw abundance. The corresponding reported CDS joins before the mRNA restriction are $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$, respectively, with valid protein-abundance counts $14410$, $3747$, $19483$, and $6351$. Thus the mRNA readout changes the finite carrier in two of the four organisms in a visible way, and the comparison must be read as a same-row restricted analysis rather than as a replacement for the full protein-abundance join.

\paragraph{Modeled-translation complement.}
Let $P\_Q$ denote the protein-abundance residual component associated with the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates after the declared controls, and let $P\_{Q\perp T\mathrm{mod}}$ denote its component left after projecting away the modeled translation readout. The computation records the squared energies
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P\_Q\|^2 &= 1092.0114815505467, & \|P\_{Q\perp T\mathrm{mod}}\|^2 &= 919.9445878158525 && \text{in \emph{D. rerio}},\\
\|P\_Q\|^2 &= 393.78692095732885, & \|P\_{Q\perp T\mathrm{mod}}\|^2 &= 261.0102870984463 && \text{in \emph{E. coli}},\\
\|P\_Q\|^2 &= 565.3850201235074, & \|P\_{Q\perp T\mathrm{mod}}\|^2 &= 484.31956581711796 && \text{in human},\\
\|P\_Q\|^2 &= 675.3275479192903, & \|P\_{Q\perp T\mathrm{mod}}\|^2 &= 151.18264752811882 && \text{in yeast}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The resulting modeled-complement fractions are $0.8424312412078521$, $0.6628211177352172$, $0.8566190269974241$, and $0.22386566043976477$. These are descriptive residual fractions: they say how much of the controlled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance component remains after the modeled translation projection, not that the remaining component has a causal transcript or protein mechanism.

\paragraph{mRNA absorption test.}
The H-candidate mRNA test projects $P\_{Q\perp T\mathrm{mod}}$ onto residualized $\log\_{10}$ mRNA on the same finite rows. The computed combined fractions and absorbed portions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d\_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8322280386743373, & a\_{\mathrm{mRNA}} &= 0.011973033879985792 && \text{in \emph{D. rerio}},\\
 d\_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.6615987644523418, & a\_{\mathrm{mRNA}} &= 0.0017706954554908886 && \text{in \emph{E. coli}},\\
 d\_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8498079619223776, & a\_{\mathrm{mRNA}} &= 0.007858222847072218 && \text{in human},\\
 d\_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.223360891719316, & a\_{\mathrm{mRNA}} &= 0.0018976054721605224 && \text{in yeast}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mRNA absorption is therefore small in all four finite panels. The largest value is approximately $0.01197$ in \emph{D. rerio}; the other three are below $0.008$. This supports only a bounded statement: within these joins and controls, residualized mRNA abundance absorbs little of the modeled-translation-complement protein-abundance residual. It does not identify mRNA abundance as a mechanism, and it does not establish that the remaining residual is produced by translation, turnover, localization, post-translational state, or any other biological layer.

\paragraph{Join-sensitivity audit.}
A self-check compares the current all-readout join to a reference full-abundance CDS join. The absolute differences in the modeled-complement fraction are $0.03219747984141397$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.00017488612263594217$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.06427590857052146$ for human, and $0.012133464712697745$ for yeast. Under the $0.02$ matching tolerance, \emph{E. coli} and yeast pass, while \emph{D. rerio} and human fail. The failures are not biological counterexamples; they show that requiring an mRNA row changes the protein-identifier join enough to move the descriptive complement in those organisms. Consequently the mRNA absorption result must be reported as a row-restricted projection, with the finite carrier explicitly named.

\paragraph{Relation to the RNA-stability companion computation.}
A separate two-organism stability computation uses the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ family and the same $24$-column, rank-$23$ control form. On stable three-way joins it retains $2720$ rows for \emph{E. coli} and $16165$ rows for human. The reported mediation fractions are $0.24697202727892137$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.1411299863319291$ for human, with right-tail permutation values $0.000999000999000999$ in both cases. These numbers are relevant here only as a finite-row warning: RNA half-life can participate in a controlled variance partition, but such a partition remains observational and does not license a codon-to-mRNA-stability-to-protein causal pathway.

\paragraph{Local admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is the following finite-row statement. In the four-organism mRNA-restricted protein-abundance panel, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family leaves substantial modeled-translation-complement protein-abundance residual energy, while residualized mRNA abundance absorbs only a small additional fraction after the declared controls. The result is a descriptive cross-layer projection boundary. It is not a translation-survival matrix $S^{QT}$, not a direct protein-omics survival matrix $S^{QP}$, and not a translation-mediated matrix $M^{QTP}$. A downstream protein-abundance interpretation would require a separately computed positive same-row translation-readout survival entry before any mediation claim is promoted.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter. mRNA abundance is used here as a controlled covariate and as a descriptive H-candidate projection surface. Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not observed translation survival, protein abundance is not a folding or function assay, and an identifier join is not biological evidence. Any assertion at one of those layers requires its own matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
