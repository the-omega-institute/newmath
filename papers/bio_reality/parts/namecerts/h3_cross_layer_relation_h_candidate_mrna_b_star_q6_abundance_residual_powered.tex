\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ mRNA-candidate abundance residual packet}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and coordinate basis.}
This record treats the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite panel indexed by organism, coding sequence, residual coordinate, modeled translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, and protein abundance.  The coordinate side has nine projected entries: $K_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{ACY}}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  Each organism is first residualized against a control block containing an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The recorded control-column count is $24$ in each organism and the residualized control rank is $23$ in each organism.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row panel.}
The mRNA-candidate projection uses four organism-level joined panels.  The row counts are $n=13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding CDS joins before the mRNA availability restriction are $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$, while the valid mRNA-by-protein counts are exactly the row counts $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$.  The valid abundance-by-protein counts are $14410$, $3747$, $19483$, and $6351$, respectively.  Thus the experiment is not a symbolic assertion about codon geometry alone: it is a finite residual projection on four explicitly joined organism panels, with mRNA availability changing the effective protein-id support in two of the four cases.

It does not establish that transcript abundance causes the residual abundance component, and it does not identify mRNA availability as the missing biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Join-stability self-check.}
The same packet compares the current all-readout join against a full-abundance CDS-join reference.  The absolute differences are $0.03219747984141397$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.00017488612263594217$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.06427590857052146$ for human, and $0.012133464712697745$ for yeast.  Using the stated $0.02$ tolerance, \emph{E. coli} and yeast match the reference join, while \emph{Danio rerio} and human fail the check.  The failed cases are not biological counterexamples; they show that mRNA availability changes the protein-id support used by the projection, so the mRNA-candidate readback must be interpreted on its actual joined panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the RNA-stability comparison.}
A separate two-organism RNA-stability mediation computation provides a useful comparison, but it remains a descriptive variance partition.  In that run the stable-id counts are $2720$ for \emph{E. coli} and $16165$ for human, with residual degrees of freedom after controls $2697$ and $16142$.  The joint $B^{*}_{Q6}$ to protein residual values are $R^{2}_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$ in \emph{E. coli} and $R^{2}_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$ in human.  After conditioning on the RNA half-life or stability readout, the values are $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$ and $0.03517257842112907$, giving mediation fractions $0.24697202727892137$ and $0.1411299863319291$.  Both reported right-tail permutation values are $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers show that an RNA-side covariate can absorb a nonzero fraction of the abundance association in specific external panels, but the computation is observational and does not by itself provide an RNA-decay pathway, an RBP mechanism, or a conserved biological law.

\paragraph{Three-matrix ordering.}
The admissible cross-layer order is strict.  A translation-readout survival matrix $S^{QT}$ must be estimated before a direct protein-readout matrix $S^{QP}$ is promoted, and any translation-mediated protein component $M^{QTP}$ must condition on the matched translation readout.  The mRNA projection above is not $S^{QT}$: it projects $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto residualized transcript abundance after the declared controls.  Likewise, the modeled tAI complement is not a measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency survival statistic.  The present finite data therefore support only a bounded statement: mRNA abundance and RNA-stability covariates have been evaluated as descriptive absorbers of already residualized abundance components on explicit organism panels.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No conclusion about translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from this packet.  A positive translation claim would require a separate same-row translation contact, such as ribosome profiling or translation efficiency, joined to the same coding sequences and recomputed under amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls.  A protein-mediated claim would additionally require that conditioning on the matched translation readout reduces the direct $Q_{i}$ to $P_{k}$ component.  Until those matrices are computed, the correct reading is a finite descriptive mRNA-candidate and RNA-stability absorption record, not a mechanism claim.
