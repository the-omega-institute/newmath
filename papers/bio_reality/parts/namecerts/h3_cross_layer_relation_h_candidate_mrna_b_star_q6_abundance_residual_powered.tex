\subsection{Translation-first handling of the $B^*_{Q6}$ mRNA candidate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-mrna-abundance-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record is a finite cross-layer panel whose rows are coding sequences joined to organism labels, $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, modeled translation weights, mRNA readouts, and protein-abundance readouts where available. The coordinate side contains nine residual directions: $K_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{ACY}}$, and $f_3$-stress. These coordinates are interpreted only after quotienting away the amino-acid sequence component and after controlling for an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $\mathrm{GC3}$, and an $M$-density baseline defined by the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in each of the four organism panels reported here.

\paragraph{Observed row counts.}
The joined mRNA-and-abundance panels are not uniform across organisms, and the row loss is part of the scientific object rather than a bookkeeping detail. The $\mathit{Danio\ rerio}$ panel has $n=13140$ rows after mRNA matching, compared with $14410$ CDS rows reported in the protein-abundance join. The $\mathit{Escherichia\ coli}$ K-12 MG1655 panel has $n=3415$ rows after mRNA matching, compared with $3739$ reported CDS rows and $3747$ valid protein-abundance rows. The $\mathit{Homo\ sapiens}$ panel has $n=11554$ rows after mRNA matching, compared with $19053$ reported CDS rows and $19483$ valid protein-abundance rows. The $\mathit{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ panel has $n=5128$ rows after mRNA matching, compared with $5815$ reported CDS rows and $6351$ valid protein-abundance rows. Thus the mRNA candidate is tested on a finite, organism-specific intersection, not on the larger abundance-only universe.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement residual.}
For each organism, the protein-abundance component aligned with the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual space was first projected away from the modeled translation-weight component. The resulting modeled-complement coefficient $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}$ is the ratio between the post-modeled-translation residual energy and the original $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-residual energy:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathit{Danio\ rerio}:\quad &\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2=919.9445878158525,\quad d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8424312412078521,\\
\mathit{E.\ coli}:\quad &\|P_Q\|^2=393.78692095732885,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2=261.0102870984463,\quad d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172,\\
\mathit{Homo\ sapiens}:\quad &\|P_Q\|^2=565.3850201235074,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2=484.31956581711796,\quad d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8566190269974241,\\
\mathit{S.\ cerevisiae}:\quad &\|P_Q\|^2=675.3275479192903,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2=151.18264752811882,\quad d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast panel is the most strongly reduced by the modeled translation-weight component, while zebrafish and human retain most of the $B^*_{Q6}$-aligned protein residual after that projection.

\paragraph{mRNA absorption test.}
The mRNA candidate is then evaluated by projecting $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA abundance on the same row set. The resulting absorption values are small in all four organism panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathit{Danio\ rerio}:\quad &\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=0.011973033879985792,\quad d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.8322280386743373,\\
\mathit{E.\ coli}:\quad &\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=0.0017706954554908886,\quad d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.6615987644523418,\\
\mathit{Homo\ sapiens}:\quad &\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=0.007858222847072218,\quad d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.8498079619223776,\\
\mathit{S.\ cerevisiae}:\quad &\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=0.0018976054721605224,\quad d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.223360891719316.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row conclusion is therefore descriptive: matched mRNA abundance explains only a small additional fraction of the modeled-translation-complement residual in this run. This does not prove that transcript abundance is irrelevant; it says only that, under this row intersection and these controls, the residualized mRNA projection is not a large absorber of the remaining $B^*_{Q6}$-aligned abundance component.

\paragraph{Join self-check.}
The mRNA join changes the protein-id universe, and the self-check records where that change is material. In zebrafish, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}$ changes from the full abundance-CDS reference value $0.874628721049266$ to $0.8424312412078521$, an absolute difference of $0.03219747984141397$, so the $0.02$ agreement check fails. In human, the value changes from $0.9208949355679455$ to $0.8566190269974241$, an absolute difference of $0.06427590857052146$, again failing the $0.02$ agreement check. In $\mathit{E.\ coli}$, the corresponding difference is $0.00017488612263594217$, and in yeast it is $0.012133464712697745$; both pass the $0.02$ check. The mRNA candidate must therefore be read as a same-row descriptive projection, with the zebrafish and human panels carrying an explicit row-selection warning.

\paragraph{Relation to mRNA stability mediation.}
A separate cross-domain stability computation gives a useful calibration for what a stronger mRNA-mediated variance partition can look like. In the $\mathit{E.\ coli}$ half-life join, $2720$ stable identifiers remain; the residual degrees of freedom after the same rank-$23$ control matrix are $2697$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. In the human half-life join, $16165$ stable identifiers remain; the residual degrees of freedom are $16142$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. Those stability numbers are not imported as causal support for the present mRNA-abundance candidate. They instead show that mRNA-layer quantities must be separated by readout type: abundance, half-life, modeled translation complement, and protein abundance are distinct matrices.

\paragraph{Three-matrix boundary.}
The admissible cross-layer ordering is $S^{QT}$ first, then $S^{QP}$, then $M^{QTP}$. A direct $B^*_{Q6}$-to-protein abundance projection is not enough to claim translation mediation, because a protein readout can retain codon-coordinate association for reasons outside observed translation efficiency. A modeled tAI or stAI coupling entry is also not the same as a controlled survival entry. The intended decomposition is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &: \text{controlled survival from residual coordinate } q_i \text{ to translation readout } T_j,\\
S^{QP}_{ik} &: \text{controlled direct association from } q_i \text{ to protein readout } P_k,\\
M^{QTP}_{ijk} &: \text{change in the } q_i\to P_k \text{ component after conditioning on } T_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
Only after a nondegenerate $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is estimated under amino-acid composition, organism, $\mathrm{GC3}$, length, $M$-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls may the protein-abundance decomposition be promoted to a translation-conditioned interpretation.

\paragraph{Local null.}
If every rank-powered $S^{QT}$ entry is nonpositive, the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates remain codon-usage residual coordinates for this purpose, and no downstream protein-readout mediation claim is licensed. The present finite row does compute modeled-complement residuals and mRNA-abundance absorption; it does not compute an organism-matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency survival matrix. Consequently, no measured translation realization, protein folding conclusion, physical admissibility conclusion, molecular function claim, mechanism claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter. Each such promotion would require its own layer-matched contact and a separately reported statistic.
