\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ mRNA-absorption boundary for abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-h-candidate-mrna-b-star-q6-abundance-residual-powered}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate set.}
This record tests whether matched mRNA abundance can account for the part of a protein-abundance residual that remains after a modeled translation-weight projection has already been removed.  The finite carrier is a panel of coding sequences joined by organism, protein identifier, codon counts, abundance readout, and matched mRNA readout.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block has nine residual coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_{AAA},\ Arg\_{AGR},\ Ile\_{AUA},\ Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3\_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$ from the same CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The reported control column count is $24$ in every organism, and the realized control rank is $23$ in every organism, so the row counts are not being consumed by a saturated control design.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
Four organism-specific panels enter the finite calculation.  The joined mRNA-readout rows are $n=13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These rows sit inside larger protein-abundance joins: $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$ reported CDS joins respectively.  The valid protein-abundance counts by protein identifier are $14410$, $3747$, $19483$, and $6351$, whereas the valid matched mRNA counts are exactly the analysis rows $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$.  Thus the mRNA test is not a universal abundance-panel statement; it is the narrower same-readout join available after requiring transcript abundance.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement residual.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the protein-abundance residual component attributed to the $B^*_{Q6}$ block after the declared controls, and let $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ denote the component left after projecting away the modeled translation-weight direction.  The finite computation reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio}:&\quad \|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=919.9445878158525,\quad d_{mod}=0.8424312412078521,\\
\mathrm{E.coli}:&\quad \|P_Q\|^2=393.78692095732885,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=261.0102870984463,\quad d_{mod}=0.6628211177352172,\\
\mathrm{Human}:&\quad \|P_Q\|^2=565.3850201235074,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=484.31956581711796,\quad d_{mod}=0.8566190269974241,\\
\mathrm{Yeast}:&\quad \|P_Q\|^2=675.3275479192903,\quad \|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=151.18264752811882,\quad d_{mod}=0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The self-check against the full abundance-CDS complement is mixed.  The absolute shifts are $0.03219747984141397$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.00017488612263594217$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.06427590857052146$ in human, and $0.012133464712697745$ in yeast.  With the stated $0.02$ tolerance, \emph{E. coli} and yeast match the complement calculation, while \emph{Danio} and human fail that tolerance.  The failed comparisons are not biological positives; they mark that the mRNA-available join changes the protein-identifier panel enough to move the modeled-complement number.

\paragraph{mRNA absorption statistic.}
The mRNA test projects $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA abundance.  The absorbed fractions are small in all four organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio}:&\quad \mathrm{absorbed\ by\ mRNA}=0.011973033879985792,\quad d_{combined}=0.8322280386743373,\\
\mathrm{E.coli}:&\quad \mathrm{absorbed\ by\ mRNA}=0.0017706954554908886,\quad d_{combined}=0.6615987644523418,\\
\mathrm{Human}:&\quad \mathrm{absorbed\ by\ mRNA}=0.007858222847072218,\quad d_{combined}=0.8498079619223776,\\
\mathrm{Yeast}:&\quad \mathrm{absorbed\ by\ mRNA}=0.0018976054721605224,\quad d_{combined}=0.223360891719316.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values say only that, inside this finite residualized panel, matched mRNA abundance explains roughly $1.20\%$, $0.18\%$, $0.79\%$, and $0.19\%$ of the modeled-complement abundance residual in the four organisms.  The result is therefore a bounded descriptive projection, not evidence that transcript abundance causes the residual, and not evidence that the remaining residual has been explained by a hidden translation process.

\paragraph{Cross-domain stability comparison.}
A related half-life mediation calculation is available for two organisms and gives a useful scale check without changing the interpretation of the mRNA-abundance projection.  In the \emph{E. coli} half-life panel, the stable-id count is $2720$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $2697$, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde M)=1$, and $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde M)=10$.  The reported values are $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In the human half-life panel, the stable-id count is $16165$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $16142$, the same ranks $23,9,1,10$ are reported, and the values are $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The joins are also concrete: the \emph{E. coli} half-life join reports $3739$ CDS joins, $2947$ half-life records, and $2720$ joined rows at growth rate $0.40\,h^{-1}$; the human consensus join reports $19053$ CDS joins, $19480$ valid consensus rows, and $16165$ joined rows, with $16150$ joins through Ensembl gene id and $15$ through the unique gene-symbol fallback.  These half-life numbers support only a statistical variance-partition comparison for mRNA stability readouts; they do not convert the mRNA-abundance absorption row into a causal pathway.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence is sufficient for the following narrow statement.  After amino-acid composition, CDS length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls, the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual can be tested against matched mRNA abundance, and the computed mRNA absorption of the modeled-complement residual is small in all four organism panels.  The record is consequently a boundary on the H-candidate interpretation: matched mRNA is a controlled covariate and a descriptive absorber, but it is not promoted to a mechanism.  No translation realization, ribosome occupancy claim, folded-structure claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, phenotype claim, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this row.  Each such assertion would require its own layer-matched measurement and a separately computed statistic, with a surviving translation-readout matrix $S^{QT}$ preceding any protein-readout or mediation interpretation.
