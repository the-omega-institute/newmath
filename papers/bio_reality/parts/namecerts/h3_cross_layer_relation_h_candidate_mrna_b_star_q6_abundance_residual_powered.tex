\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ mRNA H-candidate abundance-residual boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-h-candidate-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and coordinate panel.}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer question: after a protein-abundance residual has already been separated from the modeled translation-weight component, how much of the remaining $B^*_{Q6}$-indexed residual can be linearly absorbed by matched transcript abundance?  The finite carrier is the joined coding-sequence panel indexed by organism, by the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, by protein abundance, and by matched $\log_{10}$ mRNA abundance.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The nuisance design $Z$ has 24 declared columns: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the M-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In every reported organism the realized rank is $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, so the test is not an unconstrained row fit.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank information.}
The four organism-specific panels are large enough to define an ordinary residual projection after the controls.  The joined row counts are $n=13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The abundance-side join sizes before the mRNA restriction were 14410, 3739, 19053, and 5815 CDS records, respectively, while the mRNA-valid rows on the same protein keys were 13140, 3415, 11554, and 5128.  Thus the transcript availability filter changes the tested row set in zebrafish and human more strongly than in the bacterial and yeast panels, which is visible in the complement self-check below.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
For each organism, let $P_Q$ denote the residualized protein-abundance component associated with the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block after $Z$, and let $T_{mod}$ denote the modeled translation-weight component.  The descriptive H-candidate statistic first removes the modeled translation component and then projects the remaining protein-abundance residual onto residualized matched mRNA:
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{Q\perp T_{mod}} &= P_Q - \Pi_{T_{mod}}P_Q,\\
d_{mod} &= \frac{\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2},\\
d_{combined} &= \frac{\|P_{Q\perp (T_{mod},mRNA)}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2},\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{mRNA} &= d_{mod}-d_{combined}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed modeled-complement fractions are $d_{mod}=0.8424312412$ in zebrafish, $0.6628211177$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.8566190270$ in human, and $0.2238656604$ in yeast.  The corresponding squared energies are $(\|P_Q\|^2,\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2)=(1092.0114815505,919.9445878159)$, $(393.7869209573,261.0102870984)$, $(565.3850201235,484.3195658171)$, and $(675.3275479193,151.1826475281)$.

\paragraph{mRNA absorption readback.}
Adding the residualized mRNA covariate produces only small descriptive absorption in this finite panel.  The reported pairs $(\mathrm{absorbed}_{mRNA},d_{combined})$ are $(0.0119730339,0.8322280387)$ for zebrafish, $(0.0017706955,0.6615987645)$ for \emph{E. coli}, $(0.0078582228,0.8498079619)$ for human, and $(0.0018976055,0.2233608917)$ for yeast.  Hence the transcript covariate removes about $1.20\%$ of the original $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-residual energy in zebrafish, about $0.18\%$ in \emph{E. coli}, about $0.79\%$ in human, and about $0.19\%$ in yeast, under the stated normalization.  These are finite projection quantities, not pathway claims.

\paragraph{Complement self-check.}
The same statistic was compared against the full abundance-side complement run.  The absolute discrepancies in $d_{mod}$ are $0.0321974798$ for zebrafish, $0.0001748861$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0642759086$ for human, and $0.0121334647$ for yeast.  The $0.02$ tolerance check is passed only by \emph{E. coli} and yeast.  The failing zebrafish and human checks are interpreted locally: matched mRNA availability changes the protein-id join used by the H-candidate projection, so their mRNA-restricted panels should not be silently treated as the same empirical surface as the full abundance join.

\paragraph{Relation to RNA-stability mediation rows.}
A companion finite-row mediation calculation gives additional context without converting the present statistic into a mechanism.  In the \emph{E. coli} half-life panel, the stable joined row count is 2720, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are 2697, $R^2_{QP}=0.1163231185$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621$, and the reported mediation fraction is $0.2469720273$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  In the human half-life panel, the stable joined row count is 16165, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are 16142, $R^2_{QP}=0.0409521556$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0351725784$, and the mediation fraction is $0.1411299863$ with the same right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  These rows concern mRNA half-life or stability, not matched mRNA abundance itself; they therefore support only the narrower statement that transcript-layer covariates can be audited as variance partitions under the same nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ panel.

\paragraph{Local admissible conclusion.}
The finite statement certified here is descriptive: matched transcript abundance can be inserted as an H-candidate covariate after modeled translation-weight complement removal, and the resulting absorbed fractions are small but computable on rank-controlled organism-specific panels.  A positive or nonzero absorption value is not evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ controls mRNA production, mRNA decay, translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Translation survival would require a separately joined translation readout and a controlled $S^{QT}$ estimate; downstream protein interpretation would require a separately computed $S^{QP}$ or $M^{QTP}$ surface after that translation step.  In the absence of such layer-matched contacts, this chapter remains an abundance-residual and transcript-covariate audit only.
