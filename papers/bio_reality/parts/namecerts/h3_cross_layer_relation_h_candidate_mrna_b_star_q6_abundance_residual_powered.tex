\subsection{Rank-powered mRNA absorption boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-mrna-abundance-residual}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim scope.} This record treats the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual panel as a finite cross-layer object only after it has been quotient-separated from the encoded amino-acid sequence component. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ \mathrm{Arg}\_AGR,\ \mathrm{Ile}\_AUA,\ \mathrm{Leu}\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ \mathrm{Leu}\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&\mathrm{Ser}\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ \mathrm{Ser}\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ \mathrm{Thr}\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The biological rows are coding sequences indexed by organism, joined to protein abundance and to condition-matched mRNA abundance where the join is available. The statistic tested here is not a translation survival matrix. It is an H-candidate projection: the modeled-translation complement of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual is projected onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA after the declared control block has already been removed.

\paragraph{Control block and rank condition.} The same control design was used for all four organism panels: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the M-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors. This gives 24 nominal control columns and rank $23$ in every organism-specific joined panel. The usable mRNA-joined row counts are $n=13140$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The corresponding CDS-abundance joins before imposing the mRNA row requirement were 14410, 3739, 19053, and 5815, respectively. Thus the H-candidate computation is powered enough to avoid the small-rank pathology that occurs in three-row all-perfect residual surfaces, but it is also row-set-sensitive: the mRNA availability filter changes the protein-id universe in zebrafish and human.

\paragraph{Modeled complement before mRNA absorption.} Let $P_{Q}$ denote the protein-abundance component associated with the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates after the control block, and let $T_{mod}$ denote the modeled translation readout used for the complement calculation. The descriptive complement ratio is
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{modeled}=\frac{\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^{2}}{\|P_Q\|^{2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed finite-row values were $1092.0114815505467 \mapsto 919.9445878158525$ for zebrafish, giving $d_{modeled}=0.8424312412078521$; $393.78692095732885 \mapsto 261.0102870984463$ for \textit{E. coli}, giving $d_{modeled}=0.6628211177352172$; $565.3850201235074 \mapsto 484.31956581711796$ for human, giving $d_{modeled}=0.8566190269974241$; and $675.3275479192903 \mapsto 151.18264752811882$ for yeast, giving $d_{modeled}=0.22386566043976477$. These numbers say only how much of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual remains after the modeled translation component is removed; they do not identify a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{mRNA H-candidate absorption.} The mRNA projection then asks how much of the modeled-translation complement is absorbed by residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA on the same row set. The absorbed fractions were small in all four panels: $0.011973033879985792$ for zebrafish, $0.0017706954554908886$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.007858222847072218$ for human, and $0.0018976054721605224$ for yeast. The combined complement ratios after the mRNA projection were $0.8322280386743373$, $0.6615987644523418$, $0.8498079619223776$, and $0.223360891719316$, in the same organism order. The resulting finite-row readback is therefore a bounded negative result for transcript-level absorption: in these joined panels, mRNA abundance explains only a thin slice of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ modeled-translation complement.

\paragraph{Join self-check.} The computation records an explicit comparison against the full protein-abundance CDS join. Zebrafish changed from the reference $d_{modeled}=0.874628721049266$ on 14410 rows to $0.8424312412078521$ on 13140 all-readout rows, an absolute difference of $0.03219747984141397$, so it failed the $0.02$ join-stability check. Human likewise changed from $0.9208949355679455$ on 19053 rows to $0.8566190269974241$ on 11554 rows, an absolute difference of $0.06427590857052146$, also failing the check. \textit{E. coli} remained stable, with $0.6629960038578532$ on 3739 rows versus $0.6628211177352172$ on 3415 rows, absolute difference $0.00017488612263594217$. Yeast also remained within tolerance, with $0.21173219572706703$ on 5815 rows versus $0.22386566043976477$ on 5128 rows, absolute difference $0.012133464712697745$. The self-check is part of the claim: the mRNA-filtered readout is not interchangeable with the larger abundance-only panel in all organisms.

\paragraph{Relation to stability mediation rows.} A related half-life/stability mediation panel supplies an independent comparison point, not a causal upgrade. In the \textit{E. coli} half-life join, 2720 stable rows were obtained from 3739 CDS joins and 2947 valid half-life records, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ and growth rate $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$. Its joint nine-dimensional mediation fraction was $0.24697202727892137$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. In the human half-life join, 16165 stable rows were obtained from 19053 CDS joins and 19480 consensus records, with reported CDS hit rate $0.9779294769799313$; 16150 rows joined by Ensembl gene id and 15 by unique gene-symbol fallback. The corresponding mediation fraction was $0.1411299863319291$, with $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, and the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. These stability rows show that mRNA-related covariates can carry measurable descriptive variance in separate panels, but they do not turn the present abundance-residual absorption statistic into a mechanistic transcript pathway.

\paragraph{Local verdict.} The finite-row result is ready as a bounded cross-layer relation: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual complement can be computed on large, rank-sufficient joined panels, and matched mRNA abundance can be tested as an H-candidate covariate. The tested mRNA absorption is small in every organism here, while the row-set audit warns that zebrafish and human are sensitive to the all-readout join. The record therefore supports the following narrow statement: mRNA abundance was evaluated as a descriptive absorber of the modeled-translation complement, and it did not absorb most of that complement in any of the four panels.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.} No translation-readout survival entry $S^{QT}$ is established by this H-candidate projection. A modeled coupling matrix is not the same object as a controlled translation survival matrix, and a direct protein-abundance association is not a mediated $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ chain unless the $Q_i \to T_j$ step survives first and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct $Q_i \to P_k$ component. Nothing in this chapter establishes ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, a biochemical mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule. Each of those interpretations would require its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.
