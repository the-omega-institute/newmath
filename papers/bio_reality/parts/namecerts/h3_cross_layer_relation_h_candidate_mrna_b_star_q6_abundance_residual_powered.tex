\subsection{Rank-Powered mRNA Absorption of the $B^*_{Q6}$ Protein-Abundance Residual}
\label{subsec:bq6-mrna-abundance-residual-powered}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether matched mRNA abundance accounts for a portion of the protein-abundance residual already left after a modeled translation readout has been removed from the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate projection. The finite carrier is a panel of coding sequences joined by organism, protein identifier, codon-derived $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, modeled translation weights, protein abundance, and matched mRNA abundance. The tested coordinate block has nine columns:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, where $M$-density is the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. In every organism the control column count is $24$ and the observed control rank is $23$, so the test is not an unconstrained fit to the readout.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The joined all-readout panels are large enough to make the residual projection meaningful. The four organism rows are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}:&\quad n=13140,\quad n_{\mathrm{CDS}}=14410,\quad n_{\mathrm{protein}}=14410,\quad n_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=13140,\quad \operatorname{rank}(Z)=23,\\
\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}:&\quad n=3415,\quad n_{\mathrm{CDS}}=3739,\quad n_{\mathrm{protein}}=3747,\quad n_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=3415,\quad \operatorname{rank}(Z)=23,\\
\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}:&\quad n=11554,\quad n_{\mathrm{CDS}}=19053,\quad n_{\mathrm{protein}}=19483,\quad n_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=11554,\quad \operatorname{rank}(Z)=23,\\
\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}:&\quad n=5128,\quad n_{\mathrm{CDS}}=5815,\quad n_{\mathrm{protein}}=6351,\quad n_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=5128,\quad \operatorname{rank}(Z)=23.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mRNA join is therefore the row-limiting operation in each organism. This matters because the test is not asking whether the original protein-abundance join alone contains a signal; it asks whether the all-readout join still leaves a $B^*_{Q6}$-indexed protein residual after the modeled translation component and matched transcript abundance are both accounted for.

\paragraph{Modeled translation complement.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the protein-abundance projection on the residualized nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block, and let $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ denote the part left after removing the modeled translation-weight component. The measured complement fractions are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}:&\quad \lVert P_Q\rVert^2=1092.0114815505467,\quad \lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2=919.9445878158525,\quad d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8424312412078521,\\
\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}:&\quad \lVert P_Q\rVert^2=393.78692095732885,\quad \lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2=261.0102870984463,\quad d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172,\\
\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}:&\quad \lVert P_Q\rVert^2=565.3850201235074,\quad \lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2=484.31956581711796,\quad d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.8566190269974241,\\
\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}:&\quad \lVert P_Q\rVert^2=675.3275479192903,\quad \lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2=151.18264752811882,\quad d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding self-check against the full-abundance complement run is mixed. The absolute difference is $0.03219747984141397$ in zebrafish and $0.06427590857052146$ in human, both outside the $0.02$ tolerance; it is $0.00017488612263594217$ in $E.\ coli$ and $0.012133464712697745$ in yeast, both inside the tolerance. Thus the all-readout join is stable in the microbial rows and not stable under the same tolerance in the zebrafish and human rows. The failed rows are not discarded; they identify the protein-identifier join as a finite source of variation when mRNA availability changes the row set.

\paragraph{mRNA absorption statistic.}
The H-candidate test projects $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA and records how much of the modeled-translation complement is absorbed. The computed absorption is small in all four organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}:&\quad a_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=0.011973033879985792,\quad d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.8322280386743373,\\
\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}:&\quad a_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=0.0017706954554908886,\quad d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.6615987644523418,\\
\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}:&\quad a_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=0.007858222847072218,\quad d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.8498079619223776,\\
\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}:&\quad a_{\mathrm{mRNA}}=0.0018976054721605224,\quad d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.223360891719316.
\end{aligned}
$$
On these rows, matched transcript abundance is therefore a bounded descriptive absorber rather than an explanation of the remaining protein-abundance residual. The largest absorption is the zebrafish value $0.011973033879985792$; the other three are below $0.008$. The residual left after both modeled translation and mRNA remains $0.8322280386743373$ in zebrafish, $0.6615987644523418$ in $E.\ coli$, $0.8498079619223776$ in human, and $0.223360891719316$ in yeast. The numerical outcome supports the narrow statement that the projection was computed and that mRNA absorption is bounded on the joined finite rows. It does not select an mRNA mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to RNA-stability mediation rows.}
A separate two-organism RNA-stability decomposition gives a useful comparison without changing the claim type. In the $E.\ coli$ RNA half-life join, $2720$ stable identifiers are retained, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, $2947$ valid half-life records by gene id, $3739$ reported joined CDS rows, residual degrees of freedom $2697$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$. The variance partition gives $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. In the human RNA half-life join, $16165$ stable identifiers are retained, the reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$, $19480$ valid consensus half-life records by Ensembl gene id are available, $19053$ CDS rows are reported joined, residual degrees of freedom are $16142$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$. The corresponding values are $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. These rows show that RNA-stability mediation can be measured as a finite variance partition, but they remain observational and dataset-specific.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite result is a descriptive cross-layer relation, not a promoted biological mechanism. The admissible conclusion is that a nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual protein-abundance component can be residualized against modeled translation and then tested against matched mRNA abundance on four organism-specific finite panels, and that the resulting mRNA absorption is small on the joined rows. The RNA-stability comparison shows nonzero finite mediation fractions in $E.\ coli$ and human, but those fractions are variance partitions, not causal paths. No translation-realization claim follows from this chapter unless a same-CDS measured translation readout supplies a positive controlled $S^{QT}$ entry. No protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from these numbers without a separate layer-matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.
