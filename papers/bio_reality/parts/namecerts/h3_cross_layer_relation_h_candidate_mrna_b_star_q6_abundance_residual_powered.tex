\subsection{Rank-Powered mRNA Absorption Boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Abundance Residuals}\label{subsec:h3-cross-layer-h-candidate-mrna-b-star-q6-abundance-residual-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats $B^{*}_{Q6}$ as a finite family of nine synonymous residual coordinates evaluated against abundance and transcript readouts after quotienting away the amino-acid sequence component.  The tested coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_{3}\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control design used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In every organism the control table had $24$ columns and residual control rank $23$, so the row counts, not the formal rank of the covariate table, are the limiting finite evidence.

\paragraph{Joined rows and modeled complement.}
The mRNA candidate was tested by projecting the modeled-translation complement of the protein-abundance residual, $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$, onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA.  The joined row counts were $13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K--12 MG1655, $11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding full abundance CDS joins before the mRNA availability filter were $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$ rows.  Thus the human and zebrafish rows undergo a substantial mRNA-availability contraction, while the bacterial and yeast rows remain closer to their abundance joins.

The modeled complement fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{mod}(\mathrm{zebrafish})&=0.8424312412078521,\\
 d_{mod}(\mathrm{E.coli})&=0.6628211177352172,\\
 d_{mod}(\mathrm{human})&=0.8566190269974241,\\
 d_{mod}(\mathrm{yeast})&=0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
They came from squared norms $(\|P_Q\|^2,\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2)$ equal to $(1092.0114815505467,919.9445878158525)$, $(393.78692095732885,261.0102870984463)$, $(565.3850201235074,484.31956581711796)$, and $(675.3275479192903,151.18264752811882)$ for zebrafish, \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast respectively.  Against the complement-reference abundance join, the modeled-complement self-check passed within $0.02$ only in \emph{E. coli} and yeast: the absolute differences were $0.03219747984141397$, $0.00017488612263594217$, $0.06427590857052146$, and $0.012133464712697745$ in the same organism order.  This makes the mRNA availability filter part of the observed finite object rather than a harmless bookkeeping choice.

\paragraph{mRNA absorption readback.}
After residualizing against the stated controls, mRNA absorbed only a small part of the modeled-translation complement:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 a_{mRNA}(\mathrm{zebrafish})&=0.011973033879985792, & d_{combined}&=0.8322280386743373,\\
 a_{mRNA}(\mathrm{E.coli})&=0.0017706954554908886, & d_{combined}&=0.6615987644523418,\\
 a_{mRNA}(\mathrm{human})&=0.007858222847072218, & d_{combined}&=0.8498079619223776,\\
 a_{mRNA}(\mathrm{yeast})&=0.0018976054721605224, & d_{combined}&=0.223360891719316.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite readout therefore says that transcript abundance, in this panel, is a bounded descriptive covariate for the remaining abundance residual rather than a discovered causal explanation.  The largest observed absorption is the zebrafish value $0.011973033879985792$, and the other three are below $0.008$.  Conversely, low absorption does not prove that the residual has been explained by translation; it says only that the particular residualized mRNA column in this condition does not remove much of the modeled complement.

\paragraph{Cross-domain half-life comparison.}
A related two-organism half-life mediation computation supplies a useful guardrail.  In \emph{E. coli}, the three-way panel joined $2720$ stable identifiers, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ and $2947$ valid half-life records.  In human, the panel joined $16165$ stable identifiers, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and $19480$ valid consensus records; $16150$ joins used Ensembl gene identifiers and $15$ used the unique gene-symbol fallback.  The joint rank diagnostics used $9$ $Q$ coordinates, $24$ control columns, rank $Z=23$, rank $\widetilde X=9$, rank $\widetilde M=1$, and rank $\widetilde{XM}=10$ in both organisms.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $2697$ in \emph{E. coli} and $16142$ in human.

The half-life mediation fractions were $0.24697202727892137$ in \emph{E. coli} and $0.1411299863319291$ in human, with $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$ in \emph{E. coli}, and $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$ and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$ in human.  Both right-tail permutation values were $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers show that a transcript-stability covariate can partition a portion of the abundance association in a separate finite experiment, but the interpretation remains variance decomposition.  It is not a proof that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ causes RNA decay, protein abundance, or any downstream molecular state.

\paragraph{Ordered cross-layer rule.}
The admissible order is translation first, protein second, mediation third.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ abundance association may be recorded as a protein-readout statistic only after the corresponding translation-readout survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ has been computed under amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls.  A modeled coupling entry $A^{QT}_{ij}$ is not the same object as $S^{QT}_{ij}$, and an all-$1.0$ surface on a rank-saturated $n=3$ design is not a powered survival result.  If a later same-row panel contains $Q_i$, measured or explicitly bounded translation readout $T_j$, protein abundance $P_k$, and transcript control, then the relevant decomposition is $S^{QT}$ first, direct $S^{QP}$ second, and the translation-mediated component $M^{QTP}$ only after conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct $Q_i\to P_k$ component.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The present row set supports a narrow statement: residualized mRNA abundance and, in a companion two-organism panel, RNA half-life can be used as finite covariates for partitioning $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residuals.  It does not by itself establish translation survival, ribosome mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Each of those stronger biological readings requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  Until such a contact is attached, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ remains a synonymous residual coordinate system with bounded descriptive projections, not a promoted mechanism.
