\subsection{Rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mRNA absorption boundary for protein abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-mrna-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate scope.}
This record concerns a finite panel of coding sequences in which each row carries an organism label, a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, a protein-abundance readout, a matched mRNA abundance covariate, and the standard control block used to separate codon-composition geometry from lower-order sequence effects. The nine residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_{AAA},\quad Arg\_{AGR},\quad Ile\_{AUA},\quad Leu\_{CUN\_vs\_UUR},\quad Leu\_{UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\_vs\_AGY},\quad Ser\_{UCA\_vs\_UCG},\quad Thr\_{ACR\_vs\_ACY},\quad f3\_{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The covariate block $Z$ consists of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. Thus each organism is tested after quotienting away the amino-acid sequence component and after retaining the synonymous residual coordinate as a separate row variable.

\paragraph{Observed row support.}
The computed abundance--mRNA panel is nondegenerate at the row level. For \emph{Danio rerio}, the all-readout join has $n=13140$ rows, while the protein-abundance CDS join reports $14410$ rows and the valid mRNA-by-protein count is $13140$. For \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the corresponding row count is $n=3415$, with $3739$ reported CDS joins, $3747$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $3415$ valid mRNA rows by protein. For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the row count is $n=11554$, with $19053$ reported CDS joins, $19483$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $11554$ valid mRNA rows by protein. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the row count is $n=5128$, with $5815$ reported CDS joins, $6351$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $5128$ valid mRNA rows by protein. In all four organisms the control design has $24$ columns and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, so the reported quantities are not three-row saturated fits or rank-trivial all-one residual surfaces.

\paragraph{Modeled translation complement and mRNA absorption.}
Let $P\_{Q}$ denote the protein-abundance residual component associated with the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate block after the declared controls, and let $P\_{Q\perp T\_{mod}}$ denote the residual remaining after projection against the modeled translation readout. The computed descriptive complement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
D\_{mod} &= \frac{\lVert P\_{Q\perp T\_{mod}}\rVert^{2}}{\lVert P\_{Q}\rVert^{2}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite estimates are organism-specific: \emph{Danio rerio} has $\lVert P\_{Q}\rVert^{2}=1092.0114815505467$, $\lVert P\_{Q\perp T\_{mod}}\rVert^{2}=919.9445878158525$, and $D\_{mod}=0.8424312412078521$; \emph{E. coli} has $393.78692095732885$, $261.0102870984463$, and $0.6628211177352172$; human has $565.3850201235074$, $484.31956581711796$, and $0.8566190269974241$; yeast has $675.3275479192903$, $151.18264752811882$, and $0.22386566043976477$. The mRNA H-candidate test then projects this modeled-translation complement onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA. The absorbed fractions are small in all four organisms: $0.011973033879985792$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.0017706954554908886$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.007858222847072218$ for human, and $0.0018976054721605224$ for yeast. The corresponding combined residual fractions are $0.8322280386743373$, $0.6615987644523418$, $0.8498079619223776$, and $0.223360891719316$, respectively.

\paragraph{Self-check against the full abundance join.}
The modeled complement was also compared with the abundance-only reference join. Two organisms pass the $0.02$ tolerance check and two do not. \emph{E. coli} differs by only $0.00017488612263594217$, comparing $0.6629960038578532$ on the $3739$-row abundance CDS join with $0.6628211177352172$ on the $3415$-row all-readout join. Yeast differs by $0.012133464712697745$, comparing $0.21173219572706703$ on $5815$ rows with $0.22386566043976477$ on $5128$ rows. \emph{Danio rerio} differs by $0.03219747984141397$, comparing $0.874628721049266$ on $14410$ rows with $0.8424312412078521$ on $13140$ rows, and human differs by $0.06427590857052146$, comparing $0.9208949355679455$ on $19053$ rows with $0.8566190269974241$ on $11554$ rows. These failures are not positive biological effects; they say that mRNA availability changes the protein-identifier join used by the H-candidate projection.

\paragraph{Cross-domain RNA-stability comparison.}
A separate finite mediation check uses mRNA half-life or stability as the mediator rather than matched abundance. In \emph{E. coli}, the three-way join contains $2720$ stable identifiers, with $3739$ reported CDS joins, $2947$ half-life records, and a CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$ at growth rate $0.40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$. The rank diagnostics are $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$, residual degrees of freedom $2697$, and residual energies $\lVert M\rVert^{2}=215.43086017169205$ and $\lVert P\rVert^{2}=2551.728810042108$. The scalar decomposition gives $R^{2}_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. In human, the three-way join contains $16165$ stable identifiers, with $19053$ reported CDS joins, $19480$ consensus half-life records, and CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$; $16150$ rows join by Ensembl gene id and $15$ by unique gene-symbol fallback. The rank diagnostics are $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$, residual degrees of freedom $16142$, and residual energies $\lVert M\rVert^{2}=1422.0291144063049$ and $\lVert P\rVert^{2}=22233.302987773684$. The scalar decomposition gives $R^{2}_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Ordered readback boundary.}
The admissible interpretation is therefore a three-surface order, not a direct promotion from synonymous geometry to protein biology. A translation-readout surface $S^{QT}$ must first be estimated under amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls. Only after such a nondegenerate $Q_i\to T_j$ survival entry exists may a direct protein surface $S^{QP}$ or a mediated surface $M^{QTP}$ be interpreted as a downstream protein-abundance readout. The present H-candidate computation does not supply that first measured translation survival surface. It supplies a bounded descriptive projection: residualized mRNA abundance absorbs only a small part of the modeled-translation complement, while the row-join self-check exposes organism-specific identifier and availability effects.

\paragraph{Local negative boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal pathway, or universal biological rule follows from these numbers. Matched mRNA abundance is a control and an H-candidate covariate, not a translation readout. Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not the same object as controlled translation survival. Protein abundance is a downstream measurement class, not folding, function, or mechanism. The only supported statement is the finite descriptive one: on the declared rows and controls, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual complement after modeled translation has the reported organism-specific residual fractions, and residualized mRNA explains the listed additional fractions without causal promotion.
