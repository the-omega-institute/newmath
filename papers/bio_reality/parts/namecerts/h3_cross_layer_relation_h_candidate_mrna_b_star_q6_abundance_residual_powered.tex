\subsection{B-star-Q6 mRNA absorption of the modeled translation complement}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-mrna-absorption-modeled-translation-complement}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests a narrow cross-layer question for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis.  The row carrier is a finite panel of coding sequences with organism labels, nine residual coordinates, protein-abundance rows, and matched mRNA rows where the identifier join is available.  The nine coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The covariate block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In all four organisms the recorded control-column count is $24$ and the rank of $Z$ is $23$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The actual all-readout joins are not the same as the full protein-abundance joins.  For \textit{Danio rerio}, the mRNA-matched panel has $n=13140$ rows, while the reported abundance CDS join has $14410$ rows and the valid protein-abundance count is $14410$.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the corresponding counts are $n=3415$, $3739$, and $3747$.  For \textit{Homo sapiens}, they are $n=11554$, $19053$, and $19483$.  For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, they are $n=5128$, $5815$, and $6351$.  Thus the computation is a finite matched-row projection, not a claim about every coding sequence in an organism and not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Modeled translation complement.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$-explained protein-abundance residual after the shared controls, and let $T_{mod}$ denote the modeled translation readout used in the complement calculation.  The descriptive complement coefficient is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{mod} &= \frac{\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row values are organism-specific.  For \textit{D. rerio}, $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$, $\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2=919.9445878158525$, and $d_{mod}=0.8424312412078521$.  For \textit{E. coli}, the corresponding values are $393.78692095732885$, $261.0102870984463$, and $0.6628211177352172$.  For human, they are $565.3850201235074$, $484.31956581711796$, and $0.8566190269974241$.  For yeast, they are $675.3275479192903$, $151.18264752811882$, and $0.22386566043976477$.

\paragraph{mRNA absorption.}
The mRNA test is not a translation-survival estimate.  It asks how much of the modeled-translation complement abundance residual is linearly absorbed by residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA on the matched rows.  The recorded absorption values are small in all four organisms: $0.011973033879985792$ in \textit{D. rerio}, $0.0017706954554908886$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.007858222847072218$ in human, and $0.0018976054721605224$ in yeast.  The combined residual coefficients are $d_{combined}=0.8322280386743373$, $0.6615987644523418$, $0.8498079619223776$, and $0.223360891719316$, respectively.  These numbers say that the mRNA covariate was computed and bounded as an $H$-candidate projection; they do not by themselves identify a causal transcript mechanism.

\paragraph{Join sensitivity.}
The self-check against the full-abundance complement is partly stable and partly row-sensitive.  In \textit{D. rerio}, the full-abundance reference gives $d_{mod}=0.874628721049266$ on $14410$ joined CDS rows, whereas the current all-readout join gives $0.8424312412078521$ on $13140$ rows; the absolute difference is $0.03219747984141397$, which fails the $0.02$ matching band.  In \textit{E. coli}, $0.6629960038578532$ on $3739$ rows and $0.6628211177352172$ on $3415$ rows differ by only $0.00017488612263594217$, so the check passes.  In human, $0.9208949355679455$ on $19053$ rows and $0.8566190269974241$ on $11554$ rows differ by $0.06427590857052146$, so the check fails.  In yeast, $0.21173219572706703$ on $5815$ rows and $0.22386566043976477$ on $5128$ rows differ by $0.012133464712697745$, so the check passes.  The failed cases are interpreted as negative self-checks: mRNA availability changes the protein-identifier join used by the projection.

\paragraph{Relation to mRNA-stability mediation.}
A separate finite-row stability decomposition records the same nine-coordinate source and the same style of controls for two organisms.  In the \textit{E. coli} stability join, $2720$ stable identifiers remain, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde M)=1$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $2697$.  The reported values are $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In the human stability join, $16165$ stable identifiers remain, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde M)=1$, and the residual degrees of freedom are $16142$; the corresponding values are $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These stability numbers are useful as a neighboring decomposition, but they remain variance partitions from observational datasets rather than pathway proofs.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the descriptive statement that residualized mRNA can be inserted as an $H$-candidate projection on the modeled-translation complement abundance residual, with concrete joined-row counts, ranks, complement coefficients, absorption coefficients, and row-sensitivity diagnostics.  It does not support promotion to a measured translation claim.  A positive $S^{QT}$ entry still requires an organism-matched translation readout, such as ribosome profiling or translation efficiency, estimated before downstream protein-omics interpretation and controlled by amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA where appropriate.  No conclusion about translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and statistic.
