\subsection{B\textsuperscript{*}\_Q6 Abundance Residuals and the mRNA H-Candidate Boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-mrna-abundance-residual}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and coordinate support.}
This record concerns a finite cross-layer panel in which coding sequences are indexed by organism, by a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, by a modeled translation readout, by protein abundance, and by a matched mRNA abundance covariate.  The residual coordinates are not amino-acid identities: they are the nine amino-acid-quotient-surviving coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control design uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the same codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus each organism has $24$ declared control columns and observed control rank $23$, so the test is not a raw codon-frequency correlation but a residual projection after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density have been accounted for.

\paragraph{Row counts and rank adequacy.}
The four organism panels are large enough for residual projection rather than rank-saturated interpolation.  The joined mRNA-and-abundance row counts are $n=13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding CDS joins before the mRNA restriction are $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$, respectively.  Protein-abundance-valid counts are $14410$, $3747$, $19483$, and $6351$, while the mRNA-valid counts used in the actual projection are $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$.  The rank statement is therefore local and finite: each organism contributes a high-row residual system with the same $9$-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block and the same rank-$23$ control block.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement residual.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the protein-abundance residual component associated with the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate block after the declared controls, and let $T_{\mathrm{mod}}$ denote the modeled tAI or stAI translation readout block.  The tested complement is the part of $P_Q$ left after removing the modeled-translation component, written here as $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$.  The finite values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 1092.0114815505467, & \|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 919.9445878158525, & d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
\|P_Q\|^2_{\mathrm{Ecoli}} &= 393.78692095732885, & \|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2_{\mathrm{Ecoli}} &= 261.0102870984463, & d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
\|P_Q\|^2_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 565.3850201235074, & \|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 484.31956581711796, & d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
\|P_Q\|^2_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 675.3275479192903, & \|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 151.18264752811882, & d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers show that the modeled-translation complement is organism-dependent: the complement fraction is high in fish, human, and \emph{E. coli}, but much lower in yeast.  This is a descriptive residual decomposition, not a molecular explanation.

\paragraph{mRNA H-candidate projection.}
The mRNA H-candidate is tested by projecting $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA abundance in the same joined rows.  The resulting absorption values are small in all four organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{mRNA}}(\mathrm{Danio}) &= 0.011973033879985792, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8322280386743373,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{mRNA}}(\mathrm{Ecoli}) &= 0.0017706954554908886, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.6615987644523418,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{mRNA}}(\mathrm{Human}) &= 0.007858222847072218, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8498079619223776,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{mRNA}}(\mathrm{Yeast}) &= 0.0018976054721605224, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.223360891719316.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readback is therefore negative for a strong transcript-abundance explanation of the remaining modeled-translation complement: residualized mRNA abundance removes about $1.20\%$ of the complement in fish, $0.18\%$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.79\%$ in human, and $0.19\%$ in yeast.  The result is still useful because it bounds one plausible H-candidate rather than leaving the residual protein-abundance component uninterpreted.

\paragraph{Join self-check.}
The mRNA restriction changes the row set, so the modeled-complement value must be compared with the full abundance-only complement computation.  The absolute differences are $0.03219747984141397$ for fish, $0.00017488612263594217$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.06427590857052146$ for human, and $0.012133464712697745$ for yeast.  With a $0.02$ tolerance, \emph{E. coli} and yeast match the abundance-only complement check, while fish and human do not.  The failed matches are not biological effects; they record that mRNA availability changes the protein-identifier join for those organisms.  This row-set sensitivity is part of the finite readout boundary and prevents a single pooled interpretation across all four organisms.

\paragraph{Relation to mRNA stability.}
A separate stability-mediated computation was available for two organisms and is kept distinct from the mRNA-abundance H-candidate.  In \emph{E. coli}, the three-way half-life join has $2720$ stable identifiers, a CDS join hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, residual degrees of freedom $2697$, $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, and mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In human, the three-way join has $16165$ stable identifiers, a CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$, residual degrees of freedom $16142$, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, and mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$ with the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These stability rows support a bounded variance-partition statement for mRNA half-life or stability, not a causal transcript mechanism and not an explanation of the mRNA-abundance projection above.

\paragraph{Ordered readout rule.}
The admissible order is $S^{QT}$ first, then $S^{QP}$, then $M^{QTP}$.  A modeled coupling matrix $A^{QT}$ is not the same object as a controlled translation-survival matrix $S^{QT}$, and a direct $Q_i$ to protein-abundance association cannot be promoted to a translation-mediated claim unless a matched translation readout survives first.  In symbols, the intended interpretation is only the ordered finite decomposition
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i &\longrightarrow T_j &&\text{tested by controlled } S^{QT}_{ij},\\
Q_i &\longrightarrow P_k &&\text{reported as controlled } S^{QP}_{ik},\\
Q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k &&&\text{reported only when } M^{QTP}_{ijk} \text{ reduces the direct component.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The present H-candidate result does not by itself establish a positive $S^{QT}$ entry.  It says that, after the modeled translation component has been removed from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual, matched mRNA abundance accounts for only a small additional fraction in the four observed organism panels.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No claim about translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality follows from these projections alone.  Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer covariate; modeled tAI or stAI is a bounded translation-weight readout; protein abundance is a downstream steady-state measurement.  Each higher layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The finite conclusion is therefore narrow: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual survives the mRNA-abundance H-candidate audit as a measured residual decomposition, while causal language and higher-layer biological interpretation remain blocked until separate layer-matched evidence is supplied.
