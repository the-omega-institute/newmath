\subsection{B-star-Q6 mRNA absorption as a bounded cross-layer readout}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-mrna-absorption-cross-layer}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record fixes a finite coding-sequence panel in which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are evaluated against protein abundance after a modeled translation-weight component has already been removed.  The coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control design uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from the same codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In every organism the reported control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, so the row is not read as an unconstrained residual fit.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
The four organism joins are not interchangeable, because mRNA availability changes the protein-id support.  For \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$ rows enter the all-readout join, with $14410$ CDS-protein abundance rows available and $13140$ valid mRNA rows.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=3415$, with $3739$ reported joined CDS rows, $3747$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $3415$ valid mRNA rows.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=11554$, with $19053$ reported joined CDS rows, $19483$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $11554$ valid mRNA rows.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=5128$, with $5815$ reported joined CDS rows, $6351$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $5128$ valid mRNA rows.  These row counts define the actual finite carrier of the calculation; no result below is asserted outside these joins.

\paragraph{Modeled-translation complement.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the protein-abundance component associated with the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block after the declared controls, and let $P_{Q\perp Tmod}$ denote its residual after the modeled translation-weight component.  The computed complement fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 0.8424312412078521,\\
 d_{\mathrm{Ecoli}} &= 0.6628211177352172,\\
 d_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 0.8566190269974241,\\
 d_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 0.22386566043976477.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding squared energies are $\|P_Q\|^2=1092.0114815505467$ and $\|P_{Q\perp Tmod}\|^2=919.9445878158525$ in \emph{Danio rerio}; $393.78692095732885$ and $261.0102870984463$ in \emph{E. coli}; $565.3850201235074$ and $484.31956581711796$ in human; and $675.3275479192903$ and $151.18264752811882$ in yeast.  The comparison against the full-abundance CDS reference join passes the $0.02$ tolerance in \emph{E. coli}, where the difference is $0.00017488612263594217$, and in yeast, where the difference is $0.012133464712697745$.  It does not pass in zebrafish, where the difference is $0.03219747984141397$, or in human, where it is $0.06427590857052146$; those failures are support-shape warnings, not biological counterexamples.

\paragraph{mRNA absorption.}
The H-candidate test projects $P_{Q\perp Tmod}$ onto residualized $\log_{10}$ mRNA within the same joined rows.  The resulting mRNA absorption is small in all four organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 a_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 0.011973033879985792, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8322280386743373,\\
 a_{\mathrm{Ecoli}} &= 0.0017706954554908886, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.6615987644523418,\\
 a_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 0.007858222847072218, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8498079619223776,\\
 a_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 0.0018976054721605224, & d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.223360891719316.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus this finite row does not promote mRNA abundance to a causal explanation of the residual protein-abundance component.  It says only that, after the modeled translation-weight complement is formed and the declared controls are applied, residualized mRNA absorbs about $1.20\%$ of the complement in zebrafish, $0.18\%$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.79\%$ in human, and $0.19\%$ in yeast.  Low absorption is also not evidence that the residual is a discovered translation mechanism; it may point to turnover, localization, condition mismatch, measurement noise, or an unmeasured layer.

\paragraph{RNA-stability comparison.}
A separate cross-domain half-life calculation supplies a useful guardrail.  In the \emph{E. coli} half-life join, $2720$ stable ids remain, the CDS join hit rate is $0.9978649586335735$, the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $2697$, $\mathrm{rank}(X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(M)=1$, and $\mathrm{rank}(XM)=10$.  The variance partition reports $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.0875945621412319$, mediation fraction $0.24697202727892137$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In the human half-life join, $16165$ stable ids remain, the CDS join hit rate is $0.9779294769799313$, the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $16142$, $\mathrm{rank}(X)=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(M)=1$, and $\mathrm{rank}(XM)=10$.  The corresponding values are $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.03517257842112907$, mediation fraction $0.1411299863319291$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These rows show that an RNA-stability readout can absorb a measurable part of the abundance association in organism-matched finite panels, but they remain observational variance decompositions.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter claim is therefore a bounded cross-layer relation: $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates may be carried into an abundance-readout discussion only through explicit joined rows, declared controls, rank diagnostics, and a separated readout equation.  The present mRNA-abundance row is computed and admissible as a descriptive H-candidate projection, with strongest numerical message that residualized mRNA abundance absorbs little of the modeled-translation complement in the four reported organisms.  No translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal pathway, or universal biological rule follows from this row.  Such a promotion would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as measured translation efficiency for $S^{QT}$, matched protein readout for $S^{QP}$, or an explicitly conditioned mediation surface for $M^{QTP}$.
