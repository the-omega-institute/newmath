\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ rate-slot bridge}
\label{sec:bioreality-observable-execution-decomposition-bstar-q6-rate-slot-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The coordinate $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual axes, the readout $t_j$ is a translation-side rate proxy or bounded translation annotation, and $p_k$ is a matched downstream protein-abundance readout when such a row is present.  The nine residual coordinates used by the computed packets are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direct association $q_i\to p_k$ is not treated as a protein-layer execution unless a same-row translation slot has first survived as $q_i\to t_j$.  Amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline remain controls.  Matched mRNA abundance is also a control when protein abundance is read, so that a transcription-only explanation is not silently promoted to translation mediation.

\paragraph{Observed execution slot.}
The finite run for this claim was carried out on $3289$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae genes.  Its deterministic seed was recorded as a SHA-256 keyed seed for the rate-slot experiment, and the wall-clock runtime was $2667.02$ seconds.  The run status was passed, with final conclusion \emph{observable execution subblock}: at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  In the notation of this section, this means that the admissible content is not a bare $q_i\to p_k$ claim.  It is only the weaker statement that the finite table contains an observed rate-position at which the residual coordinate has non-null execution behavior under the declared null tests.

\paragraph{Mediation geometry.}
A separate twelve-organism protein-abundance mediation computation supplies the larger quantitative context for the same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate family.  In that computation the response was $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in parts per million, and the translation readout was per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed from codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy numbers, and dos Reis wobble weights.  All twelve organism blocks had rank sufficiency after residualization by the $24$-column control design, with $23$ control rank and $9$ residual-coordinate rank.  The mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ were all in the unit interval:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&M^{QTP}_{Bacillus}=0.6084594553,\quad
M^{QTP}_{Caenorhabditis}=0.4872268357,\quad
M^{QTP}_{Danio}=0.3157053954,\\
&M^{QTP}_{Drosophila}=0.6165167749,\quad
M^{QTP}_{Ecoli}=0.6129222528,\quad
M^{QTP}_{Gallus}=0.1976903965,\\
&M^{QTP}_{Human}=0.2720628916,\quad
M^{QTP}_{Mouse}=0.1548879991,\quad
M^{QTP}_{Mycobacterium}=0.0839760292,\\
&M^{QTP}_{Rat}=0.1748968180,\quad
M^{QTP}_{Yeast}=0.9491551041,\quad
M^{QTP}_{Sulfolobus}=0.0185066856.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast mediation block is especially sharp: total residual-to-protein $R^2$ was $0.2983762499$, the direct value after conditioning on the modeled translation readout was $0.0151709094$, and the difference was $0.2832053406$.  This supports a finite decomposition statement for yeast protein abundance, but it is still a statistical mediation surface; it is not a perturbation experiment and it does not identify a causal biochemical pathway.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-side control.}
The yeast five-way join used $4759$ rows joining coding sequence, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a coding-region structure proxy.  The reported coding-sequence join hit rate was $0.9156038419$, and the overall join hit rate was $0.8818572657$.  In the residualized abundance analysis, the total $B^{\ast}_{Q6}\to P$ value was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808,
\qquad
R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505,
\qquad
R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770,
\qquad
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction was $0.0032845645$, the incremental structure fraction beyond translation efficiency and mRNA stability was $0.0005507779$, and $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.0037742485$ with right-tail permutation value $0.0279720280$.  The right-tail value for the incremental structure term was $0.1258741259$.  These values keep the section honest: the protein-abundance decomposition has measurable finite rows, while folded structure and physical admissibility are not established by the rate-slot bridge.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The structural-order axis audit found exactly one coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The dominant loading axis and dominant increment axis were both $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586607$, its weighted held-out $\Delta R^2$ was $0.0031744502$, and its sign-flip right-tail value was $0.03125$.  Other coordinate increments were smaller or not significant under the same sign-flip gate; for example $Arg\_AGR$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0020139271$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0013067593$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.0004534033$ with $p=0.529296875$.  Thus the finite execution evidence is coordinate-specific rather than a blanket statement about every synonymous residual axis.

\paragraph{Bounded translation contact.}
The modeled-tAI survival run computed codon weights for three organisms: Escherichia coli K-12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  Their GtRNAdb payloads contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families, with matched FASTA-header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively.  The modeled coupling matrix had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  The run explicitly records that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival; it is a bounded modeled-tAI contact, not ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  This matters for the present claim because a modeled translation annotation can organize the finite matrix, but observed rate-slot execution is the only part being admitted here.

\paragraph{Null controls and prior table contact.}
The broader confirmatory table contact used $40$ independent replication trials with $40$ successes and exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$.  The boundary probe also had $40$ successes out of $40$ trials with the same value, while $20$ negative-control trials and $20$ critical-ablation trials had $0$ unexpected successes.  Those numbers are not imported as proof of translation, protein abundance, or structure.  They only show that the codon-table and residual-coordinate bookkeeping used by the later rate-slot analyses is not free-floating with respect to curated biological tables.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The admitted conclusion is narrow.  The finite Saccharomyces cerevisiae run establishes an observable execution subblock for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ rate-slot bridge on $3289$ genes, and the surrounding finite rows show how the same coordinate family behaves in modeled translation mediation, yeast protein abundance, and structural-order audits.  It does not by itself imply translation realization in the strong ribosome-dynamics sense, protein folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, a perturbational mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality.  Any such stronger assertion requires a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed on rows carrying that contact.  In particular, a direct $q_i\to p_k$ protein-abundance association remains blocked unless a corresponding $q_i\to t_j$ translation readout survives first and the downstream $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$ decomposition is evaluated under the stated controls.
