\subsection{Observable Execution Decomposition of the $B^{\star}_{Q6}$ Rate-Slot Bridge}\label{sec:bior-observable-execution-decomposition-bstar-q6-rate-slot}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record studied here is a finite cross-layer table whose rows are coding-sequence observations and whose columns separate a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a rate-like translation or execution readout $t_j$, and a downstream protein or abundance readout $p_k$.  The primary powered run was executed for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $3289$ genes and the deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  Its registered outcome was
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 3289,\\
\mathrm{status} &= \mathrm{passed},\\
\mathrm{conclusion} &= \mathrm{observable\_execution\_subblock},\\
\mathrm{verdict} &= \text{at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a positive finite-row result for an observable execution subblock.  It is not, by itself, a claim that the synonymous residual coordinate has become a complete translation mechanism, a folding determinant, a physical-admissibility certificate, a molecular-function explanation, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Coordinate basis and admissible readback.}
The residual side of the table uses the nine computed $B^{\star}_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not admitted as protein realization unless the same finite panel first contains a surviving $q_i\to t_j$ readout.  The controls remain controls: amino-acid composition, organism identity, $\mathrm{GC3}$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline are not promoted into additional biological coordinates.  Matched mRNA abundance is likewise a transcription-layer control used to prevent a rate-slot result from being explained as ordinary transcript abundance.

\paragraph{Observed rate-slot result.}
The powered rate-slot bridge run reports a passed computation and the final class $\mathrm{observable\_execution\_subblock}$.  The phrase means that at least one observed rate proxy survived both effective null families in the finite yeast panel.  The experiment took $2667.02$ seconds and is tied to run id $365\mathrm{aa}14\mathrm{cdedc}$.  The interpretation is deliberately narrow: the table identifies a rate-like subblock in which the $B^{\star}_{Q6}$ residual surface has empirical contact after controls and null tests, but it does not assert that every coordinate in $Q$ survives, that the sign is organism-invariant, or that a single CUN/UUR or UUR/CUN direction defines a population rule.

\paragraph{Modeled-readout and selection boundaries.}
The modeled-tAI survival memory contains only $3$ organisms for the bounded coupling calculation, not a powered measured-ribosome panel.  It computed $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled value $3801961.031607896$, but it explicitly records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival when the threshold requires at least $40$ matched species.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run also stayed below the strong-imprint gate: $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, $3$ species, $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null95 maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and $\Delta DL=-258.6010084301837$ bits.  Thus the present positive statement is not an evolutionary selection law.  It is a bounded observation that one finite rate-proxy subblock passed its two null families.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore records a narrow empirical status.  The finite yeast rate-slot panel supports an observable execution subblock for $B^{\star}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  It does not license any of the following upgrades without separate layer-matched rows and their own declared statistics: measured translation survival beyond the tested rate proxy, ribosome mechanism, mature protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  A later protein, structure, localization, turnover, interaction, or phenotype claim must attach its own contact surface and must preserve the ordering $q_i\to t_j$ before any downstream $q_i\to p_k$ interpretation is allowed.
