\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for the B-star Q6 rate-slot bridge}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-rate-slot-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite empirical surface.}
This record isolates the point at which a synonymous residual coordinate is allowed to enter an observed execution account.  The finite surface consists of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the B-star Q6 residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation or rate proxy slot, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout when such a downstream row is present.  The registered rate-slot bridge was run in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} on $3289$ genes, with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}.  The run completed with status \texttt{passed} after $2667.02$ seconds and returned the finite conclusion \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}.  Its operational verdict was that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  The claim therefore enters the paper as a bounded execution subblock result, not as a free-standing claim about translation, folding, molecular function, or organism-level consequence.

\paragraph{Coordinate inventory.}
The residual side is the nine-coordinate B-star Q6 list
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row test does not permit a direct reading $q_i\to p_k$ unless the same coordinate first survives through a translation or rate proxy slot.  In the broader joined mediation run, the same nine-dimensional coordinate block was paired with per-protein modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance across $12$ organisms.  The yeast row had $5815$ proteins, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$ after the declared controls.  Its total residual-to-protein abundance coefficient was $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, the direct coefficient after conditioning on the translation readout was $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and the mediated difference was $0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  These numbers explain why the present rate-slot bridge is meaningful: the yeast protein-abundance surface contains a large translation-conditioned attenuation, while the rate-slot experiment supplies an observed execution subblock rather than only a modeled codon-adaptation proxy.

\paragraph{Control structure.}
The admissible comparison keeps amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the M-density baseline as controls rather than promoting them to biological coordinates.  Matched mRNA abundance is used to separate transcription-level signal from translation-level signal.  In the yeast structure-mediation audit, the five-way join contained $4759$ genes.  The direct residual-to-protein coefficient after control residualization was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE structure proxy changed it only to $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability reduced it to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$.  Adding the structure proxy after $T$ and $M$ gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation values were $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the structure-incremental fraction.  Thus the local decomposition is rate- and abundance-facing; the structure proxy is recorded as a small variance-partition component, not as a folding result.

\paragraph{Observed execution versus adjacent negative controls.}
The rate-slot bridge must be read alongside two finite negative boundaries.  In the dynamic mass-balance audit, $120$ genes and $718$ intervals were used.  The baseline $M$-only model had $R^2=0.9960628201518571$, the $M+E_{\mathrm{stat}}$ model had $R^2=0.996410628851051$, while the later layers dropped to $R^2=0.9712443377743347$ and $R^2=0.8953999568421264$.  The reported increment was negative, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  In the turnover audit, $3290$ genes and $200$ realized null draws gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These adjacent tests keep the execution result from expanding into mass-balance closure or turnover prediction.

\paragraph{Axis-level reading.}
The structural-order axis audit confirms that the same coordinate block can carry a specific finite signal without licensing a higher-layer interpretation.  Across $5$ folds with minimum joined size $300$, exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The dominant coordinate was $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip value $p=0.03125$.  The remaining coordinates did not pass that gate: for example $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ had increment $0.0013067592718559024$ and $p=0.0693359375$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ had increment $0.0020139270616115154$ and $p=0.142578125$, and $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ had increment $-0.00045340330387383447$ and $p=0.529296875$.  The rate-slot bridge is therefore compatible with a sparse axis identity: an observable execution subblock can be present even when most named residual coordinates do not individually pass the same held-out gate.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-contact boundary.}
The older modeled-tAI contact computed a finite codon-adaptation surface from GtRNAdb copy numbers and dos Reis wobble weights for $3$ organisms.  It covered all $61$ sense codons and all $20$ standard amino-acid families in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, human, and yeast; the local payload had $89$, $432$, and $275$ FASTA headers respectively, with $87$, $432$, and $275$ matched headers.  The modeled coupling surface had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute value $3801961.031607896$.  That finite contact is useful for constructing bounded translation readouts, but the organism count $3$ is insufficient for a powered survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.  The present rate-slot bridge is stronger in a different direction: it records an observed yeast execution subblock, while still refusing to promote modeled tAI, rate proxy, or protein-abundance association into a universal translation law.

\paragraph{Evolutionary and perturbation limits.}
The cross-ortholog imprint audit gives an explicit example of non-promotion.  It used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species, but the strong selection-imprint gate failed: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95$ percent maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-258.6010084301837$ bits.  The verdict was codon-bias expression correlation rather than a positive evolutionary selection-imprint claim.  A separate synonymous-perturbation audit found $1783$ synonymous mutants across $20$ genes, with $21$ CDS-map hits and $200$ realized null draws; that surface is too small and too specialized to convert the rate-slot bridge into a general perturbation mechanism.  These finite facts keep the chapter inside observable execution decomposition.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The allowed statement is narrow.  In yeast, a finite observed rate-proxy experiment over $3289$ genes passed both effective null classes and therefore supports an observable execution subblock for the B-star Q6 rate-slot bridge.  This can be placed next to the larger translation-mediated protein-abundance decomposition, where the yeast row had $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, but it does not identify a causal translation mechanism.  It does not establish folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality.  Any such layer would require its own matched measurement surface and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a direct residual-to-protein association remains inadmissible unless the residual first survives a translation or rate readout under the stated controls, and matched mRNA abundance remains a control variable rather than a promoted coordinate.
