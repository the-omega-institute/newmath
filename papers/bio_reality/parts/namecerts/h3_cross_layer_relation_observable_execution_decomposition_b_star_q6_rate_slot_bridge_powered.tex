\subsection{Observable Execution Decomposition for the B-Star Q6 Rate-Slot Surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-observable-execution-decomposition-b-star-q6-rate-slot}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite mediation surface whose rows are coding-sequence observations and whose columns separate three classes of coordinates. The first class is the nine-dimensional $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family
$\{K_{AAA}, \operatorname{Arg}_{AGR}, \operatorname{Ile}_{AUA}, \operatorname{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}, \operatorname{Leu}_{UUA/UUG}, \operatorname{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}, \operatorname{Ser}_{UCA/UCG}, \operatorname{Thr}_{ACR/ACY}, f_3\text{-stress}\}$.
The second class consists of translation readouts, including modeled tAI, stAI-style adaptation weights, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency when the corresponding measured rows exist. The third class consists of matched protein-abundance readouts. The carrier is not a direct residual-to-protein table: a protein row is admissible only after the residual coordinate has been tested through a translation slot. In symbols, the permitted readback has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k, \\
q_i &\in B^\ast_{Q6},\quad t_j \in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{RPF},\mathrm{TE}\},\quad p_k=\log_{10}(\mathrm{protein\ abundance}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The controls remain controls: amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance are not promoted to residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Observed execution row.}
The registered observable-execution computation ran on $3289$ genes in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. Its deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered{:}deterministic}$, the runtime was $2667.02$ seconds, and the terminal call was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{final\ conclusion} &= \mathrm{observable\_execution\_subblock},\\
\mathrm{verdict} &= \text{at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a positive finite-row execution result for an observed rate-proxy subblock. The claim is therefore not merely an identifier route or a model-only contact: within the yeast rows used by the computation, at least one rate proxy survived the two declared null classes. The result is intentionally narrower than a full protein-realization claim, because the row reports rate-slot survival rather than a completed causal chain from synonymous residuals to mature protein function.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated protein matrix.}
A separate multi-organism mediation computation supplies the scale of the surrounding $Q\to T\to P$ surface. It used the same nine residual coordinates and reported a joint mediation scalar with per-organism fractions $M^{QTP}$ in the unit interval for all $12$ organisms. The yeast row is the strongest entry in that table:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.9491551041216973,\\
R^2_{QP,\mathrm{total}} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,\mathrm{direct}} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895.
\end{aligned}
$$
Other organisms give smaller but positive mediated fractions, for example $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.4872268357227724$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse. These values support the interpretation that the yeast rate-slot call is located inside a broader finite mediation geometry, while still requiring the translation slot to be checked before the protein-abundance coordinate is read.

\paragraph{Controls and rank sufficiency.}
The yeast mediation row retained rank after residualization by the declared controls. Its control matrix had $23$ effective rank with $24$ control columns, $5792$ residual degrees of freedom after control removal, and rank $9$ for the residual coordinate block. The readout rank was $1$ for $T$, and the augmented block $[X,T]$ had rank $10$. The residual energies in the yeast mediation computation were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|T_\perp\|^2 &= 6.90537888244409,\\
\|Y_\perp\|^2 &= 2958.1865890925337,\\
\mathrm{energy}(X\to T) &= 5.671703404506426,\\
\mathrm{energy}(T\to Y) &= 941.8858618613751,\\
\mathrm{energy}(X\to Y) &= 882.6526210803958.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observable-execution result is therefore read as a rate-slot subblock inside a controlled finite matrix, not as a raw correlation between codon residuals and abundance.

\paragraph{Structure and abundance comparison.}
The yeast abundance-residual structure audit gives an additional decomposition of the same style. It joined $4759$ yeast genes across protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, coding sequence, and a $5'$ coding-region folding proxy. In that audit,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and the incremental structure fraction beyond measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$. The $Q\to S$ structure association had $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$, whereas the incremental structure probability was $0.1258741258741259$. Thus the finite rows separate an observable rate or abundance route from a much weaker structure-proxy route.

\paragraph{Dominant coordinate in the structural-order audit.}
The structural-order axis identity computation used the same coordinate list and found exactly one fold-level sign-flip significant coordinate. The dominant loading and dominant held-out increment were both carried by $f_3$-stress:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}}(f_3\text{-stress}) &= 0.0031744502294756994,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}}(f_3\text{-stress}) &= 0.03125,\\
|\ell|_{\mathrm{weighted}}(f_3\text{-stress}) &= 0.5109586606724208.
\end{aligned}
$$
The remaining coordinates did not pass that same gate: for instance $\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR}$ had $p=0.142578125$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$, while $\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA}$ had $p=0.0693359375$ and $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$. This matters for the rate-slot chapter because the observed execution result should be read as a subblock in a coordinate-resolved finite surface, not as evidence that every residual coordinate carries the same downstream behavior.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded companion rows.}
Several companion computations define the boundary of the positive rate-slot result. The cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species. Its positive-imprint gate was not met: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and $\Delta DL=-258.6010084301837$ bits. The dynamic mass-balance audit used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, but its full residual step decreased fit: $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, with $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$ and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$. The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and $200$ null draws, with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$. These rows prevent the yeast rate-slot result from being inflated into a selection law, a full mass-balance closure, or a turnover predictor.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI contact boundary.}
The modeled tAI survival contact had $3$ organisms and $61$ sense codons, with all standard amino-acid tRNA families present in the local payloads for \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast. The modeled coupling table had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, and its maximum absolute entry was $3801961.031607896$. However, the same finite row also states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that a powered survival claim requires at least $40$ matched species. Consequently, modeled tAI weights can support bounded adaptation-weight readbacks, but they cannot by themselves supply observed ribosome occupancy, translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Local admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is the following finite statement. In the yeast rate-slot experiment, one observed rate-proxy subblock passed both effective null classes on $3289$ genes. This allows the chapter to record an observable execution decomposition for a bounded $B^\ast_{Q6}$ rate-slot surface. It also allows the result to be placed beside the yeast mediation row where $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ and the indirect component is $0.28320534058057895$. It does not allow a direct $q_i\to p_k$ protein claim when the corresponding $q_i\to t_j$ step is absent, null, or untested.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter keeps the biological layers separated. A rate proxy, tAI or stAI weight, ribosome-profile route, mRNA control, or protein-abundance row is a bounded contact at its own layer. No translation claim follows from residual geometry alone. No folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, evolutionary law, or universal biological rule follows from the positive rate-slot call. Each such assertion would require its own layer-matched measurement surface and a controlled statistic after the translation step has survived.
