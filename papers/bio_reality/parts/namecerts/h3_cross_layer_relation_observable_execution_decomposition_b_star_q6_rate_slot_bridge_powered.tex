\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ rate-slot bridge}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-observable-execution-decomposition}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row and admissible surface.}
The record considered here is the finite yeast row named $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered}$.  Its observed panel contains $3289$ genes from \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, was run with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}, and terminated with status \texttt{passed} after $2667.02$ seconds.  The registered conclusion is \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}: at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  This is a statement about a finite decomposition surface, not a statement that the residual coordinates have become translation, folded structure, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Coordinate basis used by the row.}
The $Q$ side is the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis used throughout the associated finite audits:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=&\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row therefore has the form of a controlled finite matrix whose entries compare residual coordinates against observable rate proxies after ordinary sequence baselines have had their opportunity to explain the same row.  The relevant controls are amino-acid composition, organism identity where multiple organisms are pooled, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine residual axes.  Matched mRNA abundance remains a control surface, and protein abundance remains a downstream readout surface; neither is allowed to replace the rate-slot test itself.

\paragraph{What the positive verdict says.}
Let $R_a$ denote an observed rate-proxy slot and let $N_1,N_2$ denote the two effective null classes used by the registered run.  The finite verdict can be written as the existence claim
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\exists q_i\in Q\;\exists R_a\quad \Delta(q_i,R_a\mid Z)>0,\\
&\Delta(q_i,R_a\mid Z)>N_1(q_i,R_a),\qquad \Delta(q_i,R_a\mid Z)>N_2(q_i,R_a),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ is the declared baseline-control surface.  The row does not publish a named list of the winning $R_a$ slots, per-slot $\lambda$ values, or a per-slot $p$-value table in the supplied finite data; consequently the admissible readback is only the coarser one: at least one observed execution subblock is present in yeast under the two null classes.  The conclusion is stronger than a coordinate-only geometry statement and weaker than a mechanism statement.

\paragraph{Linked mediation scale.}
The broader protein-abundance mediation audit supplies the scale on which a rate-slot bridge must remain disciplined.  Across twelve organism panels, the joint nine-dimensional $Q$ surface was paired with modeled per-protein tAI and measured PAXdb protein abundance.  The joined protein counts included $5815$ yeast proteins, $3739$ \emph{E. coli} K12 proteins, $19053$ human proteins, $19395$ mouse proteins, $13295$ fly proteins, $14410$ zebrafish proteins, $11661$ worm proteins, $12418$ chicken proteins, $13322$ rat proteins, $4042$ \emph{Bacillus subtilis} proteins, $4129$ \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} proteins, and $1656$ \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} proteins.  For yeast specifically, the mediation decomposition reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}&=0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T}&=0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T}&=0.28320534058057895,\\
M^{QTP}&=0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
This yeast value says that modeled tAI accounts for a large fraction of the finite $Q$--protein-abundance association in that decomposition.  It does not turn modeled tAI into an observed ribosome trace, and it does not identify the rate slot that passed in the separate observable-execution row.

\paragraph{Structure and abundance checks.}
A separate yeast five-way join used $4759$ genes and residualized protein abundance against controls containing an intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid proportions, GC3, and $M$-density.  The single and joint values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}&=0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S}&=0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M}&=0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}&=0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row reported $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$, a right-tail permutation value $p=0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure, and $p=0.1258741258741259$ for the structure-incremental fraction.  These numbers show that the finite yeast record can separate an abundance association from a small structure-proxy component, but the structure proxy is a $5'$ CDS MFE statistic, not a folded-protein readout.

\paragraph{Mass-balance and turnover refusals.}
The observable-execution row also sits beside two negative or narrowing audits that prevent over-reading.  In a mass-balance closure audit using $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, the reported values were $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  In the turnover audit, $3290$ genes gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These two panels are important because they show that a positive rate-slot subblock is not being silently promoted into dynamic mass-balance closure or protein-turnover prediction.

\paragraph{Axis identity and modeled translation contacts.}
The structural-order axis audit selected $f_3$-stress as the single significant coordinate under its fold-level sign-flip gate: weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, rank by weighted delta $1$, and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  The remaining coordinate increments were smaller or not sign-stable at the stated gate, for example $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, and $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ had $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$.  The modeled-tAI contact audit computed $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ of $3801961.031607896$ across three organisms, but also recorded that $3$ organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the rate-slot row may be read as an observed finite subblock, while the modeled tAI row remains a bounded codon-adaptation contact rather than ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The permitted conclusion is narrow.  The finite data support an observable execution subblock for the yeast $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ rate-slot bridge: at least one observed rate proxy survives both effective null classes on $3289$ genes.  The data do not by themselves establish translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism-level phenotype, causal mechanism, evolutionary selection, or universality.  Any such stronger layer would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic after the same coordinate, baseline, mRNA-control, and downstream-readout separations are enforced.
