\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for the yeast B$^\ast_{Q6}$ rate-slot surface}\label{sec:bioreality-observable-execution-decomposition-b-star-q6-rate-slot}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}  The observation surface is the finite yeast panel in which B$^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are compared with observed rate-proxy slots under effective-null tests.  The registered run used \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=3289$ genes, deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}, and completed in $2667.02$ seconds.  Its recorded status is \texttt{passed}, with final conclusion \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}: at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  Thus the local conclusion is not that the full biological execution pathway has been recovered.  It is narrower: among the finite rows actually joined for yeast, the B$^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate surface contains a detectable rate-slot subblock that is not explained away by the two declared null families.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout decomposition.}  Let $Q$ denote the nine-coordinate residual matrix with axes $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Let $T$ be a matched rate readout or modeled translation-adaptation slot, and let $P$ be the matched abundance readout when protein abundance is used.  The rate-slot claim is recorded as a subblock of the controlled decomposition
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,\\
Q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, an $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance kept in the control layer rather than promoted into residual coordinates.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is therefore not sufficient for a protein-realization statement unless the $Q_i\to T_j$ step has first survived in the same controlled setting.

\paragraph{Positive row-level contact.}  The most direct finite fact for this subsection is the yeast rate-slot run itself: $3289$ genes and a positive observable-execution-subblock verdict. These numbers support the finite matrix discipline used here: the observed rate-slot result is interpreted through a controlled $Q\to T$ surface, and downstream protein abundance is read only through the mediated row when that row is explicitly present.

\paragraph{Mass balance and selection checks.}  Two further registered runs mark the limits of promotion.  The dynamic RPF--protein mass-balance run used $120$ genes, $718$ intervals, and reported $R^2_{L0,M}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1,M,E}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  This is not a positive closure of a full dynamic mass-balance model.  The evolutionary-imprint audit likewise stayed below a strong selection-imprint gate: $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, $3$ species, $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and maximum null95 $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, with \texttt{positive\_selection\_imprint=false}.  These negative or bounded outcomes are part of the same local reading: the yeast rate-slot surface is observable, but it is not by itself an evolutionary selection proof or a closed dynamical protein-production law.

\paragraph{Modeled adaptation contact.}  The modeled tAI contact supplies an earlier bounded readout rather than a measured ribosome trace.  In the three-organism modeled-tAI matrix, the computed B$^\ast_{Q6}$ residual axes were the same nine axes listed above; the codon-count space was $61$, the organism count was $3$, the coupling threshold was $0.01$, and $81$ entries exceeded the threshold.  The maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ and maximum absolute diagonal entry were both $3801961.031607896$.  The same record states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the result is modeled-tAI bounded coupling, not ribosome-seq or proteomics validation.  The present observable-execution result improves the empirical status only for its finite yeast rate-slot subblock; it does not erase that earlier limitation.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}  The chapter therefore licenses the following finite assertion and no larger one: in the yeast row set with $3289$ genes, at least one observed rate proxy slot yields a B$^\ast_{Q6}$ observable-execution subblock against both effective null classes.  It does not establish translation realization in the full mechanistic sense, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, selection, or universality.  Each of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are not ribosome occupancy, bulk protein abundance is not a folding or function assay, mRNA abundance is a control rather than a translation readout, and a direct residual-to-protein association remains inadmissible as protein realization unless the residual first survives the translation-readout step.
