\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The rate-slot bridge is recorded as a finite yeast execution surface rather than as a free biological law.  Its rows are Saccharomyces cerevisiae coding sequences for which the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be joined to observed rate-proxy information and the declared control columns.  The completed run used $3289$ genes, the deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}, and finished in $2667.02$ seconds.  The finite verdict was \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}: at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  Thus the admissible readback is not a direct assertion that a codon residual realizes protein abundance, folding, function, or selection.  It is narrower: some measured execution-rate block remains visible after the declared null comparisons on this yeast row set.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout discipline.}
The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual packet used throughout the finite audits: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These coordinates are not promoted to a biological mechanism by their names.  They enter only as controlled regressors or residualized predictors against observed or explicitly bounded rate proxies.  The rate-slot result is therefore a decomposition statement: if $Q$ denotes the residual-coordinate matrix, $T$ denotes a translation or rate-proxy slot, and $Z$ denotes amino-acid composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, organism indicators when present, and the $M$-density baseline, then the admissible positive statement has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
&\text{some finite slot } T_j \text{ retains a positive held-out or null-resistant contribution from } Q \\
&\text{after the comparison classes encoded by the run have been applied.}
\end{aligned}$$
It does not license $Q_i\to P_k$ as a protein-realization claim unless the intervening $Q_i\to T_j$ step is separately present and controlled.

\paragraph{Observed finite verdict.}
The registered execution-decomposition run returned \texttt{status=passed} and \texttt{final\_conclusion=observable\_execution\_subblock}.  This is a bounded positive result: the computation found a nonempty observed rate-proxy subblock that survived both effective null classes.  The result is stronger than a naming or routing event, because it is attached to a concrete yeast matrix with $n=3289$ genes.  It is also weaker than a mechanism claim, because the row set does not by itself establish ribosome causation, protein folding, post-translational state, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal rule.  The chapter therefore treats the run as an execution-layer bridge inside the finite row surface and not as an unrestricted biological interpretation.
