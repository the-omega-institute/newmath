\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object in this chapter is a yeast execution surface whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns separate three kinds of quantities: B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, translation-side rate proxies, and downstream protein-abundance or protein-state readouts.  The powered run for the rate-slot bridge used $3289$ genes from \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}.  It returned status \texttt{passed} after $2667.02$ seconds and recorded the finite verdict \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}: at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  The claim is therefore not merely that a codon residual exists, but that a residual coordinate can be placed in a controlled execution decomposition in which an observed rate proxy carries a non-null row-level signal.

\paragraph{Execution statement.}
Let $Q$ denote the residual coordinate matrix after the controls have been removed, let $T$ denote an observed or explicitly bounded translation-side rate proxy, and let $P$ denote a downstream abundance or state readout when such a readout is attached.  The rate-slot result asserts the existence of a non-null execution subblock, not a full causal pathway:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{there is a finite row block with } n=3289,\\
&Q \longrightarrow T \text{ passes both effective null classes on at least one observed slot},\\
&\text{and any } Q \longrightarrow P \text{ reading remains inadmissible unless the } Q \longrightarrow T \text{ step is present.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the local meaning of the verdict \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}.  It identifies an observed rate-side execution component.  It does not identify folding, function, physical admissibility, phenotype, a molecular mechanism, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The structural-order axis audit identifies which coordinate can dominate a separate structure-adjacent statistic, and this supplies an important caution for the execution block.  Across five folds, exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, its weighted held-out $\Delta R^2$ was $0.0031744502294756994$, and its sign-flip right-tail probability was $0.03125$.  Other axes did not pass that gate: for example $Arg\_AGR$ had $p=0.142578125$ and weighted $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$, while $Ile\_AUA$ had $p=0.0693359375$ and weighted $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$.  The rate-slot chapter therefore treats any coordinate-specific story as an audited finite-row observation, not as a general biological law.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The ready verdict is confined to the finite statement that a yeast observed rate-proxy slot in the B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ execution decomposition passed both effective null classes on $3289$ genes.  No direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is admissible unless a corresponding $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-side step has survived the declared controls.  No translation realization beyond the attached readout, no folding pathway, no physical admissibility, no molecular function, no phenotype, no causal mechanism, and no universal biological rule follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that evidence and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
