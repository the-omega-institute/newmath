\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for a  exorpdfstring{$B^*_{Q6}$}{B*Q6} rate-slot bridge}
\label{sec:bioreality-observable-execution-decomposition-bstar-q6-rate-slot-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the finite yeast panel in which the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are tested against observable rate-proxy slots rather than treated as direct protein-level explanations.  The registered run is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered}, with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}.  It used $3289$ genes from \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, ran for $2667.02$ seconds, and returned status \texttt{passed}.  Its final conclusion was \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}: at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  The finite readback is therefore not a free association $Q_i \to P_k$; it is a bounded decomposition statement about a rate-like slot that can be observed before any higher biological interpretation is allowed.

\paragraph{Coordinate inventory and readout discipline.}
The coordinate side is the same nine-dimensional residual family used by the surrounding $B^*_{Q6}$ assays:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The modeled translation-weight contact showed a nonzero bounded coupling surface for three organisms: $81$ entries exceeded the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$ and the same value on the maximum absolute diagonal entry.  That three-organism computation was explicitly not a powered survival claim: $n=3$ organisms is below the requested powered species count, and the readout was modeled tAI from GtRNAdb gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights rather than ribosome profiling or proteomics.  The present yeast execution result is therefore stronger only in the narrow sense that it uses an observed rate-proxy subblock on $3289$ yeast genes; it does not convert modeled adaptation weights into measured ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Observed execution verdict.}
Let $X_Q$ denote the residualized matrix of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, let $R$ denote the observed rate-proxy slot family used in the run, and let $Z$ collect the background controls that keep amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline from becoming promoted coordinates.  The finite claim evaluated by the run can be written as the existence of at least one rate slot $r$ whose incremental score remains positive against both effective null classes:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists r \in R\quad
\Delta(r;X_Q\mid Z)>0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\Delta(r;X_Q\mid Z)>\Delta_{\mathrm{null}}(r).
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified finite result supplies exactly this bounded conclusion through the verdict string \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock: at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes}.  It does not identify the residual coordinate as a causal driver, and it does not state that every coordinate in $Q$ survives.  It says that the observable execution layer is no longer empty for this yeast panel: at least one rate-like readout is separable from the declared null behavior on the registered finite rows.

\paragraph{Relation to protein-abundance mediation.}
A separate twelve-organism protein-abundance mediation computation gives useful scale for what the execution result is not allowed to claim by itself.  In that computation the per-protein modeled tAI readout $T$ and protein-abundance readout $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ were joined to real CDS codon counts.  The joined row counts included $5815$ proteins for \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The yeast mediation fraction was large on that modeled surface,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
Other organisms were more modest: the fractions included $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.  These numbers are mentioned here only to pin the finite neighborhood of the yeast execution result.  The present rate-slot bridge is not the same as the modeled $Q\to T\to P$ mediation surface, and it does not license a direct residual-to-protein claim unless a surviving translation readout and matched protein-abundance readout are both present on the same controlled rows.

\paragraph{Negative controls and attenuation checks.}
The surrounding finite assays also mark where the execution result must stop.  A dynamic mass-balance test on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals found $R^2_{L0,M}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1,M,E}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  A turnover-residual audit on $3290$ genes was also negative beyond its controls: $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These checks prevent the observed rate-slot bridge from being read as a general mass-balance law or a turnover predictor.

\paragraph{Structure-adjacent comparison.}
The yeast structure-mediation computation joined $4759$ genes across CDS, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy.  Its direct residual-to-protein association was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ after controls, while conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability reduced the incremental value to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$ and adding the MFE proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was only $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $S_Q$ to structure had $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$.  This comparison is important because the rate-slot bridge is observable, but it is not a folding or structure result.  A rate proxy may sit nearer to execution than a codon-count residual, but folded structure still requires its own layer-matched measurement.

\paragraph{Axis identity and evolutionary scope.}
The structural-order axis audit found exactly one significant coordinate under the fold-level sign-flip gate: $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip right-tail probability was $0.03125$.  The other coordinate increments were smaller or nonsignificant, for example $0.0020139270616115154$ for $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ with $p=0.142578125$, $0.0013067592718559024$ for $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $-0.00045340330387383447$ for $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ with $p=0.529296875$.  A cross-ortholog imprint run on $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species did not support the strong selection-imprint claim: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-258.6010084301837$ bits.  Thus the execution subblock may be reported as a finite observable bridge, but not as a population law, an adaptive mechanism, or a universal evolutionary principle.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The allowed conclusion is deliberately narrow.  The yeast finite row set supplies evidence for a nonempty observable execution subblock: at least one rate-proxy slot survived the declared effective null classes on $3289$ genes.  It does not by itself establish translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality.  Those interpretations require separate contacts at the relevant layer, joined to the same coordinate surface and evaluated after the controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler baseline ablation are already in place.  A direct association from $Q_i$ to a protein or phenotype readout remains inadmissible when the intervening rate or translation step is absent, null, or untested.
