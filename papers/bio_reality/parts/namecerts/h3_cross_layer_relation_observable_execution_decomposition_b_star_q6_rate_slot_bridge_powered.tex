The tested biological object is not a naked residual-to-protein association.  It is a controlled surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ ranges over B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $t_j$ ranges over translation or rate-proxy readouts, and $p_k$ ranges over matched downstream abundance readouts when such rows are present. It ran for $2667.02$ seconds and returned status \texttt{passed} with final conclusion \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}.  The finite verdict is therefore local and positive: at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes.

\paragraph{Rate-slot readback.}
The rate-slot bridge is supported by a direct finite execution test rather than by the existence of contact names.  In this chapter, a positive call means that an observed rate proxy was reached by the residual panel after two null filters were applied.  The statement can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
&n_{\mathrm{genes}} = 3289,\\
&\mathrm{organism}=\emph{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\\
&\mathrm{verdict}=\mathrm{observable\ execution\ subblock},\\
&\exists(q_i,t_j)\ \mathrm{such\ that}\ q_i\to t_j\ \mathrm{passes\ both\ effective\ null\ classes}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a bounded rate-readout result.  It does not by itself identify a ribosome-level causal mechanism, a folding route, a physical-admissibility certificate, a molecular function, a phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those interpretations require layer-matched measurements and their own finite tests.
