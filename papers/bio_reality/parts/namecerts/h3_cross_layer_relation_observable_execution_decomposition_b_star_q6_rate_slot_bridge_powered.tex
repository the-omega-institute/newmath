The row space is read as triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ only when the middle coordinate is an explicit translation or execution readout.  A direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is not admitted as protein realization unless a $q_i \to t_j$ step has first survived its own controlled test.  The controlled residual coordinates are the nine-element $B^{*}_{Q6}$ list used throughout the finite runs: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
