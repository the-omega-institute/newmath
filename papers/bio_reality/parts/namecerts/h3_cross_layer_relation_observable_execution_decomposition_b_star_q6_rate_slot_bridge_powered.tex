\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This chapter records an ai-discovered BioReality contact in which a B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family is tested against observable execution readouts rather than promoted directly to protein realization.  The finite surface is a gene-indexed panel in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The registered run \texttt{365aa14cdedc} used $3289$ genes and a deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}.  Its reported runtime was $2667.02$ seconds, its status was \texttt{passed}, and its finite verdict was that the contact reaches an observable-execution subblock: at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The residual side is the same nine-dimensional B$^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate family used in the matched translation-to-protein mediation panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.

$$
The execution claim is therefore not a direct assertion that a synonymous residual is a molecular function or a mature protein state.  It says only that, on a finite observed-rate surface, at least one coordinate-dependent slot remains after the declared null comparisons.  The word ``execution'' is used here in the narrow empirical sense of a rate-proxy readout that can be observed in rows and compared with null classes.

\paragraph{Readout result.}
The finite decision is positive only at the observable-execution subblock.  In symbols, the run certifies the existence of a tested slot $E_j$ for which the controlled residual contribution is not eliminated by the two effective null classes:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists q_i \in Q,\ \exists E_j \quad
\Delta(q_i,E_j \mid Z) > 0
\quad\text{after both declared null comparisons.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $Z$ denotes the ordinary biological controls that keep amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance or translation controls from being mistaken for the residual coordinate itself.
