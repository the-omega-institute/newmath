\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the B$^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as finite coordinates on coding-sequence rows, not as autonomous biological causes.  The carrier is the finite surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates, $t_j$ is an observed or explicitly bounded rate readout, and $p_k$ is a matched downstream abundance readout when such a downstream slot is present.  The rate-slot experiment was run on \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $3289$ eligible genes, deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}, and runtime $2667.02$ seconds.  Its finite verdict was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{verdict} &= \mathrm{observable\_execution\_subblock},\\
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 3289,\\
\mathrm{organism} &= \mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},
\end{aligned}
$$
meaning that at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  The result is therefore an execution-subblock claim: it licenses a finite decomposition of an observable rate proxy, not a claim that B$^\ast_{Q6}$ by itself realizes translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Observed rate-slot status.}
The rate-slot experiment supplies the chapter-specific positive contact: on the yeast finite panel, at least one observed rate proxy slot survived both effective null classes.  This is narrower than a global translation law.  The finite assertion can be written as the existence of a tested slot $t_j$ for which the residual block adds information after the declared controls and after the null comparisons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists j\quad &\Delta_{Q\to t_j} > 0,\\
&\Delta_{Q\to t_j}\ \text{survives both effective null classes on }3289\text{ yeast genes}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statement deliberately stops at an observable execution subblock.  It says that the B$^\ast_{Q6}$ residual surface has a finite rate-proxy foothold in yeast; it does not say that this foothold is causal, conserved, sufficient for protein output, or separable from every unmeasured biochemical process.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
with matched mRNA abundance, amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and M-density retained as controls rather than promoted into coordinates.

\paragraph{Nearby negative and bounded checks.}

The structural-order axis audit identified $f3\_stress$ as the only fold-level significant coordinate: it was both the dominant increment axis and dominant loading axis, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip $p=0.03125$. \paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore records the following local rule.  A positive rate-slot subblock allows the finite matrix to retain the path $Q_i\to T_j$ for the observed yeast execution surface, and it may be used as the required intermediate step before any downstream $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ decomposition is discussed.  It does not, by itself, establish translation realization in general, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law.  Each of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In the absence of such a contact, the admissible conclusion is exactly the bounded one: the B$^\ast_{Q6}$ residual block has a yeast observable execution subblock on $3289$ genes, with downstream use permitted only through an explicitly controlled rate-readout step.
