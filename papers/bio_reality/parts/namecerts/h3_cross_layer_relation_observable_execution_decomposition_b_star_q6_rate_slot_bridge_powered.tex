\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for the $B^*_{Q6}$ rate-slot bridge}
\label{sec:bior reality-observable-execution-decomposition-b-star-q6-rate-slot-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record studied here is the finite mediation surface whose rows join a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-side readout $t_j$, and a matched protein-side readout $p_k$.  The readback is intentionally narrower than a general biological interpretation: a residual-to-protein association is admitted only after a translation-side row has survived as an observed or explicitly bounded readout.  The controls retained in the row construction are amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, an $M$-density baseline on the support of the projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, and matched mRNA abundance when a translation-efficiency denominator is used.  The nine residual coordinates carried by the surrounding packet are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Observed rate-slot verdict.}
The registered rate-slot experiment ran on $3289$ genes from \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}.  Its recorded status is \texttt{passed}, and its final conclusion is \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}: at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  The runtime was $2667.02$ seconds.  This finite result licenses only the claim that a bounded execution subblock exists on the observed rate-proxy surface.  It does not by itself identify folding, function, physical admissibility, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or a universal rule.

Across twelve organism-scoped joined protein tables, each residualized design had $9$ $Q$ coordinates, rank $9$ for $X$, rank $1$ for the translation readout, and rank $23$ for the control matrix.  The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for budding yeast, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ were respectively $0.6084594553$, $0.4872268357$, $0.3157053954$, $0.6165167749$, $0.6129222528$, $0.1976903965$, $0.2720628916$, $0.1548879991$, $0.0839760292$, $0.1748968180$, $0.9491551041$, and $0.0185066856$.  In yeast, the total controlled residual-to-protein value was $R^2_{QP}=0.2983762499$, the direct value after conditioning on modeled translation was $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709094$, and the indirect difference was $0.2832053406$.  Thus the rate-slot passage is consistent with a strong yeast mediation footprint, but the mediation statistic remains statistical rather than causal.

\paragraph{Yeast decomposition checks.}
A separate yeast structure-and-abundance decomposition joined $4759$ genes, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572657$.  Its residualized abundance partition gave $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.0032845645$, while the incremental structure fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507779$.  The corresponding permutation values were $p=0.02797202797$ for $S_Q$ structure and $p=0.1258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  These numbers place the yeast rate-slot passage mainly on the translation-and-abundance side of the surface, not on a folded-structure conclusion.

\paragraph{Mass-balance and turnover boundaries.}
The dynamic mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals.  Its nested values were $R^2_{L0,M}=0.9960628202$, $R^2_{L1,M,E}=0.9964106289$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443378$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.0758443809$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693$.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and reported $R^2_{L2}=0.0557408516$, $R^2_{L3}=0.0502788722$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0054619794$, $\Delta DL=-13.6881970790$ bits, $p_{matchedbin}=0.8855721393$, and $p_{recoded}=1.0$.  These negative and nonsignificant downstream checks prevent the rate-slot record from being promoted to protein-turnover realization or a closed mass-flow mechanism.

\paragraph{Axis identity and rank warning.}
The structural-order axis audit found exactly one fold-level significant coordinate: $f3\_stress$.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502$, weighted absolute unit loading was $0.5109586607$, and sign-flip value was $p=0.03125$.  The other coordinates did not pass the same gate: for example $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ had weighted increment $-0.0003205780$ with $p=0.779296875$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ had $-0.0004534033$ with $p=0.529296875$.  The earlier modeled-tAI survival surface used only $3$ organisms, although it computed $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ of $3801961.031607896$.  Its own boundary states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the result is modeled tAI bounded coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Selection and scope.}
The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species.  It produced $\Delta R^2=0.0325051502$, description-length change $-258.6010084302$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477730$, and maximum null $95\%$ $\Delta R^2=0.0542715707$; the positive selection-imprint gate was false.  Therefore the observable execution subblock is not a population-level selection law.  The local conclusion is the bounded one: the finite yeast rate-proxy experiment supplies a positive observable execution component, while translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, and universality require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics before they can be asserted.
