\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for the $B^*_{Q6}$ rate-slot bridge}\label{sec:brioreality-q6-observable-execution-rate-slot}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.} The observable execution decomposition is evaluated on a finite Saccharomyces cerevisiae panel with $3289$ genes.  The row object is not a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  Each admissible row keeps three coordinates distinct: a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation or rate readout slot $t_j$, and a downstream observable slot $p_k$ only when that downstream slot is evaluated after the rate slot has been tested.  The registered run for this chapter is \texttt{365aa14cdedc}; it used the deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}, ran for $2667.02$ seconds, and returned status \texttt{passed}.  Its finite verdict was \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}: at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes.

\paragraph{Coordinate decomposition.} The chapter-specific readback is a controlled matrix, not a biological mechanism by name alone.  The admissible direction is
$$\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,\\
q_i &\in B^*_{Q6},\\
t_j &\in \{\text{translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, modeled tAI/stAI, rate proxy}\},\\
p_k &\in \{\text{matched protein abundance or another matched downstream observable}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The run establishes that an observed rate-proxy subblock exists for the yeast panel.  It does not say that every $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate survives, and it does not license a shortcut $q_i\to p_k$ when $q_i\to t_j$ is null, unmeasured, or rank-degenerate.  Amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance remain controls.  They are not promoted into new residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Relation to the protein-abundance mediation surface.} The broader $B^*_{Q6}$ mediation run supplies a useful finite comparison.  Across $12$ organisms, the joint nine-coordinate residual surface used the coordinates $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$.  Its joined protein panels ranged from $1656$ proteins in Sulfolobus solfataricus to $19395$ proteins in Mus musculus, with Saccharomyces cerevisiae contributing $5815$ joined proteins.  In that yeast panel the total residual-to-protein abundance coefficient was $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, the direct coefficient after conditioning on modeled tAI was $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference was $0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  These numbers are consistent with a strong bounded mediation decomposition for modeled tAI and protein abundance, but they remain statistical decompositions over matched rows rather than causal translation mechanisms.

\paragraph{Execution slot versus mass balance.} The rate-slot bridge is narrower than whole-system mass balance.  In the dynamic RPF/protein closure run, the same project family reported $120$ genes and $718$ intervals after fixed subsampling.  The mass-only fit had $R^2=0.9960628201518571$, adding the estimated rate slot gave $R^2=0.996410628851051$, while deeper layers dropped to $R^2=0.9712443377743347$ and $R^2=0.8953999568421264$.  The final incremental comparison was negative, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits; the TE-bin and time-shuffle tests gave $p=0.6831683168316832$ and $p=0.9306930693069307$.  Thus this chapter should be read as a positive observed-rate-proxy subblock, not as closure of the full RPF-to-protein mass-balance problem.

\paragraph{Negative controls from adjacent layers.} Other finite panels sharpen the boundary.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and found $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$ and $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  Its allowed reading was absence of evidence that $B^*_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover beyond expression, translation efficiency, structural confidence, and N-end controls.  The evolutionary selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; it found $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, a leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and a null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, so the strong selection-imprint gate was false.  These adjacent failures prevent the rate-slot bridge from being misread as selection, turnover, or global adaptation.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity within the same family.} The structural-order axis audit gives one positive but layer-specific identity check.  Across $5$ folds, exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate: $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  Other coordinates did not pass that gate: for example $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ had weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$, and $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ had $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$.  This supports the practice used here: the finite row matrix may isolate a specific executable subblock, but it must not convert all residual coordinates into a uniform biological law.

\paragraph{Local claim.} The local claim is therefore deliberately small.  On the yeast finite panel, the observed rate-slot bridge has a passed run and a positive execution-subblock verdict: at least one observed rate proxy survived both effective null classes.  The permitted reading is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family has a bounded observable execution contact at the rate-slot layer.  The result may be combined with the controlled $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$ mediation format only when the same finite rows include the translation or rate readout, the downstream observable, matched mRNA controls, and the declared covariates.

\paragraph{No upward promotion.} Nothing in this chapter establishes translation as a molecular mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, organismal phenotype, selection, causation, or universality.  Modeled tAI and stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readouts, not ribosome footprints.  Ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency rows, when present, do not by themselves measure folding, function, or physical admissibility.  Protein abundance rows do not by themselves measure molecular function, localization, turnover, or selection.  A stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic at that layer.
