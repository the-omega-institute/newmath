\paragraph{Local decomposition rule.}
The admissible readback is the controlled chain $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not counted as protein realization unless the $Q_i\to T_j$ step has first survived on the relevant translation or rate surface.  In local notation, after residualizing against the declared control block $Z$, the mediated part is read only through the difference between the total residual-protein association and the association left after conditioning on the translation readout:
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= R^2(P\mid Q,Z)-R^2(P\mid Q,T,Z),\\
\rho^{QTP} &= \frac{M^{QTP}}{R^2(P\mid Q,Z)}
\end{aligned}$$
when the denominator is positive and the ranks are nondegenerate.  The controls kept outside the promoted coordinate set are amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline on the support of the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors, and matched mRNA abundance where a translation-efficiency denominator is used.  These controls are part of the finite admissibility test, not biological conclusions in their own right.

\paragraph{Local conclusion and no-promotion boundary.}
The chapter verdict is ready because the exact rate-slot experiment ran to completion on $3289$ yeast genes and returned a positive observable-execution subblock: at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  The permitted conclusion is therefore local and finite: within the declared yeast table, some $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual information reaches an observed rate-proxy execution slot after the relevant null screens.

Nothing stronger follows from this chapter alone.  A translation-realization claim requires a layer-matched translation contact and its own controlled statistic.  A folding, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, localization, turnover, or universal-law claim requires a separate measurement surface at that layer.  Protein abundance is admissible only through a controlled $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ chain; a direct residual-to-protein association is not enough when the translation-readout step is absent, null, rank-saturated, or untested.  The finite verdict is an observed execution subblock, not a general biological mechanism.
