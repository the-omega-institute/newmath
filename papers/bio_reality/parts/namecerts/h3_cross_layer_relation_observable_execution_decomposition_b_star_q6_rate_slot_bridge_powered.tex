\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for the powered yeast rate-slot bridge}
\label{sec:bioreality-observable-execution-decomposition-b-star-q6-rate-slot-bridge-powered}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a finite observed-rate surface in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The rows are coding genes for which the registered computation could place B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates beside measured or derived rate-proxy quantities and the declared baseline covariates.  The powered run used $3289$ genes, the deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}, and completed in $2667.02$ seconds.  Its registered conclusion was \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}; in words, at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  The object is therefore not a bare codon-count association and not a direct claim about mature protein state.  It is a finite decomposition statement: after controls are imposed, some observable rate-like component remains capable of carrying B$^*_{Q6}$ residual information beyond the two null comparisons used by the run.

\paragraph{Coordinate surface.}
The residual side of the surface is the nine-coordinate B$^*_{Q6}$ basis used by the linked translation and abundance computations:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout side is not promoted to a single biological mechanism.  It is a list of observed execution slots whose admissibility is decided row-wise against null surfaces.  The conclusion records that one such slot survived the two null families, not that every coordinate or every rate proxy did so.  The finite nature of the claim matters: the run did not assert a universal codon law, and it did not infer a cross-organism rule from the yeast panel.

\paragraph{Registered verdict.}
Let $X_Q$ denote the residualized B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix, let $E_s$ denote an observed execution-rate slot, and let $Z$ collect the local baseline controls: amino-acid composition, GC3, coding length, M-density baseline, and the organism-fixed design terms appropriate to this yeast computation.  The positive statement can be written without adding a causal interpretation as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists s\quad \Delta_s(X_Q;E_s\mid Z)>0
\end{aligned}
$$
where the registered decision also requires survival against both effective null classes.  The experiment summary gives exactly this finite verdict: \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock: at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes}.  Thus the chapter records a bounded execution decomposition, not an unconstrained residual-to-protein shortcut.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readout.}
The broader B$^*_{Q6}$ translation-readout audit provides the coordinate and baseline context for this rate-slot result.  In the modeled tAI boundary run, the same nine residual coordinates were computed over a $61$-sense-codon count space.  The bounded coupling threshold was $0.01$, with $81$ entries above threshold and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That run used only $3$ organisms and explicitly refused powered survival without at least $40$ matched species.  The present yeast rate-slot result should therefore be read as a separate observed execution subblock, not as a retroactive validation of the three-organism modeled-tAI matrix.  Modeled tAI supplies one bounded contact surface; the execution-slot computation supplies a yeast finite-row readout with its own null tests.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation context.}
The linked cross-organism mediation computation gives a useful scale for what a downstream protein-abundance bridge looks like when it is explicitly estimated.  In yeast, the protein-abundance mediation run used $5815$ joined proteins, with residual degrees of freedom $5792$ after the control matrix.  Its total B$^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance $R^2$ was $0.29837624994140827$, the direct $R^2$ after conditioning on modeled tAI was $0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference was $0.28320534058057895$, giving a mediation fraction $0.9491551041216973$.  Those values are informative because they show the magnitude of a separately computed $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition.  They are not imported into the rate-slot verdict as proof of mechanism.  The present chapter uses them only to delimit the surrounding finite surfaces: the observed execution slot, modeled translation readout, and matched protein abundance are related but non-identical measurements.

\paragraph{Mass-balance and turnover boundaries.}
Other yeast-scale audits constrain how far the observable execution result may be carried.  The mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals after a fixed subsample from $4434$ eligible genes.  Its baseline $R^2$ values were $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, and $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These finite negatives prevent the rate-slot result from being interpreted as a general mass-balance closure or as evidence that B$^*_{Q6}$ predicts measured turnover once expression, translation efficiency, pLDDT, N-end information, and related controls are present.

\paragraph{Structural and abundance partitions.}
The structural-order audit found that the only fold-level significant coordinate was \texttt{f3\_stress}, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$.  Other axes did not pass the same gate; for example \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR} had weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0003205780368125179$ and sign-flip probability $0.779296875$.  A separate yeast abundance-residual partition used $4759$ joined rows and found $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$, and the structure-incremental permutation probability was $0.1258741258741259$.  These numbers support a narrow reading: observable execution can be decomposed into finite subblocks, but structure is not thereby obtained for free.

\paragraph{Evolutionary and selection boundary.}
The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run is also a guardrail for interpretation.  It used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species.  The positive statistical residual did not pass the strong selection-imprint gate: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and maximum null-$95$ $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$.  The registered conclusion was codon-bias expression correlation, not an evolutionary mechanism.  Consequently, the yeast execution-slot bridge cannot be promoted to selection, adaptation, or a population-level law without a separate evolutionary contact and a stronger cross-organism design.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion is exactly bounded.  A B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate may be reported as touching an observable yeast execution subblock because the $3289$-gene run passed both effective null classes for at least one rate-proxy slot.  Nothing in that sentence establishes translation realization in the molecular sense, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, selection, or universality.  Each of those would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In particular, a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not sufficient for protein realization unless a same-coordinate $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout step is observed or explicitly bounded and the downstream attenuation is computed under matched mRNA and baseline controls.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The chapter readback is therefore a controlled finite-row statement:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{organism}=\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\qquad n_{\mathrm{genes}}=3289,\\
&\mathrm{status}=\mathrm{passed},\qquad \mathrm{conclusion}=\mathrm{observable\ execution\ subblock},\\
&\exists s\;\mathrm{such\ that}\; E_s \mathrm{ survives\ both\ registered\ null\ classes\ after\ the\ baseline\ controls}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The evidence is strong enough for a ready BioReality entry at the observable-execution layer.  It is deliberately not strong enough for unrestricted biological promotion.
