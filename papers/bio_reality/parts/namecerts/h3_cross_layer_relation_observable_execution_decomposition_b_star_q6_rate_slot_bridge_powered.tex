\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ rate-slot bridge}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-rate-slot-bridge-observable-execution}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The row object for this record is a finite joined surface whose active observational unit is a coding sequence in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The registered execution used $n=3289$ genes and returned status \texttt{passed} with final conclusion \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}.  Its deterministic seed was \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}, and the recorded runtime was $2667.02$ seconds.  The finite carrier is not a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  It is a decomposition surface in which a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$ may enter a readout only through an observed rate-proxy slot, and the slot must defeat two effective null classes before it is admitted as an observed execution subblock.  In symbols, the admissible readback has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow r_j,\\
\Delta_{ij}^{\mathrm{slot}} &> \Delta_{ij}^{\mathrm{null},1},\\
\Delta_{ij}^{\mathrm{slot}} &> \Delta_{ij}^{\mathrm{null},2},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $r_j$ is a rate-proxy slot and the inequalities denote the finite audit verdict, not an unrestricted biological law.  The experiment therefore supports the statement that at least one observed rate-proxy component carries a reproducible execution signal after the declared null comparisons, while leaving the full cross-layer interpretation bounded by the measured surface.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control discipline.}
The active residual basis consists of nine coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Earlier bounded translation-readout computation placed these coordinates against modeled tAI weights for three organisms, with $61$ sense codons and $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$; the largest absolute modeled entry was $3801961.031607896$.  That three-organism modeled surface was explicitly not a powered ribosome or proteomics validation, because $3$ organisms are below the stated $\geq 40$ species condition for powered survival.  The present rate-slot bridge is therefore read as an observed execution component in yeast, not as a promotion of the modeled tAI matrix into a general translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to protein-abundance mediation.}
A separate cross-organism mediation audit gives useful calibration for the same residual basis.  Across twelve organism-matched CDS/protein abundance joins, each organism had rank-sufficient residualized design columns with $9$ $q$-coordinates and $23$ control rank.  The yeast join used $5815$ proteins, and its mediation fraction was $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, with total residual-to-protein $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, direct residual-to-protein after modeled tAI $R^2=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  Other finite fractions included $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{Escherichia coli}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, and $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse.  These values show that the broader $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ surface is numerically non-vacuous, but the present chapter uses them only as finite context for why an observed execution slot is worth separating from a direct protein-output readback.

\paragraph{Yeast rate-slot interpretation.}
The target execution result is narrower than the protein-abundance mediation table.  It says that the yeast row surface contains at least one observed rate-proxy slot that survives both effective null classes.  The row count, $3289$, is large enough to make the slot-level verdict an empirical finite-row statement rather than an identifier-routing event.  The conclusion is not that every $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate is active, nor that the dominant coordinate is fixed by this experiment alone.  The structural-order audit for the same coordinate family found exactly one fold-level sign-flip survivor, $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and one-sided sign-flip value $0.03125$; by contrast, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ had increment $-0.0003205780368125179$ and sign-flip value $0.779296875$.  Thus the available finite records favor a decomposed picture: some rate or structure-adjacent slots carry signal, while several named residual axes remain null or weaker under the same auditing style.

\paragraph{Comparison with nearby negative and bounded results.}
The execution subblock should also be read beside the negative mass-balance and selection-imprint audits.  In the dynamic RPF/protein closure run, $n=120$ genes and $718$ intervals gave $R^2_{L0,M}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1,M,E}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; it reported $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and a negative strong selection-imprint gate.  These nearby records prevent over-reading the rate-slot bridge: the bridge is a local observed-execution component, not a completed mass-balance law and not a demonstrated evolutionary selection mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The finite verdict licenses the following statement and no stronger one: in the yeast surface of $3289$ genes, at least one rate-proxy slot attached to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis passed both effective null classes, so the coordinate family is not confined to unobserved codon-count bookkeeping at this boundary.  It does not by itself establish translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality.  Any such statement requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a direct association $q_i \to P_k$ is not accepted as a protein realization claim unless the corresponding $q_i \to T_j$ step is present and survives the declared controls; matched mRNA abundance remains a control, protein abundance remains a downstream readout, and modeled codon-adaptation weights remain bounded annotations unless directly joined to observed rows.
