\subsection{Observable Execution Decomposition for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Rate-Slot Bridge}\label{sec:bioreality-observable-execution-decomposition-b-star-q6-rate-slot-bridge}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.} The record considered here is a finite execution-decomposition surface over Saccharomyces cerevisiae coding sequences. Its primary row set contains $3289$ genes, and the registered deterministic run is \texttt{365aa14cdedc} with seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}. The run completed with status \texttt{passed} after $2667.02$ seconds and returned the finite verdict \texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock}: at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes. The chapter therefore records a bounded execution readout, not a promotion from synonymous residual coordinates to a full biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinate meaning.} The coordinate side is the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family, with the usual nine projected directions
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are not protein outputs. They are finite residual labels attached to codon-count rows after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline are kept as controls. The observable-execution result says that a rate-like slot has empirical contact with the coordinate surface under the registered null comparison; it does not say that every coordinate survives, that the sign is conserved across organisms, or that the rate slot is the whole translation process.

\paragraph{Execution readout and null classes.} The finite output is best read as a decomposition claim. There is an observed rate-proxy layer $R$, a residual-coordinate layer $Q$, and a control layer $Z$. The registered run reports that at least one component of the rate-proxy layer remains visible after both effective null classes are applied:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&n_{genes}=3289,\\
&organism=\text{Saccharomyces cerevisiae},\\
&conclusion=\texttt{observable\_execution\_subblock},\\
&verdict=\text{at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both null classes}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is narrower than a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ protein-abundance claim. A direct residual-to-protein edge remains inadmissible unless the residual first survives a translation-readout step and the downstream protein row is evaluated with matched mRNA abundance as a control. The observable-execution run supplies a rate-slot contact; it does not by itself supply folding, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, or universal biological law.

\paragraph{Mass-balance check.} The mass-balance audit prevents over-reading the execution signal as a closed dynamic protein-production law. In the yeast RPF-protein closure run, $4434$ genes were eligible before a fixed subsample of $120$ genes was used, with $718$ intervals. The reported values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{L0,M}=0.9960628202,\quad
R^2_{L1,M,E}=0.9964106289,\quad
R^2_{L2}=0.9712443378,\quad
R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568.
\end{aligned}
$$
The increment from $L2$ to $L3$ was negative, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093$ and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070$ bits; the two null probabilities were $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168$ and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693$. Thus the present execution chapter cannot claim a closed RPF-to-protein mass-balance law. The positive rate-slot result is a local observable subblock, while the dynamic closure audit is negative for the stronger mass-balance interpretation.

\paragraph{Turnover and structural-axis limits.} The turnover audit also blocks promotion to protein-state realization. With $3290$ genes and $200$ null draws, the turnover run reported $R^2_{L2}=0.05574085163$, $R^2_{L3}=0.05027887219$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979434$, and $\Delta DL=-13.68819708$ bits. Its matched-bin and recoded null probabilities were $p=0.8855721393$ and $p=1.0$, so the finite conclusion is absence of evidence for extra turnover prediction beyond the registered expression, translation-efficiency, pLDDT, and N-end controls. A different structural-order audit did identify $f3\_stress$ as the dominant axis: its weighted held-out increment was $0.003174450229$ with sign-flip $p=0.03125$, while the other coordinate increments were smaller or negative, for example $0.002013927062$ for $Arg\_AGR$, $0.001306759272$ for $Ile\_AUA$, $0.001013409006$ for $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $0.0005307046180$ for $K\_AAA$, $0.0001761562637$ for $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $0.00004390882136$ for $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $-0.0003205780368$ for $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and $-0.0004534033039$ for $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$. This supports an axis-specific structural-order association in that audit, but the present rate-slot bridge remains separate from folding and physical admissibility.

\paragraph{Population and modeled-readout boundaries.} The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run also gives a useful warning. It used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; the positive-imprint gate was not passed, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515016$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.002147772985$, maximum null-$95$ $\Delta R^2=0.05427157067$, and $\Delta DL=-258.6010084$ bits. The conclusion was codon-bias expression correlation rather than a strong evolutionary selection imprint. Likewise, the modeled-tAI survival matrix used only $3$ organisms, even though it computed $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute modeled coupling of $3801961.031607896$. That run explicitly remained a bounded modeled-tAI contact, not a powered measured survival result. The observable-execution decomposition must therefore be read at its registered scale: one yeast rate-proxy subblock passed its two null classes; population law, measured ribosome occupancy, selection, and mechanism require separate matched contacts.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} The allowed conclusion is finite and operational. In the registered Saccharomyces cerevisiae surface, at least one observed rate-proxy slot carries detectable $B^*_{Q6}$-indexed execution information under the two effective null classes. The disallowed conclusions are equally important. No translation realization follows unless a direct translation readout supplies the same-row contact. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, post-translational state, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or universality follows unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that readout and the corresponding controlled statistic is recomputed. The chapter therefore closes only the observable execution-subblock statement for the finite yeast run.
