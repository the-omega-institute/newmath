\paragraph{Finite surface.} This BioReality record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows join three observable kinds of quantities: a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-side readout $t_j$, and a matched downstream protein-abundance readout $p_k$. The carrier keeps amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline as controls rather than as promoted coordinates. \paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.} The admissible statement is narrow. A $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate may enter protein-abundance fitting only through a translation readout that survives its own controlled row test, and the downstream protein abundance must remain matched to the same organism or CDS identifiers. A direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is not evidence for protein realization when the $q_i \to t_j$ step is null, absent, or untested. No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that readout and the corresponding controlled statistic is recorded. The present finite record supports only a bounded observable-execution subblock for the rate-slot bridge, with the strongest concrete primary row being the yeast run on $3289$ genes.
