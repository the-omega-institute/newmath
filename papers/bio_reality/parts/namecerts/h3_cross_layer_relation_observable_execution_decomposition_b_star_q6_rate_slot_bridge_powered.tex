\subsection{Observable execution decomposition for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ rate-slot bridge}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite cross-layer readout over triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ ranges over translation-rate or translation-efficiency readouts, and $p_k$ ranges over matched protein-abundance readouts when such a downstream row is available. The verified row for the present packet is the Saccharomyces cerevisiae rate-slot bridge run with $3289$ genes, deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_rate\_slot\_bridge\_powered:deterministic}, runtime $2667.02$ seconds, and status \texttt{passed}. Its finite verdict is exactly that an observable execution subblock is present: at least one observed rate proxy slot passed both effective null classes. Thus the object licensed here is not a direct residual-to-protein law, but a bounded execution decomposition in which the residual coordinate must first survive through an observed rate slot.

\paragraph{Coordinate list and control policy.}
The residual side is the common nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.

$$
The admissible readback keeps amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline as controls. Matched mRNA abundance is a control separating transcription from translation, not a translation readout by itself. The finite row therefore supports the existence of a rate-slot subblock only after the control surface has been respected. It does not allow a shortcut $q_i\to p_k$ unless a same-row $q_i\to t_j$ translation or rate proxy slot is observed and retained.

\paragraph{Observed rate-slot conclusion.}
Let $E_{qt}$ denote the controlled gain of a residual coordinate against a rate proxy, and let the two effective null classes be the declared null families used by the run. The verified conclusion can be written as the finite existential statement
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\exists\,q_i\in Q\;\exists\,t_j\quad
E_{qt}(q_i,t_j)>0\\
&\hspace{1.0cm}\text{with passage through both effective null classes on the }3289\text{-gene yeast panel.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the local mathematical content of the packet. It says that the residual basis is not only a codon-count geometry on this panel: one controlled rate proxy slot is empirically available. The result is deliberately weaker than a protein-realization theorem. It supplies an observable execution subblock, not folding, function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, causal mechanism, or universality.
