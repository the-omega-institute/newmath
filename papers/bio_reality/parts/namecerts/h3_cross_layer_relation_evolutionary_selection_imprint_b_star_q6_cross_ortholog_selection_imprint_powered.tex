\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Audit for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave a cross-ortholog imprint strong enough to be called evolutionary selection rather than a bounded expression-associated codon-bias signal. The finite surface consists of $240$ rows from $80$ single-copy orthogroups across $3$ fungal species, with coding sequences from Ensembl Fungi release $63$, orthogroup structure from OrthoDB v12 Fungi level $4751$, and protein abundance from PaxDb v6 integrated ppm. The tested residual coordinates are the nine-coordinate list
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6} = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The carrier is therefore not a free association between synonymous coordinates and proteins. It is a controlled finite table in which a residual coordinate may enter a downstream abundance discussion only after the relevant translation-facing readout has been kept distinct from amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Selection-imprint gate.}
The registered offline run completed and returned a bounded verdict rather than a positive selection-imprint call. The fitted residual gain was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\ clade\ out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
\max(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95}) &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
Because the observed $\Delta R^2$ is below the null $95\%$ envelope and the leave-clade-out score is negative, the positive selection-imprint indicator is false. The chapter therefore reports the finite outcome as codon-bias--expression correlation, not as a demonstrated evolutionary selection mechanism. The phrase ``passed'' attached to the run means only that the registered computation reached its bounded endpoint; it does not mean that the strong biological claim was supported.

\paragraph{Translation readout context.}
The same coordinate family has a modeled translation-contact surface, but that surface is not by itself measured ribosome occupancy. In the modeled-tAI audit, $3$ organisms were available, the codon-count space had $61$ sense codons, the coupling threshold was $0.01$, and $81$ entries exceeded the threshold. The maximum absolute modeled coupling was $3801961.031607896$, equal to the maximum absolute diagonal entry. The tRNA payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the matched FASTA header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively, with unmatched counts $2$, $0$, and $0$. These rows justify a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but $n=3$ is explicitly insufficient for powered survival. Thus the cross-ortholog imprint cannot be promoted by appealing to modeled tAI alone.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation background.}
A separate twelve-organism mediation table does show finite $Q\to T\to P$ decompositions under controls, and it is useful as a comparison boundary. The joined protein counts range from $1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ in mouse and $19053$ in human. The mediation fractions include $0.9491551041216973$ for yeast, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse, and $0.01850668564589326$ for \emph{Sulfolobus}. In yeast the total residual-to-protein abundance score was $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, the direct score after modeled tAI was $0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference was $0.28320534058057895$. Those numbers show that mediated abundance summaries can be computed on finite rows, but they do not rescue the present selection-imprint gate: an abundance mediation surface is not a population-genetic selection proof.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite checks.}
The structural-order audit found exactly one passing coordinate, $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$. All other coordinate increments were smaller or null-facing; for example $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ had increment $0.0020139270616115154$ with sign-flip probability $0.142578125$, while $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ had increment $-0.00045340330387383447$. The mass-balance audit on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals also stayed negative for the stronger closure step: $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, and $\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}}=-668.8046070381928$, with $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$ and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$. The turnover audit likewise did not support an extra $B^*_{Q6}$ turnover claim beyond its controls: $3290$ genes, $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}}=-13.68819707902732$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-mediated abundance boundary.}
The yeast five-way join supplies a finer local comparison without changing the evolutionary verdict. It joined $4759$ genes, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$. The residualized abundance partition gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction after measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, the $S_Q$ structure score was $0.003774248455422586$, the right-tail permutation probability for $S_Q$ was $0.027972027972027972$, and the right-tail probability for incremental structure was $0.1258741258741259$. This is a controlled variance partition, not a causal pathway and not an evolutionary-selection imprint.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. The finite cross-ortholog table detects a codon-bias--expression association under a registered audit, but it fails the stronger selection-imprint gate because the observed gain is below the null envelope and does not survive the leave-clade-out check. PaxDb ppm values are integrated across studies and species rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell quantities; cross-reference failures can remove proteins non-randomly; and $80$ orthogroups across $3$ species are underpowered for a strong evolutionary-selection claim. No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation law, or universality follows from this chapter. Each such layer would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic before it could be stated.
