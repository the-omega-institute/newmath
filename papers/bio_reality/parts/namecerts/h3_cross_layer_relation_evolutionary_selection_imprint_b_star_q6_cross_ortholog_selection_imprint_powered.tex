\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residuals}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave a cross-ortholog imprint that is strong enough to be read as evolutionary selection rather than as a bounded codon-bias and expression association.  The finite table has $80$ fungal single-copy orthogroups, $3$ species, and $240$ joined rows.  The source panel is OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level $4751$, CDS rows from Ensembl Fungi release $63$, and protein abundance from PaxDb v6 integrated ppm.  The residual coordinate list used by the surrounding $Q6$ packet is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row object is therefore not a direct protein-realization claim.  It is a controlled finite comparison over residual coordinates, orthogroup membership, species labels, coding sequences, and integrated abundance readouts.

\paragraph{Gate statistic.}
The selection-imprint gate was evaluated with an incremental fit against an empirical null and a leave-clade-out check.  The observed incremental coefficient of determination was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\mbox{-}clade\mbox{-}out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-imprint predicate is false because the observed $\Delta R^2$ does not clear the null envelope and the leave-clade-out increment is negative.  The completed run status is nevertheless useful: the registered offline computation completed and returned a bounded verdict rather than a missing-data failure.  The honest readback is codon-bias expression correlation, not a powered evolutionary selection-imprint result.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The sample size is the main constraint on interpretation.  With $n_{OG}=80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows, the table is too small for a strong cross-ortholog evolutionary claim.  The finite run explicitly reports $claim\_supported=false$ and $positive\_selection\_imprint=false$.  In particular, a positive residual coefficient in this surface would be an imprint criterion only; it would still not identify the molecular selection mechanism.  Here even that stronger imprint gate is not passed.  The defensible statement is that the available cross-ortholog data show a codon-bias/expression association compatible with bounded residual structure, while the powered selection-imprint assertion remains unsupported.

\paragraph{Translation context.}
The negative selection-imprint result is not a denial of all $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-layer contacts.  A modeled translation-survival computation over $3$ organisms reported $81$ modeled-tAI bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, using GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  That computation also records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the result is modeled-tAI bounded coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  Thus the present chapter keeps two facts separate: the residual coordinates can interact with modeled translation-readout weights, but the cross-ortholog selection-imprint gate does not pass.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation context.}
A broader protein-abundance mediation run joined $12$ organisms and computed a joint $9$-coordinate $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition with modeled per-protein tAI as $T$ and log$_{10}$ PaxDb abundance ppm as $P$.  The organism-level mediation fractions were finite and in $[0,1]$, including $0.9491551041216973$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6084594553474505$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, and $0.01850668564589326$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Those finite mediation numbers are statistical decompositions under controls; they do not repair the failed cross-ortholog selection-imprint gate.  The selection chapter therefore cannot promote the protein-abundance mediation context into evidence of evolutionary selection.

\paragraph{Perturbation and confirmatory anchors.}
The same packet family includes perturbation and confirmatory contacts that remain scoped to their own surfaces.  The \textit{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-output run used $1459$ variants and reported $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta L_{bits}=19.089327473282296$, $p_{matched}=0.004975124378109453$, and $p_{within}=0.004975124378109453$. These are not selection-imprint measurements.  They are listed only to keep the finite record honest: positive contacts elsewhere do not override the negative gate in the cross-ortholog selection table.

\paragraph{Local interpretation rule.}
The chapter therefore records a completed negative for the strong evolutionary selection-imprint reading.  The allowed claim is that the present finite cross-ortholog surface supports a bounded codon-bias/expression correlation report.  It does not establish translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, causation, adaptation, or universality.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step, and even a surviving translation step would still need a separate layer-matched contact for folding, function, physical admissibility, phenotype, or a general biological rule.  PaxDb integrated ppm are study-integrated abundance summaries rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell measurements, and failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins non-randomly.  Those limitations are part of the verdict, not ancillary caveats.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
For this finite row set,
$$
\begin{aligned}
positive\_selection\_imprint &= false,\\
claim\_supported &= false,\\
verdict &= codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation.
\end{aligned}
$$
The claim is ready for inclusion only as a bounded negative: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals may remain in the codon-bias, modeled-translation, and controlled protein-abundance ledger, but this specific cross-ortholog experiment does not license an evolutionary selection-imprint conclusion.
