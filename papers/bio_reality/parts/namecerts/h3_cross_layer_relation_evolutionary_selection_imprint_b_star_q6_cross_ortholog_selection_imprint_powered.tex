\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Audit for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ Residuals}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The audit surface is a finite table of fungal single-copy orthogroup rows in which each entry carries three kinds of information: a $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate computed from coding sequence, an orthogroup and species key, and an abundance-side readout used only as a bounded statistical contact.  The registered run used OrthoDB v12 Fungi level 4751 single-copy orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release-63 coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated protein-abundance ppm.  After the row join and reconciliation filters, the fitted table contained $80$ orthogroups, $3$ species, and $240$ total rows.  This size is enough to test whether a small cross-ortholog imprint is visible under the declared finite design, but it is not enough to promote a broad evolutionary selection law.

\paragraph{Tested gate.}
The positive selection-imprint gate asked whether the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual block improved the expression or abundance-side model beyond the registered baseline and whether that improvement survived a clade-held-out check and a finite null threshold.  The measured improvement was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\mbox{-}clade\mbox{-}out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null95}} &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
The in-sample increment is positive, but it is below the null envelope and the leave-clade-out increment is slightly negative.  The registered Boolean gate therefore returns $\mathrm{positive\_selection\_imprint}=\mathrm{false}$.  The run status is nevertheless a completed bounded audit: $\mathrm{status}=\mathrm{passed}$ means that the offline experiment executed and produced a finite verdict, not that the strong biological claim was supported.

\paragraph{Resulting claim level.}
The honest readback is a codon-bias expression correlation, not a demonstrated selection imprint.  In this dataset the strongest admissible statement is that $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a detectable association direction inside the fitted expression-abundance surface, while the evolutionary selection-imprint criterion fails.  The conclusion field is $\mathrm{codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation}$ and the support flag for the strong claim is $\mathrm{claim\_supported}=\mathrm{false}$.  Thus the chapter records a negative result for the powered selection-imprint claim and a narrower positive contact for codon-bias-associated expression structure.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance contacts.}
The boundary is sharpened by the nearby translation-layer audits.  A modeled-tAI survival matrix was computed for $3$ organisms with $9$ residual coordinates, $61$ sense codons, threshold $0.01$, and $81$ entries above threshold; its maximum absolute modeled coupling was $3801961.031607896$.  That run explicitly states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the result is modeled-tAI bounded coupling, not ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  A separate protein-abundance mediation audit joined $12$ organisms and reported per-organism mediated fractions such as $0.9491551041216973$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse.  Those numbers justify keeping the $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation surface as a bounded statistical object, but they do not repair the failed cross-ortholog selection-imprint gate above.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism follows from this ortholog audit by itself.  PaxDb ppm values are integrated across studies and species rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell measurements; failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins in a non-random way; and a positive residual association, even when present, is an imprint criterion rather than direct proof of molecular selection.  Therefore the present chapter does not claim ribosome occupancy, translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Each of those layers would require its own matched measurement contact and its own finite statistic after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance controls.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is negative for the strong evolutionary selection-imprint hypothesis and positive only for a narrower codon-bias expression-correlation readback.  The row counts and scalar gate values are sufficient for a standalone BioReality chapter because the experiment completed, the null boundary is explicit, and the non-promotion rule is local to the chapter.  The selection-imprint packet therefore remains bounded at correlation-level evidence until a larger multi-species, matched-condition, cross-ortholog panel produces a positive clade-robust increment above its null envelope.
