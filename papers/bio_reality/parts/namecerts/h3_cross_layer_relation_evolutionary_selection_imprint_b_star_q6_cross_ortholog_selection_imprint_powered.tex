\subsection{Cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates leave a selection-imprint trace across matched ortholog rows after the usual codon-count and abundance boundaries have been kept separate.  The finite table has $240$ rows, organized as $80$ single-copy fungal orthogroups across $3$ species.  The source panel is explicitly bounded: OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level 4751 supplies the single-copy orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release 63 supplies the CDS rows, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm supplies the abundance coordinate.  The measured object is therefore not a direct molecular-selection mechanism; it is a finite cross-ortholog regression surface whose response is an integrated protein-abundance proxy and whose admissible conclusion must be read through the controls and null comparison.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and translation boundary.}
The residual basis used by the surrounding route consists of nine named coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A related modeled-tAI contact computed from GtRNAdb copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights gives $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$ and maximum absolute diagonal entry $3801961.031607896$.  That auxiliary surface contains only $3$ organisms and is marked as insufficient for powered survival, since the declared powered route requires at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the present cross-ortholog audit may use the residual coordinates as predictors, but it may not convert modeled tAI coupling into observed ribosome occupancy, translation realization, or protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Gate statistic.}
The selection-imprint gate compares the residual improvement against an effective null and a clade-holdout stability check.  The observed increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\mbox{-}clade\mbox{-}out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null95,max}} &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-imprint decision is therefore false: the measured improvement is below the maximum null $95\%$ benchmark and does not survive the leave-clade-out direction check.  The registered run completed successfully, but its successful completion is a bounded verdict, not support for the strong evolutionary selection-imprint claim.

\paragraph{Allowed reading.}
The honest finite-row conclusion is codon-bias expression correlation.  This is weaker than a selection-imprint statement.  The negative strong-gate decision says that, on this $80$-orthogroup, $240$-row, $3$-species surface, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals do not meet the declared evidence standard for a cross-ortholog evolutionary selection imprint.  The result is still informative because it distinguishes a detectable statistical association from a stronger population-level selection claim.  In symbols, the observed report licenses only
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{finite\ association}(Q,\mathrm{expression/abundance\ proxy}) &\quad \mathrm{reported},\\
\mathrm{positive\ selection\ imprint}(Q) &\quad \mathrm{not\ supported}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run status is passed, the field \texttt{claim\_supported} is false, and the recorded verdict is \texttt{codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation}.

\paragraph{Relation to mediated protein abundance.}
A separate protein-abundance mediation surface joins real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PaxDb abundance across $12$ organisms.  On that surface the joint nine-dimensional residual block has positive mediated fractions in the unit interval, for example $0.9491551041216973$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.6084594553474505$ in \textit{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $0.4872268357227724$ in \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human.  Those numbers show that a bounded statistical mediation calculation can be performed after matched codon, translation-readout, abundance, and control rows are assembled.  They do not rescue the present cross-ortholog selection-imprint gate, because the present gate has its own orthogroup panel, its own null threshold, and its own leave-clade-out failure.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The finite-row record does not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, causation, or a universal biological law.  PaxDb ppm values are integrated across studies and species rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell protein counts.  Failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins non-randomly.  A positive residual statistic, even when present, is only an imprint criterion unless a separate contact supplies the molecular layer being claimed.  Here the strong imprint criterion itself is not met: $n_{\mathrm{OG}}=80$ is underpowered for a strong evolutionary selection-imprint claim, and the leave-clade-out increment is negative.  Consequently any direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association remains blocked from protein realization unless a same-coordinate $Q_i\to T_j$ translation step is established first and the downstream $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ surface survives the declared controls.
