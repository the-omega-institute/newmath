\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bior reality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates can be promoted from a controlled codon-bias and expression association to a cross-ortholog selection-imprint claim.  The finite surface contains $80$ fungal single-copy orthogroups, $3$ species, and $240$ species-by-orthogroup rows.  The source table is OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level $4751$ for single-copy orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release 63 for coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm for abundance.  The tested conclusion is not a positive selection-imprint result; the registered run completed and returned the bounded verdict \emph{codon-bias expression correlation}.

\paragraph{Coordinate family.}
The residual coordinates available to the test are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ axes already used by the translation-readout programs: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The finite readback is therefore not a direct map from a residual coordinate to an evolutionary mechanism.  A coordinate may contribute only through a controlled comparison in which amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline remain covariates rather than promoted biological coordinates.  The matched protein-abundance contact is also bounded: PaxDb ppm are integrated across studies and species and are not matched-condition absolute per-cell abundance measurements.

\paragraph{Imprint statistic.}
The evaluated positive-imprint gate compared the residual improvement to an empirical null and to a clade-held-out check.  The finite scalars were
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{OG} &= 80,\\
 n_{rows} &= 240,\\
 n_{species} &= 3,\\
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
 \Delta L_{bits} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
 \Delta R^2_{leave\text{-}clade\text{-}out} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
 \max null95(\Delta R^2) &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
The in-sample residual increment is positive, but it is smaller than the recorded $95\%$ null envelope and reverses under the leave-clade-out check.  The Boolean gate $positive\_selection\_imprint$ is therefore false, and the registered claim flag $claim\_supported$ is false.  The negative decision is not a failed computation: the run status is \emph{passed}, meaning that the offline experiment ran to completion and produced a bounded verdict.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-layer evidence.}
The result is consistent with a stricter layer discipline.  In a separate modeled-tAI survival computation, the contact used only $3$ organisms and explicitly marked that number as insufficient for powered survival when at least $40$ matched species would be required.  That run did compute a bounded modeled-tAI coupling surface with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, but it was labeled as modeled translation readout rather than ribosome-seq or proteomics validation.  A broader protein-abundance mediation computation joined $12$ organisms and reported positive statistical mediation fractions, for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human.  Those numbers help describe downstream abundance associations, but they do not rescue the present cross-ortholog selection-imprint test because the present gate is specifically the orthogroup-level, clade-held-out imprint statistic above.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result supports only this narrow statement: across the tested fungal ortholog table, $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals retain a codon-bias and expression-correlation footprint, but the evidence is not strong enough to call a powered evolutionary selection imprint.  The principal obstruction is not merely sample size; the clade-held-out improvement is negative, and the positive in-sample increment lies below the null threshold.  The non-random loss introduced by failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation also prevents the surviving rows from being treated as a neutral sample of all proteins.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal selection mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this chapter.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ abundance association is not enough for protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled translation-readout step $Q_i \to T_j$ and the downstream $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation is evaluated with matched mRNA abundance and the stated sequence controls.  Likewise, a positive codon-bias residual in a three-species orthogroup table is an imprint criterion only when it beats the null envelope and remains positive under clade holdout.  In this finite run it does neither, so the proper readback is a bounded codon-bias expression correlation rather than a selection-imprint claim.
