\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates leave a cross-ortholog imprint strong enough to be promoted beyond a codon-bias and expression-correlation readout. The finite table has $240$ rows, formed from $80$ single-copy fungal orthogroups across $3$ species. The data route is OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level $4751$ for orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release $63$ for coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm for abundance. The coordinate side is restricted to the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axes used in the surrounding translation-readout program: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. Amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline remain covariates rather than biological targets.

\paragraph{Decision statistic.}
The strong imprint gate asks whether the residual layer improves the cross-ortholog abundance model beyond its null envelope and remains positive under clade holdout. The completed run reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{orthogroups}} &= 80,\\
 n_{\mathrm{rows}} &= 240,\\
 n_{\mathrm{species}} &= 3,\\
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
 \Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
 \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\ clade\ out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
 \max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
The in-sample residual gain is therefore positive but smaller than the registered null $95\%$ benchmark, and the leave-clade-out value is slightly negative. The finite verdict is $\mathrm{positive\ selection\ imprint}=\mathrm{false}$. The run nevertheless completed successfully as an audit: its accepted readback is a codon-bias--expression correlation, not a powered evolutionary selection-imprint claim.

\paragraph{Relation to translation mediation.}
The selection-imprint surface is not allowed to bypass the translation layer. A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ abundance association is admissible only as a statistical association unless a same-coordinate $Q_i \to T_j$ readout has survived first. The broader modeled translation program has a bounded tAI contact with $3$ organisms, $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, and a reported maximum absolute modeled coupling of $3801961.031607896$, but that contact is explicitly modeled tAI rather than observed ribosome occupancy or proteomics validation. The powered mediation table across $12$ organisms does show positive bounded mediation fractions for protein abundance under modeled per-protein tAI, including $0.9491551041216973$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.4872268357227724$ in \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, and $0.27206289160119157$ in \textit{Homo sapiens}. Those figures support a separate statistical mediation readout, but they do not rescue the cross-ortholog selection-imprint gate, whose own leave-clade-out and null-envelope tests fail the strong promotion criterion.

\paragraph{Bounded verdict.}
The chapter therefore records an honest negative for the strong evolutionary-selection interpretation. The finite result is not that $B^*_{Q6}$ is biologically empty; it is that this particular cross-ortholog, three-species, $80$-orthogroup surface does not establish a selection imprint. PaxDb ppm are integrated across studies and species, not matched-condition absolute per-cell measurements. Failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins non-randomly. A positive residual statistic, when present, would still be an imprint criterion rather than direct proof of the molecular selection process. Here the stronger gate is not met.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from this finite table by itself. Translation requires a matched translation readout such as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI, or stAI kept in its own layer. Protein abundance requires matched proteomics rows and matched mRNA controls. Structure, function, physical admissibility, perturbation response, and evolutionary mechanism each require their own layer-matched contact and statistic. The present record stops at a bounded cross-ortholog codon-bias--expression correlation with the strong selection-imprint gate evaluated and rejected.
