\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The selection-imprint test uses a finite cross-ortholog surface whose rows are orthogroup--species entries rather than isolated codons.  The audited table contains $80$ single-copy fungal orthogroups, $3$ species, and $240$ joined rows.  Each row carries a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate summary for the coding sequence, an expression or abundance-side readout drawn from the available matched resources, and control information sufficient to keep amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the residual-coordinate support baseline from being promoted into the tested coordinate itself.  The data source for this finite table is recorded as OrthoDB v12 Fungi level 4751 single-copy orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release 63 CDS, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm abundance.

\paragraph{Tested claim.}
The strong claim would be that a synonymous residual coordinate leaves a cross-species evolutionary imprint beyond simpler codon-bias and expression structure.  The registered gate did not pass.  The evaluated finite statistic was
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{orthogroups}} &= 80,\\
  n_{\mathrm{species}} &= 3,\\
  n_{\mathrm{rows}} &= 240,\\
  \Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
  \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\ clade\ out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
  \max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.05427157066522958,\\
  \Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive selection-imprint indicator is therefore false: the observed $\Delta R^2$ is below the finite null threshold, and the leave-clade-out statistic is slightly negative.  The correct readback is a codon-bias--expression correlation, not a selection-imprint realization.

\paragraph{Coordinate inventory.}
The tested residual family is the same nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ basis used by the translation-readout packet: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These coordinates are admissible here only as finite residual descriptors of coding sequences.  They are not treated as direct measurements of translation, folded protein state, phenotype, or selection.  A separate modeled-tAI audit over $3$ organisms found $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, but that run explicitly remains modeled and underpowered for survival because $3$ organisms are below the $40$-matched-species requirement for powered survival.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation context.}
A separate protein-abundance decomposition supplies useful boundary information without rescuing the evolutionary-imprint claim.  In that finite mediation table, $12$ organisms had rank-sufficient rows after control residualization: examples include $n=3739$ joined proteins for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=19053$ for human, and $n=19395$ for mouse.  The modeled mediation fractions $M^{QTP}$ ranged from very small to large: $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, $0.08397602919861291$ in \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.6165167748739756$ in \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, and $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast.  These are statistical $Q \to T \to P$ decompositions using modeled per-protein tAI and PaxDb abundance, not causal evolutionary evidence.  They show that a translation-conditioned protein-abundance analysis can be finite and nondegenerate, while the ortholog-level selection-imprint gate still fails on its own terms.

\paragraph{Related finite negatives.}
The boundary is reinforced by other downstream audits.  In the dynamic RPF/protein mass-balance check, $120$ genes and $718$ intervals gave $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, and $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, with $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$ and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  In the turnover audit, $3290$ genes gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These finite rows do not contradict the codon-bias--expression correlation; they prevent that correlation from being inflated into mass-balance closure or turnover prediction.

\paragraph{Positive residual identity kept local.}
There is one related structural-order result that identifies $f3\_stress$ as the dominant coordinate in a separate five-fold audit.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$ with sign-flip $p=0.03125$, and no other coordinate passed that fold-level sign-flip gate.  This fact is useful for naming the residual axis that can carry structure-adjacent signal in a separate test, but it does not alter the evolutionary-imprint verdict.  A coordinate can be real as a finite residual axis and still fail the cross-ortholog selection-imprint criterion.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row conclusion is bounded and negative for the strong claim.  The experiment ran to completion, but $claim\_supported=false$ and the honest verdict is $codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation$.  PaxDb ppm values are integrated across studies and species rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell measurements; failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins non-randomly; and $80$ orthogroups are underpowered for a strong evolutionary selection-imprint assertion.  Thus the paper may report a controlled correlation surface, not an evolutionary selection mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this chapter licenses translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  A $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless a same-coordinate $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step survives the declared controls first.  Even then, a downstream protein-abundance association would remain a controlled statistical mediation readback unless a distinct layer-matched contact supplied the missing biological measurement.  In the present finite table, the selection-imprint gate is false, so the chapter stops at a codon-bias--expression correlation and records the stronger interpretation as unsupported.
