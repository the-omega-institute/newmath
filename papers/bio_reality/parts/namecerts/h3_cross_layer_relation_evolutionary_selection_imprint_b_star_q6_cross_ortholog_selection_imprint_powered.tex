\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Audit for B$^*_{Q6}$ Residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint-b-star-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates carry a cross-ortholog selection imprint rather than only a codon-bias or expression-associated residual. The finite table has $240$ rows, arranged as $80$ single-copy fungal orthogroups across $3$ species. The source surface is explicitly bounded: orthology comes from OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level $4751$, coding sequences come from Ensembl Fungi release $63$, and abundance is read from PaxDb v6 integrated ppm. The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional B$^*_{Q6}$ residual list
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The tested readback is not a free residual-to-protein declaration. A positive evolutionary-selection statement would require an imprint statistic that survives cross-ortholog controls and clade withholding, and it would still remain only an imprint criterion unless a separate biological contact supplied the molecular selection mechanism.

\paragraph{Gate statistic.}
The registered run completed with status \emph{passed}, where passing means that the finite offline experiment ran to a bounded verdict, not that the strong biological claim was supported. The evaluated selection-imprint gate returned
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\text{-}clade\text{-}out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed in-sample improvement is below the finite null threshold, and the leave-clade-out value changes sign. The gate value for positive selection imprint is therefore false. The honest finite verdict is \emph{codon-bias expression correlation}: the rows detect a statistical association in the codon-bias and abundance surface, but the stronger cross-ortholog selection-imprint reading is not licensed by this run.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and abundance contacts.}
The negative imprint verdict is consistent with the broader bounded-contact picture. The same B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family has a modeled tAI coupling contact over $3$ organisms with $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, but that run explicitly states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling or proteomics validation. A separate protein-abundance mediation surface over $12$ organisms produced positive statistical mediated fractions, for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human, with joined protein counts including $5815$ yeast proteins, $3739$ \emph{E. coli} proteins, and $19053$ human proteins. Those mediation numbers are useful finite readouts of a controlled $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition, but they do not repair the failed cross-ortholog selection-imprint gate here. They show that codon residuals can enter bounded translation or protein-abundance association surfaces; they do not prove that the fungal ortholog pattern is a selected evolutionary mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity and positive controls.}
The coordinate family is not empty or arbitrary. In the structural-order axis audit, the dominant coordinate was $f_3$-stress, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$; the run reported exactly one significant axis. In the locked genetic-code table contact, the independent replication and boundary-probe tests each had $40$ successes out of $40$ trials with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, while the negative-control and critical-ablation panels each had $20$ trials with $0$ unexpected successes. These facts support that the residual coordinates have finite mathematical and code-table contact, but they do not convert the present fungal ortholog audit into a positive selection-imprint result.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The finite conclusion is deliberately narrower than a biological mechanism claim. PaxDb ppm are integrated across studies and species rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell measurements; failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins non-randomly; and $80$ orthogroups is underpowered for a strong evolutionary selection-imprint conclusion. Therefore this section records a bounded association result with a negative strong gate: B$^*_{Q6}$ residuals reach the level of codon-bias expression correlation on this surface, but not a supported evolutionary selection imprint. No translation realization, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, mechanism claim, organismal phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this table unless an independent layer-matched contact supplies that specific readout and the corresponding controlled statistic is positive.
