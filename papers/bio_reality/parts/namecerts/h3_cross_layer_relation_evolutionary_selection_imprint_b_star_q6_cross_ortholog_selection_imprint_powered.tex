\paragraph{Verdict.}
The supported statement is that, on this finite three-species ortholog surface, $B^\star_{Q6}$ residual information is compatible with a codon-bias expression correlation, but the registered positive selection-imprint criterion fails.  The decisive failure is not merely small sample size: the in-sample improvement $0.03250515015613468$ stays below the largest null-envelope value $0.05427157066522958$, and the leave-clade-out value is negative.  The data therefore do not justify saying that a residual coordinate has crossed from codon-bias association into evolutionary selection imprint.
