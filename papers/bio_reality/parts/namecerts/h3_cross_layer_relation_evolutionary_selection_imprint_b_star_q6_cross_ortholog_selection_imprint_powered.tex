\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Test for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The selection-imprint record is evaluated on a finite cross-ortholog table rather than on an unconstrained biological narrative.  The rows are single-copy fungal orthogroup observations with a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate block, organism labels, coding-sequence controls, and an integrated protein-abundance readout.  The registered run used OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level 4751 for the orthogroup surface, Ensembl Fungi release 63 for coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm for protein abundance.  The joined table contains $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species.  Thus the evaluated object is a small finite matrix with exactly three organism levels, not a population-scale evolutionary panel.

\paragraph{Coordinate and controls.}
The admissible $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates are residual codon coordinates after amino-acid quotienting and simpler baseline controls.  The related translation-readout computation explicitly lists the available nine projected coordinates:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In this chapter they are not promoted to direct protein mechanisms.  They enter only as finite residual coordinates whose statistical imprint is tested after organism, sequence, and abundance joins have already been fixed.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is therefore not treated as sufficient evidence for protein realization; the stricter route would require a surviving translation readout $Q_i \to T_j$ and then a downstream matched protein readout $T_j \to P_k$.

\paragraph{Decision statistic.}
The registered gate compares a positive selection-imprint statistic against a finite null threshold and a leave-clade-out check.  The observed cross-ortholog improvement was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\text{-}clade\text{-}out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite table therefore does not pass the positive selection-imprint gate: the observed $\Delta R^2$ is below the null $95\%$ reference value, and the leave-clade-out statistic is slightly negative.  The run status is nevertheless successful in the computational sense: the registered offline experiment completed and returned a bounded verdict.  The supported finite-row conclusion is codon-bias--expression correlation, not a strong cross-ortholog evolutionary selection imprint.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein mediation.}
The broader $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ program contains translation and protein-abundance routes, but those routes must remain separated from the ortholog imprint verdict.  A modeled-tAI contact on three organisms reported $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$, and also recorded that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival when at least $40$ matched species are required.  A separate protein-abundance mediation run joined twelve organisms and computed positive statistical mediation fractions for the joint nine-coordinate block, for example $0.9491551041216973$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Those numbers show that bounded translation-conditioned protein-abundance decompositions exist elsewhere in the finite evidence ledger, but they do not rescue this ortholog selection-imprint test.  The present row set has only $80$ orthogroups and $3$ species, and its own positive-imprint gate is false.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The honest readback is therefore the following finite statement.  On the $80$-orthogroup, $240$-row, $3$-species fungal table, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block carries a codon-bias--expression correlation signal under the registered calculation, while the stronger evolutionary selection-imprint claim is not supported.  The evaluated gate records $positive\_selection\_imprint=false$ and $claim\_supported=false$.  The result is a bounded negative for the stronger selection statement, not a nullification of all codon-bias biology and not a permission to infer a molecular cause.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter does not claim translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, adaptation as a population law, or universality.  PaxDb ppm values are integrated across studies and species rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell measurements; failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins non-randomly; and a positive residual association would still be only an imprint criterion, not proof of the molecular selection process.  Because the actual finite gate is negative for the strong imprint claim, every higher-layer interpretation remains outside the result unless an independent layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and its own controlled statistic.
