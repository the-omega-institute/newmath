\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Audit for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A translation entry $t_j$ is allowed to be modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and the downstream $p_k$ coordinate is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein arrow.  It is a controlled matrix of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance remain controls rather than promoted coordinates.

\paragraph{Selection-imprint experiment.}
The cross-ortholog audit used OrthoDB v12 Fungi level 4751 single-copy orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release 63 coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm abundance.  The registered offline run completed with experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_cross\_ortholog\_selection\_imprint\_powered} and run id \texttt{3998a6159653}.  The finite table contained $80$ orthogroups, $3$ species, and $240$ rows.  The evaluated positive-imprint gate returned
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\mbox{-}clade\mbox{-}out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
\max(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95}) &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since the observed held-out and null-calibrated criteria do not pass the positive selection-imprint gate, the Boolean call is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{positive\_selection\_imprint}=\mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The bounded biological verdict is consequently \texttt{codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation}, not a powered evolutionary-selection imprint.

The negative selection-imprint result is not the same as absence of every lower-layer association.  A separate translation-mediated protein-abundance run computed the same nine residual coordinates across $12$ organisms. In that mediation decomposition, all reported $M^{QTP}$ fractions lay in the unit interval; examples include $0.9491551041216973$ for yeast, $0.612922252793667$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, and $0.01850668564589326$ for \textit{S. solfataricus}.  Those values are statistical decompositions using modeled per-protein tAI and PaxDb abundance; they do not by themselves establish evolutionary selection.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
A modeled-tAI survival contact was also computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights for $3$ organisms.  It produced $81$ entries above threshold with threshold $0.01$, maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, and no zero raw-$W$ fallback for human or yeast.  However, the organism count was only $3$, while the registered powered-survival condition requires at least $40$ matched species.  Thus this contact is a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not a measured ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  The row also protects the distinction between modeled translation weights and observed ribosome occupancy.

These neighboring results support a conservative reading: local residual associations and bounded readouts can be present, while the stronger cross-ortholog selection-imprint proposition remains unsupported.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore records an honest negative for the strong claim.  A $Q_i\to P_k$ abundance association cannot be read as protein realization unless the corresponding $Q_i\to T_j$ translation step is present and survives the declared controls.  PaxDb integrated ppm values are pooled across studies and species, failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins non-randomly, and the $80$-orthogroup table is underpowered for a strong evolutionary-selection conclusion.  No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and the controlled statistic is recomputed on that contact.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{claim\_supported} &= \mathrm{false},\\
\mathrm{verdict} &= \mathrm{codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $B^*_{Q6}$ remains admissible here as a bounded codon-bias and expression-correlation signal with translation-readout context, but it does not enter the paper as a powered cross-ortholog evolutionary selection-imprint result.
