\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave an evolutionary selection imprint across matched fungal ortholog rows.  The evaluated table has $80$ single-copy orthogroups, $3$ species, and $240$ organism--orthogroup rows.  The row source is OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level 4751, with coding sequences from Ensembl Fungi release 63 and protein abundance read back from PaxDb v6 integrated ppm.  The tested surface is therefore not a perturbation experiment and not a matched-condition molecular assay; it is a finite comparative table in which coding-sequence residual coordinates, orthogroup membership, species identity, and integrated protein-abundance ranks are placed in the same regression ledger.

The residual-coordinate panel is the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ basis used by the companion translation-readout calculations: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. Amino-acid sequence, organism, coding-sequence length, GC3, and the coordinate-support density baseline remain controls or stratifying context rather than biological conclusions.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The honest biological reading is narrow.  The table detects a codon-bias/expression correlation in a finite fungal ortholog panel, not a demonstrated selection mechanism.  The result is compatible with the weaker statement that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can align with protein-abundance-associated coding-sequence variation in this source surface.  It does not establish that selection acted on the residual coordinates, because the transfer check is negative and the finite null envelope is not beaten.

This boundary is strengthened by the surrounding translation and protein-abundance audits.  A modeled-tAI contact exists for $3$ organisms in the earlier translation-survival calculation, with $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, but that result was explicitly underpowered for a powered survival claim because $3$ organisms are fewer than the required $40$ matched species.  A larger protein-abundance mediation table exists elsewhere with $12$ organisms and per-organism joined protein counts ranging from $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ for mouse; in that setting, the statistical mediation fraction ranges from $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast.  Those numbers show that protein-abundance mediation can be computed on larger finite surfaces, but they do not turn this cross-ortholog fungal selection-imprint audit into a positive evolutionary result.

\paragraph{Coordinate-specific context.}
The same coordinate family has a separate structural-order identity audit in which $f3\_stress$ is the only fold-level sign-flip survivor, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip probability $0.03125$.  That context explains why a residual coordinate can be biologically interesting without being promoted into an evolutionary selection law.  Here the cross-ortholog gate is stricter: a positive in-sample expression association is insufficient when the leave-clade-out statistic is $-0.0021477729853302424$ and the observed $\Delta R^2$ does not exceed the null95 boundary.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows from this chapter alone.
