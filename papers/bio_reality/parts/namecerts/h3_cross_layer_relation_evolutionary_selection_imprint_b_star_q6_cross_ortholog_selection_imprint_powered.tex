\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record asks whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave a cross-ortholog imprint strong enough to be read as an evolutionary selection signal, rather than merely as a codon-bias or expression-correlated residual.  The finite row surface is the set of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, $t_j$ is a translation readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  A direct residual-to-protein association is therefore not admissible as a selection-imprint statement unless the residual first survives a translation-readout step.  The cross-ortholog audit used OrthoDB v12 Fungi level 4751 single-copy orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release 63 coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm abundance.  The finite table contained $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species.

\paragraph{Gate statistic.}
The tested selection-imprint criterion compared the residual-improved model against its controlled alternatives and against the finite null boundary.  The registered run completed with status \emph{passed}, but the support flag was false.  Its numerical gate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{OG} &= 80,\\
 n_{rows} &= 240,\\
 n_{species} &= 3,\\
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
 \Delta L_{bits} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
 \Delta R^2_{leave\text{-}clade\text{-}out} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
 \max \Delta R^2_{null,95} &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since the observed $\Delta R^2$ was below the null $95\%$ boundary and the leave-clade-out increment was negative, the positive selection-imprint gate was not met.  The finite conclusion is therefore codon-bias/expression correlation, not a cross-ortholog evolutionary selection imprint.  The phrase \emph{passed} names successful completion of the registered offline audit and a bounded verdict; it does not mean that the biological claim was supported.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein readouts.}
The negative imprint verdict is consistent with the stricter layer discipline imposed by the translation-mediated protein-abundance audit.  In the broader matched protein-abundance matrix, twelve organism-level surfaces were available, with joined protein counts $4042$, $11661$, $14410$, $13295$, $3739$, $12418$, $19053$, $19395$, $4129$, $13322$, $5815$, and $1656$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, \emph{Danio rerio}, \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, \emph{Escherichia coli}, \emph{Gallus gallus}, human, mouse, \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, rat, yeast, and \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Those rows yielded positive statistical mediation fractions in the bounded modeled-tAI sense, for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ in \emph{C. elegans}, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.  These values show that the residual family can be organized into a controlled $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ decomposition, but the decomposition is statistical and cross-sectional.  It does not by itself identify a historical selection mechanism.

\paragraph{Rank and readout context.}
The modeled-tAI survival precursor used only $3$ organisms, even though the powered survival condition requires at least $40$ matched species.  It computed $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but the same record labels the result as modeled-tAI bounded coupling rather than ribosome-seq or proteomics validation.  Separately, the structural-order axis audit found that $f_3$-stress was the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate, with sign-flip probability $0.03125$ and weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$.  The observable-execution decomposition also reported an execution subblock in yeast over $3289$ genes.  These positive lower-layer and adjacent-layer facts justify keeping the residual basis active as a biological readout surface.  They do not override the cross-ortholog imprint failure, because the imprint test asks for stability under clade withholding and null comparison across ortholog rows.

\paragraph{Controls and nonpromotion.}
The selection-imprint audit is controlled by the same separation principle used throughout this residual family: amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance remain controls, not promoted coordinates.  The yeast structure-mediation run illustrates why this matters.  In a five-way join of $4759$ genes, the reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$ and the overall join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$; the decomposition gave $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  That record supports a controlled variance partition, not a causal path.  The same convention is used here: a residual association may motivate a finite audit, but it is not elevated to selection unless the registered imprint gate is passed.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The chapter verdict is negative for the strong claim and positive only for the weaker finite observation.  The run supports the statement that the sampled fungal ortholog surface contains a codon-bias/expression-correlated residual signal.  It does not support the statement that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ carries a powered cross-ortholog evolutionary selection imprint.  The local decision can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468 < 0.05427157066522958 = \max \Delta R^2_{null,95},\\
 \Delta R^2_{leave\text{-}clade\text{-}out} &= -0.0021477729853302424 < 0,\\
 positive\ selection\ imprint &= false.
\end{aligned}
$$
The admissible readback is therefore a bounded codon-bias/expression-correlation finding on $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows.  No translation realization, protein realization, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, causal mechanism claim, organismal-phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this audit.  Each such statement would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The earlier genetic-code table checks, including the leave-one-codon-out module-classification target and exact one-sided binomial values $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ for $40$ successes out of $40$, establish only the lower codon-geometry contact; they do not supply the missing cross-ortholog selection-imprint evidence.
