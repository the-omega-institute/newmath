\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection Imprint for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:biorreality-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint-bstar-q6}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave a cross-ortholog imprint that is stronger than a bounded codon-bias--expression correlation.  The finite panel contains $80$ single-copy fungal orthogroups across $3$ species, hence $240$ joined rows.  The row source is OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level $4751$ for single-copy orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release $63$ for coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm for protein abundance.  The evaluated object is not a free residual-to-selection assertion: each row is read with amino-acid sequence context, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and the available abundance readout kept as controls or bounded measurement surfaces rather than promoted coordinates.

\paragraph{Coordinate list.}
The same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate family used by the surrounding translation and protein-abundance audits is the nine-coordinate list
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \\n&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \\n&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is therefore not accepted as a molecular realization claim unless the same coordinate has first survived a controlled translation readout.  The finite ortholog panel is allowed to report an imprint statistic, but it is not allowed to infer translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universality from the imprint alone.

\paragraph{Selection-imprint gate.}
The strong imprint gate compared the observed improvement against the declared null envelope and leave-clade-out stability check.  The finite numbers were
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{species}} &= 3, \\
 n_{\mathrm{orthogroups}} &= 80, \\
 n_{\mathrm{rows}} &= 240, \\
 \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{observed}} &= 0.03250515015613468, \\
 \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\ clade\ out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424, \\
 \max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.05427157066522958, \\
 \Delta \mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed $\Delta R^2$ is positive, but it remains below the null $95\%$ maximum and reverses under the leave-clade-out diagnostic.  The registered verdict is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{positive\_selection\_imprint} &= \mathrm{false}, \\
\mathrm{claim\_supported} &= \mathrm{false}, \\
\mathrm{verdict} &= \mathrm{codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite panel supports only a bounded codon-bias--expression correlation.  It does not support the stronger claim that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have been shown to carry an evolutionary selection imprint across orthologs.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated protein abundance.}
A separate protein-abundance mediation audit computed a twelve-organism $Q \to T \to P$ surface using modeled per-protein tAI as the translation readout $T$ and log$_{10}$ PaxDb abundance as $P$.  That audit found positive statistical mediation fractions for multiple organisms, for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.6084594553474505$ in \textit{Bacillus subtilis} 168, and $0.27206289160119157$ in human.  Those values are useful as bounded mediation diagnostics, not as causal statements.  They also do not rescue the present cross-ortholog selection-imprint test, because the present gate asks for a stronger cross-species imprint and requires stability beyond the null and leave-clade-out checks.  The finite outcome is therefore intentionally asymmetric: bounded translation/protein-abundance mediation may exist in other panels, while the present fungal ortholog imprint remains below the support threshold.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The translation-readout contact available for the residual coordinates includes modeled tAI weights from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  In the small modeled-tAI survival matrix, $3$ organisms were used, all standard amino-acid families were represented, and $81$ entries exceeded the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That contact is explicitly underpowered for a powered survival claim because $3$ organisms are below the declared requirement of at least $40$ matched species.  Consequently, the modeled-tAI contact can bound a translation-adaptation readout, but it cannot by itself turn the present cross-ortholog panel into measured ribosome occupancy, a protein mechanism, or an evolutionary law.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
The negative result is a scoped empirical statement, not a biological irrelevance theorem.  The finite data show that this particular $80$-orthogroup, $240$-row fungal panel produces a codon-bias--expression correlation but does not pass the positive selection-imprint gate.  PaxDb ppm values are integrated across studies and species rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell measurements; failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins non-randomly; and a positive residual, even when present, would be an imprint criterion rather than direct proof of the underlying molecular selection process.  Since $n_{\mathrm{orthogroups}}=80$ is underpowered for the strong evolutionary selection-imprint claim, the appropriate readback is a bounded negative for that strong claim, not a rejection of all possible $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ biological contacts.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The chapter therefore records a completed finite-row audit with a bounded verdict.  The allowed conclusion is that the tested cross-ortholog surface reaches codon-bias--expression correlation status and fails the stronger positive selection-imprint criterion.  No translation realization, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this result unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and its own controlled statistic.
