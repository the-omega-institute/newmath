\subsection{Cross-Ortholog Selection-Imprint Audit for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:biorality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record tested here is a finite cross-species ortholog surface rather than an unrestricted evolutionary law.  The rows are single-copy fungal orthogroup entries joined across three species, with $80$ orthogroups and $240$ total organism--orthogroup rows.  Each row carries coding-sequence residual coordinates from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ basis, matched protein-abundance readback from PaxDb integrated ppm, and the standard covariates needed to keep amino-acid content, organism identity, GC3, coding length, and the $M$-density baseline from being reinterpreted as residual coordinates.  The empirical source was OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level $4751$ for single-copy orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release $63$ for coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm for abundance.  The intended question was whether the residual basis leaves a cross-ortholog imprint stronger than simpler expression/codon-bias structure, not whether a molecular selection mechanism has been directly observed.

\paragraph{Gate statistic.}
The registered run completed with experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_cross\_ortholog\_selection\_imprint\_powered} and run id \texttt{3998a6159653}.  Its positive-imprint gate returned
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{species}} &= 3,\\
 n_{\mathrm{orthogroups}} &= 80,\\
 n_{\mathrm{rows}} &= 240,\\
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
 \Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
 \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\ clade\ out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
 \max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
The in-sample residual increment is positive, but it is below the $95\%$ null envelope and becomes slightly negative under leave-clade-out evaluation.  Consequently the strong selection-imprint predicate is false for this finite surface.  The accepted readback is the weaker verdict \texttt{codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation}: the joined data show a codon-bias/expression association, while the cross-ortholog selection-imprint claim is not supported.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein readouts.}
The same residual family has bounded contacts at lower layers, but those contacts do not rescue this imprint gate.  A modeled tAI survival audit computed nine residual coordinates, namely \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  In that modeled tAI contact, only $3$ organisms were present, the contact was explicitly labelled as underpowered for powered survival, and the matrix nevertheless had $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  A separate protein-abundance mediation audit used $12$ organism panels and found positive statistical mediation fractions in the interval $[0,1]$ for all listed organisms, including $0.9491551041216973$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse.  Those facts establish bounded statistical readouts on their own surfaces.  They do not convert the present $3$-species, $80$-orthogroup imprint audit into evidence for evolutionary selection.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite-row outcome is a negative result for the strong claim and a positive result only for a weaker association class.  In symbols, the tested gate separates the observed residual increment from the selection-imprint predicate as
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468 < 0.05427157066522958 = \max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95},\\
 \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\ clade\ out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424 < 0,\\
 \mathrm{positive\ selection\ imprint} &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the scientifically honest statement is that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates participate in a codon-bias/expression correlation on this joined fungal surface, while the powered cross-ortholog selection-imprint claim remains unestablished.  The negative gate is informative: it prevents a direct residual-to-selection narrative when the clade-held-out statistic fails and the null envelope is not exceeded.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows from this audit unless a separate translation contact supplies matched ribosome-occupancy, translation-efficiency, or explicitly bounded modeled-readout rows.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the codon residuals, the PaxDb abundance association, the positive in-sample $\Delta R^2$, or the protein-abundance wording.  PaxDb ppm values are integrated across studies and species rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell measurements; failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation can remove proteins non-randomly; and a residual statistic, even when positive, is an imprint criterion rather than direct observation of selection.  The row count $n_{\mathrm{orthogroups}}=80$ is also underpowered for a strong evolutionary-selection claim.  The chapter therefore records a bounded empirical contact with an honest negative for selection imprint and a retained lower-strength codon-bias/expression verdict.
