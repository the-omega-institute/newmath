\subsection{Cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:biorality-q6-cross-ortholog-selection-imprint}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The selection-imprint test is posed on a finite cross-ortholog surface whose rows join fungal single-copy orthogroups, coding sequences, residual codon coordinates, and integrated protein-abundance readouts. The evaluated panel contains $80$ orthogroups, $3$ species, and $240$ joined rows. The source declaration is OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level $4751$ for single-copy orthogroups, Ensembl Fungi release $63$ for coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated ppm for abundance. The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual list
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The biological question is not whether a codon-bias residual can correlate with expression. It is whether the residual carries a cross-ortholog imprint strong enough to pass the positive selection-imprint gate after the simpler baselines and clade-holdout check have had a chance to absorb the signal.

\paragraph{Gate calculation.}
The registered offline run completed with status \emph{passed}, where passing means that the finite audit ran to a bounded verdict rather than that the biological claim was supported. The fitted signal gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\mbox{-}clade\mbox{-}out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.05427157066522958.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-imprint predicate is therefore false: $0.03250515015613468$ is below the reported null $95\%$ ceiling $0.05427157066522958$, and the leave-clade-out value is slightly negative. The finite row set is large enough to report a codon-bias/expression correlation, but it is not large enough to support the stronger evolutionary selection-imprint reading. The run records the honest bounded verdict as \emph{codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation}, not as cross-ortholog selection.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance contacts.}
The same residual family has separate translation-facing and protein-facing audits, and those do not license promotion of this ortholog result. In the modeled-tAI survival calculation, only $3$ organisms were used for the initial bounded readout; the matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$, but the run itself states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that at least $40$ matched species would be needed for a powered survival request. In a distinct cross-organism protein-abundance mediation run, the joined protein counts included $5815$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $19053$ for human, and $19395$ for mouse; the yeast statistical mediation fraction was $0.9491551041216973$, while the human fraction was $0.27206289160119157$. Those numbers show that the residual coordinates can be evaluated against translation-weighted abundance surfaces, but they do not turn the $80$-orthogroup fungal imprint audit into evidence for selection. The structural-order axis audit likewise singled out $f_3$-stress with sign-flip $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, but that is an axis-identity result, not a population-genetic mechanism.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The admissible reading is narrow. On this finite panel, $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals retain a measurable codon-bias/expression relation, and the description-length statistic is favorable in-sample. The stronger claim fails because the effect does not clear the null envelope and does not persist under leave-clade-out evaluation. The chapter therefore records a negative result for the proposed evolutionary selection imprint while preserving the weaker empirical contact: residual codon structure can covary with integrated abundance in the assembled fungal ortholog table.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, folding fact, physical admissibility statement, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal selection mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from this audit. PaxDb ppm are integrated across studies and species rather than matched-condition absolute per-cell protein measurements; failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation may remove proteins non-randomly; and a positive statistical residual would at most be an imprint criterion, not direct proof of the molecular process. Here the positive criterion is not met. Any later claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality must be supplied by a separate layer-matched measurement and its own finite statistic.
