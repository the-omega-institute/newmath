\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates leave a cross-ortholog imprint that is stronger than ordinary expression-linked codon bias.  The finite surface is a table of $240$ rows formed from $80$ single-copy fungal orthogroups across $3$ species.  Each row carries an orthogroup identifier, a species identifier, a coding-sequence residual vector in the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family, matched abundance information from PaxDb integrated ppm, and control slots for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline.  The source panel is OrthoDB v12 at Fungi level 4751, Ensembl Fungi release 63 coding sequences, and PaxDb v6 integrated abundance.  The tested object is therefore finite and keyed: no statement is made about all orthologs, all fungi, or all biological conditions.

$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The present cross-ortholog test is narrower: it asks whether the residual signal, after controls, exceeds a selection-imprint gate on the finite orthogroup panel.

\paragraph{Selection-imprint statistic.}
The positive gate compares the residual improvement to a finite null envelope and a clade-holdout check.  The observed residual increment was positive in ordinary in-sample fit,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}} &= -258.6010084301837.
\end{aligned}
$$
A negative value of $\Delta L_{\mathrm{bits}}$ records a shorter description under the fitted residual model on the observed table.  This alone is not enough for the stronger imprint claim, because a selection-imprint reading must also clear the finite null boundary and remain stable when clade structure is withheld.

The decisive finite comparisons were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{observed}} &= 0.03250515015613468,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{null},95\%\max} &= 0.05427157066522958,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{leave\text{-}clade\text{-}out}} &= -0.0021477729853302424.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed increment did not exceed the recorded $95\%$ null maximum, and the leave-clade-out increment changed sign.  The finite verdict is that the table supports a codon-bias/expression correlation, not a powered evolutionary selection-imprint claim.  In the registered output this is recorded as $claim\_supported=false$, with verdict $codon\_bias\_expression\_correlation$.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The present chapter allows only the following readback: on the $80$-orthogroup, $240$-row, $3$-species panel, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has an in-sample association with expression-linked codon bias, but the stronger cross-ortholog evolutionary selection-imprint criterion fails.  The failure is not a claim that synonymous residuals are biologically irrelevant; it is a bounded verdict about this finite gate.

No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation law, or universality follows from this table.  Translation requires a separate translation readout such as organism-matched tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency with the stated controls.  Protein realization requires a downstream matched protein-abundance contact after the translation step is present.  Folding, localization, stability, function, phenotype, and mechanism each require their own layer-matched measurements.  PaxDb integrated ppm are aggregated across studies and species, not matched-condition absolute per-cell counts; failed or missing cross-reference reconciliation may remove proteins non-randomly; and a positive residual association is at most an imprint criterion, not direct proof of the molecular selection process.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite row test ran to completion and produced a bounded negative for the strong claim.  The proper scientific sentence is: the observed $B^*_{Q6}$ cross-ortholog residual pattern is compatible with codon-bias/expression correlation on this finite fungal panel, but it does not pass the registered positive selection-imprint gate because $0.03250515015613468 < 0.05427157066522958$ and the leave-clade-out increment is $-0.0021477729853302424$.  The chapter therefore records a constrained association result and keeps evolutionary selection imprint, translation mechanism, protein realization, structure, function, phenotype, and universal-law readings outside the warranted conclusion.
