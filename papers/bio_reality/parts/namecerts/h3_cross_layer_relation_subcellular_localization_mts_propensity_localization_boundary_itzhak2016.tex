\subsection{Mitochondrial-localization Boundary for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Surface}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-mts-localization-boundary-itzhak2016}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite contact.}
This record fixes a localization-level contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis without promoting that contact to a mechanism.  The finite panel is the same-CDS localization surface obtained after joining coding-sequence residual coordinates to the Itzhak-style subcellular-localization readout used for mitochondrial assignment.  The verified run contains $8696$ joined protein-coding rows, of which $657$ are marked as mitochondrial-positive rows.  The row surface was evaluated with $2000$ permutations, and the recorded runtime of the finite computation was $11.034382$ seconds.  Thus the empirical object available here is not a protein-traffic law, but a bounded binary localization panel with a mitochondrial subset of size
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{joined}} &= 8696,\\
 n_{\mathrm{mito}} &= 657,\\
 n_{\mathrm{nonmito}} &= 8696-657=8039,\\
 \frac{n_{\mathrm{mito}}}{n_{\mathrm{joined}}} &\approx 0.07555.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row imbalance is part of the claim: a localization association must be read against a mitochondrial-positive fraction of about $7.56\%$, not against a balanced two-class experiment.

\paragraph{Coordinate surface.}
The tested residual coordinate family is the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface used throughout the cross-layer packet:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_{AAA},\ Arg\_{AGR},\ Ile\_{AUA},\ Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3\_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control side of the surrounding residual program uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the projected $Q$ vectors.  In the measured-translation-efficiency contact, the same family is additionally bounded by matched mRNA abundance, with four organism-specific joins: $n=13140$ for zebrafish, $n=3415$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for human, and $n=5128$ for budding yeast, each with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ against $24$ listed control columns.  Those translation-efficiency rows are relevant only as boundary context: they do not turn the present localization panel into a translation or protein-abundance measurement.

\paragraph{Relation tested.}
The localization question is whether any $Q_i$ coordinate leaves a finite, controlled trace on a mitochondrial-localization readout after the ordinary sequence and composition controls have been respected.  The measurable relation has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QL}_i &= \Delta\mathcal{R}\bigl(L_{\mathrm{mito}}\ ;\ Q_i\mid Z,\mathcal{B}\bigr),\\
L_{\mathrm{mito}} &\in \{0,1\},\\
Z&=\{\log(\mathrm{length}),\mathrm{AA\ composition},\mathrm{GC3},M\text{-density},\mathrm{organism\ when\ pooled}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\mathcal{B}$ denotes the simpler baseline family against which a residual-coordinate gain must be compared.  A positive entry would mean only that the coordinate improves the finite localization readback relative to those controls on this joined panel.  A non-positive entry would mean that the coordinate is compressed away at this localization boundary.  The verified facts supplied for this record certify the contact size, the mitochondrial class size, and the permutation count; they do not by themselves supply a coordinate-level $S^{QL}_i$ list, a right-tail permutation $p$-value, a module assignment, or a fitted coefficient table.  Consequently, the present chapter records a localization boundary with a real joined surface, while withholding any stronger coordinate-survival assertion unless the corresponding per-coordinate statistics are attached.

\paragraph{Cross-layer context.}
The localization boundary sits downstream of several already computed descriptive cross-layer contacts.  For measured translation efficiency, the modeled-complement absorption by measured TE is small in all four reported organisms: $0.01759429001506974$ in zebrafish, $0.0010982956489726373$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0028947149257963897$ in human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ in budding yeast.  For measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI, the absorbed fractions are $0.020817102845637092$ in zebrafish, $0.00005426229300865148$ in human, and $0.001530747712165342$ in budding yeast.  For yeast turnover, the absorbed fraction is $0.0009086031889966327$ on $3640$ joined turnover rows.  These numbers motivate looking at localization as a separate finite contact, but they do not establish localization survival.  Each contact has its own row universe, identifier join, rank condition, and readout semantics.

\paragraph{Localization-specific reading.}
The mitochondrial panel is therefore a boundary test rather than a claim of trafficking mechanism.  A mitochondrial-positive subset of $657$ rows is large enough to support a finite permutation procedure, and the recorded $2000$ permutations show that the computation was actually run rather than merely named.  However, the data supplied here stop before the decisive per-coordinate readout.  The chapter may state that the localization contact exists, that it is joined to $8696$ coding rows, and that mitochondrial membership is present as a finite binary label.  It may not state that $K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $f3\_{stress}$, or any other member of $Q$ survives the localization boundary unless a coordinate-resolved score and its null comparison are supplied.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
Nothing in this localization surface licenses a translation claim, a folding claim, a physical-admissibility claim, a molecular-function claim, a trafficking mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Mitochondrial assignment is a compartment readout.  It is not ribosome occupancy, not translation efficiency, not mature protein abundance, not a folded-state assay, not a perturbation experiment, and not a causal mechanism.  Even if a future $S^{QL}_i$ entry is positive on the $8696$-row panel, the valid conclusion would be limited to a finite association between one residual coordinate and the mitochondrial-localization label under the declared controls.  Any assertion about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality must come from a separate layer-matched contact with its own finite statistic.

\paragraph{Status.}
The current finite record is ready as a localization-boundary chapter because the joined-row count, mitochondrial-positive count, permutation count, and runtime are concrete.  It remains intentionally noncommittal about coordinate-level localization survival.  The scientifically correct reading is: the Itzhak-style mitochondrial localization contact has been attached to a finite $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ row surface, but the supplied verified facts support only the existence and size of that boundary, not a positive residual-coordinate localization theorem.
