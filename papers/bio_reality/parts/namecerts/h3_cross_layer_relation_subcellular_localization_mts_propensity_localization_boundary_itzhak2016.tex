The null calculation used $2000$ permutations and completed in $11.034382$ seconds.  Thus the observed surface is not a verbal association between codon geometry and localization; it is the finite binary-response table
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{joined}} &= 8696,\\
N_{\mathrm{mito}} &= 657,\\
N_{\mathrm{nonmito}} &= 8696-657=8039,\\
B_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 2000.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
This imbalance matters mathematically: any localization statistic on this contact must be judged against a sparse positive class, not against a balanced two-class table.

\paragraph{Controlled interpretation.}
Let $Q_i$ denote one residual coordinate and let $L$ denote the mitochondrial localization indicator on the joined localization panel.  The admissible finite question is whether a residualized coordinate retains descriptive association with $L$ after the declared covariates and after the upstream translation-readout boundary have been respected.  A schematic statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QL}_{i,\mathrm{mito}}
  &= \Delta\mathcal{D}\bigl(L\mid Z, Q_i\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ must include the local controls used for codon-derived surfaces: amino-acid composition, organism where applicable, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and abundance or translation controls when the claim is translation-conditioned.  The finite numbers recorded here certify only that the localization table and permutation budget exist at the stated scale.  They do not certify a positive value of $S^{QL}_{i,\mathrm{mito}}$, because no coordinate-specific localization score, permutation $p$-value, effect-size table, or translation-conditioned attenuation table is present in this finite row.

\paragraph{Localization verdict.}
The finite localization contact is admissible as a bounded readout surface: $8696$ joined rows, $657$ mitochondrial positives, and $2000$ permutations are enough to define a non-vacuous finite test bed.  The current record, however, stops at the boundary.  It establishes that a mitochondrial-localization question can be posed against the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates on an Itzhak-style localization surface, not that any coordinate survives the controls.  The correct local statement is therefore conditional:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists i\; S^{QL}_{i,\mathrm{mito}}>0
&\quad\text{would support a bounded localization readback for coordinate }Q_i,\\
\forall i\; S^{QL}_{i,\mathrm{mito}}\le 0
&\quad\text{would leave the residual basis outside this measured localization contact.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Because the present row contains no coordinate-level $S^{QL}$ list and no reported localization permutation $p$-values, the chapter records a testable localization boundary rather than a positive localization result.

No translation claim follows from the localization table alone. The table here licenses only a finite localization question with $8696$ joined rows and $657$ mitochondrial-positive rows; all stronger biological readings remain outside the certified surface.
