\paragraph{Finite surface.} This record tests a narrow localization boundary for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The biological object is not a protein mechanism, a targeting pathway, or a universal codon rule.  It is the finite joined surface on which coding-sequence residual coordinates can be placed beside a protein-localization readout and then judged only as a controlled descriptive association.  In the localization run, the joined panel contains $8696$ proteins, of which $657$ are annotated as mitochondrial in the localization surface.  The permutation engine used $2000$ permutations and completed in $11.034382$ seconds.  Thus the observed finite support is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{join}} &= 8696,\\
N_{\mathrm{mito}} &= 657,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 2000.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mitochondrial fraction on the joined surface is therefore approximately $657/8696 \approx 0.07555$.  This number is a row-count property of the matched localization panel, not a mechanistic effect size.

\paragraph{Coordinate boundary.} The codon-side coordinates are the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual axes used throughout the finite readout program: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Their role here is only to provide residual coding-sequence coordinates after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and the $M$-density baseline have been accounted for.  The control design used in the neighboring translation-efficiency computations had $24$ control columns and rank $23$ in each reported organism-specific TE panel, with joined TE row counts $13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Those TE panels are cited here only to fix the upstream boundary: a localization claim cannot be promoted from a codon coordinate unless the relevant translation-readout step is separately joined and controlled.

\paragraph{Localization readout.} The localization surface is interpreted as a protein-level compartment readout.  It is downstream of coding sequence and downstream of translation, so the finite matrix does not license a direct route from $Q_i$ to localization.  The admissible local question is whether the joined panel supports a bounded comparison between $Q_i$ residual coordinates and mitochondrial membership or MTS-propensity labels under the stated controls.  The available finite numbers show that the localization panel is not vacuous: $8696$ joined rows and $657$ mitochondrial rows provide a concrete binary localization surface.  The $2000$-permutation run records that a permutation null was actually executed for this boundary.  What is not contained in the present row summary is a named per-coordinate statistic, a reported $p$-value, a module label, an $R$ list, an $M$ list, or a $\lambda$ value for the localization association.  Consequently the chapter records the localization boundary as computationally instantiated, while keeping the positive biological interpretation restricted to the finite row counts and the existence of the permutation test.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-feature contacts.} The measured-TE contact shows that the same nine-coordinate residual basis can be joined to ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readouts with matched mRNA controls.  In that computation, the measured-TE absorption values were $0.01759429001506974$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.0010982956489726373$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0028947149257963897$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, and $0.0002762783754375593$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  These small descriptive absorptions are useful as a warning for the localization analysis: a downstream localization row must not be read as translation realization merely because it shares the same coding-sequence coordinates.  A separate protein-feature contact also showed sparse and organism-dependent downstream feature behavior.  For example, the feature panel joined $2242$ \emph{Danio rerio} proteins, $18335$ human proteins, and $5682$ yeast proteins; Bonferroni excess appeared for human complex membership, human PTM density, human transmembrane count, and \emph{Danio rerio} PTM density, but not as a stable cross-organism mechanism.  The localization panel is therefore treated with the same discipline: it can report a bounded localization statistic when the statistic is present, but it cannot become a trafficking mechanism or a functional claim by wording alone.

\paragraph{Organism-scale caution.} The broader organism-specific summary also supports a cautious reading.  Across the complete-feature organism set, $12$ organisms were available for the computed meta tests, while the assembled table had $16$ organisms with four needing additional data for the $M^{QTP}$ summary.  The dominant-coordinate contingency test had $n=12$, Pearson chi-square $9.4$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and permutation right-tail probability $0.5814185814185814$.  The $M_{QTP}$ fraction correlations were likewise descriptive rather than decisive: versus GC3, $r=-0.3062616500469447$ with two-sided permutation probability $0.35264735264735264$; versus log protein count, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ with probability $0.913086913086913$; versus log tRNA gene count, $r=0.06029405206037823$ with probability $0.8621378621378621$.  These meta rows do not decide the localization boundary, but they prevent the finite localization panel from being narrated as a domain-general biological law.

\paragraph{Local verdict.} The localization boundary is ready as a finite BioReality chapter because the row surface is concrete: $8696$ joined proteins, $657$ mitochondrial positives, and $2000$ permutations.  The correct mathematical statement is a bounded one.  A localization statistic may be computed on this surface, and its support must be read as a finite controlled readback over the joined rows.  If every controlled coordinate entry is non-positive, then the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis remains outside the measured localization readout for this contact.  If a coordinate entry is positive under the declared controls and permutation null, it still says only that the coordinate survived in this localization surface.  It does not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, trafficking mechanism, protein function, phenotype, causation, or universality.  Each of those claims requires its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
