\subsection{A bounded translation-control readout for the GCN4 U34 switch}
\label{sec:bioreality-gcn4-u34-translation-switch}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The GCN4 U34 modification switch is recorded here as a bounded cross-layer translation-control packet rather than as a completed mechanism claim.  The finite computation attached to the packet kept $5155$ gene rows and reported a runtime of $0.0$ seconds under experiment run $\mathrm{f4e4f5c62558}$.  This is enough to certify that the row filter and packet route are nonempty, but it is not enough by itself to assert that a U34 modification variable explains translation output, protein abundance, folding, function, or phenotype.  The chapter therefore treats the GCN4 surface as a finite readout boundary: a gene-level table exists, but the admissible biological interpretation is restricted to what the table and its declared controls actually measure.

\paragraph{Residual translation coordinate interface.}
The surrounding $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation packet supplies the coordinate discipline used to state the switch.  The powered modeled-tAI run used $40$ organism rows and $9$ synonymous residual coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation readout surface used the same $9$ readout columns after projection to modeled per-codon tAI weights.  The control design had $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after residualizing against amino-acid composition and other declared sequence baselines.  The residualized coordinate relation may be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp} &= q_i - P_{\tau}q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp} &= T_j - P_{Z_T}T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \hbox{only when the residual coordinate improves the controlled translation readout.}
\end{aligned}
$$
For the powered modeled-tAI matrix, the recorded scalar was $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, with $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and a positive-entry threshold of $10^{-12}$.  The rank check is material: the earlier three-organism modeled-tAI table had all $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but that $n=3$ surface was explicitly classified as underpowered and not a biological survival result.

\paragraph{What the GCN4 switch can say.}
Within this chapter, the GCN4 U34 switch can be stated only as a candidate finite translation-control boundary over the $5155$ kept genes.  A legitimate positive claim would require a row-matched statistic showing that the U34 modification or its proxy changes a controlled translation readout after amino-acid composition, organism or batch structure, GC3, CDS length, methionine-density baseline, transcript abundance where needed, and simpler codon-usage baselines have been removed.  In the notation of the packet, the admissible assertion has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(T_j\mid Z_T),\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0,\\
\hbox{rank support} &\not\equiv \hbox{saturated finite fit.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The present GCN4 finite fact records only that $5155$ rows entered the computation.  No finite-row p-value, effect size, regression coefficient, lambda value, module classification, hit rate, measured ribosome-footprint statistic, matched protein-abundance statistic, or U34 perturbation/rescue contrast is present in the supplied record.  Consequently the chapter may preserve the route and the row count, but it may not upgrade the route into a mechanism.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The modelled tAI and stAI readout class is a codon-adaptation weight surface, not an observed ribosome-occupancy surface.  tRNA gene-copy counts are genome-annotation summaries, not charged-tRNA abundance, elongation speed, or perturbation evidence.  Per-organism codon-usage tables summarize sequence composition and sampling choices, not protein production.  Therefore a positive modeled $S^{QT}_{ij}$, even when rank-powered, would remain a bounded translation-readout association unless a separate contact supplies measured translation rows.  The GCN4 U34 packet is still further constrained: the supplied finite fact gives only $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=5155$ and runtime, with no same-row U34 modification measurement tied to translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, physical assay, molecular function, phenotype, or perturbational rescue.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
This record is ready as a scoped BioReality chapter because the finite row count and the surrounding rank-powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation surface can be stated without overclaiming.  Its local mathematical content is the boundary condition: a GCN4 U34 switch claim would break if every controlled $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is nonpositive, if the apparent fit is rank-saturated, or if the packet attempts to infer translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality without a layer-matched measurement.  The current row surface supports a disciplined target for testing; it does not yet support a biological law.
