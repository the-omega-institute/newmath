\subsection{GCN4 U34 Modification as a Bounded Translation-Switch Readout}\label{sec:bioreality-gcn4-u34-q6-translation-switch}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite organism-by-coding-sequence readout problem rather than a direct molecular mechanism claim.  Its coordinate side is the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis after quotienting away amino-acid identity, and its readout side is a bounded translation-weight surface formed from codon usage, tRNA gene-copy annotations, and modeled tAI or stAI weights.  The GCN4 U34 modification switch enters this chapter only through the finite BioReality run identified by the claim id, where the retained coding-sequence surface contains $5155$ genes and the recorded runtime scalar is $0.0$ seconds.  No finite row in the supplied record reports a measured U34 perturbation coefficient, a ribosome-footprint contrast, a GCN4-specific elongation estimate, a p-value, a fitted module label, a hit rate, or a lambda parameter.  Consequently the chapter treats the GCN4/U34 wording as a named translation-control boundary to be tested, not as an established perturbational mechanism.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate rule.}
For each synonymous coordinate $q_i$, only the amino-acid-quotient residual component is allowed to meet the readout layer.  If $P_{\tau}$ denotes projection onto the amino-acid-sequence component, the admissible coordinate is
$$\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp \tau} &= q_i - P_{\tau}q_i .
\end{aligned}$$
This matters because a raw codon-count contrast can report amino-acid composition, GC structure, length, organismal sampling, or a simpler codon-usage baseline rather than a translation-control relation.  The finite basis in the powered modeled-tAI run has $9$ coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These are not asserted to be biological laws.  They are residual labels on a finite matrix after the amino-acid projection has been removed.

\paragraph{Powered modeled readout.}
The rank-powered modeled-tAI run uses $40$ organisms and $9$ modeled readout columns with the same coordinate names as the residual basis.  Its control design has $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after the control projection.  The residualized coordinate block has rank $9$, and the reported global modeled readout fit is $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  The survival matrix has shape $9\times 9$, and the declared positive-entry threshold is $10^{-12}$.  In local notation the reported finite statistic is a controlled entrywise survival surface
$$\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &= S_{q_i}\left(T_j\mid Z_T\right),\\
Z_T &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism},\ GC3,\ \text{length},\ \text{M-density},\ \text{simpler codon baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The powered design is rank-admissible in the limited sense that $40$ organisms, $\operatorname{rank}(Z_T)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and $17$ post-control residual degrees of freedom avoid the three-organism saturation boundary.  It is not, by itself, a ribosome-occupancy experiment.

\paragraph{The three-organism refusal boundary.}
The earlier modeled-tAI table over $3$ organisms is retained only as a warning surface.  It computed the same $9$ synonymous residual coordinates in a $61$-codon count space and reported nonzero bounded modeled coupling: $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$ and maximum absolute diagonal entry $3801961.031607896$.  The three organisms were insufficient for powered survival, even though their local tRNA payload checks covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families.  Homo sapiens had $432$ FASTA headers and $432$ matched headers; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $275$ and $275$; Escherichia coli K12 had $89$ headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers.  The bounded tAI weights were computed from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights with $61$ sense codons and zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each of the three organisms.  These facts support the existence of a modeled codon-adaptation surface, but the $n=3$ matrix is rank-degenerate for survival and must not be promoted to population-level translation evidence.

\paragraph{Switch interpretation.}
Within this chapter, a U34-modification translation switch would require a finite row set in which the same organism or coding-sequence units carry the residual coordinate $q_i^{\perp \tau}$, a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout $T_j$, and controls for amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, M-density, and simpler codon-use baselines.  A positive modeled-tAI survival entry can nominate a coordinate-readout pair for follow-up, but it does not identify GCN4 regulation, U34 chemistry, ribosome dwell time, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  The finite GCN4-labelled run contributes the scalar fact that $5155$ genes were retained; it does not provide a U34 perturbation contrast, a before-after modification state, a matched ribosome-profiling denominator, or a protein-output statistic.

\paragraph{Local decision rule.}
The chapter therefore uses the following local acceptance condition.  A bounded modeled-readout statement may be retained when at least one residual coordinate has $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ after all declared controls and when the design is not rank-saturated.  A measured translation-control statement requires a separate finite contact supplying observed translation rows, such as ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency readout, joined to the same residual coordinate and control surface.  The break condition is
$$\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} \leq 0\ \text{for every}\ (i,j),
\end{aligned}$$
or any design state in which the apparent survival is explained by rank saturation.  The $40$-organism modeled-tAI run clears the rank-saturation objection for a bounded modeled surface; the $3$-organism run does not.  The GCN4/U34 claim remains locally bounded because the supplied finite rows do not contain the molecular modification, direct ribosome occupancy, or perturbational rescue evidence needed for a translation-switch mechanism.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow: the finite data support a rank-powered modeled codon-adaptation survival surface for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, with $9$ coordinates, $9$ modeled readouts, $40$ organisms, $24$ controls, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, residual coordinate rank $9$, and $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  Nothing in the supplied rows establishes measured translation, GCN4-specific U34 causation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, protein output, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Each of those claims would require its own finite contact at the corresponding layer and its own controlled statistic.
