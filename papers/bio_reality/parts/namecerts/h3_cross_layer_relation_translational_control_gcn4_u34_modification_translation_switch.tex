\subsection{A bounded translation-control surface for the GCN4 U34 packet}
\label{sec:bioreality-gcn4-u34-translation-control}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the GCN4 U34 modification packet as a finite cross-layer readout problem rather than as a completed mechanism claim.  The carrier is the retained coding-sequence table on which a synonymous residual coordinate may be compared with a translation-facing readout after explicit residualization.  In the finite run for this packet, the retained gene count was $5155$, with recorded runtime $0.0$ seconds.  This number is used only as a row-count boundary for the table under review; it is not by itself a translation statistic, a perturbation response, or a protein-output measurement.

The surrounding powered translation-readout evidence comes from a separate rank-adequate organism panel for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface.  That panel contains $40$ organisms, $9$ residual coordinates, and $9$ modeled translation-readout columns.  The residual coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The same nine labels occur on the modeled tAI readout side.  The control design used $24$ control columns, with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and $17$ residual degrees of freedom after projecting away the controls.  The residualized coordinate matrix had rank $9$, and the reported controlled fit scale was $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout separation.}
For a coding entry $e$, let $q_i(e)$ denote one of the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, and let $T_j(e)$ denote the corresponding modeled tAI-style readout coordinate.  The admissible comparison is not the raw association between $q_i$ and $T_j$.  It is the association left after removing amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, methionine-density baseline, and simpler codon-usage baselines from the translation readout.  Symbolically, the finite statistic is constrained to the residual surface
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp} &= q_i - P_{\tau}q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp} &= T_j - P_{Z_T}T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{only if } q_i^{\perp} \text{ improves the controlled readout of } T_j^{\perp}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $P_{\tau}$ denotes the amino-acid-sequence projection and $P_{Z_T}$ denotes the declared translation-control projection.  The chapter therefore records a finite controlled readout interface, not an intrinsic property of GCN4, not a direct ribosome-occupancy observation, and not a statement about protein abundance or function.

\paragraph{Rank boundary.}
A three-organism modeled-tAI surface was also recorded.  It used $3$ organisms, all $61$ sense codons, a coupling threshold of $0.01$, and $81$ entries above that threshold, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That finite table is useful as a bounded coupling check, but it is explicitly not a powered survival result.  The all-entry fit behavior at $n=3$ is a rank-saturation warning: with too few organisms, a controlled matrix can look perfect for algebraic reasons.  The powered panel avoids this specific failure mode by using $40$ organisms and retaining $17$ residual degrees of freedom after controls.  The admissible break condition remains
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} \leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for every coordinate-readout pair after the declared controls, or else saturation of the residualized rank design.  A positive modeled entry can be reported only at this bounded translation-readout layer.

\paragraph{GCN4 U34 interpretation.}
The GCN4 U34 packet is therefore not written as a claim that U34 modification has been mechanistically derived from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ geometry.  The finite row count $5155$ says that the packet has a retained gene-level surface on which such a test may be staged.  The powered modeled-tAI panel says that the broader $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis has a rank-adequate, control-aware translation-readout interface in a $40$ organism setting.  These two facts together justify a bounded chapter-level statement: the GCN4 U34 packet may be placed on a controlled translation-readout boundary, but the present finite data do not establish a measured U34-dependent switch, ribosome occupancy, elongation dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The only supported object here is a finite residual-readout testing surface.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are codon-adaptation readbacks built from tRNA gene-copy and wobble-weight assumptions; they are not measured ribosome footprints.  Codon-usage rows and tRNA gene-copy rows are sequence-annotation summaries; they are not protein-output assays.  Matched mRNA abundance, when present, belongs in the control set and does not substitute for translation.  Any promotion beyond the bounded $S^{QT}$ layer requires a separate layer-matched contact: measured ribosome profiling or translation efficiency for translation, proteomics for protein abundance, structure or stability assays for physical state, functional or perturbation assays for biological function, and broader replicated designs before any population-level or universal statement is allowed.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
Within these limits, the chapter is a ready boundary record.  It records $5155$ retained genes for the GCN4 U34 packet, a rank-powered companion translation-readout panel with $40$ organisms, $9$ residual coordinates, $9$ modeled readout coordinates, $24$ controls, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, residual coordinate rank $9$, and $R^{2}_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  It also records the rejected $n=3$ modeled-tAI surface as a saturation boundary rather than biological survival.  The mathematically admissible statement is that a controlled $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate can be tested against a bounded translation-readout surface; the biologically stronger GCN4 U34 switch interpretation remains conditional on a separate measured translation or perturbation contact.
