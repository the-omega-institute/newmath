\subsection{GCN4 U34 Modification as a Translation-Switch Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-gcn4-u34-modification-translation-switch}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record concerns a bounded cross-layer question: whether a codon-residual coordinate can be read as a switch-like translation surface once the amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, methionine-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines have been removed. The finite run associated with the GCN4 U34 modification switch retained $5155$ gene rows, with the recorded runtime scalar $0.0$ seconds. This row count is the only direct finite scalar presently attached to the GCN4-specific packet, so the chapter treats it as a filtered readout surface rather than as evidence of a completed mechanism.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate form.}
Let $q_i$ denote one of the synonymous residual coordinates in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel, and let $T_j$ denote a translation-readout coordinate. The admissible quantity is not the raw codon coordinate but its residue after amino-acid projection:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp} &= q_i - P_{\tau}q_i,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(T_j\mid Z_T).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $Z_T$ contains the controls that prevent a codon-count or amino-acid-composition effect from being mistaken for translation control. For this packet, the U34 language is therefore interpreted as a possible boundary on translational readout, not as an automatic biological switch.

\paragraph{Powered modeled-readout support.}
The broader rank-powered modeled-tAI computation supplies the finite context in which such a switch boundary can be stated. That computation used $40$ organisms, $9$ residual coordinates, and $9$ modeled tAI readout columns, producing a $9\times 9$ survival matrix. The residualization design recorded $24$ control columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$. The total modeled translation-readout statistic was $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, with a positive partial-$R^2$ reporting threshold of $10^{-12}$ and a rank-degeneracy cutoff at $0.999999$. The coordinate list was
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers make the rank condition meaningful for modeled tAI or stAI readbacks. They do not by themselves identify a U34-modification mechanism for GCN4.

\paragraph{Rank-degenerate refusal.}
The earlier three-organism modeled-tAI surface had $3$ organisms, $61$ sense codons, and the same $9$ synonymous residual coordinates. It reported $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. That surface is retained only as a bounded coupling check: with $n=3$, an all-saturated controlled matrix is not interpretable as biological translation survival. The switch claim therefore cannot be promoted from the three-organism table, even if the table contains large modeled couplings.

\paragraph{Switch readback.}
The GCN4 U34 packet is ready only as a finite boundary statement. A positive statement would require a same-row comparison in which at least one $S^{QT}_{ij}$ remains strictly positive after all declared controls and simpler-baseline ablation, and in which the residual rank is not saturated. The existing $5155$ retained gene rows show that a GCN4-oriented finite surface was constructed, while the $40$-organism modeled panel shows that nondegenerate modeled translation-readout survival is possible in the surrounding residual basis. The chapter therefore states a conditional switch form: U34 modification may be tested as a translation-control boundary only after the relevant measured or explicitly bounded readout is joined to the same residualized codon surface.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No conclusion about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbational mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from the present record. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are codon-adaptation readouts, not observed ribosome dynamics. A GCN4 U34 switch requires its own layer-matched contact: measured ribosome profiling, translation-efficiency rows, perturbation-rescue rows, or an equivalently explicit bounded readout paired with the codon coordinates and controls. Until that contact is present, the result is a controlled finite-row boundary for a translation-readout hypothesis, not a completed biological mechanism.
