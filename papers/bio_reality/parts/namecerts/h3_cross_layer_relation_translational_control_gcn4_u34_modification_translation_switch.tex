\subsection{Rank-powered Q6 residual survival at the modeled translation-readout boundary}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translational-control-gcn4-u34-modification-translation-switch}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record studies a finite organism-by-CDS readout surface in which synonymous residual coordinates from $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ are compared with translation-weight coordinates after amino-acid quotient residualization.  The biological object is not a protein-output assay and not a ribosome-footprint measurement.  It is a bounded codon-adaptation surface: per-codon modeled tAI weights are derived from organism-level tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, then projected onto the same nine residual coordinate labels used for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-usage side.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The controlled comparison therefore has a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ surface.  Its rows are $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates $q_i$ and its columns are modeled translation-readout coordinates $T_j$.  Each $q_i$ is allowed to survive amino-acid-sequence projection only through the quotient residual $q_i-P_{\tau}q_i$, and each $T_j$ is evaluated only after removing amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.

\paragraph{Rank condition and powered modeled readout.}
The powered modeled-tAI run used $40$ organisms.  The control design had $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after control projection.  The residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design had rank $9$.  The reported controlled readout fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the declared degenerate boundary at $0.999999$ and a positive partial-$R^2$ reporting threshold of $10^{-12}$.  This places the $40$-organism modeled panel on the rank-admissible side of the finite-row test: it is not the earlier all-perfect artifact and it has enough residual degrees of freedom to make the $9$ coordinate directions non-vacuous after the control projection.  The organisms include eukaryotic, bacterial, archaeal-style bacterial outgroup, plant, fungal, and metazoan entries such as \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Arabidopsis thaliana}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, \emph{Escherichia coli}, \emph{Danio rerio}, \emph{Gallus gallus}, \emph{Yarrowia lipolytica}, multiple \emph{Burkholderia} and \emph{Mycobacterium} entries, and cyanobacterial entries including \emph{Nostoc} sp. PCC 7120 and \emph{Anabaena variabilis} ATCC 29413.  The claim made here is deliberately narrower than translation realization: the finite panel supports a controlled modeled-adaptation survival surface, not observed elongation, not ribosome occupancy, and not protein abundance.

\paragraph{Rejected small-panel boundary.}
The same coordinate family also has a three-organism modeled-tAI record over \emph{Homo sapiens}, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12.  In that smaller run the sense-codon count was $61$, the modeled tAI construction had zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ for each organism, and the bounded coupling matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$.  Its largest absolute entry was $3801961.031607896$, equal to the largest absolute diagonal entry.  Those numbers show that the pipeline can form a bounded coupling surface, but the organism count $n=3$ is not rank-powered.  A saturated all-$1.0$ or effectively perfect controlled matrix at this size is treated as a null boundary, not as biological survival.  Thus the three-organism computation is useful only as a construction check and as a warning against promoting a rank-saturated projection to a population-level law.

\paragraph{Local switch record.}
The finite-row GCN4/U34 switch packet kept $5155$ genes in its local table and reported runtime $0.0$ seconds for the recorded run.  In this chapter that row count is used only as a finite accounting fact: it identifies the size of the retained gene-level surface for the switch packet.  It does not, by itself, supply a measured ribosome-footprint table, a U34 perturbation response, a protein abundance output, or a causal mechanism.  The finite switch record can therefore be placed beside the rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ modeled-readout panel as a cross-layer target, but the observed mechanism remains unclaimed unless a separate same-row perturbation or measured translation contact is attached.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$, modeled translation coordinate $T_j$, and control matrix $Z_T$, the local survival entry is read as a controlled increment over the baseline model:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
means that the residualized $q_i$ improves the chosen finite description or partial-$R^2$ score for $T_j$ after the declared controls and simpler codon-usage baselines have already been fitted.  The break condition is equally local:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0\quad\text{for every }(i,j)
\end{aligned}
$$
or a rank-saturated fit.  In either case $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ remains a code-read or bounded codon-count residual basis for this layer.  The powered modeled-tAI run avoids the $n=3$ rank artifact and supplies a non-vacuous modeled readout surface; it still does not replace measured ribosome profiling, measured translation efficiency, matched proteomics, RNA half-life, localization, structure, function, or perturbation evidence.

\paragraph{Non-promotion rule.}
The finite facts in this record authorize only a controlled modeled-adaptation statement.  No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, protein production, folding state, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, causation, or universal biological law follows from $S^{QT}$ notation, from tAI or stAI weights, from codon-usage tables, from tRNA gene-copy counts, from the $40$-organism rank condition, or from the $5155$ retained GCN4/U34 genes.  Each stronger layer requires its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In particular, modeled tAI from GtRNAdb-style gene-copy counts and wobble weights is not ribo-seq, not measured elongation speed, not ribosome occupancy, not proteomics, and not evidence for protein folding or function.  The chapter therefore records a rank-admissible modeled translation-readout boundary and a local GCN4/U34 finite-row packet, while refusing the higher biological promotions that the present rows do not test.
