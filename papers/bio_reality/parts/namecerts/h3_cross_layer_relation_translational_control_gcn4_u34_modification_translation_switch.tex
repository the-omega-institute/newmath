\subsection{GCN4 U34 Modification as a Translation-Control Boundary for Q6 Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-gcn4-u34-q6-translation-control}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This BioReality record treats the GCN4 U34-modification switch as a cross-layer translation-control test only after the codon-count geometry has been separated from amino-acid sequence content and from simpler compositional baselines.  The finite row audit for the named packet kept $5155$ genes and recorded runtime $0.0$ seconds under run \texttt{f4e4f5c62558}.  Those two numbers establish that the packet has a concrete finite data surface, but they do not by themselves identify a positive translation-survival coefficient, a ribosome-footprint effect, or a protein-level consequence.  The role of this subsection is therefore narrower: it records what may be said from the finite rows already present, and what must remain blocked until the missing measured translation rows are joined.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and readout surface.}
The controlled coordinate surface is the amino-acid-quotient form of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$.  In this form a synonymous coordinate $q_i$ is allowed to influence a translation readout only through the residual part $q_i-P_{\tau}q_i$, where $P_{\tau}$ denotes projection onto amino-acid sequence composition.  The powered modeled-tAI run computed nine residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, and used the same nine labels as modeled translation-readout columns.  The matched panel contained $40$ organisms.  The control design had $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residual-coordinate rank $9$.  The recorded modeled-readout aggregate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}\mid Z} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough to describe a rank-admissible modeled codon-adaptation readout, because the panel is not the rejected three-organism surface and the reported $R^2_{QT}$ is below the stated degenerate threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
For this packet the relevant finite statistic is not an unconditional codon-use association.  Each entry is a controlled survival term $S^{QT}_{ij}$ comparing a residual coordinate $q_i$ with a translation readout $T_j$ after removal of amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.  A positive entry has the intended finite meaning only when the design retains rank after these controls.  In schematic notation the admissible readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0
\quad\text{only after residualization against } Z_T,\\
Z_T & = \{\text{amino-acid composition, organism, }GC3,\text{ length, }M\text{-density, simpler codon baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The powered modeled-tAI packet reports a $9\times 9$ survival matrix on the $40$-organism panel.  The earlier three-organism modeled-tAI packet is retained only as a warning surface: it used $3$ organisms, produced $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, and had maximum absolute reported coupling $3801961.031607896$, but it is explicitly not a powered survival result.  Its role is to mark the rank-saturation failure mode, not to support a biological law.

\paragraph{Relation to the GCN4 U34 switch.}
The GCN4 U34-modification packet names a translation-control subject, but the available finite row for that exact subject only states that $5155$ genes were kept.  It does not yet provide the same-row U34 modification state, codon-residual coordinate, measured ribosome or translation-efficiency readout, matched mRNA abundance, and control matrix needed to estimate $S^{QT}_{ij}$ for the GCN4 switch itself.  Consequently the admissible interpretation is conditional: the Q6 residual machinery gives a finite coordinate system in which a U34-dependent translation switch could be tested, and the powered modeled-tAI panel shows that the coordinate/readout design can avoid the three-organism rank artifact.  It does not show that GCN4 translation is controlled by any particular $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate.

\paragraph{Local null boundary.}
The break condition is strict.  If every controlled entry satisfies $S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0$, or if the apparent signal comes only from a saturated design, then the coordinate basis remains a bounded codon-count or modeled-readout basis for this layer.  The present packet is therefore marked as needing additional data for the GCN4 U34 subject.  A future positive claim would require a same-organism or same-CDS matrix joining codon counts, U34 modification state, direct translation readout or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA controls, and the declared covariates.  Without that matrix, no conclusion follows about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve as bounded translation-readout annotations, but they are not measured elongation speed, ribosome occupancy, proteomics, folding evidence, or functional evidence.
