\subsection{A bounded translation-readout switch for the GCN4--U34 modification route}\label{sec:bioreality-gcn4-u34-q6-translation-switch}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite organism-by-CDS readout surface for a proposed cross-layer relation between synonymous residual coordinates in $B^*_{Q6}$ and bounded translation readouts.  The active packet named $h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translational\_control.gcn4\_u34\_modification\_translation\_switch$ kept $5155$ genes in the finite row set, with the recorded run time equal to $0.0$ seconds.  This establishes only that the named switch packet has a finite row carrier on which a translation-readout question can be posed; it does not establish U34-modification causality, GCN4 pathway control, ribosome occupancy, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Residual-coordinate readout.}
The operational coordinate family is the amino-acid-quotient residual basis $B^*_{Q6}$.  In the powered modeled-tAI run, the residual coordinate list has $9$ entries:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The same $9$ labels occur as modeled per-codon tAI readout columns, so the finite translation-readout matrix has shape $9\times 9$.  The construction first removes amino-acid-level composition from the synonymous signal; a coordinate $q_i$ is admissible for this layer only through its residual part $q_i-P_{\tau}q_i$.  The controlled survival entry is therefore not a raw codon-count association, but a residual readout after the declared controls have been applied.

The powered panel contains $40$ organisms.  The residualization design used $24$ control columns, had $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and left $17$ residual degrees of freedom after controls.  The residual coordinate side had $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  The recorded modeled translation-readout fit is
$$
\begin{aligned}

 \operatorname{rank}(Z)&=23, &
 df_{\mathrm{res}}&=17, &

The positive-entry threshold for partial $R^2$ was $10^{-12}$, and the rank-degeneracy cutoff was recorded as $0.999999$.  Since the reported $R^2_{QT}$ is below the degeneracy cutoff and the organism support is $40$, this run is not the three-organism all-$1.0$ projection artifact.  It is nevertheless a modeled tAI surface, not a measured ribosome-footprint surface.

\paragraph{Three-organism refusal boundary.}
A separate three-organism modeled-tAI packet used Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12-style data and reported all $81$ entries above a bounded coupling threshold of $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That run had $n=3$ and was explicitly marked as insufficient for powered survival.  Its purpose is boundary information: modeled tAI weights derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights can generate nonzero bounded coupling, but an all-saturated or underpowered matrix is not biological survival.  The powered $40$-organism computation is the relevant finite-rank surface for the present chapter.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$, the chapter reads the finite survival score as the controlled increment remaining after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines have been removed.  In notation, the admissible claim has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
 S^{QT}_{ij} > 0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 q_i-P_{\tau}q_i \text{ improves the bounded modeled readout } T_j
 \text{ after } Z_T .
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is equally local:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0 \text{ for all } (i,j)
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \text{rank saturation occurs}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 B^*_{Q6} \text{ remains only a codon-count residual basis for this layer.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the mathematical content of the switch record: it licenses a finite, controlled translation-readout boundary for the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, while refusing promotion to unmeasured biological layers.

\paragraph{Biological scope.}
The biology represented here is bounded: codon-usage tables, tRNA gene-copy counts, and tAI or stAI weights provide organism-scoped readout surfaces.  They do not directly observe ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, protein abundance, folded structure, physical feasibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  The GCN4--U34 wording therefore marks a target route for translational-control testing, not a completed mechanistic claim.  A stronger chapter would require a same-organism or same-CDS panel joining codon counts, measured ribosome profiling or translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, and explicit U34-modification or perturbation rows.  Until that separate contact exists, the only ready statement is the controlled modeled-readout boundary above.
