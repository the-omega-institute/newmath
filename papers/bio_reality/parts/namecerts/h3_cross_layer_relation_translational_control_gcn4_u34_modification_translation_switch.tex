\subsection{GCN4 U34 Modification as a Translation-Switch Boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-gcn4-u34-q6-translation-switch}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the GCN4 U34-modification translation-switch claim as a bounded cross-layer test on finite coding-sequence rows rather than as a completed mechanism.  The directly attached run for the named packet retained $5155$ genes and reported runtime $0.0$ seconds.  Those two numbers certify only that a finite gene surface was selected for this packet; they do not by themselves supply a measured ribosome-occupancy matrix, an edited U34 perturbation contrast, a protein-output surface, or a causal switch statistic.  The admissible mathematical object is therefore a finite organism-by-CDS readout table carrying residual synonymous coordinates $q_i$ from $B^*_{Q6}$ and translation-readout weights $T_j$ after quotienting away amino-acid sequence effects and residualizing against declared nuisance coordinates.

\paragraph{Residual-coordinate rule.}
The coordinate part of the record is the amino-acid-quotient residual rule.  A synonymous coordinate $q_i$ is allowed to enter the translation layer only through its residual component after projection onto amino-acid composition.  In notation, the admissible coordinate is not $q_i$ itself but
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp \tau} &= q_i - P_{\tau}q_i,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $P_{\tau}$ denotes the amino-acid-sequence projection.  The readout coordinate $T_j$ is evaluated only after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines have been applied.  Thus the local test statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(T_j\mid Z_T),
\end{aligned}
$$
with $Z_T$ containing the declared translation controls.  The break condition is local and explicit: if $S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0$ after those controls, or if the apparent fit is produced by rank saturation, the corresponding coordinate-readout pair is not counted as translation survival.

\paragraph{Powered modeled-readout evidence.}
The strongest finite-row evidence attached to this packet is the rank-powered modeled-tAI surface, not the GCN4 perturbation itself.  That run used $40$ organisms, $9$ residual $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, and $9$ modeled translation-readout columns.  The residual coordinates were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control design had $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after controls.  The residualized coordinate design had rank $9$, and the reported modeled-readout coefficient was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed survival matrix had shape $9\times 9$.  This is a nontrivial finite modeled-readout result because the panel has enough organism-level support to avoid the three-row all-fit artifact.  It remains a modeled translation-adaptation readout: the weights come from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, projected onto the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and are not ribosome footprints.

\paragraph{Rank-degenerate boundary.}
The earlier three-organism modeled-tAI surface is retained only as a warning boundary.  It used $3$ organisms, all standard tRNA families were represented in the local payloads, and all $81$ entries of the modeled coupling matrix exceeded the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That surface was explicitly classified as underpowered for survival: $3$ organisms are insufficient for the requested rank-powered claim, and an all-positive or all-$1.0$ controlled surface in such a small panel is a rank-saturation boundary rather than biological survival.  The powered record therefore refuses the inference
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{small-panel modeled fit} &\Longrightarrow \text{translation switch in cells}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The only admissible inference is that the three-organism matrix motivated a larger controlled panel and identified the need for a break condition.

\paragraph{Relation to the GCN4 U34-switch claim.}
The named GCN4 U34-modification packet currently contributes a finite gene count, $5155$, but no local U34-editing contrast, no per-CDS ribosome-profiling denominator, no matched mRNA control table, and no same-row perturbation statistic.  Consequently, the chapter can state a translation-switch boundary but not a completed GCN4 switch.  The powered $40$-organism modeled-tAI surface shows that $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates can survive a controlled modeled translation-readout test without the $n=3$ rank artifact.  It does not show that U34 modification changes GCN4 translation, and it does not identify any one coordinate, including $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$ or $f3\_stress$, as a population-level law.  A genuine GCN4 U34-switch result would require a same-row panel in which U34-modification state, coding-sequence coordinates, translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, and the declared controls are all present on the same finite rows.

\paragraph{Admissible statement.}
The safe claim is therefore the following bounded one.  On the attached finite data, a $40$-organism modeled translation-adaptation panel gives a controlled $9\times 9$ $B^*_{Q6}$-to-modeled-$T$ readout with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and coordinate rank $9$.  Separately, the GCN4 U34 packet has a selected finite gene surface of $5155$ genes.  These facts justify keeping the GCN4 U34-modification translation-switch conjecture open as a testable cross-layer boundary, not closing it as a measured switch.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding statement, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, causation, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readouts, not observed elongation speed.  Codon usage and tRNA gene-copy counts are external tabular contacts, not protein-production measurements.  The finite statistic $S^{QT}_{ij}$ may be reported only for the explicitly controlled readout surface on which it is computed.  Any stronger GCN4 U34 claim must be supplied by a separate layer-matched contact with measured translation rows and its own finite controlled statistic.
