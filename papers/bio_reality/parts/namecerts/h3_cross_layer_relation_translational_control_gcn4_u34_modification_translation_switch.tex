\subsection{Rank-powered translation-readout boundary for the GCN4 U34 modification switch}
\label{sec:bioreality-gcn4-u34-q6-translation-switch}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record attached to the GCN4 U34 modification switch is treated here as a finite row-level translation-control boundary, not as a completed mechanism.  The immediate switch audit retained $5155$ genes and recorded a zero-second rounded runtime entry, so the available row support for this specific packet is a finite gene surface rather than a perturbational kinetic model.  The broader coordinate context is the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual panel after amino-acid quotienting: nine residual coordinates were computed, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The corresponding modeled translation-readout surface also has nine columns with the same coordinate labels.  Thus the finite object is a $9\times 9$ coordinate/readout comparison surface, evaluated only after the amino-acid sequence projection has been removed.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a modeled translation readout $T_j$, the statistic used by this chapter is the controlled survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$.  The coordinate is first projected away from amino-acid composition, and the readout is evaluated only after controls for amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler codon-usage baselines.  In schematic form the admissible finite comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp} &= q_i - P_{\tau}q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp} &= T_j - P_{Z_T}T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
with the last inequality interpreted only on the residualized finite panel.  The powered modeled-tAI audit loaded $40$ organisms, used $24$ control columns with control rank $23$, left $17$ residual degrees of freedom after controls, and found $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  The finite controlled comparison reported $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ and a $9\times 9$ survival matrix.  This is enough to treat the modeled tAI surface as rank-admissible for the bounded readout question.  It is not enough to identify a ribosome-occupancy, protein-output, or causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Rank boundary.}
The same packet carries a three-organism predecessor surface, and that surface is explicitly not a biological survival result.  In the $n=3$ modeled-tAI audit, the contact used modeled codon adaptation weights derived from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights for Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12.  The finite matrix had $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but the run itself classifies three organisms as insufficient for powered survival.  The break rule is therefore local and numerical: a rank-saturated or all-fitted small panel may show nonzero modeled coupling, yet it must not be read as population-level translation survival.  The powered $n=40$ surface is the first admissible modeled-readout boundary in this record because the control rank, residual degrees of freedom, and residual coordinate rank are all reported together.

\paragraph{Relation to the U34 switch.}
The U34 switch name marks a translation-control subject, but the finite evidence presently attached to this exact switch is only the $5155$-gene retained surface.  The coordinate packet can ask whether a synonymous residual coordinate survives into a bounded translation-readout surface after controls, and the powered modeled-tAI packet supplies a positive rank-admissible comparison at the panel level.  What it does not yet supply is a same-row U34 perturbation table joining modification state, ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency measurements, matched mRNA abundance, codon counts, and the declared residual coordinates.  Consequently, the scientifically correct reading is conditional: the $B^*_{Q6}$ panel gives a controlled way to test whether the U34-related translation-control layer has a nonzero residual readout, while the current finite switch row count does not by itself determine the mechanism of the switch.

\paragraph{Local stop rule.}
The chapter stops at the modeled translation-readout boundary.  No measured translation realization, ribosome occupancy, folding statement, physical admissibility statement, biological function, perturbational mechanism, protein abundance, phenotype, or universal law follows from the $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry, from the $S^{QT}$ notation, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, or from the $5155$ retained gene rows unless a separate finite contact supplies that layer and is evaluated under its own controls.  In particular, modeled tAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readouts, not ribosome footprints; tRNA gene-copy counts are annotation summaries, not charging measurements; and codon-usage tables are sequence summaries, not protein-realization assays.  The break condition is reached whenever every controlled entry satisfies $S^{QT}_{ij}\leq 0$, when the fit is rank-saturated, or when the proposed promotion crosses into translation, protein, structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality without a layer-matched measurement.
