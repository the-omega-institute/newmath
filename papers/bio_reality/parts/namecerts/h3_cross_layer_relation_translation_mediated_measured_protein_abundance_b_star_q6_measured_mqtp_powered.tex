\subsection{Measured Translation-Conditioned Protein-Abundance Mediation for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-mqtp}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and row matching.}
The finite surface for this record is the $9$-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual matrix evaluated on matched coding-sequence rows with a measured translation readout and a measured protein-abundance readout.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The measured translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ computed as ribosome footprint divided by mRNA in the local ribosome-profiling rows.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm after joining by protein identifier.  The three-way joins are powered rather than rank-saturated: danio has $13140$ joined rows from $14410$ CDS rows; Escherichia coli K12 has $3415$ joined rows from $3739$ CDS rows; human has $11554$ joined rows from $19053$ CDS rows; and budding yeast has $5128$ joined rows from $5815$ CDS rows.  The corresponding measured-TE join hit rates from the translation-only audit are $0.9118667591950035$, $0.9133458143888741$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.8818572656921754$, respectively.

\paragraph{Residualization model.}
Every reported number below is computed after removing the same finite control span: intercept, $\log$ CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition columns, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the support of the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in each organism, while the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $13117$ for danio, $3392$ for E. coli, $11531$ for human, and $5105$ for yeast.  The measured translation block has rank $1$, and the joint $(Q,T)$ block has rank $10$ in all four organisms.  Thus the reported mediation fractions are not artifacts of a three-row or fully fitted design.

Those direct protein-omics values are associations with measured abundance, not translation-conditioned explanations.

\paragraph{Measured mediation fractions.}
The mediated scalar is the loss of the direct $Q \to P$ explanatory component after conditioning on the measured $T$ readout, clipped to the unit interval for the registered readback.  The raw and reported fractions are organism-specific:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{raw}(\mathrm{danio}) &= -0.04801486048721136,& M^{QTP}_{meas}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0,\\
M^{QTP}_{raw}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.04091158340950673,& M^{QTP}_{meas}(\mathrm{E.coli}) &= 0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP}_{raw}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.0027966485036786744,& M^{QTP}_{meas}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP}_{raw}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.4793069656345167,& M^{QTP}_{meas}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism ordering is therefore sharp.  Yeast shows a large measured translation-conditioned absorption of the $Q \to P$ component, E. coli shows a small positive absorption, human shows a very small positive absorption, and danio is suppressive in the raw decomposition and is read back as zero mediated fraction.  The energy diagnostics agree with this ordering: in yeast, measured $T$ explains $593.5847863333478$ units of residual protein-abundance energy and adds $269.89558844115504$ units after $Q$; in human, the corresponding values are only $2.0678517436514716$ and $0.486668572585927$.

\paragraph{Four-case discriminant.}
The modeled-tAI four-case classifier supplies a useful comparison but is not substituted for measured ribosome profiling.  In the modeled-tAI discriminant, E. coli has $7$ case-D coordinates and $2$ positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop, while yeast has $9$ case-D coordinates and no positive-positive coordinate lacking the drop.  This classification is consistent with yeast being the strongest translation-conditioned abundance case in the measured-TE decomposition, but the measured chapter result rests on ribosome-derived $TE$, not on modeled adaptation weights.

\paragraph{Joint readouts and reduction dictionary.}
A separate joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition confirms that measured translation alone is not the only reducible channel.  In the three-organism joint panel, the joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for E. coli, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The single-mediator split is also informative: yeast has $TE$-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, while E. coli has $TE$-only fraction $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only fraction $0.23052056324898387$.  The dictionary reduction audit on the yeast context join uses $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$; it reports coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  Thus the measured translation channel is real in the finite panel, but it does not exhaust the protein-abundance residual surface.

\paragraph{$f_3$-stress localization within the translation-adjacent signal.}
The $f_3$-stress dwell audit supplies a position-sensitive check on one coordinate of the same residual family.  In yeast, $n_{join}=1257$ and $base\ n=1800$ give base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a deterministic row-label null with mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95\%$ value $0.0013115362189692937$.  The $f_3$ dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\cdot 10^{-9}$.  The positional audit identifies the $5'$ segment as leading: the $5'$ window passes, the $3'$ window does not, and the weighted ramp statistic in the structural-order positional audit is positive at $0.0011165640103326878$ with sign-flip value $0.0478515625$, although the paired ramp-minus-body gate remains short of the localization criterion at $0.05859375$.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
This record is ready as a measured, cross-sectional, rank-powered statistical mediation statement.  The admissible claim is that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface has positive measured $Q \to T$ survival and positive direct $Q \to P$ survival in four matched panels, with organism-specific absorption of the direct protein-abundance component after conditioning on measured ribosome-derived $TE$.  The strongest finite row is yeast, where $S^{QT}_{meas}=0.13681931343730436$, $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, and $M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.4793069656345167$ on $5128$ joined rows.
