\subsection{Measured translation conditioning for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-mqtp-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The measurement object is a finite matched panel whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns combine nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency readout, one measured protein-abundance readout, and the standard covariate block.  The residual coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ supplied as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA in the local ribosome-efficiency files.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from the local proteomics abundance files joined by protein identifier.  Every reported quantity below is computed after residualizing against a covariate block containing an intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline; the residualized covariate rank is $23$ in all four measured-mediation organisms, with $24$ control columns before rank reduction.

\paragraph{Rows and ranks.}
The measured mediation panel is rank-powered in all four organisms.  Danio rerio has $14410$ CDS rows in the abundance join and $13140$ three-way codon--translation--abundance rows; Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $3739$ reported CDS rows, $3747$ valid abundance records, and $3415$ three-way rows; Homo sapiens has $19053$ reported CDS rows, $19483$ valid abundance records, and $11554$ three-way rows; Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5815$ reported CDS rows, $6351$ valid abundance records, and $5128$ three-way rows.  The residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$ in all four organisms, the residualized translation design has rank $1$, and the combined $Q,T$ design has rank $10$.  The residual degrees of freedom after the $Z$ block are $13117$ for Danio rerio, $3392$ for Escherichia coli, $11531$ for Homo sapiens, and $5105$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The corresponding residual energies for measured $TE$ are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$; the protein-abundance residual energies are $22165.717891059892$, $3599.0095273849333$, $13183.855314669145$, and $2146.0645009193045$.

\paragraph{Translation and protein survival.}
The measured $Q\to T$ survival values are positive in all four organisms: $0.04034217614654355$ in Danio rerio, $0.009394266754822262$ in Escherichia coli, $0.018837911344127926$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.13681931343730436$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The measured $Q\to P$ values on the same mediated panel are also positive: $0.04926578454614652$, $0.10941535951828074$, $0.04288464996165745$, and $0.3146818503437042$, respectively.  Thus the finite rows do not stop at a direct protein-omics association; they contain a measured translation coordinate on the same CDS units.  The fitted $Q$ energy on measured translation is $144.76301201119642$ in Danio rerio, $8.637891149939946$ in Escherichia coli, $38.66659710161269$ in Homo sapiens, and $103.10404057290246$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The fitted $Q$ energy on protein abundance is $1092.0114819316218$, $393.78692134854015$, $565.3850203147235$, and $675.3275481062249$.

\paragraph{Measured mediation readback.}
The mediation statistic is a joint nine-dimensional $Q$ decomposition: after $Q$ and measured $TE$ are both residualized against $Z$, the direct $Q\to P$ component is recomputed after conditioning on $T$.  The raw measured mediation fractions are $-0.04801486048721136$ for Danio rerio, $0.04091158340950673$ for Escherichia coli, $0.0027966485036786744$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.4793069656345167$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The reported unit-interval mediated fractions are therefore $0.0$, $0.04091158340950673$, $0.0027966485036786744$, and $0.4793069656345167$.  In energy terms, conditioning on measured translation leaves direct $Q$ improvement on protein abundance of $1144.4442608870015$ in Danio rerio, $377.67647487021645$ in Escherichia coli, $563.803837143658$ in Homo sapiens, and $351.63835021403213$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  Measured translation adds protein-abundance improvement after $Q$ of $68.85177862607247$, $19.45303160850625$, $0.486668572585927$, and $269.89558844115504$.  The finite conclusion is therefore asymmetric: yeast shows a large measured translation-conditioned component, bacteria and human show positive but small components, and Danio reports a suppressive raw fraction that is clipped to the null mediation side while still retaining positive $Q\to T$ and $Q\to P$ readouts.

\paragraph{Protein-omics background.}
The broader direct protein-abundance screen confirms that the $Q\to P$ surface is not a rank-degenerate artifact.  Across fifteen abundance joins, all nine coordinates survive the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold in every organism.  The direct $R^2_{QP}$ list is $0.061906245886660105$ for Bacillus subtilis 168, $0.10217906758013379$ for Caenorhabditis elegans, $0.04613529807085213$ for Danio rerio, $0.08141786718177903$ for Dictyostelium discoideum, $0.0770952887136557$ for Drosophila melanogaster, $0.10767526677985896$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.03980793596647631$ for Gallus gallus, $0.019256877907572044$ for Halobacterium salinarum, $0.04454565118019448$ for Homo sapiens, $0.049827984062539195$ for Mus musculus, $0.03209009170649934$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis mc2 155, $0.0646471273126996$ for Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, $0.02763998603083845$ for Rattus norvegicus, $0.29837624994140827$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and $0.06397514251295629$ for Sulfolobus solfataricus.  The abundance join hit rates include $0.9975320829220138$ for Bacillus subtilis, $0.8913774652193854$ for Caenorhabditis elegans, $1.0$ for Danio rerio, $0.9779294769799313$ for Homo sapiens, $0.9808334176190958$ for Mus musculus, $0.9156038419146592$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and $1.0$ for Sulfolobus solfataricus.  These numbers justify treating direct protein abundance as a real downstream finite surface, but they do not by themselves identify a translation route.

\paragraph{Translation-readout background.}
The measured $TE$ screen supplies that missing layer for four organisms.  Its join rates are $0.9118667591950035$ in Danio rerio with $13140$ genes used, $0.9133458143888741$ in Escherichia coli with $3415$ genes used, $0.6064136881331024$ in Homo sapiens with $11554$ genes used, and $0.8818572656921754$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae with $5128$ genes used.  The $R^2_{QT,meas}$ values match the measured mediation input values: $0.04034217614654355$, $0.009394266754822262$, $0.018837911344127926$, and $0.13681931343730436$.  All four entries have all nine coordinates surviving the stated positive threshold, so the measured translation contact is not merely a named route; it contributes a nonzero same-row translation statistic.

\paragraph{Four-case comparison and reduction checks.}
A modeled-tAI four-case discriminant gives a useful contrast but is kept distinct from measured ribosome-efficiency conditioning.  Under modeled tAI, Escherichia coli has seven case-$D$ coordinates and two positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop; Saccharomyces cerevisiae has nine case-$D$ coordinates.  The modeled-tAI $R^2_{QT}$ values are $0.5416736993691318$ in Escherichia coli and $0.8213457220900503$ in yeast, while the matched $R^2_{QP}$ values are $0.10767526677985896$ and $0.29837624994140827$.  The measured-TE mediation chapter therefore does not collapse modeled adaptation weights and ribosome-derived $TE$ into one object.  A separate joint $TE$ plus mRNA-stability decomposition also shows that the protein-abundance residual is only partly absorbed by measured readouts: the joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ in Escherichia coli, $0.16890712099923358$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.5195182225621592$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ in each case.  In yeast, a dictionary-style partial mediation certificate over measured $TE$, mRNA stability, and turnover reports $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n=1800$, coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$, selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These reduction checks support the interpretation that measured translation is a substantial but incomplete readout for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready at the level of measured cross-sectional mediation.  The claim supported here is that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have positive measured $Q\to T$ and positive measured $Q\to P$ entries on rank-sufficient same-CDS panels, and that conditioning on measured ribosome-derived $TE$ absorbs a measurable fraction of the direct $Q\to P$ signal, especially in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The strongest measured entry is yeast, where $S^{QT}_{meas}=0.13681931343730436$, $S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, and the measured mediated fraction is $0.4793069656345167$.  Escherichia coli and Homo sapiens are positive but small, while Danio rerio is a positive-survival, non-mediated or suppressive case under this scalar decomposition.  The result is a finite observational decomposition, not a causal pathway result.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No claim about universal translation law, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, perturbation rescue, or biological mechanism follows from these rows.  Measured $TE$ is condition-specific and comes from ribosome-footprint and mRNA measurements drawn from one or a small number of studies; PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-omics readout; the joined rows are cross-sectional and do not establish temporal ordering.  Direct protein survival $S^{QP}$ remains a protein-omics association unless the measured $Q\to T$ step is present and conditioning on $T$ reduces the $Q\to P$ component.  Even when the measured fraction is positive, it is a variance-partition readback, not an intervention, rescue, elongation mechanism, initiation mechanism, turnover mechanism, localization claim, folding claim, function claim, or universal biological rule.  Any such higher-layer statement would require its own matched measurement surface and its own finite statistic.
