\subsection{Measured translation conditioning for $B^*_{Q6}$ protein abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-mqtp}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether a measured translation readout can account for the association between the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and measured protein abundance.  The finite carrier is a joined coding-sequence table with nine residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency column, one protein-abundance column, and the same residualizing controls used across the sibling protein-omics tests.  The coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ computed as ribosome footprint divided by mRNA in the local organism-specific files.  The abundance readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in parts per million, joined by protein identifier.  Each organism is residualized against a $24$-column control design whose effective rank is $23$, including length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Matched organism rows.}
The powered measured-translation mediation fit is available for four organisms.  The three-way joins contain $13140$ danio rows from $14410$ reported CDS joins, $3415$ \textit{E. coli} rows from $3739$ reported CDS joins, $11554$ human rows from $19053$ reported CDS joins, and $5128$ yeast rows from $5815$ reported CDS joins.  The measured TE contact hit rates in the companion TE-survival table are $0.9118667591950035$ for danio, $0.9133458143888741$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.6064136881331024$ for human, and $0.8818572656921754$ for yeast.  The direct protein-omics surface is larger: for example, yeast has $5815$ abundance-joined CDS rows with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, human has $19053$ rows with hit rate $0.9779294769799313$, and the full direct-abundance panel spans fifteen organisms with all nine coordinates surviving at the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Measured mediation statistic.}
Let $X_Q$ denote the residualized nine-column $B^*_{Q6}$ design, let $T$ denote residualized measured $\log_{10}(TE)$, and let $Y$ denote residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance.  The reported scalar compares the direct $X_Q \to Y$ signal before and after conditioning on $T$; negative raw mediation is clipped to zero in the reported measured fraction.  The four measured rows are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{danio:} && S^{QP} &= 0.04926578454614652, & S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.04034217614654355, & M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.0, \\
\textit{E. coli:} && S^{QP} &= 0.10941535951828074, & S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.009394266754822262, & M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.04091158340950673, \\
\text{human:} && S^{QP} &= 0.04288464996165745, & S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.018837911344127926, & M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.0027966485036786744, \\
\text{yeast:} && S^{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042, & S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436, & M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus all four organisms have positive measured $Q \to T$ and direct $Q \to P$ surfaces, but the amount of protein-abundance signal absorbed by measured TE is strongly organism-dependent.  Danio is suppressive on the raw scale, with raw fraction $-0.04801486048721136$ and reported fraction $0$.  Human has a small measured fraction, while yeast has a large measured fraction of $0.4793069656345167$.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The rank checks are nondegenerate in every measured mediation row: $\operatorname{rank}(X_Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X_Q,T)=10$ after controls.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $13117$ for danio, $3392$ for \textit{E. coli}, $11531$ for human, and $5105$ for yeast.  The key energy diagnostics are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{danio:} && E(X_Q \to Y) &= 1092.0114819316218, & E(T \to Y) &= 16.418999670692678, & E(X_Q \mid T) &= 1144.4442608870015, \\
\textit{E. coli:} && E(X_Q \to Y) &= 393.78692134854015, & E(T \to Y) &= 35.56347808682994, & E(X_Q \mid T) &= 377.67647487021645, \\
\text{human:} && E(X_Q \to Y) &= 565.3850203147235, & E(T \to Y) &= 2.0678517436514716, & E(X_Q \mid T) &= 563.803837143658, \\
\text{yeast:} && E(X_Q \to Y) &= 675.3275481062249, & E(T \to Y) &= 593.5847863333478, & E(X_Q \mid T) &= 351.63835021403213.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values separate the organisms into four empirical regimes.  Yeast is the clearest measured-TE absorption case.  \textit{E. coli} has a positive but modest measured-TE reduction.  Human has measured TE survival but almost no absorption of the direct protein-abundance signal.  Danio has positive $S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}$ and positive $S^{QP}$, but conditioning on measured TE does not reduce the direct $Q \to P$ term on the raw decomposition.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant gives a useful bounded comparison, not a substitute for measured TE.  In the modeled discriminant, yeast assigns all nine coordinates to case $D$, while \textit{E. coli} assigns seven coordinates to case $D$ and has two positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop.  The modeled readout has $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, and $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \textit{E. coli}.  The measured-TE experiment is stricter: it asks whether ribosome-derived $TE$, not modeled tAI, absorbs the direct abundance association.  Under this measured condition, yeast remains the dominant positive case, while human and danio show that positive $S^{QT}$ and positive $S^{QP}$ alone are insufficient to assert measured translation mediation.

\paragraph{Related reduction checks.}
The broader finite-row audit supports the same caution.  The joint TE-and-mRNA-stability fit reports joint mediation fractions $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, $0.2404669586626661$ in \textit{E. coli}, and $0.16890712099923358$ in human, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ under the fixed pair-permutation scheme.  The yeast partial-mediation certificate uses $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations; it obtains coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ over the readout dictionary $\{\text{measured TE},\text{mRNA stability},\text{turnover}\}$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ after the selection-aware null threshold $0.029149007242956115$.  The irreducibility check uses the same $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$ context, and its status records that the source signal is real while at least one listed reduction family refutes the stronger irreducibility hypothesis.  For the $f3\_stress$ coordinate in yeast, the dwell-mediated abundance test has $n=1257$, base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null $95\%$ value $0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $f3$--dwell correlation $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Its positional counterpart classifies the dominant segment as $5prime$, with the $5prime$ window passing and the $3prime$ window not passing.  The structural-order positional readout is classified as $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$, with weighted ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip value $0.05859375$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance claim is ready as a finite statistical readback.  The admitted statement is that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive against measured TE and measured protein abundance in a rank-powered joined panel, and that conditioning on measured TE absorbs a large part of the direct protein-abundance association in yeast, a small part in \textit{E. coli}, almost none in human, and none after clipping in danio.  This is not a causal mediation result.  It does not establish translation mechanism, elongation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, localization, turnover, molecular function, system phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic at that layer.  The present record is therefore a measured-readout decomposition of codon residuals, translation efficiency, and protein abundance, with explicit row counts, ranks, hit rates, and null-calibrated companion reductions.
