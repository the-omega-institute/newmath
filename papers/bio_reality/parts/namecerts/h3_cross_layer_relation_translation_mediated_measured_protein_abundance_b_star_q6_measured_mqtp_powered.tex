\subsection{Measured translation conditioning of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-translation-protein-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The finite object in this record is a same-CDS matrix over nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, and a measured protein-abundance readout $P$.  The coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation coordinate is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ computed as ribosome footprint divided by mRNA in the local organism files.  The protein coordinate is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from matched proteomics abundance joined by protein identifier.  The nuisance design has $24$ control columns, rank $23$ after residualization, and contains CDS length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  In every organism used for the measured mediation run, the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the residualized $T$ block has rank $1$, and the joint $Q,T$ block has rank $10$.

\paragraph{Joined measured panels.}
Four organism panels are sufficiently joined for the measured $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition.  The three-way CDS--TE--abundance joins have $n=13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $n=5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding measured-TE contact has join rates $0.9118667591950035$, $0.9133458143888741$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.8818572656921754$.  The broader direct protein-omics panel confirms that abundance rows are not a small-row artifact: the PAXdb joins include $14410$ danio CDS rows, $3739$ \emph{E. coli} rows, $19053$ human rows, and $5815$ yeast rows, with direct proteomics join rates $1.0$, $0.9978649586335735$, $0.9779294769799313$, and $0.9156038419146592$ respectively.

\paragraph{Measured mediation decomposition.}
The measured-mediation scalar is computed by asking how much of the residual $Q \to P$ association is removed when the measured $TE$ coordinate is included.  The raw and clipped finite-row values are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{raw}(danio) &= -0.04801486048721136, & M^{QTP}_{meas}(danio) &= 0.0,\\
M^{QTP}_{raw}(E.\ coli) &= 0.04091158340950673, & M^{QTP}_{meas}(E.\ coli) &= 0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP}_{raw}(human) &= 0.0027966485036786744, & M^{QTP}_{meas}(human) &= 0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP}_{raw}(yeast) &= 0.4793069656345167, & M^{QTP}_{meas}(yeast) &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the measured protein-abundance signal is not uniformly absorbed by measured TE.  Yeast is the strong positive case: nearly one half of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance association is accounted for by measured TE conditioning.  \emph{E. coli} is a weak positive case, human is near-zero but positive, and danio is suppressive on the raw scale and is therefore read as zero measured mediation after the unit-interval projection.  This is a four-row statistical pattern, not a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The residual degrees of freedom after controls are large in every measured panel: $13117$ for danio, $3392$ for \emph{E. coli}, $11531$ for human, and $5105$ for yeast.  The $Q$ block explains $144.76301201119642$ units of translation-readout energy in danio, $8.637891149939946$ in \emph{E. coli}, $38.66659710161269$ in human, and $103.10404057290246$ in yeast.  On protein abundance, the same $Q$ block explains $1092.0114819316218$, $393.78692134854015$, $565.3850203147235$, and $675.3275481062249$ units respectively.  Conditioning on measured $T$ leaves direct $Q$ improvements of $1144.4442608870015$ in danio, $377.67647487021645$ in \emph{E. coli}, $563.803837143658$ in human, and $351.63835021403213$ in yeast.  The yeast reduction is the clearest finite-row mediation signal; the other organisms retain substantial direct $Q$ signal after $T$ is included.

\paragraph{Relation to the four-case readback.}
The bounded four-case classifier separates translation-surviving, protein-surviving, and translation-conditioned protein-surviving behavior.  In the modeled-tAI classifier, \emph{E. coli} has case counts $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=7$, with $2$ positive-positive coordinates lacking a mediation drop; yeast has $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$.  Those modeled-tAI labels are not substituted for measured ribosome TE, but they give a useful comparison: the measured TE run preserves the yeast pattern as a strong translation-conditioned abundance route, while the \emph{E. coli} measured mediation fraction is much smaller despite positive $S^{QT}_{meas}$ and $S^{QP}$.  The classification therefore supports a local readback discipline: $S^{QP}>0$ alone is a direct protein-omics association, while a positive $S^{QT}_{meas}$ together with attenuation after conditioning on $T$ is the finite sign of measured translation-conditioned abundance.

\paragraph{Competing measured readouts.}
The joint TE--mRNA-stability decomposition supplies a second finite check on interpretation.  In the three-organism joint run, the joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The single-mediator split is organism-specific: \emph{E. coli} has measured-TE-only fraction $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only fraction $0.23052056324898387$; human has $0.00549490864190343$ and $0.15314679870183337$; yeast has $0.4697188319693239$ and $0.0626618595344251$.  This agrees with the measured mediation row: yeast is TE-dominant, whereas \emph{E. coli} and human carry much of the reducible abundance association through non-TE abundance-side covariates.  A selection-aware readout dictionary in yeast gives coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ on $n=1800$ rows.  The dictionary result says that a small measured-readout family is real but incomplete.

The $f3\_stress$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional run classifies the leading dwell segment as $5prime$, with the $5prime$ segment passing and the $3prime$ segment not passing.  A separate structural-order ramp readout is weaker: the primary $50$-codon ramp has weighted $f3$ ramp held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, ramp sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip $p=0.05859375$, so its finite classification is $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$.  These rows reinforce that the abundance residual has measured readout structure, while also showing that not every downstream biological description becomes licensed.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The record is ready as a measured, rank-powered, cross-sectional statistical decomposition.  The finite-row support is: positive measured $Q \to T$ survival in all four measured panels; positive $Q \to P$ survival in the same panels; a large yeast measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$; smaller positive measured fractions in \emph{E. coli} and human; and a suppressive danio row that is explicitly clipped to zero rather than promoted.  The correct scientific readback is therefore not that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ causes translation or protein abundance, but that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface has a measured TE-conditioned abundance component, strongest in yeast, weak in \emph{E. coli}, near-null in human, and absent after clipping in danio.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation process, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from this chapter alone.  Measured TE supplies a bounded translation-efficiency readout, and PAXdb abundance supplies a bounded protein-abundance readout; both are observational, condition-dependent, and joined across available identifiers.  A downstream claim about elongation mechanism, initiation mechanism, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, physical feasibility, function, phenotype, or evolutionary direction would require a separate matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.  Direct $Q \to P$ survival remains only a protein-omics association unless a positive $Q \to T$ step is present and conditioning on $T$ reduces the $Q \to P$ signal on the same joined rows.
