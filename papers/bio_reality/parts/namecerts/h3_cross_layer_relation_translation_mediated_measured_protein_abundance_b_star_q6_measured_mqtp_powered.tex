\subsection{Measured translation conditioning for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-mqtp}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface reaches protein abundance through a measured translation-efficiency readout rather than only through a direct codon-to-protein association.  The finite carrier is the joined matrix of nine residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress, with a scalar measured translation readout $T$ and measured protein abundance $Y$ on the same coding-sequence rows.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, where $TE$ is ribosome footprint divided by mRNA abundance in the local ribosome-TE files; the protein readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm, joined by protein identifier.  The control block has 24 columns, residual rank $rank(Z)=23$, and includes length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  After residualization, every organism in the measured mediation panel remains rank-powered: $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, $rank(\widetilde T)=1$, and $rank(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$.

\paragraph{Joined measured rows.}
The measured three-way joins are non-vacuous.  Danio rerio contributes 13,140 codon--TE--abundance rows from 14,410 reported CDS joins, with 13,140 valid TE proteins and 14,410 valid abundance proteins.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 contributes 3,415 rows from 3,739 reported CDS joins, with 3,415 valid TE proteins and 3,747 valid abundance proteins.  Homo sapiens contributes 11,554 rows from 19,053 reported CDS joins, with 11,554 valid TE proteins and 19,483 valid abundance proteins.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae contributes 5,128 rows from 5,815 reported CDS joins, with 5,128 valid TE proteins and 6,351 valid abundance proteins.  Thus the test is not the old rank-degenerate three-organism boundary: each fitted organism has a full nine-dimensional residual coordinate block after the same control family.

\paragraph{Measured translation survival and direct protein survival.}
The measured $Q\to T$ survival is positive in all four measured-TE organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{danio}} &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{human}} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding direct $Q\to Y$ survival values in the same measured-mediation fit are also positive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{danio}} &= 0.04926578454614652,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &= 0.10941535951828074,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{human}} &= 0.04288464996165745,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.3146818503437042.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values justify reporting a measured translation-readout association and a protein-abundance association.  They do not by themselves establish a biological mechanism, because both readouts are cross-sectional and condition-specific, and the fitted attenuation is a statistical decomposition.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
The measured mediation scalar is computed by conditioning the direct $Q\to Y$ signal on residualized measured $T$.  The raw and clipped mediated fractions separate the four organisms sharply.  Danio has a suppressive raw value $M^{QTP,meas}_{raw}=-0.04801486048721136$, so its reported measured mediated fraction is $0$.  E. coli has $M^{QTP,meas}=0.04091158340950673$; human has $M^{QTP,meas}=0.0027966485036786744$; yeast has $M^{QTP,meas}=0.4793069656345167$.  The yeast entry is the dominant positive measured attenuation: nearly half of the direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance association is removed by conditioning on measured ribosome-TE, while E. coli and human show only small measured fractions and danio shows no positive measured fraction after clipping to the unit interval.

\paragraph{Energy diagnostics.}
The residual energy and improvement diagnostics show that the mediation statistic is computed on an actual fitted surface rather than on labels alone.  For danio, $\widetilde X$ explains 144.76301201119642 units of $T$ energy and 1092.0114819316218 units of $Y$ energy, while the direct $X$ improvement after $T$ is 1144.4442608870015.  For E. coli, the corresponding values are 8.637891149939946, 393.78692134854015, and 377.67647487021645.  For human they are 38.66659710161269, 565.3850203147235, and 563.803837143658.  For yeast they are 103.10404057290246, 675.3275481062249, and 351.63835021403213.  The yeast pattern is the clearest measured translation-conditioned case because $T$ also explains 593.5847863333478 units of $Y$ energy and adds 269.89558844115504 units after $X$; in contrast, human has only 2.0678517436514716 units of $T$-on-$Y$ energy and 0.486668572585927 units of $T$ improvement after $X$.

\paragraph{Relation to broader readout dictionaries.}
The direct protein-omics screen across fifteen organisms reported positive $S^{QP}$ entries for all nine coordinates, with examples ranging from 0.019256877907572044 in Halobacterium salinarum to 0.29837624994140827 in yeast.  That broader screen is useful as a downstream abundance boundary, but it is not a translation-mediated result.  A separate joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on yeast, human, and E. coli shows that measured TE and mRNA stability can jointly absorb substantial fractions of the same abundance residual: 0.5195182225621592 in yeast, 0.16890712099923358 in human, and 0.2404669586626661 in E. coli, with right-tail permutation value 0.000999000999000999 in each case.  The finite readout-dictionary certificate in yeast selected a small family with coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ at $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, leaving unexplained fraction 0.7546178856026946 after a selection-aware null95 of 0.029149007242956115.  The $f_{3}$ dwell test also found a yeast held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.01856473944057712$ against null95 0.0013115362189692937 and an approximate $f_{3}$--dwell $p$-value of $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These neighboring finite rows support the interpretation that the abundance residual is partly readable through measured translation and dwell-like quantities, but remains incompletely explained.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore narrow.  The measured panel supports a positive $B^{\ast}_{Q6}\to T$ readout in all four tested organisms, a positive direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}\to Y$ readout in the same rows, and a measured translation-conditioned attenuation that is strong in yeast, small in E. coli and human, and absent after clipping in danio.  It does not license a claim that synonymous residual geometry alone determines translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Such claims would require their own layer-matched contacts, perturbations or structural/function assays where appropriate, and separate controlled statistics.  Protein abundance here is a measured omics endpoint, and measured TE is a bounded translation-efficiency readout; neither is a rescue experiment or a causal ordering proof.
