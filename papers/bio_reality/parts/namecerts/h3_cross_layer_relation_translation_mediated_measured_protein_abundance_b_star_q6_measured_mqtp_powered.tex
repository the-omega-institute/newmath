\subsection{Measured translation conditioning of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance surface}\label{sec:biorality-q6-measured-translation-conditioned-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record studied here is the finite matrix surface obtained after joining coding-sequence codon counts, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, matched protein abundance, and the transcript controls needed to form translation efficiency.  The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
All entries are read after residualizing against the declared control block: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the powered measured-readout run, the control matrix has $23$ residual rank units and $24$ control columns in each organism-specific fit.  The translation readout is $T=\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$, with $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{RPF}/\mathrm{mRNA}$ from the local ribosome-profiling table; the protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ from the matched proteomics table.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The experiment is rank-powered rather than a three-organism saturated surface.  The joined measured-TE, abundance, and CDS rows are $3415$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $5128$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $11554$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $13140$ for \emph{Danio rerio}.  The corresponding reported CDS joins are $3739$, $5815$, $19053$, and $14410$, respectively.  Protein-abundance rows keyed by protein id are $3747$, $6351$, $19483$, and $14410$ in the same order, while valid measured-TE rows are exactly the three-way join counts.  After control residualization, the residual degrees of freedom are $3392$, $5105$, $11531$, and $13117$.  The residual design ranks are stable: $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$ in all four organisms, so the comparison is not a rank-degenerate all-fit artifact.

\paragraph{Measured mediation surface.}
Let $S^{QP}$ denote the direct residual association from the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block to protein abundance, let $S^{QT}$ denote the residual association from the same coordinate block to measured translation efficiency, and let $M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}$ denote the scalar fraction of the $Q\to P$ association removed or reduced after conditioning on the measured translation readout.  The computed finite-row surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{E.\ coli}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.1094153595,\quad S^{QT}=0.0093942668,\quad M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.0409115834,\\
\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.3146818503,\quad S^{QT}=0.1368193134,\quad M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.4793069656,\\
\mathrm{H.\ sapiens}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.0428846500,\quad S^{QT}=0.0188379113,\quad M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.0027966485,\\
\mathrm{D.\ rerio}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.0492657845,\quad S^{QT}=0.0403421761,\quad M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw mediation scalar for \emph{D. rerio} is negative, $-0.0480148605$, and is therefore read as a suppressive or null measured-mediation outcome rather than hidden support.  The strongest measured translation-conditioned absorption is in yeast, where nearly $47.93\%$ of the direct $Q\to P$ signal is removed by the measured TE coordinate.  \emph{E. coli} has a positive but small measured fraction, about $4.09\%$.  Human has positive translation survival and protein association but almost no measured TE absorption, about $0.28\%$.  Zebrafish has positive $S^{QT}$ and positive $S^{QP}$ but no admissible positive measured mediation fraction.

\paragraph{Energy audit.}
The scalar calls above are backed by explicit residual-energy comparisons.  For \emph{E. coli}, $X$ explains $393.7869213$ units of protein-abundance residual energy, while the direct $X$ improvement after measured $T$ remains $377.6764749$; $T$ itself explains $35.5634781$ on $Y$, and $X$ explains $8.6378911$ on $T$.  For yeast, $X$ explains $675.3275481$ on $Y$, but the direct improvement after $T$ drops to $351.6383502$; this is the finite-row source of the large measured mediation fraction.  For human, $X$ explains $565.3850203$ on $Y$ and still explains $563.8038371$ after $T$, so measured TE barely absorbs the protein-abundance association despite $X$ explaining $38.6665971$ on $T$.  For zebrafish, $X$ explains $1092.0114819$ on $Y$, and the direct improvement after $T$ is larger, $1144.4442609$, yielding the suppressive raw sign.  The measured translation residual energies are $919.4854027$, $753.5781169$, $2052.5947062$, and $3588.3788590$ for \emph{E. coli}, yeast, human, and zebrafish; the matched protein residual energies are $3599.0095274$, $2146.0645009$, $13183.8553147$, and $22165.7178911$.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The finite data support a four-case interpretation, not a single mechanism call.  Case A is translation-null and protein-null.  Case B is translation-surviving but protein-null.  Case C is direct protein-omics prediction without measured translation mediation.  Case D is measured translation-conditioned protein mediation, requiring positive $S^{QT}$, positive $S^{QP}$, and positive attenuation of $Q\to P$ after conditioning on $T$.  Under this rule, yeast is the clearest Case D entry in the supplied surface.  \emph{E. coli} is weak Case D.  Human is mainly Case C with a very small measured-mediation component.  Zebrafish is a measured translation/protein co-survival entry with suppressive conditioning, so it is not promoted to Case D.

\paragraph{Relation to the joint stability audit.}
A related four-way decomposition with measured TE and mRNA stability reports right-tail permutation values $p=0.000999000999000999$ for \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast.  In that run the TE-only fractions are $0.0197552134$, $0.0054949086$, and $0.4697188320$, while the stability-only fractions are $0.2305205632$, $0.1531467987$, and $0.0626618595$.  The joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669587$, $0.1689071210$, and $0.5195182226$.  This related audit agrees with the measured-TE chapter in placing yeast as the strongest translation-conditioned abundance case, while showing that in bacteria and human a substantial part of the abundance residual can sit outside measured TE alone.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter records a controlled, cross-sectional variance-partition result.  It does not establish a causal translation mechanism, temporal ordering, synonymous-edit response, perturbation rescue, elongation model, initiation model, turnover pathway, localization pathway, post-translational modification, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universal biological law.  A translation claim is permitted here only to the extent supplied by the measured TE contact itself.  Any stronger claim must be supplied by a separate layer-matched contact and by its own finite statistic.  In particular, direct $Q\to P$ survival is not a mechanism statement unless the measured $Q\to T$ step survives and conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the direct $Q\to P$ association.
