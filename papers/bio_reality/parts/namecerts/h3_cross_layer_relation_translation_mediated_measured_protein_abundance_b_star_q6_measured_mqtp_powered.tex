\subsection{Measured translation conditioning of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-measured-mqtp}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the finite row surface obtained by joining coding-sequence codon counts, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, and measured protein abundance.  The nine residual coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}\text{-stress}$.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ defined as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA in the local ribosome-profiling files.  The abundance readout is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in ppm from the matched proteomics rows.  Each organism-level fit first removes the same covariate block: intercept, coding length, twenty amino-acid composition coordinates, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  In all four measured mediation organisms the control block has $24$ columns, residualized control rank $23$, residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ rank $9$, residualized translation rank $1$, and combined $Q,T$ rank $10$.

\paragraph{Joined measured rows.}
The measured mediation surface is not an organism-level codon table alone; it is a same-row codon--translation--protein panel.  The joined row counts are $13140$ for Danio rerio, $3415$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $11554$ for Homo sapiens, and $5128$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  These joins are grounded in larger CDS and readout inventories: Danio has $14410$ CDS-abundance rows and $13140$ valid translation-efficiency rows; E. coli has $3739$ reported CDS rows, $3747$ valid abundance rows, and $3415$ valid translation-efficiency rows; human has $19053$ reported CDS rows, $19483$ valid abundance rows, and $11554$ valid translation-efficiency rows; yeast has $5815$ reported CDS rows, $6351$ valid abundance rows, and $5128$ valid translation-efficiency rows.  The companion measured-TE survival audit gives ribosome-TE join hit rates $0.9118667591950035$ for Danio, $0.9133458143888741$ for E. coli, $0.6064136881331024$ for human, and $0.8818572656921754$ for yeast.  The larger direct protein-omics scan also reports high abundance joins across the broader organism panel, including $0.9978649586335735$ for E. coli, $0.9779294769799313$ for human, $0.9156038419146592$ for yeast, and $1.0$ for Danio.

\paragraph{Residual decomposition.}
Let $X$ denote the residualized nine-column $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ matrix, $T$ the residualized measured translation-efficiency column, and $Y$ the residualized measured protein-abundance column.  The mediated fraction used here is a finite variance-partition scalar: it compares the direct $X \to Y$ fit with the remaining $X \to Y$ improvement after conditioning on $T$, and truncates suppressive negative mediation to zero for readback.  The organism-level measured survival and mediation values are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Danio:} \quad & S^{QP}=0.04926578454614652,\quad S^{QT}=0.04034217614654355,\quad M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{raw}}=-0.04801486048721136,\quad M^{QTP}=0,\\
\text{E. coli:} \quad & S^{QP}=0.10941535951828074,\quad S^{QT}=0.009394266754822262,\quad M^{QTP}=0.04091158340950673,\\
\text{Human:} \quad & S^{QP}=0.04288464996165745,\quad S^{QT}=0.018837911344127926,\quad M^{QTP}=0.0027966485036786744,\\
\text{Yeast:} \quad & S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042,\quad S^{QT}=0.13681931343730436,\quad M^{QTP}=0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
All four mediated-fraction readbacks lie in the unit interval after the declared truncation rule.  The conclusion is therefore not a uniform yes/no statement.  Danio has positive measured translation survival and positive direct protein-abundance association, but the measured translation readout does not absorb the direct signal in this scalar decomposition.  E. coli and human have positive but small measured mediated fractions.  Yeast is the strongest measured case: its $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association is large, its measured translation survival is also substantial, and conditioning on measured translation removes about $0.4793069656345167$ of the direct $Q \to P$ signal in this finite fit.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The four-case discriminant separates direct abundance association from translation-conditioned absorption.  In the modeled-tAI discriminant audit, E. coli has case counts $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=7$, with two positive-positive coordinates lacking a mediation drop, while yeast has $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$.  The measured-TE mediation surface in this chapter is stricter: it uses ribosome-profiling $TE$ rather than modeled tAI.  Under this stricter measured contact, yeast remains the principal positive-mediated organism; E. coli and human are weak measured-mediated cases; Danio is positive for both $Q \to T$ and $Q \to P$ but reads back as non-mediated or suppressive in the declared scalar.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible statement is only that the finite joined rows support a powered, controlled, cross-sectional statistical decomposition of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual association with measured protein abundance through a measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency column.  It does not establish causal mediation, temporal ordering, elongation mechanism, initiation mechanism, synonymous-edit rescue, perturbation response, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.  A translation-layer measurement is present here, but a broader translation mechanism or biological law would require a separate contact with its own finite rows and its own controls.  Likewise, protein abundance is not a folded-structure, localization, turnover, post-translational-modification, pathway, or function assay.  The safe readback is therefore: yeast gives a strong measured translation-conditioned attenuation of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual; E. coli and human give small positive measured attenuation; Danio gives positive translation and abundance associations without measured attenuation under this scalar; all higher biological interpretations remain outside the finite evidence supplied by this row surface.
