\subsection{Measured translation conditioning of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-translation-protein-conditioning}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row and readouts.}
This record tests a cross-layer claim about the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$ on a matched coding-sequence panel.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ computed as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA abundance from the organism-local ribosome-translation-efficiency rows.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from matched proteomics abundance rows joined by protein identifier.  The fitted surface uses a joint nine-dimensional $X_Q$ block, a one-dimensional measured translation block $T$, and protein abundance $Y$, after residualizing against amino-acid composition, organism-scoped codon controls, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls.

The four organisms have nondegenerate joined panels.  Danio rerio has $14410$ reported CDS abundance rows and $13140$ three-way joined TE-abundance-codon rows.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $3739$ reported CDS rows, $3747$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $3415$ three-way joined rows.  Homo sapiens has $19053$ reported CDS rows, $19483$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $11554$ three-way joined rows.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5815$ reported CDS rows, $6351$ valid abundance rows by protein, and $5128$ three-way joined rows.  In every organism the control matrix has $24$ columns, residual rank $rank_Z=23$, $rank(X)=9$, $rank(T)=1$, and $rank(X,T)=10$, so the reported surface is not an all-fit rank artifact.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $13117$ in Danio rerio, $3392$ in Escherichia coli, $11531$ in Homo sapiens, and $5105$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

\paragraph{Controlled mediation statistic.}
The statistic compares three residualized projections: the codon-residual block to measured translation, the codon-residual block to protein abundance, and the remaining codon-residual to protein-abundance signal after the measured translation coordinate is included.  In notation, the finite-row computation separates the measured translation survival $S^{QT}$, the protein-abundance survival $S^{QP}$, and the measured statistical mediation fraction $M^{QTP,meas}$.  The mediation fraction is truncated to the unit interval when the raw decomposition is suppressive, so a negative raw value is reported as measured non-mediation rather than being promoted into a mechanism.

$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{Danio} &= 0.0403421761, & S^{QP}_{Danio} &= 0.0492657845, & M^{QTP,meas}_{Danio} &= 0.0000000000,\\
S^{QT}_{E.coli} &= 0.0093942668, & S^{QP}_{E.coli} &= 0.1094153595, & M^{QTP,meas}_{E.coli} &= 0.0409115834,\\
S^{QT}_{Human} &= 0.0188379113, & S^{QP}_{Human} &= 0.0428846500, & M^{QTP,meas}_{Human} &= 0.0027966485,\\
S^{QT}_{Yeast} &= 0.1368193134, & S^{QP}_{Yeast} &= 0.3146818503, & M^{QTP,meas}_{Yeast} &= 0.4793069656.
\end{aligned}
$$

The direct protein-abundance signal is therefore present in all four organisms, but the measured translation readout absorbs very different fractions of that signal.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae is the strongest finite-row support case: $S^{QT}=0.1368193134$, $S^{QP}=0.3146818503$, and the measured mediated fraction is $0.4793069656$.  Escherichia coli is a weaker positive case with $M^{QTP,meas}=0.0409115834$.  Homo sapiens is positive but nearly direct, with $M^{QTP,meas}=0.0027966485$.  Danio rerio has a positive translation-readout survival and a positive protein-abundance survival, but its raw mediation fraction is $-0.0480148605$, so the admissible measured mediation readback is $0$.

\paragraph{Energy decomposition.}
The residual energy terms show why the four organisms should not be collapsed into a single qualitative label.  In Danio rerio, $X_Q$ explains $1092.0114819316$ units of residual protein-abundance energy, while measured $T$ explains only $16.4189996707$ units on $Y$ alone; after conditioning on $T$, the remaining direct $X_Q$ improvement is $1144.4442608870$.  This is a suppressive or non-mediating pattern, despite positive $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$.  In Escherichia coli, $X_Q$ explains $393.7869213485$ on $Y$, $T$ explains $35.5634780868$ on $Y$, and the direct $X_Q$ improvement after $T$ is $377.6764748702$, leaving only a small measured mediation component.  In Homo sapiens, $X_Q$ explains $565.3850203147$ on $Y$, whereas $T$ explains $2.0678517437$ on $Y$ and the direct $X_Q$ improvement after $T$ remains $563.8038371437$, making the measured translation-conditioned absorption almost absent.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $T$ explains $593.5847863333$ units on $Y$, $X_Q$ explains $675.3275481062$ units on $Y$, and the direct $X_Q$ improvement after $T$ is reduced to $351.6383502140$, giving the only large measured mediation fraction in the panel.

The corresponding translation-side energies are also organism-specific.  The $X_Q$ block explains $144.7630120112$ units of residual measured translation energy in Danio rerio, $8.6378911499$ in Escherichia coli, $38.6665971016$ in Homo sapiens, and $103.1040405729$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  Since the measured translation residual energies are $3588.3788590219$, $919.4854026799$, $2052.5947062420$, and $753.5781168800$, respectively, the positive $S^{QT}$ entries are bounded translation-readout associations rather than direct statements about ribosome mechanism.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The finite row supports a four-case interpretation after controls.  Case A is translation-null and protein-null.  None of the four organisms falls into this case because all four have positive $S^{QT}$ and positive $S^{QP}$.  Case B is translation-surviving but protein-null.  It is also not observed here, because $S^{QP}$ remains positive in each organism.  Case C is direct or confounded protein-omics survival: $S^{QP}$ is positive, but measured translation does not materially absorb the $Q \to P$ association.  Danio rerio belongs to this class because the raw mediated fraction is negative and the admissible measured mediation is $0$; Homo sapiens is also practically in this class because $M^{QTP,meas}=0.0027966485$ despite positive survival on both marginal readouts.  Case D is measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation: $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ are positive and conditioning on $T$ removes or reduces the direct $Q \to P$ association by a nontrivial amount.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae is the clear Case D row, while Escherichia coli is a weak positive Case D row.

\paragraph{Adjacent nontranslation check.}
A separate localization-oriented finite row tests whether the modeled complement of the measured-translation abundance residual can be absorbed by coarse UniProtKB reviewed subcellular-localization categories.  That row uses the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and $24$ control columns, but joins to localization availability rather than to the measured TE-abundance panel.  The joined localization counts are $1984$ in Danio rerio, $16008$ in Homo sapiens, and $4656$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with $2821$, $16406$, and $4831$ localization records respectively.  The observed localization absorption fractions are $0.0095088645$, $0.0092549133$, and $0.0112251056$, while the degree-of-freedom matched null $95$th percentiles are $0.0113213287$, $0.0013758521$, and $0.0049581170$.  Thus the localization row is not a substitute for measured translation mediation.  It is a separate downstream descriptive projection, and only Homo sapiens and Saccharomyces cerevisiae exceed their own matched null thresholds in that localization audit.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter records a finite controlled statistical relation, not a causal biological mechanism.  The measured translation coordinate comes from condition-specific ribosome-profiling TE data, the protein coordinate comes from cross-sectional abundance estimates, and the joined rows do not establish temporal order, synonymous-edit response, perturbation rescue, elongation mechanism, initiation mechanism, protein turnover, localization mechanism, post-translational state, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.  A positive $S^{QT}$ value permits only a bounded measured translation-readout association under the stated controls.  A positive $S^{QP}$ value permits only a bounded protein-abundance association under the stated controls.  A positive $M^{QTP,meas}$ value permits only the statement that conditioning on measured TE reduces part of the residual protein-abundance association in the observed finite panel.  Any stronger claim about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or a general biological law requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
