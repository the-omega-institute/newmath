\subsection{Measured Translation-Conditioned Protein-Abundance Mediation for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Surface}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-measured-translation-protein-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and matched rows.}
This record concerns a finite matched matrix whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a measured translation-efficiency readout, and a matched protein-abundance readout.  The residual coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, where $TE$ is measured as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA abundance in the local ribosome-profiling files.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm, joined by protein identifier.  The four three-way joins are substantial: $13140$ joined CDS rows for zebrafish, $3415$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ for human, and $5128$ for budding yeast.  These joins are not rank-boundary artifacts: after the shared covariate residualization, each organism has residual rank $9$ for the $Q$ block, rank $1$ for the measured $T$ block, rank $10$ for the joint $Q,T$ block, and rank $23$ for the control block.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
All reported quantities are computed after removing the same finite control family: intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $13117$ in zebrafish, $3392$ in \emph{E. coli}, $11531$ in human, and $5105$ in yeast.  The measured translation residual energies are $3588.378859021949$, $919.4854026798789$, $2052.594706241978$, and $753.5781168800304$, respectively; the matched abundance residual energies are $22165.717891059892$, $3599.0095273849333$, $13183.855314669145$, and $2146.0645009193045$. \paragraph{Measured mediation readback.}
The measured mediation scalar compares the direct $Q \to P$ abundance association with the residual direct association left after conditioning on measured $TE$.  The raw and clipped readbacks separate the four organisms sharply.  Zebrafish has positive measured $Q \to T$ and $Q \to P$ surfaces, but its raw mediation fraction is negative, $-0.04801486048721136$, so the admitted measured fraction is $0.0$.  \emph{E. coli} has a small positive measured mediation fraction, $0.04091158340950673$.  Human has a still smaller positive fraction, $0.0027966485036786744$.  Yeast is the strong measured-mediation case, with $0.4793069656345167$ of the direct abundance association absorbed by the measured translation-efficiency readout.  Equivalently, the four finite outcomes are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}_{\mathrm{danio}} &= 0.0,\\
M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}_{\mathrm{ecoli}} &= 0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}_{\mathrm{human}} &= 0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row verdict is therefore not that every positive abundance association is translation-mediated.  It is that the measured translation readout supplies a positive but uneven attenuation channel, with yeast showing a large measured component, \emph{E. coli} and human showing weak measured components, and zebrafish showing a suppressive or null measured component under the same formal decomposition.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
This subsection licenses only a controlled finite-row statement about codon-residual association, measured translation efficiency, and matched protein abundance.  It does not say that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ geometry causes translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Measured ribosome-profiling $TE$ is condition-specific and observational; PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-omics readout; the joined rows are cross-sectional and do not determine temporal order.  A stronger biological assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as perturbation, rescue, folding, localization, turnover, physical assay, or functional assay data.  Within the present finite matrix, the admissible conclusion is narrower: the measured $TE$ column is a real conditioning readout, and in yeast it absorbs nearly half of the controlled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance association, while the other organisms show weak, null, or suppressive measured mediation under the same row-wise construction.
