\subsection{Measured translation conditioning of B$^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-mqtp}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.} The measured-protein mediation packet is a four-organism finite matrix surface on nine B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency column, one measured protein-abundance column, and a shared covariate block. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism the covariate block has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residualized B$^{*}_{Q6}$ rank $9$. The readout $T$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$, where TE is ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA in the local ribosome-profiling file. The readout $P$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ from matched PAXdb-style protein abundance joined by protein identifier. The tested relation is not a bare $Q\to P$ association; it asks how much of the codon-residual protein-abundance association is absorbed after conditioning on measured $T$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.} The three-way codon--TE--protein joins are rank-powered rather than the small-organism saturated case. \emph{Danio rerio} joins $13140$ rows from $14410$ CDS rows, with $13140$ valid TE records and $14410$ valid abundance records. \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 joins $3415$ rows from $3739$ CDS rows, with $3415$ valid TE records and $3747$ valid abundance records. \emph{Homo sapiens} joins $11554$ rows from $19053$ CDS rows, with $11554$ valid TE records and $19483$ valid abundance records. \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} joins $5128$ rows from $5815$ CDS rows, with $5128$ valid TE records and $6351$ valid abundance records. In all four cases the residualized translation column has rank $1$, the joint $Q,T$ design has rank $10$, and the rank tolerance is $10^{-10}$.

\paragraph{Measured survival and mediation scalars.} The finite readback is the organism-indexed list of $S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}$, $S^{QP}$, and measured mediation fraction. The observed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio}:&\quad S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.0403421761,\quad S^{QP}=0.0492657845,\quad M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0,\\
\mathrm{E.\ coli}:&\quad S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.00939426675,\quad S^{QP}=0.1094153595,\quad M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.0409115834,\\
\mathrm{Human}:&\quad S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.0188379113,\quad S^{QP}=0.0428846500,\quad M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.00279664850,\\
\mathrm{Yeast}:&\quad S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.1368193134,\quad S^{QP}=0.3146818503,\quad M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.4793069656.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw mediated fraction for \emph{Danio} is negative, $-0.0480148605$, so the clipped measured fraction is reported as $0$. The other three organisms retain positive measured fractions. Yeast is the strongest measured-readout case: its B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals have the largest measured TE survival, the largest direct protein-abundance survival, and nearly half of the direct $Q\to P$ association is absorbed when measured TE is included.

\paragraph{Energy audit.} The row-level regression audit records both the direct codon-residual contribution and the contribution left after measured TE is included. For \emph{Danio}, $Q$ explains $1092.0114819$ units of protein residual energy, while the direct improvement after $T$ is $1144.4442609$, giving a suppressive measured-TE readback. For \emph{E. coli}, $Q$ explains $393.7869213$ and the direct improvement after $T$ is $377.6764749$. For human, the corresponding values are $565.3850203$ and $563.8038371$. For yeast, they are $675.3275481$ and $351.6383502$, the clearest attenuation in the finite table. The measured TE column alone explains $593.5847863$ units of yeast protein-residual energy, compared with $35.5634781$ in \emph{E. coli}, $16.4189997$ in \emph{Danio}, and $2.06785174$ in human. Thus the same formal surface separates a yeast translation-conditioned case from weaker or nearly direct protein-omics cases.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.} The packet realizes the D-like case only where both $Q\to T$ and $Q\to P$ survive and the measured translation readout reduces the direct $Q\to P$ signal. Yeast is the primary positive finite row under this criterion. \emph{E. coli} and human have positive measured mediation fractions, but their attenuation is small relative to their direct $Q\to P$ signal; they remain bounded statistical mediation rows rather than mechanism rows. \emph{Danio} has positive $S^{QT}_{\mathrm{meas}}$ and positive $S^{QP}$, but the measured TE conditioning is suppressive rather than absorptive, so it is read as direct or confounded protein-omics association with no measured mediation support.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.} This chapter only licenses the finite statistical statement above. Measured ribosome-profiling TE is condition-specific, cross-sectional, and often drawn from one or a small number of studies. PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-omics readout. Therefore these rows do not establish causal translation, elongation mechanism, synonymous-edit rescue, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule. Those claims require their own layer-matched contact and their own finite statistic. In particular, a positive $S^{QP}$ entry alone is not a mechanism claim, and a positive $M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}$ entry is a variance-decomposition result, not a causal mediation effect.
