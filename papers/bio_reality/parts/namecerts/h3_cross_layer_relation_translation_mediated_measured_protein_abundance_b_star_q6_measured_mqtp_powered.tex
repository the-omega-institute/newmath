\subsection{Measured Translation-Efficiency Conditioning of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Protein-Abundance Signal}
\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-measured-mqtp}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a matched finite surface whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns are the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, and a measured protein-abundance readout $P$.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}\text{-stress}$.  For each organism the $Q$ design is residualized against a fixed control block $Z$ containing length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The measured translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ from organism-specific ribosome-profiling rows, and the abundance readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ from matched proteomics rows.  The test is therefore a cross-sectional decomposition of $Q\to P$ after conditioning on measured $T$, not a perturbation or rescue experiment.

\paragraph{Joined rows and ranks.}
The four measured panels are rank-powered after $Z$ residualization.  The three-way joins contain $13140$ rows for zebrafish, $3415$ rows for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ rows for human, and $5128$ rows for budding yeast.  The reported CDS joins before the translation-abundance intersection are $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$, respectively.  The residual rank pattern is stable across the four panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z)&=23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Q\mid Z)&=9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(T\mid Z)&=1,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Q,T\mid Z)&=10.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual degrees of freedom after the control block are $13117$ for zebrafish, $3392$ for \emph{E. coli}, $11531$ for human, and $5105$ for yeast.  Thus the mediation surface is not an $n=3$ or saturated-rank artifact: each organism supplies thousands of controlled rows, the nine-dimensional $Q$ block remains full rank after residualization, and the one-dimensional measured $T$ readout is separately present.

\paragraph{Measured translation survival.}
The measured $Q\to T$ survival scores are positive in all four panels.  The values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{danio}}&=0.04034217614654355,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{ecoli}}&=0.009394266754822262,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{human}}&=0.018837911344127926,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{yeast}}&=0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The companion measured-TE survival run reports the same readout definition and the same nine surviving coordinates, with join hit rates $0.9118667591950035$ for zebrafish, $0.9133458143888741$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.6064136881331024$ for human, and $0.8818572656921754$ for yeast.  This establishes that the present table has a bounded measured translation-efficiency contact for these organisms, but it does not establish a causal translation mechanism or a universal rule about codon effects.

\paragraph{Direct protein-abundance survival.}
The direct $Q\to P$ association is also positive in the measured mediation panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{danio}}&=0.04926578454614652,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{ecoli}}&=0.10941535951828074,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{human}}&=0.04288464996165745,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{yeast}}&=0.3146818503437042.
\end{aligned}
$$
The broader direct proteomics screen gives the same qualitative boundary over a fifteen-organism panel: every listed organism has all nine coordinates surviving against PAXdb abundance after controls, with representative $R^{2}_{QP}$ values $0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, and $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish.  Those direct protein-omics entries are useful as downstream readout evidence, but they are not mechanism evidence unless the measured translation readout absorbs or materially reduces the direct $Q\to P$ signal.

\paragraph{Measured mediation scalar.}
The fitted mediation scalar is computed by comparing the $Q$ explanation of $P$ before and after conditioning on measured $T$.  The organism-level results are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP,meas}_{\mathrm{danio}}&=0,\quad M^{raw}_{\mathrm{danio}}=-0.04801486048721136,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{\mathrm{ecoli}}&=0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{\mathrm{human}}&=0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{\mathrm{yeast}}&=0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast panel is the clear positive case: measured $TE$ accounts for nearly half of the controlled $Q\to P$ association by this scalar, while the direct improvement energy of $Q$ after $T$ remains $351.63835021403213$ against a residual abundance energy of $2146.0645009193045$.  \emph{E. coli} has a small positive measured mediation fraction, human has a nearly null positive fraction, and zebrafish is suppressive in the raw calculation and is therefore clipped to zero in the reported unit interval.  In energy terms, the measured translation readout explains $593.5847863333478$ units of yeast abundance residual energy and improves by $269.89558844115504$ units given $Q$; the corresponding values are $35.56347808682994$ and $19.45303160850625$ for \emph{E. coli}, $2.0678517436514716$ and $0.486668572585927$ for human, and $16.418999670692678$ and $68.85177862607247$ for zebrafish.

\paragraph{Readback classification.}
The finite readback has four local regimes.  Zebrafish has positive $S^{QT}$ and $S^{QP}$ but no positive measured mediation fraction after clipping, so it is a positive-positive non-mediated or suppressive measured-readout case.  \emph{E. coli} and human have positive $S^{QT}$, positive $S^{QP}$, and positive but weak measured mediation.  Yeast has positive $S^{QT}$, positive $S^{QP}$, and a large measured mediated component.  This agrees with the modeled-tAI four-case run only at the level of boundary discipline: in that run yeast was placed entirely in the mediated-positive class over nine coordinates, whereas \emph{E. coli} had seven mediated-positive coordinates and two positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop.  The present chapter uses measured ribosome-derived $TE$, so the modeled-tAI readback is only a comparison surface.

\paragraph{Relation to larger readout dictionaries.}
The joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition gives additional context for why a single measured translation readout is not the whole protein-abundance story.  On a three-organism four-way join, the joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, with right-tail permutation values $0.000999000999000999$ in all three cases.  The yeast TE-only fraction in that analysis is $0.4697188319693239$, close to the measured $TE$ mediation scalar above, while the yeast stability-only fraction is $0.0626618595344251$.  A separate yeast readout-dictionary certificate reports coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ for the selected measured readouts $\{\mathrm{measured\ TE},\mathrm{mRNA\ stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$, selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$, naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ level $0.00598374993486675$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n_{join}=1800$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These numbers support partial reduction to measured biological surfaces, not closure of the residual.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  In a rank-powered matched panel, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has positive measured translation-efficiency survival and positive protein-abundance survival, and in yeast a substantial part of the direct protein-abundance association is absorbed by measured $TE$.  \emph{E. coli} and human show weaker measured mediation, while zebrafish does not support a positive measured mediation scalar despite positive $Q\to T$ and $Q\to P$ entries.  No causal translation process, no folding claim, no physical-admissibility claim, no molecular-function claim, no perturbational mechanism, and no universal biological law follows from this table.  Each such stronger assertion would need its own layer-matched contact and statistic; the present result is a controlled finite-row decomposition of codon-residual, measured-translation-efficiency, and protein-abundance readouts.
