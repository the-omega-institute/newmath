\subsection{Measured Translation Conditioning of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Protein-Abundance Signal}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-translation-conditioned-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The measured protein-abundance mediation record is a finite matrix over nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, one measured translation readout $T$ per joined coding sequence, and one measured protein-abundance readout $Y$ per joined protein.  The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ from ribosome-footprint and matched transcript rows; the protein readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in parts per million, joined by protein identifier.  All quantities are residualized against the same control family: intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine coordinates.  The resulting design has $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, $9$ residual coordinate columns, rank $9$ after control projection, one residual translation column, and a joint coordinate-plus-translation rank of $10$ in each measured organism.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
The measured three-way panels are non-vacuous.  Danio rerio contributes $13140$ coding sequences after joining $14410$ reported CDS abundance rows, with $14410$ valid abundance proteins and $13140$ valid measured-TE proteins.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 contributes $3415$ three-way rows from $3739$ reported CDS rows, $3747$ valid abundance proteins, and $3415$ valid measured-TE proteins.  Homo sapiens contributes $11554$ three-way rows from $19053$ reported CDS rows, $19483$ valid abundance proteins, and $11554$ valid measured-TE proteins.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae contributes $5128$ three-way rows from $5815$ reported CDS rows, $6351$ valid abundance proteins, and $5128$ valid measured-TE proteins.  The residual degrees of freedom after control projection are $13117$, $3392$, $11531$, and $5105$, respectively, so the mediated surface is not a rank-degenerate all-fit artifact.

\paragraph{Measured mediation numbers.}
Let $S^{QT}_{meas}$ denote the residual coordinate survival against measured $\log_{10}(TE)$, let $S^{QP}$ denote residual coordinate survival against measured protein abundance, and let $M^{QTP}_{meas}$ denote the fraction of the direct coordinate-to-protein signal removed by conditioning on measured $T$.  The finite readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Danio rerio:}      && S^{QT}_{meas} &= 0.04034217614654355, & S^{QP} &= 0.04926578454614652, & M^{QTP}_{raw} &= -0.04801486048721136, & M^{QTP}_{meas} &= 0, \\
\text{E. coli K12:}     && S^{QT}_{meas} &= 0.009394266754822262, & S^{QP} &= 0.10941535951828074, & M^{QTP}_{raw} &= 0.04091158340950673, & M^{QTP}_{meas} &= 0.04091158340950673, \\
\text{Homo sapiens:}   && S^{QT}_{meas} &= 0.018837911344127926, & S^{QP} &= 0.04288464996165745, & M^{QTP}_{raw} &= 0.0027966485036786744, & M^{QTP}_{meas} &= 0.0027966485036786744, \\
\text{S. cerevisiae:}  && S^{QT}_{meas} &= 0.13681931343730436, & S^{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042, & M^{QTP}_{raw} &= 0.4793069656345167, & M^{QTP}_{meas} &= 0.4793069656345167 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured mediation scalar is clipped into the unit interval only to prevent a suppressive negative fraction from being promoted as a positive mediator.  This matters for Danio rerio: both $S^{QT}_{meas}$ and $S^{QP}$ are positive, but the raw mediation fraction is negative, so the measured translation readout does not absorb the direct abundance association there.  In E. coli and human the attenuation is positive but small.  In budding yeast the measured translation readout absorbs nearly one half of the residual coordinate-to-protein abundance association.

\paragraph{Energy audit.}
The finite linear-algebra audit gives the same pattern in explained-energy form.  For Danio rerio, the coordinate block explains $144.76301201119642$ units on $T$ and $1092.0114819316218$ units on $Y$, while adding $T$ after $Q$ improves the protein fit by $68.85177862607247$ and the residual coordinate improvement after conditioning on $T$ is still $1144.4442608870015$.  For E. coli, these four values are $8.637891149939946$, $393.78692134854015$, $19.45303160850625$, and $377.67647487021645$.  For human, they are $38.66659710161269$, $565.3850203147235$, $0.486668572585927$, and $563.803837143658$.  For yeast, they are $103.10404057290246$, $675.3275481062249$, $269.89558844115504$, and $351.63835021403213$.  The yeast row is therefore the only row in this measured-TE panel where the translation readout is both a strong residual target of $Q$ and a substantial absorber of the $Q \to Y$ association.

\paragraph{Direct and translation-only boundary checks.}
The direct protein-omics surface is stronger and broader than the measured mediation surface.  Across the larger protein-abundance panel, every organism has all $9$ coordinates surviving in the direct $Q\to P$ fit, with $R^2_{QP}$ values including $0.10767526677985896$ for E. coli, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, and $0.04613529807085213$ for Danio rerio.  The measured translation-only surface also survives for the four ribosome-TE organisms, with all $9$ coordinates positive and $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ in E. coli, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, and $0.04034217614654355$ in Danio rerio.  Thus the finite record separates three facts: direct protein association exists, measured translation association exists, and measured translation-mediated absorption is organism-dependent.

\paragraph{Four-case and joint-readout placement.}
A modeled-tAI four-case discriminant gives a useful contrast but does not replace the measured-TE mediation row.  Under the modeled-tAI readout, E. coli has $7$ case-$D$ coordinates and $2$ positive-positive coordinates without mediation drop; yeast has $9$ case-$D$ coordinates and no positive-positive non-drop coordinates.  The measured-TE mediation row is stricter: E. coli has only $M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.04091158340950673$, while yeast has $M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.4793069656345167$.  A joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition gives additional context.  Its joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ in E. coli, $0.16890712099923358$ in human, and $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, each with deterministic pair-permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  In that joint readout, yeast has $TE$-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, while E. coli has $TE$-only fraction $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only fraction $0.23052056324898387$.

The measured translation route does not exhaust the biological residual.  In yeast, a finite readout dictionary using at most three measured readouts, namely measured $TE$, mRNA stability, and turnover, has $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, available join $4758$, active join $1800$, five folds, $120$ permutations, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$, accepted coverage $0.24538211439730545$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The $f_3$-stress dwell audit on yeast has $n=1257$, base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, permutation null $95\%$ level $0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $f_3$--dwell correlation p-value $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  A position-resolved structural-order screen classifies the ramp signal as positive but not localized: at the primary $50$-codon ramp window, weighted ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip value is $0.05859375$.

\paragraph{Scientific reading.}
The measured finite record supports a guarded statement: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive against measured translation efficiency and measured protein abundance in rank-powered rows, and in yeast the measured translation readout absorbs a large fraction of the coordinate-to-abundance signal.  The same statement is weaker in E. coli and human and fails as mediation in Danio rerio despite positive marginal survival on both measured readouts.  Therefore the chapter records a measured, cross-sectional variance-partition result, not a causal pathway.  No translation mechanism, folding effect, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, perturbation rescue, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that claim and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
