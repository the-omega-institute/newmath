\subsection{Measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for $B^\star_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-measured-translation-conditioned-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The measured mediation surface is the finite matrix obtained by placing the nine $B^\star_{Q6}$ residual coordinates against same-row measured translation-efficiency and protein-abundance readouts.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each row first removes the same control block $Z$: intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The readout $T$ is $\log_{10}$ measured translation efficiency, with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ from local ribosome-profiling files.  The readout $P$ is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in ppm from the matched proteomics table joined by protein identifier.  The experiment is therefore not a modeled-tAI statement: it asks whether measured ribosome-footprint-normalized translation readout absorbs part of the direct $Q\to P$ association.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The four organism panels are rank-powered after residualization.  The three-way joins have $13140$ rows for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ rows for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5128$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Their reported CDS joins are respectively $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$ coding sequences, with valid measured-TE counts $13140$, $3415$, $11554$, and $5128$.  In all four panels the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the residualized translation readout has rank $1$, the combined $(Q,T)$ block has rank $10$, and $Z$ has rank $23$ over $24$ control columns.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $13117$, $3392$, $11531$, and $5105$, so none of these rows is the rank-degenerate all-fit boundary.

\paragraph{Measured translation survival.}
Before asking for mediation, the translation leg must survive as an observed readout.  The measured $S^{QT}$ values are positive in all four panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{Danio} &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
S^{QT}_{E.coli} &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
S^{QT}_{Human} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
S^{QT}_{Yeast} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation reports all nine coordinates as surviving for the measured-TE readout in each organism.  This establishes a finite readout association only: it does not by itself identify elongation, initiation, ribosome pausing, or a causal translation process.

\paragraph{Direct protein-abundance survival.}
The protein-abundance leg also remains positive after the same controls.  In the mediation run the direct $Q\to P$ scores are
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_{Danio} &= 0.04926578454614652,\\
S^{QP}_{E.coli} &= 0.10941535951828074,\\
S^{QP}_{Human} &= 0.04288464996165745,\\
S^{QP}_{Yeast} &= 0.3146818503437042.
\end{aligned}
$$
The independent direct protein-omics survival run gives the same qualitative boundary across a broader organism list: for example $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, and all nine coordinates survive in each reported organism.  Those direct entries are not mechanism claims.  They are the downstream association that the measured translation readout is allowed to attenuate, fail to attenuate, or suppress.

\paragraph{Measured mediation readback.}
The measured mediation scalar is computed jointly over the nine-dimensional $Q$ block and then clipped into the admissible unit interval when the raw fraction is suppressive.  The finite readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP,meas}_{Danio,raw} &= -0.04801486048721136,& M^{QTP,meas}_{Danio} &= 0,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{E.coli,raw} &= 0.04091158340950673,& M^{QTP,meas}_{E.coli} &= 0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{Human,raw} &= 0.0027966485036786744,& M^{QTP,meas}_{Human} &= 0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{Yeast,raw} &= 0.4793069656345167,& M^{QTP,meas}_{Yeast} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus yeast has the strongest measured translation-conditioned absorption: nearly one half of the direct $B^\star_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association is accounted for by the measured TE readout.  \emph{E. coli} has a small positive measured fraction, human has a very small positive fraction, and \emph{Danio} is suppressive on the raw decomposition and is therefore read as zero mediated fraction rather than as hidden support.

\paragraph{Energy decomposition.}
The residual-energy diagnostics make the organism-specific contrast explicit.  In yeast, $X$ explains $675.3275481062249$ energy units on $P$, while conditioning on measured $T$ leaves $351.63835021403213$ direct improvement units for $X$; $T$ alone explains $593.5847863333478$ units on $P$, and the incremental $T$ improvement given $X$ is $269.89558844115504$.  In human, by contrast, $X$ explains $565.3850203147235$ units on $P$, conditioning on $T$ leaves $563.803837143658$, and $T$ alone explains only $2.0678517436514716$ units on $P$.  The readout therefore separates a yeast-like measured-translation absorption surface from a human surface where direct protein abundance mostly remains after measured TE conditioning.  The corresponding values for \emph{E. coli} are $393.78692134854015$ for $X$ on $P$, $377.67647487021645$ for $X$ given $T$, $35.56347808682994$ for $T$ on $P$, and $19.45303160850625$ for $T$ given $X$.  For \emph{Danio}, the values are $1092.0114819316218$, $1144.4442608870015$, $16.418999670692678$, and $68.85177862607247$, matching the suppressive raw mediation sign.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.}
A companion four-case discriminant using modeled per-protein tAI and measured abundance classifies yeast as nine case-D coordinates, meaning positive $S^{QT}$ and positive $S^{QP}$ with the modeled translation readout satisfying the attenuation condition.  The same discriminant classifies \emph{E. coli} as seven case-D coordinates plus two positive-positive coordinates without mediation drop.  The measured-TE mediation surface is stricter than the modeled-tAI surface: it keeps the positive $Q\to T$ and $Q\to P$ readings, but it reports the actual measured attenuation magnitudes organism by organism instead of promoting every positive-positive entry to a mechanism statement.

\paragraph{Related finite checks.}
The measured-TE result agrees with the larger variance-partition picture.  The joint TE-plus-mRNA-stability analysis reports joint mediation fractions $0.2404669586626661$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  Yeast also carries a partial dictionary reduction: with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, the best selection-aware measured-readout dictionary covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the source signal and leaves $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained, above a selection-aware null $95\%$ level of $0.029149007242956115$.  The yeast $f3\_stress$ dwell test gives a held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ over a fixed permutation null $95\%$ level of $0.0013115362189692937$, with approximate $f3$--dwell correlation $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These checks support partial reducibility to measured readout families while leaving substantial residual structure unassigned.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter records a finite, controlled association surface and a measured-readout attenuation calculation.  It does not establish causality, temporal ordering, synonym-edit rescue, perturbational mechanism, molecular function, protein folding, physical admissibility, system phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  No statement about translation as a mechanism, folded structure, physical feasibility, biological function, or universality follows from these numbers unless an additional layer-matched measurement supplies that claim and is evaluated under its own controls.  The admissible conclusion is narrower: on the rank-powered matched panels, measured TE survives as a $B^\star_{Q6}$ readout, measured protein abundance survives as a downstream readout, and the observed mediation fraction ranges from suppressive or negligible in \emph{Danio} and human to small positive in \emph{E. coli} and strong partial absorption in yeast.
