\subsection{Measured translation-conditioned protein abundance for $B^{*}_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-measured-mqtp}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite measured surface.} The finite object for this record is a joined per-CDS matrix with nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency readout $T$, one measured protein-abundance readout $Y$, and the sibling control block $Z$. The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The measured translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ computed as ribosome footprint divided by mRNA in the local ribosome-efficiency tables. The abundance readout is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in ppm from the local proteomics abundance table, joined by protein identifier. All reported quantities are computed after residualizing against the same biological nuisance block: intercept, coding length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline. The residualized control matrix has rank $23$ in all four measured organisms; the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the residualized measured-TE column has rank $1$, and the joint $Q,T$ block has rank $10$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.} Four organism panels pass the rank and row-count tests. In \emph{Danio rerio}, $14410$ CDS rows are available in the abundance join and $13140$ rows remain in the three-way codon--TE--abundance join. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $3739$ CDS rows are reported for the codon--abundance join and $3415$ rows remain in the three-way join. In \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19053$ CDS rows are reported and $11554$ rows remain after adding measured TE. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ CDS rows are reported and $5128$ rows remain after adding measured TE. These are not rank-degenerate panels: the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $13117$, $3392$, $11531$, and $5105$, respectively, and the rank tolerance is $10^{-10}$.

\paragraph{Direct and translation-conditioned readbacks.} Let $S_{QP}$ denote the direct residual association of the nine-coordinate $Q$ block with measured protein abundance, and let $S_{QT}^{meas}$ denote the association of the same $Q$ block with measured ribosome-derived $TE$. The measured mediation scalar records how much of the direct $Q \to Y$ association is reduced after conditioning on measured $T$. The finite row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{Danio rerio}:&\quad S_{QP}=0.04926578454614652,\quad S_{QT}^{meas}=0.04034217614654355,\quad M_{QTP}^{meas}=0.0,\\
\textit{E. coli}:&\quad S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074,\quad S_{QT}^{meas}=0.009394266754822262,\quad M_{QTP}^{meas}=0.04091158340950673,\\
\textit{H. sapiens}:&\quad S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745,\quad S_{QT}^{meas}=0.018837911344127926,\quad M_{QTP}^{meas}=0.0027966485036786744,\\
\textit{S. cerevisiae}:&\quad S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042,\quad S_{QT}^{meas}=0.13681931343730436,\quad M_{QTP}^{meas}=0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw fraction for \emph{Danio rerio} is negative, $-0.04801486048721136$, and is therefore clipped to the unit-interval value $0.0$. The four measured fractions are all explicitly recorded as lying in the admissible unit interval after this rule. The module-level classification is therefore suppressive-or-null in \emph{Danio rerio}, weak positive in \emph{H. sapiens}, small positive in \emph{E. coli}, and large positive in \emph{S. cerevisiae}. This classification is a finite readout of the measured matrix; it is not a universal biological partition.

\paragraph{Energy decomposition.} The same conclusion is visible in the residual energy accounting. In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the measured translation column explains $593.5847863333478$ units of abundance residual energy, while the $Q$ block explains $675.3275481062249$ units before conditioning; after measured $T$ is included, the direct $Q$ improvement is $351.63835021403213$. This is the clearest finite case where measured translation absorbs a substantial part of the abundance association. In \emph{E. coli}, the corresponding values are $35.56347808682994$, $393.78692134854015$, and $377.67647487021645$, so the measured attenuation is present but modest. In \emph{H. sapiens}, measured $T$ explains only $2.0678517436514716$ units of abundance residual energy and the conditioned $Q$ improvement remains $563.803837143658$, giving only a very small mediated fraction. In \emph{Danio rerio}, measured $T$ explains $16.418999670692678$ units of abundance residual energy, but the $Q$ improvement after $T$ is $1144.4442608870015$, larger than the unconditioned $Q$ energy $1092.0114819316218$; this is the suppressive case and is not promoted to mediation.

\paragraph{Relation to measured TE survival.} The translation-efficiency surface is independently positive in the same four organisms: the measured $S_{QT}$ values are $0.04034217614654355$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ for \emph{H. sapiens}, and $0.13681931343730436$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}. Each entry uses all nine residual coordinates. Thus the measured-TE layer is not absent. The decisive question is instead whether the measured-TE layer absorbs the downstream protein-abundance association. The answer is organism-specific: yeast gives a strong finite positive attenuation, \emph{E. coli} gives a small positive attenuation, human gives a nearly null positive attenuation, and zebrafish gives a suppressive fit that is recorded as zero mediated fraction.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.} This chapter establishes a controlled finite statistical decomposition over joined codon, measured ribosome-derived $TE$, mRNA-normalized translation-efficiency, and protein-abundance rows. It does not by itself establish a causal translation mechanism, temporal ordering, elongation-rate mechanism, initiation mechanism, perturbation response, synonymous-edit rescue, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universal biological law. A direct $Q \to Y$ protein-abundance association remains a downstream omics association unless a measured $Q \to T$ step survives and conditioning on $T$ reduces the $Q \to Y$ signal. Even when that reduction is positive, as in yeast and weakly in the other positive panels, the readout is cross-sectional and condition-specific. Higher claims require their own layer-matched measurements rather than being inferred from codon geometry, from the existence of a proteomics join, or from the notation used for the mediation scalar.
