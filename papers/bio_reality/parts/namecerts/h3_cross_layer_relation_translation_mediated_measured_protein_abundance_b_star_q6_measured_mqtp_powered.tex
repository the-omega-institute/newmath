\paragraph{Finite matrix surface.}
This record concerns a finite row design in which nine synonymous residual coordinates are joined to measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency and measured protein abundance on the same coding-sequence units.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The response $Y$ is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance from local proteomics abundance tables joined by protein identifier, and the measured translation readout $T$ is $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ from local ribosome-efficiency files.  Every fit first removes the same control family $Z$: intercept, coding length, twenty amino-acid composition coordinates, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The residualized design has $23$ effective control rank columns, $9$ residual $Q$ columns, and one residual measured-$TE$ column in each organism where the mediation calculation is performed.

\paragraph{Cross-checks against neighboring finite rows.}
The measured-TE survival row is consistent with the same four organisms: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$ for danio, $0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, with all nine coordinates surviving the positive threshold in each organism.  The broader direct protein-omics screen over fifteen organisms remains downstream rather than explanatory: $R^2_{QP}$ ranges from $0.019256877907572044$ in \emph{Halobacterium salinarum} to $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, with all nine coordinates positive under the tiny positive threshold, but that screen is only a direct $Q\to P$ association.  A separate modeled-tAI four-case calculation classified yeast as nine $D$ entries and \emph{E. coli} as seven $D$ entries plus two positive-positive entries without mediation drop, which agrees with the need to distinguish a translation-surviving coordinate from a translation-conditioned attenuation claim.  A joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability variance partition also keeps the yeast case distinct: joint mediation fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ under the fixed pair-permutation null.

\paragraph{Residual readout dictionary.}
The yeast residual is not exhausted by measured translation.  A compact readout dictionary on the yeast context join reports $n=1800$, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95$ percentile $0.029149007242956115$, certified coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The finite interpretation is that measured $TE$, mRNA stability, turnover, and nearby readouts explain a real but partial portion of the protein-abundance residual.  The $f3_{stress}$ subcoordinate gives an additional local check: in yeast, the dwell-mediated abundance component has $n_{join}=1257$, base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null $95$ percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$, and a positional readout in which the $5'$ segment is dominant while the $3'$ segment does not pass.  These companion rows support a layered reading: measured translation and local ribosome-occupancy summaries can carry part of the abundance residual, but they do not close the remaining protein-output structure.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a measured, cross-sectional variance decomposition rather than a causal mechanism.  A positive $M^{QTP}_{meas}$ entry says that conditioning on measured $TE$ reduces the direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ association with protein abundance in that finite joined panel.  It does not by itself establish translation as a universal rule, and it does not imply folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a general biological law.  Those layers require their own matched observations and their own controlled statistics.  Within the present finite matrix, the strongest measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance case is yeast, \emph{E. coli} and human are weak positive attenuation cases, and danio remains a positive translation-readout and protein-abundance association without positive measured attenuation.
