\subsection{Measured Translation Mediation of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Protein Abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-mqtp}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The measured mediation record tests a nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface against two row-matched biological readouts: $T=\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$, where measured translation efficiency is ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA, and $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ from protein abundance tables joined by protein identifier.  The nine residual coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each fit first removes the same control block $Z$: intercept, coding length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates.  The residualized design has $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$ in every organism reported here.

\paragraph{Joined measured rows.}
The row joins are non-vacuous and rank powered in all four measured-TE organisms.  Danio rerio has $13{,}140$ three-way CDS--TE--protein rows out of $14{,}410$ CDS rows, with $14{,}410$ valid abundance entries and $13{,}140$ valid TE entries.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $3{,}415$ three-way rows out of $3{,}739$ CDS rows, with $3{,}747$ valid abundance entries and $3{,}415$ valid TE entries.  Homo sapiens has $11{,}554$ three-way rows out of $19{,}053$ CDS rows, with $19{,}483$ valid abundance entries and $11{,}554$ valid TE entries.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5{,}128$ three-way rows out of $5{,}815$ CDS rows, with $6{,}351$ valid abundance entries and $5{,}128$ valid TE entries.  The matched ribosome-TE survival audit uses the same four organisms and gives TE join hit rates $0.9118667591950035$ for Danio, $0.9133458143888741$ for E. coli, $0.6064136881331024$ for human, and $0.8818572656921754$ for yeast.

\paragraph{Measured mediation statistic.}
The mediation scalar is computed from the joint nine-dimensional codon-residual design, not from a single selected coordinate.  It compares the direct codon-residual explanation of protein abundance with the remaining codon-residual explanation after conditioning on residualized measured TE.  With $S^{QP}$ denoting the direct codon-residual to abundance survival and $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}$ denoting the codon-residual to measured-TE survival, the finite measured outcomes are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.04926578454614652,\quad S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.04034217614654355,\quad M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}=0.0,\\
\mathrm{E.\ coli}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.10941535951828074,\quad S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262,\quad M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}=0.04091158340950673,\\
\mathrm{Human}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.04288464996165745,\quad S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926,\quad M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}=0.0027966485036786744,\\
\mathrm{Yeast}:&\quad S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042,\quad S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436,\quad M^{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}=0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw Danio mediation fraction is $-0.04801486048721136$, so the reported unit-interval value is the boundary value $0.0$ rather than a hidden positive mediation claim.  The other three organisms have positive measured fractions, with yeast giving the largest finite-row attenuation of the codon-residual abundance association after measured TE is included.

\paragraph{Energy readback.}
The residual energy and improvement values identify which rows carry the mediation signal.  In Danio, $X$ explains $1092.0114819316218$ units of protein-abundance residual energy and $144.76301201119642$ units of measured-TE residual energy; conditioning on measured TE leaves an $X$ direct improvement of $1144.4442608870015$ on $Y$, so the fitted TE direction does not absorb the codon-residual abundance signal.  In E. coli, $X$ explains $393.78692134854015$ on $Y$ and $8.637891149939946$ on $T$, while the remaining $X$ improvement after $T$ is $377.67647487021645$, giving a small but positive measured mediation fraction.  In human, $X$ explains $565.3850203147235$ on $Y$ and $38.66659710161269$ on $T$, but $T$ adds only $0.486668572585927$ after $X$ on $Y$, yielding a very small measured fraction.  In yeast, $X$ explains $675.3275481062249$ on $Y$ and $103.10404057290246$ on $T$, while $T$ explains $593.5847863333478$ on $Y$ and adds $269.89558844115504$ after $X$; this is the strongest measured TE-mediated abundance pattern in the table.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein-omics survival.}
The broader direct protein-abundance screen reports positive $S^{QP}$ across fifteen protein-abundance joins, with all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates surviving at the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  The direct $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.10767526677985896$ in E. coli, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.04613529807085213$ in Danio, and $0.061906245886660105$ in Bacillus subtilis.  These entries certify a downstream association surface, not a translation mechanism.  The measured mediation table is therefore the stricter readback: only where a measured TE row both survives and reduces the codon-residual abundance association can the abundance signal be described as TE-mediated in this finite sense.

\paragraph{Four-case classification and modeled-readout contrast.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant gives a useful contrast but must not be substituted for measured ribosome TE.  On the modeled-tAI surface, E. coli has $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, with seven coordinates classified as case $D$ and two positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop.  Yeast has $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, with all nine coordinates classified as case $D$.  The measured-TE mediation table is more conservative: E. coli remains positive but weak, human is nearly boundary-level, and yeast remains strongly positive.  Thus the measured table supports a finite translation-conditioned abundance component, while also showing that modeled adaptation weights and observed TE need not have the same magnitude.

\paragraph{Joint mediator context.}
A separate joint TE--mRNA-stability decomposition shows that measured TE is not the only lower-layer readout carrying abundance variance.  In E. coli, $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, giving joint mediation fraction $0.2404669586626661$, TE-only fraction $0.01975521340963268$, and stability-only fraction $0.23052056324898387$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, giving joint fraction $0.16890712099923358$, TE-only fraction $0.00549490864190343$, and stability-only fraction $0.15314679870183337$ with the same right-tail permutation value.  In yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving joint fraction $0.5195182225621592$, TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$.  This places yeast in the strongest measured-TE regime, whereas E. coli and human retain larger non-TE or stability-associated components.

\paragraph{Residual dictionary and positional checks.}
The yeast residual-abundance signal is only partially reducible to a small measured-readout dictionary.  A selection-aware dictionary run on $1{,}800$ joined yeast rows, with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, five folds, and $120$ deterministic permutations, reports coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95$ value $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ for the candidate family $[\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}]$.  The $f3\_stress$ dwell mediation audit gives base held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ on $1{,}257$ joined yeast rows, exceeding its permutation null $95$ value $0.0013115362189692937$ with $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell version identifies the $5'$ segment as dominant, with $5'$ passing and $3'$ not passing.  A separate structural-order ramp screen is weaker: for the primary $50$-codon ramp it reports weighted ramp $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, ramp sign-flip probability $0.0478515625$, paired ramp-minus-body probability $0.05859375$, and the classification $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$.

\paragraph{Non-translation residual checks.}
The finite record also tests whether the remaining abundance component is trivially explained by coarse post-translational annotations.  Localization absorption is small but above degree-of-freedom-matched nulls in the three tested organisms: Danio absorbs $0.009508864544725801$ with null $95$ value $0.011321328712802819$ and therefore does not exceed that null; human absorbs $0.009254913329269913$ with null $95$ value $0.0013758521256775384$; yeast absorbs $0.011225105608516642$ with null $95$ value $0.004958116973149795$.  Human turnover is much weaker: the human half-life join uses $8{,}057$ rows, but turnover absorption is only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ with null $95$ value $0.0004342285066808464$, so it does not exceed the null.  These checks show that the measured mediation record is not a blanket explanation of abundance: even after positive measured-TE attenuation in yeast and weaker positive attenuation in E. coli and human, a large residual component remains.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
This chapter supports a finite statistical statement: in a rank-powered matched panel, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have positive measured TE survival and positive protein-abundance survival, and in yeast, E. coli, and human the measured TE direction absorbs a positive fraction of the direct abundance association.  The result is not a causal mediation effect.  It uses cross-sectional rows, condition-specific ribosome profiling, matched abundance tables, and residualized linear projections.  No biological translation process beyond the measured TE contact, no folding claim, no physical admissibility claim, no molecular function, no perturbational mechanism, and no universal biological rule follows from these numbers unless a separate layer-specific measurement and statistic are supplied.  Direct $q_i\to P_k$ survival remains a protein-omics association unless a surviving $q_i\to T_j$ measured-readout step and a reduction of the direct $q_i\to P_k$ signal are both present in the same controlled row system.
