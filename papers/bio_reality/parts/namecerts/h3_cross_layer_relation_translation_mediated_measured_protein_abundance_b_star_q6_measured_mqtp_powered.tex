\subsection{Measured translation-conditioned protein abundance in the rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel}
\label{subsec:bq6-measured-mqtp-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The measured protein-abundance mediation packet uses a finite matrix surface with nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency readout per joined coding sequence, and one matched protein-abundance readout per joined protein.  The coordinate list is $K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_{stress}$.  Each organism row is residualized against the same control family: intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, where $TE$ is ribosome footprint divided by mRNA in the local ribosome-profiling files.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance in ppm, joined by protein identifier.  Thus the experiment distinguishes a direct $Q \to P$ residual association from the stricter measured-readout pattern in which $Q \to T$ survives and conditioning on $T$ reduces the $Q \to P$ component.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
The measured three-way joins are large enough to avoid the rank-degenerate boundary that makes tiny all-one surfaces uninterpretable.  Danio rerio joins $13140$ coding-sequence rows out of $14410$ codon/protein rows, with $14410$ valid abundance entries and $13140$ valid measured-TE entries.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 joins $3415$ rows out of $3739$ coding-sequence rows, with $3747$ valid abundance entries and $3415$ valid TE entries.  Homo sapiens joins $11554$ rows out of $19053$ coding-sequence rows, with $19483$ valid abundance entries and $11554$ valid TE entries.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae joins $5128$ rows out of $5815$ coding-sequence rows, with $6351$ valid abundance entries and $5128$ valid TE entries.  In every organism the residualized $Q$ design has rank $9$, the residualized measured-TE column has rank $1$, the joint $Q,T$ design has rank $10$, and the control basis has rank $23$ from $24$ control columns.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $13117$, $3392$, $11531$, and $5105$ for danio, E. coli, human, and yeast respectively.

\paragraph{Measured mediation readback.}
The finite readback is the organism-indexed tuple $(S^{QP},S^{QT}_{meas},M^{QTP}_{meas})$, where $S^{QP}$ is the residual protein-abundance survival, $S^{QT}_{meas}$ is the measured-TE survival, and $M^{QTP}_{meas}$ is the bounded measured mediation fraction after conditioning on the measured translation readout.  The observed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{danio:}\quad & S^{QP}=0.0492657845,\quad S^{QT}_{meas}=0.0403421761,\quad M^{QTP}_{raw}=-0.0480148605,\quad M^{QTP}_{meas}=0,\\
\text{E. coli:}\quad & S^{QP}=0.1094153595,\quad S^{QT}_{meas}=0.0093942668,\quad M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.0409115834,\\
\text{human:}\quad & S^{QP}=0.0428846500,\quad S^{QT}_{meas}=0.0188379113,\quad M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.0027966485,\\
\text{yeast:}\quad & S^{QP}=0.3146818503,\quad S^{QT}_{meas}=0.1368193134,\quad M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.4793069656.
\end{aligned}
$$
All four rows have positive measured-TE survival and positive protein-abundance survival.  The strongest measured translation-conditioned reduction is in yeast, where nearly one half of the direct codon-residual-to-protein association is absorbed by measured TE.  E. coli and human show positive but much smaller measured fractions.  Danio is explicitly suppressive at the raw mediation level and is therefore clipped to zero rather than promoted as mediation.

\paragraph{Energy accounting.}
The residual energy diagnostics show that the mediation call is not a symbolic label but a finite least-squares decomposition.  For danio, $X_Q$ explains $1092.0114819$ units of protein-residual energy and measured $T$ adds $68.8517786$ units after $X_Q$, while $X_Q$ still contributes $1144.4442609$ units after conditioning on $T$.  For E. coli, the corresponding quantities are $393.7869213$, $19.4530316$, and $377.6764749$.  For human they are $565.3850203$, $0.4866686$, and $563.8038371$.  For yeast they are $675.3275481$, $269.8955884$, and $351.6383502$.  The same calculation also records the $Q \to T$ energy: $144.7630120$ in danio, $8.6378911$ in E. coli, $38.6665971$ in human, and $103.1040406$ in yeast.  The yeast row is therefore the clearest measured mediation row: the translation readout is itself substantially predictable from $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$, and adding measured TE removes a large share of the protein-abundance association.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent finite rows.}
The direct protein-omics screen over fifteen organisms shows that $S^{QP}$ is broadly positive under the same nine-coordinate design, with all nine coordinates surviving in every listed organism and organism-level $R^2_{QP}$ ranging from $0.0192568779$ in Halobacterium salinarum to $0.2983762499$ in yeast.  That direct screen is not a mechanism statement; it only establishes that protein abundance can carry a codon-residual association after the sequence controls.  The separate measured-TE screen gives the needed $Q \to T$ side for four organisms, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.0403421761$ in danio, $0.0093942668$ in E. coli, $0.0188379113$ in human, and $0.1368193134$ in yeast.  A joint TE-and-mRNA-stability row further shows that measured TE and stability can account for a nontrivial portion of the same abundance residual: the joint fractions are $0.2404669587$ in E. coli, $0.1689071210$ in human, and $0.5195182226$ in yeast, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In yeast, the single measured-TE-only fraction in that joint row is $0.4697188320$, close to the measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656$ reported here.  A dictionary-style yeast reduction with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n=1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations covers $0.2453821144$ of the residual with selected measured readouts and leaves $0.7546178856$ unexplained; its selection-aware null $95\%$ cutoff is $0.0291490072$.  These adjacent rows support a conservative interpretation: measured translation is a real component of the abundance residual, but it is not the whole residual system.

\paragraph{Four-case classification.}
For the modeled-tAI four-case discriminant, yeast places all nine coordinates in case D, while E. coli places seven coordinates in case D and has two positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop.  That modeled-tAI classification agrees qualitatively with the measured-TE result only in the limited sense that yeast is the most translation-absorbed row.  It does not replace the measured ribosome-profiling TE calculation.  The measured row is the controlling evidence here because $T$ is built from ribosome footprint divided by mRNA rather than from codon-adaptation weights.

\paragraph{Localized residuals and remaining layer boundaries.}
Further yeast rows show that the residual is biologically structured without being exhausted by this mediation calculation.  The $f3\_{stress}$ dwell row uses $n_{join}=1257$, five folds, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations; the held-out dwell increment is $0.0185647394$, above a null $95\%$ value of $0.0013115362$, with $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441451$ and approximate $p=7.8739160572\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell row identifies the $5'$ segment as dominant, with $5'$ passing and $3'$ not passing.  A structural-order positional row reports a positive ramp signal, with weighted $f3$ ramp held-out increment $0.0011165640$, weighted body increment $-0.0002615731$, and ramp sign-flip value $0.0478515625$, but its paired ramp-body localization gate does not pass and the module conclusion remains ramp-positive but not localized.  These rows are useful for locating residual structure, not for declaring folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite result is ready for the standalone BioReality paper as a measured-readout, noncausal mediation record.  The strongest affirmative statement is that, in a rank-powered matched panel, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive against measured translation efficiency and protein abundance, and in yeast a large fraction of the direct protein-abundance association is absorbed by measured TE.  E. coli and human provide weaker positive measured mediation; danio provides positive $Q \to T$ and $Q \to P$ survival but no positive measured mediation after clipping the suppressive raw fraction.  No translation mechanism, folding state, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, system phenotype, perturbation effect, or universality follows from these rows.  Any such claim requires a distinct layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
