\subsection{Measured translation conditioning for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-translation-conditioned-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite matrix surface whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns are the nine residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The tested readout is not modeled tAI.  It is measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, recorded as $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$, paired on the same protein or CDS rows with $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance.  The fitted controls are length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the M-density baseline, with $24$ control columns and observed residual control rank $23$ in every organism used here.  The four measured panels are rank-sufficient for the nine-coordinate design: Danio rerio has $13140$ three-way joined rows, Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $3415$, Homo sapiens has $11554$, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5128$.  The corresponding reported CDS joins before the three-way restriction are $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$ rows.

\paragraph{Controlled translation and abundance numbers.}
After residualizing against the control surface, the measured translation survival values are positive in all four measured panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 0.04034217614654355,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
S^{QT}_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The paired protein-abundance survival values on the same measured-mediation run are also positive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 0.04926578454614652,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &= 0.10941535951828074,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 0.04288464996165745,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 0.3146818503437042.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured translation readout therefore does not disappear at the first gate.  It is present on the same panels in which the direct protein-abundance association is present.  The three-way row counts are large enough that the all-one rank-degenerate failure mode is not the explanation: the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $13117$, $3392$, $11531$, and $5105$, and the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design keeps rank $9$ while the measured translation column has rank $1$ and the joint $Q,T$ design has rank $10$.

\paragraph{Measured mediation readback.}
The scalar mediation readback asks how much of the direct $Q\to P$ association is removed when the measured $T$ column is included.  In the recorded run the raw fractions are $-0.04801486048721136$ for Danio, $0.04091158340950673$ for E. coli, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  The clipped measured fractions are therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP,meas}_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 0,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &= 0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a four-case outcome rather than a single universal assertion.  Danio has positive $S^{QT}$ and positive $S^{QP}$ but a suppressive or null measured mediation fraction.  E. coli and human have positive but small measured fractions.  Yeast has the strongest measured translation-conditioned absorption, with nearly half of the direct abundance association removed by the measured TE column.  The strongest organism-specific statement supported by these rows is therefore that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance association is measurably translation-conditioned in yeast, weakly translation-conditioned in E. coli and human, and not positively mediated by the measured TE readout in Danio.

\paragraph{Energy audit.}
The fitted energies give the same interpretation.  In yeast, $X_Q$ explains $675.3275481062249$ units of residual protein-abundance energy before measured TE conditioning, while the remaining direct improvement after conditioning on $T$ is $351.63835021403213$; measured TE alone explains $593.5847863333478$ units of residual abundance energy, and its incremental improvement after $X_Q$ is $269.89558844115504$.  In E. coli the corresponding quantities are $393.78692134854015$, $377.67647487021645$, $35.56347808682994$, and $19.45303160850625$.  In human they are $565.3850203147235$, $563.803837143658$, $2.0678517436514716$, and $0.486668572585927$.  In Danio they are $1092.0114819316218$, $1144.4442608870015$, $16.418999670692678$, and $68.85177862607247$, matching the negative raw mediation value.  These numbers make the readback finite and organism-specific: the same nine-coordinate geometry can be present in both $T$ and $P$, while the conditioning step can either absorb, weakly absorb, or fail to absorb the direct abundance association.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite checks.}
The same coordinate family is independently visible in measured TE survival, where all four measured panels have nine surviving coordinates and measured-TE $R^2$ values $0.04034217614654355$, $0.009394266754822262$, $0.018837911344127926$, and $0.13681931343730436$.  Direct protein-omics survival is broader but less mechanistically specific: across fifteen PAXdb panels the direct $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.10767526677985896$ for E. coli, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, and $0.04613529807085213$ for Danio, with all nine coordinates surviving in each listed organism.  A separate joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on E. coli, human, and yeast reports right-tail permutation probabilities $0.000999000999000999$ in all three panels, with joint mediation fractions $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$.  A yeast $f3\_stress$ dwell readout also passes its permutation check with $p\approx 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$.  The small dictionary compression run uses $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and reports coverage $0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These neighboring checks do not replace the measured $Q\to T\to P$ fit, but they show that the same finite coordinate family is repeatedly non-null under rank-powered readout designs.

\paragraph{Scientific boundary.}
The admitted claim is a controlled, cross-sectional variance-partition statement over measured TE and measured protein abundance.  It is not a causal mediation effect.  It does not establish temporal order, synonymous-edit rescue, elongation mechanism, initiation mechanism, protein turnover, localization, PTM state, folding pathway, molecular function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, or a universal biological law.  No broader translation claim follows beyond the measured TE contact used in the finite rows, and no folding, physical-admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality claim follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding controlled statistic.  Direct $Q\to P$ survival is therefore read as protein-omics prediction unless the same panel also has positive $Q\to T$ survival and the conditioning on $T$ reduces the $Q\to P$ signal.
