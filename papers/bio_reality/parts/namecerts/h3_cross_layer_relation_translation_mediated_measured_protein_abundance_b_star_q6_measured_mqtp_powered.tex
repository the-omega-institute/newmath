\subsection{Measured translation conditioning of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-translation-conditioned-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The tested record is a finite matrix surface over nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, one measured translation-efficiency readout, and one measured protein-abundance readout on matched coding-sequence rows.  The coordinate list is $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For each organism the response $Y$ is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance from the local proteomics abundance table joined by protein identifier, and the translation readout $T$ is $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ from the local ribosome-profiling table.  The design residualizes $Q$, $T$, and $Y$ against the same $Z$ controls: intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The control design has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and the residual $Q$ design has rank $9$ in each of the four measured mediation organisms.

\paragraph{Joined measured rows.}
The measured $Q,T,Y$ join is not an organism-name assertion; it is a row-counted table.  The four three-way joins are $13140$ rows for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3415$ rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $11554$ rows for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $5128$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding reported CDS joins are $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$.  The measured translation-efficiency contact independently reports ribosome-TE join hit rates $0.9118667591950035$, $0.9133458143888741$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.8818572656921754$ for those same four organisms.  The direct PAXdb protein-abundance contact is broader: its CDS abundance hit rates include $1.0$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.9978649586335735$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.9779294769799313$ for human, and $0.9156038419146592$ for yeast, with joined protein counts $14410$, $3739$, $19053$, and $5815$ respectively.

The verdict is therefore not a universal mechanism claim; it is a finite per-organism readback of how much measured $TE$ accounts for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual association under the stated controls.

The yeast row is the only one among these four where the measured translation readout removes a large part of the direct $Q\to Y$ energy.

\paragraph{Direct protein-omics comparison.}
Across the larger PAXdb-only organism panel, direct $Q\to P$ survival is positive but remains an omics association rather than a translation account.  The reported $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.061906245886660105$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.10217906758013379$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.04613529807085213$ in \emph{D. rerio}, $0.08141786718177903$ in \emph{Dictyostelium discoideum}, $0.0770952887136557$ in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.03980793596647631$ in chicken, $0.019256877907572044$ in \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse, $0.03209009170649934$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.0646471273126996$ in \emph{Pseudomonas aeruginosa}, $0.02763998603083845$ in rat, $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, and $0.06397514251295629$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}; all nine coordinates survive by the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold in each of these direct protein-abundance fits.  This broader list explains why the measured-mediation test is necessary: direct protein abundance is a downstream readout with turnover, localization, stability, mRNA, and condition effects mixed into it.

\paragraph{Four-case classification and auxiliary decomposition.}
The modeled translation discriminant gives a separate finite classification rather than a measured ribosome-TE mechanism.  In the modeled-tAI four-case run, \emph{E. coli} has seven case-$D$ coordinates and two positive-positive coordinates without mediation drop, while yeast has nine case-$D$ coordinates and no positive-positive coordinates without mediation drop.  The modeled-tAI $R^2$ values are $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli}, and $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ for yeast.  The measured-TE run is stricter: it replaces modeled tAI by local ribosome-derived $TE$ and shows that the large translation-conditioned attenuation is yeast-specific in the four-organism measured panel.  A joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition also reports joint mediation fractions $0.2404669586626661$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.16890712099923358$ in human, and $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, each with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  In that same decomposition, yeast is dominated by the measured-TE component $0.4697188319693239$, whereas \emph{E. coli} and human are dominated by the stability component, $0.23052056324898387$ and $0.15314679870183337$ respectively.

A yeast reduction dictionary gives a complementary bound on how much of the abundance residual is carried by a small measured-readout family.  With $n=1800$, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations, the selected family $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$ has selection-aware coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ against a selection-aware null $95$th percentile of $0.029149007242956115$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The $f3\_stress$ branch also has a yeast dwell-mediated abundance component: with $n=1257$, five folds, and $120$ row-label permutations, base $R^2$ is $0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2$ is $0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2$ is $0.01856473944057712$, the null $95$th percentile is $0.0013115362189692937$, and the $f3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Its positional audit assigns the leading segment to the $5'$ window; the structural-order ramp check is positive but not localized, with primary ramp $n=50$, weighted ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, ramp sign-flip probability $0.0478515625$, and paired ramp-minus-body probability $0.05859375$.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite readback is a four-way distinction.  A coordinate is translation-null if $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}$ does not survive; it is protein-omics-null if $S^{QP}$ does not survive; it is directly protein-omics-associated if $S^{QP}$ survives but conditioning on $T$ leaves the direct signal essentially intact; and it is measured-translation-attenuated when $S^{QT,\mathrm{meas}}$ survives and conditioning on $T$ removes a material fraction of the $Q\to Y$ association.  On the present rows, the measured-translation-attenuated case is most clearly represented by yeast, weakly represented by \emph{E. coli}, barely represented by human, and absent after clipping in \emph{D. rerio}.  The result is therefore a bounded cross-layer relation: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates are connected to measured protein abundance in several organisms, but measured ribosome-derived $TE$ accounts for a large part of that connection only in a subset of the matched panel.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
No translation mechanism, protein folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, phenotype claim, causal pathway, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  The measured $TE$ contact is condition-specific and cross-sectional; PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-omics readout; the mRNA denominator is a control and not itself translation realization; and all mediation fractions here are statistical variance partitions.  A stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as perturbation, rescue, localization, turnover, structural, physical, or functional measurements, joined on the same biological units and evaluated with its own controlled statistic.
