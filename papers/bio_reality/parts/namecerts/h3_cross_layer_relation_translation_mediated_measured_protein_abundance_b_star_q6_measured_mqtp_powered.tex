\subsection{Measured translation mediation of B$^*_Q6$ protein-abundance association}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-mqtp-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record tests whether the nine residual coordinates of $B^*_Q6$ retain a protein-abundance association after the measured translation-efficiency layer is inserted between codon usage and protein abundance. The coordinate set is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The response $Y$ is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb protein abundance in ppm, joined by protein identifier to coding-sequence codon counts. The translation readout $T$ is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ read as ribosome footprint divided by matched mRNA in the local ribosome-profiling table. The covariate surface $Z$ contains an intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the M-density baseline. The residualization has $24$ control columns and rank $23$ in every organism reported here, while the residualized $B^*_Q6$ block keeps rank $9$.

\paragraph{Rows and ranks.}
Four organism panels are finite and rank-powered. For \emph{Danio rerio}, the three-way join contains $13{,}140$ rows, with $14{,}410$ CDS rows reported, $14{,}410$ valid abundance rows, and $13{,}140$ valid measured-TE rows. After $Z$ residualization the residual degrees of freedom are $13{,}117$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X_Q,T)=10$. For \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the three-way join contains $3{,}415$ rows, with $3{,}739$ CDS rows reported, $3{,}747$ valid abundance rows, and $3{,}415$ valid measured-TE rows; the residual degrees of freedom are $3{,}392$. For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the join contains $11{,}554$ rows, with $19{,}053$ CDS rows reported, $19{,}483$ valid abundance rows, and $11{,}554$ valid measured-TE rows; the residual degrees of freedom are $11{,}531$. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the join contains $5{,}128$ rows, with $5{,}815$ CDS rows reported, $6{,}351$ valid abundance rows, and $5{,}128$ valid measured-TE rows; the residual degrees of freedom are $5{,}105$.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
The finite-row statistic is a joint nine-dimensional decomposition, not a single-coordinate anecdote. Let $S^{QT}_{meas}$ denote the residual $B^*_Q6$ survival against measured TE, let $S^{QP}$ denote residual survival against measured protein abundance, and let $M^{QTP}_{meas}$ denote the observed fraction of the $Q \to P$ association removed when measured $T$ is conditioned. The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{Danio rerio}:&\quad S^{QT}_{meas}=0.04034217614654355,\quad S^{QP}=0.04926578454614652,\quad M^{QTP}_{meas}=0,\\
\textit{E. coli}:&\quad S^{QT}_{meas}=0.009394266754822262,\quad S^{QP}=0.10941535951828074,\quad M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.04091158340950673,\\
\textit{H. sapiens}:&\quad S^{QT}_{meas}=0.018837911344127926,\quad S^{QP}=0.04288464996165745,\quad M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.0027966485036786744,\\
\textit{S. cerevisiae}:&\quad S^{QT}_{meas}=0.13681931343730436,\quad S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042,\quad M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw mediation fraction is suppressive in zebrafish, $-0.04801486048721136$, so the clipped reported value is $0$. The other three panels have positive measured mediation fractions, strongest in yeast. Thus the readback is not merely that codon residuals predict protein abundance; it separates direct residual protein-omics association from the part that is attenuated by measured ribosome-efficiency information.

The same conclusion is visible in the residual energy table. In zebrafish, $X_Q$ explains $1092.0114819316218$ units of $Y$ energy, $T$ explains $16.418999670692678$, and the direct $X_Q$ improvement after $T$ remains $1144.4442608870015$, matching the null mediated fraction. In \emph{E. coli}, $X_Q$ explains $393.78692134854015$ units of $Y$ energy, $T$ explains $35.56347808682994$, and the direct improvement after $T$ is $377.67647487021645$. In human, the measured TE layer is weak against abundance: $T$ explains only $2.0678517436514716$ units of $Y$ energy and the post-$T$ direct $X_Q$ improvement remains $563.803837143658$. Yeast is the strong positive case: $T$ explains $593.5847863333478$ units of abundance energy, $X_Q$ explains $675.3275481062249$, and the direct improvement after $T$ falls to $351.63835021403213$.

The direct protein-omics screen over the larger abundance-only panel reports positive $S^{QP}$ in all fifteen organisms, with all nine residual coordinates surviving at the $10^{-12}$ threshold; examples include $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.04613529807085213$ in zebrafish. A broader joint TE-plus-mRNA-stability decomposition gives joint mediation fractions $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, $0.2404669586626661$ in \emph{E. coli}, and $0.16890712099923358$ in human, with right-tail permutation values $0.000999000999000999$ in each of those three panels. The partial readout dictionary row in yeast uses $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $120$ permutations, $1{,}800$ joined rows, and reports selection-aware coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ against a null $95$th percentile of $0.029149007242956115$. The $f_3$ dwell row gives held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ for yeast, above the fixed-readout null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, with $p\approx 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ for the $f_3$--dwell correlation. \paragraph{Readback.}
The measured-mediation row is therefore ready as a finite statistical chapter. The strongest finite case is yeast: it has a powered three-way join, positive measured-TE survival, positive protein-abundance survival, and a measured mediation fraction near $0.48$. \emph{E. coli} and human preserve the same qualitative shape with much smaller measured mediation. Zebrafish is a boundary case: both measured-TE and abundance associations are positive, but conditioning on measured TE does not absorb the direct abundance association. The four panels therefore separate three outcomes: measured translation-mediated attenuation, weak attenuation, and direct or confounded protein-omics survival.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter licenses only the finite-row statement just described: $B^*_Q6$ residual coordinates have measured protein-abundance associations, and in some panels a measured TE covariate absorbs part of that association. It does not by itself assert a general translation law, a causal pathway, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, perturbation rescue, or universality. Those stronger readings would require their own matched contacts and finite statistics at the relevant layer.
