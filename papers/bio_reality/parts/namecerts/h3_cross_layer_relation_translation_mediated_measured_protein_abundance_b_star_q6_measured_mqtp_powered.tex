\subsection{Measured translation conditioning of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-measured-mqtp-power}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record evaluates whether the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface reaches measured protein abundance through a measured translation readout rather than through an unconditioned codon-to-protein association.  The finite carrier is a matched matrix whose rows are coding sequences and whose columns contain the residual coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, together with measured translation efficiency $T$ and measured protein abundance $Y$.  The translation readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$, with $TE$ computed as ribosome footprint divided by mRNA in the local measured-ribosome files.  The abundance readout is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance in parts per million, joined by protein identifier.  The nuisance block $Z$ contains an intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline; in every organism below the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$ and $Z$ has rank $23$.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The measured translation--protein--codon join is nondegenerate in four organisms.  Danio rerio has $13140$ three-way rows out of $14410$ CDS rows with valid abundance and $13140$ valid measured-TE rows.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $3415$ three-way rows out of $3739$ CDS rows, with $3747$ valid abundance entries and $3415$ measured-TE entries.  Homo sapiens has $11554$ three-way rows out of $19053$ CDS rows, with $19483$ valid abundance entries and $11554$ measured-TE entries.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $5128$ three-way rows out of $5815$ CDS rows, with $6351$ valid abundance entries and $5128$ measured-TE entries.  The corresponding measured-TE contact hit rates are $0.9118667591950035$ for danio, $0.9133458143888741$ for E. coli, $0.6064136881331024$ for human, and $0.8818572656921754$ for yeast.  The direct protein-abundance contact is also powered in the larger PAXdb panel: for example, the abundance joins include $19053$ human rows at hit rate $0.9779294769799313$, $5815$ yeast rows at hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, $3739$ E. coli rows at hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, and $14410$ danio rows at hit rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Measured survival values.}
After residualization, each organism gives a finite scalar pair $(S^{QT}_{meas},S^{QP})$ and a measured attenuation fraction $M^{QTP}_{meas}$.  The observed values are

\begin{aligned}

\text{E. coli K12 MG1655:}\quad & S^{QT}_{meas}=0.009394266754822262,\quad S^{QP}=0.10941535951828074,\quad M^{QTP}=0.04091158340950673,\\
\text{Homo sapiens:}\quad & S^{QT}_{meas}=0.018837911344127926,\quad S^{QP}=0.04288464996165745,\quad M^{QTP}=0.0027966485036786744,\\
\text{Saccharomyces cerevisiae:}\quad & S^{QT}_{meas}=0.13681931343730436,\quad S^{QP}=0.3146818503437042,\quad M^{QTP}=0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus all four organisms have a positive measured translation-readout survival and a positive direct protein-abundance survival, but only yeast has a large measured translation-conditioning fraction.  E. coli gives a small positive measured attenuation, human gives a very small positive attenuation, and danio gives a suppressive raw fraction that is clipped to $0$ by the unit-interval reporting rule.  This is a measured-readout statement, not an inference from modeled tAI.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The four-case interpretation is local to the measured rows.  A coordinate or coordinate block can be translation-null, protein-omics-null, direct-protein positive without measured translation absorption, or measured-translation mediated.  The present scalar joint test places yeast closest to the measured-mediation case because nearly half of the direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal is absorbed by measured $TE$.  E. coli and human remain positive but weakly mediated, while danio remains a translation-surviving and protein-surviving panel without positive measured mediation.  A related modeled-tAI discriminant classifies yeast as $9$ case-D coordinates under its modeled translation readout, while E. coli has $7$ modeled case-D coordinates and $2$ positive-positive coordinates without a mediation drop.  Those modeled labels are useful as a contrast class, but the measured-TE values above are the operative finite observations for this subsection.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein-omics survival.}
The larger direct protein-omics audit shows that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ is not a numerically vacuous protein-abundance predictor: all $15$ organisms in the direct PAXdb panel have $9$ surviving coordinates after the same family of controls.  Representative direct $R^2_{QP}$ values are $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in E. coli, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.04613529807085213$ in danio, $0.10217906758013379$ in Caenorhabditis elegans, and $0.019256877907572044$ in Halobacterium salinarum.  This direct surface is precisely why the measured conditioning step matters: direct survival alone would only say that codon residuals predict a cross-sectional protein-abundance readout; it would not identify a translation-mediated route.

\paragraph{Joint readout context.}
A separate three-organism variance partition with measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability gives additional scale.  In yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving a joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In E. coli, $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, giving joint fraction $0.2404669586626661$ with the same right-tail value.  In human, $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, giving joint fraction $0.16890712099923358$ with the same right-tail value.  The dictionary-style partial-mediation audit on the yeast context, with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n_{join}=1800$, and $120$ permutations, reports coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$, a raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95$ percent value $0.029149007242956115$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These numbers agree with the measured-TE conclusion: there is real reducible signal, but a large residual component remains outside the measured readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Positional and dwell checks.}
The yeast $f_3$-stress dwell audit gives a concrete lower-layer contact for part of the residual.  On $n_{join}=1257$ rows with $5$ folds and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, the base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the full base-plus-dwell model has held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment is $0.01856473944057712$ against null $95$ percent value $0.0013115362189692937$.  The $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional version assigns the leading dwell segment to the $5'$ window: the $5'$ test passes, the $3'$ test does not, and the primary structural-order ramp audit has weighted $f_3$ ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, sign-flip probability $0.0478515625$ for the ramp, and paired ramp-minus-body probability $0.05859375$.  These checks support a measured-readout localization of part of the signal; they still do not turn abundance mediation into a folding or function claim.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The finite rows show that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive both measured $TE$ and measured protein abundance in four rank-powered panels, and that measured $TE$ absorbs a large fraction of the protein-abundance association in yeast, a small fraction in E. coli and human, and none after clipping in danio.  This supports a cross-sectional measured-readout mediation surface only.  It does not establish causal mediation, temporal ordering, synonymous-edit rescue, translation mechanism, elongation mechanism, protein folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, or universality.  Any such assertion would require a separate layer-matched measurement or perturbation contact and its own finite statistic.
