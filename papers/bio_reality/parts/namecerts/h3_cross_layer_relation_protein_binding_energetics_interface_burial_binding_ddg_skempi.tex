Those three numbers are the whole quantitative payload used by this chapter for the binding-energy contact: they certify that an externally grounded protein-interface table was reached and de-duplicated to a finite ledger, but they do not by themselves identify a causal pathway, a universal folding rule, or a protein-function theorem.

Its admissible mathematical role is finite and descriptive: it can say that a row exists, that it belongs to the de-duplicated interface-contact set, and that the contact surface is large enough for later row-wise comparisons. $$
\begin{aligned}

$$
No coefficient, effect direction, enrichment statistic, or protein-class partition is asserted for this binding-energy table in this finite row. The chapter therefore records reachability and cardinality, not an explanatory law.

\paragraph{Negative scope.}
The SKEMPI-style row count cannot be read as evidence for translation realization, folded-structure admissibility, protein function, phenotype, trafficking, or organism-wide universality. It also cannot turn the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual geometry into a binding-energy mechanism. A later test would have to join the same protein identifiers, define an interface-burial statistic, declare the mutation or complex aggregation policy, and then compare the resulting readout against a row-matched null. Until such a joined statistic is present, the contact only says that $4829$ rows and $3452$ distinct entries were reachable in the protein binding energetics surface.
