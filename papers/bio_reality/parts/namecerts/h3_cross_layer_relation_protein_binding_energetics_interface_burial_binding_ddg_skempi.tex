\paragraph{Finite contact.}
The interface-burial binding-energy record attaches a protein-energetics readout to the BioReality ledger without upgrading it to a structural or mechanistic law.  The finite contact is the SKEMPI-side table of interface-burial and binding-$\Delta\Delta G$ rows: the audited run contains $4829$ rows, of which $3452$ are distinct finite cases, and the executable readback completed in $5.972095966339111$ seconds.  Thus the contact is not an empty name.  It is a bounded finite table over externally curated protein-binding perturbation entries, and its admissible mathematical role is to provide a row-indexed energetic readout that can be compared with other finite readouts only after the relevant identifiers and covariates have been joined.

\paragraph{Ledger variables.}
For the cross-layer ranking ledger, a candidate readout $H$ is represented by the finite tuple consisting of absorbed energy $A_H$, coordinate-support direction cosine $C_H$, a raw directional product $A_H C_H$, a description-length term $DL(H)$, and the penalized score
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \operatorname{Score}(H) &= A_H C_H - \lambda DL(H), \\
  \lambda &= 0.01, \\
  DL(H) &= \log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}}).
\end{aligned}
$$
For one-column readouts this gives $DL(H)=1$, while the twelve-column localization readout has $DL(H)=3.700439718141092$.  The protein-binding energetics table is admitted at this same bookkeeping level: it may become a candidate readout only through its finite SKEMPI rows and a declared join, not through a verbal claim that interface burial explains a codon, translation, abundance, or residual coordinate signal.

\paragraph{Observed finite scale.}
The primary protein-binding contact has only the following audited numerical payload at this stage: $n=4829$, $3452$ distinct entries, and runtime $5.972095966339111$ seconds.  These numbers are sufficient to certify that the contact is a nontrivial finite table, but they are not sufficient to certify an effect size, direction, null exceedance, or causal path.  By comparison, the direction-aware ranking ledger already reports organism-specific finite rows for other readouts.  In the human ledger, complex membership has $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, $A_HC_H=0.006566030115340095$, and $\operatorname{Score}=-0.0034339698846599055$; human mRNA has $A_H=0.007858222847072393$, $C_H=0.7921354915786852$, $A_HC_H=0.006224777217900546$, and $\operatorname{Score}=-0.0037752227820994546$; human PTM density has the larger absorbed energy $A_H=0.014884825920744861$ but weaker directional alignment $C_H=0.3604440741749213$, giving $A_HC_H=0.0053651472982577525$ and $\operatorname{Score}=-0.004634852701742248$.  This comparison fixes the intended reading of the binding-energy contact: row count alone is not a score, absorbed energy without direction is not a mechanism, and a high-energy readout can be demoted by the direction filter.

\paragraph{Boundary-set calibration.}
A separate finite boundary-set diagnostic supplies a useful scale check.  The Window6 boundary $R$ set has $10$ sense codons and, across $15$ computed organisms, all $15$ directional non-tie cases are in the avoided direction.  The one-sided binomial value for avoided preference is $3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$ and the two-sided value is $6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$.  Among individually significant organisms, $6$ of $6$ are avoided.  This is a strong finite signature for that codon-window boundary, but it still does not imply protein-binding energetics, folding, function, phenotype, or a universal law.  It is included here only to make the local standard explicit: a finite table may be strong for the exact statistic it computes while remaining silent about every stronger biological interpretation.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The SKEMPI interface-burial binding-$\Delta\Delta G$ contact is ready as a finite BioReality row source: it has $4829$ audited rows and $3452$ distinct cases.  Its present conclusion is deliberately narrow.  It establishes that protein-binding energetics can be represented as a finite external readout available for future same-row joins and null tests.  It does not yet establish that interface burial absorbs the $B^*_{Q6}$ non-translation residual, that it aligns with any residual coordinate support, or that it supplies a shared cross-organism absorber.  Any later promotion must report the joined row count, the readout columns, $A_H$, $C_H$, raw $A_HC_H$, $DL(H)$, $\operatorname{Score}$, and a degree-of-freedom-matched null status.  Missing joins remain missing data rather than negative evidence.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation claim follows from the binding-energy table alone.  No folding pathway, physical admissibility statement, biological function, molecular mechanism, organism-wide channel, or universal principle follows from the row count, the distinct count, a future rank, a future score, or a contact name.  The only current assertion is the finite one: an externally grounded protein-binding energetics table exists at the audited scale, and it may be used as a bounded readout only under an explicit finite join and a separately reported null calculation.
