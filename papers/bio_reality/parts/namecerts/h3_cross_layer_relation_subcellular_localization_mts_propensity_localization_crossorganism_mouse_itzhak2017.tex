\paragraph{Conditioned relation.}
The biological question is whether a Q6 residual coordinate has any bounded relation to protein-localization readback after the translation and abundance layers have been explicitly represented. The local readback chain is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \\
    &\longrightarrow P_{\mathrm{abundance}} \\
    &\longrightarrow L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is the same-row translation readout, $P_{\mathrm{abundance}}$ is an intermediate protein-abundance control surface, and $L_k$ is a protein-state localization label, score, enrichment, or compartmental readout. The direction of this notation is bookkeeping for the conditioned test, not a causal claim. A localization association is eligible for interpretation only when the coordinate term remains non-null after amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, methionine-density, matched mRNA abundance, translation readout, protein abundance, and simpler codon-baseline controls are all represented in the same finite panel.

\paragraph{Null compression rule.}
If the row-level translation component is missing, degenerate, or absorbed by the controls, the Q6 coordinate is not allowed to pass into a localization claim. It is compressed back to a translation-boundary or protein-abundance-boundary statement. In symbols, the admissible positive readback has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{survives}(q_i,L_k) &= 1 \\
\text{only if}\quad \operatorname{rank}(q_i \to T_j) &> 0 \\
\text{and}\quad \operatorname{survives}(q_i,L_k\mid T_j,P_{\mathrm{abundance}},C) &= 1,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $C$ abbreviates the declared row controls. When the translation term is null, the localization term is not read as a failed biological mechanism; it is simply outside the permitted inference surface for this chapter. Protein abundance is likewise not promoted into a localization mechanism. It can condition or attenuate the readback, but it does not explain trafficking, compartment identity, folding, function, phenotype, or physical admissibility.

\paragraph{Localization contact.}
The localization readout is bounded to protein-level localization surfaces such as compartment annotations, imaging screens, fractionation proteomics, or proximity-labeling measurements keyed to UniProt, RefSeq protein, locus, or CDS-derived protein identifiers. The row surface therefore permits a local question: among the $8944$ joined entries in run \texttt{94942f5abfc4}, with $505$ mitochondrial entries, does any Q6 residual coordinate retain a conditioned association with the mitochondrial or other localization readout once translation, mRNA, abundance, and sequence controls are attached? The finite counts make this a concrete matched-row question. They do not license a direct statement from codon geometry to protein localization.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter does not claim that a Q6 residual coordinate directly measures translation, folded structure, protein physical admissibility, biochemical function, trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, phenotype, fitness, or a cross-organism law. It also does not treat modelled tAI or stAI weights as observed ribosome occupancy, and it does not treat bulk protein abundance as localization evidence. A positive localization statement would require the same-row translation step to be non-null and the localization statistic to remain after the declared controls. Without that separate layer-matched contact and statistic, the correct reading is narrower: the run defines a finite localization-readback boundary with $8944$ joined rows and $505$ mitochondrial rows, while all stronger biological interpretations remain unasserted.
