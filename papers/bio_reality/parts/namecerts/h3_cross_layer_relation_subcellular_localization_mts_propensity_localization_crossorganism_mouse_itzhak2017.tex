\subsection{Mouse subcellular localization readback after translation conditioning}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-subcellular-localization-mouse-itzhak2017}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row witness.} This chapter records the mouse Itzhak 2017 localization packet as a finite matched CDS-by-organism table whose internal order is residual coordinate, same-row translation readout, matched mRNA control, protein abundance, and then localization readback. In the verified run \texttt{94942f5abfc4}, the join produced $n_{\mathrm{joined}}=8944$ rows and $n_{\mathrm{mito}}=505$ mitochondrial readouts, with runtime \(21.266\) seconds, and the verification timestamp is \texttt{2026-06-25T01:51:51+00:00}. The mitochondrial share in the joined table is therefore about \(505/8944 \approx 5.65\%\). Those are the only finite-row scalars needed to pin down the evidence surface in this chapter.

\paragraph{Conditioned readback.} The point of the packet is not to collapse sequence geometry directly into localization. A B\(^*\)\_Q6 residual coordinate is only allowed to contribute after the translation layer is present on the same row, with mRNA kept as a control and protein abundance retained as an intermediate readout rather than promoted to a mechanism. In that conservative reading, the finite table supports a conditioned relation of the form
$$\begin{aligned}
q_i \to T_j \to \text{protein abundance/control} \to L_k,
\end{aligned}$$
where the localization label is read as an observed endpoint of the matched table, not as an intrinsic property of the residual coordinate itself. If the translation step is null, missing, or not row-matched, the coordinate stays at the translation/protein-abundance boundary and is not upgraded into a localization claim.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.} The chapter does not infer folding, physical admissibility, function, trafficking mechanism, compartment identity mechanism, phenotype, or universality from the finite table. Those statements require a separate contact at the corresponding layer. Here, the localization surface is treated as an observed readback attached to a controlled finite row panel, not as proof of a downstream mechanism. The safe conclusion is modest but concrete: under organism matching, CDS matching, mRNA control, and abundance control, this packet contains a non-null localization readback for a finite subset of rows, while the remainder of the interpretation must stay at the controlled association level.

\paragraph{Interpretive note.} The same-row structure matters. The packet is not read as a loose cross-dataset correlation, but as a row-aligned witness in which the translation surface precedes the localization surface. That sequencing is what keeps the claim biologically bounded. It also explains why the protein-abundance channel stays intermediate: it helps condition the readback, but it does not by itself certify a localization mechanism. The chapter therefore preserves the distinction between sequence-derived residual coordinates, translation readout, abundance control, and localization endpoint, and it stops exactly where the finite evidence stops.
