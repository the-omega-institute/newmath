\subsection{Mouse subcellular localization packet for the B$^\ast_{Q6}$ cross-layer relation}\label{subsec:h3-cross-layer-relation-subcellular-localization-mouse-itzhak2017}

\paragraph{Finite witness.} The cross-organism mouse Itzhak2017 packet is a matched CDS-by-organism table carrying $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, same-row translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, protein abundance, and a protein-localization readout surface. The verified finite row bundle is tagged by experiment run \texttt{94942f5abfc4} and was recorded at \texttt{2026-06-25T07:38:30+00:00}. It joins $8944$ rows in $21.266\,\mathrm{s}$, with $505$ mitochondrial localization assignments. In normalized form,
$$\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{joined}} &= 8944, \\
N_{\mathrm{mito}} &= 505, \\
t_{\mathrm{run}} &= 21.266\,\mathrm{s}, \\
\frac{N_{\mathrm{mito}}}{N_{\mathrm{joined}}} &\approx 0.0565.
\end{aligned}$$
This is a finite, row-level witness on a controlled localization surface, not a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Readout order.} The row is interpreted in a fixed order. A residual coordinate $q_i$ is not taken as localization on its own; it is first paired with the same-row translation readout $T_j$, then checked against the row-aligned mRNA control and the protein-abundance control, and only then compared with the localization label $L_k$. If the translation step is absent or null, the localization entry is treated as null as well, and the row stays at the translation/protein-abundance boundary. That ordering keeps the coordinate system separate from the measured readouts and prevents a geometry-only reading of the packet.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.} The claim supported here is limited to the localization cross-layer relation in the matched finite table. Nothing in this record licenses a conclusion about translation itself, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. Those require separate layer-matched contacts with their own readouts, not merely the presence of a $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate or a localization tag. The only thing this chapter asserts is that the localized readback remains meaningful after the amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA abundance, translation readout, and protein-abundance controls are attached.

\paragraph{Interpretive note.} The internal coordinate bundle is bookkeeping for the same-row comparison; it does not generate the biology on its own. The external localization measurement is the endpoint, and the finite witness above shows that the endpoint was evaluated on $8944$ joined rows in $21.266\,\mathrm{s}$ with $505$ mitochondrial assignments. Everything beyond that endpoint remains outside the present claim until a separate contact supplies it.
