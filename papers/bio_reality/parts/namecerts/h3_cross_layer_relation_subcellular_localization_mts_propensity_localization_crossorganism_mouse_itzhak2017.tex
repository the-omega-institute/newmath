\subsection{Translation- and abundance-conditioned localization readback}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-abundance-localization-readback}

\paragraph{Finite table and observed surface.}
This record concerns a finite matched coding-sequence table in which a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^*_{Q6}$ basis is not allowed to speak directly about protein localization. The run identified by $94942f5abfc4$ joined $8944$ CDS-level rows and marked $505$ of those rows as mitochondrial-localization rows under the attached localization readout. The reported runtime for the finite join and statistic surface was $21.266$ seconds. These numbers fix the empirical scope of this entry: the chapter is about a row-bounded table with a localization-positive subset, not about a general biological law over all coding sequences.

\paragraph{Layer order.}
The admissible readback order is sequential. First the amino-acid quotient has already separated the synonymous residual coordinate from amino-acid composition. Next the same row must carry organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA controls. Only after that may a translation readout $T_j$ be used, and protein abundance is then treated as an intermediate control surface rather than as a localization mechanism. The localization value $L_k$ is read only as a protein-state measurement attached to the same finite row. In symbols, the permitted chain has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \\
    &\longrightarrow P_{\mathrm{abundance}} \ \text{as a conditioning surface} \\
    &\longrightarrow L_k \ \text{as a bounded localization readout}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If the $q_i \to T_j$ step is absent, degenerate, or not same-row matched, then the localization coordinate is not interpreted as a $B^*_{Q6}$ localization signal. The entry is then compressed back to a translation or protein-abundance boundary statement.

\paragraph{Control interpretation.}
The $8944$ joined rows supply a finite domain on which translation, abundance, and localization can be compared under matched identifiers. The $505$ mitochondrial rows specify the positive localization surface available in this run. The ratio $505/8944$ is only the finite table prevalence of the mitochondrial label in this joined surface; it is not, by itself, a discovery of a codon-localization mechanism. A residual coordinate is eligible for a localization claim only if its association with $L_k$ remains non-null after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA abundance, the same-row translation readout, protein abundance, and simpler codon baselines are included. The chapter therefore treats abundance as a control that may absorb or condition a relation, not as proof that abundance causes localization.

\paragraph{Null route.}
The local null is explicit. When the translation readout is missing, or when the apparent $q_i$ association disappears after the translation and abundance controls are attached, the claim does not fail into a stronger biological interpretation. It becomes a bounded statement that the available rows do not support a translation-conditioned localization readback for that residual coordinate. Likewise, a direct $q_i \to L_k$ association without a nondegenerate $q_i \to T_j$ step is not accepted as a localization result. The only positive object allowed here is a conditioned finite-row relation of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Rel}(q_i,L_k \mid T_j, P_{\mathrm{abundance}}, C) \neq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $C$ denotes the declared sequence, organism, mRNA, and baseline controls. If this conditioned relation is null, the localization surface remains present as measured protein annotation, but it is not attributed to the residual coordinate.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation from codon geometry alone, does not infer folded structure from localization or abundance, and does not infer physical admissibility, biological function, trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, phenotype, or universality from the joined table. Each of those claims would require its own layer-matched measurement and statistic. The localization readout used here is only a bounded protein-level surface keyed to the finite rows; the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate is tested against it only after the translation and abundance gates have been represented in the same matched table.
