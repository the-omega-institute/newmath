\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite rows support a narrow ORF-eligibility statement only where an appropriate external sequence or curated interval contact is present.  The bare codon carrier $M$, its motif-compressed subset $R$, the saturation row, and the quotient profile supply a finite enumeration scaffold for start--stop reasoning.  The curated interval row supplies a concrete biological contact for the recorded dataset and gives strong finite statistics inside that dataset.  Outside such attached contact, the packet remains a boundary record: it may specify what a start--stop eligibility test would require, but it may not assert translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.
