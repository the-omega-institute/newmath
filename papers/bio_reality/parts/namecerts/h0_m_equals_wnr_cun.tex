\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.} The finite row supports the following restricted conclusion. The codon packet $M$, its $R$-motif compression, its $f_3$ saturation behavior, and its quotient geometry provide a reproducible finite coordinate for an ORF-eligibility boundary test. The curated interval-contact experiment then supplies a separate finite check that this frozen start--stop classifier separates curated positive intervals from matched decoys on the recorded dataset. Those stronger assertions require their own independent contacts.
