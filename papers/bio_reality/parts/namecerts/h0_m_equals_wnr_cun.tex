\paragraph{Boundary status before contact.}
The ORF-eligibility conjecture attached to this packet is therefore a seed-level boundary claim.  Its admissible content is the following: start and stop codon enumeration may define a possible test for open-reading-frame eligibility, but no eligibility assertion is licensed until a separate biological interval is supplied.  The two local probes enforce this reading.  One probe breaks if the pipeline asserts ORF eligibility, translation, folding, physical admissibility, or biological function from the start--stop geometry alone.  The other breaks if an ORF eligibility claim is recorded while no sequence database entry, transcript evidence, or curated annotation can test the proposed interval.  Thus the packet is a disciplined request for contact rather than a biological conclusion.
