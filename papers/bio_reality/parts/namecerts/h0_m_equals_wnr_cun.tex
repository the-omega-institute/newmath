\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
This chapter therefore records a contact-gated boundary statement.  The set $M$ and its motif, saturation, and quotient data are verified finite codon facts.  The ORF layer may be asserted only where a separate biological interval contact is present, such as the curated interval dataset just described.  Without that contact, the row is only a boundary probe asking what evidence would be needed.  With that contact, the conclusion remains restricted to start/stop interval eligibility under the frozen classifier and curated dataset.  It still does not assert protein production, folded molecular structure, physical viability, biological role, causal mechanism, or a global rule across organisms or sequence databases.

The self-check row reports $3$ checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
