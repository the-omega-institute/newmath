\paragraph{Finite codon packet.}
The finite record attached to claim $\mathrm{h0.M.equals.WNR.CUN}$ is a codon-level packet, not a biological realization claim. Its checked enumeration says that the set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly the union named by the row as $\mathrm{WNR}\cup \mathrm{CUN}$. In sorted order the verified members are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The companion compression row gives a smaller motif union $R$ with $13$ listed codons and no missing or extra entries relative to the row's declared motif expansion:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These are finite symbolic facts about codon strings. They say which codons are present in the packet and which motif union compresses part of the list. They do not yet say that an organism contains a corresponding open reading frame, that a transcript is expressed, or that a ribosome realizes translation on any interval.

\paragraph{Saturation and quotient geometry.}
A separate finite check records that the $f_3$ saturation of $R$ reaches $18$ codons, namely all members of $M$ except $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, and that adding the mediated row component recovers all $20$ members of $M$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\

$$
The same row reports an empty $f_3$ boundary list. The quotient-geometry check gives the finite vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$ for $M$, a quotient size of $10$, and a quotient vector $(10,14,6,1)$. The verified quotient representatives are the ten binary rows

It is still internal finite combinatorics over codon strings; it is not a measurement of gene expression, protein production, folding, biochemical stability, cellular fitness, or evolutionary selection.

\paragraph{Boundary status before contact.}
The conjecture row names the biological layer as ORF eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration. The seed statement is intentionally weaker than an ORF claim: without an attached biological interval, the packet remains at boundary status. The two probes enforce this restriction. One probe says that a start--stop-window seed must not assert ORF eligibility unless an external sequence, transcript, or curated interval supplies the biological object to be tested. The second probe says that recording ORF eligibility while lacking such a contact would break the packet. Since the contact list for this row is empty and the mismatch list is also empty, the local reading is precise: the finite codon geometry is admissible as a proposed test boundary, but the row itself supplies no direct organismal interval.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
For the present row, $M=\mathrm{WNR}\cup\mathrm{CUN}$ is ready as a finite codon-enumeration fact, and the motif, saturation, and quotient rows give additional finite structure around that enumeration. The biological reading must remain sharply bounded. Start/stop-window eligibility is not translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. None of those stronger statements follows from the codon packet, from the quotient geometry, or from the saturation row. Such claims require a separate contact whose data type can actually bear them.
