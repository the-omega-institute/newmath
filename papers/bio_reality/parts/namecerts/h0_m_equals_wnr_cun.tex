\subsection{Codon-set boundary for ORF eligibility}
\label{sec:h0-m-equals-wnr-cun-orf-eligibility}

\paragraph{Finite codon packet.}
The record studied here is the claim $h0.M.equals.WNR.CUN$, read inside the biological layer named $orf\_eligibility$.  The finite codon carrier is not itself a biological observation.  It is a closed enumeration packet whose verified row states that the set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly the union denoted by $WNR \cup CUN$ in the experiment row.  The certified list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the mathematical datum is a finite codon family, not a translated molecule, not a transcript, and not an annotated genomic interval.  The packet is therefore interpreted as a boundary object for asking which additional observations would be required before an open-reading-frame eligibility assertion could be made.

\paragraph{Contact requirement.}
The ORF eligibility layer is guarded by two boundary probes.  One probe rejects any inference from start--stop-window enumeration alone to ORF eligibility, translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, or biological function.  The other rejects a packet that records an ORF eligibility assertion while lacking a sequence database entry, transcript evidence, or curated annotation capable of testing the proposed interval.  Consequently the $20$-element $M$ row is admissible here only as a finite codon-side constraint on a possible test.  It is not an observed ORF interval until an independent biological contact supplies the interval.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The chapter therefore separates three levels.  First, the codon-set rows certify finite equalities and quotient structure for $M$, $R$, $Sat_{f3}(R)$, and $\pi_M$.  Second, the ORF window row permits only external start--stop-window enumeration for one loaded window.  Third, the curated interval row supplies a separate biological contact with $168$ annotated rows and strong matched controls.  Even after that contact, the conclusion remains confined to $orf\_eligibility$ for the stated curated interval task.  Nothing in this packet establishes translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular mechanism, biological function, cross-organism universality, or a global law.  Any such higher-layer assertion would require its own independently specified contact and its own finite audit row.
