\paragraph{Finite codon row.}
This chapter records the finite row named $h0.M.equals.WNR.CUN$.  Its direct verified content is a codon-set equality: the set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly the enumerated union $WNR \cup CUN$ in the recorded coordinate system.  The certified $M$ row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run reports $|M|=20$ and records the scalar field $size=20$.  Thus the chapter begins with a closed finite enumeration, not with a biological assertion about expressed genes, translation, molecular structure, or organismal function.

\paragraph{Motif compression row.}

It does not say that the motifs are translated, selected, expressed, conserved, or functionally active.

\paragraph{ORF-eligibility boundary.}
The biological object attached to this packet is open-reading-frame eligibility from start/stop codon enumeration.  The packet is therefore deliberately weaker than an ORF claim.  The top-level contact list is empty, and the stated minimal form says that the carrier, distinctions, and readback all await a separate contact with biological data.  Consequently, start and stop windows can only define a proposed test boundary here.  Without an attached sequence, transcript, or curated interval annotation, the row must not be read as an observed ORF interval.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The finite equality $M=WNR\cup CUN$, the motif list $R$, the saturation calculation, and the quotient geometry may be used to name a reproducible codon-coordinate packet.  They do not establish translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical mechanism, biological function, organism-level consequence, or universal law.  The self-check row records this same restraint as a BioReality gate with $3$ checked gates and the boundary statement that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  Any claim beyond ORF-window eligibility requires a separate contact that supplies the relevant biological interval and the layer-specific evidence.  In this chapter, the correct conclusion is therefore a finite codon enumeration plus a disciplined testing boundary: the arithmetic row is ready, while biological promotion is blocked unless another evidence-bearing contact supplies it.
