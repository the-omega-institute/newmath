\paragraph{Finite codon packet.}
The record considered here begins with a codon-level equality, not with a biological observation.  The verified finite set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly the union named by the row as WNR together with CUN.  In explicit RNA codon notation,
$$
\begin{aligned}

&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This equality was recorded in experiment $\mathrm{a53ef0f36703}$, with both the reported size and the reported cardinality equal to $20$.  The corresponding motif-compression row isolates a smaller set $R$ of size $13$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the same row reports no missing and no extra codons in that motif union.  The third-row saturation check then records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f3}(R)&=M\setminus\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\},\\
M&=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the $f3$ boundary list empty.  Thus the codon packet is finite and exactly enumerated: the content here is a closed combinatorial datum about codon strings, not yet an empirical claim about a genome, transcript, translated product, folded molecule, viable chemistry, or biological role.

\paragraph{Boundary interpretation before contact.}
The conjectural biological layer named by the row is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration.  Before a separate contact with a sequence, transcript, or curated interval annotation is attached, the only admissible statement is conditional: if a biological interval is later supplied, the codon geometry can be used to test whether that interval satisfies the declared start--stop enumeration rule.  Without that contact, the packet remains a boundary probe.  It is not an observed ORF, not a translation event, not evidence of a folded structure, not a statement of physical admissibility, and not a functional or mechanistic biological conclusion.  It also carries no universal law about genomes or coding regions.

The self-check row reports $3$ checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  Combining that self-check with the finite codon equalities, the quotient counts, the one-window contact, and the $168$-row curated interval contact gives a precise local verdict.
