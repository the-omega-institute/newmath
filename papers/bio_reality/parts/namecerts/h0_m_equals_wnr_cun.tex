\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.} The admissible conclusion is therefore narrow. The finite codon packet, its motif compression, its saturation behavior, and its quotient coordinates can be coupled to a curated interval contact to support start--stop-window eligibility on the stated dataset and protocol. Nothing in this chapter establishes translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, organism-level causation, or a universal law over unobserved sequences. Those stronger claims would require separate contacts whose measurements target those layers directly.
