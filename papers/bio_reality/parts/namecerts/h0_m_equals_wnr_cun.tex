\subsection{ORF Eligibility as a Contact-Gated Boundary Record}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-m-equals-wnr-cun}

\paragraph{Finite codon support.}
The record starts from the finite codon packet named by the claim identifier $h0.M.equals.WNR.CUN$.  Its verified carrier is the twenty-element set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC, \\
&\quad CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit value for $|M|$ is $20$, and the recorded equality is $M=WNR\cup CUN$.  This is a finite combinatorial identity over codon names.  It is not, by itself, an observed biological interval.  The chapter therefore treats the codon packet as a closed enumeration that can organize a later test, not as a direct assertion that an open reading frame exists in an organism.

\paragraph{Motif compression and saturation.}
A second finite check compresses the motif support to the thirteen codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
R={}&\{AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The comparison reported no missing codons and no extra codons.  The $f_3$ saturation audit then gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=M\setminus\{ACA,ACG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the explicit eighteen-codon value
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite run records $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA,ACG\}$ and an empty $f_3$ boundary list.  These checks say that the codon-level shape is internally pinned down by a small finite certificate.  They do not say that any transcript was expressed, that any ribosome traversed the interval, or that any protein product was made.

\paragraph{Boundary rule before contact.}
The seed statement is deliberately negative.  A start/stop-window enumeration may mark where an ORF-eligibility test would have to look, but no ORF-eligibility claim is admitted unless a separate sequence, transcript, or curated-annotation contact supplies an observed interval.  In particular, the packet cannot be read as translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.  Those are higher biological layers and require their own observations or curated records.  The self-check records this separation explicitly: three gates were checked, and the boundary statement says that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.

\paragraph{First external window check.}
The later start/stop-window experiment supplies a minimal contact at the ORF-eligibility layer only.  It loaded one external nucleotide-window dataset, with one input window and one classified window, from a curated finite nucleotide-window manifest dated $2026$-$05$-$25$.  Its scope is recorded as external window start/stop enumeration only, and its non-promotion label is $orf\_eligibility\_only$.  Thus this contact can test whether a supplied finite window satisfies the declared start/stop enumeration rule.  It still does not establish translation, folded structure, physical feasibility, or biological role.

\paragraph{Curated interval contact.}
A stronger contact uses $168$ rows from a curated interval dataset at \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/curated\_orf\_interval\_contact\_dataset.json}.  The source is NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals from \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 complete genome accession NC\_000913.3, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$.  The input classes present are positive curated ORFs, matched non-ORF decoys, and boundary cases.  The protocol was frozen at $2026$-$05$-$29T19{:}11{:}13+00{:}00$, before the external evidence time $2026$-$05$-$30T10{:}29{:}31+00{:}00$; the classifier rules, baselines, held-out splits, and input classes were all recorded as frozen.

Within that curated interval contact, $72$ positive intervals were accepted out of $72$, giving accept rate $1.0$ and one-sided binomial upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\cdot 10^{-22}$.  The matched decoy test rejected $72$ decoys out of $72$, again with rate $1.0$ and the same recorded binomial value $2.117582368135751\cdot 10^{-22}$. Boundary cases were capped rather than promoted: $0$ of $24$ boundary cases were promoted, so the promoted fraction was $0.0$.

\paragraph{Held-out stability.}
The held-out split audit repeats the same pattern over three folds.  In the left-locus fold, $24$ positives were accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys were rejected at rate $1.0$.  The middle-locus fold has the same $24/24$ positive acceptance and $24/24$ decoy rejection.  The right-locus fold also has $24/24$ positive acceptance and $24/24$ decoy rejection.  All three held-out split records are marked as passed.  These numbers support a finite contact-level ORF-eligibility classification over this curated dataset, not a general theorem about all genomes or all possible nucleotide strings.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter therefore separates three levels.  First, the codon packet $M=WNR\cup CUN$ and its motif, saturation, and quotient checks give a finite codon-side certificate.  Second, the external window and curated interval datasets provide reality contact for ORF start/stop eligibility over specified finite records.  Third, every stronger biological interpretation remains outside the conclusion.  No translation event, protein fold, energetic admissibility, cellular function, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the codon enumeration or from the curated interval contact unless an additional contact is supplied for that specific layer.
