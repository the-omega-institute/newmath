\subsection{Start--stop eligibility as a finite boundary packet}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-m-equals-wnr-cun}

\paragraph{Finite codon carrier.}
The packet records a finite codon-level object whose immediate verified content is the equality between the marked set $M$ and the union $WNR \cup CUN$.  The enumerated carrier has cardinality $20$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run records $|M|=20$.  This is a finite combinatorial fact about the codon list.  By itself it is not an observed transcript, not a coding interval, and not a statement that any member of the list is translated in a cell.

\paragraph{Motif compression row.}
A second finite row isolates the motif-compressed region $R$ and verifies that no listed codon is missing and no unlisted codon is added.  The row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $13$ displayed codons, no missing elements, and no extra elements.  The role of this row is bookkeeping: it says that the finite motif description and the explicit enumeration agree on this restricted set.  It does not say that the motifs are selected by an organism, used by a ribosome, or conserved by evolution.

\paragraph{Saturation and boundary behavior.}
The saturation check supplies a more rigid finite comparison.  It verifies
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \operatorname{Med}(R) \cup \{ACA\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f3}(R) &= \{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
so $\operatorname{Sat}_{f3}(R)$ has $18$ elements and differs from $M$ exactly by the two codons $ACA$ and $ACG$.  The same row records an empty $f3$ boundary, reported as $0$ boundary entries.  This is useful because it locates the start--stop eligibility seed inside a closed finite enumeration rather than in a vague biological narrative.  The conclusion remains restricted to codon-window eligibility: an empty finite boundary for this classifier is not evidence of translation, folding, physical admissibility, biochemical mechanism, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{ORF eligibility contact discipline.}
The biological object named by the packet is open-reading-frame eligibility from start/stop codon enumeration.  The seed row itself has no attached contact in its own contact list, so its local meaning is a boundary test: it marks what would have to be checked once an observed interval, transcript, or curated annotation is supplied.  A separate start--stop-window enumeration row reports one externally loaded window, one classified window, scope limited to external window start--stop enumeration, and snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$.  That row explicitly classifies only within the layer $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility\_only}$.

A stronger curated-interval contact row is also present in the finite ledger.  It uses $168$ rows from a curated ORF interval dataset sourced from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 accession NC\_000913.3, snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$.  The protocol was frozen before observation: the protocol time was $2026$-$05$-$29$T$19{:}11{:}13$Z and the external evidence was observed at $2026$-$05$-$30$T$10{:}29{:}31$Z.  The row records the required input classes $\mathrm{boundary\_case}$, $\mathrm{matched\_non\_orf\_decoy}$, and $\mathrm{positive\_curated\_orf}$.  It reports $72$ accepted positives out of $72$, $72$ rejected matched decoys out of $72$, and $72$ paired baseline wins out of $72$.  Each of these three one-sided exact binomial rows has upper-tail value
$$
\begin{aligned}
p = 2.117582368135751 \cdot 10^{-22}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The boundary controls have $24$ cases and $0$ promotions, giving promoted fraction $0.0$.  Three held-out splits are stable: left, middle, and right locus folds each have $24$ positives accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys rejected at rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
The finite rows support a precise statement: the codon carrier $M$ is exactly the verified $20$-element union $WNR \cup CUN$, its motif and saturation rows behave as recorded, and the separate curated-interval contact row tests ORF interval eligibility on its finite dataset.  Nothing in these rows licenses promotion to translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, organism-level role, or a global law about genomes.  Any such higher-layer assertion requires an additional contact designed for that layer.  The self-check row records this discipline directly: it checked $3$ gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.
