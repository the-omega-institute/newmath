\paragraph{Finite codon support.}
This record concerns the codon packet whose checked finite support is the twenty-element set
$$
\begin{aligned}

& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row check for \texttt{h0.M.equals.WNR.CUN} reports both $|M|=20$ and the equality of this list with $WNR\cup CUN$.  This is a syntactic codon-coordinate fact: it identifies the eligible alphabetic support available to the packet, but it does not by itself identify an observed gene, transcript, translated product, folded molecule, admissible physical state, cellular role, biochemical mechanism, or universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Start--stop enumeration contact.}
The finite-row contact \texttt{h2.orf.start\_stop.window\_eligibility} supplies one external nucleotide-window record from the snapshot dated 2026-05-25. It is not a claim that the interval is translated, folded, physically realizable as a protein state, functional in a cell, mechanistically explanatory, or general beyond the supplied contact.

\paragraph{Local claim and boundary.}
The admissible BioReality reading is therefore deliberately restricted.  The codon packet $M=WNR\cup CUN$, its motif compression, its $f3$ saturation behavior, and its quotient geometry provide a finite coordinate record.  The ORF-eligibility layer becomes assertible only where a curated or otherwise external biological interval contact is present and only to the extent that the frozen start--stop enumeration protocol evaluates that interval.  No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, organism-level consequence, or universal law follows from the coordinate packet or from start--stop-window enumeration alone.  The self-check row \texttt{self.claim} records the same boundary discipline with three checked gates and explicitly states that codon geometry is not promoted across biological layers.
