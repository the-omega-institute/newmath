\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The combined record is therefore sharply layered.  The $h0$ rows establish exact finite codon enumerations and finite quotient geometry.  The ORF rows, only when external interval data are supplied, establish eligibility behavior for the contacted finite manifests and curated intervals.
