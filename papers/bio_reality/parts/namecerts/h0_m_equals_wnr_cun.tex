\paragraph{Finite codon packet.}
The record fixes a finite codon carrier $M$ whose observed row cardinality is $20$.  The certified row list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The equality certified by the finite run is $M=WNR\cup CUN$ at size $20$, where the equality is read as an exact row identity and not as a biological law.  In the same finite environment, the compressed motif subset is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\\
&\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with no missing rows and no extra rows in the motif compression audit.  The saturation check records
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f3}(R)=M\setminus\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and its boundary remainder is empty.  Thus the finite geometry can say exactly which codon rows are in the packet and which two rows are outside the $f3$ saturation of $R$; it cannot by itself say that any nucleotide interval in a genome is translated.

\paragraph{ORF eligibility boundary.}
The biological object tested here is open-reading-frame eligibility by start/stop codon enumeration.  The seed statement is deliberately weaker than an ORF assertion: before a sequence, transcript, or curated annotation contact is attached, the packet remains a boundary record for what would have to be tested.  The row for start/stop-window eligibility reports one external window dataset loaded on snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$, with $1$ input window and $1$ classified window.  Its declared scope is external-window start/stop enumeration only, and the recorded non-promotion label is $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility\_only}$.  Consequently, the window classifier may certify that the supplied window was classified by the finite start/stop enumeration, but it may not promote that classification to translation, structure, biochemical feasibility, or biological function.

The protocol was frozen before the external observation: classifier rules, input classes, baselines, and held-out splits were fixed, and the analysis used no translation or protein-structure input.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite codon packet establishes the row identity $M=WNR\cup CUN$, the motif compression $R$, the $f3$ saturation boundary, and the quotient face-vector data stated above.  The curated interval contact separately establishes a scoped ORF-eligibility contact on $168$ rows with perfect positive acceptance, perfect matched-decoy rejection, perfect paired baseline advantage, and non-promotion of $24$ boundary cases.  These facts justify only the layer named ORF eligibility for the stated external interval-contact setting.  They do not establish translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanistic causation, or a universal genomic principle.  Any such stronger claim would require a distinct contact whose observations are able to test that stronger layer directly.
