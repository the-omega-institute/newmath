\paragraph{ORF-eligibility gate.}
The biological layer named here is $orf\_eligibility$, and the seed row is deliberately weaker than an ORF claim.  The start--stop enumeration check reports one external nucleotide window, one classified input window, and scope restricted to external window start--stop enumeration only; its source describes a curated finite nucleotide-window manifest and records $snapshot\_date=2026$-$05$-$25$.  The row also states the operative classification as $orf\_eligibility\_only$.  Consequently, the scientific content is conditional and local: a start--stop window can be sorted by its boundary pattern once an external interval is supplied, but the codon geometry alone is not a biological interval.  In the present claim packet the contact list is empty, so the $M=WNR\cup CUN$ equality remains a boundary record for what a later interval-contact test would have to examine.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore narrow.  The finite equality $M=WNR\cup CUN$, the motif set $R$, the saturation statement, and the quotient vectors provide a reproducible codon-window coordinate system.  They can support a probe asking whether a separately supplied nucleotide interval satisfies start--stop-window eligibility.  They cannot, without that separate contact, assert an observed ORF.  Even when an interval-contact row is available, the reported layer remains $orf\_eligibility$ rather than translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal law.  The self-check row records the same limitation: three gates were checked, and no codon geometry was promoted across biological layers.  This chapter therefore records a finite symbolic boundary and the precise evidence needed to cross it, while keeping all higher biological claims outside the scope of the row.
