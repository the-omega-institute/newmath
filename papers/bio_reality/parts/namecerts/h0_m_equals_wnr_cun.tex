\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The biological conclusion remains layer-local. The finite codon sets $M$ and $R$, the quotient rows, the $f3$ saturation computation, the one-window start--stop enumeration, and the $168$-row curated interval experiment do not establish translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. Those claims would require separate contacts capable of testing those layers. The self-check records $3$ checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Accordingly, this subsection may speak about start--stop ORF eligibility under an attached curated interval contact, but it may not treat codon-window geometry as evidence for expression, folding, fitness, function, or a general law of biology.
