\subsection{Protein-feature contact for the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ non-translation residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-protein-feature-contact}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual component that remains after the modeled-translation projection is materially reduced by coarse protein-feature readouts. The finite carrier consists of CDS-indexed residual coordinates, organism-specific control matrices, a modeled-complement residual target, and four fetched feature candidates: complex membership, domain count, post-translational-modification density, and transmembrane-helix count. The nine residual coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The control block has $24$ columns before rank reduction and rank $23$ in each organismal fit. Its columns are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^\ast_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Organismal joins.}
The feature contact is not a full-CDS panel. It is a sparse annotation join, and that sparsity is part of the mathematical object being tested. For \textit{Danio rerio}, the CDS side reports $14410$ joined CDS rows, the reviewed feature file contributes $3180$ feature records, and the all-readout feature join contains $2242$ rows. For \textit{Homo sapiens}, the corresponding counts are $19053$, $18842$, and $18335$. For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, they are $5815$, $6156$, and $5682$. Each organism has four one-dimensional candidates, so $K_c=1$ and $\operatorname{rank}(H_c)=1$ for complex membership, domain count, PTM density, and transmembrane count.

\paragraph{Target stability check.}
The residual target is the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual restricted to the feature join. Let $d_{\mathrm{full}}$ denote the full abundance-CDS complement reference and $d_{\mathrm{feat}}$ the same quantity recomputed after feature availability has changed the row set. The finite check returns
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{Danio rerio}:&\quad d_{\mathrm{full}}=0.874628721049266,\quad d_{\mathrm{feat}}=0.7361678489976374,\quad |\Delta|=0.1384608720516286,\\
\textit{Homo sapiens}:&\quad d_{\mathrm{full}}=0.9208949355679455,\quad d_{\mathrm{feat}}=0.9188293375787641,\quad |\Delta|=0.002065597989181489,\\
\textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}:&\quad d_{\mathrm{full}}=0.21173219572706703,\quad d_{\mathrm{feat}}=0.21235197749007645,\quad |\Delta|=0.0006197817630094193.
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared tolerance is $0.02$. Human and yeast satisfy this tolerance, while zebrafish fails it by a wide margin. Therefore the feature readout is mathematically usable as a bounded descriptive projection, but it is not stable enough to close a cross-organism $H$ claim. The zebrafish feature join changes the residual target rather than merely adding a candidate explanatory coordinate.

\paragraph{Feature distributions.}
The joined feature distributions are finite and highly non-Gaussian. In \textit{Danio rerio}, complex membership has mean $0.31043710972346117$ with $696$ nonzero rows out of $2242$; domain count has mean $0.856378233719893$, maximum $48$, and $847$ nonzero rows; PTM density has mean $0.0012176949624096891$, maximum $0.8014705882352942$, and $211$ nonzero rows; transmembrane count has mean $0.7292595896520964$, maximum $18$, and $443$ nonzero rows. In \textit{Homo sapiens}, the joined means are $0.3626943005181347$, $1.071284428688301$, $0.006473867757948575$, and $1.0721570766293973$, with nonzero counts $6650$, $8090$, $9210$, and $4792$ out of $18335$. In yeast, the joined means are $0.3505807814149947$, $0.4074269623372052$, $0.002774535811043876$, and $1.0007039774727209$, with nonzero counts $1992$, $1574$, $2282$, and $1173$ out of $5682$. Thus the panel reads coarse annotation density and membership variables, not a dense molecular-state measurement.

\paragraph{Absorption values.}
For a candidate feature $c$, the descriptive absorption value $a_c$ is the reduction obtained by projecting the modeled-complement residual onto the residualized candidate column after the declared controls. The computed values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{Danio rerio}:&\quad a_{\mathrm{complex}}=0.00039566384726157463,\quad a_{\mathrm{domain}}=0.0008268196005307971,\\
&\quad a_{\mathrm{PTM}}=0.014211240113472575,\quad a_{\mathrm{TM}}=0.001369142724157357,\\
\textit{Homo sapiens}:&\quad a_{\mathrm{complex}}=0.0075271999877804495,\quad a_{\mathrm{domain}}=0.000011405527940317433,\\
&\quad a_{\mathrm{PTM}}=0.014884825920540636,\quad a_{\mathrm{TM}}=0.0015569064882873212,\\
\textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}:&\quad a_{\mathrm{complex}}=0.00069212177545487,\quad a_{\mathrm{domain}}=0.00007029698692511431,\\
&\quad a_{\mathrm{PTM}}=0.0005759336988655581,\quad a_{\mathrm{TM}}=0.00031741485098156394.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest single absorption is PTM density in human, $0.014884825920540636$, followed closely by PTM density in zebrafish, $0.014211240113472575$. The yeast values are all below $0.001$. These magnitudes are small in absolute terms. They show that a coarse protein-feature column can line up with a small part of the non-translation residual on the available rows, but they do not show mechanism, mediation, or a stable biological law.

\paragraph{Degree-of-freedom null.}
A $200$-trial degree-of-freedom matched null was computed for each organism-candidate pair, with a Bonferroni-corrected excess threshold. Zebrafish PTM density exceeds its threshold: the null mean is $0.0004610486745564995$, the null $95$th percentile is $0.001788151234180097$, the threshold is $0.003775603188484381$, and the observed value is $0.014211240113472575$. Zebrafish complex membership, domain count, and transmembrane count do not exceed their thresholds. In human, complex membership, PTM density, and transmembrane count exceed thresholds: the thresholds are $0.0006605662314432479$, $0.00042094892238120357$, and $0.0002697448894057371$, respectively, while the observed absorptions are $0.0075271999877804495$, $0.014884825920540636$, and $0.0015569064882873212$. Human domain count does not exceed its threshold. In yeast, none of the four candidates exceeds its threshold; the largest yeast observed value, complex membership at $0.00069212177545487$, remains below its threshold $0.001015254045679051$.

\paragraph{Comparison with nearby translation contacts.}
The feature contact should be read beside, not above, the translation-layer contacts. Direct measured translation-efficiency projection on the all-readout join gives measured-TE absorption values $0.01759429001506974$ in zebrafish, $0.0010982956489726373$ in \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.0028947149257963897$ in human, and $0.0002762783754375593$ in yeast. Measured tRNA-gene-copy tAI absorbs $0.020817102845637092$ in zebrafish, $0.00005426229300865148$ in human, and $0.001530747712165342$ in yeast. Yeast protein half-life absorbs only $0.0009086031889966327$ on $3640$ joined turnover rows. These neighboring contacts place the protein-feature result in the same descriptive family: the values are finite projections against a controlled residual target, not causal steps in a molecular pathway.

\paragraph{Cross-organism context.}
The organism-specific meta table reports $16$ assembled organisms and $12$ complete-feature organisms. For the complete-feature subset, the dominant-coordinate versus domain contingency has $n=12$, Pearson $\chi^2=9.399999999999999$, Cramer's $V=0.6258327785172862$, and right-tail permutation value $0.5814185814185814$ over $1000$ permutations. Continuous tests for the $M^{QTP}$ fraction give $r=-0.3062616500469447$ versus GC3 with two-sided permutation value $0.35264735264735264$, $r=-0.033628115789504955$ versus log proteome size with value $0.913086913086913$, and $r=0.06029405206037823$ versus log tRNA-gene count with value $0.8621378621378621$. The domain variance-ratio test has value $0.8889980452277374$ and right-tail permutation value $0.4155844155844156$. These numbers do not resolve a domain-level organizing principle for the protein-feature result. They support the narrower conclusion that the finite table is heterogeneous and exploratory.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The finite conclusion is therefore conditional and local. Protein-feature projections were computed and bounded on reviewed-UniProt and STRING-derived feature joins; several human and zebrafish candidate absorptions exceed degree-of-freedom matched thresholds, especially PTM density, but sparse feature coverage changes the row set and prevents a stable cross-organism closure. The result may be reported as a descriptive feature-contact readback for the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ non-translation residual. It may not be reported as translation survival, translation mechanism, protein abundance mediation, folding evidence, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, or universality. The localization boundary is likewise separate: the Itzhak-style localization run reports $8696$ joined rows, $657$ mitochondrial rows, $2000$ actual permutations, and runtime $11.034382$ seconds, but those numbers belong to a different localization contact and do not convert this protein-feature table into a trafficking or compartment-mechanism result.
