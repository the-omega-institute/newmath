\subsection{Per-coordinate reducibility of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component}
\label{sec:biorality-bstar-q6-per-coordinate-reducibility}

\paragraph{Question.}
The finite-row test asks whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ behave as a single inseparable abundance source, or whether individual coordinates can be read against measured biological quantities after the usual sequence-derived controls have already been removed.  The source side is the nine-dimensional residual design
$$(
\begin{aligned}
Q=&\{K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN/Leu\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA/Leu\_UUG,\\
& Ser\_UCR/Ser\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA/Ser\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR/Thr\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
)$$
The target is residualized yeast protein abundance, specifically $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the sibling control basis is applied on the unified \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the coordinate source is the same $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual construction used by the broader translation and abundance analyses.

\paragraph{Finite design.}
The powered row uses $1800$ base proteins, imposes a minimum organism gate of $500$ proteins, and produces a working join of $1258$ rows for the held-out coordinate test.  Each single-coordinate projection $P_i=\operatorname{Proj}_{x_i}(target)$ is compared with a fixed measured-readout dictionary after both the target and the coordinate have been residualized against the same control space.  The dictionary rank is $3$, the cross-validation split count is $5$, and the deterministic null uses $120$ target-label permutations with the same folds and the same residualized design.  The coverage statistic is therefore not an in-sample coefficient table; it is a held-out coverage score for each coordinate projection against a fixed readout dictionary, judged against a permutation null.

\paragraph{Observed decomposition.}
All nine rows of the coordinate table were produced.  Eight coordinates passed the real-source gate in this finite design:
$$(
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN/Leu\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA/Leu\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR/Ser\_AGY,\ Thr\_ACR/Thr\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
)$$
The remaining listed coordinate, $Ser\_UCA/Ser\_UCG$, is present in the nine-row decomposition table but is not reported among the coordinates that cross the held-out/permutation real-source gate.  This asymmetry is important: the result is not that every coordinate is equally reducible to the measured dictionary, but that the abundance component is not confined to an opaque nine-dimensional block.  At least one coordinate, and in fact eight of the nine listed coordinates, has measurable held-out support under the finite yeast join and the declared null procedure.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby measured rows.}
The coordinate finding is consistent with several separately bounded finite contacts without being promoted to their stronger interpretations.  In the same yeast abundance family, a joint $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance association reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on a $5085$-row three-way join with mRNA half-life, and conditioning on the mRNA half-life readout left $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, giving an absorbed fraction $0.09994436477872035$ with a right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The mRNA-stability row alone reported $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ on the same $5085$ yeast join, with dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN/Leu\_UUR$ and cosine $0.8040935468384264$ against the same-join abundance vector.  A position-controlled ribosome-occupancy row also found that $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$ pass a local occupancy gate with $DL=2$ and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ across $5754$ joined yeast genes.  These numbers give biological context for why per-coordinate reducibility is plausible, but the present chapter only uses them as neighboring contacts: the coordinate-decomposition verdict itself rests on the $1258$-row held-out yeast abundance decomposition.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result supports a precise decomposition claim.  After amino-acid composition, GC3, length, organism context where applicable, and the $M$-density baseline are treated as controls in the surrounding $B^*_{Q6}$ workflow, the yeast abundance component still contains coordinate-level structure that a small measured-readout dictionary can recover out of sample.  The phrase ``real source'' here means that the single-coordinate target projection exceeds the deterministic label-permutation boundary under the stated held-out design.  It does not mean that the coordinate is a biochemical cause, a ribosome mechanism, a folding determinant, or a universal law.  It also does not imply that a positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is automatically a positive survival score $S_{ij}$; a survival entry requires its own controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This chapter stops at a finite cross-layer decomposition of residual codon-coordinate signal against yeast protein abundance and nearby measured readout dictionaries.  It does not by itself establish translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.  Those conclusions would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  The correct local statement is narrower: in the powered yeast row with $1800$ base proteins, $1258$ joined held-out rows, $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, dictionary rank $3$, and control rank $24$, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ table is fully computed and eight coordinates are reported as held-out real abundance sources.
