\subsection{Per-coordinate reducibility of the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ abundance component}\label{sec:b-star-q6-per-coordinate-reducibility}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row under test.}  This chapter isolates a single-coordinate question inside the nine-dimensional $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual panel.  The object is not the full joint association of $B^\ast_{Q6}$ with abundance, nor a claim about a translation mechanism.  For each residual coordinate $x_i$, the finite computation forms the single-coordinate projection $P_i=\operatorname{Proj}_{x_i}(y)$ of the target $y$, where $y$ is the $\log_{10}$ protein-abundance readout after the same sibling controls have been removed.  The reported statistic asks whether a fixed measured-readout dictionary, after the same control residualization, covers that one-coordinate target projection better than deterministic target-label permutations.

The verified row is the yeast panel.  The organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the base coding-sequence panel has $1800$ rows, and the final unified context join used for the held-out test has $1258$ rows.  The control basis has rank $24$, the measured-readout dictionary has rank $3$, and the design uses $5$ held-out folds with $120$ deterministic permutations.  The coordinate table has all nine entries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive-source list contains eight of these nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ remains in the decomposed coordinate table but is not in the finite list of coordinates that pass the reported held-out/permutation gate.

\paragraph{Statistic and decision rule.}  The finite computation uses held-out $R^2$ for a single residualized coordinate against the residualized target and compares that value to a deterministic permutation threshold built with the same folds and the same design matrix.  In compact notation, the row being tested is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_i^{\mathrm{coord}} &= R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(P_i, D_e \mid Z_e\bigr),\\
\mathrm{pass}(i) &\Longleftrightarrow S_i^{\mathrm{coord}} > \mathrm{Null95}_i,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z_e$ is the sibling control basis and $D_e$ is the measured-readout dictionary with rank $3$.  The run status is reported as passed because the nine-row decomposition was produced and at least one coordinate exceeded its held-out permutation gate.  The stronger finite observation is that eight coordinates are listed as having a real measured-source witness under this rule, while one coordinate is not listed.

\paragraph{Relation to the abundance residual.}  The coordinate-decomposition row should be read alongside the separately verified yeast abundance rows, but it is more granular than the joint nine-dimensional statistic.  In the yeast condition comparison, the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ abundance association with $\log_{10}$ abundance gives $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD and $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD, with $f3\_stress$ dominant in both conditions and a condition-vector cosine of $0.9993775935182967$.  On the mRNA half-life join, the same family of coordinates gives $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ for abundance and $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$ after conditioning on the half-life readout, so the reported absorbed fraction is $0.09994436477872035$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers do not replace the per-coordinate test; they give the finite background showing that the yeast abundance residual is not a one-row artifact of the decomposition step.

\paragraph{Internal coordinate structure.}  The decomposed result also agrees with the finer $f3\_stress$ family audit.  In that audit, the $f3\_stress$ projection has norm square $52.76911713166682$, accounting for $0.12259712041843235$ of the target norm square $430.42705205115925$.  The family audit uses $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, and $1800$ joined yeast rows, and it lists $19$ families as passing the same kind of held-out/permutation gate.  This supports the interpretation that the per-coordinate source list is not merely a single aggregate stress coordinate hiding all finite signal; it can be decomposed further into synonym-family contributions while staying inside the yeast abundance readout.

\paragraph{Biological scope.}  The finite conclusion is deliberately narrow.  The row says that, in the yeast abundance panel with $1258$ joined rows, the measured-readout dictionary of rank $3$ covers the single-coordinate abundance projections for eight named $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinates beyond the deterministic permutation threshold.  It does not say that codon residuals cause abundance, that ribosomes implement the relation, that a translation-efficiency contact has been measured for these same rows, or that the coordinates determine protein folding, structural admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, pathway mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger interpretations would require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, the ribosome-occupancy row is a distinct local-window analysis: it reports $5754$ joined yeast genes, $200$ requested within-position-bin permutations, and three families passing the local occupancy gates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$ under $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and $DL=2$.  That ribosome row is useful biological context, but it is not substituted for the abundance decomposition here.

\paragraph{Readback.}  The chapter therefore records a finite reducibility result: after sibling controls are removed, the yeast $\log_{10}$ protein-abundance target contains coordinate-specific projections that a fixed measured-readout dictionary can recover in held-out testing.  The strongest local statement is the eight-of-nine pass list under the $5$-fold, $120$-permutation design at $n=1258$ joined rows.  The missing ninth entry, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, is retained as a negative or unresolved coordinate in this specific finite table rather than silently absorbed into the positive claim.
