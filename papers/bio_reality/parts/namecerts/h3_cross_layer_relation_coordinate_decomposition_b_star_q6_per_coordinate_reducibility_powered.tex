\subsection{Per-coordinate reducibility of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-per-coordinate-reducibility}

\paragraph{Object being tested.}
The finite row tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ remain visible after the usual sequence and context covariates have been removed, one coordinate at a time.  The source design is the nine-dimensional residual coordinate system
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress, each residualized against the same sibling control basis $Z$.  In this row the target is log$_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified yeast context join, also residualized against the same controls.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the joined yeast table contains $n=1258$ proteins after the minimum-organism gate of $500$ proteins.  The computation begins from a larger yeast base table of $1800$ records and evaluates a nine-row decomposition table.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For a single residual coordinate $x_i$, the target component attributed to that coordinate is the orthogonal projection $P_i=\operatorname{Proj}_{x_i}(y)$, where $y$ is the residualized abundance target.  The reported coverage metric asks how much of each $P_i$ is captured by a fixed measured-readout dictionary after the same $Z$ residualization.  The row uses five held-out folds and compares each single-coordinate held-out $R^2$ against a deterministic target-label permutation boundary with $120$ permutations.  The measured dictionary has residual rank $3$, so the test is not a free nine-dimensional refit of abundance; it asks whether each one-coordinate abundance component can be recovered by a low-rank measured readout panel after the common covariates have been removed.  In compact notation, the tested quantity is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_i &= R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(P_i, D_e \mid Z\bigr),\\
\mathrm{real}(i) &\Longleftrightarrow C_i > \mathrm{Null95}_i,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $D_e$ is the fixed residualized readout dictionary of rank $3$ and the null is computed with the same folds and design matrix.

\paragraph{Finite-row outcome.}
The decomposition status is passed: all $9$ coordinate rows were produced.  Eight coordinates crossed the held-out/permutation gate as real abundance sources in this yeast row: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress.  The coordinate listed in the nine-row table but not in the real-source list is $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$.  Thus the result is not merely a joint $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance fit; it separates the joint residual into coordinate-level sources and shows that the abundance readout is not concentrated in a single synonymous axis.  The deterministic seed for the row is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_coordinate\_decomposition\_powered:deterministic}, which fixes both the fold assignment and the permutation comparison.

\paragraph{Relation to surrounding measured rows.}
The per-coordinate row is consistent with the stronger yeast abundance and stability records.  On the same style of yeast protein-abundance target, the growth-condition table reports $R^2_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$ in SD and $R^2_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ in YEPD, with $f3$-stress dominant in both conditions and a condition-vector cosine of $0.9993775935182967$.  The mRNA-stability mediation row reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$, and an absorbed fraction $0.027596042074056304$, corresponding to a mediation fraction of $0.09994436477872035$ against a permutation mean $0.00019674487584783138$, Null95 $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  The yeast half-life row reports $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between $S^{QM}$ and same-join $S^{QP}$, and $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ as the dominant mRNA-stability coordinate.  The corresponding E. coli and human half-life rows have $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$ with dominant $Arg_{AGR}$ and cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, and $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$ with dominant $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ and cosine $0.6351183883924848$, respectively.  These surrounding rows show that the yeast coordinate decomposition belongs to a broader measured-readout pattern, while still remaining a finite descriptive statistic.

\paragraph{Local ribosome and tissue contacts.}
A separate position-controlled ribosome-occupancy row reports $5754$ joined yeast genes after length checks, $9$ computed synonymous families, $200$ requested within-bin label permutations, and three families passing the local occupancy gate after the description-length penalty: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f3$-stress.  That gate used $C_H>0$, $DL=2$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$.  Human tissue abundance rows also show that the same coordinate system can change its leading coordinate by context: Adult B cells have $R^2_{QP}=0.03626117434944674$ with dominant $Arg_{AGR}$ over $9244$ joined proteins, Adult Heart has $R^2_{QP}=0.02320896321347296$ with dominant $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$ over $6468$ joined proteins, and Adult Liver has $R^2_{QP}=0.026133456228248052$ with dominant $Ile_{AUA}$ over $8664$ joined proteins.  The three tissue vectors have pairwise cosines $0.6883655232187615$, $0.8990093450963941$, and $0.9176793373754919$ under $1000$ permutations.  These entries support a coordinate-resolved interpretation rather than a single universal direction.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The chapter-level conclusion is that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis is reducible into identifiable one-coordinate abundance components on the yeast finite row.  The relevant finite statement is not that every coordinate has the same biological role, but that at least one and in fact eight of the nine named residual axes survive the held-out/permutation source test against the residualized protein-abundance target.  The positive row therefore blocks the claim that the abundance association is only an artifact of amino-acid composition, coding length, GC3, or the simpler $M$-density baseline.  It also blocks the weaker claim that the joint $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal is only an inseparable aggregate with no coordinate-level readback.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
This row does not by itself establish translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  It is a controlled finite statistic on residualized yeast protein abundance.  Translation-level claims require a separate measured or explicitly bounded translation contact; folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, and mechanism require their own layer-matched contacts and their own statistics.  The result should therefore be read as coordinate-level abundance reducibility under the stated controls, not as a general biological mechanism and not as evidence that tRNA gene copy, modeled adaptation weights, ribosome occupancy, and protein output are interchangeable measurements.
