\subsection{Rank-powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ core tRNA-adaptation direction}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-core-trna-adaptation}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual surface restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinate family, with the $f_3$-stress direction singled out after amino-acid projection.  The row object is not a raw codon table and not a protein-level assay.  It is a controlled coordinate table in which synonymous imbalance, organism identity, codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation-readout rows are kept as distinct columns.  The current powered run for the core tRNA-adaptation direction passed with experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered}, run id \texttt{373843e81379}, five folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered:deterministic}.  Thus the finite row supports a bounded statement: within the powered residual design, the universal core direction is classified as a tRNA-adaptation direction.

The biological stress therefore cannot be read from topology alone.  It becomes testable only when a context-dependent tRNA, wobble, or translation-readout row breaks the third-position symmetry that the finite codon surface itself leaves unresolved.

\paragraph{Powered residual direction.}
The linked powered evidence separates three questions.  First, the non-tRNA residual optimality run classified the dominant residual axis as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}, again with five folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$.  Second, the two-axis closure test concluded \texttt{third\_axis\_present}; its reason was that $d_{\perp}$ retained systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  Third, the three-axis sufficiency test concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}, showing that the tRNA plus $f_3$ span is not the whole powered residual story.  In finite-row terms, the chapter is therefore not asserting a single-axis codon law.  It records a tRNA-adaptation core, an $f_3$-translation-selection residual direction, and a remaining higher residual component that has to be kept separate.

\paragraph{Third-axis and mechanism checks.}
The third-axis identity run provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family.  The reported mean cosine was $0.128$, with one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$, over the same powered setting of five folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$.  The associated mechanism run classified the axis as \texttt{selection-not-GC}, \texttt{not-wobble-clustered}, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional contact.  The reason recorded for that call was that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ kept significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  These rows are important because they prevent a collapse of the record into a simple GC3 or wobble-cluster explanation.  The current finite evidence points to a selected synonymous residual direction, not merely to base-composition drift.

\paragraph{Axis spectrum.}
The per-organism axis-strength spectrum was computed for $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The descriptive conclusion was that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and that the dominant-count table was \texttt{\{'d\_resid4': 11\}}.  Domain means were explicitly marked descriptive under the imbalanced domain counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho = 0.136$.  This spectrum qualifies the core tRNA-adaptation statement: tRNA adaptation is the universal core direction in the named run, but the strongest per-organism captured spectrum is led by a fourth residual direction in this powered panel.

\paragraph{R-set tRNA poverty contact.}
The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run supplies the clearest finite mechanism-adjacent row.  It computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha = 0.05$, and passed.  Its mechanism call was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance.  This is a bounded supply-side statement: it concerns tRNA-copy and wobble-weight asymmetry over the finite codon boundary, not an observed ribosome-dwell assay and not a downstream phenotype.

\paragraph{Fourth-component exclusions.}
The fourth-mechanism checks rule out several tempting promotions.  The mRNA-stability run concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; the recorded reason was that yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair run concluded \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} using five folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $50$ shuffle-null samples.  The translation-accuracy run concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy}, with $11$ organisms computed out of $16$, five folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $200$ permutations.  The elongation-dwell run in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} concluded \texttt{partial}.  Together these rows keep the residual fourth component from being renamed as mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, or translation accuracy in the present finite evidence.  The dwell row remains a partial contact rather than a complete identity.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The $f_3$-stress conjecture has a stricter readout requirement than the core-direction run.  A positive biological readback must be a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA controls.  The present finite memory does not contain a same-organism and same-CDS joined matrix with codon counts, wobble weights, measured translation-readout rows, and matched mRNA abundance for this exact $f_3$-stress statistic.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is therefore a rank-degenerate boundary, not survival evidence.  In formula form, the bounded support condition remains
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls and simpler-baseline ablation; this inequality has not been supplied for the exact measured translation-readout packet in the current finite rows.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
The chapter allows the following finite conclusion and no stronger one.  The powered residual design supports a tRNA-adaptation core direction for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$, a non-tRNA residual axis aligned most with $f_3$ translation selection, a third axis provisionally aligned to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and sign-test $p=0.000488$, and a remaining fourth component not identified with mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, or translation accuracy in the listed runs.  It also records a passed $R$-sense tRNA-poverty test over $40$ computed organisms with $1000$ permutations.  It does not establish measured translation survival for the exact $f_3$-stress packet without a nondegenerate $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ row.  It does not establish ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, mechanism beyond the bounded supply-side contact, or universality.  Each of those stronger layers would require its own matched finite contact and its own controlled statistic.
