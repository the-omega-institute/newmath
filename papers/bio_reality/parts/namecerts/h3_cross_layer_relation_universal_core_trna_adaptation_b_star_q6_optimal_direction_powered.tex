\subsection{Universal core tRNA adaptation at the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-universal-core-trna-adaptation}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual coordinate obtained after synonymous codons are first compared inside amino-acid fibres and then projected onto the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress direction.  The carrier is not a whole-genome phenotype and not a protein-level object.  It is the finite CDS-by-organism residual surface in which the tested row is the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance after amino-acid projection, matched only to translation-layer readouts such as tAI, stAI, or measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency when those rows are available.  The saturation contact fixes the relevant finite codon support explicitly.  The reported set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}
\end{aligned}
$$
is the union recorded for $M=\mathrm{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$, while the $f_3$-saturated $R$ surface removes only $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\

$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the present row is not introduced by an unresolved boundary codon; it is a closed finite residual comparison inside the stated support.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-adaptation readback.}
The powered universal-core experiment for this coordinate passed with $11$ organisms computed out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  This is the finite row that licenses the local statement that the optimal $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ direction is compatible with a tRNA-adaptation interpretation at the bounded translation-readout layer.  The companion boundary-set test was broader on the tRNA-supply side: $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ were skipped, the permutation count was $1000$, and the reported call was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  Thus the chapter-specific evidence is not merely a codon-topology statement; it uses organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA-copy or wobble-weight asymmetry, and cross-validated residual design columns.

\paragraph{Relation to the nearby residual spectrum.}
The same finite packet places the tRNA-adaptation direction inside a larger residual spectrum rather than treating it as the only surviving axis.  The non-tRNA residual optimality run passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested and identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis.  The two-axis closure test, again with $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, reported a third axis rather than closure by only the tRNA and $f_3$ span.  The third-axis identity run provisionally aligned that perpendicular direction with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism run kept the axis after GC orthogonalization and classified it as selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate contact.  The axis-strength spectrum also passed on $11$ computed organisms; its descriptive summary said that captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, domain counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and largest GC3 rank effect $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers keep the present record from overstating a single universal scalar: the tRNA-adaptation direction is a real powered component of the finite surface, but the surrounding spectrum contains additional residual structure.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions and partial contacts.}
The three-axis sufficiency run reported that a fourth mechanism is present under the same $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column design.  The mechanism probes narrow what that fourth component is not.  The mRNA-stability test classified the fourth axis as not mRNA stability in this assay, because the yeast half-life direction had low alignment with the abundance residual $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  The codon-pair test returned \emph{not codon pair} with $50$ shuffle nulls.  The translation-accuracy test returned \emph{not accuracy} using $200$ permutations, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, and the same $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The elongation-dwell run was more limited, reporting a partial call in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These classifications matter for this chapter because they prevent the tRNA-adaptation row from being silently promoted into a complete mechanism for all residual variation.  The finite evidence supports a bounded translation-adaptation component and a tRNA-poor $R$-sense boundary mechanism; it does not collapse the residual spectrum into one cause.

\paragraph{Local statistical form.}
The chapter-level survival statistic is the controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The intended controlled comparison can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
&\text{the $f_3$-stress row improves held-out translation readout after controls}\\
&\text{for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length,}\\
&\text{$M$-density, matched mRNA where applicable, and simpler codon baselines.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The powered tRNA-adaptation experiment is therefore read as a positive finite-row contact for the modeled tRNA-adaptation layer, not as a blanket statement about all translation measurements.  A rank-degenerate surface, such as an all-$1.0$ three-organism controlled matrix, remains a null boundary: it is not counted as survival, because it has no powered directional discrimination.

\paragraph{Biological boundary.}
The admissible biological statement is narrow.  After amino-acid quotienting, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate has powered evidence for a tRNA-adaptation-aligned optimal direction in a multi-organism panel, with $11$ organisms computed, $5$-fold validation, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The evidence is strongest where context tRNA or wobble weights break third-position $f_3$ symmetry, and the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty result supplies a concrete supply-side asymmetry.  Nothing in this record by itself establishes measured ribosome occupancy, mature protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, a complete mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Each of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The present chapter records a finite, controlled translation-adaptation row and its local spectral context, while keeping downstream biological interpretations outside the claim unless later rows supply them.
