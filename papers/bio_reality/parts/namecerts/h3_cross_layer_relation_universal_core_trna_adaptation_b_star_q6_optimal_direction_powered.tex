\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress direction as a bounded tRNA-adaptation record}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-trna-adaptation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection.  The coordinate under test is the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress direction: a synonymous imbalance paired by the third-position involution $f_3$, evaluated only after amino-acid composition has been quotiented away.  Its biological reading is deliberately narrow.  The object is not a protein phenotype or a structural claim; it is a controlled row in which a residual codon coordinate is compared with translation-layer quantities such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.

The finite saturation calculation gives the coordinate its closed codon support.  The marked set $M=\operatorname{Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturation of $R$ removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this displayed support:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the row is finite, explicit, and not driven by an unenumerated codon family.

\paragraph{Rank-powered survival row.}
The powered cross-organism computation for the universal-core tRNA-adaptation claim passed with experiment run $373843e81379$.  It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  The deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  These numbers support the chapter-level claim that the $B^*_{Q6}$ direction has a finite, rank-powered association with the tRNA-adaptation side of the translation layer.  They do not by themselves assert a universal rule of translation dynamics.

The companion boundary-set computation gives the mechanistic direction its finite contact: $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped organisms, permutation count $1000$, and threshold $\alpha=0.05$.  Its recorded call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  This is the relevant asymmetry condition for the $f_3$-stress row: the coordinate becomes informative only where the context weights distinguish a codon $c$ from its $f_3$ partner, so that $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$.

\paragraph{What survives and what remains bounded.}
The intended statistic is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The row is positive only when the $f_3$-stress coordinate improves the translation readout after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where applicable, rank, and simpler codon baselines.  In schematic form the admissible readout has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\quad\text{after conditioning on}\quad
Z=\{\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-density},\mathrm{mRNA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix is therefore not a survival result.  It is a boundary case showing that the row has not yet separated signal from rank saturation.

Several adjacent powered records constrain the interpretation.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row reports that its dominant residual axis is $f_3$ translation selection, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The two-axis closure row reports a remaining third axis: $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row provisionally aligns $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism row is classified as selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing a layer-matched outside measurement.  These records prevent the $f_3$-stress row from being mistaken for a complete account of synonymous selection.

\paragraph{Axis spectrum and exclusions.}
The axis-strength spectrum was computed on $11$ organisms out of $16$, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its descriptive spectrum is led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  The domain means are explicitly descriptive because the panel is imbalanced: $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest GC3 rank effect is recorded for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.

The three-axis sufficiency computation calls a fourth mechanism present, so the tRNA-adaptation row is not a closure claim over all residual biological variation.  Four candidate fourth-mechanism tests sharpen this point.  The codon-pair test reports \emph{not codon pair} with $50$ shuffle nulls.  The elongation-dwell test in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} reports a partial result.  The mRNA-stability test reports that the fourth axis is not mRNA stability, because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ is low under the fixed gates.  The translation-accuracy test reports \emph{not accuracy}, with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ and $200$ permutations.  Hence the chapter isolates a bounded tRNA-adaptation direction, while leaving the additional residual axis open to its own layer-specific measurement.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The finite row permits a translation-layer statement only when the declared survival entry is non-vacuous, non-rank-degenerate, and positive under the stated controls.  It does not follow from the codon coordinate, from the $M$ support, from tRNA gene-copy counts, or from modeled tAI or stAI weights alone that ribosomes actually occupy the transcript in the predicted manner.  It also does not follow that protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, biochemical function, organismal phenotype, mechanism in the strong causal sense, or a universal biological law has been established.  Each of those stronger readings requires its own matched contact and its own statistic.  The present record is therefore best read as a finite, controlled tRNA-adaptation direction for $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress, with a strict refusal to promote it beyond the translation-readout row.
