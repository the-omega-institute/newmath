\subsection{Rank-powered tRNA adaptation boundary for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress coordinate}
\label{subsec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-trna-adaptation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object of this record is the finite residual coordinate surface obtained from coding sequences after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position $f_3$ stress coordinate and to rows for which a translation-layer readout can in principle be matched. The row object is not a protein-state measurement. It is a finite codon-residual table with organism labels, codon-usage summaries, tRNA or wobble-weight annotations, and candidate translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, or ribosome-derived translation efficiency when those rows are actually present.

The finite saturation packet fixes the relevant codon support. The set $M = \mathrm{Med}_R \cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Its $f_3$ saturation after the boundary removal is the $18$-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The omitted codons are $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, and the recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty. Thus the finite coordinate is already an after-quotient residual: the question is not whether the third-position involution can be named, but whether it remains visible after the amino-acid and baseline controls have removed easier composition effects.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The rank-powered tRNA-adaptation run for this claim has status $\mathrm{passed}$. It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. The deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered{:}deterministic}$. In finite-row terms, this says that the universal-core direction in the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface is supported by an organism panel large enough to leave the three-organism rank-saturation boundary, while still remaining a bounded assay rather than a global biological law.

The neighboring mechanism row strengthens the interpretation of the core direction as a tRNA-supply boundary. In the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty assay, $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ were skipped, and the permutation count was $1000$ at $\alpha = 0.05$. The recorded mechanism call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts combined with dos Reis wobble weights. This supplies a finite supply-side asymmetry for the codon region in which the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate is evaluated.

\paragraph{Directional statement.}
The chapter-level claim is the following bounded implication. After amino-acid projection, the $f_3$ stress coordinate may be read as a translation-layer distinction only in rows where the context tRNA or wobble-weight surface breaks the $f_3$ symmetry. In symbols, a row can contribute to the bounded readback only when a codon $c$ and its third-position partner $c^{f_3}$ have unequal translation weights:
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
When this inequality is absent, the coordinate remains a residual codon geometry and not a dynamical translation distinction. When it is present, the admissible readback is still only the controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a specified translation readout $T_j$, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The finite facts listed above justify searching for that entry in the tRNA and wobble-weight direction; they do not by themselves assert that a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency row has already survived those controls.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The related powered rows make the finite spectrum coherent. The non-tRNA residual optimality assay computed $11$ organisms out of $16$, used $5$ folds, required $500$ proteins per organism, used $41$ synonymous-design columns, and identified $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis. The two-axis closure assay on the tRNA and $f_3$ span also computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, but concluded that a third axis remains present. The third-axis identity assay reported alignment to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism assay retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC orthogonalization, recording the summary $\mathrm{selection\text{-}not\text{-}GC}$ and $\mathrm{not\text{-}wobble\text{-}clustered}$ while leaving the CSC or positional identity as needing an external row.

The axis-strength spectrum gives a further finite check on overinterpretation. It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its descriptive summary says that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The domain means were explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced counts $n = \{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho = 0.136$. The three-axis sufficiency row also computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ with the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design and concluded that a fourth mechanism is present. Thus the tRNA-adaptation direction is a finite core direction, not an exhaustive explanation of the residual surface.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
This record does not claim that the $f_3$ stress coordinate causes translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Codon usage, tRNA gene-copy counts, and tAI or stAI weights can bound a translation-adaptation surface, but modeled adaptation weights are not the same thing as measured ribosome occupancy. A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row becomes evidence only after the same organism and same CDS rows have been joined to codon counts, wobble weights, and matched mRNA abundance, and after the declared controlled statistic has been computed. Until such a row is non-vacuous and positive, the finite result is best read as a rank-powered tRNA-adaptation direction in the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface, with an explicit open gate to measured translation readout rather than a promoted downstream biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Falsification condition.}
The bounded conjecture fails at the translation-readout layer if every admissible row satisfies
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} \leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the required controls, or if the only apparent positive surface is rank-degenerate, such as an all-$1.0$ matrix on an underpowered three-organism panel. It also fails as a directional translation claim if the apparent association persists only where $w_c = w_{c^{f_3}}$, because then the row has not broken the third-position $f_3$ symmetry. Conversely, the finite evidence supports continued testing when a powered organism panel joins the residual coordinate, the tRNA or wobble asymmetry, and a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout, and the resulting controlled survival entry remains strictly positive after simpler codon baselines are ablated.
