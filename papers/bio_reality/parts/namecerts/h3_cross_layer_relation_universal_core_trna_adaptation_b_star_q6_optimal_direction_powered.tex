\subsection{Universal Core tRNA Adaptation Direction in the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ Synonymous Surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-universal-core-trna-adaptation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite claim surface.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, organism-level matching, and restriction to the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinate family.  The tested coordinate is the $f_3$-stress direction: a third-position synonymous imbalance that is invisible as an amino-acid substitution but can become visible to translation only when codon-context tRNA supply, wobble weights, or codon-adaptation weights distinguish a codon $c$ from its $f_3$ partner $c^{f_3}$.  The finite object is therefore not a claim about proteins in general.  It is a ranked row table in which each coding sequence contributes controlled synonymous coordinates, codon-use summaries, tRNA or wobble-weight readbacks, and, where available, translation-layer rows such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Saturation boundary.}
The upstream $f_3$ closure computation gives a concrete finite boundary for the coordinate.  The measured set $M=\mathrm{Med}\,R\cup\{ACA\}$ has $20$ codons:
$AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG$.
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ set removes $ACA$ and $ACG$, leaving $18$ codons:
$AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG$.
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the finite row surface has no leftover boundary codons outside the saturated description.  In symbols the finite closure condition used here is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|M|&=20,\\
|\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)|&=18,\\
\partial_{f_3}&=\varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a coordinate admissibility fact, not a biological conclusion by itself.

The bounded mathematical reading is that, inside this finite design matrix, the fitted universal core direction is compatible with the tRNA-adaptation coordinate rather than with an unconstrained synonymous axis. \paragraph{Mechanistic support kept at the tRNA layer.}
A separate boundary-set tRNA-poverty run gives the finite mechanistic pressure behind the same direction.  In $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ organisms skipped, a $1000$-permutation test at $\alpha=0.05$ classified the $R$ sense codons as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance at the codon-readout layer.  It does not show ribosome pausing, protein abundance, folding, function, phenotype, or evolutionary necessity.  The finite support is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{computed}}&=40,\\
N_{\mathrm{requested}}&=46,\\
N_{\mathrm{skipped}}&=6,\\
B_{\mathrm{perm}}&=1000,\\
\alpha&=0.05 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the tRNA-adaptation statement is stronger than a naming convention for codons but weaker than a direct physiological mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual-axis panel.}
The same finite panel places the tRNA direction inside a broader residual-coordinate spectrum.  The non-tRNA residual optimality run passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its dominant residual axis was $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure run also passed on the same $11/16$ organism panel and concluded that a third axis is present, because the perpendicular direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity run identified that perpendicular component with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  After GC orthogonalization, the third-axis mechanism run retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, yielding the qualitative classification ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external data.''  These rows prevent the tRNA-adaptation chapter from overstating sufficiency: tRNA adaptation is a core direction, while the full residual panel still contains additional axes.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The strongest permissible readback is a bounded translation-row statement.  A positive entry would require a nondegenerate controlled survival statistic $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0$ for a translation readout $T_j$, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The supplied row packet explicitly warns that the current exact $f_3$-stress translation-readout subject lacks same-organism and same-CDS joined codon, wobble-weight, measured translation-readout, and matched mRNA-control rows for the declared statistic.  Therefore the present chapter can report a powered tRNA-adaptation alignment and associated finite-axis diagnostics, but it cannot report measured translation survival for this exact readout.  The required future statistic has the form

Until such a row exists, modeled tAI or stAI weights remain bounded codon-adaptation contacts rather than observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The chapter supports the following narrow statement: in the finite powered $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous panel, the universal core direction is aligned with tRNA adaptation, while $f_3$-stress becomes interpretable as a translation-facing coordinate only where wobble or tRNA weights break the $f_3$ symmetry.  The chapter does not assert that the coordinate changes protein abundance, protein structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  None of those conclusions follows from codon topology, tRNA-copy summaries, tAI or stAI weights, review readiness, or a passed finite design-matrix run.  Each higher layer requires its own matched biological contact and its own controlled statistic, and the translation layer itself requires a non-vacuous joined readout row before promotion beyond bounded codon-adaptation readback.
