\subsection{Wobble-broken $f_3$ stress as a bounded translation-readout target}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-f3-wobble-broken-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The object considered here is the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate after synonymous comparison and amino-acid projection.  The finite row starts from the saturation computation in which the median residual union with $ACA$ contains exactly
$AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG$.
After $f_3$ saturation, the corresponding row is
$AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG$,
with the $f_3$ boundary list empty.  Thus the finite coordinate is not a free biological assertion: it is a residual codon-surface coordinate whose tested content is whether the $f_3$-paired imbalance remains visible in a translation-layer readout once the amino-acid quotient and baseline controls have been imposed.

\paragraph{Readout equation.}
For a coding sequence $x$ in organism $o$, let $q_{f_3}(x,o)$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate restricted to the $f_3$-stress direction, and let $T_j(x,o)$ be one bounded translation readout, such as an organism-scoped tAI or stAI weight, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The admissible entry is a controlled survival statistic, schematically
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j}
  &= \Delta \mathcal{L}\bigl(T_j \mid Z\bigr)
     - \Delta \mathcal{L}\bigl(T_j \mid Z,q_{f_3}\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{ organism},GC3,\hbox{ CDS length},\hbox{ M-density},\hbox{ matched mRNA controls}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The conjectural support condition is $S_{f_3,T_j}>0$ in a rank-powered panel after simpler codon baselines are removed.  The falsifying condition is equally finite: if all such entries are non-positive, or if the observed matrix is rank-degenerate, the claimed translation-readout distinction is absent for this coordinate.

\paragraph{Wobble-breaking condition.}
The coordinate becomes biologically testable only where the third-position pairing is not symmetry-neutral at the translation layer.  In local notation, the necessary asymmetry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $c^{f_3}$ is the third-position partner of codon $c$ and $w_c$ is the context tRNA or wobble-weight assigned to $c$.  Without this broken equality, the $f_3$ residual may remain a valid coordinate but has no finite reason to produce a directed translation-readout distinction.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is therefore treated as a rank-saturated null boundary rather than as positive survival.

\paragraph{Positive finite rows already available.}
The powered core tRNA-adaptation row passed with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ organisms computed out of $16$ requested, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its deterministic seed was the row labelled by the universal-core tRNA-adaptation experiment.  A separate $R$-sense tRNA-poverty mechanism row also passed: $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ were skipped, the test used $1000$ permutations, and the declared level was $\alpha=0.05$.  These rows support the local premise that tRNA supply and wobble-weight asymmetry are legitimate bounded readout surfaces for $B^*_{Q6}$, but they do not by themselves supply a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency survival matrix for the $f_3$ coordinate.

\paragraph{Axis context.}
The broader powered panel supplies finite orientation data around this coordinate.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row identifies the dominant residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection, again with $5$ folds, $11$ organisms computed out of $16$, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The two-axis closure row reports a third axis, with the stated reason that the perpendicular direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The identity row provisionally aligns this perpendicular direction with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism row keeps that direction GC-orthogonal and reports the finite classification ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external data.''

\paragraph{Fourth-axis guardrail.}
The three-axis sufficiency row concludes that a fourth mechanism is present, while the per-organism spectrum row reports $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested and says that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count is $11$ for $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, and the largest reported GC3 rank effect is $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  Candidate fourth-mechanism checks do not license promotion beyond translation readout: mRNA stability is rejected as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life assay, codon-pair identity is rejected, translation accuracy is rejected, and elongation dwell receives only a partial call in yeast.  The local consequence is that the $f_3$-stress conjecture must stay attached to its explicit translation row rather than being reinterpreted as a protein, structure, localization, stability, function, or phenotype result.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter claims only a finite test target: after amino-acid quotienting, $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress is expected to survive in a bounded translation readout only in organisms or contexts where tRNA or wobble weights distinguish $f_3$ partners.  No translation realization follows from codon topology alone.  No ribosome occupancy follows from modelled tAI or stAI weights alone.  No protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation law, or universal biological law follows from this coordinate unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the controlled statistic is non-vacuous and positive.
