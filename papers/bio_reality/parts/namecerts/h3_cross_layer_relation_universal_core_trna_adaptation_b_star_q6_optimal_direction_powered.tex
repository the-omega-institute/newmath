\subsection{The wobble-broken $f_3$ stress direction as a bounded translation-readout conjecture}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid quotienting, restricted to the third-position $f_3$ stress direction and compared only against matched translation-layer rows. The finite codon support already fixes the relevant boundary. In the saturation run $76d3dc408a57$, the ambient set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding $f_3$ saturation of $R$ removes the two ACA/ACG boundary points and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with an empty recorded $f_3$ boundary. Thus the proposed signal is not a free biological assertion. It is a finite residual contrast on synonymous codon imbalance after amino-acid projection, and it becomes biologically testable only when an organism or context supplies asymmetric tRNA, wobble, tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency rows for the same coding sequences.

\paragraph{Powered finite-row status.}
The direct powered run for this claim, $373843e81379$, passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The run records the experiment identifier \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered} with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered:deterministic}. This is evidence for the finite coordinate being organized in the tRNA-adaptation direction within the available powered panel. It is not, by itself, an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement and not a protein-output claim.

\paragraph{Supporting spectrum.}
Several neighboring finite-row checks constrain the interpretation. The R-sense boundary set was tested in $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$; that run passed and classified the R sense codons as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights. The non-tRNA residual optimality run passed on $11/16$ organisms and identified \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection} as the dominant axis. The two-axis closure run also passed on $11/16$ organisms and reported a positive third-axis requirement rather than closure by the tRNA and $f_3$ span alone. The third-axis identity run used the same $5$-fold, $11/16$-organism, $41$-column design and aligned the perpendicular direction to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. Its mechanism companion retained cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC orthogonalization, giving the local classification ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external''. These rows say that the $f_3$ stress direction is embedded in a larger residual spectrum; they do not collapse the spectrum to GC3, codon-pair structure, or a single universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions and residual caution.}
The sufficiency check for the three proposed axes passed but concluded that a fourth mechanism is present. The fourth-mechanism probes narrow the boundary without closing it: the mRNA-stability run concluded \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}; the codon-pair run concluded \texttt{not\_codon\_pair}; the translation-accuracy run concluded \texttt{not\_accuracy} after $200$ permutations on $11/16$ organisms; and the elongation-dwell run in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} concluded \texttt{partial}. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ organisms, with all recorded per-organism dominant counts assigned to \texttt{d\_resid4}; domain means were explicitly descriptive because the panel was imbalanced with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for \texttt{d\_usage}. These facts support a finite residual landscape in which tRNA adaptation and $f_3$ translation selection are meaningful coordinates, while the remaining variation still requires layer-matched observation.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The only allowed readback for this chapter is a bounded survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after controls for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, and matched mRNA abundance where a TE denominator is used. A positive modeled tAI or stAI association may define a bounded adaptation row, but measured translation survival requires same-organism and same-CDS rows joining codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, a direct translation readout, and the declared controls. The conjecture is therefore falsified at this layer if the controlled entries remain non-positive, or if the design is rank-degenerate, such as an all-$1.0$ three-organism fitted surface.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion follows from the coordinate, from the passed powered status, or from the modeled wobble-weight rows. In particular, this chapter does not infer ribosome dynamics unless a direct ribosome or TE contact supplies joined rows; it does not infer protein abundance unless a separate matched protein row is evaluated after the translation step; and it does not infer folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality from codon geometry, residual notation, tAI/stAI weights, workflow readiness, or identifier routing. Each such layer needs its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic. The present record is consequently a finite, panel-tested direction for where a translation-readout survival entry should be sought, not a promotion of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate into downstream biological realization.
