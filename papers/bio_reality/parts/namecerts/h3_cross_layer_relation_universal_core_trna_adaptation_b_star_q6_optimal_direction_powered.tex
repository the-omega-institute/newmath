\subsection{Universal core tRNA adaptation along the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ optimal direction}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-universal-core-trna-adaptation}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record isolates a finite cross-layer claim about the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface after amino-acid projection.  The carrier is the CDS-by-organism residual coordinate table restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to rows for which codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and a bounded translation-readout route are declared separately.  The specific finite assertion certified here is not that every downstream biological layer has been reached.  It is that, in the powered multi-organism panel, the optimal direction associated with the universal core is supported as a tRNA-adaptation direction rather than as an unconstrained codon-topology artifact.

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The row for this claim is the experiment
$\texttt{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered}$, run $\texttt{373843e81379}$.  It passed with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  The deterministic seed recorded for the row is
$\texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered:deterministic}$.
These numbers are part of the claim: the chapter does not assert a theorem about all organisms, all coding sequences, or all translation assays.  It records a powered finite panel whose admissible surface is large enough to avoid the three-organism rank-saturation boundary and whose readout remains bounded to codon usage and tRNA-adaptation coordinates unless a further measurement row is attached.

\paragraph{Why the direction is tRNA-adaptation-shaped.}
The same evidence packet contains a boundary-set tRNA-poverty row, run $\texttt{d7728cc8768e}$, where $40$ organisms were computed out of $46$ requested organisms, $6$ organisms were skipped, $1000$ permutations were used, and the row passed at $\alpha=0.05$.  Its finite conclusion is that the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  This is a supply-side codon-readout statement: it identifies an asymmetry in the tRNA or wobble-weight layer, not a protein-level phenotype.

A second row, $\texttt{2a526c9fb7f4}$, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It passed and identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $\texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}$.  A third row, $\texttt{99dc7e2d5abf}$, with the same $11/16$ organism count, $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins, and $41$ design columns, concluded that a third axis remains present: the two-axis span formed by tRNA and $f_3$ does not exhaust the residual surface.  The present claim therefore reads the universal core as tRNA-adaptation-shaped, but it does not collapse the whole residual geometry to a two-axis model.

\paragraph{Residual spectrum around the core.}
The surrounding finite rows constrain the interpretation.  The third-axis identity row $\texttt{377671f96e96}$ passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with $\texttt{usage\_frequency\_within\_family}$, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row $\texttt{d0893337f0dd}$ also passed on the same $11/16$ organism panel and reported the classification
$\texttt{selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external}$.
These two rows are important because they prevent the tRNA-adaptation claim from being overstated.  The core direction is compatible with tRNA adaptation, but additional residual structure remains, and part of that structure is explicitly not resolved as a complete positional or context-specific identity by the finite rows supplied here.

\paragraph{Optimality and axis sufficiency.}
The three-axis sufficiency row $\texttt{2436cae9d455}$ passed with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its conclusion was $\texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$, meaning that the existing axes do not close every residual signal in this powered panel.  The axis-strength spectrum row $\texttt{0146012d8909}$ computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms and reported that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant count was $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$.  Its domain means were marked descriptive under the imbalanced sample sizes $\{\texttt{archaea}:1,\texttt{bacteria}:2,\texttt{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.

These rows make the certificate asymmetric.  They support a tRNA-adaptation interpretation of the universal core optimal direction, while simultaneously recording that the full residual spectrum is not exhausted by that direction.  In particular, the $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominance count of $11$ out of $11$ computed organisms is not evidence against the core tRNA-adaptation row; it says that the strongest captured residual component in that spectrum is a further component that must not be silently renamed as tRNA adaptation.

\paragraph{Local readout rule.}
The only permitted readback for this chapter is bounded: codon usage, tRNA copy or wobble-weight asymmetry, modeled tAI or stAI weight rows, and explicitly matched translation-readout rows when such rows are actually present.  The phrase ``translation readout'' is therefore conditional.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight can be used as a codon-adaptation readout, but it is not an observed ribosome-occupancy experiment.  A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row would have to be joined at the same organism or CDS level and then evaluated after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when used, and simpler codon baselines.

\paragraph{Falsification boundary.}
The conjectural survival form attached to this row can fail in a concrete way.  If every controlled entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ is non-positive after the declared controls, or if the apparent matrix is rank-degenerate in the sense of an all-$1.0$ surface on too small a panel, then the coordinate has not survived as a translation-readout distinction.  In symbols, the positive case requires a non-vacuous row of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the stated controls and baseline ablations.  The present finite row certifies the powered tRNA-adaptation direction and its surrounding residual context; it does not by itself certify such an observed ribosome or TE survival entry.
