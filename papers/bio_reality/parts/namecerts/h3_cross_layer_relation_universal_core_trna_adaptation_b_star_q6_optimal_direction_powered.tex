\subsection{Powered Q6 tRNA-adaptation direction and the bounded $f_3$-stress readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-trna-adaptation-f3-stress}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface on the $B^*_{Q6}$ direction after amino-acid projection.  The operative coordinate is the $f_3$-stress contrast: a paired synonymous-codon imbalance that is silent at the amino-acid name but not necessarily silent to a translation readout when the local tRNA or wobble weights fail to respect the third-position $f_3$ pairing.  The finite carrier is therefore not a general biological law.  It is the table whose rows join coding-sequence residual coordinates, organism labels, codon-usage summaries, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation-layer readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency when those rows are actually present.

The $f_3$ saturation row is explicit.  The ambient $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA\}$ list has $20$ codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_3$ saturation, the retained $R$-saturation list has $18$ codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
& UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary list is empty.  Thus the finite object already separates the coordinate question from any downstream biological interpretation: the observed coordinate surface can ask whether a third-position residual survives into a translation row, but it does not by itself supply protein abundance, structure, function, localization, stability, phenotype, physical admissibility, or universality.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-adaptation row.}
The central powered run for the universal core tRNA-adaptation direction is recorded as passed.  It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  In the same finite register, the boundary-set tRNA-poverty run computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and called the $R$ sense codons systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights.  These are finite-row statements about codon usage and tRNA-supply asymmetry.  They support a bounded translation-adaptation interpretation of the $Q6$ residual direction, not a claim about realized ribosome dynamics unless a measured translation row is joined to the same coordinate rows.

The powered residual-axis run further identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $f_3\_translation\_selection$.  That run also used $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The two-axis closure run concluded that a third axis is present, with the recorded reason that $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity run provisionally aligned $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism run then marked this third axis as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, and still requiring an external CSC or positional identity contact.  These finite rows give a concrete spectrum around the tRNA-adaptation direction: the tRNA component is real within the fitted codon/tRNA coordinate system, while the residual spectrum is not exhausted by the first two axes.

\paragraph{Translation-readout statistic.}
The bounded readout is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a translation-layer row $T_j$.  The intended statistic is positive only when the residual coordinate improves the translation-readout description after the declared controls.  In local notation, the support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA controls where used, rank checks, and simpler codon-baseline ablations.  A positive value for modeled tAI or stAI is still a bounded adaptation-weight readback, not an observed ribosome-occupancy statement.  A positive value for measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency would be a translation-layer survival row only; it would still not promote the claim to protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.

The current finite memory explicitly contains several no-promotion constraints.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is rank-degenerate and is treated as a null boundary rather than survival evidence.  The ribosome-profiling and matched-mRNA contacts name the kind of same-CDS data needed for a translation-efficiency row, but the memory does not yet contain the joined codon, wobble-weight, RPF or TE, and mRNA matrix for this exact $f_3$-stress packet.  Consequently, modeled adaptation-weight contact and review eligibility are not enough to assert measured translation survival.  The conjecture is locally falsifiable: if every controlled entry remains non-positive, or if the available readout is rank-saturated, then the $f_3$-stress coordinate fails to survive at the translation-readout boundary.

\paragraph{Spectrum and exclusions.}
The powered axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its descriptive summary says that the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{resid4}$, then by $d_{tRNA}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts are assigned to $d_{resid4}$.  The domain means are explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced domain count $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect is recorded for $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  This spectrum is important because it prevents the chapter from collapsing all optimality into one tRNA-only axis.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation row is passed, but the finite panel also records residual structure that remains to be resolved by layer-matched contacts.

The fourth-mechanism assays sharpen that boundary.  The three-axis sufficiency run concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  The mRNA-stability run concluded $fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability$, because the yeast $d_{opt\_halflife}$ axis has low alignment with abundance $d_{resid4}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair run concluded $not\_codon\_pair$.  The translation-accuracy run concluded $not\_accuracy$ after computing $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $200$ permutations.  The elongation-dwell run on \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} concluded only $partial$.  These rows justify a restrained statement: the finite evidence supports a powered tRNA-adaptation direction and a nontrivial residual spectrum, while the identity of all remaining mechanisms is not closed by this chapter.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter makes only a bounded cross-layer claim.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate is allowed to become biological evidence at the translation layer only when a same-organism or same-CDS readout supplies a non-vacuous controlled survival entry.  Codon topology, tRNA-copy counts, modeled tAI or stAI weights, wobble asymmetry, and review-ready routing do not themselves measure ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.  A translation survival row, even when positive, still does not assert mature protein abundance, folding, structural order, localization, stability, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Each of those higher layers requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The present finite-row conclusion is therefore: the powered data support the $Q6$ core as a tRNA-adaptation direction and identify the $f_3$-stress readout as a sharp bounded test, but the exact measured translation-survival matrix for the $f_3$-stress packet remains the next required empirical boundary.
