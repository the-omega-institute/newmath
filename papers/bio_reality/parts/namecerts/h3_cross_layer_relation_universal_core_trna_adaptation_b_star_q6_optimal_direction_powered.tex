\subsection{Universal Core tRNA Adaptation in the $B^*_{Q6}$ Direction}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-universal-core-trna-adaptation}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence residual surface, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family and read only through organism-matched codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation-layer rows.  The coordinate is not a raw codon-count axis.  It is the part of synonymous codon imbalance that remains after amino-acid projection, organism-level background, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched transcript or translation controls have been separated.  The tested powered packet is the claim that the universal optimal direction in this residual surface is carried by a core tRNA-adaptation component.

\paragraph{Closed codon support.}
The finite saturation contact fixes the relevant codon support before any biological interpretation is allowed.  The set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After the third-position $f_3$ saturation step, the retained support is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.

$$
The $f_3$ boundary residue is empty in this finite run.  Thus the chapter is not using an open-ended codon vocabulary: it is using a closed twenty-codon ambient list, an eighteen-codon saturated list, and an empty recorded boundary.

\paragraph{Powered core result.}
The powered universal-core assay reported status \texttt{passed} for \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered}.  It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The deterministic seed was \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered:deterministic}.  These numbers are the finite row boundary of the claim: the result is a powered multi-organism coordinate readback over those rows, not a statement about all organisms or all possible translation systems.

\paragraph{Relation to tRNA poverty.}
A separate finite mechanism-facing packet gives the strongest local reason to treat the core direction as tRNA adaptation rather than only codon topology.  In the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty assay, $40$ organisms were computed from $46$ requested organisms, $6$ were skipped, and $1000$ permutations were used at $\alpha=0.05$.  The recorded mechanism call was that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts combined with dos Reis wobble weights.  This does not by itself prove ribosome occupancy or protein output, but it supplies a finite asymmetry in tRNA supply at the same kind of codon layer that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate probes.

\paragraph{Residual-axis neighborhood.}
The surrounding powered records keep the core claim from being read as a one-axis explanation of all codon behavior.  The non-tRNA residual optimality assay computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns; its dominant residual axis was \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}.  The two-axis closure assay, with the same $11/16$ organism count, $5$ folds, $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous columns, concluded that a third axis is present because the orthogonal residual direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The third-axis identity assay then provisionally aligned that orthogonal direction with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Finally, the axis-strength spectrum assay again computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms over $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, with all $11$ per-organism dominant counts assigned to \texttt{d\_resid4}; its largest GC3 rank effect was reported for \texttt{d\_usage} with $\rho=0.136$.  These companion rows support a layered reading: tRNA adaptation is the universal core direction in the powered packet, while additional residual axes remain real finite structure rather than noise to be erased.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For the $f_3$-stress version of the coordinate, the relevant biological break is not merely whether a codon has a modeled tAI or stAI weight.  A translation-row claim requires a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The admissible support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA control where used, rank checks, and simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  If every controlled entry remains non-positive, or if the surface is rank-degenerate, the translation-readout claim fails even when the internal codon coordinate and the tRNA-weight asymmetry are well formed.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result says that, on the powered multi-organism panel actually computed, the optimal universal $B^*_{Q6}$ direction is best read as a tRNA-adaptation direction.  The codon support is closed, the third-position boundary was empty in the saturation run, and the powered organism panel was large enough to avoid the three-organism all-$1.0$ rank-degenerate boundary that would otherwise be a null surface.  The result therefore gives a disciplined positive reading at the codon-usage and tRNA-adaptation layer: a core synonymous residual direction survives as a tRNA-weighted adaptation signal across the computed panel.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
Nothing in this chapter says that the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate has already been observed as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight is a bounded adaptation readout, not an observed ribosome-footprint row.  A tRNA-copy or wobble-weight asymmetry is a finite supply-side contact, not a protein-output measurement.  A positive powered core direction may justify the statement that the residual optimal direction is tRNA-adaptive on the computed panel; it may not be promoted to translation dynamics, folding, physical realization, biological function, mechanism, or universality unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and a controlled statistic for that layer.
