\subsection{Universal core tRNA adaptation along the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimal direction}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-universal-core-trna-adaptation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite subject.} This record concerns a finite coding-sequence residual surface, indexed by organism rows and synonymous-codon design coordinates, after amino-acid projection.  The coordinate under examination is the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimal direction, with special attention to the $f_3$-stress component: a third-position synonymous imbalance that is invisible to amino-acid composition but can become biologically readable when tRNA supply or wobble weights distinguish a codon from its $f_3$ partner.  The admissible readouts are translation-layer rows only: organism-level tAI or stAI weights, matched tRNA or wobble weights, and, where available, ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency measurements with matched mRNA control.  No protein, localization, stability, folded-structure, function, phenotype, physical-admissibility, mechanism, or universal-law conclusion is part of this row unless a separate contact at that layer is joined to the same coordinate and survives its own controls.

\paragraph{Saturation boundary.} The $f_3$ saturation computation fixes the finite codon support.  The row records
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, while $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ appear in $M$ but not in the saturated $R$ support.  The chapter therefore treats the $f_3$ stress row as a residual-coordinate question rather than as an amino-acid identity claim.  A nonzero effect must come from a broken contextual symmetry in translation weights or tRNA supply; it cannot be inferred merely from the codon list.

\paragraph{Powered row.} The direct finite computation for the universal core tRNA-adaptation claim passed under experiment id $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered}$.  It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered:deterministic}$.  In this chapter, this is read as a finite-row positive result for the bounded claim that the dominant $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimal direction is organized around tRNA-adaptation structure in the available powered panel.  It is not read as measured ribosome occupancy and not as a protein-output result.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite rows.} Several adjacent rows constrain the same interpretation.  A boundary-set tRNA-poverty test computed $40$ organisms from $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy number with dos Reis wobble weights.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row computed $11$ of $16$ organisms and reported the dominant residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure row, also on $11$ of $16$ organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous columns, concluded that a third axis remains present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The third-axis identity row identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Its mechanism row kept the axis GC-orthogonal and summarized the result as selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring an additional contact.

\paragraph{Axis spectrum.} The per-organism spectrum row computed $11$ organisms from $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and the same $500$-protein minimum.  Across the captured spectra, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the dominant-count table was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Domain means were explicitly descriptive because the panel was imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest GC3 rank effect was recorded on $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  This spectrum supports the local interpretation that the tRNA-adaptation direction is a core finite row in the powered panel, while residual directions remain real enough that a one-axis story would be too coarse.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.} The three-axis sufficiency row concluded that a fourth mechanism is present, again on $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and the $500$-protein minimum.  The available fourth-mechanism screens narrow, but do not fully name, that extra direction.  In yeast, the elongation-dwell screen returned $\mathrm{partial}$.  The mRNA-stability screen concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$ because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ was low under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair screen concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using $50$ shuffle nulls.  The translation-accuracy screen concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$, using $200$ permutations on $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested organisms.  These rows prevent the chapter from collapsing every remaining residual direction into tRNA adaptation, codon-pair structure, mRNA half-life, or accuracy.

\paragraph{Controlled readout form.} Let $Q$ denote the finite $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and let $T$ denote bounded translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The admissible survival entry has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &= \Delta \operatorname{Score}\bigl(T_j \mid f_3\text{-stress}, Z\bigr),\\
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},\mathrm{GC3},\text{CDS length},M\text{-density},\text{matched mRNA control}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive row requires $S_{f_3,T_j}>0$ after these controls and after ablation against simpler codon baselines.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on a three-organism panel is a refusal boundary rather than survival evidence.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used as bounded codon-adaptation readouts, but they are not direct ribosome-footprint observations.

\paragraph{Claim status.} The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement: within the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate panel, the optimal direction has a reproducible core aligned with tRNA adaptation, and the $f_3$ residual component becomes a candidate translation-layer distinction only where tRNA or wobble weights break third-position symmetry.  The statement is deliberately narrower than a biological mechanism.  If future same-organism and same-CDS rows join codon counts, wobble-breaking weights, measured translation readout, and matched mRNA abundance, then the decisive test is whether $S_{f_3,T_j}$ remains positive under the stated covariates.  If all such entries are non-positive, or if the design is rank-saturated, the translation-readout claim fails for that panel.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.} This chapter does not assert that $f_3$ stress changes protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, or a general biological law.  It also does not assert measured ribosome occupancy from tAI or stAI weights alone.  The only positive finite-row content recorded here is the powered tRNA-adaptation alignment of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimal direction, the codon-support saturation facts, and the adjacent finite screens that constrain residual-axis interpretation.  Any stronger downstream assertion must be supplied by a layer-matched contact with its own controlled statistic.
