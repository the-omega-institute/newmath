\subsection{Rank-powered tRNA adaptation and the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-core-trna-adaptation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface in which each row is a CDS-by-organism residual coordinate paired with translation-layer annotations.  The distinguished coordinate is the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual after amino-acid projection: it compares synonymous codons across the third-position involution while removing amino-acid identity as the primary explanatory axis.  The relevant biological question is not whether a codon-topological coordinate can be named internally, but whether the coordinate remains visible in a bounded translation readout after the ordinary sequence and organism covariates have been accounted for.

\paragraph{Closed codon support used by the coordinate.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation check fixes the codon support on which this residual is allowed to speak.  The verified run $76\mathrm{d}3\mathrm{dc}408\mathrm{a}57$ reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite boundary is empty for the checked $f_3$ closure, while the two codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ appear in $M$ but not in $\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$.  This matters because the chapter is not free to promote a sparse or boundary-only artifact: the residual coordinate is evaluated on the finite closed list above.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-adaptation row.}
The direct powered row for this claim is run $373843\mathrm{e}81379$, experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered}$.  It passed with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  The deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The finite read of this row is therefore: within the computed rank-powered panel, the leading universal optimal direction is compatible with a tRNA-adaptation interpretation at the translation-readout layer.  The statement is deliberately finite: it is a passed controlled panel result on the available organisms, not a biological law over all genomes.

\paragraph{Position of the $f_3$ component inside the powered spectrum.}
The neighboring powered rows constrain how far the tRNA-adaptation interpretation can be pushed.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row $2\mathrm{a}526\mathrm{c}9\mathrm{fb}7\mathrm{f}4$ passed on the same $11/16$ organism scale with $5$ folds, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, and identified $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual axis.  The two-axis closure row $99\mathrm{dc}7\mathrm{e}2\mathrm{d}5\mathrm{abf}$ concluded that a third axis is present, because the orthogonal direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row $377671\mathrm{f}96\mathrm{e}96$ then aligned this orthogonal component to usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  Finally, the third-axis mechanism row $\mathrm{d}0893337\mathrm{f}0\mathrm{dd}$ kept the signal after GC orthogonalization and reported the finite conclusion ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external.''  Together these rows place $f_3$ stress in a controlled residual spectrum rather than in a single hand-picked codon contrast.

\paragraph{Mechanistic boundary from tRNA-poor $R$ codons.}
A separate finite mechanism row, $\mathrm{d}7728\mathrm{cc}8768\mathrm{e}$, tested the $R$ sense-codon boundary with GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  It passed at $\alpha=0.05$ using $1000$ permutations, with $40$ organisms computed, $46$ requested, and $6$ skipped.  Its recorded call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under the stated tGCN-plus-wobble readout, supporting a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance.  This supplies a finite mechanistic pressure on the same broad coordinate family, but it still remains a bounded tRNA-supply contact rather than direct ribosome-footprint evidence.

\paragraph{Translation-readout statistic and break condition.}
For this chapter the bounded statistic is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be a tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency readout.  The statistic is only admissible after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance where a TE denominator is used.  The intended finite comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0
\quad \text{only if the residual coordinate improves the controlled translation readout,}\\
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &\leq 0
\quad \text{or rank saturation holds, in which case the translation claim is blocked.}
\end{aligned}
$$
In particular, an all-$1.0$ matrix on only three organisms is treated as rank-degenerate and does not count as survival.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights can bound a codon-adaptation readout, but they are not the same object as observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Scope of the present claim.}
The ready claim is therefore narrow.  The powered finite panel supports the statement that the universal $B^*_{Q6}$ optimal direction has a core tRNA-adaptation component, and the surrounding residual spectrum makes the $f_3$ translation-selection axis a serious bounded readout candidate.  The evidence is strongest at the codon-usage, tRNA-copy, wobble-weight, and modeled translation-adaptation level.  It does not by itself establish mature protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, or universality.  Those conclusions require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  If an organism- and CDS-matched ribosome-profiling or TE panel later gives non-positive $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ under the stated controls, or if the only apparent signal is rank-degenerate, this chapter's translation-readout promotion fails while the finite codon-coordinate and tRNA-adaptation rows remain as bounded lower-layer facts.
