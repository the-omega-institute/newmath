\subsection{Rank-powered tRNA adaptation and the f\_3-stress boundary}\label{subsec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-trna-adaptation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The BioReality object in this record is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid quotienting, restricted to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation-layer rows that are allowed to be read only as tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency measurements.  The coordinate is not a protein-level or phenotype-level object.  Its admissible readback is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation readout $T_j$, with controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where the readout is TE, and simpler codon baselines.

The closed codon row used by the saturation side is concrete.  The set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA\}$ is
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The third-position saturation removes exactly the two $ACA/ACG$ entries from that twenty-codon row:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
& UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the finite combinatorial boundary is zero in this row: no additional boundary codon remains after the displayed saturation operation.

\paragraph{Powered adaptation row.}
The main powered experiment for this chapter is $b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered$.  It passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, with deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  This is evidence for a finite, rank-powered alignment between the universal core $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ direction and tRNA-adaptation structure, not evidence that the coordinate has already survived a measured ribosome or TE row.

The mechanism-side row is also finite.  In the R-window assay, R sense codons were called systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights.  That row passed for $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, at $\alpha=0.05$ and $1000$ permutations.  Thus the chapter records a concrete tRNA-supply pressure on the same family of codon distinctions.  The residual optimality row further identifies the non-tRNA residual optimality direction as $f_3\_translation\_selection$, computed on $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.

\paragraph{Axis structure.}
The two-axis closure assay did not close the surface: it passed with conclusion $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$ on $11/16$ organisms, using the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design.  Its stated reason is that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and that the $d_{\perp}$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  The identity assay provisionally identifies this third axis with usage frequency within synonymous family, reporting mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ on $11/16$ organisms.  The mechanism assay then reports $\mathrm{selection\text{-}not\text{-}GC}$ and $\mathrm{not\text{-}wobble\text{-}clustered}$, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as requiring another contact; its reason is that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.

The three-axis sufficiency assay also did not terminate the structure: it passed with conclusion $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on $11/16$ organisms, again with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  In the captured spectrum, $d_{resid4}$ led on average, followed by $d_{tRNA}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{resid4}$, and the largest reported GC3 rank effect was $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  The fourth-mechanism screens narrow, but do not fully identify, that further axis: mRNA stability is excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the yeast half-life assay, codon-pair structure is reported as $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$, translation accuracy is reported as $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ after $200$ permutations on $11/16$ organisms, and elongation dwell is reported as $\mathrm{partial}$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes a dynamical translation claim only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position $f_3$ symmetry.  Formally, the admissible positive row has the shape
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0,\\
T_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\},\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA\ control}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The present finite rows support the tRNA-adaptation and residual-axis geometry needed to pose that test.  They do not yet contain a same-organism and same-CDS joined matrix of codon counts, wobble weights, measured ribosome or TE rows, and matched mRNA controls for this exact $f_3$-stress packet.  Therefore an all-positive or rank-degenerate modeled surface cannot be read as survival.  A positive modeled tAI or stAI association is only a bounded translation-weight readback unless it is paired with the declared controlled survival statistic.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter makes no claim that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress changes protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  None of those layers follows from codon topology, residual coordinates, tRNA-copy summaries, tAI or stAI weights, or the powered adaptation rows alone.  Such a claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  The local conclusion is narrower: the powered rows justify treating the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ universal core as tRNA-adaptation-aligned, with an $f_3$-sensitive residual direction and a nonclosed higher-axis spectrum, while the translation-realization claim remains conditional on a nondegenerate controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry.
