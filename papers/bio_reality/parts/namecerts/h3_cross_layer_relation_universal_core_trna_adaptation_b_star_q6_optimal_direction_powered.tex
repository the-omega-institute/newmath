\subsection{Universal core tRNA adaptation for the $B^*_{Q6}$ optimal direction}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-universal-core-trna-adaptation}

\paragraph{Finite row under test.}
This record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate in $B^*_{Q6}$ and read only through matched translation-layer rows. The biological object is not a free-standing law about codons. It is a controlled finite panel in which synonymous codon imbalance, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, tRNA or wobble weights, and translation readouts are kept as separate coordinates. The principal powered run for the universal-core tRNA-adaptation claim reports status passed with experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered}, run id \texttt{373843e81379}, five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ organisms computed out of $16$ requested, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The deterministic seed is recorded as \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered:deterministic}. These numbers make the row finite and reproducible, but by themselves they do not license claims outside the translation-readout layer.

\paragraph{Codon-coordinate boundary.}
The underlying $f_3$ saturation row fixes the coordinate support. The finite set $M={\rm Med}_R\cup\{ACA\}$ is recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ set after removing $ACA$ and $ACG$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
{\rm Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. Thus the finite topology says that the stress coordinate has no remaining unsaturated boundary in this enumeration; it does not say that translation dynamics, protein abundance, structure, or phenotype has been observed. The coordinate becomes biologically testable only when a tRNA, wobble, tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency row is joined to the same organism or CDS surface under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-adaptation evidence.}
The main passed row is supported by a separate powered tRNA-poverty mechanism check on the $R$ sense codons. That check used experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered}, run id \texttt{d7728cc8768e}, $40$ organisms computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, permutation count $1000$, and $\alpha=0.05$. Its recorded call is that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance. In the present row this is used only as bounded adaptation evidence: the contact is a codon-to-tRNA supply relation, not an observation of ribosome motion, folded protein state, molecular function, or organismal fitness.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual-axis spectrum.}
The surrounding finite-row spectrum places the tRNA-adaptation direction inside a larger residual basis rather than treating it as the sole axis. The universal non-tRNA residual optimality run computed $11$ of $16$ organisms with five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, and classified the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}. The two-axis closure run, on the same $11/16$ organism scale and the same $41$ design-column surface, concluded \texttt{third\_axis\_present}: the residual orthogonal direction had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms. The third-axis identity run identified $d_\perp$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, reporting mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. A mechanism run then classified the third axis as selection-not-GC and not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity requiring further external evidence. These rows prevent over-reading the tRNA core as a complete explanation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ surface.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions and partial contacts.}
The broader powered panel also records why the chapter remains bounded. The three-axis sufficiency row concluded \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, five folds, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The codon-pair mechanism row returned \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} with $50$ shuffle-null samples. The mRNA-stability row returned \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, because yeast $d_{\rm opt\_halflife}$ had low alignment with abundance $d_{\rm resid4}$ under the fixed gates. The translation-accuracy row returned \texttt{not\_accuracy} with $200$ permutations. The elongation-dwell row, restricted to \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, returned \texttt{partial}. The per-organism strength-spectrum row computed $11$ organisms, reported dominant counts $\{\texttt{d\_resid4}:11\}$, and recorded the largest GC3 rank effect as $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\rm usage}$. These classifications make the local claim sharper: the universal core is tRNA-adaptation-like, but a residual fourth component remains, and the available finite rows do not identify it as codon pair, mRNA stability, or translation accuracy.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
Let $q$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and let $T$ be a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The only admissible survival statement for this chapter has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{q,T}>0
\quad\text{after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, }M\text{-density, and mRNA where used.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The passed tRNA-adaptation run supplies finite support for the optimal direction being tRNA-adaptation-like on the powered organism panel. It does not by itself provide a same-CDS ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency matrix for the exact $f_3$-stress survival row. A modeled tAI or stAI contact may be used as a bounded readback of codon-adaptation weights, while ribosome-profiling and matched mRNA abundance would be needed for a measured TE row. The three-organism all-$1.0$ surface is treated as rank saturation rather than survival evidence, so it is excluded from positive translation-survival interpretation.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter therefore records a finite, controlled tRNA-adaptation result for the $B^*_{Q6}$ optimal direction and an open translation-readout boundary for the $f_3$-stress coordinate. No claim about measured translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality follows from the residual geometry, codon topology, tRNA-copy summaries, tAI or stAI weights, review readiness, or passed gate status alone. Any such stronger assertion requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic on joined rows. The present record is falsified at the translation interface if the controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T}$ entries remain non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered panel; it is supported only to the extent that the declared same-row translation statistic becomes positive under those controls.
