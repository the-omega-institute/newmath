\paragraph{Direction and spectrum.}
The powered core row is supported by neighbouring finite audits that locate the same residual family inside a rank-powered spectrum.  The non-tRNA residual optimality row reports that the dominant non-tRNA residual axis aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection.  The two-axis closure row reports that a third axis is still present after the tRNA and $f_3$ span, with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed $d_\perp$ directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row identifies $d_\perp$ provisionally with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mechanism row keeps the third axis GC-orthogonal and selection-facing: its conclusion is selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate contact.  The per-organism spectrum row computed $11$ organisms and found $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leading in all $11$ dominant-count calls; its domain means are descriptive only because the domain sample is imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic rows, and the largest GC3 rank effect is $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.

The intended statistic is a controlled comparison after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA where applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablation. $$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0,\\

Z &= \{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},GC3,\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA\ control}\}.

The current finite memory does not contain same-organism and same-CDS joined codon, wobble-weight, measured translation-readout, and matched mRNA rows for this exact $f_3$-stress statistic.  Therefore the chapter may report a powered direction and a bounded route to translation testing, but it may not report measured translation survival for the $f_3$ coordinate.
