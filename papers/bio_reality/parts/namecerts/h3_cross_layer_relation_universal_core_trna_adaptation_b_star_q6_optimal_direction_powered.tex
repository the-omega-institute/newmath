\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-Stress Direction and Bounded tRNA-Adaptation Readout}\label{subsec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-trna-adaptation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The object considered here is a finite residual coordinate surface on coding sequences, indexed by organism and restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate that compares synonymous codons after amino-acid projection. The relevant symmetry is the third-position involution $f_3$, so the coordinate is not a raw codon-frequency difference: it is the residual imbalance that remains after amino-acid identity, organism-level composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and simpler codon baselines are treated as controls. The finite saturation row fixes the codon support before any biological interpretation is attempted:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the recorded carrier has $20$ codons in $M$, $18$ codons in the $f_3$-saturated $R$ support, and no remaining $f_3$ boundary codon in the finite row. The two codons present in $M$ but absent from the saturated $R$ row are $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, so the stress coordinate is interpreted only through the declared residual surface and not as a free-standing codon-membership fact.

\paragraph{Powered finite row.}
The finite experiment for the universal-core tRNA-adaptation direction passed with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $11$ organisms computed out of $16$ requested. This is enough to record a bounded direction in the finite panel, but it is not enough to promote the coordinate to a universal biological law. The associated non-tRNA residual optimality row reports that the dominant residual axis is $f_3$ translation selection, under the same $5$-fold, $41$-column, $11/16$ organism design. The two-axis closure row reports that the tRNA direction together with the $f_3$ direction is still insufficient: a third axis is present, with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms. The three-axis sufficiency row then records that a fourth component remains present, again in the same powered finite design.

\paragraph{Direction readout.}
The chapter therefore records a constrained claim: the $B^*_{Q6}$ core direction is compatible with tRNA adaptation in the powered organism panel, while the $f_3$-stress residual becomes dynamically meaningful only where context tRNA or wobble weights distinguish a codon $c$ from its $f_3$ mate $c^{f_3}$. In notation, the only permitted translation-row target is a controlled survival entry such as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a bounded translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, or an organism-matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row. The statement is not that codon topology alone causes translation output. It is that, after amino-acid quotienting and the declared controls, a non-degenerate translation readout may register the $f_3$-paired residual only when the relevant tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry.

\paragraph{Related finite structure.}
The per-organism axis-strength spectrum was computed for $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. In that row, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led the captured spectra on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant-count record was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$. The largest GC3 rank effect reported there was for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$, with Spearman rank correlation $\rho=0.136$. The third-axis identity row identified $d_\perp$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism-discrimination row kept the $d_\perp$ signal GC-orthogonalized and classified it as selection-not-GC and not wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity in need of a separate contact. These rows support the local geometry of the finite panel, but they also show that the tRNA-adaptation direction is not the whole residual story.

\paragraph{Boundary-set evidence.}
A larger boundary-set tRNA-poverty row was computed for $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ organisms skipped, significance level $\alpha=0.05$, and $1000$ permutations. That row reports that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy numbers together with dos Reis wobble weights. This supports using tRNA supply and wobble-weight asymmetry as the bounded interpretive route for the $R$-avoidance side of the coordinate. It does not, by itself, measure ribosome occupancy or protein output.

\paragraph{Downstream refusal.}
Several fourth-component tests delimit what the finite rows do not settle. The codon-pair row returned \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws. The mRNA-stability row returned \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}, because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates. The translation-accuracy row returned \texttt{not\_accuracy} with $200$ permutations, $11$ organisms computed out of $16$ requested, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The elongation-dwell row returned \texttt{partial} for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. These finite rows keep the tRNA-adaptation claim narrow: they help prevent the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress direction from being read as a general account of codon-pair bias, mRNA stability, translation accuracy, elongation dwell, or protein-level outcome.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This record authorizes only a bounded cross-layer readout claim. No translation-realization claim follows from the residual geometry unless a non-vacuous $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ entry is computed under the stated controls. No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, organismal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the finite rows recorded here. Those conclusions require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. In particular, a three-organism all-$1.0$ matrix is a rank-degenerate null boundary rather than survival evidence, and modeled tAI or stAI weights remain bounded adaptation readbacks unless they are joined to measured translation rows and matched mRNA controls.
