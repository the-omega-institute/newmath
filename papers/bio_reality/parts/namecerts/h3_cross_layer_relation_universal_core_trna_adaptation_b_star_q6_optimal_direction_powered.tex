\subsection{Universal core tRNA adaptation for the $B^*_{Q6}$ optimal direction}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-universal-core-trna-adaptation}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface in which each row is an organism-scoped CDS residual coordinate, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ optimal-direction coordinate and compared against translation-layer readouts.  The intended biological object is not a protein property and not a phenotype.  It is the controlled relation between a synonymous residual coordinate, organism-level codon usage, tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry, and a translation-readout row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency when such a row is actually available.  The primary powered run for this claim was the experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered}$, with deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered:deterministic}$.  It passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.

\paragraph{Coordinate support.}
The finite saturation row fixes the third-position $f_3$ boundary before any biological interpretation is attempted.  In that row, the ambient set $M=\mathrm{Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated $R$ row removes $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, leaving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the coordinate has no residual boundary row by itself; the biological question is whether the coordinate acquires readout power only after it is paired with organism-level translation asymmetry.

\paragraph{Powered tRNA-adaptation call.}
The powered core-adaptation result is a positive finite-row classification, not a universal law.  Its local content is that the shared $B^*_{Q6}$ optimal direction is supported as a tRNA-adaptation axis on the computed panel.  The panel is rank-powered relative to the earlier three-organism all-$1.0$ surface: the latter is a saturated null boundary, whereas the present run reports $11$ organisms, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The associated tRNA-poverty mechanism row is also finite and explicit: the $R$ sense codons were called systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, with $40$ organisms computed out of $46$ requested, $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$.  This supports a supply-side interpretation of $R$ avoidance at the codon-adaptation layer, while still leaving direct ribosome and protein claims outside the present row.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual-axis spectrum.}
The same finite panel separates the tRNA-adaptation component from other residual axes.  The non-tRNA residual optimality run identifies $\mathrm{f3\_translation\_selection}$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual axis, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The two-axis closure run reports that a third axis is present: $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The third-axis identity row provisionally aligns $d_\perp$ to usage frequency within codon family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The corresponding mechanism row says that the GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, giving the local call ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external data.''

\paragraph{Fourth-axis boundary.}
The optimality-axis sufficiency row reports that a fourth mechanism is present on the same scale: $11$ organisms were computed from $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The axis-strength spectrum is led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; every one of the $11$ per-organism dominant counts is assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  Its domain means are explicitly descriptive because the domain sample is imbalanced, with $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect is only $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  Candidate fourth-mechanism tests narrow the interpretation: mRNA stability is excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the assay, codon-pair identity is called $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$, translation accuracy is called $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$ with $200$ permutations, and elongation dwell is only partial in $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$.

\paragraph{Local readout rule.}
For this chapter the bounded readout is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The statistical form is the finite comparison
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after controlling for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when TE is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines.  The powered tRNA-adaptation run gives a positive coordinate-level classification for the optimal direction, but it is not by itself a same-CDS ribosome-profiling matrix.  The tAI or stAI rows are bounded modeled codon-adaptation readbacks unless a measured ribosome or TE row is joined to the same organism and CDS identifiers.

\paragraph{Local exclusion zone.}
Nothing in this record licenses a claim about protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism beyond the bounded tRNA-adaptation interpretation, or biological universality.  Such statements require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, codon topology, wobble-weight modeling, tRNA-copy asymmetry, and the passed powered classification do not become ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency unless a measured translation row is joined and survives the declared controls.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ matrix remains a rank-degenerate null boundary, not survival evidence.  The present conclusion is therefore narrower and stronger: within the computed finite panel, the $B^*_{Q6}$ optimal direction is supported as a tRNA-adaptation core, while every downstream biological interpretation remains blocked until a separate finite contact supplies the relevant row.
