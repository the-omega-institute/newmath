\subsection{Universal core tRNA adaptation direction in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-universal-core-trna-adaptation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.} This record concerns a finite coding-sequence-by-organism residual surface in which the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is compared with organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA-copy or wobble-weight summaries, and bounded translation-readout rows. The relevant stress direction is the third-position involution coordinate $f_3$ after amino-acid quotienting: synonymous codons are paired by the $f_3$ move, amino-acid composition is removed as a direct explanation, and the remaining question is whether the residual axis becomes visible where tRNA or wobble weights distinguish a codon $c$ from its paired codon $c^{f_3}$. The local carrier is therefore not a protein-function carrier and not a phenotype carrier. It is a finite residual-coordinate surface with controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance whenever a ribosome or translation-efficiency readout is used.

\paragraph{Saturation boundary.} The combinatorial source row gives the exact finite $M$ and $f_3$-saturation surfaces used to delimit the coordinate. The verified row records
$$\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the $f_3$ boundary is empty on this finite row, while $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ appear in $M$ but not in the reported $\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)$ list. The record is therefore sharp enough to say which codon tokens define the stress surface, but it is not by itself a biological readout.

\paragraph{Powered core result.} The universal-core tRNA-adaptation computation passed on experiment run $373843e81379$ with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its deterministic seed is recorded as $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_universal\_core\_is\_trna\_adaptation\_powered:deterministic}$. In this chapter the phrase ``core tRNA-adaptation direction'' means only the finite residual direction certified by that row: after quotienting by amino-acid composition and fitting the declared synonymous design, the selected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimal direction is treated as aligned with the tRNA-adaptation component of the panel. The computation is powered relative to the finite panel just stated, not universal over all organisms or all translation systems.

\paragraph{Nearby finite checks.} Several surrounding rows constrain how much can be inferred from the core result. The non-tRNA residual optimality row, also using $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, reports that the dominant residual axis is $f_3$ translation selection. The two-axis closure row reports a third-axis-present conclusion under the same panel size and states that the orthogonal direction has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ with same-signed directions across organisms. The per-organism spectrum row reports $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, with $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leading on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts are assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, and the largest GC3 rank effect in that row is $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. These facts make the core tRNA-adaptation row part of a finite axis system rather than an isolated scalar.

\paragraph{Mechanism-adjacent evidence.} The boundary-set tRNA-poverty row was computed for $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and significance level $\alpha=0.05$. Its mechanism call states that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, supporting a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance in that bounded assay. This is the strongest local support for reading the core direction as tRNA-adaptation-like, but it remains a supply-weight and codon-usage statement. It does not directly observe ribosome dwell time, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, or fitness.

\paragraph{Orthogonal and fourth-axis controls.} The third-axis identity row reports provisional alignment of $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ over the $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column panel. The third-axis mechanism row classifies the same direction as selection-not-GC and not wobble-clustered, while leaving CSC or positional identity as requiring outside data. The three-axis sufficiency row reports that a fourth mechanism is present. The fourth-mechanism follow-up rows then exclude or weaken several candidate explanations: the mRNA-stability assay reports ``fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability''; the codon-pair assay reports ``not\_codon\_pair'' with $50$ shuffle nulls; the translation-accuracy assay reports ``not\_accuracy'' with $200$ permutations; and the elongation-dwell assay in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} reports only a partial conclusion. These rows are important because they keep the tRNA-adaptation direction from being overread as the whole residual geometry.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.} The controlled survival entry for a translation readout has the form $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be a bounded tAI or stAI surface, a ribosome-occupancy row, or a translation-efficiency row. A positive interpretation requires a nondegenerate matched panel: codon counts, tRNA or wobble weights, the translation readout, and matched mRNA controls must occupy the same organism or CDS rows, and the fitted statistic must remain positive after the declared controls. In symbols, the admissible promotion condition is local and finite:
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{CDS\ length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA\ control}\},\\
\Delta \mathrm{MDL}(f_3,T_j\mid Z) &< 0
\end{aligned}$$
for the declared row set, together with simpler-codon-baseline ablation. If the controlled score is non-positive, or if the design is rank-degenerate, the entry is a null boundary rather than survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.} The present row supports a finite claim about the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimal direction in a powered multi-organism codon/tRNA panel. It does not establish observed translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism in the causal-intervention sense, or a universal biological law. A modelled tAI or stAI weight is a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not an observed ribosome-footprint row. A ribosome-profiling contact, when later attached, can test translation efficiency only after same-row joining and mRNA normalization. A proteomics, localization, RNA half-life, RBP, structure, perturbation, or phenotype claim would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Falsification condition.} The finite conjecture survives only as long as the powered tRNA-adaptation direction remains separated from null translation-readout promotion. It is falsified at the translation boundary if every controlled $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ is non-positive after the stated controls, or if the available matrix is rank-saturated in a way that makes an all-$1.0$ surface uninformative. It is also weakened if wobble or tRNA asymmetry fails to distinguish $c$ from $c^{f_3}$ in the contexts where the coordinate is claimed to be dynamical. The current positive row is therefore best read as a finite, panel-scoped tRNA-adaptation certificate for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ optimal direction, with downstream biological interpretation deliberately held behind separate measured contacts.
