\subsection{Mouse Polyadenylation Catalogue as an APA Contact Surface}\label{subsec:bioreality-apa-pas-strength-crossdataset-polyasite-mouse}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The mouse PolyASite contact supplies a concrete alternative-polyadenylation catalogue, not yet a complete cross-layer matrix for a synonymous-codon claim. The finite object presently recorded for this contact has $167610$ polyadenylation clusters across $20110$ genes, with run identifier $2ac7a388b975$ and a recorded runtime of $0.0$ seconds. Thus the row universe is large enough to support gene-level and cluster-level bookkeeping, but the rows currently stop at the APA catalogue boundary. They do not yet contain, on the same keyed entries, the residual $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinates, matched coding-sequence controls, measured translation columns, protein-omics columns, and mRNA controls required for a direct codon-to-protein or codon-to-APA survival statistic.

\paragraph{Admissible finite interpretation.}
The finite statement that can be made now is only that a mouse APA readout surface exists with the cardinalities
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{cluster}} &= 167610,\\
N_{\mathrm{gene}} &= 20110.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports construction of a later joined table in which each eligible coding gene may carry APA cluster summaries, coding-sequence covariates, and controlled molecular readouts. The present contact does not by itself assign a positive coefficient, survival score, mediation score, or causal direction. In particular, no $S^{QP}_{ik}$, $S^{QT}_{ij}$, or $M^{QTP}_{ijk}$ value is recorded for this APA run. The correct readback is therefore a bounded data-contact statement: a large mouse APA catalogue is available, but the cross-layer statistic has not been computed on a same-row panel.

\paragraph{Relation to existing codon-coordinate evidence.}
The wider $B^*_{Q6}$ programme already has finite codon-coordinate and protein-abundance evidence in other runs, but those numbers do not close the APA claim. A separate direct protein-omics run computed $9$ residual coordinates, including $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. In mouse, that protein-abundance contact joined $19395$ proteins with join hit rate $0.9808334176190958$, residualized against $24$ control columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and residual degrees of freedom $19372$. The resulting direct protein-abundance score had $R^2_{QP}=0.049827984062539195$ with $9$ surviving coordinates. Those protein-omics numbers are useful context for the codon-coordinate layer, but they are not APA measurements and they do not prove a PolyASite PAS-strength association.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The mandatory translation boundary is also still open for the APA packet. A separate modelled translation-readout run used $3$ organisms and computed modelled tAI weights from tRNA gene-copy data with all $61$ sense codons represented for each organism. It found $81$ entries above the bounded-coupling threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute score of $3801961.031607896$, but the same record explicitly marks $3$ organisms as insufficient for powered survival. Therefore the APA catalogue cannot inherit a translation mechanism from modelled tAI coupling, from codon composition, or from protein-abundance association. A measured translation readout $T_j$ must be attached on the same organism- and gene-matched rows before any statement of the form $q_i \to T_j$ or $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ is available.

\paragraph{Required joined matrix.}
A ready APA survival claim would require a single finite table whose rows are keyed consistently across mouse genes or transcripts. At minimum, the table must include the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate columns $q_i$, amino-acid composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, organism or batch indicators when relevant, mRNA abundance controls, measured translation readouts, protein-omics readouts if protein interpretation is attempted, and an APA/PAS-strength response derived from the $167610$ clusters over $20110$ genes. Only after this join exists can the controlled residual question be posed as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= q_i - \Pi_Z q_i,\\
\widetilde A_k &= A_k - \Pi_Z A_k,\\
S^{QA}_{ik} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for an APA readout $A_k$, with any protein-omics extension conditioned on the measured translation column. If the translation column removes the codon-to-protein signal, the result is a translation-mediated interpretation; if it does not, the result remains a direct predictive residue rather than a mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The present mouse PolyASite contact does not establish translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biochemical mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, or a universal biological law. Those claims require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics. The existing finite facts support only this narrower statement: the APA catalogue is large and concrete, and it can serve as a future readout surface, but the cross-layer $B^*_{Q6}$ APA/PAS-strength survival matrix is not yet present.
