\subsection{Mouse Polyadenylation Contact as a Cross-Layer Boundary}
\label{subsec:mouse-polyasite-apa-pas-strength-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite contact.}
The mouse PolyASite packet supplies a finite alternative-polyadenylation surface, not a completed translation or protein-output mechanism.  The attached run records $167610$ polyadenylation clusters over $20110$ genes, with run identifier \texttt{2ac7a388b975} and timestamp \texttt{2026-06-24T05:41:53+00:00}.  These rows are large enough to name a real transcript-end contact, but the recorded scalars do not yet contain a fitted PAS-strength coefficient, a codon-coordinate coefficient, a same-gene protein readout, or a mediation statistic.  The chapter therefore treats the PolyASite row count as a measured transcript-processing boundary: it says that a mouse APA/PAS table exists at finite scale, while refusing to promote that table into translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Coordinate packet.}
The codon side is the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual family after quotienting by amino-acid composition.  The finite coordinate list used by the surrounding cross-layer tests is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended matrix carrier is a finite CDS-by-organism table containing these residual coordinates, matched translation readout columns, matched mRNA controls, and matched protein-omics readout columns.  The APA/PAS contact adds a transcript-end layer to that carrier only after identifier matching and residualization are made explicit.  In its present form the mouse APA surface supplies row availability, not a survival score.

\paragraph{Known positive controls from neighboring finite runs.}
The reason this APA chapter can be written as a scientific boundary rather than as an empty template is that the same coordinate family has already been tested on several finite readout surfaces.  In the modeled tAI run over three organisms, all standard amino-acid tRNA families were represented for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12, human, and yeast; the recorded organism count was $3$, the codon count was $61$, and the bounded coupling threshold was $0.01$.  That run found $81$ entries above threshold, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but it also explicitly marks the result as modeled-tAI bounded coupling rather than powered biological survival, because three organisms are insufficient for the requested powered survival claim.

The direct protein-omics run then used measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts across $15$ organisms.  The residualized design kept $9$ $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates after a $23$-rank control block, with $24$ control columns before rank reduction.  Mouse itself had $19395$ joined proteins and join hit rate $0.9808334176190958$; human had $19053$ joined proteins with hit rate $0.9779294769799313$; yeast had $5815$ joined proteins with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 had $3739$ joined proteins with hit rate $0.9978649586335735$.  The recorded direct protein-omics partial-survival surface included
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{mouse}) &= 0.049827984062539195,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.04454565118019448,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{E. coli}) &= 0.10767526677985896.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each organism reported $9$ surviving coordinates under the stated positive threshold, but the run itself forbids interpreting those entries as translation mechanism, folding, localization, post-translational modification, pathway function, or causality.  They are direct residual associations with a protein-abundance readout.

\paragraph{APA/PAS readback.}
For the present mouse PolyASite packet, the only verified APA-side finite scalars are
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{clusters}} &= 167610,\\
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 20110,\\
\mathrm{runtime\_sec} &= 0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the valid readback is a contact-existence and scale statement: a mouse APA/PAS-strength analysis surface has been attached at cluster and gene resolution.  A stronger cross-layer statement would require a joined table whose rows contain mouse CDS codon counts, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, PolyASite cluster or gene-level APA/PAS variables, transcript abundance controls, and, for any protein interpretation, matched translation and protein measurements.  The residualized test would then report a statistic such as $S^{QA}_{i\ell}$ for codon-to-APA survival, and only after a measured translation column $T_j$ is attached could one ask whether conditioning on $T_j$ removes, reduces, or preserves any $q_i$ to protein-omics signal.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
This chapter records a finite mouse APA/PAS boundary and the available cross-layer context for the same $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate system.  It does not claim that synonymous codon residuals control APA, translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  A positive protein-omics association remains a direct residual association unless a measured translation mediator is present and an explicit mediation fit supports $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$.  A positive APA/PAS association, when computed, would similarly remain a transcript-processing association unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the next biological readout.  With the current mouse PolyASite facts, the supported conclusion is finite contact and scope discipline, not biological promotion beyond the measured APA/PAS table.
