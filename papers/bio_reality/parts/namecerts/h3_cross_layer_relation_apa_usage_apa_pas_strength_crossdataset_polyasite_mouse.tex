\subsection{Mouse Polyadenylation-Site Index as a Boundary Record for Synonymous Cross-Layer Tests}
\label{subsec:bioreality-apa-pas-strength-polyasite-mouse}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This record isolates a mouse alternative-polyadenylation contact surface from any stronger interpretation about translation, protein output, molecular mechanism, or organismal law.  The finite row certificate presently available for the PolyASite mouse contact is an index-level fact: the run \texttt{2ac7a388b975} reports $167610$ polyadenylation-site clusters and $20110$ genes, with recorded runtime $0.0$ seconds.  In the notation of this chapter, the attached finite surface is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{clusters}} &= 167610,\\
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 20110,\\
\tau_{\mathrm{run}} &= 0.0\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers certify the existence and scale of a mouse PAS-index panel, not an already computed synonymous-coordinate survival statistic.  They give a finite target on which a future row-wise matrix may be formed, but they do not yet supply per-CDS codon residuals, per-gene PAS strength values, matched transcript abundance, matched translation readouts, or matched protein-omics values on the same rows.

\paragraph{Coordinate role.}
The intended explanatory variable family is the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ amino-acid-quotient residual basis on coding sequences.  The residual coordinate list available from the connected codon-usage computations has $9$ entries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\\
&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\\
&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The point of using these coordinates is not that codon geometry is itself a biological measurement.  The point is narrower: after the amino-acid quotient has removed the direct protein-sequence class, the remaining synonymous coordinates can be tested against an attached finite readout, provided the readout and the controls live on the same rows.  For the mouse PAS panel, the currently certified rows stop at cluster and gene counts.  A PAS-strength survival matrix would require a row map from coding-sequence identifiers to PAS clusters or gene-level PAS summaries and then a residualized readout column.

\paragraph{Local statistic to be tested.}
Let $q_i$ be one of the $9$ synonymous residual coordinates and let $A_k$ be a PAS-strength or APA-usage readout derived from the mouse PolyASite panel.  The local survival score for this chapter is the added explanatory power of $q_i$ for $A_k$ after baseline controls, not a claim about translation or protein abundance.  The admissible test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_k &= Z\alpha + q_i\beta_i + \varepsilon,\\
S^{QA}_{ik} &= R^2(A_k\mid Z,q_i)-R^2(A_k\mid Z),\\
Z &\supset \{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{CDS\ length},\ \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA\ abundance}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry would mean only that a synonymous residual coordinate survives as a predictor of the APA or PAS-strength readout under the declared controls.  It would not mean that codon usage changes cleavage and polyadenylation, that translation mediates the association, or that a protein-level phenotype has been observed.  The present finite record has the scale needed for such a test, but it lacks the row-resolved values needed to compute $S^{QA}_{ik}$.

\paragraph{Why the translation boundary is explicit.}
The broader $Q6$ program already contains several controlled cross-layer contacts, and their finite numbers are useful here because they show what a computed survival record looks like.  In the direct protein-omics run \texttt{104db736a756}, the same $9$ residual coordinates were tested against measured PAXdb protein abundance after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  The per-organism controlled protein-omics readbacks include $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.29837624994140827$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, and $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse.  The mouse protein-omics join used $19395$ proteins with join hit rate $0.9808334176190958$, rank $9$ for the residualized coordinate block, rank $23$ for controls, and residual degrees of freedom $19372$.  Those are direct protein-abundance associations, not translation mechanisms.

\paragraph{Comparison with RNA-stability contacts.}
The mRNA-stability contacts also show that row-resolved readouts can be tested without promoting them to mechanism.  In yeast, the same-coordinate RNA half-life run reports $n=5085$, $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$, cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between the RNA-stability and protein-omics residual directions, and permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$ over $1000$ permutations.  In \textit{E. coli}, the half-life run reports $n=2720$, $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, and the same permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In human, the consensus half-life run reports $n=16165$, $54$ dataset columns, $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, and $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These examples justify the statistical shape of the proposed mouse PAS test, while also showing the required discipline: a positive residual association remains a readout-specific association unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the missing biological step.

\paragraph{Rank and underpowered-contact caution.}
The modeled translation contact is deliberately weaker than a measured translation contact.  The modeled tAI run \texttt{4250daa2488f} uses $3$ organisms, computes $61$ sense-codon weights per organism from GtRNAdb gene-copy and dos Reis wobble values, and records $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The same run explicitly treats $3$ organisms as insufficient for powered survival and refuses promotion to ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  This matters for the mouse PAS contact because a large index size by itself is not enough.  The PAS panel has $167610$ clusters and $20110$ genes, but the survival claim needs same-row covariates and nondegenerate residual rank after controls.  A table of clusters is not yet a tested $Q$-to-APA matrix.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The local claim allowed by the present finite record is only that a mouse PAS/APA readout surface has been identified at a concrete scale and can host a future $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual test.  No translation claim follows from the cluster and gene counts.  No folding, physical admissibility, protein function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, causation, adaptation, or universal biological law follows from the PAS index, from the synonymous residual coordinates, or from a future positive $S^{QA}_{ik}$ entry alone.  Translation requires a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout on matched rows.  Protein-omics interpretation requires matched protein abundance and the translation boundary to be reported.  Mechanism requires a mediator or perturbation design strong enough to distinguish direct association from routed association.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
This chapter is therefore ready as a bounded BioReality record: it records the finite mouse PolyASite scale, fixes the admissible $Q$-to-APA statistical shape, and states the non-promotion rule locally.  The scientific result is not a passed APA-survival theorem.  It is a prepared finite contact boundary: $167610$ clusters and $20110$ genes define the readout surface, while the missing same-row PAS-strength matrix, mRNA control, translation readout, and protein-omics mediation rows determine what remains to be measured before any positive cross-layer interpretation can be reported.
