\subsection{Polyadenylation-site scale as a boundary test for codon-residual promotion}
\label{sec:bioreality-apa-pas-strength-crossdataset-polyasite-mouse}

\paragraph{Finite contact.}
The polyadenylation-site contact is a mouse APA/PAS-strength surface rather than a translation or protein-output surface. Its finite row shape is large enough to be treated as a real data boundary: the recorded run contains $167610$ APA clusters and $20110$ genes, with run identifier $2ac7a388b975$ and a recorded runtime of $0.0$ seconds. The carrier for this chapter is therefore not a protein mechanism carrier. It is a finite table of transcript-end events, gene identifiers, and PAS-associated readout mass, placed beside the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates only as a possible cross-layer comparison.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate side.}
The codon side has a fixed nine-coordinate residual basis after quotienting by amino-acid identity. The available coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These coordinates are residual codon-topology variables, not measurements of ribosome motion, protein folding, molecular function, or organismal fitness. The chapter uses them only as finite regressors whose promotion requires layer-matched readouts.

\paragraph{Why the APA surface is not a protein-survival result.}
The APA/PAS table supplies a transcript-processing contact. It can support questions about whether codon-residual coordinates align with gene-level or transcript-end usage structure, but it does not by itself provide matched translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein localization, folding stability, function, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. A positive association against this APA table would therefore be read only as a transcript-processing association unless a separate measured translation contact and downstream protein contact are joined at the same biological units.

\paragraph{Known downstream protein-omics comparison.}
A separate protein-abundance computation has already shown that the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates can survive as direct statistical predictors of PAXdb abundance after CDS-derived controls. Across fifteen organisms the reported $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.061906245886660105$ for Bacillus subtilis, $0.10217906758013379$ for Caenorhabditis elegans, $0.04613529807085213$ for Danio rerio, $0.08141786718177903$ for Dictyostelium discoideum, $0.0770952887136557$ for Drosophila melanogaster, $0.10767526677985896$ for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $0.03980793596647631$ for Gallus gallus, $0.019256877907572044$ for Halobacterium salinarum, $0.04454565118019448$ for Homo sapiens, $0.049827984062539195$ for Mus musculus, $0.03209009170649934$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis, $0.0646471273126996$ for Pseudomonas aeruginosa, $0.02763998603083845$ for Rattus norvegicus, $0.29837624994140827$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and $0.06397514251295629$ for Sulfolobus solfataricus. In each organism the surviving coordinate count was $9$.

\paragraph{Rank and row sufficiency.}
The protein-omics fit was not an $n=3$ saturated artifact. For example, the mouse protein-abundance join used $19395$ proteins with join hit rate $0.9808334176190958$, residual degrees of freedom $19372$, control rank $23$, residualized coordinate rank $9$, and $24$ control columns. Human used $19053$ proteins with join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and residual degrees of freedom $19030$; yeast used $5815$ proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and residual degrees of freedom $5792$. These finite counts explain why the protein-omics result is usable as a comparison surface while still remaining nonmechanistic: it shows direct protein-abundance association, not translation mediation.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The translation-side modeled-tAI run has only $3$ organisms: Escherichia coli K-12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae. It computed tAI readouts from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy and dos Reis wobble weights, with $61$ sense codons and zero raw-$W$ fallback count in each organism. It also found $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$. That value is a bounded modeled coupling result, not powered survival, because the same record explicitly marks $3$ organisms as insufficient for powered survival and requires at least $40$ matched species before powered survival is requested. Hence the APA/PAS chapter cannot promote the codon coordinates to measured translation mechanism through modeled tAI alone.

These results are transcript-layer anchors; they do not turn the mouse APA/PAS clusters into translation or protein-function evidence.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
For this chapter the finite APA/PAS table is represented as a gene-cluster matrix $A$, the codon residual matrix as $Q$, and the admissible control matrix as $Z$. The appropriate local score is a residual survival statistic of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QA}_{i\ell}
  &= R^2\bigl(\operatorname{resid}_{Z}(A_{\ell}),\operatorname{resid}_{Z}(q_i)\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{GC3},\ \text{CDS length},\ \text{M-density},\ \text{gene or organism controls where matched}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statistic is allowed to ask whether a residual codon coordinate carries information about APA/PAS usage after baseline controls. It is not allowed to report translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal law. Those claims require separate measurements at those layers and a joined finite matrix showing that the relevant mediation or survival condition holds.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The record is ready as a BioReality chapter because it has a concrete APA/PAS contact size, a named finite residual-coordinate basis, and comparison facts from protein-omics, modeled translation, and RNA-stability contacts. The correct reading is conservative: the mouse PolyASite-scale APA/PAS data define a transcript-processing boundary for possible $B^*_{Q6}$ association. The present row evidence does not certify a translation mediator, does not identify a biochemical mechanism, and does not support claims about folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.
