\subsection{Mouse Polyadenylation Contact as a Separate APA Boundary}
\label{sec:bior-feat-apa-pas-strength-polyasite-mouse}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record isolates a mouse alternative-polyadenylation contact from the codon-usage and protein-omics panels.  The finite table contains $167610$ PolyASite clusters assigned across $20110$ genes, with run identifier $2ac7a388b975$ and reported runtime $0.0$ seconds.  These two row counts are sufficient to certify that the APA side of the packet is not a symbolic desideratum: it has a concrete many-cluster, many-gene measurement surface.  They are not, by themselves, a codon residual statistic, a translation statistic, or a protein-output statistic.  In this chapter the APA table is therefore treated as a layer-specific readout that can later be joined to coding-sequence rows only through explicit identifiers and controls.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The codon-side coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
They are residual coordinates after the amino-acid quotient, not raw codon-frequency summaries.  A future APA statistic would have to place these $9$ directions beside per-gene or per-transcript APA quantities, then remove amino-acid composition, organism labels when multi-organism rows are present, GC3, coding length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, and any measured translation readout used as a boundary control.  Without that joined matrix, the PolyASite counts report the size of the APA contact, not a passed $q_i$ to APA association.

\paragraph{Protein-omics comparison retained as context.}
The nearest completed downstream protein-omics panel used measured PAXdb protein abundance and real CDS codon counts across $15$ organisms.  It retained $9$ residual coordinates after $24$ control columns, with $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ in every organism-specific fit.  The reported direct protein-omics residual coefficients were positive under the very small threshold $10^{-12}$, but the biological interpretation was restricted to direct association.  The organism-level residual $R^2_{QP}$ values were $0.061906245886660105$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.10217906758013379$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.04613529807085213$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.08141786718177903$ for \emph{Dictyostelium discoideum}, $0.0770952887136557$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.03980793596647631$ for chicken, $0.019256877907572044$ for \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, $0.03209009170649934$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.0646471273126996$ for \emph{Pseudomonas aeruginosa}, $0.02763998603083845$ for rat, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, and $0.06397514251295629$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The mouse value $0.049827984062539195$ is relevant only as a protein-abundance comparison point; it is not an APA strength measurement.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
A separate modeled-translation panel used three organisms, $61$ sense codons, and dos Reis wobble weights derived from GtRNAdb tRNA-copy data.  The standard amino-acid family coverage was $20$ in \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast; the matched FASTA-header counts were $87$ out of $89$ for \emph{E. coli}, $432$ out of $432$ for human, and $275$ out of $275$ for yeast.  The modeled coupling surface had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  Because the organism count was $3$, this was explicitly not a powered survival panel.  The same boundary applies here: a mouse PolyASite table, even with $167610$ clusters, does not license a translation claim unless a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency column is attached and shown to mediate the codon-side association.

\paragraph{RNA-stability anchors.}
The mRNA-half-life panels show how a downstream RNA readout may be handled once the rows are actually joined.  In yeast, $5085$ joined rows gave $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on the same join, cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between the mRNA and protein residual directions, dominant $Q\to M$ coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, and permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ over $1000$ permutations.  In \emph{E. coli}, $2720$ joined rows gave $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, cosine $-0.7784923375569599$, dominant coordinate $Arg\_AGR$, and the same $1000$-permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In human, $16165$ joined rows and $54$ consensus half-life columns gave $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, cosine $0.6351183883924848$, and dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$.  These figures are not mouse APA evidence; they demonstrate the standard required for promoting a finite contact from row existence to a controlled cross-layer statistic.

\paragraph{APA survival form.}
Let $A_g$ denote an APA or PAS-strength readout attached to gene or transcript row $g$, and let $Q_g$ be the vector of the nine residual codon coordinates on the same row.  The admissible statistic is a residual comparison of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QA}_{i} > 0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Delta\mathcal L(A\mid Z,Q_i)<0
\quad\text{or equivalently}\quad
R^2(A\sim Z+Q_i)-R^2(A\sim Z)>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes the sequence, abundance, translation, and simpler-baseline controls named above.  The current mouse PolyASite contact supplies the scale of $A$ through $167610$ clusters and $20110$ genes, but it does not yet report $S^{QA}_i$, a PAS-strength coefficient, a cross-dataset lambda, a module class, a hit rate for CDS-to-APA row joins, or a permutation value.  The correct local verdict is therefore a grounded APA contact with the cross-layer statistic still conditional on the joined design.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter permits only the following statement: mouse APA/PAS data exist at substantial finite scale and can serve as the APA-side contact for a future controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ residual test.  No translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, or universality follows from the APA cluster counts.  A protein-omics association remains a protein-abundance association unless a measured translation mediator is present and conditioning on that mediator reduces or removes the direct $q_i\to P_k$ signal.  Likewise, an APA association, if later computed, would remain an APA readout association unless a separate layer-specific contact supplies the mechanism being claimed.
