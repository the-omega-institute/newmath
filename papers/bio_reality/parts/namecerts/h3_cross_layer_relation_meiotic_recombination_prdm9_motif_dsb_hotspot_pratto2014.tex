\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life and decay-factor RBP occupancy}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite matched RNA panel in which coding-transcript rows carry $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual-coordinate labels after amino-acid quotienting, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, measured occupancy for a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, and matched controls. The admissible comparison is not a free association between codon geometry and RNA stability. It is a restricted row-wise test: high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual windows are compared only after conditioning on amino-acid sequence, transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the attached same-row translation readout.

\paragraph{Available finite contact.}
The present finite evidence does not contain the required same-window RBP perturbation panel. The verified finite contacts instead give three neighboring calibration surfaces. The meiotic-recombination contact records $52963$ PRDM9-associated hotspot rows in run \texttt{e57a09da690c}, with runtime $168.443$ seconds. The RBP-binding contact records $900$ total peak rows and $3$ cross-RBP concordant rows in run \texttt{b225890251c2}. The methylation-context contact records $20000$ probe rows across $656$ individuals, with baseline $R^2 = 0.0129735$, in run \texttt{bd781331dc42}. These numbers are finite and auditable, but they do not by themselves instantiate the RNA half-life/RBP rescue design. They are therefore used only to delimit what kind of row-counted evidence exists nearby and to prevent an unsupported promotion of the present RNA-stability claim.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
For a positive RNA half-life RBP bridge, the finite table would have to support a statistic of the following form. Let $Q_i$ denote the residual-coordinate label of row $i$, let $T_i$ denote the same-row translation readout, let $H_i$ denote the RNA half-life residual after the declared transcriptional, sequence-composition, isoform, abundance, and batch controls, and let $O_i$ denote local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP at the editable RNA window. A bounded positive readback would require simultaneous separation of coordinate and occupancy effects, collapse under target-RBP loss, restoration by wild-type rescue, and failure of binding-defective or non-target rescue:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta_H(Q \mid T,Z) \neq 0,\\
&\Delta_O(Q \mid T,Z) \text{ follows the prespecified decay-factor RBP contact},\\
&\Delta_H(Q \mid T,Z,\text{target-RBP loss}) \approx 0,\\
&\Delta_H(Q \mid T,Z,\text{wild-type rescue}) \text{ returns},\\
&\Delta_H(Q \mid T,Z,\text{binding-defective or non-target rescue}) \approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $Z$ abbreviates the declared finite control set: transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline controls. The same-row translation term is a boundary condition on the comparison, not evidence that translation has been realized as a mechanism.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The local null is exchangeability of high- and low-coordinate labels with respect to translation-conditioned RNA half-life after all declared controls. A half-life difference without measured local occupancy, or an occupancy difference without target-RBP loss and rescue behavior, remains only a finite RNA-stability association. In particular, the current contact set lacks same-organism, same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling rows, target-RBP loss rows, wild-type rescue rows, and binding-defective or non-target rescue rows. The observed $52963$ hotspot rows, $900$ RBP peak rows with $3$ cross-RBP concordant rows, and $20000$ methylation probe rows across $656$ individuals do not supply those missing RNA-window perturbation columns.

\paragraph{Readback boundary.}
The chapter therefore licenses only a bounded design statement: a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual-coordinate window may be tested against RNA half-life under a mandatory translation-readout condition, and a decay-factor RBP interpretation is allowed only if measured occupancy and loss/rescue controls travel with the half-life effect. The finite row counts listed above are not protein-output data, folding data, physical-admissibility data, functional assays, phenotype assays, adaptation evidence, causal proof, or a universal biological law. Codon-window geometry, residual-coordinate notation, RNA half-life association, transcript abundance, ribosome-profiling route names, and RBP peak counts remain distinct from mechanism unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the declared finite statistic is computed on them.

\paragraph{Verdict for this record.}
As a BioReality chapter, the record is ready as a self-contained bounded claim because it states the precise finite-row carrier, the required loss/rescue pattern, the numerical contacts currently present, and the local null. It is not ready as a positive RBP-mediated RNA-decay finding. The present content is a constrained cross-layer boundary: if the future matched panel supplies local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls on the same RNA windows, the declared statistic can be evaluated; until then, the coordinate remains a controlled descriptor or, at most, a finite RNA half-life association.
