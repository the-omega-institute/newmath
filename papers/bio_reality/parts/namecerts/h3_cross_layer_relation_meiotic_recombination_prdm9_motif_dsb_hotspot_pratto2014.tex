\subsection{Translation-conditioned RNA half-life with an explicit RBP-decay gate}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
The record considered here is a finite matched-panel claim about synonymous residual coordinate labels in coding transcripts, not a claim about protein output or organismal fitness.  Its coordinate side consists of rows carrying a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual label after amino-acid quotienting; its readout side asks for same-row translation information, RNA half-life, measured occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, perturbation of that target RBP, and rescue arms.  The available finite-row audit does not yet contain that complete panel.  It does contain a separate meiotic-recombination finite run for the linked identifier, with $52963$ hotspot rows and runtime $168.443$ seconds under run id \texttt{e57a09da690c}.  That run supplies a row-counted reality contact for a different layer and therefore cannot by itself instantiate an RNA-half-life RBP bridge.  Two additional linked finite contacts are relevant only as boundary markers: the RBP-binding run reports $900$ total peak rows with $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases and runtime $223.648043$ seconds under run id \texttt{b225890251c2}, while the mTOR translation run reports $6494$ translation-readout rows from the declared GEO source and runtime $9.469$ seconds under run id \texttt{0c2bbfd3e2d9}.  These numbers show that finite biological surfaces exist in the surrounding ledger, but they do not yet form the same-row matrix required for the present RNA-decay claim.

\paragraph{Carrier and statistic.}
A valid row for this chapter has the form $(q_i,T_i,H_i,O_i,L_i,W_i,D_i,N_i,Z_i)$, where $q_i$ is the high- or low-coordinate synonymous residual label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $O_i$ is local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP at the editable RNA window, $L_i$ records target-RBP loss, $W_i$ records wild-type rescue, $D_i$ records binding-defective rescue or an appropriate non-target control, $N_i$ records matched abundance and transcription controls, and $Z_i$ collects local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, amino-acid composition, batch, and identifier-mapping diagnostics.  The null model is exchangeability of high- and low-coordinate labels with respect to $H_i$ after conditioning on these covariates and on $T_i$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
H_i &\perp q_i \mid T_i,N_i,Z_i,\\
\Delta_H(q) &= E[H_i\mid q_i=q,T_i,N_i,Z_i]-E[H_i\mid q_i=\mathrm{low},T_i,N_i,Z_i].
\end{aligned}
$$

The stronger RBP-decay reading requires more than $
\Delta_H(q)\neq 0$.  It requires that the half-life contrast track experimentally separated local occupancy of the prespecified RBP, collapse under target-RBP loss, return under wild-type rescue, and fail to return under binding-defective or non-target rescue.  In finite-row notation the support pattern is the conjunction

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta_H(q)\neq 0,\\
&\Delta_O(q)\neq 0,\\
&\Delta_H(q\mid L=\mathrm{loss})\approx 0,\\
&\Delta_H(q\mid W=\mathrm{wildtype})\approx \Delta_H(q),\\
&\Delta_H(q\mid D=\mathrm{binding\ defective\ or\ non\ target})\approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$

Without all five finite contrasts in the same matched panel, the record remains an RNA-stability association or a coordinate descriptor, not a decay-factor RBP mechanism.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The translation readout has a narrow role.  It prevents promotion of an RNA half-life contrast into a translation-layer claim unless $T_i$ is measured and used in the same row.  It does not convert RNA half-life into protein abundance, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, function, adaptation, causation, or a general biological law.  Likewise, an RBP occupancy contact alone is insufficient: occupancy must be local to the editable window, prespecified before testing the coordinate effect, and experimentally separated from coordinate geometry by loss and rescue arms.  Motif density, RNA structure, transcript abundance, GC content, dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, and batch effects are controls, not substitutes for target-RBP perturbation.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The present finite ledger is therefore adequate for a scoped chapter that states the exact required panel and the null boundary.  It is not adequate for a positive biological conclusion that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates realize an RNA-decay RBP mechanism.  The meiotic hotspot run contributes $52963$ rows for a linked recombination surface, the RBP-binding run contributes $900$ peak rows with $3$ cross-RBP concordant cases, and the translation run contributes $6494$ translation rows.  None of these three finite contacts supplies the same-organism, same-window RNA half-life, local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue matrix required here.  The chapter consequently records a bounded cross-layer test specification: a positive result is licensed only if the finite panel jointly satisfies the translation-conditioned half-life contrast and the RBP occupancy/loss/rescue pattern; otherwise the coordinate remains a descriptive label or, at most, a controlled RNA half-life association.
