\paragraph{Observed finite panel.}
The Pratto et al.\ 2014 meiotic recombination panel is treated here as a finite observational relation between PRDM9-associated sequence context and double-strand break hotspot calls. The recorded scan covers $n=52{,}963$ hotspots, and the extraction run completed in $168.443\,\mathrm{s}$. Those two numbers matter because they fix the scale of the claim: this is not an abstract recombination principle but a bounded empirical map over a large, explicitly counted hotspot set.

\paragraph{What the relation says.}
In this chapter the motif-bearing coordinate window is read on one side, and hotspot detection is read on the other. The claim is that, in this finite table, PRDM9-linked motif context tracks hotspot geography closely enough to be recorded as a cross-layer relation. The internal coordinate encoding keeps the sequence-side window and the recombination-side event separate, so the chapter reports correspondence rather than collapsing the two into a single mechanism. That distinction is important: the record says that the panel supports a finite association, not that the motif alone exhausts the recombination landscape.

\paragraph{Boundary on interpretation.}
This finite result does not license stronger conclusions about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality. It also does not promote the hotspot relation beyond what the contact supports: the data anchor an empirical PRDM9--DSB association, but any explanation of causation, molecular machinery, or system-wide regularity needs a separate layer-specific contact and its own statistic. Read narrowly, the chapter is a finite hotspot record over $52{,}963$ observations; read broadly, it remains only a bounded recombination-side observation.
