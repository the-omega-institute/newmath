\subsection{PRDM9 hotspot rows do not instantiate a translation-conditioned RNA half-life bridge}\label{sec:bioreality-prdm9-hotspot-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite contact.}
The finite record available for this chapter is a meiotic-recombination hotspot contact, not a transcript-decay perturbation panel.  The verified run is \texttt{e57a09da690c}, checked at \texttt{2026-06-27T00:47:31+00:00}; it contains $52963$ hotspot rows and the recorded runtime is $168.443$ seconds.  Thus the row carrier is a finite hotspot catalogue keyed to the PRDM9-motif/double-strand-break setting, while the proposed cross-layer statement asks for coding-transcript rows carrying $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual-coordinate labels, same-row translation readouts, RNA half-life measurements, measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  These are different finite sorts.  The hotspot contact can bound a meiotic-recombination surface, but it cannot by itself supply a coding-transcript RNA-decay surface.

\paragraph{Row typing.}
Let $H$ denote the verified finite hotspot table and let $|H|=52963$.  A row of $H$ may be used only as a hotspot row.  The requested RNA bridge would instead require a finite panel $P$ whose rows simultaneously carry a residual synonymous coordinate $q$, a translation readout $T$, an mRNA control $A$, an RNA half-life value $L$, a local RBP-occupancy measurement $O$, a target-RBP perturbation state $K$, and a rescue/control label $C$.  The required row shape is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
  p \in P
  \quad\mapsto\quad
  (q(p),T(p),A(p),L(p),O(p),K(p),C(p),Z(p)),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ collects transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, translation-readout conditioning, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler baseline controls.  The verified hotspot table supplies $|H|$ and hotspot membership, but it supplies none of $T,A,L,O,K,C$ for the same transcript/CDS row.  Consequently there is no typed map from the available rows into the required RNA half-life RBP panel.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The local null is exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels with respect to translation-conditioned RNA half-life after the stated controls.  A positive bridge would require a finite statistic showing that the half-life contrast tracks experimentally decoupled local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP, collapses after loss of that RBP, returns under wild-type rescue, and fails under binding-defective or non-target rescue.  In symbols, the intended positive pattern has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_L(q\_{\mathrm{high}},q\_{\mathrm{low}}\mid Z,T) &\neq 0,\\
  \Delta_L \text{ follows } \Delta_O,\qquad
  \Delta_L^{\mathrm{loss}} &\approx 0,\\
  \Delta_L^{\mathrm{WT\ rescue}} &\approx \Delta_L,\qquad
  \Delta_L^{\mathrm{defective\ or\ non\ target\ rescue}} \approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The available finite contact does not measure these quantities.  It therefore supports only the statement that a separate verified table has $52963$ hotspot rows.  It does not support the RNA half-life contrast, the RBP-occupancy bridge, the perturbation collapse, or the rescue asymmetry.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The chapter keeps the PRDM9 hotspot evidence and the proposed $Q6$ RNA-decay bridge separated.  A hotspot count, even a large finite count such as $52963$, is not a translation readout, not an RNA half-life readout, not an RBP occupancy assay, and not a perturbation/rescue experiment.  No translation realization follows from this contact.  No RNA-decay mechanism follows from this contact.  No protein output, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal sequence law follows from this contact.  Those claims require their own same-row finite contacts and their own declared statistics.  The present record is therefore a bounded negative alignment: it identifies the row-counted hotspot contact and blocks promotion of that contact into the translation-conditioned RNA half-life RBP bridge.
