\subsection{Partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary}\label{sec:bior reality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record concerns a rank-powered Saccharomyces cerevisiae panel in which the source design is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate matrix, residualized against the same sibling control basis used for the protein-abundance tests.  The target readout is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified yeast context join.  The active run reports $4759$ base rows, $4758$ available joined rows, and a deterministic working join of $1800$ rows after the fixed join rule $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the held-out evaluation uses $5$ folds.  The reduction family is a single-codon readout dictionary drawn from the measured-readout compression route; in the selected certificate the reducible dictionary has at most three named readouts, namely measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and protein turnover.

\paragraph{Certificate statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ source design and let $Y$ denote the residualized protein-abundance target.  For a dictionary subset $D$ from the measured-readout family, the run measures the fraction of the $Q \to Y$ residual signal covered by $D$ under the same folds and control basis.  The penalty parameter is $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, so the selected coverage is not merely the largest in-sample projection but the description-length penalized certificate.  The recorded quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{base}} &= 4759, & n_{\mathrm{available}} &= 4758, & n_{\mathrm{join}} &= 1800,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 24, & \lambda_{\mathrm{DL}} &= 0.01, & \rho_{\mathrm{join}} &= 0.05,\\
 C^* &= 0.24538211439730545, & 1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The unpenalized best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$.  The reported certificate coverage is $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, so the selected measured dictionary explains a nontrivial but incomplete part of the residual abundance surface.  The remaining fraction, $0.7546178856026946$, is retained as unexplained rather than promoted to a named mechanism.

\paragraph{Selection-aware null.}
The run uses $120$ deterministic permutations with seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_partial\_mediation\_certificate\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The naive fixed-subset null $95$ percent cutoff is $0.00598374993486675$, while the selection-aware null $95$ percent cutoff is $0.029149007242956115$.  The selected coverage therefore clears the multiple-search boundary by a wide margin:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
 \operatorname{Null}_{0.95}^{\mathrm{selection}} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
 C^* - \operatorname{Null}_{0.95}^{\mathrm{selection}} &= 0.21623310715434935.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite-row reason for calling the readout dictionary a partial mediator of the yeast abundance residual.  It is not a complete explanation, since most of the residual signal remains outside the selected readout span.

\paragraph{Compatibility with measured translation and stability rows.}
The same yeast surface has independent measured-TE support.  In the measured TE survival run, Saccharomyces cerevisiae uses $5128$ genes with measured TE out of $5815$ joined CDS records, with a TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$; after controls it has $\operatorname{rank}(X_{\sim})=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving at the positive threshold.  In the measured protein-abundance mediation run over the same organism, the three-way join is $5128$ rows, with $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  A separate joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on the yeast four-way join reports $n=4759$, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  Its joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  These companion rows explain why measured TE and mRNA stability are admissible members of the partial-reduction dictionary.

\paragraph{Dwell and protein-omics context.}
The f3-stress dwell run gives an additional yeast row at $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$ over a $1800$-row base, with $P_{f3}$ fraction $0.1999063916718794$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a permutation null $95$ percent cutoff of $0.0013115362189692937$.  The direct protein-omics run gives a larger yeast abundance association, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, over $5815$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and $9$ surviving coordinates.  The dictionary certificate sits between these facts: direct protein-omics association is real on the controlled rows, measured translation and stability absorb part of it, and the selected measured dictionary still leaves a large residual component.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is partial mediation, not mechanism completion.  The selected readout dictionary clears the selection-aware null and covers $24.538211439730545\%$ of the abundance residual signal, but $75.46178856026946\%$ remains outside the selected dictionary under the same test.  Therefore the chapter may state that measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover form a statistically significant partial reduction of the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual.  It may not state that translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, or universality has been established.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and a separate controlled statistic on matched rows.
