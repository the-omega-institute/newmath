\subsection{Partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary}\label{subsec:bioq6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record tests whether the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ residual protein-abundance signal can be compressed by a small dictionary of measured downstream readouts.  The active surface is the Saccharomyces cerevisiae unified context join with $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$ rows, $n_{\mathrm{available}}=4758$ eligible joined rows, and a five-fold held-out design.  The source design is the nine-dimensional synonymous residual coordinate system $B^*_{Q6}$, residualized against the same sibling control basis used in the companion protein-abundance and translation-readout runs.  The control basis has rank $24$.  The target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance.  The dictionary search allows at most three measured readouts from the family recorded as measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  Its description-length penalty is $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and the join floor is $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.  With deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_partial\_mediation\_certificate\_powered{:}deterministic}$ and $120$ permutations, the registered run passed.

\paragraph{Coverage certificate.}
The selected readout dictionary covers a nonzero but incomplete fraction of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance signal.  The selection-aware coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$.  A fixed-subset null would have $95\%$ cutoff $0.00598374993486675$, while the selection-aware null cutoff is $0.029149007242956115$.  Thus the finite-row conclusion is not merely that a preselected readout has a small positive projection; it is that the best dictionary found after the selection step still exceeds the corresponding selection-aware null.  The correct reading is partial reducibility: the residual signal is real, some of it is explainable by a dictionary of measured readouts, and most of it remains outside that dictionary.

\paragraph{Readout context.}
The dictionary result sits inside a measured cross-layer panel rather than an isolated regression.  In the measured translation-efficiency survival run, yeast has $5128$ genes with TE, a ribosome-TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$, residual TE energy $753.5781168800304$, and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all nine coordinates surviving at the positive threshold.  The measured protein-abundance mediation run on the same organism gives $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}=0.4793069656345167$.  The joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated single-mediator fractions are $0.4697188319693239$ for TE alone and $0.0626618595344251$ for mRNA stability alone, with joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$ and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers explain why the smaller dictionary certificate is meaningful: the protein-abundance association is not exhausted by a single translation proxy, but the measured readout family captures a reproducible component.

\paragraph{Direct protein-omics boundary.}
Direct protein abundance by itself is broad but not mechanistic.  Across the powered PAXdb protein-abundance panel, every organism in the listed set has nine surviving $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  The direct $R^2_{QP}$ list includes $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.10217906758013379$ for Caenorhabditis elegans, $0.08141786718177903$ for Dictyostelium discoideum, $0.0770952887136557$ for Drosophila melanogaster, $0.0646471273126996$ for Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, $0.06397514251295629$ for Sulfolobus solfataricus, $0.061906245886660105$ for Bacillus subtilis, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.03980793596647631$ for chicken, $0.03209009170649934$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis, $0.02763998603083845$ for rat, and $0.019256877907572044$ for Halobacterium salinarum.  These values justify treating the direct association as a real finite-row signal, but they do not identify its route.  The partial mediation chapter therefore uses the dictionary certificate as a discriminator: direct $q_i\to P_k$ survival can be reported as protein-omics prediction, while mechanism language is blocked unless a measured intermediate readout absorbs or reduces the association.

\paragraph{Case classification and residual routes.}
The modeled four-case run supplies a local classification check rather than a causal proof.  For yeast, the modeled-tAI four-case table classifies all nine coordinates as case D, with zero coordinates in A, B, or C and zero positive-positive entries without a mediation drop.  For E. coli, the same table gives seven case-D coordinates and two positive-positive entries without mediation drop.  The yeast measured runs agree that translation readouts matter, but the dictionary certificate keeps the conclusion weaker than full explanation: $75.46178856026946\%$ of the selected target component remains unexplained by the three-readout dictionary.  Other residual routes are visible.  The f3-stress dwell run in yeast joins $1257$ rows, reports base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null $95\%$ cutoff $0.0013115362189692937$, $f3$-dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$, and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Its positional companion identifies the leading dwell segment as the $5'$ window, with the $5'$ window passing and the $3'$ window not passing.  A separate ramp structural-order run is more restrained: its conclusion is ramp-positive but not localized, with weighted ramp $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability $0.05859375$.

\paragraph{Non-translation candidates.}
The human turnover run shows that a large non-translation residual remains difficult to compress by turnover alone.  In human, $P_Q$ has squared norm $1370.6490492302532$, the modeled complement $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ has squared norm $1262.2237678771598$, and $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9208949355679455$ on the full human join of $19053$ rows.  The turnover join has $8057$ rows and an absorption value $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, below the dof-matched null $95\%$ cutoff $0.0004342285066808464$.  Localization is somewhat different but still descriptive: on reviewed localization rows the absorbed fractions are $0.009254913329269913$ for human, $0.011225105608516642$ for yeast, and $0.009508864544725801$ for zebrafish, compared with dof-matched null $95\%$ cutoffs $0.0013758521256775384$, $0.004958116973149795$, and $0.011321328712802819$, respectively.  These side checks show why the readout dictionary is useful: it gives a finite reduction certificate without pretending that the remaining abundance residual has been assigned to turnover, localization, structure, or function.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready for a descriptive BioReality claim.  In yeast, a rank-powered matched panel supports a real $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual and a statistically nontrivial partial reduction to a small measured-readout dictionary.  The selected dictionary explains $24.538211439730545\%$ of the target component and leaves $75.46178856026946\%$ unexplained.  The accompanying measured-TE and joint TE/mRNA-stability results show that translation and stability readouts absorb part of the direct $q_i\to P_k$ signal, while the turnover, localization, dwell, and positional checks prevent the residual from being silently promoted to a stronger biological layer.  No claim of translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  Such claims would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
