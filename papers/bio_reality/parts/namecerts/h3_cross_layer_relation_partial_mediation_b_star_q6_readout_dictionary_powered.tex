\subsection{Partial readout-dictionary mediation for the powered yeast $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-partial-readout-dictionary-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite Saccharomyces cerevisiae protein-abundance surface in which the source design is the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate block, residualized against the same sibling control basis used by the measured cross-layer experiments.  The source coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified yeast context join.  The active powered fit used $n=1800$ rows from $4758$ available joined rows, with base panel size $4759$, five folds, $z$-basis rank $24$, join floor $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and dictionary penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$.

\paragraph{Dictionary certificate.}
The selected finite readout dictionary was drawn from the single-codon readout dictionary family.  The best selection-aware dictionary covered $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual protein-abundance signal, while the raw best coverage over the candidate family was $0.2745002077400602$.  The fixed-subset null was small, with $Null95=0.00598374993486675$, and the selection-aware null threshold was $0.029149007242956115$ across $120$ deterministic permutations.  Thus the observed selected coverage is not a rank or selection artifact at the declared null boundary.  The remaining unexplained fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
1-C^{*} &= 0.7546178856026946 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite conclusion is therefore partial reducibility, not exhaustion: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual is real on this yeast panel, and a dictionary of at most three measured readouts, listed in the experiment as measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover, accounts for a statistically nontrivial but incomplete portion of it.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein-omics survival.}
The partial dictionary result sits downstream of a broader direct protein-omics survival surface.  Across fifteen organism panels, direct $Q \rightarrow P$ survival after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls retained all nine residual coordinates.  The measured $R^2_{QP}$ values included $0.29837624994140827$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $0.10767526677985896$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.10217906758013379$ for Caenorhabditis elegans, $0.08141786718177903$ for Dictyostelium discoideum, $0.0770952887136557$ for Drosophila melanogaster, $0.0646471273126996$ for Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, $0.06397514251295629$ for Sulfolobus solfataricus, $0.061906245886660105$ for Bacillus subtilis 168, $0.049827984062539195$ for Mus musculus, $0.04613529807085213$ for Danio rerio, $0.04454565118019448$ for Homo sapiens, $0.03980793596647631$ for Gallus gallus, $0.03209009170649934$ for Mycobacterium smegmatis, $0.02763998603083845$ for Rattus norvegicus, and $0.019256877907572044$ for Halobacterium salinarum.  These values establish a finite downstream association surface only.  They do not by themselves identify the readout path carrying the association.

\paragraph{Measured translation and abundance conditioning.}
The measured ribosome-TE contact supplies a bounded translation-efficiency readout, not a causal translation mechanism.  In the powered measured-TE survival experiment, all four tested organisms retained nine surviving coordinates, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, $0.04034217614654355$ for Danio rerio, $0.018837911344127926$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.009394266754822262$ for Escherichia coli.  The corresponding measured-TE abundance mediation fractions were $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ for Escherichia coli, $0.0027966485036786744$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.0$ for Danio rerio after clipping a suppressive raw value.  For yeast, the three-way measured TE, abundance, and codon join used $5128$ rows; for Escherichia coli, $3415$ rows; for Homo sapiens, $11554$ rows; and for Danio rerio, $13140$ rows.  These finite readouts explain why the dictionary certificate is interpreted as partial mediation rather than as an unconditioned protein-omics correlation.

\paragraph{Joint mediator comparison.}
The joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition gives a second check on the dictionary interpretation.  In yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving a joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  The single-mediator fractions in yeast were $0.4697188319693239$ for TE alone and $0.0626618595344251$ for stability alone, with joint-minus-single-sum $-0.012862468941589822$, inside the declared descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  In Escherichia coli the joint mediation fraction was $0.2404669586626661$, and in Homo sapiens it was $0.16890712099923358$, both with the same right-tail probability $0.000999000999000999$.  The dictionary certificate is compatible with this pattern: measured readouts absorb a real fraction of the residual but leave a large remaining component.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant supplies a bounded comparison surface, not the decisive measured mechanism for this chapter.  For yeast, modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance yielded $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, with all nine coordinates assigned to case D.  For Escherichia coli, $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$; seven coordinates were case D and two were positive-positive without a mediation drop.  This sharp contrast with the measured-TE mediation fractions is important: modeled codon-adaptation weights can sort coordinates, but the readout-dictionary claim is anchored on finite measured or biologically attached readout columns and a selection-aware null.

\paragraph{Residual routes beyond the main dictionary.}
The remaining fraction $0.7546178856026946$ is not empty.  The yeast $f3\_stress$ dwell experiment joined $1257$ rows and found held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a null $95$th percentile of $0.0013115362189692937$, with $f3$-dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Its positional version identified the $5'$ segment as the leading segment; the $5'$ window passed while the $3'$ body window did not.  The structural-order ramp probe was weaker: at the primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f3$ ramp increment was $0.0011165640103326878$, the body increment was $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability was $0.05859375$, so the conclusion was ramp-positive but not localized.  Coarser H-candidate projections also remain descriptive: yeast localization absorption was $0.011225105608516642$ over a $12$-column localization basis, while human turnover absorption was only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ and did not exceed its null $95$th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The powered yeast dictionary result supports a finite statement: a selection-aware dictionary of measured readouts captures $24.538211439730545\%$ of the residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal, and $75.46178856026946\%$ remains outside that dictionary under the declared controls and null.  This is a cross-layer variance-partition certificate, not a causal chain.  No general translation claim, folded-structure claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, biological mechanism claim, or universal biological law follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measured rows and a controlled statistic at that layer.
