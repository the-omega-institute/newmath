\subsection{Partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary for $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite matrix surface whose rows are coding sequences in a joined yeast panel and whose columns are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The target readout is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The source design is the $9$-dimensional synonymous residual coordinate matrix, with the same sibling control basis used by the protein-abundance and translation-readout analyses.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the active powered analysis uses $n=1800$ joined rows out of $4758$ available joined rows, with base panel size $4759$.  The dictionary reduction family is the single-codon readout dictionary produced by the minimal dictionary compression run, and the selected explanatory set is bounded to at most three measured readouts.

\paragraph{Residualization and score.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate design, let $P$ denote residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance, and let $D_{\leq 3}$ range over dictionary subsets of size at most three from the measured readout family.  The certificate reports the best selection-aware coverage $C^*$ after penalizing the dictionary description length by $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and using a join threshold $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  The finite numerical summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{base} &= 4759,\qquad n_{join}=1800,\qquad n_{available}=4758,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 24,\qquad \lambda_{DL}=0.01,\qquad \rho_{join}=0.05,\\
 C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
 1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946,\\
 C_{raw,best} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
 N^{fixed}_{0.95} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
 N^{selection}_{0.95} &= 0.029149007242956115.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run therefore separates three quantities that should not be conflated: the raw best dictionary fit, the selection-aware null envelope, and the reported coverage after the dictionary search has been accounted for.  Since $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ is far above the selection-aware $95\%$ null level $0.029149007242956115$, the measured readout dictionary explains a real portion of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual signal.  Since the remaining fraction is $0.7546178856026946$, the same certificate also says that most of the target residual is not absorbed by this small measured dictionary.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The selected interpretation is partial reducibility to the measured readouts $\{\text{measured TE},\text{mRNA stability},\text{turnover}\}$ under a size cap of three.  This is a finite statistical compression statement, not a full mechanistic account.  The direct protein-omics surface shows that the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates survive against measured PAXdb protein abundance in a broad powered panel: for example $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse, with all nine coordinates marked surviving at the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold.  These direct entries motivate the dictionary question, but they do not by themselves identify a pathway.

\paragraph{Translation and abundance contacts.}
Measured ribosome-TE contacts provide a separate translation-layer readout.  In the measured-TE survival run, yeast has $n=5128$ genes with measured TE, a ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ after the length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  The same measured-TE statistic is $0.009394266754822262$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish.  When measured TE and protein abundance are jointly fitted, the yeast measured mediation fraction is $0.4793069656345167$, whereas \emph{E. coli} is $0.04091158340950673$, human is $0.0027966485036786744$, and zebrafish is truncated to $0$ from a raw suppressive value $-0.04801486048721136$.  Thus the yeast dictionary certificate is consistent with a real measured-TE component, but it is not exhausted by measured TE alone.

\paragraph{Stability, dwell, localization, and turnover context.}
The joint TE--mRNA-stability analysis gives a yeast joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  For comparison, \emph{E. coli} has joint mediation fraction $0.2404669586626661$ and human has $0.16890712099923358$, with the same right-tail permutation probability.  A separate yeast $f_3$-stress dwell analysis joins $1257$ rows, reports base held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a deterministic row-label permutation null with $N_{0.95}=0.0013115362189692937$ and $p\approx 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Its positional version identifies the $5'$ segment as dominant, with $5'$ passing and $3'$ failing.  These values explain why a small readout dictionary can capture a nonzero fraction of the protein-abundance residual without collapsing it to a single universal translation story.

\paragraph{Bounded conclusion.}
The certified conclusion is that, on the powered yeast join, the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual signal is real and partly reducible to a dictionary of at most three measured readouts.  Numerically, the covered fraction is $0.24538211439730545$ and the unexplained fraction is $0.7546178856026946$.  The module class is therefore partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary: it is stronger than a direct $Q\to P$ association and weaker than a complete cross-layer mechanism.  Direct protein-abundance survival remains a predictive association unless a measured translation readout survives and attenuates the direct $Q\to P$ component.  The present certificate does not imply folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, causal mechanism, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law.  Those claims require their own layer-matched empirical contact and their own controlled finite-row statistic.
