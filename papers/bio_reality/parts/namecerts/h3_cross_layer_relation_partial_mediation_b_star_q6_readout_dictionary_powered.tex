\subsection{Partial Mediation Certificate for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Readout Dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-partial-mediation-b-star-q6-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The claim is a finite-row statement about whether a measured readout dictionary accounts for a nonzero part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual signal in protein abundance.  The active source design is the $9$-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate matrix, residualized against the same sibling control basis $Z$ used by the protein-abundance and translation-readout tests.  The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on a unified Saccharomyces cerevisiae context join.  The full available join has $n=4758$ usable rows from a base yeast context of $4759$ rows, and the certificate deliberately evaluates a fixed working join of $n=1800$ rows.  The control basis has rank $24$, the held-out design uses $5$ folds, the dictionary penalty is $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and the join threshold is $\rho_{join}=0.05$.

\paragraph{Dictionary reduction statistic.}
Let $Y_Z$ denote the residualized protein-abundance target and let $X_Q$ denote the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ source design.  The tested dictionary family is the single-codon readout family produced by the minimal-dictionary compression run, with candidate measured readouts including measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The certificate reports the best selection-aware dictionary coverage $C^*$, the raw best family coverage, and the residual fraction left outside the selected dictionary:
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
C_{raw} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The selection-aware null threshold is $0.029149007242956115$ from $120$ deterministic permutations, while the naive fixed-subset null threshold is $0.00598374993486675$.  Thus the finite certificate is not merely a best-subset artifact: the selected dictionary explains about $24.54\%$ of the residual $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal, and this exceeds the family-selection null boundary by a wide margin.  The same calculation also records that about $75.46\%$ of the residual signal remains outside the selected measured readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein-omics survival.}
The partial-mediation certificate is meaningful only because the upstream direct protein-omics surface is already nonzero on powered organism panels.  In the direct $Q\to P$ survival run, all $15$ reported organisms were rank sufficient with $\operatorname{rank}(X_Q)=9$ after controls and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.  The protein-abundance readout was measured PAXdb $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ joined to real CDS codon counts.  Representative controlled $R^2_{QP}$ values were $0.29837624994140827$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $0.10767526677985896$ in Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.10217906758013379$ in Caenorhabditis elegans, $0.0646471273126996$ in Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, $0.049827984062539195$ in Mus musculus, $0.04454565118019448$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.019256877907572044$ in Halobacterium salinarum.  The yeast join hit rate for this direct protein readout was $0.9156038419146592$ with $5815$ joined proteins, while the human join hit rate was $0.9779294769799313$ with $19053$ joined proteins.  These finite rows justify treating $Y_Z$ as a real measured protein-abundance target, but they do not by themselves identify a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Measured translation and mediation checks.}
The same coordinate family also survives against measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency readouts.  In the measured TE run, the readout was $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$.  The controlled measured-TE survival values included $R^2_{QT}=0.13681931343730436$ for yeast on $5128$ genes with a ribosome-TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$, $R^2_{QT}=0.009394266754822262$ for E. coli on $3415$ genes with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$, $R^2_{QT}=0.018837911344127926$ for human on $11554$ genes with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$, and $R^2_{QT}=0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish on $13140$ genes with hit rate $0.9118667591950035$.  The measured $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition is strongest in yeast: $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  The corresponding fractions are $0.04091158340950673$ for E. coli, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0$ for zebrafish after clipping the negative raw fraction $-0.04801486048721136$ to the unit interval.  These numbers explain why the dictionary certificate is classified as partial rather than complete: measured TE is a real contributor in yeast, but not a universal absorber across the available panels.

\paragraph{Joint readout structure.}
A separate joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition supports the same interpretation.  In yeast, the single and joint residual-abundance values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
This gives a joint mediation fraction of $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In E. coli the corresponding joint fraction is $0.2404669586626661$, mostly stability-only at $0.23052056324898387$, while in human it is $0.16890712099923358$, with stability-only fraction $0.15314679870183337$.  The overlap mode was approximately independent in all three organisms under the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  The dictionary certificate therefore sits at the intersection of two finite findings: yeast has a strong measured-TE absorption component, while cross-organism panels show that mRNA stability can also absorb a nontrivial part of the direct $Q\to P$ signal.

\paragraph{Local readout refinements.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate supplies a more localized example of a measured readout reducing a protein-abundance component.  In yeast, the dwell-mediation test used $n=1257$ joined rows, $5$ folds, and $120$ deterministic permutations.  The base model held-out $R^2$ was $0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-alone $R^2$ was $0.034336580953121976$, and the full model held-out $R^2$ was $0.20412261520502883$, giving a positive held-out increment $0.01856473944057712$.  The null $95\%$ threshold was $0.0013115362189692937$, and the approximate $f_3$-dwell association value was $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ with correlation $0.1615441450901653$.  The target component was the projection of residualized protein abundance onto residualized $f_3$-stress, with $\|P_{f3}\|^2=44.20095750389323$ and fraction $0.1999063916718794$ of the target.  This local result is compatible with the dictionary certificate because it identifies a finite measured readout that reduces part of the same abundance-side residual, without exhausting it.

\paragraph{Four-case classification boundary.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant gives a useful warning about interpretation.  In yeast, all $9$ coordinates were classified as case $D$ on the modeled-tAI and measured-abundance surface, with $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  In E. coli, $7$ coordinates were case $D$ and $2$ were positive-positive without mediation drop, with $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$.  These are objective finite classifications over the selected modeled translation readout and protein-abundance readout, not a license to identify a physical pathway.  The partial-mediation certificate uses the stricter measured-readout dictionary and therefore reports a smaller, selection-aware coverage value rather than promoting the modeled-tAI classification into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready.  The selected measured readout dictionary accounts for $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal in the powered yeast context, above the selection-aware null $0.029149007242956115$, with $0.7546178856026946$ left unexplained.  The result is a partial reducibility statement: measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover-like readouts capture a nonzero part of the direct $Q\to P$ association, but they do not collapse the residual coordinate family to known measured readouts.  No translation law, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this certificate.  Such claims require their own layer-matched contacts and finite statistics; this chapter records only the measured dictionary coverage of a controlled protein-abundance residual.
