\subsection{Partial Readout-Dictionary Mediation of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Protein-Abundance Residual}\label{subsec:biorality-q6-partial-readout-dictionary-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the protein-abundance component carried by the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface can be compressed by a small measured-readout dictionary.  The source design is the $9$-coordinate synonymous residual matrix, with coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress.  Each coordinate is residualized against the same sibling control basis used in the protein-abundance and translation-readout runs: length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  On the unified yeast context join the rank of this control basis is $24$, the available join contains $4758$ rows, and the certificate fit uses $n=1800$ rows with $5$ folds.

\paragraph{Dictionary certificate.}
The measured readout family is the single-codon readout dictionary generated by the minimal dictionary compression run.  The selected certificate is not a full explanation of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual.  It is a partial reduction statement: the best selection-aware dictionary of at most three measured readouts, reported as measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover, covers $C_{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ of the target component.  The unexplained remainder is therefore $0.7546178856026946$.  The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$, but the selection-aware null must be charged because the subset is chosen after scanning the family.  The fixed penalty values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{DL} &= 0.01,\\
\rho_{join} &= 0.05,\\
N_{perm} &= 120,\\
C_{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
C_{raw} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
C_{null,95}^{selection} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
C_{null,95}^{fixed} &= 0.00598374993486675.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $C_{\ast}$ is far above the selection-aware $95\%$ null level, the finite-row verdict is that the residual protein-abundance signal is real and partially reducible to the measured dictionary.  Since $C_{\ast}<1$, the same finite-row verdict also says that most of the target component is still outside the three-readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance contact.}
The target readout is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}\to P$ surface is positive across the rank-powered protein-abundance panel, but direct protein-omics association is only a downstream statistical readout.  The per-organism $R^2_{QP}$ list is $0.061906245886660105$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.10217906758013379$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.04613529807085213$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.08141786718177903$ for \emph{Dictyostelium discoideum}, $0.0770952887136557$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.03980793596647631$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $0.019256877907572044$ for \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, $0.03209009170649934$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.0646471273126996$ for \emph{Pseudomonas aeruginosa}, $0.02763998603083845$ for rat, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, and $0.06397514251295629$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In each of these protein-abundance fits all $9$ residual coordinates survive the positive threshold.  The yeast protein-abundance join uses $5815$ CDS rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; the partial dictionary certificate then restricts to the unified yeast context join for the readout-family test.

\paragraph{Translation and mediation checks.}
Measured ribosome-derived translation efficiency supplies the translation-side contact used to separate translation-linked signal from direct protein-omics prediction.  The measured $S^{QT}$ list is $R^2_{QT}=0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, $0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ for human, and $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, with $9$ surviving coordinates in each organism.  The matched measured-TE joins contain $13140$ zebrafish genes, $3415$ \emph{E. coli} genes, $11554$ human genes, and $5128$ yeast genes; the corresponding reported TE join hit rates are $0.9118667591950035$, $0.9133458143888741$, $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.8818572656921754$.

The measured mediation list is more selective than the direct $Q\to P$ list.  The measured mediated fractions are $0$ for zebrafish, $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  The yeast line is therefore the strong same-row example: $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT}^{meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and the measured mediation fraction is $0.4793069656345167$.  Joint conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability gives a compatible variance-partition picture in yeast: $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$; the TE-only and stability-only fractions are $0.4697188319693239$ and $0.0626618595344251$.

\paragraph{Auxiliary readout localization.}
Two additional readouts refine the residual rather than closing it.  For the yeast $f_3$ stress abundance component, the dwell readout has $n=1257$, $5$ folds, base held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$.  The deterministic row-label null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, $95\%$ level $0.0013115362189692937$, and maximum $0.005001350039772926$ over $120$ permutations; the $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The position-resolved dwell test assigns the leading segment to the $5'$ window: the $5'$ segment passes, the $3'$ body segment does not, and the primary positional conclusion is a $5'$-dominant dwell increment.

Human turnover and localization give examples of candidate readouts that do not close the residual.  In the human turnover test, the large modeled-complement abundance residual has $d_{modeled}=0.9208949355679455$, but the turnover absorption is only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, below a one-dimensional null with mean $0.0001262572958089998$ and $95\%$ level $0.0004342285066808464$.  In the localization tests, yeast localization absorbs $0.011225105608516642$ with a degree-of-freedom matched null $95\%$ level $0.004958116973149795$, human localization absorbs $0.009254913329269913$ against null $95\%=0.0013758521256775384$, and zebrafish absorbs $0.009508864544725801$ against null $95\%=0.011321328712802819$.  These measurements show that some residual components can be associated with measured state annotations, but they do not convert the dictionary certificate into a complete biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For this chapter the finite-row readback is case-specific.  A coordinate in case A would survive translation but not protein abundance; case B would survive protein abundance only through the measured translation path; case C would survive protein abundance without measured translation absorption; and case D would show both translation survival and protein-abundance survival with measured attenuation of the direct path.  The yeast measurements place the aggregate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual in the partially mediated region: measured TE and mRNA stability explain a substantial fraction, the three-readout dictionary covers $0.24538211439730545$, and the unexplained fraction remains $0.7546178856026946$.  Thus the chapter supports a bounded statement: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual is not merely a rank artifact or a direct protein-omics-only association, but neither is it exhausted by the measured translation, stability, and turnover dictionary.

\paragraph{Local scope.}
The result is a finite statistical certificate on matched rows, not a causal pathway.  Measured TE, mRNA stability, turnover, dwell, localization, and protein abundance are noisy cross-sectional readouts, often drawn from different studies or conditions.  No claim about translation causation, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from this chapter.  Such claims would require a separate layer-matched measurement surface and a separate controlled statistic at that layer.  The admissible conclusion here is narrower: on the rank-powered yeast panel, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual is significant, partially reducible to a small measured-readout dictionary, and still mostly unexplained after that reduction.
