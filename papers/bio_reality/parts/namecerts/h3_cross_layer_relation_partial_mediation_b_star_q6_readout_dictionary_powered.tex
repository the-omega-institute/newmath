\subsection{Rank-powered partial mediation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The readout dictionary certificate is a finite-row cross-layer record for the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis on a yeast matched context.  The source design is the $9$-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate surface residualized against the same sibling control matrix $Z$, with coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress.  The response is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The available join has $4758$ rows, the base surface records $4759$ rows, and the tested certificate uses $n=1800$ rows over $5$ folds with $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and a deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256}:\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_partial\_mediation\_certificate\_powered}:\mathrm{deterministic}$.  The control basis has rank $24$.

\paragraph{Dictionary reduction result.}
The candidate reduction family is the single-codon readout dictionary family generated by the minimal dictionary compression computation.  The best selected dictionary has the measured readouts $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$ and is constrained to at most three readouts.  Its selection-aware coverage is $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, while the raw best coverage before accounting for selection over the family is $0.2745002077400602$.  The fixed-subset null threshold is much smaller, $0.00598374993486675$, and the selection-aware null $95$th percentile is $0.029149007242956115$.  Thus the certified dictionary coverage is well above the selected-family null, but it leaves most of the residual unexplained:
$$\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
N_{95}^{\mathrm{select}} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}$$
The finite verdict is therefore neither full reduction nor irreducibility.  It is a partial-reduction certificate: a real residual protein-abundance signal is captured in part by the measured translation-efficiency, mRNA-stability, and turnover dictionary, but $75.46178856026946\%$ of the certified target component remains outside that three-readout explanation.

\paragraph{Consistency with measured translation and protein rows.}
The dictionary certificate is anchored by separate finite-row readouts rather than by symbolic promotion.  Direct protein-omics survival over measured PAXdb abundance reports $15$ organisms with all $9$ residual coordinates surviving after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  The yeast direct protein-abundance entry has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with $5815$ joined CDS/protein rows and join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; human has $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ over $19053$ joined rows; mouse has $R^2_{QP}=0.049827984062539195$ over $19395$ joined rows; and \emph{E. coli} K12 has $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ over $3739$ joined rows.  Measured ribosome translation-efficiency survival is also finite: yeast has $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with $5128$ genes carrying measured TE and a TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, \emph{E. coli} K12 has $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ with $3415$ TE genes and hit rate $0.9133458143888741$, human has $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.018837911344127926$ with $11554$ TE genes and hit rate $0.6064136881331024$, and zebrafish has $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$ with $13140$ TE genes and hit rate $0.9118667591950035$.

\paragraph{Mediation and four-case placement.}
Measured translation conditioning shows that the dictionary has a real translation component but not a universal absorbing explanation.  In yeast, the measured mediation run gives $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$ on $5128$ three-way joined rows.  \emph{E. coli} K12 gives $S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$, and mediated fraction $0.04091158340950673$ on $3415$ joined rows.  Human gives $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.018837911344127926$, and mediated fraction $0.0027966485036786744$ on $11554$ joined rows.  Zebrafish has positive translation and protein surfaces, $S_{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$ and $S_{QP}=0.04926578454614652$, but the raw mediated fraction is negative and is clipped to $0$.  The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant places yeast in case $D$ for all $9$ coordinates, while \emph{E. coli} places $7$ coordinates in case $D$ and $2$ coordinates in the positive-positive-without-mediation-drop class.  This classification is a finite readout label: it says which rows survive which conditioning tests, not that a molecular mechanism has been established.

\paragraph{Joint dictionary context.}
A separate joint translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability decomposition explains why the selected dictionary uses more than translation alone.  In yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  In human, the corresponding joint mediation fraction is $0.16890712099923358$, with TE-only $0.00549490864190343$ and stability-only $0.15314679870183337$; in \emph{E. coli}, it is $0.2404669586626661$, with TE-only $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only $0.23052056324898387$.  The dictionary certificate therefore reads as a measured reduction family over translation, mRNA stability, and turnover, not as a single-channel translation account.

\paragraph{Residual localization, dwell, turnover, and structural tests.}
The remaining signal has finite contacts that keep it from being silently promoted.  The yeast $f_3$ dwell mediation run joins $1257$ rows, has $P_{f3}$ fraction $0.1999063916718794$, $P_{f3}$ norm squared $44.20095750389323$, base held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a permutation null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell run again uses $1257$ joined rows and identifies the $5'$ segment as dominant; the $5'$ window passes while the $3'$ body window does not.  The structural-order ramp test is weaker: at the primary $50$-codon ramp, weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, ramp sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, and paired ramp-minus-body $p=0.05859375$, giving the local verdict $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$.  Human turnover does not absorb the large modeled complement: $R^2_{Q,Tturn}=0.004615408769189838$, absorbed fraction $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, and the dof-matched null has $p95=0.0004342285066808464$ with $200$ trials, so the tested turnover readout is not the missing explanation.  Coarse localization is only a descriptive absorber: yeast absorbs $0.011225105608516642$ against null $p95=0.004958116973149795$, human absorbs $0.009254913329269913$ against $0.0013758521256775384$, and zebrafish absorbs $0.009508864544725801$ against $0.011321328712802819$.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The ready claim is narrowly finite-row.  The certificate supports a selection-aware partial reduction of the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual to a small measured readout dictionary, with substantial residual mass left over.  It does not by itself assert translation realization, folding order, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, or a universal biological rule.  Those stronger readings require their own layer-matched measurements and their own controlled finite-row statistics.  The same local caution applies to auxiliary rows: the miRNA seed-density repression contact has $11$ miRNAs, $56682$ pairs, and one-second runtime, but it is a separate repression readout rather than evidence that the $B^*_{Q6}$ dictionary explains protein function or organismal outcome.
