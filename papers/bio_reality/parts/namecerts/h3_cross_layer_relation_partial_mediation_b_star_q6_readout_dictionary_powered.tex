\subsection{Selection-aware partial mediation by a small readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Claim and finite surface.}
The record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.partial\_mediation.b\_star\_q6\_readout\_dictionary\_powered} is a finite-row statement about the part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual that can be recovered by a small dictionary of measured biological readouts.  The source design is the nine-dimensional synonymous residual coordinate surface $B^*_{Q6}$, residualized against the same sibling control basis used in the surrounding protein-abundance tests.  The target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The active join has $n=1800$ rows, drawn from $4758$ available joined rows against a base panel size of $4759$, with $5$ folds.  The control basis has rank $24$.  The compression penalty is $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and the join-retention parameter is $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.

\paragraph{Dictionary result.}
The tested dictionary is the single-codon readout family produced by \texttt{run\_b\_star\_q6\_minimal\_dictionary\_compression\_powered.py}.  The selected explanatory set has at most three measured readouts, namely measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and protein turnover.  Before selection adjustment, the best candidate family covers $0.2745002077400602$ of the residual signal.  After the selection-aware correction, the accepted coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed-subset null threshold is $0.00598374993486675$, while the selection-aware null threshold is $0.029149007242956115$ over $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  Thus the finite claim is not that the dictionary exhausts the residual.  It is that a nontrivial and selection-aware significant fraction of the residual is reducible to a small measured-readout set, while roughly three quarters remains outside that dictionary on the tested yeast surface.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance contact.}
The direct protein-omics surface supplies the downstream abundance readout.  In the same organism, measured PAXdb protein abundance joins to $5815$ coding-sequence rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  After length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls, yeast has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates surviving the direct protein-abundance screen.  This direct survival is a bounded association with measured protein abundance; it is not by itself a translation, turnover, localization, folding, functional, or causal claim.

\paragraph{Measured translation and mediation context.}
The measured ribosome-translation-efficiency contact is available on the yeast row set with $5128$ genes used and ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  Its controlled survival value is $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, again with $9$ surviving coordinates.  In the measured TE-to-protein mediation decomposition, yeast has $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, raw measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$, and clipped measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  The associated rank diagnostics have $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Q,T)=10$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, measured-TE residual energy $753.5781168800304$, and protein-abundance residual energy $2146.0645009193045$.  These values explain why measured translation efficiency is eligible as one dictionary readout, but they do not make the dictionary result a mechanism proof.

\paragraph{Stability and turnover context.}
The joint TE--mRNA-stability decomposition gives a second reason why the dictionary can reduce part of the abundance residual.  For yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The resulting joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The overlap diagnostic reports approximate additivity within tolerance: the single-mediator sum is $0.532380691503749$, the joint-minus-single-sum difference is $-0.012862468941589822$, and the descriptive tolerance is $0.05$.  Human turnover is also informative as a boundary case: on $8057$ joined human rows, projection onto log half-life gives absorbed fraction $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, below the dof-matched null $95$th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$.  Turnover is therefore a dictionary readout category, not a universal absorber of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The modeled translation-conditioned four-case surface supplies a useful classification check.  In yeast, all $9$ coordinates fall into case D under the modeled-tAI/protein-abundance readback, with no positive-positive entries lacking a mediation drop.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $7$ coordinates fall into case D and $2$ entries are positive-positive without a mediation drop.  The yeast modeled surface has $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$; the E. coli modeled surface has $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$.  This classification is a finite readout of which residual coordinates behave as translation-conditioned protein-abundance signals under the chosen modeled translation surface.  It is not a declaration that every protein-abundance association has a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The local conclusion is therefore partial and deliberately narrow.  On the tested yeast finite-row panel, a selection-aware dictionary containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover accounts for $0.24538211439730545$ of the residual protein-abundance signal, leaving $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained by that dictionary.  Direct protein abundance, measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover are separate empirical readouts.  No claim about translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal direction, or universal biology follows from this chapter unless a layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and the same residualized finite-row test supports that layer.
