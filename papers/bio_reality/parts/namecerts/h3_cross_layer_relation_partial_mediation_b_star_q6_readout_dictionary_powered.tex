\subsection{Readout-dictionary partial mediation for the yeast $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-readout-dictionary-partial-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The tested object is the yeast row surface obtained by joining coding-sequence codon counts, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, measured translation efficiency, mRNA-stability readouts, turnover-style readouts, and protein abundance on the same controlled context.  The source design is the $9$-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate block residualized against the sibling control basis $Z$: intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The active certificate is a Saccharomyces cerevisiae computation with $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$ rows sampled from $4758$ available joined rows, base row count $4759$, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and join retention parameter $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.  The fitted target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on this unified yeast context join, and the control basis has rank $24$.

\paragraph{Dictionary certificate.}
The readout dictionary search was run over the single-codon readout dictionary family and asked whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual can be compressed by a small measured-readout subset.  The best raw coverage over the candidate family was $0.2745002077400602$, while the selected certificate coverage was
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\star} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{\star} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The selection-aware null threshold was $0.029149007242956115$, and the naive fixed-subset null threshold was $0.00598374993486675$, both computed from $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  Thus the reported module class is not a complete mediation class: it is a partial-reduction class.  The finite row result says that a subset of at most three measured readouts, namely measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover, captures a statistically non-null part of the abundance residual, but roughly three quarters of the residual variance component remains outside that dictionary.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein survival.}
The same coordinate family has a strong direct protein-abundance association in yeast before readout conditioning.  On the PAXdb protein-abundance join, Saccharomyces cerevisiae used $5815$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and all nine coordinates survived the positive partial-$R^{2}$ threshold.  The direct controlled protein-abundance score was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
This direct value is useful only as the upstream abundance residual to be explained.  It does not by itself identify a translation mechanism, a protein-state mechanism, or a causal route.  The readout-dictionary certificate instead asks how much of that controlled abundance association can be reduced by measured finite readouts after the common residualization protocol.

\paragraph{Measured translation and mediation coordinates.}
Measured ribosome-derived translation efficiency is available on the yeast surface with $5128$ genes carrying TE readouts, ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and residual degrees of freedom $5105$ after the $Z$ controls.  The measured TE survival score for the nine-coordinate block is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
When measured TE and protein abundance are placed in the same three-way mediation surface, yeast has $5128$ joined rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, raw measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$, and clipped measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  This agrees with the dictionary certificate in the limited sense that measured translation readouts explain a real component of the protein-abundance residual.  It does not make the residual vanish.

\paragraph{Joint readout decomposition.}
The broader three-organism decomposition separates measured TE and mRNA stability.  In yeast, the single and joint controlled scores were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding fractions were $0.4697188319693239$ for the TE-only component, $0.0626618595344251$ for the stability-only component, and $0.5195182225621592$ for the joint TE-plus-stability component, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  For comparison, Escherichia coli had joint fraction $0.2404669586626661$ with TE-only $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only $0.23052056324898387$, while human had joint fraction $0.16890712099923358$ with TE-only $0.00549490864190343$ and stability-only $0.15314679870183337$; both reported the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These finite rows justify the dictionary interpretation: the reducible part is readout-dependent, not a single universal translation coefficient.

\paragraph{Irreducibility status.}
A separate yeast irreducibility check used the same $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and rank-$24$ control basis.  Its status semantics classify the registered source signal as real while allowing listed reduction families to refute a stronger irreducibility hypothesis.  The readout-dictionary certificate is therefore the more precise local statement: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual is neither null nor fully explained by the available measured-readout dictionary.  It is partially reducible, with $C^{\star}=0.24538211439730545$, and still leaves $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained on this finite yeast surface.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The certificate is a finite statistical decomposition over matched rows.  It supports a controlled association and a partial readout reduction, not a causal biological mechanism.  No claim about translation realization beyond the measured TE statistic, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality follows from these numbers.  Such claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled finite-row statistics.  In particular, the positive direct $q_i \to P_k$ association is not promoted to a mechanism unless the measured $q_i \to T_j$ step is present and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the downstream $q_i \to P_k$ signal under the same control discipline.
