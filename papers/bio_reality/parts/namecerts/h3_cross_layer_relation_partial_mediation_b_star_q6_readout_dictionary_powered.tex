\subsection{Partial readout compression of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The claim is evaluated on the Saccharomyces cerevisiae matched context in which the source design is the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate matrix after the same sibling control residualization used for the direct abundance and translation-efficiency assays.  The active target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified yeast context join.  The registered run uses $n_{\mathrm{available}}=4758$ joined rows, a working sample size $n=1800$, five held-out folds, control-basis rank $24$, dictionary penalty $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, join floor $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  The source coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, all read only after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density style controls have been removed.

\paragraph{Observed dictionary certificate.}
The readout dictionary family is the single-codon readout family generated by the minimal dictionary compression run.  Over that family the raw best coverage of the residual protein-abundance signal is $0.2745002077400602$, while the selection-adjusted accepted coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\star} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
\mathrm{unexplained} &= 1-C^{\star}=0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed-subset null has $95\%$ cutoff $0.00598374993486675$, and the selection-aware null has $95\%$ cutoff $0.029149007242956115$.  Thus the observed coverage is not a rank-saturated all-positive artifact and not a mere consequence of trying the candidate family.  It is a finite certificate that a small measured-readout dictionary, here reported as no more than three named entries $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$, absorbs a positive but minority fraction of the yeast $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual.  The complementary fraction remains large: about $75.46\%$ of the residual component is not compressed by this dictionary under the registered selection correction.

The unresolved part is not an empty remainder. \paragraph{Local verdict and no-promotion rule.}
This chapter therefore records a ready finite result for partial mediation by a small measured-readout dictionary in yeast.  The accepted coverage $C^{\star}=0.24538211439730545$ is far above the selection-aware null cutoff $0.029149007242956115$, and the run status is passed.  The result does not say that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ determines translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, evolution, or a universal biological law.  It says only that, on the registered yeast finite-row surface, a selected dictionary containing measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover-type readouts explains a statistically nontrivial minority of the controlled $Q\to P$ residual, while $0.7546178856026946$ remains outside that explanation.  Any stronger biological interpretation requires its own layer-matched measurement table and a new controlled statistic at that layer.
