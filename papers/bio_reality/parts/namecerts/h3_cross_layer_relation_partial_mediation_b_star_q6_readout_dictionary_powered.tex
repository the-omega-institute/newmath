\subsection{A partial mediation certificate for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ readout dictionary}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite yeast row set in which the source design is the $9$-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface, residualized against the same sibling control basis used in the associated abundance and translation readout computations.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The available unified context join contains $4758$ rows, while the certificate is computed on $n=1800$ joined rows with $5$ held-out folds.  The target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on this unified yeast context.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the readout dictionary is the single-codon readout dictionary family produced by the minimal dictionary compression run.  The certificate uses the finite regularization and join thresholds
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}} &= 0.01,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{join}} &= 0.05,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 120.
\end{aligned}
$$
The deterministic seed is recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_partial\_mediation\_certificate\_powered{:}deterministic}$, so the row choice, folds, and permutation comparison are fixed finite operations rather than an informal biological interpretation.

\paragraph{Certificate value.}
The best selection-aware dictionary certificate covers a strict positive part of the residual abundance signal but not all of it.  The reported selected coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{*} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{*} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$.  The fixed-subset null boundary is much smaller, with naive $95\%$ value $0.00598374993486675$, and the selection-aware $95\%$ boundary is $0.029149007242956115$.  Thus the selected dictionary reduction is not an artifact of scanning the finite candidate family under the reported null.  The correct readback is partial reducibility: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual is real on this yeast join and a measured dictionary of at most three readouts, namely measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and protein turnover, accounts for about one quarter of the target component.  The remaining fraction, about $75.46\%$, is still outside that dictionary on this finite design.

\paragraph{Relationship to direct protein abundance.}
The direct $Q \to P$ surface is already positive in the matched protein-abundance experiment.  Across the powered organism panel, every one of the $9$ coordinates survives the direct protein-omics readout after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  In yeast specifically, the PAXdb join reports $5815$ joined CDS/protein rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X_{Q\mid Z})=9$.  The yeast direct abundance value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving }Q\hbox{ coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
This direct signal is not, by itself, a translation claim.  It is a finite association between residual codon coordinates and measured protein abundance after the stated covariates, and it is used here only as the abundance target whose reducibility by measured readouts is being quantified.

\paragraph{Measured translation and abundance mediation.}
The measured ribosome-profiling TE readout gives a separate translation-layer contact on four organisms.  In yeast, the TE join uses $5128$ genes out of the $5815$ reported CDS/protein rows, with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  After the same class of controls, the yeast measured translation survival is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving }Q\hbox{ coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured TE to protein-abundance mediation computation then joins $5128$ yeast rows three ways and reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cross-organism measured mediation list is heterogeneous: danio gives raw $M^{QTP}=-0.04801486048721136$ and clipped measured fraction $0$, \emph{E. coli} gives $0.04091158340950673$, human gives $0.0027966485036786744$, and yeast gives $0.4793069656345167$.  This places yeast as the strongest measured-TE mediator in the finite four-organism panel, while still leaving room for non-TE dictionary components and unexplained residual structure.

\paragraph{Joint TE--mRNA-stability readback.}
The joint stability computation gives a second finite mediation check using measured TE and mRNA stability.  In yeast the four-way join contains $4759$ rows, with $5128$ valid TE genes, $5379$ valid mRNA half-life records, and a reported join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  The single and joint quantities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding fractions are $0.4697188319693239$ for the measured-TE-only component, $0.0626618595344251$ for the stability-only component, and $0.5195182225621592$ jointly, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The overlap diagnostic reports joint minus single-sum $-0.012862468941589822$, inside the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$, so the two measured readout directions behave approximately additively for this finite readout partition.  This supports the dictionary interpretation: a nontrivial part of the abundance residual is reducible to measured TE and stability, but the reduction is incomplete.

\paragraph{Four-case and dwell diagnostics.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant is consistent with treating direct protein survival cautiously.  In yeast, the modeled readout has $R^{2}_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, with all $9$ coordinates classified in case D and no positive-positive entry without a mediation drop.  In \emph{E. coli}, $7$ coordinates are case D and $2$ positive-positive entries lack the mediation drop.  This contrast keeps the yeast record in the mediated side of the four-case readback, while preventing a universal statement about every organism.

The $f3\_stress$ positional and dwell records give additional finite structure inside yeast.  On the dwell join, $n=1257$ rows are used over $5$ folds, the $f3\_stress$ dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$, and the held-out increment from adding dwell readouts is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^{2}_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^{2}_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed row-label null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95\%$ value $0.0013115362189692937$.  The positional run identifies the $5'$ segment as leading: the $5'$ ramp passes, the $3'$ body does not, and the dominant segment is $5'$.

\paragraph{Higher-layer refusal.}
The partial mediation certificate is deliberately narrow.  It does not license translation in organisms lacking a measured translation-readout row, and it does not turn protein abundance into folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Localization and turnover checks illustrate the boundary.  Human turnover joins $8057$ rows and reports only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ turnover absorption against a null $95\%$ value $0.0004342285066808464$, so that candidate does not exceed its null boundary.  Localization absorption is computed in yeast, human, and danio with $12$ localization columns; yeast absorbs $0.011225105608516642$ against a dof-matched null $95\%$ of $0.004958116973149795$, human absorbs $0.009254913329269913$ against $0.0013758521256775384$, and danio absorbs $0.009508864544725801$ against $0.011321328712802819$.  These are layer-specific projections, not causal explanations.

The structural-order ramp record is also bounded: it reports a positive ramp signal but not localization of that signal, with weighted $5'$ ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, ramp sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip $p=0.05859375$.  Therefore the present chapter closes only the following statement: on a finite yeast design, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual has selection-aware partial coverage by a small measured readout dictionary, with substantial residual mass still unexplained.  Any stronger assertion must wait for the separate layer-matched measurement that directly tests that stronger layer.
