\subsection{Partial mediation of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual by a measured readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record tests a yeast finite-row surface in which the source design is the nine-dimensional synonymous residual coordinate family $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$, residualized against the same control basis $Z$ used by the sibling protein-abundance and translation-readout computations.  The active organism is \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The target is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the sibling $Z$ controls on the unified yeast context join.  The available joined context contains $4758$ rows, the fitted held-out certificate uses $n=1800$ rows, the base panel size is $4759$, the cross-validation split count is $5$, and the residual-control basis has rank $24$.  The dictionary family is the single-codon readout family generated for minimal dictionary compression, and the certificate allows at most three measured readouts from the named set consisting of measured ribosome-based translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.

\paragraph{Compression statistic.}
The finite certificate reports a passed status under the deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_partial\_mediation\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}.  The description-length penalty is $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, the join floor is $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and the selection null uses $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  The best selected readout dictionary covers a selection-adjusted fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{\ast} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The unadjusted best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$.  The fixed-subset null threshold is $0.00598374993486675$, while the selection-aware $95\%$ null threshold is $0.029149007242956115$.  Thus the reported coverage is not a degenerate dictionary-search artifact at the registered threshold: the selected dictionary explains a real, but incomplete, portion of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual.

\paragraph{Readout classification.}
The result is a partial-reduction certificate, not an annihilation certificate.  Its local classification is that the residual signal is real and reducible in part to a dictionary of no more than three measured readouts, while approximately $75.46\%$ of the registered residual component remains outside that selected dictionary.  In the companion direct protein-omics surface, yeast has $5815$ protein-abundance rows joined at hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and the direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\text{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured ribosome-TE surface for yeast uses $5128$ genes with TE, with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\text{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured translation-conditioned abundance decomposition on the same organism reports $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and a measured mediation fraction $M_{QTP,\mathrm{meas}}=0.4793069656345167$.  A second yeast variance partition using measured TE and mRNA stability gives TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, joint fraction $0.5195182225621592$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  These companion numbers agree with the dictionary certificate in one specific sense: translation and stability readouts absorb a substantial part of the abundance residual, but do not exhaust it.

\paragraph{Dwell and positional support.}
The readout dictionary also sits beside a yeast $f_3$ dwell certificate on $1257$ joined rows.  There the base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-only readout has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment is $0.01856473944057712$.  The deterministic permutation null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, $95\%$ threshold $0.0013115362189692937$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and the approximate $f_3$--dwell correlation probability is $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional companion classifies the dominant segment as $5'$ rather than $3'$, with five-prime ramp passing and three-prime body not passing.  This makes the dictionary result biologically sharper: it is not merely saying that a generic readout correlates with protein abundance, but that the registered residual has a measurable decomposition into translation, stability, turnover, and dwell-like surfaces, with a large leftover component still present.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready for the standalone BioReality paper as a bounded statistical claim.  On the registered yeast panel, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual is neither null nor fully mediated by the measured dictionary.  A selection-aware certificate supports a partial coverage value $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ above the $0.029149007242956115$ null boundary, while preserving an unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The correct interpretation is therefore partial mediation by measured readouts, not a complete mechanism and not a collapse of the residual coordinate family into translation alone.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This chapter only licenses the finite readout statement just described.  It does not by itself establish translation as a causal process, ribosome dynamics as a universal law, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or evolutionary direction.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association remains a protein-omics prediction unless a measured $q_i\to T_j$ step survives on matched rows and conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the protein signal.  Likewise, a dictionary coverage statistic is not a perturbation experiment, rescue assay, folding assay, localization mechanism, or function assay.  Any claim at one of those layers requires an additional layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
