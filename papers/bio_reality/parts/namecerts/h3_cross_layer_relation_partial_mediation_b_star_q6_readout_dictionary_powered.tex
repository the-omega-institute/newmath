\subsection{Selection-aware partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-readout-dictionary-partial-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite yeast row surface in which the source design is the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate matrix after the same sibling control residualization used for the abundance residual experiments. The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The row pool has $4758$ available joined records, the fitted certificate uses $n=1800$ rows, and the base context count is $4759$. The source matrix is residualized against a control basis of rank $24$, with dictionary regularization parameter $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, join threshold $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, five folds, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations. The target is the residualized $\log_{10}$ protein-abundance readout on the unified yeast context join.

\paragraph{Dictionary certificate.}
The tested readout dictionary is the measured three-channel family
$R=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$,
restricted to subsets of size at most $3$. The certificate reports positive selection-aware coverage but not full reduction of the source-to-abundance residual. Its central scalars are
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\star} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
C^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\mathrm{best}} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
N^{95}_{\mathrm{selection}} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
N^{95}_{\mathrm{fixed}} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
U &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the selected dictionary explains about $24.54\%$ of the residual signal under the selection-aware null, while about $75.46\%$ remains outside the measured dictionary. The finite conclusion is therefore partial mediation, not collapse: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual is real on this yeast join and is significantly reducible to the measured readout family, but the available measured readouts do not exhaust it.

\paragraph{Readout support.}
The same yeast surface has direct measured-TE survival and direct protein-abundance survival. For measured ribosome-derived translation efficiency, the yeast join has $5128$ genes with TE, a reported ribosome-TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, and $R^{2}_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive threshold. For direct measured protein abundance, yeast has $5815$ joined proteins, a join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, and all $9$ coordinates surviving. These two surfaces justify treating measured TE and protein abundance as present readouts on the yeast finite matrix, while still keeping them as observational readouts rather than interventions.

\paragraph{Measured mediation scale.}
On the measured TE--protein abundance join, yeast has $5128$ three-way joined rows, $6351$ valid abundance records, and $5128$ valid TE records. The measured mediation decomposition gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT}^{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{raw}} &= 0.4793069656345167,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{clipped}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
This value says that conditioning on measured TE absorbs a substantial part of the direct codon-residual to protein-abundance association in yeast. It is compatible with the dictionary certificate: measured TE contributes materially, but the dictionary-level unexplained fraction remains large.

\paragraph{Joint mediator comparison.}
A separate three-readout variance partition on the yeast four-way join reports $n=4759$ joined rows, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$. The corresponding fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_{T} &= 0.4697188319693239,\\
\mu_{M} &= 0.0626618595344251,\\
\mu_{T,M} &= 0.5195182225621592,\\
\mu_{T,M}-(\mu_T+\mu_M) &= -0.012862468941589822.
\end{aligned}
$$
The right-tail permutation value for the joint mediator statistic is $0.000999000999000999$. The overlap classification is approximately independent under the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$, so measured TE and mRNA stability behave like partly separable reductions of the same abundance residual rather than a single duplicated channel.

\paragraph{Case readback.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant also places the yeast coordinate set in the translation-positive and protein-positive stratum. Its yeast readout reports $R^{2}_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, and coordinate count $9$, with case counts
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= 0,\\
B &= 0,\\
C &= 0,\\
D &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite interpretation is not that modeled tAI proves a mechanism. It is that, on the bounded modeled-readout discriminant, all nine yeast residual coordinates fall in the positive translation-positive protein-abundance class after the declared controls. The measured-readout mediation surface then supplies the stronger finite contact for the present certificate.

\paragraph{Local dwell refinement.}
For the $f_{3}$ stress coordinate on the yeast dwell join, the same style of finite residual projection gives $n=1257$, base $n=1800$, fold count $5$, and $P_{f3}$ fraction $0.1999063916718794$. The base held-out value is $R^{2}=0.1855578757644517$, the full dwell value is $R^{2}=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment is $0.01856473944057712$. The deterministic null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and the $f_{3}$--dwell correlation has approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. This is a local measured-readout refinement of one coordinate, not a replacement for the dictionary-level partial coverage number.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The certificate is a finite observational mediation certificate over measured rows. It supports the statement that, in the yeast joined panel, a selected dictionary containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover explains a statistically non-null part of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual, while leaving most of that residual unexplained. It does not by itself establish a translation mechanism, a folding statement, physical feasibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law. Any such stronger statement would require its own layer-matched measurement surface and a separate controlled statistic. In particular, direct $q_i\to P_k$ survival is only a protein-omics association until a measured $q_i\to T_j$ readout is present and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct association on the same joined rows.
