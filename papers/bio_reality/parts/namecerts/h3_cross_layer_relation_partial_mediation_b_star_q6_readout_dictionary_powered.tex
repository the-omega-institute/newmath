\subsection{Partial readout-dictionary mediation for the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-partial-mediation-b-star-q6-readout-dictionary-powered}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual signal against measured protein abundance can be compressed by a small dictionary of measured downstream readouts, rather than being promoted directly to a mechanistic biological conclusion.  The active panel is the unified \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The available context contains $4758$ joined rows, from which the held-out certificate uses $n=1800$ rows, $5$ folds, a description-length penalty $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and a join-density parameter $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.  The source design is the $9$-dimensional synonymous residual surface
$\{K_{AAA},\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR},\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA},\operatorname{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\operatorname{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\operatorname{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\operatorname{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},f_3\}$,
residualized against the same sibling control basis.  The control basis has rank $24$ on this certificate.  The target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the same yeast context join.

\paragraph{Dictionary certificate.}
The candidate dictionary is the single-codon readout family reduced to at most three measured readouts, namely measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and protein turnover.  The selection-aware certificate finds non-vacuous compression but not exhaustion of the signal:
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
C_{\mathrm{raw}} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{sel}} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{fixed}} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statistic is therefore positive after accounting for the fact that the best subset is selected from a candidate family: $C^*>N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{sel}}$.  At the same time, the unexplained fraction remains large.  The finite-row conclusion is partial reducibility, not a full mediation identity: about $24.5\%$ of the target residual component is covered by the selected readout dictionary, while about $75.5\%$ remains outside that three-readout explanation on this panel.

\paragraph{Why the readout dictionary is needed.}
The direct yeast protein-abundance surface is strong enough to make a direct $q_i\to P$ association visible, but that association is not itself a translation mechanism.  In the direct protein-omics run, yeast has $5815$ joined CDS/protein rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, and all $9$ residual coordinates survive at the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold.  The direct yeast value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured translation-efficiency contact supplies a separate bounded translation readout.  For yeast, the ribosome-TE run uses $5128$ genes with measured TE, a TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, and all $9$ coordinates survive against $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$ after the declared controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
In the measured TE-to-protein decomposition, the yeast three-way join has $5128$ rows, and the measured mediation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus a measured translation readout absorbs a substantial part of the yeast codon-residual-to-protein-abundance association, but it does not remove the need for other readouts.

\paragraph{Relation to the joint TE and stability decomposition.}
The same boundary appears in the joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition.  On the yeast four-way join, $n=4759$ rows are available, with $5128$ genes carrying measured TE and $5379$ records carrying mRNA half-life.  The single and joint residual fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The induced fractions are $0.4697188319693239$ for measured TE alone, $0.0626618595344251$ for stability alone, and $0.5195182225621592$ jointly.  The fixed paired-readout permutation gives right-tail probability $0.000999000999000999$.  The joint-minus-single-sum diagnostic is $-0.012862468941589822$ under a descriptive tolerance of $0.05$, so the measured TE and mRNA-stability components behave approximately additively at this level of resolution.

\paragraph{Local dwell evidence for the $f_3$ component.}
The dictionary certificate also sits consistently with the yeast $f_3$ dwell readout.  In the dwell-mediated abundance run, the active join has $1257$ rows inside a base context of $1800$ rows, with $5$ folds and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  The base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the full model has held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the dwell increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\operatorname{corr}(f_3,\mathrm{dwell}) &= 0.1615441450901653,\\
p_{\mathrm{approx}} &= 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positional version places the leading increment in the $5'$ window: the $5'$ segment passes, the $3'$ body segment does not, the weighted $5'$ increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the weighted ramp-minus-body contrast is $0.001378137149572267$.  This is compatible with a readout dictionary that captures part of the abundance residual through translation- and dwell-adjacent summaries, while leaving a larger residual component unresolved.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the finite readback separates four possibilities.  A coordinate can be null for both measured translation and protein abundance, survive translation but not protein abundance, survive protein abundance without measured translation mediation, or survive both with attenuation of the direct protein signal after conditioning on a measured translation readout.  The present certificate supports the last pattern only at the level of statistical attenuation and dictionary coverage.  It does not identify a complete molecular path, and it does not claim that the selected readouts exhaust the residual.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter reports a finite statistical relation among codon-residual coordinates, measured TE, mRNA stability, turnover, dwell summaries, and protein abundance on a yeast panel.  It does not establish translation causality, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law.  Those claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  In particular, a direct $q_i\to P$ association remains a protein-omics prediction unless a measured $q_i\to T_j$ readout survives and conditioning on that measured translation readout removes or reduces the corresponding $q_i\to P$ signal under the stated controls.
