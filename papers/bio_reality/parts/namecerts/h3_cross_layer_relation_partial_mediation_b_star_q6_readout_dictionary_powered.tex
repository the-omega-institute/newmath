\subsection{Partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bior_reality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the yeast $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual can be compressed by a small measured-readout dictionary without erasing the remaining residual.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The source design is the $9$-coordinate synonymous residual surface
$\{K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, f3\_stress\}$
after residualization against the same sibling control basis.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified yeast context join.  The available yeast join contains $4758$ rows, and the tested finite panel uses $n=1800$ rows from a base frame of $4759$ rows.  The fit uses $5$ folds, a description-length penalty $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and a join threshold $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.

\paragraph{Dictionary certificate.}
The selected dictionary is the single-codon readout family generated by the minimal dictionary compression procedure.  Among the candidate readout subsets, the raw best coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\mathrm{raw}} &= 0.2745002077400602,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the selection-aware retained coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\star} &= 0.24538211439730545.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed-subset null has $95\%$ cutoff $0.00598374993486675$, and the selection-aware null has $95\%$ cutoff $0.029149007242956115$ across $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  Thus the retained coverage is not explained by the selection procedure alone.  The finite readout dictionary reduces part of the abundance residual, but it does not close it: the unexplained fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
1-C^{\star} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row-level conclusion is therefore partial reducibility, not a complete reduction of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance signal to the measured dictionary.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation-efficiency and stability readouts.}
The same yeast coordinate family also has independent finite-row contact with measured ribosome-derived translation-efficiency readouts.  On the measured-TE survival panel, yeast has $5128$ genes with TE and a reported ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; after the declared controls, the measured TE survival statistic is $R^{2}_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive threshold.  On the measured TE to protein-abundance mediation panel, the yeast three-way join has $5128$ rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  On the joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition, yeast has $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$.  These related rows show that measured TE and stability are real dictionary candidates on the same biological surface, but the partial-dictionary certificate is stricter: it asks how much of the residual can be covered by a small readout family after selection-aware null calibration.

\paragraph{Direct abundance context.}
The direct yeast protein-omics survival surface is strong but non-mechanistic.  In the direct protein-abundance run, yeast joins $5815$ CDS rows to measured PAXdb protein abundance with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  After length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls, the direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance statistic is $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, again with $9$ surviving coordinates.  The dictionary certificate does not reinterpret this direct association as a pathway.  It only says that, on the yeast finite panel, a small measured-readout family accounts for $0.24538211439730545$ of the target component above a selection-aware null boundary, while $0.7546178856026946$ remains outside that finite dictionary.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This section licenses only a finite statistical statement about the yeast readout dictionary.  It does not by itself establish translation as a biological process claim, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Those interpretations require their own layer-matched contacts and their own finite-row statistics.  In particular, the positive dictionary coverage is compatible with measured TE, mRNA stability, turnover, and other readout components carrying part of the abundance residual, but the large unexplained fraction keeps the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance surface open rather than collapsed to the listed readouts.
