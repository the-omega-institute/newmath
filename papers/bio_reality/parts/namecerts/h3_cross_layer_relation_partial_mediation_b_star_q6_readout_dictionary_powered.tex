\subsection{Partial measured-readout mediation of the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}\label{subsec:bior-reality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The object considered here is a finite yeast readout table whose source design is the nine-dimensional synonymous residual coordinate family $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$, residualized against the same sibling control system used in the powered protein-abundance runs.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the target column is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified yeast context join.  The computed table has $4759$ base rows, $4758$ available joined rows before the active subsampling step, and $1800$ rows in the fitted join.  The control basis has rank $24$, the fold count is $5$, the dictionary penalty is $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and the join threshold parameter is $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.

The coordinate side is not a single codon-count summary.  It is the full powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface with nine named residual coordinates:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  On the sibling protein-abundance contact, yeast joins $5815$ CDS/protein rows with reported hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and the direct controlled protein-abundance survival is nonzero for all nine coordinates, with $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and surviving coordinate count $9$.  This establishes the finite protein-readout residual that the present dictionary test tries to compress; it does not, by itself, identify a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Dictionary certificate.}
The candidate reduction family is the single-codon readout dictionary family generated by the minimal dictionary compression run.  The selected readout family has size at most three and is reported as
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{\ast} &= \{\mathrm{measured\_te},\ \mathrm{mrna\_stability},\ \mathrm{turnover}\},\\
C_{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
C_{\mathrm{raw}} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{select}} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{fixed}} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
U &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $C_{\ast}$ is the selection-aware accepted coverage of the controlled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual by the selected measured-readout dictionary, $C_{\mathrm{raw}}$ is the best raw coverage over the candidate family before the selection-aware null correction, $N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{select}}$ is the $95\%$ selection-aware null boundary computed from $120$ deterministic permutations, $N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{fixed}}$ is the corresponding naive fixed-subset null boundary, and $U$ is the unexplained fraction left after the accepted reduction.  Numerically, $C_{\ast}$ is well above the selection-aware boundary, since
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_{\ast}-N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{select}} &= 0.21623310715434933,\\
\frac{C_{\ast}}{N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{select}}} &\approx 8.4182.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite verdict is therefore not merely that the best subset was large relative to a fixed subset null.  The selected dictionary remains above the null after paying for search over the candidate family.

\paragraph{Readout context.}
The selected dictionary is compatible with the surrounding yeast readout computations. In the measured TE/protein abundance mediation table, the yeast three-way join has $5128$ rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, raw measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$, and accepted measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.

These numbers explain why measured TE and mRNA stability enter the selected dictionary: they each carry finite, controlled parts of the yeast abundance residual, with TE carrying the dominant single-mediator component in this organism.

The turnover element is weaker and more specialized.  The human turnover run joins $8057$ abundance/CDS/turnover rows and reports $R^2_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}}=0.004615408769189838$, absorbed fraction $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, and a null boundary with mean $0.0001262572958089998$ and $95\%$ point $0.0004342285066808464$, so that the human turnover projection does not exceed its degree-of-freedom matched null.  Its role in the yeast dictionary should therefore be read as a finite candidate-family component in the compression certificate, not as a general turnover mechanism.

\paragraph{Four-case placement.}
The dictionary certificate refines the powered four-case classification rather than replacing it.  In the modeled-tAI four-case discriminant, yeast has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$, coordinate count $9$, and case counts $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$, with no positive-positive entries lacking a mediation drop.  On the measured-TE path, yeast has measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$ rather than a complete absorption.  The partial dictionary result is therefore a middle statement: the protein-abundance residual is real, a measured readout dictionary explains a significant part of it, and a large remainder remains after the best accepted dictionary compression.

This distinction is important.  The certificate supports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{measured dictionary coverage} &> \text{selection-aware null boundary},\\
\text{unexplained fraction} &= 0.7546178856026946,
\end{aligned}
$$
not a total reduction of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual to TE, mRNA stability, and turnover.  In prose: the powered yeast residual has a measured readout shadow, but approximately three quarters of the controlled residual energy remains outside this three-readout explanation at the stated certificate level.

\paragraph{Position and dwell side observations.}
The $f3\_stress$ coordinate has additional yeast-local evidence that is consistent with the dictionary being partial rather than empty.  In the dwell mediation run, the active yeast join has $1257$ rows, base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a permutation null with mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95\%$ point $0.0013115362189692937$.  The approximate $f3$--dwell correlation probability is $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$, and the $f3$ projection contributes fraction $0.1999063916718794$ of the target.  The positional run identifies the leading segment as the $5'$ window, with the $5'$ segment passing and the $3'$ segment not passing.  The structural-order positional run is more restrictive: it reports positive ramp signal but no ramp-localization gate, with weighted ramp $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, paired ramp-minus-body $\Delta R^2=0.001378137149572267$, and conclusion $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$.

These side observations help localize finite residual behavior in the yeast matrix, especially near $f3\_stress$, but they do not convert the dictionary certificate into a structural or mechanistic theorem.  They say that some measured readouts can carry pieces of the residual; they do not say that the residual has been biologically exhausted.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-level claim is the following finite-row statement.  On the powered yeast abundance join, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual has a statistically non-null partial reduction to a small measured-readout dictionary containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The accepted coverage is $0.24538211439730545$ under a selection-aware $95\%$ null boundary of $0.029149007242956115$, using $120$ deterministic permutations, $5$ folds, $1800$ fitted rows, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and a rank-$24$ control basis.  The remaining unexplained fraction is $0.7546178856026946$.

The result is therefore best read as a partial mediation certificate, not as a closure certificate.  It rules in a finite, selection-aware measured-readout shadow of the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual.  It also leaves a large residual component outside the selected readout dictionary, so the correct conclusion is partial reducibility plus residual openness.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No stronger biological claim follows from this table alone.  The calculation does not prove a causal translation pathway, a folding effect, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal rule.  Direct $q_i\to P_k$ protein-abundance association is not a mechanism unless a measured translation readout supplies a surviving $q_i\to T_j$ step and conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the $q_i\to P_k$ signal under the matched controls.  Likewise, ribosome TE, mRNA stability, turnover, dwell, localization, and positional readouts are bounded finite contacts; each can support only the statistic actually computed on its matched rows.  Any claim about folding, localization mechanism, physical feasibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
