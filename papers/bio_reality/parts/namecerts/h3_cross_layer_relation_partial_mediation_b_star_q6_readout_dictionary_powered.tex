\subsection{Partial mediation of the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual signal against protein abundance can be compressed by a small measured-readout dictionary, rather than being promoted to a translation mechanism or to any higher biological layer.  The tested organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The source design is the $9$-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate matrix, residualized against the sibling control space $Z$.  The target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified yeast context join.  The active row set has base availability $4759$, joined availability $4758$, and test fit size $n=1800$.  The control basis has rank $24$, the fold count is $5$, the dictionary penalty is $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and the join threshold is $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.

\paragraph{Dictionary statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design and let $P$ denote the residualized yeast protein-abundance target.  The candidate reduction family is the single-codon readout dictionary family from the measured-readout compression computation.  The selected dictionary has at most three measured readouts, namely measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and protein turnover.  Its selection-aware explained coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\star} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{\star} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$.  The naive fixed-subset null $95$th percentile is $0.00598374993486675$, while the selection-aware null $95$th percentile is $0.029149007242956115$ over $120$ deterministic permutations.  Thus the finite-row conclusion is not that the residual vanishes, but that a nontrivial and selection-aware significant fraction of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual is reducible to the listed measured readouts, leaving about $75.46\%$ outside this dictionary.

\paragraph{Readout context.}
The same yeast context has a direct protein-abundance survival statistic $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls, with all $9$ residual coordinates surviving at the positive threshold.  The yeast protein-abundance join used in the direct protein-omics run reports $5815$ joined CDS rows and a join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The measured ribosome-TE contact in yeast reports $5128$ genes used from $5815$ CDS rows, with hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and its measured translation-efficiency survival is $R^{2}_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving.  In the measured TE-to-abundance decomposition, the yeast three-way join has $5128$ rows and the measured mediated fraction is $0.4793069656345167$, with $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$ and $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$.

\paragraph{Relation to larger mediation fits.}
The dictionary result is consistent with, but weaker than, the broader variance-partition calculations.  In the joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability analysis for yeast, the single and joint values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The joint-minus-single-sum diagnostic is $-0.012862468941589822$, so the recorded overlap class is approximately independent at the declared descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  A separate dwell readout on yeast gives a held-out increment $\Delta R^{2}=0.01856473944057712$ over a fixed readout permutation null with $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$ and approximate $f_{3}$--dwell association probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These numbers support the local statement that measured readouts capture part of the residual geometry, not the stronger statement that the full residual has been mechanistically explained.

\paragraph{Four-case placement.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant classifies yeast as all case $D$ on the $9$ residual coordinates: $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$, with no positive-positive coordinate lacking a mediation drop in that modeled-readout fit.  This classification is useful as a row-level warning about propagation through a translation-like readout, but it is not the claim tested here.  The present dictionary certificate is narrower: it says that the yeast protein-abundance residual can be partially reduced by a small measured-readout list under a selection-aware null, while a large unexplained component remains.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion is a finite statistical one.  A $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate-to-protein association, even when partly reducible to measured TE, mRNA stability, turnover, or dwell summaries, does not by itself establish translation causality, ribosome mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law.  Each of those claims would need its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  The present surface only records that, on the powered yeast join, the residual signal is real, the three-readout dictionary captures $0.24538211439730545$ of it beyond a selection-aware null boundary, and $0.7546178856026946$ remains outside that dictionary.
