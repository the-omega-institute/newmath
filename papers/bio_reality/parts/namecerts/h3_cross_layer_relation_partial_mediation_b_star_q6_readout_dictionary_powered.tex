\subsection{Partial mediation by a small measured-readout dictionary}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether the protein-abundance component carried by the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface is merely a loose direct association, or whether a small dictionary of measured downstream readouts can account for a nontrivial part of it.  The active surface consists of the coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, always after residualization against the sibling control system $Z$.  The control rank used in the certificate is $24$, with the same biological controls used throughout this packet: amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the projected coordinates, and matched transcript-level controls when the readout requires them.  The target is the residualized yeast protein-abundance vector, namely log10 protein abundance after the sibling controls on the unified yeast context join.

\paragraph{Finite rows.}
The powered certificate is computed on \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The available unified join contains $4758$ rows, and the held-out experiment uses $n=1800$ rows from a base panel of $4759$.  The test uses $5$ folds, a deterministic seed $sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_partial\_mediation\_certificate\_powered{:}deterministic$, dictionary penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, join floor $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ row-label permutations.  The candidate reduction family is the single-codon readout dictionary family, and the selected dictionary is constrained to at most three measured readouts.  In the reported run the selected explanatory readouts are measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and protein turnover.

\paragraph{Certificate statistic.}
Let $P_{Q}$ denote the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ component of residualized protein abundance on the active yeast join, and let $D_{\leq 3}$ be the selected measured-readout dictionary.  The run reports raw best dictionary coverage $0.2745002077400602$ over the candidate family, with selection-aware accepted coverage
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{*} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
N_{95}^{sel} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
N_{95}^{fixed} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
1-C^{*} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed dictionary coverage is far above both the fixed-subset null and the selection-aware null.  The finite-row interpretation is not that the residual disappears, but that a real part of it is reducible to a compact measured-readout dictionary while the majority remains outside that dictionary.  Numerically, about $24.54\%$ of the target component is covered by the accepted three-readout certificate, leaving about $75.46\%$ unexplained by measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover on this join.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein survival.}
The dictionary certificate is evaluated against a background in which the direct codon-residual to protein-abundance association is already nonzero.  In the broader measured-protein screen, all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive against PAXdb protein abundance in each of $15$ organisms after the same style of controls.  The yeast direct protein-abundance surface is the strongest of the listed organisms, with $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and $9$ surviving coordinates on $5815$ joined CDS rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  Other direct $R^{2}_{QP}$ values include $0.10767526677985896$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.10217906758013379$ for \emph{C. elegans}, $0.08141786718177903$ for \emph{Dictyostelium discoideum}, $0.0770952887136557$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.0646471273126996$ for \emph{Pseudomonas aeruginosa}, $0.06397514251295629$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, $0.061906245886660105$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.03980793596647631$ for chicken, $0.03209009170649934$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.02763998603083845$ for rat, and $0.019256877907572044$ for \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}.  These numbers justify testing a reduction dictionary, but by themselves they do not specify a route through translation or any later protein-state layer.

\paragraph{Translation and transcript-stability context.}
The measured translation-efficiency contact is available on four powered organism joins.  The measured $R^{2}_{QT}$ values are $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast on $5128$ genes with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish on $13140$ genes with hit rate $0.9118667591950035$, $0.018837911344127926$ for human on $11554$ genes with hit rate $0.6064136881331024$, and $0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli} on $3415$ genes with hit rate $0.9133458143888741$.  In the measured $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition, the yeast measured mediation fraction is $0.4793069656345167$, while \emph{E. coli} has $0.04091158340950673$, human has $0.0027966485036786744$, and zebrafish is clipped to $0$ from a suppressive raw value $-0.04801486048721136$.  The joint TE-plus-mRNA-stability run further reports yeast $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, and joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The corresponding joint fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.16890712099923358$ for human, both with the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Case classification.}
The four-case readback over modeled tAI and measured abundance gives a useful boundary check.  On yeast, all $9$ residual coordinates fall in case $D$, with $R^{2}_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ under the modeled tAI readout.  On \emph{E. coli}, $7$ coordinates fall in case $D$ and $2$ are positive-positive without a mediation drop, with $R^{2}_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^{2}_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$.  The partial-dictionary certificate is compatible with this pattern: the residual protein-abundance component is not an unstructured leftover, but neither is it fully exhausted by the small measured dictionary.  It is therefore best read as a partial reduction result, not as a closed mechanistic account.

\paragraph{Additional readout checks.}
Two yeast $f3\_stress$ readouts sharpen the same conclusion.  The dwell-mediation run joins $1257$ rows, uses $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, and reports base $R^{2}=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^{2}=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.01856473944057712$, null $95\%$ value $0.0013115362189692937$, and $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell run on the same $1257$-row join identifies the leading segment as $5prime$, with the $5prime$ window passing and the $3prime$ window not passing.  A separate structural-order positional test reports a positive ramp signal but no localization gate: at primary ramp size $50$, weighted ramp held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body held-out $\Delta R^{2}=-0.00026157313923957926$, paired ramp-minus-body $0.001378137149572267$, ramp sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, and paired ramp-body sign-flip $p=0.05859375$.  These checks support a measured-readout decomposition around translation and dwell-like quantities, while stopping short of a structural or physical conclusion.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready.  The claim supported here is narrow: in yeast, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual protein-abundance signal is real and is partially reducible to a compact dictionary containing measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The selected dictionary covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the target component, comfortably above the selection-aware null $0.029149007242956115$, but leaves $0.7546178856026946$ outside the dictionary.  Consequently the result licenses the phrase \emph{partial measured-readout mediation} for this finite panel, not full explanation.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No stronger biological layer follows from this certificate alone.  A direct $q_i \to P_k$ association is not a translation claim unless a measured $q_i \to T_j$ step survives and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the protein-abundance signal.  The dictionary result does not establish ribosome mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, evolutionary direction, perturbation rescue, or universality.  Each of those layers would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic on the same row system or on a separately justified finite panel.
