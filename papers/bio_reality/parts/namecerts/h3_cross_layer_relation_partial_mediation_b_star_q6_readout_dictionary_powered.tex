\subsection{Partial mediation by a finite readout dictionary}\label{sec:biorreality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface can be reduced to a small measured readout dictionary after the same coding-sequence controls used in the protein-abundance tests.  The carrier is a finite row matrix over nine residual coordinates, residualized protein abundance, and candidate readouts for measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, turnover, and local dwell behavior.  The nine source coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The target is not protein function, folding, phenotype, or causation.  It is the narrower finite-row question of how much of a controlled $Q\to P$ residual can be accounted for by a small dictionary of measured or measured-derived readouts on the same yeast context join.

\paragraph{Finite panel and controls.}
The powered certificate was run on \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The available unified context join contains $4758$ rows, and the fitted certificate uses $n=1800$ rows with $5$ held-out folds.  The source design is the $9$-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate matrix after residualization against the sibling control basis.  The target is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance residualized against those controls on the yeast context join.  The reported control basis has rank $24$, the join parameter is $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and the dictionary description-length penalty is $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$.  The deterministic seed is $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_partial\_mediation\_certificate\_powered{:}deterministic}$, and the permutation calibration uses $120$ permutations.

\paragraph{Primary certificate.}
The selected readout dictionary covers a strict positive fraction of the residual signal, but it does not exhaust it.  The selection-aware coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{\ast} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$.  The naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ threshold is $0.00598374993486675$, while the selection-aware null $95\%$ threshold is $0.029149007242956115$.  Thus the observed dictionary coverage is well above the selection-aware reference level, but the unexplained fraction remains about three quarters of the controlled residual component.  The correct local reading is therefore partial reducibility: the yeast $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual is real, and a compact dictionary with at most three readouts, reported as measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover, captures a significant part of it, but a large component remains outside that dictionary.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein abundance.}
The direct protein-omics surface supplies the upstream residual signal being decomposed.  On the broad protein-abundance panel, every organism in the reported table has full residual coordinate rank $9$ after controls, with control rank $23$ and $24$ control columns.  In yeast, the measured PAXdb abundance join has $5815$ CDS rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and the direct controlled protein-abundance survival is $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ residual coordinates surviving the positive threshold.  Other examples from the same direct screen include $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse.  These values justify decomposing a nonzero $Q\to P$ statistical surface, but they do not by themselves identify a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Measured translation and abundance mediation.}
Measured ribosome-derived translation efficiency gives a separate translation readout.  In yeast, the measured TE contact uses $5128$ genes, with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and the controlled measured-TE survival is $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  The measured $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition on the same organism reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,meas} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{meas} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
For comparison, the same measured mediation run gives $M^{QTP}_{meas}=0.04091158340950673$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0$ in zebrafish after truncating a negative raw fraction.  The yeast entry is therefore the strongest measured-TE mediation entry among the four listed organisms, but the cross-sectional design still only supports a variance-partition statement.

\paragraph{Joint readout decomposition.}
The joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability run gives a more resolved dictionary view.  In yeast, the four-way join contains $4759$ rows, with $5128$ genes carrying measured TE and $5379$ mRNA half-life records.  The single and joint controlled residual values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with measured-TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$.  The right-tail permutation value for this joint readout pair is $0.000999000999000999$.  The overlap diagnostic reports single-mediator sum $0.532380691503749$ and joint-minus-single-sum $-0.012862468941589822$, within the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  This makes the dictionary interpretation concrete: measured TE carries the dominant yeast share, mRNA stability contributes a smaller share, and their overlap is not large enough to erase the partial-coverage conclusion.

\paragraph{Auxiliary readout checks.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant classifies all $9$ yeast coordinates as case $D$ under its modeled translation and measured abundance surface, with no unclassified coordinates.  This is compatible with the measured-TE result only as a classification of the fitted readout surface, not as a causal label.  The $f3\_stress$ dwell readout gives an additional yeast-local mediation check on $n=1257$ joined rows: the base model has $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-alone readout has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment is $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a fixed-readout permutation null $95\%$ value of $0.0013115362189692937$.  The $f3\_stress$ to dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell run reports the leading segment as $5'$; for the primary $50$-codon ramp, the weighted ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the ramp sign-flip value is $0.0478515625$.  The paired ramp-minus-body value, however, is $0.05859375$, so the local statement is ramp-positive but not fully localized by the paired gate.

\paragraph{Readouts that do not close the residual.}
The turnover and localization probes are useful precisely because they do not promote the residual into a completed mechanism.  In the human turnover test, the abundance residual complement has $d_{modeled}=0.9208949355679455$ on the full human abundance-CDS join, while the turnover join has $8057$ rows.  Projection onto protein half-life absorbs only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, with $R^2_{Q,Tturn}=0.004615408769189838$, below the dof-matched null $95\%$ value $0.0004342285066808464$ according to the recorded null verdict.  In the localization screen, yeast has $4656$ joined rows, $4831$ localization records, $12$ localization columns, and absorbed fraction $0.011225105608516642$ against a dof-matched null $95\%$ value $0.004958116973149795$.  Human has $16008$ joined rows and absorbed fraction $0.009254913329269913$ against null $95\%=0.0013758521256775384$.  Zebrafish has only $1984$ joined rows and a failed complement-match diagnostic, so it is retained as a reported boundary rather than as a clean explanatory reduction.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row certificate is ready as a partial mediation statement.  The strongest local claim is the following: on the powered yeast context join, a compact measured-readout dictionary gives selection-aware significant coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$ of the controlled $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual, with $0.7546178856026946$ left unexplained.  The result agrees with the separate measured-TE and joint TE-plus-stability decompositions, where yeast has nonzero $Q\to T$ survival and large attenuation of the direct $Q\to P$ surface after conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability.  The result does not say that codon geometry is a translation law, that abundance is a function assay, or that the residual identifies folding, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
