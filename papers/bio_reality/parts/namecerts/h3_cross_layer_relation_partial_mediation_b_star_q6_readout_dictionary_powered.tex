\subsection{Partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether a finite dictionary of measured readouts can account for a nontrivial part of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual without promoting the residual coordinate itself into a biological mechanism.  The source design is the nine-dimensional synonymous residual coordinate family
$\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\}$,
residualized against the same sibling control basis used for the yeast abundance experiments.  The target is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the corresponding controls on the unified \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The active run used $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$ rows, with $4758$ available joined rows before the fixed sampling rule, $5$ folds, control-basis rank $24$, dictionary penalty $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, join fraction parameter $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.

\paragraph{Dictionary and certificate.}
The candidate readout dictionary is the measured set
$$\begin{aligned}
R &= \{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\},\\
|R_{\mathrm{selected}}| &\leq 3.
\end{aligned}$$
The best selected readout subset reached raw coverage $0.2745002077400602$ over the candidate family.  After the selection-aware correction, the accepted coverage is
$$\begin{aligned}
C^{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
C^{\ast}_{\mathrm{null},95} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
C_{\mathrm{fixed},95} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
1-C^{\ast} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the finite certificate is not a total reduction of the abundance residual.  It says that the residual signal is real and that a statistically significant part of it is compressible by the three measured readout classes, while about $75.46\%$ remains outside this dictionary.  The certificate status is \emph{passed}; the module-level interpretation is partial reducibility, not collapse of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual into the readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and stability rows.}
The same yeast context is supported by direct measured-translation and joint mediator computations.  In the measured TE survival run, yeast has $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=5128$, a ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, rank $\operatorname{rank}(X_{\widetilde{Q}})=9$, and measured translation survival
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}$$
The measured TE to protein-abundance mediation run on the same organism reports
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167,
\end{aligned}$$
with $5128$ three-way joined rows, $2146.0645009193045$ residual abundance energy after controls, and a rank-$10$ joint $X,T$ residual design.  The joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on yeast gives
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838,
\end{aligned}$$
so the joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$.  Its single-readout pieces are TE-only $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only $0.0626618595344251$, with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$ under the fixed pair-permutation test.  This agrees with the dictionary certificate: measured TE and transcript-stability style readouts carry a real part of the protein-abundance residual, but they do not exhaust it.

\paragraph{Direct protein readout boundary.}
The direct protein-omics surface is stronger than a null association but weaker than a mechanism.  In the broader PAXdb abundance matrix, yeast has $5815$ joined CDS-protein rows, join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, $5792$ residual degrees of freedom after $24$ control columns, residual abundance energy $2958.1865890925337$, and direct abundance survival
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827.
\end{aligned}$$
The modeled four-case discriminant on yeast classifies all nine coordinates as case $D$ with no positive-positive coordinates lacking a mediation drop.  The modeled-tAI entries in that discriminant have $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  This is compatible with the partial mediation result because the dictionary test uses measured readouts and selection-aware compression rather than declaring every direct $Q\to P$ association to be a translation-mediated pathway.

\paragraph{Residual component left open.}
The unaccounted component is not empty noise by construction.  A separate yeast dwell-readout run on the $f_3$ component reports $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, $P_{f3}$ fraction of target $0.1999063916718794$, $P_{f3}$ norm squared $44.20095750389323$, dwell-only $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, permutation $\mathrm{Null}_{95}=0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $f_3$--dwell probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These numbers show that additional structured readouts can touch parts of the remaining residual, but the present dictionary certificate intentionally stops at the three declared readout classes.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The accepted statement is a finite statistical one: under the fixed yeast join, fixed controls, fixed dictionary, fixed fold assignment, and deterministic permutation design, a selected measured-readout subset covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual above the selection-aware null.  This does not by itself establish translation causation, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, pathway mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Such claims require their own layer-matched measurements and their own controlled finite-row statistic.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association remains only an abundance association unless a measured translation readout survives and conditioning on that readout removes or reduces the direct protein signal in the declared design.
