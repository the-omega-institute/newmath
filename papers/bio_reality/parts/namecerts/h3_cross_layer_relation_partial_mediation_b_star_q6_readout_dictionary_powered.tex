\subsection{A partial mediation certificate for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bior reality-b-star-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite-row certificate for the Saccharomyces cerevisiae unified context join.  The source design is the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface, residualized against the same sibling control basis $Z$ used by the surrounding abundance and translation tests.  The nine coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The target row value is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the yeast join.  The active certificate used $n=1800$ rows drawn from $4758$ available joined rows, with base panel size $4759$, five folds, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$.  The reduction family is a single-codon readout dictionary family, and the selected dictionary is bounded to at most three measured readouts: measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.

\paragraph{Certificate value.}
The certificate reports a passed finite-row status.  The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$, while the selection-aware accepted coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{\ast} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed-subset null threshold is $0.00598374993486675$, whereas the selection-aware null threshold is $0.029149007242956115$ over $120$ deterministic permutations.  Thus the readout dictionary is not merely a post-hoc compression of the candidate family: the accepted coverage is above the threshold after paying the selection cost.  At the same time, the unexplained component remains dominant.  The certificate therefore says that the yeast $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual is real and partially reducible to the measured readout dictionary, but it is not exhausted by that dictionary.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance contact.}
The direct protein-omics surface supplies the downstream abundance target.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the PAXdb protein-abundance join used $5815$ CDS-protein rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  After length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls, all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates survived as direct protein-abundance predictors, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
\#\{q_i:S^{QP}_{i}>0\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
This direct abundance association is the target whose reducible part is measured by the readout dictionary.  It is not, by itself, a translation mechanism, folding statement, function statement, or universality statement.

\paragraph{Measured translation-efficiency component.}
The measured translation-efficiency surface is a matched ribosome-footprint-over-mRNA readout.  In yeast, the measured TE join reports $5128$ genes with TE, $5815$ CDS rows in the abundance/CDS contact, and ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  The controlled translation readout has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT,meas} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
\#\{q_i:S^{QT,meas}_{i}>0\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured mediation fit on the same organism gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,meas} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP,meas} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the strongest measured translation-channel entry in the record.  It supports a variance-partition reading: a substantial part of the yeast abundance association is absorbed when measured TE is included.  The same computation remains observational and cross-sectional.

\paragraph{mRNA-stability and joint readout component.}
The joint TE-stability decomposition provides the mRNA-stability part of the dictionary.  On the yeast four-way join, $n=4759$ rows were computed.  The single and joint controlled values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding variance-partition fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{TE only} &= 0.4697188319693239,\\
\text{stability only} &= 0.0626618595344251,\\
\text{joint mediation} &= 0.5195182225621592.
\end{aligned}
$$
The right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$.  The overlap diagnostic classifies the joint readout as approximately independent at descriptive tolerance $0.05$, since the joint-minus-single-sum difference is $-0.012862468941589822$.  This is why mRNA stability is admitted as a measured readout in the dictionary, but it does not erase the residual left by the partial mediation certificate.

\paragraph{Dwell and positional evidence.}
The $f3\_stress$ dwell test supplies a row-local translation-related readout inside the same yeast evidence family.  It used $n_{join}=1257$ rows, base panel size $1800$, five folds, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  The base model has held-out $R^{2}=0.1855578757644517$, the full base-plus-dwell model has $R^{2}=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^{2}_{dwell} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\text{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
p_{approx} &= 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dwell-alone value is $0.034336580953121976$, and the $f3$-dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$.  The positional split classifies the dominant segment as the $5'$ window: the five-prime ramp passed, the three-prime body did not, and the leading segment by $\Delta R^{2}$ is $5prime$.  These row-local facts are compatible with translation-channel reduction, but they do not convert the dictionary certificate into a causal elongation model.

Its readout is modeled tAI, not observed ribosome occupancy, so it is kept distinct from the measured TE mediation component above.

\paragraph{Higher-layer checks that remain non-promotional.}
Several adjacent readouts sharpen the boundary.  Localization absorption in yeast used $4656$ joined rows and $4831$ localization records across $12$ categories; the localization absorption was $0.011225105608516642$ against a dof-matched null mean $0.002689902676583632$ and null $95$th percentile $0.004958116973149795$.  Human turnover, by contrast, used $8057$ joined turnover-abundance-CDS rows and gave only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ absorption, below its null $95$th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$.  The structural-order ramp test was classified as $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$: at primary ramp length $50$, the weighted ramp increment was $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment was $-0.00026157313923957926$, the ramp sign-flip value was $0.0478515625$, and the paired ramp-minus-body value was $0.05859375$.  These finite checks are recorded because they constrain tempting interpretations.  They do not license folding, localization mechanism, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a global biological rule.

\paragraph{Irreducibility status.}
The separate irreducibility certificate for yeast used the same broad row scale, with base $n=4759$, $n_{join}=1800$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $120$ permutations, and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$.  Its status semantics say that the source signal is real and that at least one listed reduction family refutes the original irreducibility hypothesis.  The partial mediation certificate gives the quantitative form of that refutation: a measured dictionary explains $0.24538211439730545$ of the target component after selection-aware testing, while $0.7546178856026946$ remains outside the selected dictionary.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is therefore a partial mediation result, not a complete mechanism.  On the yeast rank-powered matched panel, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a strong direct protein-abundance association, a measured TE association, and a selection-aware readout-dictionary coverage above its null threshold.  The certified coverage is substantial enough to reject a purely primitive abundance residual, but too small to collapse the residual into translation, mRNA stability, and turnover.  No translation-realization, folding, physical-admissibility, biological-function, mechanistic, phenotype, evolutionary, or universal claim follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that specific contact and the corresponding controlled statistic.
