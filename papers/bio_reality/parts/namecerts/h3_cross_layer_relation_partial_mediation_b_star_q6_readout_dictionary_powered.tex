\paragraph{Finite row object.}

\paragraph{Refusal of stronger readings.}
The certificate does not say that $B^*_{Q6}$ determines translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule. It says that, on a finite yeast join, a controlled protein-abundance residual associated with the nine-coordinate synonymous design is partly accounted for by a small measured readout dictionary. A separate contact is required before any higher biological layer can be asserted. Bulk protein abundance is not a folding assay; measured translation efficiency is condition-specific and cross-sectional; mRNA stability and turnover are noisy readouts from distinct experimental contexts; localization labels are coarse multi-label annotations. Direct $Q \to P$ survival remains direct prediction unless a measured $Q \to T$ step survives and conditioning on $T$ reduces the $Q \to P$ signal. The four-case modeled-tAI discriminant reinforces this caution: in yeast it classifies all nine coordinates as case $D$ under the modeled readout, while the measured dictionary result reports only partial coverage rather than a complete mechanistic closure.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The chapter records a ready finite-row claim. The powered yeast dictionary test passes: $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ exceeds the selection-aware null threshold $0.029149007242956115$ under $120$ deterministic permutations, with $n=1800$, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $Z$-basis rank $24$. The correct reading is partial measured-readout reducibility of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual, not full mediation. The surviving unexplained share $0.7546178856026946$ is a live residual target for additional measured readouts rather than permission to infer structure, function, causality, or universality.
