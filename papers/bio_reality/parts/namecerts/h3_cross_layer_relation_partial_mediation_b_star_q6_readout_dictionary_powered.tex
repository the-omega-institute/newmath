\subsection{Partial mediation by a finite measured-readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bioq6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record concerns the rank-powered yeast slice of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ cross-layer surface.  The source design is the nine-dimensional synonymous residual coordinate block
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_{3}\text{-}stress$, residualized against the same sibling control basis used for the protein-abundance tests.  The target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The finite row count is explicit: the available join contains $4758$ rows, the base context size is $4759$, and the certificate fit uses $n=1800$ joined rows with $5$ folds.  The control basis has rank $24$.  This is a finite matrix statement about a measured dictionary, not a free biological story.

\paragraph{Dictionary certificate.}
The dictionary reduction family is the single-codon readout family produced by the measured-readout compression computation.  In the selected certificate the admissible measured readout list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{\mathrm{dict}}=\{\text{measured TE},\ \text{mRNA stability},\ \text{turnover}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The certificate has status passed and reports that the residual protein-abundance signal is real but only partially reducible to at most three measured readouts from this dictionary.  The regularization and join thresholds are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{DL} &= 0.01,\\
\rho_{join} &= 0.05,\\
\text{permutation count} &= 120.
\end{aligned}
$$
The selected coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\star} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{\star} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$, while the selection-aware null $95\%$ boundary is $0.029149007242956115$ and the naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ boundary is $0.00598374993486675$.  Thus the finite certificate separates three facts: there is a nonzero reducible component, the reducible component is large relative to the selection-aware null boundary, and most of the residual abundance component remains outside this dictionary.

\paragraph{Readout anchors.}
The surrounding row checks explain why the dictionary is meaningful.  Direct protein-omics survival was computed on measured PAXdb abundance for fifteen organisms; in yeast the protein join contains $5815$ CDS-linked proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and the direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance statistic has $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive partial-$R^{2}$ threshold.  Measured ribosome translation efficiency supplies a separate translation readout: for yeast, the measured TE join contains $5128$ genes, with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and the controlled measured-TE survival is $R^{2}_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  These numbers justify treating measured TE as a real dictionary entry rather than as a name-only covariate.

\paragraph{Mediation scale.}
The measured TE abundance decomposition gives the scale of translation-conditioned absorption in the same organism.  In yeast the measured mediation computation reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,meas} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{meas} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding three-way join contains $5128$ rows.  A larger joint readout calculation over measured TE and mRNA stability reports, again for yeast,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838,
\end{aligned}
$$
with joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The dictionary certificate is therefore not claiming that all abundance residual structure is translation; it records that a finite measured family captures a statistically visible part of the signal and leaves a larger remainder.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The correct readback is a partial mediation classification.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block reaches measured protein abundance, and some of that reach is absorbed by a small dictionary containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The certificate coverage $C^{\star}=0.24538211439730545$ is the finite-row claim.  The unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ is equally part of the claim: the remaining signal is not assigned to the dictionary and must not be silently promoted to a mechanism.  A companion dwell computation on the yeast $f_{3}$ abundance component is consistent with a local readout route: on $1257$ joined rows it reports held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.01856473944057712$, null $95\%$ boundary $0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $f_{3}$--dwell association value $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  This supports the existence of finite measured readout absorption for a component, while still leaving the global residual primitive relative to the tested dictionary.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
Nothing in this certificate by itself proves translation realization as a causal pathway, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  The variables are cross-sectional finite-row readouts assembled from codon counts, measured TE, mRNA stability, turnover, and protein abundance.  Conditioning and coverage are variance-partition statements.  A mechanistic claim would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as perturbation, rescue, time ordering, direct ribosome dynamics beyond the selected readout, protein-state measurement, structure assay, or function assay.  Without such a contact, the admissible conclusion is exactly the finite one: in the powered yeast context, at most three measured readouts cover about $24.54\%$ of the residual abundance signal, while about $75.46\%$ remains outside that measured dictionary.
