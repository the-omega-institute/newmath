\subsection{Partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The powered partial-mediation certificate is a finite yeast readout calculation over the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The design uses $n=1800$ rows from $4758$ available joined rows, with a reported base panel size $4759$, five folds, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$.  The source design is the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ nine-dimensional residual coordinate matrix after the same sibling controls $Z$ used by the surrounding protein-abundance and translation-readout calculations; the target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on the unified yeast context join.  The residual-control basis has rank $24$.

\paragraph{Dictionary reduction certificate.}
The tested reduction family is the single-codon readout dictionary produced by the minimal dictionary compression run.  Over this family, the best raw coverage is $0.2745002077400602$.  After selection-aware calibration, the retained coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{*} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{*} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The certificate therefore says that the yeast residual protein-abundance signal is real, but only partially absorbed by a small measured-readout dictionary.  The selected dictionary is bounded by three named measured readouts: measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The selection-aware null threshold is $0.029149007242956115$, while the naive fixed-subset null threshold is $0.00598374993486675$, both computed with $120$ deterministic permutations under seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_partial\_mediation\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}.  Since $C^{*}$ is far above the selection-aware threshold but still far below $1$, the finite row result is neither a null outcome nor a complete explanation.

\paragraph{Readout context.}
The same coordinate system has a direct protein-omics survival surface against measured PAXdb protein abundance.  In yeast, the direct $Q\to P$ surface reports $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all nine coordinates surviving after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls; the yeast protein-abundance join contains $5815$ CDS rows with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The measured ribosome-TE calculation supplies the translation readout side: yeast has $5128$ genes with measured TE, a ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and $R^{2}_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, again with nine surviving coordinates.  The measured TE to abundance mediation calculation reports, for yeast, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  These numbers explain why a dictionary containing measured TE can absorb a nontrivial part of the protein-abundance residual, while the partial-mediation certificate records that the absorption remains incomplete.

\paragraph{Joint mediator geometry.}
The larger yeast variance partition gives a compatible readout split.  With measured TE and mRNA stability jointly conditioned, yeast has $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The associated joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The overlap diagnostic is approximately additive: the joint-minus-single-sum term is $-0.012862468941589822$ against a descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  This places the dictionary certificate in the same finite-row regime: several measured layers reduce the signal, but no listed readout family removes the residual.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant gives an important local warning.  In yeast, the modeled translation readout has $R^{2}_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and the protein-abundance readout has $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, yet the nine coordinates are classified as case D with zero positive-positive entries lacking a mediation drop.  In \emph{E. coli}, the same discriminant reports seven case-D coordinates and two positive-positive entries without a mediation drop.  Thus the dictionary result should be read as a finite readout-compression and partial-mediation statement, not as a universal label for every organism or every translation proxy.

\paragraph{Residual remainder.}
The unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ is biologically meaningful as a boundary, not as an unnamed mechanism.  Related finite contacts show why this boundary matters.  Yeast $f3\_stress$ dwell mediation has held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.01856473944057712$ against a null95 value $0.0013115362189692937$, with $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Its positional refinement assigns the leading dwell segment to the $5'$ window, with the $5'$ gate passing and the $3'$ body gate failing.  Separate human turnover testing leaves turnover absorption at only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, below its null95 $0.0004342285066808464$, while localization absorption in yeast is $0.011225105608516642$ over a null95 $0.004958116973149795$.  The structural-order ramp calculation is positive but not localized: the primary $50$-codon ramp has weighted $f3$ ramp held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.0011165640103326878$, body $\Delta R^{2}=-0.00026157313923957926$, ramp sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, and paired ramp-minus-body $p=0.05859375$.  These neighboring finite contacts support a conservative interpretation: the dictionary isolates a measurable part of the $Q6$ abundance residual, while the remaining mass is still distributed across additional measured or unmeasured layers.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This section does not promote a direct $q_i\to P_k$ association into a translation mechanism.  A positive protein-omics association is only downstream evidence unless the same rows contain a measured translation readout and conditioning on that readout reduces the protein-abundance signal.  The present certificate also does not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Those claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  Here the admissible statement is narrower: on a powered yeast panel, a small measured readout dictionary containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover covers $24.538\%$ of the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}\to$ protein-abundance signal after selection-aware calibration, while $75.462\%$ remains outside that dictionary.
