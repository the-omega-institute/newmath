\subsection{Partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}

\paragraph{Finite question.}
The tested object is a finite yeast row surface in which the source design is the $9$-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate system, residualized against the same sibling control basis $Z$.  The nine coordinates are the lysine coordinate $K_{AAA}$, the arginine coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$, the isoleucine coordinate $Ile_{AUA}$, the leucine split $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, the leucine split $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, the serine split $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, the serine split $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, the threonine split $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and the $f_{3}$-stress coordinate.  The target is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the same control residualization, evaluated on a unified Saccharomyces cerevisiae context join.  The active certificate uses $n=1800$ rows from $4758$ available joined rows, with base row count $4759$, five folds, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$.  The recorded control rank is $24$.

\paragraph{Dictionary statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ source matrix and let $P$ denote the residualized protein-abundance target.  A finite family of measured readout dictionaries is allowed to reduce the source signal, but only through measured or explicitly registered rows.  In the current run the selected family is bounded by at most three readouts, namely measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The observed best raw coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$, while the selection-aware accepted coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{*} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{*} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed-subset null threshold is $0.00598374993486675$, and the selection-aware null threshold is $0.029149007242956115$ over $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  Thus the certificate is not a total explanation of the source signal.  It records a partial reduction: a measured readout dictionary accounts for about one quarter of the residual protein-abundance component, while roughly three quarters remains outside this readout family on the present finite surface.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance contact and rank.}
The direct protein-abundance surface is already non-vacuous in yeast.  The Saccharomyces cerevisiae PAXdb join reports $5815$ joined proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  After controls, the yeast direct $Q \to P$ statistic has $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, and all $9$ residual coordinates survive the positive partial-$R^{2}$ threshold on that direct protein-omics readout.  This direct survival is the reason a reduction certificate is meaningful, but it is not by itself a biological mechanism.  It is a controlled association from codon-residual coordinates to measured protein abundance.

\paragraph{Measured translation readout.}
The measured translation-efficiency contact is separately finite.  In yeast, the ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency join reports $5128$ genes with measured TE and a join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ against $5815$ CDS rows.  The measured TE readout is $\log_{10}(TE)$ with $TE=RPF/mRNA$ in the local ribosome-profiling source.  Its controlled yeast survival is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QT,meas} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving }Q\hbox{ coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the measured $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition in yeast, the three-way join contains $5128$ rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and the measured mediated fraction is $0.4793069656345167$.  This positive fraction shows that the measured TE surface absorbs a substantial part of the codon-residual association with protein abundance in yeast, but it is still a cross-sectional variance decomposition rather than a perturbative pathway claim.

\paragraph{Stability and joint readout comparison.}
The broader joint readout run separates measured TE from mRNA stability on the same yeast class of rows.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae it records $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding single-readout fractions are $0.4697188319693239$ for TE alone and $0.0626618595344251$ for mRNA stability alone, with joint fraction $0.5195182225621592$.  The overlap audit classifies the pair as approximately independent under tolerance $0.05$, because the joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.012862468941589822$.  The right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers explain why the partial certificate can accept a small dictionary: TE carries the largest observed reduction, stability adds a smaller component, and their combination remains far from full coverage.

\paragraph{$f_{3}$-stress dwell readout.}
A separate yeast dwell experiment gives another finite readout in the same neighborhood.  It uses $n_{join}=1257$ rows, base $n=1800$, five folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, and target $P_{f3}$, the projection of residualized protein abundance onto residualized $f_{3}$-stress.  The dwell-alone value is $R^{2}=0.034336580953121976$; the base model has $R^{2}=0.1855578757644517$ and the full model has $R^{2}=0.20412261520502883$, so the held-out increment is $0.01856473944057712$.  The permutation null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The $f_{3}$-dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  This supports the local statement that some residual component can be reduced by an observed dwell-like readout, while still leaving the dictionary certificate incomplete.

\paragraph{Certificate verdict.}
The finite certificate has status passed.  Its module-level interpretation is partial reducibility: the residual signal is real, and a dictionary with at most three measured readouts, recorded as measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover, covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the accepted target component after the selection-aware null correction.  The remaining $0.7546178856026946$ is not noise by declaration and is not promoted to a hidden mechanism.  It is simply the part of the controlled $B^{*}_{Q6}\to P$ surface not reduced by the registered dictionary on this yeast panel.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, protein folding statement, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal pathway, or universal biological law follows from this certificate.  A direct $Q\to P$ association can be reported as protein-omics prediction only.  A $Q\to T\to P$ attenuation can be reported as measured-readout mediation only when the measured $T$ surface is actually joined, controlled, and finite-row positive.  Any statement about folding, localization, turnover mechanism, function, phenotype, perturbative rescue, or universality requires a separate layer-specific contact and its own controlled finite statistic.
