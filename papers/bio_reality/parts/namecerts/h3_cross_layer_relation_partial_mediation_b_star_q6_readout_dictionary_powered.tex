\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready at the level of a powered partial-mediation certificate.  The row surface supports the statement that the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual is statistically real and partially reducible to a small measured readout dictionary containing measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover, with selection-aware coverage $0.24538211439730545$ and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The certificate does not license a claim of translation mechanism, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causality, or universality.  Any such stronger claim would require an additional layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic; direct $q_i\to P_k$ survival, even when strong, remains only a controlled protein-omics association unless the relevant measured readout both survives and reduces the direct signal under the declared controls.
