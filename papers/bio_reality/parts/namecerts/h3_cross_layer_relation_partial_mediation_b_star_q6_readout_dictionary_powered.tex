\subsection{Rank-powered partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-partial-mediation-b-star-q6-readout-dictionary-powered}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.} The object is a finite matrix surface whose rows are coding-sequence entries on the unified yeast context join and whose source columns are the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  The coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_{3}\_stress$.  The target vector is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance, and the same control basis is used across the sibling tests: intercept, coding length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the partial-mediation certificate the active yeast panel has $n_{join}=1800$ rows drawn from $4758$ available joined rows, with $base\_n=4759$, $z\_basis\_rank=24$, $5$ folds, join floor $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and description-length penalty $\lambda_{dl}=0.01$.

\paragraph{Certificate inequality.} The dictionary test asks whether the residual protein-abundance signal carried by the nine-dimensional $Q$ surface can be reduced by a small set of measured readouts without erasing the remaining unexplained component.  The selected family is the single-codon readout dictionary inherited from the minimal dictionary compression run, and the admissible readout subset has size at most three.  The observed best family coverage before selection adjustment is $0.2745002077400602$.  The selection-aware certified coverage is $C^{\star}=0.24538211439730545$, while the corresponding selected-null boundary is only $0.029149007242956115$; the naive fixed-subset $95\%$ boundary is $0.00598374993486675$.  Thus the certificate is not a rank artifact of a preselected small model: the selected family clears the selection-aware null by a factor of about $8.42$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\star} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
N_{95}^{select} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
N_{95}^{fixed} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
1-C^{\star} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$

The finite readback is therefore a partial-reduction statement, not a full explanation.  The measured dictionary accounts for about one quarter of the residual protein-abundance component, and approximately three quarters remains outside this three-readout dictionary.

Each joint pair-permutation right-tail value is $0.000999000999000999$, so the dictionary is picking up a repeatable finite-row reduction family rather than a single accidental mediator.

\paragraph{Four-case classification and local readouts.} The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant is useful as a classification boundary, even though the partial certificate itself is tied to measured readout reduction.  Under modeled tAI, \emph{E. coli} has case counts $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=7$, with $2$ positive-positive entries lacking a mediation drop; yeast has $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$, with no unclassified coordinate.  The $f_{3}\_stress$ dwell test adds a more local readout: on $n_{join}=1257$ yeast rows, $P_{f3}$ has target fraction $0.1999063916718794$, dwell-alone $R^{2}=0.034336580953121976$, base $R^{2}=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^{2}=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.01856473944057712$, null $95\%$ boundary $0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $f_{3}$-dwell correlation $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell readout identifies the leading segment as $5prime$, with $five\_prime\_ramp\_passed=true$ and $three\_prime\_body\_passed=false$.  A separate structural-order positional run classifies the ramp signal as $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$: the weighted primary ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip value is $0.05859375$, so the finite module is positive at the ramp but does not justify a localized structural-order claim.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.} The certified statement is narrow.  The rank-powered yeast dictionary shows that a real $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual protein-abundance signal is partly reducible to a small measured-readout family containing measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover-type readouts, while $0.7546178856026946$ of the target component remains unexplained by that family.  It does not say that the residual coordinates are translation itself, that the selected readouts are causal mediators, or that the remaining component is folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, adaptation, or a universal law.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association remains only an omics association unless the same finite row set supplies a positive measured $q_i\to T_j$ step and conditioning on that measured translation readout removes or reduces the $q_i\to P_k$ signal.  Claims about folding, physical feasibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality require their own layer-matched measurements and cannot be inferred from this partial mediation certificate.
