\subsection{Powered partial mediation by a measured readout dictionary}\label{subsec:bioq6-partial-mediation-readout-dictionary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the yeast realization of the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual surface after the usual row controls have been removed.  The active source design is the $9$-dimensional coordinate family consisting of $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress, residualized against a sibling control design of rank $24$.  The response is the residualized yeast log protein abundance on the unified context join.  The powered run used $4759$ base rows, found $4758$ rows available for the unified join, and fixed the tested analysis surface at $n=1800$ rows with $5$ folds.  The dictionary penalty was $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, the join threshold was $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and the permutation reference contained $120$ deterministic row-label permutations seeded by the string $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_partial\_mediation\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}$.

\paragraph{Dictionary certificate.}
The measured dictionary is not a free explanatory vocabulary.  It is restricted to the finite family of single-codon readout reductions generated by the registered compression run, and the selected certificate is described by at most three measured readouts: measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and protein turnover.  On the active yeast rows the best raw coverage over the candidate family was $0.2745002077400602$.  After the selection-aware penalty and null comparison, the accepted coverage was
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C_* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite readout dictionary removes about one quarter of the residual $B^*_{Q6}$-to-protein-abundance signal while leaving roughly three quarters outside this particular three-readout reduction.  The selection-aware null boundary is decisive at this scale: the accepted coverage $0.24538211439730545$ is far above the selection-aware $95\%$ null level $0.029149007242956115$, and also above the fixed-subset $95\%$ null level $0.00598374993486675$.  The claim is therefore not that the residual disappears, but that a nontrivial and selection-aware significant component is reducible to a small measured readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation and stability rows.}
The same yeast context has independent measured-readout checks that keep the dictionary interpretation concrete.  For measured ribosome-derived translation efficiency, the yeast join used $5128$ genes against $5815$ CDS rows, with a reported join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$; after the rank-$23$ control residualization, the measured translation-efficiency survival was $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive threshold.  In the measured TE-to-protein decomposition on yeast, the three-way join used $5128$ rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and the measured mediation fraction was $0.4793069656345167$.  These numbers show that measured TE is a real entry in the local dictionary, not merely a label for modeled adaptation.

The joint TE--mRNA-stability decomposition supplies the complementary scalar audit.  On the yeast four-way join, $n=4759$, the direct residual protein-abundance association was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on measured TE gave $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, conditioning on mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and conditioning on both gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The resulting joint mediation fraction was $0.5195182225621592$, with measured-TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The descriptive overlap classification was approximately independent, since the joint fraction differed from the sum of the single-mediator fractions by $-0.012862468941589822$ under the stated tolerance $0.05$.

\paragraph{Residual primitive left after dictionary compression.}
The certificate is a partial mediation result, not a complete reduction.  The unexplained mass $0.7546178856026946$ remains assigned to the residual primitive relative to this finite readout family.  A separate dwell audit isolates one such concrete direction: in yeast, the $f_3$ stress projection of the abundance residual had $P_{f3}$ norm squared $44.20095750389323$, covered $0.1999063916718794$ of its target, joined $1257$ rows, and produced a held-out dwell increment $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ over a null $95\%$ level $0.0013115362189692937$, with approximate $f_3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  This supports the local statement that part of the residual can be organized by measured readouts, while the dictionary certificate itself still leaves a large remaining component.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite readback is a four-way discrimination over residual coordinates and measured readouts.  A coordinate can be translation-surviving, translation-null, protein-omics-surviving, or protein-omics-null after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched transcript controls have been accounted for.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is only a protein-abundance association unless a measured readout $T_j$ also survives and conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the direct signal.  In the broad direct protein-omics screen, yeast had $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and all $9$ coordinates survived, but that direct survival by itself is not a mediation statement.  The dictionary certificate refines that direct signal by recording a finite, tested reduction to measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover, with $C_*=0.24538211439730545$ and an explicitly retained unexplained fraction.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This chapter makes a statistical statement about finite joined rows, residualized coordinates, and measured readout reductions.  It does not establish a causal pathway, a universal codon law, a translation mechanism in the biochemical sense, folding order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, localization, post-translational state, or evolutionary direction.  Those claims require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled tests.  The present result says only that, in the powered yeast panel, a small measured dictionary significantly explains part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual while most of that residual remains outside the tested dictionary.
