\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation-termination contact}\label{subsec:bioreality-stop-context-readthrough-g418-wangen2020}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row certificate.}
The finite contact for the claim $h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020$ is a measured stop-context readthrough table rather than a general codon-geometry theorem.  The cited run $fe18fff6841f$ used the Wangen G418 condition sheet named ``G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged''.  The join produced $7658$ rows before restricting to nonzero readthrough signal, and the analyzed subset retained $4982$ rows.  Thus the nonzero fraction was $0.65056$, and the endpoint was the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset after rank-average scaling to $[0,1]$.  The recorded runtime was $303.534$ seconds.  A second finite contact, $h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020$, supplies the broader cross-dataset stop-readthrough boundary: run $7c85e126c888$ records $55886$ rows, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and an untreated rank association $\rho = 0.032718$.  These two row certificates are enough to support a local BioReality chapter about measured termination readthrough under a bounded condition, but they are not enough to promote the $f_3$-stress survival conjecture into a positive translation law.

\paragraph{What the row table measures.}
The observable quantity is a stop-context readthrough score under aminoglycoside perturbation.  It is a translation-termination readout attached to stop-codon neighborhood sequence rows, not a direct measurement of protein abundance, folded state, physical viability, molecular function, or phenotype.  The row subset is finite and condition-specific: only nonzero G418-induced RRTS entries survive the filter, and the response is scaled by rank averaging.  The corresponding local data object may be treated as a table
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{G418}}=\{(s_i,c_i,y_i,z_i):1\leq i\leq 4982\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $s_i$ names the stop-context sequence row, $c_i$ records the Wangen condition and join metadata, $y_i\in[0,1]$ is the rank-scaled nonzero RRTS endpoint, and $z_i$ denotes controls available from the joined finite table.  This notation is only a local bookkeeping device for the measured rows.  It does not assert that every coding-sequence window carries a matched $f_3$-stress residual, wobble-weight surface, matched mRNA denominator, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint profile.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$-stress conjecture.}
The conjecture under review asks for a same-CDS or same-window statistic in which an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, matched ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency readback, matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls all appear in the same finite row.  The Wangen G418 stop-context table supplies a real termination readout, but the verified facts reported here do not include a same-row $f_3$ residual coordinate, do not list local synonymous-codon counts for each row, do not provide observed wobble-weight asymmetry $w_c\neq w_c^{f_3}$, and do not certify a held-out improvement for $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$.  Consequently the correct interpretation is bounded: the Wangen rows demonstrate that stop-context readthrough can be represented as a finite measured translation-termination surface, while the $f_3$-stress cross-layer survival test remains uncompleted for this contact.

\paragraph{Local survival boundary.}
A positive $f_3$-stress claim would require a joined matrix with rows of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
(cds,position, f_3\text{-residual}, codon\text{-counts}, wobble\text{-weights}, RPF\text{-or-TE}, mRNA, Z, y).
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is not merely a nonzero readthrough endpoint.  It requires positive held-out improvement after amino-acid composition, position bin, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, matched mRNA, codon-count controls, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The present G418 contact passes only the narrower condition of having a finite measured termination endpoint with $4982$ nonzero rows after an explicit join and filter.  The cross-dataset Wangen contact adds a larger stop-readthrough context with $55886$ rows and a small untreated association $\rho=0.032718$, which is useful as a background calibration number but not as an $f_3$ survival statistic.

\paragraph{No-promotion statement.}
This chapter therefore records a bounded translation-termination measurement contact.  It does not claim that $f_3$-stress causes readthrough, that wobble-weight asymmetry realizes translation, that codon-window geometry predicts protein abundance, or that the observed G418 endpoint establishes folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, system phenotype, or universality.  Any such assertion would need a separate finite contact at the relevant layer and a row-level statistic computed under its own controls.  Within the current evidence, the admissible conclusion is narrower: Wangen G418 supplies a concrete finite stop-context readthrough endpoint, and that endpoint can be used as a candidate translation-termination surface for later same-row $f_3$ testing once the missing coordinate, wobble, mRNA, and position-resolved readout joins are actually present.
