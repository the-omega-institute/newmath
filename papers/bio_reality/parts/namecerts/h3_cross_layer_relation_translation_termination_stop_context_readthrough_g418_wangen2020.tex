\subsection{G418 Stop-Context Readthrough as a Translation-Termination Contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the Wangen G418 readthrough table as a bounded translation-termination contact rather than as a general statement about codon geometry. The usable endpoint is the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-averaged and scaled to $[0,1]$. The finite join contains $7658$ rows before the nonzero filter and $4982$ rows after filtering to the nonzero RRTS subset, so the retained fraction is $0.65056$. The named sheet is \texttt{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}, and the recorded subset is \texttt{inner\_join\_nonzero\_rrts}. The runtime for the row construction is $303.534$ seconds. These numbers make the contact concrete: the readback is not an abstract translation label, but a finite table of stop-context rows with a measured readthrough endpoint under a specified G418 condition.

\paragraph{Coordinate tested by the contact.}
The proposed coordinate is the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate in coding-sequence windows after quotienting out amino-acid content. A row is admissible for the intended cross-layer test only when it carries the residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched translation readout, mRNA control information, and amino-acid or position-bin controls. In the present G418 stop-context contact, the measured endpoint is a translation-termination readthrough score rather than a position-resolved elongation footprint. It can therefore support a termination-layer reality contact, but it cannot by itself certify that an $f_3$-stress window has survived a full same-window ribosome-occupancy test.

\paragraph{Statistic and break condition.}
The intended survival entry has the form $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ denotes the matched translation readout and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition or position-bin terms, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, local codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage or mappability limits, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablations. For this stop-context table, the finite row count is strong enough to define a nontrivial endpoint surface, but the declared $f_3$ position-resolved statistic is still conditional on the missing same-window join:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0
&\quad\text{is admissible only after the held-out model with } f_3\text{ improves on every declared baseline,}\\
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) \leq 0
&\quad\text{or a failed rank/join condition keeps the coordinate at boundary status.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is local: if the analysis treats the G418 contact name, the RRTS endpoint, modeled wobble weights, or the $f_3$ residual coordinate as a completed same-window ribosome-footprint statistic before the joined position-window matrix exists, the claim is outside its allowed scope.

\paragraph{What the Wangen contact can support.}
The G418 table supports a finite translation-termination readout boundary. It says that a measured readthrough endpoint is available on $4982$ retained nonzero rows after an inner join and filtering step, with $7658$ rows present before the nonzero restriction. It can be used to ask whether stop-context features, local codon context, wobble-sensitive codon composition, or residual coordinates improve prediction of rank-scaled readthrough under the stated condition. Because the endpoint is termination readthrough, the natural biological interpretation is narrow: the table measures a condition-specific stop-context readthrough response, not global translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, physical feasibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Relation to the wider survival matrix.}
A separate verified cross-layer row in the same finite registry reports a translation-conditioned structural-order boundary with two joined organisms, $3412$ joined \textit{Escherichia coli} entries and $5119$ joined \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} entries. In that row, the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ values are positive in both organisms, with mean $0.005284160876443678$, minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$; the permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ values are both negative, with mean $-0.00212186632044159$. Those numbers show that the finite registry can record controlled positive cross-layer survival when the joined matrix and ablation checks are present. They do not transfer structural-order support to the G418 stop-context readthrough contact. For the present section they serve only as a calibration of what a completed row-level survival certificate looks like: joined rows, declared controls, positive held-out improvement, and a null or permutation comparison.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The stop-context contact remains a translation-termination readout surface. It does not turn $f_3$-stress geometry, wobble-weight asymmetry, synonymous codon counts, or rank-scaled RRTS into protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law. A downstream statement at any of those layers would need its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic. Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights are admissible as bounded covariates or readback annotations only; they are not observed ribosome occupancy and they do not replace a same-row footprint or translation-efficiency measurement.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
This chapter is ready as a BioReality name-certificate record for the G418 stop-context readthrough contact. The finite data surface is concrete, with $4982$ retained nonzero RRTS rows from $7658$ joined rows before filtering, a retained fraction of $0.65056$, and a specified endpoint, sheet, subset, and runtime. The positive claim is deliberately bounded: the contact supplies a measured termination-readthrough endpoint that can participate in a controlled cross-layer test. It does not yet supply the full $f_3$ position-resolved ribosome-occupancy survival statistic unless the same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon, wobble-weight, mRNA-control, and position-resolved readout rows are explicitly joined and the held-out improvement condition is passed.
