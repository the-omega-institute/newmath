\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation-termination contact}\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough-f3-stress}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite biological contact used here is the Wangen-style stop-context readthrough panel under G418 induction, restricted to rows that survive an inner join and have nonzero readthrough signal. The verified run for claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020} reports the sheet \texttt{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}, the endpoint \emph{G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-average scaled to [0,1]}, an inner-joined row count of $7658$ before the nonzero filter, and $4982$ retained nonzero rows after filtering. The nonzero fraction is therefore recorded as $0.65056$, with runtime $303.534$ seconds for the verified computation. A second Wangen stop-readthrough verification, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020}, gives a larger crossdataset surface with $n=55886$, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and untreated rank correlation $\rho=0.032718$. These numbers make the contact concrete: the paper is not invoking an abstract translation layer, but a finite stop-context readout table with a named perturbation condition, a named endpoint, and recorded row counts.

\paragraph{Coordinate to be tested.}
The proposed coordinate is the $f_3$-stress residual member of the $B^*_{Q6}$ coding-window family after quotienting away the amino-acid component. A row in the intended test table must contain, at minimum, a coding-window key, the local synonymous codon counts, the $f_3$ residual coordinate, a wobble-weight context satisfying an observed asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$, a matched readout from the G418 readthrough or position-resolved translation surface, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls. The intended survival statistic is local and conditional rather than global:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z) > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only counts as support when the positive held-out improvement remains after amino-acid composition, position bin, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, codon-count controls, and simpler-baseline ablations. In the present G418 stop-context contact, the measured object is a translation-termination readthrough endpoint, not a direct protein, folding, phenotype, or mechanism endpoint.

\paragraph{What the Wangen numbers establish.}
The $4982$ nonzero G418 rows establish a finite translation-termination readout on which stop-context signal can be ranked and compared after filtering. The preceding $7658$ joined rows show that the nonzero surface is a subset of a larger matched table rather than a free-standing rank list. The larger Wangen crossdataset count $n=55886$ and untreated $\rho=0.032718$ record a separate finite readthrough comparison surface, useful as a guard against treating the G418 subset as the whole biological phenomenon. Together they support the following bounded reading: Wangen-style stop-context readthrough can serve as a translation-layer contact for a controlled finite-row test, but the listed facts do not yet certify the specific $f_3$-stress survival entry. The distinction matters because an endpoint can be real and finite while the proposed residual coordinate remains unpromoted.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The $f_3$-stress interpretation breaks if review status, codon-window geometry, wobble-weight notation, or the existence of a G418 readthrough table is treated as though it already computed $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$. It also breaks if modeled tAI or stAI weights are read as measured ribosome occupancy, or if a stop-context readthrough endpoint is silently replaced by protein abundance, folded structure, physical feasibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule. The admissible claim is narrower: the G418 Wangen table supplies a finite readout surface on which a same-row, control-rich $f_3$ statistic could be tested.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation-realization claim follows from the $f_3$ residual coordinate by itself. No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, system phenotype, or universal law follows from the G418 readthrough table by itself. Such claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics. In this chapter the Wangen G418 fact is therefore used as a bounded stop-context readthrough contact: it can anchor the readout side of a future same-window survival test, while the actual $f_3$-stress survival claim remains conditional on joining the required codon, wobble-weight, mRNA, position, amino-acid, coverage, mappability, rank, and baseline-ablation fields in the same finite panel.
