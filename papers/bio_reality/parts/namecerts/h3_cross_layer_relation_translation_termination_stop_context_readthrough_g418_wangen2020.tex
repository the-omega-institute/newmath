\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded termination-layer row surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite table of stop-context readthrough measurements under G418 exposure.  The verified row surface for the main contact is the Wangen-style G418 readthrough endpoint named \emph{G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-average scaled to $[0,1]$}.  Its checked source sheet is \emph{G418 2.0mg$|$mL 24hr merged}.  The join produced $7658$ rows before the nonzero readthrough filter and $4982$ rows after restricting to the nonzero RRTS subset, so the observed nonzero fraction is $0.65056$.  The recorded runtime for this extraction and scaling pass is $303.534$ seconds.  A second Wangen-derived cross-dataset stop-readthrough contact gives a larger comparison surface with $55886$ rows and an untreated Spearman-scale summary $\rho = 0.032718$, with runtime $133.552797$ seconds.  These two finite contacts are used in different roles: the G418 table supplies the perturbation-conditioned termination endpoint for this chapter, while the cross-dataset stop-readthrough table supplies a sanity check that the stop-context readthrough phenomenon is represented on a much larger row surface rather than only by the G418 nonzero subset.

\paragraph{Coordinate carried by the rows.}
Each row is read as a termination-context row, not as a protein-level or organism-level phenotype row.  The row contains a stop-context readout after filtering to nonzero G418-induced RRTS and scaling by rank average into $[0,1]$.  The bounded quantity may therefore be treated as a finite termination-layer response coordinate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{G418}}(r) &\in [0,1],\\
N_{\mathrm{joined}} &= 7658,\\
N_{\mathrm{nonzero}} &= 4982,\\
\frac{N_{\mathrm{nonzero}}}{N_{\mathrm{joined}}} &= 0.65056.
\end{aligned}
$$
The important distinction is that $T_{\mathrm{G418}}(r)$ is a readthrough endpoint attached to stop-context rows.  It is not, by itself, a direct measurement of global translation efficiency, ribosome dwell time, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal codon law.  The row surface can support only statements whose target is stop-context termination readthrough under the stated G418 condition.

\paragraph{Controlled survival question.}
The intended cross-layer question is whether a proposed upstream finite coordinate, such as an $f_3$-stress residual or another synonymous-window score, improves prediction of the same termination readout after controls have been attached.  In this chapter that question is not phrased as a law of translation.  It is a finite survival test on a fixed row table.  If $Q(r)$ is the candidate coding-window coordinate and $Z(r)$ denotes amino-acid composition, position or bin controls, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA abundance where available, and simpler codon-baseline terms, the admissible statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S(Q \to T_{\mathrm{G418}} \mid Z)
  &= \Delta \mathcal{L}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{G418}}; Z, Q \bigr)
     - \Delta \mathcal{L}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{G418}}; Z \bigr),\\
\text{support} &\Longleftrightarrow S(Q \to T_{\mathrm{G418}} \mid Z) > 0
\quad \text{on held-out rows after ablations.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Delta \mathcal{L}$ may be implemented as a held-out likelihood, description-length reduction, or equivalent rank-stable predictive improvement, provided the same finite rows are used and the simpler baselines are kept in the comparison.  The verified data in hand establish the existence and size of the G418 termination endpoint row surface; they do not by themselves establish that any particular $Q$ survives this test.

\paragraph{Relation to the larger stop-readthrough surface.}
The larger stop-readthrough contact, with $55886$ rows and untreated $\rho = 0.032718$, is useful as a boundary check.  It shows that stop-readthrough rows are not confined to the G418 nonzero subset, but the numerical summary is weak and condition-specific.  It should therefore not be imported as proof that the G418 rank-scaled endpoint has the same dependence structure.  The two surfaces can be compared only after harmonizing identifiers, stop-window definitions, treatment labels, row filters, and endpoint scaling.  Until such harmonization is performed, the larger surface is a related row reservoir and not a substitute for the G418-specific statistic.

\paragraph{What may be claimed.}
The finite evidence supports a bounded statement: a G418-conditioned stop-context readthrough endpoint exists as a joined, filtered, rank-scaled row table with $4982$ nonzero rows drawn from $7658$ joined candidates, and a related Wangen stop-readthrough surface exists with $55886$ rows.  This is enough to write a mathematically precise termination-layer contact and to define a survival statistic for candidate coding-window coordinates against that endpoint.

\paragraph{What may not be claimed.}
No translation-wide realization follows from these row counts.  No protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, molecular mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the G418 stop-context endpoint.  A modeled tAI or stAI weight, a wobble-weight asymmetry, or an $f_3$-stress coordinate may enter a later test only as a row-attached covariate.  It becomes biological support for this chapter only if the same-row statistic survives after amino-acid, position, GC3, length, M-density, matched mRNA where available, coverage, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  Without that survival step, the present chapter remains a finite termination-readout contact and not a promoted cross-layer mechanism.
