\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation-termination contact}\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This chapter records a finite translation-termination contact for the claim identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020}.  The measured surface is not a global statement about synonymous coding geometry.  It is a row-bounded table in which stop-context sequence features are joined to a readthrough response under G418 treatment.  The concrete certificate is the run \texttt{fe18fff6841f}: the joined table contained $7658$ rows before the nonzero-response restriction, and the working finite panel contained $4982$ rows after restricting to the nonzero G418-induced RRTS subset.  The nonzero fraction was $0.65056$, the analyzed endpoint was the rank-average RRTS value scaled to $[0,1]$, and the named sheet was \texttt{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}.  Thus the chapter is grounded in a specific finite table rather than in the mere name of a readthrough experiment.

\paragraph{Coordinate and response.}
For this chapter, a row may be written as $(c_i,r_i,z_i)$, where $c_i$ is the local stop-context coordinate, $r_i \in [0,1]$ is the ranked G418-induced readthrough response, and $z_i$ denotes the controls available at the row level.  The admissible statistical question is whether the stop-context coordinate improves the prediction or ordering of $r_i$ after the chosen controls are applied.  The finite support condition can be stated without claiming a biochemical mechanism:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{joined}} &= 7658,\\
N_{\mathrm{nonzero}} &= 4982,\\
\Pr(r_i>0)_{\mathrm{joined}} &= 0.65056,\\
r_i &\in [0,1].
\end{aligned}
$$
The row count is large enough to make rank-based and held-out checks meaningful, but it does not by itself license any claim about protein abundance, folding, cellular fitness, or universality.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader stop-readthrough surface.}
A second finite fact is available for the broader stop-readthrough route, under the claim identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020}.  That run, \texttt{7c85e126c888}, reports $55886$ rows and an untreated Spearman-type association value $\rho=0.032718$.  This larger crossdataset surface is useful as a scope check: it shows that stop-context readthrough can be organized into a substantially larger finite table, but the G418 chapter must still keep its own endpoint and treatment condition separate.  The G418 contact is therefore not replaced by the untreated crossdataset statistic.  The two records support a conservative reading: Wangen-style stop-context data form finite row-indexed readthrough surfaces, and the G418 subset supplies a treatment-specific ranked response on $4982$ nonzero rows.

\paragraph{Translation-termination boundary.}
The measurement concerns stop-codon readthrough under a defined drug condition.  It can support a bounded translation-termination readout when the row joins, endpoint definition, response scaling, and controls are kept explicit.  It does not establish that an $f_3$-stress synonymous residual coordinate survives against position-resolved ribosome occupancy.  Such an $f_3$ claim would require a separate same-organism, same-CDS, same-window table containing the residual coordinate after amino-acid quotient, local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, matched mRNA control, position-bin or amino-acid-bin controls, and a matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout.  In symbols, a positive $f_3$ survival statement would require a separately computed entry of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the declared controls and simpler-baseline ablations.  The Wangen G418 certificate supplies a stop-context readthrough response surface; it does not supply that position-resolved $f_3$ survival matrix.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The finite conclusion is therefore local.  The G418 Wangen table can be used as a treatment-specific readthrough contact with $7658$ joined rows, $4982$ analyzed nonzero rows, and a rank-scaled response endpoint.  The crossdataset Wangen table can be cited only as a separate larger stop-readthrough surface with $55886$ rows and untreated $\rho=0.032718$.  From these facts one may formulate controlled stop-context readthrough questions and compare treatment-specific and broader readthrough surfaces.  One may not infer translation realization from codon or window geometry alone, nor infer folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Any such higher-layer statement requires its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.
