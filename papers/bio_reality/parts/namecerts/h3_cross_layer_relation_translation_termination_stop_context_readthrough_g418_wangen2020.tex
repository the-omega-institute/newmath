\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded cross-layer row}
\label{sec:bioreality-stop-context-readthrough-g418-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record concerns the finite stop-context table obtained from the Wangen 2020 G418 readthrough surface.  The row is not a statement about translation in general; it is a bounded name certificate for one assayed readout: G418-induced relative readthrough signal on the nonzero subset, rank-averaged and scaled to $[0,1]$.  The verified run for the claim used the sheet \emph{G418 2.0mg$\mid$mL 24hr merged}.  Before the nonzero endpoint restriction, the join contained $7658$ rows; after retaining the nonzero readthrough subset, the analyzed panel contained $4982$ rows.  The observed nonzero fraction was $0.65056$, so the finite table is large enough to name a real readthrough surface, but its meaning remains tied to that perturbation, assay sheet, and filtering rule.  The recorded runtime for this row construction was $303.534$ seconds.

\paragraph{Coordinate.}
The biological object named here is a stop-context readthrough coordinate under aminoglycoside exposure, not an unrestricted protein-output coordinate.  Each admissible row is understood as a local stop-context entry with an endpoint value coming from the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset.  The mathematical role of the coordinate is to provide a ranked finite readout against which local sequence context, codon-window descriptors, wobble-weight descriptors, and matched controls may later be tested.  In symbols, the usable endpoint may be treated as a bounded scalar
$$
\begin{aligned}
  y_i &\in [0,1],\\
  y_i &= \operatorname{rankavg}(\operatorname{RRTS}_i \mid \operatorname{RRTS}_i \neq 0),\\
  n_{\mathrm{used}} &= 4982.
\end{aligned}
$$
This normalization makes the row suitable for rank-based comparison, but it does not by itself determine a mechanism of readthrough, codon decoding, termination failure, or downstream protein fate.

\paragraph{Relation.}
The cross-layer relation allowed by this row is deliberately narrow.  A proposed $f_3$-stress or $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual term may be evaluated only after it is joined to the same row family as the readthrough endpoint and after ordinary sequence and expression controls have been placed in the conditioning set.  The relevant conditioning variables include amino-acid or position-bin controls, local synonymous codon counts, GC3, length, methionine-density baseline, wobble-weight context where used, matched mRNA controls where available, and simpler sequence baselines.  The controlled survival question is therefore not whether $f_3$-stress is biologically meaningful in the abstract, but whether it improves held-out prediction of the bounded readthrough endpoint after these controls have already had their chance to explain the signal.

\paragraph{Rank boundary.}
The verified G418 panel supplies a finite endpoint surface, while the cross-dataset Wangen stop-readthrough check supplies an adjacent reality check rather than a replacement for the G418 row.  That separate check recorded $55886$ rows with untreated Spearman-type association $\rho = 0.032718$ and runtime $133.552797$ seconds.  The contrast is important: the $4982$-row G418 nonzero panel names the perturbation-specific endpoint used here, while the $55886$-row cross-dataset record shows that related stop-readthrough surfaces can be assembled at larger scale.  The two facts support finite-row contact with readthrough data, but neither licenses a universal stop-context law.

\paragraph{Admissible claim.}
The chapter can claim that a bounded, row-indexed G418 stop-context readthrough table exists for the named claim, with $7658$ joined rows before nonzero filtering, $4982$ analyzed nonzero rows, and endpoint values rank-averaged into $[0,1]$.  It can also claim that this table is an appropriate target for a controlled cross-layer survival test involving local sequence context and residual codon-window coordinates.  The admissible mathematical form is a finite comparison such as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{stop}} \mid Z) &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only when the held-out model containing the $f_3$-stress term improves over the control model containing $Z$ and the simpler baselines.  Until that controlled improvement is reported for the same joined rows, the row remains a prepared readout surface rather than a positive survival theorem.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No translation-realization claim follows merely from this coordinate, from the presence of a G418 readthrough endpoint, from wobble-weight asymmetry, or from $f_3$-stress notation.  The finite table does not establish protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, molecular mechanism, adaptation, or universality.  Those claims require separate matched contacts at their own layers.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve as bounded codon-adaptation annotations, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy; matched mRNA abundance may serve as a control, but it is not itself translation output; and a ranked readthrough endpoint under G418 does not become a general law of termination.  The present record is therefore ready as a self-contained BioReality chapter only at the finite-row readthrough boundary.
