\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded termination contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record concerns a finite translation-termination panel in which stop-context sequence features are joined to a G418-induced readthrough response.  The verified run for the G418 condition used the sheet \emph{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}.  Before restricting to nonzero response rows, the inner join contained $7658$ entries.  The analysis surface used the nonzero RRTS subset, with $n = 4982$ rows and nonzero fraction $0.65056$.  The endpoint was the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-averaged and scaled to $[0,1]$.  Thus the observable quantity in this chapter is not a latent translation principle; it is a finite ranked readthrough response attached to stop-context rows under a specified drug condition.

\paragraph{Coordinate and response.}
Let $T$ denote the finite table produced by the join, after the nonzero-RRTS restriction.  A row $r \in T$ carries a local termination context and a ranked response value $y(r) \in [0,1]$.  The bounded response coordinate is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
  y : T &\longrightarrow [0,1], \\
  |T| &= 4982.
\end{aligned}
$$
The analysis is allowed to speak about survival of stop-context information only relative to this finite response column and the controls actually present in the row construction.  It is not allowed to replace the ranked RRTS endpoint by protein abundance, folding state, global translation efficiency, or a mechanistic account of release-factor failure.  The finite value $4982$ is part of the contact: any claim about the G418 readthrough response must remain keyed to that filtered table unless a new joined surface is separately supplied.

\paragraph{Readthrough contact.}
The G418 panel is a translation-termination contact because the measured quantity is a stop-codon readthrough score under aminoglycoside exposure.  The carrier is finite and row-indexed, and the response is already normalized into a rank scale.  This supports a narrow statement: stop-context rows can be tested against a G418-induced readthrough readout on the nonzero subset.  It does not support the stronger statement that codon-window geometry alone has become measured translation, nor that a synonymous residual coordinate has survived against position-resolved ribosome occupancy.  Those are different contacts and require their own same-row joins.

\paragraph{Cross-dataset guardrail.}
A separate stop-readthrough cross-dataset record gives a larger comparison surface with $n = 55886$ rows and untreated Spearman signal $\rho = 0.032718$.  This number is useful as a guardrail because it shows that a stop-readthrough row universe can be much larger than the G418 nonzero subset, while the untreated rank association remains small.  The two finite facts should not be conflated: the G418 run reports $4982$ nonzero RRTS rows after an inner join, whereas the cross-dataset record reports $55886$ rows with an untreated $\rho$ value.  The local claim in this subsection uses the former as the drug-conditioned contact and the latter only to bound interpretation across readthrough settings.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
A self-contained survival check for this chapter has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{\mathrm{stop}}(T \mid Z)
  &= \Delta \mathcal{L}(y; Z) - \Delta \mathcal{L}(y; Z, x_{\mathrm{stop}}), \\
  \text{support} &\Longrightarrow S_{\mathrm{stop}}(T \mid Z) > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
where $x_{\mathrm{stop}}$ denotes the stop-context feature family and $Z$ denotes the finite controls available for the row table.  The formula records the only permitted promotion path: stop-context information must improve held-out description of the observed ranked RRTS endpoint beyond the controls.  If the improvement is absent, saturated, or not held out, the chapter remains a bounded contact description rather than a positive cross-layer result.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The chapter makes no claim that the G418 readthrough rows establish ordinary translation rate, position-resolved ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  The G418 endpoint is a drug-conditioned readthrough response, not a general translation law.  Likewise, modeled wobble weights, codon usage, synonymous residual geometry, or notation for survival scores cannot substitute for measured same-row readout columns.  A stronger claim would require a new finite panel in which the relevant coordinate, controls, and measured downstream readout are joined on the same biological rows and pass a held-out improvement test.
