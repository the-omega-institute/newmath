\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation-termination contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The Wangen stop-context table supplies a finite translation-termination readout rather than a general law of coding sequence geometry.  The row carrier used here is the inner join between annotated stop-context records and the G418-induced readthrough response table.  Before removing zero response rows, the joined carrier has $7658$ rows.  The analyzed response carrier keeps the nonzero G418-induced RRTS subset, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{joined}} &= 7658,\\
N_{\mathrm{nonzero}} &= 4982,\\
\frac{N_{\mathrm{nonzero}}}{N_{\mathrm{joined}}} &= 0.65056.
\end{aligned}
$$
The endpoint is the G418-induced RRTS value on the nonzero subset, transformed by rank averaging and scaled to $[0,1]$.  The source sheet recorded for this finite contact is \emph{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}.  The associated run identifier is \texttt{fe18fff6841f}, with a recorded runtime of $303.534$ seconds.  These numbers are used only as finite-row evidence that a stop-context termination response table exists after joining and filtering; they are not used as evidence for protein abundance, folding, function, phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Readout coordinate.}
For a row $r$ in the nonzero subset, let $Y_r$ denote the rank-scaled G418-induced RRTS endpoint.  Let $C_r$ collect the local stop-context sequence features available in the row, and let $Z_r$ denote controls such as local sequence composition, position or bin indicators when present, and simpler context baselines.  The bounded question is whether a proposed coordinate $Q_r$, such as an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate computed on matched coding-sequence windows, improves prediction of $Y_r$ after the control surface has already been fitted.  The admissible survival statistic has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(Y \mid Z)
  &= \mathrm{Score}(Y \sim Z + Q_{f_3})
     - \mathrm{Score}(Y \sim Z),\\
S_{f_3}(Y \mid Z) &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only when the same finite rows contain the residual coordinate, the stop-context response, and the controls.  In the present record, the verified finite table closes the response side of the contact: it establishes the nonzero G418-induced readthrough endpoint and its row count.  It does not by itself establish the joined $f_3$-stress statistic, because the row certificate does not state that every analyzed stop-context row also carries the amino-acid-quotiented $f_3$ coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight asymmetry, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency controls.

\paragraph{Cross-dataset guardrail.}
A second Wangen stop-readthrough contact records a larger cross-dataset surface with $55886$ rows and an untreated rank correlation $\rho = 0.032718$ under run \texttt{7c85e126c888}.  This larger contact is useful as a boundary check: the existence of a broader stop-readthrough surface does not promote the G418 nonzero subset into a universal termination rule.  The two finite surfaces have different endpoint scopes: the G418 panel is a nonzero induced-response subset with $4982$ analyzed rows, while the broader stop-readthrough contact reports an untreated association over $55886$ rows.  They may be compared only after their row definitions, endpoints, normalization, and control sets are aligned.  In particular, an untreated low-rank association cannot be imported as a powered survival result for the G418-induced endpoint, and the induced endpoint cannot be exported as a global property of stop codons.

\paragraph{Admissible claim.}
The chapter-specific claim is therefore narrow.  The finite Wangen contact supports the existence of a curated, rank-scaled G418-induced stop-context readthrough response table with $4982$ nonzero analyzed rows out of $7658$ joined rows.  This is a legitimate translation-termination readout surface for testing a cross-layer coordinate after the required same-row covariates have been attached.  If an $f_3$-stress coordinate is later joined to the same rows, the only admissible positive statement is a controlled improvement for that finite endpoint under the declared controls.  The row count and rank scaling do not imply a molecular mechanism for aminoglycoside-induced readthrough, do not identify a causal context grammar, and do not establish translation realization for unrelated coding-window coordinates.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The readout remains a termination-layer measurement.  Nothing in the finite table licenses claims about protein abundance, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, synonymous codon geometry, wobble asymmetry, or $f_3$ notation may be used only as proposed predictors after they are explicitly joined to the finite rows and evaluated against the G418 endpoint with controls.  A separate layer-matched contact is required before any claim about ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency outside this stop-readthrough endpoint, protein output, structure, function, mechanism, or phenotype can be made.
