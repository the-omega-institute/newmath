\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation-termination contact}\label{sec:bioreality-stop-context-readthrough-g418-wangen2020}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the Wangen stop-context readthrough assay as a finite translation-termination contact, not as a general account of translation dynamics. The row surface used for the primary contact is the G418-induced readthrough table labeled `G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged'. The verified finite join contains $7658$ rows before the nonzero-readthrough filter and $4982$ rows after restricting to the nonzero RRTS subset. The nonzero fraction is $0.65056$, and the endpoint is a rank-average RRTS value scaled to $[0,1]$. The run identifier for this finite row surface is `fe18fff6841f', with runtime $303.534$ seconds and verification time `2026-06-27T05:12:32+00:00'. A separate crossdataset stop-readthrough contact is also available under run `7c85e126c888', with $55886$ rows, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and untreated Spearman-scale summary $\rho = 0.032718$. These two row surfaces are cited here only as bounded readthrough contacts: the first is the G418-induced nonzero subset, and the second is a broader stop-readthrough crossdataset surface.

\paragraph{Coordinate under test.}
The mathematical object under inspection is the $f_3$-stress synonymous residual coordinate on coding-sequence windows after amino-acid quotienting. Each admissible row must contain a local residual coordinate, synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved translation readout or explicitly declared translation-efficiency readback, matched mRNA abundance when translation efficiency is used, and controls for amino-acid or position bin, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine density, and simpler codon baselines. The intended survival statistic is local and conditional:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or position-bin controls, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA abundance when applicable, coverage and mappability constraints, and a simpler-baseline ablation. The Wangen G418 stop-context table supplies a translation-termination readthrough contact, but it does not by itself supply the full same-window $f_3$-stress survival matrix.

\paragraph{Observed finite facts.}
The primary finite observation is that a concrete G418-induced stop-context readthrough table exists after joining and filtering: $4982$ nonzero RRTS rows remain from $7658$ joined rows. This is enough to ground a stop-context readthrough contact and to justify treating readthrough as a measurable translation-termination readout on a finite table. The broader crossdataset row surface with $55886$ rows and untreated $\rho = 0.032718$ shows that a larger stop-readthrough panel is present as a distinct finite contact. The two surfaces differ in scope: the G418 subset is a condition-specific nonzero endpoint, while the crossdataset surface is a larger readthrough panel. The chapter therefore records a bounded empirical contact rather than a promoted law.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The $f_3$-stress claim would pass the local translation-readout boundary only if the same coding windows carry ordered CDS coordinates, codon counts, wobble-weight rows with an observed asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$, matched mRNA controls where translation efficiency is used, and position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or readthrough readbacks. It is not enough to have a stop-context table and a residual coordinate separately. The row join must support a positive held-out improvement after all controls, with rank sufficiency and simpler-baseline ablation. In finite-row notation, the admissible test is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= \mathrm{score}(Z, f_3\text{-stress}) - \mathrm{score}(Z),\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}} &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $Z$ carrying the amino-acid, position, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, coverage, mappability, and baseline terms. If this inequality is not computed on the joined row surface, the record remains a contact boundary.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite readthrough rows do not authorize a claim about protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, system phenotype, or universality. They also do not turn modeled tAI or stAI weights into observed ribosome occupancy. A positive readthrough endpoint under G418 is a translation-termination readback under that condition; it is not a statement that $f_3$-stress realizes translation in general, nor that synonymous residual geometry is itself biological evidence. Any downstream protein, structure, function, or phenotype statement would require a separate row-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Status.}
The chapter is admissible as a bounded BioReality record because it cites concrete finite-row readthrough contacts and states the exact missing join needed for $f_3$-stress survival. The current verified facts support the existence and scale of the readthrough contact: $4982$ G418-induced nonzero RRTS rows from $7658$ joined rows, plus a separate $55886$-row crossdataset stop-readthrough surface. They do not yet support a positive $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ conclusion. The present scientific content is therefore a translation-termination contact with a local survival test waiting on a same-window codon, wobble-weight, mRNA-control, and position-resolved readout join.
