\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded termination readout}
\label{subsec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough}

\paragraph{Finite table.}
This record treats the Wangen stop-context panel as a finite translation-termination readout rather than as a general law about synonymous coding geometry.  The row carrier is the inner-joined G418 table in which each retained row has a stop-context sequence entry and a nonzero readthrough response score.  In the verified run for the claim, the source sheet was \emph{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}; the join contained $7658$ rows before the nonzero response filter, and the retained analysis table contained $4982$ rows.  Thus the observed nonzero fraction was $0.65056$, with endpoint described as the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset after rank-average scaling to $[0,1]$.  In this chapter the empirical object is therefore not ``translation'' in the broad biological sense, but the bounded finite row
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r &= (\text{stop context},\ \text{G418 condition},\ \text{RRTS rank score},\ \text{join status},\ \text{row controls})
\end{aligned}
$$
inside the specific nonzero subset.  A separate Wangen cross-dataset check gives a larger stop-readthrough surface with $55886$ rows and an untreated Spearman signal of $\rho = 0.032718$.  That scalar is used here only as a context check showing that weak stop-context rank signal can be recorded on a broader surface; it is not promoted to the G418-induced endpoint.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The admissible BioReality statement is that stop-context readthrough under G418 has a measured, finite-row response surface on which rank-scaled nonzero readthrough values can be associated with local stop-context features.  The claim is deliberately narrower than a mechanistic translation theory.  The evidence row says that a termination-context coordinate can be joined to a readthrough measurement under a defined perturbation, filtered to a nonzero response subset, and summarized by concrete row counts.  The finite predicate tested by the chapter is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{\mathrm{G418}}(c \mid Z) > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
only when the coordinate $c$ is read in the same retained stop-context row as the G418-induced RRTS endpoint and after the declared controls $Z$ have been respected.  The notation names a bounded survival entry for the finite table; it does not assert that the coordinate alone realizes translation, changes protein abundance, fixes folding, or determines phenotype.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$-stress boundary.}
The $f_3$-stress conjecture asks for a stronger row type: a coding-window table containing an $f_3$ residual coordinate after the amino-acid quotient, local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, matched position-resolved ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The present G418 stop-context table supplies a concrete termination readout, but it does not by itself supply the whole $f_3$-stress window matrix.  In particular, the Wangen G418 rows do not automatically provide same-CDS synonymous-window codon counts, an observed wobble-weight asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$, or a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readback for the same window.  Therefore the chapter may record a stop-context termination surface, and may use it as a reality contact for bounded readthrough response, while refusing to infer a general $f_3$ directionality principle.

\paragraph{Rank boundary.}
The row counts matter because they distinguish a finite powered surface from a named contact route.  The G418 analysis has $4982$ retained nonzero rows after filtering from $7658$ joined rows, while the broader stop-readthrough cross-dataset surface records $55886$ rows.  These numbers are sufficient to state a finite empirical table and to require held-out or controlled improvement checks.  They are not sufficient, by themselves, to certify that an $f_3$-stress coordinate survives all amino-acid-composition, position-bin, GC3, CDS-length, M-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The rank condition is therefore local: the retained stop-context response table can support a bounded termination-readthrough statistic, but an $f_3$-stress cross-layer statistic must be recomputed on a table whose rows actually contain the residual coordinate and the matched translation readout.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No broad translation claim follows from these rows alone.  The finite G418 readthrough endpoint is not protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Modeled codon adaptation weights, stop-context sequence geometry, and review eligibility do not become observed ribosome occupancy unless a separate same-row contact supplies that readout.  Likewise, a positive readthrough association under G418 would remain a perturbation-specific termination response unless downstream contacts separately attach protein abundance, structure, function, phenotype, or causal mechanism measurements.  The chapter therefore closes at the measured stop-context readthrough layer: a finite, row-indexed G418 termination surface exists, but promotion to $f_3$-stress translation survival or to any higher biological layer remains blocked until the required same-window matrix and controls are present.
