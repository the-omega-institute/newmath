\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a finite translation-termination contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the Wangen-style stop-context readthrough table as a finite translation-termination contact, not as a free-standing law of coding sequence geometry.  The primary finite-row run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020} used the sheet \texttt{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}.  Before the nonzero readthrough filter, the joined table contained $7658$ rows.  After restricting to the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, the analyzed table contained $4982$ rows, with nonzero fraction $0.65056$.  The endpoint was the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-average scaled to $[0,1]$, and the recorded run time was $303.534$ seconds.  A separate cross-dataset Wangen stop-readthrough run, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020}, retained $55886$ rows and reported untreated rank correlation $\rho=0.032718$ with run time $133.552797$ seconds.  These two finite facts are used here only to anchor the translation-termination readout surface and the existence of a larger stop-readthrough comparison surface.

\paragraph{Coordinate tested.}
The mathematical object of interest is a bounded CDS-window coordinate in which an $f_3$-stress residual is evaluated only after amino-acid quotienting and only alongside local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, position or amino-acid-bin controls, matched mRNA control, GC3, length, M-density, and simpler baseline ablations.  In this chapter, the observed stop-context table supplies a termination-layer readout row, but it does not by itself supply the complete same-CDS, same-window matrix required for a position-resolved $f_3$-stress survival statistic.  The intended statistic may be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{AA\ composition},\ \mathrm{position\ bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\ \mathrm{baseline\ codon\ counts}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The Wangen G418 rows therefore support a concrete termination-readthrough contact and a concrete nonzero subset size, while the $f_3$-stress claim remains conditional on the missing joined residual-coordinate table.

\paragraph{Rank and survival interpretation.}
The primary row count $4982$ is large enough to make the G418 readthrough contact non-vacuous as a finite ranked endpoint.  The prefilter count $7658$ also records the size of the joined surface before the nonzero RRTS condition is imposed.  However, the survival claim is not the assertion that G418 readthrough exists, nor the assertion that stop codon context matters in general.  It is the narrower assertion that an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate improves held-out prediction of the same-row termination readout after the declared controls.  The available finite facts do not include a list of fitted $R$ values, $M$ values, regularization parameters, permutation $p$-values, module classifications, or hit rates for that $f_3$-specific model.  Consequently the present chapter records a ready translation-termination contact, while refusing to treat the contact as completed proof of the proposed $f_3$ cross-layer survival.

\paragraph{Biological scope.}
The G418-induced RRTS endpoint is a translation-termination readthrough measurement under a specific perturbation and data-processing choice: nonzero RRTS rows, rank-average scaling, and the named Wangen sheet.  The larger $55886$-row cross-dataset Wangen surface gives an additional finite comparison frame, with untreated $\rho=0.032718$, but it is still a stop-readthrough comparison rather than a direct protein-output, folding, localization, phenotype, or mechanism assay.  These data may be used to ask whether codon-window features or residual coordinates survive as bounded predictors of termination readthrough.  They may not be used to infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, translation mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies those rows and the corresponding controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a BioReality entry for the G418 stop-context readthrough contact because the finite endpoint, sheet, row counts, nonzero subset fraction, and companion cross-dataset stop-readthrough surface are recorded.  It is not ready as a positive $f_3$-stress translation-survival theorem.  The correct local reading is: Wangen G418 stop-context readthrough supplies a concrete termination-layer readout surface; the proposed $f_3$-stress cross-layer relation remains a bounded test request until same-organism, same-CDS or same-window codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA controls, position-resolved readouts, rank checks, and held-out improvement over simpler baselines are joined in the same finite table.
