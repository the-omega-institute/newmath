\subsection{G418 Stop-Context Readthrough as a Bounded Translation-Termination Contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The Wangen G418 stop-context row is a bounded translation-termination contact rather than a general codon-geometry law. The finite table used here has $7658$ joined rows before filtering to nonzero reporter signal and $4982$ rows after restricting to the nonzero G418-induced RRTS subset. The retained endpoint is the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-average scaled to $[0,1]$, from the sheet named G418 $2.0\,\mathrm{mg}/\mathrm{mL}$ $24\,\mathrm{hr}$ merged. The nonzero fraction is $0.65056$, so the contact is large enough to support a finite stop-context readout, but it is still a reporter-specific termination surface under drug induction, not an unrestricted statement about translation.

\paragraph{Observed termination surface.}
Each retained row is interpreted as a stop-context observation with an attached readthrough rank, not as a protein-abundance, folding, function, or phenotype measurement. The operative finite carrier is
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{G418}} = \{(s_i,c_i,y_i,z_i): 1 \leq i \leq 4982\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $s_i$ is the local stop-context sequence key, $c_i$ records the row-level context and matching information, $y_i \in [0,1]$ is the rank-scaled G418-induced RRTS endpoint, and $z_i$ denotes the controls needed before any cross-layer statement is attempted. The larger prefilter count gives the denominator for the row-retention audit:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{nonzero}} &= \frac{4982}{7658} \\
&\approx 0.65056.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the concrete finite contact: a stop-context reporter panel with a nonzero readthrough subset under a defined G418 condition.

\paragraph{Relation to the f\_3-stress survival packet.}
The conjectural $f_3$-stress coordinate may only enter this chapter as a proposed covariate to be tested against the same finite readthrough surface. The current G418 row establishes a measured termination-readthrough endpoint, but it does not by itself attach the full $f_3$-stress window table: local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, amino-acid or position-bin controls, matched mRNA controls, and the declared rank-powered survival statistic are still separate requirements for that specific cross-layer packet. Thus the admissible mathematical claim is conditional. If $q_i$ denotes the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and $Z_i$ denotes the allowed control vector, the desired survival entry has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
  = \Delta \mathcal{P}(T_{\mathrm{position}}; q_i \mid Z_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\Delta \mathcal{P}$ must be a held-out improvement over amino-acid composition, position-bin effects, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA abundance, local codon-count baselines, wobble-weight baselines, and simpler stop-context baselines. The present finite G418 contact supplies $T$-side readout rows; it does not yet certify $S_{f_3}>0$.

\paragraph{Companion Wangen cross-dataset check.}
A separate Wangen stop-readthrough cross-dataset row reports $55886$ rows, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and untreated Spearman signal $\rho=0.032718$. That companion row is useful as a scope check: weak untreated association and a much larger cross-dataset panel do not promote the G418 nonzero subset into a universal termination rule. Instead, the two Wangen records jointly indicate that stop-context readthrough can be represented as finite reporter data, while condition, subset, and endpoint definition remain decisive. The G418 row is therefore a drug-induced, nonzero-subset termination contact; the broader row is a background scale and transfer-risk warning, not an automatic validation of any $f_3$-stress direction.

\paragraph{Local admissible statement.}
The chapter supports the following bounded statement. In the G418 $2.0\,\mathrm{mg}/\mathrm{mL}$ $24\,\mathrm{hr}$ merged Wangen panel, there exists a finite nonzero stop-context readthrough table with $4982$ retained rows out of $7658$ joined rows, endpoint rank-scaled to $[0,1]$, and nonzero fraction $0.65056$. This table is an eligible termination-layer readout for a later controlled $f_3$-stress survival test, provided the later test joins the same rows to the required codon-window, wobble-weight, mRNA, amino-acid, position, and simpler-baseline controls. The finite contact is strong enough to define a measured termination endpoint; it is not strong enough, without those joins and ablations, to claim a positive $f_3$-stress survival coordinate.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
Nothing in this record licenses translation realization from codon geometry alone. The contact also does not establish protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, cellular mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universality. Modeled tAI or stAI weights, wobble-weight asymmetry, review eligibility, or notation such as $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ are not measurements of ribosome occupancy or protein output unless a same-row readout and its controls are actually present. Any downstream statement about abundance, folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or a general biological law requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own finite statistic.
