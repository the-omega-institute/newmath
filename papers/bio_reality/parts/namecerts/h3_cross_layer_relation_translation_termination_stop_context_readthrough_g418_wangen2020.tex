\subsection{G418 Stop-Context Readthrough as a Bounded Translation-Termination Contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-stop-context-readthrough-g418-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite contact considered here is the Wangen stop-context readthrough table under G418 treatment, restricted to the nonzero readthrough subset after the source rows have been joined.  The verified run `fe18fff6841f` reports the sheet `G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged`, with $7658$ joined rows before the nonzero filter and $4982$ retained rows after filtering.  The retained nonzero fraction is $0.65056$, and the endpoint is a G418-induced RRTS value rank-averaged and scaled to $[0,1]$.  Thus the empirical object used in this chapter is not a whole-proteome translation law; it is a finite rank table of stop-context readthrough measurements under one drug condition and one declared row filter.  A second Wangen cross-dataset check, run `7c85e126c888`, gives $55886$ rows and an untreated rank correlation $\rho=0.032718$, which is useful here only as a scale warning: weak untreated association does not by itself license a mechanistic or universal claim about termination contexts.

\paragraph{Name certificate.}
Let $T$ be the retained G418 table with $|T|=4982$.  Each row $t\in T$ carries a stop-context identifier, the joined readthrough endpoint $y(t)\in[0,1]$, and whatever local sequence or annotation covariates are present in the curated row.  The certificate asserted by this chapter is the finite existence of the controlled readout surface
$$
\begin{aligned}
  T_{\mathrm{G418}} &= \{t : t\text{ is joined and has nonzero G418 RRTS}\},\\
  |T_{\mathrm{G418}}| &= 4982,\\
  y(t) &\in [0,1].
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough to ground a bounded translation-termination contact: stop-context rows can be compared against a measured readthrough endpoint in the retained G418 panel.  It is not enough, by itself, to certify an $f_3$-stress survival coordinate, because that stronger claim would require same-row codon-window residuals, wobble-weight asymmetry, matched mRNA controls, amino-acid or position-bin controls, and a held-out improvement test over simpler baselines.

\paragraph{Controlled interpretation.}
The biological statement supported at this stage is deliberately narrow.  A finite subset of Wangen stop-context rows has been joined to a nonzero G418-induced readthrough readout, and the retained endpoint has a bounded rank scale.  This permits a chapter-local readout boundary for translation termination: if a proposed coding-window or stop-context statistic is later attached to these same rows, its association with G418 readthrough can be tested as a finite supervised relation.  The row counts also constrain the evidential strength.  The analysis surface is not empty, since $4982$ nonzero rows remain after filtering, but the filter itself changes the estimand from all assayed stop contexts to the induced-readthrough-positive subset.  The prefilter count $7658$ and retained fraction $0.65056$ must therefore travel with any reported statistic.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The local promotion test is a same-row survival test, not a narrative inference from context labels.  A proposed residual coordinate $q$ may be reported as surviving this contact only if a model containing $q$ improves held-out prediction or description length for $y(t)$ after the declared controls are present.  In symbolic form, for control matrix $Z$ and a simpler baseline $B$, the acceptable positive statement has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta(q;T_{\mathrm{G418}},Z,B)
    &= \operatorname{Score}(B+Z+q)-\operatorname{Score}(B+Z),\\
  \Delta(q;T_{\mathrm{G418}},Z,B) &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under a held-out or rank-stable protocol.  If the coordinate is an $f_3$-stress residual, the required $Z$ must include at least amino-acid or position-bin structure, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, matched mRNA where available, and simpler codon-count baselines.  Without those rows and controls, $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ is notation for a desired test, not a computed biological statistic.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization from codon geometry alone.  It does not infer protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, mechanism, or universality from the G418 readthrough panel.  It also does not treat modeled tAI or stAI weights as observed ribosome occupancy, and it does not treat a stop-context readthrough endpoint as a position-resolved ribosome-footprint matrix.  Those claims require their own matched contacts.  The present record says only that a finite, bounded Wangen G418 readthrough surface exists with $7658$ joined rows before filtering, $4982$ nonzero retained rows, a retained fraction of $0.65056$, and a rank-scaled endpoint in $[0,1]$; every stronger cross-layer claim must be recomputed on a joined panel with its controls rather than inherited from this contact.
