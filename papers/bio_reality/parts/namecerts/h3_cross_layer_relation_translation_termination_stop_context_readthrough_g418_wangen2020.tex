\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation-termination row}\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This BioReality record concerns a finite table of stop-context rows from the Wangen-style G418 readthrough surface. The verified run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020} used the sheet named \texttt{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}. Before the nonzero endpoint filter, the inner join contained $7658$ rows. After restricting to the nonzero induced-readthrough endpoint, the analysis table contained $4982$ rows, with nonzero fraction $0.65056$. The endpoint was the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-averaged and scaled to $[0,1]$, and the recorded runtime was $303.534$ seconds. A separate cross-dataset stop-readthrough check, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.stop\_readthrough.stop\_readthrough\_crossdataset\_wangen2020}, reports $55886$ joined rows and an untreated rank correlation $\rho=0.032718$, with runtime $133.552797$ seconds.

\paragraph{Local mathematical object.}
The finite object used here is not an unrestricted translation law. It is a row-indexed table
$$\begin{aligned}
T &= \{(s_i,c_i,x_i,y_i,z_i):1\leq i\leq n\},\\
n &= 4982,
\end{aligned}$$
where $s_i$ denotes the stop-context or local termination-window descriptor, $c_i$ denotes available sequence or codon-context covariates, $x_i$ denotes the candidate residual coordinate when such a coordinate is attached, $y_i\in[0,1]$ denotes the rank-scaled G418-induced RRTS endpoint on the nonzero subset, and $z_i$ collects the local controls available for the finite test. The observed row count is therefore a bounded contact with a measured termination-readthrough readout, not a certificate that any upstream residual geometry has already survived a same-row translation test.

\paragraph{Termination contact and residual-coordinate distinction.}
The readthrough table supplies a translation-termination endpoint under G418 treatment. It does not by itself supply the full position-resolved $f_3$-stress survival table required for a claim of controlled cross-layer survival. For such a claim, the same row would have to contain the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context satisfying an observed asymmetry $w_c\neq w_c^{f_3}$, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls. In the present finite facts, the verified positive row count belongs to G418 stop-context readthrough, while the requested $f_3$-stress position-window survival statistic remains a separate unsupplied join.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
The admissible statistic for the G418 surface is a controlled finite-row readthrough association of the form
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta(T;Z) &= \operatorname{Score}(y\mid Z,x)-\operatorname{Score}(y\mid Z),\\
\text{with support} &\quad \Delta(T;Z)>0
\end{aligned}$$
only when the residual coordinate $x$ and the control set $Z$ are actually present on the same finite rows as the rank-scaled RRTS endpoint. The verified facts establish the available endpoint surface and its row counts; they do not establish that the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate has been joined to these rows, nor that the ablation against amino-acid composition, position bin, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA, codon-count baselines, and simpler sequence models has been carried out for this subject.

\paragraph{Cross-dataset guardrail.}
The cross-dataset stop-readthrough record with $55886$ rows and untreated $\rho=0.032718$ is useful as a scale and leakage guard: it shows that a larger stop-readthrough surface exists and that untreated correlation is small on that recorded comparison. This supports treating G418-induced nonzero RRTS as a condition-specific termination endpoint rather than as a universal codon-geometry law. The two finite records therefore point to a bounded empirical layer: one G418-induced nonzero subset with $4982$ analyzed rows, and one broader cross-dataset stop-readthrough check with $55886$ rows.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The proposed $f_3$-stress cross-layer claim fails locally unless the same-window table is present and the controlled improvement is positive on held-out rows. In symbols, a survival entry may be recorded only after a finite matrix supplies
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0
\end{aligned}$$
under the declared controls and simpler-baseline ablations. The G418 stop-context table supplies a translation-termination readout surface; it is not already the same-window $f_3$-stress survival matrix. A stop-context endpoint, a modeled wobble-weight surface, a contact name, or a rank-scaled RRTS value cannot be promoted to a position-resolved residual-coordinate law without the same-row join and the controlled survival calculation.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter permits only the following bounded statement: the Wangen-style G418 stop-context record provides a finite, condition-specific translation-termination readout with $7658$ joined rows before filtering, $4982$ nonzero analyzed rows, and nonzero fraction $0.65056$. It does not show protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, system phenotype, or universality. It also does not show that $f_3$-stress survives as a translation coordinate. Those stronger claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled finite-row statistics.
