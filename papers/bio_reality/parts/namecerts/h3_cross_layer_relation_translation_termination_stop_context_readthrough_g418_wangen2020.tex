\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation-contact row}
\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite table and observed endpoint.}
The Wangen G418 stop-context table is used here as a finite translation-contact surface, not as a completed explanation of translation termination. The row object is a matched stop-context entry with a readthrough endpoint, local sequence context, and filtering status. In the G418 condition recorded as \texttt{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}, the joined table contains $7658$ rows before the nonzero-readthrough filter and $4982$ rows after restriction to the nonzero RRTS subset. The nonzero fraction is $0.65056$, and the endpoint is the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset with rank-average scaling to $[0,1]$. Thus the finite observation available to this chapter is a bounded rank readout on the translation-termination surface:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{joined}} &= 7658,\\
N_{\mathrm{nonzero}} &= 4982,\\
\frac{N_{\mathrm{nonzero}}}{N_{\mathrm{joined}}} &= 0.65056.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated run identifier is \texttt{fe18fff6841f}, with runtime $303.534$ seconds and verification time \texttt{2026-06-27T00:47:31+00:00}. This makes the contact finite, reproducible, and row-scoped: it is a measured stop-readthrough readout under a specified perturbation and sheet, not an unrestricted law about coding sequence geometry.

\paragraph{Cross-dataset stop-readthrough contact.}
A second Wangen stop-readthrough check supplies a wider contact surface. The cross-dataset stop-readthrough run \texttt{7c85e126c888} reports $55886$ rows, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and untreated Spearman rank correlation $\rho=0.032718$. The small positive untreated rank association is useful as a calibration fact: it confirms that a stop-context readthrough surface exists at larger scale, while also showing that the unperturbed association is weak enough that the G418 table must not be promoted into a broad directionality principle. The two finite facts therefore play different roles. The G418 nonzero table gives a perturbation-conditioned endpoint for a bounded row claim; the larger untreated contact gives a weak baseline-scale comparison.
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{cross}} &= 55886,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{untreated}} &= 0.032718.
\end{aligned}
$$
Together they justify only a local statement: Wangen-style stop-context readthrough can be represented as finite row data with a rank-scaled readout, and the G418 subset supplies a concrete perturbation-conditioned surface on which controlled hypotheses can be tested.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$-stress proposal.}
The proposed $f_3$-stress coordinate belongs to a different row requirement than the stop-context readthrough contact by itself. To test whether $f_3$-stress survives as a cross-layer coordinate, each row would have to join a coding-window residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, amino-acid or position-bin controls, matched mRNA abundance, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout. The Wangen G418 table supplies a translation-termination readout, but it does not by itself supply the full same-window $f_3$-stress panel. Consequently the admissible interpretation is conditional: the stop-context table can anchor a measured translation-contact endpoint, while the $f_3$-stress survival statistic remains unavailable until the joined coding-window matrix is actually present.

The intended survival statistic is a controlled row comparison of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or position-bin controls, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, local codon-count baselines, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability checks, rank sufficiency, and ablations against simpler baselines. The current Wangen facts do not compute this quantity. They supply finite translation-readthrough measurements that may later serve as part of a cross-layer panel if the missing same-row sequence, wobble, and mRNA controls are attached.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows from the $4982$ nonzero G418 rows, the $7658$ prefilter join size, the nonzero fraction $0.65056$, or the cross-dataset untreated $\rho=0.032718$. These numbers do not establish protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or universality. They also do not turn modeled tAI or stAI weights into observed ribosome occupancy, and they do not turn codon-window geometry into a biological outcome. The only local claim licensed here is that a finite Wangen stop-context readthrough contact exists with explicit row counts and a bounded rank-scaled endpoint. Any stronger biological statement requires a separate row-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Operational reading.}
For the standalone BioReality record, this chapter should be read as a bounded contact certificate for the stop-context readthrough layer. The G418 sheet provides a concrete perturbation-conditioned table; the larger Wangen stop-readthrough check provides a broader weak-baseline comparison; and the $f_3$-stress proposal remains a testable cross-layer request rather than an accepted survival claim. A later positive result would require the same organism, same CDS or coding window, codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA, and position-resolved readout to live on one finite row table before any $f_3$-stress term is said to survive.
