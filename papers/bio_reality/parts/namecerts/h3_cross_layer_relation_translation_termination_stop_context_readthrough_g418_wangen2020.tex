\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation-termination contact}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translation-termination-stop-context-readthrough-g418-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite table.}
The contact is a finite translation-termination table rather than a free-standing claim about coding-sequence geometry.  The verified row surface is the Wangen-style G418 readthrough sheet named \emph{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}.  The recorded endpoint is the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, with rank-average scaling to $[0,1]$.  The join produced $7658$ rows before filtering to nonzero RRTS entries, and the retained analysis panel has $4982$ rows.  The nonzero fraction is $0.65056$, so the actual readthrough-bearing surface is a proper subset of the joined table rather than the whole join.  The recorded run identifier is \texttt{fe18fff6841f}, with runtime $303.534$ seconds and verification timestamp \texttt{2026-06-22T20:29:56+00:00}.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
The proposed BEDC-side coordinate is the $f_3$-stress synonymous residual in coding-sequence windows after quotienting away amino-acid identity.  The intended finite row has a residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved translation readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  In this stop-context instance, the available verified table contributes a termination-layer readout: nonzero G418-induced RRTS values ranked into a bounded response scale.  It does not by itself provide the full same-window $f_3$-stress survival matrix, because the supplied facts do not include the joined codon-window coordinate rows, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA rows, and position-bin design matrix needed to compute the proposed statistic.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
The admissible statistic for this chapter is a survival entry of the form $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ is the bounded termination or readthrough readout and $Z$ contains the controls.  The minimum controlled design is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = \{&\text{amino-acid or position bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \text{CDS length},\ \text{M-density},\\
&\text{local codon-count baseline},\ \text{matched mRNA abundance},\ \text{coverage and mappability filters}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite Wangen panel supplies a concrete response surface with $4982$ nonzero retained rows, but it is not yet a certificate that $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0$.  A positive call would require held-out improvement after the listed controls and after ablation against simpler codon-usage or position-only baselines.  The present record therefore supports the existence of a bounded termination-readout contact and fixes its row counts; it does not close the residual-coordinate survival test.

\paragraph{What the G418 table can support.}
The table can support a narrow claim: a finite, condition-specific translation-termination endpoint exists for the exact readthrough setting, and its nonzero analysis subset is large enough to define a rank-scaled response variable.  The numbers are concrete: $7658$ joined rows, $4982$ nonzero retained rows, and nonzero fraction $0.65056$.  These values make the contact usable as a candidate readout surface for a later same-row residual test.  They also keep the claim condition-specific: the sheet is a G418 $2.0\,\mathrm{mg}/\mathrm{mL}$, $24$ hour merged table, so the biological contact is drug-induced readthrough under that condition, not basal stop-codon behavior across all contexts.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The row counts and rank-scaled RRTS endpoint do not imply translation realization from $f_3$-stress alone.  They do not imply protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are also not observed ribosome occupancy in this chapter.  A wobble-weight asymmetry may motivate the coordinate, but it cannot promote the coordinate to a translation claim unless the same rows contain the readout, the controls, and a positive held-out survival step.  Thus the local conclusion is deliberately bounded: the Wangen G418 table is an eligible termination-layer contact for a controlled $f_3$-stress test, while the full cross-layer survival statement remains conditional on the missing joined matrix and the positive ablation result.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
This chapter records a ready finite-row termination-readout surface, not a completed $f_3$ survival theorem.  The concrete evidence is the verified G418-induced nonzero RRTS panel with $4982$ retained rows from $7658$ joined rows.  The correct use of this evidence is to anchor the response variable for a future controlled statistic.  The incorrect use would be to treat the existence of the response table, the contact name, or the $f_3$ residual coordinate as proof of translation, structure, function, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a general biological law.
