\subsection{G418 stop-context readthrough as a bounded translation-termination contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-g418-stop-context-readthrough-wangen2020}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite biological surface used here is the Wangen-style G418 stop-context readthrough table for translation termination.  The verified claim row is \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020}.  Its audited endpoint is the G418-induced RRTS nonzero subset, rank-average scaled to $[0,1]$, from the sheet \texttt{G418 2.0mg|mL 24hr merged}.  The join produced $7658$ rows before the nonzero-RRTS filter and retained $4982$ rows after that filter, with nonzero fraction $0.65056$ and recorded runtime $303.534$ seconds.  Thus the datum is not a loose biological label: it is a finite table whose rows are selected by an explicit inner join and by an explicit nonzero readthrough condition.

\paragraph{Observed contact.}
This contact measures a termination-layer response under aminoglycoside treatment.  The row value is a rank-scaled readthrough readout, not a direct measurement of protein abundance, folding, localization, function, or organismal phenotype.  The finite-row interpretation is therefore confined to the event that a stop-context reporter exhibits nonzero induced readthrough in the retained table.  The companion Wangen cross-dataset stop-readthrough audit gives a broader finite surface with $55886$ rows, runtime $133.552797$ seconds, and untreated Spearman-style summary $\rho=0.032718$.  That second surface helps anchor the readthrough-contact family, while the G418 chapter row remains the treatment-specific termination contact.

\paragraph{Coordinate tested by this chapter.}
The proposed BEDC-side coordinate is the $f_3$-stress residual member of $B^*_{Q6}$ after amino-acid quotienting.  The chapter does not identify that coordinate with the readthrough measurement.  Instead, it asks whether a future joined table could place an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, wobble-weight context, matched mRNA control, amino-acid or position-bin controls, and a position-resolved translation readout on the same finite row.  The relevant survival entry would have the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or position bin, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability limits, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The G418 stop-context table supplies a real translation-termination readout surface, but it does not by itself supply the full same-window $f_3$ statistic.

\paragraph{What the verified numbers license.}
The retained $4982$ nonzero G418 rows license only a bounded statement: stop-context readthrough can be represented as a finite translation-termination response table after explicit joining and filtering.  The prefilter count $7658$ and nonzero fraction $0.65056$ are important because they show that the chapter is not treating a named dataset as evidence without a row-level admissibility step.  The broader $55886$-row stop-readthrough surface, with untreated $\rho=0.032718$, is cited only as a related readthrough-contact audit, not as a substitute for the G418-specific statistic.  Together these facts support a termination-readout contact; they do not support an unconditioned law of codon geometry.

\paragraph{Required same-row survival test.}
For the $f_3$ claim to move beyond boundary status, the finite table must be enlarged so that every candidate row carries the following fields: an ordered coding-sequence window, a stop-context or position-resolved translation readout, local synonymous codon counts, an $f_3$ residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, bounded wobble weights with observed asymmetry $w_c\neq w_c^{f_3}$, matched mRNA abundance, and position or amino-acid-bin controls.  The statistical break condition is failure of held-out improvement after the full control set and after simpler codon baselines.  In display form, the positive case would require
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}(f_3;T_{\mathrm{position}},Z) &> 0,\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{ablation}}(f_3;T_{\mathrm{position}},Z,\mathrm{baseline}) &> 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The current G418 table gives the $T$-side termination response, but the same-row $f_3$ coordinate, wobble surface, mRNA denominator, and full ablation matrix are not present in the verified row.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows from $f_3$-stress geometry alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal rule follows from the G418 readthrough table alone.  The rank-scaled RRTS endpoint is a translation-termination contact under a specific treatment and filter.  A separate layer-matched contact would be needed for protein abundance; a separate structural or physical assay would be needed for folding or admissibility; a separate perturbation or functional assay would be needed for function or mechanism; and a separate population of controlled finite rows would be needed before any universality language could be considered.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
This chapter is ready as a bounded BioReality record because it has concrete row counts, an explicit endpoint, a named treatment sheet, a nonzero-filter fraction, and a related cross-dataset readthrough audit.  Its scientific role is to establish that a finite translation-termination readout surface exists for G418-induced stop-context readthrough.  Its scientific limit is equally strict: it remains only a contact and boundary record for the proposed $f_3$ cross-layer survival statistic until the same-row codon, wobble-weight, mRNA, position-window, and control fields are joined and the held-out survival condition is computed.
