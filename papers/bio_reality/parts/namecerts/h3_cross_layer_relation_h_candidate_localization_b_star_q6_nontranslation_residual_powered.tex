\subsection{Localization as a Non-translation Residual Candidate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-localization-nontranslation-residual}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite surface tested here consists of nine residual synonymous coordinates, the usual $Z$ controls, a modeled translation-complement abundance component, and a twelve-column multi-label localization readout. The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The control family has $24$ columns before rank reduction and uses an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected coordinates. In each organism the residualized control rank is $rank(Z)=23$, and the localization readout has $K=12$ active category columns.

\paragraph{Rows and localization categories.}
The finite joins are not inferred from a symbolic model; they are row-level joins between coding sequences and reviewed UniProtKB subcellular localization annotations. The three organism panels have
$$
\begin{aligned}
Danio\ rerio &: 1984\ joined\ CDS\ rows,\ 2821\ localization\ records,\ 14410\ reported\ CDS\ abundance\ joins,\\
Homo\ sapiens &: 16008\ joined\ CDS\ rows,\ 16406\ localization\ records,\ 19053\ reported\ CDS\ abundance\ joins,\\
Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae &: 4656\ joined\ CDS\ rows,\ 4831\ localization\ records,\ 5815\ reported\ CDS\ abundance\ joins.
\end{aligned}
$$
The localization basis is deliberately coarse and multi-label. For human, the largest category counts are $7119$ Membrane, $6305$ Cytoplasm, $5837$ Nucleus, $3570$ Cell membrane, $1876$ Secreted, $1529$ Cytoskeleton, $1449$ Endoplasmic reticulum, $1370$ Mitochondrion, $1096$ Golgi, $482$ Vacuole/Lysosome, $161$ Other, and $109$ Peroxisome. Yeast gives $1863$ Membrane, $1736$ Cytoplasm, $1733$ Nucleus, $811$ Mitochondrion, $426$ Endoplasmic reticulum, $274$ Cell membrane, $224$ Golgi, $217$ Vacuole/Lysosome, $165$ Cytoskeleton, $91$ Secreted, $77$ Peroxisome, and $44$ Other. Zebrafish gives $883$ Cytoplasm, $845$ Nucleus, $708$ Membrane, $247$ Mitochondrion, $230$ Cell membrane, $207$ Endoplasmic reticulum, $182$ Cytoskeleton, $125$ Golgi, $79$ Secreted, $45$ Vacuole/Lysosome, $23$ Other, and $14$ Peroxisome.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
Let $P$ be log protein abundance after the $Z$ residualization, let $T\_{mod}$ be the modeled tAI readout, and let $P\_{Q\perp Tmod}$ denote the modeled-translation complement of the $Q$-explained abundance component on the active localization join. The localization test projects $P\_{Q\perp Tmod}$ onto the residualized twelve-column localization matrix $L$ and records the absorbed fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
a\_L = \frac{\Vert \Pi\_{L\mid Z} P\_{Q\perp Tmod}\Vert^2}{\Vert P\_{Q\perp Tmod}\Vert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed values are $a\_L=0.009508864544725801$ for zebrafish, $a\_L=0.009254913329269913$ for human, and $a\_L=0.011225105608516642$ for yeast, with $rank(L)=12$ in all three cases. A $K$-matched Gaussian null with $200$ trials gives null means and $95$th percentiles
$$
\begin{aligned}
Danio\ rerio &: mean=0.0062549946178242525,\ p95=0.011321328712802819,\\
Homo\ sapiens &: mean=0.0008032852113983743,\ p95=0.0013758521256775384,\\
Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae &: mean=0.002689902676583632,\ p95=0.004958116973149795.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the human and yeast localization projections exceed their degree-of-freedom matched null boundaries, while the zebrafish projection does not exceed its null $95$th percentile. The result is therefore a finite H-candidate signal in human and yeast and a reported null-side localization outcome in zebrafish.

\paragraph{Modeled-complement consistency.}
The same computation records whether the modeled complement on the localization join remains close to the full abundance-CDS complement reference. Human has $d\_{modeled}=0.9020707321191324$ on the localization join versus $0.9208949355679455$ on the full abundance-CDS reference, with absolute difference $0.018824203448813193$, inside the $0.02$ tolerance. Yeast has $d\_{modeled}=0.1997853864617063$ versus $0.21173219572706703$, with absolute difference $0.011946809265360736$, also inside tolerance. Zebrafish has $d\_{modeled}=0.7608723943210729$ versus $0.874628721049266$, with absolute difference $0.11375632672819314$, outside tolerance; this is not hidden, because the localization-available subset has only $1984$ joined rows compared with the $14410$ full abundance-CDS join.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite row record.}
Direct protein-abundance survival is already positive in the larger protein-omics panels, but that fact alone is not a mechanism. The direct $R^2\_{QP}$ values include $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish, and $0.061906245886660105$ for \textit{B. subtilis}; all fifteen listed organisms have nine surviving coordinates under the stated positive partial-$R^2$ threshold. Measured translation-efficiency survival is smaller and condition-specific: $R^2\_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.009394266754822262$ in \textit{E. coli}. The measured mediation fractions $M^{QTP,meas}$ are $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.0$ in zebrafish after clipping a negative raw fraction. These numbers justify treating localization as an H-candidate for the residual part that is not already absorbed by the measured or modeled translation readout, rather than as a replacement for the translation test.

\paragraph{Auxiliary row diagnostics.}
The broader row record also contains independent finite checks that prevent over-reading the localization statistic. In the joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability partition, the joint mediation fractions are $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, $0.16890712099923358$ in human, and $0.2404669586626661$ in \textit{E. coli}, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ under the fixed pair-permutation design. The yeast $f3\_stress$ dwell test has $n=1257$, base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$, and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. The positional dwell test classifies the leading segment as $5prime$, with the $5prime$ arm passing and the $3prime$ arm not passing. The structural-order positional module is classified as $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$: its weighted $f3$ ramp held-out increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip value is $0.05859375$. The partial mediation certificate uses $\lambda\_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho\_{join}=0.05$, $n\_{join}=1800$, $120$ permutations, selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, and selected coverage $C^\star=0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$. The irreducibility audit uses the same $\lambda\_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho\_{join}=0.05$, $n\_{join}=1800$, and $120$ permutations, and its status semantics say only that the currently registered reduction family was tested.

\paragraph{Verdict and boundary.}
The chapter verdict is a bounded localization-residual result. Human and yeast show localization absorption above a matched null for the modeled-translation complement; zebrafish is reported as below the null boundary on the localization-available subset. This supports the statement that reviewed subcellular localization can carry part of the residual protein-abundance component left after the modeled translation readout in at least two finite organism panels. It does not by itself establish translation, protein trafficking mechanism, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causality, evolutionary direction, or universality. Those claims require separate measured contacts and their own controlled finite-row statistics. A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association remains only a protein-omics association unless a measured $q_i\to T_j$ step survives and conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the downstream $q_i\to P_k$ signal.
