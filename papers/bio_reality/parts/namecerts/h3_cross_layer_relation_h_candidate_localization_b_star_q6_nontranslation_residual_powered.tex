\subsection{Localization Absorption for the Non-Translation Residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-localization-absorption}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.} The record tests whether a coarse protein-localization readout carries any measurable part of the abundance component that remains after the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon residuals have been separated from a modeled translation readout. The finite carrier is a per-CDS matrix with nine synonymous residual coordinates, a controlled abundance target, and a twelve-column multi-label localization readout. The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The control set has $24$ columns before rank reduction: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined from the union support of the nine projected coordinates. In all three localization panels the effective control rank is $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.

\paragraph{Projection definition.} For each organism, let $X_Q$ be the $n\times 9$ matrix of residual coordinates, $Z$ the control matrix, $P$ the measured protein-abundance vector, $T_{\mathrm{mod}}$ the modeled tAI-style translation readout, and $L$ the $n\times 12$ residualized localization one-hot matrix. The tested target is not total abundance and not translation efficiency. It is the modeled-translation complement of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance component, written schematically as $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$. The reported statistic is the fraction of that target absorbed by the residualized localization columns, compared with a Gaussian readout null having the same number of localization degrees of freedom:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_L &= R^2\bigl(\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\sim L\mid Z\bigr),\\
\mathrm{signal} &\text{ is interpreted only when } A_L > Q_{0.95}^{\mathrm{dof\ null}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This makes the localization test an $H$-candidate screen: it asks whether the remaining abundance component aligns with compartment annotations after the translation-modeled part has been removed. It does not establish a causal path, a trafficking mechanism, or a functional consequence.

\paragraph{Joined rows and localization modules.} The finite row counts are organism-specific. For \emph{Danio rerio}, the localization join contains $n=1984$ CDS/protein rows, drawn from $2821$ reviewed localization records, against a background CDS abundance join count of $14410$; the localization matrix has $K=12$ columns and rank $12$. For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the join contains $n=16008$ rows, $16406$ localization records, background CDS join count $19053$, $K=12$, and rank $12$. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the join contains $n=4656$ rows, $4831$ localization records, background CDS join count $5815$, $K=12$, and rank $12$. The categories are the same twelve module labels in each organism: Cell membrane, Cytoplasm, Cytoskeleton, Endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi, Membrane, Mitochondrion, Nucleus, Other, Peroxisome, Secreted, and Vacuole/Lysosome. The category masses are visibly uneven, so the null comparison is essential. For example, the human panel has $7119$ Membrane labels, $6305$ Cytoplasm labels, $5837$ Nucleus labels, and only $109$ Peroxisome labels; the yeast panel has $1863$ Membrane, $1736$ Cytoplasm, $1733$ Nucleus, and $77$ Peroxisome labels; the zebrafish panel has $883$ Cytoplasm, $845$ Nucleus, $708$ Membrane, and $14$ Peroxisome labels.

\paragraph{Absorption readback.} The localization absorption values are small but finite. In zebrafish, localization absorbs $A_L=0.009508864544725801$ of the modeled non-translation abundance component, while the degree-of-freedom matched null has mean $0.0062549946178242525$ and $95$th percentile $0.011321328712802819$ over $200$ trials. The zebrafish value is therefore below the null $95$th percentile. In human, localization absorbs $A_L=0.009254913329269913$, against null mean $0.0008032852113983743$ and null $95$th percentile $0.0013758521256775384$ over $200$ trials; this is an above-null localization alignment. In yeast, localization absorbs $A_L=0.011225105608516642$, against null mean $0.002689902676583632$ and null $95$th percentile $0.004958116973149795$ over $200$ trials; this is also above-null. In compact form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio}:&\quad 0.0095088645 < 0.0113213287,\\
\mathrm{Human}:&\quad 0.0092549133 > 0.0013758521,\\
\mathrm{Yeast}:&\quad 0.0112251056 > 0.0049581170.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the localization readout is not uniformly positive across the three panels. It passes the finite null screen in human and yeast, while zebrafish remains below the stated null boundary on the localization-available join.

\paragraph{Join sensitivity of the residual target.} The statistic also records how much the localization-available join changes the modeled complement relative to the full abundance-CDS join. The modeled complement fraction $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}$ is $0.7608723943210729$ on the zebrafish localization join, compared with $0.874628721049266$ on the full abundance-CDS reference join, an absolute difference of $0.11375632672819314$. This fails the $0.02$ matching tolerance and is reported as join sensitivity rather than hidden. Human has $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191324$ on the localization join and $0.9208949355679455$ on the full reference join, with absolute difference $0.018824203448813193$, within the $0.02$ tolerance. Yeast has $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.1997853864617063$ on the localization join and $0.21173219572706703$ on the full reference join, with absolute difference $0.011946809265360736$, also within tolerance. The strongest interpretation is therefore organism-local: human and yeast show above-null localization absorption on joins whose modeled-complement fraction remains close to the reference abundance join, whereas zebrafish has both a below-null localization absorption value and a larger join-induced shift.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and protein-abundance surfaces.} The same project records that direct $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance survival is present as an omics association in many organisms, but that fact alone is not a localization or mechanism statement. On the broad protein-abundance surface, the reported $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, and $0.04613529807085213$ for zebrafish, with all nine coordinates surviving under the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold in those panels. Separately, measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency survival gives $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$ for human, $0.13681931343730436$ for yeast, $0.04034217614654355$ for zebrafish, and $0.009394266754822262$ for \emph{E. coli}. The measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition reports mediated fractions $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.0$ for zebrafish after truncating a raw suppressive value $-0.04801486048721136$, and $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}. These finite numbers keep the layers separated: direct abundance association, measured translation association, measured translation-conditioned abundance decomposition, and localization absorption are different readouts.

\paragraph{Verdict.} The localization test is ready as a finite-row BioReality chapter because the rows, controls, residual coordinates, localization modules, absorption values, null values, and join diagnostics are all present. Its positive content is narrow: coarse reviewed subcellular localization absorbs an above-null fraction of the modeled non-translation abundance residual in human and yeast, but not in zebrafish under the same stated null criterion. The result is an $H$-candidate localization signal, not an explanation of how codon choice produces localization. It also does not claim translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universality. Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched measurement and a new finite statistic showing that the relevant readout survives the appropriate controls.
