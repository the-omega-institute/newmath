\subsection{Localization as a non-translation residual candidate for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-localization-nontranslation-residual}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The row object in this chapter is the matched protein table on which a $9$-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual design is compared with a coarse subcellular-localization readout after the usual sequence controls have been removed.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In each localization fit the control-column count is $24$ and the residualized control rank is $23$.

\paragraph{Residual being tested.}
The tested response is not total protein abundance itself and not a translation readout.  It is the modeled-tAI complement of the protein-abundance component, written locally as $P_{\widehat{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}}$: the part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance projection not accounted for by the modeled translation surface in the companion abundance calculation.  The finite comparison asks whether residualized multi-label localization columns absorb a non-negligible fraction of this complement.  The fitted statistic is therefore descriptive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_L &= \frac{\|\Pi_{L\mid Z} P_{\widehat{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}}\|^2}{\|P_{\widehat{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}}\|^2},\\
L &= \text{the residualized $12$-column localization indicator design.}
\end{aligned}
$$
A $12$-degree-of-freedom Gaussian null with $200$ deterministic trials is used to decide whether a positive absorbed fraction is larger than the mechanical absorption expected from a $K=12$ multi-column readout.

\paragraph{Joined rows and localization modules.}
Three organisms have finite joined localization surfaces.  For \emph{Danio rerio}, $14{,}410$ CDS rows were available in the abundance join, but $1{,}984$ rows joined the localization table; the localization payload contains $2{,}821$ records. For \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19{,}053$ CDS rows were available and $16{,}008$ joined the localization table; the localization payload contains $16{,}406$ records with counts Cell membrane $3{,}570$, Cytoplasm $6{,}305$, Cytoskeleton $1{,}529$, Endoplasmic reticulum $1{,}449$, Golgi $1{,}096$, Membrane $7{,}119$, Mitochondrion $1{,}370$, Nucleus $5{,}837$, Other $161$, Peroxisome $109$, Secreted $1{,}876$, and Vacuole/Lysosome $482$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5{,}815$ CDS rows were available and $4{,}656$ joined the localization table; the localization payload contains $4{,}831$ records with counts Cell membrane $274$, Cytoplasm $1{,}736$, Cytoskeleton $165$, Endoplasmic reticulum $426$, Golgi $224$, Membrane $1{,}863$, Mitochondrion $811$, Nucleus $1{,}733$, Other $44$, Peroxisome $77$, Secreted $91$, and Vacuole/Lysosome $217$.

\paragraph{Absorption verdict.}
The localization projection is finite and non-vacuous in all three organisms, but it separates into one null-level row and two above-null rows.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, localization absorbs $0.009508864544725801$ of the modeled complement, while the $K$-matched null has mean $0.0062549946178242525$ and $95$th percentile $0.011321328712802819$; this is below the null cutoff.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, localization absorbs $0.009254913329269913$, above the null mean $0.0008032852113983743$ and null $95$th percentile $0.0013758521256775384$.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, localization absorbs $0.011225105608516642$, above the null mean $0.002689902676583632$ and null $95$th percentile $0.004958116973149795$.  Thus the finite readback is a positive localization-candidate signal for human and yeast, and a computed but not above-null localization signal for zebrafish.

\paragraph{Join sensitivity.}
The modeled complement is stable enough to use in human and yeast and unstable enough to mark the zebrafish row as join-sensitive.  The full-abundance reference and current localization join give $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9208949355679455$ and $0.9020707321191324$ in human, an absolute difference of $0.018824203448813193$, within the $0.02$ tolerance.  Yeast gives $0.21173219572706703$ and $0.1997853864617063$, an absolute difference of $0.011946809265360736$, also within tolerance.  Zebrafish gives $0.874628721049266$ and $0.7608723943210729$, an absolute difference of $0.11375632672819314$, so the zebrafish localization availability changes the row surface substantially.  The zebrafish result is therefore reported as a computed finite projection but not as evidence that localization carries the residual.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding measured surfaces.}
The localization candidate is downstream of a broader $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance phenomenon.  In the direct protein-omics surface, all $15$ organisms have rank-sufficient $9$-coordinate residual designs, and every listed organism has $9$ surviving coordinates against PAXdb protein abundance after the same sequence controls.  The corresponding $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.10217906758013379$ in \emph{C. elegans}, $0.0646471273126996$ in \emph{P. aeruginosa}, $0.06397514251295629$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}, $0.061906245886660105$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse, $0.04613529807085213$ in zebrafish, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.03980793596647631$ in chicken, $0.03209009170649934$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, $0.02763998603083845$ in rat, and $0.019256877907572044$ in \emph{H. salinarum}.  Measured ribosome-TE survival is also finite in four organisms, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.009394266754822262$ in \emph{E. coli}.  These values motivate looking for non-translation residual carriers, but they do not by themselves make localization a mechanism.

\paragraph{Mediation context.}
The measured TE-to-abundance decomposition shows why the localization row is interpreted as an $H$-candidate rather than as a translation result.  The measured mediated fraction is $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.0$ in zebrafish after truncating a raw suppressive value of $-0.04801486048721136$.  Joint TE and mRNA-stability conditioning likewise leaves substantial direct signal: the joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, $0.2404669586626661$ in \emph{E. coli}, and $0.16890712099923358$ in human, each with right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  The finite row therefore supports a narrower reading: localization can absorb part of the remaining modeled complement in human and yeast, while translation and mRNA-stability readouts do not exhaust the abundance residual.

\paragraph{Other residual carriers.}
Several companion finite rows locate other pieces of the same residual field.  In yeast, $f_3$-stress dwell mediation has $n_{join}=1257$, base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, and $f_3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell row classifies the leading segment as $5'$ with the three-prime body not passing.  The structural-ramp row reports a positive ramp but not a passed localization gate: weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip $p=0.05859375$.  A partial readout-dictionary certificate on yeast uses $n_{join}=1800$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations; the selection-aware coverage is $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ and selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$.  These numbers place localization among several finite residual carriers, not as a sole explanation.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter licenses only the following statement: after sequence controls and a modeled-translation complement projection, human and yeast localization annotations absorb more of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance complement than a $12$-column degree-of-freedom-matched null; zebrafish does not clear that cutoff and is join-sensitive.  Nothing in this row proves translation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Localization is a coarse multi-label UniProt annotation, the absorbed fraction is a projection statistic, and a direct $q_i\to P_k$ or $q_i\to L$ association cannot be promoted to a causal or mechanistic claim unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies the missing contact and a controlled finite statistic at that layer.
