\subsection{Localization as an $H$-candidate for the non-translation residual}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-h-candidate-localization}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The tested object is the residual protein-abundance component left after the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-usage coordinates have already been compared with a modeled translation readout.  The experiment uses nine projected synonymous residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  Each coordinate is residualized against the same covariate basis: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons supported by the union of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$ in each localization fit.

\paragraph{Localization readout.}
The candidate higher readout is reviewed UniProtKB subcellular localization, parsed as a twelve-column multi-label categorical matrix.  The categories are Cell membrane, Cytoplasm, Cytoskeleton, Endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi, Membrane, Mitochondrion, Nucleus, Other, Peroxisome, Secreted, and Vacuole/Lysosome.  The joined finite panels are organism-specific: Danio rerio has $1984$ joined CDS rows from $2821$ localization records, Homo sapiens has $16008$ joined CDS rows from $16406$ localization records, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $4656$ joined CDS rows from $4831$ localization records.  The corresponding full CDS abundance joins reported by the abundance layer were $14410$, $19053$, and $5815$ rows, so the localization analysis is explicitly a restricted readout join, not the full proteomics surface.

\paragraph{Observed category mass.}
The localization matrices are not synthetic rank labels.  In Danio rerio the twelve category counts are $230,883,182,207,125,708,247,845,23,14,79,45$.  In Homo sapiens the counts are $3570,6305,1529,1449,1096,7119,1370,5837,161,109,1876,482$.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae the counts are $274,1736,165,426,224,1863,811,1733,44,77,91,217$.  These finite counts are used only as a coarse protein-location annotation surface; they are not treated as trafficking kinetics, compartmental mechanism, or a perturbation assay.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
Let $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ denote the modeled-translation complement of the abundance component attached to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual design after the covariates above have been removed.  Let $L$ be the residualized twelve-column localization matrix on the same joined rows.  The reported statistic is the fraction of the complement component absorbed by projecting onto $L$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_L &= \frac{\|\Pi_L P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2}{\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted localization rank is $12$ in all three organisms.  To keep the multi-column readout honest, the same degrees of freedom are matched by a Gaussian null with $200$ trials per organism.  Thus only excess over the organism-specific null $95$th percentile is read as evidence that the localization annotation carries part of the non-translation residual.

\paragraph{Finite results.}
The absorption fractions are $A_L=0.0095088645$ in Danio rerio, $A_L=0.0092549133$ in Homo sapiens, and $A_L=0.0112251056$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The dof-matched null means and $95$th percentiles are, respectively, $0.0062549946$ and $0.0113213287$ for Danio rerio, $0.0008032852$ and $0.0013758521$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.0026899027$ and $0.0049581170$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  Consequently the localization candidate is above the null boundary in human and yeast, but not in zebrafish:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio}:&\quad 0.0095088645 < 0.0113213287,\\
\mathrm{Human}:&\quad 0.0092549133 > 0.0013758521,\\
\mathrm{Yeast}:&\quad 0.0112251056 > 0.0049581170.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a readout-specific residual localization signal in two of the three tested finite panels.  It is not a universal localization law, and it does not make the zebrafish restricted join positive.

\paragraph{Join-sensitivity audit.}
The modeled-complement fraction on the restricted localization join was also compared with the reference full abundance-CDS complement.  For Danio rerio the reference value was $0.8746287210$ and the localization-join value was $0.7608723943$, an absolute difference of $0.1137563267$, outside the $0.02$ diagnostic tolerance.  For Homo sapiens the reference value was $0.9208949356$ and the localization-join value was $0.9020707321$, an absolute difference of $0.0188242034$, inside tolerance.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae the reference value was $0.2117321957$ and the localization-join value was $0.1997853865$, an absolute difference of $0.0119468093$, also inside tolerance.  The positive human and yeast localization absorptions therefore sit on joins whose modeled-complement diagnostic remains close to the full abundance reference, while the zebrafish localization join is reported as a changed surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the neighboring readouts.}
The localization test is downstream of a broader finite row of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ cross-layer probes.  Direct protein-abundance residual survival $S^{QP}$ was positive in a powered protein-omics panel with all nine coordinates surviving in each organism; the recorded $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.1076752668$ in Escherichia coli, $0.2983762499$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $0.0445456512$ in Homo sapiens, $0.0498279841$ in Mus musculus, and $0.0192568779$ in Halobacterium salinarum.  Measured ribosome-TE survival was also computed on matched panels, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.0403421761$ in Danio rerio, $0.0093942668$ in Escherichia coli, $0.0188379113$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.1368193134$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  The measured mediation fractions $M^{QTP,meas}$ were $0$ in Danio rerio after truncating a negative raw fraction, $0.0409115834$ in Escherichia coli, $0.0027966485$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.4793069656$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  These numbers explain why the localization calculation is framed as an $H$-candidate: it looks at the complement not absorbed by the modeled translation readout, rather than promoting direct $Q\to P$ association into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Reduction context.}
Other finite reductions keep the same boundary.  A joint TE-plus-mRNA-stability variance partition gave mediation fractions $0.2404669587$ in Escherichia coli, $0.1689071210$ in Homo sapiens, and $0.5195182226$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, each with right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  A yeast partial mediation dictionary with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n_{join}=1800$, and $120$ permutations selected readouts from $\{\mathrm{measured\ TE},\mathrm{mRNA\ stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$ and covered $C^{\ast}=0.2453821144$ of the source signal, leaving $0.7546178856$ unexplained.  The $f3\_stress$ dwell experiment in yeast used $n_{join}=1257$, five folds, and $120$ permutations; it reported held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0185647394$ against a null $95$th percentile of $0.0013115362$, with $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441451$ and approximate $p=7.8739160572\times 10^{-9}$.  Its positional version classified the leading segment as $5'$ and the dominant segment as $5'$.  A structural-order ramp probe was classified as $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$, with weighted ramp $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640$, body $\Delta R^2=-0.0002615731$, ramp-minus-body $0.0013781371$, and sign-flip probabilities $0.0478515625$, $0.54296875$, and $0.05859375$.  These neighboring results support the interpretation that the residual has several measurable projections, while also showing that no single projection closes the biology.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter records a positive localization $H$-candidate in human and yeast, a null localization excess in the zebrafish restricted join, and a join-sensitivity warning for zebrafish.  The admissible statement is therefore narrow: reviewed subcellular localization annotations absorb a small but null-exceeding fraction of the modeled-translation complement in two powered panels after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls.  No translation event, folding property, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter.  Any such claim would require a separate matched contact and its own finite statistic on the relevant layer.
