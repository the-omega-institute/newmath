\subsection{Localization as a non-translation residual readout for powered $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-h-candidate-localization-b-star-q6-nontranslation-residual-powered}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record tests whether the protein-abundance component left after a modeled translation readout has been removed is partly aligned with coarse subcellular localization.  The carrier is a finite matrix surface over nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, a fixed covariate basis $Z$, a modeled translation complement $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$, and twelve multi-label localization columns.  The nine coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-}stress$.  The control basis contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In each organism the fitted control rank is $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ with $24$ control columns before rank reduction.

\paragraph{Joined localization surface.}
The localization readout is the reviewed UniProtKB subcellular-location annotation, parsed as a twelve-column multi-label categorical design.  The finite rows are not the full proteomics rows; they are the subset for which codon counts, abundance, and reviewed localization can all be joined.  For \emph{Danio rerio}, the localization join contains $1984$ proteins from $2821$ localization records, while the wider abundance-CDS join reports $14410$ CDS rows.  For human, the corresponding values are $16008$ joined proteins, $16406$ localization records, and $19053$ abundance-CDS rows.  For yeast, the values are $4656$ joined proteins, $4831$ localization records, and $5815$ abundance-CDS rows.  The localization categories retained in the design are Cell membrane, Cytoplasm, Cytoskeleton, Endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi, Membrane, Mitochondrion, Nucleus, Other, Peroxisome, Secreted, and Vacuole/Lysosome.

\paragraph{Observed absorption.}
The statistic is the fraction of the modeled translation-complement abundance component absorbed by the residualized localization design.  With $K=12$ localization columns and $\operatorname{rank}(L)=12$, the observed absorbed fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.009508864544725801,\\
D_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.009254913329269913,\\
D_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.011225105608516642.
\end{aligned}
$$
A degree-of-freedom matched Gaussian null with $200$ trials gives null $95$th percentiles $0.011321328712802819$ for danio, $0.0013758521256775384$ for human, and $0.004958116973149795$ for yeast.  Thus the human and yeast localization projections exceed the matched null boundary, while the danio projection does not exceed it.  The readback is therefore not a universal localization conclusion; it is a finite-row signal in two of the three joined localization surfaces.

\paragraph{Complement consistency.}
The modeled complement itself is checked against the full protein-abundance complement experiment.  On the localization join, the modeled-complement fractions are $0.7608723943210729$ for danio, $0.9020707321191324$ for human, and $0.1997853864617063$ for yeast.  The corresponding full abundance-CDS complement references are $0.874628721049266$, $0.9208949355679455$, and $0.21173219572706703$.  Human differs by $0.018824203448813193$ and yeast differs by $0.011946809265360736$, both within the declared $0.02$ tolerance; danio differs by $0.11375632672819314$, reflecting the much smaller localization-available join.  The danio row is therefore retained as a reported finite row, but not promoted to the same complement-stability status as the human and yeast rows.

\paragraph{Relation to the powered cross-layer panel.}
The localization calculation is downstream of a stronger protein-abundance survival surface.  Direct $Q\to P$ protein-omics survival against measured PAXdb abundance is positive for all nine coordinates across fifteen organisms; examples include $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.10217906758013379$ in \emph{C. elegans}, $0.0646471273126996$ in \emph{P. aeruginosa} PAO1, $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.019256877907572044$ in \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}.  Measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency survival is also finite-row positive in the four measured-TE organisms: $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.04034217614654355$ in danio, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.009394266754822262$ in \emph{E. coli}.  The measured mediation fractions remain organism-specific: $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0$ in danio after truncating the negative raw value $-0.04801486048721136$.

\paragraph{Why localization is an $H$-candidate rather than a translation claim.}
The localization test is deliberately aimed at the residual left outside the modeled translation explanation.  It does not say that localization is caused by codon usage, nor that localization is the mechanism of the original $Q\to P$ association.  In the broader variance-partition surface, joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability mediation fractions are $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, $0.2404669586626661$ for \emph{E. coli}, and $0.16890712099923358$ for human, with right-tail permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ in each of those three organisms.  A separate yeast dictionary reduction with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n_{join}=1800$, and $120$ permutations obtains selection-aware coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The localization projection is one finite attempt to account for part of this remaining component, not a closure of the residual.

\paragraph{Readback and boundary.}
The chapter-specific readback is: human and yeast show localization absorption above a twelve-column degree-of-freedom matched null; danio does not.  Yeast also has a separate $f_3$ dwell mediation signal with held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ for the $f_3$--dwell correlation, while the structural-order positional module is classified as $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$ with weighted ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$ and body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$.  These numbers keep the localization result in its proper place: a coarse categorical absorption statistic for a non-translation residual component.  No translation realization, folding conclusion, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this calculation.  Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite-row statistic.
