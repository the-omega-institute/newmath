\paragraph{Question.}
The finite test asks whether the portion of protein-abundance signal left after the modelled translation readout has been removed has any measurable relation to coarse protein localization annotations.  The source coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual directions
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  For each joined coding sequence, the abundance residual first removes the fixed covariate block $Z$: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the union support of those nine projected $q$-vectors.  The experiment then forms the modelled-tAI complement of the $Q$-explained abundance component and projects that residual component onto residualized multi-label localization columns.

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The localization readout has $K=12$ annotation columns in each organism.  The joined matrices are not equally powered: zebrafish has $1{,}984$ localization-joined coding sequences out of $14{,}410$ reported CDS abundance joins, human has $16{,}008$ out of $19{,}053$, and budding yeast has $4{,}656$ out of $5{,}815$.  In all three cases the control block has $24$ columns and residualized rank $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.  The parsed UniProtKB reviewed localization records contribute $2{,}821$ records for zebrafish, $16{,}406$ for human, and $4{,}831$ for yeast.  The dominant raw annotation counts are broad and multi-label rather than mechanistic: for human, for example, the largest categories are Membrane $=7{,}119$, Cytoplasm $=6{,}305$, Nucleus $=5{,}837$, Cell membrane $=3{,}570$, and Secreted $=1{,}876$; yeast has Membrane $=1{,}863$, Cytoplasm $=1{,}736$, Nucleus $=1{,}733$, and Mitochondrion $=811$; zebrafish has Cytoplasm $=883$, Nucleus $=845$, Membrane $=708$, and Mitochondrion $=247$.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the $Z$-residualized protein-abundance component explained by the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block, and let $T_{\mathrm{mod}}$ denote the modelled tAI readout used only to subtract the modelled translation component.  The residual interrogated here is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}} &= P_Q - \Pi_{T_{\mathrm{mod}}} P_Q,\\
A_L &= \frac{\|\Pi_{L_Z}P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2}{\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $L_Z$ is the $12$-column localization design after the same $Z$ residualization.  A degree-of-freedom matched Gaussian null with $K=12$ columns and $200$ trials is used because any multi-column readout absorbs some energy mechanically.  The observed absorption values are $A_L=0.009508864544725801$ for zebrafish, $A_L=0.009254913329269913$ for human, and $A_L=0.011225105608516642$ for yeast.  The corresponding null $95$th percentiles are $0.011321328712802819$, $0.0013758521256775384$, and $0.004958116973149795$, with null means $0.0062549946178242525$, $0.0008032852113983743$, and $0.002689902676583632$ respectively.

\paragraph{Readback.}

Zebrafish does not exceed its null boundary, since $0.009508864544725801<0.011321328712802819$.  The complement diagnostic also warns that the zebrafish localization join changes the readout surface substantially: its modelled complement fraction is $0.7608723943210729$ on the localization join versus $0.874628721049266$ on the full abundance-CDS join, an absolute difference of $0.11375632672819314$. Joint TE and mRNA-stability variance partitioning likewise leaves substantial residual signal: the joint mediation fractions are $0.5195182225621592$ in yeast, $0.2404669586626661$ in E. coli, and $0.16890712099923358$ in human, each with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$ under the registered paired permutation. \paragraph{Position and dwell context.}
The yeast $f_3$-stress branch gives an independent finite check on whether the residual has local translation-context structure.  The dwell mediation test joins $1{,}257$ rows, uses five folds and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, and reports a base held-out $R^2$ of $0.1855578757644517$, full held-out $R^2$ of $0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against a null $95$th percentile of $0.0013115362189692937$.  The $f_3$-dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Its positional companion classifies the leading segment as $5'$ rather than $3'$, with $five\_prime\_ramp\_passed=\mathrm{true}$ and $three\_prime\_body\_passed=\mathrm{false}$.  A broader structural-order ramp test marks the module as $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$: the primary $50$-codon ramp has weighted $f_3$ ramp $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$ for the ramp, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip $p=0.05859375$.  Thus the finite evidence supports a local dwell/ramp signal and a localization-associated non-translation residual in human and yeast, but not a folded-structure conclusion.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The localization experiment is a ready finite-row readback for the non-translation residual question.  It says that, after subtracting the modelled tAI component from the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance projection, residualized coarse localization columns absorb more than a matched null in human and yeast, while zebrafish remains below the null and has a materially altered join.  The admissible statement is deliberately narrow: localization annotations carry a descriptive fraction of the remaining abundance residual in two of the three tested organism joins.  No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, causal pathway, organismal phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this table.  Those stronger claims require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled finite-row statistics.
