\subsection{Localization absorption for the non-translation residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-localization-h-candidate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The localization test asks whether a coarse protein-location annotation carries part of the protein-abundance component left after the modeled translation readout has already been removed from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface.  The tested coordinate panel has nine residual coordinates, namely $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The nuisance design contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  In each organism the recorded control-column count is $24$ and the observed control rank is $23$.

The row joins are not inferred from a symbolic genome abstraction.  They are finite matched CDS--protein--localization joins.  The three organism surfaces are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}:&\quad n_{\mathrm{joined}}=1984,\quad n_{\mathrm{localization}}=2821,\quad n_{\mathrm{CDS\ reported}}=14410,\quad K=12,\\
\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}:&\quad n_{\mathrm{joined}}=16008,\quad n_{\mathrm{localization}}=16406,\quad n_{\mathrm{CDS\ reported}}=19053,\quad K=12,\\
\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}:&\quad n_{\mathrm{joined}}=4656,\quad n_{\mathrm{localization}}=4831,\quad n_{\mathrm{CDS\ reported}}=5815,\quad K=12.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $K=12$ is the rank of the residualized multi-label localization design used in the projection test.  The corresponding localization payloads are fixed files with byte sizes $577472$, $4594821$, and $864253$ for zebrafish, human, and yeast respectively, with SHA256 digests $491b4557da573cebd4d962c7efc74cbb76b04e228b2b98d9352d8dec9cd2a3eb$, $4a79e64ed107dac2c89b67e2f6f2a0368a9a3e5850dfcc481f8c5e1f866b44e9$, and $6a61433a589317e3be7cc94241c806c95b5fbbf186e0af8a38ed9c1c2f4d8380$.

\paragraph{Readout construction.}
The target being projected is not raw abundance.  It is the modeled-tAI-complement protein-abundance component, written here as $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$: the protein-abundance part associated with the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates after the modeled translation readout has been conditioned out.  The localization matrix is a multi-label one-hot matrix over reviewed UniProt subcellular-location annotations, residualized against the same covariates.  The reported scalar is the fraction of the modeled complement absorbed by the localization columns.  A degree-of-freedom-matched Gaussian null with the same column count supplies the local comparison.

The absorption results are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}:&\quad A_L=0.009508864544725801,\quad N_{95}=0.011321328712802819,\quad \bar N=0.0062549946178242525,\\
\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}:&\quad A_L=0.009254913329269913,\quad N_{95}=0.0013758521256775384,\quad \bar N=0.0008032852113983743,\\
\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}:&\quad A_L=0.011225105608516642,\quad N_{95}=0.004958116973149795,\quad \bar N=0.002689902676583632.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each null used $200$ trials.  Thus the human and yeast surfaces show localization absorption above the matched null $95\%$ boundary, while zebrafish does not pass that boundary on the available localization join.  The result is deliberately a descriptive projection statement: the localization columns carry detectable information about the leftover protein-abundance component in human and yeast, but this does not make localization a cause of the residual and does not identify a trafficking mechanism.

\paragraph{Compatibility with the translation and protein-abundance row facts.}
The localization readout sits downstream of several measured row surfaces.  Direct protein-abundance survival against PAXdb abundance was positive in the rank-powered protein panel: all nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates survived in each of the fifteen reported organisms.  Representative $R^2_{QP}$ values include $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.10217906758013379$ in \emph{C. elegans}, $0.0646471273126996$ in \emph{P. aeruginosa} PAO1, $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse, $0.04613529807085213$ in zebrafish, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.019256877907572044$ in \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}.  These values are direct protein-omics associations after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls; by themselves they are not translation mechanisms.

Measured ribosome-TE survival was separately computed for four organisms.  The measured $S^{QT}$ row used $\log_{10}(\mathrm{TE})$, with $\mathrm{TE}=\mathrm{footprint}/\mathrm{mRNA}$, and again all nine coordinates survived in each organism.  The observed values were:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}:&\quad R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.04034217614654355,\quad n_{\mathrm{TE}}=13140,\quad \mathrm{hit\ rate}=0.9118667591950035,\\
\mathrm{E.\ coli}:&\quad R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262,\quad n_{\mathrm{TE}}=3415,\quad \mathrm{hit\ rate}=0.9133458143888741,\\
\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}:&\quad R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926,\quad n_{\mathrm{TE}}=11554,\quad \mathrm{hit\ rate}=0.6064136881331024,\\
\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}:&\quad R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436,\quad n_{\mathrm{TE}}=5128,\quad \mathrm{hit\ rate}=0.8818572656921754.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured mediation decomposition then reports mediated fractions $0$ for zebrafish, $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  The corresponding $S^{QP}$ values in that same measured-TE mediation run are $0.04926578454614652$, $0.10941535951828074$, $0.04288464996165745$, and $0.3146818503437042$.  The localization result should be read against this background: yeast has both strong measured TE mediation and above-null localization absorption of the non-translation residual, while human has weak measured TE mediation but above-null localization absorption, suggesting that the remaining protein-abundance component is not exhausted by the measured translation readout.

\paragraph{Four-case and mediator context.}
A separate four-case discriminant over modeled tAI and measured abundance classified yeast as nine case-$D$ coordinates, with no positive-positive coordinate lacking the mediation drop.  \emph{E. coli} had seven case-$D$ coordinates and two positive-positive coordinates without the modeled-readout mediation drop.  In the joint measured-TE plus mRNA-stability partition, the joint mediation fraction was $0.5195182225621592$ for yeast, $0.16890712099923358$ for human, and $0.2404669586626661$ for \emph{E. coli}; the right-tail permutation value was $0.000999000999000999$ in all three organisms.  The single-mediator components were translation-only $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only $0.0626618595344251$ in yeast, translation-only $0.00549490864190343$ and stability-only $0.15314679870183337$ in human, and translation-only $0.01975521340963268$ and stability-only $0.23052056324898387$ in \emph{E. coli}.  This matters because the localization projection is not being asked to replace translation or stability.  It is asked whether the remaining modeled-translation complement has a detectable compartmental component.

The $f3\_stress$ row gives a more local example of residual structure inside the same family.  In yeast, the dwell-abundance test used $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, base $n=1800$, five folds, and $120$ deterministic permutations.  The base $R^2$ was $0.1855578757644517$, the full $R^2$ was $0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment was $0.01856473944057712$, above the null $95\%$ value $0.0013115362189692937$; the $f3$--dwell correlation was $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional companion classified the leading segment as $5'$ and the dominant segment as $5'$: the $5'$ window passed, the $3'$ body did not, and the ramp localization verdict was not promoted to a localized structural claim.  A later structural-order ramp test reported the module classification $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$, with weighted $f3$ ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$, body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, ramp-minus-body $0.001378137149572267$, ramp sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$, and paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip $p=0.05859375$.

\paragraph{Compression and irreducibility boundary.}
The localization result also has to be read with the dictionary-compression and irreducibility certificates.  The partial-mediation certificate in yeast used $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, available join size $4758$, five folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.  Its selected readout family covered $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ of the signal, with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ value $0.00598374993486675$, and selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$.  The irreducibility test used the same $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, available join size $4758$, and five folds.  Its recorded status means that the residual protein-abundance signal is real and at least partly reducible by a listed family, not that a universal primitive mechanism has been certified.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row localization finding is therefore positive but narrow.  Human and yeast show localization absorption of $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ above the matched null boundary; zebrafish does not, and its modeled-complement value also differs from the full abundance-CDS reference by $0.11375632672819314$ because the localization join changes the active row set.  Human and yeast match the full complement diagnostic within $0.02$: the human difference is $0.018824203448813193$ and the yeast difference is $0.011946809265360736$.  The finite judgment is that coarse reviewed localization annotations carry a detectable part of the non-translation residual in those two row sets.

This result does not license a stronger biological conclusion.  It does not show that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ controls translation, folding, physical feasibility, localization trafficking, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or a universal law.  The localization columns are categorical annotations, not perturbations.  The absorption fraction is a projection statistic, not a causal effect.  Direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is not a mechanism unless a same-row measured $q_i\to T_j$ readout survives and conditioning on that readout removes or reduces the $q_i\to P_k$ signal.  Any claim about translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality requires an additional layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
