\subsection{Localization Absorption of the Non-translation Residual}\label{sec:h-candidate-localization-b-star-q6-nontranslation-residual-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite matrix under test.}
The localization test asks whether a residual protein-abundance component left after a modeled translation readout can be partly accounted for by coarse protein-location annotations. The row objects are coding sequences joined to protein identifiers, codon-count derived $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, matched protein-abundance values, and UniProtKB reviewed subcellular-localization labels. The coordinate block has nine columns,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.
The covariate matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. In each organism the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$.

\paragraph{Readout construction.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized nine-column codon-coordinate matrix, let $Z$ denote the control matrix, let $P$ denote $\log_{10}$ measured protein abundance, and let $T_{mod}$ denote the modeled tAI abundance-linked translation readout used only to remove the modeled translation component. The tested target is not $P$ itself but the complement abundance component $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$. Localization is represented by a residualized multi-label one-hot matrix $L$ with $K=12$ location columns. The absorption statistic is the fraction of the modeled-complement residual captured by the column space of $L$ after the same control residualization. The null comparator is a $K$-column Gaussian basis with matched degrees of freedom and $200$ deterministic trials per organism.

$$
\begin{aligned}
Z &= [1,\log(\mathrm{length}),\mathrm{AA}_{20},GC3,M\text{-density}],\\
Q_e &= (I-\Pi_Z)Q,\\
L_e &= (I-\Pi_Z)L,\\
\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}} &= \text{the abundance component assigned to }Q\text{ after subtracting the modeled-translation direction},\\
A_L &= \frac{\|\Pi_{L_e}\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2}{\|\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The statistic is descriptive. It reports whether the location columns carry more of the residual component than a random $12$-column basis with the same dimension. It does not make location causal, and it does not turn a compartment annotation into a translation, folding, physical-admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal-law result.

\paragraph{Joined rows and localization modules.}
The finite-row joins are powered but uneven across organisms. In \textit{Danio rerio}, the source protein-abundance join reported $14410$ CDS rows, while the localization join used $1984$ rows from $2821$ localization records. In \textit{Homo sapiens}, the source abundance join reported $19053$ CDS rows, while the localization join used $16008$ rows from $16406$ localization records. In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the source abundance join reported $5815$ CDS rows, while the localization join used $4656$ rows from $4831$ localization records. The twelve localization modules are Cell membrane, Cytoplasm, Cytoskeleton, Endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi, Membrane, Mitochondrion, Nucleus, Other, Peroxisome, Secreted, and Vacuole/Lysosome.

The observed module counts are organism-specific. For \textit{D. rerio} the largest modules are Cytoplasm $883$, Nucleus $845$, Membrane $708$, Mitochondrion $247$, Cell membrane $230$, and Endoplasmic reticulum $207$, with smaller counts for Cytoskeleton $182$, Golgi $125$, Secreted $79$, Vacuole/Lysosome $45$, Other $23$, and Peroxisome $14$. For human the largest modules are Membrane $7119$, Cytoplasm $6305$, Nucleus $5837$, Cell membrane $3570$, Secreted $1876$, Cytoskeleton $1529$, Endoplasmic reticulum $1449$, Mitochondrion $1370$, Golgi $1096$, Vacuole/Lysosome $482$, Other $161$, and Peroxisome $109$. For yeast the largest modules are Membrane $1863$, Cytoplasm $1736$, Nucleus $1733$, Mitochondrion $811$, Endoplasmic reticulum $426$, Cell membrane $274$, Golgi $224$, Vacuole/Lysosome $217$, Cytoskeleton $165$, Secreted $91$, Peroxisome $77$, and Other $44$.

\paragraph{Absorption values against the matched null.}
The localization absorption is small in absolute scale but differs sharply from the matched null in two organisms. In \textit{D. rerio}, the localization absorption is $0.009508864544725801$, while the degree-of-freedom matched null has mean $0.0062549946178242525$ and $95$th percentile $0.011321328712802819$. This does not exceed the null $95$th percentile. In human, the localization absorption is $0.009254913329269913$, while the null mean is $0.0008032852113983743$ and the null $95$th percentile is $0.0013758521256775384$; this is a clear excess over the null comparator. In yeast, the localization absorption is $0.011225105608516642$, while the null mean is $0.002689902676583632$ and the null $95$th percentile is $0.004958116973149795$; this also exceeds the matched-null boundary.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A_L(\textit{D. rerio}) &= 0.009508864544725801 < 0.011321328712802819,\\
A_L(\textit{H. sapiens}) &= 0.009254913329269913 > 0.0013758521256775384,\\
A_L(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.011225105608516642 > 0.004958116973149795.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus the localization readout is not a uniform explanation of the residual. It is positive relative to the matched-null comparator in human and yeast, but not in the zebrafish localization join. The zebrafish failure is especially important because the localization-limited join differs substantially from the full abundance-CDS join: the modeled-complement diagnostic changes from $0.874628721049266$ on the reference full abundance join to $0.7608723943210729$ on the current localization join, an absolute difference of $0.11375632672819314$. Human and yeast remain closer to their reference complements, with differences $0.018824203448813193$ and $0.011946809265360736$, respectively.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and abundance rows.}
The localization calculation is downstream of already-computed abundance and translation-readout surfaces, but it does not replace them. Direct protein-abundance survival for the same nine-coordinate basis is positive across the broader protein-abundance panel; for the three organisms used here, $R^2_{QP}$ is $0.04613529807085213$ in \textit{D. rerio}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, with all nine coordinates surviving in each direct $Q\to P$ fit. Measured ribosome-TE survival is also positive on matched TE panels: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$ in \textit{D. rerio}, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, again with all nine coordinates surviving. However, measured mediation fractions are heterogeneous: the measured $Q\to T\to P$ fraction is clipped to $0.0$ in \textit{D. rerio} from a raw value $-0.04801486048721136$, is $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and is $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast. In the joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition, the right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$ for \textit{E. coli}, human, and yeast, with joint mediation fractions $0.2404669586626661$, $0.16890712099923358$, and $0.5195182225621592$, respectively. These numbers say that the abundance residual has real measured-readout structure, but they do not by themselves identify localization as a mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The localization H-candidate is supported only as a finite descriptive projection for human and yeast. It is not supported for the zebrafish localization join under the same null criterion. The result is therefore a qualified non-translation residual localization signal: localization annotations carry excess residual information in two of the three tested localization joins, while one join remains below the matched-null boundary and also changes the modeled-complement diagnostic relative to the full abundance-CDS join. The correct readback is a four-part finite statement: the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are present; the controls have rank $23$ after the declared $24$-column construction; the localization matrix has $12$ columns; and only human and yeast exceed the degree-of-freedom matched null. No translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, compartment-trafficking mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this calculation unless a separate layer-specific measurement supplies that claim and is evaluated under its own matched-row statistic.
