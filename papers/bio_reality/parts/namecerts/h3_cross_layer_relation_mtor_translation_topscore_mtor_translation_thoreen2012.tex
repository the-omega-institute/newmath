\paragraph{Observable panel.}
The validated payload for this claim is a finite translation-readout panel with $n=6494$ rows, drawn from the raw archive at \texttt{https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE36nnn/GSE36892/suppl/GSE36892\_RAW.tar}. The recorded execution identifier is \texttt{0c2bbfd3e2d9}, and the verification run completed in $9.469$ seconds. This is a concrete external data surface: a bounded table of same-row translation measurements that can be ranked, residualized, or compared against matched transcript-abundance controls, but only within the limits of what the source itself observes.

\paragraph{What is actually supported.}
On this record, the mTOR-linked label should be read as a statement about translation-layer readout, not as a claim that the coordinate system, the score ordering, or the residual geometry itself explains protein output. The panel can support a finite association between the observed readout and the chosen score label across $6494$ rows, and that is the full extent of the certified claim here. The source gives an empirical translation surface; it does not by itself certify folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal rule, and it does not convert the internal indexing scheme into external mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
This chapter therefore keeps a strict separation between the observable data and any higher-layer reading. A translation association may be discussed only as a bounded readout statement, and only while the matched transcript-abundance control remains in place. No inference to protein structure, mechanistic causation, pathway necessity, or general biological law is licensed unless a separate layer-specific contact supplies direct evidence at that layer. In particular, a finite row count and a rankable translation score do not, on their own, establish occupancy, loss-and-rescue behavior, or any downstream state of the encoded protein.

\paragraph{Record of scale.}
The finite size matters because it fixes the scope of the witness: $6494$ rows are enough to anchor a reproducible readout comparison, but they remain a panel-level fact rather than a global biological theorem. The runtime of $9.469$ seconds is likewise only an implementation note about the verified extraction path, not a biological parameter. Taken together, these numbers make the chapter a compact, reality-grounded record of the Thoreen 2012 translation readout, suitable for finite-panel comparison and nothing stronger.
