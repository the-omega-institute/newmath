\subsection{The $f_3$-stress axis as a bounded translation-readout target}\label{sec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record considered here is the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the third-position $f_3$ stress direction and to rows for which a translation-layer readout is explicitly available or explicitly absent.  The finite saturation row gives the ambient $M$-surface as the twenty codons $\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$.  Under $f_3$ saturation the residual list has eighteen codons, namely $\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$; the omitted pair is $\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}$, and the recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the paper treats the $f_3$ object as a finite residual coordinate, not as a direct biological observation.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row identity.}
The powered third-axis assay reports status passed for $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its finite conclusion identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with the candidate axis ``usage frequency within family'': the mean cosine is $0.128$, with one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$.  In the same finite packet, the two-axis closure assay reports that a third axis is present, because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The residual-optimality assay names the dominant non-tRNA residual direction as $f_3$ translation selection, again over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the third coordinate.}
These rows support a narrow mathematical reading: after quotienting by amino acid, the residual third coordinate is not exhausted by the tRNA axis or by a two-axis optimality span.  The finite data point is the existence of a same-signed $d_{\perp}$ direction whose best current label is usage frequency within synonymous family.  The corresponding mechanism-oriented assay reports ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external'', with the reason that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  Therefore the chapter records a third-axis identity candidate and a GC-refusal boundary at once: GC3 is not allowed to absorb the signal, but the exact positional or codon-stability explanation is still not licensed by these rows.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For this chapter the only admissible readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3,T_j}$ for a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The row is positive only if it survives amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  In symbolic form the local test is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3,T_j} &> 0 \\
&\text{after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler baselines.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite identity row does not itself provide that measured same-row translation statistic.  It gives a powered coordinate identity and a candidate direction; it does not by itself give a ribosome-footprint, translation-efficiency, or protein-output row.

\paragraph{Context break condition.}
The conjectural biological test is asymmetric in context.  If a codon context has equal third-position wobble or tRNA weight, $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$, then the $f_3$ pair is not expected to become a distinct dynamical translation row merely from topology.  If the context has $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$, then the finite coordinate may be tested against $T_j$ by the controlled survival statistic above.  The rank-powered evidence does not authorize a universal direction: it only says that the current finite organism panel has enough structure to make the $d_{\perp}$ identity visible.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is treated as a rank-degenerate boundary, not as survival evidence.

\paragraph{Related finite constraints.}
The axis-strength spectrum assay computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ and found that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; every computed organism in that assay has dominant count assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  Its largest GC3 rank effect is only reported as $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$, and the domain means are explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced counts $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty assay computed $40$ organisms out of $46$, skipped $6$, and used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$; it reports that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  These rows are relevant because they separate tRNA-supply pressure, residual axis strength, and the $f_3$ third-axis identity rather than merging them into one biological claim.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
Nothing in this chapter proves translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, a biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological law.  A separate measured or explicitly bounded contact must supply each such layer.  The current chapter may say that a finite $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate has a powered third-axis identity candidate, that the finite identity aligns with usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and $p=0.000488$, and that the next admissible biological test is the controlled entry $S_{f3,T_j}$.  It may not promote codon topology, modeled tAI or stAI weights, identifier routing, or review readiness into measured survival.  The conjecture is falsified at this layer if every controlled $S_{f3,T_j}$ is non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered organism panel.
