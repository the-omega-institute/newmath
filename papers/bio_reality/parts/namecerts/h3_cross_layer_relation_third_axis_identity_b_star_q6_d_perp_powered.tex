\paragraph{Finite coordinate under amino-acid quotienting.}
The record isolates a finite synonymous residual coordinate surface for coding sequences after amino-acid projection.  The coordinate of interest is the $B^*_{Q6}$ third residual direction, denoted here by $d_\perp$, with the proposed $f_3$ stress readout restricted to codon families where third-position interchange can matter after the amino-acid label has already been quotiented away.  The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support rather than leaving the coordinate as an informal codon-usage slogan.  The recorded set $M=\mathrm{Med}_R\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ is

\begin{aligned}

$$
\begin{aligned}

It is a finite residual coordinate whose possible biological contact must pass through organism-level synonymous imbalance, tRNA or wobble asymmetry, and a matched translation readout.

\paragraph{Powered identity row.}
The rank-powered third-axis identity experiment was run with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested.  Its status is recorded as passed.  The finite conclusion identifies $d_\perp$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family: the mean cosine is $0.128$, with one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  In symbols, the recorded identity is the bounded alignment statement
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_\perp &\leadsto \text{usage-frequency-within-family},\\
 \overline{\cos}(d_\perp,\text{candidate})&=0.128,\\
 p_{\mathrm{sign}}&=0.000488.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a third-axis identity claim for the residual coordinate surface, not a direct observation of ribosome motion.  It says that the held-out residual direction behaves like a within-family usage-frequency axis in the powered panel.  It does not by itself say that the axis changes translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or any universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite rows.}
Several neighboring rows constrain the interpretation.  The two-axis closure test, with the same $5$ folds, $500$-protein floor, $41$ synonymous columns, and $11/16$ organism coverage, records that a third axis is present: $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The residual-optimality row records the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection under the same powered dimensions.  The mechanism row reports that the third-axis signal remains after GC orthogonalization and is not classified as wobble-clustered; its finite conclusion is selection-not-GC with an external need for a CSC or positional identity.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum again uses $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum, $41$ design columns, and $11/16$ organism coverage; it records $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant per-organism axis in all $11$ computed organisms, gives the descriptive domain imbalance $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$, and reports the largest GC3 rank effect as $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  These rows make the third-axis identity non-vacuous inside the finite residual basis, while also showing that the readout is entangled with rank, organism coverage, and control design.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
For the $f_3$ stress conjecture, the biological contact is stricter than the powered identity row.  The admissible readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A modeled tAI or stAI surface may serve as a bounded codon-adaptation contact, but it is not the same thing as measured ribosome occupancy.  A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contact becomes evidential only after same-organism and same-CDS codon rows, wobble or tRNA weights, measured translation rows, and matched mRNA controls are joined.  The declared controls are amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA where applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablation.  The support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for at least one nondegenerate translation row after those controls.  If every controlled entry is non-positive, or if the surface is rank-degenerate, then the $f_3$ stress coordinate remains an internal residual distinction rather than a translation-readout survival result.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The finite data support a powered coordinate identity: $d_\perp$ aligns with usage frequency within synonymous family at mean cosine $0.128$ and sign-test $p=0.000488$ in the computed panel.  They also support the existence of a third residual axis relative to the two-axis tRNA and $f_3$ span, and they place the signal away from a simple GC explanation.  They do not support a claim that the $f_3$ stress coordinate has already been observed as translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Those claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  The correct local reading is therefore: the $B^*_{Q6}$ third residual axis is a finite, powered, usage-frequency-like coordinate, and its proposed $f_3$ translation interpretation remains conditional on a nondegenerate controlled $S_{f3\text{-stress},T_j}$ row.
