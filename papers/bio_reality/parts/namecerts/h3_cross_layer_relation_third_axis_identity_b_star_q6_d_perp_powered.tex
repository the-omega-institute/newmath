\subsection{The $f_3$-stress third axis in the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinate that remains after amino-acid projection and asks whether the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance can be read at the translation layer. The finite codon packet has a saturated third-position boundary in which
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $ACA$ and $ACG$ sit in $M$ but outside the displayed $f_3$ saturation, while the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. This is not interpreted as a biological law. It is a finite coordinate condition that makes the downstream question sharply testable: after amino-acid composition has been quotiented out, any claimed translation distinction must enter through an organism- or context-specific asymmetry in tRNA, wobble, or measured translation readout.

\paragraph{Third-axis identity.}
The powered third-axis run identifies the residual direction $d_\perp$ with the candidate axis \emph{usage frequency within synonymous family}. The computation used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns. Its finite summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{mean\ cosine}(d_\perp,\mathrm{usage\ frequency\ within\ family}) &= 0.128,\\
p_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 0.000488.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positive sign-test result supports a local identity claim for this finite coordinate panel: the third residual axis is aligned with within-family codon-usage frequency rather than being an uninterpreted leftover coordinate. A companion mechanism run, on the same $11$-of-$16$ organism scale with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, classified the axis as selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as requiring a separate measurement source. This matters for the present chapter because a third-position coordinate can become a translation-layer variable only where the readout row breaks the $f_3$ symmetry; topology alone is not enough.

\paragraph{Relation to the powered $B^\ast_{Q6}$ panel.}
The residual-axis runs place the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside a broader powered panel rather than a three-organism saturated fit. The universal residual-axis run reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection, again with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The two-axis closure run on the same finite scale reports that a third axis is present, with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed $d_\perp$ directions across organisms. The axis-strength spectrum run computed the same $11$ organisms from $16$ requested organisms and found that the captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. These facts make the third-axis record a powered residual-coordinate claim, not merely a codon-list observation.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The biological test is deliberately narrower than the coordinate identity. Let $Q_{f_3}$ denote the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and let $T_j$ range over bounded translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The admissible survival entry has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} &= \Delta\operatorname{MDL}\bigl(T_j \mid Z\bigr)
  \quad \text{or an agreed equivalent controlled partial-}R^2,\\
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism},\ GC3,\ \text{CDS length},\ M\text{-density},\ \text{mRNA control}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive row requires $S_{f_3,T_j}>0$ after these controls and after simpler codon-baseline ablation. The boundary-set tRNA-poverty run supplies one finite mechanism-side pressure: across $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, using $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, $R$ sense codons were called systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply route for testing $R$ avoidance, but it still does not by itself create a measured ribosome-occupancy row or a translation-efficiency survival entry.

\paragraph{Null and refusal boundary.}
The record is positive for a powered third-axis identity and open for the stricter translation-readout survival question. It is falsified at this layer if every controlled $S_{f_3,T_j}$ remains non-positive or if the readout matrix is rank-degenerate, including the special case in which a small all-$1.0$ surface only records saturation of the fitted rank. Conversely, a positive modeled tAI or stAI association is only a bounded translation-weight readout unless a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency contact is joined to the same organism or CDS rows. No conclusion about mature protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism in the strong causal sense, or universality follows from this chapter. Each of those assertions would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic after the mandatory translation row has first survived the stated controls.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite evidence therefore supports the following local statement. In the powered $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual panel, $d_\perp$ is provisionally identified with within-family codon-usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$ over the stated $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column design. The $f_3$-stress translation conjecture remains a controlled readout target: it becomes biologically active only in rows where tRNA or wobble asymmetry breaks the $f_3$ pairing and the matched translation statistic survives the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.
