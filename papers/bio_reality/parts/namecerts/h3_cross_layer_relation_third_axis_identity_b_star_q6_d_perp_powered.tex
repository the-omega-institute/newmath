\subsection{The third-axis identity as a bounded translation-row conjecture}
\label{sec:bior:third-axis-identity-b-star-q6-d-perp-powered}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the finite residual coordinate surface obtained from coding sequences after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress direction.  The relevant coordinate is not a free biological cause.  It is a finite synonymous residual row whose possible empirical interpretation is limited to matched translation readouts such as organism-scoped tAI, stAI, translation efficiency, or ribosome-occupancy summaries.  The present powered computation identifies the residual direction $d_{\perp}$ with the candidate axis `usage\_frequency\_within\_family' on the tested panel: the mean cosine is $0.128$, with a one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$.  The computation used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.

\paragraph{The closed $f_3$ codon row.}
The finite $f_3$ saturation row fixes the exact codon support on which the stress coordinate is allowed to speak.  The recorded mediated set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ has $20$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The saturated $f_3$ image of $R$ removes exactly the two ACA/ACG entries and leaves $18$ codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the coordinate is not being introduced by an unclosed codon-boundary remainder; it is a residual distinction inside a finite saturated codon row after amino-acid quotienting.

\paragraph{Identity call.}
The powered identity assay gives a positive but modest alignment: $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally identified with within-family usage frequency rather than with an unconstrained direction.  In finite-row form, the call is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{axis}(d_{\perp}) &= \text{usage\_frequency\_within\_family},\\
\overline{\cos}(d_{\perp},\text{axis}) &= 0.128,\\
p_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 0.000488.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a rank-powered identity statement over the available organism panel, not a universal law.  A related mechanism assay reports that the $d_{\perp}$ direction remains significant after GC orthogonalization and describes the result as selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still needing an additional empirical row.  Another powered spectrum assay reports that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ dominant in all $11$ computed organisms, while the largest GC3 rank effect is only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  These companion rows make the third-axis identity sharper: $d_{\perp}$ is a finite residual usage-frequency direction, but it is not, by these rows alone, a complete account of translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Translation-row condition.}
The biologically meaningful test is whether the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance survives in a translation readout after the declared controls.  Let $q_{f_3}$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate and let $T_j$ range over bounded translation readouts, including tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency when organism- and CDS-matched rows exist.  The local survival score is interpreted only after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance where applicable, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline controls are applied:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3,T_j} > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
means that $q_{f_3}$ improves the controlled translation-row description for $T_j$.  The conjecture is broken if every controlled entry is non-positive, or if the apparent surface is rank-degenerate, such as an all-$1.0$ matrix on a three-organism panel.  A modeled tAI or stAI surface can bound a readout, but it does not itself become observed ribosome occupancy.  A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row becomes admissible only when the same organism and same CDS rows are joined to codon counts and matched mRNA controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, a biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological rule from the $d_{\perp}$ identity call.  Even translation itself is not promoted from coordinate geometry alone.  A positive translation-layer statement requires a separate matched contact and a computed controlled survival entry $S_{f_3,T_j}>0$.  Any later claim above translation must add its own layer-specific contact and statistic after the translation row has survived.  Therefore the current content is a finite residual-axis identity plus a precise empirical break condition: the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress coordinate may be read against translation only where tRNA or wobble weights break third-position symmetry, and it is null for this purpose wherever controlled translation survival is absent or rank-saturated.
