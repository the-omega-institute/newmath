\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout target}\label{sec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.} The object considered here is the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate that records third-position $f_3$ stress and paired only with translation-layer rows.  The finite seed on the codon side is not an unrestricted codon alphabet.  The recorded $f_3$ saturation packet gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M&=\{AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)&=\{AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&\qquad UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R&=\varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the carrier has an explicit finite stress boundary: the saturation removes $ACA$ and $ACG$ from the twenty-element mediated list, and the recorded boundary set is empty.  The BioReality question is therefore not whether the codon geometry exists, but whether this finite residual coordinate survives after the amino-acid quotient as a controlled translation-readout entry.

\paragraph{Observed finite evidence.} The powered third-axis identity run completed with status passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  In that run, $d_{\perp}$ was provisionally identified with usage frequency within synonymous family: the mean cosine was $0.128$ and the one-sided sign-test value was $p=0.000488$.  The companion mechanism run, also on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms with the same fold count, minimum-protein threshold, and $41$ synonymous design columns, recorded the finite classification ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external'', with the stated reason that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ kept significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  These rows locate the third axis as a real residual direction in the finite design, but they do not by themselves turn it into an observed ribosome or translation-efficiency measurement.

\paragraph{Relation to the ranked coordinate panel.} The neighboring powered rows constrain the interpretation.  The residual optimality run reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality axis aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, a $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The two-axis closure run reports a third axis present, with $d_{\perp}$ showing systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The axis-strength spectrum row reports $11$ computed organisms, $16$ requested organisms, and dominant per-organism counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$; it also records the largest GC3 rank effect as $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  The optimality-axis sufficiency row concludes that a fourth mechanism is present under the same $11/16$ organism count, $5$-fold design, $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous columns.  Taken together, these finite rows say that $f_3$-sensitive residual structure is visible inside the ranked synonymous-coordinate design, while also warning that a three-axis description is not sufficient for all residual behavior.

\paragraph{Translation-readout test.} The bounded readback for this chapter is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive BioReality readout requires a joined finite panel in which coding-sequence rows carry the $f_3$-stress coordinate, organism identity, codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, translation readout, and controls for amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA abundance where translation efficiency is computed.  The intended statistic is local:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
only when the $f_3$-stress coordinate improves the controlled description of $T_j$ beyond the baseline and simpler codon controls.  If the measured or explicitly bounded translation rows remain non-positive, rank-degenerate, or unavailable, the chapter records a null boundary rather than a biological promotion.

\paragraph{Symmetry-breaking condition.} The coordinate becomes biologically testable only where third-position symmetry is broken by context tRNA or wobble weights.  In notation, the required local asymmetry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}} .
\end{aligned}
$$
Without such asymmetry, the finite codon topology may still be present, but it is not a dynamical translation-readout claim.  The tRNA-poverty row supports this cautionary reading: in a separate powered permutation analysis, $R$ sense codons were called systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights, with $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, $6$ skipped organisms, permutation count $1000$, and $\alpha=0.05$.  That row supplies a finite mechanism-facing pressure for $R$ avoidance, not a universal law and not a direct survival row for $S_{f3,T_j}$.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.} The present finite rows justify a narrow statement: within the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-coordinate panel, the third residual direction $d_{\perp}$ is aligned with within-family usage frequency, is not explained away as a GC-only axis in the recorded mechanism row, and is compatible with an $f_3$-translation-selection reading that must be checked against translation-layer rows.  They do not establish protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, causation, a translation mechanism beyond the bounded readout, or universality.  Any such higher-layer statement requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ or rank-saturated small-organism surface is a null boundary, not survival evidence; modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded adaptation readbacks, not observed ribosome occupancy; and a codon-coordinate identity cannot be promoted to downstream biology without a positive, non-vacuous $S_{f3,T_j}$ entry under the declared controls.
