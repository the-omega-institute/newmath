\subsection{Third-position residual identity in the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-third-axis-identity}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row under review.}
This record concerns the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection, with attention restricted to the third residual direction $d_{\perp}$ and to the possible $f_3$-stress readback at the translation layer.  The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon boundary before any biological interpretation is attempted.  The row reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite $f_3$ boundary is empty in this row: the coordinate is not being promoted by an unresolved codon-boundary exception.  The two codons present in $M$ but absent from the displayed $f_3$ saturation set are $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, so the row is specifically a controlled residual-surface statement rather than a raw codon-table assertion.

\paragraph{Observed powered identity.}
The powered third-axis identity experiment passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  In that row, $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally identified with within-family usage frequency: the reported mean cosine is $0.128$, and the one-sided sign-test value is $p=0.000488$.  Written as the finite decision recorded by the assay,
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\perp} &\leadsto \text{usage frequency within synonymous family},\\
 \overline{\cos}(d_{\perp},u_{\mathrm{family}}) &= 0.128,\\
 p_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 0.000488 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite, panel-scoped identification of a residual axis.  It is not a claim that the same coordinate has already survived as a measured ribosome-occupancy, translation-efficiency, protein-abundance, localization, stability, functional, or phenotypic readout.

\paragraph{Relation to the stronger translation-row question.}
The same finite packet asks whether an $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance remains visible after amino-acid quotienting when context tRNA or wobble weights break third-position symmetry.  The admissible readback is narrow: a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A positive translation-row statement would require same-organism or same-CDS joining of codon counts, a wobble-breaking weight surface, a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, and controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, rank, and simpler codon baselines.  In the present finite memory, that joined survival row is not present.  The existing third-axis result therefore supports the identity of $d_{\perp}$ inside the residual codon-usage geometry, while the translation-readout survival question remains a separate empirical boundary.

\paragraph{Supporting finite context.}
Several adjacent powered rows constrain the interpretation.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum was computed for $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; in that spectrum, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$ and a largest GC3 rank effect of $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The two-axis closure row found that a third axis is present, again on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, because $d_{\perp}$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The residual optimality row identified the dominant non-tRNA residual direction as $f_3$ translation selection on the same $11$-of-$16$, $5$-fold, $41$-column design.  The sufficiency row concluded that a fourth direction remains present after three axes, so the present third-axis identity should not be read as an exhaustive decomposition of all residual biological variation.

\paragraph{Negative boundaries supplied by neighboring assays.}
The neighboring fourth-direction assays help prevent over-reading.  The mRNA-stability row concluded that the fourth axis is not mRNA stability in its assay, because the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ direction had low alignment with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  The codon-pair row concluded \emph{not codon pair}; the translation-accuracy row concluded \emph{not accuracy}; and the elongation-dwell row was only partial in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These facts do not weaken the third-axis usage-frequency identification, but they do show why the chapter should remain local: the finite evidence separates a residual coordinate identity from downstream layer claims.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This row licenses the following statement only: in the powered finite panel, the third residual direction $d_{\perp}$ aligns with within-family usage frequency with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test value $0.000488$, under the recorded $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column design.  It does not by itself establish translation realization.  It also does not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Any such statement would need its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may bound a translation-layer readback, but they are not the same as observed ribosome occupancy; an all-$1.0$ rank-degenerate surface is a null boundary rather than survival; and a codon-topology or residual-axis result cannot be promoted to a downstream biological conclusion without the required controlled translation row first becoming non-vacuous and positive.
