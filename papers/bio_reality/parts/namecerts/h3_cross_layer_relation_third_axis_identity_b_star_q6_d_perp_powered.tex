\subsection{The third residual axis as a controlled translation-readout boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-third-axis-identity}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The claim concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection and restriction to the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ stress coordinate attached to the third-position involution $f_3$.  The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support before any biological readout is considered.  The recorded $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup \{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated row removes the two boundary codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the row is not a claim about an unconstrained codon universe: it is a finite residual coordinate after an explicit saturation computation, and the biological question is whether this residual coordinate retains a translation-layer distinction after the usual coding-sequence controls are applied.

\paragraph{Identity row.}
The powered identity run for this claim used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  The run passed and identified $d_{\perp}$ with the candidate axis ``usage frequency within family'' in the bounded sense recorded by the statistic
$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline{\cos}(d_{\perp},d_{\mathrm{usage\ within\ family}})&=0.128,\\
p_{\mathrm{one\ sided\ sign}}&=0.000488.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a small but sign-stable alignment, not a universal law.  Its proper interpretation is that the residual direction left after the core tRNA-adaptation span has a reproducible codon-usage-frequency identity within synonymous families under the stated finite design.  It does not by itself assert that translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or evolutionary optimality has been observed.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding powered rows.}
The two-axis closure run reports a third axis as present: $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across the computed organisms.  The mutation-versus-selection row names the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  The mechanism row then narrows the interpretation: after GC orthogonalization, $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, so the recorded conclusion is selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, while the CSC or positional identity still needs an external row.  The axis-strength spectrum gives a complementary scale check: in the $11$ computed organisms, the per-organism dominant count is $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, with the largest GC3 rank effect reported for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ at $\rho=0.136$.  These rows support a finite residual-axis identity, but they do not replace the mandatory translation-readout survival calculation.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The admissible readback for this claim is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The intended support condition is local and statistical:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where relevant, and simpler codon-baseline ablations are included.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface over three organisms is not such a survival row; it is a null boundary caused by insufficient rank.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights can provide a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but they are not direct ribosome-occupancy observations.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The conjecture fails at this layer if every controlled entry remains non-positive or rank-degenerate across a powered panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall T_j\quad S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}\leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the stated controls.  Such a failure would not make the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate mathematically irrelevant; it would only say that this finite residual coordinate has not crossed the translation-readout interface in the tested panel.  Conversely, one positive entry would establish only a bounded translation-row survival claim for that readout and panel.  It would not establish protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter therefore records a finite residual identity and its required biological gate.  The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes dynamically interpretable only where context tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry and where the translation row survives the declared controls.  No downstream biological layer is inferred from codon topology, tAI coupling, residual geometry, contact naming, or workflow readiness alone.  Translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality each require their own layer-matched contact and their own recorded statistic.
