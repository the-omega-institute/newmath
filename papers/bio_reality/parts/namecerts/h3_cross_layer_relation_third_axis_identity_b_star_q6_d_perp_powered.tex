\subsection{The f\_3-Stress Coordinate as a Translation-Readout Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-translation-readout-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record isolates the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate after amino-acid projection.  The relevant finite carrier is the organism-indexed coding-sequence residual surface restricted to the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance and then paired with translation-layer rows such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  In the saturation row, the mediated set $M = Med(R \cup ACA)$ contains exactly twenty codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,\\
& CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$ saturation of $R$ removes $ACA$ and $ACG$, leaving eighteen codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Sat_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
& UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with empty $f_3$ boundary in the finite row.  Thus the topological stress is not an unbounded codon-space assertion: it is a closed finite residue after amino-acid quotienting, and its biological question is whether any matched translation row detects the residue once ordinary composition and organism-level covariates have been removed.

\paragraph{Powered third-axis identification.}
The powered third-axis identity experiment reports status passed for $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its stated conclusion identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$.  The local numerical statement is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{mean\ cosine}(d_{\perp},\mathrm{usage\ frequency\ within\ family}) &= 0.128,\\
p_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 0.000488,\\
N_{\mathrm{computed}} &= 11,\quad N_{\mathrm{requested}}=16.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is evidence for a finite residual axis in the codon-usage geometry, not yet evidence for translation output.  The companion mechanism row classifies the same third axis as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity requiring an additional contact; it again used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The residual-axis row also reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection, while the two-axis closure row reports third-axis-present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  These finite facts justify treating the $f_3$ coordinate as a live candidate translation-interface coordinate, but they do not by themselves measure ribosome motion or protein output.

\paragraph{Relation, spectrum, and trigger.}
The chapter-specific relation is the conditional relation between an $f_3$-paired synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$.  The spectrum is the organism-by-coordinate strength profile.  The trigger is the symmetry break $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$ in context tRNA or wobble weights.  Without this break, the $f_3$-paired codons remain geometrically distinct but dynamically uncommitted at the translation layer.  The axis-strength spectrum row is consistent with this caution: the captured spectra are led on average by $d_{resid4}$ and then by $d_{tRNA}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{resid4}:11\}$, while the largest GC3 rank effect is $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  Domain means in that row are descriptive only because the domain counts are imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms among the computed rows.

\paragraph{Translation-readout statistic.}
The bounded survival entry for this packet is a controlled score $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency row.  The intended test is not whether the codon geometry is nonzero, but whether the matched translation row improves prediction after the declared controls.  In local notation the admissible support condition has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0\\
\text{after controls }Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{organism},GC3,\text{CDS length},M\text{-density},\text{mRNA control}\}
\end{aligned}
$$
with simpler-codon-baseline ablation and rank checks.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ fitted surface is not counted as survival; it is a rank-degenerate boundary.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded codon-adaptation readbacks, not observations of ribosome occupancy unless a measured ribosome or TE row is actually joined to the same organism or CDS rows.

\paragraph{Finite mechanism context.}
The tRNA-poverty boundary row gives one finite mechanism cue without closing the readout question: in $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $6$ skipped organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, the $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation row passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The optimality sufficiency row, with the same $11/16$ organism count, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous columns, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  Together these rows say that the $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry has structured tRNA and residual components, while the exact $f_3$-stress translation-readout entry still requires a same-row translation statistic.

\paragraph{Local refusal of overpromotion.}
The local claim is deliberately narrow.  A positive codon-space axis, a tRNA-poverty pattern, or a modeled wobble-weight asymmetry does not imply protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism in the strong experimental sense, or a universal biological law.  The only biological readback allowed here is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a matched translation row.  If every such entry remains non-positive after controls, or if the fitted readout matrix is rank-degenerate, then the translation-readout conjecture fails at this boundary even though the finite codon coordinate and the powered third-axis identity may remain valid as internal coordinate facts.  Any downstream statement about protein abundance, structure, physical feasibility, biological function, system phenotype, or universality must be supplied by a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.
