\subsection{Rank-powered identity of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third residual axis}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-third-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite row under audit.}
This record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate family and then tested against rank-powered organism panels.  The named row is not a general codon-usage law.  It is the narrower claim that the residual direction denoted $d_{\perp}$ is identified, on the computed panel, with a usage-frequency-within-family axis after the stronger tRNA and $f_{3}$ directions have been accounted for.  The powered third-axis run used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, used $5$ folds, and worked over $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its recorded conclusion was that $d_{\perp}$ aligns to usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$.

\paragraph{Codon support surface.}
The supporting $f_{3}$ saturation row fixes the finite codon surface on which the stress coordinate is interpreted.  The reported merged set is
$$\begin{aligned}

&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.

After $f_{3}$ saturation of the $R$ surface, the finite row removes only $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this displayed set:
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R)={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.

The recorded $f_{3}$ boundary is empty.  Thus the row is not using a hidden third-position remainder to create the third axis; it asks whether a residual direction, already present after the quotient and saturation bookkeeping, has a stable finite identity in the organism-level design.

\paragraph{Third-axis readout.}
The principal finite statistic is the powered third-axis identity run with experiment id $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_identity\_powered}$.  Its seed is recorded as $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_identity\_powered:deterministic}$.  The result is a positive but modest alignment: mean cosine $0.128$, one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, over $11$ organisms and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The word ``identity'' is therefore used in a finite operational sense: among the candidate axes scored in this assay, the residual direction is provisionally named by the usage-frequency-within-family candidate.  It is not a claim that codon topology alone determines translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Relation to the two-axis baseline.}
The companion two-axis closure row reports $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $5$ folds, the same $500$-protein organism threshold, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It concluded that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and its directions are mutually same-signed across organisms.  The residual optimality row, computed with the same organism count, fold count, protein threshold, and design-column count, named $f_{3}\_\mathrm{translation}\_\mathrm{selection}$ as the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis.  Together these rows give the local interpretation of the present claim: a tRNA-plus-$f_{3}$ span is not sufficient for the powered panel, and the leftover direction is not arbitrary noise in the recorded assay.

\paragraph{Mechanism pressure and negative exclusions.}
The third-axis mechanism row used $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It concluded ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external'', with the reason that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^{2}$.  This prevents the present chapter from collapsing the axis into a GC-composition artifact, but it also prevents a stronger mechanism claim.  The finite row can say that the residual axis has a usage-frequency-within-family identity in the powered assay; it cannot by itself say that the biological cause is a codon-stability code, positional ribosome occupancy, or a specific wobble mechanism.

\paragraph{Panel shape and fourth-axis context.}
The axis-strength spectrum row reports $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its descriptive summary says that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, then $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$ and a largest GC3 rank effect of $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The three-axis sufficiency row, on the same panel size and design shape, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  These companion rows make the present third-axis statement more precise: $d_{\perp}$ is a named third residual axis, not the whole residual structure of the powered organism panel.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The conjectural biological readback for the $f_{3}$-stress row is bounded by a mandatory translation row.  A positive interpretation requires an organism- or CDS-matched statistic $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_{j}}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  In symbols, the admissible promotion test has the form
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_{j}}>0
\quad\text{only after controls }Z={}&\{\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},\\
&\mathrm{M\text{-}density},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{rank},\mathrm{simpler\ baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
No such same-row measured translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy statistic is present in this finite row.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve as bounded codon-adaptation readouts when explicitly declared, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy.  The three-organism all-$1.0$ matrix mentioned in the packet is a rank-degenerate null boundary, not survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter therefore records a finite, rank-powered axis identity and a constrained route for future biological testing.  It does not claim that $f_{3}$ stress or $d_{\perp}$ produces protein abundance, protein structure, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  None of those conclusions follows from codon counts, residual geometry, modeled adaptation weights, workflow eligibility, or the sign-test statistic alone.  Each higher layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic, and any downstream interpretation remains blocked unless the translation-readout entry is first non-vacuous, positive, and non-rank-degenerate under the stated controls.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite row is ready as a BioReality chapter at the level of a powered residual-axis identity: $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally identified with usage frequency within synonymous families on an $11$-organism, $5$-fold, $41$-column design, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The same row remains deliberately silent about translation realization and all higher biological layers until a same-organism or same-CDS translation-readout matrix supplies the missing controlled survival statistic.
