\subsection{The $f_3$-stress third-axis row for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bior-q6-f3-stress-third-axis}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.} This BioReality record concerns a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection. The subject coordinate is the $f_3$-paired synonymous imbalance inside the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual design, restricted to rows where codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, and a translation-layer readout can in principle be matched. The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support before any biological interpretation is attempted:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AAG},\texttt{ACA},\texttt{ACG},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{AUG},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\qquad \texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UCG},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UGG},\texttt{UUA},\texttt{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AAG},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{AUG},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\qquad \texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UCG},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UGG},\texttt{UUA},\texttt{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3} &= \varnothing.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not a loose codon-usage story. It is a finite residual-coordinate claim on a specified codon panel, with $\texttt{ACA}$ and $\texttt{ACG}$ present in $M$ but absent from the reported $f_3$ saturation of $R$, and with zero reported $f_3$ boundary remainder.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row result.} The powered identity experiment for the third axis computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. On that surface, the third residual direction $d_{\perp}$ was provisionally identified with the candidate axis ``usage frequency within family'': the recorded mean cosine was $0.128$, with a one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$. In formula form, the row supports only the finite alignment statement
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{mean\_cos}(d_{\perp},\operatorname{usage\_within\_family}) &= 0.128,\\
p_{\operatorname{sign}} &= 0.000488,\\
n_{\operatorname{computed}} &= 11,\qquad n_{\operatorname{requested}}=16.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite memory places this row inside a broader residual-axis pattern: a two-axis tRNA-plus-$f_3$ span was not sufficient because a third axis was still present; the non-tRNA residual optimality row selected $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant residual axis; and the GC-orthogonalized mechanism row reported that $d_{\perp}$ kept significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC control. That mechanism row classified the result as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a further contact.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.} The row becomes a biological readout claim only after a matched translation surface is attached. The required controlled entry is a survival score $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ for a translation-layer readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The admissible test must control at least amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance when TE is used. The intended support condition is not merely a nonzero codon-topology coefficient. It is the stricter condition
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after the declared controls and simpler codon baselines. Conversely, the conjecture is rejected at this boundary when every controlled entry is non-positive, or when the row is rank-degenerate, as in a fitted all-$1.0$ surface on too few organisms.

\paragraph{Asymmetry condition.} The coordinate is expected to matter dynamically only where the third-position pairing is broken by context-specific tRNA or wobble weights. The local condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c &\neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
When this asymmetry is absent, $f_3$ pairing may still be visible as residual geometry, but it should not be promoted to a translation distinction. A related powered tRNA-poverty row computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and found that $R$ sense codons were systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. That row is compatible with a supply-side interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it does not by itself establish a positive $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ entry for the present coordinate.

\paragraph{Axis context.} The per-organism axis spectrum row computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Its captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the dominant-count summary assigned all $11$ computed organisms to $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$. The domain counts were imbalanced, with $1$ archaeon, $2$ bacteria, and $8$ eukaryotes, so domain-level contrasts are descriptive rather than decisive. The largest reported GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. This matters for the present row because it keeps the $d_{\perp}$ identity local: the row supports a finite residual-axis alignment, not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} The chapter records a finite coordinate identity and a precise translation-readout test obligation. It does not assert measured translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Those statements require their own matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used only as bounded codon-adaptation readbacks unless calibrated against organism- and CDS-matched ribosome or TE rows. A downstream protein, structure, function, or phenotype statement is out of scope unless the same coordinate first survives the translation row and then a separate layer-specific contact is supplied.
