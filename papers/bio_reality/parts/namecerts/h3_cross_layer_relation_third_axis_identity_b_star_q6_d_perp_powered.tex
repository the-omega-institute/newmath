\subsection{Third-axis identity for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record concerns the finite residual-coordinate surface obtained after quotienting coding-sequence rows by amino-acid composition and then retaining the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ direction that is sensitive to the third-position involution $f_3$.  The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support.  The mediated set with the added $ACA$ row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturated residual set removes the $ACA,ACG$ pair and leaves
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary is empty, so the finite row has no additional codon sitting outside this saturated support.  This matters because the proposed biological contact is not a free codon-frequency contrast: it is the residue of a paired synonymous imbalance after amino-acid projection, organism effects, GC3, sequence length, $M$-density, and simpler codon baselines have been accounted for.

\paragraph{Powered identity row.}
The powered third-axis identity assay used 5 folds, a minimum of 500 proteins per organism, 41 synonymous design columns, and computed 11 organisms out of 16 requested organisms.  Its finite conclusion was that $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally aligned with the candidate axis called within-family usage frequency.  The recorded mean cosine was $0.128$, with one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$.  In the notation of this chapter, the identity statement is therefore not that $d_{\perp}$ is a universal biological law, but that the finite residual axis has a reproducible direction in the available organism panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\perp} &\leadsto \text{within-family usage-frequency contrast},\\
 \overline{\cos}(d_{\perp},u_{\mathrm{family}}) &= 0.128,\\
 p_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 0.000488.
\end{aligned}
$$
The companion mechanism row keeps the same scale of computation, again with 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, 41 synonymous design columns, and 11 computed organisms out of 16 requested.  It reports that the $GC$-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ direction keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, yielding the finite label ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external data.''  Thus the present chapter may use the row as evidence for a residual usage-frequency axis, but not as evidence for a completed codon-stability code, wobble cluster, or context-specific translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$-stress conjecture.}
The conjectural BioReality reading is narrower than the axis-identity fact.  It asks whether the $f_3$-paired synonymous residual coordinate survives as a translation readout only in organisms or contexts where tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position symmetry, written locally as $w_c \ne w_{c^{f_3}}$.  The bounded statistic is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The proposed support condition is not topological alignment alone.  A positive row requires a non-vacuous controlled entry after the declared covariates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism},GC3,\text{ CDS length},M\text{-density},\text{ mRNA control}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite row therefore separates two facts.  First, the powered axis assay finds a stable $d_{\perp}$ identity with $p=0.000488$.  Second, a measured same-row translation-survival matrix for the exact $f_3$-stress packet is not present in the supplied finite data.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used as bounded codon-adaptation readbacks, but they are not ribosome-occupancy observations unless paired with an organism-matched translation dataset and the stated controls.

\paragraph{Surrounding spectrum.}
Several neighboring powered rows constrain the interpretation.  The two-axis closure assay used 5 folds, a minimum of 500 proteins per organism, 41 synonymous design columns, and computed 11 of 16 requested organisms; it concluded that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed organism directions.  The residual optimality assay, on the same 11-of-16 organism panel with 41 synonymous columns, identified the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  The axis-strength spectrum also used 5 folds, at least 500 proteins per organism, and 41 synonymous design columns, again computing 11 of 16 requested organisms.  In that spectrum, $d_{resid4}$ led on average before $d_{tRNA}$, all 11 per-organism dominant counts were assigned to $d_{resid4}$, and the largest reported GC3 rank effect was $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  Finally, the three-axis sufficiency assay, with the same 5-fold and 11-of-16 organism structure, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  These surrounding rows prevent over-compression: the $d_{\perp}$ identity is a real finite signal in the powered design, but it is not the whole translation-readout story.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization from codon topology, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ geometry, rank sufficiency, or a modeled adaptation surface alone.  It also does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, physical admissibility, phenotype, mechanism, or universality from the $d_{\perp}$ identity row.  Those statements require their own layer-matched contacts.  In particular, the three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is a rank-degenerate boundary rather than survival evidence.  The falsification condition for the present $f_3$-stress claim is correspondingly direct: if a powered panel with organism-matched codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, translation readouts, and mRNA controls leaves $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}\le 0$ or rank-degenerate across the controlled design, then the proposed translation-readout distinction fails, even though the finite $d_{\perp}$ axis identity may remain a valid residual-coordinate observation.
