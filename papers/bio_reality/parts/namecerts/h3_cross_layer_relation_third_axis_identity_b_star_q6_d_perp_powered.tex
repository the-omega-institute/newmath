\subsection{Third-axis identity for the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The object considered here is the finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface obtained after amino-acid quotienting and restriction to the $f_3$-stress component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon geometry.  The coordinate is not a protein-level observable.  It is a synonymous residual row: two coding sequences may differ in this row while carrying the same amino-acid sequence, and the row is allowed to become biologically testable only through a separately attached translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The finite $f_3$ saturation computation fixes the relevant codon support.  The mediated set used in the row is
$$\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
while the saturated $f_3$ image removes precisely $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the finite codon topology does not by itself supply a boundary exception; any later biological assertion must enter through an organism-matched row in which the codon coordinate, the wobble or tRNA weight, and the translation response are observed or explicitly bounded on the same finite panel.

\paragraph{Powered identity call.}
The powered third-axis experiment requested $16$ organisms and computed $11$ of them, using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  On this panel the third residual direction $d_\perp$ was provisionally identified with within-family usage frequency.  The numerical row is small but sign-stable: the mean cosine with the candidate usage-frequency-within-family axis is $0.128$, with a one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$.  In symbols, the finite readout supporting the identity call is only
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{mean}\cos(d_\perp,d_{\mathrm{usage\ within\ family}}) &= 0.128,\\
p_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 0.000488,\\
n_{\mathrm{organisms\ computed}} &= 11,\qquad n_{\mathrm{organisms\ requested}}=16.
\end{aligned}$$
This is an axis-identification statement inside the synonymous residual design.  It says that, after the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual fit has separated the leading tRNA-related direction and the remaining usage structure, the third direction behaves like an organism-wise codon-usage-frequency axis within synonymous families.  It does not say that the axis has already survived against a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency response.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The same powered memory places this identity in a larger finite spectrum.  The two-axis closure test computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms and reported a third axis because $d_\perp$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms.  The residual-optimality row, again on $11$ of $16$ organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous columns, identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  The per-organism axis-strength spectrum also computed $11$ organisms and reported $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the average leader, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; every computed organism had $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant count, while the largest GC3 rank effect was only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  These rows make the third-axis identity a finite residual-coordinate statement rather than a general law of codon use.  They also explain why the present chapter treats the $f_3$ direction as a candidate translation-facing axis: it is adjacent to a tRNA-weighted residual basis, but still needs a nondegenerate translation row before any dynamical claim is made.

\paragraph{Mechanistic boundary.}
The powered mechanism row for $d_\perp$ computed $11$ of $16$ organisms and concluded that the axis remains GC-orthogonalized with significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$; its summary was selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity requiring an additional outside contact.  Separately, the $R$-sense tRNA-poverty test computed $40$ of $46$ requested organisms with $1000$ permutations and passed the call that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN together with dos Reis wobble weights.  These facts support the idea that tRNA or wobble asymmetry can break the third-position $f_3$ symmetry, but they do not yet measure translation survival for the exact $f_3$-stress coordinate.  The decisive row is the controlled entry
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j} > 0,
\end{aligned}$$
where $T_j$ is a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and the controls include amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance when TE is used, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines.  If every such entry is non-positive, or if the matrix is rank-degenerate, the biological reading of the $f_3$-stress coordinate stops at the residual-coordinate level.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore records a positive finite identity call for the powered third axis, not a completed translation-realization claim.  Codon topology, modeled tAI or stAI coupling, and wobble-weight asymmetry are admissible only as bounded coordinate or modeled-readout ingredients.  They do not by themselves establish ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality.  A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, tRNA or wobble weights, a direct translation readout or explicitly bounded modeled readout, matched mRNA control, and the declared $Z$-controlled statistic on the same finite rows.  Until that row is present, the safe conclusion is narrow: in the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis, $d_\perp$ aligns with usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and sign-test $p=0.000488$, and the $f_3$-stress translation conjecture remains open at the readout boundary.
