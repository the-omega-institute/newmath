\subsection{Third-position residual identity at the translation-readout boundary}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-third-axis-identity-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The carrier for this record is the finite residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection of coding-sequence codon rows, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate and to translation-facing rows whose only admitted readbacks are codon usage, tRNA or wobble weights, modeled adaptation weights, and measured translation-efficiency contacts when those contacts are actually joined to the same organism or CDS rows.  The finite saturation computation fixes the local codon alphabet used by the third-position stress test.  The ambient set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
while the $f_3$-saturated residual set removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified boundary list for $f_3$ is empty, so the finite row does not contain a remaining unsaturated $f_3$ boundary outside this subtraction.  This is a coordinate statement about the residual alphabet, not a biological outcome statement.

\paragraph{Residual identity.}
On the powered multi-organism panel, the third residual direction $d_\perp$ is identified with the candidate axis \emph{usage frequency within synonymous family}.  The computation used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, requested $16$ organisms, computed $11$ organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its recorded mean cosine with the usage-frequency-within-family candidate is $0.128$, and the one-sided sign-test value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p = 0.000488.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row supports a narrow identity claim: among the candidate residual axes evaluated in this design, $d_\perp$ is the third-position residual direction aligned with within-family usage frequency.  The claim is not that every organism has the same causal mechanism, nor that the coordinate has already been shown to survive in a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency assay.

\paragraph{Spectrum and rank context.}
The same finite memory places the identity result inside a broader rank-powered coordinate spectrum.  In the axis-strength spectrum run, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The average captured spectrum was led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$, and the per-organism dominant-count summary was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$.  Domain means were descriptive only because the panel was imbalanced, with counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$.  The largest GC3 rank effect in that run was the usage axis, with $\rho=0.136$.  A related two-axis-closure run reported that a third axis is present: $d_\perp$ had systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across the computed organisms.  A separate residual-optimality run identified the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality axis as $f_3$ translation selection.  These facts justify treating the third-position residual coordinate as a real finite-row axis in this panel, while keeping it short of a measured translation-survival conclusion.

\paragraph{Mechanism boundary.}
The mechanism-facing row separates the third residual identity from GC composition and from direct wobble clustering.  The corresponding powered computation used $5$ folds, requested $16$ organisms, computed $11$, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its conclusion was: selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate contact.  The stated reason was that GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  In parallel, the boundary-set tRNA-poverty run computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and found that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  This supports a tRNA-supply interpretation for $R$ avoidance, but it does not by itself prove that the $f_3$ stress coordinate is realized as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The admitted readback for this chapter is therefore bounded.  A positive translation statement would require a non-vacuous controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0$ for a translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where appropriate, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines.  The present finite row contains the powered $d_\perp$ identity and related coordinate-spectrum facts, but it does not contain the same-organism and same-CDS joined matrix of codon counts, wobble weights, measured ribosome or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA controls needed to report that survival statistic.  Rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrices in a three-organism setting are null boundaries for this purpose; they are not counted as survival.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation, folded-structure, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universal-law statement follows from the finite coordinate identity alone.  Codon usage, tRNA-copy summaries, and modeled tAI or stAI weights may constrain a translation-facing coordinate, but they are not the same as observed ribosome dynamics or downstream protein-state measurements.  A downstream claim at any of those layers requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The chapter therefore records a finite third-axis identity for $B^*_{Q6}$ and a precise open boundary: the $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes a translation-readout claim only where symmetry-breaking wobble or tRNA weights are joined to nondegenerate measured or explicitly bounded translation rows and the controlled survival entry is positive.
