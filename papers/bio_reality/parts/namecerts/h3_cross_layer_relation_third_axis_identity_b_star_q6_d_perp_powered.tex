\subsection{The third residual axis as a translation-readout boundary}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Claim packet.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface restricted to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third-position stress coordinate.  The coordinate is read only after amino-acid projection: synonymous codons inside an amino-acid family may still carry an $f_{3}$-paired imbalance, but the amino-acid identity itself is not counted as the signal.  The biological target is therefore narrow.  A row can support the claim only when a controlled translation readout distinguishes the $f_{3}$-stress coordinate after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls have been accounted for.  The intended readout row is $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ may be an organism-scoped tAI or stAI surface, or an observed ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency quantity when same-row data exist.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The finite $f_{3}$ saturation rows fix the combinatorial support before any biological interpretation is attempted.  In the run labelled $76d3dc408a57$, the reported set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA,ACG\}$ consists of the $20$ codons
$AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG$.
After removing $ACA$ and $ACG$, the reported $f_{3}$ saturation set contains $18$ codons:
$AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG$.
The boundary set was empty in this finite row, so the combinatorial boundary readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|M| &= 20,\\
|\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)| &= 18,\\
|\partial_{f_3}R| &= 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes a closed finite coordinate support for the residual calculation.  It does not establish translation survival by itself; it only tells us which codon rows are available to be tested against a separately supplied translation-layer quantity.

\paragraph{Powered third-axis evidence.}
The powered third-axis identity assay was run with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms.  The run $377671f96e96$ passed and identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with the candidate axis named usage frequency within family.  The scalar alignment was small but consistently signed: the mean cosine was $0.128$, and the one-sided sign-test value was $p=0.000488$.  In the notation of this chapter, this supports the statement that the residual direction is not merely an amino-acid-composition artifact: after the quotient by amino acid, there remains a finite synonymous axis whose dominant empirical identity is a within-family usage-frequency direction.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding axis spectrum.}
The same powered panel gives a useful consistency check.  In the axis-strength spectrum run $0146012d8909$, again using $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, the captured spectra were led by $d_{resid4}$ on average and then by $d_{tRNA}$.  The per-organism dominant count was $\{d_{resid4}:11\}$, while the largest GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{usage}$.  The two-axis closure run $99dc7e2d5abf$ reported a third-axis-present conclusion, with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed $d_{\perp}$ directions across organisms.  The residual-axis run $2a526c9fb7f4$ classified the non-tRNA residual optimality as most aligned with $f_{3}$ translation selection, and the core optimal-direction run $373843e81379$ passed on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel.  These rows make the third-axis packet coherent as a finite residual-coordinate claim, not as a direct observation of ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Wobble break condition.}
The $f_{3}$ coordinate becomes biologically testable at the translation layer only when the synonymous pairing is not invisible to the readout.  If the relevant context weights obey $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$, then an $f_{3}$ swap has no translation-weight contrast at that row, and the coordinate is dynamically silent for the chosen readout.  If instead $w_c\ne w_{c^{f_3}}$, the coordinate has a possible translation-layer contrast, but the contrast still has to survive the full control set.  The local decision rule is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} > 0
&\quad\text{only after amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls},\\
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j} \le 0
&\quad\text{as a falsifying row for that translation readout and panel}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is not counted as survival here.  It is a rank-saturated null boundary, because the fitted surface has no remaining contrast with which to distinguish a real $f_{3}$ survival entry from a degenerate readout.

\paragraph{Mechanistic guardrails from finite assays.}
The mechanism-side rows narrow the local interpretation without promoting it beyond the available data.  The third-axis mechanism run $d0893337f0dd$ reported selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, with the CSC or positional identity requiring further contact; its reason was that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ retained significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  Separately, the boundary-set tRNA-poverty run $d7728cc8768e$ used $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms and $1000$ permutations at $\alpha=0.05$, and concluded that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  These finite facts make a tRNA-supply route plausible for a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but they do not replace a same-row translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy measurement.

\paragraph{Fourth-axis exclusions.}
The finite neighborhood also records what this chapter should not identify as the third-axis readout.  The three-axis sufficiency run $2436cae9d455$ concluded that a fourth mechanism is present, so the three-axis system is not a complete biological explanation of all residual variation.  Among fourth-axis tests, the mRNA-stability run $377abd67b8b3$ concluded fourth-axis-not-mRNA-stability, the codon-pair run $b519b5ebd34d$ concluded not-codon-pair with $50$ shuffle-null replicates, the translation-accuracy run $0dc44df59ba7$ concluded not-accuracy after $200$ permutations, and the elongation-dwell run $e4edcde459b7$ was partial in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  These rows are useful negatives and partials: they prevent the $f_{3}$-stress coordinate from being described as a settled downstream mechanism.  They do not supply a protein, structure, function, localization, stability, or phenotype readout.

\paragraph{Local contact requirements.}
The finite support requires several distinct observational interfaces.  Per-organism codon-usage tables can supply codon-count or frequency summaries.  tRNA gene-copy resources can supply organism-level anticodon-family counts.  tAI or stAI resources can supply bounded codon-adaptation weights, provided they remain modelled translation-layer annotations rather than observed ribosome dynamics.  Ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, if attached, must be organism-matched and preferably coding-sequence matched.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control for estimating translation efficiency, not a translation readout by itself.  The same distinction applies to proteomics: protein abundance is a downstream row and cannot be used to infer translation survival unless the $Q_i\to T_j$ step has first survived in the controlled translation readout.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The chapter supports a finite residual-coordinate identity and an open translation-readout test.  It does not assert that $f_{3}$-stress changes mature protein abundance, folded structure, localization, stability, molecular function, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological law.  It also does not assert a full translation mechanism from codon topology, modelled tAI coupling, wobble asymmetry, or review eligibility.  Those stronger statements require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  Until such rows exist, the strongest local conclusion is this: the powered panel detects a small but consistently signed within-family residual axis, with mean cosine $0.128$ and sign-test $p=0.000488$, and this axis becomes a biological translation-readout candidate only where third-position weight symmetry is broken and a nondegenerate controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry is positive.
