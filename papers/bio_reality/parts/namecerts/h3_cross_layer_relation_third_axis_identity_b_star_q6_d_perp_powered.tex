\subsection{The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third-axis identity and the $f_{3}$ readout boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns a finite coding-sequence coordinate surface in which synonymous codon information is first projected through the amino-acid quotient and then read through the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis.  The coordinate under inspection is the third residual direction $d_{\perp}$, together with its $f_{3}$-stress interpretation: a third-position paired imbalance that can become biologically visible only when the translation readout is not invariant under the corresponding $f_{3}$ pairing.  The closed finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support.  The set $M=\mathrm{Med}_{R}\cup \mathrm{ACA}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
After $f_{3}$ saturation of $R$, the surviving row removes exactly the two ACA-side terms from that displayed support:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recorded $f_{3}$ boundary set is empty.  Thus the row is a finite codon-coordinate claim, not an unconstrained biological law.

\paragraph{Powered identity row.}
The powered third-axis experiment classifies $d_{\perp}$ as aligned with usage frequency within synonymous family.  The recorded powered run used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its finite conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\perp} &\sim \text{usage-frequency-within-family},\\
 \overline{\cos}(d_{\perp},\text{usage-frequency-within-family}) &=0.128,\\
 p_{\mathrm{sign}}&=0.000488.
\end{aligned}
$$
The word ``aligned'' is used here in the statistical sense of the experiment: a positive mean cosine against the named candidate axis and a one-sided sign-test value of $0.000488$ on the powered organism panel.  The same data row does not say that the axis has already been observed as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, localization, molecular function, or phenotype.  It says only that, inside the residual synonymous coordinate model, the third direction has a reproducible usage-frequency identity under the stated finite design.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding residual spectrum.}
Several companion finite rows constrain how the third-axis identity should be read.  The two-axis optimality closure row reports a third axis present: $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^{2}$ and same-signed directions across organisms in a run with $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The residual optimality row identifies the non-tRNA residual direction most strongly with $f_{3}$ translation selection, again on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms under the same $5$-fold and $41$-column design.  The mechanism row then separates this third direction from a simple GC explanation: after GC orthogonalization, $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^{2}$, and the recorded finite conclusion is ``selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs external''.  These rows make the third axis a real finite residual feature of the powered coordinate panel, while also leaving its concrete biochemical mediation open.

\paragraph{$f_{3}$ symmetry breaking as the local readout condition.}
For a translation-layer interpretation, the coordinate must be paired with a readout whose context weights are not $f_{3}$-symmetric.  In local notation, if $c$ is a codon context and $c^{f_{3}}$ is its third-position paired context, the relevant condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 w_{c}\neq w_{c^{f_{3}}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Only under such a broken-weight condition can an $f_{3}$-paired synonymous imbalance be expected to survive beyond amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls.  The bounded readout sought by this chapter is therefore not protein output or function.  It is a controlled translation entry, denoted locally by a survival score such as $S_{f_{3}\text{-stress},T_{j}}$, for a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout $T_{j}$ such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The support condition is strict: the entry must be non-vacuous and positive after the declared controls and after simpler codon baselines have been removed.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The finite evidence does not yet contain the same-organism and same-CDS joined matrix needed for a measured $f_{3}$-stress translation-readout statistic.  In particular, the currently recorded rows do not contain joined codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, and matched mRNA abundance rows for this exact subject.  Consequently the three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface, where it appears in related memory, is a rank-degenerate boundary rather than survival evidence.  The correct null reading is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 S_{f_{3}\text{-stress},T_{j}}\leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
for every admissible translation readout row until a powered, non-saturated panel proves otherwise.  A positive modeled coupling or a positive residual-axis identity is not enough to replace this row.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter therefore permits a narrow conclusion and blocks several tempting promotions.  It permits the statement that the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ panel identifies $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, on $11$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, a $500$-protein organism filter, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  It also permits the statement that $f_{3}$-stress is the appropriate local boundary condition for a future translation-row test when wobble or tRNA context weights break $f_{3}$ symmetry.  It does not permit a claim of observed translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or universality.  Each of those stronger claims requires its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic; none follows merely from the codon residual geometry, the usage-frequency alignment, the tAI or stAI route, or the existence of review-ready packet routing.
