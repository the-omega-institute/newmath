\subsection{Third-axis identity for the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-third-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite row being tested.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence residual surface obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family and to organism rows for which synonymous codon usage, modeled adaptation weights, and the declared control columns can be assembled. The row under review is the third residual direction $d_{\perp}$ after the leading tRNA-adaptation and $f_3$-translation-selection components have been separated. Its proposed biological name is not a protein-output claim; it is a finite identity statement for a residual coordinate in a controlled synonymous design. The powered computation used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, assembled $41$ synonymous design columns, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and returned status $\mathrm{passed}$ for the identity test.

\paragraph{Boundary supplied by the $f_3$ saturation row.}
The upstream third-position involution check fixes the finite codon boundary on which this residual coordinate is allowed to speak. The row records
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
M\setminus \operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\},\qquad \partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the $f_3$ boundary itself is empty in the reported saturation computation, while the larger marked set $M$ contains $20$ codons and the saturated $R$ row contains $18$ codons. The two excluded codons, $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, mark where the finite carrier must not be silently promoted into a universal third-position law. The saturation row only licenses a coordinate restriction; it does not by itself measure ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Identity statistic.}
The powered third-axis identity run identifies $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family. The reported conclusion is that $d_{\perp}$ is aligned to the candidate axis \emph{usage-frequency-within-family}, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. In compact form, the finite result is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{\operatorname{id}}(d_{\perp}) &= \mathrm{usage\ frequency\ within\ family},\\
\overline{\cos}(d_{\perp},\widehat{u}) &= 0.128,\\
p_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 0.000488,\\
n_{\mathrm{organisms}} &= 11/16,\qquad n_{\mathrm{columns}}=41,\qquad K=5.
\end{aligned}
$$
The effect size is deliberately read as small but systematic: the cosine is not large enough to justify a mechanistic story, but the sign-test row makes the direction non-random under the finite powered panel. This is why the name is provisional and coordinate-level: $d_{\perp}$ is the residual direction that remains after the two-axis tRNA and $f_3$ span is insufficient, and in this run its best finite identity is a within-family usage-frequency axis.

\paragraph{Relation to the neighboring mechanism rows.}
The companion mechanism computation reports that $d_{\perp}$ remains selection-associated rather than GC-driven: the row says \emph{selection-not-GC}, \emph{not-wobble-clustered}, and that the CSC or positional identity still needs an additional contact. It used the same $5$-fold, $500$-protein minimum, $41$-column synonymous design, and $11/16$ organism coverage. The two-axis closure row separately reports \emph{third-axis-present}, with the reason that $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed directions across organisms. The residual-optimality row names $f_3$ translation selection as the dominant non-tRNA residual axis, while the axis-strength spectrum reports $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the average leading captured spectrum and as dominant in all $11$ computed organism rows; its largest reported GC3 rank effect is only $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. Together these rows position $d_{\perp}$ as a finite third residual direction in a larger coordinate decomposition, not as a final causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout restriction.}
The present row is allowed to motivate a translation-readout test, but it does not already contain the mandatory same-row survival statistic. A positive cross-layer interpretation would require a non-vacuous entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0$ for a matched translation readout $T_j$, such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls. The required controlled row can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0\quad \text{under the declared controls},\\
\operatorname{rank}(S^{QT}) &\text{ nondegenerate},\\
w_c &\neq w_{c^{f_3}}\quad \text{on the context in which the coordinate is interpreted}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Without those three finite conditions, the $f_3$-stress coordinate is still a BEDC-style residual coordinate with modeled or tabular contacts, not an observed translation outcome. In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism surface is a rank-saturated null boundary rather than survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter therefore accepts the powered coordinate identity and rejects all higher-layer promotions. The finite facts support the statement that, in the computed multi-organism synonymous design, the third residual direction $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally best described as usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and sign-test $p=0.000488$. They do not show protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Such claims require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics. The codon lists, tRNA or wobble weights, and residual geometry only define the coordinate surface and its finite identity row; they do not replace a measured translation-efficiency, ribosome-footprint, proteomic, structural, functional, or perturbation contact.
