\paragraph{Powered finite fit.}
The third-axis identity assay was run as experiment $b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_identity\_powered$ with seed $sha256:b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_identity\_powered:deterministic$.  It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, requested $16$ organisms, computed $11$ organisms, and used $41$ synonymous design columns.  On this finite panel the residual direction $d_{\perp}$ was provisionally identified with the candidate axis named usage frequency within family.  The numerical readback was a mean cosine of $0.128$ with one-sided sign-test value $p=0.000488$.  In compact form the tested identity row is
$$\begin{aligned}
d_{\perp} &\leadsto \text{usage-frequency-within-family},\\
\overline{\cos}(d_{\perp},u_{\mathrm{family}})&=0.128,\\
p_{\mathrm{sign}}&=0.000488,\\
(n_{\mathrm{computed}},n_{\mathrm{requested}})&=(11,16).
\end{aligned}$$
The word ``provisionally'' is material here.  The row supports a finite third-axis identity inside the controlled residual geometry; it does not by itself say that ribosomes, proteins, structures, or functions have responded to that axis.

\paragraph{tRNA-poverty contact and non-promotion.}
A separate boundary-set assay, $b\_star\_q6\_boundary\_set\_trna\_poverty\_powered$, requested $46$ organisms, computed $40$, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations, and tested at $\alpha=0.05$. This is relevant because the $f_3$-stress conjecture only becomes dynamically testable where context tRNA or wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry.  It is not enough to name a tAI or stAI surface: modeled codon-adaptation weights remain a bounded translation-layer annotation unless organism-matched measured translation rows are joined to the same CDS coordinates and controls.
