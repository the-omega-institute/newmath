\subsection{The $f_3$-stress residual as a bounded translation-readout question}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-third-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Coordinate under test.}
The record concerns a finite coding-sequence surface in which synonymous codon counts are first compared inside amino-acid fibers and then projected to the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress.  The biological question is deliberately narrow: after amino-acid quotienting, does this residual coordinate remain visible in a translation-layer row such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, once organism, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls are held fixed?  The coordinate is not treated as a protein, structure, pathway, phenotype, or universal biological rule.  It is a finite residual direction whose only admissible readout here is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a translation row $T_j$.

\paragraph{Finite saturation surface.}
The supporting finite codon set is explicit.  The set $M={\rm Med}_R\cup\{\mathrm{ACA}\}$ contains the twenty codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The $f_3$-saturation of the $R$ surface removes the two ACA/ACG boundary codons and gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
{\rm Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary set is empty, so the finite combinatorial surface itself does not supply a leftover boundary codon that can be promoted to a biological mechanism.  This is why the chapter asks for a separate translation row rather than reading dynamics directly from codon topology.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row support.}
The powered third-axis identity run was passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The recorded conclusion identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family: the mean cosine is $0.128$ and the one-sided sign-test value is $p=0.000488$.  In the same finite-row neighborhood, the universal residual optimality run reports that the non-tRNA residual optimality axis aligns most with $f_3$ translation selection, while the two-axis closure run reports a third axis because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and mutually same-signed organism directions.  The mechanism run separates this direction from GC alone: after GC orthogonalization, $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$, with the recorded summary "selection-not-GC; not-wobble-clustered; CSC/positional identity needs\_external."  These finite rows support the existence and provisional identity of the residual axis; they do not by themselves establish measured ribosome behavior.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader axis spectrum.}
The associated spectrum run also passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its descriptive summary says that captured spectra are led by $d_{resid4}$ on average, followed by $d_{tRNA}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant counts fall on $d_{resid4}$, while domain means are explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced domain sample $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$.  The largest GC3 rank effect in that summary is $d_{usage}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  The optimality-axis sufficiency run reports "fourth\_mechanism\_present," so the $f_3$-stress row should not be described as a complete basis for translation behavior.  The universal core tRNA-adaptation run passed under the same $11/16$, $5$-fold, $500$-protein, $41$-column design shape, and the separate $R$-sense tRNA-poverty permutation run passed on $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, with $6$ skipped organisms, $1000$ permutations, and $\alpha=0.05$.  Together these rows put the third-axis identity beside tRNA supply and residual usage-frequency structure, not above them.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The conjecture is positive only if an organism-matched translation row supplies a non-vacuous survival statistic.  For a translation readout $T_j$, the admissible test is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler codon baselines have been controlled or ablated.  If all such entries remain non-positive, or if the fitted surface is rank-degenerate, the conjecture is falsified at this interface.  In particular, an all-$1.0$ three-organism matrix is a saturation boundary rather than evidence of survival.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used as bounded codon-adaptation readouts, but they are not observed ribosome footprints unless a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency contact is joined at the same organism or CDS rows.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The chapter claims only this: a finite $B^*_{Q6}$ residual direction, identified in the powered table as usage-frequency-within-family aligned $d_{\perp}$ with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$, is a well-formed candidate for a controlled translation-readout survival test when $f_3$ symmetry is broken by context tRNA or wobble weights.  No translation realization follows from the coordinate alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the coordinate, from tAI or stAI weights, from codon usage tables, from the $R$ tRNA-poverty result, or from the axis-spectrum runs.  Each of those higher layers requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic before it can be asserted.
