\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ third-axis identity as a bounded translation-readout target}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-f3-stress-third-axis-identity}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record considered here is the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress direction and to translation-readout rows that can be matched organism by organism or CDS by CDS. The finite saturation row fixes the relevant third-position boundary before any biological interpretation is attempted. In the recorded $f_3$ saturation audit, the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ are present in $M$ but absent from the saturated $f_3$ row, while the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty. This gives a finite residual coordinate rather than a free biological conclusion: it says where the third-position imbalance is available for testing, not that the imbalance has already been observed as a ribosome, protein, structure, function, or phenotype effect.

\paragraph{Powered identity call.}
The powered third-axis identity run reports status passed for claim $\mathrm{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.third\_axis\_identity.b\_star\_q6\_d\_perp\_powered}$ with experiment id $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_third\_axis\_identity\_powered}$. The design used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, and used $41$ synonymous design columns. On this finite panel, $d_\perp$ is identified provisionally with the candidate axis \emph{usage frequency within family}, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$. In the notation of the row, the identity call is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_\perp &\leadsto \text{usage-frequency-within-family},\\
\overline{\cos}(d_\perp,\text{candidate}) &= 0.128,\\
p_{\mathrm{sign}} &= 0.000488.
\end{aligned}
$$
The word ``provisional'' is essential: the finite identity call is a coordinate-level alignment under the declared design, not a direct measurement of translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding axis spectrum.}
The same finite-row family gives the local setting in which the third axis is being interpreted. The two-axis closure run computed $11$ of $16$ organisms with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns; its conclusion was that a third axis is present, because $d_\perp$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and the $d_\perp$ directions are mutually same-signed across organisms. The residual optimality run, on the same $11$ of $16$ organism scale and the same $41$ synonymous design columns, reports the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection. The per-organism axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ organisms, with dominant counts $\{\mathrm{d\_resid4}:11\}$ and an imbalanced domain panel $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$; its largest GC3 rank effect is $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. These rows place the third-axis identity inside a finite residual-axis geometry in which tRNA adaptation, $f_3$ residual selection, and a further abundance-like residual direction are separated at the level of the fitted coordinate surface.

\paragraph{Mechanism-side bounds.}
The third-axis mechanism audit reports status passed on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Its conclusion is selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, with the CSC or positional identity left as needing a further outside measurement. The stated reason is that the GC-orthogonalized $d_\perp$ retains significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$. A separate boundary-set audit over $40$ computed organisms out of $46$ requested, with $1000$ permutations and $\alpha=0.05$, reports that $R$ sense codons are systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights. This supports a tRNA-supply explanation for $R$ avoidance, but it does not by itself settle the $f_3$-stress readout as a measured translation-efficiency effect.

\paragraph{Readout gate.}
The biological target is therefore deliberately narrow. The admissible readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ for a bounded translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. A positive claim requires same-organism or same-CDS rows joining codon counts, the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, a wobble-breaking tAI or stAI surface when used, a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency readout when claimed, and matched mRNA abundance controls. The control set must include amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, matched mRNA where applicable, rank checks, and simpler codon baselines. The support condition is a non-vacuous positive survival statistic after those controls; a three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is a rank-saturated null boundary, not survival evidence.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The present finite rows justify a coordinate identity and a bounded translation-readout conjecture. They do not justify a claim that $f_3$-stress already changes translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Those statements remain unavailable unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the relevant controlled statistic is computed. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve as a bounded codon-adaptation readout, but they are not observed ribosome footprints; matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate, not a translation output; and codon-coordinate geometry is not itself curated biological measurement.

\paragraph{Falsification condition.}
The record is falsified at the intended translation boundary if the powered joined panel keeps every controlled $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ entry non-positive, or if the fitted surface remains rank-degenerate after organism, composition, GC3, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls. Conversely, a strictly positive controlled entry would support only the bounded translation-readout relation for the specific readout and panel. It would not promote the coordinate to protein output, structure, function, phenotype, mechanism, or a general biological law without an additional contact at that layer.
