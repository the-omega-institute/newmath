\paragraph{Third-axis identity in the powered panel.}
The powered identity run $377671f96e96$ evaluates the candidate third direction on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, using $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns. The paper-level statement supported by this finite row is therefore narrow: the third residual direction is provisionally identified with within-family usage-frequency imbalance, not with a protein, phenotype, or global biological law.  The adjacent mechanism run $d0893337f0dd$, on the same $11$ of $16$ organism panel with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, records the mechanism label as selection rather than GC, not wobble-clustered, with CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate contact.  This keeps the third-axis identity from collapsing into a GC-composition explanation while also refusing to promote it into a complete mechanistic account.

\paragraph{Relation to the $f_3$ translation conjecture.}
The translation conjecture asks for a stricter object than a residual-coordinate identity.  It asks whether, after controls $Z$ for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler codon baselines, the controlled entry
$$\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}>0
\end{aligned}$$
survives for a translation readout $T_j$, such as bounded tAI, bounded stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  The finite evidence is compatible with such a test because the coordinate is real inside the residual design and the tRNA-related contact is not vacuous.  The tRNA-poverty run $d7728cc8768e$ computed $40$ organisms out of $46$ requested organisms, skipped $6$, used $1000$ permutations, and recorded the $R$ sense codons as systematically tRNA-poor under GtRNAdb tGCN with dos Reis wobble weights at $\alpha=0.05$.  In addition, the universal residual optimality run $2a526c9fb7f4$ identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as $f_3$ translation selection on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, with $5$ folds, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The two-axis closure run $99dc7e2d5abf$ records that a third axis remains present under the same panel size and design width, because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed cross-organism directions.  These rows justify a translation-layer hypothesis, but they do not by themselves supply the final translation-survival statistic.

\paragraph{Spectrum placement.}
The axis-strength spectrum run $0146012d8909$ gives useful context for why the $f_3$-stress coordinate is treated as a boundary-sensitive direction.  It computed $11$ organisms out of $16$, with $5$ folds, $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominant calls are $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  The domain counts are imbalanced, with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and the largest GC3 rank effect is reported for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  This spectrum prevents over-reading: the third-axis identity is finite and repeatable inside the panel, but the panel composition and GC3 effect require the translation test to remain controlled and rank-aware.

\paragraph{Allowed readback.}
The chapter therefore certifies only the following local statement.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate is a finite, post-amino-acid residual direction whose third-axis identity is supported in the powered panel by mean cosine $0.128$ and sign-test $p=0.000488$.  It may be tested against translation readouts only where tRNA or wobble weights break the third-position symmetry, and only through a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface on three organisms is not survival evidence; it is a null boundary for this purpose.  Likewise, modeled tAI or stAI weights may bound a codon-adaptation readout, but they are not observed ribosome footprints unless an organism-matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency row is attached.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No claim about translation realization beyond the stated bounded readout follows from the residual coordinate alone.  No claim about ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causation, mechanism, or universality follows from the $f_3$-stress coordinate, the codon lists, the tRNA-poverty row, the modeled tAI or stAI route, or the third-axis cosine result unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant observation and the corresponding controlled statistic is recorded.  The falsifier is explicit: if every controlled $S_{f_3\text{-stress},T_j}$ remains non-positive, or if the apparent survival is rank-degenerate after the declared controls, the translation-readout conjecture fails even though the finite residual-coordinate identity may remain true inside the codon-usage geometry.
