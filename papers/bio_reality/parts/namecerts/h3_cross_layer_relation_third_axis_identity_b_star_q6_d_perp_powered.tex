\subsection{Third-position stress as a bounded translation-readout question}
\label{sec:bior-claim-q6-f3-stress-wobble-broken-translation-readout}

\paragraph{Coordinate surface.}
The claim concerns a finite coding-sequence residual surface after amino-acid projection.  The coordinate of interest is the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ third-position stress row: a paired synonymous-codon imbalance obtained after quotienting away amino-acid identity and then asking whether the remaining $f_3$ direction has any controlled contact with a translation-layer readout.  The relevant finite saturation row is explicit.  The ambient marked set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $20$ listed codons.  The $f_3$ saturation of the $R$-restricted row removes the two $AC$ entries and gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)={}&\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
so the displayed saturated row has $18$ entries and the recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty.  This is a coordinate fact, not yet a translation observation.

\paragraph{Finite powered row.}
The finite powered computation attached to the third-axis identity used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The recorded conclusion is that $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally aligned with the candidate axis \emph{usage frequency within family}, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  In the local notation of this section, the observed finite row is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{axis}(d_{\perp})&=\operatorname{usage\_frequency\_within\_family},\\
\overline{\cos}(d_{\perp},\operatorname{axis})&=0.128,\\
p_{\mathrm{sign}}&=0.000488,\\
n_{\mathrm{organisms}}&=11,\qquad n_{\mathrm{requested}}=16,\qquad k_{\mathrm{folds}}=5.
\end{aligned}
$$
This identifies a finite residual direction inside the synonymous design.  It does not, by itself, assert that the direction changes ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or any universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the wobble-broken $f_3$ question.}
The biological reading proposed here is deliberately narrower than a general codon-effect claim.  After amino-acid composition has been quotiented out, a third-position stress coordinate can become a translation-readout candidate only where the context weights fail to respect the $f_3$ pairing.  Writing $c^{f_3}$ for the paired third-position codon context, the necessary asymmetry condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_{c^{f_3}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
When this inequality is absent, the $f_3$ pair is invisible to the declared wobble or tRNA-weight surface, and the row is a null boundary for this question.  When the inequality is present, the coordinate may be tested against bounded translation readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, but only after the covariates have been held fixed.

\paragraph{Required survival statistic.}
The controlled survival entry for this chapter is $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$, where $T_j$ is a bounded translation-layer readout.  The row is positive only if the $f_3$-stress coordinate improves the readout after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance where available, rank, and simpler codon baselines have been included.  Equivalently, the intended test is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
under the declared controls and under a nondegenerate rank design.  A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled surface is therefore not a positive survival record: it is a saturated-rank boundary.  The finite facts listed above support the existence and identity of a third residual direction, but the same-organism and same-CDS translation matrix needed for $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ is not present in the supplied rows.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite evidence.}
Several adjacent powered rows constrain the interpretation.  The residual optimality computation over the same scale, again with $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, and $41$ synonymous-design columns, records the dominant non-tRNA residual axis as $f_3\_translation\_selection$.  The two-axis closure row reports that a third axis is present because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms.  The mechanism row classifies the third-axis mechanism as selection-not-GC and not-wobble-clustered, with the positional or CSC identity still needing an additional contact; it also reports that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ keeps significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  The optimality-axis sufficiency row reports a fourth mechanism present, and the axis-strength spectrum row reports $11$ per-organism dominant counts for $d_{resid4}$, with domain counts imbalanced as $n=\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$ and largest GC3 rank effect $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{usage}$.  These rows make the $f_3$-stress question worth testing, while also warning against replacing the translation-readout test with topology alone.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The chapter records a bounded identity-and-test target, not a biological promotion.  The supplied finite rows identify a residual third direction and specify how it should be challenged by translation data.  They do not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, stability, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Each such layer would need its own matched contact and statistic.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used only as bounded codon-adaptation readbacks unless they are joined to the declared translation rows; matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate rather than a translation output; and protein, structure, function, or phenotype readings remain outside this record until a separate layer-specific contact supplies them.

\paragraph{Falsification condition.}
The conjectural row is falsified at the translation interface if, across a powered organism or CDS panel with nondegenerate rank, every controlled entry remains non-positive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall T_j\quad S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}\leq 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
It is also refused if the only apparent signal comes from a rank-saturated matrix, from codon topology without a wobble-breaking weight asymmetry, or from a direct downstream association that bypasses the mandatory $f_3$-stress to translation-readout step.  The current finite evidence is therefore best read as a precise finite coordinate identity plus a pending translation-readout challenge: $d_{\perp}$ has a powered provisional identity as usage frequency within family, while the specific $f_3$-stress survival row remains open until the joined translation matrix is computed.
