\subsection{Third-axis identity at the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-third-axis-identity-f3-stress}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ third residual direction as a finite coordinate surface on coding sequences, restricted to synonymous codon contrasts after amino-acid projection and then tested only through matched translation-readout rows.  The carrier is not a protein, structure, phenotype, or global biological rule.  It is the residual coordinate $d_{\perp}$ together with an $f_3$-stress subcoordinate, organism-indexed codon usage, organism-indexed tRNA or wobble-weight annotations, and a mandatory readout row $T_j$ such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A downstream claim is admitted only after the same coordinate has a non-vacuous controlled translation entry.

\paragraph{Finite codon surface.}
The $f_3$ boundary is anchored by the verified codon lists rather than by a symbolic slogan.  The finite $M$ surface contains twenty codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified $f_3$ saturation of $R$ removes exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from that list, leaving eighteen codons,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified $f_3$ boundary set is empty, so the row does not start from an observed exceptional boundary codon.  Its testable content is instead whether third-position paired synonymous imbalance survives when amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls are included.

\paragraph{Observed third-axis row.}
The powered third-axis identity run used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  Its recorded conclusion identifies $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family: the mean cosine was $0.128$ and the one-sided sign-test value was $p=0.000488$.  This is a finite alignment statement about the residual axis.  It does not by itself say that the axis changes ribosome motion, protein abundance, folding, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or any universal biological property.

\paragraph{Cross-checks on the same coordinate family.}
The surrounding powered rows constrain how the identity may be read.  The two-axis closure experiment, again on $11$ computed of $16$ requested organisms with five folds and $41$ synonymous-design columns, reported a third axis present, with systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed $d_{\perp}$ directions.  The residual optimality row reported that the non-tRNA residual optimum aligned most with $f_3$ translation selection.  The third-axis mechanism row reported selection-not-GC, not-wobble-clustered, and a CSC or positional identity requiring further contact; it also stated that GC-orthogonalized $d_{\perp}$ kept significant cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$.  These rows support a real residual direction in the finite design, while leaving the translation readout itself conditional on a joined dataset.

\paragraph{Rank and axis context.}
The axis-strength spectrum run computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested and found that captured spectra were led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; all $11$ per-organism dominance calls were $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$.  Domain summaries were explicitly descriptive because the panel was imbalanced, with $n=1$ archaeal, $n=2$ bacterial, and $n=8$ eukaryotic organisms.  The largest GC3 rank effect was small, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  The optimality-axis sufficiency run, with the same five-fold and $11$-organism computed design, called a fourth mechanism present.  The fourth-axis checks classified mRNA stability as not dominant in the yeast assay, codon-pair identity as not the fourth axis, translation accuracy as not the fourth axis, and elongation dwell as partial in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Thus the present row should be read as a third-axis identity and translation-boundary target, not as a completed account of all residual biological variation.

\paragraph{Translation readout condition.}
For the $f_3$-stress version of the claim, a positive bounded readback requires a joined finite row containing the residual coordinate, organism, codon counts, wobble-breaking tAI or stAI weights when used, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency measurements when used, and matched mRNA abundance.  The required statistic is the controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$.  In local notation the admissible support condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  If the same surface is all $1.0$ on a three-organism matrix, that is rank saturation rather than evidence.  If all controlled entries remain non-positive, or if rank degeneracy prevents a held-out distinction, the translation row is null even though the internal residual geometry remains defined.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded claim: the powered finite rows identify $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous family, place it in a residual axis system where a third direction is present, and specify an $f_3$-stress translation test that becomes informative only when wobble or tRNA weights break the third-position symmetry.  No translation realization beyond the declared statistic, no folded structure, no physical admissibility, no biological function, no molecular or organismal mechanism, and no universal biological law follows from the coordinate, the codon lists, the tAI or stAI route, or the review status alone.  Each such statement requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
