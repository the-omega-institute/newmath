\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA Decay and RBP Occupancy for the Q6 Coordinate}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-bounded-rna-decay-rbp}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel in which each row carries a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate label, a same-row translation readout when available, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and, for the stronger bridge claim, prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation columns. The coordinate contrast is not a free comparison of transcripts. It is a controlled comparison between high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous RNA windows after amino-acid composition, local coding context, transcript abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch, and the declared translation boundary have been accounted for. The panel is therefore read as a finite row system rather than as a genome-wide law.

\paragraph{Observed numerical anchors.}
Two finite computations are used only as scale and contact anchors for this chapter. The protein-thermal-stability run for the human meltome-linked SCD charge-patterning relation reports $n=6961$ finite protein rows, with experiment run id $8873440304f9$ and a deterministic runtime bucket recorded as $70.0$ seconds. A separate ubiquitylation-occupancy acidic-microenvironment computation reports $2883$ proteins, $10410$ sites, and $2000$ actual permutations under experiment run id $0b96a8e4da3c$. These two entries do not prove the RNA-decay RBP claim. They document that the BioReality table already distinguishes row counts, site counts, permutation budgets, and contact-specific finite statistics, so the present RNA/RBP item must be judged by the same finite-row discipline rather than by verbal analogy.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
The positive statistic, if the required columns are attached, is a bounded readback of whether coordinate-associated RNA half-life differences follow a measured decay-factor RBP occupancy signal after the translation readout and mRNA abundance control are present in the same row system. In symbolic form the allowed comparison has the shape
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta_{Q6}^{\mathrm{half\text{-}life}} \,\text{ is evaluated only after }\, T_{\mathrm{same\ row}}, A_{\mathrm{mRNA}}, C_{\mathrm{local}},\text{ and }O_{\mathrm{RBP}}\text{ are attached},\\
&\Delta_{Q6}^{\mathrm{half\text{-}life}} \text{ may be promoted to an RBP-mediated bridge only if it tracks }O_{\mathrm{RBP}}\text{ under coordinate/occupancy decoupling.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $T_{\mathrm{same\ row}}$ denotes the same-row translation readout boundary, $A_{\mathrm{mRNA}}$ denotes matched mRNA abundance, $C_{\mathrm{local}}$ denotes the declared sequence, isoform, structure, and batch controls, and $O_{\mathrm{RBP}}$ denotes measured occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP. Without $O_{\mathrm{RBP}}$, the result is at most a coordinate-associated RNA half-life association.

\paragraph{Perturbation requirement.}
The mechanism-level bridge is not licensed by correlation alone. The half-life contrast must collapse under loss of the target RBP and return under wild-type rescue, while binding-defective rescue or non-target controls must fail to restore the coordinate-associated half-life pattern. The rescue condition is local to this finite row system:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta_{Q6}^{\mathrm{WT}} \neq 0,\\
&\Delta_{Q6}^{\mathrm{target\ loss}} \approx 0,\\
&\Delta_{Q6}^{\mathrm{WT\ rescue}} \text{ restores the contrast},\\
&\Delta_{Q6}^{\mathrm{binding\ defective}} \approx 0 \quad\text{and}\quad \Delta_{Q6}^{\mathrm{non\ target}} \approx 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a row-level condition on measured RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue behavior. A high-coordinate label that remains predictive when occupancy is absent, unchanged, or not decoupled from coordinate state is not evidence for the stated RBP bridge.

\paragraph{Present verdict.}
The attached RNA half-life, matched mRNA abundance, and ribosome-profiling contacts identify the required readout surfaces, but they do not by themselves supply same-window decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective and non-target controls. The current record is therefore ready as a self-contained bounded chapter statement, but the biological conclusion is a guarded one: the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate can be reported as an RNA-stability descriptor or as a finite association target until the RBP occupancy and perturbation columns are actually joined. No translation output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, adaptation, phenotype, or universal sequence law follows from RNA half-life, codon-window geometry, mRNA abundance, or review eligibility alone. Each such higher-layer assertion would require its own layer-matched measurement and finite statistic.
