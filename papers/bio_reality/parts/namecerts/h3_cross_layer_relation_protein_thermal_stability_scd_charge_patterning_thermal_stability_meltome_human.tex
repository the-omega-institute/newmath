\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA-Decay RBP Bridge for $B^*_Q6$ Windows}\label{sec:translation-bounded-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.} The record considered here is a finite matched panel of RNA and CDS windows. Each row carries a $B^*_Q6$ synonymous residual-coordinate label, local amino-acid and sequence controls, a same-row translation readout when present, a matched mRNA-abundance control, an RNA half-life readout, and, only when externally attached, prespecified columns for decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation state. The biological question is not whether the coordinate alone predicts RNA half-life. The stricter question is whether a coordinate-associated half-life difference can be read as a bounded cross-layer bridge only after the row also contains translation information and after the observed half-life contrast follows a measured decay-factor RBP route.

\paragraph{Rows and adjacent audited surfaces.} The finite audit bundle contains a human protein thermal-stability surface with $6961$ rows under run $8873440304f9$, reported with runtime bucket $70.0$ seconds and a deterministic note that the shell execution stayed below $300$ seconds. Two adjacent cross-layer surfaces are also present in the same verification memory: a ubiquitylation-occupancy surface with $10410$ sites over $2883$ proteins and $2000$ actual permutations under run $0b96a8e4da3c$, and a terminal-degron protein-stability surface with $5303$ C-terminal rows over $5303$ proteins under run $1a932ef5684e$. These finite counts show that the surrounding BioReality audit is row-grounded, but they do not supply the missing RBP-occupancy or perturbation rows for this RNA-decay bridge.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.} A positive bridge would require a finite statistic computed on rows of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i = (&q_i, T_i, A_i, H_i, O_i, L_i, R_i, C_i, Z_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the $B^*_Q6$ residual-coordinate label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout, $A_i$ is matched mRNA abundance, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $O_i$ is measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, $L_i$ records target-RBP loss, $R_i$ records rescue state, $C_i$ records binding-defective or non-target controls, and $Z_i$ contains transcription-rate, local RNA-structure, motif-density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, amino-acid composition, abundance, batch, rank-sufficiency, and simpler-baseline controls. The bridge is allowed only if high- and low-coordinate windows separate in half-life after these controls, if that separation tracks measured decay-factor RBP occupancy under coordinate--occupancy decoupling, if target-RBP loss collapses the effect, and if wild-type rescue restores it while binding-defective or non-target controls do not.

\paragraph{Current readout.} The available contacts provide RNA half-life boundaries, matched mRNA-abundance controls, and translation-efficiency routes. They do not provide a same-organism, same-window matrix containing measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, and non-target controls for this subject. Consequently, the present record is a bounded RNA-stability association target rather than a completed RBP-mediated cross-layer bridge. The same-row translation readout is mandatory because RNA half-life alone does not become translation, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or a general sequence law.

\paragraph{Null model.} The null model says that, after transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation boundary are controlled, the high- and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life. In symbols, the permitted comparison is only the controlled contrast
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_H &= E[H_i \mid q_i = \mathrm{high}, Z_i, T_i, A_i] - E[H_i \mid q_i = \mathrm{low}, Z_i, T_i, A_i],
\end{aligned}
$$
followed by a separate test that any nonzero $\Delta_H$ is explained by $O_i$, collapses under $L_i$, and returns only under the wild-type rescue arm. Without those occupancy and perturbation columns, $\Delta_H$ is not evidence for an RBP mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} The chapter therefore records a ready finite-row formulation with a negative mechanistic verdict at the present contact boundary. The $B^*_Q6$ coordinate may organize rows and define contrasts, and RNA half-life may be read as an RNA-layer observable. No translation realization, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, molecular mechanism, adaptation claim, causal interpretation, or universal biological law follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that readout and its own finite statistic.
