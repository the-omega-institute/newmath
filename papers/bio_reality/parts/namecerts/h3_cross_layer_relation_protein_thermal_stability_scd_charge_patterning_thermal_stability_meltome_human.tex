\subsection{Translation-bounded RNA-decay RBP bridge for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ windows}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The claim is represented on a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel whose rows carry $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate labels, synonymous-window annotations, a same-row translation readout when available, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and externally attached decay-factor RBP occupancy or perturbation columns when such columns exist.  The biological object is therefore not a free-standing RNA-stability association.  It is a bounded cross-layer candidate: a coordinate-tagged synonymous RNA window is allowed to enter the bridge only after the row also records the translation boundary and the transcription-layer abundance control.  The intended finite distinction is between high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows after amino-acid composition, local sequence context, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and local RNA-structure controls have been declared.

The protein thermal-stability entry \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_thermal\_stability.scd\_charge\_patterning\_thermal\_stability\_meltome\_human} was verified with experiment run \texttt{8873440304f9} on $n=6961$ rows, with deterministic runtime recorded as $70.0$ seconds and a note that shell time was below $300$ seconds. These row counts are used only as finite anchors showing that adjacent BioReality surfaces are row-bound and auditable; none of them supplies the missing same-window RBP occupancy, loss, or rescue rows required for the present RNA-decay claim.

\paragraph{Bridge statistic.}
For the RNA-decay bridge itself, the admissible statistic must compare high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows only inside rows that also carry translation readout and matched mRNA abundance.  A positive bridge would require the half-life contrast to follow a prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy signal, not merely the coordinate label.  In schematic form, the admissible readout is a finite contrast such as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{HL}} &= E[\mathrm{half\mbox{-}life}\mid B^{*}_{Q6}=\mathrm{high}, Z, T, A]
 - E[\mathrm{half\mbox{-}life}\mid B^{*}_{Q6}=\mathrm{low}, Z, T, A],\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{RBP}} &= E[\mathrm{occupancy}\mid B^{*}_{Q6}=\mathrm{high}, Z, T, A]
 - E[\mathrm{occupancy}\mid B^{*}_{Q6}=\mathrm{low}, Z, T, A],
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ denotes the declared local and transcript-level controls, $T$ is the same-row translation boundary, and $A$ is matched mRNA abundance.  The bridge is not supported by $\Delta_{\mathrm{HL}}$ alone.  It requires a measured occupancy term, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss that collapses the half-life effect, wild-type rescue that restores it, and binding-defective or non-target controls that fail to restore the effect.

\paragraph{Current verdict.}
The present record is ready as a bounded BioReality chapter because it states a precise finite carrier, a testable statistic, the required control structure, and the local stopping rule.  It is not ready as a positive RNA-decay mechanism claim.  The currently attached contacts provide RNA half-life boundaries, matched mRNA abundance controls, and ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency routes.  They do not provide same-organism, same-window decay-factor RBP occupancy; they do not provide coordinate/occupancy decoupling; and they do not provide target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective and non-target rescue controls.  Consequently the strongest permitted conclusion is conditional: if a future finite panel supplies those columns and the declared statistic survives the controls, the coordinate-associated half-life effect may be read as a bounded translation-aware RBP-decay bridge.  Until then, the coordinate remains either a descriptor of synonymous-window geometry or, at most, a finite RNA-stability association.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone.  No protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the coordinate label, from codon/window geometry, from matched mRNA abundance, from an RNA half-life table, or from the mere presence of a ribosome-profiling contact name.  The same-row translation readout is a boundary condition, not evidence of an RNA-decay mechanism by itself.  A measured RBP mechanism requires the separate occupancy and perturbation/rescue rows described above.  If high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels remain predictive only when measured occupancy is absent, unchanged, or not decoupled from coordinate state, the RBP interpretation must be rejected.  If target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, or wild-type rescue does not restore it while binding-defective and non-target controls remain inactive, the cross-layer bridge is null.
