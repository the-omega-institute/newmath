\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA Decay and RBP Occupancy for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-bounded-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite matched RNA/CDS window panel.  Each row carries a synonymous $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate label, an RNA or CDS window identity, amino-acid and local sequence controls, matched mRNA abundance, a same-row translation readout when available, an RNA half-life readout, and, only when supplied by a separate assay, measured occupancy by a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein.  The row type is deliberately narrow: it is not a protein-state panel, not a folding panel, and not a phenotype panel.  Its only admissible positive readout is whether a coordinate-associated RNA half-life contrast remains attached to an externally measured RBP-occupancy contrast after the translation and abundance boundary variables have been represented in the same finite rows.

\paragraph{Available finite anchors.}
The repository contains a verified cross-layer thermal-stability computation for the linked human meltome-oriented claim, with $n=6961$ finite protein rows and runtime recorded as $70.0$ seconds under the deterministic reporting bucket.  This supplies a scale reference for an already materialized finite protein-level panel, but it does not supply RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, or rescue rows for the present RNA-decay question.  Two other linked cross-layer computations are also finite and externally row-bounded: the ubiquitylation-occupancy acidic-microenvironment panel records $n_{\mathrm{sites}}=10410$, $n_{\mathrm{proteins}}=2883$, and $2000$ permutations, while the terminal-degron stability panel records $n_{\mathrm{Cterm}}=5303$ and $n_{\mathrm{proteins}}=5303$ with runtime $40.3029$ seconds.  These numbers are useful as finite-row witnesses that the BioReality pipeline can bind row counts and runtime metadata to concrete biological surfaces.  They are not evidence for the RNA-decay RBP bridge itself.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
Let $q_i$ denote the residual coordinate label for a synonymous window, $T_i$ the same-row translation readout, $A_i$ the matched mRNA-abundance control, $H_i$ the RNA half-life readout, and $O_i$ the measured occupancy of the declared decay-factor RBP.  The intended finite statistic is a bounded contrast, not a mechanistic conclusion.  A positive bridge can only be considered after the panel contains both translation and RBP-occupancy rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta_H(q)=\mathbb{E}[H_i\mid q_i=q, Z_i]-\mathbb{E}[H_i\mid q_i=q_0, Z_i],\\
&\Delta_O(q)=\mathbb{E}[O_i\mid q_i=q, Z_i]-\mathbb{E}[O_i\mid q_i=q_0, Z_i],\\
&Z_i=(A_i,T_i,\text{amino-acid composition},\text{local RNA structure},\text{motif density},\text{GC and dinucleotide composition},\text{codon-pair context},\text{splice or isoform state},\text{batch}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The row is interpretable as an RNA-decay bridge only if the half-life contrast tracks the measured RBP-occupancy contrast after coordinate and occupancy are experimentally decoupled, collapses after loss of the target RBP, and returns under wild-type rescue while failing under binding-defective or non-target controls.  If any of these pieces is absent, the coordinate remains at most a bounded descriptor of RNA half-life association.

\paragraph{Boundary between association and bridge.}
The half-life contact by itself records transcript or gene rows with curated RNA half-life estimates.  The matched mRNA contact records an abundance denominator or control variable.  The ribosome-profiling contact records a translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy surface when the same row has the required join keys.  None of these three contact names is equivalent to measured decay-factor RBP occupancy.  None of them is a perturbation, loss, rescue, or binding-defective control.  Therefore the present chapter permits the conditional question but does not report a positive RBP-mediated RNA-decay result.

\paragraph{Null form.}
The local null is that, after controlling transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation boundary, high- and low-coordinate synonymous windows are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life.  In symbols, the admissible no-effect condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_i \perp q_i \mid Z_i
\end{aligned}
$$
within the finite matched panel.  A weaker observation, such as a coordinate-associated difference in RNA half-life without measured RBP occupancy, does not break this boundary.  A coordinate-associated difference that survives only in rows lacking the same-row translation readout also does not support a cross-layer bridge, because the translation boundary is part of the declared carrier.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from RNA half-life alone.  No RBP mechanism follows from coordinate labels unless the declared decay-factor RBP is externally measured in the same windows and experimentally separated from the coordinate state.  No cross-layer bridge follows unless target-RBP loss removes the half-life effect and wild-type rescue restores it under binding-defective or non-target controls.  The verified thermal-stability, ubiquitylation-occupancy, and terminal-degron panels show finite row-bounded biological computations in adjacent surfaces, but they do not fill the missing RNA-decay RBP occupancy and perturbation rows required here.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite statement is ready as a bounded BioReality record because its carrier, controls, null, and non-promotion boundary are explicit.  Its biological outcome remains negative for the RBP-bridge claim until a same-organism, same-window panel is attached with $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ labels, translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  The current finite numbers are therefore cited as row-bounded anchors from linked biological computations, not as confirmation of the RNA-decay mechanism.
