\subsection{Translation-bounded RNA-decay bridge for residual synonymous windows}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-bounded-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record considered here is a finite matched panel of synonymous RNA/CDS windows carrying $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate labels, same-row translation readouts when available, RNA half-life readouts, matched mRNA abundance controls, and externally supplied decay-factor RBP occupancy or perturbation columns when such columns are actually attached. The intended biological object is not a protein-thermal-stability measurement, even though the row bundle is linked to a cross-layer thermal-stability claim identifier. It is a narrower RNA-decay boundary question: whether a coordinate-associated half-life difference can be read as a bounded bridge through a specified decay-factor RBP after translation and abundance boundaries are represented on the same finite rows.

The finite audit payload contains three scalar reality checks that fix the scale of the surrounding BioReality run. The thermal-stability panel attached to the named claim has $n=6961$ rows and a deterministic runtime bucket of $70.0$ seconds under run $8873440304f9$. A separate terminal-degron stability panel reports $n_{\mathrm{cterm}}=5303$ and $n_{\mathrm{proteins}}=5303$ with runtime $40.3029$ seconds under run $1a932ef5684e$. A separate ubiquitylation-occupancy panel reports $n_{\mathrm{sites}}=10410$, $n_{\mathrm{prots}}=2883$, and $2000$ actual permutations under run $0b96a8e4da3c$. These numbers are useful as finite-row provenance for the surrounding run family, but they do not by themselves supply RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, or rescue measurements for the present RNA-decay bridge.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout variables.}
For each retained window row $i$, let $q_i$ denote the high-versus-low $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate class after amino-acid composition and local sequence controls, $T_i$ the same-row translation readout when present, $A_i$ the matched mRNA abundance control, $H_i$ the RNA half-life readout, and $O_i$ the measured occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP. Perturbation rows, when present, are denoted by $L_i$ for target-RBP loss, $R_i^{\mathrm{wt}}$ for wild-type rescue, and $R_i^{\mathrm{bd}}$ for binding-defective or non-target controls. The finite bridge statistic is admissible only on rows where the relevant tuple is present:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal P_{\mathrm{bridge}}
  = \{ i : q_i, T_i, A_i, H_i, O_i, L_i, R_i^{\mathrm{wt}}, R_i^{\mathrm{bd}}\ \text{are all observed or declared not applicable by design} \}.
\end{aligned}
$$
When $T_i$ is missing, the row can still contribute to a bounded RNA half-life association, but it cannot be promoted to a translation-attached cross-layer bridge. When $O_i$ or the perturbation/rescue fields are missing, the row cannot support an RBP-mediated mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Admissible positive pattern.}
A positive bridge would require more than a coordinate-to-half-life association. First, high- and low-coordinate synonymous windows must differ in $H_i$ after transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, batch, and simpler-sequence baselines are represented. Second, the half-life contrast must track measured decay-factor RBP occupancy after coordinate and occupancy are experimentally or statistically decoupled. Third, target-RBP loss must collapse the half-life effect, and wild-type rescue must restore it while binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it. These requirements can be summarized as a finite implication on the joined panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i \rightsquigarrow H_i
  \quad \text{is bridge-admissible only if} \quad
  q_i \rightsquigarrow O_i \rightsquigarrow H_i
  \ \text{survives controls and perturbation rescue.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite-row rule for allowing a bounded interpretation; it is not a universal law about codons, RNA decay, or protein behavior.

\paragraph{Null interpretation.}
The default interpretation is exchangeability of high- and low-coordinate labels with respect to RNA half-life after the declared controls and same-row translation boundary are imposed. In the current payload, the required half-life/RBP perturbation matrix is not present. The attached contacts name possible readout surfaces: mRNA half-life datasets, matched mRNA abundance controls, and ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency routes. They do not contain same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective and non-target controls for this subject. Therefore the present chapter is ready as a scoped boundary record and as a null-preserving specification, not as a positive RBP-decay result.

The scalar verified facts also do not fill that gap. The $6961$-row thermal-stability run is a finite protein-level panel, the $5303$-protein terminal-degron run is a finite stability/degron panel, and the $10410$-site ubiquitylation run is a finite post-translational occupancy panel. None of those rows supplies the same-organism, same-window RNA half-life plus decay-factor RBP perturbation and rescue matrix demanded here. They establish that the surrounding BioReality workflow has finite audited panels at neighboring layers, but neighboring finite panels are not substitutes for the specific columns needed by this RNA-decay bridge.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
The allowed conclusion is deliberately narrow. A coordinate-associated RNA half-life difference may be treated as a bounded cross-layer bridge only when the same-row translation readout is present and the half-life effect follows the prespecified measured decay-factor RBP occupancy under decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls. Without those rows, the coordinate remains either a finite descriptor or, at most, a controlled RNA-stability association. No translation output, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causal mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from RNA half-life alone. A separate layer-matched contact must provide each such readout before the claim can move beyond this bounded RNA-decay specification.
