\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA Decay and RBP Occupancy for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Coordinate}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-bounded-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.} The object of this record is a finite matched panel of synonymous RNA/CDS windows. Each row carries a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate label, the local synonymous window, the encoded amino-acid context, the same-row translation readout when available, matched mRNA abundance, and an RNA half-life measurement. A positive RNA-decay RBP bridge is not defined by the coordinate label alone. It requires an additional externally measured decay-factor RBP occupancy column, an explicit coordinate/occupancy decoupling design, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls on the same finite row family. In symbols, the admissible row shape is
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_i = (q_i, w_i, a_i, T_i, A_i, H_i, O_i, L_i, R_i, C_i, Z_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the residual coordinate, $w_i$ is the synonymous RNA window, $a_i$ is the amino-acid and local sequence context, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout, $A_i$ is matched mRNA abundance, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $O_i$ is measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, $L_i$ is target-RBP loss status, $R_i$ is wild-type rescue status, $C_i$ records binding-defective or non-target controls, and $Z_i$ contains the declared adjustment variables. Rows lacking $T_i$ are retained only as boundary rows: they may report RNA half-life association, but they cannot instantiate a translation-bounded cross-layer relation.

\paragraph{Finite audit anchors.} The present finite ledger contains a human protein thermal-stability run for the linked claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_thermal\_stability.scd\_charge\_patterning\_thermal\_stability\_meltome\_human}, with $n=6961$ rows and a deterministic runtime bucket of $70.0$ seconds under experiment run \texttt{8873440304f9}. Two neighbouring cross-layer audits give scale checks but not evidence for this RNA-decay mechanism: the ubiquitylation-acidic-microenvironment run \texttt{0b96a8e4da3c} reports $n_{\mathrm{prots}}=2883$, $n_{\mathrm{sites}}=10410$, and $2000$ permutations, while the degron terminal-motif run \texttt{1a932ef5684e} reports $n_{\mathrm{cterm}}=5303$ and $n_{\mathrm{proteins}}=5303$. These numbers are finite-row anchors for the surrounding BioReality audit surface. They do not supply an RBP occupancy panel, a perturbation/rescue experiment, a p-value for the present RNA half-life statistic, a hit rate, a lambda value, or an $R$ or $M$ list for this particular $B^{*}_{Q6}$ RNA-decay bridge.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.} A positive statistic must compare high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows only after amino-acid composition, local RNA sequence, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, local RNA structure, motif density, and same-row translation readout have been represented in $Z_i$. The intended finite contrast is not merely
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow H_i,
\end{aligned}
$$
but the stricter conditional pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow O_i \longrightarrow H_i
\end{aligned}
$$
under a design in which coordinate state and measured RBP occupancy can be separated. The support condition is therefore: high-coordinate and low-coordinate windows differ in RNA half-life only in the occupancy-resolved stratum; the effect weakens or disappears when the target RBP is lost; wild-type rescue restores the effect; and binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it. If the coordinate remains predictive while measured occupancy is absent, unchanged, or not experimentally separable from the coordinate, the result is only a coordinate-associated RNA half-life pattern, not an RBP-mediated bridge.

\paragraph{Null model.} The local null states that, after the declared controls are present, high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life. Equivalently, within matched rows satisfying the translation boundary and abundance controls, the residual coordinate should not add a stable half-life term once transcription rate, local structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and simpler baselines have been included. Failure of target-RBP loss to collapse the half-life effect, or failure of wild-type rescue to restore it relative to binding-defective and non-target controls, leaves the null unresolved in favour of a bounded RNA-stability association.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.} RNA half-life does not by itself report translation, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law. Matched mRNA abundance is a control surface rather than RNA half-life evidence. Ribosome profiling or translation efficiency is a separate translation-layer readout rather than an RNA-decay mechanism. A measured decay-factor RBP occupancy column is still insufficient unless it is joined to coordinate/occupancy decoupling and perturbation/rescue controls. Thus the strongest currently licensed reading is conditional and finite: if a same-organism, same-window panel supplies $q_i$, $T_i$, $A_i$, $H_i$, $O_i$, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls on the same rows, then the declared statistic may test whether a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate-associated RNA half-life difference is routed through measured decay-factor RBP occupancy. Without that panel, the coordinate remains a bounded descriptor or, at most, an RNA-stability association under explicit controls.

The verified finite numbers presently available are row-count and runtime anchors from linked audits, not a completed RNA-decay RBP panel. Consequently, no positive RBP mechanism, translation realization, folded-state claim, physical-admissibility claim, function claim, phenotype claim, or general sequence law is asserted here. The chapter records the testable boundary: promotion from coordinate-associated RNA half-life to a translation-bounded RBP bridge requires the missing same-row occupancy and perturbation/rescue data and the declared finite statistic.
