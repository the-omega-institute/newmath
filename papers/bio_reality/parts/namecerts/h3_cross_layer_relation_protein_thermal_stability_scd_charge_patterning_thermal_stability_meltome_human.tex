\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA-Decay Bridge for the Q6 Coordinate Panel}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate as a finite label attached to matched RNA/CDS windows, not as a biological mechanism by itself.  The carrier is a row panel in which each row has a residual coordinate label, the local synonymous RNA window, the same-row translation readout when available, a matched mRNA abundance control, an RNA half-life readout, and, for the stronger bridge claim, a prespecified decay-factor RBP occupancy column together with perturbation and rescue controls.  The biological question is whether a coordinate-associated RNA half-life contrast can be promoted from a bounded RNA-stability association to a cross-layer relation.  The promotion is allowed only in the narrow case where the translation readout is present on the same row and the half-life contrast follows measured decay-factor RBP occupancy after coordinate and occupancy have been experimentally separated.

\paragraph{Observed finite anchors.}
The currently verified thermal-stability row set for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_thermal\_stability.scd\_charge\_patterning\_thermal\_stability\_meltome\_human} contains $n=6961$ rows, with deterministic reporting time recorded as $70.0$ seconds and a note that the shell run was verified below $300$ seconds.  This number is useful as a scale anchor for the BioReality table, but it is not an RNA half-life, RBP occupancy, translation, or perturbation measurement.  The neighboring verified protein-state anchors are also finite but layer-distinct: the ubiquitylation occupancy record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.ubiquitylation\_occupancy.ub\_occupancy\_acidic\_microenvironment\_steger2024} reports $10410$ sites on $2883$ proteins with $2000$ actual permutations, and the degron terminal-motif stability record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_degron\_stability.degron\_terminal\_motif\_stability\_voutsinos2026} reports $5303$ C-terminal rows on $5303$ proteins with runtime $40.3029$ seconds.  These finite counts show that the surrounding BioReality registry is capable of carrying row-bounded protein and post-translational readouts, but they do not supply the RNA-window bridge columns required here.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
Let $q_i$ denote the residual coordinate class of a synonymous RNA/CDS window, let $T_i$ denote the same-row translation readout, let $A_i$ denote matched mRNA abundance, let $H_i$ denote RNA half-life, and let $O_i$ denote measured occupancy by the prespecified decay-factor RBP.  A positive bridge would require a finite statistic of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H(q,O,T,A,Z) &= H_{\mathrm{high}\ q,O,T,A,Z}-H_{\mathrm{low}\ q,O,T,A,Z},\\
\Delta_H(q,O,T,A,Z) &\text{ must attenuate when } O \text{ is conditioned or experimentally decoupled},\\
\Delta_H(q,O,T,A,Z) &\text{ must collapse under target-RBP loss and return under wild-type rescue}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $Z$ includes transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, batch, rank sufficiency, and simpler baseline controls.  Binding-defective rescue and non-target controls must fail to restore the effect.  Without these rows the strongest permitted statement is that a coordinate label and an RNA half-life readout can be co-listed for later testing.

\paragraph{Current readback.}
The available contacts include curated RNA half-life payloads, matched mRNA abundance controls, and translation-efficiency routes.  They establish possible boundaries for joining rows, but the present payload does not contain a same-organism same-window panel with measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  Therefore the null model remains active: after controlling transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation boundary, high-coordinate and low-coordinate windows are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone.  No protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universal sequence law follows from the coordinate geometry, the half-life contact, the mRNA control contact, the ribosome-profiling contact name, or review readiness.  A measured RBP-decay bridge can be claimed only if the finite panel contains the required occupancy and perturbation/rescue columns and the declared statistic survives the listed controls.  Until then, the record is a scoped RNA-stability boundary around $B^{*}_{Q6}$ windows, with the $6961$-row thermal-stability anchor and the $10410$-site ubiquitylation and $5303$-row degron anchors cited only as separate finite registry facts, not as evidence for this RNA-decay mechanism.
