\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA Decay RBP Bridge for $B^*_{Q6}$ Windows}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-rna-decay-rbp-bridge}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite panel of matched RNA/CDS windows whose rows carry $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate labels, synonymous-window annotations, a same-row translation readout when available, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance, and prespecified columns for decay-factor RNA-binding-protein occupancy and controls when such columns are externally attached.  The row object is therefore not a bare codon-window table.  It is a bounded joined table in which a coordinate label $q_i$, a translation readout $T_i$, an abundance control $A_i$, a half-life readout $H_i$, and an RBP-control block $O_i$ may be compared only when they occur on the same row or under a declared finite matching rule.  The admissible statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_H(q,O\mid Z,T,A)
  &= \operatorname{contrast}\bigl(H_i : q_i \in q,\ O_i \in O,\ Z_i,T_i,A_i\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes transcription-rate, local RNA-structure, motif-density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, amino-acid composition, transcript abundance, batch, rank-sufficiency, and simpler-baseline controls.  The coordinate is allowed to become a cross-layer bridge only if the half-life contrast tracks measured decay-factor RBP occupancy after coordinate and occupancy have been experimentally separated, disappears under target-RBP loss, and returns under wild-type rescue but not under binding-defective or non-target controls.

\paragraph{Available finite checks.}
The present finite-row evidence does not yet contain the required RBP occupancy and perturbation panel.  It does, however, give useful scale constraints for nearby BioReality records.  The protein thermal-stability run named \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_thermal\_stability.scd\_charge\_patterning\_thermal\_stability\_meltome\_human} reports $n=6961$ matched rows with a deterministic runtime bucket of $70.0$ seconds.  The ubiquitylation occupancy run named \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.ubiquitylation\_occupancy.ub\_occupancy\_acidic\_microenvironment\_steger2024} reports $2883$ proteins, $10410$ sites, and $2000$ actual permutations.  The degron terminal-motif stability run named \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.protein\_degron\_stability.degron\_terminal\_motif\_stability\_voutsinos2026} reports $5303$ C-terminal rows on $5303$ proteins with runtime $40.3029$ seconds.  These scalar checks show that the BioReality table discipline is finite and auditable, but they are not substitute rows for the RNA-decay RBP bridge: none of the three runs supplies same-window RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, rescue, or binding-defective control columns.  No $R$ list, $M$ list, $\lambda$ value, p-value, hit-rate, or module-classification scalar is present for this RNA-decay bridge in the supplied finite-row payload.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The null assertion is row-exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows after the declared controls and the translation-readout boundary are imposed.  In symbols, the intended finite null is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  H_i \perp q_i
  \quad\text{within matched strata of}\quad
  (Z_i,T_i,A_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
unless the RBP block supplies a stronger pattern in which occupancy and perturbation explain the residual half-life contrast.  A positive coordinate-only half-life association is not sufficient.  A positive association in the absence of measured decay-factor occupancy is also not sufficient.  The bridge must show that the same-row RNA half-life effect follows the prespecified RBP occupancy surface, that the coordinate and occupancy variables can be separated rather than merely co-labeled, and that the perturbation/rescue sequence behaves in the target-specific direction.

\paragraph{Decision boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a ready mathematical description with a negative empirical status for the current finite payload.  The coordinate table can describe bounded RNA-stability associations, and it can define the exact rows required for a future bridge statistic.  It cannot presently assert translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, RNA-decay mechanism, or a general biological law.  RNA half-life alone remains an RNA-layer readout.  Matched mRNA abundance remains a control for transcription-layer signal.  Ribosome profiling or translation efficiency remains a separate translation-layer contact.  A cross-layer RNA-decay RBP bridge becomes admissible only after one joined finite panel contains $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate labels, same-row translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls, followed by the declared controlled statistic on that same finite support.
