\subsection{Human Turnover As a Candidate Explanation for a Large Q6 Abundance Residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-turnover-q6-residual}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record tests a narrow human protein-turnover readout against the residual abundance component left after a modeled translation readout has already been removed.  The carrier is a finite matrix over nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$,
with all columns residualized against the same control family used for the human abundance calculation.  The control block has $24$ columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Its reported rank is $23$.

\paragraph{Human abundance residual before the turnover contact.}
On the full human abundance-CDS join, the matrix contains $19053$ coding-sequence rows with a CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$.  The direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance residual has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,
\end{aligned}
$$
and after removing the modeled translation component the remaining modeled complement has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2 &= 1262.2237678771598,\\
d_{mod} &= 0.9208949355679455.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same value of $d_{mod}$ is recovered against the full human join used by the complement experiment: the absolute discrepancy from the reference value is $0.0$.  A separate coverage diagnostic on the turnover-covered rows gives $0.7820306826175398$, so the turnover experiment is not being read as a replacement for the full abundance panel; it is a restricted finite-row projection on the rows where half-life is available.

\paragraph{Turnover contact and identifier join.}
The turnover contact is the Mathieson et al. 2018 human primary-cell protein half-life table, using the median half-life across cell types and replicates from the high-quality sheet.  The identifier map is
$\mathrm{gene\ name}\to$ reviewed UniProt human primary gene name $\to$ STRING $9606.ENSP$.  The turnover payload reports $8164$ valid turnover proteins, no skipped records for missing protein id, duplicate protein id, or nonpositive half-life, and a payload SHA-256 of \texttt{5e19833b82a7e31c05565f2a4c1797d52fcb477fccd0d13a856b58267a5ca54e}.  The abundance side supplies $19483$ valid abundance proteins from the PAXdb whole-organism integrated human readout, and the turnover-abundance-CDS intersection contains $8057$ rows.  The CDS side records $10996$ no-turnover-match exclusions and no exclusions for invalid length, empty sense-codon counts, missing protein id, or no abundance match.

\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
The tested scalar readout is residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life.  Projecting $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$ onto this one-dimensional turnover basis gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,Tturn} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{turnover} &= 3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6},\\
\mathrm{basis\ check} &= 3.5740764900125626\times 10^{-6},\\
d_{combined} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degree-of-freedom matched null uses $200$ trials and has rank list $[1]$.  Its mean absorbed fraction is $0.0001262572958089998$, and its $95$th percentile is $0.0004342285066808464$.  Since
$$
\begin{aligned}
3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6} &< 0.0004342285066808464,
\end{aligned}
$$
the turnover projection does not exceed the finite-row null boundary.  In this chapter, therefore, human protein half-life is not accepted as the observed explanation of the large modeled-translation-complement abundance residual.

\paragraph{Relation to direct abundance survival.}
The negative turnover result is not caused by the absence of a direct protein-abundance association.  In the direct protein-omics survival panel, all $15$ listed organisms are rank sufficient for the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design after the same broad control family.  The human direct protein-omics entry has $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ with $9$ surviving coordinates, $19053$ joined CDS-protein rows, $24$ control columns, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, and residual degrees of freedom $19030$.  Across the direct panel the reported $R^2_{QP}$ values are $0.061906245886660105$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.10217906758013379$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.04613529807085213$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $0.08141786718177903$ for \textit{Dictyostelium discoideum}, $0.0770952887136557$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $0.10767526677985896$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.03980793596647631$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $0.019256877907572044$ for \textit{Halobacterium salinarum}, $0.04454565118019448$ for human, $0.049827984062539195$ for mouse, $0.03209009170649934$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.0646471273126996$ for \textit{Pseudomonas aeruginosa}, $0.02763998603083845$ for rat, $0.29837624994140827$ for yeast, and $0.06397514251295629$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  These entries show that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface is visible to protein abundance, but the present half-life contact absorbs essentially none of the human modeled-complement residual beyond the one-dimensional null.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation readouts.}
The measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency panel gives a separate human translation-readout signal: $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.018837911344127926$ on $11554$ genes with measured TE, with a reported ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$, $9$ surviving coordinates, $24$ controls, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $11531$, and TE residual energy $2052.594706241978$.  In the measured translation-to-abundance decomposition on the human three-way join, the human values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QT,meas} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
S_{QP} &= 0.04288464996165745,\\
M^{QTP}_{meas} &= 0.0027966485036786744.
\end{aligned}
$$
The three-way human join has $11554$ rows, with $19483$ abundance proteins and $11554$ valid TE proteins.  These measured-TE values are much larger than the turnover absorbed fraction, but they are still cross-sectional variance-partition quantities, not perturbation evidence.

\paragraph{Relation to joint TE and mRNA-stability mediation.}
A separate human joint-variance partition with measured TE and mRNA stability reports $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$.  Its human joint mediation fraction is $0.16890712099923358$, with TE-only fraction $0.00549490864190343$, stability-only fraction $0.15314679870183337$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  This comparison matters because it separates transcript stability from protein half-life: the present turnover contact is a protein half-life readout, and its absorbed fraction $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ is far below its own null cutoff even though other finite-row mediation surfaces can be positive.

\paragraph{Four-case readback for this chapter.}
The local classification is therefore a negative H-candidate readback.  There is a visible direct human $q_i\to P_k$ association in the protein-abundance panel and a positive measured $q_i\to T_j$ translation-efficiency survival statistic in the ribosome-TE panel, but the specific human turnover readout does not explain the large modeled-translation-complement residual.  It is not a case where protein half-life absorbs the remaining abundance signal.  The correct finite-row reading is: large human residual remains after modeled translation, and median primary-cell half-life, as joined here, is too weak relative to a matched rank-one null to count as the missing readout.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical-admissibility statement, molecular-function assertion, causal pathway, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this chapter.  The half-life table is an observational protein-turnover contact, the PAXdb abundance table is a steady-state protein-omics contact, and the translation-efficiency values come from ribosome-footprint over mRNA readouts in their own datasets.  A positive direct abundance association is not by itself a mechanism.  Conversely, the failure of the half-life projection does not say that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ is biologically irrelevant; it says only that this finite human turnover contact, under the stated controls and join, does not account for the modeled-translation-complement abundance residual.
