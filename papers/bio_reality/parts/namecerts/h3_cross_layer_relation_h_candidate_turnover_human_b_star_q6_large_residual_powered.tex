\subsection{Human Turnover Test for the Large B$^*_{Q6}$ Protein-Abundance Residual}\label{sec:bioreality-human-turnover-large-q6-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether measured human protein half-life explains a large component of the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual association with steady-state protein abundance.  The row surface is the human join of coding-sequence codon counts, PAXdb whole-organism protein abundance, and Mathieson et al. primary-cell protein turnover.  The B$^*_{Q6}$ side has nine residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control basis has 24 columns, rank $23$, and consists of an intercept, log CDS length, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  The full human codon-abundance join contains $19053$ CDS rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ against $19483$ valid abundance proteins.  The turnover join contains $8057$ rows after mapping Mathieson gene names through reviewed human UniProt primary gene names to STRING identifiers; $10996$ CDS records have no turnover match.  The turnover table reports $8164$ valid half-life proteins, no missing protein identifiers, no duplicate protein identifiers, and no nonpositive half-lives.

\paragraph{Residual target.}
The target is not raw protein abundance.  Protein abundance is first restricted to the B$^*_{Q6}$-explained component after the control basis, and then the modeled tAI component is removed.  On the full human codon-abundance join, the B$^*_{Q6}$ abundance component has squared norm $1370.6490492302532$, while the modeled-translation-complement component has squared norm $1262.2237678771598$.  The resulting modeled complement fraction is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.9208949355679455 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The turnover-specific join preserves this modeled-complement diagnostic exactly against the reference computation: the recorded absolute difference is $0.0$, the reference join has $19053$ rows, and the turnover join has $8057$ rows.  A separate coverage diagnostic computed on the smaller join is $0.7820306826175398$, so the finite-row interpretation is tied to the explicit turnover-available submatrix rather than to an implicit whole-proteome claim.

\paragraph{Turnover projection.}
The measured turnover readout is residualized log$_{10}$ half-life, using the median half-life across Mathieson primary-cell types and replicates.  Projecting the large modeled-translation-complement abundance residual onto this one-dimensional turnover readout gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{turnover}} &= 0.0000035740764900116538,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{turnover}}^{\mathrm{basis\ check}} &= 0.0000035740764900125626,\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The basis check agrees at numerical precision, but the effect size is far below the degree-of-freedom matched null envelope.  Across $200$ one-rank null trials, the null mean is $0.0001262572958089998$ and the null 95th percentile is $0.0004342285066808464$; the observed absorbed fraction does not exceed that boundary.  Thus the finite-row outcome is a computed negative result for turnover absorption, not a hidden positive mediator.

\paragraph{Layer comparison.}
The turnover result is best read beside the measured translation and abundance surfaces.  In the direct protein-omics computation, the human B$^*_{Q6}$ residual matrix has $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ and all nine coordinates survive the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold after the same class of length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  In the measured ribosome-TE computation, the human measured TE join uses $11554$ genes, reports ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$, and gives $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$ with nine surviving coordinates.  In the measured TE to abundance decomposition, the human three-way join has $11554$ rows, $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, raw measured mediation fraction $0.0027966485036786744$, and clipped measured mediation fraction $0.0027966485036786744$.  In the joint TE and mRNA-stability partition, the human four-way join has $11322$ rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$.  The corresponding human variance-partition fractions are TE-only $0.00549490864190343$, stability-only $0.15314679870183337$, and joint TE-plus-stability $0.16890712099923358$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The human turnover table is a valid finite contact for asking whether a large modeled-translation-complement abundance residual is explained by protein half-life.  The answer on this row set is negative: turnover is measured and joined, but it absorbs only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ of the target component, below a null 95th percentile of $4.342285066808464\times 10^{-4}$.  The record therefore strengthens the boundary around the human H-candidate search.  B$^*_{Q6}$ has a measurable direct protein-abundance association in human and a measurable TE association in human, but this particular half-life surface does not explain the large residual left after modeled tAI conditioning.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
This section makes no causal statement.  The half-life projection is descriptive, condition-mixed across human primary-cell measurements, and evaluated on the $8057$ proteins that survive the turnover-abundance-CDS join.  The result does not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  A positive statement at any of those layers would require its own measured contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.  Bulk abundance, modeled tAI complement, measured TE, mRNA stability, and human turnover are kept as separate readout surfaces; no one of them is promoted into a higher-layer biological conclusion without the corresponding layer-specific data.
