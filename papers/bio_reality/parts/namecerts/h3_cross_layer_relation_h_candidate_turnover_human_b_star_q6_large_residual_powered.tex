\subsection{Human turnover as a test of the large modeled-translation complement}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-human-turnover-large-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether human protein half-life accounts for the large part of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal that remains after removing the modeled translation readout.  The active carrier is a finite joined table over real human CDS codon counts, PAXdb whole-organism protein abundance, the nine residual $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates, the standard covariate block, and a Mathieson 2018 human primary-cell protein-turnover table.  The nine tested coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The covariate block has $24$ columns before numerical rank reduction and rank $23$ after residualization; it consists of the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.

\paragraph{Join and readouts.}
The full human abundance--CDS join reports $19053$ CDS rows with hit rate $0.9779294769799313$.  The turnover table reports $8164$ valid proteins, identified by the route Mathieson gene name $\to$ reviewed human UniProt primary gene $\to$ STRING $9606.ENSP$.  After intersecting abundance, CDS, and half-life rows, the actual turnover test uses $8057$ joined proteins.  The abundance source contains $19483$ valid PAXdb proteins and the turnover source contributes $8164$ valid half-life rows; $10996$ CDS records are skipped only because no turnover match is available.  The turnover readout is residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life, using the median half-life across the Mathieson primary-cell cell types and replicates.

\paragraph{Modeled-translation complement.}
Let $Q$ denote the nine-dimensional residual coordinate matrix after the covariates are removed, let $P$ denote residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance, and let $T_{\mathrm{mod}}$ denote the modeled tAI-style translation readout used only as a bounded translation proxy.  The direct human $Q$-to-abundance component has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{Q}\|^{2} &= 1370.6490492302532,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the component left after removing the modeled-translation readout has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^{2} &= 1262.2237678771598.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the modeled readout explains a substantial part of the human direct protein-omics association, but leaves a large residual component.  The reported modeled fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.9208949355679455,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the recomputed turnover-run value matches the full human complement reference with absolute difference $0.0$.  The separate coverage diagnostic on the turnover join is $0.7820306826175398$, so the half-life table is large enough to test the candidate, but it is not identical to the full abundance panel.

\paragraph{Turnover projection.}
The turnover candidate asks whether residualized human half-life absorbs the large $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ component.  The fitted turnover projection gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.0000035740764900116538,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{T_{\mathrm{turn}}}^{\mathrm{basis\ check}} &= 0.0000035740764900125626,\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The basis check agrees to numerical precision, so the negative result is not an artifact of the basis implementation.  However, the absorbed fraction is far below the degree-of-freedom matched null envelope.  Across $200$ null trials, with rank-one turnover designs, the null mean is $0.0001262572958089998$ and the $95$th percentile is $0.0004342285066808464$.  Since
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.0000035740764900116538 &< 0.0004342285066808464,
\end{aligned}
$$
the human turnover readout does not pass as an explanatory carrier for the large modeled-translation complement.

\paragraph{Placement beside protein and translation rows.}
This negative turnover call is not a denial that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates have protein-omics signal.  In the powered direct protein-abundance panel, all $15$ tested organisms have rank-sufficient residualized coordinate matrices with $9$ surviving coordinates, and the human direct protein-abundance entry is $R^{2}_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ on $19053$ joined CDS rows.  Nor is it a denial that measured translation readouts can absorb part of the protein association: on the measured TE--abundance join, human has $11554$ three-way rows, $S_{QT}^{\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, and measured mediation fraction $0.0027966485036786744$.  The broader joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability calculation on human gives $R^{2}_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, corresponding to a joint mediation fraction $0.16890712099923358$ with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  A separate yeast dictionary certificate with $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$ finds coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ by at most three readouts and leaves unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These companion rows put the turnover result in context: human half-life is a tested candidate for the residual, but it is not the dominant measured readout in this finite table.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The human turnover candidate is therefore a computed null at the declared threshold.  The finite table supports the statement that the large human modeled-translation complement remains mostly unexplained by median primary-cell protein half-life, even though the direct human protein-abundance association and other measured-readout decompositions are nonzero.  The conclusion is descriptive and finite-row only: it does not establish translation rate, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  Any such promotion would require a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed on the corresponding joined rows.
