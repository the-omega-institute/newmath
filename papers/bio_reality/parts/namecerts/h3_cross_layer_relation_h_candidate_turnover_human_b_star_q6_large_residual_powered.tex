\subsection{Human turnover test for the large residual component}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-turnover-large-residual}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record isolates the human turnover readout as an $H$-candidate for the residual protein-abundance component left after the modeled translation readout has already been accounted for.  The finite surface has $8057$ human rows on the simultaneous CDS, PAXdb abundance, and Mathieson turnover join.  The larger abundance-CDS reference join contains $19053$ rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$ and $19483$ valid abundance proteins.  The turnover side contributes $8164$ reported and valid turnover proteins, mapped by the route ``Mathieson gene name to UniProt human reviewed primary gene name to STRING 9606.ENSP''.  The half-life column is the median half-life across cell types, and the readout used in the regression is residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control surface.}
The source coordinate block has nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  On the full human abundance-CDS join, the squared norm of the abundance projection onto the $Q$ surface is $1370.6490492302532$, while the norm after removing the modeled translation component is $1262.2237678771598$.  Thus the modeled translation readout accounts for a large fraction of the direct $Q$-abundance component:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.9208949355679455,\\
 \lVert P_{Q}\rVert^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,\\
 \lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2 &= 1262.2237678771598.
\end{aligned}
$$
The current turnover join preserves the same modeled-complement value, with absolute difference $0.0$ from the full-reference diagnostic for $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}$.  A separate coverage recomputation on the active join gives $0.7820306826175398$, which is useful as a join-sensitivity diagnostic but is not used to promote the turnover readout.

\paragraph{Turnover absorption result.}
The turnover candidate is weak on this surface.  Projecting the modeled-translation-complement abundance residual onto residualized human half-life gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
 R^2_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
 a_{\mathrm{turn}} &= 0.0000035740764900116538,\\
 d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The basis check gives $0.0000035740764900125626$, agreeing with the reported absorbed fraction to numerical precision.  Against the degree-of-freedom matched null, the observed absorption does not pass: the null mean is $0.0001262572958089998$, the null $95$th percentile is $0.0004342285066808464$, the rank list is $\{1\}$, and $200$ null trials were run.  The registered finite verdict is therefore not that turnover explains the residual; it is that turnover was tested on a large human join and did not exceed the fixed null threshold.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein abundance.}
The turnover test is meaningful because the direct protein-abundance surface is not empty.  In the broader measured PAXdb analysis, human has $19053$ joined CDS-protein rows, all nine coordinates survive the direct $Q$ to protein-abundance screen, and the reported direct human value is $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$.  Across the same direct protein-omics experiment, the human residualization design again has $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for $Z$, rank $9$ for the residualized $Q$ block, and $19030$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$.  This says that the protein-abundance association is a real finite-row association under the stated controls, but it does not identify turnover as its explanation.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation readouts.}
The measured ribosome-TE contact gives a separate comparison point.  In human, the measured TE join contains $11554$ genes, with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$, and the controlled translation readout survival is $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$ with all nine coordinates surviving.  In the measured TE to protein-abundance decomposition, the human three-way join also has $11554$ rows, with $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, and measured mediated fraction $0.0027966485036786744$.  The human joint TE-and-mRNA-stability decomposition reports $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, with joint mediation fraction $0.16890712099923358$ and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These related rows place turnover in context: measured TE alone is a weak human absorber, stability contributes more than TE in this particular human decomposition, and protein half-life does not clear its own null in the large-residual turnover test.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite readback is therefore case-specific.  Human has a direct $Q$ to protein-abundance association after the amino-acid, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls.  Human also has a measured TE association and a small measured TE-conditioned abundance fraction.  The protein half-life readout, however, absorbs only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ of the modeled-complement abundance residual and falls below the null $95$th percentile.  The correct interpretation is that the large human residual remains largely outside this turnover readout.  The result narrows the candidate list for $H$ rather than validating turnover as the mediator.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, protein folding statement, physical admissibility statement, molecular function statement, causal mechanism, phenotype claim, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter.  The half-life projection is descriptive and condition-limited: Mathieson 2018 reports human primary-cell protein half-lives summarized across cell types, while PAXdb abundance is an integrated protein-abundance readout.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is not a mechanism, and a weak $q_i\to T_{\mathrm{turn}}$ projection is not a turnover pathway.  Any stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact and a positive controlled statistic at that layer.
