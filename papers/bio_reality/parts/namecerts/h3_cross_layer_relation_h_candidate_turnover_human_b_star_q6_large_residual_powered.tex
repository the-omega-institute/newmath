\subsection{Human Turnover Against the Large Modeled-Translation Complement}\label{sec:bioreality-human-turnover-large-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether measured human protein turnover accounts for the part of the human $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal that remains after a modeled translation readout has already been removed.  The finite surface is the human CDS--protein join with $n=8057$ rows simultaneously carrying real CDS codon counts, PAXdb protein abundance, and Mathieson 2018 protein half-life values.  The broader human CDS--abundance join contains $19053$ CDS rows, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$ and $19483$ valid abundance proteins.  The turnover source reports $8164$ valid turnover proteins; the final matched panel loses $10996$ CDS rows only through missing turnover match, with no missing protein id, invalid length, empty sense-codon count, nonpositive abundance, duplicate turnover id, missing turnover id, or nonpositive half-life record reported.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The design uses the nine residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control block has $24$ columns and rank $23$: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the M-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  On the full human abundance join, the $B^*_{Q6}$ projection has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,\\
\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2 &= 1262.2237678771598,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.9208949355679455.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same modeled-complement value is recovered on the turnover computation as $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9208949355679455$, with absolute difference $0.0$ from the full-abundance reference.  A separate coverage diagnostic gives $0.7820306826175398$, so the row restriction is visible but does not change the registered modeled-complement scalar used in this certificate.

\paragraph{Turnover projection.}
The turnover readout is residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life, using the Mathieson human primary-cell protein half-life table, median across cell types and replicates.  The id route is gene name to reviewed UniProt primary gene name to STRING $9606.ENSP$.  Projecting the large modeled-translation complement $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto the residualized turnover column gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{turnover}} &= 0.0000035740764900116538,\\
\mathrm{basis\ check} &= 0.0000035740764900125626,\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two absorption computations agree to numerical precision, so the finite linear-algebra readback is internally stable.  However, the effect is tiny on the scale of the complement: turnover accounts for only about $3.57\times 10^{-6}$ of the registered residual target.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The degree-of-freedom matched null uses $200$ trials with rank one in every trial.  Its mean is $0.0001262572958089998$ and its $95$th percentile is $0.0004342285066808464$.  The observed turnover absorption $0.0000035740764900116538$ does not exceed that null envelope.  The finite-row conclusion is therefore negative for human turnover as an explanatory carrier of the large modeled-translation complement: the computation is admissible and stable, but the registered turnover readout does not carry excess absorption above the matched one-column null.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding protein and translation readouts.}
The negative turnover result should be read against the stronger human protein-abundance and translation surfaces.  On the direct human PAXdb abundance test, $B^*_{Q6}$ has $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ with all nine coordinates surviving after the same family of length, amino-acid, GC3, and M-density controls.  On measured ribosome TE in human, the matched TE join uses $11554$ genes and reports $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, again with nine surviving coordinates.  In the measured TE-to-abundance decomposition, human has $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, and measured mediated fraction $0.0027966485036786744$.  In the joint TE and mRNA-stability variance partition, human has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.04274178488511215,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04250692268197657,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.036196017359154814,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.0355223930537993.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding human joint mediation fraction is $0.16890712099923358$, with TE-only fraction $0.00549490864190343$, stability-only fraction $0.15314679870183337$, and right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  Thus the broader human readout family contains reproducible abundance-side residual structure, but this particular half-life projection is not the readout that absorbs the large modeled-translation complement.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This subsection asserts only a finite statistical projection on matched human rows.  It does not establish a causal mediator, a translation-rate mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Modeled translation weights, bulk abundance, measured TE, mRNA stability, and half-life are distinct readout surfaces.  A stronger biological interpretation would require an additional layer-matched contact and a separate statistic showing that the relevant $q_i\to T_j$ step survives and that conditioning on that measured readout removes or reduces the downstream association.  In the present finite surface, human turnover is recorded as a tested but non-excess H-candidate for the large residual.
