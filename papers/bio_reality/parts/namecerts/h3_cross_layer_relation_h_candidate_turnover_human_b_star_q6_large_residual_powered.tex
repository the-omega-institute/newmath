\subsection{Human Turnover as a Candidate for the Large Human $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-turnover-bstar-q6-large-residual}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether measured human protein half-life accounts for the large human protein-abundance component left after the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have already been compared with a modeled translation readout.  The finite carrier is a joined human table with real CDS codon counts, PAXdb protein abundance, and Mathieson et al. human primary-cell protein half-lives, all matched by the reported gene-name to UniProt reviewed primary-gene to STRING identifier route.  The active joined table contains $8057$ proteins with all abundance, turnover, and CDS readouts present.  It is a strict subset of the full human abundance-CDS join, where $19053$ CDS rows were available and the reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9779294769799313$.  The turnover source reported $8164$ valid turnover proteins, and the abundance source reported $19483$ valid abundance proteins.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control design.}
The source design uses the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These coordinates are residualized together with the same sibling control family used for the abundance and translation-readout comparisons: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  The control matrix has $24$ columns and observed rank $23$.  On the full human abundance-CDS join, the $B^*_{Q6}$ projection onto residualized protein abundance has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,\\
\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2 &= 1262.2237678771598.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the modeled translation readout removes only a small part of the human $Q$-linked abundance component, leaving a large complement for candidate downstream readouts to test.

\paragraph{Turnover projection.}
The turnover readout is residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life from Mathieson et al. 2018 primary-cell protein turnover, using the median half-life across cell types and replicates.  Projecting the modeled-translation complement $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto this one-dimensional turnover readout gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{Absorbed}_{T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.0000035740764900116538,\\
\mathrm{Absorbed}_{T_{\mathrm{turn}}}^{\mathrm{basis\ check}} &= 0.0000035740764900125626,\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The basis check agrees at the displayed precision, so the projection arithmetic is internally stable.  The effect size is nevertheless tiny: the absorbed fraction is about $3.57\times 10^{-6}$ of the modeled-translation complement.  A degree-of-freedom matched null with $200$ trials and rank-one surrogate readouts has mean $0.0001262572958089998$ and $95$th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$; the turnover projection does not exceed this null boundary.  Consequently, human protein half-life is recorded here as a computed candidate readout, not as an explanatory account of the residual.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein abundance survival.}
The same human abundance surface has a positive direct $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance association after controls: the direct protein-omics run reports human $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ with all nine coordinates surviving at the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold.  This direct association is not a turnover mechanism.  It says that the residual coordinate family carries protein-abundance information after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls.  The turnover test asks a narrower question: whether the part of that signal left after a modeled translation readout is absorbed by measured human half-life.  The answer is negative at the stated null comparison.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation readouts.}
Measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency provides a separate translation-layer comparison.  In the human measured-TE run, $11554$ genes with TE were used, the ribosome-TE join hit rate was $0.6064136881331024$, and the controlled measured translation survival was $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$ with nine surviving coordinates.  In the measured translation-conditioned abundance decomposition on the human three-way join, the reported quantities were $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, raw measured mediation fraction $0.0027966485036786744$, and clipped measured mediation fraction $0.0027966485036786744$.  The one-dimensional measured TE readout therefore detects a translation association, but it absorbs almost none of the human $Q\to P$ abundance signal.  The turnover result is even smaller and lies below its matched null.

\paragraph{Stability and mRNA context.}
A broader human variance partition using measured TE and mRNA stability also leaves most of the human abundance association direct.  In that run the human four-way join contains $11322$ rows, with $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$.  The joint mediation fraction is $0.16890712099923358$, with TE-only fraction $0.00549490864190343$ and stability-only fraction $0.15314679870183337$.  This places the turnover candidate in context: human protein half-life alone is not the same object as mRNA stability, and in the direct turnover projection it does not clear the rank-one null threshold.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The four-case interpretation for this chapter is therefore local and finite.  The table has a real human turnover contact, a large controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual, and a numerically computed projection of that residual onto residualized protein half-life.  The readback is case C-like with respect to turnover: direct protein-abundance information exists, but this tested turnover coordinate does not absorb the residual above the degree-of-freedom matched null.  It is not a case D translation-mediated protein mechanism, because neither measured TE nor turnover removes the human direct $Q\to P$ signal in the required sense.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, molecular-function statement, causal pathway, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from this chapter.  The turnover table is observational, condition-aggregated across primary-cell measurements, and used only as a descriptive projection readout.  Protein half-life absorption below the null says that this particular human candidate does not explain the large residual; it does not prove that turnover is irrelevant in other conditions, cell types, perturbations, or organisms.  Any stronger biological conclusion would require a separate layer-matched contact with its own joined rows, controls, and positive statistic.
