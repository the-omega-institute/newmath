\paragraph{Finite row object.}

The finite surface has one organism, Homo sapiens, and $8057$ joined rows containing coding-sequence codon counts, PAXdb protein abundance, and Mathieson primary-cell protein half-life. $$

The residualization design contains $24$ control columns and has $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$. The turnover side reports $8164$ valid turnover proteins, and the all-readout join keeps $8057$ rows; $10996$ CDS records are skipped because no turnover match is present.

\paragraph{Observed turnover absorption.}
Projecting the modeled-translation complement onto residualized human protein half-life gives
$$
\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{turnover}} &= 3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6},\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{basis\ check}} &= 3.5740764900125626\times 10^{-6},\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The agreement between the direct absorption value and the basis-check value is at the displayed precision, so the finite linear readback is internally stable. However, the degree-of-freedom matched null has mean $0.0001262572958089998$ and $95$th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$ over $200$ trials with rank list $[1]$. The observed absorption $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ is below this null boundary. The finite verdict is therefore not that turnover explains the residual; it is that a concrete human turnover contact was attached and tested, and that this particular turnover scalar does not carry excess absorption of the large human modeled-translation complement.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite four-way interpretation for this chapter is: positive direct protein-abundance association exists for the human $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates; a measured human translation-efficiency association exists but is weak; the human measured-TE mediation fraction is very small; and the tested human turnover readout absorbs only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ of the modeled-translation complement, below the degree-of-freedom matched null $95$th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$. The result is therefore a bounded negative H-candidate result for turnover, not a promotion to a mechanistic account.

\paragraph{Local limits.}
No translation mechanism, protein folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, molecular-function statement, phenotype statement, causal pathway, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter. The turnover contact is a statistical half-life readout from human primary-cell data, the abundance contact is a steady-state protein-omics readout, and the translation contacts are measured or modeled readout surfaces with their own scope. Any stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact and a finite statistic showing that the relevant downstream signal survives the declared controls and is attenuated by the appropriate measured translation step.
