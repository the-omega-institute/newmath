\subsection{Human turnover test for the large $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-human-turnover-large-residual}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record isolates a human protein-turnover candidate for the part of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance signal that is not absorbed by a modeled translation readout.  The finite surface has $9$ residual coordinates, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix has $24$ columns before rank reduction and rank $23$ on the human abundance join.  Its columns are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates.

\paragraph{Large residual before the turnover readout.}
On the full human CDS--protein-abundance join, $n=19053$ CDS rows are available, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$ against the protein-abundance table.  The projection of residualized protein abundance onto the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate span has squared norm $1370.6490492302532$.  After subtracting the modeled translation component, the remaining modeled-complement residual has squared norm $1262.2237678771598$.  Thus the modeled translation readout leaves a large human residual rather than exhausting the $Q\to P$ association:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,\\
\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2 &= 1262.2237678771598,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.9208949355679455.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same value $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9208949355679455$ is recovered on the current full-human reference calculation, with absolute difference $0.0$ from the complement experiment reference.  A coverage diagnostic recomputed on the turnover-available subset gives $0.7820306826175398$, which records the expected shift caused by requiring a turnover match while preserving the full-join complement value used to define the target residual.

\paragraph{Turnover rows and identifier route.}
The turnover contact uses the Mathieson et al. 2018 human primary-cell protein half-life table, taking the median half-life across cell types and replicates.  The route maps Mathieson gene names through reviewed human UniProt primary gene names to STRING identifiers of the form $9606.ENSP\cdots$.  The turnover source reports $8164$ proteins and $8164$ valid positive half-life entries.  After joining turnover, PAXdb whole-organism protein abundance, and CDS codon rows, the active table has $8057$ rows.  The abundance side reports $19483$ valid proteins, while the CDS side reports $19053$ abundance-matched rows; $10996$ CDS records are skipped only because no turnover match is available.  No turnover rows are skipped for duplicate, missing, or nonpositive half-life identifiers, and no abundance rows are skipped for duplicate, missing, or nonpositive abundance identifiers.

\paragraph{Turnover absorption statistic.}
The candidate test projects the large modeled-complement residual $P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life.  The resulting turnover association is numerically very small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{turnover}} &= 3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6},\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{\mathrm{basis\ check}} &= 3.5740764900125626\times 10^{-6},\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The rank of the turnover readout in the matched test is one.  Against a degree-of-freedom matched null with $200$ trials, the null mean is $0.0001262572958089998$ and the null $95$th percentile is $0.0004342285066808464$.  The observed absorbed fraction does not exceed this threshold.  In this finite table, human protein half-life therefore does not account for the large $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ modeled-complement abundance residual.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein-omics survival.}
The negative turnover absorption is not a negative result for the existence of direct $Q\to P$ association.  In the broader direct protein-omics survival matrix, the human abundance table has $n=19053$ joined CDS rows, residual degrees of freedom $19030$ after the $Z$ controls, $rank(Z)=23$, $rank(\widetilde X_Q)=9$, residual abundance energy $30769.5367150478$, and $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$, with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold.  Across the same direct-survival run, every listed organism has $rank(\widetilde X_Q)=9$ and all $9$ coordinates survive, with examples including $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, and $0.061906245886660105$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168.  The human turnover result says only that the human residual left after the modeled translation readout is not materially absorbed by this particular half-life readout.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and mRNA stability.}
Measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency data provide a separate finite route for translation readout, and the human measured mediation calculation has $n=11554$ three-way CDS--TE--abundance rows.  On that human measured-TE table, $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S_{QT}^{\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, and the measured mediated fraction is only $0.0027966485036786744$.  A separate joint TE--mRNA-stability variance partition on the human four-way join has $n=11322$, $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04250692268197657$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.036196017359154814$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$.  Its joint mediation fraction is $0.16890712099923358$, with TE-only fraction $0.00549490864190343$, stability-only fraction $0.15314679870183337$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers show that human abundance residual structure can be partly reduced by measured transcript and translation-associated readouts in some matched designs, but the Mathieson half-life projection tested here is not the readout that carries the large modeled-complement residual.

\paragraph{Dictionary context.}
The yeast dictionary-style reduction is included only as a scale comparison for residual reducibility.  On its unified yeast context join, the registered partial-mediation certificate uses $n=1800$ rows out of $4758$ available joined rows, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and $120$ deterministic permutations.  The best selection-aware coverage is $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$, with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ and selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$.  That comparison reinforces the local reading of the human turnover test: residual protein-abundance signal may be real and partially reducible, but a proposed readout must be tested on its own matched rows and null surface.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The human turnover candidate is computed and interpretable as a finite descriptive projection, but it is not supported as an explanatory carrier for the large human $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ modeled-complement abundance residual.  The decisive finite-row inequality is
$$
\begin{aligned}
3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6} &< 0.0004342285066808464,
\end{aligned}
$$
so the turnover absorption lies below the degree-of-freedom matched null $95$th percentile.  The record therefore preserves the direct $Q\to P$ abundance signal while rejecting this particular human half-life readout as a positive $H$-candidate for the large residual.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
All quantities in this subsection are statistical readbacks on matched finite rows.  They do not establish causality, translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality.  The modeled translation readout is not an observed perturbation, the half-life projection is not a causal mediator, and bulk protein abundance is not a structural or functional assay.  Any claim at those higher layers would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
