\subsection{Human turnover test for the large B*\_Q6 protein-abundance residual}\label{sec:bioreality-human-turnover-bstarq6-large-residual}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record tests whether measured human protein half-life carries a visible part of the large B*\_Q6 protein-abundance residual left after the modeled translation readout has already been removed.  The finite carrier is a joined human table with B*\_Q6 residual coordinates, measured PAXdb protein abundance, and Mathieson 2018 protein half-life entries, all matched at the protein identifier level.  The B*\_Q6 side has nine residual coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The shared control block has 24 columns before rank reduction: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the 20 amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the M-density baseline defined by the union support of the nine projected B*\_Q6 vectors.  The control rank is $23$, so all residual projections are taken after this finite controlled quotient rather than on raw codon counts.

\paragraph{Joined rows and source identity.}
The human abundance table reports $19053$ CDS/protein rows joined to abundance, with a CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$ and $19483$ valid abundance proteins in the PAXdb human whole-organism integrated contact.  The turnover contact is Mathieson et al. 2018 human primary-cell protein half-lives, using the median half-life across cell types and replicates from the high-quality sheet.  Its protein-key mapping is $\mathrm{gene\ name}\to$ reviewed UniProt primary gene name $\to$ STRING $9606.\mathrm{ENSP}$, and it contains $8164$ valid turnover proteins.  The three-way abundance--turnover--CDS join used for the turnover projection has $8057$ rows.  The skipped CDS records are dominated by $10996$ rows with no turnover match; no joined record is reported as lost because of empty sense-codon counts, invalid length, missing protein identifier, missing abundance match, or malformed object shape.  The turnover payload has byte size $276520$ and SHA-256 digest $5e19833b82a7e31c05565f2a4c1797d52fcb477fccd0d13a856b58267a5ca54e$; the source spreadsheet digest is $e0503f49230680cbf86fbc86db145ba439c03382e584fde8f0d83241c9826515$.

\paragraph{Residual target.}
Before adding the turnover readout, the human B*\_Q6 protein-abundance surface is large.  On the full human abundance join, the controlled B*\_Q6 projection has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,\\
\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2 &= 1262.2237678771598,\\
d_{mod} &= 0.9208949355679455.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $T_{mod}$ is the modeled tAI complement readout used to remove the modeled translation component from the abundance residual.  The value $d_{mod}=0.9208949355679455$ means that most of the human B*\_Q6 abundance residual remains after this modeled translation conditioning.  The same $d_{mod}$ is recovered on the current turnover analysis as the full-human reference value, with absolute discrepancy $0.0$ against the stored complement experiment.  A recomputed coverage diagnostic on the turnover join gives $0.7820306826175398$, recording that the $8057$-row turnover table is a substantial but not complete slice of the $19053$-row human abundance surface.

\paragraph{Projection onto turnover.}
The turnover readout is residualized as $\log_{10}$ half-life and then used as a one-dimensional candidate explanation for the already translation-conditioned abundance residual $P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}$.  The measured turnover projection is small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,T_{turn}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{turnover} &= 0.0000035740764900116538,\\
\mathrm{absorbed}_{turnover}^{basis\ check} &= 0.0000035740764900125626,\\
d_{combined} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The equality of the direct absorbed fraction and the basis-check value to the displayed precision is an internal numerical consistency check for the rank-one turnover projection.  The combined residual score $d_{combined}=0.8901589776585174$ remains high, so the human half-life readout removes only a minute fraction of the translation-conditioned B*\_Q6 abundance residual.

\paragraph{Degree-of-freedom null.}
Because the turnover readout has rank one after residualization, the comparison uses a degree-of-freedom matched null with 200 trials and rank values equal to $1$.  The null mean is $0.0001262572958089998$ and the null 95th percentile is $0.0004342285066808464$.  The observed absorbed fraction $0.0000035740764900116538$ is below both the null mean and the null 95th percentile, and the recorded decision is $\mathrm{exceeds\ null}=\mathrm{false}$.  Thus this finite test does not support human protein half-life as the carrier of the large B*\_Q6 residual that remains after modeled translation conditioning.

\paragraph{Relation to direct protein abundance.}
The same human protein-abundance layer is not empty: in the direct PAXdb survival computation, human has $19053$ joined CDS/protein rows, residual degrees of freedom $19030$ after the same rank-$23$ control block, full B*\_Q6 residual rank $9$, residual abundance energy $30769.5367150478$, and $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ with all nine coordinates surviving the positive-threshold screen.  The turnover test therefore does not say that B*\_Q6 has no human protein-abundance relation.  It says the opposite, more specific thing: the large direct abundance residual is present, but this particular human half-life contact does not explain it beyond a rank-matched null baseline.

\paragraph{Local readback.}
On the four-case reading for this chapter, the human turnover branch is a negative H-candidate for the large residual.  The input side is powered and nondegenerate: nine B*\_Q6 coordinates, rank-$23$ controls, $19053$ abundance-matched CDS rows, and $8057$ rows after adding the Mathieson turnover contact.  The direct abundance relation is positive on the protein-omics surface, but the tested half-life projection absorbs only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ of the translation-conditioned residual, below the rank-one null 95th percentile.  Therefore the readback is: protein abundance remains a real downstream statistical contact for B*\_Q6, while human median protein half-life is not the observed explanatory readout for the large leftover component in this finite table.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
This chapter makes no causal or mechanistic promotion.  The half-life table comes from human primary-cell measurements summarized across cell types, while the abundance contact is an integrated PAXdb whole-organism protein-abundance readout; the joined matrix is observational, cross-sectional, and assembled from distinct measurement programs.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association does not by itself establish translation, and the small $q_i\to T_{turn}$ projection here does not establish protein degradation control, folding state, localization, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Such statements would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In this finite record, turnover is only a tested covariate for the human B*\_Q6 abundance residual, and the tested covariate fails to clear its own null threshold.
