\subsection{Human turnover as a residual candidate for the large $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance complement}
\label{subsec:bior-h3-human-turnover-large-residual}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record tests whether human protein half-life carries any appreciable part of the protein-abundance component that remains after the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon residual surface has already been separated from a modeled translation readout.  The finite table has $8057$ joined human rows, obtained by matching coding-sequence codon counts, PAXdb human whole-organism protein abundance, and Mathieson et al. human primary-cell protein half-life measurements.  The CDS-to-abundance source reported $19053$ joined CDS rows with a hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$, while the half-life source reported $8164$ valid turnover proteins.  The turnover join uses the mapping route Mathieson gene name $\to$ reviewed UniProt human primary gene name $\to$ STRING $9606.\mathrm{ENSP}$, and the selected turnover column is the median half-life across cell types.

The residual design uses the same nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates throughout: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$ after including an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine coordinates.  On the full human abundance join, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance projection has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,\\
\|P_{Q\perp T_{mod}}\|^2 &= 1262.2237678771598,\\
d_{mod} &= 0.9208949355679455.

$$
The same $d_{mod}$ value is recovered on the current turnover run against the full human complement reference, with absolute difference $0.0$; the smaller active half-life join changes row availability but not the reference complement value used for the residual target.

The tested readout is residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life.  Projection of the modeled-translation complement abundance residual onto the one-dimensional turnover readout gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,Tturn} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\

\mathrm{basis\ check} &= 0.0000035740764900125626,\\

$$
The degree-of-freedom-matched null used $200$ trials with rank list $[1]$.  Its mean absorbed fraction was $0.0001262572958089998$, and its $95$th percentile was $0.0004342285066808464$.  The observed absorbed fraction is therefore below the matched null boundary.  In this finite table, human protein half-life is computed as a candidate downstream coordinate, but it does not absorb the large human $B^{*}_{Q6}$ modeled-translation complement at a level exceeding the one-column null.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured translation and protein-abundance surfaces.}
The turnover result is interpretable only because the surrounding finite surfaces are nonempty and rank-powered.  Direct protein-abundance survival was computed for $15$ organisms with all nine coordinates surviving after the same family of controls. The human direct surface used $19053$ joined CDS rows and all nine residual coordinates.

Measured ribosome-TE survival was also computed in four organisms.  The measured $S^{QT}$ values were $0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.009394266754822262$ in Escherichia coli, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish, with all nine coordinates surviving in each case.  The measured mediation surface then gave measured fractions $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ in Escherichia coli, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.0$ in zebrafish after clipping the suppressive raw value.  Thus the human turnover test sits in a regime where human has a positive direct protein-abundance association and a positive measured TE association, but only a very small measured TE mediation fraction and no above-null turnover absorption of the modeled-translation complement.

The broader reduction dictionary shows that the residual is not merely a data-availability artifact.  In the joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition, the three computed organisms had joint mediation fractions

The single-mediator components for human were $0.00549490864190343$ for measured TE alone and $0.15314679870183337$ for mRNA stability alone, and the overlap mode was classified as approximately independent under the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  In yeast, a restricted readout dictionary with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n_{join}=1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations achieved coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ with selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The $f3\_stress$ dwell test, also in yeast, joined $1257$ rows and found held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ against null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, with $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. These comparison rows matter because turnover fails in a specific way.  The human half-life readout is present, joined, and tested against a matched null, but its absorbed fraction is $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, far below the $0.0004342285066808464$ null $95$th percentile.  Other finite readouts can reduce nontrivial pieces of the residual surface in other organisms or readout families, so the human turnover result should be read as a negative localization of this particular candidate, not as a collapse of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ abundance residual itself.

\paragraph{Verdict and scope boundary.}
The finite verdict is that human protein half-life is a tested H-candidate for the large human modeled-translation complement, but it is not an above-null absorber on this table.  The record supports the narrower statement that the residual remains mostly beyond this one-dimensional turnover readout under the declared controls and join.  It does not support a causal degradation pathway, a translation mechanism, a folding claim, a physical-admissibility claim, a molecular-function claim, or a universal biological rule.  Those stronger readings would require a separate layer-matched measurement and a new controlled statistic at that layer.  Protein abundance, measured TE, mRNA stability, dwell, localization, turnover, and structural-order labels are therefore kept as separate readout surfaces rather than collapsed into a single biological mechanism.
