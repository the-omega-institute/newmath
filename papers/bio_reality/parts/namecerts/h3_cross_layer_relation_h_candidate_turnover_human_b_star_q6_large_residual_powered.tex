\paragraph{Projection statistic.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the Z-residualized human abundance component explained by the $9$-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design, and let $T_{\mathrm{mod}}$ denote the modeled translation readout used for the complement.  The finite computation reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,\\
\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2 &= 1262.2237678771598,\\
d_{\mathrm{modeled}} &= 0.9208949355679455.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same modeled-complement fraction is recovered on the turnover run with absolute difference $0.0$ against the full human abundance-CDS reference.  The diagnostic recomputation on the restricted coverage gives $0.7820306826175398$, which is treated as a coverage diagnostic rather than a replacement for the full-reference complement value.

\paragraph{Turnover absorption.}
The tested H-candidate is residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life.  Its projection onto the modeled-translation complement is extremely small:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{absorbed\_by\_turnover} &= 0.0000035740764900116538,\\
\mathrm{basis\ check} &= 0.0000035740764900125626,\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degree-of-freedom matched null used $200$ trials, with rank list $[1]$, null mean $0.0001262572958089998$, and null $95$th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$.  The observed absorbed fraction is below the null $95$th percentile, so the finite verdict is not a positive turnover explanation for the large human complement.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The readback is therefore a bounded negative result.  Human protein turnover was successfully attached to the same abundance-CDS coordinate surface and the descriptive projection was computed, but the half-life column does not clear its own one-dimensional null benchmark.  The large modeled-translation complement remains mostly outside this turnover readout: $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9208949355679455$ before the turnover projection and $d_{\mathrm{combined}}=0.8901589776585174$ after adding the turnover direction.  This is compatible with the broader human mediation table, where measured TE explains little by itself and mRNA stability accounts for a larger fraction than measured TE, but it does not license a turnover mechanism for the remaining signal.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folding statement, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, causal pathway, organismal phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter.  The half-life column is a descriptive human protein-turnover readout from primary-cell measurements, not an intervention, rescue assay, ribosome-occupancy measurement, folding assay, localization assay, or functional screen.  A positive higher-layer conclusion would require a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic that survives its own controls.  Here the relevant finite statistic is below the matched null threshold, so the proper conclusion is that human turnover, as measured in this panel, does not absorb the large $B^{*}_{Q6}$ modeled-translation-complement abundance residual.
