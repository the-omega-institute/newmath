\paragraph{Readback.}
The chapter-specific readback is case-boundary rather than mechanism.  The direct $Q\to P$ human abundance association exists, and measured $Q\to T$ survival exists, but this turnover table does not absorb the modeled-complement abundance residual beyond a rank-matched null.  The result therefore points away from bulk human primary-cell half-life as the missing large-residual carrier.  It leaves open condition-specific degradation, localization, ribosome dwell, RNA stability, protein-complex membership, and other post-translational or context-dependent surfaces.
