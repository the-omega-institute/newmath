\subsection{Human turnover test for the large residual abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-turnover-large-residual}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether human protein turnover can account for the part of the human protein-abundance signal that remains after the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are projected away from the modeled translation readout.  The row set is finite and joined at the protein/CDS level: the human codon/protein surface contains $19053$ CDS rows after the abundance join, while the turnover test uses $8057$ rows carrying CDS, PAXdb abundance, and Mathieson 2018 protein half-life information.  The abundance side reports a CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$, with $19483$ valid abundance proteins and $8164$ valid turnover proteins.  The turnover source is the Mathieson et al. 2018 human primary-cell half-life table, summarized by the median half-life across cell types and mapped by gene name through reviewed UniProt primary genes to STRING $9606.ENSP$ identifiers.  No turnover records were discarded for duplicate protein identifiers, missing protein identifiers, or nonpositive half-life.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control design.}
The tested source is the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family
$K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline over the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  On the full human abundance/CDS join, the norm of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance projection is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_Q\|^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,\\
\|P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\|^2 &= 1262.2237678771598.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the modeled translation readout leaves most of the human $Q$-associated abundance component in the complement.  In the recorded scalar summary this is reported as $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9208949355679455$ on the full human join.  The turnover test preserves that same complement value for the human abundance/CDS reference surface: the difference between the current and reference value is $0.0$, although the active turnover join has only $8057$ rows because $10996$ CDS records lack a turnover match.

\paragraph{Turnover projection statistic.}
The half-life readout is residualized against the same control family before testing whether it absorbs the modeled-translation complement.  The turnover projection has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{Absorb}_{\mathrm{turn}} &= 3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6},\\
\mathrm{Absorb}_{\mathrm{turn}}^{\mathrm{basis\ check}} &= 3.5740764900125626\times 10^{-6},\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The basis check agrees to numerical precision, so the tiny absorption value is not a coordinate-basis artifact.  The degrees-of-freedom matched null used $200$ rank-one trials; its mean absorption was $0.0001262572958089998$ and its $95$th percentile was $0.0004342285066808464$.  The observed turnover absorption is therefore below both the null mean and the null $95$th percentile, and the recorded null comparison has $\mathrm{exceeds\_null}=\mathrm{false}$.  In ordinary scientific terms, human half-life is measured and joined successfully, but it does not explain the large modeled-translation-complement abundance residual on this finite surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the neighboring readouts.}
The negative turnover result is meaningful only because the surrounding readout surfaces are nonvacuous.  Direct protein-omics survival for human abundance after the same broad control family gives $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving at the positive threshold on $19053$ joined CDS rows.  The measured ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency surface for human has $11554$ genes with TE, a TE join hit rate of $0.6064136881331024$, and a controlled measured-TE survival value $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.018837911344127926$, again with $9$ surviving coordinates.  In the measured translation-to-abundance decomposition, the human three-way join uses $11554$ rows and records
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.04288464996165745,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.0027966485036786744.
\end{aligned}
$$
These neighboring surfaces show a real protein-abundance association and a measurable translation-readout association, but they do not promote the half-life result into a positive turnover explanation.  The turnover surface specifically asks whether residualized human half-life absorbs the modeled-translation-complement component; the answer on the finite Mathieson/PAXdb/CDS join is no.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The local readback is a bounded negative H-candidate result.  Human protein turnover is an admissible finite readout for the question because the joined matrix contains codon-derived residual coordinates, protein abundance, and a measured half-life scalar for the same protein identifiers.  The finite estimate is nevertheless below the degree-matched null envelope: $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}<0.0004342285066808464$.  The large residual abundance component therefore remains mostly unexplained by this turnover readout, and the appropriate current statement is that turnover does not account for the human modeled-translation-complement abundance signal on the tested panel.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter makes no causal or mechanistic claim.  The half-life projection is a descriptive regression-style readback from a finite joined matrix, not a perturbation, rescue, pulse-chase mechanism, or temporal proof.  It also does not establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Any such assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic designed for that layer.  Here the only supported conclusion is the narrower one: after the stated controls and the modeled translation complement construction, the human turnover readout fails to absorb the large $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual abundance component above its rank-matched null baseline.
