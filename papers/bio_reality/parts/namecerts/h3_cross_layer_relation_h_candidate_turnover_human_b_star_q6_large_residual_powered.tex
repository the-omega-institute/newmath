\subsection{Human Turnover as a Residual H-Candidate}\label{sec:bioreality-human-turnover-h-candidate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.} This chapter tests whether human protein half-life can account for the large human abundance component left after the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are first related to a modeled translation readout. The finite table is not a causal experiment. Its carrier is the joined human CDS, abundance, and turnover surface: real CDS codon counts; nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates; the control matrix $Z$; PAXdb whole-organism protein abundance; and Mathieson et al. human primary-cell protein half-lives, reduced to a median half-life readout across cell types and replicates. The tested object is the projection of the modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual $\widehat P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}$ onto residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life.

\paragraph{Finite rows and controls.} The human abundance contact reported $19053$ CDS rows after codon-abundance joining, with a CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$ and $19483$ valid abundance proteins. The turnover contact reported $8164$ valid turnover proteins, and the all-readout join retained $8057$ human rows. The identifier route was Mathieson gene name to reviewed UniProt human primary gene name to STRING $9606.\mathrm{ENSP}$. The half-life column was the median half-life across cell types. The control surface used $24$ columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates. The residualized control rank was $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$.

\paragraph{Turnover projection.} The turnover readout was residualized against the same $Z$ controls and used as a rank-one H-candidate surface. Its direct relation to the modeled-complement residual was small:
$$\begin{aligned}

\operatorname{absorb}_{\mathrm{turnover}} &= 3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6},\\
\operatorname{absorb}_{\mathrm{basis\ check}} &= 3.5740764900125626\times 10^{-6},\\

The degree-of-freedom matched null used $200$ trials and rank-one comparison surfaces. Its null mean was $0.0001262572958089998$, and its $95$th percentile was $0.0004342285066808464$. The observed turnover absorption did not exceed that threshold. Thus the finite row verdict is not that turnover explains the large human residual; it is that the turnover projection was computed, was positive only at a minute descriptive scale, and failed the local null comparison.

\paragraph{H-candidate verdict.} The tested human turnover module is a negative H-candidate for explaining the large modeled-tAI-complement abundance residual. The positive direct abundance association is not promoted to a turnover mechanism, and the turnover projection is not large enough to explain the residual. The local module classification is therefore: computed, rank-one, null-bounded, and non-explanatory at the declared threshold. By comparison, a yeast partial readout dictionary achieved selection-aware coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $1800$ joined rows, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$; the yeast $f3\_stress$ dwell mediation run had held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, and $f3$-dwell approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. The positional dwell run classified the dominant segment as $5prime$, while the structural-order ramp run classified its conclusion as $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$ with weighted ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$ and weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$. Those companion rows show that the table system can register positive finite signals when a measured readout carries them; the human turnover row simply does not supply such an explanation for the residual under its own null.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.} No measured translation process, folding property, physical admissibility statement, molecular function, biological mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter. The result is a controlled finite-row projection of a human abundance residual onto a half-life readout. A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact with the relevant measurement and a statistic showing that the same $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate first survives at the required readout layer and then loses or changes its downstream association after conditioning on that readout. Bulk abundance, modeled tAI complement, and median human half-life do not by themselves establish such a route.
