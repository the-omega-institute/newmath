\subsection{Human Turnover as a Candidate Residual Readout}\label{sec:bioreality-human-turnover-q6-residual}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether human protein turnover accounts for the large human abundance component left after the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are compared with a modeled translation readout.  The finite table has $8057$ human rows joined across CDS codon counts, PAXdb abundance, and Mathieson et al. 2018 primary-cell protein half-life data.  The abundance side reports $19483$ valid abundance proteins, while the turnover side reports $8164$ valid turnover proteins.  The CDS join used for the full human abundance surface contains $19053$ CDS rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9779294769799313$.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control geometry.}
The tested source is the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual family
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  All projections use the same control family: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected coordinates.  The control design has $24$ columns and rank $23$.

\paragraph{Large human residual before turnover.}
On the full human abundance-CDS join, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance component has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lVert P_Q\rVert^2 &= 1370.6490492302532,\\
\lVert P_{Q\perp T_{\mathrm{mod}}}\rVert^2 &= 1262.2237678771598.
\end{aligned}
$$
The modeled-translation complement fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
d_{\mathrm{modeled}} = 0.9208949355679455,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the turnover experiment reproduces this full-join reference exactly for the reported diagnostic, with absolute difference $0.0$ and tolerance success recorded as true.  Thus the turnover test is not asking whether the human $Q$-to-protein signal exists; it asks whether a measured half-life column absorbs a meaningful part of the already large modeled-translation complement.

\paragraph{Direct abundance context.}
The direct protein-omics survival run supplies the surrounding abundance scale.  In Homo sapiens, after the same control family, all nine coordinates survive against measured PAXdb protein abundance and the direct residual association is
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
Across the larger direct protein-omics panel, every listed organism has $9$ surviving coordinates.  The human control residual degrees of freedom in that direct run are $19030$, with $y$-residual energy $30769.5367150478$ and explained energy $1370.6490494847067$.  This direct association is an omics prediction surface only; it is not already a degradation, localization, folding, function, or translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Measured translation comparison.}
The measured ribosome-TE surface gives a separate human translation-readout scale.  For Homo sapiens, the measured TE join contains $11554$ genes with TE, a ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.6064136881331024$, and a controlled translation-readout survival value

\end{aligned}
$$
The measured TE mediation decomposition for the human three-way codon, TE, and protein-abundance join reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.04288464996165745,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.018837911344127926,\\

These numbers show why the turnover question must be kept narrow.  Human abundance has a real codon-residual component, and human measured TE has a real but small same-row readout association; however, the present turnover experiment is not a perturbation chain and does not establish the temporal route from codon residual to translation to degradation to abundance.

\paragraph{Turnover absorption calculation.}
The half-life readout is the median half-life across Mathieson et al. 2018 human primary-cell measurements, mapped by gene name through reviewed UniProt primary gene names to STRING $9606.\mathrm{ENSP}$ identifiers.  On the $8057$ joined rows, the rank of the turnover readout basis is $1$.  The descriptive turnover projection reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{Q,T_{\mathrm{turn}}} &= 0.004615408769189838,\\
\mathrm{absorbed\_by\_turnover} &= 0.0000035740764900116538,\\
\mathrm{basis\ check} &= 0.0000035740764900125626,\\
d_{\mathrm{combined}} &= 0.8901589776585174.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degrees-of-freedom matched null uses $200$ trials and rank value $1$.  Its mean is $0.0001262572958089998$ and its $95$th percentile is $0.0004342285066808464$.  The observed absorbed fraction does not exceed this null boundary:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.0000035740764900116538 &< 0.0004342285066808464.
\end{aligned}
$$
The local verdict is therefore negative for turnover absorption: the human half-life column was successfully attached and projected, but it does not carry an above-null share of the large modeled-translation complement.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite-row readback is a null H-candidate test rather than a promoted mechanism.  The positive part of the story is that the human abundance residual is large, the coordinate source is a full nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual family, and the half-life table joins enough rows to run the projection.  The negative part is decisive for this chapter: the measured half-life projection absorbs only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ of the target, far below the matched null $95$th percentile.  Protein turnover, as represented by this human primary-cell half-life column, is not the visible explanation of the large human modeled-translation complement.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This chapter permits only the following finite statement: on the reported human joined panel, the turnover projection is computable and below its matched null threshold.  It does not claim that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals realize translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causality, mechanism, or universality.  A separate layer-matched contact would be required for each such assertion.  In particular, direct $q_i\to P_k$ association remains a controlled protein-abundance association, not a degradation mechanism; measured $q_i\to T_j$ survival remains a translation-readout association, not a causal translation process; and the failed turnover absorption leaves the remaining human residual open to condition-specific, localization, complex-state, post-translational, or unmeasured biological readouts rather than licensing a stronger interpretation.
