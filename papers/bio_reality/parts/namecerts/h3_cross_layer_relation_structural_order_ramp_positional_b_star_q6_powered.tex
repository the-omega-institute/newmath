\subsection{Structural-order ramp readback for the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-structural-order-ramp-positional-b-star-q6-powered}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$ component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual surface carries a position-biased increment against a structural-order readout after the usual coding-sequence controls.  The finite surface is not a claim about folding.  Its rows are coding sequences with joined readouts, its active coordinate is the $f_3$ stress residual inside the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ basis, and its comparison is between early coding-window summaries and body-window summaries.  The run identifier was \texttt{fd29bb46dde6}; the experiment identifier was \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_ramp\_positional\_powered}; the design used $5$ folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per organismal contribution, and took $50$ codons as the primary ramp length.  The computed status was \texttt{passed}, but the local conclusion was the more restrictive class \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}.

\paragraph{Ramp and body increments.}
The row-wise held-out increment is reported as a weighted $\Delta R^2$ for the $f_3$ coordinate in ramp and body windows.  Across the three tested ramp lengths, the finite table was
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{ramp}}=30 &: \quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}}=0.0007864264335388174,\quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}}=0.00023737813475316197,\quad n_{\mathrm{org}}=2,\\
N_{\mathrm{ramp}}=50 &: \quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}}=0.0011165640103326878,\quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}}=-0.00026157313923957926,\quad n_{\mathrm{org}}=2,\\
N_{\mathrm{ramp}}=70 &: \quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}}=0.0007157308766185055,\quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}}=-0.0008384704154638841,\quad n_{\mathrm{org}}=2.
\end{aligned}
$$
The primary $50$-codon line is therefore positive in the ramp and negative in the body.  Its paired contrast is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp-body}}
  &=0.0011165640103326878-(-0.00026157313923957926)\\
  &=0.001378137149572267.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both primary organisms contributing to the positional test had positive ramp increments, no primary organism had a positive body increment, and both had ramp greater than body.  The finite sign-flip summaries were $p_{\mathrm{ramp}>0}=0.0478515625$, $p_{\mathrm{body}>0}=0.54296875$, and $p_{\mathrm{ramp-body}>0}=0.05859375$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The record supports a weak, position-sensitive readback: the ramp increment is positive and sign-flip significant at the recorded threshold, while the body increment is not positive.  It does not support the stronger localization statement, because the paired ramp-minus-body gate did not pass.  In the run vocabulary, \texttt{ramp\_signal} is true, \texttt{body\_signal} is false, \texttt{body\_dominant\_gate} is false, and \texttt{ramp\_localized\_gate} is false.  The correct chapter-level statement is therefore: the $f_3$ residual has a detectable early-window increment in this finite powered panel, but the evidence stops short of a certified ramp-localized structural-order effect.

\paragraph{Relation to measured translation and protein abundance.}
The same nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ basis appears in measured translation and protein-abundance contacts, so the structural-order readback must be kept downstream of those finite surfaces rather than promoted from codon geometry alone.  In measured ribosome-TE survival, all four measured organisms had nine surviving coordinates, with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.009394266754822262$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish.  The three-way measured mediation table reported measured fractions $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ for \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.0$ for zebrafish after clipping a negative raw fraction.  These numbers establish that the coordinate family can be read against measured TE and abundance surfaces, but they do not transform the structural-order ramp signal into a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Dwell and abundance context.}
A related yeast dwell-abundance computation gives a useful scale for the $f_3$ abundance component.  On $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$ joined yeast rows with $5$ folds, the $f_3$ dwell readout had $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$ over the base model, a permutation $95$th percentile of $0.0013115362189692937$, and an $f_3$--dwell correlation of $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell run classified the leading segment as \texttt{5prime}, with \texttt{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed} true and \texttt{three\_prime\_body\_passed} false.  This agrees in direction with the structural-order ramp table: the early coding region is the finite location where the $f_3$ signal appears first.  It is still a readout-level concordance, not a causal path from synonymous residuals to structure.

\paragraph{Protein-omics scale and dictionary reduction.}
The direct protein-omics surface supplies the abundance scale for the same residual family.  Protein-abundance join rates included $0.9156038419146592$ for yeast with $5815$ joined proteins, $0.9978649586335735$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 with $3739$ joined proteins, $0.9779294769799313$ for human with $19053$ joined proteins, and $1.0$ for zebrafish with $14410$ joined proteins.  In that direct surface, $R^2_{QP}$ was $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.04613529807085213$ in zebrafish, with all nine coordinates surviving at the positive threshold.  A separate yeast dictionary reduction used $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations; its selected readout dictionary covered $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual signal, leaving $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained.  Thus the ramp signal sits inside a broader partially reducible abundance-readout family, not inside a completed structural or mechanistic explanation.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is deliberately narrow.  The finite table permits the statement that, under this powered positional design, the $f_3$ member of $B^*_{Q6}$ has a positive early-window structural-order increment and lacks a certified body-dominant or ramp-localized gate.  It does not permit a claim that synonymous residual geometry causes translation, determines folded protein structure, establishes physical admissibility, realizes molecular function, explains phenotype, or states a universal biological law.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact: measured translation for translation, a structure or physical assay for folding and admissibility, a functional or perturbation assay for function, and a suitable phenotype panel for organism-level claims.  The present chapter records a finite positional readback and its refusal to cross those layers without additional measurements.
