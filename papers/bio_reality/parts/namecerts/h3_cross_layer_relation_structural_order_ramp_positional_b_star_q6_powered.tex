\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No translation mechanism, folding pathway, physical admissibility statement, molecular function, phenotype, causal route, or universal biological law follows from this chapter.  A positive ramp increment only says that the residual $f3_{stress}$ positional readout improves held-out prediction on the recorded finite panel under the stated controls.  Any claim about actual translation dynamics requires a separately joined translation contact; any claim about protein folding or structural order requires a separate structure-level contact; any claim about physical admissibility, biological function, or organism-level phenotype requires its own measured surface and its own controlled statistic.  Direct $Q\to P$ survival and ramp-positive $f3$ behavior are therefore kept as finite readbacks, not promoted to mechanism.
