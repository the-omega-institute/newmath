\paragraph{Verdict and local boundary.}
The finite-row verdict is: $f_3$-stress has a small, positive, held-out association with the ramp structural-order readout, but the paired ramp-versus-body localization criterion is not satisfied at the primary window.  The admissible sentence is therefore $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$, not $ramp\_localized$.  No translation realization, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological rule follows from this row alone.  Such claims require their own matched measurement surface and their own controlled statistic.  The present chapter records only the finite positional readback stated above: positive ramp increment, body-null behavior at the primary window, and failure of the stricter paired localization gate.
