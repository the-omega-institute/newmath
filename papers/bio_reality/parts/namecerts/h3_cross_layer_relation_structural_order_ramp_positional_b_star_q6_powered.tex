\subsection{Position-resolved ramp signal for the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ coordinate}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-structural-order-ramp-positional}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface carries a position-resolved signal concentrated near the coding-sequence ramp rather than across the downstream body.  The calculation uses the same residual-coordinate vocabulary as the matched abundance and translation-readout assays: the coordinates are residualized against length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, organism or organism-indexed background, and an $M$-density baseline before the positional increment is evaluated.  The experiment identifier is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_ramp\_positional\_powered}; the recorded run is \texttt{fd29bb46dde6}.  The run used five folds, a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, and a primary ramp length of $50$ codons.  Only two primary organisms entered the weighted primary-organism comparison, so the numerical outcome is a finite readout of this panel rather than a broad biological law.

\paragraph{Ramp and body increments.}
For each tested ramp length $n \in \{30,50,70\}$, the statistic compares the held-out $\Delta R^2$ assigned to the $f_3$ ramp window with the held-out $\Delta R^2$ assigned to the body window.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n=30 &: \quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}} = 0.0007864264335388174,\quad
          \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}} = 0.00023737813475316197,\\
 n=50 &: \quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}} = 0.0011165640103326878,\quad
          \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}} = -0.00026157313923957926,\\
 n=70 &: \quad \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}} = 0.0007157308766185055,\quad
          \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}} = -0.0008384704154638841.
\end{aligned}
$$
At the primary ramp length $n=50$, both primary organisms had positive ramp increments, no primary organism had a positive body increment, and both primary organisms had ramp greater than body.  The weighted ramp-minus-body increment was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp-body}} = 0.001378137149572267.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign-flip probability for the ramp being greater than zero was $0.0478515625$, while the corresponding body probability was $0.54296875$.  The paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability was $0.05859375$.  Thus the finite-row evidence supports a positive ramp-side contribution, but it narrowly misses the stricter paired localization gate.

\paragraph{Recorded classification.}
The run therefore classifies the module as \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}.  In ordinary statistical language, the $f_3$ ramp increment is positive and sign-flip significant under the run's ramp-positive criterion, whereas the stronger claim that the ramp is cleanly localized against the body window is not accepted.  The local verdict is not that the body carries a competing signal: the primary body increment at $n=50$ is negative, the body-positive organism count is $0$, and the body sign-flip probability is $0.54296875$.  The reason for withholding localization is more precise: the paired ramp-body contrast has positive weighted magnitude but does not pass the paired gate.

\paragraph{Relation to measured dwell evidence.}
The positional result is consistent with the separate yeast dwell-abundance calculation, where the active join contained $1257$ rows from a base pool of $1800$, used five folds, and tested a dwell readout from file-provided $5'$ and $3'$ A-site occupancy windows.  In that calculation the $f_3$ dwell correlation was $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$, the held-out $\Delta R^2$ for adding dwell to the base design was $0.01856473944057712$, and the permutation null had mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, and maximum $0.005001350039772926$ over $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  The positional variant of that dwell calculation also reported a leading $5'$ segment, with the $5'$ ramp gate passing and the $3'$ body gate not passing.  These companion finite rows justify treating the ramp direction as a measured positional tendency, while the present structural-order run still records its own stricter classification as non-localized.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality from the positive ramp-side statistic.  A positive positional $\Delta R^2$ says only that, in this finite joined panel and under the stated residualization, the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ coordinate improves held-out prediction more in the ramp window than in the body window.  A stronger biological statement would require a separate layer-matched measurement: direct translation readouts for translation claims, structure-level contacts for folded-order claims, physical assays for admissibility, functional assays for function, perturbation or rescue for mechanism, and broader independent panels for any universal assertion.  The present result is therefore a bounded positional readout with the local outcome \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}, not a promotion to higher biological layers.
