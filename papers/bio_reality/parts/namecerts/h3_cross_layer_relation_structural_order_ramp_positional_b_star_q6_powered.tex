\subsection{Ramp-Resolved Structural-Order Boundary for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-ramp-positional-structural-order}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$ stress component of the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface has a position-resolved excess against a structural-order readout when the coding sequence is split into an N-terminal ramp window and the remaining body.  The active experiment is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_ramp\_positional\_powered}, run under identifier \texttt{fd29bb46dde6}.  The finite design used five folds, required at least $300$ joined rows for an organism to enter the panel, and selected $50$ codons as the primary ramp length.  The module classification returned by the finite computation is \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp component is positive, but the stricter paired ramp-versus-body localization gate is not met.

\paragraph{Ranked readout coordinates.}
The upstream residual basis has nine coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f_3\_stress$.  The structural-order ramp test uses the $f_3\_stress$ projection rather than the full nine-dimensional vector as a mechanism claim.  It is a finite cross-validated association test attached to a positional split, not an inference about folding dynamics.  The readout is therefore best interpreted as a bounded positional signal for one residual coordinate inside the available organism panel.

\paragraph{Primary ramp statistic.}
For the primary $50$-codon ramp, both primary organisms have a positive ramp increment, while no primary organism has a positive body increment.  The weighted held-out increments are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp},50} &= 0.0011165640103326878,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body},50} &= -0.00026157313923957926,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp-body},50} &= 0.001378137149572267.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign-flip probability for a positive ramp increment is $0.0478515625$, while the corresponding body probability is $0.54296875$.  The paired ramp-minus-body probability is $0.05859375$.  Thus the finite panel supports a positive ramp-associated $f_3$ signal, but the paired evidence stops just short of the declared localization gate.  The verdict is not that structural order is localized to the ramp; it is that the ramp-side statistic is positive under the registered finite test and the body-side statistic is not.

\paragraph{Ramp-length sensitivity.}
The same computation was repeated at ramp lengths $30$, $50$, and $70$, each with $2$ primary organisms.  At $30$ codons the weighted ramp increment is $0.0007864264335388174$ and the weighted body increment is $0.00023737813475316197$.  At $50$ codons the weighted ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$ and the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$.  At $70$ codons the weighted ramp increment is $0.0007157308766185055$ and the weighted body increment is $-0.0008384704154638841$.  This sensitivity profile is coherent with a ramp-positive signal over the tested windows, but the evidence is not strong enough to promote the result to a localized structural-order law.

\paragraph{Dwell and dictionary constraints.}
The yeast dwell experiment gives a more direct positional companion for $f_3\_stress$.  On $1257$ joined rows, with five folds and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, the base model has $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out dwell increment is $0.01856473944057712$ against a permutation null $95$th percentile of $0.0013115362189692937$.  The $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The separate partial-mediation certificate uses $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $n_{join}=1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations; its selected readout dictionary covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the residual signal and leaves $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained, exceeding a selection-aware null $95$th percentile of $0.029149007242956115$.  These companion facts argue that the ramp result is not an isolated numerical artifact, while also showing that most of the residual signal remains outside the current measured readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row claim is ready in the following restricted form: the $f_3\_stress$ coordinate has a positive ramp-side held-out increment in the powered positional structural-order test, and the body-side counterpart is non-positive at the primary $50$-codon split.  The finite classification is \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}, not \texttt{ramp\_localized}.  Consequently the chapter records a positional excess boundary, not a realized structural mechanism.  No translation mechanism, protein folding statement, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, causal pathway, organismal phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological rule follows from this result unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and the same residual coordinate survives the declared controls on that layer.
