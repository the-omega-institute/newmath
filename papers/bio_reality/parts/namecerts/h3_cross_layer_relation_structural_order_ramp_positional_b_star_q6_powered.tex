\paragraph{Readback verdict.}
The admissible statement is narrow.  The finite rows support a positive $f_3$ ramp increment at the primary $50$-codon window and at the auxiliary $30$- and $70$-codon windows.  The same rows do not support the stronger claim that the signal is localized to the ramp under the paired gate, because $p_{\mathrm{ramp-body}>0}=0.05859375$ misses the gate even though the point estimate is positive.  Consequently the chapter records a ramp-positive structural-order boundary, not a structural-order realization.
