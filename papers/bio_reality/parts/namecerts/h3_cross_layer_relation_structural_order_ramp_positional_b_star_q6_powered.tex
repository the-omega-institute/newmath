\paragraph{Readback verdict.}
The finite verdict is \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}.  The ramp signal gate is positive, since the $f_3\_stress$ ramp increment is positive and passes the reported sign-flip threshold.  The body signal gate is negative.  However, the localization gate is not passed: the paired ramp-minus-body statistic is positive but does not clear the positional localization rule, and the recorded reason is that $f_3\_ramp$ is positive and sign-flip significant while the paired ramp-body localization gate did not pass.  This makes the chapter a narrow positive-ramp record, not a claim that the coordinate has been localized to a structural-order mechanism.
