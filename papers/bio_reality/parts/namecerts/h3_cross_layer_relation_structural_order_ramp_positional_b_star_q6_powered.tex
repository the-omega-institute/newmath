The experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_ramp\_positional\_powered} passed its computation gate with five folds, a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$, and a primary ramp size of $50$ codons. \paragraph{Why the positive ramp signal is not a localization certificate.}
The module classification is \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}. In other words, the ramp term is positive and individually sign-flip significant, but the finite-row comparison does not certify that the signal is sharply localized to the ramp rather than merely stronger there than in the body.  The body-dominant gate is false, the body signal is false, the ramp signal is true, and the registered reason is that $f_3$ ramp is positive while the paired ramp-body localization gate remains open.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence is a positive ramp-readout result with a failed localization certificate.  The supported statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp},50} &> 0,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body},50} &< 0,\\
p_{\mathrm{ramp}>0} &= 0.0478515625,\\
p_{\mathrm{ramp-body}>0} &= 0.05859375.
\end{aligned}
$$
This licenses the phrase \emph{ramp-positive $f_3$ positional signal} for the tested finite rows.  It does not license the stronger phrase \emph{ramp-localized structural order}.  It also does not establish translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, evolutionary direction, phenotype, or universality.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own row-level statistic; the present computation only records the bounded positional behavior of the $f_3$ abundance component under the declared controls.
