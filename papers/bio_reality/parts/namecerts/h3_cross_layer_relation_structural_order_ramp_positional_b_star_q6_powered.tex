\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record tests whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate named $f_3\_stress$ carries a position-sensitive abundance increment concentrated near the translation-entry ramp rather than across the coding body. The finite panel is not a folded-structure assay. It is a row-level predictive surface built from coding-sequence residual coordinates, positional ribosome-occupancy summaries, and protein-abundance residuals after the same control family used throughout the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ cross-layer runs: amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, the union-support $M$-density baseline, and matched row joins where the readout exists. The experiment id is \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_ramp\_positional\_powered}; the run id is \texttt{fd29bb46dde6}; the deterministic seed is \texttt{sha256:c25e054a5a2a69b53948ecbba7d463ecbb745a4ebcd778739f689cef8074954f}. It used 5 held-out folds, required at least 300 joined rows per primary organism, and used the 50-codon ramp window as the primary window.

\paragraph{Primary numerical result.}
At the primary ramp length $n=50$, two primary organisms entered the positional comparison. The weighted held-out increment for the $f_3\_stress$ ramp design was positive, while the body design was slightly negative:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp},50} &= 0.0011165640103326878,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body},50} &= -0.00026157313923957926,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp-body},50} &= 0.001378137149572267.
\end{aligned}
$$
The one-sided sign-flip probability for a positive ramp effect was $0.0478515625$, while the body probability was $0.54296875$. The paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability was $0.05859375$. Thus the finite readback classifies the run as \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp signal itself is positive at the stated sign-flip threshold, but the paired localization gate comparing ramp against body does not pass.

\paragraph{Translation and protein-abundance context.}
The coordinate is not introduced here as a free-standing structural observable. In measured ribosome-efficiency runs, all 9 $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survived against measured TE in four organisms, with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ in \textit{S. cerevisiae}, $0.009394266754822262$ in \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish. These values justify treating $f_3\_stress$ as an abundance-linked residual coordinate for finite readback, but they still do not transform the ramp statistic into a structural, mechanistic, or causal statement.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. The finite row surface supports a small positive held-out ramp increment for the $f_3\_stress$ component, and the 50-codon primary window gives a positive sign-flip readback. The stronger localization statement fails because the paired ramp-minus-body gate has probability $0.05859375$, not a passing value under the recorded gate. The module classification is therefore \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}. In biological language, the data are compatible with a weak ramp-side predictive contribution of the $f_3\_stress$ residual to the abundance-linked positional dwell surface, but they do not show that the effect is uniquely ramp-confined.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folding state, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal path, or universal biological law follows from this chapter. The chapter uses measured and modeled row contacts only to delimit a finite predictive comparison.
