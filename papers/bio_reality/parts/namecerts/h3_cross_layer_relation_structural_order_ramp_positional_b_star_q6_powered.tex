\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The computed module classification is \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}. In the primary window, both primary organisms had a positive ramp increment, no primary organism had a positive body increment, and both primary organisms had ramp greater than body. The local gates nevertheless separate the two conclusions: \texttt{ramp\_signal} is true, \texttt{body\_signal} is false, \texttt{body\_dominant\_gate} is false, and \texttt{ramp\_localized\_gate} is false. Thus the finite conclusion is not that the $f_3\_stress$ coordinate has been localized to the ramp as a structural mechanism. The supported statement is narrower: on this rank-powered panel, the $f_3\_stress$ readback has a small positive held-out ramp-side increment, while the stricter paired localization gate does not pass.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the measured surface.}
This subsection only records a finite statistical readback over codon-derived residual coordinates and matched downstream rows. It does not claim observed translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological rule. A positive ramp-side increment is not a ribosome-dynamics measurement unless a translation contact supplies the corresponding measured translation readout. It is not a folding or structural-order measurement unless a separate structure-level contact supplies that readout on the same relevant units. It is not a mechanism unless perturbation, mediation, or attenuation evidence is supplied at the appropriate layer. The finite status is therefore: $f_3\_stress$ has a small positive ramp readback under the declared rank-powered test, while ramp localization and higher-layer biological interpretation remain unclaimed.
