\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$ component inside the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface has a position-resolved increment against a structural-order readout.  The tested coordinate family is the fixed residual list $\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\mathrm{\_stress}\}$ after the usual sequence controls: coding length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The position split is a finite ramp/body comparison, with the primary ramp length set to $50$ codons and auxiliary windows at $30$ and $70$ codons.  The run used $5$ folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per organism, and retained $2$ primary organisms for the ramp/body statistic.  Its registered finite verdict is \emph{ramp positive but not localized}: the ramp increment is positive, but the paired ramp-minus-body gate does not cross the localization threshold.

\paragraph{Primary numerical readback.}
For the primary $50$-codon ramp, the weighted held-out increment for the $f_3$ ramp feature is $0.0011165640103326878$, whereas the matched body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$.  The paired ramp-minus-body increment is therefore $0.001378137149572267$.  The one-sided sign-flip readbacks are $p=0.0478515625$ for a positive ramp signal, $p=0.54296875$ for a positive body signal, and $p=0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body contrast.  Thus the row-level statistic supports a weak positive ramp component, rejects a body-dominant interpretation, and stops short of the stricter position-localization claim.  The finite Boolean diagnostics agree with this reading: \texttt{ramp\_signal=true}, \texttt{body\_signal=false}, \texttt{body\_dominant\_gate=false}, and \texttt{ramp\_localized\_gate=false}.  Both primary organisms have positive ramp increments and ramp greater than body, while zero primary organisms have a positive body increment.

\paragraph{Window sensitivity.}
The auxiliary windows show that the positive ramp sign is not confined to the single chosen length, although its magnitude is small.  At $30$ codons the weighted ramp increment is $0.0007864264335388174$ and the body increment is $0.00023737813475316197$.  At $50$ codons the corresponding values are $0.0011165640103326878$ and $-0.00026157313923957926$.  At $70$ codons they are $0.0007157308766185055$ and $-0.0008384704154638841$.  In compact form, the tested window table is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{30} &= 0.0007864264335388174, & B_{30} &= 0.00023737813475316197,\\
R_{50} &= 0.0011165640103326878, & B_{50} &= -0.00026157313923957926,\\
R_{70} &= 0.0007157308766185055, & B_{70} &= -0.0008384704154638841.
\end{aligned}
$$
This pattern is enough for the finite-row record to say that the ramp side carries the observed positive increment.  It is not enough to say that the biological effect has been localized to a ramp mechanism, because the paired contrast remains just above the declared sign-flip cutoff.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite conclusion is therefore narrow.  The $f_3$ residual has a positive held-out structural-order ramp increment at the primary $50$-codon window, and the same sign persists at the tested $30$- and $70$-codon windows.  The body side is nonpositive at the primary window, and the ramp is larger than the body in both primary organisms.  However, the paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability is $0.05859375$, so the row is classified as \emph{ramp positive but not localized}.  The admissible statement is a position-sensitive statistical association for the $f_3$ member of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ under the finite-row controls.  No translation mechanism, folding path, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal pathway, organismal phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from this row unless another layer-matched contact supplies and passes its own controlled statistic.
