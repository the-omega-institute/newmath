\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The chapter supports the following narrow statement: in the finite rank-powered surface tested here, the $f_3\_stress$ residual has a positive held-out association with the primary ramp structural-order readout, while the paired ramp-versus-body localization criterion is not met. No translation-rate claim follows from this chapter alone; translation requires its own measured TE or bounded translation readout. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from the ramp increment, from protein abundance, from modeled codon-adaptation weights, or from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate geometry. Those claims require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled finite-row statistics.
