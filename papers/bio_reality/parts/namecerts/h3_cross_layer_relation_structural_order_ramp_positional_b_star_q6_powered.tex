\paragraph{Finite surface.}

\paragraph{Ramp and body increments.}
The position split compares held-out incremental $R^2$ for an N-terminal ramp window against the corresponding body window.  For ramp length $30$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment is $0.0007864264335388174$ and the weighted $f_3$ body increment is $0.00023737813475316197$, with two primary organisms contributing.  For the primary ramp length $50$, the ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$ and the body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$.  For ramp length $70$, the ramp increment remains positive at $0.0007157308766185055$, while the body increment is $-0.0008384704154638841$.  Thus the observed finite-row pattern is not an all-position structural-order effect: the ramp coordinate is positive across the reported windows, while the body coordinate is weak or negative at the primary and longer windows.

\paragraph{Primary gate.}
At the primary $n=50$ window the finite-row classification is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{ramp}(f_3) &= 0.0011165640103326878,\\
\Delta R^2_{body}(f_3) &= -0.00026157313923957926,\\
\Delta R^2_{ramp-body}(f_3) &= 0.001378137149572267.
\end{aligned}
$$
The one-sided sign-flip value for the ramp being greater than zero is $0.0478515625$, while the corresponding body value is $0.54296875$.  The paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip value is $0.05859375$.  Both primary organisms have positive ramp increments and both have ramp increments larger than body increments; no primary organism has a positive body increment.  The local verdict is therefore \emph{ramp positive but not localized}: the ramp signal passes its positive-sign criterion, but the paired localization gate is not strict enough to certify a ramp-specific structural-order localization claim.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row verdict is ready and deliberately narrow.  The structural-order ramp experiment supports a positive $f_3$ ramp increment at the primary $50$-codon window, with a positive ramp sign-flip value below $0.05$ and a negative body increment.  It does not certify ramp localization because the paired ramp-minus-body gate remains at $0.05859375$.  The correct current statement is therefore: $B^*_{Q6}$ has a measurable $f_3$-linked N-terminal ramp signal in the tested finite rows, but the signal is not strong enough to promote to a localized structural-order claim.
