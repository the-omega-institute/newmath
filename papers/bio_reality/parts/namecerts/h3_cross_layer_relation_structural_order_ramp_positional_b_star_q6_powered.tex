\subsection{Ramp-localized structural-order readback for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ $f_3$ coordinate}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-structural-order-ramp-positional}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question fixed by the finite rows.}
The record tested here asks a narrow positional question about the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate named $f_3\_stress$. The coordinate is not treated as a structure measurement. It is first a codon-residual axis computed on coding sequences after the usual composition controls, and the experiment asks whether the protein-abundance component aligned with that axis is better predicted by ribosome-occupancy summaries from the early coding region than by corresponding body or tail summaries. Thus the tested object is a finite matrix of genes, residualized codon coordinates, positional ribosome-occupancy summaries, and protein-abundance residuals. The biological interpretation is deliberately weaker than a structural-order claim: a positive ramp increment can indicate a position-specific translation-associated readback, but it does not by itself establish folded order, folding kinetics, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Primary finite-row certificate.}
The structural-order positional run is identified by experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_structural\_order\_ramp\_positional\_powered} and run id \texttt{fd29bb46dde6}. It used $5$ folds, required at least $300$ joined rows per admissible organism, and set the primary ramp window to the first $50$ codons. The signed readback was evaluated on three ramp cutoffs. At ramp length $30$, the weighted held-out increments were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp},30} &= 0.0007864264335388174,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body},30} &= 0.00023737813475316197,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $2$ primary organisms. At ramp length $50$, the finite row selected as primary, the weighted held-out increments were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp},50} &= 0.0011165640103326878,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body},50} &= -0.00026157313923957926,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}-\mathrm{body},50} &= 0.001378137149572267.
\end{aligned}
$$
At ramp length $70$, the weighted held-out increments were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp},70} &= 0.0007157308766185055,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body},70} &= -0.0008384704154638841.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign pattern is therefore stable in the limited sense that the ramp increment is positive at all three tested window lengths, whereas the body increment is negative at the two larger windows.

\paragraph{Decision gates.}
The primary $50$-codon decision separated three statements. First, there was a positive ramp signal: $2$ primary organisms had positive ramp increments, and the weighted ramp increment was positive. The sign-flip probability for the ramp being greater than zero was $0.0478515625$, so the ramp-positive gate passed at the finite-row level used by this run. Second, there was no corresponding body signal: the positive body primary-organism count was $0$, the weighted body increment was $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the sign-flip probability for body greater than zero was $0.54296875$. Third, the stricter localization comparison did not pass. Although the ramp exceeded the body in $2$ primary organisms and the weighted ramp-minus-body increment was $0.001378137149572267$, the paired sign-flip probability for ramp exceeding body was $0.05859375$. The recorded module verdict is therefore \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the run supports a ramp-positive positional readback but does not support a stronger claim that the signal is localized to the ramp rather than the body under the paired gate.

\paragraph{Relation to the dwell and abundance rows.}
The positional result sits on top of a separate finite protein-abundance component for $f_3\_stress$. In the dwell-mediation run \texttt{946f33083caf}, the active yeast join had $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$ rows from a base availability of $1800$, used $5$ folds, and projected the target protein-abundance residual onto the $f_3\_stress$ direction with $\lVert P_{f3}\rVert^2=44.20095750389323$. The base model had held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the full dwell model had held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
Against $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, the null mean was $-0.0032911642620130563$, the null $95\%$ value was $0.0013115362189692937$, and the null maximum was $0.005001350039772926$. This establishes that the positional experiment is not testing an isolated visual pattern; it is refining a previously positive dwell-associated abundance component. The companion positional dwell run \texttt{6db78e1bf746} used the same $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, $5$ folds, and $\lVert P_{f3}\rVert^2=44.20095750389323$, and classified the leading segment by held-out increment as \texttt{5prime}. Its finite flags were \texttt{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed=true} and \texttt{three\_prime\_body\_passed=false}. The structural-order record is consistent with that direction, but it is stricter because it requires a paired ramp-versus-body localization gate.

\paragraph{Translation and protein-abundance context.}
The same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate family has measured translation-efficiency survival rows in four organisms. The measured ribosome-TE run reports $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $0.009394266754822262$ in \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish, with all $9$ residual coordinates surviving in each organism under the stated positive-threshold convention. The measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance run then reports measured mediation fractions of $0.4793069656345167$ in yeast, $0.04091158340950673$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ in human, and $0.0$ in zebrafish after truncating the suppressive raw value $-0.04801486048721136$ to the unit interval. These numbers justify using the structural-order positional test as a downstream readback of an already measurable translation/protein-abundance interface. They do not turn the positional ramp result into a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Local admissible conclusion.}
The finite-row conclusion is this: within the tested rank-powered panel, the $f_3\_stress$ abundance component has a positive early-CDS positional readback, with primary-window weighted ramp increment $0.0011165640103326878$ and ramp sign-flip probability $0.0478515625$. The body comparison is negative at the primary window, and the paired localization gate remains just outside the declared pass condition, with ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability $0.05859375$. The correct classification is therefore not structural localization and not folded-order realization. It is a bounded positional signal: ramp positive, body not positive, paired ramp-body localization not certified.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This chapter records a measured association among residual codon coordinates, position-resolved ribosome-occupancy summaries, and a protein-abundance component. It does not claim that synonymous residuals determine translation universally, that the ramp signal is a folding mechanism, that folded structural order has been measured, that a physical-admissibility assay has been passed, that molecular function or phenotype follows, or that the result is causal. Such conclusions require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. The present certificate stops at the finite-row verdict \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}.
