\subsection{Position-Resolved Translation Boundary for the $f_3$-Stress Coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns a bounded cross-layer test for the $f_3$-stress synonymous residual coordinate in coding-sequence windows.  The admissible carrier is a finite table whose rows are local CDS windows.  A row must contain the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid identity, local synonymous codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a matched translation readout at the same transcript or CDS coordinate scale, an mRNA abundance control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates.  The intended statistic is not a codon-geometry score alone.  It is a controlled survival entry of the form $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ denotes a same-window ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, position-bin, GC3, CDS length, methionine density, matched mRNA abundance, codon-count controls, coverage and mappability limits, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablations.

\paragraph{Observed finite facts.}
The relevant translation-initiation surface currently contains a high-throughput finite table rather than the full $f_3$ same-window survival panel.  The Sample2019 translation-initiation run identified by \texttt{ed2755f0e232} reports $n=80000$ rows and runtime $172.864$ seconds, with \texttt{cross\_sample} recorded as null.  This is a real finite row count for a translation-associated assay surface, but it does not by itself certify a same-CDS, same-window join between $f_3$-stress residuals, wobble weights, matched mRNA controls, and position-resolved ribosome footprints.  A separate cross-layer structural-order run, \texttt{1b97387835f7}, is useful only as a calibration example for what a controlled positive call looks like: it records $5$ folds, joined structural-order counts $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, weighted full-control $\Delta R^2=0.004764742175882462$, aggregate full-control mean $\Delta R^2=0.005284160876443678$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  Those numbers are not imported as evidence for the present $f_3$ ribosome-window claim; they only show the finite-row standard expected before a positive cross-layer interpretation is allowed.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
The local test is the following finite comparison.  For each eligible CDS window $w$, assemble a row
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_w=(q_{f_3,w}, C_w, W_w, T_w, M_w, Z_w),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_{f_3,w}$ is the amino-acid-quotiented $f_3$ residual coordinate, $C_w$ is the local synonymous codon-count vector, $W_w$ is the bounded wobble-weight surface, $T_w$ is the same-window translation readout, $M_w$ is the matched mRNA control, and $Z_w$ contains the remaining controls.  The supported claim requires a held-out improvement after all controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The inequality is meaningful only when the wobble-weight asymmetry is measured or explicitly bounded in the row set, so that $w_c\neq w_c^{f_3}$ is not merely assumed from the coordinate definition.  It is also rank-sensitive: a saturated or tiny panel cannot be treated as a powered survival call, even if a fitted score is numerically nonzero.

\paragraph{Current interpretation.}
The present finite evidence supports a boundary statement, not a positive biological conclusion.  The $80000$-row translation-initiation table is large enough to make a future finite-row join plausible, but the recorded field \texttt{cross\_sample = null} and the contact notes leave the required same-organism, same-CDS, same-window ribosome-footprint join absent.  Consequently, the $f_3$-stress coordinate remains a proposed cross-layer predictor whose translation-readout survival has not been computed on the required position-resolved matrix.  The carrier, relation, and rank requirements are precise: a valid row set must join ordered CDS coordinates, codon counts, wobble-weight values, matched mRNA abundance, and codon-indexed ribosome occupancy before estimating $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the $f_3$ coordinate, from codon-window geometry, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, from a contact name, or from review readiness.  No protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this record.  Such statements require their own matched readout rows and their own controlled finite statistic.  In this chapter the only admissible conclusion is narrower: the $f_3$-stress packet has a well-specified position-resolved translation test, the required row schema is finite, and the present memory has not yet supplied the joined matrix needed to promote the packet beyond a bounded cross-layer boundary.
