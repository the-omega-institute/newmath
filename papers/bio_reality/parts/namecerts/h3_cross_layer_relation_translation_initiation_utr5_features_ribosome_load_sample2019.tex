\subsection{Position-resolved translation boundary for the $f_3$-stress residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats the $f_3$-stress coordinate as a proposed cross-layer coordinate only on a finite table whose rows already carry all quantities needed for a local translation-readout test. A row is not just a codon window. It must contain an ordered coding-sequence window, the $f_3$-stress residual after the amino-acid quotient, local synonymous codon counts, a context-dependent wobble-weight surface, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA abundance control from the same condition, and amino-acid or position-bin controls. In the available verified translation-initiation audit for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_initiation.utr5\_features\_ribosome\_load\_sample2019}, the finite panel has $n=80000$ rows, runtime $172.864$ seconds, and no recorded cross-sample validation entry, since the stored scalar is \texttt{cross\_sample = null}. This supplies a large translation-readout context, but it does not by itself certify the $f_3$-stress same-window statistic required here.

\paragraph{Controlled survival statistic.}
The admissible statistic is a per-window survival entry for the residual coordinate after the relevant controls have been attached. The intended object is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
  &= \Delta_{\mathrm{held\text{-}out}}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{position}}; f_3 \mid Z\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\mathrm{AA\ composition},\ \mathrm{position\ bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\ \mathrm{codon\ counts},\ \mathrm{simpler\ baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry is meaningful only if the held-out improvement remains positive after the ablation of amino-acid composition, position, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, mRNA abundance, local codon counts, and simpler codon-usage summaries. The rank condition is also part of the claim: an all-saturated or underpowered fit cannot be promoted to a biological readout. Context-specific wobble asymmetry, written locally as $w_c \ne w_c^{f_3}$, is necessary but not sufficient. It is a condition on the readout surface, not a substitute for measured or explicitly bounded translation rows.

\paragraph{What the available audits do and do not establish.}
The $80000$-row translation-initiation audit is relevant because it shows that a large finite translation-layer panel has been processed for a related ribosome-load setting. It does not record the same-organism, same-CDS, same-window join between $f_3$-stress residuals, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint occupancy. A separate linked audit, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_conditioned\_structural\_order\_boundary.b\_star\_q6\_powered}, reports a passed translation-conditioned structural-order boundary with five folds, minimum joined row threshold $300$, organism-level joined counts $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, two positive full-control organism calls, weighted full-control $\Delta R^2 = 0.004764742175882462$, aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ between $0.002688284523492457$ and $0.007880037229394898$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$. Those numbers show that some $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-conditioned audits can be powered on finite joined panels. They still do not identify the present $f_3$-stress, same-window, position-resolved translation statistic. A third translation-layer audit, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020}, reports a G418-induced readthrough subset with $4982$ nonzero rows from $7658$ joined rows before filtering and nonzero fraction $0.65056$. This confirms that translation readouts can be finite-row and condition-specific, while also emphasizing that each contact must be evaluated on its own joined matrix.

\paragraph{Local admissibility rule.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded conjectural interface rather than a positive biological law. The $f_3$-stress residual may enter a translation-readout claim only after the row space contains the coordinate, codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA control, and position-resolved ribosome occupancy for the same organism, CDS, and window. The break condition is local and strict: if review status, a contact name, modeled tAI or stAI weights, wobble asymmetry, $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ notation, or codon-window geometry is treated as the computed same-window statistic, the claim fails. The currently stored evidence supports a request for that statistic; it does not yet support the statistic itself.

\paragraph{Exclusion boundary.}
No translation realization, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, system phenotype, adaptation claim, causation claim, or universal biological rule follows from this record alone. Modeled tAI or stAI weights remain bounded codon-adaptation annotations unless joined to measured or explicitly delimited translation rows. Matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate, not a ribosome-occupancy measurement. Position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact wording names the needed readout surface, but it is not the same as a joined footprint matrix. Downstream biological interpretations require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.
