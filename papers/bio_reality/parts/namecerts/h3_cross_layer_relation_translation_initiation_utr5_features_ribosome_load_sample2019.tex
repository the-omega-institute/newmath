\subsection{Position-resolved $f_3$-stress survival against ribosome-load readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns a bounded cross-layer test for the $f_3$-stress member of $B^*_{Q6}$ on coding-sequence windows.  The intended table is not a free codon-geometry object: each row must carry an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after the amino-acid quotient, local synonymous-codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates.  The verified finite contact presently attached to the translation-initiation surface is the Sample-style ribosome-load run
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_initiation.utr5\_features\_ribosome\_load\_sample2019$, with $n=80000$ rows, runtime $172.864$ seconds, and cross-sample status recorded as null.  Thus the available row count is large enough to name a substantial ribosome-load surface, but the audit does not yet record the joined same-CDS, same-window matrix needed for the $f_3$-stress statistic itself.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
The admissible statistic is a controlled survival entry for a position-indexed translation readout.  In schematic form, the finite table should support an estimator of
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)
  &= \Delta_{\mathrm{held\text{-}out}}\bigl(
       \widehat{T}_{\mathrm{position}};
       Z \cup \{f_3\text{-stress},\,w_c\},
       Z \cup \{\mathrm{simpler\ baselines}\}
     \bigr), \\
Z
  &= \{\mathrm{AA\ composition},\ \mathrm{position\ bin},\ \mathrm{GC3},\
       \mathrm{CDS\ length},\ \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\
       \mathrm{codon\ counts},\ \mathrm{coverage},\ \mathrm{mappability}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive entry requires held-out improvement after these controls and after ablation against simpler codon-usage baselines.  The wobble-weight condition is also part of the test, not an interpretation supplied after the fact: the table must show an observed or explicitly bounded asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$ in the same window family where the translation readout is evaluated.

\paragraph{What the current finite evidence says.}
The present translation-initiation fact supplies a concrete audit row count, $n=80000$, and a recorded runtime, $172.864$ seconds, for the UTR5-feature ribosome-load surface.  It does not record a non-null cross-sample split, and it does not by itself state that $f_3$-stress, wobble weights, codon-window counts, mRNA controls, and position-resolved ribosome occupancy have been joined into one per-window design matrix.  For comparison inside the same translation-layer audit ledger, the stop-context readthrough record
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_termination.stop\_context\_readthrough\_g418\_wangen2020$ has a different finite surface: $7658$ rows before the nonzero filter, $4982$ retained nonzero rows, a nonzero fraction $0.65056$, runtime $303.534$ seconds, and endpoint recorded as G418-induced RRTS on the nonzero subset.  That second row confirms that separate translation readouts can carry concrete finite counts, but it is not a substitute for the position-resolved $f_3$-stress window matrix required here.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The chapter therefore treats the $f_3$-stress object as a proposed cross-layer coordinate with a precise survival test, not as an already observed biological law.  Codon-window geometry, modeled tAI or stAI weights, review routing, or notation such as $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ do not constitute measured ribosome occupancy.  They name the quantities that must be joined and tested.  Until the joined rows exist, no translation-realization claim follows from this record, and no inference is licensed about protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality.

\paragraph{Acceptance rule.}
The finite-row acceptance condition is narrow.  A positive result would require same-organism and same-CDS rows, fixed local windows or codon-indexed positions, local synonymous-codon counts, bounded wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, position-resolved ribosome-footprint or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout, rank sufficiency, and held-out improvement after the full control set.  Failure of this condition would not show that $Q6$ residual geometry is biologically irrelevant; it would only say that this particular $f_3$-stress coordinate has not survived the stated position-resolved translation-readout test.  Conversely, even a positive $S_{f_3}$ entry would remain a bounded translation-layer result.  Any downstream claim about protein output, folded structure, physical feasibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, or a general biological rule would require a separate finite contact at that downstream layer.
