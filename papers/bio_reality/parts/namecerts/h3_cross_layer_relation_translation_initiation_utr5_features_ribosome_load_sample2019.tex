\subsection{Position-resolved translation survival for the $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
The record considered here is the claim
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_initiation.utr5\_features\_ribosome\_load\_sample2019$, read as a bounded test of whether the $f_3$-stress member of $B^*_{Q6}$ can survive as a cross-layer coordinate only after a same-row translation readout has been attached.  The finite carrier is not a bare codon table.  Each admissible row must contain an ordered coding-sequence window, an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid identity, local synonymous codon counts, a wobble-weight context in which $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$ is observed or explicitly bounded, a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout on the same window, matched mRNA abundance, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  In this form the biological question is local: the statistic is a controlled survival entry $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, position bin, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, mRNA abundance, codon-count controls, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablations.

Therefore this row set is usable as a finite surface for a routing-level translation-readout audit, but it is not yet a cross-sample survival certificate for $f_3$-stress.  The null cross-sample field matters: it prevents the chapter from treating the present table as a held-out replication of a same-window ribosome-occupancy statistic.

Those numbers show that a different table has a positive controlled structural-order readback, not that the present $f_3$-stress position-window statistic has been computed.  The distinction is essential because structural order, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, and universality require their own matched contacts.

It is useful here only as a scale comparison for finite empirical panels: it is not a translation-readout contact and cannot replace ribosome-occupancy rows.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic.}
The proposed local statistic is a held-out controlled gain for the $f_3$ coordinate after the controls are already in the row.  In the notation of this chapter, an admissible positive call has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
  &= \mathrm{score}(T_{\mathrm{position}}; Z, f_3, W_{\mathrm{wobble}})
     - \mathrm{score}(T_{\mathrm{position}}; Z, W_{\mathrm{baseline}}) \\
  &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ is a position-resolved occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout, $W_{\mathrm{wobble}}$ is the attached wobble-weight surface, and $W_{\mathrm{baseline}}$ ranges over simpler codon, amino-acid, position, GC3, length, methionine-density, and mRNA baselines.  The statistic is not licensed by the presence of codon counts alone.  It is also not licensed by modeled tAI or stAI weights alone, because those weights are adaptation annotations rather than observed ribosome movement.

\paragraph{Current boundary.}
The present finite evidence is therefore a ready chapter-level boundary statement, not a positive biological survival result.  The Sample 2019 table supplies $80000$ finite rows and a concrete runtime, but the stored cross-sample field is null and the memory supplied here does not contain same-organism, same-CDS, same-window joined codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA rows, and position-indexed ribosome-footprint arrays for the exact $f_3$-stress statistic.  Until those rows exist and the controlled statistic above is positive under held-out evaluation, the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate remains a proposed coordinate for a translation-readout test rather than evidence of translation realization.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
This chapter makes no claim that $f_3$-stress determines translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  A positive statement at any one of those layers would require a separate matched contact and its own controlled row statistic.  In particular, a workflow-ready event, a contact name, a modeled wobble-weight surface, or symbolic notation for $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ does not create observed ribosome occupancy.  The only claim retained here is narrower: the finite Sample 2019 surface is large enough to motivate a same-row test, while the present data remain blocked from biological promotion until the joined position-window matrix and its held-out controlled gain are actually supplied.
