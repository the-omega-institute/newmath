\subsection{Position-resolved translation survival for the $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row under audit.}
The record considered here is the finite table claim
$\mathtt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_initiation.utr5\_features\_ribosome\_load\_sample2019}$.
Its verified run identifier is $\mathtt{ed2755f0e232}$, with $n=80000$ finite rows and runtime $172.864$ seconds.  The recorded cross-sample field is null.  In this chapter this null field is not treated as a negative biological result; it is treated as a missing cross-sample join certificate.  The table is therefore admissible as a large finite row surface for testing a translation-readout boundary, but not yet as a completed survival witness for the $f_3$-stress coordinate.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
The biological object is a coding-sequence window table in which each row is intended to carry an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid composition, local synonymous codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The mathematical question is not whether codon geometry itself is translation.  The question is whether the residual coordinate keeps predictive rank after the local controls have removed amino-acid identity, position, GC3, length, methionine density, mRNA abundance, and simpler codon baselines.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
For a finite window table $T$, let $q_{f_3}(r)$ denote the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate of row $r$, let $T_{\mathrm{pos}}(r)$ denote the position-resolved translation readout, and let $Z(r)$ collect the controls.  The intended survival entry is the held-out improvement
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
  &= \operatorname{score}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}; Z, q_{f_3})
     - \operatorname{score}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}; Z),\\
Z
  &= \{\mathrm{AA},\ \mathrm{position},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ \mathrm{M\ density},\ \mathrm{mRNA},\ \mathrm{codon\ baselines}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive chapter-level claim would require $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)>0$ on held-out rows, with enough rank to rule out a saturated or identifier-driven fit.  The present finite record does not yet contain the same-CDS, same-window joined matrix needed to compute that statistic.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry condition.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate is only biologically addressable when the wobble weights are rowwise data rather than assumed symmetry.  In notation, the test requires an observed or explicitly bounded surface on which
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}
\end{aligned}
$$
for the relevant codon context.  A modeled tAI or stAI row can serve as a bounded codon-adaptation annotation, but it is not an observed ribosome-footprint row.  Consequently, wobble asymmetry can help define the tested coordinate, yet it does not by itself establish translation realization.

\paragraph{What the finite rows currently certify.}
The $80000$-row translation-initiation run certifies that a large row panel has been processed for the UTR5 ribosome-load surface.  It does not certify a same-window $f_3$-stress survival statistic, because the cross-sample field is null and the memory record explicitly lacks the joined position-window matrix with codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint occupancy.  A neighboring finite run, $\mathtt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_conditioned\_structural\_order\_boundary.b\_star\_q6\_powered}$ with run identifier $\mathtt{1b97387835f7}$, records status passed, fold count $5$, joined structural-order row counts $3412$ for $\mathtt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}$ and $5119$ for $\mathtt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$, and weighted full-control $\Delta R^2=0.004764742175882462$ with sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  That linked result shows that some translation-conditioned residual tests can be powered in other finite panels, but it is not a substitute for the missing position-resolved $f_3$-stress window statistic here.  A second neighboring run, $\mathtt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.order\_memory\_execution\_signal.b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}$ with run identifier $\mathtt{26912bc3e41e}$, records status passed for its own finite-state dwell transducer experiment.  It likewise remains a separate finite claim and does not promote this chapter's translation-readout boundary.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The valid contact surface is narrower than the biological vocabulary might suggest.  Position-resolved ribosome occupancy means codon-indexed or fixed-window footprint occupancy joined to ordered CDS coordinates in the same organism, transcript or CDS identifier, codon position, and ribosome-profiling sample.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control for translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation readout by itself.  Per-organism codon usage and tAI or stAI weights are sequence or model annotations; they are not measurements of local ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
This chapter therefore records a boundary-ready but not survival-positive state.  The break condition is triggered if review status, contact names, modeled wobble weights, $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ notation, or codon-window geometry are reported as though they were a computed same-window ribosome-occupancy statistic.  The support condition is stricter: attach same-organism, same-CDS, same-window ordered coding-sequence rows, local synonymous codon counts, bounded wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA abundance, and codon-indexed ribosome-footprint occupancy; then compute the held-out controlled survival score after amino-acid-bin or position controls, GC3, length, methionine-density baseline, codon-count controls, coverage and mappability limits, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from the $f_3$-stress coordinate in this record.  Those conclusions require their own layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  The present chapter only licenses the more modest statement that an $80000$-row translation-initiation surface exists, that the specific cross-sample join needed for the $f_3$-stress survival claim is absent, and that a future positive claim must pass the stated same-window readout test rather than derive from coordinate geometry alone.
