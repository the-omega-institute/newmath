\subsection{Position-Resolved Translation Initiation as a Boundary for $f_3$-Stress}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-initiation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite table.}
The BioReality record considered here is the claim $\mathrm{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_initiation.utr5\_features\_ribosome\_load\_sample2019}$.  Its verified finite run reports $n=80000$ analyzed rows with runtime $172.864$ seconds and a null cross-sample field, $\mathrm{cross\_sample}=\mathrm{null}$.  The row type requested by the conjecture is more restrictive than a generic translation-initiation table: each admissible row must carry a coding-sequence window, an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a same-window ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The present verified run establishes that a finite translation-initiation surface of size $80000$ was processed, but the recorded scalar facts do not certify that those rows already contain the full same-CDS, same-window, wobble-weight, mRNA-controlled $f_3$ survival matrix.

\paragraph{Coordinate under test.}
The proposed coordinate is not codon usage in general.  It is the $f_3$ member of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family, interpreted only after amino-acid composition has been quotiented away.  The intended statistic is a controlled survival entry, written locally as $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ is the position-resolved translation readout and $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, position or amino-acid bin, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, local codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability limits, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The survival criterion is positive held-out improvement after these controls, not a descriptive association in the raw table.

\paragraph{Observed finite facts.}
For this specific translation-initiation row set, the recorded finite scalar facts are $n=80000$, runtime $172.864$ seconds, and no recorded cross-sample validation field.  The absence of a cross-sample value matters: it prevents the chapter from treating this table as a cross-context survival certificate.  The wider audit also contains a separate translation-conditioned structural-order result with status $\mathrm{passed}$, five folds, joined structural-order counts $3412$ for $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}$ and $5119$ for $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, weighted full-control $\Delta R^2=0.004764742175882462$, aggregate full-control mean $\Delta R^2=0.005284160876443678$, and full-control sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  That independent result shows that a different Q6-derived translation-conditioned test can cross a measured downstream boundary under controls, but it is not evidence that the present $f_3$ position-resolved initiation packet has crossed its own boundary.  A separate translation-termination contact reports $4982$ nonzero G418-induced RRTS rows after an inner join from $7658$ prefilter rows, with nonzero fraction $0.65056$ and runtime $303.534$ seconds; this demonstrates that other translation readouts in the audit can be finite and condition-specific, but it does not supply the position-resolved initiation rows required here.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
The admissible readout must be a same-row comparison between the $f_3$ residual coordinate and a translation-layer measurement.  A valid finite entry has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{position/bin},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},\mathrm{M\ density},\mathrm{codon\ counts},\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{coverage},\mathrm{mappability}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The inequality is meaningful only if the position-resolved occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout is joined to the same organism, same CDS, and same local window as the residual coordinate.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights can supply a bounded wobble-weight surface, but they cannot be substituted for observed ribosome-footprint occupancy.  Likewise, matched mRNA abundance is a control denominator or covariate, not the translation readout itself.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The present finite record is therefore best read as a prepared boundary record rather than a positive biological law.  It identifies a concrete $80000$-row translation-initiation surface and a precise residual coordinate to be tested, while keeping the missing same-window statistic explicit.  The proposed biological statement is narrow: $f_3$-stress may count as a bounded cross-layer coordinate only where wobble weights break the $f_3$ symmetry and the same windows show positive controlled improvement in position-resolved translation readout after all stated controls and ablations.  Without that positive survival entry, the table supports neither translation realization nor a mechanistic claim.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from the $f_3$ residual coordinate, from codon/window geometry, from review readiness, or from modeled wobble weights alone.  Any such stronger assertion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  In particular, the $80000$-row translation-initiation run may be cited as finite processing evidence for the boundary surface, but not as a completed $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0$ certificate unless the joined position-resolved readout matrix, rank checks, and held-out improvement are separately recorded.
