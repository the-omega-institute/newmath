\subsection{Position-Resolved Translation Survival for the $f_3$-Stress Coordinate}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-position-resolved-translation-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row under review.}
The record considered here is the cross-layer translation-initiation packet keyed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_initiation.utr5\_features\_ribosome\_load\_sample2019}. Its finite experimental row reports $n=80000$ assayed entries and a runtime of $172.864$ seconds, with \texttt{cross\_sample} recorded as null. The null cross-sample field is material: the row is a large ribosome-load or translation-initiation surface, but it is not yet a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix joining the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, wobble-weight context, matched mRNA abundance, position-resolved ribosome occupancy, and the full control set needed for a positive $f_3$ survival statement.

\paragraph{Coordinate to be tested.}
The biological question is whether an amino-acid-quotiented synonymous residual coordinate, denoted here by $q_{f_3}$, retains predictive content for a matched position-resolved translation readout once the amino-acid and readout controls have already absorbed ordinary composition effects. A valid row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_r = (&q_{f_3,r}, C_r, W_r, O_r, M_r, Z_r),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $C_r$ is the local synonymous codon-count vector, $W_r$ is the bounded wobble-weight context, $O_r$ is the codon-indexed or fixed-window ribosome-occupancy readout, $M_r$ is the matched mRNA control, and $Z_r$ contains amino-acid or position-bin controls together with GC3, length, M-density, coverage, mappability, and simpler codon-baseline terms. The proposed statistic is not a statement about codon geometry alone. It is a finite-row survival statistic, and it is admissible only after all fields in $T_r$ are present on the same ordered coding-sequence window.

\paragraph{Local survival statistic.}
For the present chapter the intended survival entry is the held-out improvement of the $f_3$ coordinate against a position-resolved translation readout after the declared controls. In notation,
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
&= \Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(\mathcal M(Z,q_{f_3}) ; O\bigr)
   - \Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(\mathcal M(Z) ; O\bigr),\\
\mathrm{pass}
&\Longleftrightarrow S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0
\quad\text{and the gain survives simpler-baseline ablation.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The asymmetry condition $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$ is also local to the finite panel. It cannot be assumed from the name of the coordinate or from a modeled tAI or stAI table alone; it must be represented by bounded wobble-weight rows aligned to the same windows as $O_r$ and $M_r$.

\paragraph{What the verified row establishes.}
The $80000$-row Sample2019 run is therefore useful as a scale and routing certificate for a translation-initiation readout, but it is not by itself a positive $f_3$ translation-survival result. The recorded value \texttt{cross\_sample = null} prevents interpreting the run as a cross-sample replication. More importantly, the finite data supplied here do not report a row count for same-CDS joins between $q_{f_3}$, synonymous codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA, and position-resolved ribosome footprints. Nor do they report a held-out $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ value, a $p$-value, a rank-powered survival status, or an ablation table for amino-acid composition, position bins, GC3, length, M-density, mRNA, and simpler codon baselines.

\paragraph{Relation to the powered structural-order record.}
A nearby verified cross-layer record shows that the broader $B^*_{Q6}$ program can be evaluated on finite joined biological panels: the translation-conditioned structural-order run used $5$ folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, and reported joined structural-order counts of $3412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. Its aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ was positive in both organisms, with mean $0.005284160876443678$, median $0.005284160876443678$, minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$ for greater-than-zero full-control improvement. That record is not promoted into the present claim. It demonstrates the kind of finite controlled surface that can carry a cross-layer assertion, while this $f_3$ position-resolved translation packet still lacks the corresponding same-window occupancy matrix.

\paragraph{Boundary of admissible interpretation.}
The present chapter therefore records a bounded request for a specific finite statistic, not a completed biological law. The data may motivate a test in which $q_{f_3}$ is joined to position-resolved ribosome occupancy and matched mRNA abundance on the same CDS windows. They do not establish translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Modeled tAI or stAI weights may enter as bounded covariates or wobble-weight surfaces, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy. A UTR5 or ribosome-load row may enter as a translation-initiation readout, but without the same-window coding-sequence join and the declared control ablations it remains outside the $f_3$ survival statistic.

\paragraph{Finite decision.}
The local decision is consequently null at the survival layer and open at the data-assembly layer. The chapter accepts the claim only in the following restricted form: a position-resolved $f_3$-stress translation-survival test is well posed when each row carries $q_{f_3}$, local codon counts, wobble-weight asymmetry, matched position-resolved ribosome occupancy or explicitly bounded translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, and the full control vector $Z$. With the finite facts currently supplied, the Sample2019 run contributes $n=80000$ translation-initiation rows and a runtime audit trail, but no positive $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ entry is claimed.
