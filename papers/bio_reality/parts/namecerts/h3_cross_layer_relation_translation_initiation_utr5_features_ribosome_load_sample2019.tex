\subsection{Position-Resolved $f_3$ Stress at the Translation-Initiation Boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-initiation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record concerns the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside coding-sequence windows, after the amino-acid quotient has already removed the direct amino-acid-composition explanation.  The proposed biological contact is not a codon-table summary by itself.  It is a finite row table in which each row must carry an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, synonymous codon counts in the same local coding window, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA abundance control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The intended statistic is a controlled survival entry, written locally as $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or position bins, GC3, coding-sequence length, an $M$-density baseline, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability filters, and a simpler-baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Finite rows actually present.}
The available translation-initiation run for this surface is keyed by the identifier \texttt{ed2755f0e232}.  It reports $n=80000$ rows and runtime $172.864$ seconds, but the cross-sample field is null.  Thus the run establishes that a finite translation-initiation analysis surface was executed at this scale, while it does not yet certify the required same-organism, same-CDS, same-window join among $f_3$ residuals, wobble weights, matched mRNA, and codon-indexed ribosome-footprint occupancy.  In the same archive, a separate translation-conditioned structural-order run reports two joined organisms, with $3412$ joined rows for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ joined rows for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; its weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ is $0.004764742175882462$, with full-control sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$.  That separate result shows the row-accounting style used by the finite-row system, but it is not imported as evidence for the present position-resolved $f_3$ claim.  A termination-side readthrough contact likewise reports $7658$ joined rows before filtering, $4982$ nonzero G418-induced readthrough rows, nonzero fraction $0.65056$, and runtime $303.534$ seconds; this is a useful contrast for what a concrete external readout looks like, not a substitute for the missing position-resolved footprint matrix here.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
For this chapter, a positive claim would require a table $\mathcal T$ whose rows are indexed by a coding sequence $g$, a codon position or local window $u$, and a sample $s$.  The tested contrast is not the raw codon count and not the modeled wobble score alone.  It is the residual contribution of the $f_3$ coordinate after the declared controls have been fitted:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)
  &= \operatorname{score}(T_{\mathrm{position}}; Z, f_3, W, C)
     - \operatorname{score}(T_{\mathrm{position}}; Z, W, C),\\
Z
  &= \{\mathrm{AA/position\ bins},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M,\mathrm{mRNA},\mathrm{coverage},\mathrm{mappability}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $W$ denotes the bounded wobble-weight surface and $C$ denotes local synonymous codon counts.  A survival statement would need $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0$ on held-out rows after rank checks and after the simpler codon, GC3, length, $M$-density, and mRNA baselines have been challenged.  The available $n=80000$ initiation run does not report this statistic, and the null cross-sample field prevents treating the run as a cross-sample survival certificate.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry requirement.}
The coordinate is admissible for testing only when the wobble context is observed or explicitly bounded so that $w_c \ne w_c^{f_3}$ is a row-level condition rather than an assumption.  If the wobble surface is merely a modeled organism-level annotation, it may enter as a bounded covariate, but it cannot be called observed ribosome occupancy.  In particular, tAI or stAI weights do not by themselves measure local footprint density, pausing, initiation load, translation efficiency, protein abundance, or downstream phenotype.  The present chapter therefore records a boundary condition: modeled codon-adaptation weights can help define a testable contrast, but the positive contact must come from matched translation readout rows.

\paragraph{Interpretation of the null boundary.}
The correct current reading is constrained.  The finite initiation run is large enough to be worth preserving as a candidate surface, yet the reported data do not include the joined position-window matrix needed for the declared $f_3$ survival calculation.  Consequently the claim remains a request for a particular finite join and statistic, not a positive biological conclusion.  The linked structural-order and termination-readthrough runs show that other finite-row contacts in the archive can report joined row counts, nonzero subsets, full-control deltas, and permutation or sign-flip quantities.  This record lacks the analogous row-resolved quantities for $f_3$ position-resolved occupancy: no per-window $R$ list, no $M$ list, no lambda parameter, no hit rate, no reported $p$-value, and no module classification are present for this exact claim.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the $f_3$ coordinate, codon-window geometry, wobble asymmetry, or the existence of an $80000$-row initiation analysis surface alone.  No protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal rule follows from this record.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  For the present chapter, the strongest admissible statement is that $f_3$-stress defines a bounded cross-layer candidate whose test would be meaningful only after same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, bounded wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency rows are joined and evaluated under the stated controls.
