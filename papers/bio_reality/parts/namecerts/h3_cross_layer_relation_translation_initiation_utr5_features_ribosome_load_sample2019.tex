\subsection{Position-Resolved Translation Boundary for $f_3$-Stress}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translation-initiation-utr5-features-ribosome-load-sample2019}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record concerns a proposed cross-layer relation between the $f_3$-stress coordinate and translation readout inside coding-sequence windows.  The carrier is a finite CDS-window table whose rows must jointly contain an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous codon counts, a wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, an mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The finite audit surface for the named translation-initiation contact contains $n=80000$ rows and was computed in $172.864$ seconds under run id \texttt{ed2755f0e232}.  Its \texttt{cross\_sample} field is null, so the row set does not yet certify a held-out cross-sample transfer.

\paragraph{Coordinate under test.}
The coordinate is not raw codon usage.  It is the $f_3$ member of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family after amino-acid composition has been quotiented away.  A window can be considered only when the wobble-weight surface is allowed to break $f_3$ symmetry, written locally as $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$, and when the same row also carries a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout.  The intended survival entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, position bin, GC3, CDS length, methionine-density baseline, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage limits, mappability limits, and simpler baseline ablations.  The statistic is positive only if the $f_3$ term improves held-out prediction after those controls, not merely if codon composition or modeled adaptation weights vary across windows.

\paragraph{Observed finite status.}
The translation-initiation row count is large enough to make the packet worth preserving as a concrete boundary target, but the stored scalar fields do not yet contain the decisive joined position-window statistic.  The current finite entry records $n=80000$, runtime $172.864$ seconds, and no cross-sample value.  It therefore supports the existence of a processed finite surface for the Sample-style translation-initiation object, not a successful $f_3$-stress translation-survival call.  A separate translation-conditioned structural-order run in the same audit family has status \texttt{passed}, fold count $5$, minimum joined count $300$, and a weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ of $0.004764742175882462$ with full-control sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  That neighboring finite result shows that positive controlled cross-layer entries can be represented in this audit format, but it is not evidence that the present $f_3$ position-resolved translation-initiation packet has crossed its own readout boundary.

\paragraph{Required survival relation.}
For this chapter, the admissible relation is a rank-powered survival claim rather than a mechanism claim.  Let each eligible row carry the tuple
$$
\begin{aligned}
(CDS,\; position,\; q_{f_3},\; W,\; T_{\mathrm{position}},\; mRNA,\; Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_{f_3}$ is the residual coordinate, $W$ is the bounded wobble-weight context, and $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ is the same-window ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout.  The relation survives only when the $q_{f_3}$ term remains positive after the full control set and after comparison against codon-count and simpler-position baselines.  Rank sufficiency is part of the claim: an apparently perfect small-rank surface, or a modeled weight table with no matched readout rows, is treated as non-decisive rather than as biological support.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
The current record does not license translation realization from $f_3$-stress geometry alone.  It also does not license protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights can describe a bounded codon-adaptation surface, but they are not observed ribosome-footprint rows.  A matched mRNA table can serve as a control for translation-efficiency residualization, but transcript abundance is not itself ribosome occupancy.  The contact name for position-resolved ribosome occupancy names the needed readout class; it does not by itself supply same-organism, same-CDS, same-window footprint rows.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The claim fails as a positive translation-survival statement if all controlled held-out improvements satisfy
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z) \leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$
after amino-acid or position-bin controls, GC3, length, methionine density, codon counts, matched mRNA, coverage, mappability, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablation.  It also fails if the only apparent signal is carried by review routing, identifier normalization, modeled wobble weights, or unjoined codon-window geometry.  Conversely, support would require a same-row matrix in which the $f_3$ coordinate contributes a positive held-out improvement to the position-resolved translation readout under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite table is therefore retained as an admissible BioReality boundary record for the $f_3$-stress position-resolved translation question.  Its concrete row count and runtime make it a real finite object, but the stored fields do not yet contain the joined occupancy statistic needed for promotion.  The correct present reading is: $f_3$-stress may be tested against translation-initiation or local ribosome-load readouts only after the same CDS windows carry codon counts, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA controls, and position-resolved footprint or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency measurements.  Until that matrix exists and the controlled survival entry is positive, the chapter records a constrained test route rather than a biological law.
