\subsection{Yeast synonymous perturbation window for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ axis}\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-synonymous-perturbation-window}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The yeast synonymous-mutation perturbation record is a finite surface whose rows are synonymous CDS edits, whose codon-coordinate columns are the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual directions, and whose readout side is restricted to measured or constructed quantities that can be joined back to the edited coding sequence.  In the registered run, the usable surface contains $1783$ synonymous mutants distributed over $20$ genes, with $21$ CDS-map hits and a permutation or resampling budget of $B=200$.  The computation completed in $289.093$ seconds under run id \texttt{6b547c2f841d}.  The codon-stability covariate is not an abstract proxy: the run records the Neymotin et al. yeast mRNA half-life table as the source, with
$\mathrm{CSC}_c=\operatorname{corr}(\operatorname{count}_c/n_{\mathrm{sense}},\log t_{1/2})$ across genes.  The observed agreement between the real codon-stability coefficient surface and the proxy surface is
$\Delta_{\mathrm{corr}}=0.39717918221186976$, and the audit records \texttt{real\_csc\_robust=true} together with \texttt{real\_csc\_absorbs=false}.  Thus the finite claim is not that codon usage alone explains the perturbation phenotype; it is that the real mRNA-half-life-derived codon-stability surface remains informative and does not collapse into the simpler proxy in this yeast window.

\paragraph{Controlled object.}
Let $Q$ denote the nine-coordinate residual panel
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast perturbation row is read as a local synonymous-edit test of this residual panel after the obvious amino-acid identity is held fixed by construction.  Its admissible interpretation is narrower than a direct protein-realization statement: the row may support a codon-window perturbation association only when the readout is explicitly part of the finite surface and when the covariates that separate codon composition, mRNA stability, and translation-level quantities are kept distinct.  The matched translation-mediated protein-abundance computation supplies the nearby yeast scale: for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ proteins were joined to real CDS codon counts and PAXdb abundance, the residualized rank checks used $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and residual degrees of freedom $5792$.  On that finite surface the total residual association was $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, the direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI was $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and the mediated remainder was $0.28320534058057895$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{yeast}}&=0.9491551041216973,\\
R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T}&=0.28320534058057895.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers are used here only as the scale of the adjacent yeast mediation surface, not as a substitute for the synonymous-mutant window itself.

\paragraph{Translation and structure boundaries.}
The modeled-tAI contact is finite but bounded.  The three-organism modeled-tAI computation used $61$ sense codons, recovered all $20$ standard amino-acid tRNA families for yeast, human, and \textit{E. coli}, and found $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The same audit records $n=3$ organisms and states that this is not powered survival: $3$ organisms are below the declared $40$-species level for a powered survival claim.  Consequently, a positive modeled-tAI coupling may be used as a codon-adaptation readout, but it does not by itself become ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein output, or mechanism.

The yeast structure-mediation surface gives an additional guardrail for this chapter.  In the five-way yeast join, $4759$ genes were joined, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  The variance partition reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation probabilities were $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the structure-incremental term.  These values show why the present perturbation row must not be promoted to folding or physical admissibility: even in the richer yeast join, the structure component is a small statistical partition, not a layer-changing observation.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite yeast perturbation record is therefore admitted as a bounded synonymous-window result: $1783$ synonymous mutants over $20$ genes, a real mRNA-half-life-derived codon-stability coefficient source, $\Delta_{\mathrm{corr}}=0.39717918221186976$, and a robustness flag that remains true when the real CSC surface is used.  The negative absorption flag is important.  It says that the real CSC surface is not merely swallowed by the proxy baseline in this finite panel, so the row can be retained as a yeast-specific perturbation contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ program.  The result does not license a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ protein-abundance interpretation for this edited panel unless a translation readout $T_j$ is present and survives its own controlled step.  It also does not establish translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality.  Each of those would require its own matched measurement layer and its own finite statistic.  The chapter’s local content is the narrower mathematical statement: in this yeast synonymous-edit window, the real codon-stability surface derived from mRNA half-life remains a nonabsorbed, robust finite-row covariate for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual program under the recorded row counts and audit budget.
