\subsection{Yeast synonymous perturbation window: codon-stability contact without protein promotion}\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-synonymous-perturbation-window}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record fixes a yeast perturbation surface in which synonymous mutant windows are compared against a codon-stability coefficient readout rather than promoted directly to protein output.  The finite table contains $1783$ synonymous mutants distributed over $20$ genes, with $21$ coding-sequence map hits in the reconstruction.  The attached stability coefficient is not an abstract codon label: it is computed from the Neymotin et al. 2014 yeast mRNA half-life table by the rule $CSC_c=\operatorname{Pearson}(\operatorname{count}_c/n_{sense},\log(t_{1/2}))$ across genes.  The run used $B=200$ effective resampling units and completed in $289.093$ seconds.  The observed comparison between the measured codon-stability coefficients and the proxy coefficient surface gave a real-versus-proxy correlation increment
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{CSC} &= 0.39717918221186976.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two Boolean gates are asymmetric: the measured codon-stability coefficient is robust, $\operatorname{real\_csc\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$, while it does not absorb the proxy surface, $\operatorname{real\_csc\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$.  The chapter therefore records a finite perturbation-level contact between synonymous windows and an RNA-stability-derived codon coefficient, not a replacement of the coefficient by a simpler proxy and not a direct protein claim.

\paragraph{Coordinate being tested.}
The biological object is a bounded window over synonymous coding-sequence edits in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  Each row is read only through the codon-stability statistic and the matching of the altered coding sequence back to a finite gene surface.  The admissible carrier has three visible slots: a synonymous-window coordinate $q_i$, a codon-stability readout $c_j$, and the finite row rank induced by the matched yeast mutant table.  A direct statement of translation, protein abundance, folding, organismal phenotype, or selection is not part of this carrier.  The useful readback is instead the controlled question: after the real yeast half-life-derived coefficient is attached, does the synonymous-window residual retain a finite perturbation signal that is not collapsed by the proxy coefficient?  The recorded answer is positive only at that bounded RNA-stability-contact level, because $\Delta_{CSC}$ is positive and the robust real coefficient does not absorb the proxy surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated surface.}
A separate yeast protein-abundance mediation run supplies a useful scale check without changing the interpretation of this perturbation record.  On the Saccharomyces cerevisiae matched CDS-protein surface, $5815$ proteins were used, with residual degrees of freedom $5792$ after the declared control set.  The joint nine-coordinate B$^*_Q6$ protein-abundance decomposition reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation readout in that comparison was modeled per-protein tAI, not observed ribosome occupancy.  The yeast row also had $T$ residual energy $6.90537888244409$, protein-readout residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, and a rank-sufficient design with $9$ residual coordinates.  These numbers show that a yeast cross-layer mediation matrix exists elsewhere in the finite evidence ledger, but they do not upgrade the synonymous-window perturbation coefficient into a measured translation mechanism.  The present chapter uses them only to keep the layer boundary explicit: a protein-abundance mediation statistic and a codon-stability perturbation statistic are different finite surfaces, even when both are yeast-scoped and both involve B$^*_Q6$ residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The modeled-tAI survival surface also remains bounded.  It used $3$ organisms, a $61$-codon sense space, and $9$ B$^*_Q6$ synonymous residual coordinates: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f3\_stress$.  The modeled coupling table had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, with no missing raw modeled tAI fallback among the $61$ sense codons for yeast, human, or Escherichia coli K-12.  The same run explicitly remained underpowered for a powered survival claim because $3$ organisms are fewer than the required matched-species scale.  Consequently, the yeast perturbation result is not interpreted as a measured ribosome-profiling result.  It is a finite RNA-stability-window perturbation contact whose strongest local statement is that the real codon-stability coefficient remains robust and does not erase the proxy-associated signal.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite verdict is a bounded positive for the yeast synonymous perturbation window at the codon-stability-contact level.  The row count is small at the gene level, $n_{genes}=20$, but the synonymous mutant count is nontrivial, $n_{mutants}=1783$, and the measured half-life-derived coefficient gives a positive real-versus-proxy increment.  The honest readback is therefore: synonymous-window variation in this yeast panel retains a finite association with the real codon-stability coefficient surface, and the measured coefficient is not merely a proxy-free eraser of the signal.  Nothing in this chapter says that the same rows establish translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, mature protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, a causal mechanism, selection, or a universal biological law.  Each of those claims would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, no $q_i\to P_k$ statement is admitted here unless the same coordinate first survives a translation-readout step $q_i\to T_j$ under the declared controls, and no phenotype or mechanism statement follows from codon-stability contact alone.
