\subsection{Yeast synonymous perturbation at the B-star window}\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-synonymous-perturbation-b-star-window}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record concerns a yeast synonymous-mutation panel in which the observable rows are not whole-organism phenotypes or protein-folding assays, but finite sequence edits with measured downstream readouts and a codon-stability control surface.  The tested surface contains $1783$ synonymous mutants over $20$ genes, with $21$ coding-sequence map hits in the finite join.  The statistic uses a codon-stability coefficient defined from the Neymotin et al. yeast mRNA half-life table: for codon $c$, the coefficient is the Pearson correlation between $\operatorname{count}_c/n_{\mathrm{sense}}$ and $\log(t_{1/2})$ across genes.  The row therefore tests whether the B-star window survives after a real yeast stability-control contact is introduced, not whether synonymous geometry alone is already a mechanistic explanation.

\paragraph{Observed finite verdict.}
The completed run has identifier $6b547c2f841d$ and used $B=200$ resampling blocks.  Its decisive finite entries are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{mut}} &= 1783,\\
 n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 20,\\
 n_{\mathrm{cds\ hit}} &= 21,\\
 \rho(\Delta \mathrm{CSC}_{\mathrm{real}},\Delta \mathrm{CSC}_{\mathrm{proxy}}) &= 0.39717918221186976.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit reports $\mathrm{real\_csc\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$ and $\mathrm{real\_csc\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$, with runtime $289.093$ seconds.  In this chapter's terminology, robustness means that the real codon-stability control remains informative in the finite mutant panel; non-absorption means the real codon-stability coefficient does not erase the B-star-window contribution.  The local claim is therefore a perturbation-level survival statement: the synonymous window retains measurable signal in this yeast mutant surface after the real stability proxy is inserted as a control.

\paragraph{Relation to translation mediation.}
A separate multi-organism protein-abundance mediation run supplies the broader cross-layer background.  In that run, the B-star $Q6$ coordinate family has $9$ coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, that run joins $5815$ proteins and reports a modeled translation-mediated decomposition with
$$
\begin{aligned}
 R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
 R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
 R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
 M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers show that, in a larger yeast protein-abundance table, conditioning on the modeled translation readout accounts for most of the direct $Q$ to protein-abundance association.  They do not turn the mutant-window result into a direct translation-mechanism claim.  The perturbation panel and the protein-abundance mediation table are distinct finite contacts: one is a synonymous-edit surface with real codon-stability control, and the other is a cross-sectional modeled-tAI mediation surface.

\paragraph{Modeled-readout boundary.}
The modeled translation-survival audit supplies an additional scope check.  It computed $9$ B-star $Q6$ synonymous residual coordinates over $3$ organisms, with $81$ modeled-tAI coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  The same audit explicitly records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the readout is modeled tAI from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy with dos Reis wobble weights, not ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  Consequently, the yeast perturbation row may be read as a bounded synonymous-edit signal with a stability-control audit, while any stronger translation-realization statement would require a separate measured translation contact on matched rows.

\paragraph{Yeast structural and abundance controls.}
A yeast abundance-residual mediation run provides another local guard against over-reading the perturbation result.  Its five-way yeast join contains $4759$ rows and reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the permutation $p$-values are $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  These values matter because they keep the yeast story layered: synonymous-edit survival, modeled translation mediation, abundance residuals, and structure proxy statistics are different surfaces with different admissible readings.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-level conclusion is that the yeast synonymous perturbation panel is positive at the bounded perturbation-and-stability level.  The finite row supports the statement that a B-star-window quantity remains robust in $1783$ synonymous mutants across $20$ genes after inserting a real yeast codon-stability coefficient, and that this coefficient does not absorb the window signal.  The result is compatible with the larger yeast modeled-translation mediation table, where $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, but compatibility is not identity.  The perturbation row does not by itself identify a ribosome-speed mechanism, a folding pathway, a mature protein-function change, a physical-admissibility constraint, a system phenotype, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
The allowed readback is narrow.  This chapter may say that the yeast synonymous-mutation surface contains a finite, controlled B-star-window signal under the reported codon-stability audit.  It may also place that signal beside a separately computed yeast modeled-tAI mediation table.  It may not claim translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality unless a separate layer-matched measurement contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding statistic is computed on those rows.  In particular, a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not treated as protein realization unless a controlled $Q_i\to T_j$ step has survived in the same evidential setting.
