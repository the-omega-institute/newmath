\subsection{Yeast synonymous-window perturbation for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-yeast-synonymous-window-perturbation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record isolates a yeast synonymous-window perturbation test around the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The finite surface is not a claim that synonymous substitutions alter protein state by themselves.  It is a bounded audit of whether a concrete set of same-gene synonymous alternatives can be placed on the already declared route from residual codon coordinates to translation readouts and then, only if the translation step is present, to matched protein abundance.  The run identified $20$ yeast genes and enumerated $1783$ synonymous mutants; $21$ coding-sequence map hits were available for the perturbation lookup.  The computation used $B=200$ null or resampling units and completed in $296.862$ seconds.  These numbers define the finite row support of the perturbation object:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 20,\\
N_{\mathrm{syn}} &= 1783,\\
N_{\mathrm{cds\ hit}} &= 21,\\
B &= 200.
\end{aligned}
$$
The proper readback is therefore a mutation-window audit over synonymous alternatives, not a protein-realization claim.  The admissible relation has the form $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout such as modeled tAI, observed translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, or a bounded equivalent, and $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  A direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is not sufficient for this chapter unless the intermediate $q_i \to t_j$ step is present and controlled.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.}
The coordinate family used by the linked protein-abundance and translation-survival computations has nine entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The controls stay outside the promoted coordinate set: amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline over the union support of the projected $q$ vectors, and matched mRNA abundance where the readout requires transcript separation.  In the yeast protein-abundance mediation run, $5815$ proteins were joined for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with a residual design rank of $9$ for $X_Q$, rank $1$ for the translation readout, and residual degrees of freedom $5792$ after the control block.  The yeast decomposition gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those values show that, in the cross-sectional abundance surface, conditioning on the modeled translation readout accounts for most of the residual-to-abundance association in yeast.  They do not show that any one synonymous edit causes a protein-abundance change, because the perturbation rows above have not supplied an edit-level measured translation and protein response matrix.

\paragraph{Translation contact and modeled-readout status.}
The modeled translation contact was computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights for three organisms in the bounded tAI survival run.  The organism count was $3$, the sense-codon count was $61$, and the nonzero bounded-coupling table had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  All three organism payloads contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families; the matched FASTA header counts were $87$ for Escherichia coli K12, $432$ for Homo sapiens, and $275$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  This is useful as a codon-adaptation readout, but it is explicitly below measured ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency validation.  The three-organism matrix is not powered for a population-level survival law, and it cannot by itself turn the synonymous-window audit into a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Yeast cross-layer context.}
The yeast structure-mediated abundance run joined $4759$ genes across protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, coding sequence, and a 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy.  Its residual protein-abundance association was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; after conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability it fell to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and adding the structure proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, while the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$ with right-tail permutation value $0.1258741258741259$.  Thus the nearby yeast evidence supports a conservative reading: the main finite signal is carried by translation and abundance surfaces, while the 5-prime structure proxy remains a small statistical partition rather than a causal structure bridge.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded linked tests.}
The linked execution-decomposition run in Saccharomyces cerevisiae used $3289$ genes and reported an observable execution subblock, but it did not license folding, function, or universality.  The mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; adding the dynamic closure layer reduced held-out fit from $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$ to $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and found $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows across $3$ species; its strong selection-imprint gate failed with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95$ percent maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$.  These linked rows keep the perturbation result from being overread: the available finite tests include positive bounded translation or execution surfaces, but not a general selection, turnover, mass-balance, or mechanism law.

\paragraph{Coordinate-level signal that may guide perturbation follow-up.}
Among the nine residual axes, the structural-order audit identified $f_3$-stress as the only axis passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, with sign-flip right-tail value $0.03125$ and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  The other axes had larger sign-flip values, including $0.142578125$ for $Arg_{AGR}$, $0.0693359375$ for $Ile_{AUA}$, $0.2294921875$ for $K_{AAA}$, $0.779296875$ for $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $0.24609375$ for $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $0.2158203125$ for $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $0.4873046875$ for $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, and $0.529296875$ for $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$.  For this chapter, that does not select a mutation mechanism.  It only says that a future edit-level assay should stratify the $1783$ synonymous alternatives by the residual coordinate they perturb and should test whether a same-coordinate translation readout moves before any protein-abundance or higher-layer interpretation is attempted.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row result is ready as a bounded synonymous-window record: $20$ genes, $1783$ synonymous mutants, $21$ coding-sequence map hits, and $200$ resampling units define the yeast perturbation surface.  The chapter permits a controlled question about whether synonymous alternatives can be projected into $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and then tested against a translation readout.  It does not permit the stronger statement that synonymous mutation changes translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or a universal biological rule.  Those conclusions would require separate layer-matched contacts: measured translation or ribosome rows for $q_i \to t_j$, matched protein abundance for $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$, and still other assays for folding, function, phenotype, physical state, or mechanism.
