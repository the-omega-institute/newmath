\subsection{Yeast synonymous-mutation window perturbation}\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-synonymous-mutation-window-perturbation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record concerns a yeast synonymous-mutation panel in which the tested object is not an amino-acid substitution and not a folded-protein assay.  The finite rows are synonymous coding-sequence mutants, evaluated through a $B^*_Q6$ window coordinate and compared against codon-level stability information.  The powered run used $1783$ synonymous mutants across $20$ genes, with $21$ CDS map hits available to the coordinate construction.  The bootstrap or resampling budget was $B=200$, and the registered computation completed in $289.093$ seconds under run id $6b547c2f841d$.  The codon-stability calibration was not a free BEDC coordinate: it used yeast mRNA half-life data from Neymotin et al. 2014, with $CSC_c$ defined as the Pearson correlation between $count_c/n_{sense\ codons}$ and $\log(t_{1/2})$ across genes.

\paragraph{Measured comparison.}
The central finite-row diagnostic is that the real codon-stability coefficient source does not absorb the $B^*_Q6$ window signal.  In the registered output, the real CSC bridge was marked robust, while the absorbing condition was false:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{mutants} &= 1783,\\
 n_{genes} &= 20,\\
 B &= 200,\\
 cds\_map\_hit &= 21,\\
 corr(\Delta CSC_{real},\Delta CSC_{proxy}) &= 0.39717918221186976,\\
 real\_csc\_robust &= \mathrm{true},\\
 real\_csc\_absorbs &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a bounded perturbation statement: within this yeast synonymous-mutant panel, replacing the proxy stability surface by the real Neymotin-derived codon-stability surface leaves a residual window effect that is not fully explained away.  The result is therefore evidence for a finite perturbation readout attached to synonymous coding changes, not a claim that the coordinate alone constitutes translation, folding, function, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated protein-abundance surface.}
A separate yeast cross-layer run gives the background reason why this perturbation record is allowed to be discussed near translation-mediated protein abundance, while still remaining bounded.  In the multi-organism $Q\to T\to P$ mediation computation, the Saccharomyces cerevisiae row joined $5815$ proteins and used modeled per-protein tAI as the translation readout and log$_{10}$ PAXdb abundance as the protein readout.  For that yeast row, the controlled decomposition reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
 c_{total} &= R^2_{QP} = 0.29837624994140827,\\
 c' &= R^2_{QP\mid T} = 0.0151709093608293,\\
 c-c' &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
 M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same yeast row had residual degrees of freedom $5792$, translation residual energy $6.90537888244409$, and protein residual energy $2958.1865890925337$ after the declared controls.  Those numbers show that yeast is not merely a symbolic host in the wider program: it has a large matched protein-abundance surface in the linked mediation audit.  However, the mediation run used modeled tAI and steady-state protein abundance, whereas the synonymous-mutation record here uses an explicit mutant panel and a codon-stability coefficient comparison.  They are compatible finite contacts, not interchangeable measurements.

\paragraph{Structure and mRNA-control context.}
A further yeast audit joined $4759$ genes across CDS codon counts, measured TE, mRNA half-life, protein abundance, and a 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy.  In that run, the direct $B^*_Q6$ to protein-abundance residual association was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability reduced the residual association to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$.  Conditioning additionally on the structure proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and the incremental structure fraction beyond TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$.  These figures are included here only to state the local boundary clearly: yeast has multiple matched readout panels, but the synonymous-mutation result does not become a folding, structure, or mechanism result merely because another yeast audit contains a structure proxy.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite claim is ready at the perturbation-readout level.  The admissible conclusion is that the yeast synonymous-mutant window test found a real-CSC-robust $B^*_Q6$ residual signal in $1783$ mutants from $20$ genes, with the real codon-stability calibration correlated with the proxy at $0.39717918221186976$ but not absorbing the window effect.  The result may be read as a controlled perturbation contact for synonymous mutation in yeast.  It must not be read as direct translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality.  Any such higher-layer statement would require its own matched measurement surface and a separate controlled statistic at that layer.
