\subsection{Yeast Synonymous-Mutation Window Perturbation Against Codon-Stability Control}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-synonymous-perturbation-b-star-window-yeast-synonymous-mutation-perturbation-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record isolates a yeast synonymous-mutation perturbation surface rather than a general codon-usage law.  The finite rows consist of $1783$ synonymous mutants mapped onto $20$ yeast genes, with $21$ coding-sequence map hits available for the sequence-side join.  The tested coordinate family is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ window basis used throughout the perturbation packet, but the readout here is not promoted to translation, folding, function, or organismal phenotype.  It is a mutation-window signal checked against a codon-stability-control contact derived from yeast mRNA half-life data.

\paragraph{Control contact.}
The stabilizing control used in this audit is a real codon-stability coefficient, denoted here by $\mathrm{CSC}_c$.  For each codon $c$, the coefficient is computed from the Neymotin et al. 2014 yeast mRNA half-life source as the Pearson correlation between $\mathrm{count}_c/n_{\mathrm{sense}}$ and $\log(t_{1/2})$ across genes.  The finite comparison reports a correlation of $0.39717918221186976$ between the real CSC perturbation contrast and the proxy contrast.  The run therefore records that the real codon-stability control is related to the proxy, but not identical to it.

\paragraph{Decision statistic.}
Let $Y$ denote the synonymous-mutant readout on the yeast perturbation rows, let $W$ denote the $B^*_{Q6}$ window residual coordinates, and let $C_{\mathrm{CSC}}$ denote the real codon-stability-control contrast.  The audit asks whether the apparent window signal is merely absorbed once the measured CSC control is used.  In the finite-row notation of the chapter, the admissible comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Y &\sim Z + C_{\mathrm{CSC}},\\
Y &\sim Z + C_{\mathrm{CSC}} + W,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains the matched sequence controls available to the perturbation surface.  The recorded outcome is $\mathrm{real\_csc\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$ and $\mathrm{real\_csc\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$ under $B=200$ resampling blocks.  Thus the finite audit supports a bounded statement: in this yeast synonymous-mutant panel, the $B^*_{Q6}$ window component is not eliminated by the real CSC control.

\paragraph{What is supported.}
The supported statement is local and statistical.  A finite yeast perturbation table with $1783$ synonymous mutants and $20$ genes retains a $B^*_{Q6}$ window-side contrast after the codon-stability control derived from mRNA half-life is attached.  The runtime for the registered computation was $289.093$ seconds, and the contact source for the control was the Neymotin et al. 2014 yeast half-life repository.  The result is therefore a bounded perturbation-readout record: it says that the window residual is not merely a restatement of the real CSC proxy in this joined finite surface.

\paragraph{Relation to translation mediation.}
This chapter is downstream of the broader $Q\to T\to P$ discipline, but it does not itself establish the whole chain.  In the separate modeled translation-mediated protein-abundance audit, the Saccharomyces cerevisiae joined surface contained $5815$ proteins, used $275$ all-tRNA copies for the modeled tAI readout, and recorded a yeast mediation fraction $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$.  Those numbers give a bounded modeled-mediation context for yeast, not a causal mechanism for this synonymous-mutant panel.  Likewise, the modeled-tAI survival contact used only $3$ organisms, had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-coupling threshold, and explicitly remained below the powered species count required for a population-level survival claim.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The yeast perturbation result must remain at its measured layer.  The chapter does not claim that synonymous mutation windows realize translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ or window-to-output association is not treated as protein realization unless a same-coordinate translation readout first survives the controlled $Q_i\to T_j$ step.  Matched mRNA abundance and CSC controls are covariates or stability contacts; they are not ribosome occupancy, protein abundance mediation, or functional assays.  Any stronger biological reading would require a separate layer-matched contact and a finite statistic on the joined rows.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The readback for this chapter is therefore narrow: the finite mutant table supports a yeast-specific, CSC-controlled synonymous-window perturbation signal with $n=1783$ synonymous mutants, $20$ genes, $21$ coding-sequence map hits, $B=200$, $\mathrm{corr}(\Delta\mathrm{CSC}_{\mathrm{real}},\Delta\mathrm{CSC}_{\mathrm{proxy}})=0.39717918221186976$, $\mathrm{real\_csc\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$, and $\mathrm{real\_csc\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$.  It is a controlled finite-row survival of the perturbation coordinate against a real codon-stability-control contact, not a promotion to higher biological layers.
