\subsection{Yeast synonymous perturbation window as a bounded cross-layer readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-synonymous-perturbation-window}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite yeast perturbation surface in which synonymous coding changes are scored against a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ window coordinate family and then checked against a codon-stability-control readout derived from measured yeast mRNA half-life.  The subject is not a free association between codon geometry and protein output.  The admissible rows are synonymous mutant rows whose coding sequence can be mapped into a yeast gene coordinate, whose window score is computed from the fixed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ nine-coordinate basis, and whose comparison statistic is evaluated against a real codon stability control rather than a proxy alone.  The completed run reports $1783$ synonymous mutants over $20$ genes, with $21$ coding-sequence map hits and $B=200$ resampling arms.  The codon stability control is defined from Neymotin et al. yeast mRNA half-life rows by
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathrm{CSC}_{c} &= \operatorname{Pearson}\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{count}_{c}}{n_{\mathrm{sense}}},\log t_{1/2}\right),\\
  n_{\mathrm{mut}} &= 1783,\qquad n_{\mathrm{genes}}=20,\qquad B=200.
\end{aligned}
$$
The local finite-row verdict is that the real CSC control is robust and does not absorb the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ window signal: $\mathrm{real\_csc\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$ and $\mathrm{real\_csc\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$.  The measured separation between the real CSC vector and the proxy is material rather than cosmetic, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta\rho(\mathrm{CSC}_{\mathrm{real}},\mathrm{CSC}_{\mathrm{proxy}}) = 0.39717918221186976.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the chapter-specific positive content: in this yeast synonymous-mutant panel, the window coordinate has not been reduced to the codon-stability-control surrogate.

\paragraph{Coordinate basis and readout discipline.}
The coordinate side is the same nine-entry residual basis used by the translation-mediated protein-abundance audit: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The yeast protein-abundance mediation run gives the relevant scale for a same-organism downstream contact: in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ protein rows were joined, the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $5792$, and the joint nine-dimensional decomposition gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
  R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
  R^2_{QTP,\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
  M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers are not imported as a perturbation claim for the mutant panel.  They only mark that, when a protein-abundance contact is used, the admissible object is a mediated $Q\to T\to P$ matrix with a translation readout in the middle.  In the present yeast synonymous-mutant record, the actual finite support is the $1783$-mutant perturbation panel and the CSC robustness test.  A direct $Q\to P$ interpretation would exceed the row evidence unless the same rows also carry a surviving translation readout and a matched downstream protein measurement.

\paragraph{Yeast structural and translation context.}
The yeast structure-mediation audit gives an independent same-organism warning about interpretation.  In a five-way yeast join with $4759$ rows, the residual association between $B^{*}_{Q6}$ and protein abundance had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, while conditioning on a 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy changed it only to $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured TE plus mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, the right-tail permutation probability for $S_Q$ structure was $0.027972027972027972$, and the right-tail probability for the incremental structure fraction was $0.1258741258741259$.  These values show why the present chapter must remain a perturbation-window and control-robustness claim, not a folding or structural-order claim.  The same boundary is visible in the modeled-tAI survival audit: only $3$ organisms were available, $81$ modeled tAI entries exceeded the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, and the maximum absolute modeled entry was $3801961.031607896$, but the audit explicitly did not promote this to powered survival because $3$ organisms are insufficient for the requested powered condition and the run used modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Local inference.}
The finite inference is therefore narrow and testable.  Let $W$ denote the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ window score on the synonymous mutant row, let $C_{\mathrm{real}}$ denote the real CSC vector from yeast half-life data, and let $C_{\mathrm{proxy}}$ denote the proxy stability control.  The observed row package supports the statement
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{mut}} &= 1783,\\
  n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 20,\\
  \Delta\rho(C_{\mathrm{real}},C_{\mathrm{proxy}}) &= 0.39717918221186976,\\
  C_{\mathrm{real}}\ \text{is robust} &\quad\text{and}\quad C_{\mathrm{real}}\ \text{does not absorb } W.
\end{aligned}
$$
This licenses a bounded BioReality reading: the yeast synonymous perturbation window survives this codon-stability-control audit on the finite panel that was actually run.  It does not license a claim that the coordinate has realized translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or a universal law.  Each of those readings would require a separate layer-matched contact: ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency for translation, matched proteomics for protein abundance, structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility, perturbation or biochemical assays for function and mechanism, and multi-organism controlled panels for any population-level or universal interpretation.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The positive status of this chapter would fail if the real CSC vector absorbed the window score, if the robust flag became false, if the mapped synonymous-mutant support collapsed below the declared finite panel, or if the readout were reinterpreted as a direct protein or mechanism result without the missing middle contacts.  With the present run, the correct status is a controlled yeast synonymous-perturbation readout: robust against the real CSC control, finite in support, and explicitly bounded away from translation, protein, structure, function, mechanism, and universality claims.
