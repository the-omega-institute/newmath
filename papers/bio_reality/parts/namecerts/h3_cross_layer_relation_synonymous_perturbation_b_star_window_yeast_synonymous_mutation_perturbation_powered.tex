\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record concerns a yeast synonymous-mutant panel in which the tested object is not an amino-acid substitution, a folded protein state, or a direct organismal phenotype.  The finite surface is the matched set of synonymous coding changes, coding-sequence identifiers, codon-stability weights, and $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates.  The registered run reports $1783$ synonymous mutants over $20$ genes, with $21$ coding-sequence map hits and $B=200$ resampling units.  The codon-stability coefficient used as the external control is defined from the Neymotin yeast mRNA half-life table as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  CSC_c &= \operatorname{Corr}\left(\frac{\operatorname{count}_c}{n_{\mathrm{sense\ codons}}},\log t_{1/2}\right),
\end{aligned}
$$
computed across genes.  In this finite panel, the correlation between the real codon-stability-control displacement and the proxy displacement is $0.39717918221186976$.  The finite verdict is therefore a controlled perturbation readout: the real codon-stability control did not absorb the $B^{\ast}$ window signal, and the audit marks the signal as robust under the real codon-stability construction.

\paragraph{Controlled readback.}
The row is read as a bounded synonymous-perturbation statement.  The reported flags are
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{mutants}} &= 1783,\\
  n_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 20,\\
  B &= 200,\\
  \operatorname{Corr}(\Delta CSC_{\mathrm{real}},\Delta CSC_{\mathrm{proxy}}) &= 0.39717918221186976,\\
  \mathrm{real\ CSC\ absorbs} &= \mathrm{false},\\
  \mathrm{real\ CSC\ robust} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The correct finite conclusion is that, on this yeast synonymous-mutant panel, the $B^{\ast}$ window coordinate retains explanatory contact after replacing the proxy codon-stability surface by the real half-life-derived codon-stability coefficients.  The conclusion is not that codon stability is irrelevant.  It is that the real CSC route does not collapse the tested window signal in this run.

No ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, selection law, organismal phenotype, or universality follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite rows and the corresponding controlled statistic.
