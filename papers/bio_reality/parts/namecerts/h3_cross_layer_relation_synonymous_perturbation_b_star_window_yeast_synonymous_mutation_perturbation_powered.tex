\subsection{Yeast synonymous-mutant windows as a bounded perturbation contact}\label{sec:yeast-synonymous-mutation-perturbation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a yeast synonymous-mutant panel in which B$^*_{Q6}$ window coordinates are tested against a perturbation readout without promoting the coordinate geometry itself to a biological mechanism. The finite table has $1783$ synonymous mutants distributed over $20$ genes, with $21$ coding-sequence map hits in the identifier reconciliation step. The controlled codon-stability coefficient used in this audit is not a synthetic proxy alone: it is built from the Neymotin et al. 2014 yeast mRNA half-life table by computing, for each codon $c$, the Pearson correlation between $\operatorname{count}_c/n_{\mathrm{sense}}$ and $\log(t_{1/2})$ across genes. The run used $B=200$ null draws and finished as a concrete finite computation in $289.093$ seconds.

\paragraph{Perturbation statistic.}
The decisive finite quantity is the relation between the real CSC vector and the proxy CSC vector after the same synonymous-window bookkeeping is imposed. The observed real-versus-proxy CSC displacement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{CSC}} &= 0.39717918221186976,\\
N_{\mathrm{mut}} &= 1783,\\
N_{\mathrm{gene}} &= 20,\\
B &= 200.
\end{aligned}
$$
The audit reports $\mathrm{real\_csc\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$ and $\mathrm{real\_csc\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$. Thus the real half-life-derived codon-stability coefficients remain informative under the packet's finite controls, but they do not absorb the B$^*_{Q6}$ window signal into a simpler codon-stability explanation. The result is therefore a bounded perturbation contact: synonymous edits in yeast carry a measurable window-level relation to the half-life-derived codon-stability axis, yet the record does not by itself identify a translation mechanism, a folding pathway, a protein function, or a universal population law.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-mediated protein surface.}
The perturbation contact is interpreted against a separate cross-layer protein-abundance audit rather than as a direct protein-realization claim. In the Saccharomyces cerevisiae slice of the larger Q-to-T-to-P matrix, $5815$ proteins were joined to real CDS codon counts and PAXdb abundance. The B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinate set contained $9$ axes: K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress. For yeast, the controlled mediation summary reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same yeast row had residual degrees of freedom $5792$, residual tAI energy $6.90537888244409$, protein-abundance residual energy $2958.1865890925337$, $X$-explained energy on $T$ equal to $5.671703404506426$, and $X$-explained energy on $Y$ equal to $882.6526210803958$. These numbers supply a context in which the yeast synonymous-mutant contact is plausible as a perturbation-facing readout, but they remain a statistical mediation decomposition, not a causal proof.

\paragraph{Structure and transcription controls.}
A second yeast-only decomposition joined $4759$ rows across CDS, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a 5-prime coding-region MFE proxy. That audit gave $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$. These values keep the yeast synonymous-window result from being read as a hidden structure claim: the available structure proxy is small, sequence-derived, and separately controlled.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
The finite conclusion is that the yeast synonymous-mutant panel supports a bounded perturbation relation for B$^*_{Q6}$ window coordinates after replacing proxy codon-stability bookkeeping with real half-life-derived CSC information. It does not license the direct implication $Q_i \to P_k$ unless a same-coordinate translation readout $T_j$ is present and survives its own controls. It also does not imply ribosome dynamics from tAI alone. The modeled-tAI contact used elsewhere was computed for only $3$ organisms in the bounded survival audit, while powered survival was explicitly withheld because $3$ organisms are insufficient for the requested powered species-level claim; nevertheless the modeled-tAI matrix had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold. The local rule for this chapter is therefore strict: translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, and universality require separate layer-matched contacts and finite statistics. The yeast synonymous-mutant evidence stops at a controlled perturbation-facing relation among synonymous windows, real CSC coefficients, and the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface.
