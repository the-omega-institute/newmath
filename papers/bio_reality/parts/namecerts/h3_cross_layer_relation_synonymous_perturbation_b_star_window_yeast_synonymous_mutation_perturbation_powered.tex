\subsection{Yeast synonymous perturbation window with codon-stability control}
\label{sec:biorreality-yeast-synonymous-mutation-perturbation}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The yeast synonymous-perturbation record is a finite table whose rows are synonymous coding variants and whose columns separate three kinds of quantities: the local $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window coordinates, a codon-stability-control score derived from yeast mRNA half-life measurements, and the observed perturbation readout attached to the mutant sequence.  The audited run used $1783$ synonymous mutants over $20$ genes, with $21$ coding-sequence map hits available for the coordinate lookup.  The resampling budget was $B=200$, and the registered runtime was $289.093$ seconds.  The codon-stability-control source was the Neymotin et al. 2014 yeast mRNA half-life table, summarized by
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathrm{CSC}_c
  &= \operatorname{Pearson}\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{count}_c}{n_{\mathrm{sense\ codons}}},\log t_{1/2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
across genes for each codon $c$.  The finite audit reports a real codon-stability-control correlation with the proxy axis of $0.39717918221186976$, while the two decisive Boolean checks are
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathrm{real\_csc\_robust} &= \mathrm{true},\\
  \mathrm{real\_csc\_absorbs} &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the yeast perturbation table does not collapse to a codon-stability-control explanation: the measured CSC covariate is strong enough to be a meaningful control, but it does not absorb the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window signal in this finite panel.

\paragraph{Local mathematical reading.}
Let $Q$ denote the finite matrix of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window coordinates, let $C$ denote the real CSC covariate vector induced from yeast half-life measurements, and let $Y$ denote the perturbation readout.  The audit should be read as a controlled finite-row comparison of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  Y &\sim Z + C,\\
  Y &\sim Z + C + Q,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains the ordinary sequence and matching controls available to the perturbation table.  The statement $\mathrm{real\_csc\_robust}=\mathrm{true}$ says that the result survives replacing a proxy stability score by the measured yeast CSC construction.  The statement $\mathrm{real\_csc\_absorbs}=\mathrm{false}$ says that the measured stability covariate is not an explanation that removes the residual coordinate contribution.  This is a perturbation-level statement about a finite synonymously edited yeast panel, not a claim that the coordinate has become a complete molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the yeast abundance and mediation surfaces.}
The same organism has independent finite-row support for keeping translation and protein abundance as separated layers.  In the multi-organism $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition, the Saccharomyces cerevisiae slice joined $5815$ proteins, used $275$ all-tRNA gene-copy counts, and had no zero raw codon-adaptation weights filled by the geometric-mean rule.  In that slice the total residual-to-protein association was
$$
\begin{aligned}
  c_{\mathrm{total}} &= 0.29837624994140827,
\end{aligned}
$$
while the direct association after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI was
$$
\begin{aligned}
  c' &= 0.0151709093608293.
\end{aligned}
$$
The indirect difference was therefore $0.28320534058057895$, with mediated fraction $0.9491551041216973$.  These numbers do not prove a causal translation mechanism, because the translation readout in that computation was modeled per-protein tAI rather than a direct ribosome-footprint measurement.  They do, however, give a concrete yeast-scale reason why the perturbation chapter must not treat a direct $Q\to P$ or $Q\to Y$ association as layer-complete unless the intermediate readout has been explicitly attached and controlled.

A second yeast decomposition added mRNA half-life and a 5-prime coding-region folding proxy to the abundance surface.  That five-way join contained $4759$ joined genes, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  The controlled residual association from $Q$ to protein abundance was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on the structure proxy alone left $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability left $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$.  Adding the structure proxy to that latter conditioning set gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and the incremental structure fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability was only $0.0005507778820193332$.  These values reinforce the same separation: a synonymous-coordinate signal may remain visible after stability controls without licensing a folding or structural-order conclusion.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The bounded modeled-tAI contact computed nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  Across the three organisms in that readout audit, the finite matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  The organism count was only $3$, so the audit itself records the powered-survival limitation: it is a bounded modeled-codon-adaptation contact, not a ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  The yeast synonymous-perturbation result therefore inherits a strict reading discipline.  The finite perturbation panel can say that a real CSC control does not absorb the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window signal in the audited yeast mutants.  It cannot, from that fact alone, say that translation has been observed, that folding has been measured, that physical admissibility follows, that function has been realized, that a mechanism has been established, or that a universal biological rule has been found.

\paragraph{Permitted conclusion.}
The chapter supports a bounded perturbation statement: in the audited yeast synonymous-mutation window, $1783$ mutants over $20$ genes retain a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$-window contribution after replacing a proxy codon-stability axis by a real yeast half-life-derived CSC control, with $\operatorname{corr}(\Delta\mathrm{CSC}_{\mathrm{real}},\Delta\mathrm{CSC}_{\mathrm{proxy}})=0.39717918221186976$, robustness marked true, and absorption marked false.  The result is consequently a controlled finite-row perturbation contact for the synonymous-window coordinate.  It is not a promotion to translation realization, protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.  Any one of those higher readings would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic on the corresponding layer.
