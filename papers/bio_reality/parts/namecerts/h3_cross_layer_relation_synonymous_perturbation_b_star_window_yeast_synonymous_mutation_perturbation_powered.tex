\subsection{Yeast synonymous-mutation window perturbation}
\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-synonymous-mutation-window-perturbation}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
The yeast synonymous-mutation audit records a finite perturbation surface in which local synonymous edits are read against $B^*_{Q6}$ window coordinates and an organism-matched codon-stability control.  The registered run $6b547c2f841d$ contains $1783$ synonymous mutants over $20$ genes, with $21$ CDS-map hits recorded during sequence reconciliation.  Its resampling budget is $B=200$, and the run time is $289.093$ seconds.  The finite control contact is not a free sequence proxy: the codon-stability coefficient is computed from Neymotin et al. yeast mRNA half-life data, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
  CSC_c &= \operatorname{Corr}\bigl(\operatorname{count}_c / n_{\mathrm{sense}},\log(t_{1/2})\bigr)
\end{aligned}
$$
across genes.  The reported real-versus-proxy codon-stability correlation shift is $0.39717918221186976$, the real codon-stability control is robust, and the real control does not absorb the $B^*_{Q6}$ window signal.  In the finite-row reading, this means the perturbation table supports a bounded synonymous-window residual association after the available yeast codon-stability correction; it does not by itself upgrade the association to a translation mechanism or a protein-state mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinate content.}
The perturbation coordinates inherit the same nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual vocabulary used by the translation-mediated abundance audit: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  The relevant finite rows are therefore triples of the form $(q_i,w_a,y_b)$, where $q_i$ is one of these residual coordinates, $w_a$ is the edited synonymous window or its induced codon-stability contrast, and $y_b$ is the measured perturbation readout for the mutant row.  The audit is strongest when read as a window-level perturbation statement: the real yeast codon-stability contact changes the proxy geometry substantially, yet it does not erase the residual window component.

\paragraph{Connection to the translation-mediated abundance surface.}
The same coordinate family has an organism-matched yeast abundance mediation audit on $5815$ joined Saccharomyces cerevisiae protein rows.  In that separate finite table, the yeast total residual-to-protein abundance coefficient is $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, the direct coefficient after conditioning on modeled translation readout is $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and the reported indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$.  The corresponding mediated fraction is $0.9491551041216973$, with the in-unit-interval check true.  The yeast rank diagnostics for that table are nondegenerate for the declared design: $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(XT)=10$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and residual degrees of freedom after controls are $5792$.  These numbers do not prove that the synonymous mutants act through modeled tAI; they show that the perturbation result is compatible with a broader yeast finite-row context in which the same residual coordinates have a bounded translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition.

\paragraph{Structure and mRNA-control context.}
A second yeast audit joins $4759$ rows across CDS, translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, protein abundance, and a $5'$ coding-region folding-free-energy proxy.  In that table, the controlled residual-to-protein abundance coefficient is $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; after conditioning on the structure proxy it is $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$; after conditioning on measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability it is $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$; and after conditioning on all three it is $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the permutation right-tail values are $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure component.  This context narrows the local interpretation of the synonymous-mutation audit: the yeast perturbation signal should be kept at window-residual and codon-stability-controlled status unless the same edited windows are joined to a measured translation readout and then to matched protein abundance under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The finite result is a positive bounded contact for a yeast synonymous perturbation surface: $1783$ mutants across $20$ genes retain a $B^*_{Q6}$ window component after the real codon-stability coefficient is introduced, and the real coefficient is not an absorbing substitute for the residual coordinate.  The local claim is therefore that synonymous edits in yeast can carry a detectable residual-window contribution beyond the available codon-stability proxy.  The admissible readback is not a direct $q_i \to P_k$ protein-realization claim.  It is a controlled perturbation record that may be placed next to the yeast mediation and structure-control tables, where the stronger downstream reading requires an explicit $q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ chain.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation realization follows from this perturbation table alone.  No folded-structure statement, physical-admissibility statement, molecular-function statement, organismal-phenotype statement, causal mechanism, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from the finite rows reported here.  A stronger layer claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency for translation, matched protein abundance for protein output, structure measurements or validated structure predictions for folded order, and separate functional or phenotype assays for higher biological consequences.  A direct residual-to-protein association remains blocked whenever the residual has not first survived a controlled translation-readout step.
