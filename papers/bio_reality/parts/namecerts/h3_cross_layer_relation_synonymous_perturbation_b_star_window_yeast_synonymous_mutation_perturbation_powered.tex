\subsection{Yeast synonymous perturbation window as a codon-stability contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-yeast-synonymous-perturbation-window}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record isolates a small yeast perturbation panel in which synonymous mutations are read against a codon-stability covariate rather than promoted directly to translation, protein folding, molecular function, or organismal phenotype.  The finite surface contains $1783$ synonymous mutant rows distributed over $20$ mapped genes, with $21$ coding-sequence map hits available for constructing the gene-to-CDS join.  The registered computation used $200$ null or resampling draws and completed in $289.093$ seconds under run id \texttt{6b547c2f841d}.  Its codon-stability coordinate was not a generic codon-usage score: the reported source is the Neymotin et al. yeast mRNA half-life table, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
  CSC_c &= \operatorname{corr}\!\left(\frac{\operatorname{count}_c}{n_{\mathrm{sense\ codons}}},\log(t_{1/2})\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
computed across genes for each codon $c$.  The observed comparison between the real codon-stability coefficient and the proxy coefficient gave correlation gap $0.39717918221186976$, and the audit marked the real CSC contact as robust while also recording that the real CSC covariate did not absorb the perturbation signal.

\paragraph{Claim readout.}
The admissible claim is therefore narrow.  In this yeast window, a real mRNA-half-life-derived synonymous-codon stability coordinate gives a stronger perturbation-facing contact than the proxy version, as witnessed by $\Delta_{\mathrm{corr}}=0.39717918221186976$ together with \texttt{real\_csc\_robust=true} and \texttt{real\_csc\_absorbs=false}.  The phrase ``does not absorb'' is important: the contact is not being used to erase the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface or to claim a complete mechanistic explanation.  It says that adding the measured yeast CSC route leaves a residual distinction that remains relevant to the fitted perturbation comparison.  The result is a codon-stability perturbation contact over finite synonymous mutant rows, not a proof that a specific synonymous edit changes mature protein abundance through translation, folding, or function.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated protein abundance.}
The same finite ecosystem contains a broader yeast protein-abundance mediation computation, but that computation must be kept separate from the perturbation-window claim.  In the matched yeast protein-abundance panel, the registered mediation decomposition used $5815$ proteins and, after residualizing controls, reported $q$-coordinate count $9$, rank $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, rank $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, rank $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$, and residual degrees of freedom $5792$.  For yeast, the total residual-to-protein abundance value was
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
  R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
  R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
  M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
That larger result is useful as a boundary condition: yeast is one organism where modeled per-protein tAI conditioning strongly attenuated the codon-residual to protein-abundance association in a cross-sectional abundance table.  It does not, by itself, convert the $1783$ synonymous mutation rows in the present window into a measured $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ perturbation chain.  The present chapter only uses it to prevent an invalid shortcut: direct $Q_i \to P_k$ language is not admissible unless the same row design first supplies a surviving translation readout $T_j$.

\paragraph{Structure and stability covariates.}
A second yeast-only decomposition attached residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates to protein abundance through measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a five-prime CDS folding proxy.  That table joined $4759$ genes and reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  Its structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, its structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, the $S_Q$ structure fit was $0.003774248455422586$, and the right-tail permutation probabilities were $0.027972027972027972$ for the $S_Q$ structure statistic and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure statistic.  These numbers help interpret the perturbation window conservatively: the yeast CSC signal lives close to RNA stability and codon-composition readouts, while the structure proxy is weak and not a license to state folding or physical-admissibility conclusions.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates used in the surrounding surface.}
The surrounding $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate basis contains nine projected coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  A separate axis audit found $f3\_stress$ to be the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip probability $0.03125$.  The other coordinates had sign-flip probabilities $0.142578125$ for $Arg\_AGR$, $0.0693359375$ for $Ile\_AUA$, $0.2294921875$ for $K\_AAA$, $0.779296875$ for $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $0.24609375$ for $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $0.4873046875$ for $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $0.2158203125$ for $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and $0.529296875$ for $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$.  This axis result does not identify the causal codon in the mutation panel, but it records which residual direction is strongest when the same coordinate family is projected against a structural-order task.

\paragraph{Translation readout boundary.}
The modeled translation-readout audit computed the same nine residual coordinates over $3$ organisms, with $61$ sense codons and a bounded coupling threshold $0.01$.  It found $81$ entries above threshold and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, using GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and dos Reis wobble weights.  The run also states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered translation-survival inference, so the result remains modeled tAI coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  This matters for the synonymous mutation window because CSC robustness cannot be silently upgraded into ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.  A perturbation row may be compatible with a codon-stability route while still lacking a measured $Q_i \to T_j$ survival statistic.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter permits exactly the following statement: in the registered yeast synonymous perturbation window, a measured mRNA-half-life-derived codon-stability coefficient is a robust finite-row contact over $1783$ synonymous mutants and $20$ genes, with real-versus-proxy correlation gap $0.39717918221186976$, and it does not absorb the residual perturbation signal.  Nothing stronger follows locally.  No translation-realization claim follows without matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency rows.  No protein-output claim follows without same-row protein abundance and a surviving translation step.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection law, or universality follows from CSC robustness, codon-window geometry, modeled tAI coupling, or direct residual association.  Each of those layers requires its own measured contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
