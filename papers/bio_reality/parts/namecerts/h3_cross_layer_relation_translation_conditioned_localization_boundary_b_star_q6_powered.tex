\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record studies a finite coding-sequence surface whose coordinates combine synonymous $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals, matched translation readouts, mRNA controls, and a downstream protein-localization slot.  The residual coordinate is not treated as a localization measurement.  It is first required to survive the amino-acid quotient and then to pass through a translation-readout boundary before any localization readback is interpreted.  The intended local relation is therefore the gated chain $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$, where $q_i$ is a synonymous residual coordinate, $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout or explicitly bounded translation proxy, and $L_k$ is a matched localization label or measurement.  In the absence of a matched localization contact, the chain is read as a null boundary rather than as a biological localization assertion.

\paragraph{Observed finite matrix.}
The powered row for this claim is experiment run \texttt{ab861f8b1269}, with status \texttt{passed}, seed \texttt{sha256:93467a1602ce6c3d589b8b5452f233689fb87fa1bbc0f686694db4bb01da7b1f}, and a five-fold held-out design.  The classifier is a standard-library linear discriminant analysis with train-fold z-scoring and a ridge shared covariance estimate.  Localization rows were joined for three organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}} &= 1908,\\
N_{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}} &= 10187,\\
N_{\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}} &= 4304.
\end{aligned}
$$
Across the three organism rows, the direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ localization discrimination surface has positive macro-AUC increments in all three cases and positive accuracy increments in two of three cases.  Its accuracy increment ranges from $-0.008385744234800874$ to $0.045548247766761585$, with mean $0.022068418153776248$ and median $0.029042750929368033$.  Its macro-AUC increment ranges from $0.073410178078059$ to $0.08299262296923093$, with mean $0.07848683797295208$ and median $0.07905771287156632$.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned readback.}
The translation-conditioned boundary verdict is \texttt{partial}.  The organism-level calls contain one row classified as crossing to the localization layer and two rows classified as partial.  The translation-conditioned increment is consistently positive across the three organism rows: accuracy has minimum $0.017295597484276726$, median $0.02257779522921366$, mean $0.023049495216764453$, and maximum $0.02927509293680297$, while macro-AUC has minimum $0.015494311048138809$, median $0.021959648901327622$, mean $0.021063283115180725$, and maximum $0.02573588939607574$.  Thus the finite row supports a bounded statement: after a translation-readout conditioning step, the residual surface retains a small but nonzero held-out localization signal in this joined panel.

\paragraph{Control-sensitive refusal.}
The stronger control surface blocks promotion to a stable localization claim.  After translation, length, amino-acid, and GC conditioning, the accuracy increment ranges from $-0.010482180293501064$ to $0.010455390334572545$, with mean $-0.000008929986309506402$ and median $0$.  The macro-AUC increment is negative in all three organism rows, with maximum $-0.0008777321759119161$, median $-0.000877887947320577$, mean $-0.001407768101771234$, and minimum $-0.002467684182081209$.  Only one of three rows has positive accuracy under this stronger conditioning, and zero of three rows have positive macro-AUC.  The finite result is therefore not a direct localization law.  It is a localization-boundary readback whose positive signal is visible before the strongest conditioning surface, but not stable enough to license a mechanism or compartment-level interpretation.

\paragraph{Local mathematical form.}
Let $Z$ denote the control block containing amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA control when available.  The admissible test is the conditional comparison
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\rightsquigarrow T_j \mid Z,\\
(q_i,T_j) &\rightsquigarrow L_k \mid Z,\\
q_i &\not\rightsquigarrow L_k \quad \hbox{unless the translation boundary is first retained or explicitly conditioned.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed run gives partial support for the middle line only in the restricted sense of a held-out classifier increment on joined localization rows.  It does not show that $q_i$ by itself is a localization determinant, and it does not show that the translation readout mechanistically causes localization.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The contact classes used here separate codon-usage summaries, modeled codon-adaptation weights, ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency routes, and matched mRNA controls from the localization endpoint.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are not ribosome footprints.  mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control, not a translation measurement.  Bulk protein abundance is not a localization readout.  The present finite row also does not establish protein trafficking, compartment identity, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, universality, or any general biological law.  Each such statement would require its own layer-matched contact and a recorded statistic after the $q_i \to T_j$ boundary has been evaluated.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The appropriate conclusion is partial and boundary-valued.  The powered finite row shows that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals carry some held-out information about localization labels in the joined organisms, and that a translation-conditioned increment is positive across the three rows.  The same evidence also shows that the signal weakens or disappears under the stricter translation-length-amino-acid-GC conditioning surface.  The chapter therefore records a translation-conditioned localization boundary: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ may be carried forward as a candidate coordinate for a $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$ analysis, but localization itself remains unclaimed until same-organism and same-CDS localization rows, translation readouts, mRNA controls, and protein-abundance controls are jointly available and survive the declared conditioning.
