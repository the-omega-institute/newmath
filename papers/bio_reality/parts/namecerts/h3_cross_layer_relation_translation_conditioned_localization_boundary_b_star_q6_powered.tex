\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Localization Boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-indexed panel in which each row carries a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from $B^*_{Q6}$, a matched or bounded translation readout $T_j$, ordinary sequence and transcript controls, and a downstream protein-localization slot $L_k$. The intended readout is not a direct arrow from codon geometry to localization. It is the gated relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where the first arrow must survive amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls before the second arrow is interpreted. The finite powered run used experiment run id \texttt{ab861f8b1269}, status \texttt{passed}, seed \texttt{sha256:93467a1602ce6c3d589b8b5452f233689fb87fa1bbc0f686694db4bb01da7b1f}, and a five-fold held-out classifier described as standard-library LDA with train-fold $z$-scoring and ridge shared covariance. The joined localization rows were $1908$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $10187$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $4304$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.

\paragraph{Observed held-out localization signal.}
Across the three organism panels, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates carried a nonzero but limited held-out localization signal. The direct localization-discrimination comparison had accuracy increments ranging from $-0.008385744234800874$ to $0.045548247766761585$, with mean $0.022068418153776248$ and median $0.029042750929368033$. The corresponding macro-AUC increments were all positive: minimum $0.073410178078059$, maximum $0.08299262296923093$, mean $0.07848683797295208$, and median $0.07905771287156632$. Thus the finite panel does not behave like a pure null for localization classification, since $2$ of $3$ organisms had positive accuracy increment and $3$ of $3$ had positive macro-AUC increment. This supports the narrow statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain partial held-out information about localization labels on the joined finite rows.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary.}
The boundary test asks whether the localization signal remains meaningful only after passing through a translation-readout condition. In the translation-conditioned increment summary, all three organisms had positive accuracy increments and all three had positive macro-AUC increments. Accuracy increments ranged from $0.017295597484276726$ to $0.02927509293680297$, with mean $0.023049495216764453$ and median $0.02257779522921366$. Macro-AUC increments ranged from $0.015494311048138809$ to $0.02573588939607574$, with mean $0.021063283115180725$ and median $0.021959648901327622$. The organism calls were not uniform: $1$ organism crossed to the localization layer, while $2$ organisms remained partial. The finite verdict is therefore \texttt{partial}, not a promoted biological law.

\paragraph{Control-sensitive attenuation.}
The more restrictive translation-length-amino-acid-GC conditioned comparison removes most of the apparent localization increment. Under that conditioning, accuracy increments ranged from $-0.010482180293501064$ to $0.010455390334572545$, with mean $-0.000008929986309506402$ and median $0.0$. Macro-AUC increments were negative for all three organisms: minimum $-0.002467684182081209$, maximum $-0.0008777321759119161$, mean $-0.001407768101771234$, and median $-0.000877887947320577$. Only $1$ of $3$ organisms retained a positive accuracy increment and $0$ of $3$ retained a positive macro-AUC increment. This is the main reason the chapter records a boundary rather than a full localization claim. The finite rows show that $B^*_{Q6}$ has partial held-out localization information, but the more controlled surface indicates that this information is unstable once translation, length, amino-acid composition, and GC-related controls are imposed.

\paragraph{Permitted readback.}
The permissible conclusion is a finite, translation-conditioned localization boundary: $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals can be tested against localization labels only through a staged readout in which translation survival or translation conditioning is evaluated first. The observed direct localization signal is real enough to motivate the staged test, but the controlled comparison does not justify interpreting $q_i$ as an independent localization determinant. The finite summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta\mathrm{AUC}_{Q6\to L} \in [0.073410178078059,\,0.08299262296923093],\\
&\Delta\mathrm{AUC}_{Q6\to T\to L} \in [0.015494311048138809,\,0.02573588939607574],\\
&\Delta\mathrm{AUC}_{Q6\to L\mid T,\ell,AA,GC} \in [-0.002467684182081209,\,-0.0008777321759119161].
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\ell$ denotes length and $AA$ denotes amino-acid composition. The first line records partial localization discrimination, the second records a positive but small translation-conditioned increment, and the third records loss of macro-AUC support under the stricter control surface.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
This chapter does not claim that synonymous residual geometry directly determines protein localization, trafficking route, compartment identity, or localization mechanism. It also does not claim translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality from the residual coordinates, modeled tAI or stAI values, ribosome-profiling contact names, mRNA controls, or bulk protein-abundance routes. Each such statement would require its own layer-matched empirical contact and its own controlled statistic. Protein abundance is not a substitute for localization. Modeled translation weights are not observed ribosome occupancy. A workflow-ready packet is not biological evidence. The present finite row only licenses the narrower statement that the powered localization panel gives a \texttt{partial} translation-conditioned boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$, with direct held-out localization information present but not stable enough under the declared controls to support a mechanistic localization claim.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The BioReality verdict for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_conditioned\_localization\_boundary.b\_star\_q6\_powered} is \texttt{partial}. The finite data support continued testing of $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$ on organism- and CDS-matched panels, especially panels that join codon counts, measured translation readouts, matched mRNA abundance, protein-abundance controls where relevant, and explicit localization labels on the same rows. Until that joined statistic is nondegenerate across organisms, the record remains a boundary result: informative, powered, and empirically non-null in part, but not a direct localization, mechanism, function, structure, physical-admissibility, or universal-law result.
