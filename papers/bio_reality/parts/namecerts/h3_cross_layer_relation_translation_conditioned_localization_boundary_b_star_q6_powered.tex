\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the finite claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_conditioned\_localization\_boundary.b\_star\_q6\_powered}.  The tested surface has rows indexed by coding sequences and organism, with four separated slots: a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation readout $T_j$, baseline controls, and a downstream protein-localization label $L_k$.  The intended readback is not a direct arrow from codon residual geometry to localization.  It is the gated relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where the first arrow must be evaluated before the localization interpretation is admitted.  The run used a standard-library linear discriminant classifier with train-fold z-scoring and ridge shared covariance, over $5$ folds, with run identifier \texttt{ab861f8b1269}.  The finite joined localization rows were $1908$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $10187$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $4304$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Thus the chapter records a finite localization boundary test rather than a general biological law.

\paragraph{Primary localization discrimination.}
The first finite statistic asks whether $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates carry held-out information for localization labels at all.  Across the $3$ organism panels, the maximum gain in accuracy was $0.045548247766761585$, the mean gain was $0.022068418153776248$, the median gain was $0.029042750929368033$, and the minimum gain was $-0.008385744234800874$.  The corresponding macro-AUC gains were all positive: minimum $0.073410178078059$, median $0.07905771287156632$, mean $0.07848683797295208$, and maximum $0.08299262296923093$.  Accuracy improved in $2$ of the $3$ panels, while macro-AUC improved in all $3$ panels.  This supports a weak finite-row discrimination statement: the residual coordinates are not empty with respect to the tested localization labels.  It does not by itself identify a localization pathway, compartment mechanism, trafficking route, or protein-state cause.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary.}
The second statistic tests whether the localization signal remains meaningful under the mandated translation-readout gate.  For the translation-conditioned increment, all $3$ panels had positive accuracy and macro-AUC increments.  The accuracy increment ranged from $0.017295597484276726$ to $0.02927509293680297$, with mean $0.023049495216764453$ and median $0.02257779522921366$.  The macro-AUC increment ranged from $0.015494311048138809$ to $0.02573588939607574$, with mean $0.021063283115180725$ and median $0.021959648901327622$.  This is the finite evidence for retaining the chain-shaped boundary rather than collapsing it to a direct $q_i \to L_k$ assertion.  In compact form, the admissible readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QT} &> 0,\\
\Delta(q_i,T_j;L_k) &> 0\quad\hbox{on the tested finite panels,}
\end{aligned}
$$
where the notation only names the observed finite increments in this run.  It is not a universal survival theorem and it is not a mechanistic statement about localization.

\paragraph{Control-sensitive attenuation.}
The stronger control surface is not positive.  After translation, length, amino-acid, and GC conditioning, the localization increment is essentially null or negative by macro-AUC.  Accuracy had maximum $0.010455390334572545$, median $0.0$, mean $-0.000008929986309506402$, and minimum $-0.010482180293501064$, with only $1$ positive accuracy panel out of $3$.  Macro-AUC had maximum $-0.0008777321759119161$, median $-0.000877887947320577$, mean $-0.001407768101771234$, and minimum $-0.002467684182081209$, with $0$ positive macro-AUC panels out of $3$.  The organism-level calls were $1$ panel crossing to the localization layer and $2$ partial panels.  Therefore the appropriate classification is partial: the finite run detects some held-out localization information and a positive translation-conditioned increment, but the more stringent conditioned surface does not support a stable cross-organism localization claim.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result should be read as a boundary theorem for the measurement design.  A $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate may enter a localization discussion only after the translation readout has been kept separate from the downstream label and the baseline controls have been applied.  Bulk protein abundance, modelled tAI or stAI weights, and codon-usage summaries are not substitutes for a matched localization readout.  The present data allow the statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates show partial held-out localization information under the recorded classifier and panels, with a positive translation-conditioned increment but no robust gain after the stricter translation-length-amino-acid-GC conditioning.  No translation mechanism, protein folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular function claim, trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that specific readout and its own controlled statistic.
