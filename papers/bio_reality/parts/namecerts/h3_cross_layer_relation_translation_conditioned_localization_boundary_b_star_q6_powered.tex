\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-indexed table whose rows carry four separated kinds of coordinates: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates $q_i$, translation readouts $T_j$, baseline controls, and a downstream protein-localization slot $L_k$.  The residual coordinate is first formed after quotienting by amino-acid identity, so the row asks whether synonymous structure remains after the amino-acid sequence has already been accounted for.  The translation readout is kept as its own coordinate rather than being folded into localization.  The localization coordinate is only read after the row has passed through a translation-readout boundary, so the admissible path is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k .
\end{aligned}
$$
The local interpretation is therefore not a direct statement that a codon residual determines a compartment.  It is a finite test of whether residual synonymous information is still visible in a downstream localization classifier after the translation layer and the declared controls have been inserted.

\paragraph{Controls and measured rows.}
The powered run used the experiment identifier \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_translation\_conditioned\_localization\_boundary\_powered} and run id \texttt{ab861f8b1269}.  The classifier was a standard-library LDA with train-fold z-scoring and a ridge shared covariance estimate, evaluated with $5$ folds.  The seed was \texttt{sha256:93467a1602ce6c3d589b8b5452f233689fb87fa1bbc0f686694db4bb01da7b1f}.  Localization rows were joined for three organisms: $1908$ CDS rows for \textit{Danio rerio}, $10187$ rows for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $4304$ rows for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These row counts make the test finite and reproducible, while the organism panel remains small enough that cross-organism stability must be treated cautiously.

\paragraph{Unconditioned localization signal.}
The first finite readback asks whether $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals carry held-out information for localization before the stronger translation-conditioned interpretation is claimed.  Across the three organism rows in the summary, the accuracy increment ranged from $-0.008385744234800874$ to $0.045548247766761585$, with mean $0.022068418153776248$ and median $0.029042750929368033$.  The macro-AUC increment was uniformly positive across the three summaries: minimum $0.073410178078059$, maximum $0.08299262296923093$, mean $0.07848683797295208$, and median $0.07905771287156632$.  Thus $2$ of $3$ summaries had positive accuracy change and $3$ of $3$ had positive macro-AUC change.  This supports the narrow statement that the residual coordinates contain partial held-out localization information in this finite experiment.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned increment.}
The gated boundary then asks whether the localization readout still has a translation-conditioned increment.  For the translation-conditioned increment summary, the accuracy changes were all positive, with minimum $0.017295597484276726$, maximum $0.02927509293680297$, mean $0.023049495216764453$, and median $0.02257779522921366$.  The macro-AUC increments were also all positive, with minimum $0.015494311048138809$, maximum $0.02573588939607574$, mean $0.021063283115180725$, and median $0.021959648901327622$.  In count form, $3$ of $3$ summaries were positive for accuracy and $3$ of $3$ were positive for macro-AUC.  The organism-level call table recorded one case crossing to the localization layer and two cases classified as partial, giving the aggregate verdict \texttt{partial} rather than a universal positive localization rule.

\paragraph{Stronger baseline check.}
A stricter conditioning surface added length, amino-acid composition, and GC controls to the translation-conditioned comparison.  Under this stronger baseline, the accuracy increment ranged from $-0.010482180293501064$ to $0.010455390334572545$, with mean $-0.000008929986309506402$ and median $0.0$.  Only $1$ of $3$ summaries had positive accuracy change.  Macro-AUC was negative in all three summaries: maximum $-0.0008777321759119161$, minimum $-0.002467684182081209$, mean $-0.001407768101771234$, and median $-0.000877887947320577$.  This is the decisive boundary for interpretation.  The data show some localization information in $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ and a positive translation-conditioned increment under the lighter comparison, but the stronger baseline removes the stable macro-AUC gain across the organism panel.

\paragraph{Scientific reading.}
The finite conclusion is therefore partial.  The table supports a chapter-level statement that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals can carry held-out localization information and that a translation-conditioned route is empirically nonempty in the powered run.  It does not support a direct codon-to-localization mechanism.  The admissible readback is only the gated relation $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$, and the stronger control surface shows that the increment is not stable enough to promote the result into a general localization claim.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI coupling cannot be counted as observed translation survival when the organism panel is rank-degenerate or underpowered, and protein abundance cannot replace a matched localization readout.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, or biological universality follows from these numbers alone.  A translation claim requires its own organism- and CDS-matched translation readout, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, and mRNA controls.  A localization claim requires the translation step first and then a matched localization surface evaluated under the same row discipline.  Structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, and mechanism each require their own layer-appropriate contacts.  The present run is best recorded as a partial finite boundary: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ has measurable localization-associated signal in this table, while the fully conditioned localization interpretation remains constrained by the stronger-control null behavior.
