\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:translation-conditioned-localization-boundary-bstar-q6}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record studied here is a finite coding-sequence surface whose columns separate three roles. The first role is the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, retained only after the amino-acid quotient has removed ordinary amino-acid composition. The second role is a translation readout $T_j$, represented either by an explicitly bounded translation-efficiency route or by a modeled codon-adaptation route when the latter is kept at its own scope. The third role is a protein-localization readout $L_k$. The intended relation is therefore not a direct arrow from codon geometry to localization, but the gated chain
$$\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k.
\end{aligned}$$
A localization interpretation is admissible only after the $q_i \to T_j$ boundary is tested under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA controls. Without that intervening boundary, a residual coordinate is only a coordinate on coding sequence, not a localization measurement.

\paragraph{Residual-to-localization discrimination.}
When $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are used to discriminate localization labels on held-out folds, the macro-AUC increment is positive in all three organism panels. The minimum, median, mean, and maximum macro-AUC increments are $0.073410178078059$, $0.07905771287156632$, $0.07848683797295208$, and $0.08299262296923093$, respectively. Accuracy increments are weaker: the minimum is $-0.008385744234800874$, the median is $0.029042750929368033$, the mean is $0.022068418153776248$, and the maximum is $0.045548247766761585$, with $2$ of $3$ organism panels positive. In compact form,
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta\mathrm{AUC}_{\min,\mathrm{med},\mathrm{mean},\max}
&=(0.073410178078059,\ 0.07905771287156632,\ 0.07848683797295208,\ 0.08299262296923093),\\
\Delta\mathrm{Acc}_{\min,\mathrm{med},\mathrm{mean},\max}
&=(-0.008385744234800874,\ 0.029042750929368033,\ 0.022068418153776248,\ 0.045548247766761585).
\end{aligned}$$
This is enough to say that the residual coordinates carry some held-out localization information on the joined panels. It is not enough to say that codon residuals determine localization, that they identify a trafficking pathway, or that they define compartment identity.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary.}
The translation-conditioned comparison is also positive at the finite-row level, but its interpretation is narrower. Across the three organism panels, the translation-conditioned increment has positive accuracy in $3$ of $3$ panels and positive macro-AUC in $3$ of $3$ panels. Its accuracy increments have minimum $0.017295597484276726$, median $0.02257779522921366$, mean $0.023049495216764453$, and maximum $0.02927509293680297$. Its macro-AUC increments have minimum $0.015494311048138809$, median $0.021959648901327622$, mean $0.021063283115180725$, and maximum $0.02573588939607574$:
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta\mathrm{Acc}^{T}_{\min,\mathrm{med},\mathrm{mean},\max}
&=(0.017295597484276726,\ 0.02257779522921366,\ 0.023049495216764453,\ 0.02927509293680297),\\
\Delta\mathrm{AUC}^{T}_{\min,\mathrm{med},\mathrm{mean},\max}
&=(0.015494311048138809,\ 0.021959648901327622,\ 0.021063283115180725,\ 0.02573588939607574).
\end{aligned}$$
The organism-level calls were $1$ crossing to the localization layer and $2$ partial. The verdict for this boundary is therefore partial: the finite rows support a translation-conditioned localization boundary, but not a stable cross-organism localization law.

\paragraph{Ablation against sequence and composition controls.}
The stricter translation-length-amino-acid-GC conditioned increment removes most of the apparent localization gain. In that comparison, the accuracy increment ranges from $-0.010482180293501064$ to $0.010455390334572545$, with median $0.0$ and mean $-0.000008929986309506402$. Only $1$ of $3$ organism panels has positive accuracy. Macro-AUC is negative in all three panels: the minimum, median, mean, and maximum are $-0.002467684182081209$, $-0.000877887947320577$, $-0.001407768101771234$, and $-0.0008777321759119161$. Thus, after the stronger control set, the residual localization increment is not a robust positive surface:
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta\mathrm{Acc}^{T,Z}_{\min,\mathrm{med},\mathrm{mean},\max}
&=(-0.010482180293501064,\ 0.0,\ -0.000008929986309506402,\ 0.010455390334572545),\\
\Delta\mathrm{AUC}^{T,Z}_{\min,\mathrm{med},\mathrm{mean},\max}
&=(-0.002467684182081209,\ -0.000877887947320577,\ -0.001407768101771234,\ -0.0008777321759119161).
\end{aligned}$$
This is the main reason the chapter records a boundary rather than an affirmative localization theorem.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result has two parts. First, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals are not empty with respect to localization labels: held-out macro-AUC increments are positive on all three joined organism panels. Second, once the translation boundary and stronger controls are imposed, the apparent localization increment is unstable and nearly disappears under the length, amino-acid, and GC-conditioned comparison. The scientifically conservative reading is therefore: $q_i$ may be carried into a localization analysis only through the separated $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$ chain, and the current finite rows justify a partial boundary call rather than a direct biological assertion about localization.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows from the residual coordinate alone. No ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, trafficking mechanism, compartment identity mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this finite localization run. Modeled tAI or stAI quantities remain modeled translation-layer readouts unless organism- and CDS-matched measured translation rows supply the missing observation. Matched mRNA abundance is a control for translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation or localization measurement. Bulk protein abundance cannot be substituted for a localization readout. A stronger localization claim would require same-organism and same-CDS rows joining codon residuals, a nondegenerate translation readout, matched mRNA controls, protein-abundance controls where used, and layer-matched localization labels, followed by the declared controlled statistic on that joined panel.
