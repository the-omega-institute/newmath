\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-indexed surface on which each row carries four separated coordinates: a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-layer readout coordinate $T_j$, a matched mRNA control coordinate, and a downstream protein-localization slot $L_k$. The intended scientific question is not whether codon residual geometry directly determines localization. The question is narrower: whether a residual coordinate that has already survived amino-acid quotienting can be tested through a required translation-readout boundary before any localization interpretation is allowed. The admissible readback is therefore the gated chain $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$, with a null localization boundary whenever the matched localization rows or the nondegenerate translation step are absent.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary.}

On the translation-conditioned increment surface, all three organism rows had positive accuracy increments and positive macro-AUC increments. The accuracy increment ranged from $0.017295597484276726$ to $0.02927509293680297$, with median $0.02257779522921366$ and mean $0.023049495216764453$. The macro-AUC increment ranged from $0.015494311048138809$ to $0.02573588939607574$, with median $0.021959648901327622$ and mean $0.021063283115180725$. Hence the finite boundary statistic is positive as a conditioned readout:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\mathrm{Acc}_{Q6\mid T\to L} &\in [0.017295597484276726,\;0.02927509293680297],\\
\Delta\mathrm{AUC}_{Q6\mid T\to L} &\in [0.015494311048138809,\;0.02573588939607574],\\

The organism-level verdict counts were $1$ crossing to the localization layer and $2$ partial calls. The combined verdict is therefore \emph{partial}, not closed.

This is the main reason the chapter records a boundary result rather than a promotion to localization biology. The finite run shows that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ can discriminate localization labels in held-out prediction and that a translation-conditioned increment is positive in this small panel, but the more heavily controlled surface does not yield stable positive macro-AUC.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
The chapter therefore permits only the following claim: in the attached finite rows, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals have partial held-out localization information, and the proposed interpretation must pass through the separated translation-readout boundary before localization language is used. No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, trafficking mechanism, compartment identity mechanism, system phenotype, or universal rule follows from these numbers alone. A modeled tAI or stAI weight is not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement; a bulk protein-abundance contact is not a localization assay; matched mRNA abundance is a control coordinate rather than a translation readout. Each higher layer requires its own layer-matched contact and statistic.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is \emph{partial}. The direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ localization classifier carries held-out macro-AUC signal in $3/3$ organism rows, and the translation-conditioned increment is positive in $3/3$ rows for both accuracy and macro-AUC. However, after the stronger translation, length, amino-acid, and GC-conditioned control surface, the macro-AUC increment is negative in $3/3$ rows and the mean accuracy increment is essentially zero. The result is therefore a translation-conditioned localization boundary: sufficient for recording a finite cross-layer testing route, insufficient for claiming localization mechanism or any downstream biological law.
