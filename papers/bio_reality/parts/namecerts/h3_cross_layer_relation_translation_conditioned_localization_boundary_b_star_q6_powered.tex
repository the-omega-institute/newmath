\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record considered here is a finite CDS-indexed table in which each row $c$ carries four separated slots: a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i(c)$ from the $B^*_{Q6}$ basis after the amino-acid quotient, a translation readout slot $T_j(c)$, matched transcript controls $m(c)$, and a downstream protein-localization label or score $L_k(c)$. The admissible readback is not a direct map from codon residual geometry to localization. It is the gated chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i(c) &\longrightarrow T_j(c) \longrightarrow L_k(c),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the first arrow must survive amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls before the localization leg is interpreted. The surface is therefore cross-layer only in a conditional sense: a localization statement is allowed only after a translation boundary has been crossed or explicitly conditioned, and otherwise the localization slot remains a null boundary.

\paragraph{Powering and joined localization rows.}
The powered finite run used three organism panels with matched localization labels: $1908$ CDS rows for \emph{Danio rerio}, $10187$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $4304$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The classifier was a standard-library LDA procedure with train-fold z-scoring and ridge shared covariance, evaluated with $5$ folds. The reproducibility seed was recorded as \texttt{sha256:93467a1602ce6c3d589b8b5452f233689fb87fa1bbc0f686694db4bb01da7b1f}, and the run status was passed. These rows are sufficient to test whether the finite $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface carries held-out localization information, but they are not by themselves sufficient to turn that information into a trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, folding claim, function claim, physical-admissibility claim, or universal biological law.

\paragraph{Observed residual-to-localization signal.}
Across the three organism panels, the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ localization discriminator had positive macro-AUC increments in all three panels and positive accuracy increments in two of three panels. The accuracy increment ranged from $-0.008385744234800874$ to $0.045548247766761585$, with mean $0.022068418153776248$ and median $0.029042750929368033$. The macro-AUC increment ranged from $0.073410178078059$ to $0.08299262296923093$, with mean $0.07848683797295208$ and median $0.07905771287156632$. Thus the finite surface does contain held-out localization information in the residual coordinates, but the sign pattern is not a complete localization claim because one accuracy panel is negative and the readout has not yet been reduced to a stable layer-matched localization mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary.}
When the localization readout is evaluated through the translation-conditioned boundary, the increment is positive in all three panels for both accuracy and macro-AUC. The translation-conditioned accuracy increment has minimum $0.017295597484276726$, median $0.02257779522921366$, mean $0.023049495216764453$, and maximum $0.02927509293680297$. The corresponding macro-AUC increment has minimum $0.015494311048138809$, median $0.021959648901327622$, mean $0.021063283115180725$, and maximum $0.02573588939607574$. This supports the finite claim that the relevant readback is the conditioned relation $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$, not an ungated residual-to-localization assertion.

\paragraph{Control-sensitive attenuation.}
The stricter translation-length-amino-acid-GC-conditioned comparison removes most of the apparent increment. Under that conditioning, the accuracy increment ranges from $-0.010482180293501064$ to $0.010455390334572545$, with median $0.0$ and mean $-0.000008929986309506402$; only one of three panels is positive for accuracy. The macro-AUC increment is negative in all three panels, ranging from $-0.002467684182081209$ to $-0.0008777321759119161$, with median $-0.000877887947320577$ and mean $-0.001407768101771234$. The organism-level calls therefore split into one panel crossing to the localization layer and two partial panels. The finite verdict is consequently partial: $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals carry some held-out localization information, and the translation-conditioned increment is positive in the finite run, but the increment is not stable after the stronger control surface is imposed.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The chapter records a bounded cross-layer condition rather than a downstream biological explanation. A valid interpretation may say that, in this finite run, $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show partial held-out localization information and a positive translation-conditioned increment across three organism panels. It may not say that the residual coordinates directly determine protein localization, trafficking, compartment identity, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality. It also may not replace a matched protein-localization readout with bulk protein abundance, nor may it treat modeled tAI or stAI weights as observed ribosome occupancy when the organism panel is rank-degenerate or underpowered. Each such stronger assertion requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Finite conclusion.}
The finite readback is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{residual coordinate} \quad q_i
&\xrightarrow{\text{translation boundary}} T_j
\xrightarrow{\text{matched localization slot}} L_k,\\
\text{verdict} &= \text{partial}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result is ready as a BioReality chapter because the run supplies concrete joined row counts, a declared classifier, a fold count, a reproducibility seed, and finite held-out increment summaries. Its scientific force is deliberately narrow: the current evidence supports a translation-conditioned localization boundary with partial cross-organism stability, not an autonomous localization law.
