\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite CDS-indexed coordinate surface rather than as direct localization evidence.  Each row carries a residual coordinate $q_i$, matched translation readout variables $T_j$, mRNA controls, standard sequence controls, and a downstream localization slot $L_k$.  The biological question is therefore not whether $q_i$ alone names a compartment, trafficking process, or localization mechanism.  The question is whether a controlled row-level decomposition
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k
\end{aligned}
$$
can be read without collapsing the translation layer into the localization layer.

The finite run attached to this record is experiment run \texttt{ab861f8b1269}.  It used a standard-library LDA classifier with train-fold z-scoring and ridge shared covariance, evaluated with $5$ folds.  The joined localization row counts were $1908$ CDS rows for \emph{Danio rerio}, $10187$ rows for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $4304$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  These are the finite rows on which the localization readout is present in the current audit surface; they do not, by themselves, turn codon residual geometry into a localization mechanism.

\paragraph{Residual-to-localization discrimination.}
The first finite summary asks whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block contains held-out information about the localization labels at all.  Across the three organism panels, the residual block had mean held-out accuracy gain $0.022068418153776248$, median gain $0.029042750929368033$, minimum gain $-0.008385744234800874$, and maximum gain $0.045548247766761585$.  The corresponding macro-AUC gain was positive in all three panels, with mean $0.07848683797295208$, median $0.07905771287156632$, minimum $0.073410178078059$, and maximum $0.08299262296923093$.  Thus the finite classifier surface reports a partial discriminative signal: $2$ of $3$ panels improved in accuracy, while $3$ of $3$ improved in macro-AUC.

This evidence is deliberately weaker than a biological localization claim.  It says that, on these joined rows and under this classifier, the residual coordinates retain some held-out label information.  It does not say that synonymous residuals determine localization, that localization follows without translation conditioning, or that a molecular trafficking mechanism has been measured.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary.}
The second finite summary enforces the layer ordering.  Under the translation-conditioned increment summary, all three panels showed positive accuracy and macro-AUC increments.  The accuracy increment had mean $0.023049495216764453$, median $0.02257779522921366$, minimum $0.017295597484276726$, and maximum $0.02927509293680297$.  The macro-AUC increment had mean $0.021063283115180725$, median $0.021959648901327622$, minimum $0.015494311048138809$, and maximum $0.02573588939607574$.  The organism call counts were $1$ crossing to the localization layer and $2$ partial calls.

The same run also reports the stricter translation-length-amino-acid-GC conditioned increment.  There the accuracy increment nearly vanishes on average: mean $-0.000008929986309506402$, median $0.0$, minimum $-0.010482180293501064$, and maximum $0.010455390334572545$, with only $1$ of $3$ panels positive in accuracy.  The macro-AUC increment is negative in all three panels, with mean $-0.001407768101771234$, median $-0.000877887947320577$, minimum $-0.002467684182081209$, and maximum $-0.0008777321759119161$.  This is why the finite verdict is \emph{partial}: the residual block can carry localization-discriminative information, and the translation-conditioned increment is positive on the reported summary, but the stricter conditioning surface does not support a stable cross-organism promotion.

\paragraph{Permitted readback.}
The admissible interpretation is the gated relation only.  A row may be read as evidence for the ordered test
$$
\begin{aligned}
B^{\ast}_{Q6}\text{ residual }q_i
  &\longrightarrow \text{matched translation readout }T_j \\
  &\longrightarrow \text{localization readout }L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
provided the translation-readout step is kept explicit and the downstream localization slot is evaluated only on joined localization rows.  The current finite verdict is therefore a boundary result, not a mechanism result.  The run supports the statement that the measured surface contains partial held-out localization information associated with $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals and that the translation-conditioned summary is positive across the three panels, while the stricter controlled increment is unstable.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No translation realization follows from a codon residual coordinate alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, phenotype, causation, adaptation claim, or universal biological rule follows from this finite row surface.  Modeled tAI or stAI variables, ribosome-profiling routes, mRNA controls, and bulk protein abundance cannot be substituted for a layer-matched localization readout unless the rows are joined and the declared conditioning statistic is actually computed.  Likewise, a direct $q_i \to L_k$ association is not accepted as a localization interpretation when the $q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout boundary is absent, rank-degenerate, or absorbed by amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, mRNA, protein-abundance, and simpler-baseline controls.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The finite row verdict is \emph{partial}.  The strongest local statement is: in run \texttt{ab861f8b1269}, using $5$-fold held-out LDA with train-fold standardization and ridge shared covariance, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals showed positive macro-AUC localization discrimination in $3$ of $3$ organism panels, positive translation-conditioned increments in $3$ of $3$ panels, and an organism-level boundary classification of one crossing and two partial calls.  The same evidence refuses promotion to a stable biological localization claim because the stricter translation-length-amino-acid-GC conditioned increment is not positive in macro-AUC for any of the three panels and is positive in accuracy for only one panel.
