\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Localization Boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as finite coding-sequence covariates whose biological readback is allowed only through measured or explicitly bounded biological rows.  The carrier for the test consists of CDS-indexed rows containing a residual coordinate $q_i$, matched sequence controls, a translation-readout slot $T_j$, and a downstream protein-localization slot $L_k$.  The amino-acid quotient is applied before the localization question is asked: a coordinate may be inspected only if it remains distinguishable after amino-acid composition is controlled, and the downstream localization statement is not read directly from the residual coordinate.

The admissible finite relation has the gated form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ denotes a translation-layer readout such as a ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency surface, and $L_k$ denotes a matched protein-localization readout.  The boundary is null when the localization row is absent, and it remains only partial when the residual coordinate predicts localization in held-out classification but does not show stable additional localization information after the declared translation and sequence controls are imposed.

\paragraph{Measured finite run.}
The powered run used standard-library LDA with train-fold $z$-scoring and a ridge shared covariance estimator.  It used five folds and the deterministic seed
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{seed} = \mathrm{sha256{:}93467a1602ce6c3d589b8b5452f233689fb87fa1bbc0f686694db4bb01da7b1f}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run identifier was $\mathrm{ab861f8b1269}$, and the recorded status was passed.  The joined localization rows were $1908$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $10187$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $4304$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Thus the localization side of the surface was not empty, but it was still organism-limited, with $n=3$ organism-level evaluations.

For the direct held-out localization discrimination test, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block produced positive macro-AUC increments in all three organism rows and positive accuracy increments in two of the three rows.  The accuracy increment ranged from $-0.008385744234800874$ to $0.045548247766761585$, with mean $0.022068418153776248$ and median $0.029042750929368033$.  The macro-AUC increment ranged from $0.073410178078059$ to $0.08299262296923093$, with mean $0.07848683797295208$ and median $0.07905771287156632$.  This is evidence that the residual block carries some held-out localization information, but it is not yet the translation-conditioned biological claim.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned test.}
The relevant boundary statistic asks whether the localization signal remains attached to the declared route through translation readout rather than appearing as an ungated residual-to-localization association.  On the translation-conditioned increment summary, all three rows had positive accuracy increments and all three rows had positive macro-AUC increments.  The accuracy increment ranged from $0.017295597484276726$ to $0.02927509293680297$, with mean $0.023049495216764453$ and median $0.02257779522921366$.  The macro-AUC increment ranged from $0.015494311048138809$ to $0.02573588939607574$, with mean $0.021063283115180725$ and median $0.021959648901327622$.

The stricter translation-, length-, amino-acid-, and GC-conditioned increment was not stable in the same way.  Its accuracy increment ranged from $-0.010482180293501064$ to $0.010455390334572545$, with mean $-0.000008929986309506402$ and median $0.0$.  Its macro-AUC increment was negative in every organism row, ranging from $-0.002467684182081209$ to $-0.0008777321759119161$, with mean $-0.001407768101771234$ and median $-0.000877887947320577$.  The row count for this stricter summary was again $n=3$, with one positive accuracy row and zero positive macro-AUC rows.  The organism calls therefore split into one row that crossed to the localization layer and two rows called partial.

\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
The finite verdict is partial.  The direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block discriminates localization in held-out evaluation, and the translation-conditioned summary is positive across the three organism rows.  However, the more stringent conditioned surface, including translation, CDS length, amino-acid composition, and GC controls, does not preserve a stable macro-AUC increment.  The record therefore supports a bounded statement: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals may be used to define a translation-conditioned localization-boundary test, and the present run finds nonzero held-out localization information, but the result is not a stable claim that residual geometry by itself determines localization.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No protein localization, trafficking route, compartment identity, folding behavior, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from the residual coordinates alone.  Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not an observed localization measurement, mRNA abundance is only a control covariate for separating transcription-layer variation, and bulk protein abundance cannot replace a matched localization readout.  A positive residual-to-localization classifier is therefore insufficient unless the same finite row surface also supplies the intervening translation-readout step and the downstream localization rows under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Accepted readback.}
The accepted readback is the finite relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
B^{\ast}_{Q6}\ \mathrm{coordinate}\ q_i
&\rightsquigarrow \mathrm{translation\ readout}\ T_j \\
&\rightsquigarrow \mathrm{localization\ readout}\ L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, and protein-abundance controls kept outside the residual coordinate itself.  The current evidence permits the chapter to state a powered partial boundary: the localization surface is populated and the finite classifier run passed, but the strongest controlled localization increment remains too unstable across the three organisms to promote the relation to a general biological localization claim.
