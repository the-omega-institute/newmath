\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies a finite CDS-indexed surface whose coordinates are $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals, matched translation readouts, mRNA controls, and a downstream protein-localization readout slot. The biological question is not whether a codon residual directly names a compartment. The admissible readback is narrower: a residual coordinate $q_i$ may be interpreted at the localization layer only after passing through a translation-readout boundary. In schematic form the permitted relation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout and $L_k$ is a matched protein-localization readout. The amino-acid quotient is imposed before the higher-layer test, so a synonymous residual must survive as a synonymous coordinate rather than merely restating amino-acid composition. The intended control family contains amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance; protein-abundance controls are allowed only as controls, not as substitutes for localization measurements.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary.}
The translation-conditioned increment summary was also positive on the reported finite rows, but its interpretation is boundary-level rather than mechanistic. The translation-conditioned increments had $n=3$, positive accuracy count $3$ of $3$, and positive macro-AUC count $3$ of $3$. The organism calls were not uniform: one row crossed to the localization layer, while two rows remained partial. The recorded verdict is therefore \emph{partial}. It says that the finite test sees a reproducible translation-conditioned improvement, not that the residual has become an unconditional localization law.

\paragraph{Control-sensitive refusal.}
The stricter translation-length-amino-acid-GC conditioned increment is the decisive caution. Under that control surface the accuracy increment ranged from $-0.010482180293501064$ to $0.010455390334572545$, with mean $-0.000008929986309506402$ and median $0.0$; only $1$ of $3$ rows was positive by accuracy. The macro-AUC increment was negative in all three rows, ranging from $-0.002467684182081209$ to $-0.0008777321759119161$, with mean $-0.001407768101771234$ and median $-0.000877887947320577$. Thus the stronger control surface removes the positive macro-AUC signal. The finite conclusion is not that $B^*_{Q6}$ determines localization. It is that a weaker translation-conditioned readout gives positive held-out increments, while a stronger baseline collapses the macro-AUC increment below zero.

\paragraph{Admissible statement.}
The admissible scientific statement is therefore a gated one. A $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate may be proposed as localization-relevant only through a checked relation of the form $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$, and only after the translation side has survived the amino-acid, organism, GC3, length, M-density, and mRNA-control surface. The present finite rows justify a partial boundary claim: $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals discriminate localization labels in held-out LDA, and a translation-conditioned increment is positive across the three organism rows, but the stronger controlled increment is not stable across organisms. The record remains compatible with a translation-conditioned localization boundary; it does not certify a general localization mechanism.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome-occupancy realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, protein-trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this row. Modeled tAI or stAI weights, codon-usage tables, ribosome-profiling route names, mRNA controls, and bulk protein abundance cannot replace a matched localization readout. A direct $q_i \to L_k$ association is not admissible unless the same finite surface first establishes the intervening $q_i \to T_j$ boundary and then evaluates the localization leg under the declared controls. The present run is therefore ready as a partial BioReality chapter row, with a local boundary rather than a promoted biological law.
