\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-indexed surface whose coordinates are synonymous $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, matched translation readouts, mRNA controls, and a downstream protein-localization label.  The intended readback is not a direct map from a codon residual to a compartment label.  It is the gated relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ denotes a residual coordinate that remains after the amino-acid quotient, $T_j$ denotes a translation-layer readout, and $L_k$ denotes a localization readout.  The finite carrier is therefore interpreted only after the translation slot has been kept distinct from the localization slot.  A direct association $q_i \to L_k$ is treated as uninterpretable unless the same finite panel also supplies the translation boundary needed to ask whether the localization signal is downstream of, conditioned by, or independent of $T_j$.

\paragraph{Measured panel and classifier.}
The powered finite row uses experiment run \texttt{ab861f8b1269} with seed \texttt{sha256:93467a1602ce6c3d589b8b5452f233689fb87fa1bbc0f686694db4bb01da7b1f}.  The fitted model is a standard-library LDA classifier with train-fold z-scoring and ridge shared covariance, evaluated with $5$ folds.  The localization-joined row counts are $1908$ CDS rows for \textit{Danio rerio}, $10187$ rows for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $4304$ rows for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Thus the finite panel has three organism blocks and a total of
$$
\begin{aligned}
1908+10187+4304 = 16399
\end{aligned}
$$
joined localization rows before any biological interpretation is attempted.

\paragraph{Residual localization information.}
The first finite statistic asks whether the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates carry held-out information about localization labels.  Across the three organism blocks, the maximum held-out accuracy increment is $0.045548247766761585$, the mean is $0.022068418153776248$, the median is $0.029042750929368033$, and the minimum is $-0.008385744234800874$.  Accuracy is positive in $2$ of $3$ organism blocks.  The macro-AUC increment is more stable: its minimum is $0.073410178078059$, median $0.07905771287156632$, mean $0.07848683797295208$, and maximum $0.08299262296923093$, with positive macro-AUC increments in all $3$ organism blocks.  In compact form, the observed residual-localization readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\mathrm{Acc}_{Q6\to L} &\in [-0.008385744234800874,\;0.045548247766761585],\\
\Delta\mathrm{AUC}_{Q6\to L} &\in [0.073410178078059,\;0.08299262296923093].
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a bounded statement that the residual coordinates contain some held-out localization information on this finite panel, especially in macro-AUC, but it does not by itself identify a localization mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary.}
The translation-conditioned increment is positive in all three organism blocks for both accuracy and macro-AUC.  Its accuracy increment has minimum $0.017295597484276726$, median $0.02257779522921366$, mean $0.023049495216764453$, and maximum $0.02927509293680297$.  Its macro-AUC increment has minimum $0.015494311048138809$, median $0.021959648901327622$, mean $0.021063283115180725$, and maximum $0.02573588939607574$.  The finite summary is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\mathrm{Acc}_{Q6,T\to L} &\in [0.017295597484276726,\;0.02927509293680297],\\
\Delta\mathrm{AUC}_{Q6,T\to L} &\in [0.015494311048138809,\;0.02573588939607574],\\
\#\{\Delta\mathrm{Acc}>0\} &= 3,\\
\#\{\Delta\mathrm{AUC}>0\} &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism-level calls classify one block as crossing to the localization layer and two blocks as partial.  The aggregate verdict is partial, not universal, because the positive translation-conditioned increments do not establish a uniform biological law across organisms.

\paragraph{Control attenuation.}
The stricter translation-length-amino-acid-GC conditioned increment is nearly null.  Its accuracy increment ranges from $-0.010482180293501064$ to $0.010455390334572545$, with mean $-0.000008929986309506402$ and median $0.0$; only $1$ of $3$ organism blocks remains positive on accuracy.  Its macro-AUC increment is negative in all three blocks, with minimum $-0.002467684182081209$, median $-0.000877887947320577$, mean $-0.001407768101771234$, and maximum $-0.0008777321759119161$.  Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta\mathrm{Acc}_{Q6,T,Z\to L} &\in [-0.010482180293501064,\;0.010455390334572545],\\
\Delta\mathrm{AUC}_{Q6,T,Z\to L} &\in [-0.002467684182081209,\;-0.0008777321759119161],\\
\#\{\Delta\mathrm{AUC}>0\} &= 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the main reason the chapter records a boundary rather than a strong localization claim: after translation, length, amino-acid, and GC controls are imposed, the remaining localization increment is not stable.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite result is a partial, translation-conditioned localization boundary.  The data permit the following bounded statement: on the joined three-organism panel, $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show held-out localization information, and the translation-conditioned increment is positive across all organism blocks before the stricter control bundle is imposed.  The same data refuse the stronger interpretation that $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual geometry directly determines localization, trafficking, compartment identity, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Translation, folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, and universality require their own layer-specific measurements; neither codon residual coordinates nor modeled translation weights can supply those conclusions by notation alone.  Protein abundance is also not a substitute for a localization readout.  The chapter therefore keeps the readback at the finite relation $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$ and records the outcome as partial rather than promoted.
