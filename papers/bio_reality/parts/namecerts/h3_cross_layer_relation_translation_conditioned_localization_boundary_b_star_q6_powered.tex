\subsection{Translation-conditioned localization boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-translation-conditioned-localization-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record studied here is a finite cross-layer surface indexed by coding sequences.  Each row carries a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-layer readout $T_j$, ordinary sequence and transcript controls, and a protein-localization slot $L_k$.  The admissible readback is not the direct edge $q_i \to L_k$.  It is the gated chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow L_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where the first arrow must survive amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density, and matched mRNA controls before the localization slot is interpreted.  This makes the row a boundary object: a residual coordinate may be kept as a candidate localization-relevant feature only after the translation boundary has been respected.

\paragraph{Measured panel.}
The powered finite run joined localization labels for three organisms: $1908$ rows for \textit{Danio rerio}, $10187$ rows for \textit{Homo sapiens}, and $4304$ rows for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The classifier was a standard-library linear discriminant analysis with train-fold z-scoring and a ridge shared covariance estimator, evaluated with $5$ folds under the recorded seed \texttt{sha256:93467a1602ce6c3d589b8b5452f233689fb87fa1bbc0f686694db4bb01da7b1f}.  The run status was passed, but the biological verdict is partial rather than closed.

\paragraph{Direct residual-to-localization discrimination.}
When $B^*_{Q6}$ residual information was tested against localization labels, the held-out accuracy increment over the comparison surface ranged from $-0.008385744234800874$ to $0.045548247766761585$, with mean $0.022068418153776248$ and median $0.029042750929368033$ over $n=3$ organism-level entries.  The corresponding macro-AUC increment was more consistently positive, ranging from $0.073410178078059$ to $0.08299262296923093$, with mean $0.07848683797295208$ and median $0.07905771287156632$.  Thus $2$ of $3$ organism entries were positive by accuracy, while $3$ of $3$ were positive by macro-AUC.  In finite-row notation the observed direct surface can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta {\rm Acc}_{Q6\to L} &\in [-0.008385744234800874,\,0.045548247766761585],\\
\Delta {\rm AUC}_{Q6\to L} &\in [0.073410178078059,\,0.08299262296923093].
\end{aligned}
$$
This is evidence that the residual coordinates contain some held-out localization information, but it is not by itself a localization mechanism and it is not allowed to bypass the translation boundary.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary.}
The translation-conditioned increment was positive on all three organism entries by both accuracy and macro-AUC.  Its accuracy increment ranged from $0.017295597484276726$ to $0.02927509293680297$, with mean $0.023049495216764453$ and median $0.02257779522921366$.  Its macro-AUC increment ranged from $0.015494311048138809$ to $0.02573588939607574$, with mean $0.021063283115180725$ and median $0.021959648901327622$.  Therefore the finite boundary supports the weaker statement that translation-conditioned residual information can cross into a localization readout on this panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta {\rm Acc}_{Q6,T\to L} &\in [0.017295597484276726,\,0.02927509293680297],\\
\Delta {\rm AUC}_{Q6,T\to L} &\in [0.015494311048138809,\,0.02573588939607574],\\
\#\{\Delta {\rm Acc}_{Q6,T\to L}>0\} &= 3,\\
\#\{\Delta {\rm AUC}_{Q6,T\to L}>0\} &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism calls were not uniform: $1$ entry crossed to the localization layer, while $2$ entries remained partial.  The panel therefore gives a boundary-positive but organism-fragile result, not a universal law.

\paragraph{Control-conditioned attenuation.}
After conditioning on translation, length, amino-acid composition, and GC-related controls, the residual increment collapsed.  The accuracy increment ranged from $-0.010482180293501064$ to $0.010455390334572545$, with mean $-0.000008929986309506402$ and median $0.0$.  The macro-AUC increment was negative for all three entries, ranging from $-0.002467684182081209$ to $-0.0008777321759119161$, with mean $-0.001407768101771234$ and median $-0.000877887947320577$.  Only $1$ of $3$ entries was positive by accuracy, and $0$ of $3$ entries was positive by macro-AUC.  In compact form,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta {\rm Acc}_{Q6\mid T,Z\to L} &\in [-0.010482180293501064,\,0.010455390334572545],\\
\Delta {\rm AUC}_{Q6\mid T,Z\to L} &\in [-0.002467684182081209,\,-0.0008777321759119161].
\end{aligned}
$$
The collapse is the main scientific constraint.  It says that the localization signal should be reported as translation-conditioned and control-sensitive.  It does not justify an unconditional residual-to-localization rule.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result is best read as a partial cross-layer boundary.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can help discriminate localization labels in held-out prediction, and a translation-conditioned surface remains positive across the three organism entries.  However, the control-conditioned increment does not remain stable once length, amino-acid composition, and GC-related structure are included.  The correct claim is therefore narrow: $B^*_{Q6}$ may be retained as a finite coordinate for testing $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$ relations, and this run supplies positive but partial evidence for that boundary.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
No translation mechanism follows from the residual coordinate alone.  No protein trafficking mechanism, compartment-identity mechanism, protein folding statement, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this panel.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are not ribosome-occupancy observations, matched mRNA abundance is a control rather than a translation readout, and bulk protein abundance cannot replace a localization measurement.  A stronger claim would require same-organism and same-CDS rows joining codon counts, a nondegenerate translation readout, matched mRNA controls, and a matched localization readout, followed by an explicit survival or mediation statistic for $q_i \to T_j \to L_k$ under the stated controls.
