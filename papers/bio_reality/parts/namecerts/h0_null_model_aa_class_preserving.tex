\paragraph{Claim and finite carrier.}
The packet identified by \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving} concerns the standard-code codon table viewed only at the code-read layer.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read through a codon display in which local tile faces, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes are distinguished before any biological interpretation is attempted.  The reality contact is a curated standard genetic-code table: it assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label, and it supplies no direct observation of translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, cellular function, evolutionary mechanism, or universal necessity.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null comparison.}
The null model preserves amino-acid class information while randomizing the finite codon-label arrangement within that constraint.  In $N=2000$ sampled class-preserving tables with seed $42$, the observed tile size was $20$ for the measured $M$-side statistic, no exact shape match was sampled, and only $7$ samples landed in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size.  Equivalently, the empirical exact-match count was $0/2000$, while the lower-tail frequency was $7/2000=0.0035$.  The packet therefore records
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact,class}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail,class}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is evidence that the observed local codon tile is atypical under the amino-acid-class-preserving finite null.  It is not evidence that the geometry alone performs translation or explains protein structure.

\paragraph{Comparison with the unconstrained shape sample.}
A related finite shape-significance run used $N=2000$ distinct samples, seed $42$, and $57377$ total sample attempts, of which $55377$ were duplicate retries.  That run found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, yielding $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$ and $p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125$.  The class-preserving null is therefore stricter in the exact-shape slot for this packet: the sampled exact count drops from $1$ in the broader shape run to $0$ in the amino-acid-class-preserving run, while the lower-tail statistic remains small at $0.0035$.

\paragraph{Local verdict and boundary.}
The finite evidence supports the claim that the window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern whose amino-acid-class-preserving null behavior is atypical in the sampled comparison.  The curated standard-code table is sufficient for codon-to-label readback, for the $13$-codon residual set, for the punctured-cube face-vector $(13,16,4)$, for the spectral values above, and for the leave-one stability tests.  It is not a structure assay, a translation experiment, a physical-admissibility measurement, a function test, a mechanism witness, or a universality theorem.  Any later claim about folding, protein realization, organism-level function, or universal biological necessity must be supplied by a separate contact that observes that higher layer directly.
