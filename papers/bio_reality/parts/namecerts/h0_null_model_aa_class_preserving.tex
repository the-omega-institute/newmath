\subsection{Amino-acid-class-preserving null model for the codon tile}

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The object under inspection is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The finite carrier is the full codon cube $Q_6$, with the ordinary curated codon-to-label table used only as a readout contact.  The row of interest isolates a local tile boundary inside this display.  Its exceptional set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly the following finite list:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records this set as a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the claim is not that codons are governed by the display as a biological necessity.  The claim is narrower: after fixing the curated standard-code labels, this finite tile boundary has a reproducible shape and a reproducible label-class behavior under the stated probes.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null.}
The null experiment preserves amino-acid class information while randomizing the finite codon-label arrangement under the stated sampling rule.  In $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$, the observed tile size was $|M|=20$.  The exact-shape count was $0$, giving an exact-shape empirical value $p=0.0$.  The lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Pr_{\mathrm{null}}(\hbox{exact observed shape}) &= 0/2000 = 0.0,\\
\Pr_{\mathrm{null}}(|M_{\mathrm{sample}}|\leq 20) &= 7/2000 = 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers say that the observed finite shape is not typical under this amino-acid-class-preserving comparison.  They do not say that the display mechanism causes translation, folding, activity, or organism-level behavior.  The experiment tests a code-layer label split, not a molecular pathway.

The spectral row gives the same finite object a numerical profile: $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, and $\mu=3.051487585298128$. These values are useful as internal diagnostics of the finite display: they bind the tile, the residual set, and the radial statistic to one measured configuration.  They are not evidence for protein conformation or biochemical admissibility.

These are code-read statistics: they evaluate how much of the held-out codon-label behavior is recovered by the finite tile and its boundary. \paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite rows support the following bounded conclusion.  In the six-coordinate codon display, the curated standard-code labels expose a local boundary tile whose residual set has size $13$, punctured-cube profile $(13,16,4)$, non-null amino-acid-class-preserving behavior, and strong leave-one recovery at the code-read level.  The exact-shape value $0/2000$ and lower-tail value $7/2000$ are the decisive values for the named null model.  The surrounding shape, spectrum, family, and module rows make the record reproducible as finite data rather than as a verbal resemblance.  The contact remains the curated codon-label table: it does not include a translation experiment, a folding assay, a physical admissibility test, a function assay, a mechanism claim, or a universality theorem.  Any assertion at those higher layers would require a separate contact of the appropriate kind.
