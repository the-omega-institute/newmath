\paragraph{Scope.}
The empirical contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, treated as a source of codon-to-label assignments. That contact can test code-layer readback and local split behavior. It cannot test translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality. Amino-acid label readback is therefore not protein structure, and a local codon tile is not a global biological law. Any future claim crossing from code labels to translation, folding, physical feasibility, organismal function, or universal mechanism would require a separate contact that observes that higher layer directly.
