\subsection{Amino-acid-class preserving null test for the window-six codon tile}\label{sec:bior-h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim being tested.}
The finite object in this record is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The biological contact is deliberately narrow: a curated standard-code table assigns each codon to an amino-acid label or a stop label.  The claim tested here is not that this coordinate display explains translation.  It is that the observed local codon tile remains unusually shaped even after the null model preserves amino-acid class sizes.  Thus the test asks whether the window-six boundary pattern is visible beyond a label-class preserving reshuffle of the table.

\paragraph{Observed finite row.}
The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$.
As a punctured cube, this row has face-vector $(13,16,4)$: thirteen occupied codon vertices, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces.  The companion observed support $M$ has size $20$.  The present finite row supplies the size of $M$ but not its codon-by-codon listing, so the comparison below uses the supplied support size and the explicit $R$ list.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
The null ensemble used $N=2000$ randomized tables with seed $42$, preserving amino-acid class structure while testing the same local shape criterion.  In that ensemble, the exact observed shape appeared $0$ times.  The estimated exact-shape probability is therefore $p=0.0$ at the displayed Monte Carlo resolution.  The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
N&=2000,\\
|M|&=20,\\
\#\{\text{exact matches}\}&=0,\\
\#\{\text{null rows with size at most }20\}&=7,\\
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}&=7/2000=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports the finite claim that the observed codon-tile shape is not typical under the amino-acid-class preserving null model.  The conclusion is a code-layer statement about codon labels and local coordinate geometry, not a statement about molecular realization.

\paragraph{Comparison with the unconstrained shape sample.}
A separate finite shape sample with $N=2000$ and seed $42$ required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and incurred $55377$ duplicate retries.  It produced $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  The corresponding probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact\ shape}}&=1/2000=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=25/2000=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid-class preserving null is therefore stricter in the sense relevant to the present row: once amino-acid label-class information is held fixed, the exact observed local tile still does not recur in the $2000$ sampled alternatives, and the lower-tail statistic remains small at $0.0035$.

\paragraph{Spectral finite check.}
The same finite packet records the spectral values
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value reported as the cubic-polynomial residual.  This is numerically consistent with the stated finite spectral relation at the tolerance used by the row.  The spectral check is internal to the displayed codon-coordinate packet: it certifies a numerical relation among the finite matrices or operators used for the tile, but it does not identify a biochemical energy, a folding score, or a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code and leave-one-codon probes.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$.  The hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$, the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$.  The leave-one-codon-out row reported anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These rows say that the finite tile retains strong local recovery behavior when one code table or one codon is withheld.  They do not say that a ribosome, tRNA population, codon-usage pressure, or organism-specific evolutionary path has been reconstructed.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The reality contact for this row is the curated standard-code table.  That contact can test codon-to-label readback and local label splits.  It cannot test translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, functional realization, or a universal biological law.  Consequently, the accepted conclusion is only this: in the supplied finite row, the window-six codon coordinate exposes a statistically unusual local boundary tile under an amino-acid-class preserving null model, with $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0$ and $p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=0.0035$ over $2000$ null samples.  No claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality follows from this packet unless another independent empirical contact supplies that higher-layer observation.
