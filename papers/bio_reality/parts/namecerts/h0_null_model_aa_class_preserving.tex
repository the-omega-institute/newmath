\subsection{Amino-acid-class preserving null model for the local codon tile}\label{subsec:h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The record studies a six-coordinate codon display whose observable readout is the standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, used here only as a codon-indexing geometry. The relevant distinguished set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\\
&\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Its punctured-cube summary has $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$: thirteen vertices, sixteen local edges, and four local square faces in the induced tile. The corresponding class-sized region $M$ is represented in this row by its observed size, $|M|=20$, rather than by a printed member list. Thus the finite claim is not that geometry alone creates a biological code, but that the standard-code labels, once read on $Q_6$, single out a sharply constrained local tile with a boundary set $R$ and a size-$20$ companion region $M$.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
The decisive null row preserves amino-acid label classes while randomizing within the finite standard-code constraints used by the experiment. In $N=2000$ sampled tables with seed $42$, the exact tile shape appeared $0$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}&=0.0,\\
 \#\{\mathrm{exact\ matches}\}&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same null also records a lower-tail comparison against the observed $M$ size. Only $7$ of the $2000$ sampled rows had size at most the observed value $20$, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}&=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035,\\
 |M|_{\mathrm{observed}}&=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the row-specific reason for keeping the claim as a local code-read result: even after amino-acid classes are respected by the null, the observed finite tile is rare in exact shape and rare in the lower tail of the companion-size statistic.

\paragraph{Independent shape and spectral checks.}
A separate shape-significance row sampled $N=2000$ cases, with $57377$ distinct sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries. It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p_{\mathrm{exact\ shape}}&=0.0005,\\
 p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The spectral row gives a second finite invariant of the same geometry. The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
 \lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
 \mu&=3.051487585298128,
\end{aligned}
$$
with radial polynomial value $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$ and the same residual for the cubic relation. This residual is numerically at roundoff scale for the recorded computation, so the spectral datum functions as a finite consistency check on the tile, not as an independent biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Leave-one readback rows.}
The leave-one-code experiment evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets. Its baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline was $0.9841269841269841$. The hit rate inside $M-T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. The leave-one-codon row records $0$ anchors, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure cases, $8$ predictable cases, and recovery rate $1.0$. These rows support a local readback interpretation: when one code row or one codon is held out, the finite tile and its boundary recover a large fraction of the omitted code-layer labels.

\paragraph{Module accounting.}
The module classification is complete for the same $13$ codons: $R$ has size $13$, the assigned count is $13$, and there are no extra module codons, no codons missing from the modules, and no unassigned codons. The modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR}&=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\},&
\mathrm{AR\_LYS}&=\{\mathrm{AAA}\},\\
\mathrm{CUN}&=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\},&
\mathrm{ILE\_MET}&=\{\mathrm{AUA}\},\\
\mathrm{SSN}&=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\},&
\mathrm{STOP\_TRP}&=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every listed module has a predictable-or-boundary status in the row. The by-family leave-one-code summary has $5$ families, with family hit rates above baseline $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast and $1.0$ for bacterial plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate families.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
The contact used here is a curated standard genetic-code table. It supplies codon-to-label assignments and therefore permits code-layer readback, local split checks, and finite null-model comparisons. It does not observe translation in a cell, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal biological law. Consequently the finite rows justify the narrower statement that the standard-code labels, read on the six-coordinate codon cube, produce a rare amino-acid-class-preserving local tile with boundary codons $R$ and a size-$20$ companion region $M$. Any claim about translation realization, folded structure, admissible biophysics, function, mechanism, or universality requires a separate observational contact of the appropriate type.
