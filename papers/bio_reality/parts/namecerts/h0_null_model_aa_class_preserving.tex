\subsection{Amino-acid-class-preserving null model for the window-six codon tile}\label{sec:bior-h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate object.}
The record works at the code-read layer of the standard genetic-code table.  The finite carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a binary display of the three nucleotide positions after the usual purine--pyrimidine and strong--weak coordinate choices have been fixed.  The observed boundary region $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row identifies this set as a punctured-cube boundary with face data
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite object under audit is not a whole biological mechanism.  It is a local labeled pattern in a codon-coordinate cube, with singleton label corners and three-point label classes exposed by the standard-code label table.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape data.}
The spectral row gives a numerical separation between the observed module size and the boundary region.  The recorded values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated radial cubic check has residual
$$
\begin{aligned}
-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12},
\end{aligned}
$$
which is within the recorded numerical tolerance.  In the unrestricted shape comparison with $N=2000$ sampled tables and seed $42$, there was $1$ exact shape match and $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$.  The corresponding empirical probabilities were
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These figures say that the displayed local shape is uncommon under the sampled comparison ensemble.  They do not say that codon biology is forced by the coordinate geometry.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null row.}
The claim-specific null model preserves amino-acid label classes and asks whether the same local geometry appears after that constraint is kept.  In $N=2000$ trials with seed $42$, the observed module size was $|M|=20$.  The exact-match count was $0$, so the empirical exact-shape probability was recorded as
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact}}=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the strongest finite-row evidence in the packet: even after amino-acid class preservation, the observed window-six local tile is rare in the sampled null distribution.  The conclusion remains a code-table conclusion.  It concerns codon-to-label readback and local boundary structure, not translation dynamics or molecular realization.

\paragraph{Leave-one-out checks.}
The leave-one-code-out row evaluated $23$ code tables and $63$ total targets.  The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$, while the recorded rates were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{hit}(M\setminus T) &= 0.9365079365079365,\\
\mathrm{hit}(M\setminus T\ \mathrm{plus\ boundary}) &= 0.9841269841269841,\\
\mathrm{hit\ above\ baseline} &= 0.9841269841269841.
\end{aligned}
$$
By family, the hit rates above baseline were $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial or plastid, candidate other, mitochondrial, and nuclear ciliate families.  The leave-one-codon-out row recorded $8$ predictable codons, $5$ boundary codons, no anchors, and recovery rate $1.0$.  Its high-coverage count was $8$.  These rows support the claim that the local tile has predictive stability under finite deletion probes at the code-label level.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The boundary codons are completely assigned to six named modules.  The recorded partition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The classification row reports $R$ size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing boundary codons, and no unassigned codons.  Hence the finite boundary is closed under this module accounting.  The statement is deliberately narrow: the table labels are used as curated contacts, and the module names are bookkeeping for the finite codon set, not evidence for folds, functions, or universal mechanisms.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, whose observed fact is that codons are assigned amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact can test code-read claims and local codon-tile label splits.  It cannot test translation realization, structural order, physical admissibility, folded protein structure, biological function, or a general law of living systems.  Therefore the finite evidence licenses the following local verdict: the window-six coordinate display exposes a rare and deletion-stable boundary-tile pattern in the standard-code labels, including the $13$-codon punctured-cube region and the $20$-size observed module row under the amino-acid-class-preserving null.  No claim above the code-read layer follows unless a separate empirical contact is added for that higher layer.
