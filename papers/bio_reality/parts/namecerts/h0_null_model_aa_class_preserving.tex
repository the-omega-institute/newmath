$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}

This is the row-specific evidence for the claim: even after the null ensemble is constrained to respect amino-acid class, the observed standard-code tile is not reproduced in the tested finite sample, and its size statistic lies in a small lower tail.
