\subsection{Amino-Acid-Class Preserving Null Model for the Window-Six Codon Tile}

\paragraph{Claim packet.}
This record concerns the finite codon-window claim identified by \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}.  The object under inspection is the standard-code codon table read at the code layer: each of the sixty-four codons is treated as a six-bit window coordinate together with its curated amino-acid or stop label.  The mathematical question is not whether the genetic code is generated by the window geometry.  The question is narrower: after the observed labels are fixed as a finite contact with biological data, does the local window-six tile remain unusual when compared with randomizations that preserve amino-acid class information?

The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$.  Its relevant distinctions are the six-bit codon display, the local tile face, the singleton label corner, and the three-point label class.  The internal operations used here are coordinate extraction, closure in the finite cube, spectral readout, and relation comparison against the curated label table.  The biological readback is only the standard-code codon-to-label assignment.  No mechanistic, translational, structural, functional, or universal biological conclusion is part of the claim.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null row.}
The null experiment preserves amino-acid class structure while testing whether the observed local shape reappears under the constrained randomization.  With seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled null tables, the observed marked set size was
$$
\begin{aligned}
|M_{\mathrm{obs}}| &= 20.
\end{aligned}
$$
The exact-shape count under the amino-acid-class preserving null was zero:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{exact null matches}\} &= 0,\\
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail comparison counted seven null samples with marked-set size at most the observed value:
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers say that the observed code-layer tile is not a typical outcome of the tested amino-acid-class preserving shuffle.  They do not say that the biological code is forced by the window cube.  The null row compares finite labeled tables; it does not observe ribosomes, tRNA charging, protein folding, evolutionary selection, biochemical feasibility, or organism-level function.

\paragraph{Boundary of the record.}
The data contact is a curated standard-code label table.  It records codon-to-label assignments and permits finite checks about local label splits.  It does not observe translation realization, folded structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, evolutionary cause, or a universal biological law.  An amino-acid label sequence is still a code-layer readback, not a protein structure.  A CUN, UUR, AGR, stop/Trp, or Ile/Met pattern is still a codon-label pattern unless a separate matched contact records the higher-layer phenomenon.  Therefore the correct conclusion is deliberately narrow: the standard-code labels exhibit a rare and transferable local window-six boundary pattern under the stated finite tests, and all claims above the code-read layer remain blocked until independent biological contacts are attached.
