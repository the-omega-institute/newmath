\subsection{Amino-acid-class-preserving null model for the codon window}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The claim tested here is that the six-coordinate codon window does not merely recover a large anonymous subset of the standard code table. It recovers a local boundary pattern whose shape is not explained by a null model that keeps the amino-acid label classes fixed while randomizing the codon placement within those classes. The biological contact used here is only the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels. The finite object under study is therefore a code-layer table: codons, labels, and local window relations. No statement in this record asserts translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biochemical function, a causal mechanism, or universality across biological systems.

\paragraph{Finite codon display.}
The coordinate carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read through the standard codon alphabet. The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
      &UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite computation identifies $R$ as a punctured-cube shape with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed boundary is not being described only by its size. It carries a local tile structure: thirteen codon vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces in the induced cubical display.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null.}
The null experiment preserves amino-acid class sizes and samples alternative codon placements under that constraint. With seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled tables, the observed distinguished set has size $20$. The exact observed shape occurred $0$ times:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{exact class-preserving matches}\} &= 0,\\
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail statistic counts samples whose corresponding distinguished size is at most the observed value. This happened $7$ times among $2000$ samples, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{lower-tail samples}\} &= 7,\\
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}} &= 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
Under this finite-row null, preserving amino-acid label classes does not make the observed local codon-window shape typical. The result is a code-table regularity test, not a biochemical explanation.

\paragraph{Unconstrained shape comparison.}
A separate shape comparison sampled $N=2000$ distinct alternatives with seed $42$. Producing those distinct samples required $57377$ attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries. The exact shape appeared once, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact\ shape}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
A relaxed comparison using Jaccard threshold $0.9$ found $25$ matches, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two null checks point in the same direction: the observed local codon-window shape is rare both as an exact finite shape and as a high-overlap shape, and it remains rare when amino-acid class preservation is imposed.

The same finite row records three scalar spectral quantities for the local construction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated cubic residual is numerically
$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
which is the same recorded value as the radial polynomial residual. In this chapter these numbers serve only as finite spectral readouts of the codon-window construction. They do not by themselves identify a molecular force, a kinetic pathway, or a protein-structure constraint.

\paragraph{Leave-one-code-out behavior.}
The cross-table code-read probe evaluated $23$ tables and $63$ targets. The baseline hit rate was $0.19821739130434785$. The hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ was $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ together with the boundary was $0.9841269841269841$. The recorded hit rate above baseline was also $0.9841269841269841$. These values support the narrow statement that the codon-window coordinates retain predictive code-layer information about omitted code entries. They do not authorize promotion from codon labels to translation machinery, folded conformation, admissible chemistry, or biological function.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon-out behavior.}
The codon-level omission probe records $0$ anchor codons, $5$ boundary codons, $8$ high-coverage closure codons, and $8$ predictable codons. The recovery rate is $1.0$. This means that, for the tested omitted codons, the local window construction recovered the relevant code-layer position through predictable or boundary behavior. The interpretation is intentionally local: recovery means label-table recovery within the finite codon display, not recovery of protein structure or organism-level behavior.

The family-wise leave-one-code-out record contains $5$ families. The hit rates above baseline are

These numbers are evidence that the finite code-read signal persists across the tested family grouping. They remain label-table statistics. They do not assert that the same window geometry is a universal biological law, nor that CUN, UUR, stop, tryptophan, arginine, lysine, isoleucine, or methionine behavior has been explained at the level of tRNA abundance, translation kinetics, or folded structure.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The module classifier accounts for all $13$ codons of $R$: assigned count $13$, no missing codons, no unassigned codons, and no extra module codons. The recorded modules are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agr} &= \{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{ar\_lys} &= \{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{cun} &= \{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{ile\_met} &= \{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{ssn} &= \{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{stop\_trp} &= \{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every module has predictable or boundary support in the finite record. This gives a complete code-layer partition of the distinguished boundary codons. The partition is not a claim about folded proteins, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or a global mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The reality contact for this chapter is the curated standard-code table. It supplies codon-to-label assignments and thereby permits local split checks, class-preserving randomization tests, spectral summaries of the finite coordinate construction, leave-one-out code-read probes, and module classification. It does not observe translation, folding, molecular stability, organismal function, or cross-system necessity. Any future claim crossing one of those boundaries must attach a separate contact at the relevant layer. Until such a contact is supplied, the conclusion is deliberately limited: the six-coordinate codon window exposes a rare and internally structured code-layer boundary pattern, and the amino-acid-class-preserving null model does not explain it away.
