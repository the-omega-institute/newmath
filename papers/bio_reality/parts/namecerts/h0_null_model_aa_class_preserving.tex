\subsection{Amino-acid-class preserving null for the window-six codon tile}
\label{subsec:bioreality-aa-class-preserving-null}

\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The claim \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving} tests whether the local window-six codon geometry survives a stricter null model that preserves amino-acid label classes.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube, read only at the code-label layer through a curated standard-code table.  The record is therefore about finite codon labels and local boundary shape, not about translation as a cellular event.  The tested remainder set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
As a punctured-cube object this same set has the finite face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed companion size is $|M|=20$.  The row data do not list the individual members of $M$ in this packet, so the chapter uses its supplied size and the supplied shape statistics rather than inferring an unrecorded membership list.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null result.}
The amino-acid-class preserving randomization used $N=2000$ sampled tables with seed $42$.  Under that null there were no exact shape matches:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\text{exact matches}\}=0,\qquad p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}}=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row separates the observed code-label geometry from a label-class-preserving null at the code-read level: preserving amino-acid classes alone did not reproduce the exact local shape in the sampled ensemble, and only $0.35\%$ of the sampled rows landed in the lower tail at or below the observed $M$ size.  The associated broader shape run, also at $N=2000$ and seed $42$, required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and rejected $55377$ duplicate retries; it found $1$ exact shape match with $p=0.0005$ and $25$ high-Jaccard matches with $p=0.0125$ for the threshold $J\geq 0.9$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The scientific content of the row is a bounded one.  The standard-code table supplies codon-to-amino-acid or stop labels, and the window-six coordinates expose a finite local tile whose boundary set, modules, null-model behavior, and spectral summaries are nontrivial in the supplied records.  The amino-acid-class preserving null is especially informative because it asks whether the same local shape appears after retaining label-class information; in $2000$ sampled rows it produced no exact shape match and a lower-tail probability of $0.0035$ at the observed $M$ size.  That is evidence for a finite code-layer separation, not evidence for a general law of living systems.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
No result in this chapter asserts translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  The curated code table is a contact for label readback only.  Amino-acid labels are not protein conformations, the local codon tile is not a translation apparatus, and CUN, UUR, stop, tryptophan, isoleucine, methionine, arginine, lysine, serine, or leucine module names do not by themselves supply organism-matched structure evidence.  Any statement about folding, structure, function, physical admissibility, translation, or global biological necessity would require a separate observation at that layer.  In the absence of such a contact, the ready claim is limited to the finite local codon-label geometry and its amino-acid-class preserving null result.
