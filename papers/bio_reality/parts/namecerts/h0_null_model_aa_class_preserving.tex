\subsection{Amino-acid-class preserving null model for the local codon tile}
\label{sec:h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}

\paragraph{Finite packet.}
This record studies a six-coordinate codon display in which the observed object is the standard-code codon table, read only as a codon-to-label assignment.  The carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with coordinates supplied by the six-bit codon presentation.  The distinguished region $R$ is the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The verified cardinality is $|R|=13$.  The same finite audit identifies $R$ with a punctured-cube shape whose face-vector data are
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(R)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the object under test is not the whole codon table and not a biochemical mechanism; it is a local finite subconfiguration with codon labels attached from the curated standard code.

\paragraph{Spectral and shape data.}
The spectral computation separates the marked local tile from its complement through the reported values
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M &= 0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R &= 0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu &= 3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}
$$
The radial cubic residual was $-6.224354365258478\cdot 10^{-12}$, so the stated polynomial relation is numerically satisfied to the recorded tolerance.  An independent shape randomization with $N=2000$ samples and seed $42$ found one exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard score at least $0.9$.  Equivalently,
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9} &= 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  These numbers support a finite rarity statement for the displayed local shape under that sampling protocol.  They do not, by themselves, identify a translation process, an evolutionary cause, a folded protein state, or a physical admissibility criterion.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
The claim indexed here is the amino-acid-class preserving null test.  The null sampling keeps the amino-acid class structure fixed while randomizing the code-level placement relevant to the local tile.  In the verified run, $N=2000$, seed $42$, the observed marked size was
$$
\begin{aligned}
|M|=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
There were no exact matches to the observed tile shape among the $2000$ amino-acid-class preserving samples:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\mathrm{exact\ matches}\} &= 0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}} &= 0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
At this finite Monte Carlo resolution, the value $0.0$ means that the event was not observed in the sampled population; it is not a proof that the exact probability is mathematically zero.  The lower-tail statistic counted $7$ samples with size at most the observed marked size, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The chapter therefore records a concrete empirical contrast: after preserving the amino-acid class partition, the observed local arrangement remains rare in the reported finite sample.

This gives a finite module-level accounting of the local code-read phenomenon: the thirteen-codon region is not an anonymous subset, but decomposes into named codon modules with complete coverage under the packet's classification rule.

\paragraph{Reality contact and local boundary.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard-code table assigning codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  That contact is sufficient for checking a codon-label split and for comparing the local tile against finite null models.  It is not a measurement of translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or universality across organisms.  Consequently, the correct conclusion is narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a statistically unusual local boundary-tile pattern under the recorded amino-acid-class preserving null model, while the labels themselves remain imported from the curated code table.  Any claim about protein structure, function, physical viability, translational mechanism, or universal biological necessity would require a separate contact carrying that kind of observation.
