\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
The record concerns the standard-code codon table read as a finite labeled display on the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  The coordinates are not treated as a biological cause.  They provide a bounded display on which local faces, singleton corners, and three-point label classes can be inspected against the curated codon-to-label table.  The observed boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite row identifies this set as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the tested object is not a vague geometric analogy: the row fixes the participating codons, the number of vertices, the number of local adjacencies or two-dimensional faces recorded by the pipeline, and the table-contact layer at which labels are read.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving randomization.}
The null model for this claim keeps the amino-acid class information available to the sampling procedure and asks whether the observed local shape is still ordinary under that constraint.  With seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled class-preserving rows, the observed $M$-region has size $20$.  The exact local shape occurred $0$ times in the class-preserving sample, so the reported exact-shape probability is
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$ out of $2000$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class,lower}}=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite-row content of the claim: even after the null model is allowed to preserve amino-acid class structure, the observed window-six local tile is not reproduced exactly in the sample, and the observed $M$ size lies in a small lower tail of the sampled distribution.

\paragraph{Independent shape and spectrum rows.}
A separate shape-significance row, also at $N=2000$ and seed $42$, reports $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  In that sample the exact shape appeared once, with $p=0.0005$, while the high-overlap threshold $J\geq 0.9$ appeared $25$ times, with $p=0.0125$.  These values are consistent with the class-preserving null result: the local tile is finite and reproducible as a rare shape event, not merely as an artifact of reading a single table entry.

The spectral row records the numerical profile of the same finite decomposition.  The reported values are

The spectrum is used here only as a finite diagnostic of the displayed codon partition.  It does not turn the coordinate pattern into a law of translation or a mechanism of molecular realization.

\paragraph{Scientific reading.}
The data support a bounded claim about the standard-code label table: window-six coordinates expose a non-generic local tile, and that tile remains rare under an amino-acid-class-preserving null model.  The curated table supplies the codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop labels needed for this readback.  It does not supply translation dynamics, ribosomal realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or organism-independent necessity.  Those would require separate contacts with the relevant biological layer.  Consequently the result is a code-layer boundary statement: the finite codon table displays a structured local split, while all stronger molecular and functional interpretations remain outside the claim.
