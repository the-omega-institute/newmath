\subsection{Amino-acid-class-preserving null model for the window-six codon tile}
\label{sec:h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}

\paragraph{Claim being tested.}
The claim concerns a six-coordinate codon display of the standard genetic code.  The biological contact used here is a curated standard-code table: each codon is assigned an amino-acid label or a stop label, and the finite computation asks whether the distinguished local tile can be recovered as more than a generic consequence of preserving amino-acid label classes.  The claim is deliberately restricted to the code-read layer.  It is not a claim about translation dynamics, folded protein shape, physical admissibility, organismal function, a biochemical mechanism, or a universal law.  Those higher layers would require different observations than a codon-to-label table.

\paragraph{Finite carrier and observed boundary set.}
The carrier is the $64$-codon cube represented by six-bit codon coordinates.  In the observed standard-code readback, the distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  The finite row gives the exact membership
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records that this set has the punctured-cube face vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed object is not merely a count: it is a finite local surface with thirteen codon vertices, sixteen codon-neighbor edges, and four filled square faces in the window-six coordinate graph.

\paragraph{Spectral finite check.}
The associated spectral calculation reports

This is at numerical tolerance scale for the run, so the spectral row supports the statement that the observed window-six tile is internally coherent as a coordinate object.  It does not, by itself, identify a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Unrestricted shape-null comparison.}
Against the broader shape null, the computation used $N=2000$ samples with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  The exact shape occurred once, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005.
\end{aligned}
$$
The high-overlap event with Jaccard score at least $0.9$ occurred $25$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values make the observed shape uncommon in that finite sampling regime, while still tying the conclusion to the coordinate-and-label experiment rather than to an unrestricted biological interpretation.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null.}
The claim identifier for this row is \texttt{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}.  In the amino-acid-class-preserving null experiment, the run again used $N=2000$ and seed $42$.  The observed $M$ size was $20$.  The finite row does not list the codon membership of $M$, so the present statement uses the supplied size and the supplied null counts rather than inventing a missing list.  The exact match count was $0$, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, yielding
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite conclusion is therefore sharper than a plain label-preserving coincidence statement: after preserving amino-acid classes, the sampled null did not reproduce the exact observed shape, and only seven of two thousand trials reached the lower tail at the observed $M$ size.

The finite row reports a hit rate above baseline of $0.9841269841269841$, a hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ of $0.9365079365079365$, and a hit rate inside $M\setminus T$ plus boundary of $0.9841269841269841$.  Separately, the leave-one-codon-out row reports anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, high closure coverage $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$.  These checks support the local robustness of the code-read tile: withheld entries are commonly recovered by the finite coordinate closure and boundary apparatus.  They do not show that ribosomes, tRNAs, protein folds, or cellular fitness are controlled by this geometry.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is that the standard-code label table, read through window-six codon coordinates, presents a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary with nontrivial shape, spectral, null-model, leave-one, family, and module checks.  The amino-acid-class-preserving null is the most relevant negative control in this row: preserving amino-acid classes alone did not reproduce the exact shape in $2000$ samples, and the lower-tail event occurred with frequency $0.0035$.  The scientifically usable conclusion is therefore local and conditional: the code table supports a distinctive codon-label tile at the code-read layer.

\paragraph{Local exclusions.}
The same data also fix the boundary of interpretation.  A curated standard-code table observes codon labels, not translation realization.  It does not observe folded structure, energetic or physical admissibility, cellular function, organismal mechanism, or universality across biology.  Amino-acid label readback is not protein structure, and a local codon tile is not a global biological law.  Any promotion beyond the code-read layer must be attached to a separate contact that directly observes the promoted layer.
