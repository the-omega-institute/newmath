\subsection{Amino-acid-class preserving null model for the window-six codon tile}
\label{subsec:bior-reality-h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}

\paragraph{Claim scope.}
This record concerns the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$.  The finite object under inspection is not translation itself, nor a biochemical mechanism.  It is a code-layer readback: codons are displayed as six-bit coordinates, a local tile is selected by the coordinate rule, and the selected codons are compared with curated amino-acid and stop labels.  The biological contact used here is only the curated standard-code assignment of codons to labels.  Consequently, the row can test whether the observed label split has a distinctive finite shape under the stated coordinate display, but it cannot by itself assert ribosomal realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, a universal mechanism, or a law of life.  Those assertions would require separate contacts beyond the codon-label table.

\paragraph{Finite codon boundary.}
The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In codon notation it is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row identifies $R$ as a punctured-cube boundary with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the contact is not a vague enrichment statement: it specifies thirteen codons, sixteen one-dimensional incidences, and four two-dimensional faces inside the local six-bit display.  The codons in $R$ split into six named modules, and the module inventory is complete: $\mathrm{AGR}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{CUN}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{ile\_met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{SSN}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$.  The module check reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no missing module codons, no extra module codons, and no unassigned codons.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class preserving null.}
The null model preserves amino-acid class while randomizing the remaining finite arrangement.  Against $N=2000$ sampled class-preserving alternatives with seed $42$, the observed tile has $|M|=20$.  The exact shape count is $0$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}&=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count for samples with size at most the observed $M$-size is $7$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The paired significance fields record the same two values, $0.0$ for exact class-preserving shape agreement and $0.0035$ for the lower-tail event.  Within this finite null, the standard-code tile is therefore not reproduced by an amino-acid-class preserving shuffle in the exact-shape slot, and only seven of two thousand samples lie in the reported lower tail.  This is evidence for a constrained code-layer arrangement, not for a biochemical cause.

\paragraph{Shape comparison outside the class-preserving null.}
A broader shape-sampling row used $N=2000$ with seed $42$.  It required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries, recording one exact shape match and twenty-five matches at Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact\ shape}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
The duplicate-retry count is part of the finite row and matters for interpretation: the search space contains repeated proposals under the sampling procedure, but the reported denominator remains the two thousand distinct sampled alternatives.  The exact and high-Jaccard rates agree with the class-preserving result in direction: the observed standard-code tile is rare under the sampled finite alternatives.

By family, there are five families: alternative yeast has hit rate $0.8333333333333334$, while bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate each have hit rate $1.0$ above baseline.  The leave-one-codon-out row gives anchor count $0$, boundary count $5$, closure coverage high $8$, predictable count $8$, and recovery rate $1.0$, again with the threshold comparison recorded as $1.0$.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following code-layer conclusion.  In the six-bit codon display, the standard-code label table determines a local boundary tile with a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary, a complete six-module decomposition, a stable spectral signature, rare exact agreement under broad sampling, and no exact agreement in the amino-acid-class preserving null sample of size $2000$.  The strongest row for this claim is the class-preserving null: exact match count $0$ and lower-tail probability $0.0035$ for observed $|M|=20$.  The conclusion is deliberately local.  A codon-to-label table can certify a label split, and the finite probes can measure how unusual that split is under the stated nulls.  The same data do not certify translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.  Any such promotion would have to be grounded by an additional observation whose resolution is not merely codon label readback.
