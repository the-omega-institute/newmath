\subsection{Amino-acid-class-preserving null row for the local codon tile}\label{sec:bioreality-h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record scope.}
This BioReality record concerns the standard-code codon table only at the level of codon-label readback.  The coordinate carrier is the six-coordinate codon cube $Q_6$, read as a finite display of the sixty-four RNA codons with curated amino-acid or stop labels attached by a standard-code table.  The row does not treat those labels as a mechanism for translation, does not infer folded protein structure, and does not promote a local combinatorial pattern to physical admissibility, function, or universality.  Its purpose is narrower: it asks whether a local window-six boundary pattern remains unusual when the randomization is constrained to preserve amino-acid class counts.

\paragraph{Finite boundary set.}
The finite row $h0.R.cardinality.13$ records a boundary set $R$ with size $13$.  The codons are
$$\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA, AGA, AGG, AUA, CUA, CUC, CUG, CUU,\\
&UAA, UAG, UCA, UGA, UUA\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The companion row $h0.R.punctured.cube$ identifies the same set as a punctured-cube boundary pattern with face-vector data
$$\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the finite object being tested is not an unrestricted codon-table summary.  It is a specific local subset inside the six-bit codon display, together with a small amount of incidence structure: thirteen vertices, sixteen one-dimensional adjacencies, and four two-dimensional faces.

\paragraph{Observed module decomposition.}
The module-classification row $h1.leave_one_codon_out.module_classification$ partitions the same thirteen codons into six named modules:
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AGR}&=\{AGA,AGG\},\\
\mathrm{AR\_Lys}&=\{AAA\},\\
\mathrm{CUN}&=\{CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU\},\\
\mathrm{Ile\_Met}&=\{AUA\},\\
\mathrm{SSN}&=\{UCA,UUA\},\\
\mathrm{Stop\_Trp}&=\{UAA,UAG,UGA\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The same row reports $R\_size=13$, $assigned\_count=13$, no extra module codons, no missing codons from the modules, and no unassigned codons.  This matters for the null model because the tested object is not an arbitrary pile of codons after the fact; every element of $R$ is accounted for by the six listed modules.  The module result is a finite coverage statement over this codon boundary, not evidence for a biological process beyond code-label assignment.

\paragraph{Spectral coordinates.}
The spectral row $h0.spectrum$ records three scalar coordinates for the local object:
$$\begin{aligned}
\lambda_M&=0.6752479308830213,\\
\lambda_R&=0.4758352843197002,\\
\mu&=3.051487585298128.
\end{aligned}$$
The radial polynomial residual is $-6.224354365258478\times 10^{-12}$, the same value recorded for the cubic-polynomial check.  Within the numerical tolerance of the finite computation, the spectral row therefore behaves as an internally coherent coordinate summary of the tile.  It is a coordinate and spectrum check, not a claim that the genetic code is forced by this geometry.

\paragraph{Unconstrained shape comparison.}
The row $h0.M.shape.significance$ gives a broader shape comparison with $N=2000$ sampled alternatives, seed $42$, $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, and $55377$ duplicate retries.  It records one exact shape match and twenty-five samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$.  The corresponding values are
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}$$
These values say that, under that sampling procedure, the observed local shape is rare in the sampled population.  They do not by themselves identify a translation mechanism, a structural motif, or a functional advantage.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null.}
The focal row $h0.null.model.aa_class_preserving$ tightens the comparison by preserving amino-acid class structure in the null model.  It reports $N=2000$, seed $42$, observed $M$-size $20$, exact-match count $0$, and lower-tail count $7$ for sampled rows with size at most the observed $M$-size.  The recorded Monte Carlo values are
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ exact\ shape}}&=0,\\
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}$$
Equivalently, no amino-acid-class-preserving sample in the run reproduced the exact observed shape, while seven out of two thousand landed in the lower tail at or below the observed size threshold.  The conclusion available from this row is therefore a finite rejection-style signal against a class-preserving null for this local boundary shape.  The signal is about the code-read layer and the curated label table; it is not a proof of biological necessity.

\paragraph{Leave-one stability checks.}
The leave-one-code row $h1.leave.one.code.out$ reports $23$ evaluated tables and $63$ total targets.  Its baseline hit rate is $0.19821739130434785$, while the hit rate above baseline is $0.9841269841269841$.  The hit rate inside $M-T$ is $0.9365079365079365$, and the hit rate inside $M-T$ plus boundary is again $0.9841269841269841$.  The family-level row $h1.leave_one_code_out.by_family$ reports five families, with family hit rates above baseline equal to $0.8333333333333334$ for alternative yeast, and $1.0$ for bacterial-plastid, candidate-other, mitochondrial, and nuclear-ciliate families.  These figures support robustness of the code-layer boundary prediction across the finite leave-one-code tests supplied here.

\paragraph{Leave-one-codon recovery.}
The leave-one-codon row $h1.leave.one.codon.out$ records $anchor\_count=0$, $boundary\_count=5$, $closure\_coverage\_high=8$, $predictable\_count=8$, and $recovery\_rate=1.0$.  The row also records $recovery\_rate\_above\_threshold=1.0$.  In the local reading, this says that the supplied codon-level deletion tests recover the relevant codon labels through predictable or boundary behavior in every tested case.  Together with the complete module assignment above, this supports the claim that the thirteen-codon boundary is a stable finite code-read pattern.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The curated standard-code table is a contact for codon-to-label assignments.  It does not observe ribosomal translation dynamics, folded protein geometry, thermodynamic admissibility, organismal function, or a universal biological law.  The invalid promotion would be to pass from a rare and stable local codon-label tile to claims about realized protein structure or biological mechanism without adding a separate observation aimed at that layer.  Accordingly, the present row is ready only as a finite code-read record: it records a thirteen-codon boundary in $Q_6$, a complete six-module assignment, spectral coordinates, rare-shape statistics, an amino-acid-class-preserving null result with exact-match count $0$ out of $2000$, and leave-one stability.  No stronger biological conclusion follows from this packet alone.
