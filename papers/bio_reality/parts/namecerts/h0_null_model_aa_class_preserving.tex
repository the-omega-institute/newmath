\subsection{Amino-acid-class-preserving null test for a window-six codon tile}
\label{sec:h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}

\paragraph{Claim under test.}
The object considered here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The finite carrier is the codon cube $Q_6$, with vertices read as codon positions and labels read from a curated standard-code table.  The question is not whether the cube geometry is biology by itself.  The question is narrower: after amino-acid class sizes are preserved under a finite null model, does the observed local tile retain a shape that is unusually difficult to reproduce by random relabelling?

The tested claim is the amino-acid-class-preserving null model for the local tile associated with the standard-code readback.  It belongs only to the code-read layer.  A codon-to-label table can support statements about assigned labels and local boundary splits, but it does not observe translation dynamics, folded structure, energetic admissibility, phenotype, biochemical function, a mechanism, or a universal law.  Any such higher-layer statement would require another contact, independent of the curated table used here.

\paragraph{Finite carrier and exceptional boundary row.}
The finite exceptional set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified row, it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row records $R$ as a punctured-cube tile with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the boundary object is a concrete finite subconfiguration, not a verbal pattern.  The observed distinguished region has size $|M|=20$, while the boundary row $R$ contributes the thirteen listed codons.  The data do not include a full displayed list for $M$ in this packet, so the chapter uses the verified cardinality $20$ and the complete listed $R$ row.

$$
The radial polynomial check gives residual
$$
\begin{aligned}

$$
which is effectively zero at the precision of the run.  This supplies a numerical shape signature for the finite tile, but it remains a code-level signature.  It does not say that a cell must realize the geometry, nor that a peptide structure follows from the codon pattern.

\paragraph{Shape significance before preserving amino-acid classes.}
A separate finite shuffle row sampled $N=2000$ distinct standard-code-size candidates with seed $42$.  The sampling process required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and rejected $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005,
\end{aligned}
$$
and it found $25$ candidates with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those counts say that the displayed tile is rare in that finite experiment.  They do not by themselves control for amino-acid class preservation, which is why the null model in this chapter is needed.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null result.}
The class-preserving null row used $N=2000$ samples, again with seed $42$.  The observed $M$ size was $20$.  Among the class-preserving samples, the exact observed shape occurred $0$ times:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{\mathrm{exact\ matches}\}=0,\qquad
p_{\mathrm{class,exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{|M'|\le 20\}=7,\qquad
p_{\mathrm{class,lower}}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within this finite run, preserving amino-acid class sizes does not erase the unusualness of the observed tile.  The exact-shape count is absent from the sampled null population, and the lower-tail statistic remains small.  The result supports a code-layer statement: the standard-code labels determine a local finite pattern that is not typical under this particular class-preserving randomization.

\paragraph{Interpretation and boundary.}
The evidence supports a restrained conclusion.  In the standard-code table, the window-six codon display exposes a finite local tile with a thirteen-codon punctured-cube boundary, a twenty-codon distinguished region, a stable spectral signature, and low finite-sample probabilities under both unconstrained shape sampling and amino-acid-class-preserving sampling.  The strongest null result in this packet is the class-preserving row: $0$ exact matches among $2000$ samples and a lower-tail probability of $0.0035$ at observed size $20$.

The scientific meaning is therefore local and conditional.  The curated code table is a contact for codon-to-label assignments.  It can ground label readback, local split tests, leave-one recovery, and module accounting.  It cannot, without additional observation, ground translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  The chapter records the finite code-layer pattern and the null-model pressure around it; it deliberately stops before any higher biological layer.
