A codon is read only as a six-bit display together with its curated standard-code label.  The admissible distinctions in this row are local: coordinate position, closure inside the codon cube, spectral behavior of the induced relation, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  The biological contact is therefore the codon-to-label table itself.  It is not a contact with translation dynamics, folded protein structure, chemical admissibility, organismal function, or a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Null model.}
The claim tested in this row is the amino-acid-class preserving null model.  The random comparison keeps the relevant amino-acid label class constraint while asking whether the same local shape appears by chance.  In $N=2000$ finite samples with seed $42$, the observed tile size is $|M|=20$, the exact-shape match count is $0$, and the verified exact-shape $p$-value is $0.0$ at the sampling resolution.  The lower-tail count at or below the observed size is $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}} &= \frac{7}{2000}=0.0035,\\
P_{\mathrm{exact\ shape}} &= \frac{0}{2000}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The broader shape-significance run with the same sample size and seed made $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, of which $55377$ were duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, yielding $p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005$ and $p_{J\ge 0.9}=0.0125$.  The amino-acid-class preserving row is therefore stronger for exact recovery in this finite batch: no exact match occurred among the $2000$ class-preserving samples, while the lower-tail event remained rare at $0.0035$.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result supports a narrow code-layer statement: the six-coordinate codon representation exposes a statistically nontrivial local boundary tile when the standard-code labels are supplied by the curated table.  The row does not license a stronger biological conclusion.  In particular, the local tile is not a law of translation, not a statement about folding, not a test of physical viability, not a functional assay, not an organism-level mechanism, and not a universality theorem.  Those claims would require independent contacts at the corresponding biological layer.  Here the empirical contact is only the curated standard-code label table, so the conclusion remains a bounded code-read result: amino-acid label readback is compatible with the local tile, while all higher-layer promotions remain blocked.
