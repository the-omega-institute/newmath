\subsection{Amino-acid-class preserving null model for the local codon tile}

\paragraph{Claim under test.}
The object considered here is the standard-code codon table viewed through a six-coordinate codon display.  The biological contact is deliberately narrow: a curated standard genetic-code table supplies codon-to-label assignments, where each codon is read as an amino-acid label or a stop label.  The tested claim is not that the coordinate geometry explains translation.  It is the finite question whether the local tile singled out by the window-six representation remains unusually organized when the random comparison is forced to preserve amino-acid label classes.

The distinguished boundary set is the thirteen-codon row
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\texttt{AAA},\texttt{AGA},\texttt{AGG},\texttt{AUA},\texttt{CUA},\texttt{CUC},\texttt{CUG},\texttt{CUU},\\
&\texttt{UAA},\texttt{UAG},\texttt{UCA},\texttt{UGA},\texttt{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite audit records $|R|=13$.  The same row is also recorded as a punctured-cube pattern with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row is not merely a list of codons: it has a fixed combinatorial shape inside the codon cube display.  The contact that supplies the labels, however, remains external to this geometry; the table is used only to attach standard-code labels to codon coordinates.

\paragraph{Class-preserving randomization.}
The amino-acid-class preserving null model keeps the label-class inventory fixed while randomizing the placement relevant to the local tile test.  In the recorded run with seed $42$, the sample size was $N=2000$.  The observed local set size was
$$
\begin{aligned}
|M|=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
Among the $2000$ class-preserving samples, the exact observed shape appeared $0$ times, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed size was $7$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\leq |M|}=7/2000=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the chapter's primary finite-row result.  It says that even after the random comparison is constrained by amino-acid class, the observed local tile is not behaving like a typical class-preserving placement.  The result is still a code-read result: it concerns label-table organization over codon coordinates, not molecular execution.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}

The standard-code table supplies labels; the six-coordinate display supplies a finite local tile; the null model shows that the observed tile is rare among amino-acid-class preserving comparisons; the leave-one audits show recovery behavior for the same code-layer organization.  What follows is a code-layer statement about local codon-label structure.

No stronger biological conclusion is licensed here.  This chapter does not establish translation realization, folded protein structure, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, a molecular mechanism, or a universal law of biology.  Amino-acid label sequences are not folded structures.  CUN, UUR, stop, tryptophan, isoleucine, methionine, arginine, lysine, serine, and leucine appearances in the row are table-label facts unless an independent contact supplies translation machinery, structural measurement, physical constraint, or functional phenotype.  Any later claim crossing that boundary must bring its own contact; it cannot be inherited from this codon-table audit.
