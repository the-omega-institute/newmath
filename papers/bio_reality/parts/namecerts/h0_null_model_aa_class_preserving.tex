\paragraph{Finite object and claim being tested.}
This record tests whether the local window-six codon tile is still exceptional after the amino-acid label classes are preserved. The finite carrier is the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$, read at the code layer through a curated standard-code label table. The row under audit is $\mathrm{h0.null.model.aa\_class\_preserving}$, with run id $\mathrm{e8d5de747a0c}$, seed $42$, and $N=2000$ class-preserving null samples. The observed tile has $|M|=20$. Under this null, the exact-shape count is $0$, and only $7$ null samples have size at most the observed $M$ size. Thus the reported probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}} &= 0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{lower\ tail}} &= 7/2000 = 0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The tested statement is therefore not that codon biology is forced by the geometry, but that the observed local code-layer tile is sharply separated from amino-acid-class-preserving randomizations at the finite resolution used here.

\paragraph{Shape significance against unrestricted samples.}
A separate null row for $\mathrm{h0.M.shape.significance}$ used $N=2000$ samples but required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts, with $55377$ duplicate retries. It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ samples with Jaccard score at least $0.9$. Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact\ shape}} &= 1/2000 = 0.0005,\\
p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\ge 0.9} &= 25/2000 = 0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
Together with the class-preserving row, this says that the observed tile remains finite-row exceptional both in direct shape comparison and after the null model respects amino-acid class counts. The class-preserving exact-shape probability is reported as $0.0$, while the lower-tail probability is $0.0035$.

\paragraph{Reality contact and local scope.}
The only external contact used here is the curated standard-code table, which assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label. That contact is adequate for code-label readback and for checking local label splits in the codon cube. It is not a measurement of translation dynamics, molecular folding, physical admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism. Consequently the chapter claims a finite local code-layer regularity: the window-six codon coordinates expose a sharply structured tile whose observed shape, boundary, spectrum, module partition, and leave-one recovery behavior are all recorded in finite rows. No claim about protein realization, folded structure, organism-level mechanism, physical feasibility, function, or universality follows from these rows unless an independent contact at that higher layer is supplied.
