\paragraph{Finite object under test.}
This record concerns a finite code-layer question on the codon cube $Q_6$.  The carrier is the six-coordinate codon display obtained by reading the standard four-letter codon alphabet as three adjacent base positions, with each base represented by a two-bit coordinate.  The observable label contact is the curated standard-code assignment of codons to amino-acid or stop labels.  The test is not a claim about translation machinery.  It asks whether the local tile singled out by the coordinate construction has a shape that remains exceptional even after the randomization is forced to preserve amino-acid label classes.

The distinguished boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$ and is the following finite row:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&AAA,AGA,AGG,AUA,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UGA,UUA\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same row is reported as a punctured-cube pattern with $f$-vector $(13,16,4)$.  Thus the observed set has $13$ codon vertices, $16$ one-dimensional adjacency faces, and $4$ two-dimensional faces in the induced local tile.  This is the finite combinatorial datum whose label-readback behavior is being tested.

\paragraph{Observed class-preserving null result.}
The amino-acid-class-preserving null model used $N=2000$ sampled tables with seed $42$.  The observed local set size was $|M|=20$.  Among the $2000$ class-preserving samples, the exact observed tile shape occurred $0$ times.  The empirical exact-shape probability was therefore reported as $p=0.0$ at this sampling resolution.  The lower-tail count for samples with $M$-size at most the observed value was $7$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Pr_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ null}}(\hbox{exact observed shape})&=0.0,\\
\Pr_{\mathrm{aa\ class\ null}}(|M_{\mathrm{sample}}|\le 20)&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the central finite-row conclusion: preserving amino-acid label classes does not make the observed local geometry typical.  The exact tile shape was absent from the sample, and the observed size lay in a lower tail of $7/2000$.

A broader shape-randomization row gives compatible pressure.  With $N=2000$ and the same seed $42$, the distinct-sample process required $57377$ attempts and $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match, so the exact-shape rate was $0.0005$.  It found $25$ samples with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$, so the high-overlap rate was $0.0125$.  These values do not identify a mechanism; they only say that the observed coordinate tile is not easily reproduced by the sampled finite nulls.

\paragraph{Reality contact and blocked promotions.}
The only reality contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table, at codon-label resolution.  That contact supplies the statement that a standard-code table assigns each codon to an amino-acid or stop label.  It does not supply ribosomal dynamics, organism-specific translation realization, protein structure, thermodynamic admissibility, biological function, or a universal mechanism.  Consequently the result may be stated as a finite code-layer alignment between a window-six codon coordinate tile and curated label readback.

The cannot-claim boundary is local and explicit.  No translation claim follows from the tile.  No folded structure follows from reading amino-acid labels.  No physical admissibility, functional role, mechanism, or universality follows from the numbers above.  A separate contact would be needed before crossing any of those layers.  In particular, the codon-to-label table can support a label split and leave-one-out recovery statistic, but it cannot turn CUN, UUR, AGR, stop, tryptophan, isoleucine, methionine, lysine, or serine labels into structural evidence by itself.
