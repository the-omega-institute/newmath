\paragraph{Finite record.}
This record concerns a six-coordinate codon display whose readout is the curated standard-code table.  The biological object under inspection is therefore not translation as a process, and not a folded protein, but the finite codon-to-label assignment supplied by the standard code.  The tested layer is code readback: a codon is represented as a point of the window-six cube, local tile faces are inspected inside that cube, and the labels are then attached by the curated table.  The relevant finite distinction is the separation between singleton label corners and three-point label classes under the standard-code labels.

The central boundary set $R$ has cardinality $13$.  In the verified run it is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same finite set is certified as the punctured-cube boundary with face-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_0,f_1,f_2)=(13,16,4).
\end{aligned}
$$
The module partition accounts for all thirteen codons in $R$: $\mathrm{AGR}=\{\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG}\}$, $\mathrm{ar\_lys}=\{\mathrm{AAA}\}$, $\mathrm{CUN}=\{\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU}\}$, $\mathrm{ile\_met}=\{\mathrm{AUA}\}$, $\mathrm{SSN}=\{\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UUA}\}$, and $\mathrm{stop\_trp}=\{\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UGA}\}$.  The module audit reports $R$-size $13$, assigned count $13$, no extra module codons, no missing members, and no unassigned codons.

\paragraph{Null comparison.}
The claim tested here is the amino-acid-class preserving null model.  The observed local support size is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|M|=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
Against $N=2000$ amino-acid-class preserving samples, with seed $42$, the exact local shape was never reproduced.  The exact-match count is $0$, giving the recorded empirical exact-shape value
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,exact}}=0.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The lower-tail count at or below the observed support size was $7$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{aa\ class,lower\ tail}}=\frac{7}{2000}=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the amino-acid-class preserving randomization does not treat the observed codon tile as an ordinary member of its finite sample cloud.  The statement is deliberately finite-sampling language: no exact match appeared among the $2000$ sampled class-preserving alternatives, and only seven samples landed at or below the observed $M$-size threshold.

A second shape-significance audit, using $N=2000$ samples with the same seed, required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and recorded $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches at Jaccard score at least $0.9$, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}=0.0005,\qquad p_{\mathrm{Jaccard}\geq 0.9}=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
This independent finite-row audit supports the same local reading: the observed tile shape is unusual under the tested randomization procedures, while remaining a code-table phenomenon rather than a biological mechanism.

The result remains a local codon-table result, not an assertion that the corresponding amino-acid string folds, functions, or is physically admissible.

\paragraph{Reality contact and scope.}
The only empirical contact used here is the curated standard genetic-code table.  It provides codon-to-amino-acid or codon-to-stop labels.  It does not observe ribosomal translation, tRNA availability, kinetic constraints, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, or cross-organism universality.  Consequently, the supported statement is narrow: window-six codon coordinates expose a local boundary-tile pattern whose label readback is non-random under the recorded amino-acid-class preserving null comparison.

The negative part is part of the result.  No conclusion about translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this finite row unless a separate empirical contact supplies that higher-layer observation.  Amino-acid label readback is not protein structure.  A local codon tile is not a global law.  The curated table is a label bridge for this finite code-layer test, and nothing in the present record promotes it beyond that layer.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The claim is ready as a finite code-readback chapter.  The amino-acid-class preserving null model gives $0$ exact matches in $2000$ samples and lower-tail value $0.0035$ against observed $|M|=20$.  The boundary set has $13$ codons, punctured-cube face-vector $(13,16,4)$, complete six-module accounting, spectral coordinates $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, $\mu=3.051487585298128$, and leave-one tests with hit rates near or at full recovery in the stated finite tasks.  The chapter therefore records a concrete local codon-table pattern, together with its strict scope limit.
