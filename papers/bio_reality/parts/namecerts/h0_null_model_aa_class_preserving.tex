\subsection{Amino-acid-class-preserving null test for the window-six codon tile}
\label{sec:bioreality-h0-null-model-aa-class-preserving}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and empirical contact.}
The record is a finite codon-window observation on the six-bit codon cube $Q_6$.  Its carrier is the standard display of the $64$ codons as binary-coordinate words, with the standard-code table used only as a codon-to-label contact.  The internal distinctions used here are a coordinate in $Q_6$, a local tile face, singleton label corners, and three-point label classes.  These distinctions are enough to test whether the standard-code label table leaves a reproducible local boundary pattern, but they do not by themselves assert a biological mechanism.  The biological object under inspection is therefore the standard-code codon table at the code-read layer: codons are read as amino-acid or stop labels, and no claim is made about translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, universal mechanism, or organism-level necessity without a separate observation supplying that layer.

\paragraph{Shape rarity against unconstrained relabeling.}
The finite row also records an unconstrained shape-significance experiment.  With seed $42$ and $N=2000$ sampled alternatives, the experiment required $57377$ distinct-sample attempts and encountered $55377$ duplicate retries.  It found $1$ exact shape match and $25$ matches with Jaccard similarity at least $0.9$.  The corresponding probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{exact}}&=0.0005,\\
p_{J\geq 0.9}&=0.0125.
\end{aligned}
$$
This says that the observed local shape is rare in that finite sampling regime.  The statement is deliberately limited: rarity of a codon-window shape under the sampled null does not imply that the shape is a law of biology.  It only says that, after the codon-label contact is fixed by the curated table, the observed local tile has a measurable non-generic profile among the sampled alternatives.

\paragraph{Amino-acid-class-preserving null model.}
The claim-specific null model preserves amino-acid label classes and asks whether the same local shape appears under that restricted randomization.  The finite run has experiment identifier $\mathrm{e8d5de747a0c}$, seed $42$, and $N=2000$.  The observed module size is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|M|=20.
\end{aligned}
$$
Within the $2000$ sampled amino-acid-class-preserving alternatives, the exact match count is $0$.  The lower-tail count at or below the observed $M$ size is $7$.  Therefore the reported probabilities are
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{class\ exact}}&=0.0,\\
p_{\mathrm{class\ lower\ tail}}&=0.0035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The exact-shape probability is reported as $0.0$ because no exact match appeared in the finite sample; the row should be read as a finite empirical count, not as a proof that the true probability is mathematically zero.  The lower-tail value $0.0035$ gives the stronger finite-row interpretation: among amino-acid-class-preserving alternatives in this run, configurations as small as the observed $M$ are rare.  This supports the code-read claim that the standard table carries a special local boundary profile relative to that null model.

\paragraph{Scope of the result.}
The ready verdict for this row is a bounded code-read verdict.  The standard-code table is a valid contact for codon labels, and the finite experiments report a coherent local tile: $|R|=13$, $f(R)=(13,16,4)$, $|M|=20$, $\lambda_M=0.6752479308830213$, $\lambda_R=0.4758352843197002$, $\mu=3.051487585298128$, no exact amino-acid-class-preserving null match in $2000$ samples, and a lower-tail probability $0.0035$.  The supporting held-out tests report $63$ targets across $23$ tables and a boundary-augmented hit rate $0.9841269841269841$, while the codon-level test reports complete recovery at rate $1.0$.  These facts justify the statement that window-six codon coordinates expose a finite local boundary-tile structure in the curated standard-code labels.  They do not justify promotion to translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, universality, or protein-structure order.  Any such promotion would require a distinct contact at the relevant biological layer.
