\subsection{Minimal compilation of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-minimal-compilation-law-b-star-q6-residual-compressibility-powered}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual source can be compressed by a small downstream readout dictionary after the amino-acid quotient has already been removed.  The finite carrier is an organism-by-CDS panel with $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, measured translation-efficiency readouts where available, matched mRNA controls, and downstream protein-state or structure-related readouts only when a row-level contact is present.  The nine residual coordinates used by the linked structural-order computation are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control set contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of these nine projected coordinates.  In the rank diagnostic associated with the structural-order contact, both computed organisms retained full residual coordinate rank: for \emph{Escherichia coli} K--12 MG1655, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $3713$; for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $5781$.  Thus the negative compilation verdict below is not caused by an immediate loss of the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate surface in those two rank checks.

\paragraph{Compression statistic.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the projected $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source and let $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ denote the residual left after recursively fitting the available downstream readout dictionary $D_o$.  The reported statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{compressibility}
  &= 1 - \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dimension count $DL(D_o)$ is the summed dimensionality of the selected $D_o$ readouts, whereas the raw baseline dimension count is the summed dimensionality of every available candidate readout before scored selection.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=605.0092168669606$, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\ TE}\}$, $DL(D_o)=1$, the raw baseline dimension count is $17$, $\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2/\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=0.9756319042010854$, and compressibility is $0.024368095798914635$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In \emph{E. coli} K--12 MG1655, $n=2517$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=225.84556319953032$, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\ TE},\mathrm{mRNA\ stability}\}$, $DL(D_o)=2$, the raw baseline dimension count is $2$, $\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2/\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=0.9942823785118229$, and compressibility is $0.005717621488177116$ with the same permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=11250$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=286.3673197177688$, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\ TE},\mathrm{mRNA\ stability},\mathrm{PTM\ density}\}$, $DL(D_o)=3$, the raw baseline dimension count is $19$, $\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2/\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=0.9736442326306781$, and compressibility is $0.02635576736932188$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; this is the only organism in the table for which the greedy $H$ dictionary selected $\mathrm{PTM\ density}$.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $n=4759$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=229.86450794006544$, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\ TE},\mathrm{mRNA\ stability}\}$, $DL(D_o)=2$, the raw baseline dimension count is $19$, $\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2/\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=0.8676586345294415$, and compressibility is $0.13234136547055853$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Verdict from the finite rows.}
The proposed minimal-compilation law is not supported by the computed panel.  The measured low-dimensional dictionary removes little of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual energy: the four compressibility values are $0.024368095798914635$, $0.005717621488177116$, $0.02635576736932188$, and $0.13234136547055853$, with median approximately $0.026$.  Equivalently, the final residual fractions remain high in all organisms, from $0.8676586345294415$ in yeast to $0.9942823785118229$ in \emph{E. coli}.  The finite conclusion is therefore a descriptive irreducibility statement relative to these measured readouts: $P_Q$ is not well summarized by the available $D_o$ dictionary in this run.  This does not prove that no biological explanation exists; it only says that the tested translation, mRNA-stability, and selected protein-state columns do not form a compact description of the residual source in the scored organism panels.

\paragraph{Downstream boundary checks.}
Two linked downstream tests sharpen the interpretation.  The translation-conditioned protein-stability boundary was marked partial, with three organisms joined to stability-like readouts.  The direct $B^*_{Q6}$ stability prediction summaries were positive but small, with minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$.  After full controls, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ had mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, and weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, with sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$ for being greater than zero.  The organism calls were two partial cases and one mediated-blocked null.  This supports a cautious reading: a stability or degradation-rate interpretation is not obtained merely by observing a residual association.  A same-row translation readout must first survive the controls, and any later stability statement must be reported as conditioned on that translation readout rather than as a direct codon-topology mechanism.

The structural-order boundary has a different finite shape.  In the translation-conditioned structural-order test, two organisms were joined, with row counts $3412$ for \emph{E. coli} K--12 MG1655 and $5119$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  The aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ was positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  The corresponding direct order-prediction summary had minimum $0.03664544696481631$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, and mean $0.09855549686555976$ over two organisms.  In the structural-order survival computation that used AlphaFold mean pLDDT as predicted structure-confidence rather than an experimental folding assay, the joined sequence-to-structure hit rates were $0.999198$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.998108$ for yeast; $3736$ matched \emph{E. coli} proteins and $5804$ matched yeast proteins were used.  The reported $R^2_{SO}$ values were $0.03059081709413562$ and $0.0715885627979685$, with all nine coordinates surviving in each organism.  These rows show that a structure-confidence readout can carry a finite association after controls, but they do not turn the minimal-compilation law into a supported law: a positive downstream association is not the same as a compact reconstruction of $P_Q$ by the selected $D_o$ dictionary.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The only supported claim here is a finite variance-partition result for the named rows and controls.  The measured translation-efficiency column is a readout variable, not a ribosome-dynamics mechanism.  The mRNA-stability and PTM-density columns are residual absorbers in the scored dictionary, not explanations of the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT, where used, is predicted structure confidence and not an experimental folded-structure measurement.  Bulk proteomics abundance is not a protein-stability, degradation-rate, localization, function, or phenotype assay.  No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, biological-function claim, phenotype claim, universal law, or direct causal route follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding controlled statistic survives.  The conclusion is therefore deliberately narrow: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source remains largely uncompressed by the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary, while downstream contacts must be interpreted only within their own finite measurement scope.
