\subsection{B-star Q6 Residual Compressibility Under Measured Readout Compilation}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-compressibility}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The finite object in this record is an organism-by-coding-sequence panel carrying $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, organism-matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and those downstream readout surfaces that were available in the same compilation run. The residual coordinates are interpreted only after amino-acid quotienting: a $q_i$ coordinate is first separated from amino-acid composition, CDS length, $GC3$, organism membership, and the $M$-density baseline before it is allowed to enter any cross-layer readout. The tested source is the projected residual energy $P_Q$, and the measured downstream dictionary $D_o$ is allowed to absorb that source only through selected low-dimensional readout columns.

\paragraph{Compilation statistic.}
For each organism, the recorded statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{compressibility} &= 1 - \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}} \rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q \rVert^2},\\
R_{\mathrm{final}}\text{ fraction} &= \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}} \rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q \rVert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The description-length accounting reports $DL_{D_o}$ as the summed dimensionality of the kept downstream readouts, while the raw baseline description length is the summed dimensionality of all candidate readouts before scored selection. The null model is not a causal statement: it asks whether the measured low-dimensional downstream readout set compresses the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual source in the observed finite panel.

\paragraph{Per-organism rows.}
Four organisms were computed. In \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$ CDS rows were used, with $\lVert P_Q \rVert^2=605.0092168669606$, $DL_{D_o}=1$, raw baseline $DL=17$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.9756319042010854$, compressibility $0.024368095798914635$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. The kept readout list was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te}\}$, with no selected $H$ readout. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=2517$, $\lVert P_Q \rVert^2=225.84556319953032$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline $DL=2$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.9942823785118229$, compressibility $0.005717621488177116$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; the kept readouts were $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$ with no selected $H$ component. In \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=11250$, $\lVert P_Q \rVert^2=286.3673197177688$, $DL_{D_o}=3$, raw baseline $DL=19$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.9736442326306781$, compressibility $0.02635576736932188$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; the kept readouts were $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{ptm\_density}\}$, with $\mathrm{ptm\_density}$ also selected in the $H$ dictionary. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=4759$, $\lVert P_Q \rVert^2=229.86450794006544$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline $DL=19$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.8676586345294415$, compressibility $0.13234136547055853$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; the kept readouts were $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$ with no selected $H$ component.

\paragraph{Cross-organism decision.}
The finite result is negative for the proposed minimal compilation law. The median compressibility is $0.026$, so the measured downstream readout dictionary does not highly compress $P_Q$. The strongest observed compression is in \emph{S. cerevisiae} at $0.13234136547055853$, while the weakest is in \emph{E. coli} at $0.005717621488177116$. Thus the residual source remains mostly unabsorbed by the available $D_o$ readouts in this descriptive test. The classification is therefore a cross-layer relation record with a rejected minimal-compilation interpretation for these measured readouts, not a proof that no biological explanation exists.

\paragraph{Translation and downstream gates.}
The same coordinate family has separate finite readouts in adjacent boundary tests, and those tests sharpen the interpretation. In the rank-powered structural-order contact, the computed $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate list had nine entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The control matrix used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline. In \emph{E. coli}, the joined structural-order panel had $3739$ codon rows, $3736$ matched proteins, hit rate $0.999198$, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and structural-order $R^2=0.03059081709413562$ with all nine coordinates surviving. In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the corresponding counts were $5815$ codon rows, $5804$ matched proteins, hit rate $0.998108$, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and structural-order $R^2=0.0715885627979685$ with all nine coordinates surviving. That record is still only a predicted-structure-confidence association, not an experimental folding or function statement.

\paragraph{Conditioned stability boundary.}
A separate translation-conditioned stability run passed its computation but yielded only a partial boundary result. Across three organisms, the direct $B^\ast_{Q6}$ stability-prediction summary had minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, maximum $0.03602995605625481$, and three positive entries. After full controls and translation conditioning, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ summary had minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, two negative entries, one positive entry, weighted $\Delta R^2=-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$. The per-organism call distribution was two partial calls and one mediated-blocked null call. The joined stability surfaces were $2517$ \emph{E. coli} rows for mRNA half-life, $6697$ human protein-turnover rows with $11322$ human mRNA-half-life rows, and $3537$ yeast protein-turnover rows with $4759$ yeast mRNA-half-life rows. These numbers allow a bounded stability-boundary report, but they do not license a folding mechanism or degradation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite evidence permits the following statement and no stronger one: in the computed panels, $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual energy is not minimally compiled by the measured downstream readout dictionary, and downstream structural-order or stability contacts must be read only through their own gated statistics. No translation realization follows from codon geometry alone. No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, degradation mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from the compressibility table, the structural-order table, or the stability table unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that claim and the same-row controlled statistic survives.
