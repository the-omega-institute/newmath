\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Residual Compressibility Boundary}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-residual-compressibility-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether the amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates $B^{*}_{Q6}$ are largely absorbed by a small measured downstream dictionary. The finite carrier is an organism-by-coding-sequence panel with residual codon coordinates, matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and downstream readout surfaces where they are available. The calculation begins from the residual source vector $P_Q$ and projects it through available measured readouts $D_o$, with translation placed before later surfaces. The reported statistic is descriptive variance absorption,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{compressibility} &= 1 - \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2},\\
R_{\mathrm{final}}\text{ fraction} &= \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $D_o$ is not treated as a mechanism. Its description length $DL_{D_o}$ is the summed dimensionality of the retained readouts, while the raw baseline length is the summed dimensionality of all available candidate readouts before scored selection.

\paragraph{Organism-level residual energies.}
The source was computed for four organisms. The $P_Q$ energies and full row counts were $605.0092168669606$ on $13140$ rows for \emph{Danio rerio}, $225.84556319953032$ on $2517$ rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $286.3673197177688$ on $11250$ rows for \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $229.86450794006544$ on $4759$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. These row counts define the finite scope of the calculation. They do not license extrapolation to species, tissues, growth states, or assays outside the joined panels.

\subsection{Measured Compression Results}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-compression-results}

\paragraph{Per-organism scores.}
The residual source was not strongly compressed by the measured low-dimensional dictionaries. In \emph{Danio rerio}, the retained dictionary was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te}\}$ with $DL_{D_o}=1$ against a raw baseline length of $17$; the final residual fraction was $0.9756319042010854$, giving compressibility $0.024368095798914635$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. In \emph{E. coli}, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$ with $DL_{D_o}=2$ and raw baseline length $2$; the residual fraction was $0.9942823785118229$, giving compressibility $0.005717621488177116$ with the same permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$. In human, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{ptm\_density}\}$ with $\mathrm{ptm\_density}$ also selected into the scored $H$ surface; $DL_{D_o}=3$, raw baseline length $19$, residual fraction $0.9736442326306781$, and compressibility $0.02635576736932188$ with $p=0.000999000999000999$. In yeast, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$ with $DL_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline length $19$, residual fraction $0.8676586345294415$, and compressibility $0.13234136547055853$ with $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Cross-panel verdict.}
The median compressibility across the computed organisms is $0.026$. Thus the minimal-compilation claim is not supported in this finite test: the observed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual source is not highly compressed by the measured low-dimensional readout set. This is a negative result about the tested dictionary, not a proof that no biological explanation exists. It says that measured translation, available RNA-stability surfaces, and the selected protein-state proxy do not absorb most of $P_Q$ under this descriptive projection protocol.

\subsection{Translation Gate and Downstream Boundaries}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-translation-gate-downstream-boundaries}

\paragraph{Rank and control surface.}
A related structural-order gate used nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$. The control set contained an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors. For \emph{E. coli}, the control matrix had $24$ columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and residual readout energy $136607.52873622958$. For yeast, the corresponding values were $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and residual readout energy $620506.1490869389$. Both panels were rank-sufficient for the nine-coordinate residual block.

\paragraph{Structural-order contact.}
The available structural-order surface was AlphaFold mean pLDDT, interpreted only as predicted structural-confidence signal on a $0$ to $100$ scale. The joined structural-order contact used $3736$ matched proteins out of $3739$ codon-joined entries in \emph{E. coli}, for hit rate $0.999198$, and $5804$ matched proteins out of $5815$ codon-joined entries in yeast, for hit rate $0.998108$. The controlled structural-order associations had $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ in \emph{E. coli} and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ in yeast, with all nine coordinates surviving the positive partial-$R^2$ threshold in both organisms. A separate translation-conditioned structural-order test reported aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ values between $0.002688284523492457$ and $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$. This supports a bounded crossing to a predicted structural-confidence layer, not a claim about experimental folding, function, phenotype, or causation.

\paragraph{Protein-stability contact.}
The stability-boundary run was less decisive. It joined $2517$ \emph{E. coli} rows with mRNA half-life as the primary readout, $6697$ human protein-turnover rows with $11322$ mRNA half-life rows available, and $3537$ yeast protein-turnover rows with $4759$ mRNA half-life rows available. The direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ stability-prediction summary was positive but small: minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$ over $n=3$ organism summaries. After full translation conditioning and controls, aggregate $\Delta R^2$ ranged from $-0.004953854025603666$ to $0.0006378518321674687$, with mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$. The call was partial, with two organism summaries classified as partial and one as mediated-blocked null. This is not evidence for a direct stability mechanism.

\subsection{Local Interpretation Rule}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-local-interpretation-rule}

\paragraph{Allowed reading.}
The correct reading is a finite, controlled readout statement. A $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate may be discussed downstream only after the same row has passed a translation readout under amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls. When a later protein-stability or degradation-rate surface is present, the result must be phrased as a translation-conditioned residual association. The minimal-compilation calculation adds a separate negative constraint: the measured low-dimensional dictionaries leave most of the residual source energy in place, with median compression only $0.026$.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folded state, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, degradation mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the residual coordinates, the modeled adaptation weights, the presence of contact names, or the compression calculation itself. Protein-stability language is permitted only where a separate stability or turnover contact is joined to the same finite rows and the translation-conditioned statistic is reported. Direct $q_i\to$ stability wording is blocked unless the $q_i\to T_j$ translation step survives and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the direct downstream association. Low compressibility also has a narrow meaning: it says that these measured readouts do not compress $P_Q$ strongly, not that the residual source is biologically inert or explanation-free.
