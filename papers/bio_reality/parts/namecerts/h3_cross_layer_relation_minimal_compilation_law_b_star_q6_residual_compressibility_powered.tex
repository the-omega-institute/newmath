\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual compressibility under measured readout compilation}
\label{sec:biorreality-bstar-q6-residual-compressibility}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which each $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate is first residualized against amino-acid composition before any cross-layer interpretation is allowed. The coordinate family has nine entries, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The control surface contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected coordinates. The translation-facing structural-order computation was rank-powered in the two organisms for which that surface was evaluated: in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $3713$, and the matched structural-order hit rate was $0.999198$ over $3736$ proteins; in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $5781$, and the matched hit rate was $0.998108$ over $5804$ proteins.

\paragraph{Compilation statistic.}
The tested minimal-compilation statistic projects the residual source $P_Q$ through the available measured readout dictionary $D_o$ and reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
  C &= 1 - \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $C$ is the descriptive compressibility and $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ is the residual after the selected readouts have been used. The tested dictionary was not uniform across organisms: \emph{Danio rerio} kept measured translation efficiency only, \emph{E. coli} kept measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability, human kept measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and PTM density, and yeast kept measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability. The human panel was the only one in which the greedy higher-layer dictionary selected PTM density as an $H$ entry. Thus the statistic is a finite variance-partition test over the measured surfaces that were actually present, not an unrestricted biological explanation of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ source.

\paragraph{Observed compression.}
The four organism rows do not support a strong minimal-compilation law. For \emph{D. rerio}, $n=13140$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=605.0092168669606$, $DL(D_o)=1$, raw baseline description length was $17$, $\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2/\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=0.9756319042010854$, and $C=0.024368095798914635$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=2517$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=225.84556319953032$, $DL(D_o)=2$, raw baseline description length was $2$, the residual fraction was $0.9942823785118229$, and $C=0.005717621488177116$ with the same permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. For human, $n=11250$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=286.3673197177688$, $DL(D_o)=3$, raw baseline description length was $19$, the residual fraction was $0.9736442326306781$, and $C=0.02635576736932188$ with $p=0.000999000999000999$. For yeast, $n=4759$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=229.86450794006544$, $DL(D_o)=2$, raw baseline description length was $19$, the residual fraction was $0.8676586345294415$, and $C=0.13234136547055853$ with $p=0.000999000999000999$. The median compressibility is therefore about $0.026$, so the finite data say that the measured low-dimensional readout set absorbs only a small part of the $P_Q$ energy in this test.

\paragraph{Downstream stability boundary.}
The same coordinate family can be carried toward protein-stability only through a measured translation step and then through a layer-matched stability or turnover surface. The available stability-boundary computation was marked passed but only partial. Across three joined organism rows, the direct $B^{*}_{Q6}$ stability-prediction summary had mean $0.02753157569086806$, median $0.027727034742559664$, minimum $0.018837736273789707$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$. After full controls, however, the aggregate translation-conditioned $\Delta R^2$ had mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, and weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, with sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$. The organism calls were two partial cases and one mediated-blocked null. The joined stability surfaces were mRNA half-life for \emph{E. coli} with $2517$ rows, protein turnover for human with $6697$ protein-turnover rows and $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows, and protein turnover for yeast with $3537$ protein-turnover rows and $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows. These numbers keep the stability claim at a translation-conditioned boundary rather than a direct codon-to-stability rule.

\paragraph{Structural-order comparison.}
A separate structural-order boundary gives a useful contrast. In two organisms, the translation-conditioned structural-order statistic crossed its finite threshold: the full-control $\Delta R^2$ summary had minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$. The underlying structural-order readout used AlphaFold mean pLDDT only as predicted structure-confidence, not as an experimental folding assay. In the direct structural-order survival calculation, \emph{E. coli} had $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ with nine surviving coordinates, and yeast had $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ with nine surviving coordinates. This comparison shows that some translation-conditioned readout surfaces can be finite and positive while the minimal-compilation law itself still fails to compress the residual source strongly.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a negative result for the strong minimal-compilation reading and a restricted positive role for downstream gated readouts. The measured $D_o$ surfaces do not make $P_Q$ highly compressible across the computed organisms. Low compressibility here means irreducibility to these measured readouts, not irreducibility in principle. The order $T \to S \to H$ is a modeling order for attribution, and changing that order would change the partition. No claim of translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, causal mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, or universal biological law follows from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, from residual geometry, from protein abundance wording, or from a nonzero association alone. Each such layer requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic before it can be stated as evidence at that layer.
