\subsection{Rank-Powered Residual Compressibility and the Translation-Conditioned Protein-Stability Boundary}\label{subsec:q6-residual-compressibility-protein-stability-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is first residualized against amino-acid composition and then tested only through bounded readout surfaces.  The coordinate family has nine entries,
$K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,\mathrm{vs}\,AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,\mathrm{vs}\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The admissible controls are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the readout is not a direct codon-to-protein assertion: an entry may reach a protein-stability boundary only after a same-entry translation readout $T_j$ has survived the declared controls.

\paragraph{Residual compression statistic.}
The descriptive minimal-compilation test writes the projected residual source as $P_Q$ and measures how much of its squared energy is absorbed by a low-dimensional readout dictionary $D_o$ after the declared ordering of measured translation, available stability-like surfaces, and scored higher readouts.  The reported statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{compressibility}
  &= 1 - \frac{\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Across the four computed organisms, the compression is small rather than law-like.  For \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$, $\|P_Q\|^2=605.0092168669606$, $D_o=(\mathrm{measured\ TE})$, $\mathrm{DL}_{D_o}=1$, the raw baseline description length is $17$, $\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2/\|P_Q\|^2=0.9756319042010854$, and the compressibility is $0.024368095798914635$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=2517$, $\|P_Q\|^2=225.84556319953032$, $D_o=(\mathrm{measured\ TE},\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, $\mathrm{DL}_{D_o}=2$, the raw baseline description length is $2$, $\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2/\|P_Q\|^2=0.9942823785118229$, and the compressibility is $0.005717621488177116$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=11250$, $\|P_Q\|^2=286.3673197177688$, $D_o=(\mathrm{measured\ TE},\mathrm{mRNA\ stability},\mathrm{PTM\ density})$, the selected higher dictionary is $H=(\mathrm{PTM\ density})$, $\mathrm{DL}_{D_o}=3$, the raw baseline description length is $19$, $\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2/\|P_Q\|^2=0.9736442326306781$, and the compressibility is $0.02635576736932188$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=4759$, $\|P_Q\|^2=229.86450794006544$, $D_o=(\mathrm{measured\ TE},\mathrm{mRNA\ stability})$, $\mathrm{DL}_{D_o}=2$, the raw baseline description length is $19$, $\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2/\|P_Q\|^2=0.8676586345294415$, and the compressibility is $0.13234136547055853$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Cross-organism verdict.}
The median compressibility is $0.026$, so the measured low-dimensional dictionary does not highly compress $P_Q$.  The finite result is therefore a negative or blocking result for a strong minimal-compilation law: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual source is not reduced, in these datasets, to the available small readout set.  This is a variance-partition result, not a causal compilation theorem.  The ordering $T\to S\to H$ is a modeling choice, and a different ordering would change attribution.  The organism rows also come from different studies or platforms, so the result is per-organism and descriptive rather than a universal biological rule.  Low compressibility means irreducibility relative to these measured readouts; it does not show that no biological explanation exists.

\paragraph{Rank and structural-order check.}
The same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface is rank-powered in the two structural-order organisms.  In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ codon rows join to $3736$ matched structural-order proteins, with hit rate $0.999198$; the control matrix has $24$ columns including $20$ amino-acid composition controls, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, and $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for the nine residual coordinates.  Its predicted-structure-confidence readout has $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ and all nine coordinates survive as finite association entries.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows join to $5804$ matched proteins, with hit rate $0.998108$; the control matrix again has $24$ columns and $20$ amino-acid composition controls, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, and $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  Its predicted-structure-confidence readout has $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$, again with nine surviving coordinate entries.  These structural-order numbers are useful as a boundary comparison, but their readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structure confidence.  It is not experimental folded structure, protein function, phenotype, localization, folding kinetics, pathway activity, or causality.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned downstream checks.}
The explicit structural-order boundary has two joined organisms, $3412$ rows for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ rows for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  The direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ prediction of order has positive values in both organisms, with minimum $0.03664544696481631$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, mean and median $0.09855549686555976$.  After full controls, the aggregate translation-conditioned order increment remains positive, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$, and two full-control-positive organism calls.  This classifies the finite structural-order boundary as crossing to a predicted structural-order layer, still without promoting the result to function, mechanism, phenotype, or a physical folding assertion.

\paragraph{Protein-stability boundary.}
The protein-stability or degradation-rate boundary is weaker.  Three organisms have a joined stability-like readout: \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 contributes $2517$ mRNA-half-life rows with mRNA half-life as the primary readout; \emph{H. sapiens} contributes $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows and $6697$ protein-turnover rows with protein turnover primary; and \emph{S. cerevisiae} contributes $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows and $3537$ protein-turnover rows with protein turnover primary.  The direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ prediction of the stability readout is positive in all three organisms, with minimum $0.018837736273789707$, maximum $0.03602995605625481$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and median $0.027727034742559664$.  However, after full controls and translation conditioning, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ does not give a positive stability boundary: minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$.  The organism calls are two partial and one mediated-blocked-null.  The permuted full-control surface is also negative in all three organisms, with minimum $-0.004840148172275405$, median $-0.002926202164063474$, mean $-0.002753469686061827$, and maximum $-0.0004940587218466019$.  The finite classification is therefore partial, not a protein-stability mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The chapter permits only the following conclusion.  In the available finite rows, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are real test coordinates for translation-gated and translation-conditioned cross-layer readouts, but the minimal-compilation law is not supported by the measured low-dimensional dictionary, and the protein-stability boundary is only partial after conditioning.  A direct $q_i\to$ stability association is not enough: the admissible interpretation must pass through $q_i\to T_j$ under amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls, and any downstream stability or degradation signal must be reported as a translation-conditioned residual association.  No translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanistic explanation, phenotype, or universal law follows from the residual geometry, the modeled adaptation weights, the rank condition, protein abundance contact names, or the stability-like row labels unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that evidence and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
