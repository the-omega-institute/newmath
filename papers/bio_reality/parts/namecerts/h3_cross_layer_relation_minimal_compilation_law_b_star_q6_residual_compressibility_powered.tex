\subsection{Translation-conditioned residual compressibility for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:q6-minimal-compilation-residual-compressibility}

\paragraph{Object and admissible reading.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are first quotiented against amino-acid composition and then compared with measured readout surfaces.  The admissible question is deliberately narrow: after controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, and available downstream readouts, how much of the residual source $P_Q$ is absorbed by a low-dimensional dictionary of measured $D_o$ readouts?  The carrier therefore consists of residual coordinates $q_i$, translation readouts $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and downstream stability or structure-related surfaces only where rows are joined.  A direct path from $q_i$ to protein stability, folding, function, phenotype, or mechanism is not licensed by this carrier.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each organism, the computed source energy is $\|P_Q\|^2$, and the terminal residual fraction is reported as $\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2/\|P_Q\|^2$.  The descriptive compressibility score is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C_Q &= 1 - \frac{\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $D_o$ is the selected finite readout list, $\mathrm{DL}_{D_o}$ is its summed dimensionality, and the raw baseline description length is the summed dimensionality of all available candidate readouts before scored selection.  The statistic is a variance-partition summary on finite joined rows.  It is not a causal compilation law, and it is sensitive to the declared readout order $T \to S \to H$.

\paragraph{Finite-row computation.}
Four organisms were computed.  In \textit{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$ rows gave $\|P_Q\|^2=605.0092168669606$, $\mathrm{DL}_{D_o}=1$, raw baseline description length $17$, $\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2/\|P_Q\|^2=0.9756319042010854$, and $C_Q=0.024368095798914635$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; the selected readout list was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te}\}$ and no $H$ readout was selected.  In \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=2517$ rows gave $\|P_Q\|^2=225.84556319953032$, $\mathrm{DL}_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline description length $2$, residual fraction $0.9942823785118229$, and $C_Q=0.005717621488177116$ with the same permutation $p$; the selected list was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$.  In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $n=11250$ rows gave $\|P_Q\|^2=286.3673197177688$, $\mathrm{DL}_{D_o}=3$, raw baseline description length $19$, residual fraction $0.9736442326306781$, and $C_Q=0.02635576736932188$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; the selected list was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{ptm\_density}\}$ with $H=\{\mathrm{ptm\_density}\}$.  In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=4759$ rows gave $\|P_Q\|^2=229.86450794006544$, $\mathrm{DL}_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline description length $19$, residual fraction $0.8676586345294415$, and $C_Q=0.13234136547055853$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$; the selected list was again $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$.

\paragraph{Cross-organism verdict.}
The finite panel does not support a minimal compilation law.  The median compressibility across the computed organisms is $0.026$, so the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary leaves most of $P_Q$ as terminal residual energy.  The strongest organism-level absorption in this run is yeast with $C_Q=0.13234136547055853$, while the weakest is \textit{E. coli} with $C_Q=0.005717621488177116$.  Thus the correct reading is not that $B^*_{Q6}$ has no biological relation, but that this particular measured dictionary does not highly compress the residual source.

\paragraph{Rank and row adequacy of the structural-order side contact.}
The structural-order side computation used the nine residual coordinates $\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}$.  For \textit{E. coli}, $3739$ codon-joined proteins yielded $3736$ matched structural-order proteins and a hit rate of $0.999198$; the residualized design had $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and $y$-residual energy $136607.52873622958$.  For yeast, $5815$ codon-joined proteins yielded $5804$ matched structural-order proteins and a hit rate of $0.998108$; the residualized design had $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and $y$-residual energy $620506.1490869389$.  The associated AlphaFold mean-pLDDT readout is only predicted structure confidence.  Its controlled association was $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ in \textit{E. coli} and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ in yeast, with all nine coordinates surviving in both organisms.  This establishes a bounded predicted-confidence association, not folded structure, function, or physical admissibility.

\paragraph{Downstream stability boundary.}
The protein-stability boundary remains translation-conditioned.  The stability-side computation joined three organisms with at least $300$ rows and used five folds.  The aggregate direct $B^*_{Q6}$-to-stability summary had $n=3$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, median $0.027727034742559664$, minimum $0.018837736273789707$, maximum $0.03602995605625481$, and all three entries positive before the full translation-conditioned interpretation.  After full controls, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ had mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, with two negative organism calls and one positive organism call; the weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ was $-0.001572140012981015$ and the sign-flip probability for positivity was $0.946441650390625$.  The finite verdict is therefore partial rather than mechanistic: a downstream stability or degradation interpretation is blocked unless the same coordinate first survives a translation readout and the downstream association is reported as attenuation or residual association conditional on that translation step.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from codon tables, tAI or stAI weights, identifier routing, or $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry alone.  No folding, protein stability, degradation mechanism, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the residual-compressibility calculation.  Protein abundance, structural confidence, and stability surfaces are separate measurement layers; they may be used only when row-matched and controlled, and they do not become mechanisms merely because their finite associations are nonzero.  The present computation is best stated as a negative minimal-compilation result with bounded side contacts: the measured dictionary $D_o$ does not compress $P_Q$ strongly, while translation-conditioned downstream tests remain layer-specific and non-causal.
