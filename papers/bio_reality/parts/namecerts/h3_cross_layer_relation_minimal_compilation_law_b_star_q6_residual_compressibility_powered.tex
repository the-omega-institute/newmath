\subsection{Residual Compressibility and the Minimal Compilation Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-minimal-compilation-boundary}

\paragraph{Object of the test.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are compared with matched downstream readouts only after the codon variables have been residualized against first amino-acid content.  The biological object is therefore not codon usage in the raw, and it is not a direct claim about proteins.  It is a controlled question about whether the projected residual source $P_Q$ can be substantially absorbed by a small measured dictionary $D_o$ containing translation, RNA-stability, and selected higher-layer readouts where such rows are available.  The operative statistic is the residual-energy compression
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{compressibility}
  &= 1 - \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The description-length count $DL_{D_o}$ is the summed dimensionality of the retained measured readouts, while the raw baseline count is the summed dimensionality of every available candidate readout before scored selection.  A positive biological interpretation would require more than a small numerical reduction in $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2$: it would need a surviving translation readout and then a separate layer-matched contact for any stability, degradation, folding, function, or phenotype claim.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controls.}
The finite $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate surface used in the adjacent translation-gated structural-order computation has nine residual coordinates: \texttt{K\_AAA}, \texttt{Arg\_AGR}, \texttt{Ile\_AUA}, \texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}, \texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}, \texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}, \texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}, \texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, and \texttt{f3\_stress}.  The control set used for the powered rank diagnostic contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $q$ vectors.  In the structural-order panel, the residualized design remained rank powered: in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655 the control-column count was $24$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ were $3713$, explained energy was $4178.935925251874$, and residual readout energy was $136607.52873622958$; in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the corresponding values were $24$, $23$, $9$, $5781$, $44421.14342043593$, and $620506.1490869389$.  These rank diagnostics show that the residual coordinates are not being erased by the stated controls, but they do not by themselves create a protein-level mechanism.

\paragraph{Per-organism compression results.}
The minimal compilation computation was completed for four organisms.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$ CDS rows were available, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=605.0092168669606$, the retained dictionary was $D_o=\{\texttt{measured\_te}\}$, $DL_{D_o}=1$, the raw baseline dimensionality was $17$, the final residual fraction was $0.9756319042010854$, and the resulting compression was $0.024368095798914635$ with permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $n=2517$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=225.84556319953032$, $D_o=\{\texttt{measured\_te},\texttt{mrna\_stability}\}$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, the raw baseline dimensionality was $2$, the final residual fraction was $0.9942823785118229$, and the compression was $0.005717621488177116$ with the same permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=11250$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=286.3673197177688$, $D_o=\{\texttt{measured\_te},\texttt{mrna\_stability},\texttt{ptm\_density}\}$, the selected higher-layer entry was $\texttt{ptm\_density}$, $DL_{D_o}=3$, the raw baseline dimensionality was $19$, the final residual fraction was $0.9736442326306781$, and the compression was $0.02635576736932188$ with $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=4759$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=229.86450794006544$, $D_o=\{\texttt{measured\_te},\texttt{mrna\_stability}\}$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, the raw baseline dimensionality was $19$, the final residual fraction was $0.8676586345294415$, and the compression was $0.13234136547055853$ with $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Verdict on the minimal compilation law.}
The finite result does not support a strong minimal compilation law for the measured dictionary.  The median compression across the computed organisms is $0.026$, so the observed low-dimensional readout set does not highly compress $P_Q$.  The strongest organism in this panel is yeast, with compression $0.13234136547055853$, while the other three organisms remain close to zero compression: $0.024368095798914635$ in zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ in \emph{E. coli}, and $0.02635576736932188$ in human.  The descriptive conclusion is narrow: $P_Q$ is not reducible to these measured readouts under this ordering and these controls.  It is not a proof that no biological explanation exists, and it is not evidence for a universal negative law.  The readout order $T \to S \to H$ is a modeling order, so attribution can change if the dictionary is reordered or if different layer-matched measurements are introduced.

\paragraph{Relation to stability and structural-order boundary tests.}
The separate translation-conditioned stability computation gives a partial boundary result rather than a direct stability mechanism.  Across three joined stability surfaces, the unconditioned $B^*_{Q6}$ stability-prediction summary had minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$, with all three entries positive.  After full controls and translation conditioning, however, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ summary had minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, two negative entries, one positive entry, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip value $0.946441650390625$.  The organism calls were two partial entries and one mediated blocked null.  This supports reporting a boundary condition, not a folding or degradation mechanism.  The same discipline applies to the structural-order boundary: two organisms produced positive full-control structural-order deltas, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip value $0.0029296875$.  The structural-order readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT, interpreted only as predicted structure confidence.  In the earlier structural-order survival computation, \emph{E. coli} had $3739$ codon-joined rows, $3736$ matched proteins, hit rate $0.999198$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$; yeast had $5815$ codon-joined rows, $5804$ matched proteins, hit rate $0.998108$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$.  These finite contacts show that adjacent layer tests can be run, but they do not rescue the minimal compilation law as a high-compression statement.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible readback is a gated matrix of the form $q_i \to T_j \to$ downstream readout, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA controls in force.  A downstream stability, degradation, structural-confidence, protein-abundance, localization, function, phenotype, or physical-admissibility statement is not licensed by $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry alone.  Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is at most a bounded translation-layer annotation unless observed translation rows support it.  Ribosome profiling is not protein abundance; protein abundance is not folding; AlphaFold confidence is not experimental structure; and none of these readouts is a universal biological law.  A mechanism claim would require the matched translation step to survive first and then require conditioning on that translation readout to reduce the direct $q_i$ to downstream association on a layer-matched finite panel.  In the present data, the minimal compilation claim is therefore recorded as a controlled negative compression result with adjacent boundary signals, not as a direct codon-to-protein mechanism.
