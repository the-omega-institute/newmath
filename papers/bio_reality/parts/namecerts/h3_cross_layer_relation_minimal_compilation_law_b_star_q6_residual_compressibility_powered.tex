\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual compressibility under translation-first readouts}\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-compressibility-powered}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.} The finite panel asks whether the amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates $B^*_{Q6}$ are compressed by a small measured dictionary of downstream readouts. The source vector is the projected residual energy $P_Q = \Pi_{Q_e} P_e$. For each organism the fitted readout sequence removes a chosen translation component $T$, then available stability or state components $S$, and then any scored higher dictionary component $H$. The reported statistic is purely a finite-row variance partition,
$$
\begin{aligned}
  C &= 1 - \frac{\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $C$ is the descriptive compressibility and $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ is the residual after the selected readouts. A large value of $C$ would support the minimal-compilation reading. A small value says only that these measured readouts do not absorb much of $P_Q$ on the joined rows.

\paragraph{Finite-row carrier.} The carrier consists of organism-indexed CDS rows carrying first-residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, matched translation readouts, matched mRNA controls, and a protein-state surface where such a surface is available. The coordinate set used by the translation-gated structural-order computation has nine coordinates: $K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_{stress}$. The control set contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. This is why the readout is not an amino-acid-composition restatement.

\paragraph{Compression table.} The computed organisms do not show strong compression of $P_Q$ by the selected readout dictionary. Danio rerio has $n=13140$, $\|P_Q\|^2=605.0092168669606$, $DL_{D_o}=1$, raw baseline dimension $17$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.9756319042010854$, and $C=0.024368095798914635$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $n=2517$, $\|P_Q\|^2=225.84556319953032$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline dimension $2$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.9942823785118229$, and $C=0.005717621488177116$ with the same permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. Homo sapiens has $n=11250$, $\|P_Q\|^2=286.3673197177688$, $DL_{D_o}=3$, raw baseline dimension $19$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.9736442326306781$, and $C=0.02635576736932188$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $n=4759$, $\|P_Q\|^2=229.86450794006544$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, raw baseline dimension $19$, $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ fraction $0.8676586345294415$, and $C=0.13234136547055853$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Selected readout dictionary.} The chosen $D_o$ dictionary is small but organism-specific. Danio rerio keeps only measured translation efficiency. Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 keeps measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability. Homo sapiens keeps measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and PTM density, with $H=\{\mathrm{ptm\_density}\}$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae keeps measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability. Thus the median compressibility is $0.026$, not a high-compression signature. The finite-row verdict is that the minimal compilation law is not supported across the computed organisms: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source is largely irreducible to this measured low-dimensional readout set. This negative result is still informative, because it blocks the stronger story that a small translation-state-protein dictionary already accounts for the residual source.

\paragraph{Translation gate before protein-state reading.} The same panel also records why downstream protein-state language must be translation-conditioned. In the protein-stability boundary test, the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ stability prediction summary over three organisms has minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$. After full controls and translation conditioning, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ surface has $n=3$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, and weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, with sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$. The organism calls are two partial entries and one mediated-blocked null. This is a partial boundary result, not a mechanism claim and not a protein-folding claim.

\paragraph{Related structural-order contrast.} A separate structural-order boundary is stronger but still bounded. Its direct $B^*_{Q6}$ prediction summary has $n=2$, minimum $0.03664544696481631$, median and mean $0.09855549686555976$, and maximum $0.1604655467663032$. After full controls, the aggregate structural-order $\Delta R^2$ has minimum $0.002688284523492457$, median and mean $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$. The permutation surface is negative in both entries, with mean $-0.00212186632044159$. In the underlying translation-gated structural-order rows, Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 joins $3739$ codon rows to $3736$ matched proteins, with hit rate $0.999198$, rank $9$ for the residual coordinate matrix after controls, rank $23$ for the control matrix, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae joins $5815$ codon rows to $5804$ matched proteins, with hit rate $0.998108$, rank $9$ after controls, rank $23$ for the controls, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$. These figures show finite associations with predicted structural-confidence readouts, not experimental folding, function, phenotype, or physical admissibility.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} The chapter therefore records a negative compression result with a translation-first downstream boundary. A $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate may be discussed at a protein-stability or protein-state boundary only after the same coordinate survives a matched translation readout under amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls. Modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not survival evidence for protein stability. Bulk protein abundance is not a degradation-rate measurement. AlphaFold mean pLDDT is predicted structure confidence, not an experimental folding assay. No translation mechanism, folding mechanism, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the finite-row compression table unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measurement and statistic.
