\subsection{Residual compressibility of $B^*_{Q6}$ after measured readout projection}
\label{sec:bior-h3-q6-minimal-compilation-residual-compressibility}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The tested object is a finite organism-by-CDS panel whose source coordinates are the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates after first quotienting out amino-acid composition.  The panel joins those residual coordinates to measured readouts where available, with mRNA controls retained as controls rather than promoted to translation evidence, and with protein-state readouts treated only after the translation-facing readout has been evaluated.  The coordinate list used in the associated $B^*_{Q6}$ survival computation has nine entries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_{AAA},\ \operatorname{Arg}\_{AGR},\ \operatorname{Ile}\_{AUA},\ \operatorname{Leu}\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ \operatorname{Leu}\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&\operatorname{Ser}\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ \operatorname{Ser}\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ \operatorname{Thr}\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The control design used for the translation-facing residual computations includes an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the rank diagnostics, the two organisms with structural-order readbacks both retained full residual coordinate rank: Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 had $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, and $y$-residual energy $136607.52873622958$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, and $y$-residual energy $620506.1490869389$.

\paragraph{Residual-compression statistic.}
For each organism, the source energy $P_Q$ was projected through the available readout dictionary $D_o$.  The reported statistic is the variance-partition quantity
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{compressibility} &= 1-\frac{\lVert R\_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2},\\
R\_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}} &= \frac{\lVert R\_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This statistic asks whether the measured low-dimensional readout set absorbs the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source.  It is not a causal parameter and it is not a biological mechanism.  The readout order is a modeling choice, so the numerical attribution belongs to this finite panel and to this order of projection.

\paragraph{Per-organism readback.}
The finite rows do not support the minimal-compilation law.  Danio rerio used $n=13140$ full rows with $P_Q$ energy $605.0092168669606$; its selected readout dictionary was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te}\}$, with $DL(D_o)=1$, raw baseline description length $17$, $R\_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}}=0.9756319042010854$, compressibility $0.024368095798914635$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 used $n=2517$ rows with $P_Q$ energy $225.84556319953032$; its dictionary was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$, with $DL(D_o)=2$, raw baseline description length $2$, $R\_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}}=0.9942823785118229$, compressibility $0.005717621488177116$, and the same permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  Homo sapiens used $n=11250$ rows with $P_Q$ energy $286.3673197177688$; its dictionary was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{ptm\_density}\}$, with $\mathrm{ptm\_density}$ selected from the scored $H$ dictionary, $DL(D_o)=3$, raw baseline description length $19$, $R\_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}}=0.9736442326306781$, compressibility $0.02635576736932188$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae used $n=4759$ rows with $P_Q$ energy $229.86450794006544$; its dictionary was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$, with $DL(D_o)=2$, raw baseline description length $19$, $R\_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}}=0.8676586345294415$, compressibility $0.13234136547055853$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The median compressibility across the computed organisms is $0.026$.  Thus the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary does not highly compress the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source in this panel.  The most favorable organism in this run is Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with compressibility $0.13234136547055853$, but even that value leaves $86.76586345294415\%$ of the source energy in the final residual fraction.  The strongest interpretation is therefore a negative one: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source is not reduced to these measured readouts by this descriptive projection test.  This does not show that the residual source has no biological explanation; it shows only that this measured dictionary, in this finite design, does not act as a compact representation of it.

\paragraph{Adjacent readout checks.}
Two downstream checks clarify the boundary of the negative compression result.  In the translation-conditioned protein-stability boundary computation, the direct $B^*_{Q6}$-to-stability prediction summary over three organisms was positive in sign, with minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$; after the full translation-conditioned control, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ had minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, and weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, with sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$.  That computation was therefore only partial, with one mediated-blocked null call and two partial calls.  By contrast, the translation-conditioned structural-order boundary crossed its finite threshold in two organisms: the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ had minimum $0.002688284523492457$, median and mean $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  The separate structural-order readout used AlphaFold mean pLDDT only as predicted structure-confidence data, with joined codon and structural-order rows of $3739$ codon rows and $3736$ matched proteins for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, and $5815$ codon rows and $5804$ matched proteins for Saccharomyces cerevisiae.  Its reported $R^2_{SO}$ values were $0.03059081709413562$ and $0.0715885627979685$, respectively, with nine surviving coordinates in each organism.  These adjacent results do not rescue the minimal-compilation law; they instead show that some downstream readouts can have finite associations while the broad residual source still remains poorly compressed by the measured dictionary used here.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
The present chapter licenses only a finite descriptive statement about residual energy and readout projection.  It does not establish translation as a realized mechanism, does not infer protein folding or degradation from $B^*_{Q6}$ itself, does not establish physical admissibility, does not assert molecular function or phenotype, and does not state a universal biological law.  Any such interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed after the same amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, mRNA, and readout-conditioning controls.  In this chapter, low compressibility means irreducibility to these measured readouts, not absence of any possible biological route.
