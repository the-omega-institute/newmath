\subsection{Residual compressibility across measured translation and state readouts}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-residual-compressibility-minimal-compilation}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The finite object in this record is an organism-by-CDS panel carrying $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, measured translation readouts, matched transcript controls, and downstream state readouts where a same-row contact is available. The tested residual source is $P_Q = \Pi_{Q_e}P_e$, and the reported compression statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{comp}(P_Q;D_o) &= 1 - \frac{\|R_{\mathrm{final}}\|^2}{\|P_Q\|^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $D_o$ is the kept readout dictionary after scored selection, while the raw baseline description length is the summed dimensionality of all candidate readouts before selection. The calculation was performed separately for four organisms. The source energies and row counts were $\|P_Q\|^2=605.0092168669606$ on $13140$ Danio rerio CDS rows, $225.84556319953032$ on $2517$ Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 rows, $286.3673197177688$ on $11250$ Homo sapiens rows, and $229.86450794006544$ on $4759$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae rows.

\paragraph{Compression readback.}
The observed residual fractions stayed close to one in three of the four organisms. Danio rerio kept only measured translation efficiency in $D_o$, with $DL(D_o)=1$ against raw baseline $17$, residual fraction $R_{\mathrm{final}}=0.9756319042010854$, compressibility $0.024368095798914635$, and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 kept measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability, with $DL(D_o)=2$, raw baseline $2$, residual fraction $0.9942823785118229$, compressibility $0.005717621488177116$, and the same permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. Homo sapiens kept measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and PTM density, with PTM density also selected in the scored higher-state dictionary; the corresponding values were $DL(D_o)=3$, raw baseline $19$, residual fraction $0.9736442326306781$, compressibility $0.02635576736932188$, and $p=0.000999000999000999$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae kept measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability, with $DL(D_o)=2$, raw baseline $19$, residual fraction $0.8676586345294415$, compressibility $0.13234136547055853$, and $p=0.000999000999000999$. The cross-organism median compressibility is therefore $0.026$, so the tested low-dimensional readout set does not strongly absorb the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source.

\paragraph{Rank and structural contacts.}
The same coordinate family contains nine residual coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. In the structural-order contact, both computed organisms retained full residual coordinate rank after the controls. Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 had $9$ projected coordinates, residual rank $9$, control rank $23$ over $24$ control columns, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and response residual energy $136607.52873622958$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae had the same $9$ projected coordinates and rank $9$, control rank $23$ over $24$ control columns, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and response residual energy $620506.1490869389$. The structural-order rows were nearly complete: Escherichia coli joined $3739$ codon rows to $3736$ structural-order proteins, using $3736$ matched proteins with hit rate $0.999198$; Saccharomyces joined $5815$ codon rows to $5804$ structural-order proteins, using $5804$ matched proteins with hit rate $0.998108$. The resulting predicted structural-confidence associations were $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ in Escherichia coli and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ in Saccharomyces, with all nine coordinates surviving as finite residual predictors in each organism.

\paragraph{Downstream boundary checks.}
The protein-stability boundary remained weaker than the structural-order boundary. Across three joined stability panels, the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ prediction summaries were positive but small: minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$. The translation-conditioned full-control change in $R^2$ had median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, two negative organism calls, one positive organism call, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip probability $0.946441650390625$ for being greater than zero. The organism calls were two partial entries and one mediated blocked null. By contrast, the translation-conditioned structural-order boundary crossed its finite test: two organisms had positive full-control values with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, median and mean $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$ for positivity, while the permuted controls were negative in both organisms.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The minimal-compilation claim is not supported by these finite rows. The measured dictionary $D_o$ does not provide a compact explanation of $P_Q$: most of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual energy remains after the selected translation, stability, and higher-state readouts are removed. The correct interpretation is descriptive and organism-scoped. Low compression here says that the residual source is not reducible to these measured readouts in this design; it does not say that no biological explanation exists. The readout order $T \to S \to H$ is part of the statistical protocol, so changing that order can change attribution. The selected readouts are residual absorbers in a variance-partition calculation, not molecular causes.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation claim, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, functional claim, mechanistic claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from the residual geometry or from the compression statistic alone. A downstream assertion is admissible only when a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding measured rows and the same coordinate survives the stated controls. Modeled tAI or stAI weights may be used only as bounded codon-adaptation annotations. Bulk protein abundance is not a stability assay, predicted structural confidence is not a folding experiment, and a direct residual-to-state association is not a translation-conditioned mechanism unless the matched translation readout survives first and conditioning on it reduces the direct residual association.
