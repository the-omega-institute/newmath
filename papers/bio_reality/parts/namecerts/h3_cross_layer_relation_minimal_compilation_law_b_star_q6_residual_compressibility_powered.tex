\subsection{Residual compressibility at the translation-conditioned boundary}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-compressibility-translation-conditioned-boundary}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
The claim tested here is a finite-row cross-layer compression claim for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates. The carrier is a matched organism-by-CDS panel in which the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinates are first residualized against amino-acid composition before any downstream readout is considered. The readout route is deliberately ordered: a coordinate $q_i$ must first be evaluated against a translation-layer readout $T_j$ under the declared controls, and only then may a protein-state or stability readout be interpreted. The local question is not whether codon topology causes folding, function, degradation, or phenotype. It is whether the residual source $P_Q$ is substantially compressed by a finite collection of measured readouts $D_o$ after projection through the available translation and downstream surfaces.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
For each organism, the tested residual source is $P_Q = \Pi_{Q_e} P_e$. The descriptive compression statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{compressibility} &= 1 - \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}} \rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q \rVert^2},\\
R_{\mathrm{final}}\text{ fraction} &= \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}} \rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q \rVert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dimensional summary $DL_{D_o}$ is the summed dimensionality of the kept measured readouts, while the raw baseline description length is the summed dimensionality of all available candidate readouts before scored selection. Thus the test is a finite variance-partition screen, not a mechanistic statement about translation, degradation, folding, or function.

\paragraph{Finite-row panel.}
Four organisms supplied computable $P_Q$ panels. In \textit{Danio rerio}, the panel contained $n=13140$ CDS rows with $P_Q$ energy $605.0092168669606$; the selected readout dictionary was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te}\}$ with $DL_{D_o}=1$ against raw baseline description length $17$. In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the panel contained $n=2517$ rows with $P_Q$ energy $225.84556319953032$; the kept dictionary was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$ with $DL_{D_o}=2$ and raw baseline description length $2$. In \textit{Homo sapiens}, the panel contained $n=11250$ rows with $P_Q$ energy $286.3673197177688$; the selected dictionary was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{ptm\_density}\}$, with $\mathrm{ptm\_density}$ also selected in the greedy $H$ dictionary, giving $DL_{D_o}=3$ against raw baseline description length $19$. In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the panel contained $n=4759$ rows with $P_Q$ energy $229.86450794006544$; the kept dictionary was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$ with $DL_{D_o}=2$ and raw baseline description length $19$.

\paragraph{Compression results.}
The resulting residual fractions stayed close to one in every organism except the yeast panel, and even there most of the residual source remained outside the measured dictionary. The organism-level values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\textit{D. rerio}:&\quad R_{\mathrm{final}}\text{ fraction}=0.9756319042010854,\quad \operatorname{compressibility}=0.024368095798914635,\\
\textit{E. coli}:&\quad R_{\mathrm{final}}\text{ fraction}=0.9942823785118229,\quad \operatorname{compressibility}=0.005717621488177116,\\
\textit{H. sapiens}:&\quad R_{\mathrm{final}}\text{ fraction}=0.9736442326306781,\quad \operatorname{compressibility}=0.02635576736932188,\\
\textit{S. cerevisiae}:&\quad R_{\mathrm{final}}\text{ fraction}=0.8676586345294415,\quad \operatorname{compressibility}=0.13234136547055853.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each organism-level permutation calculation reported $p=0.000999000999000999$ for the scored compression statistic, but the effect size is the controlling object for the claim. The median compressibility across the computed organisms is $0.026$, so the measured low-dimensional readout set does not highly compress $P_Q$. The finite-row verdict is therefore negative for the minimal compilation law: the observed residual source is largely irreducible to these measured $D_o$ readouts in this descriptive panel.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned downstream screen.}
The downstream protein-stability boundary is read only after the translation step, and the finite data do not license a direct $q_i$ to protein-stability mechanism. A separate stability-boundary run joined three organisms with at least $300$ rows per included readout and used five folds. Its direct $B^\ast_{Q6}$-to-stability summary was positive but small, with $n=3$, minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$. After full controls and translation conditioning, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ summary was not positive overall: minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, and weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, with sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$. The organism calls were one mediated blocked-null call and two partial calls. The joined readout surfaces were $2517$ \textit{E. coli} rows for mRNA half-life, $6697$ \textit{H. sapiens} rows for protein turnover with $11322$ mRNA half-life rows available, and $3537$ yeast protein-turnover rows with $4759$ mRNA half-life rows available. This is a boundary result, not a degradation-rate mechanism.

\paragraph{Structural-order comparison.}
A related structural-order screen helps locate the boundary without promoting the stability claim. In the translation-conditioned structural-order run, \textit{E. coli} and yeast supplied joined structural-order panels of $3412$ and $5119$ rows. The direct $B^\ast_{Q6}$-to-order summary had $n=2$, minimum $0.03664544696481631$, mean and median $0.09855549686555976$, and maximum $0.1604655467663032$. After full controls, the order-layer $\Delta R^2$ remained positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$. The corresponding permuted full-control values were negative, with mean $-0.00212186632044159$. This comparison shows that some translation-conditioned readout surfaces can cross into an order-associated layer, while the minimal compilation claim for $P_Q$ remains unsupported and the stability boundary remains only partial.

\paragraph{Rank and control discipline.}
The structural-order survival computation used nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$. The controls contained an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline. In the structural-order run, both \textit{E. coli} and yeast had $9$ residual coordinates, $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for the control matrix, rank $9$ for the residualized coordinate matrix, and powered-rank sufficiency marked true. The residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3713$ for \textit{E. coli} and $5781$ for yeast. The matched structural-order hit rates were $0.999198$ in \textit{E. coli}, with $3736$ matched proteins used from $3739$ codon-joined rows, and $0.998108$ in yeast, with $5804$ matched proteins used from $5815$ codon-joined rows.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row evidence rejects the strong minimal-compilation reading for this panel. The residual source $P_Q$ is not highly compressed by the measured translation, mRNA-stability, and selected downstream readouts: the cross-organism median compression is only $0.026$, and three of the four residual fractions exceed $0.97$. The correct local reading is therefore conservative. $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be carried into translation-conditioned boundary tests, and some linked readout surfaces show finite controlled associations, but the measured $D_o$ set does not explain away the residual source. No translation mechanism, protein folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function claim, degradation mechanism, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from these numbers unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that specific contact and the same controlled finite-row statistic is computed for it.
