\subsection{Translation-conditioned residual compressibility at the protein-state boundary}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-residual-compressibility-powered}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The finite object considered here is an organism-by-CDS panel in which the synonymous residual coordinates $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ are first residualized against amino-acid composition and then tested against measured or bounded downstream readouts. The panel is deliberately ordered: a coordinate $q_i$ may be discussed at a protein-state boundary only after it has passed through a translation readout $T_j$ under controls, and any later protein-stability or degradation readout is interpreted as a translation-conditioned association rather than as a direct codon-topology mechanism. The control surface includes CDS length, amino-acid composition, organism identity where applicable, GC3, an M-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls. Bulk protein abundance, protein turnover, mRNA half-life, predicted structural-order confidence, and PTM density are not treated as interchangeable measurements; each is a separate readout with its own row support and its own failure modes.

\paragraph{Compression statistic.}
For each organism, the residual source vector $P_Q$ is projected through the available low-dimensional readout dictionary $D_o$. The reported compression is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= 1 - \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $R_{\mathrm{final}}$ is the residual after the selected readout terms have been fitted. The description-length count $DL_{D_o}$ is the summed dimensionality of the kept readouts, whereas the raw baseline description length is the summed dimensionality of every available candidate readout before scored selection. This statistic is a finite variance-partition diagnostic; it is not a causal compilation rule and it does not identify a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Per-organism outcome.}
The powered rows give four computed organisms. In \textit{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$ CDS rows were joined and $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=605.0092168669606$; the selected dictionary retained measured translation efficiency only, with $DL_{D_o}=1$ against a raw baseline description length of $17$. The final residual fraction was $0.9756319042010854$, giving compressibility $C=0.024368095798914635$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=2517$ and $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=225.84556319953032$; measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability were retained, with $DL_{D_o}=2$ and raw baseline description length $2$. The final residual fraction was $0.9942823785118229$, so $C=0.005717621488177116$ with the same permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$. In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $n=11250$ and $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=286.3673197177688$; measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and PTM density were retained, with PTM density selected as the higher-state term. Here $DL_{D_o}=3$ against raw baseline description length $19$, the final residual fraction was $0.9736442326306781$, and $C=0.02635576736932188$ with $p=0.000999000999000999$. In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=4759$ and $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=229.86450794006544$; measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability were retained, with $DL_{D_o}=2$ against raw baseline description length $19$. The final residual fraction was $0.8676586345294415$, giving the largest observed compression, $C=0.13234136547055853$, again with $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Cross-organism readback.}
The median compressibility across the four computed organisms is approximately $0.026$. Thus the finite result does not support a minimal compilation law in which the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual source is strongly absorbed by the measured low-dimensional readout set. The strongest row is yeast, where about $13.2\%$ of the residual energy is absorbed by the available dictionary, but the other three organisms remain near $0.6\%$ to $2.6\%$. The correct readback is therefore negative for high compression: the residual source is largely unreduced by these measured readouts in this descriptive panel. This does not imply that no biological explanation exists; it only says that this particular set of measured translation, stability, and higher-state absorbers does not compress $P_Q$ strongly.

\paragraph{Translation and stability boundary.}
The separate translation-conditioned stability run prevents promotion of this weak compression result into a protein-stability claim. Three organisms entered the stability-side finite row. Direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ prediction of the stability-side readout was positive but small, with minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$ over $n=3$ organism-level entries. After full controls and translation conditioning, the aggregate delta-$R^2$ had mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, and maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, with two negative organism calls and one positive organism call. The weighted full-control delta-$R^2$ was $-0.001572140012981015$, and the sign-flip probability for being greater than zero was $0.946441650390625$. The finite verdict was partial rather than a stability mechanism. The joined stability readouts were mRNA half-life for \textit{E. coli} with $2517$ rows, protein turnover for human with $6697$ protein-turnover rows and $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows available, and protein turnover for yeast with $3537$ protein-turnover rows and $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows available.

\paragraph{Structural-order comparison.}
A related structural-order boundary gives a useful contrast. In the translation-conditioned structural-order run, the direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ prediction of the order-side readout had two positive organism entries, with values from $0.03664544696481631$ to $0.1604655467663032$ and mean $0.09855549686555976$. The full-control translation-conditioned delta-$R^2$ was positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean $0.005284160876443678$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$. The joined structural-order row counts were $3412$ for \textit{E. coli} and $5119$ for yeast. In the structural-order survival computation using AlphaFold mean pLDDT as predicted structure-confidence only, the joined codon and structure panels had $3739$ codon rows and $3736$ matched proteins for \textit{E. coli}, with hit rate $0.999198$, and $5815$ codon rows and $5804$ matched proteins for yeast, with hit rate $0.998108$. The residualized design retained all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates in both organisms, with rank $9$ after controls; the structural-order $R^2$ values were $0.03059081709413562$ for \textit{E. coli} and $0.0715885627979685$ for yeast. These rows show that a downstream association can be finite in another readout class while the minimal-compression claim remains unsupported.

\paragraph{Nine-coordinate basis and rank condition.}
The structural-order row also records the coordinate basis used by the powered tests: $K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. In the two structural-order organisms, the control matrix had $24$ columns, including $20$ amino-acid composition controls, and rank $23$; the residualized coordinate matrix had rank $9$ in both cases. Residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3713$ for \textit{E. coli} and $5781$ for yeast. These rank diagnostics matter because a downstream association is not interpretable when the residualized coordinate design is saturated or degenerate.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-level claim is therefore bounded and negative in its strongest form. The finite row tests whether $P_Q$ is highly compressed by the measured $D_o$ dictionary; it is not. The result leaves a residual source that is mostly unabsorbed by the available measured readouts, even though some linked translation-conditioned structural-order rows are positive and the stability boundary is partial. No translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein folding claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function, phenotype, degradation mechanism, or universal biological law follows from these numbers. Such claims would require a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed on the corresponding organism- and CDS-matched rows. The present record only licenses the finite statement that the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary does not strongly compress the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual source, and that protein-state interpretations must remain gated by a surviving translation readout plus the appropriate downstream contact.
