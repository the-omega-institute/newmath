\subsection{A residual-compressibility test for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-minimal-compilation-law}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record considered here is a finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates are first residualized against amino-acid quotient structure and are then read against matched downstream measurements.  The tested coordinates are the nine residual directions $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$.  The intended gate is sequential: a coordinate may be discussed at a protein-stability or degradation surface only after a same-row translation readout has survived the controls.  The controls used by the powered structural-order contact include an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the two structural-order organisms the control rank was $23$, the controlled design rank for the residual coordinates was $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3713$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5781$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.

\paragraph{Compressive statistic.}
Let $P_Q$ denote the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual source and let $R_{final}$ be the residual remaining after the measured readout dictionary has been fitted in the declared order.  The descriptive compressibility statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C &= 1 - \frac{\lVert R_{final}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Across the four computed organisms this statistic does not support a minimal compilation law.  The finite rows are as follows.  In \emph{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=605.0092168669606$, $DL(D_o)=1$, the raw baseline dimension is $17$, $R_{final}$ retains fraction $0.9756319042010854$, and $C=0.024368095798914635$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$.  In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=2517$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=225.84556319953032$, $DL(D_o)=2$, raw baseline dimension $2$, retained fraction $0.9942823785118229$, and $C=0.005717621488177116$ with the same permutation $p$-value.  In \emph{Homo sapiens}, $n=11250$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=286.3673197177688$, $DL(D_o)=3$, raw baseline dimension $19$, retained fraction $0.9736442326306781$, and $C=0.02635576736932188$.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $n=4759$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=229.86450794006544$, $DL(D_o)=2$, raw baseline dimension $19$, retained fraction $0.8676586345294415$, and $C=0.13234136547055853$.  The median compressibility is reported as $0.026$, so the measured low-dimensional dictionary leaves most of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual energy uncompiled.

\paragraph{Readout dictionary.}
The fitted readout set was organism-specific.  \emph{D. rerio} retained only measured translation efficiency.  \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 and \emph{S. cerevisiae} retained measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability.  \emph{H. sapiens} retained measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and PTM density, with PTM density also appearing in the selected higher-layer dictionary.  This means that the observed low compressibility is a statement about these rows and these measured absorbers, not a theorem that no biological explanation exists.  The readout order $T \to S \to H$ is part of the finite model specification, and another order would alter the variance attribution.

\paragraph{Translation-gated structural-order contact.}
The related structural-order readout is not used as proof of folding; it is a bounded check against AlphaFold mean pLDDT, interpreted only as predicted structure-confidence score on the $0$ to $100$ scale.  For this readout, \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 joined $3739$ codon rows to $3736$ matched proteins, with hit rate $0.999198$, controlled explained energy $4178.935925251874$, response residual energy $136607.52873622958$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$.  \emph{S. cerevisiae} joined $5815$ codon rows to $5804$ matched proteins, with hit rate $0.998108$, controlled explained energy $44421.14342043593$, response residual energy $620506.1490869389$, and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$.  All nine coordinates survived this finite structural-confidence association in both organisms, but the classification remains cross-sectional and non-causal.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned downstream boundaries.}
A separate powered boundary test for protein-stability or degradation-like readouts produced only a partial call.  The joined surfaces were $2517$ \emph{E. coli} rows for mRNA half-life, $6697$ human rows for protein turnover together with $11322$ human mRNA half-life rows, and $3537$ yeast rows for protein turnover together with $4759$ yeast mRNA half-life rows.  Before full conditioning, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ predictor summary for stability-like readouts had $n=3$, minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$.  After full controls, the aggregate translation-conditioned delta-$R^2$ summary had minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, two negative organism calls, one positive organism call, and weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$; the sign-flip probability for being greater than zero was $0.946441650390625$.  The module-level call is therefore partial rather than a stability mechanism.  In contrast, the powered structural-order boundary had two joined organisms, $3412$ \emph{E. coli} rows and $5119$ yeast rows, and crossed its finite structural-order layer with full-control delta-$R^2$ values from $0.002688284523492457$ to $0.007880037229394898$, mean $0.005284160876443678$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The conclusion is negative for the minimal compilation law and bounded for the downstream readouts.  The residual source $P_Q$ is not highly compressed by the measured $D_o$ dictionary: most of its energy remains after the fitted translation, RNA-stability, and selected higher-layer absorbers.  A direct $q_i$ association with protein stability, degradation rate, abundance, or predicted structural confidence is not enough to promote a mechanism.  The admissible statement is only that finite controlled associations were measured on specified rows, and that any downstream protein-stability interpretation must be conditioned on a surviving same-row translation readout.  No translation realization, folding conclusion, physical admissibility statement, biological function, mechanistic account, phenotype, or universal rule follows from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ geometry or from these finite statistics unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that evidence and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
