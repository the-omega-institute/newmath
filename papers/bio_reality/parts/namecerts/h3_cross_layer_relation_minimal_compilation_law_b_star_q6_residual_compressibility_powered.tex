\subsection{Residual Compressibility of the $B^*_{Q6}$ Cross-Layer Panel}
\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-residual-compressibility}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous coordinate is residualized against amino-acid composition before it is compared with measured readout surfaces. The tested source object is the projected residual packet $P_Q$, and the downstream dictionary $D_o$ is built only from available measured readouts after ordinary controls: organism identity, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, GC3, an $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls where those rows exist. The tested statistic is descriptive rather than mechanistic. The compression score is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  C &= 1 - \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2},\\
  \frac{\lVert R_{\mathrm{final}}\rVert^2}{\lVert P_Q\rVert^2} &= R_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dictionary dimension $DL_{D_o}$ is the summed dimension of selected readout coordinates, while the raw baseline dimension is the summed dimension of every available candidate readout before scored selection. Thus a small $C$ means that this measured low-dimensional readout dictionary leaves most of $P_Q$ unexplained; it does not mean that no biological explanation exists.

\paragraph{Per-organism computation.}
Four organisms have computed rows. In \textit{Danio rerio}, $n=13140$ CDS rows were joined, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=605.0092168669606$, $DL_{D_o}=1$, the selected readout list was $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te}\}$, the raw baseline dimension was $17$, $R_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}}=0.9756319042010854$, and $C=0.024368095798914635$ with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$. In \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $n=2517$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=225.84556319953032$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$, the raw baseline dimension was $2$, $R_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}}=0.9942823785118229$, and $C=0.005717621488177116$ with the same permutation value $p=0.000999000999000999$. In \textit{Homo sapiens}, $n=11250$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=286.3673197177688$, $DL_{D_o}=3$, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{ptm\_density}\}$ with $\mathrm{ptm\_density}$ selected in the scored $H$ dictionary, the raw baseline dimension was $19$, $R_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}}=0.9736442326306781$, and $C=0.02635576736932188$ with $p=0.000999000999000999$. In \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $n=4759$, $\lVert P_Q\rVert^2=229.86450794006544$, $DL_{D_o}=2$, $D_o=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability}\}$, the raw baseline dimension was $19$, $R_{\mathrm{final\ fraction}}=0.8676586345294415$, and $C=0.13234136547055853$ with $p=0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Cross-organism readback.}
The finite-row verdict is negative for the proposed minimal compilation law. The median compressibility across the computed organisms is $0.026$, so the measured dictionary does not strongly compress $P_Q$. The strongest compression in this panel occurs in \textit{S. cerevisiae} at $0.13234136547055853$, while the weakest occurs in \textit{E. coli} at $0.005717621488177116$. In every case most of the residual energy remains after projection through the selected readout dictionary. Written as retained residual fractions, the four values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R_{\mathrm{Danio}} &= 0.9756319042010854,\\
  R_{\mathrm{Ecoli}} &= 0.9942823785118229,\\
  R_{\mathrm{Human}} &= 0.9736442326306781,\\
  R_{\mathrm{Yeast}} &= 0.8676586345294415.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support a narrower conclusion: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source is not reducible, in this finite descriptive panel, to the measured translation, mRNA-stability, and selected PTM-density surfaces.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned downstream checks.}
Separate downstream panels prevent this negative compression result from being mistaken for a general biological null. In the protein-stability boundary test, three joined stability surfaces were present: \textit{E. coli} used $2517$ mRNA-half-life rows as its primary readout, human used $6697$ protein-turnover rows with $11322$ mRNA-half-life rows also available, and yeast used $3537$ protein-turnover rows with $4759$ mRNA-half-life rows. The direct $B^*_{Q6}$ stability prediction summary was positive but small, with min $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and max $0.03602995605625481$ over $n=3$ organisms. After full controls, however, the aggregate translation-conditioned stability increment had mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$, yielding a partial rather than promoted stability verdict.

\paragraph{Structural-order contrast.}
The structural-order records are also bounded. A translation-gated structural-order panel used nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. For the AlphaFold mean-pLDDT readout, \textit{E. coli} joined $3739$ codon rows to $3736$ matched proteins, with hit rate $0.999198$, rank $9$ after controls, and $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$. Yeast joined $5815$ codon rows to $5804$ matched proteins, with hit rate $0.998108$, rank $9$ after controls, and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$. In the separate translation-conditioned structural-order boundary, the full-control increment was positive in both organisms, with min $0.002688284523492457$, max $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, weighted value $0.004764742175882462$, and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$. This supports only a finite association with predicted structure-confidence readouts after the declared controls, not a folding, function, or phenotype claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present chapter therefore records a measured failure of strong residual compression, not a causal law. The order $T\to S\to H$ is a modeling order for variance accounting, and changing that order may change attribution. Translation, protein stability, structural order, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, and universality are not consequences of $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry alone. Each such interpretation requires its own matched contact surface and its own controlled statistic. Modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve as bounded readout annotations, but they are not ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, degradation rate, folding evidence, or a biological mechanism.
