\subsection{ARE-deletion RNA-stability boundary with a translation readout guard}
\label{subsec:bioreality-are-deletion-rna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite matched panel in which synonymously editable coding-RNA windows are allowed to carry coordinate labels, but the positive readout is restricted to RNA stability.  The row type is deliberately narrow: each row must expose the coordinate label, a same-row translation readout before any downstream interpretation, RNA half-life, transcription or mRNA abundance controls, isoform state, local sequence and structure covariates, batch, perturbation state, rescue state, and a declared break condition.  The present verified contact is the ARE-deletion half-life boundary rather than a measured decay-factor RBP bridge.  In the Siegel-type deletion table the checked finite rows consist of $226$ BEAS2B deletion rows and $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows.  The support statistic used $1000$ actual permutations and reports that a $2000$-permutation grading route is supported.  The null was computed by corrected pooled label permutation over deletions, since the within-region route is degenerate for the per-parent predictor.  The run identifier is $\mathrm{a41440ab2792}$, the runtime was $6.93$ seconds, and the sign-concordance flag is true.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $D$ denote the finite deletion rows that survive identifier and condition matching, and let $Y_d$ be the RNA half-life readout attached to row $d$.  Let $A_d$ record the ARE-deletion class or matched deletion label used by the declared pooled permutation.  The reported check is not a continuous mechanistic model; it is the finite statement that the observed direction of the ARE-deletion half-life contrast is concordant with the predeclared boundary under label randomization:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|D_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}| &= 226,\\
|D_{\mathrm{Jurkat}}| &= 2242,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000,\\
N_{\mathrm{grading}} &= 2000,\\
\mathrm{runtime} &= 6.93\ \mathrm{s},\\
\mathrm{sign\ concordant} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row count is large enough for a finite RNA-stability boundary claim, but the payload does not contain measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or non-target control rows.  Hence the supported statement is an ARE-deletion association with RNA half-life, not an RBP-mediated decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout separation.}
The coordinate labels remain descriptors unless they continue to separate RNA half-life after transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, batch, and the same-row translation-readout guard have been represented in the finite table.  The same-row translation readout is a guardrail, not a promoted conclusion.  It prevents an RNA half-life observation from being silently reclassified as translation realization.  A row may therefore support the local claim that an ARE deletion changes an RNA-stability readout, while still refusing any claim about ribosome occupancy, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a general law.

\paragraph{Comparison with adjacent RNA-stability checks.}
The same evidence family contains an endogenous $3'$UTR half-life transfer check with $14933$ genes, $2000$ permutations, $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$, baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, and an ARE coefficient $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$.  That companion result is useful as a same-layer calibration: ARE signal can transfer to an endogenous half-life surface, but it still remains an RNA-stability surface.  A separate m6A half-life boundary check reports $5837$ genes, static Spearman correlation $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log half-life, causal Spearman correlation $0.245804$ for m6A load versus $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, length-partial Spearman correlation $0.227683$, and two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$.  These adjacent RNA-stability numbers reinforce the local discipline: finite transcript-layer readouts can cross a predeclared stability boundary without becoming protein, structure, function, or mechanism evidence.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore bounded.  The Siegel-type ARE-deletion rows support a finite RNA half-life boundary for ARE deletion in the checked BEAS2B and Jurkat panels, with sign-concordant behavior under the corrected pooled deletion-label permutation.  The record does not license an RBP bridge unless the same finite rows also contain measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  If any of those controls reproduce the pattern, or if RNA half-life changes while measured RBP occupancy and perturbation-rescue behavior do not follow, the interpretation remains a finite RNA-stability association.  No translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, adaptation, causation, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter without a separate matched contact at that layer.
