\subsection{ARE deletion and the RNA-stability boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-are-deletion-mrna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This BioReality record concerns synonymously editable coding-RNA windows only after they are placed beside an explicitly bounded RNA-stability readout.  The finite row type is not a sequence law and not a protein-level assay.  A usable row contains a coordinate label for the edited window, a same-row translation readout before any higher interpretation is attempted, an RNA half-life measurement, an mRNA-abundance or transcription-rate control, isoform state, motif-density and local-structure covariates, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, batch, perturbation state, rescue state, and a declared break condition.  The biological object is therefore a matched coding-RNA window panel crossed with an RNA-decay surface; the positive reading, if any, is a relation between a finite coordinate distinction and RNA half-life under the named controls.

\paragraph{Observed finite support.}
The checked ARE-deletion run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_deletion\_mrna\_stability\_boundary\_siegel2022} used \texttt{a41440ab2792}.  It retained $226$ BEAS2B deletion rows and $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows.  The permutation count used for the finite test was $1000$, while the grading pass recorded $2000$ permutations and marked grading-permutation support as true.  The null calculation was the corrected pooled label permutation over deletions, because the within-region permutation was degenerate for the per-parent predictor.  The run finished in $6.93$ seconds and recorded sign concordance as true.  In compact notation, the checked surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}} &= 226,\\
N_{\mathrm{Jurkat}} &= 2242,\\
B_{\mathrm{test}} &= 1000,\\
B_{\mathrm{grade}} &= 2000,\\
\mathrm{sign\_concordant} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers license a finite RNA-stability boundary for ARE deletion rows.  They do not, by themselves, identify a decay-factor RBP, prove an RNA-decay mechanism, or promote the coordinate label to translation, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, causation, or a general biological law.

\paragraph{Endogenous-transfer calibration.}
A second finite check, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_endogenous\_3utr\_halflife\_transfer\_rnadecaycafe}, supplies a same-domain calibration rather than a new mechanism claim.  Its run \texttt{e96bf26e626b} used $14933$ genes and $2000$ permutations.  The baseline coefficient of determination was $0.0657771$, the test coefficient of determination was $0.0827561$, and the recorded increment was $0.016979$.  The ARE coefficient was negative, with $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and the permutation value was $p=0.00049975$.  The finite summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{genes}} &= 14933,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{baseline}} &= 0.0657771,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{test}} &= 0.0827561,\\
\Delta R^2 &= 0.016979,\\
\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= -0.0607953,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.00049975.
\end{aligned}
$$
This calibration is useful because it shows that an ARE-bearing 3UTR surface can be read as a bounded half-life predictor in an endogenous setting.  It remains separate from the deletion panel and does not supply same-window RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, rescue behavior, or binding-defective controls.

\paragraph{Licensed relation.}
The chapter-specific claim is a conditional cross-layer relation with a narrow target.  A high-coordinate versus low-coordinate synonymous label may be compared for RNA half-life only after amino-acid composition, transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch have been represented in the row system.  The relation becomes stronger only if the same rows carry measured local occupancy for a prespecified decay-factor RBP, if coordinate and occupancy variation can be decoupled, if target-RBP loss collapses the half-life effect, if wild-type rescue restores it, and if binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it.  Without those added rows, the present finite evidence stops at RNA-stability association.

\paragraph{Break conditions.}
The record breaks if the coordinate label is treated as a direct translation readout, if RNA half-life is read as protein abundance, if ARE deletion is treated as a folding or physical-admissibility assay, or if a half-life contrast is described as a mechanism without measured decay-factor RBP occupancy and perturbation-rescue rows.  It also breaks if local RNA structure, motif density, transcription, isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, or composition controls reproduce the same pattern.  A further break occurs if the same-row translation readout is absent; in that case the boundary cannot be joined to any translation-conditioned interpretation.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The finite deletion and endogenous-transfer facts support a bounded RNA half-life surface.  They do not show translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein output, protein structure, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  They also do not show an RBP mechanism unless a separate measured occupancy and perturbation-rescue contact is joined to the same finite windows.  Thus the current record is ready as an RNA-stability boundary, but the RBP-bridge reading remains conditional on additional layer-matched measurements.
