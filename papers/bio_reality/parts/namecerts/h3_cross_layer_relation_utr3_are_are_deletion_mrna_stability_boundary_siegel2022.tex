\subsection{ARE deletion as an RNA-stability boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-are-deletion-rna-stability-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The finite object considered here is a matched set of editable RNA regions in which an AU-rich element deletion is compared against its retained sequence context and then read only through RNA-stability measurements.  The row type is deliberately narrow: each row carries a deletion label, a cell-context label, an RNA half-life or stability readout, and the controls needed to prevent transcriptional abundance, isoform ambiguity, composition, and batch from being mistaken for a decay statement.  In the supplied verification record for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_deletion\_mrna\_stability\_boundary\_siegel2022}, the finite deletion panel contains $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows.  The registered null is not a mechanistic null over unobserved binding states; it is a pooled label-permutation null over deletions, with the within-region version declared degenerate for the per-parent predictor.  The run used $1000$ actual permutations, and its grading pass records $2000$ permutations with grading support present.  The measured outcome is sign-concordant across the declared rows, and the recorded runtime is $6.93$ seconds.

\paragraph{Statistic and boundary reading.}
The admissible conclusion is a finite RNA-stability boundary: the deletion of AU-rich sequence is associated with the direction of mRNA-stability change in the matched panel.  The finite statistic can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}} &= 226,\\
N_{\mathrm{Jurkat}} &= 2242,\\
B_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000,\\
B_{\mathrm{grade}} &= 2000,\\
\mathrm{sign\ concordant} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers license a row-bounded readout about ARE deletion and mRNA stability, not a broader statement about translation, protein state, or organismal function.  The evidence is strongest exactly where it is finite: deletion labels, observed stability direction, cell-context row counts, and an explicit permutation procedure.  No $R$-list, $M$-list, lambda value, module classification, or hit-rate scalar is present in the supplied record, so none is inferred.

\paragraph{Independent 3'UTR transfer contact.}
A second supplied record, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_endogenous\_3utr\_halflife\_transfer\_rnadecaycafe}, supports the same RNA-stability surface from an endogenous 3'UTR half-life transfer setting rather than from the synthetic deletion panel.  That record contains $14933$ genes, $2000$ permutations, a baseline $R^2$ of $0.0657771$, a test $R^2$ of $0.0827561$, and an incremental $\Delta R^2$ of $0.016979$.  Its ARE coefficient is negative, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, with a permutation value $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$.  Written as a finite comparison,
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{baseline}} &= 0.0657771,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= 0.0827561,\\
\Delta R^2 &= 0.016979,\\
\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= -0.0607953,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.00049975.
\end{aligned}
$$
This second contact does not replace the deletion panel; it gives a separate finite half-life surface in which ARE burden transfers with the expected negative direction.  Together, the two records justify saying that AU-rich sequence information reaches an RNA half-life boundary under finite, declared tests.

\paragraph{Why the RBP bridge is not licensed here.}
The stronger proposed bridge would require same-window measured occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  Those rows are not present in the supplied verified facts.  The deletion panel and the endogenous transfer record therefore stop before an RBP mechanism.  A half-life change without measured local RBP occupancy and perturbation-rescue behavior remains an RNA-stability association, even when the direction is replicated or the permutation support is strong.

\paragraph{Local refusal conditions.}
The chapter refuses every interpretation that crosses beyond the observed layer.  RNA half-life differences are not translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein output, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal sequence law.  The same-row translation boundary named in the conjectural carrier is a guardrail, not evidence that translation has been measured in the supplied deletion facts.  Matched mRNA abundance can control transcript-level confounding, but it is not an RNA half-life measurement by itself and cannot create a decay mechanism.  A positive bridge claim would require a new finite panel in which the same rows carry deletion or coordinate labels, local RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, RNA structure and motif-density covariates, transcription-rate controls, isoform state, abundance controls, batch labels, and the declared RBP-decay statistic.
