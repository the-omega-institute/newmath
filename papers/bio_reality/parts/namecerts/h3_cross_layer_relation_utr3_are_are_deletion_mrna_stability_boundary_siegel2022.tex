\subsection{ARE deletion as an RNA-stability boundary with a translation-readout stop}\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-utr3-are-deletion-mrna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns synonymously editable coding-RNA or transcript-window rows only after the row has been equipped with an RNA half-life readout and explicit controls separating transcript abundance, transcriptional rate, local sequence context, and isoform state from the reported stability quantity. The claim is not that an AU-rich element deletion explains translation, protein output, folding, phenotype, or a general regulatory law. The object is a finite matched panel in which the admissible row has the form $(c_i,T_i,H_i,O_i,Z_i,b_i)$, where $c_i$ is the coordinate or deletion label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout when present, $H_i$ is RNA half-life or a stability proxy, $O_i$ is measured local occupancy of a declared decay-factor RBP when such an occupancy bridge is claimed, $Z_i$ collects transcription, abundance, local structure, motif-density, composition, codon-pair, isoform, and batch covariates, and $b_i$ records the declared stopping condition. In the verified ARE-deletion run, the finite empirical support contains $226$ BEAS2B deletion rows and $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows. The null was evaluated with $1000$ pooled label permutations over deletions, using the corrected pooled-label design because the within-region permutation was degenerate for the per-parent predictor. The grading pass used $2000$ permutations, and the recorded result is sign-concordant.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
The local boundary statistic asks whether the deletion label has a stable direction against RNA half-life after the row has been prevented from being read as a translation or protein phenotype row. A compact way to state the admissible check is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H &= \widehat{H}_{\mathrm{ARE\ deleted}}-\widehat{H}_{\mathrm{matched\ control}},\\
\mathcal{N} &= \{\pi(c_i): \pi \text{ is a pooled deletion-label permutation preserving the finite payload shape}\},\\
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= \frac{1+|\{\pi\in\mathcal{N}: |\Delta_H(\pi)|\geq |\Delta_H|\}|}{1+|\mathcal{N}|}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run does not expose an $R$ list, an $M$ list, a lambda parameter, a module-classification label, or a hit-rate field; therefore those quantities are not introduced here. The available scalars are the two row counts, the two permutation counts, the corrected null-method description, the sign-concordance flag, and a runtime of $6.93$ seconds. On these data, the chapter may report a bounded RNA-stability boundary: the deletion perturbation is compatible with a finite half-life effect under the declared permutation check. It may not be promoted to a decay mechanism without measured local RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls in the same matched row system.

\paragraph{Relation to endogenous transfer.}
A separate endogenous $3'$ UTR half-life transfer run supplies a useful calibration point but not a mechanism bridge. That run used $14933$ genes, $2000$ permutations, a negative ARE coefficient $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, incremental $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, and permutation value $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$. These numbers support the narrower statement that ARE signal can transfer to an endogenous RNA half-life surface with a negative fitted direction and a finite improvement over baseline. They do not add the missing same-window occupancy and rescue rows to the deletion experiment. The deletion panel and the endogenous transfer panel therefore agree at the level of RNA-stability readout, while remaining insufficient for an RBP-mediated causal bridge.

\paragraph{Neighboring RNA-stability comparison.}
The m6A half-life boundary run gives a second RNA-stability comparison in a different molecular channel. It retained $5837$ genes, reported static Spearman association $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log half-life, a causal Spearman value $0.245804$ for m6A-load change versus half-life change, a length-partial Spearman value $0.227683$, and two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$. Its status semantics explicitly stop at support for a pre-registered boundary statistic, not a mechanism or function claim. This parallel is important because the ARE deletion result should be read with the same discipline: a finite RNA-stability surface can be crossed without authorizing translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The positive local content is the existence of a finite, sign-concordant ARE-deletion RNA half-life boundary under the recorded corrected pooled-label permutation design. The negative content is equally part of the mathematical statement. If high- and low-coordinate labels become exchangeable after transcription-rate, local-structure, motif-density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, abundance, and batch controls, the coordinate remains a descriptor only. If half-life changes but measured decay-factor RBP occupancy is absent, unchanged, not decoupled from coordinate, or not controlled by target-RBP loss and wild-type rescue, the evidence remains an RNA-stability association. If a binding-defective rescue, non-target control, structure covariate, motif-density covariate, transcriptional covariate, isoform effect, or batch effect reproduces the pattern, the bridge is refused. Thus the chapter licenses only a bounded cross-layer RNA-stability relation with an explicit translation-readout stop; no translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universal biological rule follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite rows.
