\subsection{ARE deletion and the finite RNA-half-life boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-are-deletion-mrna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns a finite matched panel in which synonymous coding-window labels are allowed to stand next to RNA-stability measurements only after the row also carries the controls needed to stop the interpretation from moving to another biological layer.  The object is not a claim that codon geometry realizes translation, protein output, folding, function, adaptation, or a universal rule.  It is a bounded RNA-half-life comparison anchored by deletion-level rows in which the relevant readout is the direction of mRNA-stability change after ARE-region intervention.  The verified finite run for the deletion panel used $n_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}=226$ BEAS2B deletion rows and $n_{\mathrm{Jurkat}}=2242$ Jurkat deletion rows.  The reported null was a pooled label permutation over deletions, with the within-region permutation rejected as degenerate for the per-parent predictor.  The run used $1000$ actual permutations, carried a $2000$-permutation grading setting, and returned sign concordance across the two assayed cell contexts in $6.93$ seconds.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
Let $D$ be the finite deletion-row set, let $c(d)\in\{\mathrm{BEAS2B},\mathrm{Jurkat}\}$ be the cell-context label, let $A(d)$ mark the ARE-associated deletion class used by the run, and let $H(d)$ be the measured RNA half-life or half-life-derived stability response attached to the same row.  The statistical surface used here is only the finite contrast between ARE-associated deletion rows and the pooled deletion-label null inside the available deletion universe:
$$
\begin{aligned}
D &= D_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}\sqcup D_{\mathrm{Jurkat}},\\
|D_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}| &= 226,\\
|D_{\mathrm{Jurkat}}| &= 2242,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000,\\
N_{\mathrm{grade}} &= 2000.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed outcome is therefore a finite RNA-stability boundary: the two cell-context panels agree in sign under the declared permutation procedure.  No additional layer is inferred from this agreement.  In particular, the same table does not by itself contain measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP depletion, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target rescue, or coordinate--occupancy decoupling.

\paragraph{Companion transfer check.}
A separate endogenous 3UTR transfer run gives a useful guardrail for scope.  In that run, $14933$ genes were used, the ARE coefficient was negative with $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, the baseline model had $R^2=0.0657771$, the ARE-augmented test surface had $R^2=0.0827561$, and the finite improvement was $\Delta R^2=0.016979$.  Its permutation count was $2000$ and its permutation value was $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$.  This companion fact supports the same narrow reading: ARE information can survive as an RNA-half-life predictor in a finite external table, but the result remains at the transcript-stability layer.

\paragraph{Licensed conclusion.}
The licensed claim is the following bounded statement.  In the Siegel-style ARE deletion panel, ARE-associated deletion labels cross a finite RNA-half-life boundary because the deletion-level contrast is sign-concordant across $226$ BEAS2B and $2242$ Jurkat rows under the declared pooled deletion-label permutation null.  Together with the endogenous transfer check on $14933$ genes, the evidence is enough to record a finite ARE-to-RNA-stability relation.  The relation is not promoted to a decay-factor RBP mechanism unless an additional same-window table supplies measured RBP occupancy, perturbation, rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and the necessary local sequence, isoform, transcription, abundance, structure, motif-density, composition, codon-pair, batch, and translation-readout covariates.

\paragraph{Local refusal conditions.}
The chapter refuses any interpretation in which RNA half-life is treated as translation output, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a general biological law.  A translation readout, if later attached, would serve as a boundary covariate rather than as a consequence of this RNA-half-life statistic.  Likewise, an RBP bridge would require direct occupancy and perturbation evidence on the same finite rows; an ARE deletion effect alone is an RNA-stability association, not an RBP mechanism.  If transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC or dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, or control rescue behavior can reproduce the observed pattern, the stronger bridge interpretation is rejected and the record remains a finite transcript-stability surface.
