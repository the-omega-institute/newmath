\subsection{ARE-deletion RNA-stability boundary with a translation guard}
\label{sec:bioreality-are-deletion-rna-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record concerns a finite panel of coding-RNA or transcript-linked rows in which an AU-rich element deletion label is compared against an RNA half-life readout. The intended cross-layer statement is deliberately narrow: a coordinate-associated RNA-stability difference may be recorded only as a bounded relation between a finite sequence-side edit label and a finite RNA-decay readout. The row set does not by itself certify translation output, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, or a universal biological rule. Those stronger interpretations require separate row-matched contacts at the relevant layer.

For the present verified ARE-deletion run, the finite evidence table contains $226$ BEAS-2B deletion rows and $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows. The declared null procedure is a corrected pooled label permutation over deletions; the within-region permutation was degenerate for the per-parent predictor and was therefore not the operative null. The run used $1000$ actual permutations, and the grading pass records $2000$ supported permutations. The scalar verdict is sign concordance across the deletion panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  n_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}} &= 226,\\
  n_{\mathrm{Jurkat}} &= 2242,\\
  N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000,\\
  N_{\mathrm{grading}} &= 2000,\\
  \mathrm{sign\_concordant} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation completed in $6.93$ seconds. No $R$-list, $M$-list, $\lambda$ value, hit-rate scalar, module class, or explicit permutation $p$-value is present in the supplied finite row for this specific ARE-deletion claim, so none is promoted into the mathematical statement.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
Let $D_i$ denote the ARE-deletion label for row $i$, let $H_i$ denote the RNA half-life readout, and let $Z_i$ collect the finite controls available or required for interpretation: transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the same-row translation readout when it exists. The verified datum supports only the existence of a finite deletion-associated RNA-stability contrast whose sign is concordant across the stated panels under the declared pooled-label permutation design. In schematic form, the licensed statement is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_H(D;Z) \neq 0
  \quad\text{as a bounded RNA-half-life contrast,}
\end{aligned}
$$
where the displayed inequality records the supported boundary direction and not a mechanism. It is not a claim that $D_i$ realizes translation, changes protein output, determines structure, or explains phenotype. The same-row translation measurement is a guardrail: without it, the RNA half-life result cannot be re-described as a translation result; with it, the result is still only an RNA-stability relation unless the translation statistic itself is separately computed and survives its own controls.

\paragraph{Comparison rows.}
The related endogenous 3-prime-UTR transfer run supplies an independent RNA-stability context, not a mechanism for the deletion panel. That run used $14933$ genes, $2000$ actual permutations, and reported a negative ARE coefficient $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$ with baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, and $\Delta R^2=0.016979$. Its permutation value was $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$, and its runtime was $256.449$ seconds. These scalars are compatible with the view that ARE-bearing 3-prime-UTR information can carry RNA-half-life signal in finite external tables, but they do not supply the missing same-window RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, or rescue rows for the deletion claim.

A second neighboring RNA-stability boundary comes from the m6A site-load half-life run. It used $5837$ genes and reported static Spearman association $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO half-life, causal Spearman association $0.245804$ between m6A-load change and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, length-partial Spearman association $0.227683$, and two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$. This comparison row shows that the BioReality table can record RNA-modification or motif-linked half-life boundaries with explicit finite statistics. It does not merge the ARE deletion result with an m6A mechanism, and it does not replace the missing decay-factor RBP bridge controls.

\paragraph{Mechanism refusal.}
The stronger RBP-bridge statement would require measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP on the same finite windows, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, and matched transcription or mRNA controls. Those data are not present in the verified ARE-deletion scalar row. Therefore the chapter records a positive RNA-half-life boundary and a negative mechanism boundary at the same time. The finite deletion evidence may be written as an RNA-stability contrast; it may not be written as an RBP-mediated decay mechanism unless the following finite implication is witnessed by actual rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  &D_i \leadsto O_i \leadsto H_i,\\
  &\text{loss of the target RBP collapses the contrast,}\\
  &\text{wild-type rescue restores it,}\\
  &\text{binding-defective and non-target controls fail to restore it.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $O_i$ denotes measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy. The supplied record contains $H_i$-level evidence and deletion labels, but not the full $O_i$ perturbation-and-rescue chain. Consequently, the accepted reading is: ARE deletion is supported as a finite RNA-half-life boundary in the verified panels; decay-factor RBP causation, translation realization, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, and universality remain outside the licensed conclusion.

\paragraph{Accepted statement.}
The ARE-deletion result is admitted as a bounded BioReality row because its finite evidence includes concrete row counts, a declared corrected permutation design, and a sign-concordant RNA-half-life outcome. The accepted statement is not that AU-rich elements constitute a general biological law. It is the more local assertion that, in the supplied finite deletion panels, ARE deletion carries a reproducible direction of RNA-stability contrast under the recorded null procedure. Any later chapter that wants to upgrade this record into an RBP-mediated RNA-decay bridge must attach same-window occupancy, loss, rescue, binding-defective control, non-target control, transcription, abundance, isoform, structure, motif-density, sequence-composition, codon-context, batch, and translation-boundary rows before using mechanism language.
