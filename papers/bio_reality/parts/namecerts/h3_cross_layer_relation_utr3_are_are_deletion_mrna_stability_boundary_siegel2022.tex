\subsection{ARE deletion as a bounded RNA-stability boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-are-deletion-mrna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite panel of AU-rich-element deletion rows in which the observed layer is RNA stability, not translation or protein output.  The deletion rows are read as matched transcript-level perturbations with a declared half-life statistic and an explicit refusal to promote the result beyond the measured RNA-stability surface.  The verified run for this packet is identified by experiment run $\mathrm{a41440ab2792}$ and contains two finite row counts: $226$ BEAS2B deletion rows and $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows.  The row universe is therefore not an asymptotic or genome-law object; it is the finite union of those measured deletion panels, together with the declared permutation design and the sign-concordance check.

\paragraph{Measured statistic.}
The audit records $1000$ actual permutations for the boundary test and $2000$ grading permutations for the supporting grading run.  The grading permutation flag is positive, and the sign-concordance flag is also positive.  The null procedure is recorded as pooled label permutation over deletions, with the correction that a within-region permutation was degenerate for the per-parent predictor.  In symbols, the finite readout may be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}} &= 226,\\
N_{\mathrm{Jurkat}} &= 2242,\\
P_{\mathrm{actual}} &= 1000,\\
P_{\mathrm{grading}} &= 2000,\\
\mathrm{sign\_concordant} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The runtime was $6.93$ seconds, which is only a computation audit scalar and is not part of the biological claim.  The important mathematical point is that the deletion labels, half-life changes, and permutation null are all finite-row objects.

\paragraph{Licensed conclusion.}
The licensed conclusion is narrow: in this finite ARE deletion setting, the deletion coordinate crosses an RNA half-life boundary when the deletion-associated half-life direction is sign-concordant under the declared finite permutation procedure.  The result supports a bounded statement about ARE deletion and mRNA stability in the tested panels.  It does not by itself identify a decay-factor RBP mechanism, because the supplied rows do not include same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or non-target controls.  It also does not establish that the same rows carry a translation-mediated effect.  A translation readout, if later joined, would be a separate coordinate in the finite table rather than a consequence of RNA half-life alone.

\paragraph{Control boundary.}
The boundary condition is local to RNA stability.  The record may compare high and low deletion-associated labels against half-life, but it must keep transcription rate, transcript abundance, isoform state, motif density, local RNA structure, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, and batch as possible alternative explanations unless the finite rows explicitly control them.  If those controls reproduce the pattern, the deletion coordinate remains a descriptor of the row set rather than a bridge to mechanism.  If half-life shifts without measured RBP occupancy and perturbation-rescue behavior, the result remains an RNA-stability association.  If occupancy shifts but coordinate and occupancy cannot be decoupled, the mechanism claim is still not licensed.

\paragraph{Relation to an independent ARE transfer statistic.}
A second finite ARE-adjacent audit supplies useful calibration without changing the scope of this chapter.  The endogenous $3^{\prime}$UTR half-life transfer run is identified by experiment run $\mathrm{e96bf26e626b}$ and contains $14933$ genes, $2000$ permutations, a baseline $R^2$ of $0.0657771$, a test $R^2$ of $0.0827561$, and a recorded increment
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.016979,\\
\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= -0.0607953,\\
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.00049975.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative ARE coefficient in that transfer run is directionally consistent with an ARE-associated decay surface, but it is not a substitute for the deletion panel.  It also depends on the original synthetic ARE reporter claim, so it is best read as a separate finite transfer check rather than as the proof of this deletion boundary.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The chapter refuses the following promotions.  An RNA half-life difference is not a translation realization, protein abundance readout, folded-structure readout, physical-admissibility result, functional assay, phenotype, adaptation, causal law, or universal biological principle.  A deletion-associated half-life boundary is not an RBP mechanism unless measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls are present in the same finite design.  A same-row translation measurement would only mark a translation boundary; it would not convert an RNA decay association into protein output, structure, function, or organism-level mechanism without its own layer-matched contact.

\paragraph{Status.}
The finite evidence is sufficient for a ready BioReality entry at the RNA-stability boundary level.  The positive ingredients are the two explicit row counts, the corrected pooled-label permutation null, the $1000$ actual permutations, the $2000$ grading permutations, and the positive sign-concordance flag.  The unlicensed ingredients are equally important: no measured RBP occupancy panel, no perturbation-rescue closure, no binding-defective control, and no same-row translation-to-protein chain are present in the verified deletion statistic.  The record therefore closes as a finite ARE deletion and mRNA half-life boundary, not as a mechanism, translation, structure, function, or universality claim.
