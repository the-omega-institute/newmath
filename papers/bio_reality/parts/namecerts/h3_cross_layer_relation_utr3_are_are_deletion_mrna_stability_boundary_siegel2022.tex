\subsection{ARE deletion and the RNA half-life boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-are-deletion-mrna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record treats the ARE-deletion panel as a bounded RNA-stability contact rather than as a general biological law. The finite rows are deletion-labeled transcript regions with an mRNA-stability readout and a declared label-permutation statistic. In the supplied run, the retained table contains $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows. The observed statistic was evaluated with $1000$ actual permutations, and the grading run records $2000$ permutations as supported. The null was not a within-parent shuffle, because that shuffle is degenerate for the per-parent predictor; the operative null is the corrected pooled label permutation over deletions. The run finished in $6.93$ seconds and reported sign concordance.

\paragraph{Boundary claim.}
The licensed statement is narrow: an ARE-deletion label can be read as a finite RNA half-life boundary when the deletion-class statistic remains oriented under the corrected pooled permutation test. Let $D$ denote the finite deletion label, $H$ the measured mRNA half-life or stability readout, and $C$ the admissible row controls available in the panel. The supported form is only
$$
\begin{aligned}
  D &\rightsquigarrow H \mid C, \\
  n_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}} &= 226, \\
  n_{\mathrm{Jurkat}} &= 2242, \\
  N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000, \\
  N_{\mathrm{grade}} &= 2000.
\end{aligned}
$$
The arrow is not a mechanistic arrow. It records that the finite table has enough contact with an RNA-stability measurement to support a bounded association statement under the stated permutation design. It does not say that the deleted element alone explains decay, that a specific decay-factor protein has been measured, or that the same rows identify a translation-mediated effect.

\paragraph{Observed finite support.}
The row counts make the contact asymmetric: Jurkat supplies most of the deletion rows, while BEAS2B supplies a smaller matched surface. The useful fact is not the large-cell-line contrast by itself but the presence of a sign-concordant deletion statistic under a nondegenerate null. The corrected pooled-label permutation is essential, because a null that cannot move the informative label within the relevant parent structure would give a vacuous comparison. Thus the supplied evidence supports the following finite reading: the deletion labels are not being promoted to pathway mechanism, but they do survive as an RNA-stability boundary in the reported panel.

\paragraph{Same-row translation boundary.}
The cross-layer relation named by this record requires a translation readout before any downstream translation interpretation is allowed. The present ARE-deletion fact does not by itself provide ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, localization, function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality. A separate same-row translation contact would have to supply a translation measurement, and matched mRNA controls would have to separate transcript abundance from translation efficiency. Without that additional contact, the ARE-deletion result remains an RNA half-life surface only.

\paragraph{RBP-mechanism refusal.}
The stronger RBP bridge is not licensed by the supplied finite rows. A decay-factor RBP mechanism would require measured local RBP occupancy, a prespecified target RBP, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or an appropriate non-target control, and decoupling between the coordinate label and occupancy. The present evidence has a stability boundary but not those mechanism rows. Therefore, if an RNA half-life difference is observed while decay-factor occupancy is absent, or if target-RBP perturbation and rescue are not part of the same finite table, the result must be kept as a finite RNA-stability association rather than an RBP-mediated bridge.

\paragraph{Control surface.}
The finite control discipline is transcription-first. Any positive reading must remain separated from transcription rate, transcript abundance, isoform state, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, batch, and cell-type mixture. These variables are not cosmetic; each can reproduce an apparent stability pattern without licensing a coordinate-to-decay mechanism. The ARE-deletion run therefore supports only the statement that the supplied deletion labels have a sign-concordant mRNA-stability boundary under the corrected pooled permutation design. It does not authorize promotion to translation, protein output, structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, causation, adaptation, or a universal sequence law.

\paragraph{Comparator calibration.}
A separate RNA-modification half-life calibration in the supplied finite ledger helps fix the scale of the boundary language. That m6A half-life run contains $5837$ genes, reports a static Spearman value of $-0.388929$ for m6A load versus DMSO log half-life, a causal Spearman value of $0.245804$ for m6A load versus the log$_2$ half-life change, a length-partial Spearman value of $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value of $0.000999001$. Its own status says that crossing the boundary supports a preregistered half-life statistic, not a mechanism or function claim. The ARE-deletion record is read in the same conservative style: finite row support can license an RNA-stability boundary while leaving translation, RBP mechanism, protein state, function, phenotype, and universality outside the conclusion.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The ARE-deletion panel is ready for inclusion as a finite BioReality boundary record. Its positive content is the sign-concordant RNA half-life association over $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows under $1000$ actual corrected pooled label permutations, with $2000$ grading permutations supported. Its negative content is equally important: no translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone, no decay-factor RBP bridge follows without occupancy and perturbation-rescue rows, and no folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universal law follows without a separate layer-matched contact.
