\subsection{ARE deletion and the RNA-stability boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-are-deletion-mrna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite panel in which AU-rich element deletion rows are read against an mRNA-stability endpoint. The admissible datum is not a general law about codons, translation, protein abundance, or organismal fitness. It is a bounded comparison between deletion labels and RNA half-life behavior in matched finite rows. In the verified deletion panel, the retained row counts were $n_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}=226$ and $n_{\mathrm{Jurkat}}=2242$. The reported boundary statistic used $1000$ actual permutations, while the grading surface records $2000$ supported permutations. The null procedure was a pooled label permutation over deletions, with the within-region alternative rejected as degenerate for the per-parent predictor. The finite result was sign-concordant across the retained deletion rows.

\paragraph{Statistic.}
Let $d_i$ be the declared ARE-deletion indicator for retained row $i$, and let $h_i$ be the corresponding RNA half-life readout after the available normalization and identifier joins. The boundary being recorded is only that the deletion coordinate retains a signed association with $h_i$ under the declared finite test. A schematic form of the tested contrast is
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= \operatorname{stat}\{(d_i,h_i,c_i): i\in I_{\mathrm{retained}}\},\\
I_{\mathrm{retained}} &= I_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}\cup I_{\mathrm{Jurkat}},\\
|I_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}| &= 226,\qquad |I_{\mathrm{Jurkat}}|=2242.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $c_i$ denotes the finite control information actually carried by the row construction and permutation design. The observation that the sign is concordant licenses an RNA-stability boundary: the ARE deletion label is not behaving as an arbitrary row name after the declared half-life readout is attached. It does not license a statement that translation has changed, that a protein has changed, or that a decay mechanism has been identified.

\paragraph{Boundary interpretation.}
The supported reading is deliberately narrow. The panel can say that ARE deletion rows have a reproducible direction with respect to mRNA stability in the tested finite setting. It cannot, from this contact alone, say that the effect is mediated by a measured decay-factor RNA-binding protein, because the same rows do not supply local RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, and non-target controls. It also cannot convert an RNA half-life difference into translation realization. A same-row translation readout is a boundary requirement before any downstream translation interpretation, and it would still be only a translation-layer contact rather than evidence for folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.

\paragraph{Transfer check.}
A related endogenous three-prime UTR half-life transfer record gives an independent finite stability surface. That record retained $14933$ genes, used $2000$ permutations, and reported $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$. Its baseline model had $R^2=0.0657771$, and adding the ARE term produced $R^2=0.0827561$, so the finite improvement was $\Delta R^2=0.016979$. The ARE coefficient was negative, with $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$. This second surface does not prove the deletion-panel mechanism, but it makes the same local boundary more specific: ARE-bearing or ARE-edited RNA rows can carry a measurable half-life signal without promoting that signal to translation, protein state, molecular function, or a universal sequence principle.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The claim is refused if the high- and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable for RNA half-life after the available controls, if transcription rate or mRNA abundance alone reproduces the pattern, if local RNA structure, motif density, GC or dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, transcript abundance, or batch absorbs the effect, or if the proposed RBP bridge is asserted without measured occupancy and perturbation-rescue evidence. A stronger RNA-decay mechanism statement would require a same-organism, same-window panel carrying the coordinate label, RNA half-life, matched transcription or abundance controls, same-row translation readout, measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and a declared finite statistic separating those alternatives.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence is ready for the RNA-stability boundary: $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows support a sign-concordant ARE-deletion half-life result under the declared pooled permutation design. The linked endogenous UTR transfer surface supplies a second finite half-life contact with $14933$ genes and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$. Together they justify a bounded statement about ARE-labeled RNA stability. They do not justify translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that layer directly.
