\subsection{ARE deletion and the RNA half-life boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-are-deletion-mrna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_deletion\_mrna\_stability\_boundary\_siegel2022} is treated here as a bounded RNA-stability chapter, not as a translation or protein-output chapter.  Its finite object is a matched panel of 3$'$ untranslated-region AU-rich-element deletion rows, with each row carrying a deletion label, a measured mRNA-stability or half-life readout, cell-context provenance, and the control information needed to keep the interpretation inside RNA abundance and decay.  The verified run used $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows.  The declared null was a corrected pooled label permutation over deletions, because a within-region permutation is degenerate for a per-parent deletion predictor.  The implemented run used $1000$ permutations; the grading contract records $2000$ permutations as supported.  The runtime was $6.93$ seconds, and the finite sign check was concordant.

\paragraph{Statistic carried by the chapter.}
Let $D_i$ denote the finite deletion or non-deletion label for row $i$, let $H_i$ denote the RNA half-life or stability readout, and let $C_i$ collect the local row controls available in the joined panel.  The chapter licenses only the finite contrast that can be read from the joined deletion rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= \operatorname{stat}\{(D_i,H_i,C_i): i \in I_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}} \cup I_{\mathrm{Jurkat}}\},\\
|I_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}| &= 226,\\
|I_{\mathrm{Jurkat}}| &= 2242,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000.
\end{aligned}
$$
The empirical reading is therefore narrow: ARE deletion labels carry a reproducible direction against the mRNA-stability readout in the observed finite rows.  The sign-concordance result is the positive finite-row fact.  It is not a statement that any synonymous coding coordinate, codon-window geometry, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, phenotype, adaptation, or global biological rule has been observed.

\paragraph{Boundary supplied by related half-life records.}
Two neighboring RNA half-life records fix the intended scale of this chapter.  The endogenous 3$'$UTR transfer record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_endogenous\_3utr\_halflife\_transfer\_rnadecaycafe} used $14933$ genes, $2000$ permutations, and reported a negative ARE coefficient $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, with baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, and $\Delta R^2=0.016979$ at permutation value $p=0.00049975$.  A separate mRNA half-life boundary record, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_modification.m6a\_site\_load\_mrna\_halflife\_boundary\_gse163500}, used $5837$ genes and reported static Spearman association $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log$_2$ half-life, causal Spearman association $0.245804$ between m6A load and $\Delta \log_2$ half-life, length-partial Spearman association $0.227683$, and two-sided partial-permutation value $p=0.000999001$.  These facts do not merge the ARE and m6A mechanisms.  They only show that the paper's RNA-stability layer is populated by finite, externally read rows rather than by an abstract coordinate assertion.

\paragraph{Licensed interpretation.}
The ready claim is the following: in the finite ARE deletion panel, mRNA stability can be discussed as an RNA-layer readout attached to deletion labels, and the support is the observed sign-concordant finite statistic under the corrected pooled deletion-label permutation.  The chapter may say that the deletion rows cross an RNA half-life boundary.  It may also say that the companion endogenous 3$'$UTR transfer result gives an independent RNA-layer scale, because the $14933$-gene record has $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}<0$ and a positive $\Delta R^2$ under permutation testing.  The chapter may not collapse these facts into a shared molecular mechanism unless the same rows carry the mechanism-specific contacts.

\paragraph{Local refusal conditions.}
The chapter refuses any promotion beyond RNA stability when the necessary row fields are absent.  A half-life difference alone is not a translation readout.  It is not ribosome occupancy, protein output, protein folding, physical admissibility, cellular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal law.  A decay-factor RBP bridge would require same-window measured RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, mRNA abundance controls, local RNA-structure controls, motif-density controls, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and a declared finite bridge statistic.  Those contacts are not supplied by the ARE deletion run itself.  They remain separate requirements.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The positive reading breaks if the deletion labels become exchangeable for RNA half-life after the relevant RNA-layer controls are attached, or if the apparent signal is reproduced by batch, isoform state, transcription rate, abundance normalization, local structure, motif density, or composition.  It also breaks if the result is described as an RBP mechanism without measured local RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue rows.  In that case the record remains an RNA-stability association, not a mechanism bridge.  The current verdict is therefore ready only for the bounded RNA half-life statement supported by $226$ BEAS2B rows, $2242$ Jurkat rows, $1000$ implemented permutations, and sign concordance.
