\subsection{ARE deletion as a bounded mRNA-stability boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-are-deletion-mrna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns synonymously comparable coding-RNA or transcript-window rows only insofar as the rows are joined to an RNA half-life or decay readout and to controls that prevent a stability signal from being promoted into another biological layer. The finite carrier for the present boundary is a matched table whose rows contain an editable or naturally indexed RNA region, a label for the local ARE-bearing context, a deletion or comparison state, a measured mRNA-stability readout, transcript abundance or transcription-side controls where available, cell or condition labels, and the declared null statistic. The same table may be placed next to translation-readout contacts, but those contacts serve as a boundary condition rather than as evidence that a half-life difference is a translation result.

\paragraph{Primary finite statistic.}
For the ARE-deletion boundary, the verified run reports experiment id \texttt{a41440ab2792}. The retained finite rows are cell-context separated: $n_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}=226$ and $n_{\mathrm{Jurkat}}=2242$. The null was evaluated by a corrected pooled label permutation over deletions, with $1000$ actual permutations and $2000$ grading permutations. The recorded runtime was $6.93$ seconds, and the sign check was concordant. In the notation of this chapter, the admissible empirical statement is therefore only that the ARE deletion label crosses a finite mRNA-stability boundary in these rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= 226 + 2242 = 2468,\\
B_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= \mathbf{1}\{\text{pooled deletion-label permutation supports concordant stability direction}\} = 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statistic is not a claim about protein output, ribosome occupancy, folded structure, phenotype, adaptation, or a general law of sequence. It is a row-level RNA-stability result under the declared deletion-label null.

\paragraph{Independent stability-scale comparison.}
A second finite row system checks that this chapter is not using a one-off stability surface as if it were a mechanism. The endogenous 3UTR half-life transfer run \texttt{e96bf26e626b} retained $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=14933$ genes, used $2000$ permutations, and reported $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$. Its baseline model had $R^2=0.0657771$, while adding the ARE term gave $R^2=0.0827561$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2 &= 0.0827561 - 0.0657771 = 0.016979,\\
\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= -0.0607953.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative sign of $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}$ is consistent with an ARE-associated destabilizing direction on the endogenous transfer surface. This comparison is used only as a bounded stability-scale cross-check. It does not supply same-window RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, a binding-defective rescue, or a non-target control.

\paragraph{Layer boundary with translation controls.}
The row logic requires a translation readout to be present before any downstream interpretation is attempted, but the half-life statistic itself does not become a translation statistic. A same-row translation-efficiency or ribosome-profiling entry would only separate the RNA-stability claim from translation-layer confounding. In this chapter the permissive conclusion is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{ARE deletion label} &\longrightarrow \text{bounded mRNA-stability readout},\\
\text{ARE deletion label} &\not\longrightarrow \text{translation realization},\\
\text{ARE deletion label} &\not\longrightarrow \text{protein structure or function}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus a translation contact can constrain interpretation, but it cannot be backfilled from the half-life result. Likewise, an mRNA abundance control can reduce transcription-side ambiguity, but it is not itself a half-life assay and is not evidence of ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Mechanism refusal for the RBP bridge.}
The stronger RBP-mediated bridge would require measured local occupancy by a prespecified decay-factor RBP, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, loss of the target RBP, restoration by wild-type rescue, failure of binding-defective or non-target rescue to restore the effect, and controls for local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and transcription rate. Those rows are not supplied by the ARE-deletion stability statistic. Therefore the present record licenses a stability boundary, not an RBP mechanism. If a future table contains half-life differences but lacks measured RBP occupancy, the interpretation remains an RNA-stability association. If it contains occupancy but lacks perturbation and rescue, the interpretation remains an occupancy-associated stability pattern. If a binding-defective rescue, non-target control, structure covariate, motif-density covariate, transcription-rate covariate, isoform state, or batch term reproduces the effect, the bridge is rejected.

\paragraph{Neighboring RNA-stability calibration.}
The m6A half-life run \texttt{7cd189f15c4b} gives a separate RNA-stability calibration surface rather than evidence for the ARE mechanism. It retained $5837$ genes, used a coverage rule requiring the minimum of mean T coverage in DMSO and STM2457 to be at least the twentieth percentile among valid joined genes, and reported a static Spearman correlation of $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log half-life. The perturbation-side association was positive: the causal Spearman statistic was $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman statistic was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was $0.000999001$. These numbers support the general existence of finite RNA-modification or RNA-element stability boundaries in the curated matrix, but they do not merge with the ARE-deletion rows into a common molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter-level conclusion is deliberately narrow. The verified finite data support an ARE-deletion mRNA-stability boundary with $2468$ retained deletion-context rows in the primary run and an independent endogenous 3UTR transfer comparison over $14933$ genes. The accepted statement is a bounded relation between local ARE-associated row labels and RNA half-life or stability readout under the declared finite nulls. No translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, decay-factor mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from this record unless another finite contact supplies the appropriate layer-specific measurement and the corresponding controls.
