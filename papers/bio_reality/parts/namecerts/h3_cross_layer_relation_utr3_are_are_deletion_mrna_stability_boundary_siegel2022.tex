\subsection{ARE deletion as a bounded RNA-stability boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-are-deletion-mrna-stability-boundary-siegel2022}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite matched panel of coding or transcript-associated RNA rows in which AU-rich element deletion is read only through an RNA-stability surface.  The measured datum is not a protein-output datum and not a structural datum.  The admissible row has a transcript or deletion identifier, a cell-context label, an ARE deletion or nondeletion label, an mRNA half-life or stability readout, the available abundance or transcript-control fields, the batch or source-study key, and the declared permutation statistic.  For the Siegel-associated ARE deletion boundary, the finite audit retained $226$ BEAS-2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows.  The reported null used pooled label permutation over deletions, with the correction that a within-region permutation was degenerate for the per-parent predictor.  The computation used $1000$ actual permutations, recorded a grading permutation budget of $2000$, and marked the grading permutation as supported.  The run time was $6.93$ seconds, and the direction was sign-concordant across the retained contexts.

\paragraph{Observed boundary.}
The finite statistic licenses only the following statement: in the retained ARE-deletion panel, the deletion label carries a reproducible RNA half-life boundary signal under the declared pooled-label null.  Let $D_i$ denote the finite deletion label for row $i$, let $H_i$ denote the RNA half-life readout, and let $C_i$ collect the row context made available by the experiment.  The permitted reading is the bounded comparison
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (D_i, C_i) &\longmapsto H_i,\\
  n_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}} &= 226,\\
  n_{\mathrm{Jurkat}} &= 2242,\\
  N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000,
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign concordance recorded for the finite retained rows.  This is enough to treat ARE deletion as touching an RNA-stability readout in this dataset.  It is not enough to identify a decay-factor RBP, to assign a binding event to the edited window, or to interpret the stability difference as translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Related transfer check.}
A separate endogenous $3'$UTR half-life transfer record gives an additional finite context for the same RNA-stability layer without converting the deletion result into an RBP-mediated explanation.  That transfer record used $14933$ genes and $2000$ permutations, with baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, and $\Delta R^2=0.016979$.  The ARE coefficient was negative, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, with permutation value $p=0.00049975$ and run time $256.449$ seconds.  These numbers support a bounded statement that ARE-related features can improve an endogenous RNA half-life readout in a held finite design.  They do not supply measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, or coordinate--occupancy decoupling for the Siegel deletion panel.

\paragraph{Cross-layer refusal rule.}
The intended cross-layer claim would require every row used for the positive interpretation to carry, at minimum, the synonymous or coordinate label, the same-row translation readout, RNA half-life, measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, transcription-rate or mRNA controls, local RNA-structure and motif-density covariates, composition controls, isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, perturbation state, and rescue state.  The present finite evidence stops earlier.  It gives a stability-boundary signal for ARE deletion and a related endogenous half-life transfer signal, but the observed data do not contain the RBP occupancy and perturbation-rescue rows needed to make the stronger bridge.  If high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels become exchangeable after the available controls, if half-life changes appear without measured RBP occupancy changes, if target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, if wild-type rescue does not restore it, or if binding-defective or non-target controls reproduce it, the interpretation remains a finite RNA-stability association only.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter therefore records a ready bounded boundary, not a completed mechanistic crossing.  The finite rows justify saying that ARE deletion is associated with an RNA half-life readout in the audited Siegel-linked panel, with $226$ BEAS-2B rows, $2242$ Jurkat rows, $1000$ actual permutations, and sign concordance.  The related endogenous transfer result, with $14933$ genes and $p=0.00049975$, strengthens the case that ARE features can be read at the RNA-stability layer.  Neither result licenses promotion to translation, protein output, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, organismal phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a general sequence law unless a separate matched contact supplies that layer-specific measurement and its own finite statistic.
