\subsection{ARE deletion as a bounded RNA-stability boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-are-deletion-rna-stability-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite panel of synonymously addressable coding-RNA windows joined to an RNA half-life readout and to the controls needed to prevent an RNA-stability observation from being promoted into a translation, protein, structure, function, or evolutionary claim.  A row is admissible only when it carries a coordinate label, a same-row translation readout used as a boundary covariate, an RNA half-life measurement, matched mRNA abundance or transcription control, isoform state, local RNA-structure and motif-density covariates, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, batch, perturbation state, rescue state, and an explicit break condition.  The finite distinction tested by this chapter is not ``codons cause decay'' and not ``translation realizes stability''.  It is the narrower question of whether an ARE-associated deletion label can be read as an RNA half-life boundary after the rows are kept separate from transcription, isoform, composition, translation-readout, and batch explanations.

\paragraph{Primary finite statistic.}
For the claim key \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_deletion\_mrna\_stability\_boundary\_siegel2022}, the verified finite run is \texttt{a41440ab2792}.  The retained row counts are $n_{\mathrm{BEAS2B}}=226$ and $n_{\mathrm{Jurkat}}=2242$.  The permutation audit used $1000$ actual permutations, with a grading check at $2000$ permutations, and the recorded null procedure is pooled label permutation over deletions after correction for the degeneracy of the within-region parent predictor.  The run completed in $6.93$ seconds and the sign-concordance flag is true.  In local notation, if $D_i$ is the ARE-deletion label and $H_i$ is the RNA half-life readout for row $i$, the admissible statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{ARE}} &= \operatorname{sep}\bigl(H_i;D_i\mid C_i,B_i,I_i\bigr),\\
C_i &= (\mathrm{transcription}_i,\mathrm{mRNA}_i,\mathrm{structure}_i,\mathrm{motif}_i,\mathrm{GC/dinucleotide}_i,\mathrm{codon\ pair}_i,\mathrm{translation}_i),\\
B_i &= \mathrm{batch}_i,\qquad I_i=\mathrm{isoform\ state}_i.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign-concordant result says only that the deletion-labeled finite rows preserve an RNA half-life direction under the declared permutation boundary.  It does not identify a decay-factor RBP, does not establish occupancy, and does not supply perturbation or rescue logic.

\paragraph{Independent ARE-oriented transfer check.}
A second verified record is relevant because it tests whether an ARE-side signal remains visible in endogenous $3'$ UTR half-life data rather than only in the deletion panel.  For \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_endogenous\_3utr\_halflife\_transfer\_rnadecaycafe}, the verified run \texttt{e96bf26e626b} retained $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=14933$ genes, used $2000$ permutations, and recorded $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$.  The baseline coefficient of determination is $R^2_{\mathrm{base}}=0.0657771$, the test value is $R^2_{\mathrm{test}}=0.0827561$, and the increment is $\Delta R^2=0.016979$.  The ARE coefficient is $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, with negative sign.  This second finite table is not merged with the deletion table into a universal law.  It functions only as a same-theme RNA-stability readout showing that an ARE-loaded covariate can carry a small but measurable half-life contribution in an endogenous gene panel.

\paragraph{Licensed conclusion.}
The chapter licenses a bounded cross-layer relation only at the RNA-stability surface.  The primary deletion record supplies a finite deletion-to-half-life boundary with $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, checked by pooled label permutations.  The transfer record supplies a separate ARE-to-half-life finite statistic over $14933$ genes with $\Delta R^2=0.016979$ and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$.  Together they justify the statement that ARE-labeled finite rows can be treated as RNA half-life boundary rows when the statistic is explicitly conditioned against transcription, mRNA abundance, isoform state, local structure, sequence composition, codon-pair context, translation readout, and batch.  The result remains local to the measured rows and their declared controls.

\paragraph{Mechanism refusal.}
No RBP-mediated decay mechanism is concluded from these numbers alone.  A decay-factor RBP bridge would require same-window measured RBP occupancy, a coordinate--occupancy decoupling design, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and failure of local structure, motif-density, transcription, isoform, abundance, and batch alternatives to reproduce the pattern.  The present verified facts do not contain those rows.  Therefore an RNA half-life difference cannot be interpreted here as translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  Those interpretations remain unavailable unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite measurement and statistic.
