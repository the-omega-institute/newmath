\subsection{Positional dwell localization of the $f_3$ stress abundance component}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a single powered yeast panel in which the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate named $f_3\_stress$ is tested against position-resolved ribosome-dwell summaries and a matched protein-abundance target.  The organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The active positional-dwell join contains $n=1257$ genes, drawn from a base availability count of $1800$ rows.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the tested $f_3$ source has rank $1$.  The target is the scalar component
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10}(\mathrm{protein\ abundance})}\bigr),\\
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f_3}\|^2}{\|\widetilde{\log_{10}(\mathrm{protein\ abundance})}\|^2} &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here tildes denote residualization against the finite row controls used by the experiment: amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, the $M$-density baseline, and the matched organism-specific design terms carried by the run.  The dwell readout is not a generic translation score.  It is a pair of per-gene positional summaries computed from file-provided A-site occupancy windows, one assigned to the $5'$ head or ramp side and one assigned to the $3'$ tail or body side.

\paragraph{Tested statistic.}
The experiment fits the base design and then asks whether adding a segment-specific dwell readout improves held-out prediction of $P_{f_3}$.  The folds are fixed at $5$, and the null is generated by $120$ deterministic row-label permutations of $P_{f_3}$ with the base, full, and fold assignments held fixed.  The positional claim is therefore a finite comparison of two segment increments, not an unrestricted search over ribosome traces:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{5'} &= R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(P_{f_3}\mid Z,\mathrm{dwell}_{5'}) - R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(P_{f_3}\mid Z),\\
\Delta R^2_{3'} &= R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(P_{f_3}\mid Z,\mathrm{dwell}_{3'}) - R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}(P_{f_3}\mid Z).
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded outcome is asymmetric.  The leading segment by held-out increment is $5'$; the dominant segment is also $5'$.  The $5'$ ramp gate passed, while the $3'$ body gate did not pass:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
\mathrm{three\_prime\_body\_passed} &= \mathrm{false},\\
\mathrm{leading\_segment\_by\_delta\_}R^2 &= 5',\\
\mathrm{dominant\_segment} &= 5'.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run status is recorded as passed under seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic} and experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered}.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell result.}
The positional call is consistent with the companion dwell-mediation computation on the same yeast context.  That run used the same base availability count $1800$, the same active join size $1257$, the same fold count $5$, the same $120$-permutation null scheme, the same $z$-basis rank $24$, and the same $P_{f_3}$ target norm and target fraction.  In the non-positional test, the base model had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed $f_3$--dwell correlation was $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Thus the positional experiment does not stand alone as a vague segment label: it refines an already positive dwell increment by showing that the finite positional split assigns the surviving segment to the $5'$ ramp rather than to the $3'$ body.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The chapter-specific readback is the following finite-row statement.  In the yeast panel, after the declared controls are applied, the $f_3\_stress$ abundance component is associated with positional ribosome-dwell information.  When the dwell information is split by window, the $5'$ ramp window is the segment that passes the permutation-calibrated held-out increment test, while the $3'$ body window does not.  This supports a localized dwell readout for the $f_3$ abundance component:
$$
\begin{aligned}
B^*_{Q6}(f_3\_stress) &\longrightarrow P_{f_3},\\
\mathrm{dwell}_{5'} &\text{ carries the recorded positional increment},\\
\mathrm{dwell}_{3'} &\text{ is not the passing segment in this finite panel}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a row-level statistical readback over the recorded yeast matrix.  It does not assert that $f_3\_stress$ is a universal translation law, a causal elongation mechanism, or a structural determinant.  It says that the measured positional dwell surface contains a $5'$-localized increment for the $f_3$-projected protein-abundance component under the stated finite controls.

\paragraph{Nearby boundaries.}
Other verified surfaces put this result in context without promoting it beyond its data.  The measured ribosome-TE survival run reports yeast $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{measured}}=0.13681931343730436$ with $9$ surviving $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, and the direct protein-omics run reports yeast $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with $9$ surviving coordinates.  Those are broad survival surfaces.  The present record is narrower: it is about the positional dwell localization of the $f_3$ abundance component.  A separate structural-order ramp analysis found a positive ramp-side structural increment at the primary ramp length $50$, with weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out increment $0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, and ramp-minus-body increment $0.001378137149572267$; however, its own conclusion was \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}, because the paired localization gate did not pass.  That nearby structural-order outcome should not be merged with the present dwell result.  The present result is a positional ribosome-dwell readout for $P_{f_3}$, not a folded-structure, protein-function, phenotype, or physical-admissibility result.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite matrix supports only the stated positional dwell readback.  No claim about translation causality, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or universality follows from this chapter.  Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic at that layer.  In particular, a passing $5'$ dwell increment does not by itself identify a molecular cause, does not establish a synonymous-edit rescue, does not prove ribosome-mediated mechanism, and does not turn protein abundance into a functional assay.
