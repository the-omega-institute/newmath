\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The supported statement is statistical and positional.  It says that, in the joined yeast table, the $f3\_stress$ component of protein abundance is better read through a $5'$ positional dwell summary than through the matched body summary, under the registered base design and permutation protocol.  It does not by itself assert translation causality, elongation mechanism, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality.  A stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic, for example perturbation, rescue, structure, localization, turnover, or function data joined to the same coding-sequence units and evaluated after the same residual controls.  Without that additional contact, the chapter remains a finite positional dwell readback for $P_{f3}$ and not a general mechanism claim.
