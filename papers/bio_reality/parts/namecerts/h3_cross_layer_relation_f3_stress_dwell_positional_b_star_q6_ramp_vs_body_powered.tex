\subsection{Position-resolved dwell readback for the $f_3$ stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the abundance component carried by the $f_3$ stress coordinate is preferentially readable through a position-resolved ribosome-dwell surface rather than through an undifferentiated coding-body summary. The carrier is the finite yeast join on which $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, the standard sibling controls, local ribosome-occupancy summaries, and measured protein abundance are all present on the same gene rows. The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the row set used for the positional dwell test has $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$ after the join, with a base sampling scale $n_{\mathrm{base}}=1800$, $5$ held-out folds, and $120$ deterministic readout permutations. The $f_3$ direction has rank $1$, the control basis has rank $24$, and the target is the projection $P_{f_3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$ stress coordinate. In this run,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f_3}\|^2}{\|P\|^2_{\mathrm{target}}} &= 0.1999063916718794,\\
\operatorname{rank}(f_3) &= 1,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 24.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positional readout is defined from file-provided A-site occupancy windows, separated into a $5'$ head or ramp segment and a $3'$ tail or body segment. The null is not a parametric asymptotic approximation: it is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f_3}$, with the base design, full design, and fold assignment held fixed, run separately for each segment and for the joint segment design.

\paragraph{Observed discriminant.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for the $5'$ window and negative for the $3'$ window. The run reports \texttt{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed = true}, \texttt{three\_prime\_body\_passed = false}, \texttt{dominant\_segment = 5prime}, and \texttt{leading\_segment\_by\_delta\_r2 = 5prime}. Thus the supported statement is not merely that a dwell readout can absorb part of the $f_3$-projected abundance component; it is that the detectable dwell increment is positionally localized to the $5'$ ramp side of the available yeast ribosome-occupancy surface. The local finite-row classification is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{status} &= \mathrm{passed},\\
\mathrm{dominant\ segment} &= 5',\\
\mathrm{ramp\ gate} &= \mathrm{positive},\\
\mathrm{body\ gate} &= \mathrm{not\ positive}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The paired dwell mediation run on the same biological theme gives the surrounding scale: with $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations, the base held-out fit has $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, dwell alone has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out dwell increment is $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$. Its permutation reference has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$. The same run records an $f_3$--dwell correlation of $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate right-tail probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. These numbers explain why the positional result is a refinement of a real dwell signal rather than a search over an absent mediation surface.

\paragraph{Ramp versus body interpretation.}
A separate structure-oriented ramp/body audit supplies a useful calibration, but it does not promote the present dwell result to a structural-order claim. At primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, while the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, so ramp minus body is $0.001378137149572267$. The sign-flip probabilities are $0.0478515625$ for ramp positivity, $0.54296875$ for body positivity, and $0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body contrast. That audit classifies the structural-order surface as \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp signal is positive, but the stricter paired localization gate does not pass. The present chapter therefore keeps a narrower conclusion. It says that the $f_3$-projected abundance component has a detectable $5'$ positional dwell readback in the yeast finite panel; it does not say that the coordinate has established folded-structure localization or a general ramp mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to the broader $B^*_{Q6}$ protein surface.}
The positional dwell signal sits on top of a larger cross-layer background in which $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are already visible in protein abundance and translation readouts. In the direct yeast protein-omics survival matrix, the yeast PAXdb join uses $5815$ CDS rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and the direct $Q\to P$ survival is $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive threshold. In the measured ribosome-TE surface, the yeast TE join reports $5128$ genes used, a ribosome-TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$, and measured translation survival $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, again with $9$ surviving coordinates. In the measured translation-conditioned abundance decomposition, the yeast three-way join has $5128$ rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$. These values justify treating the positional dwell result as a layer-specific refinement inside an already powered yeast readout system, rather than as an isolated visual pattern.

\paragraph{Compression and residual risk.}
The finite-row record is not an irreducibility certificate. A dictionary-style reduction run on the unified yeast context reports $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $n_{\mathrm{join\ available}}=4758$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, description-length penalty $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, join threshold $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, accepted coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, and unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$. The positional dwell result should therefore be read as a concrete, positive, finite-row localization of one measured component, not as a claim that all residual $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance structure has been reduced to dwell. It also remains observational: the fits use measured rows and deterministic held-out tests, but they do not perturb codons, rescue ribosome traffic, or establish temporal causation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is the following. In yeast, after residualizing the $f_3$ coordinate and protein abundance against the declared control basis, the $P_{f_3}$ abundance component has a positionally resolved dwell readback whose leading and passing segment is the $5'$ ramp, while the $3'$ body segment does not pass the same finite-row criterion. No stronger biological interpretation follows from these rows alone. In particular, this chapter does not infer translation realization beyond the measured or bounded readout used in the fit, does not infer folding or structural order, does not infer physical admissibility, does not infer molecular function or organismal phenotype, does not assert a causal mechanism, and does not state a universal biological law. Each of those stronger layers would require its own matched contact surface and its own finite statistic on the relevant rows.
