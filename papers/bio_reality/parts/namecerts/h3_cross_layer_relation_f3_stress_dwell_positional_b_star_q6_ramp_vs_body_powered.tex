\subsection{Position-resolved dwell localization of the $f_3$ stress abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}

\paragraph{Finite matrix surface.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$ stress coordinate in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel points to a positional ribosome-dwell readout when the downstream target is restricted to the part of protein abundance aligned with that coordinate.  The active finite surface is a yeast same-row join: coding-sequence codon counts define the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, the response is log-protein abundance after the declared control projection, and the readout columns are per-gene relative ribosome-occupancy summaries split into a $5'$ head window and a $3'$ tail window.  The organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the positional experiment uses $n=1257$ joined genes, $5$ held-out folds, $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, and a control basis of rank $24$.  The tested source coordinate is one-dimensional, $\operatorname{rank}(f_3)=1$, and the target vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3} &= \text{projection of the controlled log-protein-abundance vector onto controlled } f_3\text{ stress},\\
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f_3}\|^2}{\|P_{\mathrm{target}}\|^2} &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run status is passed.  The leading segment by held-out gain is the $5'$ segment, the declared dominant segment is $5'$-prime, the $5'$ ramp gate passed, and the $3'$ body gate did not pass.  Thus the finite readback is not merely that dwell features carry some information; within this row set and this target projection, the positive positional evidence is localized to the head-window side of the dwell summary.

\paragraph{Dwell increment before positional splitting.}
The unsplit dwell run gives the scale of the signal that the positional experiment refines.  On the same yeast abundance target family, with $n=1257$, $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, and rank-$24$ controls, the base held-out model has $R^2=0.1855578757644517$ and the base-plus-dwell model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  The held-out increment is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.20412261520502883-0.1855578757644517\\
&= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation reference for this increment has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The observed dwell increment is therefore far above the fixed permutation boundary.  The same run reports a dwell-only $R^2$ of $0.034336580953121976$, an $f_3$-dwell correlation of $0.1615441450901653$, and an approximate $p$-value $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ for that correlation.  These numbers justify asking where in the coding sequence the dwell readout is concentrated, but they do not by themselves identify a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Ramp-versus-body comparison.}
The positional run answers the localization question by comparing the head and tail occupancy summaries under the same row-label permutation design.  Its finite verdict is that at least one positional window has a positive base-plus-segment held-out gain above the fixed null boundary, and the window that carries the evidence is the $5'$ ramp.  A separate structure-oriented ramp/body diagnostic over the same $f_3$ direction gives useful numerical scale without changing the present claim: for the primary $50$-codon ramp window, the weighted ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the ramp-minus-body contrast is $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probabilities in that diagnostic are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Pr(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}}>0) &= 0.0478515625,\\
\Pr(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}}>0) &= 0.54296875,\\
\Pr(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp}}-\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}}>0) &= 0.05859375.
\end{aligned}
$$
For alternative ramp widths, the $30$-codon window gives ramp and body increments $0.0007864264335388174$ and $0.00023737813475316197$, while the $70$-codon window gives $0.0007157308766185055$ and $-0.0008384704154638841$.  That related diagnostic classifies the structural-order outcome as \emph{ramp-positive but not localized}; this chapter uses the more specific dwell-position result only for the finite claim that the dwell-mediated abundance component is led by the $5'$ window and not by the $3'$ body window.

\paragraph{Matched-row context.}
The yeast readout is rank-powered rather than an underdetermined all-one surface.  In the broader measured-TE row set, yeast has $5128$ genes with measured translation-efficiency readouts and a ribosome-TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$ against the $5815$ CDS rows reported for the protein-abundance join.  The direct protein-omics survival run for yeast uses $5815$ joined proteins, with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and reports $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates surviving the positive numerical threshold.  The measured-TE mediation run gives yeast $S_{QT}^{\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, and a measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  Across the four measured-TE organisms in that run, the mediation fractions are $0.0$ for zebrafish, $0.04091158340950673$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.0027966485036786744$ for human, and $0.4793069656345167$ for yeast.  The partial readout dictionary run on the yeast context uses $n=1800$ rows, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$; its selection-aware coverage is $0.24538211439730545$ with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  These surrounding rows show that the yeast $f_3$ dwell-position claim sits inside a measured, nondegenerate cross-layer panel, while still leaving a large residual component outside the tested readout family.

\paragraph{Readback and boundary.}
The finite conclusion is narrow.  The $f_3$-aligned abundance component in yeast has a positive dwell increment, and the position-resolved split places the leading finite signal in the $5'$ ramp rather than the $3'$ body window.  The admissible local reading is therefore: within the joined yeast rows, $f_3$ stress is associated with a head-window ribosome-dwell summary that improves held-out prediction of the $f_3$ abundance component beyond the base covariates and beyond the fixed permutation boundary.  This does not assert translation causation, elongation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Protein abundance, measured TE, dwell summaries, and codon residuals remain separate measured surfaces unless a further layer-specific contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding controlled attenuation or perturbation test.  In particular, the positive $5'$ dwell result is not a folded-structure claim, not a synonymous-edit rescue claim, not a pathway-function claim, and not a claim that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ geometry alone is biological evidence.  It is a finite positional readback on a rank-powered yeast matrix.
