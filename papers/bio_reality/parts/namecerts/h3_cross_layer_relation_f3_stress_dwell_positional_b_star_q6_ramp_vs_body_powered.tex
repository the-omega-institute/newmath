\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The passed result is the following finite statement: in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, for the $P_{f_3}$ projection of controlled protein abundance, the position-resolved ribosome-dwell increment is led by the $5'$ segment, the $5'$ ramp test passes, and the $3'$ body test does not pass under the fixed-design row-label permutation null. Any such stronger statement would require its own layer-matched contact and a separate finite statistic showing that the stronger layer is actually observed.
