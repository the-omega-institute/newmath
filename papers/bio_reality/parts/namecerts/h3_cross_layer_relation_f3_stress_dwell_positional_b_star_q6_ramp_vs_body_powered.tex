\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite row-level test for the $f_3$-stress coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface. $$
\begin{aligned}

$$
\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{pass}(5')&=\mathrm{true},\\
