\subsection{Positional dwell localization of the $f_3$ stress abundance component}\label{subsec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-b-star-q6-ramp-body}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The object in this record is the $f_3$ stress coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface.  The tested rows are yeast coding sequences for which codon-derived residual coordinates, the shared covariate design, protein abundance, and positional ribosome-occupancy summaries can all be joined.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the positional dwell experiment reports $n=1257$ joined rows after the active base plus positional-dwell join, with $1800$ base rows available for the fold design, $5$ held-out folds, rank $1$ for the $f_3$ coordinate, and a covariate basis rank of $24$.  The target vector is not total protein abundance itself, but the projection $P_{f3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto $Z$-residualized $f_3$ stress on that same joined table.

\paragraph{Controls and projected target.}
The residualization basis removes the ordinary sequence-composition and gene-size channels before the dwell readout is allowed to compete for explanatory power.  In the companion direct protein-omics run, yeast has $5815$ joined protein rows, a join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$, $23$ effective columns in $Z$ out of $24$ control columns, and residual degrees of freedom $5792$.  The direct $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance surface in yeast has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the measured ribosome translation-efficiency contact, yeast has $5128$ genes with measured TE, a TE join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$, and
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers locate the positional dwell test inside a real cross-layer table, but they do not themselves settle the positional question.  The positional experiment asks a narrower question: once $P_{f3}$ is isolated, is the incremental dwell signal carried preferentially by the early-window ribosome-occupancy summary or by the late-window summary?

\paragraph{Dwell localization statistic.}
The tested dwell readout consists of per-gene relative ribosome-occupancy summaries computed separately from file-provided $5'$ head and $3'$ tail A-site occupancy windows.  The deterministic null keeps the base and full designs and the held-out folds fixed, then permutes the row labels of $P_{f3}$ separately for each segment and for the joint segment design.  The experiment uses $120$ such permutations with seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic}.  The finite outcome is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
P_{f3}\hbox{ fraction of target} &= 0.1999063916718794,\\
\hbox{leading segment} &= 5',\\
\hbox{$5'$ ramp gate} &= \hbox{passed},\\
\hbox{$3'$ body gate} &= \hbox{not passed}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the row-level signal is not merely a generic association between $f_3$ stress and ribosome occupancy.  Within the available yeast dwell table, the increment that survives the held-out and permutation comparison is localized to the $5'$ window, while the $3'$ tail/body window does not independently pass the same rule.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell result.}
The non-positional dwell mediation run on the same yeast coordinate reports $n=1257$ joined rows, the same $P_{f3}$ norm squared $44.20095750389323$, a base held-out value $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, a dwell-only value $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and a full value $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  Its held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\hbox{permutation null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\hbox{permutation null mean} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\hbox{permutation null range} &= [-0.013799622026666476,\,0.005001350039772926],\\
\mathrm{corr}(f_3,\mathrm{dwell}) &= 0.1615441450901653,\\
 p_{\mathrm{approx}} &= 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positional result refines that positive dwell finding by splitting the dwell readout into head and tail components.  The head component is the dominant segment by held-out increment; the tail component does not pass the declared threshold.  The appropriate interpretation is therefore positional localization of a measured dwell increment, not a claim that every ribosome-density feature, every organism, or every $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate behaves in the same way.

\paragraph{Relation to structural ramp tests.}
A separate structural-order ramp experiment also uses yeast-centered $f_3$ ramp and body summaries, but it tests a different target.  There, the primary ramp size is $50$, the weighted ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the weighted ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probabilities are $0.0478515625$ for ramp being positive, $0.54296875$ for body being positive, and $0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body contrast.  Its recorded conclusion is ramp positive but not localized.  That neighboring structural-order result should not be read into the dwell result.  The present record is stronger only for positional dwell mediation of $P_{f3}$ in yeast, because its own finite row experiment says the $5'$ segment passed and the $3'$ segment did not.

\paragraph{Residual and dictionary context.}
The same yeast family also supports a broader partial-reduction view rather than a complete mechanism.  In the partial mediation certificate, the unified yeast context has $4758$ available joined rows, $1800$ sampled join rows, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  The best selected readout family covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the target after the selection-aware null level $0.029149007242956115$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The related irreducibility run uses the same $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $1800$ joined rows, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations, and records that at least one listed reduction family refutes the original primitive-residual hypothesis rather than certifying irreducibility.  These rows keep the positional dwell finding in scale: the $5'$ dwell component is a real reduction of the $f_3$ abundance component, but it is not a complete account of the residual surface.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
For this chapter, the local four-case readback is: case A is absent because the yeast rows do not show a translation-null and protein-null outcome for the $f_3$ abundance component; case B is absent because the positional dwell table does show a positive measured readout; case C is not the right label because the reported signal is not merely direct protein-omics survival; and case D is the admissible finite-row label for the positional dwell step, with $5'$ head dwell carrying the detected increment and $3'$ tail/body dwell failing the same gate.  This label is a statistical readback over the joined rows and controls, not a biological mechanism assignment.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The result says that in the available yeast table, the $f_3$-projected protein-abundance component has a measured positional dwell increment concentrated in the $5'$ window.  It does not by itself establish translation causality, codon-edit rescue, ribosome mechanism, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law.  Such conclusions would require separate layer-matched contacts and statistics at those layers.  Bulk protein abundance, measured TE, and dwell occupancy are useful readouts here only because their rows are joined to the same coding sequences and residualized against the declared controls; none of them turns the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate geometry into an unrestricted biological explanation.
