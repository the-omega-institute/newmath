\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the $f_3$ stress coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual family.  The finite surface is not an abstract claim about codon usage alone: it is the joined yeast matrix on which a protein-abundance component, already projected onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$ stress direction, is tested against position-resolved ribosome-dwell summaries.  The organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the active join contains $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$ rows, drawn from a base availability count $n_{\mathrm{base}}=1800$.  The fitted source direction has rank $1$, and the control basis has rank $24$.  The abundance target is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10}(\mathrm{protein\ abundance})}\bigr),\\
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f_3}\|^2}{\|\widetilde{\log_{10}(\mathrm{protein\ abundance})}\|^2} &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here tildes denote residualization against the recorded control family: length, amino-acid composition, GC3, $M$-density, and the matched row controls used by the yeast abundance context.  The numerical point is that the tested object is a one-dimensional abundance component, not the full abundance vector and not a whole-proteome mechanism.

\paragraph{Position split.}
The dwell readout is a pair of per-gene relative ribosome-occupancy summaries computed separately from file-provided $5'$ head and $3'$ tail A-site occupancy windows.  The experiment fixes the base and full designs and evaluates the positional terms by five-fold held-out prediction.  Its null model is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f_3}$ with the designs and folds held fixed, applied separately to each segment and to the joint segment design.  The registered run used $120$ permutations and the deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic}.  The finite decision is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{dominant\ segment} &= 5',\\
\mathrm{leading\ segment\ by\ }\Delta R^2 &= 5',\\
\mathrm{five\mbox{-}prime\ ramp\ passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
\mathrm{three\mbox{-}prime\ body\ passed} &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the positive dwell increment localizes to the $5'$ window in this row set.  The statement is narrower than a global positional law: at least one window passes the fixed permutation threshold, and in this run the leading and dominant window is the $5'$ segment.

The positional packet refines this non-positional mediation result by asking where the predictive dwell information is concentrated.  It does not create a new molecular pathway claim; it sharpens the finite row readback from ``dwell carries some of the $f_3$ abundance component'' to ``the detected dwell increment is led by the $5'$ head window in this joined yeast panel.''

\paragraph{Boundary against structural promotion.}
A separate structural-order ramp test on the same $B^*_{Q6}$ program records a distinct verdict: \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}.  For the primary ramp length $50$, its weighted $f_3$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, its weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the weighted ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$.  The one-sided sign-flip values are $0.0478515625$ for the ramp, $0.54296875$ for the body, and $0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body contrast.  Therefore the structural-order packet reports a positive ramp signal but does not pass its localization gate.  This is kept separate from the dwell packet: position-resolved ribosome dwell is a translation-readout surface, whereas structural order would require its own layer-matched contact and its own successful statistic.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite row conclusion is ready: in the registered yeast matrix, the $f_3$ protein-abundance component has a position-resolved dwell increment, and the leading passing segment is the $5'$ head window rather than the $3'$ tail/body window.  The exact status is \texttt{passed} for experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered}, run \texttt{6db78e1bf746}.  What follows is a bounded readout statement only.  It does not by itself establish translation causality, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, evolutionary direction, or universality.  Any such claim would require an additional layer-specific contact and a separate finite statistic on matched rows.
