\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record isolates the $f_3$ stress coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface and asks whether the abundance component attributed to that coordinate is better read through a 5-prime ribosome-dwell window or through a 3-prime/body dwell window.  The finite carrier is the joined yeast table on which codon residuals, control residuals, ribosome-occupancy summaries, and protein abundance are all present on the same gene rows.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the positional experiment uses $n=1257$ joined rows inside a base design of $1800$ rows, five held-out folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, $\operatorname{rank}(f_3)=1$, and a control basis of rank $24$.  The target vector is the projection of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$ stress direction on the active base-plus-positional-dwell join. \begin{aligned}

\paragraph{Ramp-versus-body verdict.}
The finite result is positionally asymmetric.  The leading segment by held-out increment is the 5-prime segment, the dominant segment is recorded as \texttt{5prime}, the 5-prime ramp gate passed, and the 3-prime/body gate did not pass.  The null model is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f_3}$ with the base and full designs and folds held fixed, run separately for each segment and for the joint segment design.  The resulting finite classification is therefore not merely ``dwell helps''; it says that the accepted increment is localized to the 5-prime/head window in this yeast table, while the 3-prime/tail window is not accepted by the same rule. The present dwell-positional record is sharper for ribosome dwell: at least one positional dwell window crosses its fixed permutation threshold, and the accepted window is the 5-prime/head segment.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrowly finite-row and window-specific.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, after the declared residualization and held-out testing, the $f_3$-aligned component of protein abundance has a positive positional dwell increment whose leading accepted window is the 5-prime/head ribosome-occupancy window; the corresponding 3-prime/tail window does not pass.  This supports a rank-powered cross-layer readback from the $f_3$ synonymous residual coordinate to a measured positional ribosome-dwell summary and then to the $f_3$ component of abundance, under the recorded controls and row joins.  It does not by itself assert a universal law, a causal pathway, a translation mechanism in the perturbational sense, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or evolutionary direction.  Those stronger conclusions would require their own layer-matched measurements and tests beyond this finite yeast dwell table.
