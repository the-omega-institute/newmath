\subsection{Position-resolved dwell localization of the $f_3$ stress abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}

\paragraph{Finite matrix surface.}
This record concerns the $f_3$ stress coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual family.  The active row set is a matched Saccharomyces cerevisiae panel in which coding-sequence codon counts, controlled protein abundance, and positional ribosome-occupancy summaries are available on the same gene rows.  The tested target is not total protein abundance itself, but the one-dimensional component
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3}
  &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10} P}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where both $\widetilde f_3$ and $\widetilde{\log_{10} P}$ are residualized against the shared control basis $Z$.  The verified run reports $\operatorname{rank}(f_3)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, and a base training size of $1800$ rows.  The squared norm of the target component is $44.20095750389323$, and this component accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the controlled abundance target used in the positional dwell fit.

\paragraph{Controls and readout.}
The dwell readout is explicitly positional: each gene contributes relative ribosome-occupancy summaries from file-provided $5'$ head windows and $3'$ tail windows, and the two segment families are tested separately as well as jointly.  The null comparison is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f_3}$, with base and full designs and cross-validation folds held fixed; the run uses $120$ permutations and $5$ folds.  This matters because the positive claim is not that any arbitrary ribosome-occupancy covariate correlates with the residual.  The finite assertion is narrower: after the base controls are already present, adding the segment-specific dwell readout gives a held-out gain for at least one positional window that exceeds its fixed permutation boundary.

\paragraph{Positional verdict.}
The positional run passed with dominant segment $5'$ and leading segment by held-out $\Delta R^2$ also $5'$.  The recorded flags are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
\mathrm{three\_prime\_body\_passed} &= \mathrm{false},\\
\mathrm{dominant\_segment} &= 5'.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite result localizes the dwell increment to the head or ramp-side readout, rather than to the tail/body-side readout.  The experimentally certified statement is that the $f_3$ component of controlled protein abundance has a position-resolved dwell increment, with at least one segment-specific base-plus-dwell model producing positive held-out improvement above the segment-matched permutation threshold.  The record does not say that the $3'$ body segment is negative in a mechanistic sense; it says only that the registered $3'$ body gate did not pass in this finite matrix.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell fit.}
The corresponding non-positional dwell mediation fit uses the same organism and the same $P_{f_3}$ target definition.  In that run $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, $\operatorname{rank}(f_3)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $5$ folds are used, and $120$ deterministic permutations define the null.  The base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-only readout has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the full model has held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  The resulting held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
\mathrm{corr}(f_3,\mathrm{dwell}) &= 0.1615441450901653,\\
p_{\mathrm{approx}} &= 7.873916057196935\cdot 10^{-9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positional result refines this aggregate dwell signal by asking where the dwell contribution enters.  It does not create a separate biological layer; it decomposes the same abundance-projected $f_3$ signal by ribosome-position windows.

\paragraph{Ramp comparison against an independent structural-order readout.}
A related structural-order ramp/body run also reports a ramp-positive but not localized conclusion.  At the primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, while the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, giving ramp minus body $0.001378137149572267$.  The ramp sign-flip probability for being greater than zero is $0.0478515625$, the body sign-flip probability is $0.54296875$, and the paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability is $0.05859375$.  The same run records positive ramp signal in $2$ primary organisms, positive body signal in $0$, and ramp greater than body in $2$.  These numbers are useful as a concordant positional pattern, but they do not upgrade the present dwell result into a structural-order claim.  The structural-order run itself classifies the outcome as ramp-positive but not passing the paired localization gate.

\paragraph{Translation and abundance context.}
The broader $B^{*}_{Q6}$ panel contains nine residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress.  Direct protein-omics survival has been computed on measured PAXdb abundance after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae the PAXdb join uses $5815$ proteins with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all nine coordinates surviving the positive threshold.  Other examples from the same direct protein-abundance surface include Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 with $3739$ joined proteins, hit rate $0.9978649586335735$, and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, and Homo sapiens with $19053$ joined proteins, hit rate $0.9779294769799313$, and $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$.  These direct abundance associations motivate the positional dwell decomposition, but by themselves they are not a translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Measured translation readouts.}
Measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency supplies a separate bounded readout.  In the yeast measured-TE run, $5128$ genes are used, the ribosome-TE join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$, the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $5105$, and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all nine coordinates surviving.  The same surface reports $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.009394266754822262$ for Escherichia coli on $3415$ genes, $0.018837911344127926$ for Homo sapiens on $11554$ genes, and $0.04034217614654355$ for Danio rerio on $13140$ genes.  In the measured $Q\to T\to P$ abundance decomposition, yeast has $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  Escherichia coli has measured mediation fraction $0.04091158340950673$, Homo sapiens has $0.0027966485036786744$, and Danio rerio is clipped to $0.0$ from a raw suppressive value $-0.04801486048721136$.  These values show why the yeast dwell readout is a relevant translation-adjacent layer, while also showing that the mediation strength is organism- and readout-dependent.

\paragraph{Stability and dictionary reductions.}
A joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition on yeast reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$; the right-tail permutation probability is $0.000999000999000999$.  A separate readout-dictionary reduction on the unified yeast context uses $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$ of $4758$ available rows, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$.  Its selection-aware coverage is $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$, above selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$, leaving unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The irreducibility check uses the same $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and classifies the present registered target as tested rather than as a proof of global irreducibility.  These companion facts keep the dwell-localization claim calibrated: part of the $f_3$ abundance component is reducible to measured readouts, but a large remainder is not explained by the current small readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Four-case readback for this chapter.}
For the present finite row surface, the chapter-level readback is case D for the $5'$ dwell window and non-passing for the $3'$ dwell window, in the limited sense of a translation-adjacent positional readout attached to the $f_3$ abundance projection.  The supporting chain is: the $f_3$ abundance component is nontrivial after $Z$ controls; aggregate dwell adds held-out explanatory power above a fixed row-label null; the positional split assigns the passing dwell increment to the $5'$ segment; and the $3'$ body segment does not pass its registered gate.  Formally, the finite statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\exists\, W\in\{5',3'\}\;\text{such that}\;\Delta R^2(P_{f_3};\mathrm{dwell}_W\mid \mathrm{base})>\mathrm{Null}_{95,W},\\
&\text{and in the verified row surface the leading such }W\text{ is }5'.
\end{aligned}
$$
The statement is deliberately local to this yeast matrix, this $f_3$ target projection, these positional dwell summaries, and this deterministic null construction.

\paragraph{Scientific boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion follows from this record alone.  The positional dwell result is not a claim of translation causality, ribosome mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality.  The $5'$ label identifies where the registered readout improves prediction of the controlled $f_3$ abundance component; it does not show that synonymous edits at the ramp cause abundance changes, that elongation is the unique mediator, or that structural order is determined by the coordinate.  Any such conclusion would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as perturbation, rescue, structure, localization, turnover, function, or phenotype measurements, with its own finite statistic under the relevant controls.  The present result should therefore be read as a passed position-resolved dwell association for $P_{f_3}$ in yeast, with an explicit no-promotion boundary above that level.
