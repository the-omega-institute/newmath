\subsection{Position-resolved dwell localization of the $f_3$ abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record tests whether the $f_3$ coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family has a position-localized dwell readback in the same yeast row universe used for the abundance residual. The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The tested target is not raw protein abundance, but the one-dimensional component
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10} P}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where both abundance and $f_3$ are residualized against the shared covariate basis. The run reports $\|P_{f_3}\|^2 = 44.20095750389323$, with $P_{f_3}$ carrying $0.1999063916718794$ of the controlled target component. The active positional join contains $n=1257$ genes, the training cap is $1800$ rows, the residualized $f_3$ block has rank $1$, and the control basis has rank $24$. The model comparison uses $5$ held-out folds and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations of $P_{f_3}$ with the base and segment designs held fixed.

\paragraph{Segment readout.}
The dwell surface is assembled from per-gene relative ribosome-occupancy summaries computed separately from file-provided $5'$ head and $3'$ tail A-site occupancy windows. The run classifies the leading segment by held-out increment as $5'$ and records the dominant segment as \texttt{5prime}. The $5'$ ramp gate passed, while the $3'$ body gate did not pass. In finite-row terms, the result is therefore not merely that a dwell covariate improves a yeast abundance projection; the improvement is localized to the $5'$ side of the positional readout under the fixed permutation test.

\paragraph{Dwell mediation baseline.}
The non-positional dwell mediation run on the same yeast setting supplies the scalar context for the positional split. On $n=1257$ joined genes and $5$ folds, the base design gives held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-only readout gives $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the base-plus-dwell design gives $R^2=0.20412261520502883$. The held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}} &= 0.01856473944057712,
\end{aligned}
$$
which exceeds the deterministic permutation $95\%$ boundary $0.0013115362189692937$; the same null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, and maximum $0.005001350039772926$. The observed $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. These numbers justify treating the positional test as a localization of an already positive dwell increment rather than as a search over an otherwise absent dwell signal.

\paragraph{Ramp-versus-body contrast.}
The related ramp/body structural-order probe is useful as a cautionary comparator because it also separates a $5'$ ramp from a body window, but it does not establish structural order. At the primary ramp width $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the weighted ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$. The one-sided sign-flip probabilities are $0.0478515625$ for positive ramp signal, $0.54296875$ for positive body signal, and $0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body comparison. The finite classification is \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp signal is positive, but the paired localization gate does not pass. This comparator prevents the present dwell result from being promoted into a structural claim; the supported statement is narrower, namely that the $f_3$ abundance component has a detectable $5'$ positional dwell increment in the yeast finite-row surface.

\paragraph{Protein and translation context.}
The broader protein-abundance surface confirms that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family is not empty against measured abundance in yeast. In the direct PAXdb abundance run, yeast has $5815$ joined CDS/protein rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, a residual design rank $9$ for the $Q$ block, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive threshold. The measured translation-efficiency run gives, for yeast, $5128$ genes with TE, ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, and $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving. The measured protein-abundance mediation run on yeast reports $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$. These surrounding rows place the positional dwell readback inside a rank-powered yeast translation/protein-abundance context, but the positional dwell statistic itself remains the only claim made here.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is positive for positional dwell localization of the $f_3$ abundance component: the run status is \texttt{passed}, the leading and dominant segment is $5'$, the $5'$ ramp gate is true, and the $3'$ body gate is false. The admissible reading is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{base} + \text{$5'$ dwell} &> \text{base under the fixed held-out permutation boundary},\\
\text{$3'$ body dwell} &\not\text{ pass the same positional gate}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a controlled finite-sample readout about positional ribosome-occupancy summaries and the $P_{f_3}$ abundance component in yeast. It is not a claim of translation causation, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or universality. Any such higher-layer statement would require a separate measured contact and its own controlled statistic. In particular, the positive $5'$ dwell localization does not by itself identify an elongation mechanism, a folding pathway, a structural-order state, a functional module, or an evolutionary direction; it only records that, after the declared controls and finite permutation boundary, the $f_3$-aligned abundance component is more strongly read by the $5'$ positional dwell surface than by the $3'$ body surface in the available yeast panel.
