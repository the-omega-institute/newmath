\subsection{Positional dwell localization of the $f_3$ stress abundance component}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record isolates the $f_3$ stress coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface and asks whether the protein-abundance component carried by that coordinate is better read through a positional ribosome-occupancy window at the 5-prime ramp or through the 3-prime/body window.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The finite join contains $n=1257$ genes on the active base-plus-positional-dwell panel, drawn from a base availability of $1800$ rows, with five held-out folds.  The residual target is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10} P}\bigr),\\
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f_3}\|^2}{\|\widetilde{\log_{10} P}\|^2} &= 0.1999063916718794,
\end{aligned}
$$
where all tildes denote residualization against the same finite control basis: amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The recorded control rank is $24$, and the isolated $f_3$ source has rank $1$.

\paragraph{Dwell readout.}
The measured dwell surface is not a modeled codon-weight score.  It is a per-gene positional relative ribosome-occupancy readout computed separately from file-provided 5-prime/head and 3-prime/tail A-site occupancy windows.  The same target vector $P_{f_3}$ is held fixed while each segment design is evaluated against a deterministic row-label permutation null.  The null model permutes $P_{f_3}$ while keeping the base design, full design, and fold assignment fixed, and it is run separately for the 5-prime segment, the 3-prime segment, and the joint segment design.  The registered run uses $120$ permutations and the deterministic seed $\texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic}$.

\paragraph{Positional call.}
The finite outcome is asymmetric.  The leading segment by held-out increment is the 5-prime segment, the dominant segment is recorded as $\texttt{5prime}$, the 5-prime ramp gate passed, and the 3-prime/body gate did not pass.  In the same notation,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{segment}_{\max} &= \texttt{5prime},\\
\operatorname{passed}(5\text{-prime}) &= \mathrm{true},\\
\operatorname{passed}(3\text{-prime/body}) &= \mathrm{false},\\
\operatorname{permutations} &= 120,\\
\operatorname{folds} &= 5.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the ready claim is not merely that an $f_3$ abundance component has some dwell readout.  It is the sharper finite-row statement that the detectable dwell increment is localized to the 5-prime/head window under this panel, while the 3-prime/tail body window is not the passing segment.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell test.}
The positional record is coherent with the companion dwell-mediation run on the same yeast context.  There the active join is again $n=1257$, with $1800$ base rows, $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, source rank $1$, and control-basis rank $24$.  The non-positional dwell readout has base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out increment
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These numbers supply the surrounding evidence that dwell is a real finite readout for the $P_{f_3}$ component before the positional split asks which segment carries the increment.

\paragraph{Ramp versus body comparison.}
A separate structural-order ramp/body run also reports a 5-prime positive tendency, but it does not pass the stronger localization gate.  With primary ramp length $50$, two primary organisms, and five folds, the recorded increments are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{f_3,\,\mathrm{ramp}} &= 0.0011165640103326878,\\
\Delta R^2_{f_3,\,\mathrm{body}} &= -0.00026157313923957926,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp-body}} &= 0.001378137149572267.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign-flip probabilities are $0.0478515625$ for positive ramp signal, $0.54296875$ for positive body signal, and $0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body comparison.  That run is classified as $\texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$: the ramp signal is positive, but the paired localization gate is not passed there.  The present dwell-positional record is therefore more specific: within the yeast positional ribosome-occupancy dwell surface, the 5-prime segment is the passing segment and the body segment is not.

\paragraph{Cross-layer boundary.}
The surrounding protein-omics and translation rows explain why the claim must remain narrow.  Direct protein-omics survival against PAXdb abundance is positive across the fifteen-organism panel, for example $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ in yeast, $0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.019256877907572044$ in \emph{Halobacterium salinarum}, with all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates surviving in those rows.  Measured ribosome-TE survival is also positive in the four measured-TE rows, with $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ in yeast, $0.009394266754822262$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.018837911344127926$ in human, and $0.04034217614654355$ in zebrafish.  The measured TE-to-abundance mediation fractions are nevertheless heterogeneous: yeast has $0.4793069656345167$, \emph{E. coli} has $0.04091158340950673$, human has $0.0027966485036786744$, and zebrafish is clipped to $0$ from a raw value of $-0.04801486048721136$.  A separate yeast readout-dictionary certificate leaves unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ after selection-aware coverage $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$, with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and selection-aware null $95\%$ value $0.029149007242956115$.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The ready conclusion is therefore finite and local: in the yeast positional dwell experiment, the $f_3$-projected protein-abundance component is associated with a passing 5-prime/head relative ribosome-occupancy dwell increment, while the 3-prime/tail body segment does not pass.  This is a row-level cross-layer relation between a residual codon-coordinate component, a measured positional dwell readout, and the protein-abundance projection $P_{f_3}$.  It does not by itself establish translation causality, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require an additional layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic, rather than being inferred from the positional dwell asymmetry.
