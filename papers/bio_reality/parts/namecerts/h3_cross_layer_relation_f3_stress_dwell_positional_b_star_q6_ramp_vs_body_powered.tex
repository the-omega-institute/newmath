\subsection{Positional dwell localization for the $f_3$ stress abundance component}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}  The tested surface is the Saccharomyces cerevisiae panel in which the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate named $f_3$ stress is first residualized against the same control family used by the sibling protein-abundance analyses, and the protein-abundance target is replaced by its one-dimensional $f_3$ component.  The finite target is
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}(\widetilde P),\\
\|P_{f3}\|_2^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
P_{f3}/P &= 0.1999063916718794 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The active positional-dwell join contains $1257$ genes from a base pool of $1800$ yeast rows.  The residual control basis has rank $24$, the tested $f_3$ source rank is $1$, and the held-out comparison uses $5$ folds.  The dwell readouts are not a single global ribosome-efficiency number: they are per-gene relative A-site occupancy summaries computed separately from file-provided $5'$ head and $3'$ tail windows.  The null is a deterministic row-label permutation of $P_{f3}$, with the base and full designs and the folds held fixed, run separately for each segment and for the joint positional design.  The permutation count is $120$, with seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic}.

\paragraph{Pass condition and observed call.}  The positional question is whether the $f_3$ abundance component that is already visible through dwell can be assigned to a window rather than merely to an undifferentiated ribosome-occupancy summary.  The recorded run gives status \texttt{passed}.  The leading segment by held-out increment is $5'$ and the dominant segment is also $5'$.  The finite Boolean calls are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
\operatorname{three\_prime\_body\_passed} &= \mathrm{false},\\
\operatorname{leading\_segment} &= 5' .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the chapter claim is deliberately narrow: the $P_{f3}$ component has a detectable positional dwell increment in the head-window direction on this yeast matrix, while the tail/body window is not the passing segment.  The target was explicitly defined as the projection of $Z$-residualized log10 protein abundance onto $Z$-residualized $f_3$ stress on the active base-plus-positional-dwell join, so the result is a finite regression and cross-validation statement, not a biological mechanism by itself.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell result.}  The same yeast $f_3$ abundance component also passes a non-positional dwell mediation test on $1257$ joined rows.  In that run, dwell alone has held-in explanatory scale $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, the base model has $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, and the full base-plus-dwell model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  The held-out increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{null\ mean} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\
\mathrm{null\ min} &= -0.013799622026666476,\\
\mathrm{null\ max} &= 0.005001350039772926 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The direct correlation between the $f_3$ coordinate and the dwell readout in that run is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These numbers establish the background fact that dwell is not only fitted in sample; it exceeds a fixed readout-permutation threshold.  The positional result refines that fact by separating head and tail windows and locating the passing increment at the $5'$ side.

\paragraph{Comparison with structural-order ramp evidence.}  A separate structural-order ramp calculation gives a useful contrast because it also uses ramp-versus-body language but tests a different downstream surface.  At the primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, while the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, with ramp-minus-body $0.001378137149572267$.  The ramp sign-flip probability for being greater than zero is $0.0478515625$, while the paired ramp-minus-body probability is $0.05859375$.  That run concluded \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}: the ramp signal is positive, but the stricter paired localization gate does not pass.  The present dwell-position result is therefore not a structural-order claim.  It is a ribosome-occupancy-window localization of the $f_3$ protein-abundance component, and it should not be imported into folding or structural interpretation.

\paragraph{Panel grounding.}  The yeast proteomics join used in the direct protein-omics survival surface reports $5815$ joined proteins, with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates survive against log10 PAXdb protein abundance after the stated controls in that direct association surface.  The yeast direct $R^2_{QP}$ is $0.29837624994140827$, with $9$ surviving coordinates.  The positional dwell chapter does not reuse that direct number as a mechanism claim; it uses it only to identify the same controlled abundance layer in which the $P_{f3}$ projection lives.  The shared controls are amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, M-density baseline, and the residualized coordinate basis.

\paragraph{Reduction context.}  The broader yeast dictionary experiment reports that a candidate readout family containing measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover explains a selection-aware coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$ with unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  Its raw best coverage is $0.2745002077400602$, the selection-aware null $95$ percentile is $0.029149007242956115$, the naive fixed-subset null $95$ percentile is $0.00598374993486675$, and the description-length penalty is $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$.  The irreducibility-certificate run uses the same $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations on $1800$ selected yeast rows from $4758$ available joined rows.  These surrounding finite rows say that the residual signal is real and partially reducible, not that it has been exhausted.  The $5'$ dwell localization is one such reduction surface for the $f_3$ component.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}  The admissible readback is: in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, after the stated residualization, the $f_3$ component of protein abundance has a positional dwell increment whose passing segment is the $5'$ head window, not the $3'$ tail/body window.  Nothing in this chapter establishes translation causality, ribosome mechanism, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality.  A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and statistic: for example perturbation or rescue for mechanism, structural measurement for folding, physical assay for admissibility, functional assay for function, and phylogenetically or experimentally expanded panels for generality.
