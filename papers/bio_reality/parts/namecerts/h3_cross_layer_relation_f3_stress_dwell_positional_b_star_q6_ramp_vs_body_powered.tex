\subsection{Position-resolved dwell localization of the $f_3$ stress component}
\label{sec:bior-f3-stress-dwell-positional}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record isolates the $f_3$ stress coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface and asks whether the abundance component aligned with that coordinate is better predicted by a positional ribosome-dwell readout near the coding-sequence head or by a corresponding body/tail readout.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tested target is the vector $P_{f_3}$ obtained by projecting $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$ stress direction on the joined base plus positional-dwell panel.  The finite join has $n=1257$ genes, the base design size is $1800$, the control basis has rank $24$, and the $f_3$ source rank is $1$.  The measured dwell readout is not an abstract codon score: it is a per-gene positional relative ribosome occupancy summary computed separately from file-provided $5'$ head windows and $3'$ tail windows.

\paragraph{Controlled positional test.}
The experiment \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered} used five folds and a deterministic permutation design with $120$ row-label permutations of $P_{f_3}$ while keeping the base and full designs and folds fixed.  The same null construction was run separately for each positional segment and for the joint segment design.  The finite readout reports $\|P_{f_3}\|^2 = 44.20095750389323$ and $P_{f_3}$ accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the target component used in the active joined panel.  The returned classification is positional rather than merely global: the leading segment by held-out $\Delta R^2$ is $5'$ and the dominant segment is also $5'$.  The head-window gate passed, while the tail/body gate did not pass:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{join}} &= 1257,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 24,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(f_3) &= 1,\\
 \|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
 \operatorname{frac}(P_{f_3}) &= 0.1999063916718794,\\
 \mathrm{five\text{-}prime\ passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
 \mathrm{three\text{-}prime\ body\ passed} &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The status is therefore a passed finite positional readout: at least one positional dwell window gives a positive held-out increment above the fixed-readout permutation threshold, and that window is the $5'$ segment.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell result.}
The corresponding non-positional dwell mediation run on yeast used the same $P_{f_3}$ norm, the same $0.1999063916718794$ target fraction, the same five-fold design, and the same $120$ deterministic permutations.  There the active join also had $n=1257$, with base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$.  Its permutation reference had null mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, null minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, null maximum $0.005001350039772926$, null $95\%$ point $0.0013115362189692937$, and an approximate $f_3$--dwell association probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ with correlation $0.1615441450901653$.  The positional record sharpens that earlier finite statement: the dwell increment is not read only as an undivided occupancy summary, because the head-window summary is the segment that clears the positional gate.

\paragraph{Comparison with structural ramp evidence.}
A separate structural-order ramp/body test used primary ramp sizes $30$, $50$, and $70$ and returned a more cautious verdict.  At primary ramp size $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment was $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment was $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the weighted ramp-minus-body increment was $0.001378137149572267$.  The $f_3$ ramp sign-flip probability for being greater than zero was $0.0478515625$, while the paired ramp-minus-body probability was $0.05859375$.  That run classified the outcome as ramp-positive but not localized.  The dwell-position result is therefore narrower and cleaner: it localizes an abundance-aligned ribosome-occupancy dwell increment to the $5'$ window, but it does not by itself establish a structural-order localization claim.

\paragraph{Background cross-layer coordinates.}
The same nine residual coordinates used by the broader $B^{*}_{Q6}$ powered surface are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$ stress.  In yeast, the direct protein-abundance survival run joined $5815$ CDS/protein rows with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, retained a full residual coordinate rank of $9$, and gave direct $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving a positive threshold.  The measured ribosome-TE run joined $5128$ yeast genes, had ribosome-TE hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, and gave $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$.  The measured TE-to-abundance decomposition in yeast reported $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  The joint TE and mRNA-stability partition reported yeast $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, corresponding to joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$.  These surrounding facts make the positional dwell test scientifically situated: the yeast $f_3$ head-window signal lies inside a finite panel where direct abundance association, measured TE survival, and partial TE/stability attenuation have already been quantified.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite conclusion is that the $f_3$ stress-aligned abundance component has a position-resolved dwell increment in yeast, with the $5'$ segment passing and the $3'$ body/tail segment failing on the declared permutation-controlled held-out test.  This is a finite row-level localization of a ribosome-occupancy dwell readout relative to $P_{f_3}$; it is not a claim that $f_3$ stress causes translation, defines a folding program, determines molecular function, certifies physical admissibility, or states a universal biological rule.  Translation-rate mechanism, folded structure, physical feasibility, biological function, phenotype, and universality remain outside this record unless a separate measured contact at that layer supplies its own matched rows and controlled statistic.
