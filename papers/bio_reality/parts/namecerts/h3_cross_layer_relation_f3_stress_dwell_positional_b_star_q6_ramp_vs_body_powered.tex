\subsection{Positional dwell localization of the f3-stress abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record tests whether the abundance component carried by the $f3\_stress$ coordinate is better read through position-resolved ribosome dwell in the 5-prime ramp or in the 3-prime/body window.  The finite surface is restricted to \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, because the relevant positional A-site occupancy summaries are available on a matched yeast coding-sequence join.  The response is not total protein abundance itself, but the one-dimensional target
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10} P}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where both $f3\_stress$ and $\log_{10}$ protein abundance are residualized against the sibling control basis.  The control basis has rank $24$, and the tested source coordinate has rank $1$.  On the active positional-dwell join the run used $n=1257$ joined genes, with a base design size recorded as $1800$, five held-out folds, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations of $P_{f3}$.

\paragraph{Finite-row construction.}
The readout is a pair of per-gene relative ribosome-occupancy summaries, computed separately from file-provided 5-prime/head and 3-prime/tail A-site occupancy windows.  The experiment fixes the same base and full designs across the observed and permuted evaluations, so the null does not reselect rows, folds, or covariates after seeing the shuffled target.  In the larger yeast protein-abundance surface, the direct protein-omics join contains $5815$ CDS-linked proteins with a reported join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; the positional-dwell statistic is stricter and uses only the $1257$ rows where the local dwell summaries and the $P_{f3}$ target are simultaneously present.  Thus the finite claim is about this matched positional-dwell panel, not about every yeast CDS or every organism.

\paragraph{Observed positional split.}
The run classifies the leading segment by held-out increment as 5-prime.  The recorded dominant segment is \texttt{5prime}; \texttt{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed} is true; \texttt{three\_prime\_body\_passed} is false.  The target norm and target share are also finite-row quantities:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\operatorname{frac}(P_{f3},\log_{10} P) &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positional conclusion is therefore not that all ribosome dwell explains the coordinate equally.  It is the narrower statement that, on this yeast join and under this control set, the 5-prime/head dwell window is the segment that carries the detectable held-out increment for the $f3\_stress$-aligned abundance component, while the 3-prime/body window does not pass the corresponding gate.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell result.}
The same target has an independent local-dwell mediation surface on yeast with $n=1257$, five folds, and $120$ deterministic permutations.  In that run, the base model had held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-alone model had $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the base-plus-dwell model had held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  The resulting held-out increment was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{dwell}\mid\mathrm{base}} &= 0.01856473944057712,
\end{aligned}
$$
against a fixed-readout permutation null with mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The recorded approximate $f3$--dwell association probability is $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$, with correlation $0.1615441450901653$.  The positional experiment refines this positive dwell result by showing that the detectable dwell increment localizes to the 5-prime/head segment rather than the 3-prime/body segment.

\paragraph{Comparison with the structural-order ramp surface.}
A separate structural-order ramp analysis reports a positive ramp signal but does not pass its paired ramp-minus-body localization gate.  At the primary ramp length $50$, its weighted $f3$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, its weighted $f3$ body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and its paired ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probability for the ramp term is $0.0478515625$, while the paired ramp-minus-body probability is $0.05859375$, so that surface is classified as \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}.  The present dwell-positional result is different in scope: it is not a structure claim, and it uses the dwell readout on the active $P_{f3}$ abundance component.  The shared lesson is only positional asymmetry near the 5-prime side; no folded-structure conclusion is imported from that comparison.

\paragraph{Four-case interpretation.}
For this finite row set, the readback belongs to the translation-conditioned protein-abundance boundary rather than to a higher biological layer.  The local finite facts are: $P_{f3}$ is a nontrivial abundance component, the dwell readout supplies a positive held-out increment above its permutation threshold, and the positional split marks the 5-prime/head window as the passing segment.  This supports a bounded statement of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
f3\_stress &\longrightarrow P_{f3},\\
\text{5-prime dwell} &\text{ carries the detectable positional increment for } P_{f3}.
\end{aligned}
$$
It does not by itself establish a causal chain from synonymous codon geometry to translation rate to total protein output.  The statistic is observational, cross-sectional, and readout-specific; it is a controlled held-out prediction and permutation-survival result, not a perturbation or rescue experiment.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No claim about translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from this chapter alone.  Translation-rate mechanism would require a separate same-row contact that directly supplies the relevant translation readout and shows attenuation or absorption under the declared conditioning.  Folding or structural order would require a structure-level contact.  Function, phenotype, and physical admissibility would each require their own matched assays.  The admissible conclusion here is only that, in the yeast finite matrix with $1257$ joined genes, rank-$1$ $f3\_stress$, rank-$24$ controls, five folds, and $120$ fixed-design permutations, the $f3\_stress$-aligned abundance component has a position-resolved dwell increment whose passing segment is the 5-prime/head window and whose 3-prime/body counterpart does not pass.
