\subsection{Positional dwell localization of the $f_3$ stress abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns the $f_3$ stress coordinate inside the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface.  The tested organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The response is not the whole protein-abundance vector, but the component of the $Z$-residualized log protein-abundance vector projected onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$ stress direction.  The finite target is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{f_3\mid Z}(\log_{10} P),\\
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f_3}\|^2}{\|\log_{10} P\mid Z\|^2} &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
The joined positional-dwell fit uses $n=1257$ joined genes on an active base plus positional-dwell table, with base design size $1800$, $5$ held-out folds, $\operatorname{rank}(f_3)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$.  The dwell variables are per-gene relative ribosome-occupancy summaries computed separately from file-provided $5'$ head-window and $3'$ tail-window A-site occupancy windows.  The permutation reference is deterministic: row labels of $P_{f_3}$ are permuted while the base, full designs, and folds are held fixed, separately for each positional segment and for the joint segment design.  The recorded seed is $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered{:}deterministic}$, and the permutation count is $120$.

\paragraph{Positional verdict.}
The finite matrix is positive but asymmetric across the two positional dwell summaries.  The leading segment by held-out $\Delta R^2$ is the $5'$ segment; the dominant segment is also recorded as $5'$; the $5'$ ramp gate passed, while the $3'$ body gate did not pass.  Thus the local readback is not simply ``dwell somewhere in the coding sequence''.  It is a directional localization: the same abundance component aligned with $f_3$ stress is better accounted for by a head-window occupancy summary than by a tail-window summary.  In compact notation, the recorded classification is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{dominant\_segment} &= 5',\\
\mathrm{leading\_segment\_by\_\Delta R^2} &= 5',\\
\mathrm{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
\mathrm{three\_prime\_body\_passed} &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a positional dwell readout for the $f_3$ stress abundance component in yeast: at least one positional occupancy window contributes held-out predictive information beyond the base design, and the observed contribution is localized to the $5'$ ramp side rather than to the $3'$ body side.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell run.}
The companion dwell mediation fit on the same yeast biological object gives the scale of the effect before the head--tail split.  There the base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-alone model has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  The held-out gain from adding dwell to the base design is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{base}\to\mathrm{base+dwell}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\mathrm{mean}} &= -0.0032911642620130563,\\

\end{aligned}
$$
The same run reports $f_3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ under its stated approximation.  The positional record refines this non-positional statement: the dwell increment is not treated as a homogeneous CDS-wide quantity, because the segment-resolved test assigns the leading contribution to the $5'$ window.

\paragraph{Ramp versus body calibration.}
A separate structural-order ramp-versus-body calculation gives a useful calibration for interpreting the word ``ramp''.  For primary ramp length $50$, the weighted $f_3$ ramp held-out increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, while the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, so the weighted ramp-minus-body contrast is $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probabilities are $p_{\mathrm{ramp}>0}=0.0478515625$, $p_{\mathrm{body}>0}=0.54296875$, and $p_{\mathrm{ramp-body}>0}=0.05859375$.  Across ramp sizes $30,50,70$, the recorded pairs are
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{ramp}}=30 &: (\Delta R^2_{5'},\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}})=(0.0007864264335388174,0.00023737813475316197),\\
N_{\mathrm{ramp}}=50 &: (\Delta R^2_{5'},\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}})=(0.0011165640103326878,-0.00026157313923957926),\\
N_{\mathrm{ramp}}=70 &: (\Delta R^2_{5'},\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{body}})=(0.0007157308766185055,-0.0008384704154638841).
\end{aligned}
$$
That run classifies the structural-order interpretation as $\mathrm{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}$: the ramp signal is positive, but the stricter paired localization gate does not pass.  The present dwell-position result is narrower.  It does not assert structural order.  It says that, for the measured ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries in the yeast join, the abundance component aligned with the $f_3$ residual is preferentially explained by the $5'$ positional dwell segment.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance and translation context.}
The yeast direct protein-omics surface supplies the downstream abundance setting.  In the measured PAXdb join, yeast has $n=5815$ joined CDS--protein rows, join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ equal to $5792$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X_Q)=9$.  The direct $Q\to P$ residual surface has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving above the numerical positivity threshold.  Measured ribosome-TE survival in yeast gives $n=5128$ genes with TE, join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and $9$ surviving coordinates.  The measured translation-conditioned abundance decomposition in yeast reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
These surrounding finite rows justify placing the positional dwell result inside a translation-conditioned protein-abundance neighborhood.  They do not, by themselves, identify the molecular cause of the dwell signal.

\paragraph{Reduction and irreducibility boundary.}
The readout dictionary certificate for the same yeast context reports $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, available join size $4758$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$, and accepted coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$.  Its unexplained fraction is $0.7546178856026946$.  The irreducibility-certificate run uses the same $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, available join size $4758$, and the target log protein-abundance residual on the unified yeast context join.  These rows say that measured readouts can reduce part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual, but they do not collapse the residual surface into a single simple mediator.  The positional dwell finding should therefore be read as one localized reduction channel, not as a complete explanation of the $f_3$ abundance component.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible conclusion is finite and layer-specific.  The record supports a yeast $5'$ positional dwell association for the $f_3$ stress-aligned abundance component after the stated residualization and deterministic held-out testing.  It does not establish a universal translation law, a causal elongation mechanism, a synonymous-edit rescue prediction, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, localization, turnover, pathway behavior, or organismal phenotype.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is not promoted to a mechanism unless a measured translation readout survives and conditioning on that readout removes or materially reduces the protein-abundance association under the declared controls.
