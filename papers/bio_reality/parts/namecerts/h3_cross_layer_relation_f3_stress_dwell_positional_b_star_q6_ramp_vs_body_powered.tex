\subsection{Position-resolved dwell localization for the $f_3$ stress abundance component}\label{subsec:h3-cross-layer-relation-f3-stress-dwell-positional-b-star-q6-ramp-vs-body-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record isolates a single rank-one coordinate, $f_3\_stress$, inside the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface and asks whether the protein-abundance component aligned with that coordinate is better accounted for by position-resolved ribosome dwell summaries near the $5'$ head than by corresponding $3'$ or body summaries.  The measured target is not total protein abundance itself, but
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{f_3} &= \operatorname{proj}_{\widetilde f_3}\bigl(\widetilde{\log_{10} P}\bigr),\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde f_3) &= 1,
\end{aligned}
$$
where both $\widetilde f_3$ and $\widetilde{\log_{10} P}$ are residualized against the same sibling control basis $Z$.  The active computation used Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$ joined rows on the base plus positional-dwell table, $n_{\mathrm{base}}=1800$ for the enclosing powered design, five folds, and a $Z$ basis of rank $24$.  The projected target had squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and accounted for $0.1999063916718794$ of the residual protein-abundance target used in the local $f_3$ test.

\paragraph{Positional discriminant.}
The dwell readout consists of per-gene relative ribosome occupancy summaries computed separately from file-provided $5'$ or head windows and $3'$ or tail windows.  With the base design and folds held fixed, the finite test compared segment-specific held-out improvements against a deterministic row-label permutation null for $P_{f_3}$.  The run identifier was \texttt{6db78e1bf746}; its deterministic seed was \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_dwell\_positional\_powered:deterministic}; and it used $120$ permutations for each segment and for the joint segment design.  The status was \texttt{passed}.  The leading segment by held-out $\Delta R^2$ was the $5'$ segment, the reported dominant segment was \texttt{5prime}, the $5'$ ramp gate passed, and the $3'$ body gate did not pass:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{leading\_segment} &= 5',\\
\mathrm{dominant\_segment} &= 5',\\
\mathrm{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed} &= \mathrm{true},\\
\mathrm{three\_prime\_body\_passed} &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite-row conclusion is not merely that a dwell covariate helps somewhere; it is that the detected dwell increment is positionally localized to the $5'$ side in this yeast table, while the corresponding $3'$ or body window fails the same recorded gate.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell test.}
The positional call is consistent with the companion dwell-abundance mediation run \texttt{946f33083caf}.  In that run, the same organism and $f_3$-projected abundance target had $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, five folds, rank $1$ for $f_3$, $Z$ basis rank $24$, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  The base model reached $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-only model reached $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the full base-plus-dwell model reached $R^2=0.20412261520502883$.  The held-out increment was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{\max} &= 0.005001350039772926,\\
\mathrm{corr}(f_3,\mathrm{dwell}) &= 0.1615441450901653,\\
 p_{\mathrm{approx}} &= 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The positional run therefore refines a previously positive dwell increment by assigning the leading window to the $5'$ side, rather than creating an independent claim about translation causality.

\paragraph{Comparison with the ramp-versus-body structural-order readout.}
A neighboring position-resolved structural-order run, \texttt{fd29bb46dde6}, supplies a useful contrast because it also separates ramp and body windows.  There the primary ramp length was $50$, the minimum joined-row threshold was $300$, and the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment was positive while the body increment was negative:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{f_3,\mathrm{ramp}} &= 0.0011165640103326878,\\
\Delta R^2_{f_3,\mathrm{body}} &= -0.00026157313923957926,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{ramp-body}} &= 0.001378137149572267.
\end{aligned}
$$
That run reported $p=0.0478515625$ for the positive ramp sign-flip test, $p=0.54296875$ for the body test, and $p=0.05859375$ for the paired ramp-minus-body test, giving the classification \texttt{ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized}.  The present dwell-positional record is narrower: its classification is a passed $5'$ dwell localization for $P_{f_3}$, not a folded-structure or structural-order statement.

\paragraph{Local readback.}
The finite row readback is: after residualizing the codon-derived $f_3\_stress$ coordinate and measured protein abundance against the declared controls, the $f_3$-aligned abundance component is predicted better by adding position-resolved ribosome dwell information, and the leading segment is the $5'$ window.  In the four-case language for residual readouts, this supports a bounded position-conditioned downstream association for the $f_3$ component in yeast.  It does not by itself establish a universal translation law, a causal elongation mechanism, a protein-folding route, a physical-admissibility claim, a molecular-function claim, or an organismal phenotype.  Those conclusions would require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  The admissible statement here is the finite one: on the joined yeast rows available to this computation, the $5'$ positional dwell summary passes the fixed held-out permutation criterion for the $f_3$-projected protein-abundance component, while the $3'$ or body window does not.
