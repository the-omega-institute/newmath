\subsection{Positional dwell localization of the $f_3$ stress component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional-ramp-body}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record isolates the positional part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$ stress signal on a matched yeast panel.  The tested surface is not the full nine-coordinate residual matrix, but the rank-one component obtained by projecting the $Z$-residualized log protein-abundance vector onto the $Z$-residualized $f_3$ stress coordinate.  On the active positional-dwell join, the target component has squared norm
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|P_{f_3}\|^2 &= 44.20095750389323,\\
\frac{\|P_{f_3}\|^2}{\|P\|^2} &= 0.1999063916718794.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism is Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the fitted coordinate rank is $1$, and the control basis has rank $24$.  The finite panel uses $n=1257$ joined coding-sequence rows from a base availability of $1800$ rows.  The validation design uses $5$ folds and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations of $P_{f_3}$, with the base and full designs held fixed separately for the $5'$ segment, the $3'$ segment, and the joint positional design.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
The row model keeps the $f_3$ stress coordinate downstream of ordinary compositional controls rather than letting codon composition serve as its own explanation.  The residualization set includes amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, organism-scoped coding-sequence features, and the shared $M$-density baseline used by the sibling $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance analyses.  In the broader measured-abundance screen over fifteen organisms, the same nine residual coordinates all had full residual rank after controls; for yeast the direct protein-abundance run reported $n=5815$ joined CDS/protein rows, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  That direct association is useful only as a bounded abundance readout: the positional test below asks whether the $f_3$ abundance component has a dwell-location signature after the base design is already present.

\paragraph{Segment discriminant.}
The positional dwell readout is computed from file-provided ribosome-occupancy windows, split into $5'$ head/ramp summaries and $3'$ tail/body summaries.  The registered result is asymmetric: the leading segment by held-out increment is the $5'$ window, the dominant segment is recorded as $5prime$, the $5'$ ramp gate passed, and the $3'$ body gate did not pass.  Thus the finite-row conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{segment}_{\max}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}}) &= 5',\\
\operatorname{pass}(5') &= 1,\\
\operatorname{pass}(3') &= 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The support condition is therefore not merely that a generic dwell summary improves prediction.  It is that the $f_3$-projected protein-abundance component admits a position-resolved dwell increment, and that the observed increment is localized to the $5'$ ramp side under the fixed permutation null.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell certificate.}
The non-positional dwell mediation run gives the scalar background against which this localization is interpreted.  On the same yeast organism, it joined $1257$ rows, used the same $5$-fold structure and $120$ deterministic permutations, and found a positive held-out dwell increment
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{\mathrm{base}} &= 0.1855578757644517,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.20412261520502883,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{heldout}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed-null summary for that scalar dwell readout had null mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, null minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, null maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The measured $f_3$--dwell correlation was $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional result refines this scalar certificate by locating the active dwell increment at the $5'$ ramp rather than at the $3'$ body.

\paragraph{Separation from structural-order evidence.}
A separate ramp-versus-body structural-order screen is not identical to this dwell-localization statement.  In that screen, the primary ramp length was $50$ codons; the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment was $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment was $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body increment was $0.001378137149572267$.  Its sign-flip summaries were $p=0.0478515625$ for ramp positivity, $p=0.54296875$ for body positivity, and $p=0.05859375$ for paired ramp-over-body positivity.  The registered structural conclusion was ramp-positive but not localized.  The present dwell result is narrower and cleaner: it concerns positional ribosome-occupancy dwell summaries for the $P_{f_3}$ abundance component, not folded structural order.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready at the level of a yeast positional dwell readout.  The positive statement is that the $f_3$ stress component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface has a measured dwell increment that is positionally concentrated in the $5'$ ramp window on the joined yeast panel.  The readback is finite, row-matched, rank-declared, and null-tested: $n=1257$, rank $1$ for the $f_3$ coordinate, $Z$-basis rank $24$, $5$ folds, and $120$ fixed-design permutations.

\paragraph{Local negative scope.}
This record does not by itself establish translation causality, elongation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law.  The protein-abundance target is a steady-state omics readout, and the dwell summaries are observational ribosome-occupancy summaries.  A stronger biological mechanism would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as perturbation, rescue, direct time-resolved ribosome dynamics, or an independently measured structural or functional assay.  Direct $q_i\to P_k$ association, even when positive, remains a prediction surface unless a measured translation readout survives and absorbs or reduces the protein-abundance association under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Compression context.}
The same yeast context also has a partial readout-dictionary certificate with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $n=1800$ joined rows, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, selection-aware null $95$th percentile $0.029149007242956115$, and selected coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$.  That leaves unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  The positional dwell result should be read inside that finite landscape: the $5'$ ramp dwell signal is a real localized component of the $f_3$ abundance projection, but it is not a complete reduction of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface.
