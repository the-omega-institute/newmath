\subsection{Position-resolved dwell localization for the f3-stress abundance component}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-dwell-positional}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record isolates the $f3\_stress$ coordinate inside the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis and asks whether the abundance component carried by that coordinate is better predicted by position-resolved ribosome dwell summaries near the coding-sequence head or by corresponding tail/body summaries. The finite surface is a Saccharomyces cerevisiae matched panel with $n=1257$ joined rows for the active positional-dwell fit. The target is not total protein abundance itself, but the one-dimensional projection $P_{f3}$ of $Z$-residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f3\_stress$ direction. On this active join, $\|P_{f3}\|^2=44.20095750389323$, and $P_{f3}$ accounts for $0.1999063916718794$ of the relevant target energy. The nuisance basis has rank $24$, the tested $f3\_stress$ direction has rank $1$, and the base training context uses $1800$ rows before the active positional join is formed.

\paragraph{Controls and row geometry.}
The controlled comparison is deliberately narrow. The design uses per-gene positional relative ribosome-occupancy summaries computed separately from file-provided $5'$ head windows and $3'$ tail windows. The reported statistic is a held-out increment over the base design under fixed folds: $5$ folds are held constant while deterministic row-label permutations of $P_{f3}$ provide the null reference. The permutation count is $120$, and the same base/full design structure is reused separately for each segment and for the joint segment design. Thus the positive call is a finite-row discrimination between two measured positional readouts on the same projected abundance component, not a free narrative about translation or protein biology.

\paragraph{Observed segment split.}
The positional test passed with dominant segment $5'$ and leading segment by held-out $\Delta R^2$ also $5'$. The five-prime ramp gate passed, while the three-prime body gate did not pass. In symbolic form the local readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(f3)&=1,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z)&=24,\\
 n_{\mathrm{join}}&=1257,\\
 N_{\mathrm{perm}}&=120,\\
 \mathrm{segment}_{\mathrm{lead}}&=5',\\
 \mathrm{pass}_{5'}&=\mathrm{true},\\
 \mathrm{pass}_{3'}&=\mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite verdict is therefore positional localization of the dwell increment: at least one positional dwell window gives a positive held-out increment above the fixed permutation Null95, and the window that carries the positive result is the five-prime/head segment rather than the three-prime/tail segment.

\paragraph{Relation to the non-positional dwell result.}
The segment-localized result refines the broader dwell mediation result on the same organism and projected target. In the non-positional fit, the active join had $n=1257$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\operatorname{rank}(f3)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$. The base model achieved $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, dwell alone achieved $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and the full model achieved $R^2=0.20412261520502883$. The held-out dwell increment was $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, while the deterministic row-label null had mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and Null95 $0.0013115362189692937$. The observed $f3\_stress$--dwell correlation was $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. The position-resolved result does not replace that aggregate dwell evidence; it assigns the aggregate dwell increment to the head-side readout.

\paragraph{Relation to structural ramp tests.}
A separate ramp-versus-body structural-order readout also found a weak positive ramp-side increment for $f3\_stress$, but it did not meet the paired localization gate. At the primary ramp length $50$, the weighted ramp held-out increment was $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment was $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the ramp-minus-body difference was $0.001378137149572267$. The ramp sign-flip probability for being greater than zero was $0.0478515625$, the body probability was $0.54296875$, and the paired ramp-minus-body probability was $0.05859375$. That companion result is best read as ramp-positive but not structurally localized. By contrast, the present dwell result is a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readback for the $P_{f3}$ abundance component, with the $5'$ dwell segment passing and the $3'$ segment failing.

\paragraph{Cross-layer placement.}
The same coordinate family has broad direct protein-omics survival in matched PAXdb abundance panels: for Saccharomyces cerevisiae the direct $Q\to P$ surface reports $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving after the stated controls, and the measured ribosome-TE surface reports for Saccharomyces cerevisiae $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving. In the measured TE-conditioned abundance decomposition for the same organism, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, the raw measured mediation fraction is $0.4793069656345167$, and the clipped measured mediation fraction is the same value. These surrounding finite facts make the positional-dwell result biologically anchored enough to state as a bounded cross-layer readback: the $f3\_stress$ abundance component is not merely associated with bulk abundance, and not merely associated with aggregate dwell, but has a detectable five-prime positional dwell increment on the matched yeast rows.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The ready claim is the following finite statement. On the Saccharomyces cerevisiae matched panel, after projecting protein abundance onto the $Z$-residualized $f3\_stress$ direction, positional relative ribosome occupancy improves held-out prediction above a deterministic row-label null, and the improvement is localized to the five-prime/head segment rather than the three-prime/tail segment. The numerical readback is $n=1257$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\operatorname{rank}(f3)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$, $\|P_{f3}\|^2=44.20095750389323$, $P_{f3}$ fraction $0.1999063916718794$, five-prime pass true, three-prime pass false, dominant segment $5'$. This is a measured positional-dwell association with a projected abundance component; it is not, by itself, a causal translation mechanism, a folding statement, a physical-admissibility statement, a molecular-function statement, a phenotype statement, or a universal biological law. Any such stronger interpretation would need its own layer-matched measurement and a separate finite statistic.
