\subsection{Transcript-surveillance position rule with an RNA-half-life boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.} This BioReality record concerns synonymously editable coding-RNA windows whose rows retain coordinate labels, translation readout status, RNA half-life readout, transcription or mRNA abundance controls, isoform state, local RNA-structure and motif-density covariates, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, batch, perturbation state, rescue state, and an explicit break condition. The biological object is not a free-standing sequence law. It is a finite panel in which high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels are compared only after amino-acid composition and local sequence-composition confounding have been separated, and only while the RNA readout remains paired to a translation boundary in the same row.

\paragraph{Observed finite audit row.} The Teran2021 transcript-surveillance entry is represented by the verified fact id \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.transcript\_surveillance.nmd\_position\_rule\_reality\_boundary\_teran2021}. Its recorded run id is \texttt{c362df6daa98}. The finite computation retained $38576$ rows, used $1000$ realized permutations, and completed in $55.602$ seconds. Thus the record is strong enough to say that the curated finite table reached the declared RNA-surveillance boundary statistic for the kept rows, but it does not by itself supply every mechanistic RBP row needed for an RBP-mediated decay bridge.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.} The admissible reading is a bounded transcript-surveillance relation: a coordinate-associated RNA half-life or surveillance readout may be considered only on rows where the same window also carries the required translation-readout boundary and matched mRNA or transcription controls. In symbolic form, the row schema is the finite tuple
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x_i = (&q_i, T_i, H_i, A_i, C_i, S_i, D_i, P_i, I_i, B_i, u_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the synonymous coordinate label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout, $H_i$ is the RNA half-life or surveillance readout, $A_i$ is measured transcript abundance or transcription rate, $C_i$ collects local composition controls, $S_i$ records local structure and motif-density covariates, $D_i$ records codon-pair and dinucleotide context, $P_i$ records perturbation state when present, $I_i$ records splice or isoform state, $B_i$ records batch, and $u_i$ is the declared break condition. The permitted question is whether the coordinate label remains associated with $H_i$ after these finite controls are attached. The permitted conclusion is not that the coordinate has become a mechanism, a protein-output measurement, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Mechanism gate.} A stronger RBP-decay interpretation would require additional rows beyond the verified Teran2021 finite count: measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, a coordinate--occupancy decoupling check, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, and non-target controls. The local rule is strict. If the RNA half-life readout changes but measured decay-factor RBP occupancy does not change, the result remains an RNA-stability association. If occupancy changes but target-RBP loss and wild-type rescue do not control the effect, the result remains an occupancy association rather than a mechanism bridge. If binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, local RNA structure, motif density, transcription, isoform stability, transcript abundance, codon-pair context, or batch reproduces the pattern, the bridge is refused.

\paragraph{Same-row translation boundary.} Translation readout is mandatory here as a boundary condition, not as the conclusion of the chapter. A row may contain ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency information to keep transcript surveillance separated from downstream interpretation. That information does not license a claim about translation realization from RNA half-life alone. It also does not license protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universality. Each of those claims would need its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.

\paragraph{Comparison with a neighboring RNA-half-life audit.} The same finite audit bundle contains an RNA-modification half-life boundary row, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_modification.m6a\_site\_load\_mrna\_halflife\_boundary\_gse163500}, with run id \texttt{7cd189f15c4b}. That row reports $5837$ joined genes, a static Spearman coefficient of $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log half-life, a causal Spearman coefficient of $0.245804$ for m6A load against $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, a length-partial Spearman coefficient of $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value of $0.000999001$. Its status says that the finite dataset supports a pre-registered RNA-half-life boundary statistic, not a mechanism or function claim. This comparison is useful because it fixes the intended reading of the Teran2021 row: finite RNA-readout support is acceptable, while promotion to mechanism or downstream biology is blocked unless the missing contacts are supplied on the same finite panel.

\paragraph{Decision.} The chapter is therefore accepted as a finite transcript-surveillance boundary record. The positive content is the existence of a kept-row audit for the Teran2021 NMD-position-rule surface, with $38576$ rows and $1000$ permutations, read as a bounded RNA-surveillance or RNA-half-life relation under translation and mRNA-control separation. The negative content is equally part of the result: no translation realization, protein output, protein folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, decay-factor RBP mechanism, or universal law follows from the finite row unless a separate contact supplies that layer and survives the declared controls.
