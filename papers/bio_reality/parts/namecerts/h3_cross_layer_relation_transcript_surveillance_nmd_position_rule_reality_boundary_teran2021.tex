\subsection{Transcript Surveillance Position Rules and the RNA Half-Life Boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This record concerns a finite panel of synonymously comparable coding-RNA windows whose rows keep coordinate labels, translation readout, RNA half-life, transcription or abundance control, local sequence context, isoform state, perturbation state, rescue state, and declared failure conditions in the same table. The intended biological object is not a free sequence law. It is a bounded comparison of coding-window coordinates against RNA stability, with a mandatory guard that translation information is present as a same-row boundary rather than silently inferred after the fact.

The transcript-surveillance run assigned to this record is \texttt{c362df6daa98}. Its retained table contains $38576$ finite rows, and the recorded permutation budget is $1000$ permutations. The execution time was $55.602$ seconds. Thus the available evidence is large enough to state a row-level RNA-stability boundary, but the stored scalar payload does not by itself identify a decay-factor RNA-binding protein, an occupancy contrast, or a rescue-controlled mechanism. In symbolic form, the admissible table is only
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i = (&q_i, T_i, H_i, A_i, C_i, S_i, I_i, B_i, P_i, W_i, D_i),\\
  i &\in \{1,\ldots,38576\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the synonymous coordinate label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout, $H_i$ is the RNA half-life readout, $A_i$ is the abundance or transcription control, $C_i$ collects composition and codon-pair covariates, $S_i$ records local RNA structure or motif-density covariates, $I_i$ records splice or isoform state, $B_i$ is batch, $P_i$ is perturbation state, $W_i$ is rescue state, and $D_i$ is the declared break condition. A positive bridge is not licensed merely by the existence of $q_i$ and $H_i$ in this list.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
The admissible transcript-surveillance claim is a conditional one. A coordinate-associated half-life contrast may be discussed as a cross-layer relation only when it remains visible after transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and amino-acid-composition controls are represented in the same finite row scheme. Even then, the claim is still an RNA-stability boundary unless the rows also contain a measured local occupancy readout for a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein.

The expected bridge pattern has three separations. First, high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels must not merely be proxies for amino-acid content, transcript level, local structure, isoform state, or batch. Second, the half-life contrast must track measured local occupancy of the named decay-factor binding factor under a coordinate--occupancy decoupling test. Third, the effect must collapse under target-factor loss, return under wild-type rescue, and fail to return under a binding-defective or non-target control. Without those three separations the table supports, at most, a finite association between a coding-window label and RNA half-life.

\paragraph{Comparison contact.}
A second verified RNA-stability contact is useful only as a calibration of what counts as a properly bounded half-life statistic. The m6A half-life run \texttt{7cd189f15c4b} retained $5837$ genes under the coverage rule $\min(\text{Mean T coverage DMSO},\text{STM2457})$ at or above the twentieth percentile among valid joined genes. It reported a static Spearman association of $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log half-life, a causal Spearman association of $0.245804$ between m6A load and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, a length-partial Spearman value of $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value of $0.000999001$. Those numbers show how a finite half-life boundary can cross a preregistered statistical threshold while remaining explicitly short of a mechanism claim.

The transcript-surveillance record is read in the same disciplined way. Its $38576$ retained rows and $1000$ permutations establish that a finite RNA-half-life table was evaluated, but no stored scalar in this payload reports a local decay-factor occupancy coefficient, a target-factor-loss coefficient, a wild-type-rescue coefficient, a binding-defective-rescue coefficient, or a non-target-control coefficient. Therefore the chapter may name the intended bridge architecture, but it cannot report that the architecture has been completed for this claim.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The coordinate is demoted to a descriptor if high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels are exchangeable for half-life after the declared controls are included. The RNA-stability result is demoted to a finite association if RNA half-life changes but measured decay-factor occupancy does not change. It is also demoted if target-factor loss fails to remove the effect, if wild-type rescue fails to restore it, or if binding-defective rescue, non-target perturbation, local-structure covariates, motif density, transcription rate, isoform state, abundance, or batch reproduces the pattern.

The same-row translation readout is a boundary guard, not a promotion rule. It prevents an RNA-half-life statement from being interpreted as translation realization without a matched translation surface, but it does not convert a half-life difference into protein output. No translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding readout and finite statistic.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The standalone conclusion is therefore narrow. The available finite data support a transcript-surveillance RNA-half-life boundary record with $38576$ retained rows, $1000$ permutations, and a declared execution time of $55.602$ seconds. A related RNA-modification half-life contact shows that finite half-life statistics can be reported with row counts, coverage rules, correlation values, and permutation values while still refusing mechanism promotion. For the present transcript-surveillance claim, the decay-factor RNA-binding-protein bridge remains conditional on missing same-window occupancy, perturbation, rescue, and binding-defective or non-target control rows. The chapter is ready as a bounded BioReality record precisely because it states both the finite contact and the local refusal of the stronger mechanism interpretation.
