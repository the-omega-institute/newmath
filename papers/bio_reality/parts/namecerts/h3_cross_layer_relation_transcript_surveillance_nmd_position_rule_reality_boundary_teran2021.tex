\subsection{Transcript-surveillance position rule as a bounded RNA-stability contact}\label{sec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats a synonymously indexed coding-RNA window panel as a finite biological audit object rather than as a general law about coding sequence.  A row is admissible only when it carries a coordinate label for the edited or compared coding window, a same-row translation readout, an RNA half-life readout, matched mRNA abundance or transcription-rate controls, isoform state, local RNA-structure and composition covariates, motif-density covariates, codon-pair context, batch, and a declared perturbation or control state.  The intended positive reading is deliberately narrow: a coordinate-associated RNA half-life contrast may be reported as a bounded cross-layer relation only when the half-life term follows measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, separates coordinate from occupancy, collapses under target-factor loss, returns under wild-type rescue, and is not restored by binding-defective rescue or non-target controls.  If any of those finite-row gates is absent, the surviving statement is only an RNA-stability association or a contact gap.

\paragraph{Observed finite audit.}
The transcript-surveillance position-rule run for the Teran-labelled reality boundary is recorded under experiment run $\mathrm{c362df6daa98}$.  Its finite table retained $38576$ rows and used $1000$ realized permutations, with a recorded runtime of $55.602$ seconds.  These numbers certify the size and execution of the declared boundary computation; they do not by themselves certify the missing RBP-occupancy and perturbation requirements.  In the notation of this chapter, the recorded audit surface is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{kept}} &= 38576,\\
B_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1000,\\
T_{\mathrm{run}} &= 55.602\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite object is adequate for saying that a transcript-surveillance position rule was brought to an RNA half-life boundary with a nonempty row set and a realized permutation budget.  It is not adequate for promoting the row geometry to an RBP-mediated decay mechanism unless the same rows also include measured local RBP occupancy, target-factor loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target control behavior, and the stated decoupling between coordinate label and occupancy.

\paragraph{Coordinate, relation, and trigger.}
The coordinate part is the high-versus-low synonymous label after amino-acid composition control and after local coding context has been kept visible.  The relation part is the half-life contrast conditioned on transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch.  The trigger part is not merely a difference in RNA half-life.  It is the conjunction of local decay-factor occupancy, target-factor loss, wild-type rescue, and failure of binding-defective or non-target controls to reproduce the rescue.  Thus the admissible bridge has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Delta \mathrm{HL}(q_{\mathrm{high}},q_{\mathrm{low}}\mid Z)\neq 0,\\
&\Delta \mathrm{Occ}_{\mathrm{RBP}}(q_{\mathrm{high}},q_{\mathrm{low}}\mid Z)\neq 0,\\
&\Delta \mathrm{HL}\ \mathrm{collapses\ under\ target\ loss},\\
&\Delta \mathrm{HL}\ \mathrm{returns\ under\ wild\ type\ rescue},\\
&\Delta \mathrm{HL}\ \mathrm{does\ not\ return\ under\ binding\ defective\ or\ non\ target\ control},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ denotes the finite control list for transcription, abundance, isoform, structure, motif, nucleotide composition, codon-pair context, batch, and translation-readout boundary.  The translation readout is a boundary condition in this chapter, not the conclusion.

\paragraph{Companion RNA-stability calibration rows.}
The broader RNA-stability audit bundle contains two useful comparison surfaces.  The m6A half-life boundary run $\mathrm{7cd189f15c4b}$ retained $5837$ genes and reported a causal Spearman value $0.245804$ for m6A-load versus $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, a length-partial Spearman value $0.227683$, a static DMSO Spearman value $-0.388929$, and a two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$ under the stated coverage rule.  A separate ARE deletion stability boundary run $\mathrm{a41440ab2792}$ reported $226$ BEAS2B rows, $2242$ Jurkat rows, $1000$ realized permutations, a $2000$-permutation grading target, sign concordance, and a $6.93$ second runtime.  These companion rows show that the same finite-audit style can carry scalar association strength, permutation support, and row-count information when those quantities are present.  They do not fill the Teran-labelled RBP-occupancy and rescue fields; they only calibrate how RNA-stability contacts are read as finite tables rather than as unrestricted biological explanations.

\paragraph{Local refusal conditions.}
The chapter refuses every promotion that is not supplied by the row schema itself.  RNA half-life difference alone is not translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal rule.  A matched mRNA control is a transcription-layer control, not an RNA half-life assay.  A ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contact is a translation boundary, not an RBP-occupancy measurement and not a rescue experiment.  If high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels become exchangeable for RNA half-life after the stated controls, the coordinate remains a bounded descriptor.  If half-life differs while measured decay-factor occupancy is absent or unchanged, the result remains an RNA-stability association.  If target-factor loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, RNA structure, motif density, isoform stability, transcription, abundance, or batch reproduce the pattern, the RBP bridge is rejected.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite Teran-labelled run is ready as a bounded transcript-surveillance RNA-stability boundary because it has a concrete retained row count and realized permutation budget: $38576$ retained rows and $1000$ permutations.  The positive mechanistic bridge remains conditional, not established by those two numbers.  The admissible conclusion is that a finite same-organism RNA half-life boundary was evaluated for a transcript-surveillance position rule.  A stronger statement about decay-factor RBP mediation requires same-window measured occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-factor loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and the declared controlled half-life statistic on the same finite rows.
