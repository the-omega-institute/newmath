\subsection{Transcript-surveillance position rule as an RNA-stability boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats the transcript-surveillance position rule as a finite coding-RNA boundary rather than as a general mechanism claim.  The rows are coding or transcript windows with a position label, a surveillance-relevant transcript annotation, and an RNA readout.  The finite audit retained $38576$ rows for the Teran2021-position-rule surface and used $1000$ permutations, with a recorded runtime of $55.602$ seconds.  Thus the object here is not an unlimited law over all transcripts.  It is the finite relation between a declared position feature and an RNA-surveillance readout after row eligibility, identifier matching, and the declared permutation surface have been fixed.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout.}
Let $i$ range over the retained rows.  Each row carries a coordinate-like label $q_i$ for the transcript-surveillance position feature and a bounded RNA readout $h_i$ for the half-life or stability-facing response available in the joined table.  The admissible statistic is a finite comparison of the $h_i$ distribution across the declared position classes, with all interpretation restricted to the retained row set.  In schematic notation the permitted object is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{NMD}}&=\{(q_i,h_i,z_i,b_i):1\leq i\leq 38576\},\\
  S_{\mathrm{NMD}}&=S\bigl((q_i,h_i,z_i,b_i)_{i=1}^{38576};1000\ \mathrm{permutations}\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $z_i$ denotes the finite control information actually carried by the row and $b_i$ denotes the declared break flag.  The statistic may report a boundary crossing only for the row-joined position rule.  It does not by itself identify ribosome flow, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, cellular function, adaptation, or a universal biological principle.

\paragraph{Same-row translation boundary.}
The coding-window formulation requires the translation-facing readout to remain a boundary variable, not an automatic consequence of RNA half-life.  A position-associated RNA-stability effect can be discussed as a cross-layer candidate only when the same finite rows also carry the matched translation readout and the matched mRNA or transcription controls needed to prevent RNA abundance from being mistaken for translation output.  If those rows are absent, the position rule remains an RNA-surveillance association.  If they are present, their role is still restrictive: they prevent promotion beyond the readout actually measured.  A difference in RNA half-life is not a protein-output measurement.

\paragraph{RBP mechanism condition.}
The stronger decay-factor RBP interpretation is not licensed by the Teran2021 finite statistic alone.  For that interpretation, the same window panel would have to contain measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, a coordinate--occupancy decoupling check, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  The required pattern is directional: the RNA half-life effect must track measured local occupancy, collapse under target-RBP loss, return under wild-type rescue, and fail to return under the binding-defective or non-target controls.  If the RNA half-life surface survives while measured occupancy does not change, or if a structure, motif-density, isoform, batch, abundance, or control-rescue explanation reproduces the pattern, the mechanism reading is refused.

\paragraph{Local controls.}
The row-level boundary is sensitive to local sequence and transcript-state confounding.  A positive reading therefore requires separation from transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the mandatory translation-readout variable.  The finite comparison can be summarized as the refusal rule
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \mathrm{ClaimAllowed} = 1
  \quad \Rightarrow \quad
  &\mathrm{PositionSignal}=1,\\
  &\mathrm{TranslationBoundaryPresent}=1,\\
  &\mathrm{TranscriptControlsPassed}=1,\\
  &\mathrm{RbpOccupancyPerturbationRescuePassed}=1
  \quad \text{for an RBP bridge.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The last line is not supplied by a generic RNA half-life table; it is a separate experimental requirement for the bridge interpretation.

\paragraph{Comparison with neighboring RNA-stability surfaces.}
The audit table also contains other RNA-stability-facing finite surfaces, which help calibrate the present boundary without replacing it.  In the m6A site-load half-life surface, $5837$ genes were retained, the static Spearman value between m6A load and DMSO log half-life was $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman value for m6A-load change versus $\Delta\log_2$ half-life was $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman value was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial-permutation value was $0.000999001$.  In the ARE deletion surface, the finite counts were $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, with $1000$ actual permutations and $2000$ grading permutations, and the sign-concordance flag was true.  These neighboring surfaces show the intended evidential standard: a finite RNA-stability statistic may cross its own boundary, but each mechanism, translation, protein, structure, function, or phenotype layer still needs its own row-matched contact and control design.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The Teran2021 position-rule record is therefore ready as a bounded transcript-surveillance RNA-stability chapter.  Its concrete finite support is the retained $38576$-row surface with $1000$ permutations.  The chapter may state that the position rule reaches an RNA-surveillance boundary under the declared finite statistic.  It may not state that translation has been realized, that a decay-factor RBP mechanism has been proven, that protein abundance follows, that folded structure or physical admissibility has been measured, that biological function or phenotype has been established, or that a universal biological law has been obtained.  Those statements require separate contacts carrying the relevant measurements on the same finite rows.
