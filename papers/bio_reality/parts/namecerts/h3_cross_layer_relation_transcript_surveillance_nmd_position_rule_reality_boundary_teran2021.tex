\subsection{Transcript-surveillance position as a bounded RNA-stability contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The transcript-surveillance record is a finite coding-RNA boundary test: its rows are transcript or coding-window instances carrying a position-sensitive surveillance label, an RNA abundance or half-life-facing readout, and the controls needed to keep that readout from being promoted into a translation, protein, structure, function, phenotype, or universal-law claim.  The verified Teran-facing run retained $38576$ joined rows, used $1000$ permutations, and completed in $55.602$ seconds.  In this chapter the row object is therefore not a global law about nonsense-mediated decay, nor a general rule about codon geometry.  It is a finite panel in which a position-like surveillance coordinate is compared against an RNA-layer outcome under declared controls.

\paragraph{Coordinate and readout.}
Each retained row is read as a tuple of the following kind:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 r_i = (&\text{coordinate label}_i,\ \text{RNA readout}_i,\ \text{transcription or abundance control}_i,\\
        &\text{isoform state}_i,\ \text{local sequence covariates}_i,\ \text{batch}_i,\ \text{break flag}_i).
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate label may be position-like, codon-window-like, or surveillance-class-like, but the only licensed empirical statement is at the RNA layer.  The same row must also record enough context to refuse a false promotion: transcript abundance, isoform assignment, local composition, batch, and any available translation-facing boundary are controls, not downstream achievements.  A translation-efficiency or ribosome-profiling field, if present, only prevents conflation; it does not turn an RNA half-life difference into protein production.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
The local statistic compares a high-surveillance or high-coordinate stratum with its matched low-coordinate stratum after the stated controls.  It can be written abstractly as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{RNA}}
  &= \operatorname{stat}\bigl(\text{RNA readout}\mid \text{coordinate}, Z\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\text{transcription rate or abundance},\ \text{isoform state},\ \text{local structure proxy},\\
  &\qquad \text{motif density},\ \text{GC and dinucleotide composition},\ \text{codon-pair context},\ \text{batch}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The Teran-facing computation supplies the finite scale of this boundary: $n=38576$ rows and $1000$ permutations.  These numbers are enough to report that a transcript-surveillance RNA-layer boundary was evaluated on a nontrivial finite panel.  They are not enough, by themselves, to assert a decay-factor RBP mechanism, because measured local RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, rescue, and binding-defective controls are not part of the verified scalar payload for this run.

\paragraph{Adjacent RNA-layer calibration.}
The RNA-layer interpretation is strengthened by keeping nearby verified RNA-stability contacts visible as controls on scope rather than as imported conclusions.  In the m6A site-load half-life boundary run, $5837$ genes were joined; the static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO log half-life was $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman association between m6A load and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life was $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman association was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was $0.000999001$.  In the ARE deletion boundary run, the finite surface contained $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, used $1000$ actual permutations with $2000$ grading permutations, and reported sign concordance under a pooled label-permutation procedure.  These adjacent facts do not prove the transcript-surveillance claim; they show that the paper's RNA-stability layer is consistently treated as a finite readout layer with explicit permutation support and local refusal of downstream promotion.

\paragraph{Boundary rule.}
The chapter licenses only the following statement.  A finite transcript-surveillance coordinate may be reported as associated with an RNA-layer readout when the retained rows, controls, and permutation statistic remain inside the declared RNA boundary.  If high- and low-coordinate labels become exchangeable after the transcription, abundance, isoform, local-composition, and batch controls, the coordinate is only a descriptor.  If an RNA-layer difference remains but no measured decay-factor RBP occupancy is present, no RBP-mediated mechanism follows.  If a translation-facing readout is absent, degenerate, or merely routed by contact name, the RNA result is still not translation.  If translation is present as a same-row boundary, it still does not imply protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Mechanism refusal.}
A decay-factor RBP interpretation would require a stricter finite panel: measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, and an RNA half-life statistic that survives transcription, abundance, isoform, local-structure, motif-density, composition, codon-pair, translation-readout, and batch controls.  The present Teran-facing verified payload records $38576$ kept rows and $1000$ permutations for the transcript-surveillance boundary, but it does not contain those RBP perturbation and rescue fields.  Hence the chapter records a bounded RNA-stability contact and a strict refusal of mechanism-language beyond that contact.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone.  No ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, RNA-decay mechanism, decay-factor RBP bridge, or universal biological law follows from the coordinate statistic unless a separate finite contact supplies that layer and its own declared statistic.  The empirical content here is the finite RNA-layer boundary: $38576$ retained transcript-surveillance rows, $1000$ permutations, and a declared refusal to cross into translation, protein, mechanism, function, or universality without new same-row data.
