\subsection{Transcript-Surveillance Position Rule as a Bounded RNA-Layer Reality Boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record treats the transcript-surveillance position rule as a finite coding-RNA boundary test rather than as a general biological principle.  The admissible rows are synonymously comparable coding-RNA windows whose labels distinguish higher-coordinate and lower-coordinate placements after amino-acid-composition control.  A row is informative only when the same finite record also carries the translation readout, RNA half-life or decay readout, mRNA abundance or transcription control, local RNA-structure covariates, motif-density covariates, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, perturbation state, rescue state, and a declared break condition.  The target biological object is therefore not a transcript in isolation, and not a codon-coordinate calculation in isolation, but a matched window panel in which coordinate, local surveillance relation, and trigger state are read together.

\paragraph{Observed finite contact.}
For the transcript-surveillance claim keyed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.transcript\_surveillance.nmd\_position\_rule\_reality\_boundary\_teran2021}, the available run is \texttt{c362df6daa98}.  It keeps $38576$ finite rows, uses $1000$ permutations, and reports a runtime of $55.602$ seconds at verification time \texttt{2026-06-24T19:58:25+00:00}.  These numbers are used only as the finite evidence surface for the boundary statistic: they say that a transcript-surveillance position-rule readout was computed on a nonempty, bounded row set with a declared permutation count.  They do not by themselves supply measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, rescue behavior, binding-defective controls, non-target controls, or a complete same-window perturbation panel.

\paragraph{RNA-layer calibration from a neighboring verified boundary.}
The closest available RNA-layer calibration in the supplied finite memory is the mRNA-half-life and m6A boundary run keyed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_modification.m6a\_site\_load\_mrna\_halflife\_boundary\_gse163500}.  That run used $5837$ genes, reported static Spearman association $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log-half-life, causal Spearman association $0.245804$ for m6A-load change against log-half-life change, length-partial Spearman association $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value $0.000999001$.  Its stated verdict is \texttt{crosses\_boundary}, with coverage constrained by \texttt{min(Mean T coverage DMSO, STM2457)} at or above the twentieth percentile among valid joined genes.  This companion RNA-layer result is not imported as proof of the transcript-surveillance claim.  It only fixes the expected evidential standard for a finite RNA-stability boundary: a joined row set, declared controls, scalar association, permutation accounting, and a narrow interpretation that does not promote the statistic into mechanism or function.

\paragraph{Licensed reading.}
The transcript-surveillance result licenses a bounded statement of the following form.  Within the retained finite rows, a coordinate-associated RNA readout may be treated as crossing an RNA-layer reality boundary only as long as the coordinate label remains coupled to the declared surveillance-position statistic under the stated finite controls.  If the same row family later receives measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, and non-target controls, then the claim can be sharpened into a tested RBP bridge.  Without those additional rows, the current record remains a finite transcript-surveillance boundary and not an RBP mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Break conditions.}
The claim breaks if higher-coordinate and lower-coordinate synonymous labels are exchangeable after controlling transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch.  It also breaks if RNA half-life differs while measured decay-factor RBP occupancy is absent, unchanged, or not decoupled from coordinate in a controlled way.  A further break occurs if target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, if wild-type rescue does not restore it, or if binding-defective rescue, non-target control, RNA structure, motif density, transcription, isoform stability, abundance, or batch reproduces the pattern.  In those cases the result is at most a finite RNA-stability or transcript-surveillance association.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from RNA half-life, transcript-surveillance position, or mRNA abundance alone.  No protein output, folding state, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the retained $38576$ rows or from the $1000$-permutation computation.  The mandatory translation readout is a boundary guard: it prevents an RNA-layer association from being silently reinterpreted as a translation-layer claim.  Protein abundance, protein structure, physical viability, function, phenotype, and general law each require a separate layer-matched contact and a finite statistic declared for that layer.
