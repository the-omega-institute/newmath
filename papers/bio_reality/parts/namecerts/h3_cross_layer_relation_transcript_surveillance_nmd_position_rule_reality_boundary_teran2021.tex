\subsection{Transcript-Surveillance Position Rule as an RNA-Half-Life Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-nmd-position-rule-rna-half-life-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The object of this record is a finite panel of coding-RNA windows in which synonymous coordinate labels are compared only after the ordinary sequence and expression confounders have been carried in the same row.  A row is admissible only when it names its coordinate class, local coding context, transcript or isoform state, abundance state, batch, and an RNA half-life readout.  The intended bridge is narrower than a general transcript-surveillance mechanism: a coordinate-associated half-life pattern is allowed to enter the cross-layer ledger only if the same row also carries a translation readout, matched transcription or mRNA controls, and the measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein.  The panel therefore has the finite row shape
$$
\begin{aligned}
r = (&q, T, H, O, A, S, D, C, I, B, P, W, E),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q$ is the synonymous coordinate label, $T$ is the matched translation readout, $H$ is RNA half-life, $O$ is measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, $A$ is transcript abundance or matched mRNA control, $S$ is local RNA structure, $D$ is motif density, $C$ records GC, dinucleotide, and codon-pair context, $I$ is splice or isoform state, $B$ is batch, $P$ is perturbation state, $W$ is rescue state, and $E$ is the declared break flag.  This row type is deliberately more restrictive than an RNA half-life table: half-life alone is an observed RNA-stability surface, not a translation, protein, structure, function, or mechanism readout.

\paragraph{Observed finite evidence.}
For the transcript-surveillance position-rule contact, the available checked run is \texttt{c362df6daa98}.  It retained $38{,}576$ rows and used $1{,}000$ realized permutations, with a recorded runtime of $55.602$ seconds.  These numbers establish that the cited NMD-position-rule dataset supplies a finite, audited RNA-side boundary rather than an unconstrained narrative claim.  The scalar information present for this run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{kept}} &= 38{,}576,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 1{,}000,\\
t_{\mathrm{run}} &= 55.602\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run does not, by itself, report same-window decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective rescue.  Consequently it can support a bounded transcript-surveillance RNA-readout boundary, but it cannot close the stronger RBP-mediated decay bridge.

Two nearby RNA-stability contacts show the intended calibration of this boundary.  The m6A site-load half-life run \texttt{7cd189f15c4b} retained $5{,}837$ genes and reported a static Spearman association $\rho=-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log half-life, a causal Spearman association $\rho=0.245804$ for m6A-load change against $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, a length-partial Spearman association $\rho=0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value $p=0.000999001$.  Its own status was that the boundary statistic was crossed, while explicitly not becoming a mechanism or function statement.  The ARE-deletion half-life run \texttt{a41440ab2792} supplied $226$ BEAS2B deletion rows and $2{,}242$ Jurkat deletion rows, with $1{,}000$ realized permutations, $2{,}000$ grading permutations, and sign concordance recorded as true.  These comparison rows matter here because they show the chapter's rule: RNA half-life can be an audited biological readout, but each mechanism label requires the missing matched mechanism rows rather than the mere presence of a half-life effect.

\paragraph{Licensed relation.}
The licensed statement is therefore conditional.  If high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels remain separated for RNA half-life after controlling transcription rate, mRNA abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch, then the finite panel may report a coordinate-to-RNA-stability association.  It becomes an RBP-decay bridge only under the stricter pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
q &\leadsto O,\\
O &\leadsto H,\\
\mathrm{loss}(\mathrm{RBP}) &\Rightarrow \mathrm{collapse}(q\leadsto H),\\
\mathrm{wildtype\ rescue} &\Rightarrow \mathrm{return}(q\leadsto H),\\
\mathrm{binding\ defective\ rescue} &
Rightarrow \mathrm{return}(q\leadsto H),\\
\mathrm{non\ target\ control} &
Rightarrow \mathrm{return}(q\leadsto H).
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation readout $T$ is a boundary guard in this chapter, not a conclusion.  It prevents an RNA-half-life pattern from being redescribed as translation realization without an independent translation statistic.  The same guard blocks protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, and universality unless a separate row-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The claim is refused whenever high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable for $H$ after the named controls, whenever the half-life effect appears without a corresponding change in measured RBP occupancy $O$, whenever target-RBP loss fails to collapse the effect, or whenever wild-type rescue is not separated from binding-defective and non-target controls.  It is also refused if RNA structure, motif density, transcription rate, isoform state, transcript abundance, codon-pair context, or batch reproduces the pattern.  Under any of these cases the result remains a finite RNA-stability association, or a finite transcript-surveillance boundary, but not a decay-factor RBP mechanism bridge.

\paragraph{Boundary conclusion.}
The audited NMD-position-rule run is ready for inclusion as a bounded RNA-half-life and transcript-surveillance boundary because it supplies a concrete finite row count and permutation audit: $38{,}576$ retained rows over $1{,}000$ permutations.  The chapter must not state that this proves translation, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  The strongest local conclusion is that a finite transcript-surveillance RNA-readout surface has been recorded, while the stronger coordinate-to-decay-factor-RBP bridge remains conditional on measured occupancy, perturbation, rescue, and control rows in the same finite panel.
