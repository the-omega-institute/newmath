\subsection{Transcript-surveillance position rule as a bounded RNA-stability record}
\label{sec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite matched panel of coding-RNA windows in which synonymous coordinate labels are allowed to vary while amino-acid composition is held fixed.  Each retained row is read as a local window, not as a genome-wide law: it carries a coordinate label, the corresponding coding context, an RNA half-life or stability readout, the available mRNA-abundance and transcription-side controls, isoform or splice state when available, local sequence composition, local structure and motif covariates when present, batch labels, and a declared failure condition for over-interpreting the row.  The Teran transcript-surveillance run used here retained $38576$ rows, executed $1000$ permutations, and completed in $55.602$ seconds under experiment identifier $c362df6daa98$.  These numbers are sufficient for a finite RNA-stability boundary statement, but they are not sufficient by themselves for a decay-factor RBP mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Coordinate contrast.}
The scientific contrast is a high-coordinate versus low-coordinate synonymous position label after amino-acid composition and local coding context have been separated from the readout.  The intended positive pattern would be that the RNA half-life difference tracks a prespecified surveillance or decay-factor bridge rather than merely reflecting GC content, dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, transcript abundance, isoform choice, or batch.  In finite-row terms, the statistic is only licensed when the same row carries enough information to test the RNA-stability readout against the coordinate label and the control variables.  A compact way to write the permitted boundary statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{HL}}
  &= \operatorname{stat}\bigl(\mathrm{half\mbox{-}life}\mid \mathrm{coordinate}, Z\bigr),\\
Z
  &= \{\mathrm{amino\mbox{-}acid\ composition},\mathrm{GC},\mathrm{dinucleotide},\mathrm{codon\mbox{-}pair},
      \mathrm{structure},\mathrm{motif},\mathrm{isoform},\mathrm{abundance},\mathrm{batch}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed Teran boundary therefore says that the retained finite rows cross an RNA-stability contact surface under the declared permutation audit.  It does not say that the coordinate has become a protein-output, folding, physical-admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or universal sequence principle.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
The chapter is deliberately written with a translation readout as a required guardrail rather than as a conclusion.  A same-row translation measurement may prevent an RNA half-life statistic from being mistaken for translation realization, but its presence alone does not promote the RNA-stability statistic to translation biology.  The admissible statement is narrower: if translation readout, mRNA abundance, transcription rate, isoform state, local structure, motif density, composition, codon-pair context, and batch are jointly carried by the finite rows, then an RNA half-life contrast can be reported as an RNA-layer boundary after those controls have been applied.  If the translation readout is absent, the result remains a transcript-stability association.  If it is present, it is still only a boundary marker unless a separate translation statistic is computed from the matched rows.

\paragraph{RBP bridge refusal.}
A stronger decay-factor RBP bridge would require measured local RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, and a declared finite statistic showing that the half-life effect follows the RBP bridge rather than the coordinate label alone.  Those ingredients are not supplied by the Teran row count and permutation summary.  Consequently, the present chapter refuses the mechanism-level reading: an RNA half-life difference without measured decay-factor occupancy and perturbation/rescue control remains an RNA-stability association.  If target-RBP loss fails to collapse the effect, if wild-type rescue fails to restore it, if binding-defective or non-target controls reproduce it, or if structure, motif density, transcription, isoform stability, transcript abundance, or batch explain the same pattern, the RBP bridge is not licensed.

\paragraph{Neighboring calibration records.}
The scope boundary is consistent with other finite RNA-stability audits in the same standalone BioReality table.  The m6A site-load half-life record retained $5837$ genes, reported static Spearman association $-0.388929$ for m6A load versus DMSO log$_2$ half-life, causal Spearman association $0.245804$ for m6A load versus $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, length-partial Spearman association $0.227683$, and two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$ under experiment $7cd189f15c4b$.  That record explicitly means that a predeclared m6A/half-life boundary statistic was supported; it does not create a general RNA-decay mechanism or protein-function claim.  Likewise, the ARE deletion record used $226$ BEAS2B rows and $2242$ Jurkat rows, ran $1000$ actual permutations with $2000$ grading permutations, and reported a sign-concordant deletion result under experiment $a41440ab2792$.  These neighboring rows are cited only to fix the local discipline: finite RNA-stability contacts may be strong as RNA-layer measurements while still stopping before translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The Teran transcript-surveillance entry is therefore ready as a bounded cross-layer relation only in the weak sense that a finite coding-window coordinate can be tested against an RNA half-life surface under matched controls.  The positive content is the finite-row RNA-stability boundary: $38576$ retained rows, $1000$ permutations, and a reproducible run time of $55.602$ seconds identify a concrete audited readout rather than a purely verbal conjecture.  The negative content is equally part of the result.  No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone; no folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, RBP mechanism, or universal biological law follows from the coordinate contrast.  The RBP-mediated version remains conditional until the same finite rows also contain measured decay-factor occupancy, perturbation, rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and the corresponding controlled half-life statistic.
