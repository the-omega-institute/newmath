\subsection{Transcript-surveillance position rule as a bounded RNA-decay contact}
\label{sec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite panel of coding-RNA windows in which synonymous coordinate labels are allowed to vary while the amino-acid sequence is held fixed.  Each retained row is treated as a bounded RNA-level observation, not as a direct protein-level or organism-level statement.  The carrier for the panel is a row tuple
$$(\begin{aligned}
  x_i = (&q_i,\ T_i,\ H_i,\ A_i,\ O_i,\ C_i,\ D_i,\ U_i,\\
         &G_i,\ P_i,\ I_i,\ B_i,\ E_i),
\end{aligned})$$
where $q_i$ is the synonymous coordinate label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout when available, $H_i$ is the RNA half-life or decay readout, $A_i$ is the measured transcript abundance control, $O_i$ is the local decay-factor RBP occupancy coordinate when measured, $C_i$ collects local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, and amino-acid-composition controls, $D_i$ records transcription-rate or abundance separation, $U_i$ records splice or isoform state, $G_i$ is the perturbation state, $P_i$ is the rescue state, $I_i$ is the identifier and mapping state, $B_i$ is the batch or experimental block, and $E_i$ is the declared break condition.  The Teran2021 transcript-surveillance boundary run retained $38{,}576$ finite rows, used $1{,}000$ permutations, and completed in $55.602$ seconds under experiment run $\mathrm{c362df6daa98}$.  These numbers certify the size and execution of the finite RNA-surveillance contact; they do not by themselves assert an RBP-mediated mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary statistic.}
The admissible statistic is a coordinate-to-RNA-decay comparison conditioned on the same finite row set.  In schematic form the tested surface is
$$(\begin{aligned}
  \Delta_H
  ={}& \operatorname{Stat}\bigl(H_i \mid q_i, T_i, A_i, O_i, C_i, D_i, U_i, B_i\bigr)\\
     &- \operatorname{Stat}\bigl(H_i \mid T_i, A_i, O_i, C_i, D_i, U_i, B_i\bigr),
\end{aligned})$$
with the understanding that $q_i$ is only a finite coordinate label unless the decay readout is separated from transcription, abundance, local structure, motif density, composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, and batch.  The required translation readout $T_i$ is a boundary condition rather than a promotion rule: it prevents an RNA half-life association from being silently read as translation realization.  A row may therefore support an RNA-decay boundary only when the translation layer is explicitly present or explicitly marked as unavailable before the downstream interpretation is made.

\paragraph{RBP bridge condition.}
The stronger bridge from synonymous coordinate to RNA half-life through a decay-factor RBP requires more than the Teran2021 row count and permutation execution.  It requires local RBP occupancy $O_i$, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls on the same organism and window scale.  The licensed bridge condition is
$$(\begin{aligned}
  q_i \leadsto H_i\ \hbox{through an RBP}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  (&O_i\ \hbox{changes with the coordinate contrast})\\
  &\wedge\ (\hbox{target-RBP loss collapses the effect})\\
  &\wedge\ (\hbox{wild-type rescue restores it})\\
  &\wedge\ (\hbox{binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it}).
\end{aligned})$$
If the RNA half-life contrast remains after replacing the RBP explanation with transcription rate, transcript abundance, local RNA structure, motif density, GC or dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, or batch, then the row set remains an RNA-stability association rather than an RBP mechanism bridge.  If the half-life contrast appears without measured local RBP occupancy, or if occupancy appears without perturbation and rescue separation, the claim stops at the finite RNA-decay boundary.

\paragraph{Neighboring calibration records.}
The same paper-local audit style is visible in two other RNA-stability records and fixes the scale of interpretation used here.  The m6A site-load half-life boundary record retained $5{,}837$ genes, reported static Spearman correlation $-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log$_2$ half-life, causal Spearman correlation $0.245804$ for m6A load versus $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, length-partial Spearman correlation $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value $0.000999001$.  The ARE-deletion mRNA-stability boundary record used $1{,}000$ actual permutations, a $2{,}000$-permutation grading run, $226$ BEAS2B rows, $2{,}242$ Jurkat rows, and a sign-concordant deletion result under a pooled label-permutation null.  These comparison records show that scalar support may be strong at the RNA-stability layer while still remaining barred from translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, or universal-law language unless a separate matched contact supplies that layer.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The Teran2021 transcript-surveillance record is therefore a bounded cross-layer RNA-surveillance entry.  The finite execution establishes that a large retained row set was evaluated under a declared permutation procedure, and the panel definition states what additional measurements would be needed for a decay-factor RBP bridge.  It does not assert that RNA half-life differences are translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein output, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a general sequence law.  The same-row translation readout is a guardrail for interpretation, not a proof of a translation mechanism.  Matched mRNA abundance is a control surface, not RNA half-life evidence by itself.  The RNA half-life contact is a transcript-level readout, not measured RBP occupancy.  The result becomes a positive RBP-mediated bridge only if the same finite rows also carry measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and the declared half-life statistic after transcription-rate, abundance, structure, motif, composition, isoform, codon-context, translation-readout, and batch separation.
