\subsection{Transcript-surveillance position as a bounded RNA-stability readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record fixes a finite panel of coding-RNA rows in which a synonymous coordinate label is compared with an RNA half-life or decay-surveillance readout.  The observed computation for the transcript-surveillance position-rule packet retained $38576$ rows and used $1000$ permutations, with runtime $55.602$ seconds.  These numbers are the entire positive finite contact for this packet: they say that the joined table is large enough to evaluate a position-labelled RNA-stability boundary, not that the position coordinate has already been promoted to translation, protein output, folding, phenotype, adaptation, or a universal sequence law.

\paragraph{Row variables.}
Each row is read as a tuple containing a coordinate label, a transcript-surveillance or RNA half-life readout, transcript abundance or transcription control where available, local sequence composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch label, and the declared statistic used for the finite comparison.  The relevant separation is between a high-position or low-position synonymous label and the downstream RNA-stability readout after ordinary transcript-level controls have been attached.  In symbols, the admissible observation has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i &= (q_i,h_i,z_i,b_i),\\
  q_i &\in \{\text{high-coordinate},\text{low-coordinate}\},\\
  h_i &= \text{RNA half-life or transcript-surveillance readout},\\
  z_i &= \text{local and transcript-level controls},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $b_i$ records the batch or dataset provenance.  The finite test may compare $h_i$ across $q_i$ after conditioning on $z_i$, but it may not reinterpret $q_i$ as a direct biochemical cause unless the missing mechanism rows are also present.

\paragraph{What the verified run licenses.}
The verified transcript-surveillance run supplies a finite boundary statistic for the NMD-position-rule subject: $n=38576$ retained rows, $1000$ permutations, and a completed run in $55.602$ seconds.  The scientifically usable conclusion is therefore narrow.  A coordinate-associated RNA-stability pattern can be reported as a bounded cross-layer relation between coding-position information and a transcript-level decay readout.  The result remains a transcript-surveillance boundary even when the finite statistic is stable under the declared controls.  It is not, by itself, a translation-efficiency measurement, a ribosome-occupancy measurement, a protein-abundance measurement, a folding result, a physical-admissibility assay, a functional assay, a phenotype, a mechanism, or evidence for a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Comparison with adjacent RNA-stability contacts.}
The same finite-row discipline is visible in two neighboring RNA-stability records.  In the mRNA half-life boundary for the m6A site-load contact, $5837$ genes were joined; the static Spearman coefficient between m6A load and DMSO log half-life was $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman coefficient between m6A-load change and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life was $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman coefficient was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was $0.000999001$.  That record explicitly classifies the verdict as a boundary crossing, not as a mechanism or function claim.  In the ARE-deletion stability contact, the finite check used $1000$ actual permutations, a $2000$-permutation grading layer, $226$ BEAS2B rows, $2242$ Jurkat rows, and a sign-concordant outcome under a pooled label permutation over deletions.  These two cases serve as local calibration: RNA-stability evidence may cross a readout boundary with concrete rows and permutation statistics, while still refusing every higher-layer interpretation until a layer-matched contact supplies it.

\paragraph{RBP mechanism gate.}
For the present transcript-surveillance packet, a decay-factor RBP bridge would require more than an RNA half-life or NMD-position statistic.  The finite rows would also need measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, local RNA-structure and motif-density controls, and matched transcription or mRNA abundance controls.  If the coordinate labels remain exchangeable for RNA half-life after conditioning on transcription rate, local structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch, then the coordinate is only a bounded descriptor.  If RNA half-life differs but measured RBP occupancy does not move, or if target-RBP loss and wild-type rescue do not track the effect, the record is only an RNA-stability association.

\paragraph{Translation boundary.}
A same-row translation readout is mandatory before any statement involving translation can be evaluated.  Even then, the translation row would function as a boundary variable, not as automatic proof of RNA decay mechanism.  The finite admissible extension would have the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i &= (q_i,t_i,h_i,o_i,z_i,c_i),\\
  t_i &= \text{matched translation readout},\\
  h_i &= \text{RNA half-life readout},\\
  o_i &= \text{measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy},\\
  c_i &= \text{perturbation and rescue state}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Only when $h_i$ follows $o_i$ under coordinate--occupancy decoupling, collapses after target-RBP loss, returns with wild-type rescue, and is not restored by binding-defective or non-target controls may the packet be read as a bounded RBP-mediated RNA-stability bridge.  Without those rows, the present result remains a finite transcript-surveillance position boundary.

\paragraph{Refusal clauses.}
The chapter therefore permits one local statement: the Teran-style transcript-surveillance table supplies a finite position-labelled RNA-stability boundary with $38576$ retained rows and $1000$ permutations.  It refuses the following promotions.  No RNA half-life difference is evidence of translation realization, protein output, protein structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  No coordinate-to-RNA-stability bridge is an RBP mechanism unless measured RBP occupancy, perturbation, rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls are present in the same finite panel.  If binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, local RNA structure, motif density, transcription, isoform state, transcript abundance, or batch reproduce the pattern, the bridge claim is rejected and the record remains a bounded RNA-stability association.
