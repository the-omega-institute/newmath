\subsection{Transcript surveillance position rule with an RNA half-life boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-transcript-surveillance-nmd-position-rule-teran2021}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This BioReality record concerns a finite panel of coding-RNA windows whose synonymous labels can be compared only after amino-acid composition has been held fixed.  Each admissible row is a same-window tuple carrying a coordinate label, a translation readout when available, an RNA half-life readout, transcript abundance, transcription-rate or mRNA-control information, local RNA-structure and motif-density covariates, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, batch, perturbation state, rescue state, and an explicit break flag.  The intended biological object is not an abstract codon geometry by itself, but synonymously editable coding RNA crossed with an RNA-surveillance readout and a declared translation boundary.  The row form is therefore finite and inspectable: a coordinate may be compared with an RNA half-life only inside the same declared covariate frame, and any promotion beyond that frame is disallowed.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The Teran-position-rule run attached to this record is \texttt{c362df6daa98}.  It retained $38576$ joined rows, used $1000$ permutations, and completed in $55.602$ seconds.  Those numbers certify that the record is a realized finite computation rather than an unconstrained biological assertion.  The statistic is intentionally narrow: it can support an RNA half-life boundary for matched rows, but it does not by itself report measured local occupancy of a decay-factor RNA-binding protein, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or a non-target rescue control.  In symbols, the licensed scope is only the row-level comparison
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (\text{coordinate label},\ \text{controls}) &\longmapsto \text{RNA half-life readout},\\
  \text{with } n_{\mathrm{kept}} &= 38576,\quad N_{\mathrm{perm}}=1000.
\end{aligned}
$$
It is not a statement that the coordinate has become translation output, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, causation, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Boundary rule.}
A positive cross-layer relation would require more than an RNA half-life association.  The same finite rows would have to show that high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous labels remain separated after transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and amino-acid composition are accounted for.  They would also have to carry the mandatory same-row translation readout before any downstream interpretation is attempted.  Even then, an RBP-mediated RNA-decay bridge is licensed only if measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RBP tracks the half-life effect, coordinate and occupancy can be decoupled, target-RBP loss collapses the effect, wild-type rescue restores it, and binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it.  If the half-life contrast survives without measured RBP occupancy, the result remains an RNA-stability association.  If local structure, motif density, transcription, isoform state, abundance, or batch reproduces the pattern, the RBP bridge is refused.

\paragraph{Neighboring RNA-layer calibrators.}
Other finite BioReality records show why this boundary is strict.  The m6A site-load half-life record \texttt{7cd189f15c4b} evaluated $5837$ genes and reported a static Spearman value of $-0.388929$ for m6A load versus DMSO half-life, a causal Spearman value of $0.245804$ for m6A load versus $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, a length-partial Spearman value of $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value of $0.000999001$.  That record crosses its own RNA-modification half-life boundary, but its numbers do not supply the Teran NMD-position rows with decay-factor RBP occupancy or rescue evidence.  The ARE deletion record \texttt{a41440ab2792} used $1000$ actual permutations, $2000$ grading permutations, $226$ BEAS2B rows, and $2242$ Jurkat rows, with sign concordance marked true; it supports a distinct ARE deletion stability boundary, not an NMD-position RBP mechanism.  The miRNA seed record \texttt{50f22b191476} used $55170$ rows, had median aggregate per-miRNA partial rho $-0.224966$, and replicated the gradient in $7$ cases; this is again an RNA-regulatory comparison, not evidence that the NMD-position coordinate has acquired translation, protein, structure, function, or organism-level meaning.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter therefore records a bounded transcript-surveillance reality contact.  It may say that a finite same-organism RNA half-life computation exists for the NMD-position-rule subject, with $38576$ retained rows and $1000$ permutations.  It may also say that the computation belongs to the RNA-stability side of a cross-layer screening program.  It may not say that RNA half-life is translation realization, ribosome occupancy, mature protein output, protein folding, physical admissibility, function realization, phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or a universal biological law.  Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts.  In particular, a decay-factor RBP bridge remains unavailable until the same rows include measured RBP occupancy, target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and a declared finite statistic showing that the half-life effect follows that RBP route rather than transcription, RNA structure, motif density, isoform state, abundance, composition, codon-pair context, or batch.
