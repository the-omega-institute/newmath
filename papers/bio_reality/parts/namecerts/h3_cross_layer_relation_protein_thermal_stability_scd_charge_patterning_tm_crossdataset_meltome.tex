\subsection{Position-Resolved Translation Boundary for the $f_3$-Stress Coordinate}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-protein-thermal-stability-scd-charge-patterning-tm-crossdataset-meltome}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record isolates a single finite question: whether an $f_3$-stress residual, computed after amino-acid quotienting and ordinary sequence controls, can survive as a bounded predictor of a position-resolved translation readout.  The row type is not a protein-state row and not a phenotype row.  A usable row must be indexed by organism, coding sequence, codon position modulo $3$, a fixed local coding window, local $f_3$-paired codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight surface, matched mRNA abundance, and a codon-indexed or window-indexed ribosome-occupancy readout.  The admissible statistic is therefore a residual survival entry of the form $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, position or amino-acid-bin terms, coverage, mappability, and simpler codon baselines.

These numbers certify the finite coordinate table used by the proposed readout test.  They do not, by themselves, measure ribosome motion, translation efficiency, protein stability, folding, function, or adaptation.

\paragraph{Cross-layer readout requirement.}
The biological contact required for this chapter is stronger than an organism-level codon-usage table or a modeled tAI or stAI surface.  It must join the same coding sequence and the same local window to codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and footprint occupancy.  A positive report is allowed only if the joined matrix has nondegenerate rank and the $f_3$ residual improves the position-resolved translation readout after all controls and after ablation against simpler codon summaries.  In symbols, the permissible survival call has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the comparison performed on held-out or otherwise rank-checked finite rows.  If the coefficient is nonpositive, if the design matrix is saturated, or if the effect disappears under amino-acid-bin, position, GC3, length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, or simpler-baseline controls, the coordinate remains a codon-topology residual only.

\paragraph{Relation to existing finite evidence.}
The available finite biological checks show that the surrounding BioReality pipeline can hold large row-indexed assays, but they do not supply the exact position-resolved translation matrix needed here.  The protein thermal-stability surface associated with the present claim identifier contains $8092$ matched rows, ran for $512.940125$ seconds, and reports a UniProt sequence match rate of $0.963104$.  That is a protein-state contact, not a ribosome-position contact.  Other recorded assay surfaces include $296950$ polyadenylation-site rows over $24740$ genes, an exon-inclusion hexamer comparison with $n=1037$ and collinearity $-0.008429245619$, and a mouse VASTDB splice surface with $50633$ rows and all-tissue positivity recorded as true.  These row counts show that finite cross-layer tables are admissible in the project vocabulary, but none of them substitutes for same-window ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Ramp comparison as a warning sign.}
A nearby structural-order ramp computation is informative only as a caution about small effects and localization gates.  With five folds and a minimum joined count of $300$, the primary ramp window $n=50$ gave weighted held-out increments $0.0011165640103326878$ for the ramp and $-0.00026157313923957926$ for the body, with a ramp-minus-body increment of $0.001378137149572267$.  The sign-flip probability for ramp positivity was $0.0478515625$, the body probability was $0.54296875$, and the paired ramp-minus-body probability was $0.05859375$.  The recorded classification was positive ramp signal without passing the localization gate.  This matters here because a small positive translation-window score would still be insufficient unless it survives the stricter same-window readout, rank, control, and ablation conditions.  The chapter therefore treats any unjoined or rank-degenerate $f_3$ signal as a boundary condition rather than as a promoted biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The present finite record does not claim translation realization from codon geometry alone.  It does not claim measured ribosome occupancy from modeled tAI or stAI weights alone.  It does not claim protein abundance, folding, thermal stability, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, or universality from the $f_3$ coordinate.  Those statements require their own layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  In particular, the $8092$-row thermal-stability contact is not evidence that the $f_3$-stress coordinate explains melting temperature through translation; it is only a separate protein-state surface unless the missing translation readout is joined and shown to mediate the downstream association.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a self-contained boundary record.  The coordinate side is finite and explicitly enumerated: $M$ has $20$ codons, the saturated $f_3$ set has $18$ codons, the quotient has $10$ rows, and the vector checks are fixed.  The biological survival side remains unresolved because the required same-organism, same-CDS, same-window ribosome-footprint matrix is absent.  The correct current reading is therefore conditional: $f_3$-stress may be tested against position-resolved ribosome occupancy, but no positive translation, protein, structure, function, mechanism, or universal claim follows until that finite joined statistic exists and survives the stated controls.
