\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
The finite claim is therefore admissible as a translation-readout boundary for the protein thermal-stability surface. The Meltome table contributes a real protein-level row count and match-rate anchor, while the codon and quotient rows define the $f_3$ residual coordinate. What is still missing is the single panel that joins, for the same organism, CDS, and local window, ordered codons, bounded wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome footprints or a bounded translation-efficiency residual. Until that panel exists and the statistic $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ is positive after rank checks and baseline ablation, the row cannot claim translation realization. It also cannot claim protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. A downstream thermal-stability interpretation would require this translation-readout step first, followed by a separately matched protein-level test.
