The present memory names the needed contact route for position-resolved ribosome occupancy, codon usage, modeled adaptation weights, and matched mRNA abundance, but it does not contain the joined same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix.  Consequently the translation statistic remains uncomputed here.
