\subsection{Position-resolved f\_3-stress as a translation-readout boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-ribosome-occupancy-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate under test.}
The object considered here is not a protein-stability claim and not a translation-mechanism claim. It is a finite coding-window coordinate: for each admissible row one would need an organism, an ordered coding sequence, a codon index or fixed local window, amino-acid-quotient-surviving synonymous codon residuals, local $f_3$-paired codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight surface, matched mRNA abundance in the same condition, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency residual. The proposed readback is a residual survival entry of the form $S_{f_3\text{-stress}}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ is a codon-indexed or window-indexed translation readout and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, position or amino-acid-bin terms, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler codon baselines.

\paragraph{Finite BEDC-derived coordinates available before the biological join.}
The current finite coordinate geometry is explicit. The saturated $f_3$ set over $R$ contains
$\{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}$,
while $M=\mathrm{Med}_R\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ contains the same eighteen codons plus $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$. The recorded $f_3$ boundary is empty, so this row does not derive a nontrivial biological boundary by itself. The quotient record has $|\pi_M|=10$, with $M$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and quotient vector $(10,14,6,1)$. These numbers are useful because they fix the finite residual vocabulary and make the rank question concrete, but they do not provide ribosome-footprint rows.

\paragraph{Related finite contact that is already measured.}
A separate protein thermal-stability contact for SCD charge patterning and melting temperature is present with $n=8092$, runtime $512.940125$ seconds, and UniProt sequence match rate $0.963104$. This is a real finite-row protein-level contact, but it is not the missing position-resolved ribosome-occupancy matrix required for the present $f_3$-stress translation-boundary test. It may help police downstream overreach: a protein-stability table, even when matched at high sequence rate, does not turn codon-window residuals into translation occupancy, folding dynamics, physical admissibility, molecular function, or a general biological law.

\paragraph{A nearby powered translation-adjacent signal does not close this packet.}
The structural-order ramp record has five folds, minimum joined count $300$, and primary ramp size $50$. At that size the weighted held-out $f_3$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, while the body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$ and the ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$. The sign-flip probability for positive ramp signal is $0.0478515625$, whereas the paired ramp-minus-body localization probability is $0.05859375$ and the body probability is $0.54296875$. Its own conclusion is therefore ramp-positive but not localized. This is informative because it shows that finite $f_3$-derived signals can be tested against measured rows, yet it also shows why the present claim cannot be promoted by analogy: the required position-resolved ribosome-occupancy join has not been supplied for this exact packet.

\paragraph{Admissible statistic and null boundary.}
The admissible statistic is a same-organism, same-coding-sequence, same-window regression or description-length comparison in which $f_3$-stress is computed after amino-acid projection and then asked whether it contributes beyond $Z$. In display form the intended survival gate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-stress}}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &> 0,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{f_3},X_Z) &\text{ nondegenerate},\\
\Delta_{f_3\text{-stress}} &\text{ survives simpler codon and }M\text{-density ablations.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If any of these three tests fails, the row remains a codon-topology or code-read residual only. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may serve as bounded wobble annotations, and matched mRNA abundance may serve as a transcription-layer control, but neither is an observed codon-indexed ribosome-occupancy surface. Likewise, review routing, notation such as $S^{QT}$, and the existence of a contact name are not measurements.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the finite coordinate geometry alone. No protein abundance, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation claim, causation claim, or universality statement follows from this packet unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the controlled statistic is computed on those rows. For the present subject, the exact missing object is a joined finite table containing ordered coding-sequence windows, codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency residuals. Until that table exists and the survival statistic is positive and rank-sufficient after the stated controls, the scientific status is a translation-readout boundary rather than a positive cross-layer result.
