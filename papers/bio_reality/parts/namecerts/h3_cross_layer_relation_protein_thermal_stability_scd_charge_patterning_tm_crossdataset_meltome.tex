\subsection{Finite Scope for an $f_3$-Stress Translation-Readout Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-ribosome-occupancy}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The present BioReality row concerns the conjectural coordinate $f_3$-stress in coding sequences and asks whether that coordinate can be tested against a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readout.  The carrier is a finite CDS-window table keyed by organism, coding sequence, codon position modulo $3$, local paired-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight surface, matched mRNA abundance, and a codon-indexed or local-window ribosome-footprint statistic.  This is deliberately narrower than a protein-level or phenotype-level claim: the admissible response variable is position-resolved ribosome occupancy, or an explicitly residualized translation-efficiency readout, in the same organism and coding sequence as the codon rows.

The finite codon-side memory fixes the $f_3$ saturation surface before any biological interpretation is attempted.  In the recorded run $76d3dc408a57$, the set $M=\mathrm{Med}_R\cup\mathrm{ACA}$ contains the $20$ codons
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding saturated $f_3$ row omits exactly $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ from this list, leaving the $18$-codon set
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The stored $f_3$ boundary list is empty, so the finite codon-side datum is not a measured translation result; it is a discrete residual coordinate whose biological use must be earned by a separately joined readout matrix.

These ten rows are enough to define a finite residual coordinate and to state a rank-aware test, but they do not themselves contain footprint counts, matched mRNA denominators, coverage filters, or mappability filters.  Therefore the only legitimate readback is a conditional statistic of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)>0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is the position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency residual and $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, position or amino-acid-bin controls, codon-count baselines, coverage limits, mappability limits, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Contact status.}
The present contact route is not yet a same-row ribosome-occupancy table.  The available position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact says only that ordered CDS coordinates can, in principle, be joined to codon-indexed footprint arrays.  The matched mRNA contact supplies a control covariate for translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation measurement by itself.  The tAI or stAI contact supplies bounded codon-adaptation weights, not observed ribosome occupancy.  Consequently, the row is ready as a finite boundary statement, not as a positive cross-layer survival claim.

A related finite protein-stability experiment is present for comparison but must not be conflated with this translation-boundary test.  The cross-dataset Meltome row for charge-patterning versus thermal stability used $n=8092$ rows, had a UniProt sequence match rate of $0.963104$, and ran in $512.940125$ seconds in run $72424b9e9d53$.  Those values establish that a separate protein-level finite table exists in the surrounding BioReality ledger, but they do not measure position-resolved ribosome occupancy for the $f_3$-stress coordinate.  Likewise, the structural-order ramp row reports a positive ramp-side held-out increment $0.0011165640103326878$, a body increment $-0.00026157313923957926$, and a paired ramp-minus-body value $0.001378137149572267$, with sign-flip probabilities $0.0478515625$, $0.54296875$, and $0.05859375$ respectively.  Its conclusion is ramp-positive but not localized.  Those numbers are useful evidence that finite cross-layer tests can be audited with held-out residual scores, but they are not a substitute for the missing same-window ribosome-footprint matrix in the present row.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite mathematical object is therefore admissible only as a translation-readout boundary.  The row may ask for a controlled statistic in which amino-acid-quotient-surviving synonymous-codon residuals are tested against position-resolved ribosome occupancy after organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, codon-count, position, coverage, mappability, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  If the joined matrix is rank-degenerate, underpowered, or has non-positive survival after these controls, the $f_3$-stress coordinate remains a codon-topology residual.  No translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from the codon geometry, from wobble-weight asymmetry, from tAI or stAI weights, or from review eligibility alone.  Any such downstream statement requires its own matched biological contact and its own finite statistic at that layer.
