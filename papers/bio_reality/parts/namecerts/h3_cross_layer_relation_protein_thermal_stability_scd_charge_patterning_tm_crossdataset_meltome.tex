\subsection{Charge-patterning thermal-stability readout with a translation-boundary control}
\label{subsec:bioreality-scd-charge-patterning-tm-crossdataset-meltome}

\paragraph{Finite table.}
The finite row set for this record is the cross-dataset protein thermal-stability table indexed by matched protein identifiers, sequence-derived charge-patterning coordinates, and measured melting-temperature readouts. The realized protein-stability surface contains $8092$ joined rows, was evaluated in $512.940125$ seconds, and retained a UniProt sequence match rate of $0.963104$. The chapter therefore records a real protein-layer readout, but it does not by itself authorize a translation-layer explanation for that readout. The measured endpoint is thermal stability, while the auxiliary codon-topology material supplied with this record concerns an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and a still-unfilled position-resolved ribosome-occupancy window. These are different finite surfaces, and the distinction is part of the claim.

The codon-side coordinate is restricted to synonymous residual information that survives amino-acid projection. In the finite $f_3$ saturation computation, the ambient set $M=\operatorname{Med}_R\cup\operatorname{ACA}$ is

The boundary component for $f_3$ is empty in the recorded run. Thus the codon coordinate is a finite residual contrast, not a direct measurement of ribosome movement, protein folding, or physical admissibility.

\paragraph{Thermal-stability endpoint.}
The protein-stability contact in this chapter is the measured thermal-stability table, not a ribosome-profiling table. The admissible statement is therefore narrow: sequence-level charge patterning and matched thermal-stability rows give a finite protein-layer association surface with $n=8092$ and high sequence matching. The result can be compared with other finite cross-layer records in the same data bundle: an alternative-polyadenylation surface has $24740$ genes and $296950$ PAS rows; an ESE/MaxEnt exon-inclusion comparison has $1037$ rows with collinearity $-0.008429245619$; the mouse VASTDB splice-MaxEnt surface has $50633$ rows with all tissue signs positive; and the positional structural-order run reports a primary ramp of $50$ codons with weighted held-out increments $0.0011165640103326878$ on the ramp and $-0.00026157313923957926$ in the body. Those companion facts show that the bundle contains several measured downstream surfaces, but they do not substitute for the missing translation-window rows required by the $f_3$ packet.

\paragraph{Local survival statistic.}
The translation-side statistic requested by the codon residual has the schematic form
$$(\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0,
\end{aligned})$$
where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ must be a same-organism, same-CDS, same-window ribosome-occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout, and $Z$ must include amino-acid composition or amino-acid-bin controls, organism, $\mathrm{GC3}$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability restrictions, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablation. The finite data supplied here do not contain that joined matrix. Consequently, the $f_3$ coordinate can be recorded as a codon residual adjacent to a protein-stability chapter, but it cannot be promoted into a translation-readout explanation of thermal stability.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation realization, folding mechanism, physical admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter. A protein thermal-stability table with $8092$ rows is a genuine protein-layer contact, and the codon quotient and $f_3$ saturation tables are genuine finite coordinate computations. The missing object is the mediator: a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency matrix joined to the same coding windows and residualized against the declared controls. If that matrix is rank-degenerate, absent, or non-positive after ablation, the $f_3$ coordinate remains a codon-topology residual only. If it is later supplied and $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ is positive under the full control set, the claim may be restated as a bounded translation-readout survival entry, still separate from thermal-stability mechanism unless an additional matched protein-layer mediation analysis is present.
