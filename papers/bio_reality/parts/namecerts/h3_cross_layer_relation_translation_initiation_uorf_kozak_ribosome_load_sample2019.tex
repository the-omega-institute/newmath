\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for uORF, Kozak, and ribosome-load readouts}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate-uorf-kozak-ribosome-load}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record treats the proposed $B^*_{Q6}$ to translation-readout surface as a finite rank question before it is allowed to become a biological survival claim.  The carrier is a residualized design matrix whose columns include synonymous $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, organism-indexed translation readout surfaces, and controls $Z$ for amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding length, and methionine-density baseline.  The finite translation-initiation contact attached to this packet is the uORF, Kozak, and ribosome-load sample run $22595b48828f$: it contains $41862$ fixed rows, $16739$ varying rows, a runtime of $131.941$ seconds, and a two-sample concordance flag equal to true.  These numbers justify writing down the gate as a finite-row readback, but they do not by themselves supply a non-saturated $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For a proposed coordinate-readout pair $(q_i,T_j)$, both variables are first residualized against the same control matrix $Z$.  Let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote those residuals, and let $X_{ij}$ be the post-control design containing the candidate coordinate and the permitted nuisance columns.  The admissibility test is not the in-sample fitted score; it is the survival of degrees of freedom after controls and before protein, structure, function, or phenotype language is introduced.  In the local notation of this chapter, the gate is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &< n,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}) &< n,\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij} &= n-\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}) > 0,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &\text{ is reportable only when a held-out translation readout remains falsifiable.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If these inequalities fail, the row is not a positive translation-survival row.  It is either coupling-only, if a bounded contact exists, or needs more data, if the contact cannot make the proposed survival falsifiable.

\paragraph{What the existing finite rows support.}
The earlier $B^*_{Q6}$ coupling computation run $4a783972e74b$ computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The codon count space was $61$, the loaded organisms were human, budding yeast, and \textit{E. coli} K-12, and the proxy readout was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  Under the threshold $0.01$, the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met: there were $9$ nonzero entries and the maximum absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.  This is finite contact with a translation-adjacent proxy, not a survival result.  The proxy surface lacks the held-out ribosome or translation-efficiency rows needed to turn $A^{QT}$ into $S^{QT}$.

\paragraph{Residual-basis pressure.}
The separate residual-basis extraction run $c1ef9b455187$ shows why the gate must be rank-aware.  It used the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length penalty $\log_2(1+n_{col})$ and computed organism-scoped residual curves.  Human had $16008$ joined common rows, $6$ candidate blocks, $d_{modeled}=0.9020707321191321$, $d_{difference}=0.018824203448813415$, and final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$.  Budding yeast had $3046$ joined common rows, $9$ candidate blocks, $d_{modeled}=0.2199040339633322$, $d_{difference}=0.008171838236265161$, and final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$.  \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 had $3415$ joined common rows, $2$ candidate blocks, $d_{modeled}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{difference}=0.00017488612263594217$, and final $\rho=0.9979478314995476$.  Zebrafish had $14410$ joined common rows, one candidate block, $d_{modeled}=0.874628721049266$, $d_{difference}=0.0$, and final $\rho=0.9791828971490305$.  Gallus gallus and \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} were marked needs-data.  Across the computed organisms, the residuals were classified as persistent, with a reported floor $\rho_{min}=0.9723341658092314$ and computed count $4$.  A persistent residual is therefore a reason to demand a stronger readout panel, not a reason to promote the coordinate.

\paragraph{Matched controls and neighboring finite contacts.}
The mRNA-stability contact run $74d321d44dd7$ contributes a separate finite boundary: $16500$ human rows, $18924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, runtime $46.349$ seconds, and the circularity guard labelled non-CSC tAI.  The branchpoint-usage run $a27e7c351f67$ contributes another controlled finite surface with $n=10584$, expression control enabled, and runtime $138.47155$ seconds.  These contacts are useful as examples of controlled finite panels, but neither one measures the same $S^{QT}$ boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ translation initiation.  They therefore remain context for control discipline rather than evidence of translation survival.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
The chapter assigns no positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ when the design is rank-saturated, underpowered, or supported only by modeled tAI, stAI, tRNA-copy, codon-usage, or all-1.0 in-sample fitted surfaces.  The decisive distinction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
A^{QT}_{ij} &\neq S^{QT}_{ij},\\
\text{proxy contact} &\neq \text{controlled survival contact},\\
R^2_{in}=1 &\neq \text{held-out translation survival when rank is saturated.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently, this packet is ready as a rank-admissibility and refusal certificate: it records which finite rows exist, which controls must be present, and why a nondegenerate held-out translation readout is still required before survival can be claimed.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
Nothing in this chapter asserts ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those statements require their own layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  The only local conclusion is narrower: a proposed $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival entry must pass a residualized rank and degrees-of-freedom gate before any positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ readback is admissible.
