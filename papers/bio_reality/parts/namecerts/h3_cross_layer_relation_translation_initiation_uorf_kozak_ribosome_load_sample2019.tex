\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for the uORF--Kozak ribosome-load panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record fixes a bounded translation-readout question rather than a protein, structure, or phenotype question.  The finite object is a residualized design matrix whose rows are organism-indexed or transcript-indexed observations, whose tested coordinates are the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions, and whose nuisance block $Z$ contains the controls needed to keep a codon-topology residual from being mistaken for translation realization.  In the present memory the uORF--Kozak ribosome-load contact is represented by experiment $22595b48828f$, with $41862$ fixed rows, $16739$ varying rows, a runtime of $131.941$ seconds, and a concordant two-sample readback.  These numbers certify that a finite row surface exists for the translation-initiation contact; they do not by themselves certify a surviving $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ entry.

\paragraph{Gate equation.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a translation readout $T_j$, the proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is admissible only after both sides are projected away from the declared controls.  The gate is the following finite-rank condition:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= (I-P_Z)q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= (I-P_Z)T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{is reportable only if }\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}^{\perp Z}) < n_{ij}\text{ and the held-out score is falsifiable.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $X_{ij}^{\perp Z}$ is the post-control design assembled from the tested coordinate, the translation readout, and the remaining allowed columns.  The controls include amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding length, and an $M$-density baseline; matched mRNA is a control when translation efficiency is formed from ribosome and transcript rows.  A saturated in-sample fit, including an all-$1.0$ fitted surface, is therefore not a survival result.  It is a rank artifact unless independent held-out rows remain after $Z$.

\paragraph{Existing finite evidence.}
The closest already-computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ contact is a proxy coupling matrix over three organisms, using codon usage and a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum rather than a measured ribosome or translation-efficiency matrix.  That run loaded Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12; it worked in a $61$-codon count space and computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f_3$-stress.  Its break rule was that the contact would go null if all nine $q_i$ had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout.  The rule was not met: all nine entries were nonzero above threshold, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  This is useful as a finite coupling contact, but it remains $A^{QT}$-level evidence.  It is not a $Z$-controlled, held-out $S^{QT}$ survival certificate.

\paragraph{Residual background.}
The residual-basis extraction run gives the rank gate its practical motivation.  Four organisms have computed residual summaries and two are marked as needing more data.  Homo sapiens has $16008$ joined rows, six selected candidate blocks, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191321$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.018824203448813415$, and final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $3046$ joined rows, nine candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$, and final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $3415$ joined rows, two candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.00017488612263594217$, and final $\rho=0.9979478314995476$.  Danio rerio has $14410$ joined rows, one candidate, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.0$, and final $\rho=0.9791828971490305$.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 are not computed in this packet.  Across the computed organisms the residual floor is high, with minimum final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$ and no organism classified as compressible.  The extraction used a stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with description length $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, so persistence here means unexplained residual structure after available readout blocks, not a mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Null comparisons already present.}
The degree-of-freedom matched nulls show why the gate must inspect each row surface rather than accept fitted contact.  In Homo sapiens, selected increments for PTM density, complex membership, TM count, measured tRNA tAI, domain count, and localization include values $0.014780177462400674$, $0.004618389163869967$, $0.0014438137599134737$, $0.0005032876020244136$, $0.00003840392718095609$, and $0.006528084968494762$; the domain-count step fails its local null because $0.00003840392718095609<0.00021932006910265595$.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the first four selected increments exceed their nulls, while complex membership, measured tRNA tAI, domain count, and mRNA do not; the final localization increment $0.008385002223710514$ exceeds its null $0.00708487840939486$.  Escherichia coli has an mRNA increment $0.0017706954554909888$ above null $0.0013371669456573104$, followed by measured TE increment $0.0002819723320884449$ below null $0.0010272364744326006$.  These finite comparisons support a conservative reading: some blocks can pass local nulls, but translation survival is not inherited from block selection unless the same $q_i \to T_j$ entry is estimated on an admissible post-control surface.

\paragraph{Relation to the uORF--Kozak contact.}
The uORF--Kozak ribosome-load panel contributes a translation-initiation surface with fixed and varying row classes.  Its two-sample concordance says that the contact is not merely a routing token: the finite table has a reproducible partition between the $41862$ fixed rows and the $16739$ varying rows.  The rank-admissible survival question is narrower.  One must join the same row universe to $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and to the declared controls, then ask whether any residual coordinate improves a held-out translation readout after the nuisance block has been removed.  The current packet has the translation-initiation row counts and a proxy codon/tRNA coupling result, but it does not contain a same-row matrix proving a positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ for any of the nine residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Cross-check contacts.}
Other finite contacts in the memory are consistent with this boundary.  The branchpoint-usage run contains $10584$ rows, is expression-controlled, and used UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8 log-one-plus median expression over $54$ tissue scores joined by same-strand intron overlap; its runtime was $138.47155$ seconds.  The codon-composition mRNA half-life run contains $16500$ human rows and $18924$ mouse rows, uses a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, has raw $\rho=0.126224$, and ran in $46.349$ seconds.  These contacts show that finite biological surfaces can be joined under controls, but they remain branchpoint or RNA-stability contacts unless the exact translation-readout matrix is assembled for this gate.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The present record is a rank-admissibility certificate and refusal rule, not a positive translation-survival theorem.  It permits the following statement: a proposed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation entry may be tested only when the post-control design has unsaturated rank, sufficient residual degrees of freedom, and a held-out translation boundary.  It refuses the following promotion: proxy $A^{QT}$ coupling, modeled tAI or stAI contact, a fixed-versus-varying uORF--Kozak split, or a perfect in-sample fit cannot be reported as $S^{QT}>0$ when the rank gate fails.  No ribosome-occupancy realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this packet unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding finite rows and the declared statistic is recomputed there.
