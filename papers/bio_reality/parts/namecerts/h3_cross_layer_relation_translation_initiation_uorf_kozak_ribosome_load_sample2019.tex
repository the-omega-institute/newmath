\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-initiation boundary}
\label{subsec:biorq-q6-rank-admissible-translation-initiation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite-row carrier.}
The record treated here is a finite audit object for the claim id
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_initiation.uorf\_kozak\_ribosome\_load\_sample2019$.
Its carrier consists of residual codon-topology coordinates $q_i$ in the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ family, organism-indexed translation-readout surfaces $T_j$, and a control block $Z$ containing amino-acid composition, organism labels, GC3, coding length, and an $M$-density baseline.  The intended readback is not a protein, structural, functional, or phenotypic interpretation.  It is a per-entry admissibility certificate for a proposed translation-readout survival entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$: after $q_i$ and $T_j$ are residualized against $Z$, the remaining finite design must have enough independent row support and must not be rank-saturated.

The translation-initiation contact attached to this record supplies a two-sample finite panel: $n_{fixed}=41862$ rows, $n_{varying}=16739$ rows, runtime $131.941$ seconds, and a concordance flag equal to true.  These rows support a bounded comparison between fixed and varying translation-initiation contexts, but by themselves they do not certify any $B^{*}_{Q6}$ survival entry.  The admissibility gate asks a different question: whether a proposed residual coordinate-to-readout entry remains falsifiable after the declared controls have removed simpler explanations.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For each candidate pair $(q_i,T_j)$, let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote the residuals after projection away from $Z$.  The entry is eligible for a survival score only when the residual design has nonzero degrees of freedom beyond the fitted columns and is not an interpolation artifact.  In finite-row notation the rule is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}\text{ is reportable}
&\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{rank}([Z,q_i,T_j])>\operatorname{rank}(Z) \\
&\quad\text{and the post-control held-out test is not saturated.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the residualized row space is saturated, an in-sample score of $1.0$ is treated as evidence of overfitting or design degeneracy rather than biological survival.  If the residualized row space is underpowered, the entry is left as needs-data.  If the contact only supplies codon usage, tRNA copy number, or modelled tAI/stAI weights, the entry may describe a bounded readout contact but not measured ribosome survival.

\paragraph{Coordinate evidence available before promotion.}
The linked $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coupling computation used a $61$-codon count space and computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The three loaded organisms were $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$.  The proxy readout was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a direct translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy surface.  Its coupling matrix had nine nonzero entries and maximum absolute proxy coupling $1882464.1106377328$ against threshold $0.01$; the declared break condition, requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, was false.  Thus the finite computation shows proxy contact, not survival.

The residual-basis extraction gives the stronger warning.  With the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length term $DL=\log_2(1+n_{col})$, four organisms were computed and all four were classified as persistent rather than compressible.  The joined-row counts and terminal residual curves were: $danio\_rerio$ with $n=14410$, one candidate, $d_{modeled}=0.874628721049266$, $d_{difference}=0.0$, and $\rho=(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$; $escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655$ with $n=3415$, two candidates, $d_{modeled}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{difference}=0.00017488612263594217$, and $\rho=(1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476)$; $homo\_sapiens$ with $n=16008$, six candidates, $d_{modeled}=0.9020707321191321$, $d_{difference}=0.018824203448813415$, and final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$; and $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$ with $n=3046$, nine candidates, $d_{modeled}=0.2199040339633322$, $d_{difference}=0.008171838236265161$, and final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$.  The minimum final residual ratio across these computed organisms was $0.9723341658092314$, above the stated floor $0.97$.  This is negative information: the available readout blocks did not absorb the residual coordinate energy enough to justify a broad translation-realization claim.

\paragraph{Same-boundary controls.}
The translation-initiation rows are read alongside two additional finite contacts that constrain overinterpretation.  The branchpoint motif usage run used $n=10584$ intron-overlap rows, expression control was enabled, the expression source was UCSC hg19 gtexGeneV8 with log-transformed median tissue expression, and runtime was $138.47155$ seconds.  The mRNA stability contact used a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, with $n_{human}=16500$, $n_{mouse}=18924$, raw $\rho=0.126224$, and runtime $46.349$ seconds.  These controls show that row-level biological panels can be assembled with explicit covariates, but they do not fill the missing $B^{*}_{Q6}$ rank-admissible translation-survival matrix.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
This chapter therefore records a gate rather than a positive survival call.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ may be promoted only if an organism- or CDS-matched measured translation readout is joined to the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, the controls $Z$ are applied, the post-control rank is not saturated, and held-out performance remains positive after simpler-codon-baseline ablation.  The finite evidence currently includes a large two-sample translation-initiation panel, proxy codon-to-tRNA-copy coupling above threshold, and persistent residual curves across four computed organisms.  It does not include a nondegenerate measured ribosome, TE, or protein-output matrix for this exact rank-admissibility boundary.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The allowed conclusion is narrow.  The record supports a rank-audited test specification for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation-readout survival and identifies why saturated or underpowered residual designs must be rejected.  It does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  Each of those claims requires its own layer-matched observational contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.
