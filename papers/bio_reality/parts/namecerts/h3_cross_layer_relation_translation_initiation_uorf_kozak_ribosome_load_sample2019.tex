\subsection{Rank-Admissible Translation-Survival Gate for the uORF--Kozak Ribosome-Load Sample}\label{sec:biori-q6-rank-admissible-uorf-kozak-ribosome-load}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite claim.} This record concerns the finite-row boundary at which a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate may be tested against a translation readout without being promoted by a saturated fit. The carrier is a residualized design matrix whose rows are organism-indexed or transcript-indexed readout observations, whose $Q$-columns contain $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates after amino-acid composition has been quotiented away, and whose control block $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding length, and an $M$-density baseline. For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, the local test is not the existence of a fitted coefficient alone. It is the existence of a non-saturated residual row space in which $q_i$ and the translation readout $T_j$ both retain enough independent support after the controls have been removed.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.} The directly attached translation-initiation run is the uORF--Kozak ribosome-load sample with experiment id $22595b48828f$. It records $41{,}862$ fixed rows and $16{,}739$ varying rows, and the two-sample comparison is concordant. The run time was $131.941$ seconds. These rows support a finite comparison at the translation-initiation readout boundary, but they do not by themselves certify a positive $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival entry. They are used here as a bounded readout surface on which rank admissibility can be asked.

\paragraph{Gate equation.} For each coordinate-readout pair, write $Q_i^\perp$ and $T_j^\perp$ for the residuals after projecting away $Z$. The entry is admissible only when the post-control design has residual rank and held-out degrees of freedom:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i^\perp &= (I-P_Z)Q_i,\\
T_j^\perp &= (I-P_Z)T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{is reportable only if } \operatorname{rank}(Q_i^\perp,T_j^\perp,Z) \text{ is not saturated and the held-out row test is falsifiable.}
\end{aligned}
$$
An all-perfect in-sample surface, or any fitted score whose residual degrees of freedom have collapsed, is therefore read as a projection artifact rather than translation survival. The break condition is local: if the residualized row space is saturated or underpowered, the entry returns to coupling-only or needs-data status even when the fitted score is positive.

\paragraph{Coordinate context.} The related $B^*_{Q6}$ coupling computation loaded three organisms, namely Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. It used a $61$-codon count space and computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the coupling break condition was not met: all nine entries were not below the $0.01$ threshold, and the maximum absolute proxy coupling was $1{,}882{,}464.1106377328$ with nine nonzero entries. This establishes only a finite coupling contact. The same data explicitly stop before ribosome-mediated translation realization because the true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, or translation-efficiency rows required for $S^{QT}$ are not present in that coupling matrix.

\paragraph{Residual-power background.} The residual-basis extraction run shows that the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates retain substantial unexplained signal after available non-translation readouts. Homo sapiens had $16{,}008$ joined rows and six selected candidates, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191321$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.018824203448813415$, and final $\rho=0.9723341658092314$. Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $3{,}046$ joined rows and nine candidates, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$, and final $\rho=0.9798667977250413$. Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 had $3{,}415$ joined rows and two candidates, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.00017488612263594217$, and final $\rho=0.9979478314995476$. Danio rerio had $14{,}410$ joined rows and one candidate, with $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$ and final $\rho=0.9791828971490305$. Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 remain needs-data. The regularizer is stated as $\lambda=0.01$ with description length $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, and the persistent residual label is descriptive rather than mechanistic.

\paragraph{Boundary enforced by this sample.} The uORF--Kozak run supplies a finite translation-initiation comparison, and the surrounding $Q6$ memory supplies codon residuals, organism-scoped codon usage, tRNA-copy, and modeled adaptation-weight contacts. The rank-admissibility rule prevents these surfaces from being read as a positive survival matrix unless a matched measured translation readout is joined to the residual coordinates under the declared controls. In the current finite memory, there is no organism- or CDS-matched non-saturated matrix proving any particular $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$. The correct status is therefore a ready boundary statement: the sample can host the question, and it rejects rank-saturated or underpowered promotion.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite checks.} Two neighboring cross-layer runs show why this gate is necessary. A codon-composition mRNA half-life run recorded $16{,}500$ human rows, $18{,}924$ mouse rows, raw $\rho=0.126224$, a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, and run time $46.349$ seconds. A branchpoint motif usage run recorded $10{,}584$ rows with expression controlled by UCSC hg19 GTEx gene scores and run time $138.47155$ seconds. These measurements demonstrate that finite biological contacts can be joined and controlled, but they are not substitutes for the present translation-survival statistic. mRNA stability, branchpoint usage, proxy adaptation weights, and uORF--Kozak ribosome-load comparison remain separate readout surfaces unless an explicit joined $Q_i \to T_j$ statistic is computed.

\paragraph{Local refusal.} No translation-realization claim follows merely from $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry, codon usage, tRNA gene-copy counts, modeled tAI or stAI weights, proxy coupling, or a concordant two-sample uORF--Kozak comparison. No protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this gate. Each of those assertions would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic. The present chapter records a finite rule for deciding when a proposed translation-survival entry is testable, and it refuses entries whose apparent support is created by saturated rank or missing held-out degrees of freedom.
