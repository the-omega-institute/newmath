\subsection{Rank-admissible translation initiation boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-rank-admissible-uorf-kozak-ribosome-load}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats the proposed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-initiation contact as a finite-row admissibility problem rather than as an immediate biological interpretation.  The carrier is a residualized design matrix whose columns contain synonymous $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, organism-indexed translation readout surfaces, and control columns $Z$.  The tested surface is the entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, where $q_i$ is a residual codon-topology coordinate and $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout.  The local rule is that $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is not a survival entry merely because an in-sample coupling or fitted score is positive.  It is admissible only when the post-control residual row space has enough rank to leave a falsifiable held-out residual.

For the translation-initiation row set, the finite readout reports $n_{\mathrm{fixed}}=41862$ and $n_{\mathrm{varying}}=16739$, with the two-sample concordance flag equal to true and runtime $131.941$ seconds.  This is a substantial finite surface for the uORF/Kozak/ribosome-load packet, but the present certificate uses it as a boundary surface: it can support a rank audit of the translation-readout interface, not a direct claim about protein output, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Admissibility equation.}
Let $Q_i^{\perp Z}$ and $T_j^{\perp Z}$ denote the residuals after projecting $q_i$ and $T_j$ against $Z$, where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, sequence length, and the declared M-density baseline.  The entry is eligible for survival testing only if the residual design has non-saturated rank and a remaining held-out degree of freedom:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i^{\perp Z} &= (I-P_Z)q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= (I-P_Z)T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} \text{ is admissible} &\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{rank}([Z,Q_i,T_j])>\operatorname{rank}(Z)\text{ and the fitted surface is not saturated.}
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus an all-perfect in-sample fit is treated as a warning sign when it is produced by a saturated row space.  The finite certificate distinguishes three cases: a rank-admissible translation-survival entry, a coupling-only contact, and a rank-saturated projection artifact.  Only the first case may be reported as $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$.

\paragraph{Coordinate evidence held below survival.}
The linked $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coupling computation loaded three cross-organism rows, namely homo sapiens, saccharomyces cerevisiae, and escherichia coli K12.  It computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{AUA}$, $K_{AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{CUN\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{UUA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{UCR\,\mathrm{vs}\,AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{ACR\,\mathrm{vs}\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The codon count space was $61$, the proxy readout was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, the coupling threshold was $0.01$, and the largest absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$ across nine nonzero entries.  The break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met.

This is contact at the level of codon usage and modeled translation-adaptation readback, not a translation-survival result.  The distinction matters because $A^{QT}$ is a coupling surface, while $S^{QT}$ is a controlled survival surface.  A positive or large proxy $A_{ij}$ does not become $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$ unless the same entry survives the residualized rank gate and held-out translation-readout test.

\paragraph{Residual power and negative readback.}
The residual-basis computation gives the relevant scale of the unfinished problem.  With the stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ and description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, four organisms were computed and two remained data-limited.  The computed rows were persistent rather than compressible: danio rerio had one candidate and $\rho=(1.0,0.9791828971490305)$; escherichia coli K12 MG1655 had two candidates and $\rho=(1.0,0.9982293045445091,0.9979478314995476)$; homo sapiens had six candidates and $\rho=(1.0,0.9852198225375993,0.9806696939851618,0.9792537895870558,0.9787609432955213,0.9787233550315274,0.9723341658092314)$; saccharomyces cerevisiae had nine candidates and $\rho=(1.0,0.9962498989663637,0.9929843170647626,0.9915730985485388,0.9903314906684438,0.9894054695002641,0.9885902293718664,0.9882386501212531,0.9881524582851271,0.9798667977250413)$.  The final residual floor was $0.9723341658092314$, with zero compressible organisms and four persistent organisms.  Gallus gallus and mycobacterium smegmatis strain MC2 155 remained in the needs-data class.

The dof-matched null checks reinforce the boundary.  In homo sapiens, the selected blocks included ptm-density, complex membership, tm-count, measured tRNA-tAI, domain count, and localization, with increments $0.014780177462400674$, $0.004618389163869967$, $0.0014438137599134737$, $0.0005032876020244136$, $0.00003840392718095609$, and $0.006528084968494762$ against their corresponding deterministic Gaussian null cutoffs.  In saccharomyces cerevisiae, the selected blocks included measured TE, ptm-density, tm-count, turnover, complex membership, measured tRNA-tAI, domain count, mRNA, and localization, ending with a localization increment $0.008385002223710514$ above a null p95 of $0.00708487840939486$.  These rows show finite residual structure, but they also show that the remaining residual mass is large.  The chapter therefore reads the packet as a rank and boundary certificate, not as a completed translation-realization theorem.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite contacts.}
Two linked cross-layer contacts provide scale without changing the verdict.  The branchpoint motif usage contact used $n=10584$ rows, was expression-controlled, and ran in $138.47155$ seconds.  The mRNA-stability contact used a non-CSC tAI circularity guard, with $n_{\mathrm{human}}=16500$, $n_{\mathrm{mouse}}=18924$, raw $\rho=0.126224$, and runtime $46.349$ seconds.  These contacts confirm that finite biological panels can be joined and audited, but they are not the same surface as a rank-admissible $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival entry.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.}
The local refusal is part of the mathematical content.  If the residualized row space is saturated, underpowered, or lacks a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout after controls, the entry remains coupling-only or needs-data.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights, tRNA gene-copy summaries, codon-usage tables, and the uORF/Kozak finite sample can help define the boundary, but they do not by themselves establish ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The readback of this certificate is therefore per-entry and negative-by-default: for each proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$, report the residualized rank, the degrees of freedom left after $Z$, whether the translation readout is held out rather than fitted away, and whether saturation forces refusal.  The verified uORF/Kozak/ribosome-load row counts make the packet concrete, while the rank gate prevents an in-sample or modeled readout from being promoted beyond what the finite matrix can actually test.
