\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for the uORF--Kozak ribosome-load surface}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translation-initiation-uorf-kozak-ribosome-load-sample2019}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record isolates a rank gate for deciding when a synonymous residual coordinate may be tested against a translation-layer readout.  The carrier is a finite residualized design matrix whose rows are organism- or coding-sequence-indexed observations, whose BEDC-side columns are the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, and whose biological readout columns are translation-boundary surfaces.  The control block $Z$ contains the variables needed to keep codon topology from being mistaken for translation realization: amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding length, and an $M$-density baseline.  The finite row contact attached to the uORF--Kozak ribosome-load packet reports $n_{\mathrm{fixed}}=41862$ fixed rows and $n_{\mathrm{varying}}=16739$ varying rows, with a two-sample concordance flag equal to true and a recorded runtime of $131.941$ seconds.  These numbers do not themselves certify $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ survival; they certify that the chapter is working with a nonempty finite translation-initiation readout surface rather than a purely formal symbol.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate inventory.}
The synonymous residual coordinate list used by the surrounding $Q6$ packet is explicit: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$.  The codon count space has size $61$.  A proxy coupling computation over Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K-12 found nine nonzero entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $1882464.1106377328$.  The declared break condition for that proxy packet was: return null if all nine $q_i$ have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout.  It was not met.  This is useful only as contact pressure.  It remains an $A^{QT}$ coupling surface, not a survival surface, because the proxy readout is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum rather than a matched ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency measurement.

\paragraph{Gate equation.}
For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, let $q_i^{\perp Z}$ be the residual of coordinate $q_i$ after projecting away $Z$, and let $T_j^{\perp Z}$ be the corresponding residualized translation readout.  The gate requires the residualized design to keep independent degrees of freedom after the controls have been removed:
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_{ij}^{\perp Z} &= \bigl[q_i^{\perp Z}, T_j^{\perp Z}\bigr],\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}^{\perp Z}) &< n_{ij}^{\perp Z},\\
\mathrm{df}_{ij} &= n_{ij}^{\perp Z}-\operatorname{rank}(Z)-\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}^{\perp Z})>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Only after this rank test can the entry ask whether $q_i$ improves a held-out translation readout beyond $Z$ and simpler codon baselines.  If the residualized row space is saturated, or if the degrees of freedom vanish, the readback is \emph{needs data} or coupling-only, even when an in-sample fit is positive or exactly $1.0$.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The admissible statement is therefore a gate, not a positive survival theorem.  A proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry may be reported only when the residualized design has non-saturated rank, positive degrees of freedom after $Z$, and a held-out translation readout whose improvement remains after simpler baselines.  The available finite facts give a large uORF--Kozak readout surface, a nine-coordinate $Q6$ residual inventory, a nonzero proxy coupling matrix, and persistent residual curves across four computed organisms.  They do not yet give an organism- or CDS-matched measured ribosome-load, translation-efficiency, or protein-output matrix that would turn any $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate into a surviving translation entry.  Consequently this chapter refuses the following promotions: no translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from the rank gate, proxy coupling, tAI or stAI weights, codon usage rows, or review eligibility.  Each such statement would require its own matched biological contact and its own controlled finite statistic.
