\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-initiation boundary for uORF, Kozak, and ribosome-load rows}\label{sec:bior reality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-initiation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record concerns a finite cross-layer test surface whose biological side is translation initiation and whose coordinate side is the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual panel.  The carrier is not a protein, a fold, a pathway, or a phenotype.  It is a residualized design matrix with rows indexed by organisms or coding-sequence entries, columns for the candidate $q_i$ coordinates, translation-readout columns $T_j$, and control columns $Z$.  In this chapter the relevant biological readout family is the uORF--Kozak--ribosome-load sample associated with the translation-initiation packet.  The finite run reports $41862$ fixed rows and $16739$ varying rows, with the two-sample concordance flag equal to true and runtime $131.941$ seconds.  Those numbers support a row-level contact with translation-initiation variation, but they do not by themselves certify any $S^{QT}_{ij}$ survival entry.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The candidate coordinate list is the computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual set
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.  The accompanying finite coupling computation loaded three organism rows, namely human, budding yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K12, in the $61$-codon count space.  Its proxy readout was a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a direct ribosome-occupancy measurement.  The coupling threshold was $0.01$; the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met, and the maximum absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$ across nine nonzero entries.  This establishes only that the residual coordinates touch a bounded translation-weight proxy.  It is deliberately weaker than a survived translation-readout claim.

\paragraph{Admissibility statistic.}
For each proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ the local test first residualizes both $q_i$ and $T_j$ against $Z$, where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and M-density, with matched mRNA or simpler codon baselines included when the readout requires them.  The entry is admissible only if the residual row space has enough rank left after controls to support held-out testing.  The intended finite decision can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i^{\perp Z} &= q_i-\Pi_Z q_i,\\
T_j^{\perp Z} &= T_j-\Pi_Z T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0\quad\hbox{only if}\quad \operatorname{rank}(q_i^{\perp Z},T_j^{\perp Z})<n_Z\ \hbox{and held-out loss improves beyond the baseline.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the post-control fit is saturated, the local verdict is not positive survival even when the in-sample score is positive or equal to $1.0$.  The proper readback is then coupling-only or needs-data, depending on whether the finite rows merely touch a proxy readout or fail to supply enough independent support.

These numbers argue against collapsing the translation-initiation contact into a universal rule: the residual coordinates remain only partially explained by the available readout blocks.

\paragraph{Translation and downstream non-promotion.}
The execution-selection decomposition gives an additional caution.  In budding yeast the active gene set had $n=5684$, base $R^2_{QP}=0.30732228260918726$, full-gene-set base $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $\|P_Q\|^2=864.0124192674945$, and residual norm squared $2811.4213259512776$, yet the verdict was composition\_artifact.  That finite result shows why a direct residual-to-protein association is not enough for this chapter.  A same-coordinate $q_i\to T_j$ step must first survive under the declared controls before any downstream protein-abundance mediation can even be considered.  Likewise, the mRNA half-life contact reports human $n=16500$, mouse $n=18924$, and raw $\rho=0.126224$ under a non-CSC tAI guard; this is an RNA-stability surface, not a translation-initiation survival result.  The branchpoint usage run reports $n=10584$ with expression control enabled; this supplies a separate cross-layer finite contact, not evidence for ribosome loading here.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter therefore records a rank-admissibility gate rather than a positive biological law.  The uORF--Kozak--ribosome-load sample supplies a concrete translation-initiation row surface with $41862$ fixed rows, $16739$ varying rows, and concordant two-sample behavior.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual list and the three-organism proxy coupling matrix show finite contact with codon-usage and tRNA-weight readbacks.  The survival status of any particular $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry remains conditional on a non-saturated residual rank and a held-out, $Z$-controlled improvement.  No translation realization beyond that bounded statistic follows here.  No ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies the corresponding finite rows and the controlled statistic is recomputed at that layer.
