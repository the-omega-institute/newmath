\subsection{Rank-admissible translation-survival gate for synonymous residual coordinates}\label{sec:biorality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-survival-gate}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.} The record concerns a finite cross-layer gate, not a positive biological promotion. Its carrier is a residualized design table whose rows are organism- or transcript-indexed observations and whose columns contain $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, translation readout surfaces, and controls $Z$. The controlled covariates are amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline. The gate asks whether a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is statistically admissible after $q_i$ and $T_j$ have both been residualized against $Z$. If the residual row space is saturated, or if the remaining degrees of freedom cannot support held-out falsification, the entry is not called translation survival even when an in-sample regression score is positive or exactly $1.0$.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.} The translation-initiation contact attached to this record contains two finite row classes from the uORF/Kozak/ribosome-load sample: $41{,}862$ fixed rows and $16{,}739$ varying rows. The two-sample check is concordant, and the recorded runtime is $131.941$ seconds. These numbers support a finite translation-readout boundary for the packet, because the sample has a nontrivial split between fixed and varying initiation contexts. They do not, by themselves, certify any $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate as surviving into translation realization, since the record does not provide a same-row residualized $S^{QT}$ matrix for the exact synonymous residual coordinates under the full control set.

\paragraph{Residual coordinate support.} The associated synonymous residual calculation uses a $61$-codon count space and records nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. A proxy coupling matrix was computed across $homo\_sapiens$, $saccharomyces\_cerevisiae$, and $escherichia\_coli\_k12$. Its declared threshold is $0.01$, the break condition requiring all nine coordinates to have $|A_{ij}| < 0.01$ is not met, and the maximum absolute proxy entry is $1{,}882{,}464.1106377328$ with nine nonzero entries. This is evidence for coupling against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, but the contact itself records that true tAI, stAI, ribosome profiling, and direct translation readouts are absent from that proxy computation.

\paragraph{Rank gate.} The admissibility rule can be written locally as follows. Let $Q_i^{\perp Z}$ be the residual of the synonymous coordinate $q_i$ after projection away from $Z$, and let $T_j^{\perp Z}$ be the corresponding residual translation readout. The entry may be tested only when the residual design has unsaturated rank and enough held-out support:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}\;\hbox{is admissible}
&\Longrightarrow \operatorname{rank}(X^{\perp Z}_{ij}) < n_{ij}^{\perp Z},\\
&\Longrightarrow df_{ij}=n_{ij}^{\perp Z}-\operatorname{rank}(X^{\perp Z}_{ij})>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
If either inequality fails, the correct output is a refusal of survival status, not a large effect. The all-fitted or saturated case is therefore interpreted as a projection artifact. The point of the gate is to separate three possibilities that would otherwise be conflated: a controlled survival contact, a coupling-only contact, and a rank-saturated surface that cannot be falsified on held-out rows.

\paragraph{Local verdict.} This chapter therefore records a ready boundary result: the finite data are sufficient to state and audit the rank-admissibility rule for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation survival, and they are sufficient to reject promotion from proxy coupling or saturated in-sample fit to survival. The chapter does not assert a positive $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry. It states that a positive entry requires a non-saturated residualized design, positive degrees of freedom after $Z$, and held-out translation-readout support. No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the rows above unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the required measurement and the controlled statistic is recomputed on that finite panel.
