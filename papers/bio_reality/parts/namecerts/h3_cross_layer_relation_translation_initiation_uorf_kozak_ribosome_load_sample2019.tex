\subsection{Rank-admissible boundary for translation-initiation survival}\label{sec:biorank-uorf-kozak-ribosome-load-sample2019}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite subject.}
This record concerns the proposed promotion of synonymous residual coordinates in $B^{*}_{Q6}$ from codon-topology descriptors to a translation-layer survival statement. The finite carrier is a residualized design matrix whose rows are organism- or coding-sequence-indexed observations and whose columns include the selected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates, translation-readout surfaces, and controls $Z$. The controls named by the packet are amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding length, and $M$-density. The admissibility question is not whether a fitted score can be made large in sample. It is whether a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ remains testable after $q_i$ and $T_j$ have both been residualized against $Z$, with enough remaining rank to make held-out failure possible.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The directly attached translation-initiation contact reports a Sample2019 two-sample check with $n_{\mathrm{fixed}}=41862$ and $n_{\mathrm{varying}}=16739$. The two samples are concordant, and the recorded runtime is $131.941$ seconds. This is a finite-row translation-initiation surface, not a proof that any $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate has survived as a translation mechanism. The row counts are large enough to be useful for a boundary audit, but the memory supplied here does not include a same-row table containing residualized $q_i$, a specific translation readout $T_j$, the full control matrix $Z$, and the held-out statistic for this exact $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry. The chapter therefore records a rank gate and a refusal rule rather than a positive translation-survival call.

\paragraph{Admissibility rule.}
For a candidate coordinate $q_i$ and readout $T_j$, let $\widetilde q_i$ and $\widetilde T_j$ denote residuals after projection away from the control columns $Z$. The entry is admissible only when the post-control design has non-saturated rank and positive held-out degrees of freedom. In the notation of this record, the gate can be written as
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_{ij} &= [\widetilde q_i, Z, \widetilde T_j],\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}) &< n_{ij},\\
\operatorname{df}_{ij} &= n_{ij}-\operatorname{rank}(X_{ij}) > 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
A reported in-sample fit with $R^2=1.0$, or any other saturated score, is therefore a failure of the survival test rather than evidence for survival. The support condition is stricter: the held-out statistic must remain positive after the declared controls and after simpler codon-baseline ablation. If these rank and held-out requirements are not met, the entry remains coupling-only or needs more rows, even when the in-sample association is visually strong.

\paragraph{Coupling is not survival.}
A linked codon-usage computation loaded three organisms, namely $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$, and computed nine synonymous residual coordinates: $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $K\_AAA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. Its proxy readout was the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum. The coupling threshold was $0.01$, the break condition requiring all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ was not met, and the largest recorded proxy magnitude was $1882464.1106377328$ with nine nonzero entries above threshold. This establishes only that the proxy coupling matrix is nontrivial on the finite three-organism surface. It does not by itself establish $S^{QT}_{ij}>0$, because the proxy is not a matched ribosome, translation-efficiency, or held-out translation-readout survival matrix under the stated controls.

The broader residual-basis audit also shows why this boundary is necessary. In the organisms with sufficient local readout data, the residual extraction used a stated regularizer $\lambda=0.01$ with $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, and its computed organisms remained persistent rather than compressible. The final residual ratios included $0.9791828971490305$ for $\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}$, $0.9979478314995476$ for $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}$, $0.9723341658092314$ for $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, and $0.9798667977250413$ for $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$. These numbers support the local lesson: the residual coordinates carry unresolved structure, but unresolved structure is not the same object as a translation-survival certificate.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The growth-scaling contact gives a separate finite check over $20$ requested organisms, with $20$ strengths computed, $20$ organisms having doubling-time rows, and $17$ organisms having complete strength, doubling, $GC3$, and tRNA information. Its primary strength field is held-out $R^2$ after residualizing the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous codon coordinates and log$_{10}$ protein abundance over length, $20$ amino-acid composition controls, $GC3$, and then fitting fold-local slopes. This is useful evidence that held-out residual statistics can be formed on finite biological panels. It is not a substitute for the specific $q_i\to T_j$ rank-admissible translation-initiation statistic required here.

\paragraph{Local decision.}
The finite Sample2019 surface supplies $41862$ fixed rows and $16739$ varying rows with concordance, so the chapter is not empty and the gate is meaningful. The positive statement is only that an admissibility protocol is defined for each proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry: residualize against $Z$, compute post-control rank and degrees of freedom, reject saturated fits, and demand a held-out readout boundary before promotion. The current memory does not provide the actual non-saturated held-out $S^{QT}_{ij}$ table for a named coordinate-readout pair. Consequently the scientifically correct conclusion is boundary readiness, not translation survival.

\paragraph{What is not inferred.}
No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this record alone. Those statements require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics. In particular, a proxy $A^{QT}$ coupling, a modeled tAI or stAI contact, a concordant two-sample initiation surface, or an all-perfect in-sample fit cannot be promoted to survival unless the same-row controlled and held-out rank test succeeds.
