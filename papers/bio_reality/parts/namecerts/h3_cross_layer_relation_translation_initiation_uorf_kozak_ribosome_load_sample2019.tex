\subsection{Rank-admissible translation boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-rank-admissible-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite question.}
This record asks when a synonymous residual coordinate in $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ may be treated as surviving into a translation readout, rather than merely touching a translation-shaped proxy.  The carrier is a finite residualized design matrix with rows indexed by organism or matched coding-sequence units, columns $q_i$ for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, translation readout columns $T_j$, and controls $Z$ for amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding length, and $M$-density baseline.  The intended entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$ is not a fitted correlation.  It is an admissibility-certified survival entry: both $q_i$ and $T_j$ must be residualized against $Z$, the remaining row space must have non-saturated rank, and there must be enough independent support to make a held-out failure observable.

\paragraph{Rank gate.}
For each proposed pair $(q_i,T_j)$, the finite gate is the following residual-rank test:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde q_i &= (I-P_Z)q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= (I-P_Z)T_j,\\
S^{QT}_{ij} &> 0 \quad \text{is reportable only if the residual design is non-saturated and held-out-testable.}
\end{aligned}
$$
If the post-control design has rank equal to the available degrees of freedom, an in-sample score of $1.0$ is read as a projection artifact rather than translation survival.  Likewise, a positive coupling coefficient $A^{QT}_{ij}$, a modeled tAI or stAI contact, or a fitted surface with no independent residual degrees of freedom remains below the survival threshold.  The gate therefore separates three finite cases: rank-admissible translation-survival entry, coupling-only contact, and rank-saturated refusal.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
The chapter therefore assigns the rank-admissible translation gate, not a positive biological law.  A proposed entry may be reported only when the same finite panel contains $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a translation readout, and the declared controls, with residual rank left over after $Z$ and with a held-out test that could fail.  The finite rule is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{report}(S^{QT}_{ij}>0) &\Rightarrow \mathrm{rank\ admissible}\wedge \mathrm{held\ out\ falsifiable},\\
\mathrm{rank\ saturated} &\Rightarrow \mathrm{refuse\ survival},\\
A^{QT}_{ij}\ne 0 &\not\Rightarrow S^{QT}_{ij}>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The current evidence supports the existence of bounded coordinate, proxy-coupling, residual-extraction, and translation-initiation row surfaces.  It does not yet supply the organism- or CDS-matched measured translation rows needed to certify a non-saturated $S^{QT}$ entry for this exact subject.

No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation claim, causation claim, or universal biological law follows from this gate.  Each such statement would need its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic after the translation-readout boundary has first survived the rank-admissibility test.
