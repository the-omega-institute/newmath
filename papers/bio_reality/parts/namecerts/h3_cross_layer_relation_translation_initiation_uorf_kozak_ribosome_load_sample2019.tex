\subsection{Rank-admissible translation initiation contact for the Q6 residual surface}\label{sec:biorx-q6-rank-admissible-translation-initiation}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.} This record isolates a narrow translation-initiation boundary for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  The carrier is not a protein-level or phenotype-level object.  It is a finite residualized design surface whose rows are organism- or CDS-indexed observations, whose coordinate columns are $q_i \in B^{\ast}_{Q6}$, whose readout columns are translation-facing quantities $T_j$, and whose controls $Z$ include amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the declared M-density baseline.  The sample2019 initiation contact supplies a concrete finite-row check with $n_{\mathrm{fixed}}=41862$ fixed-context rows and $n_{\mathrm{varying}}=16739$ varying-context rows.  The two-sample readback is concordant, and the recorded run time is $131.941$ seconds.  These numbers certify that the initiation packet has a real finite row surface, but they do not by themselves certify a surviving $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation entry.

\paragraph{Rank gate.} For a proposed entry $S^{QT}_{ij}$, the admissibility question is whether the residualized coordinate and readout still leave a falsifiable row space after the controls are removed.  With $Q_i$ denoting the $i$th synonymous residual coordinate, $T_j$ a translation readout, and $P_Z$ the projection onto the declared controls, the controlled objects are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde Q_i &= (I-P_Z)Q_i,\\
\widetilde T_j &= (I-P_Z)T_j.
\end{aligned}
$$
The entry is eligible for survival testing only when the controlled design has non-saturated rank and positive residual degrees of freedom.  In the finite-row terms of this chapter, the gate refuses promotion whenever the fitted surface can be explained as a rank artifact, an all-perfect in-sample fit, or a coupling-only contact without held-out translation readout.  The intended support condition is therefore not merely $A^{QT}_{ij}\neq 0$; it is a controlled, held-out improvement for $T_j$ after $Z$ and simpler codon baselines have been accounted for.

\paragraph{Observed initiation rows.} The uORF/Kozak ribosome-load contact contributes the row scale needed for a translation-initiation readout boundary: $41862$ fixed rows and $16739$ varying rows were evaluated, and the two-sample direction was concordant.  This is enough to state a row-level initiation contact, because the finite surface has both a fixed arm and a varying arm rather than a single unopposed aggregate.  The chapter uses that fact only at the boundary where translation initiation can be asked about.  It does not treat the concordance flag as proof that any particular $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate survives residualization.

\paragraph{Coupling evidence kept below survival.} The supplied Q6 coupling computation has a different status.  It used a $61$-codon count space and loaded three organisms: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12.  Nine synonymous residual coordinates were computed: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f_3$-stress.  Against the proxy tRNA-Leu CUN-vs-UUR log-copy spectrum, the threshold was $0.01$, all nine entries exceeded the all-small null, and the largest recorded absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.  Thus the proxy matrix is not empty: its break condition, namely all nine $|A_{ij}|<0.01$, is false.  But the proxy is explicitly a codon-count and tRNA-copy contact, not a measured ribosome-load or translation-efficiency survival statistic.

\paragraph{Residual basis context.} The broader residual-basis extraction also supports the need for a rank audit.  Four organisms have computed residual-basis surfaces, and two named organisms are marked needs-data.  Homo sapiens has $16008$ joined common rows, $6$ selected candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9020707321191321$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.018824203448813415$, and a residual curve ending at $0.9723341658092314$.  Saccharomyces cerevisiae has $3046$ joined common rows, $9$ candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.2199040339633322$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.008171838236265161$, and a curve ending at $0.9798667977250413$.  Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 has $3415$ joined common rows, $2$ candidates, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.6628211177352172$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.00017488612263594217$, and a curve ending at $0.9979478314995476$.  Danio rerio has $14410$ joined common rows, $1$ candidate, $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.874628721049266$, $d_{\mathrm{difference}}=0.0$, and a curve ending at $0.9791828971490305$.  Gallus gallus and Mycobacterium smegmatis str. mc2 155 remain needs-data.  The extraction used the stated regularization scale $\lambda=0.01$ with description-length term $\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, and the computed residuals are classified as persistent rather than compressible.

\paragraph{Admissibility decision.} The finite-row readback is therefore a gated statement.  The sample2019 initiation rows make a translation-facing contact available at finite row scale, and the Q6 residual system has nonempty proxy coupling and persistent residual structure.  The admissible claim is only that a proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry must pass a rank and degree-of-freedom test before it can be called translation survival.  If the controlled row space is saturated, if the degrees of freedom after $Z$ are insufficient, or if the only positive number is an in-sample proxy coupling, the entry is reported as coupling-only or needs-data.  In formula form, the local rule is
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QT}_{ij}>0 \quad &\text{is reportable only after non-saturated controlled rank,}\\
&\text{positive residual degrees of freedom, and held-out translation readout support;}\\
S^{QT}_{ij} \quad &\text{is refused when those conditions collapse to a saturated projection.}
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.} Nothing in this chapter asserts that a Q6 residual coordinate causes translation, fixes ribosome occupancy, predicts protein abundance, determines folding, establishes physical admissibility, realizes molecular function, explains a mechanism, or states a universal biological law.  Those are different biological layers.  A separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic would be required for each such assertion.  The present record stops at the translation-readout boundary: it preserves the finite row counts, the proxy coupling facts, the persistent residual-basis measurements, and the rank-admissibility refusal condition for any proposed $S^{QT}_{ij}$ entry.
