\subsection{miRNA seed repression and the RNA-half-life boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite rows.}
The record concerns a finite panel whose admissible row is a matched coding-RNA or transcript-window observation with a synonymous coordinate label, a same-row translation readout when translation is discussed, an RNA half-life or abundance readout, local composition controls, transcript abundance, batch, isoform state, and an explicitly declared perturbation or break condition.  The empirical contact used for the named miRNA-seed screen is the cross-dataset miRNA target-repression statistic indexed by
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mirna\_target\_repression.mirna\_seed\_crossdataset\_agarwal2015$.  Its verified finite run has $n=55170$ joined rows, aggregate per-miRNA partial Spearman median $\rho=-0.224966$, and $7$ replicated gradients, with runtime $400.408$ seconds.  The sign is consistent with seed-associated repression at the bounded transcript-regulation surface: rows carrying stronger target-seed signal tend, after the declared partialing, toward lower target expression or stability readback.

\paragraph{Statistic actually licensed.}
The chapter does not identify the miRNA seed statistic with translation, protein output, or a decay-factor RBP mechanism.  The admissible mathematical content is the following finite-row implication: if the row set $R$ is the joined miRNA-seed panel and $Y$ is the target repression readout after the specified covariates are partialed, then the observed screen records a negative rank association rather than a mechanistic route:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|R| &= 55170,\\
\operatorname{median}_{m\in \mathrm{miRNA}}\,\rho_{\partial}(\mathrm{seed}_{m},Y) &= -0.224966,\\
\#\{\mathrm{replicated\ gradients}\} &= 7.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough to place the row family on the cross-layer relation boundary between sequence-local regulatory labels and transcript-level repression readbacks.  It is not enough to say that a synonymous coding coordinate has changed translation, that a protein product has changed, or that an RNA decay pathway has been isolated.

\paragraph{Comparison with half-life controls.}
The same paper treats RNA half-life contacts as separate finite surfaces, not as automatic consequences of miRNA seed repression.  A matched m6A half-life boundary run on $5837$ genes recorded static Spearman $\rho=-0.388929$ between m6A load and DMSO log half-life, causal Spearman $\rho=0.245804$ between m6A-load change and log half-life change, length-partial Spearman $\rho=0.227683$, and two-sided partial-permutation value $p=0.000999001$.  A distinct endogenous 3'UTR ARE transfer run used $14933$ genes and $2000$ permutations, with baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, ARE coefficient $\beta=-0.0607953$, and permutation value $p=0.00049975$.  These two auxiliary screens show that transcript half-life boundaries can be numerically contacted by finite rows, but they do not supply the missing same-window decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, rescue, or binding-defective controls for the miRNA-seed panel.

\paragraph{RBP bridge refusal.}
A stronger claim would require every relevant row to carry a measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, a coordinate-versus-occupancy decoupling condition, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, local RNA-structure and motif-density controls, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the same-row translation-readout boundary.  Those rows are not present in the verified miRNA-seed screen summarized here.  Therefore an RNA half-life difference, even when numerically associated with a local sequence label, is not promoted to an RBP-decay mechanism.  If RBP occupancy does not move with the coordinate, if loss of the nominated RBP does not collapse the effect, if wild-type rescue does not restore it, or if a binding-defective or non-target control reproduces it, the mechanism assertion is rejected.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The accepted statement is deliberately narrow.  The finite miRNA-seed run supports a bounded negative repression association over $55170$ rows and $7$ replicated gradients.  It does not by itself establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, an RNA-decay mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Any one of those stronger readings would need its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic on the same rows, rather than being inferred from seed targeting, transcript abundance, half-life association, or codon/window geometry.
