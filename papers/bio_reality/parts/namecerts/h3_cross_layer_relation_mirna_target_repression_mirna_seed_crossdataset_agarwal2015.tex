\subsection{A finite miRNA seed-repression row with a translation and RNA-stability boundary}\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row.} The BioReality record considered here is the finite cross-dataset miRNA seed-repression claim indexed by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mirna\_target\_repression.mirna\_seed\_crossdataset\_agarwal2015}. Its observed table contains $55{,}170$ joined target rows. For each row the admissible information is a finite record of miRNA identity, seed-target coordinate, transcript or target identifier, target abundance or repression readout, available matched covariates, and the declared dataset provenance. The aggregate statistic reported by the audit is a median per-miRNA partial Spearman association
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \widetilde{\rho}_{\mathrm{partial}} &= -0.224966,
  & n &= 55{,}170,
  & g &= 7,
  & t_{\mathrm{run}} &= 400.408\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $g=7$ records the number of replicated gradient directions in the finite check. The sign is biologically oriented in the expected repression direction: higher seed-target signal is associated with lower target readout after the declared partialing step. The chapter therefore records a bounded miRNA-target repression surface, not an unrestricted mechanistic account of all post-transcriptional regulation.

\paragraph{Admissible interpretation.} The row licenses only the following scientific sentence: in this finite joined panel, miRNA seed-target information retains a negative partial association with the target readout, with median partial rank correlation $-0.224966$ across the audited miRNA aggregation and $55{,}170$ rows entering the computation. This is a cross-layer relation because a small RNA targeting coordinate is being compared against a transcript-level readout rather than against another purely symbolic coordinate. It is not promoted to translation realization, protein output, folded-state change, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal law. Those claims would require separate layer-matched contacts: ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency for translation, proteomics for protein abundance, structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility, perturbation-rescue experiments for mechanism, and phenotype assays for organismal effect.

\paragraph{Boundary against the stronger RBP-decay bridge.} The surrounding finite description also names a stronger possible bridge involving synonymous coding-window labels, RNA half-life, measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, isoform state, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, abundance, batch, and a same-row translation boundary. The Agarwal miRNA seed row does not by itself contain that full panel. In particular, the finite numbers above do not include measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP perturbation, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, or non-target rescue. Consequently, the present chapter cannot state an RBP-mediated RNA half-life mechanism. It can only state the observed miRNA seed-repression association under the declared finite controls.

\paragraph{Comparison rows inside the same audit bundle.} The scope boundary is reinforced by independent RNA-stability checks in the same finite audit payload. A separate m$^{6}$A half-life boundary row reports $5{,}837$ genes, static Spearman association $-0.388929$ between m$^{6}$A load and DMSO log$_2$ half-life, causal Spearman association $0.245804$ for m$^{6}$A load against the treatment half-life change, length-partial Spearman association $0.227683$, and two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$. Another endogenous ARE transfer row reports $14{,}933$ genes, baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, ARE coefficient $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, $2{,}000$ permutations, and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$. These comparison rows show that RNA-stability contacts can be numerically separated from the miRNA seed-repression row, but they do not fill in the missing RBP occupancy and perturbation-rescue requirements for this chapter. Their role here is diagnostic: each finite statistic remains attached to the readout it actually measured.

\paragraph{Local refusal rule.} The chapter accepts the finite negative miRNA seed-target association and refuses every stronger reading not carried by the rows. If the seed coordinate were exchangeable after the stated controls, the coordinate would remain only a descriptor. If an RNA half-life difference were present without measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls, it would remain an RNA-stability association rather than an RBP bridge. If translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or universality is desired, the corresponding finite contact must be attached and evaluated on its own rows before any such statement is made.
