\subsection{miRNA seed repression as a bounded cross-dataset RNA-output relation}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns the BioReality claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mirna\_target\_repression.mirna\_seed\_crossdataset\_agarwal2015}.  The finite object is a matched table of coding or transcript-associated rows in which a declared miRNA seed-coordinate label is compared with an RNA-output readout after nuisance structure has been separated.  The admissible row includes the seed-match or coordinate class, the transcript or window identifier, the same-row RNA abundance or stability readout used by the source statistic, local sequence and composition covariates, transcript identity, batch or dataset membership, and the declared control variables used in the partial association.  The row is not a protein row, not a folded-structure row, not a phenotype row, and not a causal perturbation row unless those measurements are separately present in the same finite panel.

\paragraph{Observed scalar contact.}
The verified cross-dataset miRNA seed run used $n=55170$ finite rows and reported an aggregate per-miRNA partial Spearman median
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{median}} &= -0.224966 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is the expected repression direction for a seed-associated target readout: larger seed-target burden is associated with lower RNA output after the stated partial controls.  The same run records a replicated gradient count of $7$ and an execution time of $400.408$ seconds under experiment run \texttt{50f22b191476}, verified at \texttt{2026-06-27T05:12:32+00:00}.  These numbers license only the finite association that was actually computed: a bounded seed-to-RNA-output relation across the joined rows.

\paragraph{Local mathematical form.}
Let $i$ range over the $55170$ retained rows.  Write $s_i$ for the finite seed-coordinate or target-burden label, $y_i$ for the RNA-output readout used by the statistic, and $z_i$ for the matched nuisance vector carrying transcript identity, abundance or stability controls, local composition, batch, and other declared covariates.  The recorded contact is the sign and magnitude of a controlled rank relation, schematically
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{partial}}(s,y\mid z) &< 0,\\
\mathrm{median}_{\mathrm{miRNA}}\bigl(\rho_{\mathrm{partial}}(s,y\mid z)\bigr) &= -0.224966,\\
\#\{\mathrm{replicated\ gradients}\} &= 7 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The formula is deliberately read as a finite-row statistic rather than as a universal law.  It states that the joined rows preserve a negative seed-associated RNA-output gradient after the stated controls.  It does not state that the seed coordinate alone explains the whole transcript state, and it does not remove the need for row-level checks against transcript abundance, isoform state, sequence composition, local structure, and dataset membership.

\paragraph{Relation to RNA-stability boundaries.}
The miRNA seed result sits in the same empirical family as other bounded RNA-output contacts, but it is not interchangeable with them.  A separate m6A site-load half-life run retained $5837$ genes and reported a static Spearman value of $-0.388929$ for m6A load against DMSO log half-life, a causal Spearman value of $0.245804$ for m6A load against $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, a length-partial Spearman value of $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial permutation value $p=0.000999001$.  A separate endogenous 3' UTR ARE transfer run retained $14933$ genes, reported $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, and $p=0.00049975$ over $2000$ permutations.  These comparison facts show that the paper uses the same finite-row discipline for RNA-output contacts, but they do not strengthen the miRNA seed statistic beyond its own rows.

\paragraph{Mechanism refusal.}
The negative miRNA seed gradient is not, by itself, a measurement of translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a general sequence rule.  It also is not a decay-factor RBP bridge.  Such a bridge would require same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and a declared half-life statistic on the same finite rows.  The present miRNA seed contact does not contain those perturbation and rescue surfaces.  Therefore the permitted conclusion is narrower: the finite cross-dataset rows carry a reproducible negative miRNA seed-associated RNA-output relation, while mechanism, translation, protein state, structure, function, phenotype, and universality remain outside the licensed statement.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The record fails if the seed coordinate is promoted from a controlled RNA-output association to a claim about translation, folding, protein output, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  It also fails if the association is described as an RBP-mediated RNA-decay bridge without measured local RBP occupancy and perturbation/rescue rows.  Conversely, the record remains valid when it is kept at the finite level: $55170$ joined rows, median partial rank association $-0.224966$, $7$ replicated gradients, and an explicit refusal to infer unmeasured layers.
