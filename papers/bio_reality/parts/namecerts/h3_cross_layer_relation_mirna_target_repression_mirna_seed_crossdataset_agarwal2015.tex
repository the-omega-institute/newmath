\subsection{miRNA seed repression as a bounded cross-dataset RNA-output relation}\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record considered here is a finite matched panel of coding-RNA or transcript rows in which a miRNA seed-coordinate label is tested against an RNA-output readout while the translation layer is kept as a required boundary rather than as an inferred consequence.  The central finite audit is the row set named \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mirna\_target\_repression.mirna\_seed\_crossdataset\_agarwal2015}.  Its verified payload contains $n=55170$ joined rows, an aggregate per-miRNA partial-rank association with median value $-0.224966$, and $7$ replicated gradient instances.  The recorded run identifier is \texttt{50f22b191476}, with runtime $400.408$ seconds and verification timestamp \texttt{2026-06-30T18:36:19+00:00}.  The negative sign is read only as a bounded repression-oriented RNA-output gradient across the joined finite rows; it is not read as protein output, cellular function, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Coordinate, relation, and trigger.}
Each row carries a finite coordinate label for the seed-target context, a same-row RNA-output quantity, and the declared control fields needed to separate a coordinate-associated repression pattern from simpler transcript-level explanations.  The local object can be written as a finite table
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x_r=(q_r,y_r,z_r,b_r), \qquad r=1,\ldots,55170,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_r$ is the miRNA seed-coordinate class, $y_r$ is the bounded repression or RNA-output readout used by the cross-dataset statistic, $z_r$ denotes row controls such as abundance, local sequence composition, transcript context, and batch-like effects when available, and $b_r$ records the dataset or perturbation block from which the row enters the joined audit.  The finite statistic is not a claim that $q_r$ itself acts as a biochemical cause.  It is the weaker assertion that the row-indexed relation between $q_r$ and $y_r$ survives the declared finite joining and control protocol with a median partial association below zero.

\paragraph{Readout statistic.}
The observed scalar summary for the miRNA-seed audit is the median, over the eligible miRNA-level summaries, of a partial-rank coefficient after the finite row controls have been applied.  In the notation of this chapter the reported number is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \operatorname{median}_{m}\,\rho_m(q,y\mid z)=-0.224966,
  \qquad n=55170,
  \qquad g=7,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $g=7$ is the count of replicated gradient cases.  The sign and magnitude license a specific finite-row statement: in this joined Agarwal-style target-repression surface, seed-coordinate organization is associated with reduced RNA-output or repression readback under the stated partial controls.  The statistic is deliberately not promoted to a translation-efficiency statement, because no row in this summary is asserted to carry the mandatory same-row ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency readout needed for that layer.

\paragraph{Separation from RNA-stability examples.}
The miRNA-seed statistic is compared locally with other finite RNA-output boundary audits only to fix the scope of the claim.  The m6A half-life audit \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_modification.m6a\_site\_load\_mrna\_halflife\_boundary\_gse163500} reports $5837$ genes, a static Spearman value $-0.388929$ for m6A load against DMSO log$_2$ half-life, a causal Spearman value $0.245804$ for m6A load against $\Delta\log_2$ half-life, a length-partial Spearman value $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value $0.000999001$.  The ARE deletion audit \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_deletion\_mrna\_stability\_boundary\_siegel2022} reports $226$ BEAS2B deletion rows, $2242$ Jurkat deletion rows, $1000$ actual permutations, $2000$ grading permutations, and a sign-concordant result.  These companion finite audits show the intended granularity: an RNA-output or RNA-stability boundary may be positive as a row statistic while still refusing protein, structure, mechanism, phenotype, or general-law promotion.

\paragraph{Admissible claim.}
The admissible claim is therefore the following bounded cross-layer relation.  In the finite joined miRNA target-repression panel, a seed-coordinate label may be treated as carrying a reproducible RNA-output repression relation when the row statistic remains negative after the declared controls and when the gradient is replicated across the finite miRNA summaries.  The local evidence is the triple
$$
\begin{aligned}
  (n,\operatorname{median}\rho,g)=(55170,-0.224966,7).
\end{aligned}
$$
This triple is sufficient for a restrained BioReality statement about miRNA seed-target repression at the RNA-output boundary.  It is not sufficient for any stronger biological interpretation unless additional layer-matched rows are supplied.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The chapter refuses several tempting readings.  A difference in RNA-output or target repression is not, by itself, a translation realization, a protein-abundance measurement, a folded-structure result, a physical-admissibility assay, a functional result, a phenotype, an adaptation, a causal mechanism, or a universal sequence law.  If the high- and low-coordinate seed labels become exchangeable after transcript abundance, local composition, isoform state, batch, and other declared controls, then the coordinate remains only a finite descriptor.  If a later RNA half-life or decay-factor-RBP interpretation is desired, the same finite rows must carry measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and transcription or mRNA-abundance controls.  If those rows are absent, the miRNA-seed record remains a bounded repression-readout statistic and nothing stronger.

\paragraph{Boundary statement.}
The positive content of the chapter is thus narrow but nonempty: the finite Agarwal-style cross-dataset audit supplies a row-counted and sign-resolved repression surface for miRNA seed-target organization, with $55170$ joined rows, a median partial association of $-0.224966$, and $7$ replicated gradients.  The negative content is equally part of the result: neither the coordinate geometry nor the RNA-output association supplies translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality without a distinct finite contact that directly measures the corresponding layer.
