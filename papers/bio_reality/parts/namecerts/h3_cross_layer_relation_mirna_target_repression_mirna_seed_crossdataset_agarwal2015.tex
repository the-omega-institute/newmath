\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite panel of transcript-level rows in which a miRNA seed relation is read against matched transcript measurements and bounded RNA-layer outcomes.  The primary finite run for the subject identifier \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mirna\_target\_repression.mirna\_seed\_crossdataset\_agarwal2015} contains $55170$ retained rows and reports an aggregate per-miRNA partial Spearman statistic
$\rho_{\mathrm{partial,median}}=-0.224966$.
The same finite run records $7$ replicated gradient directions and a runtime of $400.408$ seconds.  Thus the object is not a free biological law; it is a computed table-level relation between a seed-defined coordinate and a repression-oriented transcript readout under the controls already present in the row construction.

\paragraph{Coordinate and statistic.}
Let $W$ denote the retained finite row set, with $|W|=55170$.  Each row carries a transcript or target label, a miRNA-seed relation label, measured transcript-side readout fields, and the covariates needed by the finite partial statistic.  The reported sign is negative, so the observed direction is consistent with stronger seed-associated targeting aligning with lower target-side expression or stability readout in the aggregated per-miRNA summary.  The numerical claim is only the following bounded assertion:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|W| &= 55170,\\
\operatorname{median}_{m}\bigl(\rho_{m,\mathrm{partial}}\bigr) &= -0.224966,\\
G_{\mathrm{replicated}} &= 7.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $m$ ranges over the miRNA-indexed finite summaries used by the run, and $G_{\mathrm{replicated}}$ denotes the number of replicated gradient directions recorded by the audit payload.  No continuous population parameter is introduced by this notation; it is only a compact display of the finite computation.

\paragraph{Layer discipline.}
The chapter licenses a cross-layer relation only at the transcript-regulatory boundary: a miRNA seed coordinate is compared with a transcript-side repression readout in a finite cross-dataset table.  The negative aggregate statistic supplies evidence for a bounded seed-to-transcript relation, not for translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological rule.  A same-row translation measurement would be a separate contact, and a protein or phenotype conclusion would require its own layer-matched rows and declared statistic.  In particular, RNA half-life, mRNA abundance, and repression readouts cannot be promoted into ribosome occupancy or protein output merely because the coordinate is coding-sequence-adjacent or transcript-linked.

These numbers show that the miRNA record sits among finite transcript-layer boundary computations, but they do not supply the missing biological contacts for a mechanistic RBP-decay interpretation.

\paragraph{Declared refusal surface.}
The finite miRNA seed statistic does not include same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective rescue, non-target rescue, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, or a matched perturbation panel.  Therefore an RBP-mediated RNA-decay mechanism is not concluded here.  If a later finite table carries those fields, its claim would have to be evaluated on its own rows under transcription-rate, local RNA-structure, motif-density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and same-row translation-boundary controls.  The present table remains a bounded miRNA seed repression relation with $55170$ rows, negative aggregate partial association, and $7$ replicated gradient directions.

Within the finite data supplied, the chapter is ready as a transcript-layer BioReality record.  Its positive content is the audited miRNA seed association summarized by $\rho_{\mathrm{partial,median}}=-0.224966$ on $55170$ rows, with $7$ replicated gradient directions.  Its negative content is equally part of the result: no statement about translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, RNA-decay mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality follows without a separate finite contact at that layer.
