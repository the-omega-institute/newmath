\subsection{miRNA seed repression as a finite cross-dataset RNA readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite cross-dataset panel in which miRNA--target rows are read through matched transcript-level repression coordinates rather than through an inferred organism-wide law.  The observed table contains $55{,}170$ retained rows for the miRNA seed-repression statistic.  Each row is treated as a bounded RNA-regulatory datum: a target-side seed or site coordinate, a miRNA identity, transcript abundance or repression readout, covariates used in the partial association calculation, and the dataset provenance needed to keep the statistic finite.  The verified aggregate for this panel is a median per-miRNA partial Spearman association
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{partial,median}} &= -0.224966,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $7$ replicated gradient directions across the retained miRNA collection.  The sign is the biologically expected sign for target repression: higher seed-target burden is associated with lower target readout after the registered controls.  The number is not used as a universal repression constant; it is a finite cross-dataset coordinate showing that the miRNA seed relation survives the selected partial-control regime in this panel.

\paragraph{Layer assignment.}
The chapter classifies the statistic as a cross-layer RNA regulation relation, not as a protein, structure, function, adaptation, or mechanism theorem.  The finite readout can say that a seed-target coordinate remains negatively associated with a transcript-side repression surface in the Agarwal-style joined data.  It cannot by itself say that the transcript effect realizes translation, changes mature protein output, alters folded structure, supplies physical admissibility, produces a phenotype, or establishes a general biological law.  Those stronger conclusions would require separate rows carrying the corresponding measured layer.  In particular, a translation claim would require same-row ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency data with matched mRNA controls; a protein-output claim would require matched proteomics; a structural or physical claim would require a structure-level or physical-state contact; a function or phenotype claim would require its own assay or perturbation surface.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
Let $i$ range over retained miRNA--target rows, let $S_i$ denote the finite seed or site coordinate after the declared controls, and let $Y_i$ denote the transcript repression readout.  The verified statistic is read as the sign and magnitude of a controlled rank relation rather than as a mechanistic equation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_i &\longmapsto Y_i,\\
\operatorname{median}_{m}\,\rho_m(S,Y\mid Z) &= -0.224966,\\
N_{\mathrm{rows}} &= 55{,}170,\\
N_{\mathrm{replicated\ gradients}} &= 7.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $Z$ denotes the finite covariate and dataset-control information used by the registered analysis.  The notation records only the audited association surface.  It does not assert that the seed coordinate is the sole causal variable, that every miRNA follows the same coefficient, or that repression at the RNA layer automatically propagates to any downstream layer.

\paragraph{Boundary against RNA-decay and RBP mechanism claims.}
The same finite discipline blocks promotion to an RNA half-life or decay-factor RBP mechanism.  A transcript repression association is not a measured RNA half-life panel unless each retained row also carries a half-life readout.  It is not an RBP-mediated decay bridge unless the rows carry measured local occupancy for a prespecified decay-factor RBP, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and local structure, motif-density, transcription-rate, isoform, abundance, batch, GC, dinucleotide, and codon-context controls.  If an RNA half-life effect appears without measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, or if perturbation and rescue do not isolate the target RBP, the result remains an RNA-stability association at most.  If structure, motif density, transcription, isoform state, batch, binding-defective rescue, or non-target control arms reproduce the pattern, the bridge interpretation is refused.

\paragraph{Comparison with other finite RNA-layer surfaces.}
The linked RNA-layer checks show why this chapter stays narrow.  In the m6A half-life boundary panel, $5{,}837$ genes were retained, the static Spearman relation between m6A load and DMSO log half-life was $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman relation between m6A load and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life was $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman relation was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was $0.000999001$.  That panel explicitly concerns m6A load and mRNA half-life, not miRNA seed repression.  In the endogenous $3'$ UTR ARE transfer panel, $14{,}933$ genes were retained, the ARE coefficient was $-0.0607953$, the baseline $R^2$ was $0.0657771$, the test $R^2$ was $0.0827561$, the improvement was $0.016979$, and the permutation value was $0.00049975$.  Those numbers support their own RNA-stability boundary surfaces, but they do not fill in the missing RBP occupancy, perturbation, or rescue rows for the present miRNA seed panel.  They are included here only to calibrate the treatment of finite RNA readouts: each surface is allowed to speak at the layer it actually measures.

\paragraph{Licensed reading.}
The licensed conclusion is therefore bounded and positive at the RNA-regulatory association layer.  The Agarwal-style cross-dataset panel contains $55{,}170$ retained rows and shows a negative median per-miRNA partial association of $-0.224966$ with $7$ replicated gradient directions.  This is enough to record a finite miRNA seed-repression cross-dataset relation under the declared controls.  It is not enough to claim translation realization, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, a decay-factor RBP mechanism, or universality.  Any such claim must be carried by an additional layer-matched finite panel and by a statistic that remains nondegenerate after the corresponding controls.
