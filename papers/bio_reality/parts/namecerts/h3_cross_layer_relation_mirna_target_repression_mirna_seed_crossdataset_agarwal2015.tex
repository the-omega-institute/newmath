\subsection{miRNA Seed Repression as a Bounded Cross-Dataset RNA Readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This BioReality record treats the miRNA seed experiment as a finite cross-layer RNA readout rather than as a global law about regulation.  The rows are matched transcript or target-site observations carrying a miRNA seed label, target context, expression or repression readout, and the covariates needed to keep local sequence geometry separate from biological interpretation.  The verified finite table for the Agarwal-style cross-dataset seed-repression statistic contains $55{,}170$ retained rows, with aggregate per-miRNA partial Spearman median
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{median}} &= -0.224966,
\end{aligned}
$$
and with $7$ replicated gradient components recorded in the finite run.  The run identifier is $50f22b191476$, and the measured runtime was $400.408$ seconds.  These numbers are used here only as a row-bounded contact with observed repression direction; they do not by themselves identify a molecular mechanism, a protein-output effect, or a universal rule of miRNA action.

\paragraph{Coordinate and relation.}
The coordinate carried by the row is the seed-target label together with the matched expression context in which repression is scored.  The relation is the finite signed association between the seed-coordinate and the target readout after the declared adjustment set has been applied.  In this certificate the sign is biologically coherent: the median partial association is negative, so larger seed-target support is associated with lower target readout in the retained finite panel.  The formal finite statement is simply
$$
\begin{aligned}
N &= 55{,}170,\\
\operatorname{median}_{\mathrm{miRNA}}(\rho_{\mathrm{partial}}) &= -0.224966,\\
G_{\mathrm{replicated}} &= 7.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate is therefore allowed to cross from a sequence-side seed label into a bounded transcript-side repression readout.  The crossing is not promoted to translation, protein abundance, folded structure, function, adaptation, causation, or universality.

\paragraph{Neighboring RNA-stability checks.}
The same paper-level boundary discipline is supported by two independent RNA-stability records that remain distinct from the miRNA seed statistic.  In the m6A site-load half-life record, $5{,}837$ genes passed the stated coverage rule, the static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO half-life was $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman association between m6A load and $\Delta\log_2$ half-life was $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman value was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 0.000999001.
\end{aligned}
$$
In the ARE deletion RNA-stability record, the finite panel contained $226$ BEAS-2B rows and $2{,}242$ Jurkat rows, with $1{,}000$ actual permutations and $2{,}000$ grading permutations; the sign-concordance flag was true.  These two additional records show that RNA-level readouts can be finite, directional, and statistically separated while still remaining layer-bounded.  They are not merged with the miRNA seed panel; they serve only as local calibration that RNA half-life or repression evidence must remain tied to its own contact surface and its own declared statistic.

\paragraph{Permitted claim.}
The ready claim is narrow.  A finite cross-dataset miRNA seed coordinate can be reported as having a bounded transcript-side repression readout when the row set, covariates, and partial statistic are declared, and when the observed direction remains negative at the per-miRNA aggregate level.  The observed value $-0.224966$ is strong enough for this chapter to record a finite cross-layer relation between seed targeting and target repression in the retained panel.  The seven replicated gradients strengthen the finite-row stability of the readout, but they do not license a mechanism statement about Argonaute occupancy, RBP cooperation, decay-factor recruitment, ribosome behavior, protein concentration, protein state, phenotype, or adaptation.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The claim fails or remains merely descriptive if the seed labels become exchangeable after the declared controls, if the negative partial association disappears under the finite adjustment set, if identifier mapping creates the apparent signal, or if batch, transcript abundance, isoform state, local sequence composition, or structure explains the same pattern.  It also fails as a mechanism bridge if the same rows lack the relevant occupancy, perturbation, loss-of-factor, rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  A repression association is not an RNA-decay mechanism unless a separate finite contact measures the required decay factor and its perturbation behavior.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter records a finite RNA-layer association and nothing higher.  No translation realization follows from the miRNA repression statistic alone.  No protein output, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universal biological law follows unless another layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and a declared finite statistic.  The present certificate is ready because the row count, direction, replicated-gradient count, run identifier, and neighboring RNA-stability calibration numbers are concrete; its interpretation is deliberately confined to bounded transcript-level repression.
