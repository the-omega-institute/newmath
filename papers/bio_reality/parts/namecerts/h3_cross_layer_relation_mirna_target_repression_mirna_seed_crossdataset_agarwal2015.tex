\subsection{A finite miRNA-seed repression row as a translation-bounded RNA-stability boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Subject.}
This record concerns a finite panel in which coding-RNA or transcript-associated rows are allowed to carry a coordinate label, a same-row translation readout boundary, an RNA half-life or abundance-side readout, and controls that prevent the row from being promoted into a protein, structure, function, or mechanism claim.  The concrete external statistic attached here is the miRNA-seed cross-dataset repression check identified by the finite run \texttt{50f22b191476}.  In that run, $n=55170$ joined rows were retained, the aggregate per-miRNA partial Spearman median was $-0.224966$, and $7$ seed-gradient directions replicated under the declared finite comparison.  The run time recorded for the computation was $400.408$ seconds.  These numbers are read as a bounded repression-gradient contact: they show that the joined rows contain a finite miRNA-seed signal after the stated partial adjustment, not that the rows already contain a decay-factor RBP mechanism, protein output, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, adaptation, or a universal sequence law.

\paragraph{Finite row shape.}
Each admissible row is interpreted as a tuple of measured or declared fields rather than as a free biological narrative.  The row must keep separate the synonymous coordinate label, the local sequence or seed-context descriptor, the translation-side boundary, the RNA half-life or abundance-side readout, transcript abundance, batch, isoform state, composition covariates, local structural covariates, and the perturbation or rescue state when such a state is actually measured.  In symbols, the intended row family has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r_i ={}& (q_i, s_i, T_i, H_i, A_i, Z_i, B_i, P_i, C_i),\\
  q_i ={}& \text{coordinate or synonymous-window label},\\
  s_i ={}& \text{seed or local motif descriptor},\\
  T_i ={}& \text{translation readout boundary when present},\\
  H_i ={}& \text{RNA half-life, stability, or abundance-side readout},\\
  A_i ={}& \text{transcript abundance or transcription-side control},\\
  Z_i ={}& \text{composition, structure, motif-density, codon-pair, and isoform controls},\\
  B_i ={}& \text{batch and dataset provenance},\\
  P_i ={}& \text{perturbation or rescue state if measured},\\
  C_i ={}& \text{declared stopping condition for the claim}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The miRNA-seed computation supplies a finite repression-gradient statistic over this kind of joined row.  It does not by itself supply measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, coordinate--occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, or binding-defective rescue.  Therefore the positive object in this subsection is a bounded cross-layer RNA-regulatory association, not an RBP-decay bridge.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The primary statistic is the sign and size of the cross-dataset miRNA-seed repression gradient.  With $n=55170$ rows and aggregate per-miRNA partial $\rho=-0.224966$, the observed direction is negative: stronger seed-context evidence is associated with stronger repression in the finite joined matrix.  The $7$ replicated gradient directions are treated as finite support for a robust seed-gradient readout across the declared comparison set.  The statistic can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
  N_{\mathrm{seed}} &= 55170,\\
  \widetilde{\rho}_{\mathrm{partial}} &= -0.224966,\\
  G_{\mathrm{replicated}} &= 7.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is biologically interpretable only at the RNA-regulatory boundary: it is compatible with miRNA-associated repression in the joined rows, but it is not a measurement of translation realization unless the same rows carry the translation readout required for that stronger claim.  It is also not a measurement of RNA decay mechanism unless occupancy and perturbation rows are present for the particular decay factor named in the mechanism.

\paragraph{Scope calibration from neighboring RNA-stability contacts.}
The same finite audit bundle contains RNA-stability checks that help fix the boundary of interpretation.  In the m6A site-load half-life run \texttt{7cd189f15c4b}, $5837$ genes were retained; the static Spearman association between m6A load and DMSO log half-life was $-0.388929$, the causal Spearman association between m6A-load change and half-life change was $0.245804$, the length-partial Spearman value was $0.227683$, and the two-sided partial permutation value was $0.000999001$.  In the endogenous 3UTR AU-rich-element transfer run \texttt{e96bf26e626b}, $14933$ genes were retained, the ARE coefficient was $-0.0607953$, the test $R^2$ was $0.0827561$, the baseline $R^2$ was $0.0657771$, the increment was $0.016979$, and the permutation value was $0.00049975$ over $2000$ permutations.  These comparison rows are not imported as evidence for the miRNA-seed claim.  They are cited only to mark a local discipline: RNA-stability statistics may be finite, directional, and significant while still remaining short of a protein, structure, function, or mechanism assertion.

\paragraph{Decision rule.}
The admissible positive reading is the following bounded statement.  A finite miRNA-seed repression row may be reported when the joined matrix retains the declared rows, the partial statistic remains negative, and the replicated gradient count is nonzero under the declared controls.  A stronger RBP-decay bridge would require additional fields in the same finite rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  &\text{measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy},\\
  &\text{coordinate--occupancy decoupling},\\
  &\text{target-RBP loss},\\
  &\text{wild-type rescue},\\
  &\text{binding-defective or non-target control},\\
  &\text{transcription-rate, abundance, structure, motif-density, composition, isoform, and batch controls}.
\end{aligned}
$$
If these fields are absent, the seed-gradient statistic remains a bounded RNA-regulatory readout.  If they are present but the half-life effect remains after a binding-defective rescue, is reproduced by a non-target control, is explained by transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC or dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, transcript abundance, or batch, then the mechanism interpretation is refused.  If the high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable for RNA half-life after the listed controls, the coordinate label remains descriptive only.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The row count $55170$, the median partial value $-0.224966$, and the $7$ replicated gradients license only the finite miRNA-seed repression boundary described above.  They do not license translation output from RNA half-life or transcript abundance alone.  They do not establish ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a general biological law.  A protein-level or structure-level claim would need a separate layer-matched contact, and an RBP-decay mechanism claim would need same-window measured RBP occupancy together with target loss, rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls.  The chapter therefore records a ready finite boundary claim for miRNA-seed repression, while keeping the decay-factor RBP bridge and all higher-layer interpretations outside the licensed conclusion.
