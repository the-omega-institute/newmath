\subsection{miRNA seed repression as a bounded RNA-level relation}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite joined panel of miRNA--target rows in which a seed-mediated target relation is tested against a same-row repression readout.  The observed panel for the registered miRNA seed cross-dataset contact contains $n=55170$ joined rows.  Across the per-miRNA summaries, the aggregate partial Spearman coefficient has median $-0.224966$, and the expected repression-direction gradient is replicated for $7$ miRNA strata.  The computation was registered under run $50f22b191476$ and took $400.408$ seconds.  In the local finite-row notation, each retained row may be read as
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_i = (&\,\text{miRNA label},\ \text{target transcript label},\ \text{seed-site class},\ \text{matched expression or repression readout},\\
      &\,\text{background transcript covariates},\ \text{batch or dataset label},\ \text{declared control state}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign of the median coefficient is therefore in the biologically expected direction for target repression: stronger seed-compatible targeting is associated with lower target expression or stronger repression after the finite control adjustment used by the registered experiment.

\paragraph{Licensed interpretation.}
The empirical statement licensed here is deliberately narrow.  The rows support a bounded cross-layer relation from a finite miRNA seed-target coordinate to an RNA-level repression readout.  The numerical content is not a universal law of miRNA biology; it is a finite statistic over the registered cross-dataset table.  The relation can be written schematically as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{\mathrm{seed}\to\mathrm{RNA}} < 0
\end{aligned}
$$
with the observed finite summary represented by the median partial rank correlation $\rho_{\mathrm{partial}}=-0.224966$ over the per-miRNA summaries.  The count $7$ records how many miRNA strata reproduced the directional gradient under the declared test.  This gives a usable RNA-repression boundary: seed-compatible target structure and measured target repression co-vary in the finite panel after the experiment's controls, but the result remains tied to those rows and their joining rules.

\paragraph{Relation to RNA half-life contacts.}
The same research memory also contains RNA-stability contacts that help delimit what this chapter is not claiming.  In the m6A site-load half-life boundary experiment, run $7cd189f15c4b$ used $n=5837$ genes and reported a length-partial Spearman value $0.227683$, a causal-condition Spearman value $0.245804$, a static DMSO Spearman value $-0.388929$, and a two-sided partial permutation value $0.000999001$.  In the endogenous 3' UTR ARE transfer experiment, run $e96bf26e626b$ used $n=14933$ genes, with $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, and permutation value $0.00049975$.  These two records show that the archive can register finite RNA-half-life or RNA-stability boundaries when the rows actually contain those readouts.  They do not supply the missing decay-factor RBP occupancy, perturbation, rescue, or binding-defective controls for the present miRNA seed record.

\paragraph{Refusal of mechanism promotion.}
The finite miRNA seed statistic must not be promoted into a decay-factor RBP mechanism.  A positive RBP-mediated RNA half-life bridge would require the same organism and same window rows to carry measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, mRNA abundance controls, local RNA-structure covariates, motif-density covariates, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, transcript abundance, batch state, and a same-row translation readout kept as a boundary variable rather than as an endpoint conclusion.  Those rows are not present in the registered miRNA seed experiment.  Consequently the current result is an RNA-target repression association, not an RBP occupancy claim, not a perturbation-rescue mechanism, and not a coordinate-to-half-life causal bridge.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the RNA repression statistic.  No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or universal biological law follows from the finite coefficient $-0.224966$ or from the $7$ replicated directional strata.  A translation claim would require a separate measured translation contact on the same rows.  A protein claim would require a protein-level contact.  A folding or physical-admissibility claim would require a structure or physical assay contact.  A mechanism claim would require perturbation and rescue rows that separate seed targeting, RBP occupancy, transcription, RNA stability, isoform state, and batch.  Until such rows are attached, the safe reading is only that the registered finite miRNA seed panel exhibits a bounded RNA-level repression relation under its declared controls.
