\subsection{Cross-dataset miRNA seed repression as a bounded RNA-regulatory row system}
\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record concerns a finite panel of coding or transcript-associated RNA rows in which a synonymous or seed-coordinate label is kept separate from every downstream biological interpretation.  Each admissible row has a coordinate label, an RNA readout, a matched abundance or transcription control when available, local sequence and isoform covariates, batch information, and a declared break condition.  In the miRNA seed-repression dataset, the verified finite table contains $55170$ retained rows.  The reported aggregate statistic is a median per-miRNA partial Spearman association
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{partial,median}} &= -0.224966,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $7$ replicated gradient instances and a recorded runtime of $400.408$ seconds for experiment run $50\mathrm{f}22\mathrm{b}191476$.  The sign is biologically consistent with repression: stronger seed-compatible targeting is associated with lower expression or stronger repression in the finite joined table.  This is enough to register a bounded cross-layer RNA-regulatory relation for the specific finite panel, not enough to promote the coordinate into a general mechanism or a protein-level conclusion.

\paragraph{Admissible interpretation.}
The finite statistic licenses only the following claim: in the retained cross-dataset rows, the miRNA seed coordinate carries a reproducible negative partial association with the measured RNA-side repression readout after the covariates used by the experiment have been applied.  The statement is row-local and conditional on the join keys, normalization choices, batch structure, and the selected regression or partial-rank adjustment.  It does not say that the seed coordinate alone realizes translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, cellular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal law.  If the same coordinate is later joined to a translation-efficiency surface, that surface must remain a separate observation; a translation conclusion cannot be read out of the RNA-side association itself.

\paragraph{Decay-factor RBP bridge condition.}
The stronger conjectural bridge would require a same-organism, same-window panel in which the coordinate label is observed together with RNA half-life, measured local occupancy of a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, transcription-rate controls, mRNA abundance controls, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, and the mandatory same-row translation readout.  In symbols, the admissible row shape for that stronger bridge is
$$
\begin{aligned}
r_i = (&q_i, T_i, H_i, O_i, A_i, C_i, S_i, D_i, G_i, P_i, I_i, B_i, U_i),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the coordinate label, $T_i$ is the same-row translation readout boundary, $H_i$ is RNA half-life, $O_i$ is measured local decay-factor RBP occupancy, $A_i$ records transcription or abundance control, $C_i$ records composition controls, $S_i$ records local structure, $D_i$ records motif density, $G_i$ records GC and dinucleotide information, $P_i$ records codon-pair context, $I_i$ records isoform state, $B_i$ records batch, and $U_i$ records perturbation and rescue state.  A positive bridge would require the half-life contrast to track $O_i$, collapse under target-RBP loss, return under wild-type rescue, and fail under binding-defective or non-target controls.  The miRNA seed statistic above does not contain these RBP occupancy and perturbation rows, so it remains an RNA-regulatory association rather than a decay-factor RBP mechanism.

\paragraph{Comparison with nearby RNA-stability contacts.}
Other finite RNA-stability records show why the boundary is sharp.  The m6A site-load half-life record for $5837$ joined genes reports a DMSO static Spearman association of $-0.388929$, a causal Spearman association between m6A-load change and $\Delta \log_2$ half-life of $0.245804$, a length-partial Spearman value of $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial permutation value
$$
\begin{aligned}
p &= 0.000999001.
\end{aligned}
$$
That record crosses its own RNA half-life boundary, but it still does not supply miRNA seed repression, decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, or rescue evidence for the present claim.  Likewise, the endogenous 3-prime UTR ARE transfer record uses $14933$ genes, reports $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, and permutation value $p=0.00049975$ across $2000$ permutations.  That finite table supports a separate ARE-linked RNA half-life transfer boundary, not an miRNA seed or RBP-bridge mechanism here.  These neighboring records are useful controls on scope: they show that RNA half-life and RNA-regulatory surfaces can be quantified in finite rows, while each molecular bridge still requires its own matched contacts.

\paragraph{Refusal conditions.}
The claim is refused if high- and low-coordinate labels become exchangeable for RNA half-life after controlling transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, transcript abundance, and batch.  It is also refused as an RBP bridge if RNA half-life differs without a measured local RBP-occupancy change, if target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, if wild-type rescue does not restore it, or if a binding-defective rescue, non-target control, RNA-structure shift, motif-density artifact, transcription effect, isoform-stability difference, or batch effect reproduces the pattern.  Under those failures, the coordinate remains a bounded descriptor or an RNA-stability association only.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite miRNA seed record is ready as a bounded BioReality entry because it reports a concrete cross-dataset row count, a negative partial-rank repression statistic, and a replicated-gradient count.  Its mathematical content is the finite separation between a coordinate-associated RNA-side repression readout and every stronger biological layer.  No translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, mechanism, causation, or universality follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the declared statistic is recomputed on that joined finite panel.
