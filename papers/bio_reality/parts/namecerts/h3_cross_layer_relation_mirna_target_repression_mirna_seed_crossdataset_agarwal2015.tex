\subsection{MicroRNA Seed Repression as a Bounded Cross-Dataset Row Relation}\label{sec:bioreality-mirna-seed-crossdataset-agarwal2015}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record named \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.mirna\_target\_repression.mirna\_seed\_crossdataset\_agarwal2015} is treated here as a finite row statement about microRNA seed-target repression, not as an unrestricted account of post-transcriptional regulation.  The usable rows are matched transcript or target-window observations carrying a seed-site coordinate, a repression-oriented readout, and the covariates needed to separate the seed label from ordinary abundance, composition, batch, and transcript-context effects.  The verified run \texttt{50f22b191476} retained $55{,}170$ finite rows and reported an aggregate per-microRNA partial Spearman median
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \rho_{\mathrm{median}} &= -0.224966,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $7$ replicated gradient directions.  The sign is the expected repression direction: after the declared controls are applied, stronger seed-target coordinate load is associated with lower target expression or lower target-survival readout.  The chapter therefore licenses a bounded cross-dataset relation between a microRNA seed coordinate and a repression readout, with the numerical content fixed by the retained finite rows rather than by a genome-wide universal law.

\paragraph{Coordinate and statistic.}
Let $i$ index the retained target rows and let $m$ index the assayed microRNA family or seed class.  The finite datum for a usable row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x_i = (&\,\mathrm{seed\ label}_i,\ \mathrm{target\ readout}_i,\ \mathrm{mRNA\ abundance}_i,\ \mathrm{transcript\ context}_i,\\
         &\,\mathrm{composition}_i,\ \mathrm{site\ context}_i,\ \mathrm{batch}_i,\ \mathrm{declared\ break\ condition}_i).
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported statistic is not a raw site-count correlation.  It is a partial rank relation computed per microRNA and then summarized across microRNA-specific gradients.  The displayed median $-0.224966$ is consequently read as a finite directional summary: within the retained cross-dataset panel, seed-coordinate information continues to align with repression after the recorded controls are imposed.  The replicated-gradient count $7$ is part of the readout, because a single direction could be a dataset artifact whereas repeated directionality across microRNA gradients is the evidence-bearing finite pattern.

\paragraph{Boundary to RNA half-life records.}
The same BioReality table contains separate RNA-stability boundary records, and they are useful only as local calibration of what is and is not being claimed here.  In the m6A site-load half-life record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_modification.m6a\_site\_load\_mrna\_halflife\_boundary\_gse163500}, run \texttt{7cd189f15c4b} retained $5{,}837$ genes, reported a static Spearman value $-0.388929$ for m6A load against DMSO log-half-life, a causal Spearman value $0.245804$ for m6A-load change against half-life change, a length-partial Spearman value $0.227683$, and a two-sided partial-permutation value $0.000999001$.  In the endogenous ARE transfer record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.utr3\_are.are\_endogenous\_3utr\_halflife\_transfer\_rnadecaycafe}, run \texttt{e96bf26e626b} retained $14{,}933$ genes, with baseline $R^2=0.0657771$, test $R^2=0.0827561$, $\Delta R^2=0.016979$, $\beta_{\mathrm{ARE}}=-0.0607953$, and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}=0.00049975$ over $2{,}000$ permutations.  These two neighboring records show that the table can store finite RNA half-life statistics with explicit row counts and permutation evidence.  They do not turn the microRNA seed result into an RNA-decay mechanism; they only delimit the style of evidence required when a stability claim is made.

\paragraph{Licensed reading.}
The finite assertion is the following.  There exists a retained cross-dataset panel of $55{,}170$ rows in which microRNA seed-coordinate load has a negative partial rank association with the target repression readout, summarized by median $\rho=-0.224966$, and the direction repeats across $7$ microRNA gradients.  This is enough for a bounded cross-layer relation from a sequence-level targeting coordinate to a transcript-level repression readout.  It is not enough to identify the molecular cause of every target change, to infer protein abundance, or to infer a downstream phenotype.  The only positive content is the finite association after the declared controls; the row statistic remains tied to the row panel that produced it.

\paragraph{Local refusal conditions.}
The chapter rejects promotion beyond the measured surface.  A seed-coordinate association is refused as a translation claim unless the same finite rows also contain a matched translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout with its own denominator control.  It is refused as a protein-output claim unless a matched protein-abundance contact is joined and separately analyzed.  It is refused as folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universality unless a separate layer-specific contact supplies that readout and the corresponding finite statistic is computed.  Likewise, RNA half-life language is not licensed from the microRNA seed statistic alone.  A stability interpretation would require same-row RNA half-life measurements, transcription or mRNA-abundance controls, isoform diagnostics, local sequence and structure covariates, and an explicit break condition.  A decay-factor RBP mechanism would require measured local RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, binding-defective or non-target controls, and coordinate/occupancy decoupling on the same finite panel.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The record breaks if high- and low-seed-coordinate rows become exchangeable after the declared controls, if the negative direction is not replicated across the microRNA gradients, or if batch, transcript abundance, site-context, sequence composition, isoform state, or a simpler baseline reproduces the reported pattern.  It also breaks if the chapter is read as asserting translation realization, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a global sequence law.  Under those refusals, the chapter remains a precise finite-row statement: microRNA seed targeting in the Agarwal-style cross-dataset panel carries a reproducible negative partial rank signal at the transcript-repression boundary, and no higher biological layer is inferred without its own matched evidence.
