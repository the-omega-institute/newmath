\paragraph{Local null.}
The null model is row exchangeability of high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous residual labels with respect to translation-conditioned RNA half-life after controlling transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, batch, amino-acid composition, and the same-row translation readout.  In symbols, the admissible comparison has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
H_{0}:\quad
&\Delta H(q_{\mathrm{high}},q_{\mathrm{low}}\mid T,A,Z)=0,\\
Z={}&\{\text{transcription},\text{local structure},\text{motifs},\text{GC/dinucleotides},\text{codon pairs},\text{isoform},\text{abundance},\text{batch}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $H$ is the RNA half-life readout, $T$ is the same-row translation readout, and $A$ denotes amino-acid sequence controls.  A nonzero half-life contrast is still not an RBP bridge unless measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP changes in the decoupled direction, target-RBP loss collapses the contrast, wild-type rescue restores it, and binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it.

\paragraph{Positive bridge criterion.}
The permitted positive statement is deliberately narrow.  A finite panel supports the bridge only when the same rows carry $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ labels, translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, local decay-factor RBP occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target rescue controls.  The statistic must first show a translation-conditioned RNA half-life contrast.  It must then show that the contrast tracks measured local RBP occupancy rather than only RNA structure, motif density, nucleotide composition, codon-pair context, transcript abundance, isoform state, or batch.  Finally, the perturbation arm must be directional: loss of the target RBP removes the coordinate-associated half-life effect, wild-type rescue returns it, and the binding-defective or non-target rescue does not.  Without this full pattern, the coordinate remains a bounded descriptor or a finite RNA-stability association, not a mechanistic RBP bridge.

No translation realization follows from RNA half-life alone.  No decay-factor RBP mechanism follows from a half-life difference unless measured local occupancy, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls all appear on the declared matched panel.  No protein output, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universal biological law follows from $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ geometry, codon-window notation, same-row translation conditioning, RNA half-life association, RBP contact naming, workflow readiness, or any linked finite scalar run.
