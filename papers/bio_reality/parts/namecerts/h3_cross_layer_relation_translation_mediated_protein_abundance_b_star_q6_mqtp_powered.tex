\subsection{Translation-conditioned abundance surface for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite mediation surface whose rows join three kinds of quantities: a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-side readout $t_j$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $p_k$. The residual coordinates are the nine axes
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The translation readout used in the powered run is not ribosome profiling; it is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T = \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the coding sequence and $w_c$ is computed from organism-matched GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts. The finite computation therefore tests a statistical path $Q \to T \to Y$ on joined sequence, tRNA-copy, modeled-translation, and protein-abundance rows, while keeping amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline in the control block.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The powered surface contains twelve organism panels. The joined protein counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. Each panel reports nine $Q$ coordinates after residualization. The control design has $24$ columns before rank reduction and rank $23$ after residualization in every organism panel. The residual degrees of freedom range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse. The translation column has rank $1$, the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, and the combined $Q,T$ block has rank $10$ throughout, so the finite computation is not a rank-empty comparison. The run also records that the mediated fraction is in the unit interval in every organism panel.

\paragraph{Mediation numbers.}
The measured scalar is the reduction in residual $Q \to Y$ association after conditioning on modeled tAI. In symbols, the reported mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTY}=\frac{R^2(Q,Y)-R^2(Q,Y\mid T)}{R^2(Q,Y)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The strongest finite entry is yeast: $R^2(Q,Y)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q,Y\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, indirect $R^2=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTY}=0.9491551041216973$. Other large mediated fractions occur in \emph{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTY}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, and \emph{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$. The remaining panels are positive but smaller: $0.4872268357227724$ in \emph{C. elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ in zebrafish, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.1976903964724546$ in chicken, $0.17489681798543982$ in rat, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}. Thus the finite verdict is not a universal sign law; it is a set of organism-scoped positive decompositions under the stated modeled-tAI construction.

\paragraph{Control readback.}
The residualized energy diagnostics make the same point without converting the statistic into mechanism. In yeast, $X$ explains $882.6526210803958$ units of protein-abundance residual energy, modeled tAI explains $941.8858618613751$, and the direct $X$ improvement after $T$ falls to $44.87838061554362$. In human, $X$ explains $1370.6490494847067$ units of $Y$ residual energy, modeled tAI explains $850.7864327764183$, and the direct $X$ improvement after $T$ is $997.7463057114725$, giving a much smaller mediated fraction. Mouse has the largest protein residual energy among the panels, $48806.47856410273$, with $M^{QTY}=0.15488799907323988$. These finite differences are why the chapter records a controlled decomposition matrix rather than a single biological law.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
This chapter supports only the bounded statement that, in the registered finite panels, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a protein-abundance association whose fitted strength is partly reduced after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI. It does not show observed ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, causal translation control, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality. Modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation construction from tRNA-copy and wobble-weight data, not a ribosome-footprint measurement. PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein readout and is not separated here from mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context. Direct $Q \to Y$ association is therefore not promoted unless a $Q \to T$ step is present and explicitly used, and even then the accepted claim remains statistical mediation, not mechanism.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent finite records.}
The same boundary is reinforced by the surrounding audits. The modeled tAI survival matrix had only three organisms in the earlier bounded-coupling run, with $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold and maximum $|S_{ij}|=3801961.031607896$, but that run explicitly stated that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the contact is not ribo-seq or proteomics validation. A separate yeast structure-mediation record found $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$ and structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$; that record is also a variance partition, not a causal path. The observable execution run on yeast reported $3289$ genes and a positive subblock verdict, while the cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows but did not support the strong selection-imprint gate, with leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$ and null95 threshold $0.05427157066522958$. These neighboring finite results allow the present chapter to be read as a disciplined cross-layer abundance decomposition, not as a license to infer translation, structure, function, or evolutionary law from residual geometry alone.
