\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Protein-Abundance Surface for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite cross-layer surface whose rows join three kinds of coordinates: a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $Y$. The residual list used in the computation has nine entries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate is not an observed ribosome-profiling variable in this run. It is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the protein-specific codon count and $w_c$ is computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon-count rows. Thus the admissible readback is a controlled statistical matrix $Q\to T\to Y$, not a direct promotion from synonymous residual geometry to protein realization.

\paragraph{Joined rows and controls.}
Three organism panels were joined. The \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 panel used $3739$ protein rows and the \emph{E. coli} tRNA table, with total all-tRNA copy count $90$ and one codon weight filled by geometric mean after a zero raw $W$ value. The \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155 panel used $4129$ protein rows, the matched \emph{M. smegmatis} tRNA table, total all-tRNA copy count $49$, and one geometric-mean fill. The \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} panel used $5815$ protein rows, the matched yeast tRNA table, total all-tRNA copy count $275$, and no geometric-mean fill. In each organism the residualized design used $24$ control columns, with rank $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, rank $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, rank $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and rank $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$. The residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively. The controls keep amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance structure in the background rather than turning them into promoted coordinates.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
The fitted object is a joint nine-dimensional $X_Q$ decomposition. Let $c$ denote the controlled $Q\to Y$ association before conditioning on modeled tAI, and let $c'$ denote the remaining direct $Q\to Y$ association after $T$ is included. The reported mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c},
\end{aligned}
$$
whenever the finite fit keeps the value in the unit interval. The run reports unit-interval values for all three organisms. For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, $c-c'=0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$. For \emph{M. smegmatis}, $c=0.03209009170649934$, $c'=0.029395293228368185$, $c-c'=0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$. For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. The organism panels therefore do not collapse to the same numerical behavior: the yeast surface is strongly attenuated by modeled tAI conditioning, the \emph{E. coli} surface is moderately attenuated, and the \emph{M. smegmatis} surface shows only a small mediated component.

\paragraph{Energy audit.}
The residualized energy ledger is consistent with a finite regression decomposition rather than a biological mechanism claim. For \emph{E. coli}, the residual $Y$ energy after controls is $4020.333671802061$, the residual $T$ energy is $0.4728468725814029$, $X$ explains $432.89050065533684$ units of $Y$ energy, $T$ explains $582.5912438239341$ units of $Y$ energy, $X$ explains $0.256128714706293$ units of $T$ energy, the $T$ increment given $X$ is $317.26302294928666$, and the $X$ increment given $T$ is $167.56227978068944$. For \emph{M. smegmatis}, the corresponding values are $5729.194538214237$, $0.041191576348982066$, $183.85037813566998$, $15.813547327443837$, $0.026187928294862578$, $0.37452260494680445$, and $168.41135341317295$. For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, they are $2958.1865890925337$, $6.90537888244409$, $882.6526210803958$, $941.8858618613751$, $5.671703404506426$, $104.11162139652299$, and $44.87838061554362$. These scalars justify recording a finite controlled mediation matrix, because the designs have sufficient rank and positive explained-energy entries, but they do not by themselves identify an intervention or a ribosome-level process.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. This chapter records that, in three finite organism panels, nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be joined to modeled per-protein tAI and matched PAXdb abundance, and that conditioning on modeled tAI reduces the controlled residual-to-protein association by organism-specific amounts. The readout $T$ is a codon-adaptation model from tRNA copy numbers and wobble weights; it is not ribosome profiling. The readout $Y$ is steady-state protein abundance; it is not folding, localization, turnover, post-translational state, molecular function, or phenotype. Therefore no observed translation dynamics, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, functional realization, causal mechanism, perturbation response, rescue statement, or universal biological law follows from this surface unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that evidence and the same-coordinate controlled statistic is recomputed. Direct $Q\to Y$ association is not counted as protein realization unless the $Q\to T$ leg is explicitly present and survives the declared controls.
