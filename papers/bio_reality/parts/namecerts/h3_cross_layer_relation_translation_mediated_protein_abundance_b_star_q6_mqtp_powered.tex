\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein abundance for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite cross-layer surface with rows of the form $(q_i,T,P)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $T$ is a per-protein modeled translation-adaptation readout, and $P$ is matched measured protein abundance.  The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $n_c$ the CDS codon count and $w_c$ the codon tAI weight computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon-count rows.  The fitted object is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein assertion: a protein-abundance association is interpreted only after the same finite rows also carry a translation-adaptation coordinate.

\paragraph{Rows and rank.}
The experiment run $855d086a84bb$ joined six organism panels.  The joined row counts were $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding all-tRNA copy totals used by the modeled tAI computation were $8874$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $49$, and $275$.  Zero raw codon weights were filled by the geometric mean in only two panels: one codon in \emph{E. coli} and one codon in \emph{M. smegmatis}; the other four panels had zero such fills.

After residualizing against the declared control block, every panel had powered rank sufficiency.  The control matrix had $24$ columns with rank $23$; the residualized $Q$ block had rank $9$; the residualized translation coordinate had rank $1$; and the joint $(Q,T)$ block had rank $10$ in each organism.  Residual degrees of freedom after controls were $14387$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $4106$, and $5792$ in the same organism order.  These ranks make the reported mediation fractions non-vacuous linear-algebraic quantities rather than saturated all-fit artifacts.

\paragraph{Controlled decomposition.}
The fitted scalar is the joint nine-dimensional $Q$ mediation decomposition.  Let $c$ denote the controlled $Q\to P$ coefficient-of-determination contribution before conditioning on $T$, and let $c'$ denote the remaining direct $Q\to P$ contribution after $T$ is included.  The reported mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All six panels placed $M^{QTP}$ inside the unit interval.  The organism-specific triples $(c,c',c-c')$ were $(0.0461352981,0.0315701356,0.0145651625)$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $(0.1076752668,0.0416786997,0.0659965671)$ for \emph{E. coli}, $(0.0398079360,0.0319382893,0.0078696466)$ for \emph{G. gallus}, $(0.0445456512,0.0324264325,0.0121192187)$ for human, $(0.0320900917,0.0293952932,0.0026947985)$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $(0.2983762499,0.0151709094,0.2832053406)$ for yeast.  Thus the mediated fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=&(0.3157053954,0.6129222528,0.1976903965,\\
&0.2720628916,0.0839760292,0.9491551041),
\end{aligned}
$$
again ordered as fish, bacterium, chicken, human, mycobacterium, and yeast.  The strongest finite-row signal occurs in yeast, where conditioning through modeled tAI accounts for $0.2832053406$ of the total controlled $Q\to P$ contribution $0.2983762499$, leaving $0.0151709094$ as the residual direct term.  The weakest mediated fraction occurs in \emph{M. smegmatis}, where the indirect component is $0.0026947985$ of a total $0.0320900917$.

\paragraph{Energy audit.}
The same controlled fits also report the residual energy scales used to guard against degenerate readings.  The $T$ residual energies were $0.5747725575$, $0.4728468726$, $0.7248915610$, $1.1744686487$, $0.0411915763$, and $6.9053788824$.  The corresponding $P$ residual energies were $24915.2440718051$, $4020.3336718021$, $18586.7776841263$, $30769.5367150478$, $5729.1945382142$, and $2958.1865890925$.  The controlled $T\to P$ explained energies were $705.5230335367$, $582.5912438239$, $279.8218625433$, $850.7864327764$, $15.8135473274$, and $941.8858618614$.  The incremental improvement of $T$ given $Q$ was positive in every panel: $342.6284544658$, $317.2630229493$, $133.5504899193$, $477.8836890032$, $0.3745226049$, and $104.1116213965$.  These values support a finite statistical translation-conditioned protein-abundance readback, with the important qualification that the translation coordinate is modeled tAI rather than observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Local evidential boundary.}
This chapter licenses only the following bounded statement: in six matched CDS--protein panels, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive controlled statistical decomposition through modeled per-protein tAI into measured protein abundance, with all reported $M^{QTP}$ fractions finite and in $[0,1]$.  It does not license a claim that synonymous residuals cause translation, that tAI is an observed ribosome-profiling measurement, that protein abundance isolates translation from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, or folding, or that any one organism supplies a universal biological rule.  Translation in the strict measured sense would require a separate ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency contact on the same rows.  Folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, and universality each require their own layer-matched assay or perturbation contact.  Until such contacts are attached, the result remains a finite controlled mediation decomposition over modeled adaptation weights and matched abundance rows, not a mechanistic realization claim.
