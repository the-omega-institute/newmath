\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the finite mediation surface whose rows join a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$.  The residual coordinate list used in the computation has nine entries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate is not a ribosome-profiling measurement in this row set.  It is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the coding sequence and $w_c$ is the organism-specific tAI weight computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The regression surface residualizes the joined rows against an intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  The rank audit reports $23$ effective control columns, $9$ residual coordinates after control residualization, and one residualized translation column in every organism.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism the computation compares the total residual association $c=R^2(P\sim Q\mid Z)$ with the direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI, $c'=R^2(P\sim Q\mid Z,T)$.  The reported mediation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the value retained only as a statistical variance decomposition.  The finite $M^{QTP}$ list is positive and lies in $[0,1]$ for all twelve organisms: $0.608459$ for \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.487227$ for \emph{C. elegans}, $0.315705$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.616517$ for \emph{D. melanogaster}, $0.612922$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.197690$ for \emph{G. gallus}, $0.272063$ for human, $0.154888$ for mouse, $0.083976$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, $0.174897$ for rat, $0.949155$ for yeast, and $0.018507$ for \emph{S. solfataricus}.  The largest mediated fraction is therefore the yeast value $0.949155$, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$.  The smallest mediated fraction is the archaeal value $0.01850668564589326$, where $c=0.06397514251295629$, $c'=0.06279117466131778$, and $c-c'=0.0011839678516385038$.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation contact.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival computation confirms that the codon-weight contact was finite and nonempty before this protein-abundance mediation row was formed.  In that computation, $61$ sense codons were scored for each of \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast, with no zero raw $W$ fallback in those three modeled readouts.  The bounded modeled-tAI coupling table had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That earlier three-organism contact is explicitly underpowered for a population-level survival claim, since $3$ organisms are not the requested $40$ matched species.  The present twelve-organism mediation table is therefore stronger than the three-organism modeled contact as a protein-abundance decomposition, but it still uses modeled tAI rather than direct ribosome occupancy.

These controls show that the residual-coordinate program has finite contacts outside this mediation table, but they do not convert the present modeled-tAI mediation into a causal or universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported readback is a controlled finite matrix of the form $Q_i\to T\to P$, where $Q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T$ is modeled per-protein tAI, and $P$ is matched log protein abundance.  The positive mediated fractions mean that, in every organism in the joined panel, conditioning on modeled tAI reduces the residual-coordinate association with protein abundance by a recorded amount.  This is enough for a cross-layer statistical relation at the translation-conditioned protein-abundance boundary.  It is not enough for a claim that the residual coordinate has been realized as observed translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, selection, or a global law.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not admitted here as a protein-realization statement unless the route through a translation readout is part of the finite row.  In this chapter the route is present only through modeled tAI, not through ribosome-profiling RPF, measured translation efficiency, perturbational rescue, pulse-chase turnover, localization, post-translational modification, structure, function, or phenotype assays.  Protein abundance is also not isolated from mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational state, folding, or pathway context.  Consequently the chapter records a bounded statistical mediation result: $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals enter a protein-abundance fit through a modeled translation coordinate under the stated controls, while translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universality remain outside the claim unless supplied by separate layer-matched measurements.
