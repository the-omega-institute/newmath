\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Protein-Abundance Mediation for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer surface whose rows have the form $(q_i,T,P)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T$ is a per-protein modeled translation readout, and $P$ is matched measured protein abundance. The coordinate list used in the computation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate is not a ribosome-footprint measurement. It is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c\,w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $n_c$ the CDS codon count and $w_c$ the tAI weight derived from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein coordinate is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts. The control surface $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors; after residualization, the reported control rank is $23$ with $24$ control columns in every organism.

\paragraph{Matched rows and rank sufficiency.}
The powered matrix contains twelve organism-level joins. The joined protein counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In every organism the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the residualized $T$ block has rank $1$, and the joint residualized $(Q,T)$ block has rank $10$. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, so the finite matrix is not the earlier three-organism rank-saturated modeled-tAI boundary. The older bounded modeled-tAI contact across three organisms had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ of $3801961.031607896$, but it carried only modeled coupling and explicitly had organism count $3$ rather than a powered matched-protein surface.

\paragraph{Mediation scalar.}
For each organism, the reported statistic decomposes the residual association between the joint nine-dimensional $Q$ block and protein abundance by conditioning on modeled tAI. Let $R^2_{QP}$ denote the $Z$-residualized direct fit of $P$ from $Q$, and let $R^2_{QP\mid T}$ denote the residual fit of $Q$ after conditioning on $T$. The finite mediation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T}}{R^2_{QP}},
\end{aligned}
$$
whenever the denominator is nonzero. The observed fractions are all in the unit interval: $0.6084594553$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.4872268357$ in worm, $0.3157053954$ in zebrafish, $0.6165167749$ in fly, $0.6129222528$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.1976903965$ in chicken, $0.2720628916$ in human, $0.1548879991$ in mouse, $0.0839760292$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, $0.1748968180$ in rat, $0.9491551041$ in yeast, and $0.0185066856$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}. Thus the largest finite mediation fraction occurs in yeast, where $R^2_{QP}=0.2983762499$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709094$, and the indirect difference is $0.2832053406$. The smallest fraction occurs in \emph{S. solfataricus}, where $R^2_{QP}=0.0639751425$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0627911747$, and the indirect difference is $0.0011839679$.

\paragraph{Finite readback.}
The readback is therefore not a direct $Q\to P$ promotion. It is the controlled matrix $Q\to T\to P$ on matched CDS, tRNA-copy, modeled-tAI, and protein-abundance rows. Several organisms show substantial direct residual-to-protein association before conditioning: mouse has $X$-explained energy on $Y$ equal to $2431.9284360408$, human has $1370.6490494847$, worm has $1262.9081475839$, and zebrafish has $1149.4722117608$. The modeled translation coordinate also explains protein-abundance energy after the same residualization: yeast reports $T$-explained energy on $Y$ equal to $941.8858618614$, mouse $923.4025869376$, human $850.7864327764$, fly $823.3791598585$, and zebrafish $705.5230335367$. These values justify keeping the claim in the cross-layer relation tier as a finite mediation fit rather than reducing it to codon-count geometry alone.

\paragraph{Boundary against overclaim.}
This chapter does not infer observed translation dynamics from modeled tAI, and it does not infer folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, synonymous-edit response, perturbation rescue, or universality from the positive $M^{QTP}$ entries. A separate layer-matched contact is required for each such claim. The same discipline also separates this protein-abundance mediation from two nearby signals: the structural-order audit identified $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ as the only fold-level sign-flip significant axis, with weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502$ and right-tail sign-flip probability $0.03125$; the yeast structure-mediated abundance decomposition reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121$, with $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.0037742485$. Those numbers show that structure and translation-conditioned abundance are distinct finite surfaces, not interchangeable evidence for a common mechanism.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite rows support a ready BioReality statement at the level of statistical cross-layer mediation: the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a controlled association with matched protein abundance, and in every organism part of that association is absorbed by a modeled per-protein tAI coordinate. The claim is strongest as a bounded $Q\to T\to P$ matrix with organism-specific fractions, not as a causal pathway. The admissible conclusion is that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ enters protein-abundance fitting only through this controlled mediation readback; if a future organism or readout gives nonpositive mediated improvement, that row remains at residual, modeled-translation, or direct protein-association status and is not promoted to protein realization.
