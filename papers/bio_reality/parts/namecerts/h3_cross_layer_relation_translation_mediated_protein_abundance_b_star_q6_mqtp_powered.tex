The translation coordinate is not a measured ribosome-footprint variable in this finite run. The protein coordinate is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts.  The fitted control block has $23$ residualized control rank units and $24$ control columns, representing intercept, amino-acid composition, organism-scoped sequence baselines, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline.
