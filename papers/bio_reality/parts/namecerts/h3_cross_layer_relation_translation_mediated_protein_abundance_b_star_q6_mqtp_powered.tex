\subsection{Translation-mediated protein abundance for the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.} The record studied here is a finite surface of triples $(q_i,T_j,P_k)$.  The coordinate side has nine B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual directions: K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and f3\_stress.  The intermediate readout $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream readout $P$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, organism information through the organism-specific panels, and the M-density baseline.  The construction is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  It asks whether a finite residual direction first has a translation-readout route and whether conditioning on that route changes the residual association with matched protein abundance.

\paragraph{Rows and ranks.} The powered matrix used twelve organism panels: Bacillus subtilis subsp. subtilis str. 168 with $4042$ joined proteins, Caenorhabditis elegans with $11661$, Danio rerio with $14410$, Drosophila melanogaster with $13295$, Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 with $3739$, Gallus gallus with $12418$, Homo sapiens with $19053$, Mus musculus with $19395$, Mycobacterium smegmatis str. mc2 155 with $4129$, Rattus norvegicus with $13322$, Saccharomyces cerevisiae with $5815$, and Sulfolobus solfataricus with $1656$.  In every organism panel the residualized design had $9$ B$^{*}_{Q6}$ columns, rank $9$ for $X$, rank $1$ for $T$, rank $10$ for the joint $XT$ design, and $23$ columns in the residualized control span.  The available degrees of freedom after controls were $4019$, $11638$, $14387$, $13272$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $19372$, $4106$, $13299$, $5792$, and $1633$ in the organism order just listed.  Thus the reported mediation surface is not a rank-saturated three-organism artifact: each panel passes the declared rank sufficiency check.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.} For each organism the total residual-to-protein association is recorded as $c=R^2(Q,P\mid Z)$, the direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI as $c'=R^2(Q,P\mid Z,T)$, and the mediated component as $c-c'$.  The scalar fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve organism-level fractions lie in $[0,1]$.  The strongest mediated fraction is in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The next large fractions are Drosophila melanogaster with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 with $0.612922252793667$, Bacillus subtilis with $0.6084594553474505$, and Caenorhabditis elegans with $0.4872268357227724$.  The smaller but still positive mediated fractions are Danio rerio at $0.3157053953614641$, Homo sapiens at $0.27206289160119157$, Gallus gallus at $0.1976903964724546$, Rattus norvegicus at $0.17489681798543982$, Mus musculus at $0.15488799907323988$, Mycobacterium smegmatis at $0.08397602919861291$, and Sulfolobus solfataricus at $0.01850668564589326$.

\paragraph{Energy readback.} The residual energy accounting is consistent with a nonzero translation-conditioned partition rather than a null table.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae the modeled tAI readout explains $941.8858618613751$ units of protein-abundance residual energy, while the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ block explains $882.6526210803958$ units on $Y$ and $5.671703404506426$ units on $T$.  In Homo sapiens the corresponding values are $850.7864327764183$ for $T$ on $Y$, $1370.6490494847067$ for $X$ on $Y$, and $0.3099459586758378$ for $X$ on $T$.  In Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 they are $582.5912438239341$, $432.89050065533684$, and $0.256128714706293$.  These numbers justify reporting a controlled statistical decomposition: $Q$ and $T$ both have finite residual contact with the protein-abundance readout, and the direct $Q\to P$ score decreases after $T$ is included.

\paragraph{Translation readout boundary.} The same residual basis had a separate modeled-tAI survival computation on three organisms.  That earlier contact computed the same nine coordinates, used $61$ sense codons, and produced $81$ modeled entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  It also recorded that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the result is modeled-tAI bounded coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  The present twelve-organism protein-abundance table improves the row count and rank condition for the protein-abundance mediation problem, but it still uses modeled tAI rather than measured ribosome occupancy.  The status is therefore a finite cross-layer statistical mediation surface, not an observed ribosome-dynamics mechanism.

\paragraph{Neighboring checks.} Several nearby finite rows keep the interpretation bounded.  A yeast structure-mediation audit reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$ and structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$.  A structural-order axis audit found f3\_stress as the sole significant axis, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit was negative for the strong imprint claim: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and positive selection imprint false.  The dynamic mass-balance audit was also not a positive promotion route, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit was negative beyond its controls, with $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.

\paragraph{Allowed conclusion.} The finite conclusion is that the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has a reproducible controlled statistical route through modeled translation adaptation into matched protein abundance in the twelve organism panels.  The strongest case is yeast, but the positive mediated component is not confined to yeast: all twelve organism panels have $M^{QTP}\in[0,1]$ and positive $c-c'$.  This supports the narrow statement that the residual-to-protein association can be decomposed by adding the modeled tAI readout as an intermediate coordinate.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} No stronger biological conclusion is licensed by this table alone.  Modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling, and PAXdb abundance is not a direct measurement of translation rate, folding, mature function, localization, turnover, perturbational rescue, organismal phenotype, or evolutionary mechanism.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not treated as protein realization unless the corresponding $Q_i\to T_j$ step is present and the conditioning calculation is reported.  No claim about translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, or universality follows without a separate layer-matched measurement contact and its own finite statistic.
