\subsection{Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Decomposition for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{subsec:biorreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$: $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The coordinate list used in the powered protein-abundance run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, f_3\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this claim the translation variable is the per-protein mean modelled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The controls residualized before the mediation calculation are amino-acid composition, organism-scoped sequence covariates represented by the row block, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline; the rank diagnostics report $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for $Z$, rank $9$ for the residual coordinate block, rank $1$ for the residualized translation coordinate, and rank $10$ for the combined $X,T$ block in every organism-specific fit.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank sufficiency.}
The powered surface contains twelve organism-level joined panels.  The row counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each panel is marked rank-sufficient, with residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ ranging from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse.  The tRNA-copy totals used for the tAI calculation range from $47$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} and $49$ in \emph{M. smegmatis} to $8874$ in zebrafish; zero raw wobble weights required geometric-mean filling in only four panels: $3$ codons in \emph{B. subtilis}, $1$ in \emph{E. coli}, $1$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $1$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
The computed scalar is the joint nine-dimensional $X_Q$ mediation fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T$ is the modelled tAI coordinate and $P$ is matched protein abundance.  The organism-specific fractions all lie in the unit interval.  The strongest mediated fraction is in yeast, with $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.2983762499$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709094$, and indirect difference $0.2832053406$.  The next largest fractions are \emph{D. melanogaster} at $0.6165167749$, \emph{E. coli} at $0.6129222528$, and \emph{B. subtilis} at $0.6084594553$.  Intermediate panels include \emph{C. elegans} at $0.4872268357$, zebrafish at $0.3157053954$, human at $0.2720628916$, chicken at $0.1976903965$, rat at $0.1748968180$, and mouse at $0.1548879991$.  The smallest positive fractions are \emph{M. smegmatis} at $0.0839760292$ and \emph{S. solfataricus} at $0.0185066856$.  Thus the finite result is not a single-sign universal law; it is a bounded, organism-indexed statistical decomposition in which conditioning on the modelled translation coordinate reduces the direct residual-to-protein association in every joined panel.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-readout survival.}
A separate modelled-tAI survival calculation supplies the lower-layer translation contact used to keep the interpretation bounded.  That calculation used three organisms, computed the same nine residual coordinates, and found $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$.  It also records that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the object is modelled-tAI bounded coupling rather than ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  The present twelve-panel protein-abundance calculation therefore has a different status: it is powered for the joined CDS, tRNA, and PAXdb abundance matrix, but its translation step is still modelled tAI, not directly observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Comparison with an independent yeast decomposition.}
The yeast structure-mediation audit gives a useful local calibration of scale without changing the present claim.  In the five-way yeast join there are $4759$ genes, and the residualized association from $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein abundance has $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$.  Conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability lowers that to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, whereas conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE structure proxy alone leaves $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.0032845645$, while the structure-incremental fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability is $0.0005507779$.  This comparison is consistent with the present chapter's interpretation: the relevant finite readout is a translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition, not a folded-structure result.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible statement is that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a controlled, finite-row association with matched protein abundance that is reduced after conditioning on modelled per-protein tAI.  The result does not by itself show observed ribosome occupancy, translation causation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary selection, perturbation rescue, or a universal biological rule.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not promoted unless the same packet keeps the intervening translation readout explicit, and a stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact supplying the missing measurement.
