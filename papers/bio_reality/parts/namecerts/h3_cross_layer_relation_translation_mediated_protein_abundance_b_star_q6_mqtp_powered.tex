\subsection{Modeled Translation Mediation of Protein Abundance}\label{sec:bior-bstar-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}\label{par:bior-bstar-q6-mqtp-surface}
This record tests a bounded cross-layer question: whether a finite family of synonymous residual coordinates can be read through a translation-weight surface before reaching matched protein abundance.  The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each organism, the translation coordinate $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_g = \frac{\sum_c n_{g,c} w_c}{\sum_c n_{g,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_{g,c}$ is the real CDS codon count for protein $g$ and $w_c$ is the GtRNAdb all-tRNA-copy, dos Reis wobble-weight codon score.  The downstream readout $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The reported mediation surface is therefore not a direct $Q \to Y$ assertion; it is the finite matrix-level decomposition of the residual association after conditioning on the modeled translation coordinate $T$.

\paragraph{Joined finite rows and controls.}\label{par:bior-bstar-q6-mqtp-rows-controls}
The powered run joined twelve organism-specific CDS, tRNA, and protein-abundance tables.  The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The control design used $24$ columns before residualization, with rank $23$ for $Z$ in every organism and rank $9$ for the residualized $Q$ block.  The controlled covariates were amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the support of the nine $Q$ vectors; the decomposition is therefore a residual protein-abundance calculation rather than a codon-count-only fit.

\paragraph{Mediation decomposition.}\label{par:bior-bstar-q6-mqtp-decomposition}
The organism-level decomposition reports the total residual association $c=R^2(Q,Y)$, the direct association after the modeled translation readout $c'=R^2(Q,Y\mid T)$, and the nonnegative attenuation $c-c'$.  The mediated fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} = (&0.6084594553,\ 0.4872268357,\ 0.3157053954,\ 0.6165167749,\\
&0.6129222528,\ 0.1976903965,\ 0.2720628916,\ 0.1548879991,\\
&0.0839760292,\ 0.1748968180,\ 0.9491551041,\ 0.0185066856),
\end{aligned}
$$
listed in the same organism order as the joined rows above.  All twelve fractions were inside $[0,1]$.  The largest attenuation was in yeast, where $c=0.2983762499$, $c'=0.0151709094$, and $c-c'=0.2832053406$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041$.  The smallest mediated fraction was in \textit{S. solfataricus}, where $c=0.0639751425$, $c'=0.0627911747$, and $c-c'=0.0011839679$.  Other positive attenuation examples include \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655 with $c=0.1076752668$, $c'=0.0416786997$, $c-c'=0.0659965671$, and \textit{D. melanogaster} with $c=0.0770952887$, $c'=0.0295647500$, $c-c'=0.0475305388$.

\paragraph{Rank and readout diagnostics.}\label{par:bior-bstar-q6-mqtp-rank-diagnostics}
The rank diagnostics are nondegenerate for the declared finite surface: every organism has rank $1$ for the residualized modeled tAI column, rank $10$ for the joint residualized $Q,T$ block, rank $9$ for $Q$ as used to explain $T$, and the powered-rank flag is true.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ range from $1633$ in \textit{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse.  The modeled tAI readout itself was finite in every organism; the zero raw $W$ entries filled by geometric mean were $3$ for \textit{B. subtilis}, $1$ for \textit{E. coli}, $1$ for \textit{M. smegmatis}, $1$ for \textit{S. solfataricus}, and $0$ for the remaining eight organisms.  A separate modeled-tAI survival audit had only $3$ organisms and therefore explicitly refused powered survival, but it still found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute modeled coupling of $3801961.031607896$.  That earlier audit is useful as a translation-weight sanity check, not as ribosome-profiling validation.

\paragraph{Readback and local boundary.}\label{par:bior-bstar-q6-mqtp-boundary}
The finite result supports a narrow statement: in the joined PAXdb/CDS/tRNA rows, the joint nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a protein-abundance association whose fitted residual strength is reduced after conditioning on a modeled per-protein tAI coordinate.  This is a statistical attenuation pattern on a finite matched surface.  It does not show a causal translation mechanism, does not observe ribosome occupancy, and does not isolate mature protein abundance from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational state, folding, pathway context, or measurement bias.  A direct $Q \to Y$ association is therefore not counted as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives an admissible translation readout.  No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection law, or universality follows from this chapter without a separate layer-matched contact and a corresponding controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Relation to the yeast structure-mediated abundance audit.}\label{par:bior-bstar-q6-mqtp-yeast-comparison}
The yeast structure audit gives an internal consistency check on the protein-abundance side without changing the claim.  In a five-way yeast join of $4759$ genes, the residual $Q \to P$ association was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$; conditioning on 5-prime CDS MFE alone gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505$, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, and conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.0032845645$ and the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507779$.  This comparison is consistent with the present chapter's restraint: translation-linked and abundance-linked regressions can be finite and positive, while structure, function, and causal mechanism remain outside the established claim unless a separate readout supplies them.
