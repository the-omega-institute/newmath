\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows join three typed coordinates: a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$.  The residual side has nine computed coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation column used in the powered mediation run is not ribosome profiling.  It is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(g)=\frac{\sum_c n_{g,c} w_c}{\sum_c n_{g,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_{g,c}$ is the codon count for coding sequence $g$ and $w_c$ is the codon weight computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein column is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The module is therefore a cross-layer relation only: it measures whether the residual-to-protein association is reduced after a modeled translation-readout column is placed between them.

\paragraph{Controls and rank admissibility.}
Every organism-specific surface residualizes the same control family $Z$: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the powered run the effective control rank is $23$ in every organism, with $24$ control columns before rank compression.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are organism-specific: $4019$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168, $11638$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14387$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13272$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3716$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12395$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19030$ for human, $19372$ for mouse, $4106$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13299$ for rat, $5792$ for yeast, and $1633$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In all twelve organisms $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde{X})=9$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde{T})=1$, and $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde{X},\widetilde{T})=10$, so the mediation statistic is not a rank-empty fit.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism the reported scalar is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)},
\end{aligned}
$$
computed after $Z$ residualization for the joint nine-dimensional $Q$ block. Yeast is the largest observed fraction in this panel, but that does not make yeast a population law: it is one organism in a twelve-organism finite table, and its modeled-tAI residual energy is unusually high at $6.9053788824$ compared with $0.0170550261$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} and $0.0411915763$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}.  The finite conclusion is only that modeled translation-readout conditioning absorbs a positive share of the joint $B^{*}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance association in every joined organism in the table.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled-translation boundary.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival contact computed residual coupling from only three organisms, with $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$. The present surface is larger and protein joined, but it still inherits the same caution for the translation column: modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation annotation derived from tRNA gene-copy information, not an observation of ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The result is not a causal mediation claim.  It is a finite statistical decomposition of $Q\to P$ after conditioning on a modeled $T$ column.  It does not by itself establish observed translation, ribosome dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation rescue, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association remains insufficient for protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a translation-readout step supplied by a separate layer-matched contact.  Likewise, amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density remain controls in this record, not promoted coordinates.  The admissible reading is therefore a controlled matrix statement: within the finite joined rows, the joint nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block has a positive protein-abundance association whose fitted magnitude is reduced after modeled tAI is included, with the fraction of reduction recorded organism by organism.
