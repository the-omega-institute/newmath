\subsection{Translation-mediated protein abundance for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{subsec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record studies a finite mediation surface whose rows join three kinds of quantities: a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-layer readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $Y$.  The implemented surface uses nine residual coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\texttt{K\_AAA},\texttt{Arg\_AGR},\texttt{Ile\_AUA},\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\texttt{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each coding sequence row, $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as the codon-count-weighted average of codon tAI weights from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream response $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\text{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The row construction therefore tests a bounded statistical question: whether the residual coordinates improve a protein-abundance fit in a way that is partly absorbed when the modeled translation readout is included.

\paragraph{Matched organisms and rank condition.}
Six organism-level joined matrices were available.  The joined protein counts were $14410$ for \texttt{danio\_rerio}, $3739$ for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $12418$ for \texttt{gallus\_gallus}, $19053$ for \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, $4129$ for \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, and $5815$ for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}.  The corresponding tRNA copy totals used by the modeled tAI readout were $8874$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $49$, and $275$.  Two organisms required one zero raw codon weight to be filled by the geometric-mean rule: \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655} and \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}; the remaining four required no such fill.

The residualization control block used $24$ control columns, with residualized control rank $23$ in every organism.  After residualization, the remaining degrees of freedom were $14387$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively.  In each organism, the residualized design had rank $9$ for the $Q$ block, rank $1$ for the translation readout, and rank $10$ for the joint $Q,T$ block, so the reported mediation scalar is not a rank-saturated artifact under the declared degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Controlled mediation statistic.}
Let $X_Q$ denote the nine-dimensional residual-coordinate matrix after the amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance controls have been removed from the relevant response columns.  Let $T$ denote modeled per-protein tAI after the same control projection, and let $Y$ denote residualized log protein abundance.  The finite-row statistic compares the total $Q$ association with protein abundance to the remaining direct association after conditioning on $T$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This number is only a decomposition of cross-sectional explained variance.  It is not a perturbation effect, it is not a causal path coefficient, and it is not an observed ribosome-dynamics measurement.

The computed total $Q\to P$ $R^2$ values were $0.046135$ for \texttt{danio\_rerio}, $0.107675$ for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $0.039808$ for \texttt{gallus\_gallus}, $0.044546$ for \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, $0.032090$ for \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, and $0.298376$ for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}.  After conditioning on modeled tAI, the direct $Q\to P\mid T$ $R^2$ values were $0.031570$, $0.041679$, $0.031938$, $0.032426$, $0.029395$, and $0.015171$.  The resulting indirect differences were $0.014565$, $0.065997$, $0.007870$, $0.012119$, $0.002695$, and $0.283205$.  These yield mediated fractions $0.315705$, $0.612922$, $0.197690$, $0.272063$, $0.083976$, and $0.949155$, all inside the unit interval.

\paragraph{Energy audit.}
The same computation reports the residual energy scale on both the modeled translation readout and the protein-abundance response.  The residual protein-abundance energies were $24915.244072$, $4020.333672$, $18586.777684$, $30769.536715$, $5729.194538$, and $2958.186589$.  The residual modeled-tAI energies were $0.574773$, $0.472847$, $0.724892$, $1.174469$, $0.041192$, and $6.905379$.  The $X_Q$ block explained translation-readout energy $0.219288$, $0.256129$, $0.174822$, $0.309946$, $0.026188$, and $5.671703$ in the same organism order.  Its direct improvement on protein abundance after $T$ was $786.577633$, $167.562280$, $593.629883$, $997.746306$, $168.411353$, and $44.878381$, while the incremental contribution of $T$ after $X_Q$ was $342.628454$, $317.263023$, $133.550490$, $477.883689$, $0.374523$, and $104.111621$.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result supports a narrow cross-layer relation: in the six joined matrices, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block has a nonzero association with matched protein abundance, and a positive fraction of that association is absorbed by the modeled per-protein tAI coordinate.  The strongest fractional absorption occurs in \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, where $M^{QTP}=0.949155$ and the indirect $R^2$ difference is $0.283205$; the weakest occurs in \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, where $M^{QTP}=0.083976$ and the indirect difference is $0.002695$.  The result is therefore not a single-sign population law, and it does not justify promoting any one coordinate, such as a leucine CUN/UUR contrast, into a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The readout used here is modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights, not ribosome profiling.  The protein endpoint is steady-state abundance, not folding state, molecular function, localization, turnover, physical admissibility, phenotype, or mechanism.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is not treated as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a translation-readout step, and even then it remains a controlled statistical association unless a separate experiment supplies the layer-specific contact.  No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, function claim, causal rescue claim, perturbation claim, or universal law follows from this matrix alone.
