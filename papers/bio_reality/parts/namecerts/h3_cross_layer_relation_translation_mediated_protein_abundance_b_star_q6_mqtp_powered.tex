\subsection{Modeled-tAI mediation of $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals and matched protein abundance}\label{subsec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.} The record is a finite mediation surface whose rows join one $B^*_{Q6}$ residual vector, one modeled translation readout, and one matched protein-abundance measurement for the same coding sequence. The residual side has nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(g)=\frac{\sum\_c n\_c(g)w\_c}{\sum\_c n\_c(g)},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n\_c(g)$ is the coding-sequence codon count and $w\_c$ is computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein coordinate is $Y(g)=\log\_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm}(g))$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts. The joined organism panel contains $12$ organisms and $128{,}977$ protein rows in total: $4{,}042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11{,}661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14{,}410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13{,}295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3{,}739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12{,}418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19{,}053$ for human, $19{,}395$ for mouse, $4{,}129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, $13{,}322$ for rat, $5{,}815$ for yeast, and $1{,}656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. The tRNA-copy totals used in the same readout range from $47$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $8{,}874$ in \emph{D. rerio}; zero raw wobble weights were filled by a geometric mean in four organisms, with counts $3$, $1$, $1$, and $1$ for \emph{B. subtilis}, \emph{E. coli}, \emph{M. smegmatis}, and \emph{S. solfataricus}, respectively.

\paragraph{Controlled regression shape.} Each organism is evaluated after residualizing by a control block $Z$ containing amino-acid composition, organism scope, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance controls where applicable. In every organism the residualized design is rank sufficient: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, the reported control-column count is $24$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ range from $1{,}633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19{,}372$ in mouse, and all reported mediation fractions lie in the unit interval. This rules out the small-panel rank saturation seen in the older three-organism modeled-tAI check: that earlier contact used only $3$ organisms, although it did compute all $61$ sense-codon modeled tAI weights for \emph{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast and found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$. The present surface is therefore a protein-row mediation decomposition, not a species-count survival claim.

\paragraph{Mediation numbers.} The decomposition compares the total residual-to-protein association $c$ with the direct association $c'$ left after conditioning on $T$. The indirect component is $c-c'$, and the reported fraction is $M^{QTP}=(c-c')/c$ when the fraction is defined. The largest mediated fraction is in yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. Strong but smaller fractions occur in \emph{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, and \emph{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$. Intermediate entries include \emph{C. elegans} at $0.4872268357227724$, \emph{D. rerio} at $0.3157053953614641$, human at $0.27206289160119157$, chicken at $0.1976903964724546$, rat at $0.17489681798543982$, and mouse at $0.15488799907323988$. The smallest positive fractions are \emph{M. smegmatis} at $0.08397602919861291$ and \emph{S. solfataricus} at $0.01850668564589326$. Thus every organism in the finite panel has a positive statistical attenuation when modeled tAI is inserted between the nine-dimensional $Q$ block and protein abundance, but the strength is highly heterogeneous.

\paragraph{Energy audit.} The organism-level energy decomposition gives a second check that the surface is not a direct $Q\to P$ promotion. Yeast has the largest $X$-explained energy on $T$, $5.671703404506426$, and the largest $T$ residual energy, $6.90537888244409$, together with $T$-explained energy on $Y$ equal to $941.8858618613751$. Mouse has the largest protein residual energy, $48{,}806.47856410273$, and the largest direct $X$ improvement given $T$, $2{,}055.2519066931027$, while still having a positive mediated fraction of only $0.15488799907323988$. The archaeal row is the weakest mediated entry: \emph{S. solfataricus} has $t$ residual energy $0.01705502608336432$, $T$ incremental improvement given $X$ equal to $0.5952760091944356$, and indirect $R^2$ only $0.0011839678516385038$. These values support a bounded finite-row statement: modeled tAI can absorb part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association in each joined organism, but the amount absorbed varies by organism and by the residual energy available in $T$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.} The chapter records a controlled cross-layer association of the form $Q\to T\to P$, where $T$ is modeled per-protein tAI and $P$ is matched steady-state protein abundance. A direct $Q\to P$ association is not accepted as protein realization unless the translation-readout coordinate is explicitly present and the conditioning calculation is reported. Here that calculation is present and rank sufficient, so the finite statement is ready at the level of statistical mediation over matched CDS/protein rows. The result does not establish observed ribosome occupancy, translation causality, synonymous-edit response, perturbation rescue, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality. Modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation readout built from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights, not ribo-seq; PAXdb abundance is steady-state protein abundance, not an isolated measurement of synthesis, turnover, localization, PTM state, structure, or function. Any claim at those layers requires a separate matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
