\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates can pass from codon-composition memory to a downstream protein-abundance readout only through an explicitly named translation readout. The finite surface has triples $(q_i,T,Y)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\},
\end{aligned}
$$
$T$ is the per-protein modeled tAI score, and $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts. The translation coordinate is computed as a per-protein codon-count average of tAI weights,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The finite joined panels contain $3739$ proteins for escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655, $4129$ proteins for mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155, and $5815$ proteins for saccharomyces\_cerevisiae. The corresponding tRNA copy totals used by the modeled tAI readout are $90$, $49$, and $275$, with zero raw codon-weight fallback counts $1$, $1$, and $0$ respectively.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
The protein-abundance readout is not tested against the raw residual coordinates alone. Each organism is first residualized against a control block containing amino-acid composition, organism-level design terms, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance controls where available. The fitted control matrix has $24$ columns, numerical rank $23$, and leaves residual degrees of freedom $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$ in the three organisms. After this control step, the residual ranks are stable: $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X,T)=10$ in every organism. Thus the mediation calculation is not a one-row or rank-saturated spelling artifact; it is a finite ranked regression surface with a nine-dimensional residual-coordinate block and one modeled translation coordinate.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the calculation compares the total controlled $Q\to P$ association with the remaining direct association after conditioning on the modeled translation readout. In the notation of this chapter, $c$ denotes the controlled total $R^2$ of the nine-coordinate $Q$ block on protein abundance, $c'$ denotes the controlled direct $R^2$ after adding $T$, and $M^{QTP}=(c-c')/c$ is the statistical mediated fraction when the denominator is positive. The finite values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{E.coli}:\quad &c=0.10767526677985896,\quad c'=0.04167869969498872,\\
&M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667,\quad c-c'=0.06599656708487024,\\
\mathrm{M.smegmatis}:\quad &c=0.03209009170649934,\quad c'=0.029395293228368185,\\
&M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291,\quad c-c'=0.0026947984781311544,\\
\mathrm{S.cerevisiae}:\quad &c=0.29837624994140827,\quad c'=0.0151709093608293,\\
&M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973,\quad c-c'=0.28320534058057895.
\end{aligned}
$$
All three mediated fractions lie in the unit interval. The strongest finite entry is saccharomyces\_cerevisiae, where the modeled translation coordinate accounts for nearly all of the controlled $Q\to P$ association under this decomposition. The weakest entry is mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155, where the mediated fraction is positive but small. The escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655 panel is intermediate and still has a substantial positive indirect component.

\paragraph{Energy audit.}
The same conclusion is visible in the residual-energy accounting. In escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655, the residual protein-abundance energy is $4020.333671802061$; the $Q$ block explains $432.89050065533684$ on $Y$, the modeled translation readout explains $582.5912438239341$ on $Y$, and the incremental improvement of $T$ after $X$ is $317.26302294928666$. In mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155, the residual protein-abundance energy is $5729.194538214237$; $Q$ explains $183.85037813566998$, $T$ explains $15.813547327443837$, and the incremental improvement of $T$ after $X$ is only $0.37452260494680445$. In saccharomyces\_cerevisiae, the residual protein-abundance energy is $2958.1865890925337$; $Q$ explains $882.6526210803958$, $T$ explains $941.8858618613751$, and $T$ after $X$ contributes $104.11162139652299$. The residual translation energies are $0.4728468725814029$, $0.041191576348982066$, and $6.90537888244409$, while the $Q$ block explains $0.256128714706293$, $0.026187928294862578$, and $5.671703404506426$ on $T$ respectively. These numbers support a translation-conditioned statistical decomposition, not a direct promotion from synonymous residuals to protein realization.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled translation contact.}
A separate modeled-tAI contact establishes that the codon-adaptation readout can be computed from complete standard tRNA family coverage in the local GtRNAdb payload for three organisms. In that contact, all $61$ sense codons receive modeled tAI weights for escherichia\_coli\_k12, homo\_sapiens, and saccharomyces\_cerevisiae, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ at the codon-weight level. The bounded coupling screen reports $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$. That earlier finite panel is still explicitly modeled and organism-limited: $3$ organisms are not enough for a broad powered survival law over species. The present chapter therefore uses the modeled translation coordinate as an intermediate readout in matched CDS/protein rows, not as an observed ribosome-profiling measurement.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter-level verdict is positive for a finite statistical mediation surface. At least one, and in fact all three, organism panels have positive $c-c'$ after the declared controls, with powered rank sufficiency in the fitted matrices and with $M^{QTP}$ remaining inside $[0,1]$. This permits the following narrow statement: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a controlled association with matched protein abundance that is partly absorbed by modeled per-protein tAI in these finite joined panels. The statement is deliberately narrower than a biological mechanism. It does not say that synonymous residuals cause abundance changes, that ribosome occupancy has been observed, or that a perturbation would reproduce the same path.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
No claim about translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this record by itself. Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is not ribosome profiling. Conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional CDS/protein-abundance rows is not a proof of translation mechanism. Protein abundance is not separated here from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, pathway context, or perturbational rescue. A direct $Q\to P$ association is not admitted as protein realization unless the same finite surface also contains the translation step $Q\to T$ and the downstream attenuation pattern $Q\to T\to P$ under the controls. Higher-layer claims require their own matched measurements and their own finite statistics.
