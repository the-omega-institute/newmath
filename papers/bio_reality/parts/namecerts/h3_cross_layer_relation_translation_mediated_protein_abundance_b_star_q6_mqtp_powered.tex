\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This BioReality record fixes a finite mediation surface whose rows are matched triples $(q_i,T,P)$: a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-layer readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$. The coordinate side contains the nine computed residual axes
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q=\{&\texttt{K\_AAA},\texttt{Arg\_AGR},\texttt{Ile\_AUA},\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\texttt{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate used in the finite computation is per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(g)=\frac{\sum_c n_c(g)w_c}{\sum_c n_c(g)},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c(g)$ is the real CDS codon count for gene or protein row $g$, and $w_c$ is the GtRNAdb all-tRNA-copy and dos Reis wobble-weight tAI weight for codon $c$. The protein readout is $P(g)=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to the same CDS codon-count rows. This is therefore a finite cross-layer matrix over real CDS rows, modeled tAI rows, and matched protein-abundance rows, not an unrestricted biological law.

\paragraph{Rows and controls.}
The computation joined twelve organism-scoped panels. The joined protein-row counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In every organism, the residualized design used $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for the control matrix, rank $9$ for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block after control removal, rank $1$ for the residualized modeled-tAI readout, and rank $10$ for the combined residual coordinate plus tAI design. The controls include amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, an $M$-density baseline on the projected $B^*_{Q6}$ support, and organism-scoped normalization. All reported mediation values are after this residualization.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the finite audit compares the total residual coordinate-to-protein fit with the remaining direct fit after modeled tAI is added. In notation, $c=R^2(P\sim Q\mid Z)$ and $c'=R^2(P\sim Q\mid Z,T)$ on the residualized surface, and the reported mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c}
\end{aligned}
$$
whenever the denominator is positive and the value lies in the unit interval. This statistic is a variance-partition readback of the finite matrix. It is not a causal effect and it is not a claim that tAI is an observed ribosome-occupancy trace.

\paragraph{Finite results.}
All twelve organism panels returned a positive indirect component $c-c'$ and a mediated fraction inside $[0,1]$. The strongest mediated fraction was in \emph{S. cerevisiae}, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. The bacterial and metazoan panels also gave positive mediated fractions: \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 had $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, $c-c'=0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$; \emph{D. melanogaster} had $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$ with $c-c'=0.047530538755721025$; \emph{B. subtilis} had $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$ with $c-c'=0.037667440654802556$; and \emph{C. elegans} had $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$ with $c-c'=0.04978438377417191$. The remaining panels were positive but smaller: \emph{D. rerio} $M^{QTP}=0.3157053953614641$, \emph{H. sapiens} $0.27206289160119157$, \emph{G. gallus} $0.1976903964724546$, \emph{R. norvegicus} $0.17489681798543982$, \emph{M. musculus} $0.15488799907323988$, \emph{M. smegmatis} $0.08397602919861291$, and \emph{S. solfataricus} $0.01850668564589326$.

\paragraph{Rank and residual-energy checks.}
The finite-row computation is not a rank artifact under its own design criterion: every organism has \texttt{powered\_rank\_sufficient=true}. The residual degrees of freedom after the control block range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in \emph{M. musculus}. The modeled-tAI residual energy is small relative to protein-abundance residual energy in many panels, but nonzero: examples include $t$-residual energy $6.90537888244409$ and protein residual energy $2958.1865890925337$ in yeast, $0.4728468725814029$ and $4020.333671802061$ in \emph{E. coli}, and $1.174468648693672$ and $30769.5367150478$ in human. The corresponding residual coordinate energy explained on modeled tAI is $5.671703404506426$ in yeast, $0.256128714706293$ in \emph{E. coli}, and $0.3099459586758378$ in human. These numbers justify reading the result as a finite controlled matrix decomposition rather than as a statement about one coordinate sign or one organism-specific direction.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The supported statement is narrow. In the finite panels tested here, the joint nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block carries protein-abundance association, and conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI removes a positive fraction of that association in every joined organism panel. Thus the cross-layer record is allowed to say that the residual-to-protein association has a modeled translation-readout mediation component on this finite surface. It is not allowed to say that any single synonymous coordinate realizes translation, folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality. A direct $Q\to P$ association is not promoted by itself; the allowable readback is the controlled sequence $Q\to T\to P$ with the translation step represented here only by modeled tAI.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The computation uses modeled codon-adaptation weights and PAXdb abundance rows, not ribosome profiling, pulse labeling, perturbation rescue, folding assays, localization assays, turnover measurements, or functional measurements. Therefore no observed translation dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, selection law, phenotype, or global rule follows from this chapter. Each such assertion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic. Within the present data, the correct conclusion is a controlled statistical mediation result for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates through modeled per-protein tAI into matched protein abundance.
