\subsection{Translation-mediated protein abundance from $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{subsec:bior-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.} This record tests whether synonymous residual coordinates from the $B^*_{Q6}$ basis can enter protein-abundance fitting only through a translation readout. The finite surface consists of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of nine residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a matched translation-layer scalar, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance scalar. The computed residual list is $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$. The translation readout used here is per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI over the coding sequence. The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb abundance rows joined to real CDS codon counts. The row therefore does not test a direct $q_i \to p_k$ promotion; it tests whether conditioning through a translation scalar changes the residual-to-protein association after the declared controls are removed.

\paragraph{Joined finite rows and controls.} Three organism-level joined tables were available. The Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 table used 3739 joined proteins with the Escherichia coli tRNA-copy contact; the Mycobacterium smegmatis str. mc2 155 table used 4129 joined proteins with its matched tRNA-copy contact; and the Saccharomyces cerevisiae table used 5815 joined proteins with the Saccharomyces tRNA-copy contact. The modeled tAI construction used all-tRNA copy totals of 90, 49, and 275 respectively. One zero raw codon weight was filled by the geometric mean in each of the bacterial tables, and none was filled in the yeast table. Each regression residualized against a control block of 24 columns with rank $23$, including amino-acid composition, organism-scoped baseline terms, GC3, CDS length, and M-density controls; matched mRNA abundance remains a control route rather than a promoted coordinate. The residual degrees of freedom after the control block were 3716, 4106, and 5792. In all three tables the powered-rank condition held: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde T)=10$, and the degenerate-$R^2$ cutoff was $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Mediation decomposition.} The finite statistic is the joint nine-coordinate mediation scalar $M^{QTP}$, computed from the residual-to-protein association before and after conditioning on modeled tAI. With $c$ denoting the total residual-to-protein $R^2$ and $c'$ the direct residual-to-protein $R^2$ after $T$ is included, the reported fraction is
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}$$
For Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, the indirect difference is $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$. The residual energy of the translation scalar was $0.4728468725814029$, the residual energy of protein abundance was $4020.333671802061$, $X$ explained $0.256128714706293$ units of energy on $T$, and $T$ added $317.26302294928666$ units of protein-abundance energy after $X$. For Mycobacterium smegmatis, $c=0.03209009170649934$, $c'=0.029395293228368185$, the indirect difference is $0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$. Its translation residual energy was $0.041191576348982066$, protein-abundance residual energy was $5729.194538214237$, $X$ explained $0.026187928294862578$ units on $T$, and $T$ added $0.37452260494680445$ units after $X$. For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. Its translation residual energy was $6.90537888244409$, protein-abundance residual energy was $2958.1865890925337$, $X$ explained $5.671703404506426$ units on $T$, and $T$ added $104.11162139652299$ units after $X$. All three $M^{QTP}$ fractions lie in the unit interval, so the finite row supports a bounded statistical mediation reading rather than a malformed decomposition.

\paragraph{Translation-contact status.} The translation readout is modeled tAI from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. A separate modeled-tAI survival run covered three organisms and computed 61 sense-codon weights in each organism with zero raw fallback count $0$ at the codon-weight stage. The local tRNA payloads contained all 20 standard amino-acid families in Escherichia coli K-12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with FASTA header counts 89, 432, and 275 respectively. The modeled coupling matrix had 81 entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. That result supplies bounded modeled coupling, not measured ribosome occupancy or a multi-organism powered survival law; the organism count was $3$, below the stated $40$-species requirement for powered survival.

\paragraph{Local reading.} The finite result is positive for the narrow cross-layer question: at least one joint $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a nonzero protein-abundance association whose fitted share is absorbed by a modeled translation scalar in all three joined tables. The strongest mediated fraction is in Saccharomyces cerevisiae, where the direct residual-to-protein $R^2$ falls from $0.29837624994140827$ to $0.0151709093608293$ after modeled tAI is included. Escherichia coli K-12 also shows a substantial reduction, while Mycobacterium smegmatis shows a small but positive mediated component. This is enough for a controlled, finite cross-layer relation at the level of statistical decomposition. It is not enough to claim an observed translation process, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation response, evolutionary selection, organismal phenotype, or universal biological rule. Those claims would require their own layer-matched contacts, and a direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association remains inadmissible when the $Q_i \to T_j$ step is absent, null, or untested.
