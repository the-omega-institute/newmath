\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance surface for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record evaluates a finite cross-layer surface whose rows join coding-sequence residual coordinates, a translation readout, and matched protein-abundance values. The residual block has nine coordinates,
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The translation coordinate is not an observed ribosome-footprint measurement in this run. It is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$T=\sum_c n_c w_c/\sum_c n_c$, computed from codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights. The protein coordinate is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance after joining to real CDS codon counts. Thus the chapter supports only a bounded statistical decomposition of $Q\to T\to Y$ on finite joined rows; it does not by itself establish a ribosome-measured mechanism.

\paragraph{Rows and ranks.}
The joined panel spans twelve organisms. The analysis used 4042 proteins in \textit{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, 11661 in \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, 14410 in \textit{Danio rerio}, 13295 in \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, 3739 in \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, 12418 in \textit{Gallus gallus}, 19053 in \textit{Homo sapiens}, 19395 in \textit{Mus musculus}, 4129 in \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, 13322 in \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, 5815 in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and 1656 in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. For every organism the residualized design retained rank $9$ for $X_Q$, rank $1$ for $T$, and rank $10$ for the joint $X_Q,T$ block after a rank-$23$ control matrix. The control block has 24 columns, matching intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline. Residual degrees of freedom after controls range from 1633 in \textit{S. solfataricus} to 19372 in \textit{M. musculus}; every organism is marked rank-sufficient for the fitted decomposition.

\paragraph{Mediation scalar.}
For each organism the registered scalar is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve fitted fractions lie in the unit interval. The largest mediated fraction is in \textit{S. cerevisiae}, where $R^2(Q\to P)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect component is $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. The next largest entries are \textit{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655 with $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$, and \textit{B. subtilis} with $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$. Midrange entries include \textit{C. elegans} at $0.4872268357227724$, \textit{D. rerio} at $0.3157053953614641$, and \textit{H. sapiens} at $0.27206289160119157$. The smallest entries are \textit{M. musculus} at $0.15488799907323988$, \textit{R. norvegicus} at $0.17489681798543982$, \textit{M. smegmatis} at $0.08397602919861291$, and \textit{S. solfataricus} at $0.01850668564589326$.

\paragraph{Direct and mediated components.}
The fitted surface is not a single-sign assertion about one species. It records a cross-organism list of finite decompositions. For \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655, the total controlled residual association is $0.10767526677985896$, the direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI is $0.04167869969498872$, and the indirect component is $0.06599656708487024$. For \textit{C. elegans}, the corresponding values are $0.10217906758013379$, $0.05239468380596188$, and $0.04978438377417191$. For \textit{D. melanogaster}, they are $0.0770952887136557$, $0.02956474995793468$, and $0.047530538755721025$. In the smallest archaeal row, \textit{S. solfataricus}, the total is $0.06397514251295629$, the direct term is $0.06279117466131778$, and the modeled-tAI-mediated component is only $0.0011839678516385038$. This contrast is important: the same construction can yield a large modeled mediation fraction in yeast and a nearly direct protein-abundance association in another organism.

\paragraph{Translation-readout contact.}
The translation readout used here is bounded by the modeled-tAI contact. A related three-organism tAI survival computation showed all standard amino-acid tRNA families present for \textit{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast; it computed 61 sense-codon weights per organism with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in that three-organism contact. The same earlier contact had 81 modeled coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ value of $3801961.031607896$, but it also explicitly recorded that three organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim. The present twelve-organism protein-abundance run has stronger row support for a modeled decomposition, but its $T$ coordinate remains modeled tAI, not ribosome occupancy or measured translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The supported reading is therefore narrow. The finite data show that, after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a protein-abundance association whose fitted direct term is reduced when modeled per-protein tAI is conditioned on, with all registered $M^{QTP}$ fractions between $0.01850668564589326$ and $0.9491551041216973$. This is a statistical cross-layer decomposition on finite joined rows. It does not prove translation realization, because the translation coordinate is modeled tAI rather than a matched ribosome-profiling or measured TE row. It does not prove folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or a universal biological law. Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and separate statistics. Protein abundance is also not isolated here from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, or pathway context; those remain outside the admissible conclusion of this chapter.
