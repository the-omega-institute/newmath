\subsection{Modeled Translation Mediation of Protein Abundance by $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record concerns a finite surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The coordinate $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation coordinate $t_j$ is the per-protein modeled tAI score obtained as the codon-count weighted mean of codon tAI weights, using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate $p_k$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance after joining proteins to real CDS codon counts.  The readback is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein assertion; it is a controlled matrix of the form $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$, with $t_j$ retained as the mediating readout.

\paragraph{Rows and ranks.}
The finite rows are organism-matched at the CDS/protein level for three organisms.  The joined row counts are $3739$ proteins for $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, $4129$ proteins for $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$ str. MC2 155, and $5815$ proteins for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$.  The corresponding tRNA-copy totals used in the modeled tAI construction are $90$, $49$, and $275$.  One zero raw codon weight was filled by the geometric mean in $E.\ coli$ and in $M.\ smegmatis$, while no such filling was needed in yeast.  In every organism the residualized design uses $9$ residual coordinates, $24$ control columns before rank reduction, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively, so the finite-row fit is rank-admissible for the stated statistical decomposition.

\paragraph{Controlled decomposition.}
Let $X$ denote the joint nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual design, let $T$ denote modeled per-protein tAI, let $Y$ denote log protein abundance, and let $Z$ collect amino-acid composition, organism-local baselines, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched control columns.  The computation first residualizes $X$, $T$, and $Y$ against $Z$, then compares the residual protein-abundance fit from $X$ alone with the fit from $X$ after conditioning on $T$.  The reported mediation fraction is the finite scalar
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},\\
c &= R^{2}(Y;X\mid Z),\\
c' &= R^{2}(Y;X\mid Z,T),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the numerator is interpreted only as attenuation of the residual-to-protein association after adding the modeled translation readout.  This is a statistical decomposition on joined rows, not a causal intervention and not a molecular mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Organism-level values.}
For $E.\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, the total residual-to-protein abundance coefficient is $c=0.10767526677985896$, the direct coefficient after conditioning on modeled tAI is $c'=0.04167869969498872$, and the indirect difference is $0.06599656708487024$.  The resulting fraction is $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  The same fit records $T$-explained energy on $Y$ equal to $582.5912438239341$, incremental $T$ improvement given $X$ equal to $317.26302294928666$, direct $X$ improvement given $T$ equal to $167.56227978068944$, $X$-explained energy on $T$ equal to $0.256128714706293$, and $X$-explained energy on $Y$ equal to $432.89050065533684$.

For $M.\ smegmatis$ str. MC2 155, the same scalar surface is much weaker but remains in the unit interval: $c=0.03209009170649934$, $c'=0.029395293228368185$, $c-c'=0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.  Its residualized energies are $15.813547327443837$ for $T$ on $Y$, $0.37452260494680445$ for incremental $T$ given $X$, $168.41135341317295$ for direct $X$ given $T$, $0.026187928294862578$ for $X$ on $T$, and $183.85037813566998$ for $X$ on $Y$.

For yeast, the mediated fraction is the largest of the three finite rows.  The values are $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The residualized energy of $T$ on $Y$ is $941.8858618613751$, the incremental $T$ improvement given $X$ is $104.11162139652299$, the direct $X$ improvement given $T$ is $44.87838061554362$, $X$ explains $5.671703404506426$ units of $T$ energy, and $X$ explains $882.6526210803958$ units of $Y$ energy.  In all three organisms the stored check $M^{QTP}\in[0,1]$ is true.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled translation contact.}
The same residual family had an earlier modeled-tAI bounded-coupling contact over three organisms: $E.\ coli$ K-12, human, and yeast.  That contact computed tAI weights for all $61$ sense codons in each organism, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each modeled codon table.  It found $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This supports the use of modeled tAI as a bounded translation-layer readout, but it does not turn the present protein-abundance mediation into measured ribosome occupancy.  The present protein-abundance surface is stronger than a mere contact name because it joins real CDS codon counts to PAXdb abundance rows and computes the residualized attenuation statistic, but the translation coordinate is still modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling or direct translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The finite data support a controlled statistical statement: in three joined organism-level protein tables, the joint nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual design has a positive residual-to-protein association, and adding modeled per-protein tAI attenuates that association by fractions $0.612922252793667$, $0.08397602919861291$, and $0.9491551041216973$.  This licenses a bounded cross-layer relation between synonymous residual coordinates, a modeled translation readout, and matched protein abundance.  It does not license direct $q_i\to p_k$ promotion when the $q_i\to t_j$ step is absent or untested.  It also does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation response, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality.  Those claims require separate layer-matched measurements and their own controlled statistics.  Protein abundance remains entangled with mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, and pathway context; the present matrix only records a finite, controlled mediation-style attenuation in cross-sectional matched rows.
