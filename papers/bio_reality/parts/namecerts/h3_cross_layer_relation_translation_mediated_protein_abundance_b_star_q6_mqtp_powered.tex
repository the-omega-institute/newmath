\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,T,P)$: $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $T$ is a per-protein modeled translation-adaptation readout, and $P$ is matched measured protein abundance.  The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The module is classified as a cross-layer relation, not as a direct protein-realization theorem.  The translation readout is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_g=\frac{\sum_c n_{g,c}w_c}{\sum_c n_{g,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
with $w_c$ computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $Y_g=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The control block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, giving $24$ control columns with rank $23$ in every organism-specific fit.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank checks.}
The powered finite panel contains twelve organism-specific joins.  The joined protein counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  After residualization against the control block, the residual degrees of freedom range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse.  In each organism the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the residualized $T$ block has rank $1$, and the joint $Q,T$ block has rank $10$, so the mediation scalar is not being read from a rank-collapsed design.  The per-protein tAI calculation used tRNA-copy totals of $86$, $581$, $8874$, $290$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $407$, $49$, $417$, $275$, and $47$ respectively; the zero-raw-weight fallback counts were $3$, $0$, $0$, $0$, $1$, $0$, $0$, $0$, $1$, $0$, $0$, and $1$.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
For each organism the fitted quantity is the attenuation of the joint nine-dimensional $Q$ association with protein abundance after conditioning on the modeled translation readout.  With $c=R^2(Q\to P)$ and $c'=R^2(Q\to P\mid T)$, the reported fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve fractions lie in $[0,1]$.  The largest value is yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Large finite fractions also occur in \emph{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, and \emph{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$.  Intermediate values occur in worm, zebrafish, human, chicken, rat, and mouse: $0.4872268357227724$, $0.3157053953614641$, $0.27206289160119157$, $0.1976903964724546$, $0.17489681798543982$, and $0.15488799907323988$.  The weakest positive fractions are \emph{M. smegmatis} at $0.08397602919861291$ and \emph{S. solfataricus} at $0.01850668564589326$.  Thus the finite table supports a bounded statement: in this panel, modeled tAI absorbs a nonnegative part of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-to-protein-abundance association in every joined organism, with large heterogeneity across organisms.

\paragraph{Translation-contact status.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival calculation supplies a lower-layer contact check: three organisms had standard tRNA-family coverage for all $20$ amino-acid families, $61$ sense codons were scored, and the bounded-coupling table contained $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$.  That earlier table was explicitly underpowered as a measured survival panel because it used only $3$ organisms rather than a powered multi-organism translation-readout design.  The present mediation table is stronger in row count and rank, but its $T$ variable is still modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling or directly measured translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Auxiliary yeast structure audit.}
A linked yeast five-way join provides a useful negative boundary for overinterpretation.  It joined $4759$ genes, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and total join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  In that yeast audit, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was only $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$, and the corresponding right-tail permutation values were $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  These numbers keep the present claim focused on translation-conditioned abundance decomposition rather than folding or structural-order realization.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is finite and statistical.  The table permits the statement that a joint $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a protein-abundance association whose fitted $R^2$ is reduced after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI, under amino-acid composition, organism-specific row sets, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls.  It does not establish a causal translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, editing response, rescue effect, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universal biological law.  A direct $Q\to P$ association is not enough for protein realization unless a same-coordinate $Q\to T$ step is supplied by a layer-matched contact.  For measured translation, that contact would need organism- and CDS-matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows with matched mRNA controls; for folding, function, physical admissibility, localization, stability, interaction, or phenotype, a separate layer-specific measurement must be joined and evaluated before any such interpretation is allowed.
