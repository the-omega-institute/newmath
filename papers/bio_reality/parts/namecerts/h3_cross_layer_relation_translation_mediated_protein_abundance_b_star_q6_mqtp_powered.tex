\subsection{Rank-powered Q6 residual mediation through modeled translation readout}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite cross-layer surface whose rows join a synonymous residual coordinate, a translation-side readout, and a matched protein-abundance response. The residual coordinates are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ axes
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The translation coordinate is not a ribosome footprint. It is the per-protein mean modeled tAI value
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the coding-sequence count of codon $c$ and $w_c$ is the tAI weight obtained from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The admissible statement is therefore a controlled statistical decomposition of $Q\to T\to Y$, not a claim that a residual coordinate is directly realized as protein biology.

\paragraph{Rows and rank.}
The powered panel contains twelve organism-level joins.  The joined protein counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Every organism in this panel has sufficient residualized rank for the joint nine-coordinate design: after the control projection the diagnostics report $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, so the reported mediation fractions are not produced by an $n=3$ saturated organism surface.

\paragraph{Controlled decomposition.}
For each organism the total residual association $c=R^2_{QY}$ is compared with the direct association after the modeled translation coordinate is conditioned, $c'=R^2_{QY\mid T}$.  The reported mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTY} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve values lie in the unit interval.  The finite list is: \emph{B. subtilis} $0.6084594553$, \emph{C. elegans} $0.4872268357$, \emph{D. rerio} $0.3157053954$, \emph{D. melanogaster} $0.6165167749$, \emph{E. coli} K12 $0.6129222528$, chicken $0.1976903965$, human $0.2720628916$, mouse $0.1548879991$, \emph{M. smegmatis} $0.0839760292$, rat $0.1748968180$, yeast $0.9491551041$, and \emph{S. solfataricus} $0.0185066856$.  The corresponding total $R^2_{QY}$ values are $0.0619062459$, $0.1021790676$, $0.0461352981$, $0.0770952887$, $0.1076752668$, $0.0398079360$, $0.0445456512$, $0.0498279841$, $0.0320900917$, $0.0276399860$, $0.2983762499$, and $0.0639751425$.  The residual direct values $R^2_{QY\mid T}$ are $0.0242388052$, $0.0523946838$, $0.0315701356$, $0.0295647500$, $0.0416786997$, $0.0319382893$, $0.0324264325$, $0.0421102273$, $0.0293952932$, $0.0228058404$, $0.0151709094$, and $0.0627911747$ in the same organism order.  Thus the yeast row is the strongest finite-row attenuation case, with $c=0.2983762499$, $c'=0.0151709094$, and $c-c'=0.2832053406$, while \emph{S. solfataricus} is the weakest attenuation case, with $c-c'=0.0011839679$.

\paragraph{Controls and residual meaning.}
The control set is deliberately kept below the promoted coordinates.  It includes an intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, GC3, organism-specific row structure, and an $M$-density baseline formed from the union support of the nine residual vectors.  In the yeast structure-related companion computation the same residual coordinate family was also evaluated with measured TE, mRNA stability, and a 5-prime CDS MFE proxy; there the protein-abundance residual association was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, fell to $0.1377396770$ after measured-TE plus mRNA-stability conditioning, and to $0.1375901121$ after the additional structure proxy.  That companion row had $4759$ joined yeast genes, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and $4736$ residual degrees of freedom.  Its structure-only fraction was only $0.0032845645$, and the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507779$ with permutation $p=0.1258741259$.  These numbers are useful as a local guardrail: protein-abundance attenuation can be real as a finite statistical surface while still failing to identify folding or a causal pathway.

\paragraph{Translation-readout context.}
A smaller modeled-tAI survival contact used three organisms and produced $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$.  That contact also explicitly records that three organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  The present twelve-organism mediation surface repairs the rank problem for the protein-abundance decomposition, but it does not change the kind of readout: $T$ is still modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling.  Separately, the structural-order axis calculation over the same nine-coordinate family identified $f_3$-stress as the only fold-level sign-flip-positive axis, with weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  This supports the decision to keep $f_3$-stress in the residual coordinate list, but it does not by itself turn the present mediation matrix into structural biology.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The chapter therefore licenses the following finite readback.  On the twelve joined organism panels, the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive residual protein-abundance association after controls, and conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI reduces that association in every organism by a nonnegative amount.  The mediated fraction ranges from $0.0185066856$ to $0.9491551041$, and all row-level rank diagnostics are sufficient for the reported residualized linear comparisons.  The strongest row is yeast, where the direct residual association after $T$ is only about $5.085\%$ of the total $Q\to Y$ association.  The weakest row is \emph{S. solfataricus}, where the modeled translation coordinate explains only a small part of the residual association.  The outcome is a controlled mediation matrix, not a law of synonymous biology.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, folding behavior, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological rule follows from this matrix alone.  A direct $Q_i\to Y$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives an appropriate translation-readout step, and the present $T$ coordinate is a modeled tAI average rather than measured ribosome profiling.  Protein abundance is also not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational state, folding, or pathway context.  The finite statement is only that, under the declared controls and row joins, modeled translation readout absorbs a positive part of the residual $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance association.
