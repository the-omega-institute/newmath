\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance decomposition for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite cross-layer table whose rows join a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$.  The coordinate side is nine-dimensional:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation readout in this finite run is not a ribosome-footprint measurement.  It is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy numbers and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The fitted control set contains amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline, with organism handled by separate organism panels rather than by pooling a single global coefficient.  The residualized design has 24 control columns and rank $23$ for $Z$ in every organism panel.

\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)},
\end{aligned}
$$

The positive differences are therefore finite statistical decompositions, not proof that synonymous residual coordinates cause protein abundance.  They say that, in these joined panels, conditioning on the modeled tAI scalar removes a nonnegative part of the residual-to-abundance fit.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled translation boundary.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival check computed the same nine residual coordinates on three organisms and found $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That run used only three organisms and therefore explicitly refused a powered measured-survival interpretation requiring at least $40$ matched species.  The present protein-abundance decomposition has larger per-organism row counts, but its $T$ variable is still modeled tAI rather than a directly measured ribosome occupancy or translation-efficiency matrix.  Thus the present record may be read as a bounded mediation decomposition through an explicitly modeled translation score, not as observed ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Yeast comparison rows.}
The yeast structure-mediation run gives a useful calibration of scale.  On $4759$ joined yeast rows, $R^2(Q\to P)=0.2715520808$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid S)=0.2706601505$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T,M)=0.1377396770$, and $R^2(Q\to P\mid T,M,S)=0.1375901121$.  Its structure-only fraction is $0.0032845645$, while the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability is $0.0005507779$.  The permutation $p$-value for $S_Q$ structure association is $0.0279720280$, whereas the structure-incremental right-tail value is $0.1258741259$.  These rows place the present modeled-tAI protein-abundance decomposition beside an independently controlled yeast variance partition: the protein-abundance association is real as a fitted finite-row relation, while the structural route remains much smaller and not promoted into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity and execution subblock.}
The structural-order axis audit identifies $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ as the only significant fold-level axis in that separate powered row: weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502$ with sign-flip $p=0.03125$, and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586607$.  Other listed axes did not pass that gate; for example $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ had weighted increment $0.0013067593$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ had increment $-0.0003205780$ with $p=0.779296875$.  A separate observable-execution decomposition on yeast reports $3289$ genes and the verdict that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  These related finite rows support the decision to keep $B^*_{Q6}$ as a structured cross-layer coordinate family, while still requiring each layer to earn its own contact.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a ready finite mediation result with a strict interpretation.  A direct $Q\to P$ association is not counted as protein realization unless a $Q\to T$ translation step is present and survives the declared controls.  In this run $T$ is modeled tAI, so the result is weaker than a measured ribosome-profiling mediation claim.  No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from these numbers alone.  Any such statement would require a separate layer-matched measurement table and a new controlled statistic at that layer.
