\subsection{Modeled translation mediation from $B^*_Q6$ residual coordinates to matched protein abundance}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translation-mediated-protein-abundance-b-star-q6-mqtp-powered}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record tests a cross-layer mediation surface whose rows are matched coding sequences with three coordinate blocks: a $9$-coordinate $B^*_Q6$ residual vector $Q$, a scalar modeled translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$.  The residual coordinates used in the fit are $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$.  The translation coordinate is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T = \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the coding sequence and $w_c$ is computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb abundance after joining to real CDS codon counts.  The controls include amino-acid composition, organism scope, GC3, coding-sequence length, an $M$-density baseline, and matched transcript-layer control where available; these terms remain controls and are not promoted to residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Joined rows and ranks.}
The finite table contains three organism-scoped blocks.  For $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K-12 MG1655, $3739$ proteins are joined to CDS and abundance rows, with $90$ all-tRNA copies in the tRNA source and one zero raw tAI weight filled by a geometric mean.  For $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis}$ str. MC2 155, $4129$ proteins are joined, with $49$ all-tRNA copies and one geometric-mean fill.  For $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, $5815$ proteins are joined, with $275$ all-tRNA copies and no zero-weight fill.  After residualizing controls, the design has rank $23$ for $Z$, rank $9$ for the $Q$ block, rank $1$ for the residualized $T$ block, and rank $10$ for the combined $Q,T$ block in each organism.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively, so the reported mediation quantities are rank-supported in the finite rows rather than being artifacts of an underdetermined design.

\paragraph{Controlled energy decomposition.}
The controlled decomposition separates the total residual-to-protein association from the portion remaining after conditioning on modeled tAI.  In $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $Q$ explains $432.8905$ units of residual protein-abundance energy before $T$ is conditioned, while $T$ explains $582.5912$ units on $Y$ and adds $317.2630$ units after $Q$; the direct $Q$ improvement after $T$ is $167.5623$.  The corresponding total $R^2_{QP}$ is $0.1076753$, the direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}$ is $0.0416787$, and the difference is $0.0659966$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.6129223$.  In $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$, the same quantities are weaker: total $R^2_{QP}=0.0320901$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0293953$, indirect difference $0.0026948$, and $M^{QTP}=0.0839760$.  In yeast, the mediation is strongest in this finite table: total $R^2_{QP}=0.2983762$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709$, indirect difference $0.2832053$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551$.  All three mediated fractions lie in the unit interval, so the decomposition is numerically coherent for the joined rows.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The result is a controlled statistical mediation calculation, not a causal claim.  It says that, in these organism-scoped finite tables, the $9$-dimensional $B^*_Q6$ residual block has a protein-abundance association whose apparent path through modeled per-protein tAI is positive in all three organisms and especially large in yeast.  The translation coordinate is still modeled codon-adaptation weight derived from tRNA gene-copy information and wobble weights; it is not ribosome profiling, direct elongation measurement, or perturbative evidence.  The earlier modeled-tAI contact used $3$ organisms and was explicitly not a powered multi-species survival test, although its finite modeled coupling had $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$.  The present protein-abundance fit is powered by thousands of CDS-protein rows inside each organism, but it remains a cross-sectional mediation analysis.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not read as protein realization unless the same coordinate is carried through a translation readout.  In this chapter the readout is modeled tAI, so the supported statement is narrower: $Q \to T \to P$ is a finite controlled association pattern in matched CDS, tRNA-copy, and protein-abundance rows.  No measured translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from these numbers unless a separate measurement contact at that layer is joined and evaluated under its own controls.  Protein abundance is also not isolated from transcript abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, or pathway context.  Thus the chapter may enter the protein-abundance mediation ledger, while remaining below mechanism and higher biological realization claims.
