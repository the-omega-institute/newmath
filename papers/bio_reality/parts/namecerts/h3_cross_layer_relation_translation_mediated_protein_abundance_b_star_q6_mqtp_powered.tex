\subsection{Modeled-translation mediation of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance surface}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object in this chapter is the joined mediation surface of triples $(q_i,T,P)$ in which $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T$ is a per-protein modeled translation-adaptation readout, and $P$ is matched protein abundance.  The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation readout used in the finite run is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(g)=\frac{\sum_c n_c(g)w_c}{\sum_c n_c(g)},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c(g)$ is the codon count in the coding sequence for protein $g$ and $w_c$ is the tAI weight computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $P(g)=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb abundance rows joined to real CDS codon counts.  Thus the chapter concerns a controlled statistical decomposition on finite organism-specific tables, not a direct observation of ribosome movement.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank checks.}
The powered finite table contains twelve organism panels with organism-matched CDS, abundance, and tRNA-copy inputs.  The row counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each panel has $9$ residual coordinates, rank $9$ for the residual coordinate block after controls, rank $1$ for the translation readout block, and rank $10$ for the joined residual-plus-translation block.  The control design has $24$ columns with control rank $23$, consisting of an intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control projection range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in \emph{M. musculus}, so the reported mediation fractions are not produced by an under-ranked fit.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the run computes a total residual-to-protein association $c=R^2(Q,P)$ after the control projection and a direct association $c'=R^2(Q,P\mid T)$ after also conditioning on the modeled translation readout.  The mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c},
\end{aligned}
$$
when $c>0$.  All twelve reported fractions lie in the unit interval.  The strongest mediated fraction is in \emph{S. cerevisiae}, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The next largest fractions are \emph{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 with $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$, and \emph{B. subtilis} with $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$.  The smallest fractions remain positive: \emph{S. solfataricus} has $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$, and \emph{M. smegmatis} has $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.  The full finite list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&M_{\mathrm{Bsub}}=0.6084594553474505,\quad M_{\mathrm{Cele}}=0.4872268357227724,\quad M_{\mathrm{Drer}}=0.3157053953614641,\\
&M_{\mathrm{Dmel}}=0.6165167748739756,\quad M_{\mathrm{Ecoli}}=0.612922252793667,\quad M_{\mathrm{Ggal}}=0.1976903964724546,\\
&M_{\mathrm{Hsap}}=0.27206289160119157,\quad M_{\mathrm{Mmus}}=0.15488799907323988,\quad M_{\mathrm{Msmeg}}=0.08397602919861291,\\
&M_{\mathrm{Rnor}}=0.17489681798543982,\quad M_{\mathrm{Scer}}=0.9491551041216973,\quad M_{\mathrm{Ssol}}=0.01850668564589326.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support the finite statement that, in the modeled-tAI table, part of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ association with matched protein abundance is removed by conditioning on a translation-adaptation readout.

\paragraph{Translation contact status.}
The chapter must keep two translation notions separate.  A smaller modeled-tAI survival run over three organisms found nonzero bounded coupling in the modeled codon-adaptation matrix, with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but it explicitly had only $3$ organisms and therefore did not provide powered measured translation survival.  The twelve-organism mediation run is rank-sufficient for the modeled per-protein tAI decomposition, but its $T$ coordinate is still computed from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights rather than from ribosome profiling.  Consequently $Q_i\to T\to P$ here means a finite statistical path through modeled translation adaptation and measured abundance, not a measured ribosome-occupancy path.

\paragraph{Neighboring checks.}
The same residual family has an independent structural-order axis check in which only $f_3$-stress passed the fold-level sign-flip gate: the weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$ with sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$ and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  Other axes did not pass that gate; for example $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ had weighted increment $-0.0003205780368125179$ with probability $0.779296875$, and $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ had weighted increment $-0.00045340330387383447$ with probability $0.529296875$.  A yeast-only abundance and 5-prime CDS-MFE decomposition joined $4759$ rows and found $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$ and structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$.  Those neighboring finite facts are consistent with a narrow interpretation: the protein-abundance signal is real as a fitted residual association, while structural or mechanistic interpretations require separate measurements.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The result is therefore ready only as a controlled finite-row mediation record.  It does not establish translation realization from ribosome-profiling data, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation rescue, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal rule.  Protein abundance is a steady-state downstream readout and is not isolated from mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, or pathway context in this table.  A direct $Q_i\to P$ association is not promoted to protein realization unless a layer-matched $Q_i\to T$ contact has survived its own controls.  In this chapter the surviving $T$ coordinate is modeled tAI, so the admissible conclusion is the finite decomposition itself: across twelve organism panels, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has positive protein-abundance association and a positive modeled-translation-mediated fraction, with all reported $M^{QTP}$ values between $0.01850668564589326$ and $0.9491551041216973$.
