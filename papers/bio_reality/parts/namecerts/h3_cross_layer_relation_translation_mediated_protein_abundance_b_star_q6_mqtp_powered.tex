\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance readback for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite mediation table whose rows have the form $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation-side coordinate $t_j$ is not an observed ribosome trace in this run; it is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein-side readout $p_k$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The admissible readback is therefore a controlled statistical chain $Q \to T \to P$, not a direct promotion of a synonymous residual to protein realization.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
For each organism, the design residualizes against a control block of 24 columns, including amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the M-density baseline.  The resulting rank diagnostics are nondegenerate for the reported organisms: the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the residualized modeled-tAI block has rank $1$, and the combined $Q,T$ block has rank $10$.  Residual degrees of freedom remain large in all organism-level joins, ranging from $1633$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19372$ for \emph{Mus musculus}.  The joined protein counts are likewise finite and explicit: $4042$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168, $11661$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ in \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ in human, $19395$ in mouse, $4129$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ in rat, $5815$ in yeast, and $1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.

\paragraph{Mediation values.}
Let $c$ denote the controlled residual association from the joint nine-dimensional $Q$ block to protein abundance, and let $c'$ denote the remaining direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI.  The finite statistic reported by the run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= c-c'.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve organism-level mediation fractions lie in the unit interval.  The largest mediated fraction is yeast, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The bacterial and metazoan values are heterogeneous rather than universal: \emph{E. coli} has $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ with indirect $R^2=0.06599656708487024$, \emph{Drosophila} has $0.6165167748739756$ with indirect $R^2=0.047530538755721025$, \emph{B. subtilis} has $0.6084594553474505$ with indirect $R^2=0.037667440654802556$, human has $0.27206289160119157$ with indirect $R^2=0.012119218668341744$, mouse has $0.15488799907323988$ with indirect $R^2=0.007717756749299982$, and \emph{Sulfolobus} has only $0.01850668564589326$ with indirect $R^2=0.0011839678516385038$.  The table therefore supports a finite, organism-indexed mediation readback, not a single-sign biological law.

\paragraph{Translation contact status.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival calculation computed the same nine residual coordinates against tAI weights for three organisms and found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That calculation used $61$ sense codons and reported no zero raw-$W$ fallback for \emph{E. coli} K12, human, or yeast.  Its own boundary is important: three organisms are insufficient for a powered translation-survival claim, and the modeled-tAI surface is not ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  In the present mediation run the tRNA-copy totals are concrete but still model-based: $90$ for \emph{E. coli}, $275$ for yeast, $432$ for human, $407$ for mouse, $581$ for \emph{C. elegans}, and $8874$ for zebrafish, with geometric-mean replacement of zero raw weights occurring in only some organisms, for example $1$ in \emph{E. coli}, $3$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, and $1$ in \emph{Sulfolobus}.

\paragraph{Neighboring checks.}
The yeast structure-mediation audit gives a useful comparison surface.  In that five-way yeast join, $4759$ genes were retained, and the $Q$ to protein-abundance residual association had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability reduced this to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, while adding the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was only $0.003284564452662019$, with $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$, permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$ for the structure association, and $p=0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure term.  A mass-balance audit is also cautionary: on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, adding the higher block reduced held-out performance from $R^2=0.9712443377743347$ to $0.8953999568421264$, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  These neighboring rows reinforce that the present table is a bounded abundance mediation statistic, not a general completion of the translation-to-protein story.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The finite rows show that the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a controlled association with matched protein abundance, and that a modeled-tAI covariate absorbs a positive fraction of that association in each reported organism.  This does not by itself establish observed translation, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  A direct $Q \to P$ association is not counted as protein realization unless a same-row $Q \to T$ step is supplied by an appropriate translation contact and survives the declared controls.  A separate measured ribosome, perturbation, structure, localization, turnover, function, or phenotype contact would be required before any of those stronger biological readings could be stated.
