\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance readback for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a cross-layer relation in which a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate is allowed to approach protein abundance only through an intervening translation readout.  The finite carrier is a table of triples $(q_i,T,P)$, where $q_i$ ranges over nine residual coordinates, $T$ is per-protein modeled tAI, and $P$ is matched protein abundance.  The nine coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb-like matched abundance rows joined to real CDS codon counts.  The translation readout is the per-protein mean modeled tAI computed as codon-count-weighted tAI weight, using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.

\paragraph{Joined rows and controls.}
The computation uses twelve organism-level joined panels: $4042$ proteins for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each organism panel residualizes against a $24$-column control design whose effective rank is $23$, covering amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline together with the intercept structure.  After this residualization the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block has rank $9$, the translation-readout block has rank $1$, and the joint $(Q,T)$ block has rank $10$ in every organism, so the mediated readback is not a rank-collapsed one-dimensional restatement of the controls.

\paragraph{Decomposition statistic.}
For each organism, let $X$ denote the residualized $9$-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design, $T$ the residualized modeled-tAI column, and $Y$ the residualized protein-abundance vector.  The reported scalar is the fraction of the $Q$-to-protein association removed after conditioning on the translation readout:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}
  &= \frac{R^2(Y\sim X)-R^2(Y\sim X\mid T)}{R^2(Y\sim X)},\\
R^2(Y\sim X) &> 0,\qquad 0\leq M^{QTP}\leq 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a variance-partition statistic on matched rows, not a perturbation effect.  The direct residual-to-protein term is therefore not admissible as a protein-realization claim unless the same finite row first carries a translation-readout term.

\paragraph{Mediated fractions.}
All twelve panels have a positive indirect component and a mediated fraction in the unit interval.  The strongest mediated fraction is seen in yeast, where $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect component is $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  The next largest fractions are $0.6165167748739756$ in \emph{D. melanogaster}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, and $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{B. subtilis}.  The remaining panels are $0.4872268357227724$ for \emph{C. elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, $0.1976903964724546$ for chicken, $0.17489681798543982$ for rat, $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $0.01850668564589326$ for \emph{S. solfataricus}.  Thus the finite readback is not a universal sign law or a single-organism story: it is a bounded matrix of organism-indexed mediation strengths.

\paragraph{Energy diagnostics.}
The residualized energy checks show that $T$ is a measurable covariate on the same rows and not merely a label.  In yeast, for example, the residualized translation energy is $6.90537888244409$, the residualized protein-abundance energy is $2958.1865890925337$, $X$ explains $5.671703404506426$ units on $T$, and $X$ explains $882.6526210803958$ units on $Y$.  In human, the corresponding values are $1.174468648693672$, $30769.5367150478$, $0.3099459586758378$, and $1370.6490494847067$.  In \emph{E. coli}, they are $0.4728468725814029$, $4020.333671802061$, $0.256128714706293$, and $432.89050065533684$.  These numbers justify reading the table as a finite controlled mediation surface, while keeping the modeled-tAI and abundance coordinates distinct.

\paragraph{Relation to translation survival.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival computation found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$ across three organisms, with all standard amino-acid tRNA families represented in the local GtRNAdb payload.  That earlier contact was explicitly underpowered for a powered survival claim because only $3$ organisms were present.  The present panel enlarges the protein-abundance mediation surface to twelve organism-level joins, but its translation coordinate remains modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling.  It therefore supports a controlled $Q\to T\to P$ statistical decomposition and does not by itself become measured ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The permitted conclusion is narrow: on the finite joined panels, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has organism-indexed protein-abundance association whose direct component is reduced after conditioning on a matched modeled-translation readout.  The chapter does not infer translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality from these numbers.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights is not ribo-seq; steady-state protein abundance is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context; and a positive mediated fraction is not a synonymous-edit or rescue result.  Any such stronger layer requires its own matched contact and a separate statistic on the same or explicitly comparable rows.
