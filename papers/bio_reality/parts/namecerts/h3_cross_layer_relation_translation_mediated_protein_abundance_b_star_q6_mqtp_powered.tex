\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance surface for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.} The record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual vector can enter a downstream protein-abundance fit only through a bounded translation readout. The finite carrier consists of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the residual coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$; $t_j$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI score; and $p_k$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts. The translation readout is computed as
$$\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}$$
with $w_c$ obtained from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The residualization set is organism, amino-acid composition, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline, with 24 control columns before rank loss and $23$ effective control rank in every organism-specific fit.

\paragraph{Joined organisms and rank.} The powered mediation run used twelve organism-level joins: $4042$ proteins for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In all twelve cases the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the residualized $T$ block has rank $1$, and the joint $Q,T$ block has rank $10$, so the mediation fit is not a rank artifact under the declared degeneracy threshold $0.999999$. The tRNA copy totals span from $47$ in \textit{S. solfataricus} to $8874$ in \textit{D. rerio}; geometric-mean filling for zero raw wobble weights is absent in nine organisms and occurs only in \textit{B. subtilis} ($3$ codons), \textit{E. coli} ($1$), \textit{M. smegmatis} ($1$), and \textit{S. solfataricus} ($1$).

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.} For each organism the reported quantity is the reduction in the $Q\to P$ association after conditioning on $T$, written as
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)}.
\end{aligned}$$
The strongest mediated fraction is in yeast, where $R^2(Q\to P)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect component is $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. The other mediated fractions are $0.6084594553474505$ in \textit{B. subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ in \textit{C. elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ in \textit{D. rerio}, $0.6165167748739756$ in \textit{D. melanogaster}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.1976903964724546$ in chicken, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ in \textit{M. smegmatis}, $0.17489681798543982$ in rat, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{S. solfataricus}. Every value lies in the unit interval, and every organism has positive indirect $R^2$ in the declared decomposition.

\paragraph{Readout strength and related checks.} The modeled-tAI boundary run supplies a separate bounded translation contact: in the three-organism check, the finite modeled-tAI matrix has $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, while the same record states that three organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim. The protein-abundance mediation run supplies the powered cross-organism row surface, not a direct ribosome experiment. Nearby audits constrain the interpretation. The yeast structure-mediation decomposition gives $R^2(Q\to P)=0.2715520808117803$ and $R^2(Q\to P\mid T,M)=0.13773967696311287$, with only $0.0005507778820193332$ structure-incremental fraction beyond measured TE plus mRNA stability. The structural-order identity audit assigns the dominant axis to $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$. A rate-slot bridge on yeast reports $3289$ genes and the verdict that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes. By contrast, the evolutionary selection-imprint audit is negative for the strong claim: $n=80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null95 $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$. The dynamic mass-balance run is also not a promotion route, with $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, and final $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ over $120$ genes and $718$ intervals. The turnover audit similarly reports no positive residual support beyond expression, TE, pLDDT, and N-end controls: $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$ over $3290$ genes.

\paragraph{Local verdict.} The finite rows support a controlled statistical statement: after the declared covariates are removed, modeled per-protein tAI absorbs a positive portion of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual association with matched protein abundance in every organism-specific table, with yeast giving the largest fraction and \textit{S. solfataricus} the smallest. The result is therefore admissible as a translation-mediated protein-abundance association surface for the present data. It is not an observation of ribosome occupancy, not a causal translation mechanism, and not evidence for folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, perturbation rescue, or a universal biological rule. Those stronger readings require separate layer-matched contacts and statistics beyond the modeled-tAI and PAXdb abundance rows used here.
