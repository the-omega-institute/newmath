\subsection{Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies a finite mediation surface whose rows join a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-layer readout $t_j$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $p_k$. The residual side contains nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation readout is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c\,w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the real CDS codon count and $w_c$ is computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy numbers with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to the same CDS rows. The fitted object is therefore not a direct $Q\to P$ assertion. It is a controlled decomposition in which a residual-to-protein association is admitted to the protein-abundance layer only through the intermediate modeled translation coordinate $T$.

\paragraph{Rows and rank.}
Six organism-specific matrices are present. The joined row counts are $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The corresponding tRNA-copy totals used in the modeled tAI computation are $8874$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $49$, and $275$. Two organisms require a geometric-mean fill for one zero raw $W$ value, namely \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 and \emph{M. smegmatis}; the other four have zero fill count $0$. In every organism the residualized design has $9$ $Q$ coordinates, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{X,T})=10$. The control matrix has $24$ columns with effective rank $23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after control residualization are $14387$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively. Thus the finite rows are not rank-blocked for this statistical decomposition.

\paragraph{Controls.}
The controls keep amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance out of the promoted coordinate. The decomposition uses a joint nine-dimensional $X_Q$ residual block together with the one-dimensional modeled tAI readout. The reported residual energies show that the translation coordinate is small after controls, but nonzero: $0.5747725575$ for \emph{D. rerio}, $0.4728468726$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.7248915610$ for \emph{G. gallus}, $1.1744686487$ for human, $0.04119157635$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $6.9053788824$ for yeast. The protein-abundance residual energies are much larger: $24915.244071805108$, $4020.333671802061$, $18586.77768412634$, $30769.5367150478$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$. This asymmetry is important: the contact is a fitted mediation readback through a modeled translation scalar, not an observation of ribosome motion.

\paragraph{Mediation values.}
For each organism the total residual-to-protein association $c$ is compared with the direct association $c'$ left after conditioning on $T$. The indirect part is $c-c'$, and the reported mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite results are all inside the unit interval. In \emph{D. rerio}, $c=0.04613529807085213$, $c'=0.031570135553274765$, $c-c'=0.014565162517577362$, and $M^{QTP}=0.3157053953614641$. In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, $c-c'=0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$. In \emph{G. gallus}, $c=0.03980793596647631$, $c'=0.031938289322513524$, $c-c'=0.007869646643962787$, and $M^{QTP}=0.1976903964724546$. In human, $c=0.04454565118019448$, $c'=0.03242643251185274$, $c-c'=0.012119218668341744$, and $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$. In \emph{M. smegmatis}, $c=0.03209009170649934$, $c'=0.029395293228368185$, $c-c'=0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$. In yeast, $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The strongest finite mediated fraction occurs in yeast, where the modeled tAI conditioning absorbs most of the residual-to-protein association. The weakest finite mediated fraction occurs in \emph{M. smegmatis}, where the indirect component is positive but small. The six-organism list therefore supports a bounded statistical statement: after the stated controls, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive modeled-tAI-mediated component in matched protein-abundance rows for every organism in the finite panel. The result is cross-layer in the narrow sense that it relates codon residuals, a modeled translation-efficiency coordinate, and measured protein abundance on joined protein rows.

\paragraph{Earlier modeled-tAI contact.}
A separate modeled-tAI bounded-coupling computation used three organisms and $61$ sense codons. It found $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$ under modeled tAI weights, while also recording that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival when powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species. That earlier contact justifies the modeled translation-readout route as a bounded finite contact, but it does not by itself supply ribosome-profiling rows or measured translation efficiency. The present six-organism protein-abundance computation is therefore read as a controlled statistical mediation surface, not as a measured ribosome-survival theorem.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No causal, mechanistic, structural, functional, phenotypic, or universal conclusion is licensed by these rows. Modeled per-protein tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is not ribosome profiling. Conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional CDS and protein-abundance rows is not a proof of translation mechanism. Protein abundance is not isolated from mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or assay bias. A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association remains inadmissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives the translation-readout step under the declared controls. Likewise, no folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection claim, or population law follows unless a separate layer-matched measurement contact supplies that information.
