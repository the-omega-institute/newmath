\subsection{Modeled tAI mediation of B$^*_{Q6}$ residuals and protein abundance}\label{subsec:bq6-modeled-tai-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows join a B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual vector $Q$, a modeled translation-readout scalar $T$, and a matched protein-abundance response $P$.  The residual coordinates are the nine columns $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The joined organism panels are finite and explicit: $3739$ proteins for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 with the \emph{E. coli} tRNA table, $4129$ proteins for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155 with its matched tRNA table, and $5815$ proteins for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with its matched tRNA table.  The readout $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as a codon-count-weighted average of codon tAI weights from all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The readout $P$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.

\paragraph{Controlled mediation statistic.}
For each organism, amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA-abundance controls remain in the control block $Z$ rather than becoming coordinates of the residual packet.  After residualization on $Z$, the finite test compares a direct residual-to-protein association against the same association after conditioning on the modeled translation scalar.  The displayed scalar is therefore a statistical decomposition of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)},\\
0 &\leq M^{QTP}\leq 1
\end{aligned}
$$
when the observed fraction lies in the unit interval.  In the \emph{E. coli} panel, $R^2(Q\to P)=0.10767526677985896$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.04167869969498872$, the indirect difference is $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  In \emph{M. smegmatis}, $R^2(Q\to P)=0.03209009170649934$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.029395293228368185$, the indirect difference is $0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.  In yeast, $R^2(Q\to P)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.

\paragraph{Rank and residual-energy checks.}
The design is not a three-organism species-level survival claim.  It is a per-protein joined-row calculation with residual degrees of freedom $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$ after the control block for \emph{E. coli}, \emph{M. smegmatis}, and yeast respectively.  Each panel has $9$ residual coordinates, $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, rank $9$ for the residual block, rank $1$ for the modeled tAI block, and rank $10$ for the combined residual-plus-tAI block.  The residual protein-abundance energies after $Z$ are $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$.  The corresponding modeled-tAI residual energies are $0.4728468725814029$, $0.041191576348982066$, and $6.90537888244409$.  The residual block explains $432.89050065533684$, $183.85037813566998$, and $882.6526210803958$ units of protein-abundance energy before conditioning on $T$, while the modeled-tAI block explains $582.5912438239341$, $15.813547327443837$, and $941.8858618613751$ units of the same response after the control projection.

\paragraph{Translation readout scope.}
The modeled tAI contact is a bounded readout, not a ribosome-profile observation.  In the companion modeled-tAI contact, only $3$ organisms were present at the species table level, although the finite tRNA payloads covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families for \emph{E. coli}, human, and yeast, and the modeled tAI matrix had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That earlier contact is useful for checking that the residual coordinates can be coupled to codon-adaptation weights, but it is not powered species evidence for measured translation survival.  The present joined-row calculation instead asks whether the per-protein modeled tAI scalar absorbs part of the residual-to-protein association in matched CDS and protein-abundance rows.  The answer is positive as a finite statistical decomposition in all three panels, with the yeast fraction close to saturation and the \emph{M. smegmatis} fraction small but still positive.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter does not assert a causal translation mechanism.  A direct $Q\to P$ association is not promoted unless the same residual signal is first represented through the translation-readout coordinate $T$, and here $T$ is modeled per-protein tAI rather than measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency.  Matched protein abundance is also steady-state abundance, not folding, localization, turnover, post-translational state, molecular function, phenotype, or physical feasibility.  Thus the finite row surface supports only this bounded statement: after the stated controls, conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI reduces the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual association with matched protein abundance in three organism-specific joined panels.  No measured translation realization, folding result, physical admissibility claim, functional claim, mechanistic assertion, or universal biological rule follows from this statistic unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding observations.
