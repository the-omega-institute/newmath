\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance surface for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object considered here is the organism-indexed table of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a per-protein modeled translation-readout score, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout. The residual coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The translation column is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$\sum_{c} n_c w_c / \sum_c n_c$, computed from codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights. The protein column is $\log_{10}$ PAXdb abundance ppm joined to real CDS codon counts. The computation therefore tests a controlled statistical mediation surface, not a perturbation experiment and not a direct observation of ribosome motion.

\paragraph{Joined rows and controls.}
Twelve organism-specific joined surfaces enter the computation: \emph{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168 with $4042$ proteins, \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans} with $11661$, \emph{Danio rerio} with $14410$, \emph{Drosophila melanogaster} with $13295$, \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 with $3739$, \emph{Gallus gallus} with $12418$, \emph{Homo sapiens} with $19053$, \emph{Mus musculus} with $19395$, \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155 with $4129$, \emph{Rattus norvegicus} with $13322$, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $5815$, and \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} with $1656$. Each organism uses a control design with $24$ columns before residualization, rank $23$ for the control block, rank $9$ for the residual $Q$ block, rank $1$ for the modeled translation readout, and rank $10$ for the joint $(Q,T)$ block. The residual degrees of freedom range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, so the fitted mediation surface is not a small-row artifact.

\paragraph{Decomposition statistic.}
For each organism, the residualized protein-abundance response $P_e$, residualized residual-coordinate matrix $Q_e$, and residualized modeled translation readout $T_e$ are compared through the total residual-to-protein association and the residual association that remains after conditioning on $T_e$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  c &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e),\\
  c' &= R^2(P_e \sim Q_e \mid T_e),\\
  M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The mediated fraction is therefore positive exactly when conditioning on the modeled translation column removes part of the residual-coordinate association with protein abundance. This is a variance-decomposition statistic on finite rows. It is not a causal mediation coefficient, and it does not by itself license a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Observed mediation profile.}
All twelve organism surfaces produced a positive indirect component and a mediated fraction in the unit interval. The largest value was in yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. The next largest fractions were in \emph{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, and \emph{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$. The middle group contains \emph{C. elegans} at $0.4872268357227724$, zebrafish at $0.3157053953614641$, human at $0.27206289160119157$, chicken at $0.1976903964724546$, rat at $0.17489681798543982$, and mouse at $0.15488799907323988$. The weakest positive surfaces are \emph{M. smegmatis} with $0.08397602919861291$ and \emph{S. solfataricus} with $0.01850668564589326$. The corresponding indirect $R^2$ differences are nonnegative throughout: $0.037667440654802556$, $0.04978438377417191$, $0.014565162517577362$, $0.047530538755721025$, $0.06599656708487024$, $0.007869646643962787$, $0.012119218668341744$, $0.007717756749299982$, $0.0026947984781311544$, $0.004834145605955652$, $0.28320534058057895$, and $0.0011839678516385038$ in the organism order listed above.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The translation column is bounded because it is modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling. The supporting modeled-tAI survival computation used three organisms, found $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, and had maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$, but it explicitly remained an underpowered modeled-readout result with only $3$ organisms. Thus the present table may be read as protein-abundance association attenuated by a modeled codon-adaptation column, not as measured ribosome occupancy. A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not promoted unless the same residual first has a valid $Q_i \to T_j$ step on the declared translation surface.

\paragraph{Controls against over-reading.}
Several linked finite checks keep the interpretation narrow. The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run had $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, $3$ species, $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$; it therefore did not support the strong evolutionary selection-imprint claim. The yeast structure-mediation run found $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$, a structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$, and permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$ for the structure association, while the incremental structure fraction after translation and mRNA stability was only $0.0005507778820193332$ with permutation $p=0.1258741258741259$. The dynamic mass-balance run had $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $p_{TEbin}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{timeshuffle}=0.9306930693069307$. These finite checks are consistent with a bounded mediation readback and block promotion to selection, structure, or dynamic mass-balance closure.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The ready claim is only this: within the finite joined table, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a positive modeled-translation-mediated protein-abundance decomposition in every listed organism after amino-acid composition, organism-specific sequence composition, GC3, CDS length, and M-density baseline controls. No observed translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this chapter unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed. Protein abundance remains a downstream measurement, modeled tAI remains a bounded translation annotation, and positive $M^{QTP}$ values remain finite-row attenuation facts rather than mechanism-realization claims.
