\subsection{Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Relation for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residuals}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record treats the cross-layer object as a finite surface of triples $(q_i,T,P)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $T$ is a protein-level modeled translation readout, and $P$ is a matched protein-abundance readout. The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate used in the computed rows is not a ribosome-footprint measurement. It is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
with codon counts $n_c$, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance after joining to real CDS codon counts. Thus the admissible readback is the controlled statistical chain $Q\to T\to P$, not an unmediated assertion that a synonymous residual directly realizes protein output.

\paragraph{Rows and organism scope.}
The finite table contains three organism-level joins. For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ CDS-protein rows were used, with the tRNA organism recorded as \emph{Escherichia coli}; the all-tRNA copy total for the per-protein tAI calculation was $90$, and one zero raw $W$ entry was filled by the geometric mean. For \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $4129$ joined rows were used, the tRNA organism was the same strain, the all-tRNA copy total was $49$, and one zero raw $W$ entry was filled by the geometric mean. For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ joined rows were used, the all-tRNA copy total was $275$, and no zero raw $W$ entry required that fill. The associated modeled-tAI contact also records three organisms only. It computed sense-codon weights for all $61$ sense codons in \emph{E. coli} K-12, human, and budding yeast, with zero raw $W$ fallback count equal to $0$ in each of those modeled-tAI weight tables. Its bounded coupling audit found $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but it explicitly remains underpowered for multi-organism survival because only $3$ organisms are present rather than the requested $\geq 40$ matched species.

\paragraph{Controlled regression geometry.}
For the protein-abundance mediation table, the control block contains amino-acid composition, organism-level terms, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance controls. In each organism the control column count is $24$ and the control rank is $23$. After residualizing by this control block, the remaining degrees of freedom are $3716$ in \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $4106$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $5792$ in \emph{S. cerevisiae}. The residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$ in every organism; the residualized $T$ block has rank $1$; and the joint $(Q,T)$ block has rank $10$. These rank values are sufficient for the stated finite regression decomposition in all three organism tables, using the degenerate $R^2$ threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Mediation decomposition.}
The computed statistic is a joint nine-dimensional $Q$ mediation scalar with a per-coordinate audit behind it. Let $c$ denote the controlled $R^2$ for $Q\to P$, let $c'$ denote the controlled direct $R^2$ for $Q\to P$ after conditioning on $T$, and let
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c}
\end{aligned}
$$
whenever the denominator is positive and the fraction remains in the unit interval. The three organism values are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{E.\ coli}:\quad &c=0.10767526677985896,\quad c'=0.04167869969498872,\\
&c-c'=0.06599656708487024,\quad M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667;\\
\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}:\quad &c=0.03209009170649934,\quad c'=0.029395293228368185,\\
&c-c'=0.0026947984781311544,\quad M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291;\\
\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}:\quad &c=0.29837624994140827,\quad c'=0.0151709093608293,\\
&c-c'=0.28320534058057895,\quad M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
All three fractions are recorded inside $[0,1]$. The strongest finite entry is yeast, where almost all of the controlled $Q\to P$ association measured by this decomposition is absorbed by the modeled-tAI coordinate. The weakest finite entry is \emph{M. smegmatis}, where the mediated component is positive but small. These values are statistical decompositions over joined CDS and protein-abundance rows; they are not causal effects.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The ready claim is therefore narrow. A finite joined table exists in which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a modeled per-protein tAI coordinate, and matched PAXdb protein abundance can be residualized against the declared controls and decomposed into direct and translation-conditioned components. The positive mediated fractions in all three organism tables justify recording a bounded cross-layer relation at the level of controlled statistical mediation. They do not justify saying that the residual coordinates have been observed as ribosome occupancy, that a translation mechanism has been established, or that protein abundance has been separated from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, pathway context, or transcript-level regulation. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, selection claim, universality claim, or organism-general directionality follows from this table unless an additional layer-specific measurement supplies that evidence and is tested with the same discipline of controls.
