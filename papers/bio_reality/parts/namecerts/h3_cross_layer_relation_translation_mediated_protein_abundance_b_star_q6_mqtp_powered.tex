\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:q6-translation-conditioned-protein-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record fixes a finite cross-layer surface whose rows join three kinds of coordinates: a $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual vector $q_i$, a translation readout $t_j$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $p_k$. The residual side contains nine computed coordinates:
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The translation coordinate used in the finite run is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the coding sequence and $w_c$ is the modeled tAI codon weight computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein coordinate is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts. The run therefore tests a statistical $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition on finite matched rows, not an unconstrained residual-to-protein assertion.

\paragraph{Rows and controls.}
Three organism-level joined datasets are present. For \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, 3739 proteins were joined to CDS rows and the tRNA source was \texttt{escherichia\_coli}. For \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, 4129 proteins were joined with the same number used in the fit and the tRNA source was organism matched. For \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, 5815 joined proteins were used with 275 all-tRNA copies in the tRNA input. The per-protein modeled tAI computation used 90 all-tRNA copies for \texttt{escherichia\_coli}, 49 for \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, and 275 for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. The zero raw-weight fallback count was 1 in the bacterial rows and 0 in yeast.

The residualization control matrix has 24 columns in each organism, with rank $23$ after degeneracy removal. In every organism the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the residualized translation block has rank $1$, and the combined $(Q,T)$ block has rank $10$. The residual degrees of freedom after controls are 3716, 4106, and 5792 for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, and \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, respectively. The recorded degeneracy cutoff is $0.999999$, and all three rows are rank sufficient for the declared finite regression geometry.

\paragraph{Mediation decomposition.}
Let $X$ denote the nine-dimensional $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual block, $T$ the modeled per-protein tAI scalar, $Y$ the protein-abundance response, and $Z$ the control matrix containing amino-acid composition, organism-scoped sequence baselines, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance controls where available in the fitted design. The run computes the total residual-to-protein association $c$, the direct association $c'$ after conditioning on $T$, and the positive difference $c-c'$ as a statistical mediation component:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, $c-c'=0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$. For \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, $c=0.03209009170649934$, $c'=0.029395293228368185$, $c-c'=0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$. For \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. Each mediated fraction lies in the unit interval, so the finite decomposition is internally coherent under the stated design.

\paragraph{Energy audit.}
The translation coordinate explains nonzero residual protein-abundance energy in every organism after controls: $582.5912438239341$ in \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $15.813547327443837$ in \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, and $941.8858618613751$ in \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. The incremental protein-abundance improvement from $T$ after $X$ is $317.26302294928666$, $0.37452260494680445$, and $104.11162139652299$, respectively. The remaining direct $X$ improvement after $T$ is also nonzero: $167.56227978068944$, $168.41135341317295$, and $44.87838061554362$. Thus the finite data support a bounded cross-layer relation in which modeled tAI absorbs part, but not all, of the residual-to-protein-abundance association.

The $X\to T$ contact is weak in the bacterial rows and larger in yeast: $X$ explains $0.256128714706293$ units of $T$ energy in \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $0.026187928294862578$ in \texttt{mycobacterium\_smegmatis\_str\_mc2\_155}, and $5.671703404506426$ in \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. The residual $T$ energies are $0.4728468725814029$, $0.041191576348982066$, and $6.90537888244409$; the residual $Y$ energies are $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$. These numbers justify reading the surface as a finite mediation matrix with organism-specific strengths, not as a population law.

\paragraph{Relation to the modeled translation contact.}
A separate modeled translation-survival run records that the tAI readout was computed for 61 sense codons in \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}, \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, and \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each of those codon-weight tables. The finite modeled-tAI coupling table has 81 entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. That supporting computation uses only three organisms, so it is explicitly underpowered for a multi-species survival claim requiring at least 40 matched species. The protein-abundance mediation run is stronger in row count within its three organism datasets, but it remains a modeled-tAI and matched-proteomics decomposition rather than a ribosome-profiling validation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. The finite rows show that a nine-coordinate $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual block has a controlled statistical association with matched protein abundance, and that conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI removes a positive fraction of that association in all three organism datasets. This supports the chapter-level claim only as a translation-conditioned cross-layer relation over the declared finite rows.

No claim of observed ribosome dynamics follows from modeled tAI weights. No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, synonymous-edit effect, rescue experiment, selection rule, or universal biological law follows from these regressions. A direct $Q\to P$ association is not promoted unless the $Q\to T$ contact is present and the downstream $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition is explicitly controlled. Protein abundance is also not isolated here from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, pathway context, or unmodeled condition effects. Those layers require separate measurements before the residual coordinates can be used in any stronger biological interpretation.
