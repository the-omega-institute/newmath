\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{subsec:brioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Question.}
The finite question is whether a synonymy-residual coordinate in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ panel can enter a downstream protein-abundance fit only through a translation-layer readout.  The carrier is the finite surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The nine coordinates used in the run were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout $T$ in the finite run is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as the codon-count weighted mean of organism-specific tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The readout $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts.  The tested relation is therefore not a direct $q_i\to p_k$ promotion.  It is a residualized decomposition in which a $Q\to P$ association is compared with the remaining direct association after conditioning on $T$.

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The powered mediation run joined twelve organism-scoped tables.  The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The corresponding all-tRNA copy totals used in the modeled tAI construction were $86$, $581$, $8874$, $290$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $407$, $49$, $417$, $275$, and $47$.  The zero raw-weight fallback count was zero in eight organisms and nonzero in four cases: $3$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, $1$ in \emph{E. coli}, $1$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $1$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.

\paragraph{Residualization and rank.}
For each organism, the control matrix had $24$ columns.  These columns encode an intercept, coding-sequence length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  The residualized $Q$ block had rank $9$, the residualized $T$ block had rank $1$, and the combined $(Q,T)$ block had rank $10$ in every organism.  The reported residual degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in \emph{M. musculus}.  Thus the finite table is rank-admissible for the joint nine-coordinate mediation scalar used here, although the translation readout is modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism the run records the total residualized association $c=R^2_{QP}$, the direct residualized association after conditioning on modeled tAI $c'=R^2_{QP\mid T}$, the indirect component $c-c'$, and the bounded fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve reported $M^{QTP}$ fractions lie in $[0,1]$.  The strongest finite-row mediation fraction occurs in yeast:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.28320534058057895,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
Large bounded fractions were also observed in \emph{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} with $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$, and \emph{B. subtilis} with $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$.  Intermediate fractions were recorded in \emph{C. elegans} at $0.4872268357227724$, \emph{D. rerio} at $0.3157053953614641$, \emph{H. sapiens} at $0.27206289160119157$, \emph{G. gallus} at $0.1976903964724546$, \emph{R. norvegicus} at $0.17489681798543982$, and \emph{M. musculus} at $0.15488799907323988$.  The smallest positive bounded fractions were \emph{M. smegmatis} at $0.08397602919861291$ and \emph{S. solfataricus} at $0.01850668564589326$.

\paragraph{Translation-readout contact.}
The same coordinate family had an earlier modeled-tAI bounded-coupling check over three organisms.  That run computed all $61$ sense-codon weights in \emph{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast, found zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in all three, and reported $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}$ equal to $3801961.031607896$.  The organism count in that contact was only $3$, so it was explicitly underpowered for a population-level survival claim.  It is useful here only as a translation-layer contact showing that the tAI construction is finite and nonzero; it is not ribosome occupancy, and it is not a measured translation-efficiency matrix.

\paragraph{Relation to structural-order and yeast structure mediation.}
The structural-order axis audit on the same nine-coordinate family found exactly one fold-level sign-flip positive coordinate: $f3\_stress$, with weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502294756994$, sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$, and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  Other coordinates did not pass that fold-level gate: for example $Arg\_AGR$ had $p=0.142578125$ and weighted increment $0.0020139270616115154$, while $Ile\_AUA$ had $p=0.0693359375$ and weighted increment $0.0013067592718559024$.  The yeast structure-mediation audit is also nearby but distinct: it reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ and right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$.  Those numbers show that the same residual family can be audited against a structure proxy, but they do not turn the present protein-abundance mediation statistic into a folding or structural realization result.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite readback is a controlled statistical surface.  It says that, across the twelve joined organism panels, the joint $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block has a nonzero protein-abundance association and that conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI reduces that association by a positive bounded fraction in every reported organism.  The run is strongest as a protein-abundance mediation audit under a modeled translation covariate.  It is not a causal estimate, not an edit experiment, not a rescue assay, and not a proof that any synonymous residual changes protein output through ribosome dynamics.  The result is also not isolated from matched mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, or pathway context.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded cross-layer relation and no higher biological promotion.  A direct $Q\to P$ association is not by itself accepted as protein realization unless the translation step is represented and survives the declared controls.  In the present table the translation step is modeled tAI, not ribosome profiling or measured TE; matched mRNA abundance is not part of the twelve-organism control design.  No claim about translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality follows from these rows.  Each of those statements would require its own layer-matched contact and a separate finite statistic before it could be read as biological support.
