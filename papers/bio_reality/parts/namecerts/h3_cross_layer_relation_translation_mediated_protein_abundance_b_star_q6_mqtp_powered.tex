\subsection{Translation-mediated protein abundance for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as a finite cross-layer mediation surface rather than as direct protein-level biology.  The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional list
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Rows are matched coding sequences with real codon counts, a per-protein modeled translation readout, and measured protein abundance.  The translation coordinate $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T = \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the coding sequence and $w_c$ is the tAI weight obtained from all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to the same coding-sequence rows.  The fitted surface is therefore a controlled matrix of the form $Q_i \to T \to Y$, with $T$ acting as the translation-layer waypoint.  A direct $Q_i \to Y$ association is not interpreted as protein realization unless the same coordinate also survives the translation waypoint.

\paragraph{Matched rows and ranks.}
Three organism-level tables were loaded.  The Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 table has $3739$ joined proteins and uses Escherichia coli tRNA counts with total all-tRNA copy count $90$; one zero raw $W$ entry was filled by a geometric-mean rule.  The Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 table has $4129$ joined proteins and tRNA copy total $49$; one zero raw $W$ entry was filled in the same way.  The Saccharomyces cerevisiae table has $5815$ joined proteins and tRNA copy total $275$, with no zero raw $W$ replacement.  After residualizing the control matrix $Z$, the residual degrees of freedom are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively.  In each organism the residualized residual-coordinate block has rank $9$, the translation block has rank $1$, and the joint $[Q,T]$ block has rank $10$.  The control rank is $23$ with $24$ control columns recorded, so the finite-row calculation is rank-sufficient under the declared degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Controlled decomposition.}
The computation uses a joint nine-coordinate $Q$ block and reports a scalar mediation decomposition with per-coordinate audit support.  In Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $Q$ explains $432.89050065533684$ units of residual protein-abundance energy before conditioning on $T$, while the direct $Q$ improvement after $T$ is $167.56227978068944$.  The total $Q \to Y$ coefficient of determination is $c = 0.10767526677985896$, the conditioned direct value is $c' = 0.04167869969498872$, and the indirect difference is $c-c' = 0.06599656708487024$.  This gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Ecoli}} = 0.612922252793667.
\end{aligned}
$$
For Mycobacterium smegmatis, the corresponding values are $c = 0.03209009170649934$, $c' = 0.029395293228368185$, and $c-c' = 0.0026947984781311544$, yielding
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Msmeg}} = 0.08397602919861291.
\end{aligned}
$$
For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the corresponding values are $c = 0.29837624994140827$, $c' = 0.0151709093608293$, and $c-c' = 0.28320534058057895$, yielding
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Scer}} = 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
All three fractions lie in the unit interval.  The calculation is strongest as a finite statistical attenuation statement: conditioning on modeled tAI removes a large share of the residual-to-protein association in yeast, a substantial share in Escherichia coli, and only a small share in Mycobacterium smegmatis.

\paragraph{Translation readout contact.}
The same residual family has a separate modeled-tAI survival contact computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights in three organisms.  That contact reports all $61$ sense codons covered for Escherichia coli K12, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with zero raw $W$ fallback count $0$ at the codon-weight stage.  The bounded coupling table has $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  This establishes a finite modeled translation-weight coupling, but it is explicitly not a measured ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation panel.  The organism count there is $3$, below the declared powered survival requirement of at least $40$ matched species for a population-level translation-survival claim.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The present record is ready as a controlled cross-layer association and mediation decomposition.  It does not establish a causal effect, a ribosome-dynamics mechanism, a synonymous-edit rescue, or a perturbation response.  The translation coordinate is modeled tAI from gene-copy and wobble weights, not observed ribosome occupancy or measured translation efficiency.  The protein coordinate is steady-state abundance and is not separated from mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or degradation rate.  Consequently, no measured translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that evidence and the same $Q_i \to T \to Y$ route is recomputed under the stated controls.
