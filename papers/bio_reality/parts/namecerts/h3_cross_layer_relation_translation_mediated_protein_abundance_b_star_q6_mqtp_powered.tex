\subsection{Modeled-translation mediation of B$^*_{Q6}$ protein abundance}\label{subsec:bioq6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests a cross-layer relation on a finite table of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$. The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The translation readout $t_j$ is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as the codon-count weighted mean of organism-level tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout $p_k$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts. Thus the finite object under discussion is not an unrestricted association between codon residuals and protein output; it is the controlled decomposition of $Q \to P$ after inserting a bounded modeled-translation coordinate $T$.

\paragraph{Rows and rank.}
The computation joined twelve organism-scoped CDS/protein/tRNA panels. The joined row counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} str. 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In every organism the residualized $Q$ block had rank $9$, the residualized $T$ block had rank $1$, and the joint residualized $(Q,T)$ block had rank $10$. The control block had rank $23$ with $24$ control columns, consisting of intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and an M-density baseline. Residual degrees of freedom after controls ranged from $1633$ in \textit{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, so the fitted decomposition is rank-powered for the declared modeled-tAI surface rather than a three-organism saturation artifact.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism the total residual association $c$ is the $R^2$ of protein abundance on the nine-dimensional $Q$ block after controls. The direct residual association $c'$ is the remaining $Q \to P$ contribution after conditioning on modeled tAI. The reported mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} = \frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve mediated fractions lie in the unit interval. The largest value is yeast, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. The next highest values are \textit{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \textit{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, and \textit{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$. Intermediate values occur in worm with $0.4872268357227724$, zebrafish with $0.3157053953614641$, human with $0.27206289160119157$, chicken with $0.1976903964724546$, rat with $0.17489681798543982$, and mouse with $0.15488799907323988$. The smallest positive mediated fractions are mycobacterium with $0.08397602919861291$ and \textit{S. solfataricus} with $0.01850668564589326$.

\paragraph{Concrete energy checks.}
The residualized design also records the energy terms behind the decomposition. In yeast, $X$ explained $882.6526210803958$ units of residual protein-abundance energy, while the direct $X$ improvement after $T$ was only $44.87838061554362$; modeled tAI explained $941.8858618613751$ units on $Y$, and $X$ explained $5.671703404506426$ units on $T$. In human, $X$ explained $1370.6490494847067$ units on $Y$, while the direct improvement after $T$ was $997.7463057114725$, giving a smaller mediated fraction. In \textit{E. coli}, $X$ explained $432.89050065533684$ units on $Y$ and the direct improvement after $T$ was $167.56227978068944$. These organism-by-organism differences are retained as finite-row evidence; they are not collapsed into a universal sign law.

\paragraph{Translation contact status.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival computation used three organisms and found $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute $S_{ij}=3801961.031607896$. That calculation confirmed nonzero modeled codon-adaptation coupling, but it also recorded that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the result is not ribosome profiling or proteomics validation. The present twelve-organism mediation run supplies powered rank for the modeled-tAI-to-protein-abundance decomposition, not an observed ribosome-occupancy mechanism. It therefore upgrades the evidence from a small modeled-readout contact to a larger finite mediation table, while keeping the readout type explicit.

\paragraph{Relation to other finite rows.}
The structural-order audit on the same nine residual axes found that the dominant structural-order direction was $f3\_stress$, with held-out weighted $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$. The yeast abundance/structure partition found $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ and right-tail permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$. These rows show that protein abundance, modeled translation, and structure-proxy partitions can be separated numerically. They do not make the present modeled-tAI mediation into folding evidence.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates carry a powered, organism-scoped statistical relation to protein abundance that is substantially reduced, in several organisms, after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI. This supports a controlled $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition on the declared finite rows. It does not establish a causal translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, perturbation response, rescue experiment, selection law, or universal biological rule. A direct $Q \to P$ association is not interpreted as protein realization unless the $Q \to T$ step is separately present for the same coordinate and readout class. A measured-ribosome or matched-mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency panel would be a stronger contact than modeled tAI; without that contact, this chapter remains a bounded cross-layer mediation record rather than a mechanistic biology claim.
