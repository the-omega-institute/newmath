\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite mediation surface whose rows join three kinds of quantities: a residual coordinate $q_i$ from the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ basis, a translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $Y$.  The coordinate list is $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation readout used in this finite run is per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as the codon-count-weighted mean of organism-specific tAI weights derived from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble parameters.  The downstream readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The fitted control block contains the intercept, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, organism-scoped rows, and an $M$-density baseline; the reported residualized designs have control-column count $24$ and effective control rank $23$ in every organism.

\paragraph{Observed joined panels.}
The run joins twelve organism-scoped panels.  The row counts are $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The corresponding all-tRNA copy totals used for the modeled tAI calculation are $86$, $581$, $8874$, $290$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $407$, $49$, $417$, $275$, and $47$.  The zero raw-weight fallback counts are small and organism-specific: $3$ in \textit{B. subtilis}, $1$ in \textit{E. coli}, $1$ in \textit{M. smegmatis}, $1$ in \textit{S. solfataricus}, and $0$ in the other eight panels.  Thus the row surface is finite, keyed, and large enough for the residualized rank checks used here, while still remaining a modeled-translation surface rather than a measured ribosome-footprint surface.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism the total residual association $c=R^2(Q,Y)$ is compared with the direct residual association after conditioning on translation, $c'=R^2(Q,Y\mid T)$.  The reported scalar is $M^{QTP}=(c-c')/c$ when the denominator is nondegenerate.  In this run all twelve mediation fractions lie in the unit interval.  The finite list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=&\{0.6084594553,0.4872268357,0.3157053954,0.6165167749,\\
&0.6129222528,0.1976903965,0.2720628916,0.1548879991,\\
&0.0839760292,0.1748968180,0.9491551041,0.0185066856\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest mediated fraction is in \textit{S. cerevisiae}, where $c=0.2983762499$, $c'=0.0151709094$, and $c-c'=0.2832053406$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041$.  The smallest is in \textit{S. solfataricus}, where $c=0.0639751425$, $c'=0.0627911747$, and $c-c'=0.0011839679$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.0185066856$.  Intermediate examples include \textit{E. coli}, with $c=0.1076752668$, $c'=0.0416786997$, $c-c'=0.0659965671$, and $M^{QTP}=0.6129222528$, and \textit{H. sapiens}, with $c=0.0445456512$, $c'=0.0324264325$, $c-c'=0.0121192187$, and $M^{QTP}=0.2720628916$.

\paragraph{Rank and residual-energy checks.}
The residualized coordinate block has rank $9$ in every organism, the translation column has residualized rank $1$, and the joint coordinate-plus-translation block has rank $10$.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control block are $4019$, $11638$, $14387$, $13272$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $19372$, $4106$, $13299$, $5792$, and $1633$ in the same organism order as above.  The direct improvement energy of $Q$ after $T$ is positive in every panel: $76.7215701028$, $647.5854070281$, $786.5776326898$, $292.6547690295$, $167.5622797807$, $593.6298832489$, $997.7463057115$, $2055.2519066931$, $168.4113534132$, $422.2704555446$, $44.8783806155$, and $61.5831716268$.  The translation-mediated component is therefore not a rank artifact in this finite design: the coordinate block remains present after translation conditioning, while modeled translation also explains a nonzero part of the original coordinate-to-protein abundance association.

\paragraph{Relation to translation survival and coordinate identity.}
A separate modeled-tAI bounded-coupling run computed the same nine residual coordinates across three organisms and found $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That earlier contact is not a powered measured-translation result because it has only $3$ organisms and uses modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling or measured TE.  The present twelve-organism mediation run is stronger at the protein-abundance interface because it joins modeled per-protein tAI, real CDS codon counts, and PAXdb abundance rows.  It also remains compatible with the independent structural-order axis audit in which $f3\_stress$ was the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  That axis fact is cited here only to identify the finite coordinate basis; it does not turn the protein-abundance mediation surface into a structure or folding claim.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The supported readback is the following: in a finite set of organism-matched CDS/protein rows, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nonzero association with measured steady-state protein abundance, and a positive fraction of that association is absorbed when the modeled per-protein tAI readout is inserted as an intermediate variable.  This is a controlled statistical mediation surface $Q \to T \to Y$, not a causal intervention and not a proof that synonymous residual coordinates realize protein output through a translation mechanism.  Direct $Q\to Y$ association is therefore admissible only as a residual association unless the $Q\to T$ step is present in the same controlled surface.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No claim about measured translation, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, mechanism, or universality follows from this row set alone.  Modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation readout derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights; it is not ribosome profiling.  PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-abundance measurement; it is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or degradation.  Any stronger interpretation would require an additional layer-matched contact, such as organism- and CDS-matched ribosome profiling with mRNA control for measured TE, perturbation or rescue rows for mechanism, or structure/function assays for higher biological layers.
