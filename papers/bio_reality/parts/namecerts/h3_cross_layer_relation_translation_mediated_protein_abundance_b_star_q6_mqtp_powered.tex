\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein abundance for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record studies a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation-side coordinate $t_j$ is not a measured ribosome trace in this experiment: it is per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed from real CDS codon counts, GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts, and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein-side coordinate $p_k$ is $\log_{10}(abundance\_ppm)$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to the same CDS rows.  The fitted surface is therefore a controlled statistical decomposition of codon-residual association with steady-state protein abundance after a modeled translation readout has been inserted between $Q$ and $P$.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
Each organism was residualized against the same baseline family: intercept, CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The fitted matrix used $24$ control columns, with rank $Z=23$, rank $\widetilde X=9$, rank $\widetilde T=1$, and rank $[\widetilde X,\widetilde T]=10$ in every organism.  Thus the mediation statistic is not a one-column codon-count echo: the nine-dimensional residual block remains full rank after controls, while the modeled tAI readout contributes one residualized translation-weight coordinate.  The organism rows were powered at the rank level, with residual degrees of freedom ranging from $1633$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19372$ in \emph{Mus musculus}.  The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism the finite-row statistic compares the total controlled association $c=R^2(Q,P)$ with the remaining direct association $c'=R^2(Q,P\mid T)$ after the modeled tAI readout is added.  The reported mediated fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},\\
0 &\leq M^{QTP} \leq 1
\end{aligned}
$$
for all twelve organism rows.  The strongest finite-row mediation fraction is in yeast, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Large positive fractions also appear in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, and \emph{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$.  Intermediate values occur in \emph{C. elegans} at $0.4872268357227724$, zebrafish at $0.3157053953614641$, human at $0.27206289160119157$, chicken at $0.1976903964724546$, rat at $0.17489681798543982$, and mouse at $0.15488799907323988$.  The weakest positive rows are \emph{M. smegmatis} at $0.08397602919861291$ and \emph{S. solfataricus} at $0.01850668564589326$.  The important point is the sign and admissible range: every computed $M^{QTP}$ lies inside the unit interval, so the finite-row matrix supports a bounded translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition rather than a negative or ill-posed mediation surface.

\paragraph{Readout scale and organism-specific contact.}
The translation coordinate is finite but model-based.  Its tRNA-copy support varies sharply across organisms: all-tRNA copy totals are $86$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, $581$ in \emph{C. elegans}, $8874$ in zebrafish, $290$ in fruit fly, $90$ in \emph{E. coli}, $288$ in chicken, $432$ in human, $407$ in mouse, $49$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, $417$ in rat, $275$ in yeast, and $47$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.  Zero raw wobble weights were filled by a geometric-mean rule in three \emph{B. subtilis} entries, one \emph{E. coli} entry, one \emph{M. smegmatis} entry, and one \emph{S. solfataricus} entry, while the other eight organisms required no such fill.  This makes the contact suitable for bounded codon-adaptation readback, not for direct observation of ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Relation to adjacent finite rows.}
The broader $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival check had already computed modeled-tAI coupling in three organisms, with $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but that earlier matrix remained underpowered for a species-level survival claim because it had only $3$ organisms rather than the required $40$ matched species.  The present record changes the row geometry: it joins twelve organism-specific CDS and protein-abundance surfaces and evaluates a controlled $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition.  The yeast structure-mediation row gives a useful contrast: in yeast, the direct residual-to-protein association had $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability reduced the incremental $Q$ term to $0.13773967696311287$, and adding the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy changed it only to $0.13759011208318542$.  Its structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$.  Separately, the structural-order axis audit found that $f3\_stress$ was the only fold-level sign-flip-passing coordinate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  These adjacent rows do not turn the present mediation surface into a structural or mechanistic finding; they only show that the same residual basis has other bounded contacts with different readout layers.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite evidence supports the following chapter-local statement: the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a controlled, positive, translation-conditioned statistical decomposition into matched protein abundance when the intermediate readout is per-protein modeled tAI and the output is PAXdb abundance.  The admissible readback is the matrix $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ under the declared controls, not a direct residual-to-protein realization claim.  A row with positive $Q\to P$ association is not accepted as protein realization unless the $Q\to T$ step is present in the same finite construction.  Likewise, no observed translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological rule follows from this surface unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that contact and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
