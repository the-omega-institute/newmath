\subsection{Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Decomposition for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residuals}
\label{subsec:q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record tests a finite cross-layer surface whose rows are matched coding sequences with three observed or computed slots: a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate vector $Q$, a translation readout $T$, and a protein-abundance readout $P$. The coordinate block $Q$ has nine residual coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate is the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the coding sequence and $w_c$ is computed from organism-scoped GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real coding-sequence codon counts. Thus the admissible readback is not a direct $Q\to P$ assertion; it is the controlled decomposition of $Q\to T\to P$ on the same finite rows.

The finite matrix is therefore not blocked by rank collapse.

\paragraph{Translation-readout prior contact.}
The same coordinate family also has a bounded modeled-tAI contact over three organisms. That contact computed all $61$ sense-codon weights for \textit{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and dos Reis wobble parameters, with no zero raw-weight fallback in those organism-level codon tables. The organism count there is only $3$, so it is not a powered multi-species survival law; it supplies a bounded translation-weight route that makes the present $Q\to T\to P$ matrix interpretable as a modeled readout decomposition.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite-row verdict is positive only for this narrow claim: on the joined coding-sequence, modeled-tAI, and PAXdb abundance rows, $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates admit a controlled statistical mediation decomposition through $T$ before reading out protein abundance. The result does not by itself establish measured ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from ribosome profiling, causal mediation, synonymous-edit response, perturbation rescue, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality. A direct $Q\to P$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives an explicit translation-readout step under the declared controls. Likewise, modeled tAI is not ribosome profiling, and steady-state protein abundance is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context. Any claim at those layers requires a separate matched contact and a new finite statistic at that layer.
