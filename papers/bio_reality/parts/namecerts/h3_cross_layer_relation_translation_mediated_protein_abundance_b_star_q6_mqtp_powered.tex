\subsection{Modeled translation mediation of protein abundance}\label{subsec:bioq6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.} This record concerns a finite mediation surface with triples $(q_i,t,p)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $t$ is a per-protein modeled translation-adaptation readout, and $p$ is a matched protein-abundance readout. The coordinate list is
$$\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The readout $t$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI over the coding sequence using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The protein readout $p$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts. Thus the tested object is not a direct residual-to-protein map. It is the controlled decomposition of a residual association after the same rows also carry a translation-weight coordinate.

\paragraph{Rows and rank.} The joined finite panel contains six organism-level blocks: $14410$ proteins for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The tRNA-copy totals used in the modeled tAI readout are respectively $8874$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $49$, and $275$. Zero raw wobble weights were filled by the geometric mean in only two organism blocks: one codon class in \emph{E. coli} and one in \emph{M. smegmatis}; the remaining four blocks had zero such fills. In every organism block the control design has $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$, so the joint residual design is rank-admissible for the stated nine-coordinate mediation scalar. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $14387$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $4106$, and $5792$ in the same organism order.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.} Let $X$ denote the nine-column $B^*_{Q6}$ residual matrix, let $T$ denote modeled per-protein tAI, let $Y$ denote log protein abundance, and let $Z$ contain amino-acid composition, organism-level terms, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance controls as encoded in the finite row design. The reported scalar is the fraction of the total residual-to-protein association attenuated when the modeled translation readout is conditioned on:
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},\\
c &= R^2(Y\sim X\mid Z),\\
c' &= R^2(Y\sim X\mid Z,T).
\end{aligned}$$
The six mediated fractions are all in the unit interval:
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{danio}}&=0.3157053954, & M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{ecoli}}&=0.6129222528,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{gallus}}&=0.1976903965, & M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{human}}&=0.2720628916,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{mycobacterium}}&=0.0839760292, & M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{yeast}}&=0.9491551041.
\end{aligned}$$
The corresponding total residual-to-protein $R^2$ values are $0.0461352981$, $0.1076752668$, $0.0398079360$, $0.0445456512$, $0.0320900917$, and $0.2983762499$. After conditioning on modeled tAI, the direct residual-to-protein $R^2$ values are $0.0315701356$, $0.0416786997$, $0.0319382893$, $0.0324264325$, $0.0293952932$, and $0.0151709094$. The induced indirect components $c-c'$ are therefore $0.0145651625$, $0.0659965671$, $0.0078696466$, $0.0121192187$, $0.0026947985$, and $0.2832053406$.

\paragraph{Energy checks.} The finite residualization audit separates the three relevant improvements. For \emph{Danio rerio}, $X$ explains $1149.4722117608$ units of residual protein-abundance energy, $T$ explains $705.5230335367$, and the incremental contribution of $T$ given $X$ is $342.6284544658$. For \emph{E. coli}, the corresponding values are $432.8905006553$, $582.5912438239$, and $317.2630229493$. For chicken they are $739.9012558728$, $279.8218625433$, and $133.5504899193$; for human, $1370.6490494847$, $850.7864327764$, and $477.8836890032$; for \emph{M. smegmatis}, $183.8503781357$, $15.8135473274$, and $0.3745226049$; and for yeast, $882.6526210804$, $941.8858618614$, and $104.1116213965$. These values show that the finite surface is not empty: the modeled tAI coordinate has nonzero residual contact with protein abundance after the declared control projection, and the attenuation fraction can be computed in each organism block.

\paragraph{Interpretation.} The supported claim is narrow. The evidence permits the statement that, on these joined finite rows, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface admits a controlled statistical decomposition through modeled per-protein tAI before matched protein abundance is read. It does not establish a causal effect of synonymous residual coordinates, and it does not show that tAI is an observed ribosome-profiling measurement. The earlier modeled-tAI contact used only three organisms for the survival-matrix construction, with $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but that was explicitly not a powered ribo-seq or proteomics validation. The present six-organism protein panel is rank-sufficient for this regression surface, not a perturbation, rescue, or mechanism experiment.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not admitted as protein realization unless a same-row $Q_i\to T$ step is represented and controlled. In this chapter the translation coordinate is modeled tAI, not measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency. Therefore no measured translation claim, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, biological mechanism claim, organismal-phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from the reported positive mediated fractions. Such claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled finite statistics. Protein abundance itself also remains a steady-state downstream readout and is not isolated here from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway state, or matched mRNA effects beyond the controls present in the finite row design.
