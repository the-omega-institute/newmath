\subsection{Translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as a finite set of sequence-derived predictors whose downstream protein-abundance contact is admitted only through an intervening translation readout.  The tested residual list has nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate $T$ is the per-protein mean modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted tAI over the CDS, using GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate $Y$ is measured PAXdb protein abundance after the readback $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  The finite rows join real CDS codon counts, modeled tAI weights, and measured abundance for three organisms: $3739$ proteins in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $4129$ proteins in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ proteins in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding tRNA copy totals used for the modeled readout are $90$, $49$, and $275$, with one geometric-mean fallback for a zero raw wobble weight in each bacterial panel and no fallback in yeast.

\paragraph{Controls and ranks.}
The control block $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organismal setting where applicable, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance controls rather than promoted biological coordinates.  In each organism-specific residualization, the design uses $24$ control columns with effective rank $23$.  After $Z$ is removed, the residual degrees of freedom are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$ for \emph{E. coli}, \emph{M. smegmatis}, and yeast, respectively.  The residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the residualized translation block has rank $1$, and the joint $Q,T$ block has rank $10$ in all three panels.  Thus the fitted mediation surface is not a rank-collapsed all-row identity check; it is a finite controlled decomposition on a rank-admissible matrix.  The translation residual energies are $0.4728468725814029$, $0.041191576348982066$, and $6.90537888244409$, while the protein-abundance residual energies are $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the finite-row statistic compares the total controlled $Q \to Y$ association with the remaining direct $Q \to Y$ association after conditioning on $T$.  The reported scalar is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q \to Y\mid Z)-R^2(Q \to Y\mid T,Z)}{R^2(Q \to Y\mid Z)},\\
0 &\leq M^{QTP}\leq 1
\end{aligned}
$$
for the present rows.  In \emph{E. coli}, $R^2(Q\to Y\mid Z)=0.10767526677985896$, the direct value after translation conditioning is $0.04167869969498872$, the indirect difference is $0.06599656708487024$, and the mediated fraction is $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  In \emph{M. smegmatis}, the corresponding values are $0.03209009170649934$, $0.029395293228368185$, $0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.  In yeast, they are $0.29837624994140827$, $0.0151709093608293$, $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  All three mediated fractions lie in the unit interval, so the readout behaves as an attenuation decomposition rather than as a sign-reversing or numerically degenerate statistic.

\paragraph{Energy accounting.}
The same controlled residualization reports how much energy the translation coordinate and the $Q$ block explain on each side of the decomposition.  In \emph{E. coli}, $T$ explains $582.5912438239341$ units of $Y$ residual energy, $Q$ explains $432.89050065533684$ units of $Y$ residual energy, the incremental gain of $T$ after $Q$ is $317.26302294928666$, and the remaining direct gain of $Q$ after $T$ is $167.56227978068944$.  In \emph{M. smegmatis}, the same four quantities are $15.813547327443837$, $183.85037813566998$, $0.37452260494680445$, and $168.41135341317295$.  In yeast, they are $941.8858618613751$, $882.6526210803958$, $104.11162139652299$, and $44.87838061554362$.  The $Q\to T$ explained energies are small in the two bacteria, $0.256128714706293$ and $0.026187928294862578$, and larger in yeast, $5.671703404506426$, so the organism panels do not support a single universal sign or magnitude rule.  They support only the narrower statement that the finite modeled-tAI coordinate can absorb a positive part of the controlled $Q\to Y$ association in these joined rows.

\paragraph{Readback verdict.}
The chapter-level verdict is therefore a controlled cross-layer relation, not a causal translation mechanism.  The finite data support a statistical matrix of the form $Q_i \to T \to P$ for the joined CDS and protein-abundance rows, with the strongest mediated fraction in yeast, an intermediate mediated fraction in \emph{E. coli}, and a weak but positive mediated fraction in \emph{M. smegmatis}.  Direct residual-to-protein association is not admitted as protein realization unless the translation coordinate is carried in the conditioning path.  Matched mRNA abundance remains a transcription-layer control; amino-acid sequence, GC3, length, M-density, and organismal background remain controls; and the modeled tAI readout remains a codon-adaptation proxy rather than a ribosome-profiling measurement.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No conclusion about measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation response, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality follows from these rows alone.  The present contact contains modeled per-protein tAI and measured steady-state protein abundance, not ribosome-profiling time series, synonymously edited rescue experiments, structural assays, localization assays, turnover measurements, or functional perturbations.  A higher-layer claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed after the same $Q_i \to T$ step survives under the declared controls.  Until such contact is attached, the finite-row result remains a bounded protein-abundance mediation readout for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.
