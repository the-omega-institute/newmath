\subsection{Modeled translation mediation of B$^\ast_{Q6}$ residuals and protein abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows join a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation-layer readout $T$, and a protein-abundance readout $P$.  The residual coordinates are the nine projected $B^\ast_{Q6}$ axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation coordinate used in the finite run is per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_g=\frac{\sum_c n_{g,c} w_c}{\sum_c n_{g,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_{g,c}$ is the real CDS codon count for protein $g$ and $w_c$ is the tAI weight obtained from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $Y_g=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm}_g)$ from PAXdb abundance joined to the same CDS rows.  The control surface residualizes amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline; the fitted design has control rank $23$ and $24$ control columns in every organism-specific regression.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the finite audit compares the total residual-to-protein fit $c=R^2(Q,P)$ with the direct fit after conditioning on modeled tAI, $c'=R^2(Q,P\mid T)$.  The reported scalar mediation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c},
\end{aligned}
$$
whenever $c>0$, and all twelve reported values lie in the unit interval.  The total $R^2$, direct $R^2$ after $T$, indirect increment, and mediated fraction are: \emph{B. subtilis} $c=0.0619062459$, $c'=0.0242388052$, $c-c'=0.0376674407$, $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553$; \emph{C. elegans} $c=0.1021790676$, $c'=0.0523946838$, $c-c'=0.0497843838$, $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357$; \emph{D. rerio} $c=0.0461352981$, $c'=0.0315701356$, $c-c'=0.0145651625$, $M^{QTP}=0.3157053954$; \emph{D. melanogaster} $c=0.0770952887$, $c'=0.0295647500$, $c-c'=0.0475305388$, $M^{QTP}=0.6165167749$; \emph{E. coli} $c=0.1076752668$, $c'=0.0416786997$, $c-c'=0.0659965671$, $M^{QTP}=0.6129222528$; \emph{G. gallus} $c=0.0398079360$, $c'=0.0319382893$, $c-c'=0.0078696466$, $M^{QTP}=0.1976903965$; \emph{H. sapiens} $c=0.0445456512$, $c'=0.0324264325$, $c-c'=0.0121192187$, $M^{QTP}=0.2720628916$; \emph{M. musculus} $c=0.0498279841$, $c'=0.0421102273$, $c-c'=0.0077177567$, $M^{QTP}=0.1548879991$; \emph{M. smegmatis} $c=0.0320900917$, $c'=0.0293952932$, $c-c'=0.0026947985$, $M^{QTP}=0.0839760292$; \emph{R. norvegicus} $c=0.0276399860$, $c'=0.0228058404$, $c-c'=0.0048341456$, $M^{QTP}=0.1748968180$; \emph{S. cerevisiae} $c=0.2983762499$, $c'=0.0151709094$, $c-c'=0.2832053406$, $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041$; and \emph{S. solfataricus} $c=0.0639751425$, $c'=0.0627911747$, $c-c'=0.0011839679$, $M^{QTP}=0.0185066856$.

\paragraph{Rank and residual-energy checks.}
The design is not rank-collapsed in the reported organism rows: $rank(\widetilde X)=9$, $rank(\widetilde T)=1$, and $rank(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$ in every organism, with residual degrees of freedom ranging from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in \emph{M. musculus}.  The modeled tAI residual energy is small but nonzero in all rows: examples include $0.0170550261$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}, $0.0411915763$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, $0.2437853666$ in \emph{M. musculus}, $1.1744686487$ in humans, $4.4032713742$ in \emph{C. elegans}, and $6.9053788824$ in yeast.  The protein-abundance residual energy ranges from $980.7615793$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $48806.4785641$ in \emph{M. musculus}.  These checks justify reading the table as a finite, nondegenerate statistical decomposition of a joined codon-residual, modeled-translation, and protein-abundance surface.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The biological reading is deliberately narrow.  A direct $Q\to P$ association is not counted as protein realization on its own; it is decomposed through a bounded $Q\to T\to P$ statistic, where $T$ is modeled per-protein tAI and $P$ is matched protein abundance.  The strongest finite entry is yeast, where $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041$ and the direct residual association drops from $c=0.2983762499$ to $c'=0.0151709094$ after conditioning on modeled tAI.  Several rows are much weaker, especially \emph{S. solfataricus} with $M^{QTP}=0.0185066856$ and \emph{M. smegmatis} with $M^{QTP}=0.0839760292$, so the proper statement is heterogeneous cross-organism bounded mediation, not a uniform law.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite audits.}
The modeled-tAI contact is already nonempty at the translation layer: in the separate three-organism modeled-tAI survival run, the finite matrix has $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, while the same audit explicitly refuses powered survival from only $3$ organisms.  A yeast structure-mediated abundance decomposition gives $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.1377396770$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.1375901121$, with $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.0037742485$ and right-tail permutation value $0.0279720280$ for the structure association.  A turnover audit remains negative for measured turnover after expression, TE, pLDDT, and N-end controls: $R^2_{L2}=0.0557408516$, $R^2_{L3}=0.0502788722$, $\Delta R^2=-0.0054619794$, matched-bin $p=0.8855721393$, and recoded $p=1.0$.  These neighboring rows keep the present claim within modeled translation-mediated protein abundance, not turnover, structure, localization, function, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The finite table supports only a controlled statistical mediation statement using modeled tAI and matched protein abundance.  It does not show ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, perturbation response, rescue, or a universal biological rule.  Modeled tAI weights are not ribosome-profiling data; PAXdb abundance is not an isolated causal protein-output measurement; and conditioning on $T$ in a cross-sectional table is not a mechanism.  Any claim beyond this bounded mediation surface requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
