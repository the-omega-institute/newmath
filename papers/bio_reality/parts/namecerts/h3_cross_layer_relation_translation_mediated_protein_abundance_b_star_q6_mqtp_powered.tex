\subsection{Translation-mediated protein-abundance readback for $B^*_{{Q6}}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record evaluates a finite mediation surface whose rows join three objects: a $B^*_{{Q6}}$ residual coordinate $q_i$, a translation readout $T$, and a matched protein-abundance readout $P$.  The residual side has nine coordinates,
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation readout used here is not a ribosome-footprint measurement: it is the per-protein mean modeled tAI obtained from GtRNAdb all-tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance, joined to real CDS codon counts.  The readback is therefore a controlled statistical decomposition of $Q \to T \to P$, not a direct molecular intervention.

\paragraph{Controls and admissible ranks.}
For each organism, the residualized design first removes amino-acid composition, CDS length, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline, with the intercept included in the control block.  The resulting control block has $23$ effective rank from $24$ control columns in every organism reported here.  The $Q$ block has rank $9$, the modeled tAI block has rank $1$, and the combined $Q,T$ block has rank $10$ after residualization.  Thus the mediation scalar is not being read from a rank-collapsed design.  Across the twelve organism rows, the joined protein counts are
$$\begin{aligned}
&n_{\mathrm{B.subtilis}}=4042,\quad n_{\mathrm{C.elegans}}=11661,\quad n_{\mathrm{D.rerio}}=14410,\quad n_{\mathrm{D.melanogaster}}=13295,\\
&n_{\mathrm{E.coli}}=3739,\quad n_{\mathrm{G.gallus}}=12418,\quad n_{\mathrm{H.sapiens}}=19053,\quad n_{\mathrm{M.musculus}}=19395,\\
&n_{\mathrm{M.smegmatis}}=4129,\quad n_{\mathrm{R.norvegicus}}=13322,\quad n_{\mathrm{S.cerevisiae}}=5815,\quad n_{\mathrm{S.solfataricus}}=1656.
\end{aligned}$$
The residual degrees of freedom after the control block range from $1633$ in $S.\,solfataricus$ to $19372$ in $M.\,musculus$, and every organism is marked rank-sufficient for the fitted $Q,T,P$ decomposition.

\paragraph{Translation readout values and tRNA coverage.}
The modeled tAI layer is finite and organism-specific.  The all-tRNA copy totals used for the per-protein readout are $86$, $581$, $8874$, $290$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $407$, $49$, $417$, $275$, and $47$ for the twelve organisms in the order listed above.  Zero raw codon weights needed geometric-mean filling in only four rows: $3$ codons for $B.\,subtilis$, $1$ for $E.\,coli$, $1$ for $M.\,smegmatis$, and $1$ for $S.\,solfataricus$; the other eight organisms required no such filling.  A separate modeled-tAI survival audit on three organisms reported $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$, but that audit explicitly remained a modeled-tAI contact and not ribosome-seq or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
Let $X$ be the residualized $9$-coordinate $B^*_{{Q6}}$ block, $T$ the residualized per-protein modeled tAI scalar, and $Y$ the residualized protein abundance.  For each organism, the total residual-to-protein association is
$$\begin{aligned}
c &= R^2(Y\sim X),
\end{aligned}$$
and the direct association after the translation readout is included is
$$\begin{aligned}
c' &= R^2(Y\sim X\mid T).
\end{aligned}$$
The reported mediation fraction is
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},
\end{aligned}$$
when the denominator is positive.  In all twelve rows the fraction lies in $[0,1]$, so the fitted translation readout absorbs a nonnegative share of the $Q$-to-protein association rather than increasing it.

\paragraph{Energy diagnostics.}
The decomposition is also visible in residual energy terms.  In $S.\,cerevisiae$, $X$ explains $5.671703404506426$ units of modeled-tAI residual energy and $882.6526210803958$ units of protein-abundance residual energy, while $T$ explains $941.8858618613751$ units of protein-abundance residual energy.  In $H.\,sapiens$, the corresponding quantities are $0.3099459586758378$, $1370.6490494847067$, and $850.7864327764183$.  In $M.\,musculus$, they are $0.08224668862908166$, $2431.9284360407714$, and $923.4025869376133$.  These values explain why the fitted mediation fraction is not a universal constant: the modeled translation readout has organism-specific scale and relation to the protein-abundance residual surface.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The ready verdict is a bounded one.  The finite rows establish that the nine-coordinate $B^*_{{Q6}}$ residual block has a reproducible statistical decomposition against matched protein abundance when conditioned on modeled per-protein tAI.  The direct residual-to-protein association is not used as a standalone biological claim; it is only read through the comparison of $c$ and $c'$ after the translation readout is present.  The result is strongest as a cross-layer abundance-residual audit: the table says that a modeled translation coordinate can absorb a positive share of the $Q$-to-$P$ association under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, folding pathway, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this table alone.  Modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation annotation computed from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights; it is not a measured ribosome-footprint row.  PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-abundance readout; it is not isolated from mRNA abundance, degradation, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context.  Any stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched measurement and a new controlled statistic.  Within this chapter, the claim is therefore limited to the finite $Q \to T \to P$ statistical mediation surface reported above.
