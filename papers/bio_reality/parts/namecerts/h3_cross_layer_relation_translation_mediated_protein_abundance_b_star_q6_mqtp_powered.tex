\subsection{Translation-Mediated Protein-Abundance Readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether a finite collection of synonymous residual coordinates can be read at a downstream protein-abundance surface only after passing through a bounded translation readout.  The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The tested translation coordinate is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as the codon-count weighted mean of organism-specific tAI weights from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The row surface covers twelve organism-level joins: $4042$ proteins in \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ in \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ in \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ in \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ in \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ in \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, $13322$ in \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The total joined surface therefore contains $122935$ protein rows before organism-wise residualization.

\paragraph{Controlled mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the analysis residualizes amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline before estimating the joint nine-coordinate relation.  The design has $24$ control columns, residual rank $23$ for the control block, residual rank $9$ for $X_Q$, residual rank $1$ for the tAI coordinate $T$, and residual rank $10$ for the combined $X_Q,T$ block in every organism.  The finite statistic compares the total residual-to-protein fit $c=R^2(Q,P)$ with the direct fit after conditioning on translation, $c'=R^2(Q,P\mid T)$, and records the translation-attributed fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{c-c'}{c},\\
\Delta^{QTP} &= c-c'.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a variance decomposition on matched finite rows.  It is not a perturbation effect and does not by itself identify a molecular route.

\paragraph{Observed organism-wise values.}
The organism-wise mediated fractions are all inside the unit interval.  The largest value is in \emph{S. cerevisiae}, where $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $\Delta^{QTP}=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ on $5815$ joined proteins.  The strongest bacterial examples are \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 with $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, $\Delta^{QTP}=0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ on $3739$ rows, and \emph{B. subtilis} with $c=0.061906245886660105$, $c'=0.02423880523185755$, $\Delta^{QTP}=0.037667440654802556$, and $M^{QTP}=0.6084594553474505$ on $4042$ rows.  \emph{D. melanogaster} gives $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$ with $\Delta^{QTP}=0.047530538755721025$ on $13295$ rows, while \emph{C. elegans} gives $M^{QTP}=0.4872268357227724$ with $\Delta^{QTP}=0.04978438377417191$ on $11661$ rows.  Vertebrate values are positive but smaller: \emph{D. rerio} has $M^{QTP}=0.3157053953614641$, \emph{H. sapiens} has $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$, \emph{G. gallus} has $M^{QTP}=0.1976903964724546$, \emph{R. norvegicus} has $M^{QTP}=0.17489681798543982$, and \emph{M. musculus} has $M^{QTP}=0.15488799907323988$.  The smallest positive fractions are in \emph{M. smegmatis}, with $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$, and \emph{S. solfataricus}, with $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$.

\paragraph{Rank and energy checks.}
Every organism passes the declared finite-rank sufficiency check.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls range from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in \emph{M. musculus}.  The yeast row surface is notable because $X_Q$ explains $882.6526210803958$ units of protein-abundance residual energy, while modeled tAI explains $941.8858618613751$ units on the same protein response and $5.671703404506426$ units on the translation coordinate.  In human rows, $X_Q$ explains $1370.6490494847067$ units of protein-abundance residual energy, modeled tAI explains $850.7864327764183$ units on protein abundance, and the residual tAI energy is $1.174468648693672$.  In mouse rows, the direct $X_Q$ improvement after conditioning on $T$ remains large at $2055.2519066931027$, so the positive mediated fraction is reported together with the remaining direct association rather than promoted to full absorption.  The tRNA-copy inputs are finite and organism-specific; for example, the tRNA all-copy totals are $275$ in yeast, $90$ in the \emph{E. coli} route, $432$ in human, $407$ in mouse, and $8874$ in zebrafish.  Zero raw wobble-weight fallbacks are absent in most organisms, but occur in three codons for \emph{B. subtilis} and one codon each for \emph{E. coli}, \emph{M. smegmatis}, and \emph{S. solfataricus}.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation contact.}
A separate modeled-tAI contact on three organisms established that the finite codon-weight readout can be computed for all $61$ sense codons, with all standard amino-acid families represented in the local tRNA payloads and with $81$ modeled coupling entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold.  That earlier contact also records the limitation: three organisms are insufficient for a powered survival statement.  The present twelve-organism protein-abundance surface is therefore read as a larger matched-row mediation audit, not as ribosome profiling or as direct observation of translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The ready verdict is restricted to the following statement: a joint $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has positive controlled protein-abundance association in all twelve finite organism surfaces, and part of that association is reduced after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI.  The record permits the notation $Q \rightarrow T \rightarrow P$ only as a controlled statistical readback on matched rows.  It does not establish observed ribosome occupancy, translation realization in vivo, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, perturbation rescue, or a universal biological rule.  Any such claim requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
