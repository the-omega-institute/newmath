\subsection{Modeled-translation mediation of $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residuals into matched protein abundance}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite cross-layer table whose rows are triples $(q_i,T_o,P_o)$: $q_i$ ranges over nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $T_o$ is an organism-specific modeled per-protein tAI readout, and $P_o$ is the matched protein-abundance readout for organism $o$.  The nine residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation readout is the per-protein mean modeled tAI
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_o(g)=\frac{\sum_c n_{g,c}w_{o,c}}{\sum_c n_{g,c}},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_{g,c}$ is the codon count of codon $c$ in protein-coding sequence $g$, and $w_{o,c}$ is computed from all-tRNA copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein readout is $Y_o(g)=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb protein abundance after joining to real CDS codon counts.  The residualization matrix contains an intercept, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline; the reported control rank is $23$ with $24$ control columns before rank reduction.

\paragraph{Matched finite rows.}
The run joined twelve organism-level protein tables to CDS and tRNA-copy data.  The joined row counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The corresponding all-tRNA copy totals used in the modeled tAI calculation were $86$, $581$, $8874$, $290$, $90$, $288$, $432$, $407$, $49$, $417$, $275$, and $47$.  The geometric-mean fallback for zero raw codon weights was needed only in four organisms: $3$ codons in \textit{B. subtilis}, $1$ in \textit{E. coli}, $1$ in \textit{M. smegmatis}, and $1$ in \textit{S. solfataricus}; the remaining organisms had zero fallback counts.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, the statistic compares the total residual association $c=R^2(Q\to P)$ with the direct residual association $c'=R^2(Q\to P\mid T)$ after the modeled translation readout is included.  The mediated increment is $c-c'$, and the reported bounded fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=\frac{c-c'}{c}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Every organism in the finite table had $0\leq M^{QTP}\leq 1$ and had sufficient post-control rank: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q,\widetilde T)=10$.  The largest mediated fraction was observed in yeast, with $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, $c-c'=0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Large mediated fractions also occurred in \textit{D. melanogaster} with $M^{QTP}=0.6165167748739756$, \textit{E. coli} with $0.612922252793667$, and \textit{B. subtilis} with $0.6084594553474505$.  Intermediate values were found in \textit{C. elegans} with $0.4872268357227724$, \textit{D. rerio} with $0.3157053953614641$, \textit{H. sapiens} with $0.27206289160119157$, \textit{G. gallus} with $0.1976903964724546$, \textit{R. norvegicus} with $0.17489681798543982$, and \textit{M. musculus} with $0.15488799907323988$.  The smallest positive bounded fractions were $0.08397602919861291$ for \textit{M. smegmatis} and $0.01850668564589326$ for \textit{S. solfataricus}.

\paragraph{Residual-energy audit.}
The statistic is not a direct protein-only correlation: it is computed after projecting away the declared baseline covariates.  Yeast supplies the clearest scale check.  In \textit{S. cerevisiae}, the post-control degrees of freedom were $5792$, the residual energy of the modeled tAI readout was $6.90537888244409$, and the residual energy of log protein abundance was $2958.1865890925337$.  The $B^\ast_{Q6}$ block explained $5.671703404506426$ units of residual tAI energy and $882.6526210803958$ units of residual protein-abundance energy; after conditioning on modeled tAI, the direct residual-to-protein improvement energy was only $44.87838061554362$.  This is the local finite-row reason the yeast mediated fraction is close to one.  By contrast, \textit{S. solfataricus} had only $1633$ post-control residual degrees of freedom, $T$ residual energy $0.01705502608336432$, $Y$ residual energy $980.7615792975887$, and a small mediated increment $0.0011839678516385038$, so the same formal statistic remains bounded but biologically weak there.

\paragraph{Relation to other finite audits.}
The modeled-tAI survival calculation that feeds this record had previously computed the same nine residual coordinates and found $81$ modeled-tAI coupling entries above the threshold $0.01$ across three organisms, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$; that earlier result was explicitly labeled as modeled coupling rather than ribosome-profiling validation.  A separate yeast structure-mediation audit reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with a small structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$.  These neighboring checks are consistent with the present readback: the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ block can carry a finite protein-abundance association, and modeled translation covariates can absorb a substantial part of it, but that statement is still a controlled statistical decomposition rather than a perturbative molecular mechanism.  Two further audits sharpen the boundary.  A dynamic RPF-protein mass-balance run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals had $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits after the larger model, while a turnover-residual audit on $3290$ genes had $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$ and $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits.  These negative increments warn against promoting the present modeled-tAI mediation into turnover, kinetic closure, or broad proteostasis claims.

\paragraph{Scientific verdict.}
The finite-row result supports the following limited readback: across twelve joined organism tables, the nine-dimensional $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual block has a controlled association with matched protein abundance, and conditioning on an organism-specific modeled tAI readout removes a positive fraction of that association in every organism.  The strongest case is yeast, where $94.91551041216973\%$ of the residual-to-protein $R^2$ is represented by the modeled-tAI conditioning step; the weakest case is \textit{S. solfataricus}, where the corresponding fraction is $1.850668564589326\%$.  This is enough for a cross-layer abundance-decomposition record, because the table contains explicit $Q\to T\to P$ rows, post-control ranks, organism-level row counts, and bounded mediated fractions.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The readout $T$ is modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights, not a ribosome-profiling measurement.  The protein readout is steady-state abundance and is not isolated from transcript abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or experimental quantification bias.  Therefore this section does not claim observed translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, synonymous-edit rescue, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality.  Any one of those conclusions would require an additional layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic at that layer.  In particular, a direct $Q\to P$ association is not read as protein realization unless the intervening $Q\to T$ surface is present and survives the declared controls; here that intervening surface is a modeled adaptation readout, so the conclusion remains a bounded statistical mediation record.
