\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Protein-Abundance Mediation for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-mediated-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record evaluates a finite cross-layer table whose rows join a $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, a translation readout, and a matched protein-abundance readout.  The coordinate side has nine residual axes:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate used in the powered run is per-protein modeled tAI, computed as codon-count-weighted mean adaptation weight from GtRNAdb all-tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble penalties.  The protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb, joined to real coding-sequence codon counts.  The control block is not optional: each organism is residualized against amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, an $M$-density baseline on the nine-coordinate support, and the organism-level intercept structure.  In the implementation this gives $23$ retained control rank columns and $24$ control columns before rank reduction for every organism in the powered table.

\paragraph{Joined rows and rank.}
The powered table contains twelve organism-level joined panels.  The row counts are $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \textit{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \textit{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \textit{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In every organism the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$, the translation block has rank $1$, and the joint $Q,T$ block has rank $10$.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control block range from $1633$ in \textit{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, so the reported mediation fractions are not produced by the three-organism rank saturation that affected the earlier modeled-tAI boundary check.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
For each organism, let $X$ denote the residualized $9$-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix, let $T$ denote residualized modeled tAI, and let $Y$ denote residualized protein abundance.  The reported scalar is the fraction of the total $Q$-to-protein association removed after conditioning on $T$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Y\sim X)-R^2(Y\sim X\mid T)}{R^2(Y\sim X)},\\
c &= R^2(Y\sim X),\qquad c' = R^2(Y\sim X\mid T).
\end{aligned}
$$
All twelve fractions lie in $[0,1]$.  The observed list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}=&(0.608459,\ 0.487227,\ 0.315705,\ 0.616517,\ 0.612922,\ 0.197690,\\
&0.272063,\ 0.154888,\ 0.083976,\ 0.174897,\ 0.949155,\ 0.018507),
\end{aligned}
$$
ordered as \textit{B. subtilis}, worm, zebrafish, fly, \textit{E. coli}, chicken, human, mouse, \textit{M. smegmatis}, rat, yeast, and \textit{S. solfataricus}.  The corresponding total and direct residual $R^2$ values are concrete rather than symbolic.  For yeast, $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$, giving the strongest observed mediated fraction.  For \textit{E. coli}, $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, and the indirect difference is $0.06599656708487024$.  For human, $c=0.04454565118019448$, $c'=0.03242643251185274$, and the indirect difference is $0.012119218668341744$.  The smallest mediated fraction is in \textit{S. solfataricus}, where $c=0.06397514251295629$, $c'=0.06279117466131778$, and the indirect difference is only $0.0011839678516385038$.

\paragraph{Translation readout status.}
The translation coordinate in this powered table is a modeled per-protein tAI surface, not a measured ribosome-footprint surface.  The earlier modeled-tAI survival check computed the same nine residual coordinates on three organisms and found $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but it explicitly remained a modeled-tAI contact and not ribo-seq validation.  In the present powered table the larger organism panel supports rank-sufficient statistical decomposition through modeled tAI, while the biological interpretation remains bounded to that readout.  The tRNA-copy totals used in the powered table range from $47$ in \textit{S. solfataricus} to $8874$ in zebrafish; geometric-mean fallback for zero raw codon weights was used in three organisms, with counts $3$ in \textit{B. subtilis}, $1$ in \textit{E. coli}, $1$ in \textit{M. smegmatis}, and $1$ in \textit{S. solfataricus}, and zero such fallbacks in the other eight organisms.

\paragraph{Control interpretation.}
The result licenses a narrow statement: in this finite panel, a nonzero part of the association between the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block and matched protein abundance is attenuated after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI.  This is strongest in yeast, fly, \textit{E. coli}, and \textit{B. subtilis}; it is weak in archaeal and mycobacterial panels.  The run is therefore a powered cross-layer mediation audit for $Q\to T\to P$ with $T$ defined as modeled tAI and $P$ defined as matched PAXdb abundance.  It is not a causal experiment, and it does not show that synonymous residuals directly alter mature protein amount through a measured translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Boundary against promotion.}
No claim about measured translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, perturbation response, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this table alone.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not treated as protein realization unless a same-row $Q_i\to T_j$ step is present and survives the declared controls.  Here the $T_j$ step is modeled tAI; ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, and pathway context remain outside the finite readout.  A separate layer-matched contact would be required before moving from this statistical mediation surface to any mechanistic, structural, functional, or universal biological assertion.

\paragraph{Relation to the yeast structure audit.}
A separate yeast five-way join decomposed $B^*_{Q6}$ association with protein abundance by conditioning on 5-prime CDS MFE, measured translation efficiency, and mRNA stability.  That audit used $4759$ joined yeast genes and found $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  Its structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and its incremental structure fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$.  Those numbers are consistent with the present chapter's restraint: protein-abundance mediation can be quantified, but structure and mechanism require their own readouts rather than being inferred from codon residuals or abundance attenuation.
