\subsection{B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} abundance residual after an mRNA half-life mediator}
\label{subsec:bioreality-bstar-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record tests whether the yeast protein-abundance association carried by the nine B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} synonymous residual coordinates is absorbed when a measured mRNA-stability readout is added as a one-dimensional mediator.  The coordinate block is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The joined table is a three-way Saccharomyces cerevisiae contact among real CDS codon counts, PAXdb protein abundance, and Neymotin mRNA half-life rows.  The usable intersection contains $5085$ rows.  The CDS-to-half-life hit rate reported by the join is $0.9156038419146592$, with $5815$ CDS rows already joined to abundance, $5379$ valid half-life records by Syst identifier, and $730$ CDS records skipped for lack of a half-life match.  The half-life payload is identified by SHA256 digest \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}.

\paragraph{Controls and residual space.}
The regression is not a raw codon-to-abundance comparison.  Before the B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} block is evaluated, the design removes an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and the M-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} vectors.  This gives $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and $5062$ residual degrees of freedom after the control projection.  The mediator $M$ is $\log_{10}$ mRNA half-life in minutes from the Neymotin yeast 4tU metabolic-labeling table.  The protein readout $P$ is $\log_{10}$ abundance in ppm from the matched PAXdb abundance contact.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
Let $X$ denote the nine-dimensional residual B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} design after the same control projection, let $M$ be the residualized half-life column, and let $P$ be residualized protein abundance.  The observed direct abundance surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.24851799532219632,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by}\ M} &= 0.027596042074056304.
\end{aligned}
$$
The absorbed share is therefore not the whole B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} abundance association.  It is a finite variance partition in which conditioning on mRNA half-life removes about ten percent of the B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6}-associated abundance component:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{mediation\ fraction} &= 0.09994436477872035,\\
\mathrm{permutation\ null\ mean} &= 0.00019674487584783138,\\
\mathrm{permutation\ null}_{95} &= 0.0014571003414947088,\\
 p_{\mathrm{right}} &= 0.000999000999000999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite-row interpretation is that residualized yeast mRNA half-life explains a small but non-null part of the B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} protein-abundance residual, while most of the abundance association remains after the half-life column is included.

\paragraph{Rank and energy audit.}
The rank diagnostics rule out a vacuous saturated fit for this row.  The residualized B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} block has rank $9$, the mediator block has rank $1$, and the joint $X,M$ block has rank $10$.  The abundance residual energy is $2041.4436456219576$, and the half-life residual energy is $229.94736158731112$.  In the same residual space, $X$ explains $563.6712471096034$ energy on $P$, the direct $X$ improvement after adding $M$ is $507.335482373205$, and the mediator improvement after adding $X$ is $10.2520427731871$.  The cross-readout audit also records $X$ explaining $11.909960434207074$ energy on $M$, matching the separately computed B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6}-to-half-life survival row where $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$.

\paragraph{Coordinate context.}
The matching B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} mRNA-stability row on this same yeast join reports $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ for residualized $\log_{10}$ half-life, with dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$.  On the same joined rows, the protein-abundance surface has $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ and dominant coordinate $f_3$-stress; the cosine between the $S^{QM}$ and same-join $S^{QP}$ coordinate profiles is $0.8040935468384264$.  Thus the mediator is aligned with the abundance residual profile, but it is not identical to it.  The evidence is strongest as a controlled statistical decomposition: B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} has a measurable yeast half-life component, and that component accounts for a finite minority of the protein-abundance association.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter does not infer a biochemical pathway from codon choice to mRNA half-life to protein output.  The row is observational, single-organism, and based on a steady-state 4tU half-life estimate; reverse direction, shared upstream regulation, growth-condition effects, transcript handling, and other confounding explanations are not eliminated.  The result also does not establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Any such claim would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  The supported statement is narrower: after amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and M-density controls, the measured yeast half-life column absorbs a statistically non-null but incomplete share of the nine-coordinate B exorpdfstring{$^*_{Q6}$}{*Q6} residual association with measured yeast protein abundance.
