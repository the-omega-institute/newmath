\subsection{B*\_Q6 abundance residual mediated by mRNA half-life}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-abundance-residual-mrna-half-life}

\paragraph{Claim.}
This record tests whether the yeast protein-abundance association carried by the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates is partly accounted for by an independently measured mRNA-stability readout.  The row set is a three-way join between real yeast CDS codon counts, PAXdb protein abundance, and the Neymotin et al. 2014 $4\mathrm{tU}$ mRNA half-life table.  The joined panel contains $n=5085$ rows.  The CDS side reports $5815$ joined coding sequences before the half-life filter, the half-life table contributes $5379$ valid Syst-keyed records, and the CDS-to-half-life hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$.  The join key strips the $4932.$ prefix from the CDS protein identifier and matches the resulting key to the Neymotin Syst field.  The finite audit reports $730$ CDS rows without a half-life match and no skipped rows from empty sense-codon counts, invalid length, missing protein id, nonpositive abundance, unparsable yeast ORF, duplicate Syst keys, missing Syst keys, or nonpositive half-life.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control design.}
Let $X$ be the $5085 \times 9$ matrix of $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates with columns
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The control matrix $Z$ has $24$ columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from the same CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  The reported rank of $Z$ is $23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after this control projection are $5062$.  The protein readout is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb yeast abundance, and the mediator is $M=\log_{10}(t_{1/2}\;\mathrm{minutes})$ from the Neymotin half-life table.

\paragraph{Finite mediation statistic.}
After residualizing against $Z$, the nine-coordinate model explains a nonzero part of protein-abundance residual variance:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.24851799532219632,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by}\ M} &= 0.027596042074056304.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite mediation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}}{R^2_{QP}} &= 0.09994436477872035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same computation reports $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(M)=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(X,M)=10$, and $\operatorname{rank}(X\ \mathrm{for}\ M)=9$, with the degeneracy threshold fixed at $0.999999$.  The residual energy of $P$ after $Z$ is $2041.4436456219576$, while the residual energy of $M$ after $Z$ is $229.94736158731112$.  The $X$ block explains $563.6712471096034$ units of residual protein-abundance energy; conditioning on $M$ leaves a direct $X$ improvement of $507.335482373205$.  Conversely, $X$ explains $11.909960434207074$ units of residual half-life energy, and $M$ supplies $66.5878075095855$ units of explained energy on $P$.

\paragraph{Null calibration.}
The mediation fraction is compared with a deterministic residual-mediator permutation null using the seed string $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_mrna\_stability\_mediation\_powered|residual\_mediator\_permutation|perm\_index|index|n}$.  The null mean is $0.00019674487584783138$, the $95$th percentile is $0.0014571003414947088$, and the right-tail permutation value is $p=0.000999000999000999$.  On this finite row set, the observed fraction $0.09994436477872035$ is therefore well above the recorded permutation boundary.  This supports a descriptive statement: in this yeast join, residualized mRNA half-life accounts for about ten percent of the $B^*_{Q6}$ association with residualized protein abundance, while most of the association remains after the half-life coordinate is conditioned out.

\paragraph{Mediator support.}
The companion half-life-only statistic on the same $5085$ rows reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.05179429044975085,\\
R^2_{QP,\mathrm{same\ join}} &= 0.2761140373962526,\\
\cos(S^{QM},S^{QP}) &= 0.8040935468384264.
\end{aligned}
$$
Its dominant half-life coordinate is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, whereas the protein-abundance statistic on the same join is dominated by $f_3$-stress.  The half-life projection explains $11.909960434207074$ units out of $229.94736158731112$ residual half-life energy with rank $9$, and its permutation value is again $p=0.000999000999000999$.  These numbers show that the mediator is not merely a label attached to the abundance calculation: it has its own finite $B^*_{Q6}$ residual association under the same CDS-derived controls, and its direction is positively aligned with the protein-abundance projection.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The result is a controlled variance decomposition on one organism, one protein-abundance source, and one steady-state mRNA half-life source.  It is not a causal path from codon choice to mRNA stability to protein abundance.  It does not establish translation realization, ribosome-mediated mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  The half-life readout is a steady-state $4\mathrm{tU}$ estimate, and reverse or shared-confound explanations remain possible.  Any claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality would require a separate layer-matched contact and a separately recorded statistic.  The bounded conclusion is narrower: under amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density controls, the yeast finite row set contains a statistically calibrated mRNA-stability component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual association, with a measured absorbed fraction of $0.09994436477872035$ and a remaining direct abundance association of $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.24851799532219632$.
