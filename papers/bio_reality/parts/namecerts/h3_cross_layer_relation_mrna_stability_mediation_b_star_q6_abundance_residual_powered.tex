\subsection{B-star-Q6 abundance residual after mRNA half-life conditioning}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-abundance-residual}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record.} This BioReality record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate block retains a protein-abundance association after conditioning on a measured yeast mRNA half-life readout. The row is classified at the cross-layer-relation level, but its statistical content is deliberately narrower than a causal biological pathway. The carrier is a three-way joined table of real yeast coding-sequence codon counts, measured protein abundance, and Neymotin-style mRNA half-life data. The measured protein readout is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb-matched yeast proteomics, and the mediator readout is $M=\log_{10}(t_{1/2}\ \mathrm{minutes})$ from a 4tU metabolic-labeling mRNA half-life table. The coordinate block is the fixed list
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Finite table.} The joined table contains $5085$ usable rows. The CDS side reports $5815$ joined coding records before the half-life intersection, and the half-life side reports $5379$ valid records keyed by the Neymotin systematic identifier. The reported CDS-to-half-life hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, with $730$ CDS records skipped for no half-life match and no skipped rows from empty codon counts, invalid length, missing protein identifier, nonpositive abundance, or unparseable yeast ORF. The payload checksum for the half-life table is \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}. The join key is the CDS protein identifier after stripping the \texttt{4932.} prefix and matching it to the Neymotin \texttt{Syst} field.

\paragraph{Controls.} The residualization basis $Z$ has $24$ control columns and rank $23$. It contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. After the $Z$ projection, the residual degrees of freedom are $5062$. The test is therefore not a raw codon-usage correlation: both the abundance readout and the mRNA half-life readout are evaluated after the amino-acid, length, GC3, and simpler-codon-density terms have been removed.

\paragraph{Statistic.} Let $X$ denote the residualized $9$-column $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix, let $M$ denote residualized log half-life, and let $P$ denote residualized log protein abundance. The finite row reports the following decomposition:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.24851799532219632,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by}\ M} &= 0.027596042074056304,\\
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}}{R^2_{QP}} &= 0.09994436477872035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The interpretation is a variance-partition readout: about $9.994436477872035\%$ of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance association is absorbed when the residualized mRNA half-life coordinate is added to the conditioning set. The calculation is a joint nine-dimensional scalar decomposition, with a per-coordinate before-and-after audit recorded separately.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.} The $X$ block has rank $9$, the mediator block has rank $1$, and the combined $X,M$ block has rank $10$. The residual energy of the mediator is $229.94736158731112$, and the residual energy of the protein readout is $2041.4436456219576$. The $X$ block explains $563.6712471096034$ units of abundance energy, while $M$ explains $66.5878075095855$ units on $P$. The direct $X$ improvement after conditioning on $M$ is $507.335482373205$, and the incremental improvement of $M$ after conditioning on $X$ is $10.2520427731871$. The $X$ block also explains $11.909960434207074$ units of the mediator energy. The degeneracy cutoff used by the computation is $0.999999$, and no reported rank reaches that saturated surface.

\paragraph{Permutation check.} The mediation fraction is compared against a deterministic residual-mediator permutation null. The recorded seed is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_mrna\_stability\_mediation\_powered|residual\_mediator\_permutation|perm\_index|index|n}. The null mean is $0.00019674487584783138$, the null $95$th percentile is $0.0014571003414947088$, and the right-tail permutation probability is $0.000999000999000999$. Thus the observed fraction $0.09994436477872035$ is far outside the recorded null range for this finite design.

\paragraph{Relation to the half-life survival row.} The companion mRNA-stability row on the same yeast join reports $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals against residualized log half-life, with $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ on the same join. Its dominant mRNA-half-life coordinate is $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, while the abundance row on the same join is dominated by $f3\_stress$. The cosine between the $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ coordinate profiles is $0.8040935468384264$, and the permutation probability for the $R^2_{QM}$ row is $0.000999000999000999$. This supports the narrower conclusion that the mRNA half-life readout is not arbitrary with respect to the same residual coordinate block, while also showing that the abundance and half-life profiles are not identical coordinate signatures.

\paragraph{Coordinate-source audit.} A separate per-coordinate abundance decomposition on the unified yeast context join reports $1800$ base rows, $1258$ joined rows after its filtering rule, five folds, $120$ permutations, and a $Z$-basis rank of $24$. In that audit, the coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$ are listed as real abundance sources under its held-out and permutation criterion; $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ remains in the nine-row table but is not listed in that real-source subset. This audit is not a replacement for the mediation statistic above, but it helps localize the abundance signal as a coordinate-resolved source rather than only a single opaque nine-dimensional fit.

\paragraph{Boundary.} The ready claim is only this: in the finite yeast three-way join, conditioning on residualized mRNA half-life reduces but does not erase the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual association with measured protein abundance, and the absorbed fraction is large relative to the recorded permutation null. It does not show that codon choice causes mRNA stability, that mRNA stability causes protein abundance, or that the route is a biochemical mechanism. It also does not establish ribosome-mediated translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universality. Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. Here, the measured half-life row is a statistical mediator candidate and the protein-abundance row is a downstream abundance readout, not proof of a causal path or a general biological law.
