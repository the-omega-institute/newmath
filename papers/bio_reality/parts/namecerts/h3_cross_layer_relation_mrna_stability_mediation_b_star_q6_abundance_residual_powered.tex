\subsection{B*\_Q6 abundance residual through mRNA half-life}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Finite panel and measured rows.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate family retains a protein-abundance association after a measured yeast mRNA-stability variable is conditioned out. The row universe is a three-way join between real yeast CDS codon counts, measured PAXdb protein abundance, and the Neymotin yeast 4tU metabolic-labeling half-life table. The joined panel contains $n=5085$ rows. The CDS-to-half-life hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, with $5815$ CDS records available before the half-life join, $5379$ valid half-life records by Syst key, and $730$ CDS records skipped for no half-life match. The join key strips the $4932.$ prefix from the CDS protein identifier and matches the resulting yeast ORF key to the Neymotin Syst field. The half-life payload is recorded with SHA256 hash \texttt{cee1ee9a816f0ec1e351bc2d63d4838ae603ea20566fc3ff529514a6ba6cf7d2}.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control design.}
The source design uses the nine residual coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$. It contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors. After these controls, the residual degrees of freedom are $5062$. The mediator $M$ is $\log_{10}$ mRNA half-life in minutes from the Neymotin table, and the protein readout $P$ is $\log_{10}$ measured abundance in ppm from PAXdb.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
Let $X$ denote the residualized nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design, let $M$ denote the residualized mRNA half-life readout, and let $P$ denote the residualized protein-abundance readout. The uncontrolled-by-mediator protein association is
$$\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526.
\end{aligned}$$
After conditioning on $M$, the direct coordinate-to-protein association is
$$\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP\mid M} &= 0.24851799532219632.
\end{aligned}$$
Thus the absolute protein-abundance variance component absorbed by the half-life coordinate is
$$\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by\ }M} &= 0.027596042074056304.
\end{aligned}$$
The corresponding mediation fraction is
$$\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^{2}_{QP}-R^{2}_{QP\mid M}}{R^{2}_{QP}} &= 0.09994436477872035.
\end{aligned}$$
The permutation audit uses the deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_mrna\_stability\_mediation\_powered|residual\_mediator\_permutation|perm\_index|index|n}. Its right-tail permutation value is $0.000999000999000999$, with null mean $0.00019674487584783138$ and null $95$th percentile $0.0014571003414947088$. The observed mediation fraction is therefore far above the reported finite-row null surface for the residual mediator shuffle.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The residualized coordinate design has rank $9$, the residualized mediator has rank $1$, and the joint coordinate-plus-mediator design has rank $10$. The rank used when $X$ predicts $M$ is also $9$. The residual energy of the mediator is $229.94736158731112$, and the residual energy of the protein-abundance readout is $2041.4436456219576$. The coordinate energy explained on protein abundance is $563.6712471096034$, while the coordinate energy explained on the mediator is $11.909960434207074$. The mediator energy explained on protein abundance is $66.5878075095855$. Given the coordinate design, the mediator adds $10.2520427731871$ units of improvement energy on protein abundance; given the mediator, the coordinate design still adds $507.335482373205$ units. These diagnostics classify the result as a partial statistical mediation record: the mRNA half-life readout absorbs a nonzero minority of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association, while most of the coordinate-associated protein-abundance component remains after the mediator is included.

\paragraph{Relation to the mediator-only surface.}
On the same yeast half-life contact, the mediator-only $B^{*}_{Q6}$ readout gives $R^{2}_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$ with dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$. The same-row protein surface gives $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$ and dominant coordinate $f3\_stress$, with cosine $0.8040935468384264$ between the $S^{QM}$ and same-join $S^{QP}$ profiles. This matters for the present mediation row because the mediator is not an arbitrary covariate: it is itself a measured mRNA-stability surface with a positive but weaker $B^{*}_{Q6}$ projection. The mediation fraction near $0.10$ is therefore read as a finite variance-partition statement linking the codon-residual surface, the mRNA half-life readout, and measured protein abundance on the same yeast rows.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
This chapter does not turn the variance partition into a causal pathway. The panel is one organism, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and one half-life source, the Neymotin 2014 4tU metabolic-labeling table. The half-life value is a steady-state estimate rather than a direct elementary decay-rate mechanism. The result does not prove that codon choice causes mRNA stability, that mRNA stability causes the measured protein abundance component, or that reverse and shared confounded processes are absent. It also does not establish ribosome-mediated translation, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Any such higher-layer statement requires a separately matched contact and its own controlled statistic. The positive content here is narrower: on $5085$ finite yeast rows, after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls, residualized measured mRNA half-life absorbs $0.027596042074056304$ absolute $R^{2}$, or $0.09994436477872035$ of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance association, with permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.
