\subsection{B exorpdfstring{$^\ast_{Q6}$}{*Q6} Abundance Residual After an mRNA-Stability Mediator}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual panel retains a protein-abundance association in yeast after conditioning on a measured mRNA-stability readout. The rows are $5085$ joined \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} coding sequences for which three tables meet on the same identifier route: real CDS codon counts, measured PAXdb protein abundance, and the Neymotin et al. yeast $4$tU mRNA half-life table. The protein readout is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$, and the mediator is $M=\log_{10}(t_{1/2}\ \mathrm{minutes})$. The join key strips the $4932.$ prefix from the CDS protein identifier and matches the resulting token to the Neymotin Syst field. The finite join reports $5815$ CDS rows available before the mediator join, $5379$ valid half-life records, $5085$ final three-way rows, and a CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$; $730$ CDS records miss the half-life table, while the recorded skipped categories for empty codon counts, invalid length, missing protein identifier, nonpositive abundance, malformed yeast ORF, missing Syst, duplicate Syst, and nonpositive half-life are all zero.

\paragraph{Residual design.}
Let $X$ denote the $5085\times 9$ matrix of $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, with columns
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The control design $Z$ has $24$ columns before rank reduction: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^\ast_{Q6}$ vectors. The fitted control rank is $23$, leaving $5062$ residual degrees of freedom. All reported associations below are therefore read after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and the simpler residual-support baseline have already been removed.

\paragraph{Mediation statistic.}
The finite-row decomposition compares the protein-abundance association explained by $X$ before and after adding the mRNA-stability mediator. In the recorded run, the unmediated abundance surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Q$ denotes the nine residual coordinates and $P$ denotes protein abundance. After residualized mRNA half-life is included as a one-dimensional mediator, the remaining direct abundance association is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.24851799532219632,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by}\ M} &= 0.027596042074056304.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding mediation fraction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{M} &= 0.09994436477872035.
\end{aligned}
$$
A deterministic residual-mediator permutation check gives null mean $0.00019674487584783138$, null $95\%$ cutoff $0.0014571003414947088$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$. Thus the observed fraction is not a small numerical byproduct of the join geometry; it is far above the recorded finite-row null for this design.

\paragraph{Rank and energy diagnostics.}
The mediator and coordinate ranks are nondegenerate under the run's own threshold $0.999999$. The residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$, the mediator has rank $1$, and the combined coordinate-plus-mediator design has rank $10$. The abundance residual energy is $2041.4436456219576$, while the mRNA-stability residual energy is $229.94736158731112$. The coordinate panel explains $563.6712471096034$ units of abundance energy before mediator conditioning and $507.335482373205$ units after mediator conditioning. The mediator explains $66.5878075095855$ units on the abundance residual, and its incremental improvement after $X$ is $10.2520427731871$. Conversely, the coordinates explain $11.909960434207074$ units on the mRNA-stability residual. These numbers classify the record as a cross-layer statistical decomposition: the mediator removes a real but minority share of the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ abundance association, while most of the abundance association remains outside this one mRNA-stability coordinate.

\paragraph{Relation to the direct mRNA-stability surface.}
The companion yeast mRNA-stability surface on the same biological scale reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.05179429044975085,\\
R^2_{QP,\mathrm{same\ join}} &= 0.2761140373962526.
\end{aligned}
$$
Its dominant coordinate is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, whereas the same-join protein-abundance surface is dominated by $f_3$-stress, and the cosine between the $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ directions is $0.8040935468384264$. The mediator therefore does not merely duplicate the abundance direction; it has its own dominant coordinate while still sharing a positive finite-row direction with the protein-abundance projection. Cross-organism checks keep this relation bounded rather than universal: the E. coli half-life surface has $R^2_{QM}=0.02298556640056076$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.11632311854858134$, dominant coordinate $Arg_{AGR}$, and cosine $-0.7784923375569599$ against the abundance surface; the human relative half-life surface has $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, same-join $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$, dominant coordinate $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, and cosine $0.6351183883924848$. The yeast mediation result is consequently a concrete organism-scoped decomposition, not a sign-stable rule over all organisms.

\paragraph{Readout classification.}
This chapter's primary module is $\mathrm{cross\_layer\_relation}$: it links codon-residual coordinates, an mRNA-stability readout, and a measured abundance readout inside one finite table. It is not a translation-realization result by itself. A separate ranking computation over non-translation candidate readouts also places yeast mRNA at the top of the yeast list, with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, $A_HC_H=0.0011462622274400686$, $DL_H=1.0$, score $-0.008853737772559931$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$, null $p95=0.0004745820831535835$, and $n=5128$ joined rows. That ranking used the stated description-length penalty convention with $\lambda=0.01$ and reports mRNA as the yeast $H_o^\ast$ candidate, but this ranking is only supporting context for readout prioritization; the mediation statistic above is the direct evidence used in this section.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result supports a measured statistical decomposition: in this yeast table, residualized mRNA half-life absorbs about $9.9944\%$ of the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual association with measured protein abundance, with a right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$. It does not establish a causal codon-to-mRNA-stability-to-protein pathway. The half-life readout is a steady-state $4$tU estimate from one organism and one dataset, and the abundance readout is steady-state proteomics. Reverse direction, shared confounding, growth-condition dependence, and other codon-linked processes remain possible. No ribosome-mediated translation claim, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, mechanistic pathway claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this record unless an additional layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic are supplied.
