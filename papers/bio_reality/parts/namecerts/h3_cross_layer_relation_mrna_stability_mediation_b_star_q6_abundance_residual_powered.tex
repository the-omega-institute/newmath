\subsection{A residual mRNA-stability mediation readback for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance power}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-mrna-stability-mediation-abundance-residual}

\paragraph{Object of measurement.}
This record isolates the yeast cross-layer relation in which the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are first tested against measured protein abundance and then re-tested after conditioning on an mRNA-stability mediator. The biological row set is the three-way join among real yeast CDS codon counts, measured PAXdb protein abundance, and the Neymotin et al. 4tU mRNA half-life table. The joined sample contains $5085$ rows; the CDS-to-half-life join reports a hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$, with $5815$ CDS rows already joined to protein abundance, $5379$ valid half-life records by systematic gene identifier, and $730$ CDS records skipped for absence of a half-life match. The mediator is $M=\log_{10}(t_{1/2}\ \mathrm{minutes})$ and the abundance target is $P=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$.

\paragraph{Residual design.}
Let $X$ denote the $5085 \times 9$ matrix of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed. Its columns are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$. The control block $Z$ has $24$ columns before rank reduction: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions from real CDS counts, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline formed from the union support of the nine projected $q$-vectors. Its numerical rank on this join is $23$, leaving $5062$ residual degrees of freedom. The relevant ranks after residualization are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)&=9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde M)&=1,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde M)&=10.
\end{aligned}
$$
These ranks make the mediation calculation non-saturated under the stated degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Direct and conditioned abundance association.}
Before conditioning on mRNA half-life, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block explains a finite abundance component
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526.
\end{aligned}
$$
After adding residualized mRNA half-life as the mediator, the remaining direct association is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.24851799532219632,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{absorbed\ by}\ M} &= 0.027596042074056304.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, about one tenth of the original $Q\to P$ residual association is accounted for by the half-life coordinate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid M}}{R^2_{QP}} &= 0.09994436477872035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same calculation reports $563.6712471096034$ units of $P$-energy explained by $X$, $507.335482373205$ units of direct $X$ improvement after $M$, $66.5878075095855$ units of $M$ energy on $P$, $11.909960434207074$ units of $X$ energy on $M$, and $10.2520427731871$ units of incremental mediator improvement after $X$. The residual energies are $2041.4436456219576$ for abundance and $229.94736158731112$ for half-life.

\paragraph{Permutation scale.}
The mediation fraction is far above the deterministic residual-mediator permutation baseline used for this finite row set. With $1000$ effective right-tail resolution, the reported null mean is $0.00019674487584783138$, the null $95$th percentile is $0.0014571003414947088$, and the right-tail probability is $0.000999000999000999$. Thus the observed fraction $0.09994436477872035$ is not a small random fluctuation of the mediator labels under this design. The statement remains a variance decomposition: it says that residualized mRNA half-life absorbs a measurable part of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance component in this yeast dataset.

\paragraph{Relation to the standalone mRNA-stability surface.}
The same yeast join also gives a direct $Q\to M$ surface. On the identical $5085$ rows and the same control basis, $R^2_{QM}=0.05179429044975085$, while the abundance association on the same join is again $R^2_{QP}=0.2761140373962526$. The dominant mRNA-stability coordinate is $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, whereas the abundance-side dominant coordinate on that same join is $f_3\text{-stress}$. The cosine between the mRNA-stability and abundance surfaces is $0.8040935468384264$, with permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$. These numbers support a nontrivial alignment, but not identity, between the mRNA-half-life readout and the abundance readout.

\paragraph{Coordinate localization.}
Independent coordinate audits sharpen the interpretation. In the per-coordinate reducibility table, all nine coordinates are decomposed on the unified yeast abundance context, with $n=1258$ rows in the held-out table, five folds, $120$ target-label permutations, and $z$-basis rank $24$. Real abundance-source support is reported for $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$, while $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$ is not listed among the coordinates with real-source support in that audit. For the $f_3\text{-stress}$ family decomposition, the full $f_3$ projection has norm squared $52.76911713166682$ and accounts for fraction $0.12259712041843235$ of the target energy, with $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates, five folds, and $120$ permutations. Nineteen family subcoordinates pass the reported real-source gate, including $K_{AAA\to AAG}$, $L_{UUA\to UUG}$, $R_{AGA\to AGG}$, $S_{UCU\to UCG}$, and $T_{ACU\to ACG}$.

\paragraph{Neighboring readout classification.}
The broader candidate-readout ranking classifies this item as a cross-layer relation rather than as a translation-realization measurement. In the yeast ranking, the top candidate is mRNA with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, $A_H C_H=0.0011462622274400686$, description-length penalty $DL_H=1.0$, score $-0.008853737772559931$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0013014749238591248$, null $95$th percentile $0.0004745820831535835$, and $5128$ joined rows. The same ranking uses a regularization coefficient $\lambda=0.01$. Measured tRNA-tAI, turnover, complex membership, protein-localization columns, and measured translation efficiency appear as separate candidate readouts with their own scores; they are not substituted for the mRNA-stability mediator used here.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
For this finite yeast row set, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ retains a sizeable residual association with protein abundance after the amino-acid, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density controls, and residualized mRNA half-life absorbs a reproducible fraction of that association. The strongest numerical statement is the pair
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2761140373962526,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.24851799532219632,
\end{aligned}
$$
together with the mediation fraction $0.09994436477872035$ and the permutation right-tail probability $0.000999000999000999$. This is evidence for a statistical mRNA-stability mediation component in the yeast abundance residual. It is not, by itself, evidence for ribosome-mediated translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, a biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological law. Any one of those stronger readings would require its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.
