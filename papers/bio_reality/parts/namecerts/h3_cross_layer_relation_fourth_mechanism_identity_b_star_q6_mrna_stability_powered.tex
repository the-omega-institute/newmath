\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite spectrum.}
The exclusion is meaningful because the fourth axis is not an artifact of an otherwise empty spectrum. These rows make the mRNA-stability result sharper: the fourth axis is detected as a residual phenomenon, but the mRNA half-life candidate is rejected as its dominant identity in this assay.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The permitted biological reading is narrow. The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes a translation-facing candidate only where synonymous imbalance remains after amino-acid projection and where context tRNA or wobble weights can break third-position symmetry. A valid survival entry would have to be a controlled statistic such as $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a bounded translation readout, with organism, amino-acid composition, $\mathrm{GC3}$, CDS length, $M$-density, rank, simpler codon baselines, and mRNA controls already in the conditioning set. The present mRNA-stability row is not such a positive translation-readout survival row. Its role is to remove one proposed identity for the fourth residual direction, not to promote the coordinate into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, stability mechanism, phenotype, adaptation claim, causation claim, or universal biological law follows from this row alone. A downstream statement at any of those layers would need its own matched contact and its own finite statistic. In particular, mRNA abundance remains a control surface for translation-efficiency residualization unless a separate half-life or decay-factor panel is actually joined to the coordinate rows; modeled tAI or stAI weights remain bounded codon-adaptation readbacks unless measured ribosome or translation-efficiency rows are attached. The chapter therefore records a finite exclusion: the fourth residual axis is present in the powered coordinate spectrum, but mRNA stability is not its dominant identity under the fixed assay gates.
