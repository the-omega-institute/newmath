Thus the coordinate being tested is not a free biological label: it is a finite row of codon residual geometry whose possible readout is limited to controlled survival against declared biological measurements.

\paragraph{Assay result.}
The powered mRNA-stability identity assay was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_mrna\_stability\_powered}$ with run id $377\mathrm{abd}67\mathrm{b}8\mathrm{b}3$, five folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Its recorded conclusion is $\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}$. The stated reason is that the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt,halflife}}$ direction has low alignment with the abundance-derived $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ direction under the fixed gates. In the finite-row language, the tested half-life vector does not identify the fourth residual abundance axis in this assay.

$$
\begin{aligned}

The conclusion is a refusal of one proposed identity, not a removal of the fourth-axis phenomenon itself. The separate three-axis sufficiency assay reports $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$ on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, again with five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns. The axis-strength spectrum on the same powered scale reports $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leading every computed organism in the dominant-count summary $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$; the domain counts are imbalanced, with $1$ archaeon, $2$ bacteria, and $8$ eukaryotes, so the domain means are descriptive rather than comparative law. The largest reported GC3 rank effect in that spectrum is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho = 0.136$.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding finite discriminants.}
The fourth-axis identity packet is constrained by nearby mechanism probes. Codon-pair structure is rejected as the identity in the corresponding powered test, whose conclusion is $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$ using five folds, $50$ shuffle-null replicates, a $500$ protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous design columns. Translation accuracy is also rejected as the fourth-axis identity in the powered test over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $200$ permutations and $41$ synonymous design columns. Elongation dwell receives only a $\mathrm{partial}$ call in the yeast assay. These rows make the mRNA-stability refusal more specific: the result does not say that no fourth mechanism exists; it says that the particular RNA half-life direction tested here is not the dominant fourth-axis explanation under the stated finite gates.

The residual background is also not rank-free. The two-axis closure assay reports $\mathrm{third\_axis\_present}$ because $d_{\perp}$ has systematically positive held-out $R^2$ and same-signed directions across organisms. The third-axis identity assay provisionally aligns $d_{\perp}$ with within-family usage frequency, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p = 0.000488$. The third-axis mechanism assay reports $\mathrm{selection\text{-}not\text{-}GC}$ and $\mathrm{not\text{-}wobble\text{-}clustered}$, while leaving the CSC or positional identity as needing another contact. The universal residual-axis assay identifies the non-tRNA residual optimality most strongly with $f_3$ translation selection across $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms. These facts keep the mRNA-stability row in a narrow role: it is a tested candidate for naming the fourth axis, and it fails that naming task.

\paragraph{Local readout rule.}
The only admissible positive readback for the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a non-vacuous controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ against a declared translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, M-density, and mRNA controls present. A modeled adaptation weight may bound a translation-layer comparison, but it is not by itself an observed ribosome-occupancy row. A three-organism all-$1.0$ controlled matrix is a rank-degenerate boundary rather than support. Therefore the present mRNA-stability result cannot be promoted into translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Each of those layers would require its own matched measurement surface and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is that mRNA stability is excluded as the dominant fourth-axis identity in this powered assay. The negative identity call is compatible with the independent presence of a fourth residual mechanism, with $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ leading the computed axis-strength spectrum and with other candidate identities either rejected or only partially supported. The chapter therefore records a bounded exclusion: yeast RNA half-life alignment is too weak to name the fourth $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual axis here, while the broader fourth-axis search remains open only through separately grounded translation or downstream readout rows.
