\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ fourth-axis mRNA-stability refusal row}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-fourth-axis-mrna-stability-refusal}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The record is a finite-row refusal for the proposal that the observed fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axis is principally an mRNA-stability axis.  The carrier is the CDS-by-organism synonymous residual surface after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to rows for which translation-layer or abundance-layer readouts can be compared under the declared controls.  The finite codon boundary used by the row is explicit.  The union set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$ has twenty codons, while the $f_3$-saturation of $R$ removes the two threonine codons $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ and leaves eighteen codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) &= \{\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\qquad \mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus this row is not a free verbal association between codon usage and RNA decay.  It is a finite residual-coordinate test whose admissible signal must survive amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched transcript controls before any biological interpretation is allowed.

\paragraph{Powered assay row.}
The mRNA-stability identity assay has status passed with experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_mrna\_stability\_powered}.  It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism where organism-level panels were used, and evaluated a synonymous design with $41$ columns.  The recorded conclusion is \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}.  More precisely, the yeast half-life direction $d_{\mathrm{opt,halflife}}$ showed low alignment with the abundance-associated residual direction $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates, so the assay excludes mRNA stability as the dominant identity of the fourth axis in this finite readout.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding spectrum.}
The refusal is not a denial that a fourth mechanism exists.  A separate powered sufficiency row reports \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} on $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The axis-strength spectrum over the same computed-organism scale is led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, followed by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominant-count table records $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, with domain means only descriptive because the domain counts are imbalanced, $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$.  The largest GC3 rank effect recorded in that spectrum is $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  These numbers make the local verdict sharper: the fourth direction is present and recurrent in the computed panel, but the particular mRNA half-life candidate does not identify it in the yeast assay row.

\paragraph{Separated translation gate.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate only becomes a translation-layer object where a context-specific tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry breaks the third-position $f_3$ symmetry.  A modelled tAI or stAI row can supply a bounded codon-adaptation readout, and ribosome profiling or translation efficiency can supply a direct translation readout when same-CDS rows exist.  Neither source is present here as a positive same-row survival statistic for the mRNA-stability identity claim.  The mandatory readback is a controlled entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  Without a non-vacuous positive entry of that form, no downstream RNA-stability, protein-abundance, localization, structural, functional, phenotypic, mechanistic, or universal conclusion is licensed.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The local mathematical content is therefore a bounded negative identification:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{AxisPresent}(B^*_{Q6},d_{\mathrm{resid4}}) &\quad \text{is retained on the powered spectrum row},\\
\mathrm{Identity}(d_{\mathrm{resid4}},\mathrm{mRNA\ stability}) &\quad \text{is rejected in the fixed yeast half-life assay},\\
\mathrm{PromoteToTranslationOrHigherLayer} &\quad \text{is blocked without a separate positive contact row}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The third-axis identity row gives a useful contrast: $d_{\perp}$ is provisionally aligned to usage frequency within family with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The mRNA-stability row has no comparable positive identity statistic; its recorded finite conclusion is exclusion.  Consequently this chapter records a fourth-axis mechanism search with one candidate removed, not a claim that RNA half-life, translation efficiency, ribosome dwell, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or organism-level phenotype has been derived from the $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry.
