\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The bounded readback for the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a controlled survival entry such as $S_{f3\mbox{-}stress,T_j}$ for tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency. The controlling covariates include amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA abundance, rank sufficiency, and simpler-codon-baseline ablation. Even with a positive translation row, no conclusion about folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, localization, stability, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality follows unless a separate layer-matched contact measures that target.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite verdict is therefore a refusal rather than a discovery of an RNA-stability mechanism.  On the stated powered rows, the fourth residual axis is present, but the yeast half-life direction $d_{\mathrm{opt,halflife}}$ does not align strongly enough with $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ to identify mRNA stability as the dominant fourth-axis mechanism.  The appropriate mathematical status is a scoped negative identity claim:
$$
\begin{aligned}
B^{*}_{Q6}\text{ has a fourth residual direction in the tested design}
\quad
Rightarrow\quad
\mathrm{mRNA\ stability}\text{ is not its dominant assayed identity}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This implication is finite-row and assay-bound.  It should be read as a controlled exclusion inside the available design matrix, not as a biological law about all transcript decay, all organisms, or all translation-coupled RNA processes.
