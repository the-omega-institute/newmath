The negative mRNA-stability call is not a denial that a fourth residual direction exists. Thus the stability assay asks a narrower question: not whether $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ exists, but whether one concrete stability readout supplies its dominant biological identity.  The finite answer recorded here is no.

\paragraph{Three-axis background.}
The mRNA-stability refusal is also local to the fourth-axis identity problem.  The three-axis sufficiency assay reports $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, $5$ folds, a $500$-protein minimum per organism, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and the conclusion $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$.  In symbols, the local situation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{three-axis test} &\Rightarrow \text{additional mechanism present},\\
\text{mRNA-stability identity test} &\Rightarrow d_{\mathrm{opt,halflife}}\not\approx d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\ \text{under the assay gates}.

The two facts are compatible.  The first keeps the fourth-direction search open; the second removes mRNA stability as the dominant identity for that direction in the recorded yeast half-life comparison.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The biological interpretation is deliberately bounded.  The present certificate permits the following statement only: within the finite checked rows, the mRNA half-life direction fails to identify the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ residual mechanism.  It does not establish translation survival, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, a mechanism beyond the assayed contrast, or a universal biological law.  A positive translation claim would require a separate matched readout row such as $S_{f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0$ after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  A downstream protein, localization, stability, structure, function, or phenotype claim would require its own layer-matched contact after that translation row is non-vacuous.  In the absence of such rows, mRNA abundance and mRNA half-life remain controls or candidate readouts, not proofs of translation realization.

\paragraph{Certificate verdict.}
The finite verdict is therefore a scoped exclusion.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual program has a verified fourth-axis pressure, but the tested mRNA-stability route does not supply the fourth-axis identity.  The chapter records this as a negative identity result with concrete row counts and carrier lists, not as a general biological impossibility claim.
