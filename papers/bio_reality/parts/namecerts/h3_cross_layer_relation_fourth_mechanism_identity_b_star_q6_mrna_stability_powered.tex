\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate surface after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate and to translation-adjacent readout rows.  The finite saturation check fixes the local codon boundary before any biological interpretation is attempted.  In the recorded run $76d3dc408a57$, the finite set $M=\operatorname{Med}(R)\cup\{ACA\}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&AAA,AAG,ACA,ACG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\},
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas the $f_3$-saturation of $R$ removes the two $AC$ boundary codons:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&AAA,AAG,AGA,AGG,AUA,AUG,CUA,CUC,CUG,CUU,\\
&UAA,UAG,UCA,UCG,UGA,UGG,UUA,UUG\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported $f_3$ boundary is empty.  Thus the finite geometry itself supplies a sharply delimited residual coordinate, but not yet a biological outcome.  The only permitted readback is a controlled survival entry for a translation-adjacent row, with amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls kept in the conditioning set.

\paragraph{Powered mRNA-stability test.}
The powered mRNA-stability assay is the finite-row test with experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_mrna\_stability\_powered} and run id \texttt{377abd67b8b3}.  It used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and evaluated $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its recorded conclusion is \texttt{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}.  More specifically, the yeast half-life direction $d_{\mathrm{opt,halflife}}$ has low alignment with the abundance residual direction $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates, so this assay excludes mRNA stability as the dominant identity of the fourth residual axis.  The finite conclusion can be written as the negative classification
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{class}(d_{\mathrm{resid4}}) \neq \operatorname{mRNA\ stability}
\end{aligned}
$$
for this assay design, not as a claim that mRNA stability is irrelevant everywhere.

\paragraph{Relation to the residual-axis packet.}
The surrounding powered records make the exclusion informative.  The three-axis sufficiency test, run \texttt{2436cae9d455}, reports \texttt{fourth\_mechanism\_present} with $5$ folds, $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  The axis-strength spectrum, run \texttt{0146012d8909}, also used $5$ folds and the same $500$-protein and $41$-column gates; its recorded summary says that captured spectra are led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average and that $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ is dominant in $11$ organisms, while the largest $GC3$ rank effect is the usage axis with $\rho=0.136$.  The residual optimality record, run \texttt{2a526c9fb7f4}, identifies the dominant non-tRNA residual optimality direction as \texttt{f3\_translation\_selection}, again on $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested, $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  These finite rows say that a fourth residual direction is present and strong in the assayed panel, while the mRNA-stability row says that the fourth direction should not be named by yeast mRNA half-life alignment.

\paragraph{Neighboring exclusions and partial alternatives.}
The same fourth-axis family contains additional bounded classifications.  The codon-pair assay, run \texttt{b519b5ebd34d}, reports \texttt{not\_codon\_pair} with $5$ folds, $50$ shuffle-null samples, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous columns.  The translation-accuracy assay, run \texttt{0dc44df59ba7}, reports \texttt{not\_accuracy} over $11$ computed organisms from $16$ requested, with $200$ permutations, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous columns.  The elongation-dwell assay, run \texttt{e4edcde459b7}, reports \texttt{partial} for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} with $5$ folds.  Taken together, the finite rows support a local discriminant: mRNA stability, codon-pair structure, and translation accuracy are not the dominant fourth-axis identity under their respective gates, whereas elongation dwell remains only partially aligned in the yeast assay.  This is a classification of assayed residual directions, not a universal causal law.

\paragraph{Readout rule and refusal boundary.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes biologically interpretable only where the paired synonymous imbalance survives after amino-acid projection and where context tRNA or wobble weights break third-position $f_3$ symmetry.  A positive translation statement would require a non-vacuous controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0$ for a specified translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ matrix on a small organism panel is treated as a null boundary rather than survival.  Therefore this chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, protein stability, phenotype, a biochemical mechanism, or universality from the residual coordinate or from the mRNA-stability exclusion.  Each of those layers would require its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the translation row has first survived.
