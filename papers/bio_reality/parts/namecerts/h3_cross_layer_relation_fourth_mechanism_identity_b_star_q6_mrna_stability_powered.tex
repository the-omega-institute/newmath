\subsection{The mRNA-stability refusal for the fourth $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ mechanism}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-fourth-axis-mrna-stability-refusal}

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
This chapter records a negative mechanism identification for the fourth residual direction in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coding-sequence surface.  The object under test is not an unrestricted codon-usage association.  It is a finite CDS-by-organism residual coordinate surface restricted to the $f_3$-stress coordinate after amino-acid projection, with the translation-facing rows kept separate from abundance, stability, structure, localization, function, and phenotype rows.  The finite saturation row fixes the relevant codon support as
$$
\begin{aligned}
M = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R) = \{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},\\
\partial_{f_3}R &= \varnothing .
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row used here has twenty codons in $M$, eighteen codons in the saturated $f_3$ image, and an empty $f_3$ boundary.  The missing pair is exactly $\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}$ relative to $M$, so the downstream test is not a search over an arbitrary codon universe; it is a controlled readback from this saturated residual surface.

\paragraph{Powered assay.}
The powered fourth-axis assay was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_mrna\_stability\_powered}$ with run id $377\mathrm{abd}67\mathrm{b}8\mathrm{b}3$.  Its declared finite gates were five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The recorded status was passed.  The mechanism call was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{conclusion} &= \mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded reason is that the yeast half-life direction $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ has low alignment with the abundance-associated fourth residual direction $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  In this assay, that low alignment excludes mRNA stability as the dominant identity of the fourth axis.  The conclusion is therefore a refusal of one proposed mechanism, not a positive assignment of the fourth axis to another layer.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding residual spectrum.}
The refusal is meaningful because the surrounding finite spectrum is not empty.  In the axis-strength computation, $11$ organisms were computed out of $16$ requested organisms, again with five folds, a $500$-protein minimum, and $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The captured spectra were led by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ on average, and every computed organism had $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ as the dominant direction in the per-organism count summary.  The same finite record reports imbalanced domain counts, $n_{\mathrm{archaea}}=1$, $n_{\mathrm{bacteria}}=2$, and $n_{\mathrm{eukaryote}}=8$, and only a small largest GC3 rank effect, $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  Separately, the three-axis sufficiency assay over $11$ computed organisms concluded $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$, so the mRNA-stability result is not saying that the fourth direction is absent.  It says that this particular finite half-life alignment did not identify the fourth direction as mRNA stability.

\paragraph{Competing mechanism rows.}
The same finite packet also records nearby refusals and partial contacts.  A codon-pair assay with five folds, $500$ minimum proteins, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $50$ shuffle-null draws concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$.  A translation-accuracy assay over $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with five folds, $41$ synonymous-design columns, and $200$ permutations, concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$.  An elongation-dwell assay in $\mathit{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$ concluded $\mathrm{partial}$.  These rows narrow the interpretation: the current finite data reject mRNA stability as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the half-life assay, reject codon-pair and accuracy identifications under their respective gates, and leave elongation dwell as only partial rather than closed.

\paragraph{Translation gate and non-promotion rule.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes biologically interpretable only where a translation readout survives after the declared controls.  The admissible bounded statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j} &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA control where applicable, rank controls, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The present mRNA-stability assay does not itself supply such a positive translation-row statistic.  Modelled tAI or stAI weights may bound a codon-adaptation readout, and ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows may test translation when joined to the same CDS rows, but those contacts are distinct from mRNA half-life and protein abundance.  Therefore no translation realization, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular localization, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this negative mRNA-stability result.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite readback is a mechanism exclusion.  The fourth residual direction is present in the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ spectrum, but yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ does not align strongly enough with abundance $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates to identify mRNA stability as the dominant fourth-axis mechanism.  The strongest local statement is therefore: within this assay, mRNA stability is not the fourth-axis identity.  A positive cross-layer claim would require a same-organism or same-CDS matrix joining codon counts, wobble or tRNA weights, a direct translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and matched mRNA controls, followed by a nondegenerate controlled survival statistic.  Until that row exists, the chapter stops at the finite exclusion stated above.
