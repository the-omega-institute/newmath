\paragraph{Question tested.}
The local question is whether the fourth residual axis detected in the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ analysis is primarily an mRNA-stability axis.  The test is deliberately narrower than a translation, protein-output, or phenotype claim.  It asks whether a yeast mRNA half-life direction, denoted here by $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$, aligns strongly enough with the abundance-associated fourth residual direction $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ to identify mRNA stability as the dominant fourth-axis mechanism under the fixed gates.  The powered run used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins for the organism-level assay, and used $41$ synonymous-design columns.  The recorded conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{fourth-axis identity} \neq \text{mRNA stability}
\end{aligned}
$$
