\subsection{The mRNA-stability refusal for the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ axis}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-fourth-axis-mrna-stability-refusal}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record considered here is the finite residual-coordinate surface obtained from coding sequences after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress component of the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate family and compared with matched readout rows.  The coordinate is not interpreted as a biological effect by itself.  Its admissible readback is a controlled survival entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}$ against a translation-layer row $T_j$, such as a bounded tAI/stAI row or a ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency row.  The present finite row asks a narrower question: whether the empirically recovered fourth optimal residual direction is an mRNA-stability direction.

The $f_3$ saturation seed fixes the codon-level boundary of the coordinate.  The finite set $M=\operatorname{Med}_R\cup\operatorname{ACA}$ used in the row consists of
$$\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
After $f_3$ saturation, the listed surface removes the two $\mathrm{AC}$ third-position alternatives and keeps
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},
\end{aligned}$$
with empty $f_3$ boundary, recorded as $\partial_{f_3}R=\varnothing$.  This is a finite coordinate statement, not a claim about RNA decay, ribosome motion, or protein-level behavior.

\paragraph{Tested stability identity.}
The powered mRNA-stability assay has run id $377\mathrm{abd}67\mathrm{b}8\mathrm{b}3$ and status $\mathrm{passed}$.  It used $5$ folds, a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns.  Its declared conclusion is
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{fourth\_axis\_identity}\neq \mathrm{mRNA\ stability}
\end{aligned}$$
for this assay.  More specifically, the recorded reason is that the yeast $d_{\mathrm{opt\_halflife}}$ row has low alignment with the abundance-associated $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ direction under the fixed gates.  Thus the fourth residual direction should not be named as an mRNA-stability axis on the basis of this finite row.

This negative identity call sits inside a broader powered coordinate family.  The three-axis sufficiency row, computed on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous design columns, concluded that a fourth mechanism is present.  The axis-strength spectrum row, also on $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds and $41$ synonymous columns, found that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$, with per-organism dominant counts $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, while domain means were marked descriptive under the imbalanced domain counts $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$ and the largest GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$.  These numbers support the existence of an additional residual direction in the finite panel, but they do not identify it with mRNA half-life.

\paragraph{Nearby axes and separation from RNA decay.}
The third-axis identity row gives a useful comparison.  It computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms with $5$ folds, $500$ minimum proteins per organism, and $41$ synonymous columns, and provisionally identified $d_{\perp}$ with usage frequency within synonymous families, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  A separate third-axis mechanism row reported that the same direction remains GC-orthogonalized and is best read as selection-not-GC, not as a wobble-clustered axis; it also records that the CSC or positional identity still needs another observational row.  The boundary-set tRNA-poverty row supplies another nearby constraint: $40$ of $46$ requested organisms were computed, $6$ were skipped, the permutation count was $1000$, and the test used $\alpha=0.05$ to support systematic tRNA poverty for $R$ sense codons under GtRNAdb tGCN plus dos Reis wobble weights.  These finite results make the residual-coordinate landscape nontrivial, but none of them turns the fourth axis into an RNA-stability object.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
The correct local interpretation is therefore a refusal of one candidate identity, not a demotion of the whole fourth direction.  The assay says that, under its fixed gates, yeast half-life alignment is too weak for mRNA stability to be the dominant fourth-axis identity.  It does not say that the fourth direction is absent, since the three-axis sufficiency and spectrum rows still record an additional residual direction.  It also does not say what the fourth direction is.  Candidate alternatives such as elongation dwell, codon-pair structure, or translation accuracy must be judged by their own finite rows and their own controls.

The formal boundary can be summarized as follows.  Let $Q_{f_3}$ denote the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate and let $H$ denote the mRNA half-life row used in the stability assay.  The powered row supports the local inequality
$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{align}(Q_{f_3},H)\ \text{is not sufficient to identify}\ d_{\mathrm{resid4}}\ \text{with mRNA stability}.
\end{aligned}$$
It does not authorize the stronger statement that $Q_{f_3}$ has no translation-layer relevance, nor the statement that it has a downstream protein consequence.  A positive translation claim would require a non-vacuous controlled entry $S_{f3\text{-}stress,T_j}>0$ after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  The rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ three-organism surface is a null boundary, not evidence of survival.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
This chapter therefore records a finite negative identity result: the fourth $B^*_{Q6}$ residual direction is not identified with mRNA stability in the stated powered assay.  No translation realization, folded-structure statement, physical-admissibility statement, biological-function statement, mechanism claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological law follows from this row alone.  Codon topology, tRNA or wobble-weight asymmetry, modeled tAI/stAI coupling, and mRNA half-life alignment remain separate kinds of finite evidence.  Any promotion beyond this bounded refusal requires a separate layer-matched contact and an explicit controlled statistic at that layer.
