\paragraph{Finite coordinate being tested.}
The record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate obtained after amino-acid projection, restricted to the $f_3$-stress direction and compared against translation-layer and transcript-layer readouts. The finite saturation row fixes the codon support before any biological interpretation is attempted. The carrier set

Thus the $f_3$ boundary is empty on this finite row, while $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$ are present in $M$ but absent from the saturated $R$ row. This is a coordinate fact about the residual surface; it is not, by itself, an observation of ribosome motion, transcript decay, protein abundance, folded structure, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Assay row and decision.}
The mRNA-stability identity test was run as experiment $\mathrm{b\_star\_q6\_fourth\_mechanism\_mrna\_stability\_powered}$ with run id $377\mathrm{abd}67\mathrm{b}8\mathrm{b}3$. The finite design used $41$ synonymous design columns, $5$ folds, and a minimum threshold of $500$ proteins per organism. Under the fixed gates, the reported decision is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{conclusion} &= \mathrm{fourth\_axis\_not\_mrna\_stability},\\
\mathrm{reason} &= \text{yeast } d_{\mathrm{opt,halflife}} \text{ has low alignment with abundance } d_{\mathrm{resid4}}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite readback therefore refuses the identification of the dominant fourth axis with mRNA stability in this assay. The claim is negative and scoped: it says that this particular half-life-alignment route does not account for the abundance-associated fourth residual direction under the declared filters. It does not say that transcript decay is irrelevant to every organism, every condition, or every codon family.

\paragraph{Relation to the surrounding powered rows.}
The refusal is not an isolated label. A separate powered axis-strength spectrum computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism. In that spectrum, $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ led all $11$ per-organism dominant counts, the domain sample was imbalanced with $1$ archaeal, $2$ bacterial, and $8$ eukaryotic organisms, and the largest reported GC3 rank effect was $\rho=0.136$ for $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$. Another powered sufficiency row, also with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$, $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, and the same $500$-protein threshold, reported $\mathrm{fourth\_mechanism\_present}$. These rows jointly make the mRNA-stability refusal informative: the fourth direction is present in the powered residual assay, but the half-life candidate is not its dominant identity in the yeast stability test.

\paragraph{Competing fourth-axis routes.}
The finite comparison also keeps the other fourth-axis candidates separated. The codon-pair assay with $5$ folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, $50$ shuffle nulls, and the $500$-protein threshold concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$. The translation-accuracy assay computed $11$ of $16$ requested organisms, used $200$ permutations, $5$ folds, and $41$ synonymous design columns, and concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$. The elongation-dwell row in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} reported $\mathrm{partial}$ over $5$ folds. Consequently, the local record places mRNA stability with the refused candidates rather than with the surviving partial dwell-time route. This classification is assay-bound: it orders the named candidate identities under the available powered rows, not all possible molecular explanations.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The only admissible biological readback here is the controlled survival of the $f_3$-stress coordinate against a matched translation or transcript-associated row, after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA controls have been accounted for. The mRNA-stability experiment supplies a negative identity result for the fourth-axis hypothesis; it does not establish a positive translation-survival entry $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}>0$. In particular, neither the $20$-codon $M$ row, the $18$-codon saturated $R$ row, the empty $f_3$ boundary, nor the modeled or measured stability comparison authorizes claims about translation output, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, a general mechanism, or universality. Each of those layers would need its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic before it could be asserted.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
For the standalone BioReality record, the finite verdict is therefore: the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual coordinate remains a live fourth-axis residual subject, but mRNA stability is rejected as the dominant fourth-axis identity in the powered half-life assay. The result is strongest as a scoped exclusion. It narrows the candidate mechanism list while preserving the no-promotion rule from residual coordinate geometry to translation, protein, structure, function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal law.
