\paragraph{Powered assay and local verdict.}
The mRNA-stability assay is a five-fold powered comparison over a synonymous design with $41$ columns and at least $500$ proteins per organism in the retained panel.  The run identifier is $377\mathrm{abd}67\mathrm{b}8\mathrm{b}3$, with deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256}:22\mathrm{c}2\mathrm{c}4\mathrm{dca}83559\mathrm{af}532\mathrm{a}45\mathrm{fe}2777\mathrm{c}3\mathrm{c}21\mathrm{afc}8\mathrm{cd}39\mathrm{d}329\mathrm{c}602491409752243209$.  Its recorded conclusion is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{fourth\_axis}\neq \mathrm{mRNA\ stability}
\end{aligned}
$$
for this assay: in yeast, the half-life-derived direction $d_{\mathrm{opt,halflife}}$ has low alignment with the abundance residual $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ under the fixed gates.  Thus the finite row does not support mRNA stability as the dominant identity of the fourth axis.  The correct chapter-level statement is a negative identification, not a positive mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Context from adjacent finite rows.}
The negative call is not isolated from the surrounding powered geometry.  The three-axis sufficiency test computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, used five folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and concluded that an additional fourth mechanism is present.  The axis-strength spectrum, also with $11$ computed organisms out of $16$ requested and $41$ synonymous design columns, found that captured spectra are led on average by $d_{\mathrm{resid4}}$ and then by $d_{\mathrm{tRNA}}$; the per-organism dominance count was $\{d_{\mathrm{resid4}}:11\}$, while the domain means were explicitly descriptive under the imbalanced sample sizes $\{\mathrm{archaea}:1,\mathrm{bacteria}:2,\mathrm{eukaryote}:8\}$.  The largest recorded GC3 rank effect in that row was $d_{\mathrm{usage}}$ with $\rho=0.136$.  Separately, the third-axis identity row computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, used five folds, and identified $d_{\perp}$ provisionally with usage frequency within synonymous family, with mean cosine $0.128$ and one-sided sign-test $p=0.000488$.  The third-axis mechanism row kept positive cross-species alignment and held-out $R^2$ after GC orthogonalization and classified the signal as selection-not-GC, not wobble-clustered, with the CSC or positional identity still requiring a separate contact.

\paragraph{Discrimination among fourth-mechanism candidates.}
The fourth-axis candidate panel supplies several finite exclusions and one partial positive neighbor.  The codon-pair assay, with five folds, $41$ synonymous design columns, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and $50$ shuffle-null draws, concluded $\mathrm{not\_codon\_pair}$.  The translation-accuracy assay computed $11$ organisms out of $16$ requested, used $200$ permutations and five folds, and concluded $\mathrm{not\_accuracy}$.  The elongation-dwell row was organism-specific to $\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}$ and returned $\mathrm{partial}$.  Against that comparison set, the present row has a sharper local interpretation: mRNA half-life is tested as a candidate identity for the fourth axis and is rejected as the dominant identity in the fixed assay.  The result should therefore be read as a narrowing of the mechanism search space, not as a solved identity for the remaining axis.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The $f_3$-stress coordinate becomes biologically interpretable at the translation layer only when an organism or context supplies a wobble or tRNA-weight asymmetry $w_c\neq w_{c^{f_3}}$ and when a matched translation row survives the declared controls.  The bounded survival entry has the form $S_{f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress},T_j}$ for a tAI, stAI, ribosome-occupancy, or translation-efficiency readout, evaluated after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance, rank, and simpler-baseline controls.  A rank-degenerate all-$1.0$ surface in a three-organism panel is a null boundary, not survival evidence.  Likewise, codon topology or modeled tAI coupling alone does not establish directionality.  For this chapter, the verified row is an mRNA-stability exclusion; it is not a measured ribosome occupancy matrix and it is not a positive translation-survival statistic.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological law follows from this row by itself.  The present finite result says only that, under the declared powered assay, yeast half-life alignment is too low for mRNA stability to serve as the dominant fourth-axis identity.  A separate layer-matched contact would be required before any statement about translation efficiency, RNA decay mechanism, protein output, localization, stability, structure, function, phenotype, causation, or universality could be made.  Thus the admissible conclusion is deliberately narrow: the fourth-axis search remains open, while mRNA stability is locally excluded as its dominant explanation in the available finite-row assay.
