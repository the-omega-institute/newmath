\subsection{Human Tissue-Specific Protein-Abundance Readback for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-tissue-specific-sqp-b-star-q6}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ retain measurable association with human tissue protein abundance after the ordinary CDS-level covariates have already been removed.  The rows are $\textit{Homo sapiens}$ coding sequences joined to PAXdb tissue-specific draft proteome files from the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult tissue panel.  The readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  The tested coordinates are $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Each tissue fit uses the same $24$ control columns: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Controlled tissue rows.}
Three adult tissue contacts were usable, with no skipped tissue in this finite table.  The joined row counts are $9244$ for Adult B cells, $6468$ for Adult Heart, and $8664$ for Adult Liver.  In all three fits the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$, the control design has rank $23$, and the degeneracy guard is $0.999999$.  The controlled protein-abundance survival values are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Adult\ Bcells}} &= 0.03626117434944674, \\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Adult\ Heart}} &= 0.02320896321347296, \\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Adult\ Liver}} &= 0.026133456228248052.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding explained and residual energies are $290.65227005813716$ over $8015.52280842145$ for Adult B cells, $145.8210071220004$ over $6282.960844944177$ for Adult Heart, and $188.166380129298$ over $7200.210277808799$ for Adult Liver.  The residual degrees of freedom after the control fit are $9221$, $6445$, and $8641$, respectively.  The leading coordinate is tissue-dependent in this table: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ in Adult B cells, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$ in Adult Heart, and $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ in Adult Liver.

\paragraph{Tissue comparison.}
The finite-row result is not merely a single pooled human abundance association.  It resolves the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance readback separately across three adult tissue proteomes and then compares the resulting $S^{QP}$ profiles.  The pairwise profile cosines are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos(\mathrm{Adult\ Bcells},\mathrm{Adult\ Heart}) &= 0.6883655232187615, \\
\cos(\mathrm{Adult\ Liver},\mathrm{Adult\ Bcells}) &= 0.8990093450963941, \\
\cos(\mathrm{Adult\ Liver},\mathrm{Adult\ Heart}) &= 0.9176793373754919.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values were computed with $1000$ permutations.  Thus the tissue rows show a stable positive abundance readback in all three tissue contacts, while the dominant coordinate and the profile geometry are not identical across tissues.  The strongest profile separation in this three-tissue panel is between Adult B cells and Adult Heart; Liver is closer to both, especially Heart.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite rows.}
The tissue table is consistent with a broader human cross-layer pattern but does not borrow its conclusion from those neighboring tests.  In the human mRNA-stability row, $16165$ CDS records were joined to a consensus relative half-life readout across $54$ Agarwal--Kelley dataset columns, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9779294769799313$.  That separate human row gave $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$ on the same-join protein-abundance comparison, cosine $0.6351183883924848$ between the $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ profiles, and permutation right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$ for $R^2_{QM}$.  It therefore shows that the human abundance contact is not an isolated spelling artifact of tissue file names, while also keeping mRNA stability distinct from tissue protein abundance.  A separate per-coordinate reducibility audit on yeast abundance used $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $n=1258$ joined proteins, and found real-source support for $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  That audit is not a human-tissue measurement, but it confirms that single-coordinate abundance readbacks are being tested as finite residual objects rather than asserted from notation alone.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
For this tissue-specific human proteomics contact, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate block is not absorbed by amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  All three tissue-specific $S^{QP}$ values are positive under the stated controls, with rank diagnostics far from the declared degeneracy threshold.  The record supports a bounded cross-layer statement: in these Kim et al. adult human tissue proteome rows, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block carries a small but finite controlled association with tissue protein abundance, and the coordinate profile varies by tissue.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
This chapter does not infer ribosome-mediated translation from the proteomics table.  It also does not infer folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  The readout is steady-state tissue protein abundance from one draft human proteome study, not matched ribosome profiling, not matched mRNA-controlled translation efficiency, and not a perturbation assay.  The finite result may be used as a tissue-specific $Q \to P$ abundance readback under the stated covariates; any claim about translation realization, biochemical mechanism, structure, function, or universality requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
