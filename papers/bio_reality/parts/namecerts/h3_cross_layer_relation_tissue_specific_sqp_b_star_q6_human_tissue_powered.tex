\subsection{Human Tissue-Specific $B^*_{Q6}$ Protein-Abundance Survival}
\label{sec:bioreality-human-tissue-specific-sqp-b-star-q6}

\paragraph{Object of the row.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates in $B^*_{Q6}$ retain protein-abundance signal in adult human tissue proteomes after the usual sequence-composition controls have been applied.  The data row is not a claim about a universal translation rule.  It is a finite comparison of three human tissue readouts from the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST draft human proteome files distributed through PAXdb.  For each protein row, the response is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$, and the fitted coordinate block is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = (&K_{AAA},\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR},\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA},\operatorname{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\operatorname{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\operatorname{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\operatorname{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},f_3\text{-stress}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The control family $Z$ has $24$ columns in each tissue file and includes an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the reported survival values are residual protein-abundance fits, not raw codon-abundance correlations.

\paragraph{Finite tissue table.}
The experiment joins three adult tissue-specific proteomics tables to the human CDS-derived codon coordinate table.  The usable row counts are $9244$ proteins for Adult B cells, $6468$ for Adult Heart, and $8664$ for Adult Liver, with no loaded tissue skipped.  In all three cases the residualized coordinate design has rank $9$ and the control design has rank $23$.  The resulting tissue-specific $S^{QP}$ surface is summarized by the partial $R^2$ values
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\ Bcells}) &= 0.03626117434944674,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\ Heart}) &= 0.02320896321347296,\\
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\ Liver}) &= 0.026133456228248052.
\end{aligned}
$$
The dominant residual coordinate changes with tissue: Adult B cells are led by $\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR}$, Adult Heart by $\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}$, and Adult Liver by $\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA}$.  The fitted energies are also finite and nondegenerate: Adult B cells explain $290.65227005813716$ units of residual response energy out of $8015.52280842145$; Adult Heart explains $145.8210071220004$ out of $6282.960844944177$; Adult Liver explains $188.166380129298$ out of $7200.210277808799$.  The corresponding residual degrees of freedom after the control block are $9221$, $6445$, and $8641$.

\paragraph{Tissue comparison.}
The three fitted $S^{QP}$ coordinate profiles are not identical, but they are also not mutually orthogonal.  With $1000$ permutations used in the comparison routine, the pairwise profile cosines are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Bcells}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Heart}}) &= 0.6883655232187615,\\
\cos(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Liver}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Bcells}}) &= 0.8990093450963941,\\
\cos(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Liver}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Heart}}) &= 0.9176793373754919.
\end{aligned}
$$
The Liver--Heart and Liver--B-cell comparisons are therefore high-alignment finite rows, while the B-cell--Heart comparison is more separated.  The row should be read as evidence that the controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance projection is detectable in these three tissue proteomes and that the dominant coordinate is tissue-dependent in this dataset.

\paragraph{Neighboring human checks.}
The tissue row is compatible with two other human finite rows, but it does not inherit stronger biological meaning from them.  In the human mRNA-stability row, $16165$ genes are joined against the Agarwal--Kelley normalized relative half-life consensus, the CDS join hit rate is reported as $0.9779294769799313$, and the controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ half-life fit has $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$ with dominant coordinate $\operatorname{Leu}_{CUN/UUR}$.  On the same human stability comparison, the paired protein-abundance fit has $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$ and the cosine between the $S^{QM}$ and same-join $S^{QP}$ profiles is $0.6351183883924848$ with permutation count $1000$ and reported right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  Separately, the human candidate-readout ranking row uses $19053$ joined proteins for the measured-tRNA-tAI readout route and $11554$ joined rows for measured TE and mRNA routes.  In that row, the human complex-membership entry has $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, $A_H C_H=0.006566030115340095$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0002646171322365322$, and exceeds the threshold; the human measured-TE entry has $A_H=0.002894714925796667$, $C_H=-0.10813537216189718$, $A_H C_H=-0.00031302107580362116$, Bonferroni threshold $0.0004670613303535708$, and also exceeds its threshold.  These neighboring rows show that the human coordinate block reappears under other readout dictionaries, but they do not turn the tissue-specific proteomics fit into a causal chain.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite claim is therefore the following: after amino-acid composition, CDS length, $GC3$, and $M$-density baseline controls, the human tissue-specific protein-abundance readouts retain a small but nonzero $B^*_{Q6}$ residual component in all three loaded tissues.  The fitted effect is largest in Adult B cells among the three rows and smallest in Adult Heart, while the coordinate carrying the largest fitted component changes from $\operatorname{Arg}_{AGR}$ to $\operatorname{Ser}_{UCR/AGY}$ to $\operatorname{Ile}_{AUA}$.  The data support a bounded cross-layer relation between synonymous residual coordinates and steady-state tissue proteomics abundance in this finite human panel.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
No translation mechanism follows from this row by itself.  The readout is tissue-specific protein abundance, not ribosome profiling, not a same-row translation-efficiency matrix, and not a matched mRNA-controlled TE estimate.  The row also does not establish folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, protein-interaction mechanism, tissue physiology, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  The correct conclusion is narrower: three Kim et al. tissue proteomics contacts admit controlled positive $S^{QP}$ fits for the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block, with tissue-specific dominant coordinates and high Liver--Heart and Liver--B-cell profile alignment.  Any claim about measured translation, structural order, function, mechanism, or universality requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
