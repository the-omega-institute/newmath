\subsection{Human tissue-specific protein-abundance survival for $B^{*}_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-human-tissue-specific-sqp}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record.} This record tests whether the nine residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ retain descriptive power for human tissue protein abundance after codon-derived controls have been removed.  The finite table is built on \emph{Homo sapiens} Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST adult-tissue PAXdb readouts, using $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ as the response.  The three usable tissue contacts are Adult Liver, Adult B cells, and Adult Heart, with joined row counts
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{Bcells}} &= 9244,\\
 n_{\mathrm{Heart}} &= 6468,\\
 n_{\mathrm{Liver}} &= 8664.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual coordinate block contains the nine coordinates $K_{\mathrm{AAA}}$, $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN/UUR}}$, $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA/UUG}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR/AGY}}$, $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA/UCG}}$, $\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR/ACY}}$, and $f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$.  In each tissue the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, with controls consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions from the real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Tissue-specific survival values.} Let $X_Q$ denote the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design and let $Z$ denote the control design just described.  For each tissue response $P_t$, the reported statistic is the residual $R^2$ of $P_t$ explained by $X_Q$ after projection away from $Z$.  The finite row gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
 R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\ Bcells}) &= 0.03626117434944674,\\
 R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\ Heart}) &= 0.02320896321347296,\\
 R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{Adult\ Liver}) &= 0.026133456228248052.
\end{aligned}
$$
The leading coordinates are tissue-dependent: Adult B cells are led by $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$, Adult Heart by $\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR/AGY}}$, and Adult Liver by $\mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}}$.  The rank diagnostics are nondegenerate in the recorded sense: $\mathrm{rank}(X_Q\mid Z)=9$ for all three tissues, while the residual degrees of freedom are $9221$, $6445$, and $8641$ for Adult B cells, Adult Heart, and Adult Liver.  The explained energies are respectively $290.65227005813716$, $145.8210071220004$, and $188.166380129298$, against residual response energies $8015.52280842145$, $6282.960844944177$, and $7200.210277808799$.

\paragraph{Between-tissue geometry.} The three fitted $S^{QP}$ tissue profiles are not identical, but they occupy a common positive-alignment regime.  The pairwise cosine comparison, computed with $1000$ permutations in the finite run, reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \cos(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Bcells}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Heart}}) &= 0.6883655232187615,\\
 \cos(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Liver}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Bcells}}) &= 0.8990093450963941,\\
 \cos(S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Liver}},S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Heart}}) &= 0.9176793373754919.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite result is neither a single universal tissue direction nor a null table.  It says that the human adult-tissue abundance readouts preserve a small but measurable $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual component after amino-acid composition, GC3, length, and $M$-density have been accounted for, and that the coordinate carrying the largest share depends on the tissue contact.

\paragraph{Neighboring human readouts.} The tissue row is consistent with, but does not replace, other human cross-layer measurements.  In the human mRNA-stability row, the same nine-coordinate family gives $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$ for the Agarwal--Kelley consensus relative half-life contact over $16165$ joined rows, while the same-join protein-abundance comparison gives $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$.  The cosine between the human $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ profiles is $0.6351183883924848$, with $\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN/UUR}}$ dominant for the mRNA-stability statistic and $\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}}$ dominant for the same-join protein-abundance statistic; the reported permutation count is $1000$ with right-tail value $0.000999000999000999$.  A separate human candidate-readout table over $19053$ joined rows records that $\mathrm{complex\_member}$ is the selected $H_o^*$ for the modeled-tAI-complement abundance component; in that row $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, $A_HC_H=0.006566030115340095$, $DL_H=1.0$, $\mathrm{Score}=-0.0034339698846599055$, and the Bonferroni threshold is $0.0002646171322365322$.  The same ranking table also records the regularization choice $\lambda=0.01$ through the score rule using $DL=\log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{col}})$, with localization carrying $n_{\mathrm{col}}=12$ and $DL=3.700439718141092$.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.} The supported statement is a bounded statistical one: in these three human adult-tissue protein-abundance contacts, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family has positive residual explanatory mass after the stated CDS-derived controls.  The row count scale is large enough to make the small $R^2$ values meaningful as finite descriptive entries, but the dominant coordinate changes across tissues, so the proper readback is tissue-specific residual survival rather than a single conserved direction.

\paragraph{Boundary.} No ribosome-level translation claim follows from this tissue protein-abundance table alone.  The contact is tissue-specific mass-spectrometry abundance, not ribosome profiling, not measured translation efficiency, and not a matched mRNA-controlled translation estimate.  It also does not establish folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, causal mechanism, tissue regulation, phenotype, or a universal biological law.  Any such stronger conclusion would need its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  Raw per-coordinate significance is also not promoted without multiplicity accounting; the finite record explicitly keeps uncorrected coordinate evidence separate from Bonferroni-level reporting.
