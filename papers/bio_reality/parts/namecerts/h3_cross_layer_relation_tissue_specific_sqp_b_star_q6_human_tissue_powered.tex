\subsection{Human Tissue Protein-Abundance Survival of the $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-human-tissue-sqp-b-star-q6}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object.}
This record tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ retain a measurable association with human tissue protein abundance after the amino-acid quotient and the standard coding-sequence controls have already been imposed.  The readout is not a ribosome-profiling contact and not a matched transcript-normalized translation-efficiency contact.  It is the tissue-specific protein-abundance surface obtained from the PAXdb $9606$ Adult Kim 2014 SEQUEST files, read as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ on matched human coding sequences.  The finite row therefore belongs to a cross-layer protein-abundance comparison, not to a direct translation-mechanism assay.

The tested coordinate family is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each tissue $t$, the abundance vector $P_t$ is residualized against a control design $Z_t$ containing an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from the same real CDS codon counts, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, where $M$ is the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Each tissue fit uses $24$ control columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z_t)=23$, and the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$.

\paragraph{Finite tissue rows.}
Three adult human tissue files are usable: Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_Heart, and Adult\_Liver.  The joined row counts are substantial but not identical across tissues: Adult\_Bcells has $n=9244$, Adult\_Heart has $n=6468$, and Adult\_Liver has $n=8664$.  The controlled protein-abundance survival scores are positive in all three tissues:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Adult\_Bcells}} &= 0.03626117434944674,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Adult\_Heart}} &= 0.02320896321347296,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Adult\_Liver}} &= 0.026133456228248052.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $S^{QP}$ is the partial $R^2$ of the nine residual coordinates against the residualized tissue protein-abundance readout.  Adult\_Bcells has explained energy $290.65227005813716$ against residual response energy $8015.52280842145$, with residual degrees of freedom $9221$; Adult\_Heart has explained energy $145.8210071220004$ against residual response energy $6282.960844944177$, with residual degrees of freedom $6445$; Adult\_Liver has explained energy $188.166380129298$ against residual response energy $7200.210277808799$, with residual degrees of freedom $8641$.  The degeneracy threshold used for rank diagnostics is $0.999999$, and none of these rows is a saturated fit.

The dominant coordinate is tissue-dependent.  Adult\_Bcells is led by $Arg\_AGR$, Adult\_Heart by $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and Adult\_Liver by $Ile\_AUA$.  Thus the finite row does not merely say that one global coordinate explains all human tissue protein-abundance residuals.  It says that the same nine-dimensional residual coordinate system remains positive after the shared controls, while the leading axis changes with the tissue readout.

\paragraph{Between-tissue comparison.}
The three tissue survival vectors are not mutually orthogonal, but neither are they identical.  The pairwise cosine comparison, computed with $1000$ permutations for the tissue-comparison audit, is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos(S_{\mathrm{Adult\_Bcells}},S_{\mathrm{Adult\_Heart}}) &= 0.6883655232187615,\\
\cos(S_{\mathrm{Adult\_Liver}},S_{\mathrm{Adult\_Bcells}}) &= 0.8990093450963941,\\
\cos(S_{\mathrm{Adult\_Liver}},S_{\mathrm{Adult\_Heart}}) &= 0.9176793373754919.
\end{aligned}
$$
The liver and heart profiles are closest in this finite panel; B cells and heart are the most separated of the three.  Because the coordinate leaders are $Arg\_AGR$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and $Ile\_AUA$, the cosine structure should be read as partial profile similarity, not as equality of the biological source across tissues.

\paragraph{Relation to companion protein-abundance rows.}
The tissue-specific human row is consistent with a broader abundance-side pattern: in the yeast coordinate-decomposition computation, the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates were decomposed one coordinate at a time against residualized protein abundance on $n=1258$ joined proteins, with $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, a $24$-rank sibling control basis, and at least one single coordinate passing the held-out permutation gate.  That companion computation does not transfer a yeast conclusion to human tissue.  Its use here is narrower: it shows that the coordinate family can be read as a measurable protein-abundance source under the same kind of amino-acid, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls, rather than only as an undifferentiated nine-dimensional regression block.

A separate human mRNA-stability row gives another boundary check.  On $n=16165$ human genes, the same coordinate family explains residualized consensus relative mRNA half-life with $R^2_{QM}=0.010503670362051522$, while the same-join protein-abundance comparison has $R^2_{QP}=0.04095215557813418$ and the cosine between the human $S^{QM}$ and $S^{QP}$ directions is $0.6351183883924848$.  The dominant mRNA-stability coordinate is $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, whereas the same-join protein-abundance dominant coordinate is $Arg\_AGR$.  This companion row prevents a careless reading in which the tissue abundance signal is treated as merely an mRNA half-life signal; the available finite rows separate the protein-abundance surface from the mRNA-stability surface, while still leaving causal mediation unresolved.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite human tissue row supports a bounded statement: after controlling for amino-acid composition, coding length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have positive partial protein-abundance survival in three Kim 2014 adult human tissue proteomics readouts.  The evidence is strongest in Adult\_Bcells by partial $R^2$, with $S^{QP}=0.03626117434944674$, and remains positive in Adult\_Liver and Adult\_Heart.  The coordinate leaders differ by tissue, so the appropriate interpretation is tissue-indexed residual abundance structure rather than a single universal coordinate direction.

This chapter does not infer ribosome-mediated translation from the tissue proteomics files.  It also does not infer folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, causality, or a universal biological law.  Such claims would require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, these rows are not ribo-seq, are not matched mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency estimates, and are not perturbation experiments.  They certify only the finite, controlled association between the nine residual codon coordinates and three tissue-specific human protein-abundance readouts.
