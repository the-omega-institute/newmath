\subsection{Human tissue-specific $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance survival}
\label{subsec:bioreality-human-tissue-specific-sqp-b-star-q6}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This chapter records a tissue-resolved human protein-abundance contact for the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate system.  The measured response is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from human adult-tissue PAXdb files attributed to the Kim et al. 2014 SEQUEST draft human proteome panel.  The three usable tissue contacts are Adult\_Bcells, Adult\_Heart, and Adult\_Liver, with joined row counts
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{Bcells}} &= 9244,\\
 n_{\mathrm{Heart}} &= 6468,\\
 n_{\mathrm{Liver}} &= 8664.
\end{aligned}
$$
The tested coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = (&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress).
\end{aligned}
$$
For each tissue, the nuisance design has $24$ columns and residual rank $23$.  Its entries are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the statistic is a residual protein-abundance statistic after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and the simpler codon-support baseline have already been charged to the control side.

\paragraph{Controlled $S^{QP}$ readback.}
Let $X_t$ be the $n_t\times 9$ matrix of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates for tissue $t$, let $Z_t$ be the $n_t\times 24$ control matrix, and let $y_t$ be the tissue protein-abundance vector.  The reported scalar is the partial protein-abundance survival score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_t &= R^2\bigl(y_t; X_t \mid Z_t\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
computed only after residualizing both $X_t$ and $y_t$ against $Z_t$.  The finite rows are
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Bcells}} &= 0.03626117434944674,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Heart}} &= 0.02320896321347296,\\
S^{QP}_{\mathrm{Liver}} &= 0.026133456228248052.
\end{aligned}
$$
All three entries are positive in the controlled protein-abundance sense.  The dominant coordinate differs by tissue: Adult\_Bcells is dominated by $Arg\_AGR$, Adult\_Heart by $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, and Adult\_Liver by $Ile\_AUA$.  The fitted residual designs have full coordinate rank $9$ in all three tissue rows, and the residual degrees of freedom after the $Z$ quotient are $9221$, $6445$, and $8641$, respectively.  The explained and residual energies are
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{\mathrm{explained,Bcells}} &= 290.65227005813716,& E_{\mathrm{resid,Bcells}} &= 8015.52280842145,\\
E_{\mathrm{explained,Heart}} &= 145.8210071220004,& E_{\mathrm{resid,Heart}} &= 6282.960844944177,\\
E_{\mathrm{explained,Liver}} &= 188.166380129298,& E_{\mathrm{resid,Liver}} &= 7200.210277808799.
\end{aligned}
$$
The degenerate-fit cutoff stored with the computation is $0.999999$, so these finite rows are far from a saturated rank artifact.

\paragraph{Tissue comparison.}
The three tissue-specific $S^{QP}$ profiles are not identical, but they are not unrelated.  The pairwise cosine comparison used $1000$ permutations and produced
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos(\mathrm{Bcells},\mathrm{Heart}) &= 0.6883655232187615,\\
\cos(\mathrm{Liver},\mathrm{Bcells}) &= 0.8990093450963941,\\
\cos(\mathrm{Liver},\mathrm{Heart}) &= 0.9176793373754919.
\end{aligned}
$$
This places Adult\_Liver closer to Adult\_Heart than Adult\_Bcells is to Adult\_Heart, while Adult\_Bcells and Adult\_Liver still share a high positive profile cosine.  The coordinate identity of the leading term nevertheless changes across tissues, so the record supports a tissue-resolved abundance association rather than a single universal coordinate direction.

\paragraph{Adjacent human mRNA stability control.}
A separate human finite row in the same biological program tested $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ against a consensus human mRNA half-life readout.  That contact joined $16165$ coding sequences to an Agarwal and Kelley consensus panel with $54$ dataset columns, using an Ensembl-gene-id match with a small gene-symbol fallback.  The reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9779294769799313$.  On that distinct readout, the controlled mRNA-stability score was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QM} &= 0.010503670362051522,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the dominant coordinate $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, while the same-join protein-abundance score in that row was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP,\mathrm{same\ join}} &= 0.04095215557813418.
\end{aligned}
$$
The cosine between the human $S^{QM}$ and same-join $S^{QP}$ profiles was $0.6351183883924848$.  This adjacent row is useful here only as a boundary check: the tissue-specific protein-abundance rows above are not mRNA-controlled tissue translation-efficiency rows, and the human half-life statistic does not convert them into a causal mRNA-stability mechanism.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The tissue-specific contact gives a bounded, measured protein-abundance association: after the declared CDS-derived controls, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain positive partial $R^2$ in three adult human tissue proteomics readouts.  The record is therefore a cross-layer protein-abundance readback for human tissue panels, not merely a codon-usage geometry statement.  Its strongest local statement is that the nine residual coordinates are not wholly absorbed by amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline on these three tissue proteomics surfaces.

\paragraph{Non-promotion boundary.}
No stronger biological claim is licensed by these rows alone.  The tissue contacts are mass-spectrometry protein-abundance readouts, not ribosome-profiling or matched translation-efficiency measurements.  They do not establish translation realization, ribosome dwell dynamics, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, tissue causality, or a universal biological law.  The phrase tissue-specific here means only that three Kim et al. adult-tissue proteomics surfaces were fit and compared under the stated controls.  Any claim about translation, folding, function, mechanism, or generality would require a separate layer-matched contact and a separately reported statistic.
