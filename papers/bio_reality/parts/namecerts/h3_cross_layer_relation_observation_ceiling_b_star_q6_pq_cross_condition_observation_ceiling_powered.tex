\subsection{Translation-Bounded RNA Half-Life Ceiling for B*\_Q6 Windows}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-rna-half-life-rbp-observation-ceiling}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns synonymously editable coding-RNA windows whose rows carry B*\_Q6 residual-coordinate labels, same-row translation readouts, RNA half-life readouts, measured occupancy by a prespecified decay-factor RNA-binding protein, perturbation state, rescue state, transcript abundance, splice or isoform state, and batch labels. The operative carrier is not an unrestricted sequence law. It is a finite matched panel in which high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows are compared only after amino-acid composition, transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC content, dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, isoform state, abundance, translation readout, and batch have been kept in the control set. The finite run attached to this packet is experiment run \texttt{8f258b58b625}, executed for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; it started at \texttt{2026-06-29T13:29:24+00:00}, completed at \texttt{2026-06-29T13:37:41+00:00}, and recorded runtime $497.03086400032043$ seconds. Those numbers certify a bounded yeast-side audit event, not a positive RNA-decay mechanism.

\paragraph{Bounded statistic.}
The only statistic licensed here has the form of a finite comparison among coordinate label, same-row translation readout, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, perturbation or rescue state, and RNA half-life. In schematic notation, with $q$ the B*\_Q6 residual label, $T$ the translation readout, $O$ the measured local target-RBP occupancy, $P$ the perturbation/rescue state, $H$ the RNA half-life readout, and $Z$ the declared control block, the admissible readout is restricted to a controlled finite contrast such as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_H(q,O,P;T,Z) &= H \mid (q,O,P,T,Z) - H \mid (q',O',P',T,Z),\\
Z &= \{\text{transcription, structure, motifs, GC, dinucleotides, codon pairs, isoform, abundance, batch}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The contrast may be reported as a cross-layer relation only if coordinate and occupancy can be experimentally separated, the half-life effect tracks the measured occupancy of the named decay-factor RBP, target-RBP loss collapses the coordinate-associated half-life effect, wild-type rescue restores it, and binding-defective or non-target controls fail to restore it. A half-life shift without those rows remains an RNA half-life association boundary; it is not an RBP-mediated decay result.

\paragraph{Observation ceiling.}
The present finite-row payload supplies the yeast run identifier, organism key, timestamps, and runtime for the B*\_Q6 observation-ceiling audit, but it does not provide the joined row matrix needed for a positive bridge: no same-window measured decay-factor RBP occupancy rows, no coordinate/occupancy decoupling arm, no target-RBP loss arm, no wild-type rescue arm, and no binding-defective or non-target rescue arm are present in the supplied facts. The ceiling is therefore local and negative: B*\_Q6 geometry, codon-window labels, and the existence of translation and RNA-half-life contact names do not by themselves measure RNA decay, RBP occupancy, or causal mediation. A matched RNA half-life contact can bound an RNA-stability readout; a ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contact can bound a translation readout; a matched mRNA abundance contact can serve as a transcription-layer control. None of these contacts substitutes for measured decay-factor RBP occupancy or perturbation/rescue evidence.

\paragraph{Matched null.}
The null model makes high-coordinate and low-coordinate labels exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life after the declared controls and after the same-row translation boundary is attached. In finite-row form, the null says that any apparent half-life separation must disappear once $Z$ and $T$ are conditioned on, unless it is carried by the prespecified occupancy and perturbation pattern:
$$
\begin{aligned}
H \perp q \mid T,Z
\end{aligned}
$$
for the matched null rows, with rejection allowed only by a finite statistic that also respects $O$ and $P$. If half-life differs while measured target-RBP occupancy does not differ under coordinate/occupancy decoupling, the packet stays null. If target-RBP loss does not collapse the effect, or if wild-type rescue does not restore it, the packet stays null. If binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, RNA structure, motif density, transcription rate, isoform stability, GC, dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, transcript abundance, or batch reproduces the pattern, the packet also stays null.

\paragraph{Numerical context from adjacent finite audit.}
The linked repair-persistence audit \texttt{b3fb5017b9d7} supplies a useful scale for what a reported finite biological statistic looks like when scalar evidence is actually present: it used $12055$ windows, $1000$ permutations, permutation value $p=0.003$, control held-out $R^2=0.1878$, sequence-augmented held-out $R^2=0.1989$, incremental held-out $R^2=0.0111$ for the RSC readout, incremental held-out $R^2=0.0246$ for SWI/SNF specificity, and runtime $284.4$ seconds; it classified the RSC-specific flag as false. Those scalars are not transferred to the B*\_Q6 RNA-half-life claim. They are cited only to mark the standard of finite-row reporting: a positive B*\_Q6 RNA-decay bridge would need its own row count, occupancy contrast, perturbation/rescue effect sizes, null distribution, and controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter licenses only a bounded observation ceiling for the proposed translation-conditioned RNA half-life bridge. It does not license translation realization from RNA half-life alone, measured decay-factor RBP occupancy from half-life alone, an RNA decay mechanism from a same-row translation readout, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological rule. Each such statement would require its own layer-matched contact and finite statistic. Until the same-organism same-window panel contains B*\_Q6 labels, translation readouts, matched mRNA abundance, RNA half-life, measured target-RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls, the proper conclusion is a controlled non-promotion result: the packet identifies what would have to be measured, and it blocks the stronger biological interpretation.
