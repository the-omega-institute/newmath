\paragraph{Observation-ceiling statement.}
The admissible claim is deliberately narrow. A $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate-associated RNA half-life difference may be reported only as a bounded cross-layer relation if it is conditioned on the same-row translation readout and if the decay-factor RBP bridge is externally measured rather than inferred from codon-window geometry. The row relation has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
(q_i, T_j, H_k, O_\ell, P_m, Z) \longmapsto \Delta H,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is the residual coordinate label, $T_j$ is the matched translation readout, $H_k$ is the RNA half-life readout, $O_\ell$ is measured local occupancy of the prespecified decay-factor RBP, $P_m$ is the perturbation or rescue state, and $Z$ collects transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, amino-acid composition, translation-readout controls, and batch. The statistic is finite because it is evaluated only on the joined rows of this panel; it is not a statement about all coding sequences or all organisms.

\paragraph{Positive-support condition.}
A positive bridge requires four simultaneous observations. First, high-coordinate and low-coordinate synonymous windows must remain matched after amino-acid composition and the declared covariates are controlled. Second, the half-life contrast must track measured target decay-factor RBP occupancy at the same RNA window when coordinate and occupancy are experimentally decoupled. Third, the coordinate-associated half-life effect must collapse when the target RBP is lost and return under wild-type rescue. Fourth, the same pattern must not be restored by binding-defective rescue, non-target controls, RNA-structure covariates, motif density, transcription rate, isoform stability, GC, dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, transcript abundance, translation-readout artifacts, or batch. In compact form, the licensed bridge is the finite conjunction
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\operatorname{HalfLife}(q_i,T_j,O_\ell,P_{\mathrm{wt}},Z) \neq
  \operatorname{HalfLife}(q_{i'},T_j,O_{\ell'},P_{\mathrm{wt}},Z),\\
&\operatorname{Effect}(P_{\mathrm{loss}}) \approx 0,\\
&\operatorname{Effect}(P_{\mathrm{wt\ rescue}}) \approx \operatorname{Effect}(P_{\mathrm{wt}}),\\
&\operatorname{Effect}(P_{\mathrm{binding\ defective}}) \approx 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the additional requirement that the occupancy term $O_\ell$ is measured and not selected post hoc from the coordinate labels.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The matched null says that, after controlling transcription rate, local RNA structure, motif density, GC and dinucleotide composition, codon-pair context, splice or isoform state, transcript abundance, same-row translation readout, and batch, high- and low-coordinate labels are exchangeable with respect to RNA half-life. The run metadata for \textit{S. cerevisiae} shows that the observation-ceiling check completed, but the available payload does not contain the finite row table needed to reject this null. Therefore the chapter records the permitted shape of the claim and the organism-scoped completion of the check, not a numerical RBP-decay effect.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows from RNA half-life alone. No RNA-decay mechanism follows from a coordinate-associated half-life difference unless measured decay-factor RBP occupancy, coordinate/occupancy decoupling, target-RBP loss, wild-type rescue, and binding-defective or non-target controls are present in the same finite panel. The ribosome-profiling contact can bound a translation readout; the matched mRNA contact can serve as a transcription-layer control; the RNA half-life contact can provide a decay readout. None of these contacts, separately or by name, supplies measured RBP occupancy, perturbation collapse, rescue restoration, protein output, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or a universal sequence law. The $B^*_{Q6}$ label remains an internal residual coordinate until a layer-matched observation supplies the corresponding biological measurement.

For experiment $8f258b58b625$, the finite evidence available here supports only an observation ceiling for a translation-conditioned RNA-half-life/RBP bridge in \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The scientific content is the admissibility rule: a coordinate-associated RNA half-life effect can be called a bounded cross-layer relation only when the same-row translation boundary and the measured decay-factor RBP perturbation/rescue boundary are both attached.
