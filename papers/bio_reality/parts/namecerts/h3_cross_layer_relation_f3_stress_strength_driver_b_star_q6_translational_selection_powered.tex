\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate named $f_3$-stress, after amino-acid projection has removed the part of codon usage that is already determined by amino-acid composition. The finite surface is organism by codon readout data, restricted to synonymous fibers where an $f_3$-paired codon comparison is meaningful, together with codon-usage rows, modeled tAI or stAI wobble weights, and translation-efficiency or translation-proximal readouts when those readouts are available. The geometric background is nontrivial but finite: the quotient check reports $\pi_M$ size $10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and matching $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. In the residual-basis contact, three organisms were loaded, namely human, yeast, and $E.\ coli$ K-12, and the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ basis had nine computed residual entries. Seven of the nine entries were nonzero at the declared variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$. The listed residuals were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and the antisymmetric $f_3$-stress pair.

\paragraph{Rank condition and modeled translation readout.}
The powered modeled-tAI surface is not the three-organism bounded-contact surface. The bounded three-organism modeled-tAI matrix had all $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but that contact explicitly had only $n=3$ organisms and therefore was not treated as powered survival. The powered panel used $40$ organisms, a nine-column modeled tAI readout projected onto the same $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and controls of rank $23$ with $24$ control columns. After these controls the residual degrees of freedom were $17$, the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ design had rank $9$, and the controlled translation-readout fit had $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the degeneracy refusal threshold $0.999999$. In symbols, the finite readout condition used here is the nondegenerate case
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n &= 40,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
 \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
 \operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17,\\
 R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579 < 0.999999.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the $S^{QT}$ entries in this record are read as rank-powered modeled translation-readout survival, not as a ribosome-profiling observation and not as protein-output evidence.

\paragraph{Cross-organism strength driver.}
The strength-driver computation tested whether the observed magnitude of the $f_3$-stress coordinate is explained by total synonymous selection strength together with a fixed directional alignment. The powered run requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ usable organisms, with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds. Its reported Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was
$$
\begin{aligned}
 \rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}} &= 0.7071428571428572.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direction check was complete on the computed panel: $15$ of $15$ organisms agreed in direction, so the direction-agreement rate was exactly $1.0$. The finite interpretation is therefore not merely that $f_3$-stress appears in codon usage, but that its cross-organism strength is aligned with a translation-selection summary on the same powered readout route. The run also records the Sulfolobus negative-alignment case as direction-aware rather than as a contradiction; that flag prevents the sign of a single organism from being promoted to a universal direction rule.

\paragraph{Generality and local ribosome-window contact.}
A separate cross-organism powered check requested $16$ organisms, tested $15$ real organisms, used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, and called the result $f_3$-stress generalizes, with real threshold $8$. This supports the use of the coordinate as a recurring cross-organism residual feature rather than as a one-dataset artifact. A position-controlled ribosome-occupancy analysis gives a narrower same-window contact: $5754$ genes joined between ribosome occupancy and ordered CDS rows, with $0$ length mismatches, $9$ computed families under the within-position-bin permutation null, and all $9$ families having at least $200$ permutations. Under the local gate with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and $C_H>0$, the passing families were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f_3$-stress. This is a local occupancy separation under position-bin controls, not a causal elongation model.

\paragraph{Relation to downstream decompositions.}
The organism-level decomposition records show why the claim is kept at the translation-selection boundary. In the ordered decomposition, yeast had $T=0.11271394500088064$, $S=0.019627420469677935$, and unexplained fraction $0.8676586345294415$; human had $T=0.006011907792542833$, $S=0.006163991439034291$, and unexplained fraction $0.9736442326306781$; $E.\ coli$ K-12 MG1655 had $T=0.00035964766234144823$, $S=0.005357973825835613$, and unexplained fraction $0.9942823785118229$; zebrafish had $T=0.02436809579891462$ with the stability component skipped and unexplained fraction $0.9756319042010854$. The direction-aware version similarly left large residual fractions: yeast $0.8676586345294415$, human $0.9798666209022441$, $E.\ coli$ $0.9942823785118229$, and zebrafish $0.9756319042010854$. These values are compatible with a translation-selection driver for $f_3$-stress strength, but they do not convert that driver into a broad account of protein state, function, or organism phenotype.

\paragraph{Verdict and boundary.}
The finite-row verdict is that the $f_3$-stress coordinate is promoted from a bounded codon residual to a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation distinction only on the powered modeled-readout route where the wobble surface breaks the $f_3$ symmetry and the rank condition is nondegenerate. The decisive finite numbers are the $15/15$ direction agreement, direction rate $1.0$, Spearman value $0.7071428571428572$, $40$-organism rank-powered modeled-tAI panel, rank $9$ residual design, rank $23$ controls, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$. If the paired wobble weights are symmetric, or if the readout matrix is rank saturated as in the three-organism bounded surface, the correct status is null or needs more data. No translation mechanism, measured ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this chapter. Each of those stronger statements requires its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic beyond the finite rows used here.
