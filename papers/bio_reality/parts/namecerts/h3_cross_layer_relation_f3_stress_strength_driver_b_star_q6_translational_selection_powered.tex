\subsection{F3-Stress Strength Tracks Synonymous Selection Strength}\label{sec:biorality-f3-stress-strength-driver}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.} This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate named $f_3$-stress on a finite organism-by-codon readout surface. The coordinate is not an amino-acid composition effect: it is read after quotienting by amino-acid projection, so the measured direction is the residual coordinate $q-P_{\tau}q$ on $f_3$-paired synonymous codon fibers. The finite quotient geometry used by the packet has $\pi_M$ size $10$, with quotient face vector $[10,14,6,1]$ and matching $M_f$ vector $[20,38,26,8,1]$. On the concrete residual-basis contact, there are $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. Seven of these nine coordinates are nonzero above the declared residual-variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$ in the finite three-organism codon-count contact. This establishes that the $f_3$-stress coordinate is not empty as a codon-usage residual, but it does not by itself promote the coordinate to translation, protein, structure, function, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Powered strength-driver test.} The strength-driver test asks whether the magnitude of $f_3$-stress across organisms is explained by total synonymous selection strength together with a stable directional alignment. The powered run requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms after applying the minimum protein-count gate of $500$ proteins per organism. The held-out design used $5$ folds and the deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered{:}deterministic}$. The finite result was positive: the Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was $0.7071428571428572$, and the direction agreement was $15/15$, hence direction agreement rate $1.0$. The run also records the Sulfolobus case as a negative-alignment-and-direction case, so the result is not being phrased as a universal positive sign for all organisms. The finite summary is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{requested}}&=16, & n_{\mathrm{computed}}&=15, & k_{\mathrm{fold}}&=5,\\
 n_{\mathrm{min\ proteins}}&=500, & \rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}&=0.7071428571428572, & d&=15/15=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The chapter-level interpretation is narrower than a causal mechanism: within this finite panel, $f_3$-stress strength is accounted for by the product of total synonymous selection strength and a fixed-direction alignment rule. This is a cross-layer relation only at the level of codon-topology residuals and bounded translation-selection readouts.

\paragraph{Relation to translation readouts.} The surrounding finite evidence prevents the strength-driver result from floating free of readout rank. In the modeled tAI survival matrix, the powered contact used $40$ organisms, $9$ residual coordinates, rank $9$ after residualizing the coordinate block, control-rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$. Its modeled $S^{QT}$ surface had $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ and matrix shape $9\times 9$. The controls include organism, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and related baseline columns; the control table has $24$ columns with rank $23$. This supports a bounded modeled-translation readout, not observed ribosome occupancy or protein abundance. The separate cross-organism generality run also computed $15$ organisms out of $16$ requested, with $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, a real threshold of $8$, and status passed. Thus the strength-driver result is embedded in a rank-powered family of finite tests rather than in the earlier $n=3$ rank-saturated surface.

\paragraph{Wobble and occupancy boundary.} The $f_3$-stress coordinate is biologically interpretable only when the codon-paired wobble weights break the $f_3$ pairing. If the paired weights satisfy $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$, the coordinate remains a codon-usage residual and not a translation variable. A related position-controlled ribosome-occupancy contact joined $5754$ genes after length checking, with $5754$ overlapping gene identifiers and zero length mismatches. In that contact all $9$ families had at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations, the description-length penalty used $DL=2$ and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress under the rule $C_H>0$. This supports the plausibility of a local translation-readout interface for $f_3$-stress, but the present strength-driver chapter does not treat that occupancy result as a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Local claim and refusal line.} The accepted claim is finite and conditional: in the powered organism panel, $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous selection strength with Spearman $0.7071428571428572$ and perfect observed direction agreement $15/15$, subject to the rank, organism-count, protein-count, and wobble-asymmetry gates stated above. The claim stops at a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation-selection readout. It does not assert protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, biochemical function, phenotype, adaptation mechanism, causation, or a global biological law. Any such higher-layer statement would require a separate matched contact and a same-row statistic after the translation-readout step has survived the declared controls. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded annotations, matched mRNA abundance is a control covariate, and ribosome-profiling names do not count as joined measured rows until codon counts, wobble weights, translation readouts, and controls are actually assembled in the same finite matrix.
