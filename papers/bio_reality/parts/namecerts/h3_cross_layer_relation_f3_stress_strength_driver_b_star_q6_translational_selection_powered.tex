\subsection{The $f_3$-stress strength driver in a powered translation-selection panel}\label{subsec:bior reality-f3-stress-strength-driver}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress.  The carrier is a finite organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous codon fibres paired by the $f_3$ involution.  Each row carries codon usage, a paired wobble-weight surface such as tAI or stAI, and a translation-oriented readout.  The coordinate is not the raw codon-count vector; it is the part left after amino-acid projection, written informally as $q-P_\tau q$.  The finite quotient geometry used by the construction has $M$-side face vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$, quotient face vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and $|\pi_M|=10$.  On the observed residual-basis contact there are nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\; Arg_{AGR},\; Ile_{AUA},\; Leu_{CUN/UUR},\; Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\; Ser_{UCA/UCG},\; Thr_{ACR/ACY},\; f_3\text{-stress},
\end{aligned}
$$
and seven of the nine show nonzero finite-contact residual variance above the stated threshold, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$ on the three-organism contact.

\paragraph{Powered translation-selection readback.}
The strength-driver run used a deterministic seed, five folds, at least $500$ proteins per organism, and computed $15$ organisms out of $16$ requested.  Its primary scalar readout is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\text{total synonymous selection},\; |f_3\text{-stress}|)
  = 0.7071428571428572.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run reports direction agreement on every computed organism:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite-row interpretation is not merely that $f_3$-stress is present in codon usage.  The powered panel says that its strength tracks total synonymous-selection strength, multiplied by a fixed-direction alignment, across all $15$ computed organisms.  The run also marks the Sulfolobus row as having the negative-alignment-and-direction case, so the sign convention is not silently collapsed into a one-direction narrative; the row is retained as part of the direction-aware finite classification.

\paragraph{Relation to rank and translation readout.}
The powered modeled-tAI survival surface supplies the rank context for treating this as a cross-layer relation rather than a three-row saturated fit.  That surface uses $40$ organisms, nine residual coordinates, rank $9$ after residualization, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  Its modeled tAI readout has $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  The three-organism modeled-tAI contact is therefore not used as positive survival by itself, even though all $81$ entries exceed the bounded-contact threshold in that small matrix.  The small contact remains a coupling and routing surface; the powered interpretation requires the larger rank-sufficient panel.  Separately, the cross-organism generality run tested $15$ real organisms, used five folds and $120$ permutations, and called $f_3$-stress generalized with a real threshold of $8$.  These companion rows support the present chapter's narrower claim: strength is not an isolated residue of one codon table, but a recurrent finite pattern under the declared organism panel and controls.

\paragraph{Wobble and ribosome-window contact.}
The position-aware ribosome-occupancy contact gives a more local translation-proximal check without turning the chapter into a mechanism claim.  It joined $5754$ genes after length checking, with zero length mismatches.  The within-position-bin permutation null requested $200$ permutations, and all nine families had at least $200$ permutations.  The lambda and description-length gate used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ with $DL=2$, and the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This is consistent with the strength-driver picture: the $f_3$-stress coordinate survives as a translation-proximal distinction only where the finite readout and the wobble or occupancy surface break the paired-codon symmetry enough to be seen after controls.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter-level verdict is that $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress is a finite cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation distinction in the powered translation-selection panel.  The positive statement is deliberately bounded: the evidence is a rank-powered relation between an amino-acid-quotient synonymous residual coordinate, organism-level synonymous-selection strength, direction agreement, and translation-oriented readouts.  It does not say that $f_3$-stress by itself measures translation dynamics in every organism, and it does not promote codon topology into protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger conclusions would require their own row-matched contacts and statistics at the corresponding layer.  If the paired wobble weights were $f_3$-symmetric, or if the matrix were rank-saturated, or if the same-row translation readout failed after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and mRNA-abundance controls where available, the correct status would be null rather than positive.
