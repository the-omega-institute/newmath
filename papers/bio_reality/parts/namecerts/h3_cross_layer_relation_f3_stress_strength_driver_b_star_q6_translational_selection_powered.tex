\subsection{F3-Stress Strength as a Translational-Selection Driver}
\label{sec:f3-stress-strength-translational-selection-driver}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
The record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate named $f_3$-stress.  The finite carrier is an organism-by-codon readout table restricted to synonymous codon fibers, with the amino-acid projection removed before the $f_3$ coordinate is scored.  Thus the coordinate is not an amino-acid-composition effect: it is the residual component left after projecting codon usage through the amino-acid quotient.  The finite quotient contact used here has $\pi_M$ size $10$, with quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$ and ambient $M$ $f$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$.  On the three-organism residual-basis check, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface had $9$ residual coordinates, and $7$ of them were nonzero above the declared variance threshold, giving nonzero fraction $0.7777777777777778$.  The coordinate list was $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.

\paragraph{Selection-strength statistic.}
The powered driver test asks whether the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress coordinate is accounted for by total synonymous-selection strength together with a fixed-direction alignment.  In the finite run, $16$ organisms were requested and $15$ organisms were computable after the row filters, with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  The observed Spearman association between total synonymous-selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}} &= 0.7071428571428572 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The direction agreement count was $15$ out of $15$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{direction\ agreement\ rate} &= \frac{15}{15}=1.0 .
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite verdict of the run is therefore that $f_3$-stress strength is explained, on this bounded surface, by total synonymous-selection strength multiplied by a fixed-direction alignment.  The run also records a negative-alignment direction case for Sulfolobus, so the readout is not merely a same-sign artifact of the coordinate convention; the direction variable is part of the finite driver certificate.

\paragraph{Rank and translation-readout context.}
The driver claim is interpreted only after the rank-powered translation-readout surface is nondegenerate.  In the modeled tAI panel, $40$ organisms were loaded, the residualized control design had $24$ control columns, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual matrix had $9$ coordinates and the residualized coordinate rank was $9$.  The modeled translation-readout matrix was $9\times 9$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579 .
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the powered setting in which an $S^{QT}$-style translation-readout entry can be discussed as a bounded modeled readout.  It is distinct from the earlier three-organism contact, where the modeled tAI coupling was nonzero but underpowered: there were $3$ organisms, $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold, and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, yet that surface was explicitly not a powered survival result.  The present driver statement therefore uses the powered multi-organism row set, not the rank-saturated $n=3$ surface.

\paragraph{Cross-organism support.}
A companion finite check tested cross-organism generality for the same $f_3$-stress branch.  It requested $16$ organisms, retained $15$ real organisms, used $5$ folds, and ran $120$ permutations with real threshold $8$.  Its call was $f_3\_stress\_generalizes$.  A separate ribosome-occupancy contact gives a lower-level consistency check rather than the driver statistic itself: $5754$ genes joined between ribosome-occupancy and ordered-CDS rows after the length check, all $9$ families had at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations, and the families passing the $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and $C_H>0$ gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This supports that the same residual coordinate is not isolated to a single bookkeeping surface, but it does not by itself prove the translational-selection driver claim.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
On the finite rows available here, the claim is ready as a bounded cross-layer relation: the $f_3$-stress residual is a codon-topology coordinate whose strength tracks total synonymous-selection strength with Spearman value $0.7071428571428572$ and perfect recorded direction agreement $15/15$ on the computable organism panel.  The claim is not that $f_3$-stress is a universal biological law, a protein-output determinant, or a mechanistic cause.  It is a finite, controlled association between a residual codon coordinate and a modeled translation-selection strength surface, with rank sufficiency supplied by the $40$-organism modeled tAI panel and cross-organism consistency supplied by the $15$-organism generality run.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
No translation realization beyond the stated modeled or explicitly bounded readout follows from this paragraph alone.  No protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, post-translational state, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the $f_3$-stress coordinate, from wobble-weight asymmetry, from codon-window geometry, or from a positive driver statistic.  Any such higher-layer conclusion would require its own layer-matched contact and a same-row statistic after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA abundance where available, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline controls.
