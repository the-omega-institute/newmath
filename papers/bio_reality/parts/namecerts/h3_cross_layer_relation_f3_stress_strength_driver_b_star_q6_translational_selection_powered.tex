\subsection{Finite strength test for the $f_3$-stress translation-selection driver}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-strength-driver}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
The record studied here is the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual restricted to finite organism-by-codon and organism-by-protein readout tables.  The coordinate is not a raw codon frequency.  It is the amino-acid-quotient residual left after synonymous codon usage has been projected away from the amino-acid composition baseline.  In the finite quotient geometry used by this record, the $M_f$ vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_f &= (20,38,26,8,1),
\end{aligned}
$$
and the quotient $f$-vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{\mathrm{quot}} &= (10,14,6,1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The quotient support has size $10$.  These numbers fix the codon-topological compression before any biological readout is consulted.  On the three-organism codon-count contact used for the residual-basis check, the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate list has $9$ entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Seven of the nine residual coordinates are nonzero at the finite variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
7/9 &= 0.7777777777777778.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the coordinate is finite and nontrivial at the codon-residual layer, but this fact alone is only a codon-usage statement.

\paragraph{Wobble-broken translation-readout context.}
The cross-layer question is whether the $f_3$-stress coordinate gains explanatory power only when the codon-paired wobble-weight surface is not symmetric under the $f_3$ pairing.  The bounded modeled-translation surface supplies such a test without identifying it with measured ribosome movement.  In the three-organism modeled-tAI contact, all $9 \times 9$ entries of the modeled coupling matrix are above the finite threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
That three-organism surface is intentionally not a powered survival result, because $n=3$ cannot support the controlled rank claim.  The powered modeled-tAI readout is the separate $40$-organism panel.  In that panel, the control design has $24$ columns, $rank(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ rank $9$.  The modeled $Q \to T$ surface has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the degeneracy ceiling set at $0.999999$.  These rank numbers are the reason the $40$-organism surface may be treated as powered modeled translation-readout survival, while the all-small-panel or saturated cases remain null or data-seeking boundaries.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The present finite-row claim is stronger and narrower than the survival surface: it asks whether the magnitude of $f_3$-stress across organisms is explained by total synonymous selection strength together with a fixed direction of alignment.  The verified finite run requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms after enforcing the minimum of $500$ proteins per organism.  The cross-validation design used $5$ folds.  The resulting Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}} &= 0.7071428571428572.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direction agreement count is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
15/15 &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run also records the special Sulfolobus case as negative alignment with the corresponding direction retained, so the result is not being stated as a universal positive sign.  The finite interpretation is that $f_3$-stress strength is accounted for by the product of total synonymous selection strength and fixed-direction alignment on this tested panel.

\paragraph{Generality and local-ribosome consistency checks.}
The companion cross-organism generality run used $15$ real organisms out of $16$ requested, again with the $500$ protein minimum.  Its held-out design used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, with real threshold $8$, and classified the result as $f_3$-stress generalization.  This supplies a finite robustness check for the organism panel, not a biological law.  A separate ribosome-occupancy contact provides a position-controlled local check: $5754$ genes overlap between ribosome occupancy and ordered coding-sequence rows, the length-mismatch count is $0$, and all $9$ synonymous families have at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations requested for their null computation.  Under the rule $C_H>0$, description length $DL=2$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, the families passing all local gates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This is consistent with a translation-proximal readout of the same coordinate, while remaining descriptive and position-confound-controlled.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded statement.  On the tested organism panel, $f_3$-stress is not merely a leftover codon-count coordinate: its strength is associated with total synonymous selection strength with Spearman value $0.7071428571428572$, and its alignment direction agrees in all $15$ computed organisms.  Together with the powered modeled-tAI surface, the cross-organism generality check, and the local ribosome-occupancy screen in which $f_3$-stress passes the $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and $C_H>0$ gates, the row supports a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation-readout distinction under wobble-broken and rank-sufficient conditions.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The conclusion stops at bounded translation-readout association.  It does not say that $f_3$-stress is itself a dynamical molecular variable when the paired wobble weights are symmetric.  It does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, adaptation, or a universal biological rule.  Each of those stronger interpretations would require its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled finite statistic.  In particular, rank-saturated surfaces, three-row all-perfect fits, identifier routing, and modeled tAI or stAI weights by themselves are not measured ribosome occupancy, protein output, or downstream biological realization.
