\subsection{The f3-stress strength driver as a finite cross-layer readout}
\label{subsec:bioreality-f3-stress-strength-driver}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress, after amino-acid projection has removed the part of codon usage explained by amino-acid composition. The finite surface is organism-by-codon data restricted to synonymous fibers paired by the $f_3$ operation, together with paired codon usage, modeled tAI or stAI wobble weights, and translation-efficiency or translation-proximal readouts when the row actually contains them. The point of the test is not that codon geometry alone is biological realization. The point is narrower: if the $f_3$-stress coordinate keeps explanatory power only when the paired wobble weights break the symmetry $w_c = w_{c^{f_3}}$, then the coordinate marks a codon-topology distinction that reaches a bounded translation-readout interface.

\paragraph{Coordinate construction.}
The base quotient contact is finite. The quotient geometry has $|\pi_M|=10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and matching $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. On the codon-usage side, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel contains nine coordinates: $K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. In the finite cross-organism codon-count contact, seven of the nine residual elements are nonzero above the stated variance threshold, so the nonzero fraction is $7/9=0.7777777777777778$. This makes the residual coordinate nontrivial as a finite codon-usage readout, while still leaving translation, protein, structure, function, and mechanism outside the codon-count data itself.

\paragraph{Rank-powered translation readout.}
The powered modeled-tAI panel uses $40$ organisms and a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ matrix. After residualizing against $24$ control columns, the control matrix has rank $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $17$. The residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$, and the modeled translation-readout regression reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X_{\mathrm{res}}) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\mathrm{df}_{\mathrm{res}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a rank-sufficient modeled tAI or stAI readout, not ribosome profiling and not protein output. The finite controls include organism, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and related codon-density baselines as encoded in the run. A three-organism all-saturated fit would not count as support; here the powered matrix is explicitly separated from the earlier $n=3$ bounded coupling condition.

\paragraph{Generality panel.}
The cross-organism generality run requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ usable organism rows, with at least $500$ proteins per organism. It used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, and the finite call was $f_3$-stress-generalizes with a real threshold of $8$. This establishes that the strength-driver test is not being read from a single organism or a rank-saturated three-row contact. It is still a finite panel, not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Strength driver statistic.}
The specific strength-driver run has status passed. It computed $15$ organisms out of $16$ requested, again with a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds. The total synonymous-selection strength is positively associated with the $f_3$-stress strength, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection}, f_3\text{-}\mathrm{strength})
&= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\mathrm{direction\ agreement} &= 15/15,\\
\mathrm{direction\ agreement\ rate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run also records the Sulfolobus case as a negative-alignment and direction check, rather than forcing every organism into the same positive-direction story. The finite interpretation is therefore: $f_3$-stress strength is explained by total synonymous-selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment on this panel. The deterministic run key is $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered:deterministic}$, which identifies the row-level computation rather than adding an independent biological observation.

\paragraph{Position-aware contact.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy contact supplies a useful boundary check. The joined ordered-CDS and occupancy surface contains $5754$ genes after length checking, with $5754$ gene identifiers in overlap and zero length mismatches. The position-control procedure uses file-provided codon-index windows and amino-acid bins, and the null shuffles synonymous labels only inside the same bin. In that run, all $9$ synonymous families have at least $200$ within-bin permutations, and the gate $C_H>0$ with $DL=2$ and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ is passed by $K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress. This contact supports a position-confound-controlled local occupancy separation for the listed families, but it does not convert the strength-driver statistic into causation.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The ready claim is a finite cross-layer relation: $f_3$-stress is not merely a residual codon-coordinate when wobble-paired weights are asymmetric and a rank-powered multi-organism readout survives the declared controls. On the present rows, the strength-driver statistic is positive, direction agreement is complete across the $15$ computed organisms, and the modeled translation-readout panel is rank-sufficient. The scientifically allowed sentence is therefore that $f_3$-stress behaves as a codon-topology-to-translation-readout distinction on these finite controlled panels.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
Nothing in this chapter asserts mature protein abundance, folding, structural order, localization, post-translational state, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, adaptation in a population-genetic sense, or a universal law. A measured translation-efficiency or ribosome-footprint claim requires its own joined rows; a protein-level claim requires matched protein data; a structure, physical, function, or phenotype claim requires a separate layer-matched contact and statistic. Symmetric wobble weights keep $f_3$-stress at the codon residual boundary, and rank-insufficient or saturated matrices remain null rather than supportive.
