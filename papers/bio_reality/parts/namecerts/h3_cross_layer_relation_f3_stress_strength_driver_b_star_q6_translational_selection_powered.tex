\subsection{The $f_3$-Stress Strength Driver as a Translation-Selection Readout}
\label{sec:biorreality-f3-stress-strength-driver}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record concerns a finite organism-by-codon surface restricted to synonymous codon fibers paired by the $f_3$ operation.  On each row the codon-usage vector is first reduced by amino-acid projection, so the active coordinate is the residual
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{f_3} = q - P_{\tau}q,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $P_{\tau}$ is the amino-acid quotient projection.  The quotient geometry is finite: the recorded $M$-side face vector is $(20,38,26,8,1)$, the quotient face vector is $(10,14,6,1)$, and the quotient image has $10$ listed cells.  The residual-basis computation used three organism codon-count contacts, namely human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli}, and found $9$ named $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  Seven of the nine exceeded the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9 = 0.7777777777777778$.  The coordinate list includes $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and the antisymmetric $f_3$-stress coordinate.

\paragraph{Wobble-broken translation surface.}
The finite test is not merely that $f_3$-paired codons differ in usage.  It asks whether the residual coordinate has translation-readout power when the paired codons also have asymmetric wobble weights.  The three-organism modeled-tAI bounded contact computed all $61$ sense-codon weights for human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} with zero fallback count $0$ in each organism.  All $81$ entries of the $9\times 9$ bounded coupling matrix exceeded threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$.  That three-organism surface is treated only as bounded coupling, not as powered survival, because $n=3$ is rank-degenerate for a controlled survival claim.

\paragraph{Rank-powered modeled translation-readout panel.}
The powered modeled-tAI panel enlarged the organism set to $40$ rows.  After residualizing against $24$ control columns, the control matrix had rank $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The residual coordinate matrix had rank $9$, matching the $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  The computed $S^{QT}$ matrix was $9\times 9$, and the controlled modeled translation-readout fit had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} = 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fitted surface is therefore powered for the modeled codon-adaptation readout, while remaining bounded to that readout class.  It is not a ribosome-footprint measurement, not proteomics, and not a statement about folded proteins.

\paragraph{Position-controlled ribosome occupancy contact.}
A same-codon-window occupancy analysis also produced a finite positive boundary for three residual families.  The ribosome-occupancy join matched $5754$ genes after the length check, from $5754$ occupancy genes and $6013$ ordered-CDS genes, with length mismatch count $0$. This gives a position-confound-controlled contact for local ribosome occupancy separation, but it is descriptive and does not by itself establish a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Cross-organism generality.}
The powered cross-organism generality computation requested $16$ organisms and tested $15$ organisms with at least $500$ proteins per organism.  It used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations.  The real threshold was $8$, and the finite call was $f_3$-stress generalizes.  This establishes that the coordinate is not confined to the initial three-organism codon-count surface; it remains visible on a larger organism panel under the stated rank and sampling constraints.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The strength-driver computation is the central finite-row result for this chapter.  It requested $16$ organisms, computed $15$ organisms, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $5$ folds with deterministic seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered:deterministic}.  The total synonymous-selection strength correlated with $f_3$-stress strength by
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}} = 0.7071428571428572.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direction agreement count was $15$ out of $15$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{agreement\ rate} = \frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite interpretation is that $f_3$-stress strength is explained by total synonymous-selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment.  The Sulfolobus row is explicitly marked as having negative alignment and direction, so the result is not a claim that every organism has the same raw sign.  The invariant feature is the agreement of direction after the declared alignment convention.

\paragraph{Biological reading.}
The chapter therefore supports a bounded cross-layer statement: in a rank-powered panel, the amino-acid-quotient residual $f_3$-stress coordinate behaves as a codon-topology distinction whose strength tracks total synonymous-selection strength and whose aligned direction is stable across the $15$ computed organisms.  The strongest finite numbers are $\rho=0.7071428571428572$ for the strength relation and $15/15$ direction agreement, against the background of a powered modeled translation-readout surface with $40$ organisms, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The result does not say that $f_3$-stress causes translation dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those claims require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are codon-adaptation readouts, not direct observations of ribosome motion; ribosome-occupancy separation is descriptive unless paired with perturbation or causal design; and no protein-structure, physical-assay, molecular-function, phenotype, or universal-law conclusion follows from these finite rows.  If the wobble weights are $f_3$-symmetric, or if the residual design is rank-saturated, the coordinate remains at the codon-residual boundary rather than becoming a translation-level biological variable.
