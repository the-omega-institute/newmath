\subsection{Rank-powered translational selection alignment of the $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:biorality-f3-stress-translational-selection-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate denoted $f_3$-stress, read on finite organism-by-codon surfaces after projection away from amino-acid identity. The geometric quotient used by the packet has $\pi_M$ size $10$, with quotient face vector $(10,14,6,1)$ and ambient $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. On the codon-usage side, the residual basis contains nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,\mathrm{vs}\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,\mathrm{vs}\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,\mathrm{vs}\,ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The preliminary finite contact over human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} found $7$ nonzero residual directions among $9$ entries, hence nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$ at variance threshold $0.01$. This establishes that the coordinate is not merely a name inside the quotient; it has finite codon-usage variation after the amino-acid projection. It does not yet say that the coordinate is translated, selected, mechanistic, or universal.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-readout rank.}
The larger modeled translation panel uses $40$ organisms and projects per-codon tAI readouts, computed from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights, onto the same nine residual coordinates. In that panel the controlled design has $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residualized coordinate rank $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$. The reported $S^{QT}$ surface is a $9\times 9$ matrix with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
Because the readout is modeled tAI, not ribosome-footprint TE, the number is a bounded translation-readout statistic rather than an observation of elongation speed, protein output, folding, localization, stability, phenotype, or function. Its role here is narrower: it makes the $f_3$-stress question rank-admissible on a multi-organism codon-adaptation surface, so that the strength-driver test is not being read from an $n=3$ saturated fit.

\paragraph{Generality gate.}
A separate cross-organism finite test requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms meeting the stated protein-count condition, with at least $500$ proteins per organism. The procedure used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, with real threshold $8$, and returned the call
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{generality\_call}=\mathrm{f3\_stress\_generalizes}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This gate is important because the strength-driver claim should not be inferred from one organism or from a contact that can be absorbed by simpler codon-composition baselines. The finite-row content is still descriptive: it says that the $f_3$-stress coordinate keeps recurring on the tested panel, not that the same sign or magnitude is a law of biology.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The direct strength-driver row has experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered} and run id \texttt{a6b023b665b4}. It requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms, again with minimum $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds. The principal finite numbers are
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection},\ f_3\mathrm{\ strength}) &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\frac{\mathrm{direction\ agreements}}{\mathrm{direction\ denominator}} &= \frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row also records \texttt{sulfolobus\_negative\_alignment\_and\_direction = true}. Thus the finite panel supports the following bounded reading: $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous-selection strength with positive rank association, and the tested organisms agree in the fitted direction after the packet's alignment convention is applied. The Sulfolobus flag is not an exception to be hidden; it records that a negative-alignment case was still handled by the direction-aware convention used by the finite statistic.

\paragraph{Local ribosome-occupancy contact.}
The position-controlled ribosome-occupancy analysis supplies a compatible but narrower contact. It joined $5754$ genes after length checking, with $5754$ overlapping gene ids, $6013$ ordered-CDS genes, and zero length mismatches. The null used within-position-bin permutation, with $9$ computed families and $9$ families having at least $200$ permutations. Under the stated description-length and direction gate,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{DL} &= 0.01,\\
DL &= 2,\\
C_H &> 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress. This supports a same-layer occupancy separation statement for the supplied windows; it does not turn the strength-driver statistic into a causal elongation mechanism.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
Within the finite evidence supplied here, the $f_3$-stress coordinate is best read as a codon-topological residual whose cross-organism strength is explained by total synonymous-selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment. The claim is positive at the bounded cross-layer relation level because the powered row reports status \texttt{passed}, $15$ computed organisms, Spearman association $0.7071428571428572$, and full direction agreement $15/15$. The result is not a claim that $f_3$-stress alone determines translation efficiency. It is a claim that, when the amino-acid quotient has been taken, when rank is sufficient, and when wobble-sensitive translation-readout surfaces break the relevant symmetry, the $f_3$-stress coordinate carries selection-aligned information that survives as a finite cross-layer relation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No statement in this subsection promotes codon geometry, modeled tAI, stAI-style wobble weights, or the $S^{QT}$ notation to protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. A measured translation claim would require same-organism or same-CDS rows joining codon counts, a direct ribosome-profiling or TE readout, matched mRNA abundance, and the declared controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, rank, and simpler baselines. A protein, structure, localization, stability, function, phenotype, or physical-admissibility claim would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
