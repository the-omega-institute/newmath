\paragraph{Finite surface.}

$$
\begin{aligned}
q_{\mathrm{res}} &= q - P_{\tau}q,\\
f_3\text{-stress} &\in q_{\mathrm{res}} .
\end{aligned}
$$
The quotient geometry used by this record has $|\pi_M|=10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and ambient $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$.
