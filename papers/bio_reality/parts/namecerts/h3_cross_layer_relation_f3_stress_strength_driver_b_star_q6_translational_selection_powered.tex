\subsection{The $f_3$-stress strength driver as a bounded translation-selection record}
\label{sec:bior-reality-f3-stress-strength-driver}

\paragraph{Finite object.}
The record studies the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress on a finite organism panel.  The coordinate is not a raw amino-acid composition feature: it is the residual component that remains after the amino-acid projection has been removed, so a codon-usage vector $q$ is read through the quotient residual $q-P_{\tau}q$.  In the underlying quotient geometry, the finite $M$ surface has face vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$, the quotient has face vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and the quotient map has $10$ listed rows.  The codon-residual readout used here contains $9$ named coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f_3$-stress.  In the initial three-organism contact, these residuals were computed from human, yeast, and \textit{E. coli} codon-count tables; $7$ of the $9$ residual coordinates were nonzero at the declared variance threshold, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.

\paragraph{Driver statistic.}
The powered strength-driver experiment asks whether the absolute strength of the $f_3$-stress coordinate is explained by the total synonymous-selection strength together with a fixed direction of alignment.  The finite run requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms, with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  It returned status passed.  The rank statistic reported a Spearman association
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho(\text{total synonymous selection},\ |f_3\text{-stress}|) &= 0.7071428571428572,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the direction check gave agreement count $15$ out of denominator $15$, hence direction-agreement rate $1.0$.  The same finite record flags the \textit{Sulfolobus} case as having negative alignment while still contributing to the declared direction accounting.  The scientific reading is therefore narrow: across this finite powered panel, stronger total synonymous selection co-varies with stronger $f_3$-stress magnitude, and the sign convention is stable across all computed organisms in the panel.

\paragraph{Translation-layer admissibility.}
The strength-driver statistic is interpreted only after the coordinate has a bounded translation-readout route.  A larger modeled-tAI run used $40$ organisms and the same $9$ residual coordinates, with a control design containing $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residualized coordinate rank $9$.  That run reported $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ for the modeled tAI readout surface and explicitly treated the result as modeled translation-readout survival rather than measured ribosome occupancy or protein output.  The earlier three-organism modeled-tAI contact had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$, but it also marked $3$ organisms as insufficient for a powered survival claim.  This distinction is important: bounded codon-adaptation weights can expose a translation-proximal readout surface, while a rank-saturated or too-small matrix cannot by itself support the strength-driver conclusion.

\paragraph{Cross-organism support.}
A companion finite generality run tested $15$ real organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds and at least $500$ proteins per organism.  It used $120$ permutations and a real threshold of $8$, and its call was $f_3$-stress generalizes.  This does not say that every organism obeys a universal law; it says that within the declared finite panel, the $f_3$-stress coordinate was not a one-organism artifact.  The strength-driver run refines that observation by replacing the binary generality call with a quantitative relation between total synonymous-selection strength and $f_3$-stress magnitude.

\paragraph{Ribosome-occupancy contact.}
A separate position-controlled occupancy record supplies an additional bounded contact at the translation interface.  The ordered-CDS and ribosome-occupancy join had $5754$ overlapping genes, $5754$ genes after the length check, $6013$ ordered-CDS genes before the join, and no length mismatches.  The null computation covered $9$ families, with all $9$ families having at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations requested.  The stated gate used description length $DL=2$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $C_H>0$; the families passing all gates were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f_3$-stress.  This contact is still descriptive and position-confound-controlled rather than causal: synonymous labels were shuffled only within the same bin, and the occupancy effect was demeaned within the supplied window or codon-index bin.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite result supports the following chapter-local claim.  When the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress residual is evaluated on a rank-powered multi-organism panel with wobble-weight asymmetry available at the translation-readout surface, its strength is positively associated with total synonymous-selection strength, with observed Spearman coefficient $0.7071428571428572$ and full direction agreement $15/15$.  Together with the powered modeled-tAI surface and the position-controlled occupancy contact, this makes $f_3$-stress a bounded codon-topology-to-translation distinction in the finite records used here.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion is licensed by this chapter.  The statistics above do not establish protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, a biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Translation itself is only claimed at the explicitly bounded readout level: modeled tAI or stAI weights, and the separately joined ribosome-occupancy contact where the finite rows actually exist.  If wobble weights were symmetric across the $f_3$-paired codons, or if the matrix rank collapsed to an all-fit surface such as an $n=3$ saturated design, the same residual coordinate would remain a codon-usage boundary rather than a translation-selection record.
