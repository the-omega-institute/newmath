\subsection{Powered selection strength for the $f_3$-stress coordinate}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-strength-driver}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record isolates the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate from the amino-acid quotient and asks whether its observed cross-organism strength is better read as a finite signature of synonymous translational selection than as an arbitrary codon-count fluctuation. The carrier is a finite organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous codon fibers paired by the $f_3$ operation. The coordinate is formed only after the amino-acid projection has been removed, so the working residual is $q - P_\tau q$ rather than a raw amino-acid composition effect. The intended test is not a claim about protein abundance, folding, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law. It is narrower: when wobble-weight asymmetry and matched translation-facing readouts are available, does the signed $f_3$ residual align with total synonymous selection strength across a powered organism panel?

\paragraph{Finite coordinate background.}
The quotient geometry used by this chapter has finite combinatorial size. The verified quotient has $\pi_M$ size $10$, with quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$ and ambient $M$ $f$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. In the organism-level residual-basis contact, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual list has $9$ coordinates:

On the three-organism codon-count contact, $7$ of these $9$ residuals are nonzero above the stated variance threshold, giving nonzero fraction $0.7777777777777778$. This establishes that the $f_3$ row is not introduced as a free biological variable; it is one coordinate in a finite synonymous-residual basis after the amino-acid quotient has already been taken.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The finite-row result for the present claim has run id \texttt{a6b023b665b4}. The computation requested $16$ organisms and produced the declared statistic on $15$ organisms, with a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds. The status is \texttt{passed}. The scalar association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\text{total synonymous selection},\ f_3\text{-stress strength}) &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\text{direction agreement} &= 15/15,\\
\text{direction agreement rate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row flagged as the Sulfolobus negative-alignment case is retained as a direction-sensitive case rather than discarded: the recorded diagnostic says that its negative alignment and direction are both present. Thus the finite claim is not merely that large residuals exist, but that the strength of the $f_3$ residual is organized by total synonymous selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment across the computed organisms.

\paragraph{Relation to cross-organism generality.}
A companion powered generality computation requested $16$ organisms, tested $15$, used $5$ folds, used $120$ permutations, and set a real threshold of $8$. Its call was $f_3$-stress generalizes. The present statistic sharpens that call: the generality result says that the coordinate is not confined to one organism-specific contact, while the strength-driver result says that the magnitude ordering is associated with a total synonymous-selection scale. Together they support a cross-layer reading only at the bounded translation-readout level: $f_3$-stress behaves as a codon-topology residual whose strength is selection-weighted when the panel is sufficiently ranked.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter-level statement is therefore the following. On the finite powered panel, the $f_3$-stress residual is a legitimate cross-layer candidate because its strength tracks total synonymous selection with Spearman correlation $0.7071428571428572$ and perfect recorded direction agreement, $15/15$. This does not turn the coordinate into a direct dynamical cause, nor does it identify a universal sign law. It says that after amino-acid quotienting, the codon-paired residual has enough rank and enough organism-level alignment to be read as a translational-selection-powered residual distinction, provided the readout remains restricted to codon usage, wobble-weight or modeled tAI/stAI surfaces, and explicitly bounded translation-facing statistics.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion follows from these rows alone. In particular, this chapter does not claim measured ribosome occupancy unless a same-row ribosome or translation-efficiency contact supplies it; it does not claim protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality. The positive evidence is the finite association between $f_3$-stress strength and synonymous selection strength on a rank-powered panel. Any step beyond that bounded readout requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
