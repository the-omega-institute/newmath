\subsection{The $f_3$-stress strength driver as a translation-selection readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-strength-driver}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is the finite organism-by-codon surface obtained after projecting synonymous codon usage away from amino-acid composition and retaining the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The distinguished coordinate is the antisymmetric $f_3$-stress component: it compares codons paired by the $f_3$ involution only after the amino-acid quotient has removed the ordinary residue-class explanation.  The quotient-side check gives a concrete finite shape.  The $M_f$ vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_f=(20,38,26,8,1),
\end{aligned}
$$
while the quotient vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
(10,14,6,1),
\end{aligned}
$$
and the projected $\pi_M$ support has size $10$.  On the three-organism codon-count contact, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual list has nine named coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $7$ of $9$ coordinates nonzero above the stated residual-variance threshold, i.e. a nonzero fraction of $0.7777777777777778$.  These numbers do not by themselves assert translation.  They only establish that the codon-residual coordinate is not collapsed by the amino-acid quotient on the finite contact surface.

\paragraph{Readout gate.}
The translation-side route is explicitly separated from the codon geometry.  In the powered modeled-tAI panel, $40$ organisms were loaded, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual matrix had rank $9$, the control block had rank $23$, and the remaining residual degrees of freedom after controls were $17$.  The modeled $S^{QT}$ surface had shape $9\times 9$ and total $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$ under the organism, amino-acid, GC3, length, and density controls used in that run.  The earlier three-organism modeled-tAI surface is kept as bounded coupling rather than powered survival: it had $81$ entries above the $0.01$ coupling threshold and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but only $3$ organisms, so it is not used as a powered survival claim.  This distinction matters because an all-fitting or rank-saturated matrix would be a null diagnostic rather than biological evidence.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The strength-driver run asks a narrower question: when $f_3$-stress is already visible as a residual coordinate, is its cross-organism strength explained by the total synonymous selection strength together with a fixed-direction alignment?  The finite powered run requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms meeting the row criteria, with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  Its deterministic seed was $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The reported Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho=0.7071428571428572.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direction agreement was complete on the computed panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
15/15=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row also records that the Sulfolobus case has negative alignment and direction, so the result is not phrased as a universal positive direction.  The valid readback is instead directional coherence after the sign convention is fixed: the strength of the $f_3$-stress coordinate tracks total synonymous selection strength, and the direction rule agrees across all $15$ computed organisms.

\paragraph{Generalization and local ribosome contact.}
A separate cross-organism generality run tested $15$ real organisms out of $16$ requested, again with $5$ folds and at least $500$ proteins per organism; its threshold was $8$, and its call was $f_3$-stress generalizes.  A position-controlled ribosome-occupancy contact gives a local translation-adjacent check without turning the driver statistic into a mechanism: $5754$ occupancy genes were joined to ordered coding sequences after the length check, the permutation request was $200$ within position bins, all $9$ families reached at least $200$ permutations, and the families passing the $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and $C_H>0$ gate were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.  This supports the claim that the coordinate can be read against translation-adjacent finite rows, but it remains descriptive and position-confound-controlled.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The ready statement is therefore a finite cross-layer relation, not a global biological law.  Within the provided rows, $f_3$-stress is a codon-topological residual that becomes translation-relevant only when the readout surface has enough rank and the wobble or ribosome-contact layer breaks the paired symmetry.  The strength-driver row supplies the core numerical claim: $15$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, $500$-protein minimum, $\rho=0.7071428571428572$, and $15/15$ direction agreement.  The chapter-level interpretation is that total synonymous selection strength, together with fixed-direction alignment, explains the observed cross-organism strength of the $f_3$-stress coordinate on this finite panel.

\paragraph{Boundary of the claim.}
No translation mechanism, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universal rule follows from these rows alone.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded translation-readout annotations, not observed ribosome dynamics.  Ribosome-occupancy rows, where present, are a separate measured contact and still do not imply folding, function, or causation.  The only positive conclusion made here is the finite, rank-aware association between $f_3$-stress strength and total synonymous selection strength with complete direction agreement on the computed organism panel.
