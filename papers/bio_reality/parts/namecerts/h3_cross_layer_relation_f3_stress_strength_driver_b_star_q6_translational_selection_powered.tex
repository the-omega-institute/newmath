\subsection{A strength driver for the $f_3$-stress translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-strength-driver}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record asks whether the $f_3$-stress coordinate is merely a residual codon-usage direction or whether, under an explicitly bounded translation readout, its observed strength is organized by synonymous selection.  The finite surface is organism by codon readout data restricted to $f_3$-paired synonymous fibers.  The coordinate is formed after amino-acid projection as the residual component $q-P_{\tau}q$, and it is interpreted only where a codon-paired wobble-weight surface breaks the symmetry $w_c=w_{c^{f_3}}$.  The tested claim is not that $f_3$-stress causes translation or protein-level behavior.  It is the narrower statement that, in a rank-powered multi-organism panel, the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress translation-readout entry is predicted by total synonymous selection strength together with a fixed direction of alignment.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate background.}
The ambient quotient check gives a concrete finite geometry.  The reported $M_f$ vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_f&=(20,38,26,8,1),
\end{aligned}
$$
while the amino-acid quotient has $f$-vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{\mathrm{quot}}&=(10,14,6,1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The quotient projection has $10$ listed rows.  On the organism-level codon-count contact, the residual-basis computation used three organisms, namely human, budding yeast, and $E.\ coli$, and found $9$ named $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  Seven of the nine coordinates were nonzero above the stated variance threshold, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  The coordinate list used in the readout layer is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These rows establish that the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a nontrivial finite residual direction after amino-acid quotienting, but they do not by themselves establish any translation, ribosome, protein, structure, or functional claim.

\paragraph{Rank-powered translation-readout setting.}
The rank-powered modeled translation-readout computation used $40$ organisms and the same $9$ residual coordinates as a $9\times 9$ modeled tAI readout matrix.  After residualizing controls, the control block had $24$ columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$; the residual coordinate block had rank $9$.  The total reported $Q\to T$ fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT}&=0.9003849000036579,
\end{aligned}
$$
with positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$ and degenerate-fit cutoff $0.999999$.  This is the finite-row reason the present record is not the refused $n=3$ saturated case.  The bounded three-organism modeled-tAI contact did produce $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and a maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but that contact had only $3$ organisms and was explicitly marked underpowered for survival.  The powered result therefore depends on the $40$-organism rank condition, not on all-perfect residual fits in a rank-saturated small matrix.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The strength-driver run is the decisive finite-row entry for this chapter.  It requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms, with at least $500$ proteins required per organism and $5$ folds used in the procedure.  Its status is passed.  Across the $15$ computed organisms, the direction agreement count was $15$ out of $15$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{direction\ agreement\ rate}&=\frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was reported as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}&=0.7071428571428572.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded interpretation is that $f_3$-stress strength is accounted for by total synonymous selection strength multiplied by a fixed-direction alignment.  A further finite diagnostic records the Sulfolobus case with negative alignment and direction marked true, which means the panel is not being summarized as a naive universal positive sign.  The statistic is direction-aware: the magnitude of the coordinate is tied to selection strength only after the sign convention has been controlled by the observed alignment.

\paragraph{Cross-organism generality and local ribosome contact.}
A separate generality run supports the same boundary from another finite panel.  It requested $16$ organisms, tested $15$, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, used $120$ permutations, and set the real threshold at $8$.  The reported generality call was $f_3$-stress generalizes.  This supplies a cross-organism stability check for the coordinate, not a universal law.

The ribosome-occupancy contact is more local and has a different evidential role.  It joined $5754$ ordered CDS genes to occupancy rows, with $5754$ overlapping identifiers and no length mismatches after the length check.  Within position bins, $9$ synonymous families were computed and all $9$ had at least $200$ permutations for the within-bin null.  The position-controlled gate used description length $DL=2$, penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and a positive coordinate-support cosine rule.  The families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This supports the plausibility of a translation-layer contact for the coordinate under position-bin controls, but it remains a descriptive occupancy separation and not a causal proof.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The finite rows justify a bounded cross-layer classification: the $f_3$-stress coordinate survives as a codon-topology-to-translation-readout distinction when the panel is rank-powered and when wobble-weight symmetry is broken.  The strength-driver result adds that the size of the $f_3$-stress entry is organized by total synonymous selection strength, with perfect finite-panel direction agreement in the $15$ computed organisms and Spearman association $0.7071428571428572$.  In compact form, the supported relation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{strength}(f_3\text{-stress})
&\approx
\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection\ strength}\cdot
\mathrm{fixed\ direction\ alignment},
\end{aligned}
$$
where the approximation denotes the reported finite-row association, not an asserted mechanistic equation.

The boundary is strict.  No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, post-translational state, localization, stability, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  Matched mRNA abundance is only a control route for separating translation efficiency from transcript abundance.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are bounded adaptation readouts, not observed ribosome dynamics.  Ribosome profiling or occupancy rows can test translation-layer behavior only when joined at the organism or CDS level with the declared covariates.  Any higher-layer claim requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
Within the finite data supplied here, the claim is ready at the cross-layer translation-readout boundary.  The result uses a nontrivial post-amino-acid residual basis, a rank-powered $40$-organism modeled tAI surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, a $15$-organism strength-driver statistic with $15/15$ direction agreement, and a Spearman association of $0.7071428571428572$ between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength.  The correct reading is therefore: $f_3$-stress is a finite, rank-powered codon-topology coordinate whose translation-readout strength tracks synonymous selection under wobble-symmetry breaking.  The correct non-reading is equally important: these data do not promote the coordinate to protein realization, structure, physical admissibility, mechanism, function, phenotype, or universality.
