\subsection{The $f_3$-stress strength driver as a translation-bounded cross-layer record}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-strength-driver}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate named $f_3$-stress, restricted to a finite organism-by-codon surface on which amino-acid composition has already been quotiented out.  The relevant codon geometry is not a free biological variable: it is the residual coordinate left after projecting codon usage against the amino-acid partition.  In the finite quotient computation, the $M_f$ vector is
$$
\begin{aligned}
M_f &= (20,38,26,8,1),
\end{aligned}
$$
with quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$ and $|\pi_M|=10$.  Thus the object being tested is a small codon-topological residual surface, not a genome-wide assertion about translation or protein behavior.

The residual-basis computation used $3$ organism-level codon-count contacts, namely human, yeast, and E. coli K-12, and produced $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Seven of the nine coordinates were nonzero above the finite variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  This establishes that the coordinate is not an empty naming artifact on the finite codon tables, while still leaving open whether any translation-layer readout survives the required controls.

\paragraph{Translation-bounded admissibility.}
The readout route is translation-bounded because it uses codon-paired wobble-weight or translation-efficiency surfaces only after separating them from the residual coordinate itself.  On the modeled tAI route, the $3$-organism bounded matrix had $61$ sense codons and nonzero dos Reis wobble-weight readouts for human, yeast, and E. coli K-12, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each organism.  The bounded coupling matrix had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but this $3$-organism surface was explicitly underpowered for a survival claim.

The powered modeled-readout surface expanded to $40$ organisms.  After residualizing controls, the design had $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$.  The modeled translation-readout matrix had shape $9\times 9$ and total controlled $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  These figures are sufficient to treat the modeled tAI surface as a rank-powered translation-readout screen, but they still do not make tAI an observed ribosome-footprint measurement or a protein-output assay.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The present claim asks whether the strength of the $f_3$-stress signal is explained by total synonymous selection strength together with a stable direction alignment.  The finite powered computation requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms with at least $500$ proteins per organism, using $5$ folds and deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered{:}deterministic}$.  The core scalar result is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection},\ |f_3\text{-stress}|) &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\frac{\mathrm{direction\ agreements}}{\mathrm{tested\ organisms}} &= \frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation therefore reads the observed $f_3$-stress strength as aligned with total synonymous selection strength on this finite panel.  The special Sulfolobus diagnostic was negative in alignment while preserving the recorded direction test, so it functions as a signed boundary case rather than as evidence for a universal positive direction.

\paragraph{Cross-organism support.}
A separate powered generality computation tested $15$ real organisms after requesting $16$, again with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  Its finite decision rule used $120$ permutations and a real threshold of $8$, and the call was $f_3$-stress generalizes.  This supports the use of a multi-organism panel rather than a saturated $n=3$ fit.  The distinction matters because all-$1.0$ residual fits at $n=3$ are rank-saturated and are not counted here as positive survival.

A position-aware ribosome-occupancy contact also supplies a bounded translation-proximal check.  It joined $5754$ ordered-CDS genes with $5754$ occupancy rows after length checking, with $6013$ ordered-CDS genes before the overlap cut and length mismatch count $0$.  The within-position-bin permutation null used $200$ requested permutations, and all $9$ computed families had at least $200$ permutations.  Under the local rule $C_H>0$, description length $DL=2$, and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This does not prove a mechanism, but it gives an independently bounded translation-proximal consistency check for the $f_3$-stress coordinate.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  On the finite powered rows, $f_3$-stress is a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation distinction when two requirements are met: first, the codon-paired wobble or translation-readout surface breaks the $f_3$ symmetry; second, the same coordinate survives rank and control checks on a non-saturated organism panel.  The strength-driver result satisfies this bounded condition through $15$ computed organisms, $15/15$ direction agreement, and Spearman association $0.7071428571428572$ with total synonymous selection strength.

No stronger biological conclusion follows from these rows alone.  The record does not assert protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal law.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights are codon-adaptation readouts, not direct ribosome dynamics.  Ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency would need its own same-row joined contact, and any protein, structure, physical, functional, or phenotype claim would need a separate layer-matched measurement and statistic.  If codon-paired wobble weights are symmetric, or if the rank collapses to a saturated fit, the $f_3$-stress coordinate remains a bounded codon residual rather than a translation-level distinction.
