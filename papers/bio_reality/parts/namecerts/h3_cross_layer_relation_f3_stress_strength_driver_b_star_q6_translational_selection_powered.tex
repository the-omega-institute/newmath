\subsection{Translational-selection strength for the $f_3$-stress residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-translational-selection-strength}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The empirical object in this record is a finite organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous codon fibres carrying the $f_3$ pairing operation.  The coordinate under test is not raw codon usage.  For each organism the codon-use vector is first compared only after amino-acid projection has been removed, so the working signal is the residual component $q-P_{\tau}q$.  The distinguished coordinate is the antisymmetric $f_3$-stress entry inside the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual family.  In the finite residual-basis run, nine coordinates were computed,
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\; Arg\_AGR,\; Ile\_AUA,\; Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\; Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\; Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\; Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\; f3\_stress\_pair\_antisymmetric.
\end{aligned}
$$
The amino-acid quotient did not erase the residual surface: $7$ of $9$ entries were nonzero above the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $0.7777777777777778$.  The lower quotient geometry is also finite: the $M_f$ vector was $(20,38,26,8,1)$, the quotient $f$-vector was $(10,14,6,1)$, and $|\pi_M|=10$.  These numbers fix the coordinate as a bounded codon-topology residual rather than as a free biological variable.

\paragraph{Translation-readout gate.}
The selection-strength claim is allowed to speak about a translation-facing readout only because a rank-powered modeled translation panel is available.  In the $40$-organism modeled-tAI run, the controlled design had $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and a computed $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  The readout columns matched the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates, including $f3\_stress$.  By contrast, the earlier $3$-organism modeled-tAI contact was explicitly underpowered for survival: it had organism count $3$, all $81$ bounded-coupling entries above threshold $0.01$, and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but the recorded break condition says that powered survival needs at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the present record uses the $40$-organism rank-powered surface as the admissible readout context and treats the $3$-organism surface only as bounded coupling.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The finite row for translational-selection strength computed $15$ real organisms out of $16$ requested, with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  The measured association between total synonymous-selection strength and the $f_3$-stress strength was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection},\; |f_3\text{-stress}|)=0.7071428571428572.
\end{aligned}
$$
Direction agreement was complete on the computed panel: $15$ agreements out of denominator $15$, hence direction-agreement rate $1.0$.  The stored interpretation is that $f_3$-stress strength is explained by total synonymous-selection strength multiplied by fixed-direction alignment.  The Sulfolobus row is not hidden as an exception: the computation records negative alignment together with its direction flag, so the direction statement is finite-row and sign-aware rather than a universal positive-effect assertion.

\paragraph{Cross-organism support.}
The companion generality run tested $15$ real organisms out of $16$ requested, again with at least $500$ proteins per organism, $5$ folds, and $120$ permutations.  Its threshold for a real call was $8$, and the resulting classification was $f3\_stress\_generalizes$.  This supports the use of a multi-organism panel rather than a single species anecdote.  A separate position-controlled ribosome-occupancy analysis gives a local translation-contact check: $5754$ genes joined between ordered CDS and occupancy rows, with zero length mismatches, $200$ within-position-bin permutations requested for each of $9$ families, and all $9$ families having at least $200$ permutations.  Under the gate $C_H>0$ and $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ with $DL=2$, the families passing all local occupancy gates were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.  This does not replace the cross-organism strength statistic, but it confirms that the same residual coordinate can be read at a translation-proximal contact when position bins and synonymous-label permutations are enforced.

\paragraph{Conclusion and local boundary.}
The finite evidence supports the following bounded claim.  In a rank-powered multi-organism setting, the $f_3$-stress residual is not merely a codon-count leftover after amino-acid projection: its strength tracks total synonymous-selection strength with Spearman coefficient $0.7071428571428572$, and its direction is aligned in $15/15$ computed organisms.  Together with the $40$-organism modeled translation surface and the position-controlled occupancy contact, this makes $f_3$-stress a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation distinction in the stated finite sense.

No stronger biological conclusion follows from these rows alone.  The chapter does not infer protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, causation, or a universal biological law.  Those layers require their own same-row empirical contacts and their own controlled statistics.  If wobble weights are symmetric under $f_3$, if the residual rank is saturated, or if the only available fit is the $n=3$ all-ones type of surface, the correct reading is null or needs-data rather than positive translation survival.
