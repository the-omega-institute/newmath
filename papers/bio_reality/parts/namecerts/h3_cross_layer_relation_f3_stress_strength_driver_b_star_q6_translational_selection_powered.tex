\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite carrier for this record is the organism-by-codon readout table restricted to synonymous codon fibers in which the third-base involution $f_3$ can be compared after amino-acid projection.  The coordinate of interest is not raw codon usage.  It is the residual component obtained after removing the amino-acid quotient contribution, so that the recorded signal is the part of the codon-use vector that remains visible on paired synonymous fibers.  In the finite quotient geometry used by this record, the $M$-face vector is $(20,38,26,8,1)$, while the quotient face vector is $(10,14,6,1)$ and the quotient support has size $10$.  Thus the row object is explicitly smaller than the full codon table: it keeps a bounded codon-topology residual rather than the full biological state of the gene or organism.

\paragraph{Residual basis and row count.}
The residual-basis computation supplies nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In the three-organism codon-count contact for human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12, seven of the nine residual coordinates are nonzero above the stated variance threshold, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  This establishes that the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a nontrivial finite residual coordinate, but it does not yet make it a translation, protein, structure, function, or mechanism variable.

\paragraph{Powered selection-strength statistic.}
The strength-driver computation is the powered row that decides this chapter.  It used a deterministic seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered{:}deterministic}$, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, requested $16$ organisms, and computed $15$ organisms.  The recorded statistic compares total synonymous-selection strength against the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress effect.  The finite result is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{computed}} &= 15,\\
 n_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16,\\
 \rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection},\ |f_3\mathrm{-stress}|) &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
 \mathrm{direction\ agreement} &= 15/15 = 1.0,\\
 \mathrm{folds} &= 5.
\end{aligned}
$$
The interpretation is therefore finite and specific: within the computed panel, the strength of the $f_3$-stress coordinate is explained by total synonymous-selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment.  The Sulfolobus row is explicitly recorded with negative alignment and direction, so the statement is not that every organism shares the same signed biological effect.  The positive finite claim is that the strength driver survives as an ordered, direction-aware selection readout on the computed panel.

\paragraph{Cross-organism support.}
A separate powered generality computation requested $16$ organisms, tested $15$, used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, and returned the call $f_3\mathrm{-stress\ generalizes}$ with real threshold $8$.  This supports the same finite direction: the coordinate is not merely a three-organism artifact of the residual basis.  The row remains bounded, however.  Generalization here means that the finite codon-residual statistic reproduces across the tested organism panel under the declared design.  It is not a universal law over all organisms and not a causal statement about translation dynamics.

\paragraph{Relation to modeled translation readouts.}
The powered modeled-tAI matrix supplies a compatible translation-layer readout surface. The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ readout has nine columns, matching the nine residual coordinates, and the reported controlled matrix has shape $9\times 9$.  The powered rank is sufficient with $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the recorded degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$.  These numbers make the modeled codon-adaptation readout a useful bounded translation-facing surface.  They do not turn modeled tAI into measured ribosome motion, measured elongation speed, proteomics, folding, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Position-resolved occupancy contact.}
The ribosome-occupancy row gives an additional finite contact with position control.  The ordered coding-sequence join contains $5754$ overlapping genes after length checks, with $6013$ ordered-CDS genes available and zero length mismatches.  The position-control method demeans occupancy within supplied window and codon-index bins, and the within-bin label permutation requested $200$ permutations for each family; all nine families had at least $200$ permutations.  The description-length gate used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and $C_H>0$, and the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This is a position-confound-controlled occupancy separation, not a causal proof of dwell-time mechanism.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is ready for the stated chapter-level claim.  The powered strength-driver row says that, on the computed fifteen-organism panel, $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous-selection strength with Spearman value $0.7071428571428572$ and complete finite direction agreement $15/15$.  Together with the nontrivial amino-acid-quotient residual basis, the forty-organism modeled-tAI rank check, the cross-organism generality call, and the position-controlled occupancy separation in which $f_3$-stress passes the $\lambda_{DL}$ and $C_H$ gates, this supports a bounded cross-layer relation from codon-topology residual strength to translation-facing selection readouts.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The claim stops at that bounded relation.  No translation realization beyond the declared readout surfaces follows from the residual coordinate alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, post-translational state, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation law, or universal biological rule follows from the numbers above.  Such claims require their own same-row contact and their own controlled statistic.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are codon-adaptation weights, not observed ribosome trajectories; ribosome occupancy is a translation-proximal measurement, not protein abundance or structure; and an $f_3$-stress residual is a codon-topology coordinate until a layer-matched readout explicitly carries it.
