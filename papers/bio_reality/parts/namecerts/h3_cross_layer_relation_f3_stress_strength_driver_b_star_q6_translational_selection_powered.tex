\subsection{A powered finite-row readout for the $f_3$-stress strength driver}
\label{sec:bior_reality-f3-stress-strength-driver-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and coordinate.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-codon readout surface on the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, restricted to codon fibers in which the $f_3$-paired codons can be compared after amino-acid projection. The coordinate of interest is the residual $f_3$-stress component obtained after removing the amino-acid component from the codon-usage vector. In the finite quotient geometry supporting this readout, the upstream quotient check has $\pi_M$ size $10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and ambient $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. The residual-basis computation loaded three organism-level codon-count contacts, namely human, budding yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12, and computed nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\; Arg\_AGR,\; Ile\_AUA,\; Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\; Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\; Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\; Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\; f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Seven of the nine residual coordinates were nonzero above the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$. This establishes the coordinate as a finite codon-usage residual rather than as an inferred protein, structure, or phenotype variable.

\paragraph{Powered translation-readout panel.}
The powered modeled-translation readout used $40$ organism rows, nine residual coordinates, and nine modeled per-codon tAI readout columns derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights. The residualized control design had $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after controls. The residual coordinate block had rank $9$, so the powered matrix was not the three-row saturated surface that would make all fitted readouts vacuous. The finite statistic reported

\begin{aligned}

\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding bounded three-organism modeled-tAI contact also computed all $81$ matrix entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximal absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but that three-row surface remains underpowered for survival because $n=3$ cannot support the requested rank-powered interpretation. The present strength-driver record therefore uses the larger finite panel as the operative row set.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The powered $f_3$-stress strength-driver computation requested $16$ organisms and retained $15$ computed organisms satisfying the minimum size criterion of at least $500$ proteins per organism. It used $5$ folds and the deterministic seed
$\text{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered:deterministic}$.
The finite-row result is positive on the declared scope: total synonymous selection strength is monotonically associated with the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress coordinate, and the sign alignment is consistent across all retained organism rows. Numerically,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\text{total synonymous selection},\; |f_3\text{-stress}|) &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\frac{\text{direction agreements}}{\text{eligible organism rows}} &= \frac{15}{15},\\
\text{direction agreement rate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row labelled by the Sulfolobus diagnostic is explicitly marked as preserving the negative-alignment direction condition, so it is not silently removed to force a positive direction. The reported interpretation is therefore narrow: $f_3$-stress strength is explained in this finite panel by total synonymous selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment.

\paragraph{Cross-organism support.}
A companion powered generality computation gives the row-level context for the strength-driver statistic. It requested $16$ organisms, tested $15$, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and ran $120$ permutations. Its real threshold was $8$, and the finite call was $f_3$-stress generalizes. This does not by itself establish a universal biological law; it says that the retained finite organism panel is large enough to reject the three-row rank-saturation failure mode and to support the declared cross-organism readout on the available rows.

\paragraph{Mechanistic adjacency without promotion.}
The same coordinate appears in a position-controlled ribosome-occupancy analysis. That analysis joined $5754$ genes after length checking, from $6013$ ordered-CDS genes and $5754$ occupancy genes, with zero length mismatches. Its null was computed by within-position-bin synonymous-label permutation, with $200$ requested permutations and all nine families having at least $200$ permutation instances. The gate used $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, description length $DL=2$, and the directional rule $C_H>0$. The families passing all gates were
$$
\begin{aligned}
K\_AAA,\quad Arg\_AGR,\quad f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a useful layer-adjacent contact because it shows that the $f_3$-stress coordinate can survive a position-bin-controlled local occupancy readout in the same general codon-residual vocabulary. It is not, however, a proof of causation, a protein-output result, a folding result, or a claim about cellular fitness.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
Within the finite row set just described, the claim is ready at the level of a powered cross-layer relation between codon-topology residual strength and a bounded translation-selection readout. The operative evidence is the $15$-organism powered strength-driver statistic, the $15/15$ direction agreement, the Spearman value $0.7071428571428572$, the avoidance of the $n=3$ rank-saturation trap, and the separate rank-powered modeled-tAI survival surface with $40$ organisms and residual degrees of freedom $17$. The chapter does not assert that $f_3$-stress is a direct ribosome-dynamical variable in every organism, nor that it determines protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological rule. Those stronger statements would require separate same-row contacts at the corresponding layer, with their own controls and finite statistics.
