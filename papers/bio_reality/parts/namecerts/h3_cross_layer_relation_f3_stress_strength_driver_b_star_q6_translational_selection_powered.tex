\subsection{Translation-Selection Power for the $f_3$-Stress Coordinate}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-strength-driver}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress.  The finite carrier is an organism-by-codon readout table restricted to synonymous codon fibers after amino-acid projection, with paired codon usage, wobble-weight readbacks from tAI or stAI where available, and translation-proximal measurements when a matched readout exists.  The coordinate is not a raw codon-frequency feature: it is the residual component left after the amino-acid quotient projection $q \mapsto q-P_\tau q$.  On the finite quotient geometry used by the packet, the ambient $M$ face vector is recorded as
$$\begin{aligned}
  f(M) &= (20,38,26,8,1),\\
  f(M/\tau) &= (10,14,6,1),\\
  |\pi_M| &= 10.
\end{aligned}$$
The same finite geometry supplies nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In the three-organism codon-count contact used to establish nontriviality of the residual basis, seven of the nine coordinates exceeded the variance threshold $0.01$, so the observed nonzero fraction was $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  This is a codon-usage and residual-coordinate fact, not yet a translation fact.

\paragraph{Powered strength-driver statistic.}
The strength-driver run asks whether the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress signal across organisms is accounted for by total synonymous-selection strength together with a fixed directional alignment.  The powered panel requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms, with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds in the evaluation.  The recorded Spearman association between total synonymous-selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was
$$\begin{aligned}
  \rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}} &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
  n_{\mathrm{computed}} &= 15,\\
  n_{\mathrm{requested}} &= 16.
\end{aligned}$$
Direction agreement was complete on the computed panel:
$$\begin{aligned}
  \frac{\#\{\mathrm{direction\ agreements}\}}{\#\{\mathrm{eligible\ organisms}\}} &= \frac{15}{15},\\
  \mathrm{direction\ agreement\ rate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}$$
The finite-row interpretation is therefore that the measured $f_3$-stress strength is explained, on this panel, by total synonymous-selection strength multiplied by a common direction of alignment.  The Sulfolobus row is explicitly marked as having negative alignment together with its direction, so the result is not phrased as a universal positive-sign law.  It is a finite cross-organism alignment result with a recorded exception direction, not a claim that every lineage has the same signed biological response.

\paragraph{Relation to translation readouts.}
The same research object has a separate modeled-translation surface based on tAI readbacks.  On the powered modeled-tAI panel, $40$ organisms were loaded, the residual readout matrix had $9$ columns, the residualized control design used $24$ control columns with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $17$.  The residual coordinate design retained rank $9$, and the reported modeled translation-readout fit was
$$\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
  \operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
  \operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
  df_{\mathrm{residual}} &= 17.
\end{aligned}$$
This powered modeled surface is relevant because $f_3$-stress is only biologically interpretable as a cross-layer codon-topology-to-translation distinction when the wobble-weight surface breaks the $f_3$ pairing symmetry and the translation readout survives the stated controls.  A smaller three-organism modeled-tAI contact is deliberately weaker: it has $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but its own record states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  Thus the three-row matrix can show bounded coupling, while the powered panel is the one used for rank-sensitive survival language.

\paragraph{Position and dwell support.}
A separate ribosome-occupancy contact gives a position-controlled translation-proximal check rather than a protein-output claim.  In that run, ordered coding sequences and ribosome-occupancy rows were joined for $5754$ genes, with $5754$ passing the length check and no length mismatches.  The null used within-position-bin label permutations, requested $200$ permutations, and all $9$ families had at least $200$ permutations available.  The local description-length gate used
$$\begin{aligned}
  \lambda_{DL} &= 0.01,\\
  DL &= 2,\\
  C_H &> 0.
\end{aligned}$$
The families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This supports the narrower statement that $f_3$-stress can separate local ribosome-occupancy behavior after position-bin control in the joined yeast-scale occupancy surface.  It does not by itself identify a causal elongation mechanism, and it does not replace the need for organism-matched translation-efficiency or ribosome-footprint statistics when making a same-row $S^{QT}$ claim.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row evidence is sufficient for a chapter-level cross-layer relation: $f_3$-stress has a nontrivial amino-acid-quotient residual basis, participates in powered modeled translation-readout survival, passes a position-controlled ribosome-occupancy separation gate in the joined occupancy surface, generalizes across a $15$-organism panel, and has a powered strength-driver statistic with $\rho=0.7071428571428572$ and $15/15$ directional agreement.  The proper readback is therefore rank-powered translation-selection alignment for the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, conditional on wobble-weight asymmetry and rank sufficiency.  If codon-paired wobble weights are symmetric, or if the design is rank-saturated, the coordinate remains at the bounded codon-residual or modeled-readout boundary.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
No translation realization beyond the stated modeled or ribosome-occupancy readout follows from the coordinate alone.  No protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, localization, post-translational state, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation claim, causal law, or universal biological law is licensed by this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the controlled statistic is recomputed on that surface.  The numbers above support a finite cross-layer translation-selection relation for $f_3$-stress; they do not promote codon geometry into protein, structure, function, or organism-level biology.
