\subsection{Rank-powered translational selection driver for $f_3$-stress}
\label{sec:biorality-f3-stress-strength-driver-translational-selection}

\paragraph{Claim.}
The finite record tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate behaves as a cross-layer codon-topology distinction only after two finite requirements are met: the amino-acid quotient has left a nontrivial synonymous residual coordinate, and a translation-facing readout has enough rank to prevent a saturated fit from being mistaken for evidence. The focal result is not that $f_3$-stress is itself a universal biological variable. It is the narrower statement that, on the powered organism panel, the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress readout is aligned with total synonymous-selection strength and that this alignment has a consistent sign across the computed organisms.

\paragraph{Finite coordinate surface.}
The base quotient geometry is finite. The $M_f$ vector is $(20,38,26,8,1)$, the quotient $f$-vector is $(10,14,6,1)$, and the quotient surface has $|\pi_M|=10$ cells. On the organism-level residual contact, the $B^*_{Q6}$ construction yields nine synonymous residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. In the three-organism codon-count contact used to establish nontriviality, seven of these nine coordinates are nonzero above the declared variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$. This is a codon-usage and quotient fact, not a translation conclusion.

\paragraph{Rank condition.}
The powered modeled-translation panel supplies the rank check that the three-organism contact cannot supply. The modeled tAI panel contains $40$ organisms, a $9\times 9$ $S^{QT}$ surface, residualized control rank $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residual coordinate rank $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$. Its global modeled-readout fit is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579.
\end{aligned}
$$
The threshold for rejecting a degenerate all-fit surface is $0.999999$, so this panel is rank-powered rather than rank-saturated. The readout here is explicitly modeled per-codon tAI from tRNA copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights projected onto the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates. It is not a measured ribosome-footprint, mature protein abundance, folded structure, or functional assay.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The driver test computes the relation between total synonymous-selection strength and the $f_3$-stress strength over the powered panel. It requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms, with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds. The finite statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\text{total synonymous selection},\, f_3\text{-stress strength}) &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\frac{\text{direction agreements}}{\text{computed organisms}} &= \frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The record also marks the Sulfolobus negative-alignment case as present while preserving the direction agreement flag. The interpretation supported by these rows is therefore sign-aware: $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous-selection strength when multiplied by the fixed-direction alignment used by the finite statistic. The evidence is a powered cross-layer association, not a causal law.

\paragraph{Independent consistency checks.}
A companion generality test requested $16$ organisms, tested $15$, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $120$ permutations. Its finite call was $f_3$-stress generalizes, with real threshold $8$ and status passed. A position-controlled ribosome-occupancy contact gives a separate local readout boundary: $5754$ genes overlap between ordered CDS rows and occupancy rows after the length check, all $5754$ joined genes pass that check, and the within-position-bin null requested $200$ permutations for each of $9$ computed families. Under the local gate with $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and $C_H>0$, the passing families are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress. This supports the claim that the $f_3$-stress coordinate reaches translation-proximal readout tests in bounded finite settings, while keeping the driver statistic distinct from any protein-level or phenotypic conclusion.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The supported reading is narrow. The finite rows justify a rank-powered cross-layer association in which the $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate is aligned with translational-selection strength on a $15$-organism computed panel, with direction agreement $15/15$ and Spearman correlation $0.7071428571428572$. They do not show protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, post-translational state, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Those conclusions would require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. If wobble weights are symmetric under the $f_3$ pairing, or if the design collapses to a saturated low-rank surface such as an all-$1.0$ three-organism fit, the correct status is null or needs more data rather than survival.
