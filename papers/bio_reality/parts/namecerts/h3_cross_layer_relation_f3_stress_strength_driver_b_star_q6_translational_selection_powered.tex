\paragraph{Finite row and coordinate.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress. The coordinate is evaluated only after amino-acid projection: a codon-usage vector $q$ is first reduced to the residual component $q-P_{\tau}q$, so the row is not a restatement of amino-acid composition. In the finite contact used here, the residual basis contains $9$ coordinates, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. On the three-organism codon-count contact used for the residual-basis check, $7$ of these $9$ coordinates are nonzero above the declared variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $0.7777777777777778$. The quotient geometry attached to the same codon-topology surface has $\pi_M$ size $10$, quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and matching $M_f$ vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$. These numbers fix the coordinate as a finite codon-topological residual, not as an unconstrained biological effect.

\paragraph{Powered strength-driver statistic.}
The strength-driver experiment evaluates whether cross-organism $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous selection strength together with a fixed direction of alignment. The run identifier is $a6b023b665b4$ and the deterministic seed is $\mathtt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered:deterministic}$. The panel requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$ organisms, with at least $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds. The observed Spearman association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection},\, f_3\mathrm{\ strength}) &= 0.7071428571428572.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed-direction component is saturated in the finite row: direction agreement is $15/15$, hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{direction\ agreement\ rate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row also records the Sulfolobus negative-alignment case as direction-consistent. Thus the supported finite statement is not merely that $f_3$-stress is present, but that its cross-organism strength is ordered by total synonymous selection strength together with a stable direction choice in the computed panel.

\paragraph{Relation to rank-powered modeled translation readout.}
The strength-driver row is tied to a modeled translation-readout surface rather than to a direct protein or phenotype endpoint. In the rank-powered modeled-tAI contact, the organism count is $40$, the residualized control design has $24$ control columns, rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$. \paragraph{Cross-organism and local occupancy consistency.}
A separate cross-organism finite row tested $15$ real organisms out of $16$ requested, used $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, and called $f_3$-stress generalizing with real threshold $8$. A position-controlled ribosome-occupancy contact also reports that all $9$ families had at least $200$ within-position-bin permutations, with $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress passing the combined $\lambda_{DL}$ and direction-cosine gate. In that contact, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, and the rule was $C_H>0$. The joined occupancy table contained $5754$ genes after length checking, from $5754$ occupancy genes and $6013$ ordered-CDS genes, with length mismatch count $0$. These auxiliary rows do not prove the strength-driver claim by themselves, but they show that the same $f_3$-stress coordinate is not isolated to a single codon-count calculation.

\paragraph{Interpretation allowed by the finite row.}
The finite conclusion is that, in the computed powered panel, $f_3$-stress behaves as a codon-topology residual whose strength is explained by total synonymous selection strength multiplied by fixed-direction alignment. Equivalently, the row supports a bounded cross-layer relation from synonymous codon organization to a translation-proximal readout surface when the wobble-weight side is asymmetric and the rank condition is nondegenerate. The result should be stated as a finite rank-powered association, not as a universal law of translation.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No claim about measured translation efficiency, ribosome dynamics, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from this row alone. Modeled tAI or stAI weights are translation-proximal annotations, not direct ribosome-occupancy observations. The same-row measured translation-efficiency route would require organism- and CDS-matched codon counts, wobble weights, ribosome-profiling or equivalent translation-readout rows, matched mRNA controls, and the declared controlled $S^{QT}$ statistic. Protein, structure, physical-admissibility, function, phenotype, and mechanism claims would each require their own layer-matched contact and statistic after the translation-readout step has passed.
