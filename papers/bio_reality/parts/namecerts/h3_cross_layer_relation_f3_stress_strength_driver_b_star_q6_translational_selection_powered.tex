\subsection{Rank-powered translational-selection strength for the $f_3$-stress coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-strength-driver-translational-selection}

\paragraph{Finite readout surface.}
This record concerns the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress.  The carrier is a finite organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to $f_3$-paired synonymous codon fibers, paired codon-usage rows, wobble-weight readouts such as tAI or stAI, and translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy summaries when those rows are available.  The coordinate is not an amino-acid composition effect: it is read after quotienting by the amino-acid projection, as $q-P_{\tau}q$, and is therefore a residual codon-topology coordinate.  In the finite quotient check, the $M_f$ vector was $(20,38,26,8,1)$, the quotient $f$-vector was $(10,14,6,1)$, and $\pi_M$ had size $10$.  The residual-basis validation then computed $9$ $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates across three finite organism contacts, namely human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli}; the named coordinates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Seven of the nine residual coordinates were nonzero above the variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9=0.7777777777777778$.  These finite numbers establish that the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a real residual coordinate on the chosen codon tables, not that it is already a translation, protein, structural, or functional variable.

\paragraph{Rank and wobble asymmetry.}
The cross-layer question is whether $f_3$-stress remains visible after the codon-level residual surface is confronted with translation-layer readouts.  The powered modeled-tAI computation used $40$ organisms and a $9\times 9$ survival matrix on the same nine residual coordinates.  After residualizing $24$ control columns, the control rank was $23$, the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $17$, and the residualized coordinate rank was $9$.  The resulting modeled $Q\to T$ fit had $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, below the degeneracy cutoff $0.999999$ and therefore not an all-saturated three-row artifact.  This is the rank condition that makes the later strength-driver statistic interpretable as a finite powered panel rather than as an overfit codon table.

The smaller three-organism modeled-tAI contact is kept separate.  It had $81$ entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, and all three organisms had modeled tAI readouts computed from dos Reis wobble weights with $61$ sense codons and zero raw-weight fallback count $0$.  However, that three-organism surface is explicitly underpowered for survival, because $n=3$ is insufficient for the powered claim.  Thus the chapter uses the $40$-organism rank-powered surface for survival context and the later $15$-organism protein-level panel for the strength-driver statistic.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
The finite strength-driver experiment tested whether organism-level $f_3$-stress strength is explained by total synonymous selection strength together with a fixed-direction alignment.  It requested $16$ organisms, computed $15$, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $5$ folds.  The status was passed.  The rank-powered scalar association between total synonymous selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection},\ |f_3\text{-stress}|)&=0.7071428571428572.
\end{aligned}
$$
The direction-agreement count was $15$ out of $15$, so the finite direction-agreement rate was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{15}{15}&=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The deterministic seed was recorded as \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_f3\_stress\_strength\_driver\_powered:deterministic}.  The run also marked the Sulfolobus case as carrying negative alignment together with its direction, so the direction statistic was not obtained by silently discarding the sign-reversing organism.  The interpretation supported by these rows is narrow: within the computed panel, $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous selection strength and fixed-direction alignment.  The statistic does not say that every organism has the same magnitude, nor that the sign convention is biologically universal.

\paragraph{Cross-organism support.}
A separate powered generality run tested whether the $f_3$-stress signal generalizes across organisms.  It requested $16$ organisms, tested $15$ real organisms, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $120$ permutations.  Its real threshold was $8$, and its call was \texttt{f3\_stress\_generalizes}.  This supports treating the strength-driver result as a multi-organism finite-row pattern rather than a single-organism accident.  The support is still bounded by the actual panel: the claim is about the organisms and readout construction that were computed, not about all taxa, all codon tables, or all translational systems.

\paragraph{Position-resolved contact.}
The ribosome-occupancy contact gives a more local check on whether synonymous choices in the same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ family can separate footprint occupancy after position controls.  It joined $5754$ genes between ribosome occupancy and ordered CDS rows, with $5754$ genes surviving the length check and length mismatch count $0$ out of an occupancy set of $5754$ and an ordered-CDS set of $6013$.  The analysis demeaned occupancy within exact file-provided window/codon-index and amino-acid bins, and it used within-bin synonymous-label permutations.  All $9$ families had at least $200$ permutations for the within-position-bin Null95 computation.  With $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, description length $DL=2$, and a positive coordinate-support cosine rule $C_H>0$, the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This contact is consistent with a translation-proximal readout for $f_3$-stress, but it remains descriptive and position-controlled rather than causal.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The ready claim is therefore the following finite statement.  On the computed rank-powered panels, $f_3$-stress is a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ amino-acid-quotient residual coordinate whose strength is associated with total synonymous selection strength, with Spearman coefficient $0.7071428571428572$, and whose direction alignment is $15/15$ in the strength-driver panel.  The modeled translation-survival context has $40$ organisms, $9$ residual coordinates, residualized coordinate rank $9$, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$, which is below the declared degeneracy cutoff.  The position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact independently places $f_3$-stress among the three families passing the $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $DL=2$, $C_H>0$ gate after within-bin permutation control.  These rows justify a bounded cross-layer relation between codon-residual topology and translation-proximal selection/readout strength.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
Nothing in this chapter turns the residual coordinate itself into a measured translation mechanism.  A tAI or stAI surface is a modeled codon-adaptation readout unless same-row ribosome or translation-efficiency rows are joined and evaluated under the stated controls.  Ribosome occupancy or modeled tAI does not by itself establish protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, stability, biochemical function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal law.  Any such stronger conclusion would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic after the $f_3$-stress-to-translation step has survived in the same finite row system.
