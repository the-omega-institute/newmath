\subsection{A powered selection-strength driver for the $f_3$-stress residual}\label{sec:bior-f3-stress-selection-strength-driver}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate denoted here by $f_3$-stress.  The coordinate is not an amino-acid readout.  It is formed after amino-acid projection, so the relevant finite object is the residual component left in a codon-usage surface once amino-acid identity has been quotiented away.  In the finite residual-basis computation, nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates were present: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f_3$-stress.  Of these nine coordinates, seven were nonzero above the stated variance threshold $0.01$, giving nonzero fraction $7/9 = 0.7777777777777778$.  The underlying quotient geometry check used a finite $M$ vector with counts $(20,38,26,8,1)$, a quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$, and quotient size $10$.  These numbers fix the scope of the readout: the coordinate is a finite codon-topology residual, not a direct molecular state.

\paragraph{Powered strength-driver statistic.}
The powered selection-strength row asks whether the magnitude of the $f_3$-stress effect is explained by total synonymous selection strength together with a fixed directional alignment.  The finite run computed $15$ organisms out of $16$ requested organisms, with a minimum of $500$ proteins per organism and $5$ folds.  The resulting rank statistic was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho_{\mathrm{Spearman}}(\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection},\ |f_3\text{-stress}|) &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\mathrm{direction\ agreement} &= 15/15,\\
\mathrm{direction\ agreement\ rate} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite verdict for this row is therefore positive at the stated powered scope: $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous selection strength while retaining a fixed-direction alignment across every computed organism.  The Sulfolobus case was explicitly marked as having negative alignment and direction, so the row does not silently discard sign structure; it records that the alignment convention still accounts for that case in the powered direction check.

\paragraph{Cross-organism support.}
A separate generality run tested the same $B^*_{Q6}$ $f_3$-stress coordinate across organisms.  It again computed $15$ real organisms from $16$ requested, used $5$ folds, required at least $500$ proteins per organism, and used $120$ permutations.  The threshold for a real call was $8$, and the finite call was $f_3\text{-stress\_generalizes}$.  This is important because the strength-driver row is not merely a small bounded-contact correlation: it is attached to a powered multi-organism panel whose rank and fold structure were explicitly recorded.

\paragraph{Translation-readout boundary.}
The strongest translation-proximal supporting surface in the supplied finite rows is modeled tAI rather than measured ribosome occupancy.  The powered modeled-tAI computation used $40$ organisms, $9$ residual coordinates, $9$ readout columns, control rank $23$, and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after controls.  Its controlled statistic was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\mathrm{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\mathrm{residual\ df\ after\ }Z &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a bounded modeled translation-readout relation, not a measured ribosome-seq conclusion.  The smaller three-organism modeled-tAI matrix had all standard tRNA family payloads available for human, yeast, and \emph{E. coli} K-12, and all $81$ entries exceeded the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$ with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$; however, its own record states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  Thus the three-organism surface is useful as a finite contact check, while the powered $40$-organism row carries the modeled-tAI survival weight.

\paragraph{Ribosome-occupancy contact.}
A measured ribosome-occupancy row exists at a narrower descriptive scope.  It joined $5754$ ordered CDS genes to occupancy rows, with $5754$ genes passing the length check and zero length mismatches from $6013$ ordered CDS genes and $5754$ occupancy genes.  The position-confound control used exact window or codon-index bins together with amino-acid bins; synonymous labels were shuffled only within the same bin.  The null computation requested $200$ within-bin permutations, and all $9$ families had at least $200$ permutations.  With description length $DL=2$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and the rule $C_H>0$, the families passing all gates were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f_3$-stress.  This gives a measured, position-controlled occupancy contact for $f_3$-stress, but it remains descriptive and local to the joined window surface.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite claim is that $f_3$-stress has selection-powered translational relevance when three requirements are simultaneously met: the coordinate survives amino-acid quotienting as a nontrivial synonymous residual, wobble or translation-readout weights break the paired symmetry rather than leaving the pair invisible, and the powered organism panel has enough rank to avoid the all-fitted small-sample trap.  Under those constraints, the supplied row supports the statement that the strength of the $f_3$-stress signal is driven by total synonymous selection strength times fixed-direction alignment.  The positive support is numerical and finite: $15$ computed organisms, $5$ folds, $15/15$ directional agreement, and Spearman correlation $0.7071428571428572$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological claim follows from these rows alone.  The $f_3$-stress coordinate does not by itself establish translation realization beyond the stated modeled or measured readout contact, and it does not establish protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation as a universal law, or a global biological rule.  Those layers require their own same-row contacts and their own controlled finite statistics.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights are codon-adaptation readouts, not observed ribosome occupancy; ribosome occupancy is not protein abundance; protein abundance is not folding or function; and a positive residual relation is not a causal mechanism unless an independent perturbational or mechanistic contact supplies that layer.
