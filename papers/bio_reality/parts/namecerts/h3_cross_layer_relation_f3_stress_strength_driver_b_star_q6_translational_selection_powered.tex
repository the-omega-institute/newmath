\subsection{The $f_3$-stress strength driver for translational selection}\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-strength-driver}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record concerns the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate called $f_3$-stress, read on a finite organism panel rather than on an unrestricted biological population.  The carrier is the organism-by-codon readout surface restricted to synonymous fibers paired by the $f_3$ involution, with amino-acid composition first quotiented away.  On the finite quotient geometry the reported $M_f$ vector is $(20,38,26,8,1)$, the quotient vector is $(10,14,6,1)$, and the projected quotient has size $10$.  The residual-basis computation then produced $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  In the three-organism codon-count contact, $7$ of $9$ amino-acid-quotient residual entries were nonzero above the $0.01$ variance threshold, giving nonzero fraction $0.7777777777777778$.  This establishes that the coordinate is not vacuous on the finite codon-usage rows, but it does not by itself give a translation, protein, structure, or phenotype claim.

\paragraph{Rank-powered translation readout.}
The powered modeled-translation surface used $40$ organisms and the same $9$ residual coordinates projected against modeled per-codon tAI readouts from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The controlled design residualized $24$ control columns, with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and residual degrees of freedom $17$ after controls.  The residualized $Q$ design had rank $9$, and the reported controlled translation-readout fit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{df}_{\mathrm{resid}\,|\,Z} &= 17.
\end{aligned}
$$
The paired three-organism modeled-tAI contact is retained only as a bounded coupling check: it had $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$, but with only $3$ organisms it was explicitly not a powered survival result.  The powered interpretation therefore uses the $40$-organism rank-sufficient surface, not the rank-saturated three-row surface.

\paragraph{Strength-driver statistic.}
On the dedicated $f_3$-stress strength-driver run, the experiment requested $16$ organisms and computed $15$.  Each computed organism met the stated minimum of $500$ proteins, and the cross-validation count was $5$ folds.  The finite-row result was passed, with direction agreement count $15$ over denominator $15$, hence direction agreement rate $1.0$.  The reported association between total synonymous-selection strength and $f_3$-stress strength was Spearman correlation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rho\bigl(\mathrm{total\ synonymous\ selection},\ |f_3\mathrm{-stress}|\bigr) &= 0.7071428571428572,\\
\mathrm{direction\ agreement} &= \frac{15}{15}=1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The biological read is therefore narrow: within this finite panel, $f_3$-stress strength tracks total synonymous-selection strength together with fixed-direction alignment.  The run also marks the Sulfolobus case as having negative alignment and direction, so the result is not a claim that every organism has the same signed effect.  It is a strength-and-alignment statement, not a universal direction law.

\paragraph{Generalization and local ribosome contact.}
The companion cross-organism generality run also requested $16$ organisms and tested $15$, with $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, a real threshold of $8$, and the verdict $f_3$-stress generalizes.  A separate ribosome-occupancy contact supplies a local translation-layer consistency check: $5754$ ordered CDS rows joined to the occupancy table after length checking, with $0$ length mismatches out of $6013$ ordered CDS genes and $5754$ occupancy genes.  The within-position-bin permutation analysis computed all $9$ families, each with at least $200$ permutations requested.  Under the rule $C_H>0$ and description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ at $DL=2$, the families passing all gates were $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, and $f_3$-stress.  This supports a translation-adjacent readout boundary for the coordinate, while leaving causal mechanism and protein-level consequences outside the demonstrated finite statistic.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The chapter records a positive finite-row cross-layer relation in the restricted sense that the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate is rank-powered on modeled translation readouts and its strength is explained by total synonymous-selection strength combined with direction alignment across the $15$ computed organisms.  The admissible statement is conditional on the wobble-weight surface breaking the $f_3$ pairing and on the rank-powered design avoiding the $n=3$ saturation trap.  The chapter must not be read as saying that $f_3$-stress directly determines translation dynamics, ribosome dwell, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled finite statistics.
