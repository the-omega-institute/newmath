\subsection{mRNA-gated translation-efficiency rescue at the Q6 boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-gated-te-rescue}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
The record concerns finite CDS-by-organism rows in which each retained coding sequence carries a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^*_{Q6}$ basis, a matched transcript-abundance measurement, and a translation-layer readout such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency. The biological question is not whether codon usage, modeled adaptation weights, or a residual coordinate can be narrated as translation. The question is narrower: after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, methionine-density, and matched mRNA abundance are already in the conditioning surface, does any $q_i$ retain a nonzero relation to the translation-efficiency residual?

\paragraph{Control ordering.}
The transcript layer is a mandatory gate. A coordinate may associate with mRNA abundance and still fail to survive as a translation-efficiency coordinate. In the finite table, this means that the $Q_i \to \mathrm{RNA}$ surface is estimated before any $Q_i \to \mathrm{TE}$ interpretation is admitted. The translation readout is therefore the residual remaining after the same-row mRNA control has been attached, not a raw ribosome or codon-usage association. The local test can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{TE}_g &= \mathrm{RPF}_g / \mathrm{mRNA}_g,\\
\widehat{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{TE}}_g &= \mathrm{Residual}\bigl(\mathrm{TE}_g \mid \mathrm{AA}_g,\mathrm{organism}_g,\mathrm{GC3}_g,\mathrm{length}_g,M_g,\mathrm{mRNA}_g\bigr),\\
S_i &= \Delta\mathrm{Fit}\bigl(\widehat{\epsilon}^{\mathrm{TE}}_g ; q_{ig}\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $g$ ranges over retained CDS rows. A positive biological reading is allowed only for the residual part of $S_i$ that is not absorbed by transcript abundance or by the standard sequence controls.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistics.}
The recorded run is $842b739606af$. It retained $4419$ genes and compared top and bottom coordinate strata of size $883$ each. The main separation statistic was $D=0.23767000000000027$, while the matched RNA control contrast was $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$. The two replicate contrasts had the same sign, with $D_{\mathrm{rep\_a}}=0.19967000000000013$ and $D_{\mathrm{rep\_b}}=0.24280000000000035$, and the run explicitly records $\mathrm{same\_sign}=\mathrm{true}$. The permutation count was $10000$, with two-sided value $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\cdot 10^{-5}$ and runtime $20.019$ seconds. The declared comparison threshold was $D_{\mathrm{thresh}}=0.32192809488736235$, so the finite row signal is best read as a significant, same-direction translation-side contrast that remains numerically below the stated threshold for that scalar.

\paragraph{Interpretive verdict.}
The decisive feature is the asymmetry between $D$ and $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}$. A positive translation-side contrast of about $0.23767$ appears together with an RNA contrast close to zero and slightly negative. That pattern supports the local boundary claim: the packet is not merely reporting that $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates track transcript abundance. Instead, the run gives a finite-row reason to preserve the mRNA-gated translation-efficiency question as an admissible BioReality target. The evidence is still row-scoped and statistic-scoped. It does not turn $q_i$ into a mechanism, and it does not identify a universal codon law. It says that the translation-efficiency residual should not be collapsed into the mRNA layer for this run.

\paragraph{Layer restrictions.}
Codon-usage summaries, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling routes, and matched mRNA abundance occupy different observational roles. Codon usage provides sequence composition. Adaptation weights provide bounded modeled translation annotations. Ribosome profiling can provide occupancy or TE rows only when joined to the same CDS identifiers. Matched mRNA abundance supplies the denominator or covariate required to separate transcription from translation. None of these contacts, alone or by spelling normalization, supplies folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, protein localization, protein stability, post-translational state, or a global biological rule.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The present chapter therefore permits only the following bounded statement: in run $842b739606af$, a finite matched CDS table with $4419$ retained genes, $883$ top rows, and $883$ bottom rows produced a same-sign translation-side separation with $D=0.23767000000000027$, weak RNA-layer contrast $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$, and $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\cdot 10^{-5}$ under $10000$ permutations. No translation realization, folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, functional claim, mechanistic claim, phenotype claim, or universality claim follows unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that layer and a declared finite statistic survives its own controls.
