\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The chapter records a strict separation among codon-coordinate geometry, transcript abundance, translation-efficiency residuals, and downstream biological layers. Codon usage, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance may not be treated as translation realization unless the same row also carries the matched mRNA control and the residual translation-efficiency signal remains nonzero under the stated controls. A $q_i\to\mathrm{mRNA}$ association is not a $q_i\to\mathrm{translation}$ mechanism. Likewise, the present finite contrast gives no permission to infer protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, phenotype, causal mechanism, or a global biological rule. Each of those layers would require its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.
