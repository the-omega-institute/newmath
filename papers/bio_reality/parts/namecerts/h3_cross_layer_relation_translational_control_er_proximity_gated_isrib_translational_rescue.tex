\subsection{mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary for the ER-proximity ISRIB rescue row}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-te-boundary-er-proximity-isrib}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each retained row carries a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, standard sequence controls, a matched mRNA-abundance surface, and a translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout. The coordinate $q_i$ is used only after amino-acid identity has been quotiented out, so the tested signal is not a disguised amino-acid-composition statement. The operative comparison is therefore not simply whether $q_i$ is associated with a translation proxy. It is whether the same row still carries a nonzero $q_i \to \mathrm{TE}$ residual after conditioning on mRNA abundance together with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and methionine-density controls. In this chapter the matched transcript surface is mandatory: a $q_i \to \mathrm{mRNA}$ association is treated as transcription-layer evidence, and it is not promoted to a translation-layer statement.

\paragraph{Control logic.}
The mRNA surface is not an optional covariate. It is the gate that separates a transcription-layer association from a translation-efficiency residual. If the $q_i$ signal is absorbed by the matched transcript surface, then the row cannot be counted as translation-realized. In this run the recorded $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$ is directionally separated from the positive translation-side statistic $D=0.23767000000000027$, which supports the local interpretation that the finite contrast is not merely a positive mRNA-abundance contrast. This does not make mRNA irrelevant; it says only that the finite record contains a translation-side residual check in which transcript abundance has been explicitly attached.

\paragraph{Readout boundary.}
The chapter therefore admits the following bounded statement. For the ER-proximity ISRIB rescue row, the retained finite table supplies an mRNA-controlled comparison between $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and a translation-efficiency readout. The positive separation statistic, the two replicate statistics, the balanced $883$-by-$883$ strata, and the $10000$-permutation probability together justify entering the row into a translation-readout survival ledger as a controlled finite observation. The entry is not a claim that codon geometry alone realizes translation. It is also not a claim that modeled tAI or stAI weights are observed ribosome traffic. The only supported assertion is that, for this finite joined surface, the $q_i$ coordinate has a translation-efficiency residual comparison after the transcript control has been made part of the same-row design.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from this row. No protein-abundance statement follows unless a matched protein contact is separately joined and tested. No position-resolved ribosome-occupancy statement follows unless the readout rows contain that resolution. No translation claim follows from transcript abundance alone. The row can be used only as a finite mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary: it records a same-row residual comparison with $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=4419$, $n_{\mathrm{top}}=883$, $n_{\mathrm{bottom}}=883$, $D=0.23767000000000027$, $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$, $D_{\mathrm{rep},a}=0.19967000000000013$, $D_{\mathrm{rep},b}=0.24280000000000035$, $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$, and $D_{\mathrm{thresh}}=0.32192809488736235$.
