\subsection{mRNA-Controlled Translation-Efficiency Boundary for B*\_Q6 Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-te-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record concerns finite CDS-by-organism rows in which each coding sequence carries a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis after the amino-acid quotient has already been imposed.  The row is not accepted as a translation row merely because it contains codon usage, a codon-adaptation score, or a ribosome-associated name.  It must also carry a same-condition mRNA abundance surface, because the relevant question is whether the $Q_i$ coordinate leaves a translation-efficiency residual once transcript abundance has been charged to the transcription layer.  The finite carrier therefore has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x_g = (q_i(g), Z_g, m_g, t_g),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $g$ ranges over retained CDS rows, $Z_g$ contains the amino-acid-composition, organism, GC3, length, and M-density controls, $m_g$ is the matched mRNA abundance control, and $t_g$ is a translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout only when the same row supplies the required denominator or residualization surface.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The verified finite run is experiment $842b739606af$, checked at $2026$-$06$-$23$T$15$:$23$:$52+00$:$00$.  It retained $4419$ genes and compared the upper and lower residual-coordinate tails with $n_{\mathrm{top}}=883$ and $n_{\mathrm{bottom}}=883$.  The observed translation-layer contrast was $D=0.23767000000000027$, while the matched RNA contrast was $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$.  The two replicate contrasts were concordant in sign, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
  D_{\mathrm{rep},a} &= 0.19967000000000013,\\
  D_{\mathrm{rep},b} &= 0.24280000000000035,\\
  \mathrm{same\_sign} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation count was $10000$, giving a two-sided permutation value $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$, and the runtime was $20.019$ seconds.  The declared threshold was $D_{\mathrm{thresh}}=0.32192809488736235$, so the run records a directionally stable finite contrast but not a threshold-exceeding translation-realization certificate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  0 < D &= 0.23767000000000027 < 0.32192809488736235 = D_{\mathrm{thresh}},\\
  D_{\mathrm{RNA}} &= -0.0224999999999973.
\end{aligned}
$$
This numerical shape is the reason the chapter is a boundary record rather than a positive mechanism claim.  The signal is not absorbed by the same-row RNA control in the raw contrast comparison, but it also does not cross the stated effect threshold.

\paragraph{Local decision rule.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the admissible comparison is not $q_i$ against translation wording, but $q_i$ against a residualized translation-efficiency surface after the matched transcript abundance and the standard sequence controls have been attached.  In finite-row notation, the test asks whether a residual coefficient or description-length gain survives the conditioning step
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i \longrightarrow t_g \mid (Z_g,m_g),
\end{aligned}
$$
and refuses the stronger reading whenever the detectable association can be explained at the mRNA surface or by the baseline sequence covariates.  The present run supports a sharply bounded readout: the finite rows show a positive measured contrast in the translation-efficiency direction, a near-zero opposite-signed RNA contrast, and replicate agreement in the translation contrast, but the declared threshold is not reached.

\paragraph{Scientific boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization from codon usage, tAI, stAI, ribosome-contact vocabulary, or protein abundance wording.  A positive $Q_i$ association with mRNA abundance remains a transcription-layer association unless a same-row TE or ribosome-occupancy residual survives after mRNA control.  Likewise, even a surviving TE residual would remain a translation-readout statement only.  No folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, cellular phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation claim, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  Each such assertion requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own finite statistic.  The present numerical record therefore licenses the following local reading and no more: in $4419$ retained CDS rows, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-coordinate contrast is positive and replicate-consistent for the measured translation-efficiency direction, is not mirrored by the RNA contrast, and remains below the declared threshold for promotion beyond a guarded translation-efficiency boundary.
