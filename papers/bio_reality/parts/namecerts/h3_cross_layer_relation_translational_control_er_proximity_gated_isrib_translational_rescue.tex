\subsection{mRNA-Controlled Translation-Efficiency Boundary for Q6 Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-translation-efficiency-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite rows.}
The record concerns finite CDS-by-organism rows in which each coding sequence carries a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate family, after quotienting by amino-acid composition.  The row is not allowed to speak about translation merely because a codon statistic or a modeled codon-adaptation weight is present.  The row must also carry a same-condition mRNA abundance surface, and the translation readout is interpreted only after that transcript-abundance surface has been used as a control.  In this chapter the relevant finite panel has $n_{\mathrm{genes}}=4419$ retained genes, with $n_{\mathrm{top}}=883$ and $n_{\mathrm{bottom}}=883$ rows in the two ranked comparison tails.  The computation is therefore a finite comparison of residual-coordinate strata, not a claim about all genes, all organisms, or all translation systems.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
The finite run identified as $842b739606af$ records a positive translation-side contrast after the transcript layer is separated from the readout layer.  The observed translation-efficiency contrast is $D=0.23767000000000027$, while the matched transcript-abundance contrast is $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$.  The two replicate contrasts remain positive, with $D_{\mathrm{rep},a}=0.19967000000000013$ and $D_{\mathrm{rep},b}=0.24280000000000035$, so the finite-row signal is not being read as a same-sign mRNA abundance artifact in this run.  The permutation count is $10000$, and the recorded two-sided permutation value is $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$.  The computation finished in $20.019$ seconds and records \texttt{same\_sign=true} for the two replicate translation contrasts.

$$
\begin{aligned}
D &= 0.23767000000000027,\\
D_{\mathrm{RNA}} &= -0.0224999999999973,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},a} &= 0.19967000000000013,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},b} &= 0.24280000000000035,\\
p_{\mathrm{two}} &= 9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The declared threshold is $D_{\mathrm{thresh}}=0.32192809488736235$.  Thus the finite record is best read as a significant, replicate-consistent translation-efficiency contrast whose magnitude remains below the declared hard contrast threshold.  The chapter therefore records a boundary result: the $q_i$ coordinate is permitted to enter a translation-efficiency survival table only as an mRNA-controlled residual contrast, and the scalar size of that contrast must still be reported against its threshold rather than promoted into a stronger biological conclusion.

\paragraph{Layer separation.}
The biological object is not codon usage alone, and it is not transcript abundance alone.  It is the same-row comparison between a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate and a translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout after conditioning on amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, methionine-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance.  The negative value of $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}$ is important for interpretation: in this finite run the ranked $q_i$ contrast does not present as a positive transcript-abundance contrast of the same form.  That fact is the local reason the translation-efficiency residual is not immediately absorbed by the mRNA control surface.  Conversely, the presence of a nonzero $D$ does not make the coordinate a mechanism; it only licenses a bounded residual entry in the translation-readout matrix.

\paragraph{Readout rule.}
For each row, the admissible comparison has the form $q_i \to T_j\mid Z$, where $T_j$ is the translation-efficiency residual and $Z$ contains the mandatory controls.  The transcript abundance measurement is part of $Z$, not a downstream translation outcome.  If the $q_i$ association is carried entirely by the mRNA abundance surface, the row must be refused as translation-realized.  If a residual translation-efficiency contrast remains after the mRNA control is attached, the row may be reported as a finite translation-readout survival candidate, with the exact contrast, threshold, permutation count, row counts, and replicate signs shown locally.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome-dynamics law, protein abundance effect, folding consequence, physical admissibility claim, biological function, phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter.  Codon-usage tables, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling routes, and mRNA controls are distinct readout surfaces.  A stronger claim would require its own layer-matched contact: direct ribosome or translation-efficiency rows for translation realization beyond this bounded residual statistic; proteomics rows for protein abundance; structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility; functional or perturbation assays for biological role; and cross-condition replication before any law-like wording is allowed.  The present result says only that, on the finite panel of $4419$ retained genes with $883$ top and $883$ bottom rows, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate has a significant mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency contrast in run $842b739606af$, while remaining below the declared hard contrast threshold.
