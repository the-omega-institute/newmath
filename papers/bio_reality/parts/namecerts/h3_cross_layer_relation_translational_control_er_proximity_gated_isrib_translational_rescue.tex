\subsection{mRNA-Controlled Translation-Efficiency Boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-te-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This BioReality record concerns finite CDS-by-organism rows in which each row carries a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$, ordinary sequence covariates, a matched mRNA abundance measurement, and a same-row translation readout when that readout is available. The relevant distinction is not whether $q_i$ is associated with a transcript-level surface, but whether any $q_i$ signal remains after transcript abundance has been used as a mandatory control for the translation-efficiency surface. The row object is therefore a finite comparison table with columns of the following form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x_g = (q_i(g),\, Z_g,\, A_g,\, R_g,\, T_g),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $g$ ranges over retained CDS rows, $Z_g$ contains amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and methionine-density controls, $A_g$ is matched mRNA abundance, $R_g$ is ribosome-footprint or occupancy readout when present, and $T_g$ is the translation-efficiency residual surface evaluated after the abundance control. The chapter does not identify $q_i$ with a molecular force, a ribosome state, a protein state, or a mechanism. It records a finite rule for refusing that promotion unless the row-level translation residual survives the control surface.

\paragraph{Boundary relation.}
For each coordinate $q_i$, the admissible readout is the residual relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i \longrightarrow T \mid (A, Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
not the larger and weaker association
$$
\begin{aligned}
  q_i \longrightarrow A.
\end{aligned}
$$
A $q_i$ association with mRNA abundance is therefore classified as transcription-layer contact only. It can explain why raw ribosome counts or protein-level measurements may be confounded, but it cannot itself be counted as translation realization. The finite statistic above supports a row-level separation test: a contrast is observed after mRNA control, the RNA-only contrast is not aligned with the observed translation-side contrast, and the permutation calculation records a small empirical tail probability. The mathematical content is a gating rule on interpretation, not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Survival matrix interpretation.}
Let $S^{QT}_{ij}$ denote the rowwise residual survival score for coordinate $q_i$ against translation readout $T_j$ after the abundance and sequence controls have been applied. In this chapter, a translation-side entry may be read only when the following implication is satisfied on the finite table:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S^{QT}_{ij} > 0
  \quad\Rightarrow\quad
  q_i \text{ contributes beyond } A \text{ and } Z.
\end{aligned}
$$
The converse is not asserted. A positive raw codon-usage contrast, a modeled tAI or stAI weight, a ribosome-contact name, or a matched mRNA contact name does not by itself produce a survival entry. The finite run records $D=0.23767000000000027$ with $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$ over $10000$ permutations, but the stated threshold $D_{\mathrm{thresh}}=0.32192809488736235$ must remain visible in the interpretation. The chapter may therefore report a finite, mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary signal, not an unconditional mechanistic claim.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The cannot-claim boundary is local and strict. Codon usage, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual geometry, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome occupancy labels, and matched mRNA abundance do not imply translation realization unless a same-row translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy residual remains nonzero after the matched abundance control and the standard sequence controls. They also do not imply folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causal mechanism, adaptation, or universality. Each of those higher layers requires its own measured contact and its own finite statistic. In the present chapter, mRNA abundance is only the required transcript-level control; it is not the translation readout and not a substitute for one.
