\subsection{mRNA-Controlled Translation-Efficiency Boundary for the Q6 Residual Coordinate}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-translation-efficiency-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record concerns finite CDS-by-organism rows in which each coding entry carries a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^*_{Q6}$ basis after quotienting by amino-acid composition.  The biological row is not read as a translation outcome merely because a codon coordinate is present.  A row becomes eligible for translation-layer testing only when the same CDS entry also carries an mRNA abundance surface and a translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout.  The mRNA surface is mandatory because a residual association can live at the transcript-abundance layer rather than at the translation-efficiency layer.  Thus the admissible row has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x_g &= (q_i(g), Z(g), RPF(g), RNA(g), TE(g)), \\
  TE(g) &= RPF(g)/RNA(g), \\
  Z(g) &= (AA(g), organism(g), GC3(g), length(g), M(g), RNA(g)),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $g$ ranges over the retained CDS rows, $AA$ denotes amino-acid-composition controls, and $M$ denotes the methionine-density baseline.  The coordinate $q_i$ is therefore tested against a translation-efficiency residual only after the matched transcript-abundance term has been attached.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The finite run identified as $842b739606af$ retained $4419$ genes.  The upper and lower comparison sets each contained $883$ genes, so the readout is a balanced top--bottom contrast on the retained panel rather than an unrestricted genome-wide assertion.  The reported finite displacement was
$$
\begin{aligned}
  D &= 0.23767000000000027, \\
  D_{RNA} &= -0.0224999999999973, \\
  D_{rep,a} &= 0.19967000000000013, \\
  D_{rep,b} &= 0.24280000000000035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two replicate contrasts have the same sign, recorded as $same\_sign = true$.  The permutation count was $10000$, with two-sided permutation value
$$
\begin{aligned}
  p_{two} &= 9.999000099990002 \times 10^{-5}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The threshold scalar supplied with the run was
$$
\begin{aligned}
  D_{thresh} &= 0.32192809488736235.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently the finite row panel supports a bounded contrast in which the translation-side displacement is positive and replicate-consistent, while the transcript-abundance displacement is close to zero and opposite in sign.  The evidence is therefore naturally stated as a separation claim: the signal is not explained by promoting the $Q6$ coordinate to a transcription-only association, but it also remains below the supplied displacement threshold and must stay within the declared translation-efficiency boundary.

\paragraph{Control interpretation.}
The central distinction is between $q_i \to RNA$ and $q_i \to TE$.  A positive codon-coordinate association is not enough, and neither is a ribosome-profiling contact name.  The row must first bind codon counts, organism identity, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, methionine density, ribosome readout, and matched mRNA abundance on the same finite surface.  Only then may the residual statistic ask whether $q_i$ has remaining translation-efficiency information after the mRNA term has absorbed the transcription-layer component.  In this run the reported $D_{RNA}=-0.0224999999999973$ is a local guard against interpreting the contrast as a transcript-abundance effect, while $D=0.23767000000000027$ and the replicate values $0.19967000000000013$ and $0.24280000000000035$ give the bounded positive readback for the translation-efficiency side.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
This section does not assert that $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry is itself a measured biological object.  It also does not assert that tAI, stAI, codon usage, ribosome occupancy, or protein abundance realizes translation unless the same-row mRNA control has been applied and the translation-efficiency residual remains nonzero.  The finite statistic may enter only the translation-readout survival matrix for this particular boundary.  It does not imply protein folding, protein localization, mature protein abundance, molecular function, phenotype, physical admissibility, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or a universal biological law.  Each of those layers would require its own matched measurement contact and its own finite statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter records a ready but narrow BioReality claim.  On $4419$ retained CDS rows, with $883$ top and $883$ bottom entries, the observed contrast is positive for the translation-efficiency side, replicate-consistent, and supported by $10000$ permutations with $p_{two}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$.  The mRNA contrast is near zero and opposite in sign.  The proper conclusion is therefore not that the $Q6$ coordinate causes translation, but that this finite panel supplies a controlled translation-efficiency boundary record: $q_i$ may be compared with the TE residual after mRNA abundance and standard sequence controls have been attached, and any signal absorbed by the mRNA surface is refused as translation-realized.
