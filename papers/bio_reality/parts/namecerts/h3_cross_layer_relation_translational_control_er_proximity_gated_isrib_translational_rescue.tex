\subsection{mRNA-Controlled Translation-Efficiency Boundary for the ER-Proximity ISRIB Rescue Packet}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-te-er-proximity-isrib}

\paragraph{Finite-row carrier.}
This record treats the ER-proximity ISRIB translational-rescue packet as a finite table problem rather than as a free biological generalization.  Each retained row is a coding-sequence entry with a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, amino-acid-composition quotienting already imposed, organism and sequence covariates available, a matched mRNA abundance measurement, and a same-row translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout.  The decisive boundary is that a residual coordinate $q_i$ may not be interpreted as translation-realized merely because it associates with transcript abundance, codon usage, tAI, stAI, or a downstream protein surface.  The row must first pass through the matched mRNA control surface, and only the remaining $q_i \to \mathrm{TE}$ residual may enter the translation-readout survival matrix.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
In experiment run $842b739606af$, the joined finite panel retained $4419$ genes.  The extreme strata used $883$ top rows and $883$ bottom rows, so the reported comparison is a balanced tail contrast on the retained row set rather than a whole-table narrative.  The primary contrast was $D=0.23767000000000027$, with replicate contrasts $D_{\mathrm{rep},a}=0.19967000000000013$ and $D_{\mathrm{rep},b}=0.24280000000000035$.  The RNA-only contrast was $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$, so the supplied finite result does not support the simpler interpretation that the same signal is just transcript abundance.  With $10000$ actual permutations, the two-sided permutation value was $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$, and the replicate sign check was recorded as true.  The declared threshold was $D_{\mathrm{thresh}}=0.32192809488736235$, so the finite readout is best stated as a reproducible positive tail separation below that declared magnitude threshold, not as an unrestricted mechanistic proof.

\paragraph{Local residualization rule.}
The admissible comparison is the contrast between the residual association of $Q_i$ with mRNA abundance and the residual association of $Q_i$ with translation efficiency after the usual controls have been applied.  In local notation, the chapter only uses the following finite-row decision shape:
$$
\begin{aligned}
T_i &= \operatorname{resid}(\mathrm{TE}_i \mid \mathrm{mRNA}_i,\mathrm{AA}_i,\mathrm{organism}_i,\mathrm{GC3}_i,\mathrm{length}_i,\mathrm{Mdensity}_i),\\
R_i &= \operatorname{resid}(q_i \mid \mathrm{AA}_i,\mathrm{organism}_i,\mathrm{GC3}_i,\mathrm{length}_i,\mathrm{Mdensity}_i),\\
S_{QT} &> 0 \quad \hbox{only when } R_i \hbox{ predicts } T_i \hbox{ after the mRNA control remains attached.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a boundary condition on interpretation.  If the $q_i$ signal is absorbed by the matched mRNA surface, then the row may record a transcription-layer association, but it does not become a translation-efficiency survival entry.  The supplied numbers are consistent with a separated readout because $D$ is positive while $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}$ is near zero and of opposite sign; nevertheless the chapter does not convert that contrast into a universal rule.

\paragraph{What the packet can say.}
The finite evidence supports a local, mRNA-controlled readout statement: in the retained $4419$-gene panel, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate distinguishes the top and bottom translation-linked strata with $D=0.23767000000000027$, both replicate contrasts have the same positive direction, and the permutation readout is $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$.  The RNA-only contrast $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$ is recorded separately to prevent promotion of transcript abundance to translation control.  Thus the finite-row claim is not that codon geometry, tAI, stAI, or mRNA abundance directly observes translation; it is that the supplied panel contains a nonzero translation-efficiency residual contrast after the transcript-abundance boundary is made explicit.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No claim about translation mechanism, ribosome chemistry, ISRIB pharmacodynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality follows from this record by itself.  Codon-usage tables and modeled adaptation weights are sequence-level or model-level readbacks; matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control; ribosome profiling or translation efficiency is a bounded translation readout only when the same-row join and residualization are actually present.  Protein abundance, localization, stability, interaction, structure, physical feasibility, function, and phenotype each require their own layer-matched measurement and their own finite statistic.  The present chapter therefore licenses only the mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary statement for the supplied finite rows, with all higher-layer interpretations withheld.
