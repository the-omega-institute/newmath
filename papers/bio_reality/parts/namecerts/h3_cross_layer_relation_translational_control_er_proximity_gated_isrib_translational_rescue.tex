\subsection{ER-Proximity-Gated ISRIB Translational Rescue Under Matched mRNA Control}
\label{sec:bioreality-er-proximity-gated-isrib-translational-rescue}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each retained row carries a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis, ordinary sequence controls, a matched mRNA-abundance surface, and a translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout in the same experimental condition. The biological question is not whether codon usage, tAI, stAI, or a ribosome-profiling contact name can be narrated as translation. The question is narrower: after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA abundance have been accounted for, does any remaining $q_i \to T_j$ signal survive as a translation-efficiency residual?

\paragraph{Observed finite-row statistic.}
In run $842b739606af$, the usable panel retained $4419$ genes. The comparison used $883$ rows in the top stratum and $883$ rows in the bottom stratum. The observed controlled contrast was
$$
\begin{aligned}
D &= 0.23767000000000027,\\
D_{\mathrm{RNA}} &= -0.0224999999999973,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},a} &= 0.19967000000000013,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},b} &= 0.24280000000000035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two replicate contrasts have the same sign, while the matched RNA contrast is near zero and opposite in sign. The run used $10000$ permutations and reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{two}} &= 9.999000099990002 \times 10^{-5},\\
D_{\mathrm{thresh}} &= 0.32192809488736235,\\
\mathrm{runtime} &= 20.019\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite rows show a reproducible positive translation-side contrast together with a non-supporting RNA-side contrast. The numerical outcome is compatible with an mRNA-control gate: the signal is not licensed by transcript abundance alone, and the transcript surface is used to decide whether the translation readout remains separated from transcriptional association.

\paragraph{Controlled readback rule.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the admissible readback is the pairwise comparison between $q_i \to \mathrm{mRNA}$ association and the residual $q_i \to \mathrm{TE}$ association after the mandatory control surface has been attached. In symbols, the chapter-level test is the survival of
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \mid Z,\mathrm{mRNA},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and the simpler codon baselines available for the panel. A positive raw codon or adaptation-weight association is not enough. If the apparent signal is absorbed by the mRNA surface, then the row remains a transcription-level association and is not entered as translation survival. In this run, $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$ is the local guardrail against that promotion, while $D=0.23767000000000027$ and the same-sign replicate values record the translation-side residual contrast that remains visible in the finite comparison.

\paragraph{ISRIB rescue interpretation.}
The claim name refers to ER-proximity-gated ISRIB translational rescue, but the finite evidence here should be read as a bounded translation-control record rather than as a complete mechanism. The row statistic supports only the following local statement: in the retained panel, the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate separates a translation-efficiency contrast from a matched mRNA-abundance contrast, with $4419$ retained genes, $883$ top rows, $883$ bottom rows, $10000$ permutations, and a two-sided permutation value of $9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$. The statistic does not by itself identify an ER-localized physical process, prove ISRIB causality, or establish the molecular path by which the rescue occurs. Those interpretations require separate perturbation, localization, and mechanism-specific contacts.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The chapter therefore treats the result as a translation-readout boundary claim. It does not infer translation realization from codon usage alone, from tAI or stAI weights alone, from ribosome occupancy without matched transcript control, or from protein abundance without a separately joined protein panel. It also does not infer folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Any such statement would need its own layer-matched measurement and its own finite statistic. The present record only says that, on this finite row set, the mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency residual remains nonzero in the observed comparison while the matched RNA contrast does not carry the same positive signal.
