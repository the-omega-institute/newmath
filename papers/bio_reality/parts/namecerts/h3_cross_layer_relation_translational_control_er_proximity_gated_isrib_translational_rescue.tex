\subsection{mRNA-controlled residual translation boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-translation-efficiency-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite CDS-by-organism table in which each row carries a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^*_{Q6}$ basis after quotienting by amino-acid identity.  The row is not allowed to treat a codon residual as a translation observation merely because the same CDS has a codon-usage, tAI, stAI, ribosome-footprint, or protein-abundance annotation.  The required finite object has four attached surfaces on the same row index: the residual coordinate $q_i$, standard sequence controls, matched mRNA abundance, and a translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout.  The intended readout is therefore not the raw association $Q_i \to \mathrm{RPF}$ and not the raw association $Q_i \to \mathrm{mRNA}$, but the residual comparison left after the mRNA surface has been accounted for.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The finite run attached to this packet is experiment $842b739606af$.  It retained $4419$ genes after filtering and compared matched top and bottom groups of size $883$ each.  The observed contrast on the translation-side statistic was
$$
\begin{aligned}
D &= 0.23767000000000027,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},a} &= 0.19967000000000013,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},b} &= 0.24280000000000035,\\
D_{\mathrm{RNA}} &= -0.0224999999999973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two replicate contrasts have the same sign, while the matched RNA contrast is close to zero and points slightly in the opposite direction.  The permutation audit used $10000$ permutations and reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{\mathrm{thresh}} &= 0.32192809488736235,\\
p_{\mathrm{two}} &= 9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5},\\
\mathrm{runtime} &= 20.019\ \mathrm{s},\\
\mathrm{same\_sign} &= \mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite-row contact with a nonzero translation-side separation and a negative matched-RNA separation.  The result is useful because it distinguishes a translation-efficiency residual from a transcript-abundance association; it does not by itself identify a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Controlled readback rule.}
For each coordinate $q_i$, the admissible question is whether the translation-efficiency residual remains after conditioning on amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched mRNA abundance.  In schematic form the row-level boundary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\not\Rightarrow \mathrm{translation}\quad\text{from codon geometry alone},\\
q_i &\not\Rightarrow \mathrm{translation}\quad\text{from } q_i\to \mathrm{mRNA}\text{ alone},\\
q_i &\Rightarrow \mathrm{TE\ boundary\ contact}\quad\text{only if } q_i\to \mathrm{TE}_{\mathrm{resid}}\text{ survives the controls.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported values support exactly this boundary reading.  The RNA-side contrast $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$ prevents promotion of the signal to a transcript-abundance explanation, while the translation-side contrasts $D=0.23767000000000027$, $D_{\mathrm{rep},a}=0.19967000000000013$, and $D_{\mathrm{rep},b}=0.24280000000000035$ record a same-direction finite separation on the translation readout.  The small two-sided permutation value records that this separation is not a generic label-shape event in the audited finite table.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
The finite result is a translation-readout boundary record, not a general biological conclusion.  It permits the paper to say that, in this audited table, a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate comparison has a translation-efficiency-side separation after the matched mRNA surface is explicitly represented.  It does not permit the stronger statements that $q_i$ causes translation, that codon usage realizes ribosome dynamics, that tAI or stAI weights are measured ribosome occupancy, or that transcript abundance is a translation mechanism.  It also does not support protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, localization, function, phenotype, adaptation, causation, mechanism, or universality.  Each of those claims would require its own row-matched observational contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Status of the packet.}
The chapter-level claim is therefore ready as a finite boundary statement: matched mRNA abundance is mandatory before any $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-efficiency interpretation is made, and the attached run supplies a concrete finite contrast with $4419$ retained genes, two groups of $883$ rows, $10000$ permutations, $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$, and same-sign replicate behavior.  The packet should remain scoped to the mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency interface.  Any downstream protein, structural, physical, functional, mechanistic, phenotype, or universal-law language is outside the finite support recorded here.
