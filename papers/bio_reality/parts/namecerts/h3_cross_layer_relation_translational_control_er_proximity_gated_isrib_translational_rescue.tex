\subsection{mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary for the ER-proximity ISRIB rescue packet}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-te-boundary-er-proximity-isrib}

\paragraph{Finite-row object.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as row labels on a finite CDS-by-organism table, not as a direct biological measurement. Each row carries a residual coordinate $q_i$ after quotienting by amino-acid composition, together with standard sequence covariates and a matched transcript-abundance control. The intended readout is the residual translation-efficiency surface left after conditioning on mRNA abundance. Thus the admissible object is not a naked codon-usage association, but a controlled row comparison of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longmapsto \mathrm{mRNA}_i,\\
q_i &\longmapsto \mathrm{TE}^{\mathrm{res}}_i \mid (\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},M\text{-}\mathrm{density},\mathrm{mRNA}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The point of the construction is to prevent a transcript-layer association from being read as translation-layer survival. A coordinate may enter the translation-readout survival matrix only after the same-row mRNA surface has been attached and the remaining translation-efficiency residual is nonzero under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The finite run attached to this packet is experiment $842b739606af$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$26$T$21{:}16{:}55$+00:00. It retained $4419$ genes and used symmetric top and bottom strata of $883$ genes each. The reported contrast was
$$
\begin{aligned}
D &= 0.23767000000000027,\\
D_{\mathrm{RNA}} &= -0.0224999999999973,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},a} &= 0.19967000000000013,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},b} &= 0.24280000000000035,\\
D_{\mathrm{thresh}} &= 0.32192809488736235,\\
p_{\mathrm{two}} &= 9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5},\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 10000.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two replicate contrasts have the same sign, and both are positive. The RNA-side contrast is near zero and slightly negative, while the main contrast is positive. This is the numerical pattern expected for a boundary test whose purpose is to ask whether the signal is merely transcript abundance or whether a translation-efficiency residual remains after the mRNA control. The finite statistic is therefore useful as a controlled translation-readout contact: the transcript layer does not explain the observed signed direction in this row set, and the permutation readout gives a two-sided value of approximately $10^{-4}$.

\paragraph{Threshold reading.}
The same table also reports $D<D_{\mathrm{thresh}}$, since $0.23767000000000027<0.32192809488736235$. The chapter therefore does not promote this packet to an unrestricted translation mechanism. The correct reading is narrower: the finite rows show a positive, replicated, mRNA-separated translation-efficiency contrast with a small permutation value, but the registered threshold prevents treating the contrast as a completed strong survival certificate. The result is a boundary contact for residual translation-efficiency testing, not a claim that $q_i$ is itself translation-realized in every context.

\paragraph{Layer separation.}
The mRNA control is mandatory because codon usage, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome occupancy, and protein abundance occupy different observational layers. A positive association between $Q_i$ and mRNA abundance would remain at the transcription layer. A positive association between $Q_i$ and ribosome occupancy would still require the matched mRNA denominator before it can be interpreted as translation efficiency. A protein-abundance association would require an additional downstream contact and a mediation calculation through a surviving translation readout. In this packet, $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$ is explicitly recorded so that the transcript-control surface is visible rather than silently absorbed into the translation statistic.

\paragraph{Local admissible claim.}
The admissible statement is the following. In the retained finite panel of $4419$ genes, with $883$ top-stratum and $883$ bottom-stratum rows, experiment $842b739606af$ reports a same-sign replicated contrast for the mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary: $D=0.23767000000000027$, $D_{\mathrm{rep},a}=0.19967000000000013$, $D_{\mathrm{rep},b}=0.24280000000000035$, and $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$ over $10000$ permutations, while the matched RNA contrast is $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$. This supports keeping the packet as a finite translation-efficiency boundary record, with mRNA abundance acting as a required control rather than as evidence for translation.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
No statement about translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, or universality follows from these rows alone. The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate is a coordinate on the finite table; it is not by itself a ribosome measurement, a protein-output measurement, or a structural assay. Any claim about observed ribosome occupancy requires a layer-matched ribosome-profiling contact. Any claim about protein abundance requires matched proteomics rows. Any claim about folding, localization, stability, function, phenotype, mechanism, or a general biological law requires a separate measurement surface at that layer and a new finite statistic under its own controls.
