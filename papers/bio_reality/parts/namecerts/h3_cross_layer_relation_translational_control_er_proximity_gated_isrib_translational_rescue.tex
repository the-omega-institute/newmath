\subsection{ER-proximity-gated ISRIB translational rescue after matched RNA control}
\label{sec:bioreality-er-proximity-gated-isrib-translational-rescue}

\paragraph{Finite row under test.}
The record concerns a finite CDS-indexed comparison in which a synonymous residual coordinate is allowed to enter the translation-readout layer only after the same row has carried its mRNA abundance control. The biological question is not whether codon geometry, codon-adaptation weights, or ribosome occupancy language can be read as translation by themselves. The question is narrower: after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and matched transcript abundance are treated as controls, does the row family still show a residual translation-side contrast in the ER-proximity-gated ISRIB setting? The retained panel contains $4419$ genes, split into $883$ top rows and $883$ bottom rows for the finite contrast. The recorded run is $842b739606af$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$27T16{:}04{:}18{+}00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Observed finite statistic.}
The measured translation-side contrast is positive and reproducible across the two recorded replicate summaries, while the matched RNA contrast is close to zero and opposite in sign. The finite-row values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
D &= 0.23767000000000027,\\
D_{\mathrm{RNA}} &= -0.0224999999999973,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},a} &= 0.19967000000000013,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},b} &= 0.24280000000000035,\\
D_{\mathrm{thresh}} &= 0.32192809488736235.
\end{aligned}
$$
The permutation count was $10000$, with two-sided permutation value
$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{two}} &= 9.999000099990002 \times 10^{-5},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the runtime was $20.019$ seconds. The two replicate contrasts have the same sign, so the finite row does not present a replicate-direction conflict. At the same time, the primary contrast $D$ remains below the declared amplitude threshold $D_{\mathrm{thresh}}$. Thus the datum is best read as a bounded, mRNA-separated directional translation-side signal rather than as a completed high-amplitude realization claim.

\paragraph{mRNA-control interpretation.}
The important separation in this row is the gap between the translation-side statistic and the transcript-side statistic. The RNA contrast satisfies $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}<0$ and has much smaller magnitude than $D$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
D - D_{\mathrm{RNA}} &= 0.2601700000000026.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently, the recorded finite row does not support the interpretation that the observed direction is merely a Q-coordinate association with mRNA abundance. The matched transcript surface functions as a mandatory guard: if the signal had been absorbed by the RNA control, the row would have remained at the transcription layer and would not have entered the translation-efficiency residual ledger. Here the residual comparison survives that guard directionally, with replicate agreement and a small permutation value, but it still falls short of the declared amplitude threshold.

\paragraph{Admissible claim.}
The admissible conclusion is local and finite. In the retained $4419$-gene panel, the ER-proximity-gated ISRIB comparison shows a positive residual translation-side contrast after the matched RNA control is attached, with $D=0.23767000000000027$, concordant replicate summaries $0.19967000000000013$ and $0.24280000000000035$, and $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$ over $10000$ permutations. The same finite row also records that the RNA-side contrast is not the carrier of the effect, since $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$. This is enough to place the packet on the translation-readout boundary as an mRNA-controlled residual comparison.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No broader biological conclusion follows from this finite statistic alone. The row does not by itself establish a general translation mechanism, a ribosome-dynamics law, mature protein abundance, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, localization, post-translational state, or universality. Codon usage, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling names, and residual-coordinate notation remain insufficient unless the relevant layer has its own matched contact and its own controlled statistic. The present chapter therefore records only the mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary for this ER-proximity-gated ISRIB row: the residual signal is directionally present and transcript-separated, but it is not promoted beyond the finite translation-readout comparison.
