\subsection{mRNA-Controlled Boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Translation-Efficiency Rescue}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-mrna-controlled-te-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record concerns finite CDS-by-organism rows in which each row carries a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ system, ordinary sequence covariates, a matched mRNA abundance measurement, and a same-row translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout. The point of the construction is not to treat codon usage, modeled adaptation weights, or residual geometry as translation itself. The point is narrower: before a $q_i$ coordinate is allowed to enter a translation-readout survival table, the transcript-abundance surface must be attached as a mandatory control. A row therefore has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x_g = (q_i(g), RPF_g, RNA_g, TE_g, Z_g), \qquad TE_g = RPF_g / RNA_g,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $g$ ranges over retained CDS rows and $Z_g$ includes amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density, and simpler codon baselines where those are available. The residual coordinate $q_i$ is read only after the amino-acid quotient; it is not a protein-level, folding-level, or phenotype-level variable.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row statistic.}
The available run is identified by experiment run $842b739606af$ and was verified at $2026$-$06$-$27$T$08{:}56{:}11$+$00{:}00$. After filtering, the finite table retained $4419$ genes, with $883$ rows in the top comparison set and $883$ rows in the bottom comparison set. The observed translation-side contrast was
$$
\begin{aligned}
  D &= 0.23767000000000027,\\
  D_{RNA} &= -0.0224999999999973,\\
  D_{rep,a} &= 0.19967000000000013,\\
  D_{rep,b} &= 0.24280000000000035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two replicate contrasts have the same sign, so the finite-row effect is directionally stable across the two reported replicate summaries. The RNA-only contrast is close to zero and slightly negative, which is the essential boundary fact for this chapter: the signal reported here is not being promoted from transcript abundance alone. The permutation count was $10000$, the two-sided permutation value was $p_{two}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$, and the recorded runtime was $20.019$ seconds.

\paragraph{Threshold comparison.}
The declared comparison threshold in the run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  D_{thresh} = 0.32192809488736235.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the observed $D$ is nonzero and strongly separated from the RNA-only contrast, but it does not exceed the listed threshold:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  D - D_{RNA} &= 0.260170000000003,\\
  D_{thresh} - D &= 0.08425809488736208.
\end{aligned}
$$
This makes the chapter a translation-efficiency boundary record rather than an unrestricted realization claim. The finite rows support a concrete statement that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate can be compared against a TE residual after the mRNA surface is attached, and that the observed TE-side contrast is not mirrored by an RNA-abundance contrast. They do not, by themselves, license the stronger statement that a codon-coordinate signal has become a general translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local interpretation rule.}
For each coordinate $q_i$, the admissible comparison is the contrast between a $q_i \to RNA$ association and a $q_i \to TE$ residual after the row has been conditioned on mRNA abundance and the standard $Z$ controls. A positive or low-$p$ TE-side statistic can enter the survival matrix only in this controlled form. If the apparent signal is absorbed by matched RNA abundance, the row is read as transcription-layer association, not translation-layer survival. Equivalently, the admissible survival entry is local to the residualized surface
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S^{QT}_{ij} = \operatorname{Score}(q_i, TE_j \mid RNA, Z) - \operatorname{Score}(q_i, B_j \mid RNA, Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $B_j$ denotes the declared simpler baseline for the same readout family. This formula is only a finite-row scoring convention; it does not assert a universal biological law.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The attached contacts have sharply limited meaning. Per-organism codon-usage tables supply sequence-composition summaries. tAI or stAI weights supply modeled codon-adaptation readbacks. Ribosome profiling supplies a bounded route to RPF or TE rows when the identifiers are matched. Matched mRNA abundance supplies the transcription control used to compute or residualize TE. None of these surfaces, taken alone, establishes folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. In particular, a $q_i \to RNA$ association must not be renamed as a translation mechanism, and a $q_i \to TE$ residual must not be renamed as protein abundance or biological function without a separate layer-matched measurement.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row evidence is sufficient for a self-contained BioReality chapter about the mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary. The recorded table has $4419$ retained genes, balanced top and bottom sets of $883$ rows each, a TE-side contrast $D=0.23767000000000027$, a near-zero RNA-side contrast $D_{RNA}=-0.0224999999999973$, same-sign replicate contrasts, and a two-sided permutation value of $9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$. The chapter therefore records a controlled boundary contact: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates may be evaluated against TE residuals only after mRNA abundance and the standard sequence controls are present. No claim about translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from this record unless an additional contact at that layer supplies the corresponding finite rows and statistic.
