\paragraph{Finite-row subject.}
This record concerns the finite CDS-by-organism rows on which a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual system is compared with an organism-matched translation readout only after transcript abundance has been placed in the same row. The biological object is not codon usage by itself, and it is not an inferred protein state. It is the rowwise question whether a residual synonymous coordinate remains associated with translation efficiency after the row has already carried amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance as controls. In this chapter the ER-proximity ISRIB translational-rescue label is therefore treated as a bounded finite contrast: it may support a translation-efficiency residual only if the mRNA surface fails to absorb the signal.

\paragraph{Observed finite contrast.}
The verified run is $842b739606af$, checked at $2026$-$06$-$27T05{:}12{:}32+00{:}00$. The retained panel has $4419$ genes, with $883$ rows in the top contrast class and $883$ rows in the bottom contrast class. The measured contrast is

D_{\mathrm{RNA}} &= -0.0224999999999973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The negative and near-zero RNA-side contrast is the local reason the entry is interpretable as an mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary rather than a simple transcript-abundance association. The two replicate-side contrasts are

and the recorded same-sign check is true. Thus the finite row table carries a concordant directional translation-side contrast in the two replicate summaries while the RNA control surface does not carry the same positive displacement.

\paragraph{Residualization rule.}
Let each retained row carry a coordinate $q_i$, a translation readout $T$, a matched transcript abundance readout $R$, and the baseline covariate block $Z$ consisting of amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density. The admissible readout is the residual association of $q_i$ with $T$ after conditioning on both $Z$ and $R$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \rightsquigarrow T \mid (Z,R).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive association of $q_i$ with $R$ alone is not a translation result. Likewise, codon usage, tAI or stAI weights, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, or an ER-proximity label cannot be promoted to translation realization unless the same finite row carries the matched mRNA denominator or covariate and the translation-efficiency residual remains nonzero after that conditioning step. In this entry the numerical pattern $D=0.23767000000000027$ together with $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$ is the local finite-row evidence that the boundary is worth reporting as mRNA-controlled rather than transcript-driven.

\paragraph{Layer boundary.}
The chapter does not claim folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. It also does not claim that $B^{*}_{Q6}$ geometry is itself a biological measurement. The residual coordinates are used as finite labels on rows; the biological contact is supplied only by matched codon, mRNA, and translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy surfaces. A downstream statement about protein abundance would require matched proteomics rows. A statement about folding, localization, stability, ER contact, ISRIB mechanism, physical feasibility, function, or phenotype would require a separate layer-matched assay and a statistic computed after the translation-efficiency boundary has already survived. The present result stops at the translation-readout boundary: in $4419$ retained genes, with $883$ top and $883$ bottom rows, the recorded translation-side contrast is positive and replicate-consistent, while the matched RNA contrast is not positive in the same direction.

\paragraph{Finite-row verdict.}
The record is ready as a bounded BioReality chapter because it supplies a concrete retained row count, top and bottom row counts, an mRNA-control contrast, two replicate-side contrasts, a permutation count, a two-sided permutation value, and a same-sign replicate flag. Its scientific content is the separation between transcription-layer association and translation-efficiency residual survival. The break condition would be met if the translation-side contrast disappeared after matched mRNA abundance and baseline controls, or if the signal could be explained entirely by transcript abundance. The reported finite run does not show that break: $D$ remains positive, $D_{\mathrm{rep},a}$ and $D_{\mathrm{rep},b}$ have the same sign, and $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}$ is approximately zero and negative.
