\subsection{ER-proximity gated ISRIB rescue as an mRNA-controlled translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-er-proximity-gated-isrib-translational-rescue}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
This record concerns finite coding-sequence rows in which each retained gene carries a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate, organism- and coding-sequence covariates, a matched transcript-abundance measurement, and a translation-facing readout. The intended comparison is not a direct passage from codon usage to translation. It is a same-row test of whether a residual coordinate $q_i$ keeps explanatory mass after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance have already been attached as controls. The ER-proximity and ISRIB wording therefore names a bounded translational-rescue surface: a signal is admissible for this chapter only when it remains visible at the translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy layer after the transcriptional surface has been accounted for.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
The finite run used $4419$ genes after row filtering, with $883$ genes in the upper stratum and $883$ genes in the lower stratum. The recorded translation-side separation was
$$
\begin{aligned}
D &= 0.23767000000000027,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},a} &= 0.19967000000000013,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep},b} &= 0.24280000000000035.
\end{aligned}
$$
The two replicate contrasts have the same sign, so the effect is not a single-split sign accident in this finite table. The matched transcript-control contrast was
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{\mathrm{RNA}} &= -0.0224999999999973,
\end{aligned}
$$
which is close to zero and opposite to the positive translation-side contrast. This is the key row-level separation in the chapter: the observed finite signal is not read as a generic mRNA-abundance association. It is kept at the boundary where translation-efficiency survival may be considered only after the same-row mRNA control has been applied.

\paragraph{Permutation and threshold readback.}
The run used $10000$ actual permutations and reported a two-sided permutation value
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p_{\mathrm{two}} &= 9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The scalar threshold stored with the run was
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{\mathrm{thresh}} &= 0.32192809488736235.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite observation has a strong permutation separation while the absolute contrast remains below this stored threshold. The chapter therefore records a positive finite-row rescue association with a conservative boundary: it supports an mRNA-controlled translation-readout signal in the retained table, but it does not by itself license a stronger biological interpretation. The runtime for the computation was $20.019$ seconds, and the verified run identifier is \texttt{842b739606af}.

\paragraph{Ranked interpretation.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$, the admissible relation has the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \mid Z, M,\\
Z &= \{\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{CDS\ length},\ \mathrm{M\ density}\},\\
M &= \{\mathrm{matched\ mRNA\ abundance}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Only the residual component of $T_j$ after $Z$ and $M$ is eligible for the translation-readout matrix. A row in which $q_i$ associates with transcript abundance but loses its translation-efficiency residual after the mRNA surface is added is refused as translation evidence. Conversely, the present finite table records the intended separation pattern: the translation-side contrast is positive in both replicate summaries, while the RNA contrast is small and negative.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
This chapter does not claim that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ geometry directly realizes translation, ribosome motion, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, or universality. Codon-usage tables, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling routes, and matched mRNA controls are different measurement surfaces, and none of them can be promoted beyond its own layer without a separate row-matched contact and statistic. In particular, transcript abundance alone is only a control surface here, not evidence of translation realization. A downstream protein, localization, structure, stability, perturbation, function, or phenotype statement would require an additional layer-matched dataset and its own controlled finite-row test.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite evidence is strong enough to keep the ER-proximity gated ISRIB rescue packet as a ready BioReality record at the mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary. The decisive facts are the retained row count $n=4419$, the balanced upper and lower strata of $883$ rows each, the positive translation contrast $D=0.23767000000000027$, the same-sign replicate contrasts $0.19967000000000013$ and $0.24280000000000035$, the near-zero opposite-direction transcript contrast $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$, and the permutation value $p_{\mathrm{two}}=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5}$. The record is therefore a bounded finite-row translation-boundary result, not a general biological law.
