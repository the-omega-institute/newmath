\subsection{mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary for ER-proximity ISRIB rescue}\label{sec:bioreality-er-proximity-isrib-translation-efficiency-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This BioReality record treats the claim as a finite comparison problem on coding-sequence rows, not as a direct mechanistic assertion about protein fate.  Each retained row is a CDS-by-organism entry carrying a synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ from the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis after quotienting by amino-acid composition.  The same row must also carry the covariates used to remove ordinary sequence and organism effects: amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, methionine density, and a matched mRNA abundance surface.  Only after those controls are attached may a ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout be read as a translation-layer residual.

The operative finite panel in run $842b739606af$ retained $4419$ genes.  The comparison split used $883$ genes in the top stratum and $883$ genes in the bottom stratum.  The recorded statistic was
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{kept}}&=4419,\\
 n_{\mathrm{top}}&=883,\\
 n_{\mathrm{bottom}}&=883,\\
 D&=0.23767000000000027,\\
 D_{\mathrm{RNA}}&=-0.0224999999999973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign separation is important for the chapter: the observed translation-side contrast is positive, while the matched RNA contrast is slightly negative.  Thus the finite record does not merely restate a transcript-abundance association.  It says that, on this panel and under this run, the observed direction of the translation-side contrast is not absorbed by the matched mRNA control surface.

\paragraph{Residual test.}
Let $Q_i$ denote a residual synonymous coordinate, let $R$ denote the matched mRNA abundance readout, and let $T$ denote the translation-efficiency or ribosome-occupancy readout after row matching.  The admissible comparison is not $Q_i \to T$ in isolation.  It is the residual comparison in which $T$ is read only after the RNA surface and the standard sequence controls have been conditioned on:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 T^{\perp} &= T \mid (R,\mathrm{AA},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},\mathrm{M\mbox{-}density}),\\
 \Delta_i &= \mathrm{contrast}(Q_i,T^{\perp}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite run reports $D=0.23767000000000027$ for the translation-side contrast and $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$ for the matched RNA contrast.  The two replicate-scale contrasts were
$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{\mathrm{rep\ a}}&=0.19967000000000013,\\
D_{\mathrm{rep\ b}}&=0.24280000000000035,\\
\mathrm{same\_sign}&=\mathrm{true}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both replicate contrasts point in the same positive direction as the main statistic.  The permutation audit used $10000$ permutations and recorded
$$
\begin{aligned}
 p_{\mathrm{two}}&=9.999000099990002\times 10^{-5},\\
 D_{\mathrm{thresh}}&=0.32192809488736235,\\
 \mathrm{runtime\_sec}&=20.019.
\end{aligned}
$$
The small two-sided permutation value records a non-random finite-row separation for this run.  At the same time, the magnitude $D$ remains below the stated threshold $D_{\mathrm{thresh}}$.  The chapter therefore records the result as a bounded translation-efficiency residual contact with replicated sign agreement, not as a completed universal translation law.

\paragraph{mRNA-control gate.}
The central rule of this record is that a $Q_i$ association with mRNA abundance is not a translation mechanism.  Matched transcript abundance is a required control surface, not a downstream translation readout.  If the apparent signal disappears after $R$ is included, the record must stop at transcription-layer association.  In the present finite run, the RNA-side statistic is close to zero and negative, $D_{\mathrm{RNA}}=-0.0224999999999973$, while the translation-side statistic is positive, $D=0.23767000000000027$.  This is the row-level reason the packet may discuss a surviving translation-efficiency residual rather than only an mRNA abundance association.

The valid readout is therefore local to the finite matrix: $883$ top-stratum rows, $883$ bottom-stratum rows, and $4419$ retained genes in run $842b739606af$.  It does not identify a biochemical cause by itself.  It only states that, after amino-acid quotienting and after the matched RNA surface is present, the translation-efficiency contrast retains a positive finite-row separation with same-sign replicate support.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
This chapter does not infer protein folding, protein localization, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causation, or a universal biological rule.  It also does not treat codon usage, modeled tAI or stAI weights, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, or transcript abundance as interchangeable evidence.  A translation-efficiency residual may enter the survival matrix only when the same-row mRNA abundance control has been attached and the residual remains nonzero after the declared covariates.  Any stronger claim requires a separate layer-matched contact: a folding assay for folding, a physical assay for admissibility, a functional or perturbation assay for function, a phenotype assay for phenotype, and an explicit mechanistic experiment for mechanism.

Thus the admissible conclusion is narrow.  The finite run supports an mRNA-controlled translation-efficiency boundary for the ER-proximity ISRIB rescue record: the measured translation-side contrast is positive, the matched RNA contrast is not the carrier of that signal, and the replicate signs agree.  The record remains a controlled finite-row survival statement, not a promotion from synonymous residual geometry to translation mechanism or higher-layer biology.
