The relevant coordinate class is position-resolved nucleotide context in a guide RNA.  Its biological readout is CRISPR guide efficiency, not ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, pathway mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  The cross-protocol flag is therefore interpreted only as a finite-dataset transfer condition.  In mathematical terms, if $X_{\mathrm{pos}}(t)$ denotes the sgRNA position-nucleotide coordinate vector for a Luo row and $Y_{\mathrm{Luo}}(t)$ denotes the Luo efficiency readout, then this record licenses only a bounded relation of the form

\begin{aligned}
