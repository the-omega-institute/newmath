\subsection{Cross-protocol sgRNA-position boundary for the Luo 2020 panel}
\label{sec:bioreality-crispr-sgrna-position-luo2020}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The finite object considered here is the Luo 2020 sgRNA table after routing through the Doench 2016 source convention.  Its row count is not symbolic: the verified cross-protocol panel contains $10543$ Luo rows, with the source frozen to the Doench 2016 repository and the cross-protocol flag equal to true.  The local computation that registers this table completed in $33.705459$ seconds under run id $2e9cce255c01$ and was verified at $2026$-$06$-$30$T$20{:}51{:}14+00{:}00$.  The companion Doench-position finite check records $5310$ rows, main GC correlation $-0.0582917$, and runtime $24.6506$ seconds under run id $b6c6363256e0$.  These numbers are used only as finite-row contact facts: they certify the existence and scale of the sgRNA-position efficiency panel, not any broader molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Coordinate encoded by the chapter.}
Each row is treated as a position-indexed CRISPR-efficiency record rather than as a translation or protein-state row.  The row schema has an sgRNA identifier, a position-resolved nucleotide context, a measured or repository-normalized efficiency response, and protocol labels sufficient to ask whether a finite position-nucleotide statistic transfers from the Doench convention to the Luo panel.  The chapter therefore concerns a cross-dataset relation of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  C_{\mathrm{Luo}} &= \{(g_i, x_i, y_i, z_i) : 1 \leq i \leq 10543\},\\
  x_i &= \text{position-resolved sgRNA nucleotide context},\\
  y_i &= \text{efficiency readout in the Luo panel},\\
  z_i &= \text{protocol and source-control metadata}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding Doench-side finite check supplies an additional reference panel of size $5310$ with an observed GC main-effect correlation of $-0.0582917$.  This correlation is small and negative in the recorded computation; it is useful as a guard against silently replacing a position-nucleotide relation by a pure GC summary.

\paragraph{What is certified.}
The certified statement is narrow.  A cross-protocol sgRNA-position table exists, it is keyed to a finite Luo row set of size $10543$, and it has been routed through the Doench 2016 repository convention.  In this chapter the admissible statistic is a controlled survival or transfer statistic for position-resolved sgRNA nucleotide features against CRISPR-efficiency readout.  Abstractly, the statistic has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{\mathrm{pos}}(Y \mid Z)
    &= \mathrm{score}(Y; X_{\mathrm{pos}}, Z)
       - \mathrm{score}(Y; Z),\\
  X_{\mathrm{pos}} &= \text{sgRNA position-nucleotide coordinates},\\
  Z &= \text{protocol, source, and simpler sequence controls}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The row count $10543$ means that this is a finite empirical question, not an unrestricted biological law.  The companion Doench value $n=5310$ and $r_{\mathrm{GC}}=-0.0582917$ mark the separate within-source check that GC alone is not being silently promoted to the complete position model.

\paragraph{Relation to the f\_3-stress translation packet.}
The supplied conjectural wrapper also mentions an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate, wobble-weight asymmetry, matched mRNA controls, and position-resolved ribosome-occupancy readouts.  Those objects are not present in the Luo CRISPR row certificate.  The CRISPR table has sgRNA-position and efficiency rows; it does not contain same-CDS codon windows, synonymous codon counts, wobble weights, ribosome footprints, translation-efficiency denominators, amino-acid-bin controls, or matched mRNA abundance.  Consequently the Luo certificate cannot be used as evidence for the translation-survival statistic
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)
    &= \mathrm{score}(T_{\mathrm{position}}; f_3, Z)
       - \mathrm{score}(T_{\mathrm{position}}; Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{position}}$ would have to be a same-window ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout.  That statistic remains uncomputed for this row set because the necessary translation-layer columns are absent.

\paragraph{Local null boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a positive finite-row contact for cross-protocol sgRNA-position efficiency, together with a negative promotion rule for translation claims.  The existence of $10543$ Luo rows and a Doench-routed source does not imply translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  The same restriction applies to the Doench companion values: $n=5310$, $r_{\mathrm{GC}}=-0.0582917$, and runtime $24.6506$ seconds do not turn a CRISPR-efficiency coordinate into a codon-window translation coordinate.  Any future translation statement would need a separate finite panel with same-organism and same-CDS codon counts, bounded wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, position-indexed ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency rows, coverage and mappability checks, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Admissible reading.}
The admissible reading is thus: the Luo cross-dataset CRISPR record is a finite empirical surface for sgRNA position-nucleotide efficiency transfer, with $10543$ rows and an explicitly marked Doench 2016 source convention.  It may support only claims about that CRISPR-efficiency surface after the relevant controls are applied.  It may not be used to fill the missing $f_3$-stress translation matrix, and it may not be treated as a proxy for protein, structure, physical, functional, mechanistic, or universal biological conclusions.
