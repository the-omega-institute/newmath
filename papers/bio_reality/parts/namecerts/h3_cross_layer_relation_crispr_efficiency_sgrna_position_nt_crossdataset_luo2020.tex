\paragraph{Finite row under audit.}
The row named \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.crispr\_efficiency.sgrna\_position\_nt\_crossdataset\_luo2020} is a cross-protocol CRISPR-efficiency contact row, not a position-resolved translation row. Its finite audit record contains \texttt{cross\_protocol = true}, \texttt{frozen\_source = Doench2016 repo}, \texttt{n\_luo = 10543}, and \texttt{runtime\_sec = 33.705459}. The paired Doench-position row records \texttt{n = 5310}, \texttt{gc\_main\_r = -0.0582917}, and \texttt{runtime\_sec = 24.6506}. These two rows are therefore useful for a finite cross-dataset CRISPR position audit: one side supplies a Luo-scale held-out panel, and the other supplies the frozen Doench-derived positional source with a measured GC association. They do not, by themselves, supply codon-indexed ribosome-footprint occupancy, matched mRNA abundance, or a same-CDS translation-efficiency denominator.

\paragraph{Local mathematical object.}
For this chapter the admissible carrier is the finite CRISPR row table
$$
\begin{aligned}
  C &= \{c_1,\ldots,c_{10543}\},\\
  D &= \{d_1,\ldots,d_{5310}\},\\
  y(c) &:= \text{Luo cross-protocol guide-efficiency readout},\\

$$
The recorded scalar $\rho_{GC}=-0.0582917$ belongs to the CRISPR guide-efficiency surface. It is a small signed association in the source-side guide panel; it is not a translation-efficiency statistic and it is not a ribosome-occupancy measurement. The only cross-protocol assertion supported by the finite row is that the Luo row is evaluated against a frozen Doench-derived positional source. Thus the row can certify a CRISPR position-readout boundary, while the proposed $f_3$-stress translation-survival coordinate remains uninstantiated on this dataset.

\paragraph{Why the row does not instantiate the proposed translation coordinate.}
The proposed $f_3$-stress survival statement requires a table in which each row simultaneously contains a coding-sequence window, an $f_3$-stress residual after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, matched mRNA control, and a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout. The CRISPR rows under audit instead describe guide-position nucleotide effects and cross-protocol guide-efficiency transfer. A guide position in an sgRNA and a codon position in a coding sequence are different coordinates. Consequently the following implication is not licensed by the finite row:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \text{CRISPR position transfer on }10543\text{ Luo rows}
  \quad
Rightarrow\quad
  S_{f3}(T_{position}\mid Z)>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The right-hand side would require a joined translation matrix with controls $Z$ including amino-acid or position-bin terms, GC3, length, M-density, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability constraints, rank sufficiency, and simpler-baseline ablation. None of those translation-layer rows is present in the cited CRISPR-efficiency facts.

\paragraph{Admissible readback.}
The finite readback is therefore deliberately narrow. The row supports the statement that a cross-protocol CRISPR-efficiency table exists with $10543$ Luo rows and a frozen Doench2016 source route, and that the associated Doench-position audit has $5310$ rows with $\rho_{GC}=-0.0582917$. This is enough to discuss finite CRISPR position transfer and to keep the source provenance explicit. It is not enough to report a rank-powered $f_3$ translation survival score, a wobble-broken translation effect, or any biological output downstream of translation.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from this row. No ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding state, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from guide-position CRISPR transfer, from the frozen Doench source tag, from $n_{Luo}=10543$, from $n_{Doench}=5310$, or from $\rho_{GC}=-0.0582917$. Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: for translation, the missing contact is a same-organism and same-CDS codon-window table with measured ribosome-footprint or TE rows and matched mRNA controls; for protein abundance, structure, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality, still further readouts at those layers would be required.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
This chapter records a boundary-preserving finite fact. The CRISPR cross-dataset row is real as a guide-efficiency audit row, with concrete Luo and Doench counts and a frozen source route. It should not be promoted into the proposed $f_3$-stress position-resolved translation-survival conjecture. The correct local status is a scoped cross-layer refusal: CRISPR position evidence is retained as CRISPR position evidence, while the translation-survival coordinate remains blocked until a joined coding-sequence, wobble-weight, mRNA-control, and ribosome-occupancy matrix is actually supplied.
