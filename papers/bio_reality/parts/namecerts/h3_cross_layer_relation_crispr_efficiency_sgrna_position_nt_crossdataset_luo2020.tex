\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats the Luo 2020 CRISPR screen panel as a finite cross-protocol readout table for position-resolved sgRNA nucleotide features.  The verified run for the cross-dataset object is \texttt{2e9cce255c01}; it reports \texttt{cross\_protocol=true}, a Luo-side row count of $n_{\mathrm{Luo}}=10543$, a frozen source identified as the Doench 2016 repository, and a runtime of $33.705459$ seconds.  The row object is therefore not a free biological law about editing.  It is a bounded finite matrix in which each guide contributes a sequence-indexed nucleotide pattern and a measured CRISPR efficiency readout under the Luo protocol, while the feature route is anchored through a frozen Doench-style source rather than through an invented coordinate system.  A companion verified Doench-side run, \texttt{b6c6363256e0}, reports $n_{\mathrm{Doench}}=5310$, $r_{\mathrm{GC,main}}=-0.0582917$, and runtime $24.6506$ seconds.  These two finite counts are the local numerical support for calling the Luo table cross-protocol rather than single-assay-only:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{Luo}} &= 10543,\\
 n_{\mathrm{Doench}} &= 5310,\\
 r_{\mathrm{GC,main}}^{\mathrm{Doench}} &= -0.0582917.
\end{aligned}
$$
The chapter uses these numbers only to delimit the CRISPR guide-efficiency contact surface.  They do not supply translation, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, cellular mechanism, phenotype, or universality.

\paragraph{Coordinate and relation.}
The admissible coordinate is the ordered sgRNA position-nucleotide profile, optionally accompanied by ordinary guide-level covariates such as GC content or other sequence summaries already present in the finite table.  The admissible relation is cross-protocol persistence of a position-nucleotide efficiency signal when a Luo-side readout is evaluated under a feature route frozen from the Doench 2016 source.  In symbolic form the finite table may be read as rows
$$
\begin{aligned}
 x_g &= (b_{g,1},\ldots,b_{g,L}, z_g, y_g),\\
 b_{g,k} &\in \{A,C,G,T\},\\
 y_g &= \text{Luo efficiency readout for guide } g,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $z_g$ denoting the finite control fields actually joined to the guide.  The verified cross-protocol flag says that this is a cross-dataset route, not merely an internal restatement of one screen.  The Doench-side scalar $r_{\mathrm{GC,main}}=-0.0582917$ is useful as a local guardrail: GC alone is not being promoted to a sufficient explanation of efficiency, and the position-nucleotide table should be evaluated against simpler GC or composition baselines before any stronger survival wording is used.

\paragraph{Rank and survival wording.}
The appropriate survival claim is narrow.  A positive record may say that a position-nucleotide guide feature family remains testable across the Luo table under the frozen Doench-source route, with $10543$ Luo rows and a separately verified $5310$-row Doench-side reference surface.  It may also say that the cross-protocol route has been executed in a finite run whose recorded runtime was $33.705459$ seconds.  It may not say that this finite table establishes a general CRISPR mechanism, a universal editing rule, or a downstream molecular state.  The finite statistic has the following local shape:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 S_{\mathrm{posNT}}(Y_{\mathrm{Luo}}\mid Z)
   &= \Delta\,\mathrm{score}(\text{position-nucleotide model},\text{controlled baseline};\,10543\text{ rows}),\\
 \text{admissible positive use} &\Rightarrow S_{\mathrm{posNT}}>0\text{ on held-out or cross-protocol evaluation},\\
 \text{break condition} &\Rightarrow S_{\mathrm{posNT}}\leq 0\text{ or no improvement beyond simpler sequence baselines}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite facts record the existence and size of the cross-protocol Luo route, not a complete list of all fitted coefficients, permutation $p$-values, module labels, hit rates, or per-position effect signs.  For that reason, the chapter can certify a bounded row-level contact and a disciplined survival target, but it cannot certify a fully powered mechanistic conclusion.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
This CRISPR efficiency surface is separate from translation-layer or protein-layer claims.  A guide-position nucleotide association, even if it survives cross-protocol evaluation, is not evidence for ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, cellular phenotype, causal editing mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those claims require their own matched finite-row contacts and their own controls.  In particular, modeled adaptation weights, codon-window geometry, mRNA abundance controls, or ribosome-profiling contacts from other packets cannot be imported into this sgRNA chapter as if they were measured CRISPR mechanism rows.  The only local assertion made here is that the Luo 2020 sgRNA efficiency panel supplies a finite cross-dataset CRISPR guide-efficiency contact with $10543$ Luo rows, anchored to a frozen Doench 2016 source route and interpretable only through controlled guide-sequence statistics.
