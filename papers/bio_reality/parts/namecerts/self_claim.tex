\subsection{ORF Eligibility as a Contact-Gated Boundary Record}
\label{sec:bior reality-orf-eligibility-self-claim}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record concerns open-reading-frame eligibility as obtained from start and stop codon enumeration.  The biological object is not a translated protein, not a folded molecule, and not a cellular function.  It is the much narrower question of whether an independently supplied nucleotide interval can pass a finite start--stop window test.  In the present row, the local certificate for \texttt{self.claim} is deliberately weaker than an ORF assertion: its carrier, distinctions, and readback are all marked as awaiting independent contact with biological sequence or curated annotation data.  Thus the row is a boundary audit for the layer $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$, not a realization of that layer by codon geometry alone.

\paragraph{Finite codon geometry recorded by the row.}
The upstream finite codon tables are completely explicit.  The motif-compressed set $R$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\\
&\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The larger set $M$ has cardinality $20$ and is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\\
&\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The equality $M=\mathrm{WNR}\cup\mathrm{CUN}$ is recorded with size $20$.  A separate saturation computation gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Sat}_{f_3}(R)=M\setminus\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and the reported $f_3$ boundary is empty.  These finite identities are useful because they make the start--stop classifier reproducible at the level of codon enumeration.  They are not, by themselves, biological observations.

\paragraph{Window enumeration evidence.}
A direct start--stop window experiment reports one loaded external window dataset, one input window, and one classified window.  Its declared scope is external window start--stop enumeration only, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$.  The recorded output explicitly states that the result remains at the $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$ layer.  The self-check attached to \texttt{self.claim} then records $3$ checked gates and the boundary statement that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  In this chapter, that is the decisive audit condition: a start/stop-window pass may be a test result for a supplied interval, but an interval-free seed remains only a question awaiting contact.

\paragraph{Curated interval contact kept separate.}
The ledger also contains a distinct curated interval experiment based on NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals from \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, accession \texttt{NC\_000913.3}, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$.  That experiment loaded $168$ rows from a curated ORF interval contact dataset.  Its required input classes were boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs.  The positive interval test accepted $72$ of $72$ positives, with accept rate $1.0$ and one-sided binomial upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$.  The matched decoy test rejected $72$ of $72$ decoys, with reject rate $1.0$ and the same one-sided binomial value $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$.  The predeclared paired comparison likewise recorded $72$ wins in $72$ pairs, win rate $1.0$, and one-sided binomial value $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$.

\paragraph{Held-out stability.}
The curated contact further reports three held-out splits.  For \texttt{refseq\_nc\_000913\_left\_locus\_fold}, $24$ positives were accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys were rejected at rate $1.0$.  The middle-locus and right-locus folds record the same counts and rates: $24$ positives, $24$ decoys, positive accept rate $1.0$, decoy reject rate $1.0$, and a passing split flag in each case.  Boundary cases were not promoted: among $24$ boundary cases, $0$ were promoted, so the promoted fraction was $0.0$.  The protocol was recorded as frozen before the external observation: classifier rules, input classes, baselines, and held-out splits were fixed before the evidence timestamp $2026$-$05$-$30$T$10{:}29{:}31$+$00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The conclusion is intentionally narrow.  Start and stop codon enumeration, together with the finite codon geometry above, can define an eligibility test for an interval once a separate sequence, transcript, or curated annotation contact supplies that interval.  Without such contact, the seed does not assert ORF eligibility.  Even when the curated interval experiment is considered, the result remains confined to interval eligibility for the stated dataset and classifier.  No translation event, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, biochemical mechanism, organism-level role, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless an additional independent contact supplies evidence for that higher layer.
