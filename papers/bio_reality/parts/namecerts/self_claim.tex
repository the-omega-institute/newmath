\subsection{Self-Check Boundary for ORF Eligibility}\label{sec:bioreality-self-claim-orf-eligibility}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record being tested.}
This record concerns the layer $\mathsf{orf\_eligibility}$ for open-reading-frame eligibility as obtained from start--stop codon enumeration.  The row is intentionally not an observation of an expressed gene, translated product, folded protein, cellular role, or universal biological rule.  Its own self-check reports a boundary-only status: no codon geometry is allowed to move across biological layers, and the recorded gate count is $3$.  The relevant finite row has no attached contact in the local packet, so the scientific content of the row is a test of non-promotion: the geometry may describe a possible start--stop window, but it does not by itself say that an observed biological interval exists.

\paragraph{Finite codon geometry available to the row.}
The geometric side is concrete and finite.  The motif-compression fact identifies the set $R$ exactly as
$$

R=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\\
&\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UUA}\}.

$$

$$
\begin{aligned}

The $f_{3}$ saturation check gives $\mathrm{Sat}_{f_{3}}(R)=M\setminus\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$, namely the $18$ listed elements of $M$ other than $\mathrm{ACA}$ and $\mathrm{ACG}$, and the $f_{3}$ boundary residual is empty.  The row therefore has a precise finite combinatorial object, not a vague biological assertion.

\paragraph{Start--stop enumeration contact level.}
A separate start--stop-window enumeration run loaded one external window dataset with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$.  It classified $1$ input window, and the run reports that all $1$ windows were classified by start--stop enumeration.  Its declared scope is external-window start--stop enumeration only, and the finite output explicitly records $\mathsf{orf\_eligibility\_only}$ as the no-promotion status.  Thus even when a window source is present for enumeration, the result remains an eligibility-layer statement: it is not a translation event, not a protein structure, not a physical feasibility proof, and not a functional annotation.

\paragraph{Curated interval contact kept separate.}
A stronger external-contact experiment exists for curated intervals, but it is not imported into this seed as a reason to promote the local row.  That experiment loaded $168$ rows from a curated ORF interval contact dataset, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$, sourced from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 accession NC\_000913.3.  Its input classes were $\mathsf{boundary\_case}$, $\mathsf{matched\_non\_orf\_decoy}$, and $\mathsf{positive\_curated\_orf}$.  The protocol was frozen at $2026$-$05$-$29$T$19$:$11$:$13$+00:$00$, before the external evidence time $2026$-$05$-$30$T$10$:$29$:$31$+00:$00$; the classifier rules, baselines, held-out splits, and input classes were all frozen, and the analysis used no translation or structure input.  The positives had $72$ accepted of $72$, the matched decoys had $72$ rejected of $72$, and the paired baseline sign test recorded $72$ wins of $72$.  Each of these three exact one-sided binomial tails is $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$.  The boundary controls contained $24$ cases and promoted $0$, with promoted fraction $0.0$.

\paragraph{Held-out stability.}
The curated-contact experiment also reports three held-out splits.  For $\mathsf{refseq\_nc\_000913\_left\_locus\_fold}$, $24$ positives were accepted at rate $1.0$ and $24$ decoys were rejected at rate $1.0$.  The same $24$-positive and $24$-decoy pattern, again with rates $1.0$ and $1.0$, appears for $\mathsf{refseq\_nc\_000913\_middle\_locus\_fold}$ and $\mathsf{refseq\_nc\_000913\_right\_locus\_fold}$.  These figures show that a separate curated-contact protocol can test interval-level ORF eligibility with finite rows and matched controls.  They do not alter the present self-check row unless that contact is attached to the row under discussion.

\paragraph{Local scientific boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore narrow.  The row records a finite geometric seed and a pair of boundary probes requiring curated interval contact before an ORF-eligibility assertion is made.  It may say that a start--stop enumeration procedure has a contact gap; it may say that the combinatorial codon object is finite and audited; it may say that another curated protocol, when supplied with RefSeq interval data, has $168$ rows and strong positive--decoy separation.  It may not say that the seed alone realizes translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Any such stronger biological claim requires its own contact-bearing record and must remain outside this row until that evidence is locally attached.
