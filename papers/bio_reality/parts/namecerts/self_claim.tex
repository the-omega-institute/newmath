\paragraph{Claim-local role.}
This record is a self-check for the layer $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$, not an assertion that a biological open reading frame has been realized.  The object under inspection is open-reading-frame eligibility as obtained from start and stop codon enumeration.  Its minimal form is deliberately empty on the biological side: the carrier, the available distinction, and the readback field are all marked as awaiting contact with observed reality, while the internal structure list contains no biological mechanism.  The self-check run $0f47d2f0cf41$ records the boundary sentence that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers and reports $3$ checked gates.  In this chapter the word eligibility means only that a finite window has passed a start/stop enumeration test under a fixed classifier.  It does not mean translation, ribosomal use, transcript support, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, cellular function, mechanism, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
The self-claim therefore passes as a layer-separation check.  Before an interval contact is attached, the ORF packet is only an admissible question about what a start/stop-window test would require.  After a curated contact is separately supplied, the finite evidence may support $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$ for that contact scope, with $168$ rows, $72$ accepted positives, $72$ rejected matched decoys, $24$ non-promoted boundary cases, and three stable held-out folds.  In neither case may the result be read upward into translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Those stronger layers require their own independent contacts and their own finite checks.
