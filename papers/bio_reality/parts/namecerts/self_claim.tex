\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
The chapter therefore licenses only the following narrow reading.  Before external interval data are attached, the packet is a boundary probe: it can describe what would have to be checked, but it cannot assert ORF eligibility.  After the curated interval contact is attached, the packet may report finite ORF-eligibility behavior for that curated contact and its declared controls.  No translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, organism-wide rule, or universal law follows from the codon geometry or from the interval-contact statistics unless another contact supplies evidence at that layer.  The self-check row with gate count $3$ is exactly the guard that prevents codon enumeration from being read as a higher-layer biological fact.
