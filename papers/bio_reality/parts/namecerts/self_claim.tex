\subsection{Self-check boundary for ORF eligibility}
\label{sec:bioreality-self-claim-orf-eligibility}

\paragraph{Finite packet under audit.}
The record considered here is the self-check claim for open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration.  Its biological layer is $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$, and its own boundary field states that the check is only a BioReality self-audit: no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  The self-check run $0\mathrm{f}47\mathrm{d}2\mathrm{f}0\mathrm{cf}41$ reports $3$ checked gates.  The packet has no attached contact rows and no mismatch rows, so the claim is not an observed translation event, protein structure, molecular admissibility statement, functional assignment, mechanistic explanation, or universal biological law.  It is a finite audit of what the finite codon geometry is allowed to say before a separate biological interval contact is supplied.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The self-check claim is ready only in the narrow sense that the finite packet correctly preserves its layer boundary.  The codon sets $R$ and $M$, the saturation calculation, the quotient vector data, the one-window start-stop enumeration, and the $168$-row curated interval assay can be cited as finite evidence for an ORF-eligibility audit.  They cannot be read as evidence for translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality without an additional contact whose rows are designed to test that higher layer.  The present record therefore licenses the statement that the BioReality pipeline kept ORF eligibility separate from stronger biological assertions, while it forbids any conclusion that codon geometry by itself realizes those stronger assertions.
