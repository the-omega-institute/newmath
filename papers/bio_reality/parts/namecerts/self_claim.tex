\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter therefore has two layers of content.  First, the codon-side arithmetic supplies explicit finite sets, a saturation check, and quotient data: $|M|=20$, $|\pi(M)|=10$, vectors $(20,38,26,8,1)$ and $(10,14,6,1)$, and an empty third-boundary residue.  Second, the biological contact side supplies finite curated interval evidence: $168$ rows, $72/72$ positive acceptances, $72/72$ matched decoy rejections, $72/72$ paired wins, $24$ unpromoted boundary cases, and $3$ stable held-out splits.

The admissible claim is correspondingly narrow.  The record supports only start-stop-window ORF eligibility where the external interval contact supplies the biological row being tested.  It does not support translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  Those claims would require additional contacts of their own, with their own finite evidence and their own failure modes.
