\paragraph{Finite row under audit.}
The row with claim identifier \texttt{self.claim} is a BioReality self-check for the layer \texttt{orf\_eligibility}. Its own verified payload records a boundary result only: no codon geometry is allowed to move upward across biological layers, and the self-check reports \texttt{checked\_gate\_count}=3. The associated conjectural packet is therefore not an assertion that a biological open reading frame has been observed. It is a finite audit object whose biological target is open-reading-frame eligibility as determined by start- and stop-codon enumeration, with the additional requirement that any positive eligibility statement must be supplied by a separate curated interval, sequence, or transcript contact.

\paragraph{Local admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. The self-check row permits the statement that the finite codon enumeration, quotient data, and contact audit are coherently separated: the finite codon geometry can define a start/stop-window eligibility test, and the curated interval contact can test that layer on the supplied dataset. The row does not allow a stronger biological conclusion from the codon-coordinate calculation alone. In particular, no translation event, folded molecular structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal biological law follows unless an independent contact supplies evidence at that specific layer. The curated interval contact likewise remains an external biological annotation input, not kernel content and not a route to claims about translation, folding, molecular physics, function, mechanism, or universality.
