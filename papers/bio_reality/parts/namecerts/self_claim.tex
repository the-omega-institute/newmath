\paragraph{Local boundary of the claim.}
Those curated-interval numbers show how an observational attachment can test the ORF-eligibility layer, but they do not alter the meaning of this self row when no such contact is attached to its own carrier. The row remains a contact-gap seed for ORF eligibility. Its permissible statement is: start/stop enumeration supplies a finite codon-window criterion whose biological use requires an independently supplied sequence, transcript, or curated interval. Its impermissible statements are exactly the higher-layer promotions: it may not infer translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality from the codon coordinate or the window classifier alone. The scientific role of the row is therefore negative as much as positive: it preserves a finite audit trail for the enumeration while preventing the enumeration from being mistaken for observed biological realization.
