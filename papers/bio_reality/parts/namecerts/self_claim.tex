\paragraph{Scientific object.}
This record concerns open-reading-frame eligibility as a finite start--stop enumeration task. The biological object is not translation, protein production, folding, cellular viability, molecular mechanism, or biological function. It is the much narrower question of whether a nucleotide interval is admitted by a predeclared start--stop-window classifier once an external sequence or curated interval contact is supplied. Before that contact, the packet is only a boundary object: it identifies what would have to be tested, and it records which promotions are not licensed by codon-window geometry alone.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The self-check fact records $3$ checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Consequently, the only admitted reading is this: finite codon geometry plus the curated interval contact supports the stated ORF-eligibility layer for the supplied curated interval experiment.
