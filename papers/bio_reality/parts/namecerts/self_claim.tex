\subsection{Self-check boundary for ORF eligibility}
\label{subsec:self-claim-orf-eligibility-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row and local question.}
This record is a self-check for the claim identifier \texttt{self.claim}.  Its biological topic is open-reading-frame eligibility as obtained from start/stop codon enumeration, and its claimed layer is \texttt{orf\_eligibility}.  The row does not attach a direct biological contact of its own: the finite contact list is empty.  Consequently the row is not an observation of an open reading frame.  It is a guard on how far the finite codon calculation may be read.  The verified self-check reports experiment run \texttt{0f47d2f0cf41}, with three gates checked, and records the local boundary as: no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  In local notation, the admissible reading is only
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{start/stop enumeration} &\longrightarrow \text{eligibility boundary},\\
\text{start/stop enumeration} &\not\longrightarrow \text{translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the point of the row: it keeps a finite formal pattern from being mistaken for biological realization.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
Those linked contact numbers show what a separate biological-contact analysis can look like, but this self-check row does not absorb that contact as its own evidence.  Its job is narrower: it verifies that a geometry-only codon packet is not silently upgraded.  The row may say that a start/stop-window calculation supplies a finite eligibility boundary to be tested.  It may not say that translation has occurred, that a folded product exists, that physical admissibility has been established, that biological function follows, that a mechanism has been found, or that a universal biological law has been obtained.  Any one of those stronger statements requires an additional contact that is suited to that layer.  Thus \texttt{self.claim} is ready as a local guardrail for ORF-eligibility language, not as a biological realization claim.
