\paragraph{Finite packet.}
The record identified as \texttt{self.claim} is a self-audit of an ORF-eligibility seed, not an assertion that an open reading frame has been biologically realized.  Its finite biological object is open-reading-frame eligibility as seen through start- and stop-codon enumeration, and its declared layer is \texttt{orf\_eligibility}.  The audit row reports a boundary verdict: ``BioReality self-check only; no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.''  The same row records \texttt{checked\_gate\_count} $=3$, so the packet is not empty: it has passed three internal gate checks whose role is to prevent uncontrolled promotion from codon-window syntax to biological reality.  The carrier, distinctions, and readback fields remain in the state ``awaiting reality contact'', while the internal-structure field is explicitly ``none''.  Thus the row has enough finite content to state what would have to be tested, but it does not itself provide a sequence, transcript, annotation interval, translation product, folded structure, or functional assay.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
Those linked contacts show what a contact-bearing ORF-eligibility experiment can look like, but the present row still records its own contact list as empty and its own carrier as awaiting such contact.  Consequently this chapter asserts only the following finite discipline: codon enumeration may define a start/stop eligibility test, and curated interval data may test that eligibility when attached, but codon geometry alone does not establish translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, or universal law.  The prohibition is local and explicit.  No translation event follows unless a translation contact supplies it; no folded structure follows unless structural evidence supplies it; no physical admissibility follows unless a physical or biochemical contact supplies it; no function follows unless a functional assay or curated functional annotation supplies it; and no general biological principle follows from the finite rows without a separate argument and separate contact class.  The value of \texttt{self.claim} is therefore negative but substantive: it certifies that the ORF-eligibility seed remains a testable boundary object rather than an overclaimed biological conclusion.
