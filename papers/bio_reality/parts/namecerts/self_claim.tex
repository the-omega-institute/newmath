\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The present row remains narrower than the curated interval contact just described. Its own \texttt{contacts} list is empty and its \texttt{reality\_contact\_refs} list is empty. Therefore this row is a guard on what may be inferred from finite start--stop enumeration before an attached sequence, transcript, or curated annotation interval is supplied. It permits the statement that a test has been specified and that related finite audits exist. It does not permit the statement that translation occurred, that a protein was folded, that a molecule is physically admissible, that a biological role exists, that a mechanism has been established, or that a universal biological law has been found. Any such conclusion would require a distinct contact whose data type is able to test that layer directly.
