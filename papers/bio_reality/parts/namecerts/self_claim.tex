\subsection{Self-check boundary for open-reading-frame eligibility}
\label{sec:bioreality-self-claim}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Claim record.}
This record isolates a BioReality self-check for the layer called $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$.  Its biological object is open-reading-frame eligibility tested by start- and stop-codon enumeration.  The record is deliberately weaker than an assertion that an open reading frame is realized in a cell or in a curated genome.  Its minimal carrier is the state ``awaiting reality contact'', its only listed distinction is the same contact-waiting state, and its internal structure is empty.  Thus the mathematical role of the record is to police the boundary between a finite codon-window classifier and biological claims that require contact with observed sequence or curated annotation data.

These lists are finite combinatorial data.  They can support statements about membership, compression, and start/stop-window enumeration, but they do not by themselves supply an observed nucleotide interval, a transcript, a translated product, or a protein-level fact.

The empty boundary value is a finite algebraic check, not a biological observation.  It says that the selected combinatorial closure condition has no residual boundary in this finite computation.  It does not say that a coding interval exists in an organism, that translation occurs, that a folded structure is stable, or that any function is present.

The start/stop-window row reports one loaded external window record, with $\mathrm{window\_count}=1$ and $\mathrm{input\_windows}=1$.  That single window was classified by start/stop enumeration, so the finite classifier achieved
$$
\begin{aligned}

$$
The scope of this datum is explicitly restricted to external-window start/stop enumeration, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$.  Its own row states that no geometry-to-translation promotion is made; the output layer is $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$ only.  This is the point at which the self-check binds the interpretation: a start/stop window can be eligible under a finite rule while still lacking the biological evidence needed for any stronger claim.

\paragraph{Curated interval contact.}
A separate curated-interval contact row is present and is stronger than the seed-window row.  It loads $168$ rows from a curated interval dataset with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$, sourced from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 accession $\mathrm{NC\_000913.3}$.  The required input classes are boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs.  The frozen protocol records that classifier rules, input classes, baselines, and held-out splits were fixed before the external evidence was observed: the protocol time is $2026$-$05$-$29T19{:}11{:}13+00{:}00$, while the evidence observation time is $2026$-$05$-$30T10{:}29{:}31+00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Self-check verdict.}
The self-claim row records a BioReality self-check with $\mathrm{checked\_gate\_count}=3$.  Its boundary statement is that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  The two boundary probes enforce the same condition from opposite sides.  The first says that a start/stop-window seed remains only a test proposal until a curated interval, sequence, or transcript contact supplies the observed biological interval.  The second says that an ORF-eligibility claim is inadmissible when no such contact can test the proposed interval.  The curated interval contact can establish eligibility within its declared data scope; the seed packet without such contact cannot.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
The local rule is therefore as follows.  Codon-set geometry, quotient geometry, saturation, and start/stop enumeration may define a finite eligibility classifier.  A curated interval contact may then test that classifier against observed biological annotation within the declared dataset.  However, neither the combinatorial coordinate nor the curated eligibility result entails translation realization, folded protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal coverage.  Any assertion at one of those stronger layers must be supplied by a separate contact whose evidence type can actually test that layer.
