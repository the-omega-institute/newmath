\paragraph{Object of the record.}
The record named \texttt{self.claim} is a BioReality audit row for open-reading-frame eligibility as seen through start and stop codon enumeration.  Its carrier is explicitly marked as awaiting contact with biological reality, its only distinction is the same waiting state, its internal structure is recorded as none, and its readback is also the waiting state.  Thus the row does not assert that an interval is an open reading frame.  It records the stricter point that a start/stop enumeration can be a candidate boundary test only after an external sequence, transcript, or curated annotation supplies the interval to be tested.

\paragraph{Self-check verdict.}
The self-check row itself has experiment run $\texttt{0f47d2f0cf41}$ and reports $3$ checked gates.  Its boundary statement is that BioReality self-checking does not allow codon geometry to move across biological layers.  Consequently, the chapter's local verdict is a guarded one: start/stop enumeration may define an eligibility test for an externally supplied interval, and a curated interval contact may support that restricted test on its own finite dataset, but neither the seed packet nor the codon-coordinate rows establish translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require an additional reality contact designed for that stronger biological layer.
