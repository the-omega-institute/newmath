\subsection{Self-check boundary for open-reading-frame eligibility}\label{sec:bioreality-self-claim-orf-eligibility}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite codon substrate.}
The record concerns a deliberately narrow biological layer: eligibility of an open reading frame as detected by start and stop codon enumeration. The finite codon data used by the packet are explicit. The motif-compression row identifies
$R = \{\mathrm{AAA}, \mathrm{AGA}, \mathrm{AGG}, \mathrm{AUA}, \mathrm{CUA}, \mathrm{CUC}, \mathrm{CUG}, \mathrm{CUU}, \mathrm{UAA}, \mathrm{UAG}, \mathrm{UCA}, \mathrm{UGA}, \mathrm{UUA}\}$,
with no missing and no extra entries. The larger marked codon set is
$M = \{\mathrm{AAA}, \mathrm{AAG}, \mathrm{ACA}, \mathrm{ACG}, \mathrm{AGA}, \mathrm{AGG}, \mathrm{AUA}, \mathrm{AUG}, \mathrm{CUA}, \mathrm{CUC}, \mathrm{CUG}, \mathrm{CUU}, \mathrm{UAA}, \mathrm{UAG}, \mathrm{UCA}, \mathrm{UCG}, \mathrm{UGA}, \mathrm{UGG}, \mathrm{UUA}, \mathrm{UUG}\}$,
and the finite audit reports $|M|=20$. The saturation check records
$\operatorname{Sat}_{f3}(R)=M\setminus\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\}$,
namely the eighteen codons
$\{\mathrm{AAA}, \mathrm{AAG}, \mathrm{AGA}, \mathrm{AGG}, \mathrm{AUA}, \mathrm{AUG}, \mathrm{CUA}, \mathrm{CUC}, \mathrm{CUG}, \mathrm{CUU}, \mathrm{UAA}, \mathrm{UAG}, \mathrm{UCA}, \mathrm{UCG}, \mathrm{UGA}, \mathrm{UGG}, \mathrm{UUA}, \mathrm{UUG}\}$,
and its reported $f3$ boundary is empty. These finite rows are enough to describe which codon-window patterns the enumerator can inspect, but they are not enough to certify a biological interval.

This geometry is therefore treated as a finite classifier coordinate only. It may organize possible start--stop windows, but it does not by itself say that any nucleotide interval is present in a genome, transcript, curated annotation, translated molecule, folded protein, physical state, functional element, mechanism, or universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Boundary status of the seed row.}
The self-check row states the governing boundary explicitly: the BioReality self-check is only a guard, and no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Its finite audit reports $3$ checked gates. The conjectural row for open-reading-frame eligibility is consequently read as a contact gap: before a separate biological observation is attached, the carrier, distinctions, and readback remain at the status of awaiting contact with reality, with no internal structure available beyond the warning that the layer cannot be promoted. The two probes attached to this row encode the same local discipline. A start--stop window remains a proposed test site unless a sequence, transcript, or curated annotation supplies an observed interval; if no such contact is present, recording an eligibility claim would break the packet.

\paragraph{Observed interval contact and its limited scope.}
A separate finite contact row is present for curated interval data. It reports a dataset at \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/curated\_orf\_interval\_contact\_dataset.json} with $168$ rows, drawn from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 complete genome accession NC\_000913.3, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$. The frozen protocol precedes the external observation: the protocol time is $2026$-$05$-$29$T$19{:}11{:}13$+00:00, while the external evidence observation time is $2026$-$05$-$30$T$10{:}29{:}31$+00:00. The input classes recorded for the contact are boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs.

\paragraph{Finite statistical contact.}
Within that external interval contact, $72$ positive curated intervals are accepted and $72$ matched decoys are rejected. The positive interval exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value is $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$; the matched decoy rejection row reports the same $p$-value, with rejection rate $1.0$ over $72$ decoys. The predeclared paired comparison gives $72$ wins in $72$ pairs, again with one-sided binomial upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$. The $24$ boundary cases have $0$ promotions, so the promoted fraction is $0.0$. Held-out split stability is recorded across three folds: the left, middle, and right locus folds each contain $24$ positives and $24$ decoys, each has positive accept rate $1.0$, decoy reject rate $1.0$, and each split passes. These numbers support the narrow claim that the frozen start--stop interval classifier agrees with this curated external interval contact under the recorded controls.

\paragraph{One-window enumeration row.}
A smaller start--stop window-enumeration row is also present. It records one loaded external window dataset, one input window, and one classified window, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$. Its scope is external-window start--stop enumeration only. This row is compatible with the larger curated interval contact, but it is weaker: it shows that a supplied finite window can be classified by the enumeration rule, not that the rule has discovered translation, protein structure, cellular role, physical admissibility, or a general law of gene finding.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter therefore admits only the following reading. The finite codon sets $R$ and $M$, the saturation row, and the quotient geometry provide an exact finite coordinate system for start--stop enumeration. The curated interval contact then supplies an external biological interval dataset on which the frozen classifier is checked: $168$ rows, $72$ positive intervals, $72$ matched decoys, $24$ boundary cases, three stable held-out splits, and binomial values of $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$ for the reported exact tests. The allowed claim is restricted to ORF-eligibility agreement with that external contact. Nothing here establishes translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, causal mechanism, genome-wide completeness, or universality; each of those would require a further contact designed for that layer.
