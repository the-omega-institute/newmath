\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The seed row can say that an ORF-eligibility boundary was audited, that finite codon and quotient coordinates are available, and that the linked external curated-interval contact supports the start/stop eligibility classifier on the stated finite dataset.  It cannot say that translation occurred.  It cannot say that a folded molecular structure exists.  It cannot say that the interval is physically admissible in a cellular process, that it has biological function, that a mechanism has been identified, or that a universal biological rule has been obtained.  Any such claim requires another contact whose data are appropriate to that layer.  The present packet therefore remains a bounded BioReality record: codon geometry organizes the test, the external interval rows provide the finite biological contact, and every higher biological interpretation is kept outside the claim.
