\paragraph{Self-check verdict.}
The self-check itself reports $3$ checked gates and states that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  This is the controlling interpretation of the chapter.  The packet can be used to remember which finite gates have been checked, which outside interval contact was needed for the curated ORF-eligibility test, and which interpretations are excluded. In particular, start--stop eligibility is a boundary-level classification of intervals under a finite rule; it is not a claim that an organism expressed a protein, that the product folded, that the product was physically viable, or that the product performed a biological role.
