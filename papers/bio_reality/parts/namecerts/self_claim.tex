\subsection{Self-Gated ORF Eligibility at the Start--Stop Boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-self-claim-orf-eligibility}

\paragraph{Finite claim under audit.}
The record considered here is the self-check packet for the biological layer named \emph{open-reading-frame eligibility from start/stop codon enumeration}. Its finite audit status is deliberately narrow. The packet does not assert an observed open reading frame merely because a start codon and a stop codon can be enumerated in a finite word. It records that such an enumeration supplies only a candidate boundary for a later biological test. The local self-check reports three gates examined under experiment run \texttt{0f47d2f0cf41}, with the recorded boundary statement that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers. Thus the claim carried by this subsection is not that an ORF exists, but that the packet is required to remain below ORF-eligibility assertion until a separate biological interval observation is attached.

In particular, the presence of $\mathrm{AUG}$, $\mathrm{UAA}$, $\mathrm{UAG}$, or $\mathrm{UGA}$ in such rows does not by itself identify a translated product or even an accepted ORF interval.

\paragraph{Geometric compression is not biological promotion.}
The quotient-geometry row gives a finite shape summary rather than an organismal fact. The $M$-row has $f$-vector $(20,38,26,8,1)$, while the quotient summary has $10$ quotient elements and quotient $f$-vector $(10,14,6,1)$. The recorded quotient representatives are ten binary rows:

These numbers certify that the finite codon table can be summarized through a quotient computation. They do not certify transcription, ribosome engagement, peptide formation, folding, physical admissibility, mechanism, organismal function, or a universal rule about life. The self-check therefore treats the quotient row as a combinatorial input only.

\paragraph{Boundary enumeration row.}
The start--stop-window row records a one-window manifest: one external nucleotide-window dataset was loaded, one input window was classified, and all one of the input windows was classified by start/stop enumeration. Its scope is explicitly \texttt{external\_window\_start\_stop\_enumeration\_only}; the snapshot date is \texttt{2026-05-25}; and the no-promotion field is \texttt{orf\_eligibility\_only}. The mathematical reading is therefore conditional: a finite window can be sorted by the start/stop rule, but that sorting still lacks the evidential force of a translation claim. Written as a local gate,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{start/stop classified window}
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
\text{translation, folding, physical admissibility, mechanism, function, or universality}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Only an additional contact that supplies the observed biological interval can move the packet from a boundary test toward an ORF-eligibility assertion.

\paragraph{Curated interval contact used only within its scope.}
A separate finite row records such a contact for a curated interval experiment, and its numbers are stronger than the seed row. The dataset path is \texttt{tools/bio\_reality/data/curated\_orf\_interval\_contact\_dataset.json}, with $168$ rows from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 accession \texttt{NC\_000913.3}, snapshot date \texttt{2026-05-30}. The required input classes were present: boundary cases, matched non-ORF decoys, and positive curated ORFs. The protocol was frozen before external observation: classifier rules, held-out splits, baselines, and input classes were frozen; the protocol timestamp was \texttt{2026-05-29T19:11:13+00:00}, and external evidence was observed at \texttt{2026-05-30T10:29:31+00:00}. The positive curated intervals had $72$ accepted out of $72$, with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$. The matched non-ORF decoys had $72$ rejected out of $72$, with the same reported upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$. The predeclared paired baseline comparison had $72$ wins in $72$ pairs, again with upper-tail value $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$. Boundary cases numbered $24$, and $0$ were promoted.

\paragraph{Held-out split stability.}
The curated-contact row also records three held-out folds. For \texttt{refseq\_nc\_000913\_left\_locus\_fold}, the positive count was $24$ with accept rate $1.0$, and the decoy count was $24$ with reject rate $1.0$. The same pair of rates and counts appears for \texttt{refseq\_nc\_000913\_middle\_locus\_fold} and \texttt{refseq\_nc\_000913\_right\_locus\_fold}. These rows support the scoped statement that, within that curated interval dataset and frozen classifier, the start/stop interval rule separated the supplied positives from matched decoys. They do not change the status of a seed packet that has no attached interval observation, and they do not license claims about protein structure, physical realizability, biological function, mechanism, or global law outside the observed dataset.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite data justify a self-gated record. The packet may remember stable codon enumerations, quotient summaries, a one-window boundary classification, and the existence of a stronger curated interval contact elsewhere in the finite ledger. Its own claim remains lower than an ORF assertion unless the relevant interval contact is attached to it. Therefore the mathematically admissible conclusion is a guarded one: start/stop enumeration can define a finite eligibility test, and a curated interval dataset can test that rule within its observed rows, but no translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universal biological principle follows from the enumeration alone.
