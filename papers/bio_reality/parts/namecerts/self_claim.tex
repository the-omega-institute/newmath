\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The conclusion is therefore deliberately narrow. The packet supports an audited finite route from codon enumeration to an $\mathrm{orf\_eligibility}$ test once an empirical sequence or curated interval is supplied. It also supports the negative guard that, without such a contact, the same coordinate data must remain only a boundary record. Even with the curated interval contact, the claim stops at interval eligibility under the stated start-stop classifier and matched controls. It does not assert that a transcript is produced, that a protein is translated, that a translated product folds, that the product is physically admissible, that it has a biological function, that a mechanism has been identified, or that a universal biological law has been established. Any one of those stronger layers requires its own separately recorded contact and its own finite audit.
