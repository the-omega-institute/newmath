\paragraph{Boundary enforced by the self-check.}
The self-check is satisfied precisely because the finite rows remain separated by layer.  Codon sets, saturation rows, quotient representatives, and start--stop-window enumeration are allowed to define a combinatorial search region.  A curated interval contact may test an $\mathsf{orf\_eligibility}$ assertion for the dataset in which it is recorded.  Neither kind of row licenses any stronger biological conclusion.  In particular, the chapter makes no claim of translation realization, no folding claim, no physical-admissibility claim, no functional annotation claim, no mechanistic explanation, and no universality claim.  Any one of those would require its own contact type and its own finite audit row.

The verdict of the record is therefore local and negative in the required sense: the pipeline may retain the $\mathsf{orf\_eligibility}$ boundary packet, may cite the $3$ checked gates of \texttt{self.claim}, and may use the curated interval contact only within its declared scope.  It may not promote finite codon geometry or start--stop enumeration into a higher biological layer.
