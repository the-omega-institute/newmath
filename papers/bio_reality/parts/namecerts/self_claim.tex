\subsection{Self-check boundary for ORF eligibility seeds}
\label{sec:bioreality-self-claim-orf-eligibility-boundary}

\paragraph{Role of the row.}
The row \texttt{self.claim} is a guard record for the ORF-eligibility layer.  Its biological phrase is open-reading-frame eligibility from start and stop codon enumeration, but the row itself does not assert that an observed ORF has been found.  Its recorded boundary says that this is a BioReality self-check only and that no codon geometry is promoted across biological layers.  The self-check counted three gates, so the finite audit content is the assertion that three independent boundary checks were present before any higher biological reading was allowed.  The minimal form attached to the seed still has carrier, distinctions, and readback marked as awaiting contact with observed biological data, with no internal structure beyond the boundary record.

$$
with no missing codons and no extras in the checked motif union.  The larger finite set was checked as

\begin{aligned}
M=\{&\mathrm{AAA},\mathrm{AAG},\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG},\mathrm{AGA},\mathrm{AGG},\mathrm{AUA},\mathrm{AUG},\mathrm{CUA},\mathrm{CUC},\mathrm{CUG},\mathrm{CUU},\\
&\mathrm{UAA},\mathrm{UAG},\mathrm{UCA},\mathrm{UCG},\mathrm{UGA},\mathrm{UGG},\mathrm{UUA},\mathrm{UUG}\},

\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Sat}_{f3}(R)=M\setminus\{\mathrm{ACA},\mathrm{ACG}\},

They are not allowed to certify translation, a folded molecule, energetic feasibility, molecular mechanism, biological role, or any universal law.  The self-check row exists precisely to keep that distinction local to the finite record.

\paragraph{External interval contact kept separate.}
A distinct ORF-window enumeration row loaded one external nucleotide-window dataset with one input window and classified all one of those windows by start/stop enumeration.  Its own scope was external start/stop-window enumeration only, with snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$25$.  A stronger curated-interval row loaded $168$ records from NCBI RefSeq GenBank XML CDS intervals for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 accession NC\_000913.3, snapshot date $2026$-$05$-$30$.  In that separate contact row, there were $72$ accepted positive intervals, $72$ rejected matched non-ORF decoys, and $24$ boundary cases with $0$ promotions.  The positive accept rate and decoy reject rate were both $1.0$, and the exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value recorded for the positive, decoy, and paired baseline tests was $2.117582368135751\times 10^{-22}$.  The held-out checks used three splits, each with $24$ positives and $24$ decoys, each passing with positive accept rate $1.0$ and decoy reject rate $1.0$.

\paragraph{Boundary verdict.}
Those contacted rows may be cited as finite evidence that a curated interval protocol was run, but \texttt{self.claim} is not itself that contact.  Its contact list is empty, and its own assertion remains a restraint on promotion: start/stop enumeration can mark an eligibility question only when an external sequence, transcript, or curated annotation supplies the observed interval being tested.  Without such a separately attached contact at the row being interpreted, the scientific content is a boundary probe: it records what would have to be checked and forbids reading codon geometry as realized translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality.  The row is therefore ready as a self-contained BioReality chapter because it gives concrete finite data, cites the finite contacted interval statistics as separate context, and states locally that no higher biological layer follows from the codon geometry alone.
