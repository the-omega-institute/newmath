\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The intended cross-layer order is a gate, not a shortcut.  A coordinate $q_i$ may be considered for a later structural-confidence test only after the same coordinate has survived a translation-readout comparison against the declared controls and simpler baselines.  Symbolically, the allowed route is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow SO,\\
SO &:= \text{AlphaFold mean pLDDT read back only as predicted confidence in this finite table.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The direct phrase $q_i \longrightarrow SO$ is not an admitted biological interpretation unless the translation step $q_i \longrightarrow T_j$ is separately present for the same coordinate and the later structural-confidence dependence remains after conditioning on the translation readout and protein-abundance baseline.  The present row set supplies a controlled predicted-confidence association; it does not by itself supply measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, protein abundance mediation, or a causal chain.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore has a positive but narrow status.  It records that the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates remain full-rank after amino-acid composition, $GC3$, length, and $M$-density controls, and that the controlled coordinate block has finite association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT in the two named organisms.  It does not show that synonymous residual coordinates determine translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  tAI, stAI, ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, mRNA abundance, protein abundance, and AlphaFold confidence are distinct readout classes.  None may be substituted for another without an organism-matched table, an explicit statistic, and the relevant controls.  In particular, a structural or functional claim would require a separate layer-matched contact beyond the predicted-confidence association reported here.
