\subsection{Translation-gated structural-order readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{subsec:translation-gated-structural-order-b-star-q6-sso-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier and gate.}
This record concerns a finite surface whose rows are coding-sequence proteins and whose columns are the nine residual coordinates imported for the $B^*_{Q6}$ analysis.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.
Each row is interpreted only after conditioning on a control block $Z$ consisting of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real coding-sequence codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the finite object is not a codon-count summary by itself: it is a residual readback table in which amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and the $M$ baseline have already been separated before the $q_i$ coordinates are scored.

\paragraph{Structural-confidence readout.}
The attached structural-order readout in this finite run is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the scale $0..100$.  It is used here only as predicted structure confidence.  It is not an experimental folded-structure assay, not a physical-admissibility measurement, not a functional assay, and not a phenotype readout.  The run identifier is $\mathrm{ac05b5047c28}$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$06T08{:}22{:}45+00{:}00$.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ coding-sequence rows were joined to codon data and $3736$ matched proteins were used for the structural-confidence computation, with $3736$ proteins carrying the readout and a join hit rate of $0.999198$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ coding-sequence rows were joined to codon data and $5804$ matched proteins were used, again with $5804$ structural-confidence readouts and a join hit rate of $0.998108$.

\paragraph{Residual design diagnostics.}
The controlled design is rank-powered for both organisms in the finite table.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the control block has $24$ columns, including $20$ amino-acid composition controls; $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$.  The reported residual response energy is $136607.52873622958$, while the explained energy attributable to the residual-coordinate block is $4178.935925251874$.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the same $24$-column control block has $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and residual degrees of freedom $5781$; the residual response energy is $620506.1490869389$, with explained energy $44421.14342043593$.  In both organisms the degeneracy threshold is $0.999999$, the coordinate count is $9$, and the design is marked powered-rank sufficient.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.}
With the positive partial-$R^2$ reporting threshold fixed at $10^{-12}$, all nine residual coordinates survive the structural-confidence association screen in each organism.  The finite structural-confidence scores are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first value is recorded as a modest finite association; the second is recorded as a finite association.  Both are cross-sectional codon-usage-to-predicted-confidence associations after the declared controls.  The entries do not say that a protein folds differently, that a residue contact is physically realized, that abundance is changed, or that a biological function is affected.  They say only that, in the finite joined rows and under the stated residualization, the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block retains nonzero association with the AlphaFold mean-pLDDT readout.

\paragraph{Translation gate and local null boundary.}
The intended gate has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow SO,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T_j$ is a matched translation readout, and $SO$ is a separately attached structural-order readout.  The present finite rows attach a predicted structural-confidence readout and a controlled residual-coordinate design; they do not by themselves attach a measured translation-efficiency survival matrix.  Therefore the structural-confidence association is admissible only as a bounded cross-layer readback.  A translation claim would require the same coordinate to survive a translation-readout test under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and simpler-baseline controls.  A translation-mediated structural-order claim would further require conditioning the structural readback on that surviving $T_j$ and on matched protein-abundance controls.

\paragraph{No-promotion rule.}
The finite positive entries are not promoted beyond their measurement layer.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted-confidence score, not experimental structural order; it is also not protein function, phenotype, abundance, localization, folding kinetics, or pathway activity.  A nonzero $R^2_{SO}$ here is not causality and is not a translation-mediated mechanism.  The amino-acid composition controls are part of the design precisely so that an association cannot be attributed to amino-acid composition alone, but their presence also does not create a higher-layer biological conclusion.  No translation realization, folded-structure realization, physical admissibility, function realization, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this table unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and the corresponding controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The finite run is strong enough to record a controlled residual association between the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates and AlphaFold mean pLDDT in two organisms: $3736$ matched \emph{E. coli} proteins and $5804$ matched yeast proteins, with rank-sufficient residual coordinate blocks and all nine coordinates surviving the stated positive threshold.  It is not strong enough to collapse the gate $q_i \to T_j \to SO$ into a direct biological mechanism.  The correct standing of the chapter is therefore: structural-confidence association observed under controls; translation mediation, experimental folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, and universality remain unclaimed until separate contacts and statistics are supplied.
