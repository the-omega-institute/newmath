\subsection{Translation-gated structural-order screen for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{sec:bireality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies a finite CDS-indexed surface whose rows are encoded proteins and whose columns are the nine powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The intended biological reading is not that these coordinates are themselves structural observations.  They are codon-usage residual coordinates after quotienting away amino-acid composition, and they may enter a structural-order question only through a gated chain: first a coordinate must be tested against a translation readout, and only then may a protein-level structural readback be examined with the translation and abundance covariates still present.  The run recorded here attaches an AlphaFold mean-pLDDT readout on the scale $0$ to $100$, interpreted only as predicted structural confidence, and it keeps this readout separate from experimental folding, mechanism, function, and phenotype.

\paragraph{Controls and rank.}
The covariate block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from the CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons supported by the union of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K--12 MG1655 the control matrix has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and leaves $3713$ residual degrees of freedom; the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design has rank $9$ for its $9$ coordinates.  The corresponding explained energy is $4178.935925251874$ against residual response energy $136607.52873622958$.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the same control count gives rank $23$ and $5781$ residual degrees of freedom; the residualized coordinate block again has rank $9$ for $9$ coordinates, with explained energy $44421.14342043593$ against residual response energy $620506.1490869389$.  Both organisms therefore pass the finite-rank admissibility check used in this screen, with the degeneracy threshold set at $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
The structural-confidence table and codon table are nearly completely joined at the protein level.  For \emph{E. coli}, $3739$ codon rows are joined and $3736$ matched proteins are used, giving $3736$ structural-confidence proteins and hit rate $0.999198$.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows are joined and $5804$ matched proteins are used, giving $5804$ structural-confidence proteins and hit rate $0.998108$.  These high hit rates justify treating the finite screen as a row-matched codon-to-predicted-confidence assay, but not as an experimental structural assay.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.}
Let $X_Q$ denote the residualized nine-column coordinate matrix after projection away from $Z$, and let $y_{SO}$ be the residualized mean-pLDDT readout under the same controls.  The statistic reported here is the controlled finite association
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= R^2\bigl(y_{SO}; X_Q \mid Z\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
with a positive-entry threshold $10^{-12}$.  In \emph{E. coli} the screen gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.03059081709413562,
\end{aligned}
$$
and all $9$ residual coordinates have positive entries under the recorded threshold.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae} the screen gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.0715885627979685,
\end{aligned}
$$
again with $9$ positive residual-coordinate entries.  The finite result is therefore a nonzero cross-sectional association between the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface and AlphaFold mean-pLDDT predicted-confidence values after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.

\paragraph{Gate interpretation.}
The numbers above do not close the full translation-gated structural-order chain.  They show that, once a predicted-confidence readout is attached, the residual codon surface carries finite signal after strong composition controls.  They do not by themselves prove that the same coordinates first survive a direct translation-readout test such as measured translation efficiency, ribosome profiling, tAI, stAI, or another bounded translation readout.  The admissible statement is therefore conditional: a coordinate may be considered for a downstream structural-confidence question only after its $q_i \to T_j$ translation step has been tested under the declared controls and simpler-baseline ablation.  Without that preceding step, the structural-order reading remains a controlled predicted-confidence association rather than a translation-mediated structural claim.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, protein folding process, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, localization, abundance law, or cross-organism universality follows from these rows alone.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is used here as predicted structure-confidence metadata, not as an experimental measurement of folded order and not as evidence for function or causal mechanism.  Positive $R^2_{SO}$ entries also do not imply that amino-acid composition alone was responsible, since the twenty amino-acid proportions are explicitly included in $Z$.  The only ready conclusion is the bounded one: in two organism-matched finite tables, the powered nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual design is full-rank after controls and has a nonzero association with mean-pLDDT predicted-confidence readback, with $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ for \emph{E. coli} and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  Any stronger biological assertion requires a separate layer-matched contact and a recorded statistic at that layer.
