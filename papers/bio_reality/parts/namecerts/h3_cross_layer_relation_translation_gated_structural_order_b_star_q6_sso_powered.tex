\subsection{Translation-gated structural-order readback for the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:q6-translation-gated-structural-order-powered}

\paragraph{Finite residual surface.}
This record fixes a finite cross-layer test surface whose coordinate side is the powered amino-acid-quotient residual basis $B^*_{Q6}$.  The computed coordinate list has cardinality $9$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate vectors are imported from the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation, but the present readout is not a translation-efficiency measurement.  It is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0..100$, used only as predicted structure-confidence readback.  The finite rows therefore support a controlled association between codon-usage residual coordinates and a predicted-confidence surface; they do not by themselves report folded molecular structure.

\paragraph{Controlled design.}
For each encoded protein, the response is residualized against the same declared control family $Z$: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the $9$ projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the tested signal is not allowed to be a disguised amino-acid-composition effect, a length effect, a GC3 effect, or an $M$-density-only effect.  The positive-entry threshold used by the finite run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tau_{+} = 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$, the residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$.  The recorded response residual energy is $136607.52873622958$, with explained energy $4178.935925251874$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the same $24$-column control design has rank $23$, the residualized coordinate matrix again has rank $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $5781$.  The recorded response residual energy is $620506.1490869389$, with explained energy $44421.14342043593$.  In both organisms the powered rank diagnostic is sufficient, with the degeneracy threshold fixed at $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Joined protein rows.}
The structure-confidence contact is nearly complete on the finite coding-sequence panels.  In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ codon rows are joined, $3736$ matched proteins are used, and $3736$ proteins carry the structural-order readout, giving a join hit rate of $0.999198$.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows are joined, $5804$ matched proteins are used, and $5804$ proteins carry the structural-order readout, giving a join hit rate of $0.998108$.  These values make the association test a finite high-coverage row computation rather than a purely symbolic proposal.

\paragraph{Observed predicted-confidence association.}
After the stated controls, all $9$ powered residual coordinates survive the positive-threshold test against the AlphaFold mean-pLDDT readout in both organisms.  The finite readout reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}) &= 0.03059081709413562, & n_{\mathrm{surv}} &= 9,\\
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685, & n_{\mathrm{surv}} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The bacterial value is a modest finite association; the yeast value is larger but remains a cross-sectional predicted-confidence association.  The data therefore authorize the following local statement: in these two organism-scoped panels, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate span retains nonzero explanatory power for AlphaFold mean pLDDT after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  The statement is deliberately narrower than a biological mechanism claim, because the readout is predicted confidence and the rows are observational.

\paragraph{Gate condition and null boundary.}
The intended higher-layer route is a chain $q_i \rightarrow T_j \rightarrow SO$, where a residual coordinate $q_i$ first survives a translation readout $T_j$ under the same style of controls, and only then is a structural-order readout queried conditionally on $T_j$ and protein-abundance baselines.  The finite computation here supplies the controlled $q_i \rightarrow SO$ predicted-confidence surface, but it does not by itself supply a measured same-row translation-efficiency gate, a ribosome-occupancy mechanism, or a protein-abundance mediation matrix.  Consequently a direct promotion from $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry to translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality is excluded.  Translation requires a separate translation contact; folding or physical structure requires a separate structure-level contact; function and phenotype require their own assays; mechanism requires perturbational or otherwise layer-matched evidence.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is also not protein abundance, localization, folding kinetics, pathway activity, phenotype, or experimental structural order.  The finite row therefore stands as a controlled predicted-confidence association and as a disciplined entry point for later gated tests, not as a general law of coding sequence, translation, or protein structure.
