\subsection{Translation-gated structural-order readback for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite per-protein table whose columns are the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, organism-matched coding-sequence controls, and one attached protein-level readout.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{Q}_{6}^{*} = \{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN/UUR},\ Leu_{UUA/UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR/AGY},\ Ser_{UCA/UCG},\ Thr_{ACR/ACY},\ f3_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run identified as $86830c9a8724$ computes all nine coordinates and then asks whether the residual coordinate surface has a finite association with a protein-level predicted-structure-confidence readout after explicit removal of simpler sequence explanations.  The attached readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale.  In this chapter it is treated only as predicted confidence in a structural model, not as a direct experiment on folding, stability, activity, localization, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Controls and rank condition.}
The residualization control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the tested association is not allowed to be a disguised amino-acid-composition, length, GC3, or coarse $M$-density effect.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the control matrix has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and leaves $3713$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$; the residualized $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix has rank $9$, with explained energy $4178.935925251874$ against response residual energy $136607.52873622958$.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the same control count and rank occur, with $5781$ residual degrees of freedom, residualized coordinate rank $9$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and response residual energy $620506.1490869389$.  Both organisms pass the stated nondegeneracy check for the nine-coordinate surface under the threshold $R^{2}<0.999999$ for declaring a degenerate fit.

\paragraph{Matched rows.}
The protein-level join is nearly complete in both organisms.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ codon rows are joined to $3736$ matched proteins with structural-confidence readbacks, giving a join hit rate of $0.999198$.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows are joined to $5804$ matched proteins with structural-confidence readbacks, giving a join hit rate of $0.998108$.  These row counts make the finite table large enough to test a residual association after the declared controls, but they do not by themselves identify a mechanism.  The row join says only that the codon-derived coordinates and the predicted-confidence readout have been placed on the same encoded-protein support.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.}
With the positive partial-$R^{2}$ threshold set at $10^{-12}$, all nine residual coordinates have positive structural-confidence entries in both organisms under the fitted surface.  The organism-level structural-confidence residual scores are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{SO}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K\text{-}12\ MG1655}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^{2}_{SO}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first value is a modest finite association, and the second is a larger finite association, but both are cross-sectional associations between codon-residual coordinates and AlphaFold mean pLDDT after sequence-level controls.  The result is therefore a bounded readback: the coordinate surface has detectable residual information about predicted structure confidence in these two matched organism tables.  It is not an experimental structural-order measurement and it is not a statement that synonymous residual geometry causes a folded state.

\paragraph{Gate interpretation.}
The intended cross-layer chain has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow SO,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T_j$ is a translation readout such as a measured translation-efficiency or ribosome-profiling statistic, and $SO$ is a structure-level readback.  The finite run reported here attaches the $SO$-side predicted-confidence readout and supplies strong row coverage and rank diagnostics, but it does not turn AlphaFold mean pLDDT into a translation measurement.  A same-coordinate translation step remains a separate requirement whenever the claim is phrased as translation-gated.  A direct $q_i$ to pLDDT association may be recorded as a controlled residual association with predicted confidence; it may not be promoted to a completed $q_i \to T_j \to SO$ chain unless a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout is joined on the same rows and survives the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The permitted conclusion is narrow.  In two organism-specific finite tables, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain nonzero association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after controlling for CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density.  No claim of translation dynamics, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding kinetics, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, causality, or universality follows from this run alone.  Each of those stronger interpretations requires its own layer-matched contact and its own statistic.  In particular, tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance are not folded-structure measurements; AlphaFold mean pLDDT is not a functional assay; and a positive residual association in two organisms is not a general biological law.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The record is therefore admissible as a bounded BioReality entry for a powered, controlled association between $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon-residual coordinates and predicted structural-confidence readback.  It is also explicitly incomplete as a translation-mediated structural-order claim.  The next required contact is a same-organism and same-CDS translation readout, joined with matched mRNA and protein-abundance controls where used, followed by a conditional test asking whether the same surviving coordinate continues to explain the structure-confidence readback after conditioning on $T_j$ and the abundance baseline.
