\paragraph{Gating rule.}
The allowed interpretation is a two-stage one. A coordinate $q_i$ may be considered for a downstream structure-confidence readback only after the same coordinate is kept distinct from simpler sequence baselines and the translation-layer question remains separately testable. In this run, the downstream readback is not a folded-state experiment; it is a cross-sectional association between residual synonymous-coordinate values and AlphaFold mean pLDDT after controls. The notation $S^{SO}$ therefore denotes a controlled predicted-confidence association, not a causal path from synonymous residuals to folding.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law from the finite association above. A modeled tAI or stAI table, ribosome-profiling route, proteomics route, mRNA control, or pLDDT score cannot be substituted for another layer's measurement. Translation claims require their own translation readout with the same-coordinate gate and declared controls. Folding or physical-admissibility claims require a structure-level or physical assay contact. Function, phenotype, mechanism, and universality each require their own layer-matched evidence and cannot be promoted from $B^{*}_{Q6}$ geometry, $M$-density, codon usage, or predicted confidence alone.
