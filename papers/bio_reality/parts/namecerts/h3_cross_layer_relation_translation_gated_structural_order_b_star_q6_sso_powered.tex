\subsection{Translation-gated structural-order readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.} The record fixes a finite cross-layer surface whose rows are coding sequences with organism-matched codon counts and whose columns are the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$. These coordinates are not treated as biological measurements by themselves. They are finite sequence-derived residual coordinates, and they are admitted to a structure-facing readback only after conditioning against ordinary sequence and composition variables. The response variable in this row is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0..100$, used only as predicted structure-confidence readout. It is not an experimental folding measurement, not a protein-function assay, not a phenotype, and not evidence of a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Controlled regression frame.} For each organism, the control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions computed from the real coding-sequence codon counts, GC3 from the same codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. Thus the structural-confidence association is not allowed to pass merely because amino-acid composition, GC3, length, or the coarser $M$-density variable already explains the same variation. The positive-entry threshold is the finite scalar $10^{-12}$ for partial-$R^2$ survival.

$$\begin{aligned}
Z &= (1,\log L,AA_A,AA_C,AA_D,AA_E,AA_F,AA_G,AA_H,AA_I,AA_K,AA_L,AA_M,AA_N,AA_P,AA_Q,AA_R,AA_S,AA_T,AA_V,AA_W,AA_Y,GC3,M),\\
Q &= (K_{AAA},Arg_{AGR},Ile_{AUA},Leu_{CUN/UUR},Leu_{UUA/UUG},Ser_{UCR/AGY},Ser_{UCA/UCG},Thr_{ACR/ACY},f3_{stress}).
\end{aligned}$$

\paragraph{Rank and row diagnostics.} In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the joined codon table contributes $3739$ coding-sequence rows, of which $3736$ are matched to structural-confidence rows, giving a join hit rate of $0.999198$. The control matrix has $24$ columns and rank $23$; after residualizing against $Z$, the nine-coordinate block keeps rank $9$. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$, the structural-confidence residual energy is $136607.52873622958$, and the energy explained by the residual coordinate block is $4178.935925251874$. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined codon table contributes $5815$ rows, of which $5804$ are matched to structural-confidence rows, giving a join hit rate of $0.998108$. Its control block again has $24$ columns and rank $23$, the residualized $Q$ block again has rank $9$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $5781$, the response residual energy is $620506.1490869389$, and the explained energy is $44421.14342043593$. Both organisms are therefore rank-powered for all nine residual-coordinate columns rather than being single-coordinate or degenerate screens.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.} The controlled structural-confidence readback is positive in both organisms under the stated finite threshold. In \emph{E. coli}, the structural-confidence partial surface has
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving }Q\hbox{-coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}$$
In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the corresponding finite values are
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.0715885627979685,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving }Q\hbox{-coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}$$
The empirical content is therefore narrow but nonempty: after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density have been residualized away, the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate block retains a finite cross-sectional association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT in the two tested organisms.

\paragraph{Gate interpretation.} The result is a predicted-structure-confidence association, not an unrestricted structural law. The only admissible reading is that the same finite residual coordinate block that survives the declared controls can be carried to a structure-confidence boundary for these organism-specific joined tables. The calculation does not establish translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding kinetics, physical admissibility, molecular function, localization, pathway activity, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. A translation claim would require a separate translation-layer contact such as measured ribosome profiling or translation efficiency. A protein-output claim would require a matched proteomics contact. A folding or structural-order claim stronger than predicted pLDDT would require an external structure-level contact. Function, phenotype, and causal mechanism each require their own layer-matched measurement and cannot be inferred from codon usage, residual geometry, modeled adaptation weights, or pLDDT association alone.

\paragraph{Null boundary.} The finite row therefore closes only the following local statement: in the two matched organism panels, all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates remain rank-visible after the declared controls, and the coordinate block has positive association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT as a bounded predicted-confidence readout. It does not close the stronger chain $q_i\to T_j\to$ experimental structure, because the row does not contain a measured translation-readout survival matrix followed by a separate experimental structural contact. If a future row supplies ribosome or translation-efficiency measurements, matched protein abundance, matched mRNA controls, and an independent structure-level measurement for the same encoded proteins, then the stronger gated chain can be tested by conditioning the structure readout on the surviving translation term and the protein-abundance baseline. Until such a row exists, the present record remains a controlled codon-residual to predicted-confidence association with explicit no-promotion boundaries.
