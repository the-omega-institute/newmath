\subsection{Translation-gated structural-order readback for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{subsec:bior-reality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record fixes a finite cross-layer test for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates against a structural-order readback that is deliberately weaker than an experimental folding claim. The finite coordinate list has nine entries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_{6}^{\ast} = \{&K_{\mathrm{AAA}},\ \mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN\ vs\ UUR}},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA\ vs\ UUG}},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR\ vs\ AGY}},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA\ vs\ UCG}},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR\ vs\ ACY}},\ f_{3}\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each encoded protein contributes a row only after its coding-sequence codon counts are joined to the protein-level readback. The readback used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0,\ldots,100$, interpreted only as predicted structure confidence. It is not an observation of folded structure, activity, localization, pathway role, phenotype, or causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Controls and residualization.}
For every organism, the residual test removes the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline, defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors. Thus the structural-order statistic is not allowed to be a disguised amino-acid-composition, length, GC3, or $M$-density effect. The positive-entry threshold used for the partial-$R^{2}$ surface is $10^{-12}$, and rank degeneracy is screened at $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Rank-powered finite panels.}
Two organism panels pass the declared rank screen. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the control design has $24$ columns, with $20$ amino-acid-composition controls, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and residual degrees of freedom $3713$. The residual response energy is $136607.52873622958$, while the explained energy assigned to the residual coordinate surface is $4178.935925251874$. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the corresponding values are $24$ control columns, $20$ amino-acid-composition controls, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and residual degrees of freedom $5781$, with residual response energy $620506.1490869389$ and explained energy $44421.14342043593$. These finite rank facts matter because a positive structural-order readback is uninterpretable if the residual coordinate block has collapsed into the control space.

\paragraph{Joined structural-order rows.}
The join to predicted structure confidence is nearly complete in both panels. For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ coding-sequence rows are joined at the codon layer, $3736$ matched proteins are used, $3736$ structural-order protein readbacks are present, and the join hit rate is $0.999198$. For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ coding-sequence rows are joined, $5804$ matched proteins are used, $5804$ structural-order protein readbacks are present, and the join hit rate is $0.998108$. The test therefore concerns a finite, high-coverage codon-to-protein-confidence table, not a symbolic extrapolation from codon geometry alone.

\paragraph{Computed structural-order association.}
After the stated controls, all nine residual coordinates survive the positive-entry threshold on the AlphaFold mean-pLDDT readback in both organisms. The reported structural-order partial-$R^{2}$ values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{\mathrm{SO}}(\textit{E. coli}\ \mathrm{K\text{-}12\ MG1655}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^{2}_{\mathrm{SO}}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite interpretation is correspondingly narrow: the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block carries a cross-sectional association with predicted structure confidence after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls. The \emph{E. coli} signal is modest but finite; the yeast signal is larger on this readback, while still remaining a predicted-confidence association rather than an experimental structural-order measurement.

\paragraph{Gated interpretation.}
The scientific order of claims is asymmetric. A residual coordinate may be discussed at the structural-order boundary only after the translation-readout gate has been respected, and any later structure-facing statement must be conditioned on the relevant translation readout and protein-abundance baseline. This row set supplies a controlled predicted-confidence association for the nine-coordinate block; it does not by itself prove that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ changes translation, folding, physical admissibility, protein function, cellular phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law. A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact: measured translation efficiency or ribosome occupancy for translation, proteomics for abundance, a structure experiment or independently justified structure readout for folding or physical state, and distinct functional or perturbation assays for mechanism and phenotype. Without those contacts, the admissible conclusion is exactly the finite association recorded above, with no promotion to causality or biological universality.
