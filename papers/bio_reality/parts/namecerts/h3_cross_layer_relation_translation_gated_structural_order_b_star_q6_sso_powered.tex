\subsection{Translation-gated structural-order readback for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object in this record is a per-protein table whose columns consist of nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, organism-matched coding-sequence covariates, and a bounded structural-order readout.  The residual coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  They were imported from the finite $q$-vector construction used in run $86830c9a8724$, with verification timestamp $2026$-$06$-$12$ $11{:}06{:}10$ UTC.  The attached readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$--$100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structure-confidence score.  Thus the row type is not a protein-folding experiment and not a physical assay; it is a controlled association surface between synonymous residual coordinates and a prediction-confidence scalar.

\paragraph{Control model.}
For each organism, the residual design first removes a control block $Z$ containing an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from coding-sequence codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In both organisms the residualized coordinate matrix retained full coordinate rank:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X) &= 9,\\
\#\{q_i\} &= 9,\\
\tau_{R^2} &= 0.999999.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the control matrix had $24$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and response residual energy $136607.52873622958$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the corresponding values were $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and response residual energy $620506.1490869389$.  These ranks certify that the nine-coordinate surface is estimable after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density controls in the two finite panels.

\paragraph{Joined structural-confidence rows.}
The \emph{E. coli} panel joined $3739$ coding-sequence rows to $3736$ proteins with structural-confidence readout, giving $3736$ matched proteins and a join hit rate of $0.999198$.  The yeast panel joined $5815$ coding-sequence rows to $5804$ matched proteins with structural-confidence readout, giving a join hit rate of $0.998108$.  These row counts make the computation a concrete finite readback rather than a purely notional boundary: every reported score below is computed on a matched codon and protein-confidence table after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.}
With positive partial-$R^2$ threshold $10^{-12}$, all nine residual coordinates survived the structural-confidence association screen in both organisms.  The finite structural-order scores were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\textit{E. coli K-12 MG1655}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^2_{SO}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
The \emph{E. coli} value is a modest finite association; the yeast value is a larger finite association on the same predicted-confidence scale.  In both cases the surviving-coordinate count is $9$, so the finite readout is not restricted to a single codon family or to the Leu CUN/UUR coordinate alone.  The result should be read as an organism-scoped codon-residual association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, GC3, and $M$-density controls.

\paragraph{Gate interpretation.}
The intended cross-layer route is a gated chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow SO,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $SO$ is a structural-order readback evaluated only after the translation step and protein-abundance baseline are accounted for.  The finite rows reported here fill the structural-confidence side with AlphaFold mean pLDDT and show that the $q_i$ surface is numerically estimable against that score.  They do not, by themselves, certify the first arrow through measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, or protein-abundance mediation.  A completed translation-gated claim therefore requires the same coordinate to survive a translation-readout test under the declared $Z$ controls before the structural-confidence association may be interpreted as downstream of translation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation dynamics, ribosome mechanism, folding process, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, causal effect, or universal biological law follows from these rows alone.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted structure-confidence scalar, not experimental structural order, not mature protein abundance, not protein localization, not folding kinetics, and not pathway activity.  Positive entries in this table are cross-sectional associations after controls; they do not establish a translation-mediated mechanism and do not license a function-realization claim.  The amino-acid composition controls are part of the certificate precisely so that the surface is not merely an amino-acid-composition readback.  Any stronger assertion must be supplied by a distinct layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
