\subsection{Translation-Gated Structural-Order Boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translation-gated-structural-order-b-star-q6-sso-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This chapter records a finite cross-layer test surface whose rows are encoded proteins and whose columns are the nine residual coordinates imported from the $B^*_{Q6}$ construction.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{Q}_{6}^{*}=\{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3_{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each protein row, the observed coordinate values are residual codon-usage quantities after quotienting away amino-acid identity as far as the declared controls allow.  The tested readback in this record is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0\ldots 100$, used only as predicted structure-confidence score.  It is not an experimental measurement of a folded state.  The experiment identifier is $ac05b5047c28$, with verification timestamp $2026$-$06$-$05T22{:}14{:}25+00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Controls and residualization.}
The nuisance design $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from the same CDS counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors.  Thus a positive entry is not allowed to arise merely because long proteins, amino-acid composition, GC3, or the coarse $M$-support baseline differ across genes.  For an organism $o$, the fitted association is summarized as a controlled finite-row readback
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{SO}(o) &= R^2\bigl(\widetilde X_{Q6}(o) \to \widetilde y_{SO}(o)\bigr),\\
\widetilde X_{Q6}(o) &= (I-P_Z)X_{Q6}(o),\\
\widetilde y_{SO}(o) &= (I-P_Z)y_{SO}(o),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $y_{SO}$ is the AlphaFold mean-pLDDT vector and $X_{Q6}$ has the nine residual-coordinate columns.  The positivity threshold used in this finite run is partial $R^2>10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Rank diagnostics.}
The residual coordinate matrix remained powered in both organisms.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the control matrix had $24$ columns, $23$ residual rank, $20$ amino-acid composition controls, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, $q$-coordinate count $9$, residual coordinate rank $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, response residual energy $136607.52873622958$, and degenerate-$R^2$ threshold $0.999999$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the corresponding values were $24$ control columns, $23$ residual rank, $20$ amino-acid controls, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, $9$ residual coordinates, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, response residual energy $620506.1490869389$, and the same degenerate-$R^2$ threshold $0.999999$.  In both organisms the powered-rank flag is true, so the nine-coordinate residual surface is not collapsed by the declared controls.

\paragraph{Joined finite rows.}
The structural-confidence readback was nearly completely joined to the codon rows in the two organism slices.  In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ codon rows were joined, $3736$ matched proteins were used, $3736$ structural-confidence protein rows were present, and the join hit rate was $0.999198$.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows were joined, $5804$ matched proteins were used, $5804$ structural-confidence protein rows were present, and the join hit rate was $0.998108$.  These counts make the test a finite matched-row association rather than a statement about all proteins, all organisms, or all structural assays.

\paragraph{Observed controlled association.}
Under the declared controls, all nine residual coordinates survived the positive-threshold screen in each organism for this predicted-confidence readback.  The \emph{E. coli} slice had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
\#\{q_i:S^{SO}_i>10^{-12}\} &= 9,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the finite signal classified as modest and non-causal.  The yeast slice had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685,\\
\#\{q_i:S^{SO}_i>10^{-12}\} &= 9,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the same restriction to a cross-sectional predicted-confidence association.  The larger yeast value is a stronger association in this readout surface, not a law equating $B^*_{Q6}$ geometry with protein structure.

\paragraph{Translation gate and structural-order boundary.}
The intended cross-layer ordering is a gated chain: a coordinate $q_i$ may be examined at a structural-order boundary only after the same coordinate has survived a translation-readout test $q_i\to T_j$ under the declared controls and simpler-baseline ablation.  The present finite run supplies a controlled association between $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and AlphaFold mean pLDDT predicted confidence; it does not by itself supply ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance mediation, folding kinetics, perturbation response, or a molecular mechanism.  Consequently, the result should be read as a finite predicted-confidence association available for later translation-conditioned analysis, not as a completed translation-mediated structural-order theorem.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization, folded-structure claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function claim, mechanistic claim, phenotype claim, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter alone.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is treated here as a predicted structure-confidence score, not as experimental structural order, protein function, abundance, localization, folding kinetics, pathway activity, or phenotype.  Positive $S^{SO}$ entries record controlled cross-sectional dependence after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  Any stronger assertion requires a separate layer-matched contact: measured translation readout for $q_i\to T_j$, matched protein abundance for downstream mediation, and an independent structural or physical assay before folded structure or physical admissibility can be asserted.
