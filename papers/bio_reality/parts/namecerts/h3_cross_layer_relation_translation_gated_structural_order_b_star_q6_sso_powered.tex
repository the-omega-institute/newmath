\subsection{Translation-Gated Structural-Order Readback for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record studies a finite cross-layer surface whose rows are coding-sequence proteins and whose columns are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates carried after the amino-acid quotient. The coordinates used in the run are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_{AAA},\quad Arg\_{AGR},\quad Ile\_{AUA},\quad Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\quad Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\quad Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\quad Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\quad f3\_{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The biological question is not whether these residual coordinates themselves are protein structure. They are codon-usage residual coordinates. The only admissible structural-order entry is a later readback, after the coding sequence has been matched to an encoded protein and after the residual coordinate has been evaluated against the declared controls. In the present finite run, the attached readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structure confidence. It is not an experimental folded-structure measurement.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
The control set $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 computed from real CDS codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. Thus a positive entry is not allowed to be read as an amino-acid-composition effect or as a length, GC3, or $M$-density effect. The residualized design is powered in both organisms: for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the control matrix has $24$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and $3713$ residual degrees of freedom after $Z$; for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the corresponding values are $24$, $23$, $9$, and $5781$. The degenerate-$R^2$ threshold used for these rank diagnostics is $0.999999$, and both organisms are marked rank-sufficient.

\paragraph{Finite readback.}
For \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $3739$ codon rows joined to the protein table, $3736$ matched proteins were used, and $3736$ structural-confidence protein rows were available, giving a join hit rate of $0.999198$. The residualized structural-confidence association has
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.03059081709413562,
\end{aligned}
$$
with all nine residual coordinates counted as surviving the positive threshold in this finite association surface. The explained energy is $4178.935925251874$, against residual readout energy $136607.52873622958$.

For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows joined to the protein table, $5804$ matched proteins were used, and $5804$ structural-confidence protein rows were available, giving a join hit rate of $0.998108$. The corresponding scalar is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685,
\end{aligned}
$$
again with all nine residual coordinates counted as surviving the positive threshold in this finite association surface. The explained energy is $44421.14342043593$, against residual readout energy $620506.1490869389$. The positive-entry threshold recorded for the run is $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{What the association licenses.}
The result licenses a narrow statement: after residualizing by length, twenty amino-acid composition controls, GC3, and $M$-density, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-usage surface has a finite cross-sectional association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT predicted-confidence readback in the two named organisms. The stronger number is observed in yeast, with $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$, and the smaller but still finite association is observed in \emph{E. coli}, with $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$. The row support is large relative to the nine-coordinate surface in both organisms, and the residualized coordinate rank remains $9$ after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation-realization claim follows from this structural-confidence association by itself. No folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal biological law follows from these rows. AlphaFold mean pLDDT is predicted confidence rather than an experimental structure assay, and it is not protein abundance, localization, folding kinetics, pathway activity, or molecular function. A translation-mediated structural-order claim would require the same coordinate to pass a translation-readout gate first, and then a later structure-level readback would have to remain dependent on the coordinate after conditioning on the translation readout and protein-abundance baseline. A separate contact is required for each higher biological layer. The present chapter therefore records a powered codon-residual to predicted-confidence association, not a causal mechanism and not a promoted function or phenotype statement.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite row is ready as a bounded BioReality entry for $q_i \mapsto S^{SO}$ predicted-confidence association under the declared controls. It should be cited only with the organism names, the nine-coordinate list, the row counts, the hit rates, the rank diagnostics, and the two $R^2_{SO}$ values above. Its boundary condition is part of the claim: the association is cross-sectional, structure-confidence-only, and non-causal unless a later layer supplies its own matched measurement contact and controlled statistic.
