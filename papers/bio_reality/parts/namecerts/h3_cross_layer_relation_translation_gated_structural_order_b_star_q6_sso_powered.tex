\subsection{Translation-Gated Structural-Order Readback for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Coordinates}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite surface indexed by nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and by organism-matched protein readbacks.  The coordinate side is not a protein-structure measurement.  It is the residual codon-coordinate list
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6} = \{&K_{\mathrm{AAA}},\ \mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUR}},\ \\
&\mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUG}},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{AGY}},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UCG}},\ \\
&\mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{ACY}},\ f_3\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run records $|R_{Q6}|=9$, with the coordinate vectors imported from the finite computation named \texttt{run\_b\_star\_q6\_translation\_survival\_powered.py}.  The protein-side readout used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0$ to $100$, interpreted only as predicted structure confidence.  It is not an experimental folding assay, not a molecular-function assay, and not a phenotype measurement.

\paragraph{Controls and residualization.}
For each coding sequence, the fitted design first removes a fixed control surface $Z$.  The declared controls are an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions from real coding-sequence codon counts, GC3 from the same codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline.  The $M$-density baseline is the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the reported structural-order association is not allowed to arise merely from protein length, amino-acid composition, GC3, or the coarse $M$-support baseline.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.}
With the above residualization, the run reports a positive predicted-structure-confidence association in both organisms.  The positive-entry threshold is $10^{-12}$ in partial-$R^2$ units.  The organism-level readbacks are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\textit{E. coli}\ \mathrm{K}\text{-}12\ \mathrm{MG1655}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^2_{SO}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run records $9$ surviving coordinates for each organism at this structural-order-confidence readback.  The \textit{E. coli} signal is described as modest and finite; the yeast signal is likewise finite.  Both are cross-sectional associations from codon-usage residuals to predicted confidence after controls.  They are not evidence of causality.

\paragraph{Gate semantics.}
The mathematical ordering of the claim is a two-stage gate.  First, a residual coordinate $q_i \in R_{Q6}$ must be considered only through a translation-readout stage $T_j$ under the control surface $Z$.  Only after that stage is respected may a downstream structural-order-confidence readout be evaluated with protein-abundance and baseline controls held separate.  In the present finite row, the attached structural-order readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT; it is therefore a prediction-confidence readback, not a physical structure experiment.  The finite statistic may be written schematically as
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{SO}(q_i) &> 10^{-12}
\quad\text{after residualizing by}\quad
Z = \{1,\log L,\mathrm{AA}_{20},\mathrm{GC3},M\text{-density}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This expression records the fitted association boundary only.  It does not identify a mechanism by which codon choice changes folding, abundance, localization, or biological activity.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The result may be cited as a finite, organism-scoped association between nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual codon coordinates and AlphaFold mean pLDDT predicted confidence after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  It may not be cited as translation realization by itself.  It may not be cited as folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, phenotype, mechanism, localization, folding kinetics, pathway activity, or a universal biological law.  A stronger conclusion would require a distinct layer-matched contact: for example, measured ribosome profiling or translation efficiency for translation, matched proteomics for abundance, an experimental or explicitly accepted structure contact for structure, and separate functional or perturbation assays for function or phenotype.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite run is sufficient for a self-contained BioReality entry at the predicted-structure-confidence boundary: two organisms, nine residual coordinates, full amino-acid composition controls, powered residual rank, high protein-join hit rates, and positive organism-level $R^2_{SO}$ values are present.  The correct reading is narrow: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual codon coordinates retain finite association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after the stated controls in the recorded panels, while all mechanistic and higher-layer biological claims remain outside the evidence supplied by this row.
