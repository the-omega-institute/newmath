\subsection{Translation-gated predicted-structure-confidence readback for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:q6-translation-gated-structural-order-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite surface of coding-sequence residual coordinates, organism-matched protein identifiers, and a bounded predicted-structure-confidence readout.  The residual coordinate family has exactly nine entries:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.  These coordinates are treated as amino-acid-quotient codon-use residuals, not as protein-shape measurements.  The finite run, identified as $ac05b5047c28$ and verified at $2026$-$06$-$06T09{:}43{:}24+00{:}00$, attaches them to AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the scale $0,\ldots,100$.  The readout is therefore predicted structural confidence only.  It is not an experimental folded-structure assay and it is not a measurement of protein function, phenotype, localization, abundance, folding kinetics, or pathway activity.

\paragraph{Controlled design.}
The controlled surface residualizes the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates against an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from real coding-sequence codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the structural-confidence association is not allowed to be a restatement of amino-acid composition, sequence length, GC3, or the coarse $M$-support baseline.  In the Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 panel the control matrix has $24$ columns, rank $23$, and leaves $3713$ residual degrees of freedom after controls; the residualized $Q6$ block has rank $9$, with explained energy $4178.935925251874$ against residual response energy $136607.52873622958$.  In the Saccharomyces cerevisiae panel the same $24$-column control design has rank $23$, leaves $5781$ residual degrees of freedom, and gives residualized $Q6$ rank $9$, with explained energy $44421.14342043593$ against residual response energy $620506.1490869389$.  The degeneracy threshold used for the rank diagnostic is $0.999999$, and both organisms are rank-sufficient for the nine-coordinate test.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.}
The protein matching is nearly complete in both organism panels.  For Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $3739$ coding-sequence rows enter the codon join, $3736$ matched proteins are used, $3736$ proteins carry the predicted-structure-confidence readout, and the join hit rate is $0.999198$.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, $5815$ coding-sequence rows enter the codon join, $5804$ matched proteins are used, $5804$ proteins carry the readout, and the join hit rate is $0.998108$.  With a positive partial-$R^{2}$ reporting threshold of $10^{-12}$, all nine residual coordinates survive the predicted-confidence readback in both organisms.  The finite structural-confidence surface is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{SO}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^{2}_{SO}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685.

The first value is a modest finite association and the second is a finite association of larger magnitude on this readout.  Both remain cross-sectional associations between codon-use residual coordinates and predicted structure confidence after the listed controls.  Neither number is a causal estimate.

\paragraph{Gate interpretation.}
The intended chain is $q_i \to T_j \to SO$, where $q_i$ is a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $SO$ is a structural-order readback supplied at a separate layer.  The computed table here supplies a powered predicted-confidence readback for the same residual coordinate family after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  It does not by itself complete the stronger translation-mediated chain, because the finite row does not report a same-coordinate measured translation-readout survival matrix and then a second structural-confidence test conditional on that translation readout and protein abundance.  The correct local reading is therefore narrower: the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block has nonzero controlled association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT in two organism panels, under a rank-sufficient design, while the mediation claim remains conditional on an attached translation readout and a downstream conditional analysis.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism follows from these numbers alone.  No ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, localization, folding kinetics, pathway activity, or universal biological law follows from this finite association unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted-confidence signal, not experimental structural order.  Positive $R^{2}_{SO}$ entries also do not show that codon-use residuals cause structure, that translation mediates the association, or that the association is organism-independent.  The finite claim is exactly the controlled, organism-scoped predicted-confidence association above, with the stronger translation-conditioned structural-order claim held behind its missing readout and mediation tests.
