\subsection{Translation-gated structural-order readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record fixes a finite surface whose rows are encoded proteins and whose columns are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  Each row is joined to coding-sequence covariates and to a protein-level readout.  The run identifier is $86830c9a8724$, verified at 2026-06-12T11:06:10+00:00.  The declared layer is cross-layer relation, but the word ``cross'' here names only a controlled statistical join: it does not by itself assert translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Controls and residualized test surface.}
The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 computed from the same coding sequences, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the structural-order readback is not allowed to be an amino-acid-composition restatement and is not allowed to be a length or GC3 surrogate.  The fitted statistic is a residual association of the nine $q_i$ columns with AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0..100$, interpreted only as predicted structure-confidence readback.

$$
\begin{aligned}
X &= (q_1,\ldots,q_9),\\
Z &= (1,\log L,AA_A,\ldots,AA_Y,GC3,M),\\
S^{SO} &= R^2\bigl(\Pi_{Z}^{\perp}y_{pLDDT}\; ;\; \Pi_{Z}^{\perp}X\bigr).
\end{aligned}
$$

A coordinate is counted on this page only after the residualized design remains nondegenerate.  The positive partial-$R^2$ threshold recorded for the run is $10^{-12}$; this is a numerical positivity cutoff, not a biological-effect threshold.

\#\{q_i:S^{SO}_{i}>10^{-12}\} &= 9.

The signal is therefore finite and rank-powered, but modest and cross-sectional.

This is a finite association with predicted confidence, not a statement about measured folding order or biochemical activity.

\paragraph{Gated interpretation.}
The intended gate is a chain $q_i \to T_j \to SO$, where $q_i$ is a $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $SO$ is a separately supplied structural-order readout.  The present finite run attaches AlphaFold mean pLDDT as the protein-level readback and shows that the nine-coordinate residual surface has nonzero controlled association with that predicted-confidence readout in two organisms.  The run does not, however, turn tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, or pLDDT into the same measurement.  Translation-readout survival and structural-order association are distinct tests.  A same-coordinate translation gate must be established before any translation-mediated structural reading is asserted.

\paragraph{Local null boundary.}
The positive $S^{SO}$ entries are not promoted beyond their measured scope.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is predicted structure confidence, not experimental structural order, not protein function, not phenotype, not abundance, not localization, not folding kinetics, and not pathway activity.  The associations above are cross-sectional codon-usage residual associations after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  They do not establish causality, a ribosome-mediated mechanism, a physical-admissibility claim, a folding law, or a general rule across life.  Any future claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality must be supplied by a separate layer-matched contact with its own controlled statistic.
