It is not an experimental folding measurement and is not a measurement of protein function, phenotype, abundance, localization, folding kinetics, or pathway activity.

\paragraph{Gate definition.}
The admissible path from a residual coordinate to a structural-order statement is ordered.  A coordinate $q_i$ is first tested against a translation readout $T_j$ under the declared covariates; only after that gate can a structure-level readback be examined conditionally on $T_j$ and protein-abundance controls.  In the finite computation reported here, the attached structure-level readback is predicted confidence rather than a physical structure experiment.  The local null rule is therefore strict: the calculation does not establish measured translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality.  Each of those assertions would require a separate layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Boundary interpretation.}
The result is a per-protein codon-usage residual association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  The amino-acid composition block is essential because it prevents promotion from amino-acid content alone to a residual-coordinate claim.  The finite surface is also deliberately separated from translation measurements: tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance are not folded-structure measurements, and none of them is substituted here for a physical structure assay.  A positive $S^{SO}$ entry therefore means only that the controlled codon-residual surface retains predictive association with a predicted-confidence readback in the two named organism panels.
