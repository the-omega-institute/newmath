\paragraph{Gating rule.}
The intended logical order is $q_i \to T_j \to SO$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T_j$ is a translation readout, and $SO$ is a structural-order readback attached at a separate protein layer. A direct codon-residual association with predicted structure confidence is therefore not promoted into a structural mechanism. The same coordinate must first survive a translation-readout test under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and simpler-baseline controls before a later structural-order dependence may be interpreted as translation-gated. If no coordinate survives the translation gate, then every structural-order entry is null for the gated claim even when a direct predicted-confidence association is numerically positive.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
This finite record permits a bounded statement: in the two powered organism panels, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates jointly have positive residual association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after the declared controls, with high codon-to-protein join rates and full residual coordinate rank. It does not permit a claim of observed translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, adaptation, causation, or universality. tAI, stAI, ribosome profiling, matched mRNA abundance, proteomics abundance, and predicted pLDDT occupy different measurement roles. A claim at any higher layer requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic; codon usage, residual geometry, rank sufficiency, and mean pLDDT association cannot supply that contact on their own.
