\paragraph{Gate interpretation.}
The biological direction of the proposed chain is $q_i \to T_j \to SO$, where $q_i$ is a $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinate, $T_j$ is a translation readout, and $SO$ is a structure-order readback.  The finite run reported here supplies the controlled $q_i$ to pLDDT readback and records that the matrix is rank-powered in two organisms.  It does not by itself establish the intervening translation-readout step.  A same-coordinate translation contact, such as measured ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency with matched mRNA controls, is still required before this surface can be described as translation-mediated.  Similarly, matched protein abundance may only be used as a downstream covariate or readback when its own joined rows and controls are present.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The valid conclusion is deliberately narrow.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted-confidence score, not an experimental structure assay.  A positive $S^{SO}$ entry here is a cross-sectional codon-usage residual association with predicted structure confidence after controls; it is not causality, not folding kinetics, not physical admissibility, not protein function, not phenotype, not abundance, and not a universal biological law.  The same boundary applies to translation contacts: tAI or stAI weights, ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, and matched proteomics each test only the layer they actually measure or model.  None of them may be promoted into translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, function, phenotype, or universality without a separate layer-matched contact and a recorded finite statistic.
