\paragraph{Finite carrier.} This record treats the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual basis as a finite protein-indexed table rather than as a free-standing biological explanation. The computed residual side has exactly nine coordinates: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. These coordinates were imported from the powered $B^\ast_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation and then evaluated against a protein-level readout. The readout used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale. It is interpreted only as predicted structure confidence, not as an experimental measurement of folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, localization, pathway activity, or causality.

\paragraph{Controlled surface.} For each organism, the residual-coordinate design was tested after removing a fixed covariate block $Z$. The block contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$ computed from CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^\ast_{Q6}$ vectors. Thus the reported surface is not an amino-acid-composition claim and is not a codon-count-only claim. The positive threshold for a partial-$R^2$ entry was $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Rank and row diagnostics.} In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the joined codon side contains $3739$ CDS rows, with $3736$ matched proteins used for the predicted-confidence readout. The join hit rate is $0.999198$. The control matrix has $24$ columns, the amino-acid composition contributes $20$ of them, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, and the residual coordinate design has $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for $9$ tested coordinates. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$, the explained energy is $4178.935925251874$, and the residual readout energy is $136607.52873622958$. The rank check is therefore powered for the nine-coordinate residual surface rather than saturated.

\paragraph{Second organism check.} In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined codon side contains $5815$ CDS rows, with $5804$ matched proteins used for the predicted-confidence readout. The join hit rate is $0.998108$. The same $24$-column control block is used, again with $20$ amino-acid composition columns. The diagnostic ranks are $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, with $5781$ residual degrees of freedom after the controls. The explained energy is $44421.14342043593$, and the residual readout energy is $620506.1490869389$. This gives the second organism the same nondegenerate nine-coordinate testing status under the declared covariates.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.} The computed predicted-confidence association is finite in both organisms. In \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, all $9$ residual coordinates survive the positive threshold for the AlphaFold mean-pLDDT readout, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.03059081709413562.
\end{aligned}
$$
The signal is modest and remains cross-sectional. In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, all $9$ residual coordinates also survive the positive threshold, with

\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$
This second signal is larger, but it has the same interpretation limit: it is a controlled association between per-protein codon-usage residual coordinates and a predicted-confidence score.

\paragraph{Gating interpretation.} The proper local reading is conditional and narrow. A coordinate $q_i$ may be discussed at the predicted-structure-confidence boundary only after the relevant translation-readout route has been kept separate from the structure readout and after amino-acid composition, CDS length, $GC3$, and $M$-density have been residualized. The present computation records a controlled $B^\ast_{Q6}$ association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT in two organisms; it does not by itself establish a same-coordinate translation-mediated chain $q_i \to T_j \to SO$. If a later table supplies direct translation readouts such as measured translation efficiency, ribosome profiling, or an explicitly bounded codon-adaptation readout, then the same-coordinate gate can be tested before any downstream structural-order discussion is admitted.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.} No translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, folded conformation, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, localization, pathway role, or universal biological law follows from this finite surface. AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted confidence score and not an experimental structural-order assay. Protein abundance, mRNA abundance, tAI, stAI, codon usage, and ribosome occupancy are distinct contact types and cannot be substituted for one another. Positive partial-$R^2$ entries here only say that, after the declared controls, the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates carry a finite cross-sectional association with predicted structure confidence in the two tested organisms. Any stronger statement requires a separate layer-matched measurement and a new controlled statistic at that layer.
