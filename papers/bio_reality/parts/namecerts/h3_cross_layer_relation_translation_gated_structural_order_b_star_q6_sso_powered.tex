\subsection{Translation-gated residual access to predicted structural confidence}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record fixes a finite cross-layer testing surface rather than a global biological law.  The coordinate side consists of the nine residual directions
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These coordinates are evaluated per encoded protein after quotienting away the amino-acid composition already visible in the coding sequence.  The biological readout attached in this finite row is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the scale $0,\ldots,100$, used only as predicted structural-confidence readback.  It is not treated as an experimental structure, a folding trajectory, a physical assay, a function assay, a phenotype assay, or a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
For each organism the residual design is conditioned on the same declared control family: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 computed from those codon counts, and the $M$-density baseline given by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{*}\_{Q6}$ directions.  In both organisms the powered residual coordinate rank remains nondegenerate after these controls.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 the control matrix has $24$ columns, rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, residualized coordinate rank $9$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and response residual energy $136607.52873622958$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the corresponding values are $24$ control columns, rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, residualized coordinate rank $9$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and response residual energy $620506.1490869389$.  The degeneracy cutoff used for the rank audit is $0.999999$, and both organisms are marked powered-rank sufficient.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the $n\times 9$ residual-coordinate matrix after the declared controls, and let $y$ denote the AlphaFold mean-pLDDT vector after the same residualization.  The reported statistic is the controlled predicted-confidence association
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}\_{SO} &= \frac{\|\Pi\_{Q\mid Z}y\|^{2}}{\|y\_{\perp Z}\|^{2}},\\
S^{SO} &> 0 \quad \text{only when the partial association is above } 10^{-12}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This expression is a finite readback rule for the attached predicted-confidence rows.  It does not identify translation mediation, since a same-coordinate $q\_i\to T\_j$ translation-readout step and a downstream conditioning analysis are not supplied by this pLDDT statistic itself.  The translation gate therefore remains a logical boundary: a structural-confidence entry may be discussed only after the coordinate has first been separated from codon-count and amino-acid-composition baselines, and it may not be promoted to translation, folding, function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality without a layer-matched contact.

\paragraph{Joined rows.}
The finite row join is nearly complete in both organisms.  In \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ codon rows are joined to the structural-confidence table, $3736$ matched proteins are used, $3736$ proteins carry the structural-confidence readout, and the join hit rate is $0.999198$.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows are joined, $5804$ matched proteins are used, $5804$ proteins carry the structural-confidence readout, and the join hit rate is $0.998108$.  These high hit rates make the finite association test well populated, but they do not by themselves change the interpretation of the readout.

\paragraph{Observed bounded association.}
With the above controls and joined rows, the predicted-confidence association is positive in the bounded finite sense recorded here.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the structural-confidence statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}\_{SO} &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
\#\{\text{surviving residual coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The signal is classified as modest: it is a finite cross-sectional association and remains non-causal.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}\_{SO} &= 0.0715885627979685,\\
\#\{\text{surviving residual coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is likewise a finite association with a predicted-confidence readout only.  The two organism-specific values show that the residual-coordinate surface has nonzero explanatory power for AlphaFold mean pLDDT after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls, but they do not supply a universal law across organisms.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter records a controlled codon-usage residual association with a predicted structural-confidence score.  It does not claim observed translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, mature protein abundance, folded structure, folding kinetics, localization, pathway activity, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, causality, or a cross-organism law.  A translation claim would require an organism-matched translation contact such as measured translation efficiency or a bounded adaptation-weight readout with the same residual controls.  A folding or physical-admissibility claim would require a separate structure or physical assay.  A function or phenotype claim would require its own functional, perturbational, or organism-level contact.  In the absence of those additional contacts, the finite positive entries are kept at the level of controlled predicted-confidence association only.
