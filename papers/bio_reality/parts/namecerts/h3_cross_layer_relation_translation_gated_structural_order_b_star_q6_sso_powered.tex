\subsection{Translation-Gated Structural-Order Readout for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record fixes a finite cross-layer test for whether residual synonymous-coordinate information can enter a structural-order readout only after surviving a translation-facing gate.  The carrier is a CDS-indexed table whose rows are encoded proteins with matched codon-count residual coordinates, organism labels, length, amino-acid composition, GC3, an $M$-density baseline, and a predicted structural-confidence readout.  The residual coordinate family has exactly nine entries:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_{6}^{\ast}=\{&K_{\mathrm{AAA}},\operatorname{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}},\operatorname{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}},\operatorname{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUR}},\operatorname{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UUG}},\\
&\operatorname{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{AGY}},\operatorname{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{UCG}},\operatorname{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}\,\mathrm{vs}\,\mathrm{ACY}},f_{3}\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinates were imported from the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival computation and were evaluated in the run \texttt{ac05b5047c28}.  The structural-order quantity in this chapter is not an experimental folding measurement.  It is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, used only as predicted structure-confidence readback.

\paragraph{Control design.}
The controlled design removes simpler explanations before any residual entry is allowed to be counted.  The control block is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=\{&1,\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt}),\mathrm{AA}_{A},\mathrm{AA}_{C},\mathrm{AA}_{D},\mathrm{AA}_{E},\mathrm{AA}_{F},\mathrm{AA}_{G},\mathrm{AA}_{H},\mathrm{AA}_{I},\\
&\mathrm{AA}_{K},\mathrm{AA}_{L},\mathrm{AA}_{M},\mathrm{AA}_{N},\mathrm{AA}_{P},\mathrm{AA}_{Q},\mathrm{AA}_{R},\mathrm{AA}_{S},\mathrm{AA}_{T},\mathrm{AA}_{V},\mathrm{AA}_{W},\mathrm{AA}_{Y},\\
&\mathrm{GC3},\Delta_{M}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Delta_{M}$ is the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the reported association is not promoted from amino-acid composition, length, GC3, or the $M$-density baseline alone.  The positive-entry threshold used for the finite survival call is partial $R^{2}>10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Rank and row diagnostics.}
The two organism panels are powered after residualization by $Z$.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the joined codon table has $3739$ rows, of which $3736$ proteins are matched to the predicted structural-confidence readout; the join hit rate is $0.999198$.  The residualized design has $9$ coordinate columns, $24$ control columns, $20$ amino-acid-composition controls, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and response residual energy $136607.52873622958$.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined codon table has $5815$ rows, of which $5804$ proteins are matched; the join hit rate is $0.998108$.  Its residualized design has the same $9$ coordinate columns, $24$ control columns, and $20$ amino-acid-composition controls, with $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and response residual energy $620506.1490869389$.  Both panels clear the declared rank-sufficiency test against the degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Computed structural-confidence association.}
After the controls above, the finite structural-confidence readout gives a nonzero residual association in both organisms.  The computed structural-order partial surfaces are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{\mathrm{SO}}(\emph{E. coli}\ \mathrm{K\mbox{-}12\ MG1655})&=0.03059081709413562,\\
R^{2}_{\mathrm{SO}}(\emph{S. cerevisiae})&=0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
In both panels, all $9$ residual coordinates remain represented in the computed structural-confidence association surface.  The \emph{E. coli} value is interpreted as modest finite association, while the yeast value is a larger finite cross-sectional association.  These numbers are finite-row statistics on predicted confidence, not evidence that codon residuals cause a folded conformation.

\paragraph{Translation gate and null boundary.}
The intended biological order is a gated chain $q_i\rightarrow T_j\rightarrow S_{\mathrm{SO}}$, not a direct promotion from codon geometry to structure.  A coordinate $q_i$ is allowed to approach a structural-confidence test only if the same coordinate first survives a translation-readout test beyond $Z$ and a simpler-baseline ablation.  The structural-confidence step is then tested conditionally on the translation readout and on protein-abundance controls.  If no $q_i$ survives the translation-readout gate, the structural-order entry is null by construction, regardless of any codon-count, amino-acid, GC3, length, or $M$-density pattern.  The present powered finite-row computation records a codon-residual association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after the declared controls; it does not by itself supply measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, mature protein abundance mediation, folding dynamics, or a perturbational mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter therefore reports only this bounded claim: per-protein $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-usage residuals have a controlled cross-sectional association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT predicted structural-confidence values in the two finite organism panels above.  No translation realization, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular-function claim, system-phenotype claim, mechanism claim, causality claim, localization claim, pathway claim, abundance claim, or universal biological law follows from these rows unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that measurement and the same coordinate passes the appropriate controlled gate at that layer.  In particular, tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, and AlphaFold confidence are distinct readouts; none may be substituted for another without a separately joined and controlled contact table.
