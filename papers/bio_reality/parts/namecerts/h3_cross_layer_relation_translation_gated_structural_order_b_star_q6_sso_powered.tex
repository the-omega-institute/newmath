\subsection{Translation-Gated Residual Coordinates and Predicted Structural Confidence}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a finite cross-layer test surface whose left side is the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis and whose right side is a protein-level predicted-confidence readout.  The residual coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  These coordinates are not treated as biological measurements by themselves.  They are codon-usage residual axes computed from coding-sequence rows, then evaluated only after conditioning away simpler sequence summaries and amino-acid composition.

\paragraph{Control design.}
For each encoded protein row, the structural-order readout used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structure confidence.  The control matrix $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from the same coding sequences, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the tested residual association is not an amino-acid-composition claim and is not a codon-count-only claim.

\paragraph{Rank and row diagnostics.}
The finite computation was run under experiment id $86830c9a8724$ and verified at $2026$-$06$-$12$ $11{:}06{:}10+00{:}00$.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ codon rows were joined to $3736$ matched protein rows with a structural-confidence join hit rate of $0.999198$.  The control matrix had $24$ columns, including $20$ amino-acid controls; after residualization, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and the residual degrees of freedom were $3713$.  The residual readout energy was $136607.52873622958$, while the explained energy attributed to the residual-coordinate block was $4178.935925251874$.  The rank gate was therefore powered for all nine residual coordinates, with degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows were joined to $5804$ matched protein rows with a structural-confidence join hit rate of $0.998108$.  The same $24$-column control design gave $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and $5781$ residual degrees of freedom.  The residual readout energy was $620506.1490869389$, and the explained energy of the residual-coordinate block was $44421.14342043593$.  The rank gate was again powered for all nine coordinates under the same degeneracy threshold.

\paragraph{Observed finite association.}
The structural-confidence statistic is reported as a controlled partial-$R^2$-style survival surface with positive threshold $10^{-12}$.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the computed structural-confidence association was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, the corresponding values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.0715885627979685,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
These are finite, organism-scoped associations between codon-usage residual coordinates and AlphaFold mean pLDDT after the declared controls.  The larger yeast value is still a controlled cross-sectional association, not a mechanistic or causal conclusion.  The bacterial value is smaller and is recorded as modest rather than absent.

\paragraph{Local interpretation boundary.}
The readout in this section is predicted structure confidence, not an experimental folded-structure measurement.  Mean pLDDT is not protein function, phenotype, abundance, localization, folding kinetics, pathway activity, physical admissibility, or a translation mechanism.  A positive $R^2_{SO}$ entry therefore does not by itself establish translation realization, folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, universality, or causality.  Claims at those layers require their own layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, mRNA abundance, protein abundance, and predicted structure confidence remain distinct readout classes; none is a substitute for another merely because the same $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate appears in the design matrix.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The chapter permits only the following bounded statement: in two organism-specific finite panels, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a nonzero controlled association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  It does not permit a direct $q_i \to$ structure, $q_i \to$ function, or $q_i \to$ phenotype reading.  If a future translation-readout gate is used, the same-coordinate $q_i \to T_j$ survival step must first be established with amino-acid composition and baseline ablation before any downstream structural-confidence or structural-order statistic is interpreted.  If that translation gate is absent or fails, the structural-order path remains null by construction.
