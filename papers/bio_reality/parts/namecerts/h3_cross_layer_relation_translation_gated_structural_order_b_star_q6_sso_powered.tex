\subsection{Translation-Gated Structural-Order Boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns the finite coding-sequence surface on which the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are evaluated against a protein-level predicted-confidence readout.  The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_{AAA},\quad Arg\_{AGR},\quad Ile\_{AUA},\quad Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\quad Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\quad Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\quad Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\quad f3\_{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation therefore has $9$ residual coordinates, all imported from the recorded $q$-vector construction used in run $86830c9a8724$.  Each row is a matched protein-coding entry with codon-count information, a residual-coordinate vector, and an AlphaFold mean pLDDT value on the interval $0$ to $100$.  In this chapter the pLDDT value is treated only as predicted structural-confidence signal.  It is not an experimental folding measurement and it is not a measurement of function, phenotype, localization, pathway activity, abundance, or folding kinetics.

\paragraph{Control design.}
The residual test is conditioned on a fixed covariate block $Z$.  The block contains an intercept, $\log(cds\_len\_nt)$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 from the same codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors.  The positive threshold used for a finite association is partial $R^2>10^{-12}$.  This threshold is deliberately weak: it records only whether the residual surface is nonzero after the declared controls, not whether the association is large, mechanistic, causal, or universal.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
Two organism-specific surfaces are recorded.  For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the codon join contains $3739$ CDS rows, of which $3736$ matched proteins are used, giving $3736$ structural-confidence rows and a join hit rate of $0.999198$.  The control matrix has $24$ columns, including $20$ amino-acid composition controls; its rank is $23$.  After residualizing by $Z$, the residual degrees of freedom are $3713$, the residualized $Q6$ coordinate rank is $9$, the explained energy is $4178.935925251874$, and the response residual energy is $136607.52873622958$.  The rank diagnostic is therefore powered for all nine coordinates under the declared degeneracy threshold $0.999999$.

For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the codon join contains $5815$ CDS rows, of which $5804$ matched proteins are used, giving $5804$ structural-confidence rows and a join hit rate of $0.998108$.  The same $24$-column control block has rank $23$.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $5781$, the residualized $Q6$ coordinate rank is $9$, the explained energy is $44421.14342043593$, and the response residual energy is $620506.1490869389$.  This surface is also rank-powered for all nine coordinates under the same degeneracy threshold.

\paragraph{Observed bounded association.}
The structural-confidence readback reports a finite residual association in both organisms.  In \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the recorded structural-confidence residual statistic is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving }B^{\ast}_{Q6}\hbox{ coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The associated interpretation is modest: the entry is finite and nonzero, but it remains a cross-sectional association with predicted-confidence output only.  In \textit{S. cerevisiae}, the corresponding values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO} &= 0.0715885627979685,\\
\#\{\hbox{surviving }B^{\ast}_{Q6}\hbox{ coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is again a finite predicted-confidence association and not a causal statement.  Thus, on the finite rows actually loaded, the nine-coordinate residual block has nonzero predictive residue for AlphaFold mean pLDDT after CDS length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls in both recorded organisms.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The name of the packet is deliberately stricter than the statistic just described.  A full translation-gated structural-order claim would require a same-coordinate chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow SO,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ first survives against a translation readout $T_j$ under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls, and where the structural-confidence or structural-order readback is then tested conditional on that surviving $T_j$ and on protein-abundance controls.  The finite rows in this record do not by themselves establish that chain.  They establish a controlled association from the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block to AlphaFold mean pLDDT.  They do not show that a ribosome, tAI, stAI, translation-efficiency, or protein-abundance mediator has carried the same coordinate into the structural-confidence readout.

\paragraph{Null edge.}
If no coordinate survives the translation-readout gate in a separately joined translation matrix, then every stronger structural-order entry is null by construction.  The present rows cannot be used to bypass that condition.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted-structure-confidence readout; it is not observed ribosome occupancy, not translation efficiency, not mature protein abundance, not an experimental fold, and not a functional assay.  Positive $R^2_{SO}$ entries therefore remain bounded evidence for a codon-usage residual association with predicted confidence after the declared controls.  They do not imply translation realization, folding mechanism, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a biological law applying beyond the organisms and identifiers used here.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
Within the available finite data, the chapter records a ready bounded result: both organism panels have sufficient residual rank, high CDS-to-protein join rates, and positive controlled association between the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block and AlphaFold mean pLDDT.  The result is not ready as a full translation-mediated structural-order theorem.  That stronger reading requires an additional same-row translation-readout matrix and a conditional downstream test that keeps translation, protein abundance, and structure-level readbacks separated.
