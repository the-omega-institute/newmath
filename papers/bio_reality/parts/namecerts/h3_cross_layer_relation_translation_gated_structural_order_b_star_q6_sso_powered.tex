\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-indexed surface whose rows are encoded proteins and whose columns are the nine residual coordinates of the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ quotient.  The coordinate list is
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  These coordinates are paired with organism-matched coding-sequence rows and a bounded structural-order readback only through the declared protein identifier join.  The computation cited here is run $86830c9a8724$, verified at $2026$-$06$-$12$ $11{:}06{:}10+00{:}00$.

\paragraph{Controlled residual design.}

After residualizing against $Z$, both organisms retain full powered rank for the nine-coordinate surface:

The corresponding explained energies are $4178.935925251874$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $44421.14342043593$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The residual response energies are $136607.52873622958$ and $620506.1490869389$, respectively.  The degenerate-$R^2$ cutoff used to reject rank-saturated artifacts is $0.999999$, and both organism-level designs are marked powered-rank sufficient.

\paragraph{Structural-order readback actually used.}
The attached readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale.  In this chapter it is used only as predicted structure-confidence readback, not as an experimental measurement of folded order.  The row join is dense: in \emph{E. coli}, $3739$ codon rows were joined, $3736$ matched proteins were used, $3736$ proteins had the structural-order readback, and the hit rate was $0.999198$.  In \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows were joined, $5804$ matched proteins were used, $5804$ proteins had the readback, and the hit rate was $0.998108$.

\paragraph{Finite association result.}
With the above controls in place, the structural-order surface reports a positive but bounded association in both organisms.  The partial structural-order coefficients are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(E.\ coli) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^2_{SO}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
In each organism all nine residual coordinates are counted as surviving entries at the declared positive threshold $10^{-12}$.  The interpretation is deliberately narrow: the result says that the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface carries finite cross-sectional information about AlphaFold mean pLDDT after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density have been removed.  It does not say that the coordinates determine folding, stability, activity, localization, pathway role, organismal phenotype, or causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The intended cross-layer order is $q_i \to T_j \to SO$: a coordinate $q_i$ should first survive a translation readout $T_j$, and only then may a structural-order readback be tested conditionally on that translation readout and on protein-abundance baselines.  The finite computation above supplies a powered predicted-confidence association for the structural readback side, but it does not by itself supply a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency survival step.  Therefore the result is best read as a structural-readback boundary test waiting for the same-coordinate translation gate.  If a future joined matrix shows no positive $q_i \to T_j$ entry after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls, then every downstream structural-order entry is null for the gated claim even if an ungated pLDDT association is numerically positive.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows from these rows alone.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted confidence score rather than an experimental structure assay; tAI or stAI weights are modeled codon-adaptation readbacks rather than ribosome traces; ribosome profiling, mRNA abundance, protein abundance, localization, stability, interaction, and functional assays are distinct contact layers.  A positive $R^2_{SO}$ here is only a controlled codon-usage residual association with predicted structure confidence.  Any stronger claim requires its own matched contact and a separately recorded statistic at that layer.
