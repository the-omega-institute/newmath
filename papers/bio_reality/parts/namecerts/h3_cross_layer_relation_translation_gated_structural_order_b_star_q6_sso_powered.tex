The structural-confidence computation above shows that the finite design has rank and that the predicted-confidence surface is nonzero after controls; it does not by itself prove a translation-mediated route. $$
\begin{aligned}

If the $q_i\to T_j$ step is absent, saturated, or not computed on matched rows, then the structural-confidence row is read as a bounded association only, not as a translation-mediated structural claim.
