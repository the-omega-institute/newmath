\paragraph{Readout and gate.}
The readout in this finite run is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0..100$, used only as predicted structure confidence.  It is not an experimental folded-structure measurement and it is not a function, phenotype, abundance, localization, folding-kinetics, pathway, mechanism, or causal endpoint.  The tested association can be written as a controlled residual readback
$$
\begin{aligned}
S^{SO}(Q) &= R^2\bigl(Y_{\mathrm{pLDDT}}\,;\,\widetilde{Q}_1,\ldots,\widetilde{Q}_9 \mid Z\bigr),\\
Z &= \{1,\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt}),AA_1,\ldots,AA_{20},GC3,M\text{-density}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with a positive-entry threshold of $10^{-12}$ and a rank-degeneracy guard at $0.999999$.  The intended chain is translation-gated: a coordinate should not be carried into structural-order interpretation unless the same coordinate has first survived an appropriate translation-readout test under the declared controls and simpler-baseline ablation.  In the present finite table, the attached numerical readout is predicted structural confidence, so the result is a bounded cross-sectional association rather than a completed translation-mediated structural mechanism.

\paragraph{Local null boundary.}

AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted confidence score, not a direct assay of folded structural order, and the computation does not establish translation causality.  No claim of translation realization, ribosome mechanism, protein abundance mediation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, adaptation, or universality follows from the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates, from $M$-density, from codon usage, from tAI or stAI-style readouts, or from the positive $S^{SO}$ entries unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and the same-coordinate gate is recomputed under the declared controls.
