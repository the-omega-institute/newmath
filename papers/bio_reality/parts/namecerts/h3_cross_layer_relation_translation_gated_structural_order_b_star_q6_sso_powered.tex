\subsection{Translation-Gated Structural-Order Readback for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{subsec:bq6-translation-gated-structural-order}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record treats the nine computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as a finite surface over encoded proteins, paired with a protein-level structural-confidence readout only after codon-count, amino-acid-composition, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls have been imposed. The coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{Q}_{6}^{\ast}=\{&K_{AAA},\ Arg_{AGR},\ Ile_{AUA},\ Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f_{3}\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite readout used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0..100$, interpreted only as predicted structure confidence. It is not an experimental fold measurement, not a functional assay, and not a phenotype or mechanism readout. The computation therefore asks a bounded question: after residualizing against a declared control family, does the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface retain nonzero association with this predicted-confidence scalar?

\paragraph{Control surface.}
For each protein row, the control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3 from real CDS codon counts, and an $M$-density baseline equal to the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. Thus the tested residual coordinate is not allowed to inherit a signal merely from amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, or the coarse $M$-support baseline.

\paragraph{Rank diagnostics.}
The powered design is nondegenerate for both organisms in the finite panel. For \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the control block has $24$ columns, with $20$ amino-acid-composition columns, $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, residual response energy $136607.52873622958$, and explained energy $4178.935925251874$. For \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the same $24$-column control design has $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$, $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, residual response energy $620506.1490869389$, and explained energy $44421.14342043593$. In both organisms the recorded degenerate-$R^{2}$ threshold is $0.999999$, and the powered-rank flag is true.

\paragraph{Joined structural-confidence rows.}
The finite join is nearly complete in both organisms. In \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ codon rows were joined to $3736$ matched proteins carrying the structural-confidence readout, giving a join hit rate of $0.999198$. In \textit{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows were joined to $5804$ matched proteins, giving a join hit rate of $0.998108$. These counts make the readback a concrete finite panel rather than a statement about all organisms or all proteins.

\paragraph{Computed structural-confidence association.}
Let $Y_{SO}$ denote the AlphaFold mean-pLDDT scalar, and let $\widetilde X$ denote the nine-coordinate residual design after projection away from $Z$. The recorded statistic is the residual structural-confidence coefficient of determination
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{SO}(\textit{E. coli}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^{2}_{SO}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
With the positive partial-$R^{2}$ threshold set at $10^{-12}$, all nine coordinates are recorded as surviving the structural-confidence association surface in both organisms. The interpretation remains deliberately narrow: the \textit{E. coli} value is a modest finite association, and the yeast value is a finite cross-sectional predicted-confidence association. Neither value is a causal estimate.

\paragraph{Gating interpretation.}
The intended cross-layer order is not $q_i\to$ structure directly. The admissible route is first a translation-readout gate and only then a structural-confidence readback conditional on the relevant translation and protein-abundance controls:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,\\
(q_i,T_j,\text{protein-abundance baseline}) &\longrightarrow Y_{SO}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite computation supplies a controlled codon-residual association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls. It does not by itself prove that the same coordinate passed a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency gate, and it does not replace a protein-abundance mediation panel. A future stronger record would need same-row codon counts, measured or explicitly bounded translation readout, matched mRNA abundance, matched protein abundance where used, and the structural-confidence or structural-order readout on the same encoded proteins.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The association reported here must not be promoted into translation realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality. AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted structure-confidence number, not an experimental structural-order assay and not a function or abundance measurement. Positive residual entries therefore mean only that the finite $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual design retains cross-sectional association with predicted confidence under the declared controls in the two organisms. A claim about ribosome dynamics requires a translation contact; a claim about folding or physical admissibility requires a separate structure or physical assay; a claim about function, phenotype, mechanism, or a general biological law requires its own layer-matched evidence.
