\subsection{Translation-Gated Structural-Order Readback for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:biorReality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record studies a finite CDS-indexed table whose columns are the nine residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$.
Each row is an encoded protein for which codon-count features, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and an $M$-density baseline are available.  The intended gate has two stages.  First, a residual coordinate $q_i$ may be admitted toward a higher-layer question only if it survives a translation readout under the declared controls.  Second, after that translation step, a structural-order readback may be examined only as a conditional downstream association, not as a direct consequence of codon geometry.  The finite run reported here attaches AlphaFold mean pLDDT as a predicted-structure-confidence readout on the interval $0..100$; this is not an experimental folding measurement.

\paragraph{Controlled design matrix.}
The control block $Z$ contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$ computed from real CDS codon counts, GC3 computed from the same CDS rows, and the $M$-density baseline defined as the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors.  Thus the structural-confidence association is not allowed to be a disguised amino-acid-composition effect, a length effect, a GC3 effect, or an $M$-support effect.  In both organisms the projected residual block is rank-powered after residualization against $Z$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)_{E.\ coli} &= 9, & \mathrm{rank}(Z)_{E.\ coli} &= 23, & df_{E.\ coli} &= 3713,\\
\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)_{S.\ cerevisiae} &= 9, & \mathrm{rank}(Z)_{S.\ cerevisiae} &= 23, & df_{S.\ cerevisiae} &= 5781.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual response energies are $136607.52873622958$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $620506.1490869389$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The explained energies attributed to the residual coordinate block after control projection are $4178.935925251874$ and $44421.14342043593$, respectively.  The degeneracy threshold used for the powered-rank diagnostic is $0.999999$, and the positive partial-$R^2$ reporting threshold is $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Matched structural-confidence rows.}
The finite readback is nearly complete at the identifier-join level.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ codon rows join to the protein table, $3736$ proteins are used, and $3736$ structural-confidence rows are present, giving hit rate $0.999198$.  For \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $5815$ codon rows join, $5804$ proteins are used, and $5804$ structural-confidence rows are present, giving hit rate $0.998108$.  These row counts make the reported association a large finite-table statistic rather than an anecdotal coordinate example.

\paragraph{Observed structural-confidence association.}
After the stated controls, all nine residual coordinates are counted as surviving the positive structural-confidence threshold in each organism for this readout.  The resulting structural-confidence partial coefficients are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(E.\ coli\ K\mbox{-}12\ MG1655) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^2_{SO}(S.\ cerevisiae) &= 0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first value is a modest finite association, and the second is a stronger finite association in the same statistical sense.  In both cases the interpretation remains cross-sectional: the rows show residual codon-usage association with predicted structure confidence after controls, not a causal path, not a folding trajectory, and not a functional claim.

\paragraph{Gating interpretation.}
The mathematical content of the record is the ordered boundary
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow SO
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T_j$ is a separately supplied translation readout, and $SO$ is the attached structural-confidence readout.  The structural-confidence computation reported above occupies only the downstream readback position.  It does not by itself certify the missing upstream translation-survival step.  Therefore, the positive $R^2_{SO}$ values should be read as evidence that the controlled residual coordinate block has finite association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT in the joined organisms, while the full translation-mediated structural-order chain remains conditional on the same coordinate also surviving a measured or modeled translation readout under the declared controls and baseline ablation.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this finite table alone.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted-confidence quantity, not an experimental structural-order assay and not a measurement of function, localization, abundance, folding kinetics, pathway activity, or phenotype.  Likewise, $tAI$, $stAI$, ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, and proteomics contacts must each remain layer-specific when used.  A higher-layer assertion is admissible only when a separate contact at that layer supplies matched rows and the statistic is recomputed with the relevant conditioning variables.  In particular, codon usage, $M$-density, and the internal shape of the nine residual coordinates cannot be promoted into structure, function, mechanism, or universality without such a layer-matched readout.
