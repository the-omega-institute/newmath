\paragraph{Gated interpretation.}
The claim has a strict ordering.  A $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate may be considered for a structural-confidence readback only after it is kept separate from simpler sequence baselines and amino-acid composition.  The attached readout here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT, so the admissible statement is narrow: per-protein synonymous-residual coordinates show a finite association with predicted structural confidence under length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls in the two organism panels.  This does not by itself show a translation-mediated mechanism.  It also does not show that ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance carried the association, because those require their own same-row translation and abundance measurements.

\paragraph{Local refusal boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, localization, pathway activity, or universal biological rule follows from the statistic above.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted-structure-confidence score, not an experimental structural-order assay, not a protein-function measurement, not a phenotype, not abundance, and not a folding-kinetics trace.  A positive $S^{\mathrm{SO}}$ entry therefore cannot be promoted to causality or to a structural law.  To cross that boundary, a later table would have to attach the same coding-sequence rows to a direct translation readout, matched protein abundance where abundance is part of the conditioning set, and an independent structure-level measurement or explicitly delimited structure-prediction contact.  Until those rows exist, the finite content of this record is only the controlled association with predicted confidence: nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, two organism panels, high join coverage, powered residual rank, and the two reported $R^2_{\mathrm{SO}}$ values.
