\paragraph{Gate interpretation.}
The intended gate is ordered: a coordinate $q_i$ may be considered for a downstream structural-confidence readback only after a translation readout $T_j$ has been tested with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, $M$-density, and simpler-baseline controls.  The present finite run attaches an AlphaFold mean-pLDDT surface and shows a controlled $Q$-to-predicted-confidence association in two organisms, but it does not by itself supply a measured ribosome-occupancy, translation-efficiency, tAI-to-ribosome calibration, protein-abundance mediation, or perturbation chain.  Therefore the local statement is deliberately narrow: the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ block has a rank-powered, covariate-controlled association with predicted structure confidence in the two reported panels.
