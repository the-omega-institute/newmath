\paragraph{Gate interpretation.}
The mathematical object supported here is a controlled association between per-protein $B^{*}_{Q6}$ codon-usage residuals and AlphaFold mean pLDDT predicted confidence. It is not a folded-structure experiment, not a translation-efficiency measurement, and not a protein-function assay. The intended chain is gated: a coordinate $q_i$ may enter a structural-order discussion only after a same-coordinate translation readout $T_j$ has survived the amino-acid-composition, GC3, length, organism, and $M$-density controls, and any subsequent structural-confidence statistic must then be read conditional on $T_j$ and protein-abundance baseline controls. In the present run the pLDDT association is real as a finite controlled readback, but it is not itself evidence that ribosomes, tRNA adaptation, or translation efficiency mediated the association.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
No claim of measured translation realization follows from these pLDDT rows unless a separate ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, tAI/stAI, or equivalent translation contact supplies a matched statistic. No claim of folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows from the finite $R^2_{SO}$ values. AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted structure-confidence annotation, not an experimental structural-order assay, not abundance, not localization, not folding kinetics, and not pathway activity. The positive $R^2_{SO}$ values therefore authorize only this bounded statement: in two organism-specific finite panels with strong row matching and full amino-acid-composition residualization, the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a nonzero association with predicted protein-structure confidence after length, GC3, and $M$-density controls. Any stronger biological reading requires a distinct layer-matched contact and a new controlled statistic.
