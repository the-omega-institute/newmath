\subsection{Translation-gated structural-order readback for the powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-translation-gated-structural-order}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface and readout rule.}
This record concerns a finite CDS-indexed surface whose rows are encoded proteins and whose columns are the nine powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_{3}$-stress.  The biological question is not whether these coordinates are themselves structural measurements.  They are codon-usage residual coordinates.  A structural-order readback is allowed only as a later row-matched response, and only after the codon residuals have been evaluated against the declared controls.  In the present finite run the attached readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the interval $0..100$, interpreted only as predicted structure confidence.  It is not an experimental folded-structure assay, not protein function, not phenotype, not abundance, not localization, not folding kinetics, and not pathway activity.

\paragraph{Control design.}
The controlled model residualizes the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix against
$$\begin{aligned}
Z=\{&1,\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt}),\mathrm{AA}_{A},\mathrm{AA}_{C},\mathrm{AA}_{D},\mathrm{AA}_{E},\mathrm{AA}_{F},\mathrm{AA}_{G},\mathrm{AA}_{H},\mathrm{AA}_{I},\\
&\mathrm{AA}_{K},\mathrm{AA}_{L},\mathrm{AA}_{M},\mathrm{AA}_{N},\mathrm{AA}_{P},\mathrm{AA}_{Q},\mathrm{AA}_{R},\mathrm{AA}_{S},\mathrm{AA}_{T},\mathrm{AA}_{V},\mathrm{AA}_{W},\mathrm{AA}_{Y},\\
&GC3,\Delta_{M}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
Here $\Delta_{M}$ is the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate vectors.  The amino-acid proportions are computed from the real CDS codon counts, so the readback is not promoted from amino-acid composition alone.  The positive partial-$R^{2}$ threshold used by the row is $10^{-12}$.

\paragraph{Rank and row diagnostics.}
Two organism panels are present.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ CDS codon rows join to the structural-order table, $3736$ matched proteins are used, and the structural-order join hit rate is $0.999198$.  The control matrix has $24$ columns, with $20$ amino-acid-composition controls, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and response residual energy $136607.52873622958$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $5815$ CDS codon rows join, $5804$ matched proteins are used, and the hit rate is $0.998108$.  The same $24$-column control design has $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and response residual energy $620506.1490869389$.  In both panels the powered rank diagnostic is sufficient, with all nine residual coordinates retained after control residualization and with the degeneracy threshold fixed at $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Finite association readback.}
The resulting structural-confidence residual association is finite and nonzero on both panels:
$$\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{SO}(\emph{E. coli}\ K\text{-}12\ MG1655)&=0.03059081709413562,\\
R^{2}_{SO}(\emph{S. cerevisiae})&=0.0715885627979685.
\end{aligned}$$
Each organism reports nine surviving coordinates for this pLDDT readback.  The \emph{E. coli} signal is modest: it is a controlled cross-sectional association between codon residual coordinates and predicted-confidence values.  The yeast signal is larger on the supplied surface, but it has the same status: a finite association to predicted structure confidence after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The row therefore supports only a bounded statement: in the two supplied organism panels, the nine-coordinate powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface retains rank after the declared controls and has a positive controlled association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT.  This is a cross-layer readback from codon residuals to predicted structural-confidence values, not a claim that codon geometry determines folded structure.  The statistic is also not a translation measurement.  A genuine translation-gated chain $q_i\to T_j\to SO$ would require a same-coordinate translation readout $T_j$ and then a conditional structural-confidence or structure-contact test after $T_j$ and protein-abundance controls.  The finite row supplied here contains a pLDDT response and codon controls, but it does not by itself establish a ribosome-mediated mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from these values alone.  A tAI or stAI weight is only a modeled adaptation readout unless separately paired with measured ribosome or translation-efficiency data.  Ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, if attached later, would still not be a folded-structure measurement.  Protein abundance would not be a structural-order assay.  AlphaFold mean pLDDT is a predicted-confidence readout rather than experimental structure, causality, or function.  Any stronger statement must be supplied by its own layer-matched contact and by a row-matched statistic under the stated controls.
