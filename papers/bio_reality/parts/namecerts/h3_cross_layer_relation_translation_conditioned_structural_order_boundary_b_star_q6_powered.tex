\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:h3-cross-layer-relation-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary-b-star-q6-powered}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate has already been residualized against amino-acid quotient structure before any cross-layer interpretation is attempted. The coordinate family contains nine residual coordinates, namely $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The empirical panel is not a free biological narrative: it is a gated matrix of entries $q_i \to T_j \to O_k$, where $q_i$ is a residual synonymous-coordinate entry, $T_j$ is a translation readout that must pass its own controlled test, and $O_k$ is a structural-order readout interpreted only after that translation step is present. The controls used in the row-level regressions include an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Translation gate and rank adequacy.}
The translation-side gate is powered rather than merely named. In \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, the joined codon/structural-order table contains $3739$ codon rows, of which $3736$ proteins are matched to the structural-order readout, giving a hit rate of $0.999198$. In \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, the corresponding counts are $5815$ codon rows and $5804$ matched proteins, with hit rate $0.998108$. The controlled design matrix has $24$ control columns in each organism, with $20$ amino-acid composition controls. The rank diagnostics give $\mathrm{rank}(Z)=23$ and $\mathrm{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ in both organisms, so the nine-coordinate residual block remains estimable after the declared controls. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$ for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655} and $5781$ for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. The fitted residual energies and explained energies are organism-specific: for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, the $y$-residual energy is $136607.52873622958$ and the explained energy is $4178.935925251874$; for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, the $y$-residual energy is $620506.1490869389$ and the explained energy is $44421.14342043593$.

\paragraph{Structural-order readout.}
The downstream readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale. In this chapter it is treated as predicted structural-order confidence, not as experimental folding, not as a direct thermodynamic stability assay, and not as a functional phenotype. The row counts used for the translation-conditioned structural-order boundary are $3412$ joined rows in \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655} and $5119$ joined rows in \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}. The direct $B^*_{Q6}$-to-order surface is positive in both tested organisms: across $n=2$ organisms, $R^2$ ranges from $0.03664544696481631$ to $0.1604655467663032$, with mean and median both $0.09855549686555976$. Thus the finite panel contains a nonzero structural-order association before the mediation boundary is interpreted.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned test.}
The decisive statistic is the full-control increment after the translation conditioning step. The aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ is positive in both organisms: the minimum is $0.002688284523492457$, the maximum is $0.007880037229394898$, and the mean and median are both $0.005284160876443678$. The weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ is $0.004764742175882462$. A sign-flip test for positive full-control effect gives $p=0.0029296875$. Under the permutation control, the corresponding $\Delta R^2$ values are negative in both organisms, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, and mean and median both $-0.00212186632044159$. The finite-row classification is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{verdict} &= \mathrm{crosses\ to\ structural\ order\ layer},\\
\#\{\mathrm{full\ control\ positive}\} &= 2,\\
\#\{\mathrm{tested\ organisms}\} &= 2.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a bounded cross-layer statement: the tested residual coordinates carry a translation-conditioned association with predicted structural-order confidence in the two joined organism panels.

\paragraph{Relation to stability and compilation controls.}
The same residual family does not license a broad protein-state mechanism. In the separate translation-conditioned stability boundary, the finite panel is only partial: direct $B^*_{Q6}$ prediction of the stability-side readout is positive across three organisms, with $R^2$ ranging from $0.018837736273789707$ to $0.03602995605625481$ and median $0.027727034742559664$, but the aggregate full-control stability $\Delta R^2$ has mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, and weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, with sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$. That companion result marks stability as at most partial and does not convert the structural-order boundary into degradation, turnover, folding, or physical-admissibility evidence. Likewise, the minimal-compilation check shows weak compression of $P_Q$ by the measured low-dimensional readout set: organism-level compressibility is $0.024368095798914635$ for \texttt{danio\_rerio}, $0.005717621488177116$ for \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12\_mg1655}, $0.02635576736932188$ for \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, and $0.13234136547055853$ for \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, with median $0.026$. This says that the residual source is not mostly absorbed by the measured readout dictionary used in that descriptive test.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The positive call is deliberately narrow. It says that, in the finite powered panels, amino-acid-residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates retain a translation-conditioned association with an AlphaFold mean-pLDDT structural-order confidence readout after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, $M$-density, and matched expression-layer controls where applicable. It does not by itself establish ribosome mechanism, folding kinetics, thermodynamic stability, degradation rate, protein localization, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or a universal biological law. Any one of those stronger claims would require its own layer-matched measurement contact and a separate controlled statistic. In particular, modeled tAI or stAI coupling is not counted as survival evidence for protein stability, and a direct $q_i \to O_k$ association is not reported as mechanism unless the matched $q_i \to T_j$ translation entry survives and the conditioning step is explicitly evaluated.
