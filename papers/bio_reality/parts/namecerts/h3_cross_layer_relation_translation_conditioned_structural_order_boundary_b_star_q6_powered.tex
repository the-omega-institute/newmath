\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel and admissible reading.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are first residualized against amino-acid quotient structure and then tested against a downstream structural-order readout only through a translation-conditioned route. The admissible object is therefore not a direct claim that a codon coordinate determines structure. It is a gated matrix of entries of the form $q_i \to T_j \to O_k$, where $q_i$ ranges over $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $T_j$ is a matched translation readout, and $O_k$ is a structural-order readout. The structural-order surface used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT, read only as predicted structural-order confidence on the $0$ to $100$ scale, not as an experimental folding measurement, not as protein function, and not as phenotype.

The finite residual coordinate list used in the paired structural-order computation has nine coordinates:
$$\begin{aligned}
Q_{6}^{*}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The controls are deliberately stronger than a codon-only association: an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. Thus the reported structural-order entry is a residual association after amino-acid composition, organismal codon composition, CDS length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline have already been accounted for.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
The translation-gated structural-order panel is rank-powered in both organisms used for the structural-order readout. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the joined codon table has $3739$ CDS rows, the structural-order join uses $3736$ matched proteins, and the hit rate is $0.999198$. The control design has $24$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, and powered-rank sufficiency is true. The residual response energy is $136607.52873622958$, while the explained energy assigned to the residual coordinate block is $4178.935925251874$.

In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the joined codon table has $5815$ CDS rows, the structural-order join uses $5804$ matched proteins, and the hit rate is $0.998108$. The control design again has $24$ columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, and powered-rank sufficiency is true. The residual response energy is $620506.1490869389$, while the explained energy assigned to the residual coordinate block is $44421.14342043593$. These diagnostics are the reason the two-organism structural-order calculation is read as finite-row evidence rather than as a saturated fit.

\paragraph{Observed structural-order association before translation conditioning.}
Before the translation-conditioned boundary statistic is interpreted, the structural-order survival calculation reports a positive finite association in both organisms. For \emph{E. coli}, $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ with all nine residual coordinates surviving the structural-order surface. For yeast, $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$, again with all nine residual coordinates surviving. The powered structural-order boundary run then summarizes the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ prediction of the order readout over two organisms as
$$\begin{aligned}
\min R^2 &= 0.03664544696481631,\\
\operatorname{median} R^2 &= 0.09855549686555976,\\
\operatorname{mean} R^2 &= 0.09855549686555976,\\
\max R^2 &= 0.1604655467663032,\\
n_{+} &=2,\qquad n_{-}=0.
\end{aligned}$$
This is only the finite association surface that motivates the boundary test. It is not by itself a claim of folding, physical admissibility, function, or mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary statistic.}
The structural-order boundary run joins $3412$ \emph{E. coli} rows and $5119$ yeast rows for the powered translation-conditioned calculation, with $5$ folds and a declared minimum joined-row threshold of $300$. The aggregate full-control increment after the mandatory translation conditioning is positive in both organisms:
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full,min}} &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full,median}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full,mean}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full,max}} &= 0.007880037229394898,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full,weighted}} &= 0.004764742175882462.
\end{aligned}$$
The organism call count is therefore $2$ full-control-positive calls out of $2$. The sign-flip test for a positive full-control effect gives
$$\begin{aligned}
p_{\mathrm{signflip}} &= 0.0029296875.
\end{aligned}$$
The permutation control has the opposite sign in both organisms:
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{perm,min}} &= -0.003463794378680385,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{perm,median}} &= -0.00212186632044159,\\

\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{perm,max}} &= -0.000779938262202795,\\
n_{\mathrm{perm,+}} &=0,\qquad n_{\mathrm{perm,-}}=2.
\end{aligned}$$
The finite-row verdict is therefore that the tested $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates cross to the predicted structural-order layer under the declared translation-conditioned, full-control statistic.

\paragraph{Relation to stability and compilation controls.}
The structural-order result should not be silently transferred to protein stability. A separate translation-conditioned stability calculation exists on three organisms, but its full-control aggregate is not positive overall: the weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ is $-0.001572140012981015$, the mean is $-0.0025553576666936064$, the median is $-0.0033500708066446228$, and the sign-flip probability for being greater than zero is $0.946441650390625$. Its organism calls are two partial calls and one mediated-blocked-null call. Thus the present structural-order boundary does not license a stability, degradation, turnover, or folding-mechanism conclusion.

The minimal-compilation calculation also prevents a broad compression reading. Across the four computed organisms, the residual source is not highly compressed by the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary: median compressibility is $0.026$. The per-organism compressibilities are $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, each with permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$ in the recorded descriptive test. The structural-order boundary is therefore a narrow finite-row crossing for a particular readout, not a statement that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source has been globally compiled into a small biological mechanism set.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The result may be stated as follows: on the two powered structural-order organisms, the same residual-coordinate family that survives amino-acid, length, GC3, and $M$-density controls also has a positive translation-conditioned association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT, and the matched permutation control is negative. The allowed claim stops there. The chapter does not infer measured translation dynamics from modeled adaptation weights, does not infer folding or physical admissibility from pLDDT, does not infer molecular function or organismal phenotype from the residual association, and does not assert a universal biological law. Any stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic: ribosome or TE rows for translation itself, pulse-chase or turnover rows for stability, physical assays for admissibility, functional assays for function, and phenotype or perturbation rows for organism-level consequence.
