\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record studies a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which every $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is read only after amino-acid quotient residualization and ordinary sequence controls.  The encoded coordinates are the nine residual directions $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected residual vectors.  In the two organisms with structural-order rows, the residual design is rank-powered rather than saturated: in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3713$, and the residual response energy is $136607.52873622958$; in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, the residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $5781$, and the residual response energy is $620506.1490869389$.

\paragraph{Layered statistic.}
The structural-order readout used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structural-confidence signal.  It is not an experimental folding assay.  The matched structural-order panel joins $3412$ rows for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 and $5119$ rows for \emph{S. cerevisiae} in the translation-conditioned run.  The earlier translation-gated structural-order computation had near-complete sequence-to-readout joins, with $3739$ codon rows and $3736$ matched structural-order proteins in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, and $5815$ codon rows and $5804$ matched structural-order proteins in \emph{S. cerevisiae}; the corresponding join hit rates were $0.999198$ and $0.998108$.

\paragraph{Observed finite effect.}
Before translation conditioning is interpreted, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a finite association with the structural-order readout in both organisms.  The cross-validated order-prediction summary has $n=2$, minimum $R^2=0.03664544696481631$, maximum $R^2=0.1604655467663032$, mean $R^2=0.09855549686555976$, and median $R^2=0.09855549686555976$, with two positive entries and no negative entries.  In the translation-conditioned order boundary test with five folds and a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$, the full-control increment remains positive in both organisms:
$$\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\min} &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\Delta R^2_{\max} &= 0.007880037229394898,\\
\operatorname{mean}(\Delta R^2) &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\operatorname{median}(\Delta R^2) &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.004764742175882462.
\end{aligned}$$
The full-control sign-flip test gives $p=0.0029296875$ for positivity.  The permutation comparison is negative in both organisms, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, mean $-0.00212186632044159$, and median $-0.00212186632044159$.  The finite call is therefore \emph{crosses to the structural-order layer}: the rank-powered residual block retains a positive translation-conditioned association with predicted structural confidence after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Relation to neighboring readouts.}
This structural-order call is deliberately narrower than a protein-stability or degradation claim.  The separate translation-conditioned stability boundary has only a partial verdict: the direct $B^*_{Q6}$-to-stability summaries are positive across three entries, with mean $0.02753157569086806$, median $0.027727034742559664$, minimum $0.018837736273789707$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$, but the full-control translation-conditioned stability increment has weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, and sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$.  Likewise, the minimal-compilation readout test does not compress the residual source into the measured low-dimensional readout set: the reported median compressibility is $0.026$, with per-organism compressibilities $0.024368095798914635$ in \emph{Danio rerio}, $0.005717621488177116$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.02635576736932188$ in \emph{Homo sapiens}, and $0.13234136547055853$ in \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  These neighboring numbers keep the interpretation local: the structural-order result is a finite residual association with predicted structural confidence, not evidence that the residual basis is generally compiled into the measured downstream dictionary.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible reading is the gated matrix $q_i \to T_j \to S_k$, where $q_i$ is an amino-acid-residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate, $T_j$ is a matched translation readout that must survive its own controls, and $S_k$ is the structural-confidence surface.  The chapter does not infer translation dynamics from codon geometry alone, does not infer folding from pLDDT alone, and does not infer physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality from the positive structural-order boundary.  Any such stronger statement would require its own matched measurement layer and its own controlled statistic on the same finite rows.
