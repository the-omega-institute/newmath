\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which each $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is evaluated only after quotienting away first-order amino-acid composition. The coordinate family used in the powered structural-order test has nine entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The finite readout surface is not a direct claim about folded proteins. It is a same-protein association test from residual synonymous-coordinate variation into an AlphaFold mean-pLDDT structural-order-confidence surface, with translation readouts and ordinary sequence controls kept in the conditioning set before any downstream interpretation is made.

\paragraph{Matched rows and controls.}
The structural-order run was computed on two joined organism panels. The Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 structural-order join supplied $3412$ rows for the translation-conditioned order boundary, while the Saccharomyces cerevisiae join supplied $5119$ rows. In the supporting translation-gated structural-order contact, the codon-to-structure join used $3736$ matched proteins for Escherichia coli out of $3739$ codon-joined rows, a hit rate of $0.999198$, and $5804$ matched proteins for Saccharomyces cerevisiae out of $5815$ codon-joined rows, a hit rate of $0.998108$. The control design included an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors. The residualized design had rank $9$ for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block in both organisms, with control rank $23$, so the reported positive entries are not rank-saturated one-row artifacts.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
The powered test reports a direct residual-coordinate prediction surface and a stricter translation-conditioned structural-order surface. The direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ to predicted-order scores were positive in both organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{Q\to SO} &= 0.03664544696481631,\\
\mathrm{median}\ R^2_{Q\to SO} &= 0.09855549686555976,\\
\mathrm{mean}\ R^2_{Q\to SO} &= 0.09855549686555976,\\
\max R^2_{Q\to SO} &= 0.1604655467663032.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation-conditioned full-control increment was smaller, as expected for a boundary statement rather than an unconditioned mechanism claim, but it remained positive in both organism panels:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\mathrm{median}\ \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\mathrm{mean}\ \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.007880037229394898,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.004764742175882462.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism call table classified both panels as full-control positive. A sign-flip test for the full-control increment gave $p=0.0029296875$ for positivity. The permutation comparison moved the same statistic below zero in both panels, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, median $-0.00212186632044159$, mean $-0.00212186632044159$, and maximum $-0.000779938262202795$. Thus the finite-row verdict is that this $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ packet crosses into the structural-order-confidence layer under the declared conditioning, not that it identifies a folding law.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-gated structural-order contact.}
The same nine-coordinate family had already produced a bounded structural-order association after amino-acid, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls in the translation-gated structural-order panel. There the structural-order readout was explicitly AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structure confidence. The organism-level controlled structural-order readbacks were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685,
\end{aligned}
$$
with all nine coordinates surviving the structural-order surface in each organism. The translation-conditioned boundary here is stricter: it asks whether the downstream predicted-order association remains visible after the translation route and the control set are accounted for. The answer is positive on the finite rows, with the important qualification that the observed layer is predicted structural confidence, not experimentally measured folding, stability, localization, function, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Non-compression context.}
A separate finite compilation diagnostic prevents over-reading the positive structural-order boundary as a general explanatory compression of the $Q6$ residual source. Across four computed organisms, the measured downstream dictionary did not highly compress $P_Q$. The median compressibility was $0.026$, and the organism-level values were $0.024368095798914635$ for Danio rerio, $0.005717621488177116$ for Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, $0.02635576736932188$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.13234136547055853$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, each with permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ in that descriptive variance-partition run. This means the structural-order result is a finite positive boundary contact, while most residual energy remains outside the measured low-dimensional downstream readout set.

\paragraph{Local scope boundary.}
The claim certified by this finite panel is narrow. A $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate may be read against the structural-order-confidence surface only after amino-acid composition, organism identity, $GC3$, coding length, $M$-density, matched mRNA controls where used, and the translation-readout route have been separated. The result does not license a statement that the coordinate directly causes translation, determines folding, establishes physical admissibility, realizes biological function, explains a mechanism, predicts phenotype, or states a universal biological rule. Any one of those stronger assertions would require its own layer-matched measurement and a fresh finite statistic on rows joined at the appropriate biological level.
