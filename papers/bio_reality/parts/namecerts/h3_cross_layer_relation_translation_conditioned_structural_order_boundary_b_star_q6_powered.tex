\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bq6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are evaluated only after amino-acid quotienting and covariate residualization. The coordinate list is
$K\_{AAA}$, $Arg\_{AGR}$, $Ile\_{AUA}$, $Leu\_{CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $Leu\_{UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $Ser\_{UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $Ser\_{UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $Thr\_{ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f\_3$-stress. The controls are the intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the twenty empirical amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected coordinate vectors. In the structural-order contact, the readout is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structural-confidence score. The finite joins were organism matched: $3412$ rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The paired structural-order loading run reported $3736$ matched proteins from $3739$ codon-joined bacterial rows, with hit rate $0.999198$, and $5804$ matched proteins from $5815$ yeast codon-joined rows, with hit rate $0.998108$.

\paragraph{Rank and control geometry.}
The statistic is admissible because the residual coordinate block keeps full coordinate rank after the declared controls. In \emph{E. coli}, the control matrix has $24$ columns, $23$ effective rank, and leaves residual degrees of freedom $3713$; the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ block has rank $9$, matching the nine coordinate directions. In yeast, the same $24$ control columns again have rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom are $5781$, and the residualized coordinate block again has rank $9$. The reported explained energies for the structural-order readout are $4178.935925251874$ in \emph{E. coli} and $44421.14342043593$ in yeast, against readout residual energies $136607.52873622958$ and $620506.1490869389$, respectively. Thus the boundary is not a rank-saturated naming event: the finite matrices retain enough controlled residual dimension for a non-vacuous cross-layer readout.

\paragraph{Observed structural-order association.}
Before the translation-conditioned boundary call is interpreted, the same coordinate family is required to pass through the translation-readout gate and the matched covariates. On the structural-order surface itself, the direct controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ association with mean pLDDT is positive in both organisms. The two-organism summary for $B^*_{Q6}$ predicting structural order has minimum $0.03664544696481631$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, mean and median $0.09855549686555976$, with $2$ positive values out of $2$ and no negative entries. A separate structural-order survival computation reports $R^2\_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ for \emph{E. coli}, with all nine coordinates surviving, and $R^2\_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ for yeast, again with all nine coordinates surviving. These values are finite readout associations after the declared amino-acid, length, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls; they are not a folding experiment.

The powered five-fold run classifies the structural-order boundary as crossing into the structural-order readout layer. The full-control increment in $R^2$ is positive in both organisms, with

The one-sided sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect is $0.0029296875$. The organism call count is $2$ full-control-positive calls out of $2$. The matched permutation surface is negative in both organisms, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, mean and median $-0.00212186632044159$. The finite verdict is therefore not that codon topology explains protein structure. It is narrower: after the declared controls and the translation-conditioning requirement, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block carries a small but consistently positive association with the matched structural-confidence readout in the two organisms for which this panel was computed.

\paragraph{Comparison with adjacent readouts.}
The same coordinate family does not license unrestricted promotion across biological layers. A minimal-compilation test over available low-dimensional readouts found weak compression of the residual source: median compressibility is $0.026$, with organism values $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, each with permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$. The measured readout dictionary used $DL\_{D_o}=1$ for zebrafish, $2$ for \emph{E. coli}, $3$ for human, and $2$ for yeast, while the raw baseline dimensions were $17$, $2$, $19$, and $19$. This says that the $B^*_{Q6}$ source is not mostly absorbed by the measured readout set. An adjacent translation-conditioned stability run is also weaker than the structural-order boundary: the aggregate full-control stability increment has mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip probability $0.946441650390625$, with one mediated-blocked-null organism call and two partial calls. The structural-order result is therefore a specific finite readout crossing, not a general license for protein-stability, degradation-rate, or molecular-function claims.

\paragraph{Local boundary of interpretation.}
The admissible statement is the following finite-row claim: in the computed \emph{E. coli} and yeast panels, amino-acid-residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates retain a positive translation-conditioned association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and $M$-density controls, and the permuted control surface is negative. The result does not by itself establish observed translation dynamics, folding pathway, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, mechanism, or universality. Translation readouts, structural-confidence scores, stability measurements, protein abundance, functional assays, perturbation assays, and physical assays are distinct contact surfaces. A claim at any higher layer requires its own matched rows and its own controlled statistic; the present record supplies only the structural-confidence boundary described above.
