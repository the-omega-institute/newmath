\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record concerns a finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which each $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate is first read as a synonymous residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, then tested against a translation-layer readout before any higher protein-layer interpretation is allowed. The computed $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate list has nine entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The control design used an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected coordinates. The rank diagnostics were nondegenerate in both organisms used for the structural-order test: in \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 the design had $23$ control rank, $9$ residual-coordinate rank, $3713$ residual degrees of freedom after controls, residual response energy $136607.52873622958$, and explained energy $4178.935925251874$; in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} the corresponding values were $23$, $9$, $5781$, $620506.1490869389$, and $44421.14342043593$. Thus the structural-order boundary is not based on a saturated three-row fit or a collapsed coordinate surface; the tested coordinate block retained its full nine-dimensional residual rank after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Structural-order readout.}
The structural-order contact used AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structure-confidence score. It is not an experimental folding measurement and is not a function, phenotype, localization, abundance, or pathway assay. The join was large enough for a finite-row boundary test: the powered structural-order run used $3412$ joined rows for \emph{E. coli} and $5119$ joined rows for budding yeast, with a five-fold evaluation design and a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$. In the earlier translation-gated structural-order computation, the sequence-to-structure join was nearly complete: \emph{E. coli} had $3739$ codon-joined entries, $3736$ matched proteins used, $3736$ structural-order proteins, and hit rate $0.999198$; yeast had $5815$ codon-joined entries, $5804$ matched proteins used, $5804$ structural-order proteins, and hit rate $0.998108$. The direct residual association with predicted structure confidence was positive in both organisms, with $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ for \emph{E. coli} and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ for yeast, and all nine coordinates survived in that bounded structural-order readout.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned statistic.}
The boundary test asks whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block retains a positive association with the structural-order readout after the translation-conditioned control surface has been applied, rather than treating codon geometry as a direct structural mechanism. The powered run reported positive direct order prediction in both organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2 &= 0.03664544696481631,\\
\mathrm{median}\;R^2 &= 0.09855549686555976,\\
\max R^2 &= 0.1604655467663032,\\
 n &= 2.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation-conditioned full-control increment was also positive in both organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\mathrm{median}\;\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.007880037229394898,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.004764742175882462.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign test for positivity gave $p=0.0029296875$. The permuted full-control surface moved in the opposite direction, with maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, median $-0.00212186632044159$, and minimum $-0.003463794378680385$. The finite-row call is therefore $\mathrm{crosses\_to\_structural\_order\_layer}$, with two full-control-positive organism calls and no negative organism call in the powered structural-order panel.

\paragraph{Relation to the compilation and stability checks.}
The structural-order result should be read beside the broader residual-compressibility audit, not as a universal compression law. In the cross-organism compilation check, the measured low-dimensional readout set did not compress $P_Q$ strongly: the reported compressibilities were $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, with median $0.026$ and per-organism permutation value $0.000999000999000999$. The structural-order boundary is therefore a narrow residual association in a particular predicted-confidence layer, not evidence that the $Q6$ residual source has been explained away by a small collection of biological readouts. The linked stability panel is weaker: for the translation-conditioned stability boundary the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ had mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip value $0.946441650390625$, producing a partial rather than a stability-layer crossing. This contrast matters: the present chapter supports a structural-order-confidence boundary in the finite powered panel, while protein stability and degradation remain separate questions unless a layer-matched stability or turnover readout supplies its own finite rows and its own conditioned statistic.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The finite claim is only this: after amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, and matched expression controls where used, the powered $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block retains a positive translation-conditioned association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT in the two-organism structural-order panel. No translation mechanism follows from this result by itself. No ribosome-occupancy, folding-pathway, protein-stability, degradation-rate, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, phenotype, localization, interaction, or universal biological claim follows from the structural-order statistic. Any such statement would require a separate layer-specific measurement contact, an organism- and CDS-matched join, a surviving translation-readout step, and a conditioned downstream statistic showing the claimed layer rather than merely naming it.
