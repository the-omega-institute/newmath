\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This BioReality record tests whether amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates in the $B^*_{Q6}$ family can pass from coding-sequence composition into a downstream structural-order readout only after the translation layer has been kept explicit. The carrier is a finite organism-by-CDS panel: each row contains coding-sequence counts, nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, amino-acid composition controls, $GC3$, CDS length, an $M$-density baseline, matched translation or translation-proxy readouts where available, and a structural-order surface. The nine residual coordinates used in the powered panel are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K_{AAA},\; Arg_{AGR},\; Ile_{AUA},\; Leu_{CUN\;vs\;UUR},\; Leu_{UUA\;vs\;UUG},\\
&Ser_{UCR\;vs\;AGY},\; Ser_{UCA\;vs\;UCG},\; Thr_{ACR\;vs\;ACY},\; f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structural-order readout used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale. In this chapter it is interpreted only as predicted structure-confidence order, not as experimental folding, folding kinetics, function, phenotype, or protein mechanism.

\paragraph{Controlled residual geometry.}
Before any structural-order claim is read from the panel, each $q_i$ coordinate is evaluated after removing the declared background variables. The control family consists of an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions $A,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,K,L,M,N,P,Q,R,S,T,V,W,Y$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline defined by the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. In the translation-gated structural-order run, the joined codon and predicted-order data were nearly complete for the two computed organisms: Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 had $3739$ codon-joined proteins, $3736$ matched proteins used, $3736$ structural-order proteins, and hit rate $0.999198$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $5815$ codon-joined proteins, $5804$ matched proteins used, $5804$ structural-order proteins, and hit rate $0.998108$.

The powered-rank diagnostic was nondegenerate in both organisms. For Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, the control matrix had $24$ columns, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ rank $9$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and residual response energy $136607.52873622958$. For Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the corresponding numbers were $24$ control columns, control rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, residualized rank $9$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and residual response energy $620506.1490869389$. Thus the powered panel is not a rank-saturated three-row artifact; all nine coordinates survive the residualization step as a full coordinate family in the two-organism structural-order computation.

\paragraph{Structural-order contact.}
The direct structural-order association was positive in the finite readout. The earlier gated structural-order computation gave
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K\mbox{-}12}) &= 0.03059081709413562,\\
R^2_{SO}(\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}) &= 0.0715885627979685,
\end{aligned}
$$
with all nine coordinates surviving in both organisms. The powered translation-conditioned structural-order boundary then used $3412$ joined Escherichia coli rows and $5119$ joined Saccharomyces rows, with $5$ folds and a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$. The unconditioned $B^*_{Q6}$ prediction of structural order had $n=2$ organism entries, both positive, with minimum $0.03664544696481631$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, mean $0.09855549686555976$, and median $0.09855549686555976$.

After the full control and translation-conditioning step, the aggregate $\Delta R^2$ surface remained positive in both organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.007880037229394898,\\
\mathrm{mean}\;\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\mathrm{median}\;\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.004764742175882462.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign-flip test for positive full-control improvement gave $p=0.0029296875$. The permuted control was negative in both organism entries, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, mean $-0.00212186632044159$, and median $-0.00212186632044159$. The finite call is therefore $\mathrm{crosses\_to\_structural\_order\_layer}$ for this measured panel: the residual coordinate family retains a small but positive translation-conditioned association with the predicted structural-order surface after the stated controls.

\paragraph{Separation from stability and compilation claims.}
This structural-order boundary should not be conflated with protein stability. A related stability-boundary run over three organism entries was only partial: the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ stability prediction was positive in all three entries, with minimum $0.018837736273789707$, maximum $0.03602995605625481$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and median $0.027727034742559664$, but the full-control translation-conditioned stability $\Delta R^2$ had mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, two negative organism calls, one positive organism call, and sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$. It is therefore not licensed to read this chapter as a protein-stability or degradation-rate claim.

The record is also not a minimal-compilation law. In the broader residual-compressibility test, the measured low-dimensional downstream readout set explained only a small fraction of $P_Q$ in most organisms: Danio rerio had compressibility $0.024368095798914635$ with $DL_{D_o}=1$ and raw baseline $17$; Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 had $0.005717621488177116$ with $DL_{D_o}=2$ and raw baseline $2$; Homo sapiens had $0.02635576736932188$ with $DL_{D_o}=3$ and raw baseline $19$; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $0.13234136547055853$ with $DL_{D_o}=2$ and raw baseline $19$. The median compressibility was $0.026$, and every listed organism had permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ for that descriptive variance-partitioning run. These numbers say that the residual source is not highly compressed by the measured downstream dictionary; they do not weaken the narrower structural-order contact, but they prevent a broad law-like interpretation.

\paragraph{Local admissible reading.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. In a finite two-organism structural-order panel, the amino-acid-quotient $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family remains rank-powered after amino-acid composition, length, $GC3$, organism, and $M$-density controls, and it retains a positive translation-conditioned association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT. The magnitude is small: the weighted full-control improvement is $0.004764742175882462$, and the mean full-control improvement is $0.005284160876443678$. The chapter therefore records a controlled cross-layer statistical contact with predicted structure-confidence order.

No stronger biological claim follows from these rows. The finite association is not itself observed translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, protein function, phenotype, degradation mechanism, or universality. A translation claim requires a layer-matched translation contact; a folding or stability claim requires a layer-matched folding, stability, turnover, pulse-chase, perturbation, or comparable protein-state contact; a mechanism claim requires a separate design showing that conditioning on the translation readout absorbs the direct residual-to-downstream association. Without those contacts, $q_i \to T_j \to$ structural-order language remains a controlled readback of this finite panel, not a general biological mechanism.
