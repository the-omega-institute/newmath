\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The tested object is a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is first residualized away from amino-acid quotient structure and is then tested against a translation readout before any structural-order readout is interpreted.  The coordinate family used in the matched structural-order calculation has nine entries: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The control matrix contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an $M$-density baseline formed on the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  In the structural-order survival calculation this gave, for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, $3739$ codon-joined rows, $3736$ matched proteins, and a structural-order join hit rate of $0.999198$.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the corresponding figures were $5815$ codon-joined rows, $5804$ matched proteins, and a hit rate of $0.998108$.  The later translation-conditioned boundary run used stricter joined matrices with $3412$ rows for \emph{E. coli} and $5119$ rows for yeast.

\paragraph{Rank and residualization checks.}
The design is not a free direct regression from codon geometry to a protein-level descriptor.  The residual coordinate block is required to remain powered after the controls are removed.  In \emph{E. coli}, the control column count was $24$, the residualized control rank was $23$, the residual coordinate rank was $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3713$.  The explained energy was $4178.935925251874$ against a residual response energy of $136607.52873622958$, and the powered-rank flag was true.  In yeast, the same check gave $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for the control block, rank $9$ for the residual coordinate block, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, residual response energy $620506.1490869389$, and again a true powered-rank flag.  Thus the finite row calculation is a controlled residual test rather than a saturated naming exercise.

\paragraph{Structural-order readout.}
The structural-order surface is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, used only as predicted structure-confidence readback.  It is not an experimental folding assay, not protein function, not phenotype, and not physical admissibility.  Before translation conditioning, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ structural-order association was positive in both available organisms.  The finite summary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ predicting order had $n=2$, minimum $0.03664544696481631$, median $0.09855549686555976$, mean $0.09855549686555976$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, with $2$ positive entries and $0$ negative entries.  The earlier structural-order survival calculation reported $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ in \emph{E. coli} and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ in yeast, with all nine residual coordinates surviving in each organism.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary statistic.}
The decisive boundary test conditions the structural-order association on the matched translation readout and the full control set.  In the powered run $1b97387835f7$, with five folds and a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$, the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ remained positive in both organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\min} &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{median}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{mean}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\max} &= 0.007880037229394898.
\end{aligned}
$$
The weighted full-control value was $0.004764742175882462$.  The sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect was $0.0029296875$, and the organism call table contained $2$ full-control-positive calls.  The permutation check moved in the opposite direction: the permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ values had minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, median $-0.00212186632044159$, mean $-0.00212186632044159$, and maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, with $2$ negative entries and $0$ positive entries.  The finite verdict for this run is therefore that the tested $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual panel crosses to the structural-order layer in this bounded, translation-conditioned sense.

\paragraph{Relation to nearby stability and compression checks.}
The structural-order result should not be read as a general protein-state principle.  A separate translation-conditioned stability boundary run, $18a9672b4384$, found only a partial verdict: the full-control stability $\Delta R^2$ summary had median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip probability $0.946441650390625$, with one mediated-blocked-null call and two partial calls.  Likewise, the minimal-compilation calculation does not support a broad compression law: the reported organism compressibilities were $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, with median compressibility $0.026$ and permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$ in each computed organism.  These nearby facts keep the present chapter narrow: the structural-order boundary is positive for the matched finite panel, while stability and global compilation remain separately constrained.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The supported statement is a rank-powered relation of the form $q_i \to T_j \to S_k$, where $q_i$ is an amino-acid-quotient-residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate, $T_j$ is the matched translation readout, and $S_k$ is the predicted structural-order confidence readout.  The reported positive value is the translation-conditioned residual association after amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, matched mRNA controls, and the translation readout have been accounted for.  This does not license a direct claim that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ determines translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  Those claims require independent layer-matched measurements and their own controlled finite-row statistics.  The chapter therefore records a bounded positive boundary crossing to predicted structural-order confidence, not a folding mechanism and not a general biological theorem.
