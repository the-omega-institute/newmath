\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel and admissible readout.}
The record concerns a finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates are evaluated only after amino-acid-quotient residualization. The active coordinate list has nine entries: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. These coordinates are not treated as direct biological measurements. They are finite residual covariates whose admissible use is determined by a controlled matrix from residual coordinate $q_i$ to a translation readout $T_j$, and only then to a downstream readout. In this chapter the downstream readout is predicted structural-order confidence, measured as AlphaFold mean pLDDT on a $0$ to $100$ scale. The interpretation is deliberately narrow: mean pLDDT is a predicted structure-confidence surface, not an experimental folding assay, not physical admissibility, not function, not phenotype, and not a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Controls and rank condition.}
The controlled design uses an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and an $M$-density baseline defined by the fraction of sense codons lying on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. In the Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 panel, the joined codon table has $3739$ entries, the matched structural-order table contributes $3736$ proteins, and $3736$ matched proteins are used, for a hit rate of $0.999198$. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the joined codon table has $5815$ entries, the structural-order table contributes $5804$ proteins, and $5804$ matched proteins are used, for a hit rate of $0.998108$. The controlled design remains nondegenerate in both organisms: the residualized $q$-matrix has rank $9$ in each case, with $23$ control rank, $24$ control columns, and $20$ amino-acid composition controls. The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $3713$ in Escherichia coli and $5781$ in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. The corresponding structural-order residual energies are $136607.52873622958$ and $620506.1490869389$, while the explained energies assigned to the controlled $B^*_{Q6}$ block are $4178.935925251874$ and $44421.14342043593$.

\paragraph{Observed structural-order association.}
The translation-gated structural-order computation reports positive finite association in both organisms. The controlled structural-order scores are $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ for Escherichia coli and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with all nine coordinates surviving on this predicted-confidence readout in each organism. A separate powered five-fold run for the translation-conditioned structural-order boundary uses $3412$ joined Escherichia coli rows and $5119$ joined Saccharomyces cerevisiae rows, with minimum joined-row threshold $300$. The direct $B^*_{Q6}$ prediction of structural order has two positive organism entries, with minimum $0.03664544696481631$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, mean $0.09855549686555976$, and median $0.09855549686555976$.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary statistic.}
The powered boundary run gives a positive full-control residual contribution after conditioning. Across the two organisms, the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ has minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, and two positive entries out of two. The weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ is $0.004764742175882462$. The sign-flip test for positive full-control contribution gives $p=0.0029296875$. The permutation control moves in the opposite direction: permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ has minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, mean and median $-0.00212186632044159$, with two negative entries out of two. The local finite verdict is therefore that this coordinate family crosses into the predicted structural-order-confidence layer under the declared controls, for these two organism panels and this readout.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &\in [0.002688284523492457,\,0.007880037229394898],\\
\overline{\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.004764742175882462,\\
p_{\mathrm{signflip}} &= 0.0029296875.
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Relation to stability and compression contacts.}
The same residual family should not be promoted from this structural-order-confidence result to a protein-stability statement. The separate translation-conditioned stability run is only partial: the full-control stability $\Delta R^2$ has mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, with two negative organism entries and one positive organism entry; its weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ is $-0.001572140012981015$, and the greater-than-zero sign-flip value is $0.946441650390625$. The descriptive compression analysis also refuses a strong compression reading: across computed organisms, median compressibility is $0.026$, with per-organism values $0.024368095798914635$ for Danio rerio, $0.005717621488177116$ for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, $0.02635576736932188$ for Homo sapiens, and $0.13234136547055853$ for Saccharomyces cerevisiae. These auxiliary finite rows say that the structural-order-confidence contact is not a general reduction of $P_Q$ to a small downstream dictionary and is not a stability mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The conclusion is confined to the finite structural-order-confidence panel just described. The residual coordinates may be reported as a controlled association with predicted structural-order confidence only where the rank condition, joined rows, amino-acid composition controls, $GC3$, length, $M$-density, and translation-conditioning requirements are present. No translation mechanism follows from this result by itself. No protein folding pathway, protein stability, degradation rate, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation claim, or organism-independent law follows from the residual coordinates, from tAI or stAI weights, from ribosome-profiling contact names, from mRNA controls, or from bulk proteomics routing unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies rows for that specific claim and the corresponding controlled statistic survives.
