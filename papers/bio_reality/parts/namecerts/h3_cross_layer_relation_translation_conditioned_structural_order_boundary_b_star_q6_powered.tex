\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate is first residualized against amino-acid quotient structure before it is allowed to face a downstream structural-order readout.  The active coordinate list has nine residual directions,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The testable row object is not the bare codon table.  It is the same-CDS matrix obtained after controlling for an intercept, $\log({\rm cds\_len\_nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the M-density baseline, where M-density is the fraction of sense codons lying in the union support of the nine projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ vectors.  Thus the fitted question is whether the residual $q_i$ surface retains a finite association with a structural-order readout after translation-layer gating and the declared controls have already absorbed the simpler sequence explanations.

\paragraph{Rank and row support.}
The powered design is nondegenerate in both organisms used for the structural-order calculation.  In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the control block has $24$ columns, rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, and the residualized $Q$ block has rank $9$ for the $9$ coordinates.  The associated residual response energy is $136607.52873622958$, and the control-explained energy is $4178.935925251874$.  In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the control block again has $24$ columns and rank $23$, with residual degrees of freedom $5781$; the residualized $Q$ block also has rank $9$, with residual response energy $620506.1490869389$ and control-explained energy $44421.14342043593$.  Both organisms therefore satisfy the powered-rank condition rather than producing an all-fit artefact from an underpowered or saturated design.

\paragraph{Matched structural-order contact.}
The structural-order readout used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structural-confidence signal.  It is not an experimental folding assay, not a function assay, not protein abundance, and not a phenotype measurement.  The codon-to-structure-confidence join is nearly complete in the preceding gated structural-order panel: \emph{E. coli} has $3739$ codon-joined CDS rows, $3736$ matched proteins, and a structural-order join hit rate of $0.999198$; yeast has $5815$ codon-joined CDS rows, $5804$ matched proteins, and a hit rate of $0.998108$.  The translation-conditioned powered boundary run then uses $3412$ joined \emph{E. coli} rows and $5119$ joined yeast rows under a five-fold procedure with minimum joined-row threshold $300$.

\paragraph{Finite readout values.}
Before conditioning is summarized as a boundary statement, the direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ prediction of the order readout is finite and positive in both organisms.  Across the two organism entries, the direct order-prediction statistic has minimum $0.03664544696481631$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, mean $0.09855549686555976$, median $0.09855549686555976$, and two positive entries out of two.  The earlier gated structural-order panel reports $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ for \emph{E. coli} with all nine coordinates surviving, and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ for yeast with all nine coordinates surviving.  These values certify a finite structural-confidence association after the amino-acid, GC3, length, and M-density controls, but their meaning remains bounded to the measured readout surface.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary statistic.}
The powered structural-order boundary is decided by the full-control increment after conditioning through the translation readout.  The aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ surface has
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2 &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\max \Delta R^2 &= 0.007880037229394898,\\
{\rm mean}\,\Delta R^2 &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
{\rm median}\,\Delta R^2 &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
{\rm weighted}\,\Delta R^2 &= 0.004764742175882462.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both organism entries are positive, giving two full-control-positive calls out of two.  The one-sided sign-flip probability for a positive full-control surface is $0.0029296875$.  The corresponding permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ values are negative in both entries, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, mean $-0.00212186632044159$, and median $-0.00212186632044159$.  The finite-row verdict is therefore $\mathrm{crosses\_to\_structural\_order\_layer}$: within this panel, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates pass from the translation-gated surface into a predicted structural-confidence readout under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Relation to residual compressibility.}
The result is not a claim that the $Q$ surface is explained away by a small downstream dictionary.  In the separate residual-compressibility calculation, the measured low-dimensional readout set leaves most $P_Q$ energy unabsorbed: the reported cross-organism median compressibility is $0.026$.  The organism values are $0.024368095798914635$ for danio rerio, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, with permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$ in each computed organism.  The structural-order boundary therefore records a specific controlled readout crossing, not a general reduction of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual source to the measured downstream variables.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
This chapter asserts only a finite, controlled association from amino-acid-quotient-residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates through a translation-gated route into AlphaFold mean-pLDDT structural-confidence rows.  It does not assert observed translation realization from modeled adaptation weights, does not assert folding kinetics, degradation rate, thermodynamic stability, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or a universal biological rule.  A mechanism claim would require a separate layer-matched contact showing that conditioning on the matched translation readout attenuates the direct $q_i$ to downstream readout association.  A folding or stability claim would require its own folding, turnover, pulse-chase, degradation, or perturbation contact.  A function or phenotype claim would require an independent functional or phenotype assay.  Without those contacts, the admissible conclusion is the narrower one: in the finite powered panel, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface retains a positive translation-conditioned association with predicted structural-order confidence, and the stronger biological interpretations remain outside the scope of this record.
