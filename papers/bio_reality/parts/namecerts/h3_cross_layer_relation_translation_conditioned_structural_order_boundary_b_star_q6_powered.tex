\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Structural-Order Boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ Residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns a finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are tested against a structural-order readout only after the same residual basis has passed through translation-layer controls. The usable coordinate list has nine entries: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. Each coordinate is already amino-acid-quotient residualized before entering the organism rows, so the structural-order readout is not allowed to inherit an amino-acid-composition explanation by construction. The control block used in the precursor translation-gated structural-order computation contains an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, twenty amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline defined on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors.

\paragraph{Structural-order readout.}
The downstream surface is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, interpreted only as predicted structure-confidence. The joined structural-order boundary computation used $3412$ Escherichia coli K12 MG1655 rows and $5119$ Saccharomyces cerevisiae rows, with five folds and a minimum joined-row requirement of $300$. The experiment status is passed, and the module call is $\mathrm{crosses\_to\_structural\_order\_layer}$. The direct $B^*_{Q6}$ to predicted-order predictive scores across the two organisms were both positive:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{Q\to SO} &= 0.03664544696481631,\\
\operatorname{median} R^2_{Q\to SO} &= 0.09855549686555976,\\
\operatorname{mean} R^2_{Q\to SO} &= 0.09855549686555976,\\
\max R^2_{Q\to SO} &= 0.1604655467663032.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values are finite association scores on the measured panel. They do not identify a folding pathway, a physical state, a biological function, or a universal law.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned boundary statistic.}
The boundary test asks whether the structural-order surface still carries a residual $B^*_{Q6}$ contribution after the matched translation-readout route and the full control block have been accounted for. In the finite run, the full-control structural-order delta was positive in both organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\operatorname{median} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\operatorname{mean} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\max \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &= 0.007880037229394898,\\

$$
The organism-call table has two full-control-positive entries and no negative entries. The sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect is $0.0029296875$. A permuted comparison moved in the opposite direction, with two negative entries and summary values
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{perm}} &= -0.003463794378680385,\\
\operatorname{median} \Delta R^2_{\mathrm{perm}} &= -0.00212186632044159,\\

\end{aligned}
$$
Within this finite panel, the reported result is therefore a translation-conditioned residual association with predicted structure-confidence, not an unconditioned codon-topology claim.

\paragraph{Rank and control sufficiency.}
The precursor structural-order survival computation retained rank for all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates after the control block. In Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, the joined codon table contributed $3739$ rows, $3736$ proteins were matched and used, and the structural-order join hit rate was $0.999198$. The residualized design had $9$ projected $q$ coordinates, $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, explained energy $4178.935925251874$, and response residual energy $136607.52873622958$. In Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the joined codon table contributed $5815$ rows, $5804$ proteins were matched and used, and the structural-order join hit rate was $0.998108$. The residualized design again had $9$ projected $q$ coordinates, $24$ control columns, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, residual degrees of freedom $5781$, explained energy $44421.14342043593$, and response residual energy $620506.1490869389$. Both organisms were marked rank-sufficient under the degenerate-threshold rule $R^2=0.999999$.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and stability boundaries.}
The result should be read beside, but not collapsed into, the nearby protein-stability boundary. The separate stability run used three organisms and was only partial: the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ to stability scores were positive with minimum $0.018837736273789707$, median $0.027727034742559664$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and maximum $0.03602995605625481$, but the full-control translation-conditioned stability delta had weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$ and sign-flip probability $0.946441650390625$. Its organism calls split into two partial cases and one mediated-blocked-null case. Thus the present structural-order boundary is stronger than the stability boundary in this finite row set: predicted structure-confidence crosses the declared conditioned surface, while protein stability remains only partially supported and cannot be imported as a folding or degradation mechanism.

\paragraph{Compression context.}
A broader residual-compressibility computation also prevents over-reading the boundary. Across Danio rerio, Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, Homo sapiens, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae, the measured low-dimensional readout dictionary did not highly compress $P_Q$. The organism compressibilities were $0.024368095798914635$, $0.005717621488177116$, $0.02635576736932188$, and $0.13234136547055853$, with median $0.026$. The corresponding $P_Q$ energies were $605.0092168669606$, $225.84556319953032$, $286.3673197177688$, and $229.86450794006544$ over $13140$, $2517$, $11250$, and $4759$ full rows. Each organism-level permutation probability was $0.000999000999000999$. This context says that the structural-order crossing is a finite, controlled residual association inside one downstream readout, not a reduction of the whole $Q6$ residual source to the available biological measurements.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The only positive claim made here is narrow: in the two-organism structural-order panel, the amino-acid-quotient $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a positive full-control association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after the declared translation-conditioned route and controls, with weighted $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}=0.004764742175882462$ and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$. The readout is predicted structure-confidence, not observed folding. No translation mechanism, ribosome dynamics, protein stability, degradation rate, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurement and the corresponding conditioned statistic is computed.
