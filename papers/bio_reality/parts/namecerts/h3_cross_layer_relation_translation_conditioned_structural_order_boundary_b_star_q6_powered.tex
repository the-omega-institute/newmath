\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record concerns the finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are tested against a downstream structural-order readout only after the translation layer has been accounted for. The coordinate side contains nine amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates: $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The empirical rows used for the structural-order readout are organism-matched coding-sequence rows with amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, and $M$-density controls. The available structural-order joins are finite and explicitly counted: $3412$ joined rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ joined rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The structural-order surface is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale, so it is treated here as predicted structure-confidence, not as an experimental folding assay.

\paragraph{Control geometry.}
The tested design first residualizes the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates against amino-acid composition and the declared sequence controls before asking whether any translation-conditioned downstream association remains. In the upstream structural-order survival computation, both organisms retained full rank for the nine $Q6$ coordinates after controls: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$ for each organism. The control block used $24$ columns, including an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline. The reported residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3713$ for \emph{E. coli} and $5781$ for yeast. The same computation reported structural-order join hit rates of $0.999198$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.998108$ for yeast, with matched-protein counts $3736$ and $5804$, respectively. Thus the finite panel is not a name-only route: it has same-organism, high-hit-rate row support for the structural-order readout surface.

\paragraph{Observed structural-order association.}
The direct $B^*_{Q6}$ structural-order prediction statistic is positive in both organisms supplied to the powered run. Its finite summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\min R^2_{Q6\to SO} &= 0.03664544696481631,\\
\operatorname{median} R^2_{Q6\to SO} &= 0.09855549686555976,\\
\operatorname{mean} R^2_{Q6\to SO} &= 0.09855549686555976,\\
\max R^2_{Q6\to SO} &= 0.1604655467663032,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $n=2$ organisms, $2$ positive entries, and $0$ negative entries. In the earlier same-coordinate structural-order survival table, the organism-level $R^2_{SO}$ values were $0.03059081709413562$ for \emph{E. coli} and $0.0715885627979685$ for yeast, and all nine coordinates survived the positive-threshold screen in both organisms. These values justify treating the record as a finite cross-layer association with predicted structural confidence, while still keeping the interpretation narrower than folding, function, phenotype, or mechanism.

\paragraph{Translation-conditioned readback.}
The decisive statistic for this boundary is the full-control translation-conditioned increment. The powered run reports
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full},\min} &= 0.002688284523492457,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full},\operatorname{mean}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full},\operatorname{median}} &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full},\max} &= 0.007880037229394898,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.004764742175882462.
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign pattern is also finite and unambiguous in the supplied run: $2$ positive full-control entries, $0$ negative full-control entries, and organism call counts consisting of $2$ full-control-positive calls. The sign-flip test gives $p=0.0029296875$ for positivity above zero. The corresponding permuted control has the opposite sign pattern, with maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, mean $-0.00212186632044159$, median $-0.00212186632044159$, minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, $0$ positive entries, and $2$ negative entries. The powered verdict is therefore $\mathrm{crosses\_to\_structural\_order\_layer}$ for this finite readout and this control set.

\paragraph{Relation to low-dimensional compilation.}
The structural-order boundary should not be confused with a claim that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual source is mostly compressed by measured downstream readouts. The separate minimal-compilation run reports low residual compressibility across the computed organisms: $0.024368095798914635$ in zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ in human, and $0.13234136547055853$ in yeast, with median compressibility $0.026$ and permutation $p=0.000999000999000999$ in each listed organism. The selected readout dimensions were small: $DL_{D_o}=1$ for zebrafish, $2$ for \emph{E. coli}, $3$ for human, and $2$ for yeast, while the raw baseline dimensions were $17$, $2$, $19$, and $19$. Thus the present positive structural-order boundary is a residual association on a declared readout surface, not a statement that the full $P_Q$ source is explained by the available downstream dictionary.

\paragraph{Boundary against protein-stability promotion.}
A nearby stability-oriented run gives only a partial result and is not promoted into this structural-order record. In that finite stability run, the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ stability summary had mean $0.02753157569086806$ over $n=3$, but the full-control translation-conditioned stability increment had weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, and sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$. Its organism calls were $1$ mediated-blocked-null and $2$ partial. This contrast is important: the structural-order boundary is supported for predicted structure-confidence rows in the two-organism powered panel, while protein stability, degradation rate, turnover mechanism, and folding quality require their own layer-matched contacts and statistics.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite claim is therefore: after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, translation conditioning, and simpler-baseline checks, the supplied $B^*_{Q6}$ residual panel retains a positive association with an AlphaFold mean-pLDDT structural-order surface in \emph{E. coli} and yeast. The empirical statement is deliberately narrow. It does not say that the residual coordinates cause translation, specify a ribosome mechanism, measure folding kinetics, prove physical admissibility, establish molecular function, imply a phenotype, or define a universal biological law. Those stronger readings would require independent contacts at the corresponding biological layer and a same-row statistic showing that the required translation step survives before the downstream readout is interpreted.
