\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The positive call is deliberately narrow.  The structural-order surface is a joined protein-level confidence or order readout, not a folding trajectory, not a physical admissibility assay, not a functional assay, and not a phenotype measurement.  The earlier structural-order contact used AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale as predicted structure confidence, with $3736$ matched proteins in \textit{E. coli} from $3739$ codon-joined rows and $5804$ matched proteins in yeast from $5815$ codon-joined rows.  The corresponding join hit rates were $0.999198$ and $0.998108$.  In that bounded setting the structural-order survival summaries were $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ for \textit{E. coli} and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ for yeast, with all nine coordinates retained as surviving coordinates in each organism.  These values support a finite association between the residual coordinate panel and a predicted structural-confidence surface after controls; they do not identify a biochemical folding mechanism.
