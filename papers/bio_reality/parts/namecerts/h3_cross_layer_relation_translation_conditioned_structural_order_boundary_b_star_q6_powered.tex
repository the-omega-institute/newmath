\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Object of the finite panel.}
The record tested here is a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each synonymous residual coordinate $q_i$ is formed only after quotienting away the first amino-acid composition layer. The admissible readback has three ordered surfaces: residual codon-coordinate rows, matched translation readouts, and a downstream structural-order readout. The downstream readout is not allowed to be read directly from $q_i$. It is interpreted only after the same coordinate has been tested against the translation layer under controls for amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, an $M$-density baseline, and the matched mRNA controls available to the translation panel. In this chapter, the downstream readout is the AlphaFold mean pLDDT surface used as predicted structural-order confidence, not an experimental folding assay and not a functional phenotype.

\paragraph{Coordinate and control geometry.}
The projected $B^\ast_{Q6}$ basis contains nine residual coordinates:
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress. The structural-order survival computation used 20 amino-acid composition proportions, $\log(\mathrm{cds\_len\_nt})$, $GC3$, and the $M$-density support baseline as controls. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 MG1655, the control matrix had $24$ columns, rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $3713$, and the residualized coordinate matrix retained rank $9$. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the same control count and rank appeared, with residual degrees of freedom $5781$ and residualized coordinate rank $9$. Thus the tested coordinate family was not collapsed by the control surface in either organism.

\paragraph{Observed structural-order contact.}
The layer-matched structural-order experiment was run with $5$ folds and a minimum joined-row threshold of $300$. The joined structural-order rows were $3412$ for \emph{E. coli} and $5119$ for yeast. In the companion translation-gated structural-order contact, the codon-to-structure join was nearly complete: \emph{E. coli} had $3739$ codon-joined CDS rows, $3736$ matched proteins, and a structural-order join hit rate of $0.999198$; yeast had $5815$ codon-joined CDS rows, $5804$ matched proteins, and a hit rate of $0.998108$. The structural readout is therefore a finite, organism-matched protein surface with high identifier coverage for the two organisms actually tested.

\paragraph{Finite statistic.}
Let $Q$ denote the residualized nine-column $B^\ast_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix, $Z$ the control matrix, $T$ the matched translation readout, and $O$ the predicted structural-order confidence surface. The reported statistic is the additional cross-validated explanatory contribution of $Q$ to $O$ after the full control and translation-conditioned design has been applied. The finite-row result is summarized by
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}(\textit{E. coli},\textit{yeast}) &\in \{0.002688284523492457,\ 0.007880037229394898\},\\
\operatorname{mean}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}) &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\operatorname{median}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}) &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{weighted}} &= 0.004764742175882462.
\end{aligned}
$$
Both organisms were positive under the full-control statistic. The sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect was $p=0.0029296875$. Under permutation, the corresponding deltas were negative in both organisms, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, mean $-0.00212186632044159$, and median $-0.00212186632044159$.

\paragraph{Readout magnitude before conditioning.}
The direct $B^\ast_{Q6}$ structural-order prediction surface was positive but modest. Across the two organisms the recorded $R^2$ summary had minimum $0.03664544696481631$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, mean $0.09855549686555976$, median $0.09855549686555976$, and two positive entries out of two. The earlier translation-gated structural-order panel recorded organism-specific structural-order $R^2$ values of $0.03059081709413562$ in \emph{E. coli} and $0.0715885627979685$ in yeast, with all nine coordinates surviving the finite structural-order regression surface in each organism. These values justify reporting a bounded structural-order crossing, but their scale also keeps the interpretation descriptive rather than mechanistic.

\paragraph{Boundary against protein-stability promotion.}
A nearby stability boundary was also computed, but it does not supply the structural-order claim. In the stability run, three organisms joined enough rows for a $5$-fold computation, and the direct $B^\ast_{Q6}$ stability prediction summary was positive with minimum $0.018837736273789707$, maximum $0.03602995605625481$, mean $0.02753157569086806$, and median $0.027727034742559664$. After full controls, however, the aggregate stability delta had mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip probability $0.946441650390625$. Its finite verdict was only partial, with one mediated-blocked null call and two partial calls. Consequently, the present chapter does not infer protein stability, degradation rate, folding quality, or turnover mechanism from the structural-order result.

\paragraph{Relation to residual compressibility.}
The same coordinate family was tested in a minimal compilation-law panel and was not compressed by the measured low-dimensional downstream readouts. The per-organism compressibilities were $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast, with median $0.026$. Each organism had permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$ in that descriptive variance-partition test. This matters for interpretation: the structural-order crossing is not a statement that the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual source has been reduced to translation, stability, or a small downstream dictionary. It is a finite positive relation at one downstream readout surface after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The finite result supports the following bounded statement. In the two-organism structural-order panel, $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates retain a small positive translation-conditioned association with predicted structural-order confidence after the specified control surface, with both organism entries positive and the permutation comparison negative. The accepted layer label is therefore a structural-order boundary crossing for this finite panel, not a general biological law.

\paragraph{Local non-claims.}
No translation mechanism follows from this chapter unless a separate measured translation contact supplies the same-row translation statistic. No folding mechanism, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, degradation mechanism, protein-stability mechanism, or universality follows from the pLDDT association. Modeled tAI or stAI weights, codon-usage tables, identifier joins, rank sufficiency, and the residual-coordinate geometry are not by themselves biological observations at those higher layers. Any such interpretation would require its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic.
