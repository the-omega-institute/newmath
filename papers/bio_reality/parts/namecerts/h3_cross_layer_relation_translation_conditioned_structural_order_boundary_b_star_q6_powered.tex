\subsection{Translation-Conditioned Structural-Order Boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record is a finite organism-by-CDS panel in which each coding sequence carries amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$, matched translation-layer readouts when available, matched mRNA controls, and a downstream structural-order readout surface. The intended readback is not a direct path from codon topology to protein structure. It is a gated matrix of the form $q_i \to T_j \to O_k$, where $q_i$ is a residual codon-coordinate entry, $T_j$ is a translation readout, and $O_k$ is a structural-order readout. The controls include amino-acid composition, organism, $GC3$, CDS length, $M$-density, and the available mRNA covariates. Thus the tested object is a residual association after composition and sequence-scale controls, not an assertion that synonymous codon geometry itself observes translation, folding, stability, function, or phenotype.

\paragraph{Coordinate set and admissibility.}
The powered coordinate panel contains nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The residualization gate is nondegenerate in both organisms used for the structural-order readout. In \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, the control matrix has $24$ columns with rank $23$, the residualized coordinate matrix has rank $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $3713$. In \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the same $24$-column control design has rank $23$, the residualized coordinate matrix again has rank $9$, and the residual degrees of freedom after controls are $5781$. The powered-rank condition is therefore satisfied in both rows of the organism panel, so the reported association is not the artifact of a rank-collapsed coordinate family.

\paragraph{Structural-order contact.}
The structural-order readout used here is AlphaFold mean pLDDT on the $0$ to $100$ scale. It is treated only as predicted structural-order confidence. It is not an experimental folding measurement, not physical admissibility, not molecular function, and not a phenotype. The joined structural-order boundary contains $3412$ usable rows for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5119$ usable rows for \emph{S. cerevisiae}. The supporting structural-order-and-codon join diagnostics record $3739$ codon-joined \emph{E. coli} entries, $3736$ matched proteins used, and a structural-order join hit rate of $0.999198$. For yeast, the corresponding values are $5815$ codon-joined entries, $5804$ matched proteins used, and a hit rate of $0.998108$. These high hit rates justify evaluating the readout boundary on the finite joined panel while still keeping the prediction-confidence surface distinct from any experimental structure assay.

The direct $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual-to-order summary is positive in both organisms: the finite list has $n=2$, minimum $0.03664544696481631$, median $0.09855549686555976$, mean $0.09855549686555976$, maximum $0.1604655467663032$, positive count $2$, and negative count $0$. The translation-conditioned full-control increment is smaller, as expected for a downstream boundary statistic, but it remains positive in both rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}} &: \min = 0.002688284523492457,\\
\operatorname{median}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}) &= 0.005284160876443678,\\
\operatorname{mean}(\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{full}}) &= 0.005284160876443678,\\

\end{aligned}
$$
The weighted full-control increment is $0.004764742175882462$. The sign test for being greater than zero gives $p=0.0029296875$, and the organism calls are two full-control-positive entries. The permutation control moves in the opposite direction: its two-entry summary has minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, median $-0.00212186632044159$, mean $-0.00212186632044159$, maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, negative count $2$, and positive count $0$. The finite-row verdict is therefore that the tested residual coordinates cross to the structural-order-confidence layer under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation-gated structural-order panel.}
The companion translation-gated structural-order computation reports $R^2_{SO}=0.03059081709413562$ for \emph{E. coli} with all $9$ coordinates surviving on the structural-order readout, and $R^2_{SO}=0.0715885627979685$ for yeast with all $9$ coordinates again retained. The explained energies under the residualized design are $4178.935925251874$ and $44421.14342043593$, respectively, against residual readout energies $136607.52873622958$ and $620506.1490869389$. These numbers support the same narrow conclusion as the boundary statistic: the finite panel detects a controlled association between the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate family and predicted structural-order confidence. They do not identify a folding pathway and do not promote the residual coordinates into a structural mechanism.

\paragraph{Stability and compilation controls.}
The nearby protein-stability boundary does not justify a stronger reading. Its powered stability test has status passed but only a partial verdict: the full-control stability increment has $n=3$, mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, minimum $-0.004953854025603666$, maximum $0.0006378518321674687$, positive count $1$, negative count $2$, and sign-flip $p=0.946441650390625$. Hence the present structural-order-confidence result must not be converted into protein stability, degradation rate, turnover, folding quality, or phenotype. The minimal-compilation computation gives the same warning from another direction. Across computed organisms, the median compressibility is $0.026$, with per-organism compressibility $0.024368095798914635$ for zebrafish, $0.005717621488177116$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.02635576736932188$ for human, and $0.13234136547055853$ for yeast. The residual source is therefore not highly compressed by the measured low-dimensional readout set. The structural-order boundary is a finite controlled association, not a universal compression law.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The valid conclusion is the following finite-row statement: in the available \emph{E. coli} and yeast structural-order-confidence panels, amino-acid-quotient residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates retain a positive translation-conditioned association with AlphaFold mean pLDDT after the declared controls, with weighted full-control $\Delta R^2=0.004764742175882462$ and sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$. Nothing stronger follows from this chapter. No translation mechanism follows unless a matched translation contact supplies it. No protein stability, degradation, folding mechanism, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or organism-wide biological rule follows from the structural-order-confidence statistic. Any such claim requires its own layer-matched contact, its own joined rows, and its own controlled statistic after the same $q_i \to T_j$ gate has survived.
