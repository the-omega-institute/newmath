\subsection{Translation-conditioned structural-order boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bq6-translation-conditioned-structural-order-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This chapter records a finite organism-by-coding-sequence panel in which $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are tested against a structure-adjacent readout only after the translation layer has been made explicit. The carrier consists of amino-acid-quotient residual coordinates $q_i$, matched translation readouts $T_j$, matched mRNA controls, and a structural-order readout surface. In the present finite run the structural-order surface is AlphaFold mean pLDDT, interpreted only as predicted structure-confidence on the $0$ to $100$ scale. It is not an experimental folding assay, not a stability assay, and not a functional or phenotypic measurement.

\paragraph{Residual basis and control geometry.}
The nine retained $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$. Each coordinate is interpreted only after quotienting out amino-acid composition and after adding the declared covariates: intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length\ in\ nt})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, where $M$-density is the fraction of sense codons on the union support of the nine projected $B^*_{Q6}$ vectors. The powered design is nondegenerate in both organisms used for the structural-order readout. \paragraph{Relation to neighboring finite runs.}
The same residual source is not globally collapsed by the measured low-dimensional downstream dictionary. In the minimal-compilation computation, the final residual fraction remains $0.9756319042010854$ in zebrafish, $0.9942823785118229$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.9736442326306781$ in human, and $0.8676586345294415$ in yeast. This matters because the structural-order boundary is not a statement that $P_Q$ has been reduced to a simple downstream dictionary. It is a finite, controlled crossing into one structure-confidence readout. The translation-conditioned stability run gives a weaker neighboring outcome: the direct stability prediction summary has $n=3$ with mean $0.02753157569086806$, but the aggregate full-control stability increment has mean $-0.0025553576666936064$, median $-0.0033500708066446228$, weighted value $-0.001572140012981015$, and sign-flip probability $0.946441650390625$. The structural-order result should therefore not be generalized into a broad protein-state claim.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite result supports only a translation-conditioned residual association with a predicted structural-order confidence surface in the organisms and rows named above. It does not show observed translation dynamics unless a measured translation contact supplies the relevant same-row readout. It does not show protein folding, degradation, turnover, localization, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, causal mechanism, or a universal biological rule. Modeled tAI or stAI weights may help define a bounded translation-readout route, but they are not survival evidence for protein stability or structural mechanism. A downstream interpretation is admissible only when the same $q_i \to T_j$ entry survives under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls, and when a separate layer-matched measurement supplies the downstream readout.
