\subsection{Position-resolved $f_3$-stress as a bounded cross-layer test}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The object recorded here is a proposed finite table of coding-sequence windows.  Each row is required to carry an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout measured on the same coding window, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates.  The intended survival entry is not a free-standing biological assertion.  It is the controlled row statistic
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) > 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or amino-acid bins, position bins, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, simpler codon baselines, coverage and mappability limits, and rank checks.  The asymmetry condition is also local: the packet asks whether context-specific wobble weights separate $w_c$ from $w_c^{f_3}$ in the same window panel.  Without that joined panel, $f_3$-stress remains a coordinate and not a measured translation effect.

\paragraph{Recorded finite contacts.}
The available finite records support the existence of several relevant audit surfaces, but they do not yet supply the required same-window matrix.  The linked random-promoter expression run for claim \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.cis\_regulatory\_grammar.random\_promoter\_tf\_motif\_expression\_boundary\_deboer2020} records $75{,}111$ designed sequences, $8{,}027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subsample of $2{,}500$ high-quality rows, $200$ actual permutations, subsample seed $20260239$, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  This is a concrete finite-row expression surface, but it is not a coding-window ribosome-occupancy table and therefore cannot certify $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$.

A separate translation-conditioned structural-order run, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_conditioned\_structural\_order\_boundary.b\_star\_q6\_powered}, records five folds, minimum joined count $300$, and two organism-level joined structural-order panels: $3{,}412$ rows for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5{,}119$ rows for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Its aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ values are positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$ for positivity.  The same run reports a weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ of $0.004764742175882462$ and a verdict string \texttt{crosses\_to\_structural\_order\_layer}.  These numbers show that another bounded Q6-linked cross-layer table has passed its own finite test, but they do not transfer to the present $f_3$ position-resolved translation-survival claim.

The finite-state dwell transducer record \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.order\_memory\_execution\_signal.b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered} is recorded as \texttt{passed} under experiment id \texttt{b\_star\_q6\_finite\_state\_dwell\_transducer\_powered}.  The translation-initiation contact \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_initiation.utr5\_features\_ribosome\_load\_sample2019} records $80{,}000$ rows and runtime $172.864$ seconds, with cross-sample status null.  Both facts are useful as finite-row precedents for bounded readout accounting, but neither supplies the position-indexed CDS-window table needed here.

\paragraph{Name certificate interpretation.}
The certificate is therefore a boundary certificate rather than a positive survival certificate.  It names the minimal data shape needed for a future positive claim: same-organism, same-CDS, same-window ordered coding rows; local codon counts; bounded tAI or stAI wobble-weight rows; matched mRNA abundance; and codon-indexed ribosome-footprint occupancy or an explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout.  A row is admissible only when the $f_3$ residual is computed after amino-acid quotienting and when the readout is evaluated on the matched position window.  The rank condition prevents a saturated or underpowered surface from being reported as survival, and the simpler-baseline ablation prevents a generic codon-usage predictor from being relabeled as an $f_3$-stress effect.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The present record is ready as a BioReality null-boundary entry.  It states a concrete finite-row target and preserves the separation between coordinate geometry, modeled adaptation weights, expression surfaces, ribosome readouts, and downstream biological layers.  The support condition for promotion is narrow: a held-out model containing the $f_3$-stress term must improve the position-resolved translation readout after all declared controls, and the improvement must remain positive under rank checks and simpler-baseline ablations.  Until that condition is met on joined rows, the record cannot be read as translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation, causation, or universality.  The deBoer-style finite expression table, the powered structural-order record, the dwell-transducer pass, and the UTR5 ribosome-load sample are cited only as neighboring finite audit surfaces; they do not substitute for the missing same-window ribosome-occupancy statistic.
