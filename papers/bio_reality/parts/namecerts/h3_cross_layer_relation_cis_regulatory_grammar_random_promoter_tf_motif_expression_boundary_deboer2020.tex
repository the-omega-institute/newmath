\subsection{Position-resolved $f_3$-stress as a bounded cross-layer boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Finite-row carrier.}
The record considered here is not a general law about synonymous codons.  Its carrier is a finite table of coding-sequence windows.  A row is admissible only when it contains, in the same organism and the same ordered coding sequence, an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after the amino-acid quotient, local synonymous codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates.  Thus the proposed object is a window-level relation rather than a codon-usage slogan: the row must bind the coordinate, the readout, and the controls before any survival statement is meaningful.

\paragraph{Numerical contact currently available.}
The finite empirical run attached to the named claim is the de Boer random-promoter transcriptional-expression surface.  It contains $75{,}111$ designed sequences, of which $8{,}027$ are high-quality rows.  The recorded analysis used $2{,}500$ high-quality rows with subsample seed $20260239$, ran $200$ permutations, and took $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  These numbers are concrete row-count and runtime facts for the promoter-expression contact, but they do not by themselves supply coding-sequence windows, ribosome-footprint rows, wobble-weight rows, or matched mRNA denominators for an $f_3$-stress translation statistic.  The linked finite-state dwell transducer record is marked as passed, and the linked translation-conditioned structural-order record is also marked as passed on two organisms with $3{,}412$ joined rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5{,}119$ joined rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In that separate structural-order record, the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ is positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean $0.005284160876443678$, and sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$.  These linked values show that nearby translation-conditioned finite-row analyses can be powered, but they are not position-resolved $f_3$-stress ribosome-occupancy rows for the present claim.

\paragraph{Statistic that would be tested.}
For an admissible window table $T$, let $q_{f_3}(r)$ denote the residual $f_3$-stress coordinate in row $r$, let $T_{\mathrm{pos}}(r)$ denote the matched position-resolved translation readout, and let $Z(r)$ collect amino-acid composition or position bins, GC3, CDS length, M-density, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage, mappability, and the simpler baseline family.  The proposed survival entry is a controlled held-out improvement,
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
&= \operatorname{Score}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\sim Z+q_{f_3})
   -\operatorname{Score}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\sim Z).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign convention is deliberately narrow: positive survival means that the $f_3$ coordinate improves the held-out translation readout after the listed controls and ablations.  It does not mean that the coordinate explains protein abundance, folding, localization, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or a universal translation rule.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry condition.}
The coordinate is eligible for testing only when the wobble-weight surface is not symmetric with respect to the $f_3$ direction.  In row notation this asks for a context in which $w_c\neq w_c^{f_3}$ is observed or explicitly bounded, not merely assumed from codon geometry.  Modeled tAI or stAI weights may provide a bounded codon-adaptation surface, but a modeled weight is not an observed ribosome-footprint row.  It can help define the tested contrast; it cannot replace the position-resolved translation readout.

\paragraph{Current boundary verdict.}
The present finite evidence does not yet contain the joined matrix required for $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$.  The promoter-expression run contributes real finite counts and a permutation analysis, and the linked translation-conditioned structural-order run contributes positive controlled $\Delta R^2$ values on two organism panels, but neither contact is the same-window coding-sequence table required here.  Therefore the chapter records a boundary claim: the $f_3$-stress coordinate remains eligible for a position-resolved translation-survival test only after same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA controls, and ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency rows are joined and evaluated under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows from codon geometry, review routing, promoter-expression rows, modeled adaptation weights, or the notation $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ alone.  No protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, biological function, system phenotype, mechanism, or universality follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies rows at that layer and its own controlled statistic is computed.  A future positive $S_{f_3}$ entry would be a bounded window-level translation-readout survival result, not a license to infer downstream biology without additional measured contacts.
