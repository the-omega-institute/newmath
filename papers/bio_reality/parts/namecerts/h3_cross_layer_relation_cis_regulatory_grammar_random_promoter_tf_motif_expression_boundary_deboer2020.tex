\subsection{A finite-row boundary for the $f_3$-stress translation-readout test}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-boundary-deboer}

\paragraph{Claim surface.}
This record treats the $f_3$-stress coordinate as a proposed cross-layer variable only after it has been placed in a finite coding-sequence window table.  A row of the intended table contains an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous-codon counts, a wobble-weight context with possible asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$, a matched position-indexed ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, matched mRNA abundance, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The proposed statistic is not a statement about codon geometry alone.  It is the controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition or position-bin controls, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density, matched mRNA abundance, codon-count controls, and simpler baseline models.  The entry is allowed to be positive only if it improves held-out prediction after those controls and ablations.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
The finite run attached to the promoter-expression boundary has experiment run id $8d1d1ac78ab7$.  It reports $75111$ designed sequences, $8027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subset of $2500$ high-quality rows, subsample seed $20260239$, $200$ actual permutations, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  These numbers are usable as finite-row bookkeeping for the associated BioReality packet, but they are not the joined CDS-window table required for $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)$.  In particular, this finite promoter-design panel does not itself contain same-organism, same-CDS, same-window codon counts, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA controls, and codon-indexed ribosome-footprint occupancy rows.  The present chapter therefore records a boundary test, not a positive translation-readout finding.

\paragraph{Admissible test.}
The admissible BioReality test is a same-row comparison.  First, construct the window table by joining ordered coding-sequence coordinates, local synonymous-codon counts, bounded tAI or stAI wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint occupancy or an explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout.  Second, compute the residual $f_3$ coordinate only after amino-acid quotienting, so that the statistic is not merely an amino-acid-composition effect.  Third, fit the controlled survival surface with the control set $Z$ and require a positive held-out improvement over amino-acid, position-bin, GC3, length, M-density, matched mRNA, codon-count, and simpler-baseline alternatives.  Fourth, check rank sufficiency; a saturated or tiny design is a refusal boundary, not support.

\paragraph{Current verdict.}
The current finite data support the existence of a reviewable boundary record, not the biological claim itself.  The packet is admissible as a request for a position-resolved translation-readout statistic, because the necessary contacts are explicitly named: codon-usage rows, wobble-weight rows, matched mRNA abundance, and ribosome-footprint occupancy windows.  It is not yet admissible as a positive $f_3$ translation-survival result, because the same-window joined matrix and the computed $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}} \mid Z)$ entry are absent.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological law follows from the $f_3$ residual coordinate, wobble-weight asymmetry, modeled tAI or stAI weights, promoter-expression bookkeeping, or the existence of a review route.  Those stronger claims require their own layer-matched contacts and computed statistics.  Until the joined ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency table exists, the scientific content of this chapter is the finite boundary condition: the $f_3$ coordinate may be tested against a controlled position-resolved translation readout, but it has not yet survived that test.
