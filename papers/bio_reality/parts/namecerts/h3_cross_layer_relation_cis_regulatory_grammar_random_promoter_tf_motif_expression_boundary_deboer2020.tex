\subsection{Finite-window boundary for $f_3$-stress translation survival}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite coding-window table rather than a free-standing biological law.  A row is eligible only when it carries the ordered CDS window, the local synonymous codon counts, the $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid content, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched mRNA covariate, amino-acid or position-bin controls, and a same-window translation readout.  The intended readout is position-resolved ribosome occupancy; a translation-efficiency row may serve only when its construction is explicitly bounded and identifier-matched to the same CDS and condition.  Thus the object is not a codon-usage claim in isolation.  It is a proposed finite statistic on joined rows.

The survival entry is written locally as $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$, where $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is the position-resolved translation readout and $Z$ contains the controls that must be present before any positive statement is allowed.  In the notation of this chapter the admissible test has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)
  &= \Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(T_{\mathrm{pos}};
      f_3\text{-stress},\; Z\bigr), \\
Z
  &= \{\mathrm{AA},\;\mathrm{position\ bin},\;\mathrm{GC3},\;\mathrm{length},\;M\text{-density},\;\mathrm{mRNA},\;\mathrm{codon\ counts}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}$ denotes a positive held-out improvement over the matched baseline family, not an in-sample association.  The chapter therefore treats $f_3$-stress as a bounded coordinate whose biological contact is still conditional on the same-window readout matrix.

\paragraph{Observed finite anchors.}
The finite evidence packet carries one directly verified row-count anchor from the promoter-motif boundary record.  The De Boer boundary run reports $75{,}111$ designed sequences, $8{,}027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subsample of $2{,}500$ high-quality rows, $200$ actual permutations, subsample seed $20260239$, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  These numbers certify that the broader BioReality registry is operating with explicit finite panels and recorded run identifiers, but they do not themselves supply the missing CDS-window ribosome-footprint matrix required for this $f_3$-stress translation-survival claim.

A second finite anchor comes from the translation-conditioned structural-order boundary, which has status passed on two organisms and five folds.  Its joined structural-order counts are $3{,}412$ rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5{,}119$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The reported aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ values are positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, median $0.005284160876443678$, mean $0.005284160876443678$, and maximum $0.007880037229394898$.  The permutation comparison is negative in both organisms, with mean $-0.00212186632044159$, and the sign-flip probability for full-control improvement greater than zero is $0.0029296875$.  These values show that the registry can represent controlled cross-layer survival when the relevant joined table exists.  They are not imported as proof of the present $f_3$ coordinate; they only calibrate what a powered finite-row readback looks like.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry condition.}
The proposed $f_3$ entry is meaningful only when the wobble-weight surface fails to be symmetric along the same residual direction.  Locally this is the requirement
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_c &\neq w_c^{f_3}, \\
\mathrm{rank}\bigl(f_3\text{-stress}, Z, T_{\mathrm{pos}}\bigr) &> \mathrm{rank\ floor}, \\
\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}} &> 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first line prevents a purely geometric codon-window coordinate from being mistaken for a translation measurement.  The second line prevents saturated or rank-degenerate fits from being reported as survival.  The third line is the promotion gate: without positive held-out improvement after the listed controls and ablations, the finite row remains a boundary request rather than an observed cross-layer relation.

\paragraph{Readout separation.}
Modeled tAI or stAI weights may enter only as bounded organism-scoped codon-adaptation annotations.  They do not observe ribosome movement, do not replace footprint occupancy, and do not create translation efficiency without mRNA and RPF rows joined under the same condition.  Similarly, codon-usage summaries may supply codon-count or frequency covariates, but they do not supply the same-CDS translation readout.  The matched mRNA contact is a control surface for separating transcript abundance from translation-layer signal; it is not itself a ribosome-occupancy measurement.

For this chapter the required finite panel is therefore stricter than a contact list.  It must contain same-organism, same-CDS, same-window rows with ordered coding coordinates, codon counts, bounded wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and codon-indexed or local-window ribosome-footprint occupancy.  Only after that join exists can the statistic $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}}\mid Z)$ be estimated and compared against amino-acid composition, position-bin, GC3, length, $M$-density, mRNA, codon-count, and simpler-baseline ablations.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion boundary.}
No translation realization follows from $f_3$-stress alone.  No protein abundance, folding state, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from the coordinate, from wobble asymmetry, from modeled tAI or stAI, from codon usage, or from review eligibility.  A positive claim is permitted only at the level of the same-row statistic actually computed.  Downstream layers require their own matched contacts and their own finite statistics.

The current chapter is therefore a controlled boundary statement.  It identifies the exact finite-row object needed for the $f_3$-stress member of $B^*_Q6$ to survive as a cross-layer coordinate, records the available finite anchors from the registry, and keeps the biological interpretation below translation realization until the position-resolved ribosome-occupancy matrix is attached and passes the stated break condition.
