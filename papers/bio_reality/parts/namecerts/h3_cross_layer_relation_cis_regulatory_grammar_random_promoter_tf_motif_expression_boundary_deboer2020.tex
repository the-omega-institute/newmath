\subsection{Position-resolved $f_3$-stress as a bounded translation-readout boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is a finite-row test object for the $f_3$-stress member of the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis.  A row is admissible only when it contains, in the same coding-sequence window, the residual $f_3$ coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid identity, local synonymous-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The intended table is not a statement about translation in general; it is a controlled survival table for a single coordinate class.  The row schema is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
  r &= (g,c,w,t,m,z),\\
  g &:= \text{the } f_3\text{-stress residual coordinate},\\
  c &:= \text{local synonymous codon counts},\\
  w &:= \text{context-specific wobble weights},\\
  t &:= \text{same-window translation readout},\\
  m &:= \text{matched mRNA abundance control},\\
  z &:= \text{amino-acid, position, GC3, length, and density controls}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The survival entry is written locally as $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$.  Its support condition is positive held-out improvement after amino-acid composition, position-bin, GC3, CDS length, methionine-density, mRNA, codon-count, wobble-weight, rank, and simpler-baseline ablations.  The notation is only a name for this finite statistic.  It is not already computed merely because the coordinate, contact names, or review route exists.

\paragraph{Observed finite facts.}
The finite evidence currently attached to the named record is the run with identifier \texttt{8d1d1ac78ab7}.  That run reports $75{,}111$ designed rows, $8{,}027$ high-quality rows, and an analysis subsample of $2{,}500$ high-quality rows under seed $20{,}260{,}239$.  The permutation count is $200$, and the recorded runtime is $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  These numbers establish that the associated BioReality surface has a concrete finite analysis trace, not merely a symbolic route.  However, the recorded trace is attached to the random-promoter transcriptional boundary identified by \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.cis\_regulatory\_grammar.random\_promoter\_tf\_motif\_expression\_boundary\_deboer2020}; it does not by itself supply same-CDS, same-window ribosome-footprint rows for the $f_3$-stress translation-survival packet.

A separate linked translation-conditioned structural-order run, \texttt{1b97387835f7}, is informative only as a comparison point for how a positive cross-layer statistic is represented when it exists.  It reports five folds and two joined organisms, with $3{,}412$ joined \emph{Escherichia coli} entries and $5{,}119$ joined \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} entries.  In that linked run, the aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ values are positive in both organisms, with minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, and a sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$ for being greater than zero.  The permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ values are negative in both organisms, with minimum $-0.003463794378680385$, maximum $-0.000779938262202795$, and mean and median $-0.00212186632044159$.  These linked numbers do not certify the present $f_3$ position-resolved translation claim; they only show that the project records finite positive and permuted controls when an appropriate joined table has been computed.

\paragraph{Wobble asymmetry condition.}
The proposed $f_3$ coordinate can become a translation-readout candidate only when the context-specific wobble surface distinguishes the original coordinate from its $f_3$-transformed counterpart.  Locally, the condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  w_c \neq w_c^{f_3},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $w_c$ is the observed or explicitly bounded wobble weight in the row context and $w_c^{f_3}$ is the corresponding weight after applying the $f_3$ symmetry to the synonymous coordinate.  This inequality cannot be assumed from codon geometry.  It must be observed in, or bounded by, a contact route such as tAI or stAI weights, and then tested against a separate translation readout.  The asymmetry is therefore a necessary interface condition, not a translation measurement.

\paragraph{Rank and break condition.}
The finite panel must also be large enough to avoid rank-saturated survival.  A surface that can be fit perfectly because the row count or covariate rank is too small is not a powered translation-readout result.  The local break condition is that $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ fails whenever the held-out improvement is non-positive after the full control set and simpler-baseline ablation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) \leq 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
It also fails when the table lacks same-organism, same-CDS, same-window joins among codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-indexed ribosome footprints.  Under the currently attached facts, this second failure mode is the operative one: the finite run numbers are real, but the specific joined translation-window matrix required for the $f_3$ statistic has not been supplied.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded cross-layer boundary rather than a positive biological law.  The admissible claim is narrow: the $f_3$-stress coordinate is proposed as a candidate predictor for a same-window translation readout only after amino-acid identity, position, codon usage, GC3, CDS length, methionine density, mRNA abundance, wobble-weight context, coverage, mappability, rank sufficiency, and simpler baselines have all been attached in one finite table.  The existing $75{,}111$ designed rows and $8{,}027$ high-quality rows belong to a concrete BioReality trace for the linked transcriptional promoter boundary, while the position-resolved translation-survival packet still awaits its own joined ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency panel.

\paragraph{No promotion beyond the readout.}
No translation realization, protein abundance, folding claim, structural order claim, physical admissibility claim, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law follows from the $f_3$ coordinate, from codon/window geometry, from wobble-weight asymmetry, from tAI or stAI weights, from mRNA controls, or from a review-ready routing event.  Such statements require separate layer-matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  Even a future positive value of $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$ would support only the bounded translation-readout survival entry described above; it would not by itself identify a mechanism or authorize downstream protein, structure, function, phenotype, or universality claims.
