\subsection{A position-resolved $f_3$-stress boundary for translation readout}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is not a completed translation claim. It is a finite-row boundary object for deciding when the $f_3$-stress coordinate may be tested against a translation-layer measurement. A row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  x = (&\operatorname{cds}, i, W_i, q_{f_3}(W_i), C(W_i), w(W_i), R(W_i), A(W_i), Z(W_i)),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $W_i$ is a coding-sequence window around codon position $i$, $q_{f_3}(W_i)$ is the residual $f_3$ coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid composition, $C(W_i)$ records local synonymous codon counts, $w(W_i)$ records the wobble-weight surface, $R(W_i)$ is the position-resolved ribosome-footprint or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout, $A(W_i)$ is the matched mRNA abundance control, and $Z(W_i)$ contains amino-acid, position-bin, GC3, CDS-length, M-density, coverage, mappability, and simpler-baseline controls. The boundary is crossed only if the same window supplies both the residual coordinate and the translation readout. Codon geometry, modeled adaptation weights, and review routing do not by themselves provide $R(W_i)$.

\paragraph{Local statistic.}
The intended statistic is a controlled survival entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}} \mid Z)
  = \Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}\bigl(\mathcal{M}(Z,q_{f_3},C,w),\mathcal{M}(Z,C,w)\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ denotes the position-resolved translation readout and $\Delta_{\mathrm{heldout}}$ is positive only when adding the residual $f_3$ coordinate improves held-out prediction after the declared controls and after ablation against simpler codon baselines. The wobble condition is not a convention; the row must expose an asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$ in the contacted organism or window panel. Rank is part of the object: a saturated or underpowered design cannot be promoted to biological survival merely because the fitted surface is nonzero.

\paragraph{Numerical anchor from the promoter screen.}
The finite-row discipline is illustrated by the de Boer promoter-motif contact that is linked to this packet. That screen records $75{,}111$ designed promoter sequences, with $8{,}027$ high-quality rows available after filtering. The audit run used a high-quality analysis subsample of $2{,}500$ rows with subsample seed $20260239$, ran $200$ permutations, and completed in $530.4038379192352$ seconds under experiment run $8\mathrm{d}1\mathrm{d}1\mathrm{ac}78\mathrm{ab}7$. These numbers do not test $f_3$-stress translation. They show the finite-row standard being enforced: a cross-layer statement must identify the rows used, the filtering boundary, the resampling scale, and the exact readout on which rank and survival are evaluated. In the present packet, the analogous rows would be same-organism, same-CDS, same-window coding windows joined to codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA, and position-resolved ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Comparison with completed cross-layer finite rows.}
A separate translation-conditioned structural-order audit gives a positive example of what a completed cross-layer row table looks like after controls. It joined $3{,}412$ rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5{,}119$ rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, used $5$ folds, and reported two positive full-control entries for the structural-order readout. The aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ ranged from $0.002688284523492457$ to $0.007880037229394898$, with mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, while the permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ values were negative, ranging from $-0.003463794378680385$ to $-0.000779938262202795$. The sign-flip probability for a positive full-control effect was $0.0029296875$, and the weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ was $0.004764742175882462$. Those figures are cited only as a scale reference for a powered finite-row test. They do not transfer a structural-order result to $f_3$-stress position-resolved translation; the present chapter still requires its own joined ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency rows.

\paragraph{Negative-scope calibration.}
A second neighboring finite-row audit, the Doench guide-RNA efficiency contact, contains $5{,}310$ rows and a GC main correlation $r=-0.0582917$, with runtime $24.6506$ seconds. Its role here is again calibrating scope rather than supplying evidence for this packet. A scalar correlation from another biological layer cannot be substituted for the required same-window translation readout. The $f_3$-stress claim therefore remains conditional on the specific matrix in which $q_{f_3}(W_i)$, codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA, position controls, and ribosome occupancy are present in the same rows.

\paragraph{Admissible conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is a bounded one. The $f_3$-stress coordinate may be entered into a cross-layer survival test only after the row table contains the residual coordinate, local synonymous codon counts, a non-assumed wobble-weight asymmetry, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome-footprint or translation-efficiency readout under the same CDS-window key. Support requires
$$
\begin{aligned}
  S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}} \mid Z) > 0
\end{aligned}
$$
under held-out evaluation, sufficient rank, coverage and mappability limits, amino-acid or position-bin controls, GC3, length, M-density, codon-count controls, matched mRNA, and simpler-baseline ablation. If those rows are absent, the packet is only a specification of the measurement needed next.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from $f_3$-stress geometry alone. No protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal law follows from the coordinate, from codon-window geometry, from modeled tAI or stAI weights, from a contact name, or from review eligibility. Each stronger statement requires its own layer-matched contact and its own finite-row statistic. In particular, modeled wobble weights may help define a bounded covariate surface, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy; matched mRNA abundance is a control for translation efficiency, not a translation readout; and aggregate translation-efficiency routing is not automatically position-resolved occupancy. The packet is therefore ready only as a self-contained finite-row boundary for the future $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{pos}} \mid Z)$ test.
