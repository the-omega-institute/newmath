\subsection{Finite-row boundary for the $f_3$-stress translation-survival packet}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Claim record.}
This record fixes the BioReality status of the conjecture
$q6.f3\text{-}stress.position\text{-}resolved\text{-}translation\text{-}survival.cross\text{-}layer$.  The proposed object is not a general codon-usage law and not a claim about translation from sequence geometry alone.  It is a finite-row survival question: a coding-sequence window table must contain, in the same row, an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid content, local synonymous-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context with observed asymmetry $w_c \neq w_c^{f_3}$, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The only admissible readback is a controlled per-window entry $S_{f3}(T_{position}\mid Z)$, where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition or binning, position-bin structure, GC3, CDS length, M-density, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability limits, rank checks, and simpler-baseline ablations.

\paragraph{Finite evidence presently attached.}
The currently verified row data do not yet contain the required position-resolved ribosome-footprint matrix for this exact packet.  The strongest attached finite fact in the same claim bundle is the random-promoter cis-regulatory screen keyed by
$h3.cross\_layer\_relation.cis\_regulatory\_grammar.random\_promoter\_tf\_motif\_expression\_boundary\_deboer2020$: it records $75{,}111$ designed promoter sequences, $8{,}027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subsample of $2{,}500$ high-quality rows, $200$ actual permutations, subsample seed $20260239$, and runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  These numbers are useful as a finite-row audit precedent: the packet is allowed to speak only where the table, sampling rule, and permutation budget are explicit.  They are not ribosome-occupancy rows, and they do not instantiate $S_{f3}(T_{position}\mid Z)$.

A separate linked translation-conditioned structural-order run is also finite and positive within its own scope: it used $5$ folds, required at least $300$ joined rows, joined $3{,}412$ rows for $Escherichia\ coli$ K-12 MG1655 and $5{,}119$ rows for $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, and reported weighted full-control $\Delta R^2=0.004764742175882462$ with sign-flip $p=0.0029296875$.  Its aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ had $n=2$, minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, and two positive organism calls.  This is a nearby powered cross-layer result, but it is not a position-resolved $f_3$-stress ribosome-window statistic.  Likewise, the stop-readthrough cross-dataset run contributes $55{,}886$ rows with untreated rank correlation $\rho=0.032718$ and runtime $133.552797$ seconds; it shows that row-scale translation-adjacent assays can be audited, but it remains a different endpoint.

\paragraph{Local mathematical form.}
For this packet the admissible table has rows of the schematic form
$$
\begin{aligned}
 r &= (g, t, k, q_{f3}, C_{syn}, W, R_{pos}, M, Z),\\
 S_{f3}(T_{position}\mid Z) &> 0
\end{aligned}
$$
where $g$ is the organism or sample scope, $t$ is the transcript or CDS identifier, $k$ is the codon-index or fixed-window position, $q_{f3}$ is the residual $f_3$ coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, $C_{syn}$ is the local synonymous-codon count vector, $W$ is the wobble-weight surface, $R_{pos}$ is the position-resolved ribosome-footprint or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency readout, $M$ is the matched mRNA control, and $Z$ is the full control set.  A positive call requires held-out improvement over amino-acid, position-bin, GC3, length, M-density, codon-count, mRNA, and simpler-baseline models.  The wobble asymmetry condition must be observed in the row surface rather than assumed from notation.

\paragraph{Boundary decision.}
The chapter therefore records a ready but null-bounded BioReality entry.  It is ready because the required carrier, controls, readout shape, and break condition are fully specified, and because adjacent verified finite-row records demonstrate the discipline expected for row counts, permutation budgets, held-out comparisons, and scalar reporting.  It remains null-bounded for the exact $f_3$-stress position-resolved translation-survival claim because the necessary same-organism, same-CDS, same-window joined matrix has not been supplied.  Naming a position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact, naming a tAI or stAI wobble-weight surface, passing an operator-review route, or writing $S_{f3}(T_{position}\mid Z)$ does not create the measured statistic.

\paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation realization follows from the residual coordinate by itself.  No protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, adaptation principle, or universal biological law follows from codon-window geometry, wobble weights, mRNA abundance, or review readiness.  A downstream claim is allowed only after a separate contact supplies the corresponding layer-matched rows and after the relevant controlled survival or mediation statistic is computed on those rows.  In particular, modeled tAI or stAI weights may support a bounded codon-adaptation readback, but they are not observed ribosome occupancy.  Matched mRNA abundance may enter the control set, but it is not a translation readout.  The position-resolved ribosome-occupancy contact names the required measurement surface; it does not by itself certify the joined window table.
