\subsection{Finite-window boundary for the $f_3$-stress translation-survival conjecture}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Finite record.}
This record isolates a proposed cross-layer coordinate rather than a completed biological promotion.  The object under test is a finite coding-sequence window table in which each row contains an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after amino-acid quotienting, local synonymous-codon counts, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin covariates.  The proposed survival entry is written locally as $S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition or position bin, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, mRNA abundance, codon-count controls, rank sufficiency checks, coverage and mappability limits, and a simpler-baseline ablation.  The finite-row condition is therefore not the presence of an $f_3$ coordinate alone; it is the existence of a joined same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix in which the translation readout improves held-out prediction after those controls.

\paragraph{Available measured anchors.}
The available verified rows do not yet supply that joined ribosome-window matrix, but they do give concrete scale for nearby BioReality contacts.  The random-promoter TF-motif expression boundary associated with de Boer-style promoter design used $75{,}111$ designed sequences, $8{,}027$ high-quality rows, a $2{,}500$-row high-quality analysis subsample, seed $20260239$, $200$ actual permutations, and a runtime of $530.4038379192352$ seconds.  A separate translation-conditioned structural-order run is recorded as passed with $5$ folds, minimum joined size $300$, two organism-level positive $B^*_\mathrm{Q6}$ order entries, and a weighted full-control $\Delta R^2$ of $0.004764742175882462$; its organism joins were $3{,}412$ for \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5{,}119$ for \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The same verified block reports a full-control sign-flip probability $p=0.0029296875$, while the permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ values were negative with mean $-0.00212186632044159$.  Additional scale checks include a stop-context readthrough assay with $7{,}658$ joined rows before the nonzero filter, $4{,}982$ retained nonzero G418-induced rows, nonzero fraction $0.65056$, and runtime $303.534$ seconds, and a Doench-style sgRNA position-nucleotide efficiency run with $n=5{,}310$, $r_{\mathrm{GC}}=-0.0582917$, and runtime $24.6506$ seconds.  These numbers show that finite, controlled row panels are present elsewhere in the surrounding evidence system; they do not by themselves instantiate the $f_3$ position-resolved translation-survival table required here.

\paragraph{Local mathematical form.}
For this chapter the admissible carrier is a finite table $\mathcal{T}$ whose rows have the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
r=(c_{f3}, C_{\mathrm{syn}}, W_{\mathrm{wobble}}, T_{\mathrm{pos}}, M_{\mathrm{rna}}, Z, y),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $c_{f3}$ is the residual $f_3$ coordinate, $C_{\mathrm{syn}}$ is the local synonymous-codon count vector, $W_{\mathrm{wobble}}$ is the context-specific wobble-weight row, $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is the matched position-resolved occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, $M_{\mathrm{rna}}$ is the transcript-abundance control, and $Z$ packages the remaining controls.  A positive chapter-level call requires both asymmetry and survival:
$$
\begin{aligned}
W_{\mathrm{wobble}} &\neq W_{\mathrm{wobble}}^{f_3},\\
S_{f3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z) &>0
\end{aligned}
$$
after held-out evaluation and simpler-baseline removal.  If the rank is saturated, if the wobble asymmetry is only assumed, or if $T_{\mathrm{pos}}$ is replaced by modeled tAI or stAI weights without measured or explicitly bounded readout rows, the survival entry is not created.

\paragraph{Boundary conclusion.}
The current finite-row evidence supports a chapter-ready boundary statement: the $f_3$-stress coordinate is a candidate cross-layer coordinate only at the point where same-window ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency is joined to codon counts, wobble weights, matched mRNA abundance, and the declared controls.  The de Boer promoter panel and the other verified finite panels demonstrate concrete row-counted analysis practice, permutation accounting, and controlled-run bookkeeping, but they are not same-window ribosome-footprint rows for this conjecture.  Consequently, this chapter records a bounded request for a missing matrix rather than a positive translation-survival result.  No translation realization, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, phenotype, causation, or universal biological rule follows from the $f_3$ coordinate, codon-window geometry, wobble-weight asymmetry, contact names, or review readiness.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
