\subsection{Position-Resolved $f_3$-Stress as a Translation-Readout Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-f3-stress-position-resolved-translation-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The record considered here is a finite coding-sequence window table.  A row is not merely a codon word.  It must contain an $f_3$-stress residual coordinate after quotienting by amino-acid composition, the local synonymous codon counts in the same coding window, a bounded wobble-weight context, a matched position-resolved ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency readout, a matched mRNA control, and amino-acid or position-bin controls.  The proposed biological object is therefore a cross-layer row type: the BEDC-side coordinate is allowed to be tested against translation-layer measurements only after the row carries both the sequence-side covariates and the readout-side controls.

The local statistic is written as $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$, where $Z$ contains amino-acid composition or position-bin controls, GC3, coding-sequence length, methionine-density baseline, codon-count controls, matched mRNA abundance, coverage and mappability limits, and simpler baseline ablations.  A positive entry means that the $f_3$ residual improves a held-out position-resolved translation readout after those controls.  A missing row join, a modeled adaptation weight, or a workflow eligibility event is not such an entry.

\paragraph{Observed finite-row anchors.}
The available checked rows do not yet contain the required same-organism, same-CDS, same-window matrix for this packet.  They do, however, give the numerical scale of nearby BioReality surfaces.  The cis-regulatory random-promoter assay attached to the current claim identifier reports $75{,}111$ designed sequences, $8{,}027$ high-quality rows, an analysis subsample of $2{,}500$ high-quality rows, seed $20260239$, and $200$ actual permutations, with runtime $530.4038379192352$ seconds under run id $8d1d1ac78ab7$.  This is a finite empirical panel, but it is a promoter-expression surface; it is not a position-resolved coding-window ribosome-occupancy matrix.

A separate translation-conditioned structural-order run reports a passed status for two organisms, with $3{,}412$ joined rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655 and $5{,}119$ joined rows for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Its aggregate full-control $\Delta R^2$ has minimum $0.002688284523492457$, maximum $0.007880037229394898$, mean and median $0.005284160876443678$, and a sign-flip probability $0.0029296875$ for being greater than zero.  Its permuted full-control $\Delta R^2$ is negative in both organism entries, with mean $-0.00212186632044159$.  These numbers show that the surrounding audit system can record controlled finite-row cross-layer survival, but they do not instantiate the present $f_3$ position-window readout.

A termination-layer readout gives another bounded example: the G418-induced stop-context dataset has $7{,}658$ joined rows before nonzero filtering and $4{,}982$ nonzero RRTS rows, with a nonzero fraction $0.65056$ and runtime $303.534$ seconds.  Again, this is a concrete finite table, but it concerns stop-context readthrough rather than synonymous $f_3$-stress in coding windows.

\paragraph{Admissible relation.}
The only admissible relation for the present packet is the following bounded survival test:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)>0
\end{aligned}
$$
where the inequality is evaluated on held-out rows after all listed controls and simpler-baseline ablations.  The coordinate enters only through the residual direction left after amino-acid quotienting.  Wobble weights may be used only as bounded context rows, and the asymmetry condition $w_c\neq w_c^{f_3}$ must be observed in the attached table rather than assumed from notation.  The same row must also provide the translation readout: modeled tAI or stAI weights can describe codon-adaptation context, but they are not observed ribosome footprints and cannot replace a ribosome-occupancy or explicitly bounded translation-efficiency measurement.

The rank condition is part of the claim.  A saturated or underpowered surface is not a biological discovery; it is a refusal to promote the coordinate.  The row panel must have enough nondegenerate variation to distinguish the $f_3$ coordinate from amino-acid composition, local codon counts, GC3, position, length, methionine density, mRNA abundance, and simpler codon baselines.  If those controls absorb the effect, the correct conclusion is that the packet remains a boundary request for a better joined matrix.

\paragraph{Null boundary.}
The current finite memory does not contain the same-window source rows needed to compute $S_{f_3}(T_{\mathrm{position}}\mid Z)$.  The available contacts name the necessary surfaces: codon usage, tAI or stAI weights, matched mRNA abundance, and position-resolved ribosome occupancy.  Naming those surfaces is not equivalent to joining them.  The required next object is a table keyed by organism, transcript or CDS identifier, codon position or local coding window, ribosome-profiling sample, mRNA-control sample, and the local synonymous codon context.  Only after this table exists can the $f_3$ statistic be tested.

Consequently, this chapter records a scoped cross-layer boundary, not a positive translation-realization result.  No translation mechanism, protein abundance, folding state, structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, causation, adaptation claim, or universal biological law follows from the $f_3$ coordinate, from codon-window geometry, from review readiness, from $S_{f_3}$ notation, or from modeled wobble weights.  Any such higher-layer assertion requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite-row statistic.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The packet breaks if it treats the promoter-expression finite panel, the structural-order finite panel, the termination-readthrough panel, contact names, modeled adaptation weights, or BEDC-side residual coordinates as if they were a computed position-resolved ribosome-occupancy survival statistic for the same CDS windows.  It also breaks if it reports a general directionality law for $f_3$ without demonstrating rank-powered survival under held-out testing.  The admissible conclusion is narrower: the $f_3$-stress member is ready to be tested as a bounded cross-layer coordinate once the same-organism, same-CDS, same-window translation-readout table is attached.
