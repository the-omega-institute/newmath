\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The record tests whether the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface should be treated as an unexplained primitive after the available measured reduction families have been allowed to compete on the same yeast context. The held-out protocol used $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, description-length penalty $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and join penalty $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$. \paragraph{Outcome read as a reduction test.}
The registered status is passed in the narrow sense that the source signal is real and that at least one measured reduction family refutes the stronger hypothesis that the residual surface is presently irreducible.  The word ``primitive'' is therefore local: it marks the part of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance surface not yet explained by the finite reduction dictionary, not a proof that no future biological readout can reduce it. This is a substantial reduction of the surface, but it is explicitly a variance partition across observational tables.  It is not a causal pathway result.

\paragraph{Side reductions that do not close the surface.}
Localization and turnover probes show why the remaining component should be treated as an open residual rather than as a named mechanism.  In yeast, UniProt localization absorption over $4656$ joined records is $0.011225105608516642$ against a dof-matched null $95$th percentile $0.004958116973149795$, with $12$ localization columns and $4831$ localization records.  That is an excess descriptive projection, not a compartment mechanism.  In human turnover, the full abundance-CDS join has $19053$ rows, while the turnover-abundance-CDS join has $8057$ rows.  The turnover absorption is only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, below the dof-matched null $95$th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$, even though the large human modeled complement has $d_{\mathrm{modeled}}=0.9208949355679455$.  These two side tests show that some state variables can carry small residual projections, while at least one plausible turnover surface fails its own null comparison.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result is therefore a two-sided certificate.  It rejects the claim that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual is wholly unexplained, because measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, turnover dictionary selection, and dwell summaries reduce a nontrivial part of the surface.  It also rejects the claim that the current reduction dictionary has exhausted the surface, because the best selection-aware coverage leaves $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained.  The correct mathematical reading is a partial-reduction boundary:
$$
\begin{aligned}
0 &< C^{*}=0.24538211439730545,\\
C^{*} &> N_{0.95}^{\mathrm{select}}=0.029149007242956115,\\
1-C^{*} &=0.7546178856026946>0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently the chapter records a real, rank-powered, finite-row residual primitive only after the measured reductions have been named and subtracted in scope.
