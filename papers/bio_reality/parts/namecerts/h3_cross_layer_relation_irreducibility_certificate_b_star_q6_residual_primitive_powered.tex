\subsection{Primitive Residual Reduction Certificate for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-residual-primitive-certificate}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite Saccharomyces cerevisiae panel in which the source design is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate surface, residualized against the same sibling control system used by the protein-abundance and translation-readout tests.  The nine named coordinates are $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The target is log10 protein abundance after residualization against the sibling controls.  The active yeast context has $n_{join\_available}=4758$ available joined rows, while the certificate fit uses $n_{join}=1800$ rows, $5$ held-out folds, a description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, a join threshold $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations.  The residual-control basis has rank $24$, and the deterministic seed is recorded as $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}$.

\paragraph{Certificate meaning.}
The tested claim is not that the residual signal is globally irreducible.  It is the opposite finite certificate: the registered source signal is real on the yeast abundance target, but at least one listed reduction family is strong enough to refute the primitive irreducibility hypothesis attached to this coordinate surface.  In symbols, if $X_Q$ denotes the residualized $B^*_{Q6}$ design, $Y_P$ denotes the residualized protein-abundance target, and $R_{\mathcal{F}}$ denotes the best member of the registered reduction family after the same control projection, the observed outcome has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
&n_{join}=1800, \qquad n_{join\_available}=4758, \qquad \operatorname{rank}(Z)=24,\\
&\lambda_{DL}=0.01, \qquad \rho_{join}=0.05, \qquad B=120,\\
&X_Q \leadsto Y_P \text{ is not null on the tested surface,}\\
&\exists R_{\mathcal{F}}\; \text{such that the primitive no-reduction reading is rejected.}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a finite reduction certificate, not a universality theorem.  It says that the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ residual-to-abundance association should be read as partly compressible by measured or registered readout families rather than as an atomic primitive layer.

\paragraph{Relation to the measured readout dictionary.}
The companion partial-mediation certificate uses the same yeast availability scale, with $base\_n=4759$, $n_{join\_available}=4758$, $n_{join}=1800$, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations.  Its selected dictionary is the single-codon readout family with at most three measured readouts, named as measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover.  The raw best coverage over that candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$.  After selection-aware calibration, the retained coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95}^{selection} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
\mathrm{Null}_{95}^{fixed} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite dictionary absorbs a nontrivial fraction of the yeast residual abundance component, while most of the component remains outside this small readout family.  This is the local mathematical content of the certificate: the primitive reading is too strong, but the observed reduction is partial rather than complete.

\paragraph{Residual remainder and candidate higher readouts.}
The remaining component is not empty.  In the yeast localization projection, the current localization join has $4656$ rows and $4831$ localization records across $12$ coarse categories.  The localization absorption is $0.011225105608516642$, compared with a dof-matched null mean $0.002689902676583632$ and null $95$th percentile $0.004958116973149795$.  In the yeast $f_3$ dwell certificate, the active join has $1257$ rows, $base\_n=1800$, $5$ folds, $base\ R^2=0.1855578757644517$, $full\ R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $f_3$--dwell correlation probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell record identifies the $5'$ segment as the leading segment, with the $5'$ window passing and the $3'$ window not passing.  These readouts are downstream descriptive reductions of the residual surface; they do not turn the residual coordinate into a mechanism.

\paragraph{Interpretation boundary.}
The certificate is deliberately local.  It does not assert translation causality, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype realization, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological rule.  Direct $q_i\to P_k$ survival is a protein-omics association unless a measured translation readout $T_j$ is present on matched rows and conditioning on $T_j$ reduces the $q_i\to P_k$ signal.  Rank-degenerate all-one surfaces at insufficient organism count are not counted as biological survival.  The admissible conclusion is only that, on the finite yeast panel with the numbers above, the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual abundance signal is real, partly explained by registered measured-readout families, and therefore not a primitive irreducible residual in the strong sense.
