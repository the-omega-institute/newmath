\paragraph{Finite panel and target.}
The residual primitive test asks whether the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal in yeast remains an unreduced primitive after a fixed dictionary of lower-layer and neighboring readouts is allowed to explain it.  The tested source design is the nine-dimensional synonymous residual coordinate block
$\{K_{AAA}, Arg_{AGR}, Ile_{AUA}, Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}, Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}, Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}, Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}, Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}, f_3\}$,
residualized against the same sibling controls used throughout the powered cross-layer panel.  The target is log10 protein abundance after the sibling control basis on the unified \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The available yeast join contains $4758$ admissible rows, with the base context reporting $4759$ rows before the final active join restriction; the primitive test uses $n=1800$ rows, $5$ held-out folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and control-basis rank $24$.  The deterministic seed is recorded as $\texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}$.

The broad direct protein-omics panel is not yeast-specific noise: all $15$ listed organisms have powered residual rank $9$ for the $Q$ block, with direct $R^2_{QP}$ ranging from $0.019256877907572044$ in \textit{Halobacterium salinarum} to $0.29837624994140827$ in yeast.  This establishes a real finite association surface, but not yet an irreducible one.

\paragraph{Primitive verdict.}
The registered primitive claim was tested and the result is a negative certificate for irreducibility in the strict sense.  The source signal is present, but at least one allowed reduction family refutes the stronger hypothesis that the yeast $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block is wholly primitive relative to the measured dictionary.  The strongest compact reduction record is the partial-mediation certificate on the same yeast context: with $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$, the best selected dictionary using at most three measured readouts from $\{\text{measured TE}, \text{mRNA stability}, \text{turnover}\}$ has
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{*} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{*} &= 0.7546178856026946,\\
\text{raw best coverage} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
\text{selection-aware null}_{95} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
\text{fixed-subset null}_{95} &= 0.00598374993486675.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite-row conclusion is not that the residual signal vanishes.  It is that the current signal is partly reducible to measured neighboring readouts, leaving a large unexplained remainder rather than a clean primitive certificate.

The unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ is large enough that the chapter must not collapse the residual into translation alone.  The $f_3$ stress component supplies a more specific local reduction: on a yeast dwell join with $n=1257$ and base size $1800$, the base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the full dwell model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment is $0.01856473944057712$, above a permutation null $95\%$ value of $0.0013115362189692937$ with approximate $f_3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$. \paragraph{Non-translation candidates.}
The remaining residual is not yet assigned to localization, turnover, folding, or function.  A localization projection on yeast uses $4656$ joined rows out of $5815$ reported abundance/CDS rows, $4831$ localization records, $12$ localization categories, control rank $23$, and reports localization absorption $0.011225105608516642$ against a degree-of-freedom matched null $95\%$ value $0.004958116973149795$.  The corresponding modeled-complement diagnostic on the yeast localization join is $0.1997853864617063$, close to the full abundance reference $0.21173219572706703$.  Human turnover, by contrast, is not a useful reduction for the large human complement residual: the human turnover join has $8057$ rows, $R^2_{Q,Tturn}=0.004615408769189838$, absorbed fraction $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, and the result does not exceed the null $95\%$ value $0.0004342285066808464$.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row conclusion is therefore a residual-primitive refusal rather than an irreducibility proof.  The powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ block carries a real controlled protein-abundance signal in yeast and across the broader protein-omics panel, but the yeast signal is partly compressed by measured TE, mRNA stability, dwell structure, and other bounded readouts.  The admissible statement is: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ remains a residual coordinate source with a substantial unexplained component after the current measured reductions, not a certified primitive biological law.  No translation mechanism, folded-structure order, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal pathway, evolutionary direction, or universality follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that claim and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed on the same finite units.
