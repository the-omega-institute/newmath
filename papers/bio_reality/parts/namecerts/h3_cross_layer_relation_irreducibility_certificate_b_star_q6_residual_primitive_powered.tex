\subsection{Rank-powered residual primitivity test for the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance surface}\label{subsec:bq6-residual-primitive-powered}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance signal should still be treated as primitive after the available measured readout families are allowed to explain it.  The active source design is the $9$-coordinate synonymous residual surface consisting of $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, residualized against the same sibling control basis used throughout this packet.  The target is log$_{10}$ protein abundance after the corresponding $Z$-residualization on the unified \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The certified finite run uses $n_{join}=1800$ rows drawn from $n_{join\_available}=4758$ usable joined rows, with base panel size $4759$, five held-out folds, $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, join floor $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$.  The deterministic seed is \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}.

\paragraph{Decision functional.}
The tested question is not whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates are numerically associated with abundance; that association is already visible in the direct protein-omics run.  In yeast, the direct residual protein-abundance surface has $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all nine coordinates surviving, using $5815$ joined CDS/protein rows and a CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The stricter question is whether this source signal remains irreducible once reduction families built from measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, protein turnover, and local dwell readouts are permitted.  The finite decision can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
&X_Q \in \mathbb{R}^{1800\times 9},\qquad Y=P_{\perp Z}\in \mathbb{R}^{1800},\qquad \operatorname{rank}(Z)=24,\\
&\text{accept primitive only if the source signal is real and no listed readout family gives selection-aware reduction above its null boundary.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed result goes the other way: the registered current claim is tested, the source signal is real, and at least one listed reduction family refutes the original irreducibility hypothesis.  Thus this subsection records a residual-primitivity failure, not a proof that the coordinate system is globally primitive.

It is also not enough to collapse the whole abundance residual into those readouts, since roughly $75.46\%$ remains outside that selected family on this finite surface.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The modeled translation-conditioned four-case surface gives a useful finite classification, even though it uses modeled tAI rather than measured ribosome TE.  For yeast, all nine coordinates fall in case $D$ under the modeled-tAI/protein-abundance discriminant, with no unclassified coordinates and no positive-positive coordinate lacking a mediation drop. This supports the local statement that the abundance association is translation-conditioned in the available readout geometry, but it does not by itself convert modeled adaptation weights into observed ribosome movement.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is that the original irreducibility reading is not retained.  The yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ residual abundance signal is real on the tested surface, but it is partially reducible to measured translation, stability, turnover, and dwell-related readouts.  The appropriate current statement is therefore a residual-primitivity boundary: the coordinate family remains a useful source design, while the protein-abundance association is not a primitive unexplained biological module on this panel.  The positive reductions are statistical readbacks from finite joined tables.  They do not establish causation, translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universality.  Any such stronger interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
