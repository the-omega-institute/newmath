\subsection{Rank-powered residual-primitive test for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-residual-primitive-powered}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The tested object is the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface, with coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The active irreducibility run used the unified Saccharomyces cerevisiae context join.  The available join contained $4758$ rows out of a yeast base panel of $4759$, and the registered test used $n=1800$ joined rows with $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, join penalty $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and a sibling control basis of rank $24$.  The source design was the same $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate matrix after residualization against the sibling covariates, and the target was log10 protein abundance after the same residualization.

\paragraph{Primitive-versus-reduction decision.}
The run does not certify that the residual surface is primitive.  Its registered semantics are the opposite: the current claim is tested by requiring a real source signal and checking whether at least one listed reduction family explains enough of that signal to defeat the primitive hypothesis.  In the accompanying readout-dictionary certificate on the same yeast context, the best selected family from the single-codon readout dictionary covered
$$\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946,
\end{aligned}$$
with raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, fixed-subset null $95\%$ level $0.00598374993486675$, selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $120$ permutations, $5$ folds, $n=1800$, and $4758$ available joined yeast rows.  Thus the finite result is a partial-reduction certificate: the yeast residual is real, but it is not an irreducible primitive under the registered reduction family.  The module-level classification for this chapter is therefore reducible residual signal with a large unexplained remainder, not primitive closure.

\paragraph{Translation and protein readbacks.}
The same coordinate surface has positive finite-row contacts at translation and protein-abundance readouts, but those contacts separate association from mechanism.  For measured ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, the yeast join used $5128$ genes and reported $S^{QT,meas}$ through $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving; the corresponding join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$.  The measured abundance mediation run on yeast used $5128$ three-way joined rows and gave
$$\begin{aligned}
S^{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S^{QT,meas} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP,meas}_{raw} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}$$
For E. coli the same measured mediation surface gave $S^{QP}=0.10941535951828074$, $S^{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$, and $M^{QTP,meas}_{raw}=0.04091158340950673$ on $3415$ three-way rows.  For human it gave $S^{QP}=0.04288464996165745$, $S^{QT,meas}=0.018837911344127926$, and $M^{QTP,meas}_{raw}=0.0027966485036786744$ on $11554$ rows.  For danio it gave $S^{QP}=0.04926578454614652$, $S^{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$, and a suppressive raw value $M^{QTP,meas}_{raw}=-0.04801486048721136$, clipped to zero for the reported mediated fraction, on $13140$ rows.

\paragraph{Joint mediator profile.}
The yeast joint TE-plus-mRNA-stability decomposition reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  Its joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and a right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  E. coli gives $R^2_{QP}=0.1181743523064932$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.08975732521542036$, and joint fraction $0.2404669586626661$; human gives $R^2_{QP}=0.04274178488511215$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.0355223930537993$, and joint fraction $0.16890712099923358$.  These figures support a finite variance-partition reading: measured TE and transcript-stability contacts explain part of the protein-abundance residual, especially in yeast, while leaving a substantial remainder.

\paragraph{Residual remainder and positional clue.}
The $f_3$-stress dwell run gives a concrete example of a remaining structured component.  On the yeast active base-plus-dwell join, $n_{join}=1257$ out of base $n=1800$, $P_{f3}$ has norm square $44.20095750389323$ and fraction $0.1999063916718794$ of the target.  The base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, dwell alone has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the held-out increment is $0.01856473944057712$ against a deterministic permutation null with mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95\%$ level $0.0013115362189692937$.  The $f_3$-dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional run classifies the leading segment as $5'$: the five-prime window passed, the three-prime body window did not, and the dominant segment was $5prime$.

\paragraph{Local exclusion.}
The finite rows above justify only a measured statistical statement about controlled codon residuals, measured TE, mRNA stability, dwell summaries, and protein abundance in the available panels.  They do not by themselves establish causal translation control, folded structural order, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law.  A stronger biological interpretation would require a separate layer-matched contact: perturbation or rescue for causality, structure or stability assays for physical claims, functional assays for molecular role, and phenotype measurements for organism-level claims.  Direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is therefore treated as protein-omics prediction unless a measured $q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ attenuation step is present under the declared controls.
