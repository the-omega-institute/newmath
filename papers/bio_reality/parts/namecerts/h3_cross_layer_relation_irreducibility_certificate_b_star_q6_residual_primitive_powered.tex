\subsection{A bounded irreducibility test for the yeast $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-residual-primitive-certificate}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite matrix under test.}
The tested object is the yeast row-restricted surface obtained from the nine-dimensional $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual design after projecting away the sibling control basis $Z$.  The source design is the coordinate family
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$, residualized against length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The target row is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the same sibling controls on a unified \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} context join.  The run used $z\_basis\_rank=24$, $base\_n=4759$, $n\_join\_available=4758$, and the reported active evaluation size $n\_join=1800$.  The certificate fit used five folds, $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$, with seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}.

\paragraph{Observed source strength.}
The residual source is not a vacuous design artifact.  In the direct protein-omics screen for the same organism, the yeast CDS-to-abundance join had hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ with $5815$ joined proteins, and the controlled direct association gave
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
\#\{\text{surviving }B^{*}_{Q6}\text{ coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}$$
The measured ribosome-TE contact was also nondegenerate in yeast: $5128$ genes with TE were joined to the CDS rows, the TE join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$, and the residualized measured-TE survival was
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,meas}(\textit{S. cerevisiae}) &= 0.13681931343730436.
\end{aligned}$$
The measured abundance mediation fit on the same biological direction reported $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and a bounded measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  Thus the primitive source row has measurable protein-abundance contact and measurable translation-readout contact, but these contacts do not by themselves make the residual primitive.

\paragraph{Reduction families and the certificate verdict.}
The registered irreducibility run has status \texttt{passed} in the precise sense that the current claim was tested and the source signal was real, while at least one listed reduction family defeated the original irreducibility hypothesis.  The companion partial-mediation certificate identifies the relevant measured-readout dictionary family as a single-codon readout dictionary with candidate readouts $\{\text{measured\_te},\text{mrna\_stability},\text{turnover}\}$ and with at most three selected readouts.  On the same yeast setting it used $base\_n=4759$, $n\_join\_available=4758$, $n\_join=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $z\_basis\_rank=24$.  Its selection-aware reduction coverage was
$$\begin{aligned}
C^{*} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{*} &= 0.7546178856026946,\\
\text{raw best coverage} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
\text{selection-aware null}_{95} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
\text{fixed-subset null}_{95} &= 0.00598374993486675.
\end{aligned}$$
The numerical relation $C^{*}>0.029149007242956115$ is the local finite-row reason that the primitive interpretation does not survive unchanged: the residual is only partly reducible, but the positive selection-aware coverage is too large to call the source irreducible relative to the tested dictionary.

\paragraph{Mediation and module readback.}
The stronger yeast mediation and dwell records give the same qualitative module assignment.  Joint TE--stability mediation on yeast reported
$$\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838,\\
\text{joint mediation fraction} &= 0.5195182225621592,\\
\text{TE-only fraction} &= 0.4697188319693239,\\
\text{stability-only fraction} &= 0.0626618595344251.
\end{aligned}$$
The overlap module was classified as \texttt{approximately\_independent}, since the joint fraction differed from the sum of the single-mediator fractions by $-0.012862468941589822$ within the declared descriptive tolerance $0.05$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The $f3\_stress$ dwell test adds a narrower module readback: $n\_join=1257$, $base\_n=1800$, five folds, $120$ permutations, $R^2_{base}=0.1855578757644517$, $R^2_{full}=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null$_{95}=0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell audit located the leading segment at the $5'$ window, with \texttt{five\_prime\_ramp\_passed=true}, \texttt{three\_prime\_body\_passed=false}, and \texttt{dominant\_segment=5prime}.  These finite rows make the residual interpretable as a partly reducible cross-layer abundance surface, not as an atomic primitive.

\paragraph{Four-case consistency.}
The translation-conditioned four-case discriminant is consistent with that interpretation.  In yeast it assigned all nine coordinates to case $D$ and none to cases $A$, $B$, or $C$ under the declared threshold $10^{-12}$, with $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ for the modeled-tAI readout.  This is not used here as a causal claim.  It only says that the same residual family has translation-side and protein-abundance-side contacts whose direct protein signal is not left as a bare primitive once the tested readout families are admitted.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite-row conclusion is therefore negative for primitive irreducibility and positive for bounded partial reducibility.  The tested $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual source is real on the yeast protein-abundance surface, but the measured TE, mRNA-stability, turnover, and dwell-related readouts absorb a nontrivial and selection-aware fraction of that source.  The remaining unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ is not a license to promote the residual to an irreducible biological law; it is the part not removed by the present finite dictionary.  No translation mechanism, folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, molecular-function statement, causal pathway, phenotype claim, or universal biological rule follows from this certificate.  Such claims would require their own layer-matched contact and their own controlled finite-row statistic.
