\subsection{Rank-powered reduction certificate for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-irreducibility-certificate}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The object tested here is the nine-coordinate synonymous residual surface
$B^*_{Q6}$ on a matched yeast context join.  The active coordinate list is
$R=\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}$.
Each row is residualized against the same sibling control basis $Z$: intercept, CDS length, twenty amino-acid composition coordinates, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the registered irreducibility run, the source design is the $9$-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ matrix after this residualization, the target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance, and the yeast join has $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$ active rows drawn from $4758$ available joined rows, with base panel size $4759$ and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=24$.

\paragraph{Reduction dictionary.}
The certificate was not evaluated as a bare association.  It was tested against a named family of measured lower-dimensional readouts.  The reduction list used by the companion dictionary run is
$M=\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$,
with at most three selected readouts.  The dictionary-compression run reports raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, a selection-aware null $95\%$ cutoff $0.029149007242956115$, a naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ cutoff $0.00598374993486675$, and retained coverage
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same powered settings were used: $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $4758$ available joined rows, $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and description-length penalty $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$.  Thus the observed signal is not primitive in the strict sense: a measured readout family removes about $24.54\%$ of the residual target while leaving about $75.46\%$ unexplained.

\paragraph{Irreducibility verdict.}
The registered run with identifier \texttt{2040d8536a26} has status \texttt{passed}, but the meaning of that status is deliberately asymmetric.  It confirms that the current finite claim was tested on the powered yeast matrix and that the source signal is real enough for the listed reduction families to be evaluated.  It does not certify irreducibility.  Rather, because at least one listed reduction family refutes the original primitive-residual hypothesis, the admissible mathematical conclusion is a partial-reduction certificate:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}} &= 0.01,\\
\rho_{\mathrm{join}} &= 0.05,\\
N_{\mathrm{perm}} &= 120,\\
K_{\mathrm{fold}} &= 5,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 24.
\end{aligned}
$$
On these finite rows, $B^*_{Q6}$ remains a nontrivial residual coordinate family, but it is not an unreduced primitive for protein abundance.  The correct classification is ``partially reducible, still with a large residual complement,'' not ``irreducible.''

\paragraph{Mediation numbers.}
The measured translation-conditioned abundance run gives four finite mediation fractions.  Yeast is the strongest observed case: $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT}^{\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  The other measured fractions are $0$ for danio after a raw suppressive value $-0.04801486048721136$, $0.04091158340950673$ for \textit{E. coli}, and $0.0027966485036786744$ for human.  A separate joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition gives yeast $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, and joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The corresponding joint fractions are $0.2404669586626661$ in \textit{E. coli} and $0.16890712099923358$ in human, both with the same right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  These values explain why the irreducibility label cannot be promoted: measured translation and transcript-stability readouts remove a reproducible part of the protein-abundance residual.

\paragraph{Four-case and positional refinements.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant separates positive protein association from translation-conditioned mediation.  In yeast, all $9$ coordinates land in case D, with $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  In \textit{E. coli}, $7$ coordinates land in case D and $2$ are positive-positive without a mediation drop, with $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$.  The dwell refinements show that the yeast $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ component has an independent ribosome-occupancy route: base held-out $R^2$ is $0.1855578757644517$, full held-out $R^2$ is $0.20412261520502883$, the held-out increment is $0.01856473944057712$, and the fixed-readout permutation null $95\%$ cutoff is $0.0013115362189692937$.  The $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ to dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional readout classifies the leading segment as $5'$; the $5'$ ramp passes, the $3'$ body does not, and the dominant segment is reported as \texttt{5prime}.  This is a localization of one reduction route inside the finite yeast rows, not an explanation of all remaining residual variance.

\paragraph{Scientific boundary.}
The admissible statement is therefore narrow.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface has strong finite-row contact with measured translation efficiency and measured protein abundance, and part of its yeast protein-abundance signal is reducible to a small measured readout dictionary.  The same evidence rejects a strict primitive-residual interpretation while preserving a large unexplained complement.  No conclusion about translation causality, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, or universality follows from this chapter.  Such claims require their own matched measurements and their own controlled statistic.  Protein abundance is a steady-state omics readout; translation efficiency is condition-specific; mediation fractions here are variance partitions on joined finite rows, not perturbation, rescue, or causal pathway evidence.
