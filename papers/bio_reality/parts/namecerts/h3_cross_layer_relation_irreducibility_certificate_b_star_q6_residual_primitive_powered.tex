\subsection{A powered residual-primitive test for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance signal}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-primitive-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The tested object is a finite yeast matrix whose source side is the nine-dimensional synonymous residual design $B^{*}_{Q6}$ and whose target side is log-scaled measured protein abundance after the sibling control block has been removed.  The source design consists of the coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3\_stress$.  The controls used throughout the related protein-abundance tests are the intercept, coding-sequence length, the twenty amino-acid composition proportions, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the yeast context the abundance join reported $5815$ CDS rows in the direct protein-omics surface with a join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and the measured translation-efficiency join used $5128$ genes with a ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  The irreducibility test itself used the unified yeast context with $n_{join\_available}=4758$, sampled $n_{join}=1800$ rows against a base panel size $4759$, and kept the residual control rank at $z_{rank}=24$.

\paragraph{Registered primitive question.}
The hypothesis under audit is not whether the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surface has any protein-abundance association.  Direct survival was already visible in the yeast protein-omics surface: the yeast value was $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all nine coordinates surviving under the positive partial-$R^{2}$ threshold $10^{-12}$.  The harder question is whether that residual signal must be treated as primitive after measured readouts are allowed to compress it.  The powered certificate therefore uses a fixed finite protocol with five folds, $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  Its target is the vector of $\log_{10}$ protein abundance residuals after the same $Z$ controls.  The registered status is passed in the following restricted sense: the source signal is real, and at least one listed reduction family refutes the original irreducibility hypothesis.  It is therefore a reducibility certificate, not an irreducibility certificate in the strong biological sense.

\paragraph{Numerical certificate.}
The finite certificate is anchored by the companion partial-mediation run on the same yeast context.  That run used the same $base\_n=4759$, $n_{join\_available}=4758$, $n_{join}=1800$, $fold\_count=5$, $permutation\_count=120$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  The best selected readout dictionary from the family containing measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover reached selection-aware coverage
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{*} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{*} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw best coverage over the candidate family was $0.2745002077400602$, while the selection-aware null $95\%$ level was $0.029149007242956115$ and the naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ level was $0.00598374993486675$.  Thus the finite rows show a nontrivial reducible component, but they also leave most of the abundance residual unexplained by this small readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Measured translation and protein-abundance coordinates.}
The measured ribosome-TE surface gives the translation-side readout against which any protein-mediation statement must be bounded.  In yeast, the measured TE survival statistic was $R^{2}_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with all nine coordinates surviving; the corresponding controls had $rank(X_{\widetilde{}})=9$, $rank(Z)=23$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, translation residual energy $753.5781168800304$, and explained energy $103.10404057290246$.  For the measured protein-abundance mediation run, yeast had
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,meas} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{meas} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same run reported $X$-explained energy on abundance $675.3275481062249$, direct $X$ improvement after conditioning on measured TE $351.63835021403213$, and TE incremental improvement after $X$ equal to $269.89558844115504$.  These numbers identify a measured translation-conditioned component, but they do not exhaust the residual abundance signal.

\paragraph{Reduction families and residual remainder.}
The joint TE--mRNA-stability decomposition gives a second finite reduction route.  In yeast it reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The joint mediation fraction was $0.5195182225621592$, with a right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The single-mediator fractions were $0.4697188319693239$ for TE alone and $0.0626618595344251$ for mRNA stability alone; their sum was close to the joint fraction within the declared descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  Separately, the $f3\_stress$ dwell readout carried a positive held-out increment: base $R^{2}=0.1855578757644517$, dwell-alone $R^{2}=0.034336580953121976$, full $R^{2}=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.01856473944057712$, above a permutation null $95\%$ level $0.0013115362189692937$ with approximate $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ and $p\approx 7.873916057196935\cdot 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell split localized the leading increment to the $5'$ segment: $five\_prime\_ramp\_passed=true$, $three\_prime\_body\_passed=false$, and $dominant\_segment=5prime$.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The four-case discriminant supplies a finite label audit rather than a mechanism proof.  In the modeled-tAI abundance surface, yeast placed all nine coordinates in case $D$, while the E. coli surface placed seven coordinates in case $D$ and recorded two positive-positive entries without a mediation drop.  For yeast, the modeled-tAI readout had $R^{2}_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$; for E. coli, the corresponding values were $R^{2}_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^{2}_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$.  These classifications show that the same residual source can be read through translation and protein-abundance surfaces, but the labels remain finite statistical labels: they do not by themselves identify causality or molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row result is ready as a bounded reducibility statement.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual protein-abundance signal is not adequately described as an irreducible primitive on the current powered yeast panel, because measured readout families explain a statistically nontrivial part of the signal.  At the same time, the certificate leaves a large remainder, $0.7546178856026946$, outside the selected dictionary, so the correct mathematical conclusion is partial reducibility rather than closure.  The related structural-order ramp test also keeps the boundary narrow: its primary ramp signal was positive, with weighted ramp held-out $\Delta R^{2}=0.0011165640103326878$ and $p=0.0478515625$, but the paired ramp-minus-body localization gate did not pass, with $p=0.05859375$ and conclusion $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$.  Consequently no translation realization beyond the measured readouts, no folding claim, no physical-admissibility claim, no protein function claim, no causal mechanism, and no universal biological law follows from this chapter.  Each such assertion would require its own layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.
