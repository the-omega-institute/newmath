\subsection{A reducibility certificate for the powered $B^{*}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual}
\label{subsec:bioreality-bstar-q6-irreducibility-certificate}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite matrix surface over nine synonymous residual coordinates and matched readouts on a powered yeast join.  The source design is the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate block residualized against the same sibling control system used throughout the row family: CDS length, amino-acid composition, $GC3$, and the $M$-density baseline.  The coordinates are
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  The target is residualized $\log_{10}$ protein abundance on a unified Saccharomyces cerevisiae context join.  The registered irreducibility test used $n_{join}=1800$ rows from $n_{join,available}=4758$ available joined rows, with base organism count $base\_n=4759$, five held-out folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $120$ deterministic permutations, and control rank $rank(Z)=24$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
X &= B^{*}_{Q6}\hbox{ nine-coordinate residual design},\\
Y &= \log_{10}(\hbox{protein abundance})\perp Z,\\
Z &= \{\hbox{length},\hbox{amino-acid composition},GC3,M\hbox{-density}\},\\
n_{join} &= 1800,\quad n_{available}=4758,\quad \lambda_{DL}=0.01,\quad \rho_{join}=0.05.
\end{aligned}
$$

The module-level classification is therefore not a proof that the residual is primitive.  The tested finite claim is the opposite: the source signal is real, but at least one listed reduction family removes enough of it to refute the strong irreducibility interpretation.  The status is a reducibility certificate for the present readout family, not an irreducibility theorem.

\paragraph{Observed source signal.}
The protein-abundance surface is not empty.  In the direct $Q\to P$ protein-omics survival experiment, all fifteen organisms had full nine-coordinate survival after the same $Z$ residualization, with $rank(X\perp Z)=9$ and $rank(Z)=23$ in each organism-specific direct fit.  The strongest direct $R^2_{QP}$ was yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ on $5815$ joined CDS rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  Other direct protein-abundance values included Escherichia coli $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ on $3739$ joined rows, Caenorhabditis elegans $0.10217906758013379$ on $11661$ rows, Dictyostelium discoideum $0.08141786718177903$ on $6974$ rows, Drosophila melanogaster $0.0770952887136557$ on $13295$ rows, Pseudomonas aeruginosa $0.0646471273126996$ on $5006$ rows, Sulfolobus solfataricus $0.06397514251295629$ on $1656$ rows, Bacillus subtilis $0.061906245886660105$ on $4042$ rows, Mus musculus $0.049827984062539195$ on $19395$ rows, Danio rerio $0.04613529807085213$ on $14410$ rows, Homo sapiens $0.04454565118019448$ on $19053$ rows, Gallus gallus $0.03980793596647631$ on $12418$ rows, Mycobacterium smegmatis $0.03209009170649934$ on $4129$ rows, Rattus norvegicus $0.02763998603083845$ on $13322$ rows, and Halobacterium salinarum $0.019256877907572044$ on $1747$ rows.  Thus the primitive-candidate source is empirically live as a protein-abundance predictor after the declared controls.

\paragraph{Translation and abundance decomposition.}
The same yeast surface also has measured translation-efficiency contact, but it is not exhausted by that contact.  The measured ribosome-TE survival run reports yeast $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with all nine coordinates surviving on $5128$ TE genes, where the ribosome-TE join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  E. coli, human, and danio give $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$, $0.018837911344127926$, and $0.04034217614654355$, respectively.  In the measured $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition, yeast has $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$; E. coli has $S_{QP}=0.10941535951828074$ and mediated fraction $0.04091158340950673$; human has $S_{QP}=0.04288464996165745$ and mediated fraction $0.0027966485036786744$; danio has $S_{QP}=0.04926578454614652$ and clipped measured mediated fraction $0.0$ from raw value $-0.04801486048721136$.

On the yeast joint TE and mRNA-stability panel, the abundance residual decomposes further.  Yeast has $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  Human and E. coli also show joint fractions $0.16890712099923358$ and $0.2404669586626661$ with the same permutation probability.  These numbers show a real reducible component rather than a residue whose whole predictive content must be treated as primitive.

\paragraph{Reduction families.}
The strongest local certificate is the powered partial-mediation dictionary run in yeast.  On the same $n_{join}=1800$ active rows, five folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations, the best allowed readout dictionary drawn from measured TE, mRNA stability, and turnover covers $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ of the residual source signal.  The raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$, the selection-aware null $95$th percentile is $0.029149007242956115$, and the naive fixed-subset null $95$th percentile is $0.00598374993486675$.  The unexplained fraction remains $0.7546178856026946$, so this is partial reduction, not complete explanation.

The $f_3$-stress branch supplies an additional localized reduction clue.  On $n_{join}=1257$ yeast rows from a base set of $1800$, the dwell mediation run has base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$.  The deterministic permutation null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$ over $120$ permutations; the $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell run classifies the leading segment as $5'$: the $5'$ window passes, the $3'$ window does not, and the dominant segment is $5'$.

\paragraph{Residual boundary.}
The certificate does not collapse the whole residual into known translation or stability readouts.  The structural-order ramp run gives conclusion $ramp\_positive\_but\_not\_localized$: at primary ramp length $50$, weighted ramp held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, paired ramp-minus-body $0.001378137149572267$, ramp sign-flip probability $0.0478515625$, and paired ramp-body probability $0.05859375$.  This is a weak positional signal and not a structural-order localization claim.  The human turnover test is also not a successful reduction: $R^2_{Q,Tturn}=0.004615408769189838$, absorbed fraction $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$, null mean $0.0001262572958089998$, null $95$th percentile $0.0004342285066808464$, and the turnover absorption does not exceed that null.  Localization is mixed and descriptive: yeast absorbed $0.011225105608516642$ against null $95$th percentile $0.004958116973149795$, human absorbed $0.009254913329269913$ against $0.0013758521256775384$, and danio absorbed $0.009508864544725801$ against $0.011321328712802819$.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is therefore a reducibility certificate.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual block survives as a real predictor of measured protein abundance, and the powered yeast context supplies enough rank and held-out rows to test reduction families non-vacuously.  At the same time, measured TE, mRNA stability, dwell, localization, and readout-dictionary reductions account for a nonzero part of the protein-abundance residual.  The claim is ready when stated as: the residual source is real but not presently certified primitive.  No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, causal pathway, phenotype, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from this certificate.  Such conclusions require a separate layer-matched measurement and a separately stated statistic; direct protein abundance association, modeled codon-adaptation weights, or a positive partial reduction is not enough.
