\paragraph{Finite object.}

The status of this run is therefore not an abstract declaration that the residual basis is primitive; it is a finite test of whether the present yeast abundance signal resists the listed reduction families.

At the same time, about three quarters of the registered signal remains outside that selected family.

\paragraph{Verdict for the primitive claim.}
The finite table supports a precise negative-and-positive conclusion. The source signal is real: yeast shows strong direct protein-abundance survival, strong modeled and measured translation associations, and nontrivial measured TE plus mRNA-stability attenuation. The primitive, irreducible reading is false for the tested reduction family because a measured readout dictionary covers $0.24538211439730545$ of the registered signal, far above the selection-aware null boundary $0.029149007242956115$, and the joint TE plus mRNA-stability partition removes roughly half of the yeast direct association. The residual is nevertheless not exhausted: the partial-mediation run leaves $0.7546178856026946$ unexplained, the measured-TE mediation leaves a direct protein-abundance component after conditioning on TE, turnover is negligible in the human check, and localization is only a candidate absorber in organisms where the relevant join and null excess hold. The correct chapter-level statement is therefore: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ is not certified as a primitive irreducible biological law in the powered yeast surface; it is a real residual abundance surface that is partly reducible to measured translation and related readouts, with a large remaining component requiring further layer-matched contacts.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folding property, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, system phenotype, or universal biological law follows from this finite row certificate alone. A positive $Q\rightarrow P$ association is a residualized protein-abundance statistic; a positive $Q\rightarrow T$ association is a translation-readout statistic; an attenuation after conditioning on $T$ is a finite variance-partition result. Causal mechanism would require perturbation or temporal evidence, structure would require a structure-level contact, physical admissibility would require a physical or stability assay, function would require a functional readout, and universality would require a design beyond the organism- and dataset-scoped joins reported here.
