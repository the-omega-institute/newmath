\subsection{Rank-powered residual primitive certificate for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-primitive-certificate}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The object tested here is the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual design after the sibling control surface has been removed.  The source design is the $9$-dimensional coordinate matrix with coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The response is log$_{10}$ protein abundance after residualizing against the same sibling controls used by the protein-abundance and measured-translation panels.  In the yeast joined context the available join contains $4758$ rows from a base panel of $4759$ records, and the held-out certificate uses $n=1800$ rows.  The control basis has rank $24$, so the tested residual surface is not the rank-degenerate three-organism boundary: it is a powered finite-row matrix with enough row mass to ask whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block behaves as an irreducible primitive or as a reducible signal.

\paragraph{Certificate parameters.}
The registered run is the deterministic Saccharomyces cerevisiae certificate with seed $\mathtt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}$.  It uses $5$ folds, $120$ row-label permutations, description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and join penalty $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  Thus the tested object is not a visual association and not an unconstrained in-sample fit: a candidate reduction family must explain held-out residual abundance while paying both the finite dictionary penalty and the join penalty.  The fitted comparison can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{base} &= 4759,\\
N_{available} &= 4758,\\
N_{join} &= 1800,\\
K_Q &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 24,\\
\lambda_{DL} &= 0.01,\\
\rho_{join} &= 0.05,\\
B &= 120.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite claim is therefore sharply scoped: the run asks whether the residual protein-abundance signal carried by the nine-coordinate block remains primitive after the listed finite reduction families are allowed to compete under the same residualization.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The certificate returns a passed computational status, but the content of that pass is negative for irreducibility.  The registered semantics state that the source signal is real and that at least one listed reduction family refutes the original primitive-residual hypothesis.  In plain terms, the $B^*_{Q6}$ block is not certified as an irreducible biological module on this yeast surface.  It is a real residual predictor of the abundance readout, but at least part of the signal can be routed through a smaller measured or dictionary-like family rather than requiring the whole nine-coordinate block as a primitive explanatory atom.

\paragraph{Reduction pressure.}
The companion partial-mediation certificate gives the finite size of this reduction.  On the same yeast organism, with $N_{base}=4759$, $N_{available}=4758$, $N_{join}=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and rank-$24$ controls, the best selection-aware family drawn from the single-codon readout dictionary has raw coverage $0.2745002077400602$.  After the selection-aware null is charged, the accepted coverage is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{95}^{selection} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
\operatorname{Null}_{95}^{fixed} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the finite reason the primitive reading fails: a reduction family covers about $24.54\%$ of the target residual beyond the selection-aware null.  The larger residual share, about $75.46\%$, remains unexplained by that small dictionary.  The correct conclusion is therefore partial reducibility, not total reduction and not irreducibility.

\paragraph{Measured translation and abundance context.}
The irreducibility test sits downstream of several measured readout surfaces.  Direct $Q \to P$ protein-abundance survival is positive across a broad organism panel after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and M-density controls; in yeast the direct protein-abundance run reports $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving.  The measured ribosome-TE surface also survives in yeast, with $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ and all $9$ coordinates surviving.  When measured TE is conditioned into the protein-abundance decomposition, yeast has $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  These finite numbers explain why the residual primitive question is nontrivial: the block is not noise, and the protein surface is not empty.

\paragraph{Joint mediator comparison.}
A separate yeast joint mediation fit gives a larger structured reduction through measured TE and mRNA stability together.  On the four-way yeast join with $4759$ rows, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  The single-mediator sum is $0.532380691503749$, close to the joint fraction within the stated descriptive tolerance of $0.05$.  This does not turn the primitive certificate into a mechanism claim; it only shows that real measured readouts can absorb a large finite share of the direct abundance association.

\paragraph{Position-specific residual clue.}
The $f3\_stress$ dwell experiment provides one concrete residual subroute rather than a global explanation.  On yeast, with $n=1257$ joined rows and rank-$24$ controls, the held-out dwell increment is $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, compared with a permutation $95\%$ null of $0.0013115362189692937$.  The approximate $f3$--dwell association probability is $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$, and the dwell-alone $R^2$ is $0.034336580953121976$.  This supplies a finite local way in which one coordinate can be read through a measured ribosome-occupancy summary, but it does not exhaust the remaining $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual.

\paragraph{Classification.}
The module classification is therefore: real residual source, partially reducible, not an irreducibility certificate in the strong sense.  The pass status certifies that the current finite claim was evaluated and that the original primitive interpretation is defeated by at least one reduction family.  It does not say that the whole $B^*_{Q6}$ block has vanished, because the selection-aware dictionary certificate leaves unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  It also does not say that yeast defines a universal biological rule, because the matrix is organism-specific, cross-sectional, and readout-specific.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows merely from this certificate.  Positive measured-TE survival and positive measured mediation in related runs make translation readouts relevant, but the irreducibility run itself tests reducibility of a residual abundance surface.  No folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causal pathway, adaptation claim, or universal law follows from the finite matrix.  Any such stronger statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  Bulk protein abundance, ribosome TE, mRNA stability, dwell summaries, turnover, localization, structural order, and function are different readout layers; a positive association on one layer cannot be promoted to another layer without a measured contact for that layer and an explicit conditioning test.
