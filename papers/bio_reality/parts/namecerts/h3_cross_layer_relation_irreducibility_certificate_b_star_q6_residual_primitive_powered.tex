\subsection{Rank-powered residual primitive test for the yeast $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-residual-primitive-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
This record tests whether the observed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ protein-abundance surface should be treated as a primitive residual object, or whether a bounded family of measured readouts can account for a nonzero part of it.  The tested organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The design starts from a $9$-coordinate synonymous residual source, residualized against the same sibling control matrix used throughout the powered yeast abundance runs.  The source rows are joined on a unified yeast context: $n_{\mathrm{available}}=4758$ rows are available, the base abundance join has $4759$ rows, and the held-out certificate uses $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$ rows.  The control basis has rank $24$.  The cross-validation fit uses $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, ridge or description-length penalty $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and join threshold $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.  The deterministic seed is recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered{:}deterministic}$.

\paragraph{Readout geometry.}
The target is log-scaled protein abundance after the sibling controls have been removed.  Thus the finite object is not a raw codon table, and it is not a raw proteomics table.  It is a controlled matrix surface whose columns compare a $9$-dimensional codon-residual source against a one-dimensional controlled abundance target.  The source design is explicitly the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual family, with coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The direct abundance survival experiment supplies the larger surrounding matrix: across $15$ organisms every listed organism has rank-sufficient $9$-coordinate residual source after $23$-rank controls, and the yeast direct abundance association is the largest listed direct value, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
\#\{\mathrm{surviving\ coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
For context, the same direct protein-omics run reports $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, and $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse, again with $9$ surviving coordinates in each reported organism.  These numbers establish that the abundance surface is not a rank-degenerate all-one artifact, but they do not by themselves identify a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Reduction evidence.}
The decisive finite-row result is not an irreducibility proof.  The registered test passed in the following narrower sense: the source signal is real on the joined yeast surface, and at least one tested reduction family refutes the stronger hypothesis that the residual abundance signal is primitive.  The companion partial-mediation certificate gives the numerical shape of this refutation.  On the same yeast context family it uses $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $n_{\mathrm{available}}=4758$, $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and a rank-$24$ control basis.  Its reduction family is the single-codon readout dictionary selected from measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover candidates.  The best selected dictionary covers
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{\ast} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw best coverage before selection correction is $0.2745002077400602$.  The naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ threshold is $0.00598374993486675$, while the selection-aware null $95\%$ threshold is $0.029149007242956115$.  Since $0.24538211439730545$ is above the selection-aware threshold, the correct local statement is partial reducibility, not primitive irreducibility.

\paragraph{Translation and stability readouts.}
The measured translation-efficiency surface supplies the translation-side check.  In yeast, after the same style of length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls, measured ribosome-derived translation efficiency gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
\#\{\mathrm{surviving\ coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition gives yeast
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
The joint measured-TE and mRNA-stability decomposition gives yeast $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding fractions are $0.4697188319693239$ for measured TE alone, $0.0626618595344251$ for stability alone, and $0.5195182225621592$ for the joint pair, with right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The joint-minus-single-sum diagnostic is $-0.012862468941589822$, inside the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$, so the finite readback is close to additive at this level of resolution.

\paragraph{Dwell and positional refinement.}
The $f3\_stress$ component has a more localized readout.  On the yeast dwell-abundance run, the joined panel has $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$ over a base size $1800$, with $5$ folds and $120$ permutations.  The controlled $f3\_stress$ abundance component has squared norm $44.20095750389323$ and represents $0.1999063916718794$ of the target.  Adding dwell summaries raises held-out $R^2$ by $0.01856473944057712$ over the base model; the permutation null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95\%$ threshold $0.0013115362189692937$.  The observed $f3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$, with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional split classifies the leading segment as the $5'$ window: the $5'$ segment passes, the $3'$ body segment does not, and the dominant segment is recorded as $5prime$.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite certificate therefore has a one-sided logical form.  It confirms a real controlled yeast abundance signal and rejects the stronger primitive-residual interpretation for the registered reduction family.  It does not prove that the whole $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ abundance surface has been explained.  The residual fraction $0.7546178856026946$ remains outside the selected three-readout dictionary at the tested resolution.  The correct classification is partial reducibility of a rank-powered abundance residual, with a measured translation and stability component, plus a localized dwell component for $f3\_stress$.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folding claim, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, causal intervention result, or universal biological law follows from this certificate alone.  The protein-abundance target is cross-sectional, the translation-efficiency and stability readouts come from finite matched datasets, and the reductions are statistical variance partitions or held-out prediction increments.  A stronger biological statement would require an additional layer-matched contact and a finite statistic for that layer; codon residual geometry, direct protein-omics association, and partial mediation coverage are not substitutes for such a contact.
