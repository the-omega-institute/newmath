\subsection{Rank-powered residual reducibility certificate for $B^*_{Q6}$}
\label{subsec:bioreality-b-star-q6-residual-primitive-powered}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
The object tested here is the nine-coordinate synonymous residual surface $B^*_{Q6}$ on the unified yeast context join.  The source design consists of the coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f3_{stress}$, each residualized against the same sibling control basis.  The response is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the corresponding controls.  The run used Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with $4758$ rows available from a base yeast context of $4759$ rows and an active subsample of $1800$ joined rows.  The control basis had rank $24$, the cross-validation design used $5$ folds, and the registered description-length penalty and join penalty were $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$ and $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  The null comparison used $120$ deterministic permutations with seed $sha256:b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered:deterministic$.

\paragraph{Registered decision surface.}
The tested decision is not that the residual source is irreducible.  It is the opposite audit: the source signal must first be real, and then the listed reduction families are allowed to explain part of it.  In the companion reduction-family calculation on the same yeast context, the candidate readout list was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R &= \{\mathrm{measured\ TE},\ \mathrm{mRNA\ stability},\ \mathrm{turnover}\},\\
|R|_{max} &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The best selected family had raw coverage $0.2745002077400602$.  After selection-aware calibration the retained coverage was
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
N_{0.95}^{selection} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
N_{0.95}^{fixed} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the module classification is partial reducibility, not primitive irreducibility: a measured-readout dictionary explains a selection-aware positive fraction of the $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance residual, while roughly three quarters of the target remains outside that dictionary.

\paragraph{Protein and translation contacts used for calibration.}
The same yeast protein-abundance contact is not a mechanism by itself, but it establishes that the residual source is visible at the protein-omics readout.  In the direct $Q\to P$ calculation, the Saccharomyces cerevisiae protein-abundance join used $5815$ CDS rows with join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and the direct controlled protein-abundance survival was $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with all $9$ coordinates surviving the positive threshold.  The measured ribosome-TE contact separately used $5128$ genes, with ribosome-TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and controlled measured-TE survival $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, again with $9$ surviving coordinates.

The measured mediation calculation on the same organism reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,meas} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{meas} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
This places yeast in the mediated-but-incomplete regime: a measured translation-efficiency readout absorbs a substantial part of the $Q\to P$ association, but it does not exhaust it.  The four-case discriminant agrees with this bounded interpretation: in yeast the modeled readout table classified all $9$ coordinates as case $D$ and reported no positive-positive coordinate without a mediation drop.

\paragraph{Reduction evidence beyond a single mediator.}
The joint translation-efficiency and mRNA-stability calculation gives a second finite check on the same direction.  For Saccharomyces cerevisiae it reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The resulting joint mediation fraction was $0.5195182225621592$, with measured-TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$ and mRNA-stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$.  The right-tail permutation probability was $0.000999000999000999$.  The joint-minus-single-sum value was $-0.012862468941589822$, within the registered descriptive tolerance $0.05$, so the readouts act as approximately independent explanatory components at this resolution.

A more localized yeast readout also points to partial reducibility rather than primitive status.  The $f3_{stress}$ dwell calculation joined $1257$ rows from a base of $1800$ rows and found $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate probability $7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The base model had $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-only readout had $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and adding dwell gave held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, above the fixed readout permutation $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The positional follow-up classified the leading segment as $5'$; the $5'$ ramp passed, the $3'$ body did not, and the weighted ramp and body increments were $0.0011165640103326878$ and $-0.00026157313923957926$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite rows support a residual source that is real at the protein-abundance surface and partly reducible by measured translation, mRNA stability, turnover-family, and dwell-position readouts.  They do not support an irreducibility claim.  The certificate therefore records a refutation of primitive residual status under the registered dictionary rather than a proof that the remaining unexplained fraction is fundamental.  The unexplained value $0.7546178856026946$ is a residual accounting number, not an ontological statement.

\paragraph{Boundary of admissible interpretation.}
No translation mechanism, folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, molecular-function statement, causal mechanism, phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from these rows alone.  Protein abundance is a steady-state omics readout; measured TE is condition-specific and cross-sectional; mRNA stability, turnover, and dwell summaries are statistical readouts.  A stronger biological statement would require a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic showing that the proposed layer is actually observed after the same controls.
