\subsection{Powered residual-primitivity audit for the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance signal}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-residual-primitive-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The object tested here is the nine-coordinate synonymous residual surface $B^*_{Q6}$ on a rank-powered yeast join.  The source design is the $9$-dimensional coordinate matrix residualized against the same sibling control basis $Z$, and the target is log$_{10}$ protein abundance after the corresponding $Z$ residualization.  The active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the available same-context join has $4758$ usable rows, the base panel reports $4759$ rows, and the finite audit uses $n=1800$ joined rows with $5$ folds.  The control rank is $24$, the description-length penalty is $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, the join penalty is $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and the deterministic seed is $\mathrm{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}$.  In this chapter, ``primitive'' means not reduced, on the declared finite row surface, by the listed measured or computed readout families under the same residualization convention.

\paragraph{Observed source signal.}
The source signal is not a rank artifact.  In direct protein-omics survival, the same coordinate family has $9$ surviving coordinates against measured PAXdb abundance in each of $15$ organisms after length, amino-acid composition, GC3, and $M$-density controls.  The yeast direct value is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827,
\end{aligned}
$$
with $5815$ joined yeast CDS/protein rows and join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The cross-organism direct-abundance list includes $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.04454565118019448$ in human, $0.049827984062539195$ in mouse, $0.061906245886660105$ in \emph{B. subtilis}, and $0.06397514251295629$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.  These numbers establish a finite residual association with protein abundance, not a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Measured translation and abundance mediators.}
The measured-TE contact supplies a bounded translation readout on four organisms.  The measured TE survival values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{E. coli}) &= 0.009394266754822262,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.018837911344127926,\\
R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.04034217614654355.
\end{aligned}
$$
The measured mediation decomposition gives yeast the largest attenuation fraction:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.4793069656345167,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{E. coli}) &= 0.04091158340950673,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0.0027966485036786744,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{danio}) &= 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the yeast signal is substantially translation-conditioned, while the human and bacterial values show only small measured-TE attenuation on these rows.  This already prevents a single primitive interpretation: the same $B^*_{Q6}$ source vector can survive as direct abundance signal and also be partly absorbed by a measured translation readout.

\paragraph{Joint readout decomposition.}
On the four-way yeast join of codon rows, measured TE, mRNA stability, and protein abundance, the finite matrix has $4759$ joined rows.  The single and joint residual-abundance values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838.
\end{aligned}
$$
The resulting mediator fractions are $0.4697188319693239$ for TE alone, $0.0626618595344251$ for mRNA stability alone, and $0.5195182225621592$ jointly, with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  The joint-minus-single-sum value is $-0.012862468941589822$, inside the declared descriptive tolerance $0.05$, so the finite row surface classifies the two measured mediator channels as approximately independent for this variance partition.  The same statistic is smaller but nonzero in human and \emph{E. coli}: human joint mediation is $0.16890712099923358$, and \emph{E. coli} joint mediation is $0.2404669586626661$, each with the same right-tail probability $0.000999000999000999$.

\paragraph{Dictionary reduction.}
The direct residual source is therefore not primitive on the available yeast panel.  A separate finite dictionary audit over the single-codon readout family reports raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$ over candidate reductions and selection-aware retained coverage
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The selected readout list is $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$, with selection-aware null $95\%$ cutoff $0.029149007242956115$ and naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ cutoff $0.00598374993486675$ over $120$ permutations.  Since $C^*$ exceeds the selection-aware cutoff, the finite audit rejects full residual primitiveness for the registered source signal.  Since the unexplained fraction remains $0.7546178856026946$, the same audit also rejects complete reduction to the named readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Local residual channels.}
The $f_3$ stress coordinate gives a concrete subchannel.  On the active yeast base-plus-dwell join, $n=1257$ rows are used, $P_{f3}$ has norm squared $44.20095750389323$, the base held-out value is $0.1855578757644517$, the full held-out value is $0.20412261520502883$, and the dwell increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.01856473944057712.
\end{aligned}
$$
The fixed readout-permutation null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95\%$ cutoff $0.0013115362189692937$; the $f_3$--dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional audit localizes the leading dwell segment to the $5'$ window, with the $5'$ gate passing and the $3'$ body gate failing.  A related structural-order positional test classifies the ramp result as positive but not localized: the weighted $f_3$ ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, and the paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability is $0.05859375$.

\paragraph{Negative and bounded readbacks.}
Other candidate reductions remain bounded.  Human turnover absorbs only $3.5740764900116538\times 10^{-6}$ of the large modeled-complement residual, below the turnover null $95\%$ cutoff $0.0004342285066808464$, despite $8057$ joined human abundance/turnover/CDS rows.  Coarse localization absorbs more than its dof-matched null in some row sets but remains descriptive: yeast localization absorption is $0.011225105608516642$ with null $95\%$ cutoff $0.004958116973149795$, human localization absorption is $0.009254913329269913$ with null $95\%$ cutoff $0.0013758521256775384$, and danio localization absorption is $0.009508864544725801$ below its null $95\%$ cutoff $0.011321328712802819$.  These numbers are useful for stratifying residual channels, but they do not upgrade the abundance association to folding, localization mechanism, or function.

\paragraph{Four-case classification.}
The four-case discriminant on modeled-tAI and measured abundance gives a finite readback rather than a causal story.  Yeast has $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, with all $9$ coordinates classified as case D and no positive-positive coordinate left without mediation drop.  \emph{E. coli} has $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, with $7$ case-D coordinates and $2$ positive-positive entries without a mediation drop.  This finite classification is compatible with partial reducibility of the yeast abundance surface and with residual direct signal in other organisms.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The powered row audit therefore has a two-sided conclusion.  The signal is real enough to study: the yeast direct abundance association is large, measured TE survives, joint TE/stability mediation is positive, and the dictionary reduction exceeds the selection-aware null.  But the registered primitive reading fails: at least one measured reduction family explains a nontrivial portion of the source, so the $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual is not certified as irreducible on this panel.  The strongest local statement is partial reduction with a large remainder: measured TE, mRNA stability, turnover, dwell, and localization readouts partition some of the finite signal, while most of the residual remains unexplained by the tested dictionary.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
All claims in this subsection are finite-row claims about residualized matrices, held-out increments, permutation cutoffs, and variance partitions.  They do not establish translation causality, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, evolutionary direction, or a universal biological law.  A claim at any of those layers would require a separate matched contact and a separate controlled statistic at that layer.  Direct $q_i\to P_k$ survival is not treated as mechanism unless a measured translation readout $T_j$ survives and conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the corresponding $q_i\to P_k$ signal on the same declared row surface.
