\subsection{Residual Primitive Test for the Powered Yeast Surface}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-residual-primitive-powered}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The tested object is the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface on a unified Saccharomyces cerevisiae context join.  The source design consists of the coordinates $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$, each residualized against the same sibling control basis.  The target is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the sibling controls have been removed.  The active irreducibility test used $n=1800$ rows from $4758$ available joined rows, with base panel size $4759$, five folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, description-length penalty $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, join threshold $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and control rank $24$.  The deterministic seed was recorded as $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered{:}deterministic}$.

\paragraph{Question tested.}
The finite question is not whether the residual coordinates are interesting in a visual or semantic sense.  It is whether the abundance-directed signal carried by the nine-dimensional residual surface behaves as a primitive unexplained source after a finite family of simpler measured reductions is allowed to compete against it.  In symbols, the audit compares a residual source design $Q$ and a controlled abundance target $P$ against bounded reduction families $R$ under the same row set and control residualization:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &= \{K\_AAA, Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, f3\_stress\},\\
P &= \log_{10}(\mathrm{protein\ abundance}) \perp Z,\\
\mathrm{primitive}(Q,P) &\Longrightarrow \text{no admitted } R \text{ gives a selection-aware reduction of the } Q\to P \text{ signal.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The registered run passed in the operational sense that the source signal was tested and at least one listed reduction family refuted the primitive irreducibility hypothesis.  Thus the certificate is a negative irreducibility certificate: it records a real residual abundance signal together with finite evidence that the signal is not wholly primitive relative to the admitted reduction dictionary.

\paragraph{Reduction evidence.}
The accompanying partial-mediation certificate on the same yeast context used the same base size $4759$, $n=1800$, $4758$ available joined rows, five folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  Its best selected dictionary was drawn from the single-codon readout family with measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover as candidate readouts.  The raw best coverage over the candidate family was $0.2745002077400602$, while the selection-aware null $95$th percentile was $0.029149007242956115$ and the naive fixed-subset null $95$th percentile was $0.00598374993486675$.  The selected coverage retained after the selection audit was
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{*} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{*} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently the finite panel does not support a statement that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual source is irreducible.  It supports the narrower statement that the residual signal is real, partly reducible to a small measured-readout dictionary, and still leaves a substantial unexplained component on this row set.

These numbers explain why the irreducibility audit had a nontrivial source signal to test; they do not by themselves establish a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Joint readout decomposition.}
The joint TE--stability decomposition gives the strongest local reading of why the primitive claim fails.  On the yeast four-way join of $4759$ rows, the direct abundance residual association was $R^{2}_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on measured TE reduced the incremental $Q$ contribution to $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$; conditioning on mRNA stability alone gave $R^{2}_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$; conditioning on both yielded $R^{2}_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{TE\ only} &= 0.4697188319693239,\\
\mathrm{stability\ only} &= 0.0626618595344251,\\
\mathrm{joint\ mediation} &= 0.5195182225621592,\\
p_{perm} &= 0.000999000999000999.
\end{aligned}
$$
The overlap audit classified the TE and stability contributions as approximately independent at tolerance $0.05$, with single-mediator sum $0.532380691503749$ and joint-minus-single-sum $-0.012862468941589822$.  This decomposition is compatible with the partial-mediation certificate: a bounded measured dictionary absorbs a meaningful part of the abundance-directed residual signal, while a larger remainder stays outside that dictionary.

\paragraph{The $f3\_stress$ subcomponent.}
The $f3\_stress$ coordinate gives a more localized readback.  Its dwell-mediated abundance run used $n=1257$ joined yeast rows, base size $1800$, five folds, and $120$ permutations.  The base held-out value was $R^{2}=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-only value was $0.034336580953121976$, and the full value was $0.20412261520502883$, so the held-out gain was $\Delta R^{2}=0.01856473944057712$.  The fixed row-label permutation null had mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, minimum $-0.013799622026666476$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and null $95$th percentile $0.0013115362189692937$.  The observed $f3\_stress$--dwell correlation was $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional dwell run identified the $5'$ segment as dominant: the primary ramp window at $50$ codons had weighted ramp gain $0.0011165640103326878$, body gain $-0.00026157313923957926$, and ramp-minus-body value $0.001378137149572267$, with ramp sign-flip $p=0.0478515625$ and paired ramp-minus-body $p=0.05859375$.  The finite classification was therefore ramp-positive but not localized by the stricter paired gate.

\paragraph{Four-case status.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant is useful only as a bounded comparison, not as a replacement for measured TE.  For yeast it used $5815$ joined proteins and found $R^{2}_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^{2}_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, with all $9$ coordinates placed in case $D$ under its modeled-readout classification and zero positive-positive coordinates lacking a mediation drop.  For Escherichia coli K12 MG1655, the same classifier used $3739$ joined proteins and reported $R^{2}_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$, $R^{2}_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, seven case-$D$ coordinates, and two positive-positive coordinates without the required drop.  These modeled-readout labels support finite discrimination of readout patterns, but the primitive residual audit rests on the measured and dictionary-reduction rows above.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite row audit supports the following current-state claim.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface has a measurable yeast abundance association after amino-acid composition, length, GC3, and M-density controls, and the measured TE and mRNA-stability readouts absorb a statistically meaningful part of that association.  The selection-aware dictionary coverage $C^{*}=0.24538211439730545$ is far above its null threshold, but the unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$ prevents a complete reduction claim.  Therefore the correct certificate is neither a universal law nor a proof of primitive autonomy.  It is a finite negative test of primitive irreducibility: the residual source survives as a real abundance-associated surface, yet it is partially reducible by measured readout families on the powered yeast panel.

\paragraph{Local scope.}
No translation mechanism, folding statement, physical admissibility result, molecular function, causal pathway, phenotype, or universal biological principle follows from this chapter.  Measured TE, protein abundance, mRNA stability, turnover, localization, and dwell summaries are separate finite contacts with their own noise, row joins, and conditioning assumptions.  A direct $q_i\to P_k$ association is not a mechanism claim unless a same-row translation readout $T_j$ survives and conditioning on $T_j$ removes or reduces the $q_i\to P_k$ signal under the declared controls.  The present row set shows partial measured-readout reducibility of a yeast abundance residual; every higher biological layer requires its own matched contact and statistic.
