\subsection{Rank-powered residual primitive test for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-residual-primitive-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The tested object is the nine-coordinate synonymous residual surface $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ on the yeast matched context.  The source design consists of the coordinates $K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress, each residualized against the same sibling control space $Z$.  The target is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the same control residualization.  In the registered run $2040d8536a26$, the organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; the available join has $4758$ rows, the base panel records $4759$ rows, and the held analysis uses $n=1800$ joined rows.  The control basis has rank $24$, the fold count is $5$, the permutation count is $120$, the join threshold is $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and the description-length penalty is $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$.

\paragraph{Question tested.}
The primitive question is not whether $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ correlates with abundance.  That weaker fact is already visible on the direct yeast protein-omics surface: the measured PAXdb abundance join for yeast has $5815$ joined coding sequences, a reported join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$, and direct residual abundance survival
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP}(\text{yeast}) &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
\#\{\text{surviving coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The primitive question is sharper: after the source signal is known to exist, can the abundance-directed residual be treated as irreducible with respect to a small family of measured or bounded downstream readouts, or does a finite reduction family already absorb a nontrivial part of it?

\paragraph{Reduction family.}
The tested reduction family is the single-codon readout dictionary family attached to measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover-style readouts.  The companion partial-mediation run $a4dc9b09533a$ uses the same yeast organism key, the same $n=1800$ joined rows, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $Z$-rank $24$.  Its selected dictionary has coverage
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
\text{raw best coverage} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
\text{selection-aware null}_{95} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\
\text{naive fixed-subset null}_{95} &= 0.00598374993486675,\\
\text{unexplained fraction} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the registered primitive hypothesis is not certified.  The finite readout family explains a positive, selection-aware portion of the yeast abundance residual, while a larger residual part remains outside that family.

\paragraph{Measured translation comparison.}
The measured ribosome-efficiency contact gives a separate translation readout on the yeast rows.  In the ribosome-TE survival run, yeast has $5128$ genes with measured TE, a TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$, residual degrees of freedom $5105$, rank $9$ for the residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ design, and rank $23$ for the explicit control matrix before the reported $24$-rank basis convention. $$
\begin{aligned}

\#\{\text{surviving coordinates}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
When the measured TE readout is used in a joint protein-abundance decomposition, the yeast three-way join has $5128$ rows, $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, raw measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$, and clipped measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  These values show that measured TE is a real reducer of the abundance association, but they do not turn the residual coordinates into a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Joint mediator classification.}
The four-way yeast panel joining codon counts, measured TE, mRNA stability, and protein abundance has $4759$ joined rows and a reported join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  In the joint TE--stability decomposition, yeast has

This module classification is the operative reason the primitive label fails: the abundance residual is not fully explained, but it is not primitive with respect to the listed reducer family.

\paragraph{Position and dwell refinements.}
The $f_3$-stress dwell run supplies a more local readout of one coordinate. The base model has held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, the dwell-alone readout has $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, the full model has $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and the base-plus-dwell increment is
$$
\begin{aligned}

\text{null}_{95} &= 0.0013115362189692937,\\
p_{approx} &= 7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}.

A positional version classifies the leading segment as $5'$ rather than $3'$.  For the primary $50$-codon ramp, the weighted ramp increment is $0.0011165640103326878$, the weighted body increment is $-0.00026157313923957926$, the ramp-minus-body increment is $0.001378137149572267$, the ramp sign-flip probability is $0.0478515625$, and the paired ramp-minus-body sign-flip probability is $0.05859375$.  The recorded conclusion is ramp-positive but not localized, so this supplies a bounded positional reduction cue rather than a structural-order claim.

A separate modeled-tAI discriminant classifies the yeast coordinates as case $D$ for all nine coordinates under its own definition: case counts are $A=0$, $B=0$, $C=0$, $D=9$, with no unclassified coordinates and no positive-positive entry lacking the mediation drop. This is useful as a finite discriminant on the chosen modeled translation surface, but the irreducibility certificate is judged by the measured and dictionary reductions above, not by treating modeled adaptation weights as ribosome observations.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter verdict is a refutation of the original irreducibility reading, not a proof of complete explanation.  The source signal is real on the yeast abundance surface, and at least one listed reduction family absorbs a selection-aware positive fraction of it.  The correct finite statement is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{primitive irreducibility} &\text{ is not certified},\\
\text{partial reducibility} &\text{ is supported on the yeast finite panel},\\
\text{remaining unexplained fraction} &= 0.7546178856026946.
\end{aligned}
$$
The result remains an observational finite-row decomposition.  It does not establish translation causality, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, a biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological rule.  Any such claim would require a separate layer-matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.
