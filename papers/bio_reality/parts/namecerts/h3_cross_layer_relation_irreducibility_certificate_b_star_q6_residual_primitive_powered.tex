\paragraph{Reduction evidence.}
The decisive finite-row datum is that the registered irreducibility test did not certify irreducibility.  Its status semantics say that the tested source signal is real and that at least one listed reduction family refutes the original irreducibility hypothesis.  The companion partial-mediation certificate gives the concrete reduction scale: on yeast, with $n_{join}=1800$, $n_{join\_available}=4758$, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, $\rho_{join}=0.05$, and $120$ permutations, the single-codon readout dictionary family using at most three measured readouts $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$ attains
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946,\\
\mathrm{raw\ best\ coverage} &= 0.2745002077400602,\\
\mathrm{selection\ aware\ null}_{95} &= 0.029149007242956115,\\

This is a partial reduction rather than a full collapse: roughly one quarter of the residual abundance component is covered by the listed small readout dictionary, while roughly three quarters remains outside that finite reduction.  The correct classification is therefore ``partly reducible residual primitive'', not ``irreducible primitive''.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is a negative irreducibility certificate with positive residual content.  The source $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance signal is real on a powered yeast join, but the supplied reduction family refutes the stronger claim that this residual surface is primitive relative to the listed measured readouts.  A mathematically faithful statement is: a finite, rank-powered part of the $B^*_{Q6}\to P$ association is reducible to measured TE, mRNA-stability, turnover, and dwell-like readouts, while a substantial remainder is still not assigned to any supplied smaller biological readout family.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folding statement, physical-admissibility statement, molecular-function statement, phenotype statement, causal statement, or universal biological rule follows from this chapter.  A positive $Q\to P$ association is a protein-omics association after controls; a positive $Q\to T$ association is a bounded translation-readout association in the measured panel; a positive attenuation after conditioning is a statistical variance partition.  Any claim about translation mechanics, folding, localization mechanism, stability mechanism, molecular function, phenotype, or biological universality requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
