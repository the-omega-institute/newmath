\subsection{A finite certificate for reducibility of the yeast $B^*_{Q6}$ abundance residual}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-irreducibility-certificate}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns the rank-powered yeast panel in which the source design is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinate system, residualized against the same sibling control basis used by the surrounding translation and protein-abundance tests.  The target is residual log$_{10}$ protein abundance in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The active finite surface contains $4758$ available joined rows, of which $1800$ rows enter the certificate fit; the base matched panel reports $4759$ rows.  The control basis has rank $24$, the cross-validation split count is $5$, the deterministic permutation count is $120$, and the registered penalties are $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$ and $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$.  The deterministic seed is recorded as \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}.  Thus the chapter is not asserting a population law; it is reading a fixed finite table with declared row count, rank, penalties, folds, and permutation budget.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and controlled target.}
The source coordinates are the nine residual axes
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In the direct protein-omics survival run, these nine coordinates remain rank-sufficient after the standard controls in yeast, with $5815$ joined CDS/protein rows, residual degrees of freedom $5792$, rank $9$ for the residualized $Q$ block, rank $23$ for the control block, and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ against log$_{10}$ PAXdb protein abundance.  The same yeast row family gives a measured-TE survival value $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ on $5128$ measured-TE genes, while the measured protein mediation calculation reports $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$, and a measured mediation fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  These numbers establish that the source block is not numerically empty on the yeast table; they do not by themselves make the residual primitive.

\paragraph{What the certificate decides.}
The irreducibility test was registered as a finite reduction search on the same controlled yeast abundance target.  Its outcome is explicitly not a proof that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual is an irreducible biological primitive.  The tested outcome is the opposite boundary: the source signal is real on the registered surface, and at least one listed reduction family is strong enough to defeat the original irreducibility hypothesis.  In the companion partial-mediation certificate on the same organism and the same $n=1800$ active join, a candidate readout dictionary family using at most three readouts from $\{\mathrm{measured\_te},\mathrm{mrna\_stability},\mathrm{turnover}\}$ obtains raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, selection-aware coverage $C^*=0.24538211439730545$, and an unexplained fraction $0.7546178856026946$.  Its selection-aware null $95\%$ level is $0.029149007242956115$, while the naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ level is $0.00598374993486675$.  In formula form, the registered finite readback is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^* &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^* &= 0.7546178856026946,\\
C^* &> 0.029149007242956115.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is enough to make the residual partially reducible on the declared readout family, but not enough to exhaust the residual.

\paragraph{Relation to translation and stability readouts.}
The larger three-organism variance partition shows why the yeast certificate cannot be read as a single-layer translation result.  For yeast, the joint TE/stability run reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.1439989545940314$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.25453612246767165$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$.  The corresponding joint mediation fraction is $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and a right-tail permutation value $0.000999000999000999$.  The overlap is approximately additive at the declared tolerance, since the joint-minus-single-sum diagnostic is $-0.012862468941589822$ against a tolerance of $0.05$.  This puts the reducibility certificate in a precise position: measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability explain a substantial part of the protein-abundance association, but the remaining residual is not eliminated.

\paragraph{The $f3\_stress$ subcertificate.}
The $f3\_stress$ coordinate supplies a concrete local reduction pressure inside the same yeast ecosystem.  The dwell mediation run joins $1257$ rows, uses five folds and $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, and reports base held-out $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full held-out $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$.  The permutation null has mean $-0.0032911642620130563$, maximum $0.005001350039772926$, and $95\%$ level $0.0013115362189692937$; the $f3$-dwell correlation is $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  Positional splitting points to the $5'$ window as the leading segment: the primary $50$-codon ramp has weighted $f3$ ramp $\Delta R^2=0.0011165640103326878$, weighted body $\Delta R^2=-0.00026157313923957926$, ramp-minus-body $\Delta R^2=0.001378137149572267$, and sign-flip probabilities $0.0478515625$ for ramp positivity and $0.05859375$ for ramp-minus-body positivity.  The structural-order positional run therefore classifies the outcome as ramp-positive but not localized, rather than as a structural-order claim.

\paragraph{Module classification.}
The correct module label for this chapter is a reducibility boundary for a real residual signal.  It is not an irreducibility theorem.  The finite table supports three simultaneous statements.  First, the $B^*_{Q6}$ block has measurable associations with yeast protein abundance and measured translation readouts under the declared controls.  Second, finite readout families reduce a nontrivial fraction of the protein-abundance residual; the partial-mediation certificate reports $24.538211439730545\%$ covered and $75.46178856026946\%$ left unexplained.  Third, because a reduction family refutes the primitive-residual hypothesis, the remaining residual should be treated as an unresolved remainder rather than as a new biological principle.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows merely from this certificate.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, evolutionary direction, causal pathway, or universal biological rule follows from the direct $q_i\to P_k$ association, from the measured-TE attenuation, from the dwell subreadout, or from the partial dictionary coverage.  A stronger biological assertion would require a separate layer-matched contact: measured translation rows for translation claims, structure or stability assays for physical-state claims, function or phenotype assays for biological-role claims, and perturbation or rescue data for mechanism claims.  Within this chapter the admissible conclusion is finite and narrower: on the registered yeast surface, the residual source signal is real, it is partially compressible by measured readout families, and the original primitive irreducibility reading is not supported.
