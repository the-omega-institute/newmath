\subsection{A Primitive-Residual Audit for the Rank-Powered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Surface}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-primitive-residual-audit}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The audit uses a yeast-centered finite matrix whose rows are coding-sequence entries and whose source coordinates are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The source design is residualized against the same sibling control surface used in the protein-abundance and translation-readout runs: amino-acid composition, coding-sequence length, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline, with a reported control-basis rank of $24$ for the unified yeast context.  The irreducibility audit is not a symbolic declaration that these coordinates are primitive.  It is a finite-row test of whether the remaining abundance signal can be reduced by a listed family of measured or constructed readouts.

\paragraph{Registered audit row.}
The registered run $2040d8536a26$ uses Saccharomyces cerevisiae, with $n=1800$ rows sampled from $4758$ available joined rows and a base panel size $4759$.  The held-out design uses $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic permutations, a description-length penalty $\lambda\_{DL}=0.01$, and a join threshold $\rho\_{join}=0.05$.  The target readout is the residualized protein-abundance coordinate $\log_{10}$ protein abundance after the sibling control surface has been removed.  In finite-row notation the tested object is the residual projection
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_Q &= \operatorname{resid}_Z(B^{\ast}_{Q6}),\\
Y_P &= \operatorname{resid}_Z(\log_{10} P),\\
Z\text{-rank} &= 24,\qquad n_{join}=1800,\qquad \lambda_{DL}=0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The run status is passed in the local operational sense: the source signal is present and at least one registered reduction family defeats the original primitive-residual hypothesis.  Thus the result is a controlled refusal of irreducibility, not a certificate that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface is biologically fundamental.

\paragraph{Why the primitive reading fails.}
The direct protein-omics surface is strong enough to justify asking the question.  Across the broader protein-abundance panel, every reported organism has all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates surviving against measured PAXdb abundance after the same class of controls.  The yeast direct abundance run reports $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ with $9$ surviving coordinates on $5815$ joined proteins, while E. coli reports $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, human $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$, and mouse $R^2_{QP}=0.049827984062539195$.  These values establish that the residual surface is not empty, but they do not by themselves locate a mechanism.

The reduction runs show why the stronger primitive claim is not allowed.  The partial mediation certificate for the same yeast context uses $n=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{DL}=0.01$, and $\rho_{join}=0.05$.  Its best selected readout dictionary, drawn from measured translation efficiency, mRNA stability, and turnover candidates, covers
$$
\begin{aligned}
C^{\ast} &= 0.24538211439730545,\\
1-C^{\ast} &= 0.7546178856026946,
\end{aligned}
$$
with raw best coverage $0.2745002077400602$, naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ level $0.00598374993486675$, and selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$.  Since the selected coverage is well above the selection-aware null while still leaving most of the signal unexplained, the finite-row conclusion is partial reducibility.  A primitive-residual interpretation would require the listed readout families to fail as reductions; the observed panel does the opposite.

\paragraph{Measured translation and abundance relation.}
The measured translation-efficiency row supplies an independent layer check.  In yeast, the ribosome-derived measured-TE survival run has $R^2_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$ with $9$ surviving coordinates, while the same organism's measured mediation run has $S_{QP}=0.3146818503437042$, $S_{QT,meas}=0.13681931343730436$, and measured mediated fraction $0.4793069656345167$.  E. coli is much weaker on this path, with $S_{QT,meas}=0.009394266754822262$ and measured mediated fraction $0.04091158340950673$; human has $S_{QT,meas}=0.018837911344127926$ and mediated fraction $0.0027966485036786744$; danio has $S_{QT,meas}=0.04034217614654355$ and a clipped mediated fraction $0.0$ after a raw suppressive value $-0.04801486048721136$.  The same finite surface therefore separates three cases: direct protein-omics association, measured translation-conditioned reduction, and residual material left outside the measured translation readout.

The joint TE--mRNA-stability decomposition sharpens this point.  In yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ drops to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13047582645538838$, giving joint mediation fraction $0.5195182225621592$, with TE-only fraction $0.4697188319693239$, stability-only fraction $0.0626618595344251$, and right-tail permutation probability $0.000999000999000999$.  E. coli and human show smaller but nonzero joint fractions, $0.2404669586626661$ and $0.16890712099923358$, with the same reported right-tail probability $0.000999000999000999$.  These finite rows are incompatible with a claim that the residual abundance signal is wholly primitive relative to measured readout families.

\paragraph{Local residual structure.}
The $f3\_stress$ component also has a positional and dwell-linked reduction in yeast.  The dwell mediation run joins $1257$ rows, uses $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, and reports base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, null $95\%$ level $0.0013115362189692937$, and approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$ for the $f3$--dwell correlation $0.1615441450901653$.  The $f3$ projection accounts for fraction $0.1999063916718794$ of the target component with norm square $44.20095750389323$.  This is a concrete reduction of one residual direction to a local ribosome-occupancy summary, again supporting partial reducibility rather than irreducibility.

\paragraph{Four-case readback.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant remains useful as a bounded readback, but it is not the final mechanism.  In yeast it classifies all nine coordinates as case $D$ under the modeled translation-readout and protein-abundance surface, with $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  E. coli has seven case $D$ coordinates and two positive-positive entries without the required mediation drop, with $R^2_{QT}=0.5416736993691318$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$.  These classifications show that the finite matrix can distinguish translation-surviving, protein-surviving, and mediation-conditioned entries, but modeled tAI remains a bounded codon-adaptation readout rather than observed ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The finite-row verdict is therefore precise: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual protein-abundance surface is real on the tested yeast panel, but the registered primitive-residual hypothesis is refuted by measured and candidate reduction families.  The remaining unexplained fraction is a research target, not permission to promote the coordinate geometry into biology.  No translation mechanism, folding statement, physical admissibility claim, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal pathway, evolutionary direction, or universal biological law follows from this audit unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the appropriate controlled statistic.  Direct $Q_i\to P_k$ survival is only a protein-omics association until a positive $Q_i\to T_j$ step and an attenuation of $Q_i\to P_k$ under $T_j$ are both observed on the same controlled panel.
