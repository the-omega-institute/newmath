\subsection{Rank-Powered Residual Primitive Test for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$}
\label{sec:biorality-b-star-q6-residual-primitive}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record tests whether the nine-dimensional synonymous residual surface $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ behaves as a primitive, unreduced source for protein-abundance signal after the sibling control basis has been removed.  The tested design is explicitly finite: the source design is the $9$-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual matrix, the target is $\log_{10}$ protein abundance residualized against the same sibling $Z$ controls, and the active organism is \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The unified yeast context has $n_{\mathrm{available}}=4758$ joinable rows, the registered fit uses $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$ rows from a base panel of $4759$, and the sibling control basis has rank $24$.  The certificate uses $5$ folds, $120$ deterministic row-label permutations, description-length penalty $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, and join penalty $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, with seed \texttt{sha256:b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered:deterministic}.  In this chapter the word certificate is therefore read operationally: it records a finite attempted primitive test, not a metaphysical assertion that the coordinates are irreducible.

\paragraph{Translation readout comparison.}
The direct protein signal is not enough to identify a translation mechanism, so the row-level translation contacts are kept separate.  In measured ribosome-derived TE assays, the same coordinate family has $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.040342$ in zebrafish, $0.009394$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.018838$ in human, and $0.136819$ in yeast, again with all $9$ coordinates surviving in each powered matrix.  The corresponding measured mediation decomposition gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{zebrafish})&=0,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\emph{E. coli})&=0.040912,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{human})&=0.002797,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}}(\mathrm{yeast})&=0.479307.
\end{aligned}
$$
The yeast row is therefore the finite row where translation conditioning is strongest among the measured TE panels: $S_{QP}=0.314682$, $S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.136819$, and nearly half of the direct abundance association is absorbed by measured TE in the registered scalar decomposition.  This does not make the residual primitive; it makes the primitive hypothesis empirically vulnerable, because a named measured readout removes a large part of the apparent direct signal.

\paragraph{Reduction evidence.}
The strongest local reduction record is the partial mediation certificate on the same yeast context.  It uses $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1800$, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and rank-$24$ sibling controls.  The best selected readout dictionary using at most three measured readouts $\{\texttt{measured\_te},\texttt{mrna\_stability},\texttt{turnover}\}$ covers $C^{\ast}=0.245382$ of the residual signal, leaving $0.754618$ unexplained.  Its raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.274500$, the naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ level is $0.005984$, and the selection-aware null $95\%$ level is $0.029149$.  Thus the reduction is not a saturated bookkeeping artifact: it clears the selection-aware permutation boundary by a wide margin, while still leaving most of the signal outside the tested readout dictionary.

\paragraph{Additional mediator rows.}
The joint TE--mRNA-stability decomposition sharpens the reduction picture.  In yeast, $R^2_{QP}=0.271552$, $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.143999$, $R^2_{QP\mid M}=0.254536$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.130476$, giving a joint mediation fraction $0.519518$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.000999$.  The split is dominated by the measured-TE component, with TE-only fraction $0.469719$ and stability-only fraction $0.062662$.  In \emph{E. coli}, the joint mediation fraction is $0.240467$ with TE-only $0.019755$ and stability-only $0.230521$; in human it is $0.168907$ with TE-only $0.005495$ and stability-only $0.153147$.  These rows classify the residual surface as partially reducible to measured layer readouts in an organism-dependent way rather than as a single primitive law.

\paragraph{The $f3$ stress subcoordinate.}
The $f3\_{stress}$ coordinate provides a concrete subcase where positional translation data reduce part of the abundance component.  On the yeast dwell panel, $n_{\mathrm{join}}=1257$, $\operatorname{rank}(f3)=1$, base $R^2=0.185558$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034337$, full $R^2=0.204123$, and held-out $\Delta R^2=0.018565$.  The deterministic null has mean $-0.003291$, maximum $0.005001$, and null $95\%$ level $0.001312$, so the dwell increment is far above the registered null boundary; the approximate $f3$--dwell correlation probability is $7.873916\times 10^{-9}$.  The positional split identifies the leading segment as $5'$: the five-prime ramp passes, the three-prime body does not, and the dominant segment is recorded as \texttt{5prime}.  This is a localized readout reduction for one coordinate component, not a statement about folding or cellular function.

\paragraph{Negative and weak higher-layer candidates.}
The same discipline applies to higher-layer candidates.  Coarse localization absorbs $0.011225$ of the yeast modeled complement against a degree-of-freedom matched null $95\%$ level $0.004958$, while human absorbs $0.009255$ against $0.001376$; zebrafish absorbs $0.009509$ but fails the strict complement-match tolerance for this diagnostic join.  Human turnover is much weaker: on $8057$ joined rows it gives $R^2_{Q,Tturn}=0.004615$ and absorbed fraction $3.574076\times 10^{-6}$, below the null $95\%$ level $0.000434$, so it is not a successful reduction in the registered human half-life contact.  These facts prevent a one-word classification of the residual as either primitive or explained: some measured readouts reduce finite components, some do not, and the unexplained fraction remains substantial.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The registered primitive test is ready as a finite row-level audit of reducibility.  The source signal is real on the yeast abundance target, but the original irreducibility reading is not certified.  At least one measured reduction family refutes the strongest primitive interpretation: the dictionary certificate covers $0.245382$ selection-aware residual fraction, measured TE absorbs $0.479307$ of the yeast direct $Q\to P$ association in the four-organism mediation panel, and the joint TE--stability row absorbs $0.519518$ in yeast with permutation probability $0.000999$.  The remaining $0.754618$ unexplained fraction is equally important: the data support partial reducibility, not full explanation.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, evolutionary direction, causal pathway, or universal biological rule follows from this certificate.  A direct $Q\to P$ association is only a controlled protein-abundance association; it becomes translation-conditioned evidence only when a measured or explicitly bounded translation readout survives and conditioning on that readout removes or reduces the direct signal.  Structural order, localization mechanism, turnover mechanism, physical feasibility, function, and phenotype each require their own layer-matched contact and statistic.  The finite record here says that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual abundance signal is real, partly reducible, and not presently primitive in the strong sense.
