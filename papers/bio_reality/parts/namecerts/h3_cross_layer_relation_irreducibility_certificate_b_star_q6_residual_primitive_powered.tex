\subsection{Powered residual primitivity test for the yeast $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ surface}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-residual-primitive-powered}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The claim tested here is not that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual block is biologically primitive in an unrestricted sense.  It is the narrower finite-row question of whether the nine-dimensional synonymous residual design remains unreduced after it is placed on a powered matched yeast panel and compared with a fixed family of measured downstream readouts.  The source design consists of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates residualized against the same sibling control basis used by the surrounding protein-abundance experiments: amino-acid composition, CDS length, GC3, the $M$-density baseline, and the intercept-containing control span.  In the registered yeast run the available unified context join had $4758$ usable rows, the active training surface used $n=1800$ rows, and the base TE/abundance context reported $4759$ source rows.  The control basis had rank $24$, the cross-validation fold count was $5$, the dictionary penalty was $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, the join floor was $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, and the row-label null used $120$ deterministic permutations with seed $\mathrm{sha256{:}b\_star\_q6\_irreducibility\_certificate\_powered{:}deterministic}$.

\paragraph{What the certificate says.}
The powered result is a reducibility certificate, not a proof of irreducibility.  Its status field records that the source signal is real and that at least one listed reduction family refutes the original primitive-residual hypothesis.  The target was the residualized yeast protein-abundance vector $\log_{10}(P)$ on the unified context join, not a translation-rate measurement, not a perturbation assay, and not a structural or functional assay.  In compact notation the tested relation was
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_Q &\in \mathbb{R}^{1800\times 9},\\
Z &\in \mathbb{R}^{1800\times 24},\\
y &= \operatorname{resid}_{Z}(\log_{10} P),\\
\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}} &= 0.01,\qquad \rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05,\qquad B=120.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite conclusion is therefore negative with respect to residual primitivity: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ block is not certified as an irreducible biological primitive on this yeast abundance surface, because a registered reduction family carries a nontrivial part of the same signal.

\paragraph{Dictionary reduction.}
The most direct reduction record is the partial-mediation dictionary run on the same yeast context.  It used $n=1800$ rows from $4758$ available joined rows, $5$ folds, $\lambda_{\mathrm{DL}}=0.01$, $\rho_{\mathrm{join}}=0.05$, rank $24$ controls, and $120$ deterministic permutations.  The candidate readout dictionary was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{dict}}=\{\mathrm{measured\ TE},\ \mathrm{mRNA\ stability},\ \mathrm{turnover}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The raw best coverage over the candidate family was $0.2745002077400602$.  After selection correction, the supported coverage was $C^{\ast}=0.24538211439730545$, with selection-aware null $95\%$ level $0.029149007242956115$ and naive fixed-subset null $95\%$ level $0.00598374993486675$.  The unexplained fraction was $0.7546178856026946$.  Thus the certificate is not a collapse of the whole residual block; it is a finite statement that at least one small measured-readout family reduces enough signal to defeat the primitive-residual reading.

\paragraph{Measured translation and abundance context.}
The reducibility result sits on top of a measurable translation and abundance background.  In yeast, measured ribosome-derived TE survival for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ used $5128$ TE genes against $5815$ CDS-abundance joined rows, with TE join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  After the same control style, the measured TE surface had $R^2_{QT,\mathrm{meas}}=0.13681931343730436$ and all $9$ coordinates survived the positive threshold.  The direct protein-abundance surface in yeast had $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, again with $9$ surviving coordinates, and the abundance join reported $5815$ proteins with hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  The measured TE-to-protein mediation run on yeast used $5128$ three-way joined rows and reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{QP} &= 0.3146818503437042,\\
S_{QT,\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.13681931343730436,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{meas}} &= 0.4793069656345167.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers explain why the primitive-residual question is meaningful: there is a real finite signal to reduce.  They do not by themselves say that the codon residuals are causal, structural, functional, universal, or mechanistic.

\paragraph{Stability and dwell reductions.}
A second finite-row view comes from the joint TE and mRNA-stability decomposition.  In yeast the single and joint fractions were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.1439989545940314,\\
R^2_{QP\mid M} &= 0.25453612246767165,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13047582645538838,\\
\mathrm{joint\ fraction} &= 0.5195182225621592.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding single-readout decomposition recorded a TE-only fraction of $0.4697188319693239$, a stability-only fraction of $0.0626618595344251$, and an approximately independent overlap mode, since the joint fraction differed from the single-mediator sum by $-0.012862468941589822$ within the fixed descriptive tolerance $0.05$.  A separate yeast dwell test targeted the $f_3$ component of the abundance residual.  It joined $1257$ rows, used $5$ folds and $120$ permutations, found base $R^2=0.1855578757644517$, full $R^2=0.20412261520502883$, held-out $\Delta R^2=0.01856473944057712$, dwell-alone $R^2=0.034336580953121976$, and a permutation null $95\%$ level $0.0013115362189692937$.  The $f_3$-dwell correlation was $0.1615441450901653$ with approximate $p=7.873916057196935\times 10^{-9}$.  These reductions are compatible with a residual signal that is partly organized by measured translation-adjacent readouts rather than by an indivisible primitive coordinate block.

\paragraph{Four-case classification.}
The modeled-tAI four-case discriminant supplies a useful local classification boundary.  On yeast it classified all $9$ coordinates as case $D$ and none as case $A$, $B$, or $C$: the case-count vector was $(0,0,0,9)$ with zero positive-positive entries lacking a mediation drop.  On the same modeled-tAI yeast surface the recorded values were $R^2_{QT}=0.8213457220900503$ and $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$.  This classification is not used here as a mechanistic proof.  It is used only to show that the same finite matrix surface is not merely a direct protein-omics association detached from translation-like readouts.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible reading is deliberately narrow.  The yeast powered certificate says that the registered $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual source is finite, rank-supported, non-null, and partly reducible by measured readout families on the tested abundance surface.  It does not establish translation causality, ribosome mechanism, protein folding, localization mechanism, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, adaptation, or a universal biological law.  Those stronger interpretations would require their own matched contacts and their own finite statistics.  In particular, a direct $q_i\to P_k$ association remains only a downstream omics association unless a measured $q_i\to T_j$ step is present and conditioning on that measured translation readout removes or reduces the protein-abundance signal.  The present record therefore changes the status of the primitive-residual hypothesis, not the biological layer above it: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ is observed here as a reducible residual signal surface under a specified finite dictionary, with a large unexplained remainder still open.
