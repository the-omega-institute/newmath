\paragraph{Question being certified.}
The tested statement is not that the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ surface is intrinsically irreducible.  It is the opposite kind of finite check: if a named family of lower-dimensional or biologically matched readouts explains a nontrivial part of the same source-to-target signal beyond the selection-aware null, then the original primitive reading is rejected for this dataset.  In symbols, the decision compares a source projection $P_Q$ against a measured-readout reduction family $\mathcal R$ on the same active join:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q &\in \mathbb R^{1800\times 9},\\
Z &: \operatorname{rank}(Z)=24,\\
Y_Z &= \operatorname{resid}_{Z}(\log_{10} P),\\
C^{*} &= \max_{R\in\mathcal R,\ |R|\leq 3}\operatorname{coverage}(P_Q;R),\\
\text{irreducible on this contact} &\Longrightarrow C^{*}\leq C^{*}_{0.95}. 
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded reduction dictionary is the single-codon readout family constrained to at most three measured readouts, with selected readouts \texttt{measured\_te}, \texttt{mrna\_stability}, and \texttt{turnover}.  Its raw best coverage over the candidate family is $0.2745002077400602$. Thus the reduction is much larger than the corrected null surface, so the finite certificate rejects a primitive-only interpretation of the yeast protein-abundance residual.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The irreducibility hypothesis fails on the registered yeast panel.  The source signal is present, the row ranks are powered, and the reduction family beats both the naive and selection-aware null boundaries.  The finite certificate should therefore be read as a reducibility certificate: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ retains a large unexplained component, but it is not primitive with respect to the measured dictionary $\{\texttt{measured\_te},\texttt{mrna\_stability},\texttt{turnover}\}$ on this contact.  Numerically, about $24.538\%$ of the tested source projection is covered after selection correction and about $75.462\%$ remains outside the listed readouts.  The certified object is a bounded cross-layer statistical decomposition of codon residuals, measured translation efficiency, RNA stability, turnover, dwell, and protein abundance in yeast.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation mechanism, folding state, physical admissibility, molecular function, system phenotype, causal pathway, adaptation direction, or universal biological law follows from this certificate.  Direct $Q\to P$ survival, measured $Q\to T$ survival, dwell increments, four-case labels, and readout-dictionary coverage are finite statistical surfaces over matched rows and controls.  A stronger biological interpretation would require an additional layer-matched contact that directly measures the proposed layer and a separate statistic showing that the corresponding signal survives after the same controls.
