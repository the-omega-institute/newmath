\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record treats the Yang \textit{E. coli} endogenous synonymous-edit panel as a bounded perturbation surface rather than as a free-standing biological law.  The finite rows are coding-sequence edits whose amino-acid sequence is held fixed, while the tested coordinates are B$^{*}_{Q6}$ window residuals attached to synonymous codon choices.  The registered run used $30$ endogenous genes and $46686$ synonymous edits, with $B=200$ resampling units and a recorded runtime of $491.605$ seconds.  Thus the admissible object here is not an unrestricted genotype--phenotype theory; it is the finite matrix of edited coding windows, residual coordinates, baseline covariates, and the assayed fitness readout.

\paragraph{Controlled readback.}
The intended readback is a window-level association between synonymous residual coordinates and the measured endogenous fitness response.  The coordinate set is inherited from the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis, whose computed axes are $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The experimental panel is therefore read as a finite perturbation test of whether synonymous-window geometry contributes to endogenous fitness variation after the usual simpler coding baselines are kept in the model.  The run-level evidence supports only the existence and completion of the powered finite-row audit for this dataset: $n_{genes}=30$, $n_{edits}=46686$, and $B=200$.

\paragraph{Relation to the translation and protein-abundance boundary.}
This perturbation record sits downstream of the translation-readout boundary but does not by itself certify that boundary.  In the broader finite data ledger, modeled tAI coupling for the B$^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis was computed for three organisms, with $81$ modeled coupling entries above threshold and a maximum absolute modeled coupling of $3801961.031607896$ under the stated $0.01$ threshold.  That run explicitly remained a modeled-tAI contact rather than a ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation, because $3$ organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim.  A separate cross-organism protein-abundance mediation audit used $12$ organisms and, for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, joined $3739$ proteins; its \textit{E. coli} modeled mediation scalar had total $R^{2}_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, direct $R^{2}_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, and indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$, with mediated fraction $0.612922252793667$.  Those values are useful context for why a synonymous perturbation panel is biologically interesting, but they are not substituted for the Yang fitness statistic.  The Yang record must stand on its own finite rows.

\paragraph{Endogenous-fitness interpretation.}
The finite Yang result is best stated as follows.  A powered audit was run on a large synonymous-edit panel in endogenous \textit{E. coli} genes, with tens of thousands of amino-acid-preserving edits and a controlled B$^{*}_{Q6}$ window basis.  The appropriate claim is that the dataset supplies a direct perturbation contact for synonymous residual geometry at the fitness layer, not merely a codon-count or modeled-adaptation contact.  Since the supplied scalar ledger for this specific run reports row counts, resampling count, and runtime but not the signed effect size, null quantile, $p$-value, held-out gain, or description-length gain, the chapter does not assign a positive mechanistic direction to any individual coordinate.  In particular, it does not promote CUN/UUR directionality, $f3\_stress$, or any single codon-family axis into a general population rule.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No translation mechanism follows from this fitness panel unless a separate translation contact supplies a measured or explicitly bounded $q_i \to T_j$ step.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, protein-stability mechanism, localization mechanism, organism-wide phenotype law, or universal biological principle follows from the synonymous-edit rows alone.  A direct $q_i \to$ fitness association is a perturbation-layer observation; it is not evidence for protein realization unless the relevant translation and protein-abundance rows are joined and the mediation chain is evaluated under matched mRNA abundance, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, M-density, organism, and simpler-codon baselines.  The record is therefore admissible as a finite endogenous-fitness perturbation chapter, while its mechanistic reading remains conditional on additional layer-matched contacts.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a bounded BioReality entry because the supplied Yang run contains a concrete finite panel: $46686$ synonymous edits across $30$ endogenous genes with $200$ resampling units.  Its scientific content is the presence of a large, amino-acid-preserving perturbation surface for B$^{*}_{Q6}$ window residual auditing in \textit{E. coli}.  Its limitation is equally part of the statement: without the missing Yang-specific effect and null-distribution scalars, the prose records the finite contact and the admissible boundary, not a coordinate-specific causal or mechanistic conclusion.
