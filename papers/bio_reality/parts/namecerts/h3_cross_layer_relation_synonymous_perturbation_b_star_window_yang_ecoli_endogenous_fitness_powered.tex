\subsection{Endogenous synonymous-edit fitness surface in \textit{E. coli}}
\label{sec:bioreality-yang-ecoli-endogenous-fitness}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The Yang \textit{E. coli} endogenous-fitness packet is a finite perturbation record for synonymous edits in native coding context.  The registered run `906139f914b0` completed on $30$ genes and $46686$ synonymous edits, with $200$ bounded resampling units and runtime $491.605$ seconds.  The object is not a free-standing protein or phenotype claim.  It is a finite table whose admissible coordinates are synonymous sequence-window residuals, gene membership, and edit-level fitness readouts.  In the notation used here, the observed surface is the finite set
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Omega_{\mathrm{Yang}} &= \{(g,e,y_{g,e}) : g \in G,\ e \in E_g\},\\
  |G| &= 30,\\
  \sum_{g \in G}|E_g| &= 46686,\\
  B &= 200.
\end{aligned}
$$
The coordinate family tested against this surface is the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual window basis: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  These names identify finite residual directions in synonymous codon space; they do not by themselves name a biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Readout discipline.}
The proper readback is edit-level fitness under endogenous bacterial gene context.  The packet is allowed to say that a bounded synonymous-edit matrix was evaluated on $46686$ edits across $30$ genes.  It is not allowed to infer translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or universality from that row count.  The strongest local interpretation is that the residual coordinates have reached an endogenous perturbation assay surface in \textit{E. coli}; any movement to translation or protein output requires a separate readout at that layer.  In particular, a direct residual-to-fitness association is not a translation-mediated claim unless an intermediate translation measurement is attached on matched rows.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-mediated protein abundance.}
The broader $B^*_{Q6}$ protein-abundance mediation audit gives the relevant boundary condition.  In the multi-organism protein-abundance run `42fb6427cd4a`, the same nine residual coordinates were evaluated with modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb abundance after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline controls.  For \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $3739$ proteins were joined, the residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3716$, and the decomposition reported
$$
\begin{aligned}
  R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
  R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
  R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.06599656708487024,\\
  M^{QTP} &= 0.612922252793667.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers show why the Yang endogenous-fitness surface must be written with a layer boundary.  The protein-abundance audit uses modeled tAI as $T$ and protein abundance as $P$; the Yang run supplies synonymous edits and fitness measurements, not a matched $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ mediation chain.  Therefore the Yang chapter may be placed next to translation-mediated records, but it cannot inherit their protein-abundance conclusion.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
A separate modeled-tAI survival audit computed the same residual basis for three organisms and reported $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That audit also recorded that the three-organism surface is underpowered for a powered survival claim: $3$ organisms are insufficient for the requested powered survival criterion, which requires at least $40$ matched species.  The Yang record does not repair that limitation.  Its $30$ genes and $46686$ edits are an endogenous perturbation surface, while the modeled-tAI audit is an organism-level translation-weight surface.  The two are compatible finite contacts, but they are not the same measurement.

\paragraph{Perturbation comparators.}
Other synonymous-perturbation audits delimit the Yang result.  The \textit{E. coli} mRFP protein-output run `a18abe395a72` used $1459$ variants and reported a control-only $R^2$ of $0.12648386762689323$, a full $R^2$ of $0.1798829276988051$, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null $p$ values both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  The corresponding $5'$ boundary audit on the same variant count reported $b$-downstream $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$, $b$-downstream null95 $0.5254770343527547$, and $p_{\mathrm{block}}=0.1691542288557214$, with sign stability false.  A distributed recoding audit on \textit{E. coli} reported $47$ AmpR rows, $53$ GmR rows, $19$ multi-strata $D$ classes, AmpR independent $p=1.0$, and AmpR slope $0.0$.  These comparators show that synonymous perturbation is not a single monotone axis: one bacterial reporter surface supported a protein-output increment, one positional boundary did not survive the downstream test, and one distributed recoding surface was null on the AmpR statistic.  The Yang endogenous-fitness surface must therefore be stated as its own finite edit panel rather than as a universal law about synonymous changes.

\paragraph{Local claim.}
The chapter-level claim is the following bounded assertion.  The Yang run supplies a completed endogenous \textit{E. coli} synonymous-edit fitness table with $30$ genes, $46686$ edits, and $200$ resampling units.  It is admissible evidence that the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-window basis can be evaluated against endogenous fitness perturbations at bacterial gene scale.  It is not, by itself, evidence for translation survival, protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organism-wide adaptation, or universality.  Any such stronger statement would require a joined finite panel containing the same edit or gene rows, a translation readout such as ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, and, for protein-output language, matched protein abundance.  Without that joined panel, the Yang record remains an endogenous-fitness perturbation contact with explicit no-promotion scope.
