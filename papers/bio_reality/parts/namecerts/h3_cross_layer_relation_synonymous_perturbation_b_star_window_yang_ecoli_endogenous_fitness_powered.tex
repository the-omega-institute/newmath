\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record isolates the endogenous \emph{Escherichia coli} synonymous-edit fitness contact as a finite perturbation table rather than as a general claim about codon usage.  The evaluated surface contains $30$ genes and $46686$ synonymous edits, with $200$ null replicates recorded for the powered comparison.  The run identifier is \texttt{906139f914b0}, and the registered computation completed in $491.605$ seconds.  The row type is therefore a bounded table of triples consisting of a synonymous-edit window, its projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and the observed endogenous-fitness readout for that edited gene context.  The admissible interpretation is local: the table can test whether a residual window coordinate improves a fitness readout under the registered controls and null scheme.  It does not, by itself, identify translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Coordinate basis and readout gate.}
The coordinate side is the same nine-dimensional residual basis used throughout the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ finite-row audits: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$.  A separate modeled-tAI audit computed these residuals on $61$ sense-codon coordinates across $3$ organisms, with $81$ entries above the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$ and maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That upstream calculation supplies only a bounded codon-adaptation readout: its own record states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival requiring at least $40$ matched species.  Consequently, the Yang endogenous-fitness table is not allowed to treat modeled tAI or residual geometry as an observed ribosome-contact measurement.  Any positive fitness association must remain an edit-window association unless a same-row translation readout is attached.

\paragraph{Registered finite inequalities.}
Let $E$ be the set of synonymous-edit rows and let $G$ be the set of genes in the Yang endogenous-fitness table.  The finite dimensions recorded by the run are
$$
\begin{aligned}
|G| &= 30,\\
|E| &= 46686,\\
B_{\mathrm{null}} &= 200,\\
T_{\mathrm{run}} &= 491.605\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For an edit row $e \in E$, write $Q(e)$ for the projected residual-window vector and $F(e)$ for the endogenous-fitness readout.  The admissible test form is a controlled finite comparison
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{Q \to F}
  &= \mathrm{Score}\bigl(F \mid Z, Q\bigr)-\mathrm{Score}\bigl(F \mid Z\bigr),\\
\Delta_{Q \to F}^{(b)}
  &= \mathrm{Score}\bigl(F \mid Z, Q^{(b)}\bigr)-\mathrm{Score}\bigl(F \mid Z\bigr),\qquad 1 \leq b \leq 200,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $Z$ denotes the local control surface: amino-acid identity, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and simpler codon-window baselines when present.  The finite-row claim is not that $\Delta_{Q \to F}$ is a mechanism.  It is only that the Yang panel provides enough rows to compare the observed edit-window residual signal with $200$ matched null surfaces on $46686$ edits.

In the multi-organism protein-abundance mediation audit, the \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 joined panel used $3739$ proteins, had residual degrees of freedom $3716$ after $23$ control-rank columns, and produced a modeled-tAI mediation fraction $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ with total $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, direct conditioned $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, and indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$.  Those numbers show that a translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition can be computed when a matched protein table exists, but they are not imported as evidence for the Yang fitness table.  The Yang finite surface lacks the same-row $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ chain required for protein-realization language.  The local record therefore remains a synonymous-edit fitness window, not a translation-mediated protein-abundance proof.

\paragraph{Higher-layer refusal.}
The Yang table concerns fitness of endogenous synonymous edits, so phenotype language is tempting.  The chapter refuses that promotion.  A measured fitness score is a downstream biological readout, but the finite row facts supplied here do not include a separate ribosome-profiling matrix, matched mRNA denominator, matched protein abundance for the edited rows, folding assay, physical assay, functional rescue, or mechanism-specific perturbation.  The structural-order audit found that $f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}$ was the only significant fold-level coordinate, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$; that is a structural-order residual result, not a mechanism for the Yang edits.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit was explicitly negative for the strong imprint claim, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$.  The dynamic mass-balance audit was also negative for the registered residual increment, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$ on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals.  These finite checks keep the Yang record in its correct layer: an endogenous synonymous-edit fitness window over $46686$ edits and $30$ genes.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The admissible conclusion is that the Yang \emph{E. coli} endogenous panel is a powered finite surface for asking whether $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window residuals carry fitness information under the registered null scheme.  Its concrete scale is $46686$ edits, $30$ genes, and $200$ null replicates.  The table may support a bounded edit-window fitness association when the registered statistic beats its nulls, but no translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, selection law, or universality follows from that association alone.  To cross into those layers, the same edited rows would need an added contact: ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency for translation, matched protein abundance for protein output, structure or physical assays for admissibility, and perturbation-rescue or biochemical assays for mechanism and function.  Without those contacts, the chapter records a finite endogenous-fitness perturbation surface and a strict no-promotion boundary.
