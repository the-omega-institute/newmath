\subsection{Endogenous synonymous edits in the Yang \textit{E. coli} fitness window}
\label{subsec:bioreality-yang-ecoli-endogenous-fitness-window}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record concerns the finite surface obtained by joining synonymous edit rows in endogenous \textit{E. coli} coding sequence to the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ window readout.  The certified run for \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.synonymous\_perturbation.b\_star\_window\_yang\_ecoli\_endogenous\_fitness\_powered} used $30$ genes and $46686$ synonymous edits, with $B=200$ resampling units and a recorded runtime of $491.605$ seconds.  Thus the carrier is not a population-level law and not a whole-genome assertion; it is a finite perturbation matrix whose rows are endogenous synonymous edits and whose columns are the local residual coordinates available to the $B^*_{Q6}$ window model.  The intended readout is fitness of the edited endogenous sequence context, not protein abundance, ribosome occupancy, folding state, molecular function, or organism-wide adaptation.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The admissible coordinate list remains the same bounded residual basis used by the surrounding $Q6$ program:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
In this chapter the Yang finite row is treated as an endogenous-edit fitness contact only after the edit surface is kept separate from translation and protein-output contacts.  A residual coordinate $q_i\in Q$ may enter the local regression or resampling audit as a controlled predictor of the measured edit phenotype, but it is not allowed to become a translation claim merely because the codon change is synonymous.  Amino-acid sequence is unchanged by construction, while organism identity, GC3, CDS length, codon-count baseline, and the local $M$-density baseline remain controls or background strata rather than promoted biological coordinates.

\paragraph{Relation tested.}
The local relation is the finite map
$$
\begin{aligned}
(q_i,e_r,y_r)\quad \text{with}\quad q_i\in Q,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $e_r$ is a synonymous edit row among the $46686$ rows and $y_r$ is the observed endogenous fitness readout for that row.  The relevant question is whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ window contributes reproducible finite-row information about the Yang fitness readout after the simpler sequence baselines have had their chance to absorb the signal.  The run summary supplies the scale of the test, not a right to infer mechanism: $30$ genes is a narrow endogenous panel, and the row count is edit-rich but gene-limited.  Consequently, any positive local statistic would be a perturbation-window association inside this finite assay; any negative or unstable statistic would leave the coordinate at codon-window residual status for this dataset.

\paragraph{Mediation guardrail.}
The stronger protein-mediated $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ route is not supplied by the Yang finite row itself.  For comparison, the separate cross-organism protein-abundance mediation audit, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.translation\_mediated\_protein\_abundance.b\_star\_q6\_mqtp\_powered}, reports for \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655 a joined protein set of $3739$ proteins, $q$-coordinate count $9$, residual degrees of freedom $3716$, $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$.  In that separate surface the total residual-to-protein coefficient was recorded as $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, the direct coefficient after conditioning on modeled translation as $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, and the indirect difference as $0.06599656708487024$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ inside the unit interval.  Those numbers demonstrate how a protein-abundance mediation statement must be numerically witnessed when it is claimed; they do not turn the Yang fitness assay into a protein-abundance assay.

\paragraph{Perturbation calibration.}
A second \textit{E. coli} perturbation contact, \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.synonymous\_perturbation.b\_star\_window\_nieuwkoop\_ecoli\_mrfp\_protein\_output\_powered}, used $1459$ variants with $B=200$ and reported $R^2=0.12648386762689323$ for the control layer, $R^2=0.1798829276988051$ after adding the orthogonal $B$-window component, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matched}}=0.004975124378109453$, and $p_{\mathrm{within}}=0.004975124378109453$.  That protein-output assay is useful as a scale check for what a stronger perturbation result looks like: it contains explicit effect-size, description-length, and null-class evidence.  The Yang row currently contributes the larger endogenous edit count and gene panel size, but the finite summary provided here does not include its own $\Delta R^2$, description-length gain, fitted coefficient list, null quantiles, or $p$-values.  Therefore the chapter records the Yang contact as a finite endogenous fitness surface and not as a fully quantified protein-output analogue.

\paragraph{Axis context.}
The residual-basis audit \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.structural\_order\_axis\_identity.b\_star\_q6\_powered} identified $f_3$-stress as the only coordinate passing its fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$.  The remaining coordinate increments were smaller or unstable in that separate structural-order readout, for example $0.0020139270616115154$ for $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ with $p=0.142578125$, $0.0013067592718559024$ for $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $-0.00045340330387383447$ for $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ with $p=0.529296875$.  This axis information justifies treating the Yang edit window as a coordinate-resolved surface rather than as a one-dimensional codon-bias claim, but it does not import structural-order evidence into the endogenous-fitness assay.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite Yang record is ready as a bounded BioReality chapter because the finite-row size is explicit: $46686$ synonymous edits across $30$ endogenous \textit{E. coli} genes with $B=200$.  Its present interpretation is deliberately narrow.  It supports a chapter about a controlled endogenous synonymous-edit fitness window attached to the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis.  It does not, by itself, establish translation realization, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, evolutionary mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universality.  Any such promotion would require a separate layer-matched contact: a same-row translation readout for $Q_i\to T_j$, a matched protein measurement for $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$, and independent assays for folding, physical state, function, mechanism, or phenotype.
