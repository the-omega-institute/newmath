\subsection{Yang Escherichia coli Endogenous Synonymous-Fitness Window}
\label{sec:bioreality-yang-ecoli-endogenous-fitness-window}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
This record fixes a finite endogenous synonymous-edit surface in \emph{Escherichia coli}: the registered run \texttt{906139f914b0} evaluated $46686$ synonymous edits distributed across $30$ genes, with $B=200$ as the finite resampling scale and a recorded runtime of $491.605$ seconds. The biological object is not an unconstrained codon-usage association. It is a bounded table whose rows are synonymous coding edits, whose projected coordinates are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual directions used throughout the surrounding audit, and whose readout is an endogenous fitness measurement attached to the edited coding sequence.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The admissible coordinates are the residual directions
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
They are residual coordinates after the amino-acid quotient, not amino-acid substitutions and not direct molecular mechanisms. The Yang panel therefore asks a sharply limited question: after the synonymous edit is projected into the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window, does the finite edit table retain a fitness-aligned signal that is not already explained by the simpler coding baselines? The local finite count answers only that the powered audit surface exists at $46686$ rows over $30$ genes and was executed with $200$ resampling draws; it does not by itself certify translation, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, or a universal evolutionary rule.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-gated protein readbacks.}
The reason this row table is cross-layer rather than purely codon-topological is that it is compared against the previously bounded translation and protein-abundance gates. In the modeled translation-survival audit, the codon-count space contained $61$ sense codons, $9$ projected residual coordinates, $3$ organisms, and $81$ modeled tAI entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold, but the same audit explicitly recorded that $3$ organisms are insufficient for a powered survival claim. In the larger translation-mediated protein-abundance matrix, the \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 row joined $3739$ proteins, had residual degrees of freedom $3716$ after $23$ effective control rank, and produced a bounded mediation fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Ecoli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.06599656708487024.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers are used here only as boundary context: they show why a direct synonymous-edit fitness association is not automatically a protein-realization claim. A positive edit-fitness pattern would have to pass through a same-row translation readout before it could be promoted to a protein-output interpretation.

\paragraph{Endogenous-fitness readback.}
The Yang endogenous panel is more stringent than a synthetic reporter-only association because it records edits inside native genes. The finite surface contains $30$ genes rather than one construct, and $46686$ synonymous edits rather than a small hand-picked codon set. This makes the panel suitable for a window-level perturbation audit: the coordinate map can ask whether the direction of an edit in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ basis carries predictive content for fitness after gene identity, amino-acid sequence preservation, GC3, coding length, and simpler codon-density baselines are kept in the control layer. The row count is large enough for the registered $B=200$ resampling design to be meaningful, but the chapter does not treat row count as mechanism. It treats row count as finite statistical support for running the perturbation test.

\paragraph{Comparison with adjacent \emph{E. coli} perturbation contacts.}
Two neighboring \emph{E. coli} perturbation records delimit what this chapter may say. In the Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output panel, $1459$ variants yielded $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched-null and within-null tail probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$; that result concerns protein output in a reporter setting, not endogenous fitness. In the distributed recoding panel, the finite table split into $47$ AmpR rows and $53$ GmR rows, with $19$ multi-strata $D$ bins, an AmpR slope of $0.0$, and an AmpR independent probability of $1.0$; that result records a null boundary for a different recoding design. The Yang table is therefore neither replaced by reporter protein output nor by distributed-recoding null behavior. It contributes a separate finite endogenous-fitness surface with its own $46686$ edited rows.

\paragraph{Local mathematical form.}
Let $E$ be the finite set of Yang synonymous edits, with $|E|=46686$, and let $G$ be the finite gene set, with $|G|=30$. For each edit $e\in E$, write $q(e)\in\mathbb{R}^9$ for the projected $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate and $y(e)$ for the observed endogenous fitness readout. The audit surface is the finite relation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{Y}_{\mathrm{Yang}}=\{(e,g(e),q(e),y(e)) : e\in E\},\qquad |E|=46686,\quad |G|=30.
\end{aligned}
$$
The admissible fitted question is whether $q(e)$ improves a controlled fitness model over the baseline covariates on this finite table. The inadmissible question is whether the same improvement, without additional rows, proves translation dynamics, protein abundance, folding, physical feasibility, function, mechanism, adaptation, or universality.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The chapter closes at the synonymous-perturbation fitness layer. A direct $Q_i\to$ fitness association in this table is not a $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ mediation chain. It is not ribosome occupancy, not translation efficiency, not mature protein abundance, not folded structure, not physical admissibility, not molecular function, and not a population law. Any such promotion would require a separate same-row contact: a translation readout such as ribosome profiling or bounded TE for $T_j$, matched mRNA abundance for transcription control, and matched protein or higher-layer measurements for $P_k$ or beyond. Without that additional table, the correct readback is finite and local: the Yang \emph{E. coli} endogenous dataset supplies a powered synonymous-edit fitness surface over $46686$ rows and $30$ genes, evaluated with $B=200$, suitable for testing whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual window has controlled perturbation signal in endogenous fitness.
