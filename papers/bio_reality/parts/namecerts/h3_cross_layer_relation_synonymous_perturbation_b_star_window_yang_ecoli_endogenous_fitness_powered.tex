\subsection{Endogenous synonymous edits in \textit{E. coli} fitness rows}
\label{sec:yang-ecoli-endogenous-fitness-powered}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The Yang \textit{E. coli} endogenous-fitness packet is a finite perturbation surface whose rows are synonymous edits placed in native coding sequence rather than in a reporter cassette.  The registered run \texttt{906139f914b0} contains $46686$ synonymous edits across $30$ genes, with a bounded randomization budget $B=200$ and runtime $491.605$ seconds.  The row object is therefore not a statement about the genetic code as a whole; it is a finite matrix of edited coding contexts, observed fitness readouts, and controlled residual coordinates.  In the notation of this chapter, each edited row contributes an observed tuple
$$
\begin{aligned}
  e_\ell &= (g_\ell, x_\ell, q_\ell, y_\ell),\\
  g_\ell &\in \{1,\ldots,30\},\\
  \ell &\in \{1,\ldots,46686\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $g_\ell$ is the gene block, $x_\ell$ is the local synonymous edit context, $q_\ell$ is the projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ window residual vector, and $y_\ell$ is the endogenous fitness measurement attached to that edit.  The admissible claim is only that this finite table is large enough to test whether the residual coordinates retain predictive contact with fitness after the design controls are applied.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and the perturbation readout.}
The relevant coordinates are the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual axes used throughout the cross-layer packet: $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  In this chapter they are not interpreted as molecular mechanisms.  They are covariates whose values are recomputed on synonymous edit windows and then tested against a finite endogenous-fitness response.  A valid row-level reading has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
  y_\ell &= F(Z_\ell, q_\ell) + \varepsilon_\ell,\\
  Z_\ell &= \text{gene, amino-acid-preserving context, local composition, and simpler codon baselines},\\
  q_\ell &= (q_{\ell,1},\ldots,q_{\ell,9}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The chapter does not infer that any coordinate is translated, folded, selected, or mechanistically realized merely because it appears in this regression surface.  The endogenous-fitness layer is a perturbation phenotype layer, and the route into it must pass through the observed edit table itself.

\paragraph{Power and boundedness.}
The finite size of the experiment matters.  With $46686$ synonymous edits distributed across $30$ genes, the design has many edit rows but a small number of gene blocks.  Gene-block dependence is therefore a central limitation: row count alone cannot be read as independent biological replication.  The bounded resampling budget $B=200$ is sufficient for a registered finite audit but does not turn a row-level association into a universal law.  The powered status of the packet means that the registered computation was run to completion on the stated finite surface; it does not mean that every biological layer downstream of fitness has been measured.

\paragraph{Relation to the \textit{E. coli} reporter boundary.}
The endogenous-fitness packet should be kept distinct from the \textit{E. coli} mRFP protein-output packet.  In the mRFP perturbation run, $1459$ variants gave a bounded positive protein-output increment with $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, $p_{matched}=0.004975124378109453$, and $p_{within}=0.004975124378109453$, with the real-CAI control not absorbing the residual signal.  The 5-prime boundary audit on the same reporter family gave $R^2=0.06748192482378434$ at the first step, $R^2=0.6060702157093043$ after the second step, and $R^2=0.6049438075310105$ after adding the downstream residual term, with downstream $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$ and block-null $p=0.1691542288557214$.  These numbers show that a protein-output reporter can have a localized residual signal while a downstream boundary term remains unsupported.  They do not prove the endogenous-fitness Yang surface.  They only supply a comparison class for how a same-organism synonymous perturbation packet is allowed to make a bounded claim.

\paragraph{Mediation boundary.}
A direct residual-to-fitness association in the Yang table is not a translation claim.  It is also not a protein-abundance claim.  The multi-organism protein-abundance mediation audit used $12$ organisms and joined, for example, $3739$ \textit{E. coli} K-12 proteins, obtaining for that organism a total residual-to-protein association $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, a direct association after modeled translation conditioning $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, and an indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$ with mediated fraction $0.612922252793667$.  Those are modeled tAI-conditioned protein-abundance quantities.  The Yang endogenous-fitness packet contains a different endpoint.  Unless the same edited rows are joined to a translation readout $T$ and then to a downstream protein or phenotype readout $P$, the only admissible form is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  Q &\longrightarrow Y_{fitness},\\
  Q &\not\Rightarrow T,\\
  Q &\not\Rightarrow P,\\
  Q &\not\Rightarrow \text{mechanism}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the chapter records a finite perturbation test at the fitness layer, not a completed chain $Q\to T\to P$.

\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
The row surface does not by itself establish translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, or universality.  A translation statement would require a separate same-row or matched-row translation contact, such as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or a carefully bounded modeled translation readout.  A protein statement would require matched abundance or output data.  A mechanism statement would require perturbation structure sufficient to distinguish the proposed route from gene identity, codon-count baselines, mRNA effects, and local edit context.  A universal statement would require cross-organism and cross-design transfer beyond the $30$ genes and $46686$ edits registered here.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The finite evidence supports inclusion of this packet as a bounded endogenous synonymous-perturbation chapter.  The concrete claim is that a completed run exists on $46686$ synonymous edits in $30$ \textit{E. coli} genes with $B=200$, so the chapter has enough finite-row contact to discuss the perturbation surface and its admissible readback.  The chapter must remain conservative about layer promotion: if later analysis reports a positive residual-fitness increment, that increment is still a fitness-layer association unless the same coordinate first survives the appropriate translation or protein-contact test.  If the finite Yang table yields no positive controlled improvement after gene and baseline controls, the correct reading is not biological irrelevance of $B^{*}_{Q6}$, but failure of this particular endogenous-fitness surface to promote the residual coordinates beyond bounded perturbation status.
