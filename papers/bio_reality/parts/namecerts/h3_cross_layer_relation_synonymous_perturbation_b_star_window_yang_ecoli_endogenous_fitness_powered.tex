\subsection{Yang endogenous \textit{E. coli} synonymous-edit fitness window}
\label{sec:bioreality-yang-ecoli-endogenous-fitness-window}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns an endogenous \textit{Escherichia coli} synonymous-edit panel in which the rows are not abstract codon geometries but finite mutation observations attached to native genes.  The registered run for the claim used $30$ genes and $46686$ synonymous edits, with $B=200$ null or resampling units and runtime $491.605$ seconds.  The finite table is therefore read as a perturbation surface
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \Omega_{\mathrm{Yang}}=
  \{(g,e,q,w,y): g \in G_{30},\ e \in E_{46686},\ q \in Q_{6}^{*},\ w \in W,\ y \in Y_{\mathrm{fit}}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $g$ indexes the endogenous gene, $e$ indexes a synonymous edit in that gene, $q$ is the projected $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate evaluated on the edited coding window, $w$ records the window and baseline covariates, and $y$ is the observed fitness readout attached to the edit.  The point of the surface is not that codon residuals become biological causes by naming them.  The point is narrower: a large finite edit panel can ask whether a residual coordinate retains predictive contact with an endogenous fitness readout after the ordinary coding-sequence controls have first been placed in the design.

\paragraph{Coordinates and controls.}
The residual coordinate list used by the surrounding $Q6$ program is the nine-coordinate basis
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_{6}^{*}=\{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
In the cross-layer protein-abundance run using the same coordinate family, the matched organism panel contained $12$ organisms and joined $3739$ \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 proteins to real CDS codon counts and PAXdb abundance rows.  That run estimated, for \textit{E. coli}, a total residual-to-protein abundance coefficient $c=0.10767526677985896$, a translation-conditioned direct coefficient $c'=0.04167869969498872$, an indirect difference $c-c'=0.06599656708487024$, and a mediated fraction $0.612922252793667$ under amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls.  These numbers are not imported here as a fitness result.  They only justify treating the Yang endogenous edit panel as a downstream perturbation question after the $Q6$ translation and abundance route has already been quantified elsewhere on finite matched rows.

\paragraph{Yang readback.}
For the present chapter, the direct finite fact is the Yang run itself:
$$
\begin{aligned}
  |G| &= 30,\\
  |E| &= 46686,\\
  B &= 200,\\
  t_{\mathrm{run}} &= 491.605\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readback is therefore an endogenous-fitness audit of a $B^{*}_{Q6}$ window design, not a general statement about all synonymous mutations.  Each row asks whether a synonymous edit perturbs a controlled coding window in a way that is visible in the fitness measurement.  Because the row count is large but the gene count is only $30$, the correct interpretation is gene-local and panel-local: the experiment has many synonymous edits, but those edits are concentrated in a finite endogenous gene set.  The scientific claim is consequently about this finite panel and its registered controls, not about a population law across all bacteria.

\paragraph{Relation to translation mediation.}
The broader $Q6$ evidence ledger contains a modeled translation survival contact and a protein-abundance mediation contact.  The modeled-tAI survival run computed the same nine residual coordinates and found $81$ bounded coupling entries above threshold $0.01$ across $3$ organisms, with maximum absolute modeled coupling $3801961.031607896$.  That result was explicitly recorded as underpowered for a powered survival claim because $3$ organisms are below the declared $40$-species boundary.  Separately, the $12$-organism protein-abundance mediation run gave positive mediated fractions in unit interval for all organisms, including \textit{E. coli}, but it used modeled per-protein tAI rather than measured ribosome profiling.  The Yang endogenous fitness panel should therefore be placed after, not before, this boundary: it is a perturbation readout that can be compared with the same residual-window coordinates, while the translation step remains a required separate contact whenever a mechanistic translation statement is desired.

\paragraph{Comparison with nearby \textit{E. coli} perturbation rows.}
Two nearby finite perturbation records show why the Yang panel should be written conservatively.  The Nieuwkoop \textit{E. coli} mRFP protein-output row used $1459$ variants, $B=200$, and reported $\Delta R^{2}=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, with matched and within-null $p$-values both $0.004975124378109453$.  The Chen distributed recoding row used $47$ AmpR variants and $53$ GmR variants, but its reported AmpR independent $p$-value was $1.0$ with slope $0.0$.  Against that background, the Yang panel supplies scale and endogenous-fitness contact, while the present finite row does not itself expose a signed coefficient, $\Delta R^{2}$, description-length gain, or per-coordinate $p$-value.  The chapter can therefore report the existence and size of the powered endogenous-edit audit, but it must not invent an effect direction.

\paragraph{Admissible statement.}
The admissible statement is that a registered endogenous \textit{E. coli} synonymous-edit fitness audit was run on $46686$ edits across $30$ genes with $B=200$, using the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ window route as the coordinate family.  This supports a finite perturbation contact for the fitness layer in the sense of completed computation and row availability.  It does not, by itself, prove that a particular residual coordinate mediates fitness through translation, protein abundance, folding, or molecular function.  A stronger statement would require the row-level statistic identifying which coordinate survived, the corresponding fitness effect size, the null distribution, and a demonstrated attenuation or mediation chain through a measured translation readout.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
The boundary is local and strict.  A synonymous residual coordinate may be used as a controlled finite predictor on this panel, but no translation realization follows unless a measured or explicitly bounded translation contact is joined to the same rows.  No protein-abundance claim follows unless matched protein-abundance rows are joined and the direct residual-to-protein route is separated from the translation route.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal mechanism, selection law, or universality follows from the Yang fitness rows alone.  The run is a finite endogenous perturbation audit, and its current public numbers certify panel scale and computation, not an unrestricted biological mechanism.
