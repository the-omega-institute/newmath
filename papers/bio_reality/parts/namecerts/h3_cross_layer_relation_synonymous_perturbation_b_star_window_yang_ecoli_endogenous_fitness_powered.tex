\subsection{Endogenous synonymous fitness windows in \textit{E. coli}}
\label{sec:bioreality-yang-ecoli-endogenous-fitness}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns an endogenous \textit{E. coli} synonymous-perturbation surface in which the rows are edited coding-sequence windows, the first coordinate block is the nine-dimensional $B^*_Q6$ synonymous residual basis, and the readout is a measured fitness effect attached to the edited endogenous gene context.  The finite audit run for this packet used $30$ genes and $46686$ synonymous edits, with $B=200$ resampling or null units recorded for the powered statistic and a runtime of $491.605$ seconds.  The object is therefore not a genome-wide law and not a codon-table identity: it is a finite edited-window panel in which residual coordinates are asked whether they add predictive information for endogenous fitness after the declared sequence controls.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The admissible residual coordinates are the same nine coordinates used by the surrounding $B^*_Q6$ packet:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR,\ Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG,\\
&Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY,\ Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG,\ Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY,\ f3\_stress\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A row may be read as a synonymous-perturbation row only after amino-acid sequence has been held fixed by construction and after the remaining baseline quantities, including organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline induced by the coordinate support, are kept as controls rather than elevated to new residual coordinates.  In this chapter the endogenous fitness endpoint is downstream of the translation layer, so a direct residual-to-fitness association is not itself a translation claim.  It is a perturbation association on edited endogenous rows, with translation interpretation allowed only where a separate translation readout is present.

\paragraph{Observed finite counts.}
The Yang \textit{E. coli} run contributes the following finite row statement.  The tested edited panel has
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{genes} &= 30,\\
 n_{synonymous\ edits} &= 46686,\\
 B &= 200,\\
 t_{runtime} &= 491.605\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers are the chapter-level evidence for a powered endogenous synonymous-edit surface.  They do not by themselves report a list of per-coordinate slopes, $R^2$ increments, $p$-values, or signed lambda weights for this exact Yang panel; the finite contact supplied here is the row count, gene count, null-count scale, and completed-run status.  Consequently the chapter states a bounded fitness-window result rather than a mechanistic decomposition of the Yang panel.

\paragraph{Translation-contact context.}
The reason the Yang fitness packet is kept separate from translation realization is visible from the matched $B^*_Q6$ translation-protein audit.  In the \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 protein-abundance contact, $3739$ CDS/protein rows were joined, all $3739$ joined proteins were used, and the tRNA organism was recorded as \textit{Escherichia coli}.  The per-protein tAI computation used $90$ total all-tRNA copies and filled $1$ zero raw wobble-weight value by the geometric mean rule.  After residualization against the declared controls, the \textit{E. coli} rank diagnostics were
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}(Z) &= 23,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X) &= 9,\\
\operatorname{rank}(T) &= 1,\\
\operatorname{rank}(X,T) &= 10,\\
\operatorname{df}_{residual} &= 3716.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the same \textit{E. coli} contact, the total residual-to-protein association was $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$, the direct residual-to-protein association after conditioning on modeled tAI was $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$, and the mediated difference was $0.06599656708487024$, giving a statistical mediation fraction $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  These values show why a finite $Q \to T \to P$ language is available in a separate proteomics surface, but they do not convert the Yang endogenous fitness rows into a translation, protein-abundance, or mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Modeled-readout boundary.}
A broader modeled-tAI survival run computed the same nine residual coordinates across $3$ organisms.  Its modeled tAI matrix had $81$ entries above the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$ and the same maximum on the diagonal.  The run explicitly recorded that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival when $40$ or more matched species are required.  Thus the modeled tAI contact is a bounded codon-adaptation readout, not an observed ribosome-profiling measurement and not a license to treat Yang endogenous fitness effects as translation-realization evidence.

\paragraph{Perturbation comparison inside \textit{E. coli}.}
The nearest \textit{E. coli} synonymous-output comparison is the mRFP protein-output perturbation run.  That separate panel had $1459$ variants, $B=200$, a controls-only value $R^2=0.12648386762689323$, a controls-plus-$B^*_Q6$ value $R^2=0.1798829276988051$, and an increment $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$.  Its description-length increment was $19.089327473282296$ bits, with matched and within-panel probabilities both $0.004975124378109453$.  The real-CAI audit reported proxy correlation $0.17884920449487354$, real-CAI robustness true, and real-CAI absorption false.  This comparison supports the local plausibility of a synonymous-window residual signal in \textit{E. coli}, while keeping the Yang endogenous fitness panel as its own finite edit surface with its own gene and edit counts.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The correct readback is a controlled finite matrix of synonymous edits, residual-window coordinates, and endogenous fitness values.  In symbols, the row object is only
$$
\begin{aligned}
(e_{a}, g_{b}) \longmapsto \bigl(Q(e_a,g_b), Z(e_a,g_b), F(e_a,g_b)\bigr),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $e_a$ is a synonymous edit, $g_b$ is one of the $30$ endogenous genes, $Q$ is the nine-coordinate residual block, $Z$ is the control block, and $F$ is the measured fitness endpoint.  If a fitted statistic on this surface improves against its null and baseline controls, the supported statement is that the $B^*_Q6$ window block carries finite predictive information for this endogenous synonymous fitness panel.  If the fitted improvements are nonpositive or fail the null comparison, the packet remains at edited-window residual status.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organism-level mechanism, selection law, or universality follows from the Yang finite-row counts alone.  A translation statement would require a matched translation contact such as ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, or an explicitly bounded tAI/stAI readout for the same relevant rows.  A protein statement would require matched protein-abundance data.  A structure, function, physical-admissibility, or phenotype statement would require a separate layer-specific measurement.  The present chapter therefore records a powered endogenous synonymous fitness-window surface and its finite audit counts, while refusing to promote the residual coordinate geometry beyond the measurements actually attached to this packet.
