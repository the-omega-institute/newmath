\subsection{Endogenous synonymous-fitness rows in \textit{E. coli}}

\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite perturbation surface.}
The Yang \textit{E. coli} endogenous-fitness packet is a finite perturbation record for synonymous edits inside native coding contexts.  Its registered run, \texttt{906139f914b0}, contains $46686$ synonymous edits distributed across $30$ genes, with $200$ realized null draws and a runtime of $491.605$ seconds.  The row is therefore large at the edit level but narrow at the gene level.  The admissible carrier is the finite table whose rows are edited coding-sequence windows, whose protected background columns include amino-acid identity, organism, codon-position context, GC3, CDS length, and the $B^*_{Q6}$ support-density baseline, and whose response column is the measured endogenous fitness readout.  The table may be used to ask whether a synonymous window coordinate has predictive contact with this one fitness surface; it may not be used as if it had already measured translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, molecular activity, or organism-wide evolutionary selection.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The coordinates available to the broader $B^*_{Q6}$ family are the nine residual directions
$$
\begin{aligned}
&K\_AAA,\ Arg\_AGR,\ Ile\_AUA,\ Leu\_{CUN\,vs\,UUR},\ Leu\_{UUA\,vs\,UUG},\\
&Ser\_{UCR\,vs\,AGY},\ Ser\_{UCA\,vs\,UCG},\ Thr\_{ACR\,vs\,ACY},\ f3\_stress .
\end{aligned}
$$
For the Yang packet these directions are not promoted into independent biological causes.  They are finite regressors attached to synonymous edits after the amino-acid-preserving quotient has already removed the obvious coding consequence.  The natural finite question is whether adding the windowed $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block to simpler codon and sequence controls improves the prediction of the measured fitness value.  Because the row supplied for this run records only $n_{edits}=46686$, $n_{genes}=30$, $B=200$, and runtime, the chapter can certify the scale and admissible boundary of the packet, but it cannot report a Yang-specific slope, held-out $\Delta R^2$, description-length gain, or permutation $p$-value.

\paragraph{Relation to translation-layer evidence.}
The same residual family has a separate modeled translation-readout surface in which three organisms were evaluated using GtRNAdb copy numbers and dos Reis wobble weights.  That run computed all nine residual coordinates, used a coupling threshold of $0.01$, and found $81$ modeled entries above threshold with maximum absolute coupling $3801961.031607896$.  It also explicitly marked the organism count as $3$, below the powered-survival requirement of at least $40$ matched species.  This matters for the Yang endogenous-fitness packet because a fitness association is not a translation measurement.  Even if a synonymous edit affects growth, the route from codon residual to translation readout must be supplied by a matched translation contact before the claim may be restated as translation survival.

\paragraph{Protein-mediated comparison surface.}
A broader protein-abundance mediation run gives a useful calibration for what a completed $Q \to T \to P$ surface looks like.  In that run, the \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 join used $3739$ proteins, a per-protein modeled tAI readout, and PAXdb protein abundance.  After residualizing against $24$ control columns with rank $23$, the \textit{E. coli} row had residual degrees of freedom $3716$, $X$-to-$T$ explained energy $0.256128714706293$, $X$-to-$Y$ explained energy $432.89050065533684$, and a mediated fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{Ecoli} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
c &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
c' &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
c-c' &= 0.06599656708487024 .
\end{aligned}
$$
Those numbers are not Yang fitness effects.  They show the minimum shape required for a mediated protein-abundance statement: matched coding rows, a declared translation readout, matched protein abundance, rank diagnostics, and an explicit decomposition.  The Yang row does not contain the corresponding $T$ or $P$ columns, so the endogenous-fitness packet stays at the perturbation-fitness boundary.

\paragraph{E. coli perturbation context.}
The closest \textit{E. coli} synonymous protein-output perturbation packet is the Nieuwkoop mRFP run, which used $1459$ variants and $200$ null draws.  It reported a control-only $R^2$ of $0.12648386762689323$, a full $R^2$ of $0.1798829276988051$, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null probabilities both equal to $0.004975124378109453$.  A separate 5-prime boundary audit on the same variant count found that the downstream block had $\Delta R^2=-0.001126409133973234$, $\Delta LL=-2.082967365015851$, a block probability $0.1691542288557214$, and a non-stable sign call.  These two \textit{E. coli} controls show why the Yang packet cannot be summarized by organism name alone: an \textit{E. coli} synonymous edit can have a detectable protein-output association in one engineered reporter setting and still fail a boundary audit in a neighboring positional test.  The endogenous-fitness surface therefore needs its own finite statistic before any positive biological reading is allowed.

\paragraph{What the Yang row establishes.}
The verified Yang record establishes that the registered experiment ran to completion on a nontrivial finite surface: $46686$ synonymous edits, $30$ genes, $200$ null draws, and a recorded runtime.  This is enough to include the packet as an audited BioReality chapter because the subject is not a hypothetical coordinate name; it is an enumerated perturbation table with a bounded response class.  It is not enough to assert that any one $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinate improves endogenous fitness prediction, because the row supplied here contains no Yang-specific coefficient, no $R^2$ ladder, no description-length increment, no null tail probability, and no gene-block stability statistic.  The responsible readback is therefore: Yang provides a finite endogenous-fitness test surface for synonymous edits, but the present row supports only scale, eligibility, and boundary status.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, or universality follows from this packet without an additional layer-matched contact.  A direct association from a synonymous residual to endogenous fitness would still be only a perturbation-fitness association unless the same rows also contain a surviving translation readout such as measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, or an explicitly bounded modeled tAI/stAI statistic.  A protein-abundance statement would require matched protein abundance; a function statement would require a function assay; a mechanism statement would require perturbation, rescue, or otherwise layer-specific intervention.  The finite Yang surface is therefore admitted as an endogenous synonymous-fitness audit surface, not as a completed translation-mediated protein-realization result.

The packet is ready as a self-contained boundary chapter.  Its positive content is the finite audit scale
$$
\begin{aligned}

 n_{synonymous\ edits} &= 46686,\\
 B &= 200,\\

Its negative content is equally important: the finite row currently lacks the Yang-specific performance and null statistics needed for a stronger claim.  The chapter should therefore be read as a registered endogenous-fitness contact for future $B^*_{Q6}$ residual testing, with all translation, protein, structure, function, mechanism, and universality readings held outside the claim until separate matched contacts supply them.
