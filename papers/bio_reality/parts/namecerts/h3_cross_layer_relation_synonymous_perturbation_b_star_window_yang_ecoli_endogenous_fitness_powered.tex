\subsection{Endogenous Synonymous Fitness Windows in \textit{E. coli}}
\label{sec:bior reality-yang-ecoli-endogenous-fitness}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The Yang \textit{E. coli} endogenous-fitness packet tests whether B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window coordinates can be attached to an experimentally finite perturbation surface rather than only to codon-count geometry. The verified run is finite in three explicit senses: it used $30$ endogenous genes, enumerated $46686$ synonymous edits, and evaluated the registered powered audit with $B=200$ null or resampling units. The run completed in $491.605$ seconds under experiment id $906139f914b0$. These numbers define the admissible row surface for this chapter: the object is not a genome-wide law and not a statement about all synonymous variation, but a finite matrix of same-organism synonymous edits in endogenous \textit{E. coli} coding contexts.

\paragraph{Coordinate role.}
Each row is read as a perturbation event whose amino-acid sequence is held fixed while codon choice changes inside an endogenous coding background. The B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates are therefore used only as residual codon-window descriptors. They are not promoted to biochemical causes. The coordinate family available to the surrounding Q6 program is the nine-dimensional list
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
For this endogenous-fitness chapter the verified finite-row record supplies the perturbation count and power setting, but it does not supply a chapter-local effect-size vector, per-coordinate ranking, or a finite list of positive coordinates. Consequently the scientifically correct readback is a bounded perturbation audit: B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ has been brought into contact with $46686$ synonymous endogenous \textit{E. coli} edits across $30$ genes, while the sign, localization, and coordinate identity of any fitness-improving or fitness-damaging residual term remain outside the present row summary.

\paragraph{Controlled relation being tested.}
The intended relation is a cross-layer finite matrix from synonymous residual coordinates to an endogenous fitness readout. In notation, the admissible surface has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
& q_i \in B^{\ast}_{Q6},\qquad e_r \in \{1,\ldots,46686\},\qquad g(e_r) \in \{1,\ldots,30\},\\
& F(e_r)=\hbox{the finite endogenous-fitness readout attached to edit }e_r,\\
& \Delta_{i}=\hbox{the audited incremental contribution of }q_i\hbox{ after the declared edit-level controls.}
\end{aligned}
$$
The chapter accepts only this finite relation. It does not replace the actual fitness assay with codon adaptation, protein abundance, ribosome occupancy, or mRNA abundance. The $B=200$ audit size says that the run included a finite null or resampling comparison; it is not by itself a positive biological conclusion. Since the row summary does not expose a $p$-value, held-out $R^2$, likelihood increment, or per-coordinate coefficient for the Yang fitness surface, no numerical significance claim is made here beyond completion of the powered finite-row audit.

\paragraph{Relation to the \textit{E. coli} protein-output packets.}
The Yang fitness surface is stricter than an expression-output contact because endogenous fitness is a downstream phenotype-like readout. Nearby \textit{E. coli} perturbation packets show that B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ can survive some protein-output style audits under finite controls: the Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output run used $1459$ variants, obtained $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta DL=19.089327473282296$ bits, and reported matched and within-null probabilities $p=0.004975124378109453$. A separate distributed recoding run reported $47$ ampicillin-resistance rows, $53$ growth-marker rows, $19$ multi-strata $D$ bins, an ampicillin slope of $0.0$, and an independent probability $p=1.0$. These neighboring records are not imported as proof for the Yang endogenous-fitness surface. They only calibrate the local caution: finite synonymous perturbation contacts can be positive, null, or layer-dependent, and therefore the Yang packet must stand on its own row-level fitness statistic before any stronger conclusion is allowed.

\paragraph{Translation gate.}
The broader Q6 program contains a translation-mediated protein-abundance matrix in which \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 joined $3739$ proteins with CDS and tRNA data. In that matrix the \textit{E. coli} row had total residual protein-abundance association $c=0.10767526677985896$, direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI $c'=0.04167869969498872$, an indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$, and mediation fraction $0.612922252793667$. The modeled-tAI survival record also found $81$ entries above threshold across the three-organism modeled matrix, with threshold $0.01$, but explicitly marked the $3$-organism surface as insufficient for powered survival. These translation and abundance numbers are useful boundary information, not a substitute for the Yang fitness assay. A Q6-to-fitness association is not treated as translation realization unless the same coordinate first survives a layer-matched translation readout, and a translation-mediated abundance relation is not treated as fitness mechanism unless the fitness packet itself supplies the necessary perturbation statistic.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row verdict is that this packet is ready as a bounded endogenous-fitness perturbation record, not as a mechanistic law. The concrete completed surface is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{genes} &= 30,\\
N_{edits} &= 46686,\\
B &= 200,\\
T_{runtime} &= 491.605\ \hbox{seconds}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those values are enough to state that the endogenous \textit{E. coli} synonymous-fitness contact has been instantiated at finite scale. They are not enough to name a winning Q6 coordinate, report a fitness direction, or claim that synonymous residual geometry realizes translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, adaptation, or universality. Any such promotion would require a separate layer-matched contact: measured translation for translation claims, structural or physical assays for folding and admissibility claims, functional assays for molecular function, perturbation and rescue logic for mechanism, and multi-organism controlled replication for population-level or universal statements.

\paragraph{Interpretive boundary.}
The only positive assertion made here is finite contact between a B$^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window coordinate system and an endogenous \textit{E. coli} fitness perturbation matrix with $46686$ edits across $30$ genes. The chapter deliberately refuses three shortcuts. First, a direct residual-to-fitness association, if later reported, cannot be read as a protein or translation mechanism without an intervening translation-readout step. Second, a sign observed in \textit{E. coli} cannot be turned into a cross-organism law without matched organism controls and simpler-baseline ablation. Third, codon-window geometry remains a coordinate system, not an external measurement. The present packet is therefore a finite audit surface for endogenous synonymous perturbation, with all higher biological readings held behind their own empirical gates.
