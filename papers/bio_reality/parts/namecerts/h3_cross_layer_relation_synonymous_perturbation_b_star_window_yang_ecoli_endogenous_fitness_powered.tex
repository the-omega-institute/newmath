\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns an endogenous \emph{Escherichia coli} synonymous-edit panel in which the admissible data surface is not a free association between a codon residual and fitness.  The row object is restricted to synonymous edits inside real coding sequence context, with the $B^*_{Q6}$ window coordinates used only as finite covariates on those edited sequences.  The verified run identified $30$ genes and $46686$ synonymous edits, with $B=200$ resampling or null-comparison units registered for the powered audit.  The computation completed in $491.605$ seconds under run id $906139f914b0$.  Thus the chapter records a finite perturbation surface, not a population law: the admissible biological object is the table of edited endogenous coding rows together with the declared codon-window coordinates and the measured fitness readout attached to those rows.

\paragraph{Coordinate discipline.}
The coordinates available to the broader $B^*_{Q6}$ packet are the nine residual directions
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN\,vs\,UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA\,vs\,UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR\,vs\,AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA\,vs\,UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR\,vs\,ACY}$, and $f_3$-stress.  They are residual coding-sequence coordinates after the amino-acid quotient and baseline controls; they are not themselves measurements of translation, abundance, folding, or selection.  The linked modeled-translation audit supplies only a bounded codon-adaptation contact: in the three-organism modeled-tAI run, $81$ entries exceeded the threshold $0.01$, the maximum absolute entry was $3801961.031607896$, and the organism count was $3$.  That evidence is explicitly underpowered for a measured survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species, so it is used here only to keep the codon-window coordinates separated from any stronger biological reading.

\paragraph{Endogenous-fitness interpretation.}
For this Yang \emph{E. coli} panel, the finite assertion is that the $46686$ synonymous edits over $30$ genes provide a bounded perturbation table on which $B^*_{Q6}$ windows may be tested against endogenous fitness.  The chapter does not assert that every coordinate is active, that a particular codon-family direction has a stable sign across organisms, or that a direct residual-to-fitness association is a mechanism.  Its admissible reading is narrower: if a $B^*_{Q6}$ window improves the controlled fitness model on this finite edit panel under the registered null comparisons, then the result is evidence that synonymous coding-window geometry has a detectable perturbation-level contact in this dataset.  If the improvement disappears under the controls or under the null comparisons, the packet remains a codon-window residual record for this panel rather than a realized protein or organismal mechanism.

The larger cross-layer mediation packet shows why this chapter must remain local.  In the separate multi-organism protein-abundance audit, $12$ organisms had joined CDS and PAXdb protein rows, including $3739$ joined proteins for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $5815$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}, $19053$ for human, and $19395$ for mouse.  That audit computed a statistical $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition using modeled per-protein tAI and measured protein abundance; for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 it reported $c=0.10767526677985896$, $c'=0.04167869969498872$, an indirect component $0.06599656708487024$, and $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  Those numbers are relevant only as a separate bounded mediation surface.  They do not convert the Yang endogenous-fitness edit rows into ribosome occupancy, matched protein abundance, or a causal translation mechanism.  The Yang panel supplies perturbation rows; the protein-abundance audit supplies a distinct cross-layer matrix.

The registered finite scale is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}

This is enough to support a finite edit-panel chapter: the rows exist, the resampling scale is fixed, and the run completed. \paragraph{Cannot-claim boundary.}
The allowed conclusion is a bounded one: the Yang endogenous \emph{E. coli} synonymous-edit run contributes a finite perturbation contact for testing whether $B^*_{Q6}$ window coordinates carry fitness-relevant information in the assayed genes.  No translation claim follows unless an organism-matched translation readout, such as ribosome profiling or explicitly bounded tAI/stAI mediation, is joined and survives its own controls.  No protein-abundance claim follows unless matched protein rows are attached after the translation step.  No folding, physical-admissibility, molecular-function, organismal-phenotype, mechanistic, selection, or universality claim follows from this fitness panel alone.  Those higher readings require their own layer-matched contacts and finite statistics.
