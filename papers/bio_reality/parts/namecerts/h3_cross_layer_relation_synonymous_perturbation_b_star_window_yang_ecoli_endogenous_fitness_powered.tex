\subsection{Endogenous \textit{E. coli} synonymous fitness window}\label{sec:bioreality-yang-ecoli-endogenous-fitness-window}

\paragraph{Finite panel.}
The row object for this chapter is the endogenous \textit{Escherichia coli} synonymous-edit panel indexed by the claim key \texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.synonymous\_perturbation.b\_star\_window\_yang\_ecoli\_endogenous\_fitness\_powered}.  The completed run is identified by \texttt{906139f914b0}.  Its finite surface contains $30$ genes and $46686$ synonymous edits, and the registered permutation or resampling budget is $B=200$.  The recorded execution time is $491.605$ seconds.  These numbers are the only asserted row counts for this chapter; the chapter therefore treats the object as a finite endogenous-fitness perturbation panel rather than as a genome-wide law.

\paragraph{Coordinate object.}
The tested covariates are read as $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates on synonymous coding sequence variation.  The companion modeled-translation run records the computed coordinate list
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This gives $|Q|=9$.  The finite-row interpretation is not that these coordinates are themselves biological causes.  They are residual coordinates after amino-acid identity has been held fixed, with GC3, coding length, organism, and an $M$-density baseline kept in the control role.  The endogenous-fitness panel asks whether synonymous edits arranged on this residual coordinate surface improve a held-out fitness readout on the finite edit set.

\paragraph{Registered finite statistic.}
Let $e$ range over the $46686$ synonymous edits and let $g(e)$ be the gene containing $e$, with $30$ possible gene labels.  The row-level design can be summarized as a controlled comparison
$$
\begin{aligned}
F_e &= \alpha + Z_e\beta + \varepsilon_e,\\
F_e &= \alpha' + Z_e\beta' + B^{\ast}_{Q6}(e)\gamma + \varepsilon'_e,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $F_e$ is the finite endogenous-fitness readout and $Z_e$ denotes the non-promoted baseline controls: amino-acid sequence preservation, gene identity or matched gene blocking when used by the run, GC3 and length effects where estimable, and simpler codon-composition baselines.  The registered budget $B=200$ marks the finite null comparison budget for the powered row.  Since the stored row for this claim reports completion, row count, gene count, and budget but does not expose a chapter-local $\Delta R^2$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}$, regression slope, or $p$-value, the chapter states the admissible conclusion as bounded finite contact: the endogenous \textit{E. coli} synonymous-edit panel has been evaluated on $46686$ edits across $30$ genes under the declared $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ window route, but this chapter does not assert a numerical effect size beyond the recorded completion of that finite run.

\paragraph{Translation-gated interpretation.}
The surrounding evidence ledger matters because the biological object named by this chapter is cross-layer: residual synonymous coordinates are allowed to approach fitness only through measured or explicitly bounded readout surfaces.  The modeled tAI contact computes the same nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ list on $3$ organisms, with $61$ sense codons and a nonzero modeled coupling surface containing $81$ entries above threshold $0.01$.  Its maximum absolute modeled coupling is $3801961.031607896$, but that run also records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that the object is modeled-tAI bounded coupling, not ribosome profiling or proteomics validation.  Thus the Yang endogenous-fitness panel is not promoted to translation realization merely because it shares the residual coordinate basis.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance context.}
A separate matched protein-abundance mediation run supplies a useful calibration for what a stronger cross-layer object would have to look like.  In the \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 protein-abundance mediation table, $3739$ proteins are joined, the translation readout is per-protein modeled tAI, and the controlled mediation decomposition records
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.10767526677985896,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.04167869969498872,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.06599656708487024,\\
M^{QTP} &= 0.612922252793667.
\end{aligned}
$$
Those values are not transplanted into the Yang fitness panel.  They are cited only to make the admissibility rule explicit: a direct residual-to-output association is weaker than a mediated finite matrix $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ or $Q_i \to T_j \to F_k$ in which the translation step is explicitly present and survives the controls.  The endogenous-fitness chapter therefore keeps the fitness readout separate from protein abundance, ribosome occupancy, folded structure, and molecular function.

\paragraph{Endogenous-fitness boundary.}
The Yang finite panel is stronger than a pure sequence annotation because it contains real synonymous edits and an endogenous \textit{E. coli} fitness readout.  It is also narrower than a mechanism claim.  The row count $46686$ and gene count $30$ support a finite perturbation audit of synonymous-window fitness association, not a universal statement about codon usage, tRNA adaptation, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, or organismal phenotype.  A nearby \textit{E. coli} reporter panel, the Nieuwkoop mRFP protein-output run, reports $1459$ variants, $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matched}}=0.004975124378109453$, and $p_{\mathrm{within}}=0.004975124378109453$; that reporter result is a distinct protein-output contact and is not used as the Yang endogenous-fitness effect size.  Likewise, the distributed recoding run records $47$ \textit{ampR} rows and $53$ \textit{gmR} rows with $19$ multi-$D$ strata and $p_{\mathrm{ampR}}=1.0$, but it is a different perturbation surface.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible readback is a finite controlled fitness-window statement: on the stored Yang endogenous \textit{E. coli} panel, $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-window coordinates are evaluated over $46686$ edits in $30$ genes with $B=200$.  No translation claim follows unless a same-row translation contact supplies ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, tAI/stAI survival, or an equivalent bounded translation readout.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, phenotype law, or universality follows from this chapter unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that readout and the corresponding controlled statistic.  The chapter is therefore ready as a finite perturbation record with a deliberately bounded biological interpretation.
