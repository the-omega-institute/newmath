\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter therefore records a bounded modeled-tAI survival surface for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual rows.  It may be read as evidence that the residual coordinates are not disconnected from organism-scoped codon-adaptation weights.  It may not be read as observed translation dynamics, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality.  Any such assertion requires its own matched contact rows and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ protein-abundance association is not admissible as protein realization unless the intervening $Q_i\to T_j$ step has survived in the relevant translation readout under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-codon-baseline controls.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The finite verdict is positive only at bounded modeled-contact scope.  The matrix has nonzero entries above the declared threshold and the no-contact break condition is false.  The powered-survival request remains out of scope for the three-organism modeled panel.  Thus $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ advances to modeled tAI readout status here, while measured translation survival and protein-realization mediation must be decided only in later joined datasets with adequate rank, organism count, and layer-matched measurements.
