\subsection{Modeled tAI contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-translation-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The data surface in this record is the finite array of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates tested against an organism-scoped modeled tAI readout. The residual list is
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense codons. The empirical contact used here is not ribosome profiling: it is modeled codon adaptation, with $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from local GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and the dos Reis wobble $s$-values. Three organisms are present in the finite row: \textit{Escherichia coli} K12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. Their modeled readout tables each contain all $61$ sense codons, and the zero-raw-$W$ fallback count is $0$ in each of the three modeled-tAI computations.

\paragraph{Payload integrity.}
The local tRNA payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families for all three organisms. In \textit{E. coli} K12 the payload has $89$ FASTA headers, of which $87$ match the standard parsing route and $2$ remain unmatched. In human the payload has $432$ FASTA headers, all $432$ matched. In yeast the payload has $275$ FASTA headers, all $275$ matched. Thus the readout is not blocked by a missing standard amino-acid family or by a codon table of the wrong size; the limitation is instead statistical scope, because the contact has only $3$ organisms.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling statistic.}
The modeled-tAI matrix has a nonzero bounded coupling surface under the declared threshold. With coupling threshold $0.01$, the finite computation reports $81$ entries above threshold. The maximum absolute matrix entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is the same number,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_i| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The registered break condition is not met for the bounded modeled contact: the finite modeled-tAI matrix is not empty above threshold. However, the same row explicitly refuses a powered survival interpretation, because $3$ organisms are far below the required scale for a powered multi-species survival claim. The correct readback is therefore: modeled tAI gives a bounded translation-layer coupling contact for the nine residual coordinates, but it does not by itself establish observed translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or a universal biological rule.

These values support a statistical decomposition surface only. They do not turn the present three-organism modeled-tAI coupling row into a measured ribosome or causal protein-realization result.

\paragraph{Yeast structure and abundance control check.}
The yeast five-way join supplies a further bounded comparison: $4759$ genes are joined across CDS, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE structure proxy. The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the total join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$. In that surface, the direct residual-to-protein residual association is $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ after controls, while conditioning on the structure proxy gives $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$. Conditioning on measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and adding the structure proxy gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation $p$-values are $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction. This check is useful because it shows how a same-row downstream decomposition must be interpreted: as variance partitioning under controls, not as a proof of folding, physical feasibility, or molecular function.

\paragraph{Associated finite audits.}
Other finite rows keep the interpretation narrow. A structural-order audit identifies $f_3$-stress as the sole fold-level sign-flip survivor, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$; the other residual axes have larger right-tail $p$-values, including $0.142578125$ for Arg\_AGR, $0.0693359375$ for Ile\_AUA, and $0.2294921875$ for K\_AAA. A cross-ortholog selection-imprint row over $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species reports $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null-$95$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$, and no positive selection-imprint verdict. A dynamic mass-balance row over $120$ genes and $718$ intervals reports $R^2=0.9960628201518571$ for the $M$-only baseline, $0.996410628851051$ after adding estimated translation state, $0.9712443377743347$ at the next layer, $0.8953999568421264$ at the final layer, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$. An observable execution row in yeast over $3289$ genes reports an observable-execution subblock, while the locked genetic-code-table falsification row reports $40/40$ successes in each of two exact one-sided binomial tests with upper-tail $p=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ and $0/20$ unexpected successes in both negative and critical-ablation controls. These rows sharpen the boundary around the modeled-tAI contact rather than enlarging it.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite modeled-tAI row is ready as a bounded BioReality record. It establishes that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nonempty modeled codon-adaptation coupling surface across three organisms, with $81$ entries above $0.01$ and maximum absolute score $3801961.031607896$. It also establishes the refusal condition for stronger wording: the row has only $3$ organisms and no matched measured ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, proteomics, and mRNA-control matrix for this exact survival claim. Consequently, the chapter may say that modeled tAI supplies a controlled translation-readout contact. It may not say that translation has been observed, that a ribosome mechanism has been demonstrated, that a protein-abundance mediation chain is causally established, or that any folding, physical-admissibility, function, phenotype, selection, or universal-law conclusion follows. Each such stronger conclusion would require a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled finite statistic.
