\subsection{Modeled tAI survival for the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This chapter records a finite translation-readout test for the B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates before any claim is allowed to cross into protein realization.  The tested carrier is the finite family of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ ranges over the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual axes, $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout only when the translation step has first been evaluated.  In the present finite run, the readout is modeled tAI, not ribosome profiling and not proteomics.  The computed residual coordinates are
$$\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},
\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},
\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The codon count ambient space contains $61$ sense codons.  The modeled tAI weights were computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads and dos Reis wobble $s$-values for three organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Homo sapiens}.  These three rows are sufficient for a bounded modeled-contact calculation, but not for a powered multi-organism survival law.

\paragraph{tRNA payload and modeled readout checks.}
The local tRNA payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 the run read $89$ FASTA headers, matched $87$, and left $2$ unmatched.  For human it read and matched $432$ headers with no unmatched standard-family header.  For yeast it read and matched $275$ headers with no unmatched standard-family header.  The modeled tAI codon readout covered all $61$ sense codons in each of the three organisms, and the zero-raw-$W$ fallback count was $0$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12, $0$ for human, and $0$ for yeast.  Thus the finite table is not rejected for missing standard amino-acid families or codon-weight holes.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling matrix.}
Let $S_{ij}$ denote the finite modeled coupling score between residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled tAI readout coordinate $t_j$ after the codon-weight construction has been fixed.  The run used coupling threshold $0.01$ and found $81$ entries above threshold.  The maximum absolute entry was
$$\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}|=3{,}801{,}961.031607896,
\end{aligned}$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry was the same number:
$$\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_{ii}|=3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}$$
The finite break condition for this chapter is therefore not triggered at the modeled-contact level: the table is not the all-small matrix at threshold $0.01$.  The recorded Boolean value is $\mathrm{break\_condition\_met}=\mathrm{false}$.  This is a positive bounded coupling result for modeled tAI weights, but its layer is explicitly `translation\_realization\_modeled\_underpowered'.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The decisive limitation is organism count.  The run has $n=3$ organisms, and the registered power rule states that powered survival is unavailable without at least $40$ matched species.  The honest readback is therefore
$$\begin{aligned}
3<40,
\end{aligned}$$
so the finite matrix cannot be used as a powered survival claim.  It says that B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero modeled-tAI coupling in this bounded table; it does not say that measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, or protein abundance has survived the same coordinate test.

\paragraph{Controls and forbidden promotion.}
The admissible interpretation is a controlled matrix route $q_i\to t_j$ for a modeled translation-weight readout.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the M-density baseline remain controls or baseline covariates; they are not promoted to new biological coordinates.  A direct association $q_i\to p_k$ is not evidence of protein realization unless the same residual coordinate first survives a translation-readout step $q_i\to t_j$ under the declared controls.  Matched mRNA abundance is used only to separate transcription-layer signal from translation-layer signal when measured translation efficiency is available; transcript abundance alone is not ribosome occupancy and is not protein output.

Those rows are useful as a later statistical decomposition, but they are not substituted for measured ribosome profiling in this chapter.

\paragraph{Yeast abundance and structure audit.}
The yeast five-way join in the structure-mediation audit had $4{,}759$ joined genes, a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$, and a reported overall join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$.  In that row, residualized B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinates plus controls gave $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation values were $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  These numbers show why the chapter keeps the modeled-tAI survival question separate from structure: a small structure-associated signal can be audited, but it does not transform a modeled translation-weight matrix into folded-structure evidence.

\paragraph{Axis identity and earlier table contact.}
The structural-order axis audit also computed the same nine axes and identified $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ as the sole fold-level sign-flip survivor in that specific structural-order test, with weighted held-out increment $0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip right-tail value $0.03125$.  The earlier independent genetic-code table contact had $40$ successes out of $40$ trials in both the boundary-probe and independent-replication binomial checks, each with one-sided upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ negative-control and $20$ ablation-control rows.  Those table contacts validate a finite codon-geometry interface, but they do not license a translation, folding, function, or organism-level claim.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The chapter verdict is a bounded modeled-tAI coupling result with an underpowered survival boundary.  The finite data support the statement that at least one B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinate has nonzero modeled-tAI coupling in the three-organism matrix, with $81$ threshold-passing entries at threshold $0.01$.  The same data do not support powered translation survival, observed ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, protein abundance realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Each of those stronger readings requires its own layer-matched measurement contact and its own controlled finite statistic.
