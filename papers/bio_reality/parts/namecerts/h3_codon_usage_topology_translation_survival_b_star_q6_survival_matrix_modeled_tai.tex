\subsection{Modeled tAI coupling boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-coupling-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite matrix whose row coordinates are the nine synonymous residual directions
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout side is a modeled codon-adaptation surface, not an observed ribosome-footprint surface.  For each organism, codon weights were computed for the $61$ sense codons from local GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The finite contact contains three organisms: \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local tRNA payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each of the three organisms.  The header audits are $89$ FASTA headers with $87$ matched and $2$ unmatched headers for \textit{E. coli} K-12, $432$ matched headers out of $432$ for human, and $275$ matched headers out of $275$ for yeast.  The modeled tAI construction assigned weights for all $61$ sense codons in all three organisms, with zero raw-weight fallback count equal to $0$ in each organism.

\paragraph{Computed bounded coupling.}
The finite matrix contains a nonzero modeled-tAI coupling signal under the declared bounded-contact threshold.  With threshold $0.01$, the run reports $81$ entries above threshold and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j} |S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The relevant reading is only that the residual coordinates are not numerically orthogonal to the modeled tAI weight construction on this three-organism finite surface.  The result is therefore a bounded codon-adaptation coupling record: it is a statement about the computed matrix obtained from tRNA gene-copy and wobble-weight data, not about measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, function, selection, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Rank and power status.}
The run classifies the layer as $\mathrm{translation\_realization\_modeled\_underpowered}$ and records $n=3$ organisms.  The break condition for the packet is stated as requiring more data if the finite modeled-tAI matrix has no entry above the bounded-contact threshold, or if powered survival is requested without at least $40$ matched species.  In this row the first condition is not met: the thresholded modeled matrix is nonzero.  The second condition remains decisive for any powered biological survival claim: three organisms are insufficient for powered survival, and the run explicitly records that this is modeled-tAI bounded coupling rather than ribo-seq or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Structure and axis comparison.}
The yeast structure-mediated abundance run provides another guardrail against over-reading the present matrix.  In $4759$ joined yeast rows, with $23$ effective control rank and $4736$ residual degrees of freedom after controls, the structure proxy had $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$, permutation right-tail $p=0.027972027972027972$, structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$, and structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$ with permutation right-tail $p=0.1258741258741259$.  The single-axis structural-order audit found exactly one coordinate passing its fold-level sign-flip gate: $f_3$-stress, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  These finite rows show that structure-facing or protein-facing claims require their own row joins and controls.  They do not convert the modeled tAI coupling matrix into a folding, structure, or physical-admissibility result.

\paragraph{Boundary statement.}
The scientifically admissible conclusion is narrow.  The three-organism matrix supports a bounded modeled-tAI contact for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, because the finite computation covers the $61$ sense codons, covers all standard amino-acid families in the local tRNA payload, uses no zero-weight fallback in the modeled tAI readout, and yields $81$ above-threshold entries at threshold $0.01$.  It does not establish observed translation survival, because no organism-matched ribosome-profiling or measured translation-efficiency table is attached to this row.  It does not establish a direct protein realization, because a $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not admissible unless the corresponding $Q_i\to T_j$ step is tested and survives on matched translation rows with the declared controls.  It does not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.  Those readings require separate layer-matched contacts and finite statistics at the appropriate biological layer.

The broader finite-checking program includes an independent genetic-code-table contact in which $40$ of $40$ boundary trials succeeded with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and the independent replication again reports $40$ of $40$ successes with the same probability. That background supports the practice of separating codon-table geometry from higher biological layers.  It does not by itself add translation, protein, structure, function, phenotype, mechanism, or universal-law content to the modeled-tAI matrix considered here.
