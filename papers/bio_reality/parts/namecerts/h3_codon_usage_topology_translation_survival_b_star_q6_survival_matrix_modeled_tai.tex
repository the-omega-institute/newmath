\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record fixes a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The first coordinate $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The second coordinate $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout.  In the present finite run it is not ribosome profiling and not measured translation efficiency; it is a modeled tAI codon weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene copy data and dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  The third coordinate $p_k$ is reserved for matched protein-abundance readout, but no matched proteomics row is present in this run.  The observed contact therefore supports only a bounded modeled-translation readback, not a completed protein-abundance mediation chain.

\paragraph{tRNA payload and codon-weight construction.}
The codon space contains $61$ sense codons.  The organism set has size $3$, namely \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local GtRNAdb payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The finite header counts are $89$ total and $87$ matched FASTA headers for \emph{E. coli} K-12, $432$ total and $432$ matched headers for human, and $275$ total and $275$ matched headers for yeast.  Only \emph{E. coli} K-12 has unmatched headers, with count $2$.  For each of the three organisms, the modeled tAI construction produced weights for all $61$ sense codons with zero fallback count $0$ for raw $W$ values:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|C_{\mathrm{sense}}| &= 61,\\
N_{\mathrm{org}} &= 3,\\
N_{\mathrm{aa\ families}}(\mathrm{E.coli},\mathrm{human},\mathrm{yeast}) &= (20,20,20),\\
N_{\mathrm{zero\ fallback}}(\mathrm{E.coli},\mathrm{human},\mathrm{yeast}) &= (0,0,0).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite calculation has a complete modeled tAI row for every sense codon in every organism included in the run.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling statistic.}
The computed matrix is a modeled tAI coupling matrix rather than a powered survival matrix.  The threshold used for bounded contact is $0.01$.  Against that threshold, the run reports $81$ entries above threshold, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tau &= 0.01,\\
\#\{(i,j): |S_{ij}| > \tau\} &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896.

$$
The break condition for this finite contact is not met: the modeled matrix does contain nonzero bounded couplings above the stated threshold.  This is evidence that the nine residual coordinates can be paired with modeled codon-adaptation weights in a nontrivial finite matrix.  It is not evidence that a ribosome, proteome, folded structure, physical assay, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universal law has been observed.

\paragraph{Mediation rule.}
A direct association $q_i \to p_k$ is not admissible as a protein-realization claim on this evidence alone.  The admissible route is first to test whether a residual coordinate survives a translation readout, written schematically as $q_i \to t_j$, and only then to ask whether a matched protein-abundance coordinate is mediated downstream, written $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$.  In the present run, $t_j$ is modeled tAI, while $p_k$ has no matched proteomics row.  The controlled mediation statement is therefore only partially populated: the $q_i \to t_j$ modeled-contact layer has finite nonzero entries, but the downstream $t_j \to p_k$ and mediated $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$ portions remain untested.

\paragraph{Controls and non-promotion boundary.}
The residual coordinate is not promoted over the baseline variables.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline remain controls.  Matched mRNA abundance is also a control for separating translation-layer signal from transcription-layer signal; it is not itself a translation readout and not a protein-abundance measurement.  A mediated edge is separated from a transcription-only edge only after such matched mRNA abundance control is present.  Since the current organism count is $3$, the run is explicitly underpowered for a powered survival claim requiring at least $40$ matched species.  The finite result is consequently classified as modeled tAI bounded coupling under insufficient species power, not ribosome-seq validation, not proteomics validation, and not a cross-layer protein-realization fit.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
This chapter records a positive finite modeled-tAI contact: all three organisms have complete sense-codon modeled tAI weights, all three cover the standard amino-acid families, and the bounded matrix has $81$ entries above $0.01$.  The result is strong enough to keep the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates in the translation-readout queue, because the modeled codon-adaptation matrix is not null.  It is not strong enough to claim measured translation survival or protein-abundance mediation.  No translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or universality follows unless a separate matched contact supplies the relevant row and the declared controlled statistic is computed.
