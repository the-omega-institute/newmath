\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival and Translation-Conditioned Protein Abundance for $B^*_{Q6}$}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-protein-abundance}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record is a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$: $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The residual coordinates computed in the finite row are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates.  The translation contact in the bounded survival run is modeled tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  Three organisms were present in that modeled readout run: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Their modeled tAI tables each covered all $61$ sense codons with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$.  The local tRNA payload also covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each case; the matched FASTA header counts were $87$ of $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12, $432$ of $432$ for human, and $275$ of $275$ for yeast.

\paragraph{Bounded translation readout.}
The modeled-tAI survival matrix is not a measured ribosome-occupancy matrix.  It is a bounded codon-adaptation contact, and it is therefore read only as a finite modeled translation-readout relation.  With coupling threshold $0.01$, the finite run found $81$ entries above threshold and maximum absolute score
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| = 3801961.031607896,
\qquad
\max_i |S_{ii}| = 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for the modeled matrix was not met: the matrix did not collapse to the all-below-threshold case.  However, the organism count was $3$, so the record is explicitly underpowered for population-level survival.  The row therefore supports bounded modeled-tAI coupling, not a universal translation law and not a ribosome-profiling validation.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation rows.}
A separate finite cross-layer run joined real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb-style protein abundance rows across $12$ organisms.  The readout $T$ was per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T=\frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the codon count in the CDS and $w_c$ is the organism-specific modeled tAI weight.  The readout $Y$ was $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ after joining measured protein abundance to real CDS codon counts.  The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} mc2 155, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The control design used $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for $Z$, rank $9$ for the residual block $X$, rank $1$ for the modeled-tAI block $T$, and rank $10$ for the joint block $(X,T)$ in every organism.  The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ ranged from $1633$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $19372$ in mouse, and every organism was marked rank-sufficient for the declared finite regression surface.

\paragraph{Mediation magnitudes.}
The statistical mediation fraction $M^{QTP}$ stayed inside the unit interval for every organism.  The largest fraction was yeast, with $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$, total residual-to-protein $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2$ after conditioning on modeled tAI $0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  The next largest fractions were \emph{D. melanogaster} at $0.6165167748739756$, \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 at $0.612922252793667$, and \emph{B. subtilis} 168 at $0.6084594553474505$.  Human gave $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$, total $R^2=0.04454565118019448$, direct conditioned $R^2=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect difference $0.012119218668341744$.  Mouse gave $M^{QTP}=0.15488799907323988$ with indirect difference $0.007717756749299982$.  The smallest fraction was \emph{S. solfataricus}, $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$, with total $R^2=0.06397514251295629$ and indirect difference $0.0011839678516385038$.  Thus the finite rows contain positive modeled-tAI-conditioned protein-abundance decompositions, but the magnitudes are heterogeneous across organisms and do not collapse to a single biological sign rule.

\paragraph{Local admissible reading.}
The admissible claim is only this: the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has nonzero bounded modeled-tAI coupling in the three-organism tAI contact, and in a separate twelve-organism CDS/protein table the residual-to-protein association can be decomposed by conditioning on per-protein modeled tAI after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched abundance controls are represented in the design.  Direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not promoted by itself.  The row must pass through the translation readout $q_i\to t_j$ before it is read as a translation-conditioned protein-abundance statistic.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No claim about observed translation, ribosome dynamics, folding, molecular function, physical admissibility, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from these rows alone.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts is not ribosome profiling.  Protein abundance is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding state, or pathway context.  The present record is therefore ready as a bounded modeled-tAI and statistical protein-abundance mediation chapter, while measured translation-efficiency, perturbation, structure, function, phenotype, and mechanism claims remain outside the local evidence surface until separate matched contacts supply those observations.
