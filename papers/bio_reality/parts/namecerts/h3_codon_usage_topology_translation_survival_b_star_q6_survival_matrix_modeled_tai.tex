\subsection{Modeled tAI survival boundary for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bior-q6-modeled-tai-survival-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The registered object is a finite mediation surface whose rows are synonymous residual coordinates $q_i$, modeled translation readouts $t_j$, and downstream protein-abundance readouts $p_k$ when those downstream rows are available.  In the present finite run the translation contact is not ribosome profiling and is not measured translation efficiency.  It is a modeled tAI readout: for each sense codon, $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ is computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble weights.  The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense codons, and the residual basis contains exactly nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \\
&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \\
&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The three organism rows in this run are \emph{Escherichia coli} K--12, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Homo sapiens}.  Their local GtRNAdb payloads cover all $20$ standard amino-acid families: \emph{E. coli} K--12 has $89$ FASTA headers with $87$ matched and $2$ unmatched headers, yeast has $275$ matched headers with no unmatched header, and human has $432$ matched headers with no unmatched header.  The modeled tAI calculation returned all $61$ sense-codon weights for each organism, with zero raw-weight fallback count equal to $0$ in all three organisms.

\paragraph{Observed bounded coupling.}
The matrix calculation is a bounded codon-adaptation contact, not a measured ribosome contact.  Under the registered threshold $0.01$, the modeled tAI coupling matrix contains $81$ entries above threshold.  Its largest absolute entry and largest absolute diagonal entry are both $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\{(i,j): |S_{ij}|>0.01\}| &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for this packet is therefore not met in the narrow modeled-contact sense: the finite modeled-tAI matrix is nonzero above the declared bounded-contact threshold.  The interpretation is deliberately narrower than a powered survival claim.  The run has only $3$ organisms, while the registered powered-survival route requires at least $40$ matched species; consequently the recorded layer is $\mathrm{translation\_realization\_modeled\_underpowered}$ rather than a measured or population-powered translation result.

\paragraph{Mediation reading.}
The admissible readback is a controlled matrix $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not treated as protein realization unless the $Q_i \to T_j$ step first survives the declared translation readout.  The larger protein-abundance mediation run gives a concrete example of what a downstream matrix can look like once protein rows are joined: twelve organisms were joined with measured PAXdb abundance and modeled per-protein tAI.  Joined protein counts ranged from $1{,}656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19{,}395$ in mouse, with human at $19{,}053$, yeast at $5{,}815$, and \emph{E. coli} K--12 at $3{,}739$.  In that downstream run the joint nine-coordinate mediation fraction stayed inside the unit interval for all organisms.  Representative values were $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K--12, $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus}.  These numbers are useful as finite downstream context, but they remain a statistical decomposition using modeled tAI and steady-state protein abundance.  They do not convert the three-organism modeled-tAI contact into ribosome occupancy, causation, folding, function, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Controls and separated coordinates.}
The intended control set keeps amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline outside the promoted coordinate list.  Matched mRNA abundance is likewise a control for separating transcription-layer signal from translation-layer signal.  The yeast five-way mediation audit makes this separation concrete.  It joined $4{,}759$ genes across CDS codon counts, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy.  The direct residual-to-protein value after control residualization was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on the structure proxy changed this to $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$ and adding the structure proxy after those variables gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction after TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$.  This supports a controlled variance-partition reading; it is not a folding-pathway or causal-initiation result.

\paragraph{Companion finite checks.}
Several companion finite runs constrain the surrounding interpretation.  A structural-order audit over the same nine coordinates found exactly one fold-level sign-flip survivor: $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip value $0.03125$; the other eight axes did not pass that gate.  A cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; it reported $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and a negative description-length change of $-258.6010084301837$ bits, so the strong selection-imprint claim was not supported.  A mass-balance run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals had $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  A turnover audit over $3{,}290$ genes similarly found no positive residual claim beyond its controls: $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  By contrast, an observable execution decomposition in yeast retained a bounded positive subblock over $3{,}289$ genes, reporting that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  These companion records show why this chapter is framed as a finite modeled-contact boundary rather than as a broad biological law.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite matrix is ready for inclusion as a modeled tAI survival-boundary record.  Its positive content is precise: nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates were evaluated against a three-organism modeled tAI contact; all three organism payloads supplied complete sense-codon modeled weights; and the resulting bounded matrix had $81$ entries above $0.01$ with maximum absolute entry $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$.  Its negative content is equally important.  Since $n=3$ is below the registered powered-survival threshold of $40$ matched species, this record does not establish measured translation survival.  No measured ribosome translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universal biological regularity follows from this chapter unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that evidence and a controlled statistic is computed on the joined rows.  Protein abundance, structure, turnover, localization, and function remain downstream or separate surfaces; a direct residual-to-protein association is not admissible here without the intervening controlled translation-readout step.
