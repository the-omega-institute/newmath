\subsection{Modeled tAI Contact for the Q6 Translation-Survival Matrix}\label{sec:bior-eality-q6-modeled-tai-contact}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record fixes a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The first coordinate $q_i$ is one of the computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, the second coordinate $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and the third coordinate $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout when such a downstream row is available.  In the present finite run, the active contact is not a proteomics row and not a ribosome-profiling row.  It is a modeled tAI readout $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy input and dos Reis wobble weights.  The readout is therefore a bounded translation-adaptation contact, not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement and not a protein-output measurement.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates and control policy.}
The computed residual list contains nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates.  Amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the M-density baseline remain controls.  They are not promoted into residual coordinates.  A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is also not admissible here: a downstream protein-abundance claim would first require a surviving $q_i\to t_j$ translation step and then a matched $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$ mediation row with mRNA abundance controlled.

\paragraph{Organism rows and tRNA-family coverage.}
The run uses $3$ organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  For the modeled tAI construction, all three organism payloads contain all $20$ standard amino-acid tRNA families.  The \emph{E. coli} K-12 payload has $89$ FASTA headers, of which $87$ match the standard parsing route and $2$ remain unmatched.  The human payload has $432$ FASTA headers, all $432$ matched.  The yeast payload has $275$ FASTA headers, all $275$ matched.  The modeled tAI readout covers all $61$ sense codons in each organism, with zero raw-weight fallback count equal to $0$ for all three organisms.

\paragraph{Modeled coupling statistic.}
The bounded coupling threshold is $0.01$.  At that threshold, the modeled tAI matrix has $81$ entries above threshold.  The maximum absolute matrix entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is the same value:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_i| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers certify a nonzero modeled tAI contact between the finite residual list and codon-adaptation weights.  They do not certify powered survival in the biological sense.  The finite table has $n=3$ organisms, while the declared powered-survival route requires at least $40$ matched species before population-scale survival is requested.

\paragraph{Break condition.}
The break condition for this run is: the packet would need more data if the finite modeled-tAI matrix had no $|S_{ij}|$ above the bounded-contact threshold, or if powered survival were requested without at least $40$ matched species.  The first branch is not met, because $81$ entries exceed $0.01$.  The second branch remains active as a scope limit, because the organism count is $3$.  Thus the finite result is classified as modeled translation realization underpowered for survival, not as ribosome-profiling validation, proteomics validation, or a population law.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The admissible reading is a controlled matrix from $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates to modeled tAI weights.  The matrix may be used to say that the residual packet reaches a bounded translation-adaptation readout under the stated finite construction.  It may not be used to say that ribosomes were observed, that translation efficiency was measured, that protein abundance was mediated, that folding or physical admissibility followed, that molecular function was realized, that a mechanism was established, or that a universal biological rule was found.  Each of those stronger readings requires its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.

\paragraph{Mediation status.}
The intended full mediation surface is $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$, with matched mRNA abundance used to separate translation-layer signal from transcription-layer signal.  The present evidence stops before that surface is filled.  It supplies modeled tAI rows, codon-space coverage, organism counts, tRNA-family coverage, and a nonzero bounded coupling matrix.  It does not yet supply matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency rows, matched proteomics abundance rows, or matched mRNA-control rows joined to the same coding-sequence identifiers.  Therefore a direct residual-to-protein association remains blocked unless a positive translation step is first demonstrated under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Finite verdict.}
For experiment run $4250\mathrm{daa}2488\mathrm{f}$, the finite data support the following bounded statement: among nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and a $61$-sense-codon modeled tAI readout over $3$ organisms, the contact matrix has $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold, with maximum absolute value $3801961.031607896$.  This is enough for a modeled tAI contact record and not enough for powered translation survival, ribosome dynamics, protein-abundance mediation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
