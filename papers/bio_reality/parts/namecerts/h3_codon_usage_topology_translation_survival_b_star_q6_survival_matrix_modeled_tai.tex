\subsection{Modeled tAI survival and translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-modeled-tai-protein-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object considered here is a row-indexed mediation surface whose coordinates are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ axis consists of nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates computed from coding sequences:
$\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$.  The translation coordinate used in the finite run is modeled per-protein tAI, computed from GtRNAdb all-tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein coordinate is matched protein abundance, read as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ after joining measured PAXdb abundance to real CDS codon counts.  Thus a direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not counted as protein realization by itself; it is only inspected after the same row has a declared translation readout $t_j$ and after the translation-conditioned decomposition is computed.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The bounded modeled-tAI contact covers three organisms in the first translation run: $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$, $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, and $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$.  In all three cases the modeled tAI table contains all $61$ sense codons.  The raw tAI-weight construction needed no zero-weight fallback in any of the three organisms.  The local GtRNAdb payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism; the header counts are $89$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}$, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast, with matched FASTA header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$ respectively.  At the bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, the finite modeled matrix has $81$ entries above threshold.  Its largest absolute entry is $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$, and the largest absolute diagonal entry has the same value.  These numbers show nonzero bounded coupling to modeled tAI, but the organism count is only $3$, so the run is explicitly not a powered multi-organism survival claim.

\paragraph{Mediation design.}
The powered cross-layer run uses six organism-matched CDS/protein panels.  The joined row counts are $14{,}410$ for $\mathrm{Danio\ rerio}$, $3{,}739$ for $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}$, $12{,}418$ for $\mathrm{Gallus\ gallus}$, $19{,}053$ for $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $4{,}129$ for $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis\ str\ MC2\ 155}$, and $5{,}815$ for $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$.  Each organism uses the same nine-dimensional $Q$ block and a one-dimensional modeled-tAI translation readout.  After residualizing by the control block, the ranks are stable across organisms: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{XT})=10$.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are $14{,}387$, $3{,}716$, $12{,}395$, $19{,}030$, $4{,}106$, and $5{,}792$, respectively.  The control matrix has $24$ columns in each panel and residual rank $23$ after degeneracy handling.  This is the finite-row reason the six-organism mediation matrix is admissible as a statistical decomposition rather than a rank-saturated slogan.

\paragraph{Readback rule.}
The claim supported by these rows is narrow.  The $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a nonzero bounded modeled-tAI coupling in the three-organism translation surface, and the six-organism joined CDS/protein panels yield a rank-sufficient statistical decomposition in which modeled tAI absorbs a positive fraction of the $Q\to P$ association in every organism.  The readback is therefore a controlled matrix $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ with $T_j$ equal to modeled per-protein tAI and $P_k$ equal to matched protein abundance.  It is not a claim that synonymous residuals cause translation, that modeled tAI is observed ribosome occupancy, or that the same signal realizes folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter keeps five separations explicit.  First, amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline remain controls rather than promoted coordinates.  Second, matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control and not itself a translation readout.  Third, protein abundance is a downstream steady-state measurement affected by turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, and pathway context; it is not isolated protein realization.  Fourth, the modeled tAI readout is built from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights, so it is weaker than ribosome profiling or measured translation efficiency.  Fifth, no higher biological layer follows from this finite matrix unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant rows and a new controlled statistic is computed.  Under those restrictions, the finite evidence is ready for a BioReality record at the bounded modeled-translation and statistical protein-mediation level.
