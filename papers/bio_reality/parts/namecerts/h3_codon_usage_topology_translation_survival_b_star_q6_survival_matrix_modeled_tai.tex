\subsection{Modeled Translation Contact for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Residual Surface}
\label{subsec:bioq6-modeled-tai-translation-contact}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This BioReality record treats the synonymous-residual coordinates of $B^{*}_{Q6}$ as a finite row system whose admissible empirical reading is mediated through translation-layer readouts.  The coordinate list used in the modeled translation contact has nine rows,
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}$,
computed over the $61$ sense-codon count space.  These rows are not read directly as protein output.  A residual coordinate $q_i$ may enter a protein-abundance statement only through a row of the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ is a translation readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $P_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The amino-acid composition, organism label, GC3, coding-sequence length, and $M$-density baseline remain controls.  Matched mRNA abundance is also a control when the readout is meant to separate translation from transcription.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI contact.}
The finite translation contact used here is the modeled tAI codon-weight surface obtained from local GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  Three organisms are present: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tRNA payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The \emph{E. coli} K12 file has $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers; the human file has $432$ headers, all matched; the yeast file has $275$ headers, all matched.  In each organism the modeled tAI readout supplies weights for all $61$ sense codons, and the zero-raw-$W$ fallback count is $0$ for all three organisms.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
The modeled-tAI matrix is nonzero at the declared bounded-contact threshold.  With threshold $0.01$, the computation reports $81$ entries above threshold.  The largest absolute modeled coupling is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j} |S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the largest absolute diagonal entry is the same value.  The break condition for this contact is not met: the matrix is not empty above threshold.  The contact nevertheless remains underpowered as a survival claim, because the organism count is only $3$, while a powered cross-organism survival request requires at least $40$ matched species.  The correct status is therefore bounded modeled translation coupling, not measured ribosome validation and not proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Confirmatory boundary check.}
The earlier independent genetic-code table contact remains a lower-layer confirmation of codon geometry only.  It reports $40$ successes out of $40$ trials in the boundary probe, with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and the independent replication also reports $40$ successes out of $40$ with the same value.  The negative-control panel has $20$ trials with $0$ unexpected successes, and the critical ablation panel also has $20$ trials with $0$ unexpected successes.  This confirms that the codon-level geometry is not an artifact of the particular held-out table contact, but it does not raise the result into translation, protein abundance, structure, or function.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface has a nonzero bounded modeled-tAI contact over three organisms, and a separate matched protein-abundance mediation calculation over twelve organisms shows positive modeled-tAI-conditioned mediation fractions in finite rows.  These are statistical contacts with controlled codon and sequence covariates.  They do not establish observed ribosome occupancy, translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological rule.  Any such stronger statement requires its own layer-matched measurement and its own controlled statistic.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is therefore not promoted unless the same coordinate first survives a translation-readout step $Q_i\to T_j$ under the declared controls.
