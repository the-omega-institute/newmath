\subsection{Modeled tAI survival surface for $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-usage packet as a finite row surface rather than as a free biological assertion.  The carrier consists of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ ranges over translation readouts, and $p_k$ ranges over matched protein-abundance readouts.  The admissible reading is mediated: a residual coordinate may be compared with a protein-abundance row only after the same coordinate has first been tested against a translation-layer readout.  Direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is therefore not enough to promote the row to protein realization.

The finite residual list used in the modeled tAI run has nine coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6} = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count space has $61$ sense codons.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline are controls.  They remain background covariates and are not promoted into additional residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
The translation readout in the finite run is modeled codon tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The organism panel has $n=3$: \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Each organism supplies all $61$ sense-codon weights, and no zero raw weight fallback was needed in this modeled-tAI surface:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{zeroFallback}(\textit{E. coli})=0,\\
&\mathrm{zeroFallback}(\textit{H. sapiens})=0,\\
&\mathrm{zeroFallback}(\textit{S. cerevisiae})=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The local tRNA payload also covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The FASTA header counts are $89$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast.  The matched header counts are $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively, leaving two unmatched headers only in the \textit{E. coli} K-12 payload.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
With threshold $0.01$, the modeled tAI matrix has nonzero bounded contact.  There are $81$ entries above threshold, and the maximum absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| = 3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The maximum absolute diagonal value is the same number,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_i| = 3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for a purely empty modeled surface is therefore not met: the finite modeled-tAI contact is not zero at the declared bounded threshold.  This supports only a modeled codon-adaptation coupling statement.  It does not, by itself, establish measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, selection, organismal phenotype, or a population-level law.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The same finite result is underpowered for a population survival claim.  The organism count is $3$, while the declared powered-survival route requires at least $40$ matched species before the surface can be read as a powered multi-organism translation-survival matrix.  The appropriate classification is therefore translation realization modeled under insufficient organism-level power.  The positive modeled entries justify retaining the residual coordinates at the bounded translation-readout interface, but not promoting them to ribosome-profiling validation or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Matched protein-abundance mediation audit.}
A second finite table joins real coding sequences, modeled per-protein tAI, and measured PAXdb protein abundance for three organism-scoped row sets.  The joined protein counts are $3739$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for \textit{S. cerevisiae}.  The readout $T$ is per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T = \frac{\sum_c \mathrm{count}(c)\,w_c}{\sum_c \mathrm{count}(c)},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $w_c$ is the modeled codon weight.  The protein readout $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to observed CDS codon counts.

After residualizing against the declared controls, the control rank is $23$ with $24$ control columns in each organism table.  The residual degrees of freedom are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$ for \textit{E. coli}, \textit{M. smegmatis}, and yeast.  The residualized $Q$ rank is $9$, the residualized translation rank is $1$, and the combined residualized $(Q,T)$ rank is $10$ in all three tables, so the finite linear decompositions are rank-admissible for this modeled statistic.

\paragraph{Mediation fractions.}
The modeled mediation scalar $M^{QTP}$ lies in the unit interval for all three organisms.  The finite fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\textit{E. coli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
M^{QTP}_{\textit{M. smegmatis}} &= 0.08397602919861291,\\
M^{QTP}_{\textit{S. cerevisiae}} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding total $Q \to P$ $R^2$ values are $0.10767526677985896$, $0.03209009170649934$, and $0.29837624994140827$.  After conditioning on modeled $T$, the direct $Q \to P$ $R^2$ values are $0.04167869969498872$, $0.029395293228368185$, and $0.0151709093608293$.  Thus the modeled indirect components are
$$
\begin{aligned}
0.06599656708487024,\quad 0.0026947984781311544,\quad 0.28320534058057895.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values say that modeled per-protein tAI can account for a finite portion of the residual-to-abundance association in the joined tables, especially in yeast and \textit{E. coli}.  They do not say that synonymous residuals causally alter abundance, that tAI is an observed ribosome trace, or that abundance is isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The scientifically admissible statement is narrow.  In a finite modeled-tAI contact, the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have $81$ above-threshold entries in a $61$-codon readout construction across three organisms.  In separate matched CDS/protein tables, modeled per-protein tAI gives unit-interval mediation fractions for the $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition.  These facts justify a bounded modeled translation-readout and modeled protein-abundance mediation record.  They do not justify a measured translation claim, a direct protein-realization claim, or any higher biological claim.  Translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, causal mechanism, phenotype, and universality each require their own layer-matched measurement contact and a statistic computed under the declared controls.
