\subsection{Modeled tAI contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation boundary}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats synonymous $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates as a finite biological-contact surface rather than as direct biological realization. The coordinate list is the nine-dimensional panel
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout used here is not a ribosome-footprint measurement. It is a modeled codon-level tAI weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble $s$-value rule. The codon count space has $61$ sense codons. The finite organism panel has $n=3$ entries: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The local GtRNAdb payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism. The FASTA accounting is $89$ headers with $87$ matched and $2$ unmatched in \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ headers with $432$ matched in human, and $275$ headers with $275$ matched in yeast. For the modeled tAI computation itself, each of the three organisms has $61$ sense codons and zero raw-$W$ fallback count $0$.

\paragraph{Modeled coupling statistic.}
The computed matrix is a bounded modeled-tAI coupling matrix on $Q$ against codon-adaptation readouts. With threshold $0.01$, the matrix has $81$ entries above threshold. Its maximum absolute matrix entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}|=3{,}801{,}961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is the same number. This is a positive finite-contact result for a modeled readout: it shows that the chosen $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not numerically silent against the organism-scoped tAI construction. It does not by itself show measured translation survival. The registered break condition is therefore not met for the bounded modeled contact, because the finite modeled-tAI matrix has nonzero entries above the declared contact threshold. The same record also states the limiting condition: a powered survival claim is not available with only $3$ matched organisms, and powered survival would require at least $40$ matched species or a separate measured-readout design with adequate rank.

\paragraph{Layer separation.}
The admissible reading is narrow. A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ protein-abundance association is not used here as evidence for protein realization unless the same coordinate first passes through a translation readout $T_j$. The modeled readout $T_j$ in this subsection is tAI; it is distinct from ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency measured as RPF/mRNA, and matched proteomics. Matched mRNA abundance is a control for transcription-layer separation rather than a translation readout. Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline remain controls. They are not promoted to coordinates in this contact.

\paragraph{Downstream comparison without promotion.}
Separate downstream audits illustrate why the present chapter keeps the tAI matrix bounded. In the larger translation-mediated protein-abundance audit, $12$ organisms were joined to CDS and protein-abundance rows: $4042$ proteins for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for zebrafish, $13295$ for fly, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In that separate protein-abundance decomposition, the mediated fractions ranged from $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, with all reported fractions in the unit interval. Those numbers belong to a different finite surface with protein abundance joined by organism and protein identifiers. They are not imported into the present modeled-tAI contact as proof of ribosome dynamics.

The same caution is supported by additional audits. A yeast five-way structure-mediation join had $4759$ joined rows and reported $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$; the structure-only fraction was only $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$. A structural-order axis audit identified $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ as the only fold-level significant axis, with sign-flip $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$. A separate observable-execution audit in yeast used $3289$ genes and reached an observable-execution subblock verdict, while the evolutionary selection-imprint audit over $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species did not support the strong selection-imprint claim: its leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2$ was $-0.0021477729853302424$, below the null reference maximum $0.05427157066522958$. The mass-balance audit on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals had $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, and the turnover audit on $3290$ genes had $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$. These neighboring finite results make the boundary explicit: modeled tAI contact can be real as a bounded readout while stronger translation, protein, structure, turnover, or evolutionary interpretations may remain null or separately conditioned.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The present finite-row result is therefore accepted only as modeled-tAI bounded coupling for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinate panel. It supports the statement that the nine residual coordinates have nonzero contact with a codon-adaptation weight surface computed from three organism-specific tRNA-copy payloads. It does not establish measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality. Any such claim requires a separate layer-matched contact: measured RPF and matched mRNA rows for translation efficiency, matched proteomics for protein abundance, structural or physical assays for structure and admissibility, perturbation or functional assays for function and phenotype, and multi-organism controlled designs before a population-level law is asserted.
