\subsection{Modeled tAI survival for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record is a finite cross-layer test for synonymous residual coordinates that remain after amino-acid identity and simpler sequence summaries have been kept as controls. The carrier is the finite surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ ranges over the available $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, $t_j$ ranges over translation readouts, and $p_k$ ranges over matched protein-abundance readouts. The current row does not yet contain matched proteomics rows, so the realized finite computation stops at the modeled translation-readout face. The residual coordinates actually computed are
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense codons. The modeled readout is the codon adaptation weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy records with dos Reis wobble $s$-values. Thus the finite object tested here is not a direct claim about ribosome traffic, protein abundance, folding, physical realizability, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or a universal biological law. It is a bounded modeled-tAI contact for asking whether any $q_i$ has nonzero coupling to a translation-proxy coordinate before a downstream protein-abundance test is allowed.

\paragraph{Organism rows and tRNA coverage.}
The finite organism set has size $3$, consisting of \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. For modeled tAI, all three organism rows produced weights for all $61$ sense codons, and each row had zero raw-weight fallback count $0$. The local GtRNAdb payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism. The row-level header counts were $89$ FASTA headers in \emph{E. coli} K-12, of which $87$ matched the standard parsing route and $2$ remained unmatched; $432$ headers in human, all $432$ matched; and $275$ headers in yeast, all $275$ matched. In symbols, the checked coverage data are
$$
\begin{aligned}
&n_{\mathrm{org}}=3,\qquad n_{\mathrm{sense}}=61,\qquad n_{\mathrm{aa\ family}}=20,\\
&\mathrm{fallback}_{\mathrm{Ecoli}}=\mathrm{fallback}_{\mathrm{human}}=\mathrm{fallback}_{\mathrm{yeast}}=0,\\
&\mathrm{headers}_{\mathrm{Ecoli}}=(89,87,2),\quad
\mathrm{headers}_{\mathrm{human}}=(432,432,0),\quad
\mathrm{headers}_{\mathrm{yeast}}=(275,275,0),
\end{aligned}
$$
where each header triple records total, matched, and unmatched FASTA headers. These numbers make the modeled tAI matrix finite and reproducible at the level of codon weights, but they do not provide organism-matched ribosome profiling, matched mRNA residualization, or matched protein abundance.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling statistic.}
For the modeled-tAI face, the bounded coupling threshold was $0.01$. The computed matrix had $81$ entries above this threshold, with maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}|=3{,}801{,}961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the same value was attained on the reported diagonal maximum $\max_i |S_i|$. The finite-row result therefore satisfies the weak modeled-contact condition: the tAI-derived surface is not identically zero above the declared threshold. The break condition for this row is met only if the finite modeled-tAI matrix has no $|S_{ij}|$ above threshold, or if powered survival is demanded without at least $40$ matched species. Since $81$ entries exceed $0.01$, the zero-contact part of the break condition is false. Since only $3$ organisms are present, the powered-survival part remains unavailable. The appropriate classification is therefore modeled translation realization underpowered by species count, not ribosome-profiling validation and not proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Mediation rule.}
The admissible biological reading is ordered. A residual coordinate $q_i$ may be carried forward only through a tested translation readout $t_j$. A direct association $q_i\to p_k$ with protein abundance is not accepted as protein realization unless the intermediate step $q_i\to t_j$ survives under the declared controls. The intended controlled comparison is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance kept in the control set. In the present finite row, $t_j$ is modeled tAI; ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from RPF divided by mRNA, and matched protein abundance remain named measurement routes but are not populated by matched rows. Consequently the row supports a finite modeled-tAI coupling matrix and blocks promotion to a protein-abundance mediation claim.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter-level conclusion is narrow. The computation shows that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual list has nonzero bounded coupling to modeled tAI weights in a $3$-organism table with complete sense-codon weight coverage and complete standard amino-acid family coverage. It does not show powered translation survival, because $3<40$ matched species. It does not show observed ribosome occupancy or translation efficiency, because no organism-matched ribosome-profiling rows are present. It does not show protein realization, because no matched proteomics matrix is present and no surviving $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$ mediation entry has been estimated. It also does not show folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality. Any one of those stronger readings requires a separate measurement contact at that layer and a new controlled finite-row statistic rather than promotion from the modeled-tAI coupling matrix.
