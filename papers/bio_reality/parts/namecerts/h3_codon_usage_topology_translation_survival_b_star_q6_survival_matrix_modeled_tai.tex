\subsection{Modeled tAI survival matrix for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:biorality-q6-modeled-tai-survival-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object in this chapter is the matrix of controlled contacts between nine synonymous residual coordinates and an organism-scoped modeled translation-readout surface. The row coordinates are
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.
The translation readout is not a ribosome-footprint observation. It is a per-codon modeled tAI weight $w_c$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values. The codon universe has $61$ sense codons, and the organism panel has only three organisms: \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12, \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \textit{Homo sapiens}. This makes the surface suitable for bounded modeled contact, but not for a powered multi-organism biological survival claim.

\paragraph{Readout construction.}
For each organism $o$ and sense codon $c$, the modeled translation coordinate is a scalar $w_{o,c}$. The finite matrix tests whether a residual coordinate $q_i$ has a nonzero bounded coupling to this modeled translation surface after the codon-count coordinate has been reduced to the declared synonymous residual directions. The diagnostic threshold used here is $0.01$. The run reports $81$ matrix entries above this threshold, with maximum absolute entry
$3801961.031607896$ and the same maximum on the diagonal summary. In compact form the reported bounded-contact statistic is
$$\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{org}} &= 3,\\
N_{\mathrm{sense\ codon}} &= 61,\\
N_{|S_{ij}|>0.01} &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}$$
The break condition for the modeled packet was not met: the finite modeled-tAI matrix does contain entries above the bounded-contact threshold. The separate powered-survival condition is not satisfied, because the panel has $3$ organisms rather than at least $40$ matched species. Thus the conclusion is a bounded modeled tAI coupling, not a measured ribosome, proteomics, or cross-species survival result.

\paragraph{tRNA payload audit.}
The tRNA source is complete at the amino-acid-family level for all three organisms. The \textit{E. coli} K-12 payload has $89$ FASTA headers, of which $87$ are matched to standard tRNA families, with $2$ unmatched headers and all $20$ standard amino-acid families represented. The yeast payload has $275$ FASTA headers, all $275$ matched, with no unmatched headers and all $20$ standard amino-acid families represented. The human payload has $432$ FASTA headers, all $432$ matched, with no unmatched headers and all $20$ standard amino-acid families represented. The modeled tAI computation reports $61$ sense codons for each of the three organisms and zero raw-weight fallback count in each case. These numbers matter because the positive matrix entries are not created by a missing-family artifact in the tRNA table.

\paragraph{Boundary of the modeled claim.}
The modeled contact is a legitimate translation-layer annotation only in a narrow sense: it supplies codon adaptation weights derived from tRNA copy number and wobble rules. It does not observe ribosome dwell time, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from RPF divided by mRNA, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality. A direct residual-to-protein association is therefore not admissible from this matrix alone. Any downstream protein statement must first pass through a same-row translation readout $T_j$ and then into a matched protein readout $P_k$ under controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, the $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance.

\paragraph{Relation to the larger protein-abundance mediation table.}
A later finite mediation table uses the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates with per-protein modeled tAI and measured protein abundance. That table is stronger as a row count audit, not as causal proof. It joins $12$ organisms: $4042$ proteins for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} strain 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \textit{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In that matrix the modeled tAI mediated fractions lie in the unit interval for all organisms. They range from $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast. The yeast row has total residual-to-protein $R^2=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2$ after conditioning on modeled tAI equal to $0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$. Human has total $R^2=0.04454565118019448$, direct $R^2=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect difference $0.012119218668341744$. Mouse has total $R^2=0.049827984062539195$, direct $R^2=0.04211022731323921$, and indirect difference $0.007717756749299982$. These rows support the existence of a finite statistical mediation surface, but the readout remains modeled tAI plus steady-state abundance, not a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Yeast structure and abundance cross-check.}
The yeast five-way table gives a useful negative boundary for overinterpretation. It joins $4759$ rows across coding sequence, protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a 5-prime coding-region folding proxy. The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the full join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$. After residualizing controls, the protein-abundance association of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates has $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$. Conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy gives $R^2_{QP|S}=0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability gives $R^2_{QP|T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gives $R^2_{QP|T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, and $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ is $0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$. The structure-incremental right-tail permutation value is $p=0.1258741258741259$. This cross-check says that a sequence-derived structure proxy can be recorded as a finite covariate, but it does not convert modeled tAI coupling into folding, structure realization, or physical admissibility.

\paragraph{Structural-order coordinate audit.}
A separate held-out structural-order audit identifies exactly one significant coordinate among the same nine residual axes. The dominant loading and dominant incremental axis is $f3\_stress$, with weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, held-out weighted-delta $R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, and sign-flip right-tail value $p=0.03125$. The other coordinates do not pass the fold-level sign-flip gate: for example $Arg\_AGR$ has weighted-delta $R^2=0.0020139270616115154$ with $p=0.142578125$, $Ile\_AUA$ has $R^2=0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, and $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$ is negative at $-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$. The matrix therefore has a specific structural-order coordinate signal, but that signal is not promoted to a statement about folded proteins or function.

\paragraph{Earlier genetic-code contact.}
The phase-one codon-geometry contact supplies an independent baseline for finite-table behavior. It reports $40$ successes out of $40$ trials in the boundary probe and the same $40$ out of $40$ in the independent replication statistic, each with one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$. The negative control has $0$ unexpected successes out of $20$, and the critical ablation has $0$ unexpected successes out of $20$. Those values certify a curated genetic-code table contact for codon-window geometry. They do not certify translation, protein structure, physical admissibility, biological function, or a global biological rule.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite-row result is ready as a modeled translation-readout chapter. The correct local statement is that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nonzero bounded coupling matrix against modeled tAI weights in a three-organism tRNA-copy panel, with $81$ entries above $0.01$ and no raw-weight fallback in the modeled codon weights. The correct local refusal is equally important: $N_{\mathrm{org}}=3$ is not powered survival, modeled tAI is not observed ribosome profiling, and no protein, folding, physical-admissibility, function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universal-law claim follows without a separate matched contact and a controlled same-row statistic.
