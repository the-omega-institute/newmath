\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival Matrix and Translation-Conditioned Protein-Abundance Mediation}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-modeled-tai-protein-abundance-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}$.
The translation coordinate $t_j$ used in the finite computation is modeled codon tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream protein coordinate $p_k$ is matched protein abundance, read as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ in the joined protein-abundance rows.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  It is the controlled chain
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where the first arrow is a translation-readout survival or bounded modeled-readout step, and the second arrow is evaluated only after the translation coordinate is included in the conditioning set.

\paragraph{Modeled translation-contact computation.}
The modeled-tAI contact was computed on the $61$ sense-codon count space for three organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local GtRNAdb payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid tRNA families for each organism.  The header counts were $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast; the matched header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively, leaving $2$, $0$, and $0$ unmatched headers.  The modeled codon readout covered all $61$ sense codons in each of the three organisms, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in all three modeled-readout tables.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
The finite modeled-tAI matrix has nonzero bounded contact: $81$ entries exceed the coupling threshold $0.01$.  The maximum absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is the same number.  The break condition for the modeled contact is not met: the finite modeled-tAI matrix does contain entries above the bounded-contact threshold.  This does not upgrade the result to powered translation survival, because the same finite record also states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival and that powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the classification of this component is modeled translation realization underpowered: it is a bounded modeled readout with strong finite coupling values, not a ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation rows.}
The cross-layer protein-abundance computation uses the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and joins real CDS codon counts to measured protein abundance.  Ten organism-level joined tables are present.  The row counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for yeast.  Each organism has $24$ control columns, residualized rank $23$ for the control matrix, rank $9$ for the residual coordinate block, rank $1$ for the modeled-tAI readout, and rank $10$ for the joint residual coordinate plus modeled-tAI block.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls are respectively $4019$, $11638$, $14387$, $13272$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $19372$, $4106$, and $5792$.

\paragraph{Energy decomposition.}
The controlled residualized energy audit confirms that the translation coordinate and the nine residual coordinates are not numerically interchangeable.  For example, in yeast the modeled-tAI readout explains $941.8858618613751$ units of protein-abundance residual energy, the residual coordinates explain $882.6526210803958$ units on protein abundance, and the incremental modeled-tAI improvement given the residual coordinates is $104.11162139652299$.  In human, the corresponding values are $850.7864327764183$, $1370.6490494847067$, and $477.88368900318414$.  In mouse they are $923.4025869376133$, $2431.9284360407714$, and $546.7260575899445$.  These numbers justify keeping the finite object as a mediated matrix rather than collapsing it to a direct $q_i \to p_k$ association.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite data support a bounded modeled-tAI coupling result and a statistical translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation decomposition over ten organism-level protein-abundance tables.  They do not establish a measured ribosome-occupancy result, a causal translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law.  A direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is not promoted unless a $q_i \to t_j$ translation-readout step is present and retained under the declared controls.  A measured translation claim would require an independent ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency contact joined to matched mRNA abundance and codon counts; higher-layer claims would require their own layer-matched assays.
