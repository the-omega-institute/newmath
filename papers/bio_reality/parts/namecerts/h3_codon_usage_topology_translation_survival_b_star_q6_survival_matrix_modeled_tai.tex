\subsection{Modeled tAI survival surface for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival-surface}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite record considered here has rows indexed by three bounded biological slots: a residual coordinate $q_i$ in the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis, a modeled translation-readout coordinate $t_j$, and a later protein-abundance coordinate $p_k$ when such a row is explicitly joined.  For the present matrix the realized readout is narrower: $t_j$ is the per-codon modeled tAI weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble weights.  The computed residual list has nine coordinates,
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon table contributes $61$ sense-codon coordinates.  The organism panel has $n=3$ members: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and \emph{Homo sapiens}.  Their local GtRNAdb payloads contain all $20$ standard amino-acid families.  The FASTA header counts are $89$, $275$, and $432$, with matched-header counts $87$, $275$, and $432$ respectively; the only unmatched headers in this panel are the two recorded for \emph{E. coli} K12.

\paragraph{Readout construction.}
For each organism, the modeled tAI computation produced weights for all $61$ sense codons.  The zero-raw-$W$ fallback count was $0$ in all three organisms, so the finite surface does not depend on geometric-mean imputation at this stage.  The matrix therefore tests whether the nine residual coordinates have bounded coupling to an explicitly modeled codon-adaptation readout, not whether ribosomes were directly observed.  With coupling threshold $0.01$, the modeled matrix has $81$ entries above threshold.  The maximum absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is the same number.  The break condition for this finite panel is not met: the matrix is not empty under the bounded-contact threshold.  Its declared layer is nevertheless only modeled and underpowered, because $3$ organisms do not satisfy the powered-survival requirement of at least $40$ matched species.

\paragraph{Controls and admissible interpretation.}
The admissible comparison keeps amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline as controls rather than as promoted biological coordinates.  A positive modeled tAI entry may be read as finite coupling between the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis and a codon-adaptation weight surface.  It is not a measured ribosome-occupancy result, not a protein-output result, and not a mechanism.  Matched mRNA abundance remains a transcription-layer control for separating translation-efficiency readouts from transcript abundance.  Matched proteomics remains a later protein-abundance contact and cannot be substituted for the $q_i \to t_j$ step.  Thus a direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is not admitted as protein realization unless the same coordinate first passes a controlled translation-readout step.

\paragraph{Cross-layer context.}
A larger matched-protein matrix exists for the same nine coordinates and $12$ organisms.  In that matrix the joined protein counts range from $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ for mouse, with $19053$ human proteins and $5815$ yeast proteins.  The statistical mediation fractions $M^{QTP}$ are positive and inside the unit interval for all $12$ organisms: examples include $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}.  These values support a bounded decomposition of modeled tAI and protein abundance after controls, but they still do not turn the three-organism modeled tAI survival surface into a measured ribosome-profiling result.  The yeast structure-mediation table further illustrates the same separation: for $4759$ joined genes, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, while the structure-only fraction is only $0.003284564452662019$ and the incremental structure fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$.  Those numbers are variance partitions, not causal pathway proofs.

\paragraph{Negative and boundary checks.}
Several finite audits delimit the interpretation.  The structural-order audit over five folds identifies $f_3$-stress as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and right-tail sign-flip $p=0.03125$; this is a structural-order association, not folding realization.  The observable execution audit in yeast reports $3289$ genes and a positive subblock verdict for at least one rate-proxy slot, but it is still read at the proxy-slot level.  The mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals and found $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta DL=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$, so it does not support a stronger dynamic closure claim.  The evolutionary imprint run used $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows; it found $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, so the strong selection-imprint gate is negative.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and produced $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta DL=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$, which keeps turnover outside the present claim.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite row supports the following bounded statement.  The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual basis has a nonzero modeled tAI coupling surface over $3$ organisms, $9$ residual coordinates, and $61$ sense codons, with $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold and no zero-weight fallback in the organism-specific codon weights.  This is enough for a modeled codon-adaptation contact record.  It is not enough for powered translation survival, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, molecular mechanism, selection law, or universality.  Any such stronger claim requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic before it can enter the scientific readout.
