\subsection{Modeled tAI coupling and translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-protein-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record uses a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$.  The translation coordinate is a modeled tAI readout: per-codon $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ values were computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and dos Reis wobble $s$-values, and the per-protein readout $T$ is the codon-count-weighted mean of those tAI weights.  The protein coordinate $Y$ is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The surface is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein map.  A protein-abundance statement is admitted only after the residual coordinate is passed through the modeled translation readout and after amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, and matched abundance controls are kept as controls rather than promoted into the coordinate system.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI contact.}
The modeled tAI computation covered the 61 sense codons.  In the organism-level tRNA payload, $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli}$ K-12 had 89 FASTA headers, 87 matched headers, 2 unmatched headers, and all 20 standard amino-acid families represented; human had 432 headers, 432 matched headers, no unmatched headers, and all 20 standard amino-acid families represented; budding yeast had 275 headers, 275 matched headers, no unmatched headers, and all 20 standard amino-acid families represented.  The modeled tAI readout had zero raw-$W$ fallback count 0 for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ K-12, human, and budding yeast.  On this bounded contact, the computed matrix had 81 entries above the coupling threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute score $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$ and the same maximum on the diagonal entry.  The break condition was not met for the bounded modeled-tAI contact, but the organism count was only $3$, so this is not a powered cross-species translation-survival result and not a ribosome-profiling measurement.

\paragraph{Joined protein-abundance rows.}
The protein-abundance mediation computation used three organism-level joined CDS/protein panels.  The $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ K-12 MG1655 panel joined 3739 proteins to CDS codon counts and used the $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ tRNA organism; the $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis}$ str. MC2 155 panel joined 4129 proteins to CDS codon counts and used the same tRNA organism; the $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ panel joined 5815 proteins to CDS codon counts and used the yeast tRNA organism.  The corresponding all-tRNA copy totals were 90, 49, and 275.  One zero raw-$W$ value was filled by the geometric mean in $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ and in $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$, while yeast required no such fill.  The design used a joint 9-dimensional $X_Q$ block, a one-dimensional modeled-tAI $T$ block, and a protein-abundance $Y$ response after residualizing the declared control set.

\paragraph{Rank and residual energy.}
After controls, all three joined panels were rank-sufficient for the stated finite regression surface.  In $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, the control rank was 23 from 24 control columns, the residual degrees of freedom after controls were 3716, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde T)=10$.  Its residual energy values were $\|\widetilde T\|^2=0.4728468725814029$ and $\|\widetilde Y\|^2=4020.333671802061$.  In $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$, the corresponding residual degrees of freedom were 4106, with the same $9,1,10$ ranks and residual energies $0.041191576348982066$ and $5729.194538214237$.  In yeast, the residual degrees of freedom were 5792, again with ranks $9,1,10$, and residual energies $6.90537888244409$ and $2958.1865890925337$.  The finite-rank condition therefore permits a statistical decomposition of residual-to-protein association through the modeled translation coordinate, but it does not by itself determine causality.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
The mediation scalar $M^{QTP}$ is read as a controlled statistical decomposition, not as a molecular mechanism.  For $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ K-12 MG1655, the total residual-to-protein $R^2$ was $c=0.10767526677985896$, the direct residual-to-protein value after conditioning on modeled tAI was $c'=0.04167869969498872$, the indirect difference was $0.06599656708487024$, and the mediated fraction was $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  For $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$, the corresponding values were $c=0.03209009170649934$, $c'=0.029395293228368185$, indirect difference $0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.  For yeast, they were $c=0.29837624994140827$, $c'=0.0151709093608293$, indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Each mediated fraction lies in the unit interval.  The organism-specific magnitudes are heterogeneous: the yeast panel shows a large modeled-tAI-conditioned attenuation of the direct residual-to-protein association, the $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ panel shows a moderate attenuation, and the $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$ panel shows only a small attenuation.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite result supports the following bounded statement.  The nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates can be evaluated against a modeled tAI translation coordinate and a matched protein-abundance response on real CDS/protein rows.  In the three joined panels, the regression ranks are sufficient, the modeled-tAI coordinate has nonzero residual energy after controls, and the protein-abundance decomposition yields positive indirect differences in all three organisms.  This makes the record admissible as a modeled translation-conditioned protein-abundance decomposition.  It does not show measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, perturbational rescue, selection, or a universal law.  It also does not license a direct $q_i\to p_k$ biological reading when the $q_i\to t_j$ step is absent, null, or untested.  A stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as organism- and CDS-matched ribosome profiling with paired mRNA abundance for measured translation efficiency, and a separate assay for any structural, physical, functional, phenotypic, or mechanistic claim.
