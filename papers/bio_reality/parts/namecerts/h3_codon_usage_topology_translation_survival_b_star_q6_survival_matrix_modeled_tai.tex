\subsection{Modeled-tAI survival and translation-conditioned protein abundance for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The record considered here is the finite surface of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ in which $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  The nine residual coordinates are
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
They are evaluated inside the $61$-sense-codon count space.  Amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline remain covariates rather than coordinates to be promoted.  A direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is therefore not counted as protein realization unless a preceding $q_i \to t_j$ readout has survived the same control regime.

\paragraph{Three-organism modeled-tAI contact.}
The bounded modeled-tAI computation used GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights to obtain per-codon modeled tAI weights.  The finite panel contained $3$ organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The source payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each organism.  The FASTA accounting was $89$ headers with $87$ matched and $2$ unmatched for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ matched of $432$ for human, and $275$ matched of $275$ for yeast.  For all three organisms the modeled-tAI readout covered all $61$ sense codons with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
At coupling threshold $0.01$, the modeled finite matrix had $81$ entries above threshold.  The maximum absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_i^{\mathrm{diag}}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\tau_{\mathrm{coupling}} &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared break condition for this surface is not met: the finite modeled-tAI matrix is not empty above the bounded-contact threshold.  However, this is not a powered biological survival result.  The panel has only $n=3$ organisms, while powered survival for the cross-organism claim requires at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the modeled-tAI result is a bounded codon-adaptation coupling readout, not ribosome-profiling validation and not proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Powered protein-abundance mediation surface.}
A separate finite computation joined real coding-sequence codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and measured PAXdb protein abundance for $12$ organisms.  The joined row counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In each organism the rank diagnostics were sufficient after residualization by $23$ effective control columns from a $24$-column control design, with $9$ residual coordinates and rank $1$ for the modeled-tAI readout.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-side audit.}
The yeast five-way join for the structure-mediated abundance audit contained $4759$ rows.  Its reported coding-sequence join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, and its full join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$.  The structure proxy was the ViennaRNA RNAfold minimum free energy for the first $39$ nt of the coding region, not a measured folding state.  After the same style of residualization, the yeast abundance decomposition gave
$$
\begin{aligned}

The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond measured translation efficiency plus mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, the right-tail permutation value for $R^2_{S\leftarrow Q}$ was $0.027972027972027972$, and the right-tail value for the incremental structure fraction was $0.1258741258741259$.  These numbers support a narrow variance-partition statement, not a structural mechanism.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite evidence supports two distinct statements.  First, the three-organism modeled-tAI matrix is nonzero as a bounded codon-adaptation coupling surface, but it remains underpowered for a cross-organism survival claim.  Second, in the twelve-organism protein-abundance panel, the joint $B^*_{Q6}$ residual block has a measurable protein-abundance association whose direct component is reduced after conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI, with organism-specific mediated fractions listed above.  This licenses a controlled statistical mediation description of $Q\to T\to P$ for these rows.

\paragraph{Local exclusion rule.}
No claim about observed ribosome occupancy, translation mechanism, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from these finite rows alone.  Modeled tAI is a gene-copy and wobble-weight readout, not a ribosome-profile measurement.  PAXdb abundance is a steady-state protein-abundance measurement and is not isolated from mRNA abundance, turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context.  A direct residual-to-protein association is therefore kept below protein-realization status unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled translation-readout step, and every higher biological layer requires its own matched contact and its own finite statistic.
