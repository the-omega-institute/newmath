\subsection{Modeled tAI contact for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ translation boundary}
\label{sec:bior-reality-q6-modeled-tai-contact}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record fixes a finite mediation surface whose first coordinate is the list of nine synonymous residual coordinates
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}$.
The second coordinate is a modeled translation readout, not an observed ribosome trajectory: for each organism, a codon weight $w_c$ is computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  The third coordinate is reserved for matched protein-abundance rows, but no such rows are attached in this finite run.  The present contact therefore has the shape $Q_i \to T_j$ only, with $T_j$ equal to modeled tAI weights; it does not yet instantiate a controlled $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ protein-abundance mediation matrix.

The run identifier is $4250\mathrm{daa}2488\mathrm{f}$.  The codon-count space has $61$ sense codons, and the organism count is $3$.  The organisms are \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In the local GtRNAdb payload, the standard amino-acid-family count is $20$ for each organism.  The respective FASTA header counts are $89$, $432$, and $275$; the matched header counts are $87$, $432$, and $275$; the unmatched header counts are $2$, $0$, and $0$.  The modeled tAI readout covers all $61$ sense codons in each organism, and the zero-raw-$W$ fallback count is $0$ for each organism.  Thus the readout is finite, organism-scoped, and fully populated at the codon-weight level for the three organisms under consideration.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling statistic.}
Using the coupling threshold $0.01$, the modeled tAI matrix contains $81$ entries above threshold.  The maximum absolute entry is $3801961.031607896$, and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is also $3801961.031607896$.  In this chapter the symbol $S_{ij}$ names only the bounded modeled-contact score computed from this finite tAI surface.  It is not promoted to a powered survival statistic, because the run explicitly records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival.  The finite condition can be summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\{(i,j): |S_{ij}|>0.01\}| &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
N_{\mathrm{organism}} &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for the bounded modeled contact is not met: the finite modeled-tAI matrix has entries above the declared threshold.  This is a positive finite-contact observation at the codon-adaptation-weight layer, not a claim that a ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, or proteomics experiment has been discharged.

\paragraph{Controls and admissible reading.}
The admissible reading is a controlled residual-to-readout matrix in which the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate is tested against modeled tAI while amino-acid sequence content, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and M-density baseline remain controls.  Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control required for later translation-efficiency residualization, not a translation readout by itself.  A direct association $Q_i \to P_k$ is not accepted as protein realization unless a preceding $Q_i \to T_j$ step survives under the declared controls and the protein-abundance rows are identifier-matched to the same organism and coding sequences.  The current finite run has the first bounded contact, but it lacks the matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows and lacks matched proteomics rows; consequently it stops before protein-output mediation.

\paragraph{No-promotion boundary.}
This chapter records a modeled tAI contact for residual coordinates in coding sequences.  It does not establish observed ribosome occupancy, observed translation efficiency, mature protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.  Those claims require separate measurements at their own layer.  In particular, the Leu CUN/UUR coordinate and any organism-specific sign pattern remain finite entries in a controlled codon-weight surface; they are not a population law without a larger multi-organism design and ablation against simpler baselines.  If later matched rows make every controlled mediated improvement nonpositive, the residual coordinates remain bounded at codon-count or modeled-translation-readout status rather than entering protein-realization fitting.
