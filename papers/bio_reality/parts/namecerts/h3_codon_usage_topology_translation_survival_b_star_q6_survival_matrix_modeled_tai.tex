\subsection{Modeled tAI contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation boundary}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite matrix over nine synonymous residual coordinates and a bounded translation-weight readout.  The residual coordinates are
$\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\texttt{f3\_stress}$.  The codon domain has $61$ sense codons.  The readout is a modeled tAI codon weight $w_c$ built from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and the dos Reis wobble $s$-values, not from ribosome footprints.  Three organism rows were computed: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In all three rows the tAI readout covers all $61$ sense codons, and no zero raw $W$ value required fallback.

\paragraph{Observed finite counts.}
The tRNA payload is complete at the amino-acid-family level for the three organisms used in this bounded computation.  \emph{E. coli} K12 has $89$ FASTA headers, with $87$ matched headers, $2$ unmatched headers, and all $20$ standard amino-acid families represented.  Human has $432$ FASTA headers, all $432$ matched, and all $20$ standard families represented.  Yeast has $275$ FASTA headers, all $275$ matched, and all $20$ standard families represented.  Thus the matrix is not empty or malformed at the codon-weight layer: it has organism-scoped tRNA-copy support and a complete modeled tAI weight vector for every sense codon in each organism.

\paragraph{Matrix verdict.}
At the declared bounded-contact threshold $0.01$, the modeled tAI coupling surface contains $81$ entries above threshold.  The maximum absolute entry is $3801961.031607896$, and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is the same value.  The finite computation therefore detects a nonzero modeled readout contact between $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and the tAI weight construction.  In compact form, the audited matrix fact is
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{organisms}} &= 3,\\
 n_{\mathrm{sense\ codons}} &= 61,\\
 n_{\mathrm{coordinates}} &= 9,\\
 \tau_{\mathrm{contact}} &= 0.01,\\
 \#\{(i,j): |S_{ij}|>\tau_{\mathrm{contact}}\} &= 81,\\
 \max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
This supports a bounded modeled-adaptation readback.  It does not by itself support a powered survival claim, because the finite organism count is $3$, while the registered break condition requires more data whenever powered survival is requested without at least $40$ matched species.  The break condition is therefore not met for the bounded coupling test, but the powered translation-survival interpretation remains withheld.

\paragraph{Relation to the larger protein-abundance panel.}
A separate cross-layer run joined modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb protein abundance for $12$ organism rows, with joined protein counts ranging from $1656$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19395$ in mouse and $19053$ in human.  That run used the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates and reported joint mediation fractions in the unit interval for all $12$ organisms.  The fractions were $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ for zebrafish, $0.6165167748739756$ for fly, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.1976903964724546$ for chicken, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $0.17489681798543982$ for rat, $0.9491551041216973$ for yeast, and $0.01850668564589326$ for \emph{S. solfataricus}.  These values show that the modeled tAI route can be inserted into a finite $Q \to T \to P$ statistical decomposition, but that decomposition is still based on modeled tAI and steady-state abundance.  It is not a causal translation mechanism and is not a measured ribosome-dynamics experiment.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity check.}
The structural-order axis audit computed the same nine coordinates and found that the full $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ signal was significant, with exactly one coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The dominant coordinate was $\texttt{f3\_stress}$, with weighted held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502294756994$, weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$.  The remaining coordinate increments were smaller or nonpositive in that audit, including $0.0020139270616115154$ for $\texttt{Arg\_AGR}$, $0.0013067592718559024$ for $\texttt{Ile\_AUA}$, $0.0005307046179682497$ for $\texttt{K\_AAA}$, $-0.0003205780368125179$ for $\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $0.00017615626369389472$ for $\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $0.00004390882136492317$ for $\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $0.0010134090056701938$ for $\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, and $-0.00045340330387383447$ for $\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$.  This is useful as an identity check on the coordinate basis, but it is not a substitute for measured translation rows.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis has a finite, nonzero modeled tAI contact over three organisms, complete sense-codon coverage, complete standard amino-acid-family tRNA coverage, and $81$ matrix entries above the bounded threshold.  The same coordinate family can also participate in a separate modeled $Q \to T \to P$ statistical decomposition.  No measured translation realization follows from this chapter alone.  No ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universality follows unless a distinct layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurements and the declared controls are recomputed on the joined finite rows.  Direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is especially inadmissible as protein realization unless the intervening $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step survives under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-baseline controls.
