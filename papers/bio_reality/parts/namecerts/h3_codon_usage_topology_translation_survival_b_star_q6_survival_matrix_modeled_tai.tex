\paragraph{Local cannot-claim boundary.}
No observed translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this chapter by itself.  The modeled tAI contact supplies codon-adaptation weights derived from tRNA copy counts and wobble parameters; it does not observe ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from RPF divided by mRNA, protein folding, mature protein function, or organismal fitness.  Any measured translation claim requires organism- and CDS-matched ribosome or translation-efficiency rows with matched mRNA controls.  Any protein-abundance claim requires a downstream matched proteomics contact after the translation step.  Any folding, physical, functional, mechanistic, or universal claim requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  Thus the chapter verdict is: nonzero bounded modeled-tAI coupling is present in the three-organism surface, while powered survival and higher-layer realization remain unclaimed.
