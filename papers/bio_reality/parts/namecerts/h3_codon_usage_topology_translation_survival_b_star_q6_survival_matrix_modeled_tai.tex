\subsection{Modeled tAI survival and translation-conditioned protein abundance for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-modeled-tai-survival-protein-abundance}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$.  The translation coordinate $t_j$ used in the finite computation is modeled codon-level tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads and the dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream protein coordinate $p_k$ is matched protein abundance, read as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ after joining measured PAXdb protein abundance to real CDS codon counts.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct $q_i \to p_k$ assertion.  A residual-to-protein association is interpreted only through the controlled chain $q_i \to t_j \to p_k$, with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance kept as controls rather than as promoted coordinates.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The modeled-tAI contact was computed over the 61 sense-codon count space for three organisms: $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$, $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, and $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$.  The local GtRNAdb payload contained all 20 standard amino-acid families for each of the three organisms.  The header counts were 89 for $\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}$, 432 for human, and 275 for yeast; the matched header counts were 87, 432, and 275 respectively, leaving two unmatched headers only in the $\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}$ payload.  No codon-level raw-weight fallback was needed in the three modeled tAI readouts: each organism had 61 sense codons and zero raw $W$ fallback codons in the organism-level readout.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling statistic.}
The finite modeled-tAI survival surface has organism count $n=3$ and coupling threshold $0.01$.  Within that bounded contact, 81 entries exceeded threshold, with maximum absolute entry and maximum absolute diagonal entry both equal to $3801961.031607896$.  The computation is summarized as
$$
\begin{aligned}
|Q| &= 9,\\
|C_{\mathrm{sense}}| &= 61,\\
|O| &= 3,\\
\tau_{\mathrm{coupling}} &= 0.01,\\
\#\{(i,j): |S_{ij}|>\tau_{\mathrm{coupling}}\} &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
This is a positive bounded modeled-tAI coupling result, not a powered multi-species survival law.  The finite row itself records that powered survival should not be requested from only three organisms; the stated break condition would require more data if a finite modeled-tAI matrix had no above-threshold entries, or if powered survival were requested without at least 40 matched species.  In the present finite row the no-entry break is not met, but the species count remains far below the powered-survival threshold.

\paragraph{Matched protein rows.}
A downstream protein-abundance decomposition was also computed on same-organism CDS/protein rows for three organisms.  The joined row counts were 3739 proteins for $\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}$, 4129 for $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis\ str.\ MC2\ 155}$, and 5815 for $\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}$.  Per-protein mean modeled tAI was computed as the codon-count-weighted mean
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c N_c w_c}{\sum_c N_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $N_c$ is the protein-specific codon count and $w_c$ is the modeled tAI weight.  The all-tRNA copy totals used by the per-protein computation were 90 for the $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ tRNA payload, 49 for $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$, and 275 for yeast.  One zero raw $W$ value was filled by a geometric-mean rule in the $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ and $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$ per-protein computations, and none was filled in yeast.

\paragraph{Rank and residual controls.}
After residualizing the declared controls, the rank checks were sufficient in each organism.  The control-column count was 24, with rank $Z=23$.  The residual degrees of freedom were 3716 for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, 4106 for $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$, and 5792 for yeast.  In all three organisms, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X\widetilde T)=10$, with the nine $q_i$ coordinates retained.  The residual translation energies were $0.4728468725814029$, $0.041191576348982066$, and $6.90537888244409$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$, and yeast respectively.  The residual protein-abundance energies were $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$ in the same order.

\paragraph{Mediation decomposition.}
The finite protein-abundance analysis used a joint nine-dimensional $X_Q$ mediation scalar with per-coordinate audit.  In $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, the total residual-to-protein $R^2$ was $0.10767526677985896$, the direct $R^2$ after conditioning on modeled tAI was $0.04167869969498872$, and the indirect difference was $0.06599656708487024$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  In $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$, the corresponding values were $0.03209009170649934$, $0.029395293228368185$, $0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.  In yeast, they were $0.29837624994140827$, $0.0151709093608293$, $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Thus all three mediated fractions lie in the unit interval, but their sizes differ sharply across organisms.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is narrow.  The modeled-tAI matrix supports a bounded translation-readout coupling for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, and the matched protein-abundance rows support a statistical decomposition in which conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI reduces the residual-to-protein association in all three organisms.  The yeast and $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ decompositions show large mediated fractions, while the $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$ decomposition is much weaker.  These numbers justify recording a translation-conditioned protein-abundance mediation surface for the measured rows, but only under the stated controls and only as a statistical row-level decomposition.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No stronger biological conclusion follows from this record alone.  Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is not ribosome profiling, and a cross-sectional protein-abundance association is not a perturbational translation mechanism.  The computation does not establish folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, causality, or universality.  A direct $q_i \to p_k$ association remains inadmissible as protein realization unless the same residual first survives an explicit translation-readout step under the declared controls.  Any claim about ribosome occupancy, folding, physical state, function, mechanism, or population-wide law would require its own layer-matched contact and a separately reported statistic.
