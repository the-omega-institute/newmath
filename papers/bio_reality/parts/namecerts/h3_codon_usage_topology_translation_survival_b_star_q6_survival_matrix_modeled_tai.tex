\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival Surface for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residuals}\label{sec:bior reality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object considered here is the matrix of controlled contacts between the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and a modeled translation-readout coordinate derived from tRNA gene-copy counts.  The residual coordinate list is
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.
The codon-count support has $61$ sense codons.  The readout is modeled $\mathrm{tAI}$, computed as a codon weight $w_c$ from local GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy records and the dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  Three organisms enter this finite matrix: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Their local tRNA payloads contain all $20$ standard amino-acid families: $89$ FASTA headers for \emph{E. coli} K12 with $87$ matched and $2$ unmatched headers, $432$ matched human headers, and $275$ matched yeast headers.

\paragraph{Modeled-contact calculation.}
For each organism the modeled tAI readout covers all $61$ sense codons, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in all three organisms.  The bounded coupling audit reports $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with maximum absolute entry
$$\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}$$
This is therefore not an empty contact surface: the modeled translation-weight geometry has nonzero finite coupling to the residual coordinates.  The support condition for a bounded modeled-tAI contact is met, and the recorded break condition is not triggered for this bounded contact because the finite matrix is not below threshold.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The same finite rows do not support a powered survival claim.  The organism count is only $n=3$, whereas the registered rule requires at least $40$ matched species before a powered survival reading can be requested.  The local verdict is therefore narrower than a biological survival theorem: the matrix is a modeled-tAI bounded-coupling surface, not a ribosome-profiling or proteomics validation.  In symbols, the admissible reading is
$$\begin{aligned}
B^{\ast}_{Q6} \times \mathrm{modeled\ tAI} &\longrightarrow \text{bounded coupling},\\
B^{\ast}_{Q6} \times \mathrm{measured\ TE} &\not\longrightarrow \text{established here},\\
B^{\ast}_{Q6} \times \mathrm{protein\ abundance} &\not\longrightarrow \text{established by this matrix alone}.
\end{aligned}$$
The phrase ``survival'' in this subsection is therefore used only for the modeled readout surface and not for observed translation output.

\paragraph{Downstream comparison rows.}
A larger protein-abundance mediation audit gives a useful scale for what is not being claimed by the three-organism modeled-tAI matrix.  In the later twelve-organism protein-abundance decomposition, the same nine residual coordinates are joined to per-protein modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance.  Joined protein counts include $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $5815$ for yeast, $19053$ for human, and $19395$ for mouse.  In that matrix the total residual-to-protein $R^2$ and the translation-conditioned direct $R^2$ vary by organism: yeast has $c=0.29837624994140827$ and $c'=0.0151709093608293$, while human has $c=0.04454565118019448$ and $c'=0.03242643251185274$.  The modeled mediation fractions range from $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast.  These numbers show why the modeled-tAI surface is scientifically useful, but they also show that protein-abundance mediation is a separate finite matrix with its own rows, controls, and interpretation.

\paragraph{Controls.}
The controlled interpretation keeps amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline as controls rather than promoted coordinates.  The downstream protein-abundance matrix uses $24$ control columns, including an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the yeast structure-mediation audit, the five-way join contains $4759$ genes, and the residual association $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$ changes only slightly when conditioning on the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy alone, giving $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$.  The same audit reports $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$, and a much weaker structure-incremental right-tail value $p=0.1258741258741259$.  These rows are compatible with a bounded translation and abundance accounting, but they do not turn modeled tAI into a structure measurement.

\paragraph{Axis identity and negative controls.}
The structural-order axis audit identifies $f_3$-stress as the only fold-level sign-flip passing coordinate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  Other coordinates, including $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ with $p=0.142578125$ and $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ with $p=0.0693359375$, do not pass that same gate.  The evolutionary selection-imprint audit is also not promoted: among $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species, the positive selection-imprint gate has $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, so the strong selection-imprint call is false.  These finite comparisons keep the modeled-tAI coupling from being read as an organism-wide law.

\paragraph{Local admissible conclusion.}
The chapter records a nonempty modeled translation-weight surface for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $81$ entries exceed the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold, all three organisms have complete $61$-sense-codon modeled tAI weights, and the tRNA payloads span the $20$ standard amino-acid families.  The admissible conclusion is exactly this bounded modeled contact.  No measured translation realization, ribosome-occupancy survival, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from these rows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant measurements and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.  A direct residual-to-protein association is not treated as protein realization here unless the residual first survives an explicit translation-readout step under the stated controls.
