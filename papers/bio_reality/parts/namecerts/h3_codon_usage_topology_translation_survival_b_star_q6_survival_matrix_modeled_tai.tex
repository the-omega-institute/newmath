\subsection{Modeled tAI survival matrix for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:brioreality-bstar-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose first coordinate is a $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual, whose second coordinate is a bounded translation-layer readout, and whose downstream coordinate, when present, is a matched protein-abundance measurement.  The immediate survival computation in this section is restricted to modeled tAI weights.  The residual coordinate list is
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.
The codon count space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates, and the modeled readout uses $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  The row set is deliberately small: $n_{\mathrm{org}}=3$, namely \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local GtRNAdb payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism; the matched FASTA header counts are $87$ of $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ of $432$ for human, and $275$ of $275$ for yeast.

\paragraph{Modeled survival statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled tAI readout coordinate $t_j$, the finite computation forms a bounded coupling entry $S_{ij}$ after keeping amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline as controls rather than as promoted coordinates.  The reported threshold is $\lambda=0.01$.  In the modeled tAI matrix, $81$ entries have $|S_{ij}|>\lambda$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\lambda &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
For all three organisms the modeled tAI readout was computed on $61$ sense codons, and the zero-raw-$W$ fallback count was $0$ in \emph{E. coli} K12, human, and yeast.  Thus the finite computation is non-vacuous as a modeled codon-adaptation contact: every standard sense codon receives a modeled tAI weight, and the matrix has entries above the declared bounded-contact threshold.

\paragraph{Readback allowed by this row set.}
The correct readback is narrower than a biological mechanism claim.  This run supports a bounded modeled-tAI coupling statement for the listed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals over three organism rows.  It does not by itself establish observed ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from ribosome profiling, matched protein output, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, mechanism, or a universal rule.  The registered break condition was not met for the bounded modeled matrix, because there are entries above $0.01$; however, the same record states that powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species, and the present surface has only $3$ organisms.  Consequently the layer label is best read as modeled and underpowered: positive finite coupling exists, but population-level survival remains outside the claim.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance comparison surface.}
A separate cross-layer protein-abundance computation joins $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals to modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb abundance on $12$ organisms.  That larger surface uses the same $9$ residual coordinates and reports organism-specific joined protein counts, including $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $5815$ for yeast, $19053$ for human, and $19395$ for mouse.  In that computation the readout $T$ is per-protein mean modeled tAI, while the response $Y$ is $\log_{10}$ abundance ppm from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The mediation fractions are positive but explicitly statistical: for example $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse.  These values show that a downstream protein-abundance matrix can be formed on matched rows, but they do not upgrade the three-organism modeled-tAI survival matrix into a causal translation or protein-realization theorem.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible inference is therefore a two-level statement.  First, the three-organism modeled-tAI contact has finite, nonzero bounded coupling for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, with $81$ entries above $0.01$ and no zero-weight fallback in the modeled codon readouts.  Second, a distinct matched protein-abundance mediation surface exists on $12$ organisms, but it remains a statistical decomposition conditioned on modeled tAI and ordinary sequence controls.  No direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is admissible as protein realization unless the $Q_i\to T_j$ step survives in the appropriate readout surface, and no folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, selection law, or universality follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies that evidence.
