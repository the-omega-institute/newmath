\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival and Translation-Conditioned Protein Abundance for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-protein-mediation}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The coordinate $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate $t_j$ is a bounded readout of codon adaptation, here modeled tAI computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream coordinate $p_k$ is matched protein abundance when the row has a proteomics contact.  The construction is not a direct residual-to-protein assertion: a $q_i \to p_k$ association is admitted only as a downstream term after the same residual family is first tested against a translation-layer readout.  Amino-acid sequence composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the M-density baseline remain controls rather than promoted coordinates.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The modeled-tAI run used $61$ sense codons and three organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local GtRNAdb payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each organism.  The FASTA header counts were $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast; the matched header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$, with unmatched counts $2$, $0$, and $0$, respectively.  The modeled tAI readout assigned all $61$ sense codons in each of the three organisms.  The raw-weight fallback count was $0$ for human and yeast and $0$ for the three-organism survival-matrix computation; in the later per-protein abundance run, geometric-mean filling was needed once for the \emph{E. coli} tRNA source and once for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
At coupling threshold $0.01$, the finite modeled-tAI matrix had $81$ entries above threshold.  The largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for this run was not met: the matrix did contain entries above the bounded-contact threshold.  However, the organism count was only $3$, so the result is not a powered cross-organism survival claim.  It is a bounded modeled-tAI contact showing that the nine residual coordinates can couple nontrivially to a codon-adaptation readout under the stated finite construction.  It remains below measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, and protein-output validation.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation rows.}
A separate same-theme computation joined real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb-style abundance rows for six organisms.  The joined protein counts were $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $4129$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $5815$ for yeast.  The per-protein translation readout was the mean modeled tAI
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the CDS codon count and $w_c$ is the tAI weight.  The abundance readout was $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  Each organism retained rank sufficiency after residualizing the control set: $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$, the control-column count was $24$, the residualized $Q$ rank was $9$, the residualized $T$ rank was $1$, and the joint $(Q,T)$ rank was $10$ in every organism.  Residual degrees of freedom after controls were $14387$, $3716$, $12395$, $19030$, $4106$, and $5792$ in the same organism order.

\paragraph{Mediation fractions.}
The statistical mediation fraction $M^{QTP}$ lay inside the unit interval in all six organisms.  The values were
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{danio}} &= 0.3157053953614641,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{ecoli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{gallus}} &= 0.1976903964724546,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{human}} &= 0.27206289160119157,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{mycobacterium}} &= 0.08397602919861291,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{yeast}} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding indirect $R^2$ components, computed as $c-c'$, were $0.014565162517577362$, $0.06599656708487024$, $0.007869646643962787$, $0.012119218668341744$, $0.0026947984781311544$, and $0.28320534058057895$.  The total residual-to-protein $R^2$ values were $0.04613529807085213$, $0.10767526677985896$, $0.03980793596647631$, $0.04454565118019448$, $0.03209009170649934$, and $0.29837624994140827$.  The direct residual-to-protein terms after conditioning on modeled tAI were $0.031570135553274765$, $0.04167869969498872$, $0.031938289322513524$, $0.03242643251185274$, $0.029395293228368185$, and $0.0151709093608293$.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite rows support a cautious statement: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual family has a nonzero bounded modeled-tAI coupling surface, and in a six-organism matched CDS/protein-abundance matrix the modeled tAI coordinate can account for a positive fraction of the residual-to-abundance association.  The strongest mediation fraction occurs in yeast, where $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ and the indirect component is $0.28320534058057895$; the weakest is \emph{M. smegmatis}, where $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$ and the indirect component is $0.0026947984781311544$.  These numbers justify keeping the packet at a translation-conditioned protein-abundance fitting layer, not at a mechanism layer.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No conclusion about observed translation, ribosome dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, selection, or universality follows from these rows alone.  Modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation annotation derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights, not a ribosome-profiling measurement.  Matched protein abundance is a steady-state protein-output readout and is not isolated from mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or experimental quantification bias.  A future positive $q_i \to p_k$ association must remain blocked unless the same residual coordinate first survives a controlled $q_i \to t_j$ translation-readout step under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-baseline controls.
