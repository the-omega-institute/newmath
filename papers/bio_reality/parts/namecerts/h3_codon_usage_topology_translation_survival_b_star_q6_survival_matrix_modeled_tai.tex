\subsection{Modeled tAI survival boundary for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:biorality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
This record concerns a finite surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$: $q_i$ is one of the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation readout, and $p_k$ is a matched downstream protein-abundance readout when that downstream layer is present.  The modeled translation contact is not a direct biological observation of ribosome motion.  It is a bounded codon-adaptation readout computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble weights.  The nine residual coordinates present in the row memory are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R_{Q6}=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon count space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates.  For the three modeled organisms, the tAI readout was computed for all $61$ sense codons in each organism.  Homo sapiens and Saccharomyces cerevisiae had zero fallback codons, while Escherichia coli K-12 had zero fallback codons in this modeled-tAI run.  The local GtRNAdb payload also covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each of the three organisms: Escherichia coli K-12 had $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers; Homo sapiens had $432$ headers with $432$ matched; Saccharomyces cerevisiae had $275$ headers with $275$ matched.

\paragraph{Modeled translation score.}
The finite matrix is read as a controlled residual-to-translation table rather than as a protein-realization result.  With coupling threshold $0.01$, the modeled-tAI bounded coupling matrix has $81$ entries above threshold.  Its largest absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}|=3{,}801{,}961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the largest absolute diagonal value is the same number.  Thus the modeled contact is nonzero at the bounded readout layer.  The row does not satisfy a powered multi-organism survival standard: the organism count is $3$, and the finite-row rule explicitly says that powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species when that stronger claim is requested.  The classification is therefore translation-realization-modeled-underpowered, not measured ribosome occupancy and not a population law.

\paragraph{Downstream mediation row.}
A separate joined row memory tests the downstream statistical chain using per-protein mean modeled tAI as $T$ and PAXdb log abundance as $Y$.  This row has six organisms: Danio rerio with $14{,}410$ joined proteins, Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 with $3{,}739$, Gallus gallus with $12{,}418$, Homo sapiens with $19{,}053$, Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155 with $4{,}129$, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae with $5{,}815$.  Each organism used $9$ $Q$ coordinates, a residualized translation rank $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, a residualized $Q$ rank $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, and a joint rank $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X,\widetilde T)=10$ after $23$ effective control rank dimensions from a $24$-column control design.  The controls include amino-acid composition, organism scope, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched transcript control where the mediation row requires it.

\paragraph{Mediation fractions.}
For the protein-abundance row, the mediated fraction $M^{QTP}$ is a statistical decomposition after residualization, not a causal mechanism.  The observed fractions were positive and inside the unit interval for all six organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Danio}}&=0.3157053953614641,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Ecoli}}&=0.612922252793667,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Gallus}}&=0.1976903964724546,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Human}}&=0.27206289160119157,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Mycobacterium}}&=0.08397602919861291,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{Yeast}}&=0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding total and direct $R^2$ values show why the row is treated as mediated statistical attenuation rather than as a direct residual-to-protein claim.  For example, in Saccharomyces cerevisiae the total $Q\to P$ value was $0.29837624994140827$, the direct value after conditioning on $T$ was $0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference was $0.28320534058057895$.  In Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 the total was $0.10767526677985896$, the direct value was $0.04167869969498872$, and the indirect difference was $0.06599656708487024$.  The smallest mediated fraction occurred in Mycobacterium smegmatis, where the total was $0.03209009170649934$, the direct value was $0.029395293228368185$, and the indirect difference was $0.0026947984781311544$.

\paragraph{Readout interpretation.}
The controlled readback is only the finite matrix $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$.  The $Q_i\to T_j$ part is supported here only in the modeled-tAI sense, and the downstream $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ part is a cross-sectional statistical decomposition over matched coding sequences and protein abundance.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first passes through the translation readout under the declared controls.  Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control, not a translation observation.  Protein abundance is a downstream steady-state measurement, not a folding, function, perturbation, or mechanism assay.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The record therefore supports a bounded finite-row statement: the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nonzero modeled-tAI coupling surface in three organisms, and a separate six-organism joined row gives positive unit-interval statistical mediation fractions for modeled tAI and protein abundance.  It does not establish measured translation survival, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In particular, no CUN/UUR direction, no organism-specific sign, and no direct residual-to-protein association is promoted to a biological law from these finite rows.
