\paragraph{Mediation interpretation.}
For a residual coordinate $q_i$ to enter the intended mediation matrix, the admissible path has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $t_j$ is a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $p_k$ is matched protein abundance.  The present run fills only the modeled-tAI part of the middle coordinate.  It does not fill ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from RPF and mRNA, or protein abundance.  Matched mRNA abundance remains a control for separating transcription-layer signal from translation-layer signal; it is not itself a translation survival row.  The baseline controls remain amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and M-density baseline, with matched mRNA abundance required before a downstream protein-abundance mediation claim is made.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The finite result supports a bounded modeled-tAI coupling statement: within three organism rows and nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, the modeled codon-adaptation surface is nonzero above threshold.  It does not establish observed ribosome dynamics, direct translation efficiency, mature protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, or universality.  No claim about CUN/UUR directionality, any single-organism sign, or a population law follows from these rows alone.  A protein-abundance statement would require matched proteomics rows and a surviving $q_i\to t_j$ step; a translation-efficiency statement would require organism-matched ribosome-profiling and mRNA rows; a mechanistic or functional statement would require a further contact at that layer.  If all future mediated improvements are nonpositive after the declared controls and simpler-baseline ablation, then the $B^*_{Q6}$ packet remains bounded at codon-count residual or translation-readout status rather than moving into protein-realization fitting.
