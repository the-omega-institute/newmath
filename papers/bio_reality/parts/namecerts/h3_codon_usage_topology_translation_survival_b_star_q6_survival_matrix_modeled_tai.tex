\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival Matrix for B-star Q6 Residual Coordinates}\label{subsec:b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a finite mediation surface whose rows are synonymous residual coordinates $q_i$, whose middle coordinate is a modeled translation readout $T$, and whose downstream coordinate may be a matched protein-abundance readout $P$ only after the translation coordinate has been explicitly retained. The residual side contains the nine computed $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count side is the 61-sense-codon space. The translation readout in this row is modeled tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads and dos Reis wobble weights. The finite organism panel contains three organisms, so the row is deliberately interpreted as bounded modeled coupling, not as powered species-level survival.

\paragraph{Translation readout construction.}
The tRNA payload contains all 20 standard amino-acid families in each organism. The local FASTA counts are $89$ headers for \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The matched standard-family counts are $87$, $432$, and $275$ respectively, with two unmatched headers only in the \emph{E. coli} K12 payload. For each of the three organisms, the modeled tAI calculation assigns all 61 sense codons and has zero raw-$W$ fallback count equal to $0$. Thus the computation has a complete codon-weight readout in this finite panel.

\paragraph{Survival statistic recorded at modeled-readout scope.}
Let $S_{ij}$ denote the bounded contact score between residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled translation readout coordinate $t_j$ after the declared baseline separation. The contact threshold used in this row is $0.01$. The finite modeled-tAI matrix contains $81$ entries above threshold, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}|&=3{,}801{,}961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}|&=3{,}801{,}961.031607896,\\
\tau&=0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for the modeled contact is therefore not met: the finite modeled-tAI matrix is not empty above the bounded-contact threshold. The correct reading is narrower than biological translation validation: the row shows nonzero modeled codon-adaptation coupling for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates in a three-organism panel.

\paragraph{Downstream protein-abundance check.}
A separate matched protein-abundance calculation joins real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb protein abundance. The joined row counts are $3739$ proteins for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}. After residualizing against a control block of 24 columns with rank $23$, the residual degrees of freedom are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$. The modeled per-protein tAI readout has rank $1$, the residual $Q$ block has rank $9$, and the joint $Q,T$ block has rank $10$ in all three organisms.

\paragraph{Mediation numbers.}
The downstream calculation decomposes the residual-to-protein-abundance association by conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI. In \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, the total $Q\to P$ $R^2$ is $0.10767526677985896$, the direct $Q\to P\mid T$ $R^2$ is $0.04167869969498872$, and the indirect difference is $0.06599656708487024$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$. In \emph{M. smegmatis}, the corresponding values are $0.03209009170649934$, $0.029395293228368185$, $0.0026947984781311544$, and $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$. In yeast, they are $0.29837624994140827$, $0.0151709093608293$, $0.28320534058057895$, and $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$. All three mediated fractions lie in the unit interval, so the finite statistical decomposition is internally well-formed for this row.

\paragraph{Admissible reading.}
The chapter-level claim is that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nonzero modeled tAI contact in the finite three-organism matrix, and that matched protein-abundance rows can be decomposed by conditioning on that modeled translation coordinate. Direct $Q\to P$ language is not promoted unless the $Q\to T$ leg is kept visible. The controls remain controls: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance do not become new residual coordinates in this row.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
This row does not establish observed ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or a universal biological law. Modeled tAI from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights is a codon-adaptation annotation, not a ribosome-profiling experiment. Protein abundance is a steady-state downstream measurement and is not isolated from mRNA abundance, degradation, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or assay bias. Any stronger biological interpretation requires a separate layer-matched contact and a statistic computed on that contact.
