\subsection{Modeled tAI contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-bstar-q6-modeled-tai-contact}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record concerns the finite surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, with $q_i$ taken from the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinates, $t_j$ a translation readout, and $p_k$ a matched protein-abundance readout when such a downstream contact is present.  The specific contact evaluated here is the modeled-tAI slice: codon weights $w_c$ were computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads and dos Reis wobble weights, and the resulting readout was treated as a bounded translation-layer annotation rather than as an observed ribosome-profile measurement.  The residual coordinate list used in the finite row computation was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $|R|=9$, and the codon count space contains $61$ sense-codon columns.  The modeled-tAI contact was evaluated in three organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local tRNA payloads covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The corresponding FASTA-header counts were $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast; the matched header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$, leaving only two unmatched \emph{E. coli} headers and none in the other two organisms.

\paragraph{Bounded modeled coupling.}
For the modeled-tAI matrix, every organism had $61$ sense codons with zero fallback events in the raw $W$ table after the dos Reis wobble-weight computation.  The bounded coupling audit used threshold $\lambda=0.01$ and found $81$ matrix entries above threshold.  The largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\lambda &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
These numbers certify a nonzero finite modeled-readout contact between the nine residual coordinates and organism-scoped tAI weights.  They do not by themselves certify measured translation survival.  The experiment has $n_{\mathrm{org}}=3$, while the registered powered-survival condition requires at least $40$ matched species for that stronger reading.  The correct local statement is therefore: the modeled-tAI coupling break condition is not met at the bounded-contact threshold, but the result remains underpowered for a population-level translation-survival claim.

\paragraph{Readout discipline.}
The admissible readback is a controlled matrix $Q_i\to T_j$, where $T_j$ is a translation-layer readout such as modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not promoted unless the same coordinate first survives a $Q_i\to T_j$ step under controls for amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline.  Matched mRNA abundance belongs to the control set: it separates transcription-layer abundance from translation-layer signal and prevents a transcript-count association from being misread as ribosome execution.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density remain covariates, not promoted coordinates.

\paragraph{Downstream mediation contrast.}
A separate protein-abundance mediation run used the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and joined codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb protein-abundance rows across $12$ organisms.  The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} MC2 155, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In that finite mediation surface the reported $M^{QTP}$ fractions lay in the unit interval for every organism.  Representative values were $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse, and $0.9491551041216973$ for yeast.  The yeast row had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{\mathrm{indirect}} &= 0.28320534058057895.
\end{aligned}
$$
This contrast is useful because it shows how the modeled-tAI contact can be placed inside a larger $Q\to T\to P$ computation, while preserving the same limitation: the mediation scalar is a statistical decomposition on matched rows, not a causal translation mechanism and not a protein-function result.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-control contrast.}
A yeast five-way join further separated protein abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a 5-prime CDS structure proxy.  That join retained $4759$ genes, with reported CDS and total join hit rates $0.9156038419146592$ and $0.8818572656921754$.  In the residualized abundance decomposition, the direct $Q$ to protein-abundance quantity was
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$.  These values keep the modeled-tAI claim in its proper location: structure-related variance can be audited as a separate surface, but the modeled-tAI contact itself does not observe folding.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite-row evidence supports a bounded modeled-tAI contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis.  The support is concrete: nine residual coordinates, $61$ sense codons, three organisms with all standard amino-acid tRNA families represented, zero raw-$W$ fallback events in the modeled-tAI computation, $81$ entries above $\lambda=0.01$, and a nonzero maximum absolute score of $3801961.031607896$.  The verdict is not a measured-ribosome verdict, not a powered multi-species survival verdict, and not a downstream biological-realization verdict.  No translation execution, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this contact unless an additional layer-matched measurement supplies the corresponding finite rows and the controlled statistic is recomputed at that layer.
