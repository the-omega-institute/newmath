\subsection{Modeled tAI survival surface for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{subsec:biorality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record treats the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite family of sequence-derived contrasts and asks whether they have bounded contact with a translation-layer readout.  The row family is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon ambient space has $61$ sense codons.  The translation readout is modeled codon-level tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble-weight rule.  The finite organism panel has $n=3$ organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tRNA payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The local FASTA summaries are $89$ headers with $87$ matched standard-family headers for \emph{E. coli} K-12, $432$ of $432$ for human, and $275$ of $275$ for yeast; the unmatched counts are therefore $2$, $0$, and $0$, respectively.  For each of the three organisms the modeled tAI readout covers all $61$ sense codons, and the raw-weight fallback count is $0$ in all three cases.

\paragraph{Bounded modeled contact.}
The observed finite matrix is not a measured ribosome-profiling matrix.  It is a modeled codon-adaptation surface.  With coupling threshold $0.01$, the modeled tAI computation reports $81$ entries above threshold and a maximum absolute matrix entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is not met for the bounded modeled contact: the finite matrix does contain entries above the stated threshold.  The same record explicitly refuses a powered translation-survival conclusion, because the panel has only $3$ organisms, while powered survival was declared to require at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the local verdict is a positive modeled-tAI coupling contact, not a powered ribosome, translation-efficiency, proteomics, or protein-realization result.

\paragraph{Readout discipline.}
The admissible readback is the controlled matrix pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and $T_j$ is the modeled tAI readout.  A downstream readback
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k
\end{aligned}
$$
requires a separate matched protein-abundance table and an explicitly joined row design.  The modeled-tAI result alone does not license the shorter arrow $q_i \to P_k$ as protein realization.  Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline remain covariates or baselines; they are not promoted to biological coordinates in this chapter.

\paragraph{Genetic-code contact boundary.}
The broader finite contact audit also records a locked genetic-code-table check: $40$ of $40$ independent replication trials succeed with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and $40$ of $40$ boundary-probe trials also succeed with the same value. This supports the finite codon-geometry contact in its own layer. \paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter records a finite, positive modeled-tAI contact for the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates: $81$ modeled entries exceed the $0.01$ threshold on a three-organism tRNA-copy and wobble-weight surface, and the declared break condition for bounded contact is false.  The same evidence remains underpowered for measured translation survival, because $n=3$ is below the declared powered-species boundary.  Protein abundance, structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, and universality are outside this chapter unless a separate row-matched contact supplies that layer and the relevant controlled statistic is computed.  Direct residual-to-protein association is therefore not accepted here unless the translation-readout step is present and survives the declared controls.
