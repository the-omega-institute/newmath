\subsection{Modeled-tAI Survival Boundary for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Surface}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row and admissible readout.}
This record fixes a finite translation-layer test surface for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  The carrier is the finite table of pairs $(q_i,t_j)$ in which $q_i$ ranges over the nine residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}
\end{aligned}
$$
and $t_j$ is the modeled codon-level tAI weight computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  The codon-count ambient space contains $61$ sense codons.  The observed finite organism panel has $n=3$ organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The contact is therefore a modeled adaptation-weight readout, not a direct ribosome-footprint or protein-output assay.

\paragraph{tRNA-family coverage.}
The local GtRNAdb payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each of the three organisms used for this bounded calculation.  In \emph{E. coli} K-12, $89$ FASTA headers were present, $87$ matched the standard parsing route, and $2$ were unmatched.  In human, $432$ FASTA headers were present and all $432$ matched.  In budding yeast, $275$ FASTA headers were present and all $275$ matched.  For the modeled-tAI construction itself, each organism produced weights for all $61$ sense codons, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in all three organisms.  Thus the finite readout has complete sense-codon coverage on the modeled-weight surface even though the biological interpretation remains bounded by the kind of contact used.

\paragraph{Modeled coupling statistic.}
The bounded survival matrix was evaluated with coupling threshold $0.01$.  The finite modeled-tAI matrix had $81$ entries above threshold, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{|S_{ij}|>0.01\} &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for this row is explicit: the record would require additional data if the finite modeled-tAI matrix had no entry above the bounded-contact threshold, or if a powered survival claim were requested without at least $40$ matched species.  In the present finite row the threshold break is not met, because nonzero modeled coupling is observed.  The power break remains binding for any population-level survival statement, since $3$ organisms are not enough for the declared powered survival route.

\paragraph{What the positive matrix entries mean.}
The positive entries show that the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface is not orthogonal to the modeled tAI weight surface under the finite calculation just described.  The result should be read as a bounded codon-adaptation coupling statement: at least one residual coordinate survives contact with a modeled translation-readout coordinate on this finite surface.  It does not assert that ribosomes were observed, that translation efficiency was measured by RPF divided by mRNA, or that protein abundance was experimentally mediated by the same row.  The chapter therefore records translation-realization only in the modeled-underpowered sense: the readout is computable, finite, and nonzero under the threshold, but it is not a measured or population-powered translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Rank and downstream separation.}
A separate twelve-organism protein-abundance mediation run shows why this modeled-tAI row must not be promoted by wording alone.  In that downstream run, the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates were used with per-protein modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance, and every organism had rank-sufficient residualized design diagnostics after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline were controlled.  The mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ were finite and lay in the unit interval across all twelve organisms, including $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.9491551041216973$ for budding yeast, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, and $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse.  Those figures are useful as a downstream consistency check, but they do not change the scope of the present three-organism modeled-tAI survival row.  Here the legitimate claim is only that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a finite nonzero modeled-tAI coupling surface.

\paragraph{Controls kept outside the coordinate list.}
The finite-row distinction is that amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the M-density baseline remain controls rather than promoted coordinates.  This matters because a direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless a $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step is first present and evaluated.  Likewise, a transcription-only edge is separated by matched mRNA abundance control.  On this row, the direct translation readout is modeled tAI; matched ribosome-profiling TE and matched proteomics are named as possible later contacts, not as measurements already supplied by this finite matrix.

\paragraph{Associated finite rows.}
Two related finite rows give local orientation without changing the verdict.  In the yeast structure-mediated abundance decomposition, $4{,}759$ genes joined across the five-way table, the direct residual-to-protein statistic was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, and conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability reduced the residual statistic to $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$.  Adding the 5-prime CDS MFE proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, with structure-only fraction $0.003284564452662019$ and structure-incremental fraction $0.0005507778820193332$.  In the structural-order axis row, the only fold-level sign-flip-passing axis was $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$.  These rows support the practice of reading the nine coordinates as a finite residual basis with separable downstream contacts, not as a license to infer structure or function from the modeled-tAI matrix alone.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The cannot-claim boundary is part of the mathematical content of the record.  From this finite modeled-tAI matrix one may report nonzero bounded coupling between $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and modeled codon-adaptation weights.  One may not infer measured translation, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, selection, or a universal biological law.  Each of those interpretations requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  If a later measured translation-readout matrix has no positive controlled $S_{ij}$ entry, or if a downstream protein-abundance association appears without a surviving $Q_i \to T_j$ step, the residual coordinates remain bounded at the codon-count or modeled-translation-readout level rather than entering protein-realization fitting.
