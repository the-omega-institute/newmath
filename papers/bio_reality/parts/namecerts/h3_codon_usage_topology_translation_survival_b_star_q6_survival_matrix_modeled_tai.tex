\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival Surface for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}\label{sec:h3-codon-usage-topology-translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix-modeled-tai}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite matrix whose rows are the nine synonymous residual coordinates
$${\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}}$$
The readout column is modeled codon adaptation, computed as a codon-level tAI weight $w_c$ from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and the dos Reis wobble penalties. The codon count space has $61$ sense codons. The finite organism panel contains $3$ organisms: \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. For each organism the modeled readout covers all $61$ sense codons, with zero raw-weight fallback count equal to $0$ in all three cases. Thus the matrix is not a missing-readout object; it is a bounded modeled-contact object whose statistical power is limited by organism count.

\paragraph{Local tRNA completeness.}
The local tRNA payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each of the three organisms. The \textit{E. coli} K-12 payload has $89$ FASTA headers, of which $87$ match the standard parsing route and $2$ remain unmatched. The human payload has $432$ headers, all $432$ matched. The yeast payload has $275$ headers, all $275$ matched. These row counts matter because the modeled tAI surface is allowed to read only the organism-scoped tRNA-copy contact; it is not allowed to fill missing translation biology from the residual coordinates themselves.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
With threshold $0.01$, the modeled-tAI coupling surface has $81$ entries above threshold. The maximum absolute matrix entry is
$${\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896,
\end{aligned}}$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is the same value:
$${\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}}$$
The recorded break condition is not met: the finite modeled-tAI matrix is not empty above the bounded-contact threshold. The positive readout is therefore a modeled coupling fact for the finite $Q$ surface against codon adaptation weights. It is not a measured ribosome-occupancy claim and it is not a powered cross-species survival claim.

\paragraph{Power boundary.}
The same finite record explicitly says that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival when powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species. The correct layer name is consequently translation-realization-modeled-underpowered: the word ``modeled'' is doing mathematical work. The data support the existence of a nonzero bounded modeled-tAI matrix, but the organism count blocks a population-level survival law. In particular, the result does not license a claim that CUN/UUR directionality, any single organism sign, or any one residual coordinate is a universal translation law.

\paragraph{Axis identity check.}
The powered structural-order axis audit keeps the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and identifies exactly one significant axis under the fold-level sign-flip gate. The dominant axis is $f_3$-stress, with weighted held-out increment
$${\begin{aligned}
\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{held\ out}} &= 0.0031744502294756994,
\end{aligned}}$$
weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, and sign-flip value $p=0.03125$. The other coordinates do not pass that gate: for example, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ has $p=0.0693359375$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ has $p=0.142578125$, and $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ has a negative held-out increment $-0.0003205780368125179$. This check sharpens the reading of the modeled-tAI matrix: the finite residual surface has named axes, but axis identity is not the same thing as biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow. At the bounded modeled-contact layer, the nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface has a nonzero modeled-tAI coupling matrix across three organisms, with $81$ entries above $0.01$ and no missing sense-codon modeled readout. At the powered biological layer, the same record refuses promotion because $n=3$ is below the declared powered-survival requirement of at least $40$ matched species. The result is therefore a finite modeled translation-readout screen, not a measured translation-survival theorem.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation realization, ribosome occupancy, folding statement, physical admissibility statement, molecular function statement, mechanism claim, phenotype statement, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from this chapter by itself. Such a claim would require its own layer-matched contact: measured ribosome profiling or translation efficiency for observed translation, matched proteomics for protein abundance, a structure or physical assay for folding or admissibility, and an explicit perturbation or functional assay for mechanism or function. A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association remains inadmissible unless the same coordinate first passes a controlled $Q_i\to T_j$ readout step under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls.
