\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival and the Protein-Abundance Mediation Boundary}\label{subsec:q6-modeled-tai-protein-abundance-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The record is a finite cross-layer table whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions
$\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$.  The translation coordinate $t_j$ used in the computed survival row is modeled tAI: a per-codon weight $w_c$ obtained from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble penalties.  The downstream coordinate $p_k$ is matched protein abundance, represented in the powered mediation row as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ after joining measured PAXdb protein abundance to real CDS codon counts.  The construction therefore does not admit a bare $q_i \to p_k$ interpretation: a protein-abundance association is part of this record only after the translation readout $t_j$ has been placed in the middle of the table.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The modeled-tAI survival computation used a $61$-sense-codon space and three organisms: $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$, $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, and $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$.  The local GtRNAdb payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The matched tRNA-family counts were $87$ of $89$ FASTA headers in $\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12}$, $432$ of $432$ in human, and $275$ of $275$ in yeast; the corresponding unmatched header counts were $2$, $0$, and $0$.  For all three organisms the modeled readout assigned weights to all $61$ sense codons, with zero raw-weight fallback counts $0$, $0$, and $0$.  At the bounded coupling threshold $0.01$, the finite matrix had $81$ entries above threshold and a maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the break condition ``no bounded modeled-tAI contact above threshold'' is not met.  The positive contact is nevertheless only a modeled codon-adaptation contact: with organism count $n=3$, the row is explicitly underpowered for a population-level translation-survival claim, and it is not ribosome profiling, translation-efficiency sequencing, proteomics validation, or a perturbation experiment.

\paragraph{Controlled mediation row.}
A separate powered finite-row table evaluates the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates against per-protein modeled tAI and matched protein abundance in six organism-level joins.  The joined protein counts are $3{,}739$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}$, $5{,}815$ for yeast, $19{,}053$ for human, $14{,}410$ for zebrafish, $12{,}418$ for chicken, and $4{,}129$ for $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis\ str\ MC2\ 155}$.  The tRNA copy totals used for the per-protein modeled-tAI calculation are $90$, $275$, $432$, $8{,}874$, $288$, and $49$ in the same organism order; zero raw codon weights were filled by a geometric mean for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$ and $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$ only, one codon in each case.

\paragraph{Residualized design.}
The controlled design residualizes amino-acid composition, organism-level covariates, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA-related controls before testing the $Q \to T \to P$ surface.  In every organism the residualized design is rank-sufficient: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{T})=1$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{X})=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{X},\widetilde{T})=10$, and $\operatorname{rank}(Z)=23$ with $24$ control columns.  The residual degrees of freedom are $3{,}716$, $5{,}792$, $19{,}030$, $14{,}387$, $12{,}395$, and $4{,}106$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, yeast, human, zebrafish, chicken, and $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$ respectively.  The finite scalar mediation fraction
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= \frac{R^2(Q\to P)-R^2(Q\to P\mid T)}{R^2(Q\to P)}
\end{aligned}
$$
falls in the unit interval for all six organisms: $0.612922252793667$ for $\mathrm{E.\ coli}$, $0.9491551041216973$ for yeast, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, $0.3157053953614641$ for zebrafish, $0.1976903964724546$ for chicken, and $0.08397602919861291$ for $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$.  The largest mediated fraction is the yeast row, where $R^2(Q\to P)=0.29837624994140827$, $R^2(Q\to P\mid T)=0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$.  The smallest is $\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}$, with total $R^2=0.03209009170649934$, direct conditioned $R^2=0.029395293228368185$, and indirect difference $0.0026947984781311544$.

\paragraph{Readback.}
The finite readback is therefore a controlled mediation matrix rather than a direct protein-realization claim.  Its admissible content is: nine synonymous residual coordinates can be placed against modeled tAI; the three-organism modeled-tAI contact has nonzero bounded coupling but is not powered as measured translation survival; and, in a larger six-organism CDS/protein-abundance join, the same residual family admits positive statistical $Q \to T \to P$ decomposition after the stated controls.  The record is strongest as an instruction about what must be tested: $q_i \to p_k$ is not promoted unless the intervening $q_i \to t_j$ step is present and controlled.  The row also keeps CUN/UUR signs, organism-specific directions, and any single-species coefficient away from population-law language unless a larger multi-organism design and simpler-baseline ablations support that move.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
No statement in this chapter identifies modeled tAI with observed ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency sequencing, protein synthesis rate, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control, not a translation readout.  Bulk protein abundance is a downstream steady-state measurement and is not a folding assay, turnover assay, localization assay, function assay, or perturbation rescue.  Any claim at those layers requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.
