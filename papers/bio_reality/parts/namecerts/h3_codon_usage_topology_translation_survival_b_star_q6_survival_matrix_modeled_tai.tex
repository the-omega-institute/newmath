\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^\ast_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates, $t_j$ is a translation-layer readout, and $p_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout only when that downstream layer is explicitly joined.  The residual coordinate list used in the modeled-tAI run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense codons.  The readout in this row is not ribosome profiling and not proteomics; it is a modeled codon weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data together with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  The three organisms used for the bounded contact were \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, so the organism count is $n=3$.

\paragraph{Observed finite counts.}
The local tRNA payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each of the three organisms.  The header accounting was $89$ FASTA headers with $87$ matched headers and $2$ unmatched headers for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ headers with $432$ matched and $0$ unmatched for human, and $275$ headers with $275$ matched and $0$ unmatched for yeast.  The modeled tAI computation produced $61$ sense-codon weights in each organism, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ for all three organisms.  Thus the finite contact is internally complete for the standard amino-acid families and the sense-codon coordinate system, but it remains a modeled adaptation-weight contact rather than a measured ribosome-occupancy contact.

\paragraph{Bounded survival matrix.}
Let $S_{ij}$ denote the finite modeled coupling score between residual coordinate $q_i$ and translation-weight readout $t_j$ after the declared coordinate construction.  In the bounded modeled-tAI matrix, the threshold was $0.01$ and $81$ entries exceeded that threshold in absolute value.  The largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}|&=3{,}801{,}961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}|&=3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The registered break condition was not met for this bounded contact: the finite modeled matrix was not empty above threshold.  The correct verdict, however, is narrower than a powered biological survival claim.  With only $3$ organisms, the row is underpowered for population-level survival; the row itself states that powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species if a powered species-level claim is requested.  Consequently the evidence supports a modeled codon-adaptation coupling surface, not a measured translation-efficiency law.

\paragraph{Yeast structure and abundance boundary.}
The yeast five-way structure-mediation row joined $4759$ genes after matching CDS, protein abundance, modeled or measured translation-related quantities, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy.  Its direct $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual-to-protein-abundance fit after controls was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$.  Conditioning on the structure proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, conditioning on translation and mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond translation and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the residual-coordinate-to-structure fit was $S_Q=0.003774248455422586$ with permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$ for the structure association and $p=0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  This finite row therefore marks a small sequence-derived structure-proxy association, not folded-structure realization.

\paragraph{Cross-checks against stronger interpretations.}
The structural-order audit found exactly one significant axis, $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$; all other listed axes failed that fold-level sign-flip gate.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint run used $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; its positive-imprint gate was negative, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null95 $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and description-length change $-258.6010084301837$ bits.  The mass-balance row on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals also stayed negative at the stronger closure layer, with $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit likewise did not support a turnover residual claim beyond controls: $n=3290$, $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$.  These checks keep the modeled-tAI survival row from being read as selection, mass-balance closure, turnover prediction, folding, or function.

\paragraph{Positive low-level context.}
The observable-execution decomposition did report an observable execution subblock in yeast, with $3289$ genes and a verdict that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  The independent genetic-code-table confirmation also retained a low-level codon-geometry contact: the boundary probe had $40$ successes out of $40$ trials with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail $p=9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, the independent replication again had $40/40$ successes with the same $p$-value, and the negative and ablation controls had $0$ unexpected successes in $20$ trials each.  These facts justify using the residual coordinates as finite sequence-derived coordinates.  They do not turn the modeled-tAI contact into an observed ribosome, protein, structure, or phenotype result.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The admissible readback for this chapter is the controlled matrix $q_i\to t_j$ for modeled tAI, together with separately joined downstream rows only when those rows are explicitly present.  A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not evidence of protein realization unless the corresponding $q_i\to t_j$ step survives on the same declared route.  No measured translation realization, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this modeled-tAI matrix alone.  Each such statement would require its own layer-matched contact, identifier join, covariate set, and finite statistic.  The present row is therefore best stated as follows: the $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nonempty bounded coupling to modeled tAI weights in a three-organism finite matrix, while powered translation survival and all higher biological interpretations remain outside the claim.
