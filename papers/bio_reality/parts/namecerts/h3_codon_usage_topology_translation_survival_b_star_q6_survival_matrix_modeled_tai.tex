\paragraph{Mediation rule.}
The admissible readback is mediated.  A direct association $q_i\to p_k$ is not accepted as protein realization unless a translation row $q_i\to t_j$ first survives the declared controls.  The intended controlled form is
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\longrightarrow t_j \longrightarrow p_k,\\
t_j &\in \{\mathrm{tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\},\\
p_k &\in \{\mathrm{matched\ protein\ abundance}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this finite run only the modeled tAI part of $t_j$ is populated.  Matched ribosome-profiling translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA-abundance controls, and matched proteomics rows remain required before the chain can be promoted from modeled codon-adaptation coupling to a controlled protein-abundance mediation matrix.  Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline stay in the control set; they are not promoted into new biological coordinates.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The record is therefore classified as translation-realization modeled underpowered rather than observed translation survival or protein realization.  The finite positive modeled-tAI entries show that the residual coordinates have a nontrivial relation to a codon-adaptation weight surface under the stated construction.  They do not establish observed translation dynamics, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, biochemical mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological rule.  A separate observational contact is required at each higher layer: RPF and matched mRNA rows for translation efficiency, proteomics rows for protein abundance, structural or physical assays for admissibility, functional assays for molecular role, and multi-organism controlled replication before any population-level law is claimed.  In particular, CUN/UUR directionality and any single-organism sign remain finite-row features unless multi-organism controls and simpler-baseline ablations are supplied.

The appropriate biological reading is deliberately narrower than protein realization: the protein-abundance mediation conjecture remains conditional on future matched rows that join codon counts, translation readouts, mRNA controls, and protein measurements in the same organisms under the declared covariates.
