\subsection{Modeled tAI survival surface for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:biorality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record fixes a finite mediation surface whose left coordinates are the nine synonymous residual axes
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}$.
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates, while amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline are kept as controls rather than promoted to new biological coordinates.  The readout used here is not a measured ribosome-footprint or protein-output assay.  It is a modeled codon-adaptation readout: for each codon, a tAI weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ is computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts together with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  Thus the finite row has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i &\in B^{\ast}_{Q6},\\
T_j &= \text{modeled tAI codon-weight readout},\\
S_{ij} &= \text{bounded coupling entry after the declared control boundary}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computation is therefore a bounded translation-readout contact, not a direct biological assertion about elongation dynamics, ribosome occupancy, protein folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.

\paragraph{Organism rows and tRNA payload.}
The modeled-tAI surface was computed on $3$ organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local GtRNAdb payload contained all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each of these organisms.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12, the payload had $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers.  For human, it had $432$ FASTA headers and all $432$ were matched.  For yeast, it had $275$ FASTA headers and all $275$ were matched.  In each of the three organism rows the modeled tAI calculation covered $61$ sense codons, and the zero-raw-$W$ fallback count was $0$.  This matters because the row is not merely a symbolic coordinate construction: the codon weights were instantiated across the full sense-codon table for every organism in the finite panel.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
With coupling threshold $0.01$, the modeled matrix contained $81$ entries above threshold.  The maximum absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry was the same value,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_i| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite row therefore supports the narrower statement that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual axes have nonzero bounded coupling to modeled tAI weights on the three-organism panel.  The break condition recorded for this row is not met at the bounded-contact level: the finite modeled-tAI matrix does contain entries with $|S_{ij}|$ above the threshold.  The same row also records a stronger limitation: $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival, and powered survival would require at least $40$ matched species.  Consequently the claimed layer is best read as modeled translation realization underpowered for population survival, not as ribosome-seq validation and not as proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Mediation boundary.}
The admissible readback is ordered.  A residual-to-protein association is not promoted unless the same residual first survives a translation-readout step.  In symbols, the permitted direction is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i &\longrightarrow T_j \longrightarrow P_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $T_j$ may be modeled tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $P_k$ is a matched protein-abundance readout.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is therefore only a downstream statistical association unless a prior $Q_i\to T_j$ entry has survived under the relevant controls.  The matched mRNA abundance contact is a transcription-layer control for separating expression from translation efficiency; it is not itself a translation readout.

\paragraph{Downstream powered matrix as a separate row.}
A linked protein-abundance mediation row gives useful scale for the later, broader surface, but it does not retroactively turn this modeled-tAI row into measured ribosome evidence.  In that later matrix, the same nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates were used, with per-protein modeled tAI and PAXdb abundance joined across $12$ organisms.  The joined protein counts included $4042$ rows for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for zebrafish, $13295$ for fly, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Its mediation fractions ranged from a very small value in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, $M^{QTP}=0.01850668564589326$, to a large yeast value, $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  Intermediate examples include $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, and $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse.  These numbers show that the downstream statistical mediation surface is nontrivial when protein abundance is attached, but the row itself states that modeled per-protein tAI from tRNA gene-copy and wobble weights is not ribosome profiling, and that conditioning on modeled tAI in cross-sectional CDS/protein-abundance rows is not a proven translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite result is positive only at the bounded modeled-readout level.  The coordinates are real finite residual axes; the tRNA payload covers the standard amino-acid families; the modeled tAI weights cover all $61$ sense codons in each organism; and the modeled coupling matrix has $81$ threshold-exceeding entries with maximum absolute magnitude $3801961.031607896$.  The same evidence blocks stronger language.  It does not establish measured translation survival, ribosome occupancy, protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or a universal biological rule.  Those claims require separate layer-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics.  Until a rank-powered measured translation panel is joined, this chapter remains a modeled tAI coupling record with an explicit no-promotion boundary.
