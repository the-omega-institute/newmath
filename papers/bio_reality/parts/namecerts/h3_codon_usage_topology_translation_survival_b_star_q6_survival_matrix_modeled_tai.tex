\subsection{Modeled tAI survival matrix for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:brioreality-bstar-q6-modeled-tai-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record studies a finite matrix whose row coordinates are the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions and whose column readout is an organism-scoped modeled tAI weight surface.  The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense codon coordinates.  The readout was computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy tables with dos Reis wobble weights, producing modeled codon weights $w_{c}$ rather than measured ribosome-footprint rows.  The organism set has size $3$: \textit{Escherichia coli} K12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The data surface therefore has enough rows for a bounded codon-adaptation contact, but it is not a powered cross-organism survival panel.

\paragraph{Local contact construction.}
For each organism $o$ and sense codon $c$, the contact supplies a modeled tAI weight $w_{o,c}$.  The finite input check reached all $20$ standard amino-acid families in every organism.  The local tRNA payload contained $89$ FASTA headers for \textit{E. coli} K12, of which $87$ matched the standard parsing route and $2$ remained unmatched; it contained $432$ matched headers for human and $275$ matched headers for yeast, with no unmatched headers in either of those two cases.  The modeled tAI computation covered all $61$ sense codons in each of the three organisms, and the zero-raw-weight fallback count was $0$ for all three organisms.  These finite checks justify treating the modeled tAI table as a complete bounded readout over the ordinary genetic-code sense-codon support.

\paragraph{Matrix statistic.}
Let $S_{ij}$ denote the bounded modeled coupling score between residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled translation-readout coordinate $t_j$ after the declared codon-weight computation.  In this record the threshold for a nonzero modeled contact is $0.01$.  The computed matrix has $81$ entries above that threshold.  Its maximum absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the maximum absolute diagonal entry has the same value:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for the bounded modeled-tAI matrix was not met: the finite matrix did contain entries above the contact threshold.  The stronger powered-survival condition was also not met, because that condition requires at least $40$ matched species, whereas this table has $3$ organisms.  The correct reading is therefore a nonzero modeled codon-adaptation coupling on a finite three-organism surface, not a measured ribosome-occupancy or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Control interpretation.}
The admissible readback is a controlled matrix route $q_i \to t_j$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and $t_j$ is modeled tAI from the organism-specific tRNA-copy and wobble-weight table.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline stay in the control layer.  They are not promoted to new biological coordinates.  Direct residual-to-protein association is not licensed by this finite table, because a protein-output claim would require a separate matched protein-abundance contact and a mediation design in which the $q_i \to t_j$ step survives before any $q_i \to p_k$ statement is entertained.

\paragraph{Downstream comparison without promotion.}
A separate matched protein-abundance computation later joined real CDS codon counts, PAXdb abundance, and per-protein modeled tAI for $12$ organisms.  Its organism-specific joined protein counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for zebrafish, $13295$ for fruit fly, $3739$ for \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  In that later decomposition the mediated fraction $M^{QTP}$ lay in $[0,1]$ for every organism, with values including $0.6084594553474505$ in \textit{B. subtilis}, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse, $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{S. solfataricus}.  Those numbers show that the same residual family can be placed into a larger mediation audit, but they do not change the status of the present three-organism modeled-tAI matrix.  The present record remains a bounded modeled translation-readout contact.

\paragraph{Structure and abundance caution.}
A yeast abundance-residual decomposition provides an additional warning against over-reading the modeled contact.  In the yeast five-way join, $4759$ genes were joined across CDS, abundance, measured translation efficiency, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region folding-free-energy proxy.  The residualized association $R^2_{QP}$ was $0.2715520808117803$, while $R^2_{QP\mid S}$ was $0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}$ was $0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}$ was $0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the incremental structure fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the permutation $p$-value for that incremental structure fraction was $0.1258741258741259$.  These finite values support a narrow variance-partition reading and reinforce that modeled tAI contact, protein abundance, and structure proxy are different readout layers.

\paragraph{Residual-axis context.}
A separate structural-order audit over the same nine residual directions identified $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ as the only coordinate passing its fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment was $0.0031744502294756994$, with sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$ and weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$.  The other coordinates did not pass that gate: for example, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ had sign-flip probability $0.142578125$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ had $0.0693359375$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$ had $0.2294921875$, and $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ had $0.779296875$.  This comparison gives a useful finite-row orientation: one residual axis can dominate a structure-adjacent audit while the modeled-tAI survival matrix is still only a bounded translation-readout table.

\paragraph{Boundary statement.}
The result of this chapter is not a claim of observed translation dynamics.  Modeled tAI is a codon-adaptation readout computed from gene-copy and wobble-weight annotations; it is not ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency measured by RPF over mRNA, or a perturbation assay.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universal biological rule follows from this matrix.  Those claims require their own layer-matched contacts and finite statistics.  The phase-level genetic-code checks illustrate the discipline: the locked confirmatory genetic-code readback achieved $40$ successes out of $40$ independent trials with exact one-sided binomial probability $9.094947017729282 \times 10^{-13}$, and the negative and ablation controls had $0$ unexpected successes out of $20$ each; even there, the permitted interpretation remained confined to the genetic-code table contact.  The same containment applies here.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The modeled-tAI matrix is ready as a finite bounded-contact record.  The positive finite fact is that the $9$ residual coordinates over a $61$-codon sense space produced $81$ modeled coupling entries above $0.01$ on a complete three-organism tAI surface.  The negative finite fact is equally important: $n=3$ is below the $40$-species powered-survival requirement, and no ribosome-profiling, matched mRNA TE denominator, or matched proteomics row is supplied by this contact alone.  Thus the chapter supports bounded modeled codon-adaptation coupling for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residuals and refuses promotion to measured translation, protein realization, structure, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality.
