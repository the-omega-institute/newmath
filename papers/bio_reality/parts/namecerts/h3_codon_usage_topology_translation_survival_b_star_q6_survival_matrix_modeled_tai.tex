\subsection{Modeled tAI survival matrix for $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals}
\label{sec:bioreality-bstar-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is the finite matrix whose row coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The column readout is not ribosome profiling and not protein abundance. It is modeled codon-level tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values. The codon universe contains $61$ sense codons. The finite organism set in this run is
$$
\begin{aligned}
O=\{\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12},\ \mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\ \mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}\},\qquad |O|=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
For these three organisms, the modeled tAI readout had $61$ sense-codon weights available in each organism and no zero-raw-$W$ fallback was needed. The local GtRNAdb payload also covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism: the matched FASTA header counts were $87$ of $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ of $432$ for human, and $275$ of $275$ for yeast.

\paragraph{Matrix readback.}
The computed matrix is a bounded modeled-readout contact between residual coordinates and codon-adaptation weights. With coupling threshold $0.01$, the run found $81$ entries above threshold. The largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| = 3{,}801{,}961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the largest absolute diagonal entry was the same number. Thus the finite calculation is nonzero at the modeled-weight level: it records that the chosen $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates are not orthogonal to the tAI surface induced by local tRNA-copy and wobble-weight data. This is the appropriate positive content of the run.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The same finite numbers also impose the main limitation. The break condition for this packet was declared as follows: more data are needed if the finite modeled-tAI matrix has no $|S_{ij}|$ above the bounded-contact threshold, or if powered survival is requested without at least $40$ matched species. The first branch is not met, because $81$ entries exceed $0.01$. The second branch prevents promotion: the run has only $3$ organisms, and the recorded status explicitly states that $3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival. Therefore the observed layer is best named modeled tAI bounded coupling, not powered translation realization.

\paragraph{Relation to protein-abundance mediation.}
A separate finite panel later joined codon residuals, per-protein modeled tAI, and PAXdb protein abundance across $12$ organisms. In that panel, the same nine residual coordinates were used. Each organism had rank sufficiency after controls, with $9$ residual coordinates, one modeled-tAI column, and $23$ control rank in the residualized design. The joined row counts ranged from $1{,}656$ proteins in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $19{,}395$ proteins in mouse; human contributed $19{,}053$ proteins and yeast contributed $5{,}815$ proteins. The mediation fractions $M^{QTP}$ were positive and inside $[0,1]$ for all $12$ organisms, including $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse. These downstream values are useful consistency checks, but they do not retroactively turn the present three-organism modeled-tAI matrix into measured ribosome occupancy. They also remain statistical decompositions, not causal mechanisms.

\paragraph{Controls and nonpromotion rule.}
The intended controls for cross-layer use are amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, an $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance where translation efficiency or protein output is discussed. The modeled tAI contact alone supplies neither matched mRNA rows nor ribosome footprints. Consequently a direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless a same-coordinate $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step first survives under controls. Likewise, a transcription-only edge must remain separated from a mediated edge by matched mRNA abundance control.

\paragraph{Local biological interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is narrow and positive: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis has a nonzero bounded contact with a modeled codon-adaptation surface generated from tRNA-copy and wobble-weight data. The strongest diagonal magnitude and the $81$ above-threshold entries show that the matrix is not empty. The conclusion is also narrow and negative: this is not ribosome profiling, not measured translation efficiency, and not a powered multi-organism survival law. No translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, or universality follows from this packet unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the declared controlled statistic is computed.
