\subsection{Modeled translation mediation for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-translation-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
The record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions
$\{\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.  The translation coordinate $t_j$ is a bounded modeled tAI readout, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble weights over the $61$ sense codons.  The downstream protein coordinate $p_k$ is matched log protein abundance where the powered mediation table is available.  The row is therefore not a direct residual-to-protein assertion; it is a controlled question about whether a residual coordinate can pass through a translation readout before being read against protein abundance.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
For the three-organism modeled-tAI contact, the local tRNA payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The corresponding FASTA-header counts are $89$, $432$, and $275$, with matched-header counts $87$, $432$, and $275$; only the \emph{E. coli} payload has unmatched headers, namely $2$.  In all three organisms the modeled tAI readout has $61$ sense-codon weights and zero raw-weight fallback count $0$.  The finite bounded-coupling table has $81$ entries above the threshold $0.01$, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_i| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is not met for bounded modeled contact: there is nonzero modeled coupling above threshold.  The same row also records that $n=3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival, so this contact is not a ribosome-profiling result and not a proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Powered mediation table.}
A separate powered mediation computation joins real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and measured PAXdb protein abundance for $12$ organisms.  The joined protein counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}$\,$ mc2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{S. cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Each organism has rank-sufficient residualized design diagnostics, with $9$ $q$ coordinates, a rank-$1$ modeled-tAI column after controls, and $23$ control-rank columns from the $24$ control slots.  The controls include an intercept, amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline.

\paragraph{Coordinate identity inside the residual surface.}
The structural-order audit identifies $f_3$-stress as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip probability for a positive increment is $0.03125$.  The remaining residual coordinates have larger sign-flip probabilities: $0.142578125$ for Arg\_AGR, $0.0693359375$ for Ile\_AUA, $0.2294921875$ for K\_AAA, $0.779296875$ for Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, $0.24609375$ for Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, $0.4873046875$ for Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, $0.2158203125$ for Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, and $0.529296875$ for Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY.  Thus the finite table gives a concrete dominant coordinate for this row, but it does not license promotion to folding or function.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-conditioned comparison.}
In yeast, a five-way join of CDS codon counts, measured TE, mRNA half-life, protein abundance, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy contains $4759$ joined rows.  The reported CDS join hit rate is $0.9156038419146592$, and the full join hit rate is $0.8818572656921754$.  After the same residual controls, the total $Q\to P$ fit is $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on the MFE proxy gives $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$; conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$; and adding the MFE proxy to that conditioning set gives $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, and $R^2_{S\leftarrow Q}=0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation probability $0.027972027972027972$.  The structure-incremental right-tail probability is $0.1258741258741259$.  This comparison is useful as a variance partition around the same residual surface; it is not a folded-structure assay.

\paragraph{Boundary of interpretation.}
The admissible readback is the controlled matrix $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$ with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density, and matched mRNA abundance kept in the control layer.  A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not counted as protein realization when the $q_i\to t_j$ step is absent, saturated, or untested.  Modeled tAI and stAI weights are codon-adaptation annotations; they are not ribosome occupancy, not measured translation efficiency, and not a perturbation experiment.  Protein abundance is a downstream steady-state measurement affected by transcription, translation, turnover, localization, modifications, and measurement bias.  Therefore no claim of measured translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows from this row unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that observation and the same controls are re-applied.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The row is ready as a bounded modeled-translation and modeled-mediation record.  It has a finite residual coordinate list, organism-scoped modeled tAI weights, a nonzero bounded-coupling surface over $61$ codons, a powered $12$-organism protein-abundance mediation table, and a concrete dominant residual coordinate in the structural-order audit.  It is not ready as a measured ribosome-profiling survival claim, a causal mediation claim, or a higher-layer biological realization claim.
