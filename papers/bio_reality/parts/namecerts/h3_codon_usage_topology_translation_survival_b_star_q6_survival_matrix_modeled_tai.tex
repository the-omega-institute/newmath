\subsection{Modeled tAI contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates can make contact with a bounded translation-layer readout without being promoted to measured ribosome dynamics or protein realization.  The finite coordinate list is the nine-coordinate panel
$\{\texttt{Arg\_AGR},\texttt{Ile\_AUA},\texttt{K\_AAA},\texttt{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\texttt{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\texttt{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\texttt{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\texttt{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\texttt{f3\_stress}\}$.
The readout is modeled tAI, computed as codon weights $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads and the dos Reis wobble coefficients.  The codon-count space has $61$ sense codons, and the contact is organism-scoped over three organisms: \textit{Escherichia coli} K12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tRNA payload is complete at the standard amino-acid-family level for all three organisms: E. coli K12 has $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, $2$ unmatched headers, and $20$ standard amino-acid families; human has $432$ headers, all matched, and $20$ families; yeast has $275$ headers, all matched, and $20$ families.  The modeled tAI readout has $61$ sense codons in each organism and zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each organism.

\paragraph{Controlled statistic.}
The finite matrix is a bounded coupling matrix from residual coordinates to modeled tAI weights.  The declared threshold is $0.01$.  The computed matrix has $81$ entries above this threshold, with maximum absolute entry
$\max |S_{ij}|=3801961.031607896$ and maximum absolute diagonal value $3801961.031607896$.  The local readback is therefore nonzero at the modeled-weight interface, but it is not a powered organism-level translation-survival result.  The formal accounting is
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{organism}}&=3,\\
N_{\mathrm{sense\ codon}}&=61,\\
N_{|S_{ij}|>0.01}&=81,\\
\max |S_{ij}|&=3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is not met for the bounded modeled-tAI contact, because the finite matrix contains entries above the bounded-contact threshold.  A different condition governs powered survival: three organisms are not enough for the powered survival claim, which requires at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the row supports the status \emph{translation realization modeled underpowered}: positive modeled coupling exists, while measured ribosome or translation-efficiency survival remains unclaimed.

\paragraph{Mediation discipline.}
The admissible biological reading is only a controlled matrix $q_i\to t_j$, where $q_i$ is one of the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and $t_j$ is modeled tAI.  A direct residual-to-protein association is not counted as protein realization unless the same residual first survives a translation-readout step.  In the larger matched-protein analysis, the downstream matrix used $12$ organisms and joined per-protein abundance rows with codon-derived residuals and modeled per-protein tAI.  The organism-specific joined row counts were $4042$ for \textit{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \textit{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \textit{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \textit{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for E. coli K12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Those rows show statistical mediation fractions between modeled tAI and protein abundance, for example $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in E. coli, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{Sulfolobus}.  These values are cited only as downstream compatibility checks.  They do not convert the three-organism modeled-tAI matrix into measured translation, nor do they establish a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Controls and competing explanations.}
The baseline variables remain controls rather than promoted coordinates: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the M-density baseline.  In the yeast structure-mediated abundance audit, the matched five-way join contained $4759$ genes, with reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and readout join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$.  The residual protein-abundance decomposition gave $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$, and the structure residual permutation $p$-value was $0.027972027972027972$.  These finite rows are useful because they show how quickly apparent protein-abundance signal must be separated from structure proxies, measured translation, mRNA stability, and composition controls.  The present modeled-tAI chapter therefore treats the $q_i\to t_j$ matrix as a translation-weight contact, not as a folding, abundance, or phenotype claim.

\paragraph{Neighboring finite checks.}
Several adjacent audits define the boundary of the present result.  The structural-order audit identified \texttt{f3\_stress} as the only coordinate passing its fold-level sign-flip gate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$; the other eight coordinates did not pass that gate.  The evolutionary imprint audit on $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows reported $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and a negative strong selection-imprint verdict.  The observable execution audit in yeast used $3289$ genes and returned an observable execution subblock verdict, while the mass-balance dynamic audit used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals and had $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ with time-shuffle $p=0.9306930693069307$.  The turnover audit used $3290$ genes and gave $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, matched-bin $p=0.8855721393034826$, and recoded $p=1.0$.  These rows are not imported as support for measured translation survival; they are local guardrails showing which stronger biological readings have already been separated from the modeled-tAI contact.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite evidence supports a bounded modeled-tAI coupling statement: the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nonzero modeled translation-weight matrix over three organisms, with $81$ entries above $0.01$ and no raw-weight fallback in the modeled tAI codon table.  The same evidence does not support a powered survival claim, because $N_{\mathrm{organism}}=3$ is below the required $40$ matched species.  It also does not support measured ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection, or universality.  Any one of those stronger claims requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.  Within this chapter the surviving statement is therefore narrow: $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ reaches a modeled translation-readout surface, and the record remains explicitly bounded before measured translation or protein-realization fitting.
