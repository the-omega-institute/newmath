\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival Matrix for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The finite object considered here is the controlled matrix whose rows are $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates and whose columns are bounded translation readouts.  The row list is not open-ended: it contains the nine coordinates $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\text{-stress}$.  Each row is evaluated against a modeled codon-adaptation readout built from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and the dos Reis wobble weights.  The codon domain has $61$ sense codons, so every entry is computed on the same finite synonymous-codon support rather than on a free textual description of codon bias.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The available contact is a modeled tAI contact, not a ribosome-footprint measurement.  Three organisms were used: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Their local GtRNAdb payloads contain all $20$ standard amino-acid families.  The FASTA header counts are $89$, $432$, and $275$, respectively; the matched header counts are $87$, $432$, and $275$, with unmatched counts $2$, $0$, and $0$.  For the modeled tAI calculation, each of the three organisms supplies weights for all $61$ sense codons, and the raw-weight fallback count is $0$ in all three cases.  Thus the finite matrix has a complete modeled codon-weight surface for the organisms used in the computation.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling statistic.}
Let $q_i$ range over the nine residual coordinates and let $w_j$ denote a modeled tAI codon-weight readout.  The reported matrix is a bounded coupling surface, written here as $S_{ij}^{\mathrm{model}}$, with threshold $0.01$.  The observed finite summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{(i,j): |S_{ij}^{\mathrm{model}}|>0.01\} &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}^{\mathrm{model}}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i|S_{ii}^{\mathrm{model}}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for this modeled contact is therefore not met at the bounded-contact level: the finite modeled-tAI matrix does have entries above the threshold.  This is why the chapter may record a positive modeled coupling result.  The same numbers do not license a powered survival claim, because the organism count is only $3$ and the declared powered route requires at least $40$ matched species.

\paragraph{Controls and admissible reading.}
The intended readback is a controlled map from residual coordinates to translation-layer quantities, not a direct residual-to-protein assertion.  Amino-acid sequence composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline remain controls.  In the later protein-abundance mediation table, the same nine-coordinate block is carried with $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for the control matrix, and rank $9$ for the residual-coordinate matrix in each organism.  That downstream table joins per-protein modeled tAI to PAXdb-style abundance rows across $12$ organisms, with joined protein counts $4042$, $11661$, $14410$, $13295$, $3739$, $12418$, $19053$, $19395$, $4129$, $13322$, $5815$, and $1656$.  Its mediation fractions are all in $[0,1]$, ranging from $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast.  Those later rows show that the same coordinate family can be placed inside a larger $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ statistical decomposition, but they remain separate from the three-organism modeled-tAI survival matrix recorded here.

\paragraph{Relation to stronger tests.}
The finite modeled matrix should be read as an underpowered translation-readout contact.  It has nonzero bounded coupling, but it is not a measured ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency validation.  In the yeast structure-mediation table, a five-way join contains $4759$ genes and reports $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the right-tail permutation $p$-values are $0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  These numbers are useful because they illustrate the discipline required for cross-layer promotion: when a new layer is discussed, the row join, the controls, the residualization, and the non-promotion clauses must be carried explicitly.

\paragraph{Axis audit.}
A separate structural-order axis audit on the same nine-coordinate family identifies $f_3\text{-stress}$ as the only coordinate passing the fold-level sign-flip gate.  Its weighted held-out $R^2$ increment is $0.0031744502294756994$, its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, and its sign-flip right-tail probability is $0.03125$.  The other coordinates have larger right-tail probabilities: for example $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ has $0.0693359375$, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ has $0.142578125$, and $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ has $0.779296875$.  This supports a narrow statement about which residual axis carries the strongest structural-order signal in that audit, not a claim that the modeled tAI matrix has observed folding or function data.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The valid conclusion is limited.  The record establishes that the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a complete three-organism modeled tAI coupling matrix, with $81$ entries above the declared bounded-contact threshold and no codon-weight fallback in the modeled tAI calculation.  It also establishes that the result is underpowered for species-level survival: $n=3$ is below the declared $n\geq 40$ requirement.  Therefore no measured translation, ribosome dynamics, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from this chapter.  Any such stronger statement requires its own layer-matched measurement table and its own controlled statistic.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is also inadmissible as protein realization unless the corresponding $Q_i\to T_j$ translation step first survives under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The chapter is ready as a bounded modeled-tAI record.  Its positive content is the finite, fully populated modeled codon-weight matrix and the observed nonzero coupling summary.  Its negative content is equally important: the computation remains a modeled, three-organism translation-readout contact and must not be treated as powered ribosome profiling, proteomics validation, structure evidence, function evidence, mechanism evidence, or a population-level biological rule.
