\subsection{Modeled tAI survival boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite mediation surface.}
This record fixes a finite table whose rows are synonymous residual coordinates and whose translation column is an organism-scoped modeled tAI readout.  The residual side contains exactly nine $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation readout is not ribosome occupancy.  It is the modeled codon weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy data and dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  The codon universe has $61$ sense codons, and the finite organism contact contains three organisms.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K12 the local tRNA payload has $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, $2$ unmatched headers, and all $20$ standard amino-acid families.  For human the corresponding values are $432$, $432$, $0$, and $20$.  For budding yeast they are $275$, $275$, $0$, and $20$.  The modeled tAI computation covers all $61$ sense codons in each of these three organisms, with zero raw-weight fallback count equal to $0$ for all three.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling statistic.}
The observed finite statistic is a modeled contact matrix rather than a powered biological survival result.  With threshold $\tau=0.01$, the finite calculation reports $81$ entries above threshold and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_i^{\mathrm{diag}}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\tau &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the break condition ``no modeled-tAI matrix entry has absolute score above the bounded-contact threshold'' is not met.  The correct reading is narrower: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual table has nonzero modeled coupling to a codon-adaptation weight contact.  It does not yet supply a measured translation-efficiency row, a ribosome-profiling row, or a proteomics row for this survival-matrix claim.  The organism count is $3$, so any powered survival claim that requires at least $40$ matched species remains unavailable.  This is why the layer is recorded as modeled and underpowered rather than as measured translation realization.

\paragraph{Mediation interpretation kept local.}
The admissible readback is a controlled chain $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$, where $T_j$ is a translation readout such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, or translation efficiency, and $P_k$ is matched protein abundance.  A direct residual-to-protein association is not enough.  The residual coordinate must first pass through a translation-readout step, and any downstream protein-abundance statement must be separated from transcription-only signal by matched mRNA abundance control.  Amino-acid sequence, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and M-density remain controls rather than promoted coordinates.

A separate powered matrix for the same biological route has joined real CDS codon counts to protein-abundance rows in three organisms: $3739$ proteins in \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $4129$ in \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ in \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In that decomposition the residual design has $9$ $Q$ coordinates, the residualized translation design has rank $1$, the joint $Q,T$ design has rank $10$, the control design has rank $23$, and the control column count is $24$ in each organism.  The mediated fractions are
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{E.\ coli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}} &= 0.08397602919861291,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values lie in the unit interval, but their role here is only to state that a statistical conditioning calculation exists for matched CDS/protein-abundance rows.  They are not causal effects, not perturbation evidence, and not a proof that modeled tAI is an observed ribosome mechanism.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion boundary.}
The finite table supports a bounded modeled-tAI contact for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  It does not by itself establish translation dynamics, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, or universality.  Ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency data would be needed for a measured translation readout.  Matched proteomics can test downstream protein abundance only after the translation step is present and controlled.  Matched mRNA abundance is a covariate for separating transcription-layer signal; it is not a translation or protein readout.  Therefore the safe conclusion is: the modeled tAI matrix has nonzero finite coupling above the declared threshold, while powered measured survival and any higher biological layer remain outside the claim until separate contacts provide the corresponding rows.
