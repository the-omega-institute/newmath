\subsection{Modeled tAI survival matrix for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual surface}
\label{subsec:biorreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The finite object in this chapter is the row set formed by synonymous residual coordinates $q_i$, modeled translation readouts $t_j$, and downstream protein-abundance readouts $p_k$. The residual side has nine named coordinates: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f_3$-stress. These coordinates are evaluated in the $61$-sense-codon count space, with amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline kept as controls rather than as new biological coordinates. The readout used here is not an observed ribosome-footprint statistic. It is a modeled codon adaptation weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ built from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and the dos Reis wobble $s$-values.

\paragraph{Observed finite rows.}
Three organisms are present in the modeled matrix: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The local GtRNAdb payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism. The bacterial row has $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers; the human row has $432$ headers, all matched; the yeast row has $275$ headers, all matched. The modeled tAI readout assigns all $61$ sense codons in each organism, and the zero-raw-$W$ fallback count is $0$ for all three rows. Thus the finite modeled readout is internally complete at the codon-weight level, even though it is not a measured ribosome or proteomics panel.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
Let $S_{ij}$ denote the bounded coupling score between residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled translation readout $t_j$ after the declared controls are held fixed. The finite computation reports a threshold $\theta=0.01$ and finds $81$ entries above threshold. The maximum absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j} |S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
with the same value reported for the maximum absolute diagonal entry. The finite break condition is therefore not met for the modeled-contact computation: the modeled tAI matrix is not empty above the bounded-contact threshold. This supports a narrow conclusion: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis has nonzero modeled-tAI coupling on the three-organism finite surface.

\paragraph{Power boundary.}
The same row set also fixes the refusal boundary. The organism count is $n=3$, and the registered break condition states that powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species. Therefore the present result is a bounded modeled-tAI contact, not a powered translation-survival claim. It does not by itself establish ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from RPF/mRNA, matched proteomics output, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality. A direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a translation-readout step $q_i \to t_j$ on layer-matched rows.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation context.}
A separate twelve-organism protein-abundance computation gives a useful comparison surface. It uses the same nine residual coordinates and a per-protein modeled tAI readout, joined to measured PAXdb protein abundance as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\ ppm})$. The per-organism joins are substantial: $4042$ proteins for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In that finite mediation decomposition, every reported $M^{QTP}$ fraction lies in the unit interval. The largest reported mediated fraction is the yeast value $0.9491551041216973$, with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$. Other examples include \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655 with $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$ and indirect difference $0.06599656708487024$, and human with $M^{QTP}=0.27206289160119157$ and indirect difference $0.012119218668341744$. These numbers are compatible with a statistical mediation readback, but the readout $T$ is still modeled tAI rather than observed ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Adjacent checks.}
The yeast structure-mediation audit joins $4759$ rows across CDS, protein abundance, measured TE, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy. Its direct residual-to-protein association is $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$; conditioning on the structure proxy changes this to $0.2706601505000995$, while conditioning on measured TE plus mRNA stability gives $0.13773967696311287$, and conditioning on all three gives $0.13759011208318542$. The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the permutation right-tail value for the structure increment is $0.1258741258741259$. Thus the structure proxy is not promoted to causal structure evidence here.

\paragraph{Other finite verdicts.}
Several linked audits sharpen the boundary. The structural-order axis test computes the same nine axes and identifies $f_3$-stress as the only significant fold-level axis, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$. The cross-ortholog imprint audit uses $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species; its positive selection-imprint gate is not passed, with $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and null $95\%$ maximum $0.05427157066522958$. The dynamic mass-balance audit uses $120$ genes and $718$ intervals; its final increment is negative, with $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{TEbin}}=0.6831683168316832$, and $p_{\mathrm{timeshuffle}}=0.9306930693069307$. The turnover audit similarly reports no positive residual beyond its controls, with $3290$ genes, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-13.68819707902732$ bits, $p_{\mathrm{matchedbin}}=0.8855721393034826$, and $p_{\mathrm{recoded}}=1.0$. By contrast, the observable execution decomposition reports a positive bounded subblock in yeast over $3289$ genes, showing that some rate-proxy slot survives its null classes without licensing a universal mechanism.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter therefore records a precise status. The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a finite, nonzero modeled-tAI coupling matrix on three organisms, with $81$ entries above $0.01$ and complete modeled sense-codon weights for all three rows. This is enough to keep the translation-readout question open and mathematically concrete. It is not enough to claim powered translation survival, ribosome occupancy, protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or a general biological law. Those stronger claims require separate same-row contacts: measured ribosome or TE data, matched mRNA controls, matched protein abundance, and any higher-layer assay appropriate to the layer being tested.
