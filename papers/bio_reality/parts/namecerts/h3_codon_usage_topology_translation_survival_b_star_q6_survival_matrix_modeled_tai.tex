\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a finite mediation surface whose rows are synonymous residual coordinates, modeled translation readouts, and matched downstream abundance readouts.  The coordinate side consists of the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual axes
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon count space has $61$ sense codon coordinates.  The modeled translation readout is the codon-level tAI weight $w_c$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  The finite organism panel used for this bounded contact has $n=3$ organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  This is therefore a modeled codon-adaptation contact, not a measured ribosome-profiling panel and not a powered multi-species survival experiment.

\paragraph{Data coverage.}
The local GtRNAdb payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each organism.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 it contains $89$ FASTA headers, of which $87$ are matched to the standard family parser and $2$ remain unmatched.  For human the count is $432$ headers, all $432$ matched.  For budding yeast the count is $275$ headers, all $275$ matched.  The modeled tAI computation supplies all $61$ sense codon weights in each organism, with zero raw-$W$ fallback count equal to $0$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12, human, and yeast.  Thus the finite readout matrix has no missing modeled codon-weight coordinate at the sense-codon level.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling statistic.}
The modeled surface reports a nonzero bounded coupling between $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and tAI-derived readouts.  With contact threshold $0.01$, the finite matrix has $81$ entries above threshold.  The largest absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the largest absolute diagonal entry is the same number,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for this contact is not met: the finite modeled-tAI matrix does contain entries above the bounded-contact threshold.  The positive statement is nevertheless only a modeled contact statement.  It says that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis reaches a tAI-weight surface under the declared finite construction; it does not say that measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, or a population-level biological rule has been observed.

The joined row counts are $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. \paragraph{Dominant coordinate audit.}
The structural-order audit over the same nine $B^*_{Q6}$ axes identifies exactly one fold-level sign-flip survivor.  The dominant coordinate is $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$, with weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, held-out $R^2$ increment $0.0031744502294756994$, and sign-flip right-tail probability $0.03125$.  The remaining axes do not pass the same gate: for example $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ has held-out increment $0.0013067592718559024$ with $p=0.0693359375$, while $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$ has a negative increment $-0.0003205780368125179$ with $p=0.779296875$.  This audit is useful because it prevents the modeled-tAI contact from being read as a uniform law over every synonymous axis.  The finite evidence is concentrated, coordinate-sensitive, and tied to the declared residualization scheme.

The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the structure incremental fraction beyond measured translation efficiency and mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$, and $R^2(S\sim Q)=0.003774248455422586$ has permutation right-tail probability $0.027972027972027972$.  These numbers keep transcript abundance, modeled translation readout, structure proxy, and protein abundance in separate statistical roles.  They also mark the limit of the present chapter: a tAI-mediated or TE-conditioned abundance decomposition is not a folding experiment, a physical assay, a perturbation mechanism, or a functional phenotype test.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite modeled-tAI contact is accepted as a bounded translation-readout survival record for the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis.  The accepted statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists (i,j)\quad |S_{ij}|>0.01
\end{aligned}
$$
inside the modeled tAI surface over the three-organism GtRNAdb panel, with $81$ finite entries above threshold and complete $61$-sense-codon modeled weights for each organism.  The stronger claim of powered measured translation survival is not made from this row set because $n=3$ is below the declared powered-species requirement of at least $40$ matched species, and because the readout is modeled tAI rather than ribosome profiling or directly measured translation efficiency.  No translation mechanism, protein folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection law, or universality follows here unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that specific measurement and the same-coordinate survival step remains positive under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, matched mRNA abundance, and simpler-baseline controls.
