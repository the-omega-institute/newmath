\subsection{Modeled tAI survival matrix for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record tests whether the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates have a bounded translation-readout contact when the readout is not ribosome profiling, not measured translation efficiency, and not protein abundance, but a modeled codon adaptation weight derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble penalties. The finite row surface is the set of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$ only in the restricted sense that $q_i$ is one of the residual coordinates, $t_j$ is the modeled tAI codon-weight readout, and $p_k$ is kept out of the primary survival call unless a later matched protein-abundance row is explicitly joined through a translation step. The residual coordinates computed for this surface are
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon space has $61$ sense codons. The modeled tAI readout was computed for $3$ organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. In each organism the codon-level modeled tAI table covers all $61$ sense codons, with zero raw-$W$ fallback count equal to $0$ in all three cases. The local GtRNAdb payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each organism. The FASTA-header counts are $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast; matched FASTA-header counts are $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively, leaving only $2$ unmatched headers in the \emph{E. coli} K12 payload and none in the human or yeast payload.

\paragraph{Modeled coupling statistic.}
Let $S_{ij}$ denote the bounded-contact coupling score between residual coordinate $q_i$ and translation readout $t_j$ after keeping amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline as controls rather than promoted coordinates. In this run the declared threshold was $0.01$. The modeled matrix contains $81$ entries above threshold, and the largest absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\tau &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite result therefore supports a bounded modeled-tAI contact: the residual basis is not empty against a codon-adaptation readout computed from tRNA copy number and wobble weights. The break condition for this specific modeled matrix is not met, since the finite matrix does have entries with $|S_{ij}|>0.01$. The layer label remains deliberately narrow: this is translation-realization only in the modeled, underpowered sense. It is not a measured ribosome-occupancy result, not a measured translation-efficiency result, and not a proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The same finite row data also records why this surface cannot be promoted to a powered survival claim. The organism count is $3$, while the registered power boundary requires at least $40$ matched species before powered survival can be requested. Hence the verdict is not that $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ has a population-level translation law. The correct reading is that a finite modeled-tAI matrix exists and is nonzero under the bounded-contact threshold, but the sample size and readout type keep the claim inside a modeled-contact envelope. A positive entry in this matrix is a signal that the coordinate survives contact with a modeled codon-adaptation readout; it is not a universal codon-bias rule and it is not a direct biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance comparison.}
A separate downstream finite matrix gives a useful guardrail for interpreting the modeled-tAI result. In the matched protein-abundance mediation run, the same $9$ residual coordinates were joined to per-protein modeled tAI and PAXdb protein abundance across $12$ organisms. The joined protein counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. In that separate surface, all organisms passed the recorded rank-sufficiency diagnostic, with $q$-coordinate count $9$, residualized $T$ rank $1$, residualized $Q$ rank $9$, and combined $Q,T$ rank $10$ in each organism.

\paragraph{Mediation scalars.}
The downstream mediation fractions further show why direct residual-to-protein readings must be routed through a translation readout rather than read as immediate protein realization. The recorded $M^{QTP}$ fractions lie in the unit interval for all $12$ organisms. They are $0.6084594553474505$ for \emph{B. subtilis}, $0.4872268357227724$ for \emph{C. elegans}, $0.3157053953614641$ for zebrafish, $0.6165167748739756$ for fly, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli}, $0.1976903964724546$ for chicken, $0.27206289160119157$ for human, $0.15488799907323988$ for mouse, $0.08397602919861291$ for \emph{M. smegmatis}, $0.17489681798543982$ for rat, $0.9491551041216973$ for yeast, and $0.01850668564589326$ for \emph{S. solfataricus}. For yeast, for example, the total residual-to-protein $R^2$ is $0.29837624994140827$, the direct component after conditioning on modeled tAI is $0.0151709093608293$, and the indirect difference is $0.28320534058057895$. For human, the corresponding numbers are $0.04454565118019448$, $0.03242643251185274$, and $0.012119218668341744$. These numbers are not imported back into the three-organism survival result as proof of measured translation; they only demonstrate the intended finite route for a later $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ analysis when matched rows are present.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The admissible statement is therefore precise. The $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis has a nonzero bounded coupling to a modeled tAI readout computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights in a three-organism matrix. The matrix has $81$ entries above the $0.01$ threshold and no zero raw-$W$ fallback in the three modeled codon-weight tables. Because the surface has only $3$ organisms and because the readout is modeled tAI rather than ribosome-profiling TE, the result remains underpowered for a measured survival law. No observed translation, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, causal mechanism, organismal phenotype, or universal biological rule follows from this matrix unless a separate layer-matched measurement supplies that contact and the relevant controlled statistic is computed. Direct $Q_i \to P_k$ protein-abundance association is also not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a controlled $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step and the downstream $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ matrix is evaluated with matched mRNA abundance, amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and $M$-density controls.
