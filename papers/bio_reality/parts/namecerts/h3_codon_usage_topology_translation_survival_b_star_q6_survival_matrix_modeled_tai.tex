\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival Surface for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-modeled-tai-survival-surface}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a finite translation-readout surface rather than a direct protein-realization claim.  The coordinate side is the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual family
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}$.
The readout side is modeled codon-level tAI, computed from local GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads with the dos Reis wobble weights.  The sense-codon support has size $61$, and the finite organism panel has size $3$: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tRNA payload is not a symbolic proxy only: the local files contain all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism, with $89$ FASTA headers for \emph{E. coli} K-12 of which $87$ match standard families, $432$ of $432$ headers matched in human, and $275$ of $275$ headers matched in budding yeast.

\paragraph{Modeled readout construction.}
For each organism, the modeled tAI readout assigns a weight $w_c$ to each of the $61$ sense codons.  The finite computation reports no zero raw-weight fallback for \emph{E. coli} K-12, no zero raw-weight fallback for human, and no zero raw-weight fallback for budding yeast in this codon-level readout.  Thus the matrix is defined on the full sense-codon support for each of the three organisms.  The readout should be read as a bounded codon-adaptation weight surface, not as ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.

\paragraph{Observed finite coupling.}
The modeled matrix contains nonzero bounded coupling between the residual coordinates and the tAI readout.  With threshold $0.01$, the finite run reports $81$ entries above threshold, maximum absolute entry
$3801961.031607896$, and maximum absolute diagonal entry
$3801961.031607896$.  In the notation of this chapter, the bounded-contact condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
  \#\{(i,j): |S_{ij}|>0.01\} &= 81.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is therefore not met for the modeled contact: the finite modeled-tAI matrix does contain entries above the declared bounded-contact threshold.  The chapter may consequently report a modeled translation-readout coupling surface for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The same row also records the decisive limitation: $3$ organisms are not enough for a powered survival statement under the declared rule requiring at least $40$ matched species when powered survival is requested.  The layer label is therefore \emph{translation-realization modeled underpowered}.  This is stronger than a null row, because the finite modeled surface is nonzero; it is weaker than measured translation survival, because the readout is modeled tAI and the organism count is only $3$.  The supported statement is the existence of a bounded modeled-tAI coupling matrix, not a population law.

\paragraph{Controls kept as controls.}
The admissible readback keeps amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline as controls.  A residual coordinate is not promoted into a biological object merely because it lies in the nine-coordinate list.  The intended comparison is always against controlled codon-count and composition baselines.  The matrix is therefore a finite mediation candidate of the form $Q_i \to T_j$, where $Q_i$ is one of the nine residual coordinates and $T_j$ is modeled tAI.  A downstream $Q_i \to T_j \to P_k$ protein-abundance statement requires an attached protein row and a surviving translation step; a direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association alone is not enough.

\paragraph{Downstream consistency checks.}
The larger finite ledger is consistent with this cautious reading.  A separate twelve-organism protein-abundance mediation run joined modeled per-protein tAI and PAXdb abundance for organisms with row counts $4042$, $11661$, $14410$, $13295$, $3739$, $12418$, $19053$, $19395$, $4129$, $13322$, $5815$, and $1656$ proteins.  That run reports positive statistical mediation fractions, including $0.9491551041216973$ for budding yeast, $0.612922252793667$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, and $0.27206289160119157$ for human, but it explicitly remains a statistical decomposition using modeled per-protein tAI and matched protein abundance.  It is not a causal translation mechanism.  A yeast structure-mediation audit also preserves the boundary: the five-way join has $4759$ genes, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$, $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$, right-tail $p=0.027972027972027972$ for the $S_Q$ structure component, and right-tail $p=0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  These numbers support finite variance-partition statements, not folding or physical-realization claims.  Likewise, the cross-ortholog selection-imprint row is explicitly negative for the strong selection-imprint claim: $n_{\mathrm{orthogroups}}=80$, $n_{\mathrm{rows}}=240$, $n_{\mathrm{species}}=3$, $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, null $95\%$ maximum $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$, and positive selection imprint is false.

\paragraph{External genetic-code contact.}
The independent genetic-code contact remains below the translation-readout layer.  The locked confirmatory code-table check reports $40$ successes in $40$ independent replication trials with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and $40$ successes in $40$ boundary-probe trials with the same probability.  It also reports zero unexpected successes among $20$ negative controls and zero unexpected successes among $20$ critical-ablation controls.  These figures support the earlier codon-geometry contact, but they do not convert the modeled-tAI surface into measured ribosome dynamics or protein output.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
The chapter claim is therefore exact: the finite modeled-tAI surface for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates is nonzero above the bounded-contact threshold on a three-organism panel, and the break condition for absence of modeled coupling is not met.  The chapter does not claim measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, selection, phenotype, or universality.  Any such stronger statement needs its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, no $Q_i \to P_k$ protein-abundance association may be read as protein realization unless a same-coordinate $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step survives first under the declared controls.
