\subsection{Modeled tAI Survival of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ Residual Coordinates}
\label{subsec:biorality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates as a finite mediation surface rather than as a direct protein-output assertion.  The finite coordinate list used in the modeled translation readout is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense codons.  The translation readout is modeled per-codon tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The three-organism bounded panel is $n=3$, consisting of \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, human, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The tRNA payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each of these organisms: the bacterial payload has $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers; the human payload has $432$ headers, all matched; and the yeast payload has $275$ headers, all matched.  For the modeled tAI computation, all three organisms have $61$ sense-codon weights; the zero raw-weight fallback count is $0$ for each of the three organisms in this bounded panel.

\paragraph{Bounded survival matrix.}
Let $S_{ij}$ denote the finite modeled coupling entry from residual coordinate $q_i$ to modeled translation readout coordinate $t_j$, after the readout has been kept at the translation-weight layer.  The bounded-contact threshold is $0.01$.  In the modeled tAI matrix, $81$ entries exceed this threshold in absolute value, and the reported maximum absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| = 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The same value appears as the maximum absolute diagonal entry, $3801961.031607896$.  Therefore the finite modeled-tAI contact is not empty: the break condition requiring additional data when no $|S_{ij}|$ exceeds threshold is not met.  This is a bounded translation-weight survival record, not a measured ribosome-profiling result.  The finite panel has only three organisms, so it is explicitly underpowered for a population-level survival law; the recorded condition requires at least $40$ matched species before a powered species-level survival claim can be made.

\paragraph{Statistical mediation readback.}
For each organism, the protein readout is $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  The translation readout is per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T = \frac{\sum_c n_c\,w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the CDS codon count and $w_c$ is the modeled tAI weight from the organism-matched tRNA table.  The reported mediated fraction $M^{QTP}$ lies in the unit interval for all six organisms: $0.3157053953614641$ in \emph{D. rerio}, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.1976903964724546$ in chicken, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, $0.08397602919861291$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast. \paragraph{Interpretation at the allowed layer.}
The finite result supports a narrow statement: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nonzero bounded coupling to modeled tAI, and in a six-organism joined CDS/protein table the residual-to-protein association can be decomposed by conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI.  This is a statistical mediation decomposition over finite rows.  It is not a causal effect, not an observed ribosome-occupancy measurement, and not a perturbation or rescue result.  The modeled readout is derived from tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights; it is not ribo-seq.  The protein abundance readout is steady-state abundance and is not isolated from mRNA abundance, protein turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, or pathway context.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
No translation mechanism, folding behavior, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from these rows.  A direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless the same coordinate first survives a $Q_i \to T_j$ translation-readout step under the declared controls.  The three-organism modeled-tAI surface is kept as bounded readout contact, while the six-organism protein-abundance decomposition is kept as statistical mediation.  Any stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched contact, such as organism- and CDS-matched ribosome profiling with matched mRNA for measured translation efficiency, or perturbation data for mechanism.
