\subsection{Modeled tAI survival matrix for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record fixes a finite mediation surface whose rows are synonymous residual coordinates $q_i$, whose middle readouts are bounded translation annotations $t_j$, and whose possible downstream readouts are matched protein-abundance quantities $p_k$.  The primary finite row computes nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i \in \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout used here is modeled per-codon tAI weight, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble parameters.  The codon-count space has $61$ sense codons.  Three organisms are present in this row: \textit{Escherichia coli} K12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local tRNA payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The FASTA header audits are $89$ headers with $87$ matched in \textit{E. coli} K12, $432$ with $432$ matched in human, and $275$ with $275$ matched in yeast.  The modeled tAI computation covers all $61$ sense codons in all three organisms, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in each case.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
The finite modeled-tAI matrix is not empty.  With coupling threshold $0.01$, the bounded coupling audit reports $81$ entries above threshold.  The maximum absolute score is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_i^{\mathrm{diag}}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition is therefore not met for the bounded modeled-contact row: the matrix has nonzero entries above threshold.  The correct finite verdict is narrower than measured translation survival.  It says that the residual coordinates have a nontrivial modeled-tAI coupling surface in the three-organism row, while the row remains underpowered for a powered survival claim because $n=3$ organisms is below the declared requirement of at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the chapter records a bounded translation-readout contact, not a ribosome-profiling result and not a proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Controls and mediation discipline.}
The row is interpreted only through controlled mediation.  A direct $q_i \to p_k$ association is not admissible as protein realization unless a translation readout $q_i \to t_j$ first survives under the declared controls.  The controls retained as controls, rather than promoted to coordinates, are amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline.  In the larger protein-abundance mediation audit, the same nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual set is used with $24$ control columns and rank diagnostics requiring $\operatorname{rank}(X)=9$ after residualization.  That audit spans $12$ organisms and reports joined protein rows such as $19{,}053$ for human, $19{,}395$ for mouse, $13{,}295$ for fly, $5{,}815$ for yeast, $3{,}739$ for \textit{E. coli} K12 MG1655, and $1{,}656$ for \textit{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The same audit computes per-protein modeled tAI from all-tRNA copy totals, including $432$ in human, $407$ in mouse, $275$ in yeast, $90$ in \textit{E. coli}, and $47$ in \textit{Sulfolobus}.  Its mediation fractions remain statistical decompositions: for example $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \textit{E. coli}, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \textit{Sulfolobus}.  These numbers show that the finite machinery can form a $Q \to T \to P$ table when matched rows exist, but they do not upgrade the three-organism modeled-tAI matrix into observed ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Linked finite-row checks.}
A yeast structure-mediation row supplies a useful local check on the scale of matched data.  It joins $4{,}759$ genes across proteomics, translation-efficiency, mRNA half-life, coding sequence, and a $5'$ coding-region RNAfold MFE proxy, with reported join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$ and CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$.  In that row the residualized association values are $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, with permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$ for $S_Q$ structure association and $p=0.1258741258741259$ for the incremental structure fraction.  A separate structural-order identity row finds that the only fold-level sign-flip positive coordinate is $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  These linked rows are not evidence that the modeled-tAI matrix measures structure; they only show that later finite rows can be audited without letting the translation readout absorb higher-layer claims.

\paragraph{Local readback.}
The matrix is read as
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q_i &\longrightarrow T_j,\\
T_j &\in \{\mathrm{modeled\ tAI},\mathrm{stAI},\mathrm{ribosome\ occupancy},\mathrm{translation\ efficiency}\},\\
P_k &\in \{\mathrm{matched\ protein\ abundance}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
with the present row occupying only the modeled-tAI position.  A positive bounded modeled entry may guide where to look next, but it is not by itself a measured translation-efficiency result.  Matched mRNA abundance is used to separate transcription-layer signal from translation-layer signal; it is not itself a translation readout.  Matched proteomics is a downstream abundance contact; it is not a folding, function, stability, localization, or phenotype assay.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The chapter therefore supports the following finite statement: the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family has a nonzero three-organism modeled-tAI coupling matrix over $61$ sense codons, with $81$ threshold-passing entries and all standard amino-acid tRNA families present in the local payload.  The same statement refuses stronger readings.  No measured translation survival, ribosome occupancy, protein realization, folding consequence, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection law, or universality follows from this row.  Any such claim requires a separate layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  In particular, a $q_i \to p_k$ protein-abundance association remains blocked unless the corresponding $q_i \to t_j$ translation step survives first under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, $M$-density, matched mRNA, and simpler-baseline controls.
