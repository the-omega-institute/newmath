\subsection{Modeled tAI survival matrix for $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bior reality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This chapter records a bounded translation-readout test for the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual basis.  The finite surface consists of triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$, but the computation reported here stops at the modeled translation coordinate $t_j$: the tested readout is per-codon modeled tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy rows with the dos Reis wobble weights.  The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon count space has $61$ sense coordinates.  The modeled tAI readout was computed for three organisms: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  In each organism the tAI readout covered all $61$ sense codons and required no zero-$W$ fallback in the modeled-tAI computation.

\paragraph{Row audit.}
The local GtRNAdb payload covers all standard amino-acid families in each of the three tested organisms.  For \emph{E. coli} K12, $89$ FASTA headers were present, $87$ matched the standard parser, $2$ were unmatched, and the standard amino-acid family count was $20$.  For human, $432$ headers were present and all $432$ matched, again covering $20$ standard families.  For yeast, $275$ headers were present and all $275$ matched, also covering $20$ standard families.  Thus the finite contact is not missing a standard amino-acid family, even though it remains a modeled codon-adaptation contact rather than a ribosome-observation contact.

\paragraph{Modeled coupling result.}
Let $S_{ij}$ denote the bounded modeled-coupling score between residual coordinate $q_i$ and modeled tAI readout component $t_j$ after projecting the residual basis into the codon-count surface used by the computation.  The registered threshold was $0.01$.  The finite modeled matrix had $81$ entries with $|S_{ij}|>0.01$, and the largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| = 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest diagonal magnitude was the same value,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_{ii}| = 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The recorded break condition was not met: the finite modeled-tAI matrix was not empty above the bounded-contact threshold.  This supports a restricted conclusion: the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis has nonzero contact with a modeled codon-adaptation readout on the three-organism surface.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The same record explicitly refuses powered translation-survival promotion.  The organism count is $n=3$, whereas the powered-survival route requires at least $40$ matched species.  Consequently the layer label is \texttt{translation\_realization\_modeled\_underpowered}, not measured ribosome survival and not cross-organism biological law.  The computation is a finite modeled-tAI contact: it uses tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights to form $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$, then asks whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are nontrivially coupled to that modeled readout.  It does not observe ribosome occupancy, elongation speed, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.

Its mediated fractions were positive and in $[0,1]$ for all organisms, including $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.6165167748739756$ in fly, $0.4872268357227724$ in worm, $0.27206289160119157$ in human, and $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus}. A yeast five-way join gives an additional boundary check for protein abundance and a sequence-derived structure proxy.  The joined dataset had $4759$ genes, with a reported CDS join hit rate of $0.9156038419146592$ and an overall join hit rate of $0.8818572656921754$.  After residualization against $24$ control columns, the direct $B^*_{Q6}$ to protein-abundance residual association had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction was $0.0005507778820193332$.  The permutation $p$-value for the $S_Q$ structure association was $0.027972027972027972$, while the right-tail $p$-value for the incremental structure fraction was $0.1258741258741259$.  These values show why the translation and abundance interfaces must be read as controlled statistical surfaces rather than as a folding or mechanism claim.

\paragraph{Other finite checks on promotion.}
The broader finite ledger is mixed and therefore reinforces the bounded reading.  A yeast observable execution decomposition reported $3289$ genes and concluded that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  A structural-order axis audit over the same nine residual coordinates identified $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$ as the only significant axis, with sign-flip $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$.  By contrast, a cross-ortholog selection-imprint run over $80$ orthogroups, $240$ rows, and $3$ species did not support the strong selection-imprint gate: its observed $\Delta R^2$ was $0.03250515015613468$, the maximum null-$95$ $\Delta R^2$ was $0.05427157066522958$, and leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2$ was $-0.0021477729853302424$.  A mass-balance closure run on $120$ genes and $718$ intervals had $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ and $\Delta\mathrm{DL}=-668.8046070381928$ bits.  A turnover audit on $3290$ genes likewise stayed negative, with $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, matched-bin $p=0.8855721393034826$, and recoded $p=1.0$.

\paragraph{Local verdict.}
The finite modeled-tAI matrix is nonzero above threshold and therefore gives a real bounded readout contact for the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis.  The allowed statement is narrow: modeled codon-adaptation weights, built from tRNA copy counts and wobble parameters, couple to the residual coordinates on a three-organism surface.  No translation realization in the measured ribosome sense follows from this alone.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, causal mechanism, selection law, or universality follows unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the corresponding rows and the relevant controlled statistic.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is also not enough for protein realization unless a surviving $Q_i\to T_j$ translation-readout step is first established under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls.
