\subsection{Modeled tAI survival boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The $q_i$ coordinates are the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residuals computed in the finite run: Arg\_AGR, Ile\_AUA, K\_AAA, Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY, and $f3\_stress$.  The translation coordinate $t_j$ in the primary run is modeled codon-level tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble weights.  The codon count space has $61$ sense codons.  Three organisms entered the modeled-tAI boundary calculation: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The finite contact is therefore a modeled translation-weight contact, not a direct ribosome-occupancy, protein-output, folding, function, phenotype, or universal-law observation.

\paragraph{Modeled tAI contact.}
The GtRNAdb payload covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  For \emph{E. coli} K12, $89$ FASTA headers were present, $87$ were matched, and $2$ remained unmatched.  For human, all $432$ headers were matched.  For yeast, all $275$ headers were matched.  The modeled readout assigned weights across all $61$ sense codons in each organism.  The zero-raw-$W$ fallback count was $0$ for \emph{E. coli} K12, $0$ for human, and $0$ for yeast in the codon-level survival run.  Thus the modeled tAI layer is finite and populated for the organisms in the row set, although it remains a model-derived codon-adaptation readout rather than a measured translation experiment.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
The bounded coupling audit used threshold $0.01$.  It found $81$ entries above threshold, with maximum absolute entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and maximum absolute diagonal entry
$$
\begin{aligned}
  \max_i |S_i| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite break condition for the modeled contact was not met: the matrix was not empty above the bounded-contact threshold.  This supports the narrow statement that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual surface has nonzero modeled-tAI coupling under the encoded codon-weight construction.  It does not by itself establish powered organism-level survival, because the organism count is $3$, while the finite-row audit explicitly records that powered survival would require at least $40$ matched species.  The correct classification of this row is therefore translation-realization modeled underpowered: useful as a bounded codon-adaptation matrix, insufficient as a population-level translation-survival law.

\paragraph{Mediation readback.}
The downstream protein-abundance row supplies a second finite check on the same family of coordinates.  It used the nine-dimensional $X_Q$ residual block and per-protein modeled tAI as $T$, with measured PAXdb protein abundance as $Y=\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$.  The joined protein counts were $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. mc2 155, and $5815$ for yeast.  After residualizing controls, each organism had rank $9$ for $X_Q$, rank $1$ for $T$, and rank $10$ for the joint $(X_Q,T)$ block.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls were $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively.  The mediation fractions were $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli}, $0.08397602919861291$ in \emph{M. smegmatis}, and $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, all within the unit interval.  The corresponding indirect $R^2$ components, computed as $c-c'$, were $0.06599656708487024$, $0.0026947984781311544$, and $0.28320534058057895$.  These numbers show that a controlled statistical decomposition can be formed on matched CDS/protein rows, but the decomposition remains statistical and model-conditioned.

\paragraph{Admissible reading.}
The admissible biological reading is deliberately local.  A residual coordinate may be discussed as surviving into a modeled tAI readout only through the finite $Q_i\to T_j$ matrix.  A residual-to-protein abundance association may be discussed only through the conditioned $Q_i\to T_j\to P_k$ decomposition, and only after the modeled translation coordinate is kept in the middle.  Direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not sufficient evidence of protein realization when the translation step is absent, null, or untested.  Matched mRNA abundance belongs in the control set that separates transcriptional abundance from translation-layer signal; it is not itself a translation readout.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, CDS length, and M-density baseline remain controls rather than promoted coordinates.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No measured ribosome dynamics, no folded structure, no physical admissibility, no molecular function, no mechanism, no phenotype, and no universal biological rule follows from this row.  Modeled tAI weights are not ribosome profiling.  Protein abundance is not folding, stability, localization, turnover, post-translational state, function, or organismal phenotype.  The finite data justify a bounded modeled-tAI coupling record and a controlled protein-abundance mediation decomposition on available rows; any stronger translation, structure, physical, functional, mechanistic, or universal claim requires a separate layer-matched contact and a separately recorded statistic.
