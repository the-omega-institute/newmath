\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This chapter records the bounded translation-readout contact for the $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates.  The finite row object is a matrix whose rows are residual coordinates and whose columns are modeled translation-adaptation readouts computed from organism-scoped tRNA gene-copy tables and dos Reis wobble weights.  The residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $|R|=9$.  The codon universe used for the modeled readout has $61$ sense-codon columns.  The organism panel has size $n=3$, with \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} represented in the local GtRNAdb payload.  The tRNA payload check reached all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The FASTA header counts were $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast; the corresponding matched header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$, leaving only two unmatched headers in the \emph{E. coli} table and none in the human or yeast tables.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout.}
For each organism, the chapter uses the modeled codon weight $w_c$ obtained from tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  All three modeled tAI readouts covered the full $61$ sense-codon set.  The zero-raw-weight fallback count was $0$ in \emph{E. coli} K-12, $0$ in human, and $0$ in yeast for this bounded-coupling run.  The resulting finite matrix is therefore not a missing-contact object at the modeled-weight layer: every sense codon has a modeled readout in every organism in the three-organism panel.

\paragraph{Survival statistic and bounded verdict.}
Let $S_{ij}$ denote the finite modeled coupling score between residual coordinate $q_i\in R$ and modeled tAI readout coordinate $t_j$.  The declared contact threshold is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_{\mathrm{contact}}=0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The computed modeled-tAI surface had $81$ entries above this threshold, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}|=3{,}801{,}961.031607896,
\qquad
\max_i |S_{ii}|=3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The registered break condition for the bounded modeled run is not met: the matrix does contain entries with $|S_{ij}|>0.01$.  The correct interpretation is narrower than a measured translation-survival claim.  This is a modeled tAI coupling contact on three organisms, not ribosome profiling, not matched proteomics, and not a powered multi-organism survival assay.  The finite result supports the statement that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis has nonzero bounded contact with modeled tAI weights, while the stronger claim requiring at least $40$ matched species remains unavailable for this row set.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation context.}
A separate downstream finite panel evaluates the same nine residual coordinates in a modeled $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition, where $T$ is per-protein mean modeled tAI and $P$ is log-scaled PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts.  That downstream panel is larger than the three-organism survival surface: it includes $12$ organisms and reports positive in-interval modeled mediation fractions for each organism.  Examples include $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$ in yeast with $5815$ joined proteins, $0.612922252793667$ in \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655 with $3739$ joined proteins, $0.27206289160119157$ in human with $19053$ joined proteins, and $0.15488799907323988$ in mouse with $19395$ joined proteins.  These values are useful for locating the downstream protein-abundance route, but they do not retroactively turn the three-organism modeled tAI surface into measured ribosome occupancy or causal translation mechanism.  They also do not remove the need for matched mRNA abundance controls when a protein-abundance statement is made.

\paragraph{Controls and non-promotion rule.}
The admissible readback is a controlled finite matrix of the form $q_i\to t_j$ and, only in the separate mediation setting, $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$.  Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline remain covariates or baselines, not promoted coordinates.  A direct residual-to-protein association is not admissible as protein realization unless a same-coordinate translation-readout step is first established.  Matched mRNA abundance is a transcription-layer control used to separate translation efficiency from transcript abundance; it is not itself a translation readout.

\paragraph{Boundary against higher-layer claims.}
This chapter does not infer folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality from the modeled tAI surface.  The Phase 1 codon-geometry contact gives a useful comparison point: the locked genetic-code table readback had $40$ successes out of $40$ independent replication trials with one-sided binomial upper-tail probability $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, and the same $40$ out of $40$ count for the boundary probe, while $20$ negative controls and $20$ critical ablations had $0$ unexpected successes.  Those numbers certify a separate codon-table contact, not a translation-output or protein-output claim here.  In the present chapter, translation beyond modeled codon-adaptation weights, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, and universal biological law each require a separate layer-matched contact and its own finite statistic.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The chapter is ready as a bounded modeled-readout record.  The finite facts are: $9$ $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, a $61$-sense-codon modeled tAI readout, $3$ organisms, complete standard amino-acid-family coverage in the local tRNA payload, $81$ modeled entries above the $0.01$ threshold, and maximum absolute modeled score $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$.  The supported conclusion is modeled translation-adaptation coupling under an underpowered organism count.  The unsupported conclusions are measured ribosome survival, protein-realization mediation from this matrix alone, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, selection, and universality.
