\subsection{Modeled tAI survival matrix for $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:h3-codon-usage-topology-translation-survival-b-star-q6-survival-matrix-modeled-tai}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
This BioReality record fixes a finite mediation surface whose first coordinate is the nine-dimensional $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual basis, whose middle coordinate is a translation-layer readout, and whose downstream coordinate is a matched protein-abundance readout only when such a downstream row is actually present.  The residual coordinates used in the modeled-tAI run are
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense codons.  The modeled translation readout is a per-codon tAI weight $w_c$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  The finite organism panel contains three organisms, so the matrix is a bounded modeled-contact matrix rather than a powered multi-species translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Observed finite counts.}
The local GtRNAdb payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families for each organism in the panel.  For \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, the FASTA header count is $89$, with $87$ matched headers and $2$ unmatched headers.  For \emph{Homo sapiens}, the header count is $432$, with all $432$ matched.  For \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, the header count is $275$, with all $275$ matched.  In each of the three organisms the modeled tAI computation covers all $61$ sense codons, and the zero-raw-weight fallback count is $0$.  Thus the finite matrix is not blocked by missing standard tRNA families or by missing codon weights inside this modeled readout.

\paragraph{Modeled coupling verdict.}
With coupling threshold $0.01$, the modeled matrix has $81$ entries above threshold.  Its largest absolute entry is $3801961.031607896$, and the largest absolute diagonal entry is also $3801961.031607896$.  In compact notation, if $S_{ij}^{\mathrm{tAI}}$ denotes the bounded modeled-tAI score for residual coordinate $q_i$ against modeled readout coordinate $t_j$, the observed finite condition is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\{(i,j): |S_{ij}^{\mathrm{tAI}}|>0.01\}| &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}^{\mathrm{tAI}}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}^{\mathrm{tAI}}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The registered break condition is not met for the modeled-contact question: the finite modeled-tAI matrix does have entries above the bounded-contact threshold.  The stronger powered-survival question remains unavailable here because $3$ organisms are not enough for the declared $40$-species powered survival requirement.

\paragraph{Controls and admissible readback.}
The only admissible interpretation is a controlled readback from $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates into modeled codon adaptation weights.  Amino-acid composition, organism identity, GC3, CDS length, and the $M$-density baseline remain controls rather than new promoted coordinates.  A residual-to-protein association is not admissible unless the same residual first survives a translation-readout step.  The modeled-tAI statistic also must remain separate from transcription-only effects: matched mRNA abundance belongs in the control set, not in the translation coordinate itself.

\paragraph{Comparison with a downstream protein-abundance mediation surface.}
A separate finite mediation run joined modeled per-protein tAI and measured protein abundance for $12$ organisms.  In that downstream surface, the same nine residual coordinates were used, and every listed organism had rank sufficiency after the control set: the residualized control rank was $23$, the residualized $Q$ rank was $9$, and the joint $Q,T$ rank was $10$ in each organism.  The per-organism joined protein counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The resulting mediation fractions ranged from $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast.  The yeast row had total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$.  These numbers support a statistical decomposition through modeled tAI, not a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Yeast structure-side check.}
A yeast five-way join also supplies a useful boundary check for overreading the protein-abundance route.  That join contains $4759$ rows and uses $5'$ coding-region minimum free energy as a CDS-only structure proxy.  In that surface, $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction is $0.0005507778820193332$, and the permutation $p$-value for the structure-incremental right tail is $0.1258741258741259$.  This keeps the modeled translation and protein-abundance surfaces from becoming a folded-structure claim.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The finite verdict is therefore positive only for bounded modeled-tAI coupling: $B^\ast_{Q6}$ residual coordinates make contact with a modeled translation-weight matrix, and the observed modeled matrix is nonzero above the declared threshold.  It is not evidence by itself for measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, mature protein realization, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, mechanism, selection, or a universal biological law.  Any one of those stronger claims requires its own layer-matched contact and its own controlled statistic.  A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ fit, even when numerically positive, remains blocked from protein-realization language unless the corresponding $Q_i\to T_j$ step is tested and survives under the stated controls.
