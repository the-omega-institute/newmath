\subsection{Modeled tAI survival for the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual panel}\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record considered here is a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$. The $q_i$ coordinate is one of the nine B$^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual directions
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{q_i\} &= \{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\quad \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation readout $t_j$ is, in this row set, a modeled tAI codon weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values. The codon universe has $61$ sense codons. The three organism rows used for the modeled contact are \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}. The local GtRNAdb payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism: the E. coli payload has $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers; the human payload has $432$ headers and $432$ matches; the yeast payload has $275$ headers and $275$ matches. No raw codon weight required geometric fallback in these three modeled tAI computations: the zero-fallback count is $0$ for all three organisms.

\paragraph{Bounded translation readout.}
The finite modeled-tAI matrix has nonzero bounded coupling at the declared numerical cutoff. With threshold $0.01$, the matrix contains $81$ entries above threshold, and its maximum absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The maximum absolute diagonal entry has the same value, $3801961.031607896$. This supports a bounded modeled-readout statement: the residual coordinates are not merely symbolic labels, since their finite codon-weight pairing with the three organism tAI surfaces produces above-threshold entries. The break condition for this narrow test is therefore not met: the matrix is not entirely below the bounded-contact threshold. However, the same finite row set is explicitly underpowered for a population survival claim, because $n=3$ organisms is below the declared $40$-species requirement. Thus the status is best stated as modeled tAI bounded coupling, not as measured ribosome occupancy, not as ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, and not as proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Controlled downstream mediation.}
A larger matched protein-abundance computation supplies the relevant downstream boundary for the same nine residual coordinates. Across $12$ organisms, the matched CDS--protein-abundance rows are: $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} subsp. subtilis str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12 MG1655, $12418$ for \emph{Gallus gallus}, $19053$ for \emph{Homo sapiens}, $19395$ for \emph{Mus musculus}, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, $13322$ for \emph{Rattus norvegicus}, $5815$ for \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}. Each organism has rank sufficiency for the residualized design: $9$ B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinates, $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for the control matrix, rank $9$ for the residual coordinate block, rank $1$ for the modeled-tAI readout, and rank $10$ for the joint coordinate-plus-tAI block. The response is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb protein abundance joined to real CDS codon counts, while the translation covariate is per-protein mean modeled tAI.

\paragraph{Mediation scalars.}
For the downstream protein-abundance rows, the finite mediation scalar is reported as a statistical decomposition of $Q\to P$ after conditioning on modeled $T$, not as a causal pathway. The mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ are all in the unit interval:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP} &= 0.6084594553\ (\mathrm{B.\ subtilis}),\ 0.4872268357\ (\mathrm{C.\ elegans}),\ 0.3157053954\ (\mathrm{D.\ rerio}),\\
&\quad 0.6165167749\ (\mathrm{D.\ melanogaster}),\ 0.6129222528\ (\mathrm{E.\ coli}),\ 0.1976903965\ (\mathrm{G.\ gallus}),\\
&\quad 0.2720628916\ (\mathrm{H.\ sapiens}),\ 0.1548879991\ (\mathrm{M.\ musculus}),\ 0.0839760292\ (\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}),\\
&\quad 0.1748968180\ (\mathrm{R.\ norvegicus}),\ 0.9491551041\ (\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}),\ 0.0185066856\ (\mathrm{S.\ solfataricus}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The total residual-to-protein $R^2$ values range from $0.0276399860$ in rat to $0.2983762499$ in yeast. Yeast is the largest mediated fraction in this panel, with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and indirect difference $0.28320534058057895$. Human has total $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$, direct $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.03242643251185274$, and indirect difference $0.012119218668341744$. These numbers justify reporting a finite controlled mediation matrix for modeled tAI and protein abundance; they do not justify replacing the modeled translation coordinate by observed ribosome dynamics.

\paragraph{Yeast structure and abundance audit.}
A yeast five-way join gives a separate check on whether the residual-to-protein relation is merely a structure proxy. The join contains $4759$ rows, with a reported CDS join hit rate $0.9156038419146592$ and an overall join hit rate $0.8818572656921754$. The residualization controls are an intercept, $\log(\mathrm{CDS\ length})$, the $20$ amino-acid composition proportions, GC3, and an M-density baseline. For the yeast abundance residual, the single and joint values are
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\quad R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\quad R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction is $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured TE plus mRNA stability is $0.0005507778820193332$. The residual coordinate block explains a small but nonzero fraction of the 5-prime CDS MFE structure proxy, $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$, with right-tail permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$. The structure-incremental right-tail permutation value is $p=0.1258741258741259$, so this audit should be read as a variance partition around a CDS-only MFE proxy, not as a folding mechanism or a mature structural claim.

\paragraph{Axis identity.}
The structural-order axis computation on the same nine-coordinate panel identifies exactly one fold-level sign-flip survivor. The dominant increment and dominant loading axis are both $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$. Its weighted absolute unit loading is $0.5109586606724208$, its weighted held-out $\Delta R^2$ is $0.0031744502294756994$, and its one-sided sign-flip value is $p=0.03125$. The other coordinates do not pass this gate: for example, $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$ has $p=0.142578125$ with weighted $\Delta R^2=0.0020139270616115154$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$ has $p=0.0693359375$ with weighted $\Delta R^2=0.0013067592718559024$, and $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$ has a negative weighted $\Delta R^2=-0.00045340330387383447$ with $p=0.529296875$. This is useful for locating which coordinate carries the finite structural-order signal, but it remains downstream of the controlled readout boundary.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
The chapter-level conclusion is deliberately narrow. The modeled tAI contact gives a finite and above-threshold codon-adaptation readout for the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual panel, and the larger protein-abundance panel gives a controlled statistical mediation surface in which the same nine coordinates can be conditioned through modeled per-protein tAI. The result does not say that a residual coordinate is translated by the ribosome, folded into a structure, physically admissible, functional, mechanistic, phenotypic, selected, or universal. A direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not promoted unless the $Q_i\to T_j$ translation step is present in the same controlled row system. The Phase 1 table-contact audit illustrates the same discipline at another layer: $40$ of $40$ independent replication trials and $40$ of $40$ boundary-probe trials succeed, each with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$, while both the negative-control and critical-ablation panels have $0$ unexpected successes out of $20$. Those table contacts support their own codon-geometry boundary only; they do not supply translation, protein, structure, function, mechanism, or universality for the present B$^*_{Q6}$ modeled-tAI surface.
