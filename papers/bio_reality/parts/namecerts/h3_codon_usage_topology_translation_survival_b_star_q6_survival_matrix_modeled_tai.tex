\subsection{Modeled tAI survival surface for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:biorality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record uses a finite mediation surface whose first coordinate is one of nine synonymous residual directions in the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ packet and whose readout coordinate is an organism-scoped modeled tAI weight.  The residual directions computed for the surface are $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f_3\mathrm{\_stress}$.  The codon space has $61$ sense codons.  The readout is not an observed ribosome-footprint measurement: it is a modeled codon weight $w_c$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy records together with the dos Reis wobble weights.  The three organism rows are \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}; hence the organism count is $3$.

\paragraph{tRNA payload and codon-weight coverage.}
The local GtRNAdb payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each of the three organism rows.  In \emph{E. coli} K12, $89$ FASTA headers were present, $87$ matched the standard family parser, and $2$ were unmatched.  In human, all $432$ FASTA headers matched.  In yeast, all $275$ FASTA headers matched.  For the modeled tAI computation itself, each organism supplied $61$ sense-codon weights and no zero raw $W$ entry required fallback imputation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
N_{\mathrm{sense}} &= 61,\\
N_{\mathrm{fallback}}(\mathrm{E.\ coli}) &= 0,\\
N_{\mathrm{fallback}}(\mathrm{human}) &= 0,\\
N_{\mathrm{fallback}}(\mathrm{yeast}) &= 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
This makes the row finite and nonempty, but it does not make the modeled weights direct observations of translation speed or ribosome occupancy.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
The bounded-contact threshold used for the modeled matrix was $0.01$.  The computed matrix had $81$ entries with absolute score above this threshold.  The largest absolute matrix entry and the largest absolute diagonal score were both $3801961.031607896$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tau &= 0.01,\\
\#\{(i,j): |S_{ij}| > \tau\} &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_i| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
On this restricted modeled-tAI surface, the break condition was not met: the finite matrix was not empty above the bounded-contact threshold.  The correct reading is therefore a positive modeled-weight coupling record for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates, not a powered translation-survival theorem.  The same finite ledger explicitly records that $3$ organisms are insufficient for a powered survival call, and that powered survival would require at least $40$ matched species if the claim were to be promoted to that population scale.

\paragraph{Relation to downstream protein abundance.}
A separate protein-abundance mediation computation uses a larger finite row set and should not be confused with this modeled-tAI contact.  In that downstream computation, $12$ organisms were joined to real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb abundance rows.  The joined protein counts include $4042$ rows for \emph{Bacillus subtilis} str. 168, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for zebrafish, $13295$ for fruit fly, $3739$ for \emph{E. coli} K12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  The mediation fractions there range from $0.01850668564589326$ in \emph{S. solfataricus} to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast, with all reported fractions lying in the unit interval.  Those numbers support a statistical decomposition of $Q \to T \to P$ for joined abundance rows, but they still do not convert the present $3$-organism modeled-tAI matrix into observed ribosome profiling or a causal mechanism.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite checks.}
The broader finite ledger contains two useful calibration facts.  First, the locked genetic-code table contact had $40$ successes in $40$ independent trials and $40$ successes in $40$ boundary-probe trials, with exact one-sided binomial upper-tail value $9.094947017729282\times 10^{-13}$ in both cases, while $20$ negative controls and $20$ critical ablations had zero unexpected successes.  Second, a structural-order axis computation over the same nine residual coordinates identified only $f_3\mathrm{\_stress}$ as passing its fold-level sign-flip gate, with $p=0.03125$ and weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$.  These rows help locate the modeled-tAI result inside the larger finite audit: the residual system is not numerically empty, but every biological promotion still has to pass through its own row-matched contact and control set.

\paragraph{Local no-promotion rule.}
The chapter claim is intentionally bounded.  A nonzero modeled-tAI coupling may be used as a codon-adaptation readout for the nine residual coordinates under the stated finite rows.  It is not, by itself, evidence of observed ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency measured by RPF over mRNA, mature protein abundance, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, organismal phenotype, selection, causation, mechanism, or universality.  Any such stronger statement requires a separate layer-matched contact: observed ribosome profiling or translation-efficiency rows for translation, matched proteomics rows for protein abundance, structure or physical-assay rows for folding and admissibility, and functional or perturbational rows for mechanism or phenotype.  In particular, a direct $Q_i \to P_k$ association cannot be read as protein realization unless the corresponding $Q_i \to T_j$ step first survives under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, $M$-density baseline, and matched mRNA controls.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
The finite statement is ready at the modeled-readout level: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a computed, nonempty modeled-tAI coupling matrix over $3$ organisms, with $81$ entries above the $0.01$ bounded-contact threshold and complete $61$-sense-codon modeled weight coverage in every organism row.  The same statement is not ready as powered translation survival, ribosome-profile validation, protein-realization evidence, or a biological law.  Its proper status is a bounded modeled-tAI survival-surface record with an explicit underpowered boundary.
