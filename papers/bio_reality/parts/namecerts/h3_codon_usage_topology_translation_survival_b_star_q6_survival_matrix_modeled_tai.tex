\subsection{Modeled tAI survival boundary for $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}
\label{sec:bioreality-bstar-q6-modeled-tai-survival-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite matrix whose rows are the nine synonymous residual coordinates
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, f3\_\mathrm{stress}\}$
and whose translation readout is a modeled codon-level tAI weight $w_c$ computed from local GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts and the dos Reis wobble-weight scheme.  The codon count space has $61$ sense codons.  The observed organisms in this finite run are $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$, $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, and $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, so the organism count is $n=3$.  The tRNA payload is complete at the amino-acid-family level for all three organisms: $20$ standard amino-acid families are represented in each case, with FASTA header counts $89$, $432$, and $275$, respectively.  The matched header counts are $87$, $432$, and $275$, leaving two unmatched headers only in the $E.\ coli$ payload.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
For each of the three organisms, a modeled tAI vector was computed over all $61$ sense codons, and the zero-raw-weight fallback count was $0$ in all three organism rows.  The matrix is therefore not a missing-weight artifact.  With coupling threshold $0.01$, the bounded modeled-tAI coupling surface contains $81$ entries above threshold, and the maximum absolute entry is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\tau_{\mathrm{coupling}} &= 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for this packet is explicit: the result would require more data if the finite modeled-tAI matrix had no $|S_{ij}|$ above the bounded-contact threshold, or if a powered survival claim were requested without at least $40$ matched species.  In the present run the first break condition is not met, because nonzero bounded modeled coupling exists; the second condition blocks any powered cross-species survival interpretation because only three organisms are present.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The admissible reading is narrow.  At least one $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinate has a nonzero bounded association with the modeled tAI surface after the packet constructs the declared residual coordinates.  This licenses a modeled translation-readout boundary statement, not an observed ribosome-occupancy statement.  The result is also not a direct protein-output statement.  The finite contact is a codon-adaptation calculation tied to tRNA gene-copy counts and wobble weights, whereas ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, matched mRNA abundance, and proteomics are separate measurement surfaces.

\paragraph{Downstream mediation context.}
A later finite protein-abundance mediation run supplies a useful guardrail for the same nine-coordinate family without changing the interpretation of the modeled-tAI survival packet.  In that run, $12$ organisms were joined to measured PAXdb protein abundance and modeled per-protein tAI.  The joined protein counts included $4042$ rows for $B.\ subtilis$, $11661$ for $C.\ elegans$, $14410$ for $D.\ rerio$, $13295$ for $D.\ melanogaster$, $3739$ for $E.\ coli\ K12\ MG1655$, $12418$ for $G.\ gallus$, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for $M.\ smegmatis$, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for $S.\ solfataricus$.  The mediated fractions $M^{QTP}$ were positive and in $[0,1]$ for every organism, ranging from $0.01850668564589326$ in $S.\ solfataricus$ to $0.9491551041216973$ in yeast.  Representative total-to-direct decompositions include yeast, where $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$, and $E.\ coli$, where $R^2_{QP}=0.10767526677985896$ and $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.04167869969498872$.  These numbers show why the translation step must remain explicit: a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ association is not read as protein realization unless the intervening $Q_i\to T_j$ readout is present and controlled.

\paragraph{Controls and non-promotion rule.}
The controlled surface keeps amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the $M$-density baseline as covariates rather than promoted coordinates.  In the yeast structure-mediation audit, the control block had $24$ columns, rank $23$, and $4736$ residual degrees of freedom after control residualization; the same nine coordinates produced $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, $R^2_{QP\mid S}=0.2706601505000995$, $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$, and $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, and the structure-incremental permutation $p$-value was $0.1258741258741259$.  This reinforces the local rule: modeled translation association, mRNA structure association, and protein abundance association are different finite readouts and must not be collapsed into a single biological mechanism.

\paragraph{Cross-checks against overreading.}
Other finite audits keep the same boundary in view.  The structural-order axis audit identified $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$ as the only fold-level sign-flip-supported coordinate, with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$.  The cross-ortholog selection-imprint audit was negative for the strong selection-imprint gate: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and maximum null95 $\Delta R^2=0.05427157066522958$ over $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows.  The mass-balance audit likewise did not support a stronger dynamic closure, with $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, and $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$ over $120$ genes and $718$ intervals.  The turnover audit was also negative beyond the stated controls, with $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, matched-bin $p=0.8855721393034826$, and recoded-null $p=1.0$ over $3290$ genes.

\paragraph{Chapter verdict.}
The chapter therefore records a bounded modeled-tAI survival boundary.  The finite rows support the existence of nonzero modeled codon-adaptation coupling for the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates in a three-organism surface, with $81$ entries above the stated threshold and complete modeled tAI readouts over $61$ sense codons in every organism.  They do not support a powered multi-species survival law, observed translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality.  Any such stronger claim requires a separate layer-matched contact and a new finite statistic on joined rows.
