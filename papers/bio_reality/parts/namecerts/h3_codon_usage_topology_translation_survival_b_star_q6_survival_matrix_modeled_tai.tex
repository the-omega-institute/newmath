\paragraph{Finite surface.}
This record concerns a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The first coordinate $q_i$ ranges over the computed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates; the second coordinate $t_j$ ranges over translation readouts; the third coordinate $p_k$ ranges over matched protein-abundance readouts when such rows are actually present.  In the finite run considered here, the realized translation contact is modeled tAI, computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy payloads with dos Reis wobble weights.  The protein-abundance coordinate remains a required downstream contact rather than an observed part of the present matrix.  The computed residual list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $|Q|=9$ residual coordinates are tested against modeled translation weights inside a $61$ sense-codon count space.  The row universe is not a direct $Q_i\to P_k$ protein-abundance table.  A residual-to-protein association is admissible only after an intervening $Q_i\to T_j$ translation row has survived under the declared controls.

\paragraph{Translation contact actually attached.}
The attached readout is modeled tAI $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$, not ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from RPF/mRNA, or proteomics.  The organism count is $3$, with rows for \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  For all three organisms the modeled-tAI computation covered all $61$ sense codons and used no zero-raw-weight fallback:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{sense\ codons}=61,\qquad \mathrm{zero\ raw\ }W\mathrm{\ fallbacks}=0,\\
&\mathrm{organisms}=3.
\end{aligned}
$$
The local tRNA-family payload also covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each organism.  The FASTA-header counts are $89$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast.  The matched-header counts are respectively $87$, $432$, and $275$, with unmatched-header counts $2$, $0$, and $0$.  These figures support bounded organism-scoped codon-adaptation readback, but they do not by themselves observe ribosome motion, co-translational dynamics, mature protein amount, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, or a population law.

\paragraph{Controlled matrix statement.}
Let $Z$ denote the retained control block: amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and matched mRNA abundance whenever a translation-efficiency or protein-abundance row is used.  The intended positive mediation claim has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_i\longrightarrow t_j\longrightarrow p_k,
\end{aligned}
$$
where the first arrow must survive before the second arrow can be interpreted.  In the current finite run, only a modeled-tAI bounded-coupling matrix is present.  The nonzero summary is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\#\{|S_{ij}|>0.01\}&=81,\\
\max |S_{ij}|&=3{,}801{,}961.031607896,\\
\max |S_i|_{\mathrm{diagonal}}&=3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The threshold for this bounded contact is $0.01$.  The recorded break condition is not met for this modeled-tAI contact, because the finite matrix has entries above threshold.  Nevertheless the row type is explicitly underpowered for a powered survival claim: $3$ organisms are below the stated requirement of at least $40$ matched species when powered survival is requested.

\paragraph{Readback discipline.}
The positive finite values therefore have a narrow interpretation.  They say that the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have nontrivial bounded coupling to modeled tAI weights in the three-organism table.  They do not say that the same coordinates survive in ribosome profiling, translation efficiency, matched proteomics, or protein abundance.  A transcription-only edge is also separated from a mediated edge: mRNA abundance enters as a control for isolating translation-layer signal, not as a translation readout and not as a protein-output measurement.  Amino-acid sequence, organism, GC3, CDS length, and M-density remain covariates in $Z$ rather than promoted coordinates in the residual surface.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
No translation-realization conclusion follows from this chapter unless a separate organism-matched ribosome-occupancy or translation-efficiency contact supplies the corresponding rows.  No protein-realization conclusion follows unless matched proteomics rows are joined after a surviving $Q_i\to T_j$ step.  No folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological law follows from the modeled-tAI coupling numbers.  In particular, CUN/UUR directionality and any single-organism sign are not population laws.  The present status is a bounded modeled-translation readout: it supports finite matrix contact with tAI weights, while leaving ribo-seq validation, matched mRNA residualization, proteomics mediation, and higher biological layers open.
