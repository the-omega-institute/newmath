\subsection{Modeled tAI survival for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual surface}\label{subsec:bior reality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}  The record considered here is a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The first coordinate $q_i$ ranges over the nine computed $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The translation coordinate $t_j$ is the modeled codon-adaptation readout obtained from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream coordinate $p_k$, when used, is matched protein abundance.  The admissible readback is therefore not a direct $q_i\to p_k$ assertion.  It is a controlled matrix in which a residual coordinate must first pass through a translation readout $q_i\to t_j$ before any downstream protein-abundance comparison is interpreted.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}  The finite codon count space has $61$ sense codons.  The modeled tAI readout was computed for three organisms: \textit{Escherichia coli} K-12, \textit{Homo sapiens}, and \textit{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  For each of these organisms the modeled table contains all $61$ sense-codon weights, with zero raw-weight fallback count equal to $0$.  The local GtRNAdb payload covers all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each case.  The corresponding FASTA header counts are $89$ for \textit{E. coli} K-12, $432$ for human, and $275$ for yeast; the matched header counts are $87$, $432$, and $275$, respectively, with unmatched counts $2$, $0$, and $0$.  These numbers support a bounded organism-scoped tAI contact, but they are still modeled adaptation weights rather than observed ribosome occupancy or measured translation efficiency.

\paragraph{Survival matrix result.}  The computed modeled-tAI coupling threshold is $0.01$.  At that threshold the finite matrix has $81$ entries above threshold, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max |S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,\\
\max |S_i|_{\mathrm{diagonal}} &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
The break condition for this bounded test is not met: the matrix is not empty at the declared contact threshold.  However, the organism count is only $3$, and the recorded rule says that powered survival is not available without at least $40$ matched species.  The correct classification is therefore modeled-tAI bounded coupling under insufficient organism power, not measured ribosome survival and not protein realization.  The positive entries show that the residual coordinates can be paired with a finite modeled translation-readout surface; they do not by themselves establish a biological law, a causal mechanism, or an observed translation process.

\paragraph{Matched protein-abundance mediation audit.}  A separate same-theme finite audit joins real CDS codon counts, modeled per-protein tAI, and PAXdb protein abundance.  It uses the same nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates.  The joined row counts are $3739$ proteins for \textit{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $4129$ for \textit{Mycobacterium smegmatis} str. MC2 155, and $5815$ for yeast.  The per-protein tAI readout is the codon-count-weighted mean
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $n_c$ is the CDS codon count and $w_c$ is the modeled tAI weight.  The tRNA all-copy totals in this audit are $90$, $49$, and $275$, with zero raw weights filled by geometric mean in $1$, $1$, and $0$ cases, respectively.

\paragraph{Controlled ranks and residual energies.}  After residualizing against the declared controls, each organism has $24$ control columns with residual control rank $23$.  The residual degrees of freedom are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$.  The residualized translation rank is $1$ in each organism; the residualized $Q$ rank is $9$; and the joint residualized $(Q,T)$ rank is $10$.  The residual translation energies are $0.4728468725814029$, $0.041191576348982066$, and $6.90537888244409$.  The residual protein-abundance energies are $4020.333671802061$, $5729.194538214237$, and $2958.1865890925337$.  These rank checks show that the finite regression surfaces are not rank-collapsed at the fitted level, even though the modeled-tAI survival claim remains organism-underpowered as a population statement.

\paragraph{Mediation scalars.}  The statistical mediation fractions $M^{QTP}$ are finite and lie in the unit interval for all three organisms:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{E.coli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{M.smegmatis}} &= 0.08397602919861291,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{S.cerevisiae}} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
The corresponding total $Q\to P$ $R^2$ values are $0.10767526677985896$, $0.03209009170649934$, and $0.29837624994140827$.  After conditioning on modeled tAI, the direct $Q\to P$ $R^2$ values are $0.04167869969498872$, $0.029395293228368185$, and $0.0151709093608293$.  Thus the indirect differences $c-c'$ are $0.06599656708487024$, $0.0026947984781311544$, and $0.28320534058057895$.  These are statistical decompositions on finite matched rows, not causal effects.  They are useful because they keep the direct residual-to-protein route separate from the translation-conditioned route.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}  This chapter permits only the following conclusion: the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface has nonzero bounded coupling to modeled tAI weights, and a matched protein-abundance audit can decompose $Q\to P$ association by conditioning on modeled per-protein tAI in three organisms.  No measured translation survival, ribosome occupancy, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, organismal phenotype, selection claim, or universal biological rule follows from these rows.  Any such stronger assertion requires a separate layer-matched contact: measured ribosome profiling or translation efficiency for translation survival, independent protein measurements for abundance, and distinct structural, physical, functional, perturbational, or phenotype assays for higher biological claims.
