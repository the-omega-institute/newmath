\subsection{Modeled tAI Contact for the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ Translation-Survival Boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival-boundary}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The finite object tested here is a controlled matrix whose rows are synonymous residual coordinates and whose readout columns are modeled translation-adaptation weights.  The coordinate side contains the nine residual axes
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_{3}\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}$.
The codon surface has $61$ sense-codon coordinates.  The translation readout is not measured ribosome occupancy; it is the modeled codon weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts together with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  The three organism rows are \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}, so the organism count is $n=3$.

\paragraph{Local tRNA coverage.}
The local GtRNAdb payload contains all $20$ standard amino-acid families in each of the three organism rows.  In the bacterial row there are $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, and $2$ unmatched headers.  In the human row there are $432$ headers and all $432$ match the standard parsing route.  In the yeast row there are $275$ headers and all $275$ match.  The modeled tAI computation covers all $61$ sense codons in each organism.  The zero-raw-weight fallback count is $0$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12, $0$ for human, and $0$ for yeast, so the finite modeled-weight table is complete on the sense-codon support used by the residual matrix.

\paragraph{Modeled coupling statistic.}
Let $q_i$ denote one of the nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and let $T_j$ denote the modeled tAI readout induced by the organism-specific tRNA-copy and wobble-weight table.  The bounded contact statistic is read only as a finite coupling score between the residual coordinate and the modeled translation-adaptation surface:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}^{\mathrm{model}}&=S(q_i,T_j\mid Z),\\
Z&=\{\text{amino-acid composition},\text{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\text{CDS length},\text{$M$-density baseline}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
At threshold $0.01$, the modeled matrix has $81$ entries above threshold.  Its maximum absolute entry is $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$, and the maximum absolute diagonal entry is also $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$.  Thus the finite table is not empty: there is a nonzero bounded modeled-tAI coupling surface for the nine residual coordinates.  This is a modeled contact result, not a powered survival claim.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The break condition for this record has two parts.  First, the record would need additional data if the finite modeled-tAI matrix had no $|S_{ij}|$ above the bounded-contact threshold.  That part is not met, because $81$ entries exceed $0.01$.  Second, a powered survival claim is not allowed without at least $40$ matched species.  That part remains binding: the present run has only $3$ organisms.  The correct layer is therefore translation realization under an underpowered modeled readout, not measured translation survival and not protein realization.  In symbols, the admissible statement is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\exists (i,j)\; |S_{ij}^{\mathrm{model}}|>0.01,\\
&\text{but } n_{\mathrm{organism}}=3<40,\\
&\text{so } S_{ij}^{\mathrm{model}}\text{ is a bounded modeled contact rather than a powered biological-survival entry.}
\end{aligned}
$$

\paragraph{Downstream comparison without promotion.}
Other finite rows show why this chapter must keep the modeled-tAI result in a narrow scope.  A later $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition over $12$ organisms used matched CDS and PAXdb protein-abundance rows; for example, the joined protein counts were $3{,}739$ for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, $5{,}815$ for yeast, $19{,}053$ for human, and $19{,}395$ for mouse.  In that larger finite panel the yeast modeled mediation fraction was $0.9491551041216973$, with total $R^2_{QP}=0.29837624994140827$ and direct conditioned $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.0151709093608293$; human had mediation fraction $0.27206289160119157$, total $R^2_{QP}=0.04454565118019448$, and direct conditioned $R^2_{QP\mid T}=0.03242643251185274$.  Those numbers belong to a separate statistical mediation panel.  They do not retroactively turn this three-organism modeled-tAI surface into measured ribosome occupancy, nor do they prove a molecular mechanism.

\paragraph{Adjacent finite checks.}
The same residual family has finite contacts at nearby biological layers, but the outcomes are mixed and layer-specific.  In a yeast abundance-residual decomposition with $4{,}759$ joined rows, the direct residual-to-protein statistic was $R^2_{QP}=0.2715520808117803$, while conditioning on measured TE and mRNA stability gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M}=0.13773967696311287$ and conditioning additionally on the structure proxy gave $R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S}=0.13759011208318542$.  The structure-only fraction was only $0.003284564452662019$, with $S_Q$ structure $R^2=0.003774248455422586$, permutation $p=0.027972027972027972$ for the structure association and $p=0.1258741258741259$ for the structure-incremental fraction.  In a structural-order audit over five folds, the dominant coordinate was $f_{3}$-stress with weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$ and sign-flip $p=0.03125$; the other eight axes did not pass that fold-level sign-flip gate.  In an evolutionary-imprint panel with $80$ orthogroups and $240$ rows across $3$ species, the strong selection-imprint gate was negative: $\Delta R^2=0.03250515015613468$, leave-clade-out $\Delta R^2=-0.0021477729853302424$, and the null $95\%$ bound was $0.05427157066522958$.  These finite rows support a cautious reading: $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates can have bounded contacts in some readouts, but no single contact licenses a global biological law.

\paragraph{Perturbation and execution context.}
Further finite experiments reinforce the same boundary.  An observable-execution decomposition in yeast used $3{,}289$ genes and concluded that at least one rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.  A mass-balance run used $120$ genes and $718$ intervals, with $R^2_{L0}=0.9960628201518571$, $R^2_{L1}=0.996410628851051$, $R^2_{L2}=0.9712443377743347$, $R^2_{L3}=0.8953999568421264$, and $\Delta R^2=-0.07584438093220836$.  A turnover audit over $3{,}290$ genes gave $R^2_{L2}=0.055740851626933985$, $R^2_{L3}=0.050278872193227464$, $\Delta R^2=-0.005461979433706483$, matched-bin $p=0.8855721393034826$, and recoded-null $p=1.0$.  In contrast, an \emph{E. coli} mRFP synonymous-variant protein-output panel with $1{,}459$ variants had $\Delta R^2=0.05339906007191191$, $\Delta \mathrm{DL}=19.089327473282296$ bits, and matched and within-null $p=0.004975124378109453$.  These rows are useful for calibration, but the present chapter does not merge them into the modeled-tAI statistic.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is finite and local.  The nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface has a nonempty modeled-tAI coupling matrix on three organisms, all with complete $61$-sense-codon modeled-weight coverage and all standard amino-acid families represented.  The finite matrix has $81$ entries above $0.01$ and therefore passes the bounded modeled-contact nonemptiness check.  It does not pass the powered-survival requirement because $3$ organisms are far below the declared $40$-species threshold.  No translation mechanism, measured ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, phenotype, selection law, causal pathway, or universality follows from this record unless a separate layer-matched contact supplies the relevant finite rows and the corresponding controlled statistic.
