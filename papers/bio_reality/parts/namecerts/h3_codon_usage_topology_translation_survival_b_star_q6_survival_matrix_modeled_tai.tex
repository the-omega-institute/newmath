\subsection{Modeled tAI survival surface for $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates}\label{sec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-survival}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Finite row object.}
This record treats the $B^{*}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual packet as a finite mediation surface rather than as a direct biological law. The coordinate side consists of nine residual directions computed on the synonymous codon-count background:
$\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $f3\_\mathrm{stress}$. The translation readout used here is not ribosome profiling and not matched proteomics. It is a modeled codon-adaptation readout: $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ is computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with the dos Reis wobble $s$-values. The finite codon space has $61$ sense codons, and the current organism panel has $n=3$ organisms.

\paragraph{Contact rows and readout construction.}
The local tRNA payload covers all standard amino-acid families for the three modeled organisms. For $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$ the input contains $89$ FASTA headers, $87$ matched headers, $2$ unmatched headers, and all $20$ standard amino-acid families. For $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$ the input contains $432$ headers, all $432$ matched, no unmatched headers, and all $20$ families. For $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$ the input contains $275$ headers, all $275$ matched, no unmatched headers, and all $20$ families. The modeled tAI readout gives $61$ sense-codon weights for each organism, with zero raw-$W$ fallback count equal to $0$ in all three modeled-tAI organism rows. Thus the row is finite and computable as a bounded translation-layer annotation, but it remains a model-derived codon-weight contact rather than a measurement of ribosome movement.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling result.}
The finite modeled-tAI matrix is nonzero under the declared contact threshold. With coupling threshold $0.01$, the matrix has $81$ entries above threshold. Its largest absolute entry is
$$\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j} |S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}$$
and the largest absolute diagonal entry is the same value,
$$\begin{aligned}
\max_i |S_i| &= 3801961.031607896.
\end{aligned}$$
The break condition for this particular bounded readout is therefore not met: the modeled matrix does not collapse to all entries below threshold. The correct local interpretation is narrower than a powered survival claim. The entry establishes that the nine-coordinate $B^{*}_{Q6}$ residual surface has a nontrivial finite coupling against modeled tAI weights in this three-organism panel. It does not establish measured translation efficiency, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, mechanism, phenotype, selection, or a universal biological rule.

\paragraph{Rank and power boundary.}
The row explicitly classifies the layer as $\mathrm{translation\_realization\_modeled\_underpowered}$. The reason is finite and numerical: $n=3$ organisms are insufficient for powered survival, and the row itself declares that powered survival requires at least $40$ matched species if the same survival language is to be promoted beyond bounded modeled coupling. Consequently, the matrix may be used as a codon-adaptation readback, but not as a population-level translation-realization result. A positive modeled entry is also not a substitute for an organism-matched ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency panel with amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, and M-density baseline controls.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance mediation audit.}
A separate downstream audit gives the current status of the $Q\to T\to P$ route when modeled per-protein tAI is paired with measured protein abundance. In that audit the same nine $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinates are used, and the protein readout is $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from PAXdb joined to real CDS codon counts. The joined row counts are $3739$ proteins for $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12\ MG1655}$, $4129$ for $\mathrm{Mycobacterium\ smegmatis\ str\ mc2\ 155}$, and $5815$ for $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$. The residualized control design has $24$ control columns, rank $23$ for $Z$, rank $9$ for the $Q$ coordinates, rank $1$ for the modeled tAI coordinate, and rank $10$ for the combined $Q,T$ design in each organism row. The residual degrees of freedom after $Z$ are $3716$, $4106$, and $5792$, respectively.

\paragraph{Mediation scalars.}
The modeled per-protein tAI audit reports a statistical mediation fraction $M^{QTP}$ in the unit interval for each organism. The fractions are
$$\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{E.\ coli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{M.\ smegmatis}} &= 0.08397602919861291,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{S.\ cerevisiae}} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}$$
The corresponding indirect $R^2$ differences $c-c'$ are $0.06599656708487024$, $0.0026947984781311544$, and $0.28320534058057895$. The total $Q\to P$ $R^2$ values are $0.10767526677985896$, $0.03209009170649934$, and $0.29837624994140827$, while the direct $Q\to P$ values after conditioning on modeled $T$ are $0.04167869969498872$, $0.029395293228368185$, and $0.0151709093608293$. These numbers show that a finite statistical decomposition can be computed, and that modeled tAI can absorb part of the $Q\to P$ association in the joined rows. They do not convert the modeled-tAI contact into ribosome profiling, causal mediation, perturbation evidence, synonymous-edit rescue, folding evidence, or functional realization.

\paragraph{Local admissible statement.}
The admissible conclusion is therefore the following. The $B^{*}_{Q6}$ coordinate packet has a finite, nonzero modeled-tAI coupling surface over $61$ sense codons and three organism-level tRNA-copy contacts, with $81$ matrix entries above $0.01$ and maximum absolute entry $3801961.031607896$. In a separate protein-abundance audit, the same nine-coordinate residual surface supports computable $Q\to T\to P$ statistical decompositions in three organism-specific CDS/protein tables, with all reported $M^{QTP}$ fractions lying in $[0,1]$. The row remains bounded at modeled translation-readout and statistical mediation scope. No claim of measured translation, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, phenotype, selection, or universality follows unless an additional layer-matched contact supplies that readout and the corresponding controlled statistic is computed.
