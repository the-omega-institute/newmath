\subsection{Modeled tAI survival and translation-conditioned protein abundance for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{subsec:bioreality-q6-modeled-tai-protein-mediation}

\paragraph{Finite surface.}
The record uses a finite mediation surface whose rows are triples $(q_i,t_j,p_k)$.  The residual coordinate $q_i$ ranges over nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates: $\mathrm{Arg\_AGR}$, $\mathrm{Ile\_AUA}$, $\mathrm{K\_AAA}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR}$, $\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG}$, $\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY}$, $\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}$, and $\mathrm{f3\_stress}$.  The translation coordinate $t_j$ is the modeled codon adaptation readout computed from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts together with dos Reis wobble weights.  The downstream coordinate $p_k$ is matched protein abundance, read as $\log_{10}(\mathrm{abundance\_ppm})$ from measured PAXdb abundance after joining to real CDS codon counts.  Amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, CDS length, M-density baseline, and transcript-level controls remain covariates; they are not promoted into residual coordinates.

\paragraph{Modeled translation contact.}
The modeled-tAI contact is finite and organism-scoped.  Three organisms were used: \emph{Escherichia coli} K-12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  The local GtRNAdb payload contained all 20 standard amino-acid families for each organism.  The FASTA-header counts were 89 for \emph{E. coli} K-12, 432 for human, and 275 for yeast; matched standard tRNA-family headers were 87, 432, and 275 respectively, leaving two unmatched headers only in the \emph{E. coli} K-12 payload.  For the modeled codon weights, all three organisms supplied 61 sense-codon readouts, and the zero-raw-$W$ fallback count was zero in the organism-level modeled-tAI matrix.  Thus the readout is not an inferred structural or functional measurement; it is a bounded translation-weight table attached to codon counts.

\paragraph{Bounded coupling statistic.}
With coupling threshold $0.01$, the finite modeled-tAI matrix contains 81 entries above threshold.  The largest absolute entry is $3{,}801{,}961.031607896$, and the largest absolute diagonal entry has the same value.  A compact way to state the observed bounded-contact result is
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\{(i,j): |S_{ij}|>0.01\}| &= 81,\\
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896,\\
\max_i |S_{ii}| &= 3{,}801{,}961.031607896.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes nonzero modeled coupling at the codon-adaptation readout level.  It does not by itself establish measured ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency from ribosome profiling, protein abundance, folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, biological mechanism, selection, or a universal organism-level law.  The organism count is three, so the stronger powered multi-organism survival route remains unavailable under the declared requirement of at least 40 matched species.

\paragraph{Joined protein-abundance rows.}
A second finite table joins per-protein codon residuals, modeled per-protein tAI, and measured protein abundance for three organisms.  The joined row counts are 3739 proteins for \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, 4129 proteins for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis} strain mc2 155, and 5815 proteins for \emph{S. cerevisiae}.  The corresponding tRNA all-copy totals are 90, 49, and 275.  The per-protein tAI computation filled one zero raw-$W$ value by a geometric-mean fallback in \emph{E. coli} and one in \emph{M. smegmatis}; yeast required no such fill.  After residualization on the control block, each organism retained sufficient rank for the joint nine-coordinate residual design: $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde X)=9$, $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde T)=1$, and $\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde{XT})=10$.  The residual degrees of freedom after controls were 3716, 4106, and 5792 respectively, with 24 control columns and control-block rank 23 in each organism.

\paragraph{Mediation decomposition.}
The protein-abundance table evaluates a statistical $Q\to T\to P$ decomposition, not a causal intervention.  The total residual-to-protein association, the direct association after conditioning on modeled tAI, and the resulting indirect component are finite scalar readouts.  For \emph{E. coli} K-12 MG1655, the total $R^2_{QP}$ is $0.10767526677985896$, the conditioned direct value is $0.04167869969498872$, and the difference is $0.06599656708487024$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.612922252793667$.  For \emph{M. smegmatis}, the total value is $0.03209009170649934$, the direct value is $0.029395293228368185$, and the difference is $0.0026947984781311544$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.08397602919861291$.  For yeast, the total value is $0.29837624994140827$, the direct value is $0.0151709093608293$, and the difference is $0.28320534058057895$, giving $M^{QTP}=0.9491551041216973$.  All three mediated fractions lie in the unit interval.  In displayed form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{E\ coli}} &= 0.612922252793667,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{M\ smegmatis}} &= 0.08397602919861291,\\
M^{QTP}_{\mathrm{S\ cerevisiae}} &= 0.9491551041216973.
\end{aligned}
$$
These values support a bounded modeled-tAI mediation reading for matched protein abundance in the joined tables.  They do not show that synonymous residuals cause translation changes, that ribosome dynamics were observed, or that protein abundance is isolated from turnover, localization, post-translational modification, folding, pathway context, or transcript abundance.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The admissible conclusion is narrow.  A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not counted as protein realization unless the same residual coordinate first passes through a translation readout $q_i\to t_j$ under the stated controls.  Modeled tAI is acceptable here as a codon-adaptation contact, and the protein table is acceptable as a downstream abundance contact, but neither contact supplies folding, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality.  Those claims require separate finite contacts at the corresponding biological layer.  Therefore this chapter records positive bounded coupling and positive statistical mediation fractions for the finite rows above, while keeping the result below measured ribosome-profiling validation and below any higher-layer biological promotion.
