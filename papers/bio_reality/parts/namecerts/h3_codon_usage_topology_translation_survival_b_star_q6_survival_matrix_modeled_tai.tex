\subsection{Modeled tAI survival boundary for $B^*_{Q6}$ residuals}
\label{sec:bior-q6-modeled-tai-survival-boundary}

\paragraph{Finite row surface.}
The object studied here is a finite mediation surface with rows indexed by synonymous residual coordinates $q_i$, translation readouts $t_j$, and matched downstream protein-abundance readouts $p_k$.  The coordinate side is the nine-entry $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family
$\{\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},f_3\mathrm{\_stress}\}$,
computed in the $61$-sense-codon count space.  The first translation readout attached to this packet is a modeled tAI codon weight $w_{\mathrm{codon}}$ derived from GtRNAdb tRNA gene-copy counts with dos Reis wobble $s$-values.  Three organisms were present in this finite modeled-tAI contact: \emph{Escherichia coli} K12, \emph{Homo sapiens}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae}.  Their tRNA FASTA header counts were respectively $89$, $432$, and $275$; the matched standard-family header counts were $87$, $432$, and $275$; and each organism covered all $20$ standard amino-acid families.  The two unmatched K12 headers are treated as parsing residue, not as biological signal.

\paragraph{Bounded modeled-tAI coupling.}
The finite tAI computation produced all $61$ sense-codon weights for each of the three organisms, with zero raw-weight fallback count $0$ in K12, human, and yeast.  At coupling threshold $0.01$, the bounded modeled-tAI matrix had $81$ entries above threshold.  The largest absolute entry was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|S_{ij}| &= 3801961.031607896,
\end{aligned}
$$
and the largest absolute diagonal entry was the same number.  This is a positive finite contact with the modeled adaptation-weight surface, but it is not a powered translation-survival result: the organism count is $3$, whereas the powered-survival request was explicitly blocked without at least $40$ matched species.  Thus the local conclusion is a bounded modeled-readout statement, not a measured ribosome-occupancy or proteomics validation.

\paragraph{Mediation convention.}
The admissible downstream reading is a controlled chain $q_i\to t_j\to p_k$.  A direct $q_i\to p_k$ association is not promoted unless the corresponding residual first survives a translation-readout step.  In the powered protein-abundance decomposition, the translation readout $T$ was the per-protein mean modeled tAI,
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= \frac{\sum_c n_c w_c}{\sum_c n_c},
\end{aligned}
$$
joined to log$_{10}$ PAXdb abundance and real CDS codon counts.  Across the twelve-organism panel, the joined protein counts were $4042$ for \emph{Bacillus subtilis}, $11661$ for \emph{Caenorhabditis elegans}, $14410$ for \emph{Danio rerio}, $13295$ for \emph{Drosophila melanogaster}, $3739$ for K12 MG1655, $12418$ for chicken, $19053$ for human, $19395$ for mouse, $4129$ for \emph{Mycobacterium smegmatis}, $13322$ for rat, $5815$ for yeast, and $1656$ for \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}.  Every organism had rank-sufficient residual design after controls, with nine $q$ coordinates and residual degrees of freedom ranging from $1633$ in \emph{Sulfolobus} to $19372$ in mouse.

The statistical mediation fractions $M^{QTP}$ were inside the unit interval for every organism.  The largest mediated fraction occurred in yeast,

\begin{aligned}

R^2_{QP} &= 0.29837624994140827,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.0151709093608293,\\
R^2_{QP}-R^2_{QP\mid T} &= 0.28320534058057895.

$$
Other large mediated fractions were $0.6165167748739756$ in \emph{Drosophila}, $0.612922252793667$ in K12 MG1655, and $0.6084594553474505$ in \emph{Bacillus subtilis}.  Smaller but positive fractions were observed in \emph{Sulfolobus} ($0.01850668564589326$), \emph{Mycobacterium} ($0.08397602919861291$), rat ($0.17489681798543982$), mouse ($0.15488799907323988$), chicken ($0.1976903964724546$), human ($0.27206289160119157$), zebrafish ($0.3157053953614641$), and nematode ($0.4872268357227724$).  These values describe a variance decomposition after amino-acid composition, GC3, CDS length, organism-scoped baselines, and the $M$-density coordinate support are accounted for; they do not by themselves establish a causal translation mechanism.

\paragraph{Yeast structure and rate-side checks.}
The yeast five-way join supplied $4759$ rows linking CDS codon counts, protein abundance, measured TE, mRNA half-life, and a $5'$ coding-region MFE proxy.  The reported CDS join hit rate was $0.9156038419146592$, and the full join hit rate was $0.8818572656921754$.  In this joined table the direct residual-to-protein association after the control set had
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2_{QP} &= 0.2715520808117803,\\
R^2_{QP\mid S} &= 0.2706601505000995,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M} &= 0.13773967696311287,\\
R^2_{QP\mid T,M,S} &= 0.13759011208318542.
\end{aligned}
$$
The structure-only fraction was $0.003284564452662019$, the structure-incremental fraction beyond measured TE and mRNA stability was $0.0005507778820193332$, and $S_Q$ structure $R^2$ was $0.003774248455422586$ with right-tail permutation value $0.027972027972027972$.  The incremental structure permutation value was $0.1258741258741259$, so the structure proxy is recorded as a small residual association rather than as a folding or physical-admissibility claim.  A separate observable-execution check on yeast used $3289$ genes and concluded that at least one observed rate-proxy slot passed both effective null classes.

\paragraph{Dominant coordinate.}
In the powered structural-order audit, exactly one coordinate passed the fold-level sign-flip gate.  The dominant loading and dominant increment axis was $f_3\mathrm{\_stress}$, with weighted absolute unit loading $0.5109586606724208$, weighted held-out $\Delta R^2=0.0031744502294756994$, and sign-flip right-tail value $0.03125$.  The remaining eight coordinate increments did not pass that gate: their sign-flip values were $0.142578125$ for Arg\_AGR, $0.0693359375$ for Ile\_AUA, $0.2294921875$ for K\_AAA, $0.779296875$ for Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR, $0.24609375$ for Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG, $0.2158203125$ for Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY, $0.4873046875$ for Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG, and $0.529296875$ for Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY.  This makes the coordinate-level readback narrow: the finite rows single out $f_3$ stress as the structural-order carrier in this audit, not the full nine-coordinate list as independently significant axes.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
The chapter therefore records a controlled, finite, modeled translation-readout contact and a downstream statistical mediation table.  It does not assert measured ribosome occupancy from modeled tAI, does not infer protein folding from abundance, does not infer physical admissibility from codon residuals, and does not infer molecular function, phenotype, mechanism, selection, or universality from any single residual coordinate or organism sign.  A stronger translation-realization claim would require same-organism or same-CDS measured ribosome-profiling or translation-efficiency rows, matched mRNA rows, and the declared controlled survival statistic.  A stronger protein-realization claim would additionally require the translation step to survive before reading any $q_i\to p_k$ association as mediated propagation.  Structure, localization, stability, interaction, function, perturbation, and phenotype each require their own layer-matched measurement before entering the claim surface.
