\paragraph{Finite readout.}
The claim concerns the fixed Window6 boundary set $R$ inside the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-coordinate packet.  The test does not infer the set $R$ from protein abundance.  Instead, $R$ is first fixed by the internal Window6 coordinate construction and is then asked a bounded biological question: across organism-matched abundance tables, are the codons in this boundary set preferentially used in high-abundance proteins, avoided in high-abundance proteins, or directionally neutral?  The finite row contains $10$ sense codons in $R$ out of $61$ sense codons, with $15$ requested and $15$ computed organisms, each passing the minimum protein-count gate of $500$ proteins.  The computed organisms are Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655, Mycobacterium smegmatis str. MC2 155, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Homo sapiens, Danio rerio, Gallus gallus, Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, Bacillus subtilis subsp. subtilis str. 168, Mus musculus, Caenorhabditis elegans, Drosophila melanogaster, Halobacterium salinarum, Sulfolobus solfataricus, Dictyostelium discoideum, and Rattus norvegicus.

\paragraph{Observed direction.}
All $15$ computed organisms place the Window6 boundary set in the avoided direction and none place it in the preferred or zero direction.  The directional sign test is therefore not a weak majority but a unanimity result over the computed finite panel:
$$
\begin{aligned}
 n_{\mathrm{avoided}} &= 15,\\
 n_{\mathrm{preferred}} &= 0,\\
 n_{\mathrm{zero}} &= 0,\\
 n_{\mathrm{directional}} &= 15,\\
 \widehat p_{\mathrm{majority}} &= 1.0.
\end{aligned}
$$
The one-sided binomial probability for avoided direction is $3.0517578125\times 10^{-5}$, and the two-sided binomial probability is $6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$ at the reported two-sided level $\alpha=0.05$.  The separately reported individual-permutation diagnostic used $1000$ permutations and found $6$ individually significant organisms: Homo sapiens, Danio rerio, Gallus gallus, Pseudomonas aeruginosa PAO1, Mus musculus, and Rattus norvegicus.  Thus the row supports the local call that Window6 boundary $R$ has a cross-organism avoided abundance-preference signature, while the significant-organism subpanel also remains unanimous: $6$ avoided, $0$ preferred, and $0$ zero.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite conclusion is therefore narrow but real: the Window6 boundary set $R$ is consistently avoided in the abundance-preference direction across all $15$ computed organisms, with a two-sided sign-test probability of $6.103515625\times 10^{-5}$ and an individually significant avoided subpanel of $6$ organisms.  The result is a reproducible cross-organism association between a fixed codon-coordinate boundary and abundance-direction readback.  It is not evidence that the boundary codons cause abundance, encode a translation program, determine folding, define physical admissibility, realize biological function, explain phenotype, or establish a universal law.  Those stronger claims would require separate layer-matched contacts and separate statistics.  In this chapter the allowed statement is only that the fixed Window6 boundary set has a finite, externally tested avoided abundance-preference signature, while the surrounding translation, tRNA, turnover, localization, and feature rows remain descriptive projections with organism-specific coverage and bounded explanatory power.
