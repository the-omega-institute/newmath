\subsection{ADAR editing as a finite dsRNA-context boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-adar-editing-dsrna-context-isoform-boundary-kleinova2023}

\paragraph{Finite row.}
This record treats the ADAR editing packet as a bounded cross-layer relation, not as a mechanistic reconstruction of RNA editing.  The finite audit row is keyed by $\texttt{h3.cross\_layer\_relation.rna\_editing.adar\_editing\_dsrna\_context\_isoform\_boundary\_kleinova2023}$ and carries experiment run $599\mathrm{de}6\mathrm{cd}4\mathrm{a}6\mathrm{c}$.  The replayed contact has $3137$ editing-site rows, uses $1200$ realized permutations, and completed in $15.7578$ seconds.  The chapter therefore records a finite row-level contact with an ADAR/dsRNA/isoform boundary, while withholding any statement that the row itself reconstructs the cellular editing apparatus.

\paragraph{Observable ledger.}
The retained numerical content is deliberately small.  Let $S$ denote the set of editing-site rows admitted by the Kleinova-style ADAR contact, and let $\Pi$ denote the realized permutation ledger.  The row certifies only
$$
\begin{aligned}
|S| &= 3137,\\
|\Pi| &= 1200,\\
\tau_{\mathrm{run}} &= 15.7578\ \mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
$$
These three numbers are enough to make the contact replayable as a finite boundary: the test has a concrete row count, a concrete resampling budget, and a concrete runtime trace.  They are not enough to infer an editing mechanism, an isoform-regulatory program, a structural dsRNA grammar, a protein-level effect, or a universal biological law.

\paragraph{Relation to the direction-aware ranking ledger.}
The ADAR row is attached to a broader cross-layer ranking regime whose canonical quantities are absorbed energy $A_H$, coordinate-support cosine $C_H$, and a penalized score
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{Score}(H) &= A_H C_H - \lambda\,\mathrm{DL}(H),\\
\lambda &= 0.01,\\
\mathrm{DL}(H) &= \log_2(1+n_{\mathrm{columns}}).
\end{aligned}
$$
In that ledger, the largest raw direction-aware terms remain small.  For Danio rerio, measured TE has $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, $A_HC_H=0.011566051846174291$, and $\mathrm{Score}=0.001566051846174291$; mRNA has $A_HC_H=0.01073458857797759$ and $\mathrm{Score}=0.000734588577977589$.  For Homo sapiens, complex membership has $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, $A_HC_H=0.006566030115340095$, and $\mathrm{Score}=-0.0034339698846599055$, while mRNA has $A_HC_H=0.006224777217900546$ and $\mathrm{Score}=-0.0037752227820994546$.  A high-energy but weakly aligned human readout illustrates the reason for the cosine filter: PTM density has $A_H=0.014884825920744861$ but only $C_H=0.3604440741749213$, giving $A_HC_H=0.0053651472982577525$ and $\mathrm{Score}=-0.004634852701742248$.

\paragraph{Negative and organism-specific readback.}
The ranking table does not supply a shared universal absorber.  The best organism-level readouts are heterogeneous: Danio rerio selects measured TE under the penalized score, Homo sapiens selects complex membership among one-column readouts, Gallus gallus selects complex membership with $A_HC_H=0.004241514781443171$, and Saccharomyces cerevisiae selects mRNA with $A_HC_H=0.0011462622274400686$ and $\mathrm{Score}=-0.008853737772559931$.  Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 has only weak mRNA and measured-TE entries, with mRNA at $A_HC_H=0.0015297976209788734$ and $\mathrm{Score}=-0.008470202379021127$.  These values frame the ADAR row as one more finite cross-layer contact, not as evidence that a single readout channel explains the non-translation residual across organisms.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The accepted statement is only this: the ADAR editing contact supplies a finite dsRNA-context and isoform-boundary readout with $3137$ sites under a $1200$-permutation audit.  It can be carried as a cross-layer relation row in the BioReality ledger and compared with other finite contacts by row count, permutation budget, runtime, and the declared boundary type.  The contact does not by itself prove translation realization, folded structure, physical admissibility, molecular function, phenotype, editing causality, pathway mechanism, or universality.  Any such stronger claim would require a separate layer-matched measurement with its own statistic and its own null model.
