\subsection{B-star-Q6 Translation Survival Matrix: Proxy Coupling and Blocked Survival}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Object of the record.}
This record concerns the proposed survival matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ for nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates against translation-relevant readouts. The intended readout family is $r_j\in\{$tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, protein abundance$\}$, and the declared control family is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\text{amino-acid composition},\text{ organism context},\mathrm{GC3},\text{ length},M\text{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The residual coordinate list used in the finite computation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY}, f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ coding codon coordinates. The biological question is not whether these nine directions exist as formal residual coordinates, but whether any one of them keeps predictive mass for a direct translation-layer readout after the controls $Z$ and the simpler codon baseline have already been charged to the null model.

\paragraph{Finite proxy computation.}
The completed finite-row computation used three organism rows,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal O=\{\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}\},
\end{aligned}
$$
and paired organism-level codon usage with a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. The available readout in this run is therefore a proxy spectrum, not a direct ribosome-profiling, stAI, tAI, or proteomic measurement. For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the computation evaluates a synonymous-projection coupling against the proxy readout, with threshold $0.01$ for the null screen. In the reported row, the null screen was not met: all nine residual coordinates produced nonzero entries above the absolute coupling threshold, and the largest observed absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Equivalently, the finite proxy matrix has $9$ nonzero above-threshold coordinate contacts under the declared proxy comparison, so the condition ``all nine $q_i$ have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$'' is false for this proxy run. This is a real finite obstruction to the most naive absorption hypothesis, but it is still only a coupling result. It is not a measured survival score $S_{ij}$, because the direct translation readouts and the full $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length calculation are not yet present in the row.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR gate row.}
A separate finite row isolates the Leu CUN/UUR association across the same three organisms. The observed log-ratio signs are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,-1,+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the sign is not uniform across organisms. The reported Pearson statistic is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. This is a sharp finite-row alignment, but the sample size is three and no population-level inference is licensed by that number. In particular, the negative middle sign is part of the record: the row says that the association can be context-dependent, not that it has a universal direction.

\paragraph{What the row supports.}
The supported statement is a bounded one. The nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not all annihilated by the available codon-usage/tRNA-copy proxy screen: with threshold $0.01$, the maximum absolute entry is $1882464.1106377328$ and there are $9$ above-threshold nonzero entries. The Leu CUN/UUR subrow also records a three-organism partial-correlation value of $1.0$, again with the explicit warning that $n=3$ is a finite table rather than a stable cross-organism estimate. The proper mathematical reading is therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists q_i\in\mathcal Q\quad |A_{ij}|>0.01
\end{aligned}
$$
for the proxy readout that was actually present, not
$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists q_i,r_j\quad S_{ij}>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a direct translation readout. The latter formula remains an open survival assertion until organism-matched tAI or stAI weights, ribosome profiling, protein abundance, and matched mRNA controls are attached and the declared residual statistic is computed after all controls in $Z$.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
This chapter does not infer ribosome-mediated translation realization from codon counts or tRNA gene-copy counts alone. It does not read tRNA gene-copy ratio as tRNA abundance, charging state, elongation rate, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output. It also does not infer protein folding, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or a universal biological law from the finite proxy matrix. A positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is only a reason to keep the survival test alive; it is not itself a positive survival entry $S_{ij}$. Any later claim about translation, folding, physical admissibility, function, mechanism, or universality must be backed by its own matched measurement contact and its own controlled statistic.

\paragraph{Status.}
The current status is a ready finite proxy-coupling chapter with a blocked translation-survival conclusion. The proxy data reject the all-small-coupling break condition for the three-organism codon-usage/tRNA-copy table, while the survival-matrix claim remains unverified because no direct organism-matched translation readout has yet been processed through the full control set $Z$ and the simpler-codon-baseline ablation.
