\subsection{B-Star-Q6 Residual Coupling at the Translation-Readout Boundary}\label{sec:b-star-q6-residual-coupling-translation-boundary}\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Record.} This BioReality record isolates the finite-row status of the claim named \texttt{h3.codon\_usage\_topology.translation\_survival.b\_star\_q6\_coupling\_matrix}.  The biological object under review is the proposed survival matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ for nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates against translation-relevant readouts.  The declared controls are amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline.  The available computation does not yet instantiate that full survival matrix.  It instantiates a proxy coupling test against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum in three organisms, while preserving the amino-acid quotient through the synonymous projection.

\paragraph{Finite row.} The computed residual-coordinate list is
$$\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ codon coordinates after stops are excluded.  The organism row is
$$\begin{aligned}
O=\{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}.
\end{aligned}$$
For the proxy contact, the break condition was that all nine residual coordinates satisfy $|A_{ij}|<0.01$.  That break condition was not met: the threshold was $0.01$, the number of nonzero proxy-coupling entries was $9$, and the reported maximum absolute coupling was $1882464.1106377328$.  In the notation of this record, the proxy stage therefore supports only the finite statement
$$\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_{i,\mathrm{proxy}}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{i: A_{i,\mathrm{proxy}}\neq 0\} &= 9,\\
\text{all-zero proxy break} &= \mathrm{false}.
\end{aligned}$$
This is evidence that the nine-coordinate residual basis is not numerically silent against the chosen tRNA-copy spectrum.  It is not, by itself, an estimate of $S_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Leucine gate contact.} A related three-organism CUN/UUR Leu contact reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ with $n=3$ after the stated finite-row control.  The log-ratio row is
$$\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}$$
with sign pattern $(1,-1,1)$.  Hence the sign is not uniform across organisms.  The positive finite-row partial correlation and the sign reversal must be read together: the row shows a small-sample organism-indexed association, not a stable direction law.

\paragraph{Survival-matrix gap.} The target survival claim requires, for each residual coordinate $q_i$ and each translation readout $r_j$, a residualized readout weight $\ell_j^{\perp}$, a covariance coupling $A_{ij}=q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp}$, and then a controlled survival score such as partial $R^2$ or description-length improvement after the full control set $Z$.  The available row contains the proxy coupling against tRNA-Leu copy spectrum and the three-organism CUN/UUR association.  It does not contain the organism-matched ribosome-profiling, tAI, stAI, protein-abundance, and matched mRNA rows needed to populate the declared matrix $S_{ij}$.  In particular, no positive entry $S_{ij}>0$ is presently certified by this row.

\paragraph{Local boundary.} The contact supports a bounded codon-usage and tRNA-copy comparison.  It does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization, translation efficiency, protein abundance, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological law.  tRNA gene-copy number is kept as a genome-annotation contact and is not identified with tRNA abundance, charging state, ribosome occupancy, or protein output.  A positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is also not promoted to a positive survival score $S_{ij}$ without the declared $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length computation.

\paragraph{Verdict.} The finite-row computation is usable as a BioReality boundary record: the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nonzero proxy coupling profile in a $61$-codon space over three organisms, and the Leu CUN/UUR row has a reported partial Pearson value of $1.0$ with nonuniform signs.  The survival conjecture remains open at the translation-readout layer because the full matrix $S_{ij}$ has not been computed under amino-acid composition, organism, GC3, length, and $M$-density controls.
