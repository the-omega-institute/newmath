\subsection{A Proxy Coupling Matrix for the $B^*_{Q6}$ Translation-Survival Question}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-proxy-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Record.}
This BioReality record concerns the nine synonymous-residual coordinates associated with the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-usage packet and asks whether any of them survives as a positive contributor to a translation-related readout after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence covariates have been imposed. The computed finite row does not yet contain the declared survival matrix $S_{ij}$. Instead, it contains a proxy coupling calculation against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum. The row is therefore informative about whether the residual coordinates are numerically silent against the available proxy, but it is not by itself a translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Finite carriers.}
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates. Inside this space, the calculation instantiated the following nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg}_{\mathrm{AGR}},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{\mathrm{AUA}},\ \mathrm{K}_{\mathrm{AAA}},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{CUN}/\mathrm{UUR}},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{\mathrm{UUA}/\mathrm{UUG}},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCA}/\mathrm{UCG}},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{\mathrm{UCR}/\mathrm{AGY}},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{\mathrm{ACR}/\mathrm{ACY}},\ f_3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The organism rows loaded for this computation are Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the implemented statistic compares $q_i$ with the available proxy readout through the residual-count covariance. The reported proxy readout is the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, or a matched mRNA-residualized translation measurement.

\paragraph{Coupling readout.}
Let $A_i$ denote the proxy coupling between residual coordinate $q_i$ and the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum in the finite row. The null break condition for this proxy run was the simultaneous inequality $|A_i| < 0.01$ for all nine residual coordinates. That break condition was not met. The run reports $9$ nonzero proxy-coupling entries above the nonzero-entry criterion, with maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the available finite table rejects the narrow claim that all nine $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates are numerically invisible to the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR proxy at threshold $0.01$. This is a statement about proxy covariance contact only. It does not promote $A_i$ to a controlled survival score $S_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
A linked finite row focuses on the Leu CUN-versus-UUR coordinate and the corresponding tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contact across the same three organisms. In that row the Pearson statistic is reported as $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson statistic is also $1.0$ with $n = 3$. The organism-level log-ratio entries are
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702,
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
+1,\quad -1,\quad +1.
\end{aligned}
$$
Consequently the sign is not uniform across the three organism rows. The perfect three-row correlation is recorded as a finite-table feature, not as a population estimate and not as a universal direction. The sign reversal is important because it prevents the row from being read as a single global biological orientation for CUN/UUR usage.

\paragraph{What the row supports.}
The row supports a bounded negative conclusion about silence: the nine residual coordinates are not all annihilated against the available tRNA-Leu proxy under the stated threshold. Equivalently, the finite proxy matrix contains a detectable residual-coordinate contact before the full translation-readout survival analysis has been attached. This is weaker than the desired survival statement. The intended survival quantity is a controlled score $S_{ij} = S_{q_i}(r_j \mid Z)$ for translation readouts $r_j$, where $Z$ includes amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and an $M$-density baseline. The present row supplies the proxy side of that program but not the $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement table.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization follows from this row alone. In particular, the tRNA gene-copy contact is not treated as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output. The proxy coupling matrix also does not imply protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, pathway participation, or any universal biological law. Those claims would require separate organism-matched contacts and their own controlled statistics. A future positive survival entry would have to be stated as a specific $S_{ij} > 0$ result for a named residual coordinate and a named translation readout after all declared controls and the simpler-codon-baseline ablation have been applied.

\paragraph{Status.}
The present record is therefore a proxy-coupling support row for the $B^*_{Q6}$ survival-matrix program. It is ready to be included as a finite numerical boundary: the proxy matrix is non-silent, the largest observed absolute coupling is $1882464.1106377328$, all nine computed residual coordinates have nonzero proxy entries, and the three-organism Leu gate has partial Pearson $1.0$ with non-uniform signs. The survival conjecture itself remains open until direct tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, protein-abundance, and matched mRNA-control rows are paired with the codon-count table and the declared $Z$-controlled survival statistic is computed.
