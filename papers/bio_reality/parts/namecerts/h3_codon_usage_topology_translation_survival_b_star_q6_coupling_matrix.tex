\subsection{Finite coupling matrix for the $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival packet}
\label{sec:bior-eality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Object under test.}
The packet isolates nine synonymous residual coordinates in the $B^*_{Q6}$ codon-usage layer and asks whether any one of them survives as a predictor of a translation-related readout after the amino-acid quotient and ordinary sequence covariates have been imposed. The computed residual list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \{&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ \mathrm{K}_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ambient codon-count space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates. The finite row therefore tests a $9$-coordinate residual basis against a proxy readout, not against a completed translation-survival matrix. The organisms loaded in the finite computation are $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$.

\paragraph{Statistic actually computed.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the finite computation formed a coupling entry $A_{ij}$ against a proxy spectrum: the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy readout. The declared break condition for this proxy row was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{null} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad |A_{ij}| < 0.01\ \text{for all nine residual coordinates}.
\end{aligned}
$$
This break condition was not met. The coupling threshold was $0.01$, all $9$ entries were nonzero above the reported nonzero criterion, and the maximum absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row records that the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates are not annihilated by the amino-acid-synonymous projection when tested against this tRNA-copy proxy spectrum. This is a codon-usage/topology contact with a tRNA-copy readout, not a ribosome-measured survival entry.

\paragraph{Leu gate row.}
A companion finite row tested the $CUN/UUR$ Leu association across the same three organisms. The recorded log-ratio signs were
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern $(+,-,+)$. The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms. The finite Pearson value was $1.0$, and the reported partial Pearson value after the available control was also $1.0$ with $n=3$. This is a sharp finite-row association, but $n=3$ is too small to support a population-level direction, a universal sign, or an organism-independent law. The negative middle sign is biologically important for the interpretation: the row supports context dependence rather than a fixed global orientation.

\paragraph{Survival target.}
The intended survival statistic is not merely the proxy coupling $A_{ij}$. The survival matrix entry is a controlled score $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ for a direct readout $r_j$, where $r_j$ may be tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance, and where
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (&\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ GC3,\ \text{coding length},\\
&\text{simpler codon-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive translation-survival claim would require at least one controlled entry satisfying $S_{ij}>0$, equivalently a positive partial-$R^2$ or description-length improvement after the listed controls and after ablation by the simpler codon-density baseline. The current finite evidence does not contain such an $S_{ij}$ table. It contains a non-null proxy coupling row and a three-organism Leu association row.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The finite data support the following bounded conclusion. Within a $61$-codon count space, the nine listed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates have a nonzero coupling profile against a tRNA-Leu $CUN/UUR$ log-copy proxy across three organisms, with threshold $0.01$, $9$ nonzero entries, and maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$. The associated Leu gate row has Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$, but its signs are $(+,-,+)$, so the row should be read as a finite context-sensitive contact rather than as a universal directional statement.

\paragraph{Local exclusion boundary.}
No translation-survival conclusion follows from the proxy row alone. In particular, tRNA gene-copy counts are not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output. The finite matrix does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological law. Those conclusions would each require their own organism-matched measurement contact and their own controlled statistic. The present chapter only records that the residual codon-usage coordinates survive a finite proxy-coupling null test and that the stronger $S_{ij}$ translation-survival matrix remains open until direct translation readouts and the full control set $Z$ are attached.
