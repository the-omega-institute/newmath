\subsection{B-star-Q6 residual coordinates against a translation-proxy spectrum}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object and finite row.}
This record concerns the nine residual coordinates of $B^*_{Q6}$ after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed.  The computed coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
R = \{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\ \mathrm{K\_AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\ \mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The ambient codon-count space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates.  The finite organism row is
$$
\begin{aligned}
O = \{\mathrm{homo\_sapiens},\ \mathrm{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{escherichia\_coli\_k12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For this row the available readout is not a direct ribosome, tAI, stAI, or protein-abundance measurement.  It is the proxy spectrum given by the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy contrast.  The computation therefore tests whether the $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates have a nontrivial covariance contact with that proxy spectrum, while keeping the amino-acid quotient explicit through the synonymous projection.

\paragraph{Coupling statistic.}
For each residual coordinate $q_i$ and proxy readout direction $\ell$, the recorded coupling has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_i = q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell^{\perp},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\ell^{\perp}$ denotes the synonymous-projected readout direction and $\Sigma$ is the residual count covariance used by the finite row.  The declared break condition for this proxy contact is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| < 0.01.
\end{aligned}
$$
The observed row does not meet that break condition.  All nine entries are nonzero above the stated threshold, with
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| = 1882464.1106377328, \qquad \#\{i: |A_i| \geq 0.01\}=9.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite row says that the nine-coordinate residual basis is not numerically silent against the Leu tRNA-copy proxy.  It does not yet say that any coordinate survives against a direct translation readout after the full control set is applied.

\paragraph{Leu gate row.}
A second finite row records the cross-organism Leu CUN/UUR contact using the same three organism names.  In that row the Pearson statistic between the Leu codon-usage contrast and the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contrast is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\qquad
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The nonuniform sign pattern is important.  It prevents the row from being read as a universal direction statement.  The row is a finite contact showing that the CUN/UUR Leu contrast can be measured across paired codon-usage and tRNA-copy summaries, not a claim that the sign is stable in all organisms.

\paragraph{Survival matrix status.}
The target matrix for the stronger claim is a controlled survival matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $q_i$ ranges over the nine $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over direct translation-layer readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  The intended control block is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z=(\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{CDS\ length},\ \mathrm{M\ density\ baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite row supplies $A_i$-type proxy coupling evidence only.  It does not supply a computed $S_{ij}$ entry, partial $R^2$, description-length improvement, p-value, or baseline-ablation result for a direct ribosome, tAI, stAI, or protein-abundance readout.  Consequently the stronger survival assertion remains open: a positive proxy coupling may motivate the survival test, but it is not itself a positive controlled survival score.

\paragraph{Local scope limit.}
The data used here are codon-usage summaries, tRNA gene-copy summaries, and a three-organism Leu contrast.  They do not establish ribosome-mediated translation, tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, protein output, folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biochemical function, mechanism, or any population-level law.  Each of those stronger biological statements would require its own matched measurement and its own statistic.  In particular, tRNA gene-copy ratio is kept as a genome-annotation proxy; it is not treated as measured tRNA abundance or charged tRNA availability.  Likewise, the proxy coupling matrix is kept separate from any future $Z$-controlled survival matrix.

\paragraph{Verdict for this BioReality row.}
The finite result is a ready topology-level record: in a $61$-codon count space, the nine listed $B^*_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates all show nonzero coupling above the $0.01$ threshold against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  A companion Leu row has Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$, but with signs $(+1,-1,+1)$ across the three organisms.  The correct readback is therefore bounded: the residual coordinates have a finite proxy contact that survives the stated coupling null, while the direct translation-survival matrix $S_{ij}$ still awaits organism-matched translation readouts and full $Z$-controlled estimation.
