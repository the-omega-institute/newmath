\subsection{A finite B$^*_{Q6}$ coupling row at the translation-survival boundary}
\label{subsec:b-star-q6-coupling-matrix-boundary}

\paragraph{Question.}
The record tests whether the nine synonymous residual coordinates selected by the B$^*_{Q6}$ codon-usage packet leave any detectable trace after the amino-acid quotient is imposed.  The intended survival object is a matrix $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over nine residual codon coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-related readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  The control block $Z$ consists of amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline.  The present row does not yet contain that full survival matrix.  It contains the precursor coupling calculation against a proxy readout, together with an independent three-organism Leu CUN/UUR contact used to mark the scale and limits of the observed association.

\paragraph{Finite carrier.}
The calculation was run on the $61$-codon count space across three organism rows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
O&=\{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The nine residual coordinates were
$$
\begin{aligned}
R=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},
\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},
\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each coordinate $q_i$, the recorded statistic is a residual covariance coupling $A_{ij}=q_i^{\mathsf T}\Sigma\ell_j^\perp$ against the proxy readout
$$
\begin{aligned}
\ell_j^\perp=\mathrm{log\ copy\ spectrum\ for\ tRNA\text{-}Leu\ CUN\ versus\ UUR}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The null threshold for this finite row was $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ for every one of the nine coordinates.  The null condition was not met: all nine entries were nonzero above that threshold, with maximum absolute coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1{,}882{,}464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus this row records a genuine proxy coupling signal in codon-count and tRNA-copy space, not a vanished residual coordinate system.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR contact.}
The associated three-organism Leu gate isolates the CUN/UUR codon-usage contrast against the tRNA-Leu copy-ratio contrast.  On the three available organism rows it reports Pearson correlation $1.0$ and partial Pearson correlation $1.0$ after the declared control, with $n=3$.  The signed log-ratio readback was
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,-1,+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign pattern is therefore not uniform across organisms.  This matters mathematically: the row supports a finite association in the available table, but it does not license a direction of effect that is shared by all organisms, and it does not supply a population-level estimate.

\paragraph{What survives this row.}
The finite evidence is exactly the following bounded statement.  In the three-organism codon-count table, after passing to synonymous residual coordinates, the nine named B$^*_{Q6}$ coordinates are not all orthogonal to the tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR proxy spectrum under the chosen covariance readout.  Equivalently, the break condition
$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall i\in\{1,\ldots,9\}\quad |A_{ij}|<0.01
\end{aligned}
$$
fails for the proxy readout.  The observed failure is strong in magnitude because the largest absolute entry is about $1.88\times 10^6$, and the count of above-threshold nonzero entries is $9$.

\paragraph{What is not established.}
This row is deliberately weaker than the survival conjecture.  A positive or large proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is not the same object as a positive survival score $S_{ij}>0$.  The survival score requires organism-matched translation readouts and an explicit $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement calculation with amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, length, and the $M$-density baseline included.  The present finite row has no true tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, or protein-abundance matrix entry.  It also supplies no recorded $p$-value for the proxy coupling and no row-expanded null distribution beyond the three-organism Leu contact.

\paragraph{Local claim boundary.}
No ribosome-mediated translation realization follows from this row alone.  No folding claim, physical-admissibility claim, biochemical-function claim, mechanism claim, protein-output claim, or universal biological law follows from codon usage and tRNA gene-copy coupling unless an additional matched measurement supplies that layer and the declared controls are applied there.  In particular, tRNA gene copy is treated here only as an annotated copy-number spectrum, not as tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein production.  The mathematical status of the row is therefore a bounded codon-usage-topology contact at the edge of the translation-survival program: it keeps the B$^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates alive as candidates for later survival testing, while leaving the survival matrix itself unclaimed.
