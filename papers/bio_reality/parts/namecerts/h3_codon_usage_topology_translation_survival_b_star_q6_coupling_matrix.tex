\subsection{The $B^*_{Q6}$ translation-survival coupling matrix}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Record.}
This record isolates the translation-facing part of the nine-coordinate $B^*_{Q6}$ residual system.  The carrier is a rectangular matrix whose rows are the residual synonymous coordinates
$K_{AAA}$, $Arg_{AGR}$, $Ile_{AUA}$, $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$, $Leu_{UUA/UUG}$, $Ser_{UCR/AGY}$, $Ser_{UCA/UCG}$, $Thr_{ACR/ACY}$, and $f3$-stress, and whose columns are translation-related readouts when such readouts are present.  The intended survival entry is a controlled score $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$ after removing amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline.  In the finite contact reported here the observed matrix is not yet the full $S_{ij}$ survival matrix.  It is the preceding coupling table $A_{ij}$ against a proxy readout, namely the tRNA-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum, with codon usage and tRNA-copy contacts available for Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12.

\paragraph{Finite-row construction.}
The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates.  The amino-acid quotient is imposed before testing the nine residual coordinates, so the row directions are synonymous residual directions rather than amino-acid-composition directions.  For a residual coordinate $q_i$ and a readout weight $\ell_j$, the tested proxy coupling has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{ij} &= q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp},\\
\ell_j^{\perp} &= \ell_j-\Pi_Z\ell_j,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\Sigma$ is the finite residual count covariance and $\Pi_Z$ denotes projection onto the declared controls.  The actual survival score, when a direct translation readout is attached, is the controlled description-gain or partial-$R^2$ score
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\hbox{amino-acid composition},\hbox{ organism},GC3,\hbox{ length},M\hbox{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The present finite run computed all nine synonymous residuals and found that the proxy-coupling break condition was not met.  The break condition was that every row should have $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout.  Instead, all nine rows were nonzero above the threshold, with maximum absolute entry $1882464.1106377328$ and nonzero-entry count $9$ at threshold $0.01$.

\paragraph{Observed proxy contact.}
The three-organism Leu gate gives a sharper finite subcontact for the $Leu_{CUN/UUR}$ row.  With organisms Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12, the log-ratio signs are $(1,-1,1)$ and the recorded log-ratios are $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$.  The reported Pearson value is $1.0$, and the partial Pearson value after the available control is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  This is retained only as a finite table contact: the sign is explicitly non-uniform, the sample size is three organisms, and the statistic is not promoted to a population law.

\paragraph{Modeled translation readout panel.}
A larger modeled translation panel is available for a bounded $S^{QT}$ computation using per-codon tAI generated from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  That run covers $40$ organisms, uses the same nine residual coordinates, has $9$ modeled readout columns, and reports $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  The residualized control design has $24$ control columns, rank $23$, residual degrees of freedom $17$, and residualized row rank $9$ for the $B^*_{Q6}$ coordinates.  This supports a rank-powered modeled translation-readout surface, not a measured ribosome-occupancy surface.  The positive threshold for partial-$R^2$ entries is $10^{-12}$, and the degenerate-fit guard is $0.999999$.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance and complement checks.}
Several downstream checks constrain how far the translation-facing record may be read.  In Saccharomyces cerevisiae GFP abundance, the same nine coordinates were tested under two conditions.  The YEPD condition joined $2517$ rows before taking the shared set, the SD condition joined $2442$ rows, and the shared protein count is $2209$.  The condition-specific $S^{QP}$ values are $0.3280825603424665$ for YEPD and $0.3050385538305022$ for SD; both have residual degrees of freedom $2186$, control rank $23$, and coordinate rank $9$.  The dominant coordinate is $f3$-stress in both conditions, with condition-comparison cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ over $1000$ permutations.

For the modeled-tAI complement of the abundance component, the six-organism complement fractions are also finite and organism-specific.  The complement-projection values are

d_{Ecoli} &= 0.6629960038578532, & m_{Ecoli} &= 0.3370039961421468,\\

The corresponding $R^2_{QP}$ values are $0.04613529805760884$, $0.10767526667768725$, $0.03980793595345331$, $0.04454565117192484$, $0.032090091625816136$, and $0.29837624987287115$ in the same organism order.  The row counts used for the six complement panels are $14410$, $3739$, $12418$, $19053$, $4129$, and $5815$, each with control rank $23$ and $24$ control columns.

\paragraph{Direction-aware candidate ranking.}
A separate candidate-readout ranking reports the regularized score $A_HC_H-\lambda DL_H$ with $\lambda=0.01$ and $DL_H=\log_2(1+n_{col})$.  For one-column readouts $DL_H=1.0$; for the localization panel with $12$ columns, $DL_H=3.700439718141092$.  The leading candidates are organism-specific: Danio rerio selects measured translation efficiency with $A_H=0.017594290015070787$, $C_H=0.6573753096184687$, and score $0.001566051846174291$ on $13140$ joined rows; Homo sapiens selects complex membership with $A_H=0.007527199987780841$, $C_H=0.8723071163246565$, and score $-0.0034339698846599055$ on $18335$ rows; Saccharomyces cerevisiae selects mRNA with $A_H=0.001897605472160837$, $C_H=0.6040571890503664$, and score $-0.008853737772559931$ on $5128$ rows.  Escherichia coli K12 and Mycobacterium smegmatis do not yield a selected $H_o^*$ in that table.  No p-value is reported in these records; the available null comparison is the Bonferroni-style threshold and the permutation-derived null quantile.  For example, Danio measured translation efficiency exceeds its threshold $0.000719994971498518$ against null $p95=0.0003297458343745959$, while Escherichia coli mRNA does not exceed threshold $0.001860998420625413$ against null $p95=0.0011620497987012218$.

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
The result establishes that the nine-row $B^*_{Q6}$ residual basis is not silent against the available proxy and modeled translation-facing readbacks.  The strict finite statement is narrower: the three-organism proxy coupling matrix has all nine entries nonzero above $0.01$, the maximum absolute proxy entry is $1882464.1106377328$, and the larger modeled-tAI panel gives a nondegenerate $9\times 9$ controlled surface with $R^2_{QT}=0.9003849000036579$.  These data support continued testing of the translation-survival matrix, but the proxy contact itself is not ribosome profiling, not measured elongation speed, not protein abundance, and not a biochemical mechanism.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
Nothing in this chapter says that $B^*_{Q6}$ determines translation in general.  tRNA gene copy is not treated as tRNA abundance or charging.  Modeled tAI is not treated as measured ribosome occupancy.  Protein-abundance regressions are not treated as folding, structural order, molecular function, physical admissibility, pathway evidence, phenotype realization, mechanism, or a universal biological law.  A positive $A_{ij}$ entry is only a coupling statistic; a positive $S_{ij}$ entry requires an organism-matched direct translation readout and the declared $Z$-controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length computation.  Any stronger biological conclusion must be supplied by a distinct measurement layer and its own finite statistic.
