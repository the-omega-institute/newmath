\subsection{Residual Codon Coordinates and Translation-Layer Survival Boundary}
\label{sec:b-star-q6-translation-survival-coupling-audit}

\paragraph{Record.}
This BioReality record concerns the nine residual synonymous codon coordinates collected under the $B^*_{Q6}$ name and asks whether any one of them survives as a predictor of a translation-related readout after quotienting away amino-acid composition and ordinary contextual controls. The tested residual coordinate list is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg}_{AGR},\ \mathrm{Ile}_{AUA},\ K_{AAA},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{CUN/UUR},\ \mathrm{Leu}_{UUA/UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser}_{UCA/UCG},\ \mathrm{Ser}_{UCR/AGY},\ \mathrm{Thr}_{ACR/ACY},\ f_3\text{-stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The intended survival matrix has entries $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$, where $q_i$ ranges over these nine residual coordinates, $r_j$ ranges over translation-facing readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance, and $Z$ contains amino-acid composition, organism context, GC3, coding-sequence length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline. The current finite row does not yet contain that full matrix. It contains a proxy coupling audit against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum and a separate three-organism Leu CUN/UUR association row.

\paragraph{Finite computation.}
The codon count space in the coupling audit has size $61$. The loaded organism set is exactly
$$
\begin{aligned}
\{\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens},\ \mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae},\ \mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For the proxy readout, the break test was declared as follows: the proxy coupling row would be null if all nine residual coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$. That break condition was not met. The threshold was $0.01$, all $9$ residual coordinates had nonzero proxy entries, and the largest absolute coupling reported was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite proxy audit says that the nine-dimensional $B^*_{Q6}$ residual family is not numerically invisible to the tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR copy-number contrast in this three-organism row. This is a codon-usage and tRNA-copy contact only: it is a coupling measurement $A_{ij}$, not a controlled survival estimate $S_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR gate.}
The companion Leu CUN/UUR row uses the same three organisms and reports a Pearson value of $1.0$ for the finite comparison. The partial Pearson value recorded after the stated control is also $1.0$ with $n=3$. The organism-level log-ratio signs are not uniform:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\ -0.24869078488829677,\ 1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign vector
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\ -1,\ +1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The row therefore supports a finite association check for the Leu CUN/UUR codon-usage and tRNA-Leu copy-number interface, while simultaneously recording context dependence of sign. The value $1.0$ at $n=3$ is not treated as a stable population estimate, and the negative middle entry prevents any reading in which the sign is declared universal.

\paragraph{Survival matrix status.}
The survival statement to be tested is stronger than the proxy result. It requires a direct translation-layer readout and the controlled statistic
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid \mathrm{AA},\ \mathrm{organism},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \mathrm{length},\ M\text{-density})
\end{aligned}
$$
for each residual coordinate and readout. In the present record, true tAI or stAI weights, ribosome-profiling translation efficiency, matched protein abundance, and matched mRNA abundance are listed as required contacts, but no organism-matched, control-adjusted $S_{ij}$ table is present. The proxy audit therefore licenses only the narrower statement that $B^*_{Q6}$ residual coordinates show nonzero finite coupling to the tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR copy-number contrast under the recorded three-organism contact. It does not license the claim that any entry has $S_{ij}>0$ under the full control set.

\paragraph{Local interpretation.}
The finite data are best read as a ranked experimental target rather than as a completed biological conclusion. The nine residual coordinates have been computed; the proxy coupling threshold $0.01$ was exceeded; the maximum absolute proxy coupling was $1882464.1106377328$; and the Leu CUN/UUR side row reports Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$ with signs $(+1,-1,+1)$. These numbers make the translation-readout survival test worth carrying forward, because the residual coordinates are not erased by the available codon-usage and tRNA-copy contrast. They do not replace the missing ribosome, tAI, stAI, proteomics, or matched mRNA rows needed to compute the survival matrix itself.

\paragraph{Boundary.}
No translation-realization conclusion follows from this record unless a separate contact supplies the relevant direct translation readout and the declared $Z$-controlled statistic is computed. No ribosome-mediated mechanism follows from tRNA gene-copy counts alone. No tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, protein folding, physical admissibility, biological function, pathway participation, protein-output claim, or universal law follows from the proxy coupling matrix or from the three-organism Leu CUN/UUR row. The present result remains a finite codon-usage and tRNA-copy measurement with a clearly open survival-matrix test.
