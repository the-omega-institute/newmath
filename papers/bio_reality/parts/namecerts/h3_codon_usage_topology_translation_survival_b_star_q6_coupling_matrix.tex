\subsection{A bounded proxy matrix for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ translation-survival question}
\label{subsec:biorality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record isolates the finite data currently attached to the claim that the nine residual coordinates of $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ may survive amino-acid quotienting when tested against translation-layer readouts. The intended survival statistic is a controlled matrix $S_{ij}$, where $q_i$ ranges over the residual codon-usage coordinates and $r_j$ ranges over translation-related observables such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance. The control set is
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z = (&\text{amino-acid composition},\ \text{organism context},\ \mathrm{GC3},\ \text{CDS length},\\
&\text{and the } M\text{-density baseline}).
\end{aligned}
$$
The finite contact now in hand is weaker than that target. It computes a proxy coupling matrix against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, not a ribosome, stAI, tAI, or protein-output survival matrix. Therefore the present chapter treats the computation as a bounded precursor to $S_{ij}$, not as a positive translation-survival result.

\paragraph{Finite rows and coordinates.}
The computation used a codon-count space of $61$ sense codons and loaded three organism rows: Homo sapiens, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, and Escherichia coli K12. The nine synonymous residual coordinates were
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\quad \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\quad \mathrm{K\_AAA},\quad \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\\
&\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\quad \mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\quad \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\\
&\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\quad \mathrm{f3\_stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each residual coordinate $q_i$, the proxy calculation forms a synonymous-projected readout direction $\ell^{\perp}$ and reports a covariance coupling
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_i = q_i^{T}\Sigma\ell^{\perp}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared null test for this proxy row was: declare the row null if every one of the nine coordinates has $|A_i| < 0.01$ against the proxy readout. That null was not met. All nine entries were nonzero under the reported threshold, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_i |A_i| = 1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite proxy contact says that the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis is not numerically orthogonal to this tRNA-Leu copy-number spectrum in the three-row table.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR gate row.}
A second attached finite row reports the organism-level relation between log CUN/UUR codon usage and log tRNA-Leu CUN/UUR copy ratio. In the same three organisms, the logged ratios were
$$
\begin{aligned}
1.3514980212896148,\quad -0.24869078488829677,\quad 1.0086640520051702,
\end{aligned}
$$
with signs
$$
\begin{aligned}
+1,\quad -1,\quad +1.
\end{aligned}
$$
The reported Pearson correlation was $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value was also $1.0$ with $n=3$. This is an exact finite-row statistic, but it is not a population estimate. Its sign is explicitly not uniform across organisms, since the second organism row is negative while the first and third are positive. The row therefore records a context-sensitive finite contact rather than a universal direction of Leu CUN/UUR usage.

\paragraph{What follows.}
The proxy matrix supports only the following bounded statement. In the three-organism codon-count and tRNA-copy table, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates have nonzero proxy covariance couplings against the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum, and the stated all-small-coupling null at threshold $0.01$ fails. This is evidence that the residual coordinate system can make contact with a translation-adjacent genomic annotation once the amino-acid quotient is kept explicit.

It does not yet establish a positive survival score $S_{ij}$. The target survival claim requires a direct translation readout and a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length-improvement calculation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} = S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z) > 0.
\end{aligned}
$$
No finite $S_{ij}$ entries are recorded here, no partial-$R^2$ survival values are attached, and no baseline-ablation table against the full $Z$ control set is present. In particular, a positive $A_i$ or a large $|A_i|$ is not promoted to survival unless the controlled survival statistic is computed on matched translation readouts.

\paragraph{Local limitation.}
The chapter is confined to codon usage, tRNA-copy annotation, and proxy covariance. It does not infer ribosome-mediated translation realization from tRNA gene copy alone. It does not infer tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, protein abundance, protein folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, pathway activity, mechanism, or a universal biological law. Those conclusions would require separate organism-matched contacts and their own statistics. Ribosome profiling, matched mRNA abundance, and proteomics are named as possible future contact classes, but in this finite row they are not yet populated with the controlled survival matrix needed for the stronger translation-survival claim.
