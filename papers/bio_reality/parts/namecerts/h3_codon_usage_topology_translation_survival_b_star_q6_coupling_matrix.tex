\subsection{Residual codon-usage coupling against translation-side contacts}
\label{sec:bior-eality-b-star-q-six-translation-survival-matrix}

\paragraph{Question.}
This record tests whether the nine residual coordinates of the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-usage basis leave any measurable trace after the amino-acid quotient has been imposed.  The intended object is a survival matrix $S_{ij}$, where the row index $q_i$ ranges over nine synonymous residual coordinates and the column index $r_j$ ranges over translation-relevant readouts such as tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, and protein abundance.  The row list used in the finite computation is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q=\{&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\mathrm{K\_AAA},\mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\mathrm{f3\_stress}\}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The declared codon-count ambient space has dimension $61$, corresponding to sense codons after stop-codon exclusion.  The target statistic is not a raw codon-count association.  For each coordinate $q_i$ and each readout $r_j$, the translation readout must first be projected to its synonymous component $\ell_j^{\perp}$, and the residual coupling must be computed on the covariance left after amino-acid identity, organism context, GC3, coding length, and the simpler $M$-density baseline have been accounted for.

\paragraph{Observed finite row.}
The attached run \texttt{4a783972e74b} loaded three organisms: \texttt{homo\_sapiens}, \texttt{saccharomyces\_cerevisiae}, and \texttt{escherichia\_coli\_k12}.  On this row the available readout is only a proxy, namely the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum; direct tAI, stAI, ribosome-profiling, translation-efficiency, and protein-abundance rows are not yet present.  With that limitation made explicit, the coupling test used the threshold $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ as the null break condition for all nine residual coordinates.  The break condition was not met: all nine entries were nonzero against the proxy readout, and the largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}|=1882464.1106377328.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the finite proxy row says that the residual basis is not numerically invisible to the available tRNA-copy spectrum.  It does not yet say that any entry of the survival matrix is positive, because $A_{ij}$ is only a residual covariance coupling and $S_{ij}$ requires a controlled partial-$R^2$ or description-length comparison against the full baseline.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR gate row.}
A second attached finite row, run \texttt{675fdddb3187}, evaluates the Leu CUN/UUR cross-organism gate on the same three organisms.  The organism-level log-ratio signs are $+1,-1,+1$, with numerical log ratios
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702).
\end{aligned}
$$
The sign is therefore not uniform across organisms.  Within this three-row table the Pearson statistic is reported as $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson value is also reported as $1.0$ with $n=3$.  This is a useful finite diagnostic because it shows a strong association in the small table while simultaneously recording context dependence of the sign.  It is not a population estimate, and the row itself marks the directionality as organism-dependent rather than universal.

\paragraph{Survival statistic.}
The intended survival entry has the form
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij} &= S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z),\\
Z &= (\mathrm{amino\ acid\ composition},\mathrm{organism},\mathrm{GC3},\mathrm{length},\mathrm{M\mbox{-}density\ baseline}),\\
A_{ij} &= q_i^{\mathsf T}\Sigma\ell_j^{\perp}.
\end{aligned}
$$
A positive proxy coupling $A_{ij}$ is only an eligibility signal for the survival test.  A positive survival claim requires the fitted translation readout to improve over the control model after the synonymous projection and after the simpler-codon baseline has been ablated.  In the present finite data, the proxy contact shows nonzero residual coupling for all nine rows, while the survival entries $S_{ij}$ remain unfilled because the direct translation readout matrix has not been attached.

\paragraph{Local boundary.}
This chapter does not infer translation realization from codon usage and tRNA gene-copy counts alone.  Gene-copy counts are annotation-level genomic summaries; they are not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein output.  The ribosome-profiling, matched mRNA, and proteomics contacts named for the full test remain required measurement routes, but their names do not by themselves supply the corresponding rows.  No conclusion about folding, structural order, physical admissibility, biological function, biochemical mechanism, or a universal biological law follows from the current table.  Such conclusions would need separate measured contacts and their own controlled statistics.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The current BioReality row is therefore a bounded positive proxy result and a blocked survival result.  The positive part is concrete: in a $61$-codon count space, across three organisms, all nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous residual coordinates have nonzero coupling to the tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy proxy, with maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$, and the Leu CUN/UUR gate reports partial Pearson $1.0$ at $n=3$ while retaining non-uniform signs.  The blocked part is equally concrete: there is not yet an organism-matched $S_{ij}$ matrix against direct tAI, stAI, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein-abundance readouts under the full control set $Z$.  The record is ready as a finite proxy-coupling chapter, but the stronger translation-survival assertion remains conditional on the missing controlled readout matrix.
