\subsection{A finite B-star-Q6 coupling matrix at the translation boundary}
\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}

\paragraph{Object.}
The record concerns the nine-dimensional $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ synonymous-residual coordinate system tested against translation-adjacent readouts after quotienting by amino-acid composition.  The coordinate list is
$K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The first finite matrix uses the $61$-codon count space and reads the coordinates against a proxy $tRNA$-Leu $CUN$-versus-$UUR$ log-copy spectrum in three organisms: $Homo\ sapiens$, $Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae$, and $Escherichia\ coli\ K12$.  The statistic is a coupling matrix $A\_{ij}$ on residual count covariance, not a survival matrix $S\_{ij}$.

\paragraph{Finite-row coupling result.}
For the proxy contact, all nine residual coordinates were computed and all nine entries exceeded the declared nonzero threshold in absolute value.  The break condition was that every coordinate satisfy $|A\_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout; this condition was not met.  The largest absolute coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max\_{i,j}|A\_{ij}| &= 1882464.1106377328,\\
\#\{(i,j): |A\_{ij}|\geq 0.01\} &= 9,\\
\#\{\hbox{organisms}\} &= 3.
\end{aligned}
$$
This establishes a finite proxy-coupling contact for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis.  It does not by itself establish a positive $S\_{ij}$ survival entry, because the proxy readout is tRNA gene-copy spectrum rather than measured ribosome occupancy, measured translation efficiency, stAI survival, or protein abundance under the full covariate set.

\paragraph{Leucine gate row.}
The associated $CUN/UUR$ leucine gate was computed on the same three-organism scale.  The partial Pearson value after the stated control was $1.0$ with $n=3$, while the organism signs were not uniform: the logged ratios were $1.3514980212896148$, $-0.24869078488829677$, and $1.0086640520051702$, with signs $+1,-1,+1$.  This makes the row useful as a directional stress test but not as a universal directionality claim.  The finite table says that the contact is present and nontrivial; it does not say that the sign is stable across biological contexts.

\paragraph{Modeled translation survival context.}
A larger modeled-$tAI$ calculation supplies a stronger translation-adjacent comparison while remaining bounded to modeled adaptation weights.  Across $40$ organisms, the nine $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates were residualized against $24$ controls with rank $23$, leaving residual degrees of freedom $17$.  The modeled readout matrix had shape $9\times 9$, rank $9$ after residualization, and
$$
\begin{aligned}
R^2\_{QT} &= 0.9003849000036579,\\
\#\{\hbox{organisms}\} &= 40,\\
\#\{\hbox{readout columns}\} &= 9.
\end{aligned}
$$
The readout here is modeled per-codon $tAI$ from GtRNAdb anticodon copy counts and dos Reis wobble weights.  A separate organism sweep over $41$ organisms reported an $R^2\_{QT}$ distribution with minimum $0.1579271837024072$, first quartile $0.26353954700074694$, median $0.33934845811598124$, mean $0.38466555440501393$, third quartile $0.5143951545826744$, and maximum $0.8213457220900503$, using $1000$ permutations.  These rows support the statement that the residual coordinate family remains visible to modeled translation weights after the controls, while still not replacing measured ribosome or protein-output contacts.

\paragraph{Measured ribosome-position check.}
A local ribosome-occupancy analysis joined $5754$ genes after the ordered-CDS length check, with $5754$ gene identifiers overlapping the occupancy file and no length mismatches.  The position confound was handled by exact file-provided window and codon-index bins with amino-acid bins, and the null shuffled synonymous labels within the same bin.  Nine families had at least $200$ permutations.  With $\lambda=0.01$, $DL=2$, and the rule $C\_H>0$, the families passing all gates were $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, and $f3\_stress$.  This is the closest measured ribosome-contact row in the packet, but its conclusion is local codon-window occupancy separation, not a completed organism-level $S\_{ij}$ matrix for every coordinate and readout.

\paragraph{Protein-abundance and complement rows.}
The protein-abundance rows show that the same residual coordinate family has downstream statistical associations, again with explicit limits.  In the modeled-$tAI$ complement calculation, six organisms were evaluated with $24$ controls and rank $23$.  The abundance component explained by residualized $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ coordinates had $R^2\_{QP}=0.29837624987287115$ in yeast, $0.10767526667768725$ in $E.\ coli$ K-12 MG1655, $0.04613529805760884$ in zebrafish, $0.04454565117192484$ in human, $0.03980793595345331$ in chicken, and $0.032090091625816136$ in $Mycobacterium\ smegmatis$.  The modeled-$tAI$ complement fractions $d$ were $0.21173219572706703$ in yeast, $0.6629960038578532$ in $E.\ coli$, $0.874628721049266$ in zebrafish, $0.9208949355679455$ in human, $0.9400341806302775$ in chicken, and $0.8972672018746489$ in $M.\ smegmatis$.  Thus yeast is the clearest case where the modeled translation component absorbs a large share, with $m=0.788267804272933$, while the other organisms retain larger modeled-translation-complement components.

These ranked rows classify the residual signal as readout-dependent rather than a single-module biological law.

\paragraph{Condition and tissue rows.}
Condition-specific yeast abundance comparisons used shared $n=2209$ proteins.  The $YEPD$ row had $R^2\_{QP}=0.3280825603424665$ and the $SD$ row had $R^2\_{QP}=0.3050385538305022$; both had dominant coordinate $f3\_stress$, with cosine $0.9993775935182967$ and Pearson $0.9983880256003688$ across the two condition vectors. \paragraph{Per-coordinate decomposition.}
A yeast per-coordinate decomposition used $n=1258$ joined proteins, $5$ folds, $120$ permutations, and $24$ controls.  It reported all nine coordinates in the decomposition table and found real-source support for $K\_AAA$, $Arg\_AGR$, $Ile\_AUA$, $Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR$, $Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG$, $Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY$, $Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY$, and $f3\_stress$.  The $Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG$ coordinate remained listed in the nine-row decomposition but was not in the real-source support list.  For the $f3\_stress$ family decomposition, $21$ synonymous-family subcoordinates were computed at $n=1800$ with $5$ folds and $120$ permutations.  The full $f3\_stress$ projection had $P\_{f3}$ norm squared $52.76911713166682$ and fraction of target $0.12259712041843235$ against target norm squared $430.42705205115925$; $19$ of the $21$ family subcoordinates were reported as real-source families.

\paragraph{Local conclusion.}
The finite evidence is ready for a bounded BioReality chapter about $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual coordinates meeting translation-adjacent and protein-adjacent contacts.  The core proxy coupling row is positive in the sense that the threshold break condition failed with nine nonzero proxy-coupling entries and maximum absolute coupling $1882464.1106377328$.  The surrounding rows show modeled-$tAI$ survival, local ribosome-window separation, protein-abundance association, mRNA-stability association, mediation partitioning, and condition or tissue dependence.  The correct reading is therefore not that translation, folding, physical admissibility, biological function, mechanism, or universality has been proved.  Those stronger readings require separate measured contacts and their own controlled statistics.  In this chapter, the admissible claim is narrower: after amino-acid, GC3, length, organism-context, and $M$-density controls where the rows provide them, the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis repeatedly leaves finite statistical traces at the translation boundary and downstream abundance boundary, while the original three-organism proxy coupling remains a proxy-coupling result rather than a completed $S\_{ij}$ survival matrix.
