\subsection{B-star Q6 residual coupling against a Leu-copy proxy}\label{sec:bioreality-b-star-q6-coupling-matrix}
\origin{ai}

\paragraph{Object of the finite row.}
This record concerns a nine-coordinate residual test for the $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ codon-usage layer after imposing the amino-acid quotient.  The carrier is a matrix of residual coordinates $q_i$ against translation-adjacent readouts $r_j$, with the intended survival entry written as $S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)$.  The control family $Z$ is not optional: it includes amino-acid composition, organism context, $GC3$, coding-sequence length, and an $M$-density baseline.  In the present finite row, the available computed object is not the full survival matrix $S_{ij}$.  It is the proxy coupling matrix $A_{ij}$ obtained from synonymous residual coordinates against a tRNA-Leu CUN-versus-UUR log-copy spectrum.  The codon-count ambient space has $61$ sense-codon coordinates, and the organism rows loaded for the finite computation are $\mathrm{Homo\ sapiens}$, $\mathrm{Saccharomyces\ cerevisiae}$, and $\mathrm{Escherichia\ coli\ K12}$.

\paragraph{Residual coordinates actually computed.}
The row contains exactly nine synonymous residual coordinates:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathrm{Arg\_AGR},\quad \mathrm{Ile\_AUA},\quad \mathrm{K\_AAA},\quad \mathrm{Leu\_CUN\_vs\_UUR},\quad \mathrm{Leu\_UUA\_vs\_UUG},\\
&\mathrm{Ser\_UCA\_vs\_UCG},\quad \mathrm{Ser\_UCR\_vs\_AGY},\quad \mathrm{Thr\_ACR\_vs\_ACY},\quad f3\text{-}\mathrm{stress}.
\end{aligned}
$$
For each such coordinate, the computation keeps the amino-acid quotient explicit through the synonymous projection $P_{\tau}$.  Thus the observed signal is not a raw codon-composition association: it is a residual association after the amino-acid class has been separated from synonymous variation.  The break condition for the proxy matrix was the event that every one of the nine coordinates had $|A_{ij}|<0.01$ against the proxy readout.  That break condition was not met.  The largest absolute proxy coupling was
$$
\begin{aligned}
\max_{i,j}|A_{ij}| = 1882464.1106377328,
\end{aligned}
$$
and all $9$ residual coordinates contributed nonzero proxy entries above the zero-entry level recorded by the run.

\paragraph{Leu CUN/UUR cross-organism row.}
A second finite row isolates the Leu CUN-versus-UUR gate across the same three organisms.  The recorded log-copy ratios are
$$
\begin{aligned}
(1.3514980212896148,\;-0.24869078488829677,\;1.0086640520051702),
\end{aligned}
$$
with sign pattern
$$
\begin{aligned}
(+1,\;-1,\;+1).
\end{aligned}
$$
The three-row Pearson coefficient is $1.0$, and the amino-acid-composition-controlled partial Pearson coefficient reported for the same finite row is also $1.0$ with $n=3$.  The sign is not uniform across organisms.  This matters for interpretation: the row supports a finite paired contact between codon usage and tRNA-Leu copy-ratio geometry, but it does not license a universal direction for the effect.  The middle organism has the opposite sign from the other two rows, so the correct reading is context dependence inside a very small table, not a global monotone rule.

\paragraph{What the proxy row establishes.}
The positive finding is narrow.  The nine-coordinate $B^{\ast}_{Q6}$ residual basis is not annihilated by the available tRNA-Leu proxy contact under the stated $0.01$ coupling threshold.  Equivalently, within the three-organism codon-count and tRNA-copy table, the synonymous residual geometry leaves measurable proxy covariance after amino-acid quotienting.  The result is therefore a codon-usage topology contact with a translation-adjacent annotation, not a completed translation-survival result.  The computation supplies $A_{ij}$-type evidence for residual coupling, but it does not by itself supply the controlled survival score $S_{ij}$, partial $R^2$, or description-length improvement for any direct translation readout.

\paragraph{Blocked survival promotion.}
The intended survival statement would require at least one entry satisfying
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{ij}=S_{q_i}(r_j\mid Z)>0
\end{aligned}
$$
for a direct readout such as $\mathrm{tAI}$, $\mathrm{stAI}$, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or matched protein abundance, with all declared controls retained and the simpler-codon baseline ablated.  That statistic is not present in the finite row.  The row explicitly says that true survival requires ribo-seq, stAI, or protein-abundance readouts under controls, and that the current codon-count plus tRNA-copy proxy does not establish ribosome-mediated translation realization.  Consequently, a positive $A_{ij}$ entry cannot be promoted to a positive $S_{ij}$ entry.  The present chapter records a proxy coupling that keeps the survival question open.

\paragraph{Local non-promotion rule.}
No translation realization, folding conclusion, physical admissibility conclusion, biological function, mechanistic explanation, pathway claim, protein-output claim, population-level estimate, or universal biological law follows from this row alone.  tRNA gene-copy counts are genome-annotation summaries, not tRNA abundance, tRNA charging, ribosome occupancy, translation efficiency, or protein abundance.  Ribosome-profiling and matched mRNA rows, when attached, would still need organism-matched identifiers and the full $Z$-controlled statistic before any $S_{ij}>0$ entry could be reported.  Matched proteomics would test a downstream abundance readout only after its own mapping and controls are supplied.  Thus the current evidential status is: a finite three-organism residual coupling matrix is observed; a Leu CUN/UUR finite row has Pearson $1.0$ and partial Pearson $1.0$ with $n=3$ but non-uniform signs; the translation-survival matrix remains uncomputed until direct translation or protein readouts are paired with codon usage and controls.
